
According to statistics, during the 2017 Spring Festival travel rush, Didi Hitchhiking transported 4.2 million passenger trips. Converted based on existing railway capacity, this was equivalent to adding 1,909 conventional slow trains or 5,874 8-car EMU trains, which was 4 times the number of passengers transported by trains during the same period last year. A total of 2.8 million car owners participated in empty seat sharing. Even on New Year's Eve (22:00 on Jan 27 to 6:00 the next day), 70,000 people still embarked on their journey home for reunion. During the Spring Festival travel rush (Jan 28 - Feb 1), cross-city hitchhiking was mainly for short-distance home returns, visiting relatives, and short-distance tours around surrounding cities. ^(2)

From the perspective of passenger structure, people from Wenzhou prefer to travel alone, with 66.3% of Wenzhou people returning alone via hitchhiking; people from Guangzhou prefer to travel in groups, with 52.11% of Guangzhou people choosing to return via group hitchhiking.

Under the carpooling and hitchhiking models, idle resources are utilized to the maximum extent, becoming an effective way to relieve traffic pressure. Most importantly, the government and enterprises have obtained real first-hand data on people's travel through hitchhiking and carpooling. The government formulates congestion relief plans and decisions based on this, and enterprise services are more precise.

In addition to carpooling for small cars, other modes of transportation have also attempted carpooling. For example, in October 2017, the Xi'an Railway Bureau launched train carpooling through crowdfunding. Passengers participating in the crowdfunding boarded the train on time at the agreed time, and the train adjusted its original departure time to a time convenient for passengers, avoiding the embarrassment of passengers having no cars or buses after arriving at their destination late at night. This is another form of carpooling, presented in a crowdsourcing manner. If it develops maturely, multiple special trains can be customized through carpooling to avoid empty runs (see Figure 4.2).

Of course, large buses like Dudu Bus also have carpooling and hitchhiking models. Regardless of the model, the data precipitated by the platform is the most real and valuable. It is precisely with the support of these data that big data transportation becomes more efficient and intelligent.

Liu Xianglong introduced that Customized Bus is an important component of public transport.
