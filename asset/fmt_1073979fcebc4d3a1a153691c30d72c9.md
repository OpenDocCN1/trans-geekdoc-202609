
#### 9.2.5.6 RMSProp

$$g_{t} = \beta g_{t - 1} + \left( {1 - \beta} \right)\left\lbrack \frac{\partial L}{\partial w_{t - 1}} \right\rbrack^{2}.$$

$$
\left. \begin{matrix} 
{w_{t} = w_{t - 1} - \frac{\eta}{\sqrt{E\left\lbrack g^{2} \right\rbrack}}\frac{\partial c}{\partial w_{t - 1}}} \\ 
\quad \\ 
{{E\left\lbrack g^{2} \right\rbrack}_{t} = \beta{E\left\lbrack g^{2} \right\rbrack}_{t - 1} + \left( {1 - \beta} \right)\left\lbrack \frac{\partial c}{\partial w_{t - 1}} \right\rbrack^{2}} \\ 
\end{matrix} \right\}, \quad (9\text{--}14)
$$

其中 `E[g]` 是梯度的平方的移动平均值，而 `∂c/∂w` 是成本函数相对于权重的梯度。

#### 9.2.5.7 Nadam

$$\theta_{t + 1} = \theta_{t} - \frac{\eta}{\sqrt{{\widehat{v}}_{t}} + \epsilon}\left( {\beta_{1}{\widehat{m}}_{t} + \frac{1 - \beta_{1}}{1 - \beta_{1}^{t}}g}_{t} \right), \quad (9\text{--}15)$$

其中 ${\widehat{m}}_{t} = \frac{m_{t}}{1 - \beta_{1}^{t}}$ 且 ${\widehat{v}}_{t} = \frac{v_{t}}{1 - \beta_{2}^{t}}$。
