
Multimodal transport refers to a logistics transportation process completed by the connection and transshipment of two or more modes of transport. With the development of the national economy, vigorously developing multimodal transport is a key task for comprehensive transportation development during the "13th Five-Year Plan" period. Strategically, it is an important focus for China to promote supply-side structural reform in transportation; from an enterprise perspective, it is an effective way to reduce logistics costs.

In the process of strategic innovation in multimodal transport development, various regions have currently shown an important leap toward big data transportation.

On May 19, 2017, the Zhengzhou Airport International Logistics Multimodal Transport Data Trading Service Platform was officially launched. The launch of this platform realized information sharing between domestic highway, railway, seaport, and other transport modes and the full-process transportation chain of international aviation and overseas land transport, guaranteeing the formation of information linkage in areas such as Zhengzhou Airport, the Pilot Free Trade Zone, and bonded parks.

就政府而言，郑州市相关部门表示将提供一站式信息服务，实现物流企业信息服务“只进一扇门”。此外，他们将依托龙头骨干企业，建设涉及多式联运企业的互联网数据交互平台，以持续释放大数据资源红利。

在多式联运过程中，建设大数据平台相当于为多式联运所有参与者植入“中枢神经”。在已建成应用的多式联运综合信息平台上，大数据运输技术建立了订单信息平台、箱管理信息系统和公路物流系统等分支系统，可实现对车辆、集装箱、货物的全程信息监控。

### 7.6.1 铁路供应链中大数据的应用空间

铁路货运在多式联运中发挥着重要作用。大数据技术对铁路供应链转型的支撑也需要拓展更多空间。事实上，互联网、大数据、云计算等科技手段能让铁路货运凭借其原有优势，寻找新的改进机遇。

目前，铁路货运在多式联运中仅扮演承运人的角色，其揽货优势在于运费低廉和距离终端客户较远。铁路货运利用其他物流运输渠道的能力不足，无法与公路、水路、航空运输资源顺畅共享数据。

因此，铁路货运必须善用大数据优势，通过有效推广集装箱多式联运构建自身独特的数据资产。然后，根据多式联运运营模式，实现上下游物流企业的数据共享与融合，完成跨界融合。最终，可实现大数据资源的有效变现。

### 7.6.2 多式联运路线的优化与调度

在多式联运的物流配送过程中，企业最关心的是产品质量和运输速度。为保证产品质量并降低运输成本，企业必须持续优化和调度路线。

通过多式联运车辆内部的感应系统，在装卸产品时，车载系统可将产品上传感器的数据信息上传至服务器。服务器接收数据后，可实时分析车辆和产品信息，并根据实际运力、客户偏好和配送路线数据进行动态计算，从而获得最新最优路线。随着数据的沉淀和技术的进步，可结合 `GPS` 定位系统与路况情况进行实时更新。

从宏观角度看，远程控制系统也可通过大数据分析，在多式联运过程中获取剩余配送时间，以便及时通知客户提货。因多式联运是一个连续过程，环节断裂易导致整个系统失效。因此，必须利用大数据实现从生产厂家到客户的整体联动，将破损率降至最低，将等待时间减至最小，节约物流企业成本，提高配送效率。

### 7.6.3 大数据推动集装箱多式联运的改进

标准化集装箱是多式联运的基本单位和运作基础。然而，在我国公路运输和铁路运输中，集装箱利用率相当低，制约了多式联运的发展。

针对集装箱多式联运的现有困境，集装箱单元细分将能采用尺寸更小、适合小型配送车辆的运输标准。相应的管理也必须利用大数据提高效率。在集装箱多式联运新模式下，大数据、传感器、云计算等技术手段可实时采集、获取和分析海量信息，并通过各类通信终端平台实现新型集装箱内的配置平衡。这样可大幅降低设备建设和运营的高昂成本，提高多式联运业务占比。

通过大数据技术的应用，多式联运流程可持续优化。未来，将能实现多式联运箱管理参与方（如货代、报关企业、铁路、港口、监管场站等）关键数据信息的同平台互联互通，从而实现全流程电子化作业和全环节实时跟踪，提升整体物流效率。

### 7.7 大数据运输与跨境物流

在全球化背景下，跨境电商蓬勃发展。然而，由于跨境物流存在海关监管等环节，加之各国政策、法律环境、商业环境、文化环境、支付方式、税收政策和汇率制度不同，跨境物流的复杂性显著增加。

鉴于此，跨境物流引入大数据运输系统的关键性已迫在眉睫。

2017 年 12 月 20 日，“共享仓”跨境生态服务平台正式上线。该平台可为跨境物流海外仓出口提供专属服务。在跨境物流通关服务领域，利用大数据、云服务、互联网等新服务技术，挖掘海外物流仓储等闲置资源，创造跨境物流共享经济模式。

通过“共享仓”服务，外贸企业可实现跨境物流上下游企业间的数据互联与共享交互，从而解决中国跨境出口企业面临的物流瓶颈。

事实上，跨境物流的运输流和信息流已实现实时联网。跨境物流模式具有自身特点，因此数据碎片化现象始终存在。此外，跨境物流的个性化需求日益增长。快速、高效、安全、个性化的跨境物流解决方案离不开大数据、物联网和云计算的结合。

以下是跨境物流未来可通过大数据技术获得的推广方向：

### Cross-border Logistics Big Data Application Strategies

#### (1) Establish a Unified Cross-border Logistics Big Data Platform

Currently, there is an urgent need to form a unified cross-border logistics big data platform. Led by government departments, this platform develops and integrates enterprise resources to form fourth-party logistics services, thereby building smart cross-border logistics. This is an important way to solve the problem.

For example, on December 26, 2017, at the China-Myanmar Border Economic and Trade Fair, the construction of the Southeast Asia Smart Logistics Big Data Center was officially launched, reflecting the future development direction.

On the new cross-border logistics big data platform, a full-service chain should serve as the platform to integrate various resources, thereby providing cross-border logistics data information and value-added data service chain solutions.

#### (2) Establish a Cross-border Logistics Big Data Information Center

Currently, China is conducting economic and trade exchanges with surrounding countries and regions. In this process, the production, warehousing, and transportation of goods generate massive amounts of data. Among them, the variety of data types, uncertainty in management between data and models, diversity of logistics objects, and the advancement of data collection, analysis, and processing tools are all characteristics that traditional technologies cannot fully cope with. Therefore, establishing a cross-border logistics big data information center requires focusing on the collection, cleaning, integration, screening, and updating of data from all periods and links of logistics cooperation among various countries and regions. Then, data mining technology is used to mine the potential information in the data to realize data value-added. Finally, results are presented in a visual manner to achieve precise guidance for improving cross-border logistics service quality.

#### (3) Build a Big Data Transnational Logistics System

The construction of a cross-border big data information center is the first step to realize a big data transnational logistics system. An intelligent logistics system based on big data technology can enable the mutual integration of online information and offline logistics, achieving coordinated development.

First, the accuracy of logistics information identification in the cross-border logistics process should be improved through digital and visualization technologies in big data.

Second, big data should be used to define the logistics resources that need to be configured according to the expected logistics needs and conditions of the service objects, thereby planning more efficient one-stop cross-border logistics solutions.

Third, big data technology, `GPS` systems, and Geographic Information Systems (`GIS`) should be effectively combined to accurately obtain real-time information on the location of goods during the logistics process. Furthermore, `RFID` technology should be used to collect the status of goods at different time points and locations in real-time. The aforementioned data and information are shared synchronously with customers to facilitate the adjustment and optimization of real-time logistics plans.

It is worth noting that cross-border logistics carries certain risks. For cross-border enterprises using self-operated logistics, products may become unsalable and pile up after entering the local market. In this case, the cost of shipping products back to the home country is huge, leaving only destruction as an option. However, with the help of big data analysis, enterprises can effectively integrate big data, analyze global commodity demand and freight information, and judge product market placement through data analysis results. For example, big data technology can analyze regional product preferences by integrating customer click, browsing, preference, review, and purchase data, thereby guiding logistics operations.

#### (4) Improve Overseas Warehouse Utilization Rate

Warehousing management and utilization efficiency are important factors affecting logistics service levels. Many cross-border logistics enterprises are in a passive position in overseas warehouse utilization due to a lack of data support. In fact, the decision basis for setting up overseas warehouses depends on big data analysis. Enterprises must conduct systematic analysis based on data such as their own business scale, economic conditions of the trading country, and logistics orders, and judge whether to lease or self-build overseas warehouses through direct results. Then, based on data such as the economic conditions of the trading country, the number of consumers, and the maturity of the transportation network, the optimal site is selected.
