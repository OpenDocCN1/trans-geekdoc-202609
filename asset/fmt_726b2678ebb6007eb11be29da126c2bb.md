
The Hangzhou big data traffic management model also actively introduces third-party enterprise forces. Data from Didi, Baidu Maps, etc., are also imported into the Hangzhou Big Data Traffic Platform and released by the Hangzhou Traffic Police Department. For example, with the help of third-party enterprise big data statistics, the Hangzhou Traffic Management Department found that as of the end of February 2017, the number of female drivers in Hangzhou reached 1.3964 million, accounting for 36.3% of the total number of motor vehicle drivers, which can provide data support for the formulation of traffic regulations. Parking lot construction, drainage planning, and route planning can take the female group into consideration, making them more in line with Hangzhou's traffic characteristics.

In December 2017, another major big data traffic event occurred in Hangzhou. On December 2, 23 big data enterprises established the Zhejiang Traffic Big Data Maker Space, featuring "Internet + Traffic," to serve traffic in Hangzhou and even the whole of Zhejiang Province. Among them were not only institutions such as the Zhejiang Provincial Traffic Information Center, the Zhejiang Provincial Highway Bureau Settlement Center, the Zhejiang Provincial Tourism Information Center, and the Zhejiang Expressway Information Engineering Technology Co., Ltd., but also Baidu, Huawei, Alibaba Cloud, Prologis, Beijing E-Hualu, Wangshen, and the three major operators, jointly constructing a complete new big data management model. In the future, the big data management model will inevitably present a collaborative posture of "departmental institutions + third-party enterprises," effectively integrating forces from all parties to find the key to urban traffic development.

### 10.6 Big Data Traffic Credit Model

The operation of traffic big data also requires credit support. This is because, whether it is data collection, screening, analysis, or release, the grassroots masses are often at the receiving end. If information frequently deviates, it will inevitably lead to public distrust of big data traffic, affecting the operation of big data.

那么，我们应该从什么角度来解决大数据交通的信任问题？

#### 10.6.1 多渠道与专业发布

发布大数据时，交通部门除自身平台外，还应与其他渠道合作，特别是官方平台和认证平台，如本地交通广播电台、电视台、车载终端、车辆滚动显示屏、可变情报板和交通警示标志。大数据应及时与这些渠道对接，第一时间发布信息，让公众第一时间了解相关动态。

此外，在公共场所，交通部门还应与区域微信公众平台和微博建立合作关系，向运营商提供相关信息并通过其平台发布数据。必须选择认证平台合作，以确保信息真实有效地推送给公众，提升数据发布的公信力。一旦缺乏与这些渠道的合作，必然导致虚假信息和谣言信息迅速传播，大数据的价值无法体现，进而影响交通管理部门的公信力。

#### 10.6.2 通俗易懂与专业解读

对于大数据交通信息，因涉及专业性，许多内容必须再加工，以便公众轻松理解。但“通俗易懂”不代表“不专业”，否则易引起公众对大数据信息的误解和不信任。

因此，在让大数据通俗易懂的过程中，交通部门还应从专业角度进行解读，让公众了解大数据运行机制并心服口服。例如，发布数据时应强调交通流分析、超时预测和时段识别，公众可通过历史对比看清问题。同时，结合静态照片和动态视频分析路况，让用户感受到有价值的信息，也能看到大数据如何挖掘信息、分析信息、决策，从而大幅提升大数据的信用价值。

一旦建立对大数据的信任，公众将主动参与信息采集。例如，大多数移动 `APP` 具有定位功能。当公众意识到定位能有效进行数据传输并进一步优化交通路线时，也会主动上传地理位置、开启监控功能，从而推动大数据采集更加完善。

#### 10.6.3 联合公安部门打击谣言

交通关乎公众基本生活，是关注焦点，也是谣言高发区。若交通管理部门不处理这些谣言，易导致虚假信息扩散，严重影响正常交通信息发布，引发对大数据交通管理的质疑。因此，交通部门应主动配合公安部门打击交通谣言，避免虚假信息进一步扩散。

例如，2014 年 7 月，广东省肇庆市广宁县突然传出消息：前一天在广宁县南街镇路段发生重大交通事故，致 4 人死亡，并附 3 张图片。该消息在当地迅速传播，大量网友转发，当地群众质疑交警工作。随后当地交警部门迅速介入，查明这是一起网络谣言。犯罪嫌疑人为出风头，在其 `QQ` 空间发布虚假信息。交警部门与公安部门迅速联动，依法拘留嫌疑人，最终辟谣，交警部门形象得以恢复。

对此类事件，交通部门必须严肃对待数据，否则易使公众对交通部门产生负面印象。一旦发生，即便发布准确的大数据信息，也难获公众信任，大数据交通管理的意义将不复存在。

# 第 11 章

# 大数据交通的未来发展

大数据交通发展多年，逐渐成熟，但也面临诸多挑战。且随行业细分，为未来更好服务公众，交通大数据需持续改进技术、持续融合多层级内容与技术，使未来大数据交通更智能、更人性化。

### 11.1 大数据交通行业的个性化服务

虽中国大数据交通行业因涉及基本民生起步较晚，但在大商业公司快速布局下，行业呈现快速发展势头。过去中国大数据交通需依赖美国等发达国家技术，现已成为行业领跑者，各类新技术、新概念层出不穷。展望行业发展，未来大数据交通行业将进一步渗透至个性化服务，甚至形成“一个人的交通大数据”模式，实现“定制专属服务”。

以下部分个性化服务已进入研发阶段，部分仍处于立项阶段，它们勾勒出大数据交通行业未来的发展方向。

#### 11.1.1 智能车路协同技术

目前中国汽车市场已开始尝试“智能系统”。比亚迪、长安等一系列自主品牌在新车型上尝试车路协同技术。例如，`MG` 汽车引入的“斑马技术”集成多种导航模式，可实时显示当前路况拥堵情况，有效辅助驾驶员重新规划路线、优化路网资源。这是典型的“车路协同”尝试。

但虽有厂商开始尝试，大规模应用尚未全面铺开。特别是高速行驶过程中，远距离环境感知尚不成熟。同时，车路协同标准规范体系未建立，导致技术混乱、系统上线后用户体验不佳。

预计未来几年，“智能车路协同技术”将迎来井喷期，甚至成为汽车标配之一。该技术可有效结合驾驶员，捕捉个人驾驶习惯，结合大数据定制优化驾驶模式和路线规划。

#### 11.1.2 自动驾驶技术

`Autonomous driving technology` is the most watched new transportation revolution in recent years. Currently, most automotive enterprises worldwide have begun R&D on autonomous driving technology, and China's Baidu has successfully launched its own autonomous vehicles, marking a critical step forward for autonomous driving.

Compared with traditional driving modes, autonomous driving technology relies entirely on big data operations. It is a high-end artificial intelligence capable of real-time road condition judgment based on massive map data. Compared to manual driving, autonomous driving technology will play an ultimate role in routing, driving modes, and emergency avoidance, maximizing the benefits of big data. Therefore, future autonomous driving technology will gradually replace traditional driving modes, and big data will become the primary "natural person" of autonomous driving.

#### 11.1.3 Connected Vehicles (Internet of Vehicles)

Compared with vehicle-road coordination technology and autonomous driving technology, connected vehicles have seen widespread application. The characteristic of connected vehicles is implanting internet capabilities into traditional driving modes, such as real-time voice broadcasting of road conditions, voice association between mobile phones and vehicles in critical road conditions, rapid payment upon entering highways, and intelligent parking payment. Many brand vehicles have integrated mobile payment, which is an effective attempt at internet technology.

For example, vehicle health checks are an effective application of big data via the internet. Through big data, connected vehicles collect usage data from all users, analyze vehicle pros and cons, maintenance cycles, and tire pressure, and send this information to owners via onboard screens or mobile `Apps`. Thus, owners will fully understand vehicle conditions and perform timely maintenance, avoiding sudden vehicle breakdowns on the road. When vehicles are in healthy condition, traffic accidents will also be significantly reduced.

#### 11.1.4 Rapid Promotion of ETC Technology

Currently, `ETC` technology has been actively and effectively promoted in many provinces. It is also a manifestation of personalized services in the big data transportation industry. `ETC`, or `Electronic Toll Collection System`, is currently the most advanced toll collection method in the world. After a vehicle installs an `ETC` card, it can utilize microwave dedicated short-range communication between microwave antennas at `ETC` lanes before highway toll stations, and use computer networking technology for backend settlement processing with banks. Vehicles can complete payment without waiting, greatly alleviating the queuing and payment problem at highway entrances/exits.

Undoubtedly, `ETC` technology can be used for traffic optimization, applicable not only at highway toll stations but also in scenarios suitable for payment. In the future, `ETC` will further play the role of "traffic resource optimization," deeply integrating with bus cards and electronic traffic management cards to achieve the goal of "three cards in one." This will further form the "Transportation + Big Data" model. One card can meet multiple needs such as driving, riding, and payment, greatly improving traffic efficiency and alleviating traffic congestion and resource waste issues.

#### 11.1.5 Deep Integration with Individuals
