
## 政治决策层面的呼吁

然而，在政治决策层面，本书应当翻译以反映我的研究立场：网络恐怖主义是一种具体、独特、社会性的现象，值得进一步的犯罪学评估和执法支持，需要投入大量额外资源。后者可能包括对所有警察、调查人员和检察官实施至少基础的数字意识和取证培训。

网络恐怖主义的调查优势在于——如果正确调查——存在丰富的（数字）证据链。这个在线的宝库可以揭示许多关联的个人、事件以及实物证据。进入数字犯罪现象已有二十年，令人震惊的是，大量事后调查报告和恐怖相关事件的调查显示，家人、朋友、同事或其他计算机网络用户多次提出关于个人严重威胁的警报。线索在于了解违规者的在线行为。显然，这类信息应当被视为至少与其他任何重要的社区情报同等重要。太多时候，这种深刻且勇敢的报告（恐怖主义线索）被视为低风险，因为它们难以验证，或家庭闲谈被视为传闻、gossip。直白地说，网络赋能的恐怖案件往往被推诿给家庭或企业去处理。

因此，我们认为，关键的预防挑战之一是确保那些勇敢地站出来（提供关键信息并投入大量个人时间和精力）的人，能够更加了解现有的法律，以帮助地方警察动员正式调查或干预，从而可能以起诉和公共安全解决方案结束。

## 历史教训与计算机伦理

最后，我们将通过强调历史上最严厉但最少被学习的教训来结束本项目。那些处于影响力位置——如父母和监护人、教师、企业领袖和立法者——未能积极推广、鼓励和（必要时）强制执行他们所负责的对象的行为伦理，将导致任何良好系统腐烂、破裂并最终失效。我们的数字生态系统亦是如此。计算机伦理至关重要。每位用户都应学习它们、遵守它们、教导它们、向他人展示它们。

此时，我们可以回到“我们之前做过的”模式，向读者介绍或提醒 `Ramon Barquin` 的象征性“计算机伦理十诫”：

1. `Thou shalt not use a computer to harm other people.`
2. `Thou shalt not interfere with other people’s computer work.`
3. `Thou shalt not snoop around in other people’s computer files.`
4. `Thou shalt not use a computer to steal.`
5. `Thou shalt not use a computer to bear false witness.`
6. `Thou shalt not copy or use proprietary software for which you have not paid.`
7. `Thou shalt not use other people’s computer resources without authorization or proper compensation.`
8. `Thou shalt appropriate other people’s intellectual output.`
9. `Thou shalt think about the social consequences of the program you are writing or the system you are designing.`
10. `Thou shalt always use a computer in ways that insure consideration and respect for your fellow humans` (`Ramon Barquin`, Computer Ethics Institute: 1992).

If, for whatever reason, we can’t bring ourselves to lead in this space and commit to these, then maybe it’s worth reflecting upon former NSA technical director `Robert Morris`’s prophetic words. After chasing the first generation of cyber criminals and helping to jail (and recruit) the most dangerous, Morris discovered it was his own son who bore responsibility for unleashing the world’s worst computer worm. His offspring reportedly brought down a significant chunk of the then embryonic Internet. In retirement-mode, Morris senior reportedly opened up to an Infosec event audience:

```
“The three golden rules to ensure computer security are: do not own a computer; do not power it on; and do not use it”
```

([WebDeveloperNotes.com](http://WebDeveloperNotes.com)). I’m not, at this point, seriously advocating these nuggets of wisdom. But we do need to go back to basics somewhat and treat our Internet with kid gloves.

## 附录 A：关键组织与数字平台

### Action Counters Terrorism (ACT)

UK only. Run by `Counter Terrorism Policing`, `ACT` provides the online reporting portal for suspected terrorism and violent extremism offences and behaviours. Good baseline advise for ascertaining behaviour and actions that may be fairly considered reportable is provided online as well as basic security training and information about the ‘Run Hide Tell’ campaign.

**Website:** [`act.campaign.gov.uk/`](https://act.campaign.gov.uk/)

**Twitter (now X):** [`Twitter.com/TerrorismPolice`](https://Twitter.com/TerrorismPolice)

### America Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA)

`AICPA` has developed a set of `Trust Services Criteria (TSC)`, which is now widely applied within the US and, increasingly, around the world for audit procurement purposes. Service organisations are expected to undertake `System and Organisational Controls (SOC)` audits. A `SOC 2` audit report provides detailed information and assurance about a service organisation’s security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality and privacy controls. These are the five benchmarks to satisfy `AICPA`’s now omnipotent `TSC` requirements.

You can learn more about `AICPA`’s `SOC` for cyber security here:

[`www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/learn-about-soc-for-cybersecurity`](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/learn-about-soc-for-cybersecurity)

You can learn more about `AICPA`’s `SOC` for the supply chain here:

[`www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/learn-about-soc-for-supply-chain`](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/learn-about-soc-for-supply-chain)

### Australia’s Cyber and Infrastructure Security Centre (CISC)

Assists critical infrastructure personnel to understand cyber risk and meet regulatory requirements in Australia. `CISC Australia` deploys a network of outreach officers and publishes “risk assessment advisories”. It recruited an inaugural `National Cyber Security Coordinator` in 2023. Follow links below to find out more:

**Website:** [`www.cisc.gov.au/`](https://www.cisc.gov.au/)

**Twitter (now X):** [`Twitter.com/CyberGovAU`](https://Twitter.com/CyberGovAU)

### Big Brother Watch (BBW)

Based in the UK, and a little like the American non-profit `EFF` (see below), `BBW` has fought and prevailed in several landmark legal cases whereby its supporters perceive counter terrorism and crime prevention initiatives using surveillance have been deployed unlawfully. `BBW` describes itself as *“a UK civil liberties campaign group fighting for a free future. We’re determined to reclaim our privacy and defend freedoms at this time of enormous technological change”.*

**Website:** [`bigbrotherwatch.org.uk/`](https://bigbrotherwatch.org.uk/)

**Twitter (now X):** [`Twitter.com/BigBrotherWatch`](https://Twitter.com/BigBrotherWatch)
