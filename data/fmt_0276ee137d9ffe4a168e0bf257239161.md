```
x_t = sqrt(1 - beta_t)x_{t-1} + sqrt(beta_t)eta_t,    eta_t ~ N(0, I).
```

```
x_t = sqrt(bar{alpha}_t)x_0 + sqrt(1 - bar{alpha}_t)eta_t,    eta_t ~ N(0, I).
```


其解析得分函数为：
