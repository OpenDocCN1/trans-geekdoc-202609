
# Analysis of Intelligent Traffic Management Modes and Travel Modes

In December 2017, the Beijing Municipal Commission of Transport, jointly with the Beijing Municipal Public Security Bureau Traffic Management Bureau and the Beijing Municipal Economy and Information Technology Bureau, issued two guiding documents: the "Guiding Opinions on Accelerating the Road Testing of Autonomous Vehicles in Beijing" and the "Implementation Rules for the Management of Road Testing of Autonomous Vehicles in Beijing." This will undoubtedly have a very positive impact on the further development of autonomous vehicle technology and management models.

The establishment of intelligent traffic management modes should be based on people's travel patterns and must highlight their humanized and intelligent considerations. From the perspective of the number of travel users, people's travel patterns can be divided into two main categories.

## 1. Free Travel

In the free travel mode, individual travelers have complete independent control and usage rights over the travel tool, can freely allocate its internal space and facilities, and can meet people's personalized travel needs.

## 2. Shared Travel

In the shared travel mode, two or more travelers jointly use one travel tool, influencing each other. The allocation of internal space and facilities is subject to certain constraints, and it also involves certain privacy protection issues. Strictly speaking, only individual self-driving travel counts as free travel.

## 3. Special Case: Taking a Taxi

There is a common special case between free travel and shared travel. When a person takes a taxi alone, the taxi driver, like the taxi itself, belongs to the service provider, not a sharer. The taxi driver is an independent individual with independent consciousness; the passenger cannot ignore their existence, so the passenger's freedom is restricted. If encountering an unethical taxi driver, the passenger's personal and property safety may be threatened.

## 4. Advantage Comparison

- The advantages of **free travel** are mainly reflected in convenience, safety, and comfort;
- The advantages of **shared travel** lie mainly in low cost and being green and environmentally friendly, which can effectively reduce the total number of urban cars, alleviate traffic congestion and parking difficulties, and improve resource utilization efficiency.

Under specific economic conditions, most people tend to choose free travel, and the awareness of sharing needs to be improved.

## 5. Classification by Travel Tool Ownership

From the perspective of travel tool ownership, people's travel modes can be further divided into two categories: **private car travel** and **public travel**. When the ownership of the vehicle used does not belong to the user themselves, it is regarded as a public travel mode, for example: taxis, ride-hailing cars, buses, subways, commuter buses, vans, as well as using family or friends' private cars.

Public travel in a manned driving state, as well as travel shared by two or more people in a private car, do not belong to free travel. With the mature development of autonomous driving technology, taking a driverless taxi will be similar to self-driving travel, thereby becoming true free travel.

In other words, **driverless taxis** will achieve the deep integration of free travel and public travel, allowing people to enjoy the convenience, comfort, and safety advantages of free travel while also gaining the low-cost and green advantages of public travel. Considering that driverless cars are expensive and have limited users in the early stages of development, and that driverless taxis can reduce labor costs, it is only natural that they take the lead in large-scale market application.

## 12.4.2 Driverless Taxis and Free Travel Modes

In August 2016, Singapore taxi startup `nuTonomy` launched a driverless taxi trial operation in Singapore. Eligible passengers could hail the service via smartphones. The trial phase deployed 6 driverless taxis, with 12 passengers gaining experience qualifications. The operation scope was limited to a 2.5-square-mile commercial and residential area, and passengers were required to board and alight at designated locations. According to `nuTonomy`'s plan, as more driverless cars are put into use, the total number of cars in Singapore will be reduced from 900,000 to 300,000 in the future. In October 2017, `Delphi Pike Electric` announced the completion of the full acquisition of `nuTonomy` for $450 million.

For countries like Singapore with small land areas and developed economies, the difficulty of promoting driverless taxis is relatively small. Their application will bring many conveniences to people's travel and have a positive impact on alleviating traffic congestion. It is worth noting that public transportation is a core component of Singapore's transportation system. The application of driverless taxis is not intended to completely replace traditional taxis, but to further improve them. Singapore's released future transportation development plan expects that by 2025, public transportation will undertake up to 75% of travel services during peak hours in Singapore.

Companies like `Uber` are testing driverless cars not only in Singapore but also in multiple cities such as California, Michigan, and Pittsburgh, attempting to launch driverless taxi services. Especially for ride-hailing companies like `Uber`, after applying driverless taxis, the platform can customize and design perfect travel solutions for people's daily travel. Through intelligent systems, people's travel demands can be effectively predicted, realizing efficient scheduling of vehicle resources; more importantly, combined with big data and cloud computing technologies, targeted adjustments can be made to the allocation of transportation resources in different regions to better meet peak travel demands and reduce empty running rates.

The algorithm competition hosted by the DiDi Research Institute in collaboration with `Uda Xue Cheng` and `Zhihu` had the slogan: "The dream of changing the world can come true. Your algorithm will be used in the real travel world. Perhaps one day you can say, 'In this city, my algorithm governs which car you choose to take'." If driverless taxis can be widely promoted and popularized, people will no longer need to purchase, repair, or regularly maintain private cars, thereby eliminating various problems such as parking difficulties, car insurance, congestion, breakdowns, and en-route accidents, which highly aligns with people's yearning for a better life.

Children, the elderly, people with disabilities, and those without driver's licenses can also enjoy travel freedom through driverless taxis. This will significantly reduce the number of private cars, contributing to environmental protection, reducing traffic accidents, and improving travel efficiency and experience.

Scientific research has found that if driverless taxis are promoted as the mainstream mode of urban motorized travel and the number of private cars is strictly limited, the total number of urban vehicles will be reduced by nearly 50%, parking land area will be reduced by 40%, and the accident rate will be reduced by about 90%. If driverless cars are upgraded to electric and other new energy models, vehicle exhaust emissions will be reduced by about 80%. At that time, cities will have more space to improve residents' quality of life, such as building more parks, football fields, and other public leisure facilities; bicycle lanes can also be widened.
