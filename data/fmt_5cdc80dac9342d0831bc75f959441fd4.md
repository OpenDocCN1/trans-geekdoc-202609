
# 10.1.3 Application of Traffic Data Processing Modules

After data collection and analysis are completed, the next step is the final step—processing. Processing directly relates to the status diagnosis of big data transportation, thereby enabling targeted traffic adjustments. Generally, traffic data processing modules process distributed data and make final decisions. For instance, when significant congestion occurs at an intersection, data will be rapidly analyzed, followed by a decision: optimize signal light duration, prioritize north-south bound vehicles, and have east-west bound vehicles wait appropriately.

Traffic data processing is not only about adjusting traffic management details but also about accumulating data and predicting conditions in other surrounding areas. Through the analysis of abnormal traffic behaviors and comparison with relevant data in the database, transportation departments can grasp road conditions over a period of time, thereby formulating more scientific plans. These data may seem chaotic, but through the optimization of big data classification, they can be directly applied to work.

Ultimately, for the purpose of traffic status diagnosis, big data needs to go through three stages: information collection, information analysis, and information processing, to build a sound transportation network. With the continuous upgrading of big data technology, more transportation-related content will be incorporated, such as flights, high-speed rail, urban construction, and urban maintenance. The richer the data, the more accurate the status diagnosis. The future development of big data transportation lies herein. By fully utilizing big data technology, an efficient and safe traffic management model will be realized.

# 10.2 Information Analysis of Big Data Transportation

With the rapid development of urbanization in China, most cities and even rural areas face severe traffic congestion. Traffic congestion not only causes road crowding but also leads to serious waste of transportation resources and even affects economic development. Taking the United States as an example, each car-owning household loses $1,700 annually due to traffic congestion. China is in a period of rapid growth in vehicle ownership. In addition, urban and rural reconstruction also results in economic waste due to poor traffic flow.

Therefore, the focus of big data transportation application lies in solving problems of poor traffic flow, congestion, and road optimization. Congestion causes vary across different regions; big data must be used to analyze information. Only by identifying the root cause of the problem can the right remedy be applied.

## 10.2.1 Defects of Traditional Information Analysis

There are many reasons for road congestion: some are due to excessive traffic volume exceeding road carrying capacity; some due to imperfect infrastructure preventing normal traffic flow; some due to a lack of surrounding parking lots leading to arbitrary parking by car owners; and some due to urban construction development and increased road-occupying construction causing congestion.

Of course, congestion in many areas is the result of a combination of the above factors. The aforementioned reasons interact with each other. Therefore, single information analysis struggles to formulate effective solutions. However, traditional traffic information analysis, due to lagging technical capabilities, results in obvious loopholes in information analysis.

(1) **Data is not comprehensive enough.** Traffic monitoring systems in most cities often only cover main arterial roads, neglecting data monitoring of branch roads and intersections. The received information cannot comprehensively reflect traffic conditions, making it difficult to formulate effective strategies.

(2) **Insufficient random sampling.** Traditional traffic information analysis usually relies on random sampling. However, due to the requirement for massive human and financial resources, samples are insufficient, randomness is high, and data lacks representativeness; analysis naturally has problems.

(3) **Regional restrictions.** In the past, there was little information flow between departments, imposing restrictions on traffic data collection. For example, urban building reconstruction falls under the responsibility of the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD), and transportation departments cannot obtain relevant data, making it difficult to conduct traffic diversion and leading to congestion in surrounding areas. Without information sources, correct analysis and prediction are difficult.

## 10.2.2 Information Analysis and Promotion of Big Data Models

The combination of transportation and big data can solve the shortcomings of traditional information analysis. Because under the big data model, information analysis will exhibit the following characteristics:

(1) **Full data monitoring.** Big data identifies intelligent devices distributed in every corner of the city and collects information uniformly, paying attention to all traffic data rather than random sampling.

(2) **Data enrichment.** During the information analysis process, big data is not limited to single data sources. Simultaneously, due to large storage capacity, data analysis is richer. The combination of static and dynamic data makes information analysis more accurate and valuable.

(3) **Real-time information analysis.** A characteristic of big data is that data can enter the database immediately, allowing for dynamic real-time analysis and avoiding data lag. This enables grasping traffic operation status in a short time, thereby formulating effective traffic solutions.

(4) **Microscopic data collection.** Although big data focuses on the whole, data collection originates from the microscopic level, making analysis more targeted. For example, when analyzing bus information, starting from each passenger's bus IC card, combined with mobile phone GPS data. Data is precise down to every individual and every time node.

Microwaves, loop detectors, GPS, license plates, bus IC cards, mobile phone Apps, etc., all constitute effective data sources. ### 大数据分析重塑贵阳城市交通

正是通过此类大数据信息分析，贵阳市对城市交通进行了彻底变革。贵阳位于山区；与平原城市相比，其基础交通建设相对落后。尽管近年来建设力度加大，但随着人口、机动车和房地产的快速发展，交通拥堵、停车难等问题日益凸显。

为解决这些问题，2014 年，贵阳交通部门引入了大型数据库软件，如 `MySQL` 数据库，并开始对 269 个交通监测点的大数据进行筛选。结果，这一大数据应用取得了显著成效。2014 年 9 月 1 日至 13 日，大数据累积了 1.54 亿条原始数据。通过大数据库的筛选，贵阳的交通特征被迅速发现。

例如，根据“黄金路与枣山路交叉口”的大数据调查发现，该路段每天早上 6:00 交通流量显著增加，在 8:00–9:00 达到高峰，期间近 8000 辆车通过。从 10:00 到 19:00，一直处于高峰的交通流量略有减弱。22:00 又出现一个小高峰。

过去，仅靠基层工作人员的调查无法实现此类数据分析。但凭借大数据强大的计算能力，数百台监测设备协同工作，很快便找到了贵阳各路口的交通数据。有了这些数据，城市交通的变革便能精准有效。

经过数天的大数据分析，贵阳交通部门发现了一些问题。例如，私家车保有量过高，占比超过 70%，这意味着贵阳公共交通存在明显短板。未来，公共交通的发展必将成为重点，以有效缓解交通拥堵问题。特别是对一环路及一环路内路段的监测发现，此处路口是拥堵的主要地点，也是未来解决问题的主要方向。同时，与其他城市相比，贵阳并非仅有早高峰拥堵的简单城市。贵阳的拥堵全天候且延续至午夜前，因此照搬其他城市经验并不奏效（见图 10.1）。

# 10.3 大数据交通的技术变革

大数据与交通的结合并非一成不变。随着大数据技术的持续进步，交通管理技术也在不断变革中，以适应时代发展。因此，对于基层交通部门而言，有必要持续升级大数据技术、采购新型大数据检测设备，以使工作持续改进、始终符合时代发展要求。

## 10.3.1 硬件设备升级

早期的大数据检测往往只是给传统交通设备加装视频拍摄功能和数据回传功能。但随着技术的持续创新，硬件设备有了显著提升。特别是这些硬件设备的应用，为大数据检测提供了更丰富的手段。

(1) **物联网传感器**。与传统传感器相比，物联网传感器的信息采集内容大幅提升。交通管理部门在路面嵌入物联网传感器，可在移动设备上实时观测车辆通行量和客流信息，并监管车辆尾气排放。物联网传感器可实现多车辆数据同步采集，效率更高。这为指挥交通管理系统奠定了基础，并能更高效地采集数据。
