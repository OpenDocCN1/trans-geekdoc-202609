
# 虚拟化与 Kali Linux

## 虚拟化

虚拟化是系统管理员多年来在数据中心使用的一种技术，它是云计算基础设施的核心。这项技术允许计算机的物理资源（`CPU`、`内存`、`硬盘`、`显卡`等）被虚拟机（`VMs`）共享。

想想过去，单一物理硬件平台——服务器——专用于单一服务器应用程序，例如作为 `Web` 服务器。事实证明，典型的 `Web` 应用程序服务器并没有充分利用服务器上可提供的许多底层硬件资源。为了讨论起见，让我们假设运行在我们其中某台物理服务器上的 `Web` 应用程序利用了 `30%` 的硬件资源。这意味着 `70%` 的硬件资源被闲置了。通过虚拟化，我们现在可以利用服务器的 `90%` 资源。

## Kali Linux

`Kali Linux` 保持免费，并提供广泛的无线设备支持。`Backtrack` 的启动是为了满足 `Mati Aharoni` 的需求，他需要一个工具来应对无法携带硬件笔记本电脑的任务，因为电脑在任务结束后会被收回。`Mati` 是 `Kali Linux` 的创始人和核心开发者，也是 `Offensive Security` 的首席技术官。有趣的是，`Kali` 是带来自我死亡的印度教女神。我认为这个软件的名字起得恰恰当。

我们在书中检查了许多工具，现在我们到了我最喜欢的工具之一。`Kali` 中的某些工具在书中有专门的章节介绍，例如 `Metasploit Framework`、`Nmap`、`Wireshark` 和 `Burp`。掌握任何技能或工具的最好方法都是实践。你可以采取的一种方法是将这些工具加载到你的计算机上，并使用它们来检查你的个人系统。这是一个非常好的介绍，但扩展性不佳。我们大多数人的私有网络中没有许多系统，可能无法充分发挥这些工具的用处。你可以使用这些工具来检查 `Google`、`Yahoo!` 或网络上的其他某些生产系统，但这样做的一个主要问题是你没有权限这么做。这可能会让你陷入很多法律纠纷。另一种替代方案，也是我最常用的方法，是使用虚拟化。

Most of what I teach is done virtually with customers over teleconferencing. The most difficult thing to do with virtual training is to engage the student. They don't see me, and I don't see them. I do not have the ability to read body language. I cannot see the crinkle between their eyes when they're confused. I also can't see when they have gotten up to get coffee or are sidetracked by emails and phone calls, so student engagement is key. One of conversations in the Nope Vulnerability Management class centers around the cybersecurity philosophy of viewing you and your ecosystem as an attacker would. I ask the question, “What do pen testers or hackers download as their operating system of choice?” I'm surprised by many blue-teamers have never heard of Kali Linux. I've been playing Kali since before it was Kali and was called Backtrack.

Kali Linux debuted in 2013 as a rewrite of the free Linux distribution called Backtrack. Backtrack was based when they went on the Knopix OS, whereas Kali Linux is based on the Debian OS and is funded and maintained by Offensive Security. Kali Linux remains free with a wide range of wireless device support. Backtrack was started as an answer to Mati Aharoni's need for a tool to take on an engagement where he could not bring any hardware laptop, which would be taken from him at the end of the engagement. Mati is the founder and core developer of Kali Linux and is the CTO at Offensive Security. Interestingly enough, Kali is the Hindu goddess who brings the death of the ego. I think this software is aptly named.
