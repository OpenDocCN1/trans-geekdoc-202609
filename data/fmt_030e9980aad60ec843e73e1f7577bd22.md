
# Baidu, Alibaba, and Tencent's Layout in Big Data Transportation

Baidu is undoubtedly the strongest. Baidu Maps and Baidu's driverless cars are Baidu Group's direct initiatives to enter the big data transportation industry. Subsequently, a series of investments and acquisitions have also been advancing rapidly. In November 2017, Baidu announced a strategic investment in Shouqi Yueche (Shouqi Limousine & Chauffeur). Baidu Capital, NIO Capital, and Silk Road Huachuang led the investment, completing a total financing of 700 million RMB. Baidu expressed its attention to the big data transportation industry with concrete actions and continues to cultivate this field deeply.

Alibaba's pace is also very firm. In October 2017, Alibaba Cloud cooperated with Shanghai Metro to develop technologies such as voice ticket purchasing and facial recognition entry, and announced its entry into the big data intelligent transportation field. Prior to this, Alibaba had accumulated rich experience in the big data transportation industry through a series of investments and acquisitions. In 2013, Alibaba showed strong interest in the taxi sector, investing successively in the real-time bus service provider "Chelaile" (Bus Coming) and the commuter shuttle "Jiewo Yunbanche" (Pick Me Up Cloud Shuttle). At the same time, YITONG Technology and AutoNavi (Gaode Map) are also Alibaba's investment brands.

Alibaba's big data technology is the leading technology among domestic tech companies. The Alibaba Cloud system has established cooperation with numerous commercial institutions, therefore possessing extremely high proficiency in the application of transportation big data.

Tencent also has a rapid layout in the big data transportation industry. In December 2017, China Marine Information Electronics (Zhonghai Technology) announced cooperation with Tencent, whereby Tencent entered the big data intelligent transportation market. Tencent values Zhonghai Technology's experience in the transportation industry. Relying on Zhejiang University, Zhonghai Technology engages in urban rail transit system construction and integrated ticketing system construction, having carried out business in subway projects in cities such as Shenyang, Xi'an, and Zhengzhou, and successively innovated and developed rail transit driverless signaling systems and intelligent train related products. It can be seen that Tencent has also been coveting the big data transportation industry for a long time.

Whether startups or Internet giants, the participation of an increasing number of enterprises in the big data transportation industry means that the industry's outbreak period is approaching. Transportation concerns people's livelihood and reflects the development vitality and potential of a city; therefore, we have reason to believe that the future development of the big data transportation industry will become increasingly mature and lead world trends.

## 11.4 Policy Guidance for the Big Data Transportation Industry

The development of big data transportation in the commercial field is in full swing, and the national policy level also pays high attention to it, continuously introducing policies to guide and encourage industry development.

### 11.4.1 Clear Support in the "13th Five-Year Plan"

In the "13th Five-Year Plan", the goal of building a modern comprehensive transportation system and achieving networked and intelligent development prospects was clearly proposed. The Outline of the Plan pointed out: "We must build a comprehensive transportation network and realize the integrated connection of transportation hubs. We must gradually optimize the spatial layout of comprehensive transportation hubs and improve the construction of the three-level comprehensive transportation hub system at the national, regional, and local levels." To achieve this goal, it is necessary to actively access the big data system, form a data system model, and effectively connect all links in the transportation field. This Plan Outline will further stimulate industry vitality and lay the foundation for future industry trends.

### 11.4.2 The State Council's Guiding Opinions on Actively Promoting the "Internet Plus" Action

In 2015, the State Council issued the *Guiding Opinions on Actively Promoting the "Internet Plus" Action*, making explicit elaboration on the application of big data in the transportation field. The Guiding Opinions pointed out:

(1) **Innovative Development, Support and Leadership**. Fully utilize information technologies such as the Internet, big data, and cloud computing to optimize transportation organization models, provide diversified products, and better meet diversified needs. Take intelligent transportation development as the guide to enhance industry innovation capabilities and cultivate new forms and models of business development.

(2) **Market Operation, Quality Improvement and Efficiency Enhancement**. Fully exert the initiative of traditional transportation enterprises and Internet enterprises, and encourage all-round cooperation in forms such as capital operation, technical cooperation, and management cooperation. Fully exert technical and market advantages, focus on customers, and improve the overall operational efficiency and service quality of the comprehensive transportation system.

By 2018, implement the "Internet Plus" Convenient Travel Key Demonstration Projects, basically realize the public's access to dynamic road traffic information via mobile Internet terminals, and complete the one-stop passenger transport service covering navigation, ticket purchasing, and payment, enhancing user travel experience. Basically realize the interconnection and interoperability of "Transportation One Card" in key urban agglomerations and "Unified Network Management" of key operating vehicles (ships). Promote the accelerated integration of online and offline enterprises, and take the lead in realizing "Single Order System" (through billing) throughout the process in national trunk logistics channels. Realize the interconnection and interoperability of transportation infrastructure, vehicles, and operation information, making system operation safer and more efficient.

The focus lies in improving traffic capacity, alleviating traffic congestion, reducing emissions, and increasing the national expressway ETC lane coverage rate. Improve the convenience of ETC system installation and payment, and increase user development volume. Focus on improving the utilization rate of various operating vehicles such as roads, vehicles, and taxis, striving to achieve a public bus ETC utilization rate of over 50% within three years. Study and promote standard box truck ETC. Improve the service level of customer service outlets and provincial online settlement centers, and build an efficient settlement system. Promote the deep integration of the ETC system with the Internet, and realize the wide application of the ETC system in transportation fields such as expressways, urban public transportation, taxis, parking, road passenger transport, and railway passenger transport.

各交通运输企业与互联网企业应充分发挥各自优势，鼓励线上线下资源融合，为公众提供多元化、高品质的服务。应挖掘并满足乘客潜在需求，依托线下资源拓展线上业务，延伸服务链条，创新商业模式。应充分发挥互联网个性化需求、及时响应、高效组织等特性，积极整合线下资源，通过定制客运、网约出租车、分时租赁等方式，在城市交通、道路客运、货运、停车、车辆维修等领域发展“互联网+”交通新业态，逐步实现规模化、网络化、品牌化发展，促进大众创业万众创新。鼓励交通运输企业与互联网企业开展战略合作，实现信息资源、资金、技术、业务的深度融合，并与上下游产业链相结合。

### 11.4.3 交通运输部通知

2015 年 6 月，交通运输部发布《交通运输部办公厅关于进一步加快城市公共交通智能化应用示范工程建设的通知》，进一步推动大数据智能交通发展，并提出明确要求：

为保障城市公共交通智能化应用示范工程有序建设，分类型、分步骤、分阶段推进不同类型城市建设。

(1) 2015 年底前，完成首批 10 个试点城市示范工程主体建设。已申报首批示范工程建设的济南、郑州、大连、哈尔滨、深圳、南京、西安、长沙等省级交通主管部门及北京、重庆市交通主管部门，应以城市为主体，进一步加快示范工程建设进度，于 2015 年底前完成示范工程主体建设，包括公共交通数据资源中心、企业运营智能调度平台、行业监管平台、乘客出行信息服务平台，同时确保示范工程建设符合相关标准规范。
