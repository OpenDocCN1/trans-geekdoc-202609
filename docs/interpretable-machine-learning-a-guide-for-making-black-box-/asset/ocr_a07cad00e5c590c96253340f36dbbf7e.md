In at least three aspects, the way I published this book is unconventional. First, it is available both as website and as ebook/pdf. Secondly, I self-publish the book on the platform Leanpub, instead of working with a traditional publisher. And third, I published the book as in-progress book, which has helped me enormously to get feedback and to monetize it along the way. Many thanks to leanpub for making this possible and handling the royalties fairly. I would also like to thank you, dear reader, for reading this book without a big publisher name behind it.
