DeepSeek

DeepSeek is a Chinese artificial intelligence company that develops open-source large language models (LLMs). It was founded in May 2023 and is based in Hangzhou, Zhejiang. DeepSeek is owned and funded by High-Flyer, a Chinese hedge fund.

DeepSeek is known for its focus on efficiency and affordability in developing its AI models. Its DeepSeek-R1 model, for example, provides responses comparable to other leading LLMs but is trained at a significantly lower cost and with less computing power. This has allowed DeepSeek to compete with larger and more established AI companies, even amidst US sanctions on China for chips used in AI development.

DeepSeek also makes its AI models and training details open-source, allowing its code to be freely used, modified, and shared. This approach has fostered a vibrant community around DeepSeek's models and has contributed to the rapid advancement of its technology.

In addition to its LLMs, DeepSeek has also released a free chatbot app based on the DeepSeek-R1 model. The app quickly gained popularity, surpassing ChatGPT as the most downloaded free app on the iOS App Store in the United States in January 2025.

DeepSeek's success has been attributed to its innovative approach to AI development, its focus on open-source technology, and its ability to compete effectively in a rapidly evolving field. However, the company has also faced scrutiny over its data collection practices and compliance with Chinese government censorship policies.

Despite these challenges, DeepSeek remains a significant player in the AI industry, and its contributions to the development of LLMs and other AI technologies are likely to continue to shape the future of the field.

Deepseek originate.

DeepSeek originated from the vision of Liang Wenfeng, a Chinese AI enthusiast and co-founder of the hedge fund High-Flyer. Here's a breakdown of its origins:

●     High-Flyer's AI Roots: In 2016, Liang Wenfeng co-founded High-Flyer, initially focusing on stock trading. They quickly integrated AI and deep learning models into their trading strategies, eventually relying exclusively on AI-driven trading by 2021.

●     Building an AI Powerhouse: Recognizing the potential of AI beyond finance, Liang Wenfeng began stockpiling Nvidia GPUs in 2021, anticipating US restrictions on AI chip exports to China. This foresight allowed High-Flyer to amass a significant amount of computing power for future AI development.

●     DeepSeek's Birth: In April 2023, High-Flyer announced the creation of an artificial general intelligence lab to explore AI tools beyond finance. This lab was officially incorporated as DeepSeek in July 2023, with High-Flyer as its primary investor and backer.

●     Focus on Open-Source and Efficiency: DeepSeek was founded with a strong emphasis on open-source technology, aiming to make AI more accessible and collaborative. The company also prioritized efficiency, developing models that require less computing power and cost compared to competitors.

DeepSeek's origins are rooted in a combination of AI expertise, strategic foresight, and a commitment to open-source principles.
