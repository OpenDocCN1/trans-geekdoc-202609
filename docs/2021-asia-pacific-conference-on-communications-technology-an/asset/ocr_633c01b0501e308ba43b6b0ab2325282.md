**Abstract—**With the rapid development of industry, people's daily life has become more and more rich than ever, while the pleasure brings a large number of garbage to carry. Various types of garbage are produced, and people lack the knowledge related to garbage classification, which leads to the difficulty of manual or robot classification. An algorithm based on deep learning convolutional network is introduced to help garbage classification. In the image recognition task, in order to solve the problem of small amount of data and different sizes of objects, Feature extractor is used to extract the features of objects. To avoid the obvious effect on small batch in the network, we replace BN with GN (Group Normalization). In order to emphasize the importance of target object, the attention mechanism is introduced. After the output of the model, we add attention mechanism to enhance the important information of the target object, so that the model can focus on the key features and better identify the image; according to the above process, we classify into four categories (Recyclables, Kitchen garbage, Hazardous garbage). The other result shows that the model can effectively classify and identify the types of garbage. The experimental results show that the accuracy of the algorithm is high, with good performance and robustness. In the practical significance of the research, this reliable method can help people know the type of garbage and the types of garbage for robot sorting, so it has very important application scenarios and significance.
