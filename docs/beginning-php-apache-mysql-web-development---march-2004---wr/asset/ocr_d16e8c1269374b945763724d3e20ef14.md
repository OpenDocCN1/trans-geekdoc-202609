This next case is also quite similar to the Publish case, only this time, after checking the article ID, you set is_published to 0 and erase the date_published field. Retracting an article in this case simply returns it to its pre-published state, but you might think of better ways to retract an article. Perhaps you could have separate columns for is_retracted and retract_date. There is no right or wrong way to do it, and of course, depends on the needs of your own site.
