# A Word About Spam (and SPAM)

With the advent of the computer, mailing lists have been brought to a whole new level. Now you can (and no doubt) be told on a daily basis that Cathy really wants to visit your Web site, that a little blue pill will solve all of your personal problems. Occasionally an e-mail sits in your Inbox informing of your new job postings, new posts on PHPBuilder.com, or dates for Jimmy Buffet. But we think you know what mailing lists are primarily used for: spam!
