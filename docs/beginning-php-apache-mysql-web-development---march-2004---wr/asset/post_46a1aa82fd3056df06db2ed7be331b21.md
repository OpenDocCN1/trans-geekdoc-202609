# About the Authors

## Michael “BuzzLY” Glass

Michael Glass has been a gladiator in the software/ Web development arena for more than eight years. He has more than ten years of commercial programming experience with a wide variety of technologies, including PHP, Java, Lotus Domino, and Vignette StoreServer. He divides his time between computer programming, playing pool in the APA, and running his Web site at www.ultimates.com. You can usually find him slinking around the PHPBuilder.com forums, where he is a moderator with the nickname BuzzLY.

Thanks, Stacy, for putting with long and late hours at the computer. Elizabeth and Jason, it wouldn’t have the same project without you. And thanks to testers at www.ultimates.com: Spidon, Kaine, Garmy, Spiderman, Ping, Webhead, and FancyDan. You guys rock!

To Donna and Gerry, who have influenced my life more than they can ever know, and who taught me the importance of finishing what you've started.

## Yann “Bunkermaster” Scouarcnec

Yann is the senior developer for Jolt Online Gaming, a British gaming company. He is a moderator at PHPBuilder.com and a developer of open source PHP software for the gaming community. He has also worked for many software corporations as a software quality expert.

> I think all the innocent bystanders who pushed around because of this project: Debra and Nancy, who were patient enough not to have homicidal thoughts; and my wife and kids, who barely saw me for six months.

## Elizabeth Naramore

Elizabeth has been programming with computers since a very young age, and, yes, Graduating from Miami University at age 20 with a degree in Organizational Behavior, she found a world of opportunity appearing—in corporate marketing. Her first love was computers, however, and she found herself back to the programming world in 1997 through Web design and development (once a geek, always a geek). While she plans to return to Miami to get her Masters in Computer Science, currently stays busy running several Web sites. Her main focus is e-commerce and running www.giftsforeengine.com.

Elizabeth has spent the past six years developing Web sites and coordinating all phases of Web site publication and production. She is currently a moderator at PHPBuilder.com, an online help center for PHP. Her other interests include poetry, arts and crafts, camping, and juggling the many demands of career, family, and the “other duties as assigned” that come along in life. She lives in Cincinnati, Ohio, with her husband, beautiful daughter and a new baby on the way.

## Gary “trooper” Mailer

After graduation from university in 1998, Gary worked in a major software house in central London as quality assurance engineer, and also as the departmental Web developer (using ASP). This gave him a taste of Web development. After a few years, he made the jump into full-time Web development and has not looked back since.

Gary has worked in a few different sectors, including communications (Siemens) and hotels (Hilton), as well as in “traditional” development houses.

He is currently a freelancer for a European communications company. Gary has been and continues to be an active member and contributor to the PHPBuilder site.

## Jeremy “stolzboy” Stolz

Jeremy is a Web developer at Cloverfish Inc. (www.cloverfish.net), a Web development company in Fargo, North Dakota. Jeremy is primarily a PHP/MySQL developer, but he has also worked with many other languages. When not working, he frequents the Internet and tries to keep up his skills. He is a contributor and moderator at PHPBuilder.com. He also frequents many other computer-related Web sites to keep his skills sharp and up to date.

Jason currently spends his days working as a Web developer in Cincinnati and burns free time complaining about lack of support for Web standards and abusing XML.
