# Chapter 13

+   1. Open your browser and open user.php. You should see a form that looks very much like the one in 13-3.

![](bbox=[0.196, 0.137, 0.742, 0.532])

Figure 13-3

+   2. Enter your e-mail address and first and last name, choose a mailing list to subscribe to, and click Subscribe.

You will see a Thank You screen (shown in Figure 13-4) and receive a confirmation e-mail at the e-mail you entered.

+   4. Click the link, and it takes you back to the Thank Page, this time thanking you for confirming your subscription.

You will get another e-mail informing you about your subscription, with a link that allows you to remove yourself from the mailing list. Don't click that just yet. First you're going to send an e-mail to the mailing list you just subscribed to:

+   1. Open admin.php, and click the link at the bottom, "Send a quick message to users."

+   2. In the Quick Message page, choose the mailing list that you just subscribed to in the previous steps, and enter a subject. Then type a quick message.
