When they update their information, they will be told that the information was indeed updated and then they will be allowed to update the information again if they wish (for example if on review realize they input something incorrectly). That page will look like the one in Figure 11-14.

Finally, the delete page looks similar to the one shown in Figure 11-15. This appears once users choose the Delete Account link on the display page. From here, if users choose Yes, their account is deleted, their logged session will be destroyed, and they will be redirected to the index page.
