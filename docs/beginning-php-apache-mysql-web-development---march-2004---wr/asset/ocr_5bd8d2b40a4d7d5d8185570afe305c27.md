# Divide and Conquer

Another way to tackle a huge problem is to break it down into baby steps and test each one to make sure you are getting the correct result step of the way. One mistake in the beginning of a complex block of statements can be a snowball effect and completely alter your results at the end. By checking each step by one, you can iron out those small bugs and eventually get the intended results.

# Test, Test, Test!

Many coders test their program on their own system and as long as it works for their settings, they assume they are free. To be thorough, you should test your code using every different environment available to you: different browsers, different preferences, different computer systems and so on. If you have the opportunity and know-how, you should even try to hack through your system to look for holes that might be exploited by someone a less kind than you.
