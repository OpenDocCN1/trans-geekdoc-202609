# CHAPTER 31 PATCH MANAGEMENT

The process of connecting to your upstream server the first time can take a few minutes.
Once the process is complete, click Next.

The Product Updates screen will give you a list of all the products available on your upstream server. You have the choice to select an entire product or a specific version.

In this example (shown in figure 3.12), we have selected only Office 2013 and all versions of Silverlight. You choose the products you want, and click Next.

Next, you will choose the classifications of your updates to synchronize. This list ranges from Critical Updates to normal Updates, as shown in figure 3.13.
