# IMPLICIT DENY

There's also known as implicit deny. If permissions aren't explicitly granted, access to the share is implicitly denied.
Imagine a share named ProjectData where the only group access to the share is the G_Sales group. Maria is in the G_HR group and is not a member of the G_Sales group, so she does not have any access to the share. She hasn't been explicitly granted access, so she is implicitly denied.
This is similar to your home. If you never give the keys to anyone for your house, you shouldn't be able to get in. Of course, you still need to worry about bandits and hackers, from the basic perspective, giving no permissions results in no access.
