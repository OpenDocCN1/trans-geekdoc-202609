Before deploying updates and configuring Group Policy objects (GPOs), let's review a few other options in the console that you should consider in the final steps of your configuration.

The first one we want to look at is computers. How do you want your devices to be added to WSUS? By opening the Options section of the Update Services console (see Figure 31.14), you can choose the Computers object, and it will allow you to use the Update Services console to assign computers to groups or use Group Policy (registry settings) to assign.
