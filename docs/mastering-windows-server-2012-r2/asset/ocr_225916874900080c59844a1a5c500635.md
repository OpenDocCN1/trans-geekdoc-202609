# # About the Authors

**Mark Minasi** is a best-selling author, popular technology columnist, commentator, keynote speaker, and IT consultant. He first got the chance to play with a computer at a university class in 1973. At that time, he learned two things:

+   - First, computers are neat. (People still said 'neat' back in 1973. Hey, it was back in the 20th Century).
- Second, many technical people are very nice folks, but they can put you to sleep in an instant while explaining technical things.

Mark transformed two insights into a career making computers and networking easier and more fun to understand. He did that by writing over a thousand computer columns, selber dozen best-selling technical books, and operating system and networking planning, installation, maintenance, and repair to crowds from two to thousand. Independent voice hailed him "Favorite Technical Author" by CertCities four times out of four, Mark has the unusual ability to take the most technical topics, filter out the hype and explain them in plain English. That's why whenTarget hired him to deliver a webinar on PC tuning, he drew three servers, and he has been hired to deliver keynote addresses at hundreds of tech conferences around the world.

Mark is probably best known for his *Windows Server* and *Complete PC Upgrade and Maintenance* guide books, both of which have seen 12 editions and over 1 million copies.

Mark's humorous, provocative and informative style makes him a favorite of audiences around the world. Through his firm, MR&D (www.minasi.com), Mark offers technical seminars, classes, and talks, as well as a newsletter. You can also sign up for Mark's newsletter.

**Kevin Greene**, who led the team of authors on this book, is a Microsoft MVP for System Cloud and Datenter Management and has been working in IT since 1999. He is employed as a subject matter expert for System Center at Ergo in Dublin, Ireland. In this role, he works with clients to deliver enterprise-grade solutions with Windows Server and System Center. In the past, he has held such roles as IT administrator, technical team lead, and senior consultant. He has been on the Microsoft certification track since the days of Windows 4.0 and holds qualifications that include MCSE, MCSA, MCIT, MCT and CTS. Kevin is an active participant in the Windows Server and System Center (WSSC) community through his blog at http://kevengreeneblog.blogspot.com, and he can also be found around Twitter @kgreene.

Kevin regularly delivers presentations and participates in podcasts on Windows Server and System Center is a co-author of *Mastering System Center 2012 Operations Manager* (Sybox, 2012).

Kevin lives in Sallins, Co Kildare, Ireland, with his wife, Laura, and Matthew and Dylan. When he's not working on his laptop, he spends his free time with his family and supporting Manchester United. He also holds a second-degree black belt in freestyle kickboxing, and although not involved in the sport as he used to be, he's still an avid follower of the martial arts.
