Enter a GPO name prefix in the empty field, and click Next to move on.

6. The Summary dialog box presents you with the settings that you have chosen, and in our example it lists the GPOs that will be deployed to your managed DHCP, DNS, and NPS servers. Before you click Apply, be warned! Once you click Apply, you cannot change the provisioning method, and you should wish to revert to a different method, you will need to reinstall IPAM completely.

When you're happy with your choice of provisioning method, click Apply.

7. Once the IPAM provisioning configuration has completed successfully, you can click
Close to exit the wizard.
