The answer lies in two basic conflict-resolution rules for GPOs:

**Rule 1** Listen to the last policy that you heard from.

**Rule 2** Execute policies from the bottom up, as they appear in the GUI.

Reading from the bottom of the dialog box, you see that the system will first see the policy that sets the background gray; then the one that sets it, then the one that sets red, and finally the one that sets blue. Because blue is the last one heard, it wins, and the effects of the previous three are obviated.

You can also click the Group Policy Inheritance tab, which will display the order in which the GPOs apply within Active Directory. As you can see in Figure 9.14, the blue background policy wins out the others.

But what if you want the red background policy to win? Notice the up and down arrows to the left of the Linked Group Policy Objects tab? You can shuffle them around to your heart's content.
