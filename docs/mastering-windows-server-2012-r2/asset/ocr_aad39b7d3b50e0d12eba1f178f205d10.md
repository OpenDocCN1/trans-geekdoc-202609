GPOs, by default, can be created by a member of the Administrators for the domain or by members of the group classed Group Policy Creator Owners. However, although members of Administrators have full control of GPOs, members of Group Policy Owners can only modify policies they have created, unless they have been specifically granted permission to modify a policy. So, if you put a delegated Group administrator into the security group Group Policy Creator Owners (that's almost as awkward as Active Directory Users and Computers), then person can create new policy objects and modify them.

To delegate to create a GPO in your domain, you will head down to the Group Policy Objects in GPMC. Once you click this node, you will select the Delegation tab in the right pane to view the list of users and groups that have been granted the ability to create GPOs in the domain, as shown in Figure 9.35.
