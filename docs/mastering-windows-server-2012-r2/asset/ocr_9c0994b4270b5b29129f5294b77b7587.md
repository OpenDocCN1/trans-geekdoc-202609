# ABOUT THE AUTHORS

Christian Booth was previously a Microsoft MVP in the System Center: Cloud and Datacenter discipline and recently retired his MVP to full time as senior manager for the System Center team, focusing on the Cloud and Datacenter Management MVP program.

He has worked as a director, lead architect, and solutions engineer in the Seattle area for the last 17 years. His experience and focus have always been on the Microsoft platform with specialization in System Center, Windows Server, and virtualization.

Christian has contributed as a subject matter expert, exam writer, trainer designer, and technical reviewer on many Microsoft Official Curriculum (MOC) courses. You can find out more about Christian at http://about.me/cbooth

He has been a Microsoft Certified Professional for the last 16 years, and currently holds many other Microsoft certifications including MCSE, MCSA 2012, MCITEP, and MCTS for SCCM 2012.

Robert lives in Seattle, Washington, with his wife, Nicole, and two children. Alice may find more information on Robert on his System Center blog, http://butler.me, or by following him on Twitter @butler_me
