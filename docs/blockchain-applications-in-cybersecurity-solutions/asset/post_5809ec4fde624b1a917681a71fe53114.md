The MD5 hashes can be utilized and blockchain can be applied to verify the integrity of downloaded software to prevent foreign intrusion. ## Securing Domain Name System (DNS) and Distributed Denial of Services (DDoS)

Attacks like DNS and Distributed Denial of Services (DDoS) happen when targeted user resources like networks, servers, or websites are denied the service or access. These kinds of attacks may result in slowing down the system or shutting it down. Domain Name System (DNS) has a centralized technology that makes it an easy target for hackers, who want to invade the connections between the website name and IP address. Blockchain’s decentralized solutions can decrease the attacks by decentralizing the entries of the Domain Name System (DNS), and it can also remove the risky points that can be easily targeted by hackers.

### Advantages of Blockchain

- Remove Human Involvement: Transactions on the blockchain network remove almost all the human involvement because the transactions are approved by a network of thousands of computers.
- Improve Accuracy: Less human involvement results in an accurate record of information because it eliminates human errors.
- Cost Reduction: Blockchain removes the third-party verifications as well as the costs associated with that. In the case of bitcoin, there is a limited transaction fee associated.
- Transparent Technology: Blockchain technology works on open-source software that gives transparency to the users and anyone can view the code. Auditors can also review the cryptocurrencies like bitcoin for security.
- Secured Transactions: The authenticity of each transaction has been verified by the blockchain network. Details of purchasing went through the thousands of computers to check the accuracy; after the validation, the details were added to the blockchain block.
- Data protection: The technology of blockchain provides data protection and privacy by allowing selective access to information and transactions. It does not allow hackers to cut privacy at any level. It is hard to modify the information in the blockchain system.
- **Public Key Infrastructure Management:** Digital signatures are one of the core aspects of blockchain technology that provides security. The public key defines the digital identity and the private key authorizes the actions. The private key includes verification of transactions, secured encryption, and sign-in.

### Disadvantages of Blockchain

- **Speed efficiency:** It has been estimated that blockchain networks can only manage seven transactions per second. Other cryptocurrencies are also available to give better performance and a Legacy brand visa can process 24,000 transactions per second.
- **History of illegal activity:** While protecting users from hacking and preserving privacy, blockchain network allows for such activities as trading that can be illegal in some way. Some websites allow browsing without being tracked which can be used by the user in the illegal purchasing of cryptocurrencies.
- **Technology Cost:** Consuming the computational power on higher-level results in high technology costs although blockchain saves the user money by saving transaction fees. But technology is not free and expenses that are associated with the technology make it costly.
