**Smart contracts** make the most sense when a network of participants conducts frequent transactions and the counterparties undertake manual or redundant processes or duties for each transaction. Smart contracts, in a nutshell, automate the exchange of values between parties without the need for an intermediary or human intervention in the event of a contract.

Agents were heavily reliant on **traditional contracts** and agreements to complete paperwork and other legal requirements. The primary trust criteria for transferring money or other valuable assets were agents and go-betweens. Smart contracts, which are coded into the system to automatically execute activities based on a predetermined set of discussions between the parties, have replaced agents with the help of Blockchain. The trust is built up through the use of secure blockchain technology. The expense of managing the flow of value is dramatically reduced, as is the time it takes to complete the agreement. Similarly, when 'value' is exchanged using the Blockchain's distributed ledger technology, new blockchain chains are formed and kept permanently without the possibility of manipulation.

# *Some of the example that shows the value exchanges due to Smart contracts are given below:*

# a). Freelance Work Exchanges

When a customer decides to increase the scope of a project or delays payment for an unknown reason, many freelancers are helpless. Companies who hire freelancers, on the other hand, may feel misled if they realise that someone's professional experience is false and that they are unable to execute the job for which they were engaged. Smart contracts encourage more open and honest communication between freelancers and clients, resulting in a better experience for both.

The contract's conditions include the project's scope, deadlines, and payment arrangements. At the outset of an assignment, payments are kept in escrow in a smart contract, to be released upon successful completion of work milestones. This helps freelancers prevent the scope creeps, ensuring that any new job does not hold up payments, and enabling them to get paid pretty for additional work.
