## CONCLUSION

Blockchain technology has the ability of decentralization which makes it efficient to deal with cyber threats. Even in the coming days, it will be a trending technology that can be applied to every enterprise. There are challenges associated with the technology but they will lead to growth. Moving towards digitalization, companies need cybersecurity and privacy of their data and it can be managed by blockchain technology. It also gives the opportunities of maintaining data safety, network resilience, and scalability. To implement the blockchain technology model, a risk model should be developed so that risks associated with security threats can be evaluated. While enabling the private blockchain in any infrastructure, it should be secured and resilient. Automation of data storage eliminates human errors and can protect the data by dealing with unauthorized ways and cybercrimes. All layers’ security is important so that it can be implemented more strategically and vulnerabilities can be considered at all the layers while bringing them into the operations. Cybercrime is a threat to any industry in the world and traditional security technologies are not able to deal with these vast cybercrimes. Blockchain has created trust in many industries to secure the data and gives potentially strong cybersecurity. Many industries are using blockchain technology to prevent their information and data from cybersecurity theft. To improve cybersecurity and make it more secure, Blockchain technology is the most reliable technology and also beneficial to almost all industries.
