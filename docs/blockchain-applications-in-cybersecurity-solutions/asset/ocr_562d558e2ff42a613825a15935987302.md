# How Military and Defense Using Blockchain in Cybersecurity

The defense and military of the countries share important data and information with dispersed groups all over the world. Great innovative technological changes have been made in the defense sector in the past years and blockchain in cybersecurity can become the next breakthrough technology in the defense and military sector. It has been found by Accenture that 86% of the companies are planning to adopt blockchain, especially in cybersecurity in the coming years. Blockchain can safeguards and maximize the privacy of the confidential data and sensitive information of aerospace, defense, and military.

The technology branch of the United States Army is majorly interested in blockchain encryption and secure data transfer. Engineers are also working on creating a messaging system based on blockchain technology for the U.S. military. That can be used by military personnel to share information without worrying about hackers.

The Chinese military also thinks that blockchain encryption is a perfect tool to improve efficiency and it can defend against the attacker’s attempt. China’s government is looking at blockchain cybersecurity to protect their communication, data, and information from intelligence.

# How Banking Systems Use Blockchain in Cybersecurity

> “Banks are looking at blockchain for solutions to cybersecurity issues”, said Jamie Dimon (Chairman and CEO of JP Morgan). Trillions of transactions and cash flow make banks an easy target for hackers and fraud. Outdated cybersecurity protocols of traditional banking make it easier for hackers and due to this many multinational banks experienced many cyber-attacks. An an annual report of the United States Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) suggests that multi-layered secured decentralized technological tools exactly like blockchain can provide safety to the banks.

Some banks have adopted blockchain technology like Santander, which was the first bank in the United Kingdom that has adopted blockchain to secure payments for their international-level transactions. Blockchain technology allows its users to pay between the accounts in Europe and South America securely.
