
## Countermeasures against fork after withholding (FAW) Attack

To solve the FAW attack problem, Kwon et al. [31] time-controlled beacons are introduced by the pool operators assigned to miners. In response to each assignment, the miners find and determine how much POW they have and send the answer, together with the beacon value, to the pool operator. After a few seconds, the beacon value is updated to find a rogue miner if the valid solution is retained and later propagated across the web. However, the authors also observed that this technique might not be practicable in some scenarios, concluding that research and academic communities are still grappling with the issue of FAW attacks.

Chang and Park [32] proposed Silent Timestamping as a defense mechanism against the FAW attack that ensures share ordering and essentially negates the FAW's incentive effects. The behaviour of the ideal attacker against silent timestamping was studied, and it was discovered that the best FAW attacker approach is forced to decrease to ethical mining. Silent Timestamping removes the requirement for networking and does not require any modifications to the miner's code.
