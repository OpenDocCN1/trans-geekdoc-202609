# Challenges of IoT Devices

IoT devices come in a wide range of varieties. These devices communicate with other devices in different ways, such as wired or wireless communication, using different protocols depending on the end communication device. Due to the heterogeneity of the devices, there is no standardization of the communication protocols. This raises a concern in terms of the security, integrity, and confidentiality of the device and its associated data. And also, these devices are more easily prone to security attacks because of their limited resources and computational capacity. Due to the wide range of devices, IoT devices face many challenges, which are tabulated in Table 2.

# Table 2. Challenges of IoT Devices.

| S.No. | Challenge | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Confidentiality | As the Devices maintain sensitive information about the user, there is a need to maintain the confidentiality of the data. |
| 2 | Integrity | As the devices communicate with other devices or servers, it is transmitted through a network. Hence the integrity of the data is necessary |
| 3 | Authentication | Data transmission or reception happens between various IoT devices to enhance functionality. Hence a proper authentication mechanism is needed before setting up the communication. |
| 4 | Power Efficient | Many small-range IoT devices lack the resources to maintain the power for computation and working. |
