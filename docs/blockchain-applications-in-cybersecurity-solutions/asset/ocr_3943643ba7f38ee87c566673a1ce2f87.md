# Coinbase

Coinbase works as a mediating platform that allows users and buyers to exchange digital currency. Trading of Bitcoins, Litecoins, or Ethereum can be done on the company’s secured platform blockchain. The system of coin base is entirely supported by encryption security. To ensure the users, the company saves the database of wallets and passwords with high security. The impact of coinbase on industries has been expended over 20 million coinbase users and more than 150 billion dollars has been processed in trades.
