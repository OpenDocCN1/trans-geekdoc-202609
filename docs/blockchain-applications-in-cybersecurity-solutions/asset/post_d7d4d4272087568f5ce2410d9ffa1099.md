Every individual and their virtual or physical assets will have blockchain identities by 2030, if not sooner. By providing a variety of identity arrangements, blockchain will aid in the improvement of frameworks. Because current identity systems are ineffective and untrustworthy, blockchain innovation will serve as a single source of verification for both individuals and assets. This will help:

- Increase protection and productivity.
- Decentralize and verify the information gathered.
- Store data in a single record.
- Reduce the danger of security penetration.

Create a new identity system that is decentralized and not constrained by a central authority. This builds transparency. Some use cases of blockchain in the identity area are business records, tax identification records, government records, certificates and healthcare records, and reputation scores.

## Considerable Improvements in the Global Standard of Living

Today, destitution and wage fraud are the most difficult concerns that humanity must deal with. Over 10% of the population, or over 750 million people, live on less than $2 each day. Over 2 billion people are considered unbanked since they do not use financial services. Blockchain has the potential to significantly reduce the poverty gap. This is accomplished in three different ways, which are:

- **Reducing Corruption** – The blockchain ensures the integrity of legitimate records. All titles, including land, will be recorded on permanent (sealed), plain, and secure digital records that will be completely open to the public. Susceptibility to any advantage reduces its recognizability potential and resource cost. Creating a decentralized, asset-tracking framework will aid in the expansion of global wealth. Sifting through this maze will have enormous positive monetary consequences for the global economy.

- **Financial Inclusiveness** – This is an evident benefit of digital currencies such as Bitcoin. Unbanked people can use cryptocurrencies and blockchain to get banked and, as a result, receive payment. There is no compelling reason to rely on a centralized institution, such as a bank or government, to grant approval for opening a financial account when blockchain innovation is used. It will provide easy access to a crypto exchange where users can buy and sell digital currencies using their smartphones. With a large number of merchants around the world accepting cryptocurrencies, digital currencies will become a true standard by 2030, similar to the United States Dollar, which is widely accepted now.

- **Tokenization of Value-Generating Assets** – Tokenization refers to the use of digital tokens to demonstrate asset ownership. The blockchain facilitates the tokenization of large-scale assets. This means that even a rancher from a rural area can become a part-owner of an income-generating asset, such as an inn or a gold mine. When compared to traditional investments, this will help open up a prospective financial investor base to a larger market, reduce exchange time, and increase liquidity.

## Findings and Suggestions

The results of the 30 research papers revealed that blockchain was becoming increasingly practical in IoT, system, and data storage security. Because there are currently 9 billion IoT devices, the focus on blockchain IoT security is justified. These devices have weak security designs, and many of them are hacked and added to botnet networks. The Mirai Botnet, which has been used with great success against large targets such as Dyn DNS, one of the Internet's leading domain name service firms, is one such botnet that has integrated IoT devices. As a result, a number of security researchers are looking at ways to ensure that these devices are secure using blockchain.

The second major focus of blockchain cyber security research is data storage. This is due to the increased number of data breaches in which attackers have had the ability to steal information from corporations with billions of clients. For instance, the Yahoo data breach in 2014 resulted in the theft of information from three billion users. As a result, security researchers are investigating blockchain security solutions for data storage, such as cloud platforms. It can also be noticed that scientists are looking at the use of blockchain for ensuring the security of systems. Since current system safety attempts, such as security through WPA encryption, can be subverted, the significant majority of the examination in these areas is focused on authentication. Finally, there is a lot of interest in blockchain security solutions for identity verification. The great bulk of the experiments are evaluating strategies for ensuring authenticated data by using a widely used blockchain authentication mechanism. Clients will no longer be required to share their data with organizations; instead, organizations will verify clients using the blockchain.

## Conclusion

In the cutting-edge world, blockchain innovation continues to evolve and explore new use cases. Cybersecurity is one of the appropriate areas where it has been studied and used. The blockchain foundation allows it to be extremely practical in dealing with contemporary security concerns in areas such as IoT devices, systems, data transfer and storage. Most blockchain security scientists appear to be concentrating their efforts on the adoption of blockchain security for IoT devices. Alongside this, other significant areas of blockchain security are systems and data. Blockchain technology can be utilized to ensure the security of IoT devices by utilizing increasingly robust authentication and data transfer mechanisms. These can prevent attackers from breaking into these devices, which often have weak security designs. The innovation can also be used to ensure the security of systems by utilizing the secure framework to prevent unauthorized connections and communication [15]. Finally, blockchain ensures data transmission and storage through encrypted blocks that can only be opened by the transmitting parties and are not subject to control. Despite the fact that other use cases are being explored, these three have become the primary focus. Future researchers should look into the feasibility of using a single blockchain to establish security arrangements, because the vast majority of current arrangements use several blockchains, which makes integration difficult.
