Switching to a blockchain-based system is more beneficial for almost all industries rather than sticking to the traditional system. Each technology came with challenges and drawbacks associated with it. So, organizations need to be ready and prepare themselves to deal with the complications while adopting blockchain technology [22]

It has been found by Peck, 2017 [17] that some areas of blockchain in cybersecurity need further investigation, based on a survey result and observations, the Blockchain for the Internet of Things (IoT) network is a utmost priority need of the industry that needs to be improved. The security of Internet of Things (IoT) systems may revive if they get support from blockchain technology. Furthermore, in the modern computing system, the data captured by different sources can be effectively secured by Artificial Intelligence (AI) technology. But intentionally or unintentionally, the data can be integrated by a malicious third party that could be based on adversarial inputs. As a popular ledger technology Blockchain has the potential to be leveraged in different areas of cyber security and reduce the attempts of financial fraud. It has also been observed by Hasanova [23] that blockchain is a technology that brings an exorbitant array of possible solutions for finance, logistics, healthcare, and cyber security.

Traditional weaknesses like destructive actions and positive false errors can be eliminated and watched out through the blockchain's consensus algorithm without any interference from the central authority. Authentication and record management cannot be fooled by hackers attack [24]. However, Blockchain has many non-traditional features also, and it takes the advantage of its important cybersecurity tool which is encryption. A blockchain system can give a shield to many vulnerable devices like security cameras, etc. It has been found in a Palo Alto Network report that 98% IoT devices traffic were unencrypted.

New challenges have been arising to improve cybersecurity with the development of the smart grid. With cutting-edge technologies, the smart grid is also emerging with other services of the smart cities like transportation, water/gas supply, etc. Several ways have been proposed to make the smart grid flexible against cyber-attacks and among the several methods, blockchain is the method that gives hope to protect the smart grid cybersecurity. It has also been found that blockchain can improve cybersecurity for data collection and other executions in the smart grid as blockchain is the most promising solution for the improvement of cybersecurity for the smart grid.
