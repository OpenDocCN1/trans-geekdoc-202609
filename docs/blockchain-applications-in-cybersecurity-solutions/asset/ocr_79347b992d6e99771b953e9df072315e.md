# Medium Storage Decentralization

Organizations now have primary concerns related to business data hacks and theft, most companies use a centralized form of storage which can be simply exploited by hackers. To get access to entire business data stored in the system, hackers can get it by finding a single weak point. These kinds of cyberattacks may give a huge loss of sensitive data related to business such as financial data records, *etc.* Blockchain can protect sensitive data by ensuring a decentralized storage form. This method will make it impossible for the hackers to get into the systems and attack the storage. Storage companies are now adopting the ways of blockchain to protect their data from hacker attacks. For example, Apollo Currency Team has already adopted blockchain technology to their systems as The Apollo Data Cloud.

# Data Transfer Protection

# MAJOR PROS AND CONS OF BLOCKCHAIN FOR CYBERSECURITY

Blockchain has the capabilities as a cybersecurity solution but it also draws parallel measures of risk associated with cybersecurity.

Strengths of Blockchain for cybersecurity are discussed in Fig. (2) as major pros of blockchain for cybersecurity. Blockchain records data that provide secure data storage and processing. It also enables secure and safe data transfers. Decentralized systems make them more resilient so there is no single point of failure. The history of transactions can be easily traced in blockchain due to data transparency and traceability feature. Blockchain network also provides user confidentiality that authenticates users by verifying users through their signature. Data privacy and transparency increase customer trust in different businesses.
