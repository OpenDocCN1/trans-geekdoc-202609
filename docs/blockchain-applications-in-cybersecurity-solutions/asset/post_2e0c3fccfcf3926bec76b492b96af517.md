### IoT (Internet of Things) Security

IoT devices have been used by the hackers and they are easy to target for them like through routers they gain access to the overall system very quickly. Artificial Intelligence obsession makes it easier for hackers to get access to the entire system by home automation as the edge devices have the smart switches option, and most of the IoT devices have this feature. In such cases, the devices can be secured by the blockchain's decentralized technology by using it in their administration. It will make the device capable of taking auto security decisions and securing it by detecting suspected activity from unknown networks. Hackers target the central administration of the device to get full control over it and by decentralized technology such device authority will not get harmed and blockchain ensures that this kind of attack will not be executed.
