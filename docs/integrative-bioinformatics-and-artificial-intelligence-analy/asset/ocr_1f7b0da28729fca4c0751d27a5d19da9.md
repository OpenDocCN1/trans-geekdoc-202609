86 Studies have shown that MDD affects the functional activity of the brain which can be
87 identified by analyzing data on aberrations in the neuronal network [17,18]. Moreover, the
88 heterogeneity of depressive disorders may also be related to neuronal plasticity correlated with
89 different symptomatology [19]. Besides, it is suggested that MDD results from
90 systemic changes in the signaling and biochemical pathways involved in the regulation of
91 moods, cognitive functions and position [20]. Therefore, a comprehensive understanding of
92 affected or associated biological pathways in MDD is of high importance to reveal
93 the molecular mechanism of MDD and to identify accurate targets for the MDD diagnosis.

94 To date, machine learning (ML) as an application of AI approaches is successfully
95 applied in medical studies, by training computer models to process and understand complex
96 patterns within big-data which facilitate classifications or predictions of new cases [21,22].
97 Moreover, using OMiCs strategies is amongst the most applied approaches at the forefront of
98 personalized medicine in psychiatry with high efficiency [22,23], such as transcriptomics
99 which showed increasing evidence of the potential to detect, and substantially improve the
100 treatment of such complex cognitive disorders [24,25]. Furthermore, there is a rising interest
101 in using integrative Omics and neuroimaging data to understand how an altered genetic profile
102 in psychiatric disorders could influence brain structure and function. This can provide a deeper
103 understanding of molecular mechanisms and uncover endotypes-neurobiological
104 changes because of genes implicated in depression [26].

105 In this study, the key aim is to compare transcriptomics data of MDD patients to healthy
106 controls using bioinformatics and ML approaches and to identify key biomarkers that can aid
107 in the diagnosis and prediction of the onset of patients with depression.
