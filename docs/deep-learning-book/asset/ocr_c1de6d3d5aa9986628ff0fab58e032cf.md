# 20.10.4 Generative Adversarial Networks

Generative adversarial networks, or GANs (Goodfellow et al., 2014c), are another generative modeling approach based on differentiable generator networks.

Generative adversarial networks are based on a game theoretic scenario in which the generator network must compete against an adversary. The generator network directly produces samples x = g(z; θ^(g)). Its adversary, the discriminator network, attempts to distinguish between samples drawn from the training data and samples drawn from the generator. The discriminator emits a probability value given by d(x; θ^(d)), indicating the probability that x is a real training example rather than a fake sample drawn from the model.
