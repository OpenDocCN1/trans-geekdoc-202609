FIGURE 11.2: Check Amount by Fraud Indicator

Gridlines are incorporated from matplotlib. For a bivariate plot (also called a joint plot), the commonly specified parameters are x, y, and the dataframe. The values of x and y are the variable names entered as strings, while data refers to the dataframe object. Similar to the univariate case, the type of graphic can be changed by swapping the graph type and including the relevant parameters.

Most of the checks were not fraudulent, and their points are plotted on the bottom horizontal grid line. We see the four fraudulent checks on the top horizontal grid line where is_fraud=1. The check amounts appear to roughly be valued at 1000, 4500, 5000, and 10000. We can also inspect the fraud records in the dataset:
