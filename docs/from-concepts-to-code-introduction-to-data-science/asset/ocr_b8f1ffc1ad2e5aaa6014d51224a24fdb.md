# 18.5 Resources

# Certification and Degree Programs

In addition to the textbooks throughout this book, there are several other places to learn data science. There is a wide array of online courses from vendors including Coursera, Udemy, Udacity, and Codecademy. There are several free courses on YouTube. A course that I found particularly valuable in my data science career is the Stanford CS229 Machine Learning course taught by Andrew Ng. This course offers a detailed, mathematical treatment of many essential models and techniques.

Several companies offer bootcamps, certifications, and degree programs. There are several online master programs, such as the program where I teach at the UVA School of Data Science. When deciding to make a large investment of resources, it is best to understand costs, curriculum content, support, networking opportunities, and exit opportunities. It can be helpful to review testimonials from the various programs.
