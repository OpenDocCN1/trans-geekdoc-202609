+   1. $\S$ You are asked to validate a linear regression model. What might you verify as part of checking the conceptual soundness of the model?
2. $\S$ You have discovered a powerful predictor, and its correlation with the target variable is 1. What is your next step?
3. $\S$ Why is a benchmark model important?
4. You discover that the predictive performance of a logistic regression model is better than the champion network model. All else equal, which should you choose?
5. A technology startup has developed a sophisticated model to predict fraud. The startup believes that the F1 score is the most important metric to your target customer, and through testing, the model has a high F1 score. Assuming that F1 score is the only thing that matters to the customer, are they likely to use the model? Explain your answer.
6. $\S$ Explain how stress testing is different from sensitivity analysis
7. A hedge fund is designing a set of stress tests. Select all of the tests that may be useful.
   a) The S&P falls by 1%
   b) The S&P falls by 25%
   c) An asteroid hits company headquarters
   d) Inflation skyrockrockets by 10%
8. You would like to conduct sensitivity analysis on a Lasso regression model. List some sensitivity tests that you could run.
9. $\S$ Explain the difference between monitoring and observability. Hint: here is a dependency between them?
10. Why is it important to establish baselines for monitoring?
11. $\S$ How might you detect factor drift? How could you mitigate factor drift?
12. Visit http://huggingface.co/ and review the model cards of some models. One good example is bert-base-uncased. Note anything that you find interesting.
