# 8.5 Matrix Algebra

Matrix, Vector, and Scalar

A $matrix$ **A** is a rectangular array of numbers with $m$ rows and $n$ columns written

$$\begin{matrix} a_{11} & a_{12} & a_{13} & \dots & x_{1n} \ a_{21} & a_{22} & a_{23} & \dots & x_{2n} \ :vdots & :vdots & :vdots & \vdots & :vdots \ a_{m1} & a_{m2} & a_{m3} & \dots & a_{mn} \end{matrix}$

Matrices (plural of matrix) will be useful when we organize our data for calculations. They also provide a compact notation for solving systems of equations, which we encounter in the upcoming regression modeling.
