# 2.8 Take Ownership

This section is not about conquering territories, bending people against their will, and running a tyrannical empire. It is about earning the privilege to manage and leads by taking full responsibility for outcomes. Management entails keeping things running smoothly, generally by following established practices, leading entails breaking new ground and providing a vision for the future. Management and leadership are essential, and they both require ownership.

A guiding principle to keep in mind is that your boss likes wants a promotion, and to get a promotion means delivering great outcomes. When you are interviewing with a hiring manager, and when you are on the job, the manager is asking herself: “Will this person an asset to the team?” If the answer is “no,” then the probability of getting the job or the promotion will be low. When the boss assigns a project to a person or a team, she needs rest assured that it will be completed within the timeframe with proficiency and a good attitude. The basics of project management apply: the project members need to know the tasks, the roles, the deliverables, and the deadlines. You don't need to raise your hand on everything, but you need to deliver on your promise.

To get in the mindset of an owner, you might need to adopt some ways of thinking. First, you can start by thinking that if you don't do something, it won't get done. This may be accurate if you are the only person with the required skills. Second, you can assume responsibility when things go wrong. If a teammate makes a mistake and customers complain, can you blame yourself? The manager didn't give clear instructions was your fault not asking for clearer instructions. Is this reasonable? Perhaps, and taking the blame will make you more likely than directing blame at others. Nobody likes to be blamed for things. You might get the opportunity to manage small projects or mentor a junior teammate. As you see success, you might get to show larger roles, such as building and managing a team or a product. This will bring responsibility and a likely sense of satisfaction.
