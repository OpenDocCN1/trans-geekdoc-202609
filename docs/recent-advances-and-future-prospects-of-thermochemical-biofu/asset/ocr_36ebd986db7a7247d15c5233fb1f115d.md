renewable energy systems have receive much attention with its high efficiency and environmental benefits [128]. Intermittency issues of renewable energy become additional consideration for these integrated systems. Thus, the complexity for optimization of such integrated system increased substantially. Besides being able to establish a comprehensive date set for biomass and respective thermochemical processes, the required data set should be expanded towards a much wider parameters while responded to the fluctuating energy condition during the thermochemical processes rapidly. The well-developed ML model that approximates the complex function can be used as a surrogate model to reduce the computational cost of optimizing the whole integrated process. When the optimization of the integrated systems is assessed for a given feedstock and renewable energy system, the choice of the thermochemical conversion process will be suggested by an advanced ML model. Either with various feedstocks blend to tackle the unstable supply of single feedstock or alternating between different energy sources. The corresponding operating conditions could be suggested by the ML model to produce biofuel stably and efficiently.

# 6. Conclusions

Recent development of ML models for thermochemical biofuel conversion processes were reviewed. ML models have been developed to predict the yield and composition of biofuel products, as well as reaction kinetics. The predictive performance of ML models can be improved by increasing the number of data points, provided relevant hyperparameters are set properly. Although many experimental studies have been conducted, there is still a barrier attributed to the complex composition of biomass, preventing the utilization of all published data. In addition, the various types of reactors, catalysts, and reactants prevent the standardization of operating conditions and process designs for ML modelling. A comprehensive dataset that unifies various biomass characteristics and operating conditions and theory-integrated ML models are highly desired and will promote the design and optimization of thermochemical conversion processes. Categorical input variables were better defined using numerical variables to improve the interpretability of input variables.
