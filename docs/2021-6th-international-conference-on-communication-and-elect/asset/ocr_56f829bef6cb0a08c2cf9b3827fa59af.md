# ABSTRACT—

Due to the growing population and people's need for comfort, more automobiles are being purchased, particularly in urban areas. This results in heavy traffic, indicating that traffic violations are becoming more dangerous in every corner of the world. As a result, people's wealth decreases, and there are more accidents resulting in the loss of many lives. This necessitates the need to develop traffic violation detection systems to automate and eliminate the unaware among population. The proposed traffic violation detector can identify signal violations who are unaware that they are apprehended if they break traffic law. The proposed system is faster and easier than human, as traffic police are the only one who can capture images of violating traffic but not one violation simultaneously. The proposed system can detect most common types of violations in real-time through computer vision techniques and leaves good results with great accuracy.
