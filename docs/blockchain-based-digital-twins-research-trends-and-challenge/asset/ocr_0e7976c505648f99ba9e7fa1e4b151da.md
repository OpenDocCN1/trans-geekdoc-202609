# 2.7 BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY FOR SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT

Handling the movement of goods and services between various stakeholders is the responsibility of Supply Chain Management (SCM). The traditional SCM is modeled for a centralized approach, with all other sub-offices receiving instructions from the main office, which is handled at the central headquarters [24, 25]. Current SCM’s primary challenges include security, transactional transparency, traceability, stakeholder engagement, product duplication, increased latency, corruption, and unreliability. On the other hand, blockchain and SCM technology manage data effectively and transparently while establishing trust. Moreover, researchers need to focus on transacting the payments directly to a party or with supply chain transaction permission with valid verification.
