The major benefits of predictive maintenance:

+   1. Fewer Failures of Machinery: Regular monitoring of the machine conditions and process systems helps to minimize failures by predicting the need for maintenance in advance.

+   2. Increased Equipment Lifespan: Prevention of failure reduces the severity of impairment and the dissemination of defects. It helps to improve the lifespan of machines.

+   3. Effective Management/Decision Making: The ability of predictive maintenance to use real-time data enables managers to make decisions on when to replace machinery.

+   4. Increased Safety: Early prediction of problems minimizes the possibility of fatal accidents and injuries.

+   5. Cost Reduction: As predictive maintenance predicts the health status of the machines and decides when the parts need to be replaced or repaired, the need for unnecessary inspection and maintenance can be avoided. Hence, the cost incurred due to this unnecessary maintenance can be eliminated.
