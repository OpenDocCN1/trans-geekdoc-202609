# 1.2.7 BLOCKCHAIN IN CRYPTURRENCY

A cryptocurrency, or crypto, is a decentralized digital currency that uses blockchain technology to record and secure every single transaction. The “crypto” in cryptocurrency refers to cryptography that allows for the creation and processing of digital currencies that do not allow counter-feiting or spending multiple times [37].

The first decentralized cryptocurrency using blockchain technology created is Bitcoin (BTC). There are plenty of other cryptocurrencies known as altcoins (Alternative Coins) other than Bitcoin. Some of the altcoins are Tether (USDT), Ethereum (ETH), Cardano (ADA), USD Coin (USDC), Binance Coin, XRP, Litecoin, Polkadot, IOTA, Ripple, etc. During the year 2022, CoinMarketCap provided a source that there are around 22,000 cryptocurrencies worth $850 billion.

Few advantages of cryptocurrency:

+   - A cryptocurrency transaction is a transfer of a variety of cryptocurrencies anywhere in the world with little time on the blockchain.
- Transactions can be done easily through mobile, desktop, or hardware wallets at a low cost without having a bank account.
- Decentralized cryptocurrencies make secure systems as they use the techniques of cryptography and blockchain security.
- Crypto markets work the clock without any break. Disadvantages of cryptocurrency:
- Understanding cryptocurrency takes time and effort.
- It is difficult for the government to track the exchange of money involving illegal transactions.
- Crypto mining requires special hardware like Graphics Processing Units, Solid-State Drives for crypto mining, Application-Specific Integrated Circuits, mining software, and a wallet.
- Crypto mining cannot be done on regular computers.
