After that, the primary focus of the early stage will be on the duties involved in connecting the VO and the PO, assuming that the PO is actually present at the moment design is being carried out. When the VO is still in the design stage, there is a possibility that its PO element will not even appear at all. In this scenario, a prototype PO can be developed in order to direct the development of the VO. In addition, certain design decisions can be evaluated by combining the model with the VO. Each VO will, in the end, reflect and replicate the PO or its model. This will be the case regardless of whether or not the PO existed when the concept was conceived. It may be possible to anticipate certain problems in the product by using simulation, particularly in situations where the product is subjected to strain or is used in hazardous conditions [14]. The digital twin’s ongoing synchronization with both the manufacturing systems and its development are significant qualities; for instance, updates in cabling or modifications to the physical or fixed point should be taken into account in the VO. As a result of this, a solution that is developed needs to be adaptable and able to take into account any recent shifts in engineering models or operational procedures.
