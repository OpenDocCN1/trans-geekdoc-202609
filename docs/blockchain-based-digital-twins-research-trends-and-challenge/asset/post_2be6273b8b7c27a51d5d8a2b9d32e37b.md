### 5.4.1.4 STATISTICAL MODEL

The statistical model defines the linkages and assemblages of relations between the components that carry out the roles and behaviors. ## 5.5 TECHNIQUE FOR THE INTEGRATION OF MODELS

Digital twins will enable the integration of complicated models that are connected to one another, producing a view of the system that is more all-encompassing.

The integration needs to take a number of different considerations into account, including the following:

- 1. How do different parts of a system interact with one another to form more complicated?: This suggests that models work together in order to accurately represent the actions of POs. It is important to keep in mind that the conclusions reached by other independent models can be altered or made incorrect based on the decisions made by some models. As a consequence of this, it is possible for there to be outcomes that are inaccurate or contradictory if the models do not communicate information to one another and do not act in a coordinated manner.
- 2. How does the digital twin connect with the environment, specifically how the decisions made by the digital twin have an effect, either directly or indirectly, on the physical processes that are taking place?: It might be challenging to determine the bodily impact right off the bat. The selections should be transmitted by the digital components that use the physics principles of the PO so that the effects of these choices and any potential inconsistencies may be assessed. This will allow the digital components to determine whether or not there are any discrepancies.

It is possible for errors to occur in large and varied projects if the relationships and dependencies involved are not adequately described. This indicates that if each model is exact when viewed in isolation, the exactness of the entire system may depend on how the models interact with one another. This is the case even if each model is accurate when viewed in isolation. Because of all of these factors, the step of model integration in the digital twin development process is one that is both difficult and essential.

As a consequence of this, the physical system receives input based on the judgments and decisions made within the virtual representation. ## 5.7 CONCLUSION

The proliferation of interactive Internet of Things devices has led to a considerable increase in the volume of multi-structured data that is produced, communicated, and kept in local and remote storage systems. The creation of digital twins for Internet of Things devices is currently in progress with the intention of better comprehending and articulating the structural features and behaviors of these devices in a range of scenarios. In fact, digital twins are able to simulate the behavior of physical objects in response to changing environmental conditions.

This chapter provided a comprehensive analysis of the myriad of potential ways in which the merger of these two technologies could bring a variety of digital advances as well as disruptions in the sector. This will make it possible for a variety of user cases, business cases, and technology cases to be envisioned and put into reality. In light of this, the field of digital twins is expected to witness tremendous growth in the years to come as a direct result of a close synchronization with blockchain.
