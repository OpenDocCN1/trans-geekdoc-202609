# FIGURE 2.7 Open research challenges in AI-powered healthcare insurance fraud detection.

# 2.8.1.1 CLASS IMBALANCE

HIC fraud classifiers are constructed using machine learning models that perform poorly due to class imbalance, which is a significant challenge in recent research. With more records than the minority class, the majority class uses a computer modeling approach to categorize the imbalances. It is more difficult to determine the minority class as it has fewer HIC records. As a result, a comprehensive and elaborate approach to the class imbalance issue must be put forward.

# 2.8.1.2 STANDARDIZATION OF DATA EXCHANGE RULE

No standard law or regulation pertains to the cross-border exchange of HIC data. Different governments have privacy laws governing the trade of HIC data. The benefits of blockchain and AI-enabled HIC fraud detection systems while data sharing may be constrained when multiple authorities are involved in HIC data. Consequently, more research is needed to standardize the retrieval and storage of HIC data, as well as legislation and regulation.

# 2.8.1.3 ACCESSIBILITY OF THE DATASET

Public and private healthcare sectors should have increased accessibility to actual HIC data. Real-world HIC data have proven to come by of issues with the law, privacy, and competition [29]. Future studies using open-source HIC real-world datasets are crucial for enabling other researchers to verify their findings. To obtain the suggested solutions employing actual healthcare insurance data, researchers need to work with the healthcare insurance industry.
