# 8.2.1 DIGITAL TWINS

With their data, functions, and communication capacities, DTs represent tangible items or persons in the electronic realm. The term DT refers to a digital model that resembles an entity's real-world behavior based on data. To be very realistic with actual information, the virtual model employs deep fundamental and ecological modeling and insights collected from deep monitoring and assesses the characteristics of a physical entity. The fundamental distinction between a DT and an online scenario is how a digital twin is created, which depends on a subject's personal traits, remote monitoring, and current information.

The device known as DTs replicates physical things in the virtual realm and operates on the same principles as it does in the physical one. Because it embodies them like twins, the fact of the online environment is referred to as electronic twins. Initial DTs replicated the artificial world's realism, yet current advancements in IoT, computer, and AI technologies have made it possible to incorporate a variety of scenarios that are not possible to imitate owing practical constraints.
