### 2.6.1 OPEN RESEARCH CHALLENGES AND FUTURE DIRECTIONS OF THE ORACLE

-   Trust and Security Issues: Security and dependability are the primary advantages of blockchain, as it provides cryptocurrency-based methods and consensus algorithms. On the other hand, Oracles, are outsiders that provide data to the blockchain ecosystem, lack authenticity. Dealing with unauthorized data sources raises security concerns and difficulties. Typically, they do not offer strong assurances on the authenticity of the data, potentially introducing inaccurate or outdated data to the blockchain [22, 23], ensuring the accuracy and integrity of the data becomes impossible. Several design considerations have been implemented to guarantee the dependability and integrity of Oracles. The significance of blockchain lies in maintaining security and dependability to ensure the success of cryptocurrency approaches, but it is crucial to address issues by improving security and privacy-related design and operations of Oracles.

-   Performance Evaluation of QoS: Towards overall investigation, Oracle has not yet been thoroughly examined or analyzed to measure QoS performance and its fundamental characteristics. Even though several studies have been addressed and some performance parameters are to be evaluated in the blockchain-Oracles ecosystems such as integrity of data, throughput, transactional delay, flexibility, scalability, and all basic metrics that must be considered for future advancement.

#### 2.8.1.1 CLASS IMBALANCE

HIC fraud classifiers are constructed using machine learning models that perform poorly due to class imbalance, which is a significant challenge in recent research. With more records than the minority class, the majority class uses a computer modeling approach to categorize the imbalances. It is more difficult to determine the minority class as it has fewer records. As a result, a comprehensive and elaborate approach to the class imbalance issue must be put forward.

#### 2.8.1.2 STANDARDIZATION OF DATA EXCHANGE RULE

No standard law or regulation pertains to the cross-border exchange of HIC data. Different governments have privacy laws governing the trade of HIC data. The benefits of blockchain and AI-enabled HIC fraud detection systems while data sharing may be constrained when multiple authorities are involved. Consequently, more research is needed to standardize the retrieval and storage of HIC data, as well as legislation and regulation.

Public and private healthcare sectors should have increased accessibility to actual HIC data. Real-world HIC data have proven to come by of issues with the law, privacy, and competition [29]. Future studies using open-source HIC real-world datasets are crucial for enabling other researchers to verify their findings. To obtain the suggested solutions employing actual healthcare insurance data, researchers need to work with the healthcare insurance industry.
