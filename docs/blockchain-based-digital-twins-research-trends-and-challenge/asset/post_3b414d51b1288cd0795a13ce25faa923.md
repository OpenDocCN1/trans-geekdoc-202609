## 11.8 CONCLUSION

Using the proposed technology, all the resources needed for the crops can be reduced. Moreover, it reduces waste by increasing the efficiency of the crops' productivity, reduces the infected crops due to diseases or viruses, reduces investments in the field, predicts future progress so that it can be planned accordingly, reduces the manual work in the field, increases the quality of the crops, fruits, and vegetables, and also maintains the quality of the soil. These are the advantages of the proposed method. The only problem is here or data information maintenance and security. By using big data, the issue can be nullified. It is also better to use the cloud to store all the data because it is closer to the 6th generation (6G). Due to that, the cloud has faster accessibility. The data and records will be safely stored in Blockchain.
