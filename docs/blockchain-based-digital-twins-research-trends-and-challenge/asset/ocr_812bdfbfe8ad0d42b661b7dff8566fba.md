FIGURE 9.1 Process for defining digital twin.

Digital twins are distinct from related notions due to the data link, which is
frequently but not always two-way. Users may query the data to learn more
about the condition of the item or process through this link, and actions
expressed through the digital twin can affect its physical counterpart [12]. The
key addition to the original definition is synchronized at a set frequency and
quality according to the Digital Twin Consortium, an organization that
promotes standards and works to develop the market.
