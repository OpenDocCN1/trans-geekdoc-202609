# 7.3.5 IIOT AND BLOCKCHAIN INTEGRATION

The IoT digitizes and streamlines manual processes, allowing for unprecedented levels of information to be gleaned from the copious volumes of data it generates. This information is paving the way for the development of intelligent apps that will improve city administration and the standard of living for its residents as a result of the widespread digitalization of city services. In recent years, advancements in cloud computing have provided the IoT with the tools to analyze, interpret, and translate data into real-time movements and information. There has been extraordinary evolution in IoT, which has opened up several possibilities for people to interact with one another and with information. The principle of open data lies at the heart of these initiatives. As has been the case in a number of previous attempts, however a lack of trust is a major weakness. The advent of centralized systems, as the cloud computing concept, has been extremely beneficial to IoT. Data openness is secondary, and network participants are in the dark about what happens to the information they provide.

The benefits of combining IoT with cloud computing have become clear. Furthermore, we acknowledge that blockchain might significantly alter the IoT landscape.
