+   - When many transactions are generated at once, the time it takes for each transaction to be confirmed may be significantly longer.
- The originator of operations can increase transaction costs to encourage the miner to provide those activities precedence and better public services in order to address this contradictory rivalry.

#  7.4.1.3 SMART CONTRACTS

+   - Secured code execution, also known as a smart contract, is a computer-based operation that may be executed independently of a trusted third party.
- Once certain conditions are satisfied, the smart contract becomes active and enacts the relevant contractual requirements.
- The smart contract can operate as a real-time auditor since all transactions in the distributed ledger must be logged and validated.
- A smart contract can either be implemented externally, where the receiver checks the transactions using the smart contract address, or internally, where the code is run.
- Blockchain technology allows for tracking all aspects of performance.
- The smart contract is dynamically executed independently on each node of the blockchain network.
