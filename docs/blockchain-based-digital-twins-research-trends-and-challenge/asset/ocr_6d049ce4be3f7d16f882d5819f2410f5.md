# 1.2.3.2 MANAGING E-GOVERNANCE

Blockchain technology can help to eliminate intermediaries in digital transactions, making the process faster, more efficient, and more secure. This can reduce bureaucratic procedures and streamline interactions between government agencies and citizens. It can also reduce costs associated with intermediaries and intermediaries' errors, leading to greater transparency and trust in public services [25].

The combination of DTs and blockchain technology can bring transparency, security, and efficiency to urban city management services, including traffic management, public transportation, and governance. Blockchain technology can help facilitate secure and transparent data exchange between government and citizens, while DTs can provide an accurate and real-time simulation of the physical objects and infrastructure in a city. This can help city development planners and authorities to make more informed decisions, optimize resources, and improve the quality of life for citizens [26].
