# 11.1 INTRODUCTION

Most of the countries are agricultural-based nations. Agriculture is very important because it is the source of our life. In fact, in India, 68% of the people are based on income from agriculture and allied sectors. It is the main source of human food. Even small-scale productions and cottage businesses depend on farming production centers. Not only for food but also in our daily lives, all the necessary products are produced by agriculture (meaning all products' raw materials are from agricultural plants and trees). Therefore, in the current situation, the population is increasing drastically. Similarly, with the increase in population, pollution is increasing. The nutrient level in the soil has decreased significantly, and the soil is heavily affected by the chemicals entering the rivers and lakes [1].
