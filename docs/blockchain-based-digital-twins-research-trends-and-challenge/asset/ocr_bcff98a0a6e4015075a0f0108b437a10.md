The combination of blockchain and DT can help overcome the problem of counterfeit products. By introducing DTs during product development, companies can predict the success and failure of the product. When this technology is integrated with blockchain, it ensures the product’s originality. Blockchain technology provides data transparency and enables the creation of digital certificates, while DT technology ensures the digitization of products and assets, providing a more secure and reliable system.

The amalgamation of blockchain and DTs can enhance the credibility of data and lead to more efficient and accurate decision-making in realtime applications such as logistics and the utility industry, resulting in improved productivity [18].
