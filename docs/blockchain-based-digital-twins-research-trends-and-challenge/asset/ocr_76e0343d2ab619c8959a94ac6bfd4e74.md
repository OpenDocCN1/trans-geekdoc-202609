# 11.2.1 PROBLEM STATEMENT

Many countries' development depends on agriculture. Increasing world population and estimations show that in 2050 the population will be approximately 9 billion. Due to urbanization, people are moving to cities, specifically, 65% of people are residing in cities. It causes challenges in shelter and food production. Not only is the population increasing, but also the availability of areas is not. Due to this fact, agriculture needs to be improved in terms of production with fewer resources. The other main problem in agriculture is crops that are damaged due to diseases and insects. Even though insects help to maintain soil structure, fertility, and nutrients, pathogenic organisms like bacteria, viruses, and protozoa cause diseases in plants. This results in an average of 40% loss in agricultural production.
