## 6.2 RELATED WORKS

Several methods and techniques have been strongly advocated by many for making smart devices. One of them is the development of digital twins for IoT devices. Digital twins continuously gather useful information from their corresponding physical twins and process it to extract actionable insights from data masses. The learned new knowledge is relayed to the physical tools to properly energize them for accomplishing their tasks. They learn to adapt their behavior wisely to the rapidly changing environment thanks to this ongoing nurturing [6].

One of the primary difficulties is deciding how to safely convey data in real-time and how to disseminate simulation data. Sensitive information from data owners may be contained in data generated by physical assets, and if this data is leaked to an enemy, serious privacy issues may arise. Consider exchanging data with other data users to maximize the usability of data from digital twins. To overcome these problems, we suggest an architecture of the system for the appropriate exchange of information on digital twins [7]. The proposed system concept makes use of blockchain technology for data verification and cloud computing for effective data exchange.

An artificially created human being, typically in the form of a virtual, is called a digital twin. This image can be used as a patient stand-in in a medical setting or for research reasons. The development of a digital twin has the potential to improve patient care and research in the healthcare sector [8]. It can aid in decision-making regarding how to utilize equipment and what repairs are required by giving a better understanding of how it is functioning. For example, by producing a precise clone of a patient’s brain, researchers can study illnesses more fully and understand how factors impact living tissue. By using digital twins instead of actual patients, hospitals can save money on labor costs and research programs. Healthcare professionals are currently talking a lot about digital twins [9]. They help hospitals improve patient care and research while also lowering the cost of research programs. Digital clones are identical duplicates of physical items or infrastructure, including a person’s mind or an engine from a vehicle. Models are produced using computational modeling and aid researchers in understanding how systems operate by permitting viewers to witness exactly which specific factors were modified [10].

By utilizing a virtual model that accurately investigators may examine different therapies on an individual’s brain without administering any medications. Digital twins have recently begun to play the role of actual patients in the research of diseases such as cancer, which has long been the focus of academics [11]. By conducting clinical trials with virtual patients rather than actual patients, doctors can save money on resources, labor costs, and research initiatives. To avoid investing money in new resources before knowing if they will be efficient to be used in actual patient care, hospitals may use digital twins to replace existing personnel during operations [12].

Digital twins enable digital patient management in life-threatening circumstances anywhere in the world. ### 6.3.2 NETWORK PARADIGM

A blockchain network, or technical infrastructure, provides contract (chain code) services and an application ledger. The primary purpose of smart contracts is to establish transactions, which are subsequently transmitted to all their peer nodes in the network and permanently recorded on each of their respective ledgers. Users of applications could be blockchain network administrators or end users utilizing client applications [14]. Typically, a collaboration of organizations forms the network, and when the network is initially configured, the collaboration decides on a set of regulations that will govern which organization’s permissions are granted access to the network.

The described approach gives a network paradigm that utilizes blockchains for cloud-based digital twin scenarios as stated in Figure 6.2. The proposed concept consists of four parts: a blockchain, a cloud server, a client side, and a data consumer. A more thorough explanation of each entity can be found below:

- 1. **Cloud Server:** The web server has the processing power to run simulations and retain the outcomes. Following a mutual key agreement, Data owners’ physical assets are accessed by the cloud server. The information is used by a cloud server to create virtual twins. The findings of the simulations are subsequently communicated to the record via the server and provided to customers according to the consent.

Individuals, on the other hand, utilize the hash function to sure there is no change in the information.
