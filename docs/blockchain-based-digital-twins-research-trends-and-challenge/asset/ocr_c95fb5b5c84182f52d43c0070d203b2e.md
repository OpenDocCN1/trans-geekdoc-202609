# 5.3.2 FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

This phase will outline the goal as well as the functional requirements in order to develop a digital twin’s specification. Additionally, it details the data that we are required to retrieve and explain in compliance with the PO. Digital twin specifications must be defined with precision, and as much data as is humanly possible must be gathered in order to design the system. It is absolutely necessary for the process of system modeling to have a fruitful requirement analysis. Digital twins have been used for a wide variety of purposes, including the optimization of processes, security monitoring, forecasting, education, and training. A digital twin will typically integrate one or more of these capabilities into a single offering.

+   Optimization: It is possible to study the complete output of data generated by the digital twin using sophisticated data analysis tools in order to obtain reliable information. Therefore, a digital twin has the potential to improve the functionality and efficiency of a system, as well as the ability to minimize costs or risks, and has gained attention. The digital twin is able to run simultaneously with a CPS, which allows them to conduct activities such as analyzing the level of safety and security provided by the system when it is being used with a CPS in order to perform tasks such as analyzing the level of safety and security provided by the system while it is being used. In their study, Willard et al. [16] conducted a comprehensive review of the deployment of a digital twin in the context of a cyber safety program in CPS. CPSs are composed of two levels that are interconnected, and those levels are the core network layer and the digital layer. Both components are shielded from potential hazards so that the system can continue to work normally.
