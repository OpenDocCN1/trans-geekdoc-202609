Private blockchains are usually used in healthcare systems because of their enhanced security and privacy features. These systems require strict access control and privacy measures to protect sensitive health data, and private blockchains can provide these capabilities. They can also facilitate the interoperability of health data between different entities securely and transparently. A framework that is a collaboration of blockchain-based DT for providing an alerting service for COVID-19-like pandemics as the declaration of quarantine, lockdown protocols, etc., was developed by authors [35]. They defined their framework with four layers, namely in the high level – physical layer, next level – blockchain-based DT layer, intermediate level – data analytics layer, and at the core level – decision-making layer. The physical layer would likely involve humans (doctors, nurses, pharmacy, government sector, etc.) and non-human participants (IoT devices like CCTV, sensors for measuring blood pressure, ventilator, ECG, etc.) that collect patient data, which would then be fed into the DT layer built on top of blockchain technology. The data analytics layer would use machine learning and other algorithms to process and make sense of the data, while the decision-making layer would use this information to make clinical decisions and recommendations.
