# ABSTRACT

Agriculture is the major source of the world’s food and fabrics. Increasing population and globalization affect the demand for food. So, there is a need to develop cost-effective technologies to improve agriculture. One such technology is the digital twin. This twin technology is the computer-generated imaginary illustration of the factual article in the computer-generated imaginary world. A digital twin is a virtual replica to monitor, detect, prevent, predict, and customize using real-time data. In agriculture, the digital twin acquires data using sensors, satellites, drones, historical soil information, and soil probes. From observations, first, it creates a database using API data, live sensor data, and manual records. Then it processes the data and provides historical data as output. Comments on feedback, and errors, predicts future conditions, and suggests further improvement. It provides photos, digitized paper maps, farm data, moisture content, weather, and crop properties. Furthermore, the specified data are stored using blockchain technology where the blockchain is a chain of blocks that contain information. It is used for computing and to store transactions and records. This chapter explores the usage of Blockchain and digital twins in the agriculture sector. It also deals with the sensing, computing, and communicating models for various agriculture applications. Finally, it enumerates the virtual model for healthy crops and non-healthy crops in the agriculture field.

# 11.1 INTRODUCTION

Most of the countries are agricultural-based nations. Agriculture is very important because it is the source of our life. In fact, in India, 68% of the people are based on income from agriculture and allied sectors. It is the main source of human food. Even small-scale productions and cottage businesses depend on farming production centers. Not only for food but also in our daily lives, all the necessary products are produced by agriculture (meaning all products' raw materials are from agricultural plants and trees). Therefore, in the current situation, the population is increasing drastically. Similarly, with the increase in population, pollution is increasing. The nutrient level in the soil has decreased significantly, and the soil is heavily affected by the chemicals entering the rivers and lakes [1].

Due to the decreased nutrient level, crops are being affected at an early stage. After the nutrients, crops are further affected by pests and fungi at a higher rate. Even though farmers use pesticides and fungicides to control them, the crops still get affected. Moreover, farmers cannot identify all these issues at an early stage. Therefore, there is a need for automation to identify and address these issues at the initial stages. Looking at automation in agriculture, there are many innovations in management [2]. However, all these advancements face a common challenge, which is the collection, storage, and processing of data for management.

As agricultural fields contain millions of crops, the data collected for further processing is extensive. There is no backend option to retrieve this data for future use. Therefore, we decided to create a blockchain integrated with big data automation to monitor the crops, along with Artificial Intelligence (AI) to predict diseases at an early stage [3]. In the blockchain component, the data collected from the automation process is stored in the cloud for easy access at any time and for seamless transmission to the AI for evaluation metrics. Most of the plants are affected by diseases. To control the issue, it is necessary to know the status and condition instantly. With the ongoing revolution in the industry, digital twin technology plays a significant role. Therefore, the proposed method involves using “Digital Twin” technology to monitor the crops. This can be achieved by creating a virtual representation of all crops in farmland [4].

In Digital Twin technologies, a simulation is created for the farmland and the crops to gather data such as water content in the soil, temperature, plant health, growth, and more. The proposed simulation includes adding external features like AI to continuously monitor the crops (24/7) and provide real-time updates on the status of the plants and crops, as shown in Figure 11.2.

For modeling and training, the proposed simulation needs some deeplearning concepts. All these technologies occupy more memory space. To overcome this specific disadvantage, it is planned to process the data and records into another form, i.e., “Blockchain.” Blockchain is known as distributed ledger technology. It creates a database of digital assets that are chained together and stored for processing. It is decentralized, and real-time data can be accessed. Blockchain technology is encrypted and immutable. It is used in many applications like healthcare, gaming, and industries. In the proposed field, it is used for computing and storing transactions and records. Therefore, it improves the efficiency in the transmission of data at a higher speed.
