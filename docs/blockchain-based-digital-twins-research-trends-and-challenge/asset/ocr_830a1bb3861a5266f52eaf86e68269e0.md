#  5.7 CONCLUSION

The proliferation of interactive Internet of Things devices has led to a considerable increase in the volume of multi-structured data that is produced, communicated, and kept in local and remote storage systems. The creation of digital twins for Internet of Things devices is currently in progress with the intention of better comprehending and articulating the structural features and behaviors of these devices in a range of scenarios. In fact, digital twins are able to simulate the behavior of physical objects in response to changing environmental conditions.

This chapter provided a comprehensive analysis of the myads of potential ways in which the merger of these two technologies could bring a variety of digital advances as well as disruptions in the sector. This will make it possible for a variety of user cases, business cases, and technology cases to be envisioned and put into reality. In light of this, the field of digital twins is expected to witness tremendous growth in the years to come as a direct result of a cool synchronization with blockchain.
