Kumar et al. [15] projected research work to facilitate the data analysis of the critical processes in the Industrial Internet of Things. The two major security elements – integrity and authenticity, are secured by adopting a blockchain-based data communication scheme. Also, the authors projected a Deep Learning-based algorithm for detecting intrusion in blockchain learning the long-distance feature.
