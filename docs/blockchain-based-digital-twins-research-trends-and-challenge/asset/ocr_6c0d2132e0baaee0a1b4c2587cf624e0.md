# 10.4 CONCLUSION

In this chapter, a novel framework for predictive maintenance of the IoT-enabled industry was proposed by leveraging the benefits of edge computing, DT, and blockchain technologies. It utilized the LSTM for feature extraction and predicted the major maintenance activities like possible time and type of failure of different machine equipment and which parts of the machine require replacement or regular preventive maintenance activities at what time. The effectiveness of prediction functions was improved by managing the DTs of multiple parts of the machine. Parallel governance of multiple DTs provides a comprehensive insight into the functionalities of machines. The data collected from the DT enables the framework to identify the difference between expected target characteristics and the current behaviors of the machine. The incorporation of blockchain technology ensures the security of data of different machine parts and allows the smooth sharing of it among diverse stakeholders.
