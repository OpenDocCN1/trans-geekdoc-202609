In the healthcare blockchain, when a medical examiner creates a medical record, a digital signature is created using a private key that is unique to the examiner. This digital signature ensures that the document is authentic and has not been tampered with. Other participants in the blockchain can then verify the digital signature using the examiner's public key to ensure that the document is valid and can be trusted. The patient can have access to their medical records and contribute to them by adding information obtained through their personal health devices. The data stored in the data lake can be analyzed for insights, which can be used for personalized healthcare services and better decision-making. Access to this data is limited to authorized individuals and the blockchain ensures the integrity and security of the data.
