In Ref. [6], they presented a review paper on digital twin applications in greenhouse horticulture. Many papers focused on the cultivation process, energy management, and climate control. Specifically, unmanned horticulture is introduced using the digital twin in the proposed review paper. IoT along with the digital twin is used to make automation. The gained performance metrics using the predictive and prescriptive applications are used at their early stage itself. In Ref. [7], a review paper based on the espousal of digital twins in civil engineering was proposed. The drivers are identified for digital twin adoption in the civil engineering field and classified. The essential need for the digital twin in civil engineering business was emphasized. Therefore, it is aimed to execute an acceptance model which is a combination of mixed reality and Digital Twin MRDT.

In Ref. [8], they proposed a solution for farmers to solve environmental factors, planning, and task management using IoT. Agriculture faces great challenges in supplying substantial resources for the increasing number of residents around the world. In order to address the proposed issue, productivity needs to be increased. The proposed paper presented a classical procedure and strategic scheme for decision-making in intelligent farming. In Ref. [9], they presented a model to introduce digital twins in sustainable agriculture. Through IoT, productivity, self-protection, energy saving, and automation based on environmental changes are improved. The digital twin contributes by monitoring, planning, operating, and optimizing. In Ref. [10], they proposed a design based on IoT and Wireless Sensor Network (WSN) for monitoring the greenhouse.
