# About the Editors

### A. Prasanth, PhD
Associate Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai, India

A. Prasanth, PhD, is currently working as an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology, Tamil Nadu, India. He has published more than 40 research articles in international journals and conference proceedings, among which 15 were indexed in SCI and 22 were indexed in Scopus. In addition, he has published eight patents and 10 books under a reputed publisher. He has served as a recognized Anna University supervisor with four scholars conducting their research under his guidance. One scholar has completed his PhD under Professor Prasanth. His research interests include the Internet of Things, blockchain, machine learning, wireless sensor networks, ad hoc networks, and computer networks. He received his BE in Electronics and Communication Engineering, ME in Computer Science and Engineering (with a specialization in Networks), and his PhD degree in Information and Communication Engineering, all from Anna University, Chennai, India.

### Vandana Sharma, PhD
Associate Professor, CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Delhi NCR, India

Vandana Sharma, PhD, is an Associate Professor at CHRIST (Deemed to University), Delhi NCR, India. Dr. Sharma has served at NAAC-accredited A++ institutions such as Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, Delhi, Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, Delhi; Galgotias University, Greater Noida; and Amity Institute of Technology, Amity University. She is a senior member of IEEE, a member of Computer Science Teachers Association, and a member of IEEE Consumer Society. As a keen researcher, she has published more than 50 articles in SCI-indexed international journals and conferences. Dr. Sharma has presented as a keynote speaker, session chair, and program committee member. Currently, she is a postdoctoral fellow at Lincoln University, Malaysia, and an Adjunct Professor at Beni Suef University, Egypt. Her primary areas of interest include artificial intelligence, blockchain technology, and Internet of Things. Dr. Sharma earned her doctoral degree at Amity University and her Master of Technology and Bachelor of Engineering degrees from Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, Delhi.

### Rajesh Kumar Dhanaraj, PhD
Professor, Symbiosis International (Deemed University), Pune, India

Rajesh Kumar Dhanaraj, PhD, is a Professor at Symbiosis Institute of Computer Studies and Research, Symbiosis International (Deemed University), Pune, India. He has contributed more than 25 authored and edited books, over 50 research papers in journals and conferences, as well as book chapters. He holds 21 patents. His research interests include machine learning, cyberphysical systems, and wireless sensor networks. He is a senior member of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers and a member of the International Association of Engineers. He is an expert advisory panel member of Texas Instruments Inc., USA. He received his BE in Computer Science and Engineering and his MTech and PhD degrees, all from Anna University, Chennai, India.

### Pethuru Raj, PhD
Chief Architect and Vice President, Reliance Jio Platforms Ltd. (JPL), Bangalore, India

Pethuru Raj, PhD, is Chief Architect and Vice President at Reliance Jio Platforms Ltd. (JPL), Bangalore, India. Previously, he worked as a cloud architect at IBM Global Cloud Center of Excellence (CoE), as an enterprise architect in Wipro Consulting Services (WCS), and as a software architect in Robert Bosch Corporate Research. In total, he has gained more than 22 years of IT industry experience and eight years of research experience. He finished his CSIR-sponsored PhD degree at Anna University, Chennai, and continued with his UGC-sponsored postdoctoral research in the Department of Computer Science and Automation at the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore. He was granted international research fellowships (JSPS and JST) to work as a research scientist for several years at two leading Japanese universities. He has authored and edited more than 45 technology books, published more than 60 research papers, and contributed more than 50 book chapters. Dr. Raj has also submitted a couple of international patents. He completed several IT architecture certifications (TOGAF and ITIL) as well.
