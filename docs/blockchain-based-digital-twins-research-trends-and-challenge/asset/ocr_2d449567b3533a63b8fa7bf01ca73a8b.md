Applications that use sixth-generation (6G) wireless networks for the Internet of Everything (IoE) have a variety of requirements for latency, dependability, data rate, and user-specific performance measures. As a result, to run and enhance the 6G wireless network and other related IE services, it is necessary to create a new framework. On DTs, a new architecture for 6G could be established with the help of related algorithms, communication technologies, computing platforms, privacy mechanisms, and the virtual depiction of the physical 6G system (e.g., blockchain). In Ref. [21], the author proposed the essential design specifications for utilizing a DT to enable 6G. Architectural trends and elements twins based on edge-cloud, cloud, and edge are discussed. Also, a comparative description of different twins done.

The potential for DT technology to be used in business and industry has recently brought it to the forefront. In DT atmospheres, information produced by physical assets is sent to a distant server, which runs imitations using DTs in a virtual environment. The owner of the data or other users can receive the simulation's findings. The use of DT technologies in the real world is not without difficulties. Identifying secure sharing methods for simulation data and real-time data are two of the major issues. Sensitive information from data owners may be included in the data created by physical assets, and privacy issues could arise if data were to be disclosed to an enemy.
