# ABSTRACT

Blockchain innovation is more established than Bitcoin. It is an essential central part of most cryptocurrency organizations. A decentralized, distributed, publicly accessible electronic ledger must maintain a highly permanent record of all recently confirmed transactions. Internet of Things (IoT) and blockchain advances are, by and large, heavily taken advantage of and utilized in many spaces, particularly for e-medical care. The blockchain is designed to be highly resistant to change. Blockchain addresses various issues by providing decentralized algorithms and storage for IoT data. Blockchain technology certainly offers modern prospects for the functionalities of smart contracts. The fundamental idea of a smart contract is the automatic execution of specific tasks upon the fulfillment of certain conditions. The supply chain is the biggest adopter of blockchain after crypto.
