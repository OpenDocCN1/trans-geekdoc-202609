The potential for DT technology to be used in business and industry has recently brought it to the forefront. In DT atmospheres, information produced by physical assets is sent to a distant server, which runs imitations using DTs in a virtual environment. The owner of the data or other users can receive the simulation's findings. The use of DT technologies in the real world is not without difficulties. Identifying secure sharing methods for simulation data and real-time data are two of the major issues. Sensitive information from data owners may be included in the data created by physical assets, and privacy issues could arise if data were to be disclosed to an enemy. To optimize the availability of DT data, it should also be thought about sharing data with other consumers. The authors in Ref. The suggested system concept makes use of blockchain technology for data verifiability and cloud computing for effective data sharing. Communication plans for the suggested paradigm to ensure data security and privacy protection through wireless channels are provided.

In Ref. [23], the authors imagine how blockchain may change and alter DTs to enable safe production that ensures transparency, adherence, integrity, and security. Based on important criteria, the DT literature (for example: DT tiers, design stages, use cases in the industry, important goals, supporting technology, and fundamental applications) is categorized. Blockchain technology can improve the idea of DTs by ensuring openness, decentralized storage space, data integrity, and peer-to-peer interaction in industrial sectors. The DTs play a critical role in preserving and managing Industry 4.0 since they assist in visualizing designs in three dimensions, conducting testing and simulations virtually before the construction of any physical component. A Systematic Literature Review on predictive maintenance, which is considered as a method for developing more sustainable, secure, and successful industry, is provided in Ref. [24]. Lack of failure data is one of the biggest obstacles to the development of predictive maintenance systems since machines are routinely repaired before they fail. The predictive maintenance algorithm can employ data from DT, which offers a depiction of the physical world equipment in real-time and creates information about asset depreciation. Since 2018, there has been an upsurge in the scholarly works on the use of DTs for condition-based preservation, signifying the need for an extensive investigation. To open the door for more research, this study gathers and synthesizes works that concentrate on predictive maintenance employing DTs.

In a manufacturing organization, predictive maintenance is strategically important to preserve high quality in production and prevent unplanned stoppages. To plan preservation cycles and maximize productivity in this situation, the future health status of the machines must be predicted. The suggested method is based on the application of Electronic Design Automation methods that have been translated from the electronic field to the field of manufacturing lines. In Ref. ## 10.3 FRAMEWORK OF BLOCKCHAIN-BASED DIGITAL TWIN FOR PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE

A framework of Blockchain-based DTs for the predictive maintenance of industrial machines has been proposed. The various stages in the development of the framework are elaborated.

### 10.3.1 PRE-DESIGN PHASE

In this phase, information required for constructing the DT so that the DT will imitate the functionalities of real-time physical machines is identified. The required information is classified into functional information and operational influencing information. The functional information provides insight into the sequence of operations to be replicated by the DT for predictive maintenance like parts of the equipment, the functionality of each part, physical characteristics of each part, operational sequence, logical and physical connection among the parts, etc. The operational influencing information refers to the factors that affect the performance of the machines and external factors that influence the function of machines like temperature, oil, number of hours operated, humidity, etc.

- Virtual Entity (VE): Based on the information identified in the pre-design phase, virtual entities corresponding to their physical counterparts and additional virtual entities required so that the functionality of physical parts can be replicated in the DT need to be identified.

- Hierarchy Relation of VE: The collection of virtual entities and the hierarchical relationship among them creates the virtual representation of the physical entity to be imitated by the DT. Hence, based on the operational sequence of the physical entity to be replicated, the hierarchy of the VEs has to be created by establishing their functional relationships. The hierarchy of VE will form the DT of the sub-physical part of the complex machine.
