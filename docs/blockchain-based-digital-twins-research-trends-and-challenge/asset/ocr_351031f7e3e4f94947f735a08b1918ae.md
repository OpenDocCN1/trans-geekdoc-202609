Unlike IoT and IIoT, CPS are distinct but related technologies [34]. Just as IoT has the potential to significantly impact everyday life, IIoT has the potential to have a profound effect on manufacturing processes by facilitating the evaluation of tasks with more information and real-time responses via wireless networks, continuous improvement, conversion efficiency, expenses, effluents, and many other crucial landmarks within the business context. The IIoT has an impact on large-scale operations in agribusiness, oil, gasoline, and other industries as well as manufacturing. Similarly, IIoT is having a big impact on the sector thanks to IoT. There are many crucial areas in both IoT and IIoT that guarantee the smooth operation of connected equipment.
