# 9.2.2 SIMULATION VERSUS DIGITAL TWIN

Digital twins and simulations may utilize the virtual model to replicate the multiple operations of any system. The digital twin may be more helpful for study because it is essentially a virtual scenario. A simulation and a digital twin differ in scale. A digital twin may contain many relevant simulations as necessary to simultaneously investigate several processes, whereas simulations normally analyze one procedure at a time. There are likely more differences. For example, real-time data is frequently advantageous for simulations.

Digital twins, on the other hand, are created to work with multiple information flows that start whenever the system processor receives crucial data from object sensors.
