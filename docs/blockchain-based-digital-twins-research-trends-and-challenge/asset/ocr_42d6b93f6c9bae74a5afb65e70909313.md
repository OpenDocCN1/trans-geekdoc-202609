# ABSTRACT

Agriculture is the major source of the world’s food and fabrics. Increasing population and globalization affect the demand for food. So, there is a need to develop cost-effective technologies to improve agriculture. One such technology is the digital twin. This twin technology is the computer-generated imaginary illustration of the factual article in the computer-generated imaginary world. A digital twin is a virtual replica to monitor, detect, prevent, predict, and customize using real-time data. In agriculture, the digital twin acquires data using sensors, satellites, drones, historical soil information, and soil probes.
