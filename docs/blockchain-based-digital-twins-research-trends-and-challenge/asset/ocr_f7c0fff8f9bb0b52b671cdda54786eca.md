+   2. Data Security: DTs are stored as blocks in all nodes of a blockchain system, making it nearly impossible for hackers to manipulate or hack data without finding and compromising all instances of the DTs. Additionally, if any attempt to hack or modify data is made, it will be immediately visible to all other nodes, and the corrupted block will be rejected, ensuring that the blockchain system remains secure.

+   3. Real-Time Data Analytics and Predictions: The data and behavior of DTs can be analyzed for prediction of the behavior of the system, streamlining many processes.

The combination of DT with blockchain removes any loopholes and makes the system more trustworthy for users for their everyday operations.

# 1.2.1 DT IN AUTOMOTIVE AND AEROSPACE

By creating a digital twin of a machine, it becomes possible to monitor its performance in real-time and collect data about its usage and maintenance history. This information is used to make predictions about the machine's remaining lifespan and the optimal time to replace its parts. This process is known as predictive maintenance and can help businesses save time and money by avoiding unexpected downtime and reducing maintenance costs.

By creating a digital twin of a physical object and collecting data on its behavior and performance over time, it becomes possible to predict when maintenance will be required, as well as to identify potential areas for improvement and optimization. This can lead to significant cost savings, improved efficiency, and increased lifespan for the physical object.
