Industry 4.0 is a term used by industry professionals to describe the integration of new technologies such as IoT [3, 4 and 5], AI, VR, Cloud Computing (CC), and blockchain into their production processes. These technologies are incorporated into smart factories that feature embedded software, advanced sensors, and robotics to collect and analyze data for decision-making. Businesses can also benefit from better logistics, supply chain tracking, and improved customer service. By implementing AI-powered sensors and devices, manufacturing errors can be reduced and saved. With a smartphone, a person can monitor the operations of their manufacturing unit with minimal investment. The application of machine learning algorithms can detect errors immediately, preventing costly issues down the line.

Industry 4.0 technology is being implemented in many fields, from agriculture to medicine. # 1.1.1 BLOCKCHAIN

In a centralized network, all nodes are connected to a single server. If the server fails, the operations of the other nodes will be paused, and no processing of information can take place as all the nodes depend on the central server for data. In contrast, in a decentralized network, there is no concept of a single server that monitors every other node. Each node in the network has its entity and contributes to the network.
