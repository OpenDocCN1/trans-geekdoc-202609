# 5.6 DIGITAL TWIN DEVELOPMENT

The second stage of the procedure consists of putting the digital twin into action. This section looks at the standards, procedures, and protocols that were utilized in the process of creating the PO and synchronizing with its VO.

# 5.6.1 PROTOCOLS OF COMMUNICATION AND SYNCHRONIZATION OF DATA

The network is absolutely necessary for the interaction that must take place between the PO and its VO. The link makes it possible to not only collect data in real-time but also exchange instructions with one another. Because there is data exchange in both directions between the PO and its VO, the information that is gathered starting from the PO is used to bring the positions that are held in the digital model up to date. As a consequence of this, the physical system receives input based on the judgments and decisions made within the virtual representation. PO-to-VO interaction typically needs to go through these 3 stages to be successful.

+   i. Gather the pertinent data, which may include precise observations of the real objects;
ii. Analyze the information in a manner that is compatible with the degree of abstraction;
iii. Use the data.

There is more than one way to connect sensors and gateways, and the method that is used will depend on the kind of system that is being used. Numerous commercial solutions have been developed with the goal of offering services and solutions for Internet of Things devices and enabling data processing and transfer. This mode of interaction can also be utilized to relay just important incidents rather than providing information on a more routine basis.
