## 5.1.2 BLOCKCHAIN

For the digital twin paradigm to achieve the level of success that was envisioned, data is of the utmost importance. In a linked world, new data streams would typically consist of the attributes of physical items, the data pertaining to their interactions, as well as their future states. Both the physical and digital worlds participate in the data exchange that takes place. Digital twin instances, also known as digital twins, are simulations of real-world entities and activities [6].

A violation of a digital twin will result in the compromise of one product as a result of this collaboration. In the event of a breach of the digital twin, involved items will be at risk. The blockchain technology has the inherent capacity to overcome security gaps which are necessary in order to get digital twins back. Because blockchain technology possesses the characteristics of decentralization and immutability, digital twins that use blockchain can innovate more effectively and quickly than they could otherwise.

The cryptographic properties of blockchain make it possible to transfer data in a safe and reliable manner [7]. Multiple parties, including users, collaborators, and stakeholders, can be gathered under a single roof by utilizing consensus mechanisms built into the blockchain. stakeholders have evolved into gatekeepers who are responsible for enforcing transparency and accountability provided by blockchain technology. As a result, sensitive information about both the company and customers can be shared without worry.

The Internet of Things, digital twins, artificial intelligence, and other cutting-edge technologies are smartly integrated and empowering blockchain technology. The synchronization of these technologies with blockchain, on the other hand, is providing them with a boost that is both beneficial and significant. It is common knowledge that one of the most significant challenges facing businesses around the world in the modern era is the forging of their products. Thanks to the development of modern technology and tools, fraudsters now have the ability to quickly produce duplicates of a wide variety of high-priced products, which they can then sell to customers at a reduced cost. This precarious situation not only tarnishes the reputation of the brand but also results in significant financial losses [10].

When combined, digital twin technology and blockchain have the potential to deliver an effective answer to the problem of combating certain types of fraud. Together, they provide reassurance that the products are legitimate. The digitalization of tangible artifacts is much easier with the help of blockchain. Blockchain, with its capacity for decentralization, will bring about the transparency that is so desperately required. Any digital data stored will benefit from this increased level of protection.

## 5.1.3 BLOCKCHAIN FOR DIGITAL TWIN

The introduction of blockchain technology has recast the trajectory of development for digital twins. At a macro-level, blockchain technology makes it possible to store data in a distributed manner without a central authority having access to all of it [11]. New transactions may be added, but the ones already there cannot be altered or corrupted in any way. The integrity of the data and the transparency of the process are all ensured by this. The creation of a digital twin on a blockchain would make it possible to record in an unchangeable format any and all data relating to a physical product. Additionally, the transaction records associated with the product can be retained.

A digital twin that is constructed using conventional technology is required to have a centralized intermediary in order to store digital data and do data analytics [12]. The idea of digital twins will almost certainly go through some kind of fundamental adjustment as a result of the rapid development of blockchain technology. The immutability and security that are all assured by blockchain technology will propel digital twins to the next level. Evidently, blockchain is currently being promoted as a technology that may prevent counterfeiting as well as theft. If companies around the world put this into practice with the due care and attention it requires, then the problems caused by forgery and fabrication will be much easier to solve.

The confidence that individuals have in a variety of products receives a considerable boost [13]. Instantly, purchasers have access to all the important information regarding any product. Everyone would have access to in-depth information about each product, and the idea of unconfirmed ownership would become a thing of the past as a result. This invigorating combination is making its way into every industrial vertical, from the manufacturing sector to healthcare, advertising, and the logistics industry.
