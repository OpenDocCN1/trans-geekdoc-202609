# ABSTRACT

Digital twins (DTs) have been made possible by the quick development of computer, storage, communications, and networking technology. It is undoubtedly the digitized version of any physical feature, item, or machine. Further, it can be used for prototyping, simulation, validation, and 3-D design before the real component is built. A DT can be employed for configuration, surveillance, diagnostics, and prognostics once a physical component has been put into service. It is anticipated that the Internet of Things (IoT) and DTs will attract a lot of attention soon and will be crucial for the Healthcare Industry. However, current DT construction techniques, platforms, and tools are heavily centralized and unable to provide trustworthy data provenance, audit, and traceability.
