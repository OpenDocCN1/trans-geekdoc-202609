Designers and engineers utilize the same modeling and CAD tools to create digital twins throughout the initial product development process. The distinction between the digital twin and the traditional twin is that the digital twin is handled via Product Lifecycle Management software and retains and updates the model for use in subsequent phases of the product’s lifespan such as inspection and maintenance.

IoT sensors are frequently used as the physical links between the thing and its twin, and a developed IoT implementation is frequently needed for digital twins [13].
