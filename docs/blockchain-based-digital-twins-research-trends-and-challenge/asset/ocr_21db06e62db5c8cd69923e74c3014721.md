+   - **Spoofing**: The capacity of the attacker to pose as someone else on the system is referred to as spoofing.
- **Data Integrity**: It is violated by tampering attacks on the protected system's data.
- **Repudiation**: The capacity to retract one's previous statements or actions is known as repudiation.
- **Information Disclosure**: This category includes violations of confidentiality.
- **Elevated Privileges**: A privilege escalation attack occurs when a user can take over the system with unlawful levels of authority.

In the context of blockchain technology, we can categorize elevated privileges according to whether the attacker has unauthorized access to a user's account, a significant amount of control over the blockchain system (as in a 51% attack), or unauthorized permission access to a smart contract. For describing the probable effects that specific flaws or attacks may have on the security of a system, the STRIDE framework is helpful.
