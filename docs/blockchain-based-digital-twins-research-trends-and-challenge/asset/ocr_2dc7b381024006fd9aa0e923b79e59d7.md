+   1. Attributes: These are a person's name, age, gender, address, ideology, language, culture, religion, ethnicity, education level, and salary, among other things. In convergence of unique characteristics, we can forecast the patient's state of health and provide treatment recommendations.

+   3. Online Presence: This encompasses a variety of professional and social media apps that convey individuality. As a result, human digital twins are becoming increasingly popular. A digital twin of a human being help determine whether or not that person is at risk of contracting a disease that could endanger their life. Additionally, it can provide insights on how to change one's lifestyle in order to be healthy and live for a longer period of time.

Digital copies of physical twins are currently undergoing painstaking construction.
