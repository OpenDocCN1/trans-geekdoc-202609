# 10.3.1 PRE-DESIGN PHASE

In this phase, information required for constructing the DT so that the DT will imitate the functionalities of real-time physical machines is identified. The required information is classified into functional information and operational influencing information. The functional information provides insight into the sequence of operations to be replicated by the DT for predictive maintenance like parts of the equipment, the functionality of each part, physical characteristics of each part, operational sequence, logical and physical connection among the parts, etc. The operational influencing information refers to the factors that affect the performance of the machines and external factors that influence the function of machines like temperature, oil, number of hours operated, humidity, etc.

+   1. Virtua Entity (VE): Based on the information identified in the pre-design phase, virtual entities corresponding to their physical counterparts and additional virtual entities required so that the functionality of physical parts can be replicated in the DT need to be identified.

+   2. Hierarchy Relation of VE: The collection of virtual entities and the hierarchical relationship among them creates the virtual representation of the physical entity to be imitated by the DT. Hence, based on the operational sequence of the physical entity to be replicated, the hierarchy of the VEs has to be created by establishing their functional relationships. The hierarchy of VE will form the DT of the sub-physical part of the complex machine
