Three major categories of digital twins can be distinguished, each of which illustrates a different use for the technique:

+   i. Digital Twin Prototype (DTP): Before a tangible item is created, something is done;
ii. Digital Twin Instance (DTI): This is done following the creation of a product to test it in various scenarios.
iii. Digital Twin Aggregate (DTA): This program compiles DTI data to assess a product’s capabilities, perform prognostics, and evaluate operational conditions.

The broad categories may be used to accomplish a variety of activities, including system planning, product creation and redesign, logistical planning, and quality control and management. A digital twin can be used to reduce time and costs whenever testing is necessary for a product or process, whether for design, deployment, monitoring, or improvement.
