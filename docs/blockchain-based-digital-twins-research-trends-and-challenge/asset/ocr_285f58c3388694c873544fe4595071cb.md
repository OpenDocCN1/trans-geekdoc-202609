FIGURE 5.1 Benefits of blockchain-based digital twins.

+   1. DTS Decentralized and Immutable Data Transactions: It is essential that both the platform and the data of digital twins are protected. The use of cryptographic hashing techniques and the storage of digital twin data on distributed ledgers, which cannot be readily altered and are not under the jurisdiction of any centralized authority, contribute to the increased security provided by blockchain technology. One of the characteristics that sets blockchain apart from other distributed ledger technologies is its immutability, which assures that the distributed ledger can never be altered and maintains an unchangeable record of all digital twin transactions.
