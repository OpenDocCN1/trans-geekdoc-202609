# ## ABSTRACT

A twin is a virtual software system that simulates the workings of an actual object or process. The majority of digital twin information is centralized and not adequately support data management security, integrity maintenance, or trustworthiness and precision of timeconsuming processes. It is imperative that blockchain be integrated with digital twins to address these restrictions. With blockchains, digital twin data can be shared across secured connection systems with confidence in terms of both accuracy and transmission speed. Blocks of data that track network transactions make up blockchains. These blockchains are maintained on distributed ledgers and are incorporated into the chain as new blocks.
