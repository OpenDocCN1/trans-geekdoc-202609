The two challenges discussed above can be addressed using Aggregation and the Creation of blocks at the source end. But as mentioned already, IoT-enabled devices like sensor-fixed machines do not have enough computing power and storage. Hence, edge computing is considered a suitable solution for implementing these two processes.
