# 3.1 INTRODUCTION – THE TRUTH ABOUT BLOCKCHAIN

Without the use of any middlemen or centralized authorities, peer-to-peer exchange of tangible and digital assets is made possible by blockchain technology. It was first created to promote using Bitcoin as a cryptocurrency for financial transactions. Although blockchain was initially developed as the technology behind Bitcoin, its potential benefits go far beyond digital money. Decentralized technology has made significant progress with the development of blockchain. Virtually all monetary transactions in the internet marketplace are processed by independent financial institutions, raising transaction fees, capping the accurate minimum purchase price, and opening more doors to fraud than trust [1]. Because it is an encrypted electronic payment system that does not rely on fault, blockchain was developed as a distributed technology to circumvent the fundamental shortcomings of this trust-based approach. Through hashing, timestamping, tree architectures, and consensus procedures, blockchain enables peer-to-peer transactions over decentralized networks, removing the need for intermediaries [2].
