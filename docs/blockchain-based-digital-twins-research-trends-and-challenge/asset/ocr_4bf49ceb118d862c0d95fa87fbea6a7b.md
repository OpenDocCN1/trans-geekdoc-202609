Most of the above problems related to secure data can be solved by augmenting DTs with blockchain technology. A framework for the COVID-19 pandemic alerting system which involves the collaboration of blockchain, and DT technology was developed and discussed [34].

In a broader sense, the collaborative blockchain-based DTs to combat COVID-19 are depicted in Figure 1.4. The healthcare system is a combination of the physical and cyber worlds. The physical world includes doctors, patients, and other healthcare professionals, while the cyber world includes technologies like machine learning, CC, blockchain, DTs, AI, and big data. These two worlds work together to improve decision-making processes for hospitals, health organizations, and governments.
