The data repository is the key source for data generation in digital twin (DT) and connects all components of the DT. Data-based digital twins (DTs) emphasize product design, manufacturing, production, operational control, and so on in the industrial realm. DTSs, for example, provide more exact predictions, logical judgments, and informed planning by enhancing communication between the design and manufacturing phases. A data-driven DT system performs fault diagnosis, finds the unreliable behavior of the actual object, and provides design principles for maintenance.
