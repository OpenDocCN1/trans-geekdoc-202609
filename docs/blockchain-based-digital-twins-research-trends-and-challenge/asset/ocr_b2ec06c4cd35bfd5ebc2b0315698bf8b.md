+   2. Identity and Legitimacy of DTS: Immutable copies of all product-related data are stored in digital twins that are empowered with blockchain technology. In addition, records of their transactions are kept, which enables buyers to learn information such as the location of the factory where a certain product was manufactured, its previous owners, and so on. This contributes to the creation of evidence that verifies the legitimacy and identity of digital twins.

+   3. Tracking DTS Globally with High Accuracy: Creating digital twins on a blockchain platform can make it easier to identify and keep track of them around the globe with more precision. Blockchain enables decentralized parties to securely access distributed databases from multiple locations without the need for a third party. Since the distributed ledger's information can be quickly accessed and tracked, blockchain-based digital twins make recordkeeping, provenance tracking, and auditability much simpler tasks to perform. Through the use of blockchain, it is possible to track the history of digital twins all the way from their inception to the present day. A tracking method that is as accurate as this can be used to investigate and locate instances of fraud in any aspect of digital twins.

+   4. Access Rights and Trusted DTS: The majority of traditional access control measures are susceptible to being attacked by malicious cyber attackers. Blockchain, on the other hand, reduces the hazards of illegal access, which is the most common cause of tampered data. Access control that is based on a blockchain is able to manage all of the digital twins' information in a distributed manner, including policies and rules.
