Most of the plants are affected by diseases. To control the issue, it is necessary to know the status and condition of the plants/crops/trees instantly. Based on the plant’s condition, precautionary steps can be taken to maintain high efficiency and protect the plants from viruses and diseases. With the ongoing revolution in the industry sector, digital twin technology plays a significant role in the 4th industrial revolution. Therefore, the proposed method involves using “Digital Twin” technology to monitor the conditions and status of the plants and crops. This can be achieved by creating a virtual representation of all the crops in the farmland [4].

In Digital Twin technologies, a simulation is created for the farmland and the crops to gather data such as water content in the soil, temperature, plant health, growth, and more. The proposed simulation includes adding external features like AI to continuously monitor the crops (24/7) and provide real-time updates on the status of the plants and crops.
