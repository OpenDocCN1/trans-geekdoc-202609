# 9.3 HISTORY OF DIGITAL TWINS

The digital twin technology concept was first introduced in 1991 by David Gelernter in the book “Mirror Worlds.” Dr. Michael Grieves, a professor at the University of Michigan, developed the idea of DT software and implemented it in 2002. John Vickers of NASA then coined the term “digital twin” in 2010.
