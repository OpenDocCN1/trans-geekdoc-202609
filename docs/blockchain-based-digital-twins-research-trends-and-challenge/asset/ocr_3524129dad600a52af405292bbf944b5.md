Blockchain technology can help centralized companies address low security, high costs, and inefficient operations [5]. Blockchain technology enables peer-to-peer exchange of tangible and digital assets without intermediaries or centralized authorities. Initially developed to facilitate the use of Bitcoin as a cryptocurrency, the Bitcoin protocol served as the foundation for the first blockchain implementation. The double spending of digital currency was one of the issues the Bitcoin Blockchain was created to address. It is very challenging to stop users from copying digital things in the current era. Despite having little success, the film and music businesses have been battling unauthorized downloading for years. If users can make fake copies whenever they want, digital money is not viable. The feat achieved by “Satoshi Nakamoto”—the alias of the creator(s) of Bitcoin—is enabling peer-to-peer trades of digital currency safely. The Bitcoin system establishes trust between strangers in a completely decentralized manner through cryptographic methods and financial incentives.
