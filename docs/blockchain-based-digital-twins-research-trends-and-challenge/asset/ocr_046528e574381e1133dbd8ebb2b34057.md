# 6.3 SUGGESTED APPROACH

The utilization of digital twin technologies in the real world is not always without complications. Finding a method to accurately convey and modify simulation facts in real-time is one of the main challenges. The information generated by physical property may also include sensitive information from information owners, and if this information is disclosed to an enemy, serious private difficulties may arise. The suggested machine concept makes use of blockchain technology for data verifiability and cloud computing for effective fact exchange.
