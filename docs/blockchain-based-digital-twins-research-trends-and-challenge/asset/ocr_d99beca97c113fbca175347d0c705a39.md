# ABSTRACT

The concept of blockchain was first introduced in October 2008, as a mean of providing security features for Bitcoin transactions without the need for third-party intermediaries like banks. Blockchain technology operates on a peer-peer network, a distributed architecture that connects computing devices without a central server. Decentralization of data pooling, data immutability, and one-to-one communication between computers or Internet of things (IoT) devices are the key concepts of blockchain. Digital twins (DT) are virtual replicas of physical objects or systems, created using data and information from sensors, IoT devices, and other sources.
