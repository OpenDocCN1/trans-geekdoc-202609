# 1.1 INTRODUCTION

The first Industrial Revolution, also known as Industry 1.0, occurred in the 17th century and involved the introduction of large-scale manufacturing, aided by coal and steam as energy sources. This brought about a significant increase in productivity and the replacement of manual labor with machinery such as looms, gears, steam engines, and ships. The second Industrial Revolution, Industry 2.0, took place in the 19th century with the discovery of electricity and oil fields, resulting in the development of the assembly line for streamlining production [1].

Industry 3.0 emerged around 1970 in the 21st century with the introduction of automation in factories through the use of computers to replace repetitive tasks.
