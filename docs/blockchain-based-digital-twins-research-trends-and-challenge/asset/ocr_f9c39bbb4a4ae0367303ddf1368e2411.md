# ABSTRACT

The digital twin (DT) of the diagnostic patients is created using augmented reality (AR) and maintained if a patient is facing an emergency during the unavailability of the doctor. The virtual item is sent by the already assigned doctor to the operating doctor to grasp the internal status of the patient and treat the patient, among others. After a physical object is operational, a DT can be used for configuration, monitoring, diagnostics, and prognostics.
