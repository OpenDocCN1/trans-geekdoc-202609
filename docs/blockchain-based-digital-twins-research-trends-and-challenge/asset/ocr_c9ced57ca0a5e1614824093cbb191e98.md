+   Kafka: It is commonly used as a major event streaming platform to build
scalable and dependable digital twins and digital threads for real-time
streaming sensors. It provides an important event streaming platform for
real-time communication, processing, and integration.
Digital twin characteristics and their relationship to Kafka:

+   Connectivity: Real-time scalability is provided by Kafka Connect
for connectivity to IoT interfaces, cloud services, and big data. Other
middleware and IoT systems are not in competition with the Kafka
ecosystemystem; rather, they are complementary.

+   Homogenization: Or the actual decoupling between producers and
consumers, is one of Kafka's key benefits. The administration and
enforcement of schema via various technologies (JSON Schema,
Protobuf, Avro, etc.) promote data awareness and standards.

+   Reprogramable and Smart: Due to its programmability and
intelligence, domain-driven design, and concern segregation (DDD)
have become the de facto standard for microservice architectures.
Versioning, A/B testing, and canarying should be used when
launching new decoupled applications.

+   Digital Traces: These are used by the distributed commit log known
as Kafka. Events are immutable, added, and preserved for as long as
desired (possibly indefinitely with retention time = -1).
