### 2.1.2 PERSISTENCY
To provide decentralization, the blockchain in a network’s transactions is automatically stored in blocks with a distributed-entire network, leading to latency and redundancy. Furthermore, by storing the data in many nodes, improper identification, falsifications, or missing data frequently occur during transactions. For example, digitally signed information can only be confirmed if the relevant certificates exist. However, the short lifespan of certificates undermines their long-term resilience and verifiability.

### 2.1.3 ANONYMITY
In blockchain technology, banking systems always maintain and identify valid transactions along with valid person’s details [5]. It can be easily tracked, but does not provide enough anonymity for transactions. By using multiple addresses and interacting with the generated wallet, a person can keep their identity private, and there is no central repository for node’s data. In each transaction, the addresses are associated with wallet, and the transactions are publicly verified while maintaining their privacy. Still, the transaction cannot assure who issued or owned the blockchain address.

### 2.1.4 TRANSPARENCY
By providing decentralized and consistent records, the blockchain network ensures wider transparency. The importance of transparency cannot be underestimated in supply chain and logistics sectors. By constructing a transparent system, blockchain reduces the number of forgeries, and it is obvious how the data are coordinated or automated from inputs to several parties in smart contracts. Furthermore, the inherent transparency of blockchains frequently conflicts with organizational rules and data protection regulations, which minimizes the transaction process.

### 2.1.5 LOSS OF PRIVACY
A significant amount of privacy is protected in blockchain using public key. For each transaction, authentication is maintained to protect user identification. In contrast, the ledger collects the data and enables transactional security publicly because all identifiers and balances are made available. Still, data privacy works against policies and procedures. Therefore, blockchain is more suitable for various fields, even though it has not yet achieved its maximum development for privacy.

## 2.2 RESEARCH TRENDS AND CHALLENGES IN BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGY

### 2.2.1 ELECTRONIC HEALTH RECORDS
Electronic Health Records store health information, a revolutionary invention in the 20th century. In networks and communication, it offers decentralized networks among all nodes by improving health record management. It provides privacy, security, and accessibility to distributed P2P networks and does not depend on a central authority. In distributed P2P networks, data is allocated to all users securely or privately. In conventional methods, both the sender and receiver acknowledge the transaction details depending on a trusted party, which leads to few security issues and operational privacy. Millions of data in blockchain are highly beneficial for healthcare providers, but the open challenge for researchers is to fix data breaches and external attackers creating security issues. Therefore, monitoring and providing suggestions for verification and validation is essential.

If monitoring and suggestions are to be validated, the decentralized system for tracking data can be accessed when needed to avoid security threats. However, if validation is not scrutinized properly, the authentication protocol cannot transmit the data. If the intended value exceeds the stored block is not eligible to be added to blockchain. This addresses the limitation in EHR management, and it is essential to provide a secure environment for exchanging data with the help of Artificial Intelligence and edge computing.

### 2.2.2 EHR WITH ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
Integrating blockchain with artificial intelligence techniques like machine learning and deep learning is more effective in healthcare applications. In contrast, it helps to automate and design real-time applications with greater potential to share data confidentially. Researchers are mainly focused on developing machine learning-based EHR applications. Recently, IBM has declared few plans to implement blockchain with AI, where an AI agent performs multiple tasks including law enforcement, updated recordkeeping, and encouraging smart contracts over a wide network [6]. Machine learning algorithm is used to identify fraudulent data to achieve the above parameters. With the help of this development, old corrupted medical records and disease prediction can be retrieved.

### 2.2.3 EHR WITH EDGE COMPUTING
Edge computing is also the best choice to share and store enormous healthcare data based on network load and data size. It includes massive data storage, broad networking, and high computing power, and also supports distributed applications with flexibility in a controlled and secure approach [7, 8]. ## 2.3 BLOCKCHAIN IN PHARMACEUTICAL INDUSTRY
Blockchain technology is an advanced technology in recent digital platforms. It improves different automated industries because the pharmaceutical industry suffers from a lack of simplicity and transparency, leading to transmission difficulties, including products that may reach an expired condition. Blockchain technology has adopted different applications like preventing forgery in drug distribution, enhancing security, monitoring transportation, ensuring data quality, governance, turnover, and improving the perception of the drug with the distributed ledger. It makes it possible to record transactions among various nodes in a trustworthy, sustainable, and efficient manner. Also, security systems such as encryption and decryption under cryptography are used to ensure data accuracy.

Moving to the next level of the system, Blockchain technology includes authentication with verifiable serial codes for the source document. With the help of the serial code, drug quality control and tracking are carried out with digital signatures deployed from the producer to the pharmacy to maintain the stable quality of the drug and prevent theft. Serialized prevention of forged pharmaceutical products includes security characteristics that customers can confirm, distinguishing between authentic and fraudulent items with the help of chain code-based transactions. People expose society to threats by forging trust in companies and providing poor-quality drugs. Numerous opportunities, including an anti-forgery medicine system and IPFS network, are used to protect against forgery in the Ethereum blockchain:

- Set ownership constraints for non-retail and retail drugs to prevent drug cloning.
- Create practical ACMS administration with Ethereum contracts using IPFS networks and the host system.
- Activate the initiative for small business firms.
- Analyze the outcome.

Protection from scams can be effectively achieved through ACMS, where blockchain code is generated to initiate transactions by the client. The next verification step involves a digital signature, which sends the order service and validation to assess the quality of product distribution.
