The handling of the components and its simulation process is made easier by digital twins. So, as in agriculture, the agricultural fields are being shown virtually by digital twin using the factual information extracted from the previous process.

The information received from the AI prediction is made into 3D visualization. The agricultural field is made into a virtual model and, in that, the affected crops are being located by the location data obtained from the sensor. On looking into the particular location, the 3D visualization of the crop is made visible [24]. On the right side of the virtual model, the predicted data is shown for reference (disease name, disease spread percentage, nutrient content, required maintenance, etc.). On the other hand, the estimation of healthy species and their levels are displayed.

As shown in Figures 11.6 and 11.7, by using digital twin technology, field management is made easier, allowing farmers to identify diseased crops accurately and at an earlier stage. This helps satisfy the further requirements for the crops to recover from diseases. The virtual model of each crop can be viewed with options for rotation and zooming, making analysis more flexible and faster. As a result, the crop yield percentage increases, and due to effective management, the nutrient content remains rich.
