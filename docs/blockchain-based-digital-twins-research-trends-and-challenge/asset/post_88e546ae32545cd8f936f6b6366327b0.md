- 4. Access Rights and Trusted DTS: The majority of traditional access control measures are susceptible to being attacked by malicious cyber attackers. Access control that is based on a blockchain is able to manage all of the digital twins' information in a distributed manner, including policies and rules. ## 5.3 DIGITAL TWIN DESIGN

In order to complete the first stage of the process of developing a digital twin, it is important to model the static characteristics of the system. The purpose of this design is to determine the parameters and requirements of the system, including its functions and the breakdown of those functions. It is important that the modeling of data exchanges and communication, as well as the structures and reasonable layout of the system, as well as the conceptual prerequisites for putting the remedy into practice, which include both the software and hardware components, be taken into consideration.

In most cases, the Virtual Object (VO) is developed so that it can function as a reflection of the Physical Object (PO). As a consequence of this, it is possible for both components to coexist for the duration of the digital twin's existence.

After that, the primary focus of the early stage will be on the duties involved in connecting the VO and the PO, assuming that the PO is actually present at the moment design is being carried out. When the VO is still in the design stage, there is a possibility that its PO element will not even appear at all. In this scenario, a prototype PO can be developed in order to direct the development of the VO. In addition, certain design decisions can be evaluated by combining the model with the VO. Each VO will, in the end, reflect and replicate the PO or its model. This will be the case regardless of whether or not the PO existed when the concept was conceived. It may be possible to anticipate certain problems in the product by using simulation, particularly in situations where the product is subjected to strain or is used in hazardous conditions [14]. The digital twin’s ongoing synchronization with both the manufacturing systems and its development are significant qualities; for instance, updates in cabling or modifications to the physical or fixed point should be taken into account in the VO. As a result of this, a solution that is developed needs to be adaptable and able to take into account any recent shifts in engineering models or operational procedures.

### 5.3.1 SPECIFICATIONS OF THE SYSTEM

The goals and functional needs of the digital twin were originally established by the function model. This determines, whether, the digital twin will help with safeguarding, regulating, anticipating, or optimizing the processing. The next thing that needs to be done is to figure out which parts or processes within it will be part of the digital twin. In order to accomplish this, we need to create a plan for the process that takes into account both the procedure and the digital twin features, select the devices to be used, select the modeling aspect that will be used (such as mechanical systems, 3-dimensional areas, electrical, or any others), and determine the connection between the two parts, i.e., how well the parts will communicate with one another. In addition to this, it is necessary for us to determine which digital twin demands are not operational [15].

### 5.3.2 FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS

This phase will outline the goal as well as the functional requirements in order to develop a digital twin’s specification. Additionally, it details the data that we are required to retrieve and explain in compliance with the PO. Digital twin specifications must be defined with precision, and as much data as is humanly possible must be gathered in order to design the system. It is absolutely necessary for the process of system modeling to have a fruitful requirement analysis. Digital twins have been used for a wide variety of purposes, including the optimization of processes, security monitoring, forecasting, education, and training. A digital twin will typically integrate one or more of these capabilities into a single offering.

- Optimization: It is possible to study the complete output of data generated by the digital twin using sophisticated data analysis tools in order to obtain reliable information. Therefore, a digital twin has the potential to improve the functionality and efficiency of a system, as well as the ability to minimize costs or risks, and has gained attention. The digital twin is able to run simultaneously with a CPS, which allows them to conduct activities such as analyzing the level of safety and security provided by the system when it is being used with a CPS in order to perform tasks such as analyzing the level of safety and security provided by the system while it is being used. In their study, Willard et al. [16] conducted a comprehensive review of the deployment of a digital twin in the context of a cyber safety program in CPS. CPSs are composed of two levels that are interconnected, and those levels are the core network layer and the digital layer. Both components are shielded from potential hazards so that the system can continue to work normally.
