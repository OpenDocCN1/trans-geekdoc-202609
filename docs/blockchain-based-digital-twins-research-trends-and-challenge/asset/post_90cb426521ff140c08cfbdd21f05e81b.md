## 1.4 CONCLUSION

Blockchain and DTs have the potential to revolutionize many fields, including logistics, manufacturing, healthcare, and more. A DT replicates the processes of any real-life objects like buildings, cities, machines, windmills, etc., and simulates them to help gather the history of their activities and predict their performance. DTs can help organizations monitor and optimize the performance of physical assets, while blockchain can provide secure record-keeping for transactions and data sharing. The amalgamation of blockchain and DTs leads to strong, credible, and correct decision-making in many real-time applications like logistics, where the industry leverages for improved productivity.
