In Ref. [23], the authors imagine how blockchain may change and alter DTs to enable safe production that ensures transparency, adherence, integrity, and security. Based on important criteria, the DT literature (for example: DT tiers, design stages, use cases in the industry, important goals, supporting technology, and fundamental applications) is categorized. Blockchain technology can improve the idea of DTs by ensuring openness, decentralized storage space, data integrity, and peerto-peer interaction in industrial sectors. The DTs play a critical role in preserving and managing Industry 4.0 since they assist in visualizing designs in three dimensions, conducting testing and simulations virtually before the construction of any physical component. Because DTs can be utilized for configuring, observing, diagnosing, and prognosticstating, it is anticipated that they will become more common in the future near.

A Systematic Literature Review on predictive maintenance, which is considered as a method for developing more sustainable, secure, and successful industry, is provided in Ref. [24]. Lack of failure data is one of the biggest obstacles to the development of predictive maintenance systems since machines are routinely repaired before they fail. The predictive maintenance algorithm can employ data from DT, which offers a depiction of the physical world equipment in real-time and creates information about asset depreciation. Since 2018, there has been an upsurge in the scholarly works on the use of DTs for condition-based preservation, signifying the need for an extensive investigation. To open the door for more research, this study gathers and synthesizes works that concentrate on predictive maintenance employing DTs.
