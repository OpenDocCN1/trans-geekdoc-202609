Combining Blockchain with Digital Twins has the potential to drastically improve a variety of industries. As a result, the two technologies improve the security and efficiency of industries that they use. They also assist businesses in widening their horizons.

This chapter discusses the overview of both Blockchain and Digital Twin technologies in terms of features, workflow, applications, and open-source frameworks. Open-source frameworks of Blockchain such as Hyperledger Fabric, Ethereum, Corda, Quorum, Solidity, Geth, Remix, Mist, Solium, Truffle, Parity, DApp Board, Embark, MyEtherWallet, etc., have been discussed. This chapter also discusses a few open-source tools of Digital Twin such as Ansys Digital Twin Builder, Ditto, Kafka, AWS Digital Twin, IoT TwinMaker, Twinbase, etc.

It emphasizes the benefits of a Blockchain-based Digital Twin model and explains the deep understanding of the building blocks of a Blockchainbased Digital Twin model. It emphasizes the conceptual framework of a Blockchainbased of a few opensource platforms like Dovetail, EtherTwin, SmartTwin, Remix, Solidity, TIBCO Cloud™ Live Apps, Watson IoT Platform, etc., for implementing a blockchain-based digital twin.

The integration of Blockchain and Digital Twins will provide a safe, cost-effective, and scalable solution that combines digital identification and data tracking via blockchain traceability. Blockchain reduces the cost of Digital Twins, allowing them to be built for a wider range of sectors.
