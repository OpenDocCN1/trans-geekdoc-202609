The purpose of digital twins is to continuously collect data that adds value to their interrelated physical twin and then analyze that data to generate actionable insights out of mountains of data. To achieve and obtain the value that has been promised for digital artifacts, however, there is a requirement for the highest possible level of security and transparency due to the rapid growth of digital assets and data everywhere. Therefore, the unique integration of digital twin and blockchain technologies is being affirmed to provide an effective method to alleviate any security holes and boost the confidence of users in the utilization of digital assets for their day-to-day transactions [3]. This assertion and presentation are being made in light of the fact that these technologies have recently been brought together for the first time.
