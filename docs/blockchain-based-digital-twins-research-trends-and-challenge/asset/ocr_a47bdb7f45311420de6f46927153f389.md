# 4.3.2 COMPONENTS OF BLOCKCHAIN

The key workings of Blockchain are as follows:

+   1. **Distributed Ledger Technology**: The system uses a shared and distributed ledger to record transactions one at a time in order to prevent duplicates.
2. **Immutable Records**: It prevents any data in the shared ledger from being transformed, changed, or tampered with.
