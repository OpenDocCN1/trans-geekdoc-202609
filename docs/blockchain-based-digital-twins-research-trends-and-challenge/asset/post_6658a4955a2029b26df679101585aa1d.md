## 1.1.3 ADVANTAGE OF AMALGAMATION OF DATA ANALYSIS, BLOCKCHAIN, DTS TECHNOLOGIES

- 1. Data Integrity: Blockchain technology guarantees data integrity and transparency by using encrypted data transfer, which also enables the traceability of transactions.
- 2. Data Security: DTs are stored as blocks in all nodes of a blockchain system, making it nearly impossible for hackers to manipulate or hack data without finding and compromising all instances of the DTs. Additionally, if any attempt to hack or modify data is made, it will be immediately visible to all other nodes, and the corrupted block will be rejected, ensuring that the blockchain system remains secure.
- 3. Real-Time Data Analytics and Predictions: The data and behavior of DTs can be analyzed for prediction of the behavior of the system, streamlining many processes.
- 4. Data Sharing: Enables project teams to avoid using data that has already been utilized, preventing duplication of data analytics.

The combination of DT with blockchain removes any loopholes and makes the system more trustworthy for users for their everyday operations.

## 1.2 APPLICATIONS OF AMALGAMATED DT AND BLOCKCHAIN

### 1.2.1 DT IN AUTOMOTIVE AND AEROSPACE

By creating a digital twin of a machine, it becomes possible to monitor its performance in real-time and collect data about its usage and maintenance history. This information is used to make predictions about the machine's remaining lifespan and the optimal time to replace its parts. This process is known as predictive maintenance and can help businesses save time and money by avoiding unexpected downtime and reducing maintenance costs.

By creating a digital twin of a physical object and collecting data on its behavior and performance over time, it becomes possible to predict when maintenance will be required, as well as to identify potential areas for improvement and optimization. This can lead to significant cost savings, improved efficiency, and increased lifespan for the physical object.

Digital twin technology is widely used in aircraft and vehicle management for various purposes such as fuel filling, tracking, weather prediction, weight monitoring, and detecting defects. By creating a virtual model of the aircraft or vehicle, digital twin technology can be used to monitor and analyze the performance of various components in real time and predict maintenance needs before they occur. This helps improve the efficiency and safety of aircraft and vehicles and can also save costs by avoiding unexpected downtime or repairs [16].

### 1.2.2 DTS AND BLOCKCHAIN IN INDUSTRY

Industry 4.0 technologies have revolutionized data collection in various industries, providing benefits and optimization. By implementing DT and IoT technologies, the supply chain can have access to more critical and secure data, mitigating the risk of trusting external parties with their Intellectual Property Rights (IPR). DT technology can be used to create a virtual model of a physical product before it is built. This model can be tested and improved in great detail, enabling the identification of any potential problems that may arise. By using this technology, businesses can identify and resolve issues before finalizing the product, ensuring a high-quality outcome [17].

DT technology enables product development teams to understand the impact of improving the product in later versions and anticipate the product output for future years. Creating a DT of a product on a blockchain network enables the storage of information about the product and its manufacturers. Manufacturing companies can then track their supply chain globally, which can help eliminate the theft of goods.

Counterfeit products pose a significant problem in today’s market, as they can harm a company’s property rights and affect stakeholders’ investments, brand value, and product reputation. Therefore, companies must take measures to ensure that the products they manufacture are not counterfeit. The combination of blockchain and DT can help overcome the problem of counterfeit products. By introducing DTs during product development, companies can predict the success and failure of the product. When this technology is integrated with blockchain, it ensures the product’s originality. Blockchain technology provides data transparency and enables the creation of digital certificates, while DT technology ensures the digitization of products and assets, providing a more secure and reliable system.

The amalgamation of blockchain and DTs can enhance the credibility of data and lead to more efficient and accurate decision-making in realtime applications such as logistics and the utility industry, resulting in improved productivity [18]. Today, almost all entities, as as homes, hotels, hospitals, and public and private communication systems, have undergone digital transformation. They generate vast amounts of data that are stored in the cloud and used to optimize their operations through the use of AI algorithms and robust optimization algorithms. As technology advances, consumers are becoming increasingly sophisticated and are seeking more to meet their individual needs. This trend is driving manufacturers to implement more customizable features in their products and become increasingly competitive in the marketplace [19].
