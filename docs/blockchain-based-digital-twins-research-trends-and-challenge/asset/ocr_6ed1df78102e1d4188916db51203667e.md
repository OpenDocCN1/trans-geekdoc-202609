The combination of blockchain, DTs, and AI has a significant impact on the management of urban cities. Blockchain technology can provide a secure and transparent system for storing and sharing data related to the city's infrastructure, while DTs can simulate and optimize the city's operations in real-time. AI can further enhance the system by analyzing the data and providing insights for better decision-making. The combination of technologies can improve various aspects of urban management, including transportation, energy management, waste management, and more.

By utilizing the data obtained from the IoT technology and blockchain, the transport department can gain insights into the usage patterns of private cars and can offer discounts or incentives to encourage people to use public transport. This can help reduce congestion and improve overall traffic management in urban areas. Additionally, it can also lead to a reduction in air pollution and carbon emissions, contributing to a cleaner and healthier environment.

With IoT, smart city operations can be simulated and with blockchain, it can be implemented securely [24]. With IoT, the city can be modeled virtually and many questions as discussed below can be answered through this virtual model:

- What can be done to reduce pollution?
