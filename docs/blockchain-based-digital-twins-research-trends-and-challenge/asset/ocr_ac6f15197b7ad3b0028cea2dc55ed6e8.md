Digital twin technology is widely used in aircraft and vehicle management for various purposes such as fuel filling, tracking, weather prediction, weight monitoring, and detecting defects. By creating a virtual model of the aircraft or vehicle, digital twin technology can be used to monitor and analyze the performance of various components in real time and predict maintenance needs before they occur. This helps improve the efficiency and safety of aircraft and vehicles and can also save costs by avoiding unexpected downtime or repairs [16].

Industry 4.0 technologies have revolutionized data collection in various industries, providing benefits and optimization. DT technology can be used to create a virtual model of a physical product before it is built. This model can be tested and improved in great detail, enabling the identification of any potential problems that may arise in all possible situations. By using this technology, businesses can identify and resolve issues before manufacturing or finalizing the product, ensuring a high-quality outcome [17].

DT technology enables product development teams to understand the impact of improving the product in its later versions and anticipate the product output for future years, thereby allowing for the provision and planning for its continued improvement. Creating a DT of a product on a blockchain network enables the storage of information about the product and its manufacturers.
