
Because the obtained data drives logical decisions, it is critical to establish the dependability of the data source. DTschain can play an important role in preventing device tampering by collecting information from registered devices.

- 2. **Immutable and Decentralized Data Transactions of DTs:** It is crucial to protect the DT's platform and data. Blockchain enhances security by introducing distributed ledgers that store DTs data that are hard to alter and uncontrollable by any central authority, as well as using cryptographic hashing methods. One of the unique features of blockchain is immutability, ensuring that the ledger remains unchanged and maintains the unalterable history of DTs transactions. This feature improves the effectiveness and efficiency of the DTS audit process.

- 3. **Identity and Legitimacy:** All product information is stored in an immutable record through blockchain-enabled DTs. Buyers can also check the transaction records to determine where a specific product was manufactured, who owned it previously, and other details. This simplifies establishing legitimacy and identifying DTs.

- 4. **Global DT Tracking with High Accuracy:** The blockchainbased generation of DTs can aid in their highly accurate global identification and tracking. Blockchain-based Digital Twin accesses distributed databases safely from several locations and it does not require the participation of a third party. Recordkeeping, provenance tracking, and auditability are simplified with blockchain-based DTs by the ease of access to and tracking of the DT' information. The usage of blockchain allows one to monitor the evolution of DTs from their genesis to the present. Such tracking features can be utilized to detect fraud in any DT component.

- 5. **Data Coordination with Trusted DTs and Access Privileges:** Most conventional access control methods are susceptible to hackers. However, blockchain reduces the risks of unauthorized access, which commonly leads to data change. All of the DTs’ information, including their policies and regulations, is managed in a distributed manner utilizing blockchain-based access control, allowing each DT to function independently. Furthermore, the secure sockets layer, which manages the data on blockchains, strengthens security in blockchain-based access control. DTs, on the other hand, often use IoT-based technology to interact and cooperate with their actual goods.

- **Transparency and Accountability:** Blockchain imposes transparency and accountability by utilizing encryption and control tools as smart contracts and chain codes. Each blockchain is allotted a public address and any authorized user can check these transactions. Furthermore, blockchain technology has user-defined control mechanisms that support accountability enforcement.

- Virtual space;
- Services;
- Data wrangler;
- Data synchronization;

The significance of each component is explained as follows [17]:

- 1. **Physical Space:** Through asset data, the physical part assists the virtual part.
- 2. **Virtual Space:** Through decision-making, and simulation, the virtual portion governs the actual portion.
- 3. **Services:** The service assists in the operation, evolution, and optimization of both virtual and physical areas. The data repository is the key source for data generation in digital twin (DT) and connects all components of the DT. Data-based digital twins (DTs) emphasize product design, manufacturing, production, operational control, and so on in the industrial realm. DTSs, for example, provide more exact predictions, logical judgments, and informed planning by enhancing communication between the design and manufacturing phases. A data-driven DT system performs fault diagnosis, finds the unreliable behavior of the actual object, and provides design principles for maintenance.
- 4. **Data Wrangler**: The data wrangler is in charge of removing missing invalid data and converting dissimilar data types into a similar one before entering them into real and virtual environments. During the car manufacturing process, data is received from a variety of physical sources like sensors and actuators, and it is fed as input into physical and virtual domains.
- 5. **Data Synchronization**: It performs a consistent digital-physical mapping between sensor data gathered from the production unit and inbuilt performance parameters obtained from the storage unit to ensure data consistency.
- 6. **Data Fusion**: It is the method of binding data from numerous sources to create a consistent interpretation for a certain object. For example, critical information such as order details, supply chain data, simulation data, and consistency checks are already available for usage in the repository before the commencement of the automobile production process. Furthermore, data wrangling and data fusion are critical for obtaining and merging data after the automotive manufacturing process. This information can be used by threat intelligence to research the enemy's domain and alter S&S standards for covert activities.
- 7. **Blockchain**: It secures data handling for all transactions. Transactions between nodes serve as the essential building blocks of communication in the blockchain and are maintained in a shared ledger as appendonly-timestamped records. A consensus technique is used to build a secure trustworthy network. A block of transactions is assembled and appended to the ledger using the consensus algorithm. In the Blockchain framework, the hash value of the block enforces immutability and makes tampering with a block and hence transactions easier to detect.
- 8. **Composability**: To integrate and communicate with them, the framework takes advantage of composability, which aggregates numerous replicas or DTs.
