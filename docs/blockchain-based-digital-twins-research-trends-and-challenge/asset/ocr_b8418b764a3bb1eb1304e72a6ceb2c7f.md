# 1.2.3.4 MANAGING WASTE

The use of blockchain and DT technology can greatly improve various services in a city, including waste management, water supply, air quality control, and park and garden management. By utilizing sensors and data analysis, DTs can provide real-time information about the status of city infrastructure and services. Blockchain can ensure the security and privacy of this data and allow for more efficient and transparent interactions between city authorities and citizens. This can lead to more sustainable and effective urban planning and management.

Figure 1.3 shows the interconnection of IoT devices with AI and other technologies to achieve the best smart city. The physical layer of a smart city infrastructure typically consists of various physical objects like sensors, actuators, and other devices that communicate through IoT protocols such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, LAN, and WAN [28]. Blockchain technology can be implemented in the software layer to provide a secure and decentralized way of managing data and transactions related to smart city services. Different blockchain software platforms such as Ethereum, NXT, and Hyperledger Fabric can be used for this purpose. Depending on the needs of the specific application, the blockchain implementation can be either public or private. The ultimate goal is to create a smart city that is more efficient, sustainable, and responsive to the needs of its citizens. Traffic management systems like RITM2 (SIMETRA), Traffic maps, Intelligent Roadway Information Systems, KITS, etc., were examined.
