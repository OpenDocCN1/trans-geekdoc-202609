The use of technology in healthcare facilities (such as hospitals and labs) should be increased to improve clinical procedures, digital simulations, and patient care in general. However, despite the growing usage of digital twins in the healthcare system, it is suggested that they will continue to be pricey and out of reach for most people. Digital twin technology will start to be seen as a perk available only to those with more financial means, leading to inequalities in the healthcare system.

Data Quality: Artificially intelligent digital twins can adapt from the available biological data, but they are constantly aware that the quality of the data may be low because it was gathered by for-profit organizations. As a result, it is difficult to analyze and portray such data. In the end, this has a severe impact on the models, which also has a bad impact on the model’s dependability throughout the diagnostic and treatment procedures.

3. Data Privacy: As a result of the deployment of digital twins, data on each individual will need to be gathered more often by healthcare and insurance companies. These health organizations progressively get a full awareness of an individual’s biological, genetic, physiological, and lifestyle-related information. Such unique data may be exploited for commercial objectives as opposed to people’s interests. One example would be an insurance company using the data to identify individuals with greater accuracy.

## 9.7 FUTURE OF DIGITAL TWIN

There is a significant shift occurring in current operating paradigms. Industries that strongly rely on physical assets are going through a disruptive digital transformation that necessitates a combined physical and digital perspective of all the tools, facilities, and operational processes that are accessible. Digital twins are a key element in such reconfiguration. Because more and more cognitive resources are being dedicated to their utilization, digital twins have nearly unlimited potential. Along with continuing to produce the insights required to improve goods and streamline operations, digital twins must continually develop new abilities.

Products, machinery, industries, structures, and communities are no longer only physical objects; they now have virtual analogs. Digital twins of persons exist as well. In the metaverse, users will encounter the next generation of the internet in real-time 3D, along with the interconnectedness of systems, gadgets, and people. The metaverse is creating an entirely new market having endless possibilities for interactive content in cross-digital and blended reality domains, whether they are used to manage facility renovations or customize a vehicle purchase. One such economy is 3D marketing.

Researchers are pleased to introduce people to their summit on the future of digital twins, which is taking place as the top trend in digital twins continues in 2018. System components, programming models, or digital twins are expected to represent billions of things within a few years. By 2020, it is anticipated that over 21 billion digitally connected sensors and 50% of big industrial enterprises will be using digital twins, which may result in billions of dollars in maintenance, repair, and operating savings. Additionally, it is anticipated that up to 60% of manufacturers will observe the performance of the system through the DTs and the quality of products by the year 2020.

Digital twins will be utilized up to 60% of international businesses to improve customer service. The proliferation of IoT-designed sensors is driving the employability of digital twins and the future of both is intertwined. The operation and reaction of an object to its environment may be revealed by sensors and the usage of digital twins can improve analysis, condition, and value. Digital twins are important, and this fact suggests that they will continue to expand and be used for billions of years.

## 9.8 CONCLUSION

The combination of real-time data collection and AI-powered analysis found in HDT technology moves healthcare one step closer to completely tailored therapy. The larger group of patients present in HDTs is utilized to simplify clinical trials and guide the users to select proper health policy in addition to individual patients. Data fusion accuracy, patient and provider literacy, remuneration, and governance of HDTs and associated secondary data will be crucial factors to take into account HDTs begin to permeate clinical workflows. Integration of digital twin technology, blockchain, and AI technologies deliver smart healthcare services to all human beings. Thus, digital twin technology was an essential tool in revolutionizing the healthcare Industry and its standards.
