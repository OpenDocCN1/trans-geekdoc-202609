Autonomous vehicles augmented with AI can potentially solve the problem of traffic congestion by enabling efficient, safe, and smart transportation. With the use of sensors, cameras, and advanced algorithms, autonomous vehicles can monitor and adapt to traffic patterns in real-time, avoiding traffic congestion by choosing the optimal route and speed [21]. Furthermore, autonomous vehicles communicate with each other and with traffic management systems, enabling smooth traffic flow and reducing the likelihood of accidents. This technology can also help reduce the number of vehicles on the road by enabling ridesharing and reducing the need for individual vehicle ownership.

DT technology can help urban and regional planners make better decisions by providing a virtual replica of the city or region. This DT can be used to simulate different scenarios and predict the outcome of different decisions. However, it is worth noting the widespread adoption of autonomous vehicles would require significant investment and infrastructure changes, as well as addressing issues related to liability, privacy, and cybersecurity [22].

The use of autonomous vehicles augmented with AI can greatly reduce the number of accidents caused by human errors. Technology has the potential to significantly reduce accidents on the roads. Autonomous vehicles can reduce the risk of accidents caused by human errors such as drunk driving, distracted driving, and tiredness. ## 1.2.3.2 MANAGING E-GOVERNANCE

Blockchain technology can help to eliminate intermediaries in digital transactions, making the process faster, more efficient, and more secure. This can reduce bureaucratic procedures and streamline interactions between government agencies and citizens. It can also reduce costs associated with intermediaries and intermediaries' errors, leading to greater transparency and trust in public services [25].

The combination of DTs and blockchain technology can bring transparency, security, and efficiency to urban city management services, including traffic management, public transportation, and governance. Blockchain technology can help facilitate secure and transparent data exchange between government and citizens, while DTs can provide an accurate and real-time simulation of the physical objects and infrastructure. This can help city development planners and authorities to make more informed decisions, optimize resources, and improve the quality of life for citizens [26].

## 1.2.3.3 MANAGING ELECTRIC SUPPLY

With the application of Blockchain and DT technology, households with solar panels can generate surplus electricity and share it with other households in the grid, creating a decentralized energy system. This can help reduce the overall demand for energy and promote sustainability. Blockchain technology can be used to track energy transactions between households, ensuring that the energy is distributed fairly and transparently. The DT technology can be used to simulate the energy demand and supply in the grid, helping households to better manage their energy usage and reduce waste [27].
