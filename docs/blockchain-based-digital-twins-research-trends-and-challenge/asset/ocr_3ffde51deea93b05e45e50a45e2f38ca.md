# 8.2.4 POTENTIAL OF INTEGRATION

The ability to save data from DTs and communicate with blockchain is a chief advantage of employing electronic twins by means of this technology. DTs can engage in blockchain networks with other related technologies to create blockchain technology that is built on interaction. This first demands that the data related to DTs be unchanging and then blockchain is crucial for protecting them. In order to confirm the data source, one option is to store data integration content in the blockchain rather than retaining it in a conventional system. The requirement for DT interaction underscores the significance of merging blockchain with DTs. So, the two core elements of blockchain technology are the immutable network and the consensus mechanism. This consensus mechanism should thus be executed in DTs. Additionally, machine learning may be employed to forecast and determine if events occurred in the automated systems.
