# 11.3.1 WORKING

In the proposed process, two places exist. One is the field area. In that, plants, crops, and some gadgets are available to obtain real-time data. The second part is the simulation center. In that, the current ongoing process can be viewed, along with previous actions and details of past data stored in a specific space. The future process of the crops can also be predicted. In simple terms, real-time input is collected from the field area, transmitted to the simulation center, processed, and stored as shown in Figure 11.4.
