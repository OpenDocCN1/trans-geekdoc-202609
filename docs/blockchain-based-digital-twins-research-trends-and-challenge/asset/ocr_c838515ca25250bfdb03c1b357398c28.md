- Step 2: The specific parameters are monitored, collected, and processed while mirrored in the logical space and physical space at the data layer.
- Step 3: The gathered data is transferred to the storage layer along with a complete family of data and actions.
- Step 4: Models are created at the twinned system and stored at the storage layer using data gather, domain expertise, and system history.
- Step 5: The agile feedback loop is completed when the application layer requests scheduling or model calibration services after continuously analyzing data to identify manufacturing issues.
