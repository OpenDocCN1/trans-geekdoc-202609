### 9.6.1 HOW CAN DIGITAL TWIN TECHNOLOGY APPLY TO THE MEDICAL FIELD?

A digital twin is an exact reproduction of the equipment, personnel, procedures, and systems used by enterprises. Using computer models, digital twins are used in healthcare systems to create digital representations of healthcare data, including hospital surroundings, lab findings, human physiology, etc. Virtual twins are created using information about the person, population features, and environment.

### 9.6.2 ROLE OF THE DIGITAL TWIN IN HOSPITAL

Digital twin technology is used to build a virtual twin of hospitals. Operational strategies, allocation of resources, staffing, and healthcare service models may be studied to identify areas for improvement, foresee future challenges, and fortify organizational plans. As a result, hospital digital twins may be utilized to create facility copies, which in turn allows for:

- **Resource Optimization:** Hospital administration is better able to spot bed shortages, organize staff schedules, and manage spaces by integrating history and current data on hospital operations as well as the surrounding region (such as car accidents, COVID-19 cases, etc.) to develop digital twins.

- **Risk Management:** To make data-driven strategy choices in a delicate and challenging context, digital twins provide a safe environment to assess changes in system performance (staffing levels, operating theater vacancies, device maintenance, etc.).

### 9.6.3 DIGITAL TWIN OF HUMAN BODY

Digital twins are also employed to imitate organs and single cells in addition to a person's genetic makeup, physiological features, and lifestyle decisions to produce tailored drug and treatment plans. By using such models of internal organ systems, medical treatment and patient care are enhanced.

- **Personalized Diagnosis:** Vital information (such as oxygen levels, blood pressure, etc.) may be collected and used at the individual level by using digital twins. This helps users track persistent diseases and, as a result, overall priorities and contacts with doctors by supplying basic knowledge. Thus, clinical trials and laboratory research data are built on such personalized data.

To choose the optimal course of therapy, therapists avoid utilizing large samples by concentrating on each patient individually. Instead, track how each patient responds to various therapies using individualized simulations, which increases the accuracy of the whole treatment plan. Despite the interest in growing volume of attempts for personalized treatment, there are presently no uses for digital twins for actual patients.

- 2. **Treatment Planning:** Using sophisticated models of the human body, clinicians may find pathology before diseases become noticeable, explore remedies, and enhance surgical readiness.

#### 9.6.3.1 EMPLOYING DIGITAL TWINS TO DESIGN NEW DRUGS AND DEVICES

The creation, testing, and oversight of novel pharmaceuticals and medical equipment can be enhanced with the use of digital twins. For instance:

- 1. **Medications:** Using digital twins of chemical compounds and researchers may redesign or change drugs while taking into account particle size and composition properties to enhance delivery effectiveness.

- 2. **Medical Devices:** Developers can examine a product's functions or features, alter its composition or design, and then evaluate the effects of changes in a virtual setting before the product is produced. This reduces the costs related to failures while significantly enhancing the performance as well as the safety of the end product.
