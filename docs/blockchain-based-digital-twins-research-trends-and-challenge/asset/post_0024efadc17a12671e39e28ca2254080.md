# ABSTRACT

The digital twin (DT) of diagnostic patients is created using augmented reality (AR) and maintained if a patient is facing emergency. After a physical object is operational, DT can be used for configuration, monitoring, diagnostics and prognostics. The suggested approach also creates decentralized storage space to store, retrieve and share. Here, DT is shared using Ethereum blockchain Contract Account to secure data from third parties, and it is only accessible after the encryption process by the end user. As data in the medical field is hermetic, the Double Encryption Standard (DAES) algorithm is introduced to create a strong ciphertext. The highly privileged medical ledger is transferred in a regionalized manner, and it assists in saving many diagnostic patients.

## 6.1 INTRODUCTION

Digital twins in a wide range of industries, including business and industry, have noted significant developments in the area of digital twin. With genuine data produced by real assets, it is feasible to recreate occurrences in a virtual scene.

One of the key challenges is the need for accurate and up-to-date data. In many cases, the data used to create the digital twin is inaccurate or out-of-date. This can lead to inaccurate results from the simulation. Another difficulty of digital twins has the potential to transform how we do business. By replicating real-world events in a virtual scenario, we can gain valuable insights that would otherwise be unavailable. However, the implementation of digital twin technology is not without its challenges. In this chapter, we will explore some of the difficulties involved with using digital twins.
