The consensus algorithm may be termed the heart of any blockchain environment, also known as a crucial component of the blockchain network. Each and every blockchain network needs a protocol or rule required to be defined, which can bind participants or network members together who are involved in the blockchain network. So, when a centralized authority is missing in any network, we find another alternative to a central authority. Consensus algorithm acts as that term in the blockchain. The consensus algorithm answers the following questions in the blockchain network.

+   - Who can create the transaction?
- How is the transaction validated?
- How are transactions approved?
- How are blocks generated?
- How would consensus be achieved?
- How can multiple network participants approve or agree to a specific fact/rule?

Specific rules and regulations are required if we want no one to derail the system. These rules and regulations will take care of everything. There is no dependency on anyone. Everyone has to follow a group of rules/scenarios. How consensus achieves this without a central authority. The network behaves smoothly with an extensive blockchain network.
