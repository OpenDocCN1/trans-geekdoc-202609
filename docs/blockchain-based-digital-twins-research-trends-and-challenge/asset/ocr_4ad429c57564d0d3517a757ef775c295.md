HDT systems create virtual twins using deep phenotyping, the automated analysis and fusion of distributed data gathered from biological data, patient records, and portable sensors. The patient reaction to the medicine, behavior modification, and environmental variables may be measured and then anticipated using the physical twin data.

Prediction analysis for real-time applications introduces additional chances for accurate prevention and treatment systems, such as the identification and severity grading of abdominal aortic aneurysms.
