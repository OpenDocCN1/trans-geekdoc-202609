# 5.3 DIGITAL TWIN DESIGN

In order to complete the first stage of the process of developing a digital twin, it is important to model the static characteristics of the system. The purpose of this design is to determine the parameters and requirements of the system, including its functions and the breakdown of those functions. It is important that the modeling of data exchanges and communication, as well as the structures and reasonable layout of the system, as well as the conceptual prerequisites for putting the remedy into practice, which include both the software and hardware components, be taken into consideration.

In most cases, the Virtual Object (VO) is developed so that it can function as a reflection of the Physical Object (PO). As a consequence of this, it is possible for both components to coexist for the duration of the digital twin's existence.
