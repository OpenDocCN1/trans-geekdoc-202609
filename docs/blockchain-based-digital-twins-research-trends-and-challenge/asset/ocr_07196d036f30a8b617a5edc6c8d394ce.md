For the predictive maintenance of physical equipment, the health status of individual machine subparts needs to be regularly monitored. Hence, the data of vital parameters which describe the current state of machine parts should be monitored. In the present time, most machines are constructed by assembling various parts manufactured by different industries. Hence, predictive maintenance seeks the involvement of different organizations, and it is required to ensure integrity and transparency in the parameter values to be shared among them.

In Blockchain, the blocks that carry the real data are stockpiled in a distributed manner [5]. Basically, the chain of blocks is wareoused in a distributed peer-to-peer network. Each block also contains the hash of its data value and the hash of the previous block. The cryptographic nature of this hash ensures the genuineness, transparency, and integrity of the data. The verification process adopted by the blockchain to permit the addition of new blocks in the chains create trust among the organizers who share the data. Also, the consensus algorithm adopted in blockchain ensures that the data cannot be modified without the consensus of all the stakeholders or nodes in the network. This very basic characteristic of blockchain makes it suitable for DT-based predictive maintenance of the equipment that is assembled using parts manufactured by different vendors.
