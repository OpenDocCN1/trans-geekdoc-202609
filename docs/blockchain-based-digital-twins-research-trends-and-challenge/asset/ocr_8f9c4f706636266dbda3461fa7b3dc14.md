# ### 2.1.5 LOSS OF PRIVACY

A significant amount of privacy is protected in blockchain using public key cryptography. For each transaction, authentication is maintained to protect the user identification. In contrast, the ledger collects the data and enables transactional security publicly for providing transactional anonymity because all transaction identifiers and balances for each cryptographic key are made available to the public. Still, data privacy works against privacy policies and procedures. Therefore, blockchain is more suitable for various fields internationally, even though it has not yet achieved its maximum development for improving privacy.

# ### 2.2.1 ELECTRONIC HEALTH RECORDS

Electronic Health Records digitally store health information, a revolutionary invention in the 20th century. In networks and communication, it offers distributed and decentralized networks among all the nodes without central authority by improving health record management. It provides privacy, security, and accessibility to distributed P2P networks and does not depend on a third entity. In distributed P2P networks, the digital data is allocated to all the users securely, publicly, or privately.

In conventional methods, both the sender and receiver acknowledge the transaction details depending on the trusted third party, which leads to few security trust issues and operational privacy with the lengthy transaction. But millions of data in blockchain are highly beneficial for healthcare providers, but the open research challenge for researchers is to fix the data breaches and external attackers creating security issues while transmitting the information. Therefore, monitoring and providing suggestions for improving verification and validation is essential.
