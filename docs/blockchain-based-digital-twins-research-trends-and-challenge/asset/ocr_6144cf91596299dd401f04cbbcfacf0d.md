# 10.3.3.2 DIGITAL TWIN BLOCKCHAIN

The DTs on each physical part of real-time machines based on Blockchain are made up of a blockchain network of interconnected DTs. It is a network of multiple DTs connected through the blockchain. The DTBC ledger stores information about the status of all virtual entities of DT, the owner of DT, the recording time at which status is being recorded, the synchronous time at which the DT is being synchronized with its physical counterpart, and data being exchanged between the DTs.
