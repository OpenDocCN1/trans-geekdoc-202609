# 4.3 INTRODUCTION TO BLOCKCHAIN

Blockchain technology is a distributed, immutable database that monitors asset and business transaction processes. A material asset, such as a home, money, vehicle, or plot, or an intangible asset, such as a patent, intellectual property, copyrights, and brand awareness, can be monitored by a blockchain network. This network can record and trade almost anything of value, reducing risk and lowering costs for parties during transactions.

In other words, Blockchain preserves data and information in a digital format without the need for a central authority. Data is gathered from numerous sources and organized into blocks on a Blockchain. Additionally, after they are full, these blocks link with subsequent blocks after including the sets of information. Thus, this network of data links and chains is referred to as a blockchain. Additionally, it stores the data and uses cryptography to link the blocks together.
