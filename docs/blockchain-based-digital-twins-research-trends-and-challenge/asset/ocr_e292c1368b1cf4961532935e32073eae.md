# 10.1.1 BLOCKCHAIN FOR PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE

Smart predictive maintenance relies on the real-time data collected from industrial machines to learn predictive models. The decentralized and immutable characteristics of blockchain technology draw the attention of researchers/industries and are considered to an optimal solution for safeguarding the sensitive information collected from the industry in real-time training for the predictive maintenance model. Blockchain technology can minimize cybersecurity risks [4].
