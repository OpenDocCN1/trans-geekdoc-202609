Proponents of blockchain implementation in e-voting models argue that the cryptographic properties of the network can maintain the secrecy of individual voters. Through the use of a hashing technique to generate unique identifiers for each user and register them on the blockchain, double voting can be prevented. A model that ensures convenience through a web-based interface that requires minimum input from the user was proposed [39].
