#  10.3.4 CONSTRUCTION OF DEEP LEARNING MODEL

To model the temporal relationship of sequential data collected from realtime IoT-enabled machines and its digital counterpart is the collections of DTs, the RNN-based learning model is considered for the proposed framework. To predict the failures and recognize the patterns that require timely maintenance, it is required to model the longer-time dependencies from the data that describe the status of machine parts collected at different periods. To predict the abnormality in the operational behavior of machines, it is needed to identify the difference between expected target characteristics and the current behaviors of the machine.

This is supported in the proposed framework through the two kinds of blockchain RMBC and DTBC. The DTBC provides the expected behavior of the machine.
