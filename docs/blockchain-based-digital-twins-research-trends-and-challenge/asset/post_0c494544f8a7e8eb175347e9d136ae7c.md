## 2.8.1.5 LACK OF PROFESSIONAL TALENT

One of the most quickly evolving technologies in the modern era is the combination of blockchain and AI. It takes highly qualified individuals who are familiar with intricate AI, cryptography, and advanced mathematics techniques to develop blockchain-based fraud detection apps. At the moment, only a small number of researchers and experts are focused on developing robust and adaptable blockchain and AI-based systems. Due to a lack of qualified personnel, implementing fraud detection in AI and blockchain will be complicated.

## 2.8.1.6 IMPROVING SMART CONTRACT DEVELOPMENT

Executable Solidity programs known as “smart contracts” are loaded on Ethereum Virtual Machines and used to regulate behavior under agreements. Because smart contracts cannot be changed after they are distributed in the blockchain network, it is necessary to fix the software fault before it is done. To apply the smart contract effectively and reliably, research solutions should be reinforced.

## 2.9 CONCLUSION

Due to the involvement of several interconnected parties and ongoing complex processes and challenges in digital twins, cost management, risk assessment, and efficiency improvement need to be the focus. To analyze, anticipate, and optimize all processes before real-world implementation, one of the potential options to maximize efficiency is to develop a digital twin that mirrors every aspect of the underlying system or process. Furthermore, the research community must look into the research and deployment challenges. Digital twins are created by integrating AI and blockchain technologies to provide predictive maintenance and technological intelligence.
