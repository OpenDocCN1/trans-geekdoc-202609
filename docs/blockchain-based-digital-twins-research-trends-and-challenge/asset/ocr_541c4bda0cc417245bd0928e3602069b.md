# 9.4 TYPES OF DIGITAL TWINS

Digital twins may be many different things, based on how much the technology has been improved. The primary area of operation where these twins diverge has emerged. It is typical for many kinds of digital twins to work together in a system or process:

+   1. Component Twins/Parts Twins: A component twin, which is the most compact representation of an operational component, is the essential building block of a digital twin. Twin components are almost identical yet relate to far less important elements.
2. Process Twins: The macro-level of magnification used by the process twins shows several systems work together to build a full manufacturing complex. Using process twins, it may be possible to identify the precise temporal patterns that eventually affect overall efficacy.
3. Asset Twins: Any time two or more things function successfully, an asset is produced. Asset twins allow researchers to study the interactions between these factors. It produces a large amount of operational data that may be analyzed to yield valuable insights.
