Flaws (leads to vulnerability) noted in the BT devices are: (i) When Bluetooth low energy devices (like a fitness tracker, smart thermostat, smart speaker, or smart home assistant) is primarily paired to a mobile app, it broadcasts a UUID – a universally unique identifier – to permit the corresponding apps on the phone to identify the Bluetooth device, creating a link that allows the phone and device to talk to one another.
