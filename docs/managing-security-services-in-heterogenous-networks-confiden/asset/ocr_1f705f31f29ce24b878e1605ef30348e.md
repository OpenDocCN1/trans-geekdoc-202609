# 4.4 Conclusion and Future Work

Consensus algorithms are used for the systems in a distributed environment to reach a collective agreement. There cannot be any distributed or decentralized system without a consensus mechanism implemented to reach an agreement. The nodes within a network may not trust each other, but they have to comply with the consensus algorithms to decide on a common goal. Blockchain may build blocks from innumerable transactions, forming a database, but they would not be decentralized in nature without the consensus algorithms. The famous POW consensus mechanism is also not free from criticism of its computational and resource-intensive process. Our new mechanism, proof of virtue, will overcome these drawbacks at the same time and is resistant to 51% of Sybil attacks and any kind of malfunctioning. Though the mechanism has been carefully devised, intensive testing needs to be done – this will be a scope for future work.
