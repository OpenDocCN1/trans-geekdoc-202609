# 12.2.1 Fourth Generation Long-Term Evolution (4G-LTE)

4G LTE is for mobile devices and consists of many advancements and features compared to 3G and earlier LTE, giving speeds and power they need to function properly. It has only that great integration in metropolitan areas. To overcome this, an ingenious idea of combining 3G and 4G LTE was engineered to meet these requirements:

+   - Highly advanced bandwidth and data transfer speeds up to 1 Gbps
- Faster responses (shorter round-trip delay)
- Enhanced quality of service controls
- Lower mobility implementation cost due to simple and architecture
- Orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (OFDM) for downlink
- Single carrier frequency division multiple access (SC-FDMA) for uplink
- Multi-input, multi-output (MIMO) for enhanced throughput
- Reduced power consumption
- Higher efficiency (RF) amplifier efficiency (less battery power used by handsets)
- Increased ARPU (annual revenue per unit)

5G is not what we have seen so far. It is set to revolutionize the telecommunications industry, and not only that, it will be for other systems that need higher speeds, unlimited to the broadband world. Its primary goal is to trailblaze for networks and internet connections. It has features such as:

+   - Seamless seamless connections for great cloud deployments
- Improved signal efficiency
- Significantly lower latency compared to 4G
- Enhanced spectral reliability and efficiency
- Improvements to browse, download, and upload data files from any place to anywhere
- Reduced network energy usage, which will, in turn, increase the battery life of the device
- Increase in the user's density over the unit area times, which will help users use high bandwidth for a longer period
- Improved internet of things (IoT), machine-to-machine communication, and device-to-device communication
- Increased object-oriented works and data management

Through the deployment of 5G technology, users will develop frameworks to utilize a machine-to-machine communication system. The onset of this architecture will increase business and benefit consumer demands. It will always establish these characteristics:

+   - Improved reliability and security: With the onset of things like cars and planes connected via 5G, the need for reliability and security is obvious.
- Traffic prioritization: Within dense populations, calling for an emergency will take priority over users.
- Internet of things: A major point is out, and seamless communication among various devices is a part of 5G.
- Radio interoperability performance: An enhancement over the existing 4G radio will provide capabilities that manage single physical cases.

It is blatant that 5G brings so much to the table, at the same time, will bring its own set of new challenges to overcome. However, that's exactly what we need to advance and succeed.
