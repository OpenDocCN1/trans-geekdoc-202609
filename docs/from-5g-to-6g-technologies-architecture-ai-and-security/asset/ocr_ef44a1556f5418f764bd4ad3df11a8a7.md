# 2.13 3GPP

Without the combined intelligence of the 3GPP organization, cellular services would not be what they are today. The 3GPP was established in 1998 and is an organizational grouping of telecommunication standard development organizations. 3GPP is not to be confused with 3PP2, which is an organization for standards on the competing technology of CDMA.
