# 6.1 Introduction

5G service is available in North America and East Asia, and new launches are still being released in Europe. By 2022, we should see the final launches of 5G in those areas. Interestingly, China actually accounts for over half of the world's 5G connection. In 2021, nearly a thousand new 5G devices were announced, including phones, modules, modems, tablets, and many other devices. That makes one fact as to the future of these devices, as these will not be fully functional to result in new markets - it will be a few years until we reach its full potential of balancing broadband operators with growth into new markets.

As noted in Chapter 1, 5G has gone through two releases, Rel-15 and Rel-16. The 5G technology is assumed to result in a high data rate that can enhance mobile broadband, ultra-reliable, and low latency services and many connections (including beam management), channel structure, and multi-antennas, which partially fulfill the requirement of performance of International Mobile Telecommunication (IMT-2020).

In order to support the performance requirements of IMT-2020, several new technologies such as orthogonal multiple access, ultrareliable, and low latency communication, vehicle to X communication, integrated access, backhaul, terminal saving, and positioning have been introduced, which can also help in widening the use of 5G networks. Unlike 1G, 2G, 3G, as well as 4G, the 5G communication applications possess diverse abilities, including three main features: high Gbps speed of the mobile broadband, billions of connections of machine type communication, and 99.9% of the microsecond delay of ultra-low latency transmission to meet society's demands in the coming decade.

The 5G network is expected to have a massive impact on the life of humans, the global economy, and society. The communication network is expected to be on its evolutionary path after 2020 for the maximum optimization of the features and expand deployment scenario as the next-terrestrial network (NTN), aerial vehicle, and what is expected to increase the operating band up to ~114 GHz.
