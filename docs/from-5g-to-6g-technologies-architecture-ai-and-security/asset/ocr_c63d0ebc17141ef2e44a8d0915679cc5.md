# 4.3.4 6G Era's Edge Intelligence and Cloudification

Since there is a strong relationship between AI and computing, it is natural to combine the two. In 6G wireless applications, it is critical to move processing to the edge. The deployment of AI/ML algorithms to acquire, store, or process data at the edge is edge intelligence (EI). In an edge server collects data from many network devices. Data is shared around numerous edges for training, which are evaluated and forecasted, allowing devices to benefit from quicker feedback, reduce latency, and lower costs while enhancing their operations. However, data is collected from various sources, AI/ML algorithms' outcomes become data-dependent [7]

According to Zhang et al., EI is subject to a range of security vulnerabilities since data is collected from some sources and the outcomes of AI/ML algorithms are mostly data-dependent. In user identification, account control and data integrity, platform validation will require all trust in EI. It is demonstrated that Blockchain may be used to protect edge devices and secure resources from multiple nodes. Blockchain ensures the consistency of constructed tasks and bits of learning data required for AI implementation. Edge computing is distributed by many nodes making it harder to carry out attacks such as data theft.

Hackers might exploit the dispersed nature of edge computing and its corresponding dependencies to execute attacks such as data poisoning, data evasion, or a privacy attack, damaging the AI/ML algorithms' results and undermining the benefits of EI. Furthermore, edge service may necessitate secure routing algorithms and trust architectures. Edge devices may carry sensitive data like a user's location, health records, or manufacturing information, other things, security and privacy are tightly linked in EI.

In edge models, federated learning is one way to enable local models with privacy-driven data training. Experts are also using safe computation and homomorphic encryption to build privacy-preserving AI framework configuration in relations. 6G will be the main communication technology for intelligent healthcare services in the future. As a result, ensuring secure communication, device authentication, and access for billions of IoT and wearable devices will be major challenges in the 6G era. Privacy and protection of the ethical components of user data or electronic health records will be critical challenges in the healthcare system.
