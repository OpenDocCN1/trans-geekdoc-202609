# 1.5 Threats from Hackers

One of the biggest problems in the cybersecurity world is hackers. Many people do not realize how broad the term hacker can be. There are three different types of hackers. Black-hat hackers are hackers who break laws, spread viruses, and steal information. They are often portrayed on TV and in the media, breaking into people's networks. You have gray-hat hackers, who sometimes venture over the dark side, but they are kind of neutral [23].
