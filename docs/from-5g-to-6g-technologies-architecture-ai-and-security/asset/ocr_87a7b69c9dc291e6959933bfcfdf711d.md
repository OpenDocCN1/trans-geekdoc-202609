# **6.2.1** Use Cases, Requirements, and Metrics

The current generation of technology, 5G, is an important step in the progression and evolution of technology. It boasts upwards of 25 Mbps data rates, supports VPNs, and is the testbed for the viability of a large, pervasive mobile network, which does not come without its own problems. It is not cheap to implement, being one of the more expensive ways to build out a mobile network. Security is poor getting better, and is still actively researched for its viability.

6G, on the other hand, may be the next milestone in human civilization and technology as a boast. A booming-whipping 10 Gbps data rate, as well as being cheap to implement, and massive, and 6G could literally change the world. Communications from last space would become faster and easier to maintain. Energy could be transmitted wirelessly, which would allow for lower price of energy sources outside of the Earth. For for homes and businesses could be improved several times with new, lightning-fast response and real-time monitoring of resources. 6G could be the biggest leap in technology yet; however it has many problems. With security, privacy and data ownership would need to be a big talking point before implementation. Security around these links would need to be improved several times before it becomes viable. Through planning and research will have to be done before any of these things get implemented, but I believe that this technology could be the turning point for the technological world.
