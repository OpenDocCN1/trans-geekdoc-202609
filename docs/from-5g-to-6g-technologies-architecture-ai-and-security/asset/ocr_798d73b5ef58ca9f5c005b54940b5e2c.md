# 6.3 6th Generation Networks: A Step Beyond 5G

Even though 5G are still in the deployment phase, there are increasing discussions surrounding 6G networks. The increasing innovations of data-intensive applications such as brain-computer interface (BCI), flying vehicles, e-healthcare, and extended reality shape the need for 6G networks. A plethora of research argues that 6G revolutionize the evolution of networks from “connected” to “connected intelligence” (A research project conducted by the University of Oxford on the extension of the 5G ecosystem and innovation of 6G). The resulting “connected intelligence” will wisely adapt and control key performance indicators (KPIs). Thus, 6G vision’s summary will be primarily focused on achieving “ubiquitous wireless intelligence” [14] by possessing multiple and new aspects of network requirements such as technologies, resources, and operational improvement.
