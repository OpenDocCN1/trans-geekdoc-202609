# 5.3 Enabling Technologies the Drive for 6G

Originally, 6G networks were primarily built upon the 5G framework, acquiring the gains in 5G, given the past progress of mobile networks. Various technological (Figure 5.2) advances will be introduced, while others from 5G will be upgraded to 6G. As a result, variety of technologies would drive the 6G infrastructure.

# 5.3.1 Artificial Intelligence

The basic property of 6G autonomous networks is intelligence. As a result, AI is the most important and recently launched technology for 6G communications networks. Partially or restricted AI will be supported by the next 5G communications. Nevertheless, AI will significantly assist 6G in automation [6]. The entire capacity of radio communications will be realized with AI-powered 6G, allowing the transition from smart radio to smart radio. Increasing smart systems for real-time connectivity in 6G are being born thanks to advances in machine learning (ML). The use of artificial intelligence in communication will make the transmission of real-time data faster and more efficient.

AI can determine how truthful right job is accomplished using a variety of statistics. AI boosts productivity and minimizes the time it takes for communication stages to processes. AI would be used to quickly time-consuming processes like transmission and connectivity service.M2M, machine-human, and human-to-machine (H2M) communications will benefit from AI. AI will also enable BCI interaction. Nanostructures, smart structures, intuitive networking, communication technologies, adaptive cognitive radio, wireless systems, and deep learning will be used to enable ML communication systems. AI-based encoder-decoders, deep neural networks for channel state prediction, and automatic modulation categorization are used in the physical layer. Deep learning-based allocation of resources and smart traffic modeling have been intensively investigated to meet the 6G needs for the data link layer and the transport layer [6].
