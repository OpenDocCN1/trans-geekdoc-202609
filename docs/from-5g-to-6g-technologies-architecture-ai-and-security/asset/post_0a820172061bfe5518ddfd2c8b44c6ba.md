## 8.12 Conclusion

It is easy to be caught in the ideas of what 6G can do, and how it might be better than the generations of the past, and what the bar is set for future generations. When thinking of how great it can be, however, it is important to keep in mind some challenges and flaws that may occur. There is nothing wrong with being caught in the bigger picture but thinking about the smaller things is important for all of us.

The need for wireless communications grows every day, as was expected during the COVID-19 pandemic. Now that the pandemic is over, there is no turning back. Many jobs are now virtual. People are recognizing that things they thought to be in person can be done more effectively virtually. Holograms and multisensory communications are the next steps. This will allow for better virtual communication as people communicate virtually. It will make people more connected virtually. Doctors could perform surgeries or technicians perform repairs. Holograms would require a large amount of data to be transmitted. The transmission rate is expected to be 4-2 Tbps. This is something that 6G is not capable of providing. Virtual communications using five senses would change the world significantly.

The development of 6G will be very important to help implement these technologies. Holograms are one of the technologies 6G could require massive bandwidths and will need ultra-low latency to deliver a good experience. We will feel simulator sickness if the latency is too high. Synchronization is too important for this technology. This is important to deliver the arrival of packets. It is important to have the knowledge of flows. Security is an important part of all technology and will become even more important as technologies like holograms are implemented. For some cases, ensuring CIA becomes essential if someone's face could be faked for the person. To avoid potential lawsuits for the hospital, before anything else security and the network will have to be perfect. Another challenge is securing the security of multiple flows as an attack could affect all the others. This will make cybersecurity and network professionals essential, as the demand and need will only grow. Resilience, availability, and reliability are all very important parts of holographic communications. Holographic technologies will take up a large number of computer resources, so computation is a big part. Balancing computation, latency, compression, and bandwidth will be the big challenge to get holographic communications to work properly. Tactile and haptic communications are another development for robotic automation, autonomous driving, and healthcare. The latency will need to be 1 ms as it takes 1 ms for a human to receive tactile signals. Data rates need to be 1 Gbps. Synchronization is also important due to the reaction times of the human brain. Security will be a forethought for these applications as it will be for many other things in the future. Networks should be able to prioritize traffic based on how critical they are. Reliability will be very important depending on the application, it could need to be 99.999% reliable.
