# 3.4 The Impact of 6G on Healthcare Systems

6G technology will dramatically change the healthcare industry by creating an intelligent healthcare system. There are a plethora of applications in the field in which 6G technologies can improve or make them possible at all. Telehealth touches, telesurgery, telehealth, and more once 6G is available. It will improve both the quality of service and the quality of patient experience. Figure 3.6 shows the possibilities of an intelligent healthcare system.

One major benefit of 6G technology will be development of intelligent devices that can care patients. The devices would monitor body functions such as heartbeat, blood pressure, and other health conditions [10]. Basic tests could be quickly sent to a doctor or nurse without going from home to a clinic, and AI and ML could be utilized to advise the patient on what to do during minor health and body issues/events. Such devices as these will be especially helpful for elderly patients and those who are immunocompromised. It will lessen their need to be around large amounts of sick people in a hospital or clinic setting. Outside of the immunocompromised, intelligent wearable devices would allow everyone to have fewer hospital/clinic visits and lower their healthcare costs.
