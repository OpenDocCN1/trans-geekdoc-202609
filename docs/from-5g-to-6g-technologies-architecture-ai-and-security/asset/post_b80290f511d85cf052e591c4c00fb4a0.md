## 8.2 The State of 5G Networks

5G networks have been in the works for years and the development stage is gradually coming its end. Once 5G networks are perfected, it will bring very high performance increase from the fourth generation. The emergence of these 5G networks is also going to bring new network technology combined with other technologies for applications and also realize the great vision of the Internet of Things (IoT) [1]. The state of 5G networks in 2022 is actually complex. It is growing as many countries gain access to it, but it is actually slowing down. So as connectivity for 5G increases, its performance worldwide will be diminishing: not many countries experiencing “true” 5G at all. There has even been a study on how it is slowing down; Over the past year from 2020 to 2021, the global 5G download speed fell to 166.3Mbps, down from 206.2Mbps in Q3 2020. When it is growing and slowing down, there are many countries that are unable to use 5G networks. Out of all countries, the US is the country with the best 5G availability, followed by China with a download speed of 50.83Mbps and 53.08Mbps. See Figure 8.1. In the US, the three major providers have some form of a high-speed standard network. Using these methods, providers are able to get much better throughput and data rates.

Looking at these factors the problem with 5G, such as low latency and high return and slowing down over time, it is important to look at the solutions to these problems and the key technologies that will be needed. To optimize deployment-5G networks and applications can be improved by synchronizing network-side applications and deployment to the RAN and UE (user equipment). With more power control, deployment of 5G networks will be more flexible, and better the metrics are achieved, the better will be accessible HD video, UHD video, and more. A lot of services that are high-bandwidth and caching, can handle bandwidth and less delays, improving the quality of services (QoS) of the whole network. Those with low-latency will allow administrators to configure the network to three parties to quickly deploy applications and services for mobile users, enterprises and virtual industries.

5G has had plenty of time, as it has been upon the 4G architecture, and the key technologies have been decided for it. One major key technology that would be good for 5G would be the use of the Internet of Things. The internet of things is a collective network of many devices that gather data and use them for different reasons. Another key technology for 5G is the radio millimeter waves. Using the five networks will also need to be a big change in mobile networks that they can connect super-high traffic while also being extremely mobile. There will also be a few basic models for 5G of their development, and there are many 5G networks already deployed but are far from perfect. Figure 8.1 is a picture of one of the basics.

In 2021 there was a decent amount of progress towards the deployment of 5G networks, and one big part of it is global standardization. Many groups worked on the standardization of the structure, elements, gateways, configurations, etc. Overall 2021 was a great year for the deployment of 5G and the development of key technologies for it.

## 8.2 Applications and Services of 5G

5G technologies are no doubt more powerful than the fourth generation, and with these new speeds will come applications and services that power a wide range of industries from retail to education to smart homes to healthcare. These new applications and services will affect the quality of service for many people. There is a lot of expected revenue from these technologies and services. For example, the COVID-19 pandemic exposed the value of fast wireless communications. With offices closed and schools in lockdown, a tiny company called Zoom saw its stock price soar, and other companies were fast to jump on board. Rather than drive to an office or to conference hours, people met online. Students received virtual instruction – many districts showed gaps in learning environments, technological access. Now that the pandemic is over, there is no turning back. One of the most recent reveals was that if 5G were generic with multiple aspects of life, we will need ubiquitous high-speed mobile networks. 5G networks are already showing that they are very innovative and fast. Ultra-low latency with high-speed internet can be used in a lot of applications like self-driving cars and the like. With 5G networks, there can be latencies as low as a millisecond. 5G networks are supposed to be reliable, and with 5G downloads will be faster and faster. Another service that will be provided by 5G networks is secure to cloud applications. This means we can do more tasks at higher speeds, making the use of data for many different tasks. Speaking of 5G, 5G networks will provide new opportunities for hardware and software developers, which in turn will create more job opportunities for people. For example, 5G services are also supposed to include better cell coverage, maximum data transfer and low power consumption, etc.

Entertainment and media are big factors for 5G networks. Most of the traffic used is to stream high-definition video. Since the fifth-generation networks are a good deal better than the fourth, they will allow high-definition video. It will be able to stream HD video at high speed, which will improve the QoS. This will also be for a different number of operations from using their phones to watch HD video to large corporations being able to stream high definition with high resolution and dynamic ranges of video streaming with little to no internet.

One huge aspect when dealing with networks is augmented reality and virtual reality. They usually require resource-heavy HD videos and low latency, which is what 5G networks are able to provide with performance. Video games will also be more complex, as there will be able to hold all of their data and have high-speed internet for online play.

The internet of IoT is another aspect that 5G services are able to provide, at least partially. The IoT is a network that would need a powerful network like 5G to operate. It connects everything – every object, appliance, sensor, device, and application to the internet. All these sensors and devices will be connected to collect vast amounts of data to analyze and process information in many ways. Using the IoT, 5G networks can enable smart home networks to be better seen by apps and devices and connected to you personally.
