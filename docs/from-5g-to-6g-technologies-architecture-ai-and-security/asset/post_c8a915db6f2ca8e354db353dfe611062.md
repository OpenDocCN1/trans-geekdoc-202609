## 10.2 What Is Artificial Intelligence (AI)?

Technology has been around for centuries, adopted for different purposes of life as new inventions expanded its reach. For example, typewriters, we invented in 1714, significantly changed communication, affecting every aspect of life, from business use to government operations sending letters to friends [13].

Technology has secured variations as more enhanced, effective, and efficient technology has been produced based on the advancement in science and increased demands globally. The typewriter, breakthroughs such as smartphones and computers, which perform tasks and operations at the highest possible speed, have revolutionized every aspect of life, including communications, education, engineering, and scientific operations [14].

AI is one of the most significant breakthroughs in this decade. Artificial intelligence is human intelligence that is simulated and processed by different computer systems and machines. It includes three main processes: learning, reasoning, and self-correction. The learning aspect deals with acquiring information and related rules for the data for a specific purpose.

Reasoning relies on the rules that include reaching the most relevant content; it is definite in terms of the required information or given instructions. Self-correction corrects irrelevant information or any flaw in the process or information that is provided [15]. The technological advancements do not stop with artificial intelligence; they have made their way into development and improvements in four types of artificial intelligence.

### 10.2.1 Reactive Machines

Reactive machines are the most basic type of artificial intelligence. They cannot use past experiences and do not form memories for making decisions or not in the future [16]. An example is the chess-playing supercomputer of IBM [17].

Self-driving cars are the biggest examples of the limited memory of artificial intelligence, including observation of the environment, speed of other vehicles, directions, and directions. This limitation in memory prevents the vehicle from being able to store and search for memories from the past [18].

### 10.2.3 Theory of Mind

Another type is a more psychological aspect of AI machines, and how it involves people feeling about the development of machines and their intuition into that have always been the real of human beings. This includes detection of people, creatures, objectives, and expression of people. Therefore, it deals with the theory of mind that understands human social dynamics and norms in interactive, first person, and, second person and not like conventional scenario of the third person, and will not be achievable until in the future [19].

### 10.2.4 Self-Awareness

Type III AIs, as discussed above, are included in this type. This type is a saner and more extensive form of type III AIs. It consists of a self-aware system, forming representations not only about the world but also about other entities to understand that each of us has thoughts, expectations, and feelings that they have to cope and adjust their behavior accordingly [20].

## 10.3 The Transformative Power of AI

Considering artificial intelligence as an advanced technology that engagement based on cognitive abilities, machine learning, and automation, AI contains significant transformative power to shape the way people perform tasks and foster a future based on the interaction of artificial intelligence, automation, and cognitive engagement. Therefore, the transformative power of AI is demonstrated by evaluating the current progress, changes, and it has enhanced it in different aspects of life and in businesses and organizations elements. The cognitive automation of robotics has resulted in significant automation in manufacturing processes. This application is known as robotic process automation (RPA) for completing different tasks as physical tasks and digital tasks [21]. Big Data analysis has been dealt with and effectively, made possible through cognitive insight [22]. For example, algorithms are used for Big Data analysis. This Big analytics has increased efficiency in detecting the signature of data and detecting the patterns in large volumes of data. Artificial intelligence will transform the way operations are performed by employees and make workplaces more autonomous than they are right now.

Artificial will not replace humans with the automation of different tasks because artificial intelligence can only bring automation to the operations or that is operated or in a job rather than them entirely. Still, it requires human interactions. But this transformative power can still reduce the number of employees or time needed to perform a task significantly. It will enable employees to perform tasks more efficiently and provide more productive outcomes [23].

AI will transform the working environment and foster an environment in which tools are free for employees to make better decisions. Machine engagement with clients and customers. Major performance enhancements will lead to performance gains in terms of human name and machine collaboration in organizational contexts [24].
