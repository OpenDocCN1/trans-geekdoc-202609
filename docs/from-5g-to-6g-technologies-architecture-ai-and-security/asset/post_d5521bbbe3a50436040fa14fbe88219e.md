## 6.3.1 6G and the Fundamental Features

5G has the need for better flexibility from its need and success in meeting diverse requirements and accommodating different technologies. To achieve flexibility in the network we need three things: awareness, availability of options, and adaptation/optimization. Despite the success of 6G in meeting flexibility needs, it will be even more difficult to accommodate due to new applications and requirements. To prepare for 6G there needs to be a better exploration of awareness regarding the various facets of communication by using artificial intelligence, expanding the technology options, and providing proper utilization of existing options. Most literature on 6G doesn't follow the flexibility perspective.

The major objective of most research studies for 6G is to identify future applications, services, and requirements. There are three main categories that these items fall into: 6G eMBB, ultra-reliable and low latency communications (uRLL), massive machine-type learning (mMTC). There is also a list of potential applications for 6G like unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV), vehicle-to-everything (V2X), VR, AR, holographic conferencing, smart objects, healthcare, and more.

Ultra-physical layer (PHY) and medium access control (MAC) are layers for the multi-numerology systems. Multiple waveforms can be used in a single frequency for the purposes of 6G, which can serve as radar sensing for multiple networks. The waveform NOMA technique improve resource allocation for 6G. The MAC layer issues require flexibility: waveform assignment and network scheduling.

Ultra-heterogeneous networks are another key enablers. The flying access points allows for flexible networking and seamless trajectories for different applications. Improvements to satellite networks are also considered. Slicing and cell-free networks allow for user-centric systems with the flexibility of multiple networks under one umbrella. Blockchain technology and quantum communications can also contribute to the future.

Technologies like sensing and awareness, location, and context awareness falls under this category.
