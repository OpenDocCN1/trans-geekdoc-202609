## 1.3 AI and Cybersecurity: Paying the Way for the Future

In the twenty-first century, cybersecurity and AI have presented numerous opportunities for the world while facing challenges behind the scenes. There is a counter to cyberattacks, turning to AI and machine learning. The government methods to combat a new type of intrusion in a completely different environment. While it might seem that there are many challenges for cybersecurity and AI, there are also many opportunities for advancements in all types of technology.

Through recent advances in AI, we see that it is truly possible. Image recognition can analyze an image with a precision that would take hours a human to do. AI will bring challenges to the community that they have not previously faced. There are various security concerns with these applications that are still vulnerable.

There are many complexities involved with these systems, and they need to operate in all environments. There are four components integrated in AI systems: perception, learning, decisions, and actions. Each of these components must be managed independently from its counterpart, has unique vulnerabilities.

Since these AI systems can have high risks, trustworthiness becomes needed. Areas that need to be addressed through decision-making using performance metrics, developing techniques, making AI systems explainable and accountable, improving domain-specific training and managing data.

Even when AI does on many tasks, vulnerabilities are still produced from corrupt inputs that produce inaccurate results. The current challenges face the community that "AI systems are vulnerable to surveillance where adversaries query the systems and learn internal logic, knowledge bases, or the training data. This is often a precursor to an attack on secure-learning data sources or to crack the intellectual property embedded in AI" [1].

You can see how cybersecurity could be affected by AI. Cybersecurity can use AI to increase its awareness, react in real time, and improve its effectiveness. This means that its self-adaptation and adjustment to facing attacks and the ability to alter its course of action. It can help weaknesses in an adversary's strategies by analyzing and reacting in real time.

## 1.4 Fusion of IoT, AI, and Cybersecurity

In the world of IoT, people are reluctant to adapt to the threat of connected products by security concerns. These devices are more vulnerable to security threats because they are connected over a network with many paths that can be attacked. Practices in play in strengthening authentication and integrating IoT devices. However, AI and 5G may add some of the concerns people have about IoT devices [14].

IoT has utilized home security, the media field, utilities, entertainment, and so much more. With nearly every aspect of our daily life utilizing technology, cybersecurity becomes crucial. To understand the importance of cybersecurity, you must first understand what cybersecurity is and how you can protect. Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting networks, data, and devices from unauthorized users. These unauthorized users may be trying to access data for financial gain, blackmail, or identity theft. According to statistics from TechJury, 203 million websites were hacked daily. As of March 2021, 20 million records were breached [22]. The statistics are increasing significantly each year (1.5).

It is necessary to not only have good security practices but also know about attacks so you can better defend yourself.

- Malware attacks use malicious software to download onto a computer's system. This attack is typically done through unsecure, fraudulent links.
- Password attacks are done when hackers use password generator to crack a user's password.
