# 1.4.1 Where Did AI Begin?

Before 1949, computers lacked a prerequisite for intelligence. Computers, at the time, could not store commands, could only execute them. They were expensive. The average cost of leasing a computer was equivalent to almost $200,000 a month. From 1957 to 1974, AI flourished. Computers were now cheaper and more accessible, and data could be stored. However, it was eventually met with a mountain of obstacles. Then computers were simply too “weak” at the time, could not store enough information or process it fast enough. In the 1980s, the use of algorithm toolkit and an increase in funds helped revive the development of AI. John Hopfield and David Rumhart popularized “deep learning” techniques that allowed computers to learn user experience.

# 1.4.2 Role of AI

AI is to the use of understand human intelligence. In this concept, intelligence is the computational part of the ability to achieve goals. AI algorithms designed to make decisions from real-time data. In order to operate on real-time data, AI must use vast amounts of data. Data quality and quantity drive effectiveness. The goal of AI is to use no human interaction. Human interaction is needed to extract features. A example of AI is autonomous driving (Tesla is a well-known autonomous). In autonomous driving, the vehicle receives data via cameras and through different technologies. AI uses the data from these features and produces an output, such as switching lanes or coming to a stop. It allows a passenger to ride in the vehicle without touching the steering wheel. Another common example of AI is face detection technology. Face detection technology allows the user to unlock a device by looking into the camera. The AI identifies features and grants access based on what those features are. This is a common feature with iPhone.

As much as AI has to offer, it has its disadvantages. For example, there is high cost implementation. Using AI-based systems is costly because of the complexity and engineering of getting into a building. Not is it only expensive to build it can also be expensive to maintain or repair. Another disadvantage is that AI would not improve with experience. Human beings are able to develop as age. Children cannot after their responses to changing environments. AI machines are unable to determine what is rightly or wrong because they are unaware of what is ethical and legal. If an AI is not programmed for a certain situation, the user cannot be certain how the machine will respond. When the goal of AI is to have no human interaction, humans simply cannot be replaced. However, AI are able to perform repetitive tasks that many humans do so. According to a study conducted by McKinsey Global Institute, intelligent agents and robots could displace 30% of the world’s current labor market by 2030.
