# 6.5.3 High Data Rates

One of the focuses of 6G is to offer extremely high data rates compared to the existing 5G networks. It is expected to deliver peak data rates of over 1 Tbps per user average. This means that the upcoming networks will need very high bandwidths in the THz band. Besides, it will require OAM multiplexing, user communication, and over 1000 antenna elements. Research is also focusing on integrating quantum communication to increase the data rates. Consequently, upcoming innovative technologies are bound to benefit from this.

# 6.5.4 High-Frequency Bands

Data rates keep going up, especially on cellular wireless networks. Wider bandwidths and higher frequencies are required to match the increasing-data rates. For 6G, network developers and researchers are focusing on sub-THz and TH bands that can offer rich spectrum for electromagnetic and light waves communication. The utilization of such frequencies will support multi-THz data rates in transmitting information for indoor and outdoor scenarios. THz frequencies are likely to solve inter-cell interference. Its ability to support high-frequency bands, it is vital to solve the arising challenges for futures.

# 6.5.5 Ultra-Reliability

Reliability is set to increase by 100% from the existing 5G networks. Applications such as telehealth, industrial IoT, and smart cities need very high reliability to function optimally. Hence, 6G networks should attain QoS requirements for efficiency and efficiency.

# 6.5.6 Security and Privacy

All networks, 3G, 4G, 5G, and 6G, require security and privacy. Since 6G is set to support massive data rates, it demands improved security. Research shows that quantum computing is likely to secure these networks. However, blockchain technology will come in hand due to the potential challenges as they are difficult to manipulate.
