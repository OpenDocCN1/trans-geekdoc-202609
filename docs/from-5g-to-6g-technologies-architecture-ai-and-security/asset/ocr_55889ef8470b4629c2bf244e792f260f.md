# 10.3 The Transformative Power of AI

Considering artificial intelligence as an advanced technology that engagement based on cognitive abilities, machine learning, and automation, AI contains significant transformative power to shape the way people perform tasks and foster a future based on the interaction of artificial intelligence, automation, and cognitive engagement. Therefore, the transformative power of AI is demonstrated by evaluating the current progress, changes, and it has enhanced it in different aspects of life and in businesses and organizations elements. The cognitive automation of robotics has resulted in significant automation in manufacturing processes. This application is known as robotic process automation (RPA) for completing different tasks as physical tasks and digital tasks [21]. Big Data analysis has been dealt with and effectively, made possible through cognitive insight [22]. For example, algorithms are used for Big Data analysis. This Big analytics has increased efficiency in detecting the signature of data and detecting the patterns in large volumes of data. Artificial intelligence will transform the way operations are performed by employees and make workplaces more autonomous than they are right now.

Artificial will not replace humans with the automation of different tasks because artificial intelligence can only bring automation to the operations or that is operated or in a job rather than them entirely. Still, it requires human interactions. But this transformative power can still reduce the number of employees or time needed to perform a task significantly. It will enable employees to perform tasks more efficiently and provide more productive outcomes [23].

AI will transform the working environment and foster an environment in which tools are free for employees to make better decisions. Machine engagement with clients and customers. Major performance enhancements will lead to performance gains in terms of human name and machine collaboration in organizational contexts [24].

Research projects have been initiated to identify and predict the results that AI implementation will have on business.
