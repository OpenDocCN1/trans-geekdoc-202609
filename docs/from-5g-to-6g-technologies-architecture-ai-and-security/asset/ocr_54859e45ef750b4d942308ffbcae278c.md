Revolutionary technologies are still in the exploratory stage and not reach maturity by 2030. To improve the performance of the physical layer, we need to develop new technologies such as photonics-defined and holographic optics. Optical holography can be used to record the electromagnetic field in space based on the interference of electromagnetic waves, and radio frequencies are electromagnetic waves, so continuous aperture antenna can be used to record the electromagnetic field in space. The terahertz band is the last part of the radio spectrum and is used for 6G, but it is not yet commercially available. The characteristics of the terahertz spectrum are unique, including high bandwidth and low attenuation, but the spectrum is also prone to shadowing and blocking due to the diffraction effect at short wavelengths. There are many challenges in the implementation of TH, including ultra-wide processing and very large-scale antenna, which could lead to a super-speed broadband processing with high power consumption. Commercial products for TH communications are now available, and it is expected that these products will also hit data rates in the 6G era.
