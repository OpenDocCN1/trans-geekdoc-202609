Release 16 of the LTE standard offers a new radio in the 5 and 6 GHz bands, and a sidelink for direct communication between devices. Release 16 adds support for IoT, URLLC Phase 2, IAB, device power savings, mobility enhancements, and enhanced multi-link multi-output, multi-output (MIMO) with multiple reception and reflection capabilities. Release 17 will also include a larger coverage with Cellular Internet of Things (CIoT) services. It will also support further advanced networking, power saving, and massive machine type communications (mMTC), and unmanned aircraft systems (GEO, MEO, LEO, and HAPS satellites are in the sky in Earth’s orbit). ## 6.3 6th Generation Networks: A Step Beyond 5G

Even though 5G are still in the deployment phase, there are increasing discussions surrounding 6G networks. The increasing innovations of data-intensive applications such as brain-computer interface (BCI), flying vehicles, e-healthcare, and extended reality shape the need for 6G.
