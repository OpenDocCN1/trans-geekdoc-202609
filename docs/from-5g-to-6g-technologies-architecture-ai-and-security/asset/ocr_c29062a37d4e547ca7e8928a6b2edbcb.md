# 12.6.13 Human-Bond Communications

In coming times, the ability of people to communicate sensations will improve by incorporating the five sensory characteristics into the messages and potentially adding expressive and wholesome sensory exchange through communication methods [37]. Human-bond communication (HBC) is just a vision, but scientists are hopeful that it will be attainable. HBC is a newly described idea that gathers touch, olfactory, and tactile sensations to achieve an attainable human-centered communication for coming networks.

For HBC, there is a proposed baseline architecture. HBC comprises seducers (sense transducers) responsible for the sensory transduction of stimuli to electrical indicators [37]. The second component of HBC is human perceivable transporter, responsible for transposing data. The third component is human-bond sensorium, which gathers, processes, and transmits the data [37]. With research focusing on development and launching of the 6G networks, there are debates concerning how 6G technology will affect human health.
