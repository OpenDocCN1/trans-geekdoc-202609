# 6.2 G Network Architecture Vision

The gradual steady evolution of wireless networks from the first generation to the fifth generation is consistent when it comes to data rate, latency, services, capacity, and coverage. The massive increase in mobile data traffic has revolutionized the new face of the internet and multimedia technology. The future is to be a data-driven society in which people and things become universally connected in a matter of seconds or a few milliseconds. Many aspects of the 6G wireless network communication are still under scientific exploration. Mature study and development of the physical layer are still needed to make them practical and feasible engineering.
The 6G network will support an average of over 1000 nodes per person in the next 10 years and to provide a better rate per second. One goal of 6G wireless communication network is to provide instant holographic connectivity – anytime from anywhere – and create a quick link between people and things
