# 5.5 6G Communications Using Intelligent Platforms

Society is increasingly digitized and will continue to be so into the future. The world is moving toward the deployment of 5G wireless networks. Wireless connectivity is ubiquitous to connect people, and things machines without the need for human intervention; 5G New Radio (NR) network should be able to handle high connection density and high user latency; 3G Release 15 defines the basic framework for NR, which supports ultra-low latency through a very short mini-slot. The next of 5G will feature enhanced URLLC (ultra low latency), mMTC (massive Machine Type Communications), and NoAI, NB-IoT and LTE-M will be integrated into 5G NR for MCT services. LPWANs with Sigfox, LoRa, and In have been developed for MCT applications. The 5G network can be used to provide MCT and URLLC services, though data rates are limited and the used cases are rarer. To ensure robustness, connectivity solutions for MCT scenarios may require multiple radio access technologies. Although 6G is still in the exploration phase, many publications have already been presented on what 6G will be.

MTC is expected to be a key driver in this increasing trend making towards more autonomous and intelligent IoT. 6G will enable smart cities with 30 billion devices connected by 2030. Industry 5.0 innovation will play a fundamental role in facilitating the user experience of augmented/virtual/mixed reality, and in enabling novel and efficient machine interfaces. MTC will need to be energy-efficient, low-latency, and error-free to enable zero-touch systems. In 6G, data markets will require data suppliers and consumers will need APIs like kind of information, privacy, and location accuracy. 4G industry will be flexible, agile, and adaptable, supporting both autonomous and human-machine interactions. Services of automobiles will be connected in 2050, requiring robust connectivity solutions. Virtual reality will also be deeply integrated into the 6G era. At the edge of the IoT, machines will be lower-power sensors that can keep things working.
