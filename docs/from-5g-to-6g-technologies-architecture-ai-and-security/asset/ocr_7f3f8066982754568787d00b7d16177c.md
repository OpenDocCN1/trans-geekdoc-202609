# 4.7 Conclusion

Both physical and virtual communications helped mitigate the harmful impacts of the COVID-19 pandemic on several sectors, including health, education, transportation, and industry. The epidemic would have had a bigger impact if these communication technologies had not been available, and many industries heavily rely on telecommunications networks to support their daily operations. Because these networks have been subjected to unprecedented traffic demands. Therefore, understanding the role of AI in the era of 5G and 6G communication is key.
