Another key technology for 6G would be orbital angular momentum (OAM) technology which has been recently researched and looks promising in 6G networks. The main advantage of OAM is the ability to enable multiple channels to significantly increase their transmission capacity and spectrum efficiency due to the characteristics of its waves. Artificial intelligence is AI in all, while fifth-generation networks had partial inclusion. Going beyond the fifth generation to the sixth is going to require full integration of AI. Artificial intelligence in 6G will be able to automate almost every part of it. This automation will simplify many aspects including the transmission of data and speed up processes significantly. ## 8.4 Application and Services

Now that we have discussed the key enabling technologies for 6G, we can finally get to what we are able to do with them! There are many futuristic applications and services that have been thought of for 6G networks that may be able to offer.

Figure 3 shows some of the applications and services that can be provided with 6G technologies. It also shows a model of the different aspects of the network itself. One of the most researched and desired devices will be able to provide self-driving cars. 6G networks will bring many autonomous vehicles near. Drone delivery UAV systems are also feasible and will save a lot of time transporting packages and boost the economy. Both of these technologies use sensors and GPS positions within the current networks, but they are not good enough; 6G networks will change that. Smart devices also have a variety of different uses including military, commerce, science, agriculture, recreation, law and order delivery, surveillance, aerial photography, disaster management, and drone racing [11].

Another possible service is virtual reality/immersive. 6G networks will be able to provide better brain-computer communication and interaction. This sounds out of a sci-fi film. There is an interface called the brain-computer interface that is supposed to allow control of all aspects. How it works is having a direct communication between the brain and the smart devices by actually getting signals from the brain. These signals are transmitted to devices where they are analyzed and integrated with commands. With the features and crazy improvements from 5G to 6G, this should be possible.

Another possible service from 6G networks would be haptic communication, communicating using the sense of touch. This will allow end users to feel different textures and temperatures, such as feeling the texture of a surface as they pass over it. This is all possible due to the high amounts of data being able to go through 6G networks and the new technology that will advance with it.

A huge application of 6G services would be smart healthcare. Healthcare will have a huge impact on the world and 6G networks will be able to provide it. It will allow remote surgeries from anywhere in the world. This will decrease the cost of healthcare as it becomes more accessible and allow more people to get the help they need, especially in remote areas. There would be a doctor at your fingertips when you need one on the advanced network. Network stability would be essential if the signal were cut out during doing something like surgery, it could be fatal. This is why 6G networks are supposed to be extremely stable.

Another exciting service that could be provided by 6G networks would be five-sense immersion transferring. Humans will experience the world using only five senses: touch, taste, smell, and hearing. 6G networks would allow for complete immersion of the five senses. The BCI technology mentioned earlier will definitely help this technology thrive.
