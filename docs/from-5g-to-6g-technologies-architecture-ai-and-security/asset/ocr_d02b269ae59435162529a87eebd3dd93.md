One of the most researched and desired devices will be able to provide self-driving cars. 6G networks will bring many autonomous vehicles near. Drone delivery UAV systems are also feasible and will save lot of time transporting packages and boost the economy. Smart also have a variety of different uses including military, commerce, science, agriculture, recreation, law and order delivery, surveillance, aerial photography, disaster management, and drone racing [11].

Another possible service is virtual reality/immersive. 6G networks will be able to provide better brain-computer communication and interaction. This sounds out of a sci-fi film. There is an interface called the brain-computer interface that is supposed to allow all control of aspects. How it works is having a direct communication between the brain and the smart devices by actually getting signals from the brain.
