# 5.2 Important Aspects of Sixth-Generation Communication Technology

The transition from analog to digital communication of 6G has paved road for flexibility by means of frequency reuse multiple modulation schemes, adaptive equalization, and dynamic frequency allocation. The third-generation (3G) and fourth-generation (4G) cellular systems incorporated bundles of services such as data, voice, and video streaming. Access technologies such as code division multiplexing (CDMA) and orthogonal frequency multiplexing (OFDM) have enhanced multiplexing methods, flexible and adaptive rate, and interference management via the utilization of frequency farming and multiple streaming.
