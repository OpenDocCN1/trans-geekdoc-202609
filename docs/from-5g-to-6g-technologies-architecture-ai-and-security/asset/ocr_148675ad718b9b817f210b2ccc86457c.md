Since 6G aims to turn our wireless world into an AI-based intelligent network connecting much of the world as possible, networks of the future will require massive amounts of bandwidth- which will create other problems faced by our current networks. To combat this, we will need to operate on higher frequency bands like terahertz (THz). THz will be a requirement because contiguous spectrum allowing massive amounts of bandwidth are not possible below 90 GHz [17]. The high transmission frequency of the THz band will allow us to operate high-directional antennas with extremely low beamwidths. # 3.8 The Future of 6G and Its Role in IT

6G is being researched, and several countries are taking the first steps to create it. Countless hours of work will need to be done before it is rolled out to the public - perhaps not for another decade. Once it does come, there will undoubtedly be bugs and glitches that will need addressing. IT professionals will likely have their hands full, helping consumers with troubleshooting, debugging hardware, installing and maintaining new infrastructure, or doing further research into the future, perhaps even regardless of what job each IT professional handles, the 6G will likely play a large part in its not-so-distant future.
