The sixteenth and seventeenth releases of Release 16 will define the definition of use cases, items, and work toward 6G. Release 16 of the LTE standard offers a new radio in the 5 and 6 GHz bands, and a sidelink for direct communication between devices. Release 16 adds support for IoT, WRCCS Phase 2, IAB, device power savings savings, mobility enhancements, and enhanced multi-link multi-output, multi-output (MIMO) with multiple reception and reflection capabilities. Release 17 will also include a larger coverage with Cellular Internet of Things (CIoT) services. It will also support further advanced networking, power saving, and massive machine type communications (mMTC), and unmanned aircraft systems (GEO, MEO, LEO, and HAPS satellites are in the sky in Earth’s orbit). They act as relay nodes and base stations and can be used for a variety of purposes. Broadband IoT based on narrow band and long term evolution/machine-type communications (LTE/MTC), will contribute nearly 34% of LTE connections by 2025. The releases of 16 and 17 will make it easier to deploy and operate 5G networks. Release 18 is expected to be under release to enhance its capabilities. 6G is still under development and will provide a new interface or service enhancing the 5G Advanced new network. While the deployment of 6G systems still ahead, many programs and projects will provide support for the acceleration of 6G standards. It is entering the development of applications high real-time (tactile communications), tactile/haptic communications, medical/health, vertical feeling, environmental sensing, imaging, and public safety services, transportation.

The commercialization of 5G is taking shape rapidly in many countries as it attempts to improve the performance of the existing networks. The rise of smart devices is massively increasing, ultimately increasing the data rates [10]. Many users can access to the services they receive from using smart devices. Furthermore, organizations and companies can benefit from revenues because of the multiple business ideas that. There is increasing demand for use-cases of high-ends of 5G and 6G networks. The increase in network capacity is the reason why companies are struggling to upgrade networks’ capacities. Currently, 5G networks are game-changers because they connect a massive number of devices and machines. For instance, according to Ericsson Mobility (2019) [11], the 5G NR (NR) can cover approximately 65% of the total population by 2025.
