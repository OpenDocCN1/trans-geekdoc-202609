# 1.2.7 The Internet of Everything

The seventh topic in this chapter is the IoE, which is people, processes, and things. The IoE is the world where billions of devices have sensors to detect and assess the states of the environment. They are all connected over a public or private network using protocols both standard and private. The main characteristics of IoE are data, processes, and things. The IoT is the network of physical objects accessed through the internet. The main difference is that IoE has four distinguishable characteristics, while IoT has one. You will hear the term IoE as or IoT due to the vast range and expanse of high-speed data transfer and accompanying technology.
