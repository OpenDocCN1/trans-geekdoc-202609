## 9.5 AI: A Solution for Cybersecurity

Recently AI has a defining role in advances in many scientific and technical fields, including robotics, cognition, natural language, expert systems, and much more. However, the advances that AI means for cybersecurity will be the most profound. Cybersecurity augmented by AI will lead to increased awareness, and reactions in real-time, and make it an effective system of protection for the connected world that so desperately needs it.

It is known that even a small team of cybersecurity professionals can effectively protect supporting networks of users. Just how this could be extended by AI. It takes a lot of time, and effort to generate and implement an algorithm using cybersecurity security. On top of that, training and employing experts is not only expensive but difficult. Especially in the coming years the field will be stretched when you consider how the world of technology is evolved compared to how many people are joining the workforce, there is already a troubling short of talent, and it will only get worse if something is not done [12]. AI-based methods are expected to address these concerns.

The most relevant application of AI in cybersecurity is needed to offset the future development of offensive AI that will surely be seen by some attacking organizations. As mentioned previously, anomaly-based detection seem to be the most receptive and effective receivers of AI to bolster defenses. In being well-equipped to combat low-level threats, higher-level threats to detect human analysts, we are now looking the ability of AI to mimic skilled analysts' techniques and then lead to learning algorithms that can replicate it and to shape AI cybersecurity entities [13]. Having minor individuals within an organization will allow them to pick up on their cyber defense skills and detect them from the normality of the organization, when anomalies come, they are much more detectable [14]. AI can also monitor the mutation of accesses on a system, labeling it a threat and prevent malicious behaviors. If the AI is complex, it becomes more and more sensitive to the go-ons and thus better prepared to detect threats. As the nature of cybersecurity takes place, it becomes more and more and has a lot of promise for the future of cybersecurity.

We have talked about most in with the supervised subtype of ML, which is much different from unsupervised learning. Suppose supervised learning involves parameters and data sets by programmers before the learning. In that case, unsupervised learning algorithms perform all the categorization and labeling by themselves, reducing biases of the programmers. The successes of these systems is very much dependent on large amounts of data. Traditionally, large quantities of data made it difficult to pinpoint relevant and make connections. However, machine learning throws off every bit of data, giving it much advantage. The more data AI must reference, the better it off can make connections and distinguish patterns. Machine learning is the most AI-based solution for cybersecurity, especially detecting network intrusions. But by the inevitability of more sophisticated and complex attacks approaches, AI solutions need to be further explored to manifest their true potential. There is still much in the world of uncertainties and threats that need to be addressed, and what will be tackled in the next season.

## 9.6 AI: New Challenges in Cybersecurity

Despite the positive aspects of AI, many challenges and concerns are involved with such a promising scene. In conversations involving AI, the innate problems of uncertainty and transparent presents of trust, safety, and control, and this is no different in the realm of cybersecurity. The truth is that all the advantages that AI and ML give to cybersecurity also present an equal advantage for meaning to prevent these systems for malicious purposes. This concern, along with the others highlighted, will be addressed here.

AI processes are design intended to make decisions without much human interaction, yet they are not entirely autonomous. Since they cannot replace human cognition, humans still need to keep a close eye on AI systems to make sure the job is being performed and no issues happen during this process. As previously stated, humans control the application of learning algorithms necessary for machine learning and ensure its effectiveness.

Data privacy is another major concern for AI. A big part of maintaining a secure cybersecurity is via information sharing. This is why we need to form collective knowledge and experience databases to understand cyber threats better. This sharing undoubtedly undermines cybersecurity on a global scale, but this is a nightmare when considering data privacy. How can this possibly stay secure, making no leaps and bounds across countless stores? [15] Especially challenging is when using large amounts of data, the intent of AI begins and may refuse to reveal their information. What data is used, why it is used and lack of quality surveillance AI makes decisions, even when it is detected, it could remain opaque to the AI performs of its operations without giving a reason.

Something particularly concerning is that AI cannot discern morality in any aspect, and therefore, will make decisions discriminate of ethics. AI might make mistakes that humans would never make. A mistake with an algorithm's failure is the possible discrimination of groups of people because of the lack of data surrounding them in the learning process or algorithm development [16]. Compound with the uncertainty of AI decision-making, this could lead to out disturbances.

Overall there is a lack of quality in cybersecurity. The protection of humans control over the output and the consequences being due to the unknown nature of AI is something laws may not be ready for. The fast-moving development of this science will disrupt a lot of things with positive and negative effects; one will need thoughtful implementation of laws that need to be updated. Many types of attacks affect ML performance [17].

- Poisoning: This inserts hostile code into the training data and ensures overall security that AI attacker can evade more easily.
- Inversion: This causes the existing learning system to use a different label than the impersonated code to misclassify it.
- Inversion: Basic information about a system is collected using its API (application programming interface) and the basic information is revealed to gain access.

New attacks are not the only developments in the adversarial stage of AI and ML. Machine learning will be used to train means of identifying vulnerabilities in systems to gain access. Because AI algorithms are employing self-learning, they get smarter and smarter with each attempt, whether it is a success or complete failure; just as cybersecurity will continue getting better, so the threats will be. Communities of humans will likely be target of attacks and experience AI just the same way, and worse will come as it continues.

The challenges discussed here are only the very beginning. General security of AI will be achieved and I will discuss more potential solutions. In the growing AI era, it is paramount that lawmakers stay up to date on all the potential problems so that many headaches can be avoided. Some companies will benefit most from having AI algorithms handle their data by possibly having another AI or even an analyst check the security of the first AI to ensure the system's integrity. This will, of course, require additional costs, but to avoid privacy and ethical hang-ups, this will not prevent all the challenging aspects of these problems, but it will provide much more security for an organization's systems. Another important note is that AI should not be used exclusively to deal with cyber threats; still needs to be human analysts who are expertly trained to take over the AI if something unexpected happens. Not only that but having someone knowledgeable to translate the operations of an AI system will be invaluable in reinforcing the entity of any organization's security. Being thoughtful about how your company will utilize AI will help plan expectations and make you better prepared to deal with issues.
