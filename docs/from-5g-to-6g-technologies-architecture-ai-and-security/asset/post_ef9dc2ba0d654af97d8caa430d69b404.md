## 10.5.1.1 Domestic Risks

The domestic risks and impacts of cybersecurity that are undermined by artificial intelligence are the implications of implying AI to act as a watchdog, keeping civilians in a state of surveillance in national security. The people are experiencing a situation in which they feel a state of inequality - they do not know what information governments or corporations are retaining about their personal lives. There must be a solution where enforcement of the law does not ignore people's privacy. There have also been privacy concerns about the issue with security cameras and consideration of their captured image as evidence, and more recently, with the massive DNA data volunteered to companies to trace one's ancestry. Such concerns are growing as advancements in technology continue.

## 10.5.1.2 Local Risks

In the local debate, many people feel a sense of invasion of privacy. From smart apps to virtual assistants like Alexa and Siri, surveillance is being imposed upon us in ways never experienced before. It is one of the most negative aspects of the advancements in artificial intelligence that are currently shaping. The enhancement of the proximities of capabilities and development also gives us a whole spectrum of concerns to deal with. With the advancement of artificial intelligence, there will be issues associated with such concerns.

Legal scholars are assessing the legal implications of some issues. Some cases have developed with this aspect, like those associated with security cameras and artificial intelligence, and others have spawned changes in the law to protect individuals.

## 10.5.1.3 National Risks

AI is only as smart as the data it feeds, it is called data viability. When it comes to national aspects, data input can create cybersecurity risks. Training factors or data quality can result in falsehood and, therefore, some major actors. In this case, the application of AI and cybersecurity in national security can open a new attack vector based on the vulnerability of the data. The corruption from Microsoft's AI Chatbot is the best demonstr of such risk and its impact on the national aspect and security [34].

Based on identified factors for national security, data used to train AI and AI-based systems must be viable. This can be done by testing data on a proper schedule, testing, and simulations to identify any factor to eliminate vulnerabilities and any underlying factor that is risky.

## 10.6.2 Protection from Malicious Use of AI and Automated Cyberattacks

As discussed, with the advancements of AI, cybersecurity has advanced due to its adaptation, but cyberattacks and attackers have gained significant advantages as well, which resulted in potential impacts and outcomes. AI based tools and devices have provided ease to attackers as cracking security protocols, and the use of machine learning has enabled them to carry attacks in a more sophisticated way that is even more effective. This was proven in the DARPA Grand Challenge where autonomous hacks were performed [38].

The AI-based defense system is much more than ever, but cyberattacks are getting stronger and more complicated. Therefore, it is necessary to institute the right communication channels and processes to ensure the integrity and enhancement of AI-based security services.
