Non-IP networking protocols: Decade-old IPv6 has long been researched for an upgrade, the onset of a 6G connected society will make it much more justified that not 50% of networking traffic does not originate or become terminated at the wireless edge

Information-centric networks (ICNs) and intent-based networks (IBNs): Unlike the NGP (next-generation protocols), ICNs are actively in research. They are a step toward separating content from location where it originates. To make the connection between networking design and operational management, intent-based networking and service design have popped their head. It is a lifecycle management framework for networking infrastructure, continuing continual network monitoring and real-time optimization processes to adapt to any change in network/service state.

360-connectivity and privacy-by-engineering: 6G requires that end-to-end solutions be as well as top-down. Privacy-by-engineering will ensure that messages are natively built into the protocols and architecture, preventing the vending of forecasted packets.

Future-proofing emerging technologies: As technologies continue to improve by day, it will be necessary that 6G adapts to technologies that will be invented in the future so they may be embedded quicker and more efficiently into the architecture. Such technologies include but are not limited to artificial intelligence (AI), distributed ledger (DLT), and quantum computing.
