
## Advancing Digital Forensic Intelligence

Forensic professionals in this sector use specific tools and procedures to examine and unearth pertinent information from smartphones, tablets, and other mobile devices. They rebuild digital actions and timelines by recovering deleted texts, photographs, videos, phone records, and app data.

Mobile forensics also includes geolocation data, GPS information, and network connections to determine physical movements and interactions. Social media forensics, on the other hand, is the investigation of social media platforms such as Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, and LinkedIn in order to collect evidence connected to cyberbullying, harassment, online threats, fraud, and more.

To identify relationships, motives, and behavioural patterns, investigators trace online identities, follow interactions, and evaluate user-generated information. In the digital era, mobile and social media forensics play an important part in current investigations, allowing the finding of essential evidence and assisting in settlement.

## EdgeAI in Law Enforcement

The integration of EdgeAI techniques with law enforcement has transformed DFI, enabling real-time threat identification and proactive investigation. This case study focuses on the use of EdgeAI in law enforcement, demonstrating its usefulness in improving capabilities. The agency in question was dealing with the growing complexity and volume of digital evidence discovered (Figure 3.10). Traditional digital forensic approaches were unable to keep up with the rapid speed. To solve these issues, the agency developed an EdgeAI platform that makes use of networked computing and ML methods. Investigators obtained the ability to process data in real time by installing EdgeAI-enabled devices at the network edge, drastically lowering the time required for digital evidence examination. SVMs and DL models, for example, were trained to detect anomalies, classify digital artifacts, and find patterns of criminal activity.

One significant achievement of EdgeAI implementation is that EdgeAI devices continuously watched network traffic and user behaviour, deactivating suspicious transactions. An alarm was produced when an unexpected pattern was observed. EdgeAI's real-time capabilities enabled investigators to track actions in real time, collect evidence, and quickly identify criminals. The proactive approach, combined with the capability of EdgeAI, allowed the agency to dismantle major damage. Furthermore, EdgeAI's privacy protection capabilities were critical in maintaining integrity. Because the system was distributed, sensitive data remained localized, reducing the risk of unwanted access and protecting the privacy of persons who were participating.

## EdgeAI in Corporate and Cybersecurity Investigations

EdgeAI approaches have shown to be a game changer in corporate and cybersecurity investigations, enabling real-time analysis, rapid incident response, and effective evidence collection. This case study examines how EdgeAI aids digital forensic investigators. XYZ Corporation, a worldwide corporation with extensive digital infrastructure (Figure 3.11), uses EdgeAI technology to undermine essential systems. The firm intentionally puts intelligent agents and ML algorithms at important spots inside its network architecture using edge computing capabilities to limit potential harm.

The organization benefits from reduced latency and increased responsiveness for processing and analysing data at the network edge. The EdgeAI-enabled system detects suspicious activity as the attack progresses. Deep neural networks and other ML algorithms evaluate patterns to classify dangerous behaviours. The EdgeAI system notifies incident response, initiating an instant response to contain the threat and safeguard key assets. At the same time, the EdgeAI system collects and stores digital evidence relating to the occurrence. Edge computing's distributed nature means evidence is securely stored at the network edge, protecting integrity. Digital artifacts such as network logs, system snapshots, and audit records are conveniently recorded and stored for future analysis.

EdgeAI also helps with post-incident analysis. DL and advanced analytics reveal hidden relationships and determine the root cause of the attack. This insight enables the organization to strengthen its defences, update security measures, and fix vulnerabilities proactively. The use of EdgeAI techniques in corporate and cybersecurity investigations speeds up the investigation process and improves response capabilities. The combination of real-time analysis and proactive threat detection enables investigators to quickly identify and reduce hazards. EdgeAI-enabled technology enables efficient evidence collecting while also protecting the admissibility of digital evidence in court processes.
