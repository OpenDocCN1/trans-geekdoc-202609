# AI- and IoT-Based Intrusion Detection System for Cybersecurity

According to Herold, well-engineered and extensively tested AI-based IDS can detect signals of proactive breaches, or attacks launched from compromised IoT devices sooner than prior generations of IDS. This can then help to prevent access to corrupted IoT devices via digital ecosystems [4–10]. “AI-based IDS can also help defenders to take action quickly in order to slow down attackers,” she said. “Well-designed AI-based tools can automate the detection of attacks launched within digital ecosystems and as those launched from the network's edges.”

There are two types of IDS: network (NIDS) and host (HIDS). NIDS are IDS that are strategically positioned in the network. NIDS examines the network's total traffic to determine whether not malicious attacks are taking place. NIDS aids in the detection of attacks from your own hosts and is an essential component of most organization networks' security, HIDS are IDS that are installed on all network clients (hosts); if it finds anomalous behavior, it will raise an alarm. The detection technique may also classify IDS [11–15]. Signature-based detection (recognizing patterns, such as malware) and anomaly-based detection (detecting departure from a model of “good” traffic, based on machine learning) are the most well-known variants. Another popular method is detection by reputation.

It is difficult, not impossible, to create an IDS that is completely successful. Most modern systems contain various security issues [16–22]. Not all types of incurs are recognized. In addition, hackers are employing machine learning techniques to find new ways into networks. Rapid identification of these attacks will aid in identifying potential intruders and limiting harm. As a result, establishing an effective and accurate IDS will aid in the reduction of network security risks. The major contributions of this chapter are follows:
