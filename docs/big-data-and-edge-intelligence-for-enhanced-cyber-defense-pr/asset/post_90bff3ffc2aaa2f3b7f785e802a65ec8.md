In the context of the IDS's performance across various attacks, several observations can be made. Firstly, for benign network traffic, the IDS exhibits a high TP Rate of 0.94, with no (FP Rate of 0), resulting in a precision of 1. This indicates the system's ability to effectively identify normal behavior. On the other hand, attacks such as SSH (PATATOR) and FTP (PATATOR) demonstrate excellent performance, boosting TP Rates of 1 and low FP Rates of 0.003, indicating high precision in classifying these attacks. The IDS struggles with the HEARTBLEED and WEB ATTACK (SQL INJ) categories, with a complete failure to detect any positives in these cases. Attacks like DOS (SLOWHTTPTEST), INFILTRATION, DD, and BOT enjoy near-perfect detection, with TP Rates and Positive Predicted Value (PPV) of 1, demonstrating the system's robustness in recognizing these threats. Overall, these metrics underscore the IDS's varying degrees of effectiveness in distinguishing between benign and malicious activities, showcasing its strengths in certain attack scenarios while revealing areas for potential improvement in others. A similar outcome has been observed for test case #2 to test case #10.

It can be seen that the IDS engine generally detects threats across all cases. The prediction accuracy by the detector is 97.12% with a low misclassification rate of 2.87%. Moreover, the IDS generates few false alarm of 0.02% which is negligible.
