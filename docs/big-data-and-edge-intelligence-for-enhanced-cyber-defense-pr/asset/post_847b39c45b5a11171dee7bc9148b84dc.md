## 3.4.2 PORTABLE EdgeAI FORENSIC WORKSTATIONS

By introducing EdgeAI devices into investigative processes, PDFWs have changed the area of DFI. These high-tech workstations are outfitted with cutting-edge hardware and software, allowing forensic evidence to analyse, and interpret digital evidence with unprecedented speed and accuracy. EdgeAI devices with PDFWs enables investigators to undertake real-time data processing and analysis at the crisis scene, avoiding the need for time-consuming tasks to centralized forensic laboratories.

PDFWs use the power of EdgeAI devices, as small but powerful neural units (NPUs) and specialized hardware accelerators, to accomplish tasks like image and video recognition, text extraction, and voice analysis. These machines can analyse amounts of data quickly, allowing forensic key evidence among the digital clutter. Furthermore, PDFWs are portable, can transport these sophisticated tools immediately to the scene of the crime, saving significant time and resources.

EdgeAI integration with PDFWs also provides security capabilities, safeguarding data during the forensic process. Advanced encryption methods, secure boot processes, and separate memory areas protect the evidence's integrity and secrecy. Furthermore, PDFWs firstly tamper-evident seals and hardware-based write-blockers to ensure that digital evidence is preserved in its original state.

PDFWs' capabilities go beyond data extraction and processing. In addition, they offer a comprehensive suite of forensic tools designed exclusively for digital investigations. Data recovery, password cracking, network analysis, and virus identification. are all tasks that these technologies help forensic investigators with. PDFWs enable investigators to tackle complicated cases more efficiently and effectively by combining the capabilities of EdgeAI devices with these software tools.

## 3.4.3 WEARABLE DEVICES

Wearable devices and EdgeAI technologies have transformed the area of DFI, giving investigators significant tools for gathering evidence while maintaining mobility and efficiency. These cutting-edge gadgets feature AI capabilities that be seamlessly integrated, allowing them to handle and analyze data directly at the scene rather than depending on traditional centralized computer infrastructure. Wearable devices integrated with EdgeAI technology (Figure 3.4) may identify and extract useful information from multiple digital sources, such as smartphones, PCs, or IoT devices, using the power of AI algorithms.

Furthermore, these wearable gadgets include advanced capabilities such as biometric authentication, which allows safe access to critical information while preventing unwanted manipulation. As result, DFI has become more streamlined, proactive, and agile, giving investigators the ability to keep ahead of emerging cyber threats. The integration of wearable devices with EdgeAI technology represents a key milestone in the field of digital forensics, providing investigators with unparalleled powers while preserving evidence integrity and authenticity.

## 3.4.4 DRONE FORENSICS

Drone forensics has evolved as an important component of DFI, utilizing the capabilities of EdgeAI devices to examine and extract valuable data from UAVs. These advanced flying vehicles are outfitted with a variety of sensors and onboard systems that generate a large amount of data, such as flight records, GPS locations, photographs, videos, and communication logs.

Forensic professionals can easily collect and evaluate much of information by using EdgeAI devices, allowing them to recreate flight trajectories, discover patterns, and extract evidence connected to probable criminal actions or security breaches. The incorporation of AI algorithms at the edge enables investigators to do real-time analysis on-site, reducing data transfer time and enabling timely decision-making.

Furthermore, EdgeAI devices used in drone forensics can detect automatically, such as unauthorized flight paths or suspicious communications, assisting investigators in identifying potential risks and implementing relevant steps. The convergence of drone forensics and EdgeAI devices has transformed the area of DFI, aiming investigators with strong tools for uncovering important evidence and improving overall security.

## 3.4.5 IoT DEVICE ANALYSERS

IoT device analysers, in combination with Edge-EdgeAI technology, have emerged as indispensable instruments in the field of DFI, providing investigators with unparalleled capabilities in the study of IoT devices. These advanced analysts take advantage of the potential of EdgeAI, allowing them to process and analyse data directly at the edge rather than on centralized resources.

These devices can easily extract and correlate information from a wide range of IoT devices, including smart appliances, wearables, industrial sensors, by utilizing AI algorithms [31]. This capacity enables investigators to find digital artifacts, follow communication patterns, and recreate events, assisting in the resolution of cyberimes and other operations. The incorporation of IoT device analysts and EdgeAI in DFI is a significant step forward, allowing investigators to efficiently navigate the complex landscape while ensuring the accuracy and reliability of findings.

## 3.4.6 VEHICLE FORENSICS TOOLS

Vehicle forensic technologies that are connected with cutting-EdgeAI devices have emerged as vital assets in the field of DFI. These sophisticated techniques enable detectives to extract, analyse, and interpret key information from cars in criminal activity by combining the capabilities of EdgeAI and wearable devices [32].

This connection enables real-time analysis, minimizing the need for time-consuming data transfers and shortening investigation times. These EdgeAI-enabled forensic technologies can quickly spot patterns, abnormalities, and suspicious activity, allowing forensic specialists to unveil evidence related to crimes, accidents, or cybersecurity breaches. Furthermore, these instruments provide expanded capabilities to safeguard licence plate recognition, vehicle identification, and driving analysis, which enhances the investigation process.

These EdgeAI devices safeguard the privacy and integrity of sensitive information by processing data locally and securely, maintaining the chain of custody in a digital forensic engine. The integration of vehicle forensic tools and EdgeAI devices represents a significant step forward in DFI, providing investigators with efficient and effective tools to navigate the complex landscape of vehicle data analysis while ensuring the accuracy and reliability of findings.

## 3.4.7 FACIAL RECOGNITION SYSTEMS

Facial recognition systems embedded in EdgeAI devices have emerged as vital tools in the field of DFI, transforming how investigators study and identify individuals in criminal activity. These modern technologies use AI algorithms to quickly and reliably match traits acquired from a variety of sources, such as surveillance film or photographs obtained from social media sites [33].
