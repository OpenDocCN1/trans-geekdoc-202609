# Incident Response and Investigation
1. Digital Evidence Collection and Preservation.
2. Analysis of system logs, network traffic and metadata.
3. Recovery of deleted files and artifacts.
4. Identification of malicious software and intrusion detection.
5. Reconstruction of digital events and timelines.

# Data and Security Breach Analysis
1. Investigation of data breaches and unauthorised access incidents.
2. Identification of compromised systems and vulnerable entry points.
3. Analysis of system vulnerabilities and patch management.
4. Examination of network traffic for signs of intrusions.
5. Forensic Analysis of stolen or leaked data.

# Applications

# Mobile and Social Media Forensics
1. Extraction and analysis of data from mobile devices.
2. Recovery of deleted messages and images.
3. Tracking of online activities and social media profiles.
4. Analysis of geolocation data and call records.
5. Identification of digital evidences related to cyberbullying or harassment.

# Intellectual Property and Trade Secret Protection
1. Analysis of digital counterfeits for IP theft and industrial espionage.
2. Detection of unauthorized access to networks.
3. Examination of data transfers and file sharing activities.
4. Recovery of encrypted or decrypted intellectual property.
5. Digital watermarking and tamper detection techniques.

# Cybercrime and Fraud Detection
1. Identification and tracking of cybercriminals.
2. Analysis of financial transactions and digital payments.
3. Detection of online fraud, phishing, and identity theft.
4. Examination of email headers and metadata for evidence.
5. Forensic analysis of malware and ransomware attacks.
