# 4.1 INTRODUCTION

Data analysis is increasingly using edge and fog computing, which adds value to these latency-sensitive applications. Using deep learning, machine learning, and streaming data analytics to solve challenges Edge artificial intelligence (AI), a new transdisciplinary technology that enables distributed intelligence via edge devices was born out of edge data [1–5]. The study of EdgeAI and its commercial applications are still in its infancy in order to provide its residents with improved services while also making sure that resources, physical infrastructure, and commercial infrastructure are used effectively and efficiently. A concept known as the Internet of Things, or IoT for short, enables connectivity between people and technological equipment via the Internet. A smart city needs solutions for the integration and administration of this concept. These devices can communicate with one another, including smart homes, smart businesses, intelligent autos, and smart transit [6–9]. For many industries, the IoT offers a wide variety of solutions that can effectively and efficiently improve their productivity. The IoT has numerous benefits, but also carries drawbacks, including centralized data analytics, lack connectivity options, information security risks, and other hardware limitations. With the help of blockchain (BC) technology, a dispersed, secure, and decentralized network is made possible. AI is a recent technological development that has proven to be one of the most difficult. The interest in AI to urban planning is increasing daily. Smart city regions are anticipated to manage rising urbanization, energy use, and green condition in order to raise the financial and expectations [10–15]. By combining BC with AI and Machine Learning, a decentralized network is created that helps eliminate security concerns in future transportation, paving the way for safe, secure, and environmentally friendly travel with a surveillance system in a smart city and highlighting the value of human life [16–21]. BC and AI technologies work long-term to revolutionize the architecture of smart cities and build-term ecosystems for IoT applications. These technological and scientific developments, however, also offer opportunities and challenges for developing long-lasting IoT applications.

In order to take advantage of the opportunities that urbanization presents, many countries have created projects to transform their towns into smart cities.
