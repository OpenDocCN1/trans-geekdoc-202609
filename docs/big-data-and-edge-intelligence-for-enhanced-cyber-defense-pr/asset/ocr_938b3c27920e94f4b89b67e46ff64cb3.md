# 7.2 INTERNET OF THINGS ATTACKS, VULNERABILITIES, AND THREATS

For a number of reasons, IoT systems are more vulnerable to security breaches than traditional computing systems [27, 28]. First, there is a great deal of diversity in IoT systems in terms of devices, platforms, communication methods, and protocols. Second, IoT systems consist of "things" that aren't intended to be online, such as control devices that connect physical systems. But because people and devices are mobile, there are no clear boundaries in IoT systems; they are always changing. Therefore, all or a portion of IoT systems would be physically unsafe. Because IoT devices have limited energy, it is typically difficult to implement sophisticated security tools and approaches on IoT devices [29].

Hundreds of nodes with specialized tasks, such as monitoring heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems, are frequently found in an IoT-based network. Sensors and control use various network protocols such Bluetooth, WiFi, and ZigBee for communication [27]. An IoT gateway connects to the Internet, addressing privacy and security concerns at each layer of standards, services, and technologies. The IoT-based environment, while presenting security concerns similar to the Internet, cloud, and mobile networks, have distinct characteristics and security controls. The devices can share data, but they also have limits in computing capacity and can connect to numerous normed IoT devices [28].
