# Internet of Things Intrusion Detection System

One approach involves supervised learning algorithms where the AI model is trained on labeled datasets containing normal and malicious network traffic patterns. Through this, the AI learns to recognize patterns associated with various types of cyber threats, allowing it to identify and flag suspicious activities in real time. Additionally, unsupervised learning techniques are employed to detect anomalies within the network traffic without the need for predefined labels. These algorithms can identify deviations from normal behavior, signaling potential intrusions or security breaches [87].

AI-based IDS continuously adapt and improve their detection capabilities over time [72]. They can evolve their detection mechanisms by learning from new data and adjusting their algorithms to recognize emerging threats, thereby enhancing the overall security posture of IoT systems. Furthermore, AI swift swipes respond to security incidents by isolating affected devices or blocking suspicious network traffic, minimizing the impact of potential cyberattacks on IoT networks [56]. AI-powered NIDs for IoT systems employ ML algorithms, both supervised and unsupervised, to analyze network traffic and identify security threats or anomalies. These systems continuously learn and adapt, improving their ability to detect and respond to emerging threats in real time, fortifying the security infrastructure of interconnected IoT devices [35].
