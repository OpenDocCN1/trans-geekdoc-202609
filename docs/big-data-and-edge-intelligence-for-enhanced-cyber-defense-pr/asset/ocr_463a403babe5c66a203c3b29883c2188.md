# 4.6 CONCLUSION

An interconnected BC and AI-based architecture for IoT applications was suggested in this chapter. The goal of this study was to enable safe and scalable IoT transactions at the device, fog, and edge levels. Analysis of the performance of the proposed design took account both types of evaluations. Standard taxonomy was made available to BC-oriented AI and AI-oriented BC for qualitative measurement. A city’s operations can be greatly enhanced by the complementary technologies of AI and BC. The initial concepts for combining BC and AI on data analysis. Scalable anded database are made available by BC for the purpose of data collection and analysis for AI. BC are secure and unchanged even in the event of a failure. BC consensus mechanisms increase transparency and lower the danger of manipulation. They enhance stakeholders' willingness to engage as a result strengthening social projects. Intelligent agents will be used to assist people, make judgments about operations, and manage traffic in urban areas. The integration of AI and BC still offers several challenges despite their huge potential. One of the concerns is the issue of privacy, especially when it comes to extremely sensitive data and pertinent laws. Future study can improve the recommended design by utilizing techniques like feature extraction and extension to address the categorization difficulty.
