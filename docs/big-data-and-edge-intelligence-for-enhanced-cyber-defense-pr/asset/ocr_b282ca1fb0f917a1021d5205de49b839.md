# 3.4.2 PORTABLE EdgeAI FORENSIC WORKSTATIONS

By introducing EdgeAI devices into investigative processes, PDFWs have changed the area of DFI. These high-tech workstations are outfitted with cutting-edge hardware and software, allowing forensic evidence to analyse, and interpret digital evidence with unprecedented speed and accuracy. EdgeAI devices with PDFWs enables investigators to undertake real-time data processing and analysis at the crisis scene, avoiding the need for time-consuming tasks to centralized forensic laboratories.

PDFWs use the power of EdgeAI devices, as small but powerful neural units (NPUs) and specialized hardware accelerators, to accomplish tasks like image and video recognition, text extraction, and voice analysis. These machines can analyse amounts of data quickly, allowing forensic key evidence among the digital clutter. Furthermore, PDFWs are portable, can transport these sophisticated tools immediately to the scene of the crime, saving significant time and resources.

EdgeAI integration with PDFWs also provides security capabilities, safeguarding data during the forensic process. Advanced encryption methods, secure boot processes, and separate memory areas protect the evidence's integrity and secrecy. Furthermore, PDFWs firstly tamper-evident seals and hardware-based write-blockers to ensure that digital evidence is preserved in its original state.

PDFWs' capabilities go beyond data extraction and processing. In addition, they offer a comprehensive suite of forensic tools designed exclusively for digital investigations. Data recovery, password cracking, network analysis, and virus identification. are all tasks that these technologies help forensic investigators with. PDFWs enable investigators to tackle complicated cases more efficiently and effectively by combining the capabilities of EdgeAI devices with these software tools.
