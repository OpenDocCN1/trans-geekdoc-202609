Comparing the while loop and the for loop

Although the while and for loops have different syntaxtes, they both fulfill the same role, namely to repeat the execution of a block of code as long as a given condition is true. However, in some cases, one may be preferred to the other, depending on the context.

In general, the for loop is used more frequently than the while loop, but this can also depend on the preferences and habits of individual developers.
