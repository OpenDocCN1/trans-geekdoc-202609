# 1.3.3 InfraGard.¹⁸

InfraGard is a nationwide program in the United States that brings together representatives from information technology departments in industry and academia for information sharing and analysis, especially to help protect critical infrastructure against cyberattacks and also to support the FBI in its cybercrime investigations and education projects.¹⁹
