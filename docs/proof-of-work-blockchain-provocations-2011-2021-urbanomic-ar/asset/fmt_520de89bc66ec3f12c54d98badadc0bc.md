
# 区块链术语词汇表

## Proof of Stake（权益证明）
An alternative to Proof of Work that uses the ‘staking’ of large amounts of a blockchain’s cryptocurrency to establish who gets to mine the next block. This uses a lot less energy than Proof of Work.

## Proof of Work（工作量证明）
Performing a computationally-intensive task to prove that the accompanying data is being provided in good faith. Originally developed to prevent email spam. Bitcoin and other early cryptocurrencies use it to secure their blockchains by making it costlier to attack the chain than it is to extend it properly.

## Public-Key Cryptography（公钥加密）
The use of two separate but mathematically entangled encryption keys, one shared publicly and one kept private, when encrypting or signing messages. Knowledge of the public key does not give anyone any knowledge of the private key, so the public key can be shared to allow messages to be encrypted to its owner and to check cryptographic signatures from them without this being a security risk.

## Pump（拉盘）
To artificially inflate the price of a coin or token through nefarious means before dumping it.

## Rare Art（稀有艺术）
An early name for NFT-based digital image art.

## Rare Pepes（稀有佩佩）
An early and deeply ironic (quasi-)NFT project based on the CounterParty blockchain, featuring images of ‘Pepe The Frog’. Rare Art stripped out the irony and moved to Ethereum from CounterParty, leaving a historical gap.

## Scam（诈骗）
A scam or, if you are being accused of one, simply anything your accuser disagrees with or dislikes.

## Schelling Point（谢林点）
A ‘focal point’ value that people might be expected to arrive at independently. Named after the economist, not the philosopher.

## SHA-256
A popular cryptographic hash algorithm. A ‘salted’ SHA-256 hash adds extra information to the data to be hashed in order to make it harder to guess from the resulting hash value—this is useful for passwords, for example.

## Shard（分片）
A smaller partition of a blockchain that is able to communicate with other shards while not having to process all their transactions. This is one way of increasing the number of txes a blockchain can process.

## Shibe（柴犬）
From ‘Shiba Inu’, the Dogecoin mascot. A Dogecoin hodler, an excellent person. Like ‘comrade’ or ‘choombatta’.

## Shitcoin（垃圾币）
A minor cryptocurrency that offers nothing compelling. Possibly a scam.

## Sidechain（侧链）
A smaller blockchain attached to a larger one but isolated from it by different rules.

## Side Channel Attack（侧信道攻击）
Using information leaked through contingent sources to break an encryption algorithm or extract secret information from a system. Timing of network responses, electromagnetic or loudspeaker noise, etc.

## Smart Contract（智能合约）
A program stored on the blockchain that executes in response to blockchain txes.

## Techno-libertarianism（技术自由意志主义）
The belief that in order to maximise its liberatory potential, technology should not be restricted by the state.

## Token（代币）
A cryptographic token built on top of a cryptocurrency blockchain. The representational proxy for an identifiable and quantifiable ownership claim on some other value or object, real or imagined.

## Trusted Third Party（可信第三方）
An intermediary that can interfere in a social relationship between peers and must therefore be trusted not to.

## Tx, Txes（交易）
A blockchain transaction or transactions.

## Wallet（钱包）
A piece of software, hardware, or paper used to secure cryptocurrency account keys.

## Web 2.0
The social media platform and ecommerce era of the internet. Contrast with Web 1.0, which was more about homepages. And Web 3.0, which is about blockchain-backed identity and commerce.

## Wei（微以太）
The smallest unit of the Ether cryptocurrency, equivalent to a Satoshi for Bitcoin. Named after the cypherpunk cryptographer Wei Dai.

## Whale（巨鲸）
A hodler of very large amounts of cryptocurrency or NFTs.

## Wow（哇）
What a doge says when it thinks something is amaze.

## Wrapped（封装代币）
A fungible token that can be exchanged for a specific kind of NFT, effectively wrapping the NFT in a currency representing it.

## Zero-knowledge Proof（零知识证明）
A family of algorithms for encoding and demonstrating that you know a piece of information without having to reveal it or anything else that it describes.

## Zk-SNARKs
‘Zero Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Argument of Knowledge’, an algorithm that allows the existence of secret knowledge to be proven without revealing its content.

# 推荐阅读

For those wishing to explore blockchain technology, the following books are recommended reading:

- B. Vickers (ed.), *The White Paper by Satoshi Nakamoto* (London: Ignota, 2019).
- F. Brunton, *Digital Cash: The Unknown History of the Anarchists, Utopians, and Technologists Who Created Cryptocurrency* (Princeton, NJ: Princeton University Press, 2020).
- A.M. Antonopoulos, *The Internet of Money* (Merkle Bloom, 3 vols, 2017).
- A. Narayanan, J. Bonneau, E. Felten, A. Miller, S. Goldfeder, *Bitcoin and Cryptocurrency Technologies: A Comprehensive Introduction* (Princeton, NJ: Princeton University Press, 2016).
- R. Catlow and P. Rafferty (eds), *Radical Friends: Decentralised Autonomous Organisations and the Arts* (Torque Editions, 2022).
- R. Catlow, M. Garrett, S. Skinner and N. Jones (eds), *Artists Re: Thinking the Blockchain* (London: Furtherfield, 2017) [Contains earlier versions of ‘Blockchain Poetics’ and ‘Bad Shibe’.]
