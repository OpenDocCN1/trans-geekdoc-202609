# Horses (Worldbuilding Tweets)

The RAFFLE Act (Retreat Away From Flood Level Elevations) mandated withdrawing from rather than fighting rising sea levels. It bankrupted entire cities and made landowners in the ‘RAFFLE Belt’ into trillionaires. As a legal dodge, all property by the sea was declared to be worth $1.

These properties were purchased by funds that mixed them with property far inland and sold them as bundles, showing a massive profit. Until various DAOs bought the bundles using flash loans, broke them up, and sold the prime land for an even higher profit.

The DAOs held on to the junk properties, ostensibly to save the gas fees (which were more than the properties were worth). But then asteroid mining started. Asteroids were aerobraked onto the planet and mined by cheap human labour rather than expensive offworld robots.

Aerobraking a planet-smasher dumps ridiculous amounts of ash, soot, and grit into the atmosphere. Which reduces the sunlight that hits the surface of Earth. Which cools it. Suddenly the oceans weren’t going to rise as high as expected.

So the DAOs holding junk properties were suddenly quintillionaires. They cut up submerged buildings and glued them together on the new high ground and undercut RAFFLE Belt landlords. The RAFFLE Zones became vibrant economic and cultural hubs.
