Schematic representation of previous works on using machine learning (ML) for mechanistic study. (a) using multi-head deep neural networks (Multihead DNN) to extract kinetic rate constant and equilibrium constant given a pre-determined CE voltammograms where a single-electron quasi-reversible charge transfer (E step) follows a reversible solution reaction (C step). Every 100 data points are extracted for the forward and backward scan at 1, 2, 3, ..., and 100% of peak current, which is defined as the maximum or minimum baseline-corrected current. kf, kinetic rate constant in the forward direction of the C step; Keq, equilibrium constant of the C step. Simulated voltammograms are digitized into 100 × 100-pixel gray images Adapted with permission from Ref. [11]. Copyright 2019 American Chemical Society. (c) the procedure using residual neural network (ResNet) deep learning (DL) model to categorize five mechanisms and evaluate importance via model's "attention": a E mechanism with a single-electron quasi-reversible charge transfer step (E step), a EC mechanism in which the E step is followed by a reversible solution reaction (C step), a CE mechanism in which the E step is preceded by a C step, a ECE mechanism in which two E steps connected by an irreversible rate-limiting C step with the second E step being more thermodynamically facile than the first one, and a DISP1 mechanism where the E step is followed by an irreversible C step with the product from the C step undergoing an irreversible disproportionation reaction.
