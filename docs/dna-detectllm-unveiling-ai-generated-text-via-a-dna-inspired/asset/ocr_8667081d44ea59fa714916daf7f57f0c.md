Such complexity hinders traditional training-free methods that rely on fixed statistical scores. In contrast, DNA-DetectLLM appropriately computes repair scores for intricately constructed input texts by aligning them with their respective ideal AI-generated sequences. This flexible repair-based scoring allows for more accurate detection under distributional overlap and ambiguous cases, underscoring the practical utility of DNA-DetectLLM in complex detection scenarios.
