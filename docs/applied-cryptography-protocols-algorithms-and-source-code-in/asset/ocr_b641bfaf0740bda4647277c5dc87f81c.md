# Secret Sharing with Cheaters

There are many ways to cheat with a threshold scheme. Here are just a few of them. Scenario 1: Colonels Alice, Bob, and Carol are in a bunker below some isolated field. One day, they get a coded message from the president: "Launch the misses. We are going to eradicate the last vestiges of neural network research in the country." Alice, Bob and Carol reveal their shadows, Carol enters a number. She is actually a pacifist and dont the misses launches. Alice doesn't enter the correct secret, the secret they recover is the wrong secret.

Scenario 2: Colonels Alice and Bob are sitting in the bunker with Mallory. Mallory has disguised himself as a colonel and none of the others is the wiser. The same message comes from the president, and everyone reveals their shadows. "Bwa-ha-ha!" shouts Mallory. "I faked message from the president. Now I know all of your shadows." Races up the staircase and escapes before anyone can catch him.

Scenario 3: Colonels Alice, Bob and are sitting in the bunker with Mallory, who is again disguised. Mallory doesn't have a valid shadow." The same message comes from the president and everyone reveals their shadows. Mallory reveals his shadow only after he has heard the three. Since only three shadows are needed to reconstruct the secret, he can quickly create a valid shadow and reveals that. Now, not does he know the secret, but no one realizes that isn't part of the scheme.

Some protocols that handle these sorts of cheaters are discussed in Section 23.2.

# Secret Sharing Without Trent

A bank wants its vault to open only three out of five officers their keys. This sounds like a basic [3,5]-threshold, but there's a catch. No one is to know the entire secret. There is no Trent to divide the secret into five pieces. There are protocols by which the five officers can create a secret and get each a piece, such that none of the officers knows the secret until they reconstruct it. I'm not going to discuss these protocols in this book; see [756] for details.
