Security is only as strong as the weakest link, and the further away you get from the mathematics, the weaker the links become.

The 1995 world of Applied Cryptography, Second Edition, was very different from today's world. That was a singular time in academic cryptography, when I was able to survey the entire field of research and put everything under one cover. Today, there's too much, and the task of compiling it is just too great. For those who want a more current book, I recommend Cryptography Engineering, which I wrote in 2010 with Niels Ferguson and Tadayoshi Kohno. But for a review of those heady times of the mid-1990s, and an introduction to what has become an essential technology of the Internet, Applied Cryptography still holds up surprisingly well.
