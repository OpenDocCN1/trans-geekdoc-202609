# About the Author

BRUCE SCHNEIER is an internationally renowned security technologist, called a “security guru” by The Economist. He is the author of twelve books — including his seminal work, Applied Cryptography: Protocols, Algorithms, and Source Code in C, and Secrets & Lies: Digital Security in a Networked World which has become a classic as well as hundreds of articles, essays, and academic papers. His newsletter “Crypto-Gram” and blog “Schneier on Security” are read by over 250,000 people. You can read his blog, essays, and academic papers at www.schneier.com. He tweets at @schneierblog.
