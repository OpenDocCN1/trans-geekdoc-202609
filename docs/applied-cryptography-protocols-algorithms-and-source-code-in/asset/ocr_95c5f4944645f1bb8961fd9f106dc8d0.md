# CHAPTER 5 Advanced Protocols

# The Mafia Fraud

When discussing his zero-knowledge identification protocol, Adi Shamir [1424] said: "I could go to a Mafia-owned store million successive times and they will still not be able to misrepresent themselves as me."

Here's how the Mafia can. Alice is eating at Bob's Diner, a Mafia-owned restaurant. Carol is shopping at Dave's Emporium, an expensive jewelry store. Bob and Carol are both members of the Mafia and are communicating by a secret radio link. Alice and Dave are unaware of the fraud.

At the end of Alice's meal, when she is ready to pay and prove her identity to Bob, Bob signals Carol that the fraud is ready to begin. Carol chooses some diamonds, gets ready to prove her identity to Dave. Now, as Alice proves her identity to Bob, radio and Carol perform the same protocol with Dave. When Dave asks a question in the protocol, Carol radios the question back to Bob, and asks it of Alice. When Alice answers, Bob radios the correct answer to Carol. Actually, Alice is proving her identity to Dave, and Carol are simply sitting in the middle of the protocol passing messages back and forth. When the protocol finishes, Alice has proved herself to Dave and has purchased some expensive diamonds (which Carol displays).

# The Terrorist Fraud

If Alice is willing with Carol, they can also defraud Dave. In this protocol, Carol is a well-known terrorist. Alice is helping her into the country. Dave is the immigration officer. Alice and Carol are connected by a secret radio link.

When Dave asks questions as part of the zero-knowledge protocol, Carol radios them back to Alice, who answers herself. Carol recites these answers to Dave. In reality, Alice is proving her identity to Dave, with Carol acting as a communications path. When the protocol finishes, Dave thinks that Carol is Alice and lets her into the country. Three days later, Carol shows up at some government building with a minivan full of explosives.

# Suggested Solutions

Both the Mafia and Terror frauds are possible because the conspirators communicate via a secret radio. One way to this requires all identifications to take place inside Faraday cages, which block all electromagnetic radiation. In the terrorist example, this assures immigration officer Dave that Carol was not receiving answers from Alice. In the Mafia example, Bob could build a simple Faraday cage in his house, but Dave and Carol would not be able to communicate. To solve the Chess Grandmaster Problem, Bob and Carol would be forced to sit in her seat until the end of a game.

Thomas Beth and Yvo Desmed proposed another solution, using accurate clocks [148]. If each step in the protocol must take a place at a time, one would be available for the conspirators to communicate. In the Chess Grandmaster Problem, if every move in each game must be made as a clock strikes one minute, then Alice will have no time to run from room to room. In the Mafia story, Bob and Carol will have no time to pass questions and answers to one another.
