# Practice Question 1 Explanation

A. is incorrect because social engineering is not a virus or a form of malware, so an antivirus product will have no effect on the problem.

B. is correct because social engineering is the practice of obtaining confidential data by contacting users and impersonating someone with a legitimate need for data.
