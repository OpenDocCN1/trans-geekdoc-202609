# Practice Question 2

Alice is working the IT help desk when she receives a call from the Director of Human Resources, informing that an employee had just been terminated and had to be escorted out of the building by Security. The director instructed her to disable all of the employee's accounts, change the passwords for all network devices to which the employee had access, and have the data center rekeyed. Alice was surprised to receive these instructions because company employees had been terminated before without these precautions.

Which of the following terms best describes the director's specific concern in asking Alice to do these things?

+   A. Social engineering
B. Internal threats
C. War driving
D. External threats
