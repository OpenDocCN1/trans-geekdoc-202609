Data centers can range in size from a dedicated room in a company office building to gigantic underground complexes with armed guards and vault doors. Whatever its size, however, a data center needs certain characteristics to function efficiently, including environmental controls, a stable power source, and most important, a carefully designed internal architecture that implements all of the necessary equipment connections in a manner that is organized, understandable, and easily serviceable.
