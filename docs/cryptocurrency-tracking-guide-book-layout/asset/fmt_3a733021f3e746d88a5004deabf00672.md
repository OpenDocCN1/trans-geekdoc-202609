
# HODL

The abbreviation or acronym stands for Hold On to Dear Life or a misspelling of the word HOLD. The story behind the misspelled HOLD by a crypto investor by the name GameKyuubi posted on the Bitcoin talk forum on the 18th of Dec 2013. He stated that due to him being drunk and being bad at investing he decided to just hold his bitcoin and not sell which was the reason why he ended up with the misspelling of the word on his post.

# Hopium

It is the combination of two words Hope and Opium. Hopium is often used on social media platforms like Reddit, Telegram etc. by crypto investors to show tremendous hope and uncontrollable feeling of a positive outlook of the future. In layman's terms this is referring to someone getting high on hope.

# I

# ICO

The abbreviation or acronym stands for Initial Coin Offering. The term is copied from the stock market for an Initial Public Offering IPO. In the cryptoverse it is when a new project is offering its coins or tokens to raise funds to help kick start capital for the company. The coins can be a representation of holding a stock or stake in the new project.

# Immutable

In the cryptoverse this means once the code is deployed it cannot be changed. All transactions on the distributed ledger are verified, confirmed and secured on the blockchain and they are unchangeable. This is why blockchain technology proves to be the evolution in solving the trust code.

# Impermanent Loss

This is a temporary permanent loss usually refers to Defi and Liquidity Pool providers. Take for example the Defi Uniswap protocol where anyone can exchange and swap coins or tokens. In order for this to happen there is AMM Liquidity Pools that makes these swaps and exchanges possible which in turn changes the ratios of each coin in the pools.

The changes can go up or go down causing the total amount of coins to change which also changes the total amount of your provided LP to change. If it goes down in comparison to the entry amount that means you have experienced impermanent loss if you withdraw your LP but if you kept it in the LP there is no impermanent loss.

# Jager

# K

# KYC

This abbreviation or acronym stands for K now Y our C lient. It is the verification process used to identify if this is a real human and truthful owner of the crypto wallet that is sending or receiving the cryptocurrency. Mainly applies to most Central Exchanges which helps the investors from being scammed and also protects the exchanges from getting shut down for not following its host governments regulations.

In the cryptoverse it goes hand in hand with doxing the project founders and cofounders as a verification process to validate they are the true humans behind the project. This is a very important process for any business and also in the cryptoverse as it grows because of little to no regulations governing these new asset platforms. A great assurance for the investors and the cryptoverse as a whole.

# L

# Light Node

A light node requires less memory to work and does not store the complete copy of the blockchain. Light Nodes can be run on devices like smartphones and are usually dependent on the full node to run.

# Litecoin

Litecoin is an altcoin and fork of the original bitcoin code. Developed by Charlie Lee an MIT grad and former Google engineer in 2011. Litecoin has a maximum amount of 84 million coins in circulation and its mining block is considered to be faster than that of Bitcoin. Litecoin as it came into the cryptoverse was the silver standard coin in comparison to Bitcoin as the gold standard.

# M

# Market Cap

A Market Cap or Market Capitalization or MCAP is the total number of circulating coin supply in the market multiplied by its valued price. This is a great way to compare which altcoin project has more exposure and value than others.

# Meme Coins

These are altcoins that came about the Cryptoverse as a joke and was based on the Meme culture and movement. One of the most popular of the meme coins was Dogecoin which Elon Musk himself tweets about on social media. The majority of these meme coins will have a picture of a Shiba dog, cats or any sort of animal that summons a great following on social media.

# Mempool

This acronym stands for Memory pool. This is where all the unconfirmed transactions are located and have not been added to the block by the miners. If the transactions remain unconfirmed in the mempool for a long time that means the miners did not accept it due to low fees setup.

# Merkel Root

This is the hash of all the transactions within the block.

# Message

This is the input of the computational process as in the hash function.

# Message Digest

This is the output of the computational process as in the output of the hash function.

# Miners

These are the full nodes or individual computers that mine the blocks. These individual computers verify the transactions and are rewarded for their block verification which is then added to the blockchain.

# Mining

It is the POW or Proof of Work mining process that utilizes the individual computing power through hashing which verifies transactions and adds blocks to the blockchain.

# Mining Difficulty

This is the measurement of how difficult it is for any individual computer to mine a block within the Proof of Work system.

# Minning Rig

This can be a regular gamers computer used to mine cryptocurrency. Mining rigs are specifically designed to hold more than one graphics card that are powerful enough to mine blocks for Bitcoins and all other altcoins that rely on Proof of Work consensus.

# Mooning

This is the call by many cryptocurrency investors on social media platforms indicating a rapid price increase in a specific altcoin or meme coin project. It is also related to saying “ To the Moon” which means this coin will be flying straight to the moon.

# Multisig

This is a combination of two words, Multi and Sig. Multi comes from the original word multiple meaning many and sig comes from the word signature. It means multiple signatures in the cryptoverse. This is used as a security measure which needs multiple signatures in order to access the funds within the wallet. It assures the community that the funds are safe from having only one person controlling all of it.

# NaaS

The abbreviation or acronym stands for Node as a Service.

# NFT

The abbreviation or acronym stands for Non Fungible Token. These are very unique cryptographic tokens coded on the blockchain which are used to tokenize digital art work and many more real world tangible properties or assets. Due to its cryptographic uniqueness each NFT code is never identical to one another which is used to prove ownership for a digital asset on the blockchain.

Non Fungible Tokens is a game changer for the way the world does business in the field of selling, buying and holding assets like art work, real estate properties and anything tangible properties that have value. All of this real world tangible assets will be represented by a secured digital and immutable smart contract on the blockchain as an authentic digital certificate.

# Node

Nodes can also be called a Master Node, Full Node or a Light Node. A node is a computer that is connected to the cryptoverse and helps secure the blockchain. This computer is connected to the internet with enough memory to support and run a complete copy of the blockchain software code.

The complete copy meaning it consists of the Block Header and Block transactions. They verify every transaction throughout the network by enforcing the consensus rules and also validate each block before they chain it together.
