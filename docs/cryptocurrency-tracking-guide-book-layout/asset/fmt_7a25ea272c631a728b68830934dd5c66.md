## A

ADA: The Cardano Blockchain founded by Charles Hoskinson in 2015 a former co-founder of the Ethereum Blockchain is one of the largest Altcoin proof of stake protocols in the cryptoverse. Its native cryptocurrency is ADA named after a female mathematician Ada Lovelace from the 19th-century. She was also recognized as one of the pioneers of computer programming. One Ada equals one million Lovelace's.
