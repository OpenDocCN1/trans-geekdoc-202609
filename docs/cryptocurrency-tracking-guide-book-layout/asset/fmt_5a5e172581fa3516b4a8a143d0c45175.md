
# ADDRESS

An address is very similar to having a home address or email address. Since the inception of the internet 1.0 we started using email addresses to send and receive informational data online but now we are being introduced to cryptoverse addresses to send and receive cryptocurrencies. These addresses consist of alphanumeric characters and they are very specific to each blockchain and its native coins or tokens.

The majority of blockchain addresses are public and can be seen on the blockchain explorer confirming the transactions going in and out of any address. An exception to these public addresses are the privacy-based blockchains like Grin and Monero. It is impossible for any human to read, memorize and process the long random strings of alphanumeric lines of a blockchain address but rest assured that a computer has no problems processing it.

Addresses are unique and anonymous even though they are public, the owners of the addresses' real identification is never revealed. Although this case is true for the majority of people dealing with cryptocurrency, the real identity of founders and co-founders of major token projects and exchanges are actually revealed. One example is the founder of Ethereum Vitalik Buterin where his public address is known by all who work on the Ethereum Blockchain network.

# Airdrop

An airdrop is the term given to things like supplies or propaganda leaflets being dropped from the sky by a plane. The strategy is also used in the cryptoverse by a new cryptocurrency project for its native coins or tokens. It is electronically distributed to early adopters of the project to create visibility and buzz which in turn provide more demand by the mass. All you need to have is a compliant wallet which is capable of receiving the airdrop on the scheduled released time and date. You need to register for any airdrop.

# Altcoin

Alt-coin or Alternative coin is the name given to all other coins that came after Bitcoin. These coins are native to their own blockchain software code and they were soft fork or hard fork of Bitcoin in layman's terms they are copies of the original. Bitcoin Cash, Litecoin and Raven coin to name a few altcoins that were forked from the original bitcoin code. Other coins like Ethereum have their own code or blockchains which were created to solve some of the problems or issues that Bitcoin cannot solve.

One major improvement by the Ethereum Blockchain is the introduction of smart contracts and smart properties. Altcoins were born out of necessity to solve the problems other coins have like scalability to handle the need for speed to process a transaction and lessen the fees charged for such transactions. The popularity of meme culture, some coins were also produced just to poke fun against Bitcoin. One particular altcoin that gave birth to this meme culture is Dogecoin.

# ASIC

The abbreviation or acronym stands for Application-Specific Integrated Circuits. These specific integrated circuits are designed on silicon chips to perform only one special task and that is to mine and validate certain codes. In the case of Bitcoin the asic processes SHA-256 with regards to proof of work protocols to mine and validate every transaction.

# ASIC MINER

An asic miner is when all the specific integrated circuits are connected together with other hardware like power supply to make a special computer that does only one task and that is to mine for cryptocurrencies utilizing the proof of work protocols. These asic miners are connected to the internet and tend to make a lot of noise in comparison to a regular home computer when turned on. Because they run the proof of work protocols they tend to consume a tremendous amount of electricity energy.

# ATH

The abbreviation or acronym All Time High refers to how high a cryptocurrency’s value peaked according to its price and market capitalization. This usually happens when people FOMO into a new project speculating it will rocket to the moon and make them filthy rich. It's no longer a secret that a cryptocurrency will hit All Time High once it is listed to any of the top 5 exchanges in the cryptoverse.

# ATL

The abbreviation or acronym All Time Low refers to how low a cryptocurrency’s value dips according to its price and market capitalization. This usually happens when there is a panic sell amongst the investors and when certain influences of the news media, wall street's tend to leak into the cryptoverse. It is always safe to buy and hold rather than panic and sell at a loss.

# Apeing

They usually buy in without taking the time to do a thorough research or background check on the particular cryptocurrency project. It is the fastest way to get rekted and lose money in the cryptoverse. This is not investing, it's called gambling.

# API

The abbreviation or acronym stands for Application Programming Interface. This set of protocols are software applications programming interfaces. These application's protocols interact in a specific manner on how to use the data and what actions it needs to execute.

# AMM

The abbreviation or acronym stands for Automated Market Maker in Layman’s terms it is a software code utilized by an exchange protocol that operates autonomously on a decentralized exchange (DEX). An example of such Automated Market Maker is the Uniswap exchange launched in 2018.

# Bitcoin

Bit-coin is the first coin in the Cryptoverse created by Satoshi Nakamoto in 2009. This coin is the first crypto-currency native to the Bitcoin blockchain and is rewarded to the miners for verifying the cryptography code by solving a block. This open computer software code was developed by Satoshi Nakamoto to solve the problem of trust between peer to peer exchanges of money electronically. Bitcoin is a new technologically positive disruptor not only for the financial system but the tech world as a whole.

There are an estimated 21 million Bitcoins in the whole world and the year 2140 is predicted to be the year the last Bitcoin will be mined. Even though Bitcoin is a decade old this innovative tech is still in its infancy stage which puts you in a great position to learn, invest and create great wealth for you and your family. As of the publication of this book Bitcoin as many in the cryptoverse sees it as a great storage of value in comparison to precious metals like gold. Its value derives from its scarcity, supply and demand.

# Bitcoin Cash

An Altcoin and hard fork of the Bitcoin original code created in 2017 by some Developers (DEVs) and cryptocurrency miners. Bitcoin Cash was forked to solve the problems of block speed and scalability that Bitcoin original code lacks. BCH’s code increased the block sizes from 1MB to 8MB which was recently upgraded to 32MB in 2021 to adapt to an ever-increasing transaction speed and processing time.

This is one example of an Altcoin that was forked to address some of the problems that the original Bitcoin code Satoshi Nakamoto created. There are many other Altcoins that you will come across of similar stories as you dive deep into the cryptoverse.
