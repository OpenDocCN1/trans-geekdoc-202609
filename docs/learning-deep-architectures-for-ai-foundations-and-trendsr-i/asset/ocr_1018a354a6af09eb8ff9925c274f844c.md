# 6.1 Layer-Wise Training of Deep Belief Networks

A Deep Belief Network [73] with $\ell$ layers model the joint distribution between observed vector $x$ and $\ell$ hidden layers $\mathbf{h}^k$ as follows:

```
$P(x, \mathbf{h}^1, \dots, \mathbf{h}^l) = \prod_{k=0}^l P(\mathbf{h}^k|\mathbf{h}^{k+1}) P(\mathbf{h}^{k-1}, x), \quad (6.1)$
```

where $x=\mathbf{h}^0, P(\mathbf{h}^{k-1}|\mathbf{h}^k)$ is a visible-given-hidden conditional distribution in an RBM associated with level $k$ of the DBN, and $P(\mathbf{h}^{k-1},\mathbf{h}^k)$ is the joint distribution in the top-level RBM. This is illustrated in Figure 6.1.
