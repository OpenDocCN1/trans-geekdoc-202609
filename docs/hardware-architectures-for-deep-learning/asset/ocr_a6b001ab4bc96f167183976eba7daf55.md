# 1.5 Conclusion

In this chapter, an introduction to the NNs with emphasis on the classification and regression problems was presented. At first, natural and ANNs were introduced and then appropriate structures of ANNs in classification and regression problems were explained. Next, simple structure NNs and their restrictions were explained and then some developed multilayer and deep learning NNs were introduced. At the end, the emerging CNN model and some well-known CNN structures were explained briefly.
