As reported, increasing the number of processing cycles to nine cycles has raised the misclassification rates in the proposed hybrid design. An advantage of the proposed design compared to random bit-stream-based designs is that the results are deterministic. The produced outputs depend on the selected combination of Sobol sequences for the bit-stream generation set and therefore have a standard deviation of zero and are reproducible. ### 4.7 Summary

In this chapter, we proposed a low-cost and energy-efficient design for hardware implementation of CNNs. LD deterministic bit-streams and simple AND gates are used to perform fast and accurate multiplication in the first layer of the NN. Compared to random bit-stream-based designs, the proposed design achieves a lower misclassification rate for the same processing time. Evaluating LeNet5 NN with MNIST dataset as input, the proposed design achieved the same classification rate as the fixed-point with 70% saving in the energy consumption of the first layer. If accepting slight inaccuracies, higher energy savings are also feasible by processing shorter bit-streams.
