In the NeuRAM3 demonstrator, these are mapped on four different local arrays in the CxQuad board. A limited number of inter-connections are then only present in this ECG instantiation. But this global synaptic connectivity strongly increases with the application size and can take up significant area on the chip and also consume a major amount of energy. So, to deal with this undesired area and energy explosion, we have proposed new scalable dynamic segmented bus interconnect synaptic communication network for large-scale neuromorphic platforms with a circuit-switched data plane and a control plane based on full virtualization [34]. The network architecture comprises run-time-controlled segmented buses clusters. The segmented bus intersections are controlled by a lower power circuit switch implemented with TFTs. The selected TFT material exhibits ultralow-leakage power consumption. When scaled up, these devices reside between the metal layers in a many-layer-back (BEOL) metal connection stack [35]. The proposed 3D-VLSI implementation scheme with segmented buses based on the BEOL circuits implemented with TFT devices results in a compact low-power global synaptic communication network.

When we return to our ECG demonstrator, the cluster communication can be mapped and scheduled in several ways on a segmented bus for global synapse communication, as discussed in the general approach of [34].
