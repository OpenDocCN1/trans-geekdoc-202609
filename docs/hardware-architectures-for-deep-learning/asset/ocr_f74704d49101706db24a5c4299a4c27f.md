Figure 3.11 Throughput under practical applications on 128-node networks. The outcomes are normalized with Cmesh

In these experiments, we set the saturation latency to 50 cycles. As demonstrated in Figure 3.11, ClosNN delivers a higher throughput than H-NoC and Cmesh. The main reason behind this performance is that ClosNN offers a shorter average hop count and better support for collective communication, so it yields higher throughput compared to other two architectures. ClosNN can deliver up to four packets to all PEs per cycle, while H-NoC can deliver only a single packet to all PEs per cycle. Moreover, ClosNN's diameter is three hops, while H-NoC's diameter is five hops. Due to these topological advantages, ClosNN offers up to 2.4× higher throughput (average 1.93×) than H-NoC.

# 3.9.3 Power comparison

Figure 3.12 shows the power consumption of the three considered NoCs, when they work at the maximum throughput. Different benchmarks inject the same traffic rate to the networks at maximum throughput. Therefore, they show close power consumption profile and we just report the results of ECG analysis as a representative benchmark. As expected, because of shorter hop count, ClosNN consumes less power than the other two networks.
