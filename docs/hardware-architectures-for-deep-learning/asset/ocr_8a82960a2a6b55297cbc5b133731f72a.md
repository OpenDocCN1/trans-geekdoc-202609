Figure 9.17 shows the sensitivity of accuracy of MS-CNN to the bit-length of the parameters at each iteration. The optimal bit-length is derived from a saturation point, after which increased bit-length does not enhance the accuracy. As shown in Figure 9.17, this saturation point is pushed to lower bit-lengths for earlier iterations. Hence, for MS-CNN, rather than selecting an optimal bit-length for the entire network, subnetworks deploy different bit-lengths allowing further reduction in the number of parameters.
