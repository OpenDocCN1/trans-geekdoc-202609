In addition to the authors, another important co-citation analysis factor are journal sources. This form of analysis can provide perspective pertaining to the intellectual base of a specific knowledge domain. Consequently, the journal sources network depicted in Figure 9 contains just over 110 journals sources with equal or more than 25 citations.
