A population of negative (non-reactive) samples is instantiated by randomly generating 10 values in the range 0-0.99 (for the MR parameter) and 5-25 (for the FCN parameter). The positive (reactive) samples will range 0.2-0.5 and 15-35, respectively. The points are plotted and separated by a line representing the MR cut-off for the two populations. A shift in this cut-off will not alter the clustering of the data. The second panel of the plot draws instead an amplification profile of a reaction. The solid line depict a threshold level that intersect the curve at a given x value. Shifting the threshold (TH) causes a shift in the intersecting x value altering the results. The initial cut-offs are depicted by solid lines; shifting is represented by arrows; the resulting cut-offs are indicated by dashed lines. X-values are depicted by dotted lines.
