# Listing 2.3.

The cycles above the chosen cut-off of 1000 light units are retrieved from the fluorescence signal of the high-input sample; the first value of the series is saved in the variable crossOverH. A blow-up of the amplification profile of this sample is plotted together with the cut-off line. The amplification points before and after the cut-off line are also plotted, corresponding to crossOverH and crossOverH – 1. The Cq is visually determined on the intersection between the amplification curve and the cut-off line, and the value store in the variable cq_high. The procedure is repeated for the low-input sample. In the continuation of the listing, the copy numbers are calculated and the standard curve is plotted alongside the two samples. Arrows are used to show the interpolation that allows the quantification of the unknown samples.
