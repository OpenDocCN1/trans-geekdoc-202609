Figure 3.8. Validation of the SVM classification. Visual inspection of the results provided by SVM. Upper panel. The samples of the SIV set are shaded according to the classification provided by SVM: positive for reactive; negative for non-reactive. The reactions visualized in the lower panels are marked with a square and the identification number (index). The first panel (lower left) shows a sample of reactions from very low FCN. Note how they all have an amplification profile compatible with low template input, indicating that this group includes truly non-reactive reactions. All samples in this cluster have been identified as negative by the SVM classification. Conversely, the second panel shows how reactions from the central cluster display a reactive amplification profile. All samples in this cluster have been identified as positive by the SVM classification.
