# **3.12.3.2** Future research

Classification and regression algorithms have a wide range of possible applications in the future. Among these are creating reliable algorithms that can deal with noisy and missing data, creating advanced ensemble techniques, and figuring out how to understand the outcomes of ML models better. Additionally, research might look into combining neural networks with existing ML algorithms and applying unsupervised ML approaches for classification and regression problems.
