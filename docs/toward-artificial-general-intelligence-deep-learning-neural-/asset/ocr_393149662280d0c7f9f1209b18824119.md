# 9.3.4 Regulatory requirements and standards for AI transparency

Regulatory requirements and standards for AI transparency focus on establishing rules and frameworks that ensure that automated decisions made by an AI system are explainable and traceable. They are intended to protect the rights of individuals and businesses by ensuring that AI-driven decisions are transparent and accountable. Increasingly, regulatory bodies are taking a proactive role in promoting industry standards around AI transparency [8]. AI transparency requirements vary across jurisdictions, but certain key elements are commonly included: Disclosure of AI processes, limits on data usage and protection, safeguards to protect vulnerable populations and minorities, and ongoing monitoring of results. In some cases, these requirements can require specific measures such as establishing privacy impact assessments, creating public dispute resolution systems, and ensuring end-user access to algorithms and data. These measures help ensure that AI-driven decisions made by an entity are explainable to stakeholders, traceable to the data used, and auditable to ensure that algorithms are not biased or discriminatory.
