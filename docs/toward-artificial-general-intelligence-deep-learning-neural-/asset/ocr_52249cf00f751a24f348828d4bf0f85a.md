Ensuring that our model is not overfitting the data is a crucial fac- tor to consider while utilizing polynomial regression. Testing our model using cross-validation and leveraging techniques to prevent this is crucial. A potent method, polynomial regres- sion is beneficial when working with data that exhibits a nonlinear pattern.
