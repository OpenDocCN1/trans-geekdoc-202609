
The research ensures the confidentiality of each interviewee by coding them and not disclosing their personal information in the thesis. The data collection process took approximately one and a half weeks starting on October 11, 2023, and ending on October 20, 2023. All interviews were conducted in English at the agreed time via Microsoft Teams and Google Meet platforms.

## Data Analysis Method

Qualitative content analysis was used to systematically identify recurrent patterns in respondents’ answers. Respondents are coded as numbers from 1 to 10 (R1, R2, etc.) and their answers are provided in a question-respondent matrix. After organizing the data into a question-respondent matrix, segments that share similar thoughts were analyzed and the main ideas were summarized under the related topics. To get meaningful aspects from the answers provided, main concepts, patterns, and recurrent trends gained from the data were compared and systematically examined.

## Data Analysis and Research Results

The inclusion of a demographic table serves as a reference point, offering a visual representation of respondents’ diversity and providing a comprehensive overview of their characteristics (Table 2). Respondents were examined demographically and contacted before the interview to confirm if they belong to one of the age groups (A20-30 & A40-50). The division of age groups allows to compare the perspectives and behaviors of distinct generations. To get a bigger picture of the divergence of the respondents, their gender, residence, and occupation were also collected.

During the interview, their behavior with digital ads was also analyzed. The data collected showed that all of the respondents often engage with digital marketing campaigns, except for the R7 who engages only sometimes. Regarding the recent action made with the ad online, five of the respondents (R1, R2, R4, R8, R9) clicked on the ad while the other five (R3, R5, R6, R7, R10) bought something from the ad. ## Consumer Preferences

To better understand consumer preferences, the respondents were asked about the platforms they engage the most in and the elements that greatly attract them in digital marketing campaigns.

### Preferred Online Platforms

The main idea behind question 3 was to understand which online platforms can reach more people and what factors attract them to engage there. Most of the respondents engage in more than one platform depending on what they need at that specific time. Instagram is the most mentioned platform by eight respondents (R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R7, R9) as it is one of the most used platforms which makes it easy to communicate with friends. Seven respondents (R2, R3, R5, R7, R8, R9, R10) engage in Facebook platform because they can easily interact with friends there, the platform provides recommendations and is easy to use. Google was mentioned by two respondents (R2, R10) and they used it to search for something specific. LinkedIn was mentioned by R10 to find events or information related to the respondent’s job, Reddit was mentioned by R1 to find interesting information, and YouTube was mentioned by R5 to watch content made by foreigners.

The most mentioned elements were relevant message and design. Five respondents mentioned clarity; three focused on interactive content and the other three focused on simplicity. Subtlety, call-to-action button, and good-quality audio were mentioned two times each. Age group A20-30 puts an emphasis on the clarity of the ad, relevance, and design. They do not like too intrusive or pushy ads, mostly reacting to simple and minimalistic ads. Two respondents of this group reacted quite well to the good-quality audio that matches the ad. The younger age group also mentioned eye-catching elements (R2), diverse display formats (R3), and relevant landing pages (R5). Quite similarly, the age group A40-50 reacts best to ads that have relevant message, aesthetic design, and provide clear information. Two of the respondents in this group engage with interactive content like video or games. The older age group also mentioned the importance of the shown effect in the ads (R6) and the advantages of including social aspects in digital marketing campaigns (R10).

## Online Engagement Experiences

To get more insights into different online engagement experiences, respondents were asked about their perspectives on which of the experiences they respond to the most, does personalization contribute to increasing their engagement, and what multimedia components the ads should involve to improve those experiences.

### The Most Engaging Online Experiences

A deep understanding of the audience’s behavior helps to optimize the whole user experience and provide them with relevant content that is expressed as one of the most appealing elements of digital ads. Therefore, understanding to which engagement experiences people respond the most is of crucial importance (Question 5).

Table 4 shows the online engagement experiences that interviewees respond to the most. The most responsive online engagement experience is informational as it gives more details about the product and differentiates the brand from others (R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, R7, R8, R10). These interviewees react quite well to informative articles and believe that ads should provide clear benefits. Three of the respondents said that the most engaging experience for them is personal identity as it aligns customers’ values with the brand’s and focuses on personal perspective and content (R2, R6, R10). Three respondents also react to entertainment experience as interactive elements attract their attention such as videos, games, quizzes, audio, and humour elements (R3, R4, R9). All of the respondents in a group of A20-30 react to informational experience and two of them engage with entertainment one, while the group of A40-50 reacts mostly to informational and personal identity experiences.

To provide better engagement experiences, it is important to identify the most effective multimedia components in digital marketing campaigns. Table 5 provides those multimedia components that respondents find the most eye-catching and engaging. The most mentioned one is video ads, although R10 from age group A40-50 said that there is no time to see the videos, the other four respondents (R2, R3, R4, R7) find it very engaging. Three respondents (R3, R4, R5) from age group A20-30 mentioned the importance of the audio within the ads. For the age group A40-50 audio is not that important and text attracts them the most while colourful and creative visuals are also important for two respondents (R8, R10) within the group. Simple and limited multimedia components matter for two respondents (R1, R8), animated banner ads work for another two (R2, R6), and only one respondent R9 said that multimedia components are not important at all.
