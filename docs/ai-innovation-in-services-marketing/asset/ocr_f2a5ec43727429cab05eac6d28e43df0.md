# KEY TERMS AND DEFINITIONS

**Account-Based Marketing:** Is a highly targeted marketing strategy that focuses on tailors and customizes marketing efforts to specific individual accounts or a select group of high-value accounts rather than targeting a broad audience.

**Artificial Intelligence:** Is the simulation of human intelligence in machines, allowing them to perform various tasks.

**Case Study:** Is a research method that involves an in-depth examination and analysis of a particular individual, group, event, or situation.

**Coherent Communication:** Is the act of conveying information, ideas, or messages in a clear, logical, and organized manner to ensure that the recipient can easily understand and follow the intended message.

**Invest Lithuania:** Is the national agency responsible for attracting foreign investment to Lithuania and supporting businesses in the country.

**Personalization:** Is the practice of tailoring products, services, content, or experiences to individual preferences, needs, or characteristics.

**Storytelling:** Is the art of conveying a message or information through a narrative, often involving characters and emotions, to engage an audience.

**Synergy:** Refers to the combined or cooperative interaction of multiple elements, where the result is more significant or effective than each element's individual contributions or efforts on its own.
