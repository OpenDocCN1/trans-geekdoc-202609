# Analysis of Secondary Data

Secondary data analysis was performed using content analysis within NVivo, a technique with a history spanning more than 60 years that has found applications in various fields, including business (Neuendorf, 2017). Krippendorff (2018), a recognized authority in the field, defines content analysis as a method for drawing replicable and valid inferences from texts or other meaningful materials within their contexts of use. Content analysis is not limited to written text; it can encompass verbal, textual, visual, sound, print, and electronic forms (Bryman, 2016), including images (Bell et al., 2022).

When conducting a case study of an organization, content analysis primarily aims to scrutinize media items, various texts, and documents that are either produced by the organization, such as annual reports, or written about it, like articles in the business press (Bell et al., 2022). In this context, the researcher examined diverse content generated by Invest Lithuania, namely internal documents and reports related to previous marketing campaigns and their outcomes. Both sources are conceptually rich and offer valuable insights into the organization’s description, historical evolution, and the development and management of its core concepts.

# LIMITATIONS

The qualitative research approach has its limitations. According to Goulding (2002), qualitative research is often criticized for being purely descriptive, needing a more structured and straightforward procedure, and potentially producing subjective data that may not meet rigorous standards. Furthermore, concerns are raised about the need for more transparency in the evolving procedures and processes adopted during the research (Miles & Huberman, 1994). Additionally, as Denzin and Lincoln (2011) pointed out, the criteria for evaluating and interpreting qualitative research can be problematic and may lead to misinterpretation. This chapter addresses these limitations by explaining the rationale for selecting methods such as content analysis and detailing how they were employed.
