# THE POWER OF AI IN HEALTH

AI is the ability of machines to make intelligent decisions, beyond being a revolutionary innovation and a new way of programming things. The way brands and customers interact has been transformed thanks to AI, as it allows companies to focus on meeting customer needs in real time, which constitutes an advantage for marketing professionals who can focus more on their clients (Ameen et al., 2021).

AI can be employed in various marketing processes, empowering professionals in the field to reach their audience in different contexts. This application enables improving the effectiveness and productivity of systems and operations. By implementing AI, companies can connect more efficiently with their customers, as they can develop personalized and more engaging ads (Haleem et al., 2022). According to Dicuonzo et al. (2023), AI has great potential to improve health, enhance processes, reduce costs, improve people’s work efficiency, and ensure new mechanisms of growth.

Furthermore, there are tools belonging to AI that assist in the enhancement of this technology, whether in healthcare or in any other area, such as machine learning (ML), which is a field that allows computational systems to analyze, learn, and interpret data using algorithms and statistical methods. These algorithms can identify patterns and trends in information, learn from large datasets, and become an important ally in studies in the healthcare sector. Algorithms can also be trained to identify three important steps: diagnosis, prognosis, and treatment, for example, they can recognize groups or subgroups of people with similar symptoms, calculate the chances of recovery from these symptoms, and evaluate the best options for treating patients.
