The Generative AI CRM engine is responsible for managing all the channels that brands have to manage their relationships with their customers (e.g., chatbots, social networks). This engine is capable of personalizing interactions with each customer by taking advantage of customer input to understand their profile at all times. Generative AI plays a key role here due to its ability to constantly adapt the way in which customer interests are managed, combining them with business interests. The ability to perceive insights turns the vision of managing a brand and creating or modeling new or existing products into a linear process of managing interests where supply tends to adapt to demand in a “perfect” way.

This approach will contribute to generate in customers a greater sense of belonging to the company and a connection that induces in the customer a feeling of almost apparent control – the customers feels that they are the focus in a more tangible way.

Upstream of the proposed model is the business process management engine through generative AI. This component uses AI to obtain insights into the downstream functioning of the model (i.e., the customer interaction component), allowing the company’s strategic management level to evaluate and validate proposals for creating new brands and products. This component also allows you to generate AI-driven knowledge.

In this way, better, more effective and faster strategic decisions will be made, feeding and empowering top management tasks and marketers who will refine their strategies with greater probability of success.

However, the vision of a conceptual model that tends towards a state of perfect equilibrium is not without limitations.
