**Personal Identity:** Identification of positive role models or admired personalities in the media, which reinforces customers’ ideals. By being consistent with their values and beliefs, ads can foster a feeling of personal identity and involve affirmation of one’s core values. It is a process of connecting with significant people represented in the media, learning from positive role models, and developing an understanding of one’s character.

**Social Interaction:** Engaging advertising strategies promote social sharing and communication, which improve a person’s feeling of community belonging and interpersonal relationships. These strategies include social media engagement and online groups where people connect by sharing their own experiences with the brand.

**Entertainment:** Ads that are entertaining capture customers’ attention and make the ad experience more enjoyable through storytelling, videos, or interactive games. This engagement experience helps to deliver the brand’s message in a relaxing way and offers emotional satisfaction.

These days it is extremely difficult to engage with customers as they easily incorporate multiple digital platforms into their everyday lives. Thus, there is a need for a systematic method that includes different processes of communication into a cohesive campaign strategy (Sestino et al., 2023). In 2010, Dave Chaffey created a RACE framework which is a comprehensive model for businesses that helps to outline a strategy that addresses the issues of reaching and engaging their customers (Chaffey, 2023). The framework outlines planning, reaching, acting, converting, and engaging actions, and is completely consumer-focused. It offers a thorough guideline for boosting consumer engagement and assists marketers in understanding their customer base by analyzing the quickly changing digital environment (Sestino et al., 2023). Although it covers a variety of customer journey stages, it is vital to understand that each element is crucial in promoting consumer involvement. Engagement itself is an ongoing process that requires a clear foundation of understanding the audience, its behaviors, and preferences.
