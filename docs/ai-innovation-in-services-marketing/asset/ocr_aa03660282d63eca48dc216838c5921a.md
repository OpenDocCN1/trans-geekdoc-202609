The domain of influencer marketing is also experiencing a notable transformation through the integration of artificial intelligence (AI), as elucidated by the co-founder and CEO of SocialPubli, an acclaimed influencer marketing platform, Qudsi (2023). This innovative application of AI aids businesses in identifying the most suitable influencers for their marketing campaigns by analyzing social media content to assess the demographics, reach, and engagement levels of influencers. Such an approach enables marketers to optimize the impact of their influencer partnerships by aligning with individuals whose follower base mirrors their target audience closely. Moreover, influencers are leveraging AI to enhance their content creation process, employing AI algorithms for brainstorming content ideas and utilizing AI-powered tools for content generation, editing, and even background removal. This technological empowerment allows influencers to succinctly articulate their creative requirements, enabling AI to produce visually compelling graphics on their behalf. Artificial intelligence (AI) has also introduced virtual influencers, which compete with traditional human influencers and reshape digital marketing. Kim & Park (2024) highlight their growing popularity, emphasizing how these AI-created personas offer brands innovative engagement opportunities.

Concurrently, as the sophistication of AI applications in advertising escalates, the importance of ethical considerations and consumer privacy has become increasingly pronounced. The advertising industry is witnessing a heightened focus on transparency and consent in data collection practices, influenced by regulatory frameworks such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). These regulations are steering the development of AI systems towards prioritizing user privacy. Leading companies, including Apple, are accentuating privacy features within their advertising ecosystems as a strategy to foster consumer trust.

In summary, the landscape of AI applications in advertising is marked by rapid evolution and innovation, as evidenced by the current trends. Advertisers are harnessing the power of AI to craft more potent and personalized advertising strategies, spanning from hyper-personalization and conversational AI to the pivotal considerations of ethics and privacy. The real-world applications of these trends by industry leaders illustrate a shifting advertising paradigm, one that offers consumers a more customized and engaging experience.
