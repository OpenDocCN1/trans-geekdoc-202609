# INTRODUCTION

In the fast-paced and ever-evolving landscape of digital marketing, businesses are compelled to continuously refine their strategies to enhance engagement and thereby, the effectiveness of their marketing campaigns. In an age where consumer attention is fragmented across various digital platforms, engagement emerges as a crucial metric, influencing consumers’ buying decisions and brand loyalty. Engagement with digital ads is pivotal as it can directly influence consumer behavior and purchase decisions, making it essential for companies to harness effective strategies that resonate with their target audience (Araujo et al., 2020; Calder et al., 2009).

The digital environment is fiercely competitive, and success often hinges on a brand’s ability to engage effectively with its audience. According to recent studies, highly engaged consumers are more likely to buy, promote, and show loyalty to a brand, which underscores the importance of fine-tuning engagement tactics (Calder et al., 2009). Research further suggests that the quality of engagement can significantly affect a company’s bottom line, highlighting the direct correlation between consumer engagement and profitability (Chaffey, 2023).

Moreover, the technological advancements in digital marketing tools have introduced new methods of measuring and increasing engagement through data analytics and consumer insights, which allow marketers to tailor their approaches more precisely to consumer needs (Stolle, 2022). This technological pivot necessitates an ongoing examination of consumer behaviors and preferences to craft strategies that are not only effective but also adaptable to the rapid changes in digital marketing dynamics.

The chapter analyzes consumer preferences and provides recommendations for increasing customer engagement in digital marketing campaigns. Specifically, the research problem is to find what are the best practices for reaching the optimal solution that increases customer engagement in digital marketing campaigns.

The chapter begins with a literature review that delves into existing research on digital marketing engagement, highlighting key theories and models that have shaped current marketing strategies, and reviews scholarly articles and industry reports to provide a comprehensive overview of the state of digital marketing engagement. It then discusses major frameworks used in understanding and enhancing customer engagement, including the RACE Framework and the Consumer Engagement Behavior (CEB) model, providing insights into their application in real-world scenarios. Following this, the chapter presents original research conducted for this study, detailing the methodology, data collection, and analysis processes used to gather insights on consumer preferences and behaviors.
