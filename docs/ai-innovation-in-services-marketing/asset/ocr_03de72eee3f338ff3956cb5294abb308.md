a new customer and a brand. The second dimension investigates different forms of
involvement that classify customer behaviour according to participation in a brand’s
activities or development processes, for example, submitting feedback, identifying
areas for improvement, or filing complaints. Talking about scope, involvement can
be locally or globally spread. It means people can reach a wider and more global
audience by writing a post on social media sites or reach only a small and local
audience by simply recommending a brand to a friend. The fourth dimension is
the nature of impact which considers the time it takes for consumer engagement to
have an impact, for instance, a left review might affect others instantly. Besides, this
dimension takes into account the degree of that effect and how many individuals it
reaches, meaning that if it reaches a large number of people, it might have a tremendous
impact. The length of the consequences of client engagement is also important as
long-lasting online comments may have an impact on potential consumers, while a
single review might be quickly forgotten. Finally, the last dimension is customer goals
which outlines the idea that the entire impact of a client interaction is influenced by
how well consumers’ and company’s objectives line up (Van Doorn et al., 2010).
Considering all dimensions, the CEB framework is an essential tool for boosting
customer engagement since it evaluates and customizes the tactics to fit consumer
needs and helps to develop more individualized interactions.

# The Role of AI Increasing Customer Engagement
in Digital Marketing Campaigns

Artificial intelligence (AI) is pivotal in augmenting customer engagement within
digital marketing campaigns, with its capacity to personalize marketing, sift through
data methodically, and streamline interactions. Here are varied avenues through
which AI fosters engagement:
AI empowers the crafting, sharing, and provision of customized experiences
for customers. The adoption of AI for tailored user experiences has been met with
approval by customers who value the unique journeys and solutions explicitly curated
for them, thereby bolstering engagement (Kumar et al., 2019). AI systems adeptly
handle customer information to sculpt the future context of online engagement.
Understanding and reacting to customer behavior online permits businesses to refine
their marketing strategies more adeptly, leading to more effective engagements
(Perez-Vega et al., 2020). Incorporating AI into marketing not only streamlines
customer experiences but also bolsters digital marketing outcomes through enhanced
insights into customer data. This synergy facilitates more precise anticipations of
customer behavior and preferences (Theodoridis & Gkikas, 2019). AI enhances the
online experience by delivering content personalized to the customer’s preferences.
