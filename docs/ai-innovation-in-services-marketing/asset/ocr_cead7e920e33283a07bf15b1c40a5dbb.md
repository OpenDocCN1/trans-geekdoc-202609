In Chapter 2, the focus shifts to the technological evolution propelling companies towards innovative solutions supported by AI. This chapter contextualizes AI within organizations, elucidating its potential for empowering brands in the digital marketing realm. By exploring machine learning, natural language processing, and predictive analytics, the chapter demonstrates how AI enables personalized experiences and data-driven decisions. Additionally, it presents a conceptual model for streamlining digital marketing functions, offering insights into its practical applications.

Chapter 3 undertakes an empirical analysis to uncover factors contributing to increased customer engagement in digital marketing campaigns. Through qualitative research methods and semi-structured interviews, the study identifies key tactics that shape user behavior and foster active engagement with digital ads. By leveraging insights from the research, managerial recommendations are provided using the RACE framework, offering a strategic roadmap for enhancing customer engagement across different phases of digital marketing campaigns.

The focus of Chapter 4 is on the intersection of information and communication technology (ICT) with preventive health, exploring how AI technologies can revolutionize healthcare marketing. By harnessing web and mobile applications, AI facilitates the storage and interpretation of medical data, thereby promoting preventive healthcare practices. This chapter examines the potential of AI to enhance patient care, optimize hospital operations, and foster a proactive approach to healthcare management.

Chapter 5 delves into the effects of AI on job performance among Tunisian employees, with a focus on gender and experience as moderating and mediating variables, respectively. Through empirical analysis, the study examines the relationship between AI and job performance, shedding light on the nuanced dynamics at play. While the direct impact of AI on job performance is found to be negligible, the study highlights the significant influence of employee experience on performance outcomes.

The profound influence of AI on advertising is explored in Chapter 6, tracing its historical evolution and current trends. From predictive analytics to hyper-personalization and conversational AI, the chapter illuminates how AI-driven strategies enhance user experience and target advertising efforts. Ethical considerations surrounding privacy and data security are also addressed, underscoring the transformative impact of AI on advertising practices.
