# Examples of a Positive Experience with Digital Marketing Campaign.

Examples of positive experiences with digital ads provided by the respondents help to get a bigger picture of the factors that contribute to increasing their engagement. Moreover, they provide the names of the brands that they engage with and those can work as examples for enhancing digital marketing campaigns.

Table 6 presents the factors that contribute the most to a positive experience of digital advertising. Most of the respondents mentioned relevance and a clear message as factors that make the digital marketing campaign experience more positive. The shown effect of the product was important for two respondents. Elements that were not mentioned before were infrequency, confirmation factor, consistency, and excitement. Humour elements and sustainability focus were mentioned by respondents of age group A20-30. Most of the brands and categories provided by the age group A20-30 are cheaper options (IKEA, McDonald’s, second-hand store) while those provided in the age group A40-50 are considered to be more expensive (ODA, Rolls Royce, travel ads).
