## Conclusion of the Empirical Research

These findings are important as they help to develop recommendations that can significantly enhance customer engagement in digital marketing campaigns. The analyzed data revealed that personalization and tailored messaging are essential for fostering involvement with digital ads. It was found that Facebook and Instagram are the most popular platforms for connecting with customers and that the most appealing aspects include relevance, clear message, and design. Informational engagement is the main online experience, and multimedia elements like video and audio are the most effective. However, multimedia elements differ for age group A40-50 as it finds visual images and videos more appealing but audio is said to be less important. Age group A20-30 likes more minimalistic and simple design, whereas A40-50 likes to engage with interactive content such as videos or games. Both groups mentioned sustainable and social actions as positive elements in the ads, and the younger generation also placed an emphasis on creativity such as including humour elements in the digital ads. It was also found that preventing excessive exposure to ads and sending out emails with discounts are crucial for retaining clients’ interest. Furthermore, other customers significantly impact the respondents, which emphasizes the need to take appropriate action in response to negative feedback such as fixing the issue, making improvements, and acknowledging the feedback. There were some differences between the two age groups in the way of providing suggestions on how negative feedback should be handled - the younger generation focuses on communicating with customers, while the older one desires compensation. Overall, it is recommended to stay relevant, not push customers to buy the product and find new ways to be creative. Understanding these combined customer engagement dynamics can lead to coordinating effective recommendations for increasing customer engagement.

### Reach

Implementation of advanced targeting and diversification of ad placements focus on the part of the “Reach” phase in the RACE framework (Table 10). Together, these solutions ensure that the content is exposed to a large and relevant audience that sparks interest and encourages the start of the engagement.

### Implement Advanced Targeting

Advanced targeting that reaches customers according to their demographics, interests, and online behaviors, is an integral managerial solution to increase engagement. It allows to precisely identify and connect with customers who are likely to be engaged with digital marketing campaigns and reach them through Google search ads, Google display network (GDN), Facebook ads manager or real-time bidding (RTB). Search ads enable precise targeting of users who actively search for specific keywords, GDN extends this reach to a broader audience through visual content, Facebook ads manager focuses on social media platforms and the benefit of RTB lies in its access to diverse local websites. From the empirical research conducted before, it is clear that customers need relevant and personalized content as they are exposed to too many ads and overloaded with information. Advanced targeting guarantees that customers see only relevant content within their area of interest. While users are more likely to connect with content that holds personal significance for them, relevance acts as a way to encourage user interaction. Advanced targeting guarantees the effective use of advertising resources, maximizing the impact of the budget and lowering the acquisition of expenses by focusing only on customers who are likely to be interested. Moreover, it reduces ad fatigue that most of the consumers are experiencing. Engagement can highly suffer as a result of customer’s exposure to irrelevant messaging. By regularly optimizing campaigns based on consumer behaviour, it is possible to keep consumers’ attention on their campaigns by customizing content for certain target segments. Finally, it provides a lot of data from which the company can learn about the interests, trends, and behaviors of its users by leveraging data analytics. Advanced targeting makes it possible to use data insights to reach higher levels of engagement, enabling the creation of more effective digital marketing campaigns.

### Diversify Ad Placements

Diversification of ad placements is another managerial solution that provides a way to increase engagement by aligning content to the preferences of each unique customer. The digital audience is made up of different people, each with their own interests and habits. Ad placement diversification acknowledges the intrinsic variability of the digital audience and recognizes that different customers visit different platforms and websites, and respond better to different ad formats. By using diverse ad formats, the companies could greatly increase their reach by appealing to different audience preferences. From the empirical research, it is clear that people respond differently to the ads’ formats. Thus, adopting many formats makes the content accessible to more people. For example, video content works best for platforms like YouTube and Instagram as it can be easily shared. Visuals perform well on Facebook and Instagram, appealing to users who are drawn to visually appealing information. Stories (format 1080x1920) and feed posts (format 1080x1080) in these platforms can even be seamlessly integrated as a part of the user experience rather than a disruptive marketing campaign. Moreover, the younger generation likes to listen to podcasts on YouTube or Spotify and it provides a possibility to easily reach these customers with high-quality audio material. Therefore, understanding in which online platforms the target audience engages the most and to which ad formats it responds the best helps to maximize user engagement and their browsing experience.
