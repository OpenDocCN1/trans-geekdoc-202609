# Create Captivating Calls to Action

Clear call-to-action buttons provide a direction and an incentive for customers to engage with digital marketing campaigns. CTAs act as a link between an intended action and the purpose that the consumer is trying to achieve. As customers are overloaded with information, they seek a clear way to complete an action online and search more deeply into the product offered. Call-to-action buttons encourage a quick response and attract customers to click and look at the website. As expressed by the respondents in the empirical research, clear CTA is important as without it people become confused and uninterested, resulting in a decreased effectiveness of the campaign. If the ad is relevant for consumers and they are ready to be engaged, it is essential to provide them with a quick way to the engagement. CTAs help to navigate through the campaign effectively by offering a structured way to engage with the ad and save customers time as they are clearly guided to take a simple and easy action that gets them to a relevant landing page. Therefore, a well-designed call-to-action button meets consumers’ needs for navigation, clarity, and simplicity which increases their satisfaction and engagement levels.

# Optimize Landing Pages

From the empirical research conducted before, it is clear that people need optimized landing pages to get an easy-to-use experience when they want to take action. To provide an incentive for the next action to be taken, it is important to present the content in an understandable and user-friendly way, ensuring that the page loads quickly and has a mobile-responsive design to suit any device. In this way, customers can do the intended action of making a purchase, signing up, or contacting the company more easily and they are more likely to act further. Optimized landing pages in digital marketing campaigns would eliminate the barriers that stop consumers from engaging, such as waiting for a landing page to load, seeing poorly presented content, or not finding the exact information the user was looking for. Consequently, by optimizing its landing pages, advertisers would present its digital ads as clear and consistent with accurate and precise information.
