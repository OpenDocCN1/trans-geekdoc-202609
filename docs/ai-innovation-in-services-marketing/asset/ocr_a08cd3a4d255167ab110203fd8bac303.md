The Early Mover Advantage idea (Lieberman & Montgomery 1988; Roberts & Amit 2003; Vashishtth & Jhamh, 2021) and the Performance Gap theory, for example, both emphasize that while innovation is risky and success is not always guaranteed, it positively influences organizations’ performance (Zaltman et al. 1973; Wischnevsky & Damanpour 2006).

Process performance and result performance, which includes financial and non-financial performance, are two categories under which a company’s performance can be divided (Avolonitis et al. 2001; Song et al. 2009). Utilizing cutting-edge technology and non-technological elements to create new models or modify old models makes it easier to produce service performance, financial incentives, non-financial rewards, or market positioning (Wind and Mahajan 1997; Benner & Tushman 2003). Additional performances could be expanding markets, giving current clients more advantages, enhancing the worth of existing services, or completely developing a new service. Process performance is measured based on (a) criterion cost, (b) effectiveness of innovation, and (c) speed of innovation activity in the firm. The result performance is measured based on financial and non-financial performance. Considering the financial result performance, which is further classified as (1) business performance: measured based on (a) firm’s turnover, (b) market share, (c) productivity, (d) exports, (e) profit for the firm and (f) return on investment equity and assets (2) organization performance, which is measured based on (a) sales per employee, (b) net profit per employee, (c) gross margin and (d) growth in employment and (3) innovation performance, which is measured based on (a) time and cost of innovation, (b) frequency of innovation, and (c) new or upgraded features in product/services. Focusing on non-financial results, performance can be classified as (1) internal non-financial result performance, such as employee satisfaction and involvement in the strategic or competitive advantage of the firm, and (2) external non-financial performance, like customer satisfaction, customer loyalty, service quality, and behavioral intention of the customer.
