**1.5. Service Innovation as Disruptive Business Strategy:** The contemporary catastrophic events, including the COVID-19 pandemic, have significantly affected service providers. These incidents have significantly caused economic disruption, including changes in consumer behavior, work practices and government policies. According to the International Monetary Fund, the global financial crisis of 2009 had a real GDP year-on-year percentage change of -0.1% and the great lockdown that began in 2020 had a change of -3%. The total output loss during 2020-2021 from the pandemic crisis was approximately £9 trillion. The IMF has also stated that both advanced economies, emerging and developing economies, are in a state of recession. Despite these challenges, service providers are trying to resume normal operations because of the importance of economic stability and growth. Therefore, service providers are working to overcome the challenges, address significant changes and continuously provide value to customers and stakeholders while supporting communities and promoting social and environmental responsibility. Thus, endeavoring to adjust to new consumer behaviors and market conditions. The pandemic has accelerated the shift towards digital marketing channels, with more businesses focusing on online advertising, social media marketing and e-commerce. This has increased investment in digital marketing technologies and strategies like search engine optimization, SEO pay-per-click PPC advertising and email marketing.

Furthermore, with a rise in online shopping, a preference for contactless payment methods and a higher demand for health and safety products, the focus has shifted to more new consumer needs and preferences. Empowering the existing service framework with Technology tools like Artificial intelligence, Machine Learning techniques, Automation, Augmented and Virtual Reality, Robotics, Quantum computing, etc., can help service providers analyze and interpret large amounts of customer data, providing insights for improving service personalization.
