### ABSTRACT

The study aims to analyze how customer engagement can be increased in digital marketing campaigns to improve connections with customers. To achieve this aim, empirical research was conducted to get valuable insights into factors that contribute the most to increasing engagement in digital marketing campaigns. The study employed a qualitative research method, and 10 semi-structured interviews were conducted with potential digital marketing campaigns customers dividing them into two age groups of 20-30 and 40-50. The research showed that engagement tactics enhance the performance of digital marketing campaigns and substantially influence users’ willingness to interact with digital ads actively. Relevance, clear message, aesthetic design, personalized content, and informativeness have the greatest impact on shaping user behavior and starting active engagement. Taking research results into consideration, managerial recommendations were provided using the RACE framework regarding each phase of the engagement: plan, reach, act, convert, and engage.

### INTRODUCTION

In the fast-paced and ever-evolving landscape of digital marketing, businesses are compelled to continuously refine their strategies to enhance engagement and thereby, the effectiveness of their marketing campaigns. In an age where consumer attention is fragmented across various digital platforms, engagement emerges as a crucial metric, influencing consumers’ buying decisions and brand loyalty. Engagement with digital ads is pivotal as it can directly influence consumer behavior and purchase decisions, making it essential for companies to harness effective strategies that resonate with their target audience (Araujo et al., 2020; Calder et al., 2009).

The digital environment is fiercely competitive, and success often hinges on a brand’s ability to engage effectively with its audience. According to recent studies, highly engaged consumers are more likely to buy, promote, and show loyalty to a brand, which underscores the importance of fine-tuning engagement tactics (Calder et al., 2009). Research further suggests that the quality of engagement can significantly affect a company’s bottom line, highlighting the direct correlation between consumer engagement and profitability (Chaffey, 2023).

Moreover, the technological advancements in digital marketing tools have introduced new methods of measuring and increasing engagement through data analytics and consumer insights, which allow marketers to tailor their approaches more precisely to consumer needs (Stolle, 2022). This technological pivot necessitates an ongoing examination of consumer behaviors and preferences to craft strategies that are not only effective but also adaptable to the rapid changes in digital marketing dynamics.

The chapter analyzes consumer preferences and provides recommendations for increasing customer engagement in digital marketing campaigns. Specifically, the research problem is to find what are the best practices for reaching the optimal solution that increases customer engagement in digital marketing campaigns.

The chapter begins with a literature review that delves into existing research on digital marketing engagement, highlighting key theories and models that have shaped current marketing strategies, and reviews scholarly articles and industry reports to provide a comprehensive overview of the state of digital marketing engagement. It then discusses major frameworks used in understanding and enhancing customer engagement, including the RACE Framework and the Consumer Engagement Behavior (CEB) model, providing insights into their application in real-world scenarios. Following this, the chapter presents original research conducted for this study, detailing the methodology, data collection, and analysis processes used to gather insights on consumer preferences and behaviors. The findings from the empirical research are analyzed, discussing how they align with existing theories and what they suggest about future trends in digital marketing engagement. Practical recommendations for digital marketers are offered based on the research findings, translating theoretical and empirical insights into actionable strategies that can be implemented in current marketing practices. The chapter concludes by summarizing the key points discussed throughout and reflecting on the implications for future research and practice in the field of digital marketing.

### LITERATURE REVIEW

Digital marketing is used to advertise products or services online and reach customers through digital platforms. It provides a new communication tactic of connecting customers and businesses globally, providing support and a high level of interaction, which significantly improves client experience with the brand (Rautela, 2021). The drastic change in advertising procedures has emerged from the rise of digital media. Individuals now have the possibility to choose from many different options as advertisers have a better understanding of their behavior and can offer exactly what they need at the right time. The control over advertising has shifted towards consumers deciding whether to engage with the ads or ignore them. To offer relevant content, advertisers are looking for the most effective ways to reach highly specific audiences through personalization. In the digital era, the new goal is to improve customer activity tracking to provide more customized solutions and achieve better engagement and conversion rates. Consequently, companies have to invest in the development of digital marketing campaigns that increase customer engagement while analyzing customer behavior and preferences (Araujo et al., 2020).

Engagement can be described as the consumers’ intentional and committed allocation of their mental, physical, and social energies when they interact with a brand (Araujo et al., 2020). According to Calder et al. (2009), engagement in digital advertising involves a variety of different experiences, some being utilitarian ones where consumers look for guidance or purely pleasant interactions that provide the feeling of relaxation. The same information might evoke distinctive reactions from different customers due to the existence of several pathways to engagement, each driven by unique experiences (Figure 1). Four main engagement experience categories help to involve customers with the content provided: informational, personal identity, social interaction, and entertainment (Calder et al., 2009).

- **Informational**: Consumers seek opinions, suggestions, and guidance on making decisions. Their curiosity and self-learning may engage them to find valuable information such as tutorials, educational content, or simply details about the product or service and how using it might help them. Digital ads can engage people by offering useful information that is relevant to their needs.

- **Personal Identity**: Identification of positive role models or admired personalities in the media, which reinforces customers’ ideals. By being consistent with their values and beliefs, ads can foster a feeling of personal identity and involve affirmation of one’s core values. It is a process of connecting with significant people represented in the media, learning from positive role models, and developing an understanding of one’s character.

- **Social Interaction**: Engaging advertising strategies promote social sharing and communication, which improve a person’s feeling of community belonging and interpersonal relationships. These strategies include social media engagement and online groups where people connect by sharing their own experiences with the brand.

- **Entertainment**: Ads that are entertaining capture customers’ attention and make the ad experience more enjoyable through storytelling, videos, or interactive games. This engagement experience helps to deliver the brand’s message in a relaxing way and offers emotional satisfaction.

These days it is extremely difficult to engage with customers as they easily incorporate multiple digital platforms into their everyday lives. Thus, there is a need for a systematic method that includes different processes of communication into a cohesive campaign strategy (Sestino et al., 2023). In 2010, Dave Chaffey created a RACE framework which is a comprehensive model for businesses that helps to outline a strategy that addresses the issues of reaching and engaging their customers (Chaffey, 2023). The framework outlines planning, reaching, acting, converting, and engaging actions, and is completely consumer-focused. It offers a thorough guideline for boosting consumer engagement and assists marketers in understanding their customer base by analyzing the quickly changing digital environment (Sestino et al., 2023). Although it covers a variety of customer journey stages, it is vital to understand that each element is crucial in promoting consumer involvement. Engagement itself is an ongoing process that requires a clear foundation of understanding the audience, its behaviors, and preferences.
