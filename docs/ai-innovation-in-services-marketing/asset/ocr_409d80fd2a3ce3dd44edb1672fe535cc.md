7.1. Collaboration/ Shared Economy: The changing world scenario has given center stage to collaborative or sharing economies (Belk, 2014). The conceptual foundations of “shared economy” are getting rooted deeply and have remodeled common ideas about ownership and consumption. Consumers understand the advantages of paying to access or share products rather than owning them temporarily (Matzler et al., 2015). It is an emerging form of business model propagating usage rather than ownership, banking upon the networks or communities of people with the support of an internet-based ecosystem.
