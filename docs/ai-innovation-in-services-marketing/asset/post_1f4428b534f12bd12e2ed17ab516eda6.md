### ABSTRACT

The widespread adoption of artificial intelligence (AI) has ignited transformative changes across diverse domains, including business and marketing. Consequently, this study immerses itself in the initiatives undertaken by Invest Lithuania, a non-profit foreign direct investment development agency, which orchestrated an inventive marketing campaign through the synergy of account-based marketing (ABM), storytelling, and artificial intelligence (AI) techniques. The campaign's overarching goal was to effectively engage target contacts and increase business opportunities in the game development sector. In this dynamic environment, innovative marketing approaches, like those explored in this study, can help build long-term relationships and promote sustainable business growth. The results showed that the potential is there.

### INTRODUCTION

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has recently experienced widespread adoption across various sectors, including marketing. Leading companies such as Google and Spotify have utilized AI to enhance operational efficiency (Jarek & Mazurek, 2019; Vlačić et al., 2021). However, AI's role extends beyond mere operations. According to Haleem et al. (2022), strategically employing AI can be a vital asset for marketers, facilitating the development of innovative strategies and targeted marketing campaigns tailored to specific audiences.

Given this context and with a focus on B2B, innovative marketing strategies such as Account-Based Marketing (ABM) can assist in achieving that goal. ABM empowers marketers to customize initiatives to engage and influence key decision-makers within targeted companies or accounts (Burgess & Munn, 2021). When ABM is coupled with AI capabilities and compelling storytelling techniques, marketing efforts can be elevated to new heights, enhancing customer experiences and deeper connections (Spanos, 2021; Vidrih & Mayahi, 2023), potentially shaping future actions. This chapter is dedicated to delving into the dynamics of such transformative strategies.

The current study explores the synergy between ABM, storytelling, and AI, using a case study of Invest Lithuania, a non-profit agency that promotes foreign direct investment (FDI) in Lithuania. Considering challenges such as the global disruption caused by the COVID-19 pandemic to conventional marketing approaches (He & Harris, 2020), the organization implemented innovative strategies. Among these strategies was a One-to-Few ABM campaign (Mullen et al., 2022), designed to engage ten selected game development companies by involving storytelling and AI techniques.

The methodology of this study follows a qualitative interpretative approach. The chapter is divided into sections on the theoretical background, methodology, Invest Lithuania case study, conclusions, and recommendations for further research. Through this structured approach, the chapter endeavors to provide valuable insights that inform both academic discourse and practical applications in the field of marketing.

### THEORETICAL BACKGROUND

A critical approach to marketing proves essential, as it plays a pivotal role in effectively connecting with target audiences and driving business expansion (Burgess & Munn, 2021). Given that, this section focuses on an emerging B2B strategy, account-based marketing, which is examined in conjunction with storytelling and AI. This fusion will be thoroughly explored, uncovering its transformative impact on reshaping marketing strategies and nurturing deeper connections with target audiences.

### Account-Based Marketing

According to Vajre (2016), “The essential definition of account-based marketing is focused B2B marketing” (p. 10). It integrates by concentrating on specific, best-fit prospects. ABM treats these prospects, such as target companies or accounts, as distinct entities, tailoring personalized tactical initiatives that significantly impact key decision-makers (Burgess & Munn, 2021). This approach significantly diverges from conventional B2B marketing. As a result, ABM has garnered attention from researchers (Burgess & Munn, 2021) and practitioners (Vajre, 2016) for its substantial impact on business performance.

As Burgess and Munn (2021) highlighted, “there are some powerful economic, technological and social trends that are driving the need to incorporate ABM” (p. 6) into business strategies. These trends include competitive disruption, globalization and increasing complexity, evolving buyer expectations, and the growing emphasis on customization and personalization. Consequently, strategically minded businesses have acknowledged its potential and contemplated its adoption, considering its numerous benefits.

For example, ABM’s personalized and targeted approach allows businesses to enhance customer engagement and improve conversion rates significantly (Upadhay & McCormick, 2018). Furthermore, the alignment of sales and marketing teams, a fundamental aspect of ABM, has been recognized by academics like Golec et al. (2019) as a strategy that leads to higher revenue growth and improved overall business performance. ABM’s ability to foster strong, long-term customer relationships translates into increased customer loyalty and higher customer lifetime value, further contributing to sustained business success (Bacon, 2021). Hence, empirical evidence highlights ABM as a robust strategy for enhancing customer experience and elevating business performance, thanks to its customer-centric and relationship-focused nature. However, a crucial inquiry emerges: What tools can effectively complement and bolster this approach? An intriguing response to this query emerges in the form of storytelling.
