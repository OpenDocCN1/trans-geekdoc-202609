The first stage of the framework is the “Plan” which includes a detailed examination of the market, a company’s internal analysis, and clearly stated goals. The next step is “Reach” which concentrates on making customers aware of a brand through different media channels. The key factor lies in the “Act” phase which emphasizes the value of involving customers. To have high levels of engagement, it is important to provide clear and relevant elements in the ads that evoke customers’ attention and participation to take a first action such as clicking on the ad. After that, the “Convert” phase follows when visitors take action, for example, purchasing a product or service, registering, or becoming leads. Finally, the final step “Engage” promotes long-term connections with clients that result in increased loyalty and retention rates which greatly enhance a company’s reputation (Rautela, 2021).

The nature of client involvement can also be analyzed through customer engagement behaviors (CEB). According to Van Doorn et al. (2010), CEB includes five characteristics that assist in understanding, evaluating, and enhancing consumer engagement and its tactics: valence, the form and modality, scope, nature of impact, and customer goals. Firstly, valence focuses on the active involvement of consumers that can either benefit or negatively impact the brand, for instance, a written comment online or personal referrals. Even seemingly positive actions such as recommending a brand to others might carry the potential for negativity if there is a mismatch between a new customer and a brand. The second dimension investigates different forms of involvement that classify customer behaviour according to participation in a brand’s activities or development processes, for example, submitting feedback, identifying areas for improvement, or filing complaints. Talking about scope, involvement can be locally or globally spread. It means people can reach a wider and more global audience by writing a post on social media sites or reach only a small and local audience by simply recommending a brand to a friend. The fourth dimension is the nature of impact which considers the time it takes for consumer engagement to have an impact, for instance, a left review might affect others instantly. Besides, this dimension takes into account the degree of that effect and how many individuals it reaches, meaning that if it reaches a large number of people, it might have a tremendous impact. The length of the consequences of client engagement is also important as long-lasting online comments may have an impact on potential consumers, while a single review might be quickly forgotten. Finally, the last dimension is customer goals which outlines the idea that the entire impact of a client interaction is influenced by how well consumers’ and company’s objectives line up (Van Doorn et al., 2010). Considering all dimensions, the CEB framework is an essential tool for boosting customer engagement since it evaluates and customizes the tactics to fit consumer needs and helps to develop more individualized interactions.

## The Role of AI Increasing Customer Engagement in Digital Marketing Campaigns

Artificial intelligence (AI) is pivotal in augmenting customer engagement within digital marketing campaigns, with its capacity to personalize marketing, sift through data methodically, and streamline interactions. Here are varied avenues through which AI fosters engagement:
AI empowers the crafting, sharing, and provision of customized experiences for customers. The adoption of AI for tailored user experiences has been met with approval by customers who value the unique journeys and solutions explicitly curated for them, thereby bolstering engagement (Kumar et al., 2019). AI systems adeptly handle customer information to sculpt the future context of online engagement. Understanding and reacting to customer behavior online permits businesses to refine their marketing strategies more adeptly, leading to more effective engagements (Perez-Vega et al., 2020). Incorporating AI into marketing not only streamlines customer experiences but also bolsters digital marketing outcomes through enhanced insights into customer data. This synergy facilitates more precise anticipations of customer behavior and preferences (Theodoridis & Gkikas, 2019). AI enhances the online experience by delivering content personalized to the customer’s preferences.

Marketers employing AI can leverage real-time data to devise and implement digital marketing strategies that resonate personally with each customer, thereby increasing engagement (Kumar et al., 2019).

To maximize the potential of AI in marketing, it is essential not only to personalize experiences and optimize ad campaigns but also to confront challenges like data privacy and biases within algorithms. Navigating these hurdles is critical to fully capitalizing on AI in marketing (Kumar et al., 2023). The impact of AI on marketing communication is profound, as it efficiently analyzes customer needs to boost sales and revenue. By harnessing techniques such as Big Data analytics and machine learning, AI enables firms to precisely target and reach customers, thereby streamlining marketing efforts (Malviya et al., 2022).

## Empirical Research

The main goal of the research is to identify the factors and examine optimal solutions that contribute to increasing customer engagement in digital marketing campaigns.

## Objectives of the Research

To achieve the aim of the empirical research, four objectives have been set:

- 1. To understand consumer needs and identify the most preferable factors in digital marketing campaigns.
- 2. To investigate how personalization can be employed to achieve higher levels of engagement in digital marketing campaigns.
- 3. To examine which practices that increase customer engagement work best in the real-world scenario of digital marketing.
- 4. To provide recommendations on how to maximize consumer engagement based on the insights gained from the respondents.

## Theoretical Foundations of the Research

In this research the topic of increasing customer engagement is analyzed with the use of three theoretical models discussed in the literature review – Calder’s engagement experiences approach, the RACE framework, and the CEB model. They provide a base for analyzing aspects of engagement that most closely match customer preferences and improving the dynamics of consumer involvement in marketing campaigns. All theories claim the importance of consumer engagement for better campaign results and stronger brand recognition.

The research for increasing customer engagement is significantly shaped by Calder's engagement experiences approach which helps marketers build an individualized and valuable interaction for each unique customer. Different experiences require different approaches such as selecting multimedia components or deciding on the best channel for enhancing distinctive engagement experiences.

RACE framework offers an organized method for developing digital marketing strategies and reaching higher levels of client engagement. Five phases of the model (Plan, Reach, Act, Convert, Engage) outline how a customer's journey develops, and each stage contributes to increasing customer engagement. By comprehending the audience and objectives, the planning step creates the foundation for successful engagement methods. While the “Reach” stage is mainly concerned with raising awareness, it may also spark interest and start the involvement phase. It is also important to use the “Act” stage and encourage customers to take action to deepen the connection between the brand and the customer. Achieving success in the “Convert” stage suggests a higher degree of client loyalty, opening possibilities for further engagement initiatives. Finally, the “Engage” phase culminates with a clear focus on active consumer involvement, cultivating long-lasting relationships. Following this framework helps more easily adjust the marketing plans in response to customer preferences, thus, it is used as the main model to provide managerial solutions based on the empirical research data.

Customer Engagement Behavior (CEB) model is closely related to the research as it provides a theoretical framework for understanding and improving consumer engagement. It emphasizes several aspects of high involvement levels that are shown by customers’ active participation, such as reviewing products or getting involved in word-of-mouth activity. The approach also recognizes that customers can show a company in either a positive or negative way, easily affecting the involvement of potential customers.

In conclusion, these models emphasize different aspects of engagement as Calder's model explores the experiential components of engagement, CEB focuses on consumer behavior, while the RACE framework offers a systematic method for developing a digital marketing plan. Therefore, together they can embrace comprehensive recommendations to enhance customer engagement by involving a systematic approach to engagement dynamics as an integral component of empirical research.
