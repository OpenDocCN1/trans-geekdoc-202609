
The ABM campaign ran from January to March 2023. As discussed earlier, it revolved around ten carefully chosen game development companies, emphasizing pinpointing their most iconic game characters. After these game characters were identified, the researcher started the process of AI-powered image creation (Figure 1). DALL-E 2 software was selected for this purpose due to its remarkable ability to generate lifelike images and artwork from natural language descriptions (Liu et al., 2022).

As depicted in Figure 1, the postcards generated by the researcher using AI portrayed game characters embarking on a business trip to Vilnius, Lithuania's capital. These characters, acting as virtual Vilnius “ambassadors”, sent four postcards (from left to right) to the target contacts. The first image depicted the arrival in Vilnius and the game character’s mission initiation. The second image introduced the landscape of Vilnius, its inhabitants, and noteworthy places to explore. The third one provided an overview of the city’s infrastructure. Lastly, the fourth image delved into the potential within the game development ecosystem and detailed their departure from Vilnius. (A visualization of these texts is shown in Figure 2.)

With guidance from a marketing team to a creative agency regarding the campaign’s concept and tone of voice of the game characters, artfully designed narratives were employed on the postcards. These narratives effectively communicated the journey, immersing recipients in their fictional adventure and providing an engaging glimpse into Vilnius and its potential for future game development collaborations. This innovative approach aligns with the research conducted by Brynjolfsson and McAfee (2014) on AI-driven personalization, which enhances engagement and fosters a deeper connection. It is worth emphasizing that each card included a QR code (see Figure 2) that directed individuals to the game development landing page on the Invest Lithuania website when scanned. Furthermore, each postcard incorporated symbolic details associated with the game character to elevate the sense of personalization, for example, the inclusion of bird-foot prints. ## The Results of the ABM Campaign

The representatives from eight of the ten target companies who received the postcards responded positively after receiving the sales team’s first or second follow-up message. It is crucial to highlight that due to postage issues, not all recipients received postcards; in fact, some postcards were returned. This situation influenced the overall response rate. However, in B2B marketing, especially within niche sectors like game development, even a few meaningful engagements can hold significant value (Taylor, 2017). Several notable patterns and experiences were observed from the responses that were collected. The most prominent theme in most responses was the positive feedback about the marketing campaign. Respondents appreciated the campaign's creativity, originality, and the meticulous planning and execution that was put into it. Strategic-level managers were impressed with the campaign. Here are a few noteworthy examples of their feedback:

> I got to say that your campaign was well thought out & original! Big kudos to the team who thought about that! (G1)

> [...] very clever move with the envelopes as well, I enjoyed it. (G7)

Following on from that, many of the respondents expressed interest in exploring potential future opportunities. For instance, G4 stated: “Thanks for the messages. Can you tell me more about what you would like to discuss?”. Additionally, some acknowledged that the current environment may be challenging for technology companies, including gaming, but remained open to the possibility of future collaborations or discussions:

> As you know, the current context is pretty tough for Tech companies in general and gaming in particular, but who knows. Future is full of opportunities (G2).

Expanding on this, a few individuals conveyed their intentions to initiate discussions regarding the campaign and potential collaborations with their respective management or relevant teams. For example, G7 expressed keen interest, stating, “I’d love to hear more [...]. Let me talk to my Management Team and get back to you”. Similarly, other responses included statements such as, “I will share this cool experience with my peers internally in charge of business development” (G2) and “I can forward your information to some colleagues” (G8). These responses indicate a willingness to explore further engagement with the Vilnius gaming ecosystem.

Of particular interest is the response from one of the target contacts who expressed a desire to receive information about game development studios in the area. Their statement, “If you have a deck with the interesting game development studios in your area, I’ll be happy to have a look” (G3), suggests a potential inclination towards exploring partnerships or collaborations within the gaming industry. In line with Burgess and Shercliff (2022), this response hints at the campaign’s ability to generate immediate interest and pave the way for future collaborations and business opportunities. Furthermore, G5 expressed, “I have no doubt Vilnius offers great opportunities for not only Gaming companies [...]”, thus demonstrating a comprehensive grasp of Vilnius’ ecosystem.

A notable aspect of the responses was the expression of positive impressions about Vilnius as a city. Several individuals appreciated Vilnius, which can be considered a potential advantage in future discussions or collaborations. The favorable views of the city, such as “I love Vilnius!” (G6) and “I’ve been to Vilnius once, for a football tournament for my oldest son, I really enjoyed the city!” (G7), indicate that the campaign not only piqued their professional interest but also rekindled their positive memories of Vilnius as a destination. This positive sentiment may be a foundation for building rapport and fostering future collaborations (Souiden et al., 2016). It suggests that beyond the specific marketing campaign, it effectively displayed Vilnius as an attractive and appealing location for potential business endeavors within the gaming industry.

Lastly, several people mentioned they were not looking to expand their studios geographically. Such positioning indicates that while they appreciated the campaign, they might not actively seek new locations now. For example, G5 mentioned:

> We highly appreciate your interest in [us] and in case such opportunity arises I will reach out. All the best!

Additionally, G8 expressed their position as follows:

> I think at this point we are not looking to expand our own studio geographically [...], but I appreciate you reaching out and inviting us. I wish you all the success
