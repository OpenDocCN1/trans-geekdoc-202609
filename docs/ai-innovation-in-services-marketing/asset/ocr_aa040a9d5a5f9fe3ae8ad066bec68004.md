Chapter 8 emphasizes the need for innovation in service industries, particularly in developing robust business models that incorporate non-technical components. By synthesizing organizational, strategic, and marketing innovations, this chapter advocates for a holistic approach to service innovation. Various new business models are discussed, highlighting the importance of customer engagement and experience management in service-based firms.

In Chapter 9, the far-reaching impact of AI on the Indian service sector is explored, with a focus on consumer attitudes and perceptions. Through qualitative survey data, the chapter provides valuable insights into Indian consumers’ views on AI adoption, addressing concerns about job displacement and workforce upskilling. Ethical dilemmas and future possibilities surrounding AI in the service industry are also examined, offering a comprehensive understanding of its implications in the Indian market.

We invite you to embark on this journey with us, as we traverse the intricate intersections of AI and services marketing. Together, let us explore, analyze, and envision the future of marketing innovation in the AI era.
