Given this context and with a focus on B2B, innovative marketing strategies such as Account-Based Marketing (ABM) can assist in achieving that goal. When ABM is coupled with AI capabilities and compelling storytelling techniques, marketing efforts can be elevated to new heights, enhancing customer experiences and deeper connections (Spanos, 2021; Vidrih & Mayahi, 2023), potentially shaping future actions. This chapter is dedicated to delving into the dynamics of such transformative strategies.

The current study explores the synergy between ABM, storytelling, and AI, using a case study of Invest Lithuania, a non-profit agency that promotes foreign direct investment (FDI) in Lithuania. Considering challenges such as the global disruption caused by the COVID-19 pandemic to conventional marketing approaches (He & Harris, 2020), the organization implemented innovative strategies. Among these strategies was a One-to-Few ABM campaign (Mullen et al., 2022), designed to engage ten selected game development companies by involving storytelling and AI techniques.

The methodology of this study follows a qualitative interpretative approach. The chapter is divided into sections on the theoretical background, methodology, Invest Lithuania case study, conclusions, and recommendations for further research.
