# 4.2 Explanatory Factor Analysis

To ascertain the underlying factors of several observable variables, exploratory factor analysis is frequently employed (Auerwald & Moshagen, 2019). The Kaiser-Meyer-Olkin (KMO) index and Bartlett tests showed that the data were suitable for factor analysis (Hair et al., 2010; Kaiser, 1974). In Kaiser's (1974) classification, values between 0.5-0.7 are considered mediocre, 0.7-0.8 good, 0.8-0.9 great, 0.9-1.00 superb, and < 0.5 unacceptable. In our case, we determined a KMO\(_{overall}\) coefficient of 0.874. In addition, the KMO coefficients for artificial intelligence awareness and job performance are higher than 0.7 (i.e., KMO\(_{AI}\) = 0.793, KMO\(_{JP}\) = 0.901). We also computed Bartlett's test. Our results show that the chi-square value of Bartlett's sphericity test is 3947.084 (p<0.001).
