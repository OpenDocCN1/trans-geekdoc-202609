
- **ChatGPT**: ChatGPT is an AI-based conversational agent that helps marketers generate original texts, brainstorm ideas and get answers to questions. Its natural language processing capabilities enable seamless communication and ideation.

- **Hootsuite Insights**: Hootsuite Insights is a tool for social media analytics, that uses AI to monitor content and trends across multiple platforms. It provides marketers with valuable insights and actionable intelligence to inform their social media strategies and campaigns.

- **Adobe Sensei GenAI**: Adobe Sensei GenAI offers advanced data analytics, content optimization, and campaign improvement features. Integrated into Adobe’s suite of marketing tools, it allows marketers to personalize experiences, personalize audiences, and optimize digital marketing strategies.

- **Salesforce Einstein**: Salesforce Einstein is an AI platform integrated with Salesforce’s Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system, that offers personalization, predictive analytics, marketing automation, and insights capabilities, empowering marketers to deliver personalized experiences and drive customer engagement.

- **DALL-E 2**: DALL-E 2 is an AI model that generates images based on textual descriptions (prompts). It allows marketers to create visually appealing content quickly and easily, expanding creative possibilities.

- **DALL-E 3**: The DALL-E 3 builds on the capabilities of the DALL-E 2, offering significant improvements in generating realistic images and artwork from natural language descriptions. Integrated with ChatGPT, it provides marketers with enhanced creative tools and capabilities.

- **Synthesia**: Synthesia is an AI tool for creating and publishing videos. It allows marketers to generate videos with AI avatars and voiceovers in 120+ languages, serving diverse audiences and increasing engagement.

The adoption of AI-supported tools has transformed the marketing landscape, offering unprecedented opportunities for marketers to increase efficiency, creativity and effectiveness. From social media management and content creation to data analysis and personalization, AI-based tools empower marketers to achieve marketing success in today’s competitive environment. By leveraging AI capabilities, marketers can streamline workflows, generate compelling content, analyze data-driven insights, personalize experiences, and drive strategic decision-making. As AI continues to evolve, it will be imperative for marketers to adopt these innovative tools to stay ahead of the curve and deliver effective marketing campaigns.

## CONCEPTUAL MODEL PROPOSAL

As already mentioned, the role of AI has become central in optimizing business processes and in the ability to generate competitive advantages. However, the inclusion of AI in companies will quickly become a mandatory requirement for companies/brands to be able to respond effectively in an increasingly demanding time-to-market. In order to understand the role of AI and to design an AI layer in the context of companies/brands, this chapter presents a proposal for a conceptual model where AI plays a role in facilitating and empowering companies’ ability to understand their markets and, accordingly, to make better and faster decisions.

The proposed model highlights the role of generative AI, which has resulted in a kind of democratization of the use of AI for the most varied purposes, whether in a context of personal use or in a context of business use. What is certain is that AI is no longer a “taboo” subject and/or mentally aligned with technological companies or pure research contexts.

The emergence of many tools supported by AI, for multiple purposes and simple to use (e.g., ChatGPT), has made some of the tasks that were time-consuming and/or only within the reach of professionals with higher technological skills simple and fast for everyone. This reality – generative AI – which can be understood in its succinct flow of operation according to figure 1, has provided brand managers with a wealth of precious resources that empower their role and their ability to understand and make decisions. In this context, AI-driven tools will increasingly become productivity tools for marketers and business decision-makers.

Even though the current stage of AI penetration in companies is the inclusion of AI-tools (and this stage will probably be maintained for a long time in small companies), the future will be the full addition of a whole new layer of AI in the remaining layers, and their processes, of organizations, as happened with the layer of classical information systems (non-AI).

Think about how AI can be strategically and functionally included in companies, in order to create a functional link between understanding the market and, in particular, customers and their aspirations, and decision making, from its operational to strategic aspects, should govern any new information systems architecture. However, in our opinion, the inclusion of this AI layer should guarantee the most automated operation possible, where AI can be an autopilot for the company that, without prejudice to the strategy approved by the human decision maker, is a layer that analyzes, understands and takes decisions.

The model presented targets large companies, which have the financial and technological capabilities to implement (or subcontract the implementation) an AI layer across all business processes. In the context we explore, small and medium-sized companies, which do not have the capacity for such implementation, will take advantage of the AI-tools but will not be capable of implementing an implementation similar to what we propose.

A typical company that the model contemplates is a holding company, which has several brands, in different business sectors and/or in the same line of business. In this context, there is a need to manage several brands.

The model presents the “autopilot” vision for brand management, which means that it is up to AI to understand how to manage a given brand, whether through the extinction or creation of brands. It is up to AI to understand the insights that intelligent analysis of data obtained through customer interactions can offer. In this way, the company’s response could be the extinction of a brand that is not profitable and/or the creation of new brands that embody new products that will predictably be successful with customers. The capacity for such perception materializes a concept where a brand and its products can be the result of the customers’ vision and not the company’s own – customers design the products that the company should offer them, shaping them over time.

The Generative AI CRM engine is responsible for managing all the channels that brands have to manage their relationships with their customers (e.g., chatbots, social networks). This engine is capable of personalizing interactions with each customer by taking advantage of customer input to understand their profile at all times. Generative AI plays a key role here due to its ability to constantly adapt the way in which customer interests are managed, combining them with business interests. The ability to perceive insights turns the vision of managing a brand and creating or modeling new or existing products into a linear process of managing interests where supply tends to adapt to demand in a “perfect” way.

This approach will contribute to generate in customers a greater sense of belonging to the company and a connection that induces in the customer a feeling of almost apparent control – the customer feels that they are the focus in a more tangible way.

Upstream of the proposed model is the business process management engine through generative AI. This component uses AI to obtain insights into the downstream functioning of the model (i.e., the customer interaction component), allowing the company’s strategic management level to evaluate and validate proposals for creating new brands and products. This component also allows you to generate AI-driven knowledge.

In this way, better, more effective and faster strategic decisions will be made, feeding and empowering top management tasks and marketers who will refine their strategies with greater probability of success.

However, the vision of a conceptual model that tends towards a state of perfect equilibrium is not without limitations.

It is important to understand that generative AI brings with it a very important set of strengths and opportunities. However, like any technology, it has weaknesses and threats. Figure 3 succinctly presents the result of a reflection on generative AI in the form of a SWOT analysis.

Competition will continue to exist between organizations that have the capacity to implement such models. The evolution of generative AI (e.g., as we see in the ChatGPT example) will be constantly evolving, with better models and more accurate capabilities for predicting and understanding insights. We are of the opinion that in the near future, the quality of the AI that a given company can develop or acquire (from companies specialized in AI) will be the most important asset and the greatest generator of the degree of success and competitive advantage that the company can have. The very computational capacity that a company disposes of will influence its ability to obtain better and faster insights. The field of research about the role of human intuition in decision-making will be called into question as never before. Will visionaries continue to exist?
