### WHAT IS SERVICE MARKETING?

Within the marketing domain, service marketing is a specialized area that concentrates on selling and marketing intangibles, such as advantages, experiences, and knowledge, as opposed to actual goods. Due to the intrinsic qualities of services, it requires a different set of tactics and considerations than typical product marketing. These include:

- Intangibility: Since services cannot be felt, tasted, or seen before being acquired, it is essential to concentrate on expressing their worth through narratives, case studies, and illustrations.
- Inseparability: Since the creation and consumption of services frequently occur concurrently, the relationship between the service provider and the client directly affects the quality of the service. Establishing confidence and providing a satisfying experience is crucial.
- Variability: Consistency and standardization are important components of service marketing because the quality of a service might change based on the provider, the time of day, and other circumstances.
- Perishability: Once a service is provided, it cannot be kept or returned, underscoring the significance of prompt delivery and effective service procedures (Nandini, 2019).

### NEED FOR INNOVATION IN SERVICE MARKETING

In marketing, innovation encompasses not just the use of new technologies but also the application of fresh, practical concepts. Innovation is crucial since it enhances your good or service. You can expand your market reach and boost revenue and sales by being innovative.

Innovation can impact different aspects of a business and take many different shapes. You can implement innovative concepts or technology to help your business differentiate itself from the competition. You may see increased sales, profit, and customer happiness after making minor adjustments. Depending on your demands and issues, think about incorporating design, manufacturing, and product innovation. By using creative campaigns and marketing tactics, you may identify the best way to reach and engage your target audience. Find out through research what features your product needs to improve the user experience. A positive customer experience will benefit both your clients and your company.

Innovations in services occur globally and in all industries. Naturally, this involves service providers, but service innovations can also be developed by businesses that are product-based. A service innovation modifies the customer experience to generate income for the business and value for the client. A business may see a rise in revenue through new service innovation if its current clients spend more, the company’s reputation improves, they make recommendations, etc. A measurable aspect is product innovation. Enhancing a product’s functioning or appearance by physical means is known as improvement. Innovation also adds value to services because it is an intangible good.

Both service-based and product-based businesses should value service innovation, although product-based businesses should prioritize it and service-based businesses frequently do not need it.

Understanding and meeting client demands is at the heart of service marketing’s mission to build enduring relationships and, eventually, propel corporate success. There are several strategies involved in this:

- Building strong relationships with customers: Service marketing prioritizes customer interactions and experiences to build trust, loyalty, and positive word-of-mouth, which in turn secures recurring business and long-term value.
- Differentiating a service from rivals: To stand out in a crowded market, service marketing emphasizes the special features and advantages of a service. This could entail cutting-edge delivery strategies, excellent customer support, or specialized knowledge.
- Increasing reputation and brand awareness: In addition to bringing in new business, successful service marketing techniques build a favourable brand perception that fosters customer loyalty and increases market share.
- Promoting growth and profit for the organization (Leslie de Chernatony, 2010).

### EVOLUTION OF AI AND ITS IMPACT ON THE INDIAN ECONOMY

The evolution of Artificial Intelligence (AI) has been a long and complex journey, with its roots dating back to ancient times. The concept of AI was first introduced by Alan Turing in 1950, who proposed the Turing Test to determine a machine’s ability to exhibit human-like intelligence. The 1956 Dartmouth Conference marked the birth of AI as a field, with early AI programs like the Logic Theorist and General Problem Solver. However, the enthusiasm for AI declined in the 1970s and 1980s due to challenges in symbolic AI and the complexity of real-world problems.

The resurgence of AI began in the 1990s with the advent of machine learning approaches, which allowed systems to learn from data and improve over time. Neural networks, inspired by the human brain structure, gained prominence, leading to breakthroughs in pattern recognition and speech processing. The development of the backpropagation algorithm for training neural networks was a pivotal moment in this era (Edem Gold, 2023).

The 21st century saw an unprecedented acceleration in AI development, driven by big data convergence, increased computational power, and advanced algorithms. Machine learning, particularly deep learning, became the driving force behind AI’s resurgence, with breakthroughs in computer vision, natural language processing, and speech recognition fueling applications like image recognition, language translation, and virtual assistants.

The concept of artificial general intelligence (AGI) has gained attention, with contemporary AI systems excelling in specialized domains such as image and speech recognition, language understanding, and autonomous decision-making. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is poised to drive economic growth in India, transforming various sectors and contributing to job creation. As India enters the fourth industrial revolution, AI is positioned as a key catalyst for this transformation.
