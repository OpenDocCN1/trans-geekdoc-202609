# RECENT OF INNOVATION IN SERVICE MARKETING

# 1. Augmented Reality (AR) in Retail Services:

Augmented Reality (AR) has become a game-changer in the retail industry, significantly enhancing the way customers interact with products and make purchase decisions. One of the prominent examples of AR in retail is the IKEA Place app. Launched by the Swedish furniture giant, this app allows users to virtually place IKEA furniture in their homes using their smartphones or tablets. By leveraging the device’s camera and AR technology, customers can see how a specific piece of furniture fits into their living space in terms of size, color, and style.

The IKEA Place app addresses a common challenge in online furniture shopping—uncertainty about how a piece will look in a real-world setting. This innovative use of AR not only provides a virtual try-before-you-buy experience but also significantly reduces the likelihood of returns, enhancing customer satisfaction and confidence in their purchase decisions.

AR has also been applied in the fashion industry. For instance, brands like Gap and Converse have developed AR-powered apps that enable customers to virtually try on clothes and shoes. By superimposing digital images of clothing items onto the user’s real-world environment, these apps offer a more engaging and personalized online shopping experience.

Another notable example is the use of AR in beauty and cosmetics. L’Oréal’s Makeup Genius app, for instance, enables users to try on different makeup products virtually. By utilizing facial recognition technology, the app accurately maps the user’s facial features, allowing them to experiment with various makeup looks in real time. This not only simplifies the product discovery process but also encourages users to explore and purchase new beauty products with confidence. In the automotive industry, AR is being employed to enhance the showroom experience. BMW’s Augmented Reality Visualizer app, for instance, enables customers to visualize and customize their desired BMW model in real time. Users can explore different colour options, configurations, and features by simply pointing their mobile device at a physical space, such as a parking lot. This interactive and immersive experience contributes to a more informed and personalized decision-making process when purchasing a vehicle. AR is not limited to individual brands; it has also been adopted by online marketplaces. For example, Shopify, a popular e-commerce platform, has integrated AR functionalities into its platform, allowing merchants to showcase products in AR directly on their online stores. This democratization of AR technology enables a wide range of retailers to provide immersive product experiences to their customers, irrespective of their size or industry (Syed Shah Alam, 2021).
