## The Impact of Other Customers’ Perceptions

Consumers frequently look for reviews as a reliable source of insights about a company and its products and they can affect consumers’ trust and decision-making processes. Positive reviews can boost brand credibility, while negative ones can deter potential customers. Question 10 aimed to analyze what impact other customers’ reviews have on respondents’ engagement with the brand. Seven respondents (R1, R2, R3, R6, R8, R9, R10) stated that the perceptions have a big impact on their engagement as negative reviews help to avoid scams and not to buy poor quality products. R3 also said that it is important to try the product to create an opinion but negative reviews sometimes are too difficult to ignore. Two respondents (R4, R5) said that they care only about close friends’ reviews and R7 stated that the perceptions of other people have no impact.

Respondents were also asked to provide suggestions on how a company could handle negative reviews online. It is essential to understand the best practices for managing negative reviews online as they directly influence a company’s reputation. Eight respondents (R1, R2, R4, R6, R7, R8, R9, R10) said that it is important to fix the issues of products before launching a new series of them and make improvements. Two respondents (R1, R10) highlighted the importance of acknowledging the feedback, while two others (R4, R5) recommended reaching out to customers and communicating with them. R2 stated that companies should be understandable and provide polite feedback that they will work on the mistakes. R3 focused on communicating the positive sides of the product and sharing accurate information. R6 also provided the idea of offering samples that customers could try the product themselves, R8 said that companies should somehow compensate for the mistakes, and R10 offered for a company to ask consumers for suggestions. Even though both groups are looking for remedies for negatively reviewed products, age group A20-30 focuses more on the communication side, while age group A40-50 shows a need for getting something in exchange.
