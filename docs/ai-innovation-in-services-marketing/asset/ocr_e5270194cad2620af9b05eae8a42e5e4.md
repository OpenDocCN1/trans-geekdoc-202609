# CONCEPTUAL MODEL PROPOSAL

As already mentioned, the role of AI has become central in optimizing business processes and in the ability to generate competitive advantages. However, the inclusion of AI in companies will quickly become a mandatory requirement for companies/brands to be able to respond effectively in an increasingly demanding time-to-market. In order to understand the role of AI and to design an AI layer in the context of companies/brands, this chapter presents a proposal for a conceptual model where AI plays a role in facilitating and empowering companies’ ability to understand their markets and, accordingly, to make better and faster decisions.

The proposed model highlights the role of generative AI, which has resulted in a kind of democratization of the use of AI for the most varied purposes, whether in a context of personal use or in a context of business use. What is certain is that AI is no longer a “taboo” subject and/or mentally aligned with technological companies or pure research contexts.

The emergence of many tools supported by AI, for multiple purposes and simple to use (e.g., ChatGPT), has made some of the tasks that were time-consuming and/ or only within the reach of professionals with higher technological skills simple and fast for everyone. This reality – generative AI – which can be understood in its succinct flow of operation according to figure 1, has provided brand managers with a wealth of precious resources that empower their role and their ability to understand and make decisions. In this context, AI-driven tools will increasingly become productivity tools for marketers and business decision-makers.

Even though the current stage of AI penetration in companies is the inclusion of AI-tools (and this stage will probably be maintained for a long time in small companies), the future will be the full addition of a whole new layer of AI in the remaining layers, and their processes, of organizations, as happened with the layer of classical information systems (non-AI).

Think about how AI can be strategically and functionally included in companies, in order to create a functional link between understanding the market and, in particular, customers and their aspirations, and decision making, from its operational to strategic aspects, should govern any new information systems architecture.
