# Consumer Preferences

To better understand consumer preferences, the respondents were asked about the platforms they engage the most in and the elements that greatly attract them in digital marketing campaigns.

**Preferred Online Platforms.** The main idea behind question 3 was to understand which online platforms can reach more people and what factors attract them to engage there. Most of the respondents engage in more than one platform depending on what they need at that specific time. Instagram is the most mentioned platform by eight respondents (R1, R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R7, R9) as it is one of the most used platforms which makes it easy to communicate with friends. Seven respondents (R2, R3, R5, R7, R8, R9, R10) engage in Facebook platform because they can easily interact with friends there, the platform provides recommendations and is easy to use. Google was mentioned by two respondents (R2, R10) and they used it to search for something specific. LinkedIn was mentioned by R10 to find events or information related to the respondent’s job, Reddit was mentioned by R1 to find interesting information, and YouTube was mentioned by R5 to watch content made by foreigners.

**Effective Digital Ads’ Characteristics.** Question 4 focused on providing the most appealing attributes and elements in digital ads. Understanding the key factors that contribute to engagement with digital ads can yield impactful campaigns and positive results. Respondents provided essential factors that significantly increase their engagement and resonate with them.
