## DISCUSSION OF THE MODEL THROUGH SCENARIOS

This chapter reflects on some scenarios that help to discuss the proposed conceptual model, not in an attempt to validate it but by discussing some finer and more concrete aspects that help to understand the model from a more operational perspective. However, the technological aspects of technology implementation are not discussed.

### Brand Internationalization Scenario

AI, along with the suggested model, has the potential to assist organizations in making well-informed choices regarding potential strategies for international expansion.

In this context, AI can carry out market research in order to increase the understanding of the needs of international customers and adapt the company’s products and services to potential international customers and understand market trends. So, using artificial intelligence, companies will be able to collect, organize and analyze data on potential international markets, in order to identify trends and patterns.

It also allows the company to obtain information about market entry requirements, such as legal requirements.

With the help of AI, brands can communicate in real time in different languages, humanize communication according to the profile of their customers, as well as adjust and translate communication artifacts.

Chatbot systems with Generative AI allow you to establish dialogues with customers in their language, making communication more natural.

In this context, the proposed model based on AI provides companies with skills that speed up and promote the chances of successful penetration into international markets.

### Penetration in Market Niches Scenario

Given a company’s strategy of penetrating new market niches, the proposed model supported by AI makes it possible to facilitate and speed up marketing actions, including the identification of market niches, understand the behavior and environment of potential consumers, identify sales and distribution channels, customize communication content according to consumers’ needs and preferences, trigger communication automation mechanisms to seduce potential customers, among others.

As such, AI can analyze large volumes of data (Big Data) to identify patterns and trends allowing companies to identify market niches.

Based on the analysis of target audience behavior, especially footprint and behavior in the digital world, Deep Learning can be used to segment target audiences and design more assertive communication and marketing strategies. Using intelligent algorithms and big data, brands and/or companies can identify keywords relevant to their market niche and create optimized content, aiming to increase their visibility in search engines.

Still based on data analysis, AI can also be used to identify products and services that may be of interest to potential consumers for certain market niches.

Companies and/or brands can also develop automation of marketing actions, such as sending emails, customer service chatbot, publishing content on social networks, among others, with the aim of seducing and attracting potential customers.

### Launching a New Brand Scenario

Similar to other scenarios already described, the proposed model supported by AI could assist in the launch of new brands by allowing: analyzing large volumes of data, allowing trends and patterns to be identified in the market; carry out market studies identifying and characterizing both the target audience and the competition; automate various marketing actions, among others.

By analyzing the feelings of the target audience and/or consumers on social networks and other online platforms, it will be possible to obtain feedback on how brands are being received by the market and monitor the public’s reaction to communication based and marketing actions and campaigns.

AI can, based on data analysis, help predict future trends, anticipating market demand. This forecast may allow you to adjust and adapt the launch strategy for both products and brands. Additionally, it is possible to segment the audience more effectively and direct marketing actions and campaigns to the right people at the right time.

Launching a new brand generally involves developing different communication channels, such as a website, mobile apps and social networks, but also content to publish on these channels, such as texts, images, videos. So, in this context, a model based on AI tools will simplify and accelerate the production of both communication channels and the production of content and its publication.

### Rebranding Scenario

Companies will be able to use AI to monitor brands and quickly understand any decline in demand or even the rejection of one of their brands. This perception could lead to informed decision-making and be the stimulus to promote redesign or eventually introduce profound changes to the brand, in this case opting for rebranding.

AI can assist and accelerate the development of the brand’s visual identity, including creating the logo, choosing colors and typographic fonts, creating graphics, images and videos, among others. It also allows you to generate copywriting ideas and assist in the production and publication of content.

Just like in other scenarios, in a rebranding scenario, AI can also be very useful in helping to carry out market studies, analyzing data to infer trends and patterns and understanding market sentiments, customizing communication in function of market segmentation but also of the individual profile of each customer, monitoring the success of marketing actions and campaigns.

## CONCLUSION

Companies have always sought to improve their competitive capacity and generate wealth. In this evolution, information systems have leveraged a whole set of opportunities from increased performance, the ability to act globally, to meet time-to-market, among several other dimensions. AI, whose first works date back to the 1950s, today presents a completely new dimension with the development of generative AI. Partly because Generative AI reached people and increased the discussion around this technology.

Current events show that ordinary people are taking advantage of generative AI, as are companies, but on a completely different scale and goals. Increasingly, generative AI will be “the great critical success factor” for the success/survival of companies.

In this context, this work discusses how AI can empower brands, the main contributions are: It frames the importance of brands and how they can represent a factor of competitiveness and value creation; contextualizes the way in which AI is transforming organizational processes and reflects on the opportunities that AI represents for brands and organizations. We emphasize that the incorporation of artificial intelligence as a brand’s autopilot means a fundamental change in the way companies operate and interact with consumers.

After identifying an eclectic set of AI tools that can be used by organizations in the context of marketing, we propose a conceptual model supported by generative AI which aims to enable organizations to leverage and empower their brands.

Finally, we present four scenarios in which AI can help to obtain competitive advantages by streamline and accelerate the processes inherent to the internationalization of brands, the penetration of market niches, the launch of new brands and rebranding.

Inevitably, there are certain constraints inherent in this work. Considering that the topic of generative AI is still quite recent, the bibliography on the topic is not abundant, especially the bibliography on the application of generative AI in business areas. Regarding the use of generative AI applications by organizations, as a disruptive technology, it is not yet being used en masse as the tools are still in the maturing phase and developing advanced functionalities.
