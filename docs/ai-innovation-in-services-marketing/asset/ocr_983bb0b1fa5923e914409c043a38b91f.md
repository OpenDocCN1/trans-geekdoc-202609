By analyzing the feelings of the target audience and/or consumers on social

how brands are being received by the market and monitor the public’s reaction to

AI can, based on data analysis, help predict future trends, anticipating market

right time.

Launching a new brand generally involves developing different communication
channels, such as a website, mobile apps and social networks, but also content to
publish on these channels, such as texts, images, videos. So, in this context, a model
based on AI tools will simplify and accelerate the production of both communication
channels and the production of content and its publication.

# Rebranding Scenario

Companies will be able to use AI to monitor brands and quickly understand any
decline in demand or even the rejection of one of their brands. This perception
could lead to informed decision-making and be the stimulus to promote redesign or
eventually introduce profound changes to the brand, in this case opting for rebranding.

AI can assist and accelerate the development of the brand’s visual identity,
including creating the logo, choosing colors and typographic fonts, creating graphics,
images and videos, among others. It also allows you to generate copywriting ideas
and assist in the production and publication of content.

Just like in other scenarios, in a rebranding scenario, AI can also be very useful
in helping to carry out market studies, analyzing data to infer trends and patterns
and understanding market sentiments, customizing communication in function of
market segmentation but also of the individual profile of each customer, monitoring
the success of marketing actions and campaigns.
