AI plays an irreplaceable role in the era of the digital economy, providing a vital opportunity for organizations that wish to increase their performance and competitiveness (Li et al., 2024). According to Lee et al. (2023), who carried out a systematic review of the literature, the majority of AI implementations are motivated by process improvement and production efficiency. Also Mikalef et al. (2023) emphasizes that AI skills must be seen as management skills that go beyond the technical aspect, and used in a creative way to add value to the organization, proposing that these skills be considered central, not just secondary.

AI is transforming decision-making processes in organizations by taking over decision-making responsibilities previously assigned only to humans. AI self-learning capacity improves the quality of decisions, marking a fundamental change (Vincent, 2021). One of the examples is, according to Chatterjee et al. (2021), the integration of AI into Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems that is transforming the analysis of large volumes of customer data in organizations where, to efficiently deal with the resulting opportunities and challenges, organizations are developing skills and processes that enhance their agility, aligning with artificial intelligence-powered customer service.

Research conducted by Sullivan & Fosso Wamba 2024) on AI and adaptation to market changes, revealed significant interactions between environmental-hostility and also dynamism in the relationships between AI-powered capabilities and the Adaptive Response to Market Changes (ARMC), as well as ARMC being observed to positively influence company’ innovation and performance.

Regarding the role of AI in branding, according to Musaiqer & Hamdan (2023), AI has become a powerful tool for organizations to improve their strategy and brand-building efforts. Also, according to (Rahman et al., 2023), luxury brands in the fashion sector have been adopting AI-powered digital assistance to enhance the online shopping experience and influence customer engagement. In a study conducted by Ho & Chow (2023), on the role of AI in Hong Kong brand preference for retail banks, AI-driven marketing efforts showed a significant positive effect on brand preference, influencing customer repurchase intention. This suggests that AI marketing efforts should not only be seen as improving the customer experience, but also as an essential tool in building brand image, supporting (Godey et al., 2016).
