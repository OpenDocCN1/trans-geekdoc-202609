# # THEORETICAL BACKGROUND

# ## Services Marketing

According to the Marketing Accountability Standards Board (MASB) (2023a), marketing is a set of institutions and processes for creating, communicating, delivering, and exchanging offerings that have value for consumers, clients, partners, and society as a whole. Marketing is simultaneously considered both an ‘art’ and a ‘science’. Through marketing, new products are created and introduced into the market, and professionals in the field strive to develop innovative solutions to introduce brands, products, and services into the market to meet consumer needs (Keller & Kotler, 2013).

Marketing began in a traditional form with the use of advertising through various communication channels such as radio, television, newspapers, among others. As the demand for new means of communication increased, new areas within marketing emerged such as relational marketing, digital marketing, content marketing, product marketing, service marketing, among others (Bhayani & Vachhani, 2014).

Relational marketing aims to foster brand and/or company loyalty among customers. To make them true brand advocates, it is necessary to establish and maintain a lasting and trusting relationship between the company/brand and its customers. The main idea is to keep the consumer engaged and satisfied, as a loyal customer is more likely to make new purchases, recommend the company to others, and even acquire products and services at premium prices. Within relational marketing, several main strategies are used including personalized service, the use of loyalty programs, exclusive promotions, frequent and targeted communication across various channels, among others (Keller & Kotler, 2013; Mailchimp, 2023b; Zinkhan, 2002).

On the other hand, digital marketing combines a set of marketing strategies to promote products, services, and/or brands through digital means. These digital strategies aim to attract, engage, and convert potential customers into real customers by utilizing tools such as Search Engine Optimization (SEO), online advertising, marketing automation, data analysis, and other techniques. Digital marketing has become increasingly important for companies as consumer behavior undergoes constant changes and most people spend a significant amount of time connected to the Internet, whether through mobile devices, computers, or tablets. Because of this, investing in digital marketing is necessary for companies to show up where their audience is, allowing them to reach and connect with their customers more efficiently and effectively (Krishen et al., 2021; Mailchimp, 2023a; Torres, 2009).
