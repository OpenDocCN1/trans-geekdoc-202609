# LITERATURE REVIEW ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN SERVICE SECTORS

Literature wise also the use of AI in service sector has been quite in discussion. Chatbots and virtual assistants are increasingly being used to handle customer inquiries, provide technical support, and personalize customer interactions for long (Lemon & Cerpa, 2016). Also, AI algorithms are employed for targeted advertising, lead generation, and dynamic pricing to optimize marketing campaigns and improve sales conversions (Brynjolfsson, Hitt, & Kim, 2017).AI-powered systems are used for route optimization, predictive maintenance, and automated inventory management to streamline operations and reduce costs (Kearney, 2018). AI algorithms are utilized for fraud detection, credit risk assessment, and personalized financial advice, leading to greater efficiency and improved customer engagement (PWC, 2017). AI is revolutionizing healthcare through medical diagnosis, personalized treatment plans, and robotic surgery, offering greater accuracy and improved patient outcomes (Esteva et al., 2019). Madlberger (2023) found that AI is being used in a variety of ways in the service sector, including to improve customer service, personalize marketing, increase sales, and streamline operations. Bharadwaj et al. (2020) argued that AI is the next frontier for value creation in the service sector. Lee et al. (2022) provided a comprehensive review of the literature on AI in the service sector, highlighting the various ways in which AI is being used to improve customer service, personalize marketing, increase sales, and streamline operations.
