## BACKGROUND OF SERVICES MARKETING AND USE OF AI IN SERVICE SECTORS

Artificial intelligence (AI) has been steadily infiltrating the service sector for years, and its impact is only deepening. The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in service sectors has been transformative, reshaping processes, enhancing customer experiences, and driving operational efficiency. This portion explores the landscape of AI applications in services, examining its historical trajectory, current trends, and future implications. Let’s take a glimpse into the historical background of its use:

### Early Exploration (1960s-1990s)

The initial foray of AI in services focused on automating basic tasks and replicating human expertise. Rule-based systems and chatbots emerged, handling straightforward inquiries and scheduling appointments (Melnik & Shah, 2003). Expert systems captured knowledge in specific domains, aiding decision-making in areas like medical diagnosis (Shortliffe, 1979). However, these early systems were limited in robustness. ### Simple Automation

The commencement of exploring AI based services dates back to basic usage. The earliest applications focused on automating basic tasks like scheduling appointments, routing calls, and generating basic reports. It started with the rudimentary chatbots and rule-based systems.

### Expert Systems

AI captured human expertise in specific domains (e.g., medical diagnosis) to guide decision-making could be traced back as early usage. Though these systems were limited in scope and adaptability.

### Predictive Analytics

Early forms of machine learning emerged, enabling basic data analysis and basic predictions like customer churn, fraud detection.

### Rise of Modern AI (2000s-Present)

The dawn of the 21st century ushered in an era of advanced AI techniques, fueled by increased computing power and vast data availability. Machine learning algorithms enabled predictive analytics, forecasting customer churn or identifying fraudulent activities (Brynjolfsson & McAfee, 2014). Natural language processing blossomed, leading to the development of sophisticated chatbots and virtual assistants capable of engaging in natural conversations and offering personalized customer service (Ghoosh, 2020). AI algorithms began informing complex decision-making, influencing pricing strategies, credit risk assessments, and even insurance claims processing (Chen & Moore, 2012). ### Advancements in Computing Power and Data

Increased processing capabilities and vast data availability fueled the development of more sophisticated AI techniques like deep learning and natural language processing.

### Personalization and Customization

AI enabled businesses to personalize customer experiences, tailor recommendations, and offer dynamic services based on individual needs and preferences.

### Chatbots and Virtual Assistants

Conversational AI revolutionized customer service with chatbots that handle basic inquiries, schedule appointments, and even offer emotional support.

### Automated Decision-Making

AI algorithms started informing pricing strategies, credit risk assessments, and even insurance claims processing.

### Current Landscape of AI

The current landscape of AI in services is teeming with innovation.

### Increased Automation

AI is automating ever-more complex tasks, including document analysis, medical image diagnosis, and financial forecasting, freeing human employees for higher-level cognitive work (Manyika et al., 2017).

### Hyper-Personalization

AI continues to refine its ability to personalize experiences, predicting individual needs and desires in real-time across various service sectors. As already highlighted, AI's ability to personalize experiences is reaching new heights, predicting individual desire and transferring tailored services in real-time across various sectors based on those needs and desires (Sarker & Sarker, 2020).

### Emergence of Robotic Process Automation (RPA)

Software robots mimic human actions to automate repetitive tasks, further streamlining operations and boosting efficiency. (Van der Auwermeulen et al., 2017).

Looking ahead, AI promises to transform services even further. Self-driving cars and drones could revolutionize delivery (Amazon Prime Air for last mile delivering AI-powered drones to deliver packages directly to customers' doorsteps; these drones can autonomously navigate predetermined routes, avoiding obstacles and adhering to safety regulations, Ford and Walmart have partnered to develop a fleet of self-driving delivery trucks; these trucks use AI to optimize routes, navigate traffic, and safely deliver goods to their destinations, DHL utilizes AI-powered drones equipped with LiDAR and machine vision technology to automate inventory counting and tracking; these drones can quickly scan shelves and identify discrepancies, improving accuracy and efficiency, Zipline operates a network of fixed-wing drones that can autonomously deliver critical medical supplies, such as vaccines and blood products, to remote villages and healthcare facilities.) while AI-powered medical diagnostics and treatment may reshape healthcare. Personalized education and entertainment tailored to individual preferences are also on the horizon.

## LITERATURE REVIEW ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN SERVICE SECTORS

Literature wise also the use of AI in service sector has been quite in discussion. Chatbots and virtual assistants are increasingly being used to handle customer inquiries, provide technical support, and personalize customer interactions for long (Lemon & Cerpa, 2016). Also, AI algorithms are employed for targeted advertising, lead generation, and dynamic pricing to optimize marketing campaigns and improve sales conversions (Brynjolfsson, Hitt, & Kim, 2017). AI-powered systems are used for route optimization, predictive maintenance, and automated inventory management to streamline operations and reduce costs (Kearney, 2018). AI algorithms are utilized for fraud detection, credit risk assessment, and personalized financial advice, leading to greater efficiency and improved customer engagement (PWC, 2017). AI is revolutionizing healthcare through medical diagnosis, personalized treatment plans, and robotic surgery, offering greater accuracy and improved patient outcomes (Esteva et al., 2019). Madlberger (2023) found that AI is being used in a variety of ways in the service sector, including to improve customer service, personalize marketing, increase sales, and streamline operations. Bharadwaj et al. (2020) argued that AI is the next frontier for value creation in the service sector. Lee et al. (2022) provided a comprehensive review of the literature on AI in the service sector, highlighting the various ways in which AI is being used to improve customer service, personalize marketing, increase sales, and streamline operations.
