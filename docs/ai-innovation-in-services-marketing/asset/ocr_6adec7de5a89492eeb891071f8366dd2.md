# Implement Sustainable and Socially Responsible Initiatives

Nowadays, companies are under increasing pressure to address the ideas of social and sustainable practices and they need to connect with the changing values of their customers. As sustainable efforts satisfy consumers’ need to make socially conscious decisions, consequently it improves the brand's attractiveness and creates long-lasting relationships. The empirical research data results show that social and sustainable practices exceed consumer expectations and have a positive effect on how a brand is perceived. Customers are engaged with those kinds of campaigns and share positive feedback as they show a desire to support socially conscious companies. Moreover, it empowers people to look for ways to change the world, taking part in those campaigns and using their experiences as self-expression. Consumers are spreading the word about the campaign, engaging in different discussions, and promoting it within their social network which creates an emotional connection with a brand. By including these aspects in digital marketing campaigns, advertisers would notice higher engagement levels as these ads encourage a purpose of shared values that are of deep importance to customers.
