Ad placement diversification acknowledges the intrinsic variability of the digital audience and recognizes that different customers visit different platforms and websites, and respond better to different ad formats. By using diverse ad formats, the companies could greatly increase their reach by appealing to different audience preferences. From the empirical research, it is clear that people respond differently to the ads’ formats. Thus, adopting many formats makes the content accessible to more people. For example, video content works best for platforms like YouTube and Instagram as it can be easily shared. Visuals perform well on Facebook and Instagram, appealing to users who are drawn to visually appealing information. Stories (format 1080x1920) and feed posts (format 1080x1080) in these platforms can even be seamlessly integrated as a part of the user experience rather than a disruptive marketing campaign. Moreover, the younger generation likes to listen to podcasts on YouTube or Spotify and it provides a possibility to easily reach these customers with high-quality audio material. Therefore, understanding in which online platforms the target audience engages the most and to which ad formats it responds the best helps to maximize user engagement and their browsing experience.

# Act

Clear call-to-action (CTA) buttons, optimized landing pages, and A/B testing are solutions that work together to generate an action in the “Act” phase of the RACE framework (Table 11). CTA encourages users to complete an intended action, landing pages direct customers to do that action, and A/B testing matches user preferences to build a coherent journey that drives a user to act.
