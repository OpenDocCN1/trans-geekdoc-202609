# Preface

In the ever-accelerating realm of business, the fusion of artificial intelligence (AI) with services marketing has surged to unprecedented levels. The dynamic landscape of AI is reshaping how services are conceptualized, delivered, and experienced. Welcome to AI Innovation in Services Marketing, a meticulously curated exploration into the transformative potential of AI within the services industry.

As editors, we are thrilled to present this compendium that delves deep into the profound implications of AI for services marketing and its profound impact on consumer behavior. This book is a testament to the evolving nature of marketing, where AI stands as a pivotal force driving innovation and disruption.

Our primary aim is to provide readers with a comprehensive understanding of AI's role in revolutionizing services marketing. Through a diverse array of real-world examples and case studies, we elucidate how AI amplifies productivity and enriches customer experiences. Moreover, we conscientiously navigate the ethical considerations surrounding AI adoption in services marketing, recognizing the importance of responsible innovation.

This book is a valuable resource tailored for a diverse audience. Whether you are a marketing scholar, educator, student, researcher, or industry practitioner, our aim is to equip you with the knowledge and insights needed to navigate the AI-powered landscape of services marketing.

Throughout these pages, you will encounter a rich tapestry of topics ranging from the convergence of services and AI to the ethical dimensions of AI-enhanced marketing. Our curated collection spans from foundational concepts to cutting-edge trends, ensuring relevance and applicability in today's dynamic marketplace.

Chapter 1 delves into the transformative potential of artificial intelligence (AI) within marketing, particularly through the lens of an inventive campaign orchestrated by Invest Lithuania. By synergizing Account-Based Marketing (ABM), storytelling, and AI techniques, the campaign aimed to engage target contacts and bolster business opportunities in the game development sector. The study reveals how innovative marketing approaches can cultivate long-term relationships and foster sustainable business growth, showcasing the promising prospects that lie ahead.
