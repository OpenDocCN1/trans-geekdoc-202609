# Rise of Modern AI (2000s-Present)

The dawn of the 21st century ushered in an era of advanced AI techniques, inflamed by increased computing power and vast data availability. Machine learning algorithms enabled predictive analytics, forecasting customer churn or identifying fraudulent activities (Brynjolfsson & McAfee, 2014). Natural language processing blossomed, leading to the development of sophisticated chatbots and virtual assistants capable of engaging in natural conversations and offering personalized customer service (Ghoosh, 2020).
