# 10. CONCLUSION

Service innovations powered with tools like Artificial intelligence, Machine Learning, Augmented and Virtual Reality and Robotics, etc., offer unlimited disruptive business strategies for the firms to excel. Service innovation is viewed as a multidimensional construct and is now adopted by firms for value creation and value delivery. This “synthesis approach,” based on both products and services, is an evolution over and above the earlier assimilation approach of service innovation and fueling new-age business models. The business frameworks based on the “shared economy” have transformed the general concept of ownership and pushed consumers to share the access products, thus leading to sustainability. On the same lines, the open innovation system leads to the pooling of better ideas from external knowledge sources. Firms with IT-based businesses vehhemently use this idea as open source software and disrupt the monopoly of proprietary software worldwide. On the other hand, innovative business models used by Uber or Netflix represent the essence of the Gig economy, where work is performed based on the demand generated. It consists of autonomous contractors and freelancers networked with their target market through common digital platforms managed by companies like AirBnB or Swiggy. In a nutshell, all disruptions provide customers more power with more personalized strategies and incessant innovative approaches adopted by service firms.
