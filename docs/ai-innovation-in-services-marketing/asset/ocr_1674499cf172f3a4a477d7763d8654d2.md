# Include User-Generated Content

Empirical research has shown that customers’ views have a significant impact on their needs for trust and informed decision-making, thus, it is essential to find a solution that addresses these needs through transparent and honest marketing practices. User-generated content fulfills the need for honest and accurate information, providing a sense of social proof and authenticity of a product that both have an impact on people’s choices and are essential for making purchases. User-generated content reduces worries and uncertainties by showcasing satisfaction and real-life experiences and providing confidence and reassurance through positive reviews and comments. It allows people to share their experiences of the product, encouraging user engagement and participation which builds a community and increases customer loyalty. When consumers are continuously sharing their experiences and opinions, it guarantees that the information is reliable and up-to-date which can encourage people to convert. Moreover, UGC might even increase organic website traffic and engagement by raising organic search visibility. To sum up, the companies would benefit by including user-generated content in its campaigns as it contributes to establishing trust and credibility that raise a possibility of quicker engagement and conversion.

# Use Remarketing Campaigns

The goal of remarketing is to get prospective customers back to a website by reminding them of products and services they have already looked at. Advertisers can improve their capacity to target certain consumer groups with personalized ads by using first-party identifiers such as cookies to generate comprehensive user profiles and by using remarketing lists for search ads or remarketing on Google display network (RLSA & RGDN) (Israfilzade & Guliyeva, 2023).
