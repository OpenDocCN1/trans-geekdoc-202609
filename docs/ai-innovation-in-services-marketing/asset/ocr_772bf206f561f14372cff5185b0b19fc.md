**Negative Sides of Digital Ads.** Question 11 focused on the common mistakes in digital marketing campaigns that tend to irritate consumers. Understanding these elements can help avoid mistakes that might harm a company’s reputation, create a more enjoyable online experience, and ensure more effective digital marketing campaigns.

Table 8 presents negatively perceived elements in digital marketing campaigns. The most mentioned were pop-up ads as they are considered too pushy and distracting by the respondents. Two respondents’ arguments on the annoyance of the pop-up ads are presented below:

> “Pop-up ads are probably the worst as it feels like someone is trying to get money from you.” (R1)

> “Pop-up ads stop my interest immediately. I don’t even look at it and subconsciously I can’t remember what it was about and want to close it as soon as possible.” (R5)
