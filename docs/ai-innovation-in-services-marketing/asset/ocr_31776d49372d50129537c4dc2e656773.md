# 2.3. The Role of Gender in the Relationship Between AI and Job Performance

As discussed above, gender is an important individual characteristic that significantly influences job performance (Abusalma, 2021; Hoque et al., 2019; Khan & Vieito, 2013). On the one hand, many contributions (Abusalma, 2021; Allison et al., 2023; Hoque et al., 2019; Igbaria & Shayo, 1997) ascertained that men tend to outperform women. Due to their talents and diligence, only male employees were more likely to achieve excellent performance (Igbaria & Shayo, 1997). On the other hand, Kiefer et al. (2022) stated that male-led businesses perform better than female-led businesses. However, they also added that female-led companies are outperforming their male-led competitors in terms of performance results in certain instances. In the same vein, Khan & Vieito (2013) stated that companies tend to have the lowest levels of risks when it is led by female CEOs. Therefore, this reduced amount of risk may help the company perform better.
