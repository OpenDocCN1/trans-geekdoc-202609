From the client perspective, results from a study conducted by McLean et al. Data privacy concerns and fears of unauthorized use negatively impact consumer brand engagement through VAs, highlighting the need to address these issues to promote greater trust.

AI offers numerous opportunities to gain competitive advantages, but faces challenges, especially in implementing AI technologies for customer-service. This involves dilemmas such as the Personalization-Privacy Paradox, where personalization is crucial but there are privacy concerns. To establish a customer-centric ecosystem, it is necessary to balance AI-driven value co-creation and value co-destruction concerns (Chaturvedi & Verma, 2023a). We highlight the importance of people’s participation in the creation of new AI-based mechanisms to achieve a desired balance between technology and the human dimension – only then will we have a situation where everyone wins.

# AI AS AN AUTOPILOT FOR BRANDS

In modern business, AI integration has emerged as a transformative push, re-engineering brand strategies and customer experiences. This chapter investigates the concept of using AI as an autopilot for brands, exploring its multifaceted role in revolutionizing customer interactions, driving strategic decision-making processes and promoting sustainable growth in an increasingly competitive.

A comprehensive understanding of customer preferences and behaviors is at the heart of effective brand management (Heding et al., 2020). AI algorithms, equipped with advanced data analytics capabilities, empower brands to extract actionable insights from vast data sets. By analyzing multiple data sources, such as social media interactions, web transactions, and customer feedback, AI makes it easier to create detailed customer profiles.
