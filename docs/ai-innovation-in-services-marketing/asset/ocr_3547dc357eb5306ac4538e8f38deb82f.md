It is characterized by choice to perform, autonomy due to the absence of supervisors and focus on the moment’s needs (Ravenelle, 2019, pp. 5-6). **7.3.1. Uberizations:** In the gig economy, Uberization is the business model practised, transforming the market for services with the help of the latest technology, especially smartphones. In this business model, digital platforms or mobile apps replaced the middlemen required to connect talent to the right audience. Such platforms enabled comprehensive interaction between the provider and customer and ensured the minimum quality standards. The model got its name from Uber, creating a platform for people to hire and pay for rides. This uberization extended to any on-demand services such as food, stay, transportation, etc.

**7.3.2. Netflix Model:** The business model adopted by Netflix is an example of integrating the latest technology to fulfill customer expectations by disrupting established market structures. It redefined most of the value creation and value proposition elements by transforming DVD on rent to video streaming services. To retain customers and keep them interested, Netflix invests in new and exclusive content as its value proposition, thus reaching beyond pricing. Another innovative feature that catalyzes success is its recommendation system, which offers suggestions to users based on their consumption patterns. This data mining tool has taken Netflix to another level in terms of value creation and key resources as the basis for the core competencies of its business. It is pre-emptive by forecasting future growth and endowing high-scalability solutions in the gig economy.

**7.3.3. Cloud Sharing:** Cloud technology provides an exciting value proposition for businesses to outsource their Information technology ecosystem. It results in less cost and more avenues for service-based models, leading to business models’ operational success and sustainability. The cloud-based business model can be Software as a Service (SaaS), Platform as a Service (PaaS), and Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS). Interestingly, many more innovative cloud models are evolving every day as per the requirements of business processes.
