# ANALYSIS OF THE INVEST LITHUANIA CASE STUDY

After a thorough review of internal documents and reports related to the company’s strategy, including past marketing campaigns and their outcomes, it became apparent that there is a lack of knowledge and know-how in identifying the most effective marketing tools, channels, and potential target lists. There was also a noticeable absence of constructive collaboration between the marketing and sales teams, an essential element for the successful execution of campaigns and overall organizational performance. According to Vajre (2016), “marketing must play a part in sales acceleration, and sales must play a part in marketing” (p. 41). Despite this, Invest Lithuania’s marketing and sales teams have continuously executed campaigns within their target industries in recent years. These initiatives involved testing various channels, tools, and strategies and adapting and modifying strategic KPIs while exploring automation options via customer relationship management (CRM) systems.

In response to these evolving circumstances and the insights gained from past endeavors, it was determined that Invest Lithuania’s marketing and sales teams should embrace a new approach. As a result, the decision was made to implement ABM, a personalized B2B marketing strategy to target specific companies. This strategic shift aims to enhance engagement with target foreign investors by tailoring content to their specific needs and preferences (Burgess & Munn, 2021). The expectation is that the personalized approach inherent in ABM can facilitate smoother communication and lead to increased sales opportunities.

The decision to implement the One-to-Few ABM campaign was deliberate and well-considered. This approach was selected for its suitability when targeting a specific subset of potential clients within the broader target audience (Agaba, 2021). By focusing on a select group, the campaign aimed to create more personalized and tailored interactions by optimizing the chance of engaging these specific prospects effectively. This strategic choice aligns with the campaign’s core objectives, enhancing its impact and success.

Given this, the imperative strategic decision to utilize AI tools for creating a more captivating marketing campaign emerged. This choice was particularly significant considering the characteristics of the target contacts. It is well-established that an extraordinary effort is required to truly captivate game developers, embodying creativity and delivering the element of surprise (Brynjolfsson & McAfee, 2014). It is essential to acknowledge that this undertaking goes beyond traditional marketing methods. The aim is to establish an emotional connection and evoke a sense of awe, with the overarching objective of leaving a remarkable and enduring impression on the target audience.
