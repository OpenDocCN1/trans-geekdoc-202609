Current events show that ordinary people are taking advantage of generative AI, as are companies, but on a completely different scale and goals. Increasingly, generative AI will be “the great critical success factor” for the success/survival of companies.

In this context, this work discusses how AI can empower brands, the main contributions are: It frames the importance of brands and how they can represent a factor of competitiveness and value creation; contextualizes the way in which AI is transforming organizational processes and reflects on the opportunities that AI represents for brands and organizations. We emphasize that the incorporation of artificial intelligence as a brand’s autopilot means a fundamental change in the way companies operate and interact with consumers.

After identifying an eclectic set of AI tools that can be used by organizations in the context of marketing, we propose a conceptual model supported by generative AI which aims to enable organizations to leverage and empower their brands.

Finally, we present four scenarios in which AI can help to obtain competitive advantages by streamline and accelerate the processes inherent to the internationalization of brands, the penetration of market niches, the launch of new brands and rebranding.

Inevitably, there are certain constraints inherent in this work. Considering that the topic of generative AI is still quite recent, the bibliography on the topic is not abundant, especially the bibliography on the application of generative AI in business areas. Regarding the use of generative AI applications by organizations, as a disruptive technology, it is not yet being used en masse as the tools are still in the maturing phase and developing advanced functionalities.
