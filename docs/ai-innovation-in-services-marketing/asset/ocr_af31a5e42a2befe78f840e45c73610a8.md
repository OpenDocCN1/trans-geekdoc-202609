After all the postcards had been prepared and printed, they were dispatched sequentially, every three to four days, to the target contacts via courier using the envelopes displayed below (Figure 3).

These envelopes were also given a “gamified” touch, with pixelated postage stamps on the front side and a heart-shaped pixelated image along with the hashtag #LITHUANIA LOVES GAMEDEV on the back side. In alignment with the viewpoint Barnard and Parker (2012) presented, it is imperative to maintain consistency across all communication facets of the campaign. This study includes visuals, text, tone of voice, packaging, digital tools, and channels, such as QR codes and landing pages. Maintaining consistency is vital for creating an elevated experience and receiving positive feedback.

Proactive communication from the sales team was also crucial to enhance the campaign’s effectiveness (Vajre, 2016). They utilized semi-personalized texts and aligned their “follow-up” actions with the narrative conveyed in the postcards, yielding evident results.

# The Results of the ABM Campaign

The representatives from eight of the ten target companies who received the postcards responded positively after receiving the sales team’s first or second follow-up message. It is crucial to highlight that due to postage issues, not all recipients received postcards; in fact, some postcards were returned. This situation influenced the overall response rate. However, in B2B marketing, especially within niche sectors like game development, even a few meaningful engagements can hold significant value (Taylor, 2017).
