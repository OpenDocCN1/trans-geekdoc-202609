# DESIGN OF THE CHAPTER

The chapter would have two portions as already mentioned. The first portion would mainly deal with the contemporary literature review on the background and related aspects of use of AI in service sectors through a chronological data exploration. The second portion would deal with a miniscule survey with Indian target consumers for service sectors in the age range of eighteen to above fifty years to check the awareness, knowledge, frequency of usage, areas of usage and satisfaction level of the aforementioned class of respondents and their comfort level with the use of AI in service sector. There has been also an endeavour to seek the answers of whether there is any mental apprehensions working at the back of the mind of the respondents regarding fostering the AI in service sector or not, since this is comparatively an emerging trend in the service sectors. This study is based on qualitative analysis through a theme based pattern coding or category coding . The questionnaire used is a semi structured questionnaire which has been surveyed through online forms, WhatsApp messages, video and phone conferencing with some open ended and nominal response based dichotomous/ multiple choice questions. The sampling technique is purposive with the condition of the respondents to be familiar with the word AI, irrespective of the proper awareness or not.
