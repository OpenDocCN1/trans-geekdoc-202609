# A BRAND AS A VALUE CREATION FACTOR

In the contemporary world, brands are almost omnipresent, becoming part of our daily lives. Their influence can be observed across multiple industries, cultural sector and even as a mechanism for affirmation of territories.

The brand concept can be defined as: “marketing-related intangible asset including, but not limited to, names, terms, signs, symbols, logos and designs, or a combination of these, intended to identify goods, services or entities, or a combination of these, creating distinctive images and associations in the minds of stakeholders, thereby generating economic benefits/values” (ISO 10668:2010).

For any company, brands are the intangible asset most valuable (Keller & Lehmann, 2003). However, the concept of brand is multifaceted and relatively complex, both from the point of view of companies that provide services or create goods under their own brand, and from the point of view of consumers who perceive that brand. The reality is that the brand image desired and projected by the organization does not always coincide with the brand’s perception by customers. Additionally, brands are under the influence of changes constantly, in marketing and the external environment, requiring constant adaptation to market trends and consumer demand.

Presently, technological advances have led to a highly interconnected global landscape, necessitating a new evaluation in the field of branding research from the standpoints of businesses, customers, and the community. According to Swaminathan et al. (2020), the hyperconnectivity has enabled established brands to expand their geographic reach and social influence, while new types of brand entities (such as ideas, people, places, and organizational brands) are further expanding the brand landscape.

A brand brings together a set of attributes, benefits, symbols and experiences that identify and differentiate a product, service, company or person from its competitors. Brands have the power to influence consumers’ perceptions, preferences, attitudes and behaviors, generating loyalty, trust, satisfaction and recommendation.
