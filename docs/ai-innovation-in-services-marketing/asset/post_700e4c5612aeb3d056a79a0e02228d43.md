
## Create Captivating Calls to Action

Clear call-to-action buttons provide a direction and an incentive for customers to engage with digital marketing campaigns. CTAs act as a link between an intended action and the purpose that the consumer is trying to achieve. As customers are overloaded with information, they seek a clear way to complete an action online and search more deeply into the product offered. Call-to-action buttons encourage a quick response and attract customers to click and look at the website. As expressed by the respondents in the empirical research, clear CTA is important as without it people become confused and uninterested, resulting in a decreased effectiveness of the campaign. If the ad is relevant for consumers and they are ready to be engaged, it is essential to provide them with a quick way to the engagement. CTAs help to navigate through the campaign effectively by offering a structured way to engage with the ad and save customers time as they are clearly guided to take a simple and easy action that gets them to a relevant landing page. Therefore, a well-designed call-to-action button meets consumers’ needs for navigation, clarity, and simplicity which increases their satisfaction and engagement levels.

## Optimize Landing Pages

From the empirical research conducted before, it is clear that people need optimized landing pages to get an easy-to-use experience when they want to take action. To provide an incentive for the next action to be taken, it is important to present the content in an understandable and user-friendly way, ensuring that the page loads quickly and has a mobile-responsive design to suit any device. In this way, customers can do the intended action of making a purchase, signing up, or contacting the company more easily and they are more likely to act further. Optimized landing pages in digital marketing campaigns would eliminate the barriers that stop consumers from engaging, such as waiting for a landing page to load, seeing poorly presented content, or not finding the exact information the user was looking for. Consequently, by optimizing its landing pages, advertisers would present its digital ads as clear and consistent with accurate and precise information.

## Experiment With A/B Testing

A/B testing provides the freedom to test different ad components like visuals, headlines, and call-to-actions to determine the ones that connect with the target audience in the best way. For example, Optimizely can be used to conduct A/B tests that simultaneously improve layout and design, producing aesthetically pleasing user interfaces and determining the versions that complement a particular target group’s preferences. By adding a variety of features, the content becomes more engaging and motivates customers to take action by meeting their demand for interactive and appealing experiences. It guarantees that the message is easily understandable while experimenting with different words, ideas, and communication styles. Empirical research results showed that people’s preferences vary and it is important to have different versions of ads made for each unique target group – the same product can be promoted to some people by providing clear and structured information, while to others by including interactive or humour elements. A/B testing ensures that the right message reaches the right people and offers unique content to each one of them. It is an ongoing process that can maximize campaigns online presence by deeply analyzing its customers and continuously enhancing the campaigns to match their preferences. In the long term, it can even reduce bounce rates as A/B testing ensures that all elements work together to engage and retain users.

## Include User-Generated Content

Empirical research has shown that customers’ views have a significant impact on their needs for trust and informed decision-making, thus, it is essential to find a solution that addresses these needs through transparent and honest marketing practices. User-generated content fulfills the need for honest and accurate information, providing a sense of social proof and authenticity of a product that both have an impact on people’s choices and are essential for making purchases. User-generated content reduces worries and uncertainties by showcasing satisfaction and real-life experiences and providing confidence and reassurance through positive reviews and comments. It allows people to share their experiences of the product, encouraging user engagement and participation which builds a community and increases customer loyalty. When consumers are continuously sharing their experiences and opinions, it guarantees that the information is reliable and up-to-date which can encourage people to convert. Moreover, UGC might even increase organic website traffic and engagement by raising organic search visibility. To sum up, the companies would benefit by including user-generated content in its campaigns as it contributes to establishing trust and credibility that raise a possibility of quicker engagement and conversion.

## Use Remarketing Campaigns

The goal of remarketing is to get prospective customers back to a website by reminding them of products and services they have already looked at. Advertisers can improve their capacity to target certain consumer groups with personalized ads by using first-party identifiers such as cookies to generate comprehensive user profiles and by using remarketing lists for search ads or remarketing on Google display network (RLSA & RGDN) (Israfilzade & Guliyeva, 2023). They direct the customer towards the desired action such as making a purchase while satisfying a need for personalized and relevant content. By looking at past interactions, advertisers could deliver unique offers with discounts that attract customers’ attention. From the empirical research conducted before, it is clear that special discounts appeal to many consumers, and remarketing campaigns work best for including them and encouraging users to convert. Moreover, remarketing campaigns are an excellent tool to remind consumers of the brand and that was identified as a need in the empirical research to maintain customers’ interest. These campaigns ensure that customers would remember the brand they were previously interested in and think of it in case they are ready to engage again. By targeting consumers based on their past behaviors, remarketing becomes pertinently relevant as the content aligns with each unique individual’s preferences. It also provides personalized experiences with offers and communication styles that are tailored to customers’ browsing history and interests. Therefore, using remarketing campaigns can reinforce brand recall for customers who were interested in the advertiser digital marketing campaigns before and encourage them to convert with personalized and unique offers.

Interactive content and sustainable and socially responsible initiatives actively involve users and relate to the part of the "Engage" phase of the RACE framework (Table 13). ## Include Interactive Content

Interactive content is a base for creating engaging and entertaining experiences for consumers who are expressing a need for interactive and original content. Interactive content such as engaging videos, quizzes, or polls capture customers’ attention and foster a pleasant relationship with a brand. Customers can fully immerse themselves in a brand’s experience when it uses gamification techniques of adding features such as games, competitions, and contests to its marketing campaigns (Wang, 2021). Companies would benefit by including interactive content in their digital marketing campaigns as they become more memorable and shareable. The emphasis placed on high-quality videos, visuals, and audio caters to the special needs of customers seeking entertainment and captivating user experiences.
