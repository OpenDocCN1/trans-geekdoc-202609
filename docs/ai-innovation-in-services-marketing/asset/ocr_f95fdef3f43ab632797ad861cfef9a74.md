# ## Managerial Solutions

The last part of the chapter presents managerial solutions to implement in order to increase customer engagement in its digital marketing campaigns. Suggestions are provided according to the RACE framework and based on the empirical research conducted before.

First, the company must develop a concise plan for its digital marketing campaigns focusing on its target audiences. Table 9 is suggested as a guide to creating effective and engaging digital marketing campaigns, along with the proposed solutions.

Table 9. The steps for a “plan” phase

| Action | Reasoning |
| :--- | :--- |
| Strategy consultation meeting | Conduct a strategy consultation meeting to uncover key insights about the company, understand its objectives, set budget, and align on the strategic directions for the upcoming digital marketing campaigns. |
| Industry analysis | Understand the industry the client is operating in, and its company’s size, and stay informed about industry trends and challenges to tailor campaigns according to current market dynamics. |
| Competitor analysis | Conduct a thorough competitor analysis within the industry to identify opportunities and differentiation points. |
| Audience segmentation analysis | Analyze the clients’ existing customers based on their demographics, interests, behaviors, and preferences. |
| Channel selection | Analyze the effectiveness of different digital channels for reaching target audiences. Select the channels based on budgets, industry norms, platform preferences, and popularity. |
| Development of customer journey maps | Map the customer journey for each target audience segment, identifying relevant and personalized content to guide them through the entire engagement process. |
| Measurement of KPIs | Align KPIs for the specific objectives of each B2B client, ensuring the metrics reflect the campaign’s success. |
| Continuous analysis of the campaigns | Establish a strategy for adaptation based on feedback and emerging opportunities. |

Note. Developed by the authors.
