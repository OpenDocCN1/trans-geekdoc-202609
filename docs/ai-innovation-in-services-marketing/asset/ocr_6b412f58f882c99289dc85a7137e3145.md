# 6. GROWTH OF SERVICE INNOVATION

**6.1. Geographic and Industry:** The previous few decades have considerably increased service innovation. The main driver of innovation has been the industrial revolution. During that time, innovation spread around the globe. Since the first industrial revolution, innovation has been the primary force behind economic expansion. It mostly affected the various parts of Europe. The geographical patterns of development and income distribution have also been influenced by it. Every succeeding revolution had a unique topography. North America joined the elite group of wealthy nations during the Second Industrial Revolution, expanding the industrialized areas of Europe. ICT life sciences and biotechnologies, financial engineering, and advancements in transportation and logistics were all part of the third industrial revolution, which began around 1980. Innovation and economic growth extended throughout northeast Asia from Japan to the Republic of Korea and then to China. Beijing, Shenzhen, Tokyo, Seoul, and Beijing have all developed into megacities that influence the course of technological advancement.
