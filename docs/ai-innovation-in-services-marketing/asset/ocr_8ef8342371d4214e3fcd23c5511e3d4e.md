**Examples of Personalized Content.** For digital marketing campaigns to be effective, it is important to tailor the content to each unique customer and provide accurate recommendations that align with the client's interests and preferences. The examples provided by the respondents mostly included the personalization of products (R2, R3, R4, R5, R6, R7) and four respondents provided examples of services (R1, R8, R9, R10). Four respondents said that they saw an ad on Instagram (R1, R2, R5, R6), and R5 also provided an example of two ads on Facebook and YouTube while the others did not provide the platform. Elements that attracted the respondents in those ads were clarity (R1, R4, R8), relevance (R2, R3, R5, R7, R8, R9, R10), highlighted social aspects (R4), aesthetic visuals (R5, R10), and the effect of using the product (R6). According to the respondents, ads that appeal to the interests and personal identity of the customers positively affect them as they provide the most relevance.
