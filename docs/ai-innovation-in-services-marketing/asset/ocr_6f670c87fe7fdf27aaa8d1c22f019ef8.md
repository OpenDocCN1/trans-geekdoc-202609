A/B testing ensures that the right message reaches the right people and offers unique content to each one of them. It is an ongoing process that can maximize campaigns online presence by deeply analyzing its customers and continuously enhancing the campaigns to match their preferences. In the long term, it can even reduce bounce rates as A/B testing ensures that all elements work together to engage and retain users.

# Convert

User-generated content (UGC) and remarketing campaigns play a key role in transforming users into engaged customers in the “Convert” phase of the RACE framework (Table 12). UGC provides social proof that encourages users to go towards a conversion while remarketing campaigns re-engage those who have shown an interest but did not convert.
