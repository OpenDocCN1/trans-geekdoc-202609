# BACKGROUND OF SERVICES MARKETING AND USE OF AI IN SERVICE SECTORS

Artificial intelligence (AI) has been steadily infiltrating the service sector for years, and its impact is only deepening. The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in service sectors has been transformative, reshaping processes, enhancing customer experiences, and driving operational efficiency. # Early Exploration (1960s-1990s)

The initial foray of AI in services focused on automating basic tasks and replicating human expertise. Rule-based systems and chatbots emerged, handling straightforward inquiries and scheduling appointments (Melnik & Shah, 2003). Expert systems captured knowledge in specific domains, aiding decision-making in areas like medical diagnosis (Shortliffe, 1979). However, these early systems were limited in robustness.
