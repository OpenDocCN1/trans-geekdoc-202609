
## 3. CURRENT TRENDS

Following an in-depth exploration of the historical evolution of artificial intelligence (AI) within the advertising sector, it is pivotal to turn our analytical lens towards the present milieu. This section aims to scrutinize the contemporary advancements and applications of AI in advertising, through a critical examination of case studies and empirical instances that illustrate how marketers of today are harnessing AI to forge advertising strategies that are not only pertinent but also engaging, thereby elucidating the revolutionary capacities of the technology.

An analysis of the distribution of AI application across various marketing strategies offers insightful revelations about prevailing trends in the domain of AI-driven advertising. This analytical perspective facilitates an understanding of how AI is being prioritized and integrated into marketing strategies (Figure 1), reflecting on the technology’s profound impact on contemporary advertising practices.

Central to the discourse on the modern utilization of artificial intelligence (AI) in advertising is the concept of predictive analytics-based hyper-personalization. This methodology epitomizes the strategic employment of AI to analyze user behavior, preferences, and historical data with the objective of forecasting future consumer behaviors. A quintessential example of this application is observed in e-commerce platforms such as Amazon, which leverages AI to deduce consumer preferences from their historical purchasing patterns. This enables Amazon to present product recommendations uniquely tailored to individual user interests, thereby augmenting the likelihood of conversion and enhancing the overall user experience.

Amazon’s mastery in deploying product suggestions as a potent marketing instrument is evident in its utilization of tags like “recommended for you” or “customers also bought”. These tags facilitate the compilation of personalized product recommendations following a purchase or when an item is added to a shopping cart. Such strategies are ingeniously designed to incentivize consumers to augment the value of their shopping carts, thereby driving sales. The customization of AI-powered product recommendations for each consumer underscores the platform’s commitment to delivering relevant product suggestions, aiming to resonate with the consumer’s preferences and interests.

The operational mechanism behind Amazon’s recommendation system, which utilizes item-to-item collaborative filtering, is particularly noteworthy. This system is adept at analyzing extensive datasets, discerning correlations and dependencies between products beyond mere product descriptions. By harnessing the capabilities of AI to sift through and interpret vast amounts of data, Amazon’s recommendation engine effectively transforms passive browsing into an active and dynamic shopping experience. The engine’s ability to proactively anticipate consumer needs and offer pertinent suggestions based on a comprehensive analysis of past consumer interactions, such as search history, recent purchases, and viewed products, is a testament to the transformative power of AI in revitalizing online retail platforms into vibrant sales channels (Dresma, 2022).

Secondly, the ongoing development of programmatic advertising marks a significant phase in the evolution of digital marketing, wherein artificial intelligence (AI) plays a pivotal role in automating the procurement and distribution of advertisement inventory. Central to this process is the AI’s capability to instantaneously analyze vast datasets, thereby facilitating rapid decision-making concerning the placement of advertisements. A salient example of this AI application is manifested in the form of smart bidding, an automated bidding strategy propelled by AI that seeks to optimize bids for conversions across platforms such as Google Ads. This technological advancement ensures that advertisements are delivered to the appropriate audience at the most opportune moments, thereby maximizing the efficiency of advertisement expenditure and heralding a new era of precision in digital advertising strategies (Davenport et al., 2020).

Thirdly, the advent of conversational AI, epitomized by the integration of chatbots and virtual assistants, heralds a significant trend within the advertising sector. This trend is underscored by a WebFX survey, which reveals that 38.5% of marketers now deploy AI to enhance consumer interactions through these technologies. Conversational AI stands out for its ability to engage with consumers in real-time, providing personalized recommendations, addressing inquiries, and facilitating a seamless customer journey. For instance, renowned apparel companies such as H&M leverage chatbots on their websites to streamline the shopping experience, guiding customers to apparel and accessories that align with their preferences.

Furthermore, AI is catalyzing a transformative shift in the creative domains of advertising by enabling the generation of excellent, contextually relevant content. A paradigmatic example of this innovation is the GPT-4 language model developed by OpenAI, which has significantly impacted the advertising field. Through this advanced AI model, advertisers are equipped to craft compelling email content, social media posts, and advertising copy. This not only streamlines the content creation process but also ensures that the output resonates with the brand's voice and effectively engages the target audience, thereby redefining the intersection between creativity and AI-driven automation in advertising.

Additionally, the integration of AI-powered image identification technologies into advertising platforms, notably on social media sites such as Pinterest and Instagram, has marked a significant advancement in the realm of digital marketing. These AI systems excel in analyzing images to discern their context and content, thereby enabling more precise ad targeting. This innovation underscores the shift towards leveraging AI for enhancing the relevance and effectiveness of advertising content presented to users.

Furthermore, augmented reality (AR) represents another burgeoning trend within the advertising sector, driven by the aim to offer immersive and interactive consumer experiences. An exemplary application of this technology is seen in an AR application developed by IKEA, which permits customers to visualize furniture arrangements within their actual living spaces prior to making purchase decisions. This not only enhances the shopping experience but also aids in making more informed purchasing choices (Thompson, n.d.).

Moreover, the optimization of advertising for voice search has emerged as a critical development, propelled by the widespread adoption of speech-activated devices, such as smart speakers. AI-driven voice recognition systems have significantly improved the interpretation of user queries, thereby refining the targeting mechanisms of advertisements. A notable example of a company adapting to this trend is Domino's Pizza, which has integrated AI into its voice-activated ordering system. Through its virtual assistant, Dom, Domino's introduced voice ordering capabilities, allowing customers to place orders via mobile devices and smart home systems using simple voice commands. The launch of AnyWare in 2015 further expanded this functionality, enabling pizza orders through voice commands on a variety of platforms including Siri and Amazon Echo. By aligning its service offerings with the increasing consumer preference for speech-activated devices and voice search, Domino's has effectively distinguished itself in a competitive market, showcasing the strategic application of speech AI technology to facilitate a convenient and hands-free ordering experience (Westwater, 2023).

The domain of influencer marketing is also experiencing a notable transformation through the integration of artificial intelligence (AI), as elucidated by the co-founder and CEO of SocialPubli, an acclaimed influencer marketing platform, Qudsi (2023). This innovative application of AI aids businesses in identifying the most suitable influencers for their marketing campaigns by analyzing social media content to assess the demographics, reach, and engagement levels of influencers. Such an approach enables marketers to optimize the impact of their influencer partnerships by aligning with individuals whose follower base mirrors their target audience closely. Moreover, influencers are leveraging AI to enhance their content creation process, employing AI algorithms for brainstorming content ideas and utilizing AI-powered tools for content generation, editing, and even background removal. This technological empowerment allows influencers to succinctly articulate their creative requirements, enabling AI to produce visually compelling graphics on their behalf. Artificial intelligence (AI) has also introduced virtual influencers, which compete with traditional human influencers and reshape digital marketing. Kim & Park (2024) highlight their growing popularity, emphasizing how these AI-created personas offer brands innovative engagement opportunities.

Concurrently, as the sophistication of AI applications in advertising escalates, the importance of ethical considerations and consumer privacy has become increasingly pronounced. The advertising industry is witnessing a heightened focus on transparency and consent in data collection practices, influenced by regulatory frameworks such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). These regulations are steering the development of AI systems towards prioritizing user privacy. Leading companies, including Apple, are accentuating privacy features within their advertising ecosystems as a strategy to foster consumer trust.

In summary, the landscape of AI applications in advertising is marked by rapid evolution and innovation, as evidenced by the current trends. Advertisers are harnessing the power of AI to craft more potent and personalized advertising strategies, spanning from hyper-personalization and conversational AI to the pivotal considerations of ethics and privacy. The real-world applications of these trends by industry leaders illustrate a shifting advertising paradigm, one that offers consumers a more customized and engaging experience.
