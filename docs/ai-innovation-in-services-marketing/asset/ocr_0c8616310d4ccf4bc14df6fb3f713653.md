What can be included as a “document” involves a broad range of textual materials, for example, organizational documents, notes, cards, case records, email threads and reports, as well as various technological, digital, and social media materials, such as websites, social networking sites, and visual or sound materials (Coffey, 2014; Tight, 2019). Documentary research can be considered secondary data research (Tight, 2019) because the researcher does not directly collect or produce such secondary data. This study considered various internal organizational documents.

# Internal Organizational Documents

Organizational documents are data or evidence of how an organization represents and accounts for itself (Coffey, 2014). Therefore, internal organizational documents, such as business and marketing plans, research reports, and other additional documents related to Invest Lithuania’s business management, were analyzed. Internal documents are essential in business research because they help describe an organization and its history (Cassell & Symon, 2004). They further provide organizational value structures (Kabanoff et al., 1995) or help generate new business models for creating shared value (Michelini & Fiorentino, 2012).

# ANALYSIS OF PRIMARY DATA

In this study, data analysis was conducted using NVivo 12 Pro, a software package developed by QSR International. It is worth noting that no single industry-leading data analysis program exists (Bell et al., 2022). The choice of software primarily depends on its alignment with the research methodology, the researcher’s experience, and its effectiveness in facilitating the analysis process (Silver & Lewins, 2007). NVivo 12 Pro was selected for its extensive features, including the ability to hierarchically create and organize nodes, seamlessly open or initiate projects, user-friendly navigation, robust search functionality, and support for importing various formats of materials (Bell et al., 2022).

Creswell and Báez (2020) point out that text data are dense and time-consuming to analyze and interpret. However, the coding process is a crucial step in making sense of such data, and NVivo 12 Pro was employed to aid the researcher in this endeavor. A set of codes was established before the analysis to streamline the coding process. These codes were formulated based on the research objectives and critical factors identified in the literature review.

During the analysis process, a structure of themes emerged. The themes were as follows: positive feedback, exploring collaboration, future opportunities, interest in game development studios, positive impressions of Vilnius and geographic limitations.
