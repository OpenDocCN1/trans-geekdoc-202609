The ABM campaign ran from January to March 2023. As discussed earlier, it revolved around ten carefully chosen game development companies, emphasizing pinpointing their most iconic game characters. After these game characters were identified, the researcher started the process of AI-powered image creation (Figure 1). DALL-E 2 software was selected for this purpose due to its remarkable ability to generate lifelike images and artwork from natural language descriptions (Liu et al., 2022).

As depicted in Figure 1, the postcards generated by the researcher using AI portrayed game characters embarking on a business trip to Vilnius, Lithuania's capital. These characters, acting as virtual Vilnius “ambassadors”, sent four postcards (from left to right) to the target contacts. The first image depicted the arrival in Vilnius and the game character’s mission initiation. The second image introduced the landscape of Vilnius, its inhabitants, and noteworthy places to explore. The third one provided an overview of the city’s infrastructure. Lastly, the fourth image delved into the potential within the game development ecosystem and detailed their departure from Vilnius. (A visualization of these texts is shown in Figure 2.)

With guidance from a marketing team to a creative agency regarding the campaign’s concept and tone of voice of the game characters, artfully designed narratives were employed on the postcards. These narratives effectively communicated the journey, immersing recipients in their fictional adventure and providing an engaging glimpse into Vilnius and its potential for future game development collaborations. This innovative approach aligns with the research conducted by Brynjolfsson and McAfee (2014) on AI-driven personalization, which enhances engagement and fosters a deeper connection. It is worth emphasizing that each card included a QR code (see Figure 2) that directed individuals to the game development landing page on the Invest Lithuania website when scanned. Furthermore, each postcard incorporated symbolic details associated with the game character to elevate the sense of personalization, for example, the inclusion of bird-foot prints.
