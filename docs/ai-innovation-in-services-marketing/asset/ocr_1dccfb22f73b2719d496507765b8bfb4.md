The main topic of discussion is regional concentration within a process of global dispersion. **6.2. Technological and Non-Technological Innovation:** In particular, innovation helps businesses easily navigate crises amid the current global crisis. Technological innovations are often characterized by creating or utilizing new technologies, such as new technical knowledge and technological creations. Non-technological innovations can affect corporate operations without necessarily involving a change in technology or adopting new technology. Instead, they may focus only on implementing new organizational principles, business practices, or other immaterial methods. The growth of the service sector is influenced by several variables, including technological innovation, the need for change, and increased client expectations. Urbanization, changing lifestyles, and rising purchasing power have increased the need for services that can offer more comfort and quality. (Nair 2018). Online food delivery apps, online travel and tourist booking, and online education and learning are just a few examples of advances in the service industries.
