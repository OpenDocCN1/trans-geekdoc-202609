# About the reviewers

Nishanth Koganti received B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur, India in 2012, M.E and PhD in Information Science from Nara Institute of Science and Technology, Japan in 2014, 2017 respectively. He is currently a Postdoctoraler researcher at the University of Tokyo, Japan. His research interests are in assistive robotics, motor-skills learning, and machine learning. His graduate research is on the development of a clothing assistance robot that helps elderly people to wear clothes.
