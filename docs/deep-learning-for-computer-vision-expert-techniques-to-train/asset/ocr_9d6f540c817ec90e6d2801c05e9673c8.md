As you can see, separate captions are generated for objects and actions in the image; hence the name; dense captioning. Here is the architecture proposed by Johnson et al.:

The architecture is essentially a combination of Faster-RCNN and LSTM. The region is generated producing the object detection results, and the visual features of the regions are used to generate the captions.
