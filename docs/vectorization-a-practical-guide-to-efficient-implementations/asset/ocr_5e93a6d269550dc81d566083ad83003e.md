where W, V, b, and c are learnable weights of the dense layer. A successful variant is to let the nonlinear activation function σ = Swish, obtaining what is known as SwiGLU, i.e.

SwiGLU(x, W, V, b, c, β) = Swish_β(xW + b) ⊗ (xV + c)

In practice, however, researchers have found that dropping the bias terms b and c and setting β = 1 (i.e. use SiLU) are more helpful for training the model. Therefore, the final form of the feedforward layer with SwiGLU becomes

FFN_SwiGLU(x, W, V, U) = (SiLU(xW) ⊗ (xV)) U

where W, V, and U are learnable weights for feedforward layers. We can implement the new feedforward layer above as the following:

