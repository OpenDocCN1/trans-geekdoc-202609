This means for the sentence/lyric above that covers a total of four measures, we need to make sure the full sentence is sung within (4 beats/measure) × (4 measures)/(80 beats/minute) = 12 seconds. In addition, in TTS and SVS modeling tasks, it is more common to represent temporal units of the time series as “frames,” where each frame denotes a fixed time duration, say 5 ms. This is because it is more typical to model the audio signal as a spectrogram in the time-frequency domain, where each frame corresponds to a temporal window used to perform the Fourier transformation. Therefore, 12 seconds corresponds to 2400 frames or 150 frames per beat. Lastly, we can define even finer-grained temporal constraints by groups of words, based on the duration of the notes.
