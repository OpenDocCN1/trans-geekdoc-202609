Cloud GIS systems allow spatial data, information and knowledge to be accessed, shared, analysed and used anywhere and at any time.

The availability of low-cost devices plus reduced computing and memory costs, the innovation in network technologies that enable communication among devices and direct device connectivity, the emergence of clouding computing and the advancement in big data analytics led to the rapid and widespread adoption of the internet of things (IoT) from the early 2010s onward.
