# Box 2.3 AQUAMAPS

![](bbox=[0.75, 0.1, 0.84, 0.16])

AQUAMAPS – the Global Spatial Database on Water and Agriculture – was developed by the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) of the United Nations. As part of FAO’s global water information system, it provides open-access regional and global spatial data and information related to water resources, water uses and agricultural water management across the world to support agricultural and rural development through sustainable use of water and land resources and monitor the UN Sustainable Development Goal 6, ‘Ensure availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all’.

The current (year 2022) version of the spatial database contains a collection of spatial datasets organised under five themes:

+   - Hydrological basins: global and regional layers of hydrological basins.
+   - Rivers: global and regional layers of stream networks.
+   - Irrigation and infrastructure: global layers of irrigation areas and percentage of irrigated area serviced by groundwater; regional layers of dams in Africa, Central and South-East Asia and Middle East and regional layers of the suitability for solar irrigation in the Sahel Region, West Africa.
+   - Climate: global layers of aridity, wet day frequency, monthly and yearly means of precipitation and reference evapotranspiration, precipitation flux (the total volume of water precipitated over the period 00 h–24 h local time per unit area, per day, per month or per year), minimum and maximum air temperature, relative humidity, solar radiation flux (the total amount of energy provided by solar radiation at the surface over the period 00–24 h local time per unit area and time) and wind speed.
+   - Analyses: derived data based on spatial analysis and modelling of the data from the four preceding themes, such as the agricultural systems at risk, the distribution of physical water scarcity by major hydrological basins, the proportion of freshwater withdrawal for the agriculture sector per major river basin and the proportion of land salinized due to irrigation.
