Dissimilarity between two time series does not depend on their ordering, that is, the dissimilarity between time series A and time series B is the same as the dissimilarity between B and A. The diagonal members are defined as zero, meaning, that zero is the measure of dissimilarity between an element and itself. The dissimilarity matrix is then clustered using a k-means or k-medoids algorithm. Figure 6.26 shows three clusters of countries with similar increasing and decreasing
