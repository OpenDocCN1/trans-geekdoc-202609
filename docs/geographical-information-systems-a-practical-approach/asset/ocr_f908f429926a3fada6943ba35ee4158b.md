# CHAPTER 2

# Spatial Data and Data Management

This chapter discusses the nature of spatial data; introduces the concepts of georeferencing, geographical coordinate systems, map projections and projected coordinate systems; discusses the data models and data structures for representing and managing spatial data in computers and provides a brief introduction to spatial databases and database management.

# Learning Objectives

After studying this chapter, you should be able to

+   - explain the characteristics of spatial data;
+   - conceptualise geographical space with the object and field views;
+   - understand the concepts of map scale, map generalisation and their impacts on the representation of geographical space;
+   - understand how spatial locations are defined and measured in geographical and projected coordinate systems;

+   - select appropriate map projections and projected coordinate systems for specific GIS applications;
+   - identify and transform georeferencing systems associated with spatial data in GIS;
+   - know how spatial data are represented, structured and organised using computers;
+   - understand differences between vector and raster representations and compare their advantages and disadvantages and apprehend how spatial data are managed in spatial databases.
