# ## 4.1 Cartographic Representations

In a GIS environment, spatial data analysis often starts with maps and also ends with maps. Maps are a major means of exploring data, examining intermediate analysis results and presenting final results or findings. GIS provides easy-to-use and flexible mapping tools that allow users to produce their own maps without applying cartographic rules. However, such a map may be understandable by the analyst who produced the map but may not be effective in communicating spatial information and research findings to a wide audience.
