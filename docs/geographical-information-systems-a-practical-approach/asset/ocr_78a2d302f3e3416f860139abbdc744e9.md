The cow icons shown in Figure 4.36a are clickable point features (called Google Earth placemarks), to which the outputs from a locally calibrated pasture growth (SGS) model are linked. Virtual DemoDairy was evaluated by 20 local stakeholders in a workshop. The end-user evaluation indicated that a combination of animation and 3D visualisation in a virtual globe could enhance the capacity to communicate the result of complex climate change modelling. However, the evaluation also suggested that there was greater support for more conventional mapping and graphic representations than for interactive 3D visualisation and realistic renderings.
