# ### 3.1 Spatial Data Capture

GIS projects are as varied as the places which they are designed to investigate. We may want to trace pollution in a stream, plan a wildlife reserve or identify land use conflicts, for example. Whatever the case may be, we need to study information about a geographical location to gain a better understanding of that area’s environment. The key to the success of such projects depends on having the necessary spatial data.
