For example, we may consider the geographical space of a city consisting of many discrete objects, such as buildings, streets, highways, railways, rivers and streams, sport grounds and parks. According to the geometric shape or dimensionality of an object, we may classify the objects into point objects (such as individual buildings), line objects (such as streets and streams) and area objects (such as parks and sport grounds). They are also called point, line and area features.
