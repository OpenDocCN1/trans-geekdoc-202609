Spatial data from different sources may adopt different map projections or

projected coordinate systems. They must be converted to a common projected coordinate system before they can be integrated. The ability to convert spatial data to a common georeferencing system is an important consideration in GIS. GIS software packages generally provide the ability for conversion between a geographical coordinate system and a projected coordinate system, or among projected coordinate systems.
