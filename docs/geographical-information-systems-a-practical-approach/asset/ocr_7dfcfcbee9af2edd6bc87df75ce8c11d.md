Every symbol that appears on the map body should appear in the legend. The scale shows the relationship between distances on the map and on the ground. A graphic scale is preferred in a GIS environment because representative fractions change when the map is drafted at different scales. The orientation of the map is indicated using a north arrow, which may be supplemented by graticules of latitude and longitude. The preceding elements are found on virtually all maps. The title describes what the map is about. It is often useful to include the location of the mapped area.
