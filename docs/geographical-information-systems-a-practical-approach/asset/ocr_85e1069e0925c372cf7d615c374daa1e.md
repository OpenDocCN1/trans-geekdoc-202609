This chapter introduces and illustrates fundamental GIS operations for spatial analysis, including spatial query, reclassification, geometric and distance measurement, overlay analysis, map algebra and spatial interpolation.

+   - understand the concept of spatial analysis and modelling;

+   - understand the principle of map algebra and apply map algebra in model building with spatial data and
+   - explain the assumptions and techniques of spatial interpolation and apply them for spatial data analysis and modelling.

# 5.1 Scope of Spatial Analysis and Modelling

Spatial analysis and modelling refer to a set of methods used to examine, summarise, manipulate, analyse and interpret spatial data to discover, understand and predict spatial patterns, spatial relationships, trends and their underlying causes. The distinctive feature of spatial analysis compared with classic statistical analysis and other analytical methods is that its results change when the locations of the geographical phenomena being analysed change (Longley et al. 2011, p. 353).
