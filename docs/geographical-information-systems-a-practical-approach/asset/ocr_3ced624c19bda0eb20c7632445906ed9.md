# 6.6 Modelling of Spatial Relationships

Spatial patterns result from either exogenous (induced) or endogenous (inherent)

processes occurring in space and time. Spatial autocorrelation is largely an effect of endogenous processes. For instance, endogenous processes such as seed dispersal, gene flow and competition tend to make plants within patches resemble each other.
