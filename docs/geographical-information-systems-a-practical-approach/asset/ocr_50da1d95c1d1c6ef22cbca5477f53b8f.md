+   - Weights-of-evidence is a statistical method for combining evidence in support of a hypothesis. It involves the estimation of a response theme based on a set of evidence themes. It is mainly used to predict the likelihood of occurrence of particular geographical entities.
+   - An artificial neural network is a computational model based on the structure and functions of biological neural systems. Individual elements of the network are artificial neurons. They read an input, process it and produce an output. A network of many neurons is an adaptive system, which can exhibit rich and intelligent behaviours. Artificial neural networks have been used to extract patterns and detect trends.
