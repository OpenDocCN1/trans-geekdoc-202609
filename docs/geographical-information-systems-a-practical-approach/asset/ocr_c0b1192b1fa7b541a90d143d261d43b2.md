# 4.3 Multivariate Mapping

Multivariate mapping involves showing two or more attributes or variables of geographical features simultaneously. For example, we may want to show temperature, rainfall and evaporation all together on a map. The simultaneous display of multivariate attributes allows for comparison of multiple attributes or analysis of cross-correlation between them.
