# 6.4.1 Experiment setting

To better evaluate BBNA architecture, in addition to the 64-128-128 BMLP network we mentioned earlier, we also used one BNNP to implement a small binary CNN (BCNN) without bagging, which has two CONV layers with 32 and 64 convolution filters and an FC layer with 128 neurons. All CONV layers apply a structure in an order of C-B-A-P (CONV, BN, Activation, Pooling) and have 5 × 5 filters. The benchmarks for both models are MNIST handwriting datasets consisting of 60 K 28 × 28 gray images for training and 10 K images for testing [2]. The BBNA-BMLP we deployed on hardware aggregates eight BNNPs and uses hard voting, which has only 0.61 Mbit parameters and 1.22 M operations and achieves 97.7% accuracy. The BBNA-BCNN has more complex network with 21.74 M operations and 0.45 Mbit parameters, and it achieves over 99.2% accuracy.

We implemented FPGA-based prototypes of both models on the DE5-net board with an Intel Stratix-V GXA7 FPGA. Both models are implemented with Verilog HDL and synthesized, placed, and routed by Intel Quartus Prime 17.1, and used TimeQuest Timing Analyzer tool to constrain the system clock to 200 MHz. We used a power meter to measure the power consumption of boards.
