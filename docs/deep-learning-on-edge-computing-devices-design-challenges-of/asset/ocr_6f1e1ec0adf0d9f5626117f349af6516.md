In this chapter, we present another strategy that attempts to explore the optimal network structure of the BNN to improve classification accuracy. As illustrated in Fig. 5.1, the proposed Cellular Binary Neural Network (CBN-Net) features three characteristics inspired by the human brain. Firstly, similarly to the human visual neural network, which consists of multiple subnets [18], the CBN-Net is also a hierarchical network, which consists of multiple cells of subnets. Secondly, research showed that different neural subnets were connected by sparse global lateral dendrites in the brain cortex [19].
