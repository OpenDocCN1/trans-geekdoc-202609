**Analysis of Hyper-parameters:** Finally, we evaluated the CBN-Net (LC&KD) with two subnets and various hyperparameters of soft ratio \( \rho \) and temperature \( T \). We fixed the regularization coefficient \( \gamma = 1.0 \times 10^{-4} \) in these experiments.

As shown in Fig. 5.7, we first fixed \( \rho = 0.99 \) and tuned \( T \) in the range of 1 to 20 to see the influence of \( T \). The lowest mean error rate 10.08% was achieved when \( T = 5 \), which reduced the shift of output distribution caused by binary quantization. The highest mean error rate was obtained when \( T = 1 \), which indicated that the softened probabilities were useful for knowledge distillation in BNNs.

On the other hand, we fixed \( T \) as 5 and tuned \( \rho \) in the range of 0.1 to 1.0 to analyze the effect of \( \rho \). The highest error rate 10.80% was obtained when \( \rho = 0.1 \), which meant that, potentially, the KL loss was more important than the CE loss. Therefore we recommended to use a large value of the soft ratio, e.g., \( \rho = 0.99 \), for better performance, which was coherent with [26].
