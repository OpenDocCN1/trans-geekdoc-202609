# 12.4 Preparing You for a Successful Career

We started this chapter by looking at the organizational culture, structure, and the economic impact that AI is likely to have on the workforce. These topics help an AI organization in understanding the importance of fostering a well-functioning environment to enable talented staff to succeed. In this section, we elaborate further on tools and techniques that can facilitate a successful career. We also address, in more detail, approaches relevant to guidelines 3–6 in tale 12.1.

Over a decade ago, we developed a useful construct for anyone in a technology-based organization to assess his or her day-to-day activities in both the near term and long term. We refer to this construct as the “Circle of Balance (CoB),”depicted in figure 12.2. One of his book’s authors (David Martinez), after transitioning from leading a commercial company into an R&D environment, recognized that both work environments share many similarities in staff priorities and emphases. We will now take a deeper dive into each of the six slices of the CoB construct.
