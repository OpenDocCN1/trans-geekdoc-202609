Patrick McGovern, an American businessman who was the chairman and founder of the International Data Group, built a multibillion-dollar corporation by focusing on a mission and vision while embracing emerging technologies. Glenn Rifkin, in his book Future Forward: Leadership Lessons from Patrick McGovern [2], highlights one of these lessons, exemplified in the following quote from McGovern: “In a fastchanging business, long-term vision plus short-term operational excellence will outperform any other strategy.” This lesson is also consistent with the important message mentioned earlier: to plan the work and work the plan. Plan—meaning formulating a strategic plan—should encompass multiple years, as shown in the horizons framework discussed in part I. The execution approach should be short-term and focused on operational excellence.
