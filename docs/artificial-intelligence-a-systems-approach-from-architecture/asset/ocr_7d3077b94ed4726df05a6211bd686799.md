# 10.8 Main Takeaways

Since every AI project has very specific requirements and challenges, in this chapter we have presented a macro-view, rather than a prescriptive description, of the foundational structure needed for a successful AI deployment. Hopefully, this has helped the reader understand “how” systems engineering is applied to the development and deployment of AI systems.
