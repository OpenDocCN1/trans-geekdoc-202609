## Use-Case Example 4: Managing Energy for Smart Homes (MESH)

The environment is where we all meet, we all have a mutual interest; it is the one thing of all us share.

> —Lady Bird Johnson, former First Lady

In this chapter, we present the strategic map developed by Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) for artificial intelligence (AI)-powered energy management system as Managing Energy for Smart Homes (MESH).

The increase of greenhouse gases is the largest contributor to climate change [1]. The reliance on fossil fuels such as coal, oil, and gas is essential to address [2]. The transition to solar, wind, and hydropower is key to sustainability [3]. MESH aims to use decentralized energy to de-carbonize the environment.

The team's envisioned future is illustrated in figure 17.1. It lays out the transition of energy management for consumers and businesses.

MESH’s offering is centered around three key system applications—energy optimization for homes and businesses,

The team seeks to de to optimize the consumption and distribution of renewable energy:

- **Energy optimizer:** Analyze consumption to provide consumer and business recommendations.
- **Forecasting:** Aggregate data to provide grid operators with forecasting.
- **Grid stabilizer:** Use data to ensure energy stability.

The team defines the mission and vision as follows:

**Mission:** To enable de-carbonization through optimization of smart energy management.

**Vision:** To provide consumers and businesses with access to de-carbonized energy.
