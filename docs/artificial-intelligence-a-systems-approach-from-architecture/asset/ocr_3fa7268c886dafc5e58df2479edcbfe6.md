For example, machine learning (ML) algorithms learn from patterns seen in other stroke patients as robots share information and use an Internet of Things (IoT) ecosystem. Horizon 3 (5+ years) integrates context in the robot decision support system. An example of context might be observing that an elderly person with the propensity to have a stroke is not going to be bad at a normal time and appears to be unable to speak (one of the BEFAST indicators). The robot can respond by calling a nearby family member or neighbor. This example is drawn from a family member of one of the authors (David Martinez). A close relative was nonresponsive, sitting on a couch until a relative found her the next day. The person had suffered a massive stroke that could have been prevented if there was an immediate action taken; calling a nearby relative and responding in a more timely manner to minimize stroke severity.
