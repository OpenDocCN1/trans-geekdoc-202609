**AI and Recruiting:** While the impact of AI on the labor market is clear, the concern is not only around the jobs that AI will automate away, but also those that AI may gate-keep. A 2014 AI recruiting tool, rolled out by Amazon to screen résum and identify potential candidates, was scrapped after it was shown to discriminate against women [46]. This is because Amazon's tool was trained to verify new applications by observing and identifying patterns in résums submitted to the company over a ten-year period. Most of them came from men, the recruiting AI engine taught itself to prefer male candidates over female candidates. Such a system highlights the potential role of AI in reinforcing historical biases and inequities, as well as the need for identifying and mitigating representation biases in data sets.
