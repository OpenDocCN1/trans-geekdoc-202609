# 9.6 AI Strategic Road Map: A Blueprint

The AI strategic road map bridges the five early subcomponents of the AISDM framework—long-term mission and vision and envisioned future (section 9.3), the organization core values and strategic direction (section 9.4), and the AI value proposition (section 9.5)—with the implementation blueprint. As we have emphasized this chapter, a strategy without an implementation blueprint is not going to bring business value to an AI-driven organization. The seven elements of an AI strategic map are:

+   - Business needs: A clear delineation of the stakeholder’s requirements to ascertain that the AI value proposition is aligned with the value capture from the viewpoint of the customers.

+   - Risk management: Risk categories and risk levels.
