The analysis also identifies external opportunities. Namely, the proposed solution also demonstrates great potential for serving as a strategic platform for the transition to and management of renewable energy sources, given the growing interest and shift toward renewable energy.

The SWOT analysis also calls out some challenges in the form of internal weaknesses and external threats. The weaknesses are primarily due to the large effort needed to enter and try to transform an existing market, as well as the challenge of acquiring the right talent to help the team deliver their solution. The team also identifies threats, including smart home energy competitors such as Google Nest and Leap, which provide edge computing capabilities. In addition, there is concern that users may resist yet another device in their home. This is especially a threat to the success of the smart grid, as the efficacy of the Al-powered energy management system relies on the widespread adoption and collection of data from a large base of users.
