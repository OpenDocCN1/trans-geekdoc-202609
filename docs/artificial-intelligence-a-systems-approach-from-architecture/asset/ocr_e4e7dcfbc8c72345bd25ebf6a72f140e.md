   a. Design
   b. Data
   c. Development and deployment
   d. All of the above
5. What are some examples of important RAI considerations that should be made throughout stage of the AI development life cycle?
6. All societal harms are reducible to demonstrable harms.
   a. True
   b. False
7. Review each of the nine case studies in this chapter to classify the types of bias (preexisting, technical, and emergent), and the harms (individual, collective, and societal) presented by the AI system.
