Let us now describe in detail each of the seven elements of the AI strategic road map, starting with business needs. There have been a number of academic and how-to articles on the effective mapping of a business digital transformation [35–37]. An emphasis in these recommended approaches is to understand the points of the stakeholder (e.g., the customer), and how the value proposition addresses them. They point out the importance of focusing not just on technology, but instead on the business value (i.e., the customer’s value captured from the AI value proposition).

As discussed in chapter 2, the strategic road map implementation—specifically, the AI value proposition—hinges on the effective execution of the AI architecture implementation framework in figure 2.2. We will discuss the AI strategic implementation in more detail in chapter 10. In chapter 3, the questions to answer were focused on the data, ML algorithms, and computing requirements (as shown in table 3.1). Business understanding for creating value out of AI capabilities is intrinsic to the key phases of the cross-industry standard process for data mining (CRISP-DM) [38].

In formulating the AI road map, we need to ask higher-level questions on the expected value to the customer. Table 9.2 addresses the questions. Again, the AI team must be customer focused. Answers to these questions, during the formulation of the AI strategic development map and prior to the implementation phase, are critical to the successful design, development, and deployment of AI capabilities.
