# Table 10.4 Four distinct organizational clusters [13]

| Organizational Clusters | Characteristics |
|---|---|
| Pioneers (19% of respondents) | - Organizations that both understand and have adopted AI<br>- These organizations on the leading edge of incorporating AI into both these organizations’ offerings and internal processes |
| Investigators (32% of respondents) | - Organizations that understand AI but are not deploying beyond the pilot stage<br>- Their investigation into what AI may offer, emphasizing looking before leaping |
| Experimenters (13% of respondents) | - Organizations that are piloting or adopting AI without deep understanding<br>- Organizations that are learning by doing |
| Passives (36% of respondents) | - Organizations with no adoption or much understanding of AI |

As shown in table 10.4, most of the respondents either fell into the category of Investigators (32 percent) or Passives (36 percent). Another 19 percent fell into the category of Pioneers. The remaining 13 percent were Experimenters.

Several insights were drawn from the respondents' survey. We only highlight and contrast the Pioneers compared to the Passives since they show very different approaches in terms of the people (i.e., organization) dimension. Investigators and Experimenters fit well between the Pioneers and Passives. The Investigators were closer to the same dimensions achieved by the Pioneers, and the Experimenters were closely aligned with the emphasis of Passives.

Pioneers, as well as Investigators, showed the following similarities in incorporating AI within their respective organizations. They demonstrated strengths in the following capabilities:

+   - The organization collaborates effectively.
- The organization is open to change and receptive to new ideas.
- Executives have the vision and leadership to navigate the coming changes (a capability directly coupled to the AISDM framework discussed in chapter 9).
- Their analytics capabilities are better than those of their competitors.
- The organization plans for the long term in terms of return on investment (a capability directly coupled to the AISDM framework).
