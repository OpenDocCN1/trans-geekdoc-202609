Van Ma succinctly what culture is: “The organizational culture is not visible as, for example, the organization’s management structure, but it is the glue that drives the operating environment of an organization” [30]. As we pointed out, organizational core values can be thought as the guideposts. How leaders and staff effect or oppose those values in practice (i.e., walk the talk [31]). Organizational culture encapsulates its purpose while balancing long-term versus short-term gains.

Werman et al., in their article “Building Digital-Ready Culture in Traditional Organizations” [32], discussed areas to advance the state of traditional organizations as they go through a digital transformation by adhering to the following steps:

+   * **Build** an environment for rapidly experimenting, self-organizing, and driving decisions with data.
* **Preserve** practices of acting with integrity and seeking stability by attracting and retaining talent, fostering customer loyalty, and ensuring stakeholder confidence.
* **Reorient** the business direction, anticipating customers’ needs, driving account-ability for results, and setting rules that prevent abuses.

These steps are essential recommendations for building a digital-ready culture. They provide guidelines very consistent with our emphasis in this chapter.
