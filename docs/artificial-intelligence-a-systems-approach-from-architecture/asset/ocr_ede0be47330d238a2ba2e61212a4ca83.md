Since then, several other organizations and authors have emphasized the importance of adopting the Vee-model representation when developing and deploying complex systems [9, 18, 20].nThe Vee-model representation is well matched to our systems engineering approach as applied to AI. The relevance is a holistic view of the development of complex systems. Specifically, in their original paper, K. Forsberg and H. Mooz [19] emphasized the importance of starting at the highest level–stakeholders' requirements—and continuing with a more detailed architecture decomposition, as shown on the left side of figure 2.1. Upon defining the key subcomponents (lower-level system elements), one then proceeds with the architecture integration, verification, and validation shown on the right side of figure 2.1, and culminates with a higher-level system capability. A very important function is to ascertain that the stakeholder requirements formulated at the start of the system development are met at the last stage of the solution/system realization.
