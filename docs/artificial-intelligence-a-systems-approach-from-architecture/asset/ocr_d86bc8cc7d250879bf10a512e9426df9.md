A strong organizational culture is important for following these guidelines. An organizational culture drives the ability for leaders and technical AI practitioners to achieve alignment between the overall organization’s strategy and the AI strategic road map, as described in our AISDM framework.

In addition to a strong organizational culture, AI-based organization must be internally organized to facilitate innovation, including having talented staff with a sense of urgency, while cementing coalitions inside and outside the organization. Such efforts must be consistent with the AI strategic road map.

After our discussions, at a macro-level, we presented a useful construct called the CoB, which has stood the test of time as a way to assess near-term and long-term priorities. The six slices of the CoB are technical depth, management and leadership excellence, mentoring and networking, personal health and wellness, and family, and career. We emphasized that no one—regardless of where they are in their careers and years of experience—no can perform all these elements perfectly. The CoB is most useful when leadership and staff recognize that it is important to balance these priorities, ultimately affecting job performance, and personal satisfaction.

This transition from technical depth to technical breath does not losing technical prowess. In fact, it means the opposite.
