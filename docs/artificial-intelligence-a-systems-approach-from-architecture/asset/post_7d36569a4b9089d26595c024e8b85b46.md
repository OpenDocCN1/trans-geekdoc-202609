# 1: Overview

The greater danger for most of us lies not in setting our aim too high, but in setting our aim too low. This book introduces architecture principles, development stages, and the integration of subsystems into an end-to-end system. It is obviously necessary but not sufficient to have good data, verified algorithms, a computing environment to deploy either in the cloud or at the edge, and other subcomponents.

As a guide to the book's content, the reader can refer to figure 1.1, which illustrates the three parts of the book:

- Part I: We start with a detailed discussion of AI system architecture, concentrating primarily on enabling technologies.
- Part II: We focus on strategic principles beginning with AI strategic blueprint (e.g., strategic vision, envisioned future, governance, infrastructure, fostering an innovative team) and succeeding in deployment.
- Part III: We bring parts I and II together for the reader by AI application cases. All of the cases represent AI capabilities augmenting human intelligence. All of our discussions center on narrow AI* (see the simplified definition in the "Narrow AI" box).

# Narrow AI

The theory and development of computer systems that perform tasks augment human intelligence such as perceiving, learning, classifying, abstracting, reasoning, and acting.
