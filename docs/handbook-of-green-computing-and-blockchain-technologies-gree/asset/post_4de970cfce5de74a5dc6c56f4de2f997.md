At the first stage, the system will collect all the data from different phases from various resources. A system is constructed to keep an eye on data like temperature, soil moisture, light, humidity, pH, seeds, fertilizers, and so on, generated in the pre-production phase. Some data like harvest, physical transport, irrigation is generated during the production crop. After production, data about processing, cleaning, blending, storage, and warehousing, etc. is generated which is very useful for consumers to know how food is processed and cleaned. Some vital data is produced during industrial processing, such as packing, expiry date, and details of manufacture.

At the second stage, we use smart contracts because smart contracts are automated which means when conditions match, they work as a trigger. Smart contract is written to get data from the first stage and send to decentralized ledger blockchain. At the third stage, we can use a private blockchain like Hyperledger Fabric, or any. All the data generated at the first stage is stored in a blockchain with the help of smart contracts. In the end, we can use that data for different purposes, such as land usages, crop efficiency, traceability, and fraud prevention, which the authors will explain in the following sections.

## II. Benefits of blockchain in agriculture:

Figure 6.10 shows some important benefits of blockchain use in agriculture which are explained below:

- **Clear supply chain**: With the help of shared blockchain ledger, all stakeholders can manage agricultural shipping and monitor the complete process, which improves the worth of agronomic goods, because crops, livestock, and machinery can be monitored by the beneficiary to ensure ethicality and quality [14]. Consumers can easily trace and verify the journey of product from the farm field to his door because consumers are willing to know about the origin of food.

- **Improves production process**: Data is the modern-age oil and is the biggest asset for an organization after workforce. All the data collected from sensors and other sources are uploaded in the blockchain which helps to improve production process [15, 16].

- **Increase efficiency and traceability**: Demand for local agricultural produce and organic produce is increasing daily. With the help of blockchain implementation in agriculture, consumers can easily trace and verify the journey of product from the farm field to his door. Blockchain stores all the data about who grows this product, who harvests, who produces, and in the end who transports this product [17].

- **Agriculture cryptocurrency**: Bitcoin is the leading cryptocurrency globally. After the huge success of bitcoin, approximately 4,000 cryptocurrencies were invented and circulated in the market. With the help of blockchain, farmers can have their own cryptocurrency or trade in cryptocurrency.

- **Stock exchange**: There are many cryptocurrencies in the market for trading and have a stock exchange for that. If blockchain is used in agriculture and cryptocurrency is used, so start-ups can get investment using stock exchange.

- **Smart contracts**: With the help of smart contracts, growers can get their payment on time because smart contracts are automated.

- **Attract investment**: Blockchain implementation in agriculture provides an opportunity for growers to attract funds with the help of ICO or Initial Coin Offering, which is a new way for crowdsourcing using cryptocurrency.

## III. Limitations of blockchain in agriculture

While focusing on profitability in the Indian agriculture sector, under unfavorable weather conditions, growers face many challenges. Some of them are given below and shown in Figure 6.11:

- **Inaccuracy and complexity of data**: Data is the new weapon and if data is wrong anybody can lose the war. Data collected from sensors or inserted by the people cannot always be trusted. Thus, the collected information may not be consistent. Data collected from agricultural supply chain is very complex [18, 19].

- **Consume too much energy**: The first blockchain platform is bitcoin which uses PoW consensus mechanism which depends upon the hard work of miners and miners get some reward to solve a mathematical problem. The system uses very high energy to solve these mathematical puzzles [20]. After bitcoin, many blockchain frameworks come with different consensus algorithms which use less energy but still energy consumption is a major problem with blockchain.

![](img/c5c79333a43e0e9a69d1119635e3c244_101_0.png)

FIGURE 6.11 Limitations of blockchain technology in agriculture.

- **Maturity of technology**: Blockchain technology is only ten years old and not much used in agriculture. This means that blockchain is a new technology which needs time to be mature.

- **Interoperability issues**: One major limitation of blockchain is the interoperability issue. In the market, there are different types of blockchain platforms which work in totally different ways and try to solve problems in their own way. This creates an issue where different blockchain platforms cannot communicate effectively [21, 22].

- **Cost and implementation struggle**: Blockchain technology implementation cost is huge, although some platforms like Hyperledger are open source, still need huge investment from enterprises that want to pursue it.

- **Limited knowledge of domain**: Blockchain technology is only ten years old and not much used in agriculture. This means there is a lack of expert workforce. To implement blockchain in agriculture, they require multiple experts from different domains, and it is always hard to find suitable workforce with domain expertise.

- **Speed of transaction**: Bitcoin blockchain takes one to ten minutes to complete a transaction. Blockchain implementation in agriculture is affected by this transaction speed. Transaction speed plays a vital role when choosing a blockchain platform.

- **Policy and regulatory risks**: One of the major challenges faced by developers in agriculture is country policy and regulation. India has very different policies for agriculture. Indian govt. issues some regulations for cryptocurrencies also which affect blockchain implementation.

- **Costly data collection**: Data is the modern-age oil and is the biggest asset for an organization after workforce. All the data collected from sensors and other sources are uploaded in the blockchain and this data collection is very costly because farmers need to use many types of sensors. For small farmers, it is one of the major limitations.

- **Dependency on the farm size**: One of the major challenges of using blockchain in the agriculture sector is that sometimes benefits depend upon the size of farmland. For example, small growers may struggle to integrate and collect farm data, as due to low budget they cannot afford blockchain infrastructure; on the other hand, small farmers can get farm insurance via blockchain.
