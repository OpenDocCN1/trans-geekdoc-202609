# 5.6 OPEN CHALLENGES

There are many open challenges related to the technology of blockchain but the most common challenge is to convince the public to adopt it as most users have a misconception in their mind relating blockchain technology to cryptocurrency which most people think is an illegal currency or currency used by hackers and fraud users. Another main main challenge faced in the technology is speed as this system used for transactions is very slow as compared to other transactions systems used in modern-day technologies.
