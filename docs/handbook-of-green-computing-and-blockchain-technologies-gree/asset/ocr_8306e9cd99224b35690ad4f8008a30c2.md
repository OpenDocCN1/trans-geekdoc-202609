Blockchain as secured as it could be also faces a couple of security problems. One such problem is “the majority attack.” If any entity at any point has more than 51% of the computing power, they have the ability to assume control of blockchain. Another issue is fork problems. These problems are related to the version of the node when software is due an upgrade. Forks are of two types: soft and hard [40].

Another major issue with blockchain is the problem of scalability. The traditional transactions systems are accepted widespread because of their speed of processing transactions. On the other hand, Bitcoin and Ethereum, the two largest networks running on blockchain are lacking by a huge margin in this aspect. There is also not much clarity about blockchain as such, which acts as a significant barrier for it to be adopted by masses [41].
