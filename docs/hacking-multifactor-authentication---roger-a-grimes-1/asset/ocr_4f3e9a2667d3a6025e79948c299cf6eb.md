# Defeating Apple Touch with Gelatin Fingers In this example (www.iphoneincancada.com/news/touch-id-bypassed-fingerprint-gelatin-finger), white-hat hackers successfully hacked Apple's fingerprint scanner using a fingerprint imprinted on plastic by smeared glue to fix it. A second hacker did it using common food gelatin. A video of the former method is shown here: www.youtube.com/watch?v=hMB0dB8SkNG. A second video successfully hacking Apple's fingerprint using another method: www.youtube.com/watch?v=2u4ZGsw1zo.

Tencent Fingerprint Hack At Tencent, a large Chinese security company, researchers used fingerprints printed on glass (www.tomguide.com/news/hackers-unlock-using-phone-photographed-fingerprints-in-just-20-minutes) to fool superbly every fingerprint reader they tried, using materials that cost $140. They demoied their technique at a Chinese security conference. They asked a member of the audience to bring up him, they took a picture of his fingerprint, and in under 20 minutes they had hacked his off.
