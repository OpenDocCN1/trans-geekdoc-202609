# 184 Hacking Multifactor Authentication

SMS is the most popular application used on a cell phone, surpassing using it as a telephone. For many users, SMS is the primary way you communicate with another person for many reasons, the least of which is that people can easily communicate with other people without outwardly noisy or distracting other people. All around the world you can see people with their heads, typing and reading SMS messages. Many parent has admitted their children to verbally communicate, and people often send messages to each other even though they are in the same room or right next to each other.

SMS is a popular way for businesses and organizations to communicate with their customers and members. Importantly, and for this chapter, smart-based MFA solutions are very popular. Many companies and organizations use SMS-based MFA solutions because they are fairly easy to deploy and use "out-of-band" for authentication (that is a good thing regardless of the other issues), and because nearly everyone uses SMS.
