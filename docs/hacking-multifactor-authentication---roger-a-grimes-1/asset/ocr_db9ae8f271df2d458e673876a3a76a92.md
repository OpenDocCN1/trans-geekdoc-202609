A simple fake “Your Bloomberg account password has expired” phishing email would likely work across more users than you would think.

The user's name is either their Bloomberg Terminal Username or corporate email address (as shown in Figure 21.4). So, any hacker knowing a Bloomberg Terminal user’s business email address can use that to login as the victim. There are a lot of well-known trader names, and the companies they are known as. A good password hacker can find out a victim's address or take a few guesses at it. It is not hard to find out what type of email naming format a particular company uses and then apply it to the victim's Bloomberg logon.
