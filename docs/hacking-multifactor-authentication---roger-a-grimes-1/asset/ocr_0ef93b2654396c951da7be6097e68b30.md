The article concludes with the following sentence: “Ways an attacker might maximize side-channel signals have been investigated and found to be very effective.” That pretty says it all right there. So, in theory, an attacker could monitor a smartcard as it uses and determines its secret key and other information.

This white paper covers a dozen types of attacks against smartcards (www.infosecwriters.com/text_resources/pdf/known_Attacks_Against_Smartcards.pdf) and tells anyone how they, can do it. It's a great smartcard resource. Essentially, you can physically cut the smartcard chip from the card, use an acid to remove any attachment resin and then use an optical microscope to map out the chip's different boundaries and connections. A series of metallic probes are used to send and receive information (different I/O scenarios), and at least one of those is attached to a digital signal processor card and/or oscilloscope (or other measuring device). It also includes many different types of attacks. And there are many.

A 2014 paper (portal.sinteza.singidum.ac.rs/Media/files/2014/43-46.pdf) says that “The Results of this study show that leakage can be easily exposed as side-channel by an attacker to extract information about the secret key in cryptographic hardware in CMOS crypto-design, where TDPL [three-phase dual pre-charge logic] can be a reliable countermeasure to use in design of smartcards. So, if using TDPL gates perform in more evenly distributed power consumption working on 1s and 0s, which has higher for different power attacks to succeed. Almost all smartcards still use the traditional, more hackable design. And even if you solve this attack type (different power analysis), there are dozens of other methods that will be successful.

**NOTE** If you are interested in learning more, there is a great two-hour video on how to hack smartcards and other MFA tokens using side-channel attacks: vimeo.com/26765734.

The key lesson is that even specialized hardware designed to protect authentication secrets can be beaten, dozens of different ways, and there are thousands of people who know how to do it. It takes awareness of these specialized side-channel attacks and how they are accomplished to defeat them. And sometimes if an attacker looks hard enough at the microscopic level, they can even defeat those defenses.
