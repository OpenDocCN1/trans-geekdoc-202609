These types of CAPTCHAs have started having increased failure rates because some users don't comprehend the instructions and some pictures are difficult to understand. For example, in Figure 3.5, the very bottom of the highway sign is intruding a tiny bit into the second row. Should that square be part of what the user picks, since the sign is indeed part of square, or is the sliver of the sign so minute that the the CAPTCHA creates didn't include it as a correct answer?

In any case, CAPTCHAs are weak authenticators. They only try to distinguish between automated malware programs and real human beings, and they don't have great accuracy once an attacker starts
