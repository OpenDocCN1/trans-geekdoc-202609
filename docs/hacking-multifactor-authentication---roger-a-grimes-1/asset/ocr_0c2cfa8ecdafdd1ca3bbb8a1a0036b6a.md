# Passwords Will Be Us for a While

You will constantly read stories about how passwords are harmful and will be gone. I've been hearing that story for more than 30 years. I now have more passwords than ever. Passwords have a ton of issues, many of which we will cover in the next section, but passwords are the only authentication option for the vast majority of sites and services that require authentication. There is not a non-password option that works for all sites and services. Not even close. If you wanted to get rid of all passwords, you couldn't.

Passwords are actually a good option to keep for many authentication scenarios. They accept authentication on most sites and services (when other options are allowed), and they are easy to implement and use. For low-security sites, not protecting personal or confidential information, they can even be the right authentication solution. Sure, for example I use passwords on many and messaging websites. Sure for sites that get compromised, even if they do, what's the worst that can happen? Someone can log on as me and write things that are contrary to my own interests. They can say horrible, terrible, and some people might mistakenly believe I said them. That's what can happen. It's not uncommon occurrence. Apple, Google, Facebook, Microsoft.. .they've all had their far more popular media sites taken over by hacking groups that then said fraudulent posting as the legitimate. Almost everyone immediately recognized the accounts were compromised, and other than some short-term embarrassment and the effort it took to reclaim the accounts, no harm was done. I'm not saying that I would love any account I control to be taken over, but if I don't have my personal or confidential information, there's only so much that can happen. Worse things in life occur. With that, if you could implement something better than passwords and it makes sense for the situation, do it. Passwords have inherent weaknesses, some of which I covered earlier.
