# Security Isn’t Usually Binary

Computer security is rarely binary—on or off, black or white. Computer security is along a continuum from no security to absolute, total security, and everything in between. Although in an ideal world we want to stop all malicious hackers with a reasonable amount of effort and resources,

There are many things that are better being binary. Is your heart beating? Or does the engine working or not while you're flying? Does the bulletproof stop the bullet? Are the car’s brakes working? Are the building beams strong enough to support the building’s weight? Does the parachute’s ripple work? There are lots of life and death decisions in the real world that we need to protect our nuclear secrets and military weapons. We don't want just anyone manipulating or using them in an unauthorized manner. And so, the physical and computer security surrounding our nuclear and military weapons arsenal is appropriately the physical and computer security surrounding our nuclear and military weapons arsenal is appropriately strong. We want not to tolerate even one hack.

For almost everything else, okay security is good enough. We want most of our personal and business transactions protected by good, not perfect, security. We want it to be good enough most of the time. It is possible for security to be too secure.
