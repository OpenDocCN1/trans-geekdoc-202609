# Privacy

I think most people in our society want sort of basic, guaranteed level of privacy. I’m surprised it isn’t in the U.S. Bill of Rights—yet. Dare to dream! Part of the reason why it’s so hard to get and use anonymous identities is that it nearly the world is going against us being anonymous. In fact, they want to identify us in every way. Advertisers want to track and target us. They want to know our utmost secrets so they can track us in specific ways. The websites want to make money and track us (using browser cookies and a thousand other ways). Most governments, even claiming they want to protect your privacy (not all governments make this claim), really don’t. It is in their best interests to know who you are and what you are up to all the time. Law enforcement wants to be able to see everything you are doing, although some are kind enough to note that they should have a valid, legal search warrant.
