# Birthday Attack Method

Attackers using the brute-force method can have great increases in speed by randomly guessing among the total population of guesses. Traditional brute-forcing starts at the beginning and sequentially increments one value at one position after another until it solves the problem. By simply randomly guessing among all the possible values, brute-force guessers can be successful faster. This phenomenon is known as the birthday attack method (among other names).

It's widely known that the birthday method works because of simple experiment to demonstrate the logic anyone in a mid-sized group. In the experiment, which is best performed with at least 20 people, each person is asked, one at a time, to announce their birthday (July 19th, September 22nd, etc.), without any.. And any time someone guesses a birthday that someone else, the person with the matching birthday announces the match and the experiment ends.

Ignoring the realities of naturally occurring clusters and cultural aggregations (like after a war), people can generally be born on any day of the year.
