# Read MFA Industry Reviews

A great way to narrow down MFA vendor lists is by checking the "official" product reports from the technology industry researchers, including Gartner (www.gartner.com), IDC (www.idc.com), and Forrester (go.forrester.com/research). Each of these research agencies has decades of experience in reviewing products and industries. Their annual industry reports are some of the most soughtafter reading. They usually cover the industry in general, summarize the major players, and pick winners and losers. An MFA vendor has to be for more than four years and have a stable customer base and revenue before they be listed in a report. The reports will let you know what customers think about each product and what the strengths and weaknesses of each are. They will cover the maturation of the industry overall and tell you where it is going. If I have limited time and want to get a quick overview of the top vendors, I'd read one of their reports.

> NOTE Many of these review services cater to larger enterprises and the concerns of large enterprises. may not cover cases that are important to small and medium-sized organizations.

The big negative is that these reports can be expensive to purchase outright (many thousands of dollars), but they are usually worth whatever you might pay. You can sometimes get the reports for free from the vendor winning of these reports, or by figuring out the "winners" and who is calling and able to share their reports. Many times a winner can show how they have "placed" in one of the reports but is unable to share that report or review. Because the industry research group makes money selling their report to you. Still, if you have some money, here are some MFA reports:

+   - Gartner MFA reports (www.gartner.com/search?keywords=multifactor%20authentication)
- IDC MFA reports (www.idc.com/search/simple_perform..query=multifactor+authentication&page=1&hits=25&sortBy=RELEVANCY&lang=English&srchin=ALLRESEARCH&src=&at=10&cmp=10&oid=7761627&siteContext=IDC)

# Conduct a Pilot Project

When you're doing your research, choose at least two or three MFA solutions for pilot projects. A good pilot project involves the whole team and user representatives across your organization. Participants should be involved in evaluating all selected MFA solutions so they can provide comparative feedback. You want to find out how easy the solution is to install, manage, and administer? How easy is it to provision depression users? How well does the authentication work?
