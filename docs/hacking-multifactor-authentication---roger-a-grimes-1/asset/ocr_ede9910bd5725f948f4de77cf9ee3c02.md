I was in my brother's brand-new Tesla car recently and I was showing me how he activated the optional PIN so that thieves could not easily steal his car just because he accidentally left the operating token (i.e., keys) in or accidentally left it running. Not only is it easier to leave keyless cars running, but Tesla, in particular, leaves its mirrors extended out (at least temporarily) as one of the indications that the car is unlocked. Owners require that drivers enter a PIN (called PIN to Drive) each time they want to use the car.
