Personal-knowledge questions ask a person's favorite car. Its probably not an AMC Pacer or Ford Pinto. It could be, but odds are less likely. Its more likely to be a “sexy” car like Mustang, Charger, Corvette, Lamborghini, or Lexus. In any case, if the personal-knowledge question involves cars, the number of potential guesses an attacker has to make are far less than a person password might be (unless the attacker already knew the person's previous passwords or interests).
