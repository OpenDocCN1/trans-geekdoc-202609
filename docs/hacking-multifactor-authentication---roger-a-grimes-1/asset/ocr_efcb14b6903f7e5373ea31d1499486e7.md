# Hacking Multifactor Authentication

You may be wondering, “What should we do for automated password recovery then?” Although relying on the standard types of simple personal-knowledge questions is a poor security practice, advanced recovery systems (covered in a moment) are still based on personal-knowledge questions implemented in a way that does not allow easy social engineering or guessing.

No matter how it was stolen, an attacker has successfully taken over the victim's account, they usually deify MFA solution, "downgrading" the victim's account to use something secure. They always change the password, often change the phone number (to phone-based account recovery processes), and change other information as well. They then utilize the stolen account to steal money or use it to pose as the victim to other potential victims. It can take the real victim hours or days to recover the account. I've read of people who never regained use of their stolen account and some permanently lost the content they had saving in the account for decades.
