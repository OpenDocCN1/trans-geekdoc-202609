# Cryptography Apocalypse: Preparing for the Day When Quantum Computing Breaks Today's Crypto

ISBN: 978-1-119-61819-5; November 2019

Cryptography Apocalypse is a crucial resource for every IT and InfoSec professional for preparing for the quantum-computing revolution as unfortunately, many current cryptographic methods will soon be obsolete. The encryption technologies we rely on every day—HTTPS, TLS, WiFi protection, VPNs, cryptocurrencies, PKI, digital certificates, smartcards, and most-factor authentication—will be useless unless you prepare. Post-quantum crypto algorithms are a reality, but implementation will take significant time and computing power. This practical guide helps IT leaders and implementers make the appropriate decisions today to meet the challenges of tomorrow.

# Hacking the Hacker: Learn From the Experts Who Down Hackers

ISBN: 978-1-119-39621-5; May 2017

Hacking the Hacker takes you into the world of cybersecurity to show what goes on behind the scenes, and introduces you to the men and women fighting this technological arms race. Twenty-six of the world's top white hackers, security researchers, and leaders, describe what they do, with each profile preceded by a no-experience explanation of the relevant technology. Dorothy Denny discusses persistent threats, Martin Hell discusses how he invented public encryption, Dr. Charlie Miller talks about hacking, and other cybersecurity experts from around the world detail the threats, defenses, tools, and techniques. Light on jargon and intrigue, this book is designed to be an introduction to the field for aspiring hackers of all ages.
