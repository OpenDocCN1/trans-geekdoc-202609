Today, detecting side-channel leaks is another common way that nation-states and law enforcement get of authentication secrets. Hundreds of research papers and presentations are available that detail the successful ways people bypassed the security protection on electronic devices. And the people who can prevent side-channel eavesdropping attacks is a subspecies of computer security defense. > There are many cases of unintentional secret leaking from the sound or electrical signals from someone typing on a keyboard (see.berkeley.edu/news/media/releases/2005/09/14_key.shtml). Monitors supposedly leaked information from unintentional ultrasonic emanations (www.wired.com/story/monitor-ultrasonic-sounds-reveal-content-side-channel). Apparently 3D printers are the new dot-matrix printers of the past as far as side-channel attacks are concerned (scholarship.org/uc/item/6c52g94). It has even been shown that talking and signing can be remotely recovered from changes in lighting due to sound vibration impacts (www.zdnet.com/article/lamphone-attack-lets-threat-actors-recover-conversations-from-your-light-bulb). The broad category name for sound side-channel attacks is acoustic cryptanalysis.
