> - I forgot my B-Unit, Can I still access the Bloomberg Professional Services software?

Yes. Your Bloomberg Terminal, when prompted for B-Unit code, click "Log Assistance" in the bottom-left corner of the window, click "Request an order" and follow the instructions. If you log into BIA, click "Log in with your B-Unit" and follow the prompts.

Bloomberg users with supported Android phones can access the Terminal via the B-Unit app. For more information about how to download the app and get started, visit https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/product/b-unit-app-android.
