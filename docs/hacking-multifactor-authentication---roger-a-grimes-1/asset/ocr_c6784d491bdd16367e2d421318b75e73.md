# Endpoint Compromise

As any MFA solution, if the user's endpoint is "pwned", it's over. If the user's computer is controlled by a hacker or malware program, they can do whatever the user can. It doesn't take admin or root access. The malware or hacker can wait until they have detected a period of inactivity but make sure they send "keep-alive" moves to the Bloomberg Any terminal, because it locks out after 60 minutes. So, the attacker could keep the terminal always active and unlocked, and have unlimited access until the victim was away andor asleep.
