The problem is that any attribute used to evaluate authentication or access control now becomes a critical evaluation point. Before DAC/CAP, the values of text fields on a user's account such as Department and Country did not mean much. They couldn't impact security, access control, and authentication. They were interesting, but superfluous to most. Most people who fill in might not even care if they spell the text values correctly. But now with DAC/CAP, happens to know the original people who put them, they are being to evaluate authentication and access control. When someone decided to use Department and Country as access control attributes, they increase the security over who could or modify and change those attributes? Did anyone monitoring over those attributes look for and report unauthorized changes? Not most computer people see those fields and still think of them as textbased attributes that are part of a user's account description. By including them as evaluation decisions in DAC/CAP, they are critically important and should be protected as you protect login names, passwords, and password hashes.
