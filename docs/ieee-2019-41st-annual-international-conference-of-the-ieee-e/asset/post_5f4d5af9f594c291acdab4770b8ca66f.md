Experiments on a practical approach of application classification show that our method offers higher accuracies where other deep learning techniques significantly lower results. Similar to the advancements made in "deep learning", it is our assertion that the formulation of dictionary learning provides the basis to develop more efficient dictionary algorithms and help in advancing state-of-the-art.

In the future, we plan to test the robustness of dictionary learning in the presence of noise, and limits of training sample. We also plan to apply this technique to other problems such as biometrics, vision, and speech processing. Further, there has been a lot of work on supervised dictionary learning; we expect to improve the results ever by incorporating techniques from supervised learning.
