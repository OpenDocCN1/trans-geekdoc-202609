# Trip Planning

It can help in planning your trip. Booking.com says that about one third would request a computer to plan their next trip for them. The computer would set up the plan after scanning the past travel history of the person. With the help of artificial intelligence, travel companies will be able to offer customized recommendations based on the preferences of the host. They are looking forward to using artificial intelligence to raise their revenues as a person who is seeing on his screen the photos of a destination which he likes, is more likely to visit it. This technology offers travel companies the ultimate way to inspire the customers and also keep them engaged on the website for a long time, therefore, increasing the conversion rate of the visitors.

# Bookings

This is another major issue for hotels. Hotel owners are always worried about the sky-high rates of abandonment. One in twenty-people would be real bookers who make a reservation. With the right recommendations to the right persons, the abandonment rates can be lowered. This can be done by using an artificial intelligence model that can act as a virtual assistant for hotel management. It can help visitors know what the hotel has to offer, what are the latest rates, what are the tips and what is the distance of the hotel from some hot favorite tourist destinations. This makes it easier for the traveler to reach a decision. The more you facilitate the consumer, the higher will be the rate of conversion.

What happens if an artificial intelligence system doesn’t know the answer to a question that a visitor asks it? Keep in mind that the artificial intelligence system can always transfer the question to a human assistant. Also, the artificial intelligence model will learn from each experience. It means that if the system doesn’t know the answer to a particular question, it will know the same the next time by recording the question and the answer that is given by the hotel staff member. This is what is special about an artificial intelligence model. It never loses an opportunity to educate itself. It keeps on learning until it reaches perfection. Even after that, it keeps on learning from its mistakes.
