# Types of AI

If I could tell you about something that has brought a revolution to the 21st century, it would be artificial intelligence. Different artificial intelligence models have entered our lives and that’s why you must understand the different types of artificial intelligence that exist today. As you know that artificial intelligence is now being used to detect different objects, make decisions that are wiser than that of humans, and solve complex problems that were previously not in the domain of a human being.

# Artificial Narrow Intelligence

This is one type of artificial intelligence, also known as the weak artificial intelligence system. These machines can perform low level and narrowly defined set of tasks. The machine doesn’t have the power to think and it just performs a certain set of some pre-defined functions. Its examples are Alexa from Amazon, Google Assistant, Siri, Tesla’s self-driving cars, and other such systems. They are weak in the sense that their functions are limited because their developers have made them as such.

# Artificial General Intelligence

This is a relatively stronger artificial intelligence as compared to the previous type of artificial intelligence. It is stronger in the sense that AGI is considered as the stage from which artificial intelligence is likely to evolve. Machines are likely to possess the ability to think themselves and make decisions without any external interference. This brings artificial intelligence closer to the human brain. They have more autonomous power than a self-driving car
