## Chatbots

Artificial Intelligence helps businessmen build interactive chat programs. A chatbot is a great way to use AI technologies, while at the same time improving customer service and engagement in the retail industry. Chatbots have AI running on the backend, which enables them to communicate with the customers and answer some crucial questions. You might have met a chatbot if you are fond of online shopping. Nowadays many websites have adopted chatbots, not quite sophisticated in all cases, to communicate with customers. These chatbots offer you answers to common questions in a fraction of a minute and make your customer service experience fast and smooth. While these chatbots are answering your questions, they also are collecting valuable customer data such as their name, email addresses, choices, and interests. The system stores this data and uses it to make future decisions more informed.

## Personalization

AI retail system identifies shoppers and adapts to product pricing, displays, and service with the help of biometric recognition to reflect customer profiles, unlocked rewards, and loyalty accounts. This helps retailers create a personalized shopping experience for visitors. Retail stores use AI and advanced algorithms to make out what a customer usually searches while he or she is on the internet. They also collect information like social media searches, posts and behavior, demographic data, and buying habits. This data is immensely helpful in shaping up a personalized experience for buyers. For example, if a person loves to buy joggers, retail outlets can create a personalized experience for him by displaying their jogger products. At the same time, he gets online on their respective websites. Not only in the online space but this experience can also help retail outlets personalize the physical shopping experience.

## Customer Engagement

Retailer outlet owners can use IoT technologies to interact with customers in a better way and to gain valuable insights into consumer behavior preferences without even directly engaging them. This saves time for consumers as well as retailers. Kodisoft tablet is one example of indirect or remote interaction with customers to provide the goods and services. The tablet is developed to allow customers to browse menus, order food, and play games. The tablet is built by machine learning technology and has greatly leveraged customer data. It collects data on consumers’ behavior and helps retailers transform their services accordingly. This allows companies to increase and improve customer engagement, which is good for their business.

Artificial Intelligence helps retailers improve conversational support to the customers. AI-backed assistants attempt to use natural language processing to aid buyers in navigating questions, troubleshooting, FAQs. They are also designed to redirect the customers to a human agent if they need the same. This greatly improves customer experience as retailers can fulfill their demands by making sure that both AI-driven and human supports are available all the time.

## Price Predictions

Price forecasting is one of the essential elements in the retail business. Business owners must predict a product price based on demand, seasonal trends, and the release of new models of the same product. You can use the technology to forecast price and change it before your competitors do the same. This makes you proactive. AI makes it smooth for retailers. The price prediction feature can boost up customer loyalty as the customers consider you more reliable and credible.

AI apps can also help retailers adjust the prices of different products by visualizing the outcomes of different prices. For example, if a product is not selling at $20. AI apps can adjust the prices according to customers’ buying habits and behavior, and come up with the desired results. You can know if it will sell better at $25 or $15. This is how you can adjust the prices and boost your sales. It is beneficial for retail businesses where the sale of each item matters. AI systems collect data about similar products in the market, recommend a price adjustment, and boost sales. Kroger and eBay are the leaders in applying AI for the optimization of prices and promotions of products accordingly.

## Supply Chain

Logistics and supply chain management are the two sectors that are considered as the most crucial in the retail business. If retailers fail to manage them properly or they execute them poorly, it may result in significant losses for retailers. Sometimes retailers have to deal with leftover products and with out-of-stock situations. Artificial intelligence can help retailers deal with these problems effectively and efficiently. As a retailer, you can use AI in supply chain management for timely restocking. AI will calculate the original demand for a product by analyzing the sale history of the product, the weather conditions and their effects, the promotional campaigns, the location of the stores, and the density and intensity of the online presence of the retail business. Other parameters can also be included in the analysis. AI can help you replenish your stock before an out of stock scenario starts creating problems for you. You will never have to worry about shelf gaps in your store.

## No Cashier

Are you worried about spending loads of money on the salary of your cashier? If you robotize your stores, you will have one less thing to worry about. The installation of robots in stores will cut down long lines in the store and will allow you to cut down the number of employees. This will result in slashing expenses and help you in saving money that would otherwise be spent on operations. Amazon has become the market leader in offering checkout-free zones in the stores. Just Walk Out and Amazon Go react when you pick something from a shelf. When you leave the store with products in hand, your Amazon account will be debited with the cost of your purchase. This has made it possible for Amazon to reduce the number of employees in physical stores. You can follow into the footsteps of Amazon and allow yourself to make progress and evolve your business.

## In-Store Aid

Another form in which AI can help you run your business effectively is by eliminating the need for using paper price tags. Using paper price tags is perhaps one of the most arduous tasks in running a physical store. You have to take each product separately and paste on it a paper price tag. Instead, AI has made it possible for store owners to use smart shelf tags. This tech allows you to feed the system with video ads and promotional content inside the stores. You can use an AI system that can speak with customers in the language they speak. This makes customer service easy and satisfactory.

## Visual Search

Have you ever used the Google Reverse Images system? If you have used that, you might know that the system allows you to upload an image on the search engine and make a search on similar images. For example, if you like a product that your friend has purchased and you want to buy it, you can simply ask her to send you its picture. You can then upload the picture on a retail store’s search bar and look out for similar products. If you upload the product on a general search engine, you will be able to see a variety of products that have similar patterns, shapes, and colors. The accuracy touches 95% if the image you are uploading is clear and high resolution. This allows a consumer to scan different products of the same category, compare prices, quality, and reliability of brands.
