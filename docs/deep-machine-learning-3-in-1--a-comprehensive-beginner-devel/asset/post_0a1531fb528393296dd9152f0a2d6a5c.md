## Introduction

This book on artificial intelligence contains proven steps and strategies on how to build a deep learning model that can work better than humans. There was a time when I thought that artificial intelligence was just a dream and that there was nothing to be worried about. Over time, the dust settled down and everything became crystal clear, like flowing water. However, as I grew up artificial intelligence grabbed hold of many ordinary things that were once in the domain of humans. That was scary, to say the least.

One of the things I feared and worried about the most is the fear of losing my job. People said and magazine articles spun the scare that artificial intelligence was going to take over the jobs that humans once had. One night I had a dream. I saw in the dream that there was a giant robot that was developed by a company to lift heavy pieces of steel to build bridges. The robot, named Humongousteel, was so big that it would take a vertical chain of twenty humans to match its height. Everyone in the dream was happy because the robot had made it the job easy for humans by easing off the heavy burdens. Humongousteel was powered by artificial intelligence systems that acted on the orders of human programmers.

The main job of Humongousteel was to pick up logs of steel from the warehouse and carry it toward the site where a bridge was to be built. Humongousteel was controlled by a dozen men who would coordinate its movements and actions on the site. There was a big trailer that would transport Humongousteel from the warehouse to the site where Humongousteel would unload itself and get to work. The site, to say the least, was spectacular when the robot started working. It would carry the steel pieces and install them and connect them. Everyone appreciated it until it messed up at a working site.

One day Humongousteel was working at the site when it lost contact with the control room from where it used to receive instructions. We expected that the robot would not move until the contact with the control room resumed, but that was not the case. The contact was never lost. Something else had taken over the control of Humongousteel. This was our first assumption. However, later on it was revealed that the artificial intelligence system that powered the robot had become autonomous. The robot destroyed the bridge that it had built and made the site a mess. It could not be controlled until the rescue team arrived and successfully cut off the power cords that connected the batteries to the neural system. Everyone had a sigh of relief once the robot was brought under control. The dream ended and I thanked God that it happened in a dream.

Since that night, I started studying artificial intelligence systems just to end my curiosity. Since the day I entered this world, I realized that there was nothing scary about artificial intelligence systems. They are not here to eliminate jobs but they are here to facilitate creative jobs. Machines can only do the things which we order them to do. They don’t think creatively. Creativity is something that is native to humans. And it should stay like that. While machines do the boring stuff humans can focus on the creative stuff like storytelling and developing characters.

So, this caused the start of my journey toward artificial intelligence systems. I learned how to develop them and how to use them to ease off tough tasks. It turned out to be fun.

This book is for those who are curious about artificial intelligence. An artificial intelligence system is backed by deep learning models that are built by Python programming language. Therefore, it is necessary that if you are looking forward to entering the world of artificial intelligence and machine learning, you should be an expert in writing code in Python language.

This book, for the ease of reading, is divided into different chapters that are then further divided into different sections so that you will be able to skim it and read it fast and easily.

The first section of the chapter will explain to you what Python variables are and how you can use them in coding different programs. These variables are the basic features of programming because you will have to use them for storing different types of information such as texts, floats, and integers.
