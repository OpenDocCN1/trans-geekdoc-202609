## Fraud Detection

For many financial institutions especially banks, security is always one of the top concerns that they must address if they have to win the loyalty and confidence of their customers. There has been a constant fear of hacks and frauds, which can lead a financial institution to devastation. With the help of AI, bank management can cut down the rates of false identity incidents, fraud attempts, and other stealing attempts. Artificial intelligence can reduce fraud attempts by its ability to make accurate interpretations of insights of different trends. It is a blend of supervised and unsupervised machine learning. The combination of the two machine learning types helps you determine if a transaction has fraudulent tendencies or not.

As more financial institutions move toward digital advancements, payment fraud is rising at a sharp pace. These kinds of attacks have a digital footprint which makes them invisible. AI helps banks install a security layer to protect it against any fraudulent attempt. AI keeps an eye on any kind of trend swerving and detects a fraud swiftly even if it is being conducted at a massive scale. This gives banks an advantage in battling out fraud attempts and securing the reserves. Generally, banks use predictive analytics.

## Cost Cut

The reduction in operational costs is another advantage of AI in the banking sector. In the banking sector, a big amount of time is spent on identifying, digitizing, and onboarding document templates. With the help of the digitization process, banks reduce the amount of time that is otherwise spent on completing this large circle. If as a banker, you automate the process of digitization in the branches, you can slash the time that your employees would have spent on the same process. This is how the cycle times are improved and you can redirect employees to other important projects.

## Customer Service

The customer service sector is another sector that can be improved with the help of AI tools. Customer experience is something that affects almost every business around the world, including the banking sector. It leaves an impact on the way people would normally perceive a particular organization. Where banks are concerned, people want access to their money, even if the bank is closed due to holidays. They prefer banks that offer them swift transaction processes. Take the example of a person who has to withdraw a thousand dollars because he is on vacation with his family and he has run out of hard cash. He goes to the automated teller machine and gets his card captured due to an unknown reason. It is midnight and there is no employee available at the bank. This is where AI-powered chatbots play an important role in guiding the customer and solving his problem. Chatbots don’t sleep and they are not bound by any time zone so they can help customers irrespective of time and place.

You might think that nowadays almost every online business has a chatbot so the question is whether it is a simple chatbot or an AI-powered chatbot. There is a big difference between the two types of chatbots. A simple chatbot that most businesses have deployed on their websites is static. It doesn’t learn through its own experience until you feed it with data from the backend. However, an AI-powered chatbot can learn through customer interactions which helps improve them, and in turn, improves customer experience. A brilliant example of chatbots is Erica, the VA of the Bank of America. You can request her to send you to push notifications of your credit scores and also help you in paying bills or conducting financial transactions online.

## Risk Management

Credit cards are a problem when it comes to securing financial transactions. There is always a fear of a fraud or a malafide transaction. The best way to mitigate this risk is by conducting a thorough analysis of the potential customer so that the right person is authenticated. AI can help you do real-time identification of the client. This is how AI can help the banking sector save millions of dollars that are otherwise wasted on fraudulent practices. You can deploy several technologies like facial recognition scans, iris scans, and voice recognition, etc.

## AI in Manufacturing

AI is inching toward making its space in the manufacturing industry. Here is a rundown of how AI is helping manufacturers.

## Quality Control

One of the most important aspects of the manufacturing business is quality control. Some flaws in different products are quite small to be fully noticed with the naked eye even if your inspector has years of experience. On the other hand, machines can be equipped with different cameras that are much more sensitive and precise than the naked eye. That’s why they can detect little defects. The AI-powered system can keep an eye on the product from the production line through the packaging line. It helps them spot any kind of imperfections. The process can be made fully automated and you can remove the need to have a human expert to analyze a flaw. An AI system can detect a flaw, analyze and alert the management.

## Failure Mode Prediction

There is always a chance that a human expert may end up making false conclusions about processes and products. Products may fail due to a variety of reasons which doesn’t usually relate to visual inspection. A product may look perfect but it may still break down after the user has used it once. A product that may look flawed may perform perfectly when it is in the hands of a user. The way we observe different objects is usually biased. Many things may turn out to be different than what they look like. With big amounts of data in the system, AI can help you identify the areas that should be given more attention than the rest.

## AI in Travel

Artificial intelligence is gradually changing the way we used to travel. They are slowly infiltrating almost every element of the traveling process. There are in-room VAs, chatbots, and customer agents that are powered by artificial intelligence. Now they are making their way into the travel industry as well. An artificial intelligence system makes reaching your favorite destination easier and smooth. Take the example of Google Flights which now can predict any expected delays in flight arrival or departure. It announces the delay even before the artificial intelligence model does.

## Trip Planning

It can help in planning your trip. Booking.com says that about one third would request a computer to plan their next trip for them. The computer would set up the plan after scanning the past travel history of the person. With the help of artificial intelligence, travel companies will be able to offer customized recommendations based on the preferences of the host. They are looking forward to using artificial intelligence to raise their revenues as a person who is seeing on his screen the photos of a destination which he likes, is more likely to visit it. This technology offers travel companies the ultimate way to inspire the customers and also keep them engaged on the website for a long time, therefore, increasing the conversion rate of the visitors.

## Bookings

This is another major issue for hotels. Hotel owners are always worried about the sky-high rates of abandonment. One in twenty people would be real bookers who make a reservation. With the right recommendations to the right persons, the abandonment rates can be lowered. This can be done by using an artificial intelligence model that can act as a virtual assistant for hotel management. It can help visitors know what the hotel has to offer, what are the latest rates, what are the tips and what is the distance of the hotel from some hot favorite tourist destinations. This makes it easier for the traveler to reach a decision. The more you facilitate the consumer, the higher will be the rate of conversion.

What happens if an artificial intelligence system doesn’t know the answer to a question that a visitor asks it? Keep in mind that the artificial intelligence system can always transfer the question to a human assistant. Also, the artificial intelligence model will learn from each experience. It means that if the system doesn’t know the answer to a particular question, it will know the same the next time by recording the question and the answer that is given by the hotel staff member. This is what is special about an artificial intelligence model. It never loses an opportunity to educate itself. It keeps on learning until it reaches perfection. Even after that, it keeps on learning from its mistakes.
