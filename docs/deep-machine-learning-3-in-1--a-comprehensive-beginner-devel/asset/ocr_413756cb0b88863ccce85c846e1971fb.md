# Artificial Super Intelligence

Artificial Super Intelligence is considered the next stage of Artificial General Intelligence. It is the stage when artificial intelligence systems will surpass human intelligence. At the present moment, this kind of artificial intelligence is just a figment of human imagination. You only can see this kind of scenario in Hollywood movies and science fiction novels, where you would see a bunch of machines taking control of the world.

However, for how long this type of artificial intelligence will remain our figment of the imagination is not yet known. Machines that are powered by artificial intelligence will not take long to reach this point. They will ultimately reach this point.
