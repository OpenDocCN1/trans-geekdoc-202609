Elon Musk, the Chief Executive Officer of Tesla, and Mark Zuckerberg, the Chief Executive Officer of Facebook, have been caught in a verbal duel or to say a Twitter duel over the future of artificial intelligence. Although the former is considered the pioneer of bringing artificial intelligence to life by infusing them into Tesla cars, yet he is the one who fears most the revolution in artificial intelligence. He would not expect anyone to develop an artificial intelligence system that could surpass humans. He is against the development of a super-intelligent machine that would wreak havoc on humans. However, Mark believes that artificial intelligence is the future and the progress in this field is inevitable. He is looking forward to launching a smart artificial intelligence system that would control the house by acting on the speech of the owner of the house. For example, the house would run on the verbal instructions of the owner. If he says, ‘light the stove’, the fire will start heating up the house. If he orders to turn the lights off, there will be dark. If the owner directs the artificial intelligence system to draw the curtains, the curtains will cover the windows and if he times the artificial intelligence system to remove the curtains at a specific time in the morning, the system will act accordingly. This is super smart but yet it is not something that humans should be afraid of.

What Elon Musk is afraid of is the race in the field of defense. He indirectly wants that the military should stay away from artificial intelligence systems. He fears the advent of artificial intelligence weapons that would be programmed to kill the enemy. He says that there is never a surety that an artificial intelligence system would be so efficient that it would easily differentiate between an enemy and a friend. Even if it learns to differentiate between the two, who would educate it about when to fire or hold off? This is something that is still under debate.
