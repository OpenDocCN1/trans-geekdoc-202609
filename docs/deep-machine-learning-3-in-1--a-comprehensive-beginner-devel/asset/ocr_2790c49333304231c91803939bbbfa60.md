# Fraud Detection

For many financial institutions especially banks, security is always one of the top concerns that they must address if they have to win the loyalty and confidence of their customers. There has been a constant fear of hacks and frauds, which can lead a financial institution to devastation. With the help of AI, bank management can cut down the rates of false identity incidents, fraud attempts, and other stealing attempts. Artificial intelligence can reduce fraud attempts by its ability to make accurate interpretations of insights of different trends. It is a blend of supervised and unsupervised machine learning. The combination of the two machine learning types helps you determine if a transaction has fraudulent tendencies or not.

As more financial institutions move toward digital advancements, payment fraud is rising at a sharp pace. These kinds of attacks have a digital footprint which makes them invisible. AI helps banks install a security layer to protect it against any fraudulent attempt. AI keeps an eye on any kind of trend swerving and detects a fraud swiftly even if it is being conducted at a massive scale. This gives banks an advantage in battling out fraud attempts and securing the reserves. Generally, banks use predictive analytics.

# Cost Cut

The reduction in operational costs is another advantage of AI in the banking sector. In the banking sector, a big amount of time is spent on identifying, digitizing, and onboarding document templates. With the help of the digitization process, banks reduce the amount of time that is otherwise spent on completing this large circle. If as a banker, you automate the process of digitization in the branches, you can slash the time that your employees would have spent on the same process. This is how the cycle times are improved and you can redirect employees to other important projects.
