# In-Store Aid

Another form in which AI can help you run your business effectively is by eliminating the need for using paper price tags. Using paper price tags is perhaps one of the most arduous tasks in running a physical store. You have to take each product separately and paste on it a paper price tag. Instead, AI has made it possible for store owners to use smart shelf tags. This tech allows you to feed the system with video ads and promotional content inside the stores. You can use an AI system that can speak with customers in the language they speak. This makes customer service easy and satisfactory.

# Visual Search

Have you ever used the Google Reverse Images system? If you have used that, you might know that the system allows you to upload an image on the search engine and make a search on similar images. For example, if you like a product that your friend has purchased and you want to buy it, you can simply ask her to send you its picture. You can then upload the picture on a retail store’s search bar and look out for similar products. If you upload the product on a general search engine, you will be able to see a variety of products that have similar patterns, shapes, and colors. The accuracy touches 95% if the image you are uploading is clear and high resolution. This allows a consumer to scan different products of the same category, compare prices, quality, and reliability of brands.

# Fitting Rooms

With AI in the town, there is hardly any need left for physical fitting rooms. They are an amazing way for the customers to save time and find out the perfect outfit that is a brilliant match for them. It does that in minutes. A sophisticated AI system has the power to measure your body from thousands of points in seconds. You may miss out on certain mismatching or misfit points while you are trying an outfit physically but an AI app cannot miss out on even a single point. This is what makes artificial intelligence more efficient and reliable in physical stores.
