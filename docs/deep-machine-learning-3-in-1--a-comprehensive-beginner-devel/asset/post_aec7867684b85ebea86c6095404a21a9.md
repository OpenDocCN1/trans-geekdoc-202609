## Artificial Intelligence

This chapter will walk you through Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its applications in the real world. Presently, we are surrounded by smart systems in our everyday lives. A smart system can be in the form of internet search, conversion of spoken words into text, and biometric face recognition. Whatever it is, artificial intelligence remains at the heart of these systems. These systems are complex applications. AI runs these applications with the help of algorithms and mathematics.

## Why AI?

Artificial intelligence, the term, came into existence in 1956. However, it only gained popularity today due to advanced algorithms, availability of volumes of data, improved storage capacities, and fast computing power. In the 1950s, AI dealt with problem-solving techniques. In the next decade of the 1960s, AI grabbed the attention of the US Department of Defense, which started training computers so that it can mimic the human power of reasoning. Defense Advanced Research Projects (DARPA) finished several street mapping projects during the 1970s. This early period of research shaped the future of automation that you can see in present-day computers.

AI became the subject of science fiction novels and Hollywood movies. Hollywood, in the movies, depicted AI as super robots who have more powers than humans and who eventually take over the world. However, the present-day evolution of AI has not turned out to be that much scary. These systems are not that much smart that they can control humans. On the contrary, AI has evolved positively and has brought great benefits to every industry.

AI has the power to leave deep impacts on our lives. AI as a field attempts at deciphering the behaviors and patterns of different entities. With the help of AI, we attempt at building smart systems. The human brain is a marvel in itself. We wonder how the human brain does lots of thinking with such ease. We easily identify objects, learn new things and languages, communicate with different people by responding to their questions, and perform several sophisticated tasks that we might not have imagined ever.

AI helps machines learn from experience, tune them to new inputs, and perform the tasks that only humans have performed. Most of the AI examples that we come across in our everyday lives usually rely heavily on natural language processing (NLP) and deep learning. Computers, by using the latest technologies, are trained to achieve certain tasks after they have processed big amounts of data and recognized certain patterns that exist in the data.

## Importance of Artificial Intelligence

AI is beneficial because it offers to automate repetitive learning. However, it is different from robotic and hardware-backed automation. Instead of the automation of manual tasks, AI attempts to perform frequent, computerized, and high-volume tasks without the slightest of fatigue. This sort of automation demands human inquiry if you want to set up an efficient system that carves out and asks the right questions.

Artificial intelligence makes products intelligent. It is not sold within an individual status. Rather, it improves products. Take the example of Siri which was added a new feature to Apple products.

Artificial intelligence revolves around learning algorithms that allow the data to the program. AI attempts at detecting various structures and regularities in data so that algorithm learns some skills. The algorithm keeps learning and turns into a predictor. Take the example of an automated chess player. The algorithm learns to make moves in a game of chess. It also can learn what product to recommend to you after you have made one purchase. Ever wondered why you see similar products on your computer screen after you buy one from an online eCommerce store. The models adopt when they are consistently fed with new data. The learning technique is known as backpropagation that permits a model to adjust itself through training if an answer is wrong.

AI attempts at analyzing deeper data by using neural networks that possess lots of hidden layers. A fraud detection system couldn’t be built in the past because the computing power was slow and big data were absent. AI models demand ample amounts of data so that they can encompass all features of a model. For example, if the model is designed to detect a stop sign, you should feed the model with thousands of pictures of a stop sign, taken from different angles and in different countries. All sizes, colors, dimensions, and shapes of stop signs should be fed to the model so that it becomes efficient in detecting the stop sign. The higher the amount of data is, the more accuracy you will have.

AI can be highly accurate if you design and train it well. Deep learning models power Amazon’s Alexa, Google Photos, and Google Assistant. They keep on improving themselves as they are exposed to new data.

## Types of AI

If I could tell you about something that has brought a revolution to the 21st century, it would be artificial intelligence. Different artificial intelligence models have entered our lives and that’s why you must understand the different types of artificial intelligence that exist today. As you know that artificial intelligence is now being used to detect different objects, make decisions that are wiser than that of humans, and solve complex problems that were previously not in the domain of a human being.

### Artificial Narrow Intelligence

This is one type of artificial intelligence, also known as the weak artificial intelligence system. These machines can perform low level and narrowly defined set of tasks. The machine doesn’t have the power to think and it just performs a certain set of some pre-defined functions. Its examples are Alexa from Amazon, Google Assistant, Siri, Tesla’s self-driving cars, and other such systems. They are weak in the sense that their functions are limited because their developers have made them as such.

### Artificial General Intelligence

This is a relatively stronger artificial intelligence as compared to the previous type of artificial intelligence. It is stronger in the sense that AGI is considered as the stage from which artificial intelligence is likely to evolve. Machines are likely to possess the ability to think themselves and make decisions without any external interference. This brings artificial intelligence closer to the human brain. They have more autonomous power than a self-driving car has. At the moment, there are no examples of this kind of artificial intelligence. However, there are debates that these systems will soon be developed and brought to life. There will be machines as smart as humans are, and they will produce more work in a short time as compared to humans.
