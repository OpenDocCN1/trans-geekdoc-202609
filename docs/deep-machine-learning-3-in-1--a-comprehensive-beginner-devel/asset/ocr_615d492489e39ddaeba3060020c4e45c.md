# Supply Chain

Logistics and supply chain management are the two sectors that are considered as the most crucial in the retail business. If retailers fail to manage them properly or they execute them poorly, it may result in significant losses for retailers. Sometimes retailers have to deal with leftover products and with out-of-stock situations. Artificial intelligence can help retailers deal with these problems effectively and efficiently. As a retailer, you can use AI in supply chain management for timely restocking. AI will calculate the original demand for a product by analyzing the sale history of the product, the weather conditions and their effects, the promotional campaigns, the location of the stores, and the density and intensity of the online presence of the retail business. Other parameters can also be included in the analysis. AI can help you replenish your stock before an out of stock scenario starts creating problems for you. You will never have to worry about shelve gaps in your store.

# No Cashier

Are you worried about spending loads of money on the salary of your cashier? If you robotize your stores, you will have one less thing to worry about. The installation of robots in stores will cut down long lines in the store and will allow you to cut down the number of employees. This will result in slashing expenses and help you in saving money that would otherwise be spent on operations. Amazon has become the market leader in offering checkout-free zones in the stores. Just Walk Out and Amazon Go react when you pick something from a shelf. When you leave the store with products in hand, your Amazon account will be debited with the cost of your purchase. This has made it possible for Amazon to reduce the number of employees in physical stores. You can follow into the footsteps of Amazon and allow yourself to make progress and evolve your business.
