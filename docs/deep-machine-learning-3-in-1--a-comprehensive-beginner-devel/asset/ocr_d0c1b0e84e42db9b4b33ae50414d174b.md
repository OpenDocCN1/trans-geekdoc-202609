# Chatbots

Artificial Intelligence helps businessmen build interactive chat programs. A chatbot is a great way to use AI technologies, while at the same time improving customer service and engagement in the retail industry. Chatbots have AI running on the backend, which enables them to communicate with the customers and answer some crucial questions. You might have met a chatbot if you are fond of online shopping. Nowadays many websites have adopted chatbots, not quite sophisticated in all cases, to communicate with customers. These chatbots offer you answers to common questions in a fraction of a minute and make your customer service experience fast and smooth. While these chatbots are answering your questions, they also are collecting valuable customer data such as their name, email addresses, choices, and interests. The system stores this data and uses it to make future decisions more informed.
