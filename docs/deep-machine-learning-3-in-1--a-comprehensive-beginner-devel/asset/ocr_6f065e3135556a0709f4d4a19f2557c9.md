# Artificial Intelligence

This chapter will walk you through Artificial Intelligence (AI) and its applications in the real world. Presently, we are surrounded by smart systems in our everyday lives. A smart system can be in the form of internet search, conversion of spoken words into text, and biometric face recognition. Whatever it is, artificial intelligence remains at the heart of these systems. These systems are complex applications. AI runs these applications with the help of algorithms and mathematics.

# Why AI?

Artificial intelligence, the term, came into existence in 1956. However, it only gained popularity today due to advanced algorithms, availability of volumes of data, improved storage capacities, and fast computing power. In the 1950s, AI dealt with problem-solving techniques. In the next decade of the 1960s, AI grabbed the attention of the US Department of Defense, which started training computers so that it can mimic the human power of reasoning. Defense Advanced Research Projects (DARPA) finished several street mapping projects during the 1970s. This early period of research shaped the future of automation that you can see in present-day computers.

AI became the subject of science fiction novels and Hollywood movies. Hollywood, in the movies, depicted AI as super robots who have more powers than humans and who eventually take over the world. However, the present-day evolution of AI has not turned out to be that much scary. These systems are not that much smart that they can control humans. On the contrary, AI has evolved positively and has brought great benefits to every industry.
