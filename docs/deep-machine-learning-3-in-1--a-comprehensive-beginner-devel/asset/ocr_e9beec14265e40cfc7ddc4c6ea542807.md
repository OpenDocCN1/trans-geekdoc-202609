# AI in Manufacturing

AI is inching toward making its space in the manufacturing industry. Here is a rundown of how AI is helping manufacturers.

# Quality Control

One of the most important aspects of the manufacturing business is quality control. Some flaws in different products are quite small to be fully noticed with the naked eye even if your inspector has years of experience. On the other hand, machines can be equipped with different cameras that are much more sensitive and precise than the naked eye. That’s why they can detect little defects. The AI-powered system can keep an eye on the product from the production line through the packaging line. It helps them spot any kind of imperfections. The process can be made fully automated and you can remove the need to have a human expert to analyze a flaw. An AI system can detect a flaw, analyze and alert the management.

# Failure Mode Prediction

There is always a chance that a human expert may end up making false conclusions about processes and products. Products may fail due to a variety of reasons which doesn’t usually relate to visual inspection. A product may look perfect but it may still break down after the user has used it once. A product that may look flawed may perform perfectly when it is in the hands of a user. The way we observe different objects is usually biased. Many things may turn out to be different than what they look like. With big amounts of data in the system, AI can help you identify the areas that should be given more attention than the rest.
