# *Assistance*

In the travel industry, you can use artificial intelligence for chatbots and assistants to serve the needs of customers. As technology gets highly sophisticated, artificial intelligence can now offer on-property assistance by answering common questions asked by customers. They can even carry out certain requests and offer precise real-time recommendations. A chatbot can be shaped as a flirty personality that can amuse the customers. This is going to revolutionize the travel industry.
