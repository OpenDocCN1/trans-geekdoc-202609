class Bike():

    """This is a simple attempt for the representation of a car."""

    def __init__(self, bmake, bmodel, byear):

        self.bmake = bmake

        self.bmodel = bmodel

        self.byear = byear

        self.odometer_b_reading = 0

    def get_descriptive_bname(self):

        long_bname = str(self.byear) + ' ' + self.bmake + ' ' + self.bmodel

        return long_bname.title()

    def reading_odometer(self):
        print("The heavy bike has " + str(self.odometer_b_reading) + " miles on the meter.")

    def updating_odometer(self, bmileage):
        self.odometer_b_reading = bmileage

        if bmileage >= self.odometer_b_reading:
            self.odometer_b_reading = bmileage
        else:
            print("Sorry, you are not allowed to roll back the odometer!")

    def increment_b_odometer(self, bmiles):
        self.odometer_b_reading += bmiles

### What Is Deep Learning?

Deep learning educates computers to do the chores that humans do naturally. Deep learning systems stand out to be efficient because, just like humans, they learn by following an example. If you take a glance around you, you will see driverless cars or at least hear about them in magazines or on TV. The technology behind these cars is known as deep learning systems. These cars amaze people by detecting a stop sign and discriminating between a lamppost and a pedestrian. The same technology can be spotted in your tablets, wireless speakers, smartphones, and tablets. Deep learning has gained and is gaining tremendous attention for quite some time. It has achieved results that were never possible before.

A computer model attempts to classify sounds, images, and pieces of text. Deep learning modes have the potential to achieve top-level accuracy that could match humans. Labeled data trains deep learning models. Another thing that is crucial to the training of the models is neural network architectures that possess multiple layers.

In simple words, you can say that deep learning is a certain type or branch of artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) that imitates the whole process of learning abilities of humans. It is considered an important aspect of data science, which includes predictive modeling.

Artificial intelligence (AI) has remained a subject of extreme hype across the media houses. There have been countless articles on the pros and cons of machine learning, AI, and deep learning. Even celebrities got themselves embroiled in verbal duels and Twitter fights over the future of AI and its dangers to the human race. Elon Musk, the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Tesla, is one of the few celebrity billionaires who advocated against an unbridled race to achieve brilliance in artificial intelligence. Whatever the facts are, presently we have been promised a fresh and evolved race of intelligent chatbots and Virtual Assistants (VAs).

Some argue that deep learning systems will paint the human future in a grim picture. Those will be utopian times where humans will lose jobs in mass quantity and robots will handle most of the economic activity. Well, there is a silver lining as well. In my opinion, media releases and debates are overhyped. AI will only improve the quality of human lives. It will help us survive in a better way. It will facilitate humans in maximizing their work output by easing off tough tasks and by aiding in wrapping up physically exhausting jobs in a fast yet efficient manner. AI is the future and you must play an active role in this revolutionary future.

You might hear different identical terms like artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning. This chapter will walk discuss these terms so that you have a clear concept of what each means and their significance.

### Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence came into existence in the 1950s when some pioneers of computer science began asking the crucial question if computers could think as humans did. The ramifications of that question are still being explored today. Artificial Intelligence, as a field, can be defined as the effort to so automation of certain intellectual tasks that are inherent to humans. While AI is a general field, deep learning and machine learning are the sub-fields. However, the field also encompasses those fields for which little learning was needed. For example, chess programs involved hardcoded rules that were written by programmers. They didn’t fall into the category of machine learning. Experts opine that human-level AI can only be achieved if programmers handcraft explicit rules for the sake of manipulation of knowledge. The approach can be dubbed as symbolic AI and it dominated during the 1950s through the 1980s.

### Machine Learning

A machine learning system is generally trained rather than programmed. Programmers present different examples that are relevant to a certain task. If you are planning to automate the task of form a collection of the pictures of your spouse, you can feed the machine learning system with different examples of her pictures. The system learns statistical rules for linking several pictures to particular tags.
