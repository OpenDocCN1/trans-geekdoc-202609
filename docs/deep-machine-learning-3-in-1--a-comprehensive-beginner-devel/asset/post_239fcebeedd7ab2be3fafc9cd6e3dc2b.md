## Applications of AI

Artificial Intelligence is gradually making its mark on different industries across the world. Take the example of the healthcare industry which is perfectly ripe for undergoing major changes. The opportunities for technology to make its way through healthcare are endless. Shortly, you will see technology to be deployed in disease diagnosis and risk assessment sectors to get efficient, precise, and impactful interventions in a patient's care. With the evolution of payment structures, patients' demands rise. At the same time, the volume of input data doubles up at an astonishing rate. AI will soon be the engine that would drive growth in the health sector.

### AI in Healthcare

There are several advantages that AI offers to the healthcare sector. With AI stepping in the healthcare field, we can now safely set aside clinical decision-making and traditional analytics. The learning algorithm can be highly precise because they interact with a huge influx of data. Their ability to scan and sift through big blocks of data allow healthcare professionals to have insights into a variety of treatments, diverse diagnostics, healthcare processes, and their respective outcomes.

### AI in Medical Imaging

Artificial intelligence can be immensely helpful in conducting medical imaging diagnosis. In the radiology sectors, the entrance of artificial intelligence enjoys much fanfare over the past few years. The massive inflow of investment in this direction is the proof of how medical imaging is on the agenda when it boils down to the integration of AI in the healthcare sector. As AI has entered the field of medical imaging, healthcare professionals can now personalize treatments and smoothly transmit the results. Doctors are now able to diagnose a variety of cardiovascular disorders, bone fractures, and deep wounds. It would not be an exaggeration to say that AI has transformed and revolutionized the healthcare industry.

### Virtual Nurses

Artificial Intelligence can be used to build virtual nurses that can efficiently assist and monitor patients. They also can monitor and regulate the medication timetables in between follow up visits. You can feed these nurses with a variety of data to make them stronger. The more encyclopedic knowledge these nurses possess, the better they are at interaction with different patients. A thick knowledge base can enable them to provide necessary information to patients without the mediation of a doctor. This will save time and resources in the healthcare sector. You can model AI to understand different types of text and images. You also can incorporate it with human empathy to personalize the patient's experience.

### Targeted Diagnosis

With a diversity of healthcare data flowing in from different sides, artificial intelligence must sort out the data first and then use it to educate itself and form a network. In the universe of healthcare data, AI can sort out structured or unstructured data. Structured learning involves Neural Network System, Machine Learning (ML), and Modern Deep Learning. However, all unstructured data makes use of Natural Language Processing (NLP).

In the machine learning techniques, the system uses analytics algorithms to detect particular patient traits that include the information that is harvested when a patient pays a visit to a practitioner. The traits include physical state, lab tests, details of medications, symptoms, diagnosis, diagnostic imaging, and disease-specific data. Machine learning techniques help healthcare professionals determine different outcomes in the treatment of a patient. For example, the technique of neural networking is used in the diagnostic process of breast cancer.

The most common kind of machine learning is dubbed as supervised learning in which the system picks up the database information of a patient and pairs it up with the physical traits of the patient to make the outcome more targeted. Modern deep learning takes the same input as deep learning and channelizes it toward a neural network, which can be defined as a hidden layer that simplifies the output. Eventually, the process helps healthcare professionals to summarize and simplify the outcome of multiple diagnoses. This adds to a definitive conclusion of a diagnosis.

Natural language processing is akin to structured data processes. It focuses on the unstructured data in a clinical setting. This type of data flows from clinical notes to processing of the text when a doctor examines a patient. The data encompasses lab reports and physical examination narratives. This process scans historical databases that are replete with relevant keywords, which makes diagnosis fast and efficient, saving the time of the healthcare professional.

Cancers and cardiovascular diseases are at the top in terms of affecting humans on a mass scale. Therefore there must be no shortage of resources for early detection and treatment. Artificial intelligence offers a wide range of benefits in early detection and diagnosis by pinpointing risk alerts that a patient may confront.

### AI in Retail

Artificial intelligence is gradually carving out its way through many industries, however, lots of people don't understand the fact of what it is and how much potential it has. Artificial intelligence encompasses predictive analytics and machine learning that collects and analyzes data and use the same to make accurate predictions, forecasts, and decisions. The tech uses advanced analytical abilities to convert raw data that is harvested through IoT and relevant resources into some kind of actionable insights.

The retail industry in the present day is built around data-driven retail experiences and elevated consumer expectations. However, the personalized shopping experience is what can add value to a customer's purchase. It also can add value to a customer's buying decision as he or she is in a better position to decide on what to buy and what to leave aside. Digital and physical sales and purchase channels are blending as technologies make progress. Retailers who are willing to innovate and embrace change will position themselves as market leaders. Different studies suggest that over 80% of enterprises will adopt artificial intelligence for their retail business in the next few decades. Those who stay indecisive about adopting it will be left behind by their competitors and will lose their market share.
