# History of Artificial Neural Networks

The history of artificial neural networks dates back to the 1940s when Walter Pitts and Warren McCullough built an algorithm-based system that was built to function like the human brain. With the conception of the _perceptron_, an algorithm, there was progress in the field of building artificial neural networks. Perceptron was capable of performing some complex operations such as recognition tasks.

Artificial neural networks (ANNs) are revolutionizing businesses and everyday life. ANNs, have brought AI to the next level. Machines can be trained to learn and recognize different patterns. They can make predictions which previously only humans could do. That’s how they also can solve different problems that humans come across every day.

ANNs are computational models that are essentially algorithms. They have the unique ability to carve out meaning from complex or imprecise data to locate patterns and also detect trends that appear to be too convoluted for the human brain. Artificial neural networks have eased off different difficulties of humans. You can spot them in Gmail, Uber, and Amazon. Once they are trained effectively, they don’t need your assistance from onwards. They will learn from whatever data comes their way. The human brain consists of neurons that make the fundamental blocks of the brain. Artificial neural networks are the same as the human brain in the sense that they too contain many neurons that process information and transmit it as per needs.
