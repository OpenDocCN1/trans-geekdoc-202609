Take the example of a person who has to withdraw a thousand dollars because he is on vacation with his family and he has run out of hard cash. He goes to the automated teller machine and gets his card captured due to an unknown reason. It is midnight and there is no employee available at the bank. This is where AI-powered chatbots play an important role in guiding the customer and solving his problem. Chatbots don’t sleep and they are not bound by any time zone so they can help customers irrespective of time and place.

You might think that nowadays almost every online business has a chatbot so the question is whether it is a simple chatbot or an AI-powered chatbot. There is a big difference between the two types of chatbots. A simple chatbot that most businesses have deployed on their websites is static. It doesn’t learn through its own experience until you feed it with data from the backend. However, an AI-powered chatbot can learn through customer interactions which helps improve them, and in turn, improves customer experience. A brilliant example of chatbots is Erica, the VA of the Bank of America. You can request her to send you to push notifications of your credit scores and also help you in paying bills or conducting financial transactions online.

# Risk Management

Credit cards are a problem when it comes to securing financial transactions. There is always a fear of a fraud or a malafide transaction. The best way to mitigate this risk is by conducting a thorough analysis of the potential customer so that the right person is authenticated. AI can help you do real-time identification of the client. This is how AI can help the banking sector save millions of dollars that are otherwise wasted on fraudulent practices. You can deploy several technologies like facial recognition scans, iris scans, and voice recognition, etc.
