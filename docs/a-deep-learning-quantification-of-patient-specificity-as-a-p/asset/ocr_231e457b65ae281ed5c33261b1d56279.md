Recent research suggests that specificity on a range of therapist tasks has benefits, positive association with CBT sessions. Our exploration of treatment length was limited by the available data - notably individuals could attend but were not in the treatment center. Future application of machine learning could help identify relevant concepts. Our findings indicate that the role of specificity in enhancing treatment engagement might be beneficial, at a minimum, our research suggests that patients using a higher degree of concrete, specificity and task (actions) in dialogue attended a greater number of sessions, above the effects of established predictors of treatment attendance, such as age, gender, medication use, use, and baseline severity.

The positive and negative effects between therapist tasks and the role of specificity in treatment is complex. We have previously proposed flexible movement between specificity, concrete and general approaches is more important for mental health than a focus on concrete details. Our research provides evidence for the flexibility of application of specificity when challenging negative thoughts (beliefs). This of this study should also be addressed, including that dialogue specificity was not related to measures of concrete concepts explored in the literature (rum, et al.), and change over time. Our deep learning was also validated on test which showed experiencing depression, and that our measure of specificity might be biased towards lately characteristic of depressive presentations. The findings and results should be interpreted in line with this. Further research is needed to determine whether increased specificity improves continued treatment attendance or outcomes.

There are a number of novel interventions translated from cognitive science sciences that target either specificity (Mayry et al., 2019; Raes et al., 2009), flexible movement between general and specificity levels of

Our research needs future studies to explore what experience of teaching specificity, or specificity interventions to beginning (or begins) of patient CBT could help improve the number of CBT sessions.
