# 2.5. Sympt measures

The Patient Questionnaire (PHQ-9; Kroeke et al., 2001) is a valid and reliable index of depression, and was used as our primary outcome measure. The PHQ-9 is administered at each CBT session, as well as the Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7-item scale (GAD; Spitzer et al., 2006), which we pre-registered as a secondary outcome. We also intended to examine Work and Social Anxiety (Witzman et al., 2008), but were unable to obtain this data. As we used real-service data, our conceptualisation of clinical outcomes was limited by the available date.
