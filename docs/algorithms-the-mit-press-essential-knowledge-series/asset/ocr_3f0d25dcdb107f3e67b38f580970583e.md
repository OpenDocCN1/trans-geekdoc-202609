The same can we be said for another simple sorting algorithm that we'll describe now. Like selection sort, this is a sorting method that is easy to understand beyond computers. In fact, this is the way we may sort our hand in a card game.

Imagine that you play a game of cards in which you are dealt cards (for example, you could be playing Rummy). As you take one card after the other, you want to sort them in your hand. We assume that the card rank, from the low- to highest, is:

2 3 4 5 6 7 8 9 J Q K A

In fact, in many games (and Rummy), the ace can be the lowest- and highest-ranking card, but we'll assume there is a single order only.
