This is an important concept, which brings us to the so-called randomized algorithms: those algorithms that use an element of chance in their operation. This runs contrary to our intuition; we expect algorithms to be the ultimate deterministic beasts, slavishly following the instructions we lay down for them on a preordained path. And yet randomized algorithms have blossomed in recent years, as it has turned out that chance can help us solve problems that remain intractable to more standard approaches.³
