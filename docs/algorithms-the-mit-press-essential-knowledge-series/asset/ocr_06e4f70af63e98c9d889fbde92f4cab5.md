# EPILOGUE

On July 15, 2019, Mark Carney, the Bank of England governor, presented the design of the new £50 note, expected to enter circulation about two years later. The Bank of England had decided in 2018 to celebrate a character from science with the new banknote and opened a six-week public nomination period for the selection. It received a total of 227,299 nominations for 989 eligible characters. From this, the Banknote Character Advisory Committee decided on a short list of 12 options. Then the governor made the final decision, selecting Alan Turing. He commented, “Alan Turing was an outstanding mathematician whose work had an enormous impact on how we live today. As the father of computer science and artificial intelligence, as well as war hero, Alan Turing’s contributions were far ranging and path breaking. Turing is a giant on whose shoulders so many now stand.
