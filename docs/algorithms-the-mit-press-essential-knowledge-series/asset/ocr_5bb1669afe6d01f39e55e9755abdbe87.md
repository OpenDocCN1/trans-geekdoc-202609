This seems like the ultimate pass-the-buck game, but look at what happens if we try to see it unfold with an example. We start with the numbers 95, 59, 15, 27, 82, 56, 35, 51, 21, and 79. We give them to Alice (A), who splits them in two, and passes them to Bob (B) and Carol (C). You can see that in the first level of the upside-down tree below:

![](bbox=[0.11, 0.318, 0.933, 0.558])

Then Bob splits his numbers into two, and passes them on to Dave (D) and Eve (E). Similarly, Carol splits her numbers, and passes them on to Frank (F) and Grace (G). Our cast of characters continue passing the buck. Dave divides his numbers to Heidi (H) and Ivan (I); Eve distributes her two numbers to Judy (J) and Karen (K); Frank and Grace split to Leo (L) and Mallory (M) and Nick (N) and Olivia (O), respectively.
