In the operation of the SNS, some components, such as high-voltage power supplies, should run so that pulses are distributed in timing slots as evenly as possible. An algorithm devised to do the distribution is essentially the same as the rhythm-making algorithm and Euclid's algorithm, taking us from numbers to subatomic particles to music.³
