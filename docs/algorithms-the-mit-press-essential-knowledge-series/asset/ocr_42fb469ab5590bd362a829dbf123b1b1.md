The way the weights and biases of the neurons are updated is similar to the way a single neuron is updated. Again, the updates are calculated based on mathematical derivatives. You can think of the whole neural network as an enormous function whose variables are the weights and biases of all the neurons. Then we can calculate the derivative of each and every weight and bias with respect to the loss, and use that derivative to update the neuron.
