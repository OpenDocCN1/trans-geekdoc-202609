The real Metaverse is the next level.

## Where did it all begin?

Metaverse was once a far-fetched concept that we may never imagine materializing soon. This fictional virtual world was introduced by American author Neal Stephenson in his 1992 novel “Snow Crash.”

The Metaverse in the story appeared like an urban environment on a 100-meter wide road which Stephenson referred to as the Street. It was an undeveloped avenue that allowed participants to make their own 3D buildings, structures, mini-streets, signs, special neighborhoods, and even free-combat zones. The three-dimensional space was accessible through personal terminals and VR goggles.

The futuristic life-like avatars in the story and other digital reality features have sparked the interest of the gaming products developers, architecture, fashion, design, and other related industries. Soon afterward, Oculus Quest and VR headsets models were released in the market and become widely popular to access virtual games.

Stephenson wrote:

“A speech with magical force. Nowadays, people don’t believe in these kinds of things. Except in the Metaverse, that, where magic is possible. The Metaverse is a fictional structure made of code. And code is just a form of speech- the form that computers understand. The Metaverse in its entirety could be considered a single vast Nam-Shub, enacting itself on L. Bob Rife’s fiber-optic network.”

## So, what exactly is Metaverse?

The Metaverse is an integrated network or social sphere that operates in the digital space. It is a continuum of several immersive virtual experiences in the digital spaces. All the synchronous experiences enhance the sensation of interactions. It is far better than the present feed-based social media video calls.

The closest way to explain a Metaverse experience is through video games. These games utilize 3D virtual reality features to deliver immersive experiences to users.

Most of them have elements and services that are already crossing over the aspects of our lives. Roblox, for instance, has been hosting virtual meetups, concerts, and events for its users.

Users of the Metaverse or augmented reality world would meet and socialize with other participants using personalized avatars in real-time. The different range of activities is accessible using specialized devices like virtual reality headsets, smartphones, digital glasses, goggles, and more.

In short, the Metaverse is an embodied internet that provides a scaled-up world in the virtual space. Just like the world where we are in, it is a place where we connect with our families, friends, work colleagues, clients, and other significant people.

Other functions of the Metaverse:

*   Provides a remote platform where a group of individuals socializes, works, talks, and virtually does anything
*   Allows active interaction and strong connection regardless of each other’s location
*   Interconnecting different platforms just like what the internet is doing- allowing the users to access multiple websites with a single browser
*   Offers the things we want to experience with other people- hanging out, playing games, attending events, watching concerts, dating, shopping, working, accessing educational opportunities, visiting remote locations, and a whole lot more
*   Uses the technologies behind virtual reality (VR), augmented reality (AR), holographic avatars, spatial video and audio, and other forms of communication

*   Delivers functions that enable groups of people to collaborate for better productivity or host activities to conduct business

For instance, real estate companies can offer virtual property tours to prospective clients and investors. Remote teams can plan and discuss important matters seamlessly wherever their individual locations are. Users and consumers can easily access the immersive digital content using smartphones or digital glasses.

Metaverse also provides an immersive environment where the computing world is embedded in the physical realm and vice-versa. The fusion results in the creation of a new platform layer that allows transcendence and interactive connection among humans.

This simulated digital space mimics the real world and allows users to choose how to experience this simulated reality.  As it continues to grow, Metaverse offers an alternative virtual space where we and others co-exist, just like the real world.

## What does it take to build the Metaverse?

Unlike other virtual reality ideas that are embodied in the apps, developing a Metaverse is more complicated. It is not created using the agile development model that is commonly used today.

To create a complex virtual space, it is important to make interconnecting layers such as infrastructure, human interface, spatial computing, decentralization tools, user experiences, creators economy, and content engine drivers.

*   Infrastructure involves GPUs and connectivity technologies such as Wi-Fi, 5G, and cloud
*   Human interfaces are devices and technologies that will help users to leverage the benefits of the Metaverse such as headsets, haptics, AR glasses, and more

*   Spatial computing refers to modeling frameworks and 3D visualization
*   Decentralization elements include blockchain, edge computing, AI, and other democratization tools

*   User experiences involve skills in digital apps for work, gaming, shopping, or events

*   Creator's economy is the selection of digital assets, tools for design, and e-commerce shops
*   Content engine drivers help in fostering engagement such as social media, reviews, ads, and ratings

## Is Metaverse the Web 3.0?

Web 1.0 was yesterday’s internet. It existed between 1991 and 2004 when people who consumed it were more than the number of content creators.

Web 2.0 is today’s internet, where creators dominated the platform and people interact through social media like Facebook and online platforms like YouTube. We post, comment, and share images and videos.

In Web 2.0 that we are utilizing now, we cannot monetize and control shared content. The platforms we are enjoying are highly centralized and controlled by big players in the tech and business industries. These companies are also developing and selling products or services in a centralized manner. They own our content on their platforms and control our creations.

To use their platforms and services, we need to register, provide our private data, and accept their terms and conditions. When they want to block or ban us from using their platforms, they can do it without our consent.

Even the gamers worldwide which are estimated to be over 2.5 billion do not own the in-game assets despite paying for them with real money.

But as the technology advances and developers become more creative, Web 2.0 is quickly evolving into the next-generation concept of the internet or Web 3.0. This evolution is believed to allow users to own and control online content, creations, online identities, and digital assets. We can make content, control, and monetize it to our own advantage.

Web 3.0 will be open to everyone who wants to leverage digital services. We do not need to register before accessing the services of any platform. As sole custodians of our private data and content, it will be our responsibility to protect them.

Web 3.0’s backbone would be blockchain technology. The blockchain’s financial transactions would use cryptocurrency.

*   Blockchain enables peer-to-peer networking. The decentralized nature of blockchain allows users to own the data and conduct independent business with other users. The full authority on how and when to share the data is returned to the users. The assigned private keys help users to access the account.
*   Cryptocurrency is virtual money or token used for transactions, both online and in the real world. It is stored in an electronic or digital wallet.

This is an example of what the eventual transformation of Web 2.0 to Web 3.0 looks like:

*   From a centralized system to a decentralized blockchain
*   From Chrome to Brave (a fast browser that offers more privacy to users)
*   From Facebook to Steemit (the rewards-based social blockchain platform where users get paid for their creations and curated content)
*   From bank to Metamask (the crypto wallet that is powered by the Ethereum blockchain)
*   From a limited liability company or LLC to a decentralized autonomous organization or DAO (a type of LCC where no one has major control behind decisions)
*   From Amazon Web Services or AWS to InterPlanetary File System or IPFS (a peer-to-peer hypermedia protocol that powers the distributed web)
*   Twitter has rolled out its NFT profile pic verification

Here’s a quick look at the features and advantages of Metaverse:

1\. Democratized architecture and operations

With a democratized architecture, the Metaverse platforms are not owned by a single entity, group, or institution.

Holders of the platform’s cryptocurrency have a right to engage in the network’s decision-making. This means that it is no longer possible to make algorithm changes without the consent of the users.

2\. Open-source

Since open-source is one of the key elements of the Metaverse, builders need to make their source codes available to everyone.

Global tech players are required to make their technologies, AI, and image synthesis accessible to help developers, startup companies, and other interested parties who want to develop their own platforms.

With more upscale simulation capacity, Metaverse can easily advance scientific research in different fields including aerospace, healthcare, and manufacturing.

At present, we are using the two-dimensional format when accessing online information. It involves visuals and texts, a 2D screen, and other input peripherals that allow us limited interactivity.

The realization of the Metaverse is regarded as a major input-out exchange revolution. With the help of hand controllers, voice commands, and eye-tracking features, we can easily interact with the visualized data.

Non-fungible token (NFT) explained

The non-fungible token or NFT came around in 2014 but only gained notoriety recently. During the early days, there were already securitized digital art versions and iconic NBA games video clips on Instagram.

These digital assets were sold online using cryptocurrency and other modes of payment. Since 2017, sold NFTs were valued at $174 million and counting.

Compared to other digital creations with infinite supply available, NFTs have a very limited run. Another thing that sets NFTs different from other digital commodities is their built-in authentication that gives buyers assurance that they are buying original items. Collectors call this proof of ownership the “digital bragging rights.”

The most expensive NTF was artist Pak’s “Merge”. It was sold at a record-breaking $91.8 million. Next was Mike “Beeple” Winklemann's daily drawings called EVERYDAYS: The First 5000 days. The collection was sold at $69.3 million during the auction at Christie’s

“Essentially, NFTs create digital scarcity,” Yellow Umbrella Ventures managing director Arry Yu said.

NFTs have unique identifying codes similar to cryptocurrencies since they are created using the same underlying software.

However, the likeness ends there. Every NTF comes with a digital signature, making it non-fungible or cannot be exchanged for another. Crypto and physical money are fungible, which means they can be exchanged or traded.

How are NFTs created?

The first-ever NFT was “Quantum.” It was created in May 2014 by Kevin McCoy and Anil Dash. The token was a video clip of Jennifer, McCoy’s wife. He registered it on the Namecoin blockchain. Dash bought it for $4. The pioneering duo called the NFT technology the “monetized graphics.”

The attempt to make a big NFT project occurred in October 2015. The project Etheria was composed of 457 hexagonal tiles which went unsold. In 2017, the awareness about tradable NFTs heightened when the online game CryptoKitties began monetizing their cat NFTs. The market gained traction. After 5 years, the remaining tiles of the Etheria project which were valued at 1 ETH or $0.43 during the launching day were sold out for a whopping $1.4 million.

Another notable example of a non-fungible token is the CryptoPunks, a collection of 10,000 characters that are stored on Ethereum. This project has inspired the CryptoArt movement.

It also shaped the development of the open standard interface or the ERC-721 that powers the majority of the digital collectibles and art. This standard has given the NFT a currency value.

When people believe that Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies are the digital answer to fiat currency, NFTs become the digital answer to owning rare, valuable collectibles. The unique data of every NFT is easy to verify and validate as the authentic certificate is generated by blockchain technology.

Most NFTs reside on the Ethereum blockchain and exchanges take place in crypto specialist sites. Their value is influenced by the demand for the item and set by the market.

A non-fungible token (NFT) is minted or created from digital objects to represent the intangible and tangible items of real-world items which include:

*   Videos and sports highlights
*   Digital art
*   Music
*   Game and related items
*   Collectibles
*   Video game skins and virtual avatars
*   Designer sneakers
*   GIFs

*   Domain names
*   Essays and digital content

Why NFTs have become popular

The resurgence of NFTs around 2017 was due to several factors.

Popularity of cryptocurrency

The exciting things about crypto lie in the framework that combines the laws of scarcity and the economics of royalties. Investors, traders, and consumers want to get hold of the digital currency to leverage its potential. As for the NFT, this digital asset represents ownership of unique digital art or content.

Use of blockchain technology

Ethereum blockchain powers the NFTs in terms of trading, validating, and recording transactions. It eliminates peer-to-peer transactions that reduce the profits of an NFT creator or owner. All products that use Ethereum blockchain share a similar “backend” which makes NFTs portable and easy to sell to other users.

After confirmation of the transaction confirmation, no one can forge or manipulate the ownership data. Finally, Ethereum is stable and secure which means that your NFTs are always up for grabs.

Increased value due to online presence

The more the NFT is seen on social media and other online platforms, the higher value it gains. When it is sold, the current owner of the asset gets 90% of the revenue and the original creator enjoys a 10% share. There is a strong potential for ongoing revenue and increasing value as the assets are traded in the NFT markets.

Unique and distinct value

NFTs are original works, hence authenticity is 100% guaranteed. These digital collectibles have verifiable distinguishing information that is recorded in the blockchain. The record ensures that there will be no fake collectibles to circulate as every NTF can be traced to the issuer or the creator. Moreover, because there are no two NTFs alike, they cannot be exchanged directly.

How do NFTs work?

Technically, NFTs are digital collector’s items. They are blockchain-based assets with recorded information in smart contracts. Those who purchase them get a digital file rather than physical forms like actual paintings or CDs. Upon purchase, the buyer gets the exclusive rights to own the file. The unique data of the NFTs makes it convenient to transfer tokens between the rightful owners, validate the ownership, and store certain information. Creators and artists can include their signatures in the metadata of the NFTs.

With NFTs, content creators and artists have found a unique venue to monetize their works. Rather than the traditional way of relying on auction houses or putting their items up in galleries, they can sell them directly to consumers worldwide as NFTs.  This means enjoying more profits compared to allowing a third party to sell their creations.

Artists like painters, musicians, composers, and the likes can also program in royalties so whenever their creation is purchased by another owner, they will receive a corresponding sales percentage.

Why is it important to consider the market cap of the Metaverse tokens?

The market cap measures the value of the cryptocurrency. It provides the crypto coin or token’s detailed overview, allowing investors to compare its value against other cryptocurrencies. It also helps in determining the coin’s volatility and potential growth. To find out the market cap, simply multiply the total number of mined coins by the single coin’s current price in the market.

To demonstrate:

*   Crypto X has 500,000 coins in circulation. Each token is valued at $1. The market cap of Crypto X is $500,000.
*   Crypto Z has 200,000 coins in circulation. Each coin is valued at $2. The market cap of Crypto Z is $400,000.

Like the conventional investment, it is important to consider the market cap or the total dollar value of mined coins.

The larger the coin’s market cap, the greater the stability that it offers to investors. Crypto coins with smaller market cap are susceptible to the constant changes in the market, creating dramatic gains or losses.

Generally, there are three types of market capitalization – large-cap (companies with $10 billion or more), middle or mid-cap (with $2 to $10 billion), and small-cap (with $300 million to $2 billion).

*   The large-cap companies are the major players in the industry and have been around for a longer time.

While they do not necessarily guarantee huge returns within a short-term investment period, the longer-term investment rewards their investors.

*   The mid-cap companies are those showing a rapid growth in the industry. Their potentials are great and can give a good return on investment.
*   The small-cap companies are younger in the industries or serving a niche market. Typically, they have fewer resources and are vulnerable to economic slowdowns.

Classification of Metaverse projects

To reiterate, the Metaverse project is a computer-generated space. This highly-immersive, 3D parallel universe is focused on providing real-world-like social interactions and activities. Users have their own avatars to represent their virtual identity to engage with other players or participants.

There are two primary classifications of Metaverse:

1\. Blockchain-based Metaverse - It is a platform that delivers real-world value to the virtual space through non-fungible tokens (NFTs), blockchain, and decentralized finance (DeFi) technologies. Examples are Axies Infinity, Decentraland, and The Sandbox.

2\. Collaborative virtual environment – It is a virtual world where users gather and collaborate for recreation, entertainment, or business purposes. Examples of this type of Metaverse are Facebook, Roblox, and Fortnite.

# Chapter 1: What Is Cryptocurrency and How Does It Work?

You may have heard someone refer to cryptocurrency as “digital money” before. This term is technically true since you can use cryptocurrency to purchase goods and services online. Online transactions made by cryptocurrencies are secure because they use online ledgers that are protected by strong cryptography. However, the term “digital currency” doesn’t do cryptocurrency justice as it doesn’t capture the reason why it’s so appealing to investors all over the world.

You can think of cryptocurrencies as a value system. People, or investors, purchase them because they believe that their value will increase in the future. This is similar to how investors buy company stocks because they believe that the company will perform better in the future, increasing share prices.

The difference is that cryptocurrencies are decentralized. This means that there is no central company or controlling structure. Their values can’t be manipulated and are not affected by government interference. The value of cryptocurrency is determined by only two factors: the cryptocurrency’s blockchain utility and the investors’ demand level. While this makes their exchange rate highly volatile, it also makes them resistant to inflation.

## How Do They Work?

You may be wondering what a blockchain is. Blockchains are a vital aspect of several cryptocurrencies. A blockchain is an organizational means of guaranteeing transactional data’s integrity. In other words, it is a digital ledger of transactions and is dispersed across a network of various computer systems. This means that the ledger is not controlled by just one system. The decentralized network allows the blockchain to keep working while authenticating all the transactions that go through.

Transparency, heightened trust, and reinforced shared data security are all characteristics of blockchain technology. Some say, however, that blockchains can also be unmanageable, inefficient, and energy, and financially consuming.

Investors buy a cryptocurrency when they are confident in the strength and unity of the underlying blockchain. As we said, the majority of cryptocurrencies use blockchain. This means that when people invest in a cryptocurrency, they are betting on how attractive, functional, and resilient that blockchain is.

Cryptocurrency transactions are continuously recorded on the fundamental blockchain. Transactions are then grouped in “blocks” and added to the “chain.” This is what authenticates the reliability of the transactions and allows the network to resume running. A shared public ledger is then used to record all transactions. This ledger is accessible to everyone, which means that anyone can view the transactions made on Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other major blockchains.

But why are blockchain transactions validated using computing power? Simply because they are reimbursed with the principal cryptocurrency. This incentive-based system is known as the PoW or proof-of-work mechanism. You may have heard of the term “crypto mining.” In the case of PoW, computers that are used to prove a cryptocurrency transaction’s validation or authenticity are known as miners. Cryptocurrency can be earned by using high-power computers to solve cryptographic equations to authenticate data blocks and add transaction records to the ledger.

Crypto assets aren’t held in regular bank accounts since they’re not exactly tangible. Each asset has its own digital address that comes with both private keys, which allow users to unlock and send cryptocurrency, and public ones, which are available publicly to allow anyone to send cryptocurrency to users. These keys are long strings made up of letters and numbers. Digital addresses allow users to deliver funds and receive them.

## Pros and Cons of Cryptocurrency

Cryptocurrencies are essentially created to make the transfer of funds a lot easier between two parties. They eliminate the need for third-party establishments, such as credit card providers and banks. These transactions, as you know, are highly secure as they use private and public keys, as well as different consensus methods, such as proof-of-work and proof-of-stake mechanisms. User account addresses or wallets are provided with a public key, which enables the collection of funds. They are also secured by private keys, which are only known by the holder, and can be used to send transactions. The best thing about cryptocurrencies is that minimal transaction fees are required to complete the transaction, unlike wire transfers and other expensive methods.

Unfortunately, since cryptocurrency transactions are somewhat anonymous and untraceable, they are ideal for a wide array of illegal activities, such as tax evasion and money laundering. The privacy level depends on the cryptocurrency you’re using. For example, Bitcoin is not as private as Zcash, Monero, and dash, which are extremely hard to trace since its blockchain holds forensic analysis. It has actually made it possible for the authorities to arrest criminals. The following chapters will discuss how you can protect yourself from the risks of investing in cryptocurrency.

## Different Types of Cryptocurrencies

Bitcoin, as you probably know, was the first cryptocurrency. To this day, it remains the leading cryptocurrency in both value and popularity. You may be surprised to learn that there are now thousands of cryptocurrencies that come with different stipulations and functions. Some altcoins are technically copies or clones of Bitcoin, while others are created from square one. The term “altcoin” refers to any cryptocurrency that isn’t Bitcoin. It is basically an alternative (alt) to the leading cryptocurrency.

Bitcoin came to life in 2009 and was created by Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonym for either a person or a group of people. More than 18.8 million bitcoins were circulating in the market as of August 2021. This equals around $858.9 billion. The exact amount is often updated, depending on the value of Bitcoin. To prevent the manipulation of Bitcoin’s value and avoid inflation, there are only 21 million bitcoins in existence.

Ethereum, Litecoin, EOS, Namecoin, Cardano, and Peercoin, which you may have heard of before, are some examples of altcoins. These cryptocurrencies hit the market after Bitcoin’s undeniable success. Bitcoin, at the moment, makes up about 46.5% of the aggregate value of all cryptocurrencies available ($1.8 trillion) by August 2021.

Altcoins

Bitcoin and altcoins share the same fundamental framework. This means that you may think of all of them as one huge computer (the miner) that processes great amounts of transactions and data simultaneously. In fewer words, they all act as peer-to-peer systems and share code. Some altcoins, however, were created to become a less expensive alternative for Bitcoin.

Although Bitcoin has set the standard for other altcoins, being the first cryptocurrency iteration, it underperforms in several areas. For instance, the proof-of-work mechanism, which we had just discussed, is very time and energy-consuming, limiting the cryptocurrency’s smart contract proficiency. This is why many altcoins came up with a competitive consensus method known as Proof-of-Stake or PoS. This mechanism authenticates transactions and creates blocks in less time, using less energy.

Ether, which is the second leading cryptocurrency in terms of market capitalization, is another example. Ether is used for gas, the payment for transaction costs, in Ethereum’s smart contracts. Some altcoins compete against Bitcoin by addressing its price volatility. These are known as stablecoins and are great for everyday transactions.

While Bitcoin proves to be the most powerful cryptocurrency, altcoins could still establish an attractive market for themselves. There are several types of altcoins in existence. They are characterized according to their utility and consensus mechanisms.

## Types of Altcoins

Stablecoins

Cryptocurrencies have been known for their high volatility ever since they hit the markets. As mentioned above, stablecoins are created to reduce this price volatility by attaching their value to a basket of goods. This could be other cryptocurrencies, fiat currencies, or precious metals. This basket serves as a reserve in case the cryptocurrency fails or encounters issues. The prices surely fluctuate; however, it does so within a controlled range.

Mining-Based

Mining-based altcoins, as you can probably infer, are created by mining. Most of these altcoins use a proof-of-work mechanism. Monero, Litecoin, Zcash, and most top altcoins fall under the mining-based category. Pre-mined coins, which aren’t generated through an algorithm, are alternatives to mining-based altcoins. Ripple is an example of pre-mined altcoins and was distributed before being listed on the cryptocurrency market.

Security Tokens

Security tokens are very similar to the securities or stocks that one would trade in a stock market. The only difference is that security tokens, as you know, have digital addresses. They are similar to regular stocks and usually guarantee equity for ownerships or dividends for payouts to users. Investors are offered security tokens through ICOs or initial coin offerings.

Utility Tokens 

You can use utility tokens to buy a service, redeem rewards, or pay fees within a certain network. These altcoins can’t provide dividends or stakes for ownership.

Meme Coins

If you spend most of your time on social media platforms, then perhaps you already know that these coins are inspired by jokes. But what kind of jokes? These are silly takes on other major cryptocurrencies. These coins typically gain popularity for a short while, and only then are they mostly used by retail investors and crypto influencers to make short-term gains.

## Pros and Cons of Altcoins

Many altcoins are generally better versions of Bitcoin since they aim to address its weak points. However, they tend to have smaller investment markets in comparison to Bitcoin. Altcoins, particularly stablecoins, show a promising future for serving as a vehicle for daily transactions. Additionally, some mainstream institutions use certain altcoins, like Ripple and Ether, which results in a higher valuation. However, since altcoins lack regulation and investment specifications, the market is only distinguished by thin liquidity and limited investors, which results in even more volatile prices than Bitcoin. The great thing about altcoins is that there are numerous cryptocurrencies, each addressing a certain need and fulfilling a specific purpose in the crypto market. On the downside, this versatility and the abundance of options create a lot of confusion when it’s time to invest. The increased number of altcoins inevitably results in their lack of activity, which sinks the user’s invested dollar amount. The following chapters will explore whether it’s good to invest in altcoins.

# Chapter 2: Trading and Investing in Cryptocurrency

Many people have made incredible profits by trading and investing in cryptocurrencies. In retrospect, others have suffered great losses after investing in cryptocurrencies. ## Factors That Move the Crypto Markets

We’ve already explained that cryptocurrencies are decentralized and can’t be regulated nor manipulated by the government. This is why the value of cryptocurrency is purely determined by supply and demand. Cryptocurrencies are typically not affected by the factors that affect fiat or traditional currencies, such as political and economic circumstances. You can’t be entirely certain when it comes to cryptocurrencies. Even so, there are a few factors that affect the price of cryptocurrency.

For one, supply is a huge determinant. The price of Bitcoin and other altcoins is determined by the total number of coins in existence and the rate at which they are being released into the market, destroyed, and even lost. Market capitalization is another significant factor when it comes to deciding the value of a cryptocurrency. The current value of all the existing coins, alongside user forecasts and their perception of how the cryptocurrency is developing, are additional indicators. Another factor is press reviews of the currency, how it is being presented in the media, and the amount of coverage and publicity it is currently receiving. The fourth factor is the currency’s integration capabilities. How easily does the cryptocurrency integrate into prevailing platforms and infrastructure? Is it an acceptable form of payment on e-commerce platforms or transaction networks? Finally, key events and occurrences that are experienced by the cryptocurrency greatly affect its value. This includes economic obstacles, frequent updates, and security breaches.

## Trading vs. Investing

When you invest in cryptocurrency or any other market, you don’t need to worry about short-term market and price trends. This is because you are aiming to buy cryptocurrency at a price lower than what you would sell. This requires you to hold your cryptocurrencies for a long time. When you trade cryptocurrencies, you opt to generate short-term profit by buying at a low price and selling high during a short time. This requires you to keep an eye on short-term market trends and prices. While investing is more concerned with the fundamentals of cryptocurrencies, trading is concerned with the technicalities of the market. In a way, we can say that an investor’s main goal is to acquire more coins, while a trader’s goal is to obtain more dollars.

You don’t have to be one or the other. You can trade and invest simultaneously, as long as you know what you’re doing. Investors and traders must both be prepared to take losses. Regardless of their activities, anyone who partakes in the market must have good emotional control and high-risk tolerance. Cryptocurrencies can, at times, lose up to 80% and 90% of their worth on paper.

## Security Level

When people think about investing or trading in the markets, one question immediately pops into mind aside from the possible gains. That is: “how secure is it?” Since cryptocurrencies are created using blockchain technology, the ledger is generally very hard to tamper with. Besides, to carry out transactions, one must go through a two-factor authentication process. Most cryptocurrencies, the best ones at least, ask you to enter an authentication code, which you typically receive via text message after entering your username and password. There are many security measures taken to ensure the safety and protection of cryptocurrency users and holders. However, you must keep in mind that this doesn’t mean that cryptocurrencies are entirely threat-free. Cryptocurrencies, in some cases, can be hacked. Many starting cryptocurrencies had to face devastating setbacks after enduring costly hacks. For instance, BitGrail was hit with a  $195 million hack in 2018. During the same year, a hack cost Coincheck a good  $534 million.

Understanding all the risks you may face as a cryptocurrency investor can help you prepare for the worst, set reasonable expectations, and avoid disappointment. The point of being aware of the risks is to not adopt a pessimistic mindset or always anticipate the worst. However, it helps you come up with effective, detailed plans, pay better attention to market trends, make wiser decisions, and avoid unnecessary troubles.

## Underlying Risks

Although they are highly secure, you should know that cryptocurrency exchanges are highly susceptible to hacking and falling victim to illegal activities. They are even more vulnerable than traditional stock exchanges. These breaches and possible hacks can result in devastating losses to crypto-asset holders and investors.

Ensuring that your cryptocurrency is safely stored is not an easy task. Owning bonds and stocks is not nearly as complicated. Luckily, many cryptocurrency exchanges can make it easier for people to trade, purchase, and sell Bitcoin, Ethereum, and other altcoins. However, the high theft and cyber-attack risk make storing digital assets on cryptocurrency exchanges unfavorable for several people.

Although it can get quite taxing, a storing alternative to cryptocurrency exchanges would be cold storage or offline options. These include paper or hardware wallets. One of the riskiest things about resorting to offline storage options is the possibility of losing your private key. If this happens, accessing your cryptocurrency would be impossible.

If you decide to invest in one of the less popular altcoins, you need to keep in mind that there is no guarantee that it will take off. Competition is very high, and since the value of the currencies depends on supply and demand and publicity, the chances that an unpopular altcoin will succeed are near impossible. A limited number of cryptocurrencies can actually thrive. You also need to be aware of scams when exploring the market.

The technology being used is also something to look out for. Cryptocurrencies are relatively new to the world. They are made using unique and special technologies. Since these technologies are still under development and aren’t properly tested for daily life scenarios, they may pose a risk for investors.

## Tips for Safe Investment

Now that you know of the plausible risks that come with trading and investing in cryptocurrency, you need to take the necessary measures to keep yourself protected. While cryptocurrencies are among the riskiest ventures to invest in, they also prove to be one of the most sought-after commodities right now. Any avid investor knows that diversification is one of the main components of any successful investment strategy. For instance, if you plan on investing in Bitcoin, it would be wise not to put all your money in it. Explore the endless other currencies or even other investment options, and evaluate potential candidates. This way, you are not betting all your money on a single establishment. The chances that all your investments will fail are low.

Digital Currency Storage 

We’ve already established how several people struggle with their cryptocurrency storage. This is why you need to know where you are planning to keep yours. Search the web and conduct comparisons between the top digital wallets and exchanges available. There are several types of wallets in existence, and each has its own requirements, benefits, and levels of security. If you plan on using your exchange’s services, their storage options are something you should look into before you invest.

Volatility Preparedness

At this point, you are probably knowledgeable about the high volatility of the cryptocurrency market. You need to accept that you will witness dramatic swings and substantial ups and downs in the market. Your investment portfolio, as well as your physical, mental, and emotional wellbeing, need to stay resilient against these challenges. If you have your doubts, then investing in cryptocurrency may not be for you.

There are many factors to consider when it comes to investing in cryptocurrency. ## Storing Cryptocurrency

As you know, there are different cryptocurrency storage options. We will explain the difference between the several digital crypto wallets to make it easier for you.

Hardware Wallets

These work by storing your private key on a hardware device. For example, you can use a USB to do so. This is the best option in terms of security because it protects your private key from online threats, such as unauthorized access. You can use a hardware wallet to access your cryptocurrency from several devices.

Online Wallets

Online wallets store your private key on the cloud, and like hardware wallets, they can be accessed from any device. These are generally the easiest use. However, they are the least secure options, considering that they’re being controlled by a third party.

Mobile Wallets

As you can guess, mobile wallets allow you to access your private key through an application on your smart device. These wallets are highly advantageous because they allow you to make transactions wherever your cryptocurrency is accepted.

Desktop Wallets

As the name implies, desktop wallets are installed on your computer, which is a safer alternative to online wallets.

The wallet you decide to use determines how secure and convenient your cryptocurrency usage will be. There is also software available to aid you with tracking your cryptocurrency.
