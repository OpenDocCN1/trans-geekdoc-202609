# Arezoo Mirshamsi

Arezoo Mirshamsi Faculty of Engineering, University of Isfahan, Isfahan, Iran Arezoo Mirshamsi has been an MS student of Biomedical Engineering of the University of Isfahan since 2019. She graduated from Hamedan University of technology in 2018 with a BS Degree in Biomedical Engineering. The subject of her BS project was stress reduction with biofeedback by investigating heart rate variation while playing an Android game. She was also a member of the Editorial Board of the Student Journal of Medical Engineering (2015–2017). Her research interests include EEG and EMG signal processing, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning.

# Mohammad Reza Mohebbian

Mohammad Reza Mohebbian Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, University of Saskatchewan, Saskatoon, Canada Mohammad Reza Mohebbian studied biomedical engineering at the University of Isfahan for BSc and MSc degrees. He ranked in the top two of the BSc and MSc students. Presently, he is studying electrical and computer engineering at the University of Saskatchewan, Canada. He won a devoted scholarship from the University of Saskatchewan in 2019 and conducted research for Philips Canada during his PhD program. His career is mainly focused on machine learning, deep learning, pattern recognition, and image processing in biomedical fields.
