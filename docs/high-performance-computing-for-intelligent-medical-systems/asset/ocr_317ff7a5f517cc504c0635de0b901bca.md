# Sengul Dogan

![](bbox=[0.134, 0.153, 0.253, 0.252])

**Sengul Dogan** Digital Forensics Engineering, Technology Faculty, Firat University, Elazig, Turkey

**Sengul Dogan** received a master’s degree in bioengineering and a PhD in electrical and electronics engineering from Firat University, Elazig, Turkey, in 2007 and 2011, respectively. She is currently an Associate Professor with the Digital Forensics Engineering, Technology Faculty, Firat University. Her main research interests include computer forensics, mobile forensics, image processing, and signal processing. She has been working actively on developing algorithms in machine learning for biomedical data.

# Vandana Dubey

![](bbox=[0.134, 0.382, 0.253, 0.485])

**Vandana Dubey** Department of Electronics, Manipal University Jaipur, India

**Ms Vandana Dubey**, PhD (pursuing) from Manipal University Jaipur-Rajasthan, in Information and Technology Engineering. Received ME (72.8%) from IET-DAVV Indore-M.P., BE (73.13%) from BANSAL R.G.P.V. Bhopal-M.P., Diploma (67.01%) from S.R.G.W.P. R.G.P.V. Sagar-M.P., degrees in Electronics and Communication Engineering, in 2010, 2006 and 2003, respectively. She received a ‘Best student award’ in Diploma. She started her academic career in 2007. Since January 2015, she has been with the IPS Academy, an autonomous institute, Indore, where she is currently an Assistant Professor. Her research interests include physical layer security, PID controller, reinforcement learning, machine learning and artificial intelligence. She has published 13 research papers in national/international conferences and journals. She is a lifetime member of the IETE (Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers).
