# **8.9.5 Cat swarm optimization (CSO-BMA)**

Hadi _et al_ [56] suggested a novel Nature-inspired SC technique for ME called Cat Swarm Optimization (CSO-BM). The CSO-BM is an evolutionary technique. Since the algorithm doesn’t follow any definite search pattern, the chances of getting the global minimum accurately will be more and with a better quality solution. The optimization technique mimics the cat’s behavior.

A cat’s approach can be seen in two phases. The cat is accustomed to observing its target very closely while pretending to be at rest, which can be referred to and framed as seeking model. The next phase of the cat’s approach is framed as a tracing model where the cat goes behind the target. A matching MB is denoted by a cat in the search area in reference or current frame. In each iteration the position of the cat is modified using CSO strategy till a position with minimum cost function, that is, best match is found.

**CSO Movement = Seeking model + tracing Model.**
