# ## Recent trends in medical image segmentation with special focus on brain tumours and retinal images

# **Mithlesh Prasad Singh, Aheibam Dinamani Singh, Rajesh Kumar, Kishorjit Nongmeikapam and Wahengbam Kanan Kumar**

In a variety of areas from diagnosis and prediction to treatment based on the course of a disease, medical imaging plays a pivotal role. Image segmentation is a process that extracts the area of interest automatically or semi-automatically. Multimodality imaging is a concept used to capture an area of interest in the format used by various types, such as computer tomography (CT), magnetic resonance imaging (MRI), positron tomography (PET), etc. Various literatures demonstrated that their combination generates better classification of normal and abnormal tissues of brain, retina, lungs, kidney, etc. This chapter surveys state-of-the-art researches on biomedical image segmentation for brain tumours and retina segmentation, abnormality detection, advantages, disadvantages, and automated-learning techniques ranging from traditional machine learning to deep learning. The study also examines popular databases and contemporary approaches published so far. The chapter also provides a glimpse of key gaps and potential future scope for biomedical image segmentation of retina and brain tumours.
