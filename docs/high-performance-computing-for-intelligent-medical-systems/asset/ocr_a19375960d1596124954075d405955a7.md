# 8.10 Results and discussion

The chapter discusses the few BMAs which are inspired and based on the concept of natural phenomenon, and they are found to be efficient in the field of ME also. There are many more Nature-inspired SC techniques used for block matching that could not be explained in the chapter such as: Four-step GA-BMA (4SGA), Genetic Rhombus pattern Search (GRPS), Memetic PSO-BMA, Clonal PSO-BMA, Efficient Fuzzy Search (EFS), Momentum-directed GRPS (MD-GRPS), Pattern-Based PSO-BMA, Mutation and simplex integrated PSO-BMA, Simulated Annealing-BMA, etc. The available research studies have proven that the rigidity of regular BMAs for the ME process is addressed very effectively by the SC techniques used for ME. The problem of local optimum and the fixed pattern search is dealt with in all these algorithms by using the concept of dynamic initial velocity, random locations, early detection, mutation, cross-over, and many such biological events.

The various SC techniques that have been used as BMA for ME are surveyed and many of them are implemented on many video sequences. The average search points that are required to check for the best match and the average PSNR values are shown in the said table. The overall results and performance show that the application of SC techniques greatly enhances the process of ME and these will speed up the video compression process.
