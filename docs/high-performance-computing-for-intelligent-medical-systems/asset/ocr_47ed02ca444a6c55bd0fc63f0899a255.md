# # Preface

The medical and healthcare domain is a cornerstone of society, but modern medicine is highly dependent on engineering. There are machines and systems for analyzing patients, thus making diagnosis possible. X-ray and electrocardiography (ECG) are examples of such systems. X-ray, CT-scan and MRI (medical resonance imaging) provide visual representations, whereas ECG and EEG generate biological signals. Similarly, there are machines for management of patients as well. In the medical and healthcare sector a large amount of data is generated in every step, from the sensing of the ailment, to examination, correlation, classification and its management too.

The present era is witnessing a large-scale development in the field of computational intelligence (CI). High performance computing has emerged as a powerful solution to deal with complex problems. The development happening around the high performance computing and related fields is affecting us in a very positive way. The field of medical science has benefited greatly from the field of the computational intelligence. This has led to the development of intelligent medical systems (IMS). IMS are a result of research collaboration of high performance computing with medical science. The development of IMS requires a large amount of data generation, storage and processing. To apply high performance computing in the medical domain, in depth knowledge and understanding of the medical system is required.

This large amount of data is a necessity for correct analysis and correlation of disease. The generation, collection, storage and labeling of such large data is quite a challenging task. With the advent of wearable devices data generation is increased manyfold. The high performance computing tools seem a powerful option for working on such a large amount of data. They have the ability to perform complex tasks with high speed and accuracy. This knowledge helps researches to apply CI concepts for enhanced performance of medical systems. IMS are able to provide human-like ability and intelligence to existing applications. In this way, IMS can be utilized to get a deep insight into various healthcare and medical problems. It can also aid in controlling, analyzing and management of medical applications. The field of CI makes it possible to address the various issues concerning human health and medical systems. IMS will be fruitful for significant improvement in human life.

The in-depth analysis of large data is the prime requirement for the development of IMS. This can be very helpful in medical and healthcare applications. High performance computing is definitely the answer for computing needs in the medical domain. Deep computational architecture, with capabilities like low-level feature extraction and data augmentation can give a new direction to the present research in medical systems. Considering the future aspects of high performance computing and its involvement in the medical field, IMS is a widely researched area.
