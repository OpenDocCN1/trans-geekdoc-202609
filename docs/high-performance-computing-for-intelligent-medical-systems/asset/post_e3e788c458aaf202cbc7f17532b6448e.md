This CI approach provides an effective solution for disabled people based on a man–machine interface employing surface electromyogram signals. Chapter 3 demonstrates dysfunction of speech or voice that leads to a wide range of psychiatric disorders resulting from stigmatization from peers and the community. Different aspects of voice pathology research, applications for the Internet of Things, different datasets, feature extraction, and classification techniques are discussed. Chapter 6 presents four different functional models for Covid data analysis of Indian Covid patients accessed from Kaggle. Chapter 7 explores a model for improved patient feedback in diagnostics. The work focuses on a model that helps healthcare consumers to formulate and express their opinions quickly and easily about a perceived diagnostic phase in their care. Chapter 8 presents digital radiographs and other computerized methods used for the diagnosis of different diseases. It is required to compress and process the data efficiently with limited hardware resources, hence various soft computing techniques are applied in the field of video compression to make the process faster by accelerating the motion estimation. Chapter 9 describes how the brain manages the control system and blood circulation by nerve cells, so it becomes important to know about the symptoms of initial stage neuron disturbance in the body system. Chapter 10 presents mammogram images enhancement to improve the image quality for better interpretation and detection of breast cancer. In chapter 11 retina imaging helps in the diagnosis of proper eye disease along with complications of diabetes retinopathy, hypertension, myocardial infarction, etc. Therefore, the anatomy of the eye, physiology of the various eye diseases, retinal imaging methods-2D fundus imaging, and 3D optical coherence tomography (OCT) and their comparative analysis are done. Chapter 12 provides a survey on biomedical image segmentation for brain tumors and retina segmentation, abnormality detection, advantages, disadvantages, and automated-learning techniques ranging from traditional machine learning to deep learning are done. Also, it provides a glimpse of key gaps and potential future scope for biomedical image segmentation of retina and brain tumors. Chapter 13 discusses an AI-based proportional integral derivative (PID) controller with various biomedical systems and a comparative study of all primitives and novel control strategies for the AI-based PID controller regarding the health care system is explored.
