After clinical assessment with laryngeal stroboscopy, a safe and unhealthy vocal fold of individuals was established. In clinical cases, the perceived severity of voice disorders has been assessed at a scale of 1 to 3, with 3 being the worst. On the basis of a panel of three medical experts, each sample was connected to a severity ranking. The database contains three different types of documents. The sampling frequency in the database was 44 kHz and the speech was captured in the computerized speech lab (CSL) software. Several specialist physicians at King Abdul Aziz University Hospital have tested and confirmed the voice disorders recorded in this database [86].

# 3.4 Acoustic speech features with machine learning for voice pathology classification

There are two subsections in this section. The first one addresses several important speech pathology features extraction procedures. Research works using handcrafted features and ML classifiers are discussed in the second subsection.

# 3.4.1 Feature extraction techniques

This is the most critical step in any ML process. This includes the conversion of a voice signal into acoustic features by digital signal processing techniques.

+   A. Acoustic analysis. This is an estimate of the audio metadata in the voice. Acoustic assessment results may be used to determine the severity of a speech disturbance. Any activity related to acoustic voice signal analysis can be as follows. (A) Abnormalities around the basic pitch and the peak amplitude. (B) The frequency range of the noisy signal. (C) Waveform pattern from cycle to cycle. (D) Natural features of the frequency. (E) Properties of signal transformation.

+   B. Mel frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCC). Essentially, this is used for the diagnosis of speech deficiency. The key benefits compared to other voice features are that they can fully represent the vocal tract. Once the vocal tract has been precisely established, the phoneme produced by the vocal tract can be precisely determined. The vocal tract is distributed in a short-term continuum envelope, and this envelope is properly represented in the MFCC. The following procedure defines the MFCCs. [87].
