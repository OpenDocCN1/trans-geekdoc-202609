## 13.2 Types of chronic disease

### 13.2.1 Cancer

Human cells normally divide and expand to produce fresh cells which are a requirement of the body. After cells grow and finally become old or damaged, they die and are substituted by fresh cells. In all forms of cancer, any of the body's cells start to split uncontrollably and spread into adjacent tissues. Cancer may begin in almost any part of the body.

Cancer cells versus normal cells: The key difference among normal cells and cancer cells is that cancer cells progressively divide without discontinuing. Normal cells develop to perform special functions while cancer cells do not.

- Cancer classification:
- (a) Common types of cancers: lung, breast, skin, prostate.
- (b) Less common cancers: stomach, brain, ovarian, leukemia.
- (c) Cancers occurring in children: osteosarcoma, neuroblastoma, acute lymphoblastic leukemia, childhood leukemia

Carriers of cancer: The three main types of genes that are affected and contribute to cancer are tumor suppressor genes, DNA repair genes and proto-oncogenes. These variations are sometimes referred to as ‘drivers of cancer’.

Tissue changes are sometimes misinterpreted as cancer: when cells within a tissue divide faster than normal pace then it is termed hyperplasia. This can be caused due to mere chronic irritation. When extra abnormal cells build up in an unusual manner, this is termed dysplasia, a more severe condition than hyperplasia. It has a greater chance of being converted into cancer.

Cancer controlling technique: An AI based PID controller is a tool to treat cancer in a better way. Cancer treatment is usually highly expensive. To curb the expenses and make it available to the everyone, a PID controller using Computed Torque Control (CTC) can be used that gives the best administration procedure for every patient. A feedback linearization-based controller has been created that is founded on the Hahnfeldt model [31]. The Hahnfeldt model uses a control engineering perspective for tumor regulation based on an optimal controller that decreases the dimensions of the tumor in the shortest way and also reduces the amount of medication, thus reducing the cost of treatment. It must be kept in mind that like any other medication, care should be taken to check any unforeseen result. This is a Robust Fixed-Point Transformation (RFPT) based control mechanism which decreases the total inhibitor concentration and at the same time increases the robustness of the controlled system. It is high time we should depart from the traditional model-based cancer chemotherapy practices. We can use a reinforcement learning-based (RL-based) method, a model-free method for the close control of cancer chemotherapy medicine dosing [32].

One of the important aspects of projected RL-based technique is that it requires less computational exertion and is less composite with respect to genetic algorithm-based approaches. In clinical pharmacology, progressive infusion of hormones and medicines can be optimized using RL. The use of RL-based monitoring was evaluated in the first closed-loop clinical trial for regulating propofol infusion in humans. Another RL-based technique is projected for the administration of anesthesia for ICU patients who require critical care. RL-based controllers are also used for the demonstration of robust control of propofol infusion and very good performance.

In [33–36] researchers used optimization technique with cancer chemotherapy control algorithms. In [35], an adaptive parameter controlling model of predictive control (MPC) is used for chemotherapy treatment. A cancer dynamics optical controlling problem as shown in figure 13.1 was investigated in [36]. Chemotherapy treatment can also be monitored and controlled by using genetic, computer controlled and evolutionary based methods [37, 38]. Diagnosis accuracy, speed and clinical managerial can be improved by the integration of AI techniques in cancer for improved health outputs.

### 13.2.2 Blood pressure

The heart pumps blood in arteries during beat time and the pressure experienced by the arteries is measured as BP. Further, blood pressure is of two types: one is systolic which is during beat time, and the other is diastolic which is during rest time between two beats. BP is measured in two numbers which shows systolic and diastolic pressures. The standard way to write the BP is to write systolic first then diastolic, e.g. 110/80 (110 is systolic and 80 is diastolic).

Much of the time high-BP (H-BP) does not show any symptoms. The only way to find the H-BP is with regular monitoring of BP. Commonly, H-BP has two types: primary and secondary. Primary H-BP is a very general type of BP and comes with age, while secondary H-BP occurs due to some other medical issues or medication. One may eliminate it by proper treatment or by stopping the medication that causes it. H-BP affects the heart function and that of other organs like the kidney. If H-BP stays for a long time, it may cause heart and kidney failure. For H-BP cure, it is necessary to change medicines and lifestyle related to the heart.
