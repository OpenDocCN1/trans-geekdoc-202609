# 11.2 Anatomy of the eye

The sight organ of the human body is the eye, which is a slightly symmetrical globe measuring an inch in diameter, approximately 25 mm (figure 11.1). The remarkability of the eye is that it can detect a single light photon. The perception of the colour, depth, motion, distance, speed, lightning etc, makes it the primary sense organ of the human body. When you see yourself in the mirror, the front part of the eye includes:

+   1. Iris: this is the coloured portion;
+   2. Cornea: this is clear domed shaped over iris;
+   3. Pupil: this is circular opening black in colour in the iris for incoming light;
