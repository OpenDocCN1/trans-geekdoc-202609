For controlling and monitoring of biomedical systems, a well-known controller is used widely, i.e. a PID Controller, which performs very well in a variety of biomedical applications, for example arterial blood pressure (ABP), imtube/control, muscular rest in operation, combined slant control in unnaturally activated muscle, kidney transplant, glycemia control, control of unconsciousness etc. The wide-ranging applications of PID control are in industry and in the biomedical system, because it does continuous monitoring which is also essential in these fields. There should be a smart examination lab with smart doctor for respective labs. A lot of research has been done previously for the aforementioned workings [10–13]. Fast treatment and recovery of patients depend on the strong surroundings of the HCU such as drinking water, water used in toilets, nursing bed with commode facility. These constraints can be fulfilled by using an intelligent PID control system with fuzzy based tuning. A PID can be tuned in such a way that it can automatically control the input/output flow of supply water for wash out and clean-up purposes [14].

The Mamdani fuzzy deduction scheme has been investigated to manage a vapor engine and container amalgamation using a linguistic set of laws [15, 16]. Fuzzy based tuning is used to design PID controllers for several applications [17–19]. In [19], a relative comparison displays that fuzzy based tuning is better in comparison to Cohen–Coon, trial-error, and Ziegler–Nichols methods. A coupled tank’s level can also be controlled by using PID [20, 21]. PID control is tempting for calculating blood glucose because it reproduces the reaction of initial and subsequent stages that are applied to conceal insulin through β-cells in the pancreas in contradiction of constantly examined glucose. For a patient’s glucose control and maintenance, a switching technique based PID control algorithm is used [22]. Researchers studied Iletin Lente and internal secretion of a patient and used ‘Meal Simulation model’ and ‘Havorka model’ and show that the PID control technique achieves better results in several cases, for example Iletin Lente sensitivity.

In [23], researchers achieved success in systolic pressure controlling with the help of a nonlinear digital PID controller and by adding sodium nitroprusside into the drug. By this technique, high blood pressure can be controlled during open-heart surgery. Systolic pressure can also be controlled with the use of automatic control strategies [24] like PID control and variation in their parameter, best control, intelligent monitor, rule-based monitor counting fuzzy logic, and artificial neural system monitor. In [25] researchers applied automatically adjusting PID controlling technique for muscular rest in operation. A joint angle regulation scheme is also presented for the unnaturally activated muscle in [26]. A special PID system for a controlling and enhanced PID switching regulator for blood glucose control, a special PID and an advanced PID switching technique are presented in [27, 28], PID technique is also used in liver transplantation, which models vecuronium pharma-co-kinetics and pharma-co-dynamics [29]. Use of anesthesia in children can also be controlled by using a smart PID control technique [30].
