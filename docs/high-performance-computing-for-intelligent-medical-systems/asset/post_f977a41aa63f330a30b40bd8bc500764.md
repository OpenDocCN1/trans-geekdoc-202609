## 13.2.3 Diabetes

Diabetes is the common terminology for its medical name diabetes mellitus (DM). It is a metabolic disorder which affects the insulin production that causes uncontrolled blood glucose (BG) [45, 46]. There are various Types of DM with different signs, and which affect the health undesirably such as: Type 1 DM (T1DM), Type 2 DM (T2DM), Gestational DM (GDM), Double DM, Genetic related DM, and some extra forms of DM also occur by various types of disorder [47]. To regulate the glucose in the blood a system is used called Artificial Pancreas (AP). Insulin delivery in a diabetic body can also be managed by AP such as decreased insulin dose in a hyperglycemia patient and increased insulin dose in hypoglycemia.

T1DM: When the body is not able to make sufficient insulin to control blood sugar levels (BS-L). Insulin dependent and juvenile are other names for T1DM.

T2DM: Insulin independent and adult-onset are the name of T2DM. T2DM patients have the problem of high blood sugar and low insulin in the body, which causes repeated urine, mysterious weight loss and need for liquids.

Diabetes controlling technique or control algorithms for AP: Since DM is such a well-studied field of biomedical engineering, nearly every control method has been recorded. In [48] the researcher proposed an optimal technique to control blood glucose level (BGL) of a diabetic patient. However, without a general solution to the problem, there are highly valuable ways to concentrate upon the standard MPC, fuzzy logic centered, traditional PID control, or vigorous control methods [49–51]. Simply put, every control algorithm takes into account the same principles: the fulfilment of defined quality and quantity properties. PID control was the first attempt in this field, and it is even the best commonly utilized classical monitoring procedure in the collection. However, the fundamental idea of PID control is simple, for the AP concept, advanced solutions such as robust PID [52, 53] or switching PID [54] have been used. In various carbohydrate ingestion and insulin sensitivity situations, the improved PID algorithm may work well. When compared to the standard PID algorithm, control efficiency is significantly improved, and hypoglycemia is avoided. With the use of PID, MPC and Fuzzy Logic a marvelous improvement has been noted of computerization rate of insulin mixture in AP [55, 56].

## 13.2.4 Arthritis

Joint pain is one of the most prevalent problems nowadays. In this there occurs swelling and stiffness in the joints. Osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis are the most common types today. In osteoarthritis, there is breakdown of the tissue, while in rheumatoid arthritis, problem starts with the lining of joints. Arthritis is mostly seen in women and older people. Primary symptoms are problems in the motion of joints which can later lead to inability to do daily life activities. The worse case may be permanent joint changes [57].

- Types of arthritis:
  - (i) Inflammatory arthritis: In this type the immune system attacks joints causing their decay. It may also lead to injury in internal organs. Rheumatoid arthritis is an example of inflammatory arthritis.
  - (ii) Degenerative arthritis: Excessive weight or hereditary traits may cause this type of arthritis. In this, the cushioning soft surface of the bone (cartilage) tears away, joints lose strength and may cause severe pain. Osteoarthritis is its example. Smoking, less frequent physical activity and some genetic factors are some of the possible reasons of osteoarthritis.
  - (iii) Metabolic arthritis: If the uric acid level in the body is not properly maintained by our glands, its excess causes the formation of some crystalline structure in the joints which causes pain. This arthritis may cause permanent disability in some part of the body.

Treatment: regular physical exercises, medicines, balancing of body mass index, the last option is surgery [58].

Arthritis controlling techniques: Proper care and control can avoid the symptoms of arthritis. Involvement of education and self-management by the patient can recover adherence and improve the efficiency of early treatment. The possibilities of involvement of new symptoms can be reduced by the combination of pharmacologic and rehabilitation management. Professional psychotherapy is a health environment that aims to enhance the daily routine activities by the patient. Multi-disciplinary follow up is essential for accurate management of the illness on different characteristics of life [59]. By the use of intelligent controlling techniques, the problem of arthritis may be controlled at some particular level. The problem of transferring secure and sufficient anesthesia during surgery can also be solved by using a strongly tuned PID controller [60].
