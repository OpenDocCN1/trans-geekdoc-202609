# 8.9.10 ‘Deterministically starting-GA’ (GA_Det)

More et al [64] postulated a BMA called ‘Deterministically Starting-GA’ (GA_det) based on the property of adaptive convergence and early deterministic population of GA. This BMA is fruitful for x265 standard type video codec and it adopts the concept of survival of the fittest. The reduction of search points for the best matching MB is the motto of this BMA. Predicted and bi-directional frames are taken to have three reference frames. The early population that is created is taken to be at different spatio-temporal positions in the frames and at pre-specified HEX locations and all are a function of pre-encoded coding units. The newly designed BMA is beneficial for deployment of x256 video codec standard structure. In x265 CODEC’s framework, GA_Det reduces the search point at selected classes of videos which are used for experiment (figure 8.13).
