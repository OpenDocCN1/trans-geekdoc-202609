+   6. Root mean square (RMS): It is defined as squared mean of sub band and can be given as,

RMS = \(\sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N x_i^2}\) (1.11)

+   7. Standard deviation (STD): It is the dispersion of signal with respect to the mean, it can be formulated as,

STD = \(\frac{1}{N-1} \sum_{i=1}^{N-1} (x'_{i+1} - x'_i)^2\) (1.12)

+   8. Log detector (LOG): It provides an estimate of blood force exerted on the walls of arteries, logarithmic change of nonlinear detector is defined as LOG detector. It can be computed as,

LOG = \(e^{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N x_i^2}\) (1.13)

where, \(x_i\) is the \(i\)th subband with \(N\) number of samples.
