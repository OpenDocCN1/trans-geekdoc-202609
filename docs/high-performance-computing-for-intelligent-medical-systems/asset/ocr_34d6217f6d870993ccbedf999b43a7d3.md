# 3.5 Discussion and conclusion

Based on this survey, we can summarize our review as follows.

+   - The unnecessary delay between the data collection and the processing and analysis capacity is often the reason IoT is not fully and efficiently utilized in the healthcare industry.
+   - The big challenge that any patient faces is the unavailability of doctors and care in severe situations, and in particular when living in rural areas. Therefore, a new architecture needs to be established through the use of IoT devices.
+   - There are also cases in which, for example, people with a chronic illness need long-term monitoring. Continual supervision is highly necessary in this respect.
+   - In order to be able to communicate everywhere the IoT healthcare network has to have the potential to facilitate the flow of patients. This will involve an advanced IoT environment that takes into account networking customization without undermining data protection.
+   - Most studies concentrated on voice disease and its results only distinguish between a voice that is normal and a voice that has a pathological disorder where a full IoT system for the diagnosis of a particular disease, such as laryngeal cancer, is required. Since laryngeal cancer is a life-threatening condition, early diagnosis of laryngeal cancer requires a new and successful system / model.
+   - Although ML is being used in medical diagnosis, its design must be sufficiently precise to prevent any mistakes in the classification process. In addition, cybersecurity requirements, as well as legal and privacy challenges must be considered for the development of proven algorithms in order to benefit from the faith of patients and healthcare service providers.
