Patients eager to report more comprehensively about their experience may continue to the advanced section after submitting the first quick-response section (see figure 7.2). Phase 2 contains free space for narrative text (open commentary). Specific aspects related to the doctor’s compassion (e.g., empathy, etc), other questions, and remarks and additional material can be delivered in this item. Phase 3 provides an opportunity for patients to present their views on the model’s usefulness and design (table 7.1, phase 3). Phase 3 is needed for quality improvement and maturation purposes and is thus useful for any kinds of feedback systems (figure 7.3).
