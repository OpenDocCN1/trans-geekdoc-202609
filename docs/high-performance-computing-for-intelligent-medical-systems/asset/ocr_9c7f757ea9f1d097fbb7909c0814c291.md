# A model for advanced patient feedback procedures in diagnostics

Marjo Rissanen, Antti Rissanen and Kalle Saastamoinen

In healthcare, patients’ experience is monitored in many ways. However, accuracy of diagnostics is a common concern of healthcare clients. This chapter proposes a model for improved patient feedback in diagnostics. The proposed model is beneficial for organizations because it offers more precise information on patient opinions. Moreover, it helps healthcare consumers formulate and express their opinions quickly and easily about perceived diagnostic phase in their care. This approach combines clients’ free-text feedback and structured responses in areas of physician competence and compassion in diagnostics. Breakthroughs in biomedical information processing like advanced data, text mining techniques, and machine learning algorithms are at the forefront of success in controlling information overload and text clustering problems. These aids are beneficial in categorizing free-text feedback and binary answers, as well in analysis and in visualization. The proposed model represents intelligent medical management and such computational intelligence which can benefit patients, doctors, and health organizations. The advantage of the model is its economics for patients but also for quality auditors, and data management sites.

# 7.1 Introduction

Diagnostic errors result in higher healthcare costs, unnecessary deaths, and mental suffering [1–3]. Diagnostic errors lead easily to increased numbers of doctor visits, longer hospital stays, or to inappropriate treatments [1, 4]. Evaluative information gained from patient feedback plays an essential role in responding to patient concerns and making necessary changes to diagnostic practices. To improve patient safety related to diagnostics, feedback systems focused on diagnostic evaluation should be clear and efficient.
