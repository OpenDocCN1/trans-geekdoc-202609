# 11.5 Current status of retinal analysis

The last 160 years saw the progression of imaging of the retina, which is now the backbone of clinical care of the eye patient having various retinal and systemic diseases. These techniques are used successfully for the diagnosis of diseases and disorders of high blood sugar, high blood pressure, dementia, atherosclerosis and other related cardiovascular diseases, which are detected by fundus photography. The popularly chosen methods are optical coherence tomography (OCT) and fluorescein angiography. In addition to this, OCT is also used in viore-retinal surgery and its follow up.

# 11.5.1 Fundus imaging

In fundus imaging, there is a two-dimensional image projection of 3D retinal tissue. The intensity of the photo is directly proportional to the quantity of reflected light. The fundus imaging is cheap providing fine observation to the analyser.

The various categories of fundus imaging are fundus photography method, colour fundus photography method, stereo fundus photography method, hyperspectral imaging technique, scanning laser ophthalmoscopy technique, adaptive optics SLO, fluorescein angiography and indocyanine angiography methods.

+   (I) Fundus image analysis for the detection of diabetic retinopathy

The principle behind the detection of diabetic retinopathy from fundus images is based on the micro diagnosis of micro aneurysms, blood leakage and micro blood clots observed on the fundus, which reflects the inner parts of the retina.

+   (II) Image acquisition

This is the first step in the direction of detection of diabetic retinopathy. The acquisition of fundus colour image and its analysis helps us to observe abnormalities of the retina. Actually, in the process of fundus imaging there is projection of 3D half transparent tissues of the retina on the imaging plane. A special fundus camera is use to take the high-resolution image. The various manufacturers of cameras are Topcon, Canon, Kowa etc. The camera can be categorised into two types: mydriatic camera involving dilation of the pupil, and the other category of non-mydriatic camera. There are many databases available having numerous fundus images such as DRIVE, STARE, MESSIDOR, DIARETDB1, ARIA and ROC.

+   (III) Image pre-processing

Due to many limitations, the image quality of the image get deteriorated because of many factors such as low focus, unwanted motion of the patient, non-uniformity of light and improper calibration.
