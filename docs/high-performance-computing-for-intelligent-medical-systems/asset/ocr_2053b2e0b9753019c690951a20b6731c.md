# Harsh Goud

![](bbox=[0.133, 0.333, 0.26, 0.435])

Harsh Goud Department of Electronics, Indian Institute of Information Technology Nagpur, India Dr Harsh Goud, PhD received his BE degree in Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering from Shri Govindram Seksaria Institute of Technology and Science, Indore in 2005, and ME degree in Digital Techniques and Instrumentation from Shri Govindram Seksaria Institute of Technology and Science, Indore in 2008. His PhD is in Electrical Engineering from Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal in 2019. He joined Institute of Engineering and Science IPS Academy Indore as an Assistant Professor in 2010. Since January 2021, he has been with the Faculty of Electronics and Communication Engineering Department, Indian Institute of Information Technology, Nagpur, where he is currently an Assistant Professor. His research interests include instrumentation, control system and robotics. Dr Harsh has authored/co-authored 11 publications in various high impact factor, peer-reviewed, journals. He has presented three papers in international conferences.

# Kapil Gupta

Kapil Gupta PDPM IIITDM Jabalpur, Jabalpur, India Mr Kapil Gupta is a research scholar in Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing Jabalpur, India. He received a BE degree with Honors in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Oriental Institute of Science and Technology, Bhopal, India in 2011, and an MTech degree in Nano Technology, from School of Nano Technology, Rajiv Gandhi Technical University, Bhopal, India in 2013.
