# PSO-BM Algorithm

+   1. Eight particle locations are selected in a different direction and initialized with random velocities.
+   2. Calculate the fitness values, that is, SAD for each particle.
+   3. Personal best ($P_{best}$) and Global best ($G_{best}$) are updated using equation (8.3) by the PSO algorithm based on a particle’s fitness value.
+   
+   $$Y_i(t+1) = \begin{cases} Y_i(t) & \text{if } f(X_i(t+1)) \geqslant f(Y_i(t)) \\ X_i(t+1) & \text{if } f(X_i(t+1)) < f(Y_i(t)) \end{cases}$$ (8.3)
+   
+   $X_i$ is the position vector of the particle ’$i$’ $f(X_i(t+1))$ is the ’$i$’th particle’s fitness value at $X_i(t+1)$ position $Y_i(t)$ gives the current $P_{best}$ of the particle ’$i$’
+   4. Position vector and velocity vectors of the particle are updated using equations (8.1) and (8.2).
+   5. Until the stopping conditions are met, the above steps are followed.
+   6. The matching MB location and its MV will be the $G_{best}$ position.

# 8.9.4 Harmony search block matching algorithm

Geem *et al* [56] had suggested a new population-based optimization approach called Harmony Search (HS) which mimics the process of continuously polishing the pitches for getting better harmony by a musician. Fitness approximation model attached with HS to get the best MV to get better harmony in music. Figure 8.8 is the block level diagram of HS-BM. The time of execution is greatly saved in doing ME accurately because of the integration of the fitness calculation strategy for finding out the MV.

The solution is also known as ‘Harmony’ in HS and is represented by n-dimensional real vectors. Harmony Memory (HM) is the initial memory in HS where harmony vectors are stored which are generated randomly. A new Harmony candidate is produced or created by applying pitch modification strategy or randomized re-initialization. The newly generated Harmony candidate is matched with the poorest harmony and HM is updated. Until the limiting condition is not met, a better candidate harmony vector replaces the poorest harmony vector.
