## Miguel Ángel Mañanas

![](img/75406d5bb714dfd3e68fbbc717bd8f67_25_2.png)

**Miguel Ángel Mañanas** Escola Tècnica Superior d'Enginyeria Industrial de Barcelona (ETSEIB), Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya-Barcelona Tech, Barcelona, Spain

**Miguel Ángel Mañanas**, PhD, received his Telecommunications Engineering degree and PhD in Biomedical Engineering (BME) from Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (UPC) in 1993 and 1999, respectively. He is currently an Associate Professor at the Department of Automatic Control (ESAII) at the same university, member of the Biomedical Engineering Research Center (CREB) at the UPC and member of the CIBER-BBN. He is the founder and leader of the multidisciplinary BIOsignal Analysis for Rehabilitation and Therapy Group (BIOART, UPC) composed of engineers and physicians. His research is focused on applying engineering techniques in health areas. His career has been particularly focused on biomedical signal processing and biological system modeling, in three fields: (1) neuromuscular: evaluating neuromuscular disorders and motor rehabilitation by high-density electromyography; (2) neurological: evaluating brain activity by electroencephalography and magnetoencephalography; (3) respiratory: modeling the respiratory control system and simulating pulmonary diseases and mechanical ventilation. He is the co-author of 65 JCR articles (31 in Q1 and 23 in Q2), has more than 100 peer-reviewed scientific communications, and co-invented two patents. He has co-supervised eight PhD theses. He has also been PI of 30 national/international projects. He received the Leonardo Award from BBVA Foundation as a career recognition in 2016.
