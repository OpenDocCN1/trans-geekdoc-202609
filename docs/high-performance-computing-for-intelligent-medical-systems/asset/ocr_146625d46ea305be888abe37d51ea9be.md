# 2.5 Results and discussions

# 2.5.1 Dataset

In this article, a basic hand movements dataset is utilized. This dataset is publicly available and widely used for hand movement recognition experiments and it can be downloaded from the UCI website [51]. This dataset consists of sEMG signals of five subjects consisting of three females and two males who are 20–22 years old. A two-channel Delsys Bagnoli™ Handheld EMG set was located on the arm. To acquire the sEMG data, six basic hand movements were presented by subjects. The used hand movements are cylindrical (CY), hook (HO), lateral (LA), palmar (PA), spherical (SP), and tip (TI). This dataset contains 180 sEMG signals for each hand and person because each movement was repeated 30 times. The frequency of the sEMG data is 500 Hz and the sEMG signals are stored with 6 s. Therefore, each signal has 3000 values [51].
