Through the work of [86, 87], good image segmentation in lesion-affected hypertension and DR images have been achieved by using features such as Gabor, Binary Hausdorff symmetry measure, and multi-fractal features with the SVM classifier. In the random forest classifiers, visual attention modeling was used to deal with issues of near parallel vessels, CVR, slender vessels having low contrast, lesions and non-uniform illumination [88].

# E. Unsupervised techniques

The main advantage of unsupervised segmentation techniques of blood vessels is that manual annotation is not necessary. The pixel is grouped into a vessel and non-vessel category by means of image features. Vessel segmentation has also utilized the GMM-expectation maximization (EM) technique. The EM algorithm was entrusted by the task to impart either a maximum or most like probability to pixels as belonging to the vessel or non-vessel category [89]. In this technique, the enhancement of blood vessels have been carried out with either top-hat transformation or high-pass filters. Here, optic disc and blood vessels have been processed by using a combination of GMM and gray voting. In another research work [90], the FCM soft clustering approach was implemented with multiple filters such as Frangi’s, Gabor wavelets, and matched filter for eliminating blobs in retinal fundus image, and enhancing thin blood vessels in due course [90]. Natuired inspired algorithms like bee colony optimization have also been used for identifying blood vessel specific cluster, and identifying background by evaluating texture and spatial dependence probabilities [91].

# 12.3 Brain tumour segmentation

Cell differentiation and irregular development in the brain tissue are brain tumours. Brain tumours can be classified according to their origin, as either primary or metastatic brain tumours. The primary cell’s origin is cells with brain tissues, whereas metastatic cancer cells spread to the brain or some other part of the body. Gliomas are a form of glial cell brain tumour. It is the dominant brain tumour type and many of the research areas concentrate on the segmentation of brain tumours.
