# Amit Kumar Verma

![](bbox=[0.133, 0.153, 0.26, 0.253])

Amit Kumar Verma Department of Pharmacy, MJP Rohilkhand University Bareilly, Biharipur, UP, India Amit Kumar Verma (Academician and Researcher, Gold medalist, Shikshak Shree Awardee). A renowned Academician working in the Department of Pharmacy MJP Rohilkhand University, UP Govt State University, District Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh. He was the merit holder in his BPharm. He did his postgraduation

in pharmaceutical chemistry (MPharm) from Manipal College of pharmaceutical sciences (MCOPS) Manipal, Karnataka India. He took PG project training at Central Drug Research Institute (CDRI) Lucknow. The research topic of his PhD was polymer chemistry and drug delivery. He has wide experience of academic, research and administration. He has published 12 research papers in high-impact publications of SCI, Scopus, UGC etc, with 5 books and more than 12 book chapters to his credit. He is the editor of a book series on COVID-19. He is presently working on a research project on COVID-19. His interest areas include application of artificial intelligence in the disease management and herbal formulation development. He has four patent publications and copyrights to his credit. He has supervised 15 postgraduate students. In the interdisciplinary contribution, he is the research supervisor of the dissertation work of MSC microbiology and chemical engineering students of the University. He is a member of Institutional Ethical Committee supervising 600 Colleges and also the science representative member of the intellectual property right IPR cell of the University, covering around 600 Colleges. In an administrative capacity, he is working as Assistant Dean Student Welfare ADSW, Training placement coordinator, career counsellor and mental counsellor to students during COVID-19. Through the use of electronic and print media Doordarshan, Akashwani radio, newspapers etc, he has enriched the knowledge of Indian citizens. Delivering more than 20 expert invited lectures in renowned institutions of the Army, ITBP Police, educational and in villages indicates his capability of percolating knowledge through society. In corporate life he is discharging the duties of advisor (non-remunerative basis) in the Alobhin Herbal Pharmaceutical private limited, his innovation led to the development of product 'Daisy care' which is available in the commercial market, now working as Brand Ambassador of innovation cell, Ministry of Education. His social contribution creation of ZERO TB Digital platform, an innovative initiative enabling digital caring of tuberculosis patients to help achieve the goal of a TB-free India, is the first in the world. He also runs a self-financed malaria and COVID-19 eradication awareness program in District Bareilly.
