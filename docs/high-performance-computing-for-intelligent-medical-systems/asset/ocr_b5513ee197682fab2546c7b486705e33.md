# 8.6 Standard techniques for motion estimation

BMAs are often divided into three groups. Pattern-Based BMAs (PBBMA) are the first group because they are based on various search point patterns such as rectangular-shaped, square-shaped, hexagon, or diamond-shaped, etc Three-Step Search (TSS) [2], New TSS (NTSS) [3], Quick TSS [4], Reduced Three-Step Logarithmic Search (RTSLS) [5], 4-Step Search (4 s) [6, 7], Diamond Search (DS) [8, 9], Cross Diamond Search (CDS) [10], New CDS (NCDS) [10], Hexagon shaped BMA (HEXBMA) [11], Simple and Effective Search (SES) [12], etc. The Second Category considers and uses the similarity among the current MB and the adjacent MBs to estimate current the MB’s movement, for example, Adaptive Rood Pattern Search (ARPS) [12–14], Inter-block correlation-based BMA [15], Correlation Search Algorithm (CSA) [16], Correlation Based Rood Pattern Search (CBRPS) [17], etc. The third group consists of all BMAs that are based on the concept and behavior of natural genetics or natural intelligence events which are called soft computing based BMAs. These are also referred to as BMAs inspired by Nature, such as the ME using Genetic Algorithm [18–27], Particle Swarm Optimization based BMAs [28–38], Artificial Bee Colony (ABC-BM) [39] based BMA, etc.

# 8.7 Soft computing techniques for motion estimation

The practice of achieving a better solution, that is, better than the current best solution of a problem is known as optimization and is the method followed to make the algorithm operate as meritoriously or as efficiently as possible. In recent times, Nature-inspired algorithms (NIAs), evolutionary algorithms, swarm intelligence, SC techniques, etc have become buzzwords in the field of optimization. Many computer science field problems together with other engineering disciplines are using SC techniques for finding optimal solutions for real-life problems. The optimization approach adopted by them to implement the concept and process of design to solve everyday problems motivated the researchers to explore the field further. Out of all biological or Nature-mimicked algorithms, Swarm Intelligence Algorithms (SIAs) [40] have the highest acceptance in the field of optimization. Besides being a non-deterministic algorithm, an algorithm for stochastic optimization may be heuristic or meta-heuristic type by nature.
