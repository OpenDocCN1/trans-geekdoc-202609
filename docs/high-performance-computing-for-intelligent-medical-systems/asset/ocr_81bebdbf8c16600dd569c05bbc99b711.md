Another MS lesion identification technique for FLAIR brain MRI image that uses a combination of adaptive mixture model (AMM) and MRF has been proposed in [111]. In a unique approach, the content-based active contour model (CBAC) was used to initially demarcate tumour afflicted regions, after which a group of different feature descriptors were used for extracting features. The feature descriptors employed in their work are—directional Gabor, Laplacian of Gaussian (LoG), circular Gabor, rotation-invariant local binary patterns (RILBPs), GLCM and pixel intensity features. The final stage consisted of the PCA algorithm to reduce the feature vectors, after which the ANN was employed for training [112]. Brain tumour in MRI images has also been segmented by using kNN and CRF in [113]. The investigation was carried out using 40 MRI images, out of which 30 and 10 samples were derived from individuals having HG and LG tumours, respectively. Researchers of [114] used hard k-means with FCM clustering techniques initially, after which the level was set and adaptive thresholding techniques were applied. Before this final segmentation, the brain MRI images were filtered by median filter and brain surface extractor algorithm. Their experimentation on three data sets yielded accuracies of 90.5%, 100%, and 100% having 22, 152 and 81 images, respectively. In another research work, the GMM algorithm was used for extracting rich features from different tumour tissues, which were then combined with random forest and MRF for segmenting brain tumour images. They used a total of 520 brain images derived using multiple modalities—T1, T1C, FLAIR, and T2 [115]. The authors of [116] extracted features from T1 and FLAIR MRI brain images by using 80 different Histogram of Oriented Gradient (HOG) features, 20 GLRLM, 256 IBP, and 112 GLCM features. It resulted in the creation of a feature vector having 475 diverse descriptors. It was reduced to 20 dimensioned feature vectors by applying PCA. A hybrid technique that uses FCM clustering and Hopfield Neural Network has been investigated on the PDC and IBSR databases for image segmentation and visualisation capability [117]. Before training, they performed skull stripping by using morphological operation. The technique by [118] used global thresholding, morphological operation and watershed segmentation on brain MRI images. Another research work [119] also implemented Otsu’s thresholding, hard k-means, and soft clustering for segmenting brain tumour images. An overall accuracy of 90.57% was attained.

The deep learning model (DL) of [120] uses 2D patches having different dimensions as inputs for two separate pathway CNN-layer stacks for performing convolution. The features liberated from the two CNNs were concatenated together. In a similar, yet improved version, the researchers of [121] investigated a two-pathway DL technique by having 3D patches as input. Their network used residual connection and produced multiple voxels as output. Another DL model that is used widely is the U-Net [122], which uses encoder-decoder model.
