## 11.7.2 Dementia detection using retinal imaging

The basic principle behind the detection of dementia is that the neuronal retinal cells share similar properties to those of the central nervous system neurons, similarly that arterioles venules of retina pattern are the same as cerebral vessels of blood, so it is considered that degradative changes in retinal features can be extrapolated for neuro-degenerative changes and these degenerative changes are an early indicator of dementia.

## 11.7.3 Heart diseases detection using retinal imaging

The fundamental concept that lies in the detection of heart diseases is analysis of microvasculature and the appropriate vascular design. For maintaining the proper and healthy functioning of the heart correct vascular design is very important for proper flow of blood and its efficient diffusion. On the other hand, if there is a problem in the geometry of microvasculature then it gets mirrored in the retinal vasculature, which is the hallmark for the early detection of cardiovascular diseases. It is observed that in hypertensive condition there is thinning of the arterioles of the retina, similarly the parameter of arteriolar tortuosity is a marker of blood pressure so increased pressure of the blood causes increase in the tortuosity of anatomical features of the retina. Stiffness of the arterioles is another parameter to define the normal functioning of the CVS System. QUARTZ-Quantitative Analysis of Retinal Vessel Topology and Size is the automatic software for performing the retinal imaging.

## 11.7.4 Choroidal melanoma detection using retinal imaging

This melanoma of the eye can be detected on recognising its characteristic feature of orange coloured pigment on the surface of the tumour; the tumour is touched within the optic disc up to three millimetres.

## 11.7.5 Advantages of retinal imaging

- 1. Retina image template file size is small, therefore, the time consumed in the process is very small and can be saved for future use.
- 2. It is a painless and safe technique.
- 3. It is superior to overdilation because overdilation allows the doctor to see only 16% of the eye, while in retinal imaging this percentage is 80%.
- 4. There is no temporary light sensitivity or blurring of eye vision.

## 11.8 Comparative analysis between various retinal imaging methods

In the field of ophthalmology, eye examination was initially done with indirect operating ophthalmoscopy, direct operating ophthalmoscopy, then by slit lamps involving various lenses, through angiography technique of fluorescein, photography of fundus, 3D photography, laser techniques and OCT to novel techniques of multimodal imaging. Photography of the retina based on film is now no longer in use so we can say that the conventional system has been overtaken by a digital system. In telemedicine mode various cameras of different resolution are used, many non-mydriatic cameras are now used. The assessment of high blood pressure, various types of cancers and bacterial infection can be done with colour fundus photography of the retina, providing the correct scenario of health of eye.

The technique of fundus autofluorescence is useful for detecting the physiological changes that happened in the retina during autofluorescence. Angiography technique is now commonly used where there is I.V. injection of dye which is fluorescent in Nature. OCT due to its high resolution and low coherence is widely used for obtaining the cross-sectional ocular tissues photos. Fast scanning and repeatability of calculations makes it a front runner of the race. OCT angiography is the novel technique of eye retinal imaging based on motion contrast, which does not require dye injection and is much preferred. Many research projects are being started using the novel concepts of multimodal imaging providing high speed and fine resolution imaging with increased illumination penetration.

## 11.9 Conclusion

Retina imaging helps in the diagnosis of proper eye disease along with complication of diabetes retinopathy, hypertension, myocardial infarction etc. Retina analysis is a non-intrusive diagnosis technology. Through the use of image processing techniques, the various eye diseases can be detected at a very early age of their initiation. Rapid development in techniques of retinal imaging focus on enhanced resolution, non-invasiveness and rapid speed.

There will be efforts to establish the relationship between retina and metabolic-cellular level through fluorescence lifetime imaging technology. It is predicted that the image acquisition and subsequent image analysis works to establish the integrity of analysis, some imaging concepts or techniques are still in the research and developmental phase which may provide in-depth knowledge about the various pathological processes. The modification of images for better understanding has changed the concepts of image modalities, novel OCT platforms are front runners for better judging of the retina, similarly, further advances in laser and optical technologies are providing wings to the future of telemedicine.

Within the next decade, the retinal imaging techniques utilization will creep up beyond the direct requirements of eye disease management. The advancement in the retinal examination helps in the assessment of various systemic disease both for patient health care and for population case studies. In future, diseases like multiple sclerosis, Huntington’s disease, psychiatric diseases and neuropathies can be soft targets of retinal eye imaging.
