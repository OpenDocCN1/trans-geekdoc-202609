# Chapter 1

# Automatic detection of hypertension by flexible analytic wavelet transform using electrocardiogram signals

Kapil Gupta, Smith K Khare, Varun Bajaj and Irshad Ahmed Ansari

Hypertension (HPT) is a critical health problem that comes from the higher blood pressure force on the walls of arteries. HPT occurs when systolic blood pressure (SBP) and diastolic blood pressure (DBP) are more than 140 mmHg and 90 mmHg, respectively. It becomes more severe when SBP is greater than 180 mmHg and DBP is more than 120 mmHg. HPT has no direct symptoms but continuous higher blood pressure can urge heart muscles enlargement which will be reflected in the electrocardiography (ECG) report. Due to the non-stationary nature of ECG signals it is difficult to detect HPT manually. Hence, an automatic detection of HPT is proposed using flexible analytic wavelet transform (FAWT) in this chapter. The input signal is decomposed into sub-bands using FAWT and features are extracted from these sub-bands. A set of highly discriminatory features are identified using the Kruskal–Wallis test. These features are fed into the input of different classifiers to classify the HPT and normal ECG signals. The effectiveness of the proposed system is checked by applying tenfold cross-validation and obtained specificity, sensitivity, accuracy, negative predicted value, false positive rate, and false negative rate of 96.74%, 96.95%, 96.60%, 99.69%, 3.04%, and 3.24%, respectively, using ensemble bagged tree classifier.

# 1.1 Introduction

Hypertension (HPT) is defined as higher pressure of blood, and it is a severe health condition that comes from the large blood pressure force acting on the walls of the arteries [1]. It creates higher blood pressure around the arteries, as the heart works hard to pump oxygenated blood through the rest of the body [1]. According to statistics of the World Health Organization, HPT is the third most common cause of global fatality and responsible for more than eleven million deaths annually [2]. HPT, if not treated in a timely manner may induce various diseases related to kidney, brain and heart [3, 4].
