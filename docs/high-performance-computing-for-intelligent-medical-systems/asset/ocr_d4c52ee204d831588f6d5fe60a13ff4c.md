# Dhyan Chandra Yadav

![](bbox=[0.13, 0.15, 0.26, 0.25])

Dhyan Chandra Yadav Department of Computer Applications, VBS Purvanchal University, Jaunpur, India Dr Dhyan Chandra Yadav, Post-Doc., received his MCA from Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University, Jaunpur in 2008, and his PhD in Computer Applications from SV University Gajraulla, Amroha JapiNagar, U.P. in 2016. He is currently employed as a Post-Doctoral Fellow at Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University, Jaunpur. Pattern recognition, statistical image processing, and knowledge discovery are among his research interests. Dr Yadav has authored/co-authored 25 publications in peer-reviewed journals with high impact factors. Dr Yadav has published two book chapters and has given four presentations at international conferences. Dr Dhyan Chandra Yadav has served as a conference reviewer and a journal reviewer.

# Ram Narayan Yadav

![](bbox=[0.13, 0.43, 0.26, 0.53])

Ram Narayan Yadav Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal, India Ram Narayan Yadav received the BTech degree in Electronics from Motilal Nehru National Institute of Technology, Allahabad, India in 1993, MTech degree in Digital Communications from Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology Bhopal, India in 1997 and PhD in Electrical Engineering from Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur, India in 2005. He joined the department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology Bhopal, India in 1997 where he is currently a professor. He has published more than 70 papers in various international journal and conference including journals of IEEE, IET, Elsevier and Springer. He has supervised 25 masters and 10 doctoral students. He is a reviewer of several IEEE and Elsevier journals. His current research interests are communication systems, image processing, neural networks and applications.
