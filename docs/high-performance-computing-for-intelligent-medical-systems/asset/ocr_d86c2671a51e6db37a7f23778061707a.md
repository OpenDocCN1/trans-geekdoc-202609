Panahi et al [54] created Fast COVID-19 Detector (FCOD) for classifying x-ray images of COVID from non-COVID subjects. They used an open-source dataset from Github (https://github.com/ieee8023/covid-chestxray-dataset) and then selected 940 x-ray images of COVID-19 and 940 x-ray images of non-COVID-19. They separated the dataset into two parts: 80% for training and 20% for test. Data augmentation was used, and the size of the training set was then increased to 3760 images. FCOD consisted of several depthwise separatable convolutional layers. Following each layer, a max-pooling layer and a batch normalization layer were used, and a dropout layer was used at the end. They achieved 93% sensitivity, 97% specificity, 97% precision, 96% accuracy, and 96% F1-score. They did not follow any specific protocol, and CI was not reported.
