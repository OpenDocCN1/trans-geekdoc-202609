## Chapter 13

### Analysis of AI based PID controller for health care system

Vandana Dubey, Harsh Goud and Prakash Chandra Sharma

Ambiguity establishes one of the most difficult features in biomedical and control systems which aim to capture the physiology of a patient using mathematical modeling. In this chapter, we will elaborate a review of biomedical modeling and controller applied in a patient with chronic disease. A chronic disease is one that generally persists for three calendar months or longer and can become poorer with time. Some of the well-known chronic illnesses are cancer, heart disease, stroke, diabetes, and arthritis. Even so, one of the key conflicts to be taken into description is the huge intra-patient inconsistency, acknowledged using wide-reaching sensitivity examination using a conventional PID (Proportional Integral Derivative) controller of the biomedical system. Nowadays stakeholders of the health sector are putting emphasis on the point that Artificial Intelligence (AI) is going to gain greater importance in handling back-office activities in almost all the biomedical areas. To enhance the accuracy and stability of a biomedical system, the name of PID controller comes at the top of the list. In this chapter, we will discuss an AI based PID controller with various biomedical systems and also analyze how this study may be useful to enhance the stable state along with transient outcome of the biomedical system. A comparative study of all the primitive and novel control strategies for AI based PID controllers regarding the health care system will be address in this chapter. This study concludes with the superiority of the controller which copes with biomedical instruments effectively.

### 13.1 Introduction

Let us recall a famous quote: ‘Prevention is better than cure’. This quote aptly applies to the transformation of the health care system in the United States (US). Earlier the focus was on treating the illness when it arose, rather than taking prior actions to prevent it. There are multiple changes in the processes which aim for the betterment of overall health of the patient. When compared to the health expenses of other countries, expenditure on health is persistently rising for the residents of the US [1–5]. Now the time has come to make a drastic change in this health system and use AI for betterment. AI will help in the automation and proper information synthesis of the data of the patients and their family members. One thing must be kept in mind is that there should not be any misuse of data collected. In accordance with Oxford English Dictionary, AI is the ability of computers or additional devices to display or simulate intellectual performance. In accordance with Merriam-Webster dictionary, AI is a branch of computer science dealing with the simulation of intellectual human actions in computers. Now the way we define AI has been changed from a collection of human-like abilities of computers to something big [6].

Diabetes, high blood pressure, cancer etc are some of the most common diseases today. These are also called non-communicable diseases (NCDs). NCDs are chronic in nature and are dependent on genetic, physiological and environmental factors. Today about 41 million people die every year due to NCDs and this figure is 71% of the total number of deaths every year. The World Health Organization (WHO) says this figure will rise to 55 million by 2030, if timely prompt actions are not taken. According to a recent report of WHO, one in four Indians has a risk of dying from an NCD before they reach the age of 70. The Indian Government, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare says that the NCDs' burden has increased from 30% in 1990 to 55% in 2016 and also the proportion of deaths has also increased from 37% to 61% [7].

An illness or state which generally survives for three months or longer and can grow worse by time is called a chronic disease (CD). CD tends to happen in older adults and may generally be handled but not cured. The foremost common categories of CD are cancer, heart disease, stroke, diabetes, and arthritis. The US spends about $5.3 trillion every year on its health care. Some of the common causes of CD are: tobacco use, nutrition deficiency, physical inactivity and alcohol addiction [8]. Extra care of the patient is essential for a healthcare center in today's lifestyle. Most of the electronic-healthcare systems have developed very well, however, some advanced research is required for the successful outcome from the entire health care unit (HCU). To know about a patient's history, present state of health etc, all are the part of an HCU. Nowadays, various controllers are presented and executed successfully. But, in the computer-controlled age, there is a chance to employ a novel controlling technique, which can comprise a large amount of information, beyond that of an ordinary controller. To fulfill the requirement of this computer controller era, there is a need to design and analyze a smart controller—a classical controller, with the help of AI techniques like genetic, neural networks and fuzzy etc, except with non-classical designing techniques, to design its parameters. The idea of the intelligent control scheme is unwrapped and simply designable. The effect of a conventional PID controller is not attractive for the requirements of complex and non-linear systems [9]. However, better results can be achieved by using smart PID controllers instead of conventional PID controllers.
