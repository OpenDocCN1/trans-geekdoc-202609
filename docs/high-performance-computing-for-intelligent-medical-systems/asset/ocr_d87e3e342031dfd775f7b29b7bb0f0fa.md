# 11.7.1 Glaucoma detection using retinal imaging

The thickness of the retinal nerve fiber layer (RNFL) [31] is the major hallmark for the diagnosis of this disease. In the case of glaucoma, fundus imaging is one of the most reliable techniques and photos can be easily accessed. After the analysis is done the results obtained can be interpreted to detect the disease [32–34].

The process of glaucoma diagnosis is depicted in the diagram in figure 11.2 hence the sequence involved in the process is as follows:

+   (i) There is observation and analysis of the provided image;
+   (ii) Pre-processing of analysed entity mainly depends on sampling process, region of interest, extraction and selection of channel;
+   (iii) Segmentation of provided photo image;
+   (iv) There is reorganization of analysis pattern by applying the technique of segmentation;
+   (v) There is the extraction of characters and then their selection;
+   (vi) Now the role of the classifiers comes into the play.
