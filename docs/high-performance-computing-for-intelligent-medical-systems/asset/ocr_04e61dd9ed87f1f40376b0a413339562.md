# 11.7.5 Advantages of retinal imaging

+   1. Retina image template file size is small, therefore, the time consumed in the process is very small and can be saved for future use.
+   2. It is a painless and safe technique.
+   3. It is superior to overdilation because overdilation allows the doctor to see only 16% of the eye, while in retinal imaging this percentage is 80%.
+   4. There is no temporary light sensitivity or blurring of eye vision.

# 11.8 Comparative analysis between various retinal imaging methods

In the field of ophthalmology, eye examination was initially done with indirect operating ophthalmoscopy, direct operating ophthalmoscopy, then by slit lamps involving various lenses, through angiography technique of fluorescein, photography of fundus, 3D photography, laser techniques and OCT to novel techniques of multimodal imaging. Photography of the retina based on film is now no longer in use so we can say that the conventional system has been overtaken by a digital system. In telemedicine mode various cameras of different resolution are used, many non-mydriatic cameras are now used. The assessment of high blood pressure, various types of cancers and bacterial infection can be done with colour fundus photography of the retina, providing the correct scenario of health of eye.

The technique of fundus autofluorescence is useful for detecting the physiological changes that happened in the retina during autofluorescence. Angiography technique is now commonly used where there is I.V. injection of dye which is fluorescent in Nature. OCT due to its high resolution and low coherence is widely used for obtaining the cross-sectional ocular tissues photos. Fast scanning and repeatability of calculations makes it a front runner of the race. OCT angiography is the novel technique of eye retinal imaging based on motion contrast, which does not require dye injection and is much preferred. Many research projects are being started using the novel concepts of multimodal imaging providing high speed and fine resolution imaging with increased illumination penetration.

# 11.9 Conclusion

Retina imaging helps in the diagnosis of proper eye disease along with complication of diabetes retinopathy, hypertension, myocardial infarction etc. Retina analysis is a non-intrusive diagnosis technology. Through the use of image processing techniques, the various eye diseases can be detected at a very early age of their initiation. Rapid development in techniques of retinal imaging focus on enhanced resolution, non-invasiveness and rapid speed.
