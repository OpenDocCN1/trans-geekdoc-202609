# Chapter 13

# Analysis of AI based PID controller for health care system

Vandana Dubey, Harsh Goud and Prakash Chandra Sharma

Ambiguity establishes one of the most difficult features in biomedical and control systems which aim to capture the physiology of a patient using mathematical modeling. In this chapter, we will elaborate a review of biomedical modeling and controller applied in a patient with chronic disease. A chronic disease is one that generally persists for three calendar months or longer and can become poorer with time. Some of the well-known chronic illnesses are cancer, heart disease, stroke, diabetes, and arthritis. Even so, one of the key conflicts to be taken into description is the huge intra-patient inconsistency, acknowledged using wide-reaching sensitivity examination using a conventional PID (Proportional Integral Derivative) controller of the biomedical system. Nowadays stakeholders of the health sector are putting emphasis on the point that Artificial Intelligence (AI) is going to gain greater importance in handling back-office activities in almost all the biomedical areas. To enhance the accuracy and stability of a biomedical system, the name of PID controller comes at the top of the list. In this chapter, we will discuss an AI based PID controller with various biomedical systems and also analyze how this study may be useful to enhance the stable state along with transient outcome of the biomedical system. A comparative study of all the primitive and novel control strategies for AI based PID controllers regarding the health care system will be address in this chapter. This study concludes with the superiority of the controller which copes with biomedical instruments effectively.
