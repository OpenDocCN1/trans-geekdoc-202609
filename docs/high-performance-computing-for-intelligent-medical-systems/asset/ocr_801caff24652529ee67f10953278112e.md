# Kishorjit Nongmeikapam

![](bbox=[0.133, 0.15, 0.25, 0.25])

**Kishorjit Nongmeikapam** Department of CSE, Indian Institute of Technology, Manipur, India **Kishorjit Nongmeikapam**, PhD received his BE degree in Computer Science and Engineering from PSG College of Technology, Coimbatore, India in 2003. He completed his ME and PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from Jadavpur University, Kolkata, India in 2010 and 2016, respectively. He worked as a faculty member in Manipur University for 13 years from 2003, and has also currently been a faculty member in Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Manipur since 2016. His research interests are in the area of image processing, computer vision, pattern recognition, natural language processing, and programming. He has published over 40 scientific papers in many research journals, international and national conferences of repute.

# Saurabh Pal

![](bbox=[0.133, 0.43, 0.25, 0.53])

**Saurabh Pal** Department of Computer Applications, VBS Purvanchal University, Jaunpur, India **Dr Saurabh Pal** is Head of the Department of Computer Applications at Jaunpur’s Veer Bahadur Singh Purvanchal University. Machine learning, pattern recognition, software engineering, cloud computing, and other topics are among his research interests. Dr Pal has authored or co-authored 80 publications in peer-reviewed journals with high impact factors. Dr Pal has 25 book chapters to his credit and has given about 30 papers at international conferences. Dr Saurabh Pal has been a reviewer for a number of conferences and journals.

# Antti Rissanen

**Antti Rissanen** Department of Military Technology, National Defence University, Helsinki, Finland **Dr Antti Rissanen**, PhD received his BSc, MA and PhD degrees in Physics from Helsinki University, Finland in 1981, 1984 and 2011, respectively. He obtained licentiate in technology degree (electric engineering) in 1992 in University of Technology in Tampere. In 1997 he joined the National Defence University (Finland) as a Senior Lecturer for General Technology where he currently works as a researcher. He is also the leader of the Educational Research Group at the Military Technology Department.
