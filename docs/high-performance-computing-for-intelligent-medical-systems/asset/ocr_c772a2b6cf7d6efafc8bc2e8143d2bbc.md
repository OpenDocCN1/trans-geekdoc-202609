A novel corona virus (nCoV) or COVID-19 is a new chain of virus previously unidentified in humans.

'Middle East Respiratory Syndrome' was determined to have been transmitted from dromedary camels to humans and Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome from civet cats to humans. These two syndromes were determined by various experiments but the novel viruses are not easily identified.

In general, clinically Corona viruses present some symptoms in the infected person but some will not display symptoms. Some symptoms may include:

+   - respiratory symptoms;
+   - feeling high fever;
+   - feeling hard or dry cough;
+   - trouble with breathing;
+   - obesity;
+   - liver damage;
+   - complexity in chest function.

The World Health Organization (WHO) provide advice or suggestions to people that are at a higher risk of getting severe COVID-19 disease. This includes people who are suffering in old age with serious chronic illnesses such as:

+   - diabetes;
+   - cardiovascular disease;
+   - chronic respiratory disease;
+   - cancer;
+   - hypertension;
+   - chronic liver disease.

The WHO makes suggestions for social care and health protection if any signs and symptoms of COVID-19 illness appear in a healthcare facility.

The WHO suggests preventative measures to protect against the new corona virus such as:

+   - being up to date on the latest information with COVID-19;
+   - frequent hand hygiene;
+   - avoiding touching your body parts;
+   - use of a medical mask for hands and face;
+   - keeping social distance from individuals with respiratory symptoms.

They advise people not to avoid but to seek medical care if any illness symptoms appears such as: fever, cough and difficulty breathing.
