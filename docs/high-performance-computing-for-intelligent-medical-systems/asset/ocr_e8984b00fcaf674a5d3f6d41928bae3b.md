+   * The next challenge for scientists is the extraction of features from the samples. The survey indicates that most of the researchers are more optimistic in using the frequency domain functions, although few researchers focus on time-domain measures for a particular pathology. There are also no unusual acoustic characteristics. It is a long calculation, though, and may be vulnerable to the patient’s anatomy. Also, standard practice as can be seen in the survey, is the multiple feature analysis. The survey also revealed that the CNN-based time–frequency scalogram of voice pathology detection is a very new technique. However, 2D CNN image-based techniques such as GAN can further develop the model to expand the dataset. The scope for further reducing deep learning time can also be provided by using modeling to fuse multiple time–frequency scalograms, like Morse, Bump and Amor into a single scalogram.
