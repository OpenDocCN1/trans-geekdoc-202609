# ## Hussain Ahmed Choudhury

![](bbox=[0.13, 0.6, 0.26, 0.71])

**Hussain Ahmed Choudhury** CVR College of Engineering, Ibrahimpattanam, Hyderabad, India

**Dr Hussain Ahmed Choudhury**, PhD received his BTech degree from Assam University silchar, MTech from NERIST-AR and PhD degrees in Computer Science and Engineering from National Institute of Technology, Silchar, Assam, India in 2010, 2014 and 2020, respectively. He joined Jain University Bangalore as Assistant Professor in CSE-Specialization from 2018 to 2020. Since December 2021, he has been working as an Associate Professor with the Department of Computer Science-IT department of CVR College of Engineering, Hyderabad under JNTU, India. Dr Hussain has authored/co-authored 16 publications in various high impact factor, peer-reviewed, journals. He has written one book chapter and he presented about six papers in international conferences. His area of research includes image processing, data visualization, computer networks and soft computing techniques.
