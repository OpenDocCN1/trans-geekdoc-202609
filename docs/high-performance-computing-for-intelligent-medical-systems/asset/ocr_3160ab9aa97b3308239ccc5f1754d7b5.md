# Prakash Chandra Sharma

![](bbox=[0.135, 0.15, 0.255, 0.25])

**Prakash Chandra Sharma** Manipal University Jaipur, Santoshi Nagar, Khamatarai, Raipur, India **Dr Prakash Chandra Sharma** is currently working as Senior Assistant Professor in School of Computing & Information Technology at Manipal University Jaipur, India. He has received his honored degree of PhD from Indian Institute of Technology Indore in discipline of Computer Science & Engineering. He completed his Master of Engineering from SGSITS Indore. He has teaching and research experience of 16 years in various areas of Computer Science & Engineering. His research interest includes graph theory, theoretical computer science, artificial intelligence, image processing and wireless network. He has published a number of research papers in various international/national journals (including IEEE, Springer, Elsevier etc.) and in proceedings of reputed international/national conferences as well. He has also published number of book chapters in various edited books with reputed publishers like Elsevier, Springer, CRC Press etc. He is an active reviewer and editorial member of various reputed international journals in his research areas. He has filed and published 10 copyrights and patents. He has chaired many technical sessions in international conferences. He is a member of various professional international societies (including IEEE, CSI, IAENG, Institute of Engineers India). He is a recipient of many awards and recognitions at national level.

# Mehdi Shirzadi

**Mehdi Shirzadi** Escola Tècnica Superior d'Enginyeria Industrial de Barcelona (ETSEIB), Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya-Barcelona Tech (UPC), Barcelona, Spain **Mehdi Shirzadi** received BS and MS degrees in Biomedical Engineering from Islamic Azad University of Najaf Abad, Najaf Abad, Isfahan, Iran and the University of Isfahan, Isfahan, Iran, in 2015 and 2018, respectively (raked in the top two students). He is a PhD candidate of Biomedical Engineering at the Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya-Barcelona Tech (UPC), Barcelona, Spain. He won a four-year scholarship from the Ministry of Economy and Competitiveness (MINECO) in Spain in 2019. His research interests include electromyographic signal processing, optimization, medical data mining, and machine learning.
