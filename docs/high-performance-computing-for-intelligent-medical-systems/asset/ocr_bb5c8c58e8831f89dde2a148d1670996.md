# 8.4.2 Block matching concept

The concept behind the BM is to partition the images into a non-overlapping block called macro-blocks (MBs) of pixels assuming that all pixels within the MB undergo the same amount of displacement and to perform ME efficiently by finding out MV, that is, coordinate difference between the anchor MB and the most similar MB in the target frame based on certain matching meanings. The goal of all BMAs is to evaluate the matching meanings within a predefined search range (denoted by ‘p’) to determine the most correlated MB in the target frame, and the efficiency, speed, and accuracy of BMAs are vital for faster and accurate video compression.
