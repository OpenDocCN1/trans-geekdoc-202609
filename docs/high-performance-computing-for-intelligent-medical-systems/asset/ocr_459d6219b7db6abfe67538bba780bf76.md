# 8.3.6 Types of coding and frames

When the videos are taken and stored for processing, the frames must be encoded using some reference frames, either past or future frames. But as we know when we capture the first frame, there is no past frame nor future frame to refer to. In such cases, such images/frames are treated as still images and only spatial redundancy will exist which will be directly encoded by a process called Intra-frame coding and the frame is called I-frame. Temporal redundancies will not exist in such cases. Here the information is high and the bit requirement will be high. There is another way to encode a frame that uses the previous or coming frame as a reference called the predictive frame (P-frame), and the process is called inter-frame coding or predictive coding. The frames for which both the past frames and the future frames are used as a reference for coding them are known as B-frame and the process is called Bi-Directional prediction, which is also a part of inter-frame prediction.
