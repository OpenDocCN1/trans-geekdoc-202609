# Lalita Gupta

![](bbox=[0.13, 0.145, 0.26, 0.245])

**Lalita Gupta** Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal, India

**Lalita Gupta** is Associate Professor at the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal, India. She received her BE degree in Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering from Pt. Ravi Shankar Shukla University Raipur in 2003, MTech degree in Digital Communication from Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal, India in 2007 and PhD in Electronics and Communications Engineering from Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal, India in 2012. Since July 2004 she has been associated as a faculty member with the Maulana Azad National Institute of Technology, Bhopal. She is a member of IEEE, IETE, ICEIT. Dr Gupta has 30 research publications in various international journals and conferences of repute. Her research interests are signal and image processing.
