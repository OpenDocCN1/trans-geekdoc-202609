# 13.2.3 Diabetes

Diabetes is the common terminology for its medical name diabetes mellitus (DM). It is a metabolic disorder which affects the insulin production that causes uncontrolled blood glucose (BG) [45, 46]. There are various Types of DM with different signs, and which affect the health undesirably such as: T(Type)1DM, T(Type)2DM, G(Gestational)DM, D(Double)DM, Gr (Genetic related) DM, and some extra forms of DM also occur by various types of disorder [47]. To regulate the glucose in the blood a system is used called Artificial Pancreas (AP). Insulin delivery in a diabetic body can also be managed by AP such as decreased insulin dose in a hyperglycemia patient and increased insulin dose in hypoglycemia.
T1DM: When the body is not able to make sufficient insulin to control blood sugar levels (BS-L). Insulin dependent and juvenile are other names for T1DM.
T2DM: Insulin independent and adult-onset are the name of T2DM. T2DM patients have the problem of high blood sugar and low insulin in the body, which causes repeated urine, mysterious weight loss and need for liquids.
Diabetes controlling technique or control algorithms for AP: Since DM is such a well-studied field of biomedical engineering, nearly every control method has been recorded. In [48] the researcher proposed an optimal technique to control blood glucose level (BGL) of a diabetic patient. However, without a general solution to the problem, there are highly valuable ways to concentrate upon the standard MPC, fuzzy logic centered, traditional PID control, or vigorous control methods [49–51]. Simply put, every control algorithm takes into account the same principles: the fulfilment of defined quality and quantity properties.
