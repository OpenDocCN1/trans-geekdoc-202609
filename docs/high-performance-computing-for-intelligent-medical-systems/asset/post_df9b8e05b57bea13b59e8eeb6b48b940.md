### Hussain Ahmed Choudhury

![](img/75406d5bb714dfd3e68fbbc717bd8f67_21_2.png)

**Hussain Ahmed Choudhury** CVR College of Engineering, Ibrahimpattanam, Hyderabad, India

**Dr Hussain Ahmed Choudhury**, PhD received his BTech degree from Assam University Silchar, MTech from NERIST-AR and PhD degrees in Computer Science and Engineering from National Institute of Technology, Silchar, Assam, India in 2010, 2014 and 2020, respectively. Dr Hussain has authored/co-authored 16 publications in various high impact factor, peer-reviewed, journals. He has written one book chapter and he presented about six papers in international conferences. His area of research includes image processing, data visualization, computer networks and soft computing techniques.
