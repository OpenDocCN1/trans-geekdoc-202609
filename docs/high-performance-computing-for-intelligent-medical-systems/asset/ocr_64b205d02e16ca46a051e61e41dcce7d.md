**Step 3:** Cats’ positions are used in fitness function, that is, BDM is computed for every cat. The position of the cat with the best solution (xbest) is stored in memory, which is the best solution calculated till now.
**Step 4:** Based on flag value, cats make a move. Let’s say cat, is observing, then the seeking model or the tracing mode process is used for cat,
**Step 5:** Based on MR values of cats, they are selected, and tracing mode is applied to them and the remaining cats will be in seeking mode.
**Step 6:** If the limiting condition is met, end the algorithm and the best cat position with the lowest BMD will be selected. Otherwise, steps 3 to 5 are repeated.
