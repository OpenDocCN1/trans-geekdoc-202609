# Chapter 11

# Computational intelligence for eye disease detection

Amit Kumar Verma

High performance computing has an important role to play as the medical field depends on fast sensing, proper examination and analysis. Computational intelligence thus has a greater role to act in the detection of eye diseases, especially related to the retina. Changes in the retina may be the indicator of various eye diseases along with diseases or disorder related to the central nervous system and cardio-vascular circulation. The retina is a membranous layer of tissue which is thin and provides central vision. Many eye diseases manifested through the retina are retinal tear, glaucoma, optic nerve disorder, diabetic macular edema, diabetic retinopathy, detachment of retina, hole in macula, degeneration of macula and other cardiovascular disorders like myocardial infarction and atherosclerosis. The retina is vulnerable to various organ specific as well as many systemic diseases, while retina imaging helps in the diagnosis of proper eye disease along with complications of diabetes retinopathy, hypertension, myocardial infarcion etc. Retina analysis is a non-intrusive diagnosis technology. Through the use of computational intelligence via image processing techniques, the various eye diseases can be detected at a very early age of their initiation. This chapter focuses on the anatomy of the eye, physiology of the various eye diseases, retinal imaging methods-2D fundus imaging and 3D optical coherence tomography (OCT) and their comparative analysis. The chapter actually deals with retinal imaging and its clinical implications in the diagnosis or detection of eye diseases. In addition of this, the chapter also discusses different methods which are used for detection of glaucoma.
