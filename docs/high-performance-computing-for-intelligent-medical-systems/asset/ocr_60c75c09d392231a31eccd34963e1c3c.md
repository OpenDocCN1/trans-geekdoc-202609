# 8.4 Motion estimation and the necessity of it in video coding?

ME is the vital component of video compression. ME with motion compensation is used for the elision of temporal redundancy. Since a video is a collection of frames and there is little difference between two closely located frames, there is no need to encode every frame of a video. Rather we can intra-code the first frame and encode the change in information amongst the frames which are represented by MV and pass the same to the decoder side. The ME block of codec determines the displacement of objects in a video in terms of coordinate difference known as MV which is used by a decoder to regenerate the original video. The process of finding the MV is known as ME and this is the most complex part of video compression. The encoding of only the difference of frames, that is, MV will greatly reduce the bit budget to represent the video as the bits required to encode only MV are higher in comparison to the bits required to encode only whole frame. The efficiency of the compression process heavily depends on the complexity of ME process. So, the target of the research work is always to design such a methodology that the whole ME becomes fast and accurate. There are many approaches available for ME but due to the simplicity and speed, the block matching approach is widely accepted and research is ongoing to make the ME process more easy, accurate, and fast by developing new Block Matching Algorithms (BMAs). # 8.4.1 Forward and backward motion estimation

In the video coding techniques, while removing temporal redundancies, frames are encoded concerning some other past or future frame. This type of coding is known as inter-frame coding or predictive coding. The frames that correspond to this type of coding are known as P-frame. In the encoding process, if the frame k (current frame) is encoded using the past frame say (k−1)th frame (previous frame) to predict what frame number k is going to be, we term it as forwarding prediction (FP) as future prediction is done for the kth frame by calculating MV.
