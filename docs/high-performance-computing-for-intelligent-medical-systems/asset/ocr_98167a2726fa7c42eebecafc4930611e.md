# 7.9.2 Maturing and validating patient-targeted feedback systems

Well-functioning patient feedback systems in diagnostics are an essential tool for healthcare organizations to improve their image quality. Easy-to-use feedback systems can assure patients that their voice is heard at the most critical stage of the healthcare process. Healthcare organizations can thus increase their image quality value in a remarkable way through intensive and flexible feedback possibilities.

In the area of product quality, patient-targeted feedback systems should focus particularly on privacy, ease of use, and credibility. Customers are only interested in giving feedback if they believe it will be helpful. To ensure customer quality, feedback on the functionality of the diagnostic process is essential. Because doctors are also users of the feedback system, they should also be consulted on how well a feedback system serves them in assessing the diagnostic process's quality.

Diagnostics-focused feedback systems should provide hospital administrators with information on the current state of patient satisfaction about diagnostics and how feedback systems can be improved. This information will provide additional input and material for physician training to enhance the diagnostic process. Patient targeted diagnostics focused feedback system aims to improve the diagnostic process's safety and reliability and thus improve overall process quality.

Patient-targeted feedback systems need their maturation stages to reach acceptable levels of innovation quality. AI brings new opportunities to the field of product design. Systems must evolve through the feedback they receive about their usefulness to ensure that all patients can benefit from the feedback system. System development should not cease at the first well-functioning state in the field of application design.

# 7.10 Conclusion

The correctness of diagnostics in healthcare is a key area of concern for patients. To ensure patient legal protection, it is important to develop feedback systems that are well-suited to providing feedback on the accuracy of diagnostics. Feedback delivery should be easy for the client and provide care planners and caregivers with sufficiently informative information about the patient's perspective. Narrative feedback defends its place because it gives the patient a free hand to give feedback. The transformation of free-form text into informative and usable data has evolved in part thanks to artificial intelligence applications. However, giving feedback on the diagnostic portion is not easy for the patient in all cases. For this reason, the chapter also presents a format model representing structured feedback that promotes the patient's ability to provide useful and sufficiently informative feedback. Because patients' willingness to use the time required for feedback naturally varies, it is important to consider different levels of time use when developing feedback systems
