# C. Child health and heart studies in England (CHASE_DB1)

It contains different varieties of disorders prevailing in a retinal fundus image and their ground truth vessels [28]. The fundus images have been acquired with a TIF camera of 35 degree FOV and 1280 × 960. The picture has been captured with a complete exposure in the center of the optical disk. The database consists of 14 pairs of left and right eyes obtained from children. Two specialists were used for creating a ground truth mask of the vessel regions. However, diseases are not clearly registered. All videos are of high quality and well distinguishable.

# D. HRF

Jan Odstrcilik and his group of researchers developed the high-resolution fundus (HRF) dataset to undertake an automated segmentation architectures critical review. 45 retinal fundus images are provided by the HRF database. It includes 15 samples for healthy eyes, 15 sample images for glaucoma and 15 sample images for DR. All images have a high resolution of 3504 × 2336 [29].

# E. Messidor

For carrying out empirical studies on multiple segmentation algorithms for retinal fundus images the French Ministry of Science and Defense has established this dataset. It is a compilation of 1200 retinal images obtained by the non-mydriatic Topcon TRC NW6. Three of its ophthalmology divisions took part in the image acquisition process, which used 45-degree screen settings in TIFF format with a total colour-channel resolution of 8 bit, 2304 × 1536, 2240 × 1488, and 1440 × 936. 400 sample images does not have pupil dilation while others were taken with dilation. For each picture in the data set, the experts suggested two diagnoses of the potential macular oedema and retinopathy [30].

# F. Retinal vessel images for estimation of width (REVIEW)

It consists of 16 mydriatic and 193 segmented retinal blood vessel images. It is an initiative of the University of Lincoln (United Kingdom), in the year 2008. In order to mark the divisions of blood vessels, three specialists used a special drawing technique. This set of data has been subdivided into four series consisting of 8 photographs with high definition, 2 with central light reflective pictures, 4 of images of vascular abnormalities and 2 pictures of kicks point [31].

# G. ImageRet

The ImageRet project is FinnWell’s technology project for diabetic retinopathy (DR), which began in 2008. This collection has 219 retinal images named as DIARETDB0 and DIARETDB1. iDIARETDB0 includes 130 fundus images where 110 healthier and 20 DR. And in DIARETDB, 84 images have DR and the remaining are healthy. The images were acquired in PNG format with a 50° fundus camera bearing 1500 × 1152 resolution [32].
