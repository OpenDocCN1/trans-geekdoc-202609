
# Introduction

Introduction will be the first section and consists of four chapters. Will focus on familiarizing readers about Bitcoin. Will cover the blockchain ecosystem, other cryptocurrencies and blockchain projects, definition, need and necessity of Bitcoin, overview of topology and consensus mechanisms and use cases.

## [Chapter 1](#c01.xhtml)

Will give the readers glimpse of ecosystem to which Bitcoin is the part and introduces readers to other cryptocurrencies and blockchain projects and overall Bitcoin ecosystem.

## [Chapter 2](#c02.xhtml)

Will understand the technologies enhancements that were taking place which resulted in the inception of Bitcoin network and the major events happened since Bitcoin came into existence. Will also explain the meaning and purpose of Bitcoin, the impact it may have on our future and why learning Bitcoin is still important.

## [Chapter 3](#c03.xhtml)

Will understand about the network topology of Bitcoin and will understand Bitcoin consensus network. The Bitcoin network topology will include types of nodes, their interactions. The Bitcoin consensus network will include its components, their interactions and a high-level view of how payments in bitcoin can be seen as transaction creation, transaction signing, propagations, and finally inclusion into blockchain.

## [Chapter 4](#c04.xhtml)

Will understand about different ways people and organizations use Bitcoin and interact with Bitcoin network.

# Bitcoin Consensus Network

Bitcoin Consensus Network will be the second section consisting of four chapters. Will focus on understanding the basics of Bitcoin’s proof of work consensus mechanism by explaining Bitcoin Whitepaper, verifying blocks in blockchain, building and verifying transactions, understanding the process of mining and finally understanding the strength and weakness of Bitcoin in detail.

## [Chapter 5](#c05.xhtml)

Will understand the Bitcoin Whitepaper section by section in detail.

## [Chapter 6](#c06.xhtml)

Will analyze Bitcoin blockchain, block and transaction structure, understand different indexes used by Bitcoin Core software, understand mining in greater detail and try to build a solo miner.

## [Chapter 7](#c07.xhtml)

Will dive deep into the node-to-node communication. Will include establishing Bitcoin network, discover nodes, broadcast transactions and blocks, and learn to download and synchronization of blockchain.

## [Chapter 8](#c08.xhtml)

Will understand the limitation, vulnerabilities and the potential threats for Bitcoin. Will understand that the scaling and privacy limitations were introduced in the design and during implementation and were necessary and different ways it can be overcome. Will understand different ways Bitcoin network may be attacked or may become vulnerable to attacks in the future and how this may be mitigated or may not be an issue. Will also understand other forms of attacks or mistake due to which individuals can lose bitcoins.

# Programming Bitcoin

Programming Bitcoin will be the third section consisting of three chapters. Will go into details of Bitcoin scripting and use them to build more complex use cases including smart contracts. Will also dive deep into wallets for receiving and accessing bitcoins.

## [Chapter 9](#c09.xhtml)

Will learn to access and secure bitcoins. Will learn to generate private keys from random number which is used in signing a transaction before sending it to the network. Will learn to generate public keys and addresses which are used for receiving bitcoins. Will also learn to create deterministic wallet which will help simplify receiving, sending and securing bitcoins.

## [Chapter 10](#c10.xhtml)

Will deep dive into transaction verification and creation. Will learn to parse a transaction and learn about the scripting language used to lock and unlock bitcoins in a transaction. Will create the standard transactions and publish them over the network.

## [Chapter 11](#c11.xhtml)

Will understand to use scripting for creating variety of useful use cases which can be used in building more complex smart contract.

# Scaling Bitcoin

Scaling Bitcoin will be the fourth and final section of the book which consists of two chapters. The first of the two chapters will focus on segregated witness enhancement while the second one focusses on Lightning Network which is the layer two protocol added to the Bitcoin blockchain. Both chapters will provide different approaches to scale Bitcoin. Each chapter will go deeper into explaining the topic and will not be limited to scaling. Lightning Network will not include programming.

## [Chapter 12](#c12.xhtml)

Will understand Segregated Witness enhancement. Will understand the different benefits of the enhancement. Will also learn to parse segregated witness transactions.

## [Chapter 13](#c13.xhtml)

Will understand Lightning Network the second layer added to the Bitcoin protocol. Will understand the Lightning Network channel management and smart contract in some detail. Will also understand channel monitoring, routing mechanism and user experience with Lightning Network.
