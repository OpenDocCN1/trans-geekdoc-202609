Recall that the gradient of $f$ at $x$, denoted $
abla f(x)$, points in the direction of steepest ascent. Conversely, the negative gradient points in the direction of steepest descent. Therefore, if we take a small step in the direction of the negative gradient, we will decrease the value of the function.

The update performed is

```
$$\mathbf{x}_{i+1} = \mathbf{x}_i - \alpha_i \nabla f(\mathbf{x}_i)$
```

where $\alpha_i > 0$ is the step size. We may choose $\alpha_i$ to be a fixed constant, but in many cases it is decayed to zero over the course of training.
