- 75. B. Windows operating system that utilizes BitLocker on computers are dependent on having a Trusted Platform Module (TPM) chip on the motherboard for full-disk encryption. This chip generates and stores the actual encryption keys. It can automatically unlock your PC’s drive when it boots so that you can sign in just by typing your Windows login password. The TPM chip is doing the hard work under the hood. If someone tampers with the PC or removes the drive from the computer and attempts to decrypt it, it cannot be accessed without the key stored in the TPM.
