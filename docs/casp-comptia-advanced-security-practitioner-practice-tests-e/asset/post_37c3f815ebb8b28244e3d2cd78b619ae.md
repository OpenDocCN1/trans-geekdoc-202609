- **151.** C. When a situation arises, such as a service interruption or some other significant incident, the security operations center (SOC) receives word via their monitoring system. Critical issues must be addressed quickly, as any downtime can have a tremendous negative impact on the organization, from lower revenue to lost customers. This puts a lot of pressure on NOC managers to handle any and all incidents with the utmost attention given to quality and turnaround time. The problem comes into play when businesses are still relying on antiquated systems to manage their incident response processes. The result is a huge margin for human error and unnecessary delay.
