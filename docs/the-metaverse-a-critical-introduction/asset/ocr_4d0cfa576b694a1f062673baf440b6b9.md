# ## Second Life and OpenSim

*Second Life* (2003, www.secondlife.com), also known just as SL, is the creation of Philip Rosedale and Linden Lab. *Second Life* reflected many of the same ideas as Active Worlds but delivered a more complete execution. It is worth spending some time looking at *Second Life* as it is still one of the best implementations of a proto-Metaverse environment (Au, 2023; Lamb, 2022).
