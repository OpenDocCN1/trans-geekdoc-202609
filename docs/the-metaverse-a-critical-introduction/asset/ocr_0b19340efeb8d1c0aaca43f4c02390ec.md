# Epilogue

We hope that this book has provided you with a (relatively) concise and critical introduction to the concept of the Metaverse, its history, technology, potential benefits and challenges, and some idea of how the Metaverse might evolve over the coming years, decades, and even centuries.

If you would like to explore further any of the ideas presented in this book, then we refer you to the rest of the books that are being published in this series. Books already underway have been listed at the front of this book, but we are actively identifying and commissioning new authors as we go to press, and for long after this initial book is published.
