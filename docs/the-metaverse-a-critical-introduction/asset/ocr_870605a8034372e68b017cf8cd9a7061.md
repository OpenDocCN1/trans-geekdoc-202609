# Is Second Life the Metaverse?

As may be apparent from Chapter 1, Second Life and its associated open-source and hypergrid-enabled derivative OpenSim already provide many of the features that are specified for a Metaverse and can be correctly termed proto-Metaverses. This is borne out by their graphs in Figure 2.5. They may not be the Metaverse, but they are still the closest current experience that there is to it.
