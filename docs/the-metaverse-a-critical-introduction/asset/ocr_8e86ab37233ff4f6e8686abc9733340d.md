# Flow

Flow is defined as “the state of intense involvement in an activity, an extreme version of immersion, losing self-consciousness, being unaware of fatigue and having a modified sense of time” (Csikszentmihalyi, 1990) and is linked to immersion and presence. Learners, those performing professional activities (e.g. musicians), and even gamers are believed to perform at their best when in a flow state and when the level of challenge of the activity is appropriate to their skill level. In VR, anything that jars with the experience, or where the user consciously has to think about how to use the environment, will immediately drop them out of the flow state and should be avoided.
