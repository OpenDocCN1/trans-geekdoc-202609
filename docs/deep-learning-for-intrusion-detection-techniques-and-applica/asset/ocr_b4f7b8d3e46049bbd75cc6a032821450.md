# 11.5 Applications of Deep Learning-Based IDS

The fast-paced development of wireless networks, especially IoT, 5G, and MANETs has thrown up new security challenges that require sophisticated intrusion detection solutions [20]. The problem is that we can no longer rely on the old security mechanisms that were built into our computers. Why? Because we no longer have computers the way we used to. Instead, we now have decentrally distributed networks that we access through a wide variety of devices, many of which are under our direct control—mainly smartphones and tablets, but also smart TVs, home routers, and a growing number of other devices that are “smart” in one way or another. Moreover, all of these devices have their own operating systems, software, and built-in security imperatives. So, in a very real way, we are all inside the IoT [12].

This section covers DL techniques and their applications in IDSs. It focuses mainly on IoT security, 5G network security, and man-portable MANET security. It delves into effective DL methods and their corresponding challenges. It ranges from security vulnerabilities and the newly minted attack surfaces for the connected world of things to the potential for DL methods to offer not only improved performance for IDSs but also for the connected world of things.
