
DL-driven IDS is automatic and secure. Once DL-driven AIs achieve XAI—allowing them to evaluate, understand, and see why something works—we can totally trust them to make detection decisions. Defeating the adversary without exposing your network or data to sensitive vulnerabilities has become edge computing’s next outstanding achievement in privacy-preserving, distributed intrusion detection. Wireless networks now span the globe. With the expansion of 5G, the IoT, and next-generation communication technologies, intelligent, adaptive, and resilient (if not provably secure) IDS solutions are crucial.

## 11.7 Conclusion

DL-based IDSs have emerged as a powerful new way to secure wireless networks, the IoT, and next-generation communication infrastructures (5G, 6G). These systems are not the signature-happy but mostly ineffective wired IDSs of the past that struggled to identify evolving cyber threats. Powering the current generation of our next-level IDSs are DL algorithms. Unsupervised, semi-supervised, and fully supervised learning strategies are used to train the systems. The key advantage of using DL models to perform next-generation anomaly detection is that they are less brittle than traditional detectors and work much better when real-world adversaries are up to no good. To ensure that the AI-driven IDS is effective and practical, the researchers devised a multipronged approach. They developed adversarial-resistant DL models that counter evasion techniques and that now constitute improved security defenses. They optimized the models for low-power and resource-constrained environments, ensuring that they could be deployed in such venues as the IoT, edge computing, and mobile networks. They slowed down the exponential growth of the number of parameters in the models, thereby enhancing computational efficiency. The improved explainability of the models through the use of an XAI framework now ensures that security personnel will have an interpretable reasoning process in which to place their trust. They leveraged FL and edge computing to ensure that the AI-driven IDSs will serve privacy-preserving, decentralized solutions that are effective across the large and distributed architecture of the next generation of the Internet. This architecture—a construct of 5G and 6G, IoT, and cloud-edge systems—will serve as a much larger target for the penetration of even more sophisticated cyberspace threats.
