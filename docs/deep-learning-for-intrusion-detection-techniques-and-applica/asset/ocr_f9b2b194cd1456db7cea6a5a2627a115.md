A self-learning system has emerged as an effective method to address present-day attacks. This system uses supervised, unsupervised, and semi-supervised mechanisms of ML, the machine learns the patterns of various activities—normal and malicious—with a large corpus of normal and attack networks and host-level events [23]. DL performs tasks such as intrusion detection, traffic analysis, android malware classification, ransomware detection, network traffic prediction, malicious URL detection, encrypted text categorization, anomaly detection, and malicious domain name detection [24].

DL, which has gained wider attention and recognition, is being used for intrusion detection. Experiments show that anomaly detection can be performed through the DL technique. With the help of the NSL-KDD dataset, DL can be applied for a network IDS that is found to be very efficient and well-performing. Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Naive Bayes, SVM, K-Means, Random Forest, and KNN are among the most prominent algorithms used in ML. As a repository for intrusion detection, many research groups have meticulously prepared cybersecurity datasets, like KDD Cup 1999 Dataset, ISOT (Information Security and Object Technology) Dataset, HTTP CSIC 2010 Dataset, CTU-13 (Czech Technical University) Dataset, and UNSW-NB15 Dataset [25].

Application of DL to new problem situations is growing rapidly and the trend is likely to continue. The growth trend shows an accelerated rise of investment in DL, types and applications of DL are growing rapidly. DL is built on ANNs, which is a mathematical modeling of biological assemblies of neurons. The goal of an ANN is to create AI in computers and to “mimic the biological mechanisms of cognitive processes in animal brains.” The idea is to model the biological neural networks with mathematics and then to program the computers with such models and enable them to perform tasks which hitherto are believed to be possible only by humans. Thus, the electronic brain, which was a dream, became reality [26].

DL ANNs have made significant progress in the field of NLP in the way how people communicate in their language. NLP, and image recognition—which were functions of human brains—are now possible for computers through DL. With regard to image recognition (computer vision), DL has out-performed humans by achieving far better results. Computer vision has become highly reliable for a few tasks: motor cars commonly offer certain features based upon computer vision, even cars can self-drive; CCTV monitors in shopping malls and airports are used, and some CCTV monitors can identify known thieves and alert the staff, or even the local police automatically after spotting them [11].
