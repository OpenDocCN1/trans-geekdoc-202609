+   - **False Positives and Negatives:** The dynamic nature of wireless networks and open characteristics can lead to high false positives and negatives, making it difficult to distinguish between legitimate and malicious conduct. As a result, IDSs often experience false positives (legitimate activity incorrectly identified as malicious activity) and false negatives (malicious activity not detected). This can consequently cause privacy breaches and network interruptions.

# ### 10.3.5  Wireless Medium Weaknesses

+   - **Eavesdropping:** Because wireless signals are broadcast; they can be thwarted by any device within range. This allows attackers easy access to private information such as user data, passwords, or control signals.
+   - **Data Theft:** Attackers employ security flaws to gain access to sensitive information, and use it for nefarious purposes like fraud, identity theft, or intellectual property theft. Attackers could gain unauthorized access to the network by impersonating identities or simulating legitimate nodes and processes.
+   - **Denial of Service (DoS) Attacks:** This attack uses jamming to disrupt wireless signals to restrict communication. This can block legitimate nodes from sending or receiving data, resulting in DoS to the authorized party.
+   - **Network Disruption:** Other device or environmental interferences can affect signal quality, which leads to packet loss, increased latency, and overall network downtime. This can diminish the effectiveness of the IDS by making it challenging to monitor and analyze network traffic efficiently.
