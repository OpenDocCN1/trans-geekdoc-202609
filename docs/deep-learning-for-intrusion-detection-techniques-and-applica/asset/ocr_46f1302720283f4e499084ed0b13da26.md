# 7.1 Introduction

# 7.1.1 Overview of Intrusion Detection Systems (IDSs)

Recent advancements in machine learning have revolutionized various fields, including image recognition, natural language processing, and cybersecurity. Among these, deep learning—a subfield of machine learning—has emerged as a powerful paradigm, particularly in tasks requiring feature extraction and selection. Unlike traditional machine learning methods, which often rely on manually engineered features, deep learning models can automatically learn hierarchical and meaningful representations directly from raw data. This capability not only reduces the time and effort associated with manual feature engineering but also allows models to uncover complex patterns that might be overlooked by traditional techniques. These advancements have made deep learning a cornerstone of modern intrusion detection systems (IDSs), where the quality of features plays a critical role in distinguishing between legitimate and malicious activities [1].

IDSs are critical components of modern cybersecurity frameworks, designed to monitor network traffic and system activities to identify unauthorized access, malicious behavior, or potential security breaches. These systems play a pivotal role in safeguarding sensitive information and ensuring the integrity of networks and devices. As cyber threats evolve, IDSs have become indispensable for protecting assets across industries, ranging from finance and healthcare to government and defense sectors.
