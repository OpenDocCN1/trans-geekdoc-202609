DL artificial intelligence (AI) mimics the innate neural network embedded in the human brain, enabling computers to discover patterns automatically and arrive at decisions from a vast amount of unstructured data. # 5.2 Deep Learning

DL is a branch of machine learning (ML). It works on an artificial neural network (ANN) architecture—a network constituted in line with the structural and operational principles of human neurons. These are also known as neural nets or neural networks, and they employ layers of interconnected nodes called neurons as shown in Fig. 5.1. Such a network of neurons processes and learns from the data injected into it. Artificial neurons, known as units, are found throughout ANNs. An ANN has three layers namely (i) input layer, (ii) output layer, and (iii) hidden layers [1].
