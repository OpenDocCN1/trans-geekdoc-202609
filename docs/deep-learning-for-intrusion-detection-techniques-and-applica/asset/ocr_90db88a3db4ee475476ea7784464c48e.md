c) **Time Series:** Time series involves a sequence of measurements taken over a specific time period, considering an observation unusual if its likelihood of occurring at that time is exceptionally low.

Statistical-based IDS offers benefits, such as detecting previously unknown attacks without relying on signatures, simplified maintenance without frequent updates, and effectiveness in detecting DoS and DDoS attacks. However, drawbacks include the time required to create a legitimate profile, potential changes in the legitimate profile over time, and the necessity for accurate and effective statistical distributions to ensure precision and efficiency.

***IDS Based on Rule***  An IDS utilizing a rule-based approach employs pre-established rules to recognize potential intrusions within network traffic. These rules can be perceived as patterns, created through artificial intelligence techniques based on attack patterns. Unlike signature-based IDS, which demands thousands of signatures to detect various attacks, a rule-based IDS can identify multiple attacks using a single rule. This approach requires fewer rules in comparison, making the management of the IDS simpler. Furthermore, this system is adept at identifying new attacks, as alterations in attack patterns may not be significant enough to completely change intrusion patterns. Employing multiple rules in an IDS might be necessary to ensure the identification of all potential network attacks.

***IDS Based on Heuristic***  The heuristic-based IDS operates by recognizing malicious behaviors, establishing a model for detecting acceptable behaviors, and flagging any deviations. This approach depends on knowledge and experience to effectively distinguish between normal and attack behaviors. Analysis of collected execution traces is conducted to identify suspicious patterns, triggering an alert upon detection. This method is capable of identifying both unknown (zero-day) attacks and known attacks, with the ability to discover and include new attack types in the list of malicious behaviors. However, the heuristic engine may be susceptible to evasion by certain attacks employing hiding techniques.

***IDS Based on Pattern***  The IDS employing a pattern-based approach identifies attacks by extracting characters, strings, and structures from collected data to recognize significant patterns. This approach specifies the malicious instruction sequences in attacks, and the identified patterns within the IDS are termed signatures. While the pattern-based approach is adept at swiftly and effectively detecting known attacks, it is less efficient in identifying most zero-day attacks due to the unknown nature of their patterns or signatures. The implementation of a pattern-based detection system is straightforward. Pattern-matching algorithms are categorized as multiple pattern matching and single pattern matching.
