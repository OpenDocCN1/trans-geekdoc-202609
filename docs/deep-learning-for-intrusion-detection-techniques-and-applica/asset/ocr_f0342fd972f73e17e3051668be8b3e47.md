However, their effectiveness is constrained by a restricted view of the internal state of the monitored systems, presenting a potential challenge in the detection process [12]. NIDSs frequently experience a significant number of false negatives and false positives. Initially, many of these IDSs rely on signature-based detection techniques to identify known and straightforward attacks. While network-based IDSs possess advanced capabilities for detecting attacks, they also come with limitations. For instance, they are unable to identify attacks occurring on encrypted network traffic and may struggle to perform comprehensive analysis during periods of high network traffic. Additionally, the use of stateful protocol analysis by IDS sensors introduces the possibility of certain events going undetected.

This system serves to protect endpoints and encompasses various data types, including system logs, traffic details, and file access and modifications.
