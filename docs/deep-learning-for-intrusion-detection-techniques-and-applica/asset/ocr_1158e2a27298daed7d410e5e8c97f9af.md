However, securing smart environments by way of deep learning is not a challenge that cybersecurity experts alone can solve. Rather, it requires interdisciplinary cooperation: a combined effort among cybersecurity experts, researchers in machine learning, hardware manufacturers, and legislators, all of whom ensure security is implemented at multiple tiers within the system—from hardware development to the design of software to its regulation. Lastly, as smart environments mushroomed everywhere, the significance of securing these systems grew proportionately. Deep learning will find its way as a primary tool for the protection of such systems by allowing complex anomaly, intrusion, and adaptive defenses. This, however, calls for interdisciplinary collaboration that pools together expertise within cybersecurity, machine learning, hardware design, and policy development in order to fully realize the potential of deep learning in securing smart environments [6]. Only by applying a holistic approach to security can we ensure the safety, privacy, and integrity of the connected world we are rapidly building.

# 9.1.1 Understanding Smart Environments and Their Security Needs

Smart environments based on a series of interconnected technologies make it possible for devices, systems, and applications to operate in a completely integrated dynamic way. Connectivity, automation, and data collection and analytics are the three core pillars of these environments, all working together to ensure optimum performance, efficiency, and experience. Let's take a closer look at each of these features [5].
