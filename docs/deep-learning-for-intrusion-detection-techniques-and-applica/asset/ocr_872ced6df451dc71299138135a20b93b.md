The growth of computer networks and their increased applications have enabled cyberattacks like brute force attacks, browser attacks, and distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks [20]. The rise in the sophistication of malware (malicious software) has emerged as a significant problem for the international community and a security threat for internet users like the zero-day attack. In addition, around the world, a large number of cybercriminals are in search of opportunities to illegitimately receive revenues, steal information, and find new targets. The new generation of malware is more ambitious, targeting banks and taking millions of dollars in one attack. Malware is created intentionally to compromise computer systems and to take advantage of weaknesses in IDSs [5].

Rise in the internet-based technology has resulted in new areas of applications for computer networks. The use of network applications such as LAN (local area network) and WAN (wide area network), has increased in many fields like security, finance, business, industry, and healthcare sectors. All these applications make networks susceptible to attack by malicious users or hackers who collect information from the organizations’ internal systems and cause vulnerabilities like lapses in administration, software bugs, and systems left at default configurations. The integration of internet into society has witnessed new threats like worms and viruses imported into the systems. Further, few malignant users apply different techniques to crack passwords and detect unencrypted text, causing harm to the system. This highlights the significance of security of the network, both private and public. The firewall technique is the most common protection technique that is used for the protection of the private network from the public network, whereas IDS is used for network-related activities, credit card frauds, medical applications, insurance agencies, etc. [9]. The primary aim of an IDS is the identification of different kinds of malware at the earliest possible time, which is unachievable by a traditional firewall [5].

IDS observes network traffic for prevention of malicious transactions and immediately sends alerts once it is observed. It is software that observes a network or system for malicious activities or violation of policy. Each illegal activity or violation is mostly recorded either centrally using a security information and event management tool (SIEM) system—that helps improve security by identifying potential risks, analyzing security incidents to understand their impact, and automating responses to threats—or notifying an administration. It monitors a network or system for prevention of malicious activity and secures a computer network from unauthorized use and access, including perhaps insiders. Its task is to build a predictive model—a classifier—capable of distinguishing between “good (normal) connections” and “bad connections” (attacks/intrusion) [6].
