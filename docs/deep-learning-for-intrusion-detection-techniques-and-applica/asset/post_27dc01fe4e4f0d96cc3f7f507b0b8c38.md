## About the Editors

**Faheem Syeed Masoodi** is an Associate Professor of Cybersecurity at Bahrain Polytechnic University. He has previously served as a Senior Assistant Professor at the University of Kashmir, India; an Assistant Professor at the Jazan University, Saudi Arabia; and a Research Scientist for the NMEICT-EDRP project, sponsored by the Ministry of HRD, Government of India.

Dr. Masoodi holds a PhD in Network Security and Cryptography from Aligarh Muslim University and completed his Master’s and Bachelor’s degrees in Computer Science from the University of Kashmir. His research focuses on Cryptography and Network Security, Intrusion Detection, Post-Quantum Cryptography, Financial Security, and the Internet of Things (IoT).

Dr. Masoodi, an active contributor to the global research community, has authored and edited several books, book chapters, and high-impact publications with reputed publishers. He is also an active member of professional cryptology associations. His work has been recognized through prestigious fellowships, including the CEMSAC-2014 Fellowship (Centre Henri Lebesgue, University of Rennes, France), SP-ASCRYPTO-2011 Fellowship (Advanced School of Cryptography, University of Campinas, Brazil), Maulana Azad National Fellowship (MANF-2010), and the INTI Research Fellowship (2024), Malaysia.

**Alwi Bahmdi** is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Sciences, College of computing (Al Qunfudhah), at Umm Al-Qura University, Saudi Arabia. He received his BSc (2008) degree in Computer Science from King Abdul Aziz University in Jeddah, Saudi Arabia, and his MSc (2010) and PhD (2014) in Computer Science, respectively, from Heriot-Watt University, Scotland, UK. His research interests include Computer Networking, Mobile Ad Hoc Networks, Wireless Sensor Networks, Internet of Things, Cloud Computing, Information & Cyber Security, Digital Investigations & Forensics, Blockchain Technology, Computer Vision, Software Engineering, Performance Evaluation and Automation of Accounting Procedures. He has published many papers in his specialized areas of MANETs as well as in other areas of Computer Science and Information Technology within the scope of his research and teaching activities.

## Foreword

In an era where cyber threats grow more sophisticated by the day, safeguarding our digital environment has never been more critical. The book *Deep Learning for Intrusion Detection: Techniques and Applications* comes at a time when our digital environment is growing at a remarkable pace, bringing with it both opportunities and challenges. Among these challenges, safeguarding our networks and systems from ever-evolving cyber threats has become a critical priority. The integration of deep learning into intrusion detection is not just a technical advancement—it is a necessary step toward ensuring the safety, reliability, and trustworthiness of our digital world.

The chapters in this volume bring together knowledge, experiences, and perspectives from experts who have spent years working in this area. Together, they offer readers a balanced blend of foundational understanding and practical approaches that can be applied in diverse contexts. This makes the book a valuable resource not only for researchers and students but also for industry professionals, policy-makers, and anyone seeking to understand how modern technologies can be used to protect what matters most.

I am especially proud that this work is connected with Bahrain Polytechnic. It reflects our strong commitment to fostering innovation, encouraging inter-disciplinary collaboration, and promoting research that addresses real-world challenges. Publications like these help position our institution as a contributor to global knowledge while also showing how academic work can have a meaningful and lasting impact beyond the classroom.

I congratulate the editors for their dedication, hard work, and vision in bringing this important project to life. I believe the ideas and insights shared in this book will inspire further research, stimulate new collaborations, and support ongoing progress in building a secure and resilient digital future.

Prof. Ciarán Ó Catháin

Chief Executive Officer

Bahrain Polytechnic
