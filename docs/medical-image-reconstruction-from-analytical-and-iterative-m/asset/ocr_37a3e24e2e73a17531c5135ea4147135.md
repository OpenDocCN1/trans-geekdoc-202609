# 6.9.1 ART

The ART algorithm is a row-action algorithm. It considers one ray sum at a time and can be expressed as

$X^{\text{next}} = X^{\text{current}} - \frac{A_i X^{\text{current}} - p_i}{||A_i||^2} A_i^T, \quad (6.44)$

where $A_i X$ performs the forward projection along the $i$th projection ray, $p_i$ is the measured projection from the $i$th projection bin, $||A_i||^2 = \sum_j a_{ij}^2$ is the sum of the squared “contribution factors” along the $i$th ray, and $c A_i^T$ backprojects the value $c$ along the $i$th ray. If we rewrite the above algorithm in the following form:

$X^{\text{next}} = X^{\text{current}} - \left( \frac{A_i X^{\text{current}}}{||A_i||} - \frac{p_i}{||A_i||} \right) \frac{A_i^T}{||A_i||}, \quad (6.45)$
