where \( R \) is the radius, \( h \) is the helix pitch, \( \bar{x} = (x, y, z) \) is the reconstruction point, \( s \) is the orbit parameter, and \( I_{\pi}(\bar{x}) = [s_b, s_t] \), which is determined by the \( \pi \)-segment of the point \( \bar{x} \).

The cone-beam projection is represented in a local coordinate system as \( g(\bar{\theta}, \bar{a}) \), where \( \bar{\theta} \) is a function of the local coordinate system \( (\bar{a}, \bar{\beta}) \) (see Figure 5.29) with a parameter \( \gamma \) and is defined as

\( \bar{\theta}(\gamma) = (\cos \gamma)\bar{a} + (\sin \gamma)\bar{\beta}, \quad - \frac{\pi}{2} < \gamma < \frac{\pi}{2} \. \quad \text{(5.54)}\)

The unit vector \( \bar{a} \) is defined as the direction from the focal point to the reconstruction point. The unit vector \( \bar{\beta} \) is the filtering direction on the detector plane (see Figure 5.30).
