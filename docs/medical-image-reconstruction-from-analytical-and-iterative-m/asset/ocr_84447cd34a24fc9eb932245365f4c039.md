```
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} . \quad (6.128)
```

The rank of \( A \) is 3. The system \( AX = P \) is also not consistent. Therefore, there is no solution for this problem. We can use SVD-based method to find a pseudo-solution. # Example 5: Let \( A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \). Find the conjugate direction \(\bar{u}_1\) of \(\bar{u}_0 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}\).

# Solution

The conjugate direction is defined by this relationship: \(\bar{u}_0(\text{ATA})\bar{u}_1 = 0\), where

```
A^T A = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} . \quad (6.129)
```
