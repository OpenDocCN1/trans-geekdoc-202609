We must point out that after changing the variables, a convolution operation (with respect to variable s) may not turn into a convolution (with respect to variable γ) automatically, and some mathematical manipulation is needed to turn it into a convolution form. Like parallel-beam algorithms, the fan-beam algorithms include combinations of ramp filtering and backprojection or combinations of the derivative, Hilbert transform, and backprojection (DHB).

Researchers treat the flat detector fan-beam and curved detector fan-beam differently in reconstruction algorithm development. In a flat detector, the data points are sampled with equal distance Δs intervals, while in a curved detector, the data points are sampled with equal angle Δγ intervals (see Figure 3.6). A fan-beam algorithm can be converted from one geometry to the other with proper weighting adjustments.
