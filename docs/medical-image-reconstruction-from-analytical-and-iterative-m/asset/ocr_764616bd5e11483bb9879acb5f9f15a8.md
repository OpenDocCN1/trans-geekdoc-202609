# 6.9.4 ML-EM

The emission data ML-EM algorithm is the most popular iterative algorithm in emission tomography and is expressed as

![](bbox=[0.315, 0.186, 0.825, 0.222])

where the summation over $\hat{j}$ is the projector identical to \(Ax\) in Section 6.9.1, and the summations over \(i\) are the backprojectors. This algorithm compares the measured projection \(p_i\) with the forward projection of the current estimate \(Ax\) as a ratio. This ratio is backprojected to the image domain. The summation \(\sum_i a_{ij}\) is the backprojection of constant 1 to the image domain. The ratio of these two backprojected images determines a modification factor to update the current estimate of the image.
