If n is an even number, the filter is a 1D ramp filter combined with the (n-2)-th-order 1D derivative operator with respect to the variable s. For the 2D Radon transform, the tomographic filter is just the ramp filter. The ramp filter is the combination of the first-order derivative and the Hilbert transform.
As for the n-D ray transform, the inversion formula will depend on the imaging geometry similar to the situation in Section 5.4.2.
