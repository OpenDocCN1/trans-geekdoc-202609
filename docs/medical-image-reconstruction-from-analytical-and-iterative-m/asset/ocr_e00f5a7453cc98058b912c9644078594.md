Finally, we perform the fan-beam backprojection with a distance-dependent weighting [D/(D-s)]\(^2\), where s is the distance from the reconstruction point to the virtual detector, which is placed at the center of rotation for convenience.

Feldkamp's algorithm is almost the same as this fan-beam algorithm, except that the backprojection is a cone-beam backprojection. The ramp-filtering is performed in the row-by-row fashion. There is no filtering performed in the axial direction. Let the axial direction be the z-direction (see Figure 5.25), then
