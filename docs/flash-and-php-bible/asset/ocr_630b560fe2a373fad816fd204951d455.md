Real-world applications are meant to be complete products that have been tested and are ready to be deployed for the general public to enjoy them. This chapter focuses on building a series of applications that do just that. The starting files have been provided so you can focus on the core of the applications, which would be the development process.

Each example is broken into its individual classes, files, and packages depending on what is required. If you have never worked with classes, I strongly recommend that you read Chapter 10. This will ensure the examples and coding styles are easy to follow.
