41. What type of management involves minimizing the impact of disruptions to services or operations?

+   A. Incident management

+   B. Problem management

+   C. Continuity management

+   D. Availability management

+   A. Incident management is focused on limiting the impact of disruptive events on an organization and their services, returning their state to full operational status as quickly as possible.

+   B, C, and D are incorrect. Continuity management, or business continuity, is focused on planning for the successful restoration of systems and services following an unexpected outage, incident, or disaster. The focus of problem management is to analyze and identify potential issues and to put processes and mitigations in place to prevent predictable problems from ever occurring in the first place. Availability management is focused on making system resources, processes, personnel, and toolsets properly allocated and secured to meet SLA requirements for performance.
