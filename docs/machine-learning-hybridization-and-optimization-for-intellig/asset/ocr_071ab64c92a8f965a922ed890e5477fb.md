# 2.2.4 CENTRALIZED INFORMATION MERGING AND DISTRIBUTED DECISION-MAKING (CIMDD)

In a multi-UAV collaborative hunt, the majority of the research depends upon consolidated hunt maps for the incorporation of data and distributed DM for the robots [13, 14]. The UAVs can piece together centrally stored information which comprises data related to the terrain and other UAVs. Depending on the availability of the centrally stored information, the UAVs are capable of planning the paths in a distributed manner. The algorithms in this domain incorporate artificial implicit fields [15], machine learning (ML) algorithms [16], group dissipation patterns [15], mixed integer direct programming [18], and evolutionary algorithms [19] as collaboration procedures in order to minimize the implicit imbrications in the routes of UAVs. In these methods, it is concluded that the reduction of imbrications in the path of UAVs can ameliorate the effectiveness of collaborative hunting. No sophisticated information is required in these approaches. They only need a system for modernizing the centrally stored information grounded on their detector compliances and previous information. Their approaches model either a perfect detector which does not have any discovery crimes [17] or a detector that only has mis-findings. In these approaches, it is assumed that there is no noise during the process of communication between the base station and the robots.
