# 6.1 INTRODUCTION

One of the prevalent and major research areas in medical imaging is diagnosis using Computer Aided Detection (CAD) techniques. This has several breakthrough records in diagnostic radiology too. These techniques require extensive big data to build reliable and accurate models. However, it is extremely difficult to collect images in huge volumes from the latest modalities as they are not available to the public. Especially in the medical domain, collecting huge amounts of data is difficult. Thus, maximizing the utilization of medical images that are very limited for the purpose of interpretation and analysis appears to be a significant field for research. This work focuses on building frameworks for the classification and annotation of images with limited medical image datasets.
