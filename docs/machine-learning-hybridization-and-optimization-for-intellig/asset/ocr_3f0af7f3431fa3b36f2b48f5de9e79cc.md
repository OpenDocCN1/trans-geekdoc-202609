IoT is one of the best networks involved in every sector, such as agriculture, home networks, defense, and the health sector. The IoT system makes work easier in the health sector, as machines used in healthcare rely on the Internet. For instance, instruments like digital thermometers and dosimeters employ IoT to measure patient conditions and provide digital results. However, these devices may face security issues when sharing their network [9, 10]. The health sector experiences critical security challenges, including disturbance in system privacy, DDoS attacks, and unauthorized access.
