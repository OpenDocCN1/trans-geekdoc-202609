# 4.3 PROPOSED SYSTEM

The objective of the AgTech Revolutionize Farming Practices using IoT [33] is to enhance plant health monitoring by the gathering and [32] analysis of real-time data on critical parameters such as temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and nutrient levels. It enables soil monitoring and analysis, including measurement of pH, nutrient levels, and other critical factors. Machine learning and predictive analytics are used to support agricultural activities such as planting, fertilizing, and harvesting decision-making. Data-driven scheduling and management are used to optimize irrigation operations based on soil moisture and weather conditions. It creates an intuitive interface for farmers to access and engage with the intelligent farming [36] assistant, including data input, reports, alerts, and recommendations. It also integrates with other farming systems and technologies [37, 38, 39, 40, 41], such as weather forecasts, pest control, and irrigation.
