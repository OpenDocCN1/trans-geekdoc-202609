# ## 3.2.3 Literature Survey

The section presents a literature survey of various literatures which researched COVID-19; most of them would be new, as the problem of COVID-19 itself was new, but the ideas or models used in the literature might be the ones that can frequently be used. We take a few models which have been used in these chapters to explore the possibility of forecasting COVID-19 cases in India and do an elaborate comparison of the models that have been used.

Furquan et al. [6] analyzed how various ML models were used to predict the future number of cases that might occur due to COVID-19; in particular, they used models like linear regression, least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO), support vector machines (SVM), and exponential smoothing. The results were obtained by comparing the performance of the models in the prediction of upcoming infected cases, deaths, and patient recovery rates for the upcoming 10 days. Out of all the models, exponential smoothing performed the best in prediction followed by linear regression and LASSO regression, with SVM performing poorly in all the scenarios.
