## 8.6 SECURITY CHALLENGES IN APPLICATIONS OF BANKING AND FINANCE USING IOT

### 8.6.1 ATMs

IoT allows an information stream for ATMs to allow banks to screen user actions, recognize ATM use patterns, and choose where to put new ATMs based on demand. Changing to cost-saving operational phases requires data on the ATM site climate, such as room temperature, light, and movement, from IoT sensors. Adapting air-conditioning and lighting to ATM site activity allows for lower power consumption. IoT solutions assist in the management of events, such as the use of skimming devices, card reader issues, and cash shortages, by ensuring continuous monitoring of ATM operations and cash levels.

As technology drives the financial industry forward, new regulations are put in place to ensure that innovation does not come at the expense of user security. These regulations cover various potential issues, including data security and user privacy. As these regulations become more complex and comprehensive, banks must increase their expenditure on compliance to ensure that they can keep up.

### 8.6.1.1 Authentications

Issues such as weak passwords and using insecure or non-secure networks make individuals vulnerable to online hacking or fraudulent charges, such as login credential theft, which could result in unauthorized bank transactions.

### 8.6.1.2 End-to-End Encryption

Security that offers end-to-end encrypted protection may be compromised if a third party gains access to the network at the receiving end of the transmission. Not only can they read existing messages, they can also send new ones. That is why it is crucial to use passwords or biometrics to safeguard control of your device.

### 8.6.2 SMART BRANCHES

IoT solutions for smart branches improve customer experience by monitoring queues, providing customers with information on how long they can expect to wait, or directing them to a less crowded branch that can handle their transactions, streamlining operations and requiring fewer staff members.

### 8.6.2.1 Lack of Standards in Network Issues

Whenever we use the network, the risk we face is new technologies and network disruptions. Service security and efficiency can impact or compromise your accounts if your internet connection is slow or completely interrupted. Additionally, regardless of how advanced the technology is, bank servers are still susceptible to both planned and accidental downtime.

### 8.6.3 INSURANCE

IoT gadgets detect the condition of safeguarded articles and alert the supporters of any variations so they can intervene and take appropriate measures to reduce the risk. Insurers also utilize IoT-generated data to adopt a preventive approach and estimate incidents. For example, an insurer can identify an asset failure and alert a policyholder before any damage can occur. This approach helps decrease the number of insurance claims as well as detect insurance fraud.

### 8.6.3.1 Vulnerable Apps

If their mobile banking apps are not regularly maintained and updated, hackers could potentially access and steal users' savings. Alternatively, even if the apps are secure, users' smartphones may not be. This provides an entry point through which data can be stolen by thieves.

### 8.6.3.2 Outdated Mobile Experiences

Nowadays, every bank or credit union has its own branded mobile apps. However, not all of these apps are optimized to be used as effectively as possible. A bank's mobile experience should be fast, easy to use, fully featured, secure, and consistently updated to keep customers satisfied. Some banks have even begun to reimagine what a banking app could be by introducing mobile payment functionality that allows users to treat their smartphones as secure digital wallets and instantly transfer money to friends and family.

### 8.6.4 AUDITING AND ACCOUNTING

The IoT enables communication of the client's payment systems and automates common accounting tasks such as data entry, reconciliation, and invoicing. IoT enables accountants to monitor financial data in real time and gain accurate insights into business operations to improve their consulting roles. IoT also enhances transparency and automation in auditing, allowing CPAs to monitor transactions and manage audit trails instantly to detect data variations and prevent fraud.

### 8.6.4.1 Data Compromises

To enhance their IoT devices and environments, most banks have partnered with major tech companies. A data breach at any of these companies could potentially compromise the data privacy of banks and their customers.

### 8.6.4.2 Risk-Based Authentications

Risk-based authentication (RBA) is a method of applying varying levels of rigidity to authenticate processes based on the likelihood that admitting the given system could result in harm. As the level of risk increases, the authentication measures become more restrictive.

## 8.8 STEPS FOR SECURING HOME DEVICES

### 8.8.1 THE SETUP OF ROUTER

The main pathway for the IoT is the Wi-Fi router, which connects each device to the Internet. So the first security that is needed is for the routers. And these may be more susceptible to being hacked easily by others. So home network security starts with router security. There are three main levels which can secure these routers.

### 8.8.1.1 Altering the Default Name of Router

For each router, a default name is created which is commonly known to that router, and this may be visible to all network users. This default name can pose a high risk of unauthorized access, meaning that neighboring devices may also connect to these routers without permission. So, by changing the default name, it may provide a secure way since it will only be known to the main users and others may be confused when connecting to that device.

### 8.8.1.2 Unique Password to the Router

As discussed above, changing the name of the device may help secure the device, but there may still be a chance that other users can still connect to it. For this reason, another technique is changing the password for the router, which provides full security. This means that changing the password for the router will not allow access to other users unless the password is known.
