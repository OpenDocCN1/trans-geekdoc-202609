ML algorithms will be used to help in better decision-making for handling such a pandemic situation. This chapter attempted to predict future cases of COVID using ML algorithms that have been used in the prediction of various problems such as stock price prediction, cardiovascular disease prediction, breast cancer prediction, and many others. This chapter focuses on comparing various algorithms based on their performance and accuracy in the prediction of future cases.

The chapter’s main goal is to predict future cases so as to take necessary measures against the further spread of the virus and take necessary precautions. In this chapter, various machine learning algorithms, such as linear regression, lasso regression, and support vector machines; and various types of recurrent neural networks, such as LSTM, Bi-Directional LSTM, and Encoder-Decoder LSTM with the addition of univariate and multivariate forms of LSTM, were trialed for the analysis of COVID-19. This section compares the results obtained from the above models to evaluate their performance and accuracy obtained from them. A case study is performed on how various countries focus on bending the curve and considers the features that could be used to reduce the curve.
