IoT, which connects a variety of devices to the Internet and generates enormous amounts of data, has been evolving quickly. Big data management, storing, and analyzing the data produced by these devices have become significant challenges for enterprises with the emergence of IoT. # 20.2.2 Start-Ups

Some start-ups have grown extremely quickly, while others are about to. To deal with the influx of new businesses and start-ups, more Silicon Valleys have been established throughout the world [16]. Start-ups are frequently viewed as newly formed businesses founded in the backyard by creative young individuals developing low-cost portable devices and high-tech gadgets. Many definitions can be used to describe start-ups [17, 18]. Start-ups are always looking for a firm notion and work in search mode, producing something out of nothing, in opposition to established companies that operate in well-known marketplaces aiming to maximize effective and optimal execution [19]. Since they develop innovative products and services and transform business models using new technology, start-ups also serve as a source of innovation. Start-ups are seen as a source of outside innovation even more by companies that use an open innovation process. As a result, start-ups and their creative methods and outcomes are inextricably linked [18].
