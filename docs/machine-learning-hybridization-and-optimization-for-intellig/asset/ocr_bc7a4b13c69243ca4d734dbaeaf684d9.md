Due to the fact that the suggested arcade game is based on the idea of an “escape room”, the objective of the game is for the player to decipher the enigmas and locate the hints hidden throughout the environment. Because the environment is completely dynamic, the player has the ability to shift the position of things that are kept around him or her and modify how they are oriented. Because this is a mobile game, the players may touch and slide their finger over the screen to change the camera angle, allowing them to look around and investigate different areas of the level. In the first level of the game, which takes place in the restroom of an office building, the player’s objective is to locate the key to the building in order to access the restroom. Figure 17.5 (a) shows the area where the player may interact with the item by touching it or clicking on it to move it or rotate it in order to peek inside of it or behind it. Once he or she locates the key, the next step is to locate the appropriate keyhole into which the key may be inserted. Once the player has located the key to the office, he or she may enter the building by clicking on the door of the office. Figure 17.5 (b) shows the player observance in the setup of a workplace with many workstations and PCs at this point. The player must now locate the appropriate computer in order to retrieve the necessary code in order to proceed to the next level, which will take place in the mysterious chamber. Following the acquisition of the code, the player will proceed into the key house (level 2). Figure 17.5 (c) shows the positions where the player will have fewer clues to work with as they progress through subsequent stages, making it more challenging for them to complete the game.
