# About the Editors

![](bbox=[0.181, 0.254, 0.4, 0.491])

Tanvir Habib Sardar is an assistant professor at the Department of CSE at GITAM University, Bengaluru campus. He has more than 15.5+ years of experience in industry and academia. He received his Ph.D. degree from Visvesvaraya Technological University, Karnataka, India, and patented his Ph.D. work at the Indian patent office. He obtained his B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from West Bengal University of Technology, Kolkata, India, and received his M.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from Visvesvaraya Technological University, Karnataka, India. His research domain encompasses big data, machine learning, fuzzy logic, and distributed computing using MapReduce. He has published more than 35 research articles and presented at more than 8 international conferences. He has been a reviewer of many reputed peer-reviewed journals. He has authored two books and edited three and has published more than ten patents.
