Bishwajeet Kumar Pandey is a professor at the Department of Intelligent Systems and Cyber Security, Astana IT University, Kazakhstan. He is also a visiting professor at Eurasian National University, Astana, Kazakhstan (QS World Rank 355), and UCSI University, Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia (QS World Rank 300). He was the research head of the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Jain University (NIRF India Rank 68), Bengaluru, India. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Gran Sasso Science Institute, Italy. He received his M. Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT), Gwalior, India. With over 15 years of experience, he has worked as a research consultant and associate visiting professor. He has also held positions as an assistant professor at various universities, including Chitkara University, Chandigarh, India (QS World Rank 1200–1400). He has visited 47 countries and attended 98 conferences, receiving best paper awards in multiple countries. He has authored over 170 papers and has published 5 books. With interests in green computing, high-performance computing, cyber-physical systems, machine learning, and cyber security, he has several Indian patents and serves on the board of directors for student-founded start-ups. He has 2800+ citations and 26 H-index. He has generated a revenue of 2+ crores in his start-up Gyancity Research Consultancy Pvt Ltd since 2018. He is a program committee chair of the 7th International Multi-Topic Conference on Engineering and Science (IMCES), April 25–26, 2024, Bath Spa University UAE Academic Center, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE, https://imces.tech/. He is the main organizer
