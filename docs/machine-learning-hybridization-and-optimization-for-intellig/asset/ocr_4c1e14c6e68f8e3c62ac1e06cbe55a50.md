A CAD system was developed to deal with diverse varieties of abnormalities, which combines and normalizes several detected abnormalities subscores into a single TB score using a supervised classifier.
The calculated final overall TB score can be adapted to different operational settings or different operational requirements using automated CAD systems.

An approach to annotating abnormal regions in CT scan images is introduced in [47]. The proposed approach is a novel perspective that includes CT images of the lungs. The abnormal regions are separated into nine diverse groups of lung diseases, which could be applied to diagnosis and research using CAD and will be useful in medical education too.

An innovative approach [82] was put forward based on texture to find the important section in whole slide images (WSIs). The regions were selected with higher magnification to detect and diagnose breast cancer effectively by performing a more sophisticated analysis of the tissue formation. A Support Vector Machine (SVM) classification algorithm was trained to identify and differentiate clinically irrelevant patches. A novel automatic approach [44] was proposed which helps in identifying rare diseases like cardiac amyloidosis from electronic health records (EHRs), which consists of 73 positive and 97 negative instances and resulted in an F1 score of 0.98.

The work reported in [74] revealed that evolutionary computing methods such as preprocessing, feature extraction, segmenting, classification, clustering, and feature selection along with dimensionality reduction techniques play a vital role in optimizing CAD techniques for diabetic retinopathy (DR) disease. Several algorithms like ant and bee-colony optimization along with particle-swarm optimization have been studied. The comparisons of efficiencies to find the best algorithm have been presented. A presented work in [5] is one such CAD approach that inserts a source domain lesion seamlessly into a final target image. The data augmentation technique is tested on a CT and mammography dataset that helps in training various classifiers. The proposed work [38] introduces a CAD framework for identifying shapes and boundaries in dermoscopic images. The framework uses a saliency optimization algorithm for delineating lesions effectively.
