The IoT enables communication of the client's payment systems and automates common accounting tasks such as data entry, reconciliation, and invoicing. # 8.6.4.2 Risk-Based Authentications

Risk-based authentication (RBA) is a method of applying varying levels of rigidity to authenticate processes based on the likelihood that admitting the given system could result in harm. As the level of risk increases, the authentication measures become more restrictive.
