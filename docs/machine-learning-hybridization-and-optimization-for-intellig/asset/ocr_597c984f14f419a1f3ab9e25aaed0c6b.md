Integrating security measures with consumer approval is the start-up industry’s biggest problem. Instead of feeling that the system controls them, the user should believe that they are in charge of any information that relates to them. To the best of our knowledge, this integration creates fresh requirements that have never been considered. Security in the IoT refers to the safeguarding of networks and linked devices. IoT security guards against “malicious attacks,” “illegal access to services,” “theft or alteration of data,” “denial-of-service assaults,” “Internet risks,” and “network security.” But the IoT’s capacity to link numerous networked autonomous devices and its support for remote communication and management make it more probable that malicious attacks will proliferate [26]. IoT devices gather, process, and send private information across the network. The user must know what private information is being handled, while this data needs to be well protected from attackers. New IoT big data trends are reshaping how data is gathered, processed, stored, and evaluated. Some of the key themes influencing the IoT big data landscape are “edge computing,” “blockchain,” “5G networks,” and “cloud computing.” Businesses can get a competitive edge in the IoT market and promote innovation and growth by wisely utilizing these trends.

Before using an IoT device, individuals’ characteristics should be taken into account to protect their privacy and security.
