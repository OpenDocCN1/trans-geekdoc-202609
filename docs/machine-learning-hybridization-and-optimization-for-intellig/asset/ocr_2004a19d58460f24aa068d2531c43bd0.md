Nations has made the projection that there will be 900 million people who are hard of hearing in the world by the year 2050 [3]. As a result, SLR is garnering a lot of attention at the moment. SLR has the potential to eradicate the language barrier that exists within the hand-talk community (deaf-mute).

The following are some more ways that SLR contributes to the improvement of communication:

+   • It lessens the level of frustration felt by members of the hand-talk community.
+   • The elimination of the communication barrier caused by SLR results in improved communication.
