An approach with completely distributed cooperation has been put forward in [17] in which the UAVs match in terms of participating double detector compliances. Here, the system considers a realistic detector model for both types of crimes; however, limitations in dispatches are not included.

In [18], Hu et al. devised a multi-UAV collaborative approach which uses both communication and seeing limitations. This approach also focuses on agreement among the UAVs in order to maintain analogous charts about each robot with a limited number of compliances.
