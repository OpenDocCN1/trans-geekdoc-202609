The second significant contribution to ML came from Arthur Samuel, who developed the first game of checkers and implemented machine learning for the first time while developing checkers [15, 16]. The learning algorithm of the game of checkers was used to find the moves responsible for the winning strategies, and subsequently, the computer improved the game by determining those strategies and incorporating them into the game, which was developed in 1952 [17, 18].

Later, Frank Rosenblatt worked on simulating human neurons for the thought process of the human brain and designed neural networks for the first time in 1957. This was a breakthrough in the field of ML and made many things easier and possible, especially in deep learning, image processing, NLP, sequence learning, and other applications.

In 1974, backpropagation was introduced to aid neural networks in learning to recognize patterns. It has also been used in various machine learning applications, such as performance optimization and generalizing from datasets to new instances. Backpropagation aims to increase a model's accuracy by adjusting its weights to predict future outputs more accurately.The nearest neighbor algorithm was written in 1967 and was initially used for recognizing similar patterns and later used to find paths for the traveling salesman problem. Nowadays, it is widely used for classification problems using KNN.
