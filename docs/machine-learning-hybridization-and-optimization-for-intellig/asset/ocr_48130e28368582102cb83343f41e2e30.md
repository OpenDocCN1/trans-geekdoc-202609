# 5.1 INTRODUCTION

The lists of pending cases are huge in number in Indian courts, out of which some have remained pending for more than decades. These pending cases will affect the efficiency of courts and will ultimately slow down the process of giving justice. The study of National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG) results shows 3,89,41,148 district level cases, and 58,43,113 Taluk level cases are still pending. These pending cases reduce people's access to justice and are not handled effectively. The delay in our justice delivery system may give enough time for the perpetrators to erase all evidences because of its slow process of analyzing cases.

Fortunately, the foremost top courts have already started using e-filing, and most court proceedings are conducted through online video conferencing. Artificial intelligence (AI) and newer technologies like transfer learning can now help in reducing the number of pending cases significantly. Therefore proposing any innovative approach towards digitization of law processes will facilitate the speeding up of the justice delivery process. Therefore it is understood that AI will impact the coming two or three decades and will influence the Indian judiciary, which has a largely unstructured dataset.
