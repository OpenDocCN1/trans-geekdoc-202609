Liang et al. [18] developed a model that combined residual thought. This was done as a measure to counter overfitting and problems such as degradation. This approach provided accuracy and F1 scores of 96.7% and 92.7%. Luján-García et al. [19] proposed a model that applies transfer learning by using the weights of a pre-trained model. For this purpose, they used the Xception network weights, using which they obtained an accuracy score of 84% and an F1 precision score of 91%. Manickam et al. [20] proposed a deep learning-oriented solution with U-Net architecture-based segmentation, and classification was done with pre-trained models such as ResNet50, InceptionV3, and InceptionResNetV2, with the ResNet50 model achieving the highest accuracy score of 93.06% against other models.

When consulting recent literature works having common features, Showkat et al. [21] designed a twofold study wherein they assessed the performance of ResNet models. They were applied on chest X-ray images requiring classification. They also built a custom ResNet model to compare its performance, and the latter model was able to achieve accuracy. The metrics they used were specificity and sensitivity. The findings for the same were 95.65%, 92.74%, and 92.95%, respectively. Alharbi et al. [22] used a fusion model approach, wherein image segmentation and machine learning models were combined to achieve an improved version of the model. They proposed an improved BoxENet model by integrating ImgNet and SqueezeNet’s transfer learning methodologies. The proposed BoxENet model outperforms other models when compared against execution time, binary, and multi-classification models. Avola et al. [23] explored the effectiveness of established neural network models through the transfer learning paradigm by exploring the 12 known pre-trained ImageNet models for classification tasks of the 6,330 images. Most models reached an average F1 score of 84.46%. Sardar et al. [24] designed a novel MapReduce-based fuzzy C-Medoids clustering algorithm. This proved extremely vital in efficient data preprocessing and feature selection. Jayakanthan et al. [25] proposed a novel architecture with the aid of separable convolutions, residual connection, and residual attention learning.
