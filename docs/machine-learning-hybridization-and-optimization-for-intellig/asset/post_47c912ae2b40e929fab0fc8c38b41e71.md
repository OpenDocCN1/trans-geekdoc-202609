### 10.1 INTRODUCTION

Land use and land cover (LULC) describes the different land features that exist on the surface of the earth. Land use refers to how the land is being utilized for various purposes, such as agricultural areas, industrial areas, and residential areas, whereas land cover refers to the actual forms of land, such as towns and built-up regions, forests, water bodies, and grasslands [1]. Understanding LULC at regional and global levels makes it possible to investigate a range of earth-affecting phenomena, including degradation, migration, climate change, and storms. Land use and cover are a direct or indirect indicator of underlying patterns in various ecological and social phenomena [2].

Evidence from LULC is vital to the comprehension of subjects ranging from climate change to policy planning.
