Recurrent neural networks (RNNs) are efficient architectures for natural language processing (NLP) tasks such as language translation, question and answer functions, image captioning, and video captioning. Although simple or “vanilla” RNNs can take as many time steps as needed, in order to remember dependencies over a longer period of time – say, dozens or even hundreds of time steps – a special sort of RNN called long short-term memory (LSTM) can be used. Gated recurrent units (GRUs), a mild variant of the LSTM, have fewer gates and no memory cells.
