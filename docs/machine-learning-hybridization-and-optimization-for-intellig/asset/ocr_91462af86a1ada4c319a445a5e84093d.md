# 8.4.4.2 Insufficient Quality

The materials provided by certain manufacturers do not conform to international quality standards and are compromised for cost reduction. This reduces their market potential and jeopardizes the reputation of Indian products globally.

# 8.4.5 REMOTE TRAINING

IoT can assist military staff in preparing for genuine combat zone battles. Development sensors, acoustic sensors, and more can monitor the staff during readiness or practice and send information and insights to the mentors who prepare them. A remote-controlled weapon station (RCWS) or remote weapon station (RWS), also known as a remote weapon framework (RWF), is a remotely controlled robust framework often equipped with a shoot control system for light- and medium-type weapons, which can be installed on ground combat vehicles or sea- and air-based combat platforms. Such equipment is used on modern military vehicles as it allows a heavy weapon specialist to remain within the overall security of the vehicle.

# 8.4.5.1 Budgetary Issues

A lack of capital expenditure on defense manufacturing and research and development has been a significant challenge to India's defense objectives. While India's defense budget has increased in the long term, a significant portion of it is spent on personnel costs such as salaries and pensions, thus reducing the funds available for defense production. Additionally, of the total defense budget for 2020–2021, 58.6% was allocated to salaries, pensions, and salaries, while only 22.7% had been allocated for capital expenditure. Furthermore, India's budget allocation for research and development was only 4% of the entire defense budget for 2020–2021.
