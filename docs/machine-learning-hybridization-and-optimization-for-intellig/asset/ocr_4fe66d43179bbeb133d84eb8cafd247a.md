Remote sensing is a field of science that uses technology to assess an object’s or a surface’s characteristics from a distance. RS data are data that are continuously collected from sensors in space and the air, as well as certain additional data-gathering measurements taken around the earth [39, 40, 41, 42]. The exponential growth in data volume and the escalating degrees of diversity and complexity have led to the term big data for remotely sensed (RS) data. The majority of the initial efforts have been focused on defining and discussing the area of big data, however, as it is still a relatively new idea [12]. Big data has completely transformed how issues are approached. It is a big collection of datasets in which the volume and rate of data are at a scale that is significantly greater than the most sophisticated systems. It is also feasible to identify what RS big data genuinely employs in the domains of remote sensing and earth sciences. For the RS, big data, as the name suggests, refers to the variety and intricate nature of the data as well as the quantity and velocity of the data, which are greater than what can be stored and computed [13]. The enormous volume and velocity of RS data, its diversity, and its intricacy, specifically its increasing dimensionality, are some of the characteristics and aspects of RS big data that need to be examined [14].

The contemporary era that we currently reside in is the era of big data. Terabytes, petabytes, and even exabytes are the sizes of the geographic data that have been collected or stored, including remote sensing data [43, 44, 45]. Numerous freely accessible remote sensing datasets, as well as other geographic information system (GIS) data, are made available by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA), the United States Geological Survey (USGS), the European Space Agency (ESA), and the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) and can be used for LULC mapping. Currently, there is a vast amount of geographic location information produced by space organizations for LULC mapping. Improved LULC maps can be created to achieve advancements in big data’s era of data availability and algorithm development.
