The method proposed in [12] uses a disentanglement technique for lesion classification. This technique combines the specific representation of one image with the unspecified representation of another image. This combination produces a new sample for classification among different variants of specific classes. The authors in [26] have introduced two advanced deep CNN based networks to detect the risk of cardiovascular disease, and the model is trained on coronary calcium CT datasets. The proposed two models adapt the multitask CNN model with a median filtering technique. This efficiently classifies epicardial tissues and segments thoracic tissues. A novel CNN model [105] that uses a combination of distance regression has been proposed along with a fusion scheme method. This will learn specific features from 6,000 2DCT and MRI kidney datasets. The authors in [73] have introduced an efficient multistage CNN model for classifying instances of gland adenocarcinoma. The model proposed is a multitask algorithm and is validated on the MICCA1 dataset. This proposed work yields better F1 score values with an accurate measure of index dice.

The effectiveness of deep neural networks has been established in numerous applications [13,69,131] connecting the lungs, liver, breast, kidney, and so on, for example, classifying liver cancer, segmenting liver tumors and so on. A new data augmentation approach has been introduced by the authors in [40] to reconstruct super-resolution slices of liver tumors in CT using multiframe technique. The method proposed [18] employs a supervised pattern classification technique for classifying liver, breast, and blood neoplasias to diagnose tumors based on machine learning methods.
