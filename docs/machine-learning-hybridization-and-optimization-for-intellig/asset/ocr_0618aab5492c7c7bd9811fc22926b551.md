# ## 3.1 INTRODUCTION

The COVID-19 pandemic originated in Wuhan, China, and its first case was registered at the end of December 2019. The disease, which was initially thought to be the common flu, has become a human-killing machine that has spread across the globe and has caused an economic crash to a level that had never before been witnessed in human history. COVID-19, also named SARS-COV-2 by the World Health Organization (WHO), is a communicable disease, which means that people must stay apart from each other to stop its spread. The disease has diverse effects on the human body, initially starting with the symptoms of the common cold and headaches to the adverse effects of pneumonia and acute respiratory diseases. One of the major issues is that there could be cases that might be asymptomatic, which means a person could have COVID-19 and yet still not know that he has been infected by it, which could lead to an even faster spread of the virus among others.
