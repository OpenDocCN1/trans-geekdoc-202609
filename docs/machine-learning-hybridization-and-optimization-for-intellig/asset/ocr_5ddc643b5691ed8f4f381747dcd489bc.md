From the literatures [8, 9, 10, 11], it is seen that most NFTs are used for economic analysis and hardly used for boosting the gaming potential. This is because of the following reasons:

+   - The gaming community and developers have taken an intense interest in NFTs. Many games use these systems to keep track of who own what, power economies, and offer other benefits to their players [12].
+   - In many generic games, you may stock up on various weapons, armor, and other accessories. If, on the other hand, the object was an NFT, the player may get his or her money back by selling it when it is no longer needed. If the item’s worth rises over time, the player may even make a profit [13]. In addition to helping players, this technique also helps developers in several ways.
+   - The developers of an NFT also get a royalty from each and every sale made of that NFT. As a consequence, both players and developers may benefit from the growing intermediate NFT market [14]. This creates a more mutually advantageous economic structure. This also implies that players retain ownership of their in-game property even if the game’s makers stop providing updates and support.
+   - While NFTs have many potential applications, privacy and security concerns are among the most pressing threats to consider. When doing business, you should assume that any and all information pertaining to your transaction may be intercepted while being sent over the Internet [15].
+   - NFTs are not only applicable to digital assets; they may be used for real pieces of art as well, facilitating the same kind of asset exchange that is possible with digital ones. NFTs, which may be of many different media types, can be bought and sold on a wide variety of marketplaces.

In addition, NFTs have found applications in many other fields, such as education, where they are used for licensing and certification; the fashion industry, where they are used to differentiate between items; and the sports industry, where they have created a new revenue stream via basketball card NFTs. There are a number of problems that arise from the widespread adoption of NFTs, such as the absence of industry-wide security standards for smart contracts, the fuzziness of intellectual property rights, the risk of fraud through artist impersonation, the transparency that violates user security and privacy, and the severe negative environmental effects that result from NFTs’ heavy energy consumption.
