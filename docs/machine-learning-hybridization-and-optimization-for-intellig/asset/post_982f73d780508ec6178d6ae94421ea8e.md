### 14.1.1 Related Works

To develop a system for recognizing digits written by hand, deep learning networks are used. The CNN classifier’s superiority over neural networks was discussed at length in [5]. This was accomplished with much enhanced computational efficacy and unrelenting execution, trained on the MNIST dataset, which includes over 70,000 handwritten picture examples. Furthermore, there are libraries like NumPy, Pandas, TensorFlow, and Keras. Importing photographs, adjusting their size and color, creating a visual representation of the data, and then translating the categories into numbers are all part of the preprocessing phase. In order to obtain the image’s input characteristics, a simple activation function was used in the convolution layer. Finally, the written digit was viewed and predicted by the CNN model.

Machine learning for recognizing handwritten digits was demonstrated in [6]. It was decided to adopt a six-step process, the first of which was picture capture. The pictures in this case were derived using a training dataset that included the document’s scanned form. In the second phase, preprocessing techniques take center stage; here, we primarily focused on noise reduction and also performed skew correction, normalization, and binarization. The next phase is segmentation, which involves breaking the entity down into smaller, more manageable pieces. The requested work was resolved for segmentation using projection approaches, both vertically and horizontally. Then, the support vector machine (SVM) technique was used for classifying data, and the convex hull approach was used to extract features. Finally, post-processing comes into play when improved accuracy is achieved via the use of various methods.

In the literature [7], the different approaches to handwritten character identification up to this point are discussed and compared in terms of their benefits, drawbacks, and performances. The three-layer ANN network was suggested in [8] where the input sample and feature vectors are both represented as bitmaps and back-propagation bounce-back accuracy, with the unseen test picture increased to over 95% after the introduction of artificial neural network training, testing, and a scaled conjugate gradient. Also, in the literature [9], the creation of a smartphone app that uses character recognition to decipher written material embedded in a picture is proposed. It took a picture of the document with the phone’s camera, and OCR took it from there. Input data supplied to the system determines the final product. At first, the photograph is taken and uploaded to the custom Android app. The OCR engine does the further processing. For your convenience, the on-screen text transformation is automatically recorded as text that may be modified and used again. The method is predicted to provide 90% accuracy, and it was found that printed text provided more accuracy than handwritten text.

### 14.1.2 SIGNIFICANT CONTRIBUTIONS OF THE PRESENT STUDY

In this chapter, convolutional neural networks (CNNs) are developed to process pixel data. The major function of this CNN in artificial neural networks is to detect the image and process it [10]. There are three major layers used in CNN, which include the convolutional, pooling, and fully connected layers. The CNN functions in the pattern detection of the input, such as lines, faces, and gradients [11]. These neural networks are extremely effective for computer vision due to their quality and ease of accessibility. By using CNN architecture, the accuracy of the digit recognition is received at 80% [12], and in literature [13], the loss was reduced approximately to 0.02%. To add on, the use of the Adam optimizer for the MNIST database yielded better recognition of classification accuracy [14]. Further, a minimal fine-tuning of hyper-parameters proved essential in improving the performance of the CNN architecture. A hybrid deep learner, namely the CNN-SVM model [15], could recognize handwritten digits by automatic feature generation with higher accuracy. The CNN was used as a generative model, while the SVM was used as the classifier. The recognition of handwritten digits is well achieved by combining the advantages of CNN and SVM classifiers. In addition to this, instance-based learning [16] leads to higher accuracy by virtue of the nature of the k-nearest neighbor (k-NN) model and the random forest (RF) model. It is proven that the hybrid deep learning CNN-ELM-DL4J model [17] is better than conventional CNN models in terms of recognition accuracy and computational time. It is concluded that adding more hidden layers led to an elevation in network complexity and computational time. RTL coding is also observed to accelerate CNN on the FPGA platform [18].

Research was carried out to develop a successful methodology for handwritten character recognition. As a result, this deep learning model accepts or outputs data in sequences such as audio and video clips, text streams, and time-series data. In neural networks, particularly the CNNs and multilayer perceptron, apart from the model, the activation function plays a significant role in deciding and achieving the output. The SoftMax function is one of the most frequently used activation functions in neural network models that apply machine learning methods. A vector of numbers is transformed into a vector of probabilities via this function, where the probability of each value is inversely proportional to the comparative scale of each rate in the vector [19]. There is another observation in this method that the final value always equals to one when summed. ReLU is the next most popular activation function in deep learning models. This is a nonlinear or piecewise linear function that produces an output that is directly the input, if the input is positive, and gives a zero function if the input is negative.
