# 11.1 INTRODUCTION

Long distances are covered by power transmission lines in order to deliver power loads to consumers. Overhead power lines (OPLs) are often distributed worldwide. Overhead power line conductors do not have insulation and must be supported on poles or towers so that current does not flow to earth (i.e., the conductors must be properly insulated from the supports). An insulator is a nonconducting device used to support and protect electrical conductors from the ground or other conductors. The images captured by UAV will have a complex background, and it is difficult to perform by robot and manually as well as being a critical and time-consuming process. OPL images with noisy backgrounds like insulators require accurate identification and segmentation. Damages to OPL components may result in environmental disasters. Overhead power line inspection has therefore been the subject of several scientific studies, including helicopter inspections and climbing robot inspections, but no comprehensive review of all possible approaches has been published, apart from a brief technical review that deals exclusively with aerial inspections. Critically evaluating solutions and identifying problems that need to be solved efficiently are some of the tasks performed.
