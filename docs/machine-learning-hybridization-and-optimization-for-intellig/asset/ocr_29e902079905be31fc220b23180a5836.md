Blockchain technology serves as the foundation for the creation of NFT, a video game where the uniqueness of things is tracked [2]. In fact, that is the fundamental idea of nonfictional games. You may get money by selling rare items in the game's marketplace. Financial incentives have replaced just competitive ones [3]. As an example, say your goal is to equip your favorite character's armor. This is a game-exclusive armor that can only be purchased during a special event. You may, of course, get a replica; nevertheless, its worth will not be the same as that of the original. The assumption that they have such worth to the people who typically pay the prices gives NFT things their value. The potential for making money in the blockchain and cryptocurrency businesses is large since these sectors of the global economy are expanding rapidly [4]. NFT games are a cutting-edge method of advertising your business's offerings, bringing in more gamers and more money at the same time. One measure of a game's worth is the number of people who play it on a regular basis. Thus, NFT options improve the overall quality of the game by enticing more participants with more engaging choices [5]. It was the Ethereum-based game CryptoKitties that initially brought NFT goods to light in late 2017 [6], but the advent of COVID-19 is widely credited for fueling the market's meteoric rise since then. In the wake of the World Health Organization's declaration of a global pandemic, existing research has indicated that the bitcoin market liquidity surged dramatically [7]. With global interest rates falling dramatically, investors flocked to the cryptocurrency markets, particularly NFT [8, 9]. The lockdown procedures used during the pandemic significantly increased digital participation and further drove the expansion of NFT. Investors and amateurs alike have taken notice of NFT, and now academics are starting to take an interest as well. Though there is a lot written about Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies, there isn't nearly as much study done on NFT [10]. NFT is still in its infancy, which means that there is immense promise but also ambiguity, and not enough study material or data to conduct a huge number of investigations. Another reason is that NFT is interdisciplinary in nature. Research in this area necessitates that academics have a breadth of knowledge and expertise across several fields.
