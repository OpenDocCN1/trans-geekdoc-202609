Undoubtedly AI/ML technology plays a major role in aiding lawyers and judges to make innovative decisions. But, in the near future, increased use of AI technologies will however pay the way for active lawyering. Though traditional algorithms are already good for case management and diligence, AI-based intelligent tools like Kiara and Luminous are considered far better. But these elementary tools are in turn requiring several years to mature as reliable technologies in operation.

While the existing projects are just an initiative taken to increase the transparency and accountability of court systems, there is no assurance that this can represent a solution to the problem of holding cases in the justice delivery system. This needs to be supported by a consistent, solid approach by providing timely appointments for judgments and successfully aiding in setting up the ICT basic infrastructure in courts.
