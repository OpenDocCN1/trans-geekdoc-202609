Machine learning, on the other hand, is a subfield of artificial intelligence that enables computers to learn on their own from their past knowledge and data [28, 29]. The term _big data_ refers to enormous or abundant data that is challenging to keep and that typical database systems cannot handle manually [7]. It consists of both structured and unstructured data [30]. By using statistical data, computers can learn on their own, thanks to a new technique called machine learning. Machine learning uses a variety of approaches to build mathematical models and predict outcomes based on previously acquired data or information. Speech recognition, recommender systems, email filtering, Facebook auto-tagging, and image identification are just a few of the many modern uses for it [8].

Due to the overwhelming volume of remote sensing data they must deal with, large remote sensing applications are unquestionably recognized as typical data-intensive challenges [10]. The massive data proliferation has made it extremely difficult to handle, analyze, and comprehend these RS big data.
