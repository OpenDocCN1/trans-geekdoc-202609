# 8.6 SECURITY CHALLENGES IN APPLICATIONS OF BANKING AND FINANCE USING IOT

# 8.6.1 ATMs

IoT allows an information stream for ATMs to allow banks to screen user actions, recognize ATM use patterns, and choose where to put new ATMs based on demand. Changing to cost-saving operational phases requires data on the ATM site climate, such as room temperature, light, and movement, from IoT sensors. Adapting air-conditioning and lighting to ATM site activity allows for lower power consumption. IoT solutions assist in the management of events, such as the use of skimming devices, card reader issues, and cash shortages, by ensuring continuous monitoring of ATM operations and cash levels.

As technology drives the financial industry forward, new regulations are put in place to ensure that innovation does not come at the expense of user security. These regulations cover various potential issues, including data security and user privacy. As these regulations become more complex and comprehensive, banks must increase their expenditure on compliance to ensure that they can keep up.
