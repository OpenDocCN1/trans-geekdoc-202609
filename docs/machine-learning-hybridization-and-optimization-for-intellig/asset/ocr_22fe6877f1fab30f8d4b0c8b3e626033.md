The Landsat 8 OLI-Thermal Infrared Sensor (TIRS) data collection, path 143, and row 51 were used to produce the 2018 satellite image. The land cover classifications in the research area were examined and identified using Landsat imagery from various periods. A supervised classification method called maximum likelihood classifier (MLC) was used in the present study. The user accuracy for three categories (water bodies, developed terrain, and forest) was greater than 80%. The user accuracies in the barren and agricultural land were 68.25% and 77.77%, respectively.

Andrea et al. [8] performed a test on object-oriented classification using the combination of a simple non-iterative clustering (SNIC) algorithm and a gray-level co-occurrence matrix (GLCM) method. The machine learning techniques random forest (RF) and support vector machine (SVM) are combined. In order to perform object-oriented classification, change different parameters (such as choosing the input bands and classification algorithm, and testing various segmentation scales), and compare it to a pixel-based approach; the proposed method is implemented in a user-friendly, open-source Google Earth Engine code. Data from Landsat 8 were used in a 154 km² study area close to Lake Trasimeno (middle Italy) to carefully evaluate the proposed methodology. Various experiments on various datasets in the research region produced intriguing results.

A technique to examine the land use and land cover mapping of Shiraz, Iran, using machine learning classifiers has been developed by Jamali et al. [9]. It is important to note that recent droughts brought on by climate change have caused people to move from rural to urban areas. Iran’s population growth has, nevertheless, caused the rate of urbanization to increase. Two well-known machine learning models, complex tree (CTree) and support vector machine (SVM), were used to map the land cover in this work. Three separate scenarios using Landsat 8 images with spatial resolutions of 30 m and 15 m are established in order to examine the influence of data preprocessing on the final expected LULC maps.
