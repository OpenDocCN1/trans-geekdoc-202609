# 16.1 INTRODUCTION

NFTs, short for “non-fungible tokens,” are digital assets that stand in for other forms of intellectual property or creative works such as songs, paintings, games, gifs, and videos. Each NFT token is a one-of-a-kind entity representing a single thing, thus the term “non-fungible.” Tokens cannot be traded for one another. These tokens are pieces of digital information – music, video, or images – whose worth may be measured in cryptocurrency. Although both NFTs and Ethereum coins are technically a part of the Ethereum blockchain, the former are fungible, meaning they may be traded for other cryptocurrencies with the same characteristics. Basically, NFT games are the same as any other kind of video game. Usually, obtaining new levels or winning a battle is the only way to get new things or money in older video games [1]. However, these rewards remain in-game, and the user is never kicked out. The NFT games reported in [1] are different from the norm since players may move their cash from one game to another or even trade with other players using cryptocurrencies.
