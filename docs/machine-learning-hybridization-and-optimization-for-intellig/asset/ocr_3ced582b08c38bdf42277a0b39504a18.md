The ECG pulse is generated by the electrical movement of the heart. When electrical activity from the SA node to the Purkinje fibers is maintained in a coordinated way, the heart pumps normally and produces the ECG waveform. The typical ECG waveform is shown in Figure 9.1, labeled with the components of the ECG wave: 1) P wave, 2) QRS complex, 3) T wave, and 4) U wave. Each component of the ECG wave has its own characteristics in time, which are documented based on various trials.
