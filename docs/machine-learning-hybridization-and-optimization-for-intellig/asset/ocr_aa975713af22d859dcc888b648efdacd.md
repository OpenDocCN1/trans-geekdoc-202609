Coronavirus, as we know, has been widespread, but before we get into its prediction, let us talk about how this pandemic occurred and changed everyone’s lives. So, before we look into how it has spread, let’s discuss the various factors affected by COVID-19, the precautions taken against it, and so on. We have to take a deeper look into how the virus originated so that we get a better idea about how to proceed. The current pandemic is caused by a coronavirus known as SARS-COV-2. Coronavirus is a large group of viruses that causes various respiratory diseases, some of which are serious, like Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS) or Middle East Respiratory Syndrome (MERS), which are known for high mortality rates and were discovered in 2003 and 2012. Coronavirus comes under four different viruses: alpha, beta, gamma, and delta. The coronavirus which is known to infect humans was in the category of alpha and beta. The coronavirus spreads among animals as well, causing respiratory diseases, but the ones which transmit between animals and humans are called zoonotic viruses and the event is known as a spill-over event.
