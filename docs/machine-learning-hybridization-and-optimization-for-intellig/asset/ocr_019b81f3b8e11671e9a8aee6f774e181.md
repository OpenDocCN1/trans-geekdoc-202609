# 5.2.1 SIMILAR GLOBAL INITIATIVES TAKEN BY FOREIGN NATIONS

Technological advancements and tools are already very popular, becoming integral to justice delivery systems. Several countries like the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, Singapore, and China have taken initiatives in the digitization process. For instance, an AI-based ‘Safety Assessment’ (PSA) tool in US courts helps judges decide the judgment based on the risk score. This risk score is calculated based on different predetermined parameters.

The SOS (system of systems) tool is designed to facilitate smart courts; powered by advanced ML technologies, it is being used in China at various locations. This system will act as a centralized system. It connects the functioning of all systems in courts across the country.
