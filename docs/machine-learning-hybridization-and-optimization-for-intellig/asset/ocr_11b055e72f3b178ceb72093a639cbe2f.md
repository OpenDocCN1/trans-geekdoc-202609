A method is proposed in [8] for detecting missing insulator caps in glass and porcelain insulators due to the prevalence of missing insulator caps. In order to generate candidate boxes, first similar characteristic regions are extracted from inspection images based on their grayscale and color characteristics. In order to identify and locate the insulators, these boxes capture images that are fed into an SVM classifier. A classifier with greater than 90% accuracy, recall, and average accuracy has been developed in [8].
