The network branches' local and global characteristics are incorporated in their established framework. To anticipate pneumonia, in order to pinpoint the part of the image that needs to be analyzed, heat maps were used. Xu et al. [8] suggested a solution that applied hierarchical CNN. The loss function they applied was sine-loss, which gave them good results. The outcomes showed that the deep learning-based model performs better than several traditional machine learning techniques [9, 10].

Hashmi et al. [14] proposed a model that applied an ensemble learning approach. They designed a classifier that applied various deep learning models, and, based on weights given to the various models, the output was determined. The models they applied were Resnet18, InceptionV3, Xception, DenseNet121, and MobileNetV3. This approach achieved an accuracy and AUC score of 98.4% and 99.76% on the validation data, respectively.
