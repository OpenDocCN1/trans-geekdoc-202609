# ## 19.4 DEEP LEARNING AND ONWARDS

In 2006, deep learning was introduced to achieve more accurate results using neural networks. Geoffrey Hinton published "A Fast Learning Algorithm for Deep Belief Nets," the first paper on deep learning. In this paper, he demonstrated how a deep belief network could be used to train a computer to recognize patterns in photos.

The concept of deep neural networks (DNN) was introduced in 2011 by a group of researchers who were inspired by Hinton's work. DNNs were capable of being trained on millions of examples and brought significant advancements to image processing, speech recognition, and computer vision.In 2011, Google Brain, a research team focused on AI and machine learning, was established by Google.
