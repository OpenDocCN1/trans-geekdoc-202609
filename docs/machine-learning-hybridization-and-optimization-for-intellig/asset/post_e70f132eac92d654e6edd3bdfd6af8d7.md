- Since the market for NFT games is underdeveloped, you will not have to deal with any intense rivalry. It does not take much time at all to get to the top as a top-tier NFT game developer.

- The scalability of NFT games is higher than that of conventional video games. Developers can cement their presence in the business by providing NFT gamers with fresh, engaging games that enable them to earn and invest in Bitcoin.

- Unlike the gaming businesses of the past, today's credible and experienced NFT gaming firms adhere to a community-first strategy. The game is meant to allow users to invite their actual friends, engage with other players, and create relationships.

- Interoperability – NFT games provide the mobility of assets. This implies that weapons and avatars may be transferred across games without losing ownership information using the Metaverse’s interoperability features.

- The vast majority of well-known and established Bitcoin-based games and activities (now numbering in the hundreds) are very transparent. This decreases the likelihood of cheating by both gamers and developers.

- NFTs gained during gaming are tradeable in-game and on the NFT trading platform. For instance, uncommon weapons in first-person shooters, player cards in soccer games, and RPG equipment have value outside of the game in which they are utilized. Developers get feedback that is functional. Given that the internal economics of NFT games are directly dependent on user activity, the designers of such projects pay close attention to user requirements and modify the smart contract protocol as quickly as feasible.

- Some cryptocurrency games offer decentralized governance frameworks in which holders of NFTs may vote on proposed modifications. Hours of marketing are the most apparent benefit. Almost every activity in NFT games is rewarded monetarily, which is what makes them so appealing.

There are a few shortcomings in the proposed gaming design which is left as a research gap for the future researchers:

- The design is often made by NFT admirers in general, rather than by notable game designers. The majority resemble web games from the previous decade. As a consequence, such endeavors should not be anticipated to provide stories or gameplay discoveries, although this issue is being addressed gradually and more serious firms are focusing on game creation.

- Most current NFT games use the Ethereum blockchain, which is notorious for its sluggish and expensive transactions. This has a significant effect not just on in-game action but also on the purchase and sale of unique things outside the game. There are alternatives, but there are currently relatively few.

- Numerous instances of NFT digital artwork that have sold for hundreds of thousands to millions of dollars demonstrate the profitability of investing in these digital assets. Experts are speculating that the phenomenon will ultimately explode, similar to the dot-com bubble in the late 1990s and other such occurrences. Certain NFTs have shown their worth and growth potential. Nonetheless, it is essential to realize that not all digital assets are valuable. NFT investments are still susceptible to overspeculation and crowdsourcing.

- There are environmental issues associated with blockchain technology. The underlying blockchain technology that produces NFTs has a large environmental effect, which is a key disadvantage of NFTs. Similar to the disadvantages of cryptocurrency, tokenizing digital data requires a substantial amount of processing resources. The energy usage of the computers, servers, and cooling systems required to mine Bitcoin and power the system is comparable to a million transatlantic trips. More generally, expanding the usage of NFTs will raise the energy requirements of blockchain technology. Recall that fossil fuels are still used to create power on a global scale. The emission of greenhouse gases from the combustion of fossil fuels is a key factor in the current climate crisis. Additionally, renewable technologies have environmental costs. NFT essentially inherits the flaws of blockchain technology.

## 16.5 CONCLUSION

NFT games are challenging the status quo in multiple ways with regard to our perception of games and the notion that they are merely “leisure activities.” NFTs have made it possible for millions of people, particularly in developing nations where scores of dollars can go a long way, to incorporate gaming into their daily lives. In the typical centralized gaming model, users must pay for the game, frequently paying a subscription fee to continue playing or to remain online, as well as purchasing weapon bundles, skins, and so on to enhance their gaming experience. Having to spend more money on in-game assets can add up to hundreds, if not thousands, of dollars for long-term players. If a player departs, there is no way to recover your investment; it will go directly to the stockholders. The proposed design for NFT games is similarly promising for the gaming industry as all other blockchain technologies have been for other industries. They invert the conventional approach by providing greater control and incentives to the system’s constituents as opposed to the central authorities. You can anticipate leaving the table with more money than you brought.

## 16.6. FUTURE SCOPE

NFT establishes a flourishing ecosystem by having uncontested ownership of all player-created and player-purchased content in the game. So, at the end of the day, players can trade or sell their NFTs using a secure marketplace (skins, buildings, lots, weapons, etc.). This indicates that they will recoup their initial investment in the game. NFTs bring to the gaming industry the same decentralized, transparent, and highly profitable model that cryptocurrencies introduced to the financial sector. Once NFT games match the user experience and gameplay of massively popular multiplayer online role-playing games such as Fortnite, World of Warcraft, and Grand Theft Auto, consumers will prefer to pay for games rather than enjoy free ones in the future. No one ever conceived of the idea. In NFT games, microeconomies are created, allowing participants to earn money while engaging in their preferred activities. It is the dream of every gambler, and depending on the expense of living, it could represent a full-time job, part-time employment, or winning the lottery. In future, the techniques in [16, 17, 18, 19, 20] will be explored to incorporate the ideologies for effective gaming technologies.
