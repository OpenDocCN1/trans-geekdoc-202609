## Glossary

### A

AI A computer science branch, focusing on the creation of intelligent machines that process data and take appropriate decisions by itself.

ACC It is the degree of closeness between a measurement and the true value.

AV The value is obtained by measuring the available data.

Alzheimer's disease A progressive neurodegenerative disorder that affects memory and cognitive functions, often occurring in older individuals.

Analytics – The discovery of insights in data.

Artificial intelligence – Developing intelligence machines and software that are capable of perceiving the environment and that take corresponding action when required and even learn from those actions.

### B

BAG It is an ensemble estimator that fits the base classifier on random subsets and aggregates the predictions to form a final prediction.

BCS A multinational cyber defense exercise.

BDA Examining vast datasets to obtain insights.

BC Collection of quantum benchmark circuit files.

Business intelligence (BI): Using data analysis tools and techniques to gain insights and make data-driven decisions in business.

Big data – Big data is an all-encompassing term for any collection of datasets so large or complex that it becomes difficult to process them using traditional data processing applications.

### C

CA It is the ratio of the number of correct predictions to the total number of input samples.

CCF It is a form of theft that involves unauthorized access of another’s credit card information for the purpose of charging purchases to the account or removing funds from it.

CM It is a performance measurement for machine learning classification problem where output can be two or more classes.

CNNs A special form of neural network design that is excellent for jobs requiring image recognition.

CE The entire impression formed by a customer’s interactions.

CEM Strategies to improve satisfaction with customers and loyalty.

CNOT Fundamental quantum gates.

Conventional software tools: Traditional software tools and technologies.

Cutting-edge technologies: The latest and most advanced technologies.

Computational complexity: The computational resources required to perform a given task.

COVID-19 Coronavirus disease 2019, a highly infectious respiratory disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus that emerged in 2019 and has since become a global pandemic.

Cloud computing – A distributed computing system over a network used for storing data off-premises.

Clustering analysis – The process of identifying objects that are similar to each other and clustering them in order to understand the differences as well as the similarities within the data.

### D

DBNs A special form of neural network design that excels at unsupervised learning tasks like feature extraction and dimensionality reduction.

DL A subset of machine learning, which is essentially a neural network with three or more layers.

DT It is a nonparametric supervised learning algorithm, which is utilized for both classification and regression tasks.

Décor A style or mode of decoration, as of a room, building, or the like.

3D Refers to the three spatial dimensions of width, height, and depth.

3DMAX A computer graphics program for creating 3D models, animations, and digital images.

Digital data: Information that is stored in digital form.
