# 3.2.2 THE SPREAD

The reason for the spread of COVID-19 across the globe was at the time the virus was first discovered, around December; a lot of people from various parts of the globe came to China to welcome and celebrate the new year, which is why Wuhan became a hotspot for the disease. The disease spread among people and, when they flew back home, they became one of the initial cases, which in turn created new hotspots in those countries.

# 3.2.2.1 China

As we mentioned previously, the outbreak of the novel coronavirus was reported on 19 December 2019 at a seafood market in Wuhan, and the first death was reported on 11 January 2020; by this time, cases were being reported across China and one or two cases across the globe. China managed to control the virus the fastest and also most effectively because of the strict restrictions in lockdown, which other countries weren’t able to follow.

# 3.2.2.2 USA

The first case from the USA was registered on 21 January and was found to have travelled to China; this led to the further spread of COVID-19 across the country, making total number of cases in the USA one of the fastest growing and causing several deaths. By the end of July, the USA was at the top in the total number of confirmed cases, and various protests took place during the pandemic, causing an increase in the case count.

# 3.2.2.3 India and Other Countries

This chapter will focus more on the effects COVID had in India and how in that situation, India was the leading country providing the maximum number of positive cases around the globe every day. The first case in India occurred on 27 January 2020 in Kerala. India tried to handle it by initially applying various lockdowns, but due to a financial crisis, the country had to start unlocking, which worked well initially but later led to a second wave.
