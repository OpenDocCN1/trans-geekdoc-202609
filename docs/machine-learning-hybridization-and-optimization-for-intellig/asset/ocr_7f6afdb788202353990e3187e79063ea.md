# ## 8.1 INTRODUCTION

IoT security is one of the best firewalls for the increase in devices that are connected to a network. Devices like laptops perform a wide variety of tasks, such as transmitting data to other devices. The purpose of IoT devices is to establish protocols to secure the devices while sharing information. As a result, IoT devices can develop recognizable and unique patterns for the behavior of those devices. These IoT devices utilize artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques to create distinctive and identifiable patterns for such devices. Once these patterns are established, IoT can establish a baseline between the devices and the network and monitor and detect attacks on the devices. After monitoring such attacks, devices can send alert notifications to the administrator through communication formats such as SMS or device notifications, and these steps are automated by the system [1]. Upon receiving the alert message from the system, the administrator can also receive a sequence of steps to secure the device from those breaches or attacks. Identifiable and unique patterns can be used to reallocate the devices to resume their tasks normally after securing the devices from the attacks. These patterns also serve a crucial function in establishing a firewall between the devices during information sharing or when attacks occur.
