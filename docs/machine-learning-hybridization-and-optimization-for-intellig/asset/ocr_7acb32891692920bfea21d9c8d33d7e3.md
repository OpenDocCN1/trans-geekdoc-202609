One of the ways to find out the origin of the virus is to look at its genetic makeup; the reason to do so is that in the case that there are viruses present of similar genetic makeup, we would be able to restrict the geographic locations of that virus and thereby control its spread. SARS-COV-2 had a genetic makeup similar to SARS-COV, isolated among the bat population, which was the reason why there were initial speculations that zoonotic events could have happened from bats to humans. We did come to know that the zoonotic event occurred but we were not able to find out the first person who got infected by this spill-over; however, the first case of coronavirus was registered on 19 December 2019 in Wuhan, China.

After the first case was registered, the genetic makeup was looked into, which, as mentioned, was similar to SARS-COV originally discovered in 2003 and isolated among bats; however, this virus occurred among bats only in a few places like Asia, Africa, the Middle East, and Europe. The reason why SARS-COV-2 was able to affect humans was that it was able to easily adapt to human cell receptors and exploit the cells.

Once the first case of COVID-19 was detected, investigations began to find the point at which spill-over had occurred so that a minimum amount of geographical restrictions would need to be put into place. As the first case was encountered in Wuhan, China, it was soon found that the epicentre for the virus was the local seafood market called Hunan Wholesale Food Market. The rise in cases from the people in the market, vendors and regular consumers, led to the shutdown of the market; however, it was too late, as the spread of COVID-19 had begun across China at this point. The main issue to date is that no one was able to find who was infected first and how the spill-over occurred, even though the first case was registered around December 2019. Reports are saying that it could be very much possible that the first infected case of COVID-19 was in around November of 2019, which is why it has become really hard to control the virus. Until the source of this virus is identified, there are chances of re-introduction of the virus back to the human population even after passing through this pandemic.
