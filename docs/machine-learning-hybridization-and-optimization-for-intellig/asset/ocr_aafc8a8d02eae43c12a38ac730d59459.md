Plant health is monitored using near-infrared fluorescent H2O2 nano-sensors [9]. Nano letters [5, 6] detail the study of various machine learning models to identify plants. The recognition methodologies include scanning leaf digital fingerprints using laser technology and tracing a plant’s outline using camera technology.

The studies [7, 13, 16] detail the advancement of IoT [35] in the field of agriculture, examining issues and challenges associated with the deployment of IoT in farming. Various case studies are explored for better improvement of farming. The studies [8, 12, 20] are about the identification of disease in crops by computer vision techniques using deep learning methods. A deep convolutional neural network is used to train the public data set consisting of both diseased and non-diseased crop species. The study [10] details the usage of IoT technology for pepper production, proposing the use of a No InfraRed (NoIR) camera and Global Positioning System (GPS) module with Raspberry Pi to analyze the health of black pepper plants and track the coordinate location of the plants. The NoIR camera has a filter on the lens which allows it to use infrared photography and take pictures in low light (twilight) environments. The IoT is designed to transfer data over a network without requiring human-to-human or human-to-computer interaction. The utilization of the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) is included in the prototype to determine the health of pepper plants.

In [14, 15], a system has been proposed to adopt precision agriculture using wireless sensor networks, which enable remote monitoring of soil fertility and other parameters, namely soil moisture, pH, and temperature. This data is transmitted to the cloud, and the corresponding values are displayed on a mobile application. In [11, 22] a Raspberry Pi–based IoT [34] device is proposed which sends images of plants to classify diseases and updates environmental parameters like air temperature, humidity, soil moisture, and pH in a MySQL database in real-time.
