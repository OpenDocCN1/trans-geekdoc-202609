Mahendra et al. [27] have suggested using a deep convolutional neural network to evaluate Mysuru City’s vegetation cover from 2009 to 2019. The authors have performed the LULC image classification for the assessment of LULC class and vegetation cover of Mysuru City. The assessment results of the vegetation cover and other LULC classes in this work will help to carry out the development activity within the city. A convolutional neural network (CNN)-based multiple training round (CNN-MTR) deep learning model has been proposed to classify visuals from imagery collected remotely. The classified findings were examined to determine how the city’s vegetation cover had changed over a 10-year period. According to estimates, the area covered by Mysuru City Corporation’s vegetation in 2019 was expected to be 39.09%, down from 43.32% in 2009. These findings show that Mysuru City’s vegetation cover has declined by 3.43% over the past 10 years. According to estimates, the suggested CNN-MTR model’s overall classification accuracies were 95.20% in 2009 and 94.17% in 2019.
