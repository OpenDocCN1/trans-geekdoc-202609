Sometimes, newbie farmers are not aware of the diseases and their occurrence period. Generally, diseases can occur on any plant at any time [5]. However, continuous monitoring may prevent disease infection. The detection of plant disease is one of the important research topics in the agriculture domain. Detection of various diseases from images is one of the exciting research areas in the agriculture sector. So nowadays, detecting diseases in different plant parts is significant and relevant in our society. Image processing and machine learning provide a variety of techniques for most of the steps in the workflow sequence to analyze images that can be effectively utilized for disease detection in plants [6].

The Rig Vedic hymns mention rice and porridge in hymns such as 8.83, 8.70, 8.77, and 1.61 in some text versions [7]. The Mahabharata mentions a single grain of rice that satisfied the hunger of Durvasa Muni and his thousands of disciples [8]. Rice is an essential staple food in India. In recent years, India has been facing huge losses due to diseases affecting its paddy fields, resulting in poor cultivation annually. There are various rice diseases, but only three types of diseases, namely, bacterial leaf blight, brown spot, and leaf smut, are dealt with here. There are 1,900 images across these three disease classes. The automatic identification and diagnosis of rice diseases are highly desired in agricultural information [9].

Rice is the primary food for nearly half of the world’s population, and it is cultivated almost all over the world, especially in Asian countries [10]. Increasing grain production is essential in those areas where food is scarce. Increasing grain production by controlling crop diseases and pests in time should be effective [11]. Maintaining the quality and volume of rice production depends on the timely and precise identification of rice plant diseases.
