Improved versions of centralized decision-making, like centralized information merging and distributed information merging, can be used to solve even complex problems. In simple distributed decision-making, the UAVs are unable to communicate with each other; hence we have the problem of data redundancy. This also results in overlapping of the paths of the UAVs, thereby reducing the efficiency of the system. However, this problem has been solved in centralized decision-making and distributed decision-making, where information is stored centrally, but the UAVs are free to decide their own paths. Hence, to conclude, depending on the complexity of the environment, we need to choose the appropriate algorithm accordingly.
