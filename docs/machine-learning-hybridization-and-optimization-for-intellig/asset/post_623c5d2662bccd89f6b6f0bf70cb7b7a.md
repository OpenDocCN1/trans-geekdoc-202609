Blockchain technology serves as the foundation for the creation of NFT, a video game where the uniqueness of things is tracked [2]. In fact, that is the fundamental idea of non-fungible games. You may get money by selling rare items in the game's marketplace. Financial incentives have replaced just competitive ones [3]. As an example, say your goal is to equip your favorite character's armor. This is a game-exclusive armor that can only be purchased during a special event. You may, of course, get a replica; nevertheless, its worth will not be the same as that of the original. The assumption that they have such worth to the people who typically pay the prices gives NFT things their value. The potential for making money in the blockchain and cryptocurrency businesses is large since these sectors of the global economy are expanding rapidly [4]. NFT games are a cutting-edge method of advertising your business's offerings, bringing in more gamers and more money at the same time. One measure of a game's worth is the number of people who play it on a regular basis. Thus, NFT options improve the overall quality of the game by enticing more participants with more engaging choices [5]. It was the Ethereum-based game CryptoKitties that initially brought NFT goods to light in late 2017 [6], but the advent of COVID-19 is widely credited for fueling the market's meteoric rise since then. In the wake of the World Health Organization's declaration of a global pandemic, existing research has indicated that the bitcoin market liquidity surged dramatically [7]. With global interest rates falling dramatically, investors flocked to the cryptocurrency markets, particularly NFT [8, 9]. The lockdown procedures used during the pandemic significantly increased digital participation and further drove the expansion of NFT. Investors and amateurs alike have taken notice of NFT, and now academics are starting to take an interest as well. Though there is a lot written about Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies, there isn't nearly as much study done on NFT [10]. NFT is still in its infancy, which means that there is immense promise but also ambiguity, and not enough study material or data to conduct a huge number of investigations. Another reason is that NFT is interdisciplinary in nature. Research in this area necessitates that academics have a breadth of knowledge and expertise across several fields. In the same way as there are three major fields of study into cryptocurrencies, there are three key areas of research into NFT: computer science, economics and finance, and law and other domains [11].

From the literatures [8, 9, 10, 11], it is seen that most NFTs are used for economic analysis and hardly used for boosting the gaming potential. This is because of the following reasons:

- The gaming community and developers have taken an intense interest in NFTs. Many games use these systems to keep track of who own what, power economies, and offer other benefits to their players [12].
- In many generic games, you may stock up on various weapons, armor, and other accessories. If, on the other hand, the object was an NFT, the player may get his or her money back by selling it when it is no longer needed. If the item’s worth rises over time, the player may even make a profit [13]. In addition to helping players, this technique also helps developers in several ways.
- The developers of an NFT also get a royalty from each and every sale made of that NFT. As a consequence, both players and developers may benefit from the growing intermediate NFT market [14]. This creates a more mutually advantageous economic structure. This also implies that players retain ownership of their in-game property even if the game’s makers stop providing updates and support.
- While NFTs have many potential applications, privacy and security concerns are among the most pressing threats to consider. When doing business, you should assume that any and all information pertaining to your transaction may be intercepted while being sent over the Internet [15].
- NFTs are not only applicable to digital assets; they may be used for real pieces of art as well, facilitating the same kind of asset exchange that is possible with digital ones. NFTs, which may be of many different media types, can be bought and sold on a wide variety of marketplaces.

In addition, NFTs have found applications in many other fields, such as education, where they are used for licensing and certification; the fashion industry, where they are used to differentiate between items; and the sports industry, where they have created a new revenue stream via basketball card NFTs. There are a number of problems that arise from the widespread adoption of NFTs, such as the absence of industry-wide security standards for smart contracts, the fuzziness of intellectual property rights, the risk of fraud through artist impersonation, the transparency that violates user security and privacy, and the severe negative environmental effects that result from NFTs’ heavy energy consumption.
