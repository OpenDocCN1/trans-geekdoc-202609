# 10.3 BIG DATA IN REMOTE SENSING

The method of discovering an object’s or surface’s characteristics from a distance is called remote sensing (RS). RS data are measurements of the globe that are continuously collected via sensors in orbit, sensors in the air, and various other methods of data collection. Big data is an enormous collection of datasets whose volume and rate are at a level that is substantially above even the most potent technological breakthroughs, changing the way problems are addressed. Due to the likelihood that it may define what RS big data really is, remote sensing and earth sciences are also impacted. Some of the attributes and characteristics of RS big data that need to be managed include its enormous volume and rate, diversity, and complexity, especially its growing dimensionality [31].

As a result of recent developments in sensors and earth observation methods, we are currently at the beginning of the high-resolution observation period (EOS-4), as illustrated in Figure 10.2. Multi-spatial, multi-temporal RS data are currently being collected from multiple instruments by more than 200 on-orbit satellite sensors. These constant worldwide measurements can span the earth’s atmosphere, surface, and oceans [24].
