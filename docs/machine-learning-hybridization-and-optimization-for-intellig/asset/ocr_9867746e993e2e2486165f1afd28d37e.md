Implementing applications with security and confidentiality capabilities that adhere to the data reduction principle and prioritize data control over data collecting is crucial in IoT. Consequently, there will be a strong justification for developing an IoT standard that will meet a higher standard of security and privacy. Last but not least, security mechanisms must be developed to enable users to secure their private information rather than relying on fully realized elements in IoT frameworks to respect their privacy.
