The objective is to propose a system that can help to reduce the gap between farmers and researchers for the sake of development in the agricultural field. Here, a system that follows image processing and pattern recognition algorithms is proposed to identify the rice disease correctly to help the farmers. For the analysis purposes of the proposed system, image datasets of rice plant diseases are considered, which are publicly available in [14]. Finally, for the improvement of the accuracy of the results for rice disease analysis, a genetic algorithm (GA)-based optimized ensemble architecture is proposed. After the combined application of InceptionV3 and ResNet152V2 model, GA is applied with the use of all GA components. It is observed that the optimized result shows a more accurate result for the prediction of rice disease data.
