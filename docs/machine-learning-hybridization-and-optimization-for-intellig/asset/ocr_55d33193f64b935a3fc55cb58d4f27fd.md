Due to the fact that the NUS I dataset had low levels of segregation between the various classes and the NUS II dataset has extremely complicated backgrounds, it is abundantly clear that the NUS II dataset is of a complex type. Hand coordinates are the most effective characteristic for categorizing images within them, as they represent the location of hand regions in 3D space. In addition, the application of a CNN is preferable to ORB in this scenario, as described by J. Gangrade [19], because, with the aid of convolutional filters, a CNN can identify both the low-level and high-level features required for distinguishing labels of such complex datasets, whereas a representative ORB keypoint of the same features can be the same for multiple classes.
