# **18.3.2 PROPOSED MODEL**

By expressing the retrieved characteristics as a GA chromosome, the evolutionary technique and the dimensionality reduction technique are combined in the GA-based classifier. A feature set is part of the preprocessing. A suitable representation that a genetic algorithm could use must be taken into account. The chromosome is defined for acceptable representation in GA consideration. The appropriate weights are applied to the attributes during the training phase in order to distinguish between the various rice plant illnesses. These weights are employed in the validation stage, and the effectiveness of the GA-based classifier is then assessed
