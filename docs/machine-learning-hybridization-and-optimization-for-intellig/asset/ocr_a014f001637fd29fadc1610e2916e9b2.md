# 8.5 SECURITY BREACHES IN IOT FINANCIAL AND BANKING SECTOR

IoT helps gather data from multiple sources to assist in improving navigation. Many business decisions, including financing decisions, rely on in-depth data analysis, business pattern analysis, and market research. Companies can use IoT devices to collect and analyze a customer's data to gain valuable insights into their needs.

Finance and accounting (F&A) processes involve the collaboration of various departments for the efficient collection of data. Instead of manually collaborating, companies can automate the entire process. As shown in Figure 8.4 , data can be collected and updated in real time using IoT devices [5, 6]. IoT helps track employee and business performance in real time. IoT devices like wearables help track productive hours and also alert you if anything is amiss. It also provides data on whether your devices like ATMs and customer kiosks are operating efficiently.
