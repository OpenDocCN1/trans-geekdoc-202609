# ## 18.1 INTRODUCTION

Agriculture, the main backbone of India, is the development of plants for food, biofuel, medicinal plants, and other products used for sustaining and enhancing individual life [1]. The history of agriculture goes back thousands of years, and its growth has been motivated and defined by several atmospheres, cultures, and technologies. Industrial agriculture founded on large-scale monoculture farming has become the dominant agricultural methodology. In the past century, agriculture has been categorized by improved productivity, substituting synthetic fertilizers and pesticides for water pollution, and farm subsidies. In current years, traditional agriculture's external environmental effects have been removed, resulting in organic and sustainable agriculture movements. Nowadays, plant diseases and land degradation are the most critical problems in agriculture [2]. Therefore, agricultural improvement is monitored by data mining techniques achieved through improved information and communication processes [3].
