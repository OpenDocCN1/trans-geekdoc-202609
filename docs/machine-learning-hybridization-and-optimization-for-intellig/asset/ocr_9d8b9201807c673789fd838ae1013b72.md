# 12.3 EVOLUTION OF THE PATH PLANNING PROBLEM

For humans, getting from one location to the next is a simple undertaking. One makes a decision about how to proceed. Such a simple and straightforward operation is a significant difficulty for robots. Path planning is a crucial challenge in robotic applications [30]. Finding a sensible route for a robot to travel from starting location to a destination position, whether it be a cleaning robot, a mechanical arm, or a mysteriously drone, is indeed a standard challenge. Determining a way from an initial place to a desired location is the issue, taking into account the modeling framework, the sort of robots, the purpose, and so on. This problem was approached in many ways in this study. A path optimization method that is productive is necessary for safe and reliable mobile robot navigation because the integrity [31] of the successful approach has a significant impact on industrial applications.
