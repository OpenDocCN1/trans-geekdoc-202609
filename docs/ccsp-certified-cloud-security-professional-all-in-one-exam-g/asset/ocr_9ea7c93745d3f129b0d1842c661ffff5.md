Domain 5 extensively covers the components and concepts from ITIL, a best of practices for IT service management throughout throughout the IT industry, a comprehensive approach to operations management that can be applied to any application or service. Ten main components are addressed, including ones very familiar to all that work in IT systems and services, such as change management, configuration management, and information security management.

As of operations management, the conducting of risk assessments with cloud components is very important to the cloud customer, and will be a large task of the Cloud Security Professional. This risk assessment process follows through four stages: framing, assessment, responding, and monitoring. Each stage builds in sequence, starting with defining the risk and categories, undertaking the assessment, determining appropriate responses or accepting any risks identified, and then monitoring those risks for future evolution or change in response strategies. Overall, risk can be accepted, avoided, transferred, mitigated, or a combination of approaches.

Another part of operations is the collection and preservation of electronic records for use as digital evidence. These systems and programs need to be an integral part of an operational plan, and not something that only addressed when the need arises. Domain 5 covers these topics from an introductory operational standpoint, which are expanded more from a regulatory standpoint in Domain 6.

Lastly, Domain 5 covers as an important aspect of operations the appropriate communication with relevant stakeholders. The stakeholders include those at all levels of an engagement or contract, including vendors, customers, partners, regulators, and any other potential stakeholders based on the particulars of the data, application, or system. Proper and efficient communication is important for fostering and maintaining strong relationships, and may be specifically required in both frequency and level of detail, as required by contracts, SLAs, or regulations.
