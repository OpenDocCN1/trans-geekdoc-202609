# Questions and Answers

1. Which of the following regulations specifies the length that financial records must be kept?
- A. HIPAA
- B. EU
- C. SOX
- D. Safe Harbor

SOX specifies how long financial records must be kept and preserved, as well as many other regulations for transparency and confidentiality protection. The EU and HIPAA guidelines for European Union privacy protections and healthcare data protection, respectively. The Safe Harbor program is not a series of regulations, but a voluntary program to bridge the gap between rules and laws between the United States and Europe.

2. What type of audit report would be suited for the general public to review to ensure confidence in a system or application?
- A. SAS-70
- B. SOC 1
- C. SOC 2
- D. SOC 3

SOC 3 audit reports are meant for general consumption and to be shared with a wider and open audience. The other types of audit reports listed—SAS-70, SOC 1, and SOC 2—are all restricted audit reports that are only used internally, with current customers, and with regulators.

3. Which of the following would NOT be part of an audit scope statement?
- A. Deliverables
- B. Cost
- C. Certifications
- D. Exclusions

Cost would not be part of an audit scope statement. The audit scope covers the breadth and depth of the audit, as well as the timing and tool sets used to conduct it. Cost is not part of the audit scope at this level, nor part of planning. The deliverables, exclusions, and certifications covered or required are all part of the audit scope.

4. Which of the following would be appropriate to include in an audit restriction?
- A. Time when scans can run
- B. Type of device the auditors use
- C. Length of audit report
- D. Training of auditors

Specifying the time when scans can run is appropriate for an audit restriction, so as to not impact production operations or users. The type of devices the auditors use, the length of the final report, as well as the particular training of the audits would not be part of audit restrictions.

5. What is the correct sequence for audit planning?
- A. Define objectives, define scope, conduct audit, lessons learned
- B. Define scope, conduct audit, prepare report, remediate findings
- C. Conduct audit, prepare report, remediate findings, verify remediation
- D. Define objectives, conduct audit, prepare report, management approval

Define objectives, define scope, conduct audit, lessons learned is the correct sequence for audit planning. The other options have either incorrect choices, choices that are really subsections of real sections, or incorrect ordering.

6. Which of the following is NOT a domain of ISO/IEC 27001:2013?
- A. Personnel
- B. Systems
- C. Network
- D. E-mail

E-mail is not a domain covered under ISO/IEC 27001:2013. The other options—personnel, systems, and network—are all separate and distinct domains under the standard.

7. Which of the following is NOT a specialized regulatory requirement for data?

FIPS 140-2 is a certification and accreditation program for cryptographic modules, not a specialized regulatory requirement for data. HIPAA is for healthcare.
