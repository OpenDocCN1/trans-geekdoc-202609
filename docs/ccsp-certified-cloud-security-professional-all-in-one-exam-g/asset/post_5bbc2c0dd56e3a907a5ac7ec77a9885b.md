Without the proper controls and processes in place, the data could possibly be scrutinized and deemed invalid or inadmissible as evidence. The Cloud Security Professional should always ensure that any collection of data and evidence is limited to the scope of what is requested or required, and not to go beyond that extra data. The cloud customer should never disclose any collection requirements, or activities to any other tenants.
