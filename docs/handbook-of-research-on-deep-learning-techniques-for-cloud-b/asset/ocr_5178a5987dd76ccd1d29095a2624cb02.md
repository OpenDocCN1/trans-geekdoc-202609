# THE PAGE

THE MACALLAN

SINGLE MALT SCOTCH WHISKY

PRODUCT OF SCOTLAND

This is the story of how the Macallan came to be. It is a tale of passion, patience and the pursuit of perfection. From the moment the first spirit touched the oak, a destiny was sealed.

Nestled in the heart of Speyside, Scotland, the Macallan distillery has been crafting exquisite single malt whisky since 1824. The journey from barley to bottle is one of meticulous craft, where every element matters.

The oak casks, sourced from the forests of Spain and America, are the soul of the Macallan. Each cask is seasoned, toasted and charred to extract the perfect balance of flavours.

# THE CASKS

Our commitment to exceptional oak casks is unrivalled. We spend more on oak than any other distillery, ensuring that every drop of the Macallan is imbued with rich, complex character.

> > "The Macallan is not just a whisky, it is an art form." — Michael James, Master Distiller

Sixteen distinct cask types are used in the creation of our whiskies, each contributing unique nuances of vanilla, spice, citrus and dried fruit to the final expression.

# THE MATURATION

Maturation is where the magic happens. For a minimum of twelve years, our whiskies rest in the darkness of our warehouses, slowly drawing character from the wood, until they reach their full potential.

# THE LEGACY

For nearly two centuries, the Macallan has remained true to its founding principles: to craft the world's most precious single malt whisky. It is a legacy carried forward by our craftsmen, whose hands have shaped every bottle.

As you hold a glass of the Macallan, remember that you are holding history. A moment frozen in time, distilled from the heart of Scotland.
