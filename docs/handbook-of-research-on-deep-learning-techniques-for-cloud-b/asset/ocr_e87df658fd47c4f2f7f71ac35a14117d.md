Another challenge is the lack of reliable connectivity in rural areas, where most farms are located. This can hinder the implementation of IoT technologies, as sensors and other devices need a stable internet connection to function correctly. The implementation of IoT in the agricultural sector offers several benefits, however, it also faces various challenges.
