Further examination of the passive methods reveals three subcategories connected to facial recognition: stereo vision, SfS, and SfM. The skull biometric is the least-used biometric system, as it has high complexity in the capturing of a 3D image of a human skull. Its scope is very different than other biometric methods, as it usually involves a cadaver, which is very difficult to recognize by other human body features [10, 18].
