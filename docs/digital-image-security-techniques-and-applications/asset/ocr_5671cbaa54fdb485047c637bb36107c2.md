# 2.2 CLASSICAL CRYPTOGRAPHIC SYSTEM

Information security is more crucial for data storage and transmission today. Images are employed in many procedures since they may convey a lot more info. As a result, picture encryption and decryption algorithms play a crucial role in the security of image data. Digital images are employed in a variety of industries, including the medical, military, and private sectors. In a public setting, when unauthorized users and hostile attackers are present, safe communication between two parties is accomplished using cryptography.
