+   - **Phase 3: Threat Analysis:** In this phase of the CTI life cycle, complete data analysis is performed to discover fundamental answers to the queries made during Phase 1. During this stage, the responsible team translates, transcripts, and interprets the information into final actionable data points and advises valuable propositions to the enterprise.
+   - **Phase 4: Intelligence Usage and Disseminations:** In this phase of the CTI life cycle, the team delivers a report to the stakeholders of the enterprise by conveying their findings of the research simply and conveniently. This report must be in simple language without any technological interpretation and understandable to the layman with brief pictorial representations.
