
# The marketing industry has seen the introduction of a number of innovations

Artificial intelligence is notable for its ability to utilize big data collected from multiple connected devices through Internet of Things (IoT) technology. Blockchain technology provides security and transparency for transactions and data. [Huang & Rust, (2021)](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor128) propose a comprehensive perspective on the role of Artificial Intelligence in marketing. AI has transformed marketing by enabling experts to gather and analyze data to comprehend market trends and consumer behavior ([Campbell et al., 2020](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor115); [Castro et al., 2023](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor116)). [Huang & Rust, (2021)](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor128) and [Stone et al., (2020)](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor170) propose that AI can enhance strategic marketing by enabling effective and relevant marketing actions through segmentation, targeting, and positioning. This facilitates the personalization of messages and offers ([Haleem et al., 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor126)), which has a significant effect on consumers' perceived value and purchase intent ([Yin & Qiu, 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor179)). Artificial intelligence systems can encourage customer interaction and foster future engagement ([Perez-Vega et al., 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor158)).

Several works have demonstrated the growing importance of artificial intelligence and machine learning applications in marketing. [Ma & Sun, (2020)](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor147) emphasize the potential benefits of these methods for enhancing marketing research. ML employs algorithms and statistical models to enable computers to learn and improve from data without explicit programming ([Campbell et al., 2020](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor115)). This approach aims to identify trends to better understand behavior and generate insights for informed decision-making ([Ma & Sun, 2020](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor147)). [De Mauro et al., (2022)](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor119) proposed taxonomy of eleven¹ recurring uses of machine learning in marketing, grouped into four improvement families. “Purchasing principles and consumer experiences” refer to the experiences of consumers, while “decision-making and financial applications” refer to the experiences of businesses. These technologies enable companies to improve customer experience and competitiveness ([Keegan et al., 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor135); [Volkmar et al., 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor176)).

Chatbots and virtual assistants are becoming increasingly popular in marketing and customer service practices ([Ngai et al., 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor154)). These devices utilize artificial intelligence to enhance customer experiences ([Kushwaha et al., 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor142)) by providing round-the-clock support for customer requests, resulting in faster response times and reduced human effort ([Ngai et al., 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor154)). [Selamat & Windasari, (2021)](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor166) found that anthropomorphism, perceived pleasure, and perceived usefulness positively impact customers' purchase intention when using chatbots.

However, some authors have noted the difficulties marketers face with AI errors, as well as the human biases that can compromise their systems ([Volkmar et al., 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor176)). The environmental and ethical impacts of AI use require particular attention ([Huang & Rust, 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor128)).

Augmented reality (AR) ([Rauschnabel et al., 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor160)), virtual reality (VR) ([Adeola et al., 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor102); [Berberović et al., 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor107)), extended reality (XR) ([Wagner & Cozmiuc, 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor177)), and mixed reality (MR) ([Bec et al., 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor106)) offer great potential for marketing. Integrating virtual elements into the physical world enables companies to offer interactive and immersive experiences to their customers, which can positively influence their behavior and perception ([Flavián et al., 2019](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor121); [Wedel et al., 2020](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor178)). For instance, the utilization of augmented reality eases product preview and adjustment, thereby decreasing customer uncertainty ([Gallardo et al., 2018](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor122)) and perceived risk ([Lim et al., 2024](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor144)). As a result, this impacts customer satisfaction, commitment ([Wagner & Cozmiuc, 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor177)), and purchase intention ([Kazmi et al., 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor134)).

In the field of marketing, the Internet of Things (IoT) represents a significant advancement. It enables real-time data collection via connected sensors and smart objects, providing marketers with useful information on consumer behavior ([Grewal et al., 2020](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor125); [Khargharia et al., 2023](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor136)). [Lo & Campos, (2018)](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor146) suggest that IoT solutions can be used to explore new market segments while driving value creation and customer engagement ([Wagner & Cozmiuc, 2022](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor177)). It is also a vector for relationship marketing, which is achieved through the creation and development of lasting relationships and positive experiences for customers ([Lo & Campos, 2018](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor146)).

Blockchain has revolutionized modern marketing by providing a transparent and secure way to conduct transactions ([Gleim & Stevens, 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor123)). This technology is characterized by trust, immutability, and confidence in the recording of data, which reduces the risk of fraud and counterfeiting without the need for intermediaries ([Stallone et al., 2021](#979-8-3693-3172-9.ch002.bits-pdf.xhtml#_idTextAnchor169)).
