### Attijariwafa Bank's AI Chatbots

Attijariwafa Bank, one of Morocco's leading commercial banks, has deployed AI-driven chatbots and virtual assistants across its digital channels. These chatbots provide personalized support to customers, offering assistance with account inquiries, transactional activities, and product information. By leveraging AI, Attijariwafa Bank enhances customer engagement, reduces response times, and optimizes service delivery.

BMCE Bank of Africa leverages AI to enhance customer satisfaction, improve operational efficiency, and drive digital adoption.
