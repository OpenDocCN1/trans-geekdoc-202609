# Conclusion

Many digital tools are capable of providing specific information on different subjects of interest. The five-layer model used in this chapter is just one framework, and many services and digital platforms combine many layers. Our main point is that the remarkable combination of information from all the five layers of the cyber domain makes it possible to conduct a more effective TAA. Therefore, focus should not be in one layer or one feature but collecting the behavioural indicators that manifest themselves in different forms in all the layers. As noted above, combining information allows gathering data about digital TAs’ geographic information, demographics, personality, behaviour and conditions, all of which are required for an effective TAA.

The type of TAA we suggest here is an attempt to utilize the emerging possibilities provided by a global, digital environment. It serves also as a warning of emerging cyber threats that do not come in the purely technical form that cyber threats are often perceived.
