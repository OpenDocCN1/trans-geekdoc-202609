# Celebrating and awarding the ‘Russian way'

development of RuNet can be illuminated through the lens of ‘RuNet award’. The ‘RuNet award’, i.e. ‘a national award for the contribution to the development of the Russian segment of the Internet’ is an annual award for the best initiatives, companies and applications issued by the Federal Agency on Press and Mass Communication (Rospechat) starting from 2004.

In 2004 RuNet celebrated its 10th anniversary as, in 1994, the domain.ru was registered and the official history of the Russian Internet had begun. (Schmidt et al., 2006, 51–52) have described the 10th anniversary celebrations as aimed at integrating the Internet into an official cultural context and making the use of Internet as a national pride. In the first ‘RuNet show’ 15 companies and projects contributing to the development of ‘the Russian segment of the Internet’ were awarded. The show was featured by a prima ballerina of the Bolshoi Ballet and blessed by the Patriarch of Moscow Alexei II (Schmidt et al., 2006).

The ‘RuNet award’ can be seen as one of the first events where state takes over ‘RuNet-thinking’. State-organized competitions are a Soviet phenomenon. Under Socialism everything was different and different competitions supported the ideology. The guiding principle was to get better, compete, to improve and then compete again. Competitions, their calls and instructions and award categories indicate what is considered valuable and influencing by familiarizing and personalizing. Issues that are excluded eliminate the ‘not meaningful’ issues from broad-based debate and contestation. In the RuNet case the award categories what is needed for the development of Russian ‘unified information space’ and for ensuring safety.

In 2004 RuNet awards were given in three different categories:
1) RuNet Technologies (provider, equipment, software and developer);
2) Business RuNet (communication with RuNet consumers, e-Commerce in RuNet, business solutions in RuNet, and financial tool in RuNet)
3) RuNet for users (search engine, e-mail, free service of the RuNet, content, entertainment project and community-resource).
Generally, the winners were companies and software providing services in Russian (Yandex, Rambler, mail.ru). Others, a special nomination contribution for the formation and development of the domain.ru was given to A. P. Platonov, Director of the Russian Research Institute for the development of public networks (RosNIIROS).
