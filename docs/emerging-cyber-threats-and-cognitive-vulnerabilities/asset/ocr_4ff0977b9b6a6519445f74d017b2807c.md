The model in Fig. 5.2 was estimated using partial least squares (PLS), and a composite-based approach to structural equation modelling (Lohmöller, 2013). To estimate the model, the SmartPLS (Ringle, Wende & Becker, 2017) was employed. After an initial examination of loadings onto each construct, one item was omitted because of low loadings from religiosity scales. After this, the reliability and validity of the measures were used. We employed two measures of reliability: Cronbach's alpha and composite reliability. As shown in Table 5.2, both reliability measures exceed the benchmark 0.70.
