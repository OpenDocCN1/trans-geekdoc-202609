For example, there is a northeast cable project designed to connect networking activities between different continents. Nowadays the problem is that fibre optic and power supply are transmitted through the same cable. Vulnerabilities and risks have increased, though formally, the goal is to harmonize Eastern and Western cable functionalities (Buchanan, 2018; Shackelford et al 2017). The study shows that the most troublesome and significant threats to national security and vital functions are related to politicians and political projects. This state-level policy dimension may prevent the utilization of the smart hybrid energy model.
