# Legislation

Legislation exists to support the techniques used in the investigation of traditional organized crime. For example, the Regulation of Investigatory Powers Act 2000 (HMG, 2000):

+   - Part I of the legislation covers Interception and the acquisition and disclosure of communications data.

+   - Part II covers Human Intelligence that covers the use of surveillance and CHIS (this includes the deployment of undercover police officers).

+   - Part III covers the investigation of electronic data protected by encryption.
