# Management of situational awareness in Finland

The Ministry of Finance of Finland is responsible for the steering and development of the state's information security (Ministry of Defence, 2010). The Government Situation Centre ensures that the state leaders and central government authorities are informed continuously in Finland. The Government Situation Centre was set up in 2007, and it has the duty to alert the government, permanent secretaries and heads of preparedness and to call them councils, meetings and negotiations at exceptional times required by a disruption or a crisis. The ministries have the duty to submit the situational picture for their entire administrative branch to the Government Situation Centre and notify the centre of any security incidents in their field of activity. In urgent situations, the Government Situation Centre also receives reports of security incidents directly from the authorities. In addition, the Government Situation Centre follows public sources and receives SA information in its role as the national focal point for certain institutions of the EU and other international organizations.
