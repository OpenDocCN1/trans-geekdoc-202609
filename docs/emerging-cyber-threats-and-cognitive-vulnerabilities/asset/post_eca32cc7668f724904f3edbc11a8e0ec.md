## Introduction

Humans always need to develop intimate relationships and engage in private activity (Rachel, 2017). If this information is leaked, it might affect individual well-being and cost them more than (Etzioni, 2017).

Online privacy is one of the major problems for internet users (LaRose & Rifin, 2006). It has attracted much research attention, particularly in the online and e-commerce settings. Past studies mainly focus on technological aspects of privacy with minimal shed on individual views. Although privacy is a right that all should protect, yet interpretation of the scope of privacy made it difficult to investigate. In addition, information and communication technologies (ICTs) change the nature of privacy. With the excessive use of search engines and access to, individual concerns have increased mainly in improper use and manipulation of personal information (Moor, 1997; Wilford, 2004).

Studies into information privacy have been conducted in corporate and commercial environments (Dine & Hart, 2006), while in recent years the focus has shifted to individual privacy levels (Saridak et al., 2016). Surveys showed that online privacy concerns are the main reason for not using e-commerce (Metzger & Doctor, 2003). In society, online privacy became an international issue (Smith et al., 1996). This chapter will look at the literature of online privacy and its relation to technical, behaviour, companies, social network and religiosity, which affect cybersecurity. Also, it will explain how individual religiosity affects user privacy, which in return has impact on cybersecurity in general.

## Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity entails the activities of protecting data and information systems such as applications, data centres, computers and networks. Cybersecurity should be undertaken using appropriate technological and procedural measures. Organizations use technological solutions, antivirus software and firewalls to protect computer network systems and personal data (Wall, 2008). However, this is insufficient because cyberterrorists keep multiple ways to commit crime that supersede the ability of security measures. Besides implementing cybersecurity infrastructure, it is vital to educate the population on how to use it appropriately. There is a need to integrate cyber safety and ethics in the educational process from the start.

Most cyberattacks are indiscriminate and automated and tend to exploit vulnerabilities instead of targeting certain organizations. In most cases, issues of cybersecurity lead to the disruption of operations, which can lead to considerable reputational and financial damage to an organization. According to Deibert (2012), an organization can lose clients, business, reputation and assets in case of a cyberattack. Additionally, it causes regulatory litigation and fines. Cybersecurity can be enhanced through the development of a solid foundation where cybersecurity technology is grown.

The advancement of technology in today's evolving world means that people can store large volumes of information in cyberspace, which makes it easier for various individuals to access it. It becomes more comfortable and fast to obtain information over the internet than it was decades ago. The sensitivity of data stored in cyberspace requires the implementation of sound cybersecurity strategies to avoid unauthorized access to sensitive information relating to finances, government data and personal information. The following write-up presents a review of various literature dealing with the issue of cybersecurity.

The advancement of security means that every transaction or activity conducted on online platform faces several risks from hackers, spyware and malware. According to Deibert (2012), organizations have been forced to employ reliable and effective cybersecurity strategies to deal with emerging cyber threats including warfare, espionage and cybercrime. The risks have led to the transformation of the domain into an issue of national security, which has become a critical force influencing global communication today. The threat has also led to policymakers developing strategies to deal with the ever-developing range of cyber threats due to the advancement of technology with innovations coming with new threats.

Bada and Sasse (2014) state that the increase in the application of technology has led to an increase in cybercrimes while also enhancing cybersecurity. The enhancement of cybersecurity is through data protection methods such as the use of one-password protection, detection of malicious attacks and server virtualisation. Cybersecurity is a complicated issue that cannot be reduced merely into an approach of individual security and network security. It relates to the broader point of the economy, nation, society and state. Technology has become part of our everyday life, which further emphasizes the needs for cybersecurity. Cybersecurity lies in the interlink between the threatened party, the threat itself and the source. The aspect of technology in different areas has become critical because of the increase in the integration of technology such as finance, banking, entertainment, national defence, communications, e-commerce and government departments (Deibert, 2011). The application of technology has made it possible to increase work efficiency, simplify work and solve problems. However, it has also exposed firms to the risk of losing information due to cyber threats. Thus, protection of cybersecurity measures is critical in the organization.

## 5. The relationship between user religiosity and preserved privacy 

Wall (2008) perceives the lack of clear laws and regulations has led to an increase in cyber threats. Despite millions of people around the world receiving cyber threats, the rate of prosecution is still low. As a result, it has led to uncertainty through the progress of social migration, which impedes individuals from enjoying the full benefits of the internet. The advancement of technology in recent decades has not backed up by the justice system. Lange and von Solms (2012) state that children are the most vulnerable group to online dangers. The advancement of technology means that children become acclimatised from an early age. However, they do not possess the expertise and knowledge to protect themselves. They also state that there is an apparent lack of education on the online behaviours of children. On the other hand, parents are also unaware and unprepared for the cyber threats as they lack the relevant expertise and experience. This leads to unconscious and unsafe online demanour.

Social media has been used as an excellent reconnaissance tool when scammers engineer victims when they neglect their privacy settings or publicly put personal information, photos or posts (Kesan & Hayes, 2014). Phoney Facebook updates have been used to pray on users to click on a link pointing to offer free merchandise of participating in surveys. The relaxed environment on social media has led to phishing being an easy expedition, which not only puts the user at risk but also the entire organization.

Lang and Solms (2012) state that the lack of awareness and know-how about the cyber risks that social media pose to the risky practices that expose them to crime. Halevi, Lewis and Menon (2013) add that the advancement of the internet has led to the increased popularity of social media platforms such as Twitter, Email and Facebook. Scammers have used social media to extract monetary gain or personal information.
