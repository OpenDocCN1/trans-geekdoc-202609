Comparing the results and the overall practicality (such as time consumed) of these methods would show whether or not human identification is necessary for satisfactory results. Conducting several tests with various percentages of internet access would yield additional information on the application of different methods. To make the test in laboratory conditions avoid the real world complexity of TAS, while tests with real audiences seem have clear cause and effect correlations.

The third method would be to access data from an already conducted TAA and perform the TAA with cyber personality on the same TA. The challenge with this approach is that the conditions of the TA may have changed and the social networks may not be comparable.

Disseminating and analyzing a cyber personality through the five layers in the way presented in this chapter may also include the benefit of recognizing bots and other algorithms. One suggestion for further studies would be to look at what features artificial personalities in comparison to humans in the five cyber layers and whether these are platform-specific.
