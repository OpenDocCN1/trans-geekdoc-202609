Artificial entities may also express components that people generally do not have, such as constant activity devoid of any resting periods or overwhelmingly prolific creativity. These factors may help in making the distinction between human and artificial actors, which in turn help avoid false or biased observations based on volumes of information produced by the latter. As it is likely that advances in AI technology will make the cyber domain harder, this approach of looking for missing human components in other layers may provide some solutions.
