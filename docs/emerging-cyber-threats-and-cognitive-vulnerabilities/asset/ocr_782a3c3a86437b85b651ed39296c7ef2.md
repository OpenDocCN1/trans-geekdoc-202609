# Policy/strategy

To support the investigation techniques and legislation to work effective in combating cybercrime, there need to be effective policy and strategy. The Cabinet Office (2014) reports on the UK government progress on delivery of its 2011 Cyber Security Strategy. The document focuses on the progress with small business, the critical infrastructure and the funds that have been allocated to the setting up of the National Cyber Crime Unit, Regional Cyber Units and the Metropolitan police Falcon team. These units contain a small number of specialist officers and there is no acknowledgement in this document of any strategy to support the mainstream investigation of cybercrime. The Home Office Strategy on Cybercrime (2010) highlights the need of a classification for the recording of cybercrimes, but where five years later enforcement still does not have classification codes so remains remains central collation of any statistics. The strategy does not focus on the generic police investigators, their training and any required legislation changes.

Furthermore, strategy needs to be shared to be effective.
