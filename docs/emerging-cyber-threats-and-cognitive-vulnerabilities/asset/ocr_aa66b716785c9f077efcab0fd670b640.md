The factual ‘RuNet award’ is a 43-centimetre statue made of bronze and completely covered with gold weighting around 5 kg. The statuette represents a column crowned with a three-dimensional letter designation of the Russian domain zone RU (‘RuNet award’ is sometimes called the ‘Internet Oscar’). On the front side of the sculpture on a special plate is engraved ‘RuNet award’, year and the name of the winner.

To win this nationwide award symbolized a patriotic/heroic act, i.e. it motivates individuals and companies to consider their ideas and knowledge to a common project that promotes the sense of nationalism and political legitimacy of ‘unified information space’. Here the Russian sense of security is established by celebrating the ‘Russian way’ of doing things. Participation in the ritualistic events links people to the community’s worldview and perception of its own history.
