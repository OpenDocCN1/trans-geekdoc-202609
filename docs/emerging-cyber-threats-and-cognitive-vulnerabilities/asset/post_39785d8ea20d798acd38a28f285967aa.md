## Introduction

The concept of cyberterrorism has grown in response to the increased use of technology. Over the years, terrorists change their tactics and targets to the digital domain. Terrorist organisations have invaded cyberspace and made it a battleground. They no longer rely on military force such as weapons and bombs only. Instead, they become more and more savvy, their strategies and tactics have technological orientation (Zerri, 2017). The possibility that terrorists use cyber tools as part of their arsenal has grown over the years. This chapter looks at clarifying the threat of cyberterrorism so as to analyze the underlying threat.

In order to understand the concept of cyberterrorism, it is important to place in context of terrorism in general. A discussion follows on.
