Fabrication is when the user is trying to disguise his/identity using false information; protect is when the user uses technology to protect his/privacy and withhold when the user refuses to provide information or patronize websites. Another three privacy rules have been prescribed by Metzger (2007), which are withholding information, falsifying information and intention seeking. Furthermore, a simpler classification consists of two dimensions: protection and active protection by Dolnicar and Jordaan (2006) and Yao, Rice and Wallis (2007), respectively. Passive protection is depending on others such as government law to protect privacy, whereas active protection is when users take action to protect their privacy.
