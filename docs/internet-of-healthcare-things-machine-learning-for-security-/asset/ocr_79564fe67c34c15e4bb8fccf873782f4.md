# Abstract

Healthcare Sector is the core of a nation's development, and a fit body can make
great contributions to the development of a nation. Since taking care of the
brain will almost result in maintaining the health of the human body, so this chap-	er will focus on one of the important concerns in the healthcare sector i.e., brain
tumor. The brain tumor is deadly for everyone, but it specifically affects children
or patients with genetic neurological disease. The symptoms of the brain tumor
vary as the physiques of a patient which causes the delay in the diagnosis of the
tumor. The fatality of these brain illness depends on the grades of brain cancer
and some of the factors which will be further discussed in this chapter along
with these grades of a brain tumor in detail. Furthermore, there will be a discus-\sion about various challenges that may arise during the classification of types of
brain tumor. There are any automatic or semi-automatic constructs for sleuting
brain tumor, so the focus will be on the best methods in each mode for segmenta-
ton and detection of brain treatment. Treatment of brain cancer does not lead to the
longed existence of patients. This chapter will also provide the survival rate of
patients and these survival rates will be based on the different types of tumor.
this chapter is an attempt to summarize all necessary information which needs to be
 explored for a brain tumor as well as trust in IOHT and machine learning
regarding brain cancer.
