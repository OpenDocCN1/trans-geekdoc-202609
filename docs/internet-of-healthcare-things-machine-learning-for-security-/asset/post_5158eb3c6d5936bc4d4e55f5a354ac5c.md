## 3.6 Conclusion

The potential of big data in healthcare is endless, but there are some obstacles to its true potential, including technical difficulties. Big Data Security and Data Protection are considered major obstacles for analysts in this field.

This chapter has outlined the privacy and security problems at each stage of the big data lifecycle, as well as the advantages and disadvantages that arise in the current evolution of Big Health Security & Privacy.

We discussed the various laws in different countries. We also discussed the five rules of HIPAA.

We mainly looked at the latest confidentiality practices in healthcare and how to use encryption and anonymity strategies to ensure accuracy and limitations in healthcare.
