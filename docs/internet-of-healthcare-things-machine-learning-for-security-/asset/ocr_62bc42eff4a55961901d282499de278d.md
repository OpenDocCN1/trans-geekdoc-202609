# ANALYZING TRENDS & PUBLIC SENTIMENT For IOHT 103

IoHTs dataset is analyzed for finding the most trending words which are commonly used online. In Figure 5.4, World Cloud is used to display word occurrence from a given dataset. These words show that the top words in the IoHTs dataset are Internet of Things, IoT, Healthcare, COVID 19, Market and Medical. IoHTs and COVID 19 word show that IoHTs have a significant role in the recent COVID 19 pandemic.

News articles extracted from the internet include unwanted text, universal resource locator (URL), numbers, user handlers, special characters and punctuation marks in the IoHTs dataset. In pre-processing, the IoHTs database undergoes pre-removing unwanted characters and texts. The regular expression is applied to the original text from the dataset to remove unnecessary text '@ user handlers, '#tags', numbers, universal resource locator (URL) and punctuation mark. In information, the word is not in form of its root word. The context of words is important for performing topic modeling and sentiment analysis. The word context is derived from using stemming and lemmatization in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP). Stemming is used to reduce words but stemmed word may not be a valid word. Lemmatization transforms words into their context and lemmatized Lemmatization is the root form of the input word. In this work, lemmatization is performed on the IoHTs dataset to pre-process text into its context of words. The pre-processed dataset is passed to topic modeling and sentiment analysis for the Internet of Healthcare Recent trends and its impact on the future health crisis.
