# 4.4.3 Benefits Associated With Healthcare Based on the IoT

The most profound advantages associated with the IoT based Healthcare systems are being mentioned below:

+   - Cost Reduction: The decline in the frequent visits for the consultancy to the doctors with admission in the hospital and bring a change in the cost invested.
- Improved Treatment: The physician got the full transparent information about the healthcare of the patient who helps to give the best treatment.
- Faster Disease Diagnosis: With help of continuous monitoring of the healthcare of a person can help to detect the disease at the early stages which helps to the diagnosis of that disease.
