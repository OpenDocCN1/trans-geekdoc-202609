# 5.6 Conclusion and Future Scope

The World Health Organization has discussed various health issues and disorders from time to time. Most governments are spending very low portions of their budget for the healthcare sector. It leads to the suffering of people from inaccessible healthcare services. High cost and scarcity of healthcare resources are some for inefficient government programs. The pandemic has caused several economic issues as well as health issues. People are suffering from depression and anxiety similar to various mental-related problems.

This research work has given a view on the dynamics of sentiment analysis and its effect on the Healthcare system, IoHT and its possible impact on the existing system with government intervention with different policy and support. The proposed work is a contribution towards providing a solution to offer effective utilization of IoHTs for numerous health issues.
