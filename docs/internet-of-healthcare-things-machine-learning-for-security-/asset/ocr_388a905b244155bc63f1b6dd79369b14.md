The main aim is to achieve effective data management with efficient sharing and processing while safeguarding privacy and security when dealing with stakeholders.

In this chapter, Healthcare systems and their challenges, followed by Blockchain and its integration with Healthcare are discussed. Blockchain in Healthcare Systems can be securely implemented in sharing, storing, and creating electronic medical records and prescriptions, personalized medicine, remote monitoring, mobile health applications, and the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) which is vital to patient care and improves its quality. Fraud detection, Insurance claims, Medical transactions, checking Drug Counterfeiting, and Tracking, with other utilities for patients, doctors, and healthcare institutes, can also be achieved by using blockchain in healthcare. Other useful applications include Public Health Management and supervising clinical trials. Various Machine Learning (ML) and deep learning algorithms can also be applied to data to improve the effectiveness and efficiency of treatment. Also discussed are the challenges faced while implementing blockchain in healthcare.

# 8.2 Healthcare Industry

The Healthcare Industry is vast and has various sectors for providing healthcare facilities and products for patients. The interaction between different Healthcare stakeholders involve parties like the patient, the Doctor, the Hospital staff and Management, the Pharmaceutical company, the Insurance company, or the Government. Such parties and all intermediates need to have effective and error-free data conveyance for reliability and to avoiding fraudulent.

Healthcare mainly concentrates on maintenance and health improvement by prevention, management, diagnosis, treating diseases, illness, injuries, and other physical and mental ailments. Healthcare availability to the public varies across different countries and communities and is largely driven by the social and economic environment and government policies. However, healthcare organizations are established to meet the needs of the general public [1].
