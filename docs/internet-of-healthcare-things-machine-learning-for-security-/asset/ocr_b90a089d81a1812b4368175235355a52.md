# # 11.6 Conclusion

Over the few years, Internet of Things (IoT) has witnessed the transition in a lifetime which offers the possibility to examine mutually both the real-time data and historical data by the changing role of data mining and artificial intelligence practices. In the early phase through the IoT the medical environment, the precise and efficient method for breast cancer diagnosis is particularly required for the treatment and recovery. In this research study, a couple of diagnostic system for accurate diagnosis of breast cancer are discussed in detail. In constructing the scheme for the machine learning acting like the forecasting model, many methods of machine learning were used for its discovery.

The existing state-of-the-art method does not successfully identify breast cancer in the initial stages, and most of the females have suffering from this potentially hazardous disease. Consequently, the early discovery of breast cancer substantially poses a major challenge for healthcare professionals and scientists. To address the problem of early on-stage exposure of breast cancer, proposed a machine learning-centered diagnostic system that essentially organizes the benign and malignant individuals into the environment of IoT. In the foreseeable future, other features selection procedures, increasing the efficiency (optimization), and deep neural network categorization methods will be exploited to further boost the performance of the diagnostic procedure for BC diagnosis.
