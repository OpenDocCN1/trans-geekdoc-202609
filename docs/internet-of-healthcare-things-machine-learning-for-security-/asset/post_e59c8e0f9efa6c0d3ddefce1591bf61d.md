## 8.5 Challenges of Blockchain in Healthcare

Already discussed the challenges faced in implementing blockchain technology. Now we are going to look at the challenges specific to implementing it in Healthcare.

**Storage:** With rapid digitization, now facing storage issues because of the generation of tremendous amounts of medical records, images, documents and laboratory reports collected by sensors and wearable IoMT devices. Blockchain architecture is limited by on-chain data storage, meanwhile, while our current system produces a lot of data and yet to find a solution to this problem.

**Costly:** Blockchain architecture is very costly to implement and it only becomes even more expensive as it increases the scalability of our system. Its implementation requires the most effective infrastructure and connectivity, which startups cannot afford easily. Data storage and current consensus algorithms are contributing to its expensive nature. Addressing the cost-effectiveness of the technology should involve addressing data problems and adopting alternate state of the art consensus algorithms which are concerned fields of study revolving around this technology.

**Modification:** The immutability feature of blockchain doesn't allow any modification. Blockchain applications should be developed keeping in mind the immutable nature of technology that changes are only committed to the once ever is confirmed.

**Scalability:** The scalability issue needs to be taken seriously to ensure blockchain popularity. Because of its decentralized nature, scalability is somewhat easy, however, there are private clinics, small healthcare centers, small organizations, which might not have the architecture needed to support the system. Blockchain technology also requires tremendous computation power, which calls for higher electricity consumption.

**Privacy and regulations:** Blockchain maximizes the security of its content repeatedly. Cryptographic, decentralized and immutable architecture makes sure that its content is at the highest security. All the large medical information files of the patients are sensitive information and therefore, it is often risky to store a copy of these data in every node. The foremost demanding problem for the blockchain technology currently being used is the storage of Electronic Health Record (EHR) and Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

**Lack of Knowledge:** There is a lot of reluctance in regards to adopting this new technology from various organizations that play a key role in health-systems. These organizations are unaware of its working and benefits. They must educate themselves about this rising technology. Blockchain is still evolving and a lot of organizations are not aware of its existence. There's still a lot of work needed to promote it across various domains.
