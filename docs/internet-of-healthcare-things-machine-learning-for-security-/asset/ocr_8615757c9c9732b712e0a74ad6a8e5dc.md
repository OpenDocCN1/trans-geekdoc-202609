# Abstract

Internet of Things is part of modern human life as smart devices are accepted and accessible to everyone. Smartphones are a common example that shows IoT devices' reachability. Digitization is the focus of businesses and governments to offer better services to individuals. The digital medium has been adopted as a platform for sharing information about topics ranging over various domains. Internet of Healthcare Things (IoHTs) plays significant importance in offering medical services to a broader population using digital infrastructures. The proposed work objective is to present a sentiment analysis and topic modeling approach for early warning of a health crisis which can be integrated with the IoHTs framework and can be fruitful in assisting medical specialists. In this research, information related to IoHTs is collected followed by generation. Topic modeling is performed on the proposed IoHTs dataset for predicting trends in IoHTs domains. Sentiment analysis towards IoHTs applicability is evaluated to find the overall sentiment orientation of peoples. In the current pandemic situation, variation in sentiment orientation of users towards IoHTs system is evaluated and analysis is carried out to find the effectiveness of IoHTs systems.

**Keywords:** Internet of Healthcare Things, topic modeling, sentiment analysis, VADER, latent dirichlet allocation
