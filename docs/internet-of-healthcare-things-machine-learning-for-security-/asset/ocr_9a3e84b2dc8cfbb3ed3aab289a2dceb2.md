# 12.3.3 Grades of Brain Tumors

Brain tumor is classified into two types i.e., primary brain tumor which can also be known as low-grade tumor (Benign) and secondary brain tumor (Malignant) known as a high-grade tumor. The low-grade tumor is known to be less lethality in contrast to a high grade tumor. These types of brain tumor are further alienated into grades of tumor which ranges from grade I to grade IV. This grading of tumor was done by World Health Organization to further comprehend the risk level and provide appropriate type of treatment to the patients. These different grades of tumor are characterized according to the structure of tumor perceptible through the microscope. Grades I and II are considered primary brain tumor while grades III and IV are considered secondary brain tumor. As previously it was distinguished that the benign tumor was less deadly compared to the Malignant tumor accordingly grades I and II become less deadly compared to grades III and IV. +   Grade I tumor: A grade I tumor can be effortlessly removed as it seldom invades the brain to other parts of the human body and it has an advantage over other types of tumor that it grows at a slow rate. Gangliocytoma, Pilocytic astrocytoma, Ganglioglioma, and Craniopharyngioma are some of the tumors that fall under the category of this grade tumor.
