## 2.3 IoHT Process Flow

In a typical use case of diagnostics in the healthcare system, data from the patient is collected through devices incorporating sensors, and processed by special and specific applications in a range of equipment like a normal computer or custom-built terminals. User terminals are connected to a gateway through short coverage communication protocols, usually conforming to industry adopted IEEE 802.15.4 standard [3]. This gateway connects to a medical server or cloud services for processing and storage—critical functions in the system operation. There is a choice of segregating the storage of data—either centrally or in a distributed manner depending upon the system architecture and configuration. Segregation and decentralization enhance the level of security for the data; however, could bring down the efficiency of operation and response time to data handling. Simulated testing of scenarios of data storage and handling is recommended step in system design and integration as well.

IoHT can support use cases in the healthcare environment—patient registration, scheduling appointments, clinical records in the form of noting, images, and reports, recommended and administered medicines, tracking the prognosis of health conditions and follow up procedures. For each of these use cases, custom-built devices and applications are available and it is the task and responsibility of the system integrator to put the pieces together following standards and specifications for optimal functioning of the system. It is at this stage that the consideration of security and privacy issues need special mention in that they seamless absorption at the design phase.
