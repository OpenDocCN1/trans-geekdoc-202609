+   **1) Primary Care:** This is the first healthcare-related service received by a patient. These services usually consist of a general practitioner/family physician who acts as the first contact for the patient in the treatment process. The Primary Care doctor should be able to accurately diagnose the patient based on his broad sense of knowledge.
**2) Secondary Care:** This primarily involves acute care, usually given in hospital emergency departments. Here only essential treatment is given and if necessary, the patient is referred to a tertiary health department. Therefore, the care given by a secondary physician is usually for a brief window of time.
**3) Tertiary Care:** This is a specialized healthcare-related service given to patients on referral from primary or secondary health departments. This sector requires more care and time to heal the illness, usually for complex medical and surgical processes.

# 8.2.2 Health Information Technology (HIT)

Before studying the Healthcare sector and its stakeholders, it is essential to know about HIT or Health IT and its components related to this chapter for better understanding later on. Health Information Technology (HIT) involves the processing and management of healthcare data that leads to efficient and effective communication and decision making [2].
Various components of HIT are:

+   - Electronic Health Record (EHR)—EHR stores extensive medical history of patients and organizes records from multiple providers together in one place.
