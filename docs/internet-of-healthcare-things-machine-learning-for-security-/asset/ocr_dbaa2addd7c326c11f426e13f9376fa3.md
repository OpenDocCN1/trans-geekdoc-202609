# 4.2.1.5 Smart Agriculture

The world’s population will grow every day to surpass nearly 10 billion by 2050. Then it will become a challenging task to cultivate and provide food to everyone. Our cultivation practices must also be modified. New technologies can be used, as “Smart Greenhouse” The greenhouse farming method increases crop yield by controlling the environmental factors that influence cultivation. Although managing causes a lack of production and loss, high labor costs reduce the efficiency of the whole operation. The greenhouse approach uses smart connected devices that promote the tracking and control of environmental variables such as humidity, pH balance, nutrients content, in the crop area [22, 23].

# 4.2.1.6 Industrial Internet of Things

Another application of IoT is the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) which makes our manufacturing and production system smart with the integration of smart machine and monitoring systems. As the unexpected downtimes and machine faults in such area trigger human causality, the manual fault diagnosis and correction system is replaced with smart sensors that can predict and track the fault. These smart systems will generate the alarm that is intended to take immediate steps to avoid any casualties at the industrial site [24].
