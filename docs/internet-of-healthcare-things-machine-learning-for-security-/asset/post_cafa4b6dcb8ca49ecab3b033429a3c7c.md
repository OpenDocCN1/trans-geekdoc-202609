Some symptoms that are dependent on the position of brain cancer are as follows:

- **Frontal Lobe symptoms**: The symptoms such as variation in the personality leading to annoyance, exertion in talking as well as struggle in walking, and unresponsiveness on any side of the body which may cause loss of aroma are some of the symptoms related to tumors located on the frontal lobe of the brain.
- **Temporal Lobe symptoms**: Understanding problem leading to even misperception about ordinariness, absentmindedness which causes loss of power to learn any new information or recognizing any object are some of the symptoms of brain tumor related to temporal lobe location of brain tumor.
- **Parietal Lobe symptoms**: Symptoms associated with the parietal lobe are one or another similar to the temporal lobe in case of recognition of substances, but it also has supplementary symptoms like both eye and hand have a problem in synchronization with each other, Sensory problems like hear, touch, smell, taste also struggle in retorting to any kind of agony or any interior wound.
- **Occipital Lobe symptoms**: The symptoms such as vision loss either fully or partially, phantasm is some of the symptoms related to tumors located on the occipital lobe.
- **Brainstem symptoms**: Tumors located in the brainstem have symptoms which have a combination of almost all the above symptoms such as variation in the personality leading to annoyance, Sensory problems like hear and sight, both eye and hand have a problem in synchronization with each other as well as other symptoms like exertion in swallowing, tiredness etc.
- **Cerebellum symptoms**: Tumors located in Cerebellum has a vision-related problem such as giddiness, obstreperous movement and muscle-related problems such as movement of arms, legs or any other part of the body, etc.

### 12.4.1 Sleuthing of Brain Tumors

Brain tumor segmentation and detection's method contrasts and is dependent on numerous aspects, some of these aspects are a type of tumor, the intensity of tumor, location and size of tumor, time and cost, sequences of images used in dataset as well as several types of method preferred as generative or discriminative method, instance, or semantic methods, automatic, semi-automatic, or manual methods differ so does the best method for segmentation and detection might differ for individual's dependent on the above stated aspects.

In general, the discriminative method works better in comparison to the generative method because the former depends on the features mined from brain tumor images and the latter depends on the preceding knowledge of the patient's health and oncology issue.

Among methods for instance or semantic segmentation, semantic segmentation seems to be better than instance segmentation because the semantic method can provide similar colors to the tumor region which belong to a precise class the instance method provides separate colors to the tumor region belonging to a precise class which makes the work difficult for researchers. Some of the best semantic segmentation includes an encoder (or contracting path) and decoder (or an expansive path) [7, 8].

Among automatic, semi-automatic, or manual segmentation methods, the performance of each method vary case to case basis and on some of the aspects stated above. For the convenience of individuals, the fully automatic and semi-automatic segmentation method seems to better in comparison to manual segmentation methods because this method is very time consuming and costly as it requires an expert and the expert's decision may not be accurate. As for fully automatic or semi-automatic segmentation methods, the performance level depends on the type of result the researchers want.

In general, a fully automatic segmentation method can be stated better because neither prerequisite expert for assigning segmented borderline user interaction is obligatory. Some of the best fully automatic-method for brain tumor can include deep CNN (Convolutional Neural Network) and its different models such as ResNet (Residual Network) [7, 9], VGGNet (Visual Geometry Network) [10], DenseNet (Convolutional Network) [7, 13], Xception [7], and MobileNet2 [14].

## 12.5 Survival Rate of Brain Tumors

The survival of a patient is reliant on the nature of treatment provided and can be surgery, chemotherapy, radiation, therapy, drugs, or a combination of some of these. The survival rate for 5 years means the percent of people who remain alive for 5 years after treatment is completed. This percent of people survived is calculated out of 100 while for relative survival rate, the percentage of tumor cases in adolescents and young adults (AYAs) has its range i.e., 48% in the case of adolescents and young adults 63% in the case of young adults 30–39 years [3].

Benign and borderline brain tumor has approximately 97% relative survival of 5 years [3, 5]. This survival rate is higher altogether in all groups of adolescents and young adults (AYAs) [3, 5].

The survival rate depends on numerous aspects other than just treatments provided to the patient, some of these aspects upon which survival rate are given below:

- The survival rate depends mainly on the kind of tumor a patient has. The severeness of the patient's condition directly depends on tumor type as benign tumor's level of severeness is diverse from a malignant tumor.
- The survival rate for tumor varies depending upon the grade of tumor. It can be stated that low-grade tumor has higher chance of survival compared to higher-grade tumor.
- The survival rate depends on where the tumor's position is situated in brain early detection of tumor is hindered by a protective layer termed a skull. Tumor's location is vital, but the size of tumor also has a crucial part that may hamper the detection or appropriate treatment to be provided to the patient. For example, if the tumor is situated at a place where the skull hinders in the detection of this tumor but if the size of tumor is bulky it becomes easy to detect tumor even at that part of brain and vice versa.
- The physique of a patient also plays an imperative part in the survival rate. Patients having genetic health issues may not show its effect on the early detection of tumor but also on the survival rate.
