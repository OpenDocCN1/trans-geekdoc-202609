# 12.4.2 Challenges During Classification of Brain Tumors

Identification of brain tumor accompanied by its type and grade of tumor is the decisive and most challenging period. Some of those challenges which might affect the classification of brain tumor are as follows:

+   Recognition of correct symptoms of brain tumor as well as its grade in a patient is the most challenging task as a recommendation of treatment or further medical exam to verify tumor from non-tumor depends on this recognition.
After recognition, another crucial task that needs accuracy is analysis of tumor using small portion of tumor as a sample which is further treated as a block to classify tumor. The selection of sample is vital as the entire study on brain tumor depends on the sample provided to it. If the selection of sample is not balanced, then analysis of brain tumor becomes impractical.

+   For an analysis to be done during de novo neurosurgeons on a tumor's sample is challenging as the gathering of samples is problematic. As it is known, the doctor can't disclose a patient's medical details and so it affects the number of samples collected as there might be some legal complications.

+   Making classification of tumor fully automatic based on features selected is a challenging task.
