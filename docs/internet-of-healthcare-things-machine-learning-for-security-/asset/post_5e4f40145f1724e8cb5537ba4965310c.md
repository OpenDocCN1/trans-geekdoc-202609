## 5.4 Performing Topic Modeling on IoHTs Dataset

Health monitoring is one of the challenging issues among government and non-government organizations. It requires human resources, materialistic resources and at a large scale to implement public health monitoring at a large scale. Digital platforms and digital resources provide solutions to develop a health monitoring system. A digital solution is cost-effective and resource-efficient. Data analysis is one of the possible solutions and can be used to develop a public health monitoring system. A digital health monitoring platform for all is one of the popular agenda among governments to provide healthcare solutions at low cost with access to everyone. Technology has facilitated digital solutions and low-cost storage of health records of peoples. This information can be utilized for monitoring, education and decision making [2, 10].

Topic modeling algorithms are adopted to find thematic structure from given documents by word analysis. Annotation and labeling of texts are not required for performing topic modeling. In topic modeling, derived from Dirichlet texts are the representation of words distribution. Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA), a probabilistic topic modeling algorithm is applied in this research to find relevant topics to the Internet of Healthcare Things. An LDA model is used to find words similar observation and groups into topics proposed from IoHTs datasets [4]. News data from various sources is selected as the source of information to perform topic modeling to categorize text into latent topics. The dataset is pre-processed for removing unwanted information such as hyperlinks. Removing stop words and transform inflected words into their lemma using lemmatization. Pre-processed texts are passed to the LDA model applied a bag of words approaches on given texts to derive word vectors. The latent topic probabilistic distribution representation over given texts [12, 16].

## 5.5 Performing Sentiment Analysis on IoHTs Dataset

Sentiment analysis is well known as opinion mining in the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP). It is used to study subjective information from a given text. Lexical and sentiment-based VADER (Value Aware Dictionary for sEntiment Reasoning), Text Blob and deep learning LSTM model are already adopted for analyzing sentiment orientation for numerous domains. VADER is a rule based sentiment analysis tool designed for sentiment analysis. Valence Dictionary for sEntiment Reasoning (VADER) is a lexicon and rule based sentiment analysis tool for analyzing sentiments expressed in textual information [2, 7]. It emphasizes sentiment intensity using qualitative and quantitative methods and suitable for analyzing short text from the microblogging platform [5].
