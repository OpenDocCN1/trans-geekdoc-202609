+   Grade II tumor: This grade of tumor is somewhat diverse as it look slightly different than usual brain tissues and may spread to other parts of the body which may intensify its level of deadliest. Pineocytoma, “Diffuse” Astrocytoma, and Pure oligodendroglioma are a few examples of this grade of tumor.

+   Grade III tumor: This grade of tumor grows faster than the above-mentioned grades so it's difficult to provide the appropriate treatment and if treated may recur. This grade of tumor spreads to other parts of body at an extremely high speed which if not treated at the precise time may lead to death. Anaplastic oligodendroglioma, Anaplastic ependymoma, and Anaplastic astrocytoma are some of these grades of tumor.

+   Grade IV tumor: To keep up quick development grade IV shapes fresh blood vessels. This grade of tumor is the most deadliest tumor which has dead cells and is difficult to eradicate because eradication may lead to the elimination of some of the normal working cells. This type of tumor may not be detached only with the surgery but may also prerequisite additional treatment as it quickly grows back to other parts of the body. Sometimes there may be conditions where both low grade and the highgrade tumor may be found in a patient, then this type of tumor is categorized based on the type of tumor which prevails more. Medulloblastoma, Glioblastoma multiforme, Ependymoblastoma, and Pineoblastoma are some of grade IV.
