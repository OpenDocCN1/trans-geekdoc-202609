# 8.4.6 Clinical Trials

Data generated during Clinical Trials are used for research purposes approval of new treatments. The integrity of this data is essential because it holds importance for various stakeholders like the researchers, journal editors, drug and device companies, government regulators, and essentially, prospective patients and the general public. Earning of the companies that have invested in research and development is at stake. Thus, the security of the data obtained from clinical trials is of utmost importance [20].

There are several risks to Clinical trial data like alterations, by mistake or even with malicious intent to achieve the expected result and false or selective reporting of an outcome of an experiment. Such manipulation of data results in variation from actual bona outcomes, causing problems for the stakeholders and even serious health hazards for the prospective patients.

The introduction of Blockchain in Healthcare for improved Clinical Trial Dataset Management would not only provide privacy to patients' records but also ensure that the data shared amongst the researchers and publishers did not tamper. This would maintain confidence between the various stakeholders as now, only authorized and trusted parties are given access to its creation and manipulation.
