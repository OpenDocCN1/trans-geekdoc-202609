## 10.7 Conclusion

The principle target of this work is to assess the utilization of ANN with Multilayer perceptron neural network for analysis of heart diseases. The performance of the back propagation neural network with the prediction accuracy is satisfactory but to increase the level of accuracy and to know the type of heart disease the CBR technique is integrated with ANN. Those patients who developed heart failure are matched against a set of control patients on age, gender, and clinics, and ended up with 45,000 control patients. Notice that in this study there are fewer cases with heart failures than the controls without heart failures. This is pretty typical because, in a real-world scenario, patients with specific disease conditions are often harder to obtain while there are a lot patients without that disease available to serve as controls. To summarize, in a case-control study, the first cases are identified, then try to match them to a set of control patients. In the cohort study, all patients exposed to risk are identified, and the matching criteria are not involved. Evaluation of trained models is one of the most crucial steps in the pipeline. Using some training samples, the idea is to devote the model, but test this training model on some other unseen samples, ideally from future data. It may be noted that the training error may not be very useful because it can easily overfit the data by using a complex model which does not generalize to future samples. For a better approximation of the performance of the model on future samples, the testing error is the key metric. It would be interesting for further work, if any old record is not matched with the present expert doctor advice can be taken by certain computing methods like mobile, cloud, and so forth.
