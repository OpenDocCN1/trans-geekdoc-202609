# Abstract

Telemedicine is the use of technology to clarify various elements of medical care from a distance. Telemedicine is defined as the use of telecommunications and information technology to deliver healthcare data and administrations to patients who are located far away. The data is moved from one segment to the next via electronic signals. With the utilization of electronic imaging gadgets, it is getting perceived as implied far off clinical consideration and conferences with various patients. Telemedicine incorporates EMR (Electronic Medical Records) of patients at one spot which can diminish the number of cases, where specialists cannot get to patient's records operational. AI is generally utilized in different uses of Telemedicine. It is an indispensable part in numerous applications like medical science, accounting, and insecurity. AI plays an important role to find designs from clinical information sources and gives incredible capacities to anticipate infections. Organizing medical services is a smart method to use media transmission technology, which has been used to address a wide range of health concerns in a variety of ways. Telemedicine administrations can provide remedies, diagnostic tests, and preventive arrangements, as well as patient guidance. It is useful for correspondence between specialists, drug specialists, patients, and those who live in different parts of the world. Telemedicine administrations are progressively used by patients, clinicians, and organizations.
