# 8.2.3.4 The Government

The government plays an important role in the whole healthcare industry due to its influence. It includes financial and material resources for Medical institutions, funding the development of human resources and research opportunities in public health services. Other role of government include implementation of health-related policies, enforcing hygiene and safety standards on various institutions and industries, promoting health via different initiatives, and creating awareness. Its functioning and policies are prone to faults and loopholes owing to the scale of its policies introduced and the number of people involved [3].

# 8.2.3.5 Insurance Company

Health insurance covers the whole or a part of the cost for the services and prescription a person took for his/her treatment. It is a binding contract between health insurance providers and their policyholders. This contract usually involves monthly/annual payments of premiums. The Health Insurance provider cites in writing the type and amount of healthcare costs that will be covered under the policy. All these are provided by an Insurance company [4].

Such insurance systems have problems such as poor access to the patient data assignment of wrong policies, manipulation of details of the patient’s record leading to the incurrence of inaccurate insurance claims. These call for a consistent database of records, which is immutable, ensuring the Insurance Company in the insurance they provide accurate and adequate for the treatment. This would develop trust among the Insurance Company, the Healthcare Service provider and the Patient.

Blockchain integrated into the Healthcare system seems to provide solutions to the above-mentioned issues faced by the stakeholders. Now focus on blockchain technology and its working before and explore the health-care aspect of it.
