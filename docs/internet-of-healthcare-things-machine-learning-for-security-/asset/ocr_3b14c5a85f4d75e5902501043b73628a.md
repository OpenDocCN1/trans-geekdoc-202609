# 5.1 Introduction

The digital platform is widely used to disseminate information among users from news publishers, government organization, non-government organization and individuals. Digital infrastructure is witnessing more interest and investment in this decade. Smart devices are associated with offering services to everyone without their awareness. Most of the services which are offered online are using IoT devices as backend [1]. IoHTs use properties of the Internet of Things (IoT) to provide healthcare services and touch human lives in regions that are unattended by basic healthcare services. Healthcare systems are playing an important role in making government policy, the financial health of an individual and the demands of a medical specialist. Individual and government are very concerned about existing health infrastructure. Every country is facing challenges in terms of unavailability of sufficient health infrastructure and the relative gap in comparison to demand and supply. Internet of Health Things can play an important role in providing support to existing health infrastructure by incorporating more technology. It requires capital investment and time to establish health infrastructure which is not feasible for many countries. A government and health specialist can utilize the IoHTs to support the demand of healthcare services.

The recent pandemic situations have created so chaos in individuals and propose challenges of inefficiency and scarcity of health infrastructures to almost every government. The pandemic has caused tremendous impact on every individual life all over the world. Every country has been affected by the frequently occurring outbreak of the pandemic and infectious disease. It puts tremendous pressure on existing health infrastructures which are already reeling under patient load and scarcity of medical practitioners. It has been felt that governments are not to provide sufficient healthcare to individuals and lagging in healthcare services to everyone. An individual, especially from the economically poor sector, has been affected very badly. They are not in the situation to adopt standard healthcare practices. The world has introduced various measures to tackle the situation but all are not efficient. During this pandemic, measures that are being adopted by the world have a tremendous impact on the individual from physical health as well as mental health [2, 3].

The digital media platform is used to aware people of health information in a health crisis. Online news health platform has become a popular platform for sharing and tracking health information during the current pandemic. Healthcare topics are one of the trending topics among netizens
