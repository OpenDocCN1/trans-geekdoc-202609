# 4.1 Introduction

With increasing application in our daily lives, the Internet of things (IoT) had become one of the most contributing applications. Increasing IoT applications has accelerated our movement from the physical world to the virtual world. Different heterogeneous things or sensors communicated with each other with the integration of IoT. Out of several applications of IoT, the healthcare sector is one of the most promising applications.

On the Internet of Healthcare Things (IoHT) applications, IoT smart sensor nodes, and interconnected networks are deployed for the health assessment of patients. Generally, patients suffering from chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, hypertension, etc. need emergency services as compared to regular treatments. Internet of Things (IoT) collects data from sensing devices through middleware. To handle effectively secure transmission over middleware is an area of concern and it is a quite challenging task [1–5].

While dealing with sensitive information of patient's data over an insecure channel, it is required to ensure security and privacy. By synchronization of smart sensors, gateway, or storage servers, privacy is a major concern in healthcare applications. Some of the major challenges are discussed below [6]:

+   - Failure of sensing nodes randomly
- Privacy while collecting data;
- Disruption while transmission of data;
- Malware attack;
- Intrusion.

According to recent research, a lot of security issues have been focused to ensure cybersecurity or network security. That's why it is required to design and develop an approach that takes these security concerns into account and understand all aspects.
