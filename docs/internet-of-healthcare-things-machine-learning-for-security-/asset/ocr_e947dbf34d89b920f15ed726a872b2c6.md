# 5.3 Overview of the Internet of Healthcare Things

Technology has a significant role in the evolution of human beings and every decade witnesses new trends of technology completely changing human behavior and perception. Technology is also playing an important part in developing new technology in numerous domains for example, health informatics, biotechnology, e-commerce and logistics. The Internet is witnessing the growth of connected devices using the Internet of Things as predominant technology. IoT has been seen as a business opportunity by offering smart services to users with affordability. IoT represents a network of computing objects embedded with microprocessors to perform numerous tasks in our lives. IoT is the interconnection of devices having and communication capabilities. These smart devices are designed to transfer data to the backend network for performing intelligent operations. IoT based system can be made from machine learning, embedded system, real-time analytics and control systems [1, 9, 12].

In this research, IoHTs stated from numerous internet resources. The information is collected including opinions, news articles and expert review over IoHTs related system and applications. This information selected due to its impact on broader audiences that are directly or indirectly affected. Government and non-government organizations weigh individuals' opinion for decision making. Technology adaptation needs time and the healthcare sector is also adopting IoT based technical solutions. Technology evolutions follow the view of general users and their expectation. The information which is collected for this work includes user's need and expectation towards IoHTs technology. It is optimistic to assume that information someways affects evolutions of IoHTs based system applications.
