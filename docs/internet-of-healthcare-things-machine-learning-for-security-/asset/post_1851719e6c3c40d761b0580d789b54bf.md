# Blockchain in Smart Healthcare Management

Jayant Barak, Harshvardhan Chaudhary, Rakshit Mangal, Aarti Goel and Deepak Kumar Sharma

Department of Information Technology, Netaji Subhas University of Technology, New Delhi, India

# Abstract

Blockchain is one of the latest and emerging technology advances that facilitate verifiable activities between parties reliably and rapidly. It builds on the idea of a P2P (Peer-To-Peer) network and provides a decentralized, distributed, shared, and immutable database ledger where block data contain a list of all transactions linked by cryptographic hashes. Blockchain eliminates the need for a legitimate central supervisory authority. It uses the state of consensus and cryptographic algorithms. When new transactions are added to Blockchain, these algorithms are used to verify data's authenticity in each node. Blockchain technology also makes it difficult to directly alter a part of the blockchain as the hashes of subsequent nodes of the altered node be recomputed. This requires infeasible computational power, making the security fool-proof and increasing exponentially with the chain size. The application of Blockchain in such a manner makes it easier to verify the legitimacy of transactions and at the same time provide transparency, reliability, and integrity. Blockchain is implemented in a wide range of domains as Supply chain management (SCM), financial sector, Internet of Things (IoT), Energy Sector, and Healthcare. The rapid growth of digitization in healthcare has passed the generation of massive records of patients. This calls for protecting such highly confidential data and ensuring data integrity during transactions between authorized parties. Blockchain proposes a mechanism that is transparent and responsible. It can revolutionize healthcare by providing patients' privacy, allowing them to retain ownership
