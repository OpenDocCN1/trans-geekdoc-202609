# Abstract

With the rapid development of communication tools and techniques as well as intelligent processing and analysis had led to the development of smart Internet of Things (IoT) applications. The IoT application generates a huge amount of data every day as this application is vast. One of the most important applications of IoT is in the healthcare sector that generates large and sensitive data. So, to process such a large amount of data over an insecure network is an issue of concern. In this chapter, an overview of IoT architecture, issues, and solutions are discussed. The existing privacy-preserving inference approaches have problems such as high computation and communication overheads. In this chapter of security challenges and security models using machine learning for the Internet of Healthcare Things (IoHT) is given along with a framework proposed that can enforce security and trustworthiness on the Internet of Healthcare Things (IoHT) by amalgamating privacy and deep learning. This chapter will give a direction for future research work while designing a secure IoHT framework with low latency, and fast processing for accurate end-to-end data delivery.

# 4.1 Introduction

With increasing application in our daily lives, the Internet of things (IoT) had become one of the most contributing applications. Increasing IoT applications has accelerated our movement from the physical world to the virtual world. Different heterogeneous things or sensors communicated with each other with the integration of IoT. Out of several applications of IoT, the healthcare sector is one of the most promising applications.

On the Internet of Healthcare Things (IoHT) applications, IoT smart sensor nodes, and interconnected networks are deployed for the health assessment of patients. Generally, patients suffering from chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, hypertension, etc. need emergency services as compared to regular treatments. Internet of Things (IoT) collects data from sensing devices through middleware. To handle effectively secure transmission over middleware is an area of concern and it is a quite challenging task [1–5].

While dealing with sensitive information of patient's data over an insecure channel, it is required to ensure security and privacy. By synchronization of smart sensors, gateway, or storage servers, privacy is a major concern in healthcare applications. Some of the major challenges are discussed below [6]:

- Failure of sensing nodes randomly
- Privacy while collecting data;
- Disruption while transmission of data;
- Malware attack;
- Intrusion.

According to recent research, a lot of security issues have been focused to ensure cybersecurity or network security. That's why it is required to design and develop an approach that takes these security concerns into account and understand all aspects.

This chapter is dedicated to focusing light issues on the Internet of Healthcare Things (IoHT). In Section 4.2, an overview of IoT is discussed with their application areas. Section 4.3 discusses the issues and challenges of IoT for understanding issues of IoHT as security concern remains the same under all applications of IoT. Section 4.4 describes IoHT architecture and its challenges. Section 4.5 describes the protocols that are existing in the current scenario. In Section 4.6, different machine learning approaches are discussed for the IoHT environment. In Section 4.7, after analyzing the drawbacks of the existing system, a secure framework

for IoHT is proposed along with its key advantages. Finally, in Section 4.8, the conclusion and future research scope of this chapter are described.
