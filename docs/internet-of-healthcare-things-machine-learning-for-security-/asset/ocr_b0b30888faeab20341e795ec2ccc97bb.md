The absolute complexity of the insurance industry creates gaps that are misused to commit fraud. It is during the slow, paperwork-driven claiming procedure having various moving parts, that the claims are transferred from Insurance-Policy holders to the insurers and reinsurers. Not only does it pay the way for brokers to sell insurance policies and make money off the premiums, but also allows criminals to make multiple claims from different insurance companies for a single loss.

With the implementation of Blockchain technology in the Insurance Industry, coordination between the insurers and the insurers can be made much more efficient, thus avoiding fraud [17]. Furthermore, registering the transactions on the distributed ledger with granular access could help maintain data security. Any abnormal and suspicious behaviour could be identified and be taken care of appropriately by the insurance company as the claims would be recorded on the blockchain [18].

# 8.4.5 Drug Counterfeiting

A patient's health is largely dependent on the quality of medicinal drugs he/she takes. The research and production steps in the manufacturing of these drugs use a lot of capital. Such a high cost of medicines promotes counterfeiting. FDA defines drug/medicine counterfeiting as fake medicine that may be contaminated, contain the wrong dosage, and contain wrong or even no active ingredients in the medicine. They may contain toxic ingredients, which when consumed, can cause serious health issues.
