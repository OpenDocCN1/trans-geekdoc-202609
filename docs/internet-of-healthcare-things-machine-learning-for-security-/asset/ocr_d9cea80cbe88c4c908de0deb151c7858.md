# ANALYZING TRENDS & PUBLIC SENTIMENT For IoHT 111

An optimist approach can be helpful in a situation of an upcoming pandemic or disease outbreak. Governments can make better policies based on sentiment finding and be able to provide healthcare resources to everyone who our country is lacking such infrastructure to support millions of people who cannot afford the healthcare system due to poor economic condition. Our approach can be beneficial to measure the effectiveness of the government policy with the IoHT effect. This proposal examines topic modeling and community sentiment in the situation of a recent health crisis.

In the pandemic era, resources require better utilization and management and this work can be useful while deciding IoHTs applications. In future work, the effect of health crisis and government policy on individuals and business will be analyzed. This research work shows the promising result and can be further extended by incorporating data collected from numerous methods with diversified data.
