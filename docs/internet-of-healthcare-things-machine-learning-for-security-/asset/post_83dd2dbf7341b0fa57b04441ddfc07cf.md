## 11.1 Introduction

Breast Cancer (BC) represents a truly devastating illness amidst all the illnesses in the medical field. It represents one of the important explanations of mortality among the women all over the globe and is one of the critical diseases which significantly affect ladies as AICR (American Institute for Cancer Research) [1]. In 2018, the count of BC cases were 2 million. BC is the 5th bigger cause of death in comparison to other types of cancer. That is the early discovery of this disease may decrease the death rates. Cells of BC develop naturally and steadily enhance the influenced cell rate, leading to BC. It is a cancerous tumor produced and built of the breast. An extra tissue known as tumors, plus such cancers are categorized as benign i.e., non-cancerous and malignant as cancerous. With the development of modern technology, in various countries, the 5-year SR (survival rate) in the initial phase is about approx. 90% and fails at the initial to 24% for identification of BC [2]. For the prediction and diagnosis of BC, multiple invoice-based methods are used. Biopsy [3] is one of the methods resulting in high and good accuracy with the help of breast tissues and taking biopsy is too painful for patients. A mammogram is another method [4] considered for BC diagnosis. In a mammogram, a 2-D image of the breast is specially designed, but this method does not perform well in diagnosing the type of cancer successfully. MRI (magnetic reasoning imaging) [5] is an invoice-based method; it is a highly complex task and delivers the best results for 3-D images. More time is needed in all these methods to generate the output [6]. To help the problem of complexities in the above mentioned methods for BC diagnosis, mainly machine learning (ML) methods i.e., non-invasive are more trustworthy and effective. In literature, machine learning methods are used to categorize BC as either malignant or benign. The detailed literature of ML has been described for the detection and diagnosis of BC.
