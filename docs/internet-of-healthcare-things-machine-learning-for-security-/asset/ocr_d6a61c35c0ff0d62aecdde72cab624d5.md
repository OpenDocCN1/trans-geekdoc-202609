Azar [28] introduced an approach for BC diagnosis using RBF (radial basis function), PNN (probabilistic neural network), and MLP (multi-layer perceptron). Three classifiers were trained and tested. Performance was presented using sensitivity, accuracy, and specificity. The accuracy achieved 97.8% for training and 97.66% for testing by MLP. Alickovi et al. [29] proposed a prediction method using a feature selection method called GA (genetic algorithm) and for classification (RF) rotation forest. Dataset is divided into three parts: a) for training i.e., 50% b) for testing i.e., 25% and c) for validation i.e., 25%. Resulted obtained are 98.85% as best accuracy and 98.1% as average accuracy.
