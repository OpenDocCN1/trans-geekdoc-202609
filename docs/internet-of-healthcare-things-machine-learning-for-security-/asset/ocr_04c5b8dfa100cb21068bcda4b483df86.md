BLOCKCHAIN IN SMART HEALTHCARE MANAGEMENT 177

A centralized database to store patient data to improve the efficiency of patient care and increase the overall efficiency of healthcare systems seemed the solution. This database along with storing patient's medical history also shared the total health of patients. This system was promising and increased patient care remarkably.

There are concerns about such a centralized database system that is prone to hacking. Moreover, healthcare systems are decentralized and giving responsibility to an organization raises concerns about data breaches and data leaks. Blockchain addresses all these concerns. It provides a distributed database ledger that stores entries immutable information. Every patient has a private key and a public key. The most common way to share confidential data between authorized parties can be achieved by using the RSA public-key cryptystem.

# 8.4.2 Management System

Efficient communication, easy access to patient medical history, personalized treatments and focusing on holistic views of patients are vital and would ensure better management and drastically improve healthcare outcomes and patient care. Managing healthcare data is critical and very challenging as it involves dealing with sensitive data while maintaining the trust and faith of patients that their data is secure.

The integration of blockchain with healthcare widens its scope by closely knitting together different sectors and stakeholders of the healthcare Industry which improves efficiency and reduces cost by introducing automation via Smart contracts.

As discussed earlier, smart contracts enforce appropriate rules and policies on different authorized parties when they interact among themselves.
