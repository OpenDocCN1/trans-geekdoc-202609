The authorization ensures that only eligible and designated nodes are accessible for network services or resources. While authentication can take place between two nodes (peers), authorization is a function to be performed by a central server, which has the global view of the network with its entire functionalities and assigned nodes. Even authenticated nodes may not be authorized to carry out functions that they have not received authorization to perform. This is significant since access to sensitive data and attempts to rewrite data could be thwarted by checking the authorization- status of the device or the transaction.
