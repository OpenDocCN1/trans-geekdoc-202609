Blockchain in Healthcare Systems can be securely implemented in sharing, storing and creating electronic medical records and prescriptions, personalized medicine, remote monitoring, mobile health applications and the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) vital to patient care and its improved quality. Fraud detection, insurance claims, medical transactions, checking Drug Counterfeiting, and Tracking, along with other utilities for patients, doctors, and healthcare institutes, can also be achieved using blockchain in healthcare. Other useful applications include Public Health Management and supervising clinical trials. Blockchain can be used to ensure the credibility of information related to medical resources available online. Various Machine Learning (ML) and deep learning algorithms can also be applied to data to improve treatment effectiveness and efficiency.

# 8.1 Introduction

Blockchain is one of the latest and emerging technology advances that facilitate verifiable activities between parties reliably and rapidly. It is suggested by Forecast that global revenue generated by blockchain technology is projected to have massive growth, with the market surpassing 39 billion USD by 2025. Blockchain builds on the idea of a P2P (Peer-To-Peer) network. Its architecture eliminates the need for a central supervising authority. Isn't it amazing to explore a future where there is no need to depend on a third party for their services? Many apps adopting decentralized P2P platforms (DApps or Decentralized Apps) where users and suppliers will get connected without getting controlled by any third party. Startup DApps are giving Uber and Amazon a run for their money. Blockchain is widely used in cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc. The most common hurdle in Healthcare is having access to all medical records in one place. They are generally spread among healthcare providers and stakeholders. Integration of Blockchain with healthcare can address these issues along with other challenges that are being discussed in this chapter. Blockchain proposes a mechanism that is transparent and responsible. It can revolutionize healthcare by ensuring patients' privacy, allowing them to retain ownership of their records, simultaneously giving easy access to hospitals.
