## 8.4.4 Insurance Industry

Blockchain Technology is bringing transformation in the insurance sector by introducing optimization in the business processes and sharing the information with better efficiency, security, and transparency. It is bringing the policy shift into the insurance system from manual to automated manner using the smart contracts on the peer-to-peer networks and thereby eliminating the normal processing system. There are numerous benefits that insurance companies and individuals seeking insurance can take using Blockchain Technology. With the help of decentralization, the insurance sector will get streamlined in underwriting, payments, claims, reinsurance processes.

This technology will provide higher security because the data is not stored at any centralized place and doesn't have any single entity control, thereby providing a better level of protection and cost-saving [1].

Without forcing insurers to reconcile patient data across various databases, Blockchain technology provides patients access and control over their medical data on a case by case basis. A cryptographic signature could be stored on a blockchain system for every record on a distributed ledger [18]. The signature creates an index of the data of the insurance documents in a boxed manner and timestamps it. This avoids storing delicate information on the blockchain system.

As soon as some information is changed in an insurance document, it is registered on the shared ledger, which allows insurers to inspect the data across various organizations. Concurrently, the blockchain allows the granular permissions to adhere to the rules while allowing data anonymization and sharing for research [18].

The absolute complexity of the insurance industry creates gaps that are misused to commit fraud. It is during the slow, paperwork-driven claiming procedure having various moving parts, that the claims are transferred from Insurance-Policy holders to the insurers and reinsurers. Not only does it pay the way for brokers to sell insurance policies and make money off the premiums, but also allows criminals to make multiple claims from different insurance companies for a single loss.

With the implementation of Blockchain technology in the Insurance Industry, coordination between the insurers and the insurers can be made much more efficient, thus avoiding fraud [17]. Furthermore, registering the transactions on the distributed ledger with granular access could help maintain data security. Any abnormal and suspicious behaviour could be identified and be taken care of appropriately by the insurance company as the claims would be recorded on the blockchain [18].

## 8.4.5 Drug Counterfeiting

A patient's health is largely dependent on the quality of medicinal drugs he/she takes. The research and production steps in the manufacturing of these drugs use a lot of capital. Such a high cost of medicines promotes counterfeiting. FDA defines drug/medicine counterfeiting as fake medicine that may be contaminated, contain the wrong dosage, and contain wrong or even no active ingredients in the medicine. They may contain toxic ingredients, which when consumed, can cause serious health issues.

Counterfeited drugs are introduced into the supply chain during its transportation or production stage where counterfeit drug manufacturers even use the brand logo of legitimate manufacturers to gain the trust of the consumer. World supply counterfeits are estimated to be from 10 to 15% of the total supply in first-world countries up to 30% in developing countries. Due to the intake of the counterfeit drug, every year approximately 700,000 people die from tuberculosis and malaria. The WHO estimated that 1/5th of the deaths that occur due to malaria is because of the counterfeit antimalarial drug [19].

Thus, the challenge faced by manufacturers is that their drug reaches the intended consumer in the original form and not from the counterfeit. The Pharmaceutical industry needs a transparent Supply Chain Management (SCM) system so that consumers can be assured that the drug they are receiving for treatment is genuine. The SCM system when implemented with Blockchain ensures this. Figure 8.11 depicts the integration of Blockchain in the SCM system.

With Blockchain implementation, the manufacturer would be able to track and trace its product from manufacturing until it reaches the intended distributor. Data for that specific drug, which would be stored in a blockchain, will be immutable and it would be authenticated at each step. The consumer would be assured that the product received by him is legitimate because of Blockchain’s tamper-proof nature.

## 8.4.6 Clinical Trials

Data generated during Clinical Trials are used for research purposes approval of new treatments. The integrity of this data is essential because it holds importance for various stakeholders like the researchers, journal editors, drug and device companies, government regulators, and essentially, prospective patients and the general public. Earning of the companies that have invested in research and development is at stake. Thus, the security of the data obtained from clinical trials is of utmost importance [20].

There are several risks to Clinical trial data like alterations, by mistake or even with malicious intent to achieve the expected result and false or selective reporting of an outcome of an experiment. Such manipulation of data results in variation from actual bona outcomes, causing problems for the stakeholders and even serious health hazards for the prospective patients.

The introduction of Blockchain in Healthcare for improved Clinical Trial Dataset Management would not only provide privacy to patients' records but also ensure that the data shared amongst the researchers and publishers did not tamper. This would maintain confidence between the various stakeholders as now, only authorized and trusted parties are given access to its creation and manipulation.
