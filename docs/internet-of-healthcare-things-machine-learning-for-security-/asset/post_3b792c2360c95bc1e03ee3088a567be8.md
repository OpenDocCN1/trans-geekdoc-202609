## 4.4.2 IoHT Architecture and Data Processing Stages

The various levels are inclusive of the first sensor-based level, second co-ordinator-based level, and finally, the medical server level that has been illustrated in Figure 4.4. The illustrated architecture has been employed in the developed IoHT health monitoring systems [29, 30].

Medical equipment and controls are monitored on the sensor floor, which finally concludes the formation of a local network that can also be referred as BSN an abbreviation for the Body Sensor Network. The sensors such as the RFID, Near-Field Communications (NFC) as well as BLE are utilized in the sensor as well as server level for making the remote changes possible that are included in the Low-performance wireless infrastructure standards. The BLE secures the performance in a range of several network topologies that also include the mesh-based infrastructure or the star architecture. Also, the ultra-low power-based device to device communications that are required within implantable devices are being supported by the NFC and RFID [31]. The entire data as collected by the devices are routed to the local devices such as tablets, notepads, or more complicated servers as routers and gateways. Personal servers tend to process the patient data locally and it to central medical servers before distribution. In case there have been issues associated with the network connection to the servers there should be provision to provide operation use of the personal desktops. Healthcare practitioners, including doctors, will track patients' data online and provide patients with appropriate advice. In medical libraries with the consent of patients, early diagnostic algorithms and computer performance and tests may be carried out as well. In the last decades, several IoHT-based healthcare systems for patient tracking have been proposed, but many of them do not provide protections for security and privacy, like MobiCare, or leaving out as future work. There has been more focus on the problems and consequences forming associations with the use of power as well as usability when compared to the security and privacy-related problems or the protection of the data for the patients [32].

## 4.4.3 Benefits Associated With Healthcare Based on IoT

The most profound advantages associated with IoT are mentioned below:

- Cost Reduction: The decline in frequent visits for admission.
- Improved Treatment: IoTs improving the patient’s comfort.
- Faster Diagnosis: With help of IoT a person can detect the disease at early stages.
- Proactive Treatment: Continuous monitoring allows for proactive treatment.
- Drugs and Equipment Management: Handling of drugs and other equipment managed efficiently.
- Error Reduction: IoTs provide support with fewer numbers of errors.
