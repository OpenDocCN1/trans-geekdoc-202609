### 4.3.1 Authentication

The authentication feature validates the identity (identification of communicators) or the communications exchange (message authentication). The identity will be verified simultaneously by both the sender and recipient. It needs a variety of entities, such as IoT computers, diverse servers (e.g. cloud/fog servers), separate users (i.e. mobile/static), cloud service providers, and gateways, which require authentication of each other, depending on IoT deployment.

### 4.3.2 Integrity

Integrity means maintaining the fairness and authenticity of the proof. This guarantees that the message received during communication is not wrongly inserted, unnecessary deletion, and modification. We must defend the data from any form of malicious handling.

### 4.3.3 Confidentiality

Privacy guarantees privacy security from unauthorized individuals being accessed. Often known as anonymity, messages exchanged on the channel will be secured from any kind of attack on the sharing of information.
