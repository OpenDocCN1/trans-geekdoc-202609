IoT systems are a favored target for attackers because of the existence of applications, such as the storage of confidential data and connected to many devices as shown in Figure 7.2. Additional vulnerabilities are emerging with IoT [6] beyond the real internet security problems. Specific applications can pose a serious life or business risk [15–17]. Imagine a scenario in which an intruder takes control of a car. Other threats include private information theft (privacy/confidentiality), information alteration (authenticity), and system malfunctioning (availability and resilience). Furthermore, an affected device can pose potential threats to ensure secure connections, such as when a hacked device, along with a web camera, that is connected to the secure home network.

Unauthorized access to data and equipment raises concerns regarding patient safety in the healthcare system. Data confidentiality is one of the key aspects of protection that must be taken into consideration when sharing data.
