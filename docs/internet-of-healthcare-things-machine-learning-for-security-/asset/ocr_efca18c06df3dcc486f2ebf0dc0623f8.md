Irrespective of the choices made in the design and selection of devices, it is ultimately the customer perception that would infuse confidence in terms of security—demanding a clear translation of the design of security into demonstrable and vivid results.

With the exploding number of new devices introduced in the healthcare market every day, the designers need to keep the flexibility to scale up performance by incorporating modularity and scalability in design and implementation.

Access to the medical server and database should be strictly restricted to a special, authorized category of users. The system integrator should determine the amount of sensitive and personal data that may be made available in the cloud that is accessible to users through the internet.

Physical and virtual controls for access need consideration in terms of enabling security mechanisms and the practice of logging instances of special access and remote access are recommended for the overall upkeep of the system.
