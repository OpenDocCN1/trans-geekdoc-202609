# 2. Experimental section

# 2.1. Solanum tor Sw. Fruits

The fruits of S. torvam Sw. were collected from shelves of the Western Ghats in near Shenbagathoppu Hills (at long- tudi: 7.6225; 9.5225); Srivilliputh, Virunagagar, Tamil Nadu, India. The surface of the fruits was washed with water and dried at shade for two weeks. The dried fruits were ground into fine powder using mesh grinder (Greenree Elects Ltd., Chennai, India). The powder was then screened to vary particle size powders (0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5 mm) using several sieves (Mesh No. 35, 25, 18, 16, and 2). The samples were stored under air condi- tenter until the start of the experiment (moisture content: 10 ± 2%).

# 2.2. Chemicals and reagents

Solvent colorimetric reagents, ABTS, DPPH were supplied by Sigma Aldrich, Bengaluru, India. Methanol, Folin-Ciocalteu reagent, sodium carbonate, sodium hydride, aluminum chloride, hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) were purchased from HiMedia Pt. Ltd., Mumbai, India. All other chemicals and reagents were of analytical or equivalent grade and obtained from Merck and SRL, Ltd., India.

# 2.3. Ultrasound-assisted solvent extraction

Using an ultrasound-assisted solvent extraction (UAE), the bioactive phytochemicals were extracted from the fine powder of S. torvam Sw. The experimental apparatus had an ultrasonic water bath (temperature precision of ±1.0 °C, 220 V, and continu- us at 20 kHz intensity ultrasonic processor) including a jacketed reactor with 25 ml capacity (capacity assessed by AC Pharma, India). The concentration of phytochemicals were measured using a Shimadzu UV-1800 series UV-Visible spectrophotometer using UV Probe 262 software from Japan.

# 2.4. Preliminary experiments for identification of ideal solvent system

The preliminary experiments were conducted to identify the ideal solvent system based on the concentration of TF, TP and DPPH* using S. torvam Sw. The solvents such as methanol, ethanol, ethyl acetate, diethyl ether, chloro- form, and n-hexane were used for the preliminary experiments. The extraction was carried out in each solvent system (20 ml of 70% v/w in water) with 2 g of powdered S. torvam Sw. with the extraction conditions of particle size: 0.5 mm, ultrasound intensity: 60 W, and temperature: 65 °C.

# 2.5. Selection of process parameters

The five extraction parameters (ordered parameters) were selected as solvent concentration (55-66% v/w in water), ultrasound intensity (50-60 W cm⁻2), temperature (30-45 °C), and time (1.5-25 min) were selected for the ma- yield of bioactive phytochemicals based on the dependent parameters, such as total phenols (TP), total flavonoids (TF), 2,2'-2,1'-diphenylhydrazyl (DPPH*), 2,2'-bis(3-sulfonopropyl)-6-ionic acid (ABTS), and hydrogen peroxide (H2O₂) measuring antioxidant ability of S. torvam Sw. Further in comparison the optimal solvent was chosen as the best solvent based on the results of preliminary experimental studies (Table 1).
