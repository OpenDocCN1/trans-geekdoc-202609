Biologically important compounds based on phenolic flavonoids and their free radical scavenging potential were harvested from Solanum swartz through ultrasound-aided solvent extraction (UAE) method. The free-centered central design (FCCD) in response surface methodology was applied to optimize the extraction parameters, and adaptive-fuzzy artificial intelligence system (ANFIS), as well as machine learning algorithm models were used to predict the yield of bioactive phytochemicals. The effect of extraction parameters, methanol concentration (X₁: 55-66% in v/w), ultrasound-intensity (X₂: 50-60 cm²), temperature (X₃: 30-45 °C), time (X₄: 15-25 min) and particle size (X₅: 0.5-1.5 mm) for each level (+2.3) were studied for optimized yields of total phenolic (TP; y₁), total flavonoid compounds (y₂), and their free radical scavenging abilities (%DPPH% (y₃), %ABTS% (y₄), and %ORAC% (y₅₅)). The optimal condition was achieved at X₁: 65%, X₂: 40 cm², X₄: 25 min, and X₅: 1.5 mm, where the highest content of gallic acid (GAE) was 5.38 mg/g (y₇); y₈ = 75.22%, and y₉ = 85.02% was obtained. Most of the experimental results were well matched with the predictions of the RSM model. Further, ANFIS and random forest machine learning algorithm were utilized to compare and validate the optimized extraction parameters. Also, GC-MS and LC-MS were used to investigate the presence of various bioactive phytochemicals in the extract.

Cancer is the most dreadful disease that can significantly reduce the quality of life of an individual, and is the leading cause of mortality (Stucker and Basu, 2011). Based on survey and data collection, International Agency for Research and Cancer (IARC) reported 10 million deaths in 2019 and 19.3 million deaths in 2020 (Morgan et al., 2023). The number of cancer worldwide is predicted to reach 28.4 million in 2040, which is 47 percent increase from 2020 (Yan et al., 2023). Currently, cancer is treated by various therapeutic strategies, chemotherapy, hormonal therapy, immunotherapy, and followed by radiation therapy (Yang et al., 2005). Though chemotherapics have potential solutions in reducing cancer symptoms and help to extend the lives of the patients, many of the chemotherapics have undesirable side effects (Brianis, 2023). Use of natural, particularly bioactive compounds from plants enhance the area of drug discovery. Naturally, plants provide a wide range of secondary metabolites in the phenolic compounds in their tissues to protect themselves against various pathogens, pathogens, and diseases (Ganes et al. 2018). Recently, bioactive compounds from plants gained more attention due to their therapeutic potential (Chandrasekhar, 2020).
