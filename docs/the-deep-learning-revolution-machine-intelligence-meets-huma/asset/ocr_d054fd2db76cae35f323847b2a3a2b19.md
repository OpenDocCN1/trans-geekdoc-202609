Deep space probes were being sent out to the far reaches of the solar system, but the signals coming back were weak and noisy. Shift register sequences and error-correcting codes greatly improved communication with space probes, and the same mathematics laid the foundation for modern digital communications.

Golomb hired Andrew Viterbi at JPL, another distinguished information theorist, and introduced him to Irwin Jacobs, whom he had invited to visit JPL on a sabbatical from MIT.
