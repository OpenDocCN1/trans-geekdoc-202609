# Figure 9.4

Picture captioning with deep learning. The upper panel illustrates the procedure that analyzes the photo. The ConvNet (CNN) in the first step labels the objects in the photo and passes this on to the recurrent neural network (RNN). The RNN was trained to output an appropriate string of English words. The bottom four panels illustrate a further refinement that uses attention (white cloud) to indicate the referent of the word in the photo. Top: From M. I. Jordan and T. M.
