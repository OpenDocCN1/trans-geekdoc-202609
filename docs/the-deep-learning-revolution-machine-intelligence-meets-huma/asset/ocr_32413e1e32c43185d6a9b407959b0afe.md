The fourth clue is that our brains are filled with billions and billions of tiny neurons that are constantly communicating with one another. This suggests that, for solutions to the hard problems in artificial intelligence, we should be looking into computers with massively parallel architectures rather than those with von Neumann digital architectures through which data and instructions are fetched and executed one at a time.
