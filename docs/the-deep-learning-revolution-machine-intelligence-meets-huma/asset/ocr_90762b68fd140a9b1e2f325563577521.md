Cover headline for Noam Chomsky's 1971 takedown of B. F. Skinner in the *New York Review of Books*. Chomsky's essay would influence a generation of scientists to abandon behavioral learning and take up symbol processing as a way to explain cognition. But with the symbolic approach, artificial intelligence never achieved cognitive levels of performance. B. F.
