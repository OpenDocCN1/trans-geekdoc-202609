**Figure 16.2**
Halle Berry cell. The responses to photos of a single neuron recorded from the hippocampus of a patient. Spikes (blue tics) from six individual trials are shown below each photo, along with the average (histograms). (A) Photos of Halle Berry and her name elicited a burst of spikes, whereas (B) photos of other actresses and their names did not. Halle Barry starred in the 2004 action superhero film Catwoman (photo 3). From A. D. Friederici and W. Singer, “Grounding Language Processing on Basic Neurophysiological Principles,” Trends in Cognitive Sciences 19, no. 6 (2015): 329–338, figure 1.
