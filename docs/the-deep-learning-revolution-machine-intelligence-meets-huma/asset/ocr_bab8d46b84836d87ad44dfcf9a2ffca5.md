# Figure 5.11

Hierarchy of visual areas in the monkey brain. Visual information from retinal ganglion cells (RGC) in the retina project to the lateral geniculate nucleus (LGN) of the thalamus, whose relay cells project to the primary visual cortex (V1). The hierarchy of cortical areas terminates in the hippocampus (HC). Nearly all of the 187 links in the diagram are bidirectional, with feedforward connection from a lower area and feedback connection from the higher area. From D. J. Felleman and D. C. Van Essen, “Distributed Hierarchical Processing in Primate Visual Cortex,” Cerebral Cortex 1, no. 1 (1991): 30, figure 4.
