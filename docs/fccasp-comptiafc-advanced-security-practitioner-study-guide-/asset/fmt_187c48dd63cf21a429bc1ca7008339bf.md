
## 8. Know how and when to apply security controls

Controls may or may not be applied. All companies have only limited funds to implement controls, and the cost of the control should not exceed the value of the asset. Performing a quantitative or qualitative risk assessment can help make the case for whether a control should be applied.

## 9. Know the various ways to address risk

Threats coupled with vulnerabilities can lead to a loss. To prevent or deal with the potential risk, several approaches can be used, which include accept, avoid, mitigate, or transfer.
