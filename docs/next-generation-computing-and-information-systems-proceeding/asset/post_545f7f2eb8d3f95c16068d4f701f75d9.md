Decision Tree as well as Random Forest models performed exceptionally well, achieving perfect precision, recall, along with the F1 score for both diabetic and non-diabetic cases. Lastly, SVM demonstrated perfect precision (1) for diabetic cases but had a lower recall as 0.39 and F1 score as 0.56, indicating challenges in identifying all diabetic cases.
