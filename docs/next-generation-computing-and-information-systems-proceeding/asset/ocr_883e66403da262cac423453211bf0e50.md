These findings provide valuable information to select the most appropriate or best machine learning model based on specific application requirements. Nevertheless, it is crucial to recognize the constraints of our study. The success of machine learning models relies heavily on the caliber as well as volume of data. With the ongoing progress in machine learning algorithms and healthcare data collection, there is potential for future research to delve into more advanced algorithms and diverse datasets. This could lead to even more accurate and widely applicable results.
