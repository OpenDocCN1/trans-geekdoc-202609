
We have used different evaluation metrics to evaluate the performance of different models. These metrics provide a more holistic assessment of a categorization model's performance instead of relying only on accuracy. Prediction accuracy refers to the proportion of positive situations that happen. The F1-score is computed by taking the harmonic mean of the accuracy and recall. Recall, in contrast, quantifies the proportion of true positive cases properly detected by the model. Uneven class distribution may be effectively addressed by ensuring a balance between recall and accuracy, which offers significant advantages.
