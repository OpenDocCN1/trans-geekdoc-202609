Data analysis reveals that Shamir method and Secure Hash Algorithm-256 (SHA-256) are reportedly the most used, for reasons such as strong encryption and compatibility with other methods, apart from the threshold signature scheme. Multiplicative Secret Sharing is also commonly used. However, it is not strong enough and is only compatible with a few other methods. A bitcoin wallet uses SHA-256 for stronger encryption.
