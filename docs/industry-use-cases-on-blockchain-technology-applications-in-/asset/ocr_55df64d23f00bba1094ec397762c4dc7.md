# Table 3. Advantages and disadvantages of the Blockchain technology across sectors

| Industry | Area of Application | Advantages | Disadvantages |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Governance | • Voting<br>• Land transactions<br>• Tax regulations<br>• Education<br>• Public services<br>• Inheritance<br>• Charity organizations<br>• Labor rights<br>• Authentication | • A transparent, reliable and verifiable system<br>• Data security in the case of natural disasters<br>• Protection of assets, reporting and accounting, accessibility, cost savings and effective collateral management | • Vulnerability of internet-based systems<br>• Slow-down of the system due to newly-added blocks<br>• Failure to maintain privacy under all circumstances<br>• Selfish miners putting the system in trouble |
| Health | • Storing medical records<br>• Health services | • Security of patient data<br>• System with which the patient is actively involved rather than a system controlled by a centralized structure<br>• Storage and analysis of health information as a whole | • Obligation to train the patient for enabling his/her involvement with the system<br>• The possibility that the confidential data will be released due to technical failure although unauthorized access is not allowed. |
| Finance | • Mutual funds<br>• Crypto exchanges<br>• Stocks<br>• Insurance<br>• Credit records<br>• Crowdfunding | • A fast, low-cost and simple system<br>• Reliable system by virtue of controllable and non-tampering blocks<br>• A recoverable system in the case of a failure | • Difficulty in implementing the entire BC structure due to the fact that banking systems which are used in daily life need a certain degree of centralization<br>• Prolongation of the verification time for the system along with growing number of blocks and the subsequent slow-downs in the system |
| Technology | • Cloud storage<br>• Data backup<br>• IoT management<br>• Cloud computing<br>• Messaging applications | • Data consistency and rapid verification of operations<br>• Enhancement of productivity and flexibility and low cost by virtue of eliminating third-party agents<br>• The formation of fast moving systems with BC open-source code sharing | • Cost and capacity constraints<br>• Manipulation of data<br>• Cloud servers are susceptible to cyber-attacks during downtime<br>• Data theft |
| Others | • Supply chain<br>• Copyright protection<br>• Food safety<br>• Car-sharing<br>• Internet advertisements<br>• Forecasting systems<br>• Energy management<br>• Human rights<br>• Customer recognition systems<br>• Quality control<br>• Logistics<br>• Waste management | • Transforming the traditional structure for creating reliable, decentralized, scalable and unique structures<br>• Ensuring integrity by creating a common database<br>• Protection of author's copyright | • 51% of the BC is seized and the system is at great risk<br>• Fork problem in the system<br>• High cost of converting existing system infrastructure to BC structure<br>• Uncertainty about regulations<br>• Lack of clarity about smart contracts<br>• Synchronization problem in non-interoperable systems |

Merits and demerits of the BC technology vary but do also share similarities depending on the industry where the BC technology is used. That is why, addressing BC applications in a way to be specific to each industry will raise the likelihood of success.
