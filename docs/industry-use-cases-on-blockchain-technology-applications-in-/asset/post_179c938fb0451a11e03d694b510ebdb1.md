## Blockchain in Automotive Industry: Advantages and Disadvantages

Digital technologies which shape the future have presented a variety of connections and software to the automobiles and their manufacturers. In the context of IT data security, this situation has become a source of anxiety for all parties involved in the production and delivery value chain. However, the emergence of the BC technology has played a key role in relieving this anxiety.

### Table 6. Challenges for the automotive industry in BC applications

| Stakeholder | Challenges Likely To Be Faced |
| :--- | :--- |
| Car owners and lenders / buyers and sellers of pre-owned cars | - Lack of transparency about automobile's history<br>- Unexpected vehicle maintenance and repair costs<br>- Absence of trust in the outcome of maintenance and repair works<br>- Lack of informed purchase options<br>- Absence of car insurance options<br>- Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles<br>- High-level transactional experience for consumers while reducing costs incurred by them |
| Fleet management companies / Car leasing or sharing (car-sharing, ride-sharing or ride-hailing) companies | - Lack of transparency about automobile's history<br>- Unexpected vehicle maintenance and repair costs<br>- Absence of trust in the outcome of maintenance and repair works<br>- Lack of interoperability with business partners<br>- High operational costs, low profit margins<br>- High risks in car sharing, ride-sharing and ride-hailing economy<br>- Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles |
| Car-sharing, ride-sharing or ride-hailing passengers | - Driving/riding cars more affordably<br>- Better maintained cars<br>- Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles<br>- Absence of a common platform for providing mobility<br>- Lack of instant payments |
| Car entrepreneurs | - High vehicle leasing costs<br>- Lower car-sharing, ride-sharing or ride-hailing partnership fees<br>- Difficulties in setting up business, unfair competition<br>- Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles<br>- Absence of information sharing |
| Car dealers and retailers | - Updated vehicle ownership records<br>- Updated repair and maintenance records<br>- Updated purchase records<br>- Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles<br>- Absence of information sharing |
| OEM / Car manufacturers and suppliers | - High warranty claim costs<br>- Forcing dealers to adhere to recommended maintenance and repair prices<br>- Customer complaints because of automobile dealers’ acts in violation of maintenance fees recommended by automobile manufacturers<br>- Lack of control over the vehicle maintenance services performed by the authorized dealers<br>- Low level of customer loyalty<br>- Cyber-attacks and system failure risks in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles<br>- Control of logistics<br>- Absence of information sharing |
| Insurance companies | - Inflexible and non-customized policy pricing<br>- 5-10% of all claims across the world are fraudulent<br>- Costly and inefficient claim management<br>- Erroneous customer policy pricing<br>- Lack of oversight over the quality and pricing for collision repair |
| Independent repair shops | - Underutilized capacity<br>- Customer retention<br>- Low profit margins<br>- Lack of brand confidence |
| After-market (producers, distributors and retailers of spare parts, garages) | - Inefficient stock management<br>- Counterfeit spare parts market<br>- Lack of transparency in the warranty monitoring and enforcement<br>- Low profit margins<br>- Lack of brand confidence |
| Governments and public organizations | - Updated state records (e.g. vehicle maintenance records, ownership rights, vehicle taxes, traffic fine history)<br>- Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles<br>- Adherence to the existing regulations especially in terms of driver liability or data protection<br>- Enhanced interconnectivity along with open-source traffic data and infrastructure data offered through a data cloud, and access to shift to digital radio and universal network coverage<br>- Greater use of anonymization and pseudonymization in data collection, and processing comprehensive information and providing vehicle owners and drivers with information on which data are collected and by whom.<br>- Real-time notifications about road conditions and traffic congestion<br>- Reliable data on accident research and airbag sensors |
| Financial institutions | - Updated vehicle ownership records and automobile insurance, maintenance and lien records<br>- Absence of a single reference point for all transactions |
| Telecommunication and tech companies, content and service providers | - Guaranteeing stable and secure vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication to assure efficient and safe vehicle coordination and cooperation.<br>- Lack of reliable connection between vehicles and between vehicles and infrastructure |
| Scrappage/recycle and environmental groups | - Control of greenhouse gas emissions<br>- Full traceability of components<br>- Long-term sustainability |
| Academia | - Guaranteeing vehicle safety, security, and autonomy<br>- More efficient driving, development of the optimized Human-Machine Interfaces (HMI)<br>- Tackling traffic management of highly and fully automated vehicles under mixed traffic conditions |
