# Congestion Analysis

Figure. 16 illustrates transactions in an 8-member blockchain network for 6 imports from the farmer. The authors computed a congestion analysis for a blockchain environment of 8 participants (p1 to p8 – p1: farmer, p2: logistic company, p3: logistic company, p4: JLP meats, p5 to p6 are clients) and 6 imports as illustrated in 6 series. Here, p4 is JLP Meats that creates thousands of products from commodities.

There are more than one P4 type participants in most of the supply chains and that leads to creating transaction congestion on blockchains and delays transaction verification. That is, future blockchain-based supply chain systems should have methods and standards to manage transaction congestion. For example, keeping redundant information locally while recording the hash of this information on the blockchain to assure trust. However, the private channels, local data stores, and plug-and-play modular architecture of Hyperledger Fabric can be improved to develop necessary solutions.
