# Table 6. Challenges for the automotive industry in BC applications

| Stakeholder | Challenges Likely To Be Faced |
| :--- | :--- |
| Car owners and lenders / buyers and sellers of pre-owned cars | • Lack of transparency about automobile's history\n• Unexpected vehicle maintenance and repair costs\n• Absence of trust in the outcome of maintenance and repair works\n• Lack of informed purchase options\n• Absence of car insurance options\n• Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles\n• High-level transactional experience for consumers while reducing costs incurred by them |
| Fleet management companies / Car leasing or sharing (car-sharing, ride-sharing or ride-hailing) companies | • Lack of transparency about automobile's history\n• Unexpected vehicle maintenance and repair costs\n• Absence of trust in the outcome of maintenance and repair works\n• Lack of interoperability with business partners\n• High operational costs, low profit margins\n• High risks in car sharing, ride-sharing and ride-hailing economy\n• Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles |
| Car-sharing, ride-sharing or ride-hailing passengers | • Driving/riding cars more affordably\n• Better maintained cars\n• Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles\n• Absence of a common platform for providing mobility\n• Lack of instant payments |
| Car entrepreneurs | • High vehicle leasing costs\n• Lower car-sharing, ride-sharing or ride-hailing partnership fees\n• Difficulties in setting up business, unfair competition\n• Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles\n• Absence of information sharing |
| Car dealers and retailers | • Updated vehicle ownership records\n• Updated repair and maintenance records\n• Updated purchase records\n• Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles\n• Absence of information sharing |
| OEM / Car manufacturers and suppliers | • High warranty claim costs\n• Forcing dealers to adhere to recommended maintenance and repair prices\n• Customer complaints because of automobile dealers’ acts in violation of maintenance fees recommended by automobile manufacturers\n• Lack of control over the vehicle maintenance services performed by the authorized dealers\n• Low level of customer loyalty\n• Cyber-attacks and system failure risks in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles\n• Control of logistics\n• Absence of information sharing |
| Insurance companies | • Inflexible and non-customized policy pricing\n• 5-10% of all claims across the world are fraudulent\n• Costly and inefficient claim management\n• Erroneous customer policy pricing\n• Lack of oversight over the quality and pricing for collision repair |
| Independent repair shops | • Underutilized capacity\n• Customer retention\n• Low profit margins\n• Lack of brand confidence |
| After-market (producers, distributors and retailers of spare parts, garages) | • Inefficient stock management\n• Counterfeit spare parts market\n• Lack of transparency in the warranty monitoring and enforcement\n• Low profit margins\n• Lack of brand confidence |
| Governments and public organizations | • Updated state records (e.g. vehicle maintenance records, ownership rights, vehicle taxes, traffic fine history)\n• Lack of trust in autonomous and IoT-connected vehicles\n• Adherence to the existing regulations especially in terms of driver liability or data protection\n• Enhanced interconnectivity along with open-source traffic data and infrastructure data offered through a data cloud, and cagemess to shift to digital radio and universal network coverage\n• Greater use of anonymization and pseudonymization in data collection, and processing comprehensive information and providing vehicle owners and drivers with information on which data are collected and by whom.\n• Real-time notifications about road conditions and traffic congestion\n• Reliable data on accident research and airbag sensors |
| Financial institutions | • Updated vehicle ownership records and automobile insurance, maintenance and lien records\n• Absence of a single reference point for all transactions |
| Telecommunication and tech companies, content and service providers | • Guaranteeing stable and secure vehicle-to-vehicle (V2V) and vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication to assure efficient and safe vehicle coordination and cooperation.\n• Lack of reliable connection between vehicles and between vehicles and infrastructure |
| Scrappage/recycle and environmental groups | • Control of greenhouse gas emissions\n• Full traceability of components\n• Long-term sustainability |
| Academia | • Guaranteeing vehicle safety, security, and autonomy\n• More efficient driving, development of the optimized Human-Machine Interfaces (HMI)\n• Tackling traffic management of highly and fully automated vehicles under mixed traffic conditions |
