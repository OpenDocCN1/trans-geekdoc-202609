# Developing E-Government Projects: Frameworks and Methodologies

This text presents frameworks and methodologies for strategies for the design, implementation of e-government projects. It illustrates the best practices for successful adoption of e-government and thus becomes essential for policy makers, practitioners and researchers for the successful deployment of e-government planning and projects.
