9.5 ☕☕☕ (Quadratic empirical process) In Exercise 8.36, we bounded the deviation of the empirical mean from the sample mean for any subgaussian process. Now, let's do the same for the L² mean by extending Theorem 9.1.1. Take any star-shaped⁹ class ℱ of real-valued functions on a domain Ω, and let X, X₁, X₂, …, Xₙ be i.i.d. random points in Ω. Consider the L² metric on ℱ

\[ d(f,g) := \big( \mathbb{E}(f(X) - g(X))^2 \big)^{1/2} \]

and assume that

\[ \|f(X) - g(X)\|_{ψ₂} \le K d(f,g) \quad \text{for all } f,g ∈ ℱ. \]

Then show:

\[ \mathbb{E} \sup_{f∈ℱ} \Big| \Big( \frac{1}{m} \sum_{i=1}^{m} f(X_i)^2 \Big)^{1/2} - \big( \mathbb{E} f(X)^2 \big)^{1/2} \Big| \le \frac{C K^2 γ₂(F,d)}{\sqrt{m}}, \]

where in the right hand side we have the γ₂ functional from Section 8.5. Can you see why this result contains Theorem 9.1.1 if T ⊂ S^{n-1}?
