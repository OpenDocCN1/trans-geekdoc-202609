As an extra feature offered by Optuna, with a few simple additions to the previous code, you can store your study in a project database and restart optimization at any time. Optuna can integrate its optimization procedures with SQLite if, at the time of the creation of your study, you declare the name of the study and a target database:

```python
sqlite_db = "sqlite:///sqlite.db"
study_name = "optimize_GBoost"
study = optuna.create_study(storage=sqlite_db, study_name=study_name,
direction="maximize", load_if_exists=True)
study.optimize(objective, n_trials=60)
print(study.best_params)
print(study.best_value)
```
