Suppose your searches around data relative to the public or macroeconomic sphere, as transportation, energy, political participation, commerce, industrial production, consumption, and so on. In that case, the data portals should provide where to look for open data (http://dataportals.org/), which covers the world, and Open Data Monitor (https://opendatamon.eu/), which specializes in the European region. Another good source is the National Statistical Service. You can browse for specific examples in this comprehensive list provided by the United States Census Bureau (https://mng.gov/).

Apart from these specific examples, the best way to come into contact with the type of open data is undoubtedly through the dataset search engine Google, which can be found at https://dataset.search.google/. The Google Dataset Search, comprising many possible sources and scattered repositories, should give you access to what you are looking for through search results.
