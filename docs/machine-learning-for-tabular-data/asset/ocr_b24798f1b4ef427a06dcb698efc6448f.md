The critics of deep learning with tabular data have contributed research to make their case, including the following papers:

+   * Why Do Tree-Based Models Outperform Deep Learning on Tabular Data? (https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.08815)
* Tabular Data: Deep Learning Is Not All You Need (https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.03253)
* Deep Neural Networks and Tabular Data: A Survey (https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01889)

This list, and the preceding list of pro deep learning papers, are by no means exhaustive. However, it is fair to say that more research is published that advocates for deep learning with tabular data than research published to argue against deep learning with tabular data.
