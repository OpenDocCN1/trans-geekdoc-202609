In our experience, choosing a forward or backward selection depends on the decision you may have to leave out some slightly important feature from your set. With forward addition, you are sure to keep only the essential features but risk leaving marginally relevant out. With the backward elimination, you are assured that all the key features are in the set, for some redundancy.

Besides choosing a forward or backward procedure, sequential selection will help you build models in training and prediction and will be much easier to interpret and maintain because of limited number of features used.
