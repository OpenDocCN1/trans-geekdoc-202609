Figure 2. Incremental prognostic value of major adverse cardiovascular event (MACE) across 5 different models. Discriminatory value for MACE (CREATION registry primary end point: all-cause death, myocardial infarction, coronary revascularization, and stroke). Across five different models in the training cohort (A) and the testing cohort (B). Receiver operating characteristic curves of a model with clinical characteristics included sex, age, diabetes, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, smoking, obesity, family history of coronary artery disease (CAD), and nonhigh density lipoprotein (HDL) cholesterol levels (model 1), a model with clinical and coronary artery calcium score (CACS; model 2), a model of coronary computed tomography angiography (CCTA; 6-features included percent lipid plaque volume (PV), vessel volume, lipid PV, presence of high-risk plaque (HRP), stenosis and total plaque volume (TPV; model 3), a model with clinical and CCTA features (model 4), and a model combined clinical, CACS, and CCTA features (model 5). AUC indicates area under the curve.
