# Subgroup Analysis

The ML model was subsequently evaluated within selected subgroups stratified by gender (female versus male), chest pain (symptomatic versus asymptomatic), and CAD onset ages (early onset versus late-onset). The CCTA model similarly exhibited consistently improved discrimination ability for predicting MACE compared with traditional clinical risk factors and CACS across these 3 subgroups (Online Appendix; Tables S9 through S11, and Figures S7 through S9).

# Sensitivity Analysis

In this study, the proportion of individuals with normal coronary arteries was relatively high. Therefore, a sensitivity analysis was performed, excluding the normal coronary arteries, only focusing on individuals with coronary atherosclerosis and CACS >0. The discriminatory performance of the 5 models did not show significant changes (Online Appendix; Tables S12 and S13; Figures S10 and S11). The MACE prediction remained consistent when compared with the entire suspected CAD population.
