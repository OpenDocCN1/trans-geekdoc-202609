The results presented in Table 4 and Table S7 illustrate the calibration of the clinical model, clinical+CACS, CCTA, and combined models within both the training and testing cohorts, as assessed quantitatively using the Brier score. All 5 models demonstrated good calibration, with the calibration improving progressively in combined models. We calculated the NRI by comparing the CCTA model with the clinical+CACS model. In the training cohort, the NRI was 0.241 (95% CI, 0.153–0.330), while in the testing cohort, the NRI was 0.420 (95% CI, 0.247–0.593). The findings indicate that the CCTA model offers superior predictive accuracy for MACE compared with the clinical + CACS model and significantly enhances risk reclassification. The observed NRI increase underscores that the CCTA model has a notable advantage in identifying patients with high risk, who were not sufficiently predicted by the clinical + CACS model. Overall, the CCTA model remains excellent in calibration, making it reliable and practical for clinical applications.

## Figure 1. Feature importance ranking of the extreme gradient boosting (XGBoost) machine learning model for major adverse cardiovascular event (MACE). A, The location of each feature on the y-axis ranks its importance according to the model prediction in descending order. For each feature, 1 dot represents a single patient, and the dot's color is an indicator that that feature value, where blue represents the lower value while red denotes the higher value. A dot's position along the x-axis (ie, the actual Shapley additive explanations [SHAP] value) illustrates the impact that the feature had on the model's output (positively or negatively) for that specific patient. Mathematically, this corresponds to the (logarithm of the) likelihood of MACE across patients (ie, a patient with a higher SHAP value has a higher likelihood of MACE relative to a patient with a lower SHAP value). B, Similarly, the SHAP summary plot illustrates the top 20 features contributing to the initial XGB model's prediction of MACE. Features are positioned along the y-axis based on importance. The best features with the highest information gain were percent lipid plaque volume (PV), vessel volume, lip PV, presence of high-risk plaque (HRP), stenosis, and total plaque volume (TPV). CPV indicates calcified plaque volume; LAD, left anterior descending coronary artery; LM, left main coronary artery; NCPV, noncalcified plaque volume; and RCA, right coronary artery.
