In the fluid mechanics community, the first investigation of the ability of DRL to produce robust control strategies was presented by Tang et al. (2020).⁶ In this work, the DRL agent was trained on a range of Reynolds numbers rather than a single fixed number, as was done in previous studies. This means that the agent is trained on a range of flow conditions that, while broadly exhibiting the same patterns, still differ in the precise behavior and responsiveness of the wake. Experiments showed that the DRL agent is able to learn a robust global control strategy, and to perform control over a wide range of Reynolds numbers, including ones not seen during training. The main findings of this work are visualized in Figure 18.5. Such findings are very promising for applications in the real world, where one would expect a similar variation of flow properties.
