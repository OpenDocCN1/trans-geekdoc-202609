are cosine functions. The solution via EF expansion (see Mendez & J.M-Buchlin 2016) is

$$\hat{u}(\hat{y},\hat{t}) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \phi_{\mathcal{E} n}(\hat{y})\,\sigma_{\mathcal{E} n}\,\psi_{\mathcal{E} n}(\hat{t}),\tag{8.34}$$

with

$$\phi_{\mathcal{E} n} = \cos\!\left[(2n-1)\frac{\pi}{2}\hat{y}\right],$$
$$\sigma_{\mathcal{E} n} = \frac{16\hat{p}_A}{(2n-1)\pi\sqrt{16\mathcal{W}^4 + (2n-1)^4\pi^4}},\tag{8.35}$$
$$\psi_{\mathcal{E} n} = (-1)^n \cos\!\left[\hat{t} - \tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{\mathcal{W}}{[(2n-1)\pi]}\right)\right]^2.$$

Note that the spatial structures are orthogonal but the temporal ones are not. The amplitudes of the modes decay as $\propto 1/(2n-1)^3$ when $\mathcal{W} \to 0$ and as $\propto 1/(2n-1)$ when $\mathcal{W} \to \infty$. Finally, note that all the amplitudes tend to $\sigma_{\mathcal{E} n} \to 0$ as $\mathcal{W} \to \infty$: as the frequency of the perturbation increases, the oscillations in the velocity profile are attenuated.
