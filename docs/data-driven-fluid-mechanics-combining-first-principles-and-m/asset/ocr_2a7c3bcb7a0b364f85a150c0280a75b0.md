Since H* H ≠ I, this is not a similarity transform. Hence the eigenvalues of these two matrices are different: there is no simple link between σ_P and the σ_P,H, nor between the POD of the data and the POD of the filtered data. We do not attempt to build such a connection: it suffices to notice that the POD of the filtered data is the optimal basis for the portion of data whose spectra fall within the band-pass region of the filter. In the terminology of MRA (see Chapter 4), we call this a scale.
