# Computation of the basic mode:

The basic mode is a time-averaged flow and absorbs a potential inhomogeneity of the steady boundary condition,

$$\boldsymbol{u}_0(\boldsymbol{x}) := \frac{1}{M} \sum_{m=1}^{M} \boldsymbol{u}^m(\boldsymbol{x}). \tag{14.8}$$

One example is the uniform free-stream condition $\boldsymbol{u} = U\hat{\boldsymbol{e}}_x$ in the far-field, where $\hat{\boldsymbol{e}}_x$ is the unit vector in streamwise direction. The remaining fluctuation $\boldsymbol{u}' = \boldsymbol{u} - \boldsymbol{u}_0$ satisfies homogenized boundary conditions, for example, $\boldsymbol{u}' = 0$ in case of the uniform free-stream condition. This implies that any expansion mode $\boldsymbol{u}_i$ will satisfy these homogenized boundary conditions. In other words, the expansion (14.4) satisfies the full boundary conditions for arbitrary choices of amplitudes $a_i$, $i = 1,\dots,N.$

# Computation of the correlation matrix:

The $M \times M$ correlation matrix $\boldsymbol{R}$ has the elements

$$\boldsymbol{R}_{mn} := \frac{1}{M} (\boldsymbol{u}^m - \boldsymbol{u}_0, \boldsymbol{u}^n - \boldsymbol{u}_0)_\Omega, \quad m,n = 1,\dots,M. \tag{14.9}$$

# Spectral analysis of the correlation matrix:

This Gramian matrix $\boldsymbol{R}$ is symmetric and positive semi-definite. This implies real and nonnegative eigenvalues as well as orthogonal eigenvectors. Let

$$\alpha_i := \begin{bmatrix} \alpha_i^1 \\ \vdots \\ \alpha_i^M \end{bmatrix}$$ 
be the $i$th eigenvector of the correlation matrix, that is,

$$\boldsymbol{R}\,\alpha_i = \lambda_i\,\alpha_i, \quad i = 1,\dots,M. \tag{14.10}$$

Without loss of generality, we assume the real eigenvalues to be sorted in decreasing order

$$\lambda_1 \ge \lambda_2 \ge \cdots \ge \lambda_M = 0,$$

and that the eigenvectors are orthonormal

$$\alpha_i \cdot \alpha_j = \sum_{m=1}^{M} \alpha_i^m \alpha_j^m = \delta_{ij}, \quad i,j = 1,\dots,M.$$

Note that the $M$th eigenvalue must vanish, because $M$ snapshots span at most an $M-1$-dimensional hyperplane.

# Computation of the POD modes:

The POD modes are given by

$$\boldsymbol{u}_i(\boldsymbol{x}) := \frac{1}{\sqrt{M\,\lambda_i}} \sum_{m=1}^{M} \alpha_i^m \left(\boldsymbol{u}^m(\boldsymbol{x}) - \boldsymbol{u}_0(\boldsymbol{x})\right), \quad i = 1,\dots,N. \tag{14.11}$$

Here, $N \le M - 1$ is the number of expansion modes in (14.4). The POD modes are orthonormal by construction:

$$(\boldsymbol{u}_i, \boldsymbol{u}_j)_\Omega = \delta_{ij}, \quad i,j = 1,\dots,N.$$
