
# Randomized Response

Finally, we must correct for the randomness of the coin flips to estimate the true proportion of the population that engages in `XXX`. The expected number of “yes” votes is one-quarter of the participants who do not engage in `XXX` and voted “yes” due to flipping two heads, plus three-quarters of the participants who do engage in `XXX` since we expect one-quarter of them to respond “no” due to flipping a heads then a tails.
