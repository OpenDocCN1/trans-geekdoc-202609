### Probabilistic Inference Methods

With complex probability models—and even with some seemingly simple ones—computing quantities such as posterior distributions, marginal distributions, and the maximum probability configuration often require specialized methods to achieve results efficiently, even approximate ones. This is the field of *probabilistic inference*. Later we review some widely used probabilistic inference methods, including probability propagation, sampling and simulated annealing, and variational inference.
