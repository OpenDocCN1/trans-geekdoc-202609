However, this representation does not contain any information about the meaning of the words or their relationships to each other. To rectify this word embedding models aim for more nuanced continuous representations. For example, the representations might look like those in [Table 11.2](#Ch012_433-471_B9780443158889000121.xhtml#t0015).

Each column of the table is a *word embedding* (a.k.a. a *word vector)* for the corresponding word, denoted `v[w]` for word `w`. The dimensionality `K` of the embeddings is a user-selected hyperparameter. Word embeddings use the similarity of vectors to encode similarity of meaning. Here, the highlighted words *fox* and *dog* are two words that describe somewhat similar animals, and they have similar vector representations. A very different word, such as *jumps*, would have a very different embedding ([Fig. 11.3](#Ch012_433-471_B9780443158889000121.xhtml#f0020)).
