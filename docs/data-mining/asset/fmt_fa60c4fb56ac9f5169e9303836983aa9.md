```
$P{({\mathbf{v},\mathbf{h}^{(1)},\ldots,\mathbf{h}^{(L)};\theta})} = P(\mathbf{v} \mid \mathbf{h}^{(1)})[\prod_{l = 1}^{L - 2}P(\mathbf{h}^{(l)} \mid \mathbf{h}^{({l + 1})})] P({\mathbf{h}^{({L - 1})},\mathbf{h}^{(L)}}),$
```
![Equation](images/F000248si120.png)

where, as before, the model is defined in terms of a visible layer `v` and `l` = `l`, …, `L` hidden layers `h`^(`l`). The conditional distributions are all products of `Bernoulli` distributions with sigmoidal parameterizations.

The top two layers are parameterized as an `RBM`. If they have directed rather than undirected connections they can be decomposed into the form `P`(`h`^(`L`−1), `h`^(`L`)) = `P`(`h`^(`L`−1)|`h`^(`L`)) `P`(`h`^(`L`)), where the conditional distribution is another sigmoidally parameterized `Bernoulli` product and `P`(`h`^(`L`)) is the product over a separate distribution for each `P`(`h`^(`L`)). The result is known as a deep sigmoidal belief network.
