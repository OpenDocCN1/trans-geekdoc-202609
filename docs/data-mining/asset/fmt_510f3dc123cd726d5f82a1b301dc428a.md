
The importance of methods for calibrating class probability estimates is now well-established. [Zadrozny and Elkan (2002)](#References_705-736_B978044315888900025X.xhtml#fur585) applied the PAV approach and logistic regression to calibration and also investigated how to deal with multiclass problems. [Niculescu-Mizil and Caruana (2005)](#References_705-736_B978044315888900025X.xhtml#fur415) compared a variant of logistic regression and the PAV-based method in conjunction with a large set of underlying class probability estimators and found that the latter is preferable for sufficiently large calibration sets. They also found that multilayer perceptrons and bagged decision trees produce well-calibrated probabilities and do not require an extra calibration step. [Stout (2008)](#References_705-736_B978044315888900025X.xhtml#fur510) describes a linear-time algorithm for isotonic regression based on minimizing the squared error.

The `t`-SNE method for visualizing high-dimensional data via manifold learning is due to [van der Maaten and Hinton (2008)](#References_705-736_B978044315888900025X.xhtml#fur542). The UMAP method of [McInnes et al. (2018)](#References_705-736_B978044315888900025X.xhtml#fur385) aims to improve on this method leveraging topological theory, and it consequently tends to be more reliable than `t`-SNE.

# Chapter 7 Ethics: what are the impacts of what’s been learned?

## Abstract
This chapter discusses ethical issues arising from artificial intelligence and data mining technologies, and the responsible AI practices that may help to address these issues. We overview the issue of privacy in the context of data mining and technologies that can help address it, such as `k`-anonymity, differential privacy, and federated learning. We then tackle fairness and bias issues in machine learning, including the sources of bias and both technical and nontechnical interventions to mitigate them. Finally, we explain how AI alignment problems can potentially lead to harmful, or even catastrophic, results.

### Key Words
AI ethics; responsible AI; fairness and bias in AI; privacy; deepfakes; AI safety; AI and society
