### Generalized Linear Models

Linear regression and logistic regression are special cases of a family of conditional probability models known in statistics as “generalized linear models,” which were proposed to unify and generalize linear and logistic regression. In this formulation linear models may be related to a response variable using distributions other than the Gaussian distribution used for linear regression. Generalized linear models can be created for any distribution in the exponential family ([Appendix A.2](#TableA1_617-636_B9780443158889000212.xhtml#s0010) introduces exponential-family distributions).
