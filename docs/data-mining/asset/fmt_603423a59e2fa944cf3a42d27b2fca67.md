Notice the semicolon—this implies that the mean and covariance will be treated as parameters. In contrast the “|” (or “given”) symbol is used when the parameters are treated as variables and their uncertainty is to be modeled. Treating parameters as random variables is popular in Bayesian techniques such as latent Dirichlet allocation.
