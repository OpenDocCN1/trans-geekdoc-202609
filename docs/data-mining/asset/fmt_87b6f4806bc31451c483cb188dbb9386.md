## 14.4 Conditional Probability Models

You may be surprised to learn that the regression models of [Section 4.6](#Ch004_91-164_B9780443158889000054_a.xhtml#s0095) correspond to the simplest and most popular types of conditional probability model. The Linear and Polynomial Regression as Probability Models section views linear regression through the lens of probability, and we go on to examine the multiclass extension to logistic regression, expressing this in both scalar and matrix-vector forms. The leap to the matrix-vector form reveals that key aspects of modeling and learning can be expressed in compact and elegant ways. This allows computer implementations to be accelerated by using libraries—or hardware—for matrix-vector manipulation, which are highly optimized and exploit modern computing hardware. Graphics processing units (`GPUs`) can yield execution speeds that are orders of magnitude faster than standard implementations.
