# **MIS identified and listed immunogenic EBV epitopes**

To assess the ability of MIS to identify immunogenic epitopes from EBV proteins (BRIF1, BRLF1, LMP2 and EBNA3), we used ELISpot assays on PBMCs from 3 HLA-I type individuals using oversampling 15 peptide pools per individual. This method identified that all individuals had no immunogenic ELISpot response to at least one of the 15 peptide pools, providing robust strategy for testing individual predicted peptides within these individuals. We then assessed T cell activity by restricting the HLA-A*0201 allele (Fig. 6a). The HLA-type-specific peptides were restricted from 300 to 11 to include HLA-A*0101, HLA-A*0402, and HLA-B*0702, which were not present in the HLA-I peptide stability assessments. Of these 97 unique HLA-I-type peptides tested, we identified 21 immunogenic peptides predicted by our model were not previously identified or identified in the EBV (Fig. 6b), illustrating the ability of MIS to predict novel T cell epitopes, even in an extensively studied pathogen like EBV.
