Accurate silico detection of CD8+ T cell epitopes would greatly enhance cell-based vaccine development, but current prediction models are not reliably successful. Here, motivated by recent successes using machine learning to complex biology, we curated a dataset of 61,237 unique leukocyte antigen class I (HLA-1) ligands and developed MUNIS, a deep learning model that identifies peptides presented by HLA-1 ligands MUNIS showed improved performance over existing models in predicting peptide presentation and CD8+ T epitope immunodominance hierarchies. Moreover, application of MUNIS to proteins from Epstein–Barr virus led to successful identification of both known and novel HLA-1 epitopes which were experimentally validated by vitro HLA-peptide stability and T immunogenicity assays. MUNIS performs comparably to an experimental assay in immun prediction, suggesting that deep learning can reduce experimental burden and accelerate detection of CD8+ T cell epitopes for rapid vaccine development.
