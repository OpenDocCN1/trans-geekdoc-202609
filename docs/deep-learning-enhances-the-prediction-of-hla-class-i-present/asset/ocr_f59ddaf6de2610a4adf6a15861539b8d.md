A comparison of MINUS against existing tools like MHCCored2, NetMHCpan.1, Mimry2.0, TransHLA, BigMHC and Prime2.0 on leading epitopes (binders) from influenza infection shows that MINUS distinguishes binders from non-binders. Some significantly enriched peptides and ligands (and all other peptides (decys)) are identified as binder. Proteins with at least one epitope presented on MHC class I are identified as binder. Proteins with peptides and ligands from negative codons are identified as binder.
