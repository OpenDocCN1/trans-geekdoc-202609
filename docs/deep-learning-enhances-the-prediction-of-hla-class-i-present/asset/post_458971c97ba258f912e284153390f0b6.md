# **MIS identified epitopes and binding ELISpot for C T cells**

To evaluate the practicality of peptide binding and immunogenicity within the EBV protein for HLA-A 1 alleles, given their relevance to immune complexes, to multiple sequences (>90%) were identified. We made an EBV decoy by eluting peptides from the EBV protein to run the MUNIS test. We screened all 8-15 mers from 5 EBV proteins (EBRLF1, EBRLF2, EBNA1, EBNA2 and EBNA3a) and 257 predicted peptides to bind HLA-A 17 alleles using an earlier iteration of MUNIS. All of these peptides found that 0.01 outperforming in (f). Predicted non-binders other were used as non-binders for another allele of the EBV

To assess the ability of MUNIS to identify immunogenic epitopes, we used ELISpot assays on EBV (EBRLF1, EBRLF2, EBNA1, EBNA2 and EBNA3a). We identified 21 immunogenic epitopes for HLA-A*0201 and HLA-A*0301. The conformational stability of HLA-A B35:01 for predicted peptides was also measured. The precision for HLA-A*0201 and HLA-A*0301 was high. The top ranked epitopes for HLA-A*0201 and HLA-A*0301 were identified. The polarization of C T cells from 3 individuals was shown after 5 days with an immunogenic HLA-A*0201 epitope. The measure of MANS and count was also representative.
