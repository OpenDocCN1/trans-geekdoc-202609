Energy efficient approaches: Several energy efficient approaches could be implemented in various fields to reduce the carbon footprint.

Carbon capture utilization and storage (CCUS) technology: The adoption of low-carbon technologies such as CCUS can play a crucial role in reducing carbon emissions generated by industrial processes. CCUS technologies capture carbon dioxide before it is released into the atmosphere and store it either underground or use it in other processes [24].
