# Blockchain chain and IoT integration

+   a. **Data integrity and security**: Blockchain can provide a tamper-proof and transparent ledger for recording IoT device data. Each data transaction can be securely recorded by the blockchain, ensuring data integrity and pre-venting unauthorized modification.
b. **Trusted device identity**: Blockchain-based identity management can enable secure and decentralized device identity verification, authentication, and access control for IoT devices.
c. **Device coordination and automation**: Smart contracts on the blockchain can facilitate automatic coordination and interaction between IoT devices without the need for centralized control, as shown in Figure 5.19. This can enable decentralized decision-making and autonomous IoT networks.
d. **Supply chain transparency**: Blockchain can enhance supply chain management in IoT applications by providing an immutable and transparent record of every step in the supply chain, ensuring traceability, authenticity, and accountability of IoT devices and components.
