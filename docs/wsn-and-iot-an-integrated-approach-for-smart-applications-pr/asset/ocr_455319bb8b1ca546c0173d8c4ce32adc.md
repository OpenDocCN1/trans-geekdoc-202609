# 4.4.2  Deep Learning

A group of algorithms known as “deep learning” (DL) are built upon modeling abstractions at the highest levels made up of several nonlinear modifications. The use of DL allows for the learning of data representations [14]. For instance, there are numerous methods to represent a picture, but not all of them are effective for performing the task of face recognition. Therefore, DL seeks to identify the optimum representation technique for completing this work. An example would be its use in computer vision, to recognize speech and music.
