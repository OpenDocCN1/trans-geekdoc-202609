# Summary of Deep Learning Algorithm Models

| Model | Application used in healthcare | Advantage of models | Limitation in the models |
|---|---|---|---|
| CNN (Convolutional neural networks) | Abnormal heart recognition, [37] Myocardial infarction | For 2D data, achievement is stronger. Modeling quickly learned. | Categorization data is required for grouping. |
 RNN (Recurrent network) | Identification of heart problems, categorization of lung malfunctions. | Analyzes a model of time dependent and sequential events. Greater precision is needed for activities related to NLP as well as speech and character recognition. | Massive data sets are necessary, and fading gradients causes a number of issues. |
 DBN (Deep belief networks) | Anticipate a drug mixture [38], and acknowledge type 1 diabetes. | Reinforces supervised and unsupervised learning models. | Expensive training procedure. |
 DNN (Dense neural network) | Heart sound recognition, phonographically. | Better accuracy. | The process of learning during the training is not simple or quick. |
 GAN (Generative adversarial networks) | Creating artificial CT scans the brain, reshaping real-world images from brain activity, and a medical imaging platform [39]. | Decent process for training classifiers. | Learning for generating and training discrete data is challenging.|
