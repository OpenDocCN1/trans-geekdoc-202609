insufficient to send huge datasets to specific computers. So, researchers should devise
efficient strategies for utilizing the bandwidth between devices and reducing the trans-
mission time as much as possible. This could also make IoT applications use less energy
during transmission, eventually reducing CO2, and temperature emission rates.

# 12.6 CONCLUSION
