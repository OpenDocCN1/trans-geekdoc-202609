## 16.2.2 OPPORTUNITIES OF INDUSTRY 5.0

Industry 5.0 provides various opportunities, as shown in Figure 16.4, and discussed below:

- 1. Automation will have favorable impact on employment across various sectors in industries.

- 2. Customization will be possible according the need of the customer using automated manufacturing system.

- 3. Industry 5.0 will allow chances for creative people to work, which promotes the improvement of labor productivity.

- 4. Industry 5.0 will assist the customer digitally in handling frequent follow-up tasks by giving the flexibility needed to generate a high-level option based on employee needs [10].

For example, Industry 5.0 in the automotive industry can be used to manufacture customized and eco-friendly vehicles, and in healthcare it can be used to develop personalized devices. Both large and small industries can benefit from Industry 5.0, as some of the applications are shown in Figure 16.5 and discussed next.

*Healthcare*: One of the keys of Industry 5.0 is in hospitals, also known as “smart hospitals”. With the COVID-19 pandemic, natural language processing (NLP) and ML can be used to inspect patients remotely, and robots can perform surgery [14, 15].

*Manufacturing*: Industry 5.0 focuses mainly on human-machine communication. The goal of Industry 4.0 training is to minimize human involvement and give priority to machines; however, the goal of Industry 5.0 training is to develop a collaboration between autonomous machines and humans [16, 17]. To make production sustainable, Industry 5.0 establishes mechanisms for resource recycling and reuse [18, 19]. Production facilities can be located near sources of cheap materials, and cloud manufacturing will be used to manage operations [20]. The production efficiency, value addition, and market share of industries can be increased by the cost-effective virtualized platform used to handle manufacturing services [21].

*Supply chain management*: According to [22], Industry 5.0 emphasizes the importance of collaboration between people and robots. For supply chain management, manufacturing processes require the use of robots, which is a challenging task. According to [24], products can be personalized and customized without human involvement. However, the supply chain needs to run smoothly from beginning to end, including the choice of raw materials and taking into consideration customization requirements of every customer. Industry 5.0 aims to combine ecosystems with social interaction. The development of efficient operations and the personalization of end-user experiences are made possible by the incorporation of human factors [25].

*Education*: Education is seen as a necessity for every country's technological reformation. The goal of Industry 4.0 training is to minimize human involvement and give priority to machines; however, the goal of Industry 5.0 training is to develop a collaboration between autonomous machines and humans. As an example, a Lead Robotics Officer is an expert on the interface between machines and human operators and having prior training in robotics and AI. His or her job is to make decisions based on this training, and he or she can do this with a combination of technology, leadership, and communication.

*Disaster management*: Disasters are sudden, catastrophic events that cause damage to life and property, and their prevention and management measures can help us to lessen their effects. Disaster assistance is a crucial element for any organization, but it focuses only on short term goals. A lot of disaster recovery plans have been updated as result of the COVID-19 pandemic, which could mean long-term resilience will take the place of disaster recovery methods in favor of the future, especially after the 7.0 magnitude disaster that happened in Indonesia in 2018. The study revealed that Industry 4.0 has issues with disaster recovery and management, that including human intervention in AI and IoT systems can assist in resolving problems related to disaster. Any type of disaster, including earthquakes and pandemics, can be managed with Industry 5.0.
