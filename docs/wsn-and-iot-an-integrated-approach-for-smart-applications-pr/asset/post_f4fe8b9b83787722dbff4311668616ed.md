Much of the research discussed traumatic brain injury [60] along with its origin and causes, and some authors looked into pharmacological preconditioning of the brain [61]. Based on such research, the following are the innovative new techniques responsible for incorporating advanced advances with technology to predict disorder at an early stage, enabling remote patient monitoring and improving patient outcomes:

- 1. Internet of Things and wearables: To monitor patient remotely, the collaboration of IoT [62] and wearables is required. This allows for data collection on real-time applications to make predictive analysis in order to prevent fatal diseases in patients and provide timely treatment.

- 6. Blockchain: To ensure integrity of data, interoperability, management of medical records, blockchain technology [64] enhances data privacy as security, while sharing data from one place to another.

Telemedicine and virtual reality (VR): To consult experts remotely, this technology [65] provides a way for patients to minimize the need to visit in-person and promotes timely treatment and recommendations where virtual reality helps experts in visualizing complex structures in the brain while considering 3-dimensional space to provide clear and better understanding of tumor location, size and extent. However, telemedicine refers to the process which considers communication technology for providing medical care from distance to provide remote consultations to patients in underserved areas.
- 8. Predictive analytics and big data: For identification of patterns in order to make accurate and efficient predictions, techniques of predictive analytics and big data [66] come into play.

## 11.5 NEW OPPORTITIES IN THE 21 ST CENTURY

The following are the new opportunities which have been made available due to the integration of IoT and DL techniques for diagnosing brain tumors in smart healthcare, as shown in Figure 11.10.

- 1. **Disease diagnosis**: There are various algorithms based on DL that have demonstrated great potential in diagnosing diseases by analyzing medical images as input. CNNs have been used in this way and are major contributors to providing efficient and accurate results in healthcare diagnosis and classification of diseases [6].
- 2. **Health monitoring**: Smart healthcare systems enable collection of physiological data for patient health using IoT devices [5] which help to diagnose abnormalities, predict health related issues and suggest precautions to maintain good health.
- 3. **Predictive analysis**: RNNs can analyze data based on times-series from IoT-based devices to identify symptoms of any disorder experienced by an individual at an early stage, progress of an illness, and increase the life expectancy of patients [67].
- 4. **Remote monitoring**: DL techniques can monitor patients remotely with the help of IoT wearables or sensors so any abnormalities can be detected and diagnosed by alerting the patient to consult with a specialist without delay [68].
