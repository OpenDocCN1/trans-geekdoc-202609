A revolutionary approach that prioritizes collaboration between humans and machines in order to build a more sustainable and human-centric manufacturing environment.

The need to overcome the limitations and challenges of Industry 4.0, and to increase the advantages it provides, is what motivates the shift from Industry 4.0 to Industry 5.0. In contrast to Industry 4.0, which places a strong emphasis on automation, Industry 5.0 aims to reintroduce the humans back into the manufacturing process while retaining the advantages of automation and digitalization. It focuses on creating an environment where humans and machines work in collaboration to achieve common goals. This shift is thought to be necessary to overcome Industry 4.0's limitations like lack of flexibility, skills gap, and the ethical and social considerations that arise from the increasing use of automation and AI. The difference between Industry 4.0 and Industry 5.0 is discussed in Table 16.1.
