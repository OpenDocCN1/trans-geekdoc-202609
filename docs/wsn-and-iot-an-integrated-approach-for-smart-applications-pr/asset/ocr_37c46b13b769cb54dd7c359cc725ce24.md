In this case, we investigate the viability of installing an IoT-based smart transit system in a mock metropolitan environment. IoT technology was used to improve transportation operations as the system’s main strategy for combating pollution, wasteful resource use, and traffic congestion. The potential it brought and the outcomes it delivered in improved traffic flow, congestion, and increased transportation efficiency were thoroughly examined.

The first city where the smart transport system was implemented had a huge population and plenty of traffic. The system tracked and evaluated traffic patterns, vehicle locations, and infrastructure utilization in real-time IoT included sensors, a wireless network, and cloud computing.
