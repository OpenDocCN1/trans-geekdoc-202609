An effective and dependable solution to the problems of content delivery and network performance degradation brought by dynamic topology and sporadic connectivity is the extension of the CCN architecture [34]. By integrating CCN into VANET, the network can gain priority to content-centric communication, putting emphasis on individual node-to-node communication and more on the delivery and accessibility of content. By using distinct names rather than exact addresses, the CCN extension in VANET provides content-based routing and caching methods.
