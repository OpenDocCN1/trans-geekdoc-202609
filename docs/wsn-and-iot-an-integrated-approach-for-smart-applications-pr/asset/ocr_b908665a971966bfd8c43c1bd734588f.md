+   **Switch to battery-less power sources:** As we know, batteries are the most common solution for powering the IoT devices, which has a lot of disadvantages in terms of cost, performance, toxicity, and e-waste. To overcome this problem, researchers are finding ways to move toward battery-less power sources by proposing different energy harvesting techniques[25].

+   a. **Piezoelectric:** The main property of piezoelectric material is converting mechanical energy into electrical energy which can be used to power smart IoT devices. These materials offer a promising avenue for powering IoT devices in a sustainable and environmentally friendly way. Numerous types of piezoelectric materials include Piezoelectric Ceramics, Piezoelectric Polymers, and Piezoelectric Composites. Recently, researchers have developed a new piezoelectric material which is 40 times more flexible than competing materials. This new achievement could lead to better energy harvesting in wearable IoT devices.

+   *Waste heat recovery:** Thermoelectric materials can be used to recover waste heat from industrial processes, engines, or electronic devices and convert it into electricity to power IoT sensors and devices.
