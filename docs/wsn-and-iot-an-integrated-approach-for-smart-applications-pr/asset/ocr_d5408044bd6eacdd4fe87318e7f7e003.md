# 6.7.2.3 Dynamic Route Planning

MaaS platforms can build routes on the spot by using real-time traffic information, the accessibility of various modes of transport, and the preferences of specific users. These platforms can promote switching, which lowers congestion and optimizes travel times, by suggesting the most effective and environmentally friendly routes.
IoT has significant potential for future sustainable smart transportation systems.
