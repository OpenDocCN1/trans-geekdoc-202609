# 5.2.1 DEFINITION AND CORE PRINCIPLES OF BLOCKCHAIN

Blockchain technology maintains a continuously growing list of records called blocks. Each block contains a time-stamped batch of transactions that are geographically linked to the previous block, forming a chain of blocks. The core principles of blockchain include decentralization, immutability, transparency, and cryptographic security. These principles ensure that the Blockchain is resistant to tampering and provides a trusted and transparent record of transactions.

Blockchain allows multiple parties to maintain a shared record of transactions or information in a secure and transparent manner. It operates on a peer-to-peer network, where each participant, known as a node, has a copy of the entire blockchain-chain [6].
