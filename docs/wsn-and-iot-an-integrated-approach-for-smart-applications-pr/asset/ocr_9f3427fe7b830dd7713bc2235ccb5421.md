# 16.8 SECURITY STANDARDS AND REGULATIONS

Security standards and regulations are crucial for the safety and integrity of IIoT systems. The weakness of these systems against cyberattacks and other security threats can have a serious impact on the safety of workers, the reliability of manufacturing processes, and the confidentiality of sensitive information.

To address these issues, several standards and regulations have been implemented. One such example is the Industrial Internet Security Framework (IISF), developed by the Industrial Internet Consortium (IIC), which offers guidelines on how to secure IoT systems. Additional security-related topics covered by the IISF secure architectures, identity and access management, data protection, and incident response.
