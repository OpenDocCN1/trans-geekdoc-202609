To ensure that IoT devices are used in a way that is sustainable and responsible, it is important for individuals and organizations to consider the environmental factors of their actions and the products they use. This can include choosing devices that are made with environmentally friendly materials that are designed for reuse or recycling, and disposing of waste responsibly. In addition, it is important for governments and industries to develop and implement regulations and standards that promote the responsible use of IoT devices and reduce their environmental impact. By taking these actions, we can reduce the adverse effects of IoT on the environment and pave the way for a more sustainable future [26]. # High-tech companies and organizations working on manufacturing sustainable IoT devices

To make IoT devices sustainable, different organizations and high-tech companies are designing products with the sustainable development goals (SDGs) of the United Nations in mind [39, 40]. They are following the three Rs approach, i.e., Reduce, Reuse, and Recycle. Below is a list of some of these companies and organizations:

+   LG is electronics and appliance maker whose aim is to achieve some of the SDGs by 2030 by reducing greenhouse gas emissions from product manufacturing by 50%; incorporating 60,000 tons of recycled plastic into its manufacturing process; and increasing the recovery of electric waste by up to 8 million tons by 2030.

+   Schroeder is a Belgium headquartered company that is dedicated to providing innovative and sustainable lighting solutions for public spaces, while minimizing its own environmental impact.
