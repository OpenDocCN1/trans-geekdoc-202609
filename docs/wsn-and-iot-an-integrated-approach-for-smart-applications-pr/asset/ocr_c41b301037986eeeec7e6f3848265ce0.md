Hybrid models analyze images as well as sequence-based medical data, and this collaboration of two or more DL models produce efficient and accurate outcomes for diagnosing brain tumors, along with providing recommendations for treatment. For instance, hybrid models have the ability to analyze clinical notes and EHR comprised of a structured form of lab results, patient health records, etc., or unstructured patient symptoms over a period of time.



Transfer learning utilizes the concept of pretrained DL networks for extracting features in terms of pretrained weights on a large dataset to overcome issues when data is scarce. It can also reduce time-consuming processes in nowadays as the transferring facility of these networks enables them to learn from past experiences, resulting in improved performance of networks in smart healthcare. Thus, the patterns or features of one model during the training process can be utilized by any other model without training in order to provide quick and efficient outcomes in diagnosis. For instance, a model which is pretrained on MRI images can be provided with a fine-tuning feature to ensure efficient outcomes while diagnosing a brain tumor or segmentation of a brain. Thus, these features can be transferred from one another for the desired operation in healthcare.
