# A Sustainable IoT-Based Smart Transportation System for Urban Mobility

Mahendra Balkrishna Salunke, Shailesh V. Kulkarni, Shraddha Ovale, Bhushan M. Manjre, Suresh Limkar, Farhadeeba Shaikh, and Vyasa Sai

In today's metropolitan centers, commuting is a significant problem. The exponential rise in the number of vehicles on the road has led to traffic congestion and air pollution, which affects the environment. Modern transportation systems need to be improved to handle these challenges. As the Internet of Things (IoT) expands, transportation systems will adapt. By using sensors, wireless connectivity, and cloud computing, smart transportation can achieve a sustainable goal and reduce energy consumption.

Intelligent transportation systems use IoT to manage the complex dynamics of traffic. By utilizing real-time data, analytics, and informed decision-making, smart transportation systems can reduce traffic congestion and air pollution. The primary benefit of IoT-based transportation is its ability to provide users with information that allows them to travel more efficiently. This technology also helps in optimizing the distribution of resources, potentially leading to more sustainable environments.

The use of IoT-based transportation allows for the monitoring of traffic patterns, which is essential for reducing travel times. This approach ensures that transportation systems are used effectively to minimize the environmental impact. By integrating sensors and real-time data, smart transportation can offer a better quality of life for urban dwellers.

The cornerstone of intelligent transportation lies in the use of IoT to provide data-driven insights. This allows for better decision-making regarding traffic management and resource allocation. By adopting these technologies, smart transportation systems can reduce the number of vehicles on the road and decrease overall emissions. The ultimate goal is to create a system that is both efficient and sustainable for the environment.
