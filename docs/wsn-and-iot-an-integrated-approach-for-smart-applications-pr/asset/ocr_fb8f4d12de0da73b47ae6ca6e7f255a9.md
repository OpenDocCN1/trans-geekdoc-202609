Cost management: The costs relating to an in-person visit to a medical facility are reduced in part via remote patient monitoring and telehealth. Processing of health data saves medical professionals' time and money, allowing them to refocus their efforts on the more urgent problems. Enhanced patient experience: IoMT makes it feasible to employ new-technology that can ease pressure on outpatient self-service devices in healthcare facilities and even decrease the necessity for in-person visits [18]. Accuracy: IoMT offers additional information, providing medical professionals with more precise insights into patients' medical situations. Logistics: In hospitals, IoMT devices are utilized to monitor the equipment and trigger alerts when maintenance or other problems need to be addressed. They are also employed as trackers to follow patients and medications across the campuses of medical facilities, resulting in mix-ups and errors [18].



IoMT is a fast-evolving field of IoT applications in healthcare. IoMT is positioned to transform patient care, improve monitoring capabilities, and boost healthcare outcomes as a result of technological improvements and rising acceptance. In this section, we discuss the current status of IoMT in India, as well as in other countries.



India has begun integrating IoMT into its hospitals and medical equipment thanks to the adaptability of the technology which enables it to be used in clinical and non-clinical situations.

The adoption of IoMT devices is anticipated to play an important role in determining the future of Indian healthcare, and the Indian government is actively supporting public–private partnerships to upgrade the nation's healthcare infrastructure. The potential benefits of IoMT for India's healthcare system are numerous and include improving healthcare access, especially in rural situations, quality, efficiency, and affordability. However, implementing IoMT technology poses challenges, including ensuring data security, accessibility for all individuals, and the high cost of infrastructure requirements [36].
