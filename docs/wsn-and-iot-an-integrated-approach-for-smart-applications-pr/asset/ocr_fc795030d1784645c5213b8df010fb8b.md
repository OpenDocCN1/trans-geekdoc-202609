# 4.4.5 ROBOTICS

Robotics is a multidisciplinary field of engineering and science that encompasses mechanical engineering, electronic engineering, information engineering, computer science, and other related disciplines. It encompasses the disciplines of designing, constructing, operating, and utilizing robots, with developing computer systems to control them, provide sensory input, and process information. These technologies are utilized to create machines capable of replacing people and imitating human behaviors. Robots are utilized in various scenarios and serve multiple functions. They are presently employed in hazardous settings such as inspecting radioactive materials, deactivating bombs, and participating in manufacturing processes. Additionally, robots are deployed in environments where human presence is unfeasible, such as space, underwater, high-temperature conditions, and the cleanup and containment of dangerous substances.
