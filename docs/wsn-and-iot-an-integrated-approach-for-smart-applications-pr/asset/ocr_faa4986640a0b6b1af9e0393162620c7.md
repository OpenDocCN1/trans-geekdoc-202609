# 2.3.1.2 IoT in Healthcare

IoT in healthcare refers to the incorporation of IoT technologies into various healthcare processes and systems. The emergence of IoT has opened up possibilities for life-saving applications in the healthcare sector. By collecting the real-time data from bedside devices, enabling real-time monitoring of patient information and promoting prompt diagnosis, the complete system of patient care can be enhanced and improved [53]. By the year 2020, it is estimated that 87% of healthcare organizations will have successfully implemented IoT technology. More than one third of healthcare organizations fail to utilize real-time data from connected devices which leads to risk of errors in diagnosis and treatment, potential for data loss and other insufficiencies. Efficient and accurate healthcare relies on timely and correct information and the advent of IoT has promoted the integration of a vast array of medical devices [54]. As implementation of IoT technology continues to gain traction, we are witnessing an increase in the connectivity of various medical devices, which enhances the potential for better healthcare outcomes.
