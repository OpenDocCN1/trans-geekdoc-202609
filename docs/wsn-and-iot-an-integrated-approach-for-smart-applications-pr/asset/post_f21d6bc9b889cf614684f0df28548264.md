### 15.3 APPLICATIONS OF HYERSPECTRAL IMAGING

The broad spectrum data from hyperspectral imaging (HSI) may be used for analysis and decision-making, opening up new applications [6]. Here are some of the most significant uses:

- **Agriculture and crop monitoring**: Farmers may make more educated judgments about irrigation, fertilization, and pest control.
- **Environmental monitoring**: Hyppectral photography has grown to be a crucial component of environmental monitoring for tracking plant health, land use, and identifying trends.
- **Mineral exploration**: Geologists can locate possible mineral resources and map the distribution of these resources using spectral analysis.
- **Urban planning and infrastructure assessment**: The assessment of urban infrastructure and land use is made easier by the use of HSI, which also provides useful information on plant cover.
- **Forest and deforestation monitoring**: HSI may be used to track forest health, identify species, and locate regions that have experienced deforestation or degradation.
- **Medical diagnostics**: Potential applications for HSI in medical diagnosis include the design of particular biomarkers, the identification of malignant tissue, and the description of skin lesions.
- **Remote sensing and Earth observation**: HSI is frequently utilized in these fields to investigate atmospheric composition, keep an eye out for natural disasters, and evaluate how climate change is affecting ecosystems.
- **Archaeology and cultural heritage**: Numerous industries, including archaeology and the preservation of cultural objects, use hyperspectral photography to find hidden characteristics and define material composition.
