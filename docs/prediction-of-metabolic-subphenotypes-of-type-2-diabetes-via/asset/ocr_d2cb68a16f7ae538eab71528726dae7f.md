# Glucose from OGTT vs IIGI

![](bbox=[0.115, 0.115, 0.935, 0.425])

Extended Data Fig. 3 | Concordance analysis of plasma glucose concentration during OGTT and IIGI. Glucose time-series from OGTT and IIGI are overlaid on each other, showing their high similarity. Each box represents a participant, the x-axis represents time in minutes relative to the start of OGTT or IIGI, and the y-axis represents glucose concentration in mg/dL. Average Pearson correlations between each individual's glucose time-series from OGTT and IIGI equals 0.82, with a standard deviation equal 0.16.
