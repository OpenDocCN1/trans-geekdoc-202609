**Extended Data Fig. 4 | Plasma C-peptide concentration during OGTT and IIGI.** C-peptide concentrations were obtained at seven timepoints (0, 15, 30, 60, 90, 120, and 180 min). Each box represents a participant, the x-axis represents time in minutes relative to the start of OGTT or IIGI, and the y-axis represents C-peptide concentration in ng/mL. The area between the two curves is calculated to infer the incretin effect.
