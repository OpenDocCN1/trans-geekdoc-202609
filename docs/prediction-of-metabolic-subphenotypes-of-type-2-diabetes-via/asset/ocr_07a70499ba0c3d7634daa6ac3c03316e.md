# Correlation between OGTT curve features and reduced representation (top 2 PC)

![](bbox=[0.08, 0.08, 0.92, 0.22])

**Extended Data Fig. 7** | Relationship between the reduced representation of the glucose time-series and the extracted curve features. We calculated the correlations between the top 2 PCs (OGTT_G_ReducedRep) and the extracted features (OGTT_G_Features). Variances explained by PC1 and PC2 are 43.8% and

18%, respectively. As illustrated in the Figure, PC1 strongly negatively correlated with the incremental area under the curve (iAUC), and positively correlated with the negative area under the glucose curve (nAUC). This means, that higher positive values on the PC1 are associated with lower iAUC.
