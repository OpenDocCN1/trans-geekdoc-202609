# 16.2.1.3 Multi Temporal Fusion

Multi temporal fusion is, fusion of same scene taken at different times to detect changes among them. The purpose of this process is to identify the changes in the image that are captured at different times and give out a fused image with more details. The application areas are in satellite imagery, medical imagery, and different general applications [23]. The resultant image shows the variation among the captured input images at different times.
