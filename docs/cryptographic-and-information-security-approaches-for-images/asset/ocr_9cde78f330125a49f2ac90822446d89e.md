As forensic artifacts, pixels defects are not very reliable or potent. One immediate problem with them is that most cameras have the ability to detect these defects and correct them by post-processing their images on-board. Reliable source identification therefore requires us to turn to a more consistent as well as persistent artifact.

# b. Pattern Noise:

Pattern noise, also known as sensor noise, is the name given to the unique uncorrelated pattern of noise that every digital camera introduces in every image or video that it captures. This noise is the result of inhomogeneity of the CCD or CMOS sensors used in the camera, which arises due to the variations in the sensitivities of the silicon wafers used in these sensors. Pattern noise follows the hierarchy illustrated in Figure 23.14.
