The DCT coefficients of the cover medical image are replaced by the message bits and then quantization is performed. Inverse 2D-DCT returns the stego-medical image into space domain [12].

DWT is another transform technique that obtain both frequency and temporal information of a 2D image. It is especially used for compression and denoising of image. DWT decompose the image into low and high frequencies in multiple levels of resolution with filters such as Haar, Daubechies Biorthogonal, Symlets etc. In DWT, the high-pass filter H determines the detail coefficients and the low-pass filter L represents the approximation coefficients of the medical image. The decomposition stage is repeated until a number of levels, as shown in Figure 22.14. Two level decomposition of the 2D-DWT generates four sub-bands which are LL (approximation), LH (horizontal), HL (vertical) and HH (diagonal). The LL sub-bands contains the lowest resolution with a half-sized of the medical image, but it has the most information to reconstruct the medical image. The other sub-bands are the vertical, horizontal and diagonal details of the medical images. The DWT of medical image $f(x, y)$ with $M \times N$ size is represented in Equations 22.18 and 22.19 [56].
