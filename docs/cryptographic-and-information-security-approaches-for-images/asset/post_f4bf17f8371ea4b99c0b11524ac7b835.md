From this phenomenon; it can be seen that proposed compounded strategy is a uniform distributed cipher. Especially when facing a data with intensive contrast information, BLE scheme has better adaptiveness and stability for encryption, whether in view of HA, VA, and DA correlation distribution or volatility criteria.
