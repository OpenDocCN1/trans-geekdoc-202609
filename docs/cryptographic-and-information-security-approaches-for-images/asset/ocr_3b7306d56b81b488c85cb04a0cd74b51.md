# Table 8.2 Summary of Compression + Encryption Techniques for Security of Images

| Existing Techniques | Type of Compression Method | Nature of Compression Method | Type of Encryption Method | Nature of Key in Encryption Method | Type of Image |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wu and Kuo (2005) [20] | Multiple Huffman Tables or QM | Lossless | Stream Cipher | Symmetric | Grayscale |
| Ou et al. (2006) [21] | DWT, Significance-Linked Connected Component Analysis (SLCCA) | Lossless | AES | Symmetric | Grayscale and Color |
| Loussert et al. (2008) [22] | DCT | Lossy | Bit XOR Operation | Symmetric | Grayscale |
| Krikor et al. (2009) [23] | DCT | Lossy | Selective Encryption and Bit Stream Cipher | Symmetric | Color |
| Hermassi et al. (2010) [24] | Renewing Huffman Coding Tree | Lossless | Stream Cipher | Symmetric | Not Mentioned |
| Benabdellah et al. (2011) [25] | Faber-Schauder Multi-Scale Transform (FMT) | Lossy | DES or AES | Symmetric | Grayscale, Color, and Medical |
| Chen et al. (2011) [26] | Entropy Coding | Lossless | Lookup Table | Symmetric | Not Mentioned |
| Samson and Sastry (2012) [27] | 2-D Multilevel Wavelet Transformation | Lossy | Permutation | Symmetric | Grayscale |
