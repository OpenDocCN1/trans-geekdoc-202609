We then examined the inherent frailty of digital evidence with the help of some illustrative examples, which led us to the discussion about the necessity of digital visual media forensics and the basic tasks associated therewith, including the concepts of evidence collection/recovery/repair, evidence enhancement and authentication, hidden data detection, and evidence identification and interpretation. We studied the concept of identifying traces and examined the various methods by which such traces can be used during source camera identification, integrity verification, content authentication, and tamper detection. We also explored the variants of digital visual media forensics, i.e., active/passive forensics and blind/non-blind forensics. The chapter concluded with an analysis of the domains of anti-forensics and counter anti-forensics.
