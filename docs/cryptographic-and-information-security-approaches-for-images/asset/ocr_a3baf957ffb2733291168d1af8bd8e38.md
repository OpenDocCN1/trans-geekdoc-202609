These constraints can be used in the absence of knowledge of the second key to retrieve the plaintext. As reported in reference [24], such an attack algorithm works in two stages. The aim of the first stage is to obtain the intermediate phase truncated function Z(u, v) without the knowledge of key K2. To achieve this each iterative step consists of 1 cycle of ERA followed by 39 cycles of HIOA. Since the known quantity for this stage is only the ciphertext, therefore, the constraint used is the support, which is evaluated from the inverse Fourier transform of the ciphertext.
