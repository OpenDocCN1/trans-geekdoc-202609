In order to predict novel perovskite compounds, all the possible combination of ABO3, AA'BB'O6, AA'B2O6, A2BB'O6, ABX3, A'ABB'X6, AA'B2X6, A2BB'X6 (where X is halide element) and ABF3, AA'BB'F6, AA'B'2F6, A2BB'F6 compounds are prepared using elements up to Bi except C, N, S, P and noble gases [24]. These sets of compounds are screened based on charge neutrality. It suggests that the cations (A and B) with the sum of valence equal to 6 in single oxide perovskites and the sum of valence equal to 3 in single halide perovskites are the only valid compounds. Similarly, the cations (A, A', B and B') with the sum of valence equal to 12 in double oxide perovskites and the sum of valence equal to 6 in double halide perovskites are the only valid compounds. This leads to a specific dataset of oxide and halide perovskite dataset (Dp) of 18,248,197 compounds.

# 2.1. Formability and stability databases

Formability training dataset contains 1962 experimentally known oxides, fluoride, and halide perovskites of the form AB O3, AA'BB' O6 and AA'BB' X6, respectively. The halide X is composed of Cl and Br in this dataset and it is composed of 1453 perovskites and 509 non-perovskites. The dataset is prepared based on literature like Zhang et al. [34], Balacharan et al. [38], Vasala et al. [39], Talapatra et al. [24], Li et al. [40], Li et al. [41] and an existing database like Inorganic Crystal Structure Database (ICSD). The stability training database is composed of all possible compositions i.e. AB O3, AA'BB' O6, AA' B2O6 and AB X3, AA'BB' X6, AA'B2 X6, A2BB' X6 compounds. The total number of oxides, fluoride, and halides is 4075 compounds. Of these, 2053 compounds are thermodynamically stable and 2022 compounds are thermodynamically unstable.
