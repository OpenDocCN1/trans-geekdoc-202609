# 7.3.2 EFFECT OF DOPING

Increasing the doping of source and drain in an identical pattern will lead to two primary effects: Effect 2 and Effect 1. When the doping of source and drain are increased at an equal rate from a low to high value, a major part of the depletion region will penetrate inside the channel [37] rather than the source (source-channel junction) or drain (drain-channel junction), which is basically Effect 1. The aggregate gate depletion layer width keeps on reducing with increased doping [58], which is basically Effect 2. So both Effect 1 and Effect 2 will be present when the doping of source and drain are increased from a low to high value. Effect 2 is more dominant at low doping, which leads to reduction of the depletion layer width, thereby increasing the effective channel length. Effect 1 is more dominant at high doping, which leads to penetration of the depletion layer side inside the channel, resulting in a decrease of the effective channel length.
