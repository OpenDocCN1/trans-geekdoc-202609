### 9.1 INTRODUCTION

Nowadays, low-power consumption and minimal leakage current are essential requirements for highly efficient electronics devices [1, 2]. To accommodate the emerging need of this industry, MOSFET scaling has reached the nanometer range, which is limiting the further reduction of its supply voltage and rapid switching, which, in turn, is enhancing the short-channel effects. Therefore, it has become a necessity to explore some better alternative devices that can overcome the limitations of the MOSFET. The tunnel field effect transistor’s (TFET) improved efficiency, rapid switching, hardware compatibility, and reduced short-channel effects make it a superior alternative to the MOSFET. TFET works on the band-to-band-tunneling (BTBT) phenomena instead of thermionic emissions [3–5]. However, the TFET has few major parameters, which still need many improvements like IOFF, ION, steeper subthreshold slope, and Iamb [6].
