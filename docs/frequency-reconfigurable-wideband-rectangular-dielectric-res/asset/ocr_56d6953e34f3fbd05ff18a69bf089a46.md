# 2.2. Mathematical modelling

The antenna being proposed in this study is a hybrid antenna, which combines different features to enhance its performance. The resonant frequency of this antenna is determined by the presence of two dielectric layers: Epoxy FRA (4.4) and Alumina (Al2O3). The effective height Heff and the effective permittivity εreff are crucial parameters that characterize the behaviour of the proposed antenna. According to [16], The effective height (Heff), and effective permittivity (εreff) is given by Eqs. (1) and (2):

H_eff = H + H_S

ε_reff = H_eff / (H/ε_RDRA + H_S/ε_sub)

According to [3] the transcendental equation is used to calculate the fundamental TE111 mode’s resonance frequency

k . tan(k * d / 2) = √((εreff - 1)k0² - kz²)

Where, kx, ky, and kz are wave numbers, they satisfy the following equation

kx² + ky² + kz² = (εreff)k0²

Where, kx = π/b and ky = π/b
