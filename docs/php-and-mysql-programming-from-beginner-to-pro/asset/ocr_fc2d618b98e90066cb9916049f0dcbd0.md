+   Integration of Anti-CSRF Tokens: Incorporating anti-CSRF tokens in forms and validating them server-side can significantly counter CSRF exploits.

+   Setting Same-Site Cookie Attributes: Applying the SameSite attribute to cookies can restrict them to first-party requests, effectively mitigating CSRF vulnerabilities.

+   Adopting Double Submit Cookie Patterns: The double submit cookie method, where a token is both sent as a cookie and included in requests, serves as an effective CSRF countermeasure.

+   Utilization of Custom Headers: Leveraging custom headers in AJAX requests can safeguard against CSRF, as these headers are not automatically included in cross-origin requests.
