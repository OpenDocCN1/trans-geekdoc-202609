The concept of decomposed services, particularly through the lens of microservices, deconstructs applications into an array of small, autonomous services, each dedicated to executing a distinct business logic. This decomposition fosters adaptability, simplifies deployment processes, and enhances the system's scalability. Docker and Kubernetes are instrumental in facilitating the efficient deployment and management of such decomposed services.

# Containerization and Management Techniques

Containerization, epitomized by Docker, encapsulates applications along with their operational ecosystems, ensuring uniformity across various developmental and operational stages. Kubernetes, serving as an orchestration mechanism, autonomously manages the deployment, scaling, and operational tasks of these containerized applications, particularly within microservices environments.

# Distributed Data Handling Techniques

Advanced strategies for distributed data management, including partitioning and sharding, are crucial for efficiently overseeing expansive data sets within distributed databases. Partitioning divides a table into distinct parts based on certain criteria, whereas sharding distributes the data across multiple database instances to facilitate scalability.
