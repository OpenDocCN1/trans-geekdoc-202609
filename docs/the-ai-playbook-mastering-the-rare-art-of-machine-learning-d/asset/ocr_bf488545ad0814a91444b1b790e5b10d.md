# Learning the Easy Way

Even after my experience with gay.com, I still hadn't learned my lesson. A single experience was not enough for me to see a pattern. Besides, I'd been paid well for my time, so I wasn't tremendously motivated to evolve.

But there are two ways for your second ML project to succeed. One is by experiencing a reckoning that leads you to intentionally follow a well-honed enterprise practice. The other is by dumb luck.

When I landed my second gig as an ML consultant, I got lucky. Sometimes, you learn your lesson the easy way rather than the hard. The next chapter kicks off with that story.
