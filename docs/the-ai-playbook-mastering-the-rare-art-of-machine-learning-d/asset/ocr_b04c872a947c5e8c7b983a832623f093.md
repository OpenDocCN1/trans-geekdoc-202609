Now multiply this problem by 1,000. The system you develop must handle these logistics every day for 1,000 shipping centers in the United States. Across this mammoth operation, every moment counts. One minute per driver per day costs $14.5 million per year. Likewise, one mile is worth $50 million. Really, though, no pressure.

Overall, millions of gallons of fuel and thousands of metric tons of emissions are on the line annually. Okay, maybe there's a little pressure.
