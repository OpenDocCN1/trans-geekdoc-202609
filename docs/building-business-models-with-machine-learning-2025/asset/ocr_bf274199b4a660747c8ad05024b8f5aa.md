# Role of Sentiment Analysis Using AI

Artificial Intelligence is the buzzword of the modern world, permeating every aspect of human existence. Artificial intelligence (AI) is becoming a hot topic as and more systems, particularly in the health care sector, are being automated. Public engagement with research and media representations can greatly influence future investments due to their adaptability.

Scenario: In this context, we investigate the concept that public fear of artificial intelligence (AI) stems from bad media coverage of AI particularly the purported use of "Terminator movie graphics. This idea is brought up as a possible roadblock to the public and media interactions that AI researchers and developers could have with the public. This feeling of peril is what we call the "Terminator Syndrome". It can be interpreted as blind beliefs that could put a stop to the public conversation about artificial intelligence before it begins.

This study used both quantitative and qualitative methodologies to evaluate the notion that news outlets and social media platforms like Facebook and Twitter have negative coverage of artificial intelligence. However, contrary to what some have asserted, it was found that the evidence does not reflect the reportedly negative sentiment on AI in the mainstream media.
