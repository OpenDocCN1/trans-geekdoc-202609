+   7. How are users' network for Hudi?

Users are the most important part of Hudi, explains its CTO. Initially, the startup took the classic data approach by focusing on how people share data to collect it in centralized or distributed servers that rely on multiple databases to manage this huge stream of data. Nowadays, Hudi has reached 300,000 users and solved the Search Engine Optimization (SEO) problem by developing a peer-to-peer protocol, Interplanetary Data Wallet, where the user (data owner) has a data wallet and is able to interact with the data buyer. This protocol is a storage that does not rely on a single database, but on all devices connected among them. SEO problems affect internet users because their data are scattered in infinite servers, not interconnected them. The main focus of Hudi is creating value out of data. Indeed, during transactions, there can also be a Binance transaction, where the data buyers can incentivize users to share data with them. The data wallet will manage the infrastructure to manage the data being shared in the peer-to-peer network. In addition, Hudi also developed connectors to standardize these transactions in its protocol, and made it completely open source, as also the wallet.
