
The founders of Hudi are building both an infrastructure and a specific protocol that allows people to own their data. In fact, the company’s CEO states that users of Hudi can own cryptocurrencies in a data wallet, they are able to own their data and then access the data market.

### 3. How Hudi develop?

To enable users to take part in the data industry, Hudi’s founders think in a decentralized way, without intermediaries and platforms. They developed a peer-to-peer data storage and exchange protocol called "Interplanetary Data Wallet" which was developed by Hudi’s CTO and cofounder, which aims at turning data into value assets.

### 4. Which is Hudi’s approach with data and how difficult was it to overcome privacy concerns?

According to Hudi’s CTO, the main revolution of the European Union GDPR is the legal part of data meaning that companies must be compliant while using data and letting users accept terms of use. Indeed, the protocol is something that makes the users aware of how data will be shared with partners, and all data transactions involve users' authorization. Hudi is not acting as a data broker, rather it uses a website compliant with a protocol to act as a data provider and a connector among users and companies. The approach of data collection for Hudi is human-centric, the collection and monetization of data by driven interactions.

Indeed, Hudi works closely with GDPR and is developing new technologies to empower people to collect all their data scattered in platforms, and with access to delete data under users' request. No value is assigned to data by Hudi itself because it does not work as a data broker, it acts as an enabling agent to monetize data. Hudi’s plan - explains the company’s CEO - is to allow people to collect their data using the GDPR and then wipe these from other services, making companies who are in need of users' data to use these services and pay them for the asset they want to.

### 5. How was DataMask developed to deal with privacy?

The DataMask extension is for the full ecosystem which links the data wallet to internet browsing, making users earn de-to-earn rewards by surfing online. It is the first Chrome extension provided by humainconnect.com that allows users to get rewards while browsing the internet.

It works by blocking unwanted advertising and allowing people to give permission to trusted websites. Through its features, it blocks pop-ups, annoying banners, cookies, and third-party trackers, protects users’ privacy while allowing them to earn while surfing the internet by a passive earning, Hudi works normally while browsing. Hudi’s CTO explains that the company has no data stored in their servers and has no access to users' personal data. The founders decided to design a protocol by considering each data transaction as a transaction that will have a history in the users’ personal wallet. Hence, users have transparency with everything with this data inside of the wallet in the same way they do with crypto. Moreover, DataMask users can have a password manager with an extension that enables interaction between the protocol and the webpage, while data is stored on the users’ wallet exclusively.

### 7. How is the users' network for Hudi?

Users are the most important part of Hudi, explains its CTO. Initially, the startup took the classic data approach by focusing on how people share data to collect it in centralized or distributed servers that rely on multiple databases to manage this huge stream of data. Nowadays, Hudi has reached 300,000 users and solved the Search Engine Optimization (SEO) problem by developing a peer-to-peer protocol, Interplanetary Data Wallet, where the user (data owner) has a data wallet and is able to interact with the data buyer. This protocol is a storage that does not rely on a single database, but on all devices connected among them. SEO problems affect internet users because their data are scattered in infinite servers, not interconnected them. The main focus of Hudi is creating value out of data. Indeed, during transactions, there can also be a Binance transaction, where the data buyers can incentivize users to share data with them. The data wallet will manage the infrastructure to manage the data being shared in the peer-to-peer network. In addition, Hudi also developed connectors to standardize these transactions in its protocol, and made it completely open source, as also the wallet.

### 8. How is the value exchange between crypto and money decided? What are the criteria for this value exchange?

The value exchange between crypto and money is decided by the demand, and the criteria for this value exchange are the data and attention, explains the CTO.
