Machine learning is modifying PCs to provide an exhibition model utilising machine information or previous experience. The model is generalised as dependent upon certain boundaries, and learning is the execution of a PC program/model to enhance the boundaries of the models utilising the preparation or previous experience. ML programs are models that help predict outcomes based on the inputs or make predictions based on the acquired information.

Machine learning is the field that develops PCs that can improve their performance based on the experience they gain during their deployment and usage. Machine learning is a subfield of artificial brainpower that includes the advancement of calculations and measurable models that empower PCs to work on their representation of undertakings through experience. models and algorithms are developed to make decisions or predictions based on the available data. As shown in Figure 1, AI/ML-based models can be used either for prospecting customers, approving loan applications in a few seconds, providing support to managing the risks of the organisations by predicting loan defaults/ fraudulent activities/banking. The models are developed so that they can improve their efficiency once deployed. Different types of AI/ML, such as managed, unpaid, or support learning exist. Managed learning involves developing the model based on the labelled information, whereas unpaid learning involves developing a model with unlabelled information. Support learning includes preparing a model through experimentation.

Machine learning has other roles, similar to image and speech recognition, natural language processing and recommendation systems (RS). Based on the users' activities, the systems recommend products and services to the users. The recommender systems play a cyclic role to recommend products on the individuals' purchase history (Lu et al., 2015) and Kouabra et al., 2020). Later, the application was extended to different sectors like online booking, online articles and books (either scientific or news, like LIBRA, which is a recommender system), online advertising and marketing and more (Shi, Larsonson & Hanjalis, 2015). A recommender system is a programme designed to suggest the most appropriate products or services to a user (Beheshti et al., 2020). This goal is to offer personalised services by retrieving the most relevant information and services from the vast amount of data collected on platforms such as online, private, social and IoT (Internet of Things) data. Artificial Intelligence, encompassing Machine Learning, Deep Learning and Natural Language Processing, along knowledge representation (Beheshti et al., 2020), user experience technologies (Henzahl & Tractinsky, 2006), and crowdsourcing have the possibility to revolution recommender systems.

Personalised offers are offered to users, aiming to gain a competitive edge to the merchants who have deployed AI-based systems. Personalisation on the principle of offering personalised services to users based on their data to merchants. AI/ML-based systems integrate the available data and provide unique personalised suggestions and recommendations to each individual (Winter et al., 2021). As companies and merchants have access to individual data, they can design personalised advertising campaigns based on individual data and purchase history. In terms of STP (segmentation, targeting and positioning), the companies and merchants can target individuals based on individual-specific attributes (Grigorios et al., 2022). AI-powered platforms provide consumers with the most pertinent and precise marketing strategies (Habil et al., 2023).

Machine learning techniques are replacing conventional methods in several businesses these day.
