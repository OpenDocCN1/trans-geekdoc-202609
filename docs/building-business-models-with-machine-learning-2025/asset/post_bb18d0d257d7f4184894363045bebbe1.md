Developed as part of the Horizon 2020 Big Data Value (BDV) project, the DDI framework is grounded in scientific research and empirical data. It consists of the DDI Canvas, DDI Navigator, and DDI Tools, offering users structured guidance in systematically evaluating relevant variables across both the supply and demand sides of data-driven innovation (https://ddi-canvas.com/how/). ## The Case of Hudi: Human Data Income

### Theoretical Sampling: Why Hudi?

Following previous studies based on a single case study (Ferrigno, Zordan, and Di Minin, 2022), in this chapter we have selected Hudi as representative case study for three reasons. First, Hudi (Human Data Income Ltd.) is a data monetization decentralized platform, which enables individuals and businesses to gather, enrich, exchange and monetize their personal data.

Second, Hudi exploits the increasing digital leverage to create a universal basic income generated by the economic value of users' data and by the market itself. As such, Hudi collects, aggregates data and redistributes value to the users by means of Web3 technologies, such as cryptocurrencies and blockchain.

Third, Hudi works as a data bank where the individual user decides whether and what data to share with the organizations that request it, in exchange for royalty. Hudi's users create value assets by means of cryptocurrencies, while the aim of Hudi is building the first universal income based on data by redistributing up to 75% of the stakeholders, and 50% to the data owner. Typically, data brokers extract data from individuals and companies to resell to others, extracting value from the data and making a profit without redistributing to it, users. Hudi's mission is to create a protocol for data management by creating a free and open market based on 3 principles: transparency, control, and remuneration. Hudi allows users to create a profile with all data of their choice, bringing together in a single place all the data they already have on platforms such as Facebook, Google, Netflix, etc. In this, unique and complete data profile is created by no other company has, and that it must go through Hudi and pay a fee. Hudi will therefore profit from this extracting value from the data, which in turn will remunerate the users, the owners of these data profiles.

### History and Development

Hudi was founded in 2017 as a startup in London, UK by Francesco Ballarani (CEO), Gianlu Ballarani (CMO), and Andrea Silvio Vital (CTO). Hudi's founders and users built together a decentralized platform, and this participatory spirit of users was a key factor for the success of the company. The launch of Hudi was based on the idea of users to let others know about Hudi, using a decentralized marketing strategy. In fact, during the launch, anyone who spoke the word about Hudi various actions were remunerated with Hudi Tokens. The Hudi Token (HUD) is a utility token created on the Binance Smart Chain for on-chain data exchange and monetization. It follows the BE-20 standard.

The company, founded in 2017, launched in 2021 with the Hudi Release on September 10th and the IDO on Pancake swap on September 15th. In October and November were activated the Active Data Monetization Online (BETA) and the Ambassador Program, which in 2022 were introduced new data sources in Q1, Q2, Q3, such as Binance, Coinbase, Facebook, Google, LinkedIn, Reddit, and TikTok. In Q4 the Hudi Protocol (Alpha) was released as well as the first data providers on the protocol. For what concerns the 2023 Q1, Hudi AD network and DataSwap were presented.

Nowadays, the company offers Decentralized Finance (De-Fi) solutions to data owners, including staking, liquidity, and lending, which revolve around Hudi dApp.

HudiApp is a wallet aggregator, where users can access Hudi, Data Wallet, and Crypto Cashback. The Crypto Wallet allows users to buy, receive, send, and handle Hudi tokens from many shops. Moreover, the Data collects data to connect friends and brands. 

Hudi also DataMask to protect data and earn crypto, which is an extension that gives users control over their data and monetizes them. Beyond that, it blocks third-party trackers, pop-ups, and invasive advertising. The ultimate aim, however, is the formation of Decentralized Autonomous Organisation (DAO), which is defined as "new organization format that the management and operational rules are encoded on blockchain in the smart contracts and can autonomously operate without centralized control or third-party intervention" (Wang et al., 2019, p. 870). This new organization represents an evolution of limited company, where all Hudi Token holders, not just the owner, can make decisions.

## Interviews

The interviews were conducted on May 26th, 2023, with Francesco Ballarani (CEO and co-founder) and Andrea Silvi. The interviews lasted 1 hour. In the following we present the questions and the insights of the specific answers.

### 1. What is Hudi?

The CEO of the company explains that Hudi is a simple concept rooted in users' generation of data on the internet. The Data is for technology and technological innovation, hence the idea to bring back to the people the ownership of data and empowering them to join the data market being rightful owners of their data. The data industry is a market valuing than a trillion-dollar industry each also understanding data brokerage, market research, and digital advertising. Indeed, every minute one million dollars of people's data is being sold and bought by companies.

### 2. How was the idea to create Hudi?

The CEO and co-founder of Hudi answers that he had always worked with data in the private investigation and intelligence sector, and in 2010 studied digital marketing. As time passed, he discovered even more importance of data and information in relation with digital platforms, such as Google, Amazon, Facebook, etc. He states that data allow to target define and advertise specific products and services, while the data market is a closed market, and data is being collected and exploited. Since 2017, the founders started working on the technological side of data and began working on Hudi.
