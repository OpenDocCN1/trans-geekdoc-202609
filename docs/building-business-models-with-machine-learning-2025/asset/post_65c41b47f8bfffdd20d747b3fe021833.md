### KEY TERMS AND DEFINITIONS
- **Big Data**: Large amounts of data available in the contemporary environment.
- **Business Model**: Description of how an organization functions.
- **Data Monetization**: Process of extracting economic value from data.
- **Data Monetization Issues**: Issues (e.g., privacy, ethical and legal considerations) related to the process of data.
- **Value Capture**: techniques and strategies used by companies to translate the value they create into revenues.
- **Data-Driven Innovation Framework**: framework that aids entrepreneurs in evaluating practical data-driven opportunities by systematically analyzing the interplay between supply and demand.
- **Data-Driven Innovation Canvas**: main outcome of the Data-driven innovation that focuses on business.
