## Chapter 8

Renewable energy technologies, particularly solar photovoltaic (PV) systems and fuel cells, are essential to the global transition towards sustainability. However, their intermittent power generation hinder widespread adoption. This chapter proposes a novel solution to enhance the performance of Solar PV-Fuel Cell Hybrids (SPV-FCH) through the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI). By combining solar energy's variability with the consistent output of fuel cells, this approach aims to create a more reliable energy system. The chapter discusses the AI-enabled control systems that improve energy yield, grid stability, and cost-effectiveness, contributing to the growing field of sustainable energy technologies.

## Chapter 9

## Chapter 11

Building upon the previous chapter, this section proposes an advanced machine learning-based framework for human activity recognition (ML-HARF) that addresses challenges related to sensor data processing. By integrating multiple sensor modalities, such as accelerometers and gyroscopes, the framework captures complex spatiotemporal patterns. It uses a hierarchical classification approach to extract and categorize features from raw data, achieving state-of-the-art accuracy in various activity recognition tasks. The chapter discusses the framework's scalability and efficiency, emphasizing its real-world applications in sectors like healthcare and sports.

### ABSTRACT

Financial fraud remains a persistent threat to various sectors including the public sector, government and finance industry, with fraudsters continuously adapting their strategies to exploit weaknesses in existing preventive measures, particularly with the increasing reliance on new technologies. This chapter delves into the potential of Machine Learning (ML) applications to support fraud detection capabilities within the financial sector. A comprehensive review of literature is conducted to investigate the effectiveness of various ML algorithms, including Support Vector Machine (SVM), Logistic Regression (LR), Random Forest (RF) and Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN), identifying the fraudulent activities. The research categorizes existing literature based on several criteria, such as the type of fraud analyzed, ML algorithms utilized and efficiency of various detection strategies of financial fraud. Additionally, the authors outline future directions.

### I. INTRODUCTION

Online fraud detection is a critical component in safeguarding users and consumers. The shift of business operations to digital platforms causes a major obstacle to e-commerce growth and contributes to substantial economic losses. As industries increasingly depend on credit cards, the accuracy of fraud detection is vital for reducing losses. According to Wang et al. (2020), the surge in CC transactions presents a challenge for researchers due to the scarcity of publicly available datasets, including transaction chronicles (Mehbdoida et al., 2021). Recent research reveals that the practice of publishing online reviews significantly undermines trust; Singh et al. (2022) argued that when respondents are aware that reviews have been manipulated, their trust remains affected. Meanwhile, the manual aspect involves investigators examining individual transactions (Singh et al., 2022). CC fraud occurs when an unauthorized individual utilizes CC details to make purchases without the consent of the cardholder (Alghandi et al., 2024). Using techniques like SMOTE and Adaboost, ML models can solve issues in datasets. All Et-Adaboost and XGB-Adaboost models notably reach a Matthew Correlation Coefficient (MCC) of 0.99. However, the researchers intend to expand their verification with the suggested framework on supplementary CC fraud datasets obtained from financial institutions (Ileberi et al., 2021).
