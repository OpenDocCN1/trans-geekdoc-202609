# Chapter 11

Machine Learning-Based Framework for Human Activity Recognition ................................... 193

Allampalli Harini, Pragati Engineering College, India
Manjula Devarakonda Venkata, Pragati Engineering College, India
Doodala Kondababu, Pragati Engineering College, India

This paper proposes a novel Machine Learning-Based Framework for Human Activity Recognition tailored to address the complexities and challenges inherent in accurately identifying and categorizing human activities from sensor data. ML-HARF integrates advanced machine learning algorithms with a comprehensive data preprocessing pipeline to extract meaningful features from raw sensor data. Leveraging a diverse array of sensors including accelerometers, gyroscopes, and magnetometers, HARF captures rich spatiotemporal patterns of human activities. The framework employs a hierarchical classification approach, wherein low-level features are initially extracted and subsequently aggregated to higher-level activity labels. ML-HARF outperforms other methods in extensive experiments on benchmark datasets, attaining state-of-the-art accuracy rates in a variety of activity recognition tasks. In real-world applications like sports analytics, healthcare monitoring and human-computer interaction systems, the framework's efficiency and scalability are also demonstrated.
