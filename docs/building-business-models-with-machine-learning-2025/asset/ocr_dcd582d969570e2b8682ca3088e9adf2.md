# About the Contributors

**Ambika N.** is an MCA, MPhil, and Ph.D. in computer science. She completed her Ph.D. from Bharathiar University in the year 2015. She has 18 years of teaching experience and works for St.Francis College, Bangalore. She has guided BCA, MCA, and M.Tech students in her projects. Her expertise includes wireless sensor networks, the Internet of things, and cybersecurity. She gives guest lectures on her expertise. She reviews books, conferences (national/international), encyclopedias, and journals. She is an advisory committee member of some conferences. She has many publications in National & international conferences, international books, national and international journals, and encyclopedias. She has some patent publications (National) and has received 5 copyrights in the computer science division.

**Vishal Jain** is presently working as Professor (CSE) at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, School of Engineering and Technology, Sharda University, Greater Noida, India. Before that, he worked for several years as an Associate Professor at Bharathi Vidyapeeth's Institute of Computer Applications and Management (BVICAM), New Delhi. He has more than 18 years of experience in the academics. He has earned degrees: Ph.D (CSE), M.Tech (CSE), MBA (HR), MCA, MCP, and CCNA. He has more than 1900 research citations with Google Scholar (h-index score 21 and i-index 49) and has authored more than 150 research papers in professional journals and conferences. He has authored and edited more than 60 books (most of them are indexed at the Scopus) with various publishers, including Elsevier, Springer, I, Academic Press, CRC, Taylor and Francis Group, Scriver, Wiley, Emerald, Nova Science, River Publishers, I-Global and Bentham Science. He has more than 160 Scopus publishers. He is the book editor of 10 book series with the reputed international publishers. His research area includes machine learning, information retrieval, semantic web, ontology engineering, data mining, ad hoc sensor networks, and network security. He received a Young Member Award for the year 2012-13 from the Computer Society of India, and Best Faculty Award for the year 2017 and Best Researcher Award for the year 2019 from BVICAM, New Delhi.
