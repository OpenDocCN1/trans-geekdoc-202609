# Recollecting Important Terminologies in Twitter Media

**Tweet:** A tweet can also be called microbe. It is a message or status posted by a user. Tweets can contain specific keywords, hashtags, URLs, and other content, but they are limited to 140 characters.

**Hashtags:** You can use a hashtag to draw attention to specific terms in a tweet. To indicate that a term is hashtag, it needs to be preceded with a # symbol. Hashtags are ideally used to indicate topics that are important now.

**Trends:** A term or hashtag that is trending on Twitter at a specific period may show up in the list of Trending Topics, which are the subjects that people are talking about at that moment. Twitter declares a set of trending topics periodically – may be every day which allows users an easy way to follow these on topics popular.
