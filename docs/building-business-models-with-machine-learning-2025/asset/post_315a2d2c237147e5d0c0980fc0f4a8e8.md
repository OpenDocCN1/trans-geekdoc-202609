## The Future View of Business Models

Businesses will probably implement new models and tactics in the future to capitalize on developing technologies and shifting market dynamics. Future business models that could be used include:

- 1. Platform-Based: Companies that use websites or applications as digital platforms to link clients with products or services are platform-based firms. Companies frequently link clients with the finest suppliers using algorithms and data. They might also offer extra services like customer service or payment processing.

- 2. Chain-Based: Distributed ledger technology, blockchain, enables safe, open, and decentralized transactions. In the future, firms can begin employing blockchain chain-based models to support the creation of new types of value such as tokenized assets, decentralized networks, and smart contracts.

- 3. Subscription-Based: A subscription-based firm is one that sells clients access to a good service, typically for a regular fee. Subscription companies often add additional perks like loyalty points or access to unique content and use data algorithms to facilitate the experience for each individual customer.

- 4. Sharing Economy: Is a business concept where products or services are shared by individuals or organizations, frequently via a digital platform. In addition to lowering transaction costs and promoting creativity and teamwork, this strategy can provide value by maximizing the use of unused assets.
