# An Application of DDT Canvas in Big Data Companies: The Case of Hudi

Big data has become a useful resource and an asset for enterprises, providing previously unattainable insights and chances for innovation (Acciarini et al., 2023; Cappa et al., 2021; Del Vecchio et al., 2018; Urbinati et al., 2019). Businesses require specific capabilities and the adoption of peculiar business models to fully realize the promise of big data (Acciarini et al., 2023; Johnon et al., 2017). A formal framework is offered by the DWI Canvas for defining, examining, and improving a company's data-driven innovation in big data companies (Zieliller, 2024; Zililler, 2021).

This chapter examines the business model used in big data businesses, concentrating on the case of Hudi, showing its importance in comprehending and improving businesses' operations. What emerges from this analysis is that big data enables new and established businesses to provide new value. The scoping of data-driven business prospects must consider a variety of viewpoints, from demands, data availability, and technological capabilities to ensure the sustainable formation of alliances and ecosystems, to capture these new sorts of value. The framework for data-driven innovation offers a tried-and-true technique for all participants in the BDI ecosystem to provide direction when examining and sizing up data-driven business prospects.
