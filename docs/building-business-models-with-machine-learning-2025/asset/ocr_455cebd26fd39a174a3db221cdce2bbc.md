**3. Prescriptive Analytics:** Provides specific procedures for handling ideal results and based on historical data, makes recommendations for handling similar situations in the future based on historical performance data. This kind of business analytics is not only capable of predicting results; it can also suggest the precise steps that must be taken in order to achieve the best conclusion. Complex neural networks and deep learning are frequently used to perform these analytics. This kind of business analytics is often used to match different solutions to a customer's current demands.
