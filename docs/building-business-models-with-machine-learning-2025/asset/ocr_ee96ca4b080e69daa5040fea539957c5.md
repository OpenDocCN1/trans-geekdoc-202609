A study by Pang et al. (2019) conducted a techno-economic analysis of a grid-connected solar PV-fuel cell hybrid system. They found that the hybrid system reduced the overall cost of electricity generation compared to standalone PV or fuel cell systems. The system's operation based on weather forecasts and electricity demand patterns resulted in improved energy efficiency and financial viability.

(Ghenai et al., 2020) created a hybrid PV/fuel cell power plant to handle the electric load of a desert-area residential subdivision. The system was developed with a renewable component of 40.2% and a leveled cost of energy of $15/MWh in the method. The method proposed to be both economically and environmentally sustainable.

The system met the residential community's AC primary load with low unmet demand. The system generated 52% of its power from solar PV and 48% from gasoline. The researcher came to the conclusion that the suggested renewable energy is a realistic choice for satisfying the electric load of desert towns. The system is both economically and environmentally feasible, with a high renewable component.

# 2. Energy Management Strategy for Hybrid Systems:

Chen et al. (2017) proposed an energy management strategy for a grid-connected solar PV-fuel cell system. The study focused on achieving load leveling and energy self-sufficiency through coordination of the PV system, fuel cell, and energy storage. The proposed strategy showed promise in minimizing grid dependency and ensuring a continuous power supply.

(Hu et al., 2021) suggested fuzzy control technique for HESS in ships may split the charge and discharge functions of the HESS to fulfill the ship's power requirement. The method ensures that the HESS is in good working order, which is critical for the ship's safety and reliability.

The experimental findings demonstrate that the proposed technique is successful and can increase the HESS's performance.

The findings of this work have implications for the distribution and capacity configuration of HESS. The findings indicate that fuzzy control could be a promising strategy for managing HESS in ships, leading to the development of more efficient and dependable HESS systems.

(Wang et al., 2023) offer an energy management technique based on reinforcement learning that can efficiently distribute the power's supplying charging and discharging circumstances, preserve the state of charge (SOC) of the battery, and fulfill the demand for power working while consuming less energy. They use simulations to test the proposed technique, and the findings demonstrate that it can considerably improve the efficacy and lifespan of the hybrid energy storage system.
