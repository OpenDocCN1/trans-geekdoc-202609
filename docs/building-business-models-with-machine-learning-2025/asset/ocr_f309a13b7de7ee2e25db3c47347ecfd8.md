+   * Cost: The cost of fuel cells, especially those using precious metals like platinum as catalysts, remains a barrier to widespread adoption.

+   * Durability: Some types of fuel cells may face challenges related to the durability of components, affecting their lifespan.

# 5. Applications:

+   * Transportation: Fuel cells are used in hydrogen fuel cell vehicles (FCVs), providing an alternative to traditional internal combustion engines.

+   * Stationary Power Generation: Fuel cells can be used in homes, businesses, and utilities to generate electricity and heat.

+   * Portable Electronics: Small, lightweight fuel cells are used to power portable electronic devices.

# 6. Future Trends:
