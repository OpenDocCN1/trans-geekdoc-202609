
The company provides a value provision driven by data and capitalizes on Big Data from the study of customers and data usage. Their approach is aimed at giving back users control of their data and assuring them a fair compensation for their valuable asset. Moreover, Judi allows businesses to have access to high-quality data, enhancing their ability to improve the decision-making process and providing better individualized experiences. The company cooperates with data providers and an open-source network, all inside the data ecosystem. Its strategy revolves around community building, continuous innovation, and customers acquisition, has pillars trust, ethics, and proper governance. Its revenue comes from various sources such as transaction, premium services, and analytics capabilities. Finally, Judi type of business is about turning data into value through capturing data. In the peculiarity of the single case, further research could explore examples of a similar approach, like Datum (https://datum.org/), Ocean Protocol (https://oceanprotocol.com/), Streamr (https://streamr.network/), and Brave (https://brave.com/), alongside the giants Google, Amazon, Meta, further research could investigate cases from smaller enterprises and startups, like Kode (https://www.code-solutions.net/), and BDV (https://www.bigdata-value.eu/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/BDV-Startups-and-ME-characterization-v1.0_FINAL_Annexes.pdf) and Soidata (https://www.soidata.eu/). In conclusion, this chapter offered a thorough examination of converting data into valuable assets, with emphasis on the Hudi case. The theoretical framework, empirical studies, and use of DDI have all been used to obtain insights into the potentialities of monetization. The findings emphasize the significance of data as a strategic asset, the requirement for new business models, and ethical issues surrounding ownership. Thus, by presenting a theoretical framework and useful insights via the case of Hudi, this chapter acknowledges data monetization. Overall, the data economy keeps advancing, the lessons from Judi can direct future endeavors in turning data into value assets and shaping the future of data-driven world.
