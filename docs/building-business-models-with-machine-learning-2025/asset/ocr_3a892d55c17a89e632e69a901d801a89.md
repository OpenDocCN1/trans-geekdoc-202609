We will choose the k value where the inertia decrease stabilizes using the elbow approach.
The data are not yet clustered when k=1 since inertia is at its maximum. The descent stabilizes at k=4 and proceeds linearly to generate an elbow at k=4. This indicates that 4 is the optimum of customers per group.
