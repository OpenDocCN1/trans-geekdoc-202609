### THEORETICAL LITERATURE REVIEW

Financial goals, services, and investments created specifically to support environmentally friendly projects are referred to as "green financing." It involves allocating funds to initiatives that benefit the environment, such as waste management, sustainable agriculture, clean technology, and renewable energy. The objectives of green funding are to combat climate change, enhance resource efficiency, and aid in sustainability (2023).

Sustainability finance models are financial structures and methods that prioritize the long-term health of people, communities, and the environment. These include social, environmental, and governance factors in financial decision-making. The goals of sustainability finance are to ensure the fair allocation of opportunities and resources, encourage social equity, and promote inclusive economic growth (2022).

A company’s decisions and initiatives to conduct business in an ethical and responsible manner, considering the interests of many stakeholders—such as customers, employees, communities, and the environment—are referred to as corporate social responsibility (CSR). It entails choosing to go above and beyond the law and incorporating environmental and social concerns into operations (2023).
