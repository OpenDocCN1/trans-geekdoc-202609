# K. Decision Tree Algorithm Enhanced with Regression Analysis for Fraud Detection in CC Transactions

Baghi and his co-authors (2021) added regression analysis to a decision tree algorithm to create an enhanced credit card fraud detection system. Regression analysis and decision trees were integrated in order to improve the system's capacity to detect fraudulent transactions with greater accuracy and to efficiently track suspicious activity. The resulting system demonstrated a high level of effectiveness, with an accuracy rate of 81.6%, indicating its strong performance in classifying transactions. Additionally, the system successfully detected all test intrusions, showcasing its reliability in identifying fraudulent activities. However, the study also highlights several drawbacks. The regression analysis and DT are together which increases the system complexity, which leads to the management and execution is more difficult. The risk of over-fitting is more challenging which is faced by the system. The untested datasets are poorly developed that again leads to the over-fitting issues. The complexity has been increased due to the inoperative of the real-world applications. High volume datasets. This inefficiency leads to the complicated infrastructure that decrease the accuracy and slower processing times. This meets the issues such as raises of cost and complexity of the system. Ultimately, by combining decision tree and regression can yield powerful insights, the associated difficulties necessitate careful consideration and ensure effective deployment and maintenance.
