
To establish CNN on 2D irregular grids such as a triangular grid, the original definition of “*r-*neighbourhood” is generalised from the rectangular grid to handle arbitrary grids, in which the grid points are positioned arbitrarily and linked to their neighbouring points. The *r-*neighbourhood of the cell *C*(*i*) associated with the grid point *i* is defined by

`N[*r*](*i*) = {C(*j*)|links(*C*(*i*),*C*(*j*))≤*r*}` (4.11)  

The state of a cell in the autonomous CNN is expressed by the following equation:

`$$C_{x}\\frac{dv_{xi}(t)}{dt} = -\\frac{1}{R_{x}}v_{xi}(t) + \\sum_{c(j)\\in N_{r}(i)}A(i;j)v_{yj}(t) + I_{i}$$` (4.12)

where the subscripts *i* and *j* denote the respective quantities at the grid points *i* and *j* associated with the cells *C*(*i*) and *C*(*j*).

To discretise the Pennes bio-heat transfer equation on the 2D irregular grids, the CNN formulation is achieved with the assistance of the concept of control area \[[55](#C11_chapter4.xhtml#ref4_55)\]. The problem domain is divided into a finite number of non-overlapping control areas with computation (grid) points located at centres, over which the conservation of energy is enforced in a discrete manner. An example of a control area of the point *i* is illustrated in [Figure 4.3](#C11_chapter4.xhtml#f4_3), and it is formed by connecting each centroid of the triangles and the mid-points of the edges adjacent to P[*i*].

For the point *i* enclosed by the control area *S*[*i*], the discrete form of the Pennes bio-heat transfer equation can be expressed as

`$$\int_{S_{i}} (\\rho_{i}C_{i}\\frac{\\partial T_{i}(t)}{\\partial t} + w_{b,i}C_{b,i}(T_{i}(t)-T_{a,i}) - Q_{i} - H_{i}) dS = \\int_{S_{i}} \\nabla \\cdot (k_{i}\\nabla T_{i}(t)) dS}$$ (4.13)

Applying Gauss’s divergence theorem yields

`$$\\begin{array}{l} \\int_{S_{i}} (\\rho_{i}C_{i}\\frac{\\partial T_{i}(t)}{\\partial t} + w_{b,i}C_{b,i}(T_{i}(t)-T_{a,i}) - Q_{i} - H_{i}) dS \\\\ = \\int_{\\partial S_{i}} k_{i}\\nabla T_{i}(t) \\cdot ndl = \\int_{\\partial S_{i}} k_{i}(\\frac{\\partial T(t)}{\\partial n})_{i} dl \\end{array}$$` (4.14)

where **n** is the unit normal vector pointing outwards at the boundary of *S*[*i*].

Rearranging the above equation yields

`$$\\rho_{i}C_{i}\\frac{\\partial T_{i}(t)}{\\partial t} = \\frac{1}{S_{i}}\\int_{\\partial S_{i}} k_{i}(\\frac{\\partial T(t)}{\\partial\\mathbf{n}})_{i} dl - w_{b,i}C_{b,i}(T_{i}(t)-T_{a,i}) + Q_{i} + H_{i}$$` (4.15)

which can be approximated by

`$$\\begin{matrix} \\rho_{i}C_{i}\\frac{\\partial T_{i}(t)}{\\partial t} = \\frac{1}{S_{i}}\\sum_{j\\in N_{r}(i)}\\frac{(k_{j}T_{j}(t)-k_{i}T_{i}(t))}{l_{ij}}(l_{uv}+l_{vw}) \\\\ - w_{b,i}C_{b,i}(T_{i}(t)-T_{a,i}) + Q_{i} + H_{i} \\end{matrix}$$` (4.16)

where `l_{ij} = \\|\\overrightarrow{\\mathbf{P}_{i}P_{j}}\\|`, `l_{uv} = \\|\\overrightarrow{\\mathbf{u}v}\\|` and `l_{vw} = \\|\\overrightarrow{\\mathbf{v}w}\\|`.

Rearranging the above equation leads to

`$$\\begin{matrix} \\rho_{i}C_{i}\\frac{\\partial T_{i}(t)}{\\partial t} = -\\left(\\frac{1}{S_{i}}\\sum_{j\\in N_{r}(i)}\\frac{k_{i}(l_{uv}+l_{vw})}{l_{ij}} + w_{b,i}C_{b,i}\\right)T_{i}(t) \\\\ + \\frac{1}{S_{i}}\\sum_{j\\in N_{r}(i)}\\frac{k_{j}(l_{uv}+l_{vw})}{l_{ij}}T_{j}(t) + w_{b,i}C_{b,i}T_{a,i} + Q_{i} + H_{i} \\end{matrix}$$` (4.17)

It can be seen that Eqs. (4.12) and (4.17) are remarkably similar; therefore, the discrete form of the Pennes bio-heat transfer equation (Eq. 4.17) is embedded into the autonomous CNN (Eq. 4.12) by associating the following parameters:

* `C[x] = ρ[i]C[i]`
* `v[x]i(t) = T[i](t)`
* `v[y]j(t) = T[j](t)`
* `I[i] = w[b,i]C[b,i]T[a,i] + Q[i] + H[i]`

`$$A(i;i) = -\\left(\\frac{1}{S_{i}}\\sum_{j\\in N_{r}(i)}\\frac{k_{i}(l_{uv}+l_{vw})}{l_{ij}} + w_{b,i}C_{b,i}\\right) + \\frac{1}{R_{x}}$$` (4.18)

`$$A(i;j) = \\frac{1}{S_{i}}\\frac{k_{j}(l_{uv}+l_{vw})}{l_{ij}}$$`

### [4.3.3 CNN Formulation of Bio-Heat Transfer on 3D Grids](#C11_chapter4.xhtml#rsecc004_5_3)

#### [4.3.3.1 3D Regular Grids](#C11_chapter4.xhtml#rsecc004_5_3_1)

The CNN formulation on 3-D regular grids can be expressed using a generalisation of 2D CNN to higher dimension cases, by a 3D rectangular array of cells. Define a grid point *i*, *j*, *k* associated with the cell *C*(*i*,*j*,*k*), and `Δw_{k-1} = \\|\\overrightarrow{\\mathbf{P}_{i,j,k-1}P_{i,j,k}}\\|` and `Δw_{k} = \\|\\overrightarrow{\\mathbf{P}_{i,j,k}P_{i,j,k+1}}\\|` where **P**[*i*, *j*, *k*] is the position of the grid point *i*, *j*,  *k*. In analogy to the 2D regular grid case using FDM, the discrete form of the Pennes bio-heat transfer equation on a 3D regular grid can be expressed as

`$$\\begin{matrix} \\rho_{i,j,k}C_{i,j,k}\\frac{\\partial T_{i,j,k}(t)}{\\partial t} = -\\left(\\frac{2k_{i,j,k}}{\\Delta u_{i-1}\\Delta u_{i}} + \\frac{2k_{i,j,k}}{\\Delta v_{j-1}\\Delta v_{j}} + \\frac{2k_{i,j,k}}{\\Delta w_{k-1}\\Delta w_{k}} + w_{b,i,j,k}C_{b,i,j,k}\\right)T_{i,j,k}(t) \\\\ + \\frac{2k_{i+1,j,k}T_{i+1,j,k}(t)}{\\Delta u_{i}(\\Delta u_{i-1}+\\Delta u_{i})} + \\frac{2k_{i-1,j,k}T_{i-1,j,k}(t)}{\\Delta u_{i-1}(\\Delta u_{i-1}+\\Delta u_{i})} \\\\ + \\frac{2k_{i,j+1,k}T_{i,j+1,k}(t)}{\\Delta v_{j}(\\Delta v_{j-1}+\\Delta v_{j})} + \\frac{2k_{i,j-1,k}T_{i,j-1,k}(t)}{\\Delta v_{j-1}(\\Delta v_{j-1}+\\Delta v_{j})} \\\\ + \\frac{2k_{i,j,k+1}T_{i,j,k+1}(t)}{\\Delta w_{k}(\\Delta w_{k-1}+\\Delta w_{k})} + \\frac{2k_{i,j,k-1}T_{i,j,k-1}(t)}{\\Delta w_{k-1}(\\Delta w_{k-1}+\\Delta w_{k})} \\\\ + w_{b,i,j,k}C_{b,i,j,k}T_{a,i,j,k} + Q_{i,j,k} + H_{i,j,k} \\end{matrix}$$` (4.19)
