# 7.3.2 IFS

Atanassov [1], IFS is a generalization of FSs and is defined as

$$A = \{<x, \phi_{A}(x), \psi_{A}(x)>|x \in X\}$$

where, $\phi_{A}(x), \psi_{A}(x): X \rightarrow [0,1]$ are the membership and nonmembership function of $x \in X$ with $\pi_{A}(x)=1-\phi_{A}(x)-\psi_{A}(x)$ as the intuitionistic index or hesitation index of $A$ in $A$.

# 7.3.3 INTUITIONISTIC FUZZY VARIABLE

For a time variable, $t$, $\zeta(t)=\left(\phi_{\zeta(t)}, \psi_{\zeta(t)}\right)$ is called the intuitionistic fuzzy variable.

In general, If $t = t_{1}, t_{2}, \ldots, t_{p}$, then $\zeta\left(t_{1}\right), \ldots, \zeta\left(t_{p}\right);\left(\phi_{\zeta\left(t_{i}\right)}, \psi_{\zeta\left(t_{i}\right)}\right) \in[0,1]$ be the intuitionistic fuzzy numbers (IFNs) collected at different periods.

The concept was proposed by Xu and Yager [15].
