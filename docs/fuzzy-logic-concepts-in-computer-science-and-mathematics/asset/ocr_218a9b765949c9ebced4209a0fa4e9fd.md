Definition 5.4.4. If T is an IFS with \(^{\backslash}(v_{i} \bar{z}_{j})\) as a minimal IFT of \(\mathbb{H}_{G}^{(v_{i} \bar{z}_{j})}\) and \(\forall\left(v_{i}, z_{j}\right) \in(0,1)\) for which \(\sum_{u_{i} \in v} \mu_{y}\left(u_{i}\right) \leq s_{1}\) and \(\sum_{u_{i} \in v}\left(1-v_{i}\left(u_{i}\right)\right) \leq s_{2}\) and \(\forall 1 \leq i \leq m, 1 \leq j \leq n\) implies T is known by locally minimal IFT of IFTHG.
