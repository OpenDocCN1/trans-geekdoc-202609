# 9.4.2 SELECTION OF CRITERIA

The nine financial ratios of six oil companies which have enlisted at NIFTY on 16.08.2023 in NSE have been taken for six financial years, that is, FY 2017–2018, FY 2018–2019, FY 2019–2020, FY 2020–2021, FY 2021–2022, and FY 2022–2023.

+   1. Sources of data: NSE website.

+   2. Type of data: Secondary data.

+   3. Period of study: 6 years (FY 2017–2018 to FY 2022–2023).

+   4. Technique used: F-GTMA.
