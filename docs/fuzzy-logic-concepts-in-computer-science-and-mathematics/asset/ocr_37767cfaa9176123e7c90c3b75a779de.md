The information given is in the form of ITrFNs, which is aggregated using DIFWA operator. Further, the opinion of three domain experts is considered while taking the decision. Decision-making is made and the alternatives are ranked on the basis of the calculated value of closeness coefficient. More the value of closeness coefficient, more the preference will be given to the alternative. This model can be utilized for other such situations to avoid the unnecessary expenditure on surveys. The model is more suitable to perform initial investigation of the problem in hand under uncertainty.
