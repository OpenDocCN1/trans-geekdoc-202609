# **7.3.5 DYNAMIC INTUITIONISTIC FUZZY WEIGHTED AVERAGING (DIFWA) OPERATOR**

Xu and Yager [15], let $\zeta(t_1),\ldots,\zeta(t_q)$ be a collection of IFNs collected at $q$ different periods and $\tau$ be the weight vector of the periods $t_*$, then $DIFWA_{\tau(t)}$ $(\zeta(t_1),... ,\zeta(t_q)) = \tau(t_1)\zeta(t_2) \oplus ........ \oplus \tau(t_q)\zeta(t_q)$ is called DIFWA operator.

$$\begin{gathered} DIFWA_{\tau(t)}(\zeta(t_1),...,\zeta(t_q)) \\ = \left(1-\prod_{k=1}^{q}(1-\phi_{\zeta(t_k)})^{\tau(t_k)},\prod_{k=1}^{q}\psi_{\zeta(t_k)}^{\tau(t_k)}, \prod_{k=1}^{q}(1-\phi_{\zeta(t_k)})^{\tau(t_k)}-\prod_{k=1}^{q}\psi_{\zeta(t_k)}^{\tau(t_k)} \right) \end{gathered}$$

where, $\tau(t_k) \geq 0 ; \sum_{k=1}^{q}\tau(t_k) = 1$

In this chapter, information is taken in the form of ITrFN and decision- making is taking with the help of DIFWA operator.
