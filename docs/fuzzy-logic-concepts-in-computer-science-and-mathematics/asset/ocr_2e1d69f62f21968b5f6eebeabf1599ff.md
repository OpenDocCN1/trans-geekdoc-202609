# ABSTRACT

In intuitionistic fuzzy threshold hypergraphs (IFTHGs), a transversal is a hyperedge that cuts more than two hyperedges instead of a line that intersects two lines in geometry. The intuitionistic fuzzy transversal (IFT), minimum IFT, locally minimal IFT, and intuitionistic fuzzy transversal core of IFTHG are defined and it has been established that every IFTHG has a nonempty IFT. Some of the characteristics of transversals of IFTHGs were additionally examined. Also, this chapter explores the application of transversals in the context of IFTHGs for optimizing drip irrigation practices in agriculture.
