# 6.2.2 PROPERTIES

Let *A* and *B* be two IFSs. We define some operations and relations on *A* and *B* as follows:

+   1) Inclusion: $A \subseteq B \leftrightarrow \mu_{A}(e) \le \mu_{B}(e)$ and $v_{A}(e) \ge v_{B}(e) \ \forall e \in U$
2) Equality: $A = B \leftrightarrow \mu_{A}(e) = \mu_{B}(e)$ and $v_{A}(e) = v_{B}(e) \ \forall e \in U$
3) Negation: $\overline{A} = \{ \langle e, v_{A}(e), \mu_{A}(e) \rangle | e \in U \}$
4) Union: $A \cup B = \{ \langle e, max(\mu_{A}(e), \mu_{B}(e)), min(v_{A}(e), v_{B}(e)) \rangle ; e \in U \}$
5) Intersection: $A \cap B = \{ \langle e, min(\mu_{A}(e), \mu_{B}(e)), max(v_{A}(e), v_{B}(e)) \rangle ; e \in U \}$
6) Symmetric difference: $A \triangle B = \{ \langle e, max[ min(\mu_{A}, v_{B}), min(\mu_{B}, v_{A}) ], min[ max(v_{A}, \mu_{B}), max(v_{B}, \mu_{A}) ] \rangle ; e \in U \}$
7) Cartesian Product: $A \times B = \{ \langle \mu_{A}(e)\mu_{B}(e), v_{A}(e)v_{B}(e) \rangle ; e \in U \}$

# 6.3.1 ALGEBRAIC STRUCTURES

There has been research on the lattice and algebraic structures of the IFSs [44]. Additionally, there have been studies on the algebraic structures of IFSSs. One paper discusses the algebraic structures of complex IFSSs linked with groups and subgroups [33]. Another study demonstrates that the space of intuitionistic fuzzy values (IFVs), when equipped with a linear order determined by a score and accuracy function, exhibits a similar algebraic structure as the space generated by a linear order based on a similarity function and an accuracy function. Furthermore, this space is both a topological space and a lattice [41].
