# 9.1.8 NOVELTIES

Fuzzy numbers have been studied by numerous researchers using the MCDM methodologies AHP, TOPSIS, MARCOS, and COPRAS. Under the GTMA MCDM paradigm, hardly any study has been conducted with triangular fuzzy numbers (TFNs). The equations for TFN defuzzification have been created and applied. Additionally defined is the distance measured between two TFNs. To compute the triangular fuzzy weight of factors and sub-factors, formulas have been created. A method for combining the opinions of multiple decision-makers into a single complete value in terms of TFN has been created.
