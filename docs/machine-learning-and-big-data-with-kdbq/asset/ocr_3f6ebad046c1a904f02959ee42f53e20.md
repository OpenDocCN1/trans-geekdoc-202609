# 13.11 CROSS-VALIDATION

Finally, we extend the concept of in/out sample testing and introduce cross-
validation. The cross-validation is extended out-of-sample testing. The primaryobjective is to choose which model, among those, fits the best model the
in terms of out-of-sample performance. The in/out sample splitting presented in the
previous section relies on one type of splitting. The out-of-sample is fixed,
and this may affect the outcome of the test. If, for the out-of-sample is
affected by exogenous noise, the models tested can prove weaker while
they may be on the rest of the test. This would make sense to try any
in/out splitting schemes and derive the result from a such of outcomes.

cross-validation scheme, which is the k-fold cross-validation. It is non-exhaustive
