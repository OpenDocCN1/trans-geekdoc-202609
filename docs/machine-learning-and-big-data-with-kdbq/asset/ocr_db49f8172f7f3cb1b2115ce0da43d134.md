# 7.2.9 asof

The asof verb has similar functionality to aj but also supports the typical “noun verb” mode. The right-hand side argument is a dictionary or table, where the last key or column plays a role of the reference time for which we want to find last valid observation from the dictionary/table has more than one key/column, the last one is considered to be time while the remaining ones play the role of the grouping column (e.g. sym ) demonstrated in the aj verb.

Let us recall two tables from the previous demonstrated:

```
at5

t sym a
-- ---
0.1 a 10
5.1 a 20
7.1 a 30
1.1 b 40
5.1 b 50
6.1 b 60
```

and

```
at6

t sym b
-- ---
3 a 300
4 a 400
8 a 800
9 a 900
10 a 1000
3 b 30000
4 b 40000
8 b 80000
9 b 90000
10 b 100000
```
