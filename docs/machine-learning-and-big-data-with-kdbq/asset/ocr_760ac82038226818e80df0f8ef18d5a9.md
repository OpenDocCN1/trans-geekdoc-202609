# 11.1 ROLLING VWAP

The first example originates in high-frequency finance. Any update in the market will result in an update (tick) in our data. It is of fundamental importance for any market participant to understand the current state of the market. For various statistics and indicators are needed to monitor market activity. Let us assume that we wish to calculate moving VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price) over a rolling window. We will cover two use cases.

# 11.1.1 Tick VWAP
