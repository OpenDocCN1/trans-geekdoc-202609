# 16.1 INTRODUCTION

Technology is quickly reshaping industries, and one of the leading industries at the time of this transformation is healthcare. One highly considerable innovation is the implementation of 5G, which stands for the fifth-generation wireless technology [1]. Ultra-high speeds, low latency, and improved connectivity 5G is a revolution in the delivery of healthcare, improving patient care through telemedicine applications, remote monitoring, and the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) [2]. Seamless real-time communication and data transfer would break geographical and logistical barriers with the provisioning—making quality healthcare available at one's behest. This chapter investigates the influence of 5G technology on different aspects of healthcare. It discusses the ways in which 5G technology can enable remote consultation, advanced monitoring, and artificial intelligence (AI) integration for personalized medicine. Moreover, the paper provides an overview of current applications of 5G technology in healthcare, based on examples of implemented solutions that have already shown good results, and describes the problems and possibilities of further development impelled by 5G technologies.

The most important of these is that 5G will support ultra-high data speeds—with theoretical maximum speeds reaching 10 Gbit/s. This compares with 1 Gbit/s for 4G and 2 Mbit/s for 3G.
