# 16.3.2 Limitations of existing communication technologies in healthcare

Such 3G and 4G networks have made great contributions in the healthcare field by enabling services as telemedicine, electronic health records, and wearable health monitoring devices [13]. However, these technologies still have several limitations, which result in major barriers to fully exploiting advanced healthcare solutions. Figure 16.2 shows the limitations of existing communication technologies in healthcare. High latency is one limitation, occurring when levels range from 50 to 100 ms or more in 3G and 4G networks. The kind of delay hampers real-time interaction, which is essential for applications like remote surgeries or live consultations. The lag may compromise the quality of telemedicine services, as it would be challenging for healthcare professionals to control remote devices to perform accurate and time-sensitive procedures from a distance [14].

Limited bandwidth is another issue that affects video transmission in telemedicine. Available communication networks restrict bandwidth, potentially degrading the quality of these transmissions. Data transfer speed is also a concern, as 3G and 4G networks result in low data transfer speeds, inhibiting the timely processing of large volumes of medical data—such as high-resolution images, patient visuals, and complex diagnostics.
