# 1.3.1. Normal lung sound

Most lung sounds are tracheal, bronchial, vesicular, and bronchovesicular [76]. Auscultation location, manufacture, appearance, and auditory attributes differ. The trachea creates noise due to turbulent airflow [77]. This sound is detected above the sternum during inspiratory and expiratory auscultation.
