# 6.4.2 Emergency response and support

Description: Emergency response drones equipped with life-saving equipment, such as external defibrillators (AEDs), can be deployed to incident locations further than conventional emergency services (Baker & Carter, 2023; Hall & King, 2022). This rapid delivery is critical for improving survival rates during cardiac or trauma-related emergencies, as shown in Figure 6.4.
Components: These drones include secure equipment compartments, HMI interfaces enabling emergency own personnel to request drone support, and high-definition cameras with microphones for real-time situational awareness (Foster & Gray, 2023; Walker & Harris, 2022).
