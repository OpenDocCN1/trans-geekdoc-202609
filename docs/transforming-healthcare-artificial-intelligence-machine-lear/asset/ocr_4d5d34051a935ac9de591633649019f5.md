# 15.2 NETWORK OPTIMIZATION AND MANAGEMENT

The incorporation of ML and AI into mobile network systems has significantly advanced network optimization and management, leading to more efficient, reliable, and adaptive networks. Traditional network management techniques, which rely heavily on manual intervention and static configurations, are increasingly insufficient to handle the complexities of modern mobile networks. AI and ML offer dynamic and automated solutions that enhance the overall performance and reliability of these networks.

# 15.2.1 Self-organizing networks (SON)

One of the most transformative applications of AI in network optimization is the development of SON. SON technology leverages AI to automate the configuration, optimization, and healing processes of mobile networks. This reduces the need for human intervention and allows networks to self-adjust in real time based on changing conditions. For example, SON can dynamically optimize network parameters such as frequency allocation, power levels, and handover processes to ensure optimal performance and coverage. This is particularly beneficial in dense urban environments where network conditions can change rapidly due to varying traffic patterns and user mobility.

# 15.2.2 Predictive maintenance

Predictive maintenance is another critical area where ML has made a substantial impact. By analyzing data from network components, ML algorithms can predict potential failures before they occur. This proactive approach enables network operators to perform maintenance activities preemptively, reducing downtime and maintenance costs. Predictive maintenance not only boosts network reliability but also prolongs the lifespan of network equipment by averting catastrophic failures that could cause more damage.
