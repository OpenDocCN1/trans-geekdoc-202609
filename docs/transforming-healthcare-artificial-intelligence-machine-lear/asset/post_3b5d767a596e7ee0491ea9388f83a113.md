## 18.3.4 Feature selection

This work proposes a unique meta-heuristic method called the white shark optimiser (WSO). It is modelled after the erratic actions of great white sharks, which have excellent sensation of fragrance and earshot for hunting and navigating [29]. In Melanoma detection, white shark optimisation can be modified and called adaptive white shark optimisation, primarily when skin colour is utilised as the feature selection. By altering the optimisation technique to unique needs of melanoma detection, this adaptive technique improves the efficiency and accuracy of feature selection pertinent to. The algorithm may more effectively discriminate between benign and malignant cases by constantly adjusting its search based on the data [30].

As incredibly versatile and proficient hunters, white shark takes down a wide range of prey, as seabirds, dolphins, small whales, and crabs. Great sharks hunt by sunrise, using these skills to attack their prey with devastating havoc [31]. (1) stalking prey through prey's movements, (2) haphazardly scouring the ocean floor for food sources, and (3) tracking nearby prey [32,33]. The white shark optimiser behaviours are a guide to update its position and identify the best solutions.
