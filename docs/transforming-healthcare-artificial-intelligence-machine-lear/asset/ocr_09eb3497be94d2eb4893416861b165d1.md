# 2.1 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the artificial mimicking of human intelligence by simulation of various processes owned by human beings but with the help of machines (computers). To mention human processes, the learning, reasoning, and auto-correction are some of the major processes. If it needs to carry out more number of processes that are difficult for human reasoning, it is suggested to go with AI in order to make them happen.

The purpose of AI is to develop a system that can able to do analysis with the given dataset by comparison or testing and to obtain efficient decisions. These decisions are more likely to be implemented or adopted across the industries of healthcare environment.

If experts provide valuable insights, expertise and recommendations, and future predictions, AI-based tools will help individuals to obtain intelligent decisions and good outcomes with a reduced risk of errors.

In general, artificial intelligence, or simply AI, is originated from the roots of computer science, and it uses advanced technologies, which will enable the processing unit to perform various tasks or challenges with intelligence [1]. Some of the tasks are listed as follows:

+   * Perception
* Understanding and transformation of languages
* Data analysis
* Human reasoning
* Understanding from the previous experience

AI is functioning with combination of more number of data with high processing speed and also intelligent computational algorithms to have automatic learning from certain data features or patterns. Some special programmes can work from the past learning by analyzing complex natural data and improving their performance without any human intervention. In general, AI is carried out by simulating human intelligence by using computers and various computational algorithms.
