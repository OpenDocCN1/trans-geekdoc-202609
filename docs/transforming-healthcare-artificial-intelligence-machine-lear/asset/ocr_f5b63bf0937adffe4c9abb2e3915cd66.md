It is the intention of this research paper to critically analyze how 5G
technology drives the evolution of healthcare through telemedicine, remote
monitoring, and overall health service deliveries. The present research
deavours to show that 5G, ultra-low latency, high-speed data transfer,
and advanced connectivity technology help create innovative patient care
solutions, solving the problems of inaccessibility and inefficiency in health-
 services with their quality. Moreover, this study tries to comprehend
how 5G may be integrated with frontier technologies like AI and the IoMT
