# II.1 INTRODUCTION

Cancer is one of the most deadly diseases of the century. It occurs in almost all parts of the human body including brain, bones, blood, skin, and stomach and affects humans irrespective of their ages. Detection of cancer in the initial stages may have the probability of survival against cancer. Brain tumor [1] is an imperative type of cancer that has both initial and cancerous stages and is difficult to detect in the initial stages. MRI, Computer-aided tomography, several machine learning (ML), transformer-based models and deep learning models, and combination of artificial intelligence (AI) techniques were employed in the detection of tumors in benign stages as it has higher probability of survival.
