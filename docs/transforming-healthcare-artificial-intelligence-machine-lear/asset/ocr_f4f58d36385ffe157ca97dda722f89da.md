# 3.5 RESULTS AND DISCUSSION

In this chapter, concerning the results of the various assessments, an analysis of the application of AI and ML for diagnosing diseases such as osteoporosis, autism, skin cancer, and eye disease is performed. Such models can augment diagnostic capabilities, decrease the time needed for the analysis, and even suggest personalised treatment approaches by employing complex high-order learning algorithms and optimisation approaches. The rest of the sections are devoted to the detailed performance evaluation of such models, emphasizing performance measures such as accuracy, recall, precision, and FI-score. Besides, the effect on the efficiency and the time for model training with the help of optim algorithms such as Particle Swarm Optimisation (PSO) and Red-Coded Genetic Algorithms (RCGA) is studied. This study presents the potential evolution of AI and ML in medicine and points out areas for further developments in medical diagnostics and therapies.
