Here, I proposed a SS technique based on DL. In CR systems, the suggested architecture enhances the management of spectrum resources for secondary users. The suggested approach used the raw data signal without any prior knowledge of the PU signal, including noise density. Generally, the suggested SS technique consists of online detection and offline training.

The stages followed in this experimentation are as follows:

At first, this strategy proposes a DNN that is learned by utilizing data gathered during the primary and secondary user sensing procedure.

Second, in the online detection process, the well-trained DNN receives fresh information every time it is received, using which data and probabilities are obtained. The presence or absence of the PU was preceded by those probability. Third, in the offline training phase, SS is conceptualized as a binary hypothesis testing scenario. Similarly, the proposed deep learning model approach is approached as a binary classification problem.

CR devices are proposed as a solution to the spectrum scarcity problem in radio frequency environments, aiming to optimize overall spectrum utilization. However, SS in CR systems, particularly in high-performance and energy-efficient systems (SS-CRS), poses challenges. SS performance often decreases with longer SS times. This inefficiency impacts the efficiency of secondary users (SUs) who must allocate significant time and resources to SS tasks that do not directly contribute to data transmission.

ML enables computers to optimize performance by learning from sample data or existing knowledge. In the context of SS, ML algorithms approach the problem by treating it as a classification task. Whether supervised or unsupervised, the classifier must decide between two states for each frequency channel: whether it is free or occupied.
