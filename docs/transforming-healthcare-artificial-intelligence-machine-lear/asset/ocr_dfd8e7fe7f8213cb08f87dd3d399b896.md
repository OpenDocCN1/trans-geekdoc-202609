CNN and GRU obtained 86% and 89% accuracy in wheeze classification, whereas the combination model reached 93%. The CNN+GRU model consistently outperforms other models in categorizing crackles and wheezes, with 92% accuracy, 90% precision, 89% recall, 89.5% F1-scores.
The hybrid model identified normal respiratory sounds with the best accuracy of 95% for healthy lungs. The hybrid model reduces misclassification and captures small lung sound fluctuations better than the CNN and GRU models, as seen by its improved accuracy, recall, and F1-scores across all sound classes. Lung sound categorization is more reliable and accurate using the hybrid CNN+GRU model, which improves respiratory illness diagnosis.
