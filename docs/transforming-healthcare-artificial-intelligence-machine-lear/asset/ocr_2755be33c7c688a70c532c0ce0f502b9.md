DL identifies illnesses using electronic stethoscope or mobile device biometrics. DL and AI are used in COVID-19 [25,26,27], lung auscultation, lung chest radiology, and cough sound analysis. A revolutionary breathing sound approach will enhance AI analysis and application. DL may account for medical staff ausclassification mistakes to expedite lung diagnosis and therapy [28].

Medical experts utilize obsolete methods [29]:

+   1. Lung tests measure oxygen production and function. Tests usually include spirometry. Inhaling into a spirometer measures exhaled air.
2. Chest X-ray: Detects emphysema and lung issues. COPD is primarily emphysema.
3. CT scan: Rarely used when other methods fail. CT scans may suggest COPD surgery.
4. Analyzing arterial blood gas measures breathing-induced oxygen.

Limitations of current methods:

+   1. Surgery and cardiac patients cannot use spirometry.
2. Testing might cause dizziness, nausea, and dyspnoea.
3. Radiation from CT and X-rays endangers patients.

Under constraints and with the rise of artificial intelligence, ML and DL algorithms were investigated, created, and used to help doctors identify respiratory diseases quickly. With the correct methodology, respiratory organ images or sounds may identify respiratory diseases.
