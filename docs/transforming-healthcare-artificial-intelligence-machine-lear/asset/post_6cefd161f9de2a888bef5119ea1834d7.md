# 9.3 PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT DETECTION

Personal protective equipment (PPE) is a vital piece of equipment used in the workplace to lower risks and prevent accidents from mechanical, electrical, and other hazards. Gloves, safety glasses, hard helmets, respirators, and full-body suits are few examples. To guarantee worker safety, PPE needs to be well-designed, kept in good condition, and fit comfortably. When administrative or engineering controls are insufficient, employers bear the responsibility of supplying and guaranteeing the appropriate usage of PPE. Despite safety rules, workplace accidents continue to be a serious concern, and traditional methods of monitoring are labor-intensive and prone to human error. Using YOLOv9-based CV system, the suggested solution tackles these issues and provides two essential features: real-time fall detection for prompt intervention and automatic PPE detection to guarantee appropriate usage and adherence to safety procedures. The system is trained on 7,784 photos for fall detection and 1,613 images for PPE detection utilizing datasets from Roboflow. These images cover categories including person detection, boots, helmets, and vests. The objectives of this automated system are to improve worker safety, lower accident rates, advance data-driven safety procedures.

    - Partial Occlusions: Enhancing the model to address situations in which PPE is partially concealed, such as when a helmet is covered by a structural component.
