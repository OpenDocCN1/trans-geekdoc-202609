# 2.4.1.3 Blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) by pulse oximeter

A pulse oximeter is an instrument used for self-monitoring of blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), respiration rate, and pulse rate. The process of pulse oximetry is carried out in a sitting position with the person's hand kept at heart level. Index finger or middle finger is the choice for doing pulse oximetry. The pulse oximeter holds the finger (not too tightly or not loosely) for about 30–60 seconds. Wait until you have the highest stable reading of SpO2. It is possible to have inaccurate values due to the following factors:

+   i. Finger shaking
ii. Low battery
iii. Irregular waves
iv. Fluctuations in readings
vv. Frequent movement of fingers or meter
vi. Change in lying position of hand
vii. Shivering of hand
viii. Chillness or sweating in hand.
