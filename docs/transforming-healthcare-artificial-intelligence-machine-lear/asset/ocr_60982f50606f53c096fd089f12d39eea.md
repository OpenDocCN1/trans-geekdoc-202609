# 15.4 QUALITY OF SERVICE ENHANCEMENT

Quality of service (QoS) is a critical aspect of mobile network systems, encompassing various performance metrics such as latency, bandwidth, jitter, and packet loss that directly affect user experience. As mobile networks evolve to support a diverse range of applications from simple voice calls to high-definition video streaming and real-time gaming ensuring QoS has become increasingly complex [8]. ML and AI offer advanced solutions for enhancing QoS and providing dynamic, real-time adjustments to maintain optimal network performance.

# 15.4.1 Adaptive streaming technologies

One of the primary applications of AI in QoS enhancement is adaptive streaming, which adjusts video quality based on real-time network conditions and device capabilities. AI algorithms analyze data traffic, network connection, and device performance to determine the optimal streaming quality that maximizes user experience while minimizing buffering and latency. For instance, during periods of high network load, AI can preemptively lower the resolution of a video stream to reduce bandwidth consumption, ensuring a smooth playback experience without interruptions.

# 15.4.2 Latency reduction techniques

AI and ML are instrumental in reducing latency, a crucial factor for applications requiring real-time responsiveness, such as online gaming, VR, and AR. By predicting and mitigating potential delays, AI optimizes data packet routing and prioritize latency-sensitive traffic. For example, edge computing, empowered by AI, processes data closer to the end-user, significantly reducing round-trip time and improving response times. AI can preemptively identify congestion and reroute traffic to less congested paths, further minimizing delays.
