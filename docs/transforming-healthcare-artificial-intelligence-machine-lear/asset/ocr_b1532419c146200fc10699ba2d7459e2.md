# A deep learning-based diabetic retinopathy detection model

Moderate DR: The second stage of the illness is known as moderate NPDR. The blood vessels in the eye enlarge to the point where the retina cannot get enough blood at this stage, which is referred to as proliferative retinopathy. The retina may change physically; as a result, they do not carry blood as well as they did formerly.

Severe DR: Non-proliferative diabetic eye disease (NPDR), in which a significant fraction of the retina's blood vessels gets clogged, significantly reducing blood flow to the retina's tissues. In response, the retina can begin forming today to produce new blood vessels.

Proliferative DR: The most advanced stage, known as proliferative retinopathy (PDR), is distinguished by the development of new blood vessels on the retina. But frequently, these blood arteries are weak and aberrant, which can cause blood to seep into the eye, impair vision, and even cause blindness. The classes of the datasets are as follows:
