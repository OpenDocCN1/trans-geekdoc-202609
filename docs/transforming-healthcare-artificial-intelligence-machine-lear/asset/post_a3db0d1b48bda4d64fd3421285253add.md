## 10.5 CONCLUSION

SS is not only an essential component of CR, but it is also a fundamental technology that can be utilized to increase spectrum utilization efficiency and distribute resources in a way that is both productive and acceptable. This has ramifications for research as well as practice. While traditional ever-more-complex sensing environment, researchers are tackling these growing hurdles in this sector by using cutting-edge techniques like deep learning and deep reinforcement learning. In this study, we review the fundamental ideas, benefits, and drawbacks of numerous SS algorithms and provide a brief summary of recent optimization and enhancement by scholars based on well-established conventional techniques. Following that, a comprehensive synopsis of the state of DNN research and its deep learning applications is provided. The following part provides a detailed overview of the state of research and applications of many popular DNNs in deep learning for SS. The advantages of deep learning-based SS methods over conventional algorithms are also discussed in this part, including these methods' ability to handle complex data, detect probabilities with high accuracy, and be flexible. The current state of deep learning and deep reinforcement learning for cooperative SS, as well as recent advances, is then discussed. To offer research suggestions to scientists studying deep learning-based SS, the existing barriers to deep learning implementation are at least acknowledged in the discipline, and suggestions and approaches for further research are provided.

## 10.6 SCOPE OF THE FUTURE WORK

The following avenues may be pursued to learn more about the proposed work:
