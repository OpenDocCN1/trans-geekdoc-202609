**Infrastructure Limitations:** In many cases, especially in rural or underserved areas, healthcare organizations may lack the infrastructure necessary to support advanced digital technologies. High-speed internet, reliable electricity, and adequate technical support are essential for the successful deployment of digital health solutions. Without this foundational infrastructure, the benefits of digital transformation may not be fully realized (Figure 17.4).

This section highlights the key barriers healthcare organizations face during their digital transformation journey, focusing on compliance, security, operational integration, and resource management.
