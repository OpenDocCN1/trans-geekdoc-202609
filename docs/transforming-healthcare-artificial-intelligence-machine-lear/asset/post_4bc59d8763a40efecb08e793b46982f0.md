## 3.6 CONCLUSION

AI and ML have profoundly changed the diagnostics and disease treatment world. The evolution of medicine towards precision, dynamism, and personal engagement has begun. As exhibited in the previous sections of this chapter, deep learning-based methods among others, these models have been effective in detecting multiple diseases, from autism to skin cancer and even cardiovascular and ocular diseases. They have led to increased diagnostic accuracy and improved timing and preparation of treatment, thus improving patients' lives. The benefit recognised as the most incredible about AI and ML in healthcare is that the amount of data available does not limit them; they collate and learn the regularities on their own and identify aspects which may have eluded the sight of the clinicians. This ability is helpful in a situation where a timely and correct diagnosis can mean success or failure in treating complicated and ambiguous illnesses. AI models are also capable of assimilating new information, and as a result, they make adjustments to evolutions in practice and breakthroughs in medical research. Nevertheless, even if progress is evident, there are still problems. Areas such as privacy, how the model is understood, or the fact that we need more diverse data for AI to be more effective are critical issues that require more work to improve their state. Other problems include the risk of overfitting and the lack of ability of the Applications of AI & ML in diagnostics and disease treatment models to translate their training into practice, which require appropriate measures to be taken using extensive tests. There is also a need to ensure that systems are integrated within the clinic's daily workings with an awareness of the ethical and legal aspects of the systems. To summarise, it must be noted that the introduction of AI and ML technologies in the diagnostics and treatment of various diseases today is a turn for a better perspective in future medicine. These areas will not replace healthcare workers but rather help them forcefully by equipping them with means aimed at more diagnostic and therapy accuracy, as well as faster and individual approaches towards clients. This development continues in I&D in that area of science, even more breakthroughs are expected to revolutionise healthcare for the better, enhancing the quality of care and patient care.
