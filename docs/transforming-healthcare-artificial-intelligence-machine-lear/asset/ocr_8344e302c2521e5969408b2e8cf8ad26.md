# 11.4.1 Dataset

+   1. LC25000
LC25000 dataset comprises 25,000 lung images of five distinct classes with 5,000 images of each class. The five distinct classes of cancer-infected lungs include colon adenocarcinomas, benign lung tissues, lung adenocarcinomas, benign colonic tissues, and lung squamous cells. For our proposed work, we have used only 5,000 images as the proposed optimal CCT works for the minimal number of datasets. For each class, there are thousand images.

+   2. CT Scan images
CT scan consists of 364 images, out of which 126 images are non-cancerous images and the remaining are the cancerous images. The datasets have only two classes: cancerous and non-cancerous. The images in the datasets are expanded to two thousand images by incorporating data augmentation techniques. The dataset gathered with the help of experts and different patients having cancers and other lung diseases from Iran.

+   3. IQ-OTH/NCCD
The IQ-OTH/NCCD is employed to analyze the severity of the diseases. The dataset comprises 1,190 images from 110 patients. The datasets have three classes of cancer stages: benign, malignant, and non-cancerous. The dataset is enlarged to three thousand images by implementing data augmentation techniques.
