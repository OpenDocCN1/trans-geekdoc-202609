Logistic Regression, while not as complex as Random Forest or XGBoost, is frequently applied for its simplicity and interpretability, particularly in studies involving binary classifications such as malnutrition or no malnutrition. Additionally, DL techniques, particularly NN, are highlighted in studies requiring more complex predictions, such as predicting child growth behavior.

The selection of algorithms varies depending on the complexity of the problem. For simpler binary classifications, models such as Logistic Regression and Decision Trees are often sufficient. However, for more nuanced and multiclass predictions, ensemble models such as Random Forest or boosting algorithms like XGBoost are preferred for their robustness and higher accuracy.

# 4.5.3 Main predictions

Predictive models are increasing in importance as a tool for detecting children who are at risk of obesity, stunting, malnutrition, especially in the field of healthcare. A lot of research has been conducted to use advances in ML and data analytics to enable early diagnosis of nutritional deficiencies and other health-related problems. Predictive models can categorize children into risk groups by evaluating a variety of demographic, and health variables. This gives practitioners the essential information they need to make prompt and well-informed decisions. This makes it possible to implement interventions that are more successful overall, to use resources more wisely, and to enhance child health outcomes in general.
