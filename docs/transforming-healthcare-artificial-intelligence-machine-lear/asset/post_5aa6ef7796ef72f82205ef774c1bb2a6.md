## II.2 LITERATURE REVIEW

Recent advancements in AI paved several ways for the detection of cancer with its severity by incorporating ML, transformer models, and deep convolutional networks and their combinations for the precision in classification. Multi-axis vision transformer model is employed to detect the cervical cancer, which incorporates the hybrid model of convolutional networks and vision transformers for cancer detection. The model greatly reduces the number of parameters and the time period required for classification [21]. Adding additional convolutional layers in between the transformer model offers high accuracy in the classification with reduced computational complexity [21].

Deep convolutional networks outperforms the existing ML techniques in object classification tasks [1]. However the deep learning techniques rely only on local features for classification of diseases in medical image processing. For cancer detection, local attributes are not enough, but also broad features are essential in knowing the relationship between the distinct portions of the image [1]. The integration of convolutional and transformer blocks by feature fusion with the attention mechanism in transformers keeps track on attributes essential for the detection of brain cancer and enhances the performance of the proposed model.

Segmentation of images is performed by employing the benefits of convolutional networks and the swarm transformers with the encoder and the decoder blocks, as well as feature fusion techniques to integrate the features from the convolutional layers from deep learning and transformers [22]. Self-attention mechanism focuses only on the attributes with high probability for segmentation, in conjugation with the skip connections to improve the performance of the model [22]. The proposed models override the existing deep learning models and transformer models.

The modified CCT is created by using convolutional blocks [23] with image processing techniques such as contrast limited adaptive histogram equalization techniques, techniques to remove the noises, and artifacts in the input images [23]. The enhanced CCT model performs well with the minimal datasets and also reduces the time for training and detection without compromising the accuracy in detection of diabetic retinopathy.

The convolutional layers in conjunction with transformer model are incorporated in image segmentation process to find the similarity in attributes and their relationship to proper enhancement in image segmentation [24].

Lung cancer is detected by combining the encoder and decoder in vision transformer architectures and convolutional neural networks for initial attributes detection, transformers for capturing the inter-relationship among the images, with distinct loss function and the attention mechanism accurately classifies lung cancer [25]. This in turn offers better performance for the real-time datasets by incorporating processing techniques [26]. Amalgamate CNN with vision transformers segments the thyroid node by gathering more relevant features and fusion techniques and offers expected performance than the existing models. Oral cancer can be detected by comparing convolution neural networks and transformer models [27]. The DenseNet architecture of CNN works better than transformer models, but shot learning techniques outperform both convolutional modules and transformer-based models.

Classification of diseased image by incorporating convolutional layers for feature extraction by depthwise separable convolution [28] and a module to draw out the relationship between the pixels to fine grained attributes and transformer model for classification by attaining notable accuracy in classification [29]. A study was conducted to compare the performance of convolutional neural networks and vision transformer models to find the one which best suits for detecting lung cancer. The convolutional neural network models outperform the transformer models from the experimental study [30]. Existing hybrid models of CNN with transformers fail to carry forward the local attributes to the end due to patch embedding. Patch embedding is reframed to capture attributes along its spatial information. Attention mechanism is also reframed in attention pooling to obtain the remarkable accuracy [31] in medical dataset classification. Hybridization of transformers and CNN models is presented in the transformation has been made in the convolutional layers, decoder blocks, and attention blocks to retain the attributes without any losses so to acquire the expected precision in detection of diseases from CT scan [32]. Deep learning techniques and their integration with the transformer techniques, especially the vision transformer techniques, outperform the existing techniques in breast cancer detection [33].
