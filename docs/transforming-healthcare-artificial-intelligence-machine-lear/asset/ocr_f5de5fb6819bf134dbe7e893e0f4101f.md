# 14.4.7.1 Key features of IoT and ThinkSpeak

**Remote Data Collection:** Sensors continuously gather data like temperature, heart rate, and blood pressure for real-time monitoring of health or environmental conditions.

**Data Processing and Storage:** Data is processed by embedded systems and sent to the ThinkSpeak cloud platform for storage and display, allowing access to historical data for tracking changes and informed decision-making.

**Consultation and Accessibility:** A cloud link with patient data is shared with healthcare professionals for easy access, ensuring timely interventions and personalized care.

**Applications of IoT:** IoT is used for patient and environmental monitoring, enabling continuous health tracking and providing insights for managing environmental factors like air quality and temperature.

In summary, ThinkSpeak plays a vital role in IoT by efficiently storing, processing, and displaying data, supporting better patient care.

# 14.4.8 Support vector machine classifier

Figure 14.10 illustrates the hyperplane in SVM classification. The goal of SVM is to find a hyperplane in an N-dimensional space that separates different classes of data points.

# 14.4.8.1 Key concepts

**Hyperplane Definition:** A hyperplane is a flat subspace of one dimension less than its ambient space. In 2D, it's a line; in 3D, it's a plane.

**Classification Objective:** SVM seeks to find the optimal hyperplane that maximizes the margin between data points from different classes. Maximizing this margin improves generalization to unseen data, reducing classification errors.
