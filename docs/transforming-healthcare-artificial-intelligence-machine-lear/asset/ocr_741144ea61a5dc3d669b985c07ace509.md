# 3.1 INTRODUCTION

A precise diagnosis is essential since it determines the treatment plan.
Strategies for treating diseases are used to control or cure them once
they are diagnosed. Pharmacological therapies, such as drugs, and non-
pharmacological methods, such as lifestyle modifications, physical therapy,
or surgery, can be used as treatments. The treatment decision is influenced
by the wellness of the individual, the kind and stage of illness, and the
therapy goals as a whole. There is a continual improvement in both diagno-
s and therapy due to modern technology and medical research. Significant
ancements in discipline can be seen in innovations in both medi-
cine, which customised are based on each patient's unique genetic profile,
an advanced imaging methods, which offer more precise images of the
body's internal components. Ultimately, enhancing patient outcomes, low-
ering disease burden, and promoting public health on the synergy
between accurate diagnosis and efficient treatment.
