# 17.4.3 The role of IoT in connected health via 5G networks

+   - Internet of Medical Things (IoMT): 5G plays a crucial role in the development and deployment of the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT). Connected health devices, including wearable sensors, implantable devices, and smart home medical equipment, all benefit from 5G's capacity to handle large volumes of data while maintaining low latency and high reliability [6,7].

+   - Interoperability: 5G networks facilitate better integration and interoperability among various healthcare devices. Seamless connectivity enables healthcare systems to aggregate data from multiple sources—such as heart monitors, glucose sensors, and fitness trackers—providing a comprehensive view of a patient's health.

+   - Predictive Health Monitoring: IoT devices, powered by 5G, can collect and analyze patient data continuously. When combined with AI and big data analytics, this ecosystem can predict potential health issues, providing early warnings to patients and healthcare providers for timely interventions.
