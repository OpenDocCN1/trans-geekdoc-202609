The proposed model is designed to detect body poses and train on these poses for accurate recognition. Utilizing depthwise separable convolutions as seen in MobileNet, the model efficiently handles pose detection. This sequence of layers processes images through a variety of convolutions and normalizations in the detection of body poses ultimately classifying the key points with a final dense layer.

Figure 13.3 represents the PoseNet model used for yoga pose estimation. The neural network begins with a series of layers that progressively decrease in size for initial extraction of features from the input. As the layers narrow towards the output, these features are further refined to achieve accurate pose estimation even in challenging conditions. The interactive system provides the user with a systematic way of learning six fundamental poses—Mountain, Tree, Down Dog, Warrior I, and Chair.
