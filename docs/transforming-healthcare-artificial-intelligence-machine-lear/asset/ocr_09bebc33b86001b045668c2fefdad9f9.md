# 15.3.5 Scalability and future networks

As mobile networks evolve toward 5G and beyond, the scalability of AI-driven traffic management and load balancing becomes more critical. The increased density of 5G networks, coupled with the higher data rates and lower latency requirements, demands more sophisticated traffic management solutions. AI and ML provide the scalability needed to handle these complexities, enabling real-time optimization and management of highly dynamic network environments.

15.3.5.1.1 Scenario: major sports event

During a major sports event, such as the final match of a popular football tournament, the demand for mobile network resources spikes dramatically. Thousands of spectators at the stadium and millions of viewers streaming the event online create a significant load on the network. Traditional load balancing methods may struggle to handle such dense and intense traffic, leading to congestion, buffering, and a poor user experience.
