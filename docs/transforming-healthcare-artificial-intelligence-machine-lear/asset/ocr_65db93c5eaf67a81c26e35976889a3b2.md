# 13.4.3 About Yoga Mode

Figure 13.8 depicts the About Yoga interface, which provides users with an introductory overview of yoga, covering its origins, historical significance, and its physical, mental, and emotional benefits. This mode also offers insights into the fundamental principles of yoga, such as balance, strength, and mindfulness. Additionally, it includes detailed explanations of the six essential poses—Mountain, Tree, Downward Dog, Warrior I, Warrior II, and Chair—highlighting their benefits and proper execution techniques to help users better understand the practice and its holistic impact.
