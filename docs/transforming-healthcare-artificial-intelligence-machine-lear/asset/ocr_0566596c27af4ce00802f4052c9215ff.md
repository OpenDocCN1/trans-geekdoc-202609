# Design and implementation of personal protective equipment detection system using YOLOv9 for industrial environments

Praveen Kumar Devulapalli, Alekya Kasoju, Sai Priya Gaddam, and Sai Teja Alli

# 9.1 INTRODUCTION

The use of personal protective equipment (PPE) has been increasingly important in recent years as a means of preserving compliance and safety in a variety of industries, including manufacturing, healthcare, and construction. Making sure people are appropriately outfitted with PPE, such as masks, gloves, vests, and helmets, greatly lowers their chance of becoming hurt or exposed to dangerous situations. Conventional manual PPE monitoring and inspection techniques are time-consuming, prone to human error, and impractical for large-scale implementations. Automated systems that use of cutting-edge computer vision (CV) techniques have become a viable answer to these problems [1]. The YOLO (You Only Once) model has become well-known among them due to its rapid and highly accurate real-time object identification capabilities. YOLOv9, the most recent version, includes notable enhancements. YOLOv9 introduces a more refined architecture, includes improved feature extraction, and increased computational efficiency, making it particularly suited for real-time applications.nThis paper explores the development and implementation of a PPE detection system using YOLOv9, aiming to achieve robust and reliable recognition of various PPE types in diverse environments. The system is designed to operate in real time, providing instant feedback and ensuring that PPE compliance is maintained consistently and effectively. By integrating YOLOv9's advanced detection capabilities, the proposed solution not only enhances safety protocols but also streamlines the monitoring process, contributing to a safer and more efficient working environment.
