
Mobile-Health (m-Health) is the term that utilizes the application of mobiles and telecommunication services of wired/wireless for providing healthcare services in remote places. The basic principle behind healthcare is 'Store-and-Forward' mechanism, which means the symptoms of the patients have been observed with suitable elements; the data have been stored first, packed with data encryption for protection purpose, and finally forwarded to the destination where the physician or healthcare professionals are accessible. Then, further medication in terms of medicines or treatment is to be suggested by them to the patients. There are various means of treatment, which are followed. Some of them are:

- i. Tele-triage:
It involves screening patients in remote places to assess the patient's health condition and the care required.
- ii. Teleconsultation:
It provides interaction on one-to-one basis between the patient and other healthcare professionals.
- iii. Tele-diagnosis:
In the case of tele-diagnosis, symptoms and conditions are observed by patients and the same have been transmitted. After examining the information, it is directed to consult with suitable experts for further follow-up and treatment.
- iv. Tele-monitoring:
In the case of people who need continuous monitoring of their health conditions, the process involves acquiring physiological parameters and forwarding them to physician or healthcare professionals for further diagnosis and recovery. This overall monitoring is said to be tele-monitoring.
Tele-monitoring is otherwise called as remote line patient monitoring, which consists of data transmission, data registration, data processing of human body parameters, and further medical treatment or therapeutic to be suggested via digital communication mode.
Patients' physiological parameters have been acquired by using the necessary integrated sensors up wired or wireless devices, wearable or implantable sensors. This allows for continuous monitoring of patients' health conditions in the case of chronic diseases based on the needs of patients.

## 2.3.1 Societal benefits

The process of telehealth can be achieved by integrating various sensors, which are wired of wireless/implantable type. It is possible to provide continuous monitoring and support for the management of chronic diseases like breast cancer/blood cancer/lung cancer, stroke, cardiovascular attack, etc., as per the requirement of the patients. When it is embedded with AI algorithms, it is easy for disease surveillance and also early diagnosis. Data acquisition and retrieval with the utilization of GPS tracking facilities will be helpful for the physicians and medical/health professionals to offer healthcare in terms of medicinal and psychological support. It is very clear that telemedicine is a part of telehealth and will not be treated as a tool. Various sensors arranged as the assembly; suitable AI algorithms, proper internet connection, and GPS tracking or location finder setup are necessary for utilizing the benefits of telemedicine or telehealth [16–18].

For the purpose of tele-healthcare, various virtual software platforms such as synchronous and asynchronous methods are preferred. In the case of synchronous method of platform, patients can use mobile phones with audio and video signals, video conferencing, etc., whereas in the case of asynchronous method, they can use e-mail transactions, web portal, virtual consultation, etc.

## 2.4 TELE-HEALTHCARE: OBSERVATION AND ANALYSIS

When providing tele-healthcare for patients from remote place, two stages are considered:

- Clinical Services: Measurement of body temperature, body blood pressure, blood oxygen saturation (SpO2), and ECG waveform.
- Non-Clinical Services: Treatment for mental disorders like stress, anxiety, insomnia, dementia, and schizophrenia.

### 2.4.1 Clinical services

Patients who suffer from cough, cold, Dizziness (Giddiness), or fainting need continuous monitoring with an integrated assembly of sensors such as digital thermometer, digital blood pressure apparatus, pulse oxymeter, and ECG setup with electrodes and cable wires. ### 2.4.1.1 Measurement of body temperature

Measurement of body temperature is essential since it is an indicator of knowing our body’s status and serves as a warning symptom if something is abnormal with our body. It is well known that normal body temperature is 98.6°F or 37°C, though slight variations based on various factors such as gender, age, obesity, and body where it is measured, time, outside environment (rainy, sunny), working environment, and infection/illness state. For use of usage in rural side people, it is advised to use digital thermometer to read the body temperature periodically without any false reading.
