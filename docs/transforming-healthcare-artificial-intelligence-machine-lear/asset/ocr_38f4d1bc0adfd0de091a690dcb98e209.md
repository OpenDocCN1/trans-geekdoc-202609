# 17.2.2 Benefits of adopting digital technologies in healthcare

Adopting digital technologies in healthcare improves to help all the stakeholders of the system. Patients get timely and personalized treatment. Real-time data accessing and sharing become possible and easy. These technologies also reduce operational costs, streamline workflows, and provide better collaboration between healthcare providers and patients.
