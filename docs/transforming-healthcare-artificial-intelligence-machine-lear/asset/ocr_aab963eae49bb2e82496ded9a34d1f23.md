From the above table, as per the physician's advice, it is observed that the blood pressure is 132/60, it is considered as Hypertension (Stage 1) since the top number lies in the range (130–139) mmHg.

High blood pressure cannot be identified by the symptoms, hence it is said to be asymptomatic one. Since it does not cause symptoms or indications, it will further lead to serious health issues as stroke or cardiac arrest. Due to this reason, high blood pressure is also mentioned as a silent killer. There will be some side effects pointed by the physicians, which are given below:

+   1. Severe headache
2. Dizziness or giddiness
3. Paleness in face
4. Chest pain
5. Nose bleeding
6. Breathing trouble
7. Irregular heartbeat
8. Blood in urine.

If any of the above symptoms are observed by the patient at their home, they are further advised to have interaction or meeting with a physician for further testing or treatment. They provided risk factors and common causes of high blood pressure for prevention. They are:

+   1. Obesity
2. Diabetic history
3. Sedentary lifestyle
4. Poor dieting
5. Smoking
6. Alcohol drinking
7. Old ageing
8. Family history of high BP.

Similar to high blood pressure, it also points out the causes, symptoms, and advice for low blood pressure. When the blood pressure is 90/60, it is considered as low blood pressure. It can also affect the body. Some of the common symptoms observed from the patients are:

+   1. Body weakness
2. Dizziness/giddiness
3. Blurred vision
4. Nausea
5. Tiredness
6. Pale and sweating in skin
