# Application of AI for tele-healthcare system 35

In addition to telemedicine, AI can be integrated with it, thereby expanding its features and providing enormous possibilities for giving solution to their medical requirements. This integration can assist physicians to provide good medical services and recommendations to patients/people who seek medical advice and treatment for the problems [5].

Due to the combination of telemedicine and AI, it is possible to obtain improved healthcare services and also to enhance the experience faced by patients/people. It will further speed up the testing and diagnosis of the patient's diseases/problems, make effective diagnosis, and reduce physical visits to hospitals by patients/people. Hence, people prefer integrated telemedicine with AI for their physical and mental problems whenever for longer time. It is also possible to get medical advice and follow-up actions from expert doctors with whom it is tough to get appointment to person.

With the integrated AI and telemedicine along with the reliable communication and effective internet connections across various components of healthcare system, supported by remote places, the expert physicians can interact with patients/people who need medical assistance for their health problems and provide the possible medical assistance and further follow-up in terms of medicines, physical exercises, etc. The record of the patients' details will be kept secure for further reference and will be accessible from any place of hospitals or other medical experts, if required.

As the record of 'World Health Organization (WHO)', the telemedicine is said to be 'the delivery of healthcare services, where distance is a primary factor'. It can be carried out by medical experts use two-way communication for the exchange of information such as symptoms about their physical and mental health conditions for the disease diagnosis, recommendations for further treatment and suggestions for prevention of further diseases, and research and evaluation.

For the purpose of providing telemedicine, recent telecommunication technologies have been used in order to offer clinical services in remote places such as disease based on symptoms mentioned by people and medical treatment suggested by physicians. Communication between patients and physicians can be done by means of audio or video calls by using mobile phones itself [6–12].
