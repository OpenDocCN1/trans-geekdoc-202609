# Transforming Healthcare

As per survey made by the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR), 1 among 7 people suffers from a mental health disorder. During 2017, there are about more than 197 million people who have experienced mental health problems. Most of the people faced depression as a mental health issue. As per World Health Organization (WHO) report, there were many men committing suicide than due to sadness.

Mental health problems can have more significance in one's own personal life, which further affects their personal relationship, employment, status, etc.

Some of the common mental health problems experienced by many people are as follows:

+   1. **Depression**: People feel stressed or frequently, do not show interest in their activities, feel lonely or helpless, become irritated by their actions, and get angry and frustrated.
2. **Anxiety**: People feel nervous and tension, are scared about their actions, feel that they are in dangerous situation, and are not feeling relaxed.
3. **Insomnia**: Symptoms of insomnia include difficulty in getting enough quality sleep and not feeling well rested.
4. **Dementia**: Symptoms of dementia include forgetfulness and limited thinking skills due to which person feels impaired.
5. **Schizophrenia**: It is a type of mental disorder that affects a person's ability to feel, think, and act clearly. People often experience false belief, as well as difficulties with concentration and memory.
