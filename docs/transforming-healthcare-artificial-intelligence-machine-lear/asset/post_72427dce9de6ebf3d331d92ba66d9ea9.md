## 2.1 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the artificial mimicking of human intelligence by simulation of various processes owned by human beings but with the help of machines (computers). To mention human processes, the learning, reasoning, and auto-correction are some of the major processes. If it needs to carry out more number of processes that are difficult for human reasoning, it is suggested to go with AI in order to make them happen.

## 2.2 TELEMEDICINE

The purpose of telemedicine is to provide access to clinical services in remote places in real time through two-way communication between the patient, seeker, and expert doctors. In healthcare systems, telemedicine is considered as an innovation and it is helpful for people who need medical assistance where it is difficult to visit hospitals. Via telemedicine, anyone can access healthcare services digitally and get diagnosis, treatment, and recommendations for physical and mental problems [2–4].

In addition to telemedicine, AI can be integrated with it, thereby expanding its features and providing enormous possibilities for giving solutions to medical requirements. Due to the combination of telemedicine and AI, it is possible to obtain improved healthcare services and also to enhance the experience faced by people. This will further speed up the testing and diagnosis of diseases/problems, reduce physical visits to hospitals. Hence, people prefer integrated telemedicine with AI for their physical and mental problems. It is also possible to get medical advice and follow-up actions from expert doctors with whom it is tough to get an appointment.

With the integrated AI and telemedicine along with reliable communication and effective internet connections across various components of healthcare system, supported by remote places, the expert physicians can interact with people who need medical assistance for their health problems and provide the possible medical assistance and further follow-up in terms of medicines, physical exercises, etc. [5].
