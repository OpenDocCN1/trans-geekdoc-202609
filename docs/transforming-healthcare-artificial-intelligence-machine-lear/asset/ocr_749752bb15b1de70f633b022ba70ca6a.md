# 14.5.1.1 Key features of the hardware design

Vital Signs Monitoring: The system includes sensors for measuring.
Blood Pressure: Systolic and diastolic blood pressure.

Heart Rate: Beats per minute, offering insights into cardiovascular health.

Pulse Oximeter: Measures SpO$_2$ to monitor oxygen levels in the bloodstream.

Temperature Sensor: Tracks body temperature, helping identify potential infections or conditions.

Data Accuracy and Reliability: The system achieves about 85% accuracy in correlating actual health data with observed data, especially for conditions like hypertension and lung diseases, ensuring effective diagnosis and treatment planning.

Accessibility of Patient Data: Healthcare professionals can remotely monitor patient data, facilitating informed decisions without being physically present. This is especially useful for managing chronic illnesses or routine check-ups.

Support during Epidemics or Crises: The system aids in rapid data collection and analysis, helping healthcare providers track trends, allocate resources, and respond quickly during crises or epidemics.
