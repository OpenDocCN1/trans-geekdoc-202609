# Leveraging ML & AI for advanced mobile network systems

# 15.9 CASE STUDIES AND REAL-WORLD APPLICATIONS

Case studies and real-world applications showcase the practical implementation of ML and AI in mobile network systems, highlighting their transformative effects on network performance, user experiences, and operational efficiency [10]. These examples demonstrate how AI-driven solutions address specific challenges and deliver tangible benefits to mobile network operators and users alike.

# 15.9.1 Case study I: AI-powered network optimization

In a case study conducted by a leading mobile network operator, AI-driven network optimization solutions were deployed to improve network performance and user experiences. By investigating large amounts of data, AI algorithms identified areas of congestion, predicted traffic patterns, and optimized resource allocation in real time. As a result, the operator achieved significant improvements in network capacity, throughput, and latency, leading to higher customer satisfaction and retention. Moreover, AI-powered predictive maintenance reduced equipment downtime and maintenance costs, further enhancing operational efficiency.

# 15.9.2 Case study 2: energy efficiency optimization

A telecommunications company implemented AI-driven energy management solutions to optimize energy consumption across its network infrastructure. By analyzing data from base stations, edge servers, and network components, AI algorithms dynamically adjusted operational parameters to minimize waste and reduce carbon footprint. As a result, the company achieved significant energy savings, lowered operational costs, and contributed to environmental sustainability [11]. Moreover, AI-enabled predictive maintenance systems identified energy inefficiencies and equipment failures proactively, further enhancing energy efficiency and network reliability.
