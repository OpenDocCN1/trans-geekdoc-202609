# 15.2.3 Mobile edge computing

+   - Scenario: A telecom operator deploys edge computing to reduce latency and improve service quality for applications such as augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR).
- Application: AI models dynamically manage edge resources based on workload characteristics and user proximity. This ensures that latency-sensitive applications receive the necessary resources for optimal performance. For example, during a VR gaming session, the network can allocate more processing power and bandwidth to the edge servers closest to the user, reducing latency and enhancing the gaming experience.

# 15.2.3.3 Energy efficiency

+   - Scenario: A telecom operator aims to reduce energy consumption and operational costs.
- Application: AI algorithms dynamically adjust the power consumption of network components based on real-time traffic load and environmental conditions. For instance, during periods of low usage, the network can reduce power to certain components or put them into sleep mode, resulting in significant energy savings.

# 15.3 TRAFFIC MANAGEMENT AND LOAD BALANCING

Effective traffic management and load balancing are critical to ensuring optimal performance and user experience in mobile network systems. As traffic surges due to the widespread use of smartphones, Internet of Things (IoT) devices, and multimedia services, conventional traffic management methods are becoming less effective. ML and AI offer sophisticated solutions to dynamically manage network traffic and balance loads, thereby enhancing network efficiency and reliability.
