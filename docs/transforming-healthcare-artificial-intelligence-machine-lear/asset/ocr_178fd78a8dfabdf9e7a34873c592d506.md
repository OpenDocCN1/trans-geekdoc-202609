# Building network slice for healthcare equipment over 5G services

# 8.2.2 Deep Learning for Intelligent and Automated Network Slicing in 5G Open RAN (ORAN)

Yeh et al. (2024) explore the integration of AI and deep learning in the text of 5G Open RAN (ORAN) deployment. The paper emphasizes the role of AI in optimizing network slicing, a critical technology that allows multiple virtual networks to operate on a shared physical infrastructure. The integration of AI-driven techniques into the RAN Intelligent Controller (RIC) enhances the efficiency and quality of network slices.

# 8.2.3 Open AI Cellular (OAIC): An Open Source 5G O-RAN Testbed for Design and Testing of AI-Based RAN Management Algorithms

The work by Upadhyya et al. (2023) introduces the Open AI Cellular (OAIC) testbed, an open-source platform designed for the development and testing of AI-based RAN management algorithms. This testbed is compliant with 3GPP standards and supports low-latency use cases, making it an ideal platform for experimenting with new algorithms for managing healthcare networks. The paper discusses the challenges and opportunities associated with AI integration in RAN management.

# 8.2.4 iSlicer: Enabling Flexible and Automated Orchestration of 5G End-to-end Network Slices

Catalan-Cid et al. (2023) presents iSlicer, a flexible solution for the orchestration of 5G standalone (SA) network slices. The paper emphasizes the importance of automation in managing network slices, particularly in multi-tenancy and multi-service environments.

# 8.2.5 Orchestration Architecture for Automatic Deployment of 5G Services from Mobile Edge Computing Infrastructure

Chirivilla-Perez et al. (2018) presents an orchestration architecture that will allow a business to provide end-to-end service in 90 minutes. It showcases automation across the infrastructure layers and demonstrates how it can be directly or indirectly automated, working with environments composed of bare metal edge scenarios.
