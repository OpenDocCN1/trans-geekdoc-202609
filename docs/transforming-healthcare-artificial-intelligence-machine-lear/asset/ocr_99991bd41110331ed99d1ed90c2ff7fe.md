# 17.6 OTHER ENABLING TECHNOLOGIES

# 17.6.1 Blockchain for data integrity and security in healthcare

+   * Immutable Records: Blockchain technology ensures integrity in healthcare by creating immutable, tamper-proof records of patient data [10,11]. Every transaction or update in the healthcare system is securely recorded in a decentralized ledger, providing a transparent and auditable.

+   * Data Security and Privacy: With healthcare data being highly sensitive, blockchain can secure patient records from unauthorized access or breaches. By decentralizing data storage and using encryption protocols, blockchain minimizes the risk of single points of failure found in centralized systems, thus enhancing data privacy and protection.

+   * Interoperability and Data Sharing: Blockchain also facilitates secure data exchange among different healthcare providers, allowing interoperability without compromising security. This is particularly beneficial in environments where patient data needs to be shared across hospitals, labs, and specialists for coordinated care.

# 17.6.2 Virtual reality and augmented reality in medical training and surgery

+   * Medical Training: Virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) technologies are transforming medical education by offering immersive simulations where students and professionals can practice surgical techniques or medical procedures in a controlled, risk-free environment. VR provides a lifelike experience, allowing learners to visualize the human body in 3D, while AR overlays digital information on real-world settings, enabling more dynamic learning [12].
