# 7.2 RELATED WORKS

As a chronic illness, DR must be identified and treated as soon as possible. For detecting drug resistance early, several researchers and medical professionals have developed many methodologies. As a result, several studies utilizing ML and DL algorithms have been presented in this report.
Microaneurysm recognition was concentrated by Meindert Niemeyer et al. [4], which is a basic test for mechanical screening. Here, the image is resized with a specific width and standardized by subtracting a gauge of the base and shifting to the center, hence utilizing a major part. After standardization, the application identification is performed using model-based grouping technique. To investigate the proposal, additional analysis was performed to examine the ability of various frameworks ending up with picture-level DR picture identification in the presence of microaneurysms through combination of sores.
