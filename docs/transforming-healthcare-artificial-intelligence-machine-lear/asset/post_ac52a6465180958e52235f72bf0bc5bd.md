## Building network slice for healthcare equipment over 5G

### 8.1 INTRODUCTION
In recent years, the healthcare industry has been experiencing a digital revolution, with the advent of 5G, the fifth generation of communication, stands out as a game-changer. The integration of 5G into healthcare systems presents a unique opportunity to enhance patient care. By leveraging advanced network slicing techniques, this system is designed to meet the diverse requirements of medical devices.

### 8.2 LITERATURE REVIEW

#### 8.2.1 Intelligent resource orchestration in 5G edge
One of the foundational works in this domain is the study by Moreno-Vozmediano et al. (2024), which underscores the increasing importance of edge infrastructures. The authors introduce an integer programming model to optimize virtual machine (VM) placement, considering server usage, load balancing, and latency reduction.

#### 8.2.2 Deep Learning for Intelligent and Automated Slicing in 5G Open RAN (ORAN)
Yeh et al. (2024) explore the integration of AI and deep learning in 5G Open RAN (ORAN) deployment. The paper emphasizes the role of network slicing, a critical technology that allows multiple virtual networks to operate on a shared physical infrastructure. The integration of AI-driven techniques into the RAN Intelligent Controller (RIC) enhances quality of service.

#### 8.2.3 Open AI Cellular (OAIC): An Open Source 5G O-RAN Testbed for Design and Testing of AI-Based RAN Management
The work by Upadhyya et al. (2023) introduces the Open AI Cellular (OAIC) testbed, an open-source platform for the development and testing of AI-based RAN management. This testbed is compliant with 3GPP standards and supports low-latency use cases, making it an ideal platform for experimenting with new algorithms for healthcare networks. The paper discusses the challenges and opportunities associated with AI integration in RAN management.

#### 8.2.4 iSlicer: Enabling Flexible and Automated Orchestration of 5G End-to-end Network Slices
Catalan-Cid et al. (2023) presents iSlicer, a flexible solution for the orchestration of 5G standalone (SA) network slices. The paper emphasizes the importance of automation in managing network slices, particularly in multi-tenancy and multi-service environments.

#### 8.2.5 Orchestration Architecture for Automatic Deployment of 5G Services from Mobile Edge Computing Infrastructure
Chirivilla-Perez et al. (2018) presents an orchestration architecture that will allow a business to provide end-to-end service in 90 minutes. It showcases automation across the infrastructure layers and demonstrates how it can be directly or indirectly automated, working with environments composed of bare metal edge scenarios.
