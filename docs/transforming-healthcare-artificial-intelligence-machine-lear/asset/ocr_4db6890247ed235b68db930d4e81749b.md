Existing CNN models lag in capturing global contexts of images [15]. Cervical cancer detection, which integrates vision transformer with attention mechanism, extracts features with notable accuracy in classification when compared with the deep learning models [17]. The attention mechanism allows the system to focus only on the desired attributes and skip the noise by increasing the model's ability in classification of cervical cancer, the frequent cancer detected in women [17]. The spatial and channel attention mechanisms in integration with the vision transformer techniques decoder block by extracting the desired attributes and depending long-term relationship between the images [18].

Transformer-based models not only detect and classify the cases but also analyze the severity of diseases. A pre-training transformer with the symmetric mask techniques is employed for severity analysis [19].

The combined hybrid model use of the benefits of convolutional neural networks, and vision transformers are proposed to detect attributes required for the classification of breast cancer. The Meta-heuristic algorithm is employed to allow only the features vital for classification and enhance the performance of the model by reducing computational complexity without compromising the accuracy in classification of cancer. On analyzing both vision transformers and convolutional neural networks for breast cancer detection, convolutional neural networks with especially efficient architecture surpass the performance of vision transformers in cancer detection. Swim transformers offer higher performance than other transformers, but not more than efficient architecture [20].
