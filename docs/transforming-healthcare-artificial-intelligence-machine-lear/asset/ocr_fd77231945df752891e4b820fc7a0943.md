# ## 11.4.1 Augmentation techniques

Distinct augmentation techniques, including horizontal and vertical flip, clockwise and anticlockwise rotation, scaling, cropping at random, color contrast, and color jittering, are used to improve the classification accuracy of the proposed model.

# ## 11.4.2 Architecture of DBCT

The proposed DBCT architecture employs residual convolutional blocks proceeded by dense block layers and dimensionality reduction techniques. The image is reshaped to fed into the transformer blocks.

The architecture of the DBCT involves a combination of convolutional and transformer components, DBCT, as the name implies, is the composition of convolutional layers and transformer layers. It enjoys the benefits of both architectures.

Convolutional blocks comprise convolutional layers with the predefined kernel size to draw out local attributes from the input image. CCT has the ability to learn from the minimal datasets in short time without compromising the performance in comparison with convolutional networks and transformer blocks.
