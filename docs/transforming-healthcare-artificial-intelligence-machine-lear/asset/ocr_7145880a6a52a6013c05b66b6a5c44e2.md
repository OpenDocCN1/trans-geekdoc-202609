# 1.6 CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK

CNN and GRU were utilized to create a simple, resource-efficient hybrid model that diagnoses lung diseases from sound waves. The method works well in experiments. The astonishing results have two key factors. Deconstructing the signal using DCT reveals unique and essential MFCC properties that distinguish lung sounds. Second, hybrid deep-learning affects results. CNNs find important input patterns, whereas GRUs use time-dependent information. The proposed method identified ICBH (2017) dataset respiratory sounds using MFCC features. The best ML and DL model by Accuracy, Precision, Recall, and F1-Score is the DL model with 97% lung disease classification accuracy and 93% lung sound classification accuracy. The training enhanced lung disease and sound classification over individual classification. This study may help the doctor diagnose and urgently treat lung problems.
