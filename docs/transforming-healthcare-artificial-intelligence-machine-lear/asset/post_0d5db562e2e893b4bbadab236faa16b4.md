# Transforming Healthcare

This book comprehensively discusses the latest trends and developments in healthcare applications, including artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, machine learning algorithms, deep learning algorithms, and 5G technology. It covers important topics such as 5G-based smart healthcare network security, as well as data management and security.

Features:

- Covers key challenges and considerations in healthcare applications and 5G technology, including safety, reliability, and cost-effectiveness.
- Discusses the latest trends and developments in healthcare applications including artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, and machine learning algorithms.
- Provides an in-depth analysis of the technical aspects of 5G technology, healthcare automation, and the social and economic implications of the latest technologies.
- Bridges the gap between artificial intelligence, machine learning, and deep learning with advanced communication technologies used in healthcare applications.
- Highlights the latest design and problem-solving skills of engineering with health sciences to advance healthcare treatment using 5G technology.

It is primarily written for senior undergraduates, graduate students, and academic researchers in the fields of electrical engineering, electrical and communications engineering, computer science and engineering, and bio-medical engineering.

# Preface

5G will widen the mobile ecosystem by offering faster speeds, more dependability, and essentially zero latency. Across all industries, 5G will have an influence, bringing about digitalized logistics, precise agriculture, safer transportation, and remote healthcare, among other things. In particular, 5G will enable remote patient monitoring at scale when compared to other connectivity solutions through its greater reliability and service security.

This book aims to provide essential understanding about the many applications of 5G technology in the healthcare domain. This primary goal of this book is to demonstrate how the production and upkeep of 5G technologies contribute to trash production and the consumption of significant resources that have negative environmental effects. In the upcoming years, we anticipate that digital transformation will affect every face of the healthcare ecosystem, including patient experiences, clinical and operational systems, and the qualifications and work culture of healthcare professionals.

This book also covers key challenges and considerations in healthcare applications and 5G technology, such as safety, reliability, and cost-effectiveness. It discusses the latest trends and future developments in healthcare applications, including artificial intelligence, Internet of Things (IoT), machine learning algorithms, deep learning algorithms, and 5G technology.

This book is designed for students, researchers, and engineers who are interested in 5G and healthcare technology. It can serve as a comprehensive guide for those who are new in the field or a reference for those who want to deepen their knowledge. This book provides an in-depth analysis of the technical aspects of 5G technology and healthcare automation while also examining the broader social and economic implications of the latest technologies.

We hope this book will be valuable resource for anyone who wants to understand the current state and future direction of 5G technology in healthcare automation.
