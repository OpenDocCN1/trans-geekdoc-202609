These matrices converge with periodic consistency loss for randomly audio samples. This approach outperformed baseline techniques by 7.67% on the ICBIH 2017 respiratory sound categorization benchmark. It beats self-supervised methods. A novel DL method for self-supervised quasiperiodic respiratory sound copatrickening is presented. The method solves typical classification problems.
