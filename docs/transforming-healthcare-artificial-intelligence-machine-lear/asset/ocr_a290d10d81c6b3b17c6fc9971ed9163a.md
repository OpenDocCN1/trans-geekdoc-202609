# 2.5 CONCLUSION

With the effective implementation of tele-healthcare system, is observed that they will be possibly providing healthcare services under clinical factors (physical health) and non-clinical factors (mental health) successfully. Because of AI, effective analysis and decision-making can be possibly made. It is essential to assure the internet connection without breaking its connectivity at both patient's side (remote place) and physicians' side. Another important thing is awareness from the patients' side to understand the procedure to acquire data from sensor assembly and send the files without any data loss or corruption. A camp is to be arranged to provide awareness about the tele-healthcare system and its purpose, utilization, and benefits.
