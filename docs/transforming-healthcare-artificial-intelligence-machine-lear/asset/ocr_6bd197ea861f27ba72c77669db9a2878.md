The work aims to develop a real-time human gesture recognition system that tracks and recognizes body motions via Mediapipe and OpenCV, then forwards the motions to Arduino for further processing. This demonstrates the integration of computer vision and hardware control based on human hands for gesture recognition. By applying processing techniques to recognize and assess human landmarks, the camera is able to take pictures and pinpoint the position of people inside a specific region of robotic hand landmarks. The digital representations of the final states of the human hand are delivered to an Arduino board via serial communication. Using the MATLAB code interface, we created a single 3D model of a robotic hand and simulated it in a V-REP simulation environment. The efficiency of the results allows for a comparison with the real-time outcomes.
