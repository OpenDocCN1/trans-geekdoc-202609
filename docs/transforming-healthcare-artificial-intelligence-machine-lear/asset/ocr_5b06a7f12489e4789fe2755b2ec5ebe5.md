# 16.6.3 AI and ML in diagnostics and personalized medicine

Indeed, the connection of AI and ML with 5G technology in the health sector has completely revolutionized diagnostics and personalized medicine. The high speed of connectivity and ultra-low latency facilitate the quick collection and processing of enormous patient-related data, allowing AI and ML algorithms to offer precise, individualized healthcare solutions. Enhanced diagnostics benefit significantly; both AI and ML models utilize large datasets of medical imaging, patient history, and genetic information for pattern recognition and anomaly detection. With 5G, high-resolution images—MRI, CT scans, X-rays—can be transmitted in real time to remote diagnostic AI systems, which can quickly scan the images to help experts identify conditions like tumors, broken bones, or infections with greater precision. The low latency specifications of 5G ensure that results from diagnostics are nearly instantaneous, speeding up decision-making in critical environments [4].

Personalized treatment plans are made possible through AI and ML by leveraging genomic data, lifestyle factors, and the medical history of the patient. Healthcare providers can continuously monitor patient data reactions in real time on 5G-enabled networks and adjust treatment protocols accordingly. In oncology, for instance, AI can evaluate feedback, recommend dosage adjustments or alternative treatments to the physician [2]. Real-time predictive analytics are how a patient's tumor is responding to therapy, and based on real-time feedback also enhanced with 5G and the IoMT. Wearable and implantable devices can transmit patient data to AI without disrupting its bliss, enabling ML algorithms to recognize early signs of ill health, such as irregular heartbeats or changes in blood sugar levels. This allows for predictions of potential health crises before they occur, leading to proactive healthcare interventions that mitigate associated risks.
