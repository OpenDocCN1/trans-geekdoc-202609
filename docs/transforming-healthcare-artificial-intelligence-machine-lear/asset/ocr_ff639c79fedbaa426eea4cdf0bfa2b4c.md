# 6.7.3 Experimental results

Evaluation metrics include:

+   * Delivery Efficiency: Reduction in medical supply delivery times (Baker & Carter, 2023; Rivera & Gomez, 2022).
* Response Time: Faster arrival to emergency locations (Hall & King, 2022; Martinez & Lopez, 2022).
* Health Monitoring Accuracy: High correlation between drone-acquired data and standard medical devices (Chen & Liu, 2022; Bennett & Turner, 2022).
* Agricultural Monitoring: Enhanced crop health detection and improved yield forecasts (Anderson & White, 2024; Uddin & Ahmed, 2024).
* System Reliability: Stable operation under various environmental conditions (Nguyen & Tran, 2021; Edwards & Harris, 2020).

# 6.7.4 Expected outcomes

The system is expected to deliver:

+   * Improved Delivery and Response: Reduced logistics times and faster medical intervention (Baker & Carter, 2023; Martinez & Lopez, 2022).
* Accurate Health Data: Reliable patient monitoring leading to informed treatment decisions (Chen & Liu, 2022; Hall & King, 2022).
* Enhanced Agricultural Productivity: Optimized farming operations through precision monitoring and AI-driven recommendations (Anderson & White, 2024; Fisher & Green, 2024).
* High System Reliability: Consistent performance in varied and challenging conditions (Nguyen & Tran, 2021; Edwards & Harris, 2020).
