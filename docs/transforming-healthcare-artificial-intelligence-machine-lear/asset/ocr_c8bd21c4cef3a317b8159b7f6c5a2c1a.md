# 16.8 CONCLUSION

# 16.8.1 Summary of key findings

5G technology in healthcare is a turning point, through which unprecedented opportunities are offered for improving patient care, telemedicine, remote monitoring, and overall delivery of health. Several other technical features associated with 5G, like ultra-low latency, high-speed data transfer, and massive connectivity, actually go on to redress many limitations of past generations (4G, 3G) and make real-time applications in the domain of health feasible. These bring endless real-time application opportunities for 5G, such as remote-controlled robotic surgeries, IoT for continuous patient monitoring, AI in diagnostics, and personalized treatment. It may be laden with enormous applications; however, 5G deployment in healthcare also poses serious challenges related to very high infrastructure costs, cybersecurity issues, and complicated regulation. On the brighter side, 5G has the latent capability, with collaborative efforts along with further research and strategic planning towards reshaping the context of how health delivery is practiced but particularly maintained within rural and underserved regions.

# 16.8.2 Implications for healthcare providers and policymakers

Healthcare providers need to adapt to the evolving technological landscape by investing in 5G-compatible devices and upgrading their digital infrastructure. The adoption of 5G can enhance patient care through improved telemedicine services, real-time data transmission, and advanced diagnostic tools. Providers should prioritize cybersecurity measures to protect sensitive patient data and maintain trust.

For policymakers, the rapid advancement of 5G in healthcare necessitates the development of comprehensive regulatory frameworks that address data privacy, security, and ethical use of technology. Policymakers should facilitate the equitable deployment of 5G infrastructure, particularly in rural and underserved areas, to ensure that the benefits of this technology reaches all segments of the population.
