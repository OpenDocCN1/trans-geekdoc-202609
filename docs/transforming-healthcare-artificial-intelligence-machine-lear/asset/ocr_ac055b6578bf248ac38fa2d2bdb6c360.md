# 13.1 INTRODUCTION

The art of yoga has its origin in the distant past and unites body, mind, and spirit into one bond of perfect synchrony. This ancient practice has become an essential part of physical and mental well-being for people around the world, improving flexibility, strength, balance, and mindfulness—all of important components of a holistic health program. However, the immense benefits of yoga depend on a great degree on the correct performance of poses or asanas. Poor alignment and technique reduce the effectiveness of the practice and can lead to injury; hence, precision in posture is crucial for enhancing benefits and safety. The last couple of years of growing interest in using technology to support yoga practitioners opened new avenues for improving accuracy and safety in the practice of yoga. With the integration of digital tools and advanced algorithms into yoga instruction on posture and alignment, yoga today is becoming more accessible and safe to a wide range of practitioners.

The proposed model uses some of the current techniques in the field of deep learning, machine learning, and computer vision to analyze and classify whether a yoga pose is performed correctly or from live video streams in real time. It identifies complex body movements through the deep learning aspect of the model, while computer vision identifies key points in assessing posture. Over any incorrect pose detected, automatically stops the timer of the session and gives real-time voice assistance to the practitioner in adjusting posture without having to look on the screen. This will also allow the user to pay attention to body alignment while creating instructions, connecting them with their practice. When correct alignment is accessed, the timer then turns back, ensuring that the practitioner spends the required time in each asana with proper form, not speeding from one pose to another.
