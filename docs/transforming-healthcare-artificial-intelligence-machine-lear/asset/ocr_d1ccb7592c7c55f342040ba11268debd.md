# 6.6 EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS

The deployment of AIoT-enabled drones is expected to deliver substantial improvements in several key performance areas.

+   - **Delivery Efficiency**: Significant reductions in delivery times for medical supplies are anticipated, especially in remote and inaccessible regions, owing to optimized flight paths and autonomous navigation (Baker & Carter, 2023; Martinez & Lopez, 2022).
- **Response Time**: In emergency situations, drones are projected to arrive at incident locations more quickly than conventional services, thereby improving survival rates (Rivera & Gomez, 2022; Hall & King, 2022).
- **Health Monitoring**: Data collected from remote areas is expected to be both accurate and reliable, supporting timely diagnosis and intervention (Chen & Liu, 2022; Bennett & Turner, 2022).
- **System Integration**: Smooth integration with healthcare systems will enable real-time data exchange, enhancing operational efficiency and coordination (Garcia & Martinez, 2023; Lopez & Garcia, 2022).
