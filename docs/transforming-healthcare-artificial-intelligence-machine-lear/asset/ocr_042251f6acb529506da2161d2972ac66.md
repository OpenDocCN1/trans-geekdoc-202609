# 7. Proactive Fault Detection and Resolution

+   - Technique: AI identifies and resolves network issues before they impact users.
- Example: AI-powered predictive analytics detect patterns that precede network faults or performance degradation. By address-ing these issues proactively, AI ensures continuous and reliable network performance, minimizing latency. For instance, AI can detect and resolve potential hardware failures in base stations before they cause significant delays.

# 15.4.2.1 Dynamic QoS management

Dynamic QoS management involves real-time monitoring and adjustment of network parameters to ensure service quality. AI-driven systems continuously analyze network performance and user behavior to adjust QoS settings dynamically. This can include reallocating bandwidth, adjusting transmission power, and modifying priority levels of different data packets. For instance, during a live event where many users are streaming video, AI can allocate more bandwidth to video streams to maintain high-quality viewing while temporarily reducing the bandwidth for critical services.

# 15.4.2.2 Proactive fault detection and resolution

AI-powered predictive analytics play a significant role in maintaining high QoS by identifying and resolving network issues before they impact users. ML models analyze historical data to detect patterns that precede network faults or performance degradation. By predicting these issues, network operators can take proactive measures to address potential problems, such as reconfiguring network resources or scheduling maintenance activities during off-peak hours. This proactive approach minimizes downtime and ensures a more reliable network performance.

# 15.4.2.3 Personalized user experiences

AI and ML also enable the personalization of user experiences by tailoring QoS parameters based on individual user preferences and usage patterns. By analyzing user data, AI systems can prioritize services that are most important to each user, enhancing their overall experience. For example, a user who frequently engages in video calls may benefit from higher bandwidth allocation and lower latency settings during call sessions. Personalized QoS adjustments ensure that each user receives an optimized experience based on their needs.
