# 11.3 COMPARISON WITH THE EXISTING SYSTEMS

Compact Convolution transformer combines the benefits of convolutional neural networks and vision transformers. It is a lightweight model when compared to vision transformers, and it requires minimal parameters and proffers better accuracy with compact datasets. It reduces the computational time and complexity in the vision transformer models and offers impressive precision in comparison with architectures of convolutional neural networks. There is no possibility for problem of overfitting as it operates on smaller datasets.

The main drawback in employing deep learning models in detection and classification is that they require larger number of datasets. Compact transformers make use of minimum number of datasets in comparison with the other architectures preferred for plant disease detection and classification. Compact convolution transformers are much lighter than vision transformer models. It learns to classify and detect the diseases with smaller datasets without any degradation in performance and F-score. It reflects the hasty nature of CCT model.

Compact convolution transformers outperform the other transformer model as they do not depend largely on positional embeddings. It proffers elasticity in terms of performance and size of the feeder image. It offers amazing performance with the small datasets and largely deduces memory requirements.

# 11.4 PROPOSED METHODOLOGY

### 11.4.1 Dataset

- 1. LC25000
LC25000 dataset comprises 25,000 lung images of five distinct classes with 5,000 images of each class. The five distinct classes of cancer-infected lungs include colon adenocarcinomas, benign lung tissues, lung adenocarcinomas, benign colonic tissues, and lung squamous cells. For our proposed work, we have used only 5,000 images as the proposed optimal CCT works for the minimal number of datasets. For each class, there are thousand images.

- 2. CT Scan images
CT scan consists of 364 images, out of which 126 images are non-cancerous images and the remaining are the cancerous images. The datasets have only two classes: cancerous and non-cancerous. The images in the datasets are expanded to two thousand images by incorporating data augmentation techniques. The dataset gathered with the help of experts and different patients having cancers and other lung diseases from Iran.

- 3. IQ-OTH/NCCD
The IQ-OTH/NCCD is employed to analyze the severity of the diseases. The dataset comprises 1,190 images from 110 patients. The datasets have three classes of cancer stages: benign, malignant, and non-cancerous. The dataset is enlarged to three thousand images by implementing data augmentation techniques.

## 11.4.1 Augmentation techniques

Distinct augmentation techniques, including horizontal and vertical flip, clockwise and anticlockwise rotation, scaling, cropping at random, color contrast, and color jittering, are used to improve the classification accuracy of the proposed model.

## 11.4.2 Architecture of DBCT

The proposed DBCT architecture employs residual convolutional blocks proceeded by dense block layers and dimensionality reduction techniques. The image is reshaped to fed into the transformer blocks.

The architecture of the DBCT involves a combination of convolutional and transformer components, DBCT, as the name implies, is the composition of convolutional layers and transformer layers. It enjoys the benefits of both architectures.

Convolutional blocks comprise convolutional layers with the predefined kernel size to draw out local attributes from the input image. CCT has the ability to learn from the minimal datasets in short time without compromising the performance in comparison with convolutional networks and transformer blocks.
