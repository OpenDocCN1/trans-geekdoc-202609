# 3.2 DIAGNOSTICS AND DISEASE TREATMENT

# 3.2.1 Diagnostics in healthcare

Diagnostics is one of the most essential sciences in healthcare practice since it allows diseases to be overcome by being determined early. Diagnosis is the first step in the entire process of treatment and management of the illness, alerting and guiding better patient outcomes. The scope of diagnostics incorporates many, including medical interviews and history-taking physical examinations, imaging, laboratory tests, and molecular diagnostics. Historically, methods were purely clinical, based on observation and various reported signs and symptoms. However, the advent of medical technologies has helped to improve the processes of diagnosing diseases in a much more specific and faster way. Now, diagnostic systems contain things like genetic testing, MRI, CT scans, and ever computed models capable of predictive recognition using comprehensive databases of medical conditions.

Disease management is more commonly referred to as the treatment of disease, which deals with patients and aims at alleviating ailments and promoting health. The degree of treatment differs according to the category, seriousness, and reason for the condition being treated. They may involve drugs, operations, behavioural changes, or a mix of these methods. The treatment history has gone through a significant transformation from the use of medicines of herbal nature and basic operations to the development of pharmacology, less work with minor surgeries, and better treatment methods.
