# 2.2 TELEMEDICINE

The purpose of telemedicine is to provide access to clinical services in remote places in real time through two-way communication between the patient, help seeker, and expert doctors or medical physicians utilizing digital audio/video means. In healthcare systems, telemedicine is considered as an innovation, and it is helpful for people who need medical assistance from rural places where it is difficult to visit hospitals for diagnosis and medical treatment. Via telemedicine, anyone can access the available healthcare services digitally and get solutions and recommendations for their physical and mental problems [2–4].
