# 10.1 INTRODUCTION

Mobile communication has progressed from 1G to 2G voice and text, 3G multimedia, 4G mobile Internet, and 5G self-driving. Based on the expansion of the 6G scale, network coverage and intelligence will be reinforced in the 6G era. Currently, millimetre-wave channels over 6 GHz are not eligible for 5G, but the low-frequency spectrum below 6 GHz is. Spectrum resources, and additional spectrum will be required for 6G. The increase in communication demand has exacerbated the shortage of spectrum resources. Wireless communication technologies are continually to meet the ever-expanding requirements and wants of humankind. Radio spectrum is becoming more and more necessary as wireless technologies and systems advance. Covered terminals are a major obstacle in SS. To solve the issue of underutilized spectrum, cognitive radio (CR) has become a feasible solution. CR aims to increase the efficiency of the exploitation of existing radio spectrum. Spectrum sensing (SS) is an essential task for CR as it aids in the identification of spectrum gaps, or unused frequency ranges. The energy detection (ED) methodology is the simplest and most straightforward of these techniques. On the other side, low SNR signals make ED less reliable or weaker. Therefore, ED utilizing the entropy method was employed to overcome the issue at low SNR.
