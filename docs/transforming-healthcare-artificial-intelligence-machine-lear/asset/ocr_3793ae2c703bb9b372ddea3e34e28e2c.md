Its duties comprise reading the sensor, angularly shifting the joint, generating a line primitive in the scene (along with an auxiliary point primitive where a detection occurred), and proceeding to the subsequent angular position. It is not threaded; therefore, the child script has to manage the number of positions that the joint would have changed its angle in throughout a single simulation session.
