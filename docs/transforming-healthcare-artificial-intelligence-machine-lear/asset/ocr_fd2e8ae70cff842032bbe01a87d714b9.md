Hospitals will become “smart,” leveraging real-time data and automation to optimize operations and enhance patient experiences. Cross-technology innovations will lead to more efficient collaboration among healthcare providers, improving outcomes while reducing costs. As healthcare continues to digitize, the ultimate goal is to create a system that is not only more efficient and accessible but also more patient-centered, with technology serving as a key enabler for delivering high-quality, equitable care to everyone,.
