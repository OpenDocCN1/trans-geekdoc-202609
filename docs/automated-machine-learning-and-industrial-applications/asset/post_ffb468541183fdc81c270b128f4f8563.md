### Abstract

With the increase in machine learning attention over the recent few years, the next focus is on Automated Machine learning (AutoML), which democratizes ML by automating model selection, tuning of hyperparameters, and the feature engineering process as well. The work delves into the ecosystem of the AutoML, where the initial and basic tasks of ML are converted into a single step. The work focuses on the different frameworks on AutoML along with few open-source AutoML libraries like Auto-sklearn, TPOT, AutoKeras, MLBox, etc. The benefits, challenges, and limitations of incorporating AutoML are discussed in brief. Models, like Fully Automated, Human-in-the-loop, and Model Assisted are the major types of AutoML that are in practice. Though AutoML has a significant efficiency in automating the ML process, the challenges associated with it are quite noteworthy. The intricacy of the model increases with large datasets, along with minimal transparency in the framework. AutoML lacks customization of the models, with obscurity in the algorithms used, decision-making processes, and similar others. Flexibility is a great problem with AutoML since the ML pipeline is automated, and thus, it is quite challenging for the users who move from an ML to an AutoML platform. This work mainly concentrates on the limitations and challenges associated with the AutoML. The quality of data is a major concern, since AutoML relies on the quality of input data. Noisy and biased data may provide substandard predictions. Further, other factors, like pre-processing, outlier detection, and missing values can affect the model's efficiency. A detailed description of the challenges and limitations posed by AutoML are discussed.

Keywords: Auto-sklearn, TPOT, MLBox

### 9.1 Introduction

The method of implementing machine learning to data is automated using the technology of Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) [2]. AutoML can be arranged to iterate through many data transformations, machine learning systems, and hyperparameters to get the best model for a given dataset. The field of machine learning and data science is experiencing a distinguished increase in the usage of Automated Machine Learning. The conventional manual method of developing machine learning models is gradually becoming time consuming and inefficient due to the increasing complexity and scale of data.

Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) is considerably revolutionizing the field of machine learning and data science by offering effective and scalable techniques for constructing the models. Despite the increasing complexity and volume of data, the predictable method of making machine learning models has been proven insufficient in terms of both time and efficiency. The AutoML framework offers a means to enhance efficiency in this process through the automation of method selection, feature engineering, model optimization, and hyperparameter tuning. In the future, the developers will offer a more inclusive examination of the diverse constituents and advantages of AutoML, while also examining its practical implementations in real-life situations.
