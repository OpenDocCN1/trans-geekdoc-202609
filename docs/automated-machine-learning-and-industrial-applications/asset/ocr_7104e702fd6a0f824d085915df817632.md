$A(\theta) = E[max(P(\theta) - P(\theta +), 0)] \qquad (5.18)$

where \( P(\theta) \) is the performance metric for hyperparameters \( \theta \), and \( \theta+ \) is the current best set of hyperparameters. Both TPOT and H2O autoML facilitate model adaptation over time by iteratively updating and re-evaluating the models or pipelines with new data instances and samples. This is conceptualized as a continual learning process via Equation 5.19:

\( \theta_{new} = Update(\theta_{old}, D_{new}) \qquad (5.19) \)

where, \( \theta_{old} \) is the parameters of the current model or pipeline, \( D_{new} \) is the new data, and update represents the re-training process. Based on this, the integration of TPOT and H2O autoML in edge devices for predictive maintenance offers a powerful and flexible framework for automated machine learning.
