Despite all of AutoML’s incentives, it is necessary to remain mindful that there may not be one AutoML solution suitable for all circumstances. Companies ought to carefully consider the benefits and drawbacks of AutoML techniques and adopt a comprehensive strategy that brings together domain knowledge with automated machine learning. Additionally, compliance with regulations, moral challenges, and information privacy should be the primary concerns when using AutoML systems across categories.

# Architecture of AutoML

In recent trends, deep learning has started wide application across diverse fields choosing amazing success in addressing complex AI problems [13]. Its implementation stands within areas, such as image classification, object detection, and language modeling, where it has brought attractive results.

The revolution moment occurred in 2012 when AlexNet outshined in the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) processing traditional methods. However, more complex and deeper neural networks were introduced with models, like ResNet-50, featuring 50 layers and approximately 25.6 million parameters [1]. The architecture consists of steps with varying filter sizes, beginning with 64 filters in the initial stage and completing with 1,024 filters in the fifth stage. Each stage contains residual blocks, each of which contains two 3 × 3 convolutional layers and a crosscut connection.

In modern times, AutoML systems can integrate many techniques making a straightforward and user-friendly end-to-end ML pipeline system. Our focus is to create a way of personalized AutoML methods directing to the development of the most comfortable AI system.
