Its distinctive adaptive method can more effectively balance the overall condition and the local search method increasing search accuracy. The Locust optimization method divides the entire method into an exploration method and a development method to imitate the eliminating and finding of foodstuff behavior of a locust population in the natural world. Algorithm is principally a compound of three components. The following formula illustrates its scheme process:

$$X_i = S_i + G_i + A_i$$

In this equation, Xi represents the location of the i-th locust, Si represents the attraction between individual operations, Gi represents the gravitational pull of the i-th locus, and Ai represents the amount of wind the i-th locust is exposed to. Social action has the most significant impact on locust placement, and the formula for determining Si is as follows:

$$S_i = \sum_{j \neq i}^N s(|X_j - X_i|) \frac{X_j - X_i}{d_{ij}}$$

In this case, N stands for the total number of locusts in a community, dij for the range between locusts i and j, and S for the social strength function. Here is how estimating process works:

$$S(r) = fe^{-r/l} - e^{-r}.$$
