# Abstract

The current study investigates the profound effect of Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) in study and rehabilitative sectors, with an emphasis on analysis, interpretation, and assessment. The work gives context for applying machine learning to enhance medical care and highlights the utility of AutoML in biological products and medical settings. Machine learning applications offer valuable tools for monitoring and evaluating the efficacy of treatments in healthcare. These applications can aid in diagnosis, prognosis, and the development of optimal treatment plans for identified diseases. Nevertheless, we must also consider the limitations, challenges, and opportunities presented by machine learning technology in the healthcare domain. A significant challenge in machine learning is compiling a dataset that is both high-quality and representative of the diverse range of potential inputs. Ideally, the model should be trained on data that precisely mirrors the format and quality of what will be encountered in real-world usage. This paper discusses the principles of AutoML, including various components, effectiveness, and benefits and downsides of adoption.
