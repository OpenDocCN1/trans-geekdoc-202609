**Efficient Model Development:** Model development is accelerated using AutoML. By letting AutoML take care of the details, practitioners can concentrate on solving problems rather than spending time manually fine-tuning models, which facilitates effective model deployment, speedy testing, and rapid prototyping

**Automated Feature Engineering:** The requirement for human feature engineering is decreased since AutoML automatically creates appropriate features from raw data. It enhances churn prediction, fraud detection, tabular data analysis, and many more.

**Computer Vision:** AutoML may be used to create object detection systems, image classification models, and even neural architecture searches for vision problems. Applications include quality assurance in manufacturing, autonomous vehicles [77], and medical imaging, among others.

**Natural Language Processing (NLP):** Language modeling, named unit identification, text classification, and sentiment analysis are all aided by AutoML. Personalized recommendations, content control, chatbots, and more are examples of its applications.

**Time-Series Forecasting:** When working with time-series data, AutoML may forecast values for the future by using patterns from the past. Forecasting demand, modeling energy use, and stock market prediction are a few examples of applications.

**Recommendation Systems:** Personal recommendation engines are built with the use of AutoML, which enhances music streaming services, multi-media platforms, and e-commerce.

**Healthcare and Biomedical Research:** AutoML also supports patient risk prediction, medication development, and disease diagnosis [78]. It helps in personalized medicine, genetics, medical image analysis, and other applications.

**Financial Services:** To detect fraud and optimize portfolios, AutoML helps with credit rating, which helps autonomize Investment management, insurance, banking [79], and other fields.
