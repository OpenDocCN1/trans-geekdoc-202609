# 3.1 Introduction

Machine learning is transforming the medical field through examining massive volumes of facts, retrieving information, and optimizing decisions. Machine learning has improved medical operations by allowing for better diagnosis, customized treatment plans, and better patient outcomes [1, 2]. As healthcare statistics expands quickly, from electronic medical records to radiological records and genomes, advanced statistical techniques are becoming increasingly crucial. Utilizing machine learning for healthcare purposes have proven effective in areas like disease prediction, image categorization, and drug development [3].

# 3.1.1 Rise of AutoML

The advancement of artificial intelligence has led to the rise of AutoML resulting in an evolution in how algorithms are developed and deployed. AutoML accelerates the technique of machine learning by automating critical operations like data preprocessing, feature engineering, model selection, and hyperparameter tuning [4].

The rise of AutoML can be attributed to the democratic transformation of machine learning expertise that has rendered it accessible to specialists working in the area and professionals with limited programming and data science experience. Automation of difficult tasks reduces the barrier toward entry allowing for the speedy development and execution of ML models within healthcare contexts [4].
