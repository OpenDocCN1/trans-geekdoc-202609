The increase in computer accuracy is aided by the machine learning process, which entails collecting and analyzing large volumes of data. Supervised learning is commonly used in machine learning techniques, in which labeled data are sent into a method to train the algorithm [15].

Deep Belief Network (DBN) and CNN are standard DL techniques [16, 17]. DBN uses restricted Boltzmann machines, which significantly speed up the preparation process compared to a standard huge neural network. Most often, image feature extraction scenarios use CNN. These approaches, however, do not account for pattern sequences or remember changes in pattern sequences due to the length of the gaps between these sequences. Recurrent neural networks (RNNs), which have internal memory capabilities, are employed in numerous applications to address these problems and show promise [18]. In several time-series applications for prediction and classification, deep learning based on RNNs obtained great accuracy.

Three different RNN architectures are based on the recurrent units used to construct them. The primary and quick, simple recurrent units (SRUs) form the foundation of the initial RNN architecture [19]. The second RNN design is based on gated recurrent units (GRUs), which employ two gates to address gradient vanishing and accuracy instability [20]. Enterprise management is starting to place more and more emphasis on financial risk management. To reduce the risk of fierce market competition and encourage the achievement of maximum enterprise value economic goals, operators should improve their understanding, prediction, and control of financial risks; actively conduct financial analysis; prevent financial risks; and establish an early warning indicator analysis system appropriate for the business. In addition to raising risk awareness, an enterprise’s long-term survival and growth depend on integrating risk assessment with performance assessment to create a single measuring system. The simulation of credit risk in corporate financial management is the subject of this research. It attempts to offer a fresh line of inquiry for an existing corporate financial management credit risk problem based on machine learning algorithms.
