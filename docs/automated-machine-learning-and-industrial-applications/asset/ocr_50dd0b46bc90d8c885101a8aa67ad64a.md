# 6.1 Introduction

Nowadays, customer relationship management is explicitly driven by emerging technologies. Companies developing and using Customer Relationship Management (CRM) are dependent on technology that penetrates the mind of the customer to know what they think about their products. Technologies, such as predictive modeling, deep learning, augmented reality, and machine learning, play a vital role in developing many applications related to decision support in CRM systems. It is further extended to assessing customer's needs, analyzing them, and offering products they are likely to purchase. Artificial intelligence is helping companies, like Google, Amazon, Flipkart, Facebook, and many more, to know customer reviews comprehensively, analyze customer needs and demands, and provide a better customer experience, which automatically increases sales and production for the companies. On a daily basis, every minute, billions of data are generated by every customer and marketer in public forums in the form of tweets, posts, comments, pictures, and videos. These are treated as customer reviews, online purchase reviews, and online activities. Corporates are mining this database to offer products based on customer interests and likeliness.
