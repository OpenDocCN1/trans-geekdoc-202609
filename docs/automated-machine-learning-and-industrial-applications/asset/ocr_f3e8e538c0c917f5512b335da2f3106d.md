# Data Scientist vs AutoML

While speed and risk reduction are two areas where AutoML technologies outperform human data scientists, there are still areas where the human brain excels over technology. AutoML just cannot match the degree of complexity, intelligence, and creative problem solving that a data scientist brings to the process. Based on the analysis, approximately 43% of the work performed by data scientists can be completed by machines entirely, while 28.57% of the work can be completed by humans and machines working together. The remaining 28.57% of the work will require human labor only. It is also clear from the fact that firms’ most recent job descriptions list AutoML solutions as a preferred skill for data scientists, for instance, Polaris and Growth Analytics.
