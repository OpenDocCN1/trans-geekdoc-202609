# Abstract

The cornerstone of successful and precise prevention of plant disease in complex environments lies in the accurate identification of the specific disease affecting the plants. Advances in decision support, intelligent analysis, and planning are made possible by the digitization and data-driven detection of plant diseases brought about by the quick development of smart farming. This research introduces a mathematical model for diagnosing and recognizing plant diseases using deep learning, aiming to improve training efficiency, accuracy, and applicability. The leaves are first identified and located in complex situations using the region proposal network version 2 (RPNv2) algorithm. Next, the Chan–Vese Level-II (LCV) method is applied to retrieve the symptom features from images that were segmented based on the RPNv2 algorithm’s results. Proposing the use of Extended-EfficientNet (E-ENet) deep learning architecture, this study suggests its application in plant leaf disease classification, with an evaluation of its effectiveness compared to other current deep learning models. Models were skilled using data from PlantVillage. Training datasets comprising 55,448 and 61,486 images, respectively, were used to train all the models.
