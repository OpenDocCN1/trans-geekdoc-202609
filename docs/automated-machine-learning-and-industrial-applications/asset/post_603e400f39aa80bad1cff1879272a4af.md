Sentiment analysis and opinion mining, rooted in early 20th-century opinion research, have witnessed remarkable growth, evolving into a major research focus, especially with the advent of online product reviews in the mid-2000s. The surge in scholarly articles on this subject reflects its rapid expansion, with a notable increase from 101 articles in 2005 to almost 5,699 in 2015—an almost 50-fold rise, positioning sentiment analysis as a peculiar and most quickly advancing area in recent years [8]. Now, people can share their views on personal web pages, blogs, social platforms, forums, and reviews, thanks to the rich and diverse information provided in different versions of web applications propelling rapid progress in the field of opinion mining [9].

AutoML emerges as a focal point in machine learning research driving substantial magnification and fascination in ML applications. AutoML has significantly democratized the process of building and deploying sentiment analysis models enabling organizations to harness the power of machine learning without the need for extensive expertise.
