## Abstract

The application of artificial intelligence (AI) has shown to be transformational in
the field of precision agriculture. In recent years, the demand for effective agricultural
practices has been growing considerably, and thus, the proper utilization
of AI capabilities will certainly resolve some of the challenging issues and further
develop the processes for environmental preservation. Also, the AI’s transformative
effect on agriculture has developed itself by replacing some substandard practices
with modern and effective approaches. The proposed system describes the idea of
an AI application that uses plant scanning technologies to solve agricultural issues.
The goal of this smartphone application is to give farmers, agricultural professionals,
and other related stakeholders a simple tool for the early detection and proactive
management of plant-related difficulties. The proposed system uses AI tools,
including machine learning and computer vision techniques, to analyze images that
the potential users can take using a mobile smartphone to investigate plants diseases.
The software instantly analyzes the images after scanning a plant to produce a
detailed report explaining the implicit faults found along with possible remedies and
interventions. Eventually, our system offers comprehensive solutions for effectively
predicting and addressing the impeding agricultural issues that scan through various
plants. Further, the proposed system tends to provide solutions for monitoring
the state of the plant and enhance the space for high crop yielding, which, in turn,
reduces losses and paves the way for sustainable agricultural practices. Let us explore
the cutting-edge world of AI and automated machine learning (AutoML) in the context of sustainable agriculture with a focus on the rapid and accurate identification of plant diseases. This chapter delves into the latest advancements that promises to revolutionize the agricultural industry offering innovative solutions to address plant health challenges and ensure sustainable food production.

Keywords: Plant leaf disease, AI, ResNet, leaf symptoms, CNN, SVM

## 4.1 Introduction

Although agriculture plays a vital role in providing food security, producers have been overcoming some considerable difficulties in efficiently managing and safeguarding their cultivation against diseases. Traditional disease detection techniques often lack in accuracy and are not able to act accordingly resulting in poor productivity and loss of financial benefits. Due to the intrinsic complication of agricultural data and the request for effective and easily accessible tools, solutions, including artificial intelligence, are needed to predict diseases that is easy for users to recognize. To overcome these problems, an AI-based tool that integrates preprocessing, disease diagnosis, prediction, and data gathering is required. Such a tool will authorize farmers and recover crop management techniques.

The formation of a cutting-edge AI-based explanation for agricultural leaf disease prediction and prevention is the foremost goal of this research. Our aim is to develop a comprehensive system that smoothly integrates AI and optimizing every process of the procedure from joining data to forecasting diseases. By adding characteristics that are easy to use, such as image capture of relevant data points, we hope to increase the effectiveness of data collection while creating the tool accessible to farmers with diverse levels of technological developments. To assure the accuracy of the data taken for disease prediction, a data preprocessing method will be used to manage missing data, outliers, and normalization. By analyzing preprocessed data, the system will use advanced AI algorithms to provide farmers with actionable recommendations and accurate predictions of diseases.

Creating an attractive and user-friendly mobile application interface is a main priority to allow farmers to produce well-informed decisions by developing a culture. With developments in deep learning, IoT and sensor integration, personalized results, and ongoing model refinement, the future of AI-based tools for agricultural disease prediction is quite exciting. Widespread application of AI in agriculture for more precise and effective disease prediction is predicted to be made possible by collaborative platforms and regulatory frameworks, which will support responsible adoption and increase farming production worldwide.

In the remainder of this chapter, a literature survey and an analysis of agricultural diseases are presented. The proposed approach is detailed highlighting the pros and cons. Then, the conclusions are summarized.

## 4.2 Literature Survey

As the agriculture field faces many problems from cultivating to harvesting, plant disease detection methods are highly emphasizing the integration of image processing and machine learning techniques. Numerous research papers have been extensively focused on sustainable agriculture, particularly on plant leaf disease detection and prediction. Our interest in this chapter rests in machine learning approaches; thus, this literature survey covers appropriate research writings.

The paper [1] explores artificial intelligence applications and their significance in the field of plant disease detection offering various techniques, algorithms, and datasets. The authors of ref. [2] present a detailed literature survey on robust hybrid algorithm design and implementation for real-time plant disease detection.

The survey in ref. [3] explains a brief overview of the application of intelligence in agriculture and available techniques and also explaining various methods for detection of diseases in crop. This paper highlights the potential benefits of AI in improving crop yield and addressing disease-related challenges. Besides, it introduces the concepts of artificial intelligence and its rapid advancement across various industrial sectors. It emphasizes AI's ability to solve complex problems and contribute to environmental preservation. The transformative impact of AI on agriculture is noted through replacing traditional methods with more efficient techniques.

The researchers in ref. [4] explore the integration of AI techniques to enhance plant disease recognition in plants. The paper emphasizes the importance of timely disease identification for crop yield and quality. It presents a hybrid algorithm that combines computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning approaches to analyze plant images and diagnose diseases accurately in real time. Further, the strengths and limitations of various AI techniques are discussed and a design of hybrid algorithms, describing its implementation as well as results and outlining potential future directions for enhanced capabilities, is proposed. Overall, the paper highlights the transformative potential of AI in revolutionizing plant disease management and its implications for sustainable agriculture.
