## Predictive Maintenance in Industrial Settings: Video Analytics at the Edge with AutoML

Madala Guru Brahmam and Vijay Anand R.*
School of Computer Science Engineering and Information Systems,
Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore, India

### Abstract

In industrial environments, the need for predictive maintenance has never been more critical for both safety and operational efficiency. Traditional methods often involve labor-intensive, manual inspections that are not only costly but also less effective in timely fault detection. This chapter explores the amalgamation of video analytics, edge computing, and automated machine learning (AutoML) in industrial settings to revolutionize predictive maintenance strategies. The proposed framework aims to automate and enhance the real-time monitoring and analytics of machinery, thereby proactively identifying potential issues before they escalate into failures. By deploying edge computing devices equipped with video cameras and autoML algorithms, we achieve localized, real-time data processing reducing latency and bandwidth usage. Algorithms, such as auto-Sklearn, TPOT, and H2O’s autoML, are leveraged to automate the machine learning process, thereby allowing the system to adapt and improve over temporal instance sets. The preliminary results suggest a significant reduction in unplanned downtime by up to 30% and an increase in the accuracy of fault detection by approximately 20% compared to traditional monitoring methods. Moreover, the utilization of autoML minimizes the need for specialized machine learning expertise making the solution scalable and cost effective. The impact of this work has the potential to be transformative for industries reliant on heavy machinery, reducing maintenance costs and enhancing operational efficiency, while also contributing to safer working conditions. This innovative approach represents a paradigm shift in predictive maintenance leveraging the power of cutting-edge technologies to solve long-standing industrial challenges.

### 5.1 Introduction

In the ever-evolving landscape of industrial operations, the quest for efficiency, safety, and cost effectiveness has led to the adoption of advanced technological solutions. The significance of predictive maintenance in this context cannot be overstated, as it stands at the forefront of industrial innovation directly impacting operational longevity and safety. This chapter delves into the transformative potential of integrating video analytics, edge computing, and automated machine learning (autoML) in the realm of predictive maintenance within industrial settings.

The traditional approach to maintenance in industrial environments has predominantly been reactive or preventive characterized by scheduled inspections and maintenance activities. While these methods have their merits, they are often plagued by inefficiencies such as high labor costs, significant downtimes, and the inherent limitations of human error. In contrast, predictive maintenance represents a proactive strategy focusing on predicting failures before they occur, thus enabling timely intervention. The integration of video analytics and edge computing with autoML offers a paradigm shift in this domain.

Video analytics, the process of extracting meaningful information from video footage, becomes a pivotal tool in monitoring and analyzing the condition of machinery. When combined with edge computing, where data processing occurs close to the source of data generation (i.e., the industrial machinery itself), the system benefits from reduced latency and lower bandwidth usage. This localized processing is crucial for real-time monitoring and decision making in industrial environments.

Automated machine learning, or autoML, is the technological cornerstone of this framework. autoML platforms, like auto-Sklearn, TPOT, and H2O's autoML, automate the process of applying machine learning algorithms making it accessible even to those without deep expertise in the field. This democratization of machine learning enables the predictive maintenance system to continually adapt and improve enhancing its accuracy and reliability over time.

The introduction of this integrated framework in industrial settings is not just an incremental improvement but a substantial leap forward. Early results indicate a notable reduction in unplanned downtime and a significant increase in fault detection accuracy. This increase in operational efficiency is coupled with a reduction in maintenance costs, thereby addressing two of the most pressing concerns in industrial operations. Furthermore, by minimizing the need for specialized machine learning expertise, the autoML-driven approach offers a scalable and cost-effective solution making advanced predictive maintenance strategies accessible to a broader range of industries.

This chapter will explore the components of this innovative approach in detail highlighting the synergistic effects of video analytics, edge computing, and autoML in transforming predictive maintenance. It will also discuss the practical implications of this framework providing insights into its implementation, challenges, and the future scope of this technology in industrial applications. Through this exploration, the chapter aims to underscore the transformative impact of these technologies not just in enhancing operational efficiency but also in contributing to safer working conditions in industries reliant on heavy machinery.

### Motivation and Contribution

The impetus for exploring the confluence of video analytics, edge computing, and automated machine learning (autoML) in predictive maintenance is rooted in the pressing challenges faced by the industrial sector. The motivation for this innovative approach stems from a need to address several key issues as follows:

- 1. **Inefficiency of Traditional Maintenance**: Traditional maintenance methods, while familiar, are often reactive and labor intensive. They rely heavily on scheduled checks and manual inspections, which are not only costly but also prone to human error. The inefficiency of these methods, particularly in fault detection and downtime management, necessitates a more proactive and technologically advanced approach.

- 2. **Rising Costs and Downtime**: Unplanned downtime in industrial operations can lead to significant financial losses. Moreover, the costs associated with traditional maintenance are substantial spanning labor, equipment, and lost production time. Reducing these costs and improving uptime is a priority for any industrial operation.
