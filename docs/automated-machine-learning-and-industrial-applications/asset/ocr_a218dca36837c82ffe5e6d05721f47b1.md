Blockchain generation is a form of allotted ledger technology that is protected by using public and personal protection keys. It operates on a decentralized ledger. All community customers have access to the public key, while only those who are involved in the transaction have access to the non-public key. Therefore, the transaction will be available to all community customers via the general public key, while the stakeholders and details of the transaction will be visible to people who possess the non-public key. It ensures the protection of the stakeholders’ sensitive financial information while maintaining transparency.
