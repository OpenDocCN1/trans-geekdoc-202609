## 8.5 Conclusion

This research aims to analyze financial credit risk with banking monitoring using a machine learning-based Gaussian Encoder Belief Network (GEBN) and metaheuristic autoregressive kernel principal swarm optimization model (MAPSO). This research suggests a deep learning-based prediction and assessment technique for financial market investment analysis. RMB, euro, yen, Canadian, Australian, pound, and Swiss franc are the seven major international exchange rate markets from which this study examined historical data from to estimate exchange rates. The extraction procedure is capable of handling input text that has varying lengths. After extraction, it is possible to efficiently filter out false information from financial communications, which speeds up the subsequent categorization. After extracting the abstracts, sentiment classification is carried out using the GRU model by extracted abstracts. The proposed technique in this research is validated through a financial message dataset from a financial social network.

The proposed method's efficacy may also be demonstrated using abstract extraction and GRU strategies. The recognition rate has improved by 2.87% in compared to the method of not extracting abstracts. Data restrictions prevent all or hidden debt threats from being incorporated into the early warning signal method, which is the cause of the shortfall. The methodology for assessing government debt risk undergoes further refinement and optimization considering the disclosure of illicit concealed debt data from audits and the official calculations about hidden social security debt figures.
