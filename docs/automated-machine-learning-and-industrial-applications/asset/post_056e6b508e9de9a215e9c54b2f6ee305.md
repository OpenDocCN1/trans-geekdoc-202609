The setup is tailored to simulate a real-world industrial environment with a focus on accuracy, efficiency, and adaptability of the maintenance predictions. It is discussed as follows:

### Data Collection and Preparation

- Data source: Industrial machinery datasets, including operational data and video feeds.
- Data size: Approximately 10 TB of historical data, including time-series sensor readings and high-definition video recordings.
- Sampling Rate: Sensor data sampled at 1 Hz and video recorded at 30 frames per second.
- Preprocessing: Data normalization, noise filtering, and time-window segmentation (window size: 5 min).

### Edge Computing Setup

- Hardware: Edge devices equipped with quad-core CPUs, 16-GB RAM, and GPU acceleration.
- Operating System: Linux-based OS optimized for edge computing.
- Networking: 5G connectivity with an average latency of 10 ms and a bandwidth of 100 mbps.

### AutoML Configuration

- Auto-Sklearn
  - Time Budget: 12 h per model.
  - Ensemble Size: Maximum of 50 models.
  - Resampling Strategy: Fivefold cross validation.
- TPOT
  - Generations: 100.
  - Population size: 50.
  - Crossover Probability: 0.9.
  - Mutation Probability: 0.1.
- H2O
  - Max models: 100.
  - Max Runtime: 18,000 s (5 h).
  - Stopping metric: LogLoss.
- Validation strategy: Stratified 10-fold cross validation.

### Model Evaluation Metrics

- Primary Metrics: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 score.
- Secondary Metrics: Computational time, model adaptability score (based on performance over time).

### Experiment Phases

- 1. Phase I—Model Training and Selection
  - Train multiple models using historical data.
  - Select top-performing models based on cross-validation scores.
- 2. Phase II—Real-Time Processing
  - Deploy models on edge devices and scenarios.
  - Process real-time data for predictive maintenance analysis.
- 3. Phase III—Model Adaptation
  - Implement online learning mechanisms to adapt models based on new data.
  - Evaluate model performance over successive temporal instance sets.

### Validation and Testing

- Validation Set: Separate dataset comprising 20% of the historical data; not used in training.
- Testing: Live operational data from industrial machinery over a period of 3 months.

### Performance Monitoring and Analysis

- Monitor the system's predictive performance continuously.
- Analyze system response time, model update frequency, and maintenance prediction accuracy.

## 5.5 Conclusion and Future Scope

The exploration and implementation of an integrated framework combining video analytics, edge computing, and automated machine learning (autoML) in predictive maintenance within industrial environments, as detailed in this chapter, have yielded significant advancements in operational efficiency and safety. The results demonstrate the clear superiority of the proposed model over existing methods [4], [15], and [24], in various critical aspects including accuracy in fault detection, reduction in down-time, computational efficiency, scalability, and cost effectiveness.

The accuracy in fault detection has been notably enhanced, a direct consequence of the sophisticated feature extraction capabilities of the CNNs and the adaptive, self-improving nature of the autoML algorithms. This has led to a reduction in unplanned downtime by 30%, a substantial improvement that not only minimizes operational disruptions but also translates into significant cost savings.

The deployment of edge computing has addressed key challenges associated with latency and bandwidth making real-time data processing feasible and highly efficient. This, coupled with the scalability of the model, supports the notion that this approach is well suited for diverse industrial environments, regardless of their size or complexity.

The financial implications of this model are equally significant. With a marked reduction in maintenance costs, the proposed model presents a cost-effective solution democratizing access to advanced predictive maintenance technologies for a broader range of industries.
