## 6.5 Conclusion

The prominent intent of these analyses is to provide recommendations for future organizations to involve AI-enabled services for customers enhancing business volume. AutoML will offer businesses a new dimension in the future leading to significant changes in business. It paves the way for better relationships between customers and business organizations with quality service and sustainability. In this study on opinion mining, we proposed a methodology named AutoCRM, with random hyper-parameter tuning based on Autokeras features, to analyze customer reviews from various types of organizational domains. This approach proved to be successful with notably high accuracy.

The evaluation of opinion mining for customer relationship management, based on AutoML and conventional code-based approaches, uses the aforementioned performance metrics. It was found that hyper-parameter optimization will automatically increase the accuracy of the classifier. Instead of using code-based approaches, the proposed automated classification will give better results for these types of problems. Additionally, this study exhibits the significance of identifying the tuning parameters that elevate the potential of the prototype and also deepen understanding of the AutoML core. Concurrently, directing on subsequent improvement, this study could evaluate Cloud-based AutoML and supplementary AutoML stacks such as Google AutoML and H2O.ai. Incorporating those subfields into opinion classification will lead the way to impressive outcomes.
