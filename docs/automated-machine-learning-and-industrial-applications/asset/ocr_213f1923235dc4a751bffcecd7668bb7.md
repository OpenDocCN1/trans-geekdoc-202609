The authors of ref. [5] primarily focus on the potential benefits, challenges, and prospects of implementing AI technology in the agricultural sector. The authors emphasize the significance of agriculture in the economy and highlight the challenges faced by the industry including climate changes, natural calamities, and limited resources. Also, they shed light on how AI can revolutionize the agricultural sector. The authors identify several key areas where AI-based systems can have a transformative impact that includes increasing crop yield, crop disease detection, precision farming, automation, data analytics, IoT-enabled solutions and sustainability. In addition, this paper addresses challenges disturbing the adoption of AI in agriculture such as data accessibility, cost, lack of technical expertise, and ethical concerns.

The paper in ref. [6] discusses the significant role that agriculture plays in the economy along with the challenges presented by the increasing global population. With rising demands for food and employment, traditional farming methods become inadequate and increase the demand for automated techniques into AI application in agriculture. It highlights the transformative impact of automation and AI technologies on various aspects of agriculture ultimately contributing to improved productivity and sustainability in the sector.

The authors of ref. [7] discuss the application of AI techniques, particularly machine learning and image processing for detection of diseases in plants. The importance of early disease detection in crops, to prevent economic losses and ensure the quality and safety of agricultural products, is highlighted. The authors cite and discuss various specific studies using techniques like fractal descriptors, neural networks, Gabor filters, and color space transformations for disease identification in different crops such as rice, wheat, soybean, cotton, and more.

The paper in ref. [8] discusses the importance of agricultural development, challenges posed by plant diseases, and how AI and drones can be used to address them. The proposed approach involves using an improved efficient Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) for plant disease detection and an improved EfficientNetV2-B4 model for classifying plant diseases using deep learning. Moreover, this article showcases a comprehensive approach to using AI and drones for plant disease detection that demonstrates the performance of the proposed model on a standard dataset.

The authors in ref. [9] explore data collection, preprocessing, feature extraction, and model selection or training or evaluation methodology in plant disease identification. Empirical evaluation of model performance is followed by the limitations of traditional methods and the potential advantages of using artificial intelligence techniques.
