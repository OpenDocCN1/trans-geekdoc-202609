# Traditional Predictive Maintenance Models

+   1. **Statistical Models:** Early predictive maintenance models were heavily reliant on statistical methods such as regression analysis and time-series forecasting. These models, while useful for basic trend analysis, often lacked the granularity and adaptability necessary for complex industrial machinery.

+   2. **Rule-Based Systems:** Another traditional approach involved rule-based systems, where maintenance was triggered based on predefined criteria or thresholds. While straightforward, these systems were rigid and could not easily adapt to varying operational conditions or unexpected anomalies.
