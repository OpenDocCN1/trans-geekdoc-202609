If the sentiment-bearing term is accompanied by a modifier that tends to increase the strength of the specific term, then the score is updated by incrementing the original score by 1, obtaining 1.42 as far as this example is concerned. Alternatively, if the term is associated with a modifier that tends to reduce its strength, then the updated score is obtained by decrementing the original score by 1, obtaining −0.58, in this case. Now, the problem is that this approach to scoring the sentiment-bearing terms does not take into account the trilateral modifiers introduced in the proposed methodology for chatbot sentiment analysis. With respect to improving the chatbot’s understanding of the customer’s inquiries, it is necessary to analyze the customer’s text in a fine-tuned manner so that the chatbot can provide a tailored response satisfying the customer. To accomplish this task, the proposed trilateral modifiers must also be considered while computing the scores. A few examples of trilateral modifiers are shown in Table 7.5.
