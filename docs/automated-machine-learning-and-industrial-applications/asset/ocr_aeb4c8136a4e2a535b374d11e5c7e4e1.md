[2] This paper explores the Koko app, Sarcasmbots , and Taybots. Koko was working on making an empathetic chatbot. They aimed at incorporating empathy into all the popular conversational agents, such as Siri, Alexa, and Cortona. A demo version of Alexa released by Koko was sufficiently empathetic in situations where the user conveys his or her sadness. In such cases, Alexa suggested taking a small walk or listening to music. But the recommendations were not enough when the user conveyed information about missing loved ones or some form of stress. On the other hand, sarcasmbots are open-source modules for generating sarcastic responses from chatbots. Such bots respond to user input in a sarcastic way. The module involves eight types of sarcasm generators. Based on specific criteria for user input, the appropriate type of sarcasm generator is chosen. The article addresses the topic of using sentiment analysis in chatbots and explains why a strong chatbot is initially necessary. Pre-processing, data cleansing, the bag of words technique, N-grams, and negation handling are all part of the suggested method for emotion recognition. Their technique is innovative in that it attempts to conceptualize the customer–chatbot relationship as more traditional meaning that the user should feel as though they are conversing with a human. **Gap: The methodology lacks modifier or intensifier handling in detecting sentiments.**
