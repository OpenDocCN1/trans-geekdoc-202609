# 13.2.2.1 Clinical Documentation

NLP is used to convert spoken to written format and also helps in better storage and organization of our data. It helps in better categorization and standardization for electronic health data.

# 13.2.2.2 Disease Prediction

NLP can be deployed on a larger scale to conduct sentiment analysis and track the outbreak of any disease or epidemic beforehand. Analysis of social media, hospital records for strange cases, etc., can all be seen as potential uses of this.

# 13.2.2.3 Chatbots and Virtual Assistants

With the rise in use of chatbots virtually in all sectors, even in healthcare, it is much more useful to have users communicate with chatbots for faster 24/7 support, and the virtual assistants can help reduce demand on hospital staff, who can help oversee and step in if the bot flags someone having an emergency.

# 13.2.2.4 Report Analysis

Majority of the lab tests conducted convey a standard result of positive or negative and also whether the count of something, like platelets, for example, is low or not. With NLP, we can help speed this process up and directly provide the result of the analysis.
