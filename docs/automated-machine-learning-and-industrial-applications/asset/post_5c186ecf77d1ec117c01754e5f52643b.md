## Abstract

Automated Customer Relationship Management system is designed to analyze customer-related data such as call notes or service requests. Its primary objective is to enable organizations to gain a comprehensive understanding of customer issues, respond promptly to service concerns, and strategically plan process enhancements. Through the utilization of this system, organizations cannot just promptly address immediate concerns but also proactively identify and implement long-term improvements contributing to magnificent customer gratification and well-organized functional processes. The article shifts its focus to the broader realm of organizational management as a whole to emphasize how crucial it is to mine the continuous stream of service demand flows into and within the business. Therefore, they can point out matters of substantial concern to their customers and monitor issues evolving every time from groups of customers, classes of products, or other proportions and recommend solutions for problems that persist. These insights lead to text analytic process with AutoML-driven-based self-learning system in contrast to rule-based techniques. This research article delves into the world of opinion mining or sentiment analysis, particularly in text classification tasks, and explores the potential of an Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) tool to enhance the process. The study showcases AutoKeras as a catalyst for advancing sentiment analysis in text classification. Its automation of model search and hyper-parameter tuning processes introduces a novel path for crafting precise opinion analysis models. This innovation holds the potential to empower organizations to extract valuable insights from voluminous text data while minimizing the need for manual intervention.

The article's trajectory also encompasses a comparative analysis between AutoML techniques, specifically AutoKeras, and traditional machine learning methods. This analysis aims to spotlight the advantages of AutoML in addressing organizational challenges related to unstructured textual customer feedback. In summation, the article encapsulates two significant domains: Text classification using AutoML tool and the transformative potential of AutoML-driven text analytics in customer service management. On a daily basis, every minute, billions of data are generated by every customer and marketer in public forums in the form of tweets, posts, comments, pictures, and videos. These are treated as customer reviews, online purchase reviews, and online activities. Corporates are mining this database to offer products based on customer interests and likeliness.

### 6.1.1 Opinion Mining or Sentiment Analysis

Social network competes against exceedingly a significant position in the daily lives of nearly everyone providing an environment for individuals to denote or convey their conception and sentiments regarding by-products on e-commerce websites. This expression is commonly referred to as an opinion or reviews [1]. Its goal is to ascertain the writer’s mood or the speaker’s attitude, which can lean either positively or negatively toward the product. The emotions, whether favorable or unfavorable, articulated by individuals are termed sentiment.

There are several challenges because an opinion word may seem positive in one situation but becomes negative in another. Another challenge is that people may not provide the proper or exact words related to their opinion. In addition, confusion arises when both positive and negative comments are present in one single review, which is understandable for humans but very complicated for machines to process [2]. Therefore, the process involves the use of Natural Language Processing, text analysis, and computational linguistics to recognize and derive instinctive direction from the reference material. Text categorization can be done using ML or DL techniques.
