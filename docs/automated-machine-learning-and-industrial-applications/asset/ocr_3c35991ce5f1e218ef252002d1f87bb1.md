# ## 6.1.1 Opinion Mining or Sentiment Analysis

Social network competes against exceedingly a significant position in the daily lives of nearly everyone providing an environment for individuals to denote or convey their conception and sentiments regarding by-products on e-commerce websites. This expression is commonly referred to as an opinion or reviews [1]. Its goal is to ascertain the writer’s mood or the speaker’s attitude, which can lean either positively or negatively toward the product. The emotions, whether favorable or unfavorable, articulated by individuals are termed sentiment.

There are several challenges because an opinion word may seem positive in one situation but becomes negative in another. Another challenge is that people may not provide the proper or exact words related to their opinion. In addition, confusion arises when both positive and negative comments are present in one single review, which is understandable for humans but very complicated for machines to process [2]. Therefore, the process involves the use of Natural Language Processing, text analysis, and computational linguistics to recognize and derive instinctive direction from the reference material. Text categorization can be done using ML or DL techniques.

With the primary goal of assisting in strategic planning and business decision making, the machine learning approach is based on text analysis and classification.
