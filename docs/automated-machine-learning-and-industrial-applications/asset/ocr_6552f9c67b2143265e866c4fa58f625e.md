# 7.4.3 Evaluation

It is very important to evaluate the performance of the chatbot with respect to two parameters, namely, content evaluation and customer satisfaction [16]. The former is adopted from the research domains of information retrieval and NLP. It deals with the setting of the chatbot’s responses to customer service queries. Instead, customer satisfaction is the most commonly used parameter for chatbot performance evaluation because the responses may vary, and there is no single correct response. The chatbot’s performance is determined based on the contentment of the customer. The second parameter, customer satisfaction, is more suitable for assessing the performance because it is only for evaluation because it is on the customers’ end to decide whether the responses provided satisfy their requirements.
