## Preface

The field of Machine Learning (ML) has rapidly advanced in recent years, driving exploration and experimentation across various domains. As businesses refine their strategies and leverage data-driven insights, the demand for efficient machine learning models continues to grow. Developing and optimizing these models requires extensive expertise in data science, particularly for industries seeking to implement cutting-edge solutions at scale.

This is where Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) comes into play, bridging the gap between machine learning and real-world challenges. AutoML eliminates barriers by enabling industries to harness advanced analytics without requiring deep knowledge of underlying algorithms. This book, Automated Machine Learning and Industrial Applications, aims to provide a clear understanding of how AutoML can be applied in industrial settings to deliver optimal solutions. It serves as a practical guide for engineers and data scientists while also offering valuable insights for decision-makers looking to integrate ML into their workflows.

The book covers a wide range of topics, including applications of AutoML in healthcare, next-generation industries, sustainable agriculture, customer support, cybersecurity, and more. It also explores the AutoML ecosystem, its integration with blockchain, and various open-source tools available on the AutoML platform. Each chapter illustrates different aspects of the technology, addressing the unique challenges of real-time applications. Additionally, numerous case studies demonstrate how AutoML enhances performance, reduces time and costs, and improves decision-making.

We would like to extend our heartfelt gratitude to all the authors who contributed their time and expertise to this book. Their dedication has been instrumental in bringing this project to fruition. We also appreciate the invaluable feedback and suggestions from our reviewers, which have helped enhance the quality of each chapter. Furthermore, we express our deepest thanks to Martin Scrivener and the team at Scrivener Publishing for their guidance and support throughout the publication process.
