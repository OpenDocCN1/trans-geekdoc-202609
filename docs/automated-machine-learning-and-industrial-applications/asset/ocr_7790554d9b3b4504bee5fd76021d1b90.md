# 4.2 Literature Survey

As the agriculture field faces many problems from cultivating to harvesting, plant disease detection methods are highly emphasizing the integration of image processing and machine learning techniques. Numerous research papers have been extensively focused on sustainable agriculture, particularly on plant leaf disease detection and prediction. Our interest in this chapter rests in machine learning approaches; thus, this literature survey covers appropriate research writings.

The paper [1] explores artificial intelligence applications and their significance in the field of plant disease detection offering various techniques, algorithms, and datasets. The authors of ref. [2] present a detailed literature survey on robust hybrid algorithm design and implementation for real-time plant disease detection.

The survey in ref. [3] explains a brief overview of the application of intelligence in agriculture and available techniques and also explaining various methods for detection of diseases in crop. This paper highlights the potential benefits of AI in improving crop yield and addressing disease-related challenges. Besides, it introduces the concepts of artificial intelligence and its rapid advancement across various industrial sectors. It emphasizes AI's ability to solve complex problems and contribute to environmental preservation. The transformative impact of AI on agriculture is noted through replacing traditional methods with more efficient techniques.

The researchers in ref. [4] explore the integration of AI techniques to enhance plant disease recognition in plants. The paper emphasizes the importance of timely disease identification for crop yield and quality. It presents a hybrid algorithm that combines computer vision, machine learning, and deep learning approaches to analyze plant images and diagnose diseases accurately in real time. Further, the strengths and limitations of various AI techniques are discussed and a design of hybrid algorithms, describing its implementation as well as results and outlining potential future directions for enhanced capabilities, is proposed. Overall, the paper highlights the transformative potential of AI in revolutionizing plant disease management and its implications for sustainable agriculture.
