# 12.10 Benefits and Challenges

The combination of blockchain and AutoML in banking and finance has received numerous attention because of its capacity to revolutionize the enterprise. Blockchain generation can offer a decentralized, hassle-free, and transparent manner of monetary transactions. On the other hand, AutoML can automate the records using evaluation technique reducing the time and cost associated with model evaluation. Together, technology can be used to create an effective tool that could increase efficiency, improve accuracy, and enhance safety in banking and finance. However, implementing this integration presents numerous demanding situations that must be addressed for it to be successful. Scalability is one of the important demanding situations, as the era must be able to handle simple responsibilities. Interconnectivity is another subject, as financial institutions may additionally use special systems making seamless integration difficult [35]. Compliance is likewise vital, as present laws have to be observed to make sure that the merger is legally permissible. Despite those challenges, the combination of Blockchain and AutoML in banking and finance has the potential to create an extra efficient, expedient, and transparent device. The advantages of this integration are substantial, and with the proper motives, the enterprise can conquer implementation-demanding situations to attain its complete ability.
