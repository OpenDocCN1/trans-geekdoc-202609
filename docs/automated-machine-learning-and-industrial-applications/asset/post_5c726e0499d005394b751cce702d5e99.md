## 4.3 Preliminary Analysis for Agricultural Diseases

It is possible to enhance the efficiency and accuracy of predicting agricultural disease during preliminary investigation through appropriate utilization of autoML techniques. To find some complex patterns and connections that might not be immediately visible using conventional approaches, AI systems can process enormous information that includes historical crop performance, weather patterns, and pest and disease incidences. Machine learning models [13] provide a proactive approach to disease control by using a variety of indicators to forecast disease outbreaks. Besides, the AI application for remote sensing has been performing qualitative work on processing satellite images of the crop fields and helps to detect early diseases of the plant leaf. The analysis becomes more data-driven and adaptive to the changing agricultural ecosystems with the integration of AI technology.

Image-processing techniques can be used to detect minute signs of plant leaf disease by analyzing the visual characteristics of the leaves. The following are some techniques in which image processing can be applied for this purpose:

- Color analysis: Image-processing algorithms can identify the color of plant leaves and recognize any abnormal discoloration or spots that may specify disease.
- Texture analysis: The image-processing techniques can identify indications or patterns with diseases like powdery mildew or rust.
- Shape analysis: Image processing can be used to detect the shape and structure of the leaves and identify any irregularities that may be the reason of the disease.
- Feature extraction: Image-processing algorithms can identify specific characteristics from the leaf images like lesions, spots, or patterns that can be noted as indicators of disease.
- Machine learning: Image processing can also be integrated with machine learning algorithms to train models that can identify patterns with many diseases making it possible to detect indirect signs that may not be directly apparent to the human eye.

Thus, the training of reliable datasets, normalization and scaling of data, as well as the selection of appropriate methods for feature extraction and classification are vital.

### 4.3.1 Datasets and Descriptions

To analyze an image dataset for an artificial intelligence application to identify agricultural diseases, high-resolution images of leaves must be found. These photos need to highlight the developmental phases, detailed descriptions, and illness parameters. In addition, it also needs to verify that each image is rightly tagged to check for disease presence on the leaf or not. It is significant to cover the dataset that holds the correct depiction of both healthy and ill samples. When training a healthy model that can correctly identify and predict agricultural diseases based on pictorial signals, the ability and diversity of the image collection are serious factors.

### 4.3.2 Normalization and Scaling

A vital step in making an Auto ML-based tool for agricultural disease prediction [14] is data preprocessing, which intends to improve the input dataset and make it suitable for effective machine learning model training. As it was stated by authors [14], data cleaning is an important step that removes unnecessary or redundant information and fills any gaps in the data by imputation or elimination. The consistency of the image can be restored through proper image scaling and normalization that provide uniform pixel values and resolutions across images. By exposing the dataset to a variety of changes, data augmentation techniques, like rotation and flipping, improve model generalization. To avoid biases during training, which can be caused by oversampling or under-sampling strategies [15], classes must be balanced. To ensure model compatibility, label encoding converts categorical labels into numerical representations. The dataset is then further split into training and testing subsets for assessment. Whenever there is a need for refinement, feature scaling is implemented once outliers are located and represented by the system. Eventually, a comprehensive quality check verifies the preprocessed dataset integrity by providing the foundation for developing a reliable and accurate model that can successfully predict agricultural illnesses.

### 4.3.3 Feature Extraction and Classification

The fundamental step in image processing is feature extraction that aims to extract relevant information from images for different computer vision applications. As the image analysis tasks needs to substantiate crucial traits, patterns, and other inherent details of the image, it requires some standard extraction procedures such as object recognition, picture classification, and segmentation. Some of the standard procedures for extraction are measures of pixel intensity, picture translation to other color schemes, and pixel distributions represented by appropriate histograms.
