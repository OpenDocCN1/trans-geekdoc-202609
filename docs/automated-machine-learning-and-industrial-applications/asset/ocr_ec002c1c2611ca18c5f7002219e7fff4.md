# 7.1.4 Technical Highlights

+   - The Stanford NLP parser is used to carry out the PoS tagging process. It is used to examine the grammatical structure of a sentence.
+   - The TextInspector tool is used to know the statistical summary of the text involved in the conversation between the customer and chatbot.
+   - The trilateral modifiers proposed in the methodology are used to categorize the modifiers associated with the sentiment.
+   - The proposed nonary scale is utilized to detect the polarity of the sentiment involved in the customer–chatbot conversation.

# 7.1.5 Objectives

+   - The overall objective of the planned methodology is to achieve sentiment analysis in chatbots to improve the competence of a customer support team. This objective is achieved by the following two sub goals:
+   - To carry out subjectivity detection in the conversational text involved in the customer–chatbot interaction.
+   - To perform sentiment polarity detection in the subjective text involved in the conversation.

# 7.1.6 Common Chatbot Use Cases

AI chatbots are used by consumers for a variety of determinations, including interacting with mobile apps and utilizing specially designed appliances like smart kitchen appliances and thermostats. The range of business use is also equal: AI-powered chatbots are used by marketers to customize customer experiences and expedite e-commerce operations; by IT and HR departments to facilitate employee self-service; and by contact centers to handle incoming calls and refer clients to available resources.

Interfaces for conversations can also differ. AI chatbots are frequently utilized in private websites and applications, social network messaging apps, independent messaging platforms, and even in phone conversations (where they are referred to as integrated voice response or IVR).
