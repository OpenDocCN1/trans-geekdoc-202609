Since the mentioned surveys have not covered forecasting, detection, and classification of diseases and have also not fully explored recent deep learning-based techniques, the review carried out in this survey work focuses on filling the existing differences in these subjects. Moreover, it highlights the development of such machine learning tools and provide farmers with data-driven decision-making assistance systems. In this way, farmers can be assisted in decreasing the need for pesticide application and the damage that it causes while preserving and sustaining crop quality and production. It contributes to the continued availability of food to meet global population demands while performing less destruction and damage to the planet.
