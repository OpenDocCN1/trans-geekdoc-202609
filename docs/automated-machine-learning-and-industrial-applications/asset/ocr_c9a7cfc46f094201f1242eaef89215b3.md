# 10.1 Introduction

Indeed, it is an undeniable reality that artificial intelligence is progressively and steadfastly taking over tasks that were traditionally performed by humans in the contemporary era. Artificial intelligence (AI) is a rapidly developing area of technology that focuses to generate machines that can think like humans using algorithms that are trained to solve complex problems. AI refers to the emulation of human intellect in machines enabling them to replicate human thought processes and behaviors like problem solving and learning; within this domain, machine learning plays a crucial role allowing systems to recognize, comprehend, and analyze patterns in data. In the advanced realm of computer science, artificial intelligence stands as a major focal point for research and development. Due to its robust application in problems that are primarily beyond the capabilities of traditional computing structures and humans, as well as its rapid technological advancements, this technology is becoming increasingly prevalent rapidly [1].

Despite the availability of much improved technology, farmers still face many difficulties from planting to harvesting. The application of artificial intelligence approaches has empowered us to comprehend the intricate details of various situations allowing for the provision of optimal solutions tailored to specific issues. The growth of various AI methods is enabling innovative solutions to complex challenges. To address the issues facing agriculture today, a number of approaches have been put forth, such as database integration and decision support systems [2]. The proximity of artificial intelligence (AI) and agriculture, while initially unconventional, has gained relevance in recent times. Despite agriculture being a cornerstone of human civilization for millennia, the rapid evolution of AI, even in its simplest forms over the past few periods, has ushered in transformative fluctuations.
