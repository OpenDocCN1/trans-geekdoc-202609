# Abstract

As a consequence of the rapid expansion of social networks, such as consumer review networks, discussion forms, blogging and publishing networks, and other such Internet-based networks, the volume of comments and evaluations about day-to-day activities has increased tremendously. Sentiment analysis is a significant research area that serves as a powerful and efficient technique capable of analyzing such reviews and comments to gather valuable insights regarding the various aspects of a product or service. Businesses, governing bodies, and individuals may all benefit from public sentiment by gathering data and making decisions based on it.

One of the interesting fields where sentiment analysis finds application is evaluating customer’s interactions with chatbots. With an effort to enhance customer service and reduce corporate expenses, AI chatbots have been designed for customer support. The practice of understanding a customer’s tone of voice and words while communicating with a chatbot—whether they are happy, sad, fearful, or angry—is known as chatbot sentiment analysis. Just because a customer interacted with a chatbot does not mean they will ignore or forget a bad experience.

Customers seek assistance in resolving issues they may be having with the item or service you provide. If the chatbot or virtual assistant is unable to assist them, they will revert to the default procedure, which is typically interacting with an agent in person (a live agent). They will choose to work with a different brand if they are unable to receive assistance there. So, there is a greater need to carry out sentiment analysis on customer interactions with a chatbot.
