# Abstract

In industrial environments, the need for predictive maintenance has never been more critical for both safety and operational efficiency. Traditional methods often involve labor-intensive, manual inspections that are not only costly but also less effective in timely fault detection. This chapter explores the amalgamation of video analytics, edge computing, and automated machine learning (AutoML) in industrial settings to revolutionize predictive maintenance strategies. The proposed framework aims to automate and enhance the real-time monitoring and analytics of machinery, thereby proactively identifying potential issues before they escalate into failures. By deploying edge computing devices equipped with video cameras and autoML algorithms, we achieve localized, real-time data processing reducing latency and bandwidth usage. Algorithms, such as auto-Sklearn, TPOT, and H2O’s autoML, are leveraged to automate the machine learning process, thereby allowing the system to adapt and improve over temporal instance sets. The preliminary results suggest a significant reduction in unplanned downtime by up to 30% and an increase in the accuracy of fault detection by approximately 20% compared to traditional monitoring methods. Moreover, the utilization of autoML minimizes the need for specialized machine learning expertise making the solution scalable and cost effective. The impact of this work has the potential to be transformative for industries reliant on heavy machinery, reducing maintenance costs and enhancing operational efficiency, while also contributing to safer working conditions. This innovative approach represents a paradigm shift in predictive maintenance
