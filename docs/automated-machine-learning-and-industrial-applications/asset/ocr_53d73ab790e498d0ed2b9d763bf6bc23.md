scientists, mathematicians, statistics experts, and researchers. There is a shortage of trained data scientists and experienced industrialist workers. The important constraint is time. When many solutions are taken using machine learning models, it is difficult to get quick results. Few ML results take months to deploy, which is not helpful for the company. Due to high manual dependence, there may be errors during the workflow. Many companies find it difficult to transform the predicted model into to easily understandable format for stakeholders. Even by using the explainability and interpretability of ML systems, the lack of knowledge and experience does not help during implementation.

# ## Conclusion

AutoML reduces manual dependencies and time to create models within a few weeks or days. As AutoML takes care of the repetitive task, humans concentrate on designing, understanding the problems, preparing data, and deploying the model. AutoML produces more accurate results that can be achieved in less time when working with data scientists.
