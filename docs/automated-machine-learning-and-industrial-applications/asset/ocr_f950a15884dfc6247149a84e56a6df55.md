# 12.15 Future Directions and Emerging Trends

+   AutoML is evolving rapidly reflecting the changes in technology. To lead developments, it is important to look at emerging trends and future directions that are expected to affect its development and operations.

+   The outstanding feature of AutoML is to enhance the methods for dealing with complex data types, enhance model interpretation, model reliability, and robustness, and researchers are working tirelessly to develop AutoML advanced methods that can meet the growing demand for complex machine learning models.

+   Another trend fueling interest in AutoML is the rise of edge computing devices and the call for privacy-preserving machine learning solutions. Federated learning is growing as a decentralized method of machine training learning models on multiple edge devices while maintaining data confidentiality.

+   Definable AI and responsible AI practices are also gaining traction as quintessential components to ensure transparency, fairness, and accountability in automated decision-making systems. Future AutoML platforms are expected to include sleep-enabled features manifested largely and defined in model forecasts.

+   Domain-specific AutoML solutions are emerging as a trend tailored to the unique needs and challenges of specific industrial sectors such as healthcare, finance, and manufacturing. These developments enable organizations to use AutoML to perform well in their particular field.
