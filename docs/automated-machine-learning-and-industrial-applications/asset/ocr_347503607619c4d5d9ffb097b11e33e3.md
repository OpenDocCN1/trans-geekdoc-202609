# 3.6 Ethical Implications

Here we will discuss the ethical implications of AutoML [33, 34].

# 3.6.1 Data Privacy and Security

The application of Automated Machine Learning in healthcare needs a thorough study of security and confidentiality of data issues. Given the very delicate nature of medical information, strong safeguards are needed to ensure anonymity. To prevent against unauthorized accessing and database violations, protecting technology, maintaining records, and rigorous safety measures must be implemented. Ethical problems involve gaining patients’ expressed agreement for the utilization of their confidential information for simulation purposes.

# 3.6.2 Fairness and Bias Considerations

The use of AutoML in healthcare applications includes two key legitimate components: neutrality and bias. Modeling based on previous medical data may unintentionally reinforce inherent biases resulting in errors in diagnosis and medication. To prevent this hazard, a proactive strategy will be required throughout the model building process.

Developing fairness-aware strategies and using prejudice identification algorithms are essential phases toward identifying and eliminating flaws in AutoML systems. This leads to comparable results across multiple patient categories, which aligns with the core ideals of impartiality and neutrality in medical judgments.
