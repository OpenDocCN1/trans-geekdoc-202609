The pre-processed text is fed into the subjectivity detection module, wherein the text is tagged using a PoS tagger to tag the tokens with their appropriate grammatical counterparts, such as nouns, adjectives, verbs, etc. In this module, it is identified whether the text is subjective or not. Subjective texts are the source of sentiments. The next task is to use trilateral modifiers to find the types of adverbs associated with the sentiment. Based on the intensifying capability of the modifiers, the real tone of the sentiment can be understood. Finally, the chatbot sentiment polarity detection is carried out by applying the framed nonary scale that detects the accurate tone or polarity of the sentiment expressed in the text.
