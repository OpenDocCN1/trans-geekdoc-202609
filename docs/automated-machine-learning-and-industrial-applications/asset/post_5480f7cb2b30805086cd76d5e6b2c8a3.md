## Abstract

Body sensor network (BSN), a monitoring system utilized in a healthcare setting based on Internet of Things (IoT) technology, consists of wearable or implanted devices. Due to the limited battery capacity and energy supply for sensors in BSN, extending the service cycle of the network is a significant challenge. Increasing energy efficiency and energy collection is essential for the network to remain sustainable. Operators are looking to automate network diagnosis and management using machine learning (ML) to operate complex optical communication networks cost effectively. This research proposes a novel, sustainable, network-secure data transmission technique based on automated machine learning (AutoML). Here, the healthcare network monitoring uses a Reinforcement Bayesian Regressive Vector Machine. The secure data transmission uses quantum blockchain automated transfer machine graph learning. The experimental analysis of throughput, scalability, packet delivery ratio, and data integrity is carried out.

Information and communication technology (ICT)-based health surveillance systems connect people and infrastructure to improve overall quality of life. Wireless body area networks (WBANs), which scientists and researchers have developed, are made up of tiny sensors with wireless communication capabilities that can be put inside patients’ bodies. The devices continuously gather biological signals from patients in hospitals and quarantine facilities using various sensors and then transmit the data collected to the local analysis unit (LPU). To improve health warnings so that healthcare practitioners can quickly respond by taking preventative measures when they see patients’ falling health conditions or any emergency, LPUs should be robust and capable of processing health data in real time. LPU data may also be transferred to distant databases (DB) for long-term study and preservation [2]. WBANs have several advantages, including enabling doctors to monitor patient-specific parameters remotely, improving diagnostic accuracy and efficacy, and lowering healthcare costs per patient by giving doctors a convenient way to track patients’ well-being all the time.
