

### Efficient Model Development
Model development is accelerated using AutoML. By letting AutoML take care of the details, practitioners can concentrate on solving problems rather than spending time manually fine-tuning models, which facilitates effective model deployment, speedy testing, and rapid prototyping.

### Automated Feature Engineering
The requirement for human feature engineering is decreased since AutoML automatically creates appropriate features from raw data. It enhances churn prediction, fraud detection, tabular data analysis, and many more.

### Computer Vision
AutoML may be used to create object detection systems, image classification models, and even neural architecture searches for vision problems. Applications include quality assurance in manufacturing, autonomous vehicles [77], and medical imaging, among others.

### Natural Language Processing (NLP)
Language modeling, named unit identification, text classification, and sentiment analysis are all aided by AutoML. Personalized recommendations, content control, chatbots, and more are examples of its applications.

### Time-Series Forecasting
When working with time-series data, AutoML may forecast values for the future by using patterns from the past. Forecasting demand, modeling energy use, and stock market prediction are a few examples of applications.

### Recommendation Systems
Personal recommendation engines are built with the use of AutoML, which enhances music streaming services, multi-media platforms, and e-commerce.

### Healthcare and Biomedical Research
AutoML also supports patient risk prediction, medication development, and disease diagnosis [78]. It helps in personalized medicine, genetics, medical image analysis, and other applications.

### Financial Services
To detect fraud and optimize portfolios, AutoML helps with credit rating, which helps autonomize investment management, insurance, banking [79], and other fields.

## 14.5 Future Scope

The future of AutoML is poised to transform the landscape of ML and artificial intelligence significantly. Key areas of development include the following:

- Democratization of AI: AutoML will make advanced machine learning techniques accessible to non-experts enabling a broader range of industries and individuals to leverage AI without needing specialized knowledge.
- Enhanced Efficiency: By automating repetitive and time-consuming responsibilities, such as data preprocessing, feature engineering, and hyperparameter tuning, AutoML will greatly improve productivity and allow data scientists to focus on more intricate and innovative aspects of machine learning.
- Integration with Big Data: As data sizes continue to grow, AutoML will play a critical role in efficiently managing and analyzing large datasets, which is important for fields, like healthcare, finance, and retail, where data-driven decision making is critical.
- Environmental Sustainability: Future developments in AutoML will also consider the environmental impact of machine learning models aiming to make more energy-efficient algorithms and systems.

Overall, the future of AutoML is set to revolutionize the way machine learning models are developed and deployed making advanced AI capabilities more accessible, efficient, and ethical across many industries.

## 14.6 Conclusion

AutoML remains a relatively new and rapidly expanding research area. Researchers are actively addressing key challenges, and we anticipate swift advancements in the field. These developments will make AutoML systems more powerful, versatile, and user friendly. Developing an effective AutoML system involves addressing various concerns, such as data collection, cleaning and preprocessing, feature engineering, model selection, model estimation, and optimization. These skills are now essential for machine learning professionals. This review covers different aspects of AutoML providing explanations for each method, which can be used to build future AutoML systems. With ongoing academic research and commercial innovations, the real-world application of AutoML is expected to grow significantly. Our survey aims to foster this development, provided that newcomers to AutoML have access to a comprehensive overview. Without careful implementation, automation can have negative consequences. Therefore, there is a strong need for thoughtful design and deployment of AutoML systems to address these concerns. Modern frameworks allow domain specialists to create effective ML pipelines without needing extensive knowledge of ML or statistics.
