
## 3.6 Ethical Implications

Here we will discuss the ethical implications of AutoML [33, 34].

### 3.6.1 Data Privacy and Security

The application of Automated Machine Learning in healthcare needs a thorough study of security and confidentiality of data issues. Given the very delicate nature of medical information, strong safeguards are needed to ensure anonymity. To prevent against unauthorized accessing and database violations, protecting technology, maintaining records, and rigorous safety measures must be implemented. Ethical problems involve gaining patients’ expressed agreement for the utilization of their confidential information for simulation purposes.

### 3.6.2 Fairness and Bias Considerations

The use of AutoML in healthcare applications includes two key legitimate components: neutrality and bias. Modeling based on previous medical data may unintentionally reinforce inherent biases resulting in errors in diagnosis and medication. To prevent this hazard, a proactive strategy will be required throughout the model building process.

Developing fairness-aware strategies and using prejudice identification algorithms are essential phases toward identifying and eliminating flaws in AutoML systems. This leads to comparable results across multiple patient categories, which aligns with the core ideals of impartiality and neutrality in medical judgments.

## 3.8 Future Directions and Trends

Here, in this part of the study, we will discuss the future trends and directions.

### 3.8.1 Integration with Emerging Technologies

The expected direction of AutoML for therapeutic medicine has been fully incorporated with emerging advancements creating new opportunities for study and development. Several significant trends influence this integration procedure as follows:

- Integration with Edge Computing: The combination of AutoML and edge computing techniques is expected to transform healthcare delivery. Edge computing enables real-time extraction and examination of data at the point of medication reducing latency and permitting quick decisions. AutoML simulations used at the edge can enhance diagnosis, monitor individual well-being, and aid in customized therapies in environments with limited resources.

- Synergy with Artificial Intelligence (AI) Assistants: AutoML is likely to serve an important role in improving artificial intelligence-powered robotic assistant in medicine. These assistants, powered by AutoML algorithms, can offer personalized health insights, address patient questions, and help clinicians make decisions. The combination of AutoML with NLP techniques will enhance the abilities of artificial intelligence assistants resulting in more natural and logical interactions.

### 3.8.2 AutoML in Clinical Trials and Research

The deployment of AutoML in clinical studies and scientific studies has the potential to transform the landscape regarding proof creation and medical advancements. The key developments within this domain are as follows:

- Optimization of Clinical Trial Design: AutoML will help optimize the design of clinical experiment by analyzing historical experiment data, identifying pertinent biomarkers, and forecasting patient response to certain therapies. This can result in cheaper and more effective experiment, hence expediting the drug research pipeline.

- Patient Stratification for Precision Medicine: AutoML's capacity to analyze various patient information will help enhance the categorization of patients in experiments. AutoML permits researchers to create tailored therapies and analyze therapy effectiveness accurately, thereby promoting advances in precision medicine.
