## 12.14 Ethical Considerations and Responsible AI

As companies continue to join in AutoML to drive innovation and increase productivity, accountable AI practices and ethical considerations are central to every stage of the pipeline. Ethical challenges can arise during data collection, modeling and implementation. The outstanding ethical considerations are as follows:

Transparency and explicability: AutoML models often exhibit a great agreement of complexity and ambiguity creating challenges in understanding how decisions occur. Companies should strive to increase model transparency and interpretability, and empower stakeholders to understand the rationale behind model predictions and recommendations.

Responsibilities and governance: Organizations should define clear responsibilities and governance for AutoML projects, and clarify roles and responsibilities for data management, model development, and decision making. Normal audits and critiques ought to be carried out to ensure compliance with ethical requirements and legal requirements.

Informed consent: When introducing an AutoML model that affects the lives or decisions of individuals, companies must obtain informed consent from their stakeholders providing clear explanations on how their data will be used, role, and possible outcomes of model predictions.

Addressing these ethical considerations entails a multidisciplinary approach that requires collaboration among data scientists, ethicists, legal experts, and other stakeholders. Through ethical principle diversity to control the design, development, and deployment of AutoML solutions, companies can improve reliability, reduce risks, and advocate for responsibility in AI applications in technology.

## 12.15 Future Directions and Emerging Trends

- AutoML is evolving rapidly reflecting the changes in technology. To lead developments, it is important to look at emerging trends and future directions that are expected to affect its development and operations.

- The outstanding feature of AutoML is to enhance the methods for dealing with complex data types, enhance model interpretation, model reliability, and robustness, and researchers are working tirelessly to develop AutoML advanced methods that can meet the growing demand for complex machine learning models.

- Another trend fueling interest in AutoML is the rise of edge computing devices and the call for privacy-preserving machine learning solutions. Federated learning is growing as a decentralized method of machine learning models on multiple edge devices while maintaining data confidentiality.

- Explainable AI and responsible AI practices are also gaining traction as quintessential components to ensure transparency, fairness, and accountability in automated decision-making systems. Future AutoML platforms are expected to include explainable features manifested largely and defined in model forecasts.

- Domain-specific AutoML solutions are emerging as a trend tailored to the unique needs and challenges of specific industrial sectors such as healthcare, finance, and manufacturing. These developments enable organizations to use AutoML to perform well in their particular field.

- Finally, open collaborations, such as the AutoML Benchmark and the Open Neural Network Exchange (ONNX), facilitate collaboration and knowledge exchange in the AutoML community, accelerate innovation, and democratize the use of AutoML tools and resources.

- By adapting to these emerging trends and embracing a culture of continuous learning and innovation, organizations can develop a strategy to lead the adoption of AutoML by harnessing its transformational potential role to promote sustainable development and competitiveness in the industrial sector.

## 12.16 Future Scope

Enforcing blockchain technology is not without its challenges. Blockchain is employed by hundreds of financial institutions, and blockchain shares are recognized as investment alternatives despite numerous barriers. It is clear that the financial sector is aware of the potential benefits of blockchain technology and believes that it will become even more critical to the banking sector in the future. Blockchain technology is a form of distributed ledger technology that is protected by using public and private keys. It operates on a decentralized ledger. All network participants have access to the public key, while only those who are involved in the transaction have access to the private key. Therefore, the transaction will be available to all network participants via the public key, while the stakeholders and details of the transaction will be visible to people who possess the private key. It ensures the protection of the stakeholders’ sensitive financial information while maintaining transparency.

Blockchain’s capability to hold a single source of truth, automate interactions between businesses using smart contracts and consensus mechanisms, provide oversight across disparate systems, extensively lessen interdependent imbalances, use near real-time reporting as opposed to outdated methods, and streamline and standardize intercompany supply chain tactics will revolutionize intercompany relationships and transactions for large establishments. Blockchain can prevent these issues in the future ensuring that up-to-date information is consistent between systems and by generating an audit trail of changes made to client data. Financial executives believe that this blockchain use case will save money, enhance safety, expedite actions, eliminate friction, and promote transparency while reducing financial crimes. Within the years to come, blockchain technology can even permit the implementation of “smart contracts,” which are self-executing contracts that can be used to automate human tasks like processing claims, compliance, and distributing a will's contents. The banking industry has replicated its existing asset transfers in an attempt to test Blockchain. This permits some flexibility in how a blockchain solution may impact productivity; however, it does not take into account how it could impact the ecosystem. The foundation for the real-time movement of digital assets among market participants is provided by the blockchain software. Blockchain makes it feasible to redraw methods and question common business model conventions. With the use of this technology, marketplace contributors could be more transparent leveling the playing field. As applications for blockchain technology unfold for many industries, it will become increasingly more crucial to not overlook blockchain's function inside the financial industry within the coming years.

Both blockchain and Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) have emerged as revolutionary technologies with the potential to transform banking and finance. This new technology has the capability to innovate, enhance performance, and enhance the customer experience. Therefore, it is important for banks and financial institutions to embrace it to remain competitive in the rapidly evolving environment. By adopting this technology, banks can streamline their operations, reduce costs, and improve their bottom line while providing better services to their customers.
