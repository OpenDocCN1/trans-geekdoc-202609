# 12.14 Ethical Considerations and Responsible AI

As companies continue to join in AutoML to drive innovation and increase productivity, accountable AI practices and ethical considerations are central to every stage of the pipeline. Ethical challenges can arise during data collection, modeling and implementation. The outstanding ethical considerations are as follows:

Transparency and explicability: AutoML models often exhibit a great agreement of complexity and ambiguity creating challenges in understanding how decisions occur. Companies should strive to increase model transparency and interpretability, and empower stakeholders to understand the rationale behind model predictions and recommendations.

Responsibilities and governance: Organizations should define clear responsibilities and governance for AutoML projects, and clarify roles and responsibilities for data management, model development, and decision making. Normal audits and critiques ought to be carried out to ensure compliance with moral requirements and prison necessities.
