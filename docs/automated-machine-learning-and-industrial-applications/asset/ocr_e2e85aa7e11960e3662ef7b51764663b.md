# 13.4.5 Financial and Resource Constraints

Financial constraints are another major challenge. The high cost of developing, implementing, and maintaining AI systems can be prohibitive for many healthcare organizations, especially in under-resourced settings [23]. This is compounded by the need for substantial investment in training and development for healthcare professionals to effectively utilize AI technologies.

# 13.5 Challenges and Ethical Concerns

# 13.5.1 Privacy and Data Security

The usage of data for model training in AI often utilizes sensitive patient data. It is vital to ensure these data are not misused or accessed by unauthorized users.

# 13.5.2 Bias and Fairness

Depending on the data gathered, the model trained may show bias. Some of the bias are due to either data gathered being skewed and not labeled properly or due to certain diseases not being that common making it absent from our training set and our model unable to classify it.

# 13.5.3 Doctor-Patient Relationship

Overreliance on AI can cause doctor–human relations to deteriorate further. With no proper contact with human doctors, empathy and human touch would diminish in the healthcare industry.

# 13.5.4 Accountability and Liability

There is a legal gray line between accountability and liability concerns when using such models for making medical predictions. The developer of the software needs to be sure that their model works under every case imaginable as well as making sure it undergoes heavy testing.
