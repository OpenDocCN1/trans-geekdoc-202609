According to the laid out explore plan, nearly 600,000 tests of exchange information were collected, analyzed, and handled (over 540,000 examples from Bank A and more than 40,000 examples were obtained from Bank B). Information showing areas of strength for corporate business was screened out. There were four dimensions to the features: credit-related static information, dynamic change data of the advance, loan customer sub-account capital stream, and complete capital stream of the advance client’s different records. After various tasks, including separating and participating from January to June 2020, 103 substantial fields were chosen from 263 fields in eight tables.

Table 8.1 shows an analysis based on various financial credit datasets. The datasets analyzed are GDPR, Advanced Analytics of Credit Registry, and WIND in terms of accuracy, mean average precision, recall, F1_Score, and normalized error.

In Figure 8.3, the analysis for accuracy is shown. Here, for the GDPR dataset, the proposed technique attained an accuracy of 90%, existing SVM attained 80%, and CNN attained 83%. For the Advanced Analytics of Credit Registry Dataset, the proposed technique attained an accuracy of 93%, existing CNN attained 82%, and SVM attained 84%. For the WIND dataset, the proposed technique attained an accuracy of 96%, existing SVM attained 81%, and CNN attained 85%.
