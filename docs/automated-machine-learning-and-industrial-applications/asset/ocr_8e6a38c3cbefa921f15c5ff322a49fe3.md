# ## Abstract

Technological advancements in Blockchain and Automated Machine Learning (AutoML) have revolutionized the traditional practices in the banking and financial sector in recent years. This chapter focuses on the complementarity of Blockchain and AutoML, and discusses practical applications, benefits, challenges, and the future of the confluence of these two technologies. Real-world case studies are cited to show how integrations of these technologies are bringing improvisations in fraud detection, risk assessment, and customer relationship management in the finance sector. For example, the immutability and decentralized nature of Blockchain can overcome data tampering and bring transparency to financial transactions. AutoML automates complex analysis tasks that could be both time consuming and difficult for humans providing fast and reliable solutions for decision making through learning from large amounts of data.

**Keywords:** AutoML, finance, banking, blockchain, stakeholders, inventors, industries
