# Abstract

Automated Customer Relationship Management system is designed to analyze customer-related data such as call notes or service requests. Its primary objective is to enable organizations to gain a comprehensive understanding of customer issues, respond promptly to service concerns, and strategically plan process enhancements. Through the utilization of this system, organizations cannot just promptly address immediate concerns but also proactively identify and implement long-term improvements contributing to magnifient customer gratification and well-organized functional processes. The article shifts its focus to the broader realm of organizational management as a whole to emphasize how crucial it is to mine the continuous stream of service demand flows into and within the business. Therefore, they can point out matters of substantial concern to their customers and monitor issues evolving every time from groups of customers, classes of products, or other proportions and recommend solutions for problems that persist. These insights lead to text analytic process with AutoML-driven-based self-learning system in contrast to rule-based techniques.
