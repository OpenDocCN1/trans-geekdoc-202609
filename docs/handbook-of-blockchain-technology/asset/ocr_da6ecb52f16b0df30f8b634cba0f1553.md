**Björn Hanneke** is a research assistant and PhD student at the Chair of Information Systems and Information Management at Goethe University Frankfurt/Main. Björn has over seven years of strategy consulting experience in the financial industry. His research interests include Digital Platforms, the Token Economy, and Token-based Applications.

**Stavros Ioannou** holds an MSc in Blockchain and Digital Currency from the University of Nicosia (UNIC). This program is recognized as the world’s pioneering master’s degree in decentralized digital currencies and Blockchains. His primary areas of specialization encompass Blockchain, Cryptocurrency, NFT’s as well as Accounting and Finance. Further research interests include: Tokenization of Assets, Real World Assets (RWA’s), NFT’s and DeFi. He presently holds the position of Crypto Liquidity Manager, utilizing his past experience and skills in Accounting & Finance to his advantage in the fast-paced world of cryptocurrencies.

**Dr Elias Iosif** is Assistant Professor at the Department of Digital Innovation, School of Business, University of Nicosia (UNIC), as well as scientific lab co-leader at the Distributed Ledgers Research Centre, Institute for the Future, UNIC. Dr Iosif also serves as Associate Head of the Department of Digital Innovation. He is teaching MSc in Blockchain and Digital Currency at UNIC, which is the first-degree programme, globally, on decentralized digital currencies and Blockchains. Also, he is participating in several EC-funded projects focused on Blockchain technologies. Dr Iosif has a PhD degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering. His areas of expertise include Blockchain, Machine Learning, and Human Language Technologies (Natural Language Processing, Spoken Dialogue Systems). In addition, he has experience in the respective startup ecosystem, and has authored/co-authored over 75 peer-reviewed scientific publications.

**Dr Marius Rohde Johannessen** is Associate Professor in Information Systems at the University College of Southeast Norway. He received his PhD on social media use in eParticipation from the University of Agder in 2013 and has a background in the media industry. His research interests are on eParticipation and eGovernment, with emphasis on understanding how the government can apply new media technologies to improve communication with citizens. His work has been published in Information Systems and eGovernment journals and conferences such as *Communications of the AIS* and the *International Journal of Public Information Systems*.

**Dr Evgenia Kapassa** received her BSc in Digital Systems from the Department of Digital Systems, University of Piraeus, Greece. Her thesis topic was “Web Platform for the storage and retrieval of geo-referenced data in 3D models”, where she delivered a set of innovative mechanisms, and focused on data as a service technologies. She received her MSc in Advanced Information Systems, also from the Department of Digital Systems, University of Piraeus, Greece, in 2019. Her diploma thesis was a “Complete Service Level Agreements Management Framework in 5G Environments”, focusing on the management of the network services’ lifecycle, taking into consideration QoS and QoE parameters from heterogeneous recourses. Between 2017 and 2019 she was a researcher at the Research Center of the University of Piraeus, focusing on 5G/SDN networks with an emphasis not only on technology-specific aspects but also on real-world cases-adopting cutting-edge and high-impact approaches that address the ICT industry needs. In 2024 Evgenia completed her PhD studies at the University of Nicosia, Cyprus. Her PhD dissertation focuses on the investigation of the V2G/V2V energy trading concept using Blockchain technology.
