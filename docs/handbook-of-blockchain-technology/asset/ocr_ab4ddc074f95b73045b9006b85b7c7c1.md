Currently, the impact of NFTs in the Metaverse cannot be predicted accurately, as we are still in the early stages of this area. New technical developments, NFTs, and Metaverse use cases and utilities are expected to uncover even more dynamics, not only within the developing Metaverse ecosystem, but across the entire digital landscape. There is, therefore, a need for more systematic research on this topic, and we hope that the identified NFT dynamics reported in this chapter will help researchers and organizations to better understand and investigate this complex environment. The identified dynamics can serve as guidelines for future research in this domain.
