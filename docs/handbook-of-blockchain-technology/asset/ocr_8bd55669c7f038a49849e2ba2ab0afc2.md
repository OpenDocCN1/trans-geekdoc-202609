However there seems to be no agreement on the remaining layers despite their importance. Based on our insights from the mentioned approaches we suggest that the following layers are essential in the Metaverse: (a) experiences; (b) interactivity; (c) discovery; (d) AI; (e) content creation; (f) spatial computing; (g) decentralization; (h) Metaverse economy; (i) protocols and standards; (j) governance; (k) security; and (l) infrastructure. These layers have been discussed in the mentioned approaches. The layers not included in our proposal from sections 1.4.1–1.4.4 of the approaches are:

+   - Zhang (2022): (a) integration; (b) services components; and (c) QoS layers. While these are crucial aspects of Metaverse architecture, they refer to sub-elements within a layer.
+   - Ball (2021) explores user behavior, emphasizing social aspects over technical ones in their exploration of this layer. Despite how user behavior influences interactions, in the Metaverse Ball’s (2022) analysis seems to overlook user behavior as an aspect of the Metaverse. As a result, the choice to leave out the user behavior layer from the discussion appears consistent across both of Ball’s works.

To aid in comparing and analyzing the perspectives of each layer in Sections 1.4.1–1.4.5, a designation is outlined. This designation consists of the letter of the author who introduced that layer followed by its numerical position. For instance, the Radoff seventh layer is denoted as R7.
