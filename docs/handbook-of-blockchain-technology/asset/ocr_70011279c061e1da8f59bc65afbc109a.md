Reynolds (2023) suggested that Bitcoin Request for Comment 20 could be considered equivalent to ERC-20 tokens within the context of Bitcoin, although there are differences between the two mainly due to the absence of smart contract functionality in BRC-20. As depicted in Table 5.2, there are differences between ERC-20 (Christodoulou et al., 2022) and BRC-20, such as the blockchain they operate on, the consensus algorithm they use, and the features, tools, and smart contracts utilized. There are also differences in terms of the infrastructure, the maturity level, the block time, transaction execution speed, transaction fees, and types of applications they can support.

BRC-20 tokens were initially designed to support the deployment, minting, and transfer of Bitcoin NFTs (Domo, 2023). Their introduction has transformed Bitcoin NFTs into an environment that enables the convergence of Bitcoin NFTs and memecoins to give rise to something new. Subsequent to the integration of BRC-20, a remarkable increase in the proliferation of memecoins within the Bitcoin network has been observed. Notably, tokens such as Ordinals ($ORDI), Pepe ($PEPE), Meme ($MEME), and numerous others have emerged.

The first token contract that was created using BRC-20 was $ORDI. $ORDI pays tribute to Bitcoin as its maximum supply is 21 million tokens, and each $ORDI token is backed by 1 Satoshi. The introduction of $ORDI generated significant excitement, and all 21 million $ORDI tokens were minted in less than 24 hours.

Shortly after, other tokens like $PUNK, $PEPE, and $MEME also made their appearance and attracted the attention of the crypto community. $ORD, $PEPE, and $MEME are particularly well-known, experiencing recent price increases. Some notable price fluctuations have been associated with these memecoins, including:

+   - Milady Meme Coin ($LADYS): On May 11, 2023, $LADYS experienced a huge increment of 13,000 percent in a single day. More than likely this increment was influenced by a Tweet made by Elon Musk.
+   - Bob ($BOB) went through an increase of 12,000 percent in one week (April 28 to May 5, 2023).
+   - Wojak ($WOJAK): During the same period as $BOB, Wojak ($WOJAK) had a growth of 1,650 percent.
