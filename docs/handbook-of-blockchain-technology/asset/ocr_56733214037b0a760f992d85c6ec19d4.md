# 16.5  CONCLUSIONS AND NEXT STEPS

This chapter serves as a theoretical foundation for an innovative approach to DRM that combines blockchain technology and IoV. It suggests a path towards a more effective, efficient, and sustainable energy management framework by capitalizing on the unique strengths of these technologies. The chapter introduces a conceptual model that provides a more engaging and reliable DRM system by building on blockchain features like transparency, immutability, and decentralization. The relationships between the proposed model’s variables highlight their interdependence and demonstrate the complexity of DRM. Each of these variables influences the effectiveness of DRM and contributes to the development of a complex system. For instance, tokenized incentives and transparent distribution can motivate user participation. Also, personalized charging schedules and user profiles provide a tailored approach to energy management. The proposed model sheds light on future possibilities in this area. The convergence of IoV, DRM, and blockchain technology points toward innovative solutions for energy management.

The next steps include conducting an in-depth analysis of the proposed model, utilizing a qualitative research methodology based on multiple case studies. The objective is to study the dynamics and complexities of this topic through in-depth interviews and conversations with various IoV-ecosystem players, as well as specialists in blockchain technology and DRM. The aim of these interviews is to gather insights, opinions, and knowledge from various experts to better understand the practical implications and challenges associated with managing energy demand, charging coordination, and information privacy. The goal is to empirically evaluate the proposed conceptual model. As a result, the outcome of these interviews will be thoroughly analyzed and presented in a forthcoming research paper. This will allow for a more thorough examination of the empirical aspects of the subject at hand. Future research should concentrate on refining the model and addressing potential challenges such as scalability, energy efficiency of blockchain transactions, as well as regulatory concerns.
