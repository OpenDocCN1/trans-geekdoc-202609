# 2.4.1 Artistic Expression and Identity

Artistic expression and identity depict fundamental aspects of self-representation and human creativity. This facet of interaction has been extended into the metaverse through the freedom provided by the digital landscape (Saw and Chan, 2023). To understand how cryptocurrencies and NFTs are used in the metaverse to exhibit artistic expression and identity, we must first understand what this entails within AR. This umbrella term serves to represent how an individual conveys their creativity or projection of self onto the metaverse’s landscape. This can take the form of virtually any content creation, as traditional art, music, or visual representation of one’s creativity resembles this categorization (Alston, 2023).

The art scene, specifically in the metaverse, has become one of the most prevalent sectors since the technology’s creation. Blockchain technology first developed its art sector in the form of NFTs, as digital content creators leveraged the unique technology to display new forms of their craft. This dates to 2018 when artist Jason Bailey’s tech blog, Artnome, declared, ‘art native to the blockchain has its own aesthetic and represents a new and important movement within art. This movement/aesthetic is called CryptoArt’ (Macedo et al., 2022).

This form of expression, initially coined ‘CryptoArt,’ has evolved with blockchain technology into what became the metaverse. For example, established auction houses have been engaging with NFTs for several years now. This is displayed by Sotheby’s launching its own NFT platform, Sotheby’s Metaverse, in October 2021. The first offerings were ten unminted Chromie Squiggles by NFT artist Snowfro. The concept for the work is based on generative minting, where an algorithm created by the artist determines the outcome for each individual buyer (Macedo et al., 2022).

The power of these NFTs within the metaverse has decentralized the power of content selection. Intermediaries such as studios, record labels, and art galleries now hold no authority over content creators due to the utilization of metaverse artistic creation applications. This decentralization encourages more diverse content that suits consumers’ heterogeneous preferences. With this technology, NFTs promote the ability to have fractionalized ownership of content and art. For example, SCs allow creators to diversify ownership of their art out to fans. These co-owners can then receive portions of the revenue generated from this fractionalized art (Malik et al., 2022).
