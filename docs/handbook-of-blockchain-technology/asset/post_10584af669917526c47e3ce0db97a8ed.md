## 3.4 FORCES DRIVING NFTs’ ADOPTION IN THE METAVERSE

NFTs enhance Metaverse functionality and applicability by introducing innovative opportunities for digital representation, ownership, and economic dynamics. Despite NFTs’ advantages, there are challenges and complexities that need to be considered and addressed. This section seeks to establish a comprehensive understanding of this area by exploring various forces that influence the interplay between NFTs and the Metaverse. In the following paragraphs, we explain these NFTs’ challenges to unlock their full dynamics within the Metaverse. As depicted in Figure 3.2, challenges like interoperability, NFTs’ valuation, user interface and experience, scalability, sustainability, financing and DeFi synergies, and legal and intellectual property considerations, influence the adoption of NFTs and the Metaverse.

- **Interoperability:** The emergence of multiple Metaverse platforms increases the need for NFT integration across different platforms. A major barrier to NFT integration lies in achieving interoperability across different Metaverse platforms. This is mainly attributed to the fact that many of them are based on different blockchains. This makes it difficult to trade or transfer Metaverse digital assets across different ecosystems due to the different architectures and standards of the blockchains used. The lack of standardized formats for NFTs is an obstacle to their broader adoption while at the same time limiting their utility. Specifically, different blockchains operate under varied protocols and token standards, making universal compatibility a complex task. Interesting efforts to address NFT interoperability are identified in cross-chain or multichain NFTs. In fact, cross-chain NFTs refer to the parallel bridging between compatible blockchain networks. They enable the movement of NFTs between similar blockchain architectures (e.g., EVM-based blockchains), like Ethereum to Optimism/Polygon as an example. A significant representative of this approach is Holograph’s Holographic Assets (Holograph, 2023). For instance, Holographic Assets enable NFTs to have the same contract address and token ID across all EVM-based environments. The outcome of this is to simplify ownership and transfers between different blockchains.

- The **‘burn-and-mint’ technique** is an approach that is used for interoperability. Using this technique, NFTs are “burned” on one blockchain and re-minted on another. An exemplary case of this approach can be considered the y00t’s collection transition from Solana to Polygon (Crypto Times, 2023). A slightly different method that is used is the ‘lock-and-mint’ method. Although the two techniques are similar, they have differences too. With burn-and-mint methods, NFTs are “burned” on the source blockchain by sending them to an unspendable address, making this action irreversible. Then, the NFTs could be minted on the destination blockchain, maintaining the same properties and value as their “burned” version. Lock-and-mint methods “lock” NFTs on the source blockchain by freezing their state to avoid transfers or other changes. Then, new NFTs are minted on the destination blockchain that represent the locked asset on the source blockchain. Also, in lock-and-mint methods, the “frozen” NFTs on the source blockchain can be unlocked, and the newly minted NFTs on the destination blockchain are burned. Lock-and-mint methods are gaining popularity over burn-and-mint methods. This is due to the ability of the lock-and-mint method to ensure that NFTs retain their uniqueness, ownership details, and other intrinsic properties when moving between blockchains, facilitating easy and seamless movement of NFTs without the complexities of traditional bridging methods, while also being reversible. LayerZero (LayerZero Labs, 2023) protocol is a good example of lock-and-mint methods, as it provides a framework for moving NFTs across different blockchains. A notable case of the lock-and-mint method is the Lil Pudgys bridge transfers (Pudgy Penguins, 2023). Another NFT interoperability option is the Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP) (ChainLink, 2023). This could be classified under the lock-and-mint techniques and is available on the ChainLink blockchain.

- **User Interface and Experience:** Complexities imposed by NFTs and blockchain technologies have an impact on the user interface and experience, which according to Mosco (2023), is considered a barrier to Metaverse adoption. For example, the complexities related to NFT management may lead to low user engagement. For that reason, the improvement of these interfaces and transaction mechanisms is important to enhance the user experience. This will have a positive impact as it will reduce latency and increase the overall speed and efficiency of NFT exchanges within the Metaverse. Addressing these challenges requires innovative technical solutions. In this direction, the enhancement of digital wallet integrations is pivotal for efficient management and transfer of NFTs within Metaverse platforms. Additionally, cross-chain technology solutions, such as those referred to above (ChainLink, 2023; Holograph, 2023; LayerZero Labs, 2023) and the development of robust APIs and SDKs for developers are marked as important factors in the process of facilitating easier integration of NFTs into gaming and Metaverse platforms. These solutions aim to streamline the transaction mechanisms, reduce latency, and enhance the overall speed of NFT exchanges, thereby improving the user experience and platform efficiency (Wang et al., 2023).

- **Legal and Intellectual Property Considerations:** Metaverse and NFT applications are global and decentralized in nature and go beyond national borders and legal jurisdictions. This results in an environment that has an additional layer of complexity for NFT integration into the Metaverse; the legal layer. (2022), the evaluation of legal and regulatory risks is critical, leading at the same time to a series of questions that need to be answered. Essentially, there is a need for tailored laws and regulations to address issues related to virtual property ownership and ensure user privacy (Kalyvaki, 2023).
