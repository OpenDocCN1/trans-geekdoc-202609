## Contributors

**Dr João Barata** is an Assistant Professor at the Department of Informatics Engineering, Faculty of Sciences and Technology of the University of Coimbra, Portugal. He is also a researcher at CISUC – Centre for Informatics and Systems of the University of Coimbra, and was invited to join the Polytechnic Institute of Coimbra in 2016 as an Adjunct Professor of Information Systems (IS). João has 22 years of experience as an IS unit manager and consultant and has worked with over 300 public and private organizations in Europe, America, and Africa. João holds a Licenciatura (five-year) degree in Industrial Engineering, an MSc in Informatics Engineering, and a PhD in Information Systems. His main research interests are digital transformation, enterprise architecture, data governance, supply chain management, and industry 4.0/5.0. João’s work has been published in several journals, including *Business & Information Systems Engineering*, *Journal of Manufacturing Technology Management*, *Journal of Engineering and Technology Management*, *Journal of Enterprise Information Management*, *Applied Soft Computing*, and *Total Quality Management & Business Excellence*, and he has presented at top IS conferences, like ICIS, ECIS, AMCIS, and PACIS.

**Professor Lasse Berntzen** is a Professor in the School of Business, University of South-Eastern Norway. Since 2003, his research has focused on the Information Society and ICT in the public sector, with an emphasis on e-government and e-democracy. During the last few years his research has focused on smart cities and smart communities. He has been involved in several research projects at national and international levels, author and co-author of several conference papers and articles, and program committee member and reviewer for some of the most important conferences within his field of research. His current research interests include automatic assessment of e-government services, different aspects of e-participation, and the use of geographic information systems to improve public decision-making and democratic participation, and the development of smart city concepts and technology. In 2007 and 2008, he worked on regulatory aspects of e-democracy as an expert for the Council of Europe’s Ad-hoc Committee on e-democracy. He has also been a reviewer for the European Commission. Lasse Berntzen received his Candidate of Science degree in Computer Science from the University of Oslo. His thesis work focused on object-oriented modelling of workflow processes. Since then, he has worked both as an academic and as a computer professional. During his professional career, he has written textbooks on computer networks, systems development, and web technology. In 2009, he was appointed fellow of the International Academy, Research and Industry Association (IARIA). He has been on the steering committee of ICDS since 2007, Cyberlaws since 2010, and COLLA since 2011, CENTRIC since 2013, and SMART since 2015. He is currently a member of IFIP WG 8.5: Information Systems in Public Administration, and is also a member of AIS, ACM, and IEEE.

**Dr Klitos Christodoulou** is a faculty member at the Department of Management and MIS – Digital Currency, at the University of Nicosia (UNIC). He is also a researcher at the Institute For the Future (IFF) at the same University since 2018. He holds a BSc in Computer Science and an MSc in Advanced Computer Science – with specialization in Advanced Applications, both from the University of Manchester, UK. In 2014, Klitos obtained his PhD in Computer Science from the School of Computer Science at the University of Manchester, UK. He has been an adjunct staff member of the Information Management Group (IMG) in the School of Computer Science, at the University of Manchester, where he engaged in various research and teaching activities. Klitos has served on the Program Committee of a variety of conferences. Currently, he serves as an Associate Editor at Frontiers Blockchain Journal and Guest Editor on the special issue of Future Internet (MDPI Journal) on Blockchain Applications. His research interests span both Data Management challenges, with a focus on Machine Learning techniques, and Distributed Ledger Technologies; with an emphasis on Blockchain ledgers. He has given numerous invited talks and tutorials involving Blockchain technologies. Klitos also teaches a course on Blockchain Applications under UNIC's MSc in Digital Currency programme.

**Professor Gianfranco d'Atri** studied Mathematics in Pisa and continued his education in Paris at the Pierre and Marie Curie University, where he had the opportunity to consolidate and enrich his skills in the field before becoming a Professor at the University of Calabria (UNICAL). He taught Statistics and Operations Research, and later, Economics Informatics and General Informatics, before focusing on Blockchain. He is appointed Professor of Blockchain Technology and directs the interdisciplinary research group of the Department of Computer Science “Blockchain and Cryptocurrencies”. Gianfranco has numerous scientific publications in both computer science and mathematics and published the book “From Pythagoras to Schützenberger”. He is the scientific coordinator of several R&D projects and has developed the first example of a token wallet within a university campus.

**Dr Gregoris Demetriou** worked for more than 40 years in the Training Division of the Human Resource Development Authority of Cyprus (HRDA) from where he retired at the position of Deputy Director of Training Services. During his time at HRDA, in addition to his normal duties, he served for many years as the Secretary of the governing committee of HRDA's Medical Fund and as the Chairman of the governing committee of HRDA's Welfare Fund. He represented HRDA to the Board of Directors of the Foundation for the Management of European Lifelong Learning Programmes (the National Authority for the Erasmus+ Programme) for four years till August 2023. He is an active committee member of various professional bodies such as the Cyprus Human Resource Management Association (CyHRMA), the Institute of Certified Public Accountants (ICPAC) and the Cyprus Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA Cyprus). Gregoris' studies include Electronics Engineering (BSc University of Westminster, London), Marketing (Chartered Institute of Marketing, UK), European Policy Law and Management (MSc Robert Gordon University, Aberdeen), Accounting (FCCA Fellow of Chartered Association of Certified Accountants, UK), Auditing (CIA Certified Internal Auditor, USA) and Business Administration (PhD European University, Cyprus). His research interests are in the areas of Training and Development, Learning Capability, Organisational Learning and Learning Organisations. His PhD dissertation title is “Investigating the Relationship among Learning Propensity, Capabilities and effectiveness of Organisational Learning”. Gregoris has also authored various journal and conference articles.
