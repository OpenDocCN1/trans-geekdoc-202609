In this chapter, we shed light on the design and features of the BRI, which is used to measure blockchain adoption at the country level. BRI is the outcome of a research project at the University of Nicosia and seeks to provide a comprehensive assessment of blockchain engagement worldwide. It focuses on four main pillars to offer an understanding of each country’s readiness for blockchain adoption. These four pillars include regulation, technological advancements, industry activity, and local interest. BRI assesses established frameworks of technological readiness indexes and builds upon them to offer advanced metrics and features designed for the blockchain ecosystem.

BRI addresses a gap that exists today in the blockchain space. It satisfies the needs of various entities involved in the blockchain ecosystem. The spectrum of the whole BRI service seems ideal for stakeholders operating in the public and private sectors. These may include policymakers, industry executives, and investors. The innovative BRI mechanism equips users with valuable insights that are tailored to users’ preferences. As blockchain technology continues to disrupt traditional business models across industries, tools like BRI play a crucial role in navigating this transformation. The additional versions and features beyond the BRI Standard Version allow for flexibility and customization according to the needs and preferences of institutions and individual stakeholders. The accuracy of the country rankings seems to have been proven ideal through scientific ground truth evaluation.

The expected outcome of a global adoption of BRI metrics is that industry stakeholders will be able to run their operations more efficiently in top-ranked nations and assist in their decision-making and legal management. Consequently, the BRI service aims to provide developing nations with resources and paradigms that can be used to enable blockchain technology adoption, which can serve as a solution for many local internal business processes.

Our future research on this topic is focusing on gathering feedback regarding the usefulness of the tool directly from the targeted users. To achieve this optimal evaluation assessment, a plan is set to be implemented during the following months. The fully operational BRI service will be distributed to blockchain businesses, communities, and policy makers operating in the public and private sectors. The aim is to gather feedback regarding the usefulness of this tool in relevant decision-making procedures. Internal evaluation will additionally be conducted by following the long-term status of operations in several assessed countries. Advancements of startups and policies operating in top-ranked countries will prove that the research has produced a valuable service. Further future work includes annual index updates and additional features based on feedback from active users.
