| Karamchandani et al. (2020) | *Perception-based Model for Analyzing the Impact of Enterprise Blockchain Adoption on SCM in the Indian Service Industry* | Enterprise Blockchain is a permissioned blockchain (EBC), an enterprise service system. This essay examines EBC practitioners' views. The research examines whether EBC's perceived efficacy is based on theory or hype. It measures EBC benefits. SEM tests hypotheses. The authors use the technology acceptance model for service SCM's concept of perceived utility to quantify EBC's usefulness for six supply chain characteristics. The article examines whether service sector managers regard EBC as profitable in service SCM. This study shows how EBC may boost service SCM profitability. |
| Yadav et al. (2020) | *Blockchain Technology Adoption Barriers in the Indian Agricultural Supply Chain: An Integrated Approach* | India's frequent food poisonings raise safety worries. Agriculture Supply Chain quality is important (ASC), ASC was improved with technology. This paper investigates Indian ASC blockchain adoption. Five agro-organizations, academics, and agro-stakeholders provided comments. Integrated ISM-DEMATEL integrates links and quantifies intensity for another cognitive tested using sensitivity analysis. Lack of government regulation and agro-stakeholder distrust impede Indian ASC blockchain development. The research's management implications will help the government and connected entities overcome blockchain difficulties in Indian ASC. |
