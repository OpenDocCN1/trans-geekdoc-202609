+   - **Voting-based algorithms:** In voting-based algorithms a democratic voting process takes place which results in the selection of validators though voting. Compared to proof-based algorithms, voting-based are considered more scalable but this may have an impact on decentralization as a smaller number of elected validators participates in the process. Voting-based algorithms have evolved to address scalability limitations of traditional approaches like pBFT. For example, the Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP) employs Federated Byzantine Agreement (FBA) to achieve consensus while maintaining open membership and network scalability (Kim et al., 2019). Ripple Protocol CA (RPCA) employs a similar approach, using a selected set of trusted nodes to reach agreement rapidly (Christodoulou et al., 2020). Nonetheless, these algorithms may sacrifice a certain degree of decentralization in favor of improved scalability.
