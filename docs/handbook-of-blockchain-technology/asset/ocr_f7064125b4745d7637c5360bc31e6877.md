# 1.6 INTRODUCING THE METAVERSE FIVE LAYERS APPROACH (M5LA)

The previous section pointed out that current approaches for organizing Metaverse layers may not cover all aspects. To fill this gap, we suggest the Metaverse Five Layers Approach (MSLA) as a framework for this topic. To make the Metaverse architectural layers easier to grasp, the MSLA is based on five layers, each consisting of sublayers. The top three layers positioned horizontally include the (a) infrastructure, (b) platform, and (c) application layers. In addition, there are two overarching layers: (a) interoperability, and (b) security, which support the functioning of all layers. A simplified overview of the MSLA approach is shown in Figure 1.2, with Figure 1.3 providing a more detailed view of the MSLA approach.

As illustrated in Figure 1.2, the five layers are subdivided into ten sublayers, as detailed in the below paragraphs.

**Layer 1 – Infrastructure:** The Metaverse is built upon its infrastructure, which includes the hardware and networks for creating and supporting platforms and applications. This includes cloud computing, storage systems, processing power (Central Processing Unit (CPU) or Graphical Processing Unit (GPU)), virtualization technologies like machines and containers, as well as high-speed networks such as 5G and fiber optic connections. These components are important for facilitating reliable interactions among users in the Metaverse.

**Layer 2 – Platform:** The Metaverse platform layer consists of four sublayers, with each one of them supporting operations related to the development, management, and operation of the Metaverse applications and content. The platform sublayers are presented below and include: (a) spatial computing, (b) Artificial Intelligence, (c) decentralization, and (d) content creation.

+   - **Sublayer 2.1 – Spatial computing** is a key building block of the Metaverse platform as it blends the digital and physical worlds to create virtual spaces. It handles a lot of information and turns real-world actions into lively virtual adventures. This sublayer combines various technologies like AR, VR, and MR, sensors, and other technologies (Bourlakis et al., 2009; Xie et al., 2018).
