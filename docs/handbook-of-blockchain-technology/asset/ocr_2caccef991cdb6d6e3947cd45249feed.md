# 5.2.2 Utilizing Ordinals for Bitcoin NFT Creation and Ownership

According to Bitcoin theory, each Bitcoin contains 100,000,000 Satoshis (Antonopoulos, 2015). Each Satoshi has a unique serial number that marks the position-order of a Satoshi in the list, based on the order in which they were mined and transferred (order of transaction inputs and outputs). For that reason, they are called ordinals. The initial Satoshi within the genesis block is assigned the ordinal number 0, followed by the second Satoshi with ordinal number 1, and this sequence continues. In line with Ordinal theory, these ordinal numbers serve as consistent markers for the data linked to Satoshis. For example, on January 3, 2009, 50 bitcoins were mined in the Bitcoin genesis block. This means that there are 4,999,999,999 Satoshis (5 billion Ordinals minus 1) mined in that block. It is estimated that the maximum production of NFT Ordinals is limited to 2.1 quadrillion minus 1 due to the finite supply of Bitcoins, as well as the fact that each Bitcoin consists of 100,000,000 Satoshis.
