SM has been beneficial for society as it connects people and allows them to share information and engage with content. From a business point of view, SM supports various functions such as communication, marketing, community building, brand promotion and customer engagement.

**2009 – Internet of Value (Blockchain):** The emergence of Blockchain technology in 2009 marked the beginning of a new phase in Internet evolution; the Internet of Value. Blockchain evolved to overcome problems related to lack of trust, transparency, centralized decision making and so on. It was initially used to support the operation of cryptocurrencies, but it was soon realized that its characteristics can support the exchange of value (Cunha et al., 2021; Karamitsos et al., 2022; Lacity, 2020).

**2020 – Internet of Intelligence:** The fifth phase of the Internet evolution focuses on Artificial Intelligence (AI). Although AI is not a new technology, it has gained considerable recognition in recent years especially with the introduction of ChatGPT in June 2020 and the developments in Large Language Models (LLMs) and Natural Language Processing (NLP) (Makridakis et al., 2023). Today, there is a plethora of AI tools like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot that support people and businesses (Gill and Kaur, 2023; Kashyap, 2023). In addition, AI tools can increase efficiency and productivity across various sectors. From a different perspective, AI raises a lot of concerns regarding social, legal, and ethical issues, and thus there is a need for further investigation.

**2020 – Internet of Worlds (Metaverse):** The Metaverse, represents a space where virtual and physical realities converge. While significant Metaverse applications like Second Life (2003) and Roblox (2006) emerged before 2020, we choose to designate the beginning of this era in 2020 to highlight the rise of Open Metaverses which are Blockchain based and decentralized (Themistocleous et al., 2022). In the years that followed we have witnessed a notable increase in virtual worlds platforms that leverage Blockchain technology (e.g. Decentraland, The Sandbox). Blockchain based Metaverse supports true ownership of virtual assets, decentralized governance, and novel possibilities for user-driven content creation (Christodoulou et al., 2022; Lacity and Treiblmaier, 2022). The Metaverse has the potential to redefine various aspects of our lives including social interactions, entertainment, and the economy. Within this virtual world, users can actively participate in shared experiences and overcome the limitations of the existing 2D online environments. As the Metaverse advances, it is expected to reshape our engagement and interaction with these digital spaces. This will lead to new ways for collaboration and creativity.

In comparison to the previous phases of the Internet evolution, the Metaverse integrates them all resulting in the next generation of the Internet. For instance, it operates over the WWW, and leverages SM for diverse activities, like community building or user interactions. Controls wearables haptic devices headsets and other devices that are used to interact with the Metaverse have sensors and IoT embedded in them to improve and facilitate interaction and create a more immersive experience. A few Metaverse features that are supported by Blockchain technology are (a) decentralization; (b) the digital economy; and (c) the creation sharing and administration of diverse digital assets. Blockchain technology supports various aspects of Metaverse such as (a) decentralization; (b) digital economy; and (c) the creation, distribution, and management of various digital assets. In addition, AI finds application across many areas related to the Metaverse. These range from avatar development and customization to generative digital asset creation. AI is also used in specialized algorithms that improve Metaverse performance, productivity, and efficiency.
