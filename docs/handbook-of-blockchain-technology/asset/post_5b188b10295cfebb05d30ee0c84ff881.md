Ensuring that NFTs are compatible across different legal jurisdictions is important for their continuous transfer and value, yet pose a significant challenge given the decentralized ethos of blockchain technology and the “meta” space of blockchain-enabled Metaverse platforms.

- **Scalability:** With the increasing NFT adoption and the emergence of Metaverse platforms, the demand for scalable solutions has become necessary. The efficiency of such platforms to handle a significant volume of NFT transactions has become more critical today, and immediate actions are required. Implementing blockchain scalability solutions, such as StarkNet, Arbitrum, Polygon, and many more, is essential to handle high volumes of NFT transactions without compromising transaction speed or security (Kalodner et al., 2018; StarkNet, 2023). Scalability solutions are significant to maintain the efficiency and user experience of NFT transactions in the Metaverse. They also ensure that the infrastructure can support the growing demand and complexity of NFT transactions. Furthermore, the latest developments in the blockchain ecosystem related to the emergence of modular protocols both as Layer 1 (underlying main blockchain architecture) or Layer 2 (solutions that lie on top of the underlying blockchain solutions) denote the shift to more complex transactions and increase the utility of NFTs and digital assets in the digital environment. For example, the Celestia modular data availability (DA) protocol enables specialization and optimization of modular blockchains for a given function (Al-Bassam, 2019; MantleNetwork, 2024; MantaNetwork, 2023). We observe that scalability challenges become more critical as the adoption of NFTs increases across multiple Metaverses. On the other hand, we notice that the latest developments in addressing this challenge are in the right direction.

- **NFT Market Valuation:** Another significant challenge in the NFT landscape is the valuation of its market cap. The importance of an accurate NFT market valuation lies in the valuable insights it provides about the size and potential of this market. This will encourage more institutional investors to join the NFT ecosystem and help its growth. Therefore, there are many attempts to establish a methodology that accurately calculates it, as NFTs are currently a multi-billion-dollar market and continue to grow. We believe that the NFT market is going to far exceed the fungible tokens market cap, which is currently near $3 trillion (at the time this work is written), within the next few years. Our position is based on the hypothesis that real-life assets, like real estate, gold, and so on, will be tokenized. Today we observe many attempts at asset tokenization, such as the real estate tokenization platform FUTURESTATE Alliance (https://futurestatealliance.com/). This trend is expected to grow in the future.

The methods used for the valuation of fungible tokens cannot be considered for NFTs as the valuation of the former derives from the multiplication of the token’s reference price by the current circulating supply. This cannot be applied to NFTs as the latter are unique tokens and they cannot be valued in this simple manner. - Financing and DeFi Synergies: The funding of NFTs and their synergies with decentralized finance (DeFi) is an additional challenge that impacts the wider adoption of NFTs. The important role of Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) for community-driven governance is noteworthy, as reported by Liu et al. (2021) and Papangelou et al. (2023). In a decentralized digital interconnected world where Metaverses will thrive, DAOs will enable token holders to collectively guide the direction, policies, and strategies for various cases, including NFT projects and Metaverse platforms. DAOs enable members to vote on key decisions, from platform updates to community events, promoting a decentralized and democratic governance model. While the adoption of DAOs represents important benefits in various aspects, managing a diverse group of stakeholders with varying interests can be challenging. Additionally, operating DAOs in a relatively new space, such as the Metaverse, with evolving legal regulatory frameworks, presents challenges in compliance and recognition. This chapter investigates the intersection of NFTs and the Metaverse and explains their evolution, challenges, and the dynamics of NFTs in virtual worlds. The Metaverse is an emerging and fast-moving area that has recently embraced blockchain technology to enrich and extend its application. One fundamental blockchain technology that has had a great impact on the Metaverse is NFTs. NFTs have already transformed industries and redefined interactions in the Metaverse. With the changing business landscape, technological advancements, and paradigm shifts, NFTs are set to drive innovation and influence this area.

We observe that NFTs are a catalyst of change in the Metaverse; not only do they represent digital assets, but are a driving force for virtual worlds. They assist the Metaverse in various ways, ranging from supporting its economy and shaping its interactions to redefining its boundaries. They are also instrumental in redefining ownership, identity, and value in these digital spaces. NFTs are significant as they play a key role in many industries, like digital art, gaming, and entertainment.

- Provide verifiable and secure mechanisms to establish digital assets’ ownership and authenticity in the Metaverse.
- Facilitate the development of new economic models within the Metaverse.
- Foster economic mobility by removing geographical boundaries through the Metaverse.

In this chapter, we identify dynamics (driving forces) that influence NFTs’ application in the Metaverse. We reviewed the theory (literature) and practice in this space to identify seven driving forces, as reported above. These include (a) interoperability, (b) user interface and experience, (c) legal and intellectual property, (d) scalability, (e) NFT market valuation, (f) NFT sustainability, and (g) financing and DeFi synergies. To provide a more comprehensive understanding, we explain these dynamics and provide examples and calls for future action.
