However, despite these promising benefits, tokenization also presents several challenges that need to be addressed. One of the primary concerns is the regulatory environment, which is still evolving and varies across jurisdictions. Ensuring compliance can be complex and challenging. Another issue is the need to maintain a clear link between the digital token and the real-world asset it represents, to preserve the token’s value and credibility. Cybersecurity is another significant concern, given the vulnerability of blockchain-related businesses to cyber threats. The large attack surface of blockchain systems, coupled with the potential for attacks at various levels – from data and network layers to consensus, smart contract, and application layers – underscores the need for robust security measures. Moreover, issues related to data privacy, legacy structures, and transition risks also need to be considered. Balancing the need for transparency with privacy requirements can be tricky. Additionally, integrating tokenization with existing systems and structures can pose technical and operational challenges.
