1984 – The pioneering influence of William Gibson on Cyberspace and Virtual Reality: In 1984, William Gibson released his book entitled “Neuromancer” in which he refers to cyberspace. Despite being the third person writing about cyberspace, his influence on the field has been profound. Gibson’s work in popularizing Cyberspace had a great impact on the development of VR. His vision supported the development of immersive environments as well as shared digital spaces. His influence extends to shaping how society engages with ICT, resulting in new economic opportunities across industries such as entertainment, gaming, education, and healthcare (Tearle, 2023).

1992 – Introducing the term Metaverse: Neal Stephenson introduced the concept of the Metaverse in his science fiction novel entitled “Snow Crash” in the early 1990s. According to Stephenson, the Metaverse refers to a digital world that extends beyond the physical world. Its etymology comes from (a) the Greek word “Meta” which means after or beyond, and (b) the last part of the English word universe (verse). Stephenson’s work has positively impacted the areas of VR, online communities, and the Internet, as we will explain in the below sections.



Between the 1980s and 2000s, various games, online platforms, and early developments in the digital sphere assisted in shaping the area of virtual worlds. These diverse efforts influenced influenced the evolution of virtual spaces and established the foundations for the development of the Metaverse. The Habitat multiplayer online game for example was launched in 1986 as an early implementation in this space and contributed in shaping this ecosystem. It was the first game that introduced avatars, allowed players to represent themselves in the virtual world with personalized characters and assisted users interaction (Atherton, 2023).

This early use of avatars set an example for the importance of self-representation in online spaces which contributed to the ongoing evolution of avatars within the broader Metaverse concept (Lee et al., 2023). Moreover, other games-applications released before 2003 were instrumental in further developing the idea of immersive digital spaces and community interactions.
