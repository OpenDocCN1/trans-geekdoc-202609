1984 – The pioneering influence of William Gibson on Cyberspace and Virtual Reality: In 1984, William Gibson released his book entitled “Neuromancer” in which he refers to cyberspace. Despite being the third-person writing about cyberspace, his influence on the field has been profound. Gibson’s work in popularizing Cyberspace had a great impact on the development of VR. His vision supported the development of immersive environments as well as shared digital spaces. His influence extends to shaping how society engages with ICT, resulting in new economic opportunities across industries such as entertainment, gaming, education, and healthcare (Tearle, 2023).

1992 – Introducing the term Metaverse: Neal Stephenson introduced the concept of the Metaverse in his science fiction novel entitled “Snow Crash” in the early 1990s. According to Stephenson, the Metaverse refers to a digital world that extends beyond the physical world. Its etymology comes from (a) the Greek word “Meta” which means after or beyond, and (b) the last part of the English word universe (verse). Stephenson’s work has positively impacted the areas of VR, online communities, and the Internet, as we will explain in the following sections.

Co-presence in the Metaverse is about sharing that space with others (friends, strangers, fellow users) and engaging in interactions with them. In both presence and co-presence, avatars or other digital representations are utilized within the virtual environment to facilitate interactions with others. The Metaverse enhances co-presence by offering an endless list of applications. For instance, family members or friends separated by geographical distances, can gather in the Metaverse to participate in shared activities like playing a game, chatting, watching a movie, and more. This enables them to overcome the constraints imposed by physical distance.

User engagement and other kinds of social interaction like communication, collaboration, relationship formation, and experience sharing are used in the Metaverse. Individuals, for example, might engage in social interactions by attending virtual events or collaborating on various tasks (e.g., projects). Using their avatars, the participants can express their emotions through digital gestures. Moreover, they can build relationships through shared activities like online gaming or collaborative workspaces. The Metaverse supports a wide range of social experiences, mirroring and extending real-world interactions into the digital space.

Persistence is a very important functionality of the Metaverse designed to maintain modifications, interactions, and user engagements over time. For example, you are playing a Metaverse game that focuses on Mars exploration. Imagine that you are the first explorer on this planet. Upon your arrival you might consider setting up a base or constructing your own home. After logging out, you return later to find that more players have joined the virtual Mars community. You can now observe that apart from your house there are streets with additional houses, malls, and even businesses established by other players. Obviously, these developments made by the players persist over time and create a collaborative and continuously evolving experience.

Creativity in the Metaverse involves users' ability to express innovation, artistic talents, and generate digital content for customization and sharing. Based on this characteristic many open Metaverse platforms allow users to create their own experiences, or amend existing ones, or create artifacts (e.g., clothes, accessories, tools, weapons) that can be used by other users in the same or different Metaverse applications.

In order to create interactive experiences, the Metaverse responds in real time to user actions. For instance, imagine that you are participating in a virtual art exhibition. Because of this characteristic, the exhibition could dynamically adjust the display of artworks based on your preferences or other criteria, like the popularity of specific pieces.

Digital economy is a key feature of a virtual economic system within the Metaverse. Here, users participate in various transactions (e.g., buy, sell) related to digital items. Platforms like Decentraland, The Sandbox, and others exemplify this by offering dedicated marketplaces. Within these spaces, users engage in transactions such as buying and selling digital artwork, trading virtual real estate, and exchanging or swapping digital assets, such as in-game items.

Technologies utilized in the development of Metaverse experiences result in the production of realistic and captivating virtual worlds that draw users in and cause users to lose track of reality. Envision entering a virtual concert where you can engage with other attendees via your customized avatar and feel the energy of the crowd in addition to seeing the performance. The ability to provide users with higher levels of engagement and emotional attachment that improve the general quality of digital interactions is a critical success criterion. **Interoperability** is considered an important characteristic and also a challenge for the Metaverse. It focuses on the smooth interaction and compatibility between different virtual platforms and applications. For instance, imagine playing a game and seamlessly transporting your customized avatar, or transferring digital items (e.g., in-game assets, NFTs, etc.) into another entirely different game (or virtual world). This capacity to transfer and utilize digital assets across various platforms demonstrates interoperability. However, it is important to highlight that we are currently in the early stages of interoperability development within the Metaverse. While there has been some progress in this field, substantial research efforts are still required before achieving comprehensive interoperability.

**The 3Ps of the Metaverse.** In a similar fashion to the 5Ps of innovation (product, process, position, paradigm and platform) (Tidd and Bessant, 2013) or the 5Ps of marketing (product, price, place, promotion, and people) (Kotler and Keller, 2014) we define the 3Ps of the Metaverse based on the aforementioned characteristics. The 3Ps of the Metaverse are:

- **Presence:** user presence, co-presence, social presence and social interaction.

- **Persistence:** virtual worlds, virtual economy, and creativity.

- **Portability:** user creativity, data, economy, avatars, and more should be interoperable.

Duan et al. (2021) introduced TLA to explain the Metaverse. TLA identifies the following three layers:
