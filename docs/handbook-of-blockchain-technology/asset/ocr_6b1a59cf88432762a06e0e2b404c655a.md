While the adoption of DAOs represents important benefits in various aspects, managing a diverse group of stakeholders with varying interests can be challenging. This chapter investigates the intersection of NFTs and the Metaverse and explains their evolution, challenges, and the dynamics of NFTs in virtual worlds. The Metaverse is an emerging and fast-moving area that has recently embraced blockchain technology to enrich and extend its application. One fundamental blockchain technology that has had a great impact on the Metaverse is NFTs. NFTs have already transformed industries and redefined interactions in the Metaverse. With the changing business landscape, technological advancements, and paradigm shifts, NFTs are set to drive innovation and influence this area.

We observe that NFTs are a catalyst of change in the Metaverse; not only do they represent digital assets, but are a driving force for virtual worlds. They assist the Metaverse in various ways, ranging from supporting its economy and shaping its interactions to redefining its boundaries. They are also instrumental in redefining ownership, identity, and value in these digital spaces. NFTs are significant as they play a key role in many industries, like digital art, gaming, and entertainment.
