## 11. An algorithmic model for blockchain maturity levels: a cross-country perspective

Andreas Vlachos, Klitos Christodoulou, Elias Iosif and Marinos Themistocleous

### 11.1 INTRODUCTION

This chapter outlines the business model and implications of the Blockchain Readiness Index (BRI) (Vlachos et al., 2019), an ongoing project being developed by the IFF Research Team at the University of Nicosia. BRI aims to be a service with embedded tools and metrics built to assist users in monitoring the level of blockchain engagement per country (Vlachos et al., 2023). Blockchain engagement is assessed according to each country’s progress in four main pillars, all of which are discussed in this chapter. Assessed areas involve both the public and private sectors of each country.

BRI is a sophisticated index made up of a number of indicators, which are derived from a range of relatively updated sources. A mathematical formula is constructed to form a numerical country score with equal indicator weighting. The BRI service further enables users to adjust their customized weighting of indicators and obtain country scores and rankings that are adequate for their own preferences and business logic. This chapter outlines the BRI tools and features which are built to serve the business operations and decision-making of users. BRI users may represent any type of industry stakeholders such as institutions, regulators, policymakers, executives, investors, and enthusiasts involved in the blockchain space.

The “Index Methodology” evaluates how the indicators are selected, assessed, and weighted within a scientific formula to compute the country rankings of the BRI “Standard Version.” Further explanation is provided regarding the adjustment of weights to compute country rankings with the “Weight Adjustment Version” and the “Community-Driven Version.” These versions are considered ideal for business considerations in the public and private sectors, as they are customized according to the preferences of the user. This chapter explores additional BRI features that can be exploited by business-oriented end users, which mainly arise in the form of reports and charts. The evaluation part explores the practicality of this tool via the aggregation of judgments within the industry.

#### 11.1.1 Blockchain Use in the Public and Private Sectors

The emergence of blockchain technology is disrupting numerous business models and is shifting public and private interest towards operation procedures involving a degree of decentralization (Chen and Bellavitis, 2020). The advancement of blockchain technology in terms of scalability, speed, and authorization is able to enhance efficiency within procedures involving a variety of industries and sectors (Touloupou et al., 2024; Touloupou et al., 2022).
