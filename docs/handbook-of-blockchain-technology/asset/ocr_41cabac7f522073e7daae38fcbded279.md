# 11.3.4  Additional Features

Users accessing the BRI service are expected to derive from both the public and private sectors. According to their occupation and needs, the BRI service ideally should provide additional features exploring the industry’s landscape beyond the fixed and customized country ranking criteria. These features are likely to provide a comprehensive set of tools for industry stakeholders that can assist them in business decision-making procedures and simultaneously allow the research team to develop and advance the service’s metrics and tools annually.

+   - **Pillars Insights:** Community ratings based on Likert Scale, regarding the importance of each of the four pillars and review of each pillar’s developments compared to previous years.
+   - **Indicators Insights:** Access to numerical values, statistics, and charts derived from the index data feeds.
+   - **Individual Country Reports:** Access to reports outlining analytical data for specific countries, including representations of individual SWOT schemes.
+   - **Geographical Regions Rankings and Reports:** Extensive comparisons of closely-related countries in terms of continents and economic zones.
+   - **Option to ask for specific report and metrics:** Due to the obligation to annually improve the relevance and usability of the BRI service, there will be open community discussions and reception of recommendations that may contribute to further structural development.
+   - **Option to contribute:** The research team is open to contributions that may result in more accurate and/or broader data feeds, as well as synergies with local public and private sectors for evaluation purposes of the index.

# 11.4  EVALUATION

The accuracy of country rankings is a major experiment, which will be scientifically supported by this research. To prove the high degree of accuracy in the findings of the BRI “Standard Version,” which acts as the base for the additional versions and features, a systematic effort for the construction of a quantitative BRI ground truth is deployed.

Through surveys from independent blockchain stakeholders, an aggregation of country scores is implemented (based on their own judgment) and compared to the findings of the BRI experiment. Around 100 to 175 responses per country were collected for the majority of the assessed sample. For each country, the “Blockchain Experts” score was calculated according to the average of the respective responses. The evaluation metric is the Pearson rank correlation coefficient between the Blockchain Experts score and the BRI Standard Version scores.
