**Interoperability** is considered an important characteristic and also a challenge for the Metaverse. It focuses on the smooth interaction and compatibility between different virtual platforms and applications. For instance, imagine playing a game and seamlessly transporting your customized avatar, or transferring digital items (e.g. in-game assets, NFTs etc.) into another entirely different game (or virtual world). This capacity to transfer and utilize digital assets across various platforms demonstrates interoperability. However, it is important to highlight that we are currently in the early stages of interoperability development within the Metaverse. While there has been some progress in this field, substantial research efforts are still required before achieving comprehensive interoperability.

**The 3Ps of the Metaverse.** In a similar fashion to the 5Ps of innovation (product, process, position, paradigm and platform) (Tidd and Bessant, 2013) or the 5Ps of marketing (product, price, place, promotion, and people) (Kotler and Keller, 2014) we define the 3Ps of the Metaverse based on the aforementioned characteristics. The 3Ps of the Metaverse are:

+   - **Presence:** user presence, co-presence, social presence and social interaction.
+   - **Persistence:** virtual worlds, virtual economy, and creativity.
+   - **Portability:** user creativity, data, economy, avatars, and more should be interoperable.

# ### 1.4 A COMPREHENSIVE EXPLORATION OF METAVERSE ARCHITECTURAL LAYERS

Numerous approaches reported in the literature seek to explain the architectural layers of the Metaverse including: contributions from Duan et al. (2021), Zhang (2022), Alioth et al. (2022), Ball (2021), and Radoff (2021). The following sub-sections detail the proposals presented by these scholars.

# #### 1.4.1 Three-Layer Architecture (TLA)

Duan et al. (2021) introduced TLA to explain the Metaverse. TLA identifies the following three layers:

+   - **Layer 1 – Infrastructure layer (D1):** This layer offers the necessary technologies for the establishment of the Metaverse infrastructure and it includes: (a) computational power; (b) networking and communication; (c) storage infrastructure; and (d) Blockchain. While the first four elements are foundational components of the infrastructure layer, we believe that Blockchain should be placed on a separate layer above it.
+   - **Layer 2 – Interaction layer (D2)** focuses on the intersection of the physical and virtual worlds, and it includes the content creation interface, immersive user experience and digital twins. In our view, this layer should be enhanced with other important features such as the interaction technologies that are used to engage and interact with the Metaverse like VR headsets and haptic controllers. Given that this layer bridges the virtual and physical worlds it should be further extended to include the Metaverse economy as we consider this an essential element of this layer.
