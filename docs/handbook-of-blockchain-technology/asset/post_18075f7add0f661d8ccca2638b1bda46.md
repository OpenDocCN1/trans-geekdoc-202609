
## 11.2 RELATED WORK – TECHNOLOGICAL INDEXES USED FOR DECISION MAKING BY PUBLIC AND/OR PRIVATE SECTORS

A prime example of hybrid adoption in public and private sectors is the healthcare industry. The need for patients to maintain personalized healthcare data across medical providers and treatment sites paved the way for the rise of decentralized record management systems to handle Electronic Health Records (Eklund et al., 2016). The need for confidentiality and accountability of transacting parties is highlighted through the first use cases of blockchain technology, enhancing electronic public transactions within financial services firms (Mainelli, Smith, and Mainelli, 2015).

Governmental processes are often associated with time-consuming and costly procedures. Blockchain networks can be structured and customized in permissioned and permissionless environments to fit the requirements of administrative processes like real estate registrations and the issuance of licenses and certifications (Olsen et al., 2017; Themistocleous et al., 2023). The digitalization of governmental procedures through blockchain appears to be promising, taking into account recent developments in public services, in order to reduce bureaucracy and enhance trust in recordkeeping (Alles et al., 2019; Themistocleous, 2018).

The Fourth Industrial Revolution is rapidly evolving, and blockchain technology is considered one of its most crucial components. Technologies like Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), Virtual Reality (VR) and Cloud Computing (CC), in combination with blockchain technology, are widely adopted in both the public and private sectors. Industries involving supply chain procedures like agriculture and shipping have been widely exploring blockchain networks to simplify their procedures (Mushtaq and Haq, 2019).

Considering the evolution of processes led by blockchain technology, it is challenging to weigh how each country is adopting this technology. Under this new set of circumstances, public and private sectors are faced with the challenge of adapting to technological innovation and mitigating potential risks that may result from the application of an emerging technology. This situation is creating a gray landscape when stakeholders are considering the basis of their blockchain-related operations and activities. The BRI attempts to shed light on this crucial business consideration by identifying the most promising regions worldwide for blockchain adoption.

## 11.3 INDEX METHODOLOGY

## 11.3.1 BRI Pillars and Indicators

The BRI aims to follow the paradigm set by the above technological indexes and consequently provide advanced metrics and features compared to previous studies. This research adopts a methodology that can be used to provide insights and rankings for more than 200 countries worldwide, taking into account regulation, technology advancements, blockchain industry presence, and local users’ engagement. The reviewed sample represents a sufficient number of economies compared to the aforementioned scientific indexes that mostly evaluate a limited number of countries.

The capacity of regions to enable blockchain-based activities and adopt a blockchain regulatory framework is the main objective to be addressed in this study, and its importance is often highlighted by the community (Yeoh, 2017). Organizations and investors operating in the public and private sectors do not currently have access to scientific tools that can provide systematic judgment of blockchain engagement per country and contribute towards decision-making procedures (Dannen, 2017). Regions with a low performance on specific indicators should be able to use the metrics in order to implement relevant developments and improve the effectiveness of the corresponding sectors (Dondjio and Themistocleous, 2022).

Readiness indexes tend to display a numerical representation of how advanced an examined item is – such as a country or a region – towards a specific subject or sector. Technological readiness indexes are usually developed to outline new trends and contribute towards efficient decision-making by stakeholders of the public and private sectors (Bui, Sankaran and Sebastian, 2003).

The e-Government Development Index (Potnis and Pardo, 2008) evaluates patterns of Member States’ e-Government services. The evolution of this index has assisted in measuring “the degree of connectedness in terms of back office information integration for efficient ways of offering e-Governance.” Authors claim that this index can be used by the public and private sectors of countries for human development, the design of related policies, the design of ICT-related projects, and appropriate deployment of electronic governance.

The Network Readiness Index (Soumitra and Bruno, 2020) measures “How will the various relevant stakeholders (governments, business, and citizens) cooperate (and/or compete) to fully leverage the possibilities offered by technological innovation to tackle current and upcoming challenges?”. The outcome of this research can lead to the identification of opportunities and trends and contribute towards efficient digital transformation in applied regions.

The Autonomous Vehicles Readiness Index (KPMG, 2020) is published in an annual report by KPMG and evaluates the propensity of countries adopting the public use of autonomous vehicles. The research team expects that the conclusions derived will assist in the decision making of stakeholders in the public and private sectors of examined countries.

The Automation Readiness Index (EIU and The Economist Intelligence Unit, 2018) evaluates the maturity of economies to propel in the race to enhance automation within the public and private sectors.

The Distributed Ledger Technology and Cryptocurrency Market Potential Index (Nguyen and Jeong-Hun, 2020) is one of the first cryptocurrency-related indexes that examines the usage of the technology worldwide through the ecosystem evaluation of protocols and networks. The index provides useful data for policymakers aiming to revolutionize local financial procedures through the use of blockchain technology.
