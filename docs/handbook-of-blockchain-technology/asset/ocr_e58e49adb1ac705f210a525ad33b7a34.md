NFTs could be minted on the destination blockchain, maintaining the same properties and value as their “burned” version. Lock-and-mint methods “lock” NFTs on the source blockchain by freezing their state to avoid transfers or other changes. Then, new NFTs are minted on the destination blockchain that represent the locked asset on the source blockchain. Also, in lock-and-mint methods, the “frozen” NFTs on the source blockchain can be unlocked, and the newly minted NFTs on the destination blockchain are burned. Lock-and-mint methods are gaining popularity over burn-and-mint methods. This is due to the ability of the lock-and-mint method to ensure that NFTs retain their uniqueness, ownership details, and other intrinsic properties when moving between blockchains, facilitating easy and seamless movement of NFTs without the complexities of traditional bridging methods, while also being reversible. LayerZero (LayerZero Labs, 2023) protocol is a good example of lock-and-mint methods, as it provides a framework for moving NFTs across different blockchains. A notable case of the lock-and-mint method is the Lil Pudgys bridge transfers (Pudgy Penguins, 2023). Another NFT interoperability option is the Cross-Chain Interoperability Protocol (CCIP) (ChainLink, 2023). This could be classified under the lock-and-mint techniques and is available on ChainLink blockchain.

+   - **User Interface and Experience:** Complexities imposed by NFTs and blockchain technologies have an impact on the user interface and experience, which according to Mosco (2023), is considered a barrier to Metaverse adoption. For example, the complexities related to NFT management may lead to low user engagement. For that reason, the improvement of these interfaces and transaction mechanisms is important to enhance the user experience. This will have a positive impact as it will reduce latency and increase the overall speed and efficiency of NFT exchanges within the Metaverse. Addressing these challenges requires innovative technical solutions. In this direction, the enhancement of digital wallet integrations is pivotal for efficient management and transfer of NFTs within Metaverse platforms. Additionally, cross-chain technology solutions, such as those referred to above (ChainLink 2023; Holograph, 2023; LayerZero Labs, 2023) and the development of robust APIs and SDKs for developers are marked as important factors in the process of facilitating easier integration of NFTs into gaming and Metaverse platforms. These solutions aim to streamline the transaction mechanisms, reduce latency, and enhance the overall speed of NFT exchanges, thereby improving the user experience and platform efficiency (Wang et al., 2023).
