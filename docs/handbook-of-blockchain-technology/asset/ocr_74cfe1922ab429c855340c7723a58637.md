Dr Jacinto Estima is an Assistant Professor of Information Systems at the Department of Informatics Engineering at the University of Coimbra, Portugal. He is also a senior researcher at the Centre for Informatics and Systems of the University of Coimbra (CISUC). Jacinto holds a PhD (2015) in Information Management with specialities in Geographic Information Systems and an MSc in Science and Geographic Information Systems, both from NOVA University of Lisbon.
