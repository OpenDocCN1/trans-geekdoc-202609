In the digital transformation era, technological achievements are succeeding one another at a rapid speed. Emerging fields like the Metaverse, Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs), and blockchain are notable examples of this era and influence the evolution of virtual economies (Themistocleous et al., 2022). Metaverse is considered to be the next evolution of the web, usually referred to as Web3, where the synergies between various emerging technologies including, but not limited to, blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), the semantic web, and many more will enable the digital transformation (Christodoulou et al., 2022). Digital engagements and social media were bridged together with other fundamental solutions to assist the development of immersive experiences and enhance the Metaverse. This evolution underscores the potential of the Metaverse as a platform for interconnected digital experiences and highlights its evolutionary nature, drawing from past innovations while paving the way for future developments (Themistocleous et al., 2022).
