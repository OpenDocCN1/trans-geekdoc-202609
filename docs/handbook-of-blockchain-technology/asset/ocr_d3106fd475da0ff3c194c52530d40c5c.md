+   - **Epic Ordinals** reach an extraordinary level of scarcity. This category includes the first Satoshi following each halving event, which takes place every 210,000 blocks, or about every four years. Based on the total Bitcoin supply, there will be just 32 epic Satoshis. Obviously, epic Ordinals hold significant value and desirability among collectors.
+   - **Legendary Ordinals** comprise the first Satoshi of each cycle, and there will be only five in total. According to Rodarmor (2023a), once every six halvings, a special phenomenon unfolds as the halving event synchronizes with the difficulty adjustment, resulting in what is termed a ‘conjunction’. A cycle refers to the time between these conjunctions, and it occurs every 24 years. It is estimated that the first Bitcoin cycle will happen around the year 2032, making them highly sought after within the Bitcoin community.
+   - **Mythic Ordinals**: There is only one mythic Ordinal in the history of Bitcoin as this refers to the first Satoshi of the genesis block. For that reason, the mythic Ordinal has by far the most valuable and supreme rarity level among all Bitcoin Ordinals.
+   - **Exotic Ordinals** are subjective and depend on individual perspectives and interpretations. They can involve a wide range of meaningful and distinctive elements for different individuals in the Bitcoin community. Some individuals define exotic Ordinals based on historical data, such as those Satoshis involved in:
+     - The first-ever Bitcoin sale; the famous exchange of 10,000 Bitcoins for two Papa John’s pizzas that occurred on May 22, 2010.
+     - The initial transaction between Nakamoto and Hal Finney in January 2009.
+     - Significant moments in Bitcoin history, like Segwit, Lightning Network, Taproot upgrades, or the birth of Ordinals.
+     - The uniqueness of the Satoshi serial number itself, such as having an integer square or cube root, or based on other criteria defined by users.

Each of the above-mentioned rarity levels contributes to the diversity of the Bitcoin Ordinals landscape, and offers collectors and investors unique opportunities to acquire these scarce NFTs. As the scarcity increases from common to mythic, each ordinal gains rarity and significance. This creates an attractive ecosystem within the world of Bitcoin.
