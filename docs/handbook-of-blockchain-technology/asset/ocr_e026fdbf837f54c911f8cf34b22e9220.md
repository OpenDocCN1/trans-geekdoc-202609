The book features 19 chapters by authors from both academia and industry. Of these, 15 chapters draw upon research conducted at the Department of Digital Innovation and the Institute for the Future at the University of Nicosia (UNIC), Cyprus. UNIC is a pioneer in blockchain, cryptoassets, and Metaverse education and research, and has been active in these areas since 2013. It hosts the world’s largest team of faculty and staff specializing in these areas. To date, it has provided education to over 150,000 students and professionals in the abovementioned fields. In 2014, UNIC launched the first of its kind MSc in Blockchain and Digital Currency, and in 2023, it introduced the world’s first MSc in Metaverse (Themistocleous, 2023; Themistocleous et al., 2022). In 2015, UNIC became the first university to create its own blockchain solution for the issuance and verification of academic diplomas, and in 2021, it established NFT valuations (Themistocleous et al., 2023). UNIC supports the operation of many blockchains, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, Arbitrum, and more, and conducts research and development projects as well as applied research with top-tier blockchains. It also collaborates with the European Commission, and it is the academic leader for the European Union Blockchain Observatory and Forum.



We commence this book by investigating the convergence of blockchain technology with the Metaverse. The latter is considered a recent significant development in this space, combining various technologies like blockchain, NFTs, cryptocurrencies, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Virtual and Augmented Reality (VR and AR), to build the next generation of Internet applications. While still in its infancy, the Metaverse is expected to rapidly grow and become a multi-trillion USD market by 2030. It is therefore significant to focus on this area and its interaction with blockchain technology to gain a comprehensive understanding. Given its importance, the first part of this book is dedicated to explaining the role of blockchain technology in building the Metaverse. This part introduces readers to the virtual worlds and assists them in understanding the impact of blockchain on the Metaverse as well as the impact of this combination of technologies on innovation.

Theory and practice demonstrate that blockchain technology can be used either as a standalone technology to develop applications or as a building block for the development of other applications or platforms. The latter is exhibited through the case of the Metaverse, where blockchain technology supports various layers of its architecture such as (a) decentralization, (b) Metaverse economy, (c) interoperability, or (d) security layers.
