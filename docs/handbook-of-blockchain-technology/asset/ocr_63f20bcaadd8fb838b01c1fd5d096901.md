Ensuring that NFTs are compatible across different legal jurisdictions is important for their continuous transfer and value, yet pose a significant challenge given the decentralized ethos of blockchain technology and the “meta” space of blockchain-enabled Metaverse platforms.

+   - **Scalability:** With the increasing NFT adoption and the emergence of Metaverse platforms, the demand for scalable solutions has become necessary. The efficiency of such platforms to handle a significant volume of NFT transactions has become more critical today, and immediate actions are required. Implementing blockchain scalability solutions, such as StarkNet, Arbitrum, Polygon, and many more, is essential to handle high volumes of NFT transactions without compromising transaction speed or security (Kalodner et al., 2018; StarkNet, 2023). Scalability solutions are significant to maintain the efficiency and user experience of NFT transactions in the Metaverse. They also ensure that the infrastructure can support the growing demand and complexity of NFT transactions. Furthermore, the latest developments in the blockchain ecosystem related to the emergence of modular protocols both as Layer 1 (underlying main blockchain architecture) or Layer 2 (solutions that lie on top of the underlying blockchain solutions) denote the shift to more complex transactions and increase the utility of NFTs and digital assets in the digital environment. For example, the Celestia modular data availability (DA) protocol enables specialization and optimization of modular blockchains for a given function (Al-Bassam, 2019; MantleNetwork, 2024; MantaNetwork, 2023). We observe that scalability challenges become more critical as the adoption of NFTs increases across multiple Metaverses. On the other hand, we notice that the latest developments in addressing this challenge are in the right direction.

+   - **NFT Market Valuation:** Another significant challenge in the NFT landscape is the valuation of its market cap. The importance of an accurate NFT market valuation lies in the valuable insights it provides about the size and potential of this market. This will encourage more institutional investors to join the NFT ecosystem and help its growth. Therefore, there are many attempts to establish a methodology that accurately calculates it, as NFTs are currently a multi-billion-dollar market and continue to grow. We believe that the NFT market is going to far exceed the fungible tokens market cap, which is currently near $3 trillion (at the time this work is written), within the next few years. Our position is based on the hypothesis that real-life assets, like real estate, gold, and so on, will be tokenized. Today we observe many attempts at asset tokenization, such as the real estate tokenization platform FUTURESTATE Alliance (https://futurestatealliance.com/). This trend is expected to grow in the future.

The methods used for the valuation of fungible tokens cannot be considered for NFTs as the valuation of the former derives from the multiplication of the token’s reference price by the current circulating supply. This cannot be applied to NFTs as the latter are unique tokens and they cannot be valued in this simple manner.
