## Foreword by Professor Spyros Makridakis

In a Newsweek article published in February 1995, Clifford Stoll, a computer expert, wrote, “Baloney. Do our computer pundits lack all common sense? The truth is no online database will replace your daily newspaper, no CD-ROM can take the place of a competent teacher and no computer network will change the way government works.” Stoll could not be blamed for his views since computers were slow and expensive, and the Internet was still in its infancy in the mid-1990s. He could not have imagined that mobile telephones would become personal computers, owned by everyone and running all kinds of applications using high-speed connections, while GPUs and supercomputers would lead to great advances in artificial intelligence (AI), bringing another revolution with more profound implications than the industrial one, and challenging human supremacy. The Handbook of Blockchain Technology you are reading includes some pioneering chapters and some farsighted predictions about blockchain, the metaverse, NFTs, smart contracts, and DAOs (Decentralized Autonomous Organizations), among others, that their future value should not be viewed like those of Stoll. As the handbook says, “we are still in the beginning of the blockchain era,” and accelerated technological progress is equally likely to advance it even faster than predicted by the forward-looking chapters of this handbook.

The severe financial crisis of 2008 had a profound effect on the global economic, financial, and business systems, pushing it towards greater transparency and more security, and trust. It was within this context that blockchain technology was first introduced to the world by Satoshi Nakamoto in his seminal 2008 paper. Since then, blockchain has evolved far beyond its initial cryptocurrency applications, enabling revolutionary changes across various functions and sectors including finance, supply chain management, healthcare, and the arts. This handbook is arriving at a critical time, offering a comprehensive exploration of these transformative capabilities of blockchain.

This handbook is divided into five parts and 19 chapters, out of which 15 are based on work done at the Department of Digital Innovation at the University of Nicosia in Cyprus, a pioneer in blockchain, cryptoassets and Metaverse education, and research that has been active in these areas since 2013. Its purpose is twofold. Firstly, it aims to educate the reader by demystifying the complex nature of blockchain technology and its mechanisms, applications, and potentials. Secondly, it seeks to contribute to the ongoing academic and professional discourse by presenting cutting-edge research and analysis from leading experts in the field. The contributing authors, from both academia and industry, bring a wealth of knowledge and diverse perspectives that enrich the value of the handbook and provide readers with a well-rounded perspective of the entire blockchain ecosystem.

The structure of the book is carefully designed to cater to a broad audience, ranging from novices in the field to seasoned experts. Its five parts each focus on different aspects of blockchain technology, from its integration with the Metaverse and the intricacies of NFTs and tokenization, to the governance mechanisms of DAOs, the challenges of adoption and maturity, and finally, the practical applications across various industries. The University of Nicosia, a pioneer in blockchain education and research, has played a pivotal role in shaping the content of this handbook. The institution’s commitment to advancing blockchain technology is evident in its contributions to the book, particularly through the research conducted at its Department of Digital Innovation and the Institute for the Future. These contributions not only highlight the theoretical advancements in the field but also emphasize their practical implications and their potential for future innovation.

As we stand on the edge of widespread blockchain adoption, this innovative handbook can serve as an essential guide for understanding the current landscape and navigating the future possibilities of blockchain technology. It addresses the critical challenges as well as the barriers to its widespread adoption, providing valuable insights that can help policymakers, industry leaders, and academics to successfully integrate such technology into their operations. It is the hope of the editor and authors of the chapters of this handbook to inspire you, the reader, to explore blockchain technology further and exploit its implications for your field of work or study. They encourage you to engage with the content of this handbook actively. Reflect on the discussions, question the analyses, and consider the practical applications of the technology in your own context. The field of blockchain is evolving rapidly, and your contributions, whether through further research, application development, or thoughtful critique, are valuable for its growth. Let us step into the future of blockchain together, armed with knowledge, inspired by innovation, and guided by the comprehensive research presented in this handbook.

Welcome to *The Handbook of Blockchain Technology* and enjoy reading and learning from the wealth of knowledge provided in its 19 chapters.
