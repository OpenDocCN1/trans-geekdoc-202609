### 3.3 NFTs IN THE METAVERSE

Over the years, NFTs have provided a secure and transparent framework for the ownership and trade of digital assets by utilizing blockchain technology. This increases trust and facilitates economic activity within the Metaverse. In recent years, NFTs have been transformative elements of the Metaverse and redefined the concepts of ownership, authenticity, and economic dynamics. NFTs allow the digital representation of assets and reshape virtual economies and marketplaces. Below is a summary of the contribution of NFTs in the Metaverse.

- **NFTs and their role in representing ownership and authenticity:** NFTs have revolutionized the digital landscape by providing a blockchain mechanism for asserting ownership and authenticity of digital assets within the Metaverse. Unlike traditional digital assets, which lack a verifiable mechanism to prove uniqueness and ownership, NFTs leverage blockchain technology to embed these properties inherently within each token.
- **Digital assets in the Metaverse:** In the Metaverse, digital assets range from virtual land parcels and buildings to digital art and personal avatars (PFPs). NFTs serve as the initial standard that asserts the ownership of these assets. In this way, NFTs ensure that each item can be uniquely owned, traded, or transferred. This capability transforms the digital economy by enabling real value to be associated with virtual items, similar to physical assets in the real world.
- **Integration with virtual economies:** The integration of NFTs with virtual economies has been significant in creating marketplaces. In these environments, users can buy, sell, or trade digital assets with confidence as the authenticity and ownership history of these assets are assured. This integration has facilitated the emergence of innovative economic models within the Metaverse, where creators can monetize their digital assets directly and consumers can invest in digital assets knowing their ownership rights are secure.

As illustrated in Figure 3.1, NFTs offer a novel paradigm for asset creation, tokenization, verification, trade, and ownership. This paradigm process is based on five steps:

- **Step 1 – Create Asset:** Step 1 initiates the process by creating or selecting an asset to be deployed as an NFT. Here we can select an existing asset or create a new one. This can be almost any kind of asset such as virtual real estate, art, or document (e.g., a certificate, course material, or other) (Themistocleous et al., 2023).

- **Step 2 – Tokenize into NFT:** The asset created during step 1 is converted into a unique NFT in step 2. This is achieved using appropriate standards such as ERC-721, ERC-1155, BRC-721, and so on. The selection of a standard depends on the blockchain we use, with Christodoulou et al. (2022) providing a complete list of the available standards for NFT tokenization.
- **Step 3 – Verify NFT:** In this step, there is a blockchain verification process that takes place in order to verify the authenticity and ownership of the NFT.
- **Step 4 – Trade NFT:** This step supports the users in trading their NFTs using a Metaverse marketplace or a marketplace that is outside the Metaverse.
- **Step 5 – Transfer ownership:** The last step supports the transfer of ownership from one user to another.

The normative literature reports numerous use cases that demonstrate the critical role of NFTs in representing ownership and authenticity. An example involves virtual real estate within platforms such as Decentraland (Ordano et al., 2017) or Cryptovoxels (Cryptovoxels, 2023), where land parcels are tokenized as NFTs. This allows them to be traded and owned similarly to physical real estate, with the tokenization process securing each parcel’s uniqueness and providing a transparent record of ownership on the blockchain. A study by Ordano et al. (2017) highlights how Decentraland’s marketplace facilitates the trade of virtual land, with NFTs ensuring transparency and security in transactions.

Expanding the domain of NFT utility, the education sector emerges as a significant beneficiary, overcoming geographical and financial barriers to provide universal access to contemporary and personalized educational content and credentials. A pioneering initiative by the University of Nicosia introduced the first university course delivered on-chain and in-Metaverse (Themistocleous et al., 2022), attended by 22,500 students who registered by minting the course’s registration NFT. This innovative approach allowed participants to attend live sessions in the Metaverse using the UNIC Metacampus on OnCyber and access course materials and lectures minted as NFTs on the Ethereum blockchain. Furthermore, the final examination was conducted entirely on-chain, utilizing an encrypted NFT to verify students’ responses through a complex smart contract. Successful students received personalized generative art NFT certificates, marking a new era in the distribution and verification of educational achievements.

These examples not only demonstrate how NFTs trade property rights in the digital world, facilitating real estate development, leasing, and trading in the Metaverse, but also highlight the dynamic nature of NFTs in creating value beyond traditional rules. Through these innovative applications, NFTs drive value creation, transforming how assets, achievements, and educational experiences are perceived, owned, and transferred in the Metaverse.

The influence of NFTs goes beyond their scarcity, with their value tied to their verifiable provenance. Beyond revolutionizing real estate and pioneering educational models, NFTs provide advancements across various domains, including gaming, art, fashion, and the music industry. They offer unique ways to own, authenticate, and distribute content. This broad spectrum of applications underscores the potential of NFTs to redefine digital interactions and economic transactions across multiple domains.

In this evolving landscape, personal representation within the Metaverse has transcended traditional boundaries, moving beyond simple avatars or pseudonyms. NFTs have empowered individuals to create digital personas that are not only unique and verifiable but also filled with intrinsic value (Pandey et al., 2020). These personas act as a bridge between the virtual and real worlds, facilitating an interplay of identities. Moreover, NFTs can be used in defining access within the Metaverse, functioning similarly to an “access pass” that unlocks exclusive virtual spaces, events, or experiences.
