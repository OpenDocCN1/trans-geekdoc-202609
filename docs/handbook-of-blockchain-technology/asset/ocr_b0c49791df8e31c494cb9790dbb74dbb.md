It becomes clear, in fact, that the main culprits are two different types of obstacles: first, it is difficult to find personnel with the necessary training in the field, and second, there are no regulations controlling its use. However, data security, data sets, data monetization, and computing power of intelligent system operations are the most prevalent types of applications in this specialized field. The need to integrate ML for fraud detection activities into Blockchain networks is the trendiest topic in recent studies, as evidenced by the findings, and it is a common opinion that ML algorithms can improve Blockchain design and operation. And yet, the level of regulation is a crucial and essential concern both because over-regulation is believed to prevent innovation and prohibit companies from exploring their most creative ideas, and because low regulation could give them too much leeway to work.

Considering that fraudulent transactions have a significant influence on the economics and trust of a Blockchain network, just as proof-of-work or proof-of-stake consensus methods can validate the legitimacy of the transaction, but not the nature of the individuals involved in the transaction or those verifying it, it is clear that a Blockchain network could be susceptible to fraud. Here, the use of ML algorithms is a viable strategy to eliminate fraud. Indeed, it is a widely held belief that these two technologies provide exponential benefits when considered separately, so it is easy to see how a relationship between the two worlds, which are characterized by an interesting complementarity, could lead to new and interesting scenarios that provide numerous innovations and boost security and trust in the future adoption of Blockchain technology. However, it is still in its infancy, and numerous research obstacles, such as problems with consensus protocols, a lack of standards, and so on, remain. It is hypothesized that the highlighted evidence will positively contribute to the ML path, enabling the next generation of innovations.
