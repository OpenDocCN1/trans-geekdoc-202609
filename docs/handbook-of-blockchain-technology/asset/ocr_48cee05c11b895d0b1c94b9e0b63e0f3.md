# ## 17.4 FINDINGS

The interviews lasted between ten and 15 minutes. Each question was analyzed and relevant data was extracted and summarized in Table 17.2. It is evident, as shown in Table 17.3, that 70.8 percent of the categories interviewed do not believe that ML is the key to detecting fraud in Blockchain transactions. This perspective suggests to us that many companies are not yet completely aware of the countless potentials of integrating Blockchain with ML and how important it is to adopt these technologies on a large scale. Only 29.2 percent percent say the opposite.

**Table 17.2 — Survey**

| Question | Category |
|---|---|
| 1. Do you believe that ML can be used to detect fraud in Blockchain transactions? | Business |
| 2. What industry is your business in? | Business |
| 3. How easy do you think it is to find skilled people who know how to integrate Blockchain technology with ML? | IT |
| 4. What is your expertise in ML? | IT |
| 5. How can ML be used to detect fraud in Blockchain transactions? | IT |
| 6. Are you familiar with the specific ML framework/model and training dataset that can be used for this task? | IT |
| 7. Do you think there might be obstacles in applying ML for transaction analysis? | Business |
| 8. Do you believe that any applicable laws or regulations should take into account the algorithmic analysis provided by ML? | Legal |
| 9. Are you aware of any legislation or regulations governing the use of ML in business fraud detection? | Legal |
