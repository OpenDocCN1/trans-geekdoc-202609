# 11.3.3  BRI Advanced Versions – a) ‘Weight Adjustment’ and b) ‘Community-Driven’

The equal weighting of indicators may not be the optimal tool for some stakeholders and organizations, depending on the industry they belong to. For example, policymakers of low-scored countries may wish to access the rankings and insights of the best-performing countries when setting up regulatory guidelines and attracting businesses, as ideal paradigms. In the private sector, company executives may wish to customize their scoring preferences by weighting more heavily certain indicators, demonstrating countries with business potential and local users’ engagement.

To achieve a broader use of the BRI service, the adjustment of indicators’ weights according to the needs of a business and/or individual stakeholders seems essential. The objective of this service is to provide valuable business tools for the community’s interest and use. The BRI “Weight Adjustment Version” will become available when a user accesses the metric in an online form. It is expected that a number of indicators will be considered more important than others for the majority of stakeholders, for example, Regulatory Stance vs. Internet Access.

The understanding of the community’s preferences and understanding blockchain readiness is reflected in the BRI “Community-Driven Version.” A survey-based questionnaire was constructed to obtain data regarding the importance of each indicator by the community in a Likert Scale with a range of 1–5.
