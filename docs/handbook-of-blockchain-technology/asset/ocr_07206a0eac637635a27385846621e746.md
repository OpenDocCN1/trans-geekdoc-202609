Other important early NFT projects include CryptoKitties and NBA Top Shot, both launched by Dapper Labs in December 2017 and 2020 respectively (Conti, 2023; Cornish, 2018). Another noteworthy Ethereum project is Axie Infinity (Chandra, 2022) which was released in 2018 and was the first to use the concept of the Play-to-Earn (P2E) model in gaming (Karapapas et al., 2022). Decentraland is an equally important project built on Ethereum that demonstrated the use of NFTs in gaming (Guidi and Michienzi, 2022). Both Axie Infinity and Decentraland illustrate the potential of blockchain to disrupt traditional gaming. This is achieved by providing financial incentives and digital ownership and by creating new economic models. These projects have attracted a significant number of users and have become influential for blockchain gaming and the Metaverse.

Another important addition to the NFT space is the Bored Ape Yacht Club (BAYC), which was launched in April 2021 (Kom­iners et al., 2022). In a similar fashion to CP, BAYC is a collection of 10,000 NFTs. Each BAYC NFT features a bored ape and has distinct characteristics, accessories, backgrounds, and scarcity. BAYC collection is built on Ethereum, has had major sales, and has gained increased popularity within the NFT space. The project shows the growing demand for unique digital art collectibles and the vibrant community-driven nature of the NFT market. As with many NFT projects, the value of BAYC NFTs can fluctuate based on factors such as market trends, scarcity, and community engagement.
