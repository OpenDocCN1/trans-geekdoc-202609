| Wang et al. (2019) | Understanding Blockchain Technology for Future Supply Chains: A Systematic Literature Review and Research Agenda | While blockchain technologies are young, trust is driving their growth in supply chains. These technologies improve supply chain visibility, traceability, data security, and smart contracts. This research reveals problems, gaps, and research opportunities. A blockchain-enabled supply chain was designed. |
| Taherdoost, H. (2022) | A Critical Review of Blockchain Acceptance Models – Blockchain Technology Adoption Frameworks and Applications | Blockchain is a game-changing technology with many benefits, but its adoption is slow and hindered by many obstacles. Decision-makers may resist a business-friendly framework. This article explores blockchain adoption frameworks and application cases. The results are split into five sections: supply chain, industries, financial sector, cryptocurrency, and other publications (excluded from the former fields). Most publications focused on TAM, TOE, and conceptual frameworks. |
| Liang et al. (2021) | What Drives the Adoption of the Blockchain Technology? A Fit- Viability Perspective | Blockchain might change digital security and trust. Complexity and deployment benefits limit adoption. This research reveals how blockchain adoption affects managers. Two hundred and forty-two medical and financial managers collaborated to build a value-based technology adoption plan. These organizations’ management will evaluate blockchain’s potential for storing and securing data. PLS was used to gauge managers’ willingness to utilize blockchain technology. |
| Albayati et al. (2020) | Accepting Financial Transactions Using Blockchain Technology and Cryptocurrency: A Customer Perspective Approach | Blockchain and cryptocurrency are FinTech innovations revolutionizing the global economy. This article examines Bitcoin transaction behavior to measure blockchain’s utility. This study presents a new integration paradigm for blockchain-based apps: regulatory backing and experience. People feel secure utilizing blockchain-based apps when controlled by the government. At a certain level of familiarity, consumers trust blockchain-based apps, which increases adoption. Governments and businesses should increase customer trust to embrace blockchain technology. |
