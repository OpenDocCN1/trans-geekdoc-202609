# 6.4.3  The Bibliographic Networks: Analysis and Discussion

As depicted in Figure 6.2, the VOSViewer analysis yielded five clusters of keywords, categorized based on the year of publication. These clusters highlight significant aspects and trends for each specific time period, as interpreted by the authors.

In the years leading up to and including 2019, the focus was primarily on cryptocurrencies, cryptoassets, and regulatory measures. Between 2019 and 2020, the emphasis shifted towards blockchain and its various components such as smart contracts, token classifications, DeFi, and tokenization. It appears that blockchain and tokenization were at the heart of discussions among researchers in the field. In 2022, the research spotlight was on NFTs. By 2023, the focus had evolved to encompass current and future tokenization trends, including the metaverse, virtual and extended reality, artificial intelligence (AI), Web3, and cybersecurity. These terms suggest a focus on the use of tokenization in virtual and augmented reality environments, often referred to as the “metaverse”. Tokenization could be used to represent ownership of virtual assets, facilitate transactions, or manage identities in these environments. The presence of AI suggests that AI might be used in conjunction with tokenization. This could be in the form of AI-driven trading of tokenized assets, AI in managing and securing tokenized assets, or even tokenization of AI models or services. The term Web3 refers to the next generation of internet technologies, often associated with decentralized networks and services. Tokenization is a key component of many Web3 technologies, such as blockchain and decentralized finance (DeFi). Cybersecurity suggests a focus on the security aspects of tokenization. This could involve discussions on how to secure tokenized assets, how to ensure the privacy and integrity of tokenized data, or how to mitigate risks associated with tokenization.

The comprehensive analysis illustrates the progression of the tokenization discipline over the past few years. The most recent year displays a somewhat fragmented trend, influenced by emerging transformative technologies. # 6.5  DISCUSSION AND CONCLUDING REMARKS

Tokenization, as a concept and technology, holds immense potential to revolutionize various sectors, from finance to real estate to art. By converting real-world assets into digital tokens on a blockchain, tokenization offers numerous benefits. It enhances liquidity, as even high-value assets can be fractionally owned, making them accessible to a broader range of investors. It also provides increased transparency and security, as blockchain’s immutable and decentralized nature ensures that all transactions are traceable and tamper-proof. Furthermore, tokenization can streamline and automate processes, reducing costs and increasing efficiency.
