This dynamic interaction not only allows IoV operators to reduce their reliance on costly peaking power plants, but it also provides EV owners with a financial incentive. By participating in this energy exchange, EV owners can generate income by selling their excess electricity back to the grid during times of high demand. Consequently, DR not only contributes to a more efficient and dependable IoV, but it also generates economic benefits for EV owners.

The combination of blockchain technology, IoVs, and DRM reshapes the field of energy management. More specifically, it enables P2P energy transactions between EVs and the grid and offers a secure, transparent, and efficient method for recording and validating V2G transactions. Blockchain technology emerges as an indispensable tool for managing them. The unique characteristics of blockchain make it an ideal fit for this context. This results in an increased level of trust between parties, which is essential for the effective operation of a decentralized P2P energy market. In addition, the combination of blockchain technology with IoV and DRM provides numerous benefits such as decentralization, smart contract support, and tokenization.

# 16.3.1 Research Challenges

As elaborated in earlier sections, IoVs experience significant strain due to substantial shifts in production and the spread of DERs (e.g., EVs). Increasing irregularities in power generation necessitate the emergence of innovative paradigms. DR is a well-researched strategy where EV consumers consciously modify their power consumption based on grid demands.

Consequently, intelligent energy management, which promotes the best exploitation of restricted energy resources, becomes a precondition for the development of a smart, environmentally friendly, and sustainable power grid. Nonetheless, the deployment of unpredictably scattered RES and uncoordinated EVs presents considerable obstacles to intelligent energy management. A substantial deployment of centralized generators and energy storage devices would be necessary to balance supply and demand. However, this solution brings substantial Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) and Operational Expense (OPEX). In this context, the employment of blockchain technology as a foundational platform to address the needs of the IoV has been explored.
