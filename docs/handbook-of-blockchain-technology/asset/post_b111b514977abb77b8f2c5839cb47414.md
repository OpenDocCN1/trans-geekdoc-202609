The book features 19 chapters by authors from both academia and industry. Of these, 15 chapters draw upon research conducted at the Department of Digital Innovation and the Institute for the Future at the University of Nicosia (UNIC), Cyprus. UNIC is a pioneer in blockchain, cryptoassets, and Metaverse education and research, and has been active in these areas since 2013. It hosts the world’s largest team of faculty and staff specializing in these areas. To date, it has provided education to over 150,000 students and professionals in the abovementioned fields. In 2014, UNIC launched the first of its kind MSc in Blockchain and Digital Currency, and in 2023, it introduced the world’s first MSc in Metaverse (Themistocleous, 2023; Themistocleous et al., 2022). In 2015, UNIC became the first university to create its own blockchain solution for the issuance and verification of academic diplomas, and in 2021, it established NFT valuations (Themistocleous et al., 2023). UNIC supports the operation of many blockchains, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, Arbitrum, and more, and conducts research and development projects as well as applied research with top-tier blockchains. It also collaborates with the European Commission, and it is the academic leader for the European Union Blockchain Observatory and Forum.

We commence this book by investigating the convergence of blockchain technology with the Metaverse. The latter is considered a recent significant development in this space, combining various technologies like blockchain, NFTs, cryptocurrencies, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Virtual and Augmented Reality (VR and AR), to build the next generation of Internet applications. While still in its infancy, the Metaverse is expected to rapidly grow and become a multi-trillion USD market by 2030. It is therefore significant to focus on this area and its interaction with blockchain technology to gain a comprehensive understanding. Given its importance, the first part of this book is dedicated to explaining the role of blockchain technology in building the Metaverse. This part introduces readers to the virtual worlds and assists them in understanding the impact of blockchain on the Metaverse as well as the impact of this combination of technologies on innovation.

Theory and practice demonstrate that blockchain technology can be used either as a standalone technology to develop applications or as a building block for the development of other applications or platforms. The latter is exhibited through the case of the Metaverse, where blockchain technology supports various layers of its architecture such as (a) decentralization, (b) Metaverse economy, (c) interoperability, or (d) security layers. Furthermore, blockchain enhances the functionality of the Metaverse by providing unique features like digital ownership or verifiable digital assets. In recent years, the Metaverse has emerged as a promising area for our digital future, and it holds great potential for business, economy, and society.

We begin the book with the chapter entitled “Decoding the Metaverse: An in-depth Exploration of Layers and Technological Foundations.” The chapter aims to clarify some of the confusion surrounding this area and to conduct a more thorough analysis of the Metaverse. The main contribution of this paper is to assess the existing approaches to Metaverse architectural layers, discuss their limitations and omissions, and introduce a comprehensive approach that offers a clearer definition and understanding of these layers.

The second chapter, authored by Clint Rowe and Soulla Louca, conducts an analysis of the Metaverse literature by employing an AI-assisted systematic literature review of cryptocurrency and NFT use in the Metaverse. AI is utilized as a complementary method to the traditional systematic literature review process. This was done to enhance clarity and assist in categorizing NFT and cryptocurrency usages in the Metaverse. This work can be used as a point of reference by numerous entities (e.g., policymakers, academics, industry experts); it offers insights and establishes the foundations for future research in this domain.

In Chapter 3, the contribution of Leonidas Kateralis, Marinos Themistocleous, Klitos Christodoulou, and Stamatis Papangelou investigates the role of NFTs in the Metaverse. The chapter identifies key parameters that influence NFT adoption in the Metaverse as well as Metaverse adoption. These parameters are considered to be driving forces and include interoperability, legal and intellectual property considerations, user interface and experience, scalability, and NFT market valuation.

## Part II: NFTs and Tokenization

In the second part, attention turns to NFTs and tokenization. Both areas are innovative as the former redefines ownership and authenticity, while the latter brings new ways of doing business (e.g., tokenization of assets) (Christodoulou et al., 2022). In this second part of the book, we present three papers: the first one explores the relationship between blockchain technology and the arts, the second one explains Bitcoin ordinals and BRC20 tokens, and the last one presents a systematic literature review and analysis on tokenization.

Part II starts with the chapter entitled “Blockchain and the Arts: Foes – or Unlikely Allies?” authored by Christos Makridis and Björn Hanneke. The chapter explores the utilization of NFTs in the arts and highlights their potential to enhance the financial stability of artists. Makridis and Hanneke also discuss existing challenges, obstacles, and constraints.

Chapter 5 investigates recent developments in Bitcoin NFTs. In this article, Marinos Themistocleous, Klitos Christodoulou, and Leonidas Kateralis investigate the innovative area of Bitcoin Ordinals and BRC20. The chapter conducts a comprehensive analysis to examine the applications, potentials, and limitations of Ordinals and BRC20. The chapter also provides valuable insights into this interesting area.

Part II closes with the work of Stavros Ioannou, Soulla Louca, Charis Savvides, and Elias Iosif. Their chapter employs a systematic literature review to explore the landscape of tokenization. Based on the normative literature, the authors explore the positive and negative aspects of tokenization, its transformative potential and impact, as well as recent developments and future trends.
