The Autonomous Vehicles Readiness Index (KPMG, 2020) is published in an annual report by KPMG and evaluates the propensity of countries adopting the public use of autonomous vehicles. The research team expects that the conclusions derived will assist in the decision making of stakeholders in the public and private sectors of examined countries.

The Automation Readiness Index (EIU and The Economist Intelligence Unit, 2018) evaluates the maturity of economies to propel in the race to enhance automation within the public and private sectors.

The Distributed Ledger Technology and Cryptocurrency Market Potential Index (Nguyen and Jeong-Hun, 2020) is one of the first cryptocurrency-related indexes that examines the usage of the technology worldwide through the ecosystem evaluation of protocols and networks. The index provides useful data for policymakers aiming to revolutionize local financial procedures through the use of blockchain technology.

The BRI aims to follow the paradigm set by the above technological indexes and consequently provide advanced metrics and features compared to previous studies. This research adopts a methodology that can be used to provide insights and rankings for more than 200 countries worldwide, taking into account regulation, technology advancements, blockchain industry presence, and local users’ engagement. The reviewed sample represents a sufficient number of economies compared to the aforementioned scientific indexes that mostly evaluate a limited number of countries.

The capacity of regions to enable blockchain-based activities and adopt a blockchain regulatory framework is the main objective to be addressed in this study, and its importance is often highlighted by the community (Yeoh, 2017). Organizations and investors operating in the public and private sectors do not currently have access to scientific tools that can provide systematic judgment of blockchain engagement per country and contribute towards decision-making procedures (Dannen, 2017). Regions with a low performance on specific indicators should be able to use the metrics in order to implement relevant developments and improve the effectiveness of the corresponding sectors (Dondjio and Themistocleous, 2022).

# 11.3  INDEX METHODOLOGY

The proposed BRI model is assembled with the aim of indicating countries and regions suitable for business operations related to blockchain and cryptocurrencies. The tools embedded in this service shall be able to affect decision-making and mitigate business and financial risks of users. The index is built dynamically (Robinson, 1981) and can be published annually with updated data feeds, reflecting the new developments of the industry.
