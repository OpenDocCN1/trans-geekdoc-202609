The findings of this research can serve as a starting point for further exploration by researchers and practitioners in the fields of HRM and Blockchain. Additional research methods, such as interviews and observations, could be employed to enhance the validity and reliability of the survey method, provided the necessary budget is available. The relationship between strong organizational cultures and strong client relationships can be a focus of future research. Authentic leadership and the value placed on transparency align well with the transparency-promoting nature of Blockchain technology. Further investigation can deepen our understanding of how Blockchain facilitates authentic relationships with customers.

The findings of this study support the conclusion that by applying Blockchain technology, business activity is performed with lower costs, more effectively, and more efficiently. Furthermore, the findings also support the conclusion that Blockchain technology is expected to be embraced as a tool for HRM functions and that it will create new job opportunities in the industry, both technological and non-technological, such as Blockchain designers, developers, quality engineers, algorithm engineers, and legal consultants. Blockchain will contribute to cost and time savings in the selection process, standardization of career profiles, and verification of employees’ qualifications, simplifying the recording and tracing of their career movements.
