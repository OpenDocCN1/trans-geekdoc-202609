Aside from the broad legal implications in trademarking, property, and identity, property within the metaverse poses more unique regulatory issues as well. For example, NFTs and cryptocurrency in the metaverse are considered assets, as they hold value in themselves (Smaili, 2022). In disputes such as divorce proceedings, the separation of these digital assets can be difficult. Due to the metaverse now acting as a hub for this value, cases of attempted asset concealment have been identified within the digital reality. This prompts further pursuit of analysis in metaverse holdings. Courts have now been tasked with four responsibilities regarding metaverse assets: (1) identifying them; (2) characterizing them; (3) valuing them; and (4) dividing them. This is yet another implication of how NFTs as a metaverse asset are influencing the development of metaverse regulations (Tang, 2022).

As discussed above, the metaverse offers an expansive array of different interactions, most of which are enabled through the use of cryptocurrency and NFTs. Resulting from the direct correlation between cryptocurrencies and NFTs with most interactions within the metaverse, a plethora of legal questions are presented (Agrawal, 2022). Nearly any in-game item can be considered a virtual asset due to its ability to hold value within the decentralized ledger. This makes regulating the activity in the metaverse much more difficult to police (Qin et al., 2022). This difficulty is what entices criminals to act maliciously in the space, committing crimes such as money laundering, digital theft, tax avoidance, fraudulent behavior, petty scams, and so on. This imposes a need for more cybersecurity models to be applied in the metaverse; however, organizations are already constructing these (Kabanda et al., 2022).

# 2.4.5  Technological Innovations

The technological innovations provided by the use of cryptocurrency and NFTs within the metaverse are ever-evolving with the growth of the technology. The literature surrounding these innovations describes how Web 3.0 technologies are being implemented and utilized in the progression of the metaverse. This section outlines the growing complexities of metaverse technology and the role that cryptocurrency and NFTs fulfill in these innovations.

Possibly the most prominent innovation of the metaverse is its convergence with the decentralized web. The metaverse is built on three key components: hardware resources (GPU and I/O), base communication networks, such as Sixth-Generation (6G) networks, and AI-enabled models. It operates on the underlying Web 3.0 technology, which is intelligent, fully decentralized, supports transactional payments via blockchains, and is supported via NFT-enabled cryptocurrencies. This is the foundation for nearly all additional innovations, which stem from this technology (Bhattacharya et al., 2023).

As the metaverse has advanced, more and more diverse Web 3.0 applications have been applied to it. Traditionally, the decentralized ARs operated through the Ethereum blockchain due to its SC, development, and tokenization capabilities. Platforms such as Decentraland, CryptoVoxels, and Somnium Space operate through this blockchain; however, other metaverse platforms, such as Roblox utilize completely separate systems to run their VRs. This poses the question of interoperability and the communication of various servers (Schöbel and Leimeister, 2023).
