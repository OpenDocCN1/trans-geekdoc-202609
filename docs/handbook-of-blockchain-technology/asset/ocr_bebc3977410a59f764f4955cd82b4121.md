# 1.1 FROM WEB TO METAVERSE

An ongoing wave of evolution and transformation began with the introduction of personal computers in the early 1980s. When Sir Tim Berners-Lee created the World Wide Web (WWW) later that decade in 1989 he could hardly have imagined the effects it would have on the economy, society, and means of communication. It is possible that he did not realize how the WWW would result in the Internet revolution, a new phase of the industrial revolution.

Subsequently, the WWW has grown in importance in our digitally connected world (Berners-Lee and Fischetti 1999). Numerous applications and technological advancements that have been created so far have altered our daily lives and revolutionized our societies. We have seen the Internet go through several stages of development as shown in Figure 1.1 and explained in the paragraphs that follow.

**1989 – Internet of Information (World Wide Web):** In 1989, the launch of the WWW marked a transformative milestone. The WWW not only revolutionized business practices and communication but also enhanced access to information. In addition, it shaped the way we interact, collaborate, and make decisions. The Internet originated as an expansive repository of information and has given rise to innovative models in both public and private domains. Leveraging this technology, we’ve introduced novel approaches to conducting business and facilitating interactions. These include various business models such as Business-to-Consumer, Business-to-Business, Government-to-Citizens, Government-to-Business, and Government-to-Government interactions (Doukidis et al., 1998; Schmid et al., 2013).

**1999 – Internet of Things (IoT):** In 1999 Kevin Ashton introduced a technology called Internet of Things (IoT) that supports the communication of digital objects. IoTs are equipped with embedded sensors and their applications enhance connectivity, automation, and data exchange. During the last few decades, IoTs have been widespread across different sectors, ranging from automotive industries to home devices (Elodie, 2022; Kramp et al., 2013). The application of IoTs has paved the way for a new paradigm shift since it supports machine-to-machine, machine-to-human, and human-to-human interaction. IoTs adoption has been exponential and as of 2023, there were 15.14 billion IoT devices. It is expected that their number will reach 29.42 billion by 2030 (Statista, 2023).

**2002 – Internet of People (Social Media):** Around two decades ago Social Media (SM) made its appearance and brought a new way of communication and interaction. Since then various SM platforms such as Friendster (2002), MySpace (2003), Facebook (2004), Twitter (2006), Instagram (2010), Snapchat (2011), Telegram (2013) have been launched and attracted billions of users. It is estimated that around two-thirds of the world’s population use SM today.
