This tokenization process creates fully functioning, virtual musical events in which artists can broadcast their talent to metaverse users. These events can be monetized similarly to a physical concert or club through cryptocurrencies as the medium of exchange, providing artists with direct access to funds.

In addition to musical purposes, NFTs have begun to be implemented for the tokenization of podcasts as well. The podcast industry has seen immense growth in the past decade, and many influencers are transitioning their audio-based businesses into the metaverse as well. Due to the ability to tokenize these audio clips into NFTs, podcast creators can distribute their content to listeners with direct ownership of their content. In turn, listeners can access a more immersive experience in which the podcast is complemented by 3D visuals and graphics projected within the metaverse (Fernandes et al., 2022).

One of the most apparent uses of cryptocurrency and NFTs within the metaverse is displayed within the gaming industry. The global gaming market was valued at 173.70 billion USD in 2020, and it is expected to reach a value of 314.40 billion USD by 2026, registering a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 9.64 percent over 2021–2026. These platforms are attracting hundreds of thousands of new visitors in online traffic (Seifoddini, 2022). See Table 2.3 for a visualization of the metaverse competitive landscape which depicts this.

Many game developers are capitalizing on this growth and utilizing NFTs and cryptocurrencies to facilitate metaverse gaming experiences. For example, Epic Games, a major player in the metaverse gaming industry, has recently acquired photogrammetry studios Quixel and Capturing Reality, and the digital human startup 3Lateral. These applications promote the construction of digital worlds and utilize the tokenization process used for NFTs to implement gaming libraries into the metaverse (Vaughan-Jones, 2022).
