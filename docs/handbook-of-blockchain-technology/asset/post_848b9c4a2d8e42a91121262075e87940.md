The use of NFTs in the metaverse as art also provides creators with more control over their digital artwork. This is displayed through the ability of creators to obtain immediate royalty payments from tracked usage. Any form of monetizable art within the metaverse can be subjected to smart contracts, thus giving creators immense freedom with their content. This freedom allows individuals generating art within the metaverse to avoid the constraints of traditional intermediaries and provides more allocation towards their creative expression (Macedo et al., 2022).

A byproduct of the hyper-interactivity within the metaverse has transformed the fashion industry as well. This form of creative expression utilizes NFT integrability in the metaverse. This industry is increasingly capitalizing on the metaverse to engage with younger demographics, with brands like Gucci, Ralph Lauren, and Louis Vuitton. These fashion organizations have been implementing diverse NFTs of various avatar identity ornaments into platforms such as Roblox and ZEPETO (Shin et al., 2023). The spread of the ‘untact’ culture due to COVID-19 has accelerated the digitalization of the fashion industry and the popularity of the metaverse. Consumers are moving to an online digital environment where they can meet their various needs, leading to the rapid integration of the metaverse into daily lives.

Stemming from the popularity of the sneaker and shoe collecting industry, many fashion organizations have targeted this form of digital expression in the metaverse too. For example, Gucci entered this market with the introduction of the world’s first-ever virtual sneaker, coined "The Gucci Virtual 25." This served as an NFT that users could purchase to better represent their avatar within the metaverse, allowing them to relate to similar fashion senses they have in the physical world (Vaughan-Jones, 2022). Nike pursued a similar endeavor through the acquisition of a virtual sneaker company called RTFKT, and the creation of blockchain-based digital sneakers called CryptoKicks. Nike’s early metaverse initiatives followed a ‘probe and learn’ strategy to evaluate the metaverse as a new channel for customer engagement and digital revenue streams; however, with these investments they have committed to providing metaverse users with a wide selection of expressive avatar NFTs (Sawhney et al., 2023).

This was mimicked by another fashion company, Louis Vuitton, which launched a metaverse application called Louis the Game, which allowed players to collect a variety of NFTs to represent their virtual characters. These organizations all display aspirations to continue the production of VR items to increase user identity options (Shin et al., 2023).

These fashion brands provide individuals with an outlet to stimulate avatar expression in the metaverse, and cryptocurrencies act as the medium of exchange to facilitate said expression. Similar to how money is used in the real world, these digital currencies can also be used to incentivize certain behaviors and activities, such as content creation and exchange of these fashion NFTs (Delaplaine, 2022).

The metaverse can offer more unique forms of digital identity to users as well. Due to the decentralized ledger technology, applications of heritage recording and expression are possible and can prove useful for the future. NFTs within the metaverse would allow individuals to store and digitalize historical data regarding their family and ancestry. This would allow future generations to access their heritage and provide a unique, yet immutable way to access their identity and family history (Stublić et al., 2023).

This vast array of avatar customization and expression is what makes NFTs and cryptocurrencies so relevant in the metaverse regarding digital expression and identity. Avatars interact with the metaverse, and due to the NFTs, they can resemble any form of physical expression.

River Bend School District in Raleigh, North Carolina, uses these technologies to help students meet teaching and learning standards and to start managing their own art, music, and finance-related portfolios. This in turn exposes the students to metaverse and blockchain technology, providing interest and frameworks for future adoption (Ferdig et al., 2022).

The use of AI, in addition to NFTs and cryptocurrencies, poses promising opportunities for metaverse educational utility as well. The role of AI in metaverse education is being developed in convergence with technology, in which AI programming can be implemented in non-player characters (NPCs) in the metaverse. These NPCs can fulfill the duties of tutors, peers, and tutees within metaverse educational platforms, acting as non-fungible, tokenized characters in the space (Hwang and Chien, 2022). The technology behind NFTs can leverage AI to analyze students’ behavior and interaction patterns with their performance levels in the metaverse as well. This may be monetized within the educational market utilizing digital currencies, operating under the decentralized nature of blockchain technology (Tlili et al., 2022).

Although cryptocurrency and NFTs are heavily utilized within the metaverse in general, their implementation in an educational setting is still developmental. There are select instances of this technology already providing educational value; however, in respect to other utilizations of Web 3.0 in the metaverse, educational purposes are still more theoretical. The findings show that 53 percent of the metaverse research studies were used in natural science, mathematics, and engineering, followed by general education (15 percent), and arts and humanities (11 percent) (Tlili et al., 2022).

The potential behind cryptocurrency and NFT usage in metaverse educational applications can provide technical support for the discipline, provide 3D modeling computer programs for courses, help students establish connections between experiments and virtual objects, and provide autonomous tutoring systems (Hwang and Chien, 2022). Aside from the previously mentioned literature, these use cases are still primarily in development, yet the educational benefits are already presenting themselves within many recent publications.

## 2.4.3 Entertainment

The entertainment industry has been adapting to the emergence of the metaverse by applying Web 3.0 technologies like cryptocurrency and NFTs within the experience. The outlooks of ‘entertainment’ can cover a broad variety of leisurely activities, yet these activities are already almost entirely reflected within the digital landscape of the metaverse. Sectors of entertainment such as gaming, music, social engagement, and so on, encompass what some individuals describe as their reasoning for entering the metaverse. The use of cryptocurrencies and NFTs plays a large role in the peer-to-peer leisure provided, and the literature depicting this is explored below.

Music and audio entertainment applications within the metaverse are one aspect of metaverse leisure that is facilitated through NFTs. NFTs have become a significant trend, especially in the audio industry, with the potential to reshape the value chains in creative industries. They have been used to monetize digital assets and democratize access to them, influencing more and more artists to release their music within the metaverse. This has surged an array of musical content available for users in the metaverse, as a plethora of songs can be selected for listening in VR (Fernandes et al., 2022). The musical possibilities offered within the metaverse scale beyond simple leisurely listening. The 3D visualizations promote the use of clubs and raves within the metaverse as well. These spaces utilize NFTs in the form of music and beats to reflect concerts into the augmented landscape. This tokenization process creates fully functioning, virtual musical events in which artists can broadcast their talent to metaverse users. These events can be monetized similarly to a physical concert or club through cryptocurrencies as the medium of exchange, providing artists with direct access to funds.

In addition to musical purposes, NFTs have begun to be implemented for the tokenization of podcasts as well. The podcast industry has seen immense growth in the past decade, and many influencers are transitioning their audio-based businesses into the metaverse as well. Due to the ability to tokenize these audio clips into NFTs, podcast creators can distribute their content to listeners with direct ownership of their content. In turn, listeners can access a more immersive experience in which the podcast is complemented by 3D visuals and graphics projected within the metaverse (Fernandes et al., 2022).

One of the most apparent uses of cryptocurrency and NFTs within the metaverse is displayed within the gaming industry. The global gaming market was valued at 173.70 billion USD in 2020, and it is expected to reach a value of 314.40 billion USD by 2026, registering a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 9.64 percent over 2021–2026. These platforms are attracting hundreds of thousands of new visitors in online traffic (Seifoddini, 2022). See Table 2.3 for a visualization of the metaverse competitive landscape which depicts this.

Many game developers are capitalizing on this growth and utilizing NFTs and cryptocurrencies to facilitate metaverse gaming experiences. For example, Epic Games, a major player in the metaverse gaming industry, has recently acquired photogrammetry studios Quixel and Capturing Reality, and the digital human startup 3Lateral. These applications promote the construction of digital worlds and utilize the tokenization process used for NFTs to implement gaming libraries into the metaverse (Vaughan-Jones, 2022).

These gaming experiences provide players with the ability to directly interact with other users and the environment, as decentralized digital currencies serve as the medium of exchange.
