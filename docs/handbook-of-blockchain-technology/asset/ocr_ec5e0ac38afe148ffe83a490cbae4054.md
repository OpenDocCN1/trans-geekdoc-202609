# 5.6 CONCLUSIONS

The NFT concept is not a new one, but it has been around since 2012 when CC made their appearance on the Bitcoin network. Despite the limitations imposed by the scripting capabilities of Bitcoin, CC initiated the discussion about NFTs and contributed to shaping their future. The launch of Ethereum in 2015 brought a revolution in this space and enabled more expansive NFT creation. Since then, multiple significant works have come to the surface such as the CP collection in 2017 and the Ethereum ERC-721 standard in 2018. On the Bitcoin blockchain the Taproot upgrade contributed to the development of the Ordinals Protocol. The latter allows Satoshi identification, on-chain functionality, decentralization, and supports the development of Bitcoin NFTs.

Rodarmor’s discovery further enhanced Bitcoin’s NFT prospects by embedding digital formats onto Satoshis. The Ordinals Protocol proposed by Rodarmor in 2023 opens up new avenues for research and development in the Bitcoin NFT landscape. A key difference between Bitcoin NFTs and NFTs minted on other blockchains is the additional level of scarcity inherent in the former, which is determined by the specific Satoshis they are associated with.

Although Ordinals NFTs’ benefits are impressive, there is a debate that persists among Bitcoin community members. Part of this community views Bitcoin Ordinals with skepticism as they observe a negative impact on network congestion. In addition, they argue that Bitcoin was not designed to store data. These members foresee that Bitcoin NFTs’ digital content stored on the blockchain will lead to a phenomenon called “Bitcoin blockchain pollution”. Moreover, there are environmental concerns and scalability issues that need to be addressed. On the contrary, Ordinals provide a wide range of benefits to Bitcoin. A significant one relates to the fact that it brings in additional fees from NFT transactions and increases miners’ rewards. Thus, the network becomes self-sustainable, and miners have an additional reason to participate in Bitcoin mining.

The introduction of BRC-20 in the context of Ordinals demonstrates novel tokenization possibilities on the Bitcoin blockchain. Although it is still in its infancy, its potential and opportunities for the development of new Bitcoin use cases are well recognized. Today, its role, impact on the network, and alignment with Bitcoin’s purpose have been understood. Like Bitcoin Ordinals, it has its own set of benefits and challenges, with the latter including limited functionality, immaturity, security vulnerabilities, low transaction speed, transaction fees, and more.

Ordinals and BRC-20 are not the end of NFTs journey but an evolutionary stage in the Bitcoin protocol. In the years to come, we expect additional work on both of them and anticipate further disruption in the NFTs ecosystem.
