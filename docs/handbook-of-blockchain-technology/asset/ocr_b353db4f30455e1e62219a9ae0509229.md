In the context of PoL, each round commences with the participant invoking the PoLRound function and submitting the most recent block to a designated chain. Upon the conclusion of the ROUND TIME, the participant activates the PoLMine function to create a new block. This newly minted block is then linked to its predecessor via the new block’s headers. The protocol stipulates an inspection for parent blocks in cases where the preceding block deviates from the round block (Milutinovic et al., 2016).

**Proof of Disease (PoD):** The Proof of Disease CA, is designed exclusively for use in the healthcare blockchain, aiming to provide speedy, cost-effective access to high-quality medical care data. This approach separates miners into two categories: currency miners and medical miners. Medical miners verify the legitimacy and appropriateness of medical transactions and health status inputs on the healthcare blockchain ledger. Currency miners are also responsible for evaluating financial transactions in order to detect and avoid potential threats such as Sybil and double-spending attacks. PoD’s is based on the Ethereum blockchain and thus, the transaction fee structure and reward mechanisms for currency miners are consistent with Ethereum’s existing infrastructure (Talukder et al., 2018).

**Raft:** The Raft CA (Huang et al., 2018) serves as a methodology for distributed systems, designed with an emphasis on simplicity and ease of understanding. Its aim is to address the challenge of achieving an agreement on a shared state across multiple servers, even in scenarios where one or more servers experience failures. The shared state is typically maintained with a replicated log, which is, in most cases, a data structure. The system’s full functionality is assured, provided that the majority of the servers remain operational. In the Raft model, consensus is achieved by electing a leader within the cluster. The leader is responsible for processing client requests and managing the replication of the log to other servers within the network. Moreover, Raft breaks down the complexity of consensus into three sub-problems:

+   1. **Leader Election:** In the event of the leader’s failure, the protocol necessitates the election of a new leader.
+   2. **Log Replication:** The leader must ensure the synchronization of all servers’ logs with its own, through replication.
+   3. **Safety Ensurance:** If a server has committed a log entry for a particular index, it must be guaranteed that no other server can apply a different log entry for the same index.

**Proof of Personhood (PoP)** is a CA designed specifically to address security issues such as Sybil and double-spending attacks (Borge et al., 2017). PoP ensures pseudonymous accountability by associating real-world identities with the coin minting process. This CA combines the RandHound algorithm, the Pseudonym party concept, and the ByzCoin protocol. Aspiring coin minters must first join PoP as members of the Pseudonym Party. Membership involves physical presence at a specific location where the Pseudonym Party is hosted. It’s worth noting that without attending these events, an individual cannot participate in the minting process within this blockchain. Participants in the Pseudonym Party are each given a token. This token has several functions, including authentication, validation, and use within the RandHound algorithm. The token has a set validity time during which its possessor is allowed to mint.
