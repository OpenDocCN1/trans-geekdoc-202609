It is evident that, in recent years, due to numerous uncertainties and the absence of appropriate incentives and regulations, Blockchain and ML are two technologies that, despite being successful, are utilized by only a small portion of the market, which is comprised of large corporations.

According to a report published by the Center for Data Innovation (2020) in response to the European Commission’s white paper, the “biggest challenge for the EU and its member states is that many Europeans do not trust Machine Learning and view it as a technology to be feared and held back from, rather than embraced and promoted.” Also, we must find a solution to the reality that people with the relevant qualifications frequently leave our nation to take advantage of possibilities we lack, a trend that must be reversed (see Figures 17.2 and 17.3) in order to maintain company competitiveness. Additionally, there are several programs that aim to encourage the development of new ideas and involve small and medium-sized Italian businesses in figuring out how to solve this problem.

Already in 2018, the MISE (Ministry of Economic Development) deemed it a top priority for our nation to understand, delve deeper into, and address the topic of ML in order to strengthen research in the sector, attract talent and business ideas, and encourage public and private investment (Ministry of Economic Development 2018).

In 2022, MISE provided a “Fund for the Development of Machine Learning, Blockchain, and Internet of Things Technologies and Applications” with a budget of EUR 45 million, of which EUR 25 million was for the development of ML-based pilot projects, EUR 10 million was for the development of Blockchain-based pilot projects, and EUR 10 million was for the development of Internet of Things applications (Ministry of Economic Development 2022).

On the other hand, international companies such as EY, the Ministry of Culture, and the National Development Agency (Invitalia) are promoting hackathons in order to invest in and implement viable and innovative projects based on emerging technologies such as Blockchain and ML, create partnerships, and reinvent themselves with an eye toward new developments involving various startups (EY Italy 2021). In addition, Deloitte conducted a comprehensive survey in collaboration with EFMA, a global non-profit organization founded by banks and insurance companies, which polled more than 3,000 people in financial services in 2017 and revealed a mix of concern and enthusiasm, with 32 percent of the companies indicating that they were in the process of developing ML-based applications to improve customer experience and increase the level of automation.

In 2022, new Deloitte research (Deloitte 2021) conducted on a sample of more than 2,000 global business leaders from a variety of industries revealed that 94 percent of respondents believed that the introduction of ML was crucial to the success of every business. However, despite an increase in the number of applications implemented compared to previous years, the desired results were not often achieved due to the presence of obstacles. A cultural process would need to be implemented within the companies that would possibly lead to a change in leadership to ensure the redesign of certain operating methods, the hiring of qualified individuals, and the retraining of the existing workforce. The selection of the most appropriate environments for the development of technology, with a focus on ethics and responsibility, is a further factor that should not be overlooked.
