# Dr Leonidas Katelaris

Dr Leonidas Katelaris is a post-doctoral researcher at the Institute for the Future (IFF) at the University of Nicosia. He played a crucial role in the successful implementation of UNIC’s open course on NFTs and the Metaverse, marking the first-ever university course to be delivered on-chain and within the metaverse. Joining the IFF in 2019 as a researcher, he participated in EU projects with a focus on Blockchains, DLTs, NFTs, and the Metaverse. His contributions to the institute span various roles and responsibilities, including: (a) serving as Managing Director at the Distributed Ledgers Research Centre (DLRC); (b) acting as Lecturer/Trainer for the Cyprus Police course on Financial Investigations for Digital Currencies; (c) being the Activity Manager for the European Union Agency for Law Enforcement Training (CEPOL) training course hosted by the University of Nicosia; (d) working as a Teaching Assistant for the world-leading Blockchain and Digital Currency MSc program at the University of Nicosia; and (e) being a member of the team behind the Open Metaverse Initiative (OMI) at the University of Nicosia. Leonidas holds a BSc in Digital Systems from the University of Piraeus and an MSc in Digital Systems, with a specialization in Network Oriented Systems, from the University of Piraeus. His MSc thesis focused on “Optimized Resource Provisioning based on Collective Intelligence in Service Level Agreements in Cloud Computing.” Leonidas obtained his PhD in Information Systems from the Department of Digital Systems at the University of Piraeus, where his dissertation was titled “Innovative Customer Behavior Forecasting Framework for Subscription-based Organizations.” His areas of expertise include Information Systems, Blockchain, DLTs, Non-fungible Tokens (NFTs), and the Metaverse. He has authored and co-authored scientific publications in these domains.

# Andreas Kazamias

Andreas Kazamias is a results-oriented senior executive with extensive international experience in electronic payment solutions, strategy formulation and implementation, cost management, establishing performance management frameworks and continuous improvement. Andreas has worked in Australia, Asia, SE Asia, Europe, UK and LATAM. Most of the entities that he has worked with are financial institutions, payment processors and fintechs. Since 2020, Andreas has been investing in Blockchain and cryptocurrency education at the University of Nicosia, which has included completing certification courses and the university’s MSc course in Blockchain and Digital Currency, where he graduated in June 2023 with Distinction (cum laude). Andreas is driven by establishing strategic mutually beneficial partnerships and relationships with stakeholders, vendors and service providers; adept at building and retaining high performance teams by hiring developing and motivating skilled professionals, and is equally, very hands-on with a proven ability in executing end-to-end projects across payments and technology ecosystems.
