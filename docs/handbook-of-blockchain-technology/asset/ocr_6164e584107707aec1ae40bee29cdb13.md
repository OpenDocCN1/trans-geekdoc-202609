+   - The scores of the 20 countries were cross-evaluated with the corresponding 20 country scores as displayed in Appendix A, via both the Spearman and Pearson correlation mechanisms. Both correlation coefficient scores, 0.861946 via Spearman and 0.759762 via Pearson, indicate high correlation and, therefore, a high degree of accuracy in our methodology.
