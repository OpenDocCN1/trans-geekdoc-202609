# Table 10.3 Selected articles for SLR

| Reference | Titles | Subject's Goals and Summary |
|---|---|---|
| Sharma, M. and Joshi, S. (2021) | Barriers to blockchain adoption in the Health Care Industry: An Indian Perspective | This article explores blockchain’s adoption in Indian healthcare. A literature study and semi-structured interviews with eight research limitations in India. Fifteen Indian healthcare obstacles were identified. TISM effects BC. Fuzzy MICMAC predicts roadblocks. The study faults legal illiteracy and poor management. |
| Rugviciute, A. and Mehrpouya, A. (2019) | Blockchain, a Panacea for Development Accountability? A Study of the Barriers and Enablers for Blockchain’s Adoption by Development Aid Organizations | This study examines contributors’ views on blockchain technology’s aid impact. The investigation demonstrates no common blockchain technology idea, characteristics, or engagement. Expertise, complexity, and security issues are challenges. Blockchain initiatives lack regulations, relationships, and knowledge generation. |
| Saif et al. (2022) | Blockchain Implementation Challenges in Developing Countries: An Evidence-based Systematic Review and Bibliometric Analysis | Blockchain in emerging nations is investigated. Technology, governance, organizational and environmental concerns, and knowledge were identified. Poor nations dread blockchain’s technology, governance, environment, and knowledge. Poor nations have blockchain issues. To expedite blockchain adoption in poor nations, disruptive technology practitioners, legislators, consultants, IT specialists, entrepreneurs, and senior business management must overcome these challenges. |
| Andoni et al. (2019) | Blockchain Technology in the Energy Sector: A Systematic Review of Challenges and Opportunities | This article discusses blockchain system topologies and consensus processes. Next, it analyzes blockchain literature and commercial examples for the energy industry. The study assesses 140 blockchain applications to identify their energy potential. Field implementation platform, and consensus strategy characterize these attempts. For each use case, blockchain technologies and drawbacks are explained. The article highlights challenges and market restrictions the technology must overcome to reach the mainstream. |
