Over the last several years, the ML initiatives at different companies have become a lot more complex and started to involve a lot more functions and people at the companies. I've found myself talking to business executives more about ML strategies and organizational design to enable broad adoption across their enterprise. I have been asked to help design complex ML platforms using a wide range of technologies for large enterprises across many business units that meet stringent security and compliance needs. There have been more architecture and process discussions about ML workflow orchestration and operations in recent years than ever before. And and more companies are looking to train ML models of enormous size with terabytes of data. The number of ML models trained and deployed by some companies has gone up to thousands. security-sensitive customers have also been looking for guidance on ML privacy, explainability, and data model bias. As a practitioner in ML solutions architecture, I've found the skills and knowledge required to be effective in this function have changed drastically.

So, where ML solutions architecture fit in this complex business, data, science, and technology Venn diagram? Based on my years of experience with companies of different sizes and in different industries, I see ML solutions architecture as an overarching discipline that connects the various pieces of an ML initiative covering everything from the business requirements to the technology. An ML architecture interacts with different business and technology partners, comes up with ML solutions for the business problems, and designs the technology platforms to run the ML solutions.
