# Preface

Artificial intelligence and machine learning (ML) become increasingly prevalent in many industries, there is an increasing demand for ML solutions architects who can translate business needs into ML solutions and design technology platforms. This book is designed to help people learn ML concepts, algorithms, system architecture patterns, and ML tools to solve business and technical challenges, with an emphasis on large-scale ML architecture and operations in an enterprise setting.

The book first introduces ML and business fundamentals, as the types of ML, business cases, and ML algorithms. It then dives into data management for ML and the various AWS services for building a data management architecture for ML.

After the data management dive deep, the book focuses on two technical approaches to building ML platforms: using open source technologies such as Kubernetes, Kubeflow, MLFlow, and Seldon Core, and the use of managed ML services such as Amazon SageMaker, Step Functions, and CodePipeline.

The book then gets into advanced ML engineering techniques, including distributed model training and low-latency model serving to meet large-scale model training and high-performance model serving requirements.

Governance and privacy are important considerations for running models in production. In this book, I also cover ML governance requirements and how an ML platform can support ML governance in areas such as documentation, model inventory, bias detection, model explainability, and model privacy.

Building ML-powered solutions do not always require building ML models or infrastructure from scratch. In the book's last chapter, I will introduce AI services and the problems that AI services can help solve. You will learn the core capabilities of some AI services and where you can use them for building ML-powered business applications.
