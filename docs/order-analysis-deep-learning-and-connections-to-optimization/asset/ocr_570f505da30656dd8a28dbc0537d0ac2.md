**Definition 2.6** ([74, Definition 3.2]) Under Assumption 2.2, a functional $\psi : Y \times Y \rightarrow R$ is called a $-\kappa$ representing functional iff

> $-\kappa(\tilde{y}) = \{y \in Y | \psi(y, \tilde{y}) \le 0\}$ for all $\tilde{y} \in Y$.

**Example 2.11** ([74, Example 3.5]) Let $(Y, ||\cdot||)$ be a real normed space, and let a map $L : Y \rightarrow Y^*$ be given. Then we investigate the set-valued map $\mathcal{K} : Y \rightarrow Y$ with

> $\mathcal{K}(\tilde{y}) := \{y \in Y | L(\tilde{y})(y) \ge ||y||\}$ for all $\tilde{y} \in Y$
