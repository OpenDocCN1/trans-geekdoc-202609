Chapter 6 presents the basics of deep learning, such as deep neural networks, activation functions, supervised learning, and gradient methods, along with numerical examples. The gradient methods include the usual gradient descent methods, together with a new quasi-Newton type gradient descent method, as well as different variants of the stochastic gradient methods. Deep learning with set-valued inputs is the subject of Chap. 7. The focus is on an extended activation function and on different distance terms for sets.
