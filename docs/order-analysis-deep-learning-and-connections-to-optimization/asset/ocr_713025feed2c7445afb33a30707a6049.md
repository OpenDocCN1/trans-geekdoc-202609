There is another important variant of a stochastic gradient method that is not discussed in this chapter. Kingma and Ba [86] introduced a much-cited method for adaptive moment estimation (Adam). an adaptive step size is used. The special adaptation rule uses the second raw moment of the gradient, which has a significant advantage over fixed step sizes. Adam is useful for problems with large datasets and many variables. This method, and variants of it, seems to be one of the most preferred training methods for LLMs. Adam is also implemented in MATLAB.

Finally, we would like to point out that it is possible to combine a gradient method with a stochastic approach to determine a global minimal point of the objective function. For this, we refer to a method by Schäfler [122].
