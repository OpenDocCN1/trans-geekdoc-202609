# Section 6.5

This section is dedicated to supervised learning with deep neural networks. The resulting optimization problems indeed a challenge, especially for modern applications. Examples of this include computer vision and large language models.

An introduction to the interesting possibilities of computer vision can be found at Klingler [87] from the company visi. Objects such as pedestrians, cars, trucks, etc. can be not only recognized in an image but also tracked. These are important steps toward autonomous driving. For handwritten digit recognition, we refer to a review article published by Kadhre, Desale, More, and Palhagdal [81].

Large language models (LLMs) are among the most challenging applications today. They can combine images and text (in which case they are multimodal) and generate natural language. The US company OpenAI provides a good example of this with ChatGPT-4 software. The number of parameters used in this LLM is very high, but not known (as of November 2023). See Remark 6.9 for a combination of ChatGPT with MATLAB. The linguistic startup European Alpha also combines images and text. It uses up to 300 billion parameters [4]. Such software can also answer questions about an image (see [3]). In 2023, another promising startup Mistral was founded in France. Its Mistral 8X7B uses up to 46 billion parameters (as of January 2024). Unlike others, Mistral AI works with open LLMs and supports the open-source community.
