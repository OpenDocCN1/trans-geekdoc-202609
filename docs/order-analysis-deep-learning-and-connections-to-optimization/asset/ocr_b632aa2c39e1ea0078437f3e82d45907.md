We now turn our attention to problems of set optimization. Based on the set less order relation in a real linear space $Y$, we consider a family $\mathcal{F}$ of nonempty subsets of $Y$, and we investigate minimal sets of $\mathcal{F}$.

**Definition 5.2** Let $C$ be a convex cone in a real linear space $Y$, and let $\mathcal{F}$ be a family of nonempty subsets of $Y$. A $	ilde{A} \in \mathcal{F}$ is called *minimal set* of $\mathcal{F}$ iff

$A \preceq_s A, A \in \mathcal{F} \Longrightarrow 	ilde{A} \preceq_s A.$

**Example 5.2** Let $Y := \mathbb{R}^2$ and $C := \mathbb{R}^2_+$ be chosen. Let $\mathcal{F} := \{A_1, \dots, A_5\}$ denote a family of sets as illustrated in Fig. 5.2. In this case, we have the only set inequalities $A_2 \preceq_s A_1, A_3 \preceq_s A_1, A_4 \preceq_s A_5$, and then it is evident that $A_2, A_3$, and $A_4$ are the only minimal sets of $\mathcal{F}$.

**Remark 5.2** For the following investigations, we introduce an abbreviation for the set of all minimal and maximal elements of a set, respectively. Here, we use an extension of the notion of minimality given in Definition 4.1. Let $C$ be a convex cone in a real linear space $Y$, and let $\emptyset 
eq A \subseteq Y$ be given. Let $\preceq_c$ denote the preorder induced by $C$. The set

$\min(A) := \{ 	ilde{a} \in A \mid a \preceq_c 	ilde{a}, a \in A \Longrightarrow 	ilde{a} \preceq_c a \}$

denotes the set of minimal elements of $A$, and the set

$\max(A) := \{ 	ilde{a} \in A \mid 	ilde{a} \preceq_c a, a \in A \Longrightarrow a \preceq_c 	ilde{a} \}$

is called the set of maximal elements of $A$. Of course, if the convex cone $C$ is pointed, then

$\min(A) = \{ a \in A \mid a \preceq_c A, a \in A \Longrightarrow a = ar{a} \}$
