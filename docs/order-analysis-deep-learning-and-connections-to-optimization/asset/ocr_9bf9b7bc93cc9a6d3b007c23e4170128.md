# Section 4.2

The results of this section were already developed by Jahn [74, Subsection 4.2]. Jahn and Khan [76] developed optimality conditions for concrete vector optimization problems with a variable order structure used for the objective map and the constraints. This approach does not use of representing functionals. Due to the variable order, the Lagrange multipliers are not constant but they are functions of the variables.
