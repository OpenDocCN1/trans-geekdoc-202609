# # Chapter 3
Application in Nonlinear Optimization

**Abstract** In this chapter, we investigate optimization problems with a real-valued objective functional and a general inequality constraint. We do not assume convexity except for the preorder cone, or generalized convexity, so this approach is also valid for discrete-continuous nonlinear optimization problems. We restrict ourselves to problems with a unique minimal solution for which we present optimality conditions.

# ## 3.1 Optimality Conditions for a Unique Minimal Solution

We start with the problem formulation in nonconvex nonlinear optimization.

**Assumption 3.1** Let $X$ and $Y$ be real linear spaces, let $C$ be a convex cone in $Y$, and let $	ilde{S}$ be a nonempty subset of $X$. Let a functional $f : 	ilde{S} 	o \mathbb{R}$ and a map $g : 	ilde{S} 	o Y$ be given, and let the constraint set

> $S := \{x \in 	ilde{S} \mid g(x) \in -C\}$

nonempty.

Under this assumption, we investigate the nonlinear optimization problem

> $\min_{x \in S} f(x) \quad (3.1)$

with a general inequality constraint. Notice that we do not have convexity assumptions on $f, g$, and $	ilde{S}$. By Definition 1.21, $	ilde{x} \in 	ilde{S}$ is said to be a *minimal solution* of the optimization problem (3.1) iff

> $f(	ilde{x}) \le f(x)$ for all $x \in S$.

In this chapter, we work with a stronger minimality notion. $	ilde{x} \in 	ilde{S}$ is called a *unique minimal solution* of problem (3.1) iff

> $f(	ilde{x}) < f(x)$ for all $x \in S$ with $x 
eq 	ilde{x}$.
