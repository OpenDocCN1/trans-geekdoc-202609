# 2.10 Bibliographical Notes

Section 2.8
Section 2.8 presents results given by the author in [74, Subsection 3.6]. It is based on the theory on variable order structures developed by Eichfelder [33]. It is obvious that the notion of $-\mathcal{C}$ representing functionals can be easily extended to $-\mathcal{K}$ representing functionals.

Section 2.9
The results in this section were given by the author in [74, Subsection 3.7]. Since the functional $\psi$ is defined by the negative cone $-C$, these separation results are only restricted to a separation with $C$. In contrast, scalarizing functionals are not defined only for convex cones, so that more general separation statements result; we refer to Tammer and Weidner [132] and the references therein.
