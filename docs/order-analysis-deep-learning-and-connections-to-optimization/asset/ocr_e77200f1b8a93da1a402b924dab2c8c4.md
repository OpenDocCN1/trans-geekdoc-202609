Under appropriate assumptions, a relation between the subdifferential of the $-C$ representing functional at the origin and the dual cone can be given.

Theorem 2.2 ([74, Theorem 3.1]) Let $C$ be a convex cone of a real normed space $(Y, ||\cdot||)$, and let $\psi$ be a $-C$ representing functional, which is convex with $\psi(0) = 0$ and has a nonempty subdifferential $\partial\psi(0)$. Then it holds

```
$$\text{cl} (\partial\psi(0)) \subseteq C^*.$$$
```

By the definition of the subdifferential, we have for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$
