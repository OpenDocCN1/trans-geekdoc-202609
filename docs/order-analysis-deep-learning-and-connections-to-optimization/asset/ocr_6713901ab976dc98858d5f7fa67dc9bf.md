54 2 -C Representing Functionals

# Section 2.2

In this section, we define $-C$ representing functionals, where $C$ is a convex cone in a real linear space. These functionals have already been utilized by Eichfelder [35] and Jahn [73] without using this special name. Later, $-C$ representing functionals were used by Jahn [74]. It turns out that these functionals form a general class of functionals that group together all functionals from Sect. 2.1. In a paper by Ha [52], the equality (2.1) is also called the cone representation property (P4), where $\psi$ is an abstract scalarizing function.

Investigations of the subdifferential of scalarizing functionals are known in the research literature. # Section 2.7

It is obvious for a penalty functional for the negative convex cone that all zeros of this functional equals the negative convex cone. But it is also known from Bishop-Phelps theory that in special the zeros of a Bishop-Phelps functional equal the negative convex cone; a comprehensive study of such special cases was carried out by Ha and Jahn [54]. It was shown in [54, Lemma 2.1] that a Bishop-Phelps cone $C(\ell)$ is given by an equation and only if $\|\ell\|_{Y^*} = 1$. In the norm of the continuous linear functional $\ell$ plays an important role in the theory of Bishop-Phelps cones.
