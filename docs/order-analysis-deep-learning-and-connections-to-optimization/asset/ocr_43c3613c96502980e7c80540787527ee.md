Proposition 5.1 characterizes sets arising in Definition 5.1 by inequalities in R. A rewriting of set as inequalities was already given by Hernández and Rodríguez-Marín [59, Theorem 3.10, (iii) in 2007 using an extension of the Gertzwistar scalarizing function [43]. Such a rewriting of set as inequalities was also given by Köbis and Köbis [88, Theorem 3.3 and 3.8] for Gertzwistar scalarizing functions and various order relations. For the signed distance functional can be found in the papers of Gutiérrez, Jiménez, Miglierna, and Molina [47, Proposition 4.11], Chen, Ansari, and Yao [24], own, Jiménez, Novo, and Vilchez [78, Theorem 5.1], [79, Theorem 4.1] and Ha [51, Proposition 3]. However, in Proposition 5.1 one does not need an explicit form of the scalarizing functional.

The results in Proposition 5.2 and Remark 5.1 are also marked by Köbis and Köbis [88, Remark 1] for specific scalarizing functionals. The characterization of a minimal set in Corollary 5.1 shows that set optimization problems with fixed order structure can be difficult optimization problems.

The definition of the less variable order relation (Definition 5.3) was first given by Eichfelder and Pilecka [36, Definition 2.4] together with a variety of other variable order relations for sets. For a subsequent proper see also the work of Köbis, Le, and Tammer [90]. Proposition 5.5 was first given in the proof of Theorem 4.1 by Eichfelder and Pilecka [37] and subsequently by Köbis, Le, and Tammer, Theorems 3 and 6 for special scalarizing functionals.

It is already noted on page 90 that Theorem 5.1 is only a slight extension of the result of Eichfelder and Pilecka [37, Theorem 4.1, (iii)].
