**Theorem 4.5** ([74, Theorem 4.3]) *Let Assumption 4.2 be satisfied, and $\psi$ be a $-K$ representing functional functional. Then we have*

+   (a) $	ilde{y}$ nondominated element of $T \iff \psi(y - 	ilde{y}, y) > 0$ for all $y \in T\setminus{	ilde{y}}$
(b) $	ilde{y}$ minimal element of $T \iff \psi(y - 	ilde{y}, 	ilde{y}) > 0$ for all $y \in T\setminus{	ilde{y}}$

**Proof***

(a) By Definition 4.4 (a) and Remark 2.5, we have

> $	ilde{y}$ is a nondominated element of the set $T$\nLongiff y \in T : y \le_1 \tilde{y} \text{ and } y \neq \tilde{y}$
Longiff y \in T\setminus{\tilde{y}} : \psi(y - \tilde{y}, y) \le 0$\nLongiff \psi(y - \tilde{y}, y) > 0$ for all $y \in T\setminus{\tilde{y}}$

and the assertion is shown.

+   (b) Following the lines of the proof of part (a), we obtain

> $	ilde{y}$ is a minimal element of the set $T$\nLongiff y \in T : y \le_2 \tilde{y} \text{ and } y \neq \tilde{y}$
Longiff y \in T\setminus{\tilde{y}} : \psi(y - \tilde{y}, \tilde{y}) \le 0$\nLongiff \psi(y - \tilde{y}, \tilde{y}) > 0$ for all $y \in T\setminus{\tilde{y}}$

which completes the proof.
