# 1.1.1 Neighborhood Types between Pixels

In both types of operations, it is necessary to determine the way in which two pixels establish a neighborhood relationship between them. Two types of neighborhoods can commonly be distinguished.

+   * **Neighborhood 4**. Under this approach, a neighborhood is considered to exist if a pixel is in direct relation to a neighboring pixel that is above, below, to the left, or to the right of the pixel in question (Figure 1.5a).
+   * **Neighborhood 8**. Under this approach, a neighborhood is considered to exist if a pixel, in addition to the relationships specified by neighborhood 4, also has a direct relationship with the pixels diagonal to it (Figure 1.5b).
