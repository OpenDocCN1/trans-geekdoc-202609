$$T_x : x' = x + b_x \cdot y$$
$$T_y : y' = y + b_y \cdot x$$
(3.7)
$$
\begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & b_x \\ b_y & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}
$$

# 3.1.1.4 Rotation

Allows the image rectangle to be rotated by a certain angle \(\alpha\), considering the center of the image as the center of rotation. That is:

$$T_x : x' = x \cdot \cos(\alpha) + y \cdot \sin(\alpha)$$
$$T_y : y' = -x \cdot \sin(\alpha) + y \cdot \cos(\alpha)$$
(3.8)
$$
\begin{bmatrix} x' \\ y' \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos(\alpha) & \sin(\alpha) \\ -\sin(\alpha) & \cos(\alpha) \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x \\ y \end{bmatrix}
$$
