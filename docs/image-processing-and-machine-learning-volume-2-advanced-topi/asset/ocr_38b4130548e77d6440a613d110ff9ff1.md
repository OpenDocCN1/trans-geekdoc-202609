The reverse transformation of converting from the L*a*b* model to the CIEXYZ is defined by the calculation of the following equations:

```

Y = Y_{ref} \cdot f_2(Y') \\
Z = Z_{ref} \cdot f_2 \left( Y' - \frac{b^*}{200} \right) \quad (2.53) \)
```
