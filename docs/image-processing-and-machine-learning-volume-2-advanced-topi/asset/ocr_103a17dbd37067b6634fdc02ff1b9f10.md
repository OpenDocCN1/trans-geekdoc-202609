where \( T_1 \) is a small positive constant (Commonly 0.01). The gradient extent is estimated through convolutional masks for the resulting image \( \mathbf{I} \) and its respective reference \( \mathbf{R} \), determining \( \mathbf{GI} \) and \( \mathbf{GR} \), respectively. Hence, a local gradient map is determined by Equation 5.22.

\(\nS_{GM} (\mathbf{GI},\mathbf{GR}) = \frac{2 \cdot \mathbf{GI} \cdot \mathbf{GR} + T_1}{\mathbf{GI}^2 + \mathbf{GR}^2 + T_1} (5.22)\)

Once \( S_{PC} \) and \( S_{GM} \) are computed, the FSIM determined using Equation 5.23.

\(\nFSIM = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^2 S_{PC} \cdot S_{GM} \cdot I_j}{\mathbf{GI}^2 \cdot \mathbf{GR}^2 + T_1} (5.23)\)

For determining the \( MD \), the maximal difference between a resulting image \( \mathbf{I} \) and its reference \( \mathbf{R} \) is computed by using Equation 5.24.

\(\nMD = \max_{\forall i, j \in M \times N} |\mathbf{I}(i,j) - \mathbf{R}(i,j)| (5.24)\)

The \( MSE \) is used to determine the affinity of segmented image \( \mathbf{I} \) regarding its reference \( \mathbf{R} \) by the subtraction of the pixel intensity of the image \( \mathbf{I} \) with the elements of its image \( \mathbf{R} \) and then determining the averaged value of the error. The RMS is computed using the model of Equation 5.25.

\(\nMSE = \frac{1}{MN} \sum_{i=1}^M \sum_{j=1}^N \left( \mathbf{I}(i,j) - \mathbf{R}(i,j) \right)^2 (5.25)\)

The \( NAE \) determines the affinity of segmented image \( \mathbf{I} \) regarding its reference \( \mathbf{R} \) computing the normalized absolute differences generated by Equation 5.24.

\(\nNAE = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^M \sum_{j=1}^N |\mathbf{I}(i,j) - \mathbf{R}(i,j)|}{\sum_{i=1}^M \sum_{j=1}^N \mathbf{R}(i,j)} (5.26)\)

The \( NK \) computes the similarities between the resulting image \( \mathbf{I} \) and its reference \( \mathbf{R} \) with regard to their correlation. Thus, \( NK \) is estimated under Equation 5.27.

\(\nNK = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^M \sum_{j=1}^N \mathbf{I}(i,j) \cdot \mathbf{R}(i,j)}{\sum_{i=1}^M \sum_{j=1}^N \mathbf{R}(i,j)} (5.27)\)
