\( T_x^{-1} : x = x' - \begin{cases} z \cdot \tan(\beta_x) & \text{si } r \le r_{max} \\ 0 & \text{si } r > r_{max} \end{cases} \)
\( T_y^{-1} : y = y' - \begin{cases} z \cdot \tan(\beta_y) & \text{si } r \le r_{max} \\ 0 & \text{si } r > r_{max} \end{cases} \)
(3.40)

where:

\( dx = x' - x_c \)
\( dy = y' - y_c \)
\( r = \sqrt{d_x^2 + d_y^2} \)
\( z = \sqrt{r_{max}^2} \)
\( \beta_x = \left( 1 - \frac{1}{\rho} \right) \cdot \text{sen}^{-1} \left( \frac{d_x}{\sqrt{d_x^2 + z^2}} \right) \)
\( \beta_y = \left( 1 - \frac{1}{\rho} \right) \cdot \text{sen}^{-1} \left( \frac{d_y}{\sqrt{d_y^2 + z^2}} \right) \)
(3.41)
