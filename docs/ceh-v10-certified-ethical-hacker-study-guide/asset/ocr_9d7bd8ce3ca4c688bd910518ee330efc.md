# Mobile Device Attacks

Third-party applications are the best way to get access to mobile devices. Both Google and Apple vet applications that are submitted to their app marketplaces. This is not a perfect process, however. There have been applications that have made it through this vetting process in both marketplaces that are malicious in one form or another.
