# # Overview of Ethics

Before we start talking about ethical hacking, I will cover the most important aspect of that, which is ethics. You'll notice it's not referred to as “hacking ethically.” It’s ethical hacking. The important part is in the front. Ethics can be a challenging subject because you will find that it is not universal. Different people have different views of what is ethical and what is not ethical. It’s essential, though, that you understand what ethics are and what is considered ethical and unethical from the perspective of the Certified Ethical Hacker certification.
