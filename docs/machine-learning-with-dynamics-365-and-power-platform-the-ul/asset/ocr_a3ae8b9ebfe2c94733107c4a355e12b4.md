After knowing your leave balances in detail, you can then perform actions from the same team’s chat that you are having with the chatbot. You only must enter a specific time off request, and the chatbot will itself create a draft, including a calendar view with start and end dates and your remaining balance. The draft request stays available until you decide on your dates. When you have decided the dates, just go to your saved drafts, make any edits if needed, and submit. Managers can easily review your request and approve employees’ requests with just a few clicks. This process saves a lot of time for employees and empowers them with self-service access.
