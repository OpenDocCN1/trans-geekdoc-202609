Benefits of product insights include:

+   - **Improve your product.** Product insights help you improve the quality of the product and customer experience by monitoring its usage.
+   - **Product planning.** Product insights help you identify the needs of customers using data to plan your future releases accordingly. It enables you to engage directly with customers for ideas and feedback.
+   - **Market value.** Product insights help you to discover how popular your product is. It helps you to figure out the appreciative and non-appreciative features of your product.
+   - **Proactive servicing.** Product insights use telemetry to identify problems in products/services and warn you to prioritize repairs or design fixes and maintenance.
