Now let us see who can benefit from the connected store:

+   - **Associates.** They receive timely alerts with precise instructions to execute the daily tasks efficiently.
+   - **Store managers.** They know the stores quite well. Connected stores help them with contextual data about their stores. They see store traffic and patterns and have data available to them about queues and wait times. All the contextual data that the store manager receives from one store can be consolidated and understood on a multi-store level. If making a certain change has worked for one store, it might work for all of the store branches.
