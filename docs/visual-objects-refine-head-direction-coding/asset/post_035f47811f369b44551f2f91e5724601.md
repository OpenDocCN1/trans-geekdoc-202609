## Cran surgery
Surgeries were performed anesthetized with a subcutaneous injection of ketamine (0.05 mg/kg), midazolam (5 mg/kg) and medetomidineidine (FMM complex). We were using a heating pad and kept at 37°C with a temperature-controlled probe during surgery. Local anesthesia was performed with lidocaine (Bayer, Bayern). A large cranial window was created using a skull dental drill, extension from +2.5 mm after peri, ensuring the dura remained intact. The wound was closed with sutures and cyanoacrylate (glue) and it was reinforced with dental cement (Sper-Bound). Anesthesia was reverted by administering intravenous bupentaniline (0.5 mg/kg) and injections of buprenorphine (0.1 mg/kg). After a 2-day recovery period the head was prepared, and the anesthesia was reverted by the REM combo, with ketamine (2.5 mg/kg), and naloxone (1.2 mg/kg).
