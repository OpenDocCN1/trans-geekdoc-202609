# Response delay

For each HD, all trials and stimuli were presented to get an overall response of the cell. The response delay was defined as the point when the firing rate of the cell rose above twice the standard deviation of the preceding.

# Perfusion and histology

Following the termination of the electrophysiological experiments, an animal was deeply perfused using pentobarbital (100 mg/kg) and urea (16 mg/kg) then perfused concurrently with 0.5% phosphate-buffered saline solution fixed by 4% formaldehyde.

In case of chronic recording, the electrode was retracted to remove the probe. Brains were inserted and kept for 4 hours after changing the solution to 30% sucrose phosphate-buffered saline until. After freezing in a -20°C, brains were frozen with a freezing microtome at 50μm thickness.

Sections were stained with DAPT and mounted in Mifogli medium. Sections containing probes were incubated with a primary anti-IIIβ antibody (1:200) and a secondary antibody labeled with fluorophore 488 overnight at 4°C, followed by

fluorescence microscopy was used to obtain images of neurons and the tracks of silicon probes.

In case of acute recording, neurons were collected in ACSF solution. Brains were washed and embedded in 4% ACSF. Coronal slices were cut using a vibr microtome (Vibrotome TX) with a thickness of 100μm and stained with DAPT.

Images were captured using a Leica TCS laser confocal microscopy with a 20x objective (Leica Microsystems). Images processed using LAS software and Adobe Photoshop.

# SSN model

The supragranular ring network model (46), the cytopically connected network N=50 and N=51 neurons are placed around a ring with θ = [-π, π]. The following equations describe

```
$V_i = V_{rest} + V_{max} - \sum_{j} W_{ij}(V_j)$
```

where $V_i$ and $V_j$ refer to the membrane potential and current of an unit neuron at θ₀ and θ respectively, $V_{max}$ is external input, and $W_{ij}$ is the strength of synaptic connection from θ₀ to θ, which is fixed such that the sum of the weights $W_{ij}$, $W_{ji}$ and $I$ is

```
$I_{h}(\theta_0) = I(t) + I_{max} \left[ \frac{\cos(\theta - \theta_0)}{\sin^2\theta} \right]$
```
