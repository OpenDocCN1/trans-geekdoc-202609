# 4.3.4. Energy metering, billing, and network data management

A prerequisite for many Internet Energy applications is the integration of smart meters and blockchain platforms. Smart consumption meters are part of advanced metering infrastructure, which is readily deployed in modern energy grids. AMI measures, collects, and analyzes energy usage. A smart meter has a two-way communication channel, usually assured by power-line communications (PLC), or mobile (e.g., LTE/5 G), or low-power mobile (e.g., NB-IoT), or long-range WAN networks (e.g., LoRaWAN). Apart from consumption reading, AMI may support demand-side optimization or outage management. Smart meters generally do not have unique decentralized identifiers and are simple transaction issuers (Section 3.5.2). Instead, all blockchain functions are provided by AMI gateways or even by the backend blockchain. This enables the use of the installed infrastructure, but BC identification at the level of individual meters could yield a far more accurate, verifiable, and efficient process. An early demonstration was Bankymood prepaid smart meter. It was a Bitcoin-based billing system for smart meters that operated in partnership with the crowdfunding platform, Sizuso. Donors transfer cryptocurrency to third-world schools equipped with a smart meter to provide energy directly to the school of their choice.
