Infura⁵³ focuses on instant, reliable infrastructure connecting us to Ethereum (mainnet and various test networks), Filecoin and IPFS. Off-chain applications can connect to Ethereum and IPFS via JSON-RPC over HTTPS and WebSocket, request-response times are up to 20 times faster than other services and self-hosted solutions. Infura nodes are running on the latest network upgrades with a minimum 99.9% uptime guarantee. The service is provided with per or subscription-based connections and full-time access to support teams.

A dashboard gives direct insights into the app's performance and API usage. This can be used to drill down into specific request methods or most active times to optimize the performance and better understand users.
