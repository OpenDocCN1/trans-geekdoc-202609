# 4.3 Smart energy

A traditional electric grid is an interconnected system for the production, transmiss-
elivery of electricity. The grid dynamically balances the demand and sup-
ly electric energy to assure stability and reliability of grid operation. Its immense
eeconomic dimension is reflected in the grid for accurate measures of consump-
, and well-defined billing and trading mechanisms among actors involved in the
electricity demand and supply. The electric grid is one of the critical infrastructures
 this is essential for any economic and social activity in the world.

A smart grid (SG) opens traditional electric grids, in terms of communication\and interoperability, and leverages new operational and business models related to
electric energy production, transport, storage, and communication. In terms of deploy-
ment smart grids can be considered an example of a highly geo-distributed IoT sys-
them high security and trust requirements. New technological developments and
new business, economic, and environmental expectations lead to a gradual transfor-
mward a smart grid concept. A smart grid is heavily based on information and
communication technologies, applied to the electricity domain. It enables new and
inative use cases in the utilities domain, which are technically infeasible in
the traditional grid.

The latest trends in this area are called Smart Energy, Internet of Energy, or En-
ergy Internet (EI). They indicate the ambition of sharing both energy and the related
data just as we used to sharing information on the Internet. This means that, for
example, participants can interact closely, make autonomous decisions, seamlessly
access different distributed and centralized energy resources, share energy to bal-
ance energy demand and supply, and act as prosumers – producers or consumers
of electrical power, or both. Blockchain technologies can efficiently support these
novel developments and ambitions for allowing data and monetary transactions for
energy trading. Applying distributed ledger is a viable option to provide a secure,
transparent, trusted, automated workflow for the advanced future Energy Internet
