A key decision factor should be the community gathered in the ecosystem. A successful ecosystem attracts and actively supports users and developers. A large active community that supports the ecosystem encourages an open, collaborative culture. A community with a large global footprint also indicates the level of acceptance of the platform among users and developers. The communities should not be seen only as users or customers of the ecosystem but rather as the external innovators. This attitude proved to be successful, for example, in many open source projects. Therefore, some of the DLT ecosystems have clearly defined mechanisms for collaboration with academia and enterprises.

Finally, the number and scope of use cases are or other indicators of what we can expect from a DLT ecosystem.
