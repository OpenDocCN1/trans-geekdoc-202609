# 3.6 Example of an industrial internet of things DApp

The Tetramax technology experiment CORONA60 combines distributed ledger technology with collaborative robotics. This proof of concept enables innovative IoT machine autonomy cases in manufacturing environments, as servitization and robotics-as-a-service. As an illustrative example, a 3-D printer can enhance its printing capabilities to other actors for a service. The service negotiation is then autonomously conducted through blockchain transactions and smart contracts. The printing machine records the process of production. This is used as a confirmation of the work done and is recorded in the blockchain. The basic use case diagram is depicted in Fig. 3.7.
