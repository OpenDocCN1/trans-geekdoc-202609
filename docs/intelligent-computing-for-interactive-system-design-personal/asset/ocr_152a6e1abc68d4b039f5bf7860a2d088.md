[2017], that data just became *much* more sensitive. In these situations, it is important to give users both notice (i.e., explaining what you will do with that data) and choice (i.e., an option not to use that feature). Even this can be complicated and can lead to inequity and other issues.

# ## 9.1.2.6 Q6: A General Classifier or Personal Classifiers?

Next, are you going to train one classifier that generalizes across all users, or a classifier that is personalized to each user? These are two extremes: the generalized classifier is ready out of the box, but can never adapt or change to the particular situation of the user. However, the completely personalized classifier will not work until it has enough data. In these cases, something in the middle may be preferred. One approach is to launch a service based on a generalized classifier, but to collect training data and retrain the model once it has been deployed to the user.

# ## 9.1.2.7 Q7: What Happens When the Classifier Is Wrong?

Finally, it is important to consider what will happen when your classifier infers context incorrectly, which it inevitably will do at least some of the time. Does the application misbehave in these cases? Does it give the user the ability to fix the incorrect inference? Or to disable the functionality that is connected to the classifier? It is important to consider what is the worst case scenario for an incorrect inference from your classifier, and what steps can you take to address its negative impacts?

# ## 9.2 Example: Inferring Phone Placement

One opportunity for inferring personal context from a mobile phone is to infer specific aspects of context about the phone, which in turn can be relevant for personal context. One such example is to infer where the phone has been placed. This can be useful as an aid to a flexible authentication system [Hayashi et al. 2013], it can be a useful context for interpreting the output of another sensor [Singla et al. 2018], or as a factor in a dynamic notification scheme [Weber et al. 2016, Komninos et al. 2018].
