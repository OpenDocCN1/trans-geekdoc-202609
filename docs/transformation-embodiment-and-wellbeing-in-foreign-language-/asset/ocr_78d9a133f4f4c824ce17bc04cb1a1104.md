# A Case Study in Adjustment and Transformation

Using terminology like *resistance*, *adaptive demands* and *psychological adjustment* may create the impression of extreme emotional states or intense psychological disruption. One of the companies running the subway system decided to change its policy regarding in-train announcements in English (Iki 2021). Rather than using automatic recordings of “native” speakers, train conductors were encouraged to announce coming stops in English themselves.
