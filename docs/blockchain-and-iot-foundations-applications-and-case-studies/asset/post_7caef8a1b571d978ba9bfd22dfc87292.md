The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of objects connected to the internet. Devices may now transmit sensor data online without human interaction. According to predictions, there will be about 26.66 billion IoT devices operating by 2019 and 75 billion IoT devices available by 2025. Millions of wearable sensors are among these connected gadgets, and are extensively used in healthcare. IoT-related spending reached $737 billion in 2016 and was expected to reach $1.29 trillion by 2020. Control, real-time monitoring, and independence or autonomous work are all functions of IoT. IoT tries to make life easier, people frequently invest in products that make their lives easier. This may be one of primary reasons why IoT is so popular. As a result, the number of IoT applications is increasing across numerous industries. IoT was incorporated into the child monitoring system to improve response times and provide parents a better sense of security.
