# Bitcoin Wallets

There are many Bitcoin wallets, including TREZOR, MultiBit Core, Coinbase, Blockchain, Exodus, MyCelium, Bitcoin middle, MultiBit HD, Electron, Copay, Amory. For security and privacy, Coinbase, MyCelium, Bitcoin blockchain, TREZOR, Exodus, MultiBit mobile, MultiBit HD are desirable, whereas Electrum, Copay, and Amory are not. They're unbiased wallets, except Coinbase. In large experience, Bitcoin wallets are classified into types 0, 1, and 2, on the basis of the capacities of logistic assets they require [31] (Figure 1.7).
