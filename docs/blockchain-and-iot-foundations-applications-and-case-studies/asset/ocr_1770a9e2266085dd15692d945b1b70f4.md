# SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT OF TOOLS IN SERVICING OPERATIONS OF POWER PLANTS

Siemens uses blockchain in its chain management activities specifically for managing the tools in servicing operations for power plants all over the world. The SCM DigiNiNetwork, an informal company-wide group of digitalization enthusiasts who design and implement solutions in this field, was behind the original initiative. The tool management sector where blockchain is involves a wide range of equipment and tools, from a simple cordless screwdriver to a CNC machine costing over a million euros.

The challenge that the tool management sector faces is that the contents of the return canters the individual tools shipped to the location of use in the first place. Previously, the number and condition of the devices and tools that were packed, transported, and received were recorded manually by the relevant employees. However, this manual documentation is error-prone and does not work well. This is why Siemens decided to use blockchain technology in it to guarantee the integrity of the new digitized process.

When a transport order is generated, it is automatically entered into the blockchain. This will show a list of associated and relevant partners (such as the logistics company), access to the document, and all papers associated with the freight shipment. All amendments are recorded and visible to all authorized parties. The blockchain technology used by the Siemens tool management gives Siemens the ability to view whether a tool is still in customs or being transported, whether the returned kits contain drills that need to be sharpened, or if an open-ended spanner is bent out of shape.
