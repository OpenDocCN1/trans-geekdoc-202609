# Internet of Things (IoT)

For protection, a blockchain can utilize an IoT infrastructure. There are challenges in IoT: system design (consolation bottleneck, lightweight design), facts management (tool renovation service management and discovery services), and protection (privacy, counterfeit, authentication, secure communication, and counterfeit hardware). Blockchain usage in IoT involves too overhead and scalability issues. Consequently to reduce the overhead and to beautify the authentication and authorization of factors, a three-layered modeling approach is proposed in [42–44]. In layer 1, there may be a cluster head of IoT devices and service offerings via local cluster heads. The cluster head is supposed to perform registration of the latest node, consultation key and control, and secure communication. In the second layer, cluster heads function under a base station and they gather and forward the data to the higher layer. Layer 3 collects the bottom stations to prepare the owner to control the tool to supply the statistics in the cloud server. IoT answers the challenges of hardware, records analytics, maintenance, mobility, safety, and logistics. IoT to IoT peer, IoT peer to blockchain, and hybrid architectures are proposed. There are many problems to the mixing of blockchain with artificial intelligence for sharing, securing, transactions, deposit, and aid control programs.
