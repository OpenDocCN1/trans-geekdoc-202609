# CHALLENGES AND ISSUES

**Scalability:** The integration of blockchain with the IoT poses scalability challenges, particularly in handling the increasing volume of transactions generated by IoT devices. Traditional blockchain platforms, such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, may struggle to process a large number of transactions in a timely and cost-effective manner, leading to delays and increased transaction fees.
