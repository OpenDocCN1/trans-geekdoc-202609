# CONCLUSION

The Industrial Internet of Things is now facing many difficulties, such as inadequate interoperability, heterogeneity, security and confidentiality flaws, and resource constraints. IIoT challenges can be studied by utilizing current blockchain technologies, which improve privacy, interoperability, traceability, safety and dependability. In order to undertake the investigation described in this chapter, blockchain and IIoT were combined. The term “IIoT” refers to a marriage of blockchain and IIoT, and this study conducts a thorough survey of the literature published in this area. Main, blockchain technology, and the Internet of Things. The chapter also assesses the applications of IIoT and illustrates IIoT architecture. Detailed illustrations of IIoT-capable applications have been presented, along with a list of IIoT open research issues. Due to high centralization and single point of failure, IIoT security systems, which currently exist, are inefficient. However, IIoT that is based on blockchain is particularly resistant to external hacking and other threats. The same data is kept on each blockchain node, so there is no chance of data loss. Future work will put the IIoT system into practice and demonstrate it for practical uses on-demand manufacturing and device self-service.
