# ## INTRODUCTION

In 2009 Nakamoto Satoshi developed blockchain to make Bitcoins more secure. As of January 2018, the capital fee of Bitcoin was $180 billion. Blockchain is a multi-disciplinary topic that involves mathematics, computation, big data, IoT, and cloud computing. Blockchain isn't always a proved, however, it is an alternative as it is also used as the primary software of Bitcoin. A block is part of the blockchain that consists of a block header, which turn is divided into fundamental block hash, block model, timestamp, nonce value, simple records, information, and Merkel root hash. The block also includes interactive series of information with cryptography and a dispensed shared ledger. It performs unusual operations by consensus, smart contracts, preferred ledger, and cross-chain generation. Each block is connected to different blocks (nodes) and knows the previous and subsequent hashes in a peer-to-peer network. Blocks are not deleted or changed by blockchain users. It has a verification facility by which individual blocks have to be completely verified. Blocks have the traits of decentralization, persistence, anonymity, independence, openness, immutability, safety and agreement, collective renovation, programmability, transparency, unforgeability, and auditability [5] (Figure 1.1).
