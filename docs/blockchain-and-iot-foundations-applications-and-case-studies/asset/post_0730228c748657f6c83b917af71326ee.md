# CONCLUSION AND FUTURE ENANCEMENTS

The attendance of students in a university or college and employees of an organization should be safely maintained and the database must be completely protected. For this purpose, blockchain is used. This system will be revolutionary in the future as it will be the safest relational database to date. The retrieval of information and query processing is much easier. Future enhancements are required such as adding a specific login for department.
