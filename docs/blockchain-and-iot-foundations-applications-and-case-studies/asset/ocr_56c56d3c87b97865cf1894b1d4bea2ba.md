# # SELFISH MINING

Blocks are brought to the blockchain and mined via miners. There are miners with their own mining powers, mi, such that Σ = n. To check the validity of a block, hard crypto puzzles are solved by miners. Sometimes puzzles are too hard for one miner to solve, therefore, miners will pool together to collect enough computing strength. Miners get their rewards in bitcoins when they solve the puzzle.

Extra energy will increase the rewards and the possibility of solving the crypto puzzles. Mining pools result in two types of companies: honest and egocentric [21, 35].

Egocentric miners convince the genuine miners to fix state blocks with the aid of a forking blockchain, creating a copy of the blockchain (Figure 1.6). As a result, they waste their computing energy in futile. Mining should be sincere and incentive-free. If a couple of block is introduced through miners, they create a fork into branches of a tree referred to as Merkle tree, which can be difficult or smooth depending on the basis of old and new regulations.
