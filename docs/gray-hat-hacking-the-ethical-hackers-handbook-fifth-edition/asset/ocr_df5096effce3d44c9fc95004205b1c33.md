# What Can Go Wrong

It’s important to understand where a red team engagement can go “off the rails.” There are common challenges that red teams face, and it’s important to be aware of them so that these issues can be addressed ahead of time. Justin Warner’s Common Ground blog series has a wealth of information about red teaming assessments and is a recommended resource.⁵

# Limited Scope

To be successful, a red team must be able to maneuver through an environment just as an adversary would. However, most organizations have assets that they consider invaluable that they are not willing to put at risk in the case something goes wrong. This can severely hinder a red teaming effort and limit the benefit of such an engagement.
