The following code listing shows the famous loop from the Ploutus version. It retrieves the denomination of Cassette 4 ($5, $20, $50, or $100) and then multiplies by the maximum number of bills allowed by the dispenser (in this scenario, this is 40 for NCR Personas, which is one of the targets of Ploutus) in order to calculate the total amount to withdraw 2 .
