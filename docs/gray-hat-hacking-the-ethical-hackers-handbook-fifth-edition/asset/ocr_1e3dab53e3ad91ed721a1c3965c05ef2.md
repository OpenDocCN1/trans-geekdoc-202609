There was a known case in Cancun, Mexico, where the skimmers were installed inside the ATMs and communicated with the attackers via Bluetooth. The fact that most of those infected machines were inside hotels and airports suggests that local gangs are supported by people from “legitimate” businesses. “Going to the cops would be useless at best, and potentially dangerous; Mexico’s police force is notoriously corrupt, and for all my source knew the skimmer scammers were paying for their own protection from the police,” wrote Brian Krebs.⁵

# Malware Affecting Banks

This type of malware empties the ATMs; therefore, the cardholders are not affected, but the banks are. This is done either by reusing the XFS middleware installed in the machines or by creating an XFS-compliant application. Examples of these type of threats include Ploutus, Alice, SUCEFUL, Ripper, Padpin (later named Tyupkin by Kaspersky), and GreenDispenser.
