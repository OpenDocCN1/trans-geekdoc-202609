## Preview

We are now at the point where all the effort hopefully pays off and we can synthesize our own data using the results of the analysis. The goal of the Preview step is to verify that the data we are going to send looks like what we expect prior to sending it over the air. This step could be combined with the Execute step, but I think it is important enough to warrant its own step to ensure we do not skip it and start transmitting.

Up until now, the flow graphs we created have been relatively simple with very few moving parts. To create the signal from scratch, we will be using several new blocks, as described in Table 5-3. The flow graph in Figure 5-11 includes the osmocom sink block, but notice that the arrow is grey and the block is a different color. The grey arrow and block indicate that they are disabled. Another subtle change is that we have switched to 1 MSps instead of our typical 4 MSps. Because we are synthesizing the data, we do not have to use the same sample rate as before. Additionally, the selected sample rate made it easier to show that the symbol rate was 275µs.
