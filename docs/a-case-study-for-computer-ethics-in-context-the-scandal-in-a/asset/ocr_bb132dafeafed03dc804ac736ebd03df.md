This is also a good point at which to have a discussion about social media culture and the problematic aspects of this. At least two individuals implicated in this scandal have become the subject of memes and/or deep fakes. What do the students think of meme culture? What do they think of deep fakes? Is the creation and deployment of either of these ethical? What are the implications in terms of reputational damage? Does the supposed anonymity of the Internet have an impact on the debate? The previous discussion regarding the ethics of whistleblowing can also be revisited in light of the new leaks.
