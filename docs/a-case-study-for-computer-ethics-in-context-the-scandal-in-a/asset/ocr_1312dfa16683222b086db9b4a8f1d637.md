It was James who emailed me the link to the online version of the article saying ‘Urgent: Read this now!’, then he came over to my workstation as soon as I’d had time to read it. I had no idea that Blackbriar was massaging the data in any way. To be fair, he was so aloof and hard to get time with that I had no idea what he was doing most of the time when he was actually in the lab. But he came across as competent and rather arrogant. I wouldn’t think to question what he was doing, or the data he was giving me access to. It seemed fine to me – there were no obvious red flags that I had noticed.
