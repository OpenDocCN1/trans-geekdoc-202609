For the last session, groups should focus on ranking participants into a top five of culpability, along with reasons for their ranking. They can choose anyone to be in this list, regardless of what prominence they may have had in the case study. They then present this list in groups, with each unique participant being added to a shared pool of responsibility constructed by the class. Finally, everyone should vote on who they think is most responsible from this selection, and whether the punishment – or otherwise – they received was proportional. There is no right answer here. We, as the authors, have our opinion. It couldn’t matter less though what we think.
