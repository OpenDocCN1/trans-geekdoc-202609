## Introducing #datalift

Leaders need to grasp the urgency of productionizing data analytics and machine learning. Why? Because the coolest technology is useless if not deployed for a business case. Every crisis is an opportunity. The COVID-19 pandemic is a challenging moment. It impacts our health, economy, and livelihood, and it is spurring the creation of digital data. Business and government should seize the one great opportunity we have: Scale the data economy.

### Feedforward Europe

Europe has a critical mass of experts in industry and startups across the main sectors of the economy. Moreover, one million data-literate talents will graduate by the end of 2023. So, let's break the proof-of-concept cycle and productionize data analytics and machine learning. With #datalift, we show you how business and government can scale the data economy now.

### What business needs

From 1000+ conversations with the 500+ AI Guild practitioners at 50+ events, we know that business requires:

* More and better expertise for deploying use cases successfully;
* A structure for management, experts, and talents to ship the product; and
* The will and resources to win big in the data economy.

If Europe doesn’t improve now, this likely leads to sad stories like: “Lots of data, never really utilized. The company is out of business.”

### Data careers and use cases

The AI Guild has been building up capacity for addressing the main challenges of AI adoption in Europe.

1. We are ramping up the expertise of practitioners for successfully deploying data analytics and machine learning.
2. We are competent for onboarding data talents at scale and developing teams for companies that deliver on data infrastructure and the use case architecture.
3. Facilitating communication between business, politics, and technology to advance AI adoption, and you are reading an example just now.

### For business

Expertise and experience are crucial for implementing your company's use cases. If you collaborate with the AI Guild, you raise a new generation of experts as fast as possible.

* Your simple use cases are solved immediately.
* You create a virtuous circle of deployment and learning over 1-2 years.
* You scale in-house expertise for deployment across the entire enterprise.

### For government

Return to growth by kick-starting the data economy with 1% of the stimulus package in Europe. If you collaborate with the AI Guild, we can flip the best data talents to deployable practitioners. We have done the sums and expect up to 1 million data-literate graduates by 2022: highly numerate, experienced in handling data, and coding in Python, R, and SQL. Imagine what your city, your country, and Europe could achieve with so much fresh talent. Europe has 1.2m STEM graduates per year, 30% master's degree, and 4% doctorate. The baseline is 400,000 highly data literate graduates each year.

Dânia Meira & Chris Armbruster
