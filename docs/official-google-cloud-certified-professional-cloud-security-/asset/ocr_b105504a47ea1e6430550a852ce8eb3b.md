In this section, we will look at cloud-native capabilities that are available on Google Cloud that can help you achieve continuous compliance. Continuous compliance means that you can run your compliance checks on your cloud assets against the policy that is being implemented. Whenever an asset changes its state, as a running Google Compute Engine resource with new vulnerabilities, the compliance-as-code policy executes to check the state and returns a result to indicate compliance or non-compliance. If compliant, no action is required; however, depending on your organizational maturity, you can take different actions. For example, you might just want a notification to be sent to your security and compliance teams to take action and review the finding, or you can build an automated process that means once an asset is found to be non-compliant, a Cloud Functions script will execute and take corrective action to make the asset compliant.
