## 12.5 Conclusion and Future Scope

This paper proposes a novel algorithm for low-quality fingerprint image enhancement using both spatial and frequency domain techniques. Some research gaps such as poor performance in regions of high curvature, non-distinction between ridges and valleys, poor performance in recovering highly damaged regions, and so on were found in the existing techniques. The proposed scheme is designed to fill these existing research gaps. The coherence filtering smoothens the broken ridges; the spatial filter removes noise, improves contrast, and recovers the damaged regions. The frequency band-pass filter refines the spatial domain-filtered output results by removing the blurred effect caused by it and further recovers the damaged regions.

Various analyses such as visual analysis, texture descriptor analysis, minutiae ratio analysis, and analysis based on different input modalities show that the proposed algorithm have better performance compared to existing methods. The future work related to this chapter can be using pixel processing instead of block processing, which is expected to improve the computational time.
