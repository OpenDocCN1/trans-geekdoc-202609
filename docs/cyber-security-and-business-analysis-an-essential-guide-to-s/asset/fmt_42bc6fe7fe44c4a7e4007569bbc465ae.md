
Clarity and comprehensiveness of policy documentation are critical for the successful implementation of cyber security measures. Business analysts can leverage their documentation skills to create policies that are accessible and understandable to both technical and non-technical stakeholders. Well-documented policies enhance transparency, making it easier for all stakeholders to comprehend and adhere to security guidelines.
From understanding business needs to active involvement in risk assessments and effective communication, business analysts can contribute to fortifying an organisation’s security posture through a solid cyber security policy.
