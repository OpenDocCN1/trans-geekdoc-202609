
# Cyber Security and Business Analysis

![Cover: Book titled Cyber security and business analysis, an essential guide to secure and robust systems. By Bindu Channaveerappa, with B C S logo at the bottom left corner, with image of owls is shown.](images/cover.jpg)

This book brings to life what cyber security means to business analysts and how they should consider this topic in their work. The framework developed throughout the book is simple to understand and offers a comprehensive guide. The book contains lots of practical advice which is brought to life with relatable and enjoyable anecdotes. This is a must for any business analyst wishing to learn more and grow their understanding and appreciation of cyber security.
