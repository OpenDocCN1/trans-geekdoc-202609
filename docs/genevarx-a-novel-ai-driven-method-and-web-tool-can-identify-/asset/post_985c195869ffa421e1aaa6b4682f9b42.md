## 3.2.4. Sparse models explanation

Dataset1 In Fig. 14, we provide insights pertaining to models used in the Results section. Fig. 14(a–d) show that there were statistically significant differences in predictions of healthy controls and LP patients (p-value of all differences <<0.01, obtained from a t-test), indicating that these models applied to Dataset1 are expected to be general predictors to healthy controls and LP. These results demonstrate that our optimized methods led to sparser feature vector representations than lasso.
