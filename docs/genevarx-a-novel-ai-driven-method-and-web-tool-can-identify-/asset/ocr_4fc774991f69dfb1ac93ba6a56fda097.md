Because LP is a complex disease and still has no cure, this study can help medical researchers unveil the molecular mechanisms contributing to the disease and thereby improving the personalized treatment stages through a potential linked causality (Xia et al., 2020). For example, retrieved enriched terms from Metascape demonstrated that our provided genes involved in biological pathway related to tryptophan metabolism, involved in B vitamins (together forming the so-called B complex). It has been reported that B vitamins have been prescribed and demonstrated to be more efficacious in the treatment of LP (Burgess, 1941). These results show that a dietary lifestyle change and treating nutritional deficiencies may help in the treatment of LP (Cosgarea et al., 2020; Li et al., 2020). To help medical researchers gain more biological insights about LP, we provide Metascape enrichment analysis results related to genes involved in biological pathways as well as other terms in Supplementary Metascape_Dataset1.zip, Metascape_Dataset2.zip, and Metascape_Dataset3.zip.

In this study, we aimed to develop computational methods to identify important genes discriminating between healthy controls and LP patients followed by utilizing enrichment analysis to uncover molecular insights pertaining to the disease. As LP is categorized based on morphology and site of the lesion, LP may affect a patient in different sites with different subtypes (Muradova et al., 2022; Usatine and Tinitigan, 2011). In this study, published datasets included lesion sites for cutaneous LP (Datasets 1 and 2), genital and OLP (Dataset3). In this study, we reported results for cutaneous and OLP. Furthermore, reported results in terms of a given dataset demonstrated independent biological insights pertaining to a LP type. In terms of the running time and memory space, our methods (ssvm1, ssvm2 and ssvm3) were the slowest, having the highest user CPU time (1.09 s, 3.47 s, and 4.05 s in Dataset1, Dataset2, and Dataset3, respectively). Moreover, our methods had the largest memory spaces of 141.04 MB, 401.51 MB, and 160.55 MB for running Dataset1, Dataset2, and Dataset3, respectively. On the other hand, for Dataset1, lasso was the fastest method requiring the least memory space of 7.80 MB. For Dataset2, lasso was the fastest while t-test was the method requiring the least memory space of 14.43 MB. For Dataset3, limma was the fastest while t-test was the method requiring the least memory space. We provide running time and memory space of each method in Supplementary Running Time and Memory Space.
