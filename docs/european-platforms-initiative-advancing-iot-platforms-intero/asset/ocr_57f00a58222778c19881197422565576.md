# 4.6.1. The symbIoTe Architecture

The symbIoTe architecture [24] is built around a layered IoT stack connecting various devices (sensors, actuators and IoT gateways) within Smart Spaces with the Cloud. Smart Spaces share available local resources (connectivity, computing and storage), while platform services running in the Cloud enable IoT Platform Federations and open the Interworking API¹⁸ to third parties with flexible access control. The architecture comprises four layered domains, as depicted in Figure 4.10.
