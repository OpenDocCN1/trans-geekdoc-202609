Systems that do not natively support the necessary IoT standards can join by what we call Wrappers in bloTope, i.e. software components that expose the services that should be published to the IoT level using the appropriate standards (this corresponds to pattern V) “Higher-level Service Facades” in Figure 3.2). This also signifies ensuring that data and services that are not public remain non-accessible to unauthorized parties.

In bloTope, “IoT standard” signifies a limited set of appropriate standards around the “waist” of the standards landscape as illustrated in Figure 4.4.
