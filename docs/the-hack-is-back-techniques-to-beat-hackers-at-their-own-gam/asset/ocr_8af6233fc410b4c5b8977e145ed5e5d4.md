This will allow you to even further enhance your skills because while practicing on a flat network will really help you improve your skills, at some point you will have to expand your skill set and get familiar with how a real network operates with LAN and WAN addresses, a firewall, and how Network Address Translation (NAT) works. If you configure port-redirection on the firewall, the hacker (you) can attempt to attack the internal systems they are re-directed to. If the attacker gains access to one of the internal systems, they can move laterally within the LAN. Advanced classes need to train from this perspective so participants can better understand attacks from the WAN. It also changes the perspective of the analyst as they will be examining logs and network connections from external IPs like they will encounter working in the field.
