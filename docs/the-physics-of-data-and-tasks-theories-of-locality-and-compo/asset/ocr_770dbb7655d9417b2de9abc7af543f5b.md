+   4. TASK-SPECIFIC KNOWLEDGE IS LOCALIZED The exploration of Part IV revealed a novel form of compositionality at the model itself. Our central finding is that the ability to compose and subtract tasks is enabled by weight disentanglement – an emergent property of pre-training where distinct direction parameters control functionally aspects of the model’s behavior. By showing that this property is absent at initialization and can be enhanced by linearizing the model, we provided both mechanistic explanation for task arithmetic and a practical method for improving it. This recasts the geometry of the weight space of large models as a structured space where tasks correspond to localized, composable subspaces.

Synthesizing these points, this thesis posits that deep learning is effective because it operates on a cascade of compositional structures. It begins with features in the input, builds hierarchical representations that mirror the compositional nature of the world, and culminates in a modular organization of knowledge in weight space that allows for the flexible composition of skills.
