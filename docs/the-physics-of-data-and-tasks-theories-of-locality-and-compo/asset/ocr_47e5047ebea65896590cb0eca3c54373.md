HIERARCHICAL MODELS OF IMAGES AND TEXT Generative models have been used to describe the structure of data in several contexts, including in linguistics and signal processing. For languages, hierarchically-structured formal grammars are often used as a model of their syntactic structure [Rs97]. Likewise, pattern theory formalizes the semantic decomposition of visual scenes through a hierarchy of features [Gre96; Sis+07; LSF09]. More recently, images have been described through a hierarchical decomposition in multiscale wavelet coefficients [Mar+22; Kad23a], although the underlying structure, in this case, is tree-like.
