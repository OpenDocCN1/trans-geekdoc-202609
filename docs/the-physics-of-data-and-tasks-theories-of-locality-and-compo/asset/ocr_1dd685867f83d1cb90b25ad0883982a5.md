**VISION DIFFUSION MODELS** We extend our analysis to consider using Improved Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models [ND21], trained on the ImageNet2 dataset. To compute the correlation between the image tokens, we follow recent trends in multimodal LLMs [Liu+22, Dai+23]. Specifically, we divide each image into $7\times7$ patches and use the last-layer embeddings for each patch from CLIP Vit-B32 to tokenize the image. $x_{0,i}$ denote the embedding of the $i$-th path, where $i=(k,l)$ with $k,l \in [7]$. After the forward-backward process, the variation of each patch embedding is given by $\Delta x_i(t) = x_{0,i} - x_i(t)$. We compute the average correlations between the norms of these variations:
