B.2 RFK AND NTK OF DEEP CONVOLUTIONAL NETWORKS

This appendix gives the functional forms of the RFK and NTK of hierarchical CNNs. We refer the reader to [Aro+19] for the derivation.

# Definition B.2.1 (RFK and NTK of hierarchical CNNs). Let $x, y \in \mathcal{M}^{PS} = \prod_{l=1}^{L} \mathcal{S}^{l-1}$. Denote tuples of the kind $i_l i_{l+1} \dots i_m$ with $i_l \to m$ for $m \geq 1$. For each tuple $i_l \to L+1$, note with $t_{i_l \to L+1}$ the scalar product over the $s$-dimensional patches of $x$ and $y$ identified by the same tuple, i.e

```
$t_{i_l \to L+1} = x_{i_l \to L+1} \cdot y_{i_l \to L+1}. \quad \quad (202)$
```

For $1 \leq l \leq L+1$, with $\{t_{i_l \to i_j}\}_{j=l+1}^L$ the sequence of $t^s$ obtained by letting the indices of the tuple $i_l$ vary in their respective range. Consider a hierarchical CNN with $L$ hidden layers, filter sizes $(s_1, \dots, s_L)$, $p_l \geq 1$ and all the the weights $w_j^{(l)}, w_{i_l \to i_{l+1}}^{(l-1)}$ initialized as Gaussian random numbers with zero mean and variance.

RFK. The RFK (or covariance kernel) is a function $K^{(L+1)}_{RFK}$ of the $p_1$ and scalar products $t_{i_1 \to i_2}$, which can be obtained recursively as follows. With $\kappa_1(t) = (\pi - \arccos t)t \sqrt{1-t^2} / \pi$,

```
$\kappa^{(1)}_{RFK}(\{t_{i_1 \to i_2}\}) = \kappa_1(t_{i_1 \to i_2}),\n\nkappa^{(l)}_{RFK}(\{t_{i_l \to i_j}\}_{j=l+1}^L) = \kappa_1 \left(\\frac{1}{s_l} \sum_{i_l} \kappa^{(l-1)}_{RFK}(\{t_{i_l \to i_j}\}_{j=l+2}^L)\right), \forall i \in [2 \ \dots \ L] if \ L > 1;\n\n\kappa^{(L)}_{RFK}(\{t_{i_L \to i_{L+1}}\}) = \frac{1}{p_L} \sum_{i_L} \kappa^{(L-1)}_{RFK}(\{t_{i_L \to i_{L+1}}\}) . \quad \quad (203)$
```

NTK. The NTK of the same hierarchical CNN is also a function of the $p_1=d/s$ scalar products $t_{i_1 \to i_2}, t_{i_l \to i_{l+1}}$ which can be obtained recursively as follows. With $\kappa_0(t) = (\pi - \arccos t) / \pi$,

```
$\kappa^{(1)}_{NTK}(\{t_{i_l \to i_{l+1}}\}) = \kappa_1(t_{i_l \to i_{l+1}}) + \kappa_0(t_{i_l \to i_{l+1}}),\n\n\kappa^{(l)}_{NTK}(\{t_{i_l \to i_j}\}_{j=l+1}^L) = \kappa^{(l-1)}_{NTK}(\{t_{i_l \to i_{l+1}}\}) + \frac{1}{s_l} \sum_{i_l} \kappa^{(l-1)}_{NTK}(\{t_{i_l \to i_j}\}_{j=l+2}^L),\\n\n\times \kappa_0 \left(\frac{1}{s_l} \sum_{i_l} \kappa^{(l-1)}_{RFK}(\{t_{i_l \to i_{l+1}}\}))\right), \forall i \in [2 \ \dots \ L] if \ L > 1;\n\n\kappa^{(L+1)}_{NTK}(\\{t_{i_L \to i_{L+1}}\}) = \frac{1}{p_L} \sum_{i_L} \kappa^{(L)}_{NTK}(\\{t_{i_L \to i_{L+1}}\}) . \quad \quad (204)$
```
