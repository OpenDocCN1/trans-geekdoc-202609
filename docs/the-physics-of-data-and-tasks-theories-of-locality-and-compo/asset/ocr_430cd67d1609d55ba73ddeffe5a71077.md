```
$$\int_{0}^{\infty} d\lambda D_{r}(\lambda)c^{2}(\lambda) \frac{\Lambda^{2}}{(\Lambda+\lambda)^{2}}=\Lambda^{q-r} \int \frac{y^{q-1-r}}{(1+y)^{2}}, \quad (180)$
```

where one recognizes one of the integral representations of the beta function,

```
$B(a,b)=\int dy \frac{y^{a-1}}{(1+y)^{a+b}} \frac{\Gamma(a)\Gamma(b)}{\Gamma(a+b)}, \quad (181)$
```

with $\Gamma$ denoting the gamma function. Therefore,

```
$$\int_{0}^{\infty} d\lambda D_{r}(\lambda)c^{2}(\lambda) \frac{\Lambda^{2}}{(\Lambda+\lambda)^{2}}=\Lambda^{q-r}\frac{\Gamma(q-r)\Gamma(2-q+r)}{\Gamma(2)}, \quad (182)$
```

It is interesting to notice that the assumptions $q>r$ and $q+r<2$ are required in order to avoid the poles of $\Gamma$ in the RHS of Equation 182.

We now use Equation 182 to fix the asymptotics of $\mathcal{R}(\lambda,P)$ in the scaling limit $\lambda\to0$ and $P\to\infty$ with $1/(\sqrt{\lambda}P)\to0$. The latter condition implies that $\lambda$ decays slower than $(P)^{-1/2}$, additionally terms stemming from the finite-$P$ difference between $\theta$ and $\lambda$, of $P^{-1}$ are negligible w.r.t. $\Lambda^{q-r}$. The finite-$P$ difference between $\partial_{\lambda}\mathcal{R}$, also $O(P^{-1})$, is negated too.

```
$$\mathcal{R}(\lambda,P)\sim\int_{0}^{\infty} d\lambda D_{r}(\lambda)c^{2}(\lambda) \frac{\Lambda^{2}}{(\Lambda+\lambda)^{2}}\sim\Lambda^{q-r}\sim\int_{0}^{\infty} d\lambda D_{r}(\lambda)c^{2}(\Lambda). \quad (183)$
```

Theorem 6 of [lac20b] shows the convergence of $\mathcal{E}(\lambda,P)$ towards $\mathcal{R}(\lambda,P)$ when $P\to\infty$. Specifically,

```
$|\mathcal{E}(\lambda,P)-\mathcal{R}(\lambda,P)|\le\left(\frac{1}{P}+g\left(\frac{\text{Tr}[T_{K}]}{\lambda\sqrt{\P}}\right)\right)\mathcal{R}(\lambda,P), \quad (184)$
```

where $g$ is a polynomial with non-negative coefficients and $g(0)=0$. With a decaying rate $\lambda$ such that $1/(\sqrt{\lambda}P)\to0$, both $\mathcal{R}/P$ and $\mathcal{G}(\text{Tr}[T_{K}]/(\sqrt{\lambda}P))$ tend to zero hence $\mathcal{R}$ itself, the asymptotics of $\mathcal{E}(\lambda,P)$ coincide with those of $\mathcal{R}(\lambda,P)$ and Equation 175 is proven.
