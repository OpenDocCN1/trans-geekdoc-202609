Figure 69: Sampling of RHM production rules. Mean occurrence (left) and centered covariance (right) of the production rules sampled by a diffusion model trained on $P = 16,384$ strings ($v = 16, m = 4, L = 3, s = 2$). The model, trained with early stopping $(	au = 32,768$), samples all RHM rules with a mean occurrence that is approximately uniform (up to sampling noise). Likewise, the correlations between the cooccurrence of sampled rules show that they are sampled approximately independently.
