**Figure 39:** Learning curves for deep convolutional NTKs ($v=1/2$) in a teacher-student setting with different input normalizations. In particular, we consider inputs on the microsphere $S^{d-1}$ (MS), uniformly-distributed in the $d$-hypercube $[0,1]^d$ (Cb), and with standard Gaussian distribution $\mathcal{N}(0, I_d)$ (Ga). The numbers inside brackets are the sequence of filter sizes of the kernels. Solid lines are the results of experiments averaged over 16 realizations, with the shaded areas representing the empirical standard deviations. The asymptotic scaling $\mathcal{E} \sim P^{-eta}$ predicted for inputs on the microsphere is reported as dashed lines.
