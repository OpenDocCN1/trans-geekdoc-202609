# 1.2.2 Infinite-width networks

One tool is the infinite-width limit of neural networks. As the number of neurons per layer grows, the network's behavior simplifies and, in certain regimes, becomes analytically tractable.

INFINITE-WIDTH AT INITIALIZATION When the weights of a network as in Equation 1 are initialized with i.i.d.
