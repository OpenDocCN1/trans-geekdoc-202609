Finally, another direction to explore is the stability of the task toward smooth transformations or diffeomorphisms. This form of stability has been proposed as a key element to understanding how the curse of dimensionality is beaten for image datasets [BM13; Pet+21]. Such a property can be enforced with pooling operations [BM19; BVB2]; therefore, diagonalizing the NTK in this case as well would be of high interest.
