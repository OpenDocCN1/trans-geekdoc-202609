# F.2 EXPERIMENTS ON STABLE DIFFUSION

We consider Stable Diffusion v2.1 [RFB15], a text-to-image latent diffusion model pre-trained on the LAION-2B dataset [Sch+22]. We finune this model for 262,144 steps on 8,192 samples from the LAION-10k dataset [Som+23], using a resolution of 256 × 256. During fintuning, the text encoder and encoder-decoder components are kept frozen. We use a held-out validation set of 1,024 image-text pairs to monitor the validation loss. Full training details are provided in Section F.1.
