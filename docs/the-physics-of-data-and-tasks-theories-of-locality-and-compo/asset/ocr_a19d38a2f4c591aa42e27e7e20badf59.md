For $\ell=1$, the above coincides with the joint probability of the visible token $X_1$ and the tuple of visible tokens $X_{s+1}, \dots, X_{s2}$. The correlations,

```
$C^{(2)}(\mu, \nu) = \mathbb{P} [X_1 = \mu, X_{s+1s} = \nu] - \mathbb{P} [X_1 = \mu] [X_{s+1s} = \nu],$
```

have been analyzed in Cagneta and Wyart [CW24]: the mean vanishes, with the variance, in the level of $m$, $v \to \infty$ with $f=m/v^{s-1}$ finite,

```
$\left( C^{(2)}(\mu, \nu) \right)^2 = \left( \frac{1-f}{v^{3m}} \right)^2$
```

For $\ell=2$, after following Equation 387, we get

```
$C^{(3)}(\mu, \nu) = \sum_{\mu_1=v} p_1^{(1)}(\mu_1|\mu) \left( \mathbb{P} \left[ h_1^{(1)} = \mu, \mathbf{h}_{s+1s}^{(2)} = v \right] \right] \\
- \mathbb{P} \left[ h_1^{(1)} = \mu \right] \left[ \mathbf{h}_{s+1s}^{(2)} = v \right] \times \\
 \sum_{\mu_1=v} p_1^{(1)}(\mu_1|\mu) C^{(2)}(\mu_1, \nu),$
```

where the last equality follows from noticing that the probability of level-$\ell$ hidden variables coincides with the probability of the leaves of a tree with $L-\ell$ levels. In general,

```
$C^{(\ell+1)}(\mu, \nu) = \sum_{\mu_1=v} p_1^{(1)}(\mu_1|\mu) C^{(\ell)}(\mu_1, \nu),$
```

thus

```
$\left( C^{(\ell+1)}(\mu, \nu) \right)^2 = \sum_{\mu_1} p_1^{(1)}(\mu_1|\mu) p_1^{(1)}(\mu_1|\nu) \left( C^{(\ell)}(\mu, \v) \right) \left( C^{(\ell)}(\mu_1, \nu) \right) \\
 \sum_{\mu_1} \left( p_1^{(1)}(\mu_1|\mu) \right)^2 \left( C^{(\ell)}(\mu, \mu_1) \right)^2 \left( C^{(\ell)}(\mu_1, \nu) \right) + \\
```
