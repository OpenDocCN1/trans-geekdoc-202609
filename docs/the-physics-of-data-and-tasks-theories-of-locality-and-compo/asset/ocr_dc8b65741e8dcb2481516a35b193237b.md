Let us look at the contribution to the eigenvalue $\Lambda_k$ due to the term $f_1(|	heta_j|)$:

```
$$\left(\prod_{j=1}^{S^{s-2}-1} \int_0^{\pi} d\theta_j (\sin(\theta_j))^{s-2} P_{k,s}(\cos(\theta_j)) f_1(|	heta_j|)\right)$
```

```
$$\left(\prod_{j\neq i} \delta_{k,\theta_j}\right) \frac{S^{s-2}}{S^{s-1}} \int_0^{\pi} d\theta (\sin(\theta))^{s-2} P_{k,s}(\cos(\theta)) f_1(|\theta|) = \left(\prod_{j\neq i} \delta_{k,\theta_j}\right) (f_1)_{k_i},$
(239)$
```

where we have introduced $(f_1)_k$ as the projection of $f_1$ on the $k$-th Legendre polynomial. The asymptotic decay of $(f_1)_k$ is strictly related to the differentiability of $f_1$, which in turn controlled by action of the Laplace-Beltrami operator $\Delta$ on $f_1$. As a function on the sphere $S^{s-1}$, $f_1$ depends only on one angle, therefore the Laplace-Beltrami operator acts as

```
$$\Delta f_1(\theta) = \frac{1}{\sin(\theta)^{s-2}} \frac{d}{d\theta} (\sin(\theta))^{s-2} \frac{d f_1}{d\theta}) = f_1''(\theta) + \frac{\cos(\theta)}{\sin(\theta)} f_1'(\theta)$.$
```

In terms of singular behavior near $	heta=0$, $f_1(\theta) \sim |\theta|^{2v}$ implies $\Delta f_1(\theta) \sim |\theta|^{2v-2}$, thus $\Delta f_1$ and repeated applications of $\Delta$ eventually result in a function whose $l^2$ norm on the sphere divisors. On the one hand,

```
$$\\Delta^{m/2} f_1^2 = \int_0^{\pi} d\theta \sin^{d-2}(\theta) f_1(\theta) \Delta^m f_1(\theta).$ (240)$
```

The integrand behaves $|\theta|^{d-2+4v-2m}$ near $0$, thus the integral diverges for $m \geq 2v+(d-1)/2$. On the other, from Equation 187,

```
$$\\Delta^{m/2} f_1^2 = \sum_k N_{k,s} (k(k+s-2))^m (f_1)_k^2$.$ (241)$
```

As $N_{k,s} \sim k^{-s}$ and thus must converge for $m < 2v+(d-1)/2$ and diverge otherwise, $(f_1)_k \sim k^{-(2v+s-1)}$. The projection of all terms in $\mathcal K$ on Legendre polynomials of the $p$ angles $	heta$, display a faster decay with $k$, therefore the above result implies the asymptotic scaling of local eigenvalues. Notice that such scaling with the result of [BB21], was obtained with a different argument.
