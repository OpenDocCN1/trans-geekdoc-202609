# 5.2.1.1 Dynamical correlation length

In what follows, we present our main result predicting a power law divergence of the dynamical correlation length at the phase transition.

In the mean-field approach, the average belief $p_\ell$ in the original value of a latent variable at layer $\ell$ can be computed through the iterative map

```
$p_{\ell} = F(p_{\ell-1}),$$
```

Where the functional form of $F(p)$ was derived in Chapter 4 and the initial condition $p_0$ depends on the noise level $\epsilon$ as $p_0 = 1-\epsilon/v$. In the large limit $D \to \infty$, the probability $p_D$ of reconstructing the class is given by the fixed points of $F(p)$. For RHM measures such as $p_\ell$ undergoes the phase transition, $F(p)$ has three points: two attractive ones, corresponding to $p=1/v$ and $p=1$, and a repulsive one, corresponding to the non-trivial solution of $p^*=F(p^*)$ with $p^* \in (0,1)$. $p^*$ corresponds to a critical noise level $\epsilon^*=1-p^*$.

In the vicinity of $\epsilon^*$ and the limit $L \to \infty$, we can estimate the typical distance over which taken changes are corrected by computing the number of layers $\ell$ after which the upward probability of reconstructing the latent variables $p_\ell$ approaches one of the two fixed points $p=1$ and $p=1/v$. This corresponds to the number of layers required to escape the repulsive fixed point $p^*$.

Given the iterative map of Equation 80, we can linearize it around the fixed point $p^*$ and iterate for $\ell$ layers,

```
$\Delta p_{\ell} = \left( \frac{dF(p)}{dp} \right|_{p^*} \right)^{\ell} \Delta p_0,$$
```

Where $\Delta p_\ell = p_\ell - p^*$. We have that $|\frac{df(p)}{dp}|_{p^*} > 1$ and we use the shorthand notation $F'^* = |\frac{df(p)}{dp}|_{p^*}$. We want to compute the correlation length $\ell$ after which $F'^{*}^\ell |\Delta p_0| = \mathcal{O}(1)$. In terms of the corruption noise $\epsilon$, we have $F'^{*}^\ell |\Delta \epsilon| = \mathcal{O}(1)$, where $\Delta \epsilon = |\epsilon - \epsilon^*|$. Here, $\ell \sim -\log |\epsilon - \epsilon^*| / \log F'^*$. From the $\ell$, we compute the correlation length input as space

```
$\xi^{\ell} \sim s^{\ell} = |\epsilon - \epsilon^*| \text{ with } v = \frac{\log s}{\log F'^*},
```

that diverges at the critical point: $\epsilon_c = +\infty$. This divergence of the correlation length at the class transition indicates that large blocks of tokens change in concert.
