
## CONNECTION TO MASKED LANGUAGE DIFFUSION 233

CONNECTION TO MASKED LANGUAGE MODELS: MDLMs leveraging a masked diffusion approach where the training objective is a weighted average of classical masked language modeling (MLM) losses:

```
$L_{\text{NELBO}} = \mathbb{E}_q \int_0^1 \frac{\alpha'_t}{1-\alpha'_t} \log(x_t^\theta(z_t), x^t) dt, \quad (384)$
```

where $x_t^\theta(z_t)$: The $\ell$-th token in the original sequence, the neural network's prediction for the $\ell$-th token given the noisy sequence $z_t$. The summation runs over all tokens in the sequence, establishing a connection between MDLMs and BERT-style encoders equipping them with generative capabilities.

We employ the MDLM proposed in [Sah+24] to conduct the forward-backward experiments by random texts of a fixed token length from the WikiText2 database, fixing a fraction of the tokens $t$, and then performing the backward diffusion process by the masked sequence as the initial point for the MDLM model.

Below, provide examples of texts generated by the forward-backward process using MDLM seeded from WikiText2 examples for different masking processes. Selected samples were shown in the main text in Figure 14 (a). We dub the test results after the forward-backward process as U-turn samples. It can be seen that for small masking fractions as $t/T = 0.1$, most of the tokens do not change after masking, while the amount of changed tokens far exceeds the unchanged ones near the phase transition at $t/T = 0.5$, hinting at the long-range correlations emerging.
