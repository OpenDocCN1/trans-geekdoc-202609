The nonanalyticicity of the kernel at $l_i = 1$ for all $i$ moves to $	heta_i = 0$ for all $i$, whereas those in $l_i = -1$ move to $	heta_i = \pi$ and $-\pi$. The corresponding singular expansion is obtained from Equation 210 after replacing $l_i$ with $\cos(	heta_i)$ and expanding $\cos(	heta_i)$ as $1 - 	heta_i^2/2$, resulting

```
$K_{	ext{RFK}}^{(2)}(\{\cos \theta_i\}_{i=1}^p) = 1 - \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^p\theta_i^2 - \frac{1}{3\pi}\sum_{i=1}^p|\theta_i|^3 + \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3\pi}\sum_{i=1}^p\frac{\theta_i^{3/2}}{2} + O(\theta^4).$
```

The first analytic terms are $\frac{1}{3\pi}\sum_{i=1}^p|	heta_i|^3$ and $\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3\pi}\sum_{i=1}^p\frac{	heta_i^{3/2}}{2}$
After recalling that the Fourier transform of $\|\cdot\|^2$ with $	heta \in \mathbb{R}^p$ decays asymptotically as $\|\cdot\|^{-2p}$ [Widag], has one (with $v=3$)

```
$$\frac{1}{(2\pi)^p}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi d\theta e^{ik\cdot\theta}\|theta\|^k \sim \frac{1}{3\pi}\sum_{i=1}^p|\theta_i|^3 - 3\sum_{i\neq j}k_i^4\delta_{k_i,0},$ for $\|k\| \to \infty$.
```

and

```
$$\frac{1}{(2\pi)^p}\int_{-\pi}^{\pi d\theta e^{ik\cdot\theta}\|theta\|^3 \sim \|k\|^{-4},$ for $\|k\| \to \infty$.
```

All other terms in the kernel expansion will result in subleading contributions in the Fourier transform. Therefore, the former of the two equations above yields the asymptotic scaling of eigenvalues of the local sector, whereas the latter yields the asymptotic scaling of the global sector.
