In this section, we introduce a generative model of data that mimics the structure of images (and language) while being analytically tractable. Natural images often display a hierarchical and compositional structure [Greg66]. Take, for example, the image of a snow leopard (Figure 6). This image is composed of multiple high-level components, such as the head and the paws. Each of these components, in turn, is composed of sub-features. For instance, the head comprises eyes, ears, and mouth. Further dissecting these elements, we find even more granular details, such as edges that define the finer aspects of each feature.
