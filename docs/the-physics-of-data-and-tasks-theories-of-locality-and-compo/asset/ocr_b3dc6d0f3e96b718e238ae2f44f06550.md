# APPENDIX: PROBING HIDDEN HIERARCHIES IN DATA

FORWARD PROCESS: The forward process is defined by progressively noisy a clean sequence x using a categorical distribution:

```
$q(z_t|x) = \text{Cat}(z_t; a_t x + (1 - a_t)m),$
```

where $z_t$ is the latent variable at time $t$, representing the noisy version of the input sequence, $x$ is the original clean sequence of tokens, $\text{Cat}(\cdot;)$ is a categorical distribution over the possible states, $a_t$ is the noise schedule function, strictly decreasing from 1 to 0 as $t$ increases, and $m$ is a one-hot vector representing the special masked token. At each step, a fraction of the data transitions into the masked state.

REVERSE PROCESS AND RAO-BLACKWELLIZATION: The reverse process reconstructs the original data from noisy observations. It is parameterized by a neural network approximation $x_	heta(z_t, t)$, which predicts clean tokens from noisy inputs:

```
$p_	heta(z_s|z_t) = \begin{cases} \text{Cat}(z_s; z_t), & \text{if } z_t \neq m, \ \text{Cat}(z_s; \frac{(1-a_t)m+(a_t)x_	heta(z_t,t)}{1-a_t}), & \text{if } z_t = m. \end{cases}$
```

where $z_s$ is the latent variable at a prior step $s$ (with $s < t$), $x_	heta(z_t, t)$ is a neural network approximation of $x$ given the noisy observation $z_t$ at time $t$, and $p_	heta(\cdot|\cdot)$ is the model distribution approximating the true reverse process.

The training objective is a negative log bound (ELBO), expressed as:

```
$L_{\text{diffusion}} = \sum_{i=1}^T \mathbb{E}_q \left[ \frac{a_{t(i)}\alpha_{t(i)}}{1-\alpha_{t(i)}} \log(x_	heta(z_{t(i)}), x) \right]$
```

where $T$ is the number of diffusion steps, $a_{t(i)}$ and $alpha_{t(i)}$ is the noise schedule evaluated at time steps $(i)$ and $(t)$, respectively, $\mathbb{E}_q$ is the expectation over the forward process defined by $q$, and $(x_	heta(z_{t(i)}), x)$ is the dot product between the neural network output and the original input.

CONTINUOUS-TIME LIKELIHOOD BOUND: To achieve a tighter approximation to the ELBO, the discrete objective is extended to continuous time:

```
$L_{\text{ELBO}} = \mathbb{E}_q \int_0^1 \frac{a'_t}{1-a'_t} \log(x_	heta(z_t, t), x) \ dt.$
```

where $a'_t$ is the time derivative of the noise schedule $a_t$. The integral evaluates the objective over continuous time, providing a tighter bound on the likelihood to the specific functional form of the noise schedule $a_t$, highlighting the robustness of the MDLM approach.
