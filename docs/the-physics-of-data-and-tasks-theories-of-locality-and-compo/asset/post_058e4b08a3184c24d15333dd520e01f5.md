## B.3.1 (Spectrum of hierarchical kernels)

Let $T_K$ be the integral operator associated with a $d$-dimensional hierarchical kernel of depth $L+1$, $L>1$ and filter sizes ($s_1, \dots, s_L$). Eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of $T_K$ can be associated with the layers of $L$ sectors of the kernel/network. For each $1 \leq l \leq L$, the $l$-th sector consists of $\chi_{k_{i-l+1}}^{(l)}$—local eigenfunctions: functions of a single meta-patch which cannot be written as linear combinations of functions of single meta-patches. The labels $k$ of these eigenfunctions are such that there is a meta-patch $k_{i-l+1}^{(l)}$ of $k$ with vanishing non-zero sub-meta-patches and all the $k'$ outside of $k_{i-l+1}^{(l)}$ are 0 (because the eigenfunction is constant outside $k_{i-l+1}^{(l)}$). The corresponding eigenvalue is degenerate with respect to the location of the meta-patch: we call it $\Lambda_{k_{i-l+1}}^{(l)}.$

When $\Lambda_{k_{i-l+1}}^{(l)} \to \infty$, with $k = |\Lambda_{k_{i-l+1}}^{(l)}|$,

- i. if $s_1 = 2$,
  $$\Lambda_{k_{i-l+1}}^{(l)} = \mathcal{C}_{2,j} k^{-2\nu-d_{\ell}(l)} + o\left(k^{-2\nu-d_{\ell}(l)}\right),\quad(205)$$
  with $V_{NTK} = 1/2, V_{RFK} = 3/2$ and $d_{\ell}$ the effective dimensionality of the meta-patches defined in Equation 52. $\mathcal{C}_{2,j}$ is a strictly positive constant for $l \geq 2$, whereas for $l = 1$ it can take two strictly positive values depending on the parity of $k_{i-l+1}^{(l)}$.

- ii. if $s_1 \geq 3$, then for fixed non-zero angles $k/k$,
  $$\Lambda_{k_{i-l+1}}^{(l)} = \mathcal{C}_{s_l,j} \frac{|k_{i-l+1}^{(l)}|}{k} k^{-2\nu-d_{\ell}(l)} + o\left(k^{-2\nu-d_{\ell}(l)}\right),\quad(206)$$
  where $\mathcal{C}_{s_l,j}$ is a positive function for $l \geq 2$, whereas for $l = 1$ it is a strictly positive constant which depends on the parity of $k_{i-l+1}^{(l)}$.

## B.3.1 Singular expansion of hierarchical kernels

Both RFK and NTK of ReLU networks, whether deep or shallow, are built by applying the two functions $\kappa_0$ and $\kappa_1$ [CS09] (see also Definition B.2.1).

$$\kappa_0(t) = \frac{(\pi - \arccos t)}{\pi}, \quad \kappa_1(t) = \frac{(\pi - \arccos t)t + \sqrt{1-t^2}}{\pi}.\quad(207)$$

## B.3 SPECTRA OF DEEP CONVOLUTIONAL KERNELS

The functions $\kappa_0$ and $\kappa_1$ are non-analytic in $t = \pm 1$, with the following singular expansion [BB21]. Near $t=1$, with $u=1-t$
$$\begin{aligned} \kappa_0(1-u) &= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\pi}u_{i_1}^{1/2} + O(u^{5/2}), \ \kappa_1(1-u) &= 1-u + \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3\pi}u^{3/2} + O(u^{5/2}). \end{aligned} (208)$$

Near $t = -1$, with $u=u^{3/2}$
$$\begin{aligned} \kappa_0(-1+u) &= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{\pi}u_{i_1}^{1/2} + O(u^{5/2}), \ \kappa_1(-1+u) &= \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3\pi}u^{3/2} + O(u^{5/2}). \end{aligned} (209)$$

As a result, hierarchical kernels have a singular expansion when $t_{i_2-i_{2+1}} \to \pm 1$. In particular, the following expansions are relevant for computing the asymptotic scaling of eigenvalues.

**Proposition 2** RFK (when $x = 0$). The RFK of a hierarchical network of depth $L$, filter sizes $(s_1, \dots, s_L)$ and $p_L \geq 1$ has the following singular expansion when $t_{i_2-i_{2+1}} \to 1$. With $u_{i_2-i_{2+1}} = 1-2\sqrt{2}/(3\pi)$, and $\Pi_{i}^{(L+1)} s_i=1$ if $i$ is the empty set,
$$\begin{aligned} \kappa_{RFK}^{(L+1)} &\{1-u_{i_2-i_{2+1}}\}_{i_2}} &= 1 - \frac{1}{\prod_{2\leq i' \leq L} s_{i'}} \sum_{u_{i_2-i_{2+1}}} p_{L-2} \ &+ \frac{c}{p_{L-2}} \prod_{i'=1}^{L} \frac{1}{\prod_{i'' < i' \leq i'} s_{i''}} \sum_{u_{i_2-i_{2+1}}} \frac{\Sigma_{i_{i_2-i_{2+1}}}}{\prod_{2\leq i'' \leq i'} s_{i''}} \ &+ O(u_{i_2-i_{2+1}}^{2/2}) \end{aligned} (211)$$

*Proof.* With $L=1$ has one (recall that $i_{i_2-i_{2+1}} = i_2-2$ reduces to a single index)
$$\begin{aligned} \kappa_{RFK}^{(1)} (1-u_{i_2}) &= 1-u_{i_2} + cu_{i_2}^{3/2} + O(u_{i_2}^{5/2}) \ \kappa_{RFK}^{(1+1)} (\{1-u_{i_2}\}_{i_2}) &= 1-\frac{1}{p_1} \sum u_{i_2} + \frac{c}{p_1} \sum u_{i_2}^{3/2} + O(u_{i_2}^{5/2}). \end{aligned} (211)$$

**Proposition 3** (RFK when $x=-y$). The RFK of a hierarchical network of depth $L+1$, filters $(s_1, \dots, s_L)$ and $p_1 \geq 1$ has the following singular expansion when $t_{i_2-L+1} \to -1$. If $u_{i_2-L+1}=1, c=2\sqrt{2}/(3\pi)$ and $\prod_{i_1}s_1=1$ is the empty set,
$$K_{RFK}^{(L+1)}(\{1-u_{i_2-L+1}\}_{i_2-L+1}) = b_L+\frac{c_L}{(\prod_{2\leq i_2\leq L-1}s_{i_2})}p_L\sum_{i_2\leq L-1}u_{i_2-L+1}^{3/2} + O(u_{i_2-L+1}^{5/2}), (214)$$
with $b_L=\kappa_1(b_{L-1}), b_1=0$; and $c_L=c_{L-1}-\kappa_1(b_{L-1}), c_1=c_1$.

$$K_{RFK}^{(1+1)}(\{-1+u_{i_2}\})=cu_{i_2}^{3/2}+O(u_{i_2}^{5/2}) \Rightarrow K_{RFK}^{(1+1)}(\{-1+u_{i_2}\})=\frac{c}{p_1}\sum_{i_2}u_{i_2}^{3/2}+O(u_{i_2}^{5/2}), (215)$$
Thus, for $L=2$,
$$K_{RFK}^{(2)}(\{-1+u_{i_2,i_3}\}_{i_2,i_3})=\kappa_1\frac{c}{s_2}\sum_{i_2}u_{i_2,i_3}^{3/2}=\kappa_1(0)+\kappa_1'(0)\left(\frac{c}{s_2}\sum_{i_2}u_{i_2,i_3}^{3/2}\right)+O(u_{i_2,i_3}^{5/2}), (216)$$

$$K_{RFK}^{(2+1)}(\{-1+u_{i_2,i_3}\}_{i_2,i_3})=\kappa_1(0)+\frac{\kappa_1'(0)}{s_2p_2}\sum_{i_2,i_3}u_{i_2,i_3}^{3/2}+O(u_{i_2,i_3}^{5/2}), (217)$$
