## APPENDIX: A THEORY OF CREATIVITY AND COMPOSITIONALITY

## E.1 TOKEN-LATENT TUPLE CORRELATIONS

In this section, we discuss our estimate for the magnitude of the correlations between $x_i$ and tuples of latent, level-$(\ell-1)$ features $h_{(i-1)+1:i}^{(\ell)}$ with $i=2, \dots, s$ and $\ell=1, \dots, L-1$ (level-0 latents $h^{(0)}$) correspond to visible tokens. These correlations are identical for all tuples of the same higher-level feature $h_i^{(\ell)}$, thus we can reconstruct level-$\ell$ latents. For instance, with $s=2$, so $i=2$ (see Figure 19), the correlations of $x_1$ with $(x_3, x_4)$ determine the value of $h_2^{(1)}$, while with $h_3^{(1)}, h_4^{(1)}$ determine $h_2^{(2)}$. To simplify the notation, we will use the case for $i=2$ remains remainder of the sunken. Then, the goal is to compute the statistics of

$$
C^{(\ell+1)}(\mu, v) := \mathbb{P}[X_i = \mu | \mathbb{P}[X_i = \mu | h_{s+1:s}^{(\ell-1)} = v] - \mathbb{P}[X_i = \mu | h_{s+1:s}^{(\ell-1)} = v]] \quad (385)
$$

over realizations of the RHM. For each visible token $i=1, \dots, d$, single-token probabilities can be written as products of probabilities over the single rules,

$$
\mathbb{P}[X_i = \mu] = \sum_{\mu_1, \dots, \mu_i} p_{\mu_1}^{(1)}(\mu_1) \dots p_{\mu_{i-1}}^{(i)}(\mu_{i-1}) p_{\mu_i}^{(L)}(\mu_L), \quad (386)
$$

where

- i) $i_1, \dots, i_\ell$ are such that $i_1, \dots, i_\ell$ equals the $s$-ary representation of $i=1, \dots, s$, added to ensure the representation consists of latents. In other words, the multi-index $i_1, \dots, i_\ell$ uniquely identifies the path linking the root to the $i$-th leaf.
- ii) $p_{\mu_\ell}^{(\ell)}(\mu_{\ell-1}|\mu_L)$ the probability of choosing, among the available rules starting from $\mu_\ell$, one that has the $i_\ell$-th position of the right hand.
- iii) $p_{\mu_L}^{(L)}(\mu_L)$ the probability of selecting the symbol $\mu_L$ as the root ($1/v$ for our model).

These decompositions arise naturally due to the connection between probabilistic context-free grammars and Markov processes.

## E.2 ONE-STEP GRADIENT DESCENT

Knowing that the production rules of the RHM are chosen at random,

$$
\left(C^{(p)}(\mu_1)\right)^2 \simeq \frac{v^{s-1}(\nu-1)}{m(v^{s-1})} \quad (393)
$$

and, for $\nu \neq \mu_1$,

$$
\left(C^{(p)}(\mu_1)p^{(1)}(\nu)\right)^2 \simeq \frac{v^{s-1}-1}{v^s-1} \quad (394)
$$

In addition, since $\sum_{\mu} C^{(l)} = 0$, then

$$
\sum_{\nu \neq \mu_1} \left(C^{(l)}(\mu_1)\right)^2 = - \left(C^{(l)}(\mu_1)\right)^2 \quad (395)
$$

Hence,

$$
\left(C^{(\ell+1)}(\mu, \nu)\right)^2 \simeq \frac{v^{s-1}(\nu-1)}{m(v^{s-1})} \cdot \left(C^{(l)}(\mu_1)\right)^2 \quad (396)
$$
