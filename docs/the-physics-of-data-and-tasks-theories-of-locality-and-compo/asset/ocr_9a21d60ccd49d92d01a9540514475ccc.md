**Figure 40:** Learning curves for deep convolutional NTKs ($v = 1/2$) in a teacher-student setting. (a) Depth-two learned by depth-two (matched) and depth-three (mismatched) students. Neither of these students are cursed by the input dimension. (b) Depth-three students learning depth-two and depth-three teachers. These students are cursed only in the second case. The numbers inside brackets are the sequence of filter sizes of the kernels. Solid lines are the results of experiments averaged over 16 realizations with the shaded areas representing the empirical standard deviations. The predicted asymptotic scaling $\mathcal{E} \sim p^{-eta}$ are reported as dashed lines.
