# CONNECTION TO MASKED LANGUAGE DIFFUSION 233

CONNECTION TO MASKED LANGUAGE MODELS: MDLMs leveraging a masked diffusion approach where the training objective is a weighted average of classical masked language modeling (MLM) losses:

```
$L_{\text{NELBO}} = \mathbb{E}_q \int_0^1 \frac{\alpha'_t}{1-\alpha'_t} \log(x_t^	heta(z_t), x^t) dt, \quad (384)$
```

where $x_t^	heta(z_t)$: The $\ell$-th token in the original sequence, the neural network's prediction for the $\ell$-th token given the noisy sequence $z_t$. The summation runs over all tokens in the sequence, establishing a connection between MDLMs and BERT-style encoders equipping them with generative capabilities.

We employ the MDLM proposed in [Sah+24] to conduct the forward-backward experiments by random texts of a fixed token length from the WikiText2 database, fixing a fraction of the tokens $t$, and then performing the backward diffusion process by the masked sequence as the initial point for the MDLM model.
