In this section, we discuss our estimate for the magnitude of the

h_2^{(2)}$. To simplify the notation, we will use the case for $i=2$ remains


the multi-index $i_1, \dots, i_ell$ uniquely identifies the path linking the
root of the tree to the $i$-th leaf.
ii) $p_{\mu_\ell}^{(\ell)}(\mu_{\\ell-1}|\mu_L)$ the probability of choosing, among
the available production rules starting from $\mu_\ell$ one that has
the $i_\ell$-th position of the right-hand size.

the root ($1/v$ for our model).
