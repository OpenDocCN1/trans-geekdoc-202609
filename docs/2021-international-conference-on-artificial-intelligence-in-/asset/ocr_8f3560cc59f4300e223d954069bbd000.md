# ## VI. CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK

This research aimed to help identify the possible cases for Stress, Anxiety, Depression, and assess the impact of event of the general population in the Philippines using machine learning techniques. Manual efforts can be time consuming and it might not show all possible results at first glance, especially considering the large amount of data present. The results from this research help support the growing popularity

of machine learning in the field of detection and diagnostics of mental health conditions. With COVID-19 spreading all over the world, techniques like the one used in this research can contribute significantly to find the possible contributing factors to mental health issues caused by the pandemic with high accuracy. Psychologists and researchers can pursue further studies to validate and describe the effect of these identified factors. Once established, improved policies and guidelines can be created by stakeholders to help society avoid further mental harm.
