### 3.6.3 Trojan secured scheduling of DMR CDFG

After obtaining the DMR CDFG, the next step is to schedule all the operations of the original and duplication units simultaneously on the basis of the resource configuration. The resource configuration is obtained from the candidate design solution `X`[`i`]. Once the scheduling of the DMR is performed, the next step is to allocate 3PIP cores imported from different vendors. In this context, four distinct vendor allocation rules, devised in Sengupta *et al.* ([2017c](#Chapter3.html#c03_bib28)) are used (discussed earlier in Section 3.4.1.2). As the same functional resources (3PIP cores) imported from different vendor have different area, delay and power, each vendor allocation mode has an impact on the final Trojan secured design. Hence, to obtain a low-cost Trojan secured DSP core, optimal hardware resource configuration, unrolling factor(s), and vendor allocation mode must be explored. In Sengupta *et al.* ([2017c](#Chapter3.html#c03_bib28)), this is obtained through a PSO-DSE approach (explained in the next paragraph).
