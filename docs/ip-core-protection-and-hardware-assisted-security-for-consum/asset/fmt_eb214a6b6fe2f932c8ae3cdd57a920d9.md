
# Digital Watermarking Overview

Digital watermarking is the process of embedding information into digital media and generating a watermarked media ([Liu *et al*., 2017](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib50); [Sarreshtedari *et al*., 2015](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib94); [Sequeira and Kundur, 2001](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib97); [Zhao *et al*., 2015](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib129)) which evolved from steganography techniques. A difference between steganography and cryptography is shown in [Figure 11.4](#Chapter11.html#c11_fig4). In the case of cryptography, a key is used to encrypt the message and convert into a cipher text and when it is decrypted, the original message will be exposed and will not be protected. In the case of steganography, the message is hidden in the plain text, and the Data is embedded with the original message ([Mathivanan and Ganesh, 2017](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib59); [Vijayan and Sreedivya, 2017](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib113)). The way and intent of using the method differs between steganography and digital watermarking. A watermark can be perceived as an attribute of the carrier (cover). The information that will be watermarked into the media can be anything, including, and not limited to, tracking, license, authorship, copyright, and so on.

Digital watermarking has been around for a very long time and many applications are being developed that use the watermarking for security ([Cox and Miller, 2002](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib19); [Kamaruddin *et al*., 2018](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib40); [Kirovski and Malvar, 2001](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib44); [Loan *et al*., 2018](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib51); [Memon and Wong, 1998](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib60); [Mintzer *et al*., 1997](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib61); [Barnett, 1999](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib6)), such as fingerprinting, ownership evidence, and authentication. No single watermarking technique can be universally used for all the applications, which makes the research and development of new techniques in watermarking more difficult and challenging. The watermarking technique depends on various factors including the type of application and deployment ([Kamaruddin *et al*., 2018](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib40)). There are different attacks that can be launched on a media and its watermark, and the security resistance against such attacks and performance determines the success of watermarking scheme ([Vellaisamy and Ramesh, 2014](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib112); [Wang *et al*., 2017b](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib120); [Xiang *et al*., 2014](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib123)). The two major conflicting factors of watermarking are the performance of the embedded watermark and fulfilling the desired characteristics. Various benchmarks are available for watermarking techniques ([Kaur *et al*., 2012](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib42); [Voloshynovskiy *et al*., 2001](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib115)).

There are three different parts of a watermark ([Memon and Wong, 1998](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib60); [Mohanty, 1999](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib65)):

*   The encoder
*   The watermark
*   The decoder

Algorithm that is used for marking will embed the watermark into the media object and the verification algorithm will authenticate if the object is watermarked. Spatial or frequency domain can be used to embed a watermark into the media, which can be visible or invisible. A visible watermark will be a translucent mark that the original media is overlaid on and is visible only to the consumer on careful observation. An invisible watermark cannot be seen even with careful observation and needs to be retrieved from the pixels where it is embedded into. To increase the robustness, multiple watermarks can be embedded into a single media object ([Hua *et al*., 2001](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib36); [Mohanty *et al*., 1999](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib72)), which can be used for content authentication or copyright protection.

Before a watermarking scheme is used in a consumer market, different technical, legal, and social issues need to be addressed. Watermarking schemes have been already in use for a very long time. IBM visible watermarking techniques have been used in Vatican library project ([Mintzer *et al*., 1996](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib63)). In order to develop a robust watermarking scheme or an algorithm, various properties need to be met. For example, application-specific watermarking algorithms need to be developed, which will require a formal body to be involved (ASCAP, 2018; [Cox and Miller, 2002](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib19); [Mintzer *et al*., 1998](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib62); Broadcast Music Inc., 2018; Motion Picture Licensing Corporation, 2018). Copy Protection Technical Working Group (CPTWG) is a group that focuses on sharing knowledge and information on copyright protection (CPTWG, 2018). There has been extensive research in this area and many algorithms were proposed for watermarking. Hardware integration of watermarking has also been explored for better robustness and performance. [Figure 11.5](#Chapter11.html#c11_fig5) shows a hardware watermarking chip integrated into a camera ([Mohanty, 2009](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib66); [Mohanty *et al*., 2004, 2005b, 2018](#Chapter11.html#c11_bib71)). The watermarks in the camera will be embedded in the spatial or DCT domain. The watermarking can be performed for either images or videos. An example of encoder in spatial and DCT domain modules for JPEG compression is presented in [Figure 11.6](#Chapter11.html#c11_fig6).

## 11.1.3 Hardware versus media protection

Many questions arise when it comes to the copyright protection. There is a major issue of counterfeiting which should be protected against. Many different agencies are trying to protect the copyrights of the owners. But there are always attackers who illegally claim ownership by attacking the watermark protection of the original image or tampering with it. There is media protection and hardware protection when it comes to the Copyright Safeguarding and DRMs. Compared to media, hardware watermarking schemes are better. This is because they can be optimized for a better performance with a low-power consumption. The chip area of the watermarking module can also be optimized so that the overall cost of production is lower. Media protection in some cases is protecting the media on which the copy of original content is reproduced on. For example, DVD will have an information on the discs such that they will not be able to play the content outside the region of distribution. A disc produced in an area and region locked cannot be played outside that region in another drive. The contents of the disc will not be visible for reading. The hardware watermarking can be used for copyright protection and ownership authentication of the owner.
