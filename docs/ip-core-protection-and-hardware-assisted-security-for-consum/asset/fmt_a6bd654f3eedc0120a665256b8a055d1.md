### 5.6 Conclusion

This chapter presents a symmetrical IP core protection mechanism for DSP cores. This approach incurs very low overhead in the context of design latency, design area, and embedding cost compared with baseline design. Furthermore, the fingerprint and watermark encoding mechanisms discussed in this chapter are very strong in terms of security. In addition to producing marginal overhead and providing strong robustness, this approach has fault tolerance ability, lower signature embedding time, and adaptability to any CAD tool. The robustness of the symmetrical protection discussed in chapter can be improved by embedding signature constraints in other steps of HLS like resource binding, hardware allocation, etc. This would allow executing a relative investigative study between distinctive HLS phases to explore the well-matched step for signature implanting. Additionally, robustness can also be improved by adding more variables in the signature encoding process.
