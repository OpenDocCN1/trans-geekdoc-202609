### 7.7 Conclusion

This chapter presents structural obfuscation mechanisms to protect DSP IP core. The mechanisms perform structural obfuscation through multiple compiler and loop-based transformation techniques. Additionally, some obfuscation approaches offer lower design cost and stronger robustness than the others do. The robustness of the multistage obfuscation mechanism discussed in this chapter can be improved by adding more transformation techniques on the DSP IP core. Further, the combination of structural obfuscation approach and functional obfuscation can also enhance the security level for DSP applications. This would enable to hide an application and its implementation from an adversary.
