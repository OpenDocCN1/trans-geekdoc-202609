Communication has evolved in the world at a very fast and rapid pace. Several solutions have been proposed for communications, short or long distance. Technological growth also helped this in developing high-performance computing devices, making it a necessity to look for newer inventions. One of the such major creation is ‘internet of things’ and this has become an integral part of everyday life. There are many definitions for IoT that differs depending upon the individual’s perspective. In an IoT environment, all the devices or ‘THINGS’ are connected to a network and exchange data among themselves or send to the cloud ([Mohanty *et al.*, 2016](#Chapter2.html#c02_bib57)). The concept of ‘3-I’ in the context of IoT is shown in [Figure 2.1](#Chapter2.html#c02_fig1), which includes. ‘Interconnection,’ ‘Intelligence,’ and ‘Instrumentation.’ These three are the important aspects of an IoT environment, as illustrated in the figure pertaining to the smart city environment. Interconnection is the communication platform that provides the network for the devices and Internet connection that helps in transmitting the data among themselves or to the cloud services, where post-processing take place. Intelligence is the brain of the entire environment, and Instrumentation refers to various devices and sensors that are present in the environment. There are various devices present in an IoT environment such as microcontrollers, single board computers, and sensors. Each of them should have a unique IP address to be considered as a THING for the IoT ([Mohanty, 2018](#Chapter2.html#c02_bib55)).

Figure 2.1 Smart cities 3 ‘I’s

An IoT can be defined as Any TIME connection, Any THING connection, and Any PLACE Connection as shown in [Figure 2.2](#Chapter2.html#c02_fig2) ([IEEE, 2015](#Chapter2.html#c02_bib35)). IoT is not localized to one location, but it is omnipresent and can be felt anywhere. Some of the IoT devices can be deployed in places where they are not monitored continuously. Its role is to monitor a parameter and transmit the data over the air ([Yanambaka *et al.*, 2016a](#Chapter2.html#c02_bib111)). In such cases, it should be continuously connected to the internet which needs ‘Any PLACE Connection,’ ‘Any TIME Connection,’ and ‘Any THING Connection.’ With the technological advancements, various devices and board are attaining communication capabilities which can help in implementing an IoT environment at a very low cost.

## 2.1.1 IoT – architecture

The structure of an IoT solution is described in its architecture. It includes various aspects including physical, the THINGS and virtual, the communication protocols. The IoT architecture is shown in [Figure 2.3](#Chapter2.html#c02_fig3). As shown in the figure, the IoT architecture contains three different aspects which are connected to each-other by Internet. The THINGS comprise of the physical components which include the sensors, actuators, and various IoT devices. The sensors and actuators are connected to the IoT devices which help gather the data. The IoT devices are smart devices which can process the data that is collected from the sensors. Various sensors and actuators are connected using wired or wireless connectivity. Different levels of abstractions of an IoT model are shown in [Figure 2.4](#Chapter2.html#c02_fig4).

![image](OEBPS/images/PBCS0600-02-f03.jpg)

Figure 2.3 Architecture of internet of things

![image](OEBPS/images/PBCS0600-02-f04.jpg)

Figure 2.4 Architecture levels of internet of things

There are various devices that communicate with the cloud and other IoT devices in different ways as shown in [Figure 2.5](#Chapter2.html#c02_fig5). Some of the sensors and devices communicate directly to the cloud and transmit the data. Such devices will have high performance and can also do some of the data processing before uploading the data to the cloud. There are gateway devices which act as an intermediary devices between them and the sensors. These play a major role in data acquisition. After preprocessing the data, it is then sent to the cloud. Various cloud applications are available which can provide an interface for the end-user of the data to interact with it. Using the application programming interface (API), the client will have much exploratory choices and process the data further if needed.

![image](OEBPS/images/PBCS0600-02-f05.jpg)

Figure 2.5 Various aspects of internet of things

IoT, fog computing, and edge computing are three different concepts that provide the functions to control and transfer the data to the cloud storage as presented in [Figure 2.6](#Chapter2.html#c02_fig6). The cloud computing is extended to the edge; thereby, giving it the secure control and management of software, domain specific hardware, and standard compute. While edge computing is the management and control of the standalone device either individually or by software in the fog domain.

## 2.1.2 IoT – driving technology

In an IoT environment, every THING will have a unique IP address as discussed above. Every sensors would not have the capability of communication. In such cases, the sensors combined with a device that has the capability of communication will become the THING that is necessary in an IoT environment. There are various aspects to IoT. It has spread its wings to various domains like smart city, smart healthcare, smart transportation, smart home, and so on. As shown in [Figure 2.7](#Chapter2.html#c02_fig7), various sensors are connected to different single computer boards or microcontrollers which have the capability of having a unique IP address and transmit the data over internet to form a THING. In the figure, the sensor is connected to a smart watch which will collect the data from the sensor and transmit the same to the cloud or other devices.
