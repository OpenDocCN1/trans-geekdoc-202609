After applying feature selection, only eight relevant features were retained from the eleven features. As mentioned before, it was found that five histogram features mean, energy, entropy, variance, skewness; and three GLCM features viz. contrast, homogeneity and angular moment were crucial for classification. The classification accuracy improved by 2% (to 76%). However, the specificity reduced to 68.06% and sensitivity increased to 96.43%. Hence, the reduced feature set has been able to successfully identify more of the abnormal images than the normal images.
