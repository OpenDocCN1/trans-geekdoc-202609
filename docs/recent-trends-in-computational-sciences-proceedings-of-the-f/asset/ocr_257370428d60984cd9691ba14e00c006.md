# 6 CORRELATION OF ELECTROGASTOGRAM AND ELECTROMYOGRAM

Based on the information provided, it seems that the study found significant correlations between the amplitude of EEG, electromyogram, and mechanical activity of the gastric antrum in both digestive and Integrative states. The EEG reflected electrical control activity (ECA) and electrical response activity (ERA). However, it appears that there were some discrepancies between the EEG waves and myoelectric waves, suggesting that the EEG may not be an accurate reflection of the underlying gastric activity.
