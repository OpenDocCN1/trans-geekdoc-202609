# CONCLUSION

IoT based applications have become popular with the emerging technology in the last decade. The adoption of wired and wireless monitoring and management systems in greenhouse or agriculture is one of the best uses of IoT technology. By utilizing IoT in the greenhouse, we can enhance crop productivity, monitor weather conditions such as humidity, temperature and other parameters like moisture and depth of the soil can be detected and modify the essentials for the farm. We can detect pests and pedestrians in the field using infrared. Sensors and microcontrollers can be interfaced with each other via IoT and wireless communication between sensors. This can help the farmers overcome the problems posed by the weather and therefore monitor conditions using mobile devices or laptops. These systems can provide high crop yielding and output results. It can also reduce the labour work on the farm.
