# Implementing AI on microcontrollers in fog and edge architectures

Dalibor Dobrilovic
Technical Faculty “Mihajlo Pupin”, Zrenjanin, University of Novi Sad, Zrenjanin, Serbia

ABSTRACT: We are witnessing the era of evolution of complex system architectures, starting from cloud computing and gradually to the edge, and mist computing paradigms. The development of applications in these complex systems is equally important for companies as well as for users. Branches need fast, low-cost, and effective environments for rapid prototyping. The microcontrollers grow in their performance and, simultaneously, are available for considerably lower prices. Open-source hardware movement is another important base for rapid fog and edge prototyping. This paper presents the approach of using open-source hardware boards in computing architectures investigating the possibility of implementing Artificial Intelligence on these edge devices. The performance of AI-supported edge devices is monitored and analyzed in terms of their speed, capacity, and accuracy. The Arduino, ESP8266, and ESP32 development boards are evaluated in this research.

Keywords: AI on microcontrollers, AI in edge computing, k-Nearest Neighbor, open-source hardware, intelligent sensor networks

# INTRODUCTION

Considering the rapid evolution of complex system architectures, from cloud computing to the edge, fog, and mist computing paradigms, we should analyze their impact on the market and academic institutions. The rapid changes in the development of complex systems demand the immediate answer of companies and markets to challenges. Both branches need fast, low-cost, and effective environments to rapidly prototype applications for the aforementioned systems.

Two major factors influenced the efforts to meet these demands. The rapid development of microcontrollers and development boards in their higher processing powers and significantly decreased prices. The open-source movement started approximately ten years ago and rapidly spread and overwhelmed the market. Considering the scientific databases, a significant number of research papers are using Arduino UNO, Arduino MEGA, NodeMCU, and similar platforms.

This paper presents the approach of using open-source development boards in edge computing architectures by the possibility of implementing Artificial Intelligence on edge devices such as Arduino and its clones. The motivation for this research goal lies in the fact that the ability of Arduino and clone boards to support AI can greatly enhance the teaching process and can enable the inclusion of AI implementation in fog and edge computing architectures in university curriculum.

Besides the analyses of partial utilization of AI methods on Arduino and clone boards, the paper presents a methodology for implementing AI in the wireless sensor network based on Arduino and clone development boards. This implementation gives an example of AI in a specially designed wireless sensor network to acquire solar radiation data.

# CLOUD COMPUTING ARCHITECTURE

It is important to briefly point to the differences in various architectural approaches for introducing the topic. The Cloud computing paradigm [1] is based on remotely accessible data centers. While data centers offer centralized storage, cloud power, and data processing. The users can use these resources. The service providers are responsible for the system's maintenance, and users usually pay for those services. The system's main feature, centralization, is its biggest weakness. This approach requires high bandwidth demands, high power consumption, complex storage and security issues, and high latency. Alternative approaches are required to overcome these problems [2,3].

Alternative approaches are challenged with decentralization in system architecture. The challenge for all approaches is focused on decentralizing data processing and data storage. The location of the decentralized processing and data storage can be different.

Edge computing brings data processing to devices, allowing almost real-time processing and lower latency. The side effect of this approach is balancing the cloud pressure. Fog computing processes the data and data storage between edge devices and the cloud. This approach allows decentralization of the system. The third approach, mist computing, is the newest one. It is enabled by the recent development of microcontroller boards, followed by a significant cost drop. The computing power and overall performance of microcontroller boards allow widely decentralized processing at the extreme edge of the system.

# 5 WIRELESS SENSOR NETWORK FOR SOLAR RADIATION ACQUISITION

An example of implementing AI-to-edge devices is given in the scenario of a wireless network for solar radiation acquisition. The motivation for designing this sensor network is based on a necessity for experimentation with solar-powered sensor nodes and education in that field. The complexity of the systems based on sensor nodes, the large and constantly growing number of nodes, and the need for constant operation leads to the importance of deploying solar-powered sensor devices.

The sensor network presented in this paper is designed to collect solar radiation data. Data can be obtained using the energy consumption of sensor nodes, helping in mapping solar radiation, and enhance performance through node deployment. The sensor network is shown in Figure 1. The presented network is designed to be used as (1) the platform for the development of energy-efficient solar-powered sensor nodes; (2) the platform in Mechanical engineering courses to analyze solar panels, their efficiency, and dependency on various ambient conditions; and (3) the platform for scientific research on solar radiation acquisition and analyses.

The planned configuration of the sensor nodes can be like DHT-11 or DHT-22 for ambient temperature and humidity, and for a solar temperature measuring, BH1750 and UV sensor for light intensity measuring; ACS712 or INA169 current sensor, MAX471 current and voltage sensor, etc. Sensor nodes have additionally the solar charger using managing Li-Po battery 500 mAh charging and discharging.
