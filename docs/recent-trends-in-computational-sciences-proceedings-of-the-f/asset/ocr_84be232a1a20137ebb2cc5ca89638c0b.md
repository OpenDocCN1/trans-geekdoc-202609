ABSTRACT: Ladakh, the cold region of India is blessed with 300 cloud free days. The use of solar energy in a protected structure like a greenhouse is an ideal way to tap this natural energy to grow vegetables during the peak winter when the outside temperature drops to –25°C. But high temperature variation during day and night hampers the growth and yield of vegetables in the greenhouse. Smart agriculture using IoT can be helpful to overcome these drawbacks. Using sensors of temperature controller, humidity, soil will help help maintaining the temperature inside the greenhouse. The paper discusses the application of IoT sensors in the greenhouse.

Keywords: Greenhouse, IoT, Smart Agriculture, Sensors, Cold Arid, Solar

# 1 INTRODUCTION

Greenhouse technology is the technique of growing crops by providing favourable environment conditions to the plants. It is used to grow crops from unfavourable weather conditions and to create the ideal environment for plant growth and maximum output, including the right amount of soil, light, temperature, humidity, carbon dioxide etc.However greenhouses in regions like Ladakh, which has 300 cloud-free days each year [1], It is difficult to monitor plant growth and manually turn on off temperature controllers in a traditional hand-operated greenhouse. The ideal way to address these issues is by adopting an IoT-enabled Smart greenhouse. IoT agricultural sensors can provide farmers crop production information, rainfall forecasting, soil nutrition, pest infestation, and other useful information. Agricultural sensors can measure a variety of field factors, including temperature, humidity, soil moisture etc have access of their field conditions from anywhere and interpret the data accordingly [2].

# 2 LITERATURE REVIEW

Ravi Kodali et al. 2016 [3] "IoT based Smart Greenhouse". IEEE Region. 10th Humanitarian Technology Conference (R10-HTC). In this study, they developed a Smart Greenhouse model that enables farmers to carry out work automatically without the need for intensive manual inspection. A water monitoring sensor, sensors that measure temperature and humidity, a fogger are used to control temperature and humidity. Sumnath et al. 2019 [4] "Smart Greenhouse using IoT and Cloud Computing", International Research Journal of Engineering and Technology. They have proposed the Smart Greenhouse farm employing IoT and cloud computing technology in this study. To operate the farm, several sensors like temperature, soil moisture, and sunshine are used.
