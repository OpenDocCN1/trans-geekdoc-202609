# Smart greenhouse: A bliss to the farmers of cold arid region

S. Angchuk, Aleem Ali
Department of Computer Science & Engineering, University of Chandigarh, Ladakh

ABSTRACT: The cold region of India is blessed with cloud free days. The use of solar energy in a protected structure like greenhouse is an ideal way to tap this natural energy to grow vegetables during the peak winter when the outside temperature drops to –5°C. But high temperature variation during day and night hampers the growth and yield of vegetables in the greenhouse. Smart agriculture using IoT can be helpful to overcome these drawbacks. Using sensors of temperature, humidity, soil will help maintaining the temperature inside the greenhouse. The paper discusses the application of IoT sensors in the greenhouse.

Keywords: Greenhouse, IoT, Smart Agriculture, Cold arid, Solar

# 1 INTRODUCTION

Greenhouse technology is the technique of growing crops by providing favourable environment conditions to the plants. It is used to grow crops from unfavourable weather conditions and to create the ideal environment for plant growth and maximum output, including the right amount of soil, light, temperature, humidity, carbon dioxide etc. However greenhouses in regions like Ladakh, which has cloud free days each year, it is difficult to monitor plant growth and manually turn off temperature controllers in a traditional hand-operated greenhouse. The ideal way to address these issues is by adopting an IoT-enabled Smart greenhouse. IoT agricultural sensors can provide farmers crop production information, rainfall forecasting, soil nutrition, pest infestation, and other useful information. Agricultural sensors can measure a variety of field factors, including temperature, humidity, soil moisture etc access of their field conditions from anywhere and interpret the data accordingly.

# 2 LITERATURE REVIEW

Ravi Kodali et al. 2016 [3] "IoT based Smart Greenhouse". IEEE 10th Humanitarian Technology Conference (R-HTC). In this study, they developed a Smart Greenhouse model that enables farmers to carry out work automatically without the need for intensive manual inspection. A water monitoring sensor, sensors that measure temperature and humidity, a fogger are used to control temperature and humidity.

Sumnath et al. 2019 [4] "Smart Greenhouse using IoT and Cloud Computing", International Research Journal of Engineering and Technology. They have proposed the Smart Greenhouse farm employing IoT and cloud computing technology in this study. To operate the farm, several sensors like temperature, soil moisture, and sunshine are used. In addition to these sensors, an analogue to digital converter, a microcontroller and actuators are used. When the defined climate variables cross the threshold, the sensors detect a change. Farmers can readily monitor the greenhouse farm once they have this information. The fan, water pump buzzer, and lights can all be conveniently monitored without any physical intervention. This system is being utilised to eliminate the system's issues by minimising human intervention to the greatest extent practicable.

Mohammad Wolullah et al. 2018 [5], "Internet of Smart Greenhouse: Remote Monitoring and Automatic Control", International Conference on Electronic and Intelligent Vehicles. They provide information regarding the system that monitors temperature, humidity, soil moisture and takes action based on the data in this paper. It provides a database that will be useful for analysis and reports. This device is ideal for deployment in locations such as the North Pole and cold climate countries where residents exist but not grow to harsh winters.
