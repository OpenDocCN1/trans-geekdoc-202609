# CONCLUSION

Artificial neural networks (ANN) are similar to biological networks in that they are used to gather specific data/information like human brain processes the given data. Each connection in the neuron is associated with a numeric number, which can be called a weight. ANN is being used in all fields of research and is used to solve problems by following a sequence of rules. Its advantage is that it has the ability to work with insufficient knowledge and can be forced to make predictions in unsuitable conditions.

Neural networks are used to map multidimensional input, and are alternatives to PCA (principal component analysis). They can take hours or months to train, but they have a very small scope compared to ANN's vast scale. Neural networks can learn and train any number of inputs by the user and produce two to three outputs which can have 0.005% error in the actual output value. ANN applications are used in various fields like Engineering, Psychology, Medical, Research field, etc., and in pharmaceutical research fields like interpretation of data, drug delivery, etc. In the medical field, ANN is used to segment some of the infected parts, and CT scans are performed to differentiate widespread and rare diseases.
