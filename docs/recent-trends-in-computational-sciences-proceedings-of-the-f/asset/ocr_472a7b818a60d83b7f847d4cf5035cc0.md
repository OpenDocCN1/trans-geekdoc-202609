**ABSTRACT:** These days, many people watch anime, especially the younger generations. With many different sorts of programming available, this specialist area of the entertainment industry is drawing an increasing number of people. The word “anime,” which was derived from the word “animation,” has a growing following all over the world. The anime industry has been growing rapidly in recent years, bringing in billions of dollars annually. Significant streaming providers like Netflix and Amazon Prime are paying attention to this. Researchers are working hard to apply machine learning algorithms to offer the viewer with the appropriate anime because it is currently a popular trend among the younger generation. The purpose of the research presented in this article is to further current research. From Kaggle, two datasets have been used. The first dataset for an anime comprises 7 columns and 12293 rows. The second rating dataset anime comprises 3 columns and 7813735 rows. Anime may be rated by users, who can then add it to their finished list. The top 15 matching suggestions and recommendations were then obtained using machine learning approaches (content-based filtering, collaborative filtering, and popularity-based filtering).

*Keywords:* Recommendation system, Machine learning, KNN, SVD

# 1 INTRODUCTION

Recommender structures are a type of data filtering technology that tries to infer the “rating” or “interest” of a customer for product. They adjust the information presented to a customer based on their preferences, the material’s relevance, and other factors [1]. In addition to other things, recommenders are frequently used to promote books, movies, restaurants, travel destinations, and purchases. These systems eliminate a large amount of data by concentrating on the most common data based on the input of the user and other factors such as the user’s preferences and interests. It evaluates the similarity between consumers and items as well the compatibility between the person and the thing to make suggestions.
