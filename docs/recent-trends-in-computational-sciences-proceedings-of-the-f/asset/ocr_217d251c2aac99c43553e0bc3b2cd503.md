# 3 SOCIAL ENGINEERING ATTACK SCENARIO

Social engineering may entail the victim's interests, a routine, a routed organisation such as a place of work, place of study, or even close family. After studying this relevant data, the attacker could use it to trick the target into sending them such as bank account information and passwords in exchange for friendly requests and favours [5].

Perspective of SE: A perpetrator who needs the victim's personal data to finish an important task is one who launches the con. By being a coworker, police officer, bank or tax official, or any other person with the power to know anything, the attacker starts by winning the victim's trust.

Attacker perspective: The term “attacker perspective” refers to “a worldview in which every piece of information can be leveraged to achieve a desired goal.” They identify a target, a goal, and devise a strategy to achieve it. They have the ability to look at an attack, identify its flaws, and devise various ways to attack it. This viewpoint is employed in social engineering attacks. The goal is to obtain information from the target while speaking with them on the phone or making direct eye contact.
