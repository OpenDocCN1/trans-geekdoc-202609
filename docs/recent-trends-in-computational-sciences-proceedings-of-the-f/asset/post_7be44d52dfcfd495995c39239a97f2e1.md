## 1 INTRODUCTION

Recommender structures are a type of data filtering technology that tries to infer the “rating” or “interest” of a customer for product. They adjust the information presented to a customer based on their preferences, the material’s relevance, and other factors [1]. In addition to other things, recommenders are frequently used to promote books, movies, restaurants, travel destinations, and purchases. These systems eliminate a large amount of data by concentrating on the most common data based on the input of the user and other factors such as the user’s preferences and interests. It evaluates the similarity between consumers and items as well the compatibility between the person and the thing to make suggestions. On the other hand, the younger generation is very interested in anime. In the days before the internet, fans used to discover new titles through mouth of mouth. Due to the growth of OTT services and other factors, people may now watch as much as they want, how many new anime titles have been released in recent years.
