**ABSTRACT:** Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) are widely used in various fields due to their versatile applications. They are capable of handling complex functions and can adapt to new conditions. Their applications range from data interpretation, drug design, pattern recognition, translation, credit fraud detection, medical diagnosis and more. ANNs have become an essential tool in today's world due to their ability to interpret large amounts of data, making them crucial in fields such as medical diagnosis and credit fraud detection. The versatility of ANNs makes them applicable in many industries and continues to contribute to their growing popularity. The current work focuses on exploring the wide range of applications of ANNs and their significance in our world today.

# 1 INTRODUCTION

Human brains consist of many neurons, which perform various functions. Artificial neural networks (ANN's) work like a human brain.

Artificial neural networks (ANN's) are also biologically similar neuron types as they are the programs to use specific data/information like a human brain process. The Input data is received by the input layer and transmitted to the hidden layer and is transmitted to the Output layer. ANN is being in all fields of research. It is used to solve the programs by following a sequence of rules. Each connection in the neuron is associated with a numeric number, which can be called as weight [1].
