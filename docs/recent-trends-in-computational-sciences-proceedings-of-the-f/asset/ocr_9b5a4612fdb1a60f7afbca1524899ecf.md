# 6 CONCLUSION AND FUTURE SCOPE

In this, paper aimed to perform churn prediction and used an ensemble of three neural network models. Even though the dataset used is a telecom churn dataset. The proposed model can be used over any churn dataset. So any sector can use this in their customer relation or behavior analyses. Ensemble learning considers of every constituent model. These constituent models are important. In all, the constituent models are based on neural networks. This paper shows over telecom churn dataset. Accuracy was to be 84% and the least difference between accuracies of ensemble and constituent models is 6%. This 3% difference is the improvement by proposed ensemble method. In Future networks used be optimized and can use more models to form ensemble models. Data is very important in churn prediction. Working with proper datasets can provide a positive level of results.
