# Role of social engineering in cyber security

V. Ambika, M. Spoorthi & A.D. Radhika
Vidyavardhaka College of Engineering, Mysore, India

ABSTRACT: Social engineering refers to wider ways of tactics, where an attacker sets up a social situation in a potential victim is encouraged to let down his or her guard. Social engineering schemes usually play some kind of games with their target. Major social engineering techniques used by an attacker are "instant messages", "fake antivirus", "emails", "phone calls" and many more. Actions by victims includes revealing details, downloading and installing malware, engaging in an illegal act in transaction. Social Engineering is most frequently used as a part of information security attack and easier to prevent an employee performing an act than it is to circumvent and control SE Attacks. This paper highlights the social engineering tactics used by attackers, classifications of SE attacks, recent social engineering attacks took place globally and different approaches to detect SE attacks, mitigations and prevention to avoid social engineering tactics.

Keywords: social engineering attacks, cyber-crimes, psychology, security

# 1. INTRODUCTION

Social engineering entails the use of fraud and deception to gain access to private or sensitive information. It is one of the current greatest dangers to information security. The subject is complex, and it is becoming more popular among individuals and businesses. Social engineering is concerned with the human aspects of operations. In general, cyber-attacks are increasing at an alarming rate, with one occurring every 39 seconds. [1]. Current COVID-19 pandemic have moved a wide range of everyday activities online. This has led to an increase in the number of users on these networks as well as a boost of the time used already in online activities. The rise in usage has also made web users more vulnerable to phishing and scams about Infose and the different hazards that a typical web user can encounter. According to the report, over 98% of cyber-attacks worldwide are somewhat in the form of social engineering attacks. Every year, 700 social engineering threats are presented to the typical organization [2,3]. Cyber criminals persuade their victims to violate security protocols, exposing confidential information that could be used in a more targeted attack. Many distinguishing characteristics make SE a popular attack in the tech community and a universal, and persistent threat to cyber-security. There are numerous of attacks because, phishing attacks have been conducted to adequate level of not technical but also social vulnerabilities.
