## 7 CONCLUSION

This study is a survey on social engineering tactics, current detection methods, and its countermeasures. SE exploits human behaviour and natural tendencies to fool individuals into making errors and disclosing sensitive information. Attacks do this by making proposals that are too good to be true. It is clear that attackers can wrongly affect victims, one must be very vigilant when detecting. Unfortunately, these attacks are unstoppable by technical means alone. As attacks are increasingly violent and common, causing financial and emotional harm to individuals and businesses, there is a high demand for new detection and mitigation strategies, also countries need to invest in cybersecurity to build a skilled and knowledgeable workforce.
