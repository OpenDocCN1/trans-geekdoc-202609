+   • Difficult to assess this sort of learning activities using pre-programmed smart contract without human mediation.

+   • Immutability: This can be a benefit and a drawback, as it prevents fraudulent changes to educational records but also makes legitimate changes impossible.

+   • Opposition from central authorities: There is a lack of clear regulatory guidance regarding its use in the higher education sector.

In order to address these challenges, a potential solution is to develop standardized learning objectives and assessment methods for consistent assessment of student learning. It is also important to engage with central authorities to gain their support and educate them about blockchain's potential benefits.

# CONCLUSION AND FUTURE INSIGHTS

The proposed system uses blockchain to connect students with the course instructors in a decentralized manner without intermediaries is a promising development in the field of higher education. The use of smart contracts for creating and storing digital credits to IPFS and for successful course completion has the potential to improve the accessibility, affordability, and transparency of higher education.

The future scope entails to include further development and integration of blockchain technology in research collaboration, administration of scholarships and financial aid.
