# Document verification using blockchain

Danish Hashmi, Atul Anurag, Reyjala Chethan, Sahitya Sagiraju & V. Sarasvathi
PES University

ABSTRACT: A student produces several certificates over their entire academic career, whether they are in high school, or even a postgraduate program. These certificates could include results, diplomas, or transcripts. Students must provide these certificates to universities or employers to be admitted. It becomes tiresome to track these certificates and manually verify their legitimacy. A lack of a suitable anti forge system in a situation where it is discovered that the diploma is forged. Everything needed to be digitized with the principles of Confidentiality, Reliability, and Availability to make the data more secure and safe. Our system will use the Blockchain technology along with the Planetary File System (IPFS) to solve this problem. The flow of our system is, in a nutshell, include a Certificate Issuer with student details, onto the IPFS, which breaks the file into multiple parts and returns a CID as an IPFS link. The uploading of the document involves a decentralized authority that is backed on the blockchain. Each document will have a distinct IPFS link that may be utilized by any official through the portal to verify the document's authenticity. Our system will have the advantage of reducing the possibility of a student losing or destroying or a document and making document verification simple.

# 1 INTRODUCTION

The issue of the veracity of digital information can potentially be solved with the help of blockchain, a recently created revolutionary technology. Currently, the blockchain which is widely used by cryptocur is thought of as a decentralized ledger on which anybody can initiate a transaction without the aid of middlemen, and everything is carried out by the miners. Paper [8] gives detailed information about permitted blockchain which is used to limit the access to the blockchain so that only approved members can add a block. This helps in minimizing the security risks associated with giving access to the public.

Blockchain can also be used to store data on it, although costly way of storing data. However, IPFS can be implemented for effective storing of vast variants of data and assets. IPFS is a framework based on blockchain technology for storing data and files with integrity and resilience that is decentralized. In its most basic form, IPFS is an open distributed file system that can be utilized to store and share volumes of files. IPFS returns a CID (Content Identifier) which is a combination hash of all the hashes, which makes it easy to access the document and to give authenticity and traceability. Our suggested approach uses both IPFS and blockchain smart contracts. The hash of a digital document changes whenever its contents change, indicating any changed and amended. This is collision-free, which means that any different values will produce two different hashes.
