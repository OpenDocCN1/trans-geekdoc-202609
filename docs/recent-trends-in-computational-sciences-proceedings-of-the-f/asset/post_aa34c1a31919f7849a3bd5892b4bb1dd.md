ABSTRACT: Institutes/Hospitals usually provide a directory to their available services, but these directs are the most static and do not provide interactivity features to the visitors/clients. With the rise in smart devices and technology, we see a remarkable demand for real-time location-based services. In this paper, we present a mobile application-guided navigator. Various indoor localization techniques are studied, out which the beacons wireless Indoor Positioning System (IPS) is an efficient approach. In an indoor space, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacons are installed to track the smart device. For as long as people have had the urge to detect new places and find their way, navigation has been an important key to future in those ages. However, it does not support sufficient positioning accuracy for the inside of buildings. Signals are attenuated inside of buildings or opaque high building regions because various materials between satellites and receivers establish interface to the signal propagation. Indoor positioning technologies have been to cover this problem.
