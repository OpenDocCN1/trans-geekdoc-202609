In this we try to use an unsupervised approach to cluster restaurants that are similar and recommend to users according to the ones that have ordered from the past. The models used in this project give average results given that clustering techniques have their problems and are not the most novel approaches available. However, working on real-life data sets takes a deep dive into the nuances of data mining and making predictions in big projects taken by companies. # 1 INTRODUCTION

Over the last few years, recommender systems have become increasingly popular due to the technological advancements occurring in the fields of data mining, predictive analysis, and machine learning. Massive corporations such as YouTube, Spotify, Netflix, Amazon, etc use recommender systems to recommend their products to their customers The methods used for this are usually either machine learning based, involving techniques such as collaborative filtering or clustering, two popular approaches to unsupervised learning, or they are algorithmic that is based on relevant associates with their products.

In this, we try to use an unsupervised approach to cluster restaurants that are similar and recommend to users according to the ones that they have ordered from the past. Using attributes such as the location, type, and cuisines of the restaurant, we have used clustering algorithms [2] to recommend similar restaurants to users. We also adopt the strategy of frequent item generation and generating association rules to recommend similar dishes and cuisines, based on market basket analysis.
