# 3.2 Ensemble learning

Ensemble learning is a machine learning concept dealing with use of multiple learning algorithms. Ensemble models obtained better performance than any individuals used. Better performance over constituent models is observed in ensemble learning methods why it is better to choose ensemble models over single constituent models. In ensemble learning, predictions generated by all constituent models are considered in final decision making. Opinions are taken to make final predictions. Every model has its own hypothesis to form hopefully a better hypothesis [15]. In this paper, predictions of all three models are considered. As we are dealing with a binary classification problem where decision is either “0” or “1”, we suppose we have two models predicted to be “0” and another model predicted to be “1”. As prediction “0” is the most popular vote so “0” will be a prediction. We achieve this system by adding all three predictions and then if the sum is greater than (2 or 3) then “1” will be a prediction. If the sum is either 0 or 1 then prediction is to be “0” means customers will not churn or “1” means customer will churn.
