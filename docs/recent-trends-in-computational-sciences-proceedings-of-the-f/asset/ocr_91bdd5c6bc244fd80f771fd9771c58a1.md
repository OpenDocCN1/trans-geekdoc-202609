# ## CONCLUSION

This paper introduced a method to detect and alert when car appears in their blind spots using videos from cameras installed in the car. Mean-shift algorithm is used to implement blind spot monitoring which is highly efficient and fast as no training data is required. It is directly processing the captured video frames. In this paper the algorithm proposed works in two parts one the definition of target area and second, the application of mean shift algorithm for object tracking. The algorithm continuously ensures the safety of car as the driver is duly notified against potential dangers through warning messages and serves its purpose.
