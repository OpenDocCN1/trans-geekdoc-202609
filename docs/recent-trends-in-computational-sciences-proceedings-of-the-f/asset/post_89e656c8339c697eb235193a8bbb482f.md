Keywords: Blockchain, Higher Education, Smart Contracts, IPFS

## INTRODUCTION

Blockchain technology and application have the potential to revolutionize higher education by creating a decentralized, transparent and accessible learning environment. It includes a web-based interface for students and institutions to manage academic records. The system is designed to ensure data integrity and transparency through a decentralized ledger.

Blockchain offers several key benefits:
- Decentralization: It eliminates the need for central authorities, allowing students and institutions direct control over data.
- Security: Using cryptographic hashing and consensus mechanisms, blockchain ensures that records are difficult for unauthorized entities to alter.
- Transparency: All transactions and records are recorded on a transparent ledger, fostering trust.

In this paper, we explore the implementation of a blockchain-based higher education ecosystem and discuss its impact.
