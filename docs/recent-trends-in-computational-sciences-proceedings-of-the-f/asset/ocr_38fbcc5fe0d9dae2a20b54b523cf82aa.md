# 5 CONCLUSION

Industrial worker's health is extremely crucial from both the industry as well as the worker's perspectives. Companies take preventive measures to ensure ergonomics of employees by providing effective training for them to adapt to their job profiles. Even then, there can be situations where the employees consciously or subconsciously follow improper ergonomics which can jeopardize their health in the long run. This article provides a review of research and work carried out on how to automate the monitoring of worker health and ergonomics. Finally, the paper has highlighted the method of approach for early detection of improper ergonomics of workers in various industries.
