# 2 LITERATURE REVIEW

The systematic study conducted in this work is summarized with respect to the various parameters and briefed correspondingly as follows. In the paper [6], authors developed Convolutional Neural Network model to analyze the situation of plants depending on the plant image. They used of Inception-Resnet architecture. Okra images were used to train the model. They had used about one hundred and eighty four forty seven images for testing process. Image augmentation method was applied to enlarge size of dataset.

In the paper [7], Redmi 3 prime Smartphone was used to capture the soil images. The HSV(Hue, Saturation and Value) of the soil images are calculated and it stores Saturation and Hue. The Analysis of soil prediction was done using Artificial Neural Network(ANN), Linear Regression and KNN Regression. It was found that ANN had better result.

In the paper [8], the authors discussed about the soil in the farmland of Philippines, Image processing and computer vision techniques were used to classify the soil nutrients i.e., Nitrogen,Phosphorus, Potassium and pH. Soil experiment was carried out to decide the soil’s primary nutrients. Artificial neural network (ANN) was used for fast and accurate performance. There were used four hundred and eighty captured image data, chemical soil measures were used to categorize fertility of the soil [9], EC, P,Fe, Zn, B, S, Mn, K, Cu pH were used to categorize the soil as high, medium, low fertility. Random Forests (RF), logistic regression, Naive Bayes, decision tree bagging, Support Vector Machine (SVM), Boosted Regression Tree (BRT) were used to identify the soil as high, medium, low. The RF regression result Experiment was conducted in Karnataka state profile with 11 attributes and 92832 instances.

In the paper [10], soil images are captured using Smartphone and them are measured using K-Mean Algorithm and features of the soil are determined using Hue, Saturation and Value (HSV) color processing. Finally, the features of the soil image are regressed with the actual pH value was observed in soil pH laboratory. The soil samples were taken from the different region of Guwahati zone.

In the paper [11], the datasets of soil were taken from TNAU website which included 33 districts of Tamil Nadu. The algorithms that were used the experiment are Bayes Net, IB and Naive Bayes to predict type of crop for the soil. The accuracy was measured using false positive, true positive value recall, measure, and Precision.

In the paper [12], The research was based on secondary data from Kumaun region of Uttarakhand. The data is used to develop and train FSI (subtractive based fuzzy inference system) and RBF NN (radial basis function network) on MATLAB, for predicting the soil pH based on minerals concentration i.e., Fe, P, Mn, K, Cu. Thus, the prediction performance was compared using mean square prediction error.
