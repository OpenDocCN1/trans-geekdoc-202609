In the identified recipient is not available and not in a position for accepting the available organ, the transplantation center updates the the system application. This will trigger another recipient identification process and identify the next best-suited recipient from the waiting list. This process continues until a recipient is available for accepting the organ. All these transactions will be logged in the logger.

After the successful transplantation, the transplantation center will update the status in the system application. This will remove the recipient from the waiting list. The algorithm at the OPT manager updates the waiting list and communicates the change to all the transplantation centers along the reasons for the update. Transplantation organizations can also run a query to get the reason for the new priority assigned to the recipients registered from a particular organization. This option can be utilized for patients who wanted to know their position and the reason for each position change.
