ABSTRACT: Migraine is a prevalent headache illness that affects 9.4 percent of men and 20.2 percent of women on average. The third most common and the sixth most common cause of impairment worldwide. However, studies aimed at prevalence and effects of migraine among Indian and International college students have not been conducted. The present study sought to better understand the prevalence and effects of migraine among university students in Greater Noida. Methods: University students of Greater Noida participated in the study between 1st April 2022 and the 21st of June 2022. A questionnaire-based study was used. Undergrad, postgraduate, and PhD students from various universities in Noida, India, were the target population inclusive of gender. The study included students from first to last year students who confirmed having migraine at least twice in the three months before. Having provided consent to participate in the study and being able to respond to the survey questions. The students were given access to the questionnaire using an online portal (Mail and What app). A total of 200 responses were received and analyzed. Analysis of data was done through SPSS versus, the tool used multivariate linear regression to find the association of independent variables (age, gender, nationality and course) with dependent variable headache. MANOVA tool was further used to find the association of independent variables (age, gender, nationality and course) with dependent variable headache.
