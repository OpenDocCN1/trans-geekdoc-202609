# 5.6 Temperature and humidity sensor

A simple low-cost digital temperature and humidity sensor (DHT11) is shown in Figure 7. This sensor has two parts: a capacitive humidity sensor and a thermistor. The humidity sensor detects, measures and reports both the moisture and air temperature. The temperature ranges from 0°C to 50°C, while humidity levels range from 20% to 90%. Majority of sensors are applied in the Internet of Things. There are many additional sensors besides these, but DHT11 is the most popular temperature sensor.
