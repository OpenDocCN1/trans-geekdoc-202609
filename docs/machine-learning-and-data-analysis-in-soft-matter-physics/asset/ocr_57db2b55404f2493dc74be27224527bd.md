Feature alignment techniques aim to address this issue by ensuring that the features extracted from various structures are brought into a consistent and compatible representation, enabling researchers to effectively compare, classify, or analyze them. By aligning the features, researchers can transfer knowledge and insights obtained from one soft matter structure to another, even when they come from different experimental setups or materials. Feature alignment in soft matter structure analysis is a crucial technique that ensures consistent and compatible feature representations across different soft matter systems. It allows researchers to effectively compare and analyze diverse structures, promoting knowledge transfer and advancing our understanding of complex soft matter materials.

# 8.3.5 Pseudo-labeling for Particle Tracking in Soft Matter Microscopy

Pseudo-labeling for particle tracking in soft matter microscopy is a semi-supervised learning technique that leverages the predictions of a pre-trained model on unlabeled data to generate pseudo-labels for the particles in the images. Particle tracking is a fundamental task in soft matter physics, where researchers aim to track the movements and trajectories of particles within complex materials observed through microscopy.
