+   * beta1 and beta2 are hyperparameters that control the exponential decay rates of the moving averages of the gradients and squared gradients, respectively. Typical values are 0.9 for beta1 and 0.999 for beta2.
+   * m and v are the first and second moment estimates of the gradients and squared gradients, respectively.
+   * m_hat and v_hat are the bias-corrected moment estimates, accounting for the fact that m and v are initialized to zero at the start of training.
+   * t is the time step or iteration number, starting from 1.
+   * epsilon is a small constant (e.g., 1e-8) added to the denominator to prevent division by zero.

Adam has shown superior performance in various deep learning tasks and is widely used in state-of-the-art neural network architectures. Its adaptive learning rate mechanism, combined with momentum, makes it well-suited for handling sparse gradients, converging faster, and providing better generalization in complex optimization landscapes.

# AdaDelta Deep Learning Optimizer

AdaDelta can be seen as a more robust version of the AdaGrad optimizer. It is based upon adaptive learning and is designed to deal with significant drawbacks of AdaGrad and RMS prop optimizer. The main problem with the above two optimizers is that the initial learning rate must be defined manually. One other problem is the decaying learning rate which becomes infinitesimally small at some point. Due to this, a certain number of iterations later, the model can no longer learn new knowledge.
