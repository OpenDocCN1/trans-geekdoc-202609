+   Active Matter: Active matter systems are composed of self-propelled entities, such as swimming bacteria or artificial microswimmers. The collective behavior of active matter can lead to phenomena like flocking, swarming, and active turbulence.

Research on complex soft matter systems is essential for advancing our understanding of fundamental physics, developing new materials, and creating innovative technologies. As our understanding of these systems grows, they have the potential to lead to significant advancements in fields such as nanotechnology, drug delivery, renewable energy, and biomaterials.
