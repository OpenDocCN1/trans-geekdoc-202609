# 3.2.3. Effects of parameters used in biogenesis

Particle size, shape, cluster/aggregation factors and own stability are inherited properties of nanoparticles. Particle size has been identified as a factor controlling the toxicity of biogenic AgNPs. The effects include cell uptake, ROS production and damage to DNA, mitochondria and cell membrane (Vasek et al., 2014; Albanese et al., 2012). However, there are no standard techniques for size measurements of AgNPs in biological media. The hydrodynamic size measured by light scattering (DLS) may be affected by aggregation of nanoparticles and interaction with the test media, particularly.
