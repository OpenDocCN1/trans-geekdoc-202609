# 4: The big picture

This chapter will give you some hands-on experience and a high-level view of images and containers. The goal is to prepare you for more detail in the upcoming chapters.

We’ll break this chapter into two parts:

+   - The Ops perspective
- The Dev perspective

The ops perspective focuses more on starting, stopping, deleting containers, and executing commands inside them.

The dev perspective focuses more on the application side of things and runs through application source code, building it into a container image, and running it as a container.

I recommend you read both sections and follow the examples, as this will give you the dev and ops perspectives. DevOps anyone?
