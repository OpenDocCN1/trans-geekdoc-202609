# The bad old days

Applications are the powerhouse of every modern business. When applications break, businesses break.

Most applications run on servers, and in the past, we were limited to running one application per server. As a result, the story went something like this:

Every time a business needed a new application, it had to buy a new server. Unfortunately, we weren't very good at modeling the performance requirements of new applications, and the IT departments had to guess. This often resulted in businesses buying very expensive servers with a lot more performance capability than the apps needed. After all, nobody wanted overpowered servers incapable of handling the app, resulting in unhappy customers and lost revenue. As a result, companies ended up with racks and racks of overpowered servers
