About the front cover

I love this book's cover, and I'm grateful to the hundreds of people who voted for the design.

The YAML code on the left represents the book's technical nature. The Docker whale represents the main topic. The vertical symbols on the right are container-related icons in the style of digital rain from the Matrix movies. There's also a hidden message written in Klingon.

Get involved with the community

There's a vibrant container community full of helpful people. Get involved with Docker groups and chats on the internet, and look up your local Docker or cloud-native meetup (search for “Docker meetup near me”).
