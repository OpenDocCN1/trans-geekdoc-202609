
This is not a hard-and-fast rule—it's just a method I use to gauge my price. I may alter the rate based on things like:

The point is, the preceding calculation can help get you close to figuring out your rate, but it might not be exact. At least it gives you some guidance on how to start charging for your services.

## Step 1: Learn to Identify Social Engineering Attacks
