My team and I have used this technique many times to verify data we have found and to uncover new pieces of intel. I find this form of vishing very effective because the target is not given much time to decide whether to help. Also, most companies have not properly educated their population on this vector. That combination creates a big risk for companies.

### Vishing for Full Compromise

It's possible to perform a full compromise using only vishing. The principles remain the same, and with the right pretext and supporting evidence, a professional SE can easily obtain even the most sensitive of details.
