A security policy helps all employees understand the assets and principles the organization values. With a clear policy, your staff is more likely to respect the organization's assets. Remember, staff will take policies only as seriously as the organization takes them.

A **functional policy** declares an organization's management direction for security in such
specific functional areas as email, remote access, and Internet surfing. The departments re-
sponsible for these functional policies write them. For example, human resources, information
technology, operations, and facilities would each produce their own specific functional
policies. A functional policy should use strong language, such as *will* and *must*. Most people
