# Master of Business Administration

The master of business administration degree (MBA) focuses on managing the process of securing information systems. Whereas MS programs prepare students to perform information security work, MBA programs prepare students to manage and maintain the people and environment of information security. A person who holds an MBA degree in information security fields will be prepared to manage information security or IT groups. The skills necessary to manage any technical environment are different from the skills necessary to perform technical work.
