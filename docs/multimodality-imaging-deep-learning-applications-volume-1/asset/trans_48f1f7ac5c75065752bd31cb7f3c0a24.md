
Molinari `et al` \[[23](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib23)\] 提出了无监督模糊 K 均值聚类（FKMC）的概念 \[[101](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib101)\]，用于将 CCA 图像分为三部分：斑块区域以及 LI 和 MA 边界。FKMC 也称为 CULEXia。FKMC 是无监督的，因为像素没有真实标签。FKMC 算法与 K‑means 算法相似 \[[102](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib102)\]，即初始化 K 个随机点作为 K 个聚类中心，然后根据最近均值将数据集中的所有点分配到相应的 K 个簇中。每个簇的所有点的均值用 `c[j]` 表示，以 inline‑graphic 形式出现，并伴随对应的链接 `bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9ieqn56.gif`。每个点 `a[i]` 也以 inline‑graphic 形式出现，链接为 `bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9ieqn57.gif`，其成员函数 `b[i**j]` 则以 `bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9ieqn58.gif` 链接表示，用于决定它在每个簇 `c[j]` 中的隶属度：

```math
b_{\\mathit{ij}} = \\frac{1}{\\left( {\\sum\\limits\_{k = 1}^{K}\\frac{\\parallel {a_{i} - c_{j}} \\parallel}{\\parallel {a_{i} - c_{k}} \\parallel}} \\right)^{\\frac{2}{m - 1}}}\\quad 0 \\leqslant b_{\\mathit{ij}} \\leqslant 1,
```  

(9.7)graphic style="width:38.8125em;" xlink:href="bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9eqn60.gif" position="float"/

其中 `m` 是控制模糊性的超参数，以 `bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9ieqn61.gif` 链接引用。

ROI 通过在每幅 CCA 图像上沿 MA 墙进行描绘得到，MA 墙是从图像底部开始，在每一列中找到最亮的局部最大值。图 [9.5](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9fig5)(i) 展示了 ROI 的上下限，分别为 MA 墙上下 1.25 mm 和 0.625 mm。随后，在 ROI 的每一列中，对三类像素——管腔、LI 墙和 MA 墙——进行进一步研究。利用 FKMC 方法，这些列中的像素会自动分配到相应的簇中。个别墙的轮廓通过每一列的 LI 与 MA  centroid 序列标记，如图 [9.5](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9fig5) (ii) 所示。对 200 幅超声 CCA 图像，该方法被重复使用。该方法的 cIMT 错误为 0.054 ± 0.035 mm。文中简要提到的机器学习模型仅涉及 cIMT 测量，未考虑斑块面积，直到 Elisa `et al` 的 DL 工作（2018） \[[103](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib103)\]，以及 Biswas `et al` 的同时测量 cIMT 与 PA 的工作（2020） \[[104](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib104)\]。下一节将快速概述 DL 在 cIMT 与 PA 测量中的应用。

### 9.4 Deep learning application for cIMT and PA extraction

CNNs \[[50](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib50)\], DBNs \[[106](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib106)\], autoencoders \[[107](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib107)\], residual neural networks \[[108](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib108)\], and other DL approaches are employed for image classification and segmentation \[[82](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib82), [105](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib105)\]. The fully convolutional network (FCN) \[[109](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib109)\] is a CNN variant that excludes the fully connected layers and is used solely for segmentation. Three distinct state‑of‑the‑art (SOT) methods work on ultrasound CCA images for cIMT measurement. Before applying ANNs to delineate LI and MA boundaries, Rosa `et al` \[[110](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib110)\] used autoencoders for feature extraction. Suri and his colleagues employed an FCN for cIMT and PA measurement on both whole \[[111](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib111)\] and patch \[[104](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib104)\] CCA ultrasound images, whereas Del Mar `et al` \[[112](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib112)\] used an FCN for full CCA images to estimate cIMT. The following subsections detail these works for plaque burden measurement on CCA images.

#### 9.4.1 ANN autoencoder based cIMT region segmentation

Rosa `et al` \[[110](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib110)\] employed artificial neural networks to characterize LI and MA pixels in 55 ultrasonography CCA images. To extract features from the LI‑MA interface, the authors used trained autoencoders \[[107](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib107)\]. Autoencoders are neural networks programmed to replicate the input. They are commonly used for unsupervised learning to uncover complex relationships in images. Given an input  ∈ \[0,1\]^(*d**x*)inline-graphic style="height:1.3125em;" xlink:href="bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9ieqn63.gif"/, using one or more hidden layers, the autoencoder converts it to a compressed representation **Y** ∈ \[0,1\]^(*d**y**)inline-graphic style="height:1.3125em;" xlink:href="bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9ieqn64.gif"/, with the mapping function similar to equation [9.3](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9eqn3). Finally, the compressed image is resized back to its original dimensions. This is done to understand the basic structures and relationships of the input vector and to enable training for regeneration. The characteristics are represented by a compressed representation of neighboring pixels. Figure [9.6](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9fig6)(i) shows a representation of the autoencoder. The ROI extraction method is similar to that used by \[[92](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9bib92)\]. The authors used five ground‑truth images to train two autoencoders. The two autoencoders were trained on the neighborhood pixels of the LI and MA interfaces. Two ANNs for pixel characterization were trained using the LI and MA characteristics. Finally, the LI and MA boundaries were extracted from the offline CCA images, and superfluous LI and MA pixels were removed via post‑processing. The classification’s cIMT bias was 0.0499 ± 0.0498 mm. The model is shown in figure [9.6](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9fig6) (ii) while the outcomes are shown in figure [9.6](#Chapter_9.xhtml#bk978-0-7503-2244-7ch9fig6)(iii).
