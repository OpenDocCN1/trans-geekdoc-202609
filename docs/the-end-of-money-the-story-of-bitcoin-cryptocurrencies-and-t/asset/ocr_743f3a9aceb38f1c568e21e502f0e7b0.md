# Scammers and malware

As well as the high-profile troubles that faced bitcoin, involving the FBI and other federal investigators, there were a slew of minor scams and hacks. Perhaps the very first cryptocurrency Ponzi scheme occurred in 2011, known by the name of Bitcoin Savings & Trust. Like many such scams, it promised returns on investment just too good to be true. After taking in 500,000 bitcoins, the site disappeared in 2012. A group of hackers and others from the bitcoin community discovered the operator’s true name and location later that year. That man, Trendon Shavers, was arrested in 2013, and pleaded guilty to securities fraud in 2015.
