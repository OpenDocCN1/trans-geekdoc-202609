# The exchange

Among the many people who discovered bitcoin was Jed McCaleb, co-creator of the peer-to-peer file-sharing program, eDonkey. Using an old domain name from an abandoned project related to the Magic: The Gathering card game, he started a bitcoin exchange called Mt. Gox. There, anyone could buy and sell bitcoins for real US dollars, in a price that fluctuated according to market demand. With the added publicity from Slashdot, Mt. Gox took off as the first real cryptocurrency exchange.
