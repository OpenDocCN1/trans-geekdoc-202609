# How much electricity do miners use?

The energy consumption of mining cryptocurrencies like bitcoin is not small. Extrapolating from one measured rate of energy consumption for the first ASIC-mining machine released in 2013, the global hashrate for bitcoin at that time would have consumed over 482,969 mega-watt-hours per year. That is the equivalent of over 44,000 average US homes.
