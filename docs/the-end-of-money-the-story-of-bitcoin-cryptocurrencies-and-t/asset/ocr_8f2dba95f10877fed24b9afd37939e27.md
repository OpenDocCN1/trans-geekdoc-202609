After finding a number of other synchronicities, including a Homeland Security report about fake IDs being shipped to Ulbricht's house, the FBI began surveillance. They watched as Ulbricht logged on to his computer, and DPR suddenly appeared on Silk Road. This happened time after time. Soon, they had enough evidence for a warrant and in a surprise sting, they cornered Ulbricht in a San Francisco public library, managing to seize his computer intact as he was logged in to the administrator accounts of Silk Road.
