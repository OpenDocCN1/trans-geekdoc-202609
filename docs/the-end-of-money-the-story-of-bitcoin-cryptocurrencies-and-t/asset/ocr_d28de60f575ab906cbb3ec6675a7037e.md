Rather than competing for the tiny chance of winning a whole block reward, they prefer to pool computer power and combine their hash with others. When the pool wins a block the reward is split between members according to their contributed hashrates. This has the effect of averaging out the rewards, turning the profit into a small but steady income. And the larger the pool, the more regular the payout, even if it is just fractions of a coin each day.

# Do mining pools have a downside?

It turns out that the rise of pools creates a new risk. Larger pools increase the possibility that one of them may someday acquire 51 per cent of the hashrate, making the blockchain vulnerable to attack.Already,a few heavyweight bitcoin mining pools control the vast majority of the global hashrate.
