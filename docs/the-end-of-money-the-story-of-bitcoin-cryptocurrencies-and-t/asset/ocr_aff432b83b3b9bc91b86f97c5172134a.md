# # IS BITCOIN REALLY MONEY?

# **6th century BCE**
First known coins are minted in the Iron Age Mediterranean kingdoms of Lydia and Ionia.

# **318 BCE**
Jixia Academy in China theorizes sovereign monetary policy.

# **12th century CE**
Rents and labour begin to be denominated in currency in the European Low Countries.

# **755 CE**
Emperor Charlemagne's Carolingian Reform standardizes the equivalence of pounds, shillings, and pence for most of Europe.

# **1718**
Scottish economist John Law introduces the world's first flat currency, in France.

# **1819**
UK formally adopts the gold standard.

# **1913**
The Federal Reserve Act creates the current Central Bank of the US.

# **1879**
The US enacts a gold standard.

# **1946–71**
During the Bretton Woods system, many world countries settle debts in US dollars, and the US government redeems dollars for gold only to other central banks.

# **1971**
The US stops exchanging currency for gold, and the world enters the purely fiat currency era.

# **2008**
Bitcoin, the first decentralized, 'post-fiat' cryptocurrency, is created.
