BTCC Pool had 11.9 per cent, BW had 9.2 per cent and BitFury's official pool had 8.1 per cent. Together, these top five pools control more than 60 per cent of bitcoin mining (see Figure 5.1). Yet there is a clear incentive to prevent any single pool having majority control. Should this occur, it is highly likely that confidence in the cryptocurrency would be eroded, given the technical possibility of that pool altering the blockchain. As these pools rely on bitcoin for profit, any crisis of confidence in the currency would likely destroy their business. As a result, miners often switch between pools, in a concerted attempt to prevent any one pool dominating.
