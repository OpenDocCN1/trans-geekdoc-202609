# 16.6.1 Arduino Uno

The Arduino Uno is a commercially free microcontroller board architecture by Arduino.cc and based on the Microchip ATmega328P microprocessor. The board is well equipped and constructed with digital portable pins and analogue input/output (I/O) pins that may be connected to a variety of extended boards (shields) and other circuits. The board features 14 digital I/O pins (six of which are capable of PWM output), 6 analog I/O pins, and programmable through a type B cable using the Arduino IDE (Integrated Development Environment). It can be powered by a USB cable or an external 9-volt battery, with voltages ranging from 7 to 20 volts. It is similar to the Arduino Nano and Leonardo micro. The hardware reference design is available on the Arduino website under a Creative Commons Attribution Share-Alike 2.5 licence. Some versions of the hardware have layout and manufacturing files available as well.
