# QA SYSTEM USING DL 209

QA in regional languages but the QA system should be available in the mother tongue for major portions of citizens who can communicate in their tongue only.

In this work, we have explored research-based solutions to build the QA system in one such low resource language Bengali. Bengali literature is considered to be one of the richest in the world. Collection of novels, dramas, from the ancient time to modern age written by the renowned authors are used as the repository. The same was digitized by the TDIL (Technology Development of Indian Languages) project by the IT ministry of Government of India. Broadly the repository has eighty-six categories of domains covering almost 11,300 A4 pages.

The training set is formed with the sentences from this repository and the system is trained with the training set. Then, test set—a set of questions, is formed and tested with the system for determining category among eighty-six domains. Some examples of test set-are

+   Q1. ‘Ananda Math Kar Lekha’ or ‘Who is the author of the Anandamath?’
Q2. বঙ্ক বন্দ্যচন্দ্রকে হাওড়ায় বদলি করা হয়?
obe Bankimchandra Howrahte Badli Kora Hoy?’ or ‘When Bankimchandra was transferred to Howrah’
Q3. সররাজ রাজত্রের পুত্র কে ছিলেন?
‘Sagar Rajar Putra Ke Chhilen?’ or ‘Who was the grandson of king Sagar?’
Q4. স্যারের রিচার্ড হ্যালি কোন দেশের খেলোয়াড়?
‘Sir Richard Hadley played for which country?’
Q5. রঞ্জ্নাটা নিষেধরণের সেরা উপায় কি?
‘Rokpalta Nibaroner restho Upay Ki?’ or ‘What is the best way to avoid anemia?’
Q6. বাংলা তোমার আলোর আলো..—গানের সুরকার কে?
‘Bajlo Tomar Alor Baru.’—Ganer Surkar Ke?’ or ‘Who is the composer of the song Bajajlo tomar Alor Baru.’

Classification techniques are used as supervised method to determine the class of the question and reduce the search domain. Then, Word2Vec and WordNet similarity are used to retrieve the answer of the question. There are some work in Bengali, but scope of them are very much limited. Some have only proposed the evaluation framework and some have only tried to classify the questions.
