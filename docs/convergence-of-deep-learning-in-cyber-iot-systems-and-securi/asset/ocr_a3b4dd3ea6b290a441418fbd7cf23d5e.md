# Abstract

Today's world is connected with the Internet. In our daily life, we are also connected with many things that are connected through the Internet, and that is the way that “Internet of Things” evolves. In every aspect of life, we have applications of IoT, smart home, smart city, smart transport, and many more. Healthcare is also required to be smart to reach maximum patients in minimum time. In this chapter, we focus on a chronic unbearable disease of the oral cavity characterized by infection and progressive fibrosis. This disease may cause an inability to open the mouth, but it is absolutely curable. The faster the disease is diagnosed the rate of curability is high. Our aim is to develop a model with the help of loht device for the detection of oral submous fibrosis in the early stage.

**Keywords:** Internet of Healthcare Things, noninvasive, oral submous fibrosis, ThinkSpeak, MQTT, ESP8266
