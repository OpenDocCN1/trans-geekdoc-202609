### 14.6 Conclusion and Future Work

In this section, authors have provided few of the screenshots of the UI interfaces of the proposed application named "Patron for Women" can reduce risk of women safety. Using a safety system, women can get quick support through safety application anytime they are in danger. In this paper, authors have focused on providing security to it by providing services based on location, SMS messages and GPS service. This application can be the easier and faster way to provide the necessary safety and security to any individual in just few clicks which make social threats and prove to be a useful tool for women safety and Empowerment.

In future, the authors will launch a new role, i.e., caregiver role in the portal which will be a voluntary service. These caregivers can be the frontline besides the police. Once the user enables the SOS button, the emergency message is sent to the nearby police stations and caregivers located in same area so that victims can be located and helped on right time. Authors will also add a toll-free number for users in future so they can register themselves in our portal with their contact. After registering, in case of emergency situation dialing or messaging the toll-free number will lead to tracking of every incident. Our team along with caregivers will collect the report from the police and submit a final report with the guardian. To provide better security and convenience, voice control and other accessibility features will be added in application.
