Figure 7.16 depicts user temperature vs cool fan speed graph. In the graph, user temperature is in the x-axis and cool fan speed is in the y-axis. Graph shows a decrease in temperature with an increase in cool fan speed. At 45 rpm, the temperature becomes almost saturated and decreases by very small amount, that is at very low temperature cool fan speed should be very fast to make the room cool.
