# 8.2.2 Review of Face Recognition for Masked Faces

For the last 2 years, many researchers have been working with masked faces. Like in Ge et [26], they presented a LLE-CNN-based FR technique. In 2020, Walid Hariri proposed a deep CNN model to recognize the masked faces [27]. At first, they have removed the masked face region as shown in Figure 8.1 and then CNN has been applied on the unmasked region (cropped face as in Figure 8.1) to extract the important features as shown in Figure 8.2.

Here, VGG-16 face CNN descriptor has been used for feature extraction. At the last step multilayer perceptron method has been applied for the classification. They have worked with real-world masked FR dataset (MFRFD) [28] and provide the maximum recognition rate of 91.3%.
