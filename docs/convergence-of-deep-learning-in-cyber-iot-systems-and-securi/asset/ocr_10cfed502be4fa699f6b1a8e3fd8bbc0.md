# 19.3.2 CPS Models

There are three primary types of CPS models:

Timed Actor CPS: It concentrates on aspects that are the nonfunctional of performance and timing, as well as the functional aspects of behavior and correctness. The filtration based on the “earlier-the-better” approach is the main focus since it allows for the identification of deterministic abstractions that belong to nondeterministic systems. These models are less likely to be in the hands of state explosion issues, making analytical bounds easier to derive [8].

Event-Based CPS: Before actuation decisions can be made in a model, an event must be identified by the appropriate CPS components. Individual component-time restrictions, on the other hand, fluctuate taking into consideration the system of system delay depending on the nondeterministic system induced by various CPS functions, such as actuating, communicating, and computing [9].
