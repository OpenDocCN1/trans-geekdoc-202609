### Block Diagram (Online Assessment System)
The online examination system acts as a master for the entire working of the system. It stores all the details about the students and the faculty members. It authenticates the students and the faculty members, each time they log onto the system. It receives the chapter inputs from the faculty member and the test type and subject from the students and generates necessary question and answers for those generated questions. The test is now open for the students to attempt. Once student completes the test and submits it. The answers given by the students are extracted by the system and evaluates them by comparing with those answers that were generated by the system and using similarity measures. # Actor Specification

Student
Description: This actor plays the role of a student who can register into the website and select the type of test he or she wishes to attempt. The actor then attempts the test and after completing the test, the test is submitted so that results can be evaluated.

Faculty Member
Description: This actor plays the role of a faculty member who can register onto the website and provide those chapters for which the system must generate questions and answers. This actor could also view the grade of the students.

Online Examination System
Description: This acts as the master of the website which provides certain services. It maintains the details of the students and the faculty members who have registered to the website. This system takes the chapters provided by the faculty and generate objective and subjective questions and its corresponding answers.

# 5.5 System Implementation

The five basic stages of natural language processing, shown in Figure 5.8, are

- Stage I: Lexical Analysis—this first stage of natural language processing aims in analyzing the words once after identifying them. Lexicon is a terminology that refers to words or phrases of a language under consideration. Lexical analysis process of splitting input into words.
- Stage II: Syntactic Analysis—the second stage of natural language processing focuses on analysis of words. Later, the relationship between the words is analyzed and transformed into structural format.
