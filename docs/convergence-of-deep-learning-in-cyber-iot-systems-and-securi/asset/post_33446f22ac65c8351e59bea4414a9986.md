## 9.5 Deduction of Biological Protein Structure

Joynt and Wu [16] have emphasized that the affinity between viral RNA load kinetics and disease severity among COVID-19–infected persons crumbled. In their article, they have expressed their interest to know whether viral RNA load in lung tissue or a surrogate sample like tracheal aspirate, mirrors the downfall in nasopharyngeal. With their inspection, they concluded the severe manifestations as immunologically mediated and agreed on the evident implications of the use of immunomodulatory therapies for these patients. Knowledge in protein structures benefits the diagnosis and treatment of underlying disease. But the existing procedure to know protein structure may take months or more. Thus Scientists are looking for a trustworthy computational procedure to know the protein structure from amino acid sequence. DL methods are also playing a promising role in this journey. Senior et al. [17] have elaborated their vast genomic data-fed neural-based AlphaFold for predicting protein structure. They have claimed their work significant progress in accuracy 3D models that AlphaFold generates. The predictions of protein structure aid understanding the roles of these protein structures within the body and thus it is believed to help in treating. The proposed model has also been employed in multiple COVID-19 protein. The proposed model has accurately predicted protein structures in scenario where experimentally determined structures exist. The proposed AlphaFold is still under development and focusing on predicting protein structures for scenario where similar structures exist.

## 9.6 Pandemic Modeling and Recommended Drugs

Now, the top priority for global health is a trustworthy blueprint to minimize the severity of corona pandemic. Ferguson et al. [19], the authors have concluded through their investigation that border restriction or internal restriction delays the spread of corona pandemic at most 2 to 3 weeks, unless it is more than 99% effective. The study [19] strengthens the case isolation or household quarantine strategy by concluding the fact that case isolation or home quarantine is much fruitful without school closure during the peak. Further, it is also noticed by authors that school closure during the peak declines the attack rate at a max of 40%, whereas its impact on the overall attack is negligible. Findings that immediate treatment with antiviral can reduce the symptoms and transmission. It is expected that a combination of home quarantine, reactive school closure, and medication for 50% population could reduce clinical attack rates by 50%. It is also stated that even vaccines with minimum efficiency can also decline the attack if applied in sufficient quantity.

Halloran et al. [20] have used three different models to intersect layered containment on social distancing, rapid case establishment, and targeted prevention. Generally, these preventive measures are very useful to reduce the transmission of pandemic circumstances. It is also true that timely intervention declines the ultimate number of pandemic diseases. Ferguson et al. [21] have shown that the use of social distancing and antiviral protection make possible the confinement and eradication of incipient pandemic. The work also concludes that even the incipient virus has near pandemic transmissibility then simply preventive measures are not sufficient, multiple approaches are required for the eradication and confinement of the virus. Further, it is also noted that geographically targeted policies are very much for obtaining higher degree containment along area quarantine. Social targeted measures are adequate when the transmissibility unfolds incrementally.

Ferguson et al. [22], in their hypothesis, propose two fundamental policies—mitigation and suppression (Figure 9.5) to alleviate the pandemic situation. Proposed mitigation slows down the pandemic aiding peak need to reverse the pandemic widening and decreasing case numbers to a low level. The major challenges of both policies have also been noted and what necessitates the layer of interventions even if suppression or mitigation is the overarching policy goal. Proposed suppression needed more intensive and socially disruptive measures in comparison to mitigation (Figure 9.6). Lastly, the authors agree that the feasibility and effectiveness in the social context of a country ultimately determine the choice of interventions.

## 9.7 Selection of Medicine

As of now, there is no fully systematic and effective antiviral plan/system after the economic and societal impact of the COVID-19 pandemic. To prevent this infection the necessary process is to find the right drugs for the betterment of world public health.

In absence of proven treatment for COVID-19, Zhavoronkov et al. [23] have used their proprietary generative chemistry pipeline based on the knowledge of the crystal structure and homology model of the target protein to assert the novel drug-like compound.

After the failed attempt at SARS-CoV-2 infected patient's treatment with approved drugs like lopinavir/ritonavir, Tang et al. [24] generated new chemical entities against this virus. It is also noticed by the authors that a key in the life-cycle of coronavirus is the most attractive for antiviral drug design. A novel advanced deep Q-learning network with the fragment-based drug design (ADQN-FBDD) based on a newly solved structure (PDB ID: 6LU7), is developed in the work for generating potential lead compounds targeting SARS-CoV-2.
