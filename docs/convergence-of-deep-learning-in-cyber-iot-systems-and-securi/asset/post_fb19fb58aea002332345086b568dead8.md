## Abstract

Bioweapons, nowadays, become potential for agricultural sector. It is observed that every insect have distinct properties to differentiate from others by some parameters, as releasing substances (chemically but may be other), humming noise (intensity, pitch, frequency), absorbing ability (range of different wavelength), average number in gang, velocity range, maximum/average distance covered. The proposed uses narrowband spectrum for absorbing range.
