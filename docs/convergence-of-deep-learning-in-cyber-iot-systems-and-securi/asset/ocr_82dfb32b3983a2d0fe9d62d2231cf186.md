# 5.4 Proposed System Design

Block Diagram (Student)
Once the students get registered to the system, they could now access the resources available in the system. After logging onto the system, the students are enabled to choose the type of test and the subject for which they are going to attempt the test. After selecting the test type and the subject, they are now allowed to answer the questions generated by the system for the type chosen by them. After completing the test, the students can submit their answers and can log out of the system as shown in Figure 5.4.
