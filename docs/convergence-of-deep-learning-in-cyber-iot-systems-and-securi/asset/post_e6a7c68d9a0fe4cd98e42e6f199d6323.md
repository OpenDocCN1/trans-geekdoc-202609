# Detection of Fake News and Rumors in Social Media Using Machine Learning With Semantic Attributes

Diganta Saha, Arijit Das, Tanmay Chandra, Soumyadip Saha and Ratul Das
Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Faculty of Engineering and Technology, Jadavpur University, Kolkata, West Bengal, India

## Abstract

Spreading rumors and fake news over popular social media like Facebook, Twitter, etc creates social problems. It is observed that the administration had to stop the spreading of such rumors or fake news in a large area creates another serious problem for citizens as we are heavily dependent on internet. To detect fake news, this work suggests machine learning using the Naive Bayes classifier. The system can be plugged in any social media, as it predicts the probability of news to be fake or rumor can be stopped from spreading further. This model was trained and tested in three datasets (two of English language and one Bengali language). We successfully achieved high accuracy, which is 81% for the English language and 93% for the Bengali language. This is an acceptable result comparing with recent works. Using deep learning or combination of classifiers, the result may be improved further.

**Keywords:** Fake news, machine learning, Naive Bayes, rumors, semantic attributes, NLP, natural language processing

## 4.1 Introduction

What is fake news? "fake news" is a term that can, in a broader sense classify news which are either false or clickbait or hate news. It is a kind of news consisting of disinformation and can spread via traditional newsprint media or digital media. With the advent of social networking sites, common people have become more susceptible to this kind of news and knowingly or unknowingly have become an agent to spread this news. Fake news is avoidable as it can refer to a diverse range of disinformation covering topics such as health, environment and economics across all platforms and genres. However, the term is new. Fake news started to roll out since 2016, and it was not named in Collins dictionary as official word of the year for 2017. Clickbait, misleading headlines, sloppy journalism, biased news targeted toward a person or a community are the causes of creating fake news around people.

Nowadays, social media is playing an important role in spreading news whether it is real or fake and people are ready to imbibe it. News in social media uses interesting text, images and videos which attract the readers. It is easy to access and circulated quickly. News through social media has more popularity than the headlines of the newspaper. News reports made with purposely false information are easy to spread. It is considered harmful for the news ecosystem and changes people's mind towards real news. In a nutshell, fake news and rumours create a negative effect on individuals as well as in society. To alleviate the effect of this, the detection of fake news or rumours now becomes an alluring topic to research.

For the current research, three datasets have been used. Two of them were in England one dataset called Liar has small statements and the other dataset called fake news corpus has headlines as well as body parts. The Bengali dataset is called BanFakeNews has headlines and body parts too.

Supervised Machine Learning methods have been used on the datasets. The objective of this work is to recognize fake or false news by considering different attributes of the news corpus in the classification process.

This work is one of the first works on Fake News Detection in the Bengali Language. As this work achieved an accuracy of 93%, we can say it's a very good first step towards the future.
