Srivastava developed an architecture for Hindi QA system using PurposeNet ontology as the data resource. Bagde and her group [21] compared the performance of different similarity functions like ‘Jaro-Wrinkler,’ ‘Euclidean similarity measure,’ ‘Jaccard coefficient similarity,’ ‘N-gram approach,’ and ‘text similarity’ in case of Hindi QA system. Shubot and the group [22] developed a system to answer mathematical questions expressed in Hindi or English. Questions in natural language were processed and converted to the mathematical formula using the knowledge base of Wikidata. The values of the constants were also extracted from Wikidata. Singla and the group [23] compared the performance of different similarity functions in the bilingual QA system in Hindi and English. Baghat, Prajapati, and Seth [24] developed an IVR based automatic Hindi QA system in the domain named SRHR (sexual and reproductive health and rights). Taking the Jaccard Similarity as baseline the authors BERT model in the store to generate QA pair and return as the response of Frequently Answers (FAQ). Bhattacharya and his group [25] developed multi domain multilingual QA system in Hindi and English. Hindi question is translated into English question, and CNN and RNN were applied to classify the questions. Candidate answers were extracted from the retrieved passages which were retrieved by Lucene’s text retrieval functionality and ranked using Boolean and BM25 space models. Finally, the prospective answers are ranked using different similarity functions. A good amount of accuracy is achieved in question classification. Gupta and his group [26] proposed a deep neural network-based model for generalized multilingual QA system and tested the for Hindi-English. The model has layers sentence encoding, probable answer sentence encoding, snippet encoding, and answer extraction layer. 39.44% exact match and 44.97 F1 score are observed for standard collected dataset 50.11% exact match and 53.77 F1 score are observed in the translated SQuAD dataset. Chandra et al. [27] crowd sourced questions and answers for multilingual code-mixed languages. This is 1694 Hinglish (Hindi + English) question answer pairs. Though directly not related, Chandra and Dwivedi [28] presented a good way of query expansion using Okapi BM25 ranking for Hindi query in English document retrieval. Lewis et al. [29] presented a multilingual QA system for languages which includes only Hindi as Indian language. They presented benchmark dataset and benchmark results applying ML and neural networking models for the evaluation of performance in multilingual forms in those seven languages.
