#  16.8 Conclusion

Here, we introduced a new technology that reduces the possibility of attack of locust gang to paddy field. Here our methodology track the path of locust gang through the sensing intensity of locusts gang and compare the total edge of paddy field of agriculture region. There is a map of all mixed connected paddy field, which compare with the previous traversed field of locust gang and predict the upcoming tracking path of the new coming locust gang. Based on these predicting path our system create a fencing zone or boundaries and recognize the branching point or junction where we install the sensing radar technology to divert the locust. At junction point, the radar propagates the signal by sensing high intensity of stimulus of locust and that disturbs the locust and to divert their way and break. This kind of device is very significant for the country, which fully depends on agriculture and cultivation like India. Here the tracking path of locust gang evaluate by combination of ant colony optimization, grasshopper optimization. Determination of area of mixed connected paddy field done by combination of Soled and Canby edge detection techniques, sensing of intensity of locust gang predicting the new path of them done by Bee-colony Optimization and radar technology done by Internet of Things (IoT) techniques. With a great ease of this technology, we can get high impact of societal effect in farmers' life.
