We have tried and tested many classification techniques like Logistic Regression, K-Nearest Neighbors, Naive Bayes and many more. Naive Bayes classifier stands out in the list in terms of accuracy and simplicity of the approach.

Mathematical Explanation of Naive Bayes Classifier:
We consider a given news report fake or not in the basis of calculating the conditional probability of report that contains some certain word in it.

The formula is stated in equation 4.1.

```
P(F|W) = \frac{P(W|F).P(F)}{P(W|F).P(F) + P(W|T).P(T)} (4.1)
```

where :
P(F|W): conditional probability of a report is fake given that the word W occurs in the report
