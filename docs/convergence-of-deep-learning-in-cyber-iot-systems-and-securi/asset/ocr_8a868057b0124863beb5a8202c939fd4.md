In parallel with RT-PCR test, radiographic features of CT images are also exhibiting promising sensitivity in COVID-19 detection. It is observed that the focus of infection near the pleura is most likely to be diagnostic as COVID-19-positive. To capture this propitious phenomenon, in Butt et al. [5], relative distance from the edges has been considered as extra weight to diverge the appearance and structures of different infections.
