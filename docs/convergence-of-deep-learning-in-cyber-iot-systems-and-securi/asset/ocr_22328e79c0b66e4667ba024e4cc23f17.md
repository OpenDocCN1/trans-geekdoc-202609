# 18.6 Conclusion

This chapter elaborates the study of phishing URL detection. As pandemic situation prevails throughout the globe, cyberattacks have seen an exponential increase in the financial sector and have led the Internet users becoming targets of cyber threats. The foremost features that differentiate phishing and legitimate are considered and deep learning-based approach for predicting phishing URL is presented.
