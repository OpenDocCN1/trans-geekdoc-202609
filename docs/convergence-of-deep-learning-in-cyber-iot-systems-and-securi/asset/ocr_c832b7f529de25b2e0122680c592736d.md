Block Diagram (Online Assessment System)
The online examination system acts as a master for the entire working of the system. It stores all the details about the students and the faculty members. It authenticates the students and the faculty members, each time they log onto the system. It receives the chapter inputs from the faculty member and the test type and subject from the students and generates necessary question and answers for those generated questions. The test is now open for the students to attempt. Once student completes the test and submits it. The answers given by the students are extracted by the system and evaluates them by comparing with those answers that were generated by the system and using similarity measures to compute the score. These grades are then stored in a sheet where the faculty could view the results of the students as shown in Figure 5.6.
