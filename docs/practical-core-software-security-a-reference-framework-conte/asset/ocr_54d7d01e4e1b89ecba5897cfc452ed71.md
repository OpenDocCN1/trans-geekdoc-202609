# 9.4.8 Section Summary

You saw two approaches to developing, collecting, and assessing metrics to help determine an overall maturity level of your secure development implementation efforts and programs. Although both models should lead you to improved and measurable processes, selecting the one to use must be determined by your own organization's structure, its internal development processes, and your own good judgment. Although we won't recommend one approach over the other, you should be able to see the overlaps between them and use the one that best fits your purposes.
