**Using rectified linear units:** The network we've developed at
this point is actually a variant of one of the networks used in the
seminal 1998 paper* introducing the MNIST problem, a network
known as LeNet-5. It's a good foundation for further
experimentation, and for building up understanding and intuition.
In particular, there are many ways we can vary the network in an
attempt to improve our results.
