# Colophon

The animal on the cover of Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn and TensorFlow is the fire salamander (Salamandra salamandra), an amphibian across most of Europe. Its black, glossy skin features large yellow spots on the head and back, signaling the presence of alkaloid toxins. This is a possible source of this amphibian's concern: contact with these (which can spray short distances) causes convulsions and hyperventilation. Either the painful poisons or moisture of the salamander's skin (or both) led to a misguided belief that these creatures could only survive being placed in fire but could extinguish it as well.

Fire salamanders live in shaded forests, hiding in moist crevices and under logs the pools or other freshwater bodies that facilitate their breeding. They spend most of their life on land, give birth to their young in water. They subsist mostly on a diet of insects, slugs, snails, and worms. Fire salamanders grow up to a foot in length, and in captivity, they may live as long as 50 years.

The fire salamander's numbers have been reduced by destruction of their forest habitat and the greatest threat is the susceptibility of their moisture-permeable skin to pollutants and microbes. Since 2014, they have become extinct in parts of the Netherlands and Belgium due to an introduced fungus.
