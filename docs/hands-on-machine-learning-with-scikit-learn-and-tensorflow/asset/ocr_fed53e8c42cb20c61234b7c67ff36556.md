If you try to change the classes or the random seed, you will see that the improvement drops, or even vanishes or returns. What I did called, you should be suspicious: perhaps the flashy "torturing the data until it confesses". When a paper looks too positive, you new technique does not help much (in fact, it may degrade performance), the authors tried many variants and reported only the best results (which may be due to shear luck), without mentioning how many failures they encountered on the way. Most of the time, this is not malicious at all, but it is part of the reason why some results in science can never be reproduced.
