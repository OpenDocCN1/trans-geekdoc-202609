# Evaluating frontier models

We are living in an era where frontier LLMs are being developed at a rapid race, with frequent model releases and steady increase in benchmark performance. However, this raises a key challenge: how should we meaningfully evaluate progress, especially as benchmarks can be saturated over time. Moreover, recent studies highlight limitations and potential flaws in popular benchmarks [28]. Our work in Chapter 5 explores one aspect of this question, where we propose a framework to evaluate and understand model differences. Going forward, how do we capture these differences in a scalable way remains a question, given that reasoning models have much longer outputs and agentic systems can have long trajectories. Ultimately, the research community needs to find fair and reliable methods to evaluate these advanced models, which is important for guiding the future development of frontier models.
