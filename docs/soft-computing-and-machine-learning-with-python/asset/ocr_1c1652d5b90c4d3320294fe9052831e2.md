Ibrahim et al. [22] suggested a system in which Artificial neural network is used for forecasting the defeverence day of fever in patients of dengue disease. Only clinical signs and symptoms are used by the proposed system for detection. The data are gathered from 252 hospitalized patients, in which 4 patients are having DF (Dengue fever) and 248 patients are having DHF (dengue hemorrhagic fever). MATLAB’s neural network toolbox is used. Algorithm of Multilayer feed-forward neural network (MFNN) is used in this experiment. Day of defeverence of fever is accurately predicted by MFNN in DF and DF with 90% correctness.
