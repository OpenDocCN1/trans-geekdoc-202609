What do we make of this diagram? For starters, it is called a decision tree (DT). Yes, it is upside down: it grows from the top down. I do apologize—academics are nothing if not counterintuitive. But just think of the hanging planter at grandma and grandpa’s house with the plant growing downwards over the sides. I guess Decision Shrubs didn’t catch on as a name.

The single question at the top, Cloudy?, is called the root of the tree. The scattered nodes at the bottom are called the leaves. At each leaf, we have some—possibly mixed—set of outcomes. Imagine that Belle recorded 30 cases of playing, and 5 cases of not playing, when the recorded data indicated sunny, the temperature between 60 and 75, no rain, and no wind. I’m told that those conditions are nearly ideal for playing tennis. On the flip side, weather of Cloudy, Temp < 60, Precip, Windy had 0 cases of playing and 20 cases of not playing.
