Notice that as we lower our threshold—move PredictionBar to the right—there is a flow of predictions from the right of the confusion matrix to the left. We predict more things as positive as we lower the threshold. You can think of examples as spilling over the prediction bar. That happens in the order given to the examples by the probas.
