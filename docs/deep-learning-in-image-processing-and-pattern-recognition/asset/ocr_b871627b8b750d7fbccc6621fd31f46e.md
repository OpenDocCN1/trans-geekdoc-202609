Meanwhile, the distributions of $\Delta\alpha$, $a_{0}$, $\Delta f$, and $\Delta h(q)$ for the five states are shown in Figures 11–14. From the figure, it can be seen that $\Delta f$ and $\Delta h(q)$ have subtle crossover in different states, while $\Delta\alpha$ and $a_{0}$ were mixed severely, so $\Delta f$ and $\Delta h(q)$ were selected to form the eigenvectors in this paper.
