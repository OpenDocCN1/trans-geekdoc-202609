# About the Editors

# Yuji Iwahori

Yuji Iwahori (Member, IEEE) received his B.S. degree from Nagoya Institute of Technology, in 1983, and his M.S. and Ph.D. degrees from the Department of Electrical and Electronics, Tokyo Institute of Technology, in 1985 and 1988, respectively. In 1988, he joined the Educational Centre for Information Processing, Nagoya Institute of Technology, as a Research Associate, where he became a Professor at the Centre for Information and Media Studies, in 2002. From 1991 to 2024, he was a Visiting Researcher at Computer Science Department, UBC. From 2010 to 2024, he held a position as a Research Collaborator with IIT Guwahati and at the Department of Computer Engineering, Chulalongkorn University from 2014 to 2024. In 2004, he joined Chubu University as a Professor. He acted as the Department Head of Computer Science, the Head of the Graduate Computer Science Course, and the Vice-Dean of the College of Engineering. He became an Honorary Faculty Member of IIT Guwahati in 2020. His research interests include computer vision, biomedical image processing, deep learning, and the application of artificial intelligence. He was a recipient of the KES 2008 Best Paper Award and the KES 2013 Best Paper Award from KES International. Unfortunately, he passed away on 24th December, 2024.

# Aili Wang

Aili Wang (Member, IEEE) received her B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in information and signal processing from Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, China, in 2002, 2004, and 2008, respectively. She has been a Visiting Professor in the research of 3D polyp reconstruction with the Computer Science Laboratory, Chubu University, Kasugai, Japan, in 2014. She is the author of two books and more than 160 articles. Her research interests include deep learning-based space intelligent remote sensing and image processing.

# Haibin Wu

Haibin Wu received his B.S. and M.S. degrees from the Harbin Institute of Technology, Harbin, China, in 2000 and 2002, respectively, and his Ph.D. degree in Measuring and Testing Technologies and Instruments from Harbin University of Science and Technology, Harbin, in 2008. From 2014 to 2015, he was a Visiting Scholar with the Robot Perception and Action Laboratory, University of South Florida, Tampa, FL, USA. He is the author of three books, more than 120 articles, and the creator of more than 20 inventions. His research interests include robotic vision, visual measuring and image processing, medical virtual reality, and photoelectric testing.
