## 6. Conclusions

This paper proposed an adaptive logarithmic normalization transformation, AdaLogT, for TM algorithms in order to compensate for the defects caused by the preprocessing. Based on the analysis of classical preprocessing methods, the parameter *p* was introduced in order to obtain the appropriate log-normalized curve. The optimal parameter *p* was calculated by proposed objective functions. Considering the TM algorithms based on luminance or gradient domain, the objective functions based on luminance and gradient-domain features were constructed, respectively. Furthermore, a joint domain-aware objective function was presented for DNN-based TM algorithms. The proposed preprocessing algorithm ensures that the image luminance conforms to the HVS perception of the scene brightness. State-of-the-art luminance, gradient-domain, and DNN-based algorithms were selected for the experiments, which used different preprocessing methods. The experiments were conducted by combining subjective qualitative and objective quantitative. The results show that the proposed algorithm achieves the best subjective quantitative scores with TMQI quality scores, which indicates that the method improves the subjective effect and objective quality of the images. Further work includes the improvement of the optimization method for optimal parameter *p* and the utilization of folded concave functions for more general tone mapping pre-processing.
