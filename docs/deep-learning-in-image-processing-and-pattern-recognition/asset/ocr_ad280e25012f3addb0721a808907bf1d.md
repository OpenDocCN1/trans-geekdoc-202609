In order to accurately study the technology of doctors' head angle of view and orientation tracking, we establish triangular models for the rotation of the head around different coordinate axes to determine the head and angle of view posture.

(1) Head posture positioning relative to the X-axis (pitch)

The pitch angle is the solution corresponding to the rotation of the doctor's head about the X-axis. When the doctor raises his head or nods, the pitch angle changes to $\alpha$, and for the frontal image in the triangle model, the side length $a$ of the feature triangle changes, but the corresponding height $h$ does not change. Based on prior knowledge of the distribution of facial organs, the positional relationship between the nose and the eyes can be obtained as follows:

```
$$h' = 0.8a \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad (10)$$
```

where $h'$ is the change in the height of the triangle when the doctor's head changes, and $a$ is the distance between the eyes after turning the head.

```
$$a = \arccos\frac{2\sqrt{(p-a)(p-b)(p-c)}}{0.8a^2} \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad (11)$$
```

When the pitch angle of the doctor's head changes, the angle between the two triangles corresponding to the change will also change. When the doctor raises his head, the corresponding angle $a$ is a negative number; When the doctor lowers his head, the corresponding angle $a$ is positive.

(2) Head posture positioning relative to the Y-axis (yaw)

The solution of the yaw angle corresponds to the rotation of the doctor's head around the Y-axis. When the doctor's head turns to the left or right, the pitch angle changes marked as $\Psi$, and the shape of the feature triangle changes.

If $y_2 > y_3$, the head is deflected to the left, and the deflection angle $\Psi$ is expressed as follows:

```
$$\Psi = \arccos(\frac{y_2 - y_1}{c}) - \frac{1}{2} \angle C \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad (12)$$
```

If $y_2 < y_3$, the head is deflected to the right, and the deflection angle $\Psi$ is expressed as follows:

```
$$\Psi = \arccos(\frac{y_3 - y_1}{c}) - \frac{1}{2} \angle C \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad (13)$$
```

When the doctor's head is tilted to the left, the corresponding angle $\Psi$ is positive; When the doctor's head is tilted to the right, the corresponding angle $\Psi$ is negative.

(3) Head posture positioning relative to the Z-axis (roll)

When the doctor turns his head left or right, the change of the roll angle is set to $\varphi$ as follows:

```
$$\varphi = \arccos\frac{a}{a'} \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad (14)$$
```

where $a$ is the distance between the pupils after turning the head, and $a'$ is the distance between the pupils in the forward direction. During head turning analysis, the side length $a$ of the feature triangle changes, but the corresponding height does not change.

```
$$a' = \frac{h}{0.8} \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad (15)$$
```

The solution for obtaining the final roll angle is obtained as follows:

```
$$\varphi = \arccos\frac{0.8a}{h} \qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad\qquad (16)$$
```
