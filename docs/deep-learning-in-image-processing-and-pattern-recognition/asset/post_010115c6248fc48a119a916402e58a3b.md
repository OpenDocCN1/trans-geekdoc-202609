The simulation experimental results show that the recognition rate of the proposed KADPC algorithm in this paper is higher than that of the comparison algorithm under different SNR. Because KADPC algorithm denoises the constellation and uses two clustering algorithms to extract the features of the constellation, which makes the constellation features more concentrated and sufficient, so it presents a better recognition effect as a result. Especially for BPSK, QPSK and 8PSK signals, the effect is ideal, and the applicability is good. In general, the KADPC algorithm model proposed in this chapter has a good effect on digital signal modulation recognition under different SNR conditions, especially for low SNR.

## 4. Conclusions

Aiming at the low recognition accuracy of traditional modulation recognition methods under the condition of low SNR, a modulation recognition algorithm model based on digital signal constellation features is proposed. Firstly, the signal is transformed into constellation, and the density clustering algorithm is used for noise reduction. After the features extracted by KADPC algorithm are input into multi-level SVM classifier, the modulation recognition results are obtained. This method reduces the complexity of feature extraction and the cost of classification. Compared with other five modulation recognition algorithm, the overall recognition performance of five common types of digital modulation signals is improved by about 5%. Under the condition of SNR = −2 dB, the correct recognition rate of modulation mode is improved by 8–10% on average. However, with the increase of modulation order, the difficulty of feature extraction of this algorithm increases, resulting in the slow improvement of recognition rate in high-order modulation. How to reduce the complexity of feature extraction of high-order digital signal modulation mode is a research focus in the future.
