# 3.1. DGOVGG16 Model

For the selection of convolutional neural network model, the recognition accuracy and the training convergence efficiency of the model must be comprehensively considered. Generally speaking, the deeper the network model is, and the larger the scale is, the higher the accuracy of image recognition will be. However, the more the corresponding model parameters are, the lower the recognition efficiency will be. Especially when the data set is insufficient, not only the risk of over fitting exists, but also the difficulty of model training will increase difficulty in convergence and other problems.
