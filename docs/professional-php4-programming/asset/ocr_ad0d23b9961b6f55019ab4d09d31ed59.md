# # p2p.wrox.com

For author and peer discussion join the P2P mailing lists. Our unique system provides programmer to programmer™ contact on mailing lists, forums, and newsgroups, all in addition to our one-to-one e-mail support system. Be confident that your query is being examined by the many Wrox authors and other industry experts who are present on our mailing lists. At p2p.wrox.com you will find a number of different lists that will help you, not only while you read this book, but also as you develop your own applications.

To subscribe to a mailing list just follow these steps:

+   - [ ] Go to http://p2p.wrox.com/
+   - [ ] Choose the appropriate category from the left menu bar
+   - [ ] Click on the mailing list you wish to join
+   - [ ] Follow the instructions to subscribe and fill in your e-mail address and password
+   - [ ] Reply to the confirmation e-mail you receive
+   - [ ] Use the subscription manager to join more lists and set your e-mail preferences
