## 6.2 Revolutionizing Textile Manufacturing: Harnessing IoT for Innovation and Efficiency

The textile industry stands as a colossal economic powerhouse globally, driving substantial economic growth and employment opportunities across nations. Its significance is multifaceted, serving as a linchpin in the global supply chain and contributing significantly to GDP [14]. Comprising various stages from raw material sourcing to fabric production, dyeing, finishing, and distribution, the industry boasts diverse functions and subbranches [16]. Textiles play a pivotal role not only in the fashion and apparel sector, but also in technical textiles, home furnishings, and industrial applications, underlining its pervasive importance across multiple sectors and daily life [17]. The textile industry encounters multifaceted challenges that impede its operational efficiency and growth potential. Market dynamics pose a significant hurdle, as fluctuating trends and consumer preferences constantly reshape demand, making it challenging for textile businesses to anticipate and meet market needs effectively. Additionally, the difficulty in trackability within the industry hampers efficient inventory management and supply chain operations, leading to delays and inefficiencies.

Moreover, the lack of rapid prototyping and sampling capabilities restrains the industry from swiftly adapting to changing trends and fulfilling customer demands for innovative designs [18]. The absence of seamless wireless communication between physical objects and devices further integration impedes real-time monitoring and data exchange, hindering the industry’s transition toward smart manufacturing and IoT [8].
