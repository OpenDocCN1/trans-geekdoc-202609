# ## 4.4.2 Issues with Smart City Technology

+   1. The expense of planning and maintenance is the prime difficulties to the actual deployment of smart towns. There exist many useful options for applications at low system costs.
+   2. More resources should thus be put together at the application level to realize smart cities objectives. However, platform incompatibilities brought on by heterogeneity obstruct integration and interoperability at the application level.
+   3. Information and infrastructure protection is a key aspect for smart capitals that make big data processing and analysis technique in need of upgrading.
+   4. Smart cities must also contend with waste management as a significant issue because of environmental pollution and landfilling. Any project aimed at making a city “smarter” must address failure management as well. Besides, natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes, or hurricanes could preclude system functions due to physical damages and a lack of network availability.
