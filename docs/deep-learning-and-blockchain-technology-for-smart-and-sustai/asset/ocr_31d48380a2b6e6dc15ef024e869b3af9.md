# 10.4.4 Anthracite

Anthracite is considered as the best coal and it is a dark or black variety. It has a high carbon content of around 95%, is hard, and has little moisture. Anthracite can go up to extremely high temperatures when burned. Furthermore, the earliest variety of coal is anthracite, which originated from buried biomass 350 million years ago. Because of its long-lasting interment nature, it possesses the highest energy density of 33 MJ/kg [8]. Anthracite is outstanding at rapidly heating up and burning tremendously hot since it releases such a large amount of energy when burned. It burns completely and releases less smoke than other types of coal. Hence, this type of coal is particularly used for space heating. Anthracite is suitable for use in home heating stoves since it burns cleaner than wood and for a longer period of time.
