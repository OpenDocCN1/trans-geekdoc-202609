### 1.4 Waste Management

In smart cities, waste that cannot be reused or recycled is collected from other waste, and a thin layer of waste is dumped over soil, followed by another thin layer of waste. Finally, the area is declared as unfit for constructing any buildings for next 20 years. This process is called as landfill.

Incineration is a process that will burn garbage and turn into gas and ashes. Releasing gas is not eco-friendly because it may contain carbon dioxide or carbon monoxide. However, it will reduce the amount of garbage in a larger size.

Tin containers may also be compressed so that they occupy less amount of space. This can also be recycled.

Organic waste can also be used as nutrient-rich manure. Food items which we throw daily can be left decayed after burial under a thin layer of compost, which is the best alternate solution for chemical fertilizers.
