+   - **Intelligent Traffic Management Systems (ITMS)**: These systems make use of cameras and sensors to keep an eye on traffic patterns, adjust traffic signals, and give residents up-to-date travel information.
+   - **City Surveillance Systems**: The use of CCTV cameras and video analytics helps to monitor traffic infractions, promote public safety and security, and discourage crime.
+   - **Smart Networks**: These networks maximize energy distribution, minimize losses, and enhance energy efficiency through the use of sensors and automation.
+   - **E-governance Platforms**: To increase transparency, expedite procedures, and give individuals access to government services, online portals and mobile apps are being utilized.
+   - **Public Wi-Fi**: To encourage digital inclusion and economic activity, several smart cities are providing free or reasonably priced Wi-Fi connectivity in public areas.
