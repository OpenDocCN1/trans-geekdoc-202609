In the paper by Treiblmaier et al. (2020), the authors delved into the potential implications of blockchain technology on smart cities, examining its underlying technologies and protocols. They pinpointed nine crucial sectors where blockchain technology could pay a significant role in advancing urban development, encompassing healthcare, logistics, transportation, energy, governmental processes, electronic voting, industrial operations, household management, and education. The authors analyzed ongoing developments in these sectors highlighting the impact of blockchain and suggesting directions for future investigation.

The research conducted by Salha et al. (2019) sought to investigate the possible role of blockchain technology within smart cities. Their study involved an extensive review of scholarly literature concerning blockchain, focusing on its fundamental concepts and practical applications. Furthermore, it synthesized global instance of successful blockchain implementation while also delving into the associated challenges and opportunities within the context of smart urban environments.

The study conducted by Barella Migliorini et al. (2021) sought to illustrate how smart cities could offer an alternative pathway toward achieving comprehensive sustainability across political, economic, social, and environmental realms. Through extensive surveys of smart city rankings and indexes, the research pinpointed cities excelling in various dimensions. It was found that blockchain technology played a crucial role in driving significant progress toward establishing self-regulating and sustainable smart cities. An analysis of blockchain's applicability, carried out in consultation with market experts well-versed in its implementation, revealed a predominant focus on the cognitive aspect, with comparatively less attention directed toward the operational infrastructure of cities.

The paper by Gade1 and Aitha (2020) explored the integration of blockchain technology into smart cities, highlighting its potential impact on the development of such urban areas. They addressed the need for reliable and secure online operational processes in various applications within smart cities and proposed a secure framework leveraging blockchain technology. Through thorough research and the extensive literature review, they identified multiple applications and process areas that stand to benefit significantly from this technology, enhancing their intelligence, reliability, and suitability for implementation across smart cities.
