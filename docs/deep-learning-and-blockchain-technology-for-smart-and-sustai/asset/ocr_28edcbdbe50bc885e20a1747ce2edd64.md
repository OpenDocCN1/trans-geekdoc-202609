The difficulty of dynamically orchestrating service function chains (SFCs) in edge intelligence-powered IIoT systems, where quick response times and adaptability are critical, is examined by Huang et al. [7]. In order to overcome this difficulty, it suggests using Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL), which takes delay restrictions into account and strives to maximize resource allocation and service placement choices while guaranteeing prompt service delivery. This strategy makes use of the intelligent edge to make IIoT services effective and flexible. A novel “Deep Progressive Neural Network” (DPNN) approach was developed in the article by Sharma et al. [8] to solve security concerns in the ever-expanding IIoT ecosystem. This novel algorithm efficiently detects and categorizes different risks within an IIoT network by utilizing the advantages of both progressive learning and deep learning. Through transfer learning, the DPNN effectively integrates new attack types while avoiding the loss of previously acquired knowledge. This leads to an extremely precise system that achieves 94% accuracy in threat detection against a baseline of 69.7%, demonstrating its potential to greatly improve IIoT security.

Smart factories are undergoing a revolution thanks to 5G networks and the IIoT, but security and privacy remain major obstacles. These problems are investigated at the physical, data, and application layers by Lin et al. [9]. It showcases cutting-edge techniques including edge computing for quicker reaction times, blockchain for safe data management, and deep learning for anomaly detection. Although these developments seem promising, there are still issues to be resolved, such as scarce resources, a lack of AI that protects privacy, and intricate multi-stakeholder settings. The article’s conclusion emphasizes the necessity of continued investigation and cooperation in order to provide private and secure 5G-IIoT smart factories. In order to ensure secure transaction management in IIoT applications, Alahmari et al. [10] proposed a novel approach. The Streebog cryptographic hash function is recommended by the authors as a means of augmenting security when used in conjunction with the Davis Mayer blockchain infrastructure. To further streamline the suggested method for effectively managing and controlling network resources, they provide Software-Defined Networking (SDN) as a crucial component. The goal of integrating these technologies is to improve the overall security of industrial systems by addressing the difficulties associated with safe transaction processing in IIoT contexts.
