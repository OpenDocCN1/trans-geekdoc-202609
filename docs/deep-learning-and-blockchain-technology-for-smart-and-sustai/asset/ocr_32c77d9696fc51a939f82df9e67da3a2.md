# 12.5.6 Environmental and Energy Initiatives

In the context of sustainability, blockchain technology can be integrated into smart cities to monitor and incentivize environmentally friendly practices. Blockchain-based systems can trace the origin and impact of energy consumption, track emissions, and facilitate transparent carbon trading. Additionally, decentralized energy grids powered by blockchain can enable more efficient energy distribution, reducing waste and promoting the use of renewable energy sources. The integration of blockchain in environmental and energy initiatives contributes to the overall resilience and sustainability of smart cities.

# 12.6 smartVAST Framework

# 12.6.1 Introduction to smartVAST

In response to the evolving landscape of smart city vulnerabilities and the integration of blockchain technology, the smartVAST framework is introduced as a strategic tool for assessing, addressing, and fortifying the security of smart city ecosystems. smartVAST stands for Vulnerability, Attacks, Security, and Threats, encapsulating a comprehensive approach to mitigating risks in the context of blockchain integration. This framework is designed to provide a structured methodology for smart city stakeholders, including urban planners, cybersecurity professionals, and policymakers, to analyze and enhance the security posture of their cities.
