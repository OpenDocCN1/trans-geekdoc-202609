Dr. N. Thillaiaarasu is currently working as an associate professor at the School of Computing and Information Technology, REVA University, Bangalore. He has 12 years of teaching experience. He has also served as an assistant professor at Galgotias University, Greater Noida, from July 2019 to December 2020. He worked for 7 years as an assistant professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, SNS College of Engineering, Coimbatore. He obtained his B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering from Selvam College of Technology in 2010 and received his M.E. in Software Engineering from Anna University Regional Center, Coimbatore, in 2012. He received his Ph.D. degree from Anna University, Chennai, in 2019. His areas of interest include Cloud Computing, Security, IoT, and Machine Learning.

Dr. Naresh Kshetri is a faculty member, Department of Cybersecurity, Rochester Institute of Technology, Rochester, New York, USA. He was an assistant professor of Cybersecurity and Adjunct Instructor of Computer Science at College of Science, Technology, and Health, Lindenwood University. Dr. Kshetri has completed Ph.D. (Computer Science, with concentration in Cybersecurity) from the University of Missouri – St. Louis, MS (Cybersecurity) from the Webster University, and MCA from the University of Allahabad.
