# Step 1: Baseline Assessment:

The objective of this step is to conduct a comprehensive baseline assessment of the current smart city infrastructure to establish a foundation for subsequent analysis. The following activities should be done in this step:

+   – Identify and document existing smart city components, including IoT devices, data management systems, and critical infrastructure.

+   – Evaluate the current cybersecurity measures in place and assess their effectiveness.

+   – Document the flow of data and interactions among different components.

# 1.1: Smart City Inventory

+   – Create an inventory of all smart city components, including IoT devices, sensors, and critical infrastructure

+   – Document the interconnected systems and dependencies within the smart city ecosystem.

# 1.2: Stakeholder Mapping

+   – Identify and map key stakeholders, including city officials, technology providers, and cybersecurity experts.

+   – Establish communication channels and collaboration frameworks for effective information exchange.

# 1.3: Data Collection and Analysis

+   – Collect and analyze data related to the current state of smart city infrastructure.

+   – Evaluate existing cybersecurity measures and identify potential gaps.

# Step 2: Vulnerability Analysis:

The objective of this step is to perform a detailed vulnerability analysis to identify potential weakness in the smart city infrastructure. The following are the activities done in this step:

+   – Conduct vulnerability scans and assessments on key components, focusing on potential entry points for cyberattacks.

+   – Identify and categorize vulnerabilities based on severity and potential impact.

+   – Prioritize vulnerabilities based on risk assessment, considering the interconnected nature of smart city systems.
