These technologies offer a range of features that enhance security, improve response times, and enable proactive measures to ensure a safe and reliable transportation environment for students. Live tracking systems empowered by IoT enable real-time monitoring of school buses or vehicles transporting students [15]. This functionality allows educational institutions and parents to track the precise location and movement of buses, ensuring greater accountability and security throughout the journey. Simultaneously, SMS alerts keep parents informed about bus arrival times, delays, or any unforeseen changes, enhancing communication and reducing concerns related to student transportation. Moreover, bus attendance management systems powered by IoT streamline the process of student boarding and disembarking [29]. These systems use sensors or RFID technology to automatically register when students board or exit the bus, ensuring accurate attendance records. This not only facilitates efficient tracking of students but also provides data that can be vital in emergency situations, aiding school authorities in ensuring everyone’s safety [1]. IoT-based vehicle management solutions offer advanced functionalities like driver analysis and preventative maintenance. Driver analysis systems monitor driver behavior, tracking parameters such as speed, braking, and adherence to safety guidelines. This information helps in evaluating driving patterns, ensuring adherence to traffic rules, and ultimately enhancing student safety during transit. Additionally, proactive maintenance schedules based on IoT-generated data ensure that vehicles undergo timely inspections and repairs, reducing the risk of breakdowns or malfunctions during transportation. The integration of IoT in transportation management within educational institutions marks a significant step toward ensuring student safety, enhancing communication with parents, and streamlining logistical operations [10].
