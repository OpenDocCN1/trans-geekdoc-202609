Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that enables secure and transparent recording of transactions across a network of computers. Each transaction, or block, is cryptographically linked to the previous one, forming a chain of blocks that is immutable and tamper-proof. Blockchain operates on a decentralized network, eliminating the need for intermediaries and providing trust and transparency in peer-to-peer transactions.
