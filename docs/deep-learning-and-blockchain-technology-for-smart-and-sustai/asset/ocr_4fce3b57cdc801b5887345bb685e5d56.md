Furthermore, multiple investigations have demonstrated that DL algorithms can identify the fundamental causes of seasonal changes, regional geographical links, chemical processes, and other factors that can alter the amounts of air pollutants.

The accuracy of predicting pollution concentrations has significantly increased as a result of the gradual discovery of DL technology’s benefits in handling nonlinear spatiotemporal correlations. A hierarchical model is often used in DL techniques to map input to output. The modelling concept involves assembling certain blocks or layers to create a deep network structure that spans from end to end. When interpreting historical atmospheric pollution data with spatiotemporal correlations, DL models work pretty well (Kristiani et al., 2022).

The literature review makes clear that not much research has been done on AQI prediction across different datasets. The AQI is now exceedingly difficult to anticipate and monitor, especially for metropolitan regions where there is increased transportation and industrial activity. This work first uses samples collected from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) to forecast the AQI in metropolitan areas. Following that, a hybrid model is being developed. The performance of the developed model is evaluated using the R2 score, MAE, and RMSE approaches. Finally, the performance of the proposed integrated model is compared with some recent existing models.

The main objective of the proposed work is to develop a hybrid DL model to predict AQI levels ranging from 0 to 500. Various familiar models like LSTM, RNN, GRU, Stacked LSTM, LSTM+GRU, LSTM+RNN, and RNN+GRU have been engaged for prediction. Among the different models, LSTM+GRU beats in terms of accuracy and outperforms in terms of computation time.

The proposed work consists of several modules from data collection, imputation, outlier detection and correction, data transformation, feature engineering, AQI calculation, data splitting, application of models, validation of models, and finalizing the best model based on performance metrics.
