Criminals in the cyberspace are creating various types of online scam that can be broadcasted as email, message, Ads, etc., to steal private data of the users. As online web services and digital information are increasing, new types of cyberattacks are also increasing day by day. There are different types of phishing attacks [1] as follows: Email phishing, Malware phishing [2], Phishing SMS, Spear phishing [3], Website phishing, Audio or Voice phishing, Spoofing, etc. These types of attacks need to be detected at the server side itself in order to reduce time, improve productivity, reduce transmission load, and reduce malware attacks. Among all types of attacks, email plays a vital role through which phishing attacks are carried out by the cybercriminals. Group of spam emails are created by the spammers and transmitted to the legitimate users. These spam emails carry malicious content such as spam URL [4], Malware source code, and Ads. Spammers may also include some eye-catching words in the spam email to attract the users. One of the main problems addressed due to spam email is stealing personal data. Developing awareness and improving knowledge of the users may prevent such types of cyberattacks. But, it is really difficult to do for all types of users practically. Hence, spam emails must be detected and filtered out by using advanced techniques.

According to the statistics, 96% of phishing attacks are carried out through email, 3% of attacks through malicious website, and the remaining 1% through voice call. In 2020, one email in 4,200 emails was identified as a spam email that is found by Symantec. In 2021, Tessian research reported that every year on an average 14 phishing or spam emails are received by an employee. But in industries, the number of phishing or spam emails received by the workers is increased. Every year 7.5% of email-based cyberattacks are increased and that is found by the ESET.

Also, the research of cofense evinced that the phishing or spam emails have 38% URLs to malicious websites and 36% malicious attachments. In 2021, PDF files are identified as mainly the common type of malicious attachment by the Tessian research. The number of malicious PDFs sent through email is increased in 2021, which is proposed in SonicWall's Cyber Threat report.
