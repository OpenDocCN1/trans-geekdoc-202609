## 7.4 Global Impact of Smart City

Globally, smart cities are transforming urban environments with the goal of raising citizen quality of life, sustainability, and efficiency. Their effects are widespread, and notable changes have already been observed in a few important areas:

### Economic Repercussions
- 1. **Global Smart City Market**: It is expected to reach $3.45 trillion by 2026.
- 2. Employment Creation: By 2025, there might be 160 million new smart city jobs globally, according to estimates.
- 3. **GDP Growth**: By enhancing resource management and stimulating the economy, smart cities can increase GDP by as much as 3.4%.

### Environmental Impact
- 1. **Decreased Energy Usage**: Smart grids can reduce building energy use by up to 20%.
- 2. **Reduced Greenhouse Gas Emissions**: By using sustainable energy and mobility, smart city projects can help meet climate targets.
- 3. **Better Quality of Air and Water**: Intelligent monitoring and control systems can lower pollution levels in urban areas.

### Social Impact
- 1. **Enhanced Citizen Engagement**: Transparency and involvement in decision-making can be increased with the help of open data platforms and citizen apps.
- 2. **Greater Equity in Service Accessibility**: Intelligent technologies have the potential to close the digital gap and improve access to vital services like healthcare and education.
- 3. **Enhanced Public Safety**: Intelligent monitoring and emergency response systems can lower crime rates and improve security.

## 7.5 National Impact of Smart City

India’s Smart City Mission was introduced in 2015 with the goal of converting 100 cities into thriving metropolitan areas with better sustainable practices, infrastructure, and quality of life for their citizens. Although the program is still in its early phases, it has started to have good effects in a number of areas:

### Economic Impact
- 1. **Investments**: As of July 2023, the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) reported that over 2.05 lakh crore (US$26 billion) had been invested in smart city projects.
- 2. One million new jobs are expected to be produced in smart cities by 2025.
- 3. **Enhanced GDP**: By 2030, it is anticipated to contribute 1% of India’s GDP (Smart Cities Mission Report, 2020).

### Environmental Impact
- 1. **Lower Energy Consumption**: The installation of LED street lighting in 100 cities resulted in an annual electricity savings of 75 million kWh.
- 2. **Better Air Quality**: According to MoHUA (2023), smart traffic management solutions in 42 cities resulted in a 6% decrease in air pollution.
- 3. **Enhanced Water Efficiency**: According to MoHUA (2023), smart water meters in 45 cities reduced water losses by 15%.

### Social Impact
- 1. **Better City Services**: According to 51% of residents in smart cities, public transportation is easier to access (Smart Cities Mission Survey, 2022).
- 2. **Improved Healthcare**: Over 8.5 million patients benefited from telemedicine projects in 83 cities (MoHUA, 2023).
- 3. **E-Governance Adoption**: According to the Smart Cities Mission Survey (2022), 62% of residents in smart cities used the internet to obtain government services.

## 7.6 Conclusion

India’s smart cities are presenting a positive image of urban change, with programs having a real effect on the lives of the people. Through the use of smart traffic technologies, the effort is cutting air pollution by 6% and is expected to generate one million new employment by 2025, among other advantages for the environment, society, and economy. The dedication to advancement is demonstrated by investments exceeding 2.05 lakh crore and projects like LED street lighting, which saves 75 million kWh of electricity yearly. Although issues like inclusion and financial sustainability still exist, India’s smart cities can reach their full potential by studying international best practices, concentrating on basic infrastructure, and actively involving the public. Imagine a time where seamless e-governance, data-driven healthcare, and individualized citizen services are standard operating procedures, resulting in lively, welcoming cities that enhance the lives of millions of people. Although we are still in the early phases of this fascinating adventure, India can become smarter and more sustainable.
