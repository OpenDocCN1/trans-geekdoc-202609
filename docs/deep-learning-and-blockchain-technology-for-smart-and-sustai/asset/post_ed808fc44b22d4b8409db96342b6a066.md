## 15.5 Conclusion and Future Work

The work proposes earthquake prediction for smart cities employing a novel composite LSTM model that demonstrates a significant reduction in time complexity. This innovative approach enhances computational efficiency without compromising accuracy, paving the way for real-time earthquake forecasting systems in smart cities, crucial for proactive disaster management. The investigation into the comparative efficacy of the composite LSTM model vs the standard LSTM model for earthquake prediction yields compelling findings. The composite LSTM model not only achieves a commendable reduction in computational complexity but also exhibits superior performance across critical prediction accuracy metrics, including MAE, RMSE, MSE, and R² score. The observed decrease in computational complexity is of substantial practical significance, promising increased efficiency in resource utilization, particularly in scenarios involving extensive datasets or real-time prediction requirements. Concurrently, the heightened accuracy demonstrated by the composite LSTM, as evidenced by its outperformance in key metrics, underscores its proficiency in capturing and predicting seismic events with greater precision than the standard LSTM model. The training and testing times are 311 and 10 seconds for LSTM and 63 and 6 seconds for composite LSTM. Research results show that using composite LSTM reduces computational complexity. The amalgamation of computational efficiency and heightened predictive accuracy positions the composite LSTM model as a favorable and pragmatic choice for earthquake prediction endeavors. This model holds considerable potential for application in real-world scenarios, offering the prospect of advancing earthquake monitoring systems and contributing to the development of more reliable early warning mechanisms. This study accentuates the pivotal consideration of both computational efficiency and predictive performance when assessing models for the intricate task of seismic event prediction.
