Even after many years, the study of coal petrography is still expanding and modernizing its body of knowledge in order to pursue new applications for the energy sector. The extraction of essential rudiments from the coal, as well as clean coal technologies, is given special attention. In terms of its chemical makeup, the material coal is heterogeneous. The variation in composition within this substance arises from the diverse characteristics of the plant material responsible for its peat formation. Additionally, the unique qualities result from the dynamic interplay of factors such as temporal variations, temperature fluctuations, pressure conditions, and the specific circumstances influencing the organic material during its biochemical and geochemical transformations. This intricate blend of environmental factors contributes to the distinct heterogeneity observed in the final product, showcasing the complex interplay of nature’s forces in shaping the composition of this organic material over time.

Macerals are the fundamental, homogeneous building blocks of coal’s structure in both physical and chemical aspects. From a cognitive and practical standpoint, it is significant to investigate the petrographic structure of coal. For intelligent shearer mining to be realized, accurate coal seam distribution recognition is a requirement. This project offers a fresh approach to distinguishing between coal and rock using deep Convolutional Neural Networks (D-CNN) and feature extraction. Utilizing the Active Contour Method, the relevant features have been meticulously extracted, offering a comprehensive representation of the underlying data. This method employs advanced computational techniques to delineate and capture distinctive characteristics, contributing to a nuanced understanding of the intricacies embedded in the dataset. Through the application of the Active Contour Method, the extraction process becomes not only precise but also adaptive, ensuring a more robust and detailed depiction of the inherent features. This approach serves as a valuable tool in various fields, where a thorough analysis of data nuances is imperative for meaningful insights and informed decision-making. The goal of this research is to overcome the CNN [3] overfitting issue and accelerate convergence by introducing regularization techniques such as batch normalization, weight regularization, and dropout. Second, data expansion is used to enhance the image information of the coal rock, which greatly improves performance. A number of experiments are offered by the coal rock scissors cutting experiment system, which is intended to gather more authentic photographs of coal rock. The network we created performs better in detecting photos of coal rocks, according to experimental results.
