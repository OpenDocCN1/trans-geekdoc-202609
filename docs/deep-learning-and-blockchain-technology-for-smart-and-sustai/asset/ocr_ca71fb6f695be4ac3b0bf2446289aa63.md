AQI = \frac{CB_{high}-CB_{low}}{C_{high}-C_{low}}(C - C_{low}) + CB_{low} (13.5)

where AQI is the Air Quality Index, CBhigh is the breakpoint concentration to Chigh, CBlow is the breakpoint concentration to Clow, Chigh is the concentration breakpoint ≥ C, Clow is the concentration breakpoint ≤ C, and C is the recorded pollutant concentration. The final air quality level can be calculated by taking into account the pollutant's maximum AQI value. There are six different categories of air quality: Severe (401–500), Very Poor (301–400), Poor (201–300), Moderate (101–200), Satisfactory (51–100), and Good (0–50).
