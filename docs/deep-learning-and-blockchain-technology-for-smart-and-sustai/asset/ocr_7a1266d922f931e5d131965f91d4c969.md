# a. Economic Impact:

+   - **GDP Growth**: By 2030, India’s GDP could increase by 5%–10%, thanks to smart cities that have effective resource management and infrastructure (Source: World Economic Forum).
+   - **Employment Creation**: According to the Smart Cities Mission, the building, IT, and sustainability industries will create 100 million new employment by 2025.
+   - **Enhanced Industrial Productivity**: According to McKinsey & Company, IIoT integration in manufacturing can boost output by 20%–30%.

# b. Social Impact:

+   - **Enhanced Quality of Life**: By addressing problems like air pollution, traffic congestion, and waste management, smart solutions can raise people’s standard of living by 20%–30% (Source: Smart Cities Mission).
+   - **Enhanced Citizen Engagement**: According to pilot project estimates, blockchain can increase voting turnout by 15%–20% by enhancing transparency and citizen trust in governance.
+   - **Reduced Poverty**: By empowering marginalized communities through smart healthcare and education efforts, 10–15 million people may be lifted out of poverty by 2030 (Source: World Bank).

# c. Environmental Impact:

+   - **Reduced Energy Use**: According to the Smart Cities Mission, building management systems and smart grids can cut energy use by 15%–20%.
+   - **Reduced Carbon Emissions**: By 2030, sustainable waste management and transportation strategies can cut carbon emissions by 10%–15% (Source: NITI Aayog).
+   - **Improved Water Management**: According to the World Resources Institute, smart water meters and leak detection systems can cut water waste by 20%–30%.

# d. Statistical Measures:

+   - **Operational Smart Cities**: 109 cities are currently putting smart city projects into action, making them the number of operational smart cities (Source: Smart Cities Mission).
+   - **Investment in Smart City Initiatives**: ₹100,000 crore has been set aside by the government for the creation of smart cities (Source: Smart Cities Mission). Industry adoption of IIoT: By 2025, the Indian IIoT industry is anticipated to grow to $15 billion (Source: NASSCOM).
