Dr. V. Subramaniyaswamy is currently working as a professor at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Vellore Institute of Technology, Vellore, India. He received a Ph.D. degree from Anna University, in 2013, and continued his extension work with the Department of Science and Technology Support as a Young Scientist award holder. With experience of more than 18 years as an academician and a researcher, he has contributed more than 200 articles and chapters to many high-quality technology journals and books that are being edited by internationally acclaimed professors and professionals. He is a research supervisor and successfully guided six research scholars. He is also a visiting expert at various universities in India and abroad. He has received government funded and consultancy projects from DST-SERB, ICSSR-IMPRESS, DST-CSRI, MHRD, TVS MOTORS, MHI, Royal Society, UK, and SERB-MATRICS. He is on the review boards of several international journals and has been a program committee member for several international/national conferences and workshops.
