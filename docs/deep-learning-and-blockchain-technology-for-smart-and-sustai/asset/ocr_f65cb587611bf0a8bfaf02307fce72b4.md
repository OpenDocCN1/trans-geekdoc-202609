# Components of the smartVAST Framework

![](bbox=[0.18, 0.13, 0.88, 0.29])

Figure 12.2 A step-by-step diagram showing four components: vulnerability assessment, attack analysis, security enhance, and threat mitigation of smartVAST framework.

+   - **Vulnerability Assessment**: The first step involves a thorough examination of potential vulnerabilities in the smart city infrastructure. This assessment considers factors such as the types of interconnected devices, data flows, and critical infrastructure components. The goal is to identify weak points in the system that could be exploited by malicious actors.
+   - **Attack Analysis**: Building upon the identified vulnerabilities, the framework conducts an analysis of potential cyberattacks that could exploit these weaknesses. This includes studying historical attack patterns, understanding emerging threats, and assessing the potential impact of attacks on various smart city domains.
+   - **Security Enhancement**: With a clear understanding of vulnerabilities and potential attack vectors, the smartVAST framework proposes security measures and enhancements. This may involve the integration of blockchain technology to secure data transactions, implement smart contracts for automation, and strengthen overall cybersecurity measures across smart city components.
+   - **Threat Mitigation**: The final component involves developing strategies to mitigate identified threats. This may include the implementation of proactive measures, incident response plans, and continuous monitoring to detect and respond to potential security incidents in real time.
