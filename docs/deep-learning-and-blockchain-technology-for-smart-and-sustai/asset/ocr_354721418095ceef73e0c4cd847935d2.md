# Figure 1.1 Different types of waste management.

Solid waste includes food waste, paper, plastic, wood, and glass, whereas liquid waste includes sewage and industrial waste mixed with water. Solid waste can be treated by dumping, incineration, composting, manure pits, etc. Nowadays, Bio Toilets are installed in railways. Manholes are reduced in large numbers, or a smart city is making use of jetting machines if there is a clog. In a smart city, the government is giving a list of 44 safety equipment to the employees like face masks, air compressor for blowers, fluorescent-colored jackets, etc. (Figure 1.2).
