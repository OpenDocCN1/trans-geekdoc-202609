# 7.2 Related Works

In their exploration of the growing integration of artificial intelligence (AI) in urban settings and their skillful delivery of AI applications in smart cities, Luusua et al. [1] investigate how technology might improve efficiency, sustainability, and urban living in general. The topics covered include AI-powered approaches to energy optimization, traffic control, public services, and other areas fostering the growth of intelligent urban environments. The essay also discusses possible obstacles, moral dilemmas, and how the use of AI will affect how cities develop in the future. The study conducted by Lv and Gao [2] investigates how smart city efforts affect employment in China. The study stresses the total employment creation fueled by smart city development, while acknowledging the possibility of job displacement owing to automation in some areas. It emphasizes how crucial it is to upskill and adapt the workforce in order to take advantage of smart city opportunities and traverse the shifting terrain.
