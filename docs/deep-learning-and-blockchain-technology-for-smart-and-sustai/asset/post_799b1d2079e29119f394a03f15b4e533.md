## 6.5.1 IIoT-Based Bio-Devices

IoT devices play a pivotal role in transforming healthcare by offering advanced functionalities that revolutionize patient monitoring and care delivery.

- 1. Motion Detection Monitoring: These sensors detect and track patients’ movements, aiding in fall detection for elderly or immobile patients, ensuring their safety, and alerting healthcare providers in case of any anomalies [24].
- 2. GPS Smart Soles: Embedded with GPS technology, these smart insoles help track the location and movement of individuals, particularly beneficial for patients with dementia or those prone to wandering, allowing caregivers to locate them efficiently.
- 3. Wireless Fetal Monitoring: These devices enable expecting mothers to monitor fetal health remotely, providing real-time data on fetal heart rate and movement, enhancing prenatal care and enabling timely interventions if needed.
- 4. ECG Straps: Electrocardiogram (ECG) straps continuously monitor heart activity, recording and transmitting data to healthcare professionals, enabling remote cardiac monitoring and the early detection of abnormalities [24].
- 5. Glucose Level Checker: IoT-based glucose monitoring devices allow diabetic patients to measure blood glucose levels accurately and regularly, empowering them to manage their condition effectively and enabling healthcare providers to offer tailored treatment plans.
- 6. Pressure Sensors: These sensors monitor pressure variations, commonly used in healthcare for bedsore prevention by alerting caregivers or automatically adjusting bed positions to relieve pressure, enhancing patient comfort and preventing complications.

These IoT devices contribute significantly to remote patient monitoring, early diagnosis, and personalized care, ultimately improving patient outcomes and facilitating more proactive and efficient healthcare services.
