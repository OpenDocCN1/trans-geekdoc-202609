# 6.9 Conclusion

Certainly, the integration of IoT in the tourism sector has ushered in a transformative phase, revolutionizing how travel experiences are facilitated and managed. Its impact extends across various segments, from transportation and hospitality to enhancing sustainability and operational efficiency in tourism hubs. One of the pivotal applications of IoT in tourism is real-time tracking, which allows travelers to have a comprehensive view of their journey. IoT-enabled GPS tracking systems embedded in mobile apps or travel devices offer continuous updates on routes, estimated time of arrival, and potential delays. This technology enhances convenience and safety for tourists, providing them with precise location information and enabling them to plan their itineraries more effectively. Moreover, IoT contributes to speed control, a crucial factor in ensuring both safety and efficiency in transportation. By leveraging sensors and data analytics, IoT systems monitor vehicle speeds, helping transportation services maintain optimal and safe travel speeds.
