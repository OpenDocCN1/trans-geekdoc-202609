# 17.6 XGBoost

XGBoost, an abbreviation for Extreme Gradient Boosting, stands out as a potent tool in predicting PD through voice patterns of patients in smart cities. The iterative training process of this ML algorithm entails sequentially training numerous weak learners refining predictions by concentrating on instances that were previously misclassified. Its effectiveness and scalability make it well suited for analyzing expansive medical datasets frequently encountered in PD studies. Additionally, XGBoost integrates regularization methods to prevent overfitting, ensuring reliable generalization to unseen data. Through providing insights into feature importance, it assists researchers in pinpointing crucial factors influencing PD diagnosis [13]. Additionally, XGBoost provides strategies to address imbalanced data and offers a rich set of hyperparameters for fine-tuning model performance. Despite its complexity, XGBoost maintains interpretability, empowering clinicians and researchers to comprehend the factors influencing PD prediction in smart cities. Essentially, XGBoost presents a promising pathway for improving the early detection and management of PD through precise and resilient predictive modeling [14].
