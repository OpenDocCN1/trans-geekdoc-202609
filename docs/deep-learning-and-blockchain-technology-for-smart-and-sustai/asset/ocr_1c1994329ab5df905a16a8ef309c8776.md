# Figure 14.2 Classification of sensors.

The sensors are classified into in-body sensors, on-body sensors, wearable and implant sensors. In-body sensors are positioned inside the body, whereas on-body sensors are placed on the skin surface [10]. The LM35 is a type of temperature sensor that is used to measure temperature. The operating frequency is 1 Hz, which supports a low bit rate, i.e., less than 100 bps [11]. The heart rate sensor, blood pressure sensor, oximeter and blood glucose sensors are operated at the frequency of 1 Hz and a bit rate of less than 100. These are used to measure heart activities, pressure oxygen level and glucose level from the human body. The electrocardiogram signals are sampled at high frequency, i.e., 1,000 Hz with a precision of 24 bits, resulting in a dataflow of 24 kbps [12]. The motion sensors like gyroscope, accelerometers and magnetometer are operated at different sampling frequencies ranging from 10 to 2,000 Hz and bit rates ranging from 1 to 200 kbps.
