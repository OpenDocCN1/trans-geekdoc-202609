The national air quality monitoring network in our nation periodically measures these air contaminants. The outcome AQI is posted frequently on this center’s website. The main contributors to air pollution are heating, industry, traffic, and transportation pollutants (Li et al., 2021). Furthermore, the development of urbanization negatively impacts the quality of the air. There are several suggestions to protect the public from extreme pollution. Some of these include (i) limiting public outdoor activity and (ii) encouraging patients with serious illnesses like cardiovascular diseases, respiratory conditions, pregnancy, and children to spend more time at home (Almetwally et al., 2020). Anticipating air pollution is essential for both alerting people to pollution and minimizing it in their daily lives.
