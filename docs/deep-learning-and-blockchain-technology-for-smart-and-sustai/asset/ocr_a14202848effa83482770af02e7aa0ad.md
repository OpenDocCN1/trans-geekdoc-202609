# 16.2.1.3 The Main Aspects of IoT in Agriculture

# 16.2.1.3.1 Sensor Technology

Soil sensors: Measure soil moisture, temperature, and nutrient levels to optimize irrigation and fertilization. Weather stations: Provide real-time weather information to improve planning and risk management. Crop Monitoring Sensors: Monitor crop health, growth, and yield using sensors and imaging technologies. Livestock Trackers: Monitor the location, health, and behavior of livestock to improve management.

# 16.2.1.3.2 Data Collection and Connectivity

The data can be collected from sensors and devices, processed, and analyzed. Communications: Use wireless technologies such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, LoRa, and cellular networks to transmit information from the field to centralized systems.
