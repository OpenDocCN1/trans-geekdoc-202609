# Figure 14.1 Architecture of medical cyber-physical system.

A concentrator is designed for weak functionality devices, and a cloudlet is aimed to collect from high power devices also. Since the medical data is sensitive and significant, it requires careful monitoring and processing. The cloud is responsible for secure processing, storage and computation. The fourth layer is the action layer, which converts the results into action. The communication between the devices creates a three-tier architecture: Tier1—Intra BAN communication, Tier 2—inter Ban Communication and Tier 3—beyond BAN communication. The functions of these layers are data collection, transmission and computation.
