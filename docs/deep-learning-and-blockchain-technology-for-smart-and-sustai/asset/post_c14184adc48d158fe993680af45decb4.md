## Applications of Smart City

Subhashini Asokan

### 1.1 Introduction

Leading a quality life is the ultimate goal of human beings, which is possible only in smart cities. In day-to-day life, many problems such as public security, waste, traffic, and water management were available, but they were handled in smart cities and were discussed in this chapter. Life will be easy in smart city and moving towards smartness is based on economical, geographical, and social conditions of the city.¹ Smart Economy, Smart Environment, Smart Governance, Smart Mobility, Smart Living, and Smart Human Level are some of the characteristics of smart city.²

### 1.2 Literature Survey

Piuri et al.’s book AI and IoT for Smart City Applications explains the various areas where IoT and AI can be used for sustainable development in smart cities. It also explains how robots and cloud computing can be used in the healthcare domain of smart cities.³⁻⁶
Su et al.’s 2011 International Conference on Electronics, Communications and Control (ICECC) has explained the influence of smart cities in people’s lives.⁴
Curry et al.’s Journal of Internet Services and Applications explains that technologies are implemented for developing a smart city. Then, the deployments of technology will make a smart city.⁵
