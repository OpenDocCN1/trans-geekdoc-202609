# Preface

This book, entitled _Deep Learning and Blockchain Technology for Smart and Sustainable Cities_, consists of 20 chapters. This book focuses on rapid transformations in the design and operations of deep learning models along with blockchain technology concepts for the smart cities development. Many aspects of smart cities can use the technologies promoted by deep learning and blockchain, which make full use of advanced information, effective security, and enhanced infrastructure and applications.

This book provides a forum for scientists, researchers, students, and practitioners to present their latest research results, ideas, developments, and applications. This book is organized into 20 chapters, which include explanations of topics and relevant algorithms.

**Chapter 1** introduces various applications of the smart city.

**Chapter 2** discusses the fundamentals of role of machine learning for email classification in smart city infrastructure.

**Chapter 3** discusses the integration of blockchain and deep learning for smart city infrastructure over a future perspective.

**Chapter 4** discusses the usage of blockchain, IoT, and deep learning in smart city infrastructure.

**Chapter 5** explains the implementation of blockchain and deep learning in smart city applications.

**Chapter 6** includes the study of IoT and IIoT for smart cities.

**Chapter 7** discusses on the applications of smart cities

**Chapter 8** discusses rainfall for smart city and the best urban development

**Chapter 9** briefs about IIoT usage and applications in smart city.

**Chapter 10** focuses on smart city sustainability through multiple coal classification utilizing deep learning

**Chapter 11** examines elderly fall detection for smart home in smart cities.

Chapter 12 discusses the vulnerability, attacks, security, and threats to smart cities with impact and ability in blockchain technology

Chapter 13 focuses on accurate air quality prediction in smart cities

Chapter 14 specifies about The Future of Immersive Experience: Impact on Healthcare in Smart Cities with IoT.

Chapter 15 focuses on the Analysis of LSTM-based Earthquake Forecasting for Smart City Resilience: A Time Complexity Perspective

Chapter 16 focuses on smart farming in smart cities.

Chapter 17 explains the anomaly detection of Parkinson's Disease in smart cities.

Chapter 18 briefs about location and weather-based prediction in smart cities.

Chapter 19 discusses the gesture language translator in smart cities

Chapter 20 briefs about the fundamentals of role of blockchain for early monkeypox detection

This edited book is intended to report on the latest innovations in the application of technologies to smart cities, with the main pillars including blockchain, deep learning, internet of things, cloud computing, autonomous and robotic systems, big data analytics, augmented reality, cybersecurity, simulation, system integration, and additive manufacturing.
