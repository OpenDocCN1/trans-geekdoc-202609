This study investigates the prediction of seismic tremor patterns using two specific neural models: Feed-Forward Neural Network (FFNN) [16] and LSTM Recurrent Neural Network. The FFNN is a standard non-cyclic architecture organized in layers, with each neuron receiving inputs exclusively from the immediately preceding layer. In contrast, LSTM is a specialized recurrent neural architecture addressing issues such as vanishing and exploding gradients in conventional RNNs [17]. The FFNN model comprises two hidden layers with 20 and 60 nodes, trained with the “rmsprop” learning rate over 1,000 epochs. On the other hand, the LSTM features two hidden layers with 40 units each, truncated backpropagation to 15 steps, and a dropout layer for regularization. The study assesses the models’ performance in predicting seismic tremor patterns, comparing mean squared error (Mean Squared Error, MSE) and R² scores for each variable. The results demonstrate that LSTM outperforms FFNN in capturing seismic tremor data patterns more effectively.
