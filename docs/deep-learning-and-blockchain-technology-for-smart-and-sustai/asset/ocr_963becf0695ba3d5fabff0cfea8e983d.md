# ## 19.1 Introduction

Object detection stands as a cornerstone within the realm of computer vision, pivotal in empowering machines to recognize and categorize various objects within images and videos. Much akin to the rapid discernment of objects by the human eyes, the sophisticated algorithms, extensive datasets, and high-performance GPUs are utilized by computer vision to emulate this innate capability [1]. In practical urban scenarios, such as monitoring city streets or public spaces, object detection algorithms analyze visual data to identify and classify objects present [2]. This technology operates swiftly, processing vast amounts of visual information in real time, far surpassing human perception in efficiency and accuracy. By leveraging machine-learning techniques, particularly image classification and object recognition, developers can create applications adept at accurately detecting and categorizing objects within urban visual data. Training these models involves exposing them to diverse datasets encompassing various urban objects they are expected to identify [3]. Through this iterative process, the model learns to recognize and locate objects within urban environments with impressive precision. Smart cities can harness object detection technology to glean valuable insights into urban dynamics and enhance municipal services [4].
