# # 19.1.3 Computer Vision

In our project tailored for smart cities, computer vision stands as a critical technology, empowering systems to interpret visual inputs and derive meaningful insights about objects within the urban landscape [13]. Much like human eyes, the machines “see,” observe, and understand their surroundings, playing a pivotal role in enhancing urban governance, infrastructure management, and citizen services by utilizing computer vision. Computer vision technology mirrors the accuracy and capabilities of human vision, enabling systems to detect, classify, and even track objects within images or videos [14]. In the context of smart cities, computer vision facilitates tasks such as traffic monitoring, pedestrian detection, and infrastructure inspection with remarkable precision and efficiency. To achieve these functionalities, computer vision relies on vast datasets, cameras, advanced algorithms, and continuous training [9]. Through the analysis of large volumes of visual data, computer vision systems learn to discern distinctions and recognize objects, processes, or events within urban environments.
