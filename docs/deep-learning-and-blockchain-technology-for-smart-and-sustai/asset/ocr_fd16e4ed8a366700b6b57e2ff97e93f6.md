# 14.6 Privacy Preserving Using Deep Learning Techniques

The widespread use of IoT devices has resulted in unparalleled connectivity and data gathering capacities, transforming a multitude of facets of our everyday existence and sectors. But as these gadgets are always gathering and sending private data about people, places and activities, the growing use of IoT also poses serious privacy issues. Privacy-preserving techniques are essential to ensure that IoT deployments respect users’ privacy rights and comply with data protection regulations. Deep learning, a subset of artificial intelligence (AI), offers promising solutions for preserving privacy in IoT environments by enabling intelligent data processing and analysis while minimizing the exposure of sensitive information.
