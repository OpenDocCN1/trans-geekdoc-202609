# ## 13.3.1 Dataset Description

We call the process of gathering and analyzing information as “data collection.” Data is gathered from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), the official web page of the Indian government. Daily concentrations of air contaminants and climate data for Anand Vihar, Delhi, from 2015 to 2022 are included in the dataset and the sample is listed in Table 13.2. Records for 18 states, 102 cities, and 170 stations are publicly available on the Central Pollution Control Board, which is reviewed to gather the information needed to calculate the concentration of pollutants and meteorological characteristics. Tables 13.3 and 13.4 illustrate the parameters considered by our prediction model.
