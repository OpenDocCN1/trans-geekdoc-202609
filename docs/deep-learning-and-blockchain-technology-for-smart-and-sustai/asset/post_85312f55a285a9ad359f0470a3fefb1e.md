## Energy

- Smart lighting uses LEDs with sensors to automatically alter brightness based on presence and ambient light. This lowers light pollution and saves energy.

- Integration of Renewable Energy: To encourage sustainability and energy independence, smart cities are progressively integrating solar, wind, and other renewable energy sources into their systems.

- Smart Grids: By integrating renewable energy sources and optimizing energy consumption, sensors and intelligent systems track and control energy demand and distribution. Imagine if buildings could autonomously change their energy use according to weather or occupancy.

- **Garbage Management**: By optimizing collection routes and keeping an eye on garbage levels in bins, smart sensors lower waste overflow and boost productivity.
