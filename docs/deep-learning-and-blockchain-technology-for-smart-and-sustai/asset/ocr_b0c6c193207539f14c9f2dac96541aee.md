# 9.7 IIoT Applications for Smart Cities with BCT

The combination of blockchain with IIoT offers a wide range of fascinating possibilities for smart cities, from efficiently managing garbage and energy grids to optimizing traffic and protecting citizen identities. Imagine a city where the use of energy is recorded openly, waste management is rewarded and free from fraud, traffic is efficiently managed with the help of real-time data, and water conservation is promoted with awards. The safe data exchange capabilities of blockchain promote departmental trust and cooperation, give citizens access to information, and even protect medical records and academic qualifications. These two technological innovations have the capacity to completely transform a number of facets of urban life and open the door to the development of safe, effective, and sustainable smart cities in the future. Popular applications are:

+   - Smart Grid Management
+   - Transportation Management
+   - Water Management
+   - Identity Management
+   - Environmental Monitoring

# 9.7.1 Applications of Smart Grid Management

The potential for transforming smart cities is enormous as Smart Grid Management, IIoT, and BCT come together. By collecting data from different grids, residences, and commercial spaces, IIoT sensors enable effective energy management via real-time monitoring and analysis. Blockchain guarantees safe transactions and makes P2P energy trading within the smart grid possible with its transparent and safe data storage. This integrated strategy streamlines grid operations for utility companies, enables consumers to make informed decisions about their energy consumption, and lays the groundwork for the smooth integration of renewable energy sources. In the end, this cooperation helps to create future smart cities that are safe, effective, and sustainable.
