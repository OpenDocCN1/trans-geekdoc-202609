# 16.1 Introduction

From the point of view of intelligent agriculture, the sharing of data and resources of IoT devices in the agricultural network can share its computing resources as needed [1]. For example, during peak computing times (such as during pest outbreaks or weather events), nearby devices can work together to efficiently handle the computing load. This technology enables decentralized decision-making while IoT devices are operating and generating data. IoT devices can have built-in intelligence and decision-making capabilities. For example, a smart irrigation system can analyze soil moisture data and weather forecasts to independently control irrigation and optimize water based on real-time conditions [2,3]. The scalability of the technology depends on the technology used. Additional devices can be easily integrated as the agricultural IoT network grows. Osmotic computing and scalability ensure that the system can adapt to the growing number of IoT sensors and actuators required for larger agricultural operations [4,5]. Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize agriculture by enhancing supply chain traceability, ensuring quality assurance and certification, streamlining farm management and compliance, promoting fair trade and supply chain ethics, enabling decentralized marketplaces and trade, and facilitating data sharing and collaboration.
