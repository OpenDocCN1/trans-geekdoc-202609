## 10.4.2 Lignite

Lignite, also known as brown coal, represents the least advanced grade among coal varieties. In contrast to other coal types, lignite exhibits a carbon concentration ranging from 65% to 70%, indicating a higher presence of non-carbon elements like sulfur and mercury. Lignite, the most recently formed fossil fuel, is estimated to originate from approximately 60 million years ago, contributing to its relatively brief existence and resulting in a lower energy density of 18 MJ/kg. Due to its elevated moisture content and reduced carbon concentration compared to denser black coals, lignite tends to produce a greater amount of carbon dioxide during combustion (Figure 10.5).

## 10.4.4 Anthracite

## 10.4.5 Accuracy

Accuracy (ACC) is the percentage of overall perfect forecasts to the entire test dataset. It can also be expressed as 1- ERR. There is a 1.0 maximum accuracy and a 0.0 minimum accuracy.

```
ACC = \frac{TP+TN}{TP+TN+FN+FP} \times 100
```

- True positive (TP): Predicted positive, but the right answer is positive.
- False positive (FP): Predicted positive, but the right answer is negative.
- True negative (TN): Predicted negative, but the right answer is positive.
- False negative (FN): Predicted negative, but the right answer is negative (Figure 10.8).
