For consumers, IoT in retail offers unparalleled convenience through advanced technologies. It enables personalized shopping experiences with tailored promotions, recommendations, and interactive tools such as smart mirrors for virtual try-ons or mobile apps offering instant product information, as represented in Table 6.2. Contactless checkout and smart carts streamline the purchasing process, saving time and offering a seamless transactional journey [1]. IoT-powered devices empower consumers with access to real-time inventory data, enabling informed purchasing decisions and enhancing overall satisfaction. Conversely, for retailers, IoT presents transformative opportunities [3]. It allows retailers to gain valuable insights into consumer behavior, preferences, and foot traffic patterns through data analytics, aiding in personalized marketing strategies and optimized store layouts. IoT-driven inventory management ensures accurate stock levels, reducing out-of-stock instances and enabling efficient supply chain management. Smart shelves, packaging, and price tags streamline operations, ensuring better stock control, fresher products, and dynamic pricing strategies [19]. Overall, IoT integration in the retail sector reshapes traditional business models, fostering enhanced customer engagement, operational efficiency, and competitive advantage for retailers while delivering personalized, convenient experiences for consumers.
