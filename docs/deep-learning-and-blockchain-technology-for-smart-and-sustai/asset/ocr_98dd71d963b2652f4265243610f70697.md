# 14.1 Introduction

A cyber-physical system is a means of integrating processes from the physical world into the virtual one. CPS uses sensors to measure signals from different settings, and then networks are used to transfer and process these signals. The use of cyber-physical systems is growing in a number of industries, including healthcare, smart grid, aerospace and automotive systems, thanks to recent technological breakthroughs. Among these, healthcare is crucial to day-to-day living. Medical Cyber-Physical System (MCPS) refers to the cyber-physical system used in healthcare applications. The integration of cyber-physical system with Internet of Things (IoT) creates a new dimension in healthcare applications, which is called Internet of cyber- physical systems [1]. The services in healthcare system consist of physical information, networking and computation. The services offered for healthcare using Internet of Things (IoT) are divided into six categories [2]. The categories are services provided during an emergency, services to help people with disabilities, services for medical professionals, personalized medical advisor, services for sports persons and medication management. The method used to process healthcare information is known as electronic health records (EHR) [3]. EHR is a structured information that includes image, text or both. It increases the interoperability between patient information and healthcare organizations, even though the system is suffered from security and privacy issues.
