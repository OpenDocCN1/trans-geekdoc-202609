# 7.5 National Impact of Smart City

India’s Smart City Mission was introduced in 2015 with the goal of converting 100 cities into thriving metropolitan areas with better sustainable practices, infrastructure, and quality of life for their citizens. Although the program is still in its early phases, it has started to have good effects in a number of areas:

# Economic Impact:

+   1. **Investments**: As of July 2023, the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) reported that over `2.05 lakh crore (US$26 billion) had been invested in smart city projects.
+   2. One million new jobs are expected to be produced in smart cities by 2025.
+   3. **Enhanced GDP**: By 2030, it is anticipated to contribute 1% of India’s GDP (Smart Cities Mission Report, 2020).

# Environmental Impact:

+   1. **Lower Energy Consumption**: The installation of LED street lighting in 100 cities resulted in an annual electricity savings of 75 million kWh.
+   2. **Better Air Quality**: According to MoHUA (2023), smart traffic management solutions in 42 cities resulted in a 6% decrease in air pollution.
+   3. **Enhanced Water Efficiency**: According to MoHUA (2023), smart water meters in 45 cities reduced water losses by 15%.

# Social Impact:

+   1. **Better City Services**: According to 51% of residents in smart cities, public transportation is easier to access (Smart Cities Mission Survey, 2022).
+   2. **Improved Healthcare**: Over 8.5 million patients benefited from telemedicine projects in 83 cities (MoHUA, 2023).
+   3. **E-Governance Adoption**: According to the Smart Cities Mission Survey (2022), 62% of residents in smart cities used the internet to obtain government services.
