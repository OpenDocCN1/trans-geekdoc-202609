Figure 6.3 Smart analysis in textile industry using IoT and AI.

Implementing IoT-driven smart manufacturing techniques revolutionizes the creation of ready-to-wear products by leveraging interconnected machinery and processes. Within this framework, smart sensors embedded in manufacturing equipment gather real-time data, enabling automated adjustments in production lines. This dynamic adaptation reduces lead times, boosts operational efficiency, and ensures swift production of clothing tailored to evolving market demands. Moreover, IoT-enabled tracking systems like RFID tags or QR codes play a crucial role in ensuring seamless traceability across the supply chain. These tags enable continuous, real-time monitoring of inventory, allowing for precise identification, location tracking, and seamless product movement from production to distribution stages. By enhancing transparency and minimizing errors, IoT-driven tracking optimizes inventory management [2].
