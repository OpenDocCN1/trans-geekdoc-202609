### 9.7.2 Applications of Transportation Management

BCT and the IIoT have the potential to completely transform transportation management in smart cities. The main applications are summarized as follows:

- i. Enhanced Effectiveness in Traffic Control:
  - Real-Time Data Collection: By gathering information on parking availability, vehicle health, and traffic flow, sensors and linked devices allow traffic lights to be dynamically adjusted, routes to be optimized, and traffic to be reduced.
  - Smart Parking: By doing away with the need for paper tickets and cutting down on search times, BCT can enable safe and effective parking place reservations and payments.

- ii. Increased Openness and Security:
  - Secure Data Storage: Distributed ledger technology, which is a feature of blockchain, guarantees tamper-proof storage of data pertaining to drivers, cars, and transactions, which promotes accountability and confidence.
  - Identity Management: You may increase safety and stop unwanted access by using safe, verifiable identification for users and cars.

- iii. Eco-Friendly and Sustainable Modes of Transportation:
  - Integration of Electric Vehicles: Through the safe trade of carbon credits, BCT can monitor and control energy usage and encourage the adoption of electric vehicles.
  - Shared Mobility: Blockchain can enable P2P ride- and car-sharing services, encouraging the effective use of resources and cutting emissions.

#### Key Benefits

- Seamless Payments: Blockchain makes it possible for different transportation services to accept micropayments, which is convenient and does away with the need for several ticketing systems.
- Personalized Services: By examining user data, travel alternatives and route recommendations can be made specifically for each user, improving their trip experience in general.

### 9.7.3 Applications of Water Management

Water management in smart cities has enormous potential to be revolutionized by the combination of IIoT and BCT. Water infrastructure that has IIoT sensors integrated into it can monitor flow, pressure, quality, and possible leaks in real time, giving important information for optimization. This data may be tracked and stored immutably using BCT, which is transparent and safe. This ensures accountability and building confidence. This combination permits:

- i. **Leak Detection and Prevention**: Prompt leak identification and repair reduces water loss and expenses thanks to real-time monitoring.

- ii. **Water Quality Control**: Regular monitoring of elements including turbidity, pH, and pollutants guarantees clean drinking water and prompt action when necessary.

- iii. **Smart Water Distribution**: By optimizing water allocation based on demand and weather patterns, data-driven insights help to promote effective usage.

- iv. **Decentralized Water Trading**: In areas where water is limited, blockchain-based platforms can help communities or individuals exchange water in a transparent and safe manner.

- v. **Empowering Citizens**: Residents’ adoption of water-saving habits can be encouraged via real-time data availability via smart meters and smartphone apps.

#### Key Benefits

- **Reduced Water Waste**: Losses are minimized by early leak identification and demand forecasting.
- **Better Water Quality**: Health risks are decreased and safety is guaranteed by real-time monitoring.
- **Improved Ecological Viability**: Making educated decisions encourages responsible use and conservation.
- **Enhanced Transparency**: Blockchain empowers stakeholders and fosters trust.
- **Reduced Costs**: Effective resource management is a result of data-driven insights.

### 9.7.4 Applications of Identity Management

In a smart city with many of public transportation alternatives (buses, trains, and trams), it can be hard to manage fares and access effectively while maintaining user privacy and security. Conventional ticketing systems frequently require centralized data storage, are vulnerable to fraud, and lack interoperability, which raises privacy problems.

**Solution**: Use IIoT and Blockchain to implement a decentralized identity management system:

- i. **User Identity**: On a blockchain ledger that is impenetrable, citizens are granted access to a safe digital identity. Not simply transportation services but also other city services can be accessed with this identity.

- ii. **IoT Integration**: IIoT sensors installed in public transportation vehicles identify user IDs via digital wallets holding their identification or biometric verification (such as facial recognition).

- iii. **Smart Contracts**: After being verified, Blockchain-based smart contracts immediately take out the relevant amount from the user’s digital wallet and release the car.

- iv. **Data Privacy**: On the Blockchain, user identities are anonymous. Only encrypted data is kept, such as trip history required for invoicing. Users are still in charge of their data, and they can give different service providers varying levels of access.

#### Key Benefits

- **Efficiency**: Eliminating wait times and traffic jams with seamless, contactless fare collecting.
- **Security**: Data integrity and fraud prevention are ensured by tamper-proof Blockchain.
- **Privacy**: By anonymizing user identities, more control and transparency are provided.
- **Interoperability**: Constructs a cohesive system by facilitating communication between various public transportation types.

### 9.7.5 Applications of Environmental Monitoring

This system gathers information on the amount of fill in trash cans and other environmental variables like humidity and temperature using IIoT sensors. After that, the information is kept on a blockchain, which offers a safe and unchangeable record of it. The technology can be used to save fuel usage, enhance air quality, and optimize waste collection routes.

System components include:

- i. IIoT Sensors: These sensors are placed into garbage cans and gather information on humidity, temperature, and fill level.
- ii. Blockchain: A distributed ledger that is safe from tampering and secure is where the data gathered by the sensors is kept.
- iii. Smart Contracts: Waste collection is automated with the help of smart contracts. When a garbage can is full, for instance, a smart contract may be utilized to send a waste pickup truck.
- iv. Data Analytics: Trends and patterns in waste generation can be found using the system’s acquired data. Planning and policies for waste management can be improved with the help of this information.

#### Key Benefits

- **Enhanced Effectiveness**: Fuel consumption and emissions can be decreased by using the technology to optimize waste collection routes.
- **Lower Costs**: The method can help cities save money by optimizing rubbish collection.
- **Better Air Quality**: The technology can enhance air quality by cutting down on the length of time that waste is left in landfills.
- **Increased Transparency**: Transparency is increased since waste collection data is recorded in a transparent manner when a blockchain is used.
