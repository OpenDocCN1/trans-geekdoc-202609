# 20.3.1 YOLOv5

In this process, the model is exposed to the entire training dataset multiple times in batches during each epoch. Monkeypox images are analyzed by the model within a batch, as well as their corresponding labels. After that, it predicts bounding boxes around potential lesions and determines the probability of them being monkeypox. A loss function measures how wrong these predictions were compared to what they should have been, i.e., these errors provide a measure of error for models. With this information on errors, an optimizer can adjust its internal parameters accordingly in order to detect monkeypox better in future images. Periodically, its performance is tested using a separate test dataset so that it does not learn too much from the training examples, which may cause overfitting. This leads to continuous learning and improvement on how accurately it can identify monkeypox by modeling such processes.
