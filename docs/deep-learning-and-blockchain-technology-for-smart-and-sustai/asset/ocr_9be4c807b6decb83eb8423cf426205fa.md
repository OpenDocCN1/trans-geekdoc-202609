# 14.2 Background

Ashish Singh and Kakali Chatterjee introduced a model for healthcare to ensure the privacy of the data [4]. They also explained to avoid unauthorized access. The most important problem in the rural area is a lack of healthcare centers and specialists'. People in the rural area are not aware of the medical facilities, which causes different issues. To avoid this, a cloud-based healthcare system is developed by Kashfia Sailunaz et al. [5]. The patient's age, weight, height, blood pressure and temperature are collected by sensors and analyzed. After analysis, patients are classified into any of the three categories such as emergency, healthy and alarming. Identity-based encryption is proposed for secure storage in the cloud.

Yosef Ashibani and Qusay H. Mahmoud proposed a three-layer architecture for cyber-physical systems [6]. The layers are perception layer, transmission layer and application layer. These layers are responsible for data collection, transfer and analysis. They also explained possible attacks in each layer and measures. During data transmission, it is necessary to ensure that the data is transmitted from the proper node or device. The methods used to provide authentication for devices are encryption algorithms and biometric techniques. Abdullah Alhayajneh et al. explained the biometric authentication techniques for wireless body area networks [7]. Based on the availability of devices, the methods are categorized into hand wearable devices, body portable devices and implantable medical devices. The methods are compared in terms of sample size, accuracy and requirements. Syed Taha Ali et al. highlighted a scheme to authenticate the data in WBAN using hash tree and digital signature [8]. The hash tree is formulated using the samples collected from the sensor and then signed using ARQ protocol. The simulation shows that the 99% of data is authenticated with fewer transmission overheads.
