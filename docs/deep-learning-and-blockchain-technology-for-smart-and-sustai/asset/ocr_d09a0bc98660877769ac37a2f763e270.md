This section discusses the experimental results. Based on Delhi air pollution data gathered from the CPCB between 2015 and 2022, experiments are conducted. The dataset consists of both meteorological and air quality information. Partitioning the dataset yields the best results, according to the empirical research. The dataset used in this investigation is therefore divided into two halves: 80% for training and 20% for testing. To investigate the effectiveness of the models, various DL models are tested on a testing dataset after being trained on the training dataset.

The performance evaluation metric serves as the gold standard for gauging the effectiveness and efficiency of the DL model. Construction of the model cannot begin until an extensive evaluation is finished. Thus, one of the most essential phases in the model-building process is performance evaluation, and there are many metrics available to assess a model's performance. The R2 score (R2), Mean Absolute Error (MAE), Root Mean Square Error (RMSE), and computation time are the performance evaluation metrics used in this work.
