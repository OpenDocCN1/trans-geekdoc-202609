Smart Grid: The advent of dispersed sources of sustainable energy is changing the nature of energy prosumers from being solely consumers of energy to prosumers capable of producing energy [29]. The emergence of BC technology presents chances to guarantee safe peer-to-peer energy transactions. BC technology has been recommended by some recent article as a solution to these problems. Using consortium blockchains, Li et al. [30] created a safe energy trading system. With the distributed consensus of blockchains, this method can drastically reduce trading costs without utilizing a central broker. Additionally, using blockchain technology, Aitzhan and Svetinovic [31] created a decentralized energy trading system. It was shown that this technology is helpful in maintaining business energy trading agreements in decentralized smart grid systems.
