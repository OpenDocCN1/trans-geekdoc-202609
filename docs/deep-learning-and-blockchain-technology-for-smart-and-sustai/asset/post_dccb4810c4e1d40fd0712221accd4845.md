## 4.4.2 Issues with Smart City Technology

- 1. The expense of planning and maintenance is the prime difficulties to the actual deployment of smart towns. There exist many useful options for applications at low system costs.
- 2. More resources should thus be put together at the application level to realize smart cities objectives. However, platform incompatibilities brought on by heterogeneity obstruct integration and interoperability at the application level.
- 3. Information and infrastructure protection is a key aspect for smart capitals that make big data processing and analysis technique in need of upgrading.
- 4. Smart cities must also contend with waste management as a significant issue because of environmental pollution and landfilling. Any project aimed at making a city “smarter” must address failure management as well. Besides, natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes, or hurricanes could preclude system functions due to physical damages and a lack of network availability.

Thanks to the combination of DL and BC, many jobs that require sensitive data and need to be handled with great care and security can also be done. The combination of DL algorithms along with BC techniques can offer a reliable, long-lasting, and distributed platform for the crucial information that DL-oriented apps would need to gather, handle, and utilize.

### 4.5.1 Rewards from Amalgamation of the Algorithms of DL with BC Technology

- Securing the Data: The nodes’ private keys must be kept private since they may be means of retrieving blockchain data. Decisions made using algorithms of DL are more dependable, accurate, and trustworthy as they can operate on steady data that is supplied by BC [6].
- Automatic Decision-Making: Thanks to tracking capabilities, results made with DL models can be easily audited. It also ensures that there is no data manipulation during human-assisted search phase.
- Final Assessment: Digital identity administrators often base their results on data collected about the situation. DL and swarm robots are two examples of agent-based decision-making.
- Improved performance: DL models make customers more confident in making decision. Moreover, since DL is maintained by technology, change is guaranteed.

The technique of DL searches for models that may be used to various scenarios to support decision-making once the gathered data has been analyzed. The five primary forms of models based on DL used to create various sorts of application judgments are Geometric DL, Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL), Recurrent Neural Networks, Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN), and Convolution Neural Networks. The primary application domains for blockchain-enabled artificial intelligence algorithms to guarantee data integrity include good health, IoVs, handling traffic, and privacy and security [58].
