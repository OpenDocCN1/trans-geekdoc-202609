# Autonomous Vehicles and Ride-Sharing:

■ **Blockchain**: Leverage blockchain to create secure and auditable records of autonomous vehicle transactions, including vehicle ownership, usage, and maintenance. Smart contracts can automate vehicle leasing agreements, insurance policies, and payment settlements.

■ **Deep Learning**: Deep learning algorithms are essential for enabling autonomous vehicle capabilities, such as perception, navigation, and decision-making. CNNs process sensor data (e.g., LiDAR and cameras) to detect objects, predict trajectories, and navigate complex environments, ensuring safe and efficient autonomous driving.

# Infrastructure Maintenance and Asset Management:

■ **Blockchain**: Utilize blockchain to create immutable records of transportation infrastructure assets, such as roads, bridges, and tunnels, along with maintenance activities and inspection reports. Smart contracts can automate maintenance schedules, trigger alerts for infrastructure defects, and facilitate asset management.

■ **Deep Learning**: Deep learning algorithms can analyze sensor data, satellite imagery, and LiDAR scans to assess the condition of transportation infrastructure and prioritize maintenance needs. By identifying structural defects and predicting asset deterioration, deep learning models enhance infrastructure resilience and safety.

# Public Transit and Passenger Experience:

■ **Blockchain**: Implement blockchain-based ticketing and payment systems for public transit, enabling seamless and secure transactions for passengers. Smart contracts can automate fare collection, revenue distribution, and transit subsidies.
