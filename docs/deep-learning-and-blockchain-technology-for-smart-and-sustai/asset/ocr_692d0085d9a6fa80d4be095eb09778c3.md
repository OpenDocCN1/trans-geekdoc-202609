# d. Seoul, South Korea

**Deep Learning**: Seoul uses deep learning for healthcare, analyzing medical data to improve patient care and treatment outcomes. The city also employs deep learning for environmental monitoring, analyzing air and water quality data to enhance urban sustainability.

# e. Shanghai, China

**Deep Learning**: Shanghai uses deep learning for public transportation, optimizing routes and schedules to improve efficiency and reduce congestion. The city also employs deep learning for smart building management, optimizing energy usage in commercial and residential buildings [20].

**Blockchain**: Shanghai is exploring blockchain for digital asset management and supply chain traceability. The city aims to enhance trust and security in digital transactions through blockchain technology.

**Successes**: Singapore’s implementation of deep learning for traffic management has led to a significant reduction in congestion and improved traffic flow. The use of real-time data from sensors and cameras allows for more efficient traffic signal timing and congestion management.
