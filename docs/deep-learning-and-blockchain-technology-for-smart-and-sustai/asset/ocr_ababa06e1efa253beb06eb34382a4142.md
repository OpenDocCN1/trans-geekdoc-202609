This set of fundamental pictures is used to obtain optical data utilizing thin slices. A curated set of metrics is derived from segmented minerals within each dataset, encompassing parameters related to color, saturation, intensity, and texture. These meticulously selected measurements provide a comprehensive understanding of the diverse mineral compositions present. Through an intricate analysis of these metrics, valuable insights into the mineralogical characteristics and variations within the datasets are unveiled, contributing to a nuanced exploration of geological compositions and their inherent complexities. It has been shown that textural features can be used to classify colorless minerals. On manually categorized samples of minerals, the neural network is trained. The employed technique for categorizing geological rock types from petrographic thin sections involves the utilization of the Concatenated Convolutional Neural Network (Con-CNN) method. This innovative approach (Figure 10.1) employs a concatenated structure to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of rock type classification. By harnessing the power of deep learning through Con-CNN, the intricate details embedded in petrographic thin sections are thoroughly analyzed, leading to more precise and nuanced identification of diverse geological formations. This method not only advances the field of geoscience but also showcases the potential of cutting-edge technology in unraveling the intricate complexities of Earth's geological structures.
