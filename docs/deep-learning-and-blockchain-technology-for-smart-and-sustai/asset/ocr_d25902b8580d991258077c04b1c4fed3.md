IoT-connected systems allow for real-time monitoring of public transportation vehicles, enabling accurate predictions of arrival times and service availability. This empowers travelers with up-to-date information, enhancing their travel experiences by reducing wait times and uncertainties associated with transportation schedules. The commitment to eco-friendly practices in the tourism industry has led to the emergence of IoT solutions that promote sustainability. IoT applications facilitate the monitoring and control of environmental factors, such as energy consumption and waste management, in tourist destinations and accommodations. Smart waste management systems powered by IoT sensors optimize waste collection schedules and identify areas for recycling, contributing to environmental conservation. Furthermore, IoT plays a crucial role in optimizing airport operations. Through the use of smart sensors and data analytics, airports can efficiently manage passenger flows, baggage handling, and security protocols. Real-time data from IoT devices enables airport authorities to make data-driven decisions, enhancing operational efficiency and ensuring a smoother travel experience for passengers. As the tourism industry continues to embrace IoT technologies, it not only enhances the travel experience for tourists but also streamlines operations for service providers. The collective impact of IoT in tourism contributes to a safer, more efficient, and sustainable travel ecosystem, driving growth and evolution in the global tourism landscape.
