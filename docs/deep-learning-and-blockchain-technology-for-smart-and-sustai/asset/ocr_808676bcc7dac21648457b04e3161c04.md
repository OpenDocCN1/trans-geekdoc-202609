# 1.1 Introduction

Leading a quality life is the ultimate goal of human beings, which is possible only in smart cities. In day-to-day life, many problems such as public security, waste, traffic, and water management were available, but they were handled in smart cities and were discussed in this chapter. Life will be easy in smart city and moving towards smartness is based on economical, geographical, and social conditions of the city.¹ Smart Economy, Smart Environment, Smart Governance, Smart Mobility, Smart Living, and Smart Human Level are some of the characteristics of smart city.²
