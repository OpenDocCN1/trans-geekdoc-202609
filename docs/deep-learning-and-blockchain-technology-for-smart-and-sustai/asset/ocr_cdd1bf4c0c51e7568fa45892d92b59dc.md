Reference [51] presented the KL-MLP-LSTM network, the multi-layer perceptron (MLP), and the Karhunen–Loève (KL) decomposition as methods for estimating temperature distributions throughout the thermal process. This application can be utilized in any industry that deals with nonlinear systems with a parabolic distributed parameter and feedback input signals. According to the author, the suggested method might be applied in hydrology, making it less expensive to run and lowering the computing cost.

DL-based models were presented in Reference [52] to forecast the mean monthly groundwater level using information from 33 independent piezometers. Variational Mode Decomposition (VMD)–Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) hybrids and three distinct layers of GRU structures are included in these models. With an R² of 0.86, a Root Mean Square Error (RMSE) of 0.18 m, and a Total Grade (TG) of 6.21 in the validation stage, the GRU2 × model is selected as the best model based on performance evaluation measures. The hybrid VMD–GRU model likewise functioned well, with an RMSE of 0.16 m, an R² of 0.92, and a TG of 3.34.

It is not always ensured that the expressive and trainable hypothesis spaces accurately represent the underlying performance of DL models because of the overfitting tendency of DL to train data. According to Reference [53], this leads to the study of generalization, which identifies the model's capacity to effectively adapt to new, previously unseen data taken from the same distribution as the one used to generate the model. Generalization in clinical diagnosis is necessary to confirm the model's suitability and practicality. For instance, in order to give the generalization and interpretation of their model, Reference [54] proposed explainable AI techniques. The author employed professional judgment to validate the results of testing their AI-based pneumonia diagnosis models.

Their research also discusses the low-resource conditions, which are evident during the initiation of COVID-19 and the recent monkeypox outbreak, where quick radiologic image interpretation is not always feasible. Studying TL methods for identifying monkeypox sickness has been done fairly infrequently. AI-Biruni Earth Radius Optimization, for instance, is the approach used by GoogleNet deep network to classify photos of monkeypox [55]. Around 98.8% accuracy was reported by the author for their suggested models. However, the author did not employ any model interpretation techniques to comprehend the behavior of the model's predictions.

The initial computational outcome demonstrates that the ensemble approaches, as opposed to a single model, yielded the best results.
