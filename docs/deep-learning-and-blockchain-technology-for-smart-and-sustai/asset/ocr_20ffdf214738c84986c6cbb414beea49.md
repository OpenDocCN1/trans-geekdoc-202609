# 1.5 Water Management

In a smart city, schools have access to safe drinking water by installing water filters at various places inside school campuses (Figures 1.3 and 1.4). It is planned to have rooftop rainwater harvesting in schools. Ponds and lakes are rejuvenated by government schemes like Jal Shakti Abhiyan, which aims at conserving water. Existing dams' capacity was increased, and community water tanks were constructed. Soak wells or soak pits are also constructed.
