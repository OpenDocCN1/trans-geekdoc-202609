# 4.2.1 Computing Management

As IoT devices are resource-constrained, a lot of data has usually been sent to distant cloud servers for additional processing. But the IoT’s scalability is limited by the pure cloud-based computing paradigm, which also results in network traffic bottlenecks, prolonged latency, privacy exposure, and context unawareness [13]. By transferring computational workloads between remote cloud servers and MEC servers, which are usually placed at Small BS, Macro BS, Wi-Fi APs, and IoT gateways that are nearby users, MEC [14] is becoming a more and more essential part of cloud computing. Then, the response, privacy preservation, and context awareness can all be enhanced by moving the less computationally demanding, context-aware, and latency-critical workloads between distant cloud servers and nearby MEC servers.

# 4.2.2 Network Management

Nowadays, distributed IoT can benefit from the flexibility and scalability provided by software-defined networking (SDN) technology [15]. But Kalkan and Zeadally [16] demonstrate that the single point of failure locates from the centralization of SDN. Additionally, current SDN devices (such gateways) are unable to analyze data stream computationally intensively. SDN’s drawbacks can be mitigated by integrating blockchain technology with SDN. A safe blockchain-based SDN framework for IoT, for instance, is suggested by the work of Sharma et al. [17]. To update the flow rule table securely and eliminate the need for an intermediary, a blockchain-based system has been built. Moreover, blockchain technology has the potential to enhance network security for network function visualization (NFV) management. Specifically, Alvarenga et al. [18] demonstrate that NFV configurations may be made anonymous, consistent, auditable, non-repudiable, and immutable by integrating blockchain technology.

# 4.2.3 Communications Management

The 5G communication systems’ increased resource management is being driven by the rising demands of mobile data traffic. For example, the most important resource is radio spectrum and its components are Spectrum sharing and auction [19]. Secure and dynamic spectrum management in 5G and 6G communication systems could be accomplished through the use of BC technology in Federal Communications Commission [20,21,22].
