The integration of IoT technology further facilitates virtual prototyping and sampling through digital platforms. By combining virtual simulations and 3D modeling with IoT data integration, manufacturers can expedite design modifications and prototyping without physical iterations. This approach significantly accelerates the design process, reduces material wastage, and expedites the time-to-market for new products. Moreover, IoT capabilities extend to establishing wireless communication between physical objects and manufacturing systems. Devices equipped with wireless communication capabilities enable seamless connectivity within the manufacturing environment [19]. IoT-enabled sensors and devices facilitate real-time data exchange, fostering efficient collaboration between machinery and enabling remote monitoring, predictive maintenance, and agile adjustments in production processes, as given in Table 6.1 [20]. This interconnectedness optimizes production efficiency, ensures responsive manufacturing, and empowers businesses to adapt swiftly to market dynamics, ultimately enhancing competitiveness and product quality [21].
