# 17.8 k-Nearest Neighbors

The KNN algorithm offers a promising approach for predicting PD progression. Unlike traditional parametric models, KNN is a non-parametric method that classifies data points based on their proximity to other points in the feature space [17]. In the context of PD, KNN determines the class of a data point by considering the majority class among its KNN. This algorithm is straightforward to implement and computationally efficient, making it suitable for small- to medium-sized datasets. However, careful tuning of the hyperparameter k is crucial for optimal performance. Despite potential limitations in handling large datasets and the need for hyperparameter optimization, KNN provides a valuable alternative for clinicians and researchers seeking accurate and intuitive prediction of models for PD prognosis.
