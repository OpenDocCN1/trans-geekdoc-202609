Within the poxviridae family, the infectious disease known as monkeypox is caused by the Zoonotic Orthopoxvirus, which is closely linked to smallpox and cowpox and infamous for possessing extremely intricate double-stranded DNA [10]. Primates and monkeys are the main carriers of the disease; however, the spread from person to person is also incredibly predominant [11].

The virus may also proliferate through secretions from the body. Numerous anatomical traits shared by other orthopoxviruses are present in the monkeypox virus. A dumbbell-shaped core, surface tubules, and an envelope make up its 200–250 nm size. The variola virus and the core region of the monkeypox virus genome, which encodes essential enzymes and structural proteins, have 96.3% sequence similarity. Still, the area of the genome that codes for host range factors and virulence factors is extremely different. Variola minor (1%) and variola major (30%) have greater instance death prices than monkeypox [12].

In a Copenhagen, Denmark laboratory, the virus was initially discovered in a monkey's body in 1958 [13]. As efforts to eradicate smallpox intensified in 1970, the Democratic Republic of the Congo identified the first human case of monkeypox [14]. People who live close to humid rainforests are frequently afflicted with monkeypox, which is primarily found in central and western Africa [15]. In close proximity to another sick person, animal, or item, the virus itself spread. It can be spread straight across another person, animal bites, respiratory droplets, or muculent from the mouth, nose, or eyes [16]. Those who have monkeypox may have a high temperature, muscle pain, and exhaustion at the onset of the illness. A reddish patch on the skin is the long-term result [17].

The number of cases of monkeypox is increasing even though it is not as infectious as Coronavirus Disease (COVID)-19 that has been documented thus far. In West and Central Africa, there were just 50 cases of monkeypox in 1990 [18]. Nonetheless, in 2020, there were 5,000 cases. Although monkeypox was previously thought to exist solely in Africa, numerous non-African nations in Europe and the United States revealed in 2022 that they had identified the infected persons [19]. As of December 21, 2022, 94 countries had recorded cases of monkeypox, with an overall of approximately 83,424 people, based by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) [20]. People's extreme dread and anxiety are thus gradually increasing, and this is frequently reflected in their opinions on social media [21].

As immunity against smallpox vaccination wanes, there has been a sharp rise in confirmed cases of the virus in recent years, together with its geographic spread. The monkeypox outbreak in West Africa in 2017 was deadly, with a 6% mortality price in Nigeria. In September 2018, individuals from Nigeria brought instances of monkeypox to the United Kingdom, leading to infections among group of workers. The virus was also brought to America in 2003 by mice that had been transferred from Ghana. After being housed with puppies, people are ultimately infected.
