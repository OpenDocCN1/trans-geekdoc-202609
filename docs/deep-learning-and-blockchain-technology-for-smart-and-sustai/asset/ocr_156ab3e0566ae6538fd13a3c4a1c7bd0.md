**Big Data Analytics**: Analyze large amounts of data to gain valuable insights to improve decision-making. **Predictive Analytics**: Use historical data and machine learning algorithms to predict yields, outbreaks, and market trends.

Challenges and considerations:
**Data Security**: Protecting sensitive agricultural data from cyber threats is a major concern. # 16.2.1.4 The Key Limitations of IoT in Agriculture

**High Initial Costs**: Deploying IoT devices, sensors and network infrastructure can result in high initial costs. For many farmers, especially in developing regions, it can be difficult to prepare for the upfront costs of implementing IoT solutions.
