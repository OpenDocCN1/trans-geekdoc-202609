# 9.10 Conclusion

BCT and the IIoT have the potential to completely transform our urban environments and bring in the era of “smart cities.” Utilizing the complex web of interconnected sensors and devices that is IIoT, we are able to collect real-time data on waste management, air quality, traffic movement, and energy use.
