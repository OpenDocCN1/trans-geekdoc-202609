From a social perspective, the most crucial goal of the smart city–smart community concept is the sustainable development of cities, which guarantees the social well-being of community members. For this reason, the study looks at smart city and sustainable development best practices. In addition, the pandemic’s current scope provides justification for looking into urban development initiatives in search of smart city-smart community solutions. The most important objective of the smart city is health-related. Radiology is one of the smart health domains where AI and ML are most successfully applied. Every year, more than two billion medical exams and scans, including chest X-rays, are performed worldwide. One well-known deep learning algorithm is Deep Neural Networks (DNN), which is applied to a variety of health-related images such as medical scans, including images of the bones to check for fractures and breaks. Many important applications, such the management of water or electricity resources, intelligent card services payment and verification, have adopted a data-centric approach recently. Both traffic efficiency and CO₂ emissions can be reduced by using smart mobility. The applications of smart cities and their utility are entirely dependent on data connectivity, compilation, and pervasiveness.
