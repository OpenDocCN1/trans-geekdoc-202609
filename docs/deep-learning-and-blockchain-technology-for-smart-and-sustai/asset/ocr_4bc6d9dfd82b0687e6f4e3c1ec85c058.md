# 16.2.4 Blockchain for Smart Farming in Smart Cities

Blockchain technology holds significant promise for transforming various aspects of smart farming by enhancing transparency, traceability, and efficiency throughout the agricultural supply chain. Here are some key ways in which blockchain can be utilized for smart farming:

+   1. **Supply Chain Traceability**: Blockchain provides a transparent and immutable ledger that records every transaction or movement of goods throughout the agricultural supply chain. From planting and harvesting to processing, packaging, distribution, and retail, each step is recorded in a decentralized database. This enables stakeholders, including farmers, processors, distributors, retailers, and consumers to trace the origin, journey, and handling of agricultural products. By scanning QR codes or using mobile apps, consumers can access detailed information about the production process, certifications, quality tests, and sustainability practices associated with the food they consume.
