# 1.6 Smart Parking

Our roads were mostly populated with more vehicles, which are handled smartly in smart cities by having multi-story parking systems, sensor-enabled parking systems, etc. (Figure 1.5). Nowadays, parking of vehicles at malls is based on sensors, which means that if a parking lot is free, it will be indicated by green light so that the next vehicle driver can see the light from a distance and can park his vehicle easily.
