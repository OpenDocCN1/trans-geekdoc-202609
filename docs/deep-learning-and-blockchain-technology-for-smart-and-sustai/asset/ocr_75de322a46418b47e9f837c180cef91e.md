Using blockchains to manage transactions securely and traceably without the requirement for a central middleman saves administrative costs, which is the major benefits of using blockchain technology for 5G-beyond networks. Yrjölä [23] provided numerous examples of how radio spectrum sharing might be improved by employing blockchain technology in terms of cost reductions, consensus, and reliability. A blockchain-based protocol was also proposed by Kotobi and Bilen [24] to ensure spectrum sharing in wireless communication systems between primary and cognitive users. Moreover, through traffic optimization, blockchain may be able to share link conditions to several IoT nodes while maintaining privacy, hence increasing spectral efficiency [25]. BC technology has the potential to enhance radio spectrum management as well as mobile services for clients. For instance, numerous scattered heterogeneous networks make up most 5G networks. By enabling smooth access between various networks, blockchains that are constructed on the uppermost layer of the network can aid in the combination of various networks. Working nodes can enhance their technical and service capacities with the support of a blockchain-based system, as the research of He et al. [26] shows. In the future, service providers will be able to deliver better services to users by extracting important insights from subscriber transactions based on the synthesis of BC technology and big data analytics.

# 4.2.4 Blockchain-Based Management of Edge and Cloud Computing

Resource sharing incentives and the offloading of computational tasks are two problems that are addressed when coordinating edge and cloud computing with blockchain technology. It is possible to greatly reduce the latency by shifting the computational workload to edge servers. Thus, edge-cloud interoperability is essential [27]. If every node transfers its workload to the mobile edge computing server, this could result in a single node of failure and a performance bottleneck. Dai et al. [28] presented an offloading method that considers load balancing over many MEC servers. In the interim, it is valuable to look into ways to reward cloud servers and edge servers.
