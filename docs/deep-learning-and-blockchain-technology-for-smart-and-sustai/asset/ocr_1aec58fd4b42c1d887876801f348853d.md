But ensuring reliability in decentralized, untrusted UAV networks and controlling wayward UAVs are difficult tasks [52]. # 4.4 BC Technology in Smart City

BC technology is realized as a serious facilitator of smart cities, notably in governance, transportation, asset management, utilities, healthcare, and logistics [53,54]. The solution is provided to the security risks faced by smart cities’ massive communication networks [54]. It is proposed to include blockchain-based technology in the promotion of smart cities and create a framework to assist policymakers and city owners. People have also investigated whether this method can be used to create smart cities with a low carbon and green environment in the future, focusing on the sharing of government information [55].

At present, individual organizations and companies conduct transactions with the assistance of third-party intermediaries like banks, financial institutions, standardizing bodies, or credit card providers. These transactions often require approvals, incurring costs and sometimes leading to inefficiencies that waste time and resources. BC computing has evolved as an approach for financial operations where communications are supported out online openly and transparently to discourse these issues [56].
