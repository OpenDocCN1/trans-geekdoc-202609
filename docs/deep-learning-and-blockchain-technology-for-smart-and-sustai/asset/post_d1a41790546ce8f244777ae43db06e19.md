### 15.1 Introduction

An earthquake is a natural disaster like a flood or tsunami. Earthquakes below 4.9 magnitude are considered minor earthquakes. An earthquake of magnitudes 5.0–5.9 is a weak earthquake. An earthquake of magnitudes 6.0–6.9 is a strong earthquake. An earthquake of magnitude 8.0 or greater is a major earthquake. It can destroy everything in the blink of an eye. Earthquakes have a great impact on life and the economy. The largest earthquake recorded in Chile, South America, was the magnitude-9.5 Valdivia earthquake [1]. The economic losses of this earthquake reached $550 million, and more than 1,000 people died. Earthquakes occur all over the world and cause great damage. The countries that frequently experience earthquakes are China, Japan, Turkey, Iran, and South America.
