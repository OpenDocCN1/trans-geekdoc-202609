# Question 1: Explain the terms odds ratio and log-odds in logistic regression.

Answer: In logistic regression:

+   1. **Odds ratio**: The odds ratio is a key concept that helps explain the relationship between a predictor (independent variable) and the outcome (dependent variable). It represents the ratio of the probability of an event occurring to the probability of it not occurring. Here's a more detailed breakdown: It measures the change in odds for a one-unit change in a predictor variable. An odds ratio of 1 implies no change, >1 implies an increase in odds, and <1 implies a decrease. Calculated as , where is the coefficient of the predictor variable.
+   
+   2. **Log-odds (Logit)**: It is the natural logarithm of the odds and linearizes the relationship between predictors and the outcome. Mathematically,
+   
+   $Log - odds = ln\left(\frac{P(Event)}{1-P(Event)}\right)$
+   
+   Log-odds can take any real value, making them suitable for linear modeling in logistic regression.
