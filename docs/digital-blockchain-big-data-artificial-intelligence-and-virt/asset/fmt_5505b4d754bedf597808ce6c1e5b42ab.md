
## 2.2 Role of blockchain in the pharmaceutical industry

A growing number of institutions, companies, and scholars are becoming interested in blockchain technology, especially when it comes to using Bitcoin, a type of virtual currency. A diverse array of sectors and industries, such as aerospace, automotive, computing and electronics, engineering, business and accounting, banking and finance, defense, and healthcare, have been transformed by the application of intelligent technologies other than blockchain. These technologies include AI, virtual reality, the Internet of Things (IoT), and machine learning [[10](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_010), [11](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_011), [12](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_012), [13](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_013), [14](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_014)]. There is a novel, integrated, one-of-a-kind, and innovative computing technology that is a part of blockchain technology. The system includes transparent transactions, sophisticated consensus-building processes, dynamic public key algorithm encryption, programmable smart contracts, decentralized peer-to-peer networking, and decentralized retention [[15](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_015)]. One of the numerous benefits of blockchain technology is that the contracts and transactions that are incorporated into the distributed database are impervious to modification. Decentralization, transparency, trust, privacy, and stability are among the numerous attributes of blockchain technology. A viable approach to preventing the sale of counterfeit medications has been identified through the implementation of blockchain technology in the pharmaceutical industry. Blockchain technology enables the monitoring of the procurement of raw materials, the distribution of medications, and the origin of pharmaceuticals. Furthermore, the implementation of blockchain technology leads to a decrease in the number of intermediaries, which in turn improves the safety ratings and reduces the costs of the pharmaceutical method [[16](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_016), [17](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_017)].

The supply chain system as a whole is equipped with verification processes that validate the product serial numbers within the parameters of the following technology. Additionally, the traceability of drugs has been improved to prevent theft, and quality control has been accomplished through the utilization of digital signatures. In addition, to guarantee that the quality remains consistent, blockchain chain codes, medical records, and data mining have been instituted from the supplier to the pharmacy [[18](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_018)]. Furthermore, delivery delays can hurt business operations as a whole, and in the pharmaceutical and medical sectors, they can result in fatalities or the exacerbation of maladies. The pharmaceutical supply chain has implemented blockchain technology. Drug monitoring and provenance are indispensable for regulatory compliance, patient health, and business operations. Sophisticated and secure monitoring and tracking technologies enable the timely delivery of products in transit to their intended destinations, thereby enabling the pharmaceutical business and patient management. To expedite the global shipment of pharmaceuticals, a secure global registry was established. Even though the technology offers substantial opportunities for large pharmaceutical companies, it is also beneficial for smaller businesses [[19](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_019), [20](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_020)].

Drug discovery, drug progress, as well as regulatory certification, comprise the multi-year, intricate process of pharmaceutical development and research. Blockchain is a decentralized computer network that provides its members with access to a secure ledger of transactions and operates without a central server. By time stamping each transaction made within the network, it eliminates the need for an outside organization. Every network node maintains its local ledger and maintains a local copy of the record. Modest changes made to the localized ledger are reflected across the network. Beyond just cryptocurrencies, blockchain technology enables the creation and monitoring of any sort of digital asset. Within the pharmaceutical supply chain, a product created by a manufacturer and registered on the network can be transferred to other participants like cryptocurrency, as it is considered a digital asset. Once again, blockchain ranks first for privacy protection. Each member of a blockchain network is given a key pair that serves as their identity [[21](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_021), [22](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_022)]. The blockchain approach maintains patient privacy and data security while offering a special benefit for patient recruitment. Patient recruitment times are shortened by using blockchain distributed ledgers because anonymously registered patients can be linked to various clinical trial sites. As a result, the researcher can select the necessary subjects from a group of patients by the study plan. Proposals have also been made for a blockchain structure that involves multiple contracts for clinical trials to manage the studies and involve patients [[23](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_023)]. Also, the clinical trials are tracked using the blockchain technique, which encourages others to participate in clinical research while also instilling confidence in the outcomes. Because it can result in fewer clinical trials where positive results are covered selectively, transparency is an essential part of drug discovery research. Consequently, applying blockchain technology to clinical trials encourages improved relationships between sponsors and different clinical research sites in addition to enhancing data integrity and transparency [[24](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_024)].
