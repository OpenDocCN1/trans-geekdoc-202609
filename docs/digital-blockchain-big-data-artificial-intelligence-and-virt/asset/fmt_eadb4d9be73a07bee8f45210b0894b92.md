## 13.1 Introduction

Technology has advanced significantly since the Industrial Revolution and continues to be the primary driver of growth and production. Technological developments in machines that have substituted manual labor-intensive jobs have led to significant progress in human development. Artificial intelligence (AI) is an important advancement in technology that has allowed humans to take on physical labor at bigger mental and reasonable capacities across industries, beyond the manual support provided by machines [1]. AI is a field of research and technological advances that allows intelligent machines and software applications to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. Consequently, one of AI's primary advantages is its capacity to replicate human behavior in a variety of contexts, learn from errors, and adapt to new ones. Big Data and other relevant data sources are used by AI to improve performance on tasks. Among the numerous sectors that have profited from AI's recent rapid development is the crucial healthcare sector [2]. AI has digitally replaced manual health systems with automated ones in many areas. In some applications, managing patients and medical resources basic tasks is now all that is required of humans, with AI components handling more complex procedures or leaving them to their own devices.
