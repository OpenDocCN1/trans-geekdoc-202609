
## 7.3 Integrating AI into pharma executive decision-making

The increasing usage of AI-based applications has given rise to worries regarding data security and privacy. Health information is often the target of data breaches due to its sensitivity. Thus, patient data security is crucial [[60](#9783111573861_010289.xhtml#b_9783111574288-007_ref_060)]. Legal actions have been initiated due to data sharing between major healthcare institutions and AI companies, raising concerns among patients regarding potential breaches of their privacy throughout the data collection process. After data acquisition, the ensuing challenge is the advancement of AI technology [[61](#9783111573861_010289.xhtml#b_9783111574288-007_ref_061)]. Overfitting may arise when a system identifies spurious associations between patient attributes and outcomes. It results from having an excessive number of variable parameters compared to outcomes; consequently, the algorithm predicts using unsuitable features [[62](#9783111573861_010289.xhtml#b_9783111574288-007_ref_062)]. Recent years have seen a significant increase in interest in the application of AI in the pharmaceutical industry and clinical healthcare, which has resulted in a growing corpus of relevant literature and research. It offers information on how AI can improve clinical results and patient care, which is consistent with healthcare management and personalized medicine [[63](#9783111573861_010289.xhtml#b_9783111574288-007_ref_063)]. Big data practices consider upcoming talent requirements and fresh applicants in the healthcare industry. Big data presents a significant opportunity for medical practitioners, pharmacy personnel, and health policy specialists to make data-informed decisions that will eventually improve patient care [[64](#9783111573861_010289.xhtml#b_9783111574288-007_ref_064), [65](#9783111573861_010289.xhtml#b_9783111574288-007_ref_065)]. It has the power to alter how healthcare HR practices are ingrained and equipped to boost productivity across the board, gain a deeper understanding of data repositories, and reach a stated opinion or choice. Big data has been essential to any human resources department in the following situation, not only for keeping employees but also for their ability to better understand their surroundings and make the best decisions [[66](#9783111573861_010289.xhtml#b_9783111574288-007_ref_066), [67](#9783111573861_010289.xhtml#b_9783111574288-007_ref_067)].
