
## 6.2 Computational drug discovery techniques

Contemporary medicinal chemistry techniques primarily rely on stringent testing protocols and a cyclical methodology [[11](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_011)]. These methods evaluate several potential pharmacological substances to identify those possessing the requisite qualities. However, these procedures may be expensive, labor-intensive, and produce inaccurate results [[12](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_012)]. Their limitations may stem from the lack of appropriate test substances and the challenges associated with precisely forecasting their behavior within the body [[13](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_013)]. These problems can be addressed by various artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms, including reinforcement learning, evolutionary algorithms, rule-based systems, and both supervised and unsupervised learning methods. These strategies generally rely on the analysis of extensive datasets that provide many applications [[13](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_013), [14](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_014), [15](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_015)]. These algorithms can more precisely and effectively predict the safety and efficacy of new therapeutic molecules compared to existing methods [[16](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_016), [17](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_017)]. Furthermore, AI-driven algorithms can pinpoint novel targets for pharmaceutical development, encompassing particular proteins or genetic pathways linked to illnesses [[18](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_018)]. Enhancing drug discovery beyond conventional approaches may ultimately result in the creation of novel and more efficacious therapies. These constraints can be overcome with the implementation of ML and other AI techniques. ML algorithms can discern patterns and trends in extensive datasets that may escape human researchers. It facilitates the expedited proposal of contemporary bioactive substances with fewer adverse effects compared to traditional techniques. Recently, a dataset comprising recognized medicinal substances and their associated biological processes was utilized to train a deep learning algorithm [[19](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_015)]. Subsequently, the program showed remarkable precision in forecasting the action of new chemicals. Substantial contributions to mitigating the toxicity of potential therapeutic molecules have been made by utilizing extensive datasets of recognized hazardous and non-toxic substances to train ML algorithms [[20](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_020)]. Furthermore, the use of AI in drug discovery requires a high-quality dataset. Technology and high-throughput sequencing advances have accelerated the creation of many free, open-access drug discovery databases. As seen in [Fig. 6.1](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_fig_001), the drug discovery procedure is accelerated and assisted in entering the big data era by these databases. Presently, the manually selected databases like `ChEMBL` contain more than two million compounds with characteristics resembling pharmaceuticals [[21](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_021)].

`ChEMBL` collects data on the deposited compounds’ molecular characteristics, toxicity, target interactions, metabolism, elimination, absorption, distribution, and excretion mechanisms. `ChemDB`, a publicly available database, provides physicochemical properties (such as rotational bonds, molecular mass, and dissolution rate) and information on almost 5 million readily accessible small molecules [[22](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_022)]. Moreover, `ChemDB` includes several cheminformatics tools, including `Reaction Predictor`, `MOLpro`, `AquaSol`, and `Smi2Depict` that help in drug discovery. Information on genes and gene products that may interact with medications is available in the `Drug-Gene Interaction Database (DGIdb)` [[23](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_023)]. To find drug-gene interactions, users can use the search field module to enter a list of drugs or genes, or they can browse the genes in each group. Additionally, `DGIdb` can be automatically fetched via the web-based interface’s API. `DrugBank` is one easily accessible reference drug database. It currently includes many medications along with a plethora of knowledge about drug classes, interactions, target-drug relationships, and reactions. Utilizing the integrated tools, users can search, investigate, and retrieve text, images, and structural data from `DrugBank`. At present, it is the most extensively utilized resource globally for drug screening, layout, and metabolic prediction [[24](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_024)]. The two main issues with these platforms’ integration are the strict laws governing AI usage and the projected loss of jobs. However, these technologies are meant to make the task easier rather than completely replace people [[25](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_025)]. In addition to making hit compound identification quick and error-free, AI can also identify relevant SAR, recommend the best chemical structure, understand drug-target relationships, and suggest synthesis routes for these agents. After a basic medication is added, AI can help with quick decision-making and offer vital information for improving the drug in the right dosage form. Higher-quality products can be produced batch-to-batch more quickly and consistently as a result. Through in-depth market analysis as well as forecasting, AI can ensure the best alignment and pricing in the industry and potentially assist in determining the safety and efficacy of the agents in clinical trials.

Through the analysis of patient-relevant genome-exposome attributes, AI can assist in identifying specific patient cohorts for participation in Phase II and III clinical trials. It could enable the monitoring of the existing therapy targets of the selected participants by more advanced surveillance methods [[26](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_026), [27](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_027)]. The utilization of adjunct AI capabilities, such as predictive ML and alternative predictive algorithms, facilitates the preclinical evaluation of molecules and the identification of lead compounds, thereby enhancing the forecasting of lead molecules suitable for clinical trials within the designated patient population [[27](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_027)]. Patient dropout accounts for about one-third of clinical study failures, resulting in additional enrollment requirements that must be met for the trial to be completed, which adds to the trial’s financial and time constraints. Such issues can be avoided with close patient supervision and support in following the correct clinical trial protocol [[28](#9783111573861_008368.xhtml#b_9783111574288-006_ref_028)]. In a Phase II trial, “`AiCure`” has released smartphone software that tracks patients with schizophrenia who take their medications regularly. Consequently, there was a minor percentage increase in the patients’ compliance frequency, which guaranteed the clinical trial’s success.
