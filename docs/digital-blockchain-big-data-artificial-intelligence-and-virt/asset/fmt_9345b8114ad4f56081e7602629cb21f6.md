
# AI in Pharmaceutical Regulatory Affairs

AI holds a great deal of promise for revolutionizing the pharmaceutical industry, even though its application in pharmaceutical regulatory affairs continues to be in its infancy. Through the dissemination of information regarding the most recent developments in the industry, artificial intelligence can be of assistance in the management of regulatory affairs. This is especially true regarding the potential advantages of AI for regulatory approval as well as drug discovery.

## Regulatory Affairs Overview

The term “regulatory affairs” refers to the groups or departments within pharmaceutical companies that act as a conduit to regulatory bodies, both nationally and globally, to obtain product approval in accordance with the laws and regulations that are currently in place. In turn, regulatory agencies give their approval to products based on the information and documentation that is provided throughout the entire process of drug discovery along with product development [[79](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_079)].

## Pharmacovigilance and AI

The Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO) mandates that Marketing Authorization Holders (MAHs) report the Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSR) of any drug marketed in India to both the National Coordination Center for Pharmacovigilance Program of India (NCC-PvPI) and the MAHs themselves. The management and handling of these documents are becoming more costly, time-consuming, and laborious due to the increasing volume of ICSR reports. Therefore, to cut down on the time, resources, and labor needed for processing ICSR cases, PvPI may also use machine intelligence techniques like artificial intelligence [[80](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_080)].

Regarding the acquisition of precise data, the use of AI technologies in pharmacovigilance (PV) is very advantageous. Almost all aspects of PV in handling cases and risk tracking can be automated or made easier with the help of AI tools, which contributes to a reduction in the overall processing time.

### Beneficial AI

One idea that has been proposed is that of Beneficial AI, which is artificial intelligence developed in a secure manner and for the benefit of society. To construct AI that is beneficial, two different types of measures have been proposed:

- **Extrinsic measures**: Artificial intelligence designers are required to adopt beneficial designs. Including certain requirements, incentives, and constraints is one way that these designs could be implemented.
- **Intrinsic measures**: Formation of social norms and the organization of communications.

Extrinsic measures may be impacted by the influence of intrinsic measures [[81](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_081)].

### AI Tools in Pharmacovigilance

Furthermore, artificial intelligence technologies in PV greatly aid in the accurate extraction of information processes. Almost all PV case processing and risk tracking tasks can be automated or simplified with the use of AI tools, which helps to shorten processing times overall [[82](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_082)].

#### VigiBase

The PV database known as `VigiBase` stores the information in a format that is both structured and ordered, making it possible to easily analyze the data that has been recorded. The WHO International Classification of Diseases, the WHO Drug Classification, and the WHO-Adverse Reaction Terminology/Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities (`MedDRA`) are some of the medical and drug classifications linked to the system. `VigiBase` is a web application that the general public can use to browse and easily access information on the negative effects of drugs [[83](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_083)].

#### VigiLyze

In addition, `VigiLyze` is an online resource that offers a clear and concise overview of `VigiBase`, which can be investigated online for more information [[84](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_084)].

#### VigiGrade

`VigiGrade`, an online ICSR management system, is employed for worldwide drug surveillance. It does so by gathering, preparing, and disseminating data to enable efficient data analysis. `MedDRA` and WHO Drug support the system [[85](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_085)].

#### VigiMatch

As a result, `VigiMatch` is an algorithm that finds individual case reports that are similar to one another by using probabilistic matching of patterns. It is significant to remember that the main objective of `VigiGrade` is to evaluate the degree of completeness of clinically relevant data in an organized manner on an individual’s case report. It is carried out as part of the process of informing nations about the caliber of the data [[86](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_086)].

## Electronic CTD Submissions

The various regulatory bodies are transitioning from paper-based submissions to electronic submissions of the CTD. CTD (paper-based) submissions have been stopped from being accepted by several different authorities. It has been determined to take action to expedite the submission process and centralize the procedure. Regulations worldwide have been greatly impacted by the switch from paper to electronic submission of Common Technical Documents (CTD) [[87](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_087), [88](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_088)].

The acceptance of submissions in the electronic submission of the CTD format has resulted in the optimization of resources, the increase of efficiency, and the improvement of application tracking. Regulations worldwide have been greatly impacted by the switch from paper to electronic submission of Common Technical Documents (CTD).

The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) in the United States has implemented requirements for the submission of electronic clinical trial data (`eCTD`) for a variety of applications, such as biologics license applications, new drug applications, as well as abbreviated new drug applications. There has been a positive impact on regulatory procedures as a result of the implementation of electronic submission of CTDs [[89](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_089)].

Individuals who have experience with electronic document submission (`eCTD`) can show financial savings over paper submissions and shorten the time it takes for their submissions to be approved. This phenomenon has been noted in a broad range of organizations, regardless of the magnitude or volume of submitted work. In addition, the utilization of electronic submission and review (`eCTD`) has made the process of reviewing and managing the lifecycle of electronic submissions easier, which has resulted in the harmonization of electronic submission and review initiatives [[90](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_090), [91](#9783111573861_011894.xhtml#b_9783111574288-008_ref_091)].
