## 2.1 Introduction

The business model of the pharmaceutical sector is intricate and focused, with a primary emphasis on the production, marketing, research, and development of new chemical entities (NCEs) and biological products that are designed to improve human health [[1]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_001). In the pharmaceutical industry, “advanced analytics” are utilized throughout the entire value chain, including development and research, safety, production, as well as regulation. These analytics are developed from various industries [[2]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_002). The most important component of the healthcare system is the pharmaceutical sector, which is in charge of manufacturing and distributing drugs, biological substances, and medical equipment used in disease diagnosis and treatment. Additionally, the pharmaceutical industry engages in research to create new products that are beneficial to human welfare. Because numerous pharmaceutical goods are lifesaving, maintaining the final products’ quality is crucial to avoiding any possible health hazards [[3]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_003). A straightforward prerequisite for the achievement of the international organization’s objectives for sustainable development is the establishment of a pharmaceutical manufacturing unit. Encouraging and improving the capabilities of the populace is vital to controlling their wellbeing and producing novel drugs that are both more affordable and effective. It will help achieve one of the main aims of sustainability, which is the reduction of poverty [[4]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_004), [[5]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_005).

“Traditional technology” refers to a three-step energy conversion process that was in use before the fourth industrial revolution. The needs of patients in both armed forces and civilian populations, along with healthcare providers, are not sufficiently met by the traditional manufacturing of pharmaceutical products. Traditional manufacturing is inadequately qualified to oversee and manage complex, computerized manufacturing processes for the proficient and profitable production of customized items. The capabilities of conventional technologies for the development of materials, including those used in medical applications, have reached their limits [[6]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_006). It is possible to eliminate prescription errors that are caused by selecting the incorrect options from a selection box with the assistance of AI, which plays a significant role in the process as shown in [Fig. 2.1](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_fig_001). Artificial intelligence (AI) can recognize errors like these by analyzing the patient’s medical records. Lowering the chance of drug overdose along with additional health risks contributes to an improvement in the standard of care. Human error in the diagnosis of CT and MRI scans is reduced by AI, which improves the precision of treatment. It mitigates the danger to patient wellbeing, protects the hospital’s reputation, and decreases costs related to the breach of sensitive patient information [[7]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_007). In recent decades, technology has significantly transformed every aspect of human life, including transportation, communication, manufacturing, business, and the pharmaceutical and medical sectors. The pharmaceutical industry has begun to shift away from traditional careers and toward more-advanced careers to facilitate the production of medicines for the next generation. The variety of work available in the pharmaceutical research industry is expanding as career opportunities continue to improve. As a result of technological advancements, cutting-edge career opportunities are emerging. Through innovative research, these possibilities have been shown to greatly boost the pharmaceutical industry’s output and introduce the next generation of medications onto the market [[8]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_008), [[9]](#9783111573861_002146.xhtml#b_9783111574288-002_ref_009).
