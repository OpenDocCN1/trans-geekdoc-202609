
DL approaches are not interpretable, in addition to the constraints on data. When it comes to DL methods, they are viewed as a mystery in contrast to traditional ML methods, which frequently undergo a thorough validation analysis based on mathematical reasoning. Even though DL outperforms ML on the majority of tasks, researchers frequently are unable to comprehend why ML produces such excellent results. If a DL model produces a novel outcome that deviates from prior research, its interpretability issues render the result unsatisfactory. Unlike other fields, drug discovery is rich in knowledge about the regulatory systems of biological pathways, the metabolic processes of molecules, and the mechanisms of action of compounds. A comprehensive understanding of pertinent biological processes is crucial to guarantee the safety and efficacy of medications. These processes include the physical features of medications, the proteins they bind to in the body, the binding mechanisms, the biological reactions they elicit, and their metabolic pathways. DL is restricted to the acceptance of input and the generation of predicted output; it is incapable of providing a comprehensive explanation of the process by which the output is generated [[118](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_118)].

## 4.6 Conclusion

The pharmaceutical industry is experiencing rapid growth due to digitalization, which uses information shared via system integration, mobile phones, and other technologies to understand consumer demand and improve supply chain efficiency. The digital revolution in healthcare is bringing about a "connected health" movement, improving patient outcomes, reducing per capita costs, and enhancing the care experience. Robotic technologies improve patient care, diagnosis, and treatment, while AI can find significant relationships in unprocessed documents and aid in drug development. Drug-drug interactions can be retrieved, and their effects can be predicted by machine learning algorithms. By eliminating the need for in-person visits and guaranteeing that clinicians have immediate access to all medical records, big data analysis is enabling digital health care. The pharmaceutical industry is using blockchain technology to expedite regulatory approval, drug development, and discovery. The IoT is also being used to track health issues, forecast patient-related events, transform data, save costs, and save lives. The Indian pharmaceutical industry is undergoing significant transformation, and fostering a culture of continuous learning is crucial for success. Two cutting-edge methods in smart pharma include gamification and virtual training. Educational escape rooms in higher education should focus on student learning and industry-academic collaboration to advance knowledge related to molecular, biological, and chemical pharmacology.
