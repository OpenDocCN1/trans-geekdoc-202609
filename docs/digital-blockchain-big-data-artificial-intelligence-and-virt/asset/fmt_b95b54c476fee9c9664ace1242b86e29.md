
# 13.5 Conclusion

Higher mental and intellectual capacities have replaced manual labor in many industries, including healthcare, due to AI. AI will be utilized in smarter hospitals and patient-physician interactions in the future of smart-connected healthcare. AI uses pertinent data sources to improve performance across various tasks. Technological innovations like VR alongside augmented reality (AR), which enable users to interact and immerse themselves fully in virtual environments, have the potential to raise the bar for medical and healthcare procedures. Initially, the application of AI techniques in manufacturing process control was limited to scenarios with a limited number of formal or analytical models. These applications are becoming simpler, though, due to the expansion of instrumentation, data collection, and the capacity to extract crucial process signals and features. Factories are getting smarter as AI is applied more and more in manufacturing processes. Making the best decisions about techniques and algorithms is still challenging. A comprehensive understanding of the problem, its causes, repercussions, and desired solution must be spoken or thoroughly researched to increase the likelihood of choosing an AI tool that will be effective and facilitate the processes of creating models, assessing data, and interpreting it. Numerous well-known healthcare organizations, including `HealthLand.io`, `DeHealth`, `Bump Galaxy`, `AccuVein`, `HintVR`, and `HealthBlocks`, have disclosed their current and future Metaverse initiatives. These initiatives seek to eliminate healthcare disparities, enhance mental health services, increase vascular access rates, and simplify the healthcare system. The commercial launch of `5 G` technology fuels the development of novel biomaterials, intelligent materials, smart fabrics, and sensors based on `MEMS`, remote monitoring, and disease diagnosis. The `QoS` of these systems can be used to evaluate their overall performance. The state of technology today offers numerous opportunities for improvement and innovation, with future healthcare services provided via the `H-IoT` system becoming a reality due to the development of critical technologies that has helped the research community.
