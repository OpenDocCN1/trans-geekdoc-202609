
# 确保医疗领域 XAI 的负责任部署

To ensure the responsible and beneficial deployment XAI in healthcare, it is essential to rigorously assess its ethical ramifications.

## 核心伦理原则与评估框架

Researchers emphasize the significance of AI efficacy, interpretability, and explicability in health technologies. Their findings indicate that organizations assessing healthcare technology must acquire substantial knowledge regarding the functionality of machine learning algorithms and their predictive capabilities [[57](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_057)]. The authors advocate for the development and application of tools to assess these factors to foster a sense of accountability among stakeholders.

## 诊断应用中的权衡

Certain researchers employ AI for diagnostic purposes and examine the potential benefits and drawbacks associated with the interpretability and reliability of AI. While AI has the capacity to enhance diagnostic output and accuracy, it poses a risk to the skill sets of the clinical profession. The research advocates for the utilization of AI as an adjunct to human expertise instead of attempting to supplant it.

## 偏见与公平性问题

Potential biases in AI and big data applications utilized in the healthcare sector have been highlighted by certain sources [[58](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_058), [59](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_059)]. They offer recommendations for improving the equity of AI algorithms and caution against training these algorithms with non-representative samples.

## 数据治理与激励机制

The authors contribute to the policy discussion on data oversight in the AI era by highlighting the necessity of incentivizing patients to disclose their health data. The authors contend that the revenue-generating nature of digital copyright content, such as music, can impart important knowledge and provide a GDPR-based remedy that leaves data flow unhindered [[60](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_060)]. They contend that the strategy may provide a workable and proven way to encourage data sharing without restricting access to it, which is essential for the advancement and efficacy of healthcare AI.

## 全球南方与以人为本的 AI

Academics talk about how urgently human-centered AI is needed in healthcare, especially in the so-called Global South a region mostly made up of low-resource areas [[61](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_061), [62](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_062)]. According to Okolo, biased AI systems may inadvertently widen the gaps that currently exist in these fields, so their use should be avoided. The author emphasizes how crucial it is to consider local requirements and conditions when creating AI systems to provide more equitable and effective healthcare solutions.

## 心理健康服务中的伦理考量

Similarly, researchers discuss the moral implications of integrating AI and big data into mental health services a notion the author refers to as “iHealth.” In his thorough ethical examination of the possibilities of iHealth, Rubeis identifies three double-edged swords: data mining, ecological momentary assessment (EMA), and self-monitoring [[63](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_063)].

## 透明度与问责制

## 偏见缓解策略

Preexisting biases may be reinforced and amplified by AI systems that were trained on biased data, which might have discriminatory impacts on healthcare outcomes. When implementing XAI ethically, biases must be actively and thoughtfully taken into account during the phases of data collection, preliminary processing, and model training [[64](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_064)]. XAI system bias monitoring and remediation can lead to more reasonable healthcare practices.

## 知情同意与患者自主权

Patients have a right to know if AI, including XAI, is used in their treatment. To gain informed consent, it is imperative to have clear communication about the purpose, constraints, and possible hazards associated with AI systems. It should be possible for patients to obtain human explanations and guidance, guaranteeing that their choices and autonomy are honored.

## 临床决策支持与专业责任

Important elements of ethical XAI include putting patient welfare first and making sure AI systems make choices that are in the best interests of the patient [[65](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_065)]. When making decisions, healthcare professionals should be assisted by XAI, which should be built with their specific needs and preferences in mind. Healthcare professionals must comprehend and apply XAI systems correctly. It entails upholding their clinical expertise, critically assessing AI outputs, and being aware of the constraints and potential biases of AI systems. Healthcare workers should receive sufficient XAI education and training to guarantee its responsible and efficient use.

## 伦理框架与指南遵循

XAI development and application in healthcare can be guided by adhering to accepted ethical frameworks, including the principles of kindness, nonmaleficence, independence, and justice. Healthcare-specific ethical guidelines, like those offered by governmental or professional medical organizations can help navigate the particular ethical challenges that XAI presents [[66](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_066), [67](#9783111573861_017182.xhtml#b_9783111574288-012_ref_067)].
