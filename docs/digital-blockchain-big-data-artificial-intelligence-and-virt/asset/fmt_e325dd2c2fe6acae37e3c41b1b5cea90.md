# Chapter 4 Education and training in smart pharma

## Abstract

A "connected health" movement is being sparked by the digitalization of healthcare, which also lowers per capita costs, enhances care quality, and improves patient outcomes. The pharmaceutical industry is expanding at a rapid rate due to digitalization, which uses information shared via social media, mobile phones, and other technologies to comprehend consumer demand and optimize supply chain efficiency. Machine learning algorithms can retrieve drug-drug interactions and predict their effects. Big data analysis enables digital health care by eliminating the need for in-person visits and guaranteeing that clinicians have immediate access to all medical records. The physical networking of software, embedded systems, and electronic sensors that permit data sharing across a network from any location is known as the Internet of Things (IoT). The pharmaceutical industry is using blockchain technology to expedite regulatory approval, drug development, and discovery. The Internet of Things is also being used to monitor health problems, predict events about patients, alter data, reduce expenses, and save lives. The chapter offers state-of-the-art training approaches for the smart pharmaceutical industry.

**Keywords:** pharma, virtual reality, gamification, academic, collaboration, Internet of Things

## 4.1 Introduction

The pharmaceutical sector is among the global economic domains exhibiting the swiftest growth rate. The efficiency of the supply chain is enhanced by digitalization, which involves the exchange of information through mobile devices, system integration, and numerous other channels to comprehend and anticipate consumer demand. In the aftermath of COVID-19, businesses in all sectors are more dependent on digital technology than ever to improve performance by increasing manufacturing productivity, enhancing competitiveness, improving long-term financial viability, and using more accurate forecasting and planning [[1]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_001). The digital revolution in healthcare is fostering a "connected health" movement that has the potential to benefit all parties involved by enhancing the treatment experience, reducing per capita costs, and improving patient outcomes [[2]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_002), [[3]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_003). For instance, the implementation of digitalization to create pharmaceuticals that are resistant to counterfeiting and have serial numbers that may be traced throughout the supply chain will guarantee quality and ensure adherence to forthcoming serialization regulations [[4]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_004). Pharmaceutical companies may employ digitalization as an additional tool to meet the anticipated global market demand. They could utilize digitalization to more effectively communicate with distributors and suppliers, comply with regulations, and identify cost-saving production efficiencies through cloud-based information exchanges [[5]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_005), [[6]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_006). Moreover, real-time manufacturing is made possible by the use of automation, advanced sensors, social media, and medical applications to forecast demand globally and track medication compliance [[7]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_007), [[8]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_008).

AI is capable of recognizing critical relationships in unprocessed documents that can be utilized for the diagnosis, mitigation, and treatment of disease. Almost any discipline of medicine can benefit from the application of several of the more recent techniques employed in the emerging field of computational thinking. To resolve intricate clinical issues, it is necessary to confront the challenge of collecting, assessing, and implementing a plethora of knowledge [[9]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_009). The development of numerous drug molecules from a chemical space is a difficult task due to the absence of suitable technologies. However, the drug development process can be enhanced by integrating artificial intelligence (AI) [[10]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_010). The healthcare sector has experienced a substantial increase in the utilization of robotic technologies as a result of AI. Healthcare is undergoing a revolution due to robotics and AI, which are improving patient care, evaluation, and treatment. AI systems can examine sizable datasets, including metabolomic, proteomic, and genomic data, to pinpoint possible therapeutic targets and clarify the workings of diseases [[11]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_011), [[12]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_012). Scientists can learn more about the molecular causes of different ailments and find possible targets for developing drugs through the process. Furthermore, promising drug candidates can be found in large compound libraries using AI-driven virtual screening techniques, which are more effective than conventional high-throughput screening techniques. AI algorithms can forecast the pharmacokinetic, toxicity, and biological activity of possible drug candidates [[13]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_013), [[14]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_014). As a result, less money and time are required for experimental validation. By employing deep learning (DL) techniques to forecast the properties of potential compounds, AI can facilitate the development of novel drug molecules from the ground up. Machine learning (ML) algorithms, known as generative adversarial networks (GANs), are capable of generating novel molecular structures that exhibit the desired biological function and drug-like properties [[15]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_015), [[16]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_016).

The effects of drug-drug interactions can be forecasted and mined using AI. To simplify the extraction of drug-drug interactions, researchers integrated kernel-based and feature-based methods with ML methodologies [[17]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_017). Certain researchers implemented an automated drug-drug interaction detection method to enhance drug safety surveillance in a hospital setting [[18]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_018). AI can be employed to predict the effects of drug-drug interactions. To extract drug-drug interactions efficiently, researchers integrated kernel-based and feature-based techniques with ML approaches [[18]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_018). ML can considerably enhance pharmacovigilance by enabling its models to predict a significant number of potential drug-drug interactions by learning from a limited amount of drug-drug interaction combinations [[19]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_019). Additionally, ML can be employed to identify populations that are susceptible to adverse drug reactions (ADRs) and to direct personalized care. Moreover, ML methodologies can be utilized to pinpoint specific patients, particularly those vulnerable to fluoropyrimidine toxicity. Researchers employed ML methods to analyze toxicity patterns, enabling them to predict the prevalence of individuals with DPD-related toxicity. The model may yet prove advantageous in the future, notwithstanding the risk of considerable overfitting [[20]](#9783111573861_004895.xhtml#b_9783111574288-004_ref_020).
