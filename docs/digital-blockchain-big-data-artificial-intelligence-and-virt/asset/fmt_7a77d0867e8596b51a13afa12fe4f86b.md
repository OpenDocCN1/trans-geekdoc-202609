
# Interoperability and Blockchain in Healthcare Data Management

## The Need for Interoperability

Interoperability is essential for enabling intelligent healthcare data management systems to receive requisite data input, as various data sources often store and process information in many forms. The challenge of obtaining relevant information is apparent in the immense volume of data that is continuously produced. The Office of the National Coordinator for Health Information Technology in the United States recently issued a draft rule regarding information blocking and interoperability, emphasizing the need to grant patients unfettered access to their electronic health records [[71](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_071), [72](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_072)]. Securing privacy and interoperability at the same time seems incompatible, which makes it difficult to achieve.

## Blockchain Technology for Medical Data Administration

Recently, the potential uses of cutting-edge blockchain technology for the administration of medical data have generated a lot of conversation in both academia and business [[73](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_073), [74](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_074), [75](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_075), [76](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_076)]. Blockchain technology can enhance and expedite the sharing of private health information by providing users with complete control over their data and privacy, eliminating the necessity for a centralized authority.

### Data Sharing in Chronic Diseases

Data sharing is essential in the realm of chronic diseases due to patient mobility, the involvement of multiple hospitals for diagnosis and treatment owing to specialized centers, the requirement for a "second opinion," and the use of multiple medications by patients [[73](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_073), [77](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_077), [78](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_078)].

### Pharmaceutical Supply Chain Optimization

Optimizing the pharmaceutical supply chain with blockchain technology can benefit clinical trials and medical research overall [[79](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_079), [80](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_080), [81](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_081)].

## Blockchain Implementation Approaches

By storing only the metadata, which may include patient consent, on the blockchain instead of medical records, blockchain technology can guarantee the traceability and inviolability of patient health data. Individual network nodes can store vast and sensitive healthcare data, and their intelligent representation will be kept off-chain [[73](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_073)]. An alternate method involves utilizing compliant cloud-based services for temporary storage (i.e., within a timeframe specified by the patient) and data sharing [[78](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_078)].

### FHIR Chain

The FHIR chain is a blockchain-based approach for data sharing that encapsulates clinical data by the Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources (FHIR) standard. The sharing of primary care data will be expedited and simplified while safeguarding privacy through the effective management of on-chain consent and the establishment of access-control mechanisms as specified in the consent.

### Supply Chain Applications

Applications utilizing blockchain technology throughout the supply chain are starting to materialize. These include improving the public health supply chain, protecting medical equipment, improving the usability of healthcare Internet of Things (IoT) devices, and using the immutability and transparency of the blockchain to combat counterfeit medicines [[79](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_079)]. Additionally, these applications ensure that the pharmaceutical company retains control over returned drugs. Researchers show how blockchain technology can offer features that improve pharmaceutical traceability in particular and supply chain management in general [[80](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_080)]. Ensuring compliance in the pharmaceutical supply chain is crucial, as is confirming the conditions of transportation and storage.

## Electronic Data Capture and Clinical Data Management

Pharmaceutical companies are also moving closer to their objective of obtaining patient record data directly from the source and putting it into an electronic system so that it can be sent gradually and steadily to sponsors or customers. The switch from paper-based data collection to an electronic system is referred to as "electronic data capture." Unprocessed patient or volunteer data from clinical trials is kept in a software system called an electronic data system, or EDC [[81](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_081), [82](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_082), [83](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_083), [84](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_084)]. Before being entered into the system, the information is first written down and then saved as an electronic case report form (eCRF).

### Clinical Trial Software

Clinical trial software is beneficial to all kinds of organizations, including pharmaceutical, biotech, medical, and research. These programs assist with all facets of conducting a clinical trial, encompassing study submission, archiving, and recruitment. These programs are relevant for both small-scale preliminary research and extensive investigations involving thousands of participants. The phrase "clinical data management systems" or "software" refers to the collection of applications available for data management. These programs are essential because they can manage massive volumes of data in multicentric systems or trials.

### CDM Software Examples

Many software programs, including `ORACLE CLINICAL`, `MACRO`, `RAVE`, `CLINTRIAL`, `eClinicalSuite`, `EZentry`, and others, are utilized as CDM software in pharmacies [[85](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_085), [86](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_086), [87](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_087)]. The functionality of these programs is essentially similar, so they cannot outperform one another. These software programs have the drawback of being costly, and for an organization to operate, sophisticated information technology communications must be acquired.

### Benefits of Medical Records Management Software

Several benefits are provided by the medical records management software, including the ability to expedite study timelines and control costs, provide precise clinical trial predictions, enable robust program modeling, ease recruiting, scheduling, and screening processes, support physicians, and effortlessly adjust for foreign exchange rates [[88](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_088), [89](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_089), [90](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_090), [91](#9783111573861_006599.xhtml#b_9783111574288-005_ref_091)].
