
## 3.6 Conclusion

The pharmaceutical industry needs to work with startups, innovators, and businesses from different fields to collaborate and accelerate innovations. R&D is funded through private-public partnerships. Collaborations can save expenses, postpone development, and enhance clinical results. In the pharmaceutical sector, partnerships aid in the creation of new medications. Businesses, academic institutions, governments, and nonprofit organizations work together to share resources and intellectual property to develop and commercialize goods. Academic medical centers make partnerships with pharmaceutical companies' investments. Both parties should exchange information in these partnerships. These collaborations provide value beyond financial gain by addressing significant scientific issues and creating new treatment targets. Working together with academic institutions is crucial to creating innovative medications when new therapeutic mechanisms, enabling technologies, and translational models are identified. Time horizons, confidentiality, exclusivity, and cultural differences between industry and university research are some of the challenges that face pharmaceutical collaboration. The lack of transparency and accountability in public-private partnerships can also impede global healthcare equity. Collaboration may be hampered by the disparities in organizational structures and heterogeneities between universities and businesses. Moreover, pharma AI, with assistance from organizations from the public, business, and civil society, works together to enhance health and tackle challenging health problems. Partnerships that have a shared objective, complementary specialties and equitable contributions are advantageous to public agencies. To create novel medications and biomarkers, pharmaceutical companies have teamed up with AI startups.

## AI Applications in Drug Development

In response to the rapid adoption of AI in healthcare, numerous pharmaceutical companies have established joint ventures and invested in AI companies to develop more sophisticated medical instruments. These encompass the identification of pharmacological targets, the development of new medications, and the advancement of biomarkers or diagnostics. The ML/DL analyses of the underlying data are subsequently combined to develop algorithms that significantly contribute to the advancement of AI-infused modern healthcare [[98]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_098), [[99]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_099). As a result, several international alliances between the pharmaceutical and artificial intelligence sectors have recently been formed.

### Benevolent AI

Benevolent AI is one of the AI technologies that are presently being utilized in the development of medications. It employs the text mining technique to analyze the extant patents, as well as additional genetic and biological data, to infer the connections between those entities. It leads to the development of diagrams that are highly illuminating and comprise dynamic maps with over one billion relationships [[100]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_100).

### Exscientia

Exscientia is an AI venture that focuses on the discovery of phenotypic medications. AI significantly underperforms human analysis of exceedingly intricate datasets in the realm of high-content phenotypic drug discovery. Testing each newly developed compound and subsequently comparing its anticipated performance to other molecules facilitates the rapid evolution of drug designs.

### Numerate

Numerate is also a notable AI enterprise. Numerate underscores the potential of algorithms to revolutionize the search for new medications by addressing critical therapeutic gaps through the analysis of extensive drug development datasets, with a particular emphasis on the application of classical methods to combinatorial ML, ADMET, and ligand chemistry [[101]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_101).

## Collaboration Between Pharmaceutical Scientists and AI Researchers

The development of innovative and effective remedies for a variety of diseases is contingent upon the collaboration between pharmaceutical scientists and AI researchers. They can leverage their expertise and experience to create algorithms and models that are rooted in machine learning. This will allow them to predict the efficacy of potential drug candidates and accelerate the drug discovery process. Collaboration can improve the precision and efficacy of clinical trials by utilizing AI algorithms to analyze the data acquired during these trials to identify trends and potential side effects of the medications being tested [[102]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_102). It can expedite the entire drug development process and assist pharmaceutical companies in making well-informed judgments regarding which potential drugs to pursue.

Furthermore, the affordability and accessibility of healthcare can be improved through collaboration between pharmaceutical scientists and AI researchers. The efficacy of potential drug candidates for specific patient populations can be predicted by analyzing data from large populations and identifying patterns and trends. Artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms can be employed to achieve this. It enables the personalization of remedies to accommodate the unique needs of each patient [[103]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_103).

The pharmaceutical industry's advancement may be expedited by the implementation of AI algorithms and big data, according to researchers. One of the most promising applications of AI has been designated by researchers as the pharmaceutical industry's pursuit of medications to treat uncommon ailments. Pharmaceutical companies are less inclined to allocate resources toward drug development when there is a lack of market demand [[104]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_104), [[105]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_105). In contrast, AI can determine which patients would derive the most benefit from clinical trials. The risk of failure at the most expensive stage of the pharmaceutical industry, the clinical trials phase, can be significantly reduced by developing a profile of ideal candidates. Another area of intense research concentration is the role that AI algorithms will play in the displacement of humans [[106]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_106), [[107]](#9783111573861_003373.xhtml#b_9783111574288-003_ref_107).
