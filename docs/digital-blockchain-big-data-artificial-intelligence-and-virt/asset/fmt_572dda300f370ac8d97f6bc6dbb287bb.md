In recent years, there has been a significant increase in interest in the implementation of AI in the field of medicine, as it has the potential to revolutionize the pharmaceutical industry. The long and intricate drug discovery process has historically relied on resource-intensive techniques such as high-throughput screening and trial-and-error testing to identify and propagate novel pharmaceuticals. Therefore, by enabling more precise and effective analysis of big datasets, ML along with NLP techniques in AI provides a way to speed up and improve the process. Researchers have recently shown that DL may be successfully applied to precisely forecast the efficacy of medicinal molecules [[1](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_001), [2](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_002), [3](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_003)].

Medicinal chemistry is currently characterized by the use of a trial-and-error approach and intensive screening procedures. One of these techniques is to analyze an abundance of possible therapeutic molecules to find those that meet the requirements. It has been observed, nevertheless, that these methods are costly, time-consuming, and frequently produce less accurate results. Furthermore, the scope of their application may be restricted by the availability of appropriate chemicals as well as the challenges associated with accurately predicting their physiological reactions. Numerous AI algorithms, such as unsupervised learning strategies, reinforcement learning, and evolution or rule-based algorithms, may be of great assistance in solving these issues [[4](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_004), [5](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_005), [6](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_006)].

## AI in Drug Research and Development

AI has brought about significant changes in the area of drug research and development (R&D). It involves researching proteins and small compounds, helping to plan clinical trials, and monitoring things after putting them on the market [[7](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_007)].

AI includes various methodological areas, such as information representation, reasoning, finding solutions, and, most importantly, machine learning (ML) as a foundational paradigm. Algorithms used in ML can identify patterns within a specially classified dataset. Deep learning (DL) is a specific subfield of ML that focuses on the application of artificial neural networks (ANNs). The aforementioned elements combine to create a network of linked, highly developed computing units known as "perceptions," which are designed to resemble actual neurons in the human brain and transmit electrical impulses in a comparable way [[8](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_008), [9](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_009)].

NLP algorithms can extract relevant details from unstructured data that can be found in a variety of books, articles, and patents. Additional study of the extracted information may reveal the hidden relationships of knowledge in several papers, hence relieving the workload of researchers who would otherwise need to examine every document [[10](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_010), [11](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_011)].

Furthermore, algorithms that are used to generate virtual screens based on their capacity to synthesize workable substances include nearest-neighbor classifiers, random forest (RF), extreme learning machines, support vector machines (SVMs), and deep neural networks (DNNs) [[12](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_012), [13](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_013)]. These algorithms also can forecast toxicity and in vivo activity.

To provide a complete framework that would aid in the identification and development of treatments in areas like immuno-oncology and cardiovascular illnesses, a group of biopharmaceutical firms has partnered with IT companies [[14](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_014)]. Based only on a molecule's chemical structure, the AI/ML models may also anticipate possible biological interactions of a molecule that hasn't been tested or synthesized. Scientists in the absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion (ADME) sector use AI/ML models to identify small molecules with the best potential for synthesis, testing, and process development based on their ADME profiles [[15](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_015), [16](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_016)].

Artificial intelligence (AI) facilitates the best possible operation of medication delivery systems and is a useful tool in the discovery and repurposing of pharmaceuticals. It collects information from several sources, evaluates pharmacokinetic, patient, and molecular data, and contrasts unknown compounds with recognized ones. The accuracy with which drug delivery methods are chosen, taking patient needs and illness circumstances into account, determines the efficacy [[17](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_017), [18](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_018)].

## AI in Drug Delivery Systems

AI algorithms can forecast the behavior of drugs on various levels by examining intricate connections between pharmacological properties, formulation components, and physiological parameters. They enable the prediction of the physicochemical characteristics, stability, and in vitro release profile of the medicinal compound, thereby facilitating the development of a more comprehensive understanding of efficient drug delivery systems and medication delivery processes.

The methodology previously described is also employed to conduct in vivo-in vitro correlation studies and to enhance the evaluation of in vivo pharmacokinetic parameters and drug distribution. By applying appropriate AI techniques, researchers can identify potential risks and impediments related to drug delivery systems (DDS) early in the development phase, enabling proactive alterations and modifications to reduce risks and enhance the effectiveness of medication. AI and computer modeling help to lessen the likelihood of unexpected outcomes by reducing reliance on costly and time-consuming trial-and-error trials [[19](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_019), [20](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_020)].

## Challenges and Future Directions

Even though AI applications in pharmacy seem promising, there are still many unsolved research issues. Long-term effects like medication adherence, ethical concerns about data privacy and potential biases, technical difficulties with integrating data and system maintenance, and patient and chemist usability are a few of these. The satisfaction of patients with the device is another factor. To enhance the understanding of the implications of integrating AI into the pharmacological system, scientists may address these knowledge gaps, develop solutions that optimize its benefits, mitigate its drawbacks, and ensure its ethical use [[21](#9783111573861_000727.xhtml#b_9783111574288-001_ref_021)].
