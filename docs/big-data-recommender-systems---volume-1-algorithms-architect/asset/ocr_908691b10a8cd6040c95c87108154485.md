# 7.2 Big data analytic

Big data for all machine-automated systems is nowadays admitted as a challenge by computer industry. Many concerns are in storage, management and retrieval data which is known as big data. The main issue is how to apply this data in order to enhance business and improve the living standard of people. Process of collecting, organizing and analyzing large amount of data to determine various lines and other helpful information is referred to as big data analytics. The primary objective of the big data analytics is asking organization to get better decisions in business, help with future predictions and provide them with analysis of large numbers of transactions done in organizations and update those of data used by them [12]. Extracting information from current data in order to predict future outcomes and trends or determining pattern can be done by predictive analysis; this will not act as a prophecy but with an acceptable level of reliability will predict what may happen in future, including what-if-then scenarios and risk considerations. Predictive analytics has applications, namely, customer relationship management, clinical decision support, collection analytics, cross sell, customer retention, direct marketing, Fraud detection risk management and underwriting [12].

# 7.2.1 Text analytics

Information in database is being present in textual form. Effective extraction of important information or manual analysis of these contexts is somehow impossible, due to its relevance in supplying a quantity of automatic tools for analyzing large textual data. A procedure of obtaining major information from text data is called text analytics or text mining. This procedure is used to extract meaningful data from texts. utilizes numerous ways like associations among entities, predictive rule, designs, patterns, concepts, events based on rules. Text analytics is widely used in government, research and business requirements. Let you know what people did, but text analytics let you know why they have it. All information in unstructured or semi structured data is recoverable and will extract significant information which can be categorized, and finally, based on all these categorized information, they can take decisions for business.
