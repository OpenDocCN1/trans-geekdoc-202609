### 5.5.1.2 Architectural patterns as development standards

Most components of DI fall into one three broad categories: (1) servers, (2) interfaces and (3) data transformations. DI architecture is simply the pattern made when servers relate through interfaces. The point of an architectural pattern is to provide a holistic view of both infrastructure and. Well-run organisations have development standards, and architectural patterns should be among those.

- Sim for reuse and consistency: As development and architectural patterns are applied to multiple projects, the result is simplicity, which fosters the reuse of DI artifacts (like jobs, routes, data transformations, interfaces), which in turn increases consistency in the handling of data.
- Harmony between common infrastructure and individual solutions: For a solution (like a data flow or project) to be organised in a preferred architecture, the infrastructure (especially the DI production server and the interfaces it supports) must enable that.
