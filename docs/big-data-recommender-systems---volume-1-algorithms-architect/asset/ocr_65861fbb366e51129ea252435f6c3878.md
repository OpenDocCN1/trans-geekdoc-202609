# Novel hybrid approaches for big data recommendations

Abdul Kader Saiod¹ and Darelle van Greunen¹

Hybrid approaches (HAs) based on Fuzzy–Ontology are one of the core functions to efficiently handle and process massive dataset from diverse heterogeneous sources (DHS). HAs are becoming a noticeable trend in recent times due to its wide range of functionality to run all types of problem spaces. HAs are in the high demand for organisations to run their daily business operations as numerous dataset occur every day. Therefore, big data communications are challenging in the traditional approaches to satisfy the needs of the consumer, as data are often not capturing the database management systems (DBMS) in a seasonally enough fashion to enable their use subsequently. In addition, big data plays a vital role in containing plenty of treasures for all the fields in the DBMS. However, one of the main challenges of HAs for the big integration (DI) system is the inherent difficulty to coherently manage data from DHS, as different sources have several standards and different systems. It is practically challenging to integrate diverse data into a global schema to attain what looked forward to. The efficient management of HAs using integrating DBMS presents a challenge because of incompatibility and sometimes inconsistency of data structures. As a result, no common methodological approach is in existence to effectively solve every DI problem. The challenges of H raise the need to find a better way to effectively integrate volatile data from DHS. To handle and align massive dataset efficiently, the HAs algorithm with the logical combination of Fuzzy–Ontology along with big data analysis platform has shown the results in term of improved accuracy. The proposed novel HAs will combine the promising features of Fuzzy–Ontology to search, extract, filter, clean and integrate data to ensure that users can creatively new consist of datasets.
