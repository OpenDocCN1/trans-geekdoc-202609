# ## 5.11 Real-world project: hypertension-specific diagnosis based on HIDAS

This section describes the applicability and interoperability of the proposed HIDAs. This is a simple hypothetical use real-world hypertension diagnosis which aimed to explore the specific Fuzzy-Ontology hypertension architecture. Hypertension diagnosis can get information about the different stage of blood pressure ranges such as optimal stage, normal stage, high normal stage, Stage 1 (mid), Stage 2 (moderate) and Stage 3 (severe). Input parameters for Fuzzy-Ontology decision-making systems are age, body mass index (BMI), systolic and diastolic and one output parameter is hypertension risk. The intention of generating the hypertension diagnosis is to help providers characterise and identify the exact hypertension level to make better decisions for their patients. The diagnosis result will clearly identify the specific hypertension risk level which is to be overlooked. It will represent a formal input and output (risk level and stage) parameters which will provide clinicians to acquire a general understanding hypertension diagnosis.

Hypertension is defined as blood pressure measurement takes to account how much blood is passing through veins and the amount of resistance the blood meets while it's pumping. Although hypertension has been associated with factors such as alcohol consumption, smoking, high BMI and inadequate exercise, research has suggested that the degree of association between socioeconomic status and hypertension varies between males and females. Data on hypertension prevalence for the country are available from the following 1998 South African DHS, which has a prevalence of 21% for both males and females using the 140/90 mmHg threshold.
