# 4.3 Socio-aware recommendation systems

In this section, we present each of the family of socio-aware approaches that adopt elements from SNA for improving the performance, scalability, or other factors of RS operation. We refer to cumulatively as "socio-aware," even though the use of social features can be different, e.g., social structure, social properties and context information.

# 4.3.1 Hyperbolic path-based recommendation system

Nowadays, in many online systems, it is required to add diversity to the users, in order to increase the potential of promoting items with less popularity. This is especially required in very large-scale systems, characterized by bigdata order of magnitude, where the quantity of items available is prohibitive for detailed inspections, and the thematic areas of items are voluminous and diverse as well. The approach presented in this submission aims addressing this aspect of socio-aware recommendations in big network data environments.

The main concept in this approach, which we refer to hyperbolic path-based recommendations (HPR), and it is depicted in Figure 4.3, is to cast the recommendation problem as a (path) routing problem over the item graph, after the latter has been embedded in the hyperbolic space.¹ A fundamental assumption is that knowledge on the interactions among items and users can be represented in graph form. Items corresponds to the nodes of the item graph and the users to the user graph. Links between pairs of users are determined according to a similarity function, which may correspond to any type of social, marketing, or other possible associations between users and could be of potential interest/use in the recommendation of items. Similarly, links between pairs of items are determined according to a different similarity function, which also indicates some type of association of items with respect to users and their preferences. A characteristic example of this association is the co-view metric employed for videos, defined as the ratio of number of times that two videos have been viewed independently and indicates that two videos will be both selected for viewing by a user.
