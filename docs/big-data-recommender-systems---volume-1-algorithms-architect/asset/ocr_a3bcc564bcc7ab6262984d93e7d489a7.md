The graphical structure of CVAE is represented in Figure 6.9 where the top part (the red box) indicates the VAE component and the bottom part represents the PMF component. The integration of VAE with PMF results in two advantages. First, VAE induces stochasticity in the latent Z, which results in better representations of the latent feature space compared to the deterministic ES [35]. Second, the probabilistic VAE allows the model to seamlessly integrate with other generative models such as PMF; this enables Bayesian inference techniques such as MCMC and VI. The graphical process of CVAE is shown in Algorithm 4. For the most part, the algorithm remains similar to CDAE with a major difference in steps 14–19. This block illustrates the sampling process of mean $\mu$ covariance $\Sigma$ and the latent Z that are unique to VAE. For details on the working of VAE, readers are suggested to refer to Kingma et al. [34].

# Algorithm 4: Generative process of CVAE

```
1 for each layer l $\in$ L of VAE do
2   for each column n of the weight matrix $W_l$ do
3     | Draw $W_{l,n} \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \lambda_w^{-1}I_K)$
4   end
5   Draw bias vector $b_l \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \lambda_b^{-1}I_{K_l})$
6   for each row j of $g_l$ do
7     | Draw $g_{l,j} \sim \mathcal{N}(\sigma(g_{l,j}W_l + b_l), \lambda_d^{-1}I_{K_g})$
8   end
9 end
10 Decoder
11 for each item $j \in$ J do
12   Draw $X_j \sim \mathcal{N}(0, \lambda_n^{-1}I_V)$
13 end
14 Encoder
15 for each item $j \in$ J do
16   Draw $\mu_j \sim \mathcal{N}(g_lW_\mu + b_\mu, \lambda_\mu^{-1}I_K)$
17   Draw $\Sigma_j \sim \mathcal{N}(g_lW_\sigma + b_\sigma, \lambda_\sigma^{-1}I_K)$
18   Draw $Z_j \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \Sigma)$
19 end
```
