They counteract information overload with the control of the diffusion process by formulating a model of users' influence over multiple criteria (spatial, demographic, opinion similarity), ranking users based on their influence score, and the network structure, and finally selecting the most highly rated nodes to initiate the temporal process of diffusion. The rank of a node u in a directed network is given by the equation $Rank_{predec}(u) = \sum_{v} I(u,x) + \sum_{v \in N_{out}(u)}} I(v,x)(x,z)$, where $I(u,x)$ is the influence of u on x, where x is the neighbor of u or x is the neighbor set of u. In the case of undirected network, the rank of node u is given by $Rank_{undirected}(u) = \sum_{v} I(u,e) + \sum_{v \in N(u)}} I(v,e)$, where e is an edge between two users.
