Madjid Khalilian1, Mahshid Alsadat Ehsaei1, and Salomeh TaherFard1

In recent times, the recommender system (RS) played a significant role in assisting users select the ideal product from a huge amount of data. The gradually increasing amount of customers, services and online information due to yielding the large data analysis turns into a problem for service RSs. Approaches which are greatly successful cover a variety of recommendation tasks such as video, music, image, books.

The recommendation algorithms require a variety of parameters to propose suggestions for new users, and where is limitations and challenges of the RSs emerge. Recommendation algorithms for unstructured big data and their challenges will be discussed in this chapter.

RS is an innovative technique that has transformed the content-based applications to customer centric ever. This method figures out what the customers exactly want; it can be either data or an item. Feature of collecting and computing information in recommendation techniques provide a better understanding among users and clients [1]. Large amount of data is generated from radio-frequency identification and other sensors. Therefore, big data technique is being used to handle large amount of data in producing recommendations. There are various applications which use Item collaborative filtering (CF). For instance applications in business sections, e-business, recommendation of items in online shopping, recommendation of exciting cites, web browsing, discovering music, movies, etc. Considerable efforts in Collective Intelligence are the root of the RSs. The emergence of RS goes back to the 1990s, during the time when recommendations were only based on the rating structure. Over recent years, numerous ways turn out to recognize user's interest for special items, including tagging, voting, reviewing or even the number of likes users provide. Besides, it can be in a form of a review written in blogs, descriptions about the item or a reply to an online community. In spite of how preferences are communicated or being presented, they should be defined as numerical values as described in [2].
