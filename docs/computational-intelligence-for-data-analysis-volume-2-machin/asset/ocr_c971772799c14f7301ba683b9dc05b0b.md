# Jumper Wires

The female-to-male jumper wires perfectly connect the GPIO pins on the Raspberry Pi directly to the breadboard. The reusable, soft, reliable, and strong jumper wires are easy to detect and so easy to connect and disconnect with the breadboard. These jumper wires are suitable and appropriate for prototyping. Each jumper wire Package involves 40-wires, and each wire is 300 mm long (Fig. 6).

# Breadboard

A breadboard is one of the most important parts of building circuits. A breadboard provides an extension of a single wire. There are a number of holes in the plastic box, arranged in a particular style. A breadboard design contains two types of a section called strips. Two sections are the bus strip and socket strip (Fig. 7).
