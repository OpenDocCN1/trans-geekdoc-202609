# IOMT IN CLOUD

The conventional IoMT can be hard to obtain the interconnectedness among the huge quantity of various healthcare data systems and healthcare institutions, which ends in a separated healthcare service data island; this can create replicated information in the healthcare process, resulting in a big quantity of resource wastage, as well as boom interoperability trouble among clinical records systems. Recently, the application of CC era in IoMT, including its scalability, sharing and excessive reliability, has found the leap ahead improvement in healthcare. Here, we introduce key technology and architecture of the IoMT Cloud, as well as evaluate the security of healthcare information in the cloud.
