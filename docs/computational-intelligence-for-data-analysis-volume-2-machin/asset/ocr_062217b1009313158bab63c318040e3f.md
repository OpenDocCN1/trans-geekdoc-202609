As a result, every component of the analysis can now be represented in terms of data, including at the macro level, logistically, and physiologically; this will undoubtedly be useful in forecasting the behavior of this current pandemic or unknown future pandemics. It has been documented that AI techniques have the potential to be applied to COVID-19 genome sequences to retrieve valuable info about the organism. When it comes to pattern analysis, effective computer-based tools are available that allow people, especially bioinformaticians, to examine difficult genetic and genomic data sets that are large and complex. Algorithms have been devised for the analysis of mutations in genome sequences, which are used to locate sites in genome sequences where the nucleotide bases change and quantify the mutation rate.
