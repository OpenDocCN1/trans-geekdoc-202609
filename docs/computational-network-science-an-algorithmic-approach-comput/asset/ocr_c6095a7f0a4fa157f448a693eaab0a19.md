# 1.7 CONCLUSIONS

Networks are abundantly around us. They are man-made or naturally occur. They are implicit, hidden, explicit, or articulated. They might be tangible and objectively quantified, or they might be subjective and difficult to quantify. They all tend to change in time, which is the subject of our future chapters on network dynamics.
