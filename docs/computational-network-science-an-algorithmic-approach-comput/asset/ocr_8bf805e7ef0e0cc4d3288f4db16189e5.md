In more complex markets, there are intermediaries called *traders* that facilitate an interface between buyers and sellers. The role of traders in a market for agricultural goods between local producers and consumers is illustrated here in Chapter 11 of Easley and Klein (2010) and we summarize here. The flow of goods from sellers to buyers is determined by a game in which traders set prices, and then sellers react to these prices. Each trader $t$ *offers* a bid price to seller to whom or she is connected, denoted by $b_{ti}$. This bid price is an offer by $t$ to *buy* $i$'s copy of the good at a value of $b_{ti}$. Each trader $t$ *offers* an ask price to buyer $j$ to whom or she is connected. This ask price is denoted by $a_{tj}$, and is an offer by $t$ to *sell* the copy of the good to buyer $j$ at a value of $a_{tj}$. Each trader posts bid prices to the sellers to whom or she is connected, and ask prices to the buyers to whom or she is connected. Once traders announce prices, each seller and buyer chooses at most one trader to deal with. Each seller sells his or her copy of the good to the trader he she selects, or keeps his or her copy of the good if he chooses not to sell it. Each buyer purchases a copy of the good from the trader he she selects, or receives no copy of the good if he does not select a trader. This determines a flow of goods from sellers, through traders, to buyers. There are incentives for a trader not to produce bid and ask prices that cause more buyers than sellers to accept his or her offers. There are also incentives for a trader not to be caught in the reverse, with more sellers than buyers accepting his or her offers. A trader's payoff is the profit he makes from all of his or her transactions. It is the sum of the sum of the bid prices of his or her accepted offers to sellers, minus the sum of the bid prices of his or her accepted offers to sellers. For a seller $i$, the payoff from selecting trader $t$ is $b_{ti}$ while the payoff from selecting no trader is $v$. If the exchange is actualized, the seller receives $b_{ti}$ units of money.
