Another type of intermediary is when a number of agents with low SC themselves are identified with a representative broker whose task is to secure access to a specific resource on their behalf. Between agents and their representative must be high. The representative must strive to build strong SC to targeted resource owners in order to have the capacity to deliver resource access to its constituents. The representative agent must continually review resource policies of resource owners and, when applicable, policies of coordinators. In very large NO, once coordinators and representative broker agents are identified, majority of interactions are (a) between agents and representatives, and (b) between coordinators and resource owners. The next section offers a rudimentary high-level outline for resource access using SC. Details are omitted for brevity and many variations are planned for future work.
