Unlike physical data of natural science, social science data are constructed by meanings, motives, definitions, and classifications. This means that the production of social science data involves a process of interpretation. During the interpretation processes, social scientists construct distinct types of data. Each data type requires appropriate distinct methods of analysis. The principal types of data include *attribute data* and *relational data.*
