Twitter can be used to work semi-anonymously with others. Twitter’s hashtag (i.e., #) is a feature for labeling a topic. Anyone may introduce or reuse a hashtag to attract attention. For example, #flight1549 added to a tweet labels the tweet about “flight1549.” This hashtag labeling facilitates search related to specific topics. Individuals who use specific hashtags form an implicit network in the context of their hashtags. This feature has been used for commercial marketing and anonymous coordination over social actions. The range of potential uses for hashtags is enormous, and they have been adopted by other SNSs as Facebook. On the one hand, Twitter can be used for potential organizations of crime or dissent. On the other hand, it can be used to predict and mitigate violations of law enforcement. Since Twitter provides democratization of opinion sharing and equal access for dissemination, it is seen as a social equalizer and as much it may be feared by repressive systems (e.g., government regimes). Twitter’s social authority is composed of three components: (1) the retweet rate of users’ last hundred tweets, and (2) the retweet-based model trained on users’ profile data. Tagging someone shows the Twitter id to more people, where direct messaging just puts spam in their inbox, which is generally undesirable. Websites, such as Klout.com, gauge the influence you have, for example, how active you are and how much you have been tagged on Twitter. Twitter’s lists are ways to organize others into groups. When you click on a list, you will retrieve a stream of tweets from all users included in that group. As a rule of thumb, if you want to develop relationships with Twitter, you should read other tweets, retweet good contents, and stay on top of keywords and interests that you follow. The same advice applies if you want to get retweeted.

The main tool for LinkedIn’s connections is to link people, who would like to support one another (i.e., connections). LinkedIn allows people to conduct a weak form of endorsement in regards to specific skills. This creates stronger directional links from endorsers to endorsees.
