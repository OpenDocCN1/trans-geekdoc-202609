Another perspective on SC is in the form of social manipulation ability to mobilize or constrain resources, which location is less pertinent. Nodes that possess resources themselves or are near resource sources may enact policies that give away or hoard (i.e., share or deny) resources to others. SC for the owners of capital is their manipulation capacity over access to their resources. For nonowners of capital, SC in this context is their ability to successfully deal with capital owners to provide them with access to the resources. Setting and changing exchange policies among nodes of the network is a type of social manipulation over resources. Trade policies among countries exemplify this type of resource manipulation. There are a few main elements of the SC, which have a proportional relationship to one another.

Topologically speaking, high bonding rates provide more opportunities for interaction and growth of SC. However, network structure itself is inadequate for determination of SC. We must examine the contents of interaction and dispositions that create social flows that attract or repel individuals. At the level of a single link, the nature of social flow in the link leads to accumulation of SC. Social flows can be benevolent and positive or negative and lack of benevolence. Positive social flow is an interaction that embodies friendship and sharing congruent positions on topics of interest. Whereas positive flow leads to net gain in SC, negative flow leads to loss of SC. Apart from social flow, dyadic ties harbor or promote ٹرسٹ (Fukuyama, 1995). Trust supports SC, whereas distrust erodes SC. If the topic of interaction between the pair is of mutual interest, that link positively contributes to SC. In sum, flow, trust, and link are link attributes that are proportional determinants for social influence.

Definition (link influence): Social influence in a link is measured by (a) accumulation of positive values of social flow, (b) trust, and (c) abundance of communication over a common topic. For simplicity, we suggest treating all three attributes equally.
