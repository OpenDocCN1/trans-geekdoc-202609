Accessing economic goods is commonly through buying, selling, and trader intermediaries that are discussed in Chapter 9. Economic values are influenced by forming trade blocs (e.g., North America, European Union, and Asia Pacific). Power can be exerted over economic capital positively via trade treaties or negatively via trade sanctions and trade boycotts. Economic control can be established via monopolies and monopolies. Physical resources that are nonlocal can be accessed via forming bilateral friendships. Globally, commonly desired valuable physical resources (e.g., oil or drugs) can be accessed, influenced, and even controlled via formation of coalitions (eg. cartels).
