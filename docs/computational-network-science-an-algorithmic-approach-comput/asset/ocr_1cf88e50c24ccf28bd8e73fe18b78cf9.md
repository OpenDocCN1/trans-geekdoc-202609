visualizing metaroles overlaid over a NO is very enlightening and aids in analysis of dynamic processes operating inside a NO. Webster's characterization of NOs is certainly rich and articulates many interesting features of NOs. It can be implemented in a computational decision aid. However, determining measures of social forces cannot be automated and remains subjective, which must be subjectively entered. This draw-backs its wider applicability and proliferation of his theory.

# 11.1 CONCLUSION

NOs are emerging groups that use the Internet including social media to facilitate collaboration. This has provided serendipitous and emergent interaction among individuals that exceeds traditional organizational framework. This is a nascent aura that requires attention. We envision this technology to transform information society into cohesive wholes.
