# 4.1 CONCLUSION

Balance theory has been an important sociological phenomenon applied to graphs. It has been widely studied in public policy research (O’Toole, 1997; Piqueri and Hickman, 2006; Doreian and Mrvar, 2009; Ilany et al., 2013). Applications and extensions of this theory remain an active research topic in graph theory as well as mathematical sociology (Doreian and Mrvar, 2009).
