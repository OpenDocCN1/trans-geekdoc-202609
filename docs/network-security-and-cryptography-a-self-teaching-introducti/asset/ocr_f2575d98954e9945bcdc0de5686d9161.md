A random walk on $X$ corresponds to a sequence of states, for each step of the walk. At each step, the walk switches from its current state to a new state or remains at the current state. Thus, Random walks constitute a random process consisting of a sequence of discrete steps of fixed length.

Random walks are usually *Markovian*, which means the transition at each step is independent of the previous steps and depends only on the current state. Although random walks are not limited to one-dimensional problems, the one-dimensional random walk is one of the simplest stochastic processes and can be used to model many gambling games.
