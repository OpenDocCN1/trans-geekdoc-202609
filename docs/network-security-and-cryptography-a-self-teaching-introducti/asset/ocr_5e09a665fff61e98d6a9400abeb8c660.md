The 512-bit word is divided into sixteen 32-bit words, which form $W[0]...W[15]$. A word is used in the first 16 steps of round 1. The remaining words for the remaining 64 steps are derived as follows:

$W[t] = S^1 (W[t-16] \oplus W[t-14] \oplus W[t-8] \oplus W[t-3])$ where $\oplus$: XOR.

The first-input, after $CV_q$, is added to the fourth round output, to produce the final output $CV_{q+1}$. The addition is performed modulo $2^{32}$, independently for each of the five words ($A, B, C, D, E$).
