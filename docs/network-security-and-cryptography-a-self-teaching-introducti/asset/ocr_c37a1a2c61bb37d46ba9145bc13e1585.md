Also, the two integers $a$ and $(n-a)$ will be distinct, because $a=n-a$ implies $n=2a$, which is not possible since $GCD(a,n)=1$. Therefore, for $n>2$ there will be pairs of integers less than $n$, thus, $\phi(n)$ will have even value.
