**For the transformation at round $i$ ($1 \le i \le 16$) we do the following steps:**

+   - XOR difference at the input of round $i = \Delta m_{i-1} \parallel \Delta m_i$

+   - XOR difference at the output of round $i = \Delta m_i \parallel \Delta m_{i+1}$ where $\Delta m_{i+1} = \Delta m_{i-1} \oplus F(\Delta m_i, K_i)$

+   - Thus, Round i transforms the XOR difference from $\Delta m_{i-1} \parallel \\Delta m_i$ to $\Delta m_i \parallel \\Delta m_{i+1}$

+   - The transformation at Round $i$ is a function of sub-key $K_i$

+   - The cryptanalyst will study the changes in the XOR differences at the outputs of all rounds.

+   - Since these XOR changes are a function of the sub-keys used in the successive rounds, this information is used to assign probabilities to the sub-key bits.
