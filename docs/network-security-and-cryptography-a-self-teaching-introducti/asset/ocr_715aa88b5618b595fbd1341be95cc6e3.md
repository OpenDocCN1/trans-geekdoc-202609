# 14.4.6 New Challenges Created by the IoT:

+   - Security: Prevent attackers and hackers from accessing the IoT

+   - Privacy: protect identity and privacy data from attackers and hacker access of the IoT

+   - Interoperability and standards: ensure that devices communicate securely by IoT manufacturers and ASP developers

+   - Legal and regulatory compliance: contribute toward legal, tax, and regulatory requirements regarding IoT-related business transactions that involve for goods and services by the international, federal, and state levels

+   - E-commerce and economic development issues: connectivity and information sharing to be deployed globally by IoT and the economic rules of engagement for connecting business on the World Wide Web.

# 14.4.7 Security Requirements of the IoT:

+   - Confidentiality: Ensuring that only authorized users access the data and information

+   - Integrity: Ensuring completeness, accuracy, and absence of unauthorized data manipulation

+   - Availability: Ensuring that all system services are available, when requested by an authorized user

+   - Accountability: An ability of a system to hold users responsible for their actions and operations

+   - Auditability: An ability of a system to conduct persistent monitoring of all actions and operations

+   - Trustworthiness: An ability of a system to verify an accurate identity and establish trust in a third party

+   - Non-repudiation: An ability of a system to confirm occurrence/non-occurrence of an action and an operation

+   - Privacy: Ensuring that the system obeys privacy policies and enabling individuals to control their personal data and information
