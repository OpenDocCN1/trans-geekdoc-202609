# 4.4 INTRODUCTION TO PRIMES AND CO-PRIMES

# 4.4.1 Prime Numbers

Any positive integer $p > 1$ is said to be prime if its divisors are $\pm p$ and $\pm I$. For example, $2,3,5,7,11,13,17, ...$

# 4.4.2 Co-Prime Numbers or Relatively Prime Numbers

Two integers $a$ and $b$ are said to be relatively prime (or co-primes) if the Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) of the two numbers is equal to 1, that is, $GCD(a,b)=1$. For example, 8 and $15$ are relatively prime numbers, though none of them is a prime number.

# 4.4.2.1 Special Cases of Co-Primes

# Integer 0

Integer 0 is relatively prime to integer 1, since $GCD(0, 1) = 1$. But $0$ is not relatively prime to any integer other than 1, since $GCD(0, N) = N$.

# Integer 1

Integer 1 is relatively prime to all integers $N \geq 0$, since $GCD(1, N) = 1$.
