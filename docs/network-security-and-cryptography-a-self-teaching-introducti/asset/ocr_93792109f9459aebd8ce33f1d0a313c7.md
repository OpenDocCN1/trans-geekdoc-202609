**Encryption:** $C = KP \text{ mod } 26 = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 \ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \times \text{mod } 26$

The “CD” is transmitted as “NS” after encryption.

**Decryption:** Determine the Decryption Key $K^{-1}$,

$K^{-1} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -3 \ -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 4 & -3 \ -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} -4 & 3 \ -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix}\n$$(2\times4)-(3\times3) = -1$

$P = K^{-1}C \text{ mod } 26 = \begin{bmatrix} -4 & 3 \ 3 & -2 \end{bmatrix} \times \begin{bmatrix} 13 & "C" \ 14 & \end{bmatrix} \text{mod } 26\n$$= \begin{bmatrix} 2 \ 3 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} "C" \ "D" \end{bmatrix}$

Thus, after decryption at the recipient end, the original plaintext “CD” is recovered.
