
我们自[第6章](#chapter6.xhtml)起一直在讨论提升建模。这套提升技术处理的是我们并不知道*确切地*谁会对处理产生反应这种情况，也就是说，我们只能通过收集大量数据并利用处理组与对照组的响应率差异来进行推断。简而言之，这要求我们对处理组和对照组的数据进行聚合层面、群体层面或子子子群体层面（通过预测建模）的分析，粒度细到*几乎*达到个体层面。然而，如果我们实际上确切知道谁对处理产生了反应呢？那将是一个简单得多的情况，只需要我们开发一个常规的监督学习模型来预测直接响应（或销售额）作为协变量的函数，而无需担心对照组。真的就这么简单吗？让我们考虑直接响应数据可用的两种情况。

### 零售商优惠券
想象零售商（或餐厅）经常使用优惠券或某种形式的促销代码来吸引客户的常见情况。一种典型方式是在明信片上附带优惠券。如果客户决定将其用于购买，明信片将被扫描，“直接响应”实际上会被捕获。因此，直接响应数据可以确切地说明谁对优惠券做出了反应。然而，没有优惠券的人（在第269页对照组）也可能会从零售商处购买东西。此外，治疗组中的一些人（收到带优惠券明信片的人）可能会在不使用优惠券的情况下购买——他们可能尽管接受了处理却忘记使用它，或者根本没有看到明信片。

### 电子邮件点击浏览
另一个例子是通过电子邮件营销活动在线销售零售产品。收到带有特定URL链接（或二维码）的电子邮件的人属于处理组。处理组中的客户可能会点击链接（“点击浏览”）来购买产品。但一些收到电子邮件的客户也可能从通用网站而不是点击电子邮件中的链接购买该产品。同样，未收到电子邮件的人（对照组）也可以从通用网站购买产品。这与优惠券的零售商示例类似。也就是说，可以开发一个以协变量为函数的点击浏览直接响应模型，也可以利用处理组和对照组数据开发提升模型。我们应该做哪一个？为什么不两者都做呢？

### 9.5.1 响应概率上的提升
只考虑[图9.6](#chapter9.xhtml#fig9_6)中的蓝色方框。我们首先将客户数据分为处理组和对照组。在处理组（`T`）中，有些人直接响应（`D`），有些人没有（`D^c`）。对于直接响应的人（`D`），他们已经响应（`R`）。对于未直接响应的人（`D^c`），例如未使用优惠券的人，有些人可能仍然会购买（`R`），而其他人则不会（`N`）。同样，在对照组（`C`）中，有些人可能会购买（响应，`R`），而其他人则不会（`N`）。

![图9.6 直接响应和响应数据的树状图。⏎](images/fig9_6.jpg)

图9.6 直接响应和响应数据的树状图。 [⏎](#chapter9.xhtml#b9fig9_6)

我们现在将[图9.5](#chapter9.xhtml#fig9_5a)中的树状图用数学方法表达出来。 使用我们对协变量的提升函数的记号：

`Z(x) = P(R|T,x) – P(R|C,x)`, where `R` = event of response.
`= P(R|T,D,x)P(D|T,x) + P(R|T,D^c,x)(1−P(D|T,x)) – P(R|C,x)`,
following the direct and no direct branches under Treatment (`T`) in [图9.6](#chapter9.xhtml#fig9_6), where `D` = direct response and `D^c` = no direct response, and applying the standard rules of conditional probability
`= P(D|T,x) + P(R|T,D^c,x)(1−P(D|T,x)) – P(R|C,x)`,
(9.9)
since `P(R|T,D,x) = 1`, that is, response (purchase) probability is 100% if the coupon is actually used.

第270页 Equation (9.8) has the following probabilities to be modeled:
1. `P(D|T,x)` = Direct response probability as a function of covariates for the treatment group – this can be developed using regular supervised learning for binary outcomes (note that direct response is only available in the treatment group in this model),
2. `P(R|T,D^c,x)` = Treatment response probability among those who did not use the coupon, as a function of covariates (i.e., the directly responded individuals would need to be *排除* from modeling for this probability), and
3. `P(R|C,x)` = Control response probability as another function of covariates.

Note that the probabilities in (2) and (3) above can be handled by any uplift modeling technique that provides *单独* estimates from the treatment and control response models, respectively, that is, the Two Model Approach or the Treatment Dummy Approach but not the Four Quadrant Model

Equation (9.9) can also be rearranged as:
`= P(D|T,x)[1−P[R|T,D^c,x]] + [P[R|T,D^c,x]−P[R|C,x]]`.
(9.10)

第271页 In Eqn. (9.10), the component in the first pair of parentheses is the difference in response probability between a direct responder (which is always 1.0) and someone in the treatment group who did not use a coupon. The first component, `P(D|T,x)[1−P[R|T,D^c,x]]`, is always ≥ 0. The second component, `[P[R|T,D^c,x]−P[R|C,x]]`, measures the difference in response probability between the treatment group (without using the coupon to directly respond) and the control group. The second component may not necessarily be ≥ 0. Note that Eqns. (9.9) and (9.10) are derived from the fundamental theory of conditional probability and do not require any statistical model assumptions. Modeling the various probability components in those equations as functions of covariates, however, does require empirical data and statistical/data mining models.

### Example 9.3: 直接响应模型与提升模型的整合
在此模拟示例中，一家服装零售商有兴趣通过向合适的客户邮寄优惠券来最大化其产品的响应（购买）率。零售商存储每个客户的历史数据如下：
* `Recency`: number of months ago for its most recent purchase (0, 1, 2, …, 12)
* `Frequency`: number of times purchases were made in the past year (0, 1, …)
* `Spent`: average spent amount on past purchases
* `Demographics`: `age`, `income`

在我们的模拟中，使用优惠券的直接响应是`recency`的函数，而响应率（如果未使用优惠券）是`age`和`frequency`的函数。 使用 Eqn. (9.8)，我们需要估计：
* `Direct Response Model`: Model 1: `P(D|T,x)`, Direct response probability as a function of covariates (where `recency` is the only covariate used in the actual theoretical model), developed using regular logistic regression;
* `Uplift Model`: Model 2: `P(R|T,D^c,x)` and Model 3: `P(R|C,x)`, response probabilities in the treatment group (when direct response is not used) and control groups, respectively, both as a function of `age`, amount spent, and `frequency` in the actual models; estimated using a Treatment Dummy variable approach (from [第6章](#chapter6.xhtml) or [Lo 2002](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_25)).
