# USE OF THE MAIL DROP & FOOD STORES

The MST is so long that it would be extremely difficult to carry enough food to hike the entire trail without any resupply. There are several small post offices, grocery stores, park offices and other facilities within hiking range of the MST. A mail drop of nonishable food should be addressed to:

Your Name
c/o General Delivery
Post Office Name, PA Zip Code

and marked:
HOLD FOR MID STATE TRAIL HIKER

Allow plenty of time for your mail drop to arrive before you do. It wouldn't hurt to write or call the postmaster involved ahead of time. REMEMBER: POST OFFICES ARE CLOSED ON SUNDAYS AND FREQUENTLY ON SATURDAYS AS WELL. AS OF 2012, MANY RURAL POST OFFICES HAVE REDUCED THEIR HOURS TO AS LITTLE AS 30 MINUTES PER DAY. Please give a park office advance notice if you are going to send them a food drop. Park offices are generally open on weekends in summer. Even if a park office is closed you can wait for the park ranger to show up and retrieve your package. Please visit www.hike-mst.org for a comprehensive and up-to-date list of mail drops, food stores, and other services along the Mid State Trail.
