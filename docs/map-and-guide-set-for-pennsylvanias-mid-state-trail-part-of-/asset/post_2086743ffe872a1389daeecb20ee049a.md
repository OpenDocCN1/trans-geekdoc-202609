# R. B. WINTER TO RAVENSBURG
BALD EAGLE AND TIADAGTON STATE FORESTS

Raymond B. Winter State Park is just east of the Centre-Union County line on PA 192. It is 27 km west of Lewisburg. Ravensburg State Park is on PA 880 about 5 km north of Exit 192 (near the village of Carroll, signed “Jersey Shore” on I-80 and south of Rauchtown). Check with the R. B. Winter Park office after overnight parking at both parks (570) 966-1455. Overnight parking at Ravensburg State Park is not recommended.

Primitive camping is available along Spruce Run, near Rendezvous Spring, in Fourth Gap on Nittany Mountain, near Gas Line Spring, White Deer Creek, and along the lower Dunbar Trail, Intermediate access is available at R. B. Winter and tent-only area with showers at Ravensburg. Section 12 was opened in 1981 has been relocated four times and severed twice.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

The Mid State Trail follows Bake Oven ridge to Sand Mountain when it turns north and crosses Spruce Run and Chestnut Flat before climbing over Naked Mountain. Next it crosses White Deer Creek and Nittany Mountain. North of I-80 the MST continues across the landscape by climbing over Tea Knob, Last Ridge and Brushy Ridge. In White Deer Hole the trail turns west and climbs over Big Mountain before descending to Sand Flat and then to PA 880 and Ravensburg State Park.
