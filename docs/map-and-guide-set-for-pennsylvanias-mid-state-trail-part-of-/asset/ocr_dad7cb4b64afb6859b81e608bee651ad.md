# SECTION 14: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 150 TO PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 44
WEST BRANCH SUSQUEHANNA TO RAMSEY
TIADAGHTON STATE FOREST

Section 14 begins on PA 150 across from the Susque Valley Animal Hospital between Chatham Run and Charlton. It ends on PA 44 on the outskirts of Ramsey. There is limited day parking along PA 44 at this point but do not block access to Pine Creek.

Features of this section are the flagship store in Woolrich (fill pack) and the crossing of Pine Creek between Camp Kline and Ramsey on the Pine Creek Rail Trail bridge. The only shelters along MST are on this section at Woolrich Park and Bonnell Flats.

There is intermediate access to section 14 on the Gravel Hill, Mill, Big Spring and Bull Run roads. Section 14 was opened in 1983 and relocated to the rail trail bridge in 2001.

Caution! This section is underlain by the gas-rich Marcellus Shale formation. Interruptions in the footpath are possible due to increasing gas production activity. Please check the trail conditions on http://www.hike-mst.org before you hike.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

The Mid State Trail follows high ground east of Chatham Run to Gravel Hill Road. It then follows Park Ave. through Woolrich, passing the flagship store. It crosses on open field before escaping to the woods. It climbs the Allegheny Front on private land before entering Tiadaghton State Forest.
