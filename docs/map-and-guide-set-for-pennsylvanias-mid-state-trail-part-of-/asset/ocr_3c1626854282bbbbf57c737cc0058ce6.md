# SECTION E: JACKSON TRAIL
PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 26 TO MID STATE TRAIL
ROTHROCK STATE FOREST

The Jackson Trail provides a scenic shortcut for hikers who do not need water or a place to camp. The Jackson Trail is very rocky as it follows the crest of Tussey Ridge from the Jo Hays Vista on PA 26, 3.2 km above Pine Grove Mills, to where the Mid State Trail climbs back out of Shaver Creek Valley. There is no intermediate access and there are no springs or streams. The Jackson Trail was opened in 1979

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

The Jackson Trial follows the crest of Tussey Ridge northeast from Pennsylvania route 26 to a junction with the main trail. There are few changes in elevation but the footway is frequently very rocky. There are many views to both sides of the ridge, but no springs or streams. Take your time and stop to enjoy the views.
