## SECTION 8: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 26 TO DETWEILER JUNCTION
## JO HAYS VISTA TO DETWEILER JUNCTION WITH GREENWOOD SPUR OF THE MST AND GREAT EASTERN TRAIL IN PENNSYLVANIA ROTHROCK STATE FOREST

The Jo Hays Vista on PA 26 is 3.2 km uphill from the intersection with PA 45 at the flashing traffic signal in Pine Grove Mills. There is ample parking at the vista.

Detweiler junction is reached by descending on the MST .28 km from the switchback intersection of Bear Meadows Rd. and gated Detweiler Rd. (sec.8 NB 24.05 km). There is limited parking at this location which can be accessed by driving 11.75 km south on Bear Meadows Rd. from US 322 east of Boalsburg or by driving 3.06 km north on Bear Meadows Rd. from Stone Creek Rd. just west of Alan Seeger picnic area.

Intermediate access points are Laurel Run, Bear Gap and Bear Meadows roads . This section has a fairly good supply of springs and there is primitive camping at Beaver Pond and elsewhere in Shaver Creek Valley. Primitive camping is also available on Musser Trail, San Spring Loop , Gettysburg trail, and in Detweiler Valley. Features of this section are Little Flat Fire Tower, Thickhead Wild Area, Indian Wells, and the virgin timber in Detweiler Natural Area.

This section was opened in 1972 and reached its present alignment ten relocations later.

## GENERAL DESCRIPTION

From PA 26 the trail descends the southeast side of Tussey Mountain into Shavers Creek Valley and the former Beaver Pond Recreation Area. It then proceeds upstream climbs Tussey Mountain and turns east. After passing the Musser Trail the MST continues on Skyline Trail. The MST proceeds eastward along the ridge top to Little Flat Fire Tower where it turns southwest and enters the Thickhead Wild Area. It follows the ridge of Fourth Mountain to its junction with Thickhead Mountain at Big Flat. After following Thickhead, or Fifth Mountain, across Gettys Gap it descends to Detweiler Valley and a junction is also the meeting point of the east and west routes of the Great Eastern Trail (GET) in Pennsylvania. Hiking south on the Great Eastern Trail (GET) from the junction with the Greenwood Spur below Bear Meadows Meadows leads to the northern trailhead of the Standing Stone Trail at Greenwood Furnace State Park.
