# SECTION 19: US ROUTE 6 TO PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 287
WHITNEYVILLE TO HAMMOND
PRIVATE LAND, HILLS CREEK STATE PARK, STATE GAME LAND 37, TIOGA-HAMMOND LAKES

Section 19 begins at US 6 and ends at the second crossing of PA 287.

Features include pastoral countryside, glacial remnants, a wetland and a lake. Section 19 was opened from Hills Creek State Park to Ives Run Recreation Area in 2003, and extended south to US 6 in 2008 and 2009.

Caution! This section is underlain by the gas-rich Marcellus Shale formation. Interruptions in the footpath are possible due to increasing gas production activity. Please check the trail conditions on http://www.hike-mst.org before you hike.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

MST winds through rolling farmland to Hills Creek State Park, crossover the dam and alongside the lake. From the north end of Hills Creek Lake MST climbs a ridge of glacial drift and crosses the State Park campground. A short connecting stretch of township roads leads to SGL 37 where MST ascends a ridgeline leading to the high plateau, descends the headwaters of Stephen Run, ascends a higher point on the plateau, then descends the east fork and main stem of Ives Run to Hammond Lake. MST ascends through abandoned farmland across PA 287.
