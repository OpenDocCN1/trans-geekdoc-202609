# PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 26 TO DETWEILER JUNCTION
(Jo Hays Vista to Detweiler Junction) Detailed Description, Map

NB: MST crosses PA 26 and turns sharply to the right on the Pine Grove Trail and descends Tussey Mtn. into Shaver Creek Valley. The Jackson Trail continues northeast on the ridge top and provides a shortcut for hikers who do not need water or a place to camp. Details for hiking the Jackson Trail can be found in Section E of this guidebook.

SB: Reach PA 26 near junction with Jackson Trail from right. Jo Hays vista is on far side of highway

| 1.24 | Junction with yellow blazed Ironstone Trail. The Ironstone Loop is described in Section B. | 23.09 |

NB: MST turns left on an old charcoal road and passes through the former Beaver Pond Recreation Area. Most of the campsites are on the high ground to the left of the trail. There are several intermittent springs among those sites.

SB: MST turns right on Pine Grove Trail, climbing Tussey Ridge.

NB=North bound hiking, SB=South bound hiking
