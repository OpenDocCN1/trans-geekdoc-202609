# SECTION 7: US ROUTE 22 TO PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE
26

# LOWER TRAIL TO JO HAYS VISTA
ROTHROCK STATE FOREST

The Alfarata Mid State Trailhead is located at the junction of US 22 and Alexandria Rd.(SR 4014) at the west end of the bridge over the Frankstown Branch of the Juniata River, about 1.6 km east of Water Street. The northern trailhead of the Lower Trail, with ample parking, is about 300 m east on the Alexandria Rd.. Jo Hays Vista is located on PA 26 about 3.2 km from the flashing traffic light (junction of routes PA 26 and PA 45) in Pine Grove Mills. Section 7 can also be reached by the yellow blazed Yellow Arrow - Rainbow side trails starting from a gravel parking area (former Colerain Picnic Area), which is 3.1 km northeast of Spruce Creek Village at the junction of PA 45 and Colerain Rd.. Principle features of this section are the many views from Tussey Ridge.

Intermediate access points are the Little Juniata Natural Area parking lot, Colerain Road and Pennsylvania Furnace Road. Primitive camping along the trail is available at several sites, all dry, identified in the detailed trail data.

Water is very scarce on this section. Springs are available on the bench partway down the northwest side of Tussey Ridge. Blazed state trails lead to some of these springs but others are reached only by unmarked trails. Section 7 was opened in 1972 and has since been relocated or extended nine times.
