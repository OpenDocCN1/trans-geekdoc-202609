After passing the Musser Trail the MST continues on Skyline Trail. The MST proceeds eastward along the ridge top to Little Flat Fire Tower where it turns southwest and enters the Thickhead Wild Area. It follows the ridge of Fourth Mountain to its junction with Thickhead Mountain at Big Flat. After following Thickhead ,or Fifth Mountain, across Gettys Gap it descends to Detweiler Valley and a junction is also the meeting point of the east and west routes of the Great Eastern Trail (GET) in Pennsylvania. Hiking south on the Great Eastern Trail (GET) from the junction with the Greenwood Spur below Bear Meadows Meadows. leads to the northern trailhead of the Standing Stone Trail at Greenwood Furnace State Park.
