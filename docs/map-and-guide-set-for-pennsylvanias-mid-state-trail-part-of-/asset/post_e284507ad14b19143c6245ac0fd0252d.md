
# HANDICAPPED ACCESS

Most of the Mid State Trail is an old fashioned wilderness footpath but there is one section suited for wheelchair access. This is the Penn Creek Path from near Poe Paddy State Park to Cherry Run. This is a former railroad grade and the bridge over Penn Creek has been decked and a smooth stairway built through the tunnel under Paddy Mountain. It parallels Penn Creek for 4 km with repeated views of this trout stream. Penn Creek Path is closed to all motor vehicles but is open to horses and bikes. Since it is short and flat it is of little interest to mountain bikes and is used mostly by family types. Many horses refuse to cross the old railroad bridge when blindfolded so horse use is minimal. Section 6, the Lower Trail, is entirely suitable for wheelchair access. Also, the Moccassin Trail/MST on the north shore of Cowanesque Lake, just south of the NY border, is suitable for backcountry wheelchairs for 6 km.

# ADVERSE CONDITIONS AND TRAIL DAMAGE

On the trail if you encounter horses, bikes, ATVs or industrial disruptions such as a pipeline crossing the path, a gas company representative, even just horse tracks or dung, on the trail report the incident to the applicable volunteer Regional Manager. Contact information is available on the MSTA web site: www.hike-mst.org. You may also use on the Trail Damage or Illegal Incursion Report Form from the Keystone Trails Association web site: www.kta-hike.org. Do not confront the possible illegal user or other individual, as are likely better armed than you, or may be the landowner, or both. Please report the situation to the volunteer Regional Manager. Please wait a couple of days at least for our volunteers to investigate the situation before spewing about the situation on your favorite web forum.
