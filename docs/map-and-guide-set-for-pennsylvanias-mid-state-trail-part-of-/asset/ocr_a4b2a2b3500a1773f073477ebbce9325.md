# SECTION 20: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 287 TO NY BOR-\NDER HAMMOND LAKE TO NY BORDER
PRIVATE LAND, COWANESQUE LAKE

Section 20 begins on PA Route 287 in front of U.S. Army Corps of
Engineers Ives Recreation Area on Hammond Lake between
Middlebury Center and Tioga, and ends at the New York State line
west of Lawrenceville, PA.

Features include pastoral views, waterfalls, birdwatching hot spots,
 a ghost town. Section 20 opened by stages from 2002 to 2009. During
scheduled hunting season closures, road detours will add either 1.1 km
or 4.4 km to the length of the section between Hammond Lake and
Tioga Heritage Campground. Check detailed trail description care-
fully, and also check hike-mst.org for updates.

Caution! This section is underlain by the gas-rich Marcellus for-
ation. Interruptions in the footpath are possible due to increasing
gas production activity. Please check the trail conditions on http://
www.hike-mst.org before you hike.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

into privately owned woodlots. A peak offers sweeping views of Elk-
horn Creek valley. MST then descends to Elkhorn Creek, passing a
fall. MST then ascends through both abandoned and well-used
