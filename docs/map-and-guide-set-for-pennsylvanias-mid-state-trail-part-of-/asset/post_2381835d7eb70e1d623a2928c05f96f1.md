# SECTION 20: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 287 TO HAMMOND LAKE TO BORDER COWANESQUE LAKE

Section 20 begins on PA Route 287 in front of U.S. Army Corps Engineers Ives Recreation Area on Hammond Lake between Middlebury Center and Tioga, and ends at the New York State line west of Lawrenceville, PA. Features include pastoral views, waterfalls, birdwatching hot spots, a ghost town. Section 20 opened by stages from 2002 to 2009. During scheduled hunting closures, road detours will add either 1.1 km or 4.4 km to the length of the section between Hammond Lake and Tioga Heritage Campground. Check detailed trail description, and also check hike-mst.org for updates.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

MST ascends through abandoned farmland across PA 287 into privately owned woodlots. A peak offers sweeping views of Elkhorn Creek valley. MST then descends to Elkhorn Creek, passing a fall. MST then ascends through both abandoned and well-used pastures to a high point with south then east views, past a hunting preserve and through woods and fields on private land, and then follows a narrow dirt road descending Baldwin Creek to Cowanesque Lake. MST circumnigates the south shore, head, and north shore of this reservoir on old roads, new trails, and two PENNDOT bridges, passing the former and present sites of Nelson village. A short side trail leads to Nelson Falls on Cowanesque River. After this extended passage MST leaves the lake and heads up a gravel road to the New York border.
