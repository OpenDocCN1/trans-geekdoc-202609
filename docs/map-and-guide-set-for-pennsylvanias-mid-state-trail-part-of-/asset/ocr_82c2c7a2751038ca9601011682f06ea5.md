# SECTION 11: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 45 TO PENNSYYL-
ANIA ROUTE 192
HAIRY JOHN'S TO R. B. WINTER
BALD EAGLE STATE FOREST

R. B. Winter State Park is just east of the Centre-Union County line on PA 192. It is 27 km west of Lewisburg. Check in with the park office for overnight parking at R. B. Winter (570) 966-1455.

Hairy John's picnic area is midway between Woodward and Laurelton Village on PA 45. Overnight parking at Hairy John's is not recommended.

Primitive camping along the trail is available near Buffalo and Pine Creeks, Panther Run and in Brush Hollow. Camping is prohibited in the Hook Natural Area to the east of Kessler Trail between Pine Creek and Negro Hollow Roads. Principle feature of section 11 is the Hook Natural Area, largest in the state.

There is intermediate access on Stony Run, Pine Creek and Stover Gap Roads.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

The trail climbs Winkelblech Mountain and then turns east crossing Sheesley Run and climbing over Buffalo Mountain. It then descends Sharpback Mountain, crosses Pine Creek and climbs Buck Ridge. It continues across the grain of the country by climbing over Shriner Mountain. It then turns east through Brush Hollow, which it follows all the way to PA 192 and Rapid Run. This section is entirely within Bald Eagle State Forest.
