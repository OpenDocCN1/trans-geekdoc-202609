In southern Pennsylvania and Maryland, the Great Eastern Trail divides into an east route and a west route.

GET's west route from the Potomac River traverses the Chesapeake & Ohio Canal National Historic Park to near Little Orleans, MD whence it passes through Maryland's Green Ridge State Forest to a junction with MST at the Mason-Dixon Line. GET's west route continues on MST to Detweiler Run in Huntington County.

The east route uses Tuscarora Trail north from Hancock, MD to a junction with Standing Stone Trail near Cowans Gap State Park in Fulton County, PA; hence north to Greenwood Furnace State Park where it follows MST's Greenwood spur north to Detweiler Run Natural Area.

Great Eastern Trail follows MST exclusively through northcentral Pennsylvania to the New York border where it meets Crystal Hills Trail, a branch of NY's Finger Lakes Trail crossing eastern Steuben County.

Mid State Trail Association is a participating member of Great Eastern Trail Association. For more information about GET, check out www.greateasterntrail.net.

# METRICATION

The MST was the first hiking trail in the United States to use metric measure. The second edition of the guide (1973) was completely metric. Metrication is a patriotic measure designed to help end our cultural isolation and ease our chronic balance of payments problems.
