# SECTION A: GREENWOOD SPUR
PA 305 TO DETWEILER JUNCTION
ROTHROCK STATE FOREST

Greenwood Furnace State Park is on PA 305 between Belleville and McAlevys Fort. Detweiler Junction is reached from Bear Meadows Road at the junction with Detweiler Road. The orange blazed Standing Stone Trail connecting the Greenwood Spur to the Tuscarora Trail near Cowans Gap State Park begins on the south side of PA 305 across the park office.

Water is available at several springs along this section as well as at the state park. Features of the spur are the historic sites at Greenwood Furnace, Alan Seeger Natural Area and Greenwood Fire Tower on top of Broad Mountain.

There is primitive camping at the Maggi Cutoff and along Detweiler Run but camping is prohibited in Alan Seeger NA. Intermediate access to the spur is available via Seeger and Stone Creek roads. The Greenwood Spur, the first major side trail in the Mid State system, was opened in 1974.

The Greenwood Spur is part of the Great Eastern Trail connecting the northern terminus of the Standing Stone Trail to the Mid State trail. See page 16 of this trail guide for more information regarding the Great Eastern Trail or visit the website www.hike-sst.org or www.greateasterntrail.org for up-to-date information.
