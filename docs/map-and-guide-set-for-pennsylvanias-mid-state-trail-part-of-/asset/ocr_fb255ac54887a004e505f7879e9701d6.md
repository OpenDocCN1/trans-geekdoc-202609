# PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 45 TO PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE
192 (Hairy John's TO R.B. Winter) Detailed Description, Map

| (km) | Features/Directions | SB (km) |
| 0.00 | PA 45 and Hairy John's Picnic Area. | 18.14 |

NB From PA 45 follow trail into woods. Cross a dirt road and continue through Picnic Area, passing Hairy John's spring and a small pond.

SB: Reach PA 45 at west end of Hairy John's Picnic Area

| 0.30 | NB: Bear left on Hairy John's Trail and follow it through open woods crossing a logging road, to the base of Winkelblech Mountain. Climb the steep south flank of the mountain. Near the top there is a view south through the gap between Stitzer and Bear Mountains. | 17.84 |

SB: Bear right at edge of pond in Hairy John's Picnic Area passing spring. Then cross a dirt road.

| 1.82 | NB: Turn right on Winkelblech Trail, which follows the crest of the ridge. Shortly after this junction there is a dry camp on the left. | 16.32 |

SB: Turn left on Hairy John's Trail. Shortly before this junction there is a dry camp on the right. Descend south flank of Winkelblech Mountain, passing a view through the gap between Stitzer and Bear Mountains. Then continue through open woods crossing a logging road.
