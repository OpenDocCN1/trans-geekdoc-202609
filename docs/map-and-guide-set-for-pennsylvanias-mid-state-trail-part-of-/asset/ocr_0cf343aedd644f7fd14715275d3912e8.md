# SECTION 2: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 326 TO STATE ROUTE 1004 RAINSBURG GAP TO EVERETT BUCHANAN STATE FOREST & STATE GAME LAND 97

Section 2 was the hardest part of the entire trail to locate and build. The bench on the west side of Tussey makes the trail possible but most of this bench is still in private hands. The intervening ridge tops are very tough but extraordinarily scenic. The views have been compared to those from North Fork Mountain in West Virginia. No camping is permitted on gameland and there is no reliable water source along the trail. Only on the management road at Biddle Place is water likely to be found about 200 meters below the trail.

Rainsburg Gap, on PA 326 between Rainsburg and Chaneysville can be reached by driving south on PA 326 from east of Bedford. Ashcom Rd (S R 1004) goes west through the gap in Tussey Ridge from Everett next to the PA turnpike. Nearest turnpike interchange is at Bedford.

There is intermediate access to Section 2 from top of Rainsburg Gap, Biddle Place and Ashcom but only by means of gated management roads. A yellow blazed trail leads to the gamelands from a parking area on Sherry Road near Rainsburg but does NOT connect with MST or even climb to the bench.

Section 2 was opened in 1998.
