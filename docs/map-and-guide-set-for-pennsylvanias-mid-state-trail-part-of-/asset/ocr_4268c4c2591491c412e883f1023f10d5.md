0.00 function with yellow-blazed Greenwood Spur of MST 16.41
which leads to Greenwood Furnace State Park and
Standing Stone Trail via Alan Seeger Natural Area and
Greenwood Fire Tower. This point is also the junction
of the east and west routes of the Great Eastern
Trail in Pennsylvania. See Section A of this guide for
detailed trail data on the Greenwood Spur.

NB: The main trail heads northeast on Monroe Kulp's
old logging RR grade. The tram road evaporates
after a bit and the trail climbs away from Detweiler
Run. Soon the old growth hemlocks and white pines
of Detweiler Natural Area can be seen to the right.
Finally the trail cuts through a small corner of this
forest primeval and meets a Reichlich Bros. logging RR
grade. Note the cinders in the footway. Do not camp
of Detweiler Natural Area.

SB: The MST heads northwest and climbs along bor-
der of Thickhead Wild Area.
