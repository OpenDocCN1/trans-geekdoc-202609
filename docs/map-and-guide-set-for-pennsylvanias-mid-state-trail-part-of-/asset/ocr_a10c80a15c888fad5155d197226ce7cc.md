Primitive or backpack camping (still free) is permitted elsewhere in the state forest (following published PA-DCNR primitive camping guidelines) except within sight of roads, and where posted or noted in this guide or on the MST maps, but prohibited in Natural Areas and State Game Lands. Boundaries of the natural areas are shown on MST maps but are generally not marked on the ground. The better primitive camp sites are identified in the guide. Check with the relevant state office to see if camping permits are required. As of the writing of this guide per 17 Pa. Code 21.61, they are not required for one night stands.

Primitive camp sites are operated strictly on a pack in, pack it out basis. It you packed that bottle, can or aluminum foil in full, you can certainly pack it out empty. If you find trash at a camp site or along the trail, please try and find room for it in your pack or pockets. Become familiar with and practice “Leave no Trace” principles.

At primitive camp sites, dispose of dishwasher and wash water at least 70 m (200 feet) from the nearest stream or spring. Somebody else downstream may be depending on the same stream for drinking water. Fecal matter must be buried in the top 15 cm (6 in) of soil and at least 100 m (300 feet) from the nearest water supply. Do not camp within sight (70 m) of any road.
