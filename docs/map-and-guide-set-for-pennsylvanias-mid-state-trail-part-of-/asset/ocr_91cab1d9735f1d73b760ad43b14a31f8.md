At the end of the logging road it climbs to the top of Tussey Ridge and continues along the top to another pipeline swath. It continues on a jeep road, cross-ing two more pipelines and then turns left on Tussey Mtn. Road. It then turns right on Biddle Road and continues on Lower Clover Creek Road to reach Williamsburg where it follow streets to reach the Lower Trail Parking lot at First and Liberty.
