# NB Features/Directions SB (km)

18.49 Indian Wells Overlook above Bear Meadows. On a clear day you can see Winkelblech and Buffalo mountains over 40 km away. This is one of the best views in Central Pennsylvania.

18.76 Trail crosses the first of three charcoal flats. Several of these flats are used as campsites since water can be obtained from Keith Spring.

19.27 Junction with yellow blazed side trail leading 320 m to Keith Spring and a view over Laurel Run Valley and Tussey Ridge at 480 m.

NB: MST continues across Big Flat.

20.42 Cross North Meadows Road at junction with Gettis Ridge Road.

NB: MST continues downhill on Bear Meadows Trail.

SB: MST continues across Big Flat.

20.87 NB: Turn right on trail that slabs the north flank of Thickhead Mountain. The blazed Sand Spring Loop continues ahead on the Bear Meadows Trail to Sand Spring at 0.63 km and then rejoins the MST via the Gettis Trail in 1.88 km.

SB: Turn left on Bear Meadows Trail. The blazed Sand Spring Loop returns from right.
