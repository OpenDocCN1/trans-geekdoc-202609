# SECTION 10: US ROUTE 322 TO PA ROUTE 45
SEVEN MOUNTAINS TO HAIRY JOHN’S
BALD EAGLE STATE FOREST

The Seven Mountains Roadside Rest is open from mid-spring to late fall but parking is limited to two hours. For more secure, or overnight, park call Seven Mountains Scout Camp (814) 364-1017 located on Sand Mountain Road about a mile farther north on US 322. Reach MST via Stillhouse Hollow Road.

Hairy John’s Picnic Area is on PA 45 between Woodward and the Bald Eagle State Forest office. Overnight parking at Hairy Johns is not recommended. Hairy John Vonada was a hermit who lived on the old stage road after his family died in an epidemic.

Primitive camping along the trail is available in Greens Valley, along Little Poe Creek, and Libby Run and in Bear Gap. Camping is prohibited along the railroad grade next to Penn’s Creek (Penn’s Creek Path). State park camping is available at Poe Valley and Poe Paddy at the prevailing state park fees.

There is intermediate access to Stillhouse Hollow, Millheim Siglerville, Big Poe, Cherry Run, Rupp Hollow, and Woodward Gap Roads. Section 10 was opened to Poe Paddy in 1972 and extended to Hairy Johns in 1976.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

From the culvert across US 322 the MST climbs to the crest of Long Mountain and follows this ridge to Stillhouse Hollow. It then descends steeply and follows Greens Valley to Big Flat where it turns and descends to Little Poe Valley. Beyond Poe Valley State Park it follows the crest of Little Poe Mountain. After descending to cross Little Poe Creek it climbs over Long Mountain before descending to Poe Paddy by way of Dry Hollow Trail.

A short stretch of road walking brings the MST to the abandoned railroad grade. Penn’s Creek is crossed on the old railroad bridge and the tunnel provides a way under Paddy Mountain. The MST follows the railroad grade and new trail to Cherry Run where some road walking takes the trail through Cherry Run Gap. The MST then climbs over Thick Mountain and proceeds east through Sand Hollow to Hairy John’s Picnic Area on PA 45.
