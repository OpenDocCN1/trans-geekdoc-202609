# SECTION 16: STATE ROUTE 4001 TO PENNSYLVANIA
ROUT 414
LITTLE PINE PARK TO BLACKWELL
TIADAGHTON AND TIOGA STATE FORESTS
STATE GAME LAND 75

Section 16 begins at Little Pine State Park and ends at the canoe access
are in Blackwell. It is one of the most isolated sections of hiking trail
anwhere in Penn Woods. Little Pine State Park is on SR 4001 be-
tween Waterville and English Center. Blackwell is on PA 414 between
Morris and Cedar Run.

Features include, Bark Cabin Natural Area, Wolf Run Wild Area, views
of Little Pine Creek, Oregon Hill, and Gillespie Point. Springs and
streams are fairly abundant on this section and there are plenty of
primitive campsites. Camping is prohibited in Bark Cabin Natural
Area and on the two pieces of state game land crossed the way.
Intermediate access is available from Schoolhouse, Hackett, Okome,
Barrens and Big Run roads. Crossing of Trout Run is without benefit
of bridge. Section 16 was opened on the weekend of 4 July 1985.

Caution! This section is underlain by the gas-rich Marcellus Shale for-
m. Interruptions in the footpath are possible due to increasing
gas production activity. Please check the trail conditions at http://
www.hike-mst.org before you hike.
