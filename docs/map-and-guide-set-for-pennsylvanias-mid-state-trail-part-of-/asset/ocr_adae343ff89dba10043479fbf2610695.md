# SECTION B: IRONSTONE TRAIL
INDIAN STEPS TO BEAVER POND

The Ironstone Trail is a 17 km spur of the Mid State Trail providing two connections between Stone Valley Recreation Area (Penn State University-PSU) and the Mid State Trail. The Ironstone is blazed yellow by Rothrock Forest land and yellow over blue on PSU land. Together with 6 km of the MST it makes a 23 km boot-ing circuit hike. Features of the Ironstone Loop are the steps on Leading Ridge, Shavers Creek Environmental Center and Monroe Iron Furnace. Intermediate access is available on Harry's Valley Rd., Charter Oak Rd., Shavers Creek Environmental Center, Red Rose Rd., PA 26, and Pine Swamp Rd.. The Ironstone Trail was opened in 1979. Primitive camping is permitted as per DCNR regulations on the yellow blasted portions of the trail. Camping is not permitted on the yellow sections which are on PSU land. Nice primitive campsites are located at Garner Run and at the junction with the MST at Beaver Pond. Water is available at Garner Run in Harry's Valley, a piped spring at 5.49 km NB (11.65 km SB), Shaver's Creek, and the Shaver's Creek Environmental Center.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

The Ironstone Trail descends Tussey Mtn. crosses Harry's Valley and turns southwest along Leading Ridge. It then descends into Stone Val-ley on a jeep road through the water-shed research area and traverses evergreen plantations and a meadow to a junction with Lake Trail in Stone Valley Recreation Area. Near Shaver's Creek Environmental Center the Ironstone Trail diverges from the Lake Trail for a distance before picking up Shaver's Creek to PA 26. After passing Monroe Iron Furnace the Ironstone follows old charcoal roads and trails to a junc-tion with the Mid State Trail near Beaver Pond.
