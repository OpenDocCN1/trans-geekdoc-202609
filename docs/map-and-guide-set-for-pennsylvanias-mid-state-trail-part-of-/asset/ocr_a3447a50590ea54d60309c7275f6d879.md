# NB Features/Directions SB (km)

6.11 Cross Negro Hollow Road. 12.03

NB: From here to Pine Creek Road the Kessler Trail forms the western boundary of the Hook Natural Area so camping is permitted only to the west of the trail. Walled spring to east of trail about 200 m beyond.

SB: Climb north slope of Buffalo Mountain. There is a walled spring to left of trail about 200 m before Negro Hollow Road.

6.74 NB: Reach north edge of Sharpback Mountain and descend through a thick stand of hemlocks. Just before Pine Creek pass pass a campsite on the left. 11.40

SB: Reach top of Sharpback Mountain. 

7.46 Cross Pine Creek on a foot bridge. 10.68

SB: Pass a campsite on the right and climb Sharpback Mountain through a thick stand of hemlocks.

7.66 Cross Pine Creek Road. 10.48

NB: Continue ahead on the Wise Trail which slabs up the south flank of Buck Ridge. Trail continues west along crest of Buck Ridge.

SB: Continue south on Kessler Trail. From here to Negro Hollow Road the Kessler Trail forms the western boundary of Hook Natural Area so camping is permitted only to the west of the trail.
