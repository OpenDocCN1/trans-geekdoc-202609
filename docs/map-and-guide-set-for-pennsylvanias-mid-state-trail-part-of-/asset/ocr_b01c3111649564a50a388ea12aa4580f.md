# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

The start point of section 9 is the junction with the Greenwood Spur
below Bear Meadows Road. This junction is also the meeting point
of the east and west routes of the Great Eastern Trail (GET) in Penn-
sylvania. Hiking south on the Greenwood Spur which is the eastern
route of the GET will lead to Greenwood Furnace State Park and
the northern trailhead for the Standing Stone Trail. From Detewiler
junction the MST heads northeast on the old Kulp and Reichley Bros.
raailroad grades which are followed up Detewiler Valley where the trail
skirts the edge of Detewiler Natural Area. It then crosses the south
prong of Thickhead Mountain, leaving the Thickhead Wild Area and
descending to Penn Roosevelt State Park. Lastly, it climbs Broad
mountain and follows it east to Bald Mountain. After crossing a high
valley it reaches the culvert under US 322.
