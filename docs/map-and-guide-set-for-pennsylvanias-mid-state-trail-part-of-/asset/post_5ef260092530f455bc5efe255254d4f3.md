## SECTION 4: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 36 TO PENNSYLVNIA ROUTE 164
### YELLOW CREEK TO OLD ROADSIDE REST-STATE GAME LAND 73

The southern trailhead is on PA 36 just west of New Frontier Restaurant. There is a fair amount of parking at a pullout along the highway. The northern trailhead is at the old roadside rest at the top of Tussey Ridge on PA 164. There is limited parking but do not block the gate on the old road.

There is intermediate access from unpaved Maple Run-Ravers Gap Road (changes name at township boundary) and from paved Henrietta Mountain Road. There is a sizeable parking lot on Ravers Gap Road on the east side of Maple Run but very limited parking along Henrietta Mtn. Road. No winter maintenance on Maple Run Road and not much more in summer.

Maple Run Valley is the principal feature of this section and there is plenty of water along the run but little elsewhere. Maple Run is the one place where the trail leaves the bench or top of Tussey Ridge.

Section 4 was opened in 1998.

## GENERAL DESCRIPTION

From PA 36 the trail climbs steeply on stone steps and sidehill construction to an old woods road. It continues to climb on the road before the corner of state game land. It then descends to Maple Run Valley and continues north to Ravers Gap Road without crossing the run. Here it turns left and then right again on a gated management road which is followed to Old (Henrietta) Mtn. Road. The trail then turns right and climbs to the top of Tussey Ridge where it turns left on another gated management road. This road is followed nearly to PA 164 where it turns right onto trail and climbs to a crossing of PA 164 at the ridge top across from the old roadside rest.
