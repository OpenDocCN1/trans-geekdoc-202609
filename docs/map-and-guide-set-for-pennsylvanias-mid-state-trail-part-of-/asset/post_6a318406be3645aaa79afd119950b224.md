# SECTION 5: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 164 TO PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 866
OLD ROADSIDE REST TO WILLIAMSBURG
STATE GAME LANDS 73 AND 118

The southern trailhead is the old roadside rest at the top of Tussey Ridge on PA 164 east of Martinsburg. The old route of PA 164 is gated off but there is space for several cars to park if the gate is not blocked. The northern trailhead is the parking lot for the Lower Trail on East First Street and Liberty in Williamsburg where there is plenty of parking space. Features of this section are commanding views in both directions from four pipeline swaths across Tussey Ridge. Water is very scarce along this section. There are some intermittent springs along the gated maintenance road south of Drab Corner and a more reliable spring on an old road immediately north of Drab Corner. There are six gaps along this portion of the bench and a number of old roads may lead to springs in these gaps but they have not been explored. Game commission regulations do not permit such routes to be marked. No camping on game lands.

There is intermediate access to SR 2011 at Salems Church on Mill Lane (T 369) at a crossroads identified as Beavertown on USGS map and as Drab on PA transportation map. There is plenty of parking space at a game food field on the bench. Access also available from Tussey Mtn. Road above Marklesburg. Section 5 was opened in 1997.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

From the old roadside rest the trail follows crest of Tussey Ridge paralleling the boundary of SGL 73. At the pipeline the trail turns west and picks up an old road which follows to the gated maintenance road on the bench. This road is followed to the large clearing at Drab Corner. Here the trail turns right on jeep road and then left on an old logging road. At the end of logging road it climbs to the top of Tussey Ridge and continues along the top to another pipeline swath. It continues on jeep road, crossing two more pipelines and turns left on Tussey Mtn. Road. It then turns right on Biddle Road and continues on Lower Creek Road to reach the Lower Trail Parking lot at First and Liberty.
