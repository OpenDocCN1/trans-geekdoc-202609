Finally, if you do not intend to follow all of these rules while passing through private land on the MST, please hike another trail so that polite, quiet hikers can continue to enjoy in peaceful perpetuity of the diversity of features along MST's private land sections.

# FOOT PATH ONLY

The main trail and side trails are described in both northbound and southbound directions in a table format. The trail description is separated into twenty sections divided by paved roads or highways (expecting the division between secs. 8 and 9). Crossings of roads that can be navigated by an ordinary car at sometime in the year are noted as intermediate access points. The MST is still a rugged and demanding trail.

In Rothrock State Forest the MST consists largely of ridge top trail built since 1969 by the Hiking Division of the Penn State Outing Club and other groups. East of Stillhouse Hollow in Bald Eagle, Tiadhton, and Tioga State Forests the MST follows many old trails linked with lengths of new trail. Other groups which have helped build the MST include convict laborers from Huntington State Prison, Standing Stone Garden Club trail crews, CETA, Girl Scouts, Youth Conservation Corps, Boy Scouts, adjudicated juveniles, community service workers, PSU Faculty Women's Club Hiking Interest Group, Bucknell University Outing Club, Susquehanna University Outing Club, Asaph Trail Club, as well as volunteers of the Keystone Association and of course the Mid State Trail Association.

The Mid State Trail was designed, built, and is maintained for foot travel only.
