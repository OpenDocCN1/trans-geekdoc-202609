# SECTION 5: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 164 TO PENNSYL-
VANIA ROUTE 866
OLD ROADSIDE REST TO WILLIAMSBURG
STATE GAME LANDS 73 AND 118

The southern trailhead is the old roadside rest at the top of Tussey
Ridge on PA 164 east of Martinsburg. The old route of PA 164 is
gated off but there is space for several cars to park if the gate is not
blocked. The northern trailhead is the parking lot for the Lower Trail
on East First Street and Liberty in Williamsburg where there is plenty
of parking space. Features of this section are commanding views in
both directions from four pipeline swaths across Tussey Ridge. Water
is very scarce along this section. There are some intermittent springs
along the gated maintenance road south of Drab Corner and a more
reliable spring on an old road immediately north of Drab Corner.
There are six gaps along this portion of the bench and a number of
of old roads may lead to springs in these gaps but they have not been
explored. Game commission regulations do not permit such routes to
be marked. No camping on game lands.

There is intermediate access to SR 2011 at Salems Church on Mill
Lane (T 369) at a crossroads identified as Beavertown on USGS map
and as Drab on PA transportation map. There is plenty of parking
space at a game food field on the bench. Access also available from
Tussey Mtn. Road above Marklesburg.

Section 5 was opened in 1997.
