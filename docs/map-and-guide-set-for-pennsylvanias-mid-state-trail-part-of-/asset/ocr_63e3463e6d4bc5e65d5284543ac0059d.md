11.59 Junction of old paths. MST slabs the mountainside downhill NB, uphill SB. Yellow-blazed circuit footpath to southwest descends alternately to the top of a 10 m waterfall in Nickel Run (may dry up in a very dry summer), and to the former Arnot & Pine Creek RR (built 1878, abandoned 1952, always a branch of New York State Erie Railroad leading to Morris, it never reached Pine Creek beside campsites and swimming holes on perennial Babb Creek. Following the whole side back to here adds 2.57 km to your hike, re-climbing 90 m.
