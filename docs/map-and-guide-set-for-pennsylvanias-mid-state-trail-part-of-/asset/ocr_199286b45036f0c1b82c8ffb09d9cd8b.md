# NB Features/Directions SB (km)

24.05 Intersection of Bear Meadows Road and gated Detweiler Road.

NB: Turn right onto trail and descend steeply along border of Thickhead Wild Area.

SB: Follow Bear Meadows Road uphill to right at switchback junction with gated Detweiler Road.

24.33 Junction with yellow-blazed Greenwood Spur of MST which leads to Greenwood Furnace State Park and Standing Stone Trail via Alan Seeger Natural Area and Greenwood Fire Tower. This point is also the junction of the east and west routes of the GET in Pennsylvania. See Section A of this guide for detailed trail data data on the Greenwood Spur.

NB: The MST 1.609km = 1mile, 1km = 0.62mile northeast of Monroe Kulp's old logging RR grade. The tram road evaporates after a bit and the trail climbs away from Detweiler Run. Soon the old growth hemlocks and white pines of Detweiler Natural Area can be seen to the right. The trail cuts through a small corner of this forest primeval and meets a Reichley Bros. logging RR grade. Note the cinders in the footway. Do not camp in Detweiler Natural Area..

SB: The MST heads northwest and climbs along border of Thickhead Wild Area.
