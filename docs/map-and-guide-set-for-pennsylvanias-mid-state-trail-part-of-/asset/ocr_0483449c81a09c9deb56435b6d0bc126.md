# NB SB (km) Features/Directions (km)

20.60 Unblazed side trail leads uphill west to bench in 27 m, then lane in 139 m. On the side trail, keep turning left on streets, passing United Church (formerly Methodist Church before it moved) and vending machine in front of the fire station, to reach Nelson Town Center building, including post office (16940) in 0.76 km from MST. The older homes you pass near the fire station were moved from the south shore. As of 2011 there is no store, no public telephone, and the post office is on a list of possible closures.

20.66 Long footbridge over shallow bay.

20.94 Old crossroad, gates both sides. Fishing pier 55 m towards lake. 240 m away from lake on washed out old road to Nelson Moccasin Trail parking. Sign: 3 miles to Tompkins. The homes from this area moved north across Lakeview Drive/SR 4022.

NB: Gently climb old road straight ahead.

SB: Follow old road then quickly turn left, easy to miss. If you pass the back of the Nelson sewer plant, turn back.
