In 1969 students from the Hiking Division of the Penn State Outing Club led by Thomas Thwaites began building the Mid State Trail. Over the years, as the trail grew, the need for maintainers of the trail grew as well. In 1982 Dr. Thwaites created the Mid State Trail Association (MSTA) to recruit volunteers and attract supporting members. Since then, the volunteers of the Mid State Trail Association, along with overseers of the trail, have spent countless hours maintaining and growing the trail. Bisecting the state, from the Maryland border to the New York state line, the Mid State Trail traverses some of the most scenic and beautiful parts of Pennsylvania.
