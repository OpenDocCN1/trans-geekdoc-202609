5.10 Private land, stay on trail, stay out entirely in bear and deer seasons (Nov 15-Dec 15 including Sundays). In high water or icy conditions, follow roads around. NO PARKING here.

NB: Turn right on paved Elkhorn Rd/SR 4024.
SB: Turn left on gravel Elkhorn Creek Rd. then left again to ford creek and climb steeply on other side. To detour, follow Elkhorn Rd 2.3 km to end, left on Farmington Hill Road (becomes Gee Road) 1.45 km, then left on Ridge Road 2.65 km to resume blazed trail.

5.54 Limited parking on shoulder SOUTH side of Elkhorn Road, WEST of bridge for private driveway signed #1712. Do not cross bridge onto posted private land. Do not block any driveways or pull off this narrow twisty road at any place other than this specific location.

5.94 Straight at junction of dirt Howe Hill Rd. Waterfall on south side of Elkhorn Rd. NO PARKING.

NB: If you care to avoid wet pasture ahead, turn left on dirt road, then right on Scenic View Dr to campground, same distance.
