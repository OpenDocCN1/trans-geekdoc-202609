# SECTION 10: US ROUTE 322 TO PA ROUTE 45
SEVEN MOUNTAINS TO HAIRY JOHN’S
BALD EAGLE STATE FOREST

The Seven Mountains Roadside Rest is open from mid-spring to late
fall but parking is limited to two hours. For more secure, or overnight,
park call Seven Mountains Scout Camp (814) 364-1017 located on
Sand Mountain Road about a mile farther north on US 322. Reach
MST via Stillhouse Hollow Road.

Hairy John’s Picnic Area is on PA 45 between Woodward and the Bald
Eagle State Forest office. Overnight parking at Hairy Johns is not
recommended. Hairy John Vonada was a hermit who lived on the old
stage road after his family died in an epidemic.

Primitive camping along the trail is available in Greens Valley, along
Little Poe Creek, and Libby Run and in Bear Gap. Camping is pro-
hibited along the railroad grade next to Penn’s Creek (Penn’s Creek
Path). State park camping is available at Poe Valley and Poe Paddy at
at the prevailing state park fees.

There is intermediate access to Stillhouse Hollow, Millheim Siglerville,
Big Poe, Cherry Run, Rupp Hollow, and Woodward Gap Roads. Sec-
tion 10 was opened to Poe Paddy in 1972 and extended to Hairy Johns
in 1976.
