# TRAIL OVERSEERS

Routine maintenance of the Mid State Trail is performed by volunteer overseers. Their tasks include removing blowdowns, cutting back encroaching brush, improving the footway and repainting the blazes. An individual overseer is assigned a section about 3 km long but organization may be assigned that a longer section of trail. The overseer system is responsible for the generally good condition of the Mid State Trail. An overseer is expected to make a minimum of three trips per year over his or her section. Individual overseers’ efforts are currently coordinated by volunteer Regional Managers based in the areas of Everett, State College, Woolrich, and Tioga. The “Manual for Maintenance of Mid State Trail” and the “DCNR Trail Marking Guidelines” are posted to the MSTA web site.

As with any group of volunteers there is a high turnover and sections become available when overseers move away from the area. The extensions have created many opportunities for new overseers. Some overseers come all the way from Pittsburgh, Philadelphia, and even Virginia to help maintain the Mid State Trail while others live within walking distance. If you would like to adopt a section of the MST, visit the Mid State Trail Association website at www.hike-mst.org for more information.
