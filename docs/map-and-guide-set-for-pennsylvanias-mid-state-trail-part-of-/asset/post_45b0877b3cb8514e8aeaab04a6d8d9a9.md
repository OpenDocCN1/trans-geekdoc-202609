# SECTION 13: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 880 TO PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 150
RAVENSBURG STATE PARK TO WEST BRANCH SUSQUE-HANNA TIADAGTON AND BALD EAGLE STATE FORESTS

Ravensburg State Park is on PA 880 between exit 192 (near the village of Carroll, signed “Jersey Shore”) on I-80 and Rauchtown. Call R. B. Winter office (570) 966-1455 for overnight parking at Ravensburg overnight parking at Ravensburg is not recommended.

The West Branch trail head is on PA 150 between Chatham Run and Charlton. PA 150 can be reached either from the Avis or Woolrich exits on U.S. 220.

Primitive camping is available at Great Island Path, and Yarns Run State Park tenting only camping is available at Ravensburg at the prevailing state park fees.

Intermediate access is available on Shaw Mountain, Ramm and Pine roads (T 418). Section 13 was opened in 1983. There is a fair supply of springs and streams along this section but none on the road walking portion.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

From the Ravensburg Park office trail crosses PA 880 and continues along the state forest boundary to the next gap to the. Here it climbs along the route of the Great Island Indian Path. It then traverses the mountain top to the Ramm Road where it turns north along the edge of Nippenose Valley. It leaves the road to avoid a tract of private land and then dips into Yarns Run Valley. It climbs the south side of Round Top to a natural vista of the West Branch Valley and Pine Creek. After descending the northwest corner of Round Top it follows roads, most of them paved, on the crossing of the West Branch Valley. The West Branch itself is crossed on a road bridge. Three historic sites, Fort Horn (1777), a Delaware Indian Village and ruins of the Pennsylvania Canal are passed on the valley crossing.
