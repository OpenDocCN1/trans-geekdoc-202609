From the Little Juniata Natural Area parking lot the trail climbs steeply over 400 m to a spectacular overlook. From there the MST follows the top of Tussey Ridge for 24 km, passing the famous “Indian Steps” before reaching PA 26. The footway is rocky, rugged and demanding. It is comparable to the toughest parts of the Appalachian Trail in Pennsylvania and Maine and it is difficult to get any rhythm going as you step from rock to rock. But the ridge top is without serious ups and downs and there are many overlooks, both northwest across the Barrens and southeast over Stone Valley.
