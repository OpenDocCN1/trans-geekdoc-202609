# PREAMBLE

The first edition of this trail guide was printed in 1972. Already the name had been changed from Central Allegheny (It was in the Appalachian Ridge and Valley Region NOT the Alleghenies and the name produced confusion with that trail that crosses Pennsylvania) to Mid State Trail in compliance with OUTDOOR RECREATION HORIZONS, a planning document issued in 1970 by the Department of Forests and Waters. See map on page 137 of this document for a good deal of foreshadowing of the current route of the longest trail in Penn's Woods.

The 1973 edition was the first metric and the last self-publishing edition. The 1974 to Fifth editions were held together with little brass clips allowing the insertion of new pages as the trail was relocated repeatedly during these years but the guides were printed commercially. The 6th and 7th editions were stapled and the 8th was the first to be printed by Mid State Trail Association. The 9th edition became a real paperback book. The 11th edition was the first to cover the trail through the entire state, from Maryland to New York. This edition is the 12th publication of the Guide to Pennsylvania Mid State Trail and incorporates the many spurs that offer additional access to the Mid State Trail, as well as providing elevation profiles of the various trail sections.
