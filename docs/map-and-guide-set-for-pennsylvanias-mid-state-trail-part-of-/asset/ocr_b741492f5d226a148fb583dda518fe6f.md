17.56 Register and Junction with blazed side trail to the northwest which switchbacks 440m down to Maple Spring at the edge of Brady Rd.. If Maple Spring is dry, water can usually be found just beyond Brady Road on an old woods road. There is little or no footway on this trail. Give all the empty canteens to the most sure-footed member(s) of your group. 14.69
