This section has a fairly good supply of springs and there is primitive camping in Detewiler Valley. State park camping at the usual state park fees is available at Penn Roosevelt. Features of this section are Thickhead Wild Area, the virgin timber in Detewiler Natural Area, and the Lewistown-Belfonte turnpike (1821).

## GENERAL DESCRIPTION

The start point of section 9 is the junction with the Greenwood Spur below Bear Meadows Road. This junction is also the meeting point of the east and west routes of the Great Eastern Trail (GET) in Pennsylvania. Hiking south on the Greenwood Spur which is the eastern route of the GET will lead to Greenwood Furnace State Park and the northern trailhead for the Standing Stone Trail. From Detewiler junction the MST heads northeast on the old Kulp and Reichley Bros. railroad grades which are followed up Detewiler Valley where the trail skirts the edge of Detewiler Natural Area. It then crosses the south prong of Thickhead Mountain, leaving the Thickhead Wild Area and descending to Penn Roosevelt State Park. Lastly, it climbs Broad mountain and follows it east to Bald Mountain. After crossing a high valley it reaches the culvert under US 322.
