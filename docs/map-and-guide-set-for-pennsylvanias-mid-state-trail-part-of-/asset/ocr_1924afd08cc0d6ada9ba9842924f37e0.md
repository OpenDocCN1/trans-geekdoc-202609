# NB SB (km) Features/Directions

17.90 Incline house. Tioga Coal Company built a narrow gauge railroad in 1903 to here from Rattler Mine 5 km northwest. Coal descended the mountain rim to a tipple in Morris to transfer to the Erie Railroad. Management shut the mine in 1909 by telling the mule drivers to send the other miners home while they packed their bags and hurriedly left town. Below the incline house, the unblazed incline gets steeper as it descends to private land.

NB: Drop on switchback to lower side of incline house then gently ascend old narrow gauge grade.

SB: Switchback up to pass around the rim through saplings, crossing and/or following several old skid trails, then descend to Slide Hollow.

18.75 NB: Turn right, leave old grade. Posted private land just ahead. 5.25

SB: Turn left, follow old narrow gauge grade built by 125 Polish immigrants in 1903.

18.85 Rattler Road. Parking off north side of seasonal Township road. Cross Rattler Run on road culvert. 5.15

NB: Pass through parking lot, turn left on trail under hemlocks ascending to rock formations then descending through rocks.

SB: Cross road and climb briefly through woods.
