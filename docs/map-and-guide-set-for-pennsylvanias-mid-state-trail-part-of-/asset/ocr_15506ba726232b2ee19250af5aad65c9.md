# NB SB Features/Directions (km)

13.51 Cross paved Beans Cove Road SR 3005. Large parking area with kiosk on north side of road. Both Beans Cove Road and the parking area are plowed, but Martin Hill Road which diverges north here is NOT plowed. Perennial Tussey Spring inside signed enclosure is about 150 m to west just off south side of Beans Cove Road. Gap Access Trail diverges to east just south of gate, allows circuit hike to Gap Trail without walking on the hard road. Relocation possible here, check www.hike-mst.org for updates.

NB: Climb gently off road other side of parking lot on Carl Cessna Trail into pine plantation, soon entering laurel thicket and leveling off. Evidence of mid-1960's this burn in "Pennsylvania chaparral"

SB: Pass steel gate and large routed sign to always-gated bolt double track Tussey Mountain XC Ski Trail.

15.17 Headwaters of Wildcat Run, spring 10 m down ravine west of trail. Cleared campsite 100 m south of ravine bottom, east trail, on east rim of hollow: Large lunch rock on west side of trail just south of campsite.

15.48 Cross powerline (former MST route) in sparse, burned woods.

15.67 Junction with old fire trail. Spring 23 m south, to E of trail. Fire trail to E reaches gravel Martin Hill Rd in 93 m, passing bushog turnaround/possible campsites.

NB=North bound hiking, SB=South bound hiking
