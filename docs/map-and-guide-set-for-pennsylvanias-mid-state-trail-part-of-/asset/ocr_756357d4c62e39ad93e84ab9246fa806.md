# R. B. WINTER TO RAVENSBURG
BALD EAGLE AND TIADAGTON STATE FORESTS

Raymond B. Winter State Park is just east of the Centre-Union County
line on PA 192. It is 27 km west of Lewisburg. Ravensburg State Park
is on PA 880 about 5 km north of Exit 192 (near the village of Car-
roll, signed “Jersey Shore” on I-80 and south of Rauchtown. Check
with the R. B. Winter Park office after overnight parking at both parks
(570) 966-1455. Overnight parking at Ravensburg State Park is not
recommended.

Primitive camping is available along Spruce Run, near Rendezvous
Spring, in Fourth Gap on Nittany Mountain, near Gas Line Spring,
White Deer Hole Creek, and along the lower Dunbar Trail. State Park
camping, at the prevailing fees, is available at R. B. Winter and tent-
ting only area with showers at Ravensburg.

Intermediate access is available on Boyer Gap, Sand Mountain, White
Deer Creek, Garden Hollow, Sugar Valley Narrows, Mohn Mill, Zim-
erman, Pipeline, Fourth Gap, Walters, Cove, Dunbar, Kalbfleisch
and Sand Spring roads. Section 12 was opened in 1981 has been relo-
cated four times and severed twice.
