18.98 Trail crosses and then skirts the upper edge of an extensive overlook to the northwest. Pause to enjoy the bench and stone chairs at this vista. Dry campsite can be found under large pine to east of trail. 13.27

SB: Several possible dry campsites in next 1.5km

19.30 Junction with blazed Tussey Trail. To the northwest Tussey Trail descends steeply to Brady Road in 460 m. A spring can be found below Brady Road between Buckeye Camp (5-C-68) and the unmarked continuation of Tussey Trail. Be sure to replace the screen over the spring. 12.95

19.46 Extensive views to northwest. There are several such views between Ewing Path and Tussey Trail making it the most scenic part of the MST. It is also one of the trail's roughest and rockiest sections. 12.79

21.18 Junction with blazed Ewing path. To the northwest it is 0.81 km to Brady Road and to the southeast it is 1.59 km to Pennsylvania Furnace Road. 11.07

21.55 Pass two two ancient hemlocks. These trees must have grown very slowly on the ridge top. They must have been struck repeatedly by lightning. View to northwest. 10.70

23.54 View to southeast. MST continues over open ledges with repeated views. 8.71
