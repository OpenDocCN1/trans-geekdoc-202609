6.29 Cross stream. 10.12
NB: Bear right on Crowfield Road. Then turn left into Penn Roosevelt State Park, passing a water faucet. Out of season get water from a spring upstream from bridge. MST continues along the shore of the pond, past the ruins of a Civilian Conservation Corps Camp, and then swings left below the dam. Cross the footbridges and bear right up a dirt road at the far side of the dam.

SB: Bear left on trail following old Crowfield Road.

6.59 Cross road in Penn Roosevelt State Park. Tenting-only camping area providing state park camping at the prevailing fees, is a few meters north on this road. 9.82
NB: Pass gate, cross road and continue on trail. Camping area is a few meters left on this road. The trail slabs up the hillside and then turns left up the ridge line. The slope eases off gradually and then a dry camp is passed on the left.

SB: Trail continues below dam crossing foot bridges and then bears right along shore of pond past the ruins of a Civilian Conservation Corps camp. Then pass a water faucet. Out of season, get water from a spring just upstream from road bridge. Pass junction with Thickhead Mountain Road on left.

NB=North bound hiking, SB=South bound hiking
