10.46 Junction with Rainbow Trail. Rainbow is yellow-blazed to the northwest and leads downhill to intermittent springs at the edge of Colerain Road (0.27 km) to Indian Overlook and Yellow Arrow Trail (1.10 km) and parking (former Colerain Picnic Area) on PA 45 (2.16 km). Trail passes through another dry camp.

NB: An extensive view of Spruce Creek Valley to the northwest.

SB: Three views to the southeast are then passed. MST continues along the ridge line and passes a view to the northwest.

11.69 Junction with unblazed and unmaintained Colerain Trail. To the west a spring can be found on the bench by descending the Colerain Trail. Cross Brady Road and continue downhill to an old road and turn left. The spring is near a hunting camp on this old road. To the east of MST on the Colerain Trail, a piped spring is found on the far side of the Tram Road. To return to the MST, follow the Tram Road northeast and reclimb Tussey Ridge on either Colerain Road or Wheeler Trail.
