# NB SB Features/Directions (km)

16.27 Junction with Fleet Foot Trail. This trail is not blazed all the way to Laurel Run Road and so must be hiked first from this to find where it comes out. Cool Spring is directly in front of Fleet Foot Camp at the edge of Laurel Run Road. Fourth Mountain Trail register. Sign in. View of Bear Meadows Natural Area. 8.06

17.84 Junction with North Meadows Trail. 6.49

NB: To left the blue-blazed North Meadows Trail descends very steeply to North Meadows Road in 510 m. MST turns right and follows North Meadows Trail along the crest of mountain.

SB: To right North Meadows Trail descends very steeply to North Meadows Road in 510 m MST turns left, passing repeated overlooks of Bear Meadows.

18.06 Junction with North Meadows Trail. 6.27

NB: MST turns left. North Meadows Trail turns right and descends 620 m to Bear Gap Road.

SB: MST turns right and follows North Meadows Trail alongside the crest of mountain to left North Meadows Trail descends 620 m to Bear Gap Road.

NB=North bound hiking, SB=South bound hiking
