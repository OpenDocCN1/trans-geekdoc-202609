# SECTION 6: PENNSYLVANIA ROUTE 866 TO US ROUTE 22
WWILLIAMBURG TO ALFARATA
LOWER TRAIL

The southern trailhead is the Williamsburg parking lot at First and Liberty in Williamsburg. The northern trailhead is the Alfarata parking lot on the Alexandria Road just off US 22. Features of the Lower Trail are its grade separation and historic remains of the Pennsylvania Canal and charcoal iron industries. A grassy swath is provided for hikers on the east side of the Lower Tail , a crushed stone grade in the middle for bikes and another grass strip on the west side for horses. Lower Trail is owned and operated by Rails-to-Trails of Central Pennsylvania. Lodging can be found in Williamsburg at the Blue Lantern Bed & Breakfast located at 327 High St. The phone number is 814-407-4031.

There is intermediate access from T 454, and SR 2017. There is no water along the Lower Trail.

The Lower Trail was opened in 1994.

# GENERAL DESCRIPTION

Lower Tail is unblazed and follows bed of the Petersburg branch of Pennsylvania Railroad, abandoned in 1982. The railroad had in turn followed the route of the Pennsylvania Canal. It crosses Frankstown branch once near before reaching Etna Furnace. It crosses twice more near Etna Furnace and then enters a canyon before reaching US 22 and the Alfarata trailhead. Mid State turns west on Alexandria Road (SR 4014) to reach the Hall Trail on Short Mountain.
