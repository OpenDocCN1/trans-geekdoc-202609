# SECTION 1: MARYLAND TO PA 326 2nd CROSSING
MASON-DIXON LINE TO RAINSBURG GAP
BUCHANAN STATE FOREST, PRIVATE LAND, SGL 97

Section 1 follows Tussey Mountain through Buchanan State Forest and small sections of private land and State Game Land 97. A relocation in 2009 moved the trail kilometers west to align with a realignment of Great Eastern Trail within Maryland’s Green Ridge State Forest. A second, smaller relocation moved MST off Martin Hill Road into Martin Hill Wild Area. Future relocations are planned in and near Martin Hill Wild Area so continue to check www.hike-mst.org for updates.

The southern end of the section at the Mason-Dixon line can be reached from exit 56 on I-68, north on Flintstone Creek Road to an all way stop then right on Black Valley Road, unnumbered in MD, that becomes PA 326 and MST at the border.

The northern end of the section, which is partway down the east side of Rainsburg Gap is on PA 326 between Rainsburg and Chanesville and can be reached by driving south on PA 326 from Bedford. Better parking is in the ATV access trail lot at the top of the mountain in Rainsburg Gap, nearly at the south end of Section 2.

Primitive camping along the trail is possible at a few points within the State Forest. Camping is prohibited in the Sweet Root Natural Area, and the brief interludes of State Game Land and private land.

Intermediate access is available from Beans Cove and seasonal forestry roads Martin Hill Road and Tower Road. Features of section 1 are Martin Hill Wild Area, Sweet Root Natural Area, and Martin Hill itself, the highest point on the Mid State Trail. Section 1 was opened in 1994 and significantly relocated in 2009.
