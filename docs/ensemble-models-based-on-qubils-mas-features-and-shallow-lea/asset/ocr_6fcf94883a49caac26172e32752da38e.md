# ## SUMMARY AND CONCLUSIONS

DILI has been the most frequent cause of safety-related
drug market withdrawals for the past 50 years. The DILI
estimation is a major issue for both patients and
pharmaceutical industry. In fact humans remains a challenge for
ational models. It should be shown that models based on
QSAR-based 2D-SD molecular descriptors and shallow
machine learning techniques (by using different ensembles
generation and a rigorous internal and external validation
scheme) were employed to enhance the performance
and reliability of DILI predictions.

To end this, two robust models, were developed
(M2 and M9), which present training accuracies 0.735+0.76
test accuracies 0.76+0.84 for the random and black
validation test sets, respectively. By using the prediction
probability values from the individual models, we constructed
s some ensembles, being the E15 and
best ones in DILI prediction. These ensemble yielded
important enhancements with respect to the individual models,
achieving accuracies of AC, SE, and SD of 0.853,
0.88, and 0.76, respectively. Good results were obtained
by using internal validation tests and external
validation tests (two validation sets and five external
sets), to improve the robustness and reliability of
these.
