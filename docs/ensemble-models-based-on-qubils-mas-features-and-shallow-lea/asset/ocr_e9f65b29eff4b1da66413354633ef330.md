The best (and base models) in this study showed a significantly better performance when based on a statistical and “risk analysis”, with regard to the results reported by the literature for the same data sets. The ensemble also showed a better performance compared to the prediction by the well-known Padel DDPredictor, as well as with regard to DL-Combined, DL-Liew and Vlead models established in the literature.

A variety and free classification screening of drug toxicity was achieved with DDPredictor (http://www.chem.com/priority). This software showed advantages over other methods, and it is clear that the software has the capacity to create big data sets.

In summary this study provides a highly accurate approach using a set of compounds in different development phases, that highlights the potential of the DLI compounds. This scenario demonstrates the necessity of a high priority hepatotoxicity in silico evaluation, mainly in the early stages of preclinical trials.
