This paper proposes a network approach to explore the correlation structure of the stock market. By approximating the network with an exponential random graph model, it provides an alternative approach to dimension reduction while a huge amount of stocks are taken into considerations.

In the meantime, it can be carried out without specific distributional assumptions. And most important of all, this is an attempt to build a link to complex systems.
