Note that even though this MCMDM transfer pricing model includes only four specific groups of objectives for presentation purpose, the modeling process easily adapts to all international transfer pricing problems with multiple criteria and multiple constraint levels such as maximization of after-tax profit for the parent company as another criteria or minimization of foreign taxes and tariffs as additional constraints.

The above models (54.1) and (54.2) are MC² problems with four objectives and four constraint levels. Solving this type of real-world problem is quite complicated. Therefore, this chapter proposes a fuzzy set approach to make computational task as practical as possible.
