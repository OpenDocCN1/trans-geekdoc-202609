This may be due to the fact that during the period under review, the French capital market has expanded with a dramatic increase in foreign investment flows as France is committed to encouraging foreign investments. In fact, investment regulation in France is simple and foreign investors are provided with a range of financial incentives. According to the “France investment climate statement, 2015” of the United States Department of State, France is one of the world’s leading destinations for foreign investments. The formal French investment regime is one of the least restrictive in the world, with no widespread screening of investments by non-French entities. Restrictions on foreign investments in France gradually disappeared in the 1990s. Since then, investment rules have been liberalized, firstly for business investments in the European Union, then for all foreign investments.
