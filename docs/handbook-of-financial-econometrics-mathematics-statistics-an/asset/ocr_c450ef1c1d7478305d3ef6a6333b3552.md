The asymptotic bias of 2SLS estimated coefficients can be analyzed as follows:

+   (i) The bias term depends on the structural coefficients corresponding to exogenous variables (β_j) and reduced form coefficients of explanatory endogenous variables (Y_j), Π*_j and Π*_{-j}.
+   (ii) If an equation without exogenous variables (β_j = 0), the structural parameters are always estimated consistently.

The structural form model (116.40) can be written in the following reduced form:

```
$$\begin{bmatrix}\Delta DIV_t \\ \Delta K_t\end{bmatrix}=\frac{1}{1-\gamma_1\gamma_2}\begin{bmatrix}\alpha_0+\gamma_1\beta_0 & \alpha_1 & \gamma_1\beta_1 & \alpha_2 & \gamma_1\beta_2 \\ \beta_0+\alpha_0\gamma_2 & \alpha_1\gamma_2 & \beta_1 & \gamma_2\alpha_2 & \beta_2\end{bmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1 \\ DIV_{t-1} \\ K_{t-1} \\ P^*_t \\ Q^*_t\end{bmatrix}+\frac{1}{1-\gamma_1\gamma_2}\begin{bmatrix}\varepsilon_{1,t}+\gamma_1\varepsilon_{2,t} \\ \varepsilon_{2,t}+\gamma_2\varepsilon_{1,t}\end{bmatrix}
```
