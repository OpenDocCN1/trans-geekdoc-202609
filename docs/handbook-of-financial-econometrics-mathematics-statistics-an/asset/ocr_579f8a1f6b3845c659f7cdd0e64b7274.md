Table 91.2 seems to provide evidence that when hedge fund managers experience a negative performance event, they provide more weight to the market’s influence over their returns and seem to integrate them more fully as compared against positive performance events. Further, Bollen *et al.* (2008) provide a framework for researchers to examine the conditional performance of hedge fund returns to attempt to generate an idea of how hedge fund managers are reacting to specific performance events.

An adaption of the Bollen *et al.* (2008) technique used to capture conditional effects was provided in Smith *et al.* (2017) and is illustrated as follows:

$$R_{\mathrm{Hedge},t}=\beta_0+\left(\beta^{-}_1(1-I_{t-1})+\beta^{+}_1I_{t-1}\right)R_m+\varepsilon_t, \qquad (91.4)$$
