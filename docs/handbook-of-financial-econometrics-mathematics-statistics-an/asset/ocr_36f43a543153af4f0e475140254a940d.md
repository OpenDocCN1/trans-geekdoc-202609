# 83.5.7 Collar

A collar combines a protective put and a short call option to bracket the value of a portfolio between two bounds. For example, an investor holds the JNJ stock selling at $59.38. Buying a protective put using the put option with an exercise price of $55 places a lower bound of $55 on the value of the portfolio. At the same time, the investor can write a call option with an exercise price of $62.50. The call and the put sell at $0.03 (the bid price) and $0.10 (the ask price), respectively, making the net outlay for the two options to be only $0.07.
