An example is illustrated in Figure 101.2. Suppose there are two classes (category A and category B) for the output and two features (x1 and x2). A sample of unknown category is plotted as a solid circle. KNN predicts the category of this sample as follows. To start, we choose Euclidean distance and uniform distance weight. If K = 3, in the three nearest neighbors to the unknown sample, there are one sample of category A and two samples of category B. Because there are more samples of category B, KNN predicts the unknown sample to be of category B.
