Preliminary determination showed that the model can operate in the following three situations:

+   1. The INDU positively affects with the TAIEX mainly because the INDU is one of the indicators of investor observations; when the INDU fluctuates, the TAIEX is affected.
+   2. The exchange rate negatively affects with the TAIEX mainly because fluctuations in the exchange rate will affect foreign funds. When the exchange rate increases, the NTD depreciates, and the flow of foreign funds into the stock market is diminished.
+   3. The consumer price index negatively affects the TAIEX mainly because fluctuation will affect the opportunity cost of currency holding. When the consumer price index increases, the opportunity cost of holding currency will increase, and then the investment in TAIEX will also decrease.
