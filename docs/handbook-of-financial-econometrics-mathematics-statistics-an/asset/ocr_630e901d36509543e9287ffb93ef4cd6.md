Table 53.3 reports the empirical results of the Chow Test. For each firm, we divide the sample period into pre-and post- announcement periods. We then perform the Chow test to test if there is a structural shift in the systematic risks before and after the announcement of earnings. There are 452,408 out of 987,347 observations (45.82%) exhibiting a structural shift at a 5% confidence level. Further, there are 700,354 out of 987,347 observations (67.57%) exhibiting a structural shift at a 10% confidence level. This evidence indicates that the CARs reported in Table 53.2 and plotted in Figure 53.1 may be misleading because of the failure to take into account structural changes in the market model. Albeit these empirical results are consistent to previous findings in literature.
