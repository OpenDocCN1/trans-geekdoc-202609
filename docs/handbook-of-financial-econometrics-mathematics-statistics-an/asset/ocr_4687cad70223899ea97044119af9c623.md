It is noted that when $p = 2$, equation (85.43) reduces to the bivariate case given in equation (85.32). Then the first two moments are

$$E(X_i)=e^{\mu_i+\frac{\sigma_{ii}}{2}},\tag{85.44}$$ $$\operatorname{Var}(X_i)=e^{2\mu_i}e^{\sigma_{ii}}[e^{\sigma_{ii}}-1].\tag{85.45}$$ $$\operatorname{Cor}(X_i,X_j)=\exp\left(\mu_i+\mu_j+\frac12(\sigma_{ii}+\sigma_{jj})\right)\cdot\left(\exp\left(\rho_{ij}\sqrt{\sigma_{ii}\sigma_{jj}}\right)-1\right),\tag{85.46}$$

where $\rho_{ij}$ is the correlation between $Y_i$ and $Y_j$.

For more details concerning properties of the multivariate log-normal distribution, the reader is referred to Johnson and Kotz (8572).
