In Taiwan, banks still play a vital role in providing the funds and sustainable growth to firms. The robust banking relationships help overcome the frictions caused by credit and help maintain certain advantages. Currently, the e-commerce market in Taiwan has been overgrowing for the past decade. The advantage of using social media is more pronounced in enhancing efficiency.

Using ordinary least squares (OLS), quantile regression and difference-in-differences (DID) analysis, we find that social media (Facebook, Google+ and LinkedIn) positively influence firm value, particularly for firms that have a value that ranges in the low to median quantiles. However, while the number of bank relationships negatively affects firm value, the bank debt ratio raises firm value. A complementary relationship exists between YouTube and bank relationship measured by the bank debt ratio. Social media play a similar role as banks in mitigating information asymmetry and affect firm value significantly.

# # 66.2 Literature Review

In this section, we aim to understand the impact of social media and bank relationships on firm value. The argument is that social media represents a factor that can beneficially influence firm value and bank relationships. This idea has attracted considerable attention in the literature.
