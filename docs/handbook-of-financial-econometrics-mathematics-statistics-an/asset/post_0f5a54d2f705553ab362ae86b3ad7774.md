## 25.6 The Impacts of Financial Reforms

This section provides some evidence on how the impact of the use of foreign bank debt and foreign bank debt collateral on firm performance changed with the advent of the Taiwanese Financial Reform, which effectively transformed Taiwan’s banking system from one that was an emerging market to one that was more sophisticated.[^14] We present a pseudo-event study to suggest how the increasing sophistication of Taiwan’s banking sector impacted the differences between foreign and domestic banking relationships on firm performance. We would like to stress that, due to data limitations, the results in this section are suggestive, and do not represent a rigorous analysis.
