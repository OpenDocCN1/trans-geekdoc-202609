# 61.4.1 Did the PCDTSM fail during the near zero lower bound environment?

Historically, conventional affine term structure models were not designed to be well equipped at handling interest rates at the zero lower bound because the probability of this happening has been low (Kim and Singleton, 2012). Our results imply that all studied models fit the general shape of the yield curve data over the sampled period of January 2000–December 2017. The model of Ang and Piazzesi (2003) when estimated using the full data sample did not violate the zero lower bound. JSZ severely violated the ZLB and JLS did violate the ZLB, but not as frequently as the JSZ model. These violations occurred during the “near zero environment” period covering December 2008–December 2017.

As was mentioned in Section 61.2, among other things, PCA works best when the mean and variance are sufficient statistics to describe the data (Schlens, 2005). We know that for yield curve data, higher moments are also important in explaining patterns in the data. Thus, in order to explore this issue further, we use the yield curve data to compute independent components using the FastICA algorithm of Hyvärinen, Karhunen, and Oja (2001). This method begins with a model of linear combinations of unobservable (latent) and independent random statistical factors that are assumed to explain observable random variables. In our estimation, we assume that the bond yields are the observed random variables.
