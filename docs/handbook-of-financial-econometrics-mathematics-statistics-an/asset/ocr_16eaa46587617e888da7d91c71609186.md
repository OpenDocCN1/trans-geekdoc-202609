*Notes:* This table presents the estimated coefficients of the structural model of SEM with CFA in determining the capital structure and stock returns. The latent variable are stock returns (StReturn) and the capital structure on the basis of market value and book value (Capstruc_MV and Capstruc_BV). Long-term, short-term, and convertible leverage ratios are indicators of the capital structure. Unobservable attributes, latent variables, are asset structure (AtStruct), growth (Growth), industry (Industry), uniqueness (Uni_Ind), volatility (Vol_Rate), profitability (Profit), macroeconomic factors (Macroeeco), manager characteristics (Manager), value factors (Value) and Liquidity (Liquid). The detailed definitions of latent variables and observable indicators are reported in Table 35.3. The associated *t*-statistics are in parentheses. *, **, and *** denote the statistical significance at the 10%, 5%, and 1% level, respectively.
