So, in our implementation of the JLS model, γ0 is a 2 × 1 vector of coefficients and γ1 is a 2 × 5 vector of coefficients. Building on these ideas, the authors apply invariant transformations from a generic discrete-time VAR to a pricing model with PtN, the first N portfolios, (see Joslin et al. 2013b for detail) to adopt the canonical form of JSZ. To extract three latent factors, we employ PCA to extract the first three factors driving the variation in our sample of data on zero coupon yields. To extract macro factors for use in JLS, we follow the procedure of Ang and Piazzesi (2003) exactly as described above to obtain the first PC from various measures of real activity and inflation and use these two factors in conjunction with the three principal components obtained as described above as Zt.
