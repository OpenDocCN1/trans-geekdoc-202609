# 57.2  Maximization of Explanatory Versus Discriminant Powers: Two Conflicting Objectives

Principle component analysis (PCA) and Fisher’s discriminant analysis (FDA) are two often used multivariate analysis techniques. PCA performs dimensionality reduction in high-dimensional space while preserving as much of the data variabilities, i.e., the explanatory power, as possible. FDA performs dimensionality reduction while preserving much of the class discriminatory information, i.e., discriminant power, as possible. In this section, we show the two objectives, namely, the maximization of explanatory and the discriminant power can be achieved simultaneously only under very restrictive conditions.
