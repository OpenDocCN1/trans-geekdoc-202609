at 1,092 different brokerage houses and cover 14,115 firms. On average, an analyst follows about 7.75 firms in a given year, with a standard deviation of 6.82 firms. While some analysts cover just one firm in a given year, a few analysts cover more than 40 firms. Table 31.1 presents the number of analysts, firms and analyst-pairs (when two analysts covering at least one firm at the same time) in the network in the period of 1994–2016. Our results indicate that the number of analysts increases substantially from 2,293 to 4,601 in our sample period, while the number of firms and the average number of firms an analyst cover remain quite stable in these years. The number of analyst pairs increase rapidly from 39,436 to 93,188, partly due to the increase of number of analysts in the profession.
