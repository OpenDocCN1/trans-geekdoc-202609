# 46.6.2 *Equilibrium rate of return*

The *equilibrium rate of return* is a function of the parameter triplet $(S, G, H)$, defined by

$$\rho(S,G,H)=\frac{\phi(S,G,H)}{G}$$ $$=\lambda[1-\pi_I(0)]\frac{\omega}{G}-\eta\frac{\sum_{n=1}^{S} n\pi_I(n)}{G}-\gamma-\alpha\frac{H}{G}$$ $$-\beta\frac{\sum_{k=0}^{H-1}(H-k)\pi_C(k)}{G}. \tag{46.30}$$

The interpretations of the terms on the right-hand side of equation (46.30) are analogous to their counterparts in equation (46.24).

Recall that the firm’s capital investment is $G>0$ PEMUs. Assume the minimal rate of return desired by investors is $\delta>0$. The investors may have better investment options if the rate of return is not higher than $\delta$. Therefore, one need only search the solution space of $(S,A)$ that gives rise to $\rho \ge \delta$. From equation (46.30) we have

$$\lambda[1-\pi_I(0)]\omega-\eta\sum_{n=1}^{S} n\pi_I(n)-\gamma G-\alpha H-\beta\sum_{k=0}^{H-1}(H-k)\pi_C(k)\ge\delta G$$ $$\Rightarrow \lambda\omega \ge (\gamma+\delta)G+\alpha H \ge \min\{\delta+\gamma,\alpha\}(G+H). \tag{46.31}$$

Consequently, the following bounds on $S$ and $A$ serve to reduce the search space:

$$S \le A = G + H \le \frac{\lambda\omega}{\min\{\delta+\gamma,\alpha\}}. \tag{46.32}$$
