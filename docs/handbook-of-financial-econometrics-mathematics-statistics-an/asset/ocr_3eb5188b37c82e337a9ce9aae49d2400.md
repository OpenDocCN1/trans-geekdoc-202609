We simulate the measurement errors by drawing M = 50,000 sets of N = 25 measurement errors (u₁, …, u₂₅) from a multivariate normal distribution with mean zero and covariance (1/Tσ_f²)Σ. The length T of the time series of returns used in actual pricing tests typically ranges from a few dozens of months to a few dozens of years. We therefore run simulations for T = 30, 60, 120, 240, and 480 months. In Figure 98.2, we compare the bias of the slope estimator predicted by (98.12) to its true value, which is calculated as the average over M simulated individual slope estimates.
