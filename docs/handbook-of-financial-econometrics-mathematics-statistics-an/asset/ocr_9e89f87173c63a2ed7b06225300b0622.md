# H2. External Consistency: Instantaneous Correlation and Volatility of Variance. Both, the time series of econometric estimates of variation and the time series of calibrated variances, {V_t^RV}_{t=0}^T and {V_t^SV}_{t=0}^T, satisfy discretized stochastic volatility models (following the specification in (33.5)) with the same instantaneous correlation and volatility of variance parameters.

Since calibrated variance is based on closing prices, V_t^SV is applied to explain the return over the following trading day t+1. In contrast, econometric realized variation measures characterize the return over the same trading day, which naturally follows from the algorithm for their construction. Therefore, the discretized stochastic volatility model for realized measures of variance is implemented with the corresponding shifts in time indexes. While Hypothesis 1 addresses the internal consistency of the Heston model, Hypothesis 2 relates to the external consistency between the dynamics of the calibrated and statistically estimated time series of variances.

While the value of a parameter may be influenced by the change of measure, the value of a variable must be identical under both probability measures. Research (see Andersen et al., 2007) suggests that the presence of jumps in the underlying process cannot be rejected, but jump activity is missing from a pure stochastic volatility model. However, for option contracts with longer maturities jumps effectively increase the level of volatility, but may not significantly impact the implied volatility skew (see Rebonato, 2004). If V_t is a device for generating an implied volatility surface matching the market prices of options, then calibrated variance series would generally exceed the continuous component of variation and closely follow measures of total variation.

# H3. External Consistency: Level of variance. The mean calibrated variance exceeds the mean continuous component of variation and is close to the mean realized variation.

Hypothesis 3 should be distinguished from the results concerning the volatility risk premium, which is the difference between expectations of the future accumulated volatility under different probability measures.
