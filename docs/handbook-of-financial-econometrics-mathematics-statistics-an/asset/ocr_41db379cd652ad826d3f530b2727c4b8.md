Therefore, the upper and lower tail dependence coefficients of t-Gumbel copula $\lambda_{t-\text{Gumbel}}^U$ and $\lambda_{t-\text{Gumbel}}^L$ can be derived as follows.

$\lambda_{t-\text{Gumbel}}^U = \lim_{u\to1} \frac{1 - 2u + C_{t-\text{Gumbel}}(u,u,\dots,u)}{1 - u}, \quad (38.5)$

$= \lim_{u\to1} \frac{1 - 2u + w_t C_t(u,u,\dots,u) + w_{Gu} C_{\text{Gumbel}}(u,u,\dots,u)}{1 - u}, \quad (38.6)$

$= w_t \lim_{u\to1} \frac{1 - 2u + C_t(u,u,\dots,u)}{1 - u} + w_{Gu} \lim_{u\to1} \frac{1 - 2u + C_{\text{Gumbel}}(u,u,\dots,u)}{1 - u}, \quad (38.7)$

$= w_t \lambda_t^U + w_{Gu} \lambda_{Gu}^U, \quad (38.8)$

$= w_t \lambda_t^U + w_{Gu} \cdot \left(2 - 2^{1/\alpha}\right), \quad (38.9)$

$\lambda_{t-\text{Gumbel}}^L = \lim_{u\to0} \frac{C_{t-\text{Gumbel}}(u,u,\dots,u)}{u}, \quad (38.10)$

$= \lim_{u\to0} \frac{w_t C_t(u,u,\dots,u) + w_{Gu} C_{\text{Gumbel}}(u,u,\dots,u)}{u}, \quad (38.11)$

$= w_t \lim_{u\to0} \frac{C_t(u,u,\dots,u)}{u} + w_{Gu} \lim_{u\to0} \frac{C_{\text{Gumbel}}(u,u,\dots,u)}{u}, \quad (38.12)$

$= w_t \lambda_t^L + w_{Gu} \lambda_{Gu}^L, \quad (38.13)$

$= w_t \cdot \lambda_t^L, \quad (38.14)$

where $\lambda_t^U$ and $\lambda_t^L$ are the upper and lower tail dependence coefficients of the t copula, and $\lambda_{Gu}^U$ and $\lambda_{Gu}^L$ are the upper and lower tail dependence coefficients of the Gumbel copula, respectively. The complex expression of $\lambda_t^U$ and $\lambda_t^L$ can be found in Nikoloulopoulos et al. (2009). As shown by equations (38.9) and (38.14), the t-Gumbel copula can capture both upper and lower tail dependencies, and the upper tail dependence is stronger than the lower tail dependence.
