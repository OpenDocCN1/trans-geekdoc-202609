Figure 36.6 shows the wavelet correlations at each time scale of seven developing Asian stock market returns: Hong Kong (C1), India (C2), Indonesia (C3), South Korea (C4), Singapore (C5), Taiwan (C6) and Malaysia (C7).

Figure 36.6 shows that the Asian stock market returns present a positive correlation ranging from 0.11 to 0.82 and all the seven stock markets tend to move together at short- and long-term investment horizons, which reduces the international diversification opportunities. Comparing Figure 36.2 with Figure 36.6, we notice that the Asian stock markets seem to be less integrated than the EU stock markets. At low frequencies (128–265 days), high correlation values are observed and correlation coefficients range from 0.57 to 0.82. Unlike the other Asian stock markets, The Hong Kong and Singapore markets are highly correlated at long and short frequencies where the correlation coefficients ranging from 0.59 and 78 are significant at a 5% level.
