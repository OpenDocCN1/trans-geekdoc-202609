Mathematically, the price of the American put option at this node is Max(X – St, 0.4650) = Max(20 – 18.9525, 0.4650) = 1.0475.

# 84.7.1 Cox, Ross and Rubinstein

Cox, Ross and Rubinstein (1979) (hereafter CRR) propose an alternative choice of parameters that also create a risk-neutral valuation environment. The price multipliers, u and d, depend only on volatility σ and on δt, not on drift

u = e^{σ√δt}

d = 1/u.

To offset the absence of a drift component in u and d, the probability of an up move in the CRR tree is usually greater than 0.5 to ensure that the expected value of the price increases by a factor of exp[(r – q)δt] on each step. The formula for p is

p = [e^{(r−q)δt} − d] / [u − d].
