Notes: Wavelet coherency — the cone of influence, which indicates the region affected by edge effects, is depicted by the bold line. The color code for wavelet coherency ranges from blue (negative coherency) to red (positive coherency). The thick black continuous line in the figures isolates the regions where the wavelet coherence is statistically significant at the 10% level determined from a Monte Carlo simulation of 1,000. Time and frequency are represented on the horizontal and the vertical axis, respectively. Frequency is converted into years.
