$$\hat\psi = \arg\max L_2(\psi|\hat\phi, r_t).$$

From Engle (2002), we know that $\hat\theta_T = (\hat\phi_T, \hat\psi_T)$ has asymptotic normality. Thus, the estimates of parameters can be easily tested for their significance.
