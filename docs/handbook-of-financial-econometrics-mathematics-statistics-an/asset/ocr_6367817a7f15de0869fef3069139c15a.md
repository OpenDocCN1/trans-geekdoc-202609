Binomial tree valuation can be adapted to include the possibility of exercise at intermediate dates and not just the maturity date. This feature needs to be incorporated into the pricing of American options.

The first step of pricing an American option is the same as a European option. For an American put option, the second step is to take the max of the difference between the strike price of the stock and the price of the stock at that node N and the value of the European put option at node N.
