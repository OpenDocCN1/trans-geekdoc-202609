+   I. τ_s = τ_l = 0.2;

Tax timing option is defined as TO(t0,T) = (v_OP(t0,T)-v_BH(t0,T))/v_OP(t0,T) in percentage, where v_BH(t0,T), v_OP(t0,T) are bond prices under the buy-and-hold and optimal trading strategies, respectively.

The default process is exogenously specified, with default probability λ equal to 1% and recovery rate δ equal to 50%.
