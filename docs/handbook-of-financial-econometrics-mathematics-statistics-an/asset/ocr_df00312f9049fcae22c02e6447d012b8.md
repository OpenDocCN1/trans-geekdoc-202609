# (i) The Effect of Risk Averse Coefficient on Equity Premium.

Figure 48.3 and Table 48.3 present equity premium as a function of the risk aversion coefficient, β. No matter whether treating the risky asset as a stream of consumption claims or dividend claims, it can be seen that the equity premium becomes larger when the risk aversion coefficient increases in both the prospect theory utility model and exponential utility model. Moreover, one can observe that in the degenerate model, the effect of β on equity premium is almost linear. In our model, however, it looks like prospect utility will enlarge the effect of β on the equity premium. The larger the β value, the quicker the equity premium will increase.
