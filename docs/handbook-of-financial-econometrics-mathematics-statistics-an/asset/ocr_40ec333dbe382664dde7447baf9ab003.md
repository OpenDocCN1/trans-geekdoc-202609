In the context of fractional integration, we consider the following model,

$y_t = \beta_0 + \beta_1 t + x_t, \quad t = 1,2,\dots \tag{23.2}$
$(1 - L)^d x_t = u_t, \quad t = 1,2,\dots, \tag{23.3}$

where $y_t$ is the observed time-series; $\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$ are the coefficients corresponding, respectively, to the intercept and a linear time trend, and $x_t$ is supposed to be $I(d)$ adopting different forms such as white noise, Bloomfield-type autocorrelated disturbances, and given the monthly nature of the series examined, we also impose a seasonal monthly AR(1) structure for $u_t$.

We report the fractional differencing parameter d for the three standard cases examined in the literature, i.e., the case of no deterministic terms (i.e., $\beta_0 = \beta_1 = 0$ a priori in the undifferenced equation (23.2)), including only a constant ($\beta_0$ unknown, and $\beta_1 = 0$ a priori), and finally the case of a constant with a linear time trend ($\beta_0$ and $\beta_1$ unknown).$^6$ These values were obtained by using a grid-search with the tests of Robinson (1994) and choosing the values of $d$ that produced the lowest statistics.$^7$ Together with the estimates, we also present the 95% confidence band of the non-rejection values of $d$, using Robinson's (1994) parametric tests.
