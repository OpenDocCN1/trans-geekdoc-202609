Altman’s final estimated discriminant function is

Yᵢ = 0.012X₁ + 0.014X₂ + 0.033X₃ + 0.006X₄ + 0.999X₅.  (78.5)

His results show that this discriminant function has done well in predicting non-bankrupt firms.

Using (i) — the formula for the overall cost of misclassification (E) — and (ii) — the expected error rate — , Altman classified the initial sample. He concluded that all firms having a z-score greater than 2.99 clearly fall into the non-bankrupt sector, while those firms having a z-score below 1.81 are all bankrupt. The area between 1.81 and 2.99 will be defined as the zone of “ignorance” or the gray area.
