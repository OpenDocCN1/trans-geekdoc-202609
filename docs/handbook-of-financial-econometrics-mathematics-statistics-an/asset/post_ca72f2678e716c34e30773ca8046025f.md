## 70.4.3 Forecasting the probability of large price movements

The performance of the VG-NGARCH model can be examined based on its ability to predict large price movements due to extreme events. Define a large price movement event as the log-return exceeds 0.05 or is less than −0.05. From (70.8)–(70.12), the ex ante probability of large price movements is given by

P(|Y_t| >0.05 | F_{t-1}) = ∫_0^∞ [1 − Φ((0.05 − (m + φ_t + θg))/(σ√g))] h(g)dg + ∫_0^∞ Φ((−0.05 − (m + φ_t + θg))/(σ√g)) h(g)dg, (70.21)

where h(g) denotes the probability density function of a gamma distribution with shape and scale parameters ν_t and 1, respectively. For the GARJI model, the ex post probability of at least one jump occurring is given by

P(n_t ≥ 1 | F_{t-1}) = 1 − P(n_t = 0 | F_{t-1}), (70.22)

where the ex post probability distribution P(n_t | F_{t-1}) is given in (70.6). Compared to the ex ante probability of large price movements by the VG-NGARCH model in (70.21), the ex post probabilities of at least one jump by the GARJI model in (70.22) are more susceptible to moderate price movements. For example, the VG-NGARCH identifies June 16, 2006 with log-returns −0.0103, −0.0040, −0.0109, −0.0067, and −0.0027 as large price movements with ex ante probabilities 0.0573, 0.0191, 0.0493, 0.0412, and 0.0488 for Bank of America, Wells Fargo, J.P. Morgan, CitiGroup, and AIG, respectively. The corresponding ex post probabilities of at least one jump by the GARJI model are 0.1098, 0.1045, 0.0482, 0.1027, and 0.1131, respectively.

## VG NGARCH Versus GARJI Model for Asset Price Dynamics

On the other hand, both the ex ante probability of large price movements by the VG-NGARCH model in (70.21) and the ex post probabilities of at least one jump by the GARJI model in (70.22) are susceptible to price jumps due to financial market turbulences. For example, on September 16, 2008, the log-returns for the Bank of America, Wells Fargo, J.P. Morgan, CitiGroup, and AIG were −0.2398, −0.1007, −0.1066, −0.1642, and −0.9362, respectively. The corresponding ex ante probabilities of large price movements based on the VG NGARCH model were 0.7114, 0.5368, 0.4544, 0.6621, and 0.9437, respectively; while the ex post probabilities of at least one jump based on the GARJI model were 0.8308, 0.8348, 0.6806, 0.7980, and 0.9663, respectively.
