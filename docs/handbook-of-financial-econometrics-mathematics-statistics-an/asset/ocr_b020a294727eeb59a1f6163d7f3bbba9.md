# 56.4 Conclusion

Recent advances in information technologies have expanded rapidly and provided great opportunities for innovations in the field of financial services (Prause and Thurner, 2014). This fact is confirmed by our results: rise of digital technologies has the greatest impact on the financial industry (86% of respondents). Digitization is altering the way organizations operate, affecting both employers and employees. Organizations introduce disruptive technologies such as artificial intelligence, blockchain, big data, cloud computing, machine learning, information security technologies in order to maintain competitiveness. It is worth noting that all of the listed disruptive technologies have a high degree of investment attractiveness in the financial industry, but not all of them are already live or in production.
