## 5A.4 Variables and time horizon

The variables used in the empirical testing of the model were exactly the same as those used in Spies’ model. The following section explains the methodology followed in computing the various variables used in the model.

### 5A.4.1 Dividends (DIV)

Only equity dividend was considered in the computation of this variable. Preferred dividend was paid only up to 1955, and therefore, for consistency’s sake, preferred dividend was deducted from cash flow for the period and not added to equity dividends. Also, stock dividends were not considered in the computation.

### 5A.4.2 Short-term investment (IST)

Short-term investment is the net change in the corporation’s holdings of current and other short-term assets.

### 5A.4.3 Long-term investments (ILT)

Long-term investments is defined as the change in gross long-term fixed assets and non-current marketable securities.

### 5A.4.4 Debt financing (DF)

This component is simply the net change in the corporation’s liabilities, both long-term and current.
