PageRank value of $u_t$ can be calculated as

$$PR(u_t) = \sum_{u_i \in U_t} \frac{PR(u_i)}{L(u_i)}, \tag{18.2}$$

where $L(u_i)$ is the number of outbound links from $u_i$. After that, the weight of each tweet $x$ is the weight of the user $u(x)$ who has published it, and then the weighted count is

$$PositiveCount = \sum_{x} PR(u(x)),$$

$$NegativeCount = \sum_{y} PR(u(y)), \tag{18.3}$$

where $x$ and $y$ denote the positive tweet and negative tweet, respectively.

For a given stock $S_i$ and some trading day $t_j^i$, we first calculate its positive count, and negative count, and then combine them into one sentiment score, denoted as $SC_j^i$, that is

$$SC_j^i = \frac{PositiveCount_j^i}{PositiveCount_j^i + NegativeCount_j^i}. \tag{18.4}$$

Obviously, $SC_j^i \in [0,1]$, and the larger $SC_j^i$ is, the more positive the overall emotion is. $SC_j^i$ is used as the sentiment feature for our prediction model.
