# 83.5.4 Short vertical (bear) spread

Contrary to a long vertical spread, this strategy combines a long call (or put) with a high strike price and a short call (or put) with a low strike price. For example, an investor purchases a call with the exercise price of $60 and sells a call with the exercise price of $57.50. Given X1 = $60, X2 = $57.50 and the premium for the long call option is $0.31 and the short call option is $1.89, Table 83.10 shows the values for short vertical spread at different stock prices at time T.
