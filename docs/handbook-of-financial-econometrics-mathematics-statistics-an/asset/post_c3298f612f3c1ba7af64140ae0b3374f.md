## 57.5 Conclusion

The purpose of this study is to develop a multivariate credit-scoring model that meets the two objectives, namely, the maximization of the explanatory power and discriminant power. A compromised solution for the construction of a multivariate credit-scoring model is called for. The stepwise discriminant analysis by Pinches and Mingo (1973, 1975) fails to be an efficient solution as the hybrid approach proposed in this study outperforms it at the two objectives simultaneously. This suggests that one should abandon the stepwise approach. Instead, one should seek a compromised solution for the maximization of the two objectives. This enables the development of a multivariate credit-scoring model in a more accurate and economical way. Further research in this area could focus on developing a hybrid multivariate credit-scoring model using a more advanced goal programming approach.
