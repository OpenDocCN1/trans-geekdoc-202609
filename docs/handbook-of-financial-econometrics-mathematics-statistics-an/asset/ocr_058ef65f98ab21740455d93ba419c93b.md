# 53.5 Conclusion

The market-based accounting research is essentially grounded on the joint hypothesis that the security market is efficient and the market model is well specified in describing the stock return generating process. However, the phenomenon of PEADs suggests that either the market is not so efficient as expected or the market model employed in the literature is misspecified.

This study provides an alternative approach to investigate how the revision of systematic risk could affect the behavior of PEADs. We first provide evidence that there is about 68% of the firms exhibit ARCH effect. The incorporation of the ARCH into the conventional CAPM allows us to present a market model with time-varying systematic risk. The Kalman filter was then employed to estimate how the market revises its assessment of systematic risk in the presence of conditional heteroscedasticity subsequent to earnings announcement.
