# 67.7  Concluding Remarks

The pioneering research on IPO performance and issuance of Ibbotson *et al.* (1975) on the definition of hot and cold markets, Ritter (1984), which provided researchers with a critical explanation of what actually causes hot and cold markets, and, Helwege *et al.* (2004), that provided researchers with an analysis of the cyclical nature of IPO performance and issuance, provided researchers with an excellent theoretical base to explore IPO issuance and performance. This chapter provides some suggestions about how researchers might be able to rethink research projects on the cyclical nature of IPO performance and issuance. The chapter illustrates that by incorporating public policy initiatives such as the introduction of the JOB’s Act and major market events, such as the financial collapse that occurred from 2007 to 2008, and rethinking how we measure the heat of IPO issuance and performance can fundamentally change how researchers think about the cyclicality that has been historically associated with IPO issuance and performance.
