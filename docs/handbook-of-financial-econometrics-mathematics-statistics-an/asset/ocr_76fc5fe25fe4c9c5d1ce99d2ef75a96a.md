# 66.3.2.1 Firm value

Tobin’s Q ratio has been used extensively as a measure of a firm’s value. The use of Tobin’s Q for measuring firm value is based on fact that the long-term equilibrium market value of a firm must be equal to the replacement value of its assets, giving a Tobin’s Q value close to unity. We adopted the Tobin’s Q developed by Chung and Pruitt (1994), where Tobin’s Q = (MVE + PS + DEBT)/TA. Here, MVE is the product of a firm’s share price and the number of outstanding common stock shares. PS is the liquidating value of the firm’s outstanding preferred stock, DEBT is the value of the firm’s short-term liabilities net of its short-term assets plus the book value of the firm’s long-term debt, and TA is the book value of the total assets of the firm.

# 66.3.2.2 Social media

Social media include the following social media platforms: Facebook, YouTube, Twitter and other sites such as Google+ and LinkedIn. The data were gathered manually from the companies’ social networks (Facebook, YouTube and Twitter) and then categorized as official and unofficial. Official social networks were those with access links directly provided in company websites; unofficial ones were those with links not provided in company websites and were searched for in social networks.

Available Facebook data were collected through the Facebook Application Programming Interface (API) graph of each firm. We obtained the timestamp of the posts/comments, looked for the year of establishment and counted the number of “likes” in the company pages across different years.

Available YouTube data were obtained by examining the companies’ YouTube activities from the home pages of their official websites or unofficial social media networks. We also considered and recorded the number of views and subscribers of the companies’ YouTube channels.

Available Twitter data were obtained by examining each company’s Twitter activities from the home page of the firm’s official website. We also considered and recorded the number of tweets and followers as the proxy.
