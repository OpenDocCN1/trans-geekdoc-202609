Figure 36.11 shows the wavelet correlation at each time scale of five developing Latin American stock market returns: Argentina (C1), Mexico (C2), Chile (C3), Peru (C4) and Colombia (C5).

Comparing Figure 36.11 with Figures 36.2 and 36.6, we notice that the Latin American stock markets are the least integrated regional markets. Figure 36.11 shows that the Latin American stock market returns present a positive correlation which varies from 0.15 to 0.78 and consequently all the five stock markets tend to move together, which reduces the international diversification possibility within the Latin American Market. The correlation coefficient values indicate that the highest correlations between the Latin American stock markets are observed at a scale of (128-265 days) and the correlation coefficients range from 0.49 to 0.78. Argentina is highly correlated with Mexico beyond 16 trading days.
