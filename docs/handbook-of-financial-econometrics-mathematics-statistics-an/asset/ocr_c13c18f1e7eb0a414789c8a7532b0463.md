a linear combination of the following two equations\(^1\):

$$E(R_p)=\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}E(R_i),\quad\text{or}$$

$$\operatorname{Max}$$

$$-\operatorname{Var}(R_p)=-\operatorname{Var}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}x_i e_{it}\right),\tag{81.16a}$$

subject to

$$\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}x_i=1\quad\text{and}\quad x_{n+1}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i b_i.\tag{81.16b}$$

The first constraint is equivalent to requiring that the sum of the weights of the component securities is equal to one. The second constraint requires that the weight of the market index within the portfolio returns is equal to the summation of the weighted adjustment factors of the component securities. This requirement is as described in equation (81.16b).

Combining the above two objective functions with the two constraints yields a Lagrangian function:

$$\begin{aligned}
P={}&\Phi E(R_p)-\operatorname{Var}(R_p)+\lambda_1\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i-1\right)+\lambda_2\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i b_i-x_{n+1}\right)\\
={}&\Phi\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}x_i a_i-\sum_{i=1}^{n+1}\operatorname{Var}(e_{it})+\lambda_1\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i-1\right)+\lambda_2\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}x_i b_i-x_{n+1}\right).
\end{aligned}\tag{81.16c}$$

The only difference between this maximization function and the minimization shown in either Elton et al. (2014) or Francis and Kim (2013), beyond intent, is that instead of fixing some arbitrary value of return needed (E*), an attempt can be made to quantify the level of risk aversion that the investors of the portfolio require, thereby placing the portfolio on the efficient frontier not by desired return but by level of utility, as discussed by Elton et al. (2014) and Francis and Kim (2013). This coefficient of risk aversion is denoted by the Greek letter Φ (Phi). When low values are exhibited, risk aversion is pronounced; when high values are in evidence, substantial risk-taking is allowed.

¹ The maximization of the negative of the variance is equivalent to the minimization of the variance itself. This can be viewed as originating from the negative spectrum of the number line and maximizing toward zero.
