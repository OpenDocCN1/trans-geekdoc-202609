One hundred daily returns for each firm before the announcement of earnings were used to estimate systematic risk by regressing them against the equally weighted market indices. The abnormal returns of each firms was then estimated for the 31 days holding period. The cumulative abnormal returns (CARs) were then calculated for each portfolio.
