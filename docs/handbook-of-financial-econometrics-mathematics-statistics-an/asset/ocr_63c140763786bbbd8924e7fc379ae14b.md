# 75.6 Conclusions

When dependent as well as independent variables in multiple regression models are measured with error, estimated coefficients are likely to be biased. We use the direct and reverse regression technique to bound true estimates of multiple regression model and derive standard error of reverse regression estimates. We approximate these standard errors using variances and covariances of estimated coefficients of reverse regression equation. These standard errors are needed to test the significance of the reverse regression estimates. The direct and reverse regression has potential applicability to capital market research in general and test of alternative multi-factor asset pricing models in particular.
