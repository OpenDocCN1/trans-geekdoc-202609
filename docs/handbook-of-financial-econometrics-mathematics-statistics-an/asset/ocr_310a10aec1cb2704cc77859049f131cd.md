Some strategies seem focused on providing absolute returns while others are more intent on offering risk-adjusted performance. The three segments of this sample of alternative investments that offer both above average risk-adjusted performance and returns are convertible arbitrage, distressed securities, and European equities. Strategies focused on equity short bias and the Pacific Rim fail to offer returns or risk-adjusted performance and have greater than average risk. Multi-strategy, fixed income arbitrage, merger arbitrage, and equity market neutral strategies offer greater than average risk-adjusted returns but have lower than average absolute returns and standard deviations than the average alternative investments.
