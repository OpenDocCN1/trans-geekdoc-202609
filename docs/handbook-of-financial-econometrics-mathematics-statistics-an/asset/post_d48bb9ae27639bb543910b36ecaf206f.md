## 35.5 Conclusion

This chapter utilizes the SEM with CFA approach to show how unobservable attributes affect the capital structure. Specifically, we apply the sample between 2001 and 2012 to the SEM with CFA system and jointly determine the capital structure and stock return. We have a comprehensive analysis to confirm whether the influences of important factors related to accounting information, macroeconomic and manager characters on the capital structure are consistent with corporate finance theories discussed in the previous literature.

The empirical results show that only profitability, volatility, and financial rating are significant attributes on the decision of capital structure, while all attributes become significant determinants when we determine the capital structure and stock returns simultaneously. This finding indicates that the interaction of a firm’s leverage and its stock price should be necessarily considered in capital structure research. In addition to the proof of trade-off between financial distress and agency costs in previous theories, our results confirm the endogenous supply relationship between public and private debts. Moreover, the interrelation between the leverage ratios and the stock returns verifies the signal theory under the assumption of asymmetric information between managers and investors. Only significantly negative influences of stock returns on market-value-based leverage ratios support Welch’s (2004) statement that although the stock price is of importance in dynamic capital structure, a firm would not readjust its capital structure in response to the changes in stock prices.

In sum, this chapter shows the advantage of setting latent attributes for clarification and confirmation of theories in capital structure. We would suggest using SEM with CFA to capture the appropriate factors of firms’ leverage decisions. In future research, the personal taxes (Lin and Flannery, 2013) and the differences in beliefs between the manager and outside investors (Yang, 2013) can be considered in the investigation of joint determinants of capital structure and stock return if the related information is available.
