# 19.5 Conclusion and Relevance of Factor Risk

In order to provide readers a bird’s-eye view of market characteristics, relevance of factor risk and recommended investment strategy we have provided a summarized chart for the developed markets, in Exhibit 19III: A and for emerging markets in Exhibit 19III: B.

# 19.5.1 Developed markets

As far as the various market factors models and their impact on market risk is concerned, it is observed that among the 22 developed markets studied, the AFP 5-factor model has reduced market risk to its lowest in the case of 10 countries, out of which, 7 countries have generated highest Alpha. This is followed by the AFP 6-factor model which has reduced market risk to its minimum in the case of 5 countries out of which 4 countries have generated highest Alpha. The CAPM has the lowest market risk in 5 countries, but highest Alpha in 8 countries. Compared to the above, the FF 5-factor model has produced lowest market risk in 2 countries and highest Alpha in 2 countries.
