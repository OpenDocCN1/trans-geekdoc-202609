Notes: This table reports union-root tests for the time-series of GVIX, VIX and GV-Spread, respectively. The augmented Dickey–Fuller (ADF) tests a unit-root present in an autoregressive progress. Phillips–Perron (PP) test makes a non-parametric correction for any serial correlation and heteroskedasticity in the regressive errors by modifying the ADF test statistics. Zivot–Andrews (ZA) test examines unit-root of time-series with endogenous structural break. Specifically, ZA test is a sequential test which utilizes the full sample and uses a different dummy variable for each possible break date. The break date is selected where the t-statistic from the ADF test of unit-root is at a minimum (most negative). This table presents the least ZA test statistic of each time-series of GVIX, VIX, and GV-Spread, respectively. *denotes significant rejection of union-root at the 5% level.
