Unobservable attributes include asset structure (AtStruct), non-debt tax shield (Nd_tax), growth (Growth), uniqueness (Uni_Ind), size (Size), volatility (Vol_Rate), profitability (Profit), macroeconomic factors (Macroeco), and manager characteristics (Manager).
