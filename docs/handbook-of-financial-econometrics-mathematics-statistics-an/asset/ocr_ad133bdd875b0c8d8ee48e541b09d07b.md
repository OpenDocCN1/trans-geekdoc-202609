We minimize the quadratic function in equation (42A.3) given three constraints to infer the posterior risk-neutral probability implied by TAIEX call options.

min_{P_i ≥ 0} Σ_{i=0}^n (P_i - P_i')²,  (42A.3)
s.t. Σ_{i=0}^n P_i = 1,  (42A.4)
F_b ≤ Σ_{i=0}^n (P_i × F_{T,i}) × e^{-rT} ≤ F_a,  (42A.5)
c_{b,j} ≤ Σ_{i=0}^n (P_i × c_{T,i,j}) × e^{-rT} ≤ c_{a,j},  j = 1,...,m,
c_{T,i,j} = max(F_{T,i} - K_j, 0).  (42A.6)

The implied risk-neutral probability P_i is the approximation of lognormal probability P_i'. The aggregation of implied risk-neutral probabilities at each ending nodal equals 1 shown in the constraint (42A.4). In the constraint (42A.5), the bid price F_b and the ask price F_a of TAIEX futures at the initial period represent the lower and upper bounds of expected value of TAIEX futures prices at the maturity date discounted by the riskless interest rate r. The constraint (42A.6) represents that the present value for expected payoff of TAIEX options is between the bid price c_{b,j} and the ask price c_{a,j} observed in TAIEX call options. Let c_{T,i,j} denote the payoff of TAIEX call options for the jth striking price at the ith ending nodal; F_{T,i} is the price of TAIEX futures at the ith ending nodal of the maturity date T; and K_j is the jth striking price of TAIEX call and put options.
