The estimates of a bivariate VAR system of equations (58.5) and (58.6) are reported in Table 58.2. As may be seen from columns 1 and 4, markets for Hong Kong, Thailand, and Malaysia present a positive and significant relation between VaR and the expected return on its local markets. For the cross-market effects, a positive relation between VaR and expected returns is significant for the relationship between China and the other Asian stock markets (columns 3 and 6); the only exception is Malaysia, which is statistically insignificant. However, the coefficients (columns 2 and 5) are negative between the US and the markets in Korea, Hong Kong, and Malaysia. In general, we are unable to reach a consistent conclusion for the sign and significance level with respect to the downside risk–return tradeoff, except for the downside risk from China.
