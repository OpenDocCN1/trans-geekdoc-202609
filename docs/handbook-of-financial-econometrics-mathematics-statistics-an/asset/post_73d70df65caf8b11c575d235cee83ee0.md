Notes: This table reports the descriptive statistics for a sample of Taiwanese publicly listed firms from 2008 to 2015. The variables are defined as follows: Tobin's Q = (common stock + preferred stocks + short- and long-term loans and debt)/total assets. Facebook is a dummy variable that equals 1 if a firm's home page provides a link to an official Facebook page and 0 otherwise. YouTube is a dummy variable that equals 1 if a firm's home page provides a link to an official YouTube channel and 0 otherwise. Twitter is a dummy variable that equals 1 if a firm's home page provides a link to an official Twitter account and 0 otherwise. Others is a dummy variable that equals 1 if a firm's web home page has another social media contact icon and 0 otherwise. BankRelationship is measured by the logarithm of 1 plus the total number of banks lending to the firm in a fiscal year. BankDebt_TA is measured by the total bank loans divided by the total assets. Age is the number of years since the firm was established. LnSize is the firm size measured by the logarithm of a firm's book value of total assets. Intangible is measured by total goodwill and intangible assets divided by total assets. Leverage is measured by the book value of a firm's total liabilities divided by total assets. Cash flow ratio (OCF_CL) is measured by the book value of operating cash flows divided by current liabilities. Sales growth rate (SaleGrowth) is measured by (Sales_t - Sales_{t-1})/Sales_{t-1}. Current ratio (CR_Assets) is measured by the book value of a firm's current assets divided by current liabilities. Working capital ratio (NWC_Assets) is measured by the book value of working capital divided by total assets.

Panel B summarizes the mean deviations of the main variables — Tobin’s *Q*, social media, and bank relationships across various SIC industries. The accommodation and food service industry has the highest Facebook usage rate of 0.481 and is accompanied by the highest Tobin’s *Q* of 1.771 among all. By contrast, this industry has relatively low banking relationships of 1.305 and median bank debt ratio of 0.163. The transportation and storage industry has the highest usage rate (0.138) on YouTube and is associated with the highest banking relationships of 1.957 and a relatively low Tobin’s *Q* of 0.973. These industries with higher social media usage have been rapidly developing in Taiwan.

Panel C presents the main variables across the years 2008–2015. We find that all types of social media usage increase annually. Among all, Facebook showed a significant growth rate of 60 times from 0.003 in 2008 to 0.177 in 2015, followed by YouTube which has the second-highest growth rate of 11.6 times. By contrast, bank relationships decreased annually from 1.636 in 2008 to 1.558 in 2015. The same descending trend appeared on the bank debt ratio from 0.177 in 2008 to 0.164 in 2015. Tobin’s *Q* exhibited a slightly growing trend from the trough of the financial crisis in 2008, 0.765, rose to 1.067 in 2015.
