**Notes**

### A. Return model:

(Ret1) ΔV\_H = C\_s ΔP\_s + C\_f ΔP\_f ⇒ hedge ratio = H = C\_f / C\_s , C\_s = units of spot commodity and C\_f = units of futures contract

(Ret2) R\_h = R\_s + h R\_f , R\_s = (S\_t - S\_{t-1}) / S\_{t-1}

(a) R\_f = (F\_t - F\_{t-1}) / F\_{t-1} ⇒ hedge ratio: h = C\_f / C\_s

(b) R\_f = (F\_t - F\_{t-1}) / S\_{t-1} ⇒ hedge ratio: h = C\_f / C\_s .

### B. Objective function:

- (O₁) Minimize Var(R\_h) = C\_s²σ\_s² + C\_f²σ\_f² + 2C\_sC\_fσ\_sf or Var(R\_h) = σ\_s² + h²σ\_f² + 2hσ\_sf
- (O₂) Maximize E(R\_h) − ½ Var(R\_h)
- (O₃) Maximize [E(R\_h)−R\_F]/Var(R\_h) (Sharpe ratio), R\_F = risk-free interest rate
- (O₄) Maximize E[U(W)], U(.) = utility function, W = terminal wealth
- (O₅) Minimize Γ\_v(R\_h), Γ\_v(R\_h) = −v Cov(R\_h, (1 − F(R\_h))^{v−1})
- (O₆) Maximize E[R\_h] − Γ\_v(R\_h)
- (O₇) Minimize V\_{δ,α}(R\_h) = ∫\_{-∞}^{δ} (δ − R\_h)^α dG(R\_h), α > 0
- (O₈) Maximize U(R\_h) = E[R\_h] − V\_{δ,α}(R\_h)
- (O₉) Minimize Var(W\_t) = Var(∑\_{t=1}^{T} C\_{st}ΔS\_t + C\_{ft}ΔF\_t)
- (O₁₀) Minimize Z\_α σ\_h √τ − E[R\_h] τ.

### C. Hedging effectiveness:

- (E₁) e = (1 − Var(R\_h)/Var(R\_s))
- (E₂) e = R\_h^{ce} − R\_s^{ce}, R\_h^{ce} (R\_s^{ce}) = certainty equivalent return of hedged (unhedged) portfolio
- (E₃) e = [E(R\_h)−R\_F]/Var(R\_h) ÷ [E(R\_s)−R\_F]/Var(R\_s) or e = [E(R\_h)−R\_F]/Var(R\_h) − [E(R\_s)−R\_F]/Var(R\_s)
- (E₄) e = 1 − V\_{δ,α}(R\_h)/V\_{δ,α}(R\_s).
