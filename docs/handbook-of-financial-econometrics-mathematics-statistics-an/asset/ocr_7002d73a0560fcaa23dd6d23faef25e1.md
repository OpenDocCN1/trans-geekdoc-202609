Table 23.7 focuses on the procedure for testing the homogeneity condition in a pairwise representation of the series through the Robinson and Yajima (2002) approach as mentioned above. Remember that this is a necessary condition for cointegration in the bivariate representation of the series. Performing this method, though we do not display the specific values for the statistic, we denote with “YES” in Table 23.7 the cases where the two series display a similar order of integration at the 5% level.

We observe that for most of the countries the hypothesis of equal orders of integration in a vis-à-vis representation cannot be rejected. The main exceptions are Brunei and particularly, Myanmar. For the former country, we only find five countries that satisfy the initial condition for cointegration (Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam), which could be a sign of the relative small trading power that Brunei has compared to its neighboring countries. For Myanmar, this condition is only satisfied in the cases of Brunei and Vietnam, this being proof of its closed economy in the last years. For the remaining countries we find a large number of cases of potential cointegrated relationships,9 which could be another good sign of the homogeneity among the countries in the region.
