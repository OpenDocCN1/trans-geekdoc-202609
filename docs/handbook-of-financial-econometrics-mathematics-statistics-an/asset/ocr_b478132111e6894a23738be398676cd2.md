Table 23.3 focuses on the case of autocorrelated errors by means of using the exponential spectral model of Bloomfield (1973). This is a non-parametric approach to model I(0) processes that produces autocorrelations decaying exponentially as in the AR case and that accommodates extremely well in the context of fractional integration (Gil-Alana, 2004). Thus, it approximates ARMA structures with a small number of parameters.
