where $\tilde{Y}(t)$ are earnings of the leveraged firm at time $t$, $\tilde{ROA}(t)$ is the rate of return on total asset for a leverage firm at time $t$, $\tilde{r}(t)=\frac{\tilde{ROA}(t)}{1-L}$ is the rate of return on total equity at time $t$, normally distributed with mean $\tilde{r}(t)$ and variance $\sigma^2(t)$, $A'(0)=(1-L)A(0)$ is the total equity at time 0, and $L$ is the debt to total assets ratio.

In the rest of this study, we use the firm’s earnings in terms of rate of return on equity and total equity.

In addition, we denote $\dot{n}(t)=dn(t)/dt$, where $n(t)$ is the number of shares outstanding of common stock at time $t$. We allow that a firm can finance its new investments through retained earnings, external equity, and new debt when it faces a growth opportunity. We also assume a target leverage ratio for a firm by allowing that a firm can issue a proportional debt only to meet its target leverage ratio; therefore, a firm can grow not only by issuing new debt, but also by retaining its earnings and issuing external equity. Then, we can define the change of investment as follows:

$\frac{dA(t)}{dt}=\dot{A}(t)=g(t)A(0)e^{\int_0^t g(s)ds}=\tilde{Y}(t)-\tilde{D}(t)+\lambda\dot{n}(t)p(t)+L\dot{A}(t),\quad(97.3)$

where $\tilde{D}(t)$ is the total dollar dividend at time $t$, $p(t)$ is the price per share at time $t$, $\lambda$ is the degree of market perfections, $0<\lambda\le1$, $\lambda\dot{n}(t)P(t)$ is the proceeds of new equity issued at time $t$, and $L$ is the debt to total assets ratio.

Note that the value of $\lambda$ equal to one indicates that the new shares can be sold by the firm at current market prices.

Equation (97.3) is a generalized equation which Higgins (1977, 1981, 2008) uses to derive his sustainable growth rate. Higgins’ equation allows only internal source and external debt financing, while our model, equation (97.3) also allows external equity financing.

We can further modify equation (97.3) as follows: $\dot{A}(t)[1-\tilde{r}(t)(1-\tilde{d}(t))]=\tilde{r}(t)\cdot A(t)[1-\tilde{d}(t)]+\frac{\lambda\dot{n}(t)p(t)}{1-L},\quad(97.4)$ where $\tilde{d}(t)$ is the payout ratio at time $t$.

From equation (97.4), we can obtain the generalized sustainable growth rate as follows: $g(t)=\frac{\dot{A}(t)}{A(t)}=\frac{\tilde{r}(t)[1-d(t)]}{1-\tilde{r}(t)[1-d(t)]}+\frac{\lambda\dot{n}(t)p(t)/E(t)}{1-\tilde{r}(t)[1-d(t)]},\quad(97.5)$ where $E(t)$ is the total equity at time $t$.
