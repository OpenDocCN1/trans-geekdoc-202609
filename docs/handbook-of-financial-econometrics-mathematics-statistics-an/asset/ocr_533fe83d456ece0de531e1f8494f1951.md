More specifically, the contribution of a variable m to the $h$-step ahead forecast error variance of variable n may be calculated as

$$\sum_{i=0}^{h-1}\theta_{mn}^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{h-1} (i_n' P \Phi_i i_m)^2, \qquad (60A.11)$$

where $i_s$ is the $s$th column of $I_k$. Similar to impulse-response functions, confidence intervals may be derived analytically or estimated using various resampling techniques.
