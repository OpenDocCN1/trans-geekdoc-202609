To explicitly consider option bound with skewness and kurtosis, in this section, we derive upper and lower bounds for the expected payoffs of both call and put options. Let C(t) be the current price of a call option with strike price K on a stock with current price S(t) expiring at date T. The current expectation of the terminal payoff of the call is given by R_C(t) = E_t [max[S(T) - K, 0]], where E_t(·) is the conditional expectation operator at current time t and T > t. Our objective is to find out the least upper bounds and the greatest lower bound for R_C(t) over all possible distributions with the given mean, variance, skewness, and kurtosis.
