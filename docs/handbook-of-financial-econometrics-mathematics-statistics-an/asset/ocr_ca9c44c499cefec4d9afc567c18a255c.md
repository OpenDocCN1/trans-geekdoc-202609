When one uses a Bessel function, V.G. has a closed-form solution. The multivariate extension of V.G. has conditional expectation function and slopes are dependent on the variance.

The stochastic process in V.G. changed randomly with time and that process makes gains and losses. More, the stochastic process has short and long motions and it has compound Poisson approximations. The empirical results of Madan and Seneta (1990) illustrate that when kurtosis is increased, V.G. options increase above BS values. The latter statement is consistent with the presence of jumps. Further, the results show that the relationship between kurtosis and V.G. options is similar for on-the-money options as opposed to in- or out-the-money options.

Bates (1996) developed a model to price American forex options on stochastic volatility/jumps diffusion processes under systematic jump and volatility risk. The options analyzed in Bates (1996) are Deutsche Mark (DM) options over the period from January 1984 to June 1991, on page 79 from Philadelphia Stock Exchange (PHLX). He argues that prior studies on forex options show that there are conditional and unconditional moments. Moreover, ‘unconditional distribution of log-differenced exchange rates is leptokurtic’. And when the relationship is inverse; normally there is excess kurtosis.

He proposes a stochastic volatility/jump-diffusion model. In that model, skewness is due to correlations between exchange rate or volatility shocks or due to non-zero average jumps. For kurtosis, it arises either from volatile volatility or substantial jump component. The stochastic model in Bates 19960 has square root variance and Bates (1996) state that the advantages of the square root variance are (i) it allows for systematic volatility risk, and (ii) the process generates analytical solution for pricing forex options. He went further and presented terminal payoffs under the risk-neutral probability measure and a call option which floes a constant-volatility geometric BM for the exchange rate. The later model is similar to the model derived in this chapter and it will be shown later in the chapter.
