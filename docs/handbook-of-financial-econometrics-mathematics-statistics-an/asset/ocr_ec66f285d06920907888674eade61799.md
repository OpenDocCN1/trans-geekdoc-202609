to study the possibility of structural breaks, examining how the effects of the financial crisis (2008) or the East Asian crisis (1997) could also affect the estimation of the d parameter, since these events could have eventually biased the estimation of the differencing parameter upwards thereby suggesting that shocks may take longer than they really do.

We could conclude the paper by saying that from an empirical perspective there is a considerably good trading base for the ASEAN Economic Community to be established, as the series corresponding to trade balances of its member countries do not show explosive behavior and in several cases present similar levels of differentiation. Certain cointegration levels between the countries are also perceived, which can be considered as a positive sign, with long run equilibrium relationships which are mean reverting though persistent; yet more effort should be made in implementing policies that could reinforce these cointegration relationships between the ASEAN countries. Yet, other approaches examining for instance the possibility of cyclicalities in the data should be investigated in future papers.
