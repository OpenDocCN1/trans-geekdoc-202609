Table 23.9 displays the results of testing the null hypothesis of no cointegration against the alternative of fractional cointegration through the Hausman test of Marinucci and Robinson (2001). The first two values in each case correspond to the t-statistics relative to each individual series, while the lowest value is the estimate of d obtained in the bivariate representation of the series under the assumption of equal orders of integration.
