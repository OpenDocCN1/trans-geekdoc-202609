A total of three mixture copulas are constructed in this section, among which the t-Gumbel copula can comprehensively capture the typical features of risk dependence with the least copula components and is considered the most suitable for modeling bank risk dependence. For comparison, the less favorable Gaussian–Gumbel copula and Gaussian–Gumbel–Clayton copula are also constructed. Compared with individual copulas, the mixture copula is more flexible and has stronger feature capturing capability; however, it is noteworthy that the addition of more component copulas also increases the complexity of the model and brings in extra parameters, leading to higher computational costs and efforts in model estimation and application.
