Next, we illustrate the impact of the strength of the correlations on the bias. Instead of arbitrarily varying all N(N − 1)/2 non-diagonal elements in the covariance matrix Σ, we use a simplified version of the covariance matrix parametrized by only two values: the variance σ² (set equal for all assets) and the correlation ρ (set equal for all pairs of assets). As before, the variance σ² = 8.413 is chosen as the average of the idiosyncratic variances of the 25 Fama–French portfolios while the correlation ρ is varied between ρ = 0 (i.e., uncorrelated errors) and ρ = 1 (perfect positively correlated errors). We choose the length of the time series T = 60. All other parameters are left as chosen in the previous section. Once the parameters are set, the simulation is conducted as described above.
