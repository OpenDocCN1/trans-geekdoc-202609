Again, substituting for $R_i$, $r_i$, $R_j$, and $r_j$,

$\sigma_{ij}=E\big[\big[(a_i+b_{i1}I_1+b_{i2}I_2+\cdots+b_{iL}I_L+c_i)\n-(a_i+b_{i1}\bar I_1+b_{i2}\bar I_2+\cdots+b_{iL}\bar I_L)\big]^*\n\big[(a_j+b_{j1}I_1+b_{j2}I_2+\cdots+b_{jL}I_L+c_j)\n-(a_j+b_{j1}\bar I_1+b_{j2}\bar I_2+\cdots+b_{jL}\bar I_L)\big]\big].$

Again, noting that $a_i$ cancels and combining the terms involving the same b's,

$\sigma_{ij}=E\{\big[b_{i1}(I_1-\bar I_1)+b_{i2}(I_2-\bar I_2)+\cdots+b_{iL}(I_L-\bar I_L)+c_i\big]\n\times\big[b_{j1}(I_1-\bar I_1)+b_{j2}(I_2-\bar I_2)+\cdots+b_{jL}(I_L-\bar I_L)+c_j\big]\}$.

Next, multiply out terms, again concentrating on the terms involving $b_{i1}$:

$E\big[b_{i1}b_{j1}(I_1-\bar I_1)^2+b_{i1}b_{j2}(I_1-\bar I_1)(I_2-\bar I_2)+\cdots+\n\quad b_{i1}b_{jL}(I_1-\bar I_1)(I_L-\bar I_L)+b_{i1}(I_1-\bar I_1)c_j\big].$

Because the covariance between two indexes is zero and the covariance between any residual and an index is zero,

$b_{i1}b_{j1}E(I_1-\bar I_1)^2=b_{i1}b_{j1}\sigma_{I1}^2.$

To conclude, remember that the covariance of the residuals is equal to zero and therefore,

$\sigma_{ij}=b_{i1}b_{j1}\sigma_{I1}^2+b_{i2}b_{j2}\sigma_{I2}^2+\cdots+b_{iL}b_{jL}\sigma_{IL}^2.\qquad(81B.4)$
