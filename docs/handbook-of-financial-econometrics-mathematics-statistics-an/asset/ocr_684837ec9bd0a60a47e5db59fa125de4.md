Implied standard deviation then can be solved from equation (106.13), shown below:

$\sigma = \frac{C\sqrt{2\pi}}{S\sqrt{T}}$.

Note that Brenner and Subrahmanyam's method can only be used to estimate implied standard deviation from at-the-money or at least not too far in- or out-of-the-money options.

Lai et al. (1992) derived a closed-form solution for the ISD in terms of the delta $\frac{\partial C}{\partial S}$, $\frac{\partial C}{\partial X}$, and other observable variables.
