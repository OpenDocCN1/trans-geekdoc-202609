# 6A.2 Measurable indexes of turning point method

The measurable indexes of the 4*4 matrices are (a) ratio of accurate forecasts (RAF), (b) ratio of worst forecasts (RWF), (c) ratio of accurate to worst forecasts (RAWP), (d) ratio of inaccurate forecasts (RIF).

The RAF is $\frac{f_{11}+f_{22}+f_{33}+f_{44}}{\sum\sum f_{ij}}$, $i,j = 1,2,3,4$; RWF is $\frac{f_{12}+f_{21}+f_{34}+f_{43}}{\sum\sum f_{ij}}$, $i,j = 1,2,3,4$; RAWP is $\frac{f_{11}+f_{22}+f_{33}+f_{44}}{f_{12}+f_{21}+f_{34}+f_{43}}$, $i,j = 1,2,3,4$; RIF is $\frac{f_{13}+f_{14}+f_{23}+f_{24}+f_{31}+f_{32}+f_{41}+f_{42}}{\sum\sum f_{ij}}$, $i,j = 1,2,3,4$. The most accuracy of predication model, that the higher RAF and RAWP is needed, and the lowest RWF and RIF is also needed is to be considered.
