Recall that the probability density function of the noncentral Chi-square distribution with noncentrality λ and degree of freedom v is

P_{χ_v²(λ)}(x) = ½ (x/λ)^{(v-2)/4} I_{(v-2)/2}(√(λ x)) e^{-(λ+x)/2} = P(x; v, λ).

Let Q(x; v, λ) = ∫_x^∞ P_{χ_v²(λ)}(y) dy. Then letting w' = 2w and x' = 2x, we have

C₁ = S_t e^{-rτ} ∫_y^∞ e^{-(x+w)/2} (w/x)^{1/(4-2β)} I_{1/(2-β)}(2√(x w)) dw

= S_t e^{-rτ} ∫_y^∞ e^{-(x'+w')/2} (w'/x')^{1/(4-2β)} I_{1/(2-β)}(2√(x' w')) dw'

= S_t e^{-rτ} Q(2y; v, x')

= S_t e^{-rτ} Q(2y; 2 + 2/(2-β), 2x), (109.32)

obtained by noting that (v - 2)/2, 1/(2 - β), implying v = 2 + 2/(2 - β). Analogously, with w' = 2w, x' = 2x, and I_n(z) = I_{-n}(z), we have
