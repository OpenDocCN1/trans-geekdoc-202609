It follows that, in the absence of arbitrage opportunities, portfolio C must be worth at least as much as portfolio D today. Hence:

$$p \ge K e^{-rT} - S_0.$$  (103C.5)

Therefore, we obtain the upper and lower bounds for European calls and puts, respectively:

$$S_0 - K e^{-rT} \le c \le S_0,$$  (103C.6)
$$K e^{-rT} - S_0 \le p \le K e^{-rT}.$$  (103C.7)
