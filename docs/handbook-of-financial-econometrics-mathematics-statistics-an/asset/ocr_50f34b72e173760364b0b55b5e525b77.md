may reject the SMLs even though other MV criteria may indicate they are economically reasonable.

As with the traditional SML tests, the results are sensitive to the way securities are grouped into portfolios — the size and book-to-market dataset provides strong statistical evidence against the CAPM, while the size dataset provides strong support for it. Finally, the results are sensitive to estimates of sample means, CAPM means, and betas based on a fixed 120-month or an expanding window.
