# 36.2.2 Methodology

In this study, we use the wavelet approach to examine the linkage between the global stock market returns. In this section, we give a brief overview of the wavelet analysis approach. The fundamental idea of this approach is to decompose a time series into time-frequency domains. There are generally two ways of decomposition under the wavelet method: the discrete wavelet transform (DWT) and the continuous wavelet transform (CWT). The application of DWT in economics and finance was done by Gençay et al. (2001a, 2001b, 2005), Gallegati and Gallegati (2007), Gallegati (2008), Ozun and Cifter (2010) and Habimana (2017), among others. Recently, the CWT was used for the purpose of economic and financial data analysis by Aguiar-Conraria et al. (2008), Vacha and Barunik (2012), Vacha et al. (2013), Aguiar-Conraria and Soares (2014), Sousaa et al. (2014), Filip et al. (2016), Kristoufek et al. (2016) and Das et al. (2018), among others. In this research, to transform the stock market return series, we use the CWT (wavelet coherence analysis) and the DWT (wavelet correlation and wavelet multiple cross-correlation). Before introducing wavelet correlation, wavelet coherence and wavelet multiple cross-correlation, we begin by providing basic definitions of wavelet.
