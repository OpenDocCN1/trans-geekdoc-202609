# 104.2.5 Omega ratio model

As a lower partial moment (LPM), the Omega ratio is free from the assumption of Gaussian distribution. Kapsos et al. (2014a) develop the worst-case Omega ratio model to deal with uncertainty in asset returns. In this model, the Omega ratio optimization is transformed as a linear program and hence can be solved as a mixed integer linear programming problem. When there is one scenario, i.e., i = 1 in Kapsos et al. (2014a), the portfolio is the Omega ratio maximization under certain returns. The following rebalancing model by minimizing the Omega ratio, minimizing the trading costs, and minimizing the portfolio weights of the short-sales, is further transformed into a linearized single-objective function.
