A comparison of the mean value (M) shows that users were more critical of the proposals than developers for all seven questions. Only Question 2 slightly failed to meet the 5% significance threshold, with a p-value of 0.055. For each of the other six questions, users and developers adopted systematically different response behavior. The differences in Questions 3, 5, 6, and 12 were extremely significant (p ≤ 0.001), the differences in Question 4 were highly significant (p ∈ (0.001; 0,01]), and the differences in Question 1 were significant up to the 5% threshold. The magnitude of the deviations in the response behavior is remarkable and has not yet been observed so distinctly in any comparable study.
