Equation (34.30) can also be interpreted as the total cost of hedging for an entropic exchange option. Unlike Scott (1997) who states that option jumps cannot be hedged, this study shows that jumps in exchange options can be hedged. This would imply that equation (34.30) would be usable for standard options and exotic options which are path-dependent. The latter illustration is novel. Given that equation (34.30) is an option and the risk premium should in an option environment, one explores the other (i.e., algebraic) side of the risk premium.
