We also report the simulated results using the subsample of analyst recommendation revisions made by the top-5 brokerage houses. Panel B of Table 55.2 shows that our subsample simulated results are qualitatively similar to those based on the whole sample, confirming that the reputation of brokerage houses does not matter. That is, on average, even sell-side analysts working for more prestigious brokerage houses in the UK have no sufficient skill to make valuable upward or downward revisions for stocks in any period of time.
