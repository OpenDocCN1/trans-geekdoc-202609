A positive estimated coefficient implies that loan loss provisions increased as a share of lagged total loans after the implementation of DFA in 2011, while a negative coefficient would indicate that loan loss provisions, as a share of lagged total loans, decreased after the implementation. A positive coefficient could suggest that bank management considered the pre-DFA loans from an adverse selection perspective: the provisions of the DFA prevent the negative outcomes of loan defaults, thereby encouraging relatively risky lending that would not have otherwise occurred. A negative coefficient could suggest that the bank management considers the DFA from a moral hazard perspective: prior to the DFA operational mechanisms were not in place to reduce access to relatively risky borrowers; now these are present and expected charge off expenses are reduced. Interaction terms between DF_t and the earnings, non-performing loans, capital, and size control variables are present. A statistically significant coefficient indicates a change in the marginal effect of these variables on loan loss provisions, as a share of lagged total loans, during the period SOX and DFA are in effect, while controlling for the implementation of SOX.

SOX_t is a second variable of interest. This variable takes a value of one for years 2002 and later. A positive coefficient indicates loan loss provisions increased after the passage of SOX, while a negative coefficient indicates loan loss provisions decreased after the implementation. A positive coefficient, again, suggests an adverse selection interpretation of SOX implementation; SOX prevents weakness related to governance or culture. Interaction terms between SOX_t and the earnings, non-performing loans, capital, and size control variables mentioned previously are also included. A statistically significant coefficient indicates a change in the marginal effect of these variables on loan loss provisions during the period SOX is in effect, prior to 2011. Effects of SOX and DFA on the provision for loan loss, as a share of total loans, were realized after 2011 (Table 131.3).
