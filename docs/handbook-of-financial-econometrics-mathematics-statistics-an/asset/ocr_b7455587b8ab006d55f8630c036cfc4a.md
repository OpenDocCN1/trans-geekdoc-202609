conclusion, our results are consistent with the notion that foreign banking relationships create value for emerging market firms because foreign bank monitoring creates value above and beyond that which can be provided by domestic, emerging market banks. In addition, this Taiwanese data suggests, that as emerging markets mature and become more sophisticated, the performance enhancement derived from foreign banking relationships will be diminished.
