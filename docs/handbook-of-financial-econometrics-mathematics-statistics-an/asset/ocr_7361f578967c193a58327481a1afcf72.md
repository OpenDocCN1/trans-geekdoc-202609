# 33.3.1 Dataset

**Option prices.** The first component of the dataset consists of option contracts on the S&P500 index (ticker: SPX), traded on the CBOE. All data on option prices, levels of the S&P 500 index, interest rates, and dividend yields are obtained from the OptionMetrics database via Wharton Research Data Services. The option data spans the time period from April 29, 2011 until April 29, 2016, corresponding to five years of observations.² The first stage of the empirical study consists of calibrating the Heston stochastic volatility model to market prices of option contracts on the S&P 500 index. As has been widely documented (see Gatheral, 2006), the stochastic volatility model is capable of recovering the market prices of option contracts with sufficient length of time to maturity. Thus, the Heston model is calibrated only to market prices of options with at least 0.10 years to maturity. Extremely long-dated options with maturities of 0.75 years are also excluded from the sample. The sample contains only options traded on the day at which their closing prices are reported and with bid prices exceeding zero. To reduce the effect of illiquid options, we restrict the sample to options with volume and open interest in the top three quartiles. Furthermore, deep out-of-the-money and in-the-money options (with spot moneyness either in excess of 1.20 or below 0.80) are removed from the sample.³ Finally, we winsorized the data at the 99% level to mitigate the effect of jumps in the underlying S&P 500 index.

The Heston model is re-calibrated daily, as is the standard industry practice. The calibration errors are represented by the minimized IVRMSE objective function (advocated among others by Christoffersen et al., 2009)

IVRMSE(Ψ_t, V_t) = √[ (1/N_t) Σ_{i=1}^{N_t} (P_{K_i,τ_i}(t) - SV_{K_i,τ_i}(Ψ_t, S_t, V_t))² / (∂C^{BS}/∂σ^{BS})² ], (33.6)

where Ψ_t = {λ_t, \bar{v}_t, η_t, ρ_t}, N_t is the number of option contracts in the calibration dataset corresponding to a given trading day, t, P_{K_i,τ_i}(t) is the market price of option i, SV_{K_i,τ_i}(Ψ_t, S_t, V_t) is the theoretical price obtained from the Heston model with parameters Ψ_t.⁴
