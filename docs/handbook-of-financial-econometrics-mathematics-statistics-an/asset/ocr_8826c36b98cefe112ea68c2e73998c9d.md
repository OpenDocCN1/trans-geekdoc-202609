# Table 131.3: Variables of analysis and associated explanation.

| Variable | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Interest income_{i,t-1} | Earnings before loan loss provision and taxes, scaled by lagged total loans. High earnings should result in large provisions, and low earnings should result in low provisions |
| Change in non-performing loan balance_{i,t} | Change in non-performing loans, scaled by total outstanding loans. Current provisions should be related to anticipated deterioration of loan portfolio performance |
| Volume of non-performing loans_{i,t} | Non-performing loan volume, scaled by total outstanding loans. Current provisions should account for current deteriorations in loan portfolio performance |
| Capitalization_{i,t-1} | Ratio of book equity value scaled by total assets. Capital should serve as a substitute for loan provisions |
| Assets_{i,t-1} | Natural logarithm of total assets. Larger institutions should require larger provisions; smaller institutions should require smaller provisions |
| Loan concentration_{i,t} | Concentration of total loans across residential and consumer real estate loans. Increased concentration could result in increased industry expertise or increase vulnerability to changes in loan portfolio performance |
| Commercial real estate as share of real estate loans_{i,t} | Commercial loan volume as a share of total real estate loan volume. Increased commercial loan share could result in increased industry expertise or increased vulnerability to changes in loan portfolio performance |

| SOX dummy variable_t |  |
| Interest income, during SOX period | Changes in governance disclosure requirements may affect the sensitivity of loss provisions to income |
| Volume of non-performing loans, during SOX period | Changes in governance disclosure requirements may affect the sensitivity of loss provisions to deterioration in loan portfolio performance |
| Capitalization, during SOX period | Changes in governance disclosure requirements may affect the sensitivity of loss provisions to capital as a substitute for provisions |
| Assets, during SOX period | Changes in governance disclosure requirements may affect the sensitivity of loss provisions to firm size |
| Commercial loan share of total loans, during SOX period | Changes in governance disclosure requirements may affect the sensitivity of loss provisions to concentration of loans in the commercial real estate market |
