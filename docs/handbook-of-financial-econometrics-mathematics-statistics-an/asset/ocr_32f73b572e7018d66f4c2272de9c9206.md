In this expression, the coefficients corresponding to the lags of the macro factors ρ₁ to ρ₁₂ and the covariance matrix of the bivariate process Ω are 2 × 2 matrices with its error process given by uₜᵒ ∼ N(0,I). These macro factors comprise xₜᵒ, as xₜᵒ = (fₜᵒᵀ, fₜ₋₁ᵒᵀ, …, fₜ₋₍ₚ₋₁₎ᵒᵀ). We also estimate the following system for the latent factors,

$$f_t^u = \rho f_{t-1}^u + u_t^u.$$\tag{61.7}

In this system, the 3 × 3 matrix containing the coefficients corresponding to the latent factors ρ is lower-triangular with the error process modeled as a three-dimensional shock vector uₜᵘ ∼ N(0,I). This assumption ensures identification of the entire system and in Σ the lower-right corner of the matrix is given by the 3 × 3 identity matrix. On page 761 of their paper (Ang and Piazzesi, 2013), the authors note that they impose independence between macroeconomic and latent factors so that the upper-right 24 × 3 corner and lower-left 3 × 24 corner of the Φ and Σ matrices contain only zeros. These matrices are shown below,

$$\Phi_{\text{obs}} = \begin{bmatrix}
\rho_{1(2\times2)} & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & \ddots & 0 & \vdots \\
\vdots & 0 & \ddots & 0 \\
0 & \cdots & 0 & \rho_{12(2\times2)}
\end{bmatrix}_{(24\times24)},$$

$$\Phi = \begin{bmatrix}
\Phi_{\text{obs}(24\times24)} & 0_{24\times3} \\
0_{3\times24} & \Phi_{u(3\times3)}
\end{bmatrix}_{(27\times27)}.$$

We define Ω in a similar fashion,

$$\Omega_{\text{obs}} = \begin{bmatrix}
\Omega_{1(2\times2)} & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
0 & \ddots & 0 & \vdots \\
\vdots & 0 & \ddots & 0 \\
0 & \cdots & 0 & \Omega_{12(2\times2)}
\end{bmatrix}_{(24\times24)},$$

$$\Sigma = \begin{bmatrix}
\Omega_{\text{obs}(24\times24)} & 0_{24\times3} \\
0_{3\times24} & I_{(3\times3)}
\end{bmatrix}_{(27\times27)}.$$
