Further, we examine how much variation in the diversification benefit is explained by each independent variable and what are their individual contributions. We estimate R² as

$\frac{\text{Var}(\hat{\delta}_{j,t})}{\text{Var}(\delta_{j,t})}$

where

$\hat{\delta}_{j,t}= \hat{a}_k + \hat{\beta}\delta_{j,t-1} + \sum_{i=1}^{N} \hat{\gamma}_{j,i,t}$

and $x_{j,i,t}$ is an independent variable $i$ at time $t$ of country $j$. The denominator is

$\text{Var}(\delta_{j,1}) = \frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}\frac{1}{T_j}\sum_{t=1}^{T_j}\left(\hat{\delta}_{j,t} - \bar{\hat{\delta}}\right)^2$

where

$\bar{\hat{\delta}} = \frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}\frac{1}{T_j}\sum_{t=1}^{T_j}\hat{\delta}_{j,t}$

and the numerator is defined as $\text{Var}(\hat{\delta}_{j,t}) = \frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T_j}\left(\hat{\delta}_{j,t} - \bar{\hat{\delta}}\right)^2$, where $\bar{\hat{\delta}} = \frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T_j}\hat{\delta}_{j,t}$
