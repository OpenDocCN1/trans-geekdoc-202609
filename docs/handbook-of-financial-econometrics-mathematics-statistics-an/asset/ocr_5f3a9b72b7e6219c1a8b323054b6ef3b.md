Furthermore, the evidence shows that targets take longer time to complete the deals when targets hire low-reputable financial advisors. When targets hire foreign advisors, targets can complete the deals faster. For bidding firms, the evidence indicates that bidders spend more time completing the deals when bidders hire high-reputable financial advisors relative to low-reputable counterparts. Comparing with domestic and foreign advisors, bidders hired by domestic advisors take longer time to complete the transactions than those with foreign advisors.

With regard to the impact of firm performance, the results show that targets hired by low-reputable financial advisors earn higher announcement returns than those with high-reputable financial advisors. In addition, domestic advisors create higher gains to targets relative to their foreign counterparts. However, the regression analysis cannot find any significant relationship between target announcement returns and different types of financial advisors.

Taking into account the empirical evidence for bidding firms, the results show that low-reputable financial advisors create higher value to bidders around the announcement date. However, the results are reverse showing that bidders hired by low-reputable financial advisors obtain lower post-announcement returns relative to those with high-reputable financial advisors. Interestingly, the evidence consistently reveals that domestic advisors create higher value to bidding firms both around the announcement date and during the post-announcement period. While the regression analysis does not show any significant relationship between the bidder short-term announcement returns and different types of financial advisors, the results do reveal that bidders obtain higher post-announcement returns in relation to domestic advisors. This suggests that domestic advisors can be an important determinant to influence bidder post-announcement returns.

Overall, this study reveals the importance of financial advisors on the influence of deal outcomes in M&As. In particular, the current study finds that domestic advisors can create higher value to both targets and bidding firms. As domestic advisors can better know the market and have superior information to evaluate the transactions, these advisors can create higher value to their clients leading to higher announcement returns. As a result, this study sheds lights on the importance of financial advisors in M&As.
