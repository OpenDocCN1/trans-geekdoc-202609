In the fixed threshold case as shown in Figure 111.1, we can predict the direction of selection bias. As shown in Figure 111.2, we are more likely to observe y₁ when y₂^* is in the lower tail than when in the upper tail since y₁ > y₂^* is more likely to be satisfied when y₂^* is in the lower tail than when in the upper tail.
