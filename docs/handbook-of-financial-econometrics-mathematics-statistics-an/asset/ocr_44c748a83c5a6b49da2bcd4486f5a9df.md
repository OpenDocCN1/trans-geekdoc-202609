**W**'_j **W**_j = **W**'_j **Z**_j, the 2SLS estimator for **δ**_j in equation (116.46) is then

$$\hat{\delta}_j^{2SLS} = (\mathbf{W}'_j\mathbf{W}_j)^{-1}\mathbf{W}'_j\mathbf{y}_j$$

$$= \begin{bmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{Y}}'_j\hat{\mathbf{Y}}_j & \hat{\mathbf{Y}}'_j\mathbf{X}_j \\ \mathbf{X}'_j\hat{\mathbf{Y}}_j & \mathbf{X}'_j\mathbf{X}_j \end{bmatrix}^{-1} \begin{bmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{Y}}'_j\mathbf{y}_j \\ \mathbf{X}'_j\mathbf{y}_j \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{Y}}'_j\mathbf{Y}_j & \mathbf{Y}'_j\mathbf{X}_j \\ \mathbf{X}'_j\mathbf{Y}_j & \mathbf{X}'_j\mathbf{X}_j \end{bmatrix}^{-1} \begin{bmatrix} \hat{\mathbf{Y}}'_j\mathbf{y}_j \\ \mathbf{X}'_j\mathbf{y}_j \end{bmatrix}.$$
(116.47)

Replace **Ŷ**_j by **X**(**X**'**X**)^(-1)**X**'**Y**_j in (116.46), this turns out to be equivalent to

$$\hat{\delta}_j^{2SLS} = (\mathbf{Z}'_j\mathbf{X}(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{Z}_j)^{-1}\mathbf{Z}'_j\mathbf{X}(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{y}_j,$$
(116.48)

where **y**_j = ΔDIV, **Z**_j = [ΔK 1 ΔDIV_− P], and **X** = [1 ΔDIV_− K_−1 P Q] are observed data matrices.

Here, the effect of measurement error on the 2SLS estimates of the structural parameters is investigated. To investigate the asymptotic bias of 2SLS estimator, we rewrite 2SLS estimator in equation (116.48) as follows:

$$\hat{\delta}_j^{2SLS} = (\mathbf{Z}'_j\mathbf{X}(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{Z}_j)^{-1}\mathbf{Z}'_j\mathbf{X}(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{X}'(\mathbf{Z}^*_j\delta_j + \varepsilon_j)$$

$$= \delta_j + (\mathbf{Z}'_j\mathbf{X}(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{Z}_j)^{-1}\mathbf{Z}'_j\mathbf{X}(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X})^{-1}\mathbf{X}'([\mathbf{0}\,\nu_j]\delta_j + \varepsilon_j).$$
(116.49)

Here, we define that **Z**_j = [**Y**_j **X**_j] = [**Y**_j **X***_j + ν_j] = **Z***_j + [**0** ν_j], and ν_j is the submatrix of measurement error corresponding to included exogenous variables on the right-hand side of the jth equation. Thus, we can find that the sampling error of 2SLS estimator depends on three parts: T^{-1}(**X**'**X**), T^{-1}(**X**'**Z**_j), and T^{-1}(**X**'([**0** ν_j]δ_j + ε_j)). We start with the first term T^{-1}(**X**'**X**)

+   (i) $T^{-1}(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X})$:
+   
+   $$\operatorname{plim}T^{-1}(\mathbf{X}'\mathbf{X}) = \operatorname{plim}T^{-1}((\mathbf{X}^* + \nu)'(\mathbf{X}^* + \nu))$$
+   
+   $$= \operatorname{plim}T^{-1}(\mathbf{X}^{*\prime}\mathbf{X}^*) + \theta \equiv \Sigma + \theta,$$
+   (116.50)

where **θ** is the covariance matrix of measurement error **v**. For simplify, we assume that the measurement errors are mutually independent, i.e., matrix **θ** is diagonal. By equation (116.50), we predict that the biases of **Ŷ**_j = **X**(**X**'**X**)^(-1)**X**'**Y**_j in the first stage will carry over to second stage and hence the estimates of structural parameters well be affected by the measurement error.
