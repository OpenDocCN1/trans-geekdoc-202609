(i) From the definition of the regression slope, we have

$$\hat{b}=\frac{\text{Cov}(\hat{\alpha}_j,\hat{\beta}_j)}{\text{Var}(\hat{\beta}_j)}=\frac{\text{Cov}\{[(\bar{R}_j-\bar{R}_T)-\hat{\beta}_j(\bar{R}'_m-\bar{R}_T)],\hat{\beta}_j\}}{\text{Var}(\beta_j)}=\frac{\text{Cov}[(\bar{R}_j-\bar{R}_T),\hat{\beta}_j]}{\text{Var}(\hat{\beta}_j)}-\frac{\text{Var}(\hat{\beta}_j)(\bar{R}_m-\bar{R}_T)}{\text{Var}(\hat{\beta}_j)}=\hat{d}-(\bar{R}'_m-\bar{R}_T).\tag{62.23}$$

(ii) The coefficient of determination for (62.15) can be derived as⁸:

$$R_1=\frac{\text{Cov}(x,y)-k\text{Var}(y)}{\sqrt{(\text{Var}\,y)(\text{Var}\,x)+k^2\text{Var}(y)-2k\text{Cov}(x,y)}} =\left[\frac{\text{Cov}(x,y)}{\sqrt{(\text{Var}\,y)(\text{Var}\,x)}}-k\sqrt{\frac{\text{Var}\,y}{\text{Var}\,x}}\right]\times\left[\frac{\sqrt{\text{Var}\,x}}{\sqrt{\text{Var}\,x+k^2\text{Var}\,y-2k\text{Cov}(x,y)}}\right],\tag{62.24}$$

where $x=\bar{R}_j-\bar{R}_T$, $k=\bar{R}'_m-\bar{R}_T$, $y=\hat{\beta}_j$.

Similarly, the coefficient of determination for (62.21) can be defined as

$$R_2=\frac{\text{Cov}(x,y)}{\sqrt{(\text{Var}\,y)(\text{Var}\,x)}}.\tag{62.25}$$

By comparing $R_1$ and $R_2$, it is found that the coefficient of determination of (62.15) is determined by three different components, i.e., (1) the coefficient of determination (62.21), (2) $k\sqrt{\text{Var}\,y/\text{Var}\,x}$, and (3) $[\text{Var}\,x/\text{Var}\,x+k^2\text{Var}\,y-2k\text{Cov}(x,y)]^{1/2}$. If $\text{Cov}(x,y)$ approaches zero, then $R_2^2$ will approach $k^2\text{Var}\,y\text{Var}\,x+k^2\text{Var}\,y^9$. Therefore, the magnitude of $R_1^2$ is generally different from that of $R_2^2$.
