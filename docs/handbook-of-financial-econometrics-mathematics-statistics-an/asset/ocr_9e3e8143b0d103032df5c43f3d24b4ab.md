# 93.3 Portfolio’s Efficiency Test in Bayesian Framework

Consider the ratio ρ = SR/SR*, where SR is the ex ante Sharpe ratio of the test portfolio p, and SR* is the ex ante maximum Sharpe ratio attainable by the N risky assets and the test portfolio p. Since the ratio ρ = 1 (ρ < 1) is equivalent to the non-centrality parameter λ = 0 (λ ≠ 0), then the null hypothesis H0: λ = 0 versus the alternative hypothesis H1: λ ≠ 0 is equivalent to H0: ρ = 1 versus H1: ρ < 1. In the following, Bayesian tests for the null hypothesis H0: ρ = 1 versus the composite alternative H1: ρ < 1 are reviewed. After the review, a Bayesian test based on the Bayes factor for H0: ρ = 1 versus H1: ρ < 1 is developed.

# 93.3.1 Bayesian tests without measurement error associated with ex post Sharpe ratio

The Bayesian hypothesis testing, developed by Jeffreys (1961), is based on the posterior-odds ratio that compares the probabilities that the null hypothesis is valid to that of the alternative hypothesis. The posterior-odds ratio reduces to the Bayes factor when an even prior-odds is assumed (Kass and Raftery, 1995). The details of the posterior-odds ratio and the Bayes factor are given in Appendix 93A.

In Shanken (1987), by assuming the ex post Sharpe ratio ŜR = SR without measurement error, the test statistics Vu of (93.2) is a sufficient statistics for the non-centrality parameter λ of (93.3), which can be rewritten as

λ = T × SR²/(1 + S R̂²) × (1 - ρ²)/ρ².

Thus, Vu is also a sufficient statistics for the ratio ρ. A prior distribution of the non-centrality parameter λ is given by

π(•) ∝ Tc × χ²_N(•),  (93.4)

where χ²_N(•) is a chi-squares distribution with N degrees of freedom, N is the number of risky assets, T is the time series sample size, and the constant c is

c = S R̂²/(1 + S R̂²) × [1 - [median(ρ)]²]/[median(χ²_N) × [median(ρ)]²].  (93.5)
