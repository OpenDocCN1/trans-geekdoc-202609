# 42.2.2 Investor and consumer sentiment proxies

We employ two sentiment proxies in the empirical tests. The investor sentiment proxy is the percentage of net long positions held by speculators in TAIEX futures (LongShortFutures). The data on long and short positions in TAIEX futures between 2005 and 2010 are provided by the Taiwan Economic Journal (TEJ). In TAIEX futures market, institutional traders use futures as a hedge and speculators are more likely to be individual investors.8 The net long position of speculators is derived from the holding positions of the top 10 institutional traders with large transactions. Panel A of Table 42.2 presents the summary statistics for open interest in TAIEX futures. Regarding total open interest, the average percentages of the long and short positions for the top 10 institutional traders are 32.18% and 36.08%, respectively, suggesting that the average percentage of net long positions for speculators is 3.9%. A lower percentage of net long positions for speculators implies more bearish investor sentiment.
