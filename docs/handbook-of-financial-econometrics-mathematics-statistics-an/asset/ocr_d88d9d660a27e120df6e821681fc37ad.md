In Russia, the rates of using remote access to the bank account are also the highest in two groups: from 18 to 24 years (33%) and from 25 to 34 years (37%) (NAFI, 2016). Moreover, many experts declared that globally, people from the so-called “generation Y” (25-34 years) preferred the mobile channel for their banking requirements (ARB, 2014).

We also have a shift towards citizens from megalopolises (with a population larger than 1 million people) rather than from small towns with population under 100,000 citizens. However, in Russia, smartphone and tablet penetration as well as Internet opportunities are not equally distributed geographically. Moreover, bank customers representing generation Y tended to leave rural areas and migrate to large cities (ARB, 2014).

We received a total of 206 questionnaires but only 150 of them were usable after excluding incomplete questionnaires and data cleaning according to the age threshold. Therefore, our sample tends to be quite small. However, our estimations were done using statistical methods that allowed us to work with a small sample. Actually, our sample satisfied 10-times rule for a minimum sample size (Hair et al., 2014).
