# 64.5.3 Application

Currently there is only one asset pricing study applying the MKF in estimating the state-space model with regime switching. Chang, Chen, Wang and Wu (2018) uses the MKF to detect periodically collapsing rational bubbles in stock prices. They express the stock-dividend-bubble system in a log-linearized Gaussian state-space model with Markov switching. A two-regime specification includes surviving and collapsing regimes, in which a bubble grows at a speed greater or smaller than the asset’s required rate of return, while at the same time the rational bubble condition is imposed. They apply the MKF strategy to estimate the model for both simulated observations and real US stock market indices. The methodology can fit the bubble process better and identify most of bubble collapsing episodes in history. They also discuss the feasibility and superiority of a three-regime specification.

# 64.6 Conclusion

In this chapter, we provide a brief summary of applications of filtering methods in estimating asset pricing models. We review the filtering methods for four types of state-space models, including linear Gaussian state-space model, nonlinear Gaussian state-space model, nonlinear non-Gaussian state-space model, and state-space model with regime switching. These models cover most of the asset pricing models ranging from discrete to continuous models and from structural to non-structural models. These methods can be helpful for finance researchers to estimate a rich set of asset pricing models.
