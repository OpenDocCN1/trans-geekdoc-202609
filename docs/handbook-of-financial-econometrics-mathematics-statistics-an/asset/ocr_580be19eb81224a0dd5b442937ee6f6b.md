# 47.5.1 Sample means, equilibrium means, and betas

Figure 47.1 shows the time-series plots of the sample means and seven sets of equilibrium means estimated from an expanding window in the size and book-to-market dataset. Each panel includes a plot of the risk-free rate of interest, the mean of the market portfolio, and the means of the 25 portfolios.

A simple visual inspection shows that the alternative ways of specifying the SML means are in fact different. The differences in the time-series behavior of the equilibrium means are quite marked. For example, the 25 portfolios’ equilibrium means increase in the first 460 months when the market risk premium is constant and decrease over the same period when the market portfolio’s expected excess return to variance ratio is constant.
