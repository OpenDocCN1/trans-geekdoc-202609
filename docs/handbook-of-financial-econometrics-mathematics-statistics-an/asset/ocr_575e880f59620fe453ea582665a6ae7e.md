# Figure 91.1: Standardized returns, standard deviation, and return/risk for hedge fund types.

Notes: This figure including the standardized median monthly return, standardized median monthly standard deviation, and the standardized median monthly return per unit of standard deviation for BarclayHedge indices are provided. The standardization procedure takes the individual median returns, median standard deviations and the median return per unit of risk based upon the median returns and standard deviation for all of the indices listed on the chart and standardizes each of them as compared to the rest of the indices contained in the sample. The sample was run from January 1997 to December 2013.
