At the same time, as the classification of CRSP stocks by industry sectors might capture some important correlations, this study adopts the 10 industrial portfolios of CRSP firms constructed by Fama and French as the set of risky assets that determines the minimum-variance frontier (http://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/index.html).

The 10 industrial categories including consumer non-durables, consumer durables, manufacturing, energy, business equipment, telecommunication, shops, health care, utilities, and others. The details of the 10 industrial categories are listed in Table 93.1.

Monthly returns of the aforementioned portfolios are collected from two time periods, respectively. The first time period starts from January 1941 to December 1973 and the second period starts from January 1980 to December 2012. Both time periods have the time span of L = 396 months. In Table 93.2, the summary statistics of the 10 industrial portfolios’ excess returns during the two time periods are listed, respectively. Table 93.3 summarizes the excess returns of the test portfolio, i.e., the CRSP value-weighted portfolio, during the two time periods.
