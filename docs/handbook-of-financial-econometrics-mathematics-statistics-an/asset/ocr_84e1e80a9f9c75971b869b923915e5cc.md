The entire firm sample was categorized into five portfolios according to their standardized unexpected earnings (SUE) by employing the FOS (1984) technique. Portfolio 1 represents firm with the lowest SUE, while Portfolio 5 represents firm with the highest SUE. One hundred daily returns for each firm before the announcement of earnings were used to estimate systematic risk by regressing them against the equally weighted market indices. The abnormal returns of each firms was then estimated for the 31 days holding period. The cumulative abnormal returns (CARs) were then calculated for each portfolio. We follow Brown and Warner (1985) to calculate the test statistics of the cumulative mean abnormal returns.
