Twitter as dummy variables were equal to 1 if the firm’s home page provided a link to an official Facebook page, YouTube channel and Twitter account and were equal to 0 otherwise. We added Others as a dummy variable, which was equal to 1 if the firm’s home page showed a different social media icon and equal to 0 otherwise.

# 66.3.2.3 Bank relationships

Bank relationships in this study include two measures: BankRelationship, which was measured by the logarithm of 1 plus the total number of banks that provided loans to the firm during the fiscal year; and BankDebt_TA, measured by the total amount of bank loans divided by the total assets. These data on bank relationships were obtained from the TEJ bank loan database and calculated accordingly.

# 66.3.2.4 Control variables

The controls are as follows. Firm age (Age) is measured by the number of years since the establishment of the firm. Firm size (LnSize) is measured by the logarithm of the firm’s book value of total assets. The ratio of financial structures that are considered intangible assets (Intangible) is measured by total goodwill and intangible assets divided by total assets. Financial leverage (Leverage) is measured by the book value of the firm’s total liabilities divided by total assets. Solvency is determined by the ratio of the current assets (CurrentRatio), which is measured by the book value of the firm’s current assets divided by current liabilities. Working capital ratio (NWC_Assets) is measured by the net working capital divided by total assets. Cash flow ratio (OCF_CL) is measured by the operating cash flow divided by current liabilities. The firm’s growth is measured by sales growth rate (SaleGrowth) from (Sales_t — Sales_t-1)/Sales_t-1.

Appendix B provides detailed definition about the construction of the variables. All continuous variables were winsorized at the 1% and 99% levels to limit the influence of outliers.
