# 91.2 Hedge Fund Return Issues

# 91.2.1 Opacity

Opacity, when looking at hedge fund returns, occurs when the managers of the fund have some control over when their prices are displayed to the public and how they are displayed. This occurs in the hedge fund industry because hedge fund managers are able to invest in assets that may be privately held or are priced intermittently; therefore, the pricing at the fund level is not efficient.
