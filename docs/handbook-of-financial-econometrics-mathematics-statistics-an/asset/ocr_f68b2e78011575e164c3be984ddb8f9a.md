As most of the empirical Sharpe ratios of CRSP indexes are between 0 and 0.4 (Gibbons, Ross, and Shanken, 1989), a uniform distribution within the interval (0, 0.4) is chosen for the prior distribution π(•) of the ex ante Sharpe ratio SR. Powers of rejecting the null H0: ρ = 1 for the conditional F-test, the proposed Bayes test, and Shanken’s Bayesian tests when the ex ante Sharpe ratio SR = 0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.4, respectively, are calculated. Three different levels of SR̂ are chosen: the low, medium and high Sharpe ratios SR̂ are set to SR-2σSR, SR, and SR+2σSR, respectively, where σSR = V_GMM^{1/2}, where V_GMM is the asymptotic variance in (93.9). When the sampling window length T = 60, the standard error σSR is set to 0.0840. In panel B, the median of the ratio ρ = SR/SR* is assumed to be 0.50 and 0.75, respectively. The null H0: ρ =1 is rejected when the Bayes factor >1.0.
