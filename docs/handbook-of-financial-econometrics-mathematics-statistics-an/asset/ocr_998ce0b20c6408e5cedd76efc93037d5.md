Stocks with relatively large dividend yields should provide high expected returns (and so sell for low prices) to compensate the investor for the tax. Litzenberger and Ramaswamy (1982) present some new empirical results to show that a positive and nonlinear relationship between common stock returns and expected dividend yield.
