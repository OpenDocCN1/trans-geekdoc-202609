Recently, Shaikh and Karjaluoto (2015) provided a meta-analysis of 55 papers related to mobile banking adoption from 2005 to 2014. According to their results, both perceived usefulness and perceived efforts are also the most popular factors that can be found in the most of the analyzed empirical studies. Baptista and Oliveira (2016) covered 57 papers from 2003 to 2016 and also found these factors as the most popular independent variables when predicting the intention to use mobile banking. Similar constructs were used in different cases like studying mobile banking adoption by millennial generation (Tan and Lau, 2016) or for purposes of a cross-cultural study (Mortimer et al., 2015) Based on these results, we also use these factors as a baseline framework.

The factor of perceived usefulness reflects the benefits gained from mobile banking usage, such as saving time and a decrease in transaction costs. The significance of this factor is often supported by the literature (Luarn and Lin, 2005; Gu et al., 2009). Moreover, the importance of perceived usefulness is proved not only by widespread SEM-utilizing explorations, but also by Verissimo (2016) who used a different method: fuzzy set qualitative comparative analysis (fsQCA). At least, Mortimer et al. (2015) showed that perceived usefulness was still to be a key factor predicting the intention to use mobile banking even in a cross-cultural study. # H1: Perceived Usefulness positively affects Intention to Use mobile banking.

Perceived efforts have a significant influence on perceived usefulness, perceived risks and the intention to use mobile banking. When a customer tries new services, it takes some time and knowledge to become a skillful user. The screen size of an ordinary mobile device might cause extra difficulties for new users and require extra effort (Riquelme and Rios, 2010). If the application is not adapted for small screens and keypads, it might be uncomfortable to navigate. Apps that are too complicated may also scare new customers. However, if it is easy for a consumer to operate via mobile banking application, the intention to use mobile banking will increase.
