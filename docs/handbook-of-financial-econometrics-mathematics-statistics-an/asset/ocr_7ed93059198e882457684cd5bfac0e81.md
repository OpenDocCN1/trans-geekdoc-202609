Figure 18.7 shows the prediction results with different groups of features. Not surprisingly, the stock-specific features are very useful for stock prediction. Using stock-specific features alone can achieve 0.57 in ACC and 0.54 in AUC. Both the sentiment features and the stock relatedness features are helpful, and the sentiment features play more important roles than the stock relatedness features. When we put all features together, the prediction result in terms of ACC remain the same as that with both sentiment and stock specific feature, but the results in terms of AUC can be further improved. The reason is that the imbalance in the dataset is not considered in ACC. The improvement in AUC indicates that the addition of stock relatedness feature can improve the discriminative power of our model.
