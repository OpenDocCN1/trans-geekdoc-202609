# 92.2.2.1 Model

The concept of co-integration is mainly applied to non-stationary time series variables that produce stationary residuals in models. We apply the quantile co-integrating regression developed by Xiao (2009) to examine the contemporaneous spot and futures gold prices. To avoid endogeneity, we adopted the lead-lag terms in the traditional co-integration model. The equation is specified as follows:

LnGold_{spot_t} = α + β LnGold_{future_t} + Σ_{i=-k}^{K} ω_i Δ LnGold_{future,t-i} + ε_t; (92.1)

where LnGold_{spot} is the spot gold price taken in natural logarithm, LnGold_{future} is futures gold price taken in natural logarithm, and ε denotes a residual. The number K is the length of the lead–lag terms. Here, we set K to be three.¹ In this study, we replace LnGold_{future} with Ln1m, Ln2m, Ln3m, and Ln6m to represent four different maturities of gold futures contracts of 1-, 2-, 3-, and 6-month and the following equations go along with this setting.
