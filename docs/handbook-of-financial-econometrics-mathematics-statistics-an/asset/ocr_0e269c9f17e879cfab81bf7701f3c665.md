Psychological barrier such as computer anxiety is negatively linked with IT adoption, IT use, end-user satisfaction, productivity and general individual performance in the workplace with computers (Elie-Dit-Cosaque et al., 2011). Concerning external factors, formal training enhances job performance of employees by improving their skill set required to handle customer demands and reduce their turnover intentions (Sengupta et al., 2015).

Internal and external factors influence the technology adoption. Effective acceptance of technologies in turn has a positive effect on load and productivity of labor force-accelerated workflow and also increased the efficiency of production processes and output amounts (Kurt and Kurt, 2015). Kurt and Kurt (2015) researched the effects of innovation on labor productivity for the five countries defined as BRICS (Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa) which have drawn attention in recent years due to their economic similarities by using panel data and dynamic panel data methods. The authors confirmed a positive relationship between innovation and labor productivity in these countries. However, the introduction of technology in Russia is not as active as in other countries. According to EY, slightly the average percentage of FinTech services penetration in Russia — 43% is slightly less than the same indicator on emerging markets (Brazil, China, India, Mexico and South Africa) — 46% (EY Russia, 2017).

The aim of our study is to examine the employees’ attitudes and acceptance of disruptive technologies in the Russian financial institutions. This analysis might give a deeper insight into the problem of the impact of advanced technologies on labor productivity and company performance. First of all, this study is at the junction of science and business. The interaction of business and science is important, because the growth of innovation activity in the industry is possible when industrial production is based on the modern scientific research, and various innovations find application in business without losing relevance (Schueffel and Vadana, 2015). Meanwhile, we look at organizational structure, prior training regarding specific technologies and user satisfaction (Lee et al., 2013). The detailed study of the impact of the advanced technologies on employee performance represents also the novelty from referring to the list of emerging technologies as the most influential and relevant to invest (PwC, 2017).
