Large firms can hire high-reputable financial advisors and foreign advisors to advise the deals as these advisors may have more experience and ability to advise their clients. On the contrary, M&A deals may be less complex to small firms. These firms can pay lower advisory fees to hire low-reputable financial advisors and domestic advisors in order to complete the deals. While high-reputable financial advisors and foreign advisors tend to advise large deals, this may also suggest that these advisors may possibly focus on acquiring higher advisory fees.
