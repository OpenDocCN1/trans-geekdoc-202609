$$\sum_{j=1}^n (w_j^+ + k w_j^- + p_1 l_j^+ + p_2 l_j^- + p_3 s_j^+ + p_4 s_j^-) = 1, \quad (104.3)$$ $$w_j^+ = w_{j,0}^+ + l_j^+ - l_j^-, \quad j = 1,2,\dots,n, \quad (104.4)$$ $$w_j^- = w_{j,0}^- + s_j^+ - s_j^-, \quad j = 1,2,\dots,n, \quad (104.5)$$ $$0.01 u_j \le w_j^+ \le u_j, \quad j = 1,2,\dots,n, \quad (104.6)$$ $$0.01 v_j \le w_j^- \le v_j, \quad j = 1,2,\dots,n, \quad (104.7)$$ $$u_j + v_j \le 1, \quad j = 1,2,\dots,n, \quad (104.8)$$ $$u_j, v_j \in \{0,1\}, \quad j = 1,2,\dots,n, \quad (104.9)$$

where $p_c$’s are the transaction costs of buying ($p_1$), selling ($p_2$), short sales ($p_3$), and repurchasing of short-sales ($p_4$) asset $j$, respectively; $k$ is the initial margin requirement for short selling; $w_j^+$ is the total proportion of security $j$ invested at portfolio rebalancing; $w_j^-$ is the total weight of security $j$ sold by investors at portfolio rebalancing; $w_{j,0}^+$ is the long position weight of security $j$ prior to portfolio rebalancing; and $w_{j,0}^-$ is the short position weight of security $j$ prior to portfolio rebalancing. With each rebalancing, $l_j^+$ is the buying weight of security $j$; $l_j^-$ is the short-selling weight of security $j$; $s_j^+$ is the short-selling weight of security $j$; and $s_j^-$ is the repurchasing weight of security $j$. The binary variables $u_j$ and $v_j$ are used to indicate the long- and short-selling position and to model the upper bounds of the weight. We set 1% is the minimal threshold to invest in one asset. This value can be adjusted accordingly.

Regarding the equations in the model, equation (104.2) specifies the budget allocated to buying and short selling; (104.3) shows the long position after rebalancing; (104.4) represents the short-selling position after rebalancing; and (104.5) and (104.6) define the upper and lower bounds of the total weights of each security of long position and that of short-selling position, respectively. The definition of the binary variables in equations (104.8) and (104.9) ensures that long and short positions do not happen simultaneously.
