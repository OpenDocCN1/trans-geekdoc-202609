Pastor and Veronesi (2005) leverage the work of Helwege et al. It is challenging for anyone to argue that an event similar to the financial collapse of 2007-2008 would not have a significant impact on IPO volume and performance; therefore, we question whether an event like this, which has such a significant effect on the market, should be considered a component of the hot or cold market or should it be dealt with separately.
