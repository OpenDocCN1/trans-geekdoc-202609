# *The Market Model*

Use “Regression” function in “Data Analysis” to run the market model with respect to JNJ’s returns, select the monthly returns of JNJ in “Y range” and the monthly returns of S&P 500 index in “X range,” and press “OK.” We can then get the regression results of market model with respect to JNJ in new sheet. The coefficients of intercept term and S&P 500 index are “Alpha” and “Beta” of market model with respect to JNJ’s return. The MS of residual is the “Variance of residual” of the market model. Repeat the “Regression” function, and then we can get the results of market model with respect to the monthly return of IBM and BA.
