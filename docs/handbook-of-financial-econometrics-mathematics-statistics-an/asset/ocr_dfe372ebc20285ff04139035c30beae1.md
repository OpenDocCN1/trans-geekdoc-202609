# **Example 41.4.5 (Optimal capital structure, continued).**

We use the notation and setting of Example 41.3.9, except that the process $X$ is of the form (41.45) (also assume that $\sigma<0$) and $Z$ has a mixed exponential jump distribution (41.46). For every $s<0$, let $\rho_i^s$, $1\le i\le m+1$, be the negative real solutions to $\psi(z)-1=0$. As before, we write $\rho_i$ for $\rho_i^s$ if this causes no confusion. By taking $g=1$ in (41.51), we get $R_j=0$ for $1\le j\le m$ and $R_{m+1}=1$. By Theorem 41.4.3, we get

$$G_s(x)=\prod_{k=1}^{m+1}\rho_k\left[\sum_{h=1}^{m+1}\frac{1}{\rho_h\prod_{l=1,l\ne h}^{m+1}(-\rho_h+\rho_l)}\left(\prod_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\rho_h+\eta_i}{\eta_i}\right)e^{\rho_hx}\right].\tag{41.55}$$

Hence,

$$G_s'(0)=\prod_{k=1}^{m+1}\rho_k\left[\sum_{h=1}^{m+1}\frac{1}{\prod_{l=1,l\ne h}^{m+1}(-\rho_h+\rho_l)}\left(\prod_{i=1}^{m}\frac{\rho_h+\eta_i}{\eta_i}\right)\right].\tag{41.56}$$

Similarly, by taking $g(y)=e^y$ for $y<0$ in (41.51), we get $R_j=\frac{1}{1+\eta_j}$ for $1\le j\le m+1$. By Theorem 41.4.3 again, we get

$$H_s(x)=\sum_{h=1}^{m+1}\frac{1}{\rho_h\prod_{l=1,l\ne h}^{m+1}(-\rho_h+\rho_l)}\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{m+1}\frac{1}{1+\eta_j}\left[\prod_{k=1}^{m+1}(\eta_j+\rho_k)\prod_{i=1,i\ne j}^{m+1}\frac{-\rho_h-\eta_j}{\eta_j-\eta_i}\right]\right\}e^{\rho_hx}.\tag{41.57}$$

Hence,

$$H_s(0)=\sum_{h=1}^{m+1}\frac{1}{\rho_h\prod_{l=1,l\ne h}^{m+1}(-\rho_h+\rho_l)}\left\{\sum_{j=1}^{m+1}\frac{1}{1+\eta_j}\left[\prod_{k=1}^{m+1}(\eta_j+\rho_k)\prod_{i=1,i\ne j}^{m+1}\frac{-\rho_h-\eta_j}{\eta_j-\eta_i}\right]\right\}.\tag{41.58}$$

Plugging formulas (41.56) and (41.58) into (41.23), we get the explicit solutions for the optimal default-triggering level $L^*$ (under the smooth-pasting assumption). Similarly, plugging (41.55) and (41.57) into (41.32), (41.33), and (41.31) respectively, we get closed formulas for the value of debt, the value of the firm, and hence the value of equity.
