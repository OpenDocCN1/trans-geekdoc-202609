## 56.3 Methodology

### 56.3.1 Sampling and data collection

The members of CFA Association (Russia) and graduates of HSE Banking Institute were invited to participate in the study. CFA Association (Russia) is a non-profit organization that represents the interests of CFA Charterholders and CFA candidates based in Russia and the CIS. Founded in 2004, CFA Association (Russia) is a member society of CFA Institute. In 2015, CFA Association (Russia) was recognized as the ‘Most Innovative CFA Society’ out of 146 CFA societies in 71 countries (CFA Association Russia, 2018).

HSE Banking Institute is a part of the National Research University Higher School of Economics. Founded in 1998, the Banking Institute developed MBA Investment Management and MA Financial Analyst programs which were recognized by CFA Institute Program as Partners in 2009 and 2012, respectively. Besides, more than 100 other programs on different topics were created.

The process of data collection was carried out for two groups separately. In the first round, the link to our survey was published on the official page of HSE Banking Institute in Facebook on February 21, 2018. There are 279 alumni in this group. In the second round, we have sent letters to 361 alumni of HSE Banking Institute by e-mails on March 12, 2018. Among them, 42 e-mail addresses were invalid. In the third round, we have sent letters to 304 students of HSE Banking Institute by e-mails on March 20, 2018. At the same time, we have sent letters to 600 members of CFA Association of Russia by e-mails on February 28, 2018 as the first round. On the 28th of March, we have sent letters to 449 level III CFA exam candidates by e-mails as the second round.

In total, about 2000 professionals were invited to fill the questionnaire. The final sample includes 148 valid responses (29% of CFA Association (Russia) members, 71% of HSE Banking Institute alumni and students). Response rate concerning CFA Association of Russia was 4.1%, while response rate in relation to HSE Banking Institute was 11.1%. The demographic and organizational profile of respondents is presented in Table 56.1. Table 56.2 illustrates the characteristics of organizations and industries. The male-to-female ratio was 72% to 28%. A majority of respondents (84%) were under the age of 39, age from 40 to 49 years was among 15% of respondents. Regarding education, the third part of respondents (33%) had master’s degree, 26% of respondents were specialists, and the same amount had MBA, 10% had bachelor's degree, 5% had PhD.

Most of the respondents were employed in financial services (58%), predominantly in investment departments (26%) and financial departments (21%). From 58% of the employed in financial services, there were 53% of respondents who worked in commercial bank. The number of employees in the company which most of the respondents (29%) worked in was more than 10,000, the fifth part of respondents (20%) worked in the company with 1001-5000 employees. The third part of respondents (34%) indicated that the age of company was more than 30 years, 24% — 11-20 years, 22% — 21-30 years. As for job title, a majority of respondents (48%) were specialists, the third part (29%) had the positions of head of department, and 14% of respondents were top managers. Regarding total job experience, 26% stated that they had more than 15 years, 24% — 11-15 years, 22% — 6-10 years, 20% — 2-5 years, 7% — less than two years. Job experience in the current company predominantly was less than three years (49%) and 3-7 years (30%).

### 56.3.2 Results

#### 56.3.2.1 Global trends and disruptive technologies

In the survey, we asked our respondents about the impact of global trends on the financial industry. The results are presented in Figure 56.2. The rise of digital technologies has the greatest impact on the financial industry (86%). Besides, changing customer's ethics and values (62%), volume and speed of newly available customer data (60%) are influential too. Gamification has the least impact on the financial industry (9%).
