and where

$c_2 = \frac{e^{-T(\beta-1/2)^2\sigma^2/2}}{\sqrt{\pi}2^{\frac{T-3}{4}}\left(\frac{\sigma\sqrt{T}}{T-1}\right)^{\frac{T-1}{2}}\Gamma\left(\frac{T-1}{2}\right)\frac{(T-1)}{(T-3)} = c_1\frac{(T-1)}{(T-3)},$

$v = \left[(\hat{\beta}-1/2)\left(\frac{(T-1)}{(T-3)}\right)\right]^{\frac{T+1}{2}}.$

Although the pdf of $\hat{\beta}$, given by (65.14), is formidable with three parameters, and not a member of the standard sampling distribution families, numerical methods can be used for either $f(\hat{\beta})$ or the cumulative distribution function of $\hat{\beta}$, say $F(x)=\int_{-\infty}^{x} f(\hat{\beta})d\hat{\beta}$. For example, Figure 65.1 graphs the density of $\hat{\beta}$ for $T=30$, $\sigma=1.5$ with $\beta=1$ and $\beta=1.5$, respectively. In the case of $\beta=1$, $E(\hat{\beta})=1.0$ and $Var(\hat{\beta})=0.056$. Note that both curves are slightly right skewed and they look approximately like a bell-shaped curve.
