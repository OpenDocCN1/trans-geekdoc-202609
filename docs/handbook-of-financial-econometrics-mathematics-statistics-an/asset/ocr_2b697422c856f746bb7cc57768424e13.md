This study designs question items for each construct (i.e., cognitive/emotional biases and the disposition effect) based on behavioral finance literature and collects quantitative data through a questionnaire survey. The questionnaire uses a seven-point Likert-type scale, which ranges from one (strongly disagree) to seven (strongly agree). In addition, this study measures investment performance by classifying stock returns into seven buckets, which is consistent with a seven-point Likert-type scale.
