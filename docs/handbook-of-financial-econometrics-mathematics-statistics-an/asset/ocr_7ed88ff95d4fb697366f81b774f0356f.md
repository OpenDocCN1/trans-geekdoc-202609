By contrast, the VEC-GARCH(1,1) model is written as

```
vech(H_t) = ω + A^{(1)}vech(ε_{t-1}ε'_{t-1}) + B^{(1)}vech(H_{t-1}),
(123.29)
```

where vech(H_t) = (h_{11,t}, h_{22,t}, h_{22,t})', vech(ε_{t-1}ε'_{t-1}) = (ε_{1,t-1}^2, ε_{1,t-1}ε_{2,t-1}, ε_{2,t-1}^2)', ω = (ω_j), A^{(1)} = (α_{ij}) and B^{(1)} = (β_{ij}) (i,j = 1,2,3). Comparing equation (123.28) with equation (123.29), we obtain ω = vech(Ω),

```
A^{(1)} = \begin{pmatrix} α11 & α12 & α13 \\ α21 & α22 & α23 \\ α31 & α32 & α33 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} a11^2 & 2a11a12 & a12^2 \\ a11a21 & a11a22 + a12a21 & a12a22 \\ a21^2 & 2a21a22 & a22^2 \end{pmatrix},
```

```
B^{(1)} = \begin{pmatrix} β11 & β12 & β13 \\ β21 & β22 & β23 \\ β31 & β32 & β33 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} b11^2 & 2b11b12 & b12^2 \\ b11b21 & b11b22 + b12b21 & b12b22 \\ b21^2 & 2b21b22 & b22^2 \end{pmatrix}.
```

We can see from (123.30) and (123.31) that the VEC-GARCH(1,1) model include the BEKK-GARCH(1,1) as a special case, and the parameters of the BEKK-GARCH(1,1) implicitly impose very special restrictions on those of the VEC-GARCH(1,1).

# 123.3.1.3 CCC-GARCH(p, q) model

Introduced by Bollerslev (1990) and extended by Jeanth eau (1998), in the CCC-GARCH(p,q) model, the conditional variance-covariance matrix is decomposed as

```
H_t = D_t R D_t, D_t = diag(h_{11,t}^{1/2}, ..., h_{nn,t}^{1/2}),
(123.32)
```

where D_t is the diagonal matrix of the square roots of the conditional variances, and correlation matrix (R = (ρ_kl)) is assumed to be constant over time, i.e.,

```
ρ_{kl,t} = h_{kl,t}^{1/2} / (h_{kk,t}^{1/2} h_{ll,t}^{1/2}) = ρ_kl.
(123.33)
```

The diagonal elements of H_t are formulated as

```
\bar h_t = ω + \sum_{i=1}^q A_i \bar ε_{t-i} + \sum_{j=1}^p B_j \bar h_{t-j},
(123.34)
```
