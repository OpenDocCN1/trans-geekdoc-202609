The price of risk that arises from the traditional covariance of an asset’s return with market return is $\gamma + (\gamma - 1)\theta_{h1} + (1 - \gamma)\sum_{l=1}^{L}\theta_{e1}^{l}$, where $\theta_{h1}$ is the 1st element of $\theta_h$ and $\theta_{e1}^{l}$ is the 1st element of $\theta_e$. Therefore, this model provides a distinct link between the coefficient of relative risk aversion, $\gamma$, and the various prices of risks.
