Turning to the analysis for bidding firms, the results in model specifications (5) and (8) show that bidders obtain lower announcement returns when bidders hire high-reputable financial advisors and foreign advisors, respectively. In contrast, the results in model specifications (6) and (7) reveal that bidders obtain higher announcement returns when bidders hire low-reputable financial advisors and domestic advisors, respectively. However, the results are not statistically significant. When looking at post-announcement (0,+270) period, I find an interesting finding. The results in model specification (11) reveal that there is a positive relationship between bidder post-announcement returns and domestic advisors. The coefficient is 0.132, indicating that bidders obtain higher post-announcement returns when bidders hire domestic advisors. The results are consistent with previous findings in this study. While domestic advisors can better know the market and have information advantages to evaluate potential synergies, domestic advisors can create higher gains during the long-run post-announcement period.

With regard to control variables, bidders obtain lower post-announcement returns when bidders pay cash, acquire private targets and engage in friendly deals. On the contrary, bidders obtain higher post-announcement returns when bidders are large firms.

# 76.6 Conclusion

This study examines the role of financial advisors on the influence of deal outcomes in M&As in the Australian market. In particular, the current study examines whether the performance of domestic advisors can differ from that of foreign advisors. Using 333 targets and 949 bidders during the period 1995-2011, the results show that firm size is an important determinant to influence the choice of financial advisors for targets and bidders. The evidence reveals that large firms are more likely to hire financial advisors. In addition, the evidence also indicates that bidders are less likely to hire financial advisors when bidders pay cash, acquire private targets and involve in friendly deals.
