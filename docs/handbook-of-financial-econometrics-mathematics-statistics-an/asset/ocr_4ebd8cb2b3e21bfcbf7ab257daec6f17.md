From these results, our MCLP model shows that our approach is a lot more efficient and effective in bankruptcy prediction using only financial data even in a financial crisis. In particular, our model had a 13.8% Type-I error rate. Type-I errors are misclassifications of bankrupt firms as non-bankrupt, and Type-II errors are misclassifications of non-bankrupt firms as bankrupt. In the case of bankruptcy prediction, the costs of Type-I errors are more significant than the costs of Type II-errors. For example, investors and creditors suffer actual economic damage from the bankrupt firms because of Type-I errors, but there will be no actual damage from the Type-II errors (Takahashi et al., 1984).
