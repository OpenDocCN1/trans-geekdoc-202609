The final discriminant model has an F-ratio of 12.559, which, with the appropriate degrees of freedom (6 and 45), is significant beyond the 0.005 level. Once it is established that the model does discriminate between distressed and solvent property-liability insurance firms, the individual contribution of each of the variables can be examined through the use of the t-test. The individual contribution is dominated by X₆, which is significant at the 0.0005 level; X₁, which is significant at the 0.005 level; and X₂ and X₄, which are significant at the 0.025 level. The discriminant model has been proved to be quite applicable to this sort of analysis.
