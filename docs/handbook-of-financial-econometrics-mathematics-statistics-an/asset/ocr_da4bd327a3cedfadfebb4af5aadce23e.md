final result:

$E[\hat{\gamma}_1] = E\left[\frac{\sum_{i=1}^N (\hat{\beta}_i - \bar{\hat{\beta}})(\bar{r}_i - \bar{r})}{\sum_{i=1}^N (\hat{\beta}_i - \hat{\bar{\beta}})^2}\right] \approx \gamma_1\left(1 - \frac{\sigma_u^2}{\sigma_\beta^2} + \frac{2}{(N - 1)}\frac{W_\Sigma}{\sigma_\beta^2}\right), \quad (98.8)$

with the cross-sectional variation of the measurement errors:

$\sigma_u^2 := \frac{1}{T\sigma_f^2}\left\{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \sigma_i^2 - \frac{1}{N(N-1)}\sum_{\substack{i,j=1\\i\neq j}}^N \sigma_{ij}\right\}, \quad (98.9)$

the cross-sectional variance of the population parameters:

$\sigma_\beta^2 := \frac{1}{N-1}\sum_{i=1}^N (\beta_i - \bar{\beta})^2 = \frac{1}{N-1}\beta'\beta, \quad (98.10)$

and the Rayleigh quotient:

$W_\Sigma := \frac{\beta'\Sigma_u\beta}{\beta'\beta}. \quad (98.11)$

The vector $\beta$ is defined as $\beta' := (\beta_1 - \bar{\beta}, \dots, \beta_N - \bar{\beta})$.

Since our focus is on the bias of the slope estimator, we rearrange (98.8) and get the following for the relative bias:

$\frac{E(\hat{\gamma}_1) - \gamma_1}{\gamma_1} \approx -\frac{\sigma_u^2}{\sigma_\beta^2} + \frac{2}{(N-1)}\frac{W_\Sigma}{\sigma_\beta^2}. \quad (98.12)$

Equations (98.8) and (98.12) are the central results of this chapter. They show that the bias of the slope estimator, i.e., the bias in the estimate of the pricing model's factor risk premium depends on the covariance structure of the idiosyncratic errors and, via $W_\Sigma$, also on the distribution of the test asset's true $\beta_i$. However, the errors $v_i$ in the explained variable have no impact on the bias, since they are uncorrelated with the errors $u_i$ in the explanatory variable.
