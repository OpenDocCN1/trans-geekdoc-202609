# Abstract

In this chapter, we first investigate how measurement errors can affect the estimators of CAPM, such as αⱼ and βⱼ. Then, we derive Plim b̂ assuming Rₘ and Rᵦ are measured with error. Finally, we develop an alternative hypothesis testing procedure for the CAPM.
