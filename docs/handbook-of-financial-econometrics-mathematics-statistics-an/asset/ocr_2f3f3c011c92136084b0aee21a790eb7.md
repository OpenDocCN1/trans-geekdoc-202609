# **52.5.2**  *June–July 1997*

Another jump in volatility was identified on June 11, while the speculators continued to attack the Thai baht. The scale was 8.873, pushing the estimated volatility to 10.05. This jump marked the beginning of a more volatile episode. Daily changes of more than 3% became common during this period. For example, the Thai baht appreciated 4.15% on June 17, depreciated 6.19% on June 19, appreciated 4.54% on June 27, and finally depreciated 17% on July 2. The SVIJ model estimated an 11.3% jump in the observation equation on July 2 and attributes the remainder of the 35% in changes to conditional volatility.

While the Thai baht continued to slide after July 2, the estimated volatility declined gradually, with the exception of a few sporadic occasions. The next major volatility jump happened on October 6, when the Thai baht depreciated 3.43% and appreciated 3.15% the following day. The SVIJ model identified this as a 4.9 jump in volatility.
