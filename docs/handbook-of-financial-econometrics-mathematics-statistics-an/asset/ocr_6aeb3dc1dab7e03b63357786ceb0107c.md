# 120.6.3 Bayesian estimation and testing

Bayesian analysis can be implemented in both parameter methods and semi-parameter methods for survival time data. In a Bayesian analysis, the parameters of the model are treated as random variables rather than as fixed characteristics of the population. For survival time data, Gibbs sampler algorithm from a Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) method was often used for Bayesian analysis. Interested readers could refer to Ibrahim, Chen and Sinha (2001).
