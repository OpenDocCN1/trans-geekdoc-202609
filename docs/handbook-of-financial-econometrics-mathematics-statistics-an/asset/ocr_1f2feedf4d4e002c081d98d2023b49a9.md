# 91.3 Development of a Model of Hedge Fund Performance

In this section, we review the literature pertaining to hedge fund performance estimation. There are various goals that researchers have when attempting to model hedge fund performance, but the objective of this chapter is to provide a basis from which researchers might be able to begin to explore the modeling of hedge fund returns. Pertinent literature related to estimating hedge fund performance, modelling that performance, and cloning that performance is found in the following references: Lo (2010), Grummitt and Satchell (2011), Bali et al. (2014), Chen and Tindall (2014), Lee and Lo (2014), Getmansky et al. (2015), Payne and Tresl (2015), O'Doherty et al. (2015, 2017), Smith and Mutmaz (2017), and Stafylas et al. (2017) among others. In this particular section, the goal is to provide a basis to model hedge fund performance and an extension to this line of research would be to clone hedge fund returns.
