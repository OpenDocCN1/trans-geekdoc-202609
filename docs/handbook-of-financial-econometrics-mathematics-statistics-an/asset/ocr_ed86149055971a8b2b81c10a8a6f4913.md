In Table 91.3, Smith et al. (2017) conditioned their estimates of beta, the systemic market risk factor, on the level of returns in the previous period. As Smith et al. (2017) sorted, they decreased the constant applied in equations (91.5) and (91.6) and ran the regressions in equation (91.4). When doing so, the systematic risk associated with the β₁⁻ factor increased markedly, likewise the systematic risk associated with the β₁⁺ factor decreased. According to Smith et al. (2017) the main takeaway from this analysis seems to be that when the returns in the preceding period are increasingly negative, hedge fund managers seem to be more likely to integrate greater and greater levels of systematic risk into their estimated returns.
