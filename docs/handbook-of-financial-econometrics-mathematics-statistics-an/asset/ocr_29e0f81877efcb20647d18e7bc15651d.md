# Theorem 40.4. CBOE's VIX is a special case of GVIX in that they are equal only if the stochastic process of asset's return follows Geometric Brownian Motion and Ito's Lemma as shown in (40.11) and (40.12).

The proof of the above theorem is straightforward. Based on the assumption of Geometric Brownian Motion and Ito's Lemma,

$$E\left[\ln^2\left(\frac{S_T}{S_0}\right)\right] = \left[\left(\mu - \frac12\sigma^2\right)T\right]^2 + \sigma^2 T,$$ and $$E\left[\ln\left(\frac{S_T}{S_0}\right)\right] = \left(\mu - \frac12\sigma^2\right)T.$$

From (40.13), (40.14), and (40.19), $$GVIX^2 = \frac1T\left\{E\left[\ln^2\left(\frac{S_T}{S_0}\right)\right] - \left\{E\left[\ln\left(\frac{S_T}{S_0}\right)\right]\right\}^2\right\}$$ $$= \sigma^2 = \frac1T\int_0^T \sigma^2 dt = E(V) = VIX^2.$$ Thus, if the diffusion assumptions of (40.11) and (40.12) hold, then GVIX \equiv VIX. (Q.E.D.)
