Notes: This table shows results from fixed-effects regressions of dividend payout on firm characteristics for 2,342 firms during the period from 1969 to 2011. The regressions are as follows:

ln( payout ratio_{i,t} / (1 - payout ratio_{i,t}) ) = α + β₁ Cov_{i,t} + β₂ Risk_{i,t} + β₃ D(g_{i,t} < ROE_{i,t}) · Risk_{i} + β₄ Growth_{i,t} + β₅ Cost_{i,t} + β₆ ln(Size)_{i,t} + β₇ ROE_{i,t} + e_{i,t}.

The dependent variable is the logistic transformation for dividend payout ratio. The independent variables are 5-year averages of covariance between the return on equity and the growth rate, beta risk, dummy times beta risk, growth rate, log of size, and the return on equity. The dummy variable is equal to 1 if a firm's 5-year average growth rate is less than its 5-year average ROE, and 0 otherwise. Growth rates are total asset growth (g_TA), sales growth (g_Sales), and the sustainable growth rate (g_sus) proposed by Higgins (1977). The t-statistics are presented in parentheses. Adjusted R² and p-values of the F-test on the null hypothesis show that models (1)–(3) are not different from the models (4)–(6) that are presented in the last rows. *, **, and *** indicate significance at the 10%, 5%, and 1% levels, respectively.
