Interestingly, the magnitude and the significance level of γ*_ij are uniformly greater and more significant than those of γ*_ii, meaning that the external risk (VaR) shocks, especially from the US, play a more significant role than domestic risk shocks in testing the risk–return relation for Asian markets.

Consistent with the finding in the US data reported by Bollerslev and Mikkelsen (1996), this study suggests that the optimal portfolio allocations based on the risk–return criterion may be sensitive to the investment horizon if investors’ risk behavior demonstrates long-run dependencies.
