```
PRESENT = f(MKT_ROA, Ln(POP), POPGROWTH, HHIADJ, MBB, MCU),
```

where PRESENT is a binary variable with a value of one when a large bank has a branch operating in a non-metro county; its value is zero otherwise. MKT_ROA is the average return on assets in the county the large bank operates in. Ln(POP) is the natural log of the estimated county population the large bank branch is in. POPGROWTH is the percentage change in estimated county population from 1 year to the next. HHIADJ is the Herfindahl-Hirschman index based on deposit market share in the county. In order to preserve the magnitude of this value when compared with other variables, for purposes of estimating the coefficients, it is necessary to adjust the HHI by dividing by 1000. MBB is the number of large banks in the county and MCU is the number of credit unions in the county. Dummy variables for each year in the sample; the first year of the sample is the omitted year in the specification.



The second stage of the Heckman (1977) model is estimated using a pooled OLS regression:

```
X_EFF_i = f(Ln(POP), POPGROWTH, HHIADJ, LNASETS, LOANAST, MBB, MCU, LAMBDA),
```

where the dependent variable X_EFF is the bootstrapped DEA estimate of cost efficiency for a large bank, community bank, or credit union depending on the case i. LNASETS is the natural log of the assets of the given financial institution. LAMBDA is the inverse Mills ratio of the first-stage Probit model when a large bank is present in the market. The other variables are as defined in (131.9). Dummy variables are included for each year; the first year of the sample is the omitted year. When the performance of a large bank is the dependent variable, the number of large banks and credit unions is considered. When the performance of a community bank is the dependent variable, the number of credit unions and community banks is considered.
