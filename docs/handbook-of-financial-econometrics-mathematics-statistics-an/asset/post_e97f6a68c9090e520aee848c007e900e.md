They developed a discrete trading strategy to directly control a portfolio’s maximum percentage of drawdown within a target level while maximizing the portfolio’s long-term growth rate.

## (4) Feasibility of market forecasting using regular data and big data

The literature on financial market forecasting using regular data is quite big due to about two decades of research accumulation (Pan, 2004, 2005, 2009, 2012, 2017, 2018). Forecasting US macro-economic indicators and stock index using big data has also been reported in the literature and in the media in recent years. Cavallo and Rigobon (2010) from MIT Sloan School of Business, started publicizing real-time inflation rate. Bollen et al. (2010) reported on how to forecast Dow Jones stock index using Twitter text data with a market sentiment model, reaching a hit rate of 85% (of course the reliability and stability of the forecasting is yet to be examined). Levenberg et al. (2014) from Oxford University reported on how to forecast macro-economic indicators using Internet text data. Internet search engines, such as Baidu from China have also put big data forecasting as their priority of research and development. The predictability of macro-economic indices and financial market indices as well as stock prices implies that the lead–lag ordering of the business cycles, market cycles, sector rotation for stocks and commodities as well as location rotation for real estate can be dynamically estimated or expected ahead of the market movement and the majority of market participants.

## 43.7 Conclusions and Outlook

This chapter provides, for the first time, a complete trinity of intelligent portfolio investment, including an Intelligent Portfolio Theory, a Strength Investing Methodology, and the Investability Mapping in the confluence of business and market cycles as well as sector and location rotations. Addressing the two outstanding problems in modern and post-modern portfolio theories — implementation and keeping pace with the times, the Intelligent Portfolio Theory lifts the quantitative portfolio management to a new height of intelligent portfolio trading systems in the Internet big data intelligence times, restructuring an investment portfolio in the form of a multi-asset portfolio consisting of many multi-strategy portfolios. The process of intelligent portfolio management is then driven by the methodology of strength investing, which dynamically focuses attention through hierarchical search and selection of stronger markets, sectors and assets. This process can be implemented as stronger trend following, for which four trend-following strategies are also identified. Strength investing overcomes the shortcomings of value investing on market timing and of trend following on severe drawdowns and undesired stoploss triggering.

The possibilities and opportunities of finding investable market strength will come from mapping the confluence of business cycles and market cycles as well as stock market sector rotation, real estate location rotation, and larger asset class rotation as empirically formulated in the investment clock of Merrill Lynch. We believe that sector rotation, location rotation and investment clock must be adapted to the actual locality — different national or regional economies.

The trinity of the intelligent portfolio investment provides a scientifically sound theoretical basis, an implementable methodology and a forward-looking roadmap to the further research and development of the intelligent portfolio theory and applications.
