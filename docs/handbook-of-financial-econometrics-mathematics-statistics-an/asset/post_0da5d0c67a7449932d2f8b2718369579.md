Although the MIMIC model has better goodness-of-fit measures in determining the capital structure, some effects of indicators are not consistent with theoretical predictions. For example, based on the argument of Jensen and Meckling (1976), Myers (1977), and Rampini and Viswanathan (2013), collateral assets should be negatively related to the capital structure of a firm. However, the MIMIC model shows that the ratio of inventory plus gross plant and equipment to total assets (INT_TA) is positively related to the capital structure. In addition, DeAngelo and Masulis (1980), Bowen et al. (1982), and Graham (2000) show that non-debt tax shield is negatively related to the capital structure, while the ratio of non-debt tax shields to total assets (NDT_TA) has a significantly positive effect on the capital structure. Given that the results of the MIMIC model are inconsistent with theoretical predictions, we are interested in establishing a structural equation model with better explanatory power in determining the capital structure.

## 35.4.4 Joint determinants of capital structure and stock returns by SEM with CFA

Dittmar and Thakor (2007) suggest that a firm’s stock price and its capital structure are simultaneously decided. We therefore introduce an SEM with CFA to jointly determine the capital structure and stock returns. We initially introduce 39 indicators in the structural equation model. To obtain a structural equation model with better explanatory power, we make certain adjustments on attributes and indicators in accordance with the results of determinants of capital structure in the previous SEM and MIMIC model. Specifically, we eliminate the non-debt tax shield (Nd_tax) attribute because of the high correlation to the asset structure (AtStruct) to avoid a non-positive covariance matrix or a singularity problem. We also eliminate the size attribute in accordance with insignificant results in Table 35.5. After the adjustment, there are 27 indicators and 10 attributes in the SEM with CFA model for joint determinants of the capital structure and stock returns. The conceptual diagram and the estimates of structural model for joint determinants of capital structure and stock returns are illustrated in Figure 35.8. The estimates of parameters of the measurement model and the estimated covariance matrix of attributes are presented in Tables 35.9 and 35.10, respectively.
