## 7.8 Summary and Concluding Remarks

In this chapter, we reviewed and compared parametric, semi-parametric, and non-parametric approaches for option bound determination and investigated how a parametric method incorporates both skewness and kurtosis explicitly through extending Zhang (1994) to provide bounds for the prices of options and the expected payoff, given the first two moments and skewness and kurtosis.

We have also obtained upper bounds for the option prices and the expected payoffs of options, given the first four moments of the distribution of the price of the underlying asset. When the cumulative distribution of the price of the underlying asset is known, tight bounds are derived, and the cumulative distribution is not known, semi-parametric bounds can be obtained as the maximum of the tight bounds which depend on the cumulative distributions. The main contributions of this chapter are: (i) it reviews alternative methods to derive option bounds and (ii) it provides one way to provide effective bounds for the expected payoffs and the prices of options when skewness and kurtosis are considered explicitly.

For further information about option bounds, readers can refer to the papers by Lee et al. (2019), Mathur and Ritchken (1999), Lin et al. (2012), Kothari (2006), and Lee et al. (2013).
