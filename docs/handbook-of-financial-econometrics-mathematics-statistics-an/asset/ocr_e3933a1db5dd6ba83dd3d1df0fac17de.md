The return series are firstly computed, and then standardized by subtracting the mean and divided by its standard deviation. The empirical distributions of the standardized return series for these two exchange rates are shown in Figure 73.3. The standardized Student’s t-distribution is fitted to these series (see Figure 73.4). The degree of freedom of the distribution is estimated using MLE.

The estimated degree of freedoms are then used in the MLE of the copula density functions to obtain the estimates for their parameters. The density functions are given in equations (73.9) and (73.11) for the Gaussian and Student’s t copulas, respectively.
