# 26.2.3 The cyclical component and business cycles

Cyclical patterns are long-term oscillatory patterns that are unrelated to seasonal behavior. They are not necessarily regular but instead follow rather smooth patterns of upswings and downswings, each swing lasting more than 2 or 3 years.
