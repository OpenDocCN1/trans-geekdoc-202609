FDIC monitors a bank’s asset on the discrete monitor time where the barrier line represents the minimum asset requirement according to FDIC closure rule. If the bank’s asset is below the minimum asset requirement (the barrier), FDIC takes over the bank, which its asset declines because of indirect bankrupt costs; otherwise, FDIC received the payment of deposit premium for the bank. If the bank’s asset is below the barrier on maturity date, the bank defaults, equity value equals to zero, and FDIC is responsible the payments to depositors.
