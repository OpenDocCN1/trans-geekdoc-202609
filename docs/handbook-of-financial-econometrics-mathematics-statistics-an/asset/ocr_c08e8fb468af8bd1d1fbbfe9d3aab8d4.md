This RR benefits the bond with the lower starting yield relative to the bond with the higher starting yield. Thus, it pulls the total returns together. The relative benefit of the RR to the lower-yield issue will be greater at low future RRs and less at high future rates.

As the workout time is reduced, the relative gain in realized compound yield over the workout period rises dramatically, as seen in Table 88.2.
