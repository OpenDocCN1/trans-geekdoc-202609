up fairly well with the use of an alternative subsample of analyst recommendation revisions exclusively made by sell-side analysts working for more prestigious brokerage houses under various single- and multi-factor asset pricing models. Our rolling window-based bootstrapping simulation method can be applied in other time-series investment performance to distinguish luck from skill.
