Figure 33.6 depicts the volatility scale estimates obtained from time series stochastic volatility model (33.5), fitted separately to realized variation and calibrated variance: {σ^RV}_t are parameters of model (33.5) fitted to {RV}_t, while {σ^SV}_t are parameters of model (33.5) fitted to calibrated variance. Once again, to more clearly depict their relative dynamics, {σ^RV}_t are plotted against the primary vertical axis (on the left), and {σ^SV}_t are plotted against the secondary vertical axis (on the right). While the levels of {σ^RV}_t and {σ^SV}_t are clearly different, the patterns in their dynamics are similar. The 95%-confidence intervals of {σ^SV}_t lie almost entirely below unity, which suggests that the calibrated variance overestimates the actual variation of the underlying process, invalidating Hypothesis 3.
