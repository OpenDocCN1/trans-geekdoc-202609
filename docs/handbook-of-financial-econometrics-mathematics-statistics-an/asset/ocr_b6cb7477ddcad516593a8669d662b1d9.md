Notes: The final sample thus consists of 987,347 observations corresponding to 7,537 firms during the period from 2010 to 2014. The entire firm sample was categorized into five portfolios according to their standardized unexpected earnings (SUE) by employing the FOS (1984) technique. Portfolio 1 represents firm with the largest positive SUE, while Portfolio 5 represents firm with the largest negative SUE. The sample period for every firm is divided into pre- and post- announcement periods. Each firm has observations prior and subsequent to the announcement of earnings. The observations in the pre- and post-announcement periods are used to perform the Chow test for testing the hypothesis that there is no structural shift in the systematic risks before and after the announcement of earnings. The test statistic is computed as:

```
F = (CSSSE-(SSE1+SSE2))/K / ((SSE1+SSE2)/(N1+N2-2K))
where SSE1 is the sum of squared errors for the OLS regression using the observations in the pre-announcement period, SSE2 is the sum of squared errors for the OLS regression using the observations in the post-announcement period, CSSSE is the sum of squared errors of the combined OLS regression. K is the number of parameters, N1 is the number of observations in the first separate OLS regression, and N2 is the number of observations in the last separate OLS regression.
```

systematic risks for both portfolios drift upward subsequent to the announcement of earnings. This phenomenon indicates that the CARs estimated with OLS are biased upward for both portfolios.

Kalman filter shows that the CARs in Portfolios 1 and 5 are more pronounced than those in other portfolios. The CARs in Portfolio 1 at the date of earnings announcement and the 31st day subsequent to earnings announcement are –0.014 and –0.040, respectively. The T-values are –3.258 (significant at a 5% confidence level) and –2.426 (significant at a 5% confidence level), respectively. On the other hand, the CARs for Portfolio 5 at the date of earnings announcement and the 31st day subsequent to earnings announcement are 0.029 and 0.094, respectively. The T-values are 6.913 and 6.512, respectively. Both are significant.
