The researchers used the in-sample data to establish five models to further predict the out-of-sample data: the intervention analysis integrated into the ARIMA-GARCH model, ECM, the intervention analysis integrated into transfer function model, simple average combination forecasting, and the minimum error combination forecasting model. Based on the differences between the predicted value and actual value, this paper offers an estimate of the prediction conditions of the five models by means of the MAPE, RMSE, and turning point method.

# 6.5.1 MAPE and RMSE (Appendix A)

According to Table 6.9, the MAPE shows that the intervention analysis integrated into the ARIMA-GARCH model, ECM, the intervention analysis integrated into transfer function model, the simple average combination forecasting model, and the minimum error combination forecasting model are within 5%, showing that all models have a high level of predictability in this paper. The RMSE of minimum error combination forecasting is the smallest, showing that model has the best predictive power in this paper.

# 6.5.2 Turning point method (Appendix 6A.1, 6A.2)

Based on the direction difference between predicted data and actual data, prediction capabilities can be calculated by means of the turning point method as shown in Table 6.10, which shows that intervention analysis integrated into the transfer function model and the minimum error combination forecasting model has better quality than other models. Tables 6.11 and 6.12 show that the best predicted periods are 3 and 6 months, followed by 9 and 12 months.
