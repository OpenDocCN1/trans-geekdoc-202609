The model consists of six latent variables: personal factors, digital skills, motivation, organizational support, labor productivity and company performance. Each latent variable were initially measured by 4 questions (items) using 5-point Likert scale. The data was also standardized (means equal to 0, standard deviations equal to 1) before starting the PLS-SEM algorithm (presented in Appendix 56A). The Figure 56.6 illustrates the baseline structural model results (without missing values, 114 observations), including coefficients, significances and variance explained for each dependent variable. In general, all hypotheses are supported by the data. Almost all coefficients are significant at the level of p ≤ 0.001 based on the results of the bootstrapping procedure with 5000 samples and no sign changes.

Standard methods were used to check the validity and reliability of the data (Wong, 2013). To check the indicator reliability outer loadings were used. Two measures for both personal factors and digital skills were excluded as their outer loadings were less than 0.7 and this procedure improved the quality of the model. Internal consistency reliability was satisfactory as all the composite reliability indicators were higher than 0.7 (Table 56.3). Convergent validity is also achieved as all the AVE indicators are higher than 0.5 (Table 56.3). According to the results of the Fornell–Larcker criteria (Table 56.4), the level of the discriminant validity is also acceptable. In addition, we check the collinearity problem and found that all VIF values are less than 2.
