¹⁵We also conduct a second simulation experiment in which we use ICA to extract factors and subsequently compute fitted bond yields using the JSZ normalization. In this experiment, we store the minimum value for each maturity to check the possibility of violation of the ZLB and repeat these steps over N = 1,000 iterations. We find that 53 times the 6-month fitted yield violated the ZLB, 771 times the one-year yield violated the ZLB, 947 times the two-year yield violated the ZLB, and that 730 times the three-year yield violated the ZLB. The remaining three maturities never violated the ZLB. Across these 1,000 trials, the standard deviation in the model-fitted bond yields varied very little implying that the third order polynomial density expansions used to approximate the negentropy of the observed data was stable.
