# # 13.5 Explaining the Time-Variation in Diversification Benefits

Thus far, we have shown investors still benefit from international diversification though markets are increasingly integrated. In this section, we further evaluate the extent to which economic factors impact the potential gains from international investments. Specifically, we are interested to ascertain how inflation, economic growth, risks, and dynamics of domestic and international financial markets affect the potential values of diversification. Moreover, we compare the impact of R² and correlation measuring market integration on the benefits of diversification.
