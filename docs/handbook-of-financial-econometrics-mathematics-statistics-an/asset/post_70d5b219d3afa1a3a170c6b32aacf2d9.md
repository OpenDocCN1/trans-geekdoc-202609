## Abstract

This study examines the relationships between the gold spot and futures with different maturities using a time-varying and quantile-dependent approach, that is, the quantile co-integration model. This model allows the co-integrating coefficient to vary over the conditional distribution of gold spot prices. We find that the returns of gold at lower quantiles, the co-integration among gold spot prices and one- to six-month gold futures prices are less strong than the returns at high quantiles. When the gold returns of quantiles are high, these relationships become even stronger. In terms of the co-integration between gold and VIX (CBOE Volatility Index), we find that the co-integration of gold spot prices, futures prices and VIX at high quantile are greater than those observed at low quantiles. Our work adds another cross-sectional dimension to the extant literature, which uses only the time-series dimension to examine the co-integration. Furthermore, the results suggest that while investors intend to hedge risk by exercising futures contracts, using short-term futures would be a better choice than the long-term contracts.

## 92.1 Introduction

Since 2007, due to the global financial crises and the debt crisis in Europe, reserve currencies have been on a decline, leading to the increased price of gold. With the emerging market-driven demand for gold, the gold price hit an all-time record of $1,900/oz in 2011. The US dollar has become chronically weak making the dollar-denominated gold futures all grow in value. Theoretically, the futures prices reflect the expectation of investors’ belief. This means that the futures prices are the unbiased predictors of future spot prices. However, under the efficient market hypothesis, the spot and futures returns should not be cross-correlated and the futures price should not be the unbiased estimation of the future spot price. The empirical results related to this issue are diverse, although a few studies indicated that futures prices influence spot prices and not vice versa.
