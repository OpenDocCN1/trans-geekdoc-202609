On a particular note, although the gold futures and VIX show an increasing co-integration with increasing quantiles, the co-integration for the long-term futures is lower than short-term futures.

A similar evidence has been found for gold futures prices and VIX³, indicating that co-integration at a high quantile is higher than those observed at low quantiles. Consequently, using the traditional Johansen co-integration, which is the average of all quantiles, may cause biases. That is, it would underestimate the co-integration for high quantile memberships or overestimate the co-integration for low quantile memberships. Investors should be aware of this in order to make a better decision on hedging.
