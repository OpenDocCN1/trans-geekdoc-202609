Set $X_t = \log(V_t/K_t)$ and put $\tau(T)=\inf\{c \le s \le t; X_s \le 0\}$. Then we have

$X_t = \log(V_t/K_t) + \left(r - \frac12\sigma^2 - \lambda\nu - \kappa\right) t + \sigma W_t - Z_t$ 
$= X_0 + c t + \sigma W_t - Z_t,\quad t \in R_+,\qquad (110.5)$

where $X_0 = x = \log(V_0/K_0)$, $c = r - \frac12\sigma^2 - \lambda\nu - \kappa$, and $Z_t \triangleq \sum_{n=1}^{N_t} -\log(1+U_n)$. It follows from (110.4) that the no arbitrage price of the bond is given by

$D(V_0,T)= e^{-rT} - e^{-rT}E_x[\psi(X_{\tau(T)})1_{\{\tau(T)<\infty\}}] = e^{-rT} - e^{-rT}\Phi(x,T),\qquad (110.6)$

where

$\Phi(x,T) \triangleq E_x[\psi(X_{\tau(T)})1_{\{\tau(T)<t\}}].\qquad (110.7)$

Here $E_x$ denotes the expectation conditioning on $X_0 = x$ under the risk-neutral measure $Q$. We also write $E$ for $E_0$.

In this paper, instead of trying to derive a closed form solution for the bond price, we derive an integral equation for the function $\Phi$ in (110.7). The elements of the integral equation are in explicit forms. (See Theorem 110.4 below.) Using these elements, we study the analytic properties of the bond prices. In particular, under mild conditions on jump distribution and writedown function, we show that the credit spread has a strictly positive lower bound as time to maturity tends to zero. It is interesting to compare our theoretic results with the simulation results obtained earlier by Zhou (2001) and Hilberink and Rogers (2002) (in which only some specific jump distributions and writedown functions were considered). As another application of the integral equation, we derive an infinite series expression for the bond prices. (Note that in Merton's jump diffusion model, if $1+U_j$ is lognormal, there is an infinite sum of Black–Scholes type expressions for the price of the risky zero coupon bond. (see Bingham and Kiesel 2004, Theorem 9.3.1).

The structure of this paper is as follows. In Section 110.2, we use integral equation techniques to take a first look at the bond price. Based on results in Section 110.2, we study in Section 110.3 properties of bond prices and investigate the behavior of the credit spreads as the time to maturity tends to zero. In Appendix, we justify an infinite series expression for the bond prices.
