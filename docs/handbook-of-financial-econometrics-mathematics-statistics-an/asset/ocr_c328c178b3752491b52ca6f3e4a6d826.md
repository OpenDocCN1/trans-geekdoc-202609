Panel A of Table 42.1 provides summary information on TAIEX call and put options. During the sample period, the mean and median maturities for both call and put options are 19 days. Relative to put options, call options are traded at a greater mean volume but a lower median volume. The mean and median of open interest are smaller for the call options than for the put options. We denote ln(F/K) as representing option moneyness, where F indicates the TAIEX futures price and K is the option strike price. Following Ofek et al. (2004), we specify that the absolute value of ln(F/K) for at-the-money options is less than 10%. The median moneyness of the call and put options illustrates that the proportions of at-the-money calls and puts in the full sample exceed 50%.⁵ The value of ln(F/K) for in-the-money call (put) options is between 10% (−10%) and 30% (−30%). The 95th percentile of moneyness for the call options is 20.33% and the 5th percentile of moneyness for the put options is −10.8%, indicating that our sample is characterized by a large proportion of in-the-money call options and a small proportion of in-the-money put options.⁶ The implied volatilities derived from the Black and Scholes (1973) formula show that the volatility of put option prices is greater than the volatility of call option prices.
