# 34.3 Formulation

When one writes options on assets, it is important that investors get the most out of that option investment. And in order to get the best possible value, investors need to minimize risks inherent in those option investments. One of the ways of minimizing risks is through hedging your investment position(s). In order to give a broader overview on application of jumpy exchange option, this chapter illustrates both pricing and hedging of jumpy exchange option.

# 34.3.1 Pricing of the Margrabe model

When markets are complete, the Margrabe formula with cost of carry is given by the following formula:

C[S1, S2, τ] = K S1 e^{y1 τ} N(d1) − S2 e^{y2 τ} N(d2), (34.1)

with

d1 = [ln(K S1 / S2) + ((y1 − y2 + σp²)/2) τ] / (σp √τ), (34.2)
d2 = d1 − σp √τ, (34.3)
σ̂p = √[σ1 + σ2 − 2 σ1 σ2 ρ1;2], (34.4)
