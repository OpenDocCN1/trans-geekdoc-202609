A common solution for this problem is to gather risk values of different banks together to study the general condition of the entire banking industry. For example, Rosenberg and Schuermann (2006) gathered the quarterly data of 17 banking holding companies from 1994Q1 to 2002Q4. Kuritzkes and Schuermann (2007) collected data of all banks in Y-9C regulatory reports from 1986Q2 to 2005Q1. Therefore, in line with these studies, we also gather the risk values of different banks together to construct our empirical dataset.

Because all the risk proxies are from financial statements, the sample in this empirical study includes only listed Chinese banks that release financial reports regularly. Although there are only 16 listed banks in China, their total assets accounted for 53% of the entire Chinese banking industry in 2016. The 16 Chinese listed banks and their abbreviations are shown in Table 38.3. Most of these banks were listed after 2007, and a new accounting standard was also put into effect in the same year, so the collection of our data starts from 2007. Moreover, to eliminate the scale effect, the data of earnings or losses are divided by risk-weighted assets, which are updated every half year. Ultimately, the data consist of all observations from the 16 banks, spanning from 2007 to 2016, in semi-yearly frequency.
