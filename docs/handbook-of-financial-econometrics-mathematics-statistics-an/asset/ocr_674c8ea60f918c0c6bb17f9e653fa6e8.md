FinTech has become one of the major investments for the most competitive financial firms due to driven forces (Gai et al., 2018). Financial Technology describes the high-tech products adopted by the financial service institutions, which predominantly focuses on the improvement of the service quality via Information Technology (IT) applications. This term contains a large scope of technical aspects, which includes security and privacy, data techniques, hardware and infrastructure, applications and management, and service models (EY, 2017). The main possibilities concerning the rise of FinTech are cost reduction, differentiation, retention of clients and extra revenues (PwC, 2016a).

Evidently, disruptive technologies have a disproportionately strong impact on the financial services sector. Technology forces that matter are as follows: blockchain will cause a radical restructuring of the industry; big data analytics will be the most important predictor of revenue growth and profitability; advances in artificial intelligence will start a wave of ‘re-shoring’ and localization; the cloud computing will have the strategic importance to organizations (PwC, 2016b); machine learning are increasing firms’ opportunity to analyze transactional data and forecast customers’ possible behavior outcomes; information security technologies provide an opportunity to protect organizations from the rising risk of cyber threats and make them more secure (EY, 2017). Relying on these facts, in the current study, we will consider the same list of emerging technologies in financial organizations as the most influential and relevant to invest.

Like any new technology, companies need to understand the benefits of adoption. Due to blockchain, transactions with financial instruments can be implemented with greater efficiency through faster data processing and lower costs and also with security. Using big data and analytics, firms may predict customer needs and find new paths to get profit. With artificial intelligence, companies can unlock powerful insights and move staff to higher-value work. Robots provide consistent, convenient, low-cost service and customers have grown to trust them (PwC, 2016b). The advantages of cloud-based solutions are enabling financial businesses to closely connect to the target markets by improving system performances (Gai et al., 2015) and also it makes it much easier to manage and analyze ‘big data’. Benefits of augmented and virtual reality may include faster prototyping, enhanced design and training, risk-free exploration, increased efficiencies, improved safety and cost savings (PwC, 2018).
