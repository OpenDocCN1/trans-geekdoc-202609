# 34.2.2 The effects of stock jumps on securities

# 34.2.2.1 Dynamics of financial events or parameters

The first article that explored the effect of stock price jumps on options is Merton (1976). According to Merton (1976), the BS solution is inappropriate when price dynamics are non-stochastic in nature. He argues that jumps in stock prices are due to (i) normal variations in price, and (ii) abnormal variations due to arrival of new information. Note that both the points put forward by Merton (2001) have nothing to do with incompleteness of stock markets, which this chapter takes into account. In modeling the stock price jumps, assume that prices follow geometric BM. Moreover, stock jumps are either specific to the firm or industry. He went further and illustrated probabilities in the presence of jumps and in the absence of jumps.
