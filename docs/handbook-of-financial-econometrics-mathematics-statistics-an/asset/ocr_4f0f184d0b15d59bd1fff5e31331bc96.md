# 109.6 Conclusions

The option pricing formula under the CEV model is quite complex because it involves the cumulative distribution function of the noncentral Chi-square distribution Q(z, v, k). Some computational considerations are given in the chapter which will facilitate the computation of the CEV option pricing formula. Hence, the computation will not be a difficult problem in practice.
