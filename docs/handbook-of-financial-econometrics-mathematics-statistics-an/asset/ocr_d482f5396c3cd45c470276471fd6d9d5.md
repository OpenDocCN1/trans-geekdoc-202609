# 31.4.4 Analyst performance measures

Prior papers have proposed various measures of analyst performance: earnings forecast accuracy (Hong and Kubik, 2003; Jackson, 2005), timeliness (Cooper et al., 2001; Jegadeesh and Kim, 2010; Loh and Stulz, 2011), and consistency (Hilary and Hsu, 2013). In this subsection, we describe how we construct these performance measures and compare our approach of utilizing the network structural hole measures with those three measures.
