# 92.3.2 Johansen co-integration test for the spot gold and futures gold price

We first use Johansen’s linear co-integration method to examine whether co-integration exists between spot prices and futures. The results in Table 92.3 confirm the existence of co-integrating relationships between contemporaneous spot and various maturities of futures gold contracts for both the eigenvalue and the trace test statistics. The results in Table 92.4 display the estimated long-run relationships among gold spot and various futures and confirm that all four types of gold futures have long-run information content with spot gold prices. This result implies that spot and futures gold across various maturities are co-integrated.
