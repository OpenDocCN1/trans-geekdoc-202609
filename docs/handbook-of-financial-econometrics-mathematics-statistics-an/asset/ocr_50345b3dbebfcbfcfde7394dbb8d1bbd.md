# Figure 40.3: Trend stationarity of GV-Spread with a break.

Notes: The figure depicts the time plots of Zivot-Andrews (ZA) test statistic (daily t-statistics) over the entire sample period from January 2005 to May 2014. The null hypothesis of ZA test is a unit-root process without any exogenous structural breaks, and the alternative hypothesis is a trend-stationary process with possible structural change occurring at an unknown point in time. The 5% critical value is −4.80. Therefore, any daily t-statistic that has a value below (above) −4.80 is statistically significant (insignificant). The break date of the trend is indicated by the minimum test statistic and marked by a vertical “dot-line”. The breakpoint date for the GV-Spread is September 8, 2008.
