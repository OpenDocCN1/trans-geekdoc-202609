Four tax scenarios are defined as in Tables 128.8 and 128.9. The value of a tax-timing option is defined as TO(t0,T) = [vOP(t0,T)-vBH(t0,T)] / vOP(t0,T) in percentage, where vBH(t0,T), vOP(t0,T) are bond price under the buy-and-hold and optimal trading strategies, respectively.
