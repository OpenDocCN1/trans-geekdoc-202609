After repeating the second step to get the monthly prices of IBM, BA, and S&P 500 index, the final step is the calculation of the monthly returns. The monthly returns at time t is the value of the price at time t minus the price at time t−1 divided by the price at time t. In excel, use the formula “=(B4-B3)/B3” in cell C4 to get JNJ’s monthly return in April 2000, and then by the same way, we can get the monthly returns of the individual securities and S&P 500 index.
