Table 86B.4: Definitions and formulas of Greek letters for European call and put options.

| Greek letter | Definition | European call options | European put options |
|---|---|---|---|
| Δ | The change of the option price respect to the rate of change of underlying asset price | N(d₁) | 1 − N(d₁) |
| Θ | The rate of change of the option price with respect to the passage of time | −(Sσ)/(2√t) N'(d₁) − rX* e^{−rt} N(d₂) | −(Sσ)/(2√t) N'(d₁) + rX* e^{−rt} N(−d₂) |
| Γ | The rate of change of delta with respect to the rate of change of underlying asset price | (1)/(Sσ√t) N'(d₁) | (1)/(Sσ√t) N'(d₁) |
| ν | The rate of change of the option price with respect to the volatility of the underlying asset | S * √t * N'(d₁) | S * √t * N'(d₁) |
| ρ | The rate of change of the option price with respect to the interest rate | X t * e^{−rt} N(d₂) | −X t * e^{−rt} N(−d₂) |
