
Then,

plim{T^{-1}(X'Z_j)} = plim{T^{-1}(X'X)} [Π*_j  I; Π*_{-j} 0] - [plim{T^{-1}(X*_j' + ν_j ν_j')}Π*_j 0; plim{T^{-1}(X*_{-j}' + ν_{-j} ν_{-j}')}Π*_{-j} 0] + 0 = (Σ + θ) [Π*_j  I; Π*_{-j} 0] - [θ_j Π*_j 0; θ_{-j} Π*_{-j} 0] ≡ H, (116.56)

where θ_j is the submatrix of θ, corresponding to erroneously measured exogenous variables included in the jth equation. θ_{-j} is the submatrix of θ, corresponding to erroneously measured exogenous variables that do not appear in the jth equation. As shown in equation (116.56), the structural coefficients of explanatory endogenous variables, Π*_j and Π*_{-j}, play a role in the asymptotic bias of 2SLS estimator.

(iii) T^{-1}(X'([0 ν_j]δ_j + ε_j)):

plim{T^{-1}(X'([0 ν_j]δ_j + ε_j))} = plim{T^{-1}(X'[0 ν_j]δ_j + X'ε_j)} = plim{T^{-1}X'[0 ν_j]δ_j} = plim{T^{-1}[0 X'_j ν_j; 0 X'_{-j} ν_j]δ_j} = plim{T^{-1}[0 (X*_j' + ν_j)ν_j; 0 (X*_{-j}' + ν_{-j})ν_j]δ_j} = [0 θ_j; 0 0]δ_j. (116.57)

As shown in equation (116.57), the asymptotic bias of 2SLS estimator depends on the structural coefficients (β_j) and its covariance matrix of measurement error (θ_j), corresponding to erroneously measured exogenous variables included in the jth equation.

Combining (116.50), (116.56), and (116.57), the asymptotic bias of 2SLS estimator is given by plim(δ̂_j^{2SLS} - δ_j) = -(H'(Σ + θ)^{-1}H)^{-1}H'(Σ + θ)^{-1} × [0 θ_j; 0 0]δ_j. (116.58)

The asymptotic bias of 2SLS estimated coefficients can be analyzed as follows:

- (i) The bias term depends on the structural coefficients corresponding to exogenous variables (β_j) and reduced form coefficients of explanatory endogenous variables (Y_j), Π*_j and Π*_{-j}.
- (ii) If an equation without exogenous variables (β_j = 0), the structural parameters are always estimated consistently.
