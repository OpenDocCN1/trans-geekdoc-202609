# 43.2.2 Inevitability of the intelligent portfolio theory to emerge

We think that the attribution of the application limitedness of modern portfolio theory to the implementation issues, a typical view of financial academics, corresponds only to the surface of the real problems. The missed essence of the problems is that the implementation issues of modern portfolio theory corresponds to a big problem domain, that is the theory, models and systems of technical trading. In this problem domain, by and large, the financial academics have had prejudice, are not good at, and are short of practical experience and systematic research. We believe a complete post-modern portfolio theory must comprise docking and integration of portfolio optimization and technical trading realization.

Most notably, after experiencing the recent global financial and economic crisis triggered by the US subprime mortgage crisis since 2008, it has become more and more pressing on modern financial theory to keep pace with the times. The main characteristics of the current times is that finance has entered into the era of Internet big data intelligent finance on the basis of computerization, explosive generation and accumulation of data, and the Internet going mobile and online.
