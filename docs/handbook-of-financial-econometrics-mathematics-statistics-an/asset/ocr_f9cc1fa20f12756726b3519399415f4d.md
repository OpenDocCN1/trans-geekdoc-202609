In addition to the above parametric approach, we also conducted the semiparametric “local” Whittle approach of Robinson (1995a) as described in Appendix B. The results based on this approach are displayed in Table 23.6.

We present the results for a selected group of values for m. Focusing on m = 16 = (n)^{0.5}, which is the value usually employed in most empirical applications, we observe that the unit root null cannot be rejected in a number of countries (Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand), while for all the others, the estimated values of d are strictly below 1 and thus showing mean reversion. Nevertheless, the results here seem to be very sensitive to the specification of the bandwidth parameter numbers.
