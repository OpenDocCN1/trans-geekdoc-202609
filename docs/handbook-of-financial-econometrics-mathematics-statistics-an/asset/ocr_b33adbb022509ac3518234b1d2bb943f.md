# 25.4.1 *Empirical results and analysis*

The results of the OLS regression analysis are presented in Table 25.3. The coefficient on LOGNB, the logarithm of the number of banking relationships is negative and significant, which suggests that, controlling for other relevant factors, there is a negative relationship between the number of banking relationships and firm performance. This result jibes with Yu et al. (2011), Degryse and Ongena (2001) and Bhattacharya and Chiesa (1995) and is in contrast to Von Rheinbaben and Ruckes (1998). Our result implies the existence of a potential free riders hip problem when the number of banking relationships increases.
