# Proposition 7.6 (Grundy (1991)).

Consider the true at risk-neutral distributions corresponding to the following diffusion processes

$d s_t = \mu(S_t, t) S_t dt + \delta(S_t, t) S_t dz_t \qquad (7.41)$

and

$d s_t'' = r(t) S_t'' dt + \delta(S_t, t) S_t'' dz_t,$

where $z_t$ is the standard Gauss–Wiener process. If interest rates are non-stochastic⁴ and $\mu(\cdot) \ge r(t)$, $\forall t$, then the expected return on the call over its life is at least one plus risk-free rate over the $j$ same time period.

Proof. See Proposition 7.5 of Grundy (1991).

# Proposition 7.7 (Grundy (1991)).

If the conditions of Proposition 7.6 are satisfied and $\frac{\partial C}{\partial t} < 0$, then the expected return on a call over its life is at least the mean gross return of the underlying stock over the same time period.

Proof. See Proposition 6 of Grundy (1991).
