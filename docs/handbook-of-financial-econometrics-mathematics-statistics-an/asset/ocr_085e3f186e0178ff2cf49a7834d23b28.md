Therefore, (65.6) gives the joint pdf of $W_1$ and $W_2$, for $-\infty < W_1 < \infty$, $0 < W_2$, indexed by the two parameters $\mu$ and $\sigma$, and sample size $T$. The marginal pdf of $W_1$ is obtained by integrating $g(w_1,w_2)$ over the sample space of $W_2$. That is the pdf of $W_1$ is the definite integral of (65.6) with respect to $W_2$, integrated from 0 to $\infty$, and since this is a definite integral of a convergent infinite series, integration and summation can be interchanged to give a convergent infinite series of definite integrals. The interchange is further permitted here because the functions being integrated and summed are measurable and the integrals and sums exist.$^2$

Thus, if $f(w_1)$ denoted the pdf of $W_1$, then

$$f(w_1)=\int_{0}^{\infty} g(w_1,w_2)dw_2$$

$$=c\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{\mu}{\sigma^2/T}w_1-\frac12\right)^r \frac1{r!}\int_{0}^{\infty} w_2^{r+\frac{T-1}{2}} e^{-\frac1{2\sigma^2/T}w_1^2 w_2^2} dw_2.$$\tag{65.7}

The integral on the far right in (65.7) is

$$\int_{0}^{\infty} w_2^{r+\frac{T-1}{2}} e^{-\frac1{2\sigma^2/T}w_1^2 w_2^2} dw_2$$

$$=\int_{0}^{\infty}\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}\sigma}{\sqrt{T}w_1}\right)^{r+\frac{T-1}{2}} \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{T}\sqrt{2}}\frac1{w_1}e^{-v\frac12(r+\frac{T-1}{2})-\frac12}dv$$

$$=\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}\sigma}{\sqrt{T}w_1}\right)^{r+\frac{T-1}{2}} \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{T}\sqrt{2}}\frac1{w_1}\int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-v\frac12(r+\frac{T-1}{2}-1)}dv,\tag{65.8}$$

and the integral on the far right in (65.8) may be written as

$$\int_{0}^{\infty} e^{-v} v^{\frac{2r+T+1}{4}-1} dv = \Gamma\left(\frac{2r+T+1}{4}\right).$$

To emphasize that (65.8) is a function of $w_1^2$, it can be written as

$$\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}\sigma}{\sqrt{T}}\right)^{r+\frac{T-1}{2}} \frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{T}}\left(\frac1{w_1^2}\right)^{\frac{2r+T+1}{4}} \Gamma\left(\frac{2r+T+1}{4}\right).\tag{65.8a}$$
