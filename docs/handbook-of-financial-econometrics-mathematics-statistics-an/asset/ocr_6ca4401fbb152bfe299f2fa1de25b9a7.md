# 35.4.4 Joint determinants of capital structure and stock returns by SEM with CFA

Dittmar and Thakor (2007) suggest that a firm’s stock price and its capital structure are simultaneously decided. We therefore introduce an SEM with CFA to jointly determine the capital structure and stock returns. We initially introduce 39 indicators in the structural equation model. To obtain a structural equation model with better explanatory power, we make certain adjustments on attributes and indicators in accordance with the results of determinants of capital structure in the previous SEM and MIMIC model. Specifically, we eliminate the non-debt tax shield (Nd_tax) attribute because of the high correlation to the asset structure (AtStruct) to avoid a non-positive covariance matrix or a singularity problem. We also eliminate the size attribute in accordance with insignificant results in Table 35.5. After the adjustment, there are 27 indicators and 10 attributes in the SEM with CFA model for joint determinants of the capital structure and stock returns.
