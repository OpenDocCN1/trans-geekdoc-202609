| Chen, Lee, and Shrestha (2001) | S&P 500
(4/1982–12/1991)
[weekly data] | This chapter proposes the use of the M-GSV hedge ratio. The chapter estimates the MV, optimum mean-variance, Sharpe, MEG, GSV, M-MEG, and M-GSV hedge ratios. The Jarque–Bera (1987) test and D’Agostino (1971) D statistic indicate that the price changes are not normally distributed. Furthermore, the expected value of the futures price change is found to be significantly different from zero. It is also found that for a high level of risk aversion, the M-MEG hedge ratio converges to the MV hedge ratio whereas the M-GSV hedge ratio converges to a lower value. |
| Hung, Chiu, and Lee, (2006) | S&P 500
(01/1997–12/1999)
[daily data] | This chapter proposes minimization of VaR in deriving the optimum hedge ratio. It finds cointegrating relationship between the spot and futures returns and uses bivariate constant correlation GARCH(1,1) model with error correction term. It compares the proposed hedge ratio with MV hedge ratio and hedge ratio (HKL hedge ratio) proposed by Hsin, Kuo and Lee (1994). It also finds the performance of the proposed hedge ratio to be similar to the HKL hedge ratio. Finally, the proposed hedge ratio converges to the MV hedge ratio for high risk-averse levels. |
| Lee, and Yoder (2007) | Nikkei 225 and Hang Seng index futures
(01/1989–12/2003)
[weekly data] | This chapter proposes regime-switching time varying correlation GARCH model and compares the resulting hedge ratio with constant correlation GARCH and time-varying correlation GARCH. The proposed model is found to outperform the other two hedge ratio in both in-sample and out-of-sample for both contracts. |
| Lien, and Shrestha, K (2007) | 23 different futures contracts (sample period depends on contracts)
[daily data] | This chapter proposes wavelet base hedge ratio to compute the hedge ratios for different hedging horizons (1-day, 2-day, 4-day, 8-day, 16 day, 32-day, 64-day, 128-day; and 256-day and longer). It is found that the wavelet-based hedge ratio and the error-correction-based hedge ratio are larger than MV hedge ratio. The performance of wavelet-based hedge ratio improves with the length of hedging horizon. |
