# Abstract

We examine the effect of book-tax differences on CEO compensation. We posit that CEOs can opportunistically exercise the discretion in GAAP to increase accounting income without affecting taxable income and in so doing increase their compensation. We test the data to determine which competing hypothesis dominates — efficiency or rent-seeking.
