Three tests of asset pricing restrictions as special cases of equation (12.42) are implemented. First, we test the validity of the conditional CAPM that implies that γ must be significantly different from zero. The null hypothesis of γ = 0 is tested against the alternative hypothesis γ ≠ 0. Second, the test of market hedging risk, exchange rate risk and exchange rate hedging risk as an important factor in the asset pricing model is implemented. This test implies that γ is not equal to one in equation (12.42). Otherwise, risk premia of assets are determined only by the covariance with the market portfolio. As discussed in the theoretical model of Section 12.3.2, if we only test the price of market hedging risk, V_{ih}, and currency risk, V_{ie}, we can find that the two risk values will be significant or insignificant simultaneously because they all depend on whether or not relative risk aversion, γ, is different from one. Hence, we ultimately want to know which risk is most important in terms of scale.

Equation (12.42) follows directly from the system of the intertemporal IAPM. To implement the tests of the above hypotheses, the dynamics of the variance–covariance structure in equation (12.42) must be specified. A multivariate GARCH process based on the work of Ding and Engle (1994) is used to obtain a testable version of the model.
