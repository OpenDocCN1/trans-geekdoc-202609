In every step, either asset selection, portfolio construction, or trade execution, risk management should be implemented throughout. In the step of asset selection, assets with big risk are directly ruled out. This is a hard measure of risk control. In the stage of portfolio construction, assets with bigger risk are assigned with smaller weights, or with zero weights. This is a soft measure of risk control. In the stage of trade execution, the timing of entry and exit as well the way of entry and exit such as trading the entire position at once, scaling in or out, or algorithmic trading, and the tradeoff between the profit target and stoploss levels, are all parts of risk control. The equity curve of a trading strategy is also an important source of information, which can be used as a feedback signal for starting or stopping the trading strategy on the given asset. This showcases the technical-level risk control. In addition, there are trading strategies that encompasses predictive analysis in the trading decision and control. Therefore, such a complete process of investment management comprises three-level dynamic optimization processes — asset selection, portfolio construction and trading strategy.

For the five aspects of a general financial investment management — predictive analysis, asset selection, portfolio construction, technical trading and risk management, the Intelligent Portfolio Theory (IPT) first of all integrates portfolio construction and technical trading into a framework of portfolio trading. This framework extends left to the information fusion-based predictive analysis of the markets and relative strength analysis of the assets for selecting assets with relatively strong trends, and it reaches right to the portfolio adjustment and rebalance as well as the total risk management.
