# Appendix 110A Infinite Series Expression for Bond Price

Now, consider the space $E_T \triangleq R_+ \times [0,T]$, $T > 0$. Take $E = R_+ \times [0,\infty)$ for notational convenience. For every bounded measurable function $f$ on $E$, the operator $L$ is defined by the formula,

$L f(x,t) = \int_0^t dF_{J_1}(s) \int dF(y) \int_{y^+}^{\infty} dw\, f(w - y, t - s) H(x,w,s),$

where $H$ is defined in (110.15). Then by Theorem 110.4, we can write $\Phi$ as

$\Phi(x,t) = G(x,t) + L\Phi(x,t), \quad \forall (x,t) \in E. \tag{110A.1}$

Now, after $n$th iteration of (110A.1), we have

$\Phi(x,t) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} L^k G(x,t) + L^{n+1}\Phi(x,t), \tag{110A.2}$

where $L^0 = Id$ and $K^{k+1} f = L(L^k f)$. From the definition of $f$, one sees that

$L f(x,t) = E_x\Big[ f(X_{J_1}, t - J_1) 1\big(J_1 \le t, \min_{0\le s\le J_1} X_s > 0\big)\Big].$

In general, after $n$th iteration, we have the following result.

# Lemma 110A.1. Let $f$ be a nonnegative measurable function. Then, for any $n \in N$,

$L^n f(x,t) = E_x\Big[ f(X_{J_n}, t - J_n) 1\big(J_n \le t, \min_{0\le s\le J_n} X_s > 0\big)\Big]. \tag{110A.3}$

Moreover, if $f$ is bounded, $L^n f$ converges to as $n \to \infty$ uniformly on $E_T$.

# Proof.

The proof proceeds with induction. We already have the case $n = 1$ by the definition of $L$.

Assume that for $n = k$ the conclusion of the lemma holds. Then for $n = k + 1$,

$L^{k+1} f(x,t) = E_x\Big[ L^k f(X_{J_1}, t - J_1) 1\big(J_1 \le t, \min_{0\le s\le J_1} X_s > 0\big)\Big] = E_x\Big[ E_{X_{J_1}}\big[ f(X_{J_k}, v - J_k) 1\big(J_k \le t, \min_{0\le s\le J_k} X_s > 0\big)\big] \Big|_{v - t - J_1} 1\big(J_1 \le t, \min_{0\le s\le J_1} X_s > 0\big)\Big]$
