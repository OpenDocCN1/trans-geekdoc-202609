of the characteristic equation

$$1 - \sum_{i=1}^{q} \left(\alpha_{i1} + \frac{1}{2}\alpha_{i2}\right) \lambda^{i} - \sum_{j=1}^{p} \beta_{j} \lambda^{j} = 0 \tag{123.11}$$

# 123.2.2.2 Exponential GARCH (EGARCH) (p, q) model

Nelson (1991) proposed another model to catch the leverage effects

$$\log(h_t) = \omega + \sum_{i=1}^{q} \alpha_i g(z_{t-i}) + \sum_{j=1}^{p} \beta_j \log(h_{t-j}), \tag{123.12}$$

where $g(z_{t-i}) = \kappa z_{t-i} + \varsigma(|z_{t-i}| - E(|z_{t-i}|))$, and $\omega$, $\alpha_i$, $\beta_j$, $\kappa$ and $\varsigma$ are real numbers. This specification allows asymmetry in the volatility, and also avoids positivity restrictions on the parameters of $\omega$, $\alpha_i$, and $\beta_j$ by way of specifying the log-volatility. The process of $\{h_t\}$ is stationary if all solutions of the characteristic equation

$$1 - \sum_{j=1}^{p} \beta_j \lambda^{j} = 0 \tag{123.13}$$

are greater than unity in absolute value.

# 123.2.3 Estimation of the GARCH(p, q) model

The parameter vector in the GARCH(p, q) model for equations (123.1) and (123.4) is $\theta = (\theta_1, \dots, \theta_{k+p+q+2}) = (\mu, \gamma_1, \dots, \gamma_k; \omega, \alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_q; \beta_1, \dots, \beta_p)'$. The true value of parameters is unknown and denoted as

$$\theta_0 = (\theta_{01}, \dots, \theta_{0k+p+q+2}) = (\mu_0, \gamma_{01}, \dots, \gamma_{0k}; \omega_0, \alpha_{01}, \dots, \alpha_{0q}; \beta_{01}, \dots, \beta_{0p})'.$$

The orders of the GARCH(p, q) model (namely k, p, and q) are assumed known to estimate the parameters. Though we do not assume any specific distribution of the standardized random variable $z_t$, we work with the normal distribution. Then the quasi-likelihood is

$$L_T(\theta) = L_T(\theta; y_1, \dots, y_T) = \prod_{t=1}^{T} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi h_t}} \exp\left(-\frac{\tilde{\varepsilon}_t^{2}}{2h_t}\right), \tag{123.14}$$
