The parameters used for pricing are

$$\Theta_x^{\mathbb{Q}} = (\kappa_0^{\mathbb{Q}}, \kappa_1^{\mathbb{Q}}, \Sigma_x, \rho_0, \rho_1).$$

Then,

$$P_x(x,\tau)=P(x,\tau)*(A(\tau)+B(\tau)^T x)$$ 
$$\left\{\frac{P_\tau(x,t)}{P(x,\tau)}=A'(\tau)+B'(\tau)^T x\right.$$ 
$$P_x(x,\tau)=P(x,\tau)*B(\tau)$$ 
$$\left\{\frac{P_x(x,t)}{P(x,\tau)}=B(\tau)\right.$$ 
$$P_{xx}(x,\tau)=P(x,\tau)*B(\tau)*B(\tau)^T$$ 
$$\left\{\frac{P_{xx}(x,t)}{P(x,\tau)}=B(\tau)B(\tau)^T.\right.$$

So, for process $dx_t = \mu_x(x_t)dt + \sigma_x(x_t)^T dW_t$

$$\frac{dP}{P}=\mu_x^T \frac{P_x(x,t)}{P(x,\tau)}+\frac{1}{2}tr\left[\frac{P_{xx}(x,t)}{P(x,\tau)}\sigma_x^T\sigma_x\right]-\frac{P_\tau(x,t)}{P(x,\tau)}$$ 
$$=R(x)\quad\text{(absence of arbitrage)}.$$

Plugging in the aforementioned derivatives into this expression,

$$\mu_x^T B(\tau)+\frac{1}{2}[B(\tau)^T\sigma_x\sigma_x^T B(\tau)]-A'(\tau)-B'(\tau)^T x=R(x).$$

For the JSZ normalization of the Gaussian dynamic term structure model, let $H_0=\sigma_x^T\sigma_x$ and $R(x)=\rho_0+\rho_1 x$ and $\mu_x=\kappa_0^{Q^T}+\kappa_1^{Q^T}x$, then

$$(\kappa_0^{Q^T}+\kappa_1^{Q^T}x)B(\tau)+\frac{1}{2}[B(\tau)^T H_0 B(\tau)]-A'(\tau)-B'(\tau)^T x=\rho_0+\rho_1 x.$$

Collecting terms in $x$ as well as the constant term and recognizing that both must identically be zero,

$$\kappa_0^{Q^T} B(\tau)+\frac{1}{2}[B(\tau)^T H_0 B(\tau)]-A'(\tau)-\rho_0=0$$

$$(\kappa_1^{\mathbb{Q}^T} B(\tau))x-\rho_1 x-B'(\tau)^T x=0.$$ 

Thus,

$$A'(\tau)=\kappa_0^{Q^T} B(\tau)+\frac{1}{2}[B(\tau)^T H_0 B(\tau)]-\rho_0.$$ 

Equivalently, for discrete-time and without loss of generality for time period $m$, 

$$A_{m+1}-A_m=\kappa_0^{Q^T} B_m+\frac{1}{2}[B_m^T H_0 B_m]-\rho_0.$$ 

And,

$$B_{m+1}-B_m=\kappa_1^{Q^T} B_m-\rho_1.$$
