Interestingly, the correlations between social media, bank relationships and bank debt ratios are mostly negative but insignificant. Among them, the correlation between Facebook and banking relationships is the highest at −0.057. These results imply that social media and bank relationships have a potential substitution effect on reducing the information asymmetry problem.

Each social media also correlates with each other. The correlation between YouTube and Facebook is the highest at 0.57, followed by Twitter and YouTube, at 0.382, and by Twitter and Facebook, at 0.303.
