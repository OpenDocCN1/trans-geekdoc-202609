LLP_{i,t} = INTINC_{i,t} + DNPL_{i,t} + NPL_{i,t-1} + CAPRATIO_{i,t-1}
+ Assets_{i,t-1} + Concentration_{i,t}

The dependent variable, LLP_{i,t} is the loan loss provision for year t, the year of the call report scaled by lagged total loans. This variable represents the intuitional assessment concerning the riskiness of the loan portfolio of the firm. As this value increases there is the view that the risk tolerance of the bank has been elevated. A smaller provision reflects the opposite risk awareness by bank management. Prior research has identified (Bushman and Williams, 2012, 2015; Dahl, 2013; Dou et al., 2018; Jin et al., 2018; Morris et al., 2016; Ng and Roychowdhury, 2014) relationships between management control and the loan loss provision. INTINC_{i,t} is net interest income divided by lagged total assets of the bank. It is presumed a lagged relationship between some management controls and loan loss expense (Marton and Runesson, 2017; Nicoletti, 2018). DNPL_{i,t} is the change in non-performing loans between the ends of the lagged and current years, scaled by lagged total loans. The volume of non-performing loans, at the end of the prior period (NPL_{i,t-1}) also scaled by lagged total loans, are also included in the model formation. CAPRATIO_{i,t-1} is equity capital in year t divided by lagged total assets. ASSETS_{i,t-1} is the natural logarithm of total assets from the lagged year t − 1. Loan concentration is a key driver of a bank’s overall portfolio’s credit risk, with larger values indicative of greater overall risk as risk is increasingly concentrated in one industry instead of being relatively diversified across multiple loan categories. To account for management perception of loan portfolio risk, CONCENTRATION_{i,t} is a measure of loan concentration between consumer and personal real estate loans within each bank in year t.

A variable of interest is DF_t, and its associated cross terms. These detect and measure the statistical significance of a change in regulatory treatment on loan loss provisions. The DF_t variable takes a value of one for years 2011 and later and serves to introduce an observed shift in the average loan loss provision expense-to-lagged total loans ratio.
