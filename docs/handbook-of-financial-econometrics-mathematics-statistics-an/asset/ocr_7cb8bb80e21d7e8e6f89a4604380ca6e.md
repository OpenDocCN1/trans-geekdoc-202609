# 55.5 Conclusions

We examine the time-varying performance of investment strategies — the long and short portfolios — constructed following UK sell-side analyst recommendation revisions over the period January 1997 to June 2013. We find that the abnormal returns to the long and short portfolios are not statistically significant at the conventional level in any period of time. Importantly, we develop a time-series bootstrapping simulation method confirming that the observed statistically insignificant abnormal returns to the long and short portfolios are due to sell-side analysts’ lack of skill in making valuable stock recommendation revisions, rather than their bad luck.
