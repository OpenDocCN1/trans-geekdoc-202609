# 88.7 Summary

The management of a fixed-income portfolio involves techniques and strategies that are unique to the specific area of bonds. This chapter has discussed riding the yield curve, swaps, and duration as three techniques that are familiar to all managers of fixed-income portfolios. In this chapter, we also discussed how the basic concepts of duration and maturity can be used in portfolio management, especially the issue of convexity in bond-portfolio management has been analyzed in detail. A comparison of these techniques was also presented as a case study. Overall, this chapter has related bond-valuation theory to bond-portfolio theory and has developed bond-portfolio management strategies. In Appendix 88A, we have explicitly shown how convexity can be calculated.
