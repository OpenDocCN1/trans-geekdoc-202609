In addition, the data reveals that except for accounting standards with income-decreasing effects, banks are more likely to choose early adoption during economic expansions than during contractions regardless of the income effects of accounting standards. Furthermore, the data shows that banks are most likely to adopt early accounting standards with income-increasing effects during economic expansions (20.3%) and accounting standards with only disclosure requirements during contractions (2.4%). In general, the table is consistent with the assertion that banks are most motivated during economic expansions to disclose information voluntarily to better access external financing.
