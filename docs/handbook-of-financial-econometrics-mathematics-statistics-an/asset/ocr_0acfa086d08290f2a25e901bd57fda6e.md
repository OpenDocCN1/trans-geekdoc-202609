According to Kass and Raftery (1995), the null hypothesis H0 is rejected “not worthy more than a bare mention” as the Bayes factor B10 > 1, H0 is rejected “substantially” as the Bayes factor B10 > 3.2, and H0 is rejected “strongly” as the Bayes factor B10 > 10. Here, a threshold h* is chosen so that the null hypothesis H0: ρ = 1 is rejected as the Bayes factor B10 of (93.8) is greater than h*. The threshold h* is chosen so that the likelihood of rejecting the null hypothesis H0: ρ = 1 is identical to that under the conditional F-test when the ex post difference S\hat{R}* - S\hat{R} ≤ 0.4. The threshold h* are 12 and 18 for the first and second time periods, respectively. Similarly, a threshold h** is chosen so that the null hypothesis H0: ρ = 1 is rejected as the Bayes factor B10 of (93.3) is greater than h**. The threshold h** are 2 and 1 for the first and second time periods, respectively.
