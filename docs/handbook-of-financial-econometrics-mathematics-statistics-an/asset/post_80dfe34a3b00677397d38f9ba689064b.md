## 61.3.1 Determination of structural breaks over the sample period

As a main purpose of this chapter is to evaluate the performance of PCDTSM in economic environments with low interest rates relative to the other environments, we begin by prefacing that we are interested in focusing on the performance of the PCDTSM discussed above in near zero interest rate environments. We begin our examination by searching for breaks in model performance of PCDTSM by searching for structural breaks in the relation between the actual yield and the lagged in-sample forecasted yield implied by each PCDTSM covered in Section 61.2 as well as for the actual data. In the examination of this relationship for each PCDTSM, we are interested in the determination of structural changes in the pattern of each model's ability to provide an in-sample fit to the actual data. In the manner in which we have structured the Chow test, its application enables us to search for structural changes in model performance by comparing the error, computed as the difference between the actual yield and the lagged fitted yield, produced in each sub-period. Thus, in employing the Chow test to compare model performance in this manner, we searched for changes in the pattern of the model's ability to fit the data. The plots in Figure 61.1 of each PCDTSM, as well as DRA and the actual data, provide a remarkably similar fit to the actual data and suggest four possible naturally occurring structural breaks in the pattern of the in-sample fitted yields for each PCDTSM and the actual data. The first one seems to be around July 2003 for each data series. The second one seems to be around June 2006 for each data series. The third one seems to be around November 2008 and the final one seems to be around August 2012.
