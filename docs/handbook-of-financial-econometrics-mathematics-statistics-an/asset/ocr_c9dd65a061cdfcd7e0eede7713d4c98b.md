# 10.4.7 GRS test of intra-industry securities

One assumption of the average F-test is that idiosyncratic returns across securities are uncorrelated resulting in block diagonal variance-covariance matrix of residuals, with the off-diagonal elements being zero. We now relax this assumption to make allowance for residuals of securities within an industry or sector to be cross-sectionally dependent. However, firms in different sectors or industries are still assumed to have uncorrelated idiosyncratic returns. To accomplish this, we employ GRS test that, unlike the average F-test, takes into account the variance-covariance matrix of residuals while calculating the t-statistic.
