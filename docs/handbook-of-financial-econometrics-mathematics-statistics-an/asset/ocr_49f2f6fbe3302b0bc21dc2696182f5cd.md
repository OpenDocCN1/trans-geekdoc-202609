The regulation reforms were caused by the Parmalat scandal, one of largest corporate scandals in Europe, and were not driven by trends in small and medium-size enterprises. The reorganization reform (Legislative Decree no. 35 of 2005) introduces a few provisions to facilitate the renegotiation of outstanding loans and to protect debtors, which gives the borrowers a strong bargaining position.
