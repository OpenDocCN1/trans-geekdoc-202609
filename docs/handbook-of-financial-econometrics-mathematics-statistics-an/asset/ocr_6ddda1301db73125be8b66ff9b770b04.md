# 52.5 Matching Events and Estimation Results

The historical price of the Thai baht can be interpreted as a result of the interactions between the Thai government, market speculators, and other parties in the foreign exchange market. While the players in the foreign exchange market obviously have different goals, our estimation results can be used to gain a better understanding of how different market players affect the behavior of the Thai baht. We use the chronology of the Asian financial crisis composed by Nourial Roubini¹ and a summary of Thailand’s currency crisis created by Ito (1999) as the major sources for descriptions of events. Since SVIJ and SVCJ have similar results and SVIJ is a better explanatory model, we will focus on the results of SVIJ in this section.
