two CTE's, $C_{\alpha}^{1,T}$ and $C_{\alpha}^{2,T}$, being equal, that is,

$$
\left\{
\begin{array}{l}
H_0 : C_{\alpha}^{1,T} = C_{\alpha}^{2,T}, \\
H_1 : C_{\alpha}^{1,T} \neq C_{\alpha}^{2,T}.
\end{array}
\right.
\tag{72.9}
$$

As shown in HCT-2, if the mean of the weighted portfolio return is unknown, and if $N$ and $T$ are such that $T \ll N \ll T^2$, then, as $T$ tends to infinity

$$
\sqrt{N/T}\left(\hat{C}_{\alpha}^{i,T} - C_{\alpha}^{i,T}\right) \xrightarrow{d} N(0,\sigma_i^2), \quad \text{for } i = 1 \text{ and } 2,
\tag{72.10}
$$

where, for $i = 1$ and $2$, $\hat{C}_{\alpha}^{i,T}$ is the estimate of $C_{\alpha}^{i,T}$ as defined in equation (72.7), $\sigma_1^2$ and $\sigma_2^2$ is the variance of the weighted portfolio return $\{r_t\}$ and $\{s_t\}$, respectively. To form a size-$\alpha$ test for the null hypothesis specified in (72.9) using equation (72.10), we construct the test statistic as

$$
\text{Test} = \sqrt{\frac{N}{T}} \frac{\hat{C}_{\alpha}^{1,T} - \hat{C}_{\alpha}^{2,T}}{\sqrt{\hat{\sigma}_{1,n}^2 + \hat{\sigma}_{2,n}^2}},
\tag{72.11}
$$

and reject $H_0$ if $|\text{Test}| > Z_{1-\alpha/2}$, where $\hat{\sigma}_{1,n}^2$ and $\hat{\sigma}_{2,n}^2$ are the sample variance of $\{r_t\}$ and $\{s_t\}$, respectively.

The size and the power of the test are studied in Sections 72.4.1 and 72.4.2, respectively. For simplicity, we only consider $m = 1$. First, we simulate $\{r_t\}_{t=1,\dots,n}$ from

$$
r_t = \mu_1 + V_{1,t}U_{1,t}, \quad V_{1,t} = \bar{\sigma}_1\exp(Z_{1,t}/2),
\tag{72.12}
$$

and independently simulate $\{s_t\}_{t=1,\dots,n}$ from

$$
s_t = \mu_2 + V_{s,t}U_{2,t}, \quad V_{2,t} = \bar{\sigma}_2\exp(Z_{2,t}/2),
\tag{72.13}
$$
