z = (1 × 5 × 8 + 3 × 7 × 2 + 5 × 3 × 4 − 5 × 5 × 2 − 3 × 3 × 8 − 1 × 7 × 4) / (1 × 5 × 3 + 3 × 6 × 2 + (−2) × 3 × 4 − (−2) × 5 × 2 − 3 × 3 × 3 − 1 × 6 × 4) = (40 + 42 + 60 − 50 − 72 − 28) / (15 + 36 − 24 + 20 − 27 − 24) = −8 / −4 = 2 (77A.13)

# Method 3: Matrix method

Using the example above, we can derive the following matrix equation:

[x y z]^T = [[1 3 -2],[3 5 6],[2 4 3]]^{-1} × [5 7 8]^T . (77A.14)

The inversion of matrix A is, by definition,

A^{-1} = 1 / det A * (Adj A). (77A.15)

Adjoint A is defined by the transpose of the cofactor matrix. First, we need to calculate the cofactor matrix of A. Suppose the cofactor matrix is

cofactor matrix = [[A11 A12 A13],[A21 A22 A23],[A31 A32 A33]],

A11 = |5 6;4 3| = −9,   A12 = −|3 6;2 3| = 3,   A13 = −|3 5;2 4| = 2,
A21 = −|3 -2;4 3| = −17,   A22 = −|1 -2;2 3| = 7,   A23 = −|1 3;2 4| = 2,
A31 = |3 -2;5 6| = 28,   A32 = −|1 -2;3 6| = 12,   A33 = |1 3;3 5| = −4,

therefore,

cofactor matrix = [[−9 3 2],[−17 7 2],[28 −12 −4]]. (77A.16)
