Figure 36.10 shows the degree of similarity and phase relations between the Hong Kong and Singapore stock market returns.

Like the France and Germany stock markets, Figure 36.10 shows a strong co-movement between Hong Kong and Singapore at low frequencies since the beginning of the investigated period and until the end of the period under study and consequently these stock markets are strongly integrated in the long-term investment horizons. This finding implies that an occurrence of crisis at the Hong Kong stock market is likely to be transferred to the Singapore market through the fundamental relationships. This can be verified by examining the medium frequencies. We notice that since the beginning of 1994 until the end of the sample, some strong linkage appeared at medium frequencies, which indicates that the process of contagion began with the 1994 Mexican crisis.
