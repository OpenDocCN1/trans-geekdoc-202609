# 37.2 Effects of Errors-in-Variables in Different Cases

# 37.2.1 Bivariate normal case

Suppose we have a two variate structural relationship

$$V_i = \alpha + \beta U_i.\tag{37.1}$$

Both $V_i$ and $U_i$ are unobserved, while we can observe $Y_i = V_i + \eta_i$ and $X_i = U_i + \varepsilon_i$. We assume that

+   (a) $\varepsilon_i \sim N(0, \sigma_1^2)$ and $\eta_i \sim N(0, \sigma_2^2)$.
+   (b) $E(\varepsilon_i U_i)=0$, $E(\varepsilon_i V_i)=0$, $E(\varepsilon_i \eta_i)=0$, $E(\eta_i U_i)=0$, and $E(\eta_i V_i)=0$.
+   (c) $U_i \sim N(E(X), \sigma_U^2)$ and $V_i \sim N(\alpha + \beta E(X), \beta^2 \sigma_U^2)$.

The effects of measurement error on the estimates of $\alpha$ and $\beta$ can be seen as follows:

+   (a) $\operatorname{plim}\hat{\beta}= \dfrac{\beta \sigma_U^2}{\sigma_U^2 + \sigma_1^2}$,  (37.2a)
+   (b) $\operatorname{plim}\hat{\alpha}= E(Y) - \dfrac{\beta \sigma_U^2}{\sigma_U^2 + \sigma_1^2} E(X)$.  (37.2b)

This implies that the asymptotic biases for $\beta$ and $\alpha$ are

+   (a) $\operatorname{plim}\hat{\beta} - \beta = \dfrac{-\beta \sigma_1^2}{\sigma_U^2 + \sigma_1^2}$,  (37.3a)
+   (b) $\operatorname{plim}\hat{\alpha} - \alpha = \dfrac{\beta \sigma_1^2}{\sigma_U^2 + \sigma_1^2} E(X)$.  (37.3b)
