# (c) Concluding Remarks

In general the model developed by Spies performs quite well. The evidence strongly supports a complete partial-adjustment model. The individual adjustment coefficients are reasonable for most industries, and the systems are all stable. If the capital budget is out of equilibrium, dividends and long-term investment move steadily toward their equilibrium values.
