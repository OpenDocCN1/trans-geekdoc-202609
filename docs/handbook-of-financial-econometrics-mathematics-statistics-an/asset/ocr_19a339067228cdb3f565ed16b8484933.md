# Figure 13.2: Time-series of international diversification benefits.

Notes: The figure shows time-series of benefits of international diversification under short-sales (SS) and various sets of short-sale plus diversity (SS+DV(U)) investment to US investors. The international diversification benefits for US investors are assessed by an increase in Sharpe ratio of investing the MSR portfolio (δ_SS + DV(U) = MSR_SS + DV(U) − SR_Countryx), a decrease in volatility of investing the MVP (ε_US + DV(U) = σ_MVR(SS + DV(U))), and an increase in Sharpe ratio by realizing the MSR portfolio ψ = SR_RMSR − SR_RMVW. The optimal portfolios are constructed using data gained over the previous 60 months under various investment constraints. The long-term trend is illustrated and smoothed by the filter purposed by Hodrick and Prescott (1997) for each time-series (H-P(.)) .
