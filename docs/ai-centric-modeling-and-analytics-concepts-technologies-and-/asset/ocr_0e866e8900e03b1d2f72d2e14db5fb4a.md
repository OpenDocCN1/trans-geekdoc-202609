# 8.3.2.4 Correlation Matrix with Heat Map

A correlation matrix is implied, and Pearson correlation is the most commonly used. First, a Pearson correlation heat map was used to visualize the relationship between the independent factors and the output variable diagnosis.

Sklearn was used to generate these charts (Scikit-Learn Developers, 2022). As shown in the heat map in Figure 8.4, area worst was significantly correlated (more than 0.9) with independent variables (radius mean, perimeter mean, area mean, radius worst, and perimeter worst). The worst area was chosen, and all other highly correlated features in the matrix were dropped, as shown in Figure 8.4.
