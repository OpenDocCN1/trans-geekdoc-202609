Vectors $x$ and $y$ are placed closer as opposed to $y$ and $z$ or $z$ and $x$. Mathematically, the same can be verified by applying the formula for the Euclidian distance, which is given by Equation 1.4:

$d(a,b)=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(a_i-b_i)^2}\tag{1.4}$

When applied to vectors $x$ and $y$ as Equations 1.5 and 1.6

$d(x,y)=\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(x_1-y_1)^2+(x_2-y_2)^2+(x_3-y_3)^2}\tag{1.5}$

$= 0.031$

# 6.4.2 Dot Product

The Euclidian distance concept is magnitude oriented; on the other hand, the concept of a dot product considers both magnitude and orientation.

But why consider orientation at all if magnitude works fine? This is because vector magnitude is the indicator of word frequency.
