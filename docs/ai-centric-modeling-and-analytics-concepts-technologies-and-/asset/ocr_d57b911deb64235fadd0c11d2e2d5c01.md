# # Lesson 87

In the morning, the teacher asked the students to open their books to the story of the farmer and his sons. The students read quietly while the teacher walked around the room, helping those who had questions.

After they finished reading, the teacher explained the main idea of the passage. She pointed out that the lesson in the story was about working together and sharing responsibilities. The students listened carefully and wrote down notes in their journals.

At the end of the class, the teacher asked a few students to share what they had learned. Some said that the story reminded them of helping at home, while others mentioned how important it was to be patient and kind. Everyone agreed the lesson was valuable.
