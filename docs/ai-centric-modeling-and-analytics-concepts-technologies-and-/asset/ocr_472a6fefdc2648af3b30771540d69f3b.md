# 7.5  EXISTENCE OF EQUILIBRIUM POINTS OF THE SYSTEM OF EQUATIONS

In this section, the existence of all possible equilibrium points of system (1) is discussed. There are two equilibrium points:

+   1. COVID-19-free equilibrium point
+   2. Endemic equilibrium point

For the steady-state condition, the system of equation 7.15 becomes

```
$
\begin{align*}
A-\beta S I-d_{i} S & =0 \\
\beta S I-\left(d_{1}+\mu\right) E & =0 \\
\mu E-\left(d_{1}+d_{2}+\alpha\right) I & =0 \\
\alpha I-\left(d_{1}+d_{2}+\delta\right) Q & =0 \\
\delta Q-d_{i} R & =0
\end{align*} \\
(7.15)
```

# 7.5.1  CORONAVIRUS-FREE EQUILIBRIUM POINT

For the COVID-19-free equilibrium $\Omega_{0}\left(\frac{A}{d_{1}}, 0,0,0,0\right)$ when $\left(E=I=Q=R=0\right)\cdot$

# Theorem 1

If $R_{0}<1$, then the COVID-19-free equilibrium is locally asymptotically stable. Otherwise, it is unstable.

Proof: Linearization of the model in Equation 7.16.

```
$
\begin{equation}
J\left(S_{0}, 0,0,0,0\right)=\left(\begin{array}{ccccc}
-d_{1} & 0 & -\beta S_{0} & 0 & 0 \\
0 & -\left(d_{1}+\mu\right) & \beta S_{0} & 0 & 0 \\
0 & \mu & -\left(d_{1}+d_{2}+\alpha\right) & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & -\left(d_{1}+d_{2}+\delta\right) & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & \delta & -d_{i}
\end{array}\right)
\end{equation} \\
(7.16)
```

By taking the eigenvalues

```
$
\begin{align*}
\lambda & =-d_{1} \\
\lambda & =-d_{1} \\
\lambda & =-\left(d_{1}+d_{2}+\delta\right)
\end{align*}
```

Two other eigenvalues can be of the form of Equation 7.17:

```
$
\begin{gathered}
\lambda^{2}+a \lambda+b=0 \\
\text { where } \\
a=2 d_{1}+d_{2}+\mu+\alpha>0 \\
b=\left(d_{1}+\mu\right)\left(d_{1}+d_{2}+\alpha\right)-\mu \beta S_{0}=\left(d_{1}+\mu\right)\left(d_{1}+d_{2}+\alpha\right)\left(1-R_{0}\right)>0
\end{gathered} \\
(7.17)
```
