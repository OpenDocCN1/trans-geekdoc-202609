# 15.4.5.1 Conceptual Structure of Knowledge

Aria & Cuccurullo (2020) discovered that conceptual structure investigates the key ideas and issues, and illustrates the connections between research topics. The conceptual structure of the current study is mapped using two methods, namely, co-words network and factor analysis.

The multiple correspondence analysis (MCA) technique is used to analyze the factor of the author’s keywords to reduce the dimensionality of the data. This technique is beneficial in analyzing various relationships of the categorical dependent variables, also known as an extension of correspondence analysis (Abdi & Valentin, 2007).
