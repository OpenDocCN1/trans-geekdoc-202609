# 9.1.3 Internet of Medical Things

An interconnected system of devices, known as the “Internet of Medical Things” (IoMT), offers medical services. As shown in Figure 9.2, IoMT is essentially a networked infrastructure of medical equipment, software programs, and services.

Healthcare organizations can increase the efficiency of their clinical procedures and workflow management, as well as remote patient health monitoring, by connecting equipment and sensors.

The Internet of Things (IoT) connects the digital and physical worlds to improve patient health by accelerating diagnosis and treatment, and modifying patient behavior and health status in real time.
