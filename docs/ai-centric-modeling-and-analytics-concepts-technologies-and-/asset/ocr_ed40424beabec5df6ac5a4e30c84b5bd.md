# 10.5 CONCLUSION

In this chapter, BH-ANN is suggested for use in flexible industrial sectors with robotics in real-time applications. With the robotic aspect as a guideline for future smart factories, the new cybersecurity scheme comprises neural networks that communicate with other aspects in a completely integrated system.

An industrial context dataset was collected to illustrate a manufacturing assembly process employing human–robot cooperation to determine the viability of the proposed system.

The feature extraction phase of this study uncovered the true difficulties that pose a severe threat to robotics. To demonstrate the usefulness of the suggested strategy, its aspects are presented.

A security-compromised physical asset may result in a severe safety hazard in automation, owing to the increased connection, as shown by the simulation.

Future industrial processes, including human operators and robots, will be created within the framework of revolution (Khang, 2024).
