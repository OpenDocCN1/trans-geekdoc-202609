Seven feature extraction networks, namely VGG16, AlexNet, GoogLeNet, DenseNet, inceptionV3, ResNet101, and ResNet101-SE, were combined with LSTM model and used to diagnose the N and K nutrition levels of HHZ at EPIS. The diagnostic results are shown in Table 3 and Fig. 5. AlexNet presented the worst performance of the 16 nutrient level diagnoses, with an accuracy of 73.94%, and its Area Under Curve (AUC) was the lowest, at 0.76. ResNet101 had relatively good performance, with an accuracy of 83.81% and a corresponding AUC of 0.90. After adding the SE module based on the channel domain to ResNet101, the precision and recall of ResNet101-SE increased by 4.94% and 2.45% respectively, and the AUC increased to 0.93, which presented the best performance among all the tested CNN feature networks. Therefore, ResNet101-SE was used to extract the rice features.

The ResNet101-SE-LSTM model presents good performance for diagnosing the N and K levels at INS and EPIS. Plants have different responses to nutrient stress in different growth stages, and their phenotypic characteristics are dynamic. Therefore, a single growth stage cannot reflect the overall growth state of plants. In addition, determining the optimal growth stage for trophic level diagnosis or developing separate models for different growth stages is a difficult task and may not necessarily provide reliable results for making fertilization decisions.
