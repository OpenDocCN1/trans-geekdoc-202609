# 5 Conclusion

In this study, a dynamic model of deep learning based on ResNet101 combined with the SE module and LSTM (ResNet101-SE-LSTM) was proposed to explore the feasibility of using rice canopy images collected by UAV to identify nutrient levels in 2021 and 2022. Seven convolutional neural networks were trained to identify the N and K nutrient levels. The results showed that the ResNet101-SE model can effectively extract image features from the spatial and temporal domains for the recognition of rice nutrient levels. This study also shows the importance of adding time domain extraction to a single dynamic model for diagnosing nutrition levels. In our experiment, the ResNet101-SE-LSTM model achieved the highest overall accuracies of 85.88% and 88.38% for HHZ and XS134, respectively, in 2021. Our model also showed relatively good generalization ability, with the overall accuracies of 81.25% and 82.50% for HHZ and XS134, respectively, in 2022. Compared with traditional methods involving the destructive detection of rice nutritional status, our method provides a way to identify the rice nutrient levels in EPIS, which can provide field workers with real-time data and references during rice fertilization in order to increase rice yields.
