# 2 Materials and methods

Figure 1 shows the flowchart of our experiments. The rice canopy images collected by an
RGB camera installed in the UAV were used to train different models for rice early nutrition
diagnosis. At the same time, the rice physiological data were collected to train two machine
learning models for diagnosing rice nutrient levels. Finally, these models were compared to verify
the performance of the hybrid CNN-LSTM model.

# 2.1 Experiment design

The experiments were carried out in the rice field (30°04′N ,119°55′E) of the China National
Rice Research Institute (CNRRI) in Hangzhou, Zhejiang Province, China, in 2021 and 2022. The
physicochemical properties of soil were as follows: soil alkaline hydrolyzable nitrogen content
115.37 mg kg-1, available phosphorus (P) 14.20 mg kg-1, available potassium 140.19 mg kg-1,
organic matter content 30.48 g kg-1, cation exchange capacity 18.37 mg kg-1 and pH 5.83. An
overview of the experimental fields is shown in Fig. 2.

The two tested rice cultivars were Huanghuazhan (HHZ, a variety of conventional indica rice)
and Xiushui (XS134, a variety of conventional japonica rice). The rice planting method adopted a
split-split-plot randomized block design, with rice varieties as the main plot, N fertilizer level as the
split plot, and K fertilizer levels as subplots; so the two varieties of rice were treated with 16
combinations of four different N and K dosages (Table 1). Three replicate blocks were set up to
form 96 plots. In both experiments, N fertilizer was applied once 2-3 days before transplanting and
once during the rice tillering stage, with a dosage ratio of 5:3, and K fertilizer was applied once 2-3
days before transplanting. P fertilizer was applied at a dosage of 97.5 kg P2O5/ha to all blocks.
