Each chemical or material included in an LCIA database has a separate CF for each environmental impact category in the database. The impact calculation method is shown in Eqs. (8.1) and 8.2, where c denotes a particular emitted compound (CO₂, etc.), and i denotes a particular impact category (global warming, etc.).

```
Impact_{c,i} = mass_c * CF_{c,i} (9.1)
```

```
Total_{impact_i} = Σ_{c} Impact_{c,i} (9.2)
```

As shown in Eq. (8.1), the impact of a particular compound (c) in a particular impact category (i) is the product of the mass of c emitted and the CF corresponding to c and i. The total impact in category i is the sum of all the compound-specific impacts within that category (Eq. (8.2)).
