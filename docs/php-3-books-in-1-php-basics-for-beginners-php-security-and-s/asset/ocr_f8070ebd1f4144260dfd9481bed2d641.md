# Session and Cookies

Data in a session is stored on the server, where sessions are saved in files, cookies are sent to the user’s browser, and they can be read by browsers. Cookies expire, and session data is lost when a browser is closed.

For example, if you open your web browser to visit Google Search and then close it before you visit any other pages or sites from that search bar, all information that Google collected about your first search will be deleted because of the expiration time for cookies. That also means that if you close your browser and then open another one, you will have to perform the search again, and Google will not save previous results from previous searches.

PHP, as a server-side programming language, uses cookies to store information about users in their browsers. Cookies can be used to store user preferences and other information specific to your site.

After you type in the URL for your site, click the Go! button, and then click any link on your page (it doesn’t matter which link), you will see that a new cookie is set on your computer.
