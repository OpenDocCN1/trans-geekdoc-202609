
### Exercises

1.  Let the estimate of $Z_n = (2\pi)^{\frac{n}{2}}$ be
    $$
    \widehat{Z}_n = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} w(\mathbf{x}_{1:n}^{(i)}).
    $$

2.  Show that the variance of the corresponding estimator is given by
    $$
    \text{var}\left[ \widehat{Z}_n \right] = \frac{Z_n^2}{N} \left( \left( \frac{\sigma^4}{2\sigma^2 - 1} \right)^{\frac{n}{2}} - 1 \right).
    $$

3.  Observe that for $\sigma^2 > 1/2$, which is the range of values for the above formula to make sense and guarantee a finite value for the variance, the variance exhibits an exponential increase with respect to $n$. To keep the variance small, one has to make $N$ very large, that is, generate a very large number of particles [16].

4.  **17.6** Prove that the use of the optimal proposal distribution in particle filtering leads to
    $$
    w_n(\mathbf{x}_{1:n}) = w_{n-1}(\mathbf{x}_{1:n-1}) p(\mathbf{y}_n | \mathbf{x}_{n-1}).
    $$
