
### On the optimality of the solution

The first question that is now raised concerns the optimal solution associated with the minmax optimization task in Eq. (19.11). Note that the generator `G` implicitly defines a probability distribution, `p_g(x)`, of the samples `x = G(z; θ_g)` that are generated if `z ∼ p_z(z)`.

For our analysis, we will free ourselves from the parametric modeling of the involved functions via `θ_g` and `θ_d`, and we will study the optimal solution in terms of functions `D(x)` and `G(z)`, considered in a *general* nonparametric formulation. Under such a scenario, Eq. (19.11) is rephrased as
