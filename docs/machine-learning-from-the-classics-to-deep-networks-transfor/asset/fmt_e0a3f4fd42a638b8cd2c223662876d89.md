
$J(\mathbf{\varphi}) = \mathbb{E} \left[ \middle| y_{n} - \mathbf{\varphi}^{H}{\widetilde{\mathbf{x}}}_{n}|^{2} \right],$

and following standard arguments analogous to Section [5.3.1](#Chp005_177-248_B9780443292385000111.xhtml#s0030), the minimum with respect to `φ` is given by the root of

### The widely linear LMS

Employing the Robbins–Monro scheme and fixing the value of `μ[n]` to be a constant, we obtain

$\mathbf{\varphi}_{n} = \mathbf{\varphi}_{n - 1} + \mu{\widetilde{\mathbf{x}}}_{n}e_{n}^{\ast},e_{n} = y_{n} - \mathbf{\varphi}_{n - 1}^{H}{\widetilde{\mathbf{x}}}_{n}.$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si318.png)

Breaking `φ[n]` and ${\widetilde{\mathbf{x}}}_{n}$ into their components, the widely linear LMS results.
