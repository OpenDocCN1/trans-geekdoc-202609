
### The LS estimator achieves the Cramér–Rao bound for white Gaussian noise

The concept of the Cramér–Rao lower bound was introduced in Chapter [3](#Chp003_65-117_B9780443292385000093.xhtml). There, it was shown that, under the *white Gaussian noise* assumption, the LS estimator of a real number was *efficient*; that is, it achieves the Cramér–Rao bound. Moreover, in Problem [3.9](#Chp003_65-117_B9780443292385000093.xhtml), it was shown that if **η** is zero mean Gaussian noise with covariance matrix `Σ[η]`![Equation](images/B9780443292385000123/si69.png), then the efficient estimator is given by

```
\widehat{\mathbf{\theta}} = {(X^{T}\Sigma_{\eta}^{- 1}X)}^{- 1}X^{T}\Sigma_{\eta}^{- 1}\mathbf{y},
```
