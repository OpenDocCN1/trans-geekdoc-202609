

$$
\{\hat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_i\}_{i=1}^l = \arg\min_{\{\boldsymbol{\theta}_i\}_{i=1}^l} J(\boldsymbol{\theta}_1,\ldots,\boldsymbol{\theta}_l)
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si507.png)

其中

$$
J(\boldsymbol{\theta}_1,\ldots,\boldsymbol{\theta}_l) := \frac{1}{2}\left\| \boldsymbol{y} - \sum_{i=1}^l \mathcal{K}_i\boldsymbol{\theta}_i \right\|^2 + \lambda\sum_{i=1}^l \sqrt{\boldsymbol{\theta}_i^T\mathcal{K}_i\boldsymbol{\theta}_i},
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si508.png) (11.99)

且 $\mathcal{K}_i$，$i=1,2,\ldots,l$ 为相应的 $N \times N$ 核矩阵

$$
\mathcal{K}_i := \begin{bmatrix}
\kappa_i(x_{i,1},x_{i,1}) & \cdots & \kappa_i(x_{i,1},x_{i,N}) \\
\vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
\kappa_i(x_{i,N},x_{i,1}) & \cdots & \kappa_i(x_{i,N},x_{i,N})
\end{bmatrix}.
$$
