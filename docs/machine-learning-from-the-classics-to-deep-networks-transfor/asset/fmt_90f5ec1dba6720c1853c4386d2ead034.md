The lower bound functional ℱ(*q*[𝒳^(*l*)], *q*[**θ**], **ξ**) depends on three terms. Following the alternating optimization rationale, in every iteration step we will freeze two of them and maximization will be performed with respect to the remaining one, in an alternating fashion. Optimizing the lower bound functional with respect to a distribution will take place by setting to zero a corresponding and appropriately defined KL divergence.
