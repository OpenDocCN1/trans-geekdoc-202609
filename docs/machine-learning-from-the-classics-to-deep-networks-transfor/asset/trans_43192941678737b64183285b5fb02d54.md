

## 数学假设与推导

### 噪声序列的统计性质

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack \eta_{k}(n)\eta_{k}(n - r) \right\rbrack = \sigma_{k}^{2}\delta_{r},\qquad\delta_{r} = \begin{cases} 1, & r = 0, \\ 0, & r \neq 0, \end{cases}
$$

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack \eta_{k}(n)\eta_{m}(r) \right\rbrack = \sigma_{k}^{2}\delta_{km}\delta_{nr},\qquad\delta_{km},\delta_{nr} = \begin{cases} 1, & k = m,\ n = r, \\ 0, & \text{其他情况}. \end{cases}
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si540.png)

*   噪声序列也独立于输入向量，

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack \mathbf{x}_{m}(n)\eta_{k}(n-r) \right\rbrack = 0,\quad k,m = 1,2,\dots,K,\ \forall r
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si541.png)

*   最后，在输入向量之间应用独立性假设，包括空间和时间维度，即，

$$
\mathbb{E}\left\lbrack \mathbf{x}_{k}(n)\mathbf{x}_{m}^{T}(n-r) \right\rbrack = \mathbf{O},\quad \text{若 } k \neq m,\ \forall r
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si542.png)
