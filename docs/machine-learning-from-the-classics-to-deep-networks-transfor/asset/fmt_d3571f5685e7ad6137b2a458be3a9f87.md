
$$
\left\| \mathbf{x} - \left( \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{x}_{\ast ,1} + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{x}_{\ast ,2} \right) \right\|^{2} < {\|\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{x}_{\ast ,1}\|}^{2}.
$$

1. However, due to the convexity of *C*, the point $\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{x}_{\ast ,1} + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{x}_{\ast ,2}$ lies in *C*. Also, by the definition of projection, $\mathbf{x}_{\ast ,1}$ is the point with the smallest distance, and hence the inequality in [(8.10)](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#deq24) cannot be valid. □

For the existence, one has to use the property of closeness (every sequence in *C* has its limit in *C*) as well as the property of completeness of Hilbert spaces, which guarantees that every Cauchy sequence in $\mathbb{H}$ has a limit (see chapter's appendix). The proof is given in Problem [8.11](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#o0085).
