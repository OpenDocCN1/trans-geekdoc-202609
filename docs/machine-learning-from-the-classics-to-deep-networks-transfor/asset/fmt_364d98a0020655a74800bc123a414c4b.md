The goal is to obtain an estimate of the parameters `θ` in the Bayesian framework; thus, somehow, we have to "embed" `θ` into a PDF that relates the input–output data. In this vein, a well-known and widely used technique is the *logistic regression* model, which was introduced in Section [7.6](#Chp007_293-342_B9780443292385000135.xhtml).
