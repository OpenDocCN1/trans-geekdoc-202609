The term `P_C(x)` is called the (metric) projection of `x` onto *C*. Note that if `x ∈ C`, then `P_C(x) = x`, since this makes the norm `‖x - P_C(x)‖ = 0`.

### Proof

The proof comprises two paths. One is to establish *uniqueness* and the other to establish *existence*. Uniqueness is easier, and the proof will be given here. Existence is slightly more technical, and its proof is provided in Problem [8.11](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#o0085).

To show *uniqueness*, assume that there are two points, `x*_1` and `x*_2`, `x*_1 ≠ x*_2`, such that

```
‖x - x*_1‖ = ‖x - x*_2‖ = min_{y ∈ C} ‖x - y‖.
```
