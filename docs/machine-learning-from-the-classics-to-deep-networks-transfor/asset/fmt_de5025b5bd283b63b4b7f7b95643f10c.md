
$$
\widehat{d}_n = \sum_{m=0}^{l-1} h_m e_m^b(n)
$$
![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si344.png) (4.79)

where the coefficients `h[m]` are given by

$$
\begin{aligned}
h_m &= \langle \widehat{d}_n, \frac{e_m^b(n)}{\|e_m^b(n)\|^2} \rangle \\
&= \frac{\mathbb{E}[\widehat{d}_n e_m^{b*}(n)]}{\|e_m^b(n)\|^2} \\
&= \frac{\mathbb{E}[(d_n - e_n) e_m^{b*}(n)]}{\|e_m^b(n)\|^2} \\
&= \frac{\mathbb{E}[d_n e_m^{b*}(n)]}{\|e_m^b(n)\|^2},
\end{aligned}
$$
![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si346.png) (4.80)

where the orthogonality of the error `e[n]` with the subspace spanned by the backward errors has been taken into account. From Eqs. [(4.67)](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml#deq116) and [(4.80)](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml#deq138), and taking into account the respective definitions of the involved quantities, we readily obtain

`h[m] = k[m]^(w*)`.
