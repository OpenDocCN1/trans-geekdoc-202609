
## 数学推导

$$
X = \underset{l \times l}{\underbrace{[\mathbf{u}_{1},\ldots,\mathbf{u}_{l}]}}\underset{l \times N}{\underbrace{\begin{bmatrix} {\sqrt{\lambda}}_{1}\mathbf{v}_{1}^{T} \\ \vdots \\ {\sqrt{\lambda_{l}}}\mathbf{v}_{l}^{T} \\ \end{bmatrix}}} = \left\lbrack \mathbf{u}_{1},\ldots,\mathbf{u}_{l} \right\rbrack\begin{bmatrix} {\sqrt{\lambda}}_{1}v_{11} & \ldots & {\sqrt{\lambda}}_{1}v_{1n} & \ldots & {\sqrt{\lambda}}_{1}v_{1N} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ {\sqrt{\lambda}}_{l}v_{l1} & \ldots & {\sqrt{\lambda}}_{l}v_{ln} & \ldots & {\sqrt{\lambda}}_{l}v_{lN} \\ \end{bmatrix}.
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000263/si36.png) (20.8)

Thus, the columns of `X` can be written in terms of the following expansion:^([^(155)](#fn155))

$$
\mathbf{x}_{n} = \sum\limits_{i = 1}^{l}z_{ni}\mathbf{u}_{i} = \sum\limits_{i = 1}^{m}z_{ni}\mathbf{u}_{i} + \sum\limits_{i = m + 1}^{l}z_{ni}\mathbf{u}_{i},
$$
