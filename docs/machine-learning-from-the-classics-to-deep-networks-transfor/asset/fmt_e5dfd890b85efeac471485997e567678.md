Moreover, note that, similar to the self attention, the computations involved in the masked self-attention can, also, be carried out in parallel for all positions $n = 0, 1, \dots, N' + 1$.

The final step in the layer is the computation of the Head matrix, i.e.,

$$
\text{Head} := \text{Attention}(Q, K, V) W^0 \in \mathbb{R}^{(N' + 2) \times d_m}.
$$
