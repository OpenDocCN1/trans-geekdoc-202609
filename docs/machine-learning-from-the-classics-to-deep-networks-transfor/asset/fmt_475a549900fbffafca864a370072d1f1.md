**θ**[n] = (I − X[n]^(T)(X[n]X[n]^(T))^(−1)X[n])**θ**[n − 1] + X[n]^(T)(X[n]X[n]^(T))^(−1)**y**[n].

The first term on the right-hand side is the projection `P[{x[n], …, x[n − q + 1]}]^⊥(θ[n − 1])`. This is the most natural. By the definition of the respective affine set, as the intersection of the hyperplanes
