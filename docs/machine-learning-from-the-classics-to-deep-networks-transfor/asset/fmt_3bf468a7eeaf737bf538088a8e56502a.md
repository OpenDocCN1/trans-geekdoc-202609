:= [*P*[*n*](1), …, *P*[*n*](*K*)]^(*T*)

is the vector of the respective probabilities at time *n*. The Markov chain is said to be *homogeneous* or *stationary* if the transition matrix is independent of time, that is,

*P*[*n*](**x**[*n*] = *i*|**x**[*n* − 1] = *j*) = *P*(*i*|*j*) := *P*[*i**j*], *i*, *j* = 1, 2, …, *K*

and

*P*[*n*](**x**[*n*]|**x**[*n* − 1]) = *P* = [*P*[*i**j*]].
