
### 拉普拉斯近似推导

$$
\Sigma^{-1} := -\frac{\partial^{2}g(\boldsymbol{\theta})}{\partial\boldsymbol{\theta}^{2}}\Big|_{\boldsymbol{\theta} = \widehat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\text{MAP}}}
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000184/si134.png)

由此得到近似：

$$
p(\mathbf{y}|\mathcal{M}_{i},\boldsymbol{\theta})p(\boldsymbol{\theta}|\mathcal{M}_{i}) \approx p(\mathbf{y}|\mathcal{M}_{i},\widehat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\text{MAP}})p(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\text{MAP}}|\mathcal{M}_{i}) \times \exp\left( -\frac{1}{2}(\boldsymbol{\theta} - \widehat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\text{MAP}})^{T}\Sigma^{-1}(\boldsymbol{\theta} - \widehat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\text{MAP}}) \right)
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000184/si135.png) (12.36)

将公式 [(12.36)](#Chp012_583-634_B9780443292385000184.xhtml#deq47) 代入公式 [(12.31)](#Chp012_583-634_B9780443292385000184.xhtml#deq41) 的积分中，可得：

$$
p(\mathbf{y}|\mathcal{M}_{i}) = p(\mathbf{y}|\mathcal{M}_{i},\widehat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\text{MAP}})p(\widehat{\boldsymbol{\theta}}_{\text{MAP}}|\mathcal{M}_{i})(2\pi)^{\frac{K}{2}}|\Sigma|^{1/2}
$$
