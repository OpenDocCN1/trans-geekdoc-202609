## 8.5 A parallel version of POCS

In [68], a parallel version of the POCS algorithm was stated. In addition to its computational advantages (when parallel processing can be exploited), this scheme will be our vehicle for generalizations to the online processing, where one can cope with the case where the number of convex sets becomes infinite (or very large in practice). The proof for the parallel POCS is slightly technical and relies heavily on the results stated in the previous section. The concept behind the proof is to construct appropriate product spaces, and this is the reason that the algorithm is also referred to as POCS in *product spaces*. For the detailed proof, the interested reader may consult [68].
