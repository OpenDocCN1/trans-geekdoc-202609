
## Remarks 8.8

The previous proof follows the one given in [113]; this was the first paper to adopt the notion of “regret” for the analysis of convex online algorithms. Proofs given later for more complex algorithms have borrowed, in one way or another, the arguments used there.

$$
\mathbf{g}_{n}^{T}(\mathbf{\theta}_{n - 1} - \mathbf{h}) \leq \frac{1}{2\mu_{n}}\left( ||\mathbf{\theta}_{n - 1} - \mathbf{h}||^{2} - ||\mathbf{\theta}_{n} - \mathbf{h}||^{2} \right) + \frac{\mu_{n}}{2}G^{2}.
$$
![Equation](images/B9780443292385000147/si426.png)(8.87)

Summing up both sides of [(8.88)](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#deq169), taking into account inequality [(8.85)](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#deq166) and after a bit of algebra (Problem [8.29](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#o0175)), results in

$$
\sum\limits_{n = 1}^{N}\mathcal{L}_{n}(\mathbf{\theta}_{n - 1}) - \sum\limits_{n = 1}^{N}\mathcal{L}_{n}(\mathbf{h}) \leq \frac{1}{2\mu_{N}}F^{2} + \frac{G^{2}}{2}\sum\limits_{n = 1}^{N}\mu_{n}.
$$
![Equation](images/B9780443292385000147/si427.png)(8.88)

Setting $\mu_{n} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}}$![Equation](images/B9780443292385000147/si420.png), using the obvious bound

$$
\sum\limits_{n = 1}^{N}\frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \leq 1 + \int_{1}^{N}\frac{1}{\sqrt{t}}\ dt = 2\sqrt{N} - 1,
$$
![Equation](images/B9780443292385000147/si429.png)(8.89)

and dividing both sides of [(8.89)](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#deq170) by *N*, the proposition is proved for any **h**. Hence, it will also be true for **θ**[∗]![Equation](images/B9780443292385000147/si322.png). □
