
#### Predictive distribution and the Pólya urn model

Let us now build upon the posterior DP formulation in Eq. [(13.116)](#Chp013_635-716_B9780443292385000196.xhtml#deq177). To this end, the reasoning provided in [83] will be followed. Having observed the samples **θ**[1], …, **θ**[*n*], we focus on the next draw, **θ**[*n* + 1], and we will compute the probability of the sample to lie in an interval *T*. To simplify notation, let us use *G*′ to denote the posterior *G*|**θ**[1], …, **θ**[*n*]. Then we write

*P*(**θ**[*n* + 1] ∈ *T*|**θ**[1], …, **θ**[*n*]) = *G*′(*T*).
