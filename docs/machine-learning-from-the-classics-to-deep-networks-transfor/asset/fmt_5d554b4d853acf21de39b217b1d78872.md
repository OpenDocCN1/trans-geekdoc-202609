
$$
\mathbf{e}_{n|n - 1} := \mathbf{x}_{n} - \widehat{\mathbf{x}}_{n|n - 1} = F_{n}\mathbf{x}_{n - 1} + \boldsymbol{\eta}_{n} - F_{n}\widehat{\mathbf{x}}_{n - 1|n - 1} = F_{n}\mathbf{e}_{n - 1|n - 1} + \boldsymbol{\eta}_{n}.
$$

![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si469.png) (4.116)

Combining Eqs. [(4.115)](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml#deq206) and [(4.116)](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml#deq207), it is straightforward to see that

$$
P_{n|n-1} = F_{n}P_{n-1|n-1}F_{n}^{(T)} + Q_{n}.
$$

![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si470.png) (4.117)

### Step 3: Update $\widehat{\mathbf{x}}_{n|n - 1}$

![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si462.png)

To this end, adopt the following recursion:

$$
\widehat{\mathbf{x}}_{n|n} = \widehat{\mathbf{x}}_{n|n - 1} + K_{n}\mathbf{e}_{n},
$$

![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si471.png) (4.118)

where

$$
\mathbf{e}_{n} := \mathbf{y}_{n} - H_{n}\widehat{\mathbf{x}}_{n|n - 1}.
$$

![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si472.png) (4.119)
