
## LMS 算法均值收敛性分析

$$
\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{c}[n]] = \mathbb{E} \left[ (I - \mu \mathbf{x} \mathbf{x}^T) \mathbf{c}[n-1] \right] = (I - \mu \Sigma_x) \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{c}[n-1]]
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si187.png) (5.39)

Following similar arguments as in Section [5.3](#Chp005_177-248_B9780443292385000111.xhtml#s0020), we obtain

$$
\mathbb{E}[\mathbf{v}[n]] = (I - \mu \Lambda) \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{v}[n-1]]
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si188.png)

where $\Sigma_x = Q \Lambda Q^T$ and $\mathbf{v}[n] = Q^T \mathbf{c}[n]$.

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si189.png) ![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si190.png)

The last equation leads to

$$
\mathbb{E}[\boldsymbol{\theta}[n]] \to \boldsymbol{\theta}^* \quad \text{as } n \to \infty
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si191.png)

provided that

$$
0 < \mu < \frac{2}{\lambda_{\max}}
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si192.png)

In other words, in a stationary environment, the LMS converges to the optimal MSE solution *in the mean*. Thus, by fixing the value of the step size to be a constant, we lose something; the obtained estimates, even after convergence, hover around the optimal solution. The obvious task to be considered next is to study the respective covariance matrix.
