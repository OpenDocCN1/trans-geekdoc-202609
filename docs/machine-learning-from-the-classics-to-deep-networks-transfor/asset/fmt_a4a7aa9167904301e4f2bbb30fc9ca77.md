$$
\frac{w(\mathbf{x})}{w(0)} = \exp\frac{1}{2}\left( \frac{\sigma_{1}^{2} - \sigma_{2}^{2}}{\sigma_{1}^{2}\sigma_{2}^{2}}\sum\limits_{i = 1}^{l}x_{i}^{2} \right).
