```
Σ_u := E[u_n u_n^T] =
```

where `r(k)` is the autocorrelation sequence of the input process. Because we have assumed that the involved processes are wide-sense stationary, we have

`r(n, n−k) := E[u[n]u[n−k]] = r(k)`

Also, recall that, for real wide-sense stationary processes, the autocorrelation sequence is symmetric, or `r(k) = r(−k)` (Section [2.4.3](#Chp002_19-64_B9780443292385000081.xhtml)).
