
## Proposition 8.3

Let $J$ be a convex cost function. Assume that the subgradients at all points are bounded, that is,

$$
\|J'(\mathbf{x})\| \le G, \quad \forall \mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^l.
$$

Let us also assume that the step size sequence is a diminishing one, such that

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \mu_i = \infty, \quad \sum_{i=1}^{\infty} \mu_i^2 < \infty.
$$

Then

$$
\lim_{i \to \infty} J_*^{(i)} = J(\mathbf{\theta}_*),
$$

where $\mathbf{\theta}_*$ is a minimizer, assuming that the set of minimizers is not empty.
