
## 数学推导与证明

### 优化问题表述

$$
\min_{f \in \mathbb{H}} J(f) := \sum_{n = 1}^{N} \mathcal{L}\left( y_{n}, f(\mathbf{x}_{n}) \right) + \lambda \Omega\left( \| f \|^{2} \right)
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si176.png) (11.16)

*admits a representation of the form*^[^(74)](#fn74)

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/fx006.jpg) (11.17)

*where* $\theta[n] \in \mathbb{R},\ n = 1, 2, \dots, N$![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si179.png).

### Proof

The linear span, $A := \text{span}\{\kappa(\cdot, \mathbf{x}[1]), \dots, \kappa(\cdot, \mathbf{x}[N])\}$![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si180.png), forms a closed subspace. Then each $f \in \mathbb{H}$![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si175.png) can be decomposed into two parts (see Eq. [(8.20)](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml)), i.e.,

$$
f( \cdot ) = \sum_{n = 1}^{N} \theta_{n} \kappa( \cdot , \mathbf{x}_{n}) + f_{\bot},
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si181.png)

where $f_{\bot}$![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si182.png) is the part of $f$ that is orthogonal to $A$. From the reproducing property, we obtain

$$
f(\mathbf{x}_{m}) = \left\langle f, \kappa( \cdot , \mathbf{x}_{m}) \right\rangle = \left\langle \sum_{n = 1}^{N} \theta_{n} \kappa( \cdot , \mathbf{x}_{n}), \kappa( \cdot , \mathbf{x}_{m}) \right\rangle = \sum_{n = 1}^{N} \theta_{n} \kappa(\mathbf{x}_{m}, \mathbf{x}_{n}),
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si183.png)
