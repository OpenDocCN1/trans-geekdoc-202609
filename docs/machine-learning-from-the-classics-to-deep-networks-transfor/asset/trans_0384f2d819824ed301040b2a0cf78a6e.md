

### 步骤 2：固定 $q^{(j+1)}$

$$
\boldsymbol{\xi}^{(j + 1)} = \arg\max_{\boldsymbol{\xi}} \mathcal{F}\left( p\left( \mathcal{X}^{l}|\mathcal{X};\boldsymbol{\xi}^{(j)} \right),\boldsymbol{\xi} \right).
$$

$$
