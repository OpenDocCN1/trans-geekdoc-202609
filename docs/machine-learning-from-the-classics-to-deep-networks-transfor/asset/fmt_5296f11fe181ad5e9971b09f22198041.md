
It is readily noticed that the right-hand side of Eq. [(13.30)](#Chp013_635-716_B9780443292385000196.xhtml#deq38) is of a quadratic form with respect to `θ`, and hence `q_θ^(j+1)(θ)` is Gaussian; in order to completely specify it, it suffices to compute the respective mean and covariance (precision) matrix.

Reshuffling the terms in Eq. [(13.30)](#Chp013_635-716_B9780443292385000196.xhtml#deq38), we get

```
ln q_θ^(j+1)(θ) = - 1/2 θ^T (A + E[β] Φ^T Φ) θ + E[β] θ^T Φ^T y + constant
```

which according to Eqs. (12.114), (12.116), and (12.117) of the appendix of the previous chapter (available via the book's site) results in

`q_θ^(j+1)(θ) = N(θ | μ_θ^(j+1), Σ_θ^(j+1))` (13.32)

`Σ_θ^(j+1) = (A + E[β] Φ^T Φ)^(-1)` (13.33)

and

`μ_θ^(j+1) = E[β] Σ_θ^(j+1) Φ^T y` (13.34)
