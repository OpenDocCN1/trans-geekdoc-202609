To this end, the Kalman filtering formulation will be used. Note that the MSE linear estimation, presented in Section [4.9](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml#s0105), cannot be used here. As we have already discussed in Chapter [2](#Chp002_19-64_B9780443292385000081.xhtml), an AR process is asymptotically stationary; for finite-time samples, the initial conditions at time $n = 0$ are "remembered" by the process and the respective second-order statistics are time-dependent, hence it is a nonstationary process. However, Kalman filtering is specially suited for such cases.
