
## Gaussian–Gamma Distribution

$$p(\mu,\beta;\lambda,\mathbf{v}) = \mathcal{N}\left( \mu \middle| \frac{v_{2}}{\lambda},{(\lambda\beta)}^{- 1} \right)\text{Gamma}\left( \beta \middle| \frac{\lambda + 1}{2},\frac{v_{1}}{2} - \frac{v_{2}^{2}}{2\lambda} \right),$$

which is known as the Gaussian–gamma distribution with the Gaussian having mean value $\mu_{0} = \frac{v_{2}}{\lambda}$ and variance $\sigma_{\mu}^{2} = (\lambda\beta)^{-1}$ and the defining parameters of the gamma PDF are $a = \frac{\lambda + 1}{2}$ and $b = \frac{v_{1}}{2} - \frac{v_{2}^{2}}{2\lambda}$. ## Multivariate Gaussian Case

For the more general case of a multivariate Gaussian, $\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x}|\boldsymbol{\mu}, \Sigma)$, it turns out that it is also of an exponential form and its conjugate prior is of the Gaussian–Wishart form (Problem [12.13](#Chp012_583-634_B9780443292385000184.xhtml#o0150)), that is,

$$p(\boldsymbol{\mu}, \mathbf{Q}; \boldsymbol{\mu}_{0}, \lambda, \mathbf{W}, \nu) = \mathcal{N}(\boldsymbol{\mu}|\boldsymbol{\mu}_{0}, (\lambda \mathbf{Q})^{-1})\mathcal{W}(\mathbf{Q}|\mathbf{W}, \nu),$$

where $\mathbf{Q} = \Sigma^{-1}$.
