
### k-rank matrix approximation

The best rank $k < r \le \min(m, l)$ approximation matrix, $\hat{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{(m \times l)}$, of $X \in \mathbb{R}^{(m \times l)}$ in the Frobenius, $\| \cdot \|_F$, as well as in the spectral, $\| \cdot \|_2$, norms sense is given by (e.g., [26])

$$
\widehat{X} = \sum_{i = 1}^{k}\sigma_{i}\mathbf{u}_{i}\mathbf{v}_{i}^{T},
$$ (6.22)

with the previously stated norms defined as (Problem [6.9](#Chp006_249-291_B9780443292385000123.xhtml#o0050))

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000123/fx009.jpg) (6.23)

and

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000123/fx010.jpg) (6.24)

where $\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \dots \ge \sigma_r > 0$ are the singular values of $X$. In other words, $\hat{X}$ in Eq. [(6.22)](#Chp006_249-291_B9780443292385000123.xhtml#deq52) minimizes the error matrix norms,

$\|X - \hat{X}\|_F$ and $\|X - \hat{X}\|_2$.

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000123/si124.png)

Moreover, it turns out that the approximation error is given by (Problems [6.10](#Chp006_249-291_B9780443292385000123.xhtml#o0055) and [6.11](#Chp006_249-291_B9780443292385000123.xhtml#o0060))

$$
{\| X - \widehat{X}\|}_{F} = \sqrt{\sum_{i = k + 1}^{r}\sigma_{i}^{2}},\quad{\| X - \widehat{X}\|}_{2} = \sigma_{k + 1}.
$$ (6.25)

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000123/si125.png)

This is also known as the *Eckart–Young–Mirsky* theorem.
