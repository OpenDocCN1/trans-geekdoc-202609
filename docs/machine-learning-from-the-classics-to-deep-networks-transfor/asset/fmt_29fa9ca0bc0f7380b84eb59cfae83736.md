Remarks 19.12

*   As it is pointed out in [85], setting $\lambda_t$ in Eq. (19.73) equal to 1 is equivalent to optimizing a *weighted* variational approximation bound, which downweighs the contribution of terms that are associated with small values of $t$. These terms are contributed from diffused input samples that contain small amounts of noise. Thus, putting relatively less emphasis on them helps the optimization algorithm to focus on the more noisy versions that correspond to larger values of $t$.

*   Taking the gradient of the logarithm of the Gaussian in Eq. (19.58), it is not difficult to see that

    ```
    ∇_{x_t} q(x_t | x_0) = - (x_t - √(ā_t) x_0) / (1 - ā_t) = - ε / √(1 - ā_t).
    ```
