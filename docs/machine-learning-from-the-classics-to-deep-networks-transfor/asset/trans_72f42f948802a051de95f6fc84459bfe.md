

$$
\widehat{\mathbf{\theta}} = \arg\min_{\mathbf{\theta}} J(\mathbf{\theta}), \quad J(\mathbf{\theta}) := \sum_{n=1}^{N} \left( y_{n} - \sum_{m=1}^{N} \theta_{m} \kappa(\mathbf{x}_{n}, \mathbf{x}_{m}) \right)^{2} + C \langle f, f \rangle,
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si212.png) (11.24)

其中 `C` 为正则化参数。^[^(76)](#fn76)

$$
J(\mathbf{\theta}) = (\mathbf{y} - \mathcal{K}\mathbf{\theta})^{T}(\mathbf{y} - \mathcal{K}\mathbf{\theta}) + C \mathbf{\theta}^{T} \mathcal{K}^{T} \mathbf{\theta},
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si213.png) (11.25)

其中

$$
\mathbf{y} = [y_{1}, \dots, y_{N}]^{T}, \quad \mathbf{\theta} = [\theta_{1}, \dots, \theta_{N}]^{T},
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si214.png)

且 $\mathcal{K}$ 为公式 [(11.11)](#Chp011_519-582_B9780443292385000172.xhtml#deq32) 中定义的核矩阵；后者完全由核函数和训练点决定。遵循我们现在已熟悉的论证，关于 $\mathbf{\theta}$ 最小化 $J(\mathbf{\theta})$ 可得

$$
(\mathcal{K}^{T}\mathcal{K} + C\mathcal{K}^{T})\widehat{\mathbf{\theta}} = \mathcal{K}^{T}\mathbf{y}
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si216.png)
