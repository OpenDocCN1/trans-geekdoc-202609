The eigenvector corresponding to *λ*[1] has all its components equal and positive. Hence, the invariant distribution (**p**: *P***p** = **p**), after the required scaling, becomes the uniform one, **p** = [1/5, 1/5, 1/5, 1/5, 1/5]^T. Similar arguments apply for any value of *N*. Observe that the components of all the other eigenvectors add to zero.

The components of **p**[0] were randomly chosen. [Fig. 14.9](#Chp014_717-754_B9780443292385000202.xhtml#f0050) corresponds to the case of *N* = 9. Observe that the larger the value of *N*, the slower the convergence.
