Note that the `n`th row corresponds to time `n` and it contains similarity information relating time instant `n` to previous (including the current) instants, i.e., `q[n]^(T)k[i]`, `i` = 0, 1, …, `n`, as well as to future ones, `q[n]^(T)k[i]`, `i` = `n` + 1, `n` + 2, …, `N'` + 1. In a masked self-attention mechanism, the similarity scores corresponding to future time instants are zeroed, or in other words, they are *masked*.
