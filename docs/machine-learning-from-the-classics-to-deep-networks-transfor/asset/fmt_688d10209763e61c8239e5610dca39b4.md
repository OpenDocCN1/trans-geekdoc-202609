### The proximal forward-backward splitting operator

From a first look, the iterative update given in Eq. [(8.131)](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#deq244) seems to be a bit “magic.” However, this is not the case and we can come to it by following simple arguments starting from the basic property of a minimizer. Indeed, let $\mathbf{x}^*$ be a minimizer of Eq. [(8.130)](#Chp008_343-416_B9780443292385000147.xhtml#deq242). Then we know that it has to satisfy

$$
0 \in \partial f(\mathbf{x}^*) + \nabla g(\mathbf{x}^*), \text{ or equivalently}
$$

$$
0 \in \lambda \partial f(\mathbf{x}^*) + \lambda \nabla g(\mathbf{x}^*), \text{ or equivalently}
$$

$$
0 \in \lambda \partial f(\mathbf{x}^*) + \mathbf{x}^* - \mathbf{x}^* + \lambda \nabla g(\mathbf{x}^*),
$$

or equivalently

$$
(I - \lambda \nabla g)(\mathbf{x}^*) \in (I + \lambda \partial f)(\mathbf{x}^*),
$$
