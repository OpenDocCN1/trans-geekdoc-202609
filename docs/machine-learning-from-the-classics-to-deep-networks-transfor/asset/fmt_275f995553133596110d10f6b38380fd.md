
### 15.3.2 Some hints on causality

The existence of directed links in a Bayesian network *does not* necessarily reflect a cause–effect relationship from a parent to a child node.^([^(111)](#fn111))

$$
P(x_2 = HE, x_1 = HGP) = P(x_2 = HE | x_1 = HGP) P(x_1 = HGP) = 0.75 \times 0.2 = 0.15
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000214/si75.png)

which finally results to

$$
P(x_3 = HP | x_2 = HE, x_1 = HGP) = 0.8 = P(x_3 = HP | x_2 = HE)
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000214/si76.png)

which verifies the claim. The reader can check that this is true for all possible combinations of values.
