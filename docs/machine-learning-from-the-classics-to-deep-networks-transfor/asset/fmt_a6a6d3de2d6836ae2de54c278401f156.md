
### 19.3.6 Encoder: multi-head attention

$$
\text{Softmax}\left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{d_{k}}}QK^{T} \right) = \begin{bmatrix} s_{00} & \ldots & s_{0,N + 1} \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots \\ s_{N + 1,0} & \ldots & s_{N + 1,N + 1} \end{bmatrix},
$$

where, in this case, `Softmax(·)` is assumed to be applied element-wise and the respective normalization in the denominator in Eq. [(19.33)](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#deq79) results by summing up over the elements in the corresponding row.

In a similar way, we can stack the updated transpose embedding vectors of Eq. [(19.34)](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#deq80) to form the corresponding matrix.
