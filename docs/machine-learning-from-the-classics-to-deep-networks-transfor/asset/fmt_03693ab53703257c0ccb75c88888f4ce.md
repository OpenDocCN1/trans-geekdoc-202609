
$$
\int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} \frac{(x - \mu)^2}{\sigma^3} \exp\left( - \frac{(x - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right) dx = \sqrt{2\pi}
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si154.png) (2.67)

or

$$
\sigma_x^2 := \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}\sigma} \int_{-\infty}^{+\infty} (x - \mu)^2 \exp\left( - \frac{(x - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2} \right) dx = \sigma^2,
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si155.png) (2.68)

which proves the claim.
