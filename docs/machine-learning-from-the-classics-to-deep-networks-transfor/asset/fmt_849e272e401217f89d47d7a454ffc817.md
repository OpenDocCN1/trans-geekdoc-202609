and

assuming, of course, invertibility of *Σ̂*[*yy*]![Equation](images/B9780443292385000263/si146.png). Furthermore, assuming invertibility of *Σ̂*[*xx*]![Equation](images/B9780443292385000263/si147.png), too, we end up with the following eigenvalue-eigenvector problem:

(*Σ̂*[*xx*]^(−1)*Σ̂*[*xy*]*Σ̂*[*yy*]^(−1)*Σ̂*[*yx*])**u**[*x*] = *λ*²**u**[*x*].

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000263/si148.png)(20.34)

Thus, the axis **u**[*x*, 1]![Equation](images/B9780443292385000263/si135.png) is obtained as an eigenvector of the product of matrices in the parentheses in Eq. [(20.34)](#Chp020_1103-1179_B9780443292385000263.xhtml#deq43). Taking into account Eq. [(20.30)](#Chp020_1103-1179_B9780443292385000263.xhtml#deq39) and the constraints, it turns out that the corresponding optimal value of the correlation, *ρ*, is equal to

*ρ* = **u**[*x*, 1]^(*T*)*Σ̂*[*xy*]**u**[*y*, 1] = *λ***u**[*x*, 1]^(*T*)*Σ̂*[*xx*]**u**[*x*, 1] = *λ*.
