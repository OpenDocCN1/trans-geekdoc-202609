
### 公式推导

$z[n] = u[n] + v[n]$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si288.png)

Then,

$r_z(k) = r_u(k) + r_v(k) + r_{uv}(k) + r_{vu}(k)$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si289.png) (2.118)

where the **cross-correlation** between two jointly WSS stochastic processes is defined as

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/fx022.jpg) (2.119)

The proof is a direct consequence of the definition. Note that if the two processes are **uncorrelated**, as when $r_{uv}(k) = r_{vu}(k) = 0$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si290.png)

then

$r_z(k) = r_u(k) + r_v(k)$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si291.png)

Obviously, this is also true if the processes $u[n]$ and $v[n]$ are independent and of zero mean value, since then $\mathbb{E}[u[n]v^*(n-k)] = \mathbb{E}[u[n]]\mathbb{E}[v^*(n-k)] = 0$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si292.png)

It should be stressed here that uncorrelatedness is a weaker condition and it **does not** necessarily imply independence; the opposite is true for zero mean values.
