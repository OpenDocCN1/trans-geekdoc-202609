
### 3. For $i = 1, 2, \dots$, execute the following:

#### (a) Obtain the current support:

$$
S^{(i)} := \text{supp}\left( \mathbf{\theta}^{(i - 1)} \right) \cup \left\{ \frac{\text{indices of the } t \text{ largest in magnitude}}{\text{components of } X^{T}\mathbf{e}^{(i - 1)}} \right\}.
$$

#### (b) Select the active columns:
Construct $X^{(i)}$ to comprise the active columns of $X$ in accordance to $S^{(i)}$. Obviously, $X^{(i)}$ is an $N \times r$ matrix, where $r$ denotes the cardinality of the support set $S^{(i)}$.

#### (c) Update the estimate of the parameter vector:
Solve the LS task

$$
\widetilde{\mathbf{\theta}} := \arg\min_{\mathbf{z} \in \mathbb{R}^{r}} \| \mathbf{y} - X^{(i)}\mathbf{z} \|_{2}^{2}.
$$

1. Obtain ${\widehat{\mathbf{\theta}}}^{(i)} \in \mathbb{R}^{l}$ having the $r$ elements of $\widetilde{\mathbf{\theta}}$ in the respective locations, as indicated by the support, and the rest of the elements being zero.

#### (d) $\mathbf{\theta}^{(i)} := H_{k}\left( {\widehat{\mathbf{\theta}}}^{(i)} \right)$.
The mapping $H_{k}$ denotes the *hard thresholding* function; that is, it returns a vector with the $k$ largest in magnitude components of the argument, and the rest are forced to zero.

#### (e) Update the error vector:
$\mathbf{e}^{(i)} = \mathbf{y} - X\mathbf{\theta}^{(i)}$.
