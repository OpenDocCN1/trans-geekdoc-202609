### 19.3.11 Decoder: masked self-attention

The structure of a single decoder block is shown in [Fig. 19.13](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#f0070). We will focus on the lower (input) block. During training (the case of inference is treated in Section [19.3.13](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#s0140)) the inputs comprise the target output sequence embeddings. The rest of the blocks have exactly the same structure with the only difference being that the respective inputs are the output vectors of the block below. In this subsection, the focus is on the first layer in the block, where the so-called *masked self-attention* is preformed.
