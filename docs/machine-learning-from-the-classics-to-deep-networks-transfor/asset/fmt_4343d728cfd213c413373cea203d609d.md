
## E-Step 1c

We have

$$
\ln q_{\beta}^{(j+1)}(\beta) = \mathbb{E}_{q_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}^{(j+1)} q_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}^{(j+1)}}[\ln p(\mathbf{y}, \boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\alpha}, \beta)] + \text{constant}
$$

$$
= \mathbb{E}_{q_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}^{(j+1)} q_{\boldsymbol{\alpha}}^{(j+1)}}[\ln p(\mathbf{y}|\boldsymbol{\theta}, \beta) + \ln p(\beta)] + \text{constant}.
$$

This is of the same form as Eq. [(13.36)](#Chp013_635-716_B9780443292385000196.xhtml#deq46), and following similar steps (Problem [13.3](#Chp013_635-716_B9780443292385000196.xhtml#o0115)), it can be shown that

$$
q_{\beta}^{(j+1)}(\beta) = \text{Gamma}(\beta | \tilde{c}, \tilde{d}),
$$

$$
\tilde{c} = c + \frac{N}{2}.
$$
