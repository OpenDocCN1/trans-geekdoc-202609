### 19.3.7 Encoder: add & normalize layer I

The outputs `z[n]`, `n` = 0, 1, …, `N` + 1, that constitute the Multihead matrix ([Fig. 19.10](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#f0055)), are provided as inputs to the layer above, denoted as “Add & Normalize” layer. This is the first of two layers with similar structure that are present in an encoder block. The “normalize” term indicates a normalization operation. What does the term “add” stand for? In \[189\], and in most of the follow-up implementations, the rationale of residual network, see [Figs. 18.33, and 18.34](#Chp018_885-995_B978044329238500024X.xhtml), has been adopted. Thus, in this vein, the “Add & Normalize” layer accepts as inputs the sum of the vectors,

```
${\\widehat{\\mathbf{t}}}\_{n}: = \\mathbf{x}\_{n} + \\mathbf{z}\_{n},\\ n = 0,1,\\ldots,N + 1.$
```

In the sequel, the mean and the standard deviation over all dimensions of the resulting vectors are computed, i.e.,

```
${\\widehat{\\mu}}\_{n} = \\frac{1}{d\_{m}}\\sum\\limits\_{i = 1}^{d\_{m}}{\\widehat{t}}\_{ni},\\ \\ {\\widehat{\\sigma}}\_{n} = \\sqrt{\\frac{1}{d\_{m}}\\sum\\limits\_{i = 1}^{d\_{m}}{({\\widehat{t}}\_{ni} - {\\widehat{\\mu}}\_{n})}^{2}}.$
```(19.42)
