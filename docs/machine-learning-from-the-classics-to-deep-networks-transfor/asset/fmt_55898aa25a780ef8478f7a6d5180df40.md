### Remarks 4.4

*   **Modularity.** The lattice-ladder scheme is a highly efficient, *modular* structure. It comprises a sequence of successive similar stages. To increase the order of the filter, it suffices to add an extra stage, which is a highly desirable property in VLSI implementations. Moreover, lattice-ladder schemes enjoy a higher robustness, compared to Levinson's algorithm, with respect to numerical inaccuracies.
