
Σ_b θ = λ Σ_w θ

where λ is the maximum eigenvalue of the matrix Σ_w^(−1) Σ_b (Problem [7.14](#Chp007_293-342_B9780443292385000135.xhtml#o0075)). However, for our specific case^([^(47)](#fn47))

λ Σ_w θ ∝ (μ_1 − μ_2)(μ_1 − μ_2)^T θ ∝ (μ_1 − μ_2)

since the inner product, (μ_1 − μ_2)^T θ, is a scalar. In other words, Σ_w θ lies in the direction of (μ_1 − μ_2), and because we are only interested in the direction, we can finally write

```
θ ∝ Σ_w^(−1) (μ_1 − μ_2)
```
(7.68)

assuming of course that Σ_w is invertible. In practice, Σ_w is obtained as the respective sample mean using the available observations.
