\mathcal{L}'(y_{n},\mathbf{\theta}^{T}\mathbf{x}_{n}) = - y_{n}\mathbf{x}_{n}\chi_{(-\infty,0]}(y_{n}\mathbf{\theta}^{T}\mathbf{x}_{n}),
$$

$$
