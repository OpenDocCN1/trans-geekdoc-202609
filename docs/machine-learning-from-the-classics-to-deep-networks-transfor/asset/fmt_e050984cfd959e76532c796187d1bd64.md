
$$
\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_t} \ln q(\mathbf{x}_t | \mathbf{x}_0) = - \frac{\mathbf{x}_t - \mathbf{x}_0}{\sigma_t^2}
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000251/si622.png)

and taking into account Eq. [(19.77)](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#deq152), we get

$$
\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_t} \ln q(\mathbf{x}_t | \mathbf{x}_0) = - \frac{1}{\sigma_t} \mathbf{\epsilon}, \quad t = 1, 2, \ldots, T.
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000251/si623.png)

Denoting the output of the neural network (e.g., U-Net) at step $t$, $\mathbf{s}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}(\mathbf{x}_t, t)$, the network is trained to minimize the cost function,

$$
J(\boldsymbol{\theta}) = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^{T} \lambda_t \mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{x}_0 \sim q(\mathbf{x}_0)} \mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{\epsilon} \sim \mathcal{N}(0,I)} \left[ \|\boldsymbol{\epsilon} + \sigma_t \mathbf{s}_{\boldsymbol{\theta}}(\mathbf{x}_t, t)\|^2 \right],
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000251/si625.png) (19.74)

where $\lambda_t$ is a weighting sequence (which has absorbed $\sigma_t$ in Eq. [(19.78)](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#deq155)), and $\mathbf{x}_t$ is given via Eq. [(19.77)](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#deq152). Note that this is the counterpart of Eq. [(19.74)](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#deq148), which also establishes a bridge between the two approaches; see, also, [Remarks 19.12](#Chp019_997-1102_B9780443292385000251.xhtml#enun0100).
