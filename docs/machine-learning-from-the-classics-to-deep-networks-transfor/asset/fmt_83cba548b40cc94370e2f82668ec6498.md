
$$
\min_{\widehat{M} \in \mathbb{R}^{l_1 \times l_2}} \left\| \mathbf{\sigma}_{\widehat{M}} \right\|_1, \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \widehat{M}(i,j) = M(i,j), \quad (i,j) \in \Omega,
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000263/si592.png) (20.123)

where $\|\mathbf{\sigma}_{\widehat{M}}\|_1$, that is, the sum of the singular values, is referred to as the *nuclear norm* of the matrix $\widehat{M}$, often denoted as $\|\widehat{M}\|_*$. The nuclear norm minimization was proposed in [74] as a convex approximation of rank minimization, which can be cast as a semidefinite programming task.

### Theorem 20.1

*Let, for simplicity, $M$ be an $l \times l$ matrix of rank $r$, which is much smaller than $l$, obeying [(20.121)](#Chp020_1103-1179_B9780443292385000263.xhtml#deq183). Suppose that we observe $N$ entries of $M$ with locations sampled uniformly at random. Then there is a positive constant $C$ such that if*

$$
N \ge C \mu_B^2 l \ln^2 l,
$$

*then $M$ is the unique solution to the task in [(20.123)](#Chp020_1103-1179_B9780443292385000263.xhtml#deq185) with probability at least $1 - l^{-3}$.*
