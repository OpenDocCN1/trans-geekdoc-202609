#### Complex random variables

A complex random variable, *z* ∈ ℂ

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si84.png)

is a sum

*z* = *x* + *j**y*

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si85.png) (2.36)

where *x*, *y* are real random variables and $j := \sqrt{-1}$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si86.png).

Note that for complex random variables, the PDF *cannot* be defined since inequalities of the form *x* + *j**y* ≤ *x* + *j**y* have no meaning. When we write *p*(*z*) we mean the joint PDF of the real and imaginary parts, expressed as

*p*(*z*) := *p*(*x*, *y*).

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si89.png) (2.37)

For complex random variables, the notions of mean and covariance are defined as

𝔼\[*z*\] := 𝔼\[*x*\] + *j*𝔼\[*y*\]

and

cov(*z*[1], *z*[2]) := 𝔼 \[(*z*[1]−𝔼\[*z*[1]\])(*z*[2]−𝔼\[*z*[2]\])^(*)\]
