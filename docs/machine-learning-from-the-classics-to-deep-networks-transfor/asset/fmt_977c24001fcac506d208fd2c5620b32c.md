
# 5.7 The complex-valued case

In Section [4.4](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml), when

$$y[n] \in \mathbb{C} \text{ and } \mathbf{x}[n] \in \mathbb{C}^{(l)},$$

the *widely linear* formulation of the estimation task was introduced to deal with complex-valued data that do not obey the circularity conditions. The output of a widely linear estimator is given by

$$\widehat{y}_n = \mathbf{\varphi}^{H}\widetilde{\mathbf{x}}_n,$$

where

$$\mathbf{\varphi} := \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{\theta} \\ \mathbf{v} \end{bmatrix} \quad \text{and} \quad \widetilde{\mathbf{x}}_n = \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}_n \\ \mathbf{x}_n^{\ast} \end{bmatrix},$$
