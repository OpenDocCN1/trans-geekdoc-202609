## 13.4 A variational Bayesian approach to Gaussian mixture modeling

Dealing with the Gaussian mixture modeling in Section [12.6](#Chp012_583-634_B9780443292385000184.xhtml), it was pointed out in the remarks that the standard EM approach may lead to singularities. One way to bypass this drawback is to enforce priors on the involved parameters and resort to a variational Bayesian philosophy to estimate the quantities of interest. The task was first treated in [4] and later in [13]. We will present the latter approach and comment on the underlying differences between the two later on.
