
### 数学公式

$$
\mathbb{E}[x_k] = \frac{a_k}{\bar{a}},\quad k = 1,2,\ldots,K,
$$
$$
\sigma_{x_k}^2 = \frac{a_k(\bar{a} - a_k)}{\bar{a}^2(1 + \bar{a})},\quad k = 1,2,\ldots,K,
$$
$$
\text{cov}[x_i x_j] = - \frac{a_i a_j}{\bar{a}^2(1 + \bar{a})},\quad i \neq j,
$$

其中 $\bar{a} = \sum_{k=1}^{K} a_k$。
