
$$
\mathbf{\theta}_{n} = \begin{cases} 
\mathbf{\theta}_{n - 1} + X_{n}{(X_{n}^{T}X_{n})}^{- 1}(\mathbf{e}_{n} - \mathbf{y}_{n}), & \text{if } |e_{n}| > \epsilon, \\ 
\mathbf{\theta}_{n - 1}, & \text{otherwise}, 
\end{cases}
$$

where `X[n] = [x[n], x[n-1], ..., x[n-q+1]]`, `y[n] = [y[n], y[n-1], ..., y[n-q+1]]^T`, and `e[n] = [e[n], e[n-1], ..., e[n-q+1]]^T`, with `e[n] = y[n] - x[n]^T \theta[n-1]`. The stochastic analysis of the set-membership APA [34] establishes its mean-square error (MSE) performance, and the analysis is carried out by adopting energy conservation arguments (Chapter [5](#Chp005_177-248_B9780443292385000111.xhtml)).
