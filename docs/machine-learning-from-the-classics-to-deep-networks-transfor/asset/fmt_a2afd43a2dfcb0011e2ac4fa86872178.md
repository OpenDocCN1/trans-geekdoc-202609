
### The LS estimator is BLUE in the presence of white noise

The notion of the best linear unbiased estimator (BLUE) was introduced in Section [4.9.1](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml) in the context of the *Gauss–Markov* theorem. Let $\widehat{\mathbf{\theta}}$ denote any other *linear* unbiased estimator, under the assumption that

$$\mathbb{E}[\boldsymbol{\eta}\boldsymbol{\eta}^{(T)}] = \sigma_{\eta}^2 \mathbf{I}.$$

Then, due to the linearity assumption, the estimator will have a linear dependence on the output random variables that are observed, i.e.,

$$\widehat{\mathbf{\theta}} = H\mathbf{y},\quad H \in \mathbb{R}^{l \times N}.$$
