
### 3.10.1 Linear regression: the nonwhite Gaussian noise case

Consider the linear regression model

$$y = \boldsymbol{\theta}^T \mathbf{x} + \eta.$$

We are given $N$ training data points $(y[n], \mathbf{x}[n])$, $n = 1, 2, \dots, N$. The corresponding (unobserved) noise samples $\eta[n]$, $n = 1, \dots, N$, are assumed to follow a jointly Gaussian distribution with zero mean and covariance matrix equal to $\boldsymbol{\Sigma}_\eta$.
