*   **Majority voting rule**: The previous methods belong to the family of soft-type rules. A popular alternative is a hard-type rule, which is based on a voting scheme. One decides in favor of the class for which either there is a consensus or at least $l_{c}$ of the classifiers agree on the class label, where

$$
l_{c} = \left\{ \begin{matrix} {\frac{L}{2} + 1,} & {L \text{ is even},} \\ {\frac{L + 1}{2},} & {L \text{ is odd.}} \\ \end{matrix} \right.
$$

*   Otherwise, the decision is rejection (i.e., no decision is taken).
