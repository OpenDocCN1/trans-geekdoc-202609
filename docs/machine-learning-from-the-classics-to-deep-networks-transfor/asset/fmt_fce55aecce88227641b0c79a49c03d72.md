
## 数学推导

$$q_{\mathcal{X}^{l}}^{(j + 1)}(\mathcal{X}^{l}) = \text{arg}\ \text{max}_{q_{\mathcal{X}^{l}}}\mathcal{F}\left( q_{\mathcal{X}^{l}},q_{\mathbf{\theta}}^{(j)},\mathbf{\xi}^{(j)} \right) = \text{arg}\ \text{max}_{q_{\mathcal{X}^{l}}}\int q_{\mathcal{X}^{l}}(\mathcal{X}^{l})\ln\frac{\widetilde{p}\left( \mathcal{X},\mathcal{X}^{l};\mathbf{\xi}^{(j)} \right)}{q_{\mathcal{X}^{l}}(\mathcal{X}^{l})}\ d\mathcal{X}^{l} + \text{constant}$$

其中“constant”包含所有不依赖于 $\mathcal{X}^{(l)}$ 的项。

负 KL 散度在公式 [(13.10)](#Chp013_635-716_B9780443292385000196.xhtml#deq11) 中最大化，当我们设定：

$$q_{\mathcal{X}^{l}}^{(j+1)}(\mathcal{X}^{l}) \propto \tilde{p}(\mathcal{X},\mathcal{X}^{l};\mathbf{\xi}^{(j)})$$

其中 $\propto$ 表示正比关系。
