where 𝔼[*y*|*x*] denotes the mean value with respect to *p*(*y*|*x*). This is a direct consequence of the definition of the mean value and the product rule of probabilities.

Given two random variables *x*, *y*, their *covariance* is defined as

cov(*x*, *y*) := 𝔼[(*x* − 𝔼[*x*])(*y* − 𝔼[*y*])],

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si70.png) (2.28)

and their *correlation* as

*r*[*xy*] := 𝔼[*xy*] = cov(*x*, *y*) + 𝔼[*x*]𝔼[*y*].

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000081/si71.png) (2.29)
