
$$
\mathbf{w} := \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{w}^{f} \\ \mathbf{w}^{b} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{C}^{L + l},\quad \mathbf{u}_{e,n} := \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{u}_{n} \\ \mathbf{s}_{n} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{C}^{L + l}
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si397.png)

and

$$
\mathbf{s}_{n} := \left[ s[n-L], \dots, s[n-L-l+1] \right]^{T}
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si398.png).

The target/desired response at time $n$ is

$$
d[n] = s[n-L+1]
$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000111/si399.png)

In practice, after the initial training period, the information samples $s[n-L-i]$ are replaced by their computed estimates, $\hat{s}[n-L-i],\ i = 0, 1, \dots, l-1$, which are available from decisions taken in previous time instants. It is said that the equalizer operates in the *decision-directed* mode. The basic DFE structure is shown in [Fig. 5.20](#Chp005_177-248_B9780443292385000111.xhtml#f0105). Note that during the training period, the parameter vector, $\mathbf{w}$, is trained so as to minimize the power of the error,

$$
e[n] = d[n] - \hat{d}[n] = s[n-L+1] - \hat{d}[n]
$$
