
That is, we assume that we know that the values of `θ` lie around `θ₀`, and `σ₀²` quantifies our degree of uncertainty about this prior knowledge. Our goals are first to obtain the posterior PDF, given the set of observations `y = [y₁, …, y_N]ᵀ`, and then to obtain `𝔼[θ|y]`, according to Eqs. [(3.63)](#Chp003_65-117_B9780443292385000093.xhtml#deq113) and [(3.64)](#Chp003_65-117_B9780443292385000093.xhtml#deq115) after adapting them to our current notational needs. We have

```
p(θ|y) = p(y|θ)p(θ) / p(y)
       = (1 / p(y)) (∏_{n=1}^N p(y_n|θ)) p(θ)
       = (1 / p(y)) (∏_{n=1}^N (1 / (√(2π) σ_η)) exp(-(y_n - θ)² / (2 σ_η²)))
         × (1 / (√(2π) σ_0)) exp(-(θ - θ_0)² / (2 σ_0²)).
``` (3.67)
