
$$
\Theta_{\ast} := \arg\min_{\Theta} \left\{ \mathbb{E} \left[ (y_{r} - \widehat{y}_{r})^{2} \right] + \mathbb{E} \left[ (y_{i} - \widehat{y}_{i})^{2} \right] \right\}.
$$

![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si87.png) (4.27)

Note that Eq. [(4.27)](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml#deq36) can equivalently be written as

$$
\Theta_{\ast} := \arg\min_{\Theta} \left\{ \mathbb{E} \left[ \mathbf{e}^{T}\mathbf{e} \right] \right\} = \arg\min_{\Theta} \left\{ \text{trace} \left\{ \mathbb{E} \left[ \mathbf{e}\mathbf{e}^{T} \right] \right\} \right\},
$$

![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si88.png)

where

$$
\mathbf{e} := \mathbf{y} - \widehat{\mathbf{y}} \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{y} := \begin{bmatrix} y_{r} \\ y_{i} \end{bmatrix}.
$$

![Equation](images/B978044329238500010X/si89.png)

Minimizing Eq. [(4.27)](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml#deq36) is equivalent to minimizing the two terms individually; in other words, treating each channel separately (Problem [4.7](#Chp004_119-175_B978044329238500010X.xhtml#o0040)). Thus, the task can be tackled by solving two sets of normal equations, namely,

$$
\Sigma_{\varepsilon} \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{\theta}_{11} \\ \mathbf{\theta}_{12} \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{p}_{r}, \quad \Sigma_{\varepsilon} \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{\theta}_{21} \\ \mathbf{\theta}_{22} \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{p}_{i},
$$
