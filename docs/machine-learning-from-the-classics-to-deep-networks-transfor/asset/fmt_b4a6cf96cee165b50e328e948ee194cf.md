
### Expectation step

From the general scheme presented in Section [12.4.1](#Chp012_583-634_B9780443292385000184.xhtml) (with `Y` in place of 𝒳, `X` in place of 𝒳<sup>(l)</sup>, and `Θ` in place of `ξ`) at the (`t` + 1)th iteration, we have to compute

```
𝒬(Θ, Θ^(t)) = 𝔼[ln p(Y, X; Θ)],
```

where `𝔼[·]` is the expectation with respect to `P(X|Y; Θ^(t))`. From Eqs. [(16.32)](#Chp016_805-853_B9780443292385000226.xhtml#deq64)–[(16.35)](#Chp016_805-853_B9780443292385000226.xhtml#deq67) we obtain

```
ln p(Y, X; Θ) = ∑_{k=1}^K (x_{1,k} ln P_k + ln p(y_1|k; θ_k))
```
