
We now turn our attention to the form of *Q*. Due to the (assumed) binary nature of the variables, a sensible completely factorized form of *Q* is [34]

$$Q(\mathcal{X}^{l};\boldsymbol{\mu}) = \prod\limits_{i:x_{i} \in \mathcal{X}^{l}}\mu_{i}^{x_{i}}{(1 - \mu_{i})}^{(1 - x_{i})},$$

where the dependence on the variational parameters, $\boldsymbol{\mu}$, is explicitly shown. Also, due to the adopted Bernoulli distribution for each variable, $\mathbb{E}[x_{i}] = \mu_{i}$ (Chapter [2](#Chp002_19-64_B9780443292385000081.xhtml)).
