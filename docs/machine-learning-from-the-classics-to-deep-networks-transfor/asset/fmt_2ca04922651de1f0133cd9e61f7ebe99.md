### Lemma 11.1

*The reproducing kernel associated with an RKHS* ℍ *is a positive definite kernel.*

The proof of the lemma is given in Problem [11.3](#Chp011_519-582_B9780443292385000172.xhtml#o0040). Note that the opposite is also true. It can be shown [82,106] that if *κ* : 𝒳 × 𝒳 ↦ ℝ is a positive definite kernel, then there exists an RKHS ℍ of functions on 𝒳 such that *κ*(⋅, ⋅) is a reproducing kernel of ℍ. This establishes the equivalence between reproducing and positive definite kernels. Historically, the theory of positive definite kernels was developed first in the context of integral equations by Mercer [76], and the connection to RKHS was developed later on (see, for example, [3]).

### Lemma 11.2

*Let* ℍ *be an RKHS on the set* 𝒳 *with reproducing kernel* *κ*(⋅, ⋅)*. Then the linear span of the function* *κ*(⋅,**x**), **x** ∈ 𝒳*, is dense in* ℍ*, that is,*

\mathbb{H} = \bar{\textit{span}\left\{ \kappa(\cdot,\mathbf{x}),\mathbf{x}\in\mathcal{X} \right\}}.
