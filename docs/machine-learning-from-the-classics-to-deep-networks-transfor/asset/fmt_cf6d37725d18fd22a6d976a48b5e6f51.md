$$\sum_{n=1}^{N} \lambda_n y_n = 0.$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si368.png) (11.88)

When working in an RKHS, the cost function becomes

$$\sum_{n=1}^{N} \lambda_n - \frac{1}{2} \sum_{n=1}^{N} \sum_{m=1}^{N} \lambda_n \lambda_m y_n y_m \kappa(\mathbf{x}_n, \mathbf{x}_m).$$

![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si369.png)

Observe that the only difference compared to its linearly class-separable counterpart in [(11.74)](#Chp011_519-582_B9780443292385000172.xhtml#deq164)–[(11.76)](#Chp011_519-582_B9780443292385000172.xhtml#deq166) is the existence of `C` in the inequality constraints for `\lambda[n]`. The following comments are in order:

*   From Eq. [(11.83)](#Chp011_519-582_B9780443292385000172.xhtml#deq182), we conclude that for all the points outside the margin, and on the correct side of the classifier, which correspond to `\xi[n] = 0`, we have

    $$y_n(\boldsymbol{\theta}^T \mathbf{x}_n + \theta_0) > 1,$$

    ![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si372.png)

*   hence, `\lambda[n] = 0`. That is, these points do not participate in the formation of the solution in [(11.80)](#Chp011_519-582_B9780443292385000172.xhtml#deq179).

*   We have `\lambda[n] \neq 0` only for the points that live either on the border hyperplanes or inside the margin or outside the margin but on the wrong side of the classifier. These comprise the support vectors.

*   For points lying inside the margin or outside but on the wrong side, `\xi[n] > 0`; hence, from Eq. [(11.84)](#Chp011_519-582_B9780443292385000172.xhtml#deq183), `\mu[n] = 0` and from [(11.82)](#Chp011_519-582_B9780443292385000172.xhtml#deq181) we get

    `\lambda[n] = C`.

    ![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si397.png)

*   Support vectors which lie on the margin border hyperplanes satisfy `\xi[n] = 0` and therefore `\mu[n]` can be nonzero, which leads to

    `0 \le \lambda[n] \le C`.

    ![Equation](images/B9780443292385000172/si399.png)
