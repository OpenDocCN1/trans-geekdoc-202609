For unsupervised learning, we define the energy of the joint configuration $(h^{k-1}, h^k)$ as [50]:

```
$(h^{k-1}, h^k; \theta) = -\sum_{i=1}^{D_{k-1}} \sum_{j=1}^{D_k} w_{ij}^k h_i^{k-1} h_j^k - \sum_{i=1}^{D_{k-1}} b_i^{k-1} h_i^{k-1} - \sum_{j=1}^{D_k} c_j^k h_j^k$ (47)
```

where, $\theta = (W, b, c)$ are the model parameters: $w_{ij}^k$ is the symmetric interaction term between unit i in the layer $h^{k-1}$ and unit j in the layer $h^k$, \( k = 1, \cdots, N-1 \). $b_i^{k-1}$ is the ith bias of layer $h^{k-1}$ and $c_j^k$ is the jth bias of layer $h^k$. $D^k$ is the number of units in the kth layer.
