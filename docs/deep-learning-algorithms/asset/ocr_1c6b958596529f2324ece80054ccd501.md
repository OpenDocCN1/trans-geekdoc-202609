$e_i = d_i - y_i.$ (6)

+   (3)Calculate the weight updates:

$\Delta w_{ij} = \alpha e_i x_j.$ (7)

+   (4) Adjust the weights accordingly until the correct output or that has small tolerance is obtained:

$w_{ij} \longleftarrow w_{ij} + w_{ij}$ (8)
