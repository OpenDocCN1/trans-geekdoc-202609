# 40: Why do you write if(2 == $a) instead of if($a ==2)?

Answer:
Writing ($a ==2) could accidentally become (type or just a silly mistake) ($a = 2), which would be hard to debug thing at times (depending on how large or complex the codebase is). So writing the constant before the variable saves this issue.
