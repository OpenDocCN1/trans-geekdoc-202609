
Although Bitcoin has been around since 2009, cryptocurrencies and the applications of blockchain technology in finance are still emerging, and more applications are expected in the future. With the help of this technology, it is possible to trade bonds, stocks, and other financial assets.

### How to buy cryptocurrencies?

#### Choose a platform.

The initial step is to select a platform. Usually, you can choose between a traditional broker and a specialized crypto exchange:

*   Traditional brokers. These online brokers offer the ability to buy and sell cryptocurrencies and other financial assets such as stocks, bonds, and ETFs. These platforms usually offer lower transaction costs but fewer features for cryptocurrencies.

*   Exchanges for cryptocurrencies. There are numerous cryptocurrency exchanges, each different offering cryptocurrencies, wallets, interest-bearing accounts, and more. In addition, many exchanges charge asset-based fees.

*   When comparing different platforms, pay attention to the cryptocurrencies offered, fees charged, security features, deposit and withdrawal options, and educational resources.

#### Log in to the account.

*   Once you have chosen a platform, you need to fund your account to start trading. Most crypto exchanges allow users to buy cryptocurrencies with fiat currencies (i.e., currencies issued by governments) such as US dollars, pounds sterling, or euros using their debit or credit cards.

*   Buying cryptocurrencies with a credit card is risky, and some exchanges do not support them. Some credit card companies also do not allow transactions with cryptocurrencies. It is because cryptocurrencies are very volatile, and it is not advisable to go into debt for certain assets - or pay potentially high fees for credit card transactions.

*   Some platforms also accept bank transfers and ACH. How you pay and how long you deposit or withdraw money varies from platform to platform. The time it takes for deposits to clear also depends on the payment method. Fees are an essential element to consider. These include possible transaction fees for deposits and withdrawals and transaction costs. Again, fees vary depending on the payment method and platform.

#### Place an order

Orders can be placed using your broker's or exchange's web or mobile platforms. For example, if you want to buy cryptocurrencies, you can select "Buy," choose the order type, enter the number of cryptocurrencies you want to buy, and confirm the order. The same procedure applies to sell orders. Other strategies for investing in cryptocurrencies exist as well. For example, payment services like PayPal, Cash App, and Venmo enable users to buy, sell or hold cryptocurrency.

#### How are cryptocurrencies stored?

Once you have purchased cryptocurrencies, you must keep them safe to protect them from hacking or theft. Cryptocurrencies are usually stored in cryptocurrency wallets. These devices or programs are used to safely store the private keys of cryptocurrencies. Some exchanges offer wallet services that facilitate direct storage through the platform. But not all brokers or exchanges immediately provide wallet services. Therefore, it is possible to choose between different wallet service providers. "Hot wallet" and "cold wallet":

##### Hot wallet storage.

Network software keeps assets' private keys safe when cryptocurrencies are stored in a "hot wallet."

##### Cold wallet storage

Unlike hot wallets, cold wallets, also called "hardware wallets," store private keys on devices that are not connected to the Internet.

#### What can you buy with cryptocurrencies?

When Bitcoin was first launched on the market, it was thought to be a tool for everyday transactions, allowing you to buy anything from a coffee cup to a computer to large purchases such as real estate. Unfortunately, it was not the case, and while the number of institutions accepting cryptocurrencies is growing, large Bitcoin transactions are rare. However, buying a range of products on e-commerce sites is possible using cryptocurrencies. Here are some examples:

##### Technology and e-commerce websites.

Several companies selling technology products, such as newegg.com, AT&T, and Microsoft, accept cryptocurrencies on their websites. Overstock, an online shopping platform, was one of the first sites to accept bitcoin. Home Depot, Rakuten, and Shopify also accept them.

##### Luxury Goods

Some luxury shops accept cryptocurrency as a means of payment. For example, the luxury online store Bitdials offers high-end watches from Rolex, Patek Philippe, and others in exchange for bitcoin.

##### Cars

Some car dealers, from traditional brands to luxury dealers, already accept cryptocurrencies as payment.

##### Insurance

In April 2021, the Swiss insurance company AXA announced that it would accept bitcoin as a means of payment for all its insurance lines except life insurance (due to regulatory issues). In addition, Premier Shield, which sells home and car insurance in the US, also accepts bitcoin for premium payments.

##### Tips for Investing Safely in Cryptocurrencies

Consumer Reports says that all investments have some risk, but some experts think cryptocurrencies are one of the riskiest. If you are considering investing in cryptocurrencies, these tips can help you make an informed decision.

##### Research Exchanges:

Before investing, you should research cryptocurrency exchanges. According to estimates, there are more than 500 exchanges to select from. So do your research, read reviews, and talk to experienced investors before you decide.

##### Know how to store digital currency:

When you purchase cryptocurrencies, you must store them. For example, you can store them in an exchange or a digital wallet. There are different types of wallets, all with advantages, technical requirements, and security. As with exchanges, you must read up on storage options before investing.

##### Diversify investments:

Diversification is the key to any good investment strategy, which also applies to cryptocurrency investments. So please don't put all your money into Bitcoin simply because you've heard of it. There are several alternatives, and it is prudent to diversify your investments across various currencies.

#### Is it worth investing in cryptocurrencies?

Treating your "investment" like any other highly speculative venture would be best if you consider investing in cryptocurrencies. In other words, you should be aware that you risk losing most, if not all, of your investment. In addition, as mentioned above, a cryptocurrency has no intrinsic value other than what a buyer is willing to pay at any given time. It means its price is likely to change significantly, increasing the risk of investment loss. Bitcoin, for example, plummeted from $260 to about $130 in six hours on April 11, 2013.

If you can't handle this kind of volatility, you should look for other investments that are better suited for you. While opinions remain deeply divided on the merits of Bitcoin as an investment—proponents point to its limited supply and growing use as a value driver, while critics see it as another speculative bubble—this is a debate best avoided by a conservative investor.

In the global financial system, cryptocurrency is a big topic. Cryptocurrency exchange rates are highly volatile. It means that trading these cryptocurrencies is very risky. Nevertheless, many speculators have been interested in them due to their expansion. They are easily transferable. Only when there is confidence in cryptocurrencies will they be used on a larger scale. If cryptocurrencies fail to gain this confidence, their boom may slow down. They are still in their infancy, and it is still being determined when their usage is to good use or to trade at maturity in global markets.
