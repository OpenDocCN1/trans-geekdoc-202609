## How do Blockchain and cryptocurrency come into the Web3 philosophy?

Decentralization, as previously stated, is one of the primary issues with Web2 is the concentration of power and data in the hands of a few significant corporations. Blockchain and cryptocurrency have the potential to decentralize Web3 by promoting a more equitable allocation of information and power. In addition, web3 may use blockchain-powered public distributed ledgers to increase transparency and decentralization.

### Permissionlessness

Blockchain-based initiatives replace traditional corporations' proprietary systems with publicly accessible code. Because blockchain apps are permissionless, anybody can access and communicate with them without limitations.

### Trustlessness

Blockchain and cryptocurrency remove the need to rely on a third party, such as a bank or an individual middleman. As a result, web3 users may transact without trusting anybody other than the network itself.

### Payment rails

Cryptocurrencies have the potential to act as Web3's digitally native payment infrastructure. In addition, because digital assets are genuinely borderless and do not require middlemen, they have the potential to enhance Web2's costly and unwieldy payment infrastructure.

### Ownership

Crypto already provides solutions such as self-custodial crypto wallets, which allow users to keep funds without the involvement of middlemen. Users may also connect their wallets to decentralized apps to utilize their cash in several ways or to display their digital things. Anybody can verify ownership of these monies and goods using a transparent public ledger.

### Censorship resistance

Blockchains are supposed to be censorship-resistant, meaning no party may unilaterally change the transaction record. It is virtually hard to erase a record after it has been uploaded to the Blockchain. This feature might aid in protecting all forms of expression against the government and corporate suppression.

### How do Blockchain, cryptocurrency, and web3 work?

Blockchain is a decentralized platform that allows users to create and share digital contracts. Cryptocurrencies are digital or virtual tokens that use cryptography to secure their transactions and control the creation of new units. Web 3 is a term that describes the next generation of web architecture that will allow for more efficient and transparent communication between nodes in a distributed network.
