In *Distributed Machine Learning with PySpark: Migrating Effortlessly from Pandas and Scikit-Learn* (Apres, November 2023), we demonstrated how transitioning from small data libraries like Pandas and Scikit-Learn to PySpark can be relatively straightforward. Although Pandas and Scikit-Learn are primarily designed for small data processing and analysis, transitioning is made easier by the similarity in syntax in many instances. PySpark offers functionality similar to Pandas and Scikit-Learn, including data preprocessing and feature engineering. The presence of these familiar functionalities in PySpark facilitates a smoother transition for data scientists accustomed to working with Pandas and Scikit-Learn.
