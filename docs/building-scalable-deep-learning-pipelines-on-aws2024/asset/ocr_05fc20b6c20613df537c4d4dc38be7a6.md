+   - Outcome: This is the target variable. It indicates whether the individual has diabetes or not. 1 means the person has diabetes, and 0 means the person does not have diabetes.

Notice that BloodPressure and BMI have rows with 0 values, indicating invalid readings. These will be excluded from the dataset during model training and evaluation.
