# Security Requirements

The Ponemon Institute calculated that in 2016 the average cost of a security breach to a company was US$4 million up from US$3.79 million in 2015. Breach prevention and notification is an increasingly costly proposition, with a 12 percent increase in per capita cost just since 2013. In addition to civil and criminal sanctions, security breaches can have far reaching consequences for companies in terms of loss of customer confidence and trust, customer churn, and loss of revenue, market share, brand and shareholder value.
