+   - It is considered as an emerging 'Knowledge Hub' with the establishment of various educational institutes such as IIT and IIMs.
+   - Various computer hardware associations such as Gandhinagar Computer Hardware Association (GCHA) and Gujarat Informatics ltd (GIL) have their presence in Gandhinagar.
+   - Gandhinagar has been selected as one of the 100 Indian cities to be developed as a smart city under PM Narendra Modi's flagship Smart Cities Mission.
