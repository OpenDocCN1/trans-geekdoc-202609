# QUADCOPTER:

When a multirotor is designed with four rotor blades, then it becomes a quadcopter. These devices are usually controlled by specially designed brushless type DC motors. Two of the motors are used to move in a clockwise direction whereas the other two run in the counter clockwise direction. It helps to decide a safe landing for the quadcopter. The source of battery for such devices uses to be a lithium polymer battery.

# HEXACOPTER:

A hexacopter will serve you for many potential applications with its six motor mechanism where three work in the clockwise direction and the other three move in the anti-clock wise direction. Hence, these devices are able to gain higher lifting power as compared to quadcopters. You need not worry about its mechanism as it is designed to serve as an extremely safe landing craft.
