# Autonomous Cars

# INTRODUCTION

Autonomous cars are a class of self-driving vehicles and are also termed as driverless cars. Advances in the battery storage and energy density technologies coupled with Artificial Intelligence and Machine learning-powered applications like image recognition, object identification, analytics and advanced search are enabling the technologies that have always been in the realm of celestial objects and satellites or in the low range toys to be extended to the vehicles on the ground that are used for day-to-day purposes. In this chapter, we will embark on a journey to understand the various components of the autonomous cars and see how they help these agents of automation to serve a number of benefits to humanity and what lies ahead of us in this domain.
