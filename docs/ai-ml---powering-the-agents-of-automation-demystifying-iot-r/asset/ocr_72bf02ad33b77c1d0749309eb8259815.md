# HOW DOES A DRONE WORK?

The working procedure of a drone may appear simple to most of you but the device is actually carrying a complex technology inside. Before we discuss the concept of a drones’ working, you must know the fact that differentiate between drones and its ancestors the helicopters. The former one can fly independently whereas the later needs a pilot for directions.

Actually, it is a specially designed multi propeller system inside a drone that makes this device highly independent and also assists in reduction of failures. One important thing to note about this multi propeller system is that even if any motor inside this device stops working; it will keep on flying as it gets support from propellers that are working in the group. Drones that possess a large number of motors inside are able to gain more control over their elevation and hence can carry more loads during flight. These propellers get their power from a dedicated source and most of these devices contain removable batteries so that they can stay in air for a long time. The flight time can be extended with use of powerful batteries in design.
