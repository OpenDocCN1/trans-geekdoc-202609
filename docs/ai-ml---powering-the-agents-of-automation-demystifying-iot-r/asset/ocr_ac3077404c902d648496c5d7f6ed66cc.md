# Drones

# INTRODUCTION:

Drones are the flying manifestations of AI & and ML-based technology advancements. From the limited range, battery controlled flying devices with limited range, drones are now available with most advanced features and are taking part in a number of complex projects and use cases; thanks to the advancements in the AI and ML-led applications.

For many, the drones are black boxes and many still wonder how such tiny objects flying far above in the air are able to undertake such complex array of functions performing a variety of activities.

In this chapter, we will take a close look at the varieties of drones and their applications in various domains.
