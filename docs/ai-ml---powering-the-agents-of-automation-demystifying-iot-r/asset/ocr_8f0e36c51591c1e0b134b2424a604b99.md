From ATMs of the yesteryears to the advanced voice activated personal assistants that understand our needs and moods to respond are replacing many jobs that were hitherto being done by human beings. This is a growing and irreversible trend of these manifestations of automation.

It is possible that in the future that when you pass by an ATM in a bank, it identifies you by name, recognizes your need for a loan and offers you attractive terms on the spot while enticing you to its location using a voice activated and geolocation targeted message. This then would be the foreseeable future of the banking as we know. The same can be true for any area as the power of AI is considerably enhanced with the confluence of Big data, Cloud and Machine learning technologies.
