Sasubilli and Kumar have studied that farmers have limited access to agricultural specialists who can inspect crops for diseases and offer advice on diseases [21]. They frequently receive delayed professional responses or advice that could be meaningless in the end. Automatic plant disease detection is crucial because it allows for the monitoring of vast fields of crops and recognizes the presence of disease symptoms on the plant leaves or stems. This project intends to offer quick, precise, simple-to-use, and affordable solutions to farmers. Mobile apps were developed for automatically identifying plant diseases using image processing techniques [22].
