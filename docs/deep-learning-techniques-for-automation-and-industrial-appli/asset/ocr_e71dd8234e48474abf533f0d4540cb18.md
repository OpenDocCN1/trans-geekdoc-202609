Individuals’ everyday decisions are influenced by their emotions. Emotional issues may also have a negative impact on human communication, attention, and memory. Although human beings can always recognize and understand emotions with ease, computers have a difficult time doing these tasks. Effective computing refers to techniques for detecting, identifying, and forecasting human emotions, such as happiness, rage, sorrow, trust, surprise, and anticipation, in order to modify computer systems accordingly. The ensuing computer systems are able to assist in decision-making for an individual’s emotional state as well as demonstrate empathy [10]. Twitter is a popular social network and microblogging platform where users may publish messages in the form of tweets, each of which can include up to 280 characters. Currently, 321 million people have registered on Twitter; 34.4 million of them are in India, with one of them is currently active. As a consequence, news reports are created on a variety of topics, including major news. During the 2016 US Presidential Elections, Twitter was the primary source of news articles.

Twitter has had an enormous impact on Indian politics because its success has always largely depended on its ability to communicate with the masses. It was commonly used as a means to express political thinking and engage with the common person. While there are very few Twitter users relative to the overall Indian population, the educated classes, politicians, actors, and other famous people form part of those statistics. Such “influencers” are likely to manipulate their followers’ thoughts and views based on their tweets.
