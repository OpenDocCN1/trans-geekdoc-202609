### Logistic regression.

For COVID-19 detection, several supervised learning algorithms are available, including logistic regression, SVMs, decision trees, and random forests. Logistic regression is a widely used binary classification algorithm that predicts the likelihood of a patient being infected or not [2]. SVMs are another popular algorithm that identifies the hyperplane that distinguishes infected and non-infected patients in the feature space. Decision trees and random forests are tree-based algorithms that recursively split the feature space based on the most informative features [5].

### Deep learning.

Supervised deep learning methods can also be utilized for COVID-19 detection [3]. CNNs are highly effective in image-based diagnosis, especially for medical images like chest X-rays or CT scans. CNNs learn to extract hierarchical representations of the input images and identify COVID-19-specific features, enabling them to detect COVID-19 pneumonia accurately. Meanwhile, RNNs are suitable for analyzing time-series data, such as a patient’s vital signs and symptoms. RNNs can model the temporal dependencies in the data and predict the probability of a patient having COVID-19 [6].
