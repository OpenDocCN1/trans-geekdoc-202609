Through the analysis of the features, their ambiguities are reduced, and the location of the facial feature is extracted.

The image-based approach [9–12] discussed in the previous section involves face detection through explicit modeling of facial features. This approach has faced challenges related to uncertainty in face detection and variations in environmental conditions. However, recent advancements in component-based projects have enhanced the capability to mitigate this uncertainty, with a focus primarily on head-and-shoulder and semi-frontal faces.

The AdaBoost classifier [13–15] is a combination of weak or less accurate classifiers. These classifiers are combined, and their weights are assigned. Then, each of them is trained with the sample data in each iteration with the expectation that this iterative process will lead to higher accuracy ratings.

# ### 7.3 Proposed Work

We propose a surveillance framework for individual counting via face detection.

+   1. HOG + Haar feature extraction
+   2. Application of the AdaBoost classifier
+   3. Application of the Kanade–Lucas–Tomasi (KLT) tracker
+   4. Face detection by counting the number of faces in a frame
