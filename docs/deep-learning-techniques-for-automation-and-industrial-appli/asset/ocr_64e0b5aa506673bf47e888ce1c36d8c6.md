(e) Model Selection: The next step is to select a suitable OCR model. Tesseract is a popular open-source OCR engine that can be trained on a variety of datasets. The model selection depends on the specific requirements of the project, such as the language of the text, the font style, and the image quality.

(f) Post-Processing: The final step is to post-process the OCR output to correct any errors and improve the accuracy.
