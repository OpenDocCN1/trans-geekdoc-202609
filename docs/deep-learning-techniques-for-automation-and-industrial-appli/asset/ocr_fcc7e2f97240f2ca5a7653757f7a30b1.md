# 3.3.1 Image Preprocessing

By ensuring that parameters, such as image size, meet the demands of model training and decrease noise during image collection, the number of datasets may need to be increased for preprocessing images and unifying features, such as image size and color. The more commonly used picture-preprocessing techniques are resizing, padding resizing, and letterboxing, as well as image vector normalization. The most common picture alteration techniques are flipping, blurring, enhancing colors, enhancing edge detection, logarithmic transformation, and image denoising. By implementing image processing, we have converted images into 32-bit batches. The dataset was divided into two distinct categories for the purpose of training and testing.
