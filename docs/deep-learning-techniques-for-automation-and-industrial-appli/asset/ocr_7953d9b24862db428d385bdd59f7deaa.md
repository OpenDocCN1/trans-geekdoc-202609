Sarcasm is a unique type of emotion that normally alters the direction of the opinion for a given text or social media tweets. Human beings regularly use strong apparent pressure and nonverbal cues, such as eye movement and hand motion, to convey sarcasm in spoken discussions. These apparent and gestural signs are clearly absent while communicating non-verbal sarcasm like social media tweets or post which is generally in textual form, that makes sarcasm detection a complicated job. Most of the time, humans feel trouble to comprehend sarcasm in text. In general, sarcastic sentences look like positive sentences, the overall significance becomes negative because of the presence of sarcasm.
