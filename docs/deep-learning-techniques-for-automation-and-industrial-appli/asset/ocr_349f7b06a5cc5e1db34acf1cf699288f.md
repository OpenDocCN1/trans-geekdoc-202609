# 6.3.4 Fusion Techniques

This technique takes two or more inputs from different adapted versions of dehazing techniques and yields better output [30]. The fusion of contrast enhancement and white balancing is implemented based on per-pixel fashion [31]. Lastly, to reduce artifacts, laplacian pyramid representation is applied. Optimized, fast, single dehazing method includes fusion of different techniques. To determine air light value, dark channel prior is performed followed by white balancing to retain the actual color. Finally, contrast stretching is performed to enhanced contrast of output [32]. The fusion technique proposed is based on encoder and decoder architecture. It involves fusion of contrast enhancement, white balancing and, lastly, gamma correction. The convolutional neural network involves fusion of two generative adversarial networks (GAN) [33]. The outdoor unpaired real dataset is trained by cycleGAN, while cGAN preserves the detail of the scene. These techniques work well for both real and synthetic datasets [34].
