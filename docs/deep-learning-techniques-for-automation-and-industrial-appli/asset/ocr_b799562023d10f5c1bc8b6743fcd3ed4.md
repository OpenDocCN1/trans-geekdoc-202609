Groundwater storage level fluctuations are time-dependent [8]. Hence, knowing the water availability, extraction, and recharge throughout the year can help understand this variation year over year.

# ### 9.1.1 Why Is It Difficult to Measure Subsurface Groundwater?

Surface water measurement methods are easy to execute as they are in the visible range, and equipment or sensors can be implemented comparatively easily. Maps can be drawn, and reports can be generated. Groundwater-related studies require subsurface measurements with the help of test well drilling, *in situ* borehole lithology, other data logging, etc. There are alternate approaches such as geophysical observation of parameters like density, resistivity, conductivity, electromagnetic, or radioactivity using various methods. These methods are time-consuming, costly, and require a plethora of equipment and skills to carry out a single aquifer region study.

Some of these approaches use refraction- and reflection-based seismic surveys, gravity-based surveying, induced rock-based geomagnetic surveying, electromagnetic field surveying with or without the use of ground penetrating radar, vertical electrical sounding (VES) sounding-based electrical surveying, and borehole petrophysics studies involving well data logging [12].

# ### 9.1.2 What are High Level Tasks Involved in Groundwater Measurement?

Investigation of groundwater hydrology is a time-consuming and cumbersome process. It involves the following steps [9]:

+   - Site selection and selection of geophysical methods
+   - Study of the hydrological site characteristics
+   - Conduct of tests like slugging and pumping
+   - Well-placement monitoring
+   - Drilling and subsurface sampling
+   - Water well design, installation, and development
+   - Well and borehole maintenance, rehabilitation, and abandonment
+   - Well tests and aquifer tests
+   - Groundwater sampling and analysis
+   - Characterization of groundwater bodies
+   - Groundwater assessment, data analysis, and reporting
+   - Design framework of groundwater monitoring networks
+   - Statistics for groundwater quality comparison
