## 13.2.3 Optimization Design in the Page System

The design layout of the app interactive interface should attach importance to functional area division. The processing page without standardization [8] or methodization will lead to chaos on the page, causing basic art processing and aesthetic feelings. This prevents effective information transmission on the page, and users will feel numb as well as helpless during interaction, which will affect app efficiency. In terms of a network shopping app, its interface parts can be classified into advertisement zones, category menu zones, search zones, function service zones, and member information zones. When these service types are distributed, a vision processing method should be applied to distinguish between them in order to avoid confusion between content and service so that users can quickly understand the plate content on the shopping app between various services and various layers.

Most mobile shopping apps follow the layout of a web page. Figure 13.4 shows the left sidebar information classification, the top fast search frame, a large graph banner promoted advertisement, and specific classification commodity activity promotion. Since the amount of commodity information on such a comprehensive platform is large and page layout has been standardized, uniformity between page layout at a mobile terminal and layout at a webpage terminal can largely reduce people’s learning costs. It is easy for people to use, and visual image consistency between the webpage terminal and the mobile terminal can also be saved.

Compared to comprehensive platform shopping apps, there is not much commodity information in mobile shopping apps, and they are more flexible in page layout design. There are not many color designs on product pages, and they are all based on product content. Meanwhile, a suitable blank promotes the page to be permeable. The blank separates the picture from other elements, and plays a role in guiding sight and setting up the design hierarchy to stress the key point. Vision is also transferred to elements surrounding the blank, and elements increase the impact of vision. Therefore, for shopping app interface design [9], since it is not limited to large amounts of commodity information, it is more flexible in page layout design to increase creativity. Towards the characteristics and location design of products, more corresponding layout forms in brand quality will be designed. Personalized layout design usually brings a creative vision.

## 13.3 Case Analysis

The iOS platform obtains apps through the App Store, so interface display at the App Store should not be ignored during interface design. Its brand is the first impression for clients, so it is an important reference for customers to download or not. Figure 13.5 shows the display interface of the brand Fashion Group in the app store. If considering different forms of cross-terminals, brand recognition coordination must be followed.

After entering the brand app, the guiding interface first exhales, but the guiding interface is usually the most easily one-step-escaping interface. If it is poorly designed, it is very easy for users to reduce interest in the subject content, even by directly deleting the app. A powerful vision impact enhances the user’s initial recognition memory of content. Therefore, during the design of the guiding interface, some hand-painted modeling will be made into secondary function buttons in a powerful comparison of pictures in order to enhance interest and attention attraction. Figure 13.6 adopts a waterfall form to display the picture using interactive technology [10] so that users can constantly discover new pictures. This unique-styled version design can reflect brand product characteristics at various terminals and reach the vision impact in guiding interface.
