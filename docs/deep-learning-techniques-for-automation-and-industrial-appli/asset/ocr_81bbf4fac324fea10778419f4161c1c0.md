# 2.3 Literature Survey

As explored in prior studies [1], various types of convolutional neural network (CNN) models were made to detect plant diseases with the help of deep learning. The database used here is an open database comprising approximately 90,000 images with 58 classes and 25 different types of plant leaves. Different types of CNN model architectures were made for the identification of plant diseases, like AlexNet, AlexNetOWBn, GoogLeNet, Overfeat, and visual geometry group (VGG) [1]. Although all these models show accuracy greater than 97%, the success rate of VGG turned out to be 99.53%. The improvement here can be made by choosing a variety of training data, such as data from different locations (e.g., fields), and data from different cultivation conditions, and also by making an integrated mobile application [2].

There are two types of image augmentation strategies: the traditional approach and Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) [2]. The second approach, GAN, helped in making synthetic data based on the already existing dataset so that the accuracy could be improved. The models that were used here are AlexNet, VGG19, Inception version 3, DenseNet201, and ResNet50 [3]. However, the models we used couldn’t effectively handle testing images with complex backgrounds, such as cases where leaves appeared in the background of some images.
