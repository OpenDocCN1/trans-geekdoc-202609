# 4.4.5 Xception Family

Xception is an architectural enhancement of Inception that uses depth-wise separable convolutions to replace regular Inception modules [20]. When it came to testing, the Xception model was 60% accurate. At first, the model's training and validation accuracy were similar, but as the epoch progressed, the gap between the training and validation accuracy widened (Figure 4.10(a)).
