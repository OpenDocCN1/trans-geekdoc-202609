# 8.3  Experimental Results and Analysis

All experiments are done with the help of Keras and TensorFlow libraries with GPU provided by the Kaggle online platform. The experiments are carried out on 6,000 pairs of training and 1,000 pairs of validation images. Further testing is done on 3,000 pairs of images. XuNet is not a pretrained model; thus, in this work, it is trained for a large number of epochs with a mini-batch size of 64 images. The same setting is used as described by the authors [20] for the initialization of the XuNet network. The authors in ref. [32] have discussed that in the mini-batches of training, cover and their corresponding stego images known as pair constraint (PC), can prove to be detrimental for network training in the last stages but may work better in the initial stages for convergence of the network. In this work, 100 epochs are used with the PC and a learning rate of 0.001 for XuNet. After that, images are shuffled in subsequent epochs to create mini-batches, and a mini-batch of size 64 is picked. Five hundred epochs are used to train XuNet with an initial learning rate of 0.001. The next 500 epochs are used with a learning rate of 0.0001.

For pretrained models, 50 epochs are also used with the PC and a learning rate of 0.001. After that, 100 epochs are trained with shuffled images and a learning rate of 0.0001, and the last 100 epochs with shuffled images and a learning rate of 0.00001. These learning rate values are used by trying a few in every set of epochs. A lesser number of epochs than Xu-Net are used as these are pretrained models.
