# ### 5.2.1 Literature Review of Sarcasm Detection Based on Data Analysis Without Machine Learning Algorithms

Manohar and Kulkarni (2017) state that persons use social media platform like Twitter to communicate their personal views about different subjects. Work done by them indicated that sarcasm can be distinguished effectively by utilizing specific corpus methodology. According to them, there is no such need of any type of Machine Learning algorithm, which is utilized rather corpus of active words were made and scoring based classification is done to distinguish sarcastic sentences. Finally, a comparison is made between input text and stored action words and analysed sentiment, after that; they perform sentiment aggregation over compared result for identification of sentence is sarcastic or not. In the sequence of sarcasm detection author Rendalkar and Chandankhede (2018) worked on a scoring-based methodology. Instead of making a stored corpus of active words, in this approach each word in the sentence will be scored and included, after completion of all the words in the sentence, last score will decide the general estimation of the sentence. SentiWordNet tool were used for determining scores. Many systems were utilized to distinguish sarcasm, like word and emotion-based detection, hybrid sarcasm detection which consolidates both word and emotion-based methodology sarcasm detection based on positive and negative circumstances, pattern based and interjection word beginning based sarcasm detection.

According to Bharti *et al.* (2015) detecting sarcasm is one of the most challenging tasks in natural language processing. In their work, they created two algorithms to be specific Parsing Based Dictionary Age calculation (PBLGA) and Interposition Word Start calculation (IWSA). The two calculations are structured in an unexpected way, initial one focuses more on notion and circumstance logical inconsistency while second calculation can distinguish sarcastic sentence immediately presence of the addition word in the start of the given sentence. Information related to #sarcasm was collected from Twitter in the efficient way. In this approach all types of algorithms have its own significance under specific circumstance, but it is not work efficiently when sarcastic sentences contain interjection words in the centre of the sentence. The majority of the researchers work on the tweets which are gathered and stored, yet Bharti *et al.* (2016) worked on real time streamed tweets utilizing a Hadoop system. Various methodologies were utilized to recognize sarcasm, for example, PBLGA, IWSA, positive sentiment with antonym pair (PSWAP), tweets reflect to widespread realities, tweets which are negating to time subordinate realities.
