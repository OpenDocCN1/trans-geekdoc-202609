# 11.8 Green Software

A sustainable green software algorithm is designed to perform efficiently in the environment with reduced GHG emissions. Green software aims to reduce CO₂ emissions in an environment, which is an impact caused by the software. A green and sustainable strategy to reduce energy costs will deal with infrastructure, workload distribution, and habits of individuals. The design of the database will decrease the waiting time for queries and tire architecture of applications and cloud-based data centers—these are all ways we could design, develop, and deploy green software. We have information about how much carbon was released in India until 2021 by looking at the world development indicators in the World Bank database. This data reveals the extent of carbon dioxide emissions in India, which is a matter of concern for our community.
