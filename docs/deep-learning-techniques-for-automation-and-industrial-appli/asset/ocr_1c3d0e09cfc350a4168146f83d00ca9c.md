# 13.1 Introduction

In recent years, deep learning has emerged as a powerful subset of machine learning, and its applications have extended to various domains, including computer vision, natural language processing, and recommendation systems. As mobile shopping apps continue to evolve, deep learning techniques present opportunities to augment and enhance collaborative design and case analysis processes. This chapter explores the intersection of collaborative design and case analysis of mobile shopping apps with deep learning. We delve into the potential applications of deep learning in these processes and its impact on improving user experiences, enhancing personalization, and optimizing business outcomes.

Along with technological constant progress, the internet and mobile phones gradually enter our lives and permeate many aspects of them. When the “Internet Plus” action plan is proposed, various apps constantly appear, and the mobile internet industry unprecedentedly and vigorously develops. From the perspective of the user’s experience, this article discusses, and studies related theories and problems of shopping app interactive design. Furthermore, it adopts big data acquisition from the Internet to analyze the commercial status and the tendency of mobile Internet, obtain an initial conclusion of mobile commercial development, and study further interactive design based on this conclusion. The chapter also studies the representation form of visualization to be applied in interactive brand apps, and puts forward interactively featured design rules for visualization in the brand app field. Meanwhile, it performs case analysis for current brand apps in more mature domestic mobile shopping enterprises and interprets their design framework and details by one of the two most popular retail platforms on today’s [2] mobile devices. As such, they represent the most developed examples of shopping apps for mobile devices, which may be used to study trends in contemporary interactive design.
