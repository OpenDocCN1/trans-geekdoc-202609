# ## 14.1 Introduction

The COVID-19 pandemic has caused unprecedented challenges globally in public health and economy, highlighting the need for effective and efficient diagnostic tools. Traditional diagnostic methods for COVID-19, such as polymerase chain reaction (PCR) tests, can be time-consuming, expensive, and require specialized equipment and expertise. Machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) techniques have emerged as promising tools for COVID-19 detection, providing the potential for accurate and reliable results that are faster, cheaper, and less dependent on specialized expertise. The COVID-19 pandemic has caused a global health crisis, and timely detection and diagnosis of the disease are essential for effective control and management. Both techniques have shown great promise in COVID-19 detection, enabling faster and more accurate diagnosis, and facilitating early intervention and treatment. These techniques can be broadly categorized into supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning [1–3].
