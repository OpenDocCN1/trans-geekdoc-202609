## 2.2 Exploration on Blockchain Application in Railway Field

In the railway field, some countries have also actively explored the blockchain application. DB, a German railway, cooperated with the blockchain integration platform Unibright to develop a tagging ecosystem based on the blockchain. The ecosystem has connected the different areas of the passengers' whole journey. Every part of the journey would be marked, such as mileage, service, catering, hotel reservation, local transportation, etc. Aiming to reduce the operation cost through blockchain application, the ecosystem has realized the cooperation with other tourism company interaction. RZD, a Russian railway transport company, has launched a cryptocurrency ticketing based on blockchain and a railway freight digital trading platform based on blockchain. In August 2017, more than 200 well-known freight and logistics enterprises, such as BNSF railway company, UPS, FedEx and Schneider cargo, joined the BITA alliance. BITA became the world largest blockchain business alliance. In addition, railway companies in Switzerland, the Netherlands, Canada and other countries have also begun to introduce blockchain and cryptocurrency into the railway transportation industry.

## 2.3 Problems in Blockchain Application

With blockchain becoming more and more mature, there are still some problems.
- blockchain may cause financial institutions or enterprises to face complex legal and compliance issues.
- the current distributed ledger technology is still immature, which needs more industry applications to prove [5].
- There are many technical routes of blockchain. Different types of blockchains are easy to form information isolated chains.
- Decentralization has a great impact on the existing operation pattern. There is no internal driving force for active change in related fields.
- The sustainability of basic technology maintenance of open-source blockchain is poor.
- Abuse and widespread use are obvious.
