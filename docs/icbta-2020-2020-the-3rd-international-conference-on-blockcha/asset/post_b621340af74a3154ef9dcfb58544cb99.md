## ABSTRACT

The block chain has many unique characteristics, like decentralization, stability, openness and transparency. We can apply block chain in many fields of railway to improve the comprehensive efficiency and benefit, such as railway passenger and freight service, engineering construction, big data sharing and so on. With the gradual completion of data service platform, big data lake and cloud computing in China Railway, these foundations can support the railway block chain application. The paper analyzed the development status and existing problems of block chain at home and abroad. Through the multidimensional analysis for the mainstream block chain platform architecture, the thesis gets the architecture selection of the railway block chain platform. Based on the hyperledger fabric, we propose the technical architecture and overall scheme for the railway block chain service platform respectively. The paper also designs the implementation path for the platform application construction. This research plays an important role and significance in promoting the block chain technology applying and developing in the railway field.

## 1 INTRODUCTION

With the new round of scientific revolution and industrial reform sweeping the world now, new technologies such as big data, cloud computing, internet of things, artificial intelligence, block chain are emerging and penetrating rapidly. Digital economy has deeply changed the way of human life and industrial production. As one disruptive technology, block chain is promoting the global transition from Information Internet to Value Internet. The block chain applications have the capacities to reduce costs and improve efficiency for the real economy. They can also boost the traditional industries to development well and hasten the transformation and upgrading.

In general, we divide the development process of block chain into 6 stages from 2009 to 2025: technology test, geek niche, market brewing, mainstream acceptance, industrial lending and industrial maturity. Block chain develops from block chain 1.0 to block chain 4.0, as shown in Figure 1. The rapid developments of related technologies and single application promote the wide application of block chain in industrial world.

As the artery of the national economy and the popular means of transportation, the railway transportation has made remarkable achievements after the informatization construction during more than 30 years. China Railway has built up the integrated information integration platform and multiple business information systems, which cover the passenger and freight services, business management, dispatching command, safety supervision and other fields. Recent years, China Railway has also carried out some innovative applications using the big data and artificial intelligence based on the massive data assets. These efforts can provide the enough support for the block chain application. At the same time, China Railway is also facing the challenges under new situations and circumstances, like reducing costs and increasing efficiency. The railway system has complex features, such as the large scale, the long business chain and many involved fields. The block chain technology can reduce the supervision and management costs under the certain circumstance of railway [1]. Integrated application of block chain can be beneficial to reduce the overall cost of railway passenger and freight transportation and even the whole society. That will promote the high-quality development for the railway in the new period.

The thesis comprehensively analyzed the application and development status of block chain technology at home and abroad. We summarized the problems existing in the stage of block chain industrial application. After studying the architecture and application of mainstream block chain platform, the paper selected the technical architecture for the railway block chain service platform. ## 2 BLOCK CHAIN APPLICATION STATUS AND RAILWAY FIELD EXPLORATION

### 2.1 Global Block Chain Application Status Analysis

Most countries have positive attitudes towards the block chain technology, while the industrial development stages are different. Australia, South Korea, Germany, Netherlands, Cyprus, the United Arab Emirates and Maltese have actively developed the block chain industry and set up the overall development strategy nationally. The United States, China, South Korea, the United Kingdom, Australia and the European Union attach great importance to the research and application of block chain. At the same time, China, Australia, France, Switzerland and Finland have successively developed industry laws and rules on block chain supervision [2], as shown in Figure 2 [3].

In application, block chain, which is derived from encrypted digital currency, is extending to many fields. Developed countries have carried out many practices on block chain [4]. The United States, Estonia and Dubai have promoted the digital transformation of government actively. Japan, Australia and Germany have used block chain to explore financial cost reduction and efficiency improvement. Switzerland and the United States have tried to use block chain to promote property registration and transaction distribution. Singapore and Switzerland have used block chain in the trade and logistics to provide customers the more convenient delivery paradigm. The United Kingdom and the United States have applied block chain to engage in social and livelihood undertakings to enlarge the benefits of public funds.
