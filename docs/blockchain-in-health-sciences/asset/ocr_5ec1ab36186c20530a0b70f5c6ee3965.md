# Role of Blockchain in Bioinformatics and Healthcare

Blockchain-based technology in bioinformatics and healthcare is a fast developing technology that can transform the healthcare sector. Bioinformatics applies data analytic tools to biological data Blockchain technology ensures secure and transparent data management methods [26, 74]. Blockchain applications in healthcare include safeguarding patient data, generating single patient records, improving medical supply, and optimizing the supply chain. As a result, by incorporating blockchain technology into healthcare, healthcare providers and patients may conveniently manage their medical data, while those suffering from chronic illnesses can readily save their records. The technology enables the secure exchange of medical records, and a patient's record can be saved and shared from a single location, removing the need for patients to maintain track of different papers [24]. Blockchain technology provides an unchangeable record of activities. This eliminates mediators and expensive solutions, lowering expenses.
