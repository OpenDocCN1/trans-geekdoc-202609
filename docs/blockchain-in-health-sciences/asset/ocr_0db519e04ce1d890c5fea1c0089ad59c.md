# 1.2.4 Blockchain 4.0

The fourth generation of blockchain, also known as Blockchain 4.0, is centered on improving aspects related to privacy and governance. Privacy is prioritized with the use of cryptography by hiding the identity of participants and details of transactions. Enhancing the autonomy of blockchain networks through decentralized decision-making procedures is the goal of governance improvements being investigated. Blockchain 4.0 introduces strategies and tactics to adapt blockchain technology to meet corporate needs, aligning with the demands of Industry 4.0.

Table 1.1 Difference between different blockchains.

| Type | Use for | Speed | Consensus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blockchain 1.0 | Used for Financial Transactions only | Slow | Consensus done through Proof of work |
| Blockchain 2.0 | Use of Smart Contracts | Faster due to smart contract | Consensus done through Delegated Proof of work |
| Blockchain 3.0 | Improved transaction speed with privacy | Faster than 2.0 due to in-built verification | Consensus done through Proof of Stake and Authority |
| Blockchain 4.0 | Aligned with Industry 4.0 | Faster than previous versions due to Automated Verification | Consensus done through Proof of Integrity |
