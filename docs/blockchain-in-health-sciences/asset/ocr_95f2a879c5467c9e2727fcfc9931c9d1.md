# 13.5 Challenges

There is a noticeable increase in the implementation of blockchain within the healthcare sector. Rapid technological innovation facilitated by a variety of government-integrated facilities is propelling market expansion. The pharmaceutical industry has been profoundly impacted by Blockchain technology. Blockchain may retain transaction records immutably and function as a public ledger. This characteristic may be used to create transparent permission management systems that encourage the exchange of data. There are various challenges [15] in using blockchain for pharmaceutical research, some of them are addressed here.

# 13.5.1 Throughput

More checks will need to be made as the amount of transactions in the network increases, potentially creating congestion in the system. High value of throughput is a challenge in healthcare systems because delays in access could hurt a life-saving diagnosis [16]. There are several elements that might have an impact on the scalability and performance of a framework. One of these factors is the requirements for the system receiving continual updates [17], keyword set size, network configuration, and disk space required depending on the kind of blockchain utilized by the framework. In a similar vein, [18] proposes that increased performance-related costs may be mitigated by adding certain capabilities to already-existing systems, such as global smart transactions.
