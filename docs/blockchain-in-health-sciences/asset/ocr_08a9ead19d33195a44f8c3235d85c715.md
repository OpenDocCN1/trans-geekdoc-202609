Pharmacogenomics tailors drug prescriptions to patient genetic profiles using genomic data. Genomic analysis finds disease biomarkers by identifying genetic markers [55–57]. Large datasets reveal patterns and signatures that may be diagnostic or prognostic markers for diseases, enabling more accurate and early disease detection and targeted medical therapies. However, combining bioinformatics with AI and ML algorithms has substantially improved disease detection and prediction in the healthcare sector [57, 58]. These technologies can help to construct prediction models that can detect illness, forecast outcomes, and aid in early discovery. Bioinformatics allows for the rapid processing and interpretation of large-scale biological data in drug discovery, facilitating identification, lead compound optimization, and drug-target interaction prediction, all of which can be valuable in the healthcare sector [59]. Drug discovery and development include pharmacology and toxicology, making disease and drug effects and toxicological assessments. Recently, the pharmaceutical business has implemented more projects with fewer staff and high-throughput methods for identifying promising ideas. Public health surveillance, a subset of bioinformatics, collects, analyzes, and interprets health data for public health management. Planning, executing, and analyzing public health efforts require constant, systematic health data collection, evaluation, and understanding. Public health surveillance systems track emerging health issues at the population level, such as infectious diseases and other diseases, protects populations from health emergencies, monitor and evaluate interventions, and inform public health policy and strategies [62, 63]. Data integration and analysis play an important role in healthcare, where integrating multiple biological data types is critical for identifying variables in health and designing personalized treatment. Bioinformatics techniques are useful for extracting genetic variants from raw sequencing and protein interaction data, allowing for a more comprehensive understanding of disease processes and therapy alternatives [64].
