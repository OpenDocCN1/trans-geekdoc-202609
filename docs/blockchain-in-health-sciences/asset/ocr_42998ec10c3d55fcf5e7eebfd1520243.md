# Locality (Rural and Urban)

In terms of locality, significant differences in perception were observed according to the data in Table 5.4, where the calculated value (29.097) is greater than the table value (2.59 at the 0.01 level). This can be explained by the fact that rural students suddenly transitioned to online education due to Covid-19 and have enjoyed the benefits of technology, resulting in a relatively high mean value (63.86). On the other hand, the mean value of urban students (52.23) is relatively low, as they have experiencing both the advantages and disadvantages of technology for a longer period and have been cautious in adopting blockchain technology as a whole.

# Institution (General and Engineering)

According to the institution of UG students, a significant difference in perception has been observed regarding the impact of blockchain on data security and healthcare. It can be said that the reason for this difference is that engineering students have eagerly accepted blockchain, resulting in a mean value (65.26) that is higher than the mean value (52.75) of general students.
