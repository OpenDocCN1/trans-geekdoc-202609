## 20.5 Current Blockchain-Based Approaches and Methods in Genomics

The next section delves deeper into all the erstwhile practices, approaches, and procedures that recent research leverages, thereby flaunting blockchain technology to propel advancements in genomics research. We will go across studies from different years and depict how this field keeps developing.

###ngthening Data Security and Access Control:

- [29]: In this paper, a robust Blockchain-based architecture is proposed for the safe management of genetic information. This work, therefore, stresses the introduction of encryption procedures to protect private information. The design also reflects user control features in the decision on parties who can view their genetic data. Researchers in this work also mention the responsibilities of physicians in the administration and storage of information related to patients in the database. This work shall be implemented using the Ethereum Framework, which ensures that performing transactions through this network consumes minimal resources while also ensuring security in the processing of information.

- [30]: A new form of AI working decentralizedly: Swarm Learning. This new form of AI in medicine is especially fitting for medical information because large amounts of data are stored locally on individual devices. Level-high data security and secure integration of new members into this decentralized structure—no central authority is needed—it eliminates the need for an authority that could compromise the data privacy of those concerned.

- [31]: The authors within this paper describe implementing an Ethereum blockchain-based framework for a shared system in the study of cancer genomics. The proof is given to the model about the effectiveness and some potential gains of open sharing of de-identified patient data and its XML files to facilitate collaboration among researchers and to speed up cancer treatment discoveries and personalized medicine.

- [32]: This paper aims to prove the potential of Corda—a Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)—with respect to secure data sharing in a healthcare setting. The research is focused on melanoma studies and uses a machine learning technique called “one-shot learning” for the prediction of mutations that can cause the disease. Therefore, the secure point-to-point communication through Corda avoids exposure of any attribute connected with the patients during live processing and analysis.

- [41]: The study of this paper offers a method for utilizing blockchain technology to enhance data security and access management in DNA databases. Two unique components of our technology ensure data privacy: private blockchains and robust encryption throughout. However, NFTs serve as individual IDs for each person's data, giving them the ability to manage who can access them and perhaps make money from their use. This technology provides a secure, decentralized platform for information sharing while also resolving issues with outdated systems.

- [42]: The DARC (Decentralized Anonymous Researcher Credentials) protocol allows researchers to protect their identities and selectively reveal credentials, hence strengthening data security and access control in federated genomic data. This method makes use of zero-knowledge proofs to guarantee that researchers disclose only the information required to access data and smart contracts to manage data access regulations. DARC gives researchers control over credentials and privacy by doing away with the requirement for central identity providers.

- [43]: They propose a blockchain-enabled sharing platform, BEST, for the safe and patient-controlled sharing of PHR among healthcare practitioners. The BEST platform ensures fine-grained permission management, strong security, and privacy using blockchain access control that could throttle insider threats. The facilities it gives for automatic security and privacy controls logarithmically increase their functionality through event rules. Since this is scalable, updating the access permissions in hundreds of PHRs takes milliseconds only; hence, the performance is efficient. BEST makes a huge difference in the domains of data security and access control in the healthcare industry by fusing blockchain technology with state-of-the-art models of access control.

### Facilitating Secure Data Sharing and Traceability:

- [33]: This paper discusses the potential of blockchain decentralized technology for DNA sequencing data-intensive applications, especially in genomic research. The authors present a general architecture of e-health systems based on blockchain technology to solve the crucial challenges of data storage, sharing, and privacy issues in healthcare.

- [16]: It is focused on the challenge of confidential genomic data dissemination in settings of large-scale Genome-Wide Association Studies. The framework they have proposed will ensure that it allows individuals to still be in control of their data through a decentralized network rather than these central institutions. In this way, the approach provides better security and resistance to hacking attempts. Further, a built-in privacy-preserving sharing protocol within the network has also been implemented to avoid the unauthorized redistribution of a person’s data. This protocol fragments gene sequencing data and disperses among different service providers, further minimizing the chance of re-identification.

- [40]: The BioChainReward system makes use of blockchain technology for further traceability studies with secure data exchange attributed to biomedical research. In this paper, it is a decentralized and immutable system ensuring high control over privacy for the user through encryption, access control, and AI-powered methods of preserving privacy. This means that, in addition to security and privacy, the scalability of the framework ensures the effective management of increasing data volumes. An inventive feedback incentive structure motivates the participation of patients by rewarding for providing their data. This will further lock in BCR as a solution for the healthcare industry concerning security, privacy, and user participation in data data sharing.

###izing Efficiency and Scalability:

- [7]: It further explores ways of making efficient storage and querying of personal genomic information, such as personal genetic variants and raw sequencing reads. At the core of this technology, this paper proposes a private blockchain-enabled network with nested indexing and compression techniques along with databases that drastically bring efficiency improvements to lines of data storage and retrieval. This means shorter analysis times and lower memory costs, thus helping researchers achieve more from the stored data.

- [34]: The paper discusses the challenge of securing access logs for genomic data sets. MultiChain—a platform with inherent data stream abilities—is used to put forward solutions for efficient storage and querying of user activity data. This research is very essential in ensuring the integrity and auditability of access logs, and thus preventing unauthorized access to sensitive genomic data.
