# 12.5 Conclusion

There is no doubt that blockchain based pharmaceutical supply chain system may solve many inherent problems associated with traditional pharmaceutical supply chain system. However, the technical challenges of interoperability, inconsistent availability of downstream data, and many other factors within blockchain PSC management system can be solved by incorporating good drug manufacturing and distribution policies, frequent audits by governing bodies to maintain standards, and building good interfaces for smooth interoperability. Agreeing on common policies in terms of drug production, distribution, quality control, usage, profitability, maintaining intellectual property rights and many more details of business policy within a country, across a country and for worldwide is difficult due to many factors. For example, the nation economic condition may not be well-suited with the economy of leading global countries. That may hinder the effective applicability of the common policies and practices for maintaining standard, prices and other parameters to fulfill within a generalized PSC. These barriers may be overcome by incorporating blockchain for PSC within a country and interface for cross-border interoperability. Without incorporation of all local manufacturers within a state and country, the nation-level blockchain based PSC management system would not be possible to work flawlessly. For export and cross-border exchange of medicine and medical equipment in terms of donation, business or other means, parameters must be selected for resolving policy ambiguities. At that extent only, it is possible to implement blockchain technology in PSC for tracing expired drugs and counterfeit drugs within circulation. In addition, trust and transparency can also be facilitated by the PSC management system.
