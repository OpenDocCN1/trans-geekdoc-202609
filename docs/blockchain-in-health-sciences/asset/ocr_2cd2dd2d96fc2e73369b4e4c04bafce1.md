# 7.3.6.2 Challenges Encountered by AI Technology

Blockchain technology is also susceptible to some of the challenges that AI technology is experiencing at the moment. The government and Technical AI Organizations collaborate extensively with each other. A number of concerns are emerging as a consequence of the significant increase in different obstacles such as:

+   1. Widespread consumer privacy violation
2. Siloed and unshareable knowledge
3. Biased or tampered with AI systems
