One of the most popular and regulated technology in pharmaceutical trends is blockchain as it is helping to reduce many challenges of this sector as it is not open, not able to track the record of orders being placed and does not have trust for the shipping of drug products. There are many fake drugs available in the pharmaceutical market which can be monitored and controlled with the help of blockchain technology using a set of clear standards and safeguards. In this study, various advantages and disadvantages with blockchain in the pharmaceutical industry have been discussed. # 13.1 Introduction

Blockchain is popular for its unalterable approach, maintaining the record-keeping system and providing cryptographic security which gives rise to a new revolutionary system in pharmaceutical research. It improves the consistency of the trail data by providing a secure platform to manipulate and ensure the reliability of the findings. The integration of blockchain to the clinical trials is undoubtedly very transformative as it transforms various healthcare sectors. A number of pharmaceutical businesses are nowadays using the blockchain technology for enhancing the efficiency and transparency of their clinical trials and activities. The decentralized feature of blockchain makes it more transparent and reliable in the pharmaceutical business by tracking all the transactions placed and immutable nature in the healthcare data distributed among all networks [1]. Blockchain technology has many advantages in pharmaceutical sectors because of the following factors: monitoring, reliability, traceability, authorization, and real-time functionality [2].
