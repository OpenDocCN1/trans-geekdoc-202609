# 13.2 Blockchain Technology: A New Era of Pharmaceutical Research

Blockchain technology is transforming many aspects of pharmaceutical research by providing many applications like drug discovery, development and delivery. Blockchain protects the reliability and integrity of clinical trial by providing an immutable log of transactions which improves the accountability and transparency of pharmaceutical research. Smart contracts enabled by blockchain technology facilitate safe and transparent cooperation between scientists, organizations, and other stakeholders promoting a culture of data exchange and scientific progress. Furthermore, real-time drug supply chain monitoring is made possible by blockchain-based supply chain solutions which help to prevent the circulation of fake drugs and guarantee the safety and authenticity of the pharmaceutical. Blockchain technology provides solutions to different pharmaceutical issues to improve governance and compliance in the pharma supply chain.

Blockchain reduces administrative burdens and speeds up regulatory compliance procedures by decentralizing data storage and carrying away the middlemen. This accelerates the velocity of innovation in medication discovery and developments.
