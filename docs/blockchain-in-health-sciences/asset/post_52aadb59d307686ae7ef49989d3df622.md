# Abstract

The integration of medication discovery, traceability and blockchain technology in the field of smart health science has the potential to revolutionize the pharmaceutical industry. The decentralized and transparent nature of blockchain technology makes it a promising tool for revolutionizing drug development procedures. The immutable ledger simplifies data management and stakeholder cooperation by ensuring safe and impenetrable records. The blockchain governance techniques are to highlight the crucial aspect to promote cooperation and trust in the fast developing field of smart health science. With drug research depending on teamwork and data-driven insights, intellectual property protection becomes critical. Blockchain provides a unique way to address IP protection issues by enabling transparent, time-stamped data. This chapter comprehensively explores the diverse landscape of governance structures and intellectual property protection tactics against blockchain technology. In the context of medication discovery and traceability, this chapter also sights the complex interactions among blockchain governance, intellectual property (IP) protection, and creativity highlighting the necessity for a scalable legal framework.

## 21.1 Introduction

The public health is seriously threatened by counterfeit drugs which have a negative impact on patients and treatment results [1]. Counterfeit medications are defined by the World Health Organization (WHO) as goods that are purposefully and dishonestly mislabeled about their origin or identity [2]. These fake medications frequently have deceptive packaging and may have inadequate, inaccurate or fake ingredients [3]. There are several pharmaceutical stakeholders, including hospitals, pharmacies, wholesale distributors, global health initiatives and regulatory bodies that are affected by the worldwide trade in counterfeit medications [4]. Because the illicit drug market plays a major role in the spread of phony medications [5]. The lack of sufficient technological and commercial solutions that provide traceability and provenance verification is the reason this problem continues [6]. The difficulty of guaranteeing product safety in the supply chain is made worse by the growth of unlicensed distribution channels and internet platforms [7]. Counterfeit medication entry into the market is facilitated by supply visibility of inventory and stock levels being limited [8]. Drug traceability allows stakeholders to keep an eye on transactions at every point in the supply chain by confirming the validity and authenticity of items [9, 10]. Blockchain technology has extensive applications in the management of intellectual property (IP), spanning across various categories including patents, trademarks, and all other types of IP rights [11].

Blockchain technology, a decentralized and distributed ledger system, offers a promising solution for drug traceability [12]. Blockchain which is known for powering crypto-currencies has been applied across various industries, including healthcare to facilitate secure and transparent processing. By leveraging blockchain, stakeholders can establish a shared data platform for storing and sharing transaction data securely and transparently [13]. This technology offers a proactive approach to detecting and managing counterfeit drugs in pharmaceutical supply chains. A block’s data is unchanged, protecting the integrity and avoiding cases of double-spending [14]. Moreover, every block is linked to a distinct code called a “hash” which creates a link to the previous block, making it incredibly difficult to alter or tamper with the data kept on the blockchain [15].

## BLOCKCHAIN AND IP PROTECTION IN SMART HEALTH SCIENCE

### 21.1.1 Background and Significance

Blockchain technology makes it possible to develop apps that do not require a central authority to validate transactions which allows numerous parties to interact directly over a peer-to-peer network [16, 17]. Without any single entity owning the network, every member has access to a shared ledger safely and permanently records every transaction using cryptographic techniques [18]. Supply chains businesses may use blockchain to record production modifications on a single ledger, providing a single point of reference and complete data visibility [19]. Companies can always find out the status and location of a product because all transactions are time-stamped and updated [20].

Millions of people's health is at risk due to counterfeit drugs, which are a major issue that the pharmaceutical industry bears a lot of money every year [21]. Pharmaceutical businesses may use unique serial numbers to monitor pharmaceuticals carefully along the whole supply chain to blockchain technology. Each serial number's manufacturing updates are recorded in the network ledger, which acts as an unchangeable source of truth to thwart actors' manipulation [22]. Pharmaceutical businesses can identify supply chain weaknesses that might enable the entrance of counterfeit medicines by having complete visibility across the network on a blockchain [23]. Due to the serious dangers to patient safety, the strict requirements set by the FDA and the significant investment made in drug research, recalls are especially important in pharmaceutical supply chains. When drugs are mislabeled, contaminated, or have manufacturing defects, recalls could be required [24]. During recalls, prompt action is essential to protect patient safety and minimize financial damages. The accurately identifying tainted maine batches is essential to preventing the needless disposal of safe goods [25].

This helps handle problems including product counterfeiting, noncompliance with regulations, delay, and waste. Moreover, the ledger's audit trail guarantees regulatory compliance and swift response in case of crises like product recalls [26]. Also, supply chains may automate the monitoring of transportation, quality control, and manufacturing conditions by connecting blockchain with smart technologies like the Internet of Things (IoT). To verify the legitimacy of their products and maintain supply chain procedures, businesses can also choose to provide tracking and tracing information to their customers [27]. Pharmaceutical firms may now respond to recalls more effectively by accurately identifying hazardous pharmaceuticals and limiting patient risks; thanks to block- technology technology22].]. can also help locate contaminated areas during production, which lowers the number of recall events related to poor quality. For example, pharmaceutical businesses may improve product integrity and safety by ensuring that pharmaceuticals are properly refrigerated along the supply chain by combining blockchain with temperature sensors [29].
