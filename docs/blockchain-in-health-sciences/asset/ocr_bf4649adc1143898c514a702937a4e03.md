+   - Development and Delivery
The first stage in the pharmaceutical research is to figure out the proper sources of the development of drugs. Then, for screening chemical libraries, potential candidate for the particular treatment is identified [14]. After identifying the particular candidates, preclinical test is performed to identify whether this would be safe and efficient to apply in clinical trials. These procedures are taken out to assess the safety, efficiency, and adverse effects among humans.

+   - Biomedical Science Innovation
As biomedical science advances, it is heavily focused on identifying the molecular defects, molecular basis of diseases, identifying and developing customized medicines that target specific customer requirements or figuring out therapy responses. It aims to reduce side effects and increase effectiveness by customizing treatments to meet each particular patient requirement [13].

+   - Regulatory Framework
The pharmaceutical sector is heavily regulated, with strict guidelines governing labeling, marketing, clinical trial conduct, manufacturing standards, and medication clearance. The process is monitored by the approval by regulatory agencies, which are the EMA, the FDA in the United States and other international regulatory bodies. Before supplying the drug to patients or introducing it to the market, the organizations in question control the quality, safety and efficacy level of the medications [15, 16].
