Cumulative Record Cards (CRC) and Anecdotal Record Cards (ARC) are traditional methods of recording educational and personal information about students. However, students cannot interfere with these records, raising questions about data protection. Therefore, it is expected that students, particularly those at the undergraduate (UG) level, will positively adopt blockchain technology in healthcare and data security.

While blockchain is closely associated with cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin, it now extends far beyond finance to include healthcare, management, election systems, and more. The advancement of technology and human curiosity has expanded the scope of blockchain into various fields. In this engaging era, researchers are faced with pressing questions regarding where to implement blockchain for optimal results. Blockchain technology is widely recognized for its advantages in data privacy, reducing academic fraud, minimizing the chance of hacking, facilitating digital credentials, enhancing educational transactions, and more. The authors selected this topic due to its prominent features and relevance.

In this study, the authors aim to unveil the perceptions of engineers and UG students toward blockchain technology in relation to healthcare and data privacy, considering these two elements as essential to any educational system. Particularly regarding data privacy, this approach allows students to delve into a new area of exploration. It will be intriguing to observe the level of students' perceptions regarding data security.

As we live in an era dominated by mobile devices, the majority of students today utilize their smartphones to access the internet and various resources in a highly efficient manner. They are likely to understand the importance of protecting their data privacy and being aware of issues related to cybercrime, hacking, and what it means to be a digital citizen. On the other hand, this study also focuses on the healthcare aspect for UG students. Technological advancements have historically had significant effects on people's health. It is crucial to assess the perceptions of students regarding healthcare in the context of blockchain technology, especially given the numerous articles and writings that have highlighted the negative effects of technological inventions.

Ultimately, whatever results are found after analyzing the data we must contemplate how to effectively implement blockchain technology across all levels of the educational sector, including primary and secondary levels, to maximize its benefits.
