# 3.3.1 Healthcare Internet of Things

H-IoT, or the Internet of Things, is a rapidly growing field in healthcare that combines internet-enabled devices, sensors, and software applications to improve patient outcomes, efficiency, and cost. Its applications include wearable devices, remote patient monitoring, telemedicine, and intelligent hospital systems [10, 11]. However, data confidentiality and safety are crucial for the secure use of H-IoT technologies. A study by Tsafack et al. has developed a secure cryptystem for MRI image transmission in H-IoT environments, demonstrating its potential for efficient use in medical imaging. The use of IoT in healthcare is gaining acceptance for enhancing patient outcomes, enhancing effectiveness, and reducing costs [12, 13].

Big Data in the Healthcare Sector: Big data analytics in healthcare has led to the development of predictive models, tailored treatment strategies, and enhanced patient outcomes [10]. This has resulted in decreased healthcare costs and streamlined delivery.
