# Abstract

The implementation of Internet of Things (IoT) technology has resulted in a substantial and noteworthy transformation in healthcare systems. The use of IoT devices has enhanced the efficiency, convenience, and automation of healthcarerelated services. Different IoT devices transmit vast quantities of captured data to the cloud, where it is processed to facilitate physicians' access to information. In the case of cloud-sensor architecture, where data transmission and reception incur significant latency, fog nodes are incorporated at the periphery of the sensor networks. However, due to the extremely sensitive nature of medical data, privacy, integrity, confidentiality, and access control are required. The aforementioned challenges may be resolved by employing blockchain technology, which can also foster trust between the healthcare provider and the patient. This chapter thoroughly examines the integration of IoT technologies into the health sciences. After, it discusses the significance and difficulties associated with cloud-based and fog-based data storage and computation. It also covers the security aspects and difficulties associated with IoT technology-based solutions, as well as the significance of blockchain in safeguarding medical data. Finally, it examines the future scope of this topic.

**Keywords:** IoT, healthcare, cloud computing, fog computing, blockchain, security, privacy
