# 21.5.1 Importance of Intellectual Property in Pharma

The pharmaceutical sector is significantly impacted by intellectual property (IPR) in a number of areas, including drug research, discovery, pricing, distribution, competitive analysis and the availability and afford-ability of new drugs. Increased intellectual property rights (IPR) protection in developed nations has fueled the pharmaceutical industry's explosive growth [118, 119]. The developing countries frequently attack the patent system, pointing to worries about monopolies in the market and inflated medicine costs [120]. Blockchain technology provides a way to record and validate ownership of creative works in a decentralized, transparent, and tamper-proof platform, protecting intellectual property. Blockchain creates unquestionable proof of creation and ownership with features like smart contracts and immutable ledgers [120, 121]. With facilitating quick and global intellectual property registration, it reduces the possibility of conflicts or unapproved use [122].

# 21.6 Challenges and Concerns

The healthcare sector has a number of difficulties such as dispersed data, privacy and security issues, and interoperability problems resulting from different standards among healthcare systems [123]. By guaranteeing safe and impenetrable data storage via a distributed ledger system, enabling safe data sharing to improve interoperability and protecting patient privacy via data encryption, blockchain technology is a viable remedy [124]. To address these issues, a variety of blockchain-related technologies have been used, including federated learning, health information exchanges (HIEs) and electronic health records (EHRs) [125].
