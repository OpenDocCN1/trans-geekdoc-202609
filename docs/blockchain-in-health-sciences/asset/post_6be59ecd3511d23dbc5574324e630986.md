### ### Security

Drawbacks related to security emerge wherever there are concerns privacy. As recent research findings have demonstrated, blockchain technology has an inherent security drawback. Due to the lack of care advancements in the security mechanism, blockchain is susceptible to attacks. As a consequence, the present structure is less challenging to analyze and recognize danger factors involved. The system's obsolete software exposes it to an extensive spectrum of threats and attacks. Attackers can quickly get access to the system and alter any data that is currently there in the system. This undermines patient data stored in the system, raising privacy issues in addition to existing security issues. False inputs entering the model may possess a detrimental effect on the necessary output of the model when combined with AI technology; thereby, the health and well-being of the patients may be gravely threatened [9].

### ### Scalability

Blockchain and AI technologies also come across with the problem of scalability. The reason for this relates to the ability to change the scale or size. Blockchain and AI both have scaling difficulties. Considering blockchain technology maintains a large number of cryptographic hash connections and multiple data in its databases, changes to the technology could additionally impact the amount of processing that needs to be done throughout the entire infrastructure.

In addition to that, a substantial number of datasets may be available for AI technology for generating the necessary but highly accurate and optimized outcomes. The model ought to additionally possess a high degree of efficiency. Each of these datasets requires to be effectively maintained, and the model ought not to overfit or underfit while it is trained. As a consequence, it raises concerns regarding scalability and propels additional-expenses.

### d) Interoperability and Regulatory Laws

The capacity of one blockchain to communicate with another is known as blockchain interoperability. Cross-chain communication messaging protocols, which allow blockchains read from as well as write to other blockchains, constitute the foundation of blockchain interoperability. Since patients may transfer to a different institution for extra medical attention, it is necessary for multiple institutions to exchange the patient’s medical history. Interoperability facilitates sharing and new data addition to medical records. Regulatory rules like ethical safety and standards, identity verification, data protection, financial regulations along with more are necessary for a peaceful communication between two parties. Therefore, they have been incorporated in all of these applications and implementations and are obligatory references. One of the main roadblocks to the adoption of Blockchain technology is the Ownership. As the data can be owned privately and publicly both in the Blockchain, therefore, there is absence of a feeling of authority; technological acts are absent, and several legal and regulatory law diff arise [5]. A lack of assurance between the many parties involved in the information flow across the various health organizations result in obstacles to the information flow. Furthermore, smaller healthcare organizations might not be able to afford the early setup costs associated with a blockchain-based system, even though they are more affordable in the long run.

### e) Data Storage and Resource Limitation

In this sector, each individual has unique data that is challenging to manage. Considering all of the blockchain and AI technological breakthroughs, a lot of capacity and storage should be offered, especially due to the volume of data. One of the advantages of existing blockchain technology is a restriction on the total number of network transactions that can be executed in a single second. The energy consumption of consensus-building and other protocols and standards may jeopardize the process’s speed and efficiency. Although the computational capacity necessary to process the transactions is significant, there is consequently a large consumption of energy. This brings upon the concerns regarding block-chain technology [9].

Furthermore, as data storage and capacity constraints have been increasing, recent research has shown that a significant disadvantage of the expense of data storage is a problem in the field of big data analysis. A substantial amount of data must be gathered to accurately predict the necessary output from all the data that is extracted. Highly efficient tools for gathering data are necessary for all of these.

Therefore, Blockchain hinders the processing speed because of the regulatory laws and protocols that it contains and AI also requires a large amount of data storage for the models to get trained and tested in their most efficient way. As a result, both technologies have an impact on one another and therefore, pose a challenge to the healthcare industry [47].
