+   Medical Care: With smart contracts, blockchain has significant promise for the healthcare industry. These astute connections allow two parties to draft a contract directly. Every party involved in the agreement is aware of its specifics, and as soon as its requirements are met, the agreement becomes operative [29]. Wearable personal health records that are encrypted using blockchain technology, accessible only by primary healthcare professionals holding the key can be highly helpful in the healthcare industry. They also support the HIPAA Privacy Rule, which restricts who can access patient data and preserve privacy.

+   Digital Money: Cryptocurrency is, without a question, one of the most widely used uses of blockchain technology. Who hasn't heard about the incredible rise in popularity of bitcoin? One of its many benefits is blockchain technology for cryptocurrencies with no geographical restrictions. As a result, cross-border cryptocurrency transactions are feasible [29]. The only factors to think about are exchange rates and the possibility of losing money. Even yet, this choice is still far superior to local payment applications, like Paytm in India, which are only available in a certain area and cannot be used to send money outside.

+   Certificates of Birth and Death: Many people worldwide, particularly in the world's poorer nations, lack a legitimate birth certificate. According to UNICEF, in three children under the age of five do not have a birth certificate [30]. Death certificates are a contributing factor in this issue. Blockchain, on the other hand, can assist in solving this issue by establishing a certified, safe store for birth and death certificates that is only open to authorized individuals.
