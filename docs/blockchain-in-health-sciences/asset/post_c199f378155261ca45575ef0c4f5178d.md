## BLOCKCHAIN IN PHARMACEUTICAL RESEARCH

# Blockchain Applications in Pharmaceutical Research: A Comprehensive Overview

Bhavna Sharma* and Susheela Hooda

Chitkara University Institute of Engineering and Technology, Chitkara University, Punjab, India

# Abstract

The blockchain technology in pharmaceutical industry represents a paradigm shift in terms of data management, collaboration, and security. Different applications of this technology in pharmaceutical industry are highlighted in this study. This integration helps with the various benefits for healthcare industry which includes security, integrity, verification, transparency, regulatory compliances for particular transactions. In comparative analysis from pharmaceutical industry are discussed in this papers from recent years are observed and analyzed. In supply-chain management, clinical trial and regulatory compliance blockchain technology are used to provide safe, secure and traceable environment to transactions or data. It also discussed that how technology helps to improve security and integrity of the data in pharmaceutical industry. Moreover, this study provides an overview for academics, practitioners, and policymakers navigating the rapidly evolving area of blockchain in pharmaceutical research by offering strategic ideas to maximize blockchain’s potential while addressing the difficulties associated with its application.

Keywords: Blockchain, pharmaceutical industry, decentralization, smart contracts
