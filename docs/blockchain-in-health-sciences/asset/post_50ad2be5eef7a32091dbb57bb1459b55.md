### 7.3.1 Blockchain in Healthcare

The operation of Blockchain underpins the principles of decentralization, transparency, and immutability. A block can be referred to as data storage unit that keeps record of transactions or other data. Every block or node is linked to the previous ones using cryptographic hashing and stores data permanently and ensures immutability. Updated data is introduced to a new node without affecting the stored data in an existing node [52]. Blockchain uses a Peer to Peer (P2P) network to maintain distributed ledger. The blockchain's internal organizational structure and the manner in which nodes are interconnected with one another while preserving data confidentiality and immutability are seen in Figure 7.3.

It works in different layers namely Application and Presentation layer, Consensus layer, Network layer, Data layer and Infrastructure layer. Let's examine the layers in brief. Figure 7.4 shows the hierarchy of the layers existent blockchain.

- 1. Application and Presentation layer: End users utilize the applications in the application layer to create blockchain network connectivity. A smart contract, or programmable code that controls state transitions, is the first component of the application layer. It is referred to by different names in different ecosystems, such as “programmes” in Solana and “chaincode” in Hyperledger, and can serve as an escrow, payment-channel, or vault.
- 2. Consensus layer: This layer validates the authenticity of a block before transmitting it to the network. This procedure is a component of blockchain's fault-tolerant technique for transaction verification. Different types of consensus protocols with different principles and guiding techniques. Some of the techniques are listed below.
    a) Proof of Work (PoW): This is the foundation of the Bitcoin algorithm. The new node in this algorithm requires some computational power. It has to fulfill a difficult assignment to demonstrate its non-malicious nature. The miner can only get rewarded *afterwards* [53].
    b) Proof of Stake (PoS): PoS consumes less energy than PoW. It also indicates that the likelihood of being attacked is higher on nodes with a larger cash valuation [53].
    c) Proof of Capacity (PoC): It is a consensus process that uses hard disc space that is available rather than computational resources to reach consensus. More storage space enables one to store more solutions, which raises the possibility of creating new blocks [53].
- 3. Network layer: the network layer manages how the nodes communicate with one another. This layer allows any node to join or leave the network without changing the ledger. It aids in distributing the workload and boosts system effectiveness.
- 4. Data layer: The real data for blockchain is maintained and saved at this layer. Hashing serves as one of several cryptographic techniques used to protect the transactions. Each block consists of the hash, timestamp, nonce, and root of the Merkle tree in addition to information over several transactions. The Merkle tree is a data structure that resembles a tree and has leaf nodes that hold the parent's hash [37].
- 5. Infrastructure layer: A device is referred to and utilized as a node after it connects to a blockchain network. This device makes the infrastructure layer of the Blockchain. Virtual machines, or OSs, are another component of the infrastructure layer that houses smart contracts [5].

### 7.3.2 Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

The science and engineering associated in developing intelligent machines that can reduce stress for employees without the need for additional instruction is what describes artificial intelligence. As we are surrounded by various technologies, AI has surprised us with its multidisciplinary evolutions throughout several decades. The rapidly expanding healthcare industry has been fueled by the increasing use of AI. AI’s primary contribution to human occupational alleviation of stress is its ability to make more intelligent choices on its own without assistance from outside sources. In spite of that, AI continues to contribute to the expansion of various medical service-related industries, encompassing virtual personal life coaches, advanced data analysis, and detection of cancer [7]. AI has enhanced healthcare care quality and efficiency by affirming medical theories, organizing huge volumes of medical data, and identifying clinical developments. It has also reduced the burden of healthcare personnel [59]. Moreover, AI in healthcare has also contributed in numerous medical analyses for bringing up solutions for chronic diseases like skin cancer, cancer care, mental health along with fitness rehabilitation. All these contributions result in outcomes like fitness, wellbeing, mental health management and many more [60].

Furthermore, there is a substantial increase in the implementation of AI in healthcare which can be determined by the following factors:

- 1. General-purpose accessibility and freely available AI frameworks make it easier for non-AI professionals to use advanced AI algorithms without a requirement for specialized AI training [1].
- 2. The ability to amalgamate various data sources to create massive databases for the investigation of complicated health care issues [1].

### 7.3.3 Blockchain and AI in Healthcare

Blockchain technology in healthcare proposes significantly more than mere data storage; it also protects data while it is shared and addresses security such as phishing, clone phishing, masquerade, row hammer, and buffer overflow, in addition to the mechanisms to counter these threats.

Blockchain technologies along with AI are more effective to each other in several technological aspects, with unique characteristics and functions. Thus, we addressed the benefits of blockchain and AI in the healthcare sector in this section.

#### 7.3.4.1 Advantages of Using AI in Healthcare

As a result, it has exceptional and dynamic interoperability with every other platform, including the IoT, blockchain, and cloud computing. - 2. High Efficiency: AI may utilize a wide range of algorithms and training techniques to supply us with a satisfactory outcome. Efficiency is vital within this technology. In accordance with AI’s dynamic compatibility with various technologies, the healthcare industry reaps the advantages of its efficient outcomes.
- 3. High Availability: It is a key feature of AI to be highly mobile in nature, since it is dynamically compatible with several technologies and wearable devices. The increasing availability of AI is not just limited to consumer devices. AI is being embedded in industrial machines, medical equipment, and even in autonomous vehicles.
