The necessity of strong security is also highlighted by IoT data management. When it comes to IoT data management difficulties, a cloud-based solution effectively uses Attribute-Based Encryption (ABE), traversing complexity while guarding against unauthorized access [8]. Notably, a user-friendly cloud integration of ABE places a strong emphasis on “verifiability” to ensure precise user decryption, lowering the need for user processing [9]. A new ABE system that includes validated sourced decryption considerably improves compute and bandwidth utilization in an effort to lower decryption costs and overheads [10]. Another paper presents a method for securing online health records that makes use of CP-ABE and a “policy versioning” mechanism that has been rigorously tested.
