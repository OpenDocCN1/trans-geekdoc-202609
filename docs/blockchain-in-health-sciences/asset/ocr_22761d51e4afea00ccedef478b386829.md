# 5.5 Method of the Study

A descriptive survey method has been used in this study. Therefore, various, techniques, strategies, and methods typical of descriptive survey research have been employed to collect, analyze, and interpret the data. To achieve this objective, a quantitative research approach was adopted.

# 5.5.1 Population

The population of any study refers to the total collection of elements or participants from which the researcher draws inferences. This study is conducted on UG students of Engineering and general-degree colleges in West Bengal, all UG students from these colleges in West Bengal are identified as the population of the study.

# 5.5.2 Sample and Sampling Procedure

The sample of the study refers to a set of representative individuals or participants from the population of the study. In this study, 289 UG students from both engineering colleges (159 students) and general-degree colleges (130 students) in West Bengal were taken as a representative sample for the whole population. Simple random sampling was used to select the sample.
