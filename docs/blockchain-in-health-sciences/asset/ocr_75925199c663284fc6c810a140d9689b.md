Semi-trusted entities, such as fog nodes, are installed with a trusted proxy and public key certificate to ensure secure processing and reduce transmission costs, thereby preventing hacked fog nodes from posing a threat to the system. Internet of Things (IoT) devices are wearable sensors or gadgets that are used to take physiological data from patients' bodies, such as temperature, heart rate, electrocardiogram (ECG), etc. gathering patient data using improved encryption algorithms to ensure safe transmission (temperature, pressure, ECG, etc.) Data from IoT devices is collected and transmitted via an aggregation terminal. Data must be packaged and delivered by authorized persons, like a medical professional, before being forwarded to the nearest fog node. The data is then encrypted by the device before being transmitted as partially encrypted ciphertext. IoT devices are used to gather various patient data and can send this data via a network, including blood pressure and oxygen levels. With our method, the data is encrypted on each device before it is sent to the aggregate terminal; partially encrypting, and thanks to an encryption technique, collecting, and decrypting data before sending it to the closest fog node. It guarantees that data is safely decrypted and packaged by authorized persons.
