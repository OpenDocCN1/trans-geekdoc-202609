Blockchain technology also oversees pharmaceutical supply chains and medical data. In addition, bioinformatics can help identify and mitigate public health threats like infectious disease spread and pandemic prediction. This improves attempts to prevent and control disease. Overall, Blockchain and Bioinformatics together have enormous potential to transform healthcare by enhancing data exchange, security, tailored treatment, and medical research by offering strong data security, decentralized, effective consent management through smart contracts, improved data privacy, transparent audit trails, and creative access control models designed for healthcare data management, and also transformation of healthcare through precision medicine, data-driven methods, and the use of modern technologies to enhance patient outcomes and public health. Blockchain and bioinformatics tools promote health healthcare, privacy, decentralization of patient data, personalization, and more. However, healthcare facilities encounter numerous challenges when implementing these tools. Since bioinformatics and AI-based technologies may not be able to address liability, stability, and transparency for innovative medical devices, regulatory obstacles remain. This technology is new and sophisticated; thus, software developers are scarce. This raises blockchain application development and maintenance costs. The high implementation costs of blockchain technology for enterprises limit its widespread adoption and utilization. While the main function of blockchain technology is to secure and maintain the patient’s private information and secure the network via encryption, users risk losing all of their vulnerable information if they displace their private keys. This could be a huge problem since it is nearly impossible to recover the lost private key. Many fragmented and disparate data are collected from different health center departments, and it is a challenge to create a cohesive patient record on the blockchain. Medical records’ fragmentary, inaccurate, and inconsistently maintained nature might challenge the data quality used in developing bioinformatics algorithms. Bioinformatics algorithms may not be able to predict future results from historical data to medical data native. Blockchain technology is unable to scale to accommodate healthcare’s massive data volumes. Some learning algorithms in bioinformatics applications are “black boxes”, making their decision-making processes difficult to understand. Most importantly, since the information in the blocks cannot be altered, its immutability poses a serious issue in the event of an error. The blockchain is an inefficient and ecologically unfriendly technology since it requires significant energy to validate transactions. One of the major problems could be unemployment due to the introduction of AI, bioinformatics, and blockchain, when payments and processes can be validated without the need for mediators, intermediation industries would diminish and eventually vanish, creating job losses. Healthcare organizations must meticulously consider the possible advantages and disadvantages of deploying blockchain technology and bioinformatics.

## Future Work and Research Opportunities

Healthcare systems encounter numerous challenges, including data integration, such as the inability to maintain health records, a limited budget, insufficient access, a lack of preventative care, and a lack of trust in maintaining health data. However, blockchain technology overcomes all these problems and gains trust by maintaining and building the digital infrastructure. Because it is a digital database system, it can store all types of healthcare data, including all data from online consultation apps and prescriptions. This system makes it easier for doctors to understand a patient's health history and tailor the information accurately. Blockchain protects its layers and keeps patients' data safe. Furthermore, counterfeit drugs are the most common problem worldwide. Blockchain applications can regulate drug movement, making it easy to find fake drugs on the supply chain. These are harmful to the public and may cause severe health issues. However, according to medical research, blockchain facilitates the formulation and sharing of new drugs, with clinical research playing a crucial role in ensuring drug effectiveness. Blockchain not only makes the entire system transparent and faster, but it also has the potential to revolutionize the system by ensuring security and integrity. Besides the blockchain, bioinformatics integration can aid in the development of healthcare. One example is personalized medicine, which bases drug target identification on genomics, epigenetics, proteomics, and transcriptomics studies. Today, using advanced bioinformatics speeds up genome testing in center, potentially saving many lives. AI and machine learning are transforming healthcare as a part of bioinformatics by integrating algorithms and machine learning models that could predict early interventions to enhance the clinical workflow. In today's world, the healthcare sector is demonstrating interest in technology such as blockchain and bioinformatics, which expand the infrastructure and are highly beneficial in minimizing patient care time. Along with the advancement and integration of computational approach, designing proper protocol is needed with high accuracy to address various factors.

## Conclusions

Healthcare is crucial because it allows us to live longer, healthier, and happier through preventive care, early detection, and successful treatment of ailments. Nevertheless, blockchain technology has transformed into a versatile tool used in the healthcare industry to establish an efficient healthcare system with numerous applications. Bioinformatics improves data analysis to improve patient care and forecast disease trends, innovative treatments, and vaccinations. Blockchain and bioinformatics are enabling data-driven, personalized disease prevention, diagnosis, and treatment in healthcare. Despite various drawbacks in the realms of blockchain and bioinformatics, it has practical applications in healthcare in terms of data security and management. Therefore, the trends of blockchain and bioinformatics in healthcare are essential to getting an accurate decentralized system to improve the population's health outcomes.
