# 7.4 Conclusion

This chapter's topics have included how AI and Blockchain collaborate in the healthcare sector. Blockchain technology, either separately and in combination, enhances systems, affects databases, and empowers users to elevate the healthcare industry. Blockchain technologies have the capacity to make considerable advantages and rebalance AI and ML mechanisms. Such innovations include ultra-secure and immutable ledgers, decentralization, robust consensus processes, and self-sovereign identities. Furthermore, AI enables the models required to detect patterns and feasible alternatives for blockchain databases. These technologies serve a purpose for many different applications, which includes supply chain management, telemedicine, and (EHRs) electronic health records. However, fundamental vulnerabilities in security, privacy, data storage, and other domains impact both blockchain technology and AI. But if we are able to integrate these two technologies, they have the power to change the world and move the healthcare industry further to the digital sphere. Blockchain applications are anticipated to transform the healthcare industry through AI techniques. The approach would enhance healthcare quality at a reduced cost in addition to being transparent and safe.

# 7.5 Future Works

The world of healthcare consists of numerous subfields which can be strengthened in several ways including the application of blockchain technology supported by artificial intelligence. When deep learning is integrated with blockchain technology, patient data is secure and accurate, making it an indispensable instrument for diagnosing myopia and other interior medical conditions. Before submitting a proposed model's design, it is crucial to conduct extensive study, which may also be relevant to future research goals to foster collaboration in both sectors. Additionally, with greater accuracy and precision, these advanced algorithms will be able to identify chronic illnesses including cancer, diabetes, arthritis, and some serious heart ailments. The safeguarding and confidentiality of blockchain technology influenced by AI remains capable of being substantially enhanced. Researchers in the future might examine deploying a greater degree of controls for verification and implementing the most recent hash algorithms. There are numerous advantages and disadvantages of using two and multi-step verification systems for each blockchain node, but they are not clearly defined in the context of this particular research topic. It would not be a good idea to utilize the conventional consensus protocols, such as proof-of-work and proof-of-stake, as have been around for the last ten years and have recently been found to have various shortcomings.

The AI-powered blockchain could implement new consensus protocols consisting of Assigned Proof of Stake and Proof-of-Elapsed-Time, which would significantly bolster the security and efficiency of the system [10].

The blockchain that is powered by AI would function more rapidly if it would feature a varied collection of different technologies. In the future, researchers might additionally look into encompassing the blockchain with current infrastructures. For the potential of blockchain systems powered by AI to be used within the healthcare industry on a commercial basis, it will also be crucial to verify preservation along with enhanced security.
