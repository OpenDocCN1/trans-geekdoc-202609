+   1980s: The Neocognitron, an early CNN architecture, laid the groundwork for future advancements.

This intertwined evolution of deep learning and CNNs signifies a powerful partnership that continues to shape the future of artificial intelligence.

These techniques enabled studies with complex algorithms for diagnosis and decision making at different stages of patient's care and recovery cycle.
