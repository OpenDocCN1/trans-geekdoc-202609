After the introduction of electronic medical records (EMR), the entire industry migrated to a cloud-based platform to store patient data. However, challenges remain in adopting cloud technology to transform the entire management process of the healthcare industry. The healthcare sector as a whole cannot become productive and efficient if cloud solutions are the only tools used. Healthcare firms must leverage data management, IoT, and AI technologies to fully benefit from this technology.
