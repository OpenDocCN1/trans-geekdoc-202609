# 19.4 Challenges in Genomics

Despite the significant reduction in sequencing costs, which surpasses the predicted levels based on Moore’s law, genomics still faces the difficulty of becoming affordable [24]. In a recent study by Schwarz et al. [25], the researchers examined the overall expenses associated with the utilization of genome sequencing in regular clinical practice. According to reports, if we only take into account the price of sequencing, the estimation is lower than the actual cost, which is expected to exceed $1000 per genome in a single facility [25]. The presence of a middleman in classical systems creates complexity in participating in a given sequencing project, since it prevents a direct connection between the data owner and the buyer during transactions.
