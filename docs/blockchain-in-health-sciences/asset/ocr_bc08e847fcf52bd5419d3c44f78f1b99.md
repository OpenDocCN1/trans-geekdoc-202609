# 6.1 Introduction

Providing the aging and expanding population with appropriate and beneficial healthcare is one of the 21st century's most important issues. Innovation is necessary in the healthcare sector. The government, life sciences firms, health plans, and providers are all facing growing expenses and uneven results. They are trying to reduce costs and spending while simultaneously enhancing services and health outcomes.

Health science is the study of applied science that deals with human physical and behavioral well-being.

In recent times, the healthcare industry has encountered various obstacles stemming from the global digital revolution, the pandemic, significant changes in the population, and increasing patient expectations [1, 2]. In the era of personalized healthcare, evidence-based treatment, and digital customer service, it is critical for startups, entrepreneurs, and healthcare professionals to remain up to date on the latest developments that are reshaping the sector. There are few technological innovation trends in the healthcare sector that have emerged and provide support to health sciences:

Ensuring data safety and security is the major challenge in the healthcare industry. According to the report, there were 692 large healthcare breaches reported between July 2021 and June 2022. These data breaches included missing credit card and banking information, as well as health and genomic testing records. Here, there is a necessity to enforce and tighten security in the medical data industry.

The innovation of new computer technologies like IoT, big data, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and cloud technologies play a role in almost all fields, including smart healthcare systems.
