# Testing of $H_{01}(c)$:

The UG students (both general-degree and engineering colleges) of West Bengal will have a more unfavorable perception on the potentiality of blockchain technology in healthcare and data security.

| Category | N | Mean | SD |

From the Table 5.3, on the cut-off point, the investigators verified H01(c): The cut-off point here is M + $1\sigma$. Mean = 59.63, N = 289, and $\sigma = 11.13$. This means that $59.63 + 1 \times 11.13 = 70.76$ is M + $1\sigma$, and M - $1\sigma = 59.63 - 1 \times 11.13 = 48.50$. Most UG students (211, which is 73.01% of UG students) scored between 48.50 and 70.76. It can be concluded that the perception of UG students is moderate regarding the potentiality of blockchain technology in healthcare and data security.
