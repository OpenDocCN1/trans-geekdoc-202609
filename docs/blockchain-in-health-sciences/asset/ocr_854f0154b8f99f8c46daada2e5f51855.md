# 7.4 Conclusion

This chapter's topics have included how AI and Blockchain collaborate in the healthcare sector. Blockchain technology, either separately and in combination, enhances systems, affects databases, and empowers users to elevate the healthcare industry. Blockchain technologies have the capacity to make considerable advantages and rebalance AI and ML mechanisms. Such innovations include ultra-secure and immutable ledgers, decentralization, robust consensus processes, and self-sovereign identities. Furthermore, AI enables the models required to detect patterns and feasible alternatives for blockchain databases. These technologies serve a purpose for many different applications, which includes supply chain management, telemedicine, and (EHRs) electronic health records.
