## 5.2 Literature Review

Agbo et al. (2019) conducted a systematic review of blockchain technology in healthcare following PRisma (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analysis) guidelines. After a thorough review, the authors found several applications of blockchain technology in healthcare but noted a lack of prototype implementations [18]. Febih and Lahami (2020) carried out a broader survey on the application of blockchain technology in healthcare, addressing some limitations identified in this approach. Lastly, the authors discussed open questions and further research related to this new method [19]. Shi et al. (2020) conducted a systematic review of the integration of blockchain technology with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, focusing exclusively on data security and privacy aspects. After a careful review, the authors identified numerous challenges and future prospects [20]. Haleem et al. (2021) studied blockchain technology and its pivotal advantages in healthcare, revealing 14 significant applications. They highlighted several benefits of blockchain technology in healthcare, such as improved data efficiency, reduced fear of data manipulation, and enhanced levels of data security. Specifically, blockchain technology decentralizes data, which mitigates specific threats [15]. Kuleto et al. (2022) examined a different approach at blockchain network to create the most effective sustainable educational system in Serbia, Romania, and Portugal. The results clearly imply that although blockchain technology has some disadvantages, students considered this technology to have a significant positive impact on their learning performance [12]. Njeri (2022) conducted a study on data security and privacy issues related to blockchain technology. After reviewing the literature, the author concluded that as a technology, blockchain has several shortcomings concerning data and privacy; therefore, more research is needed to understand this concept better [21]. A similar study conducted by Saed et al. (2022) aimed to provide a systematic review of the potential paradigm shift of blockchain technology in healthcare. They reviewed a total of 1,12 research studies from 2016 to 2021 on blockchain technology and identified 51 articles that enhanced healthcare through blockchain. The authors concluded that future research studies on blockchain technology could play a vital role in health diagnostics, patient healthcare processes, data integrity, and fraud prevention [22]. Wylde et al. (2022) carried out a study on data privacy and cybersecurity in blockchain technology. The authors identified and reviewed key obstacles in maintaining the knowledge gap among various private companies, organizations, government institutions, and business sectors regarding the promotion and adoption of this new technology [23]. Astaman and Mauritius (2023) endeavored to students' perceptions and implications of blockchain technology. The results indicate that students hold positive perceptions of blockchain technology and are conscious of their data security and privacy when using it. Additionally, students recognize its potential in managing educational data and information as well as in the administration process. However, they also noted the need for increased awareness and regulations in higher education [24] for the implementation of this method. Singh et al. (2023) conducted another study discussing the effective utilization of blockchain technology in the healthcare sector. Their results revealed that for the healthcare-industry, both currently and in future applications [25]. Moosavi and Taherood (2023) systematically reviewed research to blockchain's security challenges. The authors also examined fields of blockchain usage, including smart contracts and consensus mechanisms. They found that the Internet of Things (IoT) is a key context in which this provides security [26]. Adatoga et al. (2024) concluded a systematic review of blockchain technology in healthcare and associated security concerns. In this study, the authors examined the advantages, disadvantages, applications, and challenges of blockchain technology in healthcare. After critically assessing the features of blockchain, the authors concluded that this technology has high potential and could revolutionize the healthcare system [27].

From the related works mentioned above, the authors undertook an endeavor to review studies from the past 5 years (2019-2024) on blockchain technology. They found that some studies focused on systematic reviews of blockchain technology in the healthcare sector, identifying several benefits and drawbacks. The results of some studies indicated that blockchain could play a pivotal role in the healthcare sector. Additionally, some related works reviewed data security concerning learners; in this context, it was noted that research in Western countries often addresses students' data privacy. It was found that blockchain technology still has several disadvantages for data security and requires further research for improved effectiveness. This review section also explores students' perceptions of blockchain technology, revealing that learners generally have a positive attitude toward its implementation.
