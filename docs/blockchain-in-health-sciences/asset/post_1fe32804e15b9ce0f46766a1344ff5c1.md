## 21.2 Blockchain Technology in Healthcare

There are many characteristics of blockchain technology that can be used in areas other than crypto-currency. Immutability is one of these qualities; once a block is added to the blockchain, the data it contains cannot be removed and is always unchanged [36, 37]. This feature makes blockchain technology perfect for situations where transparency and data integrity are important [38]. Decentralization is another noteworthy of blockchain technology. It functions without depending on a central authority to validate transactions. It uses a network of nodes to reach an agreement on the ledger's current state [39].

Because of its decentralization, blockchain technology may be used in situations where security and trust are crucial. The other component makes it easier to build networks that easily communicates data exchange is interoperability [40]. Smart contracts are a type of blockchain technology that makes it easier to create executing contracts since the parameters of the agreement are encoded right into the code. Because of this feature, which increases automation and efficiency, blockchain technology is a good fit for applications that need simplified procedures [41]. The essential component of blockchain technology is security. Blockchain protects the integrity of data kept inside the network and guarantees transaction security through the use of sophisticated cryptographic algorithms [42]. Because of its emphasis on security, blockchain technology is especially well-suited for fields like intellectual property protection and healthcare, where strong security measures are essential [43].

### 21.2.1 Understanding Blockchain in Healthcare and Drug Discovery

Blockchain is a great solution for protecting sensitive data a system since it is a decentralized network of nodes that securely stores data [44]. This technology is a great tool for gathering all pertinent papers in one place and makes it easier to share sensitive information in a safe and private manner [45]. Also, by using a single patient database, blockchain speeds up searches for candidates who fit particular trial requirements [46]. Blockchain is defined as a decentralized peer-to-peer (P2P) network of individual computers called nodes that maintains, saves and logs action or historical data [47]. Because all network members communicate and preserve information, this system allows for dependable cooperation and ensures that previous and present experiences are continuously tracked [48]. Blockchain technology has gained respect for its immutability and safety since it can combine diverse networks to offer insights the relevance of individual treatments. The three main ideas in blockchain are miners, nodes, and blocks [49]. Blockchain technology does not consolidate data in one place like traditional systems do [50]. The blockchain is replicated and shared over a network of computers, where each machine updates its blockchain to include each new block [51].

### 21.2.2 Applications of Blockchain in Healthcare

Blockchain technology's distributed ledger architecture makes possible to transmit patient medical information securely [52]. It improves the safety of medical records, manages the pharmaceutical supply chain and helps scientists track genetic codes [53]. The incorporation of blockchain technology has the potential to mitigate issues in the healthcare by improving patient services, preventing unnecessary spending, and protecting patient data [54]. Applications for it include safely encrypting medical data and efficiently controlling disease outbreaks [55]. With its many uses, blockchain technology is essential to the advancement of digital health as:

- improving the supply chain's transparency.
- enabling electronic health records that are focused on the patient.
- using smart contracts to supply chain and insurance operations.
