# Abstract

Innovation in the field of IT also impacts healthcare by developing novel and user-friendly products and applications. Blockchain, the Internet of Things (IoT), and cloud computing are recent technologies in the field of information technology that are used in healthcare, enabling healthcare function independently and provide better treatment for patients. IoT plays a vital role in the healthcare industry by facilitating connected healthcare, remote patient monitoring, and alerts to doctors. Blockchain and cloud technologies provide additional security for medical data. All healthcare organizations are shifting towards cloud technology to enhance information security management, efficiency, and advantages over other technologies. The proposed technology offers a secure, cost-effective cloud solution integrated with blockchain and IoT for medical device connectivity. By utilizing this technology-based healthcare method, there are numerous benefits that will significantly improve the quality and efficacy of treatments, thereby enhancing patient health.

**Keywords:** Healthcare, blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), cloud technologies, medical device
