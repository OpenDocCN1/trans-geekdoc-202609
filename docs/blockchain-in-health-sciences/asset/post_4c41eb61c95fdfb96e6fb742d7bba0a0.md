## 18.8 Conclusion

This chapter focuses on the analysis of healthcare systems that utilize IoT technologies. The discussion included various care support systems, illness detection and prediction systems, and recommendation systems. The study investigated the requirements for the use of cloud and fog servers in healthcare data processing and storage. Subsequently, it examined the necessity of blockchain technology in safeguarding medical data and implementing access control. Moreover, it highlighted the essential security parameters and the challenges associated with creating an efficient and robust IoT-based healthcare system for real-world implementation. The author anticipates that this work will assist future researchers in developing solutions that are more safe, useable, and practical.
