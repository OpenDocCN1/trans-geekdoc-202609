# 5.2 Literature Review

Agbo et al. (2019) conducted a systematic review of blockchain technology in healthcare following PRisma (Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analysis) guidelines. After a thorough review, the authors found several applications of blockchain technology in healthcare but noted a lack of prototype implementations [18]. Febih and Lahami (2020) carried out a broader survey on the application of blockchain technology in healthcare, addressing some limitations identified in this approach. Lastly, the authors discussed open questions and further research related to this new method [19]. Shi et al. (2020) conducted a systematic review of the integration of blockchain technology with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems, focusing exclusively on data security and privacy aspects. After a careful review, the authors identified numerous challenges and future prospects [20]. Haleem et al. (2021) studied blockchain technology and its pivotal advantages in healthcare, revealing 14 significant applications. They highlighted several benefits of blockchain technology in healthcare, such as improved data efficiency, reduced fear of data manipulation, and enhanced levels of data security. Specifically, blockchain technology decentralizes data, which mitigates specific threats [15]. Kuleto et al. (2022) examined a different approach at blockchain network to create the most effective sustainable educational system in Serbia, Romania, and Portugal. The results clearly imply that although blockchain technology has some disadvantages, students considered this technology to have a significant positive impact on their learning performance [12]. Njeri (2022) conducted a study on data security and privacy issues related to blockchain technology. After reviewing the literature, the author concluded that as a technology, blockchain has several shortcomings concerning data and privacy; therefore, more research is needed to understand this concept better [21]. A similar study conducted by Saed et al. (2022) aimed to provide a systematic review of the potential paradigm shift of blockchain technology in healthcare. They reviewed a total of 1,12 research studies from 2016 to 2021 on blockchain technology and identified 51 articles that enhanced healthcare through blockchain. The authors concluded that future research studies on blockchain technology could play a vital role in health diagnostics, patient healthcare processes, data integrity, and fraud prevention [22].
