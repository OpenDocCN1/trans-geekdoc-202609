# Abstract

The Internet of Things (IoT) has transformed many industries, and its integration into healthcare management is especially significant. This chapter presents a complete review of IoT applications in health sciences, beginning with an explanation of the underlying concepts and historical evolution. It examines related studies to contextualize current breakthroughs and investigates essential elements of IoT systems designed specifically for healthcare administration. The chapter digs deeper into specific IoT applications, such as remote patient monitoring, smart medical equipment, and data-driven healthcare analytics. It covers the issues of IoT in healthcare, such as data security, interoperability, and system stability. The chapter also discusses how IoT technologies empower patients by improving self-management, personalized care, and involvement in their health outcomes.

**Keywords:** Internet of Things (IoT), healthcare management, remote patient monitoring, smart medical devices
