# Role of Bioinformatics in Healthcare

Combining biology, computer science, and statistics to organize, analyze, and interpret data, bioinformatics is crucial to healthcare. Healthcare data analysis use gene expression, protein-protein interactions, sequence analysis, genome annotations, comparative genomics, and other methods [54]. Medical studies using genome data have discovered hidden information regarding inherited disorders. In healthcare, genetic profiling measures illness vulnerability, which might indicate hereditary issues and provide molecular understanding.
