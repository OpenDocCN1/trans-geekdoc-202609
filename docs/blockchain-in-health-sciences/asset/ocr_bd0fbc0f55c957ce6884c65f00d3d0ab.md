(a) Healthcare: Blockchain, like artificial intelligence and the Internet of Things, has emerged as an advanced healthcare technology. In healthcare, blockchain is used to securely store and share patient data. The technology provides patients control over their medical records, allowing healthcare-providers to access them just as needed. This enables the seamless and secure sharing of medical information, leading to better treatment outcomes and lower administrative hassles.

(b)Identity management: Blockchain-based identity management systems improve security, privacy, and control over personal information. By putting identifying information on the blockchain, users can create a portable and verifiable digital identity. This removes the need for several identity documents, lowers identity theft, and streamlines the identity verification procedure [16].

(c)Voting systems: Blockchain technology can help traditional voting systems by creating safe and transparent voting platforms. Voting systems based on technology prevent voter fraud, preserve electoral integrity, and allow for remote voting while ensuring anonymity and privacy.
