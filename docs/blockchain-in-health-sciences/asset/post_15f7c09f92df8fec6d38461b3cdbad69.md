## 9.2.1 Types of Blockchain Technology

There are four primary forms of decentralized or dispersed networks in the blockchain. Figure 9.1 illustrates the four different kinds of blockchain.

- (a) **Public Blockchain Networks:** Public blockchains are permissionless and accessible to all users. Every blockchain user has the right to view, update, and validate the blockchain. Public blockchains are mostly used to mine and trade cryptocurrencies such as Litecoin, Ethereum, and Bitcoin [6].

## 9.2.2 Existing Blockchain Protocols

Various blockchain platforms can be used to construct applications, which are known as blockchain protocols. Each blockchain protocol tailors the underlying blockchain concepts to specific industries or applications. Several subsections follow, each containing an example of the blockchain protocol described in Figure 9.2.

- (a) **Hyperledger Fabric:** A collection of tools and libraries that make up the open-source Hyperledger Fabric project. Businesses can use it to swiftly and efficiently develop private blockchain applications. It is a general-purpose, modular framework with special access control and identity management features. Its qualities make it appropriate for a wide range of applications, including trade finance, supply chain tracking and tracing, loyalty, incentives, and financial asset clearing and settlement [10].

- (b) **Ethereum:** Ethereum, an open-source, decentralized blockchain platform, allows developers to create public blockchain apps. Ethereum Enterprise is designed for commercial applications.
