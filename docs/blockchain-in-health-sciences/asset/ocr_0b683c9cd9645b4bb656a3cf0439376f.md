# 19.7.1 Adoption Barriers

For the general public to use blockchain technology in genomics and healthcare, it is essential to have user-friendly interfaces and reliable software performance. Blockchain platforms undergo constant evolution and necessitate specialized expertise for implementation. This issue has been recognized as a significant obstacle in the implementation of solutions, as stated in numerous academic studies. The presence of instability and the absence of user-friendly interfaces act as significant obstacles to wide-spread public acceptance, hence restricting its utilization to those with advanced technological skills [33].

# 19.7.2 Interoperability

To implement blockchain technology in the field of genomics, enterprises must establish seamless integration and connectivity between blockchain platforms and pre-existing non-blockchain platforms. The fact that there is a wide variety of blockchain implementations, which of may not be intrinsically compatible, makes this problem much worse. Blockchains that are able to communicate with one another reduce dependency on any one blockchain platform. When it comes to genomics, most of the solutions that have been suggested are platform and feature specific. Adopting a multi-blockchain strategy, independent of any particular blockchain platform, will enhance scalability and mitigate the security vulnerabilities inherent in the chosen platform.
