62 BLOCKCHAIN IN HEALTH SCIENCES

IoT empowers patients by enhancing their engagement in the decision-making process. The technology facilitates seamless communication between patients and healthcare providers, enabling the co-creation of personalized health plans. Timely reminders, educational resources, and interactive platforms contribute to a more informed and engaged patient population. Especially during the COVID-19 pandemic, the role of IoT in telemedicine and remote monitoring has been accentuated. Patients can access healthcare services remotely, reducing the burden on healthcare systems and ensuring continuity of care. This not only improves accessibility but also empowers individuals to actively manage their health while adhering to necessary safety measures. However, amidst the promises of patient empowerment through IoT, challenges such as data privacy concerns and the need for digital literacy must be acknowledged. Ensuring inclusivity in the deployment of IoT solutions is crucial to prevent the creation of healthcare disparities [26]. In this section, we will delve deeper into the ways IoT technologies contribute to patient empowerment, exploring case studies, challenges, and future trends that shape the evolving landscape of healthcare in the era of interconnected devices.
