# SMART Healthcare Systems with IoT and Blockchain 371

Apart from the benefits for patients, IoT technology can also be used to streamline hospital operations and drug supply. Large hospitals face many problems in the administration of their departments, resources, and supplies. IoT can provide solutions for this by delivering timely and accurate information as well as performing predictive analysis. Efficient resource utilization and avoidance of inventory loss can be achieved by integrating sensors into hospital beds, wheelchairs, and other medical equipment. It can act similar to an asset tracking system, allowing real-time monitoring of the location, status, and use of hospital equipment. The use of IoT devices in hospitals can also improve patient mobility by providing real-time data on bed occupancy, staff distribution, and patient waiting times. In addition, managing medicines and checking pharmacy inventory levels can be made easier using IoT technology. It can consume trends, stock levels, and expiry dates for each drug, making healthcare supply chain logistics more efficient and automated.

Despite the many benefits of IoT technology in healthcare, it is very important to ensure the security of the overall system. All information, whether it is related to the patient or the hospital management, must be confidential and only authorized persons should have access to it. Patients' data is highly sensitive, and no one wants to get it in public domain, especially if they are suffering from leprosy, HIV, or other socially stigmatized diseases. In addition, hospital management information must be kept from unauthorized access and alteration; otherwise, they can cause the hospital's services to fail. Also, all the interconnected IoT devices must be properly authenticated because any of them starts behaving in a malicious manner, it may result in incorrect patient treatment or the lapse of the hospital's services. Therefore, confidentiality and integrity of data and information, authenticity of IoT devices and proper authorization of users are essential for IoT environments in healthcare.
