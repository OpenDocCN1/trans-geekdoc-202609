**Cryptocurrency:** Cryptocurrency is a digital asset that may be traded on a blockchain network. It is a subset of digital currency that the government does not issue. Consider bitcoin to be tokens issued by private firms or organizations that may be used to buy things sold by others on the blockchain network. As of September 2023, CoinMarketCap, a market research website, has identified 9,111 traded cryptocurrencies.

Bitcoin is the first and most widely adopted cryptocurrency. It was invented by an unknown person or group of people using the moniker Satoshi Nakamoto. Bitcoin operates on a decentralized network known as the Bitcoin blockchain, which allows participants to transfer and receive it without the use of intermediaries. Bitcoin mining, a computationally expensive operation, adds fresh bitcoin to the supply. Miners overcome complex mathematical tasks; the miner who completes the puzzle and validates the transaction receives new Bitcoin.

# 9.6 Blockchain Examples and Use Cases

Blockchain continues to evolve and gain acceptability as more businesses in many industries learn to use it. Blockchain’s use cases and industry applications have expanded far beyond its initial Bitcoin application to encompass smart contracts, cybersecurity, the Internet of things (IoT), and non-fungible tokens (NFTs). NFTs are digital assets that represent all or part of real-world artifacts like art or music. They are a popular way to purchase and sell digital artwork because they can be bought, sold and swapped online [15].
