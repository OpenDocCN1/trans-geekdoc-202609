Our system, through smart contract coding and algorithms, is broad enough for direct implementation into the supply chain framework that distributes various perishable goods, although our main focus is on the pharmaceutical supply line. The capabilities of pocket contracts, based on the dynamics of the code written in the Remix IDE, were demonstrated using various scenarios, as those aimed restoring trust in drug delivery, identifying and fixing transport problems, and speeding up refund processes. We intend to implement these smart contracts using the Hyperledger platform and open decentralized applications, also known as DApps, with a wide variety of real-world entities like medicine producers, distributors, and patients. Thus, this business objective is truly monumental in building a trustworthy and robust supply chain ecosystem underpinned by dynamic smart contract technology. Future tasks will involve deploying these intelligent contracts on Hyperledger and creating decentralized apps (DApps) that include real entities such as manufacturers, suppliers, payers, and patients. The aim is to establish an open and robust supply chain ecology.
