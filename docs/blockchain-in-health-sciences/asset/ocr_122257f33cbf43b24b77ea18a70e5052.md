# Abstract

There are many changes occurring in the field of pharmaceutical research which includes healthcare, drug discoveries, supply chain systems, breaking the historically adopted technologies and innovations. One of the most popular and regulated technology in pharmaceutical trends is blockchain as it is helping to reduce many challenges of this sector as it is not open, not able to track the record of orders being placed and does not have trust for the shipping of drug products. Many people require various pharmaceutical drugs for their daily needs. Thus, it is required to adopt technology like blockchain which can provide open access and can track records while maintaining the integrity and trust. There are many fake drugs available in the pharmaceutical market which can be monitored and controlled with the help of blockchain technology using a set of clear standards and safeguards. In this study, various advantages and disadvantages with blockchain in the pharmaceutical industry have been discussed. Initially, the chapter includes detailed description about the blockchain technology, a new era in pharmaceutical industry by providing illustrations of various applications of drug discovery supply chain, drug counterfeit etc. with their associated frameworks.
