Given the associated risks, pharmaceutical waste cannot be treated like regular waste and necessitates special handling, whether originating from a hospital, clinic, pharmacy, or private household [4]. Blockchain technology could improve information security adherence, economy of scale, and efficient garbage disposal. Electronic medical records are more accessible, secure, and shared than traditional records. In the pharmaceutical industry, blockchain can guarantee consistency and security through unchangeable transactions, trustworthy verification processes, and one authoritative source of truth. The transparency and tamper-proof nature of blockchain can facilitate drug monitoring, eliminate fraud, and enhance supply safety [5]. Blockchain rejuvenates the pharmaceutical supply chain by offering a cohesive, transparent, and secure platform available to all parties involved. A work presented a novel framework [23], for providing valuable directions to regulators, producers, and governments to implement the blockchain in the pharmaceutical industry. A hybrid framework of blockchain and IoT based has been presented [24], which can assist in reducing the counterfeit problem to provide solutions to the pharmaceutical industry. A work has been proposed to provide solutions to supply chain for selling the pharma products online by utilizing the Ethereum smart contract to maintain the authenticity between the participants [25, 26].

The fundamental characteristics of immutability, transparency, and decentralization offer a strong outcome, improving effectiveness and safety throughout the supply chain.
