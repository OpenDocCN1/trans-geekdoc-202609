# 15.6 Pharmaceutical Turnover

Blockchain technology may help reduce pharmaceutical turnover by improving supply chain operations, minimizing waste, and ensuring proper distribution of supplies. Because of this, pharmaceutical companies can save costs and patients can have their prescription when they need it (Figure 15.3) [34].

# 15.7 Prescription Drug Monitoring

To address the issues with traditional PDMPs, blockchain technology offers a decentralized, transparent, and secure solution. Here is how blockchain technology can revolutionize the way prescription medication monitoring is done:

+   - Decentralized Data Sharing: To ensure that authorized patients have access to the most current patient prescription information, blockchain enables states, pharmacies, and healthcare providers to securely transmit data in real-time. It prevents patients from taking advantage of data silos by seeking several drugs from different sources.
