# BLOCKCHAIN IN HEALTH SCIENCES

**Blockchain Network:** At the center, representing the decentralized data management system.

+   - Wearable Devices: On the top left, providing real-time health data.
- Healthcare Providers: Supply medical records and vitamin D test results on the top right.
- Mobile Health Apps: On the bottom left, tracking dietary intake, sun exposure, and supplement usage.
- Patients: On the bottom right, they receive and manage their health data through the blockchain network.

The arrows indicate the data flow from these sources to the blockchain network and the patients. This setup illustrates how blockchain technology can streamline health data management and contribute to mitigating vitamin deficiency.

Susceptibility: Blockchain can make it easier to gather information instantaneously on people's dietary habits, ultraviolet (UV) vitamin levels, and other pertinent parameters. Intelligent contracts may streamline collecting operations, such as devices which wear ultraviolet radiation or nutritional consumption, to ensure data accuracy and promptness. Youth with access to individualized data are more likely to take precautions against illness because they are more aware of their vulnerability to a lack of vitamin D and the possible health effects.

Severity: By their present dosages and associated risks, blockchain-enabled health data can offer consumers individualized information regarding the possible seriousness of sunshine insufficiency. An impression of urgency to remedy deficiencies in vitamin D can be fostered by raising the public's understanding of its severe health risks using public and unchanging medical data.

Benefits: Blockchain platforms can help spread evidence-based decision-knowledge and treatments mean to encourage young people to get enough vitamin D. Digital contracts can encourage desirable actions, such as consistent exposure to the sun or taking vitamin D supplements, providing prizes or tokens that may be exchanged for services or products associated with health. Thus, engaging youth and drive blockchain-facilitated measures makes it possible to focus on the potential benefits of maintaining appropriate amounts of vitamin D.
