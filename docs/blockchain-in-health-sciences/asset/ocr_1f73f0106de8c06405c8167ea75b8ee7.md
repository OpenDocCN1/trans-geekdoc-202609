Figure 13.3 illustrates the application of blockchain technology across various fields within the pharmaceutical industry. Each segment represents a different area, such as supply chain management, quality assurance, regulatory compliance, data security, and clinical trial transparency, where blockchain can offer enhanced security, traceability, and data integrity. This integration aims to address challenges related to data tampering, counterfeiting, and inefficiencies, ultimately ensuring a safer and more reliable pharmaceutical environment.

A number of researches have been conducted using blockchain technology in the pharmaceutical industry for different purposes. Cryptographic methods are utilized to validate blocks of transactional data for addressing the significant security challenges [6]. Multiple perspectives determine how security aspects manifest. Serialization has been used to address the security risk posed by counterfeit drugs [7]. This technology identifies that confirms serial numbers across the supply chain system. To prevent drug theft, drug traceability has also been improved [8]. The digital signatures are used to achieve the quality control and data miners, blockchain, chain codes and health information are used from manufacturer to the pharmacy to ensure the authentic quality [9].
