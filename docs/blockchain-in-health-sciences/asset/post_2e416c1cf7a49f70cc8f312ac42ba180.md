## 5.8 Conclusion

The current research is conducted to explore the perception of UG students (engineering and general-degree colleges) regarding blockchain technology and its impact on healthcare and data security in West Bengal, India. After analyzing the data, the results indicate that the overall perception of UG students shows that 73% have a moderate or average level of understanding, while very few students (10%) have a high level of perception, and the remaining students (17%) have a low level of perception regarding blockchain technology. When analyzing the overall UG student population (289 students), we can see that perceptions of blockchain technology significantly differ based on location and stream. In the analysis of general college students (130 students), perceptions significantly differ based on location, semester, and category (General, ST, SC, and OBC), but no significant differences are found regarding gender. Similarly, when the authors analyzed the data from engineering students (159), they also found significant differences in perception based on location. Several variables indicate that UG students from both Engineering and general-degree colleges have significantly different perceptions regarding blockchain technology as it relates to healthcare and data privacy. On the other hand, some variables, such as gender (289 UG students), semester (only among Engineering students), category (289 UG students), stream (general-degree college), and category (Engineering students), do not differ significantly. From the above discussion, we can conclude that the majority of UG students from both institutions have a moderate level of perception regarding regarding blockchain technology in West Bengal. This means that of 289 UG students, almost 83% are in favor of blockchain technology, highlighting its importance in students' lives. Since higher education represents a crucial phase of any nation's educational system, neglecting it may lead to unresolved issues. Notably, the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 of India also emphasizes the utilization of new technological innovations in classroom environments. By light of this, we must consider how the teaching-learning process will evolve in the future. Therefore, the importance of blockchain technology in healthcare and data privacy for students cannot be minimized. It is expected that this study will uncover new directions for blockchain technology and will prove beneficial for our social and educational context across all levels of students.
