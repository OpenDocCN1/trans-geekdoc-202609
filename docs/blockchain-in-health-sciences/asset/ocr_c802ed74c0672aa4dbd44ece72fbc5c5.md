# Abstract

The present work is an endeavor to explore the perceptions of general and engineering college students regarding the potentiality of blockchain technology in relation to their healthcare and data privacy in the state of West Bengal, India. A descriptive survey method was used. A total of 289 undergraduate (UG) students (159 from engineering colleges and 130 from general colleges) were selected as a representative sample of the population. A self-made perception scale was constructed by the researchers. Data were collected using a Google form. Mean, standard deviation (SD), t-tests, and F-tests were utilized to analyze the data. The results revealed that the level of perception of UG students toward blockchain technology is moderate. Additionally, there is no significant difference in the perception of students based on gender and category variables. However, differences in perception were observed based on location, institution, stream, and semester. Analyzing the perceptions of students from general-degree and engineering colleges separately reveals students from general-degree colleges have a moderate perception of the potential of blockchain technology in healthcare and data security.
