# Abstract

The fragmented nature of healthcare data, which frequently leads to inconsistent patient care, is the issue at hand. Our proposal makes use of the revolutionary potential of fog computing and blockchain technology to resolve this problem. Blockchain eliminates the difficulty of maintaining consistent medical records by ensuring data integrity, traceability, and access control. Sensitive medical data is protected by adding privacy-enhancing features to healthcare blockchains. We investigate how the Internet of Things (IoT) and fog computing are evolving currently, boosting data processing and management capacities, which ultimately results in better patient care. Our suggested paradigm shifts a healthcare ecosystem where patient privacy is strengthened, data-driven decision-making becomes feasible, and patient-centric concepts, and various healthcare stakeholders readily collaborate. The immutability of blockchain, combined in this architecture to enable safe blockchain Electronic Medical Record (EMR) sharing, deviating the need for patients to repeatedly tell their medical histories. We provide consumers with a user-centric solution for proactive health management with our ground-breaking Personalized Health Insights Dashboard, which offers personalized data visualization and AI-driven insights. At its core, this strategy alters healthcare data management, fostering improved wellbeing and reasoned choices.

**Keywords:** IoT, electronic medical records, access control, cloud, fog computing, CP-ABE, blockchain
