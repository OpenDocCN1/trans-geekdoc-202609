**d. Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS):** A development of the fundamental concepts behind Proof of Stake is represented by the consensus algorithm. Daniel Larimer, the man of Steamed, EOS, and Bit Shares, invented the DPoS consensus mechanism in 2014. Every staker in the Proof of Stake consensus mechanism is eligible to take part in the “minting” phase, which gives them the authority to choose two nodes that add new blocks to the blockchain and verifying existing ones [28]. To select nodes that verify blocks and uphold the DPoS system, an election method is employed These nodes are designated as “witnesses” or “block producers.”

The DPoS consensus procedure is as follows:

+   **Voting:** Voting in the DPoS consensus gives users the choice of casting their votes themselves or delegating their power to a third party to do so. Selected witnesses who verify transactions generate blocks. They are paid if they verify and sign each transaction in a block; the payment is typically divided with the voters who selected the witnesses. If a witness fails to verify every transaction within the given timeframe, they miss a block, all transactions remain unverified, and they are not entitled to any reward. The prize is increased by the following witness's award, which attaches to that block. When these transactions are gathered by the next witness, the block in question is called stolen. The percentage of each voter's vote represents their stake.
