# Abstract

Lifestyle diseases have seen an upsurge worldwide. Micronutrient deficiencies like Vitamin D (VDD) have been pointed out as the underlying cause of many disease burdens in urban and rural populations. Young adults in urban settings are especially vulnerable to such deficiencies because of their lifestyle choices. This relates to their beliefs and perceptions, shaping health behaviors. Among the many behavior theories, the Health Belief Model (HBM), shaping the theoretical foundation, is the most widely used for various disease conditions. This paper explores the existing research on VDD in India, particularly Youth.

Further, an attempt has been made to explore whether the HBM can help understand behaviors that may stimulate the Youth to engage with inadequate vitamin D intake. When addressing challenges regarding vitamin D deficiency, especially within the youth population in urban settings, this paper proposes the integration of Blockchain-Enabled Health Platforms as a transformative solution. By leveraging decentralized health data management, blockchain technology can enhance the effectiveness of health interventions and encourage youth participation in Vitamin D-deficiency behaviors. The transparency and security offered by Blockchain can facilitate real-time tracking of sunlight exposure, nutritional intake, and health outcomes, creating a trustful and immutable record. This integration aligns with the Health Belief Model and introduces a novel approach to promoting and sustaining health behaviors through innovative and secure digital platforms.
