Furthermore, centralized systems in the digital health sector have several issues, such as losing a single component, which can bring down the whole system [4-8]. In recent years, blockchain has become vital in practically all trades worldwide. The healthcare problem is one of the public issues being handled in many regions across the country, and the blockchain system has been adopted in terms of data security and handling, as health data must be secure and kept confidential. Blockchain is proposed as a tool for dealing with difficult concerns related to health security. Blockchain functions as a decentralized system that can connect multiple computers through nodes and involves no transactions to create a new block that allows the transfer of information from one individual to another, allowing patients to access all medical information via the blockchain, which is protected by cryptography. Blockchain improves data security and management at a lower cost, making it appealing to healthcare. To protect and monitor electronic health information, blockchain servers have control regulations for authorized holders. The pharmaceutical business needs blockchain’s decentralized method to combat counterfeit drugs in the supply chain. The most popular blockchain application is electronic health records, which secure store patient data for life and can be retrieved at any time. To broaden blockchain development, clinical trials, telemedicine, consensus, and security have been added [9–11]. With the advancement of blockchain technology, bioinformatics has proved its utility in the healthcare industry, including drug development, sequencing technology, personalized medicine, gene therapy, and much more. The pharmaceutical sector has already transitioned from trial-and-error to computer-based drug design, resulting in cost and time savings through successful and reliable drug design processes. Bioinformatics assists the pharmaceutical industry with computational tasks such as drug target discovery, rational drug design approaches, and drug testing and design. In addition, bioinformatics tools and approaches are used to analyze high-throughput screening data during the drug development process. However, bioinformatics is becoming increasingly important in analyzing next-generation sequencing data utilized in clinical practice. Bioinformatics is used in personalized medicine to analyze data from sequencing or gene expression studies to uncover novel variations or mutations that could affect a patient’s response to therapeutic changes.
