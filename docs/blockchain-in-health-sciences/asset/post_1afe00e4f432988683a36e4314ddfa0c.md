## 12.4 Blockchain-Based Existing PSC: Case Study

In [29], the authors have depicted a generalized blockchain ecosystem that consists of a presentation covering various types of Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), primary key components required for blockchain application development environment and various blockchain applications, and use cases in a single diagram. Large IT giants have already ventured into the blockchain domain to render the supply chain management (SCM) system more transparent and traceable. IBM, the global IT giant has developed the platform IBM Blockchain Transparent Supply for transparent operability of supply chain management system. IBM has collaborated with the Sonoco ThermoSafe, which is a leading provider of pharmaceutical distribution while ensuring assurance packaging. This collaboration targets in building an industry-scale blockchain platform, named as PharmaPortal. PharmaPortal's generic features are suitable to all vendors' generic requirements.

The aim of PharmaPortal is to enable pharmaceutical manufacturers and carriers to work on the safe delivery of biological products, conventional medicines and vaccines, and other temperature-sensitive products. This openly governed network is based on IBM Blockchain Transparent Supply. It is focused on end-to-end traceability, and aims to provide audit trail of monitoring operations of the environmental conditions. As network convener, Sonoco's objective is to form a permitted blockchain platform for the vendors that will impart increased transparency throughout the pharmaceutical supply chain [30]. Even though the initiative has seen many improvements since its inception, the cold chain pharmaceutical supply chain still suffers from inefficient processes. For, multiple handoff operations across contracted and sub-contracted handling distributors across continents create an important requirement for trusted, traceable pharmaceutical shipments and documents. With blockchain's inherent ability to trace drug provenance, and form an immutable record of the life-cycle of drugs and their handling, an open initiative industry aims to provide solutions to the challenges that the pharmaceutical industry encounters, both in sourcing and distributing drugs [30].

In [31], the authors illustrate how QR code-based digital identity system can be used along with blockchain technology in pharma supply chain. The author also includes a few case studies for this system. Merck KgaA is one of the oldest science and technology (S&T) company in PSC that operates in healthcare, life sciences, and electronics business segments. It developed SAP blockchain-based POC app in collaboration with SAP for PSC management system [32]. Blockchain technology was not used in SAP's earlier operations.

The Basel-based multi-national company Novartis initiated the PharmaLed project to explore potential applications of blockchain technology in the healthcare research industry. As a result, PharmaLed association evolved with the aim of providing the blockchain and distributed ledger technology (DLT) solution in PSC management system for better transparency and availability. Their E1 app enhances security by eliminating the use of a central server; and this app also ensures that there is no single point of failure, and promotes availability. However, there are still many obstacles to overcome. There is a lack of interoperability and an inconsistent availability data between/among the drug manufacturer, wholesaler/distributor, and the pharmacy. There exists many IT interfaces and as information is isolated from one-end-to-another, it leads to inconsistent demand management and inaccurate forecasting [31, 34]. There is a huge realm to enhance the existing well-known blockchain-based PSC management systems. In the next section, we analyze the future of blockchain in pharmaceutical supply chain management system.

A hyperledger based blockchain architecture is an ideal mechanism for solving data asset challenges in PSC. Despite the enormous efforts into streamlining pharmaceutical delivery and management, storage and supply of pharmacy drugs is often hampered by unavailability of information, discrepant security of data assets, incoherent privacy control, and restricting authorized users who are the rightful owners of data assets from controlling access. The hyperledger fabric is a framework based on blockchain technology for developing enterprise-grade applications. This distributed ledger platform possesses advanced privacy and access control wherein the nodes share data that they want between permitted nodes or network participants.

As shown in Figure 12.6, there are three main components of the hyperledger fabric, an access control module based on permitted blockchain framework, an off-chain data storage, and a user-centric mobile and web user interface. In order to ensure a high level of application performance and economic viability, the data assets related to pharmaceutical manufacturing process are encrypted and stored on a secure cloud-based repository and a hash of the asset's uniform resource identifier (URI) is stored. This method securely connects on-chain and off-chain data without increasing computational and storage load that prevails in blockchain architectures. While relying on smart contracts, the data asset owner (the manufacturer) or their designated (distributors in the supply chain) can restore access control their data in a scalable and secure fashion.

All the nodes in the network have their respective identities, which is issued by a membership services provider (MSP). MSP uses a public key to issue cryptographic certificates to each network participant. As a result of cryptographic registration, identity issuance and verification and audit of Hyperledger blockchain is performed in a trusted environment. Due to this feature, it is possible to incorporate a consensus mechanism that is significantly computationally lightweight than the Proof-of-Work (PoW) mechanism.
