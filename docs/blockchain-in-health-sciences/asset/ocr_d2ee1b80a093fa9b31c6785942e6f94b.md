A care support system monitors or tracks an individual's health and transmits the corresponding data to a centralized system. In this regard, Jeong and Shin [2] proposed a health monitoring system that can be installed in a vehicle, not limited to an ambulance. This system monitors the health of a patient during transportation and provides doctors detailed information about the patient's condition before they arrive at the hospital. This allows doctors to plan the treatment in advance, minimizing any potential delays. Jabbar et al. [3] and Ullah et al. [4] developed their own frameworks to achieve semantic interoperability across various heterogeneous devices utilized in patient monitoring. Ramirez López et al. [5] introduced a system to track the health of individuals experiencing respiratory problems. Vadaei et al. [6] developed a framework for monitoring an individual's health status in a COVID-19 environment and alerting them when necessary to maintain a safe distance. Ghashemi et al. [7] put a short home architecture that incorporates medication management and health monitoring capabilities for elderly patients. The patient monitoring system developed by Sahu et al. [8] transmits data to the cloud. In addition, the system provides an alert in the event of an emergency. An alternative monitoring system, as proposed by Bushang [9], acquires ECG data of distant patients in real-time and transmits it to the cloud. Rahman et al. [10] introduced a framework for the remote monitoring of patients with co-morbidities that have recovered from COVID. The devices acquire patient data and transmit it to a cloud. Ali et al. [11] developed a software that facilitated the transmission of patient-related data from various peripheral sensors to the cloud for the purpose of remote health monitoring. It conveys the patient's vital indicators to the physician as well.

Illness detection systems utilize IoT technology to identify health issues that may be present in patients. Illness prediction systems are implemented to forecast patients' susceptibility in developing a specific health issue by analyzing predetermined parameters.
