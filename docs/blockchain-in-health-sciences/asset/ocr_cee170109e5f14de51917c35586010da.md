# Current Developments of Blockchain Technology
in Bioinformatics and Healthcare

Abstract
Healthcare provides or receives a medical treatment that helps treat or prevent diseases through development. The systems included hospital networks and disease management, which provide healthcare to people. As an emerging field, the healthcare system has huge, valuable data on patients' diagnoses, treatments, medical conditions, etc., which can improve health advances in the future. In health care, a vast amount of data is generated from numerous sources, which may pose challenges as maintaining, storing, transferring, and assuring the security and integrity of the data. The blockchain network is utilized in healthcare to protect and exchange data from multiple sources, which can identify mistakes and improve data performance, security, and transparency. Blockchain technology is also used in bioinformatics, which uses computational techniques and tools to analyze healthcare data, including biological data. These tools also analyze, interpret, and develop healthcare data. As a result, it becomes essential to understand the diverse roles of blockchain and bioinformatics in various healthcare datasets. So, this study aims to provide an in-depth understanding of block-chain and its association with bioinformatics elements, followed by the healthcare aspects and the advancement and challenges frequently recurring.
