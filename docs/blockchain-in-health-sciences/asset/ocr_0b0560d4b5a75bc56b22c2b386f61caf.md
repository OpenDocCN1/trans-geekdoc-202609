3.1. The perception layer helps gather a variety of information along the pharmaceutical supply chain. Included in this are a number of IoT assets, such as sensors, QR codes, RFID tags, readers, and more. IoT gateway-will receive the gathered data. Of particular importance is that the data—including information regarding the source of raw materials, pharmaceutical production, logistics, etc.—are defined, configured, and analyzed by the IoT gateway. There are three hallmarks of such data: continual collection, massive data scale, and ongoing granularity refining.

3.2. The core of the off-chain layer is the intermediary software known as the blockchain gateway. Management of devices, validation of data, and data caching is its three main features. Internet of Things (IoT) device needs its own distinct identification, or “key pair” for device management purposes. Adding, removing, and changing data status is the responsibility of the blockchain gateway managers. The purpose of the validation data is to confirm the data identification and the structure of the transaction data. Keep track of financial transactions until they are published to the blockchain network, data storage is utilized. Internet of Things (IoT) assets and enterprise information systems provide the blockchain gateway with input data format in JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) is defined in advance. Stakeholder transactions involving drugs that are sent to the blockchain network are the products of the blockchain gateway.

3.3. The blockchain layer incorporates elements such as cryptography, smart contracts, consensus, and P2P networks, among others. Our team has settled on pBFT as the gold standard. When it comes to regulation, we offer a consensus technique that is both efficient and effective. Nodes on the blockchain network serve the purpose of assessing the dependability of the data stored on the blockchain. To ensure the safety of data transmission, cryptography is utilized. To accommodate all of the links in the medication distribution chain, the P2P network uses a decentralized network architecture.

3.4. The application layer comprises four services: customer identification-management, quality control, product tracking and visibility, risk analytics and smart alert. The tracking and tracing explorer connects to the blockchain network using application programming interfaces (APIs), allowing various users to request the appropriate pharmacological data.
