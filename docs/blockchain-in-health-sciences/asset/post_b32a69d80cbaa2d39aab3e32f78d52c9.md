### Abstract
Healthcare provides or receives a medical treatment that helps treat or prevent diseases through development. The systems included hospital networks and disease management, which provide healthcare to people. As an emerging field, the healthcare system has huge, valuable data on patients' diagnoses, treatments, medical conditions, etc., which can improve health advances in the future. In health care, a vast amount of data is generated from numerous sources, which may pose challenges as maintaining, storing, transferring, and assuring the security and integrity of the data. The blockchain network is utilized in healthcare to protect and exchange data from multiple sources, which can identify mistakes and improve data performance, security, and transparency. Blockchain technology is also used in bioinformatics, which uses computational techniques and tools to analyze healthcare data, including biological data. These tools also analyze, interpret, and develop healthcare data. As a result, it becomes essential to understand the diverse roles of blockchain and bioinformatics in various healthcare datasets. So, this study aims to provide an in-depth understanding of blockchain and its association with bioinformatics elements, followed by the healthcare aspects and the advancement and challenges frequently recurring.

## Introduction

Good health is the most essential aspect of life. Various factors can influence people's health; healthcare refers to maintaining and improving a patient's physical and mental health by preventing, diagnosing, treating, and managing diseases, injuries, and disabilities. The impact of healthcare varies depending on the patient's capabilities and demands. Chemists, nurses, physicians, and other healthcare professionals contribute health through preventative care. To maintain patients' health, they conduct frequent testing, examinations, and health education, in addition to managing chronic illnesses and providing long-term care. Currently, healthcare systems consist of government, politics, outside organizations, and commercial authorities. Healthcare providers, emergency personnel, and patients comprise the three primary components of healthcare. Healthcare components include fundamental medical traits and access alternatives, such as health insurance. Storage, privacy, ownership, and data-related concerns are addressed by healthcare digitalization. Digital healthcare, which is frequently abbreviated as “digital health,” is a comprehensive multidisciplinary term that encompasses the integration of information technology and digital technologies within the healthcare sector. Telehealth, electronic health records, digital medications, and other technologies enhance healthcare accessibility, reduce expenses, and streamline clinical operations [1–3]. The pandemic has forced the healthcare industry to adapt to patient needs, revealing gaps and flaws. Population growth, medical services, and costs have raised health costs. Patient data administration is complicated and expensive for healthcare providers. Healthcare data is vast, while managing it while maintaining patient privacy and data security is difficult. Moreover, medical records are still predominantly handwritten, causing complexity in the scrutinizing data. Data modification and duplication are key challenges while keeping medical records since different people are involved in distinct locations, and inaccuracies such as mismatched records, lack of details, and data duplication are also visible. In digital healthcare, electronic health records demonstrate a viable solution where the difficulties of ample storage and data retrieval remain, but there is a risk of data loss due to system failure or server delay. Furthermore, data accessibility is another issue addressed by medical suppliers, as medical data is one of the most crucial components of providing reliable information.

## BLOCKCHAIN IN BIOINFORMATICS AND HEALTHCARE

Although data recovery remains difficult in digital healthcare due to the variety of data formats, the healthcare sector faces numerous issues because of data scaling. Furthermore, centralized systems in the digital health sector have several issues, such as losing a single component, which can bring down the whole system [4-8]. In recent years, blockchain has become vital in practically all trades worldwide. The healthcare problem is one of the public issues being handled in many regions across the country, and the blockchain system has been adopted in terms of data security and handling, as health data must be secure and kept confidential. Blockchain is proposed as a tool for dealing with difficult concerns related to health security. Blockchain functions as a decentralized system that can connect multiple computers through nodes and involves no transactions to create a new block that allows the transfer of information from one individual to another, allowing patients to access all medical information via the blockchain, which is protected by cryptography. Blockchain improves data security and management at a lower cost, making it appealing to healthcare. To protect and monitor electronic health information, blockchain servers have control regulations for authorized holders. The pharmaceutical business needs blockchain’s decentralized method to combat counterfeit drugs in the supply chain. The most popular blockchain application is electronic health records, which secure store patient data for life and can be retrieved at any time. To broaden blockchain development, clinical trials, telemedicine, consensus, and security have been added [9–11]. With the advancement of blockchain technology, bioinformatics has proved its utility in the healthcare industry, including drug development, sequencing technology, personalized medicine, gene therapy, and much more. The pharmaceutical sector has already transitioned from trial-and-error to computer-based drug design, resulting in cost and time savings through successful and reliable drug design processes. Bioinformatics assists the pharmaceutical industry with computational tasks such as drug target discovery, rational drug design approaches, and drug testing and design. In addition, bioinformatics tools and approaches are used to analyze high-throughput screening data during the drug development process. However, bioinformatics is becoming increasingly important in analyzing next-generation sequencing data utilized in clinical practice. Bioinformatics is used in personalized medicine to analyze data from sequencing or gene expression studies to uncover novel variations or mutations that could affect a patient’s response to therapeutic changes. Despite the fact that bioinformatics is not exclusively associated with medicine, it has made substantial contributions to gene therapy, which entails the replacement of defective genes with functional.
