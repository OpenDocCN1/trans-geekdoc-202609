The Internet of Things: Internet of Things is a system of interconnected electronic devices that are capable of exchanging data and communicating with each another, enabling the development of intelligent knowledge. An Internet of things can be created out of any networked system comprising of “things”. Arguably the most famous application of the Internet of Things is the Smart Home, which enables all household appliances, including lighting, ACs and smoke detectors, to be connected to one platform. Security is required for this highly dispersed system, and blockchain provides it. A system’s security on the Internet of Things is only as strong as its least secure device, or weakest link. In this instance, blockchain makes sure that the data collected by IoT devices is confidential and is available to a limited number of individuals.
