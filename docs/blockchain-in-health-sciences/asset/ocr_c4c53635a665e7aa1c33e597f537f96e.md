# ### 14.6.2 Use Cases and Case Studies

Blockchain technology is revolutionizing pharmaceutical research as it has has many useful applications. For example, Pfizer and IBM’s clinical research blockchain (CRB), as well as other platforms, enable data sharing in clinical trial data management while ensuring security and openness for all stakeholders [14, 16, 17]. To prevent counterfeit drugs, chronicled leads the MediLedger project, which tracks product authenticity throughout the medical supply chain. Blockchain technology also protects intellectual property rights in the pharmaceutical industry, and groups as WIPO are researching how to use blockchain technology to manage intellectual property. Blockchain technology is used by MolecularMatch to provide secure sharing of genomic data, and VeChainThor and DNV collaborate to provide regulatory compliance solutions. These examples show how blockchain enhances data integrity, supply chain transparency, protection of intellectual property, collaboration etc.

With this technology, there is an exciting future and the number of issues can be handled in an effective manner.
