(d) Sharma et al. [36] introduced a privacy-preserving paradigm for Internet of Things (IoT) healthcare-based systems based on data-mining techniques to monitor healthcare information systems. One of the most important aspects of modern life is the Internet of Things (IoT) healthcare system, [37] which has significantly expanded the big data-driven medical system. IoT-based wearables are already being used by medical professionals to expedite the diagnostic, monitoring, prediction, and treatment processes. The ease of use and low cost of adoption of IoT are starting to transform healthcare services. The authors offered potential solutions to these problems to offer practical data analytics for IoT-based healthcare systems that protect privacy. The different IoT sensors can monitor the physical surroundings and record changes in the environment in real-time make up the IoT sensor data layer [38]. A self-adaptive filter in the recently added framework automatically recovers missing or incorrect data. As a result, the system can withstand errors.

(e) Srinivas et al. [39] introduced a cloud-based user authentication method to enable safe authentication of medical data in the Internet of Things healthcare monitoring system. Utilising this procedure, a confidential session key is generated to facilitate secure communication between the authorized user and the wearable sensor node. A security analysis was conducted by the authors of the proposed mechanism, utilising the Real-Or-Random (ROR) paradigm, in order to assess its resilience against several widely recognised attacks.

(f) Raifa Akkaoui [40] proposed a decentralised authentication technique for Internet of Things healthcare systems that utilises blockchain’s decentralised features in order to lessen the detrimental effects of distributed denial of service (DDoS) attacks on Internet of Things environments. The technique has been evaluated by the author for security and privacy research on the Ethereum network. The researcher believes that the plan safeguards the privacy, confidentiality, integrity, and anonymity of IoT users and devices.
