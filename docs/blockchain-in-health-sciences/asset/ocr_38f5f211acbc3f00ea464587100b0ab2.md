There is still a need for a reliable, decentralized system that makes sales and transactions transparent, traceable, and trustworthy. Introducing a state-of-the-art blockchain-based solution ensures the safe distribution of pharmaceuticals and establishes a decentralized ledger accessible to all supply chain participants, protecting consumers. By offering a distributed, decentralized network with an immutable ledger, blockchain technology eliminates the need for a centralized verification authority. While primarily in cryptocurrency (Bitcoin) coins, blockchain extends its utility to various domains, including exchanges, contracts, tracking, and payments. Each transaction is securely recorded on blocks distributed across multiple ledger copies, ensuring transparency and security. Blockchain stands as a disruptive influence poised to advance the effectiveness and openness of the entire supply chain, due to its efficiency, impacting aspects ranging from storage to financial transactions, scalability, and absence of centralized control. At the heart of this transformation lies the concept of smart contracts facilitated by blockchain. These digitally enforced, viable contracts ensure trustworthy without intermediaries. Smart contracts are implemented using Ethereum, an open-source, public decentralized computing platform [21, 22]. Events are guaranteed to be visible to authorized chain members on the publicly available blockchain. The integration of blockchain technology and Internet of Things (IoT) device technologies is altering pharmaceutical supply chain management and customer interactions. Blockchain facilitates transparency, empowering consumers with information. This integration addresses trust issues within the supply chain by timestamping transactions and sharing them with authorized parties, including consumers. To enhance supply chain management efficiency, IoT sensors collect data in shipment containers, ensuring product quality and authenticity. Smart contracts, particularly in critical scenarios like temperature-controlled shipments, automate processes and record events to enhance transparency and manage supply chain. The need to create an open and decentralized pharmaceutical supply chain management system is met by a framework that utilizes blockchain and IoT. The goal of the proposed solution is to improve the authenticity, dependability, and transparency of pharmaceutical goods for end users by eliminating the need for reliable third-party authenticators, introducing cutting-edge smart contract-based supply chain management, and utilizing IoT sensors in conjunction with dynamic smart contracts.
