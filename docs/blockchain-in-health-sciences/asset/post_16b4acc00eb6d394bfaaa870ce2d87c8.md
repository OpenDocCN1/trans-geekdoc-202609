# Abstract

Innovation in the field of IT also impacts healthcare by developing novel and user-friendly products and applications. Blockchain, the Internet of Things (IoT), and cloud computing are recent technologies in the field of information technology that are used in healthcare, enabling healthcare function independently and provide better treatment for patients. IoT plays a vital role in the healthcare industry by facilitating connected healthcare, remote patient monitoring, and alerts to doctors. Blockchain and cloud technologies provide additional security for medical data. All healthcare organizations are shifting towards cloud technology to enhance information security management, efficiency, and advantages over other technologies. The proposed technology offers a secure, cost-effective cloud solution integrated with blockchain and IoT for medical device connectivity. By utilizing this technology-based healthcare method, there are numerous benefits that will significantly improve the quality and efficacy of treatments, thereby enhancing patient health.

**Keywords:** Healthcare, blockchain, Internet of Things (IoT), cloud technologies, medical device

## 6.1 Introduction

Providing the aging and expanding population with appropriate and beneficial healthcare is one of the 21st century's most important issues. Innovation is necessary in the healthcare sector. The government, life sciences firms, health plans, and providers are all facing growing expenses and uneven results. They are trying to reduce costs and spending while simultaneously enhancing services and health outcomes.

Health science is the study of applied science that deals with human physical and behavioral well-being.

In recent times, the healthcare industry has encountered various obstacles stemming from the global digital revolution, the pandemic, significant changes in the population, and increasing patient expectations [1, 2]. In the era of personalized healthcare, evidence-based treatment, and digital customer service, it is critical for startups, entrepreneurs, and healthcare professionals to remain up to date on the latest developments that are reshaping the sector. There are few technological innovation trends in the healthcare sector that have emerged and provide support to health sciences:

- 1. Artificial Intelligence
- 2. Big Data
- 3. Internet of Things
- 4. AR/VR
- 5. Telemedicine
- 6. Cloud Computing
- 7. Speech Recognition
- 8. Blockchain

Ensuring data safety and security is the major challenge in the healthcare industry. According to the report, there were 692 large healthcare breaches reported between July 2021 and June 2022. These data breaches included missing credit card and banking information, as well as health and genomic testing records. Here, there is a necessity to enforce and tighten security in the medical data industry.

The innovation of new computer technologies like IoT, big data, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and cloud technologies play a role in almost all fields, including smart healthcare systems. Cloud technology supports smart healthcare systems by storing medical records in a safe and secure cloud. IoT connects everything in the world to the Internet. The role of IoT is to collect data, store it in the cloud, and share the data between hospitals for further diagnosis. The success of all these technologies relies on security as a main parameter, which can be enhanced by blockchain technologies in smart healthcare systems [3].

Blockchain has the property of an immutable ledger, which authenticates users and establishes a trusted network between parties. All healthcare records need blockchain to improve the trust and privacy of patient record sharing. Among the recent innovations in digital technologies, blockchain is such innovative technology that provides tremendous solutions to various problems across different fields. Medical research is currently being conducted by various researchers, providing several solutions to improve the efficiency of handling large volumes of data. Blockchain in the healthcare industry stores patient records and secures them against various threats such as corruption, accidents, and malicious intruders. Transaction details are tracked by the blockchain network.

Next, cloud computing adopted in the healthcare industry improves efficiency and reduces costs. Cloud computing allows records to be stored, maintained safely, and shared easily. After the introduction of electronic medical records (EMR), the entire industry migrated to a cloud-based platform to store patient data. However, challenges remain in adopting cloud technology to transform the entire management process of the healthcare industry. The healthcare sector as a whole cannot become productive and efficient if cloud solutions are the only tools used. Healthcare firms must leverage data management, IoT, and AI technologies to fully benefit from this technology.

The IT sector has been revolutionized by IoT and cloud computing, and the healthcare sector will undergo a transformation when these technologies are combined with intelligent systems. Given the volume of data that devices produce, it would be wiser to store it in a cloud environment. However, medical data are extremely private and security issues persist. Blockchain offers a potential distributed network that guarantees data security and promotes authenticity and transparency in transactions. The data used in this work was gathered from various sensor devices and sent to the cloud via the blockchain network and wireless body area network (WBAN) [4].

Blockchain and the Internet of Things are dependable technologies in a variety of scenarios where IoT devices have the capacity for autonomous data capture and sensing [5]. However, a drawback is the inability to apply cryptographic security measures. Recent research shows the impact of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and blockchain is revolutionizing various industries. The fusion of these technologies creates a new revolution in emerging fields. Artificial intelligence enables machines to think and make decisions, while blockchain provides distributed ledger technology with security and transparency features. Blockchain, along with AI, is used to create controls for developed products. Enhancements such as security can be trusted by ensuring secure deployments, and efficiency can be improved through various AI algorithms [6–8].
