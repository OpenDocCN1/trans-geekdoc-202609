- **Witnesses:** The witnesses considered in the top tier may be constrained in the range of 21 and 101. The reward given to the witnesses for validating transactions is a further addition of a block in the blockchain. It is the responsibility of the witnesses to stop the inclusion of the suspicious transaction in the block. Since voting ends, a witness in the top tier is always in danger of losing his/her position to a user who receives more votes and is consequently regarded as more reliable.

- **Delegates:** Blockchain governance is supervised by a body of delegates chosen by users of decentralised public sector platforms. They don't participate in managing transactions. The size of a block and the compensation a witness should receive for attesting to a block can be changed by the delegates. Users on blockchain have the option to vote in favor of or against the proposed modifications.

- **Block Validators:** In Distributed Proof of Stake (PoS), block validators are complete nodes that confirm witness-created blocks who adhere to the consensus guidelines. Any user can validate the network using a block validator, but there is no incentive to check blocks.

- **Proof of Borrowing (PoB):** Rather than buying hardware, validators can “burn” money via which it can no longer be retrieved. Proof of Borrowing by sending it to an address from which it is inaccessible. Validators can participate in networking by committing their money to an address that is inaccessible. A method of random selection is used to accomplish this. Depending on how proof-of-borrowing is applied, miners have to spend the currency earned for the creation of the blockchain. Their chances of being chosen to mine the following block increase with the amount of money they burn. PoB is still a resource-saving protocol, even though it provides a strong substitute for Pow.

- **Proof of Capacity:** Burst coin, one cryptocurrency, is now using this very new type of mining. Despite this, a lot of individuals think that proof of capacity is a workable substitute for the mining methods that are now in use. It makes mining possible by utilizing hard drive space. This kind of method has several advantages, and many projects are currently investigating it.

- **Proof of Elapsed Time (PoET):** A randomized timing method is used in place of the mining-intensive rights necessary for Proof of Work in the PoET process. By distributing the odds of winning among the greatest number of network users, the PoET consensus mechanism increases the likelihood that a random lottery system will choose each node. Node in the blockchain network will produce a random wait time before going to sleep for that amount of time. The node that becomes the block leader at this point is the first to wake up, (the first in the network to have its timer expire after the predetermined amount of waiting time.) This node creates the new block, signs using its private key address before adding it to the blockchain network. The required data is then sent to every peer network, and the procedure is repeated to find the next block leader.

# 1.4 Applications of Blockchain

Blockchain has several uses (shown in Figure 1.3) in the healthcare industry; some well-known ones are as follows:

- **Asset management:** In the field of Asset Management, blockchain technology contributed exceptionally. The management and trading of a person's many assets, such as fixed deposits, stocks, bonds, real estate, commodities, and other alternative investments, is referred to as asset management in its broadest definition. Conventional asset management trade processes can be very expensive, especially when transferring money between countries [29]. In these kinds of situations, blockchain can be quite useful due to the removal of intermediaries i.e., brokers, managers, organizations like banks etc. The distributed ledger reduces the error and provides simplicity in creating the chain.

- **International Payments:** Have you attempted sending money across borders in multiple currencies? The money may not arrive at its destination for several days due to the drawn-out and complex process. Cross-border transfers have become simpler because of the use of blockchain in remittance services, which offers end-to-end services with the need for middlemen. Many remittance providers offer blockchain services that enable round-the-clock international payments.

- **Medical Care:** With smart contracts, blockchain has significant promise for the healthcare industry. These astute connections allow two parties to draft a contract directly. Every party involved in the agreement is aware of its specifics, and as soon as its requirements are met, the agreement becomes operative [29]. Wearable personal health records that are encrypted using blockchain technology, accessible only by primary healthcare professionals holding the key can be highly helpful in the healthcare industry. They also support the HIPAA Privacy Rule, which restricts who can access patient data and preserve privacy.

- **Digital Money:** Cryptocurrency is, without a question, one of the most widely used uses of blockchain technology. Who hasn't heard about the incredible rise in popularity of bitcoin? One of its many benefits is blockchain technology for cryptocurrencies with no geographical restrictions. As a result, cross-border cryptocurrency transactions are feasible [29]. The only factors to think about are exchange rates and the possibility of losing money. Even yet, this choice is still far superior to local payment applications, like Paytm in India, which are only available in a certain area and cannot be used to send money outside.

- **Certificates of Birth and Death:** Many people worldwide, particularly in the world's poorer nations, lack a legitimate birth certificate. According to UNICEF, in three children under the age of five do not have a birth certificate [30]. Death certificates are a contributing factor in this issue. Blockchain, on the other hand, can assist in solving this issue by establishing a certified, safe store for birth and death certificates that is only open to authorized individuals.

- **Verification of Identity Online:** Online identity and authentication are prerequisites for completing any financial transaction. This also holds true for any service providers users may encounter in the financial and banking sectors [30]. However, blockchain can centralise identity online, allowing individuals to share their identities with any service provider after completing a single identity verification process using a cryptocurrency. Additionally, users have the option of selecting which identity verification techniques—like facial recognition or user authentication to utilise [31].
