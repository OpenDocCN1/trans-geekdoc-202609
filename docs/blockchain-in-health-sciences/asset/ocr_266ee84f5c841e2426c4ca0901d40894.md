# Advancement and Challenges

Hospitals and health care centers collect and store huge amounts of data, including patients’ personal information, diagnosis, treatment process, and other administrative work. This information is very vulnerable and should be kept secure and confidential. Therefore, hospital data is being organized using Blockchain technology more and more to improve security, interoperability, and efficiency in healthcare systems. Moreover, bioinformatics is vital as it helps transform biomedical research with genomic information towards the development of new medication. This process required several vital steps followed by data retrieval, validation, and experimental confirmation. Blockchain and bioinformatics software can be secure and transparently manage patients’ crucial records, machine learning algorithms, and vast healthcare data.
