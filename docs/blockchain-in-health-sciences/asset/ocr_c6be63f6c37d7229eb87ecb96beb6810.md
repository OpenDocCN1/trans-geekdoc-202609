# Future Work and Research Opportunities

Healthcare systems encounter numerous challenges, including data integration, such as the inability to maintain health records, a limited budget, insufficient access, a lack of preventative care, and a lack of trust in maintaining health data. However, blockchain technology overcomes all these problems and gains trust by maintaining and building the digital infrastructure. Because it is a digital database system, it can store all types of healthcare data, including all data from online consultation apps and prescriptions. This system makes it easier for doctors to understand a patient's health history and tailor the information accurately. Blockchain protects its layers and keeps patients' data safe. Furthermore, counterfeit drugs are the most common problem worldwide. Blockchain applications can regulate drug movement, making it easy to find fake drugs on the supply chain. These are harmful to the public and may cause severe health issues. However, according to medical research, blockchain facilitates the formulation and sharing of new drugs, with clinical research playing a crucial role in ensuring drug effectiveness. Blockchain not only makes the entire system transparent and faster, but it also has the potential to revolutionize the system by ensuring security and integrity. Besides the blockchain, bioinformatics integration can aid in the development of healthcare. One example is personalized medicine, which bases drug target identification on genomics, epigenetics, proteomics, and transcriptomics studies. Today, using advanced bioinformatics speeds up genome testing in center, potentially saving many lives. AI and machine learning are transforming healthcare as a part of bioinformatics by integrating algorithms and machine learning models that could predict early interventions to enhance the clinical workflow. In today's world, the healthcare sector is demonstrating interest in technology such as blockchain and bioinformatics, which expand the infrastructure and are highly beneficial in minimizing patient care time. Along with the advancement and integration of computational approach, designing proper protocol is needed with high accuracy to address various factors.
