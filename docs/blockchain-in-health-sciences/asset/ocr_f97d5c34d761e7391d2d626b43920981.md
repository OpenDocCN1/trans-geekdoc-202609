# 15.9 Conclusion

Many industries are looking for new technologies, such as blockchain, to brighten their future. There has been a recent shift in blockchain research towards its primary applications in healthcare, which includes health records, access control, and data interchange; applications like supply chain management and pharmaceutical prescription administration are presently relatively rare. Thus, blockchain offers a great deal of promise that has yet to be realized. Internet of Things (IoT)-enabled smart healthcare systems face significant security challenges and threats. To lessen these dangers one must understand the security of such systems. The security requirements of an IoT-enabled smart healthcare system cannot be met by conventional security measures due to inherent vulnerabilities, high initial investment, restricted scalability, and limited resources of IoT devices. Another is that blockchain is not perfect. Blockchain technology has an issue with scalability. The current state of the supply chain makes it difficult to implement a Blockchain solution for all participants. To ensure the success of large-scale installations involving multiple customers, far more comprehensive testing is required.
