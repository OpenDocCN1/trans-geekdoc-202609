# # 17.3 Fundamentals of Deep Learning and CNN

The area of artificial intelligence has been truly revolutionized by the ele- gant co-evolution of deep learning and convolutional neural networks (CNNs). In the field of computer vision in particular, this synergy has rev- olutionized our data analysis and understanding capabilities. Deep learn- ing emerged as a potential approach for machine learning applications in current times. DNN, or convolutional neural network, acquires feature representations by means of training and does not rely on manually crafted features as input. When the CNN features are trained appropriately using a large representative set, they are projected to output- form hand-engineered features since they have a high level of selectivity and invariance. Deep learning is a method for representation learning in which complex layers of neural network architecture learns to automati- transform input data into multiple layers of data representation. In case of input images from medical reports and other applications, pattern recognition is required as an important phase and is accomplished with CNN. When the data set are large, CNN can learn from the training models, for a said objective, by recursively adjusting its weights with back- propagation thereby extracting important and relevant features.
