# 15.3.5 Tracking and Tracing

Commodities in transportation should be monitored and traced from the point of dispatch to the destination. This makes perfect sense. Delivery delays are a headache for companies everywhere, but they can be particularly deadly in the healthcare and pharmaceutical industries. Blockchain technology is now being used by pharmaceutical supply systems [26]. Blockchain technology has the opportunity to revolutionize pharmaceutical traceability. This design not only promotes customer confidence but also allows for transparent medication monitoring, privacy for users, quality control, non-repudiation, and demand-supply management [27]. An extensive quantitative study was carried out in India to elucidate key aspects of blockchain technology, such as roles, credibility, and ability to monitor medicinal data [28]. Internet of Things (IoT) devices have also been used to assess the quality of pharmaceutical items and confirm the reliability of data sources, regardless of time or place. As an intermediary for data storage and sharing, blockchain guarantees transparency and traceability. Thanks to blockchain-based track and trace; important stakeholders in the supply chain may examine vital information on the date, location, and storage techniques of products.
