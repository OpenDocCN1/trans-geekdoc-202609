# 7.3.3 Blockchain and AI in Healthcare

Blockchain technology in healthcare proposes significantly more than mere data storage; it also protects data while it's shared and addresses privacy issues [59]. The healthcare industry is utilizing blockchain and AI to develop a new of technological applications that are linked to increased efficiency and real-time analysis. Aspects like diagnosis, analytics, validation of medical reports, and decision-making are added by the AI [11]. Blockchain offers advantages in a number of areas, including interoperability, security, and decentralization. Both Blockchain and AI have shortcomings, which will be addressed in greater detail in later sections. The shortcomings of each technology can be adequately overcome by combining these technologies [12]. The system is capable of using AI to learn, comprehend, and form conclusions because it can use blockchain to provide safe and reliable data. When blockchain technology and AI are integrated, a decentralized, safe, and immutable system can be established for the data that AI-driven systems require [6]. Initially primary data and information security occur because of this combination of AI and blockchain [4]. Additionally, the incorporation of blockchain technology with AI and IoT devices enables for the safe and prompt monitoring of patient health data, giving medical professionals trustworthy information for making decisions while safeguarding patient privacy [59].

Information security has been enhanced and automated decision-making can be trusted more when blockchain and AI are integrated. Furthermore, it offers decentralized intelligence that is highly effective [7].
