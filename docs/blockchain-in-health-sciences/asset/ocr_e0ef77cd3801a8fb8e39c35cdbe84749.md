# 64 BLOCKCHAIN IN HEALTH SCIENCES

Real-world case studies provide tangible evidence of the Internet of Things’ usefulness in encouraging active involvement by patients and improving healthcare outcomes. Looking ahead, the report sees a dynamic future in which continuous advancements and rising trends reshape the landscape of patient-centered care. The transformative potential of IoT in healthcare not only tackles current issues but also lays the groundwork for a digitally empowered and centered patient approach to healthcare delivery.
