# 300 BLOCKCHAIN IN HEALTH SCIENCES

The goal of the proposed Ethereum blockchain-based architecture, “ChainSure”, is to address the shortcomings of the current health insurance management system [14]. It provides an automated, secure, transparent and scalable system for health insurance management. This framework helps consumers choose insurance policies that best meet their needs using the TOPSIS technique. ChainSure provides a decentralized ecosystem with an interactive user interface. Empirically, ChainSure outperforms other state-of-the-art models in terms of latency, cost, and service integrity.

Blockchain technology considered a revolutionary new technology with the potential to create major breakthroughs, similar to the Internet and the internal combustion engine. Despite being aware of the excitement surrounding blockchain technology, businesses remain skeptical about its true benefits. This study [15] examines academic research and business practice to understand the extent of blockchain adoption and use in the insurance industry. The goal is to identify blockchain adoption trends in the insurance sector and conduct a comparative study of literature. The study evaluates the potential and actual fragmentation of the industry based on the knowledge of a group of people.

Systems to detect fraudulent claims and payments are needed because health insurance fraud is a major problem that costs individuals, businesses, and governments a lot of money [16]. An in-depth study that addresses health insurance security issues and proposes solutions based on blockchain and AI shows the application of blockchain technology and artificial intelligence (AI) to ensure fraud detection in Health Insurance. By leveraging blockchain technology and AI algorithms, the proposed solution provides a safe and smart way to identify health insurance fraud. To illustrate the successful operation of blockchain and AI-based systems, a case study of health insurance fraud is presented.
