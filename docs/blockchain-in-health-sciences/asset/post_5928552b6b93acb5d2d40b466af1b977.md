## 13.3 Blockchain for Securing Pharmaceutical Trades: A Layered Approach

A growing and pressing problem on a global scale, particularly in developing nations, is the manufacture and sale of fake pharmaceuticals. The yearly market value of counterfeit pharmaceuticals has surpassed billions of dollars. The pharmaceutical industry’s supply chain flows contribute to medicine counterfeiting. Drugs pass through manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors, and chemists before reaching the consumers. There is an absence of transparency in the system for the pharmaceuticals regulatory body; manufacturers have no idea what happened to their products; recalls are expensive and time-consuming, and corporations are unable to follow up with patients under the current supply chain structure. From the time a drug is created until it reaches the patient’s hands, the pharmaceutical industry will use the suggested system to monitor its journey. For statistical purposes, a database will be updated after a patient’s drug usage to reflect the drug’s effects. To ensure that only reliable users may access the network and add transactions to the blockchain, it will be implemented as

permissioned. The layers involved in the system are the perception layer, off-chain layer, blockchain layer, application layer, and user layer.

3.1. The perception layer helps gather a variety of information along the pharmaceutical supply chain. Included in this are a number of IoT assets, such as sensors, QR codes, RFID tags, readers, and more. IoT gateway-will receive the gathered data. Of particular importance is that the data—including information regarding the source of raw materials, pharmaceutical production, logistics, etc.—are defined, configured, and analyzed by the IoT gateway. There are three hallmarks of such data: continual collection, massive data scale, and ongoing granularity refining.

3.2. The core of the off-chain layer is the intermediary software known as the blockchain gateway. Management of devices, validation of data, and data caching is its three main features. Internet of Things (IoT) device needs its own distinct identification, or “key pair” for device management purposes. Adding, removing, and changing data status is the responsibility of the blockchain gateway managers. The purpose of the validation data is to confirm the data identification and the structure of the transaction data. Keep track of financial transactions until they are published to the blockchain network, data storage is utilized. Internet of Things (IoT) assets and enterprise information systems provide the blockchain gateway with input data format in JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) is defined in advance. Stakeholder transactions involving drugs that are sent to the blockchain network are the products of the blockchain gateway.

3.3. The blockchain layer incorporates elements such as cryptography, smart contracts, consensus, and P2P networks, among others. Our team has settled on pBFT as the gold standard. When it comes to regulation, we offer a consensus technique that is both efficient and effective. Nodes on the blockchain network serve the purpose of assessing the dependability of data. To ensure the safety of data transmission, cryptography is utilized.

3.4. The application layer comprises four services: customer identification, quality control, product tracking and visibility, risk analytics and smart alert. The tracking and tracing explorer connects to the blockchain network using application programming interfaces (APIs), allowing various users to request the appropriate data. Producers of pharmaceuticals, for instance, are obligated to furnish their legal certifications such as GMP or GSP to receive quality services. The tracking and tracing explorer allows the user to search the drug circulation history for product information. Smart analysis and warnings are achieved through the use of risk analytics and smart alert. To add, remove, and update members, the senior manager of this blockchain system utilizes identity management.

3.5. In the user layer pharmaceutical companies, pharmaceutical logistics companies, pharmaceutical retailers, and end users make up the four groups that comprise it. Producers must submit data pertaining to product quality to the blockchain and electronic codes to manufactured pharmaceuticals during the manufacturing process. To achieve a full chain during the drug distribution stage, logistics providers must monitor and store in the blockchain several drug storage factors, including temperature and humidity. At the same time, the blockchain-based network allows quality authorities to swiftly access drug quality information. Users can access drug quality information and get fast feedback on adverse occurrences using this tool.
