# 5.7 Future Research Opportunities

As per BIS Research statistics, by 2025, the immediate application and integration of blockchain technology in the healthcare industry could save expenses associated with IT, operations, support services, staff, and health data breaches by over $100 billion annually [28]. Blockchain technology is still in its initial stage, but the trending appearance of this technology denotes that it has a prominent future in the field of research. Therefore, it is essential to conduct more research for technological exploration and unleash new directions. Furthermore, blockchain technologies will allow transactions to be registered and verified with the permission of network participants. Blockchain will serve as the cornerstone of a new era of health information sharing by offering numerical security at the patient level through public and private encryption. Patient records, infringement prevention, improved interoperability, procedure rationalization, drug and prescription control, and medical and supply chain monitoring are all promised by this technology. In the future, it is expected that blockchain will perform extraordinarily in the healthcare sector.
