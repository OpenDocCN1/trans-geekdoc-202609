# 5.1 Introduction

Time passes according to the river's flow. Keeping up with the times, we now live in a world where everything is centered on science and technology [1]. Unquestionably, science and technology have played a significant role in the remarkable advancements made in the three primary spheres of human existence—education, industry, and health. The result of this progress is Artificial Intelligence (AI) [2]. Blockchain technology is one of the achievements of AI. Since around 2016, several industrialized nations, including the United States, the United Kingdom, Japan, and others, have recognized its significance. As a result, there is currently strong demand for it from numerous businesses and organizations across these nations3]. The late 20th century saw the emergence of the blockchain concept. We first got the idea of blockchain from Chaum's Ph.D. thesis published in 1982. Nick Szabo introduced Bit Gold, a decentralized currency, in 1998, but it publicly described in 2005 [6]. In modern times, society believes that Satoshi Nakamoto first the idea of blockchain technology in his article “Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System” in November 2008 [7]. Since its introduction, currency, particularly Bitcoin, has become more widely accepted and is currently commonly seen in the domains of finance, health, and education [8].
