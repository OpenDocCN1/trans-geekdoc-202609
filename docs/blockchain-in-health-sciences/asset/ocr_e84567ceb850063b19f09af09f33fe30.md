The study set out out to accomplish a number of particular goals, including identifying blockchain-based healthcare applications, looking at model apps created for these applications, investigating the difficulties and constraints related to blockchain-based healthcare apps, assessing the development processes used for these apps, and determining possible directions for further investigation. Blockchain technology holds promise for revolutionizing healthcare, with the potential to center the patient within the healthcare ecosystem and enhance the security, privacy, and interoperability of health data. This technology has the capacity to introduce a novel model for health information exchanges (HIE), rendering electronic medical records more efficient, disintermediated, and secure. While it is not a universal solution, this emerging field offers ample opportunities for experimentation, investment, and proof-of-concept testing. The utilization of distributed ledger technologies such as blockchain in the healthcare sector is still in its early stages, necessitating researchers to create additional proofs-of-concept and prototypes. This endeavor assist scientists in their quest to better understand and enhance technology within the healthcare systems.
