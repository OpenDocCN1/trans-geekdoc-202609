## 1.5 Future of Blockchain

When everything is considered, we should anticipate seeing even more avant-garde and creative uses of blockchain technology as it advances. Blockchain has the power to completely change a few sectors while also enhancing transparency and safety [33]. Based on historical and contemporary trends, blockchain is anticipated to see a major transformation in the coming decades. The applications are depicted in Figure 1.4.

There is no way that you can ignore the following five areas:

- 1. Cybersecurity

Today's businesses still struggle with data manipulation. Consequently, cybersecurity is one of the industries where blockchain growth is most potential. Blockchain technology allows users to verify the authenticity of content, prevent tampering, and encrypt data.

- 2. Government

Distributed ledger technology (DLT) systems can be used by governments in place of more conventional paper-based methods. Blockchain technology may also make voting easier, quicker, and safer. Blockchain technology offers an unchangeable voting record that reduces the possibility of fraud and forgeries. It will also make remote voting possible and in protecting voter identity.

- 3. Finance – Banking

El Salvador was among the first countries in 2021 to adopt Bitcoin as legal tender due to the cost of money transactions between financial intermediaries and global inflation. Many analysts predict that emerging countries will soon accept cryptocurrencies. National cryptocurrencies represent another fascinating area for upcoming developments in blockchain technology. It works with the conventional currencies that are in use today. With this money, users can transact without depending on any third parties. It also gives central banks the ability to control the amount in circulation.

## 4. Medical

Applications for maintaining patient data, managing drug supply, automated medical evaluation and treatment transactions, and more can be built with blockchain technology. Specifically, issues with the manufacture and dissemination of counterfeit vaccinations have been tackled, since blockchain technology would make it feasible to track the distribution of vaccinations and confirm the legitimacy of vaccine supply. IBM is among the leaders in the development of vaccination delivery systems using blockchain-technology.

## 5. Marketing

Blockchain technology will be useful in this situation. It lessens the possibility of advertising fraud because it can monitor and assess the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. Blockchain technology helps with automatic filtering, verification of advertising interaction, and the elimination of virtual accounts.

Blockchain technology will develop and become more widely utilized in a variety of fields and industries. Blockchain technology will improve trade efficiency and make life easier for everyone. Blockchain technology has the capability to make transformation in many industries and make life easier in our increasingly digitized society. Blockchain technology is an incredibly secure system that cannot be tampered. Thus, this technology will become increasingly important in the near future.

## 6. Healthcare

By improving the privacy, security, and interoperability of healthcare data, blockchain has the potential to have a big influence on the healthcare industry. It can facilitate the safe exchange of medical records across participating institutions and assist with a few of the issues the industry is now facing. It also eliminates the need for outside intervention and overhead expenses. Distributed databases frequently integrate blockchain technology, digital signatures, and encryption to ensure the privacy and validity of medical records.

## 1.6 Conclusion

Blockchain, with its key characteristics of auditability, anonymity, permanence, and decentralization, has shown itself to be a transformational force in well-established businesses. This article offers a thorough analysis of blockchain including its many forms and charting its development from version 1.0 to version 4.0. An extensive synopsis of blockchain technology is provided, clarifying essential elements and structural models. Interestingly, miners come to an agreement using consensus procedures, which are a key component of the block generation process.

Moreover, the discourse extends to applications and the future trajectory of blockchain technology. Particularly, the prominence of Bitcoin-based blockchain gathers attention, emphasizing its role as an immutable ledger facilitating decentralized transactions. Blockchain-powered applications are witnessing widespread adoption across diverse sectors such as financial services, IoT, and reputation management. Consensus algorithms emerge as indispensable to the operational integrity of decentralized systems, underscoring the necessity for ongoing research and innovation to propel the widespread utilization of decentralized technology.
