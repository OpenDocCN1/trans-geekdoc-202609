# 13.6 Summary

The use of blockchain technology in drug development, distribution, and discovery is revolutionizing several facets of pharmaceutical research. By offering an unchangeable transaction record, blockchain enhances the transparency and accountability of pharmaceutical research while safeguarding the dependability and integrity of clinical trial data. Blockchain has been utilized by pharmaceutical companies to enhance supply chain governance and compliance. It is as a result of the distributed nature, immutability, and transparency of blockchain technology. Smart contracts are created by blockchain technology to automate processes and keep track of each transaction. Even in the event that the compliance requirements are not met, smart contracts generate a particular transaction record and carry out alerts. In this chapter, the uses of blockchain, its layered approach as well as various challenges in pharmaceutical research have been discussed. The chapter showcased various characteristics of blockchain technology such as reliability, traceability, authorization, and security. The chapter also outlined the main domains in the pharmaceutical sector where blockchain technology is being used: product distribution, security, tracking, and prevention of drugs. Promising developments in clinical trials, tailored treatment, and expedited regulatory procedures are all part of the future of blockchain. To fully realize blockchain’s promise, though, stated obstacles must be overcome, including technological complexity, regulatory adaptation, and privacy issues.
