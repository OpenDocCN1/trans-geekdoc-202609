# AI & BLOCKCHAIN IN HEALTH: APPLICATIONS & CHALLENGES 343

Scalability concerns: Handling large volumes of health data can strain the system.

Data privacy and security: Balancing patient data protection with blockchain's transparency requires careful measures.

Regulatory and ethical challenges: The intersection of AI, blockchain, and healthcare raises ethical concerns and requires regulatory frameworks to ensure responsible adoption.

Blockchain technology faces a significant scalability challenge in managing the massive volume of health data. While blockchain's architecture guarantees data security and traceability, it's not optimized for handling high-speed data transmission and large data sets common in the healthcare industry. As a result, there are delays due to the need for multiple nodes to verify each transaction, whether by adding new data or making changes. This slows down the overall data processing efficiency.

Integrating AI and blockchain requires solving the challenge of AI's high computing needs and blockchain's limited transaction capacity. To bridge this gap, new protocols could be developed to handle AI outside of the blockchain. Alternatively, blockchain configurations with higher scalability could be explored to increase throughput.

In healthcare, worries about data privacy and security with combining AI and blockchain. Blockchain is an open record, making it hard to keep patient information private. Even though blockchain can make data safer by encrypting it and spreading it out, its openness can also put the privacy of sensitive medical information at risk. Methods are found to keep this data secret while still allowing blockchain's unchangeable nature to be used.

Balancing data accessibility with security is crucial when using AI and blockchain in healthcare. Blockchain's permission systems can restrict data access, but it must also allow AI systems to access large datasets for learning and improving patient care. Finding the equilibrium between data privacy and usability is vital to unlock the full potential of AI and blockchain in healthcare.
