# 17.1 Introduction

Deep learning is the most advanced technology currently available for machine learning. Exciting and high expectations have been brought about a result of the success of deep learning in a variety of pattern recognition applications. Deep learning, also known as artificial intelligence (AI), has the potential to bring about revolutionary improvements in the field of health care. Research conducted in the early stages of applying deep learning to the detection or categorization of lesions has demonstrated higher performance in comparison to that achieved by conventional techniques, and in certain tasks, it has even outperformed radiologists. New research and development efforts in computer-aided diagnosis (CAD) have been prompted by the possibility of integrating it with deep learning-based medical image analysis. This would allow for the provision of decision assistance to clinicians, as well as an improvement in the accuracy and efficiency of a variety of diagnostic and treatment procedures. The creation and use of deep learning tools in clinical practice encounter a number of obstaclesdespite the optimism that exists in this new era of machine learning. In the following chapter, we will address some of the challenges and efforts that are required to develop strong tools that are based on deep learning and to integrate these tools into the clinical workflow. This will allow us to make progress toward the objective of offering dependable intelligent aids for patient services. Deep learning and convolutional neural networks (CNNs) have co-evolved in a remarkable synergy, driving the field of artificial intelligence to new heights. This synergy has transformed our ability to analyze and understand data, particularly in the realm of computer vision. Deep learning utilizes artificial networks, which are mathematical constructs that draw inspiration from the biological brain. Initial versions were constrained by the limitations of computing capacity and the difficulties associated with training intricate networks. Nevertheless, the progress in back propagation algorithms and the widespread use of high-performance GPUs in the late 2000s facilitated the creation of more complex and efficient neural networks.

Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are a highly specialized form of deep neural networks that are specifically engineered to achieve exceptional performance in the task of image analysis and recognition.
