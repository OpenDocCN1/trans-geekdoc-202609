According to the Table 5.2, based on the cut-off point, the investigators verified H01(b): The cut-off point is M + $1\sigma$. Mean = 52.74, N = 130, and $\sigma = 12.09$. This means that $52.74 + 1 \times 12.09 = 64.83$ is M + $1\sigma$, and $M - 1\sigma = 52.74 - 1 \times 12.09 = 40.65$. Most general-degree college students (88, which is 67.70% of general-degree college students) scored between 40.65 and 64.83. It can therefore be concluded that the perception of general-degree college students is moderate regarding the potentiality of blockchain technology in healthcare and data security.
