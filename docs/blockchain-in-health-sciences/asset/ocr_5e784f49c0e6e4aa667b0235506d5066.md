The author discussed [11] how the healthcare insurance sector utilizes the blockchain-based B-SAHIC system to handle health insurance claims in a safe and efficient manner. It ensures confidentiality and transparency by giving all parties participating in the process access to a web-based portal. The system implements a smart contract and uses Hyperledger Fabric to construct claim processing algorithms. CouchDB is used to store OffChain data, guaranteeing a dependable world-state database. Encryption protects the privacy of claim-related data for clients; a new key is generated every time a client's private documents are submitted. Improved critical informationation security and confidentiality. With an average query latency redu-tion from 0.07 seconds (for two peer nodes) to 0.02 seconds (for six nodes) at 1300 queries per second, B-SAHIC offers a quick and scalable solution. For 300 transactions per second, the average transaction latency is about 3.5 seconds. Two peer nodes are all that are needed for the system to utilize around 350MB of resources. As a result, it is cost-effective and executed successfully.

Issues pertaining to data privacy, fraudulent claims, deceitful behav-ior, and insufficient openness are among the obstacles affecting the health insurance sector [12]. These problems might originate with patients, doc-tors, or even the insurance company itself. These problems make the health insurance market less efficient, which might result in increased premiums and worse medical care. Integrating blockchain technology into health insurance procedures may ease these worries by bringing security, privacy, and openness.

This study [13] describes the parametric insurance system and its usage in the transportation industry. It examines the application of blockchain technology to parametric transport insurance, covering issues related to implementation and design. The study focuses on the possible uses of parametric insurance in the following sectors: tourism, aviation, agriculture (including crop insurance), and catastrophe insurance. The study broadens our knowledge of the ways in which technology may be utilized to offer parametric insurance in the transportation domain and offers perspectives on the potential uses of blockchain technology across several sectors.
