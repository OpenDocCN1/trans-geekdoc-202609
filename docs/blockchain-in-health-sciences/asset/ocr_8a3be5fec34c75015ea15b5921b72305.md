# 1.3.1 How Does Blockchain Work?

A unique variation of the decentralised digital ledger idea is blockchain. This is made possible by technology that makes sure the documents are verified and preserved, so they cannot be changed on computers connected to a peer-to-peer (P2P) network, as cooperative copying and peer-to-peer transfers. It fulfils its mission to function in a safe, unchanging, transparent, democratic, and controllable manner in this way [13]. A few requirements have been met by this vision, and several common concepts across all blockchain technologies address these objectives. These include multiple stakeholders able to write records in a shared and trustworthy storage environment, the lack of trust or hierarchy among these actors who can add records, the rejection of the necessity or need for a mediator or central authority, the rejection of purposeful or inadvertent record alteration, and the requirement that users in the ecosystem have a degree of anonymity that will be decided based on fiction.

Blockchain is a method in which transactional data is stored in a secured manner. Here, blocks are connecting in a cryptographic order which is predefined. Transparency, decentralization, and security makes this technique one of the best methods for secure data storage.
