Information is enriched using the Internet in a decentralized process called a block, which comprises different kinds of information [10]. Each block has two main components: the block header and the block body. The block body evaluates and stores various types of data, whereas the block header links one to the next. Various data are kept in blocks, which are connected to create a chain [11]. This blockchain technology offers several unique features and benefits [12]: Ledger technology, where every link in a chain has access to the data and prevents third parties from altering this information; safety, where any transaction or piece of information is validated using a digital signature and cryptographic key for complete data security; privacy, in which crucial data are kept private; cryptocurrency, a consensus protocol, which enables the simple and secure exchange of information; where members in a chain or network validate transactions; and smart contracts, where data has cryptographic evidence, making it unlikely that the data will be compromised if obtained from the outside.

Currently, there is a dearth of study on the advantages of adopting blockchain in higher education, despite numerous publications about its application in other domains highlighting its benefits [13]. West Bengal is one of the developing states in India; thus, technology has not fully taken over the higher education system. However, several education institutions now have the opportunity to deploy technology as a result of COVID-19. Students consequently have the chance to learn about online learning and benefit from it. Therefore, it is anticipated that students will perceive the application of blockchain in higher education favorably.

As per the guidelines of the National Education Policy 2020 in India, students can currently transfer their academic credits to different educational institutions in West Bengal and India for higher study [14]. The benefits of using blockchain technology to implement the new policy will be more fully realized by students, instructors, and other higher education staff members.

Just as blockchain technology has incredible importance in the field of education, it also plays an important role in healthcare and data security. However, healthcare has evolved since the time of Internet 2.0 [15]. If blockchain technology can be utilized in healthcare and data security, its potential for improvement may peak even higher. It will enable the easy storage of patient data, doctor data, medical history, and more [16]. An Electronic Health Record (EHR) is a robust way to digitally secure various health-related information.
