# 6.5 IoT-Assisted Healthcare System

IoT-assisted healthcare systems bring applications to patients and health-care practitioners through remote monitoring, integration of medical devices, and the connection of sensors and devices. The role of IoT devices is to collect data from various medical cases and to provide visualization and analysis of the data with greater accuracy. # Sensor

Different types of sensors are used depending on the type of medical application. Examples include pulse oximeters, electrocardiograms, thermometers, fluid level sensors, and sphygmomanometers (blood pressure monitors) that read the current patient status (data).
