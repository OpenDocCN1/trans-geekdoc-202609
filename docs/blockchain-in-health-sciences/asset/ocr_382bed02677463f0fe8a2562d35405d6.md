# 3.5.3 Application Development

App development is a highly technical procedure that does not include physicians, patients, caretakers, or healthcare professionals [20]. As a result, the engagement of relevant individuals and agencies should be necessary during app development to assure app quality [21]. Furthermore, the software should be maintained on an ongoing schedule if new changes have to be made or a new pandemic strikes the planet [21].

# 3.5.4 Conventional Technology

The healthcare system ought to replace obsolete equipment and sensors to be compatible with the present setup [20]. Nevertheless, this will increase the cost of the healthcare system and introduce compatibility issues between the Internet of Things (“IoT”) system and current devices [21].

# 3.5.5 Expansion of Bandwidth

In the current setup, networking equipment is only used to power handheld gadgets and desktop PCs that handle hospital administration activities and a few lightweight apps [19, 20]. However, with the development of IoT, hundreds of thousands of sensors, and other linked devices, patient data must now be set to servers or clouds [21]. As transmission of data and communication rises, the medical sector requires updates and sophisticated networking equipment, including routers, switches, upgraded cabling, and enormous databases [20]. As a result, a large bandwidth is required to ensure that communication and transmission between the individual, their physician, and the health care system work smoothly and consistently [21].

# 3.5.6 Deployment of Protocols

There are variety pf IoT healthcare devices, and communication layers can operate under several scenarios, including reception, delivery, deep slumber, and sleep [21]. However, each layer’s power needs differ from the previous layers. As a result, it is challenging to implement a device identification system that certifies feature availability at the MAC layer while consuming the smallest amount of energy [22]. As a result, it is necessary to create a power-efficient technique at each layer for specific devices and settings that will be more adaptable in scenarios of pandemics [23].
