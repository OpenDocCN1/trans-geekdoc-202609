+   [42]: The DARC (Decentralized Anonymous Researcher Credentials) protocol allows researchers to protect their identities and selectively reveal credentials, hence strengthening data security and access control in federated genomic data. This method makes use of zero-knowledge proofs to guarantee that researchers disclose only the information required to access data and smart contracts to manage data access regulations. DARC gives researchers control over credentials and privacy by doing away with the requirement for central identity providers.

+   [43]: They propose a blockchain-enabled sharing platform, BEST, for the safe and patient-controlled sharing of PHR among healthcare practitioners. The BEST platform ensures fine-grained permission management, strong security, and privacy using blockchain access control that could throttle insider threats. The facilities it gives for automatic security and privacy controls logarithmically increase their functionality through event rules. Since this is scalable, updating the access permissions in hundreds of PHRs takes milliseconds only; hence, the performance is efficient. BEST makes a huge difference in the domains of data security and access control in the healthcare industry by fusing blockchain technology with state-of-the-art models of access control.
