
## 2.6 Summary

In this chapter the detailed potential of the Internet of Things (IoT) in healthcare systems has been discussed, which explores the applications, challenges and advantages of IoT in healthcare. The Internet of Things is able to handle the security and privacy issues of the medical healthcare system. This chapter defines the IoT framework for healthcare and its layered organization which defines the components and technologies employed in IoT networks for smooth running of the system. It includes integration of IoT networks with devices and sensors for healthcare applications. This strategy includes data collection, transmission processing and utilization and applications while recognizing the interdependency of many systems. The challenges associated with the integration and adoption of IoT have also been discussed to provide solutions to them. It demonstrates how IoT technology enables early detection by cooperating with healthcare professionals for the real-time operation which enhances the personalized treatment plan and outcomes. Moreover, this can also address the security and privacy issues associated in integrating IoT with healthcare devices and system. To maintain the patient confidentiality and to protect the sensitive healthcare information, it deals with vulnerabilities like data theft, unauthorized access and data violation. It is popularly known for implementing robust security measures with the help of encryption protocol which helps to improve privacy technologies. Overall this chapter can revolutionize healthcare applications and can also recognize various risks and challenges associated with the security and privacy issue of IoT. This technology can improve healthcare deliveries by providing quality life to individuals worldwide.
