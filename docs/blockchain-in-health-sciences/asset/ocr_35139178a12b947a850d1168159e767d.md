In other words, a network of transparent participants known as nodes updates, verifies, and maintains the ledger. Every node in the network stores a clone of the entire blockchain [3].

+   **Security and immutability:** Blockchain uses cryptographic methods to record transactions and essentially eliminates tampering, providing high security. Cryptographic hashes are used to store and connect blocks of information. Any attempt to edit or tamper with a block would entail changing every other block, Blockchain technology is ideal for storing sensitive data and conducting secure transactions due to its inherent security feature that ensures information immutability.

+   **Traceability and transparency:** Blockchain technology’s intrinsic transparency guarantees that all network users have access to the same data. For example, each transaction is recorded in a public ledger that is accessible to all parties. By allowing for the quick identification and correction of any inconsistency, this transparency promotes confidence and network responsibility. The ability of the blockchain to trace the origin and path of assets makes audits easier and reduces the possibility of fraudulent activity [14].

+   **Contracts with smarts:** These are software-encoded automated contracts that carry out the terms of the agreement automatically. The blockchain is used to store smart contract codes, which activate when certain criteria are satisfied. These contracts save money, expire closure times, streamline transactions, and do away with the need for middleware. They are employed in many different industries, such as finance, insurance, and supply chain management.
