
## 7.1 Introduction

The improvement of health through illness, injury, and disease prevention, diagnosis, treatment, amelioration, or cure is known as healthcare. It involves treating additional mental and physical impairments in people. Therefore, the Healthcare Sector provides a collection and integration of economic sectors that offers products and services to provide palliative, preventative, rehabilitative, and curative care for patients. It comprises medical-related business such as medical services, manufacturing of medical equipment, medical insurances and other business functions. In addition to that, it has several sub-categories composed of several patient-care activities such as security, maintenance of data along with more. Apart from these, it also includes legal disputes, supply management, logistics and other medical procedures. Both structured and unstructured sources of information are utilized in the healthcare sector. These sources include omics, electronic health records (EHRs), clinical trials, physiological and chemical sensors, and more [55]. Apart from all these, healthcare also emphasizes patients' perceptions for various AI and Blockchain powered technologies in healthcare such as the implementation of digital anti-aging processes and more [60]. Along with it, healthcare raises issues regarding privacy, security, patient outcomes and transformative changes which were brought upon with the help of AI and Blockchain [58, 59].

Although the healthcare industry is slowly evolving, a few decades ago things were very different. There was transitioning from manual data maintenance to data management with the age of diversity technology including the Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) along with more. The increase in global population has forced us to change how we provide better healthcare service and security. This calls for a greater search for innovative technology that could aid in the global recovery of healthcare-related concerns [1, 7, 51].

The medical field has changed significantly with time. Back in antiquity, the treatment was done using the spiritual practices according to different spiritual beliefs. It used herbal remedies and natural therapies for treatment. Surgical techniques in earlier times included practices like trepanation (drilling holes in the skull), amputations (removal of a body part), wound cauterization (destroying damaged tissue using hot or cold instruments). In the 19th century scientific medicines started developing Discovery of antibiotics, vaccines, etc. have led to major advancements in the healthcare sector. The advancements in surgical techniques additionally had a significant impact on increasing the life expectancy of people [48].

Furthermore, the industry went for technological advancements like Artificial Intelligence (AI), Electronic Health Records (EHRs), wearable devices, telemedicine, and more [1].

The decade of 1950 saw the beginning of development of AI concepts. The early thought was to create machines that could simulate human intelligence. Previously it focused on symbolic approach and rule-based expert systems which were domain specific. In the 20th century the focus of research shifted to machine learning approaches. Numerous new algorithms like decision trees, data mining, voter mining and many more were developed. The concept of big data increased the interest towards AI. More deep learning algorithms with neural networks with many layers achieved great accomplishment in areas like speech recognition, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and picture identification. AI continues to evolve in fields robotics, autonomous systems, unsupervised learning, and more [49].

A discussion on distributed ledger cryptography took place in 1976. Additional progress was made with the publication “How to Time-Stamp a Digital Document,” which presents the notion of timestamping the information inside of the media. Later following that, the idea of “Digital Currency” was presented. The notion of Blockchain, which adheres to protocols similar to e-cash, from this concept. There were additional concepts that emerged that relied on peer-to-peer (P2P) networks, such as hashcash and b-money. Following the release of “Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System” in 2008. Satoshi Nakamoto has been recognized as the creator of blockchain technology. Following a couple of months, an open-source program was made available to bitcoin. In addition, numerous cryptocurrencies were created, including Dogecoin and Litecoin, and more. The transparency of these currencies makes them popular. The Ethereum platform was unveiled in 2015, enabling blockchain technology to function with contracts and loans. In this regard, blockchain gains popularity [50].

Healthcare facilities are not readily available due to geographical location, socioeconomic status, insurance coverage and more. Moreover, it is very expensive in the modern world. Along with these challenges it is to ensure the quality of care. Chronic diseases like cancer, hypertension, diabetes, obesity, chronic renal disease, cardiovascular and cerebrovascular illnesses, and chronic respiratory diseases need to have long term care [8]. Also, there is not enough trained workforce for healthcare. Although Health Information Technology (HIT) has the ability to enhance outcomes for patients, effectiveness, and communication, there are also problems with interoperability, data security, privacy, user interface designs, and many other areas. Additionally, there are problems in healthcare data management (managing EHRs), medical imaging analysis (analysis of X-rays, MRIs, CT scan), healthcare fraud detection (fraud detection for healthcare-statements, invoices, and insurance), supply chain management (drug safety, streamline inventory management along with more), and many more [3].

### 7.1.1 Overview of Blockchain Technology and Artificial Intelligence

AI is among the technological devices that facilitates machine learning and decision-making based on the acquired knowledge. There are various subcategories of AI (Figure 7.1) like Deep Learning (DL), Machine Learning (ML), Neural Networks (NN) along with more which also helps building a digital healthcare industry. Let's look briefly into these subcategories of AI.
