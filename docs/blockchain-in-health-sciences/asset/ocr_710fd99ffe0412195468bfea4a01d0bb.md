The decade of 1950 saw the beginning of development of AI concepts. The early thought was to create machines that could simulate human intelligence. Previously it focused on symbolic approach and rule-based expert systems which were domain specific. In the 20th century the focus of research shifted to machine learning approaches. Numerous new algorithms like decision trees, data mining, voter mining and many more were developed. The concept of big data increased the interest towards AI. More deep learning algorithms with neural networks with many layers achieved great accomplishment in areas like speech recognition, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and picture identification. AI continues to evolve in fields robotics, autonomous systems, unsupervised learning, and more [49].

A discussion on distributed ledger cryptography took place in 1976. Additional progress was made with the publication “Hot to Time-Stamp a Digital Document,” which presents the notion of timestamping the information inside of the media. Later following that, the idea of “Digital Currency” was presented. The notion of Blockchain, which adheres to protocols similar to e-cash, from this concept. There were additional concepts that emerged that relied on peer-to-peer (P2P) networks, such as hashcash and b-money. Following the release of “Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System” in 2008. Satoshi Nakamoto has been recognized as the creator of blockchain technology. Following a couple of months, an open-source program was made available to bitcoin. In addition, numerous cryptocurrencies were created, including Dogecoin and Litecoin, and more. The transparency of these currencies makes them popular. The Ethereum platform was unveiled in 2015, enabling blockchain technology to function with contacts and loans.
