# 20.1 Introduction

DNA sequences are an important part of biological science, which include genomic information that scientists and physicians can utilize using DNA classification to forecast diseases at a very early stage of study. The National Biological Control Institute (NCBI) houses the world's biggest genetic sequence database, but right now the biggest concern is data security. With the growing world population and raised expectations of better healthcare and higher standards of living, global healthcare systems face a myriad of challenges [1]. The existing crisis in healthcare at the moment presents a major socio-economic burden that demands ingenuity in the scientific world and technology [1]. For any tremendous change in public health, this requires healthcare providers have well-built infrastructure for collecting massive amounts of data to organize [2].

The obstacles to effective data exchange are not limited to medical records; they also include important components such as genetic data [3]. The smooth flow of health information has been hampered by worries about information security by data sharing legislation and fears of possible financial consequences linked to data sharing [3].
