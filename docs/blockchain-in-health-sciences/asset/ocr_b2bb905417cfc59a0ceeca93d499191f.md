# 154 BLOCKCHAIN IN HEALTH SCIENCES

The safeguarding and confidentiality of blockchain technology influenced by AI remains capable of being substantially enhanced. Researchers in the future might examine deploying a greater degree of controls for verification and implementing the most recent hash algorithms. There are numerous advantages and disadvantages of using two and multi-step verification systems for each blockchain node, but they are not clearly defined in the context of this particular research topic. It would not be a good idea to utilize the conventional consensus protocols, such as proof-of-work and proof-of-stake, as have been around for the last ten years and have recently been found to have various shortcomings.

The AI-powered blockchain could implement new consensus protocols consisting of Assigned Proof of Stake and Proof-of-Elapsed-Time, which would significantly bolster the security and efficiency of the system [10].

The blockchain that is powered by AI would function more rapidly if it would feature a varied collection of different technologies. In the future, researchers might additionally look into encompassing the blockchain with current infrastructures. For the potential of blockchain systems powered by AI to be used within the healthcare industry on a commercial basis, it will also be crucial to verify preservation along with enhanced security.
