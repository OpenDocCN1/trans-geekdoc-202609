From the related works mentioned above, the authors undertook an endeavor to review studies from the past 5 years (2019-2024) on blockchain technology. They found that some studies focused on systematic reviews of blockchain technology in the healthcare sector, identifying several benefits and drawbacks. The results of some studies indicated that blockchain could play a pivotal role in the healthcare sector. Additionally, some related works reviewed data security concerning learners; in this context, it was noted that research in Western countries often addresses students' data privacy. It was found that blockchain technology still has several disadvantages for data security and requires further research for improved effectiveness.
