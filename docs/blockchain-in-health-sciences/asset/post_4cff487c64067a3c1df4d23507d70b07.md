## 13.2 Blockchain Technology: A New Era of Pharmaceutical Research

Blockchain technology is transforming many aspects of pharmaceutical research by providing many applications like drug discovery, development and delivery. Blockchain protects the reliability and integrity of clinical trial by providing an immutable log of transactions which improves the accountability and transparency of pharmaceutical research. Smart contracts enabled by blockchain technology facilitate safe and transparent cooperation between scientists, organizations, and other stakeholders promoting a culture of data exchange and scientific progress. Furthermore, real-time drug supply chain monitoring is made possible by blockchain-based supply chain solutions which help to prevent the circulation of fake drugs and guarantee the safety and authenticity of the pharmaceutical. Blockchain technology provides solutions to different pharmaceutical issues to improve governance and compliance in the pharma supply chain.

Blockchain reduces administrative burdens and speeds up regulatory compliance procedures by decentralizing data storage and carrying away the middlemen. This accelerates the velocity of innovation in medication discovery and developments. ### 13.2.1 Counterfeit Prevention

Patient safety, public health and integrity of the drugs in the pharmaceutical businesses are extremely affected by counterfeit medications. The pharmaceutical businesses, regulators and other members of the supply chain uses a variety of techniques to stop the fake drugs along with the supply chain. To guarantee the authenticity and integrity of pharmaceutical products from manufacturing to distribution, a significant strategy is used to put strong anti-counterfeit controls and traceability solutions in place. Pharmaceutical objects contain security features and serial numbers so that consumers can verify and tell them apart from fakes. Transparent chain code-based transactions are another way that blockchain systems improve security. For the pharmaceutical sector to function, trust and openness are essential. Without trust and security, the counterfeiting industry grows and puts the general populations at risk of toxic drugs. There is a need to develop some prevention mechanisms for anti-counterfeiting measures and traceability solutions to ensure the authenticity and integrity of pharmaceutical products from manufacturing to distribution. Several approaches, including the anti-counterfeit medicine system (ACMS), can be used to prevent counterfeiting. ACMS uses the interplanetary file system (IPFS) networks and the Ethereum blockchain [10].

### 13.2.2 Product Distribution

Blockchain technology provides possible alternatives for documenting and monitoring movements throughout the distribution process by offering decentralized and unchanging ledger. The existence of several dealers and middlemen creates a chance for dishonesty that reduces the effectiveness of the supply chain. The blockchain has been recognized for its ability to stop the sale of poor-quality medication [11]. Drugs that do not meet the quality standards are seized and their integration into the pharmaceutical supply chain is locked. Pharmaceutical distribution was made easy with the help of a ledger system, chain codes and serialization. In this process a number is assigned to each pharmaceutical item so that they may be identified and distinguished from one another [12]. Blockchain information is strictly controlled to prevent illegal access that can compromise security measures.

### 13.2.3 Tracking and Tracing

Goods in transit should be monitored and traced from point of dispatch to point of destination. In general, delivery delays are bad for business operations but in pharmaceutical and healthcare industry, delivery delays can be life threatening or worsening of illnesses. As the drug tracking and tracing are essential operations for pharmaceutical business operations, patient health for safety regulations, blockchain technology has been applied to manage the pharmaceutical supply chain. Pharmaceutical business and patient management are made possible by timely delivery of the item to their destination and secure tracking and tracing technology. Blockchain technology for tracking and tracing in the pharmaceutical industry, helps stakeholders to enhance the supply chain visibility, improve product authenticity, prevent counterfeit drugs and ensure the patient safety and regulatory compliance.

### 13.2.4 Safety and Security

Drugs are valuable and expensive assets, therefore, keeping them safe requires security. The cryptographic property of blockchain technology provides the foundation for safety and security in the pharmaceutical sector [6]. Cryptography technology improves the tracking and tracing capabilities to meet the regulatory standards [13]. In addition, unauthorized drug alteration has decreased, preventing smart pharmaceutical investors from altering medication, thus compromising quality. Strict safety and security regulations have been implemented by setting alarms when they are violated to control the secure supply chain network.

### 13.2.5 Data Governance

To improve the data governance and supply chain, blockchain and IoT technologies have been used. The drug ledger system has been used to track the traceability and security to maintain organizational and regulatory compliance in vaccine production process.

### 13.2.6 Data Quality

In the pharmaceutical sector, data quality is critical since it directly affects the patient safety, regulatory compliance and overall product effectiveness. Throughout the medication development life cycle, pharmaceutical business depends on various kinds of data sources, including production records, clinical trials, regulatory submission and post market surveillance data. To make intelligent decision, in accordance with legal obligations, it is necessary to guarantee the integrity, correctness and reliability of this data.
