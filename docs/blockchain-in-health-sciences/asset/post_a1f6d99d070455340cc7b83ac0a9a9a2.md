### Abstract

The pharmaceutical business is confronted with a variety of concerns: clinical trial inefficiency, a complex and fragmented supply network, and counterfeit medications. The sections will explore how blockchain technology can revolutionize the innovative solutions to these concerns. Blockchain technology solutions are increasingly becoming one of the most popular applications for supply chain management, pharmaceutical traceability, intellectual property rights, and clinical trial data. It offers a decentralized, encrypted, and transparent platform. Real-time drug monitoring, enhanced by blockchain's dynamic features, tracks every drug from patient distribution to manufacturing, combating counterfeit drugs and increasing patient safety. This is one of the most significant benefits of blockchain in the pharmaceutical sector, as mentioned in the Inventory section.

### 10.1 Introduction

Pharmaceutical firms manage complex supply chains that require numerous suppliers providing essential chemicals. Adhering to new serialization regulations is a growing challenge in the industry, as even a slight mistake in handling may lead to grave consequences [1]. Pharmaceutical companies were vital during the Covid-19 pandemic, providing access to medicine through their supply chain [2]. The global span and shift from intra-functional to inter-organizational operations demand traceability [3]. Counterfeit drugs, compromised quality, untraceable transport, and disassociated customer engagement in the supply chain are exacerbated by digitalization [4]. When lower-quality drugs are found to be present in the supply chain at a later stage, individuals ultimately suffer from financial loss or inferior products [5]. It should also be noted that diminished transparency and communication across the supply chain contribute to these challenges. The solution to these problems will be based on the utilization of blockchain technology and smart contracts based on Ethereum. A decentralized online platform for medicines will also be developed, enabling secure tracking of online shoppers and notifying them of different country requirements and potential fines [6]. In the last ten years, the initiative to establish a harmonized tracking framework for pharmaceuticals has paved the way for counterfeit medicines to be sold. The Pharma Chain, which utilizes Hyperledger Fabric, represents a decentralized solution that provides high transparency and better privacy, along with straightforward integration [7]. More importantly, it secures medicine from theft using approaches like double signing and data hashing, providing encryption [7]. The combination of IoT with blockchain ensures that these systems are highly secure and results in a high level of efficiency for pharmaceutical supply chain management (SCM) solutions. The look-alike system is built using the highly responsive Internet of Things (IoT) design known as Mist, Edge, Fog, Cloud Computing (IMFEC), making it an open and resilient system. Among blockchain technologies, Filecoin stands as one of the most cost-effective options created to handle higher volumes of files in the medical SCM sector [8]. The suggestion is that a new application devised that employs blockchain technology to rectify problems associated with supply chain products. Its implementation is via the Hyperledger Fabric platform, making the resulting composite better in terms of openness, honesty, and provenance.
