In pharmaceutical businesses to improve the governance and compliance of supply chain operation blockchain has been used. It is because of the key features of blockchain immutability, distributed nature, and transparency. The blockchain technology creates smart contracts to automate the operations and to maintain the record of every transaction. Smart contracts execute notifications when the compliance conditions do not meet and create a specific transaction record. The information of this transaction has been sent to all relevant companies after the notification is executed to give an signal about the drug integrity and authenticity. The integration of blockchain with supply chain in pharmaceutical businesses helps the companies to manage their inventory better with supply chain systems. # 13.3 Blockchain for Securing Pharmaceutical Trades: A Layered Approach

A growing and pressing problem on a global scale, particularly in developing nations, is the manufacture and sale of fake pharmaceuticals. The yearly market value of counterfeit pharmaceuticals has surpassed billions of dollars. The pharmaceutical industry’s supply chain flows contribute to medicine counterfeiting. Drugs pass through manufacturers, wholesalers, distributors, and chemists before reaching the consumers. There is an absence of transparency in the system for the pharmaceuticals regulatory body; manufacturers have no idea what happened to their products; recalls are expensive and time-consuming, and corporations are unable to follow up with patients under the current supply chain structure. From the time a drug is created until it reaches the patient’s hands, the pharmaceutical industry will use the suggested system to monitor its journey. For statistical purposes, a database will be updated after a patient’s drug usage to reflect the drug’s effects.
