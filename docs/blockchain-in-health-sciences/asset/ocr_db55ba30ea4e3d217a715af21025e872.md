# 7.1 Introduction

The improvement of health through illness, injury, and disease prevention, diagnosis, treatment, amelioration, or cure is known as healthcare. It involves treating additional mental and physical impairments in people. Therefore, the Healthcare Sector provides a collection and integration of economic sectors that offers products and services to provide palliative, preventative, rehabilitative, and curative care for patients. It comprises medical-related business such as medical services, manufacturing of medical equipment, medical insurances and other business functions. In addition to that, it has several sub-categories composed of several patient-care activities such as security, maintenance of data along with more. Apart from these, it also includes legal disputes, supply management, logistics and other medical procedures. Both structured and unstructured sources of information are utilized in the healthcare sector. These sources include omics, electronic health records (EHRs), clinical trials, physiological and chemical sensors, and more [55]. Apart from all these, healthcare also emphasizes patients' perceptions for various AI and Blockchain powered technologies in healthcare such as the implementation of digital anti-aging processes and more [60]. Along with it, healthcare raises issues regarding privacy, security, patient outcomes and transformative changes which were brought upon with the help of AI and Blockchain [58, 59].

Although the healthcare industry is slowly evolving, a few decades ago things were very different. There was transitioning from manual data maintenance to data management with the age of diversity technology including the Internet of Things (IoT), Blockchain, and Artificial Intelligence (AI) along with more. The increase in global population has forced us to change how we provide better healthcare service and security. This calls for a greater search for innovative technology that could aid in the global recovery of healthcare-related concerns [1, 7, 51].

The medical field has changed significantly with time. Back in antiquity, the treatment was done using the spiritual practices according to different spiritual beliefs. It used herbal remedies and natural therapies for treatment. Surgical techniques in earlier times included practices like trepanation (drilling holes in the skull), amputations (removal of a body part), wound cauterization (destroying damaged tissue using hot or cold instruments). In the 19th century scientific medicines started developing Discovery of antibiotics, vaccines, etc. have led to major advancements in the healthcare sector. The advancements in surgical techniques additionally had a significant impact on increasing the life expectancy of people [48].
