# 7.3.4 Advantages of Using AI and Blockchain Technology in Healthcare

Blockchain technologies along with AI technologies are more effective to each other in several technological aspects, with unique characteristics and functions. Thus, we addressed the benefits of blockchain and AI in the healthcare sector in this section.

# 7.3.4.1 Advantages of Using AI in Healthcare [3]

+   1. Dynamic Compatibility
With the use of numerous large datasets, AI is able to predict the desired result. As a result, it has exceptional and dynamic interoperability with every other platform, including the IoT, blockchain, and cloud computing. Artificial Intelligence (AI) analyzes complicated medical data using advanced algorithms and machine learning techniques, enabling proactive health management strategies, individualized treatment plans, and early disease identification.

+   2. High Efficiency
AI may utilize a wide range of algorithms and training techniques to supply us with a satisfactory outcome. Efficiency is vital within this technology. In accordance with AI’s dynamic compatibility with various technologies, the healthcare industry reaps the advantages of its efficient outcomes.

+   3. High Availability
It is a key feature of AI to be highly mobile in nature, since it is dynamically compatible with several technologies and wearable devices. The increasing availability of AI is not just limited to consumer devices. AI is being embedded in industrial machines, medical equipment, and even in autonomous vehicles.
