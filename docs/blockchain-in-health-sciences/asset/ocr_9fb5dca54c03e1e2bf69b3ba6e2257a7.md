# 2.1 Introduction

The traditional healthcare applications cannot meet the needs of everyone because of its large populations. Despite modern proficiency and advanced technology, everyone cannot afford or access the medical services. IoT is an evolving system which connects computing devices with software and hardware for the exchange, accumulation and communication of data. Information systems have become more important worldwide, but particularly in the healthcare industry. [1] Information technology has always helped the health sector, from cloud-based services to electronic records. Thus, it has become essential to enhance healthcare and its administration as IT advances [2]. It is one of the best solutions that is used in healthcare to assist the medical operations. IoT creates new innovative services and applications by providing interconnection of intelligent devices or objects via the internet [3]. These objects are utilized in the medical profession for early diagnosis and remote health monitoring due to their extensive human detecting capabilities [4]. Sometime ago, physical examination in the hospital was the only way for the diagnosis of disease and illness in the human body. This increased the cost of healthcare support and also put a lot of pressure on the medical facilities. These IoT based applications in the healthcare industry are very costly and can enhance patients' well-being. For example, a number of clinical analyses can be possible at any place without the assistance of any medical specialist like monitoring of blood pressure, glucose level, heart rate etc. The IoT based healthcare services will generate a revolution in the industry.
