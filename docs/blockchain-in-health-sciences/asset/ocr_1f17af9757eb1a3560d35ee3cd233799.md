# Abstract

The integration of medication discovery, traceability and blockchain technology in the field of smart health science has the potential to revolutionize the pharmaceutical industry. The decentralized and transparent nature of blockchain technology makes it a promising tool for revolutionizing drug development procedures. The immutable ledger simplifies data management and stakeholder cooperation by ensuring safe and impenetrable records. The blockchain governance techniques are to highlight the crucial aspect to promote cooperation and trust in the fast developing field of smart health science. With drug research depending on teamwork and data-driven insights, intellectual property protection becomes critical. Blockchain provides a unique way to address IP protection issues by enabling transparent, time-stamped data. This chapter comprehensively explores the diverse landscape of governance structures and intellectual property protection tactics against blockchain technology. In the context of medication discovery and traceability, this chapter also sights the complex interactions among blockchain governance, intellectual property (IP) protection, and creativity highlighting the necessity for a scalable legal framework.
