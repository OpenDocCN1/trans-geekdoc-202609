# 13.2.1 Counterfeit Prevention

Patient safety, public health and integrity of the drugs in the pharmaceutical businesses are extremely affected by counterfeit medications. The pharmaceutical businesses, regulators and other members of the supply chain uses a variety of techniques to stop the fake drugs along with the supply chain. To guarantee the authenticity and integrity of pharmaceutical products from manufacturing to distribution, a significant strategy is used to put strong anti-counterfeit controls and traceability solutions in place. Pharmaceutical objects contain security features and serial numbers so that consumers can verify and tell them apart from fakes. Transparent chain code-based transactions are another way that blockchain systems improve security. For the pharmaceutical sector to function, trust and openness are essential. Without trust and security, the counterfeiting industry grows and puts the general populations at risk of toxic drugs. There is a need to develop some prevention mechanisms for anti-counterfeiting measures and traceability solutions to ensure the authenticity and integrity of pharmaceutical products from manufacturing to distribution. Several approaches, including the anti-counterfeit medicine system (ACMS), can be used to prevent counterfeiting. ACMS uses the interplanetary file system (IPFS) networks and the Ethereum blockchain [10].

# 13.2.2 Product Distribution

Blockchain technology provides possible alternatives for documenting and monitoring movements throughout the distribution process by offering decentralized and unchanging ledger. The existence of several dealers and middlemen createse a chance for dishonesty that reduces the effectiveness of the supply chain. The blockchain has been recognized for its ability to stop the sale of poor-quality medication [11]. Drugs that do not meet the quality standards are seized and their integration into the pharmaceutical supply chain is locked. Pharmaceutical distribution was made easy with the help of a ledger system, chain codes and serialization. In this process a number is assigned to each pharmaceutical item so that they may be identified and distinguished from one another [12]. Blockchain information is strictly controlled to prevent illegal access that can compromise security measures.
