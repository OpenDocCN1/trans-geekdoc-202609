+   - Telemedicine and Remote Monitoring: The COVID-19 pandemic has accelerated the use of IoT in telemedicine and remote patient monitoring, enhancing access and reducing the burden on traditional healthcare systems, ensuring timely consultations and proactive intervention [29].
