A public blockchain differs from a private one in one respect. No information in such a registry is accessible to the general public. The administration node ensures that only members with authorization access blockchain data. Multichain and Hyperledger are two systems that may be used to construct such solutions. A blockchain network functions inside the confines of administrative control or in a limited environment, such as closed network, is known as a private blockchain. Although much smaller in scale, this blockchain type is functionally identical to a public network in terms of decentralization and peer-to-peer communication. # 15.3.2 Blockchain Applications in the Medical Field

There are several applications for blockchain technology in the pharmaceutical industry. Blocks of transactional data are validated using cryptographic techniques, which solve major security problems [17, 18]. Drug tracking has also been enhanced to fight them. Ensuring constant quality from manufacturer to pharmacy is achieved via the use of data miners, blockchain chain codes, and digital signatures for quality control (Figure 15.2) [19]. It is common practice to seek multiple ingredients for medicine before finalizing its formulation. Once the perfect formula has been found, the medicine may be administered. The medicine will pass through many hands as it moves through the supply chain, but the two main ones are the manufacturer and the patient [8].
