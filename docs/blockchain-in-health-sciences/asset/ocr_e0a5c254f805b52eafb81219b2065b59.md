# 18.2 Foundations of IoT Based Healthcare

The essential elements of IoT-based healthcare are: 1) interconnected sensors, 2) communication technologies, and 3) health data analytics. Interconnected medical sensors are a set of physical devices that communicate with one another via wireless network. They capture data from the human body, such as pulse rate, humidity, oxygen level, and other health traits, and send it to the cloud or a centralized server that physicians may also access. They come in wearable form and are easily attached to the patient's body. These devices require low computing power, which are typically wireless, long-lasting, energy-efficient, and affordable. They continually monitor human health without interruption. The sensed data is incorporated into an electronic health record and kept on a server for future decision-making.

All medical sensors perform local data processing to prevent the transmission of unnecessary information to the server. The processed data is subsequently transmitted to the IoT gateway via short-range communication protocols such as Bluetooth, Zigbee, or Near Field Communication (NFC). Subsequently, the IoT gateway connects to the internet and sends the received data to the server for storage and further processing. After accessing the information from the central server, the physician or doctor makes an informed decision. When transmitting data to the cloud or retrieving from the cloud, it is essential to incorporate security measures to ensure the confidentiality of communication and restrict access to authorized individuals only.

Although the medical sensors handle data processing locally, further processing or advanced analytics are still necessary to facilitate the physician's decision-making process. Due to the limited capacity and resource limitations of the devices, it is advisable to utilize AI tools and analytics software in alternative locations such as the cloud or other servers. The analysis process should be conducted with efficiency to enable prompt response or educated decision-making based on the available facts.
