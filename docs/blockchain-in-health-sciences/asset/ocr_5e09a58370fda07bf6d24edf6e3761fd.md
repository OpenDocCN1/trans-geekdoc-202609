# 13.4 Applications of Blockchain in Pharmaceutical Research

# 13.4.1 Accelerate Drug Tracking and Verification

The pharmaceutical industry's supply networks are notoriously complicated, which is causing some of the industry's most pressing problems. The complexity of the situation makes it harder for stakeholders to work together, in turn makes it harder to track medicines and ensure their authenticity. An application of blockchain technology to the pharmaceutical supply chain has been implemented. To keep operations smoothly, ensure patient health, and stay in line with regulations, drug tracking and traceability are crucial. Timely delivery of goods in transit is made possible by sophisticated and secure tracking and technology, which allows pharmaceutical businesses and patient management to run smoothly. To ease the transfer of pharmaceuticals worldwide, a safe international registry was established. Smaller pharmaceutical companies should also reap the benefits of this technology, despite its high potential for larger ones. Ensuring the reliability of data is of utmost importance in this digital age. Blockchain records are transparent and impossible to alter, which increases confidence in research, clinical trials, and regulatory submissions by reducing the likelihood of data tampering.
