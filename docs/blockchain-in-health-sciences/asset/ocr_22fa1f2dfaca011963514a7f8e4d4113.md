# 5.5.3 Tools Used

To understand the perception of UG students on healthcare and data security related to blockchain technology, a perception scale was used (constructed by the researchers). The tool was finalized through a process of reliability and validity. For reliability, the test-retest method was used, and for validation, expert validation (by two experts) was employed.

# 5.5.4 Data Collection

Data were collected with the help of Google Forms, from various districts of engineering and general-degree colleges of West Bengal to know the perception of UG students on healthcare and data security of blockchain technology.

# 5.5.5 Statistical Techniques

The mean, SD, t-test, and ANOVA were used to analyze the collected data and verify the hypotheses.

# 5.6 Results and Discussion

Objective 1: To ascertain the perception of UG students (general-degree and engineering colleges) on the potentiality of Blockchain Technology in healthcare and data security in West Bengal.

Testing of H01(a):The engineering college students of West Bengal will have a more unfavorable perception of the potentiality of blockchain technology in healthcare and data security.

| Category | N | Mean | SD |
|---|---|---|--|
| Engineering college students | 159 | 65.26 | 5.98 |

Table 5.1 shows the evidence of based on the investigators verified H01(a): The cut-off point is M + 1σ. Mean = 65.26, N = 159, and σ = 5.98. This means that 65.26 + 1 × 5.98 = 71.24 is M + 1σ, and M - 1σ = 65.26 - 1 × 5.98 = 59.28. Most engineering college students (126, which is 79.24% of UG engineering students) scored between 59.28 and 71.24. It can therefore be concluded that the perception of engineering college students is moderate regarding the potentiality of blockchain technology in healthcare and data security.
