Blockchain appears as a potent solution in the field of digital health where patient data management coordination and medical privacy are critical to enhancing the quality of treatment [56]. It solves the issue of disjointed healthcare systems by enabling safe data sharing and access for several parties [57]. In the end, blockchain makes it easier for data to be securely exchanged with patient consent which encourages coordination and the use of analytics to population-level medical data [58]. Blockchain is the perfect technology for security since it can keep an immutable, decentralized and transparent record of all patient data [59, 60]. In addition, although blockchain provides transparency, it also protects privacy by masking personal identities with intricate and secure codes, protecting the sensitive nature of medical data [61]. The decentralized nature of this technology also makes it easier for patients, physicians, and other healthcare professionals to share information quickly and securely [62].
