In this study, the authors aim to understand the perceptions of UG students (from engineering and general-degree colleges) regarding blockchain technology in healthcare and data security. A comparative analysis is conducted, and new variables are incorporated into this work. It is expected that these variables and methods will reveal new directions for blockchain technology application. Furthermore, it is anticipated that the results of this study will be beneficial for both educational and social contexts.

# 5.3 Objectives of the Study

+   1. To ascertain the perception of UG students (general-degree and engineering colleges) on the potentiality of Blockchain Technology in healthcare and data security in West Bengal.
a) To ascertain the perception of engineering college students on the potentiality of Blockchain Technology in healthcare and data security in West Bengal.
b) To ascertain the perception of general-degree college students on the potentiality of Blockchain Technology in healthcare and data security in West Bengal.
c) To ascertain the overall perception of UG students (general-degree and engineering colleges) on the potentiality of Blockchain Technology in healthcare and data security in West Bengal.
2. To determine the difference in the perceptions of UG students (general-degree and engineering colleges) on the potentiality of Blockchain Technology in healthcare and data security with reference to their gender, locality, and institution.
3. To determine the difference in the perceptions of general-degree college students on the potentiality of Blockchain Technology in healthcare and data security with reference to their gender and locality.
4. To determine the difference in the perceptions of engineering college students on the potentiality of Blockchain Technology in healthcare and data security with reference to their gender, locality, and semester.
