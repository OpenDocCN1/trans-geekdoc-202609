# FINTECH FOR BANKING

It’s impossible to consider the future of banking, especially in an AI era, without considering the impact of Fintech. The word is a portmanteau of ‘Financial Technology’. Originally focussing on back-end systems, in more recent times there has been increased focus on customer- (or consumer-)facing solutions. Fintech has expanded to include technology solutions for ‘financial literacy, advice and education, as well as streamlining of wealth management, lending and borrowing, retail banking, fundraising, money transfers/payments, investment management and more’. (See Figure 10.27.)

It’s big business, with Fintech start-ups receiving $17.4 billion in funding in 2016 alone according to Forbes.19 Start-ups aspire to become ‘unicorns’, an expression coined in 2013 by venture capitalist Aileen Lee, alluding to the rarity and mythical quality of these beasts. In 2017 these ‘Fintech unicorns’ were valued at over $80 billion. North America produces most of the Fintech start-ups followed closely by Asia. There’s considerable kudos to being viewed as an innovation centre that has fostered a ‘unicorn’. According to analysts CB Insights, the hottest topics for Fintech companies are:
