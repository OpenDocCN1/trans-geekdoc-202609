# Interlock between Blockchain and AI

Experts such as Francesco Corea identify a number of opportunities where Blockchain and AI meet. In his 2017 article ‘The convergence of AI and Blockchain: What’s the deal?’⁶ he points to several possible uses for AI:

+   - Management of Blockchain energy consumption: Blockchain is already recognised as being very energy-hungry, and he suggests that effective use of AI may help in managing that energy requirement.
+   - Helping with the problem of scalability: When (if) Blockchain is fully utilised, the issue of scaling is likely to become problematic and AI may help in some way by ‘pruning’ saved data, and ensuring only critical data is retained. This may avoid tying up unnecessary storage and ultimately may help create new blockchain ‘models’.
+   - Improving security: Although one of the key selling points of Blockchain is security, the effective use of AI may further reinforce that capability.
+   - Improving privacy: Data security and privacy regulations will continue to mature, and increasingly apply themselves to the Blockchain.
+   - Improving efficiency: The ultimate scale of the network will bring issues of network optimisation, and AI may prove to be a tool in improving this, working together with energy consumption reduction.
+   - Talent management: Current thinking regarding the use of Blockchain is that creating a ‘distributed ledger’ is mainly a manual operation, but Corea does not rule out the possibility of automating this using AI in some way.

Conversely Corea also suggests that the use of Blockchain may also help in the adoption of AI by:

+   - Improving trust in data systems
+   - Improving AI effectiveness, because a more secure data sharing model will lead to more data being provided and thus more able to be analysed
+   - Lowering market barriers to entry as AI becomes more accessible to smaller players by being ‘built onto blockchains’
+   - Increasing ‘artificial trust’ in direct machine-to-machine interactions

# BLOCKCHAIN IN BANKING

Amongst other capabilities, Blockchain provides the ability for banks to make cross-border payments and foreign exchange. IBM’s Blockchain World Wire allows banks to optimise and accelerate foreign exchanges, and cross border payments and remittances, using what is known as the ‘Stellar Protocol’.
