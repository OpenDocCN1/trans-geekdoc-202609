He also says that the ‘disruptive’ nature of technology, economic climate and outlook means that there’s no better time than now for banks to focus on product development.

**Sales:** As with the ‘acquisition’ function above, understanding of what we mean by the ‘sales function’ can differ between types of bank. In a retail environment, the sales function may comprise the process of selling products and services such as credit cards and health insurances, which includes cross-selling. The sales function is often commission-driven, which can lead to bad practices emerging. Increasingly sales performance management software is being used to manage direct salesforce and indirect (agency) sellers.

In an investment bank, the expression ‘sales function’ can also relate to the ‘sales and trading function’, which is one of the most important operations of the bank. In investment banking, sales involves the buying and selling of ‘securities’ (such as tradable financial assets) and other financial instruments. The expression ‘market making’ is also used where financial products are bought and sold principally to stimulate investment by clients. Underpinning the sales and trading function is the need to have detailed analytics, which help create successful trading strategies.
