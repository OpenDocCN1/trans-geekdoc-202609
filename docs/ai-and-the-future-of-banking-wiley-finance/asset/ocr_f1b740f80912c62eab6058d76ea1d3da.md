# FINANCING

TABLE 37 Examples of Fintech Companies Focussing on Finance.

| Name | Tagline | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Kiva | ‘Dreams are universal, opportunity is not. Lend as little as $25 to create opportunity for people around the world’ | Nonprofit organisation that allows people to lend money via the Internet to low-income entrepreneurs and students in over 80 countries.
Kiva’s mission is ‘to expand financial access to help underserved communities thrive’.
https://www.kiva.org |
| Prosper | ‘Hello Financial Well-Being’ | Peer-to-peer lending intermediary.
Since 2005 Prosper claims to have facilitated more than $15 bn in loans to more than 950,000 people.
https://www.prosper.com/ |
| Upstart | ‘You are more than your credit score. On Upstart your education and experience help you get the loan you deserve’. | Upstart is an online lending marketplace that provides personal loans using nontraditional variables, such as education and employment, to predict creditworthiness.
/www.upstart.com/ |

# PAYMENTS

TABLE 38 Examples of Fintech Companies Focussing on Payments.

| Name | Tagline | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Ant Financial | ‘Bring the world equal opportunities’ | Ant Financial Services Group, formerly known as Alipay, is an affiliate company of the Chinese Alibaba Group. Ant Financial is the highest valued Fintech or newly named ‘TechFin’ company in the world, and the world’s most valuable unicorn company, with a valuation of US$150 bn.
The group comprises a broad range of capabilities including mobile wallets, credit pay, online banking, wealth management and more.
http://www.antfinancial.com |
| Klarna | ‘Shop now. Pay later’ | A Swedish bank that provides online financial services such as payment solutions for online storefronts, direct payments and post-purchase payments. Their main service is to assume stores’ claims for payments and handle customer payments, thus eliminating the risk for seller and buyer.
https://www.klarna.com/uk/ |
