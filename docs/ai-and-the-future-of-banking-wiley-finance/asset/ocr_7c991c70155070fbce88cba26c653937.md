Banks have transformed themselves from being financial intermediaries to becoming the creators (and sometimes destroyers) of money. Beyond that, through their corporate desire to be innovative, they’ve also become the consumers of money as well, especially from public funds during the various bailouts. But enough has already been written about the problems that led to the collapse of Lehman’s and RBS and it’s time to move on, in every sense.

We cannot understand banking and especially the future of banking without thinking about the concept of money and also its future. There’s no need to go over old ground other than to reflect on the fact that many previous books (perhaps all of them) were written in a predigital age when AI was simply a science fiction notion. Only by understanding what we really mean by both the terms ‘money’ and ‘banking’, even in the current age, can we throw some light on what an analytically and AI-infused future might look like.
