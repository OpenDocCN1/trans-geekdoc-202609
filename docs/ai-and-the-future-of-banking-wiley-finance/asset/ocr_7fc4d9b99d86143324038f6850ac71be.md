# ASSISTANTS/PERSONAL FINANCE

TABLE 26 Examples of Fintech Companies Focussing on Assistance and Personal Finance.

| Name | Tagline | Description of Offering |
|---|---|---|
| Bambu | 'Robo for Everyone' | Bambu is a Singapore-based robo-advisor solution provider offering financial and consumer brands the ability to integrate and benefit from the ongoing digital transformation in wealth management.
https://www.bambu.life/ |
| Revolut | 'A Radically Better Account' | Revolut has disrupted the financial industry by providing customers with 'the value they won't find anywhere else' through the use of a 'personal money cloud'.
It allows customers to exchange currencies at interbank rates, send money through social networks, and spend with multi-currency card accepted anywhere.
www.revolut.com |
| Yomoni | 'Savings management efficient, simple and transparent' | First online private banking-style service in France aiming to make wealth management available to everyone.
The company aims to let all French retail investors benefit from cutting-edge portfolio management services and give them more choices for their long-term savings.
https://www.yomoni.fr/ |
| Digit | 'Save money, without thinking about it' | Analyses customer spending using algorithms influenced by the balance of the checking account, anticipated income, future bills and recent spending to understand how much is being spent. 'Automatically saves the perfect amount every day, so you don't have to think about it'.
https://financegourmet.com/blog/personal-finance/digit-review-scam-legit-necessary/
https://digit.co |
| MoneyLion | 'Borrow, Save, Invest' | Popular finance assistant app, ranking highly on Apple Store and Google Play, that uses artificial intelligence, data analysis and social media to make consumer lending decisions.
Provides additional services such as 'free credit report plus budgeting tips and the ability to invest your money directly via the app'.
https://www.moneylion.com
https://overdraftapps.com/moneylion-reviews/ |
| Personetics | 'Taking Digital Banking Personal' | Described as 'a mass-scale AI-based digital service that offers insights about a client's financials ("NOMI Insights") and a fully automated savings solution ("NOMI Find & Save").
Principally a B2B tool, Personetics helps banks to 'Increase Engagement, Satisfaction, and Response, using a series of "out of the box" scenarios, allowing rapid implementation'.
https://personetics.com/engage/ |
