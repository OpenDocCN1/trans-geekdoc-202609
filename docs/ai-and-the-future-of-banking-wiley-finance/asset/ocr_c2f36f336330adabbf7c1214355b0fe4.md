# CRYPTOCURRENCY

TABLE 35 Examples of Fintech Companies Focussing on CryptoCurrency.

| Name | Tagline | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Coinbase | ‘Coinbase is the easiest place to buy, sell, and manage your cryptocurrency portfolio’ | Digital currency exchange headquartered in California. They broker exchanges of Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash, Ethereum, Ethereum Classic, and Litecoin with flat currencies in approximately 32 countries, and manage bitcoin transactions and storage in 190 countries worldwide. https://www.coinbase.com |
| BCB Group | ‘Prime Digital Assets Partner’ | Cryptocurrency prime broker specifically designed to cater to the needs of institutional clients who need OTC (over-the-counter) best execution for their own accounts and who wish to offer cryptocurrency products and services to their own clients. www.bcbgroup.io |
| BitX | BitXchange ‘Crypto transfers’ | Online exchange to buy cryptocurrencies with a simple bank transfer. The company offers its customers favourable interest rates and good feedback, constantly monitors all trends in the financial sphere and reacts to the slightest changes. https://bitxchange.ae/ |
| Nebeus | ‘Low risk, trusted custodians, interest much better than with banking system’ | Nebeus is a full-service financial services platform enabling their customers to buy, sell, store, lend and borrow cryptocurrency. www.nebeus.com |
| Xapo | ‘Always with you’ | Founded to address two of the biggest issues with Bitcoin’s success and adoption, those of accessibility and security. In less than 4 years Xapo claims to have become the largest custodian of cryptocurrency securing over $10 billion for its members. Currently is expanding beyond Bitcoin to create a robust banking alternative that provides the same level of accessibility to and security in all facets of the customer’s financial life. https://www.xapo.com/en/about |
