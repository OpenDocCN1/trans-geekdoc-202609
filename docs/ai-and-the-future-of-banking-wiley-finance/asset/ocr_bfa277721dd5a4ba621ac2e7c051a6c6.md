It’s a complex area. In 2019 NatWest Bank announced a collaboration with Microsoft and AI specialist DreamQuark to develop what they called ‘Next Generation Complex Analytics (NGCA)’.^22 Working together, they aim to try to simulate the behaviours of different environments and sectors, and in doing so to create some sort of meaningful pattern that helps predict the future. By this, they mean predicting future outcomes in terms of risks and opportunities across industries, geographies and consumer segments. The intention is to do this through a combination of simulations and machine learning.

Accurate prediction will in itself bring greater certainty, but what does this mean in real terms? Isn’t the whole ‘game’ that of managing uncertainty, about spreading bets and about taking a bit of a risk? Can we ever really expect to operate in a risk-free environment, and if so, then what are the consequences? We have to take a step back. In the foreseeable future, it’s impossible to cope without the problem of human intervention, and humans might prove to be the weakest link in the process. The weakest link is when emotion and irrationality happen, which is something that automated systems are unlikely to allow – unless they are specifically programmed in.

# OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY

The need for operational efficiency arises because of the need for processes and procedures to operate in the most cost-effective way whilst still maintaining the quality of the service or product offered. The aim is to optimise the way that tasks are done, reducing or removing entirely those elements of the process that either add unnecessary cost, do not add value or both.
