# 5. The Disruption of Sales and Trading:

In 2014 the Volker Rule, which was passed as part of the Dodd–Frank regulation, banned investment banks from making bets with their own capital, also known as ‘prop trading’. The rationale behind this was that if the bank was making bets with its own capital, it might take higher risks, and in doing so place the whole bank and the customers’ money at risk. As a result, investment banks can now make money from trading only by charging a commission on each executed trade. Trading revenues for investment banks has plummeted. Collectively JP Morgan, Citigroup, Bank of America, Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley suffered a fall of 30% of trading revenue over a decade.

Because of this, the mood again is towards the use of automation, including blockchain. Goldman Sachs’ automated trading platform, Marquee, was launched in 2018 with a view to automate processes, reduce staff, reduce cost and improve profitability.
