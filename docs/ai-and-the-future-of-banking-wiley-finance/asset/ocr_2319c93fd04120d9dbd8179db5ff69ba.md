# Mortgage Tracking

Effective mortgage data collection and performance analytics provides valuable insight to lenders, investors, services and default agencies, providing reports that benchmark performance statistics, generate executive reports and provide regulatory oversight. In doing so, they help banks optimise staffing levels, anticipate and reduce collection expenses, validate decisioning, and calculate probabilities for prepayment risk, credit and default risk, loss severity and home price performance.

Typical mortgage analytic systems allow users to track key mortgage metrics such as:

+   - Mortgage counts
+   - Unpaid balances
+   - Current property values
+   - Corresponding unpaid balances against property values
+   - Helping understand what factors impact on the positive or negative effects on the mortgage industry, such as location or distribution channel
