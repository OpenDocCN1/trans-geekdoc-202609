According to Bank of America (BoA), Millennials are profiled into three subsets:⁹

+   1. Younger Millennials
+   2. Middle Millennials
+   3. Older Millennials

According to the BoA report, overall Millennials are reluctant to use banks:

+   - One in three think they won’t need a bank in five years.
+   - One in five think that banks will not be the primary financial institution of choice.

Despite the desire of banks to use the most current methods of communication, the Bank of America report also suggests that Millennials view the use of social media by banks as being ‘silly’ or ‘creepy’.

On the issue of banking apps covered in an earlier chapter, the BoA report says:

+   - 59% of Millennials have used their bank’s mobile app.
+   - 72% use the app a few times per week.
+   - 24% access the app on a daily basis.
+   - 74% receive mobile banking alerts.
