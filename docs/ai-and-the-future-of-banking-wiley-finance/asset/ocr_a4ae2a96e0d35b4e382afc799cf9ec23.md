Increasingly the expression ‘challenger banks’ is coming to be used for new banks, usually retail, which seek to threaten the larger more established national banks by either:

+   - Giving better interest rates\n- Offering new banking ‘propositions’\n- Providing enhanced access either to their physical site (through longer opening hours) or online\n- Any combination of all of these
