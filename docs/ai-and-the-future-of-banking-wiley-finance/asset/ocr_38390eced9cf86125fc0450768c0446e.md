# Fraud Detection

This section will specifically consider fraud and financial crime (and we will specifically deal briefly with fraudulent investment trading later in the chapter). Fraud is a major operational risk for banks. Typical fraud might comprise:

+   - Identity fraud
+   - Anti-money laundering (AML)
+   - ATM fraud

Key issues for bankers include:

+   - The need to manage fraud detection at scale
+   - The ability to investigate using limited resources
+   - The need to maintain effective customer service, and not to treat all unusual transactions as being potentially fraudulent
+   - The need to manage internal and external compliance issues (such as trade monitoring)
