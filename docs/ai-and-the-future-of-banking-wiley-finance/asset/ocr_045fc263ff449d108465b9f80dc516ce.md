# Branding and Human-Centred Design

Brand is often associated with the quality of customer experience, and may be either a positive or a negative association. As ‘customer experience’ seems to be one of the buzzword expressions, it might be helpful to see what at least one banking group was asking of its experts in that field.

A recently (and almost certainly now-filled) job vacancy advertised for a position in what they described as a ‘group roadmap and business design’ team. The task required the individual to:

+   - Challenge and shape existing thinking and align portfolios across the group.
+   - Identify how developing technology could change the way the company interacts with customers and colleagues and influence future business models and architecture.
+   - Define what the overall strategy means in practice and develop frameworks to allow Value Streams, Enterprise Enablers and Major Core Programmes to achieve strategic business and architectural objectives.
+   - Support the mobilisation of key elements of the portfolio.
+   - Coordinate across Business Design, IT Architecture and Human-Centred Design (HCD).

‘Human-centred design’ is an approach to problem solving that develops solutions to problems by taking the human perspective in all parts of the decision-making process. Its international standard, ISO 9241-210:2010(E), states:

> Human-centred design is an approach to interactive systems development that aims to make systems usable and useful by focusing on the users, their needs and requirements, and by applying human factors/ergonomics, usability knowledge, and techniques. This approach enhances effectiveness and efficiency, improves human well-being, user satisfaction, accessibility and sustainability; and counteracts possible adverse effects of use on human health, safety and performance.

Applicants were also required to be:

+   - Comfortable working in an ambiguous and rapidly changing environment, managing a diverse set of priorities
+   - Highly aware of the overall strategic landscape with customer focus, diplomacy and strong stakeholder management ability
+   - Experienced in shaping and managing complex portfolios, with a proven record
