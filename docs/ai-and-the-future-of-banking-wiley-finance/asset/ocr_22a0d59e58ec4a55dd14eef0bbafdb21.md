Effective compensation management requires:

An interesting issue arises. Traditionally the salesperson has used technology to identify the most promising ‘leads’ and to follow them up. The ability of technology to identify those leads with greater certainty now places a different onus on the ‘human’ salesperson. If the analytics seems to demonstrate that the prospect of closing a sale is so strong from a probability point of view, then the onus shifts to the salesperson to justify why a sales contact hasn’t been concluded.

Taken to its ultimate, analytics and AI have the potential to match both the aspiration of the buyer with the service/product provided by the seller, and to automatically bring them together. A sort of commercial dating agency perhaps? What then really is the human contribution to the sales role? Of course, it will always be argued that ultimately ‘selling’ is a people-to-people thing, but maybe we should be less complacent.

# Financial Markets Risk and Trade Monitoring

# Hedge Fund Management uses AI to Define Investment Strategy

UK-based hedge fund manager Man Group uses AI algorithms to help define their investment strategies. According to a September 2017 Bloomberg report, Man (and possibly the industry as a whole) are using AI to ‘find the speediest way to execute trades, make bets on market momentum and to scan press releases and financial information for keywords that might signal whether a stock will rise or fall’.30

The use of advanced and proprietary capabilities is well established in this area, but human intervention remains an important element (at least for the moment) to ensure that there is effective oversight.

Analytics coupled with high-quality visualisations provide real-time insight to ensure that:

+   - Decisions are being made that align to the risk strategy and appetite of the bank.
+   - Concerns regarding counterparty risk are rapidly and effectively dealt with.
