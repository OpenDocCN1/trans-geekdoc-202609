business case for change as the ethical argument. If the use of say, palm vein recognition proves to be a robust and cost-effective technology for the banking industry and acts as the ‘tip of the sword’, then it is only a matter of time before this same technology is used in a domestic situation to open the front doors of our homes, or unlock our (battery-powered) cars.

This book is not meant to provide an ethical compass with respect to change. Rather, its intention is to reflect how new technologies, including AI, are creating a roadmap for the future and to help inform readers to a point where they can take part in this very important debate. They are all stakeholders and are not only entitled to an opinion but positively encouraged to share it.
