+   2. Continued growth and profitability, especially as lack of growth is rarely acceptable, leading to increased focus on the customer
+   3. Effective risk and compliance management, which includes integrating risk management into everyday decisions

It’s probably fair to say that, regardless of almost any industry, these are the three main key areas that need to be focused on. Some might even argue that in an age of digitalisation, these imperatives remain ‘constants’ in the overall decision matrix, and are to a large degree generic. Overall, perhaps it’s simply a matter of how these imperatives are ‘translated’ to the specific needs of the sector.
