# NatWest Becomes First UK Bank to Launch Data Academy

NatWest is to establish a Data Academy to train staff in in how best to understand and harness data. Open to all bank employees, it will offer a wide range of training, courses and qualifications that will cover all levels of proficiency.

The move is central to both the bank’s digital transformation and to realising the full potential of a wide range of cutting-edge technologies, including artificial intelligence and machine learning. The academy will give staff the tools and training to serve customers better by offering more tailored, personalised products and services that best fit people’s financial needs.¹
