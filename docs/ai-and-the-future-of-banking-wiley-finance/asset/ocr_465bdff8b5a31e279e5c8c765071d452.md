For these exceptional groups, ordinary banking just isn’t enough. But the challenge for wealth managers is how to provide advice and information that these clients require and in doing so, how the bank creates special and sustainable relationships.

For over a decade, specialist banks, wealth managers and investment managers have been using analytics to provide better reporting and analysis so as to advise their clients of the changing value of their portfolio, using capabilities such as analysis of fund and asset management performance, across asset classes and geographies, and creating highly visual reporting dashboards with drill-down capabilities.

These portfolio management capabilities are also becoming increasingly accessible to the less wealthy – for example, those who have chosen to take greater control over their personal pension arrangements.
