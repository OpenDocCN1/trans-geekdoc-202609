As shown in the figure, the minimum of active and reactive distribution line losses are achieved in the case of light loads consumption (60% of loads). On the other side, the highest power losses are obtained in the case of high load consumption. Whether before or after DG unit installation, it is seen that the power losses are proportional with the increase in loads.
