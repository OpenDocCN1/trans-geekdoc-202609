The accepted upper and lower voltage limits for each test system are plotted with dashed red lines in Fig. 6.9. Before DG-installation, some of the bus voltages broke the permissible limits with specific loads. For example, in the IEEE 33-bus system, the buses from bus number 6 to bus 18 and from bus 26 to bus 33 had the minimum values of the voltages—these values were less than 0.95 p.u.—but after installing the PV-DGs and taking into account the changes in consumption, it is observed that the voltage profiles have significantly improved, and the minimum value has become 0.95 p.u. in bus number 18. Similarly, in WT-DGs, the voltage profiles have improved but better than PV-DGs. The same observation can be made for IEEE 69-bus and IEEE 118-bus systems—the best voltage profiles for different consumption loads are obtained based on PV-DGs and WT-DGs. Also, it can be noted that the voltages profiles for the system in light loads consumption is always better than medium and peak loads.
