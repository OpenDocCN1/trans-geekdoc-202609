# ## 5.5.2 How Bitcoin rewards miners?

Bitcoin’s monetary policy of fixing the total number of BTCs in circulation at 21,000,000 is simple — some view it as a great advantage, while others think of it as superficial. Every time a miner finds a successful block, she receives a reward via the Coinbase transaction. This reward plus all the block’s transaction fees are the ultimate reason why miners mine. The size of block rewards started with 50 BTC per block, is halved after 210,000 new blocks are mined (it happens about every four years). The genesis block was mined on January 3, 2009. The first halving occurred on November 28, 2012; the second occurred on July 9, 2016, the most recent halving occurred on May 11, 2020; see Figure 5.19. The current reward is 6.25 BTC. The total number of BTCs in circulation is limited to 21,000,000.
