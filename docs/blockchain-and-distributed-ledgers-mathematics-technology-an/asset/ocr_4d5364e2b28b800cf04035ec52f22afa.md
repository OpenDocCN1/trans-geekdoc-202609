Small hash power implies infrequent rewards with huge variability. Thus, simple economics forces miners to coalesce into gigantic pools, which goes against the grain of the distributed ethos underpinning Bitcoin’s generosity. Mining is sustainable in the long term, if only the reward exceeds the mining cost. Mining reward consists of the block reward and transaction fees. Mining costs include hardware costs and operational costs, such as electricity, labor, legal, security, and incidental expenses. What will happen when rewards are anybody's guess. In all likelihood, the Bitcoin protocol will be severely impacted. For further details see Beucci and Jaag (2017); Bissias et al. (2018); Prat and Walter (2018); Qin et al. (2018); Romiti et al. (2019).
