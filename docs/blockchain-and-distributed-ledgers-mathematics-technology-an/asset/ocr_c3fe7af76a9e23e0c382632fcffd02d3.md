It is fair to say that presently DLT is bigger than its narrow applications to cryptocurrencies.

While DLT holds a great promise, it is hard to master it properly. The reason is that DLT lies at an intersection of three somewhat disjoint fields: (a) cryptography (to ensure the integrity of transactions); (b) game theory (to establish consensus on the state of the ledger); and (c) economics (to design proper economic initiatives), which require solid understanding of all three (see Figure 1.4). Typically, it is easy to understand any two of the three, mastering all of them is a tall order. We hope that this book will help diligent readers to climb the ladder of knowledge and experience.
