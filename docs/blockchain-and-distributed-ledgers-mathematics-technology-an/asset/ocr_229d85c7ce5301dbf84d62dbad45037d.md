# 12.5.2 Trade finance

Trade finance is the lubricant on the supply wheels — in fact, international trade would be impossible without it. Trade finance is a hybrid activity involving both supply chain and financial services. International trade has experienced explosive growth over several decades due to intermediaries that have positioned themselves for finance actions between the buyers (importers) and the sellers (exporters). These intermediaries offer a wide range of options, such as a bank of credit (LC), bank guarantee, lending, forgoing, and export credit finance. Thus, trade finance requires at least three parties — a buyer, a seller, a financier — and possibly severe. Pain points of trade finance include lack of transparency, copious bureaucracy, fraud, errors, among others. As such, DLT is well-suited to reduce payment risk and facilitate international trade.
