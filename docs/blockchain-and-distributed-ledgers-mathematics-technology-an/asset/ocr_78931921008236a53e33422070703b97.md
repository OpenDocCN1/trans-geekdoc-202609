Thankfully, not all is indeed, Eq. (10.11) gives an explicit expression for the characteristic function of the generalized hyperbolic distribution. One of the most fundamental theorems of probability theory states that the characteristic function of the sum of independent variables is the product of the characteristic functions of the summands:

$\Phi_{Y_N}(\chi) = (\Phi_X(\chi))^N$. (10.25)
