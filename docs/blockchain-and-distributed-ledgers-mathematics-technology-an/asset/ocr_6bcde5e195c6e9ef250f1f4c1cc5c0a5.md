Surprisingly, the truth is somewhere in-between:

+   (5) BTC has no intrinsic value. Accordingly, it is better to think of it as chips in a giant casino. There is a fixed number of BTCs and many potential players, which is enough to maintain their value in the long run. In addition to financial gains or losses, the players also receive entertainment from trading and using their BTCs, a source of extra value. This should be compared and contrasted with conventional money, which, according to Aristotle, are derived from the law, and, hence, coercion rather than fun; see Section 12.2.4. There are some assets that be store-of-value for others than their direct usefulness; see Chapter 10. Gold is a perfect example: it has survived since times immemorial despite its debatable value and better alternatives for most industrial applications. Fiat money has survived for 300 years despite being a depreciating asset; see Chapter 2.

Although Ethereum goes further in many ways, Bitcoin is unequal in other ways, including its scarcity, adoption rate, robustness, and trust.
