Regulations being antithetical to the even principle of decentralized finance, the attractiveness of ICOs as they were implemented in 2016 and 2017 massively decreased. In January 2018, the total market capitalization of cryptocurrencies and tokens reached its all-time high of US831 billion before collapsing by more than 85%.

# 3.7.7 Decentralized finance and autonomous organizations

In parallel to the growth of cryptocurrency and tokens, the end of the decay was marked by the increasing importance of a broader ambition to decentralize finance and commerce as a whole. Rather than relying on centrally-regulated, "real-world" companies, a new notion of decentralized autonomous organization, DAO, gained traction. DAOs are the decentralized equivalent to companies or organizations; they rely on smart contracts and are tokenized on a distributed ledger such that "shareholders" are anonymous token holders. Rather than relying on articles of association, shareholders' agreements, the local law, or other complex legal documentation, the governance of DAOs, as well as its profits, can be arbitrarily defined within the contract with no relation to the laws and habits of regulated companies. Because of transparency and immutability, DAOs are inherently robust to many forms of risks (e.g., legal, jurisdiction) and by removing intermediaries as commercial registers or banks, they provide a highly efficient vehicle for international, self-enforced, anonymous organizations.

DeFi, for decentralized finance, has been the hottest in 2020 with a complete financial system being replicated by little in a fully decentralized way such that it does not depend on central banks, commercial banks, payment processors, governments, or regulators. From stablecoins providing a stable token indexed to some currency or basket of national currencies (see Chapter 8), to peer-to-peer exchanges, decentralized products, insurances, prediction markets and gambling contracts, decentralized orders, lending tools, DeFi is growing massively and over the perspective of a fully disintermediated, alternative financial system where established financial institutions lose their dominant position. Whether DeFi is yet another bubble ready to pop, whether it is the future of finance or a short-term experiment, one thing is sure: blockchain technology is far from reaching its full potential and we are prone to discover exciting new cases that today are still invisible to both skeptics and experts.
