# 5.3.2 Bitcoin transactions and mathematical induction

The concept behind Bitcoin protocol is pure mathematical. In particular, transactions are organized in chains, traced back to the so-called Coinbase transactions, or even further to the genesis block.

Recall that, to prove a statement by induction, we need to perform two steps. First, we need to verify the statement for n = 1. This verification is the genesis second. Second, assuming that the statement holds for any given n, we must show that it also holds for n + 1. This demonstration is the induction step. Together, these two steps prove the statement for every natural number n.
