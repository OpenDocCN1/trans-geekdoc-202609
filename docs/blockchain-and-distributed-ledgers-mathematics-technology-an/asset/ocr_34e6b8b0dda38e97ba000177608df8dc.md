# 3.8 Summary

This chapter reviews the philosophical roots of Bitcoin and its growth alongside the rest of the crypto economy. We introduce the fundamental building blocks of cryptocurrencies, such as the blockchain and the proof-of-work consensus, and explain how they solve the main challenge of decentralized currencies: double spending. We argue about the value of cryptocurrencies and introduce the latest trends emerging from decentralized finance.

For additional inputs, see Wüst and Gervais (2018).
