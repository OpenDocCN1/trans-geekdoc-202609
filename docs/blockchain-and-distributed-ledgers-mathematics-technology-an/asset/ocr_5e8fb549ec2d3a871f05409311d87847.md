The fitted SIS model with $m = 0.6, eta = 10.0, \sigma = 1.0, x(0) = 0.1$, provides a reasonable explanation of the stochastic behavior of the proportion of altcoins in the total capitalization of all cryptos; see Figure 10.34.

**Figure 10.34** (a) The altcoins' fraction of the total capitalization of cryptoassets; (b) The stationary probability density function for the altcoins fraction of the total. Here $\beta =10.0, \sigma =1.0, m =0.6, x_0 = 0.1$. Own graphs.

This figure shows that for sufficiently large volatility $\sigma$, the Bitcoin dominance index will hover around its initially chosen value $m = 0.6$. However, one can show that it will go to zero for smaller volatility values, similarly to the SIR model. Thus, the future of the entire cryptocurrency ecosystem depends on how volatile it is going to be.
