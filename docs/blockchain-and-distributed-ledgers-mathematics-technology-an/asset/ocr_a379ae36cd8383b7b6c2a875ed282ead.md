This is an excellent introduction to DLT which is not credible and insightful for advanced students trained in quantitative fields — but also very accessible for the reader with limited knowledge of cryptography. As the book explains, there are at least two big challenges for anyone who seeks to understand the key principles of DLT: the field is dominated by zealots and the subject requires a good knowledge of cryptography, game theory and economics — three separate skills rarely taught in combination.

However the book aims for a "balanced and well-reasoned discourse" — which delivers in style — and succinctly and effectively covers all three of the key elements of knowledge that need to be grasped to understand how DLT works.

It is thus a tour de force! It should be required reading for regulators, finance practitioners, investors and economists, particularly given that DLT will almost certainly reshape the future financial system.
