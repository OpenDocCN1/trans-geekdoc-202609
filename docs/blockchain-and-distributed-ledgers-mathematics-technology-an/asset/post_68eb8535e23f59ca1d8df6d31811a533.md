### 2.3.5 Banking regulations and monetary stability

Negative interest rates are a disturbing sign. While not exactly unheard, negative rates are prevalent. Countries whose central banks have adopted a negative-interest rate policy (NIRP) do not seem to reap any benefits. They do not experience currency depreciation, higher inflation, a robust increase in lending, and overall stimulus. More often than not, such a policy counterintuitive effects, such as a failure of the transmission mechanism to lower rates in Switzerland. In addition to ZLB and PLB, many authors have sensed the existence of the economic bound (ELB).

### 2.3.6 The pros and cons of the current system

The biggest issue affecting the existing banking system is that it is overly complicated due to commingling three activities: (a) creation and annihilation of credit through lending; (b) record keeping; (c) execution of transactions. Separating these activities is vital for making banking more agile and efficient. We shall concentrate on transactional aspects of banking and argue that judicious use of DLT can help bring banking into the 21st century.
