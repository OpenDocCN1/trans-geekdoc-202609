## About the Authors

Alexander Lipton is Co-Founder and Chief Information Officer of Scala, Partner and Dean's Fellow at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. Alex is a board member of Scala and an advisory board of several fintech companies worldwide. 2006–2016, Alex was a senior manager at Credit Suisse, Deutsche Bank. At the same time, Alex held professorships at EPFL, NYU, Imperial College, and the University of Illinois. Before becoming a banker, Alex was a Full Professor of Mathematics at the University of Illinois and Los Alamos National Laboratory. In 2000 Alex was the Inaugural Quant of the Year by Risk Magazine. In 2021 the Buy-side Quant of the Year by Risk Finance. Alex authored/edited more than a hundred scientific papers. He is a frequent speaker at Quantitative conferences and forums, and published in peer-reviewed journals including Management Science, Stochastics, Journal of Theoretical and Applied Finance.

Adrien Treccani is a software engineer specialized in high performance computing and financial engineering. He has been an active member of fintech community since 2012 and advised numerous banks on ledger technology, cryptocurrencies and decentralized finance. Adrien lectures at Ecole Fédérale de Lausanne and holds a Bachelor's degree in computer science and a Master's degree in financial engineering at Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne. He obtained a Ph.D. in mathematical finance at University of Zürich and worked in the hedge fund industry before joining Scala in 2015.
