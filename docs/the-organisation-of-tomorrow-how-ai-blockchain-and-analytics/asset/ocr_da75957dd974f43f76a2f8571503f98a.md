**Social agency** How humans define and use technology and how people apply (new) technologies to achieve their goals.

**Sociomateriality** The theoretical concept that helps researchers understand how technologies and organisations interact.

**Timestamp** A sequence of characters or encoded information identifying when a certain event occurred, usually giving date and time of day, sometimes accurate to a small fraction of a second.

**Trust protocol** A mechanism whereby trust is managed by technology in a decentralised network. Trust is established through verification or proof of work and is supported by immutability of that work and the consensus of all participants.
