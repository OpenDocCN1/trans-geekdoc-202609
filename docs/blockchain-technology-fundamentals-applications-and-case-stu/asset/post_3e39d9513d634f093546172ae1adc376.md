## 2.1 INTRODUCTION

Blockchain can be defined as a chain of transactions. The records in blockchain are stored in blocks. Each block has a hash of the previous block, which creates a secure link. This technology is used to ensure data integrity and transparency without the need for a central authority.

Blockchain technology is used in various sectors, including finance, supply chain, and healthcare. It offers potential to revolutionize how businesses is and and businesses interact with each other.
