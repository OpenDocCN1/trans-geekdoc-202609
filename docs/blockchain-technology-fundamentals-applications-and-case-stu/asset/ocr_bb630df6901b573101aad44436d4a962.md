Though the roots of this technology pertain to bitcoin, the influence of blockchain technology in the web world is still expanding. The proposed DIGBI aims to handle the issue of information silo and nonproduction using blockchain technology. The utilization of blockchain technology helps in identification of valid information for communication and ensures that nonproduction is guaranteed. The decentralized consensus mechanism in blockchain technology identifies the appropriate information for communication and ensures that no detail information sharing is done.

As of the blockchain, the information is distributed over various nodes (computers) that may be in the same or different network and enabling efficient distribution of information. Despite the distribution, the digital identification provides the most efficient means of nonproduction.
