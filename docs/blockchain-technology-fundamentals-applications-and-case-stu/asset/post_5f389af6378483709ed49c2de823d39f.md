## 5.5.1 ELECTRONIC MEDICAL RECORDS

Tanwar et al. [9] suggested a novel approach to embed blockchain network for EHR sharing. Such systems are defined with four stakeholders: patient, clinician, technician, and system administrator. The proposed system used smart contracts, and the distribution of EHR data to other stakeholders are achieved by shared symmetry and private key in block transaction. Various algorithms are proposed for control management of four different participants. The distribution of EHR data is done using hyperledger fabric and hyperledger composer. The survey in Ref. ## 5.5.2 IoT Sensors or IoT-Based Healthcare Devices

Today, various smart devices are available in the form of human wearable gadgets or smart watches in healthcare industry. These devices are designed especially for remote monitoring and to monitor various health parameters of the patient. In healthcare, IoT has been identified as subsector of Internet of Healthcare Things (IoHT) [11]. Various sensors used to retrieve medical data in IoT healthcare field are listed in Figure 5.4.

Embedding of Bit into IoHT helps to maintain the privacy of medical records. Intelligent sensor nodes [12] can accurately monitor patients' primary health parameters such as temperature, respiratory rate, heart rate, blood pressure, and so on. IoT-enabled devices can be easily deployed to obtain a patient's detailed information. This smart device helps to track the behavior change of patients and gives alert notification at the time of emergency to healthcare providers.

Badr al. [13] proposes a new protocol known as Pseudonym-Based Encryption with Different Authorities (PBE-DA) to guarantee the validity of IoT–EHR system using blockchain-based multitier framework.

## 5.6 USE CASES AND ITS SOLUTIONS IN HEALTHCARE USING BLOCKCHAIN

The revolution of blockchain in the healthcare domain has transformed the world of technology into a newly distributed and secure infrastructure. Various characteristics and features of blockchain [15–18] encourage domain experts in solving many problems, where a middleman is replaced with this recent BT. With the existing healthcare infrastructure, many researchers and health practitioners today are struggling with communication delay, reluctance in sharing medical information, and trust transactions. Moreover, incompatibility of vendor-specific healthcare system creates a big gap in the healthcare communication world. Hence, it is very difficult to define secured and trusted communication among stakeholders and to provide efficient patient-centric care.

The benefit of using case scenario in healthcare is to ensure data integrity and transparency. The use of blockchain allows for a decentralized ledger where medical records are immutable, preventing unauthorized access or data tampering. This ensures that patient data is accessible to authorized providers whenever needed while maintaining high privacy standards.

### 5.6.1 Use Case for Healthcare Providers and Insurance Companies

Health insurance is a type of insurance that covers the expenses of medical treatment. In the insurance industry, blockchain can automate the claims process using smart contracts, reducing administrative costs and speeding up payouts. By providing a shared ledger, insurance companies can verify medical records more accurately, reducing fraud and ensuring a more efficient system for both providers and patients.

### 5.6.2 Use Case for Opioid Prescription Tracking

Opioid is a type of medication used for pain relief at the time of anesthesia, post-surgery recovery, physical disorders, etc. However, the misuse of opioids is a significant public health concern. A blockchain-based tracking system can monitor the lifecycle of opioid prescriptions from prescription to dispensation. This helps in preventing fraudulent prescriptions and ensures that medications are used by legitimate patients, thereby reducing the risk of addiction and misuse.
