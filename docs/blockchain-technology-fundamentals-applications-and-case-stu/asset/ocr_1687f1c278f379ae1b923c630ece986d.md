**B. Medical databases:** In the current scenario, none of the hospitals is having patients previous medical history. If a patient wants to visit multiple medical institutions for consultation, or if is referred from one hospital to another, there is lag in communication to each health provider has its own software because of which the next physician cannot not have a log of the patient's clinical history. The disconnected traditional databases are not enough for predicting upcoming lifestyle and life threat diseases. One solution is that IDHR facilitates to integrate the disconnected medical databases, brings the birth to death clinical summary and all medical data for faster treatment (Table 7.3).
