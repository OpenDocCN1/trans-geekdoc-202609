## 4.12.7 DATA MODELS

Data models in blockchain technology are the current state of the ledger. The retrieved data are hashed and stored in the form of blocks, which are decentralized and cryptographically secured.

## 4.12.8 PROCESSES

This layer deals with the consensus protocols, which provide the proof of work (PoW) and verify the action in the networks.

## 4.12.9 SERVICES

The contracts and transactions between peers are dealt in this layer. Contract is used to verify and validate the participants of the network.
