Previously, if a customer wanted to create an account in the bank, they had to fill a lot of personal identity documents for verifying the customer. They wanted to fill a lot of handwritten papers generally in the bank. They had to fill all the details for creating an account in a particular bank. The same customer had to do the same process for creating an account in another bank, which was a time-consuming process and the details were stored in the different bank databases. If the customer wanted to create an account in 10 banks, the same process was carried out in all banks with some other additional information. It was required to maintain the customer information in all the banks; the banks did not get any revenue for it; instead, a huge amount was wasted at the cost of the end-user.
