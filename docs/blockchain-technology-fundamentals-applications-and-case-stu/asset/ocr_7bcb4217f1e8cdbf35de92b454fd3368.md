# 7.5 CONCLUSION

The proposed blockchain based Health Information Model, which provides decentralization, an immutable audit trail, provenance, robustness/availability, and security/privacyThe proposed model has the importance of blockchain-based healthcare system, which integrates patients with personal data, electronic health data, physicians, insurance companies, drug industry to improve health record management, enhanced insurance claim process, and accelerated clinical/medical research. The smart healthcare system adopts an integrated healthcare model for processing the health data of individuals through a blockchain network, ensuring the data ownership of individuals as well as transparency, integrity, confidentiality, speed, scalability, threat, and attack. However, these challenges can be addressed through careful application, design, and implementation; therefore, healthcare applications of blockchain continue to increase. Blockchain-distributed technology can advance the biomedical and healthcare domains in novel ways, and we expect many new applications to emerge.

This system also helps to achieve ONE INDIA–ONE HEALTH CARD.
