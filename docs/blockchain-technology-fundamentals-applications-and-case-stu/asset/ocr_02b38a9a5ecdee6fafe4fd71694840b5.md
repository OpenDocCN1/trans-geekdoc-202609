# 4.7 BENEFITS OF IIoT

One of the top published IIoT affords businesses is predictive maintenance. This allows organizations using real-time data generated from IIoT systems to predict defects in machinery, before they occur, enabling companies to take action to address those issues before a part fails or a machine goes down.
Another common benefit is improved field service. IIoT technologies help field service technicians identify potential issues in customer equipment before they become major issues, enabling techs to fix the problems before their inconvenience customers.
