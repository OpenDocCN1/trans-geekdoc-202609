One of the major problems of bitcoin is the scalability problem. The bitcoin network with 1 MB block size, with the transaction capacity of 300 bytes, the estimated throughput of bitcoin is 8 GB per minute. This shows that only the node with the highest computational power can participate in the blockchain, and this is against the power of blockchain and bitcoin. Several solutions are tried, which indeed result in different types of forks.

One of the solutions for the scalability problem of bitcoin is the Segregated Witness (SegWit), which deals with transaction flexibility. The problem is due to the reality as the transaction never covers all the data that give way to the hacker to modify the transaction and its hash [15]. The block size of bitcoin is increased along with the new layer that is created on top of the network, which segregates the transaction data from the signature layer that facilitate the lighting network. The lightning layer was activated in August 2017 [17]. In this lighting network, a new facility of microchannel is created to make micropayments in the network. This payment channel acts as an agreement between the sender and receiver to announce a delay in the transaction and make the actual transaction. Both the parties delay the transaction details on the network but assure their balance on the bitcoin blockchain. The victorious examination of the lighting network of the bitcoin was conducted in January 2018 [18].

# Advantages of Bitcoin [19]

+   Payment freedom
The participant can send and receive money at anytime anywhere. There are no restrictions as bank holidays in the traditional way.

+   Control and security
The transaction in the bitcoin does not involve any personal data and bitcoin defend from identity theft.

+   Information is transparent
The transaction can be traced to verify the legitimate transaction and the distributed ledger that is available; public contains all the transaction information, which makes it hard for any hacker to get involved in the transaction.

+   Very low fees
No extra fees of the merchant can be added without the prior confirmation of the participant.

# Disadvantages of Bitcoin [19]

+   Lack of awareness and understanding
People are not still aware of the digital currency; and so it is not popular as the physical currency. Some kind of awareness has to be created through advertising, awareness camp, and lectures.
