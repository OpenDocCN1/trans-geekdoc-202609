# 10.1 INTRODUCTION

Data is the heart and processed data is the heartbeat in the working of a software system. From data to Big Data, the growth has witnessed a swift rise. With the evolution of Internet, the generation of data and their transformation to information have been rapid. Handling and managing surplus information have however caused a challenge of identifying and utilizing the right information at the right time in various sectors. Corporate sectors face the maximum issue in dealing with information as their communication and flow among various departments leads to the major problem of stagnation and overhead information leads to “Information Silo”. Conventional factors such as vendor diversity, mergers and acquisitions, and migration from legacy system also cause information silo problem in corporate sectors. In addition, technological advancements in the recent years have forced information storage to move onto cloud and with the sophisticated multi-cloud communication and sharing. Despite such evolutions handling the problem, the complexity in complete elimination of information silo is still daunting the root cause of it is multidimensional. Information exists with its priorities, values, and validity, and hence sharing of it is crucial. It is generated dynamically, and hence sharing lies in both the communication and sharing. Information sharing cannot be an open-social model the shared information may be of a higher value; the level of information access is also to be considered. The level of control over the sharing may be high with limited access or with a higher access but limited control, or at times with the right control. Each department in a corporate setup has its means of sharing/communication and hence achieving systematic information flow is an extant task. Moreover, the task of identification and sharing the right information to the people at right time is a strenuous task, and it requires expertise with complete understanding of the system. There are several hitches in information sharing. For example, the research and development department in an organization selectively shares information that may be essential to marketing department. At times, nonrepudiation of information sharing too poses a challenge to the management. The information may deny sharing the information that may lead to several management issues as well. Such an issue may not only be due to internal communicators with the corporate but also to external threats. The communication issue is one research area that has been attempted by many researchers.
