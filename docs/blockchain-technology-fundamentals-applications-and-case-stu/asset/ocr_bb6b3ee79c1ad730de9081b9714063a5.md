FIGURE 5.5 Claim chain process using blockchain.

Most of the claiming procedures are as same as the traditional process. Once the bill is generated by the healthcare center, it is passed to the insurance provider using blockchain. The blockchain is built using several blocks consisting of bill information and connected in chains by mining process. Here, the claim details (patient identity certificate, secrets, bills, etc.) are passed only to authorized insurance companies by applying encryption and hashing techniques. Hence, the manual revision of the claim is reduced a lot.
