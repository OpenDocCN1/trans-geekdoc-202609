Question 1. What is the correct way to declare a variable in PHP, ensuring that the variable name adheres to the naming conventions, which include beginning with a dollar sign, followed by a letter or underscore, and consisting of any combination of letters, numbers, and underscores?

+   A. $variable name
B. $variable$name
C. $_variable123
D. $123_variable

Correct Answer: C
