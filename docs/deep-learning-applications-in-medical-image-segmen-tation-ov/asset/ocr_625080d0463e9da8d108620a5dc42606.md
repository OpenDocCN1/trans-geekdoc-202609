The next stage entails brain extraction. Initially, the CSF is detected by the Chan-Vese active contour model (Chan and Vese 2001). Subsequently, the brain is identified by a series of two steps, that is, single largest component analysis and gray level co-occurrence matrix (GLCM) based approach (Devi et al. 2014b). The largest component of the brain is obtained by equation 4.1:

$I_{brain} = C(\text{argmax } A(i)), \quad 1 \le i \le n$ (4.1)

where A(i) denotes the area of each component C(i), i = 1, 2 ... n.

For extracting the total brain volume, the GLCM matrix is computed for each component with offset d = 1 and \(\theta = 0^{\circ}\). Using this matrix, the textural features are derived. Of these, the inverse difference moment (IDM) is given in equation 4.2:

$IDM = \sum_u \sum_v \frac{1}{1+(u-v)^2} p(u, v)$ (4.2)

where p(u, v) is the GLCM matrix element at location (u, v). IDM is a measure of the component's homogeneity in the brain image. Based on experimental investigation, a homogeneity threshold of 80% is set for total brain extraction.

Subsequently, the MWM tissue is segmented from the extracted brain. For this, a novel weighted localized Tsallis entropy thresholding technique is proposed. The traditional Tsallis entropy method entails global threshold. We introduce a patch-based local thresholding approach. The brain image is first divided into many uniform blocks of equal dimension (in increments of 5 pixels). Tsallis entropy threshold is applied to each individual block and the final output is concatenated. In addition, a majority voting mechanism is utilized for detecting MWM tissue. This ensures that spurious detections and outliers are eliminated from the result (Devi et al. 2017).

To further improve segmentation performance, especially in the peripheral areas, we incorporate a new weighting mechanism. The entropy output at each block is weighed according to its similarity with the ground truth of myelin segmentation. For this, two sets of weights are obtained. While weights w1 are computed from all datasets, weights w2 are acquired after age-based segregation of the input datasets.
