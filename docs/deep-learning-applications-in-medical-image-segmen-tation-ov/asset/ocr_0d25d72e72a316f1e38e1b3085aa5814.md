DL is gaining prominence as a powerful method for analyzing histopathology images and tackling tasks such as nucleus detection, image classification, and cell and tissue segmentation (Xing et al. 2018). The domain of digital pathology analysis of images shows significant advancement of whole slide imaging (WSI), enabling the digitization of glass slides containing stained tissue sections at high resolution. In another research, Dimitriou et al. (2019) offered an overview of challenges associated with analyzing WSI images of multi-gigabytes to construct DL models.
