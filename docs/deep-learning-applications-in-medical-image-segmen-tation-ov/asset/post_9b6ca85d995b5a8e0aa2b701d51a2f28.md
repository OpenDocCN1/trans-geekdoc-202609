Within this section, segmentation techniques for medical images using GANs are organized according to the specific GAN variants implemented. The assortment of models considered encompasses U-Net-based GAN, CycleGAN, DCGAN (deep convolutional GAN), CGAN (conditional GAN), and Pix-2-Pix GAN. This classification serves to distinguish and categorize the diverse GAN architectures utilized in the context of medical image segmentation. Each model represents a distinct approach, reflecting the nuanced strategies and methodologies employed in various studies within this domain.
