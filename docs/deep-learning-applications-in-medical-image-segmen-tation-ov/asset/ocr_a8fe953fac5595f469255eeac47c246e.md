# 2.2.3 UNet

Ronneberger et al. (2015) introduced U-Net, a well-known structure for medical image segmentation, employing the concept of deconvolution. The sophisticated framework of FCN serves as the foundation for this concept. U-Net’s enhanced design of skip connections across levels of the network is an additional benefit of the network’s expanded depth to 19 layers. It makes several adjustments to avoid having to choose between localization and context sensitivity. This trade-off increases as larger regions necessitate more pooling layers, which reduces the localization precision.

Small-sized patches, on the other hand, can only see a limited amount of input context. There is a pair of paths of encoder and decoder in the suggested framework, as depicted in Figure 2.5. The encoder path is structured similarly to CNN. The decoder path consists of an upsampling layer, followed by a deconvolution layer.
