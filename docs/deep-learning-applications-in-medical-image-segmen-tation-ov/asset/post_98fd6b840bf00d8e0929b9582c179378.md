## 11.5.2.4 Integration with AI for Comprehensive Medical Decision Support Systems

Deep learning for image segmentation has the potential to be used with other AI components to form comprehensive medical decision support systems. By merging deep learning models with other AI features (e.g., disease classification, and risk prediction), healthcare practitioners can gain a more comprehensive perspective for making informed clinical decisions (Drioua et al. 2023).

## 11.6 Conclusion

In conclusion, the future of medical image segmentation using deep learning models has enormous potential for revolutionizing healthcare diagnosis and treatment planning. Despite the challenges caused by restricted data availability, class imbalances, variability in patient circumstances, and computing resource limits, the proposed path ahead involves a comprehensive and collaborative effort. By utilizing advanced techniques, such as data augmentation, domain adaptation, and multi-modal fusion, the area aims to tackle data scarcity challenges, increase model generalization, and improve segmentation accuracy across varied patient populations. Integrating self-supervised learning and uncertainty estimation techniques provides to reduce reliance on labeled data while improving model interpretability. Furthermore, optimizing models for real-time, on-device processing ensures that crucial medical applications are delivered on time. Collaboration among machine learning researchers and healthcare practitioners, combined with continuous training on ethical issues plays a very critical role in the responsible development and implementation of deep learning models in medical image segmentation. The objective of these collaborative efforts is to develop robust, transparent, and broadly applicable models that dramatically increase the precision and efficiency of medical image interpretation, ultimately leading to better patient outcomes.
