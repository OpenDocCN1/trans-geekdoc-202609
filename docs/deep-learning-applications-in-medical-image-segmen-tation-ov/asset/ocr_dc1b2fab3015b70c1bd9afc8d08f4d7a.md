Image segmentation using DL has many uses in many different fields. It helps in diagnosis and therapy planning in medical imaging by providing precise tumor delineation, organ, and anatomical structure identification. By segmenting objects of interest, it allows robots to see and interact with their surroundings in the field of robotics.
