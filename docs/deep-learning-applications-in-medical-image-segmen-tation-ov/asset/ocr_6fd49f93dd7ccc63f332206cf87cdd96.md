# 7.4.1.4 Attention-Based Approaches

Numerous studies adopted the attention mechanism in their segmentation framework attention-based models have emerged as powerful tools. Unlike the conventional U-Net architecture, these models strategically integrate attention modules within both contracting and expanding paths, resulting in capturing essential features relevant to the lung region and overall segmentation accuracy. Kim et al. presented a self-attention-based deep learning model consisting of “X-attention and Y-attention modules” (Kim and Lee 2021). The X-attention module concentrates on local features, while the Y-attention module incorporates global context from deeper layers to effectively utilize global features. Both attention modules employ channel and spatial attention, enhancing the feature extraction process. The attention maps derived from these modules are multiplied and utilized in subsequent layers through residual learning. The proposed attention modules contribute to accurate pixel-level attention information, resulting in smooth segmentation masks with reduced noise.
