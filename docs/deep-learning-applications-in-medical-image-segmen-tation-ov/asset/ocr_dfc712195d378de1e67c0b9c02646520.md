# 3.1 Introduction

Medical image segmentation, a critical facet of modern healthcare technology, underpins the precision and efficacy of diagnostic and therapeutic procedures (Sharma et al. 2018). The intricate task of delineating structures within medical images has evolved alongside advancements in imaging modalities, each step forward bringing both challenges and innovative solutions. This research embarks on a comprehensive exploration, delving into the historical tapestry of traditional approaches in medical image segmentation, aiming to illuminate their evolution, significance, and enduring impact in the landscape of healthcare.

# 3.1.1 Contextualizing Medical Image Segmentation

The landscape of medical image analysis has undergone a transformative journey, driven by the progression from conventional radiography to the sophistication of three-dimensional (3D) imaging modalities like magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) and computed tomography (CT). The demand for precise segmentation has grown exponentially with the expanding scope of medical imaging applications. From aiding in organ delineation for surgical planning to facilitating early detection of pathological conditions, such as tumors, accurate segmentation forms the cornerstone of effective medical image analysis (Gillies et al. 2016).
