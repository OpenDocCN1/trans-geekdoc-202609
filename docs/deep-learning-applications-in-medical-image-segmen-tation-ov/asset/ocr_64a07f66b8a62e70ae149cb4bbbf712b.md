# 8.4.1.2 Eye Segmentation Using GAN

The challenges stemming from fundus image acquisition limitations, such as prevalent image noise and the intricate, variable nature of the retina, pose significant hurdles for segmentation. Surprisingly, numerous GAN-based approaches outperform human experts in segmenting fundus images. Automatically segmenting fundus images presents significant challenges due to the intricate branching patterns of vessels and the prevalent background noise. Lahiri et al. achieved precise retinal blood vessel segmentation in their works (Lahiri et al. 2017; Mahapatra et al. 2017; Iqbal and Ali 2018; Javanmardi and Tasdizen 2018; Son et al. 2019; Yang et al. 2020). In their 2017 study (Lahiri et al. 2017), Lahiri et al. pioneered the use of GAN semi-supervised learning, marking an effective approach to segmenting retinal blood vessels. Iqbal and Ali (2018) (Iqbal and Ali 2018) redefined a set of loss functions to generate more realistic retinal images, aiding in the segmentation process. Yang et al.
