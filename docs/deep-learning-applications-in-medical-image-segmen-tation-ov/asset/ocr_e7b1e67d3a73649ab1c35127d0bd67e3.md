# 4.3 Methodology

The proposed methodology is divided into four sections, namely, extraction of the brain from pediatric MRI scans along with MWM delineation, computation of MI and model fitting, study of myelination differences between the right and left brain hemispheres, and analysis of myelination in preterm born babies. The technical details of each phase are elucidated below.

# 4.3.1 Input Datasets

The sample consists of pediatric brain MR images of 40 babies acquired from the Department of Radiology, Sri Ramachandra University, Chennai, India. The babies were aged between 1 day and 2 years at the time of scan. Their age distribution is as follows:

+   - 30 babies born preterm (with gestational age between 30 and 33 weeks) and scanned at 40 weeks term-corrected age.
+   - 10 older babies aged 7 weeks, 9.6 weeks, 10 weeks, 2.9 months, 5.5 months, 10 months, 11 months, 16.3 months, 18.1 months, and 21.5 months.

The MR parameters used for imaging are presented below:

+   - Scanner: Signa HDxt MR machine with field strength 1.5 Tesla.
+   - T1 images: Repetition time (TR) – 500 ms, echo time (TE) – 12 ms, flip angle – 75°, voxel size – 0.3516 × 0.3516 × 4 mm³.
+   - T2 images: TR – 5400 ms, TE – 117.488 ms, flip angle – 90°, voxel size – 0.3516 × 0.3516 × 4 mm³.
