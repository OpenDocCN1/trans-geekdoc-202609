# 1.2.7.2 CVC-ClinicDB and CVC-ColonDB

CVC-ClinicDB and CVC-ColonDB are datasets specifically designed for polyp segmentation in endoscopic images. The CVC-ClinicDB collection includes 29 distinct sequences totaling 612 still images at a resolution of 384× 288. Every image in the dataset is accompanied by a meticulously annotated ground truth that accurately delineates the boundaries of the polyp. CVC-ClinicDB contains endoscopic images captured during routine colonoscopy examinations. On the other hand, the CVC-ColonDB dataset contains 380 images. The images in CVC-ColonDB have a resolution of 576× 720 pixels. The images of both these datasets exhibit a variety of polyp types, sizes, shapes, and textures. The challenges in the CVC-ClinicDB as well as in the CVC-ColonDB dataset include polyp detection, accurate delineation of polyp boundaries, and differentiating polyps from other structures or artifacts.
