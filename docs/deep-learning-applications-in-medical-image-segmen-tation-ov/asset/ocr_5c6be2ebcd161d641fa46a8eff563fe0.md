# 4.4.3 Myelination in Right and Left Hemispheres

The myelin progress is assessed separately in the right and left hemispheres to identify possible variations in the normal myelination pattern. As shown in Section 4.2, Gompertz model is considered the model of best fit for whole myelination. To analyze hemispheric changes in myelination, two sub-models (namely, simple and complex models) are proposed based on the Gompertz growth model. The equations and parameters for these models are illustrated in Table 4.4. The F ratio between the models is 3.2705 and their p value 0.0280. As α is set as 0.05, p < α is true. We conclude that the complex model for hemispheric myelination fits significantly better. Thus, modeling the MI values separately for the two hemispheres is justified. Our results suggest significant differences between the myelination patterns in the right and left hemispheres of the pediatric brain. Specific information on the conspicuous differences in myelin progress and a comparison with the findings of other works are presented in Section 5.3.
