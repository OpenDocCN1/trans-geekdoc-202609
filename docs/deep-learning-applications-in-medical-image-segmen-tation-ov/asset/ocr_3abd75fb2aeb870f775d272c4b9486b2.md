Based on these metrics, the percentage lag in myelination (plagMI) of premature babies is given by equation 4.19:

```
plag_MI = \frac{\widehat{MI}_{term} - MI_{preterm}}{\widehat{MI}_{term}} * 100
(4.19)
```

The advantages of the proposed plagMI index are as follows:

+   * The metric enables a quantitative assessment of the delay or retardation in preterm myelination against full-term births.
+   * It is useful in longitudinal studies of premature children to demarcate the age at which the myelination gap closes.
+   * Given that preterm myelination is affected by various prenatal and postnatal conditions, such as birth complications, congenital anomalies, and environmental aspects, it is clinically significant to closely monitor myelin progress and detect anomalies early.
