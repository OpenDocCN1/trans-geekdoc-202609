+   • Precision medicine: Precision medicine aims at customizing various medical interventions and treatment plans to individual and distinctive characteristics of each patient. Hence, precise segmentation of medical images plays a significant role in advancing the principles of precision medicine. Detailed personalized treatment plans can be created by healthcare professionals by accurately delineating anatomical structures and pathological regions using precision medicine. As can be seen in the treatment of cancer, oncologists using precise segmentation of tumors are able to customize therapeutic strategies based on the specific characteristics of tumors. Moreover, segmentation also facilitates the monitoring of the disease over a period of time (Alsanea and Dutta 2024). Hence, the amalgamation of the machine learning algorithms helps in recognizing subtle patterns and variations and aids in increasing the efficiency and accuracy of the personalized medicine approaches. Overall, precise and accurate segmentation is used as a foundation in the realization of precision medicine, offering the possibility of optimizing patient outcomes and minimizing adverse effects by tailoring treatments to the distinctive attributes of each patient.
