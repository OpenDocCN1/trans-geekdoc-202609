VAEs excel in output diversity compared to GANs and avoid mode collapse. However, a notable drawback is their tendency to often produce blurred and unclear output images, which arises from the regularization term incorporated in the loss function. In recent times, a novel category of deep generative models known as diffusion models (DMs) (Sohl-Dickstein et al. 2015; Ho et al. 2020) has surfaced, showing considerable potential in generating varied and lifelike outputs. Despite this promise, DMs are still in the early stages of development and have not gained significant traction in medical applications. However, they are anticipated to offer a compelling alternative to existing generative models. A drawback of DMs is their substantial computational expense and lengthy sampling duration.
