# 2.3.1.7 Multi-organ

To protect vital organs during radiation planning, organs at risk (OAR) must be clearly defined. Before radiation for nasopharyngeal cancer, more than 20 organs must be precisely segmented. Since then, deep learning approaches have outperformed OAR segmentation due to recent developments in deep learning. Multi-organ segmentation is notoriously challenging due to the common occurrence of organ imbalances. The U-Net architecture has shown to be highly effective at segmenting medical images, making it the de facto standard. The features learned by ordinary convolution layers, on the other hand, are not distinguishable. To solve this issue, Wang et al. (2019d) suggest a unique architecture known as the densely connected deep U-Net (DC UNet). The DC U-Net comprises three components: dense connections, selective deconvolution layers incorporating upsampling, and a trans-convolutional filling function. To segment OARs in thoracic CT scans, Vesal et al. (2019) designed a deep learning framework for the following organs and structures: heart, oesophagus, trachea, and aorta.
