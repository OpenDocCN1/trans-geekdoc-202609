# ### 8.4.3.3 Segmentation Using CycleGAN

In 2021, Wang and colleagues (Wang et al. 2021b) introduced a weakly supervised learning network that relies on CycleGAN for the segmentation of lesions in OCT images. Authors in Xing et al. (2021) combined CycleGAN with FCN for the segmentation of breast lesions. This integration aimed to reduce the workload on radiologists by easing annotation tasks. Authors in Lei et al.
