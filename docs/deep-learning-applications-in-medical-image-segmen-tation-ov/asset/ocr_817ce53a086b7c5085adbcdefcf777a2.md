# ## 5.2 Classical Image Segmentation Techniques

Traditional image segmentation methods, often referred to as classical techniques, are employed to partition images into distinct sections or objects depending on specific features such as color, brightness, texture, or motion. These methods have been extensively studied and implemented in various industries, including medical imaging and industrial quality control. The list of classical image segmentation tasks includes thresholding, region growing, edge detection, clustering, and watershed (Figure 5.1). In this section, we will elaborate on the fundamental concepts and operational procedures of classical image segmentation techniques.
