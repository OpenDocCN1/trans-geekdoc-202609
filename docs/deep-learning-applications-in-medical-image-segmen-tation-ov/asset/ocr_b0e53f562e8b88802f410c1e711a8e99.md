# ## 7.5.1.6 COVID-19 Radiography Database

The “COVID-19 Chest X-ray Database” is a vast repository of medical images assembled by scholars from the “The University of Dhaka, Qatar University, Doha, and Bangladesh,” in collaboration with partners from “Pakistan and Malaysia,” along with medical professionals. This dataset has been progressively released in stages, with subsequent updates increasing the volume of images across different categories. All the CXR images are in PNG format and have a resolution of 299×299 pixels. The dataset is continuously evolving, with updates provided in stages. As of the latest information, it includes “3616 COVID-19 positive cases,” “10,192 normal images,” “6012 lung opacity images,” and “1345 viral pneumonia images.” The commitment is to regularly update the database with new radiographs for COVID-19 pneumonia patients. This database serves as a great resource for scientists to generate significant scholarly contributions toward addressing the COVID-19 pandemic. (Kaggle (2022)).

PadChest: The PadChest dataset comprises 160,868 chest X-rays derived from 109,931 studies and involves 67,000 at “San Juan Hospital” (Bustos et al. 2020) in Spain over a period from 2009 to 2017. The images are stored as 16-bit grayscale images, preserving detailed information. Among the reports associated with the X-rays, 27,593 were manually annotated by physicians. An innovative approach involved training a RNN on these labeled reports. The trained RNN was then employed to automatically label the remaining dataset based on the information extracted from the reports. The labels adhere to a hierarchical taxonomy that aligns with the standards of the Unified Medical Language System (UMLS). (Bodenreider 2004).
