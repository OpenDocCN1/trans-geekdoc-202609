OCT is a noninvasive imaging technology that uses light waves to capture high-resolution, cross-sectional images of biological tissues. It is commonly employed in ophthalmology to visualize the layers of the retina, allowing for early detection and monitoring of eye conditions such as macular degeneration and glaucoma. Additionally, OCT has applications in other medical fields, including cardiology and dermatology, where it provides detailed insights into tissue structures without the need for ionizing radiation (Table 8.11).
