Chen et al. (2017a) created an innovative voxel-wise residual network (VoxRes-Net). When training an extremely DNN, residual units can help mitigate the degradation problem. For segmenting the brain in a 3D MRI image, Wang et al. (2019c) developed a 3D convolutional model with residual units and a pyramid pooling block. While numerous deep learning techniques have been devised for segmenting brain MR images, the majority of them neglect to integrate shape priors. This omission prevents them from leveraging the consistency of brain structures, thus hindering their ability to attain optimal performance. To solve this problem, Sun et al. (2020) presented a deep learning architecture with two subnetworks to perform ROI segmentation of the brain using anatomical attention.
