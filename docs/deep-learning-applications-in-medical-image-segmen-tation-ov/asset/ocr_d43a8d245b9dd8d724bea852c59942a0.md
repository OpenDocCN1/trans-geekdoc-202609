The algorithm achieves highly accurate segmentation results, particularly in delineating the renal cortex. While the precision of the provided reference segmentations seems to set a modest upper limit on the numerical performance, the proposed solution offers dependable and accurate automatic segmentations of the renal cortex in CT images. Yang et al. (2018a) integrated 3D FCN-based architecture with pyramid pooling to enhance the extraction of features. Zhang et al. (2019) presented a two-phase framework based on a 3D FCN for segmenting kidney and tumor. To lessen class disparity and computation expense, the first step is locating the kidney in its preliminary stages and removing the extraneous background. The kidney and tumor on the cropped patch are then carefully segmented in the second stage. With a Composite Dice of 90.24%, the suggested technique places the fourth-place team out of 105 contending teams in the MICCAI 2019 Kidney Tumor Segmentation (KiTS) Challenge. To segment kidneys and kidney tumors from CT images, Zhao et al. (2020) presented a multi-scale supervised U-Net. Utilizing the KiTS19 dataset, the authors devised an architecture to improve the performance of the 3D U-Net by integrating deep supervision with exponential loss.
