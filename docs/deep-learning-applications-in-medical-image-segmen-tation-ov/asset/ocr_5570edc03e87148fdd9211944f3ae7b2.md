# ### 3.7.2 Challenges Encountered in Real-World Scenarios

Despite their historical relevance, traditional approaches face challenges when applied to real-world scenarios. In cases where pathology introduces variations in tissue characteristics, thresholding methods may struggle to adapt. The presence of noise and artifacts in medical images poses challenges for region-based techniques, impacting the accuracy of segmentation. Contour-based algorithms may encounter difficulties in scenarios with irregular shapes or overlapping structures. These challenges underscore the need for innovative solutions and pave the way for exploring the integration of computational techniques.

# ### 3.7.3 Integration of Computational Techniques

Recent case studies highlight the integration of computational techniques, particularly deep learning, to enhance the accuracy and robustness of medical image segmentation. Deep learning models have demonstrated superior performance in segmenting organs, tumors, and anatomical structures across various imaging modalities (Litjens et al. 2017). The adaptability of deep learning to complex image characteristics positions it as a promising solution to overcome the limitations of traditional approaches.

# ### 3.7.4 Leveraging Big Data for Improved Segmentation

Case studies exploring the use of large-scale datasets showcase the potential of big data in improving the capabilities of medical image segmentation. The availability of extensive and diverse datasets enables the training of models that generalize well to different patient populations and imaging conditions. Real-world applications, such as automated diagnosis and treatment planning, benefit from segmentation methodologies trained on comprehensive datasets.
