# ### 8.4.1.7 Abdomen Segmentation Using GANs

Deep neural networks have found extensive application in automating the segmentation of organs in abdominal CT scans. In abdominal scan images, distinguishing the spatial boundaries between adjacent organs poses a challenge, compounded by the substantial variations in size among different target organs. Traditional DL approaches struggle to accurately segment each organ due to these complexities.

Yang et al. (2017) and Kim and Ye (2018) applied GAN techniques specifically for liver segmentation, significantly enhancing both segmentation accuracy and processing speed. Using GAN, Liu et al. (2019a) (Liu et al. (2019b)) performed label assignments and generated probabilistic maps, enabling accurate segmentation of colorectal tumors. Ruan et al. (2020) integrated a multi-scale feature extractor and an area-of-interest locator into GAN for swift and accurate kidney tumor segmentation. For stomach and pancreas segmentation, Huang et al. (2021) and Li et al. (2021b) employed diverse adversarial networks (Table 8.7).
