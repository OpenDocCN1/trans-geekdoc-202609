The complete heart has been segmented using DL-based neural networks, according to several studies (Wang and Smedby 2018; Payer et al. 2018). Tran et al. (Tran 2016) performed pixel categorization using a fully convolutional neural network architecture for an MRI of the heart. Short-axis cardiac MRI has never seen such sophisticated semantic segmentation as is possible with the proposed FCN architecture. Han et al. (2020) developed a volumetric FCN that is cascaded. This method employs a pair of V-Nets in series. The first network pinpoints the heart, while the second divides the heart and large vessel structures into smaller parts. To aid with heart segmentation from MR images, the authors rebuilt the V-Net into a 3D V-Net. Ye et al. (2019) provided a novel deeply supervised 3D UNET that improves upon the original network through the use of multi-depth fusion to extract context information. The authors took focus loss and extended its use to multi-category challenges in the field of picture segmentation. Finally, they coupled the focused loss with the dice loss to form the new hybrid loss function. The hybrid loss can be used to address the issue of uneven categories. Shen et al. (2019) developed a unified architecture that utilizes both deep learning and the conventional level set approach to coronary Computed Tomography Angiography (CTA) segmentation. The 3D semantic properties of coronary arteries are learned utilizing a 3D FCN, which serves as a great jumping-off point for the classical level set. Xu et al. (2020) proposed a new type of GAN called a progressive sequential causal GAN (PSCGAN).

This marks the pioneering development of a CA-free Ischemic Heart Disease (IHD) technique within cine MR imaging, capable of producing both an image akin to Late Gadolinium Enhancement (LGE) and accurately segmenting tissues relevant to diagnosis concurrently. To that goal, the PSCGAN has three very useful features:

+   1) An iterative framework for image synthesis and segmentation training that uses a divide-and-conquer strategy and consists of three stages (i.e. a priori generation, conditional synthesis, and enhanced segmentation).

+   2) A sequential causal learning network (SCLN) has been devised, featuring a multi-scale, two-stream pathway along with a multi-attention weighting unit. This architecture efficiently identifies task-specific dependencies while extracting spatial and temporal connections from cine MR images. It also incorporates the GAN architecture to use adversarial training to make it easier to understand the latent space dependencies of interest in cine MR images during the entire process.

+   3) Two loss concepts developed for autonomous learning: To mitigate noise during synthesis, the authors employ a synthetic regularization loss term that makes use of the spare regularization, and in segmentation, the authors employ an auxiliary loss term that makes use of the number of pixels for each tissue to compensate for discriminating.
