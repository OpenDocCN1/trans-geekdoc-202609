# 8.4.1.5 Breast Segmentation Using GANs

Breast cancer ranks among the most prevalent cancers, particularly affecting females. Zhu et al. achieved precise segmentation of breast masses (Zhu and Xie 2016; Shen et al. 2019; Han et al. 2020; Ma et al. 2020; Xing et al. 2021; Li et al. 2021c), a crucial step for computer-assisted breast cancer diagnosis. Shen et al. (2019) pioneered the use of CGAN to produce X-ray breast mass images, incorporating U-Net to enhance segmentation precision. Additionally, merged CycleGAN with FCN for breast lesion segmentation, alleviating the burden on radiologists for annotation tasks (Table 8.5).
