## Navigating Sustainability and Perception
The theme of Navigating Sustainability and Perception highlights how consumer behavior, fashion trends, and sustainability are interconnected. It emphasizes that understanding consumer preferences can drive shifts in fashion trends, leading to a more eco-conscious marketplace.
