## Conclusion and Future Work

The primary objective of this study is to compare and contrast many clustering methods in quest of the optimal data mining strategy for early detection of cardiovascular illnesses. In this study, multiple clustering methods implemented in `Weka` are used to the dataset used for predicting cardiovascular illnesses. Clustering is an exam- ple of an unsupervised learning method that relies on seeing data. The primary goal of any clustering study is to classify data into groups based on similarities and differences. Both the `canopy` and `Cobweb` methods are com- pared based on how many clusters they produce and how accurate those clusters are. The study presented here showed how to obtain clusters and determine a new centroid for high-dimensional data sets. After three levels of filtering, it is clear that `cobweb` performs better than the other two methods, with an accuracy of 78.54%.
