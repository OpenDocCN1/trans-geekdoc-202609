## Abstract

Hydroponics provides the ability to manage crop systems with precision, but for large-scale operations, continuous monitoring and control are essential, a function that is made possible through the use of `IoT` technology. ## Introduction to Challenges in the Agriculture Industry

In this context, the integration of intelligent Internet of Things (`IoT`) systems and the adoption of hydroponic techniques hold great promise as viable solutions.
