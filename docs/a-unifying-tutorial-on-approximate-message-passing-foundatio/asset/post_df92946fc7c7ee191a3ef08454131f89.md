To this end, let \( L > 0 \) be such that \( \psi \in \text{PL}_2(r, L) \), and for each \( y \in \mathbb{R} \), define \( \psi_y, \tilde{\psi}_y : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} \) by \( \psi_y(z) := \psi(\tau_1 z, y) \) and \( \tilde{\psi}_y(z) := \psi_y(z) - \mathbb{E}(\psi_y(Z)) \), where \( Z \sim N(0, 1) \). Then by Lemma B.23(a), there exists \( K_0 > 0 \), depending only on \( \tau_1 \) and \( r \), such that \( \psi_y \in \text{PL}_1(r, K_0 L_y) \) with \( L_y := L(1 \lor |y|^{r-1}) \). For fixed \( n \in \mathbb{N} \) and \( y_1, \dots, y_n \in \mathbb{R} \), define \( \check{L} \equiv \check{L}(y_1, \dots, y_n) := (L_{y_1}, \dots, L_{y_n}) \). Let \( r' := r/(r-1) \in (1, 2] \) be the Hölder conjugate of \( r \), so that \( 1/r + 1/r' = 1 \), and note that since \( \|\cdot\|_{p'} \le \|\cdot\|_p \) for \( 1 \le p \le p' \le \infty \), we have

\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\|\check{L}\|_\infty}{n^{1/r'}} \le \frac{\|\check{L}\|_2}{n^{1/r'}} \le \frac{\|\check{L}\|_{r'}}{n^{1/r'}} = \|\check{L}\|_{n, r'} \\
= \left( \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n |L_{y_i}|^{r'} \right)^{1/r'} \le L \left( 1 + \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n |y_i|^r \right)^{1/r'} \\
= L(1 + \|y\|_{n, r'}^r)^{1/r'}.
\end{aligned}
\]
(A.26)

By Lemma B.12, there exists a universal constant \( C > 0 \) such that if \( Z_1, \dots, Z_n \stackrel{\text{iid}}{\sim} N(0, 1) \), then

\[
\begin{aligned}
P(n, t, y_1, \dots, y_n) \\

\le \exp\left( 1 - \min \left\{ \left( \frac{nt}{(Cr)^r K_0 \|\check{L}\|_2} \right)^2, \left( \frac{nt}{(Cr)^r K_0 \|\check{L}\|_\infty} \right)^{2/r} \right\} \right) \\

for every \( t \ge 0 \). Returning to (A.25), we see that

### \mathcal{H}_1(d):

For each \( n \), we have \( (\gamma, m^0, h^1) \stackrel{d}{=} (\gamma, m^0, h^{1,0}) \) by (A.23) and Lemma B.6(c), so for each fixed \( \phi \in \text{PL}_2(1) \), it follows that
