\(\bar{\mathrm{T}}^{[k+1]} \in \mathbb{R}^{(k+1)\times(k+1)}\) is again non-negative definite. Under (B4), it can be shown as in Lemma 2.2 that \(\bar{\Sigma}^{[k]}, \bar{\mathrm{T}}^{[k]}\) are positive definite for all \(k \in \mathbb{N}\), and also that (2.12)-(2.13) extends (2.11), with \(\sigma_{k-1}^2 = \bar{\Sigma}_{k-1, k-1} > 0\) and \(\tau_k^2 = \bar{\mathrm{T}}_{k, k} > 0\) for all \(k\).

# **Theorem 2.5.**

Suppose that (B0)-(B5) hold for a sequence of asymmetric AMP recursions (2.10) indexed by \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) and \(p \equiv p_n\) with \(n/p \to \delta \in (0, \infty)\). Then for each fixed \(k \in \mathbb{N}_0\), we have

\(\tilde{d}_r(\nu_n(e^k, \gamma), N(0, \sigma_k^2) \otimes \pi_{\bar{\gamma}})\)
\(= \sup_{\psi \in \mathrm{PL}_2(r, 1)} \left| \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \psi(e_i^k, \gamma_i) - \mathbb{E}(\psi(G_k^\sigma, \bar{\gamma})) \right| \xrightarrow{\mathrm{p}} 0, \quad (2.14)\)
\(\tilde{d}_r(\nu_p(h^{k+1}, \beta), N(0, \tau_{k+1}^2) \otimes \pi_{\bar{\beta}})\)
\(= \sup_{\psi \in \mathrm{PL}_2(r, 1)} \left| \frac{1}{p} \sum_{j=1}^p \psi(h_j^{k+1}, \beta_j) - \mathbb{E}(\psi(G_{k+1}^\tau, \bar{\beta})) \right| \xrightarrow{\mathrm{p}} 0,\)

\(\tilde{d}_r(\nu_n(e^0, \dots, e^k, \beta), N_{k+1}(0, \bar{\Sigma}^{[k+1]}) \otimes \pi_{\bar{\beta}})\)
\(= \sup_{\psi \in \mathrm{PL}_{k+2}(r, 1)} \left| \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \psi(e_i^0, \dots, e_i^k, \gamma_i) - \mathbb{E}(\psi(G_0^\sigma, \dots, G_k^\sigma, \bar{\gamma})) \right| \xrightarrow{\mathrm{p}} 0, \quad (2.15)\)
\(\tilde{d}_r(\nu_p(h^1, \dots, h^{k+1}, \beta), N_{k+1}(0, \bar{\mathrm{T}}^{[k+1]}) \otimes \pi_{\bar{\beta}})\)
\(= \sup_{\psi \in \mathrm{PL}_{k+2}(r, 1)} \left| \frac{1}{p} \sum_{j=1}^p \psi(h_j^1, \dots, h_j^{k+1}, \beta_j) - \mathbb{E}(\psi(G_1^\tau, \dots, G_{k+1}^\tau, \bar{\beta})) \right| \xrightarrow{\mathrm{p}} 0\)

as \(n \to \infty\). Equivalent statements hold with \(d_r\) in place of \(\tilde{d}_r\).
