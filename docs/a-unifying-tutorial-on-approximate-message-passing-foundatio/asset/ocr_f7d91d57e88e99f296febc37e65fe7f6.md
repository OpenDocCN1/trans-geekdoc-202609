\(\sup_{\psi \in PL_{k+2}(2,1)} \left| \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \psi(\psi_i^0, v_i^1, \dots, v_i^k, v_i) - \mathbb{E}(\psi(\mu_0 V + \sigma_0 U, \mu_1 V + \sigma_1 G_1, \dots, \mu_k V + \sigma_k G_k, V)) \right| \xrightarrow{\text{p}} 0 \quad (3.8)\)

as \(n \to \infty\), where \((\sigma_1 G_1, \dots, \sigma_k G_k) \sim N_k(0, \bar{\Sigma}^{[k]})\) is independent of \((U,V)\) from (M1). In other words, writing \(\breve{\nu}^k\) for the distribution of \((\mu_0 V + \sigma_0 U, \mu_1 V + \sigma_1 G_1, \dots, \mu_k V + \sigma_k G_k, V)\), we have

\(\tilde{d}_2(\nu_n(v^0, v^1, \dots, v^k, v), \breve{\nu}^k) \xrightarrow{\text{p}} 0 \quad \text{or equivalently}\)
\(d_2(\nu_n(v^0, v^1, \dots, v^k, v), \breve{\nu}^k) \xrightarrow{\text{p}} 0 \quad \text{as } n \to \infty.\)

Before discussing Theorem 3.1 and its proof, we note that as an immediate consequence of (3.8), Corollary 3.2 below yields an exact expression for the asymptotic deviation of \(\hat{v}^k = g_k(v^k)\) from \(v\) with respect to any pseudo-Lipschitz loss function of order 2. In particular, the asymptotic mean squared error and empirical correlation in (3.10) and (3.11) respectively depend only on \(\lambda\) and the state evolution parameters \(\mu_{k+1}, \sigma_{k+1}\).

**Corollary 3.2.** In the setting of Theorem 3.1, fix \(k \in \mathbb{N}\) and let \(n \to \infty\). Then taking \(G_k \sim N(0,1)\) to be independent of \(V \sim \pi\), we have

\(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \psi(\hat{v}_i^k, v_i) \xrightarrow{\text{p}} \mathbb{E}\{\psi(g_k(\mu_k V + \sigma_k G_k), V)\} \quad (3.9)\)

for all \(\psi \in PL_2(2)\). Consequently,

\(\|\hat{v}^k - v\|_n^2 \xrightarrow{\text{p}} \mathbb{E}\{(g_k(\mu_k V + \sigma_k G_k) - V)^2\} = \sigma_{k+1}^2 - \frac{2\mu_{k+1}}{\lambda} + 1 \quad (3.10)\)

and

\(\frac{|\langle \hat{v}^k, v \rangle_n|}{\|\hat{v}^k\|_n \|v\|_n} \xrightarrow{\text{p}} \frac{|\mathbb{E}(V g_k(\mu_k V + \sigma_k G_k))|}{\sqrt{\mathbb{E}(g_k(\mu_k V + \sigma_k G_k)^2)}} = \frac{|\mu_{k+1}|}{\lambda \sigma_{k+1}}. \quad (3.11)\)

**Remark 3.3.** Observe that \(\|v^k\|_n^2 \xrightarrow{\text{p}} \mathbb{E}((\mu_k V + \sigma_k G_k)^2) = \mu_k^2 + \sigma_k^2\) and \(\|\hat{v}^{k-1}\|_n^2 = \|g_{k-1}(v^{k-1})\|_n \xrightarrow{\text{p}} \sigma_k^2\) for all \(k \in \mathbb{N}\), so \(\|v^k\|_n^2 - \|\hat{v}^{k-1}\|_n^2\) and \(\|\hat{v}^{k-1}\|_n^2\) are strongly consistent estimators of \(\mu_k^2\) and \(\sigma_k^2\) respectively.
