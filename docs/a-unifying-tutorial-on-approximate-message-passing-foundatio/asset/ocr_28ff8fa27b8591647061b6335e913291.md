Contrasting the definitions of $h^{k+1,k}$ and $\bar{h}^{k+1}$ in (A.7) and (A.21) respectively for $k \in \{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$, we see that the random quantities $\alpha^k$ and $\|m^k\|_n$ in (A.7) are replaced in (A.21) with the deterministic $\bar{\alpha}^k \in \mathbb{R}^k$ and $\bar{\tau}_{k+1} \in (0, \infty)$ from (A.14) and (A.15) respectively; these turn out to be the correct limiting values in Proposition A.16(i,j) below under the non-degeneracy assumption (A4).

We are now in a position to state the main result of this subsection. To ease notation, we will often suppress the dependence on $n$ of quantities such as $h^k \equiv h^k(n)$, $v^{k,\ell} \equiv v^{k,\ell}(n)$, $\alpha^k \equiv \alpha^k(n)$ and $\Delta^k \equiv \Delta^k(n)$.

# **Proposition A.16.** For a sequence of symmetric AMP recursions (2.1) satisfying (A0)–(A5) as well as (A4), the following hold as $n \rightarrow \infty$ for each $k \in \mathbb{N}$:

+   (a) $\|\Delta^k\|_{n,r} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} 0$;
+   
+   (b) $\|h^j\|_{n,r} = O_c(1)$ for $1 \le j \le k$; $\|m^j\|_{n,r} = O_c(1)$ for $0 \le j \le k$;
+   
+   (c) $n^{-1} \sum_{i=1}^n \psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \gamma_i) \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} \mathbb{E}(\psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \bar{\gamma}))$ for every $\psi \in \text{PL}_{k+1}(r)$;
+   
+   (d) $n^{-1} \sum_{i=1}^n m_i^0 \phi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \gamma_i) \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} \mathbb{E}(F_0(\bar{\gamma}) \cdot \phi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \bar{\gamma}))$ for every $\phi \in \text{PL}_{k+1}(1)$;
+   
+   (e) $\langle m^{j-1}, m^{\ell-1} \rangle_n \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} \mathbb{E}(\bar{G}_j \bar{G}_\ell) = \bar{\mathbf{T}}_{j,\ell}$ for $1 \le j, \ell \le k+1$;
+   
+   (f) $\langle h^j, m^\ell \rangle_n = \langle h^j, f_\ell(h^\ell, \gamma) \rangle_n \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} \mathbb{E}(\bar{G}_j f_\ell(\bar{G}_\ell, \bar{\gamma})) = \mathbb{E}(f'_\ell(\bar{G}_\ell, \bar{\gamma})) \mathbb{E}(\bar{G}_j \bar{G}_\ell) = \bar{b}_\ell \bar{\mathbf{T}}_{j,\ell}$ for $1 \le j, \ell \le k$; $\langle h^j, m^0 \rangle_n \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} 0$ for $1 \le j \le k$;
+   
+   (g) $b_k = \langle f'_k(h^k, \gamma) \rangle_n \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} \mathbb{E}(f'_k(\bar{G}_k, \bar{\gamma})) = \bar{b}_k$;
+   
+   (h) $v^{k,\ell}/n = (H_k^\top m^\ell - b_\ell M_k^\top m^{\ell-1})/n \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} 0$ for $0 \le \ell \le k$;
+   
+   (i) $\alpha^k \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} \bar{\alpha}^k$;
+   
+   (j) $\|\bar{m}^k\|_n \xrightarrow{\mathcal{L}} \bar{\tau}_{k+1} = \text{Var}^{1/2}(\bar{G}_{k+1} \mid \bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k)$.
