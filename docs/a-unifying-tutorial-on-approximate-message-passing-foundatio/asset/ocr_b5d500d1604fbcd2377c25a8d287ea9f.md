Proof of Lemma B.10. For each n, denote by \(\mathcal{B}_n, \mathcal{B}'_n\) the Borel \(\sigma\)-algebras of \(E_n, E'_n\) respectively. It follows from Dudley (2002, Theorem 2.5.7) and Kallenberg (1997, Lemma 1.2) that \(\prod_{j=1}^n E'_j\) and \(\prod_{j=1}^n E_j\) are Polish spaces with Borel \(\sigma\)-algebras \(\otimes_{j=1}^n \mathcal{B}'_j\) and \(\otimes_{j=1}^n \mathcal{B}_j\) respectively. Denote by \(\mu_n, \pi_n\) the distributions of \(Y_n\) and \((Y_1, ..., Y_n)\) on \((E'_n, \mathcal{B}'_n)\) and \((\prod_{j=1}^n E'_j, \otimes_{j=1}^n \mathcal{B}'_j)\) respectively. Since \(E_n\) is a Polish space, we know from Section B.2 that there exists a regular conditional distribution for \(X_n\) given \(\sigma(g_n(X_n))\). Equivalently, there is a family of probability distributions \((Q_y^n)_{y \in E'_n}\) on \(E_n\) satisfying conditions (I) and (II) in Remark B.5, where we take \(X := X_n, Y := g_n(X_n) \stackrel{d}{=} Y_n\) and \(P^Y := \mu_n\). It follows from Remark B.5(II) that \(\mathbb{P}(X_n \in A) = \int_{E'_n} Q_y^n(A) d\mu_n(y)\) for all \(A \in \mathcal{B}_n\), and moreover that

\(\int_{B'} \mathbb{1}_B(y) d\mu_n(y) = \mathbb{P}(g_n(X_n) \in B \cap B')\)
\(= \mathbb{P}(X_n \in g_n^{-1}(B), g_n(X_n) \in B')\)
\(= \int_{B'} Q_y^n(g_n^{-1}(B)) d\mu_n(y) (B.4)\)

for \(B, B' \in \mathcal{B}'_n\). Thus, for all \(B \in \mathcal{B}'_n\), we have \(Q_y^n(g_n^{-1}(B)) = \mathbb{1}_B(y)\) for \(\mu_n\)-almost every \(y \in E'_n\).

For each \(n \in \mathbb{N}\), we now define a new measure \(\pi'_n\) on \((\prod_{j=1}^n E_j, \otimes_{j=1}^n \mathcal{B}_j)\) by

\(\pi'_n(A) := \int_{\prod_{j=1}^n E'_j} \left( \int_{E_1} \cdots \int_{E_n} \mathbb{1}_A(x_1, ..., x_n) dQ_{y_n}^n(x_n)\)
\(\cdots dQ_{y_1}^1(x_1) \right) d\pi_n(y_1, ..., y_n) (B.5)\)

for \(A \in \otimes_{j=1}^n \mathcal{B}_j\). That this a well-defined probability measure follows from Remark B.5(III, IV) and the monotone convergence theorem. For each n, we claim that

+   (i) \(\pi'_{n+1}(A \times E_{n+1}) = \pi'_n(A)\) for every \(A \in \otimes_{j=1}^n \mathcal{B}_j\);
+   (ii) \(\mathbb{P}(X_n \in A_n) = \pi'_n(\prod_{j=1}^{n-1} E_j \times A_n)\) for every \(A_n \in \mathcal{B}_n\);
+   (iii) \(\pi_n = \pi'_n \circ (g_1, ..., g_n)^{-1}\), where \((g_1, ..., g_n) : (\prod_{j=1}^n E_j, \otimes_{j=1}^n \mathcal{B}_j) \to (\prod_{j=1}^n E'_j, \otimes_{j=1}^n \mathcal{B}'_j)\) denotes the measurable map \((x_1, ..., x_n) \mapsto (g_1(x_1), ..., g_n(x_n))\).
