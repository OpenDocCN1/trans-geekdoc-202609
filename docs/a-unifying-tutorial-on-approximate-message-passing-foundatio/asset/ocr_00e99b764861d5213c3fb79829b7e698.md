for \( z \in \mathbb{R} \). (The function \( \eta^{-1} M_\eta \) is called a *Moreau envelope* of M.) We note here that \( \text{prox}_{\eta M}(z) \) in (4.25) is the unique t that achieves this minimum for each \( z \in \mathbb{R} \), and also that \( M_\eta \) is convex and differentiable with \( S_\eta(z) := (M_\eta)'(z) = z - \text{prox}_{\eta M}(z) \) for all z; see for example Rockafellar (1997, Theorem 31.5) and Parikh and Boyd (2013, Section 3.2). Moreover, \( S_\eta \) is non-decreasing and 1-Lipschitz (cf. Parikh and Boyd, 2013, Sections 2.3 and 3.1).

For \( k \in \mathbb{N}_0 \), the functions \( \bar{g}_k, g_k, f_{k+1} \) in (4.23)-(4.24) are given by

\(\bar{g}_k(u, v) = v - \text{prox}_{\bar{b}_k M}(v - u) = u + S_{\bar{b}_k}(v - u),\)
\(g_k(u, v) = \frac{S_{\bar{b}_k}(v - u)}{\bar{b}_k}, \qquad f_{k+1}(w) = -\frac{w}{\bar{c}_k}.\)

Given \( \bar{b}_0 > 0 \), \( \hat{\beta}^0 \in \mathbb{R}^p \) and \( \hat{r}^0 := y - X\hat{\beta}^0 \), we now write the GAMP algorithm (4.29) in terms of \( \hat{r}^k = y - \theta^k \) and \( \hat{\beta}^{k+1} = f_{k+1}(\beta^{k+1}) = -\beta^{k+1}/\bar{c}_k \), and obtain the recursion

\(\hat{\beta}^{k+1} = \frac{\delta \bar{b}_{k+1}}{\bar{b}_k} X^\top S_{\bar{b}_k}(r^k) + \hat{\beta}^k,\)
\(\hat{r}^{k+1} = y - X\hat{\beta}^{k+1} + \frac{\bar{b}_{k+1}}{\bar{b}_k} S_{\bar{b}_k}(r^k) \qquad \text{for } k \in \mathbb{N}_0,\) (4.53)

where \( \bar{b}_{k+1} = -1/(\delta \bar{c}_k) \). Moreover, expressing the state evolution recursion (4.6)-(4.7) for (4.29) in terms of \( \tilde{\mu}_k := \delta \bar{b}_k \mu_k \), \( \tilde{\sigma}_k := \delta \bar{b}_k \sigma_k \) and \( \tau_k := \mathbb{E}((Z - Z_k)^2)^{1/2} \) with \( (Z, Z_k) \sim N(0, \Sigma_k) \), we have \( \tau_0 = \mathbb{E}((Z - Z_0)^2)^{1/2} \) and

\(\tilde{\mu}_{k+1} = \delta \mathbb{E}(S'_{\bar{b}_k}(\bar{\varepsilon} + \tau_k G_k)), \qquad \tilde{\sigma}_{k+1}^2 = \delta^2 \mathbb{E}(S_{\bar{b}_k}(\bar{\varepsilon} + \tau_k G_k)^2),\)
\(\bar{b}_{k+1} = -\frac{1}{\delta \bar{c}_k} = \frac{\bar{b}_k}{\delta \mathbb{E}(S'_{\bar{b}_k}(\bar{\varepsilon} + \tau_k G_k))},\)
\(\tau_{k+1}^2 = \frac{\mathbb{E}(\bar{\beta}^2)(\tilde{\mu}_{k+1} - 1)^2 + \tilde{\sigma}_{k+1}^2}{\delta}\) (4.54)

for \( k \in \mathbb{N}_0 \), where \( \bar{\varepsilon} \sim P_\Xi \) is independent of \( G_k \sim N(0, 1) \).
