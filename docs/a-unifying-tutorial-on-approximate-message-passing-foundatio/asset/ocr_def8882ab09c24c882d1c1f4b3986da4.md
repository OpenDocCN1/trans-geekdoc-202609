\(||W||_{2\to 2}:=\sup_{u\neq 0}||Wu||_2/||u||_2=O_c(1)\) for \(W\sim\text{GOE}(n)\) as \(n\to\infty\) (e.g. Anderson et al., 2010; Knowles and Yin, 2013); see Javanmard and Montanari (2013, Section 4.2.1), Berthier et al. (2020, Section 5.4) and Fan (2022, Appendix D) for further details.

These perturbation arguments do not generalize straightforwardly to \(d_r\) convergence results for \(r\neq 2\) since \(||W||_{r\to r}:=\sup_{u\neq 0}||Wu||_r/||u||_r\) is not \(O_c(1)\) or even \(O_p(1)\) for \(r\in[1,2)\cup(2,\infty]\). Indeed, given \(r\in[1,2)\) and independent \(Z\sim N_n(0,I_n)\) and \(\zeta\sim N(0,1/n)\), Lemma A.14 yields

\(||W||_{r\to r}\ge||We_1||_r\stackrel{d}{=}||n^{-1/2}Z+\zeta e_1||_r=n^{1/r-1/2}||Z||_{n,r}+o_p(1),\)

where \(||Z||_{n,r}\stackrel{\mathcal{L}}{=}E(|Z_1|^r)^{1/r}\in(0,\infty)\). Moreover, \(||W||_{r\to r}=||W||_{r'\to r'}\) whenever \(1/r+1/r'=1\).

**Remark A.7.** (A5) is a non-vacuous albeit very mild condition. For any Lipschitz \(f:\mathbb{R}^2\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\), the partial derivative \(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\) is bounded on its domain of definition, which is a Borel set of full Lebesgue measure. Nevertheless, there are examples of Lipschitz \(f:\mathbb{R}^2\rightarrow\mathbb{R}\) for which \(\frac{\partial f}{\partial x}\) cannot be extended to a function on \(\mathbb{R}^2\) that is continuous \((\lambda\otimes\pi)\)-almost everywhere (see Remark B.15). That said, it is inconceivable that such pathological choices of \(f_k\) would be made in any practical AMP procedure, where the functions \(f'_k\) usually have the property that \(\{x\in\mathbb{R}:(x,y)\in D_k\}\) is finite for every \(y\in\mathbb{R}\), and hence satisfy (A5).
