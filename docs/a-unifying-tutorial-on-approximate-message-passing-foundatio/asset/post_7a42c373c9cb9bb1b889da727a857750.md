
\( h^k \equiv h^k(n) \) is a deterministic Borel measurable function of \( m^0(n), \gamma(n) \) and \( W(n) \). Moreover for all \( x, x' \in \mathbb{R}^n \) and the corresponding empirical distributions \( \nu_n(x), \nu_n(x') \) of their components, we have

\( |d_r(\nu_n(x), \bar{\mu}^k) - d_r(\nu_n(x'), \bar{\mu}^k)| \le d_r(\nu_n(x), \nu_n(x')) \le (n^{-1} \sum_{i=1}^n |x_i - x'_i|^r)^{1/r} = \|x - x'\|_{n,r} \)

since \( d_r \) is a metric, so \( x \mapsto d_r(\nu_n(x), \bar{\mu}^k) \) is continuous on \( \mathbb{R}^n \).

Since \( \{(a_1, a_2, \dots) \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}} : \lim_{n \to \infty} a_n = 0\} \) is a Borel subset of \( \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{N}} \), we conclude that \( g : (u, v) \mapsto \mathbb{P}\{\lim_{n \to \infty} g_n(u(n), v(n), W(n)) = 0\} \) is a well-defined Borel measurable function on \( E \times E \) satisfying \( g(u, v) = 1 \) for all \( (u, v) \in E^* \).

Now suppose more generally that \( (\Upsilon, \Gamma) \) and \( (W(n) : n \in \mathbb{N}) \) are independent. If \( (\Upsilon, \Gamma) \in E^* \) almost surely, then for the corresponding sequence of AMP iterates \( (h^k(n) : n \in \mathbb{N}) \) from (2.1),

\( \mathbb{P}\left( \lim_{n\to\infty} d_r(\mu_n^k, \bar{\mu}^k) = 0 \right) = \mathbb{P}\left( \lim_{n\to\infty} g_n(m^0(n), \gamma(n), W(n)) = 0 \right) = \mathbb{E}\left\{ \mathbb{P}\left( \lim_{n\to\infty} g_n(m^0(n), \gamma(n), W(n)) = 0 \mid \Upsilon, \Gamma \right) \right\} = \mathbb{E}(g(\Upsilon, \Gamma)) \ge \mathbb{E}(g(\Upsilon, \Gamma) \mathbb{1}_{\{(\Upsilon, \Gamma) \in E^*\}}) = 1, \)

where the third equality follows from the independence assumption and Lemma B.7. Therefore, \( d_r(\mu_n^k, \bar{\mu}^k) \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0 \) as \( n \to \infty \), and moreover \( \tilde{d}_r(\mu_n^k, \bar{\mu}^k) \xrightarrow{a.s.} 0 \) by Corollary B.21(a).

**Lemma A.18.** For $n \in \mathbb{N}$ and $k \in \{0, 1, \dots, n-1\}$, let $\tilde{Z}^{k+1} \equiv \tilde{Z}^{k+1}(n)$ be as in Proposition A.11, so that $\tilde{Z}^{k+1} \sim N_n(0, I_n)$ is independent of $\mathcal{S}_k$.
