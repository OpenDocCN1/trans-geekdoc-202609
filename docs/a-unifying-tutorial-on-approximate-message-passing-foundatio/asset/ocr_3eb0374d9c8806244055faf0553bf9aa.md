# 2.1 Symmetric AMP

In this subsection, we present an abstract AMP recursion that was first studied by Bolthausen (2014) in a special case,^1 and subsequently by Bayati and Montanari (2011, Section 4) and Javanmard and Montanari (2013) in greater generality. Let $(f_k)_{k=0}^\infty$ be a sequence of Lipschitz functions $f_k: \mathbb{R}^2 \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$, and for $n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $W \equiv W(n) \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ be a symmetric matrix and $\gamma \equiv \gamma(n) \in \mathbb{R}^n$ be a vector of auxiliary information.
