
For $T_{n1}$, define $\Psi : \mathbb{R}^{k+1} \to \mathbb{R}$ by $\Psi(x_1, \dots, x_k, y) := \mathbb{E}\{\psi(x_1, \dots, x_k, \sum_{\ell=1}^k \bar{\alpha}_\ell^k x_\ell + \bar{\tau}_{k+1}Z, y)\}$ with $Z \sim N(0,1)$. For each integer $n > k$, since $\bar{Z}^{k+1} \equiv \bar{Z}^{k+1}(n) \sim N_n(0, I_n)$ is independent of $\mathcal{S}_k \equiv \mathcal{S}_k(n) = \sigma(\gamma, m^0, h^1, \dots, h^k)$, we deduce from (A.38) and Lemma B.7 that

\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}(\psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \tilde{h}_i^{k+1}, \gamma_i) | \mathcal{S}_k) \\
&= \mathbb{E}\{\psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \sum_{\ell=1}^k \bar{\alpha}_\ell^k h_i^\ell + \bar{\tau}_{k+1}\tilde{Z}_i^{k+1}, \gamma_i) | \mathcal{S}_k\} \\
&= \Psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \gamma_i)
\end{aligned}

almost surely, for every $1 \le i \le n$. Now since $\Psi \in \text{PL}_{k+1}(r)$ by Lemma B.23(b), it follows from the inductive hypothesis $\mathcal{H}_k(c)$ that

\begin{aligned}
\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \mathbb{E}(\psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \tilde{h}_i^{k+1}, \gamma_i) | \mathcal{S}_k) \\
= \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \Psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \gamma_i) \xrightarrow{C} \mathbb{E}(\Psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \bar{\gamma})), \quad (A.40)
\end{aligned}

where the equality above holds almost surely for each $n > k$. Taking $\dot{\zeta}_{k+1} \sim N(0,1)$ to be independent of $\bar{G}_{[k]} := (\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k)$ and $\bar{\gamma}$, we apply Lemma B.7 again to see that

\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}(\Psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \bar{\gamma})) \\
&= \mathbb{E}(\mathbb{E}\{\psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \sum_{\ell=1}^k \bar{\alpha}_\ell^k \bar{G}_\ell + \bar{\tau}_{k+1}\dot{\zeta}_{k+1}, \bar{\gamma}) | \bar{G}_{[k]}, \bar{\gamma}\}) \\
&= \mathbb{E}\{\psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \sum_{\ell=1}^k \bar{\alpha}_\ell^k \bar{G}_\ell + \bar{\tau}_{k+1}\dot{\zeta}_{k+1}, \bar{\gamma})\} \\
&= \mathbb{E}(\psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \bar{G}_{k+1}, \bar{\gamma})), \quad (A.41)
\end{aligned}

where the final equality follows from the definition of $\bar{G}_{k+1}$ in (A.16). To complete the proof that \(T_{n1} \xrightarrow{C} \mathbb{E}(\psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \bar{G}_{k+1}, \bar{\gamma}))\), we must therefore show that

\begin{aligned}
T'_{n1} &:= \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \{\psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \tilde{h}_i^{k+1}, \gamma_i) \\
&- \mathbb{E}(\psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, \tilde{h}_i^{k+1}, \gamma_i) | \mathcal{S}_k)\} \xrightarrow{C} 0. \quad (A.42)
\end{aligned}

To this end, let $L > 0$ be such that $\psi \in \text{PL}_{k+2}(R, L)$, and for each \(v \equiv (x_1, \dots, x_k, y) \in \mathbb{R}^{k+1}\), define \(\psi_v, \bar{\psi}_v : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}\) by \(\psi_v(z) := \psi(x_1, \dots, x_k, \sum_{\ell=1}^k \bar{\alpha}_\ell^k x_\ell + \bar{\tau}_{k+1}z, y)\) and \(\bar{\psi}_v(z) := \psi_v(z) - \mathbb{E}(\psi_v(Z))\), where \(Z \sim N(0, 1)\). Then by Lemma B.23(a), there exists \(K_k > 0\), depending only on the deterministic \(\bar{\alpha}^k \equiv (\bar{\alpha}^k_1, \dots, \bar{\alpha}^k_k)\), \(\bar{\tau}_{k+1}\) and \(r\), such that \(\psi_v \in \mathrm{PL}_1(r, K_k L_{\|v\|})\) with \(L_{\|v\|} = L(1 \vee \|v\|^{r-1})\). For a fixed integer \(n > k\) and \(v^{(1)}, \dots, v^{(n)} \in \mathbb{R}^{k+1}\), define \(\check{L} \equiv \check{L}(v^{(1)}, \dots, v^{(n)}) := (L_{|v^{(1)}|}, \dots, L_{|v^{(n)}|})\). Let \(r' = r/(r-1) \in (1, 2]\) be as in the proof of \(\mathcal{H}_1(c)\), so that \(1/r + 1/r' = 1\), and note that since \(\|\cdot\|_{p'} \le \|\cdot\|_p\) for \(1 \le p \le p' \le \infty\), we have

\(\frac{\|\check{L}\|_\infty}{n^{1/r'}} \le \frac{\|\check{L}\|_2}{n^{1/r'}} \le \frac{\|\check{L}\|_{r'}}{n^{1/r'}} = \|\check{L}\|_{n,r'} = \left( \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \left( L_{|v^{(i)}|} \right)^{r'} \right)^{1/r'} \le L \left( 1 + \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \|v^{(i)}\|^r \right)^{1/r'} . \text{(A.43)}\)

By Lemma B.12, it follows as in (A.27) that there exists a universal constant \(C > 0\) such that if \(Z_1, \dots, Z_n \stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim} N(0, 1)\), then

\(P(n, t, v^{(1)}, \dots, v^{(n)}) := \mathbb{P} \left( \left| \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \bar{\psi}_{v^{(i)}}(Z_i) \right| \ge t \right) \le \exp \left( 1 - \min \left\{ \left( \frac{nt}{(Cr)^r K_k \|\check{L}\|_2} \right)^2, \left( \frac{nt}{(Cr)^r K_k \|\check{L}\|_\infty} \right)^{2/r} \right\} \right) \le \exp \left( 1 - \min \left\{ \left( \frac{n^{1/r} t}{(Cr)^r K_k \|\check{L}\|_{n,r'}} \right)^2, \left( \frac{n^{1/r} t}{(Cr)^r K_k \|\check{L}\|_{n,r'}} \right)^{2/r} \right\} \right) = E_r(n, t, K_k \|\check{L}\|_{n,r'}) \equiv E_r \left( n, t, K_k \|\check{L}(v^{(1)}, \dots, v^{(n)})\|_{n,r'} \right) \text{(A.44)}\)

for every \(t \ge 0\). Next, define the \(\mathcal{S}_k\)-measurable vectors \(v_k^{(i)} := (h^1_i, \dots, h^k_i, \gamma_i)\) for \(1 \le i \le n\). Returning to (A.42) and recalling (A.38), we see that

\(\psi(h^1_i, \dots, h^k_i, \tilde{h}^{k+1}_i, \gamma_i) - \mathbb{E}(\psi(h^1_i, \dots, h^k_i, \tilde{h}^{k+1}_i, \gamma_i) \mid \mathcal{S}_k) = \psi_{v_k^{(i)}}(\tilde{Z}^{k+1}_i) - \mathbb{E}(\psi_{v_k^{(i)}}(\tilde{Z}^{k+1}_i) \mid \mathcal{S}_k) = \bar{\psi}_{v_k^{(i)}}(\tilde{Z}^{k+1}_i)\)

for all \(1 \le i \le n\), where the final equality follows from Lemma B.7 and the fact that \(\tilde{Z}^{k+1} \equiv \tilde{Z}^{k+1}(n)\) is independent of \(\mathcal{S}_k \equiv \mathcal{S}_k(n)\). We deduce from this and (A.44) that

\(\mathbb{P}(|T'_{n1}| > \varepsilon \mid \mathcal{S}_k) = \mathbb{P}\left(\left|\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \bar{\psi}_{v_k^{(i)}}(\tilde{Z}_i^{k+1})\right| \ge \varepsilon \mid \mathcal{S}_k\right)\)
\(= P(n, \varepsilon, v_k^{(1)}, \dots, v_k^{(n)})\)
\(\le E_r\left(n, \varepsilon, K_k \|\check{L}(v_k^{(1)}, \dots, v_k^{(n)})\|_{n, r'}\right) \quad \text{(A.45)}\)

for every \(n > k\) and \(\varepsilon > 0\), where the second equality is again obtained using Lemma B.7. Now by (A.43) and Hölder's inequality, which ensures that \(\|\cdot\| \equiv \|\cdot\|_2 \le (k+1)^{\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{r}} \|\cdot\|_r\) on \(\mathbb{R}^{k+1}\), we have

\(\check{L}_k(n) := \|\check{L}(v_k^{(1)}, \dots, v_k^{(n)})\|_{n, r'} \le L\left(1 + \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \|v_k^{(i)}\|^r\right)^{1/r'}\)
\(\lesssim_{k,r} L \left\{1 + \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \sum_{\ell=1}^k (|h_i^\ell|^r + |\gamma_i|^r)\right\}^{1/r'}\)
\(= L\left(1 + \sum_{\ell=1}^k (\|h^\ell\|_{n,r}^r + \|\gamma\|_{n,r}^r)\right)^{1/r'} \quad \text{(A.46)}\)

for each \(n > k\). Since \(\|h^\ell\|_{n,r} = O_c(1)\) for \(1 \le \ell \le k\) by \(\mathcal{H}_k(b)\) and \(\|\gamma\|_{n,r} = O_c(1)\) by (A1), this means that \(\check{L}_k(n) = O_c(1)\). Thus, by Proposition 1.2, there exists \(\bar{L}_k \in (0, \infty)\) such that for integers \(n > k\), the events \(A_k(n) := \{\check{L}_k(n) \le \bar{L}_k\} \in \mathcal{S}_k(n)\) satisfy \(\sum_{n=k+1}^\infty \mathbb{P}(A_k(n)^c) < \infty\). Moreover, for each \(n > k\) and \(\varepsilon > 0\), it follows from (A.45) that

\(\mathbb{P}(\{|T'_{n1}| > \varepsilon\} \cap A_k(n) \mid \mathcal{S}_k)\)
\(= \mathbb{P}(|T'_{n1}| > \varepsilon \mid \mathcal{S}_k) \mathbb{1}_{A_k(n)}\)
\(\le P(n, \varepsilon, v_k^{(1)}, \dots, v_k^{(n)}) \mathbb{1}_{A_k(n)}\)
\(\le E_r(n, \varepsilon, K_k \check{L}_k(n)) \mathbb{1}_{A_k(n)} \le E_r(n, \varepsilon, K_k \bar{L}_k),\)

where we have used the fact that \(A_k(n) \in \mathcal{S}_k(n)\) to obtain the first equality above. Recalling the expression for \(E_r(n, \varepsilon, K_k \bar{L}_k)\) in (A.44), we see that \(\sum_{n=k+1}^\infty E_r(n, \varepsilon, K_k \bar{L}_k) < \infty\), and hence conclude as in (A.29) that for every \(\varepsilon > 0\), we have

\(\sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty} \mathbb{P}(|T'_{n1}| > \varepsilon) \leq \sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty} \mathbb{P}(\{|T'_{n1}| > \varepsilon\} \cap A_k(n)) + \sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty} \mathbb{P}(A_k(n)^c)\)
\(\leq \sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty} E_r(n, \varepsilon, K_k \bar{L}_k) + \sum_{n=k+1}^{\infty} \mathbb{P}(A_k(n)^c) < \infty,\)

which implies (A.42) in view of Proposition 1.2. Together with (A.40) and (A.41), this shows that \(T_{n1} \xrightarrow{C} \mathbb{E}(\psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \bar{G}_{k+1}, \bar{\gamma}))\) in (A.39), as claimed.

The final step in the proof of \(\mathcal{H}_{k+1}(c)\) is to show that \(T_{n2} \xrightarrow{C} 0\) in (A.39). Letting \(L > 0\) be such that \(\psi \in \text{PL}_{k+2}(r, L)\), we can apply Lemma B.24 as in (A.30) to see that

\(|T_{n2}| \leq \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n |\psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, h_i^{k+1,k}, \gamma_i) - \psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, h_i^{k+1,k} - \Delta_i^{k+1}, \gamma_i)|\)
\(\leq \dot{L}(k+2)^{\frac{r}{2}-1} \|\Delta^{k+1}\|_{n,r} \left( 1 + 2 \sum_{\ell=1}^k \|h^\ell\|_{n,r}^{r-1} + 2 \|\gamma\|_{n,r}^{r-1} + \|h^{k+1,k}\|_{n,r}^{r-1} + \|h^{k+1,k} - \Delta^{k+1}\|_{n,r}^{r-1} \right)\)
\(\lesssim_{k,r} L \|\Delta^{k+1}\|_{n,r} \left( 1 + \sum_{\ell=1}^k \|h^\ell\|_{n,r}^{r-1} + \|h^{k+1,k}\|_{n,r}^{r-1} + \|\gamma\|_{n,r}^{r-1} + \|\Delta^{k+1}\|_{n,r}^{r-1} \right)\) (A.47)

for each integer \(n > k\). Now \(\|h^{k+1,k}\|_{n,r} \stackrel{d}{=} \|h^{k+1}\|_{n,r}\) for each such \(n\), and recall from \(\mathcal{H}_{k+1}(a)\) that \(\|\Delta^{k+1}\|_{n,r} = o_c(1)\) and from \(\mathcal{H}_{k+1}(b)\) that \(\|h^\ell\|_{n,r} = O_c(1)\) for \(1 \leq \ell \leq k+1\). Thus, \(T_{n2} \xrightarrow{C} 0\) by (A.47), and we conclude from (A.39) that \(n^{-1} \sum_{i=1}^n \psi(h_i^1, \dots, h_i^k, h_i^{k+1}, \gamma_i) \stackrel{d}{=} T_n = T_{n1} + T_{n2} \xrightarrow{C} \mathbb{E}(\psi(\bar{G}_1, \dots, \bar{G}_k, \bar{G}_{k+1}, \bar{\gamma}))\), as required.
