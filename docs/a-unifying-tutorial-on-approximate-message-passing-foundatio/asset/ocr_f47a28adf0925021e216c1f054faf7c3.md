In the proofs of Proposition A.16(a, c), we apply the concentration inequality below for sums of pseudo-Lipschitz functions of independent Gaussian random variables.

Lemma B.12. There exists a universal constant $C > 0$ such that the following holds for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $r \ge 2$ and $t \ge 0$: if $Z_1, \dots, Z_n \stackrel{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim} N(0, 1)$, $L \equiv (L_1, \dots, L_n) \in (0, \infty)^n$ and $f_i \in \mathrm{PL}_1(r, L_i)$ for $1 \le i \le n$, then

\(\mathbb{P}\left(\left|\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n \{f_i(Z_i) - \mathbb{E}(f_i(Z_i))\}\right| \ge t\right) \le \exp\left(1 - \min \left\{ \left(\frac{nt}{(Cr)^r \|L\|_2}\right)^2, \left(\frac{nt}{(Cr)^r \|L\|_\infty}\right)^{2/r} \right\}\right). \quad (\mathrm{B}.6)\)

Proof. We first consider the case $n = 1$. For arbitrary $r \ge 2$ and $L > 0$, we may assume without loss of generality that $f \equiv f_1 \in \mathrm{PL}(r, L)$ satisfies $f(0) = 0$, so that $|f(x)| = |f(x) - f(0)| \le L(|x| + |x|^r) \le 2L(|x| \vee |x|^r)$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$. Thus, if $Z \sim N(0, 1)$, then

\(\mathbb{P}(|f(Z)| \ge s) \le \mathbb{P}(|Z| \vee |Z|^r \ge s/(2L)) \le e^{-\frac{1}{2} \min \left\{ (\frac{s}{2L})^2, (\frac{s}{2L})^{2/r} \right\}} \quad (\mathrm{B}.7)\)

for all $s \ge 0$, and

\(\frac{\mathbb{E}(|f(Z)|)}{L} \le \mathbb{E}(|Z| + |Z|^r) = \left( \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}} + \frac{2^{r/2}}{\sqrt{\pi}} \Gamma\left(\frac{r+1}{2}\right) \right) =: v_r\)

by direct computation. Now $\Gamma(x) < e^{1/(12x)}(x/e)^x \sqrt{2\pi/x}$ for all $x > 0$ by a non-asymptotic version of Stirling’s formula; see for example Gordon (1994, Theorem 5) and Dümbgen et al. (2021, Lemma 10). Since $r \ge 2$, we have $(r+1)/e < r$ and $(\sqrt{2}-1)r^{r/2} \ge 2(\sqrt{2}-1) > 1/\sqrt{\pi}$. Therefore,

\(\frac{v_r}{2} \le \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}} + \left( \frac{r+1}{e} \right)^{r/2} e^{\frac{1}{6(r+1)} - \frac{1}{2}} \right) \le \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}} + r^{r/2} \right) < r^{r/2}. \quad (\mathrm{B}.8)\)
