**Bound on $B_t^H$.** As the mini-batched gradients $\{\tilde{\mathbf{g}}_t^{(i)}, i \in H\}$ are mutually independent. Hence similarly to 3.13 in the proof of Theorem 3.1, we have

$$
\mathbb{E}\left[\left\|\tilde{\mathbf{g}}_t^H - \nabla \mathcal{L}_H(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)\right\|^2\right] = \frac{1}{(n-f)^2} \sum_{i \in H} \mathbb{E}\left[\left\|\tilde{\mathbf{g}}_t^{(i)} - \nabla \mathcal{L}_i(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)\right\|^2\right]
$$

$$
\leq \frac{\sigma^2}{b(n-f)}.
$$

We can then follow the same steps as for analyzing $A_t^H$, to obtain

$$
\mathbb{E}\left[B_t^H\right] = \frac{1}{|H|} \sum_{i \in H} \mathbb{E}\left[B_t^{(i)}\right]
$$

$$
\leq \frac{\sigma^2}{b(n-f)} \frac{1}{(1-\beta^2)}, \text{ for all iteration } t \geq 2. \quad (6.13)
$$

**Bound on $C_t^H$.** Recall from (6.8) that, for all $i \in H$ and each iteration $t$,

$$
C_t^{(i)} = \left\|\sum_{k=1}^t \beta^{t-k} \left(\nabla \mathcal{L}_i(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t) - \nabla \mathcal{L}_H(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)\right)\right\|^2.
$$

Applying triangle and Jensen's inequalities (in that order), we obtain that

$$
C_t^{(i)} \leq \left(\sum_{k=1}^t \beta^{t-k}\right) \sum_{k=1}^t \beta^{t-k} \left\|\nabla \mathcal{L}_i(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t) - \nabla \mathcal{L}_H(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)\right\|^2.
$$

Therefore, taking the average on both sides over all $i \in H$, we obtain that

$$
C_t^H := \frac{1}{|H|} \sum_{i \in H} C_t^{(i)} \leq \left(\sum_{k=1}^t \beta^{t-k}\right) \frac{1}{|H|} \sum_{i \in H} \sum_{k=1}^t \beta^{t-k}
$$

$$
\times \left\|\nabla \mathcal{L}_i(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t) - \nabla \mathcal{L}_H(\boldsymbol{\theta}_t)\right\|^2.
$$
