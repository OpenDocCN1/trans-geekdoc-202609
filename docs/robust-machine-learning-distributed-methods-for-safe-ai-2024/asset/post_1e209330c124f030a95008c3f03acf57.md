
Therefore,

$$\|\mathbf{v}^* - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\| \le \frac{1}{n - 2q} \left( \sum_{i\in[n]} \|\mathbf{v}_i - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\| - \sum_{i\in[n]\setminus S} \|\mathbf{v}_i - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\| + \sum_{i\in S} \|\mathbf{v}_i - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\| \right)$$ $$\le \frac{2}{n - 2q} \sum_{i\in S} \|\mathbf{v}_i - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\| .$$

Upon squaring both sides and using Jensen’s inequality, we obtain that

$$\|\mathbf{v}^* - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\|^2 \le \frac{4(n - q)}{(n - 2q)^2} \sum_{i\in S} \|\mathbf{v}_i - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\|^2 = \frac{4(n - q)^2}{(n - 2q)^2} \cdot \frac{1}{n - q} \sum_{i\in S} \|\mathbf{v}_i - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\|^2$$ $$= 4 \left(1 + \frac{q}{n - 2q}\right)^2 \frac{1}{|S|} \sum_{i\in S} \|\mathbf{v}_i - \overline{\mathbf{v}}_S\|^2 .$$

This concludes the proof.

## C.5 Coordinate-Wise Median

For input vectors $\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_n$, their coordinate-wise median, denoted by CWMed, is defined to be a vector whose $k$-th coordinate, for all $k \in [d]$, is defined to be

$[\text{CWMed}(\mathbf{v}_1, \dots, \mathbf{v}_n)]_k := \text{Median}([\mathbf{v}_1]_k, \dots [\mathbf{v}_n]_k). \quad (\text{C.12})$

**Proposition C.4** Let $n \in \mathbb{N}^*$ and $q < \frac{n}{2}$. Coordinate-wise median is $(q, \kappa)$-robust with $\kappa = 4\left(1 + \frac{q}{n - 2q}\right)^2$.

**Proof** Given that the geometric median coincides for one-dimensional inputs with their median, it follows from Proposition C.3 that, for one-dimensional inputs, median is $(q, \kappa)$-robust with $\kappa = 4\left(1 + \frac{q}{n - 2q}\right)^2$. Since CWMed applies the median operation independently for each coordinate, the proof concludes upon invoking Lemma C.2.
