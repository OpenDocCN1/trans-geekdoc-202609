Therefore, the sequence $(\gamma_t)_{t\in[T]}$ also satisfies the respective condition of Lemma 3.1. Hence, upon substituting the above values of $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3$ in Lemma 3.1, we obtain that

$U_{T+1} \le (s_o - 1)^2 U_1 \frac{4e^{-\frac{\mu\gamma_o}{2}(T-2)}}{T^2} + \frac{4L\sigma^2\gamma_o(s_o - 1)^2}{2nb\mu T^2} + \frac{8L\sigma^2}{2nb\mu^2 T}$.

From the above, upon recalling that $4(s_o - 1)^2 U_1 = 4(s_o - 1)^2 (\mathcal{L}(\theta_1) - \mathcal{L}^*) \le \Delta_o$ and $\frac{L}{\mu} = K_\mathcal{L}$, and simplifying the fractions, we obtain that

$\mathbb{E}[\mathcal{L}(\theta_{T+1}) - \mathcal{L}^*] \le \Delta_0 \frac{e^{-\frac{\mu\gamma_o}{2}(T-2)}}{T^2} + \frac{2K_\mathcal{L}\gamma_o(s_o - 1)^2}{T^2} \frac{\sigma^2}{nb} + \frac{4K_\mathcal{L}}{\mu T} \frac{\sigma^2}{nb}$.

This concludes the proof. □

From Corollary 3.2, we can draw similar conclusions as in the non-convex case. The suboptimality error rate of DMGD is equivalent to that of centralized mini-batch GD with batch-size $nb$, but with increased computational efficiency. Specifically, after completing $T$ iterations of DMGD, the server produces a model $\theta_{T+1}$ such that

$\mathbb{E}[\mathcal{L}(\theta_{T+1}) - \mathcal{L}^*] \in O_T\left(K_\mathcal{L} \frac{\sigma^2}{nb} \frac{1}{T}\right)$.

# **Remark 3.3** While we could also use a simpler diminishing learning rate of $\gamma_t := \frac{1}{t}$, it yields a convergence rate that is in $O_T\left(\frac{\sigma^2 \log T}{nb\, T}\right)$, which is not tight in $T$. The scheduled learning rate, in Corollary 3.2, helps eliminate the logarithmic factor.
