In this chapter, we have introduced the method of distributed momentum for reducing the gradient complexity of Robust DMGD, thereby obtaining an order-optimal robustness at a reduced computational cost. As we had seen in Chap. 5 that although the composition of a robust averaging with the pre-aggregation scheme of NNM yields an order-optimal training error, the resulting gradient complexity can be orders of magnitude higher than a traditional (non-robust) machine learning algorithm.
