Lie, an IEEE fellow from the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Texas Tech University, United States. He briefly introduced how fifth-generation (5G) mobile network technologies promise to deliver 10 Gbps peak data rates for 5G eMBB (enhanced Mobile Broadband) applications, sub-1ms latency and ultra-reliability for 5G mMTC (ultra-reliable machine type communication), and massive network capacity with increased availability to enable IoE (Internet-of-Everything) with x100 more wireless connected devices compared to 4G for 5G mMTC (massive machine type communication).
