# 2.3. Hardware Development

In the system, a 12 V, 5 A, AC/DC power supply adapter was connected to the HW688 module to convert 12 to 5 V. Since the ring light and the ESP32 need 5 V, the HW688 supplies the right voltage needed. Components were connected to the ESP32 and the 16 × 2 I2C LCD to show the weight by utilizing the HX711 load amplifier (Figures 3–5).
