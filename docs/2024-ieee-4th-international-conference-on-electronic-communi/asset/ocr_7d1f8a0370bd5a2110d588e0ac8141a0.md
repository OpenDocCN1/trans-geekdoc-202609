# 5. Conclusions

Amid globalization and with the influence of foreign cultures, the coastal communities of Larantuka still hold onto their cultural characteristics. People are working at sea for a living. The culture of gotong royong has been implemented in the community. The implementation of mutual cooperation is a form of lifelong education for the residents. The education forms the character of an individual in the region. In terms of character-building education through personalized e-learning, two significant factors are considered: childhood memory, which attracts interest in learning, and methods of learning and materials online, with offline guidance through traditional teaching (Table 1).
