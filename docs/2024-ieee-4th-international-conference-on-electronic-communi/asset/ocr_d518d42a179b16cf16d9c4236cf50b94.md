Moreover, CPBL struggles to attract investment or create profits similar to sports big data technology and service companies including MLB Advance Media (MLBAM). In contrast, MLB teams are significantly concerned about baseball statistics due to the multimillion-dollar player market dynamics. However, apart from improving team performance and player athletic skills, there are no other compelling reasons to attract investment in the league.

Undeniably, the collection and analysis of sports data have already occupied a significant position in professional sports, with most discussions focusing on enhancing player performance. However, there has been scarce research on the impact of these data on audiences watching the game on screen. General viewers tend to concentrate their attention on the wins and losses of the teams rather than the statistics generated in professional sports. Only a few individuals with an interest in statistics seek to understand the meaning and implications of the data provided on the screen. Zheng and Chen pointed out that complex information poses a challenge to the audience’s information-processing ability [8]. Sports data can create barriers among audiences with different experiences, making it difficult for fans with varying levels of experience to enjoy watching sports games. Their research revealed that in situations with less experience, statistical information does not assist low-engagement fans in understanding baseball events. However, visualizing player information allows for low-engagement fans to derive more enjoyment and meaning while making it easier to comprehend the game. On the other hand, high-engagement fans, due to their extensive baseball knowledge and experience, do not significantly benefit from any type of information provided during broadcast experiences.

# 6. Conclusions

Big data have brought about significant changes in various facets of daily life, bringing substantial transformations in business, daily living, and government governance. In sports, numerous research teams in Taiwan are utilizing precise technological tools to collect and analyze athletes’ trajectories. Through the use of AI algorithms, these teams provide coaches and athletes with tailored and intelligent training suggestions, transitioning athlete training from conventional methods into an era of “intelligent training” through “scientific digital analysis”. The immediate, voluminous, diverse, and long-term data characteristics on the sports field are essential for data science research teams, particularly in baseball, where countless events unfold in every game. In the future, all actions on the baseball field are bound to be covered by cameras, radars, and calculation software. However, research related to the application of big data in sports broadcasting remains scarce. Audience members have needs for media, sparking motivation and media usage behavior [9]. For audiences watching games on screen, the question arises as to whether the substantial amount of information brought about by big data can increase their motivation or enhance their viewing pleasure. Television was previously a passive form of entertainment with the primary motive for most programs being entertainment, while information-based viewing motives were more prevalent in news programs. However, with modern technological advancements, traditional television viewing experiences are no longer adequate to meet audience needs. The transformation of television media is inevitable to comply with these trends. It is needed to discuss how baseball broadcasts integrate new technologies to pique audience interest without adding burdens to viewers.
