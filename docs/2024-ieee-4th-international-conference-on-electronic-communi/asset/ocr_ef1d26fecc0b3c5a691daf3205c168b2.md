For the next-generation 6G that utilizes Terahertz (THz) communication (e.g., using D-band), its hardware design specs are expected to be even more challenging, putting a tremendous amount of pressure on IC (integrated circuit) semiconductor device technologists, as well as on the RF/mm Wave hardware IC design and system design communities. The performance and cost of RF hardware will become even more challenging and differentiating as the industry evolves into the AI (artificial intelligence)-centric 6G technologies.
