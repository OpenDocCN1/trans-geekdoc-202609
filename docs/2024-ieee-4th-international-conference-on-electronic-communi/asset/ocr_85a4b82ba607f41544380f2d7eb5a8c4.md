# # About the Editors

# Teen-Hang Meen

Dr. Teen-Hang Meen was born in Tainan, Taiwan, on Aug. 1, 1967. He received his BS degree from the Department of Electrical Engineering, National Cheng Kung University (NCKU), Tainan, Taiwan, in 1989, and obtained his MS and PhD degrees from the Institute of Electrical Engineering, National Sun Yat-Sen University (NSYSU), Kaohsiung, Taiwan, in 1991 and 1994, respectively. He was the chairman of the Department of Electronic Engineering from 2005 to 2011 at National Formosa University, Yunlin, Taiwan. He received the excellent research award of National Formosa University in 2008 and 2014. Currently, he is a lifetime distinguished professor with the Department of Electronic Engineering, National Formosa University, Yunlin, Taiwan. In addition, he is the president of the International Institute of Knowledge Innovation and Invention (IIKII), and the chair of the IEEE Tainan Section Sensors Council. He has published more than 188 SCI and SCII papers in recent years.

# Shu-Han Liao

Dr. Shu-Han Liao was born in Taipei, Taiwan, Republic of China, on September 18, 1982. He received his M.S.C.E. degree from Feng Chia University in 2008 and a Ph.D. degree in electrical engineering from Tamkang University, Taipei, Taiwan, in 2013. From 2014 to 2022, he was a Senior Engineer with the Advanced Communication System Center at the Smart & Network System Institute at the Institute for Information Industry, Taipei. In 2022, he joined the Department of Electrical Engineering, Tamkang University, where he is now an Assistant Professor. Dr. Liao serves as a Chairperson for the Committee of eCloud Mobile, Chinese Professional Management Association of Hsinchu, Hsinchu. He also served as a Deputy Secretary-General for Asia IoT Alliance, Taiwan. Dr. Liao holds 20 patents and has published more than 50 SCI and EI papers in international journals and conference proceedings. His current research interests include the Internet of Things, next-generation (B5G/6G) mobile systems, ESG, smart healthcare, and the Metaverse.

# Cheng-Fu Yang

Dr. Cheng-Fu Yang was born in Taiwan on July 12, 1964. Yang gained his BS, Master’s degree, and PhD in 1986, 1988, and 1993 from the Department of Electrical Engineering of Cheng Kung University. Since February 2004, he has served as a Professor of Chemical and Materials Engineering at the National University of Kaohsiung. He received “The Outstanding Contribution Awards of the Chinese Ceramic Society” in 2009. In January 2010, he was the first person to obtain the status of Distinguished Professor at NUK and was the sole recipient of this honor. In August 2014, he was named a fellow of “The Institution of Engineering and Technology” (IET). In May 2015, he became the “The Mingjiang Scholar and Chair Inviting Professor” of Jimei University, Xiamen, Fujian, China, and he has been the “Honorary Chair Professor” of Chaoyang University of Technology since August 2020. He has served as an editorial board member and guest editor for more than 15 international journals, including *Coatings*, *Materials*, *Applied Sciences*, etc., and he is the current Chief Editor of *Applied Functional Materials*. So far, he has published more than 390 SCI and SSCI journal papers, more than 140 EI journal papers, and over 300 domestic and international conference papers.
