# Figure 1. Results of unsupervised DBSCAN algorithm.

The DBSCAN algorithm was used to effectively group the irregular clusters. Table 3 presents several noteworthy details for each group. In Figure 1, the red cluster (Cluster 1) represents teachers who have witnessed a 1–5% increase in their students’ academic performance. The largest group, identified as the blue cluster (Cluster 2), exhibits no notable increase in academic performance. Cluster 3 (green) pertains to teachers who have observed a performance increase between 6 and 10%, while Cluster 4 (purple) denotes an increase between 11 and 20%. Lastly, Cluster 5 (pink) comprises cases where the increase exceeded 20%.
