# 2.5. Reliability and Validity Analysis

Intercoder reliability was used to test reliability, and the jury method was employed to ensure research validity. Three coders were classified to code the data and compare the analysis results. The average agreement of the three evaluators was 0.82. The reliability was calculated as follows.

Reliability = (number of evaluators × average mutual agreement)/[1 + (number of evaluators − 1) × average mutual agreement] = (3×0.82)/(1 + 2×0.82) = 0.93  (4)

The reliability was 0.93, which met the standard threshold (0.85) recommended by Kassarjian [7]. In the jury method, one professor and three graduate students familiar with the Means–End chain and the Group Decision Support System were invited.
