# 5. Conclusions

The use of digital images in student essays positively impacted students’ learning abilities, where many students increased their ability to compose from zero to close to 10%. Students needed instructions in composing. Grammar plays an essential role in an essay. It is necessary to increase the number of students to generalize results.
