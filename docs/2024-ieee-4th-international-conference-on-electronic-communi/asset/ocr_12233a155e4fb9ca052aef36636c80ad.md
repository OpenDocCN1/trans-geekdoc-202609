be studied in order to meet the changing needs of elderly persons. The success of the group home model for the Yunlin Elderly Long-Term Care Association reveals the importance of continuously improving and customizing care environments to ensure the well-being of elderly persons with dementia in Taiwan.

However, further architectural design research is necessary to improve the well-being of elderly persons with dementia, including collaborations between caregivers and architectural experts in the design of dementia care facilities. There is an urgent need for standardized guidelines concerning the environmental design of dementia care facilities. These guidelines would be a valuable and essential reference for meeting the specific needs of elderly persons with dementia in Taiwan.
