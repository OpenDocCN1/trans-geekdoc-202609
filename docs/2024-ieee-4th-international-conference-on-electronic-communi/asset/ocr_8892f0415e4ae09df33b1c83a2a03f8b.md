# 4.2. Student’s Mistakes

The most frequent mistakes in writing words were entering incorrect letters in the same pinyin, such’s:

The word “小时 (xiǎoshí)” becomes “校师 (xiàoshī)”; the word “时候 (shíhou)” becomes “时后 (shíhòu)”; the word “自己 (zìjǐ)” becomes “自已 (zìsì)”; The word “五 (wǔ)” becomes “无 (wú)”; the word “知道 (zhīdào)” becomes “这道 (zhè dào)”.
