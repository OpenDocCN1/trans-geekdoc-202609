Mobile apps are created for users to use anywhere and any time. All participants agreed that HiNative apps can be used anywhere and any time. Regarding its use in learning outside the classroom, 89 agreed that the HiNative app adopts the MALL principle for learning Mandarin, integrating various language learning activities and prioritizing personal learning [26]. This encouraged students to access learning via devices other than smartphones. Their perceptions of portability showed that the HiNative app has the characteristics of mobile learning and portability and can be used anywhere at any time to support the Mandarin language learning process. The average score for portability was the highest (4.50), indicating ease of access, which was influenced by the ease of use and usability brought by the HiNative apps.

# 5. Conclusions

Student perceptions regarding the use of HiNative apps as an AI-based native speaker online discussion forum for learning Mandarin were examined. The participants responded positively to the use of this application in learning Mandarin. The HiNative app for learning Mandarin helped students improve their Mandarin language skills. They communicated with native speakers, which increased their motivation and interest in learning Mandarin.
