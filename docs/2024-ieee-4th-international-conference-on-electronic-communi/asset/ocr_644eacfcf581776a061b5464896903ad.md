Table 1. Respondents of this study.

Table 2 shows the rating scale and criteria for the system evaluation questionnaire. The evaluation questionnaire was created to assess the level of satisfaction with the system in terms of functional suitability, performance efficiency, compatibility, usability, reliability, security, maintainability, and portability.
