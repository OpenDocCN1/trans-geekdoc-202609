## METHOD DETAILS

### In vivo experiments

A total of 4 C5BL/6J mice (Jackson Laboratory) were used in the new experiments to measure dopaminergic activity. Two Dat-Cre mice created by crossing with C5BL/6J-GCAG-ITAG2/+ (B6.Cg-Igstn148-tm148-GCAG-ITAG2/j) (Jackson Laboratory) and one transgenic Dat-Cre:Ai148 (F6 background) were used for behavior and electrophysiology in the offspring experiments with C5BL/6J mice obtained from Jackson Laboratory, aged two months at the start of experiments. After the completion of all experiments, mice were euthanized, and post-mortem histology performed to confirm the location of electrophysiological recordings. Animals were housed on 12h/dark 12h light cycle and performed the tasks during the day. Ambient temperature was kept at 75 ± 5 F, and humidity was kept below 50%. Animals were house-grouped (2–5 animals)/cage until individually housed for training and testing. Procedures were performed in accordance with the National Institutes of Health Code for the Care and Use of Animals and approved by the Harvard Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC).

### Two-photon calcium imaging

Surgeries were performed with GRE lens injection under anesthetic conditions. Mice were anesthetized with isoflurane (1–2 at 0.5 L·min⁻¹) and local anesthetic (lidocaine (2%) (0.5s) 1 minute subcutaneously (s.c.) was applied at the injection site. Analgesia (buprenorphine for pre-operative treatment, 1 mg/kg; i.p.; ketoprofen for post-operative treatment, 5 mg; i.p.) was administered for 3 days after surgery. Custom-made head was placed on the well-cleaned and dried skull with adhesive cement (Cell Metabond) containing a small amount of charcoal. To ensure delivery of inducible GCaMPa in dopamine neurons, the injection was initiated into the VTA (0 mm, 3.0 mm - 0.3 AP, 0.5 mm, 4.6 mm from dura) using UnoVector Control, position map [24]; Jackson Laboratory (60660). A third mouse was used for transgenetic surgery using DAT-CRE with Ail14d (B6-SBL/J7751-GCaMP7i-I2H4); Jackson Laboratory (063039) for injection of GCaMPa.

## Cell Process

### Neuron NeuroSource

To collect fluorescence signals across the axons then we used a dual-binding imaging photometry system with two integrated CMOS cameras and three light-emitting LEDs (LEDs) (BFMC-LED410-420, LED-460-490, CAM-500-550, LED-555-570, CAM-580-680, Doric Optics). Sequential image acquisition was performed in the cameras so that camera 1 collected the fluorescence by GCaMP was excited by LED 1 (410-420 nm, isob wavelength) and 2 (460-490 nm, calcium-detection mode), while camera 2 collected fluorescence from tdTomato (excited by LED 555-570 nm). Each wavelength was acquired at 26 Hz in a 640x640 pixel.
