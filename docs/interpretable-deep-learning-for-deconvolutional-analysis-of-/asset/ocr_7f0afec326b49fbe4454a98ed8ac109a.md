The implants were secured with Cell Metabond adhesive cement (Parkell) and dental acrylic (Lang Dental).

Behavioral training and testing protocol: Mice were dewatered in their home cage for 1-2 days before the start of training, or two weeks after surgery. During water deprivation, each mouse was weighed and maintained at 85% of its body weight. Mice were habituated to the head-fixed setup by receiving water every 4 hours (6 days), for which association behavioral training was completed. All training and testing sessions were recorded using a high-resolution top-facing camera.

Waters (LHDA123111A, The Lee Company) were used with a custom-made head-fixed apparatus based on a motorized slider (10145) and water pump. Water was delivered in saline (osmol) via a manifold (LMF50052B and LHDA121111A). The Lee Company was used for order delivery. All components were constructed from a 3D-printed base. Waters were stored in saline (osmol) during immersion (SDA9-Aldrich-110), and 30 mL of each was filled using a pipette syringe (7 $\mu$L pore size, 623-1237, Ge Heocaler; Order-91). Water was delivered at 10 $\mu$L/min. Each session consisted of one of the following lists:

acetate, p-methylene, butyrate, (-,carve), (+)-limonene, (-, α)-lflavonone. One of these orders were associated with a delivery of reward such as with other; a reward was delivered randomly from a uniform distribution of the sizes 0.3, 0.5, 1.0, 2.5, 5.0, 6.0, and 10 mL. Variable-size rewards were taken from the reward session after the session was completed.

Image acquisition was performed using a custom-built head-fixed microscope. The microscope was equipped with a diode-pumped, mode-locked laser (Mai-Tai, Spectra). All imaging was done with the laser tuned to 920 nm. Acquisition was achieved using a galvanometer and an 8 kHz resonant mirror (confocal head, Thorlabs). Laser power was controlled by a CellScope (CoMP2-500). Imaging was performed at 40 fps using a 10x objective lens (with 0.45 NA, Nikon). Excitation light was reflected using a dichroic mirror (FF775-25) and a bandpass filter (FF465-50/25). Emission light was filtered using a bandpass filter (FF525-50/25) and detected using two-channel GCaMP-photomultiplier tubes (HT22P-A40, Hamamatsu), which was sampled using high impedance amplifiers (TIAS80, Microscope). Control and image acquisition were done using ScalingCam (4/Video) Technologies. With 512x512 pixels, the temporal and spatial resolution of imaging were achieved by high-speed grayscale acquisition to eliminate motion using a mechanical shutter (SC10).

Pre-data processing: Images were processed using MATLAB for image registration registration. Motion correction was performed manually in Fiji. Region-of-interest (ROI) selection was performed manually in Fiji using the mean and standard deviation of a subset of frames. as the movement of the head introduced projections. De-contaminations were performed using FSA around each ROI. The dopaminergic fluorescent signal was fitted with a 12-point Gaussian kernel with 0.875 ms drift correction. The baseline was corrected using a linear fit of the maximum running of the 120 s time window. $\Delta F/F_0$ was calculated as $\Delta F/F_0$ using a fit to the 6th running average of a 40 s window. This fluorescence was used for data processing and analysis in the network.

Multi-photon imaging

Fluorescence surgery: Implants were performed as described above for two-photon imaging. One Dat-CRE:Ail14d:tdTomato transgenic mice, which expresses GCaMP and tdTomato in dopamine neurons, were implanted with a custom-made head plate and optical fiber inserted. The implants were secured with Cell Metabond adhesive cement (Parkell) and dental acrylic (Lang Dental).

Behavioral testing protocol: After performing other tasks not reported in this manuscript, which is the set of separate injections, the mouse was put in the head-fixed setup with a stimulus by the experimenter at random times. The duration of each session was 30 minutes.

Fluorescence absence and presence: To collect fluorescence signals across the axons then we used a dual-binding imaging photometry system with two integrated CMOS cameras and three light-emitting LEDs (LEDs) (BFMC-LED410-420, LED-460-490, CAM-500-550, LED-555-570, CAM-580-680, Doric Optics).
