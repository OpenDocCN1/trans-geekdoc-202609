# ## METHOD DETAILS

# ### In vivo experiments

A total of 4 C5BL/6J mice (Jackson Laboratory) were used in the new experiments to measure dopaminergic activity. Two Dat-Cre mice created by crossing with C5BL/6J-GCAG-ITAG2/+ (B6.Cg-Igstn148-tm148-GCAG-ITAG2/j) (Jackson Laboratory) and one transgenic Dat-Cre:Ai148 (F6 background) were used for behavior and electrophysiology in the offspring experiments with C5BL/6J mice obtained from Jackson Laboratory, aged two months at the start of experiments. After the completion of all experiments, mice were euthanized, and post-mortem histology performed to confirm the location of electrophysiological recordings. Animals were housed on a 12h/dark 12h light cycle and performed the tasks during the day. Ambient temperature was kept at 75 ± 5 F, and humidity was kept below 50%. Animals were house-grouped (2–5 animals)/cage until individually housed for training and testing. Procedures were performed in accordance with the National Institutes of Health Code for the Care and Use of Animals and approved by the Harvard Institutional Animal Care and Use Committee (IACUC).
