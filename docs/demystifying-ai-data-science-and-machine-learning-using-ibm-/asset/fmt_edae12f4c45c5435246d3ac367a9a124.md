## Model Interpretation

Similar to Random Forest, Gradient Boosting is an ensemble of trees, meaning it consists of many decision trees combined in a boosting ensemble model. As a result, there is no single tree or specific hierarchy to explain, as the prediction process involves multiple trees working together. Instead, we will focus on the variable importance chart to identify the most significant predictors. This chart reveals which variables contribute the most to the model's predictions, helping us understand the factors driving the model's performance.
