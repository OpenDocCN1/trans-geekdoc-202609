Based on the results, CHAID 2, QUEST 1, and QUEST 2 underperform compared to the other models, with the lowest ACC. Among the remaining models, C&R Tree 2 and C5.0 2 outperform the others, having the highest ACC. We can narrow the comparison down to these two models, which show very close performance. C&R Tree 2 has slightly higher prediction ACC, with better ACC, lower MR, and improved sensitivity and F1 score. The specificity between the two models is almost identical. Moreover, C&R Tree 2 has a better Gini coefficient and ROC index than C5.0 2.

Interestingly, CHAID 1 exhibits the highest Gini coefficient and performs best on the ROC and Lift comparison charts, outperforming other models in these metrics. However, CHAID 1's prediction ACC and F1 score are lower than both C&R Tree 2 and C5.0 2. QUEST 1 has the poorest ROC and Lift performance, while C5.0 2 and QUEST 2 also show very poor Lift results.

Ultimately, C&R Tree 2 and CHAID 1 are the top contenders. While CHAID 1 excels in Gini coefficient, ROC, and Lift, C&R Tree 2 outperforms in prediction ACC and F1 score. For demonstration purposes, I have chosen C&R Tree 2 as the champion model due to its higher prediction ACC and F1 score. Additionally, C&R Tree 2 results in a smaller, more streamlined tree compared to CHAID 1, which tends to produce larger trees that are more prone to overfitting and more difficult to interpret. Given the close performance of these models, C&R Tree 2 is the better choice for demonstration.
