As explained in [Chapters 3](#chapter3.xhtml) and [6](#chapter6.xhtml), CSL is a widely used method that allows us to evaluate and select a model based on the costs associated with misclassification rather than simply focusing on the MR. The key assumption is that different types of misclassification may incur varying costs, and our goal is not to minimize the MR itself but to minimize the total costs of misclassification.

I will demonstrate how to apply CSL using the Airline Recommendation dataset. The target variable is a binary variable with two levels: `YES` (recommend the airline) and `NO` (not recommend the airline). The CSL process will consider these two levels in the decision-making. I will illustrate CSL in two different scenarios:

1.  **Objective**: Minimize the total costs of misclassification.
2.  **Objective**: Maximize the total profits of accurate classification.
