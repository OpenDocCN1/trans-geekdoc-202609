# Preface

The fields of machine learning and artificial intelligence (AI) continue to transform industries and redefine our interactions with technology. As we move deeper into the AI era, leveraging machine learning algorithms to uncover meaningful patterns from rapidly growing datasets becomes increasingly essential. Reflecting on my journey, I vividly recall my first encounter with advanced AI, Tesla's full self-driving feature, and, more recently, the groundbreaking ChatGPT and other generative AI programs. These technologies reveal the immense potential of AI, demonstrating its ability to learn and adapt autonomously. It's an exhilarating time to be involved in this field, as AI tools continue to push the boundaries of what we once thought possible. At the core of these advancements lie data science and machine learning, fundamental components of AI programs.

## Why This Book?

Many people perceive AI applications as “black boxes”—unexplainable, overly complex, and difficult to understand. As a result, they may feel hesitant or even lack the confidence to begin learning about AI and machine learning and how to apply them in their fields. This is especially true for those without strong programming skills or a background in mathematics and computer science. It's crucial to “open the black box” and demystify AI by breaking down the foundational concepts of machine learning.

Learners from diverse fields, such as business, marketing, finance, aviation, social sciences, and the humanities, can often feel left behind amidst the emphasis on training data scientists who are skilled in programming and algorithm development. However, as software technologies advance, many applications now allow users to build machine learning models and perform data mining without writing a single line of code. These applications offer user-friendly interfaces that enable non-programmers to harness the power of machine learning.

The success of my first book, which introduced core concepts and practical skills in data science and machine learning using SAS Enterprise Miner, inspired me to continue this journey. That book focused on providing a strong foundation for learners without a technical background by using a powerful, easy-to-use tool that requires no coding. Building on that foundation, this second book is designed for learners from non-technical fields and experienced researchers alike, focusing on SPSS Modeler, a widely used data mining software that also requires no programming skills.

This book follows a similar approach by offering a structured and accessible way to understand machine learning fundamentals while providing hands-on experience with SPSS Modeler, which is popular among both academic and industry professionals. My goal is to offer learners a clear path to mastering AI concepts and practical skills, regardless of their technical expertise.
