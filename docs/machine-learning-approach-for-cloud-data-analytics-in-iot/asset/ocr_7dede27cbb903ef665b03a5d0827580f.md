# ### 8.5 Emergent Smart Cities Communication Networks

In recent years, energy demand has grown rapidly, with electrical equipment volume and scale increasing steadily. Simultaneously, significant changes are happening in the liveliness subdivision, largely due to traditional to wind transformation, sustainable energy strategies, and more efficient renewable micro-generation [17–19].

Effective electricity network operation depends on the coordination of output and use, which is a major challenge to network control. The arrangement of the grid evolves from a hierarchical and central layout in which large manufacturing units are situated on the land of the grid to a more localized one-stop, local processing system. The result is a more robust and less expensive energy supply than intermittent renewable resources. Energy stocks have increased.
