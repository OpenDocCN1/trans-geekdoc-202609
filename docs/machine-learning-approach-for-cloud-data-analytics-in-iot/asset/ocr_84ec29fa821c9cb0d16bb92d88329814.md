# 14.4.4  Power Sector

The power sector is the most diversified in the world. In Asia, India has ranked fourth. There are conventional and non-conventional sources. Conventional sources are natural gas, oil, hydro coal, lignite, and nuclear power. Non-conventional sources are solar, domestic waste, wind, solar, and agricultural$^{13}.$
