# 10.1.3 Environmental Monitoring

status and trends of environmental conditions. This is vital to human
health security, environmental sustainability, and policy growth. In many
fields, data monitoring system was widely used, particularly in the situ-

measured continuously in their system. The results are shown via Blynk I
platform in an Android and iOS application. The system is composed of
two parts that are located indoor and outdoor. The author showed another
weather station at [4]. The hardware device is based Zigbee wireless tech-
ology and Arduino board. It monitors the meteorological data including
air temperature and barometric pressure.
