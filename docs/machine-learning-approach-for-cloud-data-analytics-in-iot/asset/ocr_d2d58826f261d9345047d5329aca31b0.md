# ### 18.3 Existing Related Work

As we are all aware of advancements in electronic health data, patient data protection regulations lead to the opening of new opportunities for the revolution of health data as well as easier for accessibility and shareability of patient health data. Besides this, serious issues of protection of sensitive health-related data can be solved using Blockchain technology that ensures data security and transactions and handles smooth integration with the healthcare organization. Many researchers have put forth their valuable research findings in the area of anthropomorphism, gamification, and Blockchain technology in the transitional healthcare system. We have presented related researchers work in the above areas in tabular format for exploring preliminary designs ideas quickly and efficiently.
