When a collection of Azure Policy definitions is grouped, it is called an initiative.
Both a definition and an initiative can be assigned to a scope, such as a Subscription or a management group, and it then becomes an Azure Policy assignment.

Azure management groups are the third cornerstone for supporting management at scale, and they provide a level of scope above subscriptions.
