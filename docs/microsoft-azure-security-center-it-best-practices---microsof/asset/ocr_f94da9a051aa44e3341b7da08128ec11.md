# Policy management

Security policy in Azure Security Center reflects directly on the security recommendations that will be triggered according to your Azure workloads. Because each organization has its own set of requirements, it is important to evaluate which policies should be enabled on your subscriptions and which policies are not applicable for your scenario.

In this chapter, you will learn how security policy in Security Center integrates with Azure Policy and how this integration can be used to better monitor workloads in Azure.

# Integration with Azure Policy

It's well-known in all areas of IT (enterprise, small business, and even start up) that policy-based management streamlines and increases the effectiveness of IT operations. This is especially true with security, where the combination of technologies and processes become a potent weapon. In fact many know that if the right policies are in place—and those policies are carried out assiduously—then even less optimal technology can effectively protect the organization.

With this in mind, Azure Policy was introduced. Azure Policy allows you to create, assign, and manage a variety of policy definitions. Security Center Policies are driven by overarching Azure Policies, which improves the overall reach and management of your Security Center Policies policies. Policy definitions can be compared with your current configuration, and any resources that do not meet the requirements of your policy are determined to be out of compliance. You can then focus on the out-of-compliance assets and bring them into compliance.

A policy assignment is a policy definition that has been assigned to take place within a specific scope. A scope might range from an Azure management group to a resource group. A management group enables you to manage access, policy, costs, and compliance across subscriptions. The term “scope” refers to all the resource groups, subscriptions, and management groups to which the policy definition is assigned. Policy assignments are inherited by all child resources.
