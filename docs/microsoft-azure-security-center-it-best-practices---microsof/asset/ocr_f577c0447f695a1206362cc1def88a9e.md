# Identity and access

Today, security monitoring goes beyond actively watching the security state of your workloads. Now, you must take a broader approach that also includes identity and access. When an adversary can compromise one user's credential, the attacker might leverage this legitimate account for a much larger attack campaign. Regardless of where your workload is located—in the cloud or on-premises—it becomes imperative to monitor your user' behaviors, access, and how their credentials are being used.

You can take advantage of Security Center to understand these patterns and monitor your identity posture. To access the identity dashboard, click Identity & Access under Resource Security Hygiene, in the left navigation pane. The Identity & Access blade appears, as shown in Figure 4-32.
