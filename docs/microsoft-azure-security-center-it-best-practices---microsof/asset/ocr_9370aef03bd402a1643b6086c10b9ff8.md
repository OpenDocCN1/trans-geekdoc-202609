# Scoping your deployment of ARM templates and Azure Policy

Every ARM template deployment and Azure Policy assignment is targeted at a scope, such as management groups, subscriptions, or resource groups. Because Security Center is a subscription-level resource, ARM templates and Azure Policy deployments and assignments must be targeted at the subscription level.
