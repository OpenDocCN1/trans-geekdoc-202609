The fuzzy set is characterized by different forms of membership functions like trapezoidal, linear, triangular singleton Gaussian or piecewise, etc (Nguyen HT, 1995). The research work utilizes trapezoidal MF which efficiently fuzzifies the uncertain, limited input parameters. Figure 8a, b, and c represent the trapezoidal MF for the linguistic variables temperature, soil moisture and humidity respectively. Furthermore, the fuzzy sets of three linguistic variables are formulated in the Equation (2) to (10).

```
T_L(X) = \begin{cases} 1, & X < 12 \\ \frac{(15 - X)}{0.5}, & 12 \le X \le 15 \\ 0, & X \ge 15 \end{cases}
```
