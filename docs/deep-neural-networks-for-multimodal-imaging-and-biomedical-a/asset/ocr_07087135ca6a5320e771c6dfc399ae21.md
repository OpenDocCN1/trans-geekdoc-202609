concave regions are filled with striped lines to obtain convex hull. The compactness of the shape is described in equation (4) and (5):

```
C = \frac{1}{N} \left[ \sum_{k=1}^{k-1} \sqrt{(x_{k+1} - x_k)^2 + (y_{k+1} - y_k)^2} \right] (4)
```

```
C = \frac{\sqrt{4\pi N}}{\sum_{k=0}^{k-1} \sqrt{(x_{k+1} - x_k)^2 + (y_{k+1} - y_k)^2}} (5)
```

Here N the pixel numbers belonging to the area, xk and yk are the pixel coordinates with boundary points xK = x0, yK = y0 a closed contour.

The value for an ideal circular shape is normalized to 1 and for irregular shapes its zero. Since the images are discrete there will be deviation of small circles from theoretical values. Compactness C is the metric to increase both eccentricity and irregularity. The outline for irregularity is quantified well with the coefficient variation V for rk is mentioned below in equation (6):

```
v = \frac{\sqrt{\sum_{k=0}^{k-1} r_k^2 - (1/k) \left( \sum_{k=0}^{k-1} r_k \right)^2}}{\sum_{k=0}^{k-1} r_k} (6)
```
