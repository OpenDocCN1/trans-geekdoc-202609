where, $\sigma_{(i,j,u)}$ represents the $u^{th}$ coefficient variance at $i^{th}$ directional sub-band of $j^{th}$ scale. $\sigma^2_{U_{(i,j)}}$ denotes the noise variance at direction $i$ and scale $j$.
