# DISEASE IDENTIFICATION AND DIAGNOSIS

The major role played by the machine learning is to detect the disease through the image processing techniques, which could be very hard through the normal diagnosis. This disease could be anything like genetic disorders or cancers that is very difficult to be diagnosed at a very initial stage (K. Zhang et al. 2016).

Integration of cognitive computing with genome-based tumor sequencing done at IBM Watson Genomics is the very best example through which fastest diagnosis could be done. Therapeutic treatments are done with the AI based bio pharma followed by Berg in oncology (P.W Lavori, 2000). Moreover PIvital’s PReDicT (Predicting Response to Depression Treatment) aims in promoting normal clinical conditions for the disease diagnosis and treatment.
