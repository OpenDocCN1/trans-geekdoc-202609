# CONCLUSION

Industry 4.0 is said to be an evolution that combines manufacturing process and digital technology with the consumer life. This is known as the ‘Fourth Industrial revolution’ and Europe is known to be the highly potential country for these future biomedical industries (reported by the World Economic Forum). The workplace will also be filled with the complex organizational and complex structure that relates the coordination, decision-making, quality control, sales, and distribution and support services. Additionally, workforce also impacts higher demands in terms of complexity, problem-solving, connectivity and abstraction.
