## Indication of Thoracic Abnormalities and Condition

Quick actions are required for the patients suffering from pneumonia and pneumothorax, both are said to be dangerous situations that may lead to death of the patient. AI algorithms also help in dealing with the primary target of certain diseases. If untreated, pneumonia inflames the air sacs in both the lungs with the infection caused by the filling of fluids, which can cause issues with breathing and also threatens life (Holzinger, 2016). The condition of the lungs could be diagnosed for pneumonia with radiology images and helps in differentiating the condition from bronchitis. If the patient is already suffering from cystic fibrosis or malignancies, it will cause difficulty for radiologists to detect the disease by reading the images. Therefore, conventional imaging methods tend to cause difficulties in analyzing pneumonia if the patient is already suffering from any sort of lung problems.

If a small amount of pneumonia extends below the diaphragm, then it will be necessary for the CT scan to be taken that could be difficult for normal imaging techniques to identify, which could be avoided with the use of AI tools and algorithms (Ashburner, 2007). For effective and quickest treatment of diseases, AI algorithms promote rapid access to the X-ray images for evidence of opacities with the effective diagnosis of pneumonia (Mazura et al. 2012).

Pneumothorax is a condition that occurs when air leaks into the space between the chest wall and the lungs. The disease is caused mainly by penetrating chest injury (blunt) or certain lung infections that pose a high risk to the patients, which can be identified with AI when the patients are suspected for the disease. This disease introduces air pockets between the chest and the lungs that could also lead to trauma or invasive interruption. This condition could be treated at an early stage but when left untreated could also lead to a dangerous situation. AI helps in prioritizing the severity and the type of pneumothoraces that might require immediate treatment (Viceconti, Henney, Morley-Fletcher, 2016). The patients could also be monitored with AI tools over time. The main advantage of AI in detecting pneumothoraces is that it helps in differentiating the size of the disease from the previous state. According to ACR DSI, "though several use cases are required for the Artificial Intelligence to be tested for its efficiency, it appears to be a confident tool in the field of medical imaging". Diseases that might be difficult for the human eye to visualize could be easy for AI tools in diagnosing and making decisions, leading to the betterment of treatment for injuries, diseases and certain other situations that may be cured when identified in early cases.

## Detection of Breast Cancer

Medical imaging systems could also be used to detect the colon and breast cancer so that it might be helpful for radiologists to diagnose the disease at an early stage. Micro-calcification is a term that refers to the tiny deposits of calcium salt in the tissues that could make the imaging methodologies difficult for radiologists to detect the tumor cells as malignant or benign. Sometimes the false negative results which meant that the disease is wrongly indicated or some missed malignancies would result in unnecessary invasive testing or worse diagnosis (Shin, Roth and Gao, 2011). According to ACR DSI, "Certain variation could occur due to the interpretation of micro-calcifications done by the radiologists during the diagnosis period". The most accurate method, which uses quantitative imaging features and identifies micro-calcifications by their level, is AI. Thus, AI could decrease the rate of unnecessary non-cancerous biopsies.
