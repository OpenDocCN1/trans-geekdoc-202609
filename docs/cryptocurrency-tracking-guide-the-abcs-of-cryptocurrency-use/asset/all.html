ADA: The Cardano Blockchain founded by Charles Hoskinson in 2015 a former cofounder of the Ethereum Blockchain is one of the largest Altcoin proof of stake protocols in the cryptoverse. Its native cryptocurrency is ADA named after a female mathematician Ada Lovelace from the 19th-century. She was also recognized as one of the pioneers of computer programming. One Ada equals one million Lovelace's.

ADDRESS: An address is very similar to having a home address or email address. Since the inception of the internet 1.0 we started using email addresses to send and receive informational data online but now we are being introduced to cryptoverse addresses to send and receive cryptocurrencies. These addresses consist of alphanumeric characters and they are very specific to each blockchain and its native coins or tokens.

The majority of blockchain addresses are public and can be seen on the blockchain explorer confirming the transactions going in and out of any address. An exception to these public addresses are the privacy-based blockchains like Grin and Monero. It is impossible for any human to read, memorize and process the long random strings of alphanumeric lines of a blockchain address but rest assured that a computer has no problems processing it.

Addresses are unique and anonymous even though they are public, the owners of the addresses' real identification is never revealed. Although this case is true for the majority of people dealing with cryptocurrency, the real identity of founders and cofounders of major token projects and exchanges are actually revealed. One example is the founder of Ethereum Vitalik Buterin where his public address is known by all who work on the Ethereum Blockchain network.

 

Airdrop: An airdrop is the term given to things like supplies or propaganda leaflets being dropped from the sky by a plane. The strategy is also used in the cryptoverse by a new cryptocurrency project for its native coins or tokens. It is electronically distributed to early adopters of the project to create visibility and buzz which in turn provide more demand by the mass. All you need to have is a compliant wallet which is capable of receiving the airdrop on the scheduled released time and date. You need to register for any airdrop.

Altcoin: Altcoin or Alternative coin is the name given to all other coins that came after Bitcoin. These coins are native to their own blockchain software code and they were soft fork or hard fork of Bitcoin in layman's terms they are copies of the original. Bitcoin Cash, Litecoin and Raven coin to name a few altcoins that were forked from the original bitcoin code. Other coins like Ethereum have their own code or blockchains which were created to solve some of the problems or issues that Bitcoin cannot solve.

One major improvement by the Ethereum Blockchain is the introduction of smart contracts and smart properties. Altcoins were born out of necessity to solve the problems other coins have like scalability to handle the need for speed to process a transaction and lessen the fees charged for such transactions. The popularity of meme culture, some coins were also produced just to poke fun against Bitcoin. One particular altcoin that gave birth to this meme culture is Dogecoin.

ASIC: The abbreviation or acronym stands for Application-Specific Integrated Circuits. These specific integrated circuits are designed on silicon chips to perform only one special task and that is to mine and validate certain codes. In the case of Bitcoin the asic processes SHA-256 with regards to proof of work protocols to mine and validate every transaction.

ASIC MINER: An asic miner is when all the specific integrated circuits are connected together with other hardware like power supply to make a special computer that does only one task and that is to mine for cryptocurrencies utilizing the proof of work protocols. These asic miners are connected to the internet and tend to make a lot of noise in comparison to a regular home computer when turned on. Because they run the proof of work protocols they tend to consume a tremendous amount of electricity energy.

Blockchain: A blockchain is the innovative technology of open software code introduced to the world by Satoshi Nakamoto that Bitcoin runs on. In layman’s terms without blockchain there is no Bitcoin, Altcoin, NFT’s and Defi. It is cryptographically secured by nodes aka computers working together to record all transactions in a digital ledger.

This digital ledger is copied and shared across the whole network of computers all throughout different parts of the world that anyone can verify. Even though it's open to verification the code is secured by cryptography and it is immutable. Once a block is completed or found through the process of computer mining they are chained together hence the word blockchain. Every blockchain technology is different based on its cryptographic code.

Block: A block is the software code that uses cryptography to create digital transactions which are copied and shared across a network of nodes and computers. These recorded transactions are held in a digital ledger which are immutable.

In the case of Bitcoin the blocks are formed by the computers and asics mining, utilizing the process called proof of work (P.O.W). Some altcoins follow the same method as bitcoin utilizing proof of work to mine for the blocks and get rewarded once the block is completed or found in its own blockchain native coin. These confirmed blocks are chained together hence the word blockchain.

Block Reward: It is as straightforward as it is said to be a reward for a block. Rewards received by the miners for finding a block. Finding a block is when a computer hashes or cryptographically produces a code that is unique to that particular file. Every code hashed will never be the same and it's immutable which makes the data tamper proof. Every block reward is coins native to their own blockchain.

Halving: This is the process by which mining rewards are cut in half. An example would be the miners getting rewarded hundred coins for finding a block and then the halving happens after four years now the miners will only receive fifty coins. This is written in the source code of the Blockchain and it is immutable. As the difficulty of finding a block rises the less and less the rewards are given to the miners. This is also one way to control the supply of cryptocurrency entering the cryptoverse.

Hard Fork: When the legacy protocol source code gives birth to a new one by way of implementing new changes to the code. It can be related to the results of a disagreement on how they can move the project forward within the DEVs and miners of any cryptocurrency. Some examples are Bitcoin Cash and Ravencoin; they are both hard fork of Bitcoin.

Ethereum Classic is the hard fork example of the Ethereum Blockchain. Without getting too in depth in the technical aspects of the subject it's important to know that a Hard Fork happens when new and permanent changes are made to the old blockchain source code which are incompatible to the original consensus rules.

I:

ICO: The abbreviation or acronym stands for Initial Coin Offering. The term is copied from the stock market for an Initial Public Offering IPO. In the cryptoverse it is when a new project is offering its coins or tokens to raise funds to help kick start capital for the company. The coins can be a representation of holding a stock or stake in the new project.

Immutable: In the cryptoverse this means once the code is deployed it cannot be changed. All transactions on the distributed ledger are verified, confirmed and secured on the blockchain and they are unchangeable. This is why blockchain technology proves to be the evolution in solving the trust code.

Impermanent Loss: This is a temporary permanent loss usually refers to Defi and Liquidity Pool providers. Take for example the Defi Uniswap protocol where anyone can exchange and swap coins or tokens. In order for this to happen there is AMM Liquidity Pools that makes these swaps and exchanges possible which in turn changes the ratios of each coin in the pools.

The changes can go up or go down causing the total amount of coins to change which also changes the total amount of your provided LP to change. If it goes down in comparison to the entry amount that means you have experienced impermanent loss if you withdraw your LP but if you kept it in the LP there is no impermanent loss.

 

J:

 

Jager: This is the smallest denomination given to the Binance BNB Coin which is very similar to the smallest denomination for Bitcoin which is called the Satoshi’s or Sats.

 

K:

KYC: This abbreviation or acronym stands for K now Y our C lient. It is the verification process used to identify if this is a real human and truthful owner of the crypto wallet that is sending or receiving the cryptocurrency. Mainly applies to most Central Exchanges which helps the investors from being scammed and also protects the exchanges from getting shut down for not following its host governments regulations.

In the cryptoverse it goes hand in hand with doxing the project founders and cofounders as a verification process to validate they are the true humans behind the project. This is a very important process for any business and also in the cryptoverse as it grows because of little to no regulations governing these new asset platforms. A great assurance for the investors and the cryptoverse as a whole.

 

L:

Light Node: A light node requires less memory to work and does not store the complete copy of the blockchain. Light Nodes can be run on devices like smartphones and are usually dependent on the full node to run.

Litecoin: Litecoin is an altcoin and fork of the original bitcoin code. Developed by Charlie Lee an MIT grad and former Google engineer in 2011. Litecoin has a maximum amount of 84 million coins in circulation and its mining block is considered to be faster than that of Bitcoin. Litecoin as it came into the cryptoverse was the silver standard coin in comparison to Bitcoin as the gold standard.

 

M:

Market Cap: A Market Cap or Market Capitalization or MCAP is the total number of circulating coin supply in the market multiplied by its valued price. This is a great way to compare which altcoin project has more exposure and value than others.

Meme Coins: These are altcoins that came about the Cryptoverse as a joke and was based on the Meme culture and movement. One of the most popular of the meme coins was Dogecoin which Elon Musk himself tweets about on social media. The majority of these meme coins will have a picture of a Shiba dog, cats or any sort of animal that summons a great following on social media.

Mempool: This acronym stands for Memory pool. This is where all the unconfirmed transactions are located and have not been added to the block by the miners. If the transactions remain unconfirmed in the mempool for a long time that means the miners did not accept it due to low fees setup.

Merkel Root: This is the hash of all the transactions within the block.

Message: This is the input of the computational process as in the hash function.

Message Digest: This is the output of the computational process as in the output of the hash function

Miners: These are the full nodes or individual computers that mine the blocks. These individual computers verify the transactions and are rewarded for their block verification which is then added to the blockchain.

Mining: It is the POW or Proof of Work mining process that utilizes the individual computing power through hashing which verifies transactions and adds blocks to the blockchain.

Mining Difficulty: This is the measurement of how difficult it is for any individual computer to mine a block within the Proof of Work system.

Minning Rig: This can be a regular gamers computer used to mine cryptocurrency. Mining rigs are specifically designed to hold more than one graphics card that are powerful enough to mine blocks for Bitcoins and all other altcoins that rely on Proof of Work consensus.

Mooning: This is the call by many cryptocurrency investors on social media platforms indicating a rapid price increase in a specific altcoin or meme coin project. It is also related to saying “ To the Moon” which means this coin will be flying straight to the moon.

Multisig: This is a combination of two words, Multi and Sig. Multi comes from the original word multiple meaning many and sig comes from the word signature. It means multiple signatures in the cryptoverse. This is used as a security measure which needs multiple signatures in order to access the funds within the wallet. It assures the community that the funds are safe from having only one person controlling all of it.

 

N:

NaaS: The abbreviation or acronym stands for Node as a Service.

NFT: The abbreviation or acronym stands for Non Fungible Token. These are very unique cryptographic tokens coded on the blockchain which are used to tokenize digital art work and many more real world tangible properties or assets. Due to its cryptographic uniqueness each NFT code is never identical to one another which is used to prove ownership for a digital asset on the blockchain.

Non Fungible Tokens is a game changer for the way the world does business in the field of selling, buying and holding assets like art work, real estate properties and anything tangible properties that have value. All of this real world tangible assets will be represented by a secured digital and immutable smart contract on the blockchain as an authentic digital certificate.

Node: Nodes can also be called a Master Node, Full Node or a Light Node. A node is a computer that is connected to the cryptoverse and helps secure the blockchain. This computer is connected to the internet with enough memory to support and run a complete copy of the blockchain software code.

The complete copy meaning it consists of the Block Header and Block transactions. They verify every transaction throughout the network by enforcing the consensus rules and also validate each block before they chain it together.

 

P:

P2P: This acronym stands for Peer to Peer or from one person to another person interconnected by sharing and communicating. This interconnect ability is done withing the decentralized architecture.

Panic Sell: When cryptocurrency investors sell their coins due to news of Fear, Uncertainty and Doubt aka FUD. The cryptoverse is full of FuDster also known as the FUD messenger. They maneuver to cause panic in order to manipulate the price of the coin which in turn creates panic sell of weak and uninformed hands.

Polygon: This is a layer 2 project that is connected to Ethereum's layer 1 blockchain PoW soon to be PoS consensus. Due to the congested and slowness of Ethereum’s system, Polygon moved to solve this issue and provide cheaper transaction fees as compared to the expensive fees charged by Ethereum. Its native token is called Matic. https://polygon.technology/

PoS: The acronym stands for P roof o f S take. It is a greener processing block method in comparison to PoW consensus. By utilizing the staking of participants' cryptocurrencies and rewarding them for staking in terms of transaction fee rewards based on selected validators or nodes.

PoW: The acronym stands for P roof o f W ork. The miners utilize individual computing power or via ASIC’s mining farms burning through a tremendous amount of electricity competing with each other in finding a block. Through this consensus proof of work has shown the world that it works and it is immutably safe and secure.

Private Key: This is the private part of the wallets key pair that proves you are the rightful owner of the cryptocurrencies. This is also used to allow you access to the blockchain in order to transfer or spend the cryptocurrency assets. You cannot allow anyone access to your private key or you will lose all your coins.

Public Key: This is the public part of the wallets key pair that is used to create an address to receive incoming cryptocurrencies. This is when you are able to provide an address for any incoming transactions if someone is either paying you or airdropping some coins.

Pump and Dump: When cryptocurrency investors pump a project up through manipulation of a great hype within specific altcoin projects which causes people to FOMOed in which in turn Moons the coins value and price. Making new investors feel like they have found the keys to their new LAMBO. Then they start selling and dumping their coins causing a massive panic sell amongst the investors causing a dump. The new and unsuspecting investors are left with bags of coins that are worthless.

 

R:

Radix DLT: This acronym stands for the Radix Digital Ledger Technology. Radix DLT is a PoS consensus protocol. Radix protocol is a layer one protocol like that of Ethereum, Cardano, Solana, Cosmos, Polkadot and XRP to name a few layer one PoS consensus protocol. Radix DLT claims to have solved the problems that have plagued the majority of layer one PoS protocols including Ethereum. Vitalik Buterin its founder mentioned it as the trilemma factor problems of Decentralization, Security and Scalability. https://www.radixdlt.com/

Ravencoin : This is an altcoin and a hard fork of Bitcoin. It continues to use Proof of Work consensus through its miners. The Ravencoin project does not allow the use of ASIC’s to mine its blocks unlike Bitcoin which means anyone with a decent computer can mine Ravencoin. https://ravencoin.org/

 

S:

Satoshi: This is the smallest unit of Bitcoin.

Soft Fork: A soft fork is the changes made to the original blockchain source code that are minor and are still compatible with each other. This is usually done to help improve certain functionalities to the blockchain and add new modifications without major changes to the blockchain structure. One example of such a soft fork is the Bitcoin Segwit upgrade.

Shiba Inu Token: One of the biggest market cap in Meme Coin projects that was introduced to kill Dogecoin. https://shibatoken.com/

Solidity: This is a programming language used by Ethereum to write smart contracts on its blockchain.

Stable Coins: Stable coins means exactly what it is: a coin that is stable in its value. These coins are pegged to the value of fiat money like the US dollar or other cryptocurrencies.

Staking: This is a way for proof of stake aka PoS consensus to reward all participants within a specific altcoin project that stake their cryptocurrencies to support the blockchain and help maintain its security. It is similar to rewarding miners on a PoW consensus system.

Strong Hands: Also known in the cryptoverse as Diamond hands are the cryptocurrency investors that are HODLers . They invest long term and do not worry about the volatility of the market especially when the FUDsters manipulate the price of the coin causing panic sellers to exit their position.

 

T:

Trader: A trader in the cryptoverse is someone that trades cryptocurrency.

Tx: The abbreviation or acronym stands for Transaction. When you transfer cryptocurrencies a transaction is formed and permanently recorded on the blockchain. These transactions are layered and recorded forming a block after it is verified and confirmed either through the process of proof of work or proof of stake.

Token: A token or a cryptocurrency-token does not have its own blockchain unless it is built and migrated to live on its own blockchain. Once it migrates to its own blockchain it becomes a native coin of such a blockchain then it is no longer a token but a coin. Confusing, yes I bet you are so let me give you an example. Bitcoin is a coin not a token because it is the native coin to its own blockchain.

Ethereum's coin is called Ether (Eth) and it is native to the Ethereum Blockchain but there are other projects built utilizing smart contracts on top of the Ethereum blockchain. This project uses what is called the ERC-20 token which means they are compatible or tied to the Ether coin. Examples of such tokens are Chainlink (Link), Basic attention token (BAT), USDT (Tether) and much more.

Turing Complete: It means that a computer system that is capable of solving complex algorithmic computational languages as long as it has time with powerful memory, the necessary instructions and available resources to do it.

 

U:

Unconfirmed Transaction: An unconfirmed transaction is any transaction that is waiting in line or mempool aka memory pool to be confirmed. Once the transaction is confirmed and validated by the nodes then it will be added to the blockchain.

This usually happens to some of the blockchains like the Ethereum network because of the expensive fees. So if you send a transaction like selling or buying a certain token you will need to fork up enough gas fees to get your transactions through or it will just sit in line for a while.

 

V:

Volatility: In the cryptoverse, volatility is the rapid movements and aggressive changes in the values and prices of all cryptocurrencies from the ATH to ATL and vice versa. Cryptocurrency is well known to day traders and Noops to be very volatile especially if you do not DYOR.

 

W:

WAGMI: This abbreviation or acronym stands for We Are Gonna Make It. Investors in the cryptoverse are always chasing that next coin or token that will make them rich instantaneously. In reality they get rekt and lose more money than they bargained for and will continue to use these words to defuse their Fear Uncertainty and Doubts about the crypto market. That is why it is very important to DYOR and learn before you dive in the cryptoverse.

Wallet: Just like a regular wallet you use to carry your id cards and fiat money, this is an electronic wallet. It is an electronic wallet that holds your public and private keys used to access your cryptocurrencies. The wallet can be a hot storage meaning you are connected to the internet and cold wallet meaning you are not connected to the internet. You can also have it written down on a piece of paper and store it somewhere safe.

Whales: This name given to an individual or a group of investors that holds a great amount of cryptocurrencies in Bitcoin or Altcoins. Any movement they make in the cryptocurrency markets creates a massive change to the coin's price. They are viewed as manipulators of the cryptocurrency market's volatility.

 

Y:

YOLO: The abbreviation or acronym stands for Y ou O nly L ive O nce. This is a catchphrase used by many in social media and it has also made its way into the cryptoverse. It can be used for good when it relates to seizing the day and enjoying every minute of the moment but it can also be bad if used to rationalize your emotional decisions with cryptocurrency investing.

Maybe that you YOLO in a new project just because your gut tells you so can bring you both fortune and misfortune but maybe good fortune then it's all worth it. Just don’t blame anyone else for your own decisions. That's why it's very important to DYOR before you FOMO and YOLO in any cryptocurrency.

 

Z:

Zero Confirmation Txn: This is the same as an unconfirmed transaction
