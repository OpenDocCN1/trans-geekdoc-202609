## 3.2. Model interpretation

After constructing the model, the SHAP method is used to interpret the ME-NN model. As depicted in Fig. 5, the top ten features in the ME-NN model, reaction time, temperature, catalyst amount, and CO2 pressure exhibit the highest influence on yield, characterized by significant mean absolute SHAP values across features. In this plot, red feature colors indicate smaller values. It can be observed that for reaction time, temperature, catalyst amount, and CO2 pressure, feature values generally correlate with larger SHAP values. This pattern indicates that longer time, higher temperatures, increased catalyst amount, and higher pressures contribute to yields. Reaction time allows sufficient molecules to interact with the catalyst and CO2. Temperature affects molecular energy and reaction rates and equilibrium. The amount of ionic liquid catalyst determines the availability of acid sites for sites.

Fig. 5 demonstrates that the SHAP value is positive only when the time is less than 6 hours, suggesting that reaction time of at least 6 hours is required for complete reaction in the CO2 reaction. However, as depicted in Fig. 5, most reactions with high values are at short time. If researchers need to shorten the reaction time, increasing temperature and pressure also elevates the costs of industrial application. Therefore, there is a need to design II catalysts to ensure efficient catalysis, as much as possible to achieve that the reaction time, temperature, CO2 pressure, and catalyst amount are at low standards.
