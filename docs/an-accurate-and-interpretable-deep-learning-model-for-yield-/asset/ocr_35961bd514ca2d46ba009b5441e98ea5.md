# An accurate and interpretable deep learning
model for yield prediction using hybrid molecular
representations†

Yingwang Wang,abc Xiny Sun,abc Yuyuan Li,abc *abc
Li Wang *abc and Jing Zhang abc

In recent years, imidazolium-based ionic liquids (ILS) and pyrazolium-based ILs have shown efficient
catalytic activity in CO2 cycloaddition reactions. However, these catalysts require stringent conditions
the reactions in the absence of co-solvents, limiting their applicability. There is an\increasing demand for new ILs capable of operating under milder conditions.
Traditional methods for designing ILs through theoretical calculations or experimental
exploration are both costly and challenging. This study presents a deep learning model for predicting yield
of CO2 cycloaddition by imidazolium-based and pyrazolium-based ILs. The Model
utilizes hybrid features to capture the structural information of molecules achieved in squared
correlation coefficient (R²) value of 0.98. The Shapley Additive explanations (SHAP) technique
generation mechanism is
established to create new structures. Through a two-step strategy yield prediction
using the deep learning model and energy barriers via functional theory (DFT), 4
promising imidazolium-based ILs are identified as potential catalysts for CO2 cycloaddition
reactions with epichlorohydrin under mild conditions. This work introduces a novel learning
approach for designing imidazolium-based ILs and pyrazolium-based ILs, aimed at reducing
experimental burden and costs associated with catalyst development.
