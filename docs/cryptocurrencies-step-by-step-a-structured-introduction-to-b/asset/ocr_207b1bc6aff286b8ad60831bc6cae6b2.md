# Privacy Coins and Their Role in Financial Anonymity

Privacy coins are a class of cryptocurrencies designed to offer enhanced anonymity and confidentiality in financial transactions. Unlike Bitcoin and Ethereum, where transaction details are recorded on public ledgers and can be traced with sufficient effort, privacy coins use advanced cryptographic techniques to obscure the identities of senders, recipients, and transaction amounts. These features cater to users who prioritize financial privacy in an increasingly monitored digital landscape.

**Monero (XMR)** is one of the most widely known privacy coins. Launched in 2014, Monero employs several privacy-enhancing technologies, such as **ring signatures**, **stealth addresses**, and **ring confidential transactions (RingCT)**. Ring signatures combine a user's transaction with multiple decoy transactions, making it nearly impossible to identify the actual sender. Stealth addresses generate one-time addresses for each transaction, ensuring that the recipient's wallet address remains hidden. RingCT further obscures transaction amounts, adding an additional layer of privacy.
