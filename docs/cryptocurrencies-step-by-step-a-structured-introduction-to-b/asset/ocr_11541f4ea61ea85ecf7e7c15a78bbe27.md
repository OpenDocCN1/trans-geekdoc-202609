The rise of **modular blockchain architectures** will also contribute to sustainability. By enabling different blockchains to share resources, modular systems reduce duplication and energy waste. Networks like Polkadot and Cosmos are at the forefront of this approach, offering frameworks for interconnected and energy-efficient ecosystems.

As public awareness grows, regulatory pressures and consumer demand will push blockchain projects to adopt greener practices. Governments may incentivize sustainable blockchain solutions through tax benefits or grants for energy-efficient projects. At the same time, users and investors are increasingly favoring projects that prioritize environmental responsibility, creating a market-driven push toward sustainability.
