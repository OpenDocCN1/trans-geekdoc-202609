Institutional investors also entered the crypto market in unprecedented numbers. Companies like **Tesla**, **MicroStrategy**, and **Square** added Bitcoin to their balance sheets. The first U.S. Bitcoin futures ETF, **ProShares Bitcoin Strategy ETF**, debuted on the New York Stock Exchange, signaling growing acceptance of cryptocurrencies in traditional finance.

# 2022: Ethereum's "The Merge"

In September 2022, Ethereum transitioned from Proof of Work (PoW) to **Proof of Stake (PoS)** in an upgrade known as **The Merge**. This shift reduced Ethereum's energy consumption by over 99% and set a precedent for environmentally friendly blockchain solutions. The event marked a significant milestone in Ethereum's evolution and its leadership in the crypto ecosystem.

# 2023: Regulatory Shifts and Global CBDC Developments

Governments worldwide accelerated their efforts to regulate cryptocurrencies. The European Union introduced its **Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA)** framework, creating a unified regulatory standard across member states. The U.S. continued to grapple with classifying tokens as securities or commodities, while countries like China and India advanced their central bank digital currency (CBDC) initiatives. China's **Digital Yuan** expanded its pilot program, aiming to revolutionize domestic payments.

# 2024: Advancing Layer 2 Solutions

Layer 2 technologies like **Arbitrum**, **Optimism**, and **Polygon** matured, enabling faster and cheaper transactions on Ethereum. These solutions addressed scalability challenges, paving the way for broader adoption of decentralized applications (dApps) and DeFi platforms. Cross-chain interoperability projects also gained momentum, connecting previously siloed blockchain ecosystems.

# Glossary of Essential Cryptocurrency Terms

+   - **51% Attack:** A scenario where a malicious entity gains control of more than half of a blockchain's computational power, enabling fraudulent activity like double-spending.
+   - **Address:** A unique identifier used to send and receive cryptocurrencies on a blockchain.
+   - **Airdrop:** The distribution of free cryptocurrency tokens to users, often to promote a project.
