+   - **Altcoin**: Any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin.
+   - **AML (Anti-Money Laundering)**: Regulations designed to prevent illicit financial activities using cryptocurrencies.
+   - **Arbitrage**: Profiting from price differences of the same cryptocurrency across different exchanges.
+   - **ASIC (Application-Specific Integrated Circuit)**: Specialized hardware designed for efficient cryptocurrency mining.
+   - **Atomic Swap**: A smart contract that allows two parties to exchange cryptocurrencies without intermediaries.
+   - **Bear Market**: A prolonged period of declining cryptocurrency prices.
+   - **Bitcoin (BTC)**: The first and most well-known cryptocurrency, created by Satoshi Nakamoto.
+   - **Block**: A unit of data containing transaction information, added to a blockchain.
+   - **Block Explorer**: A tool used to view and analyze blockchain transactions and data.
+   - **Block Reward**: Cryptocurrency awarded to miners for successfully validating a block of transactions.
+   - **Blockchain**: A decentralized, immutable ledger that records transactions in chronological order.
+   - **Burning**: The process of permanently removing cryptocurrency from circulation to reduce supply.
+   - **Bull Market**: A sustained period of rising cryptocurrency prices.
+   - **Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC)**: A digital version of fiat currency issued by a central bank.
+   - **Cold Wallet**: An offline wallet used to securely store cryptocurrencies.
+   - **Consensus Mechanism**: The method used to achieve agreement on the blockchain, such as Proof of Work or Proof of Stake.
+   - **Crypto Wallet**: A tool for storing private keys and accessing cryptocurrencies.
+   - **Cryptography**: The practice of secure communication, foundational to blockchain and cryptocurrency.
+   - **DAO (Decentralized Autonomous Organization)**: A blockchain-based organization governed by smart contracts and token holders.
+   - **dApp (Decentralized Application)**: An application that runs on a blockchain rather than centralized servers.
+   - **Decentralization**: The absence of a central authority, with control distributed across a network.
+   - **DeFi (Decentralized Finance)**: Financial systems built on blockchain technology without intermediaries.
+   - **Difficulty**: A measure of how hard it is to mine a block in a blockchain network.
+   - **Double Spending**: Fraudulent activity where the same cryptocurrency is spent twice.
+   - **Dumping**: Selling off large amounts of cryptocurrency, causing its price to drop.
+   - **DYOR (Do Your Own Research)**: A term encouraging individuals to independently research crypto projects before investing.
+   - **ERC-20**: A standard for tokens built on the Ethereum blockchain.
+   - **Ethereum (ETH)**: A blockchain platform known for its smart contract capabilities.
