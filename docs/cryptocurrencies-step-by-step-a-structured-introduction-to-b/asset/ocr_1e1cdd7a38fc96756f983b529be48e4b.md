The rise of central bank digital currencies is accelerating the integration of cryptocurrencies into banking systems. While CBDCs are distinct from decentralized cryptocurrencies, their development encourages banks to upgrade their digital infrastructure. Banks are preparing for a future where CBDCs coexist with cryptocurrencies, offering customers access to both types of digital assets through unified platforms.

Despite progress, challenges remain. The volatility of cryptocurrencies raises concerns about risk management, particularly for banks managing large-scale crypto operations. Additionally, ensuring interoperability between blockchain networks and traditional banking systems requires significant technological investment. However, as demand for crypto services grows, banks are increasingly incentivized to overcome these hurdles.
