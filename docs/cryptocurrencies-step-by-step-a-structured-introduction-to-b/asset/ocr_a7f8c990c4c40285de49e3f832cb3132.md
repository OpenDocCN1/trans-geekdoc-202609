+   - **Exchange:** A platform where users can buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies.
+   - **Fiat Currency:** Government-issued currency like USD, EUR, or JPY.
+   - **Fork:** A split in a blockchain resulting in two separate chains.
+   - **FOMO (Fear of Missing Out):** A feeling of urgency to buy cryptocurrency due to rising prices.
+   - **FUD (Fear, Uncertainty, and Doubt):** Negative sentiment spread to influence markets.
+   - **Gas:** Fees paid to execute transactions on the Ethereum blockchain.
+   - **Halving:** A scheduled event that reduces the reward for mining cryptocurrency, typically in Bitcoin.
+   - **Hard Fork:** A blockchain split requiring all participants to upgrade to a new protocol version.
+   - **Hash:** A cryptographic function used to secure blockchain transactions.
+   - **Hash Rate:** The computational power used in mining cryptocurrencies.
+   - **HODL:** A slang term for holding cryptocurrency rather than selling.
+   - **ICO (Initial Coin Offering):** A fundraising method where new cryptocurrencies are sold to investors.
+   - **Immutable:** A characteristic of blockchains that prevents data from being altered once recorded.
+   - **Interoperability:** The ability of different blockchains to communicate and share data.
+   - **KYC (Know Your Customer):** A process requiring users to verify their identity.
+   - **Ledger:** A record-keeping system, such as a blockchain.
+   - **Lightning Network:** A Layer 2 scaling solution for Bitcoin that enables faster and cheaper transactions.
+   - **Liquidity:** The ease of converting a cryptocurrency to cash or other assets.
+   - **Market Cap:** The total value of a cryptocurrency, calculated by multiplying price by circulating supply.
+   - **Mempool:** A temporary storage area for unconfirmed transactions waiting to be added to the blockchain.
+   - **Merkle Tree:** A data structure used to efficiently and securely verify blockchain transactions.
+   - **Metaverse:** A virtual world integrating blockchain, cryptocurrencies, and NFTs.
+   - **Mining:** The process of validating transactions and creating new cryptocurrency.
+   - **Mining Pool:** A group of miners combining computational resources to share rewards.
+   - **NFT (Non-Fungible Token):** A unique digital asset verified on a blockchain.
+   - **Node:** A device that maintains a copy of a blockchain and validates transactions.
+   - **Nonce:** A value used in mining to create a valid block hash.
+   - **Oracles:** Services that provide real-world data to smart contracts on a blockchain.
+   - **Paper Wallet:** A physical copy of private and public keys for offline storage.
+   - **Private Key:** A secret code granting access to a cryptocurrency wallet.
+   - **Proof of Stake (PoS):** A consensus mechanism requiring validators to stake cryptocurrency.
+   - **Proof of Work (PoW):** A consensus mechanism requiring computational effort to validate transactions.
+   - **Protocol:** A set of rules governing blockchain operation.
