
Mean Square Error (MSE) = \(\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (Q_i - T_i)^2\) (5)

The final hybrid regression equation of the proposed model is thus given as the weighted summation of the cross entropy and MSE in Eq. (6):

Segmented\(_{Loss}\) = W * (WCE + MSE) (6)

where W magnify the loss on fetus head boundary during training with emphases on edges given by the Eq. (7):

W(x) = 1 + w0.exp \(\frac{D_x}{2 * \sigma^2}\) (7)

where, Dx gives the distance between the pixel x and ground truth (Target), \(\sigma\) is Gaussian kernel variance and w0 is a constant.

## 3.4. Ellipse fitting and dimension assessment

The last phase of the proposed methodology is the post-processing of the segmented output map, called ellipse fitting. As the shape of the fetus is in ellipse and also the available ground truth is in form of the ellipse, the fitting of the ellipse is an important part of the methodology. The fitment of the ellipse on the ultrasound image can help to make the assessment of the parameters easily and predicts the growth of the fetus.

An ellipse is defined as the locus of all those points in a two-dimensional plane where the distance addition from two fixed points, also known as foci, in the plane remains constant. This constant ratio is called the eccentricity of an ellipse which demonstrates its elongation. The description of the ellipse is shown in Fig. 8.

It is to be noted that the shape of the ellipse is in form of an oval, where the longest bisection in middle along the x-axis is called the major axis and the smallest bisection along the y-axis is called the minor axis. The distance between the center (intersection of major and minor axis) to surface points is called the semi-major axis and semi-minor axis. The area enclosed by the ellipse is given by the Eq. (8):

Area under ellipse = \(\pi * |\frac{M}{2}| * |\frac{m}{2}|\) (8)

## 4.3. Metrics to evaluate performance

In the proposed work, the Dice coefficient score, Difference in Head Circumference, Hausdorff Distance, and mean absolute percent error are used as a metric for performance evaluation.

The mathematical notation of these metrics is given in Eq. 10-13:

- 1. Dice Coefficient (DC): is defined as the ratio assessment on the number of pixels using segmentation output and the ground truth and is given as:

\(\text{Dice Coefficient (DC)} = \frac{2 * (Seg_{Area} \cap Grt_{Area})}{|Seg_{Area}| + |Grt_{Area}|}\) (10)

where \(Grt_{Area}\) is the ground truth area and \(Seg_{Area}\) is the area extracted from the segmentation part.

- 2. Difference Head Circumference (DHC): is defined as the difference in the fetus head perimeter assessment of the segmented region and the ground truth and is given as:

Difference Head Circumference (DHC) = \(P_{Seg} - P_{Grt}\) (11)

where, \(P_i\) represent the perimeter and \(i \in\) (segmented region, ground truth)

- 3. Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE): is defined as the absolute percentage error with respect to the available ground truth and is given as:

\(MAPE = \frac{MAE}{Grt} . 100\) (12)

where MAE is the mean absolute error and is given by

\(MAE = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (S_i - G_i)\)

here, \(S_i\) is the pixel probability in Segmented output and \(G_i\) is the actual pixel probability in the available ground truth.

- 4. Hausdorff Distance (HD): is given as the distance between two contour points and found maximum among all such combinations. It is given as:

\(HD(Seg, Grt) = max (h(Seg, Grt), h(Grt, Seg))\) (13)

where,
\(h(Seg, Grt) = max_{s \in Seg} min_{g \in Grt} \|p - g\|\)
\(h(Grt, Seg) = max_{g \in Grt} min_{s \in Seg} \|g - p\|\)

where, Seg represents the pixels in the segmented space (\(s_1, s_2, ...., s_n\)) and Grt represents the pixels in the ground truth (\(g_1, g_2, ...., g_n\)).
