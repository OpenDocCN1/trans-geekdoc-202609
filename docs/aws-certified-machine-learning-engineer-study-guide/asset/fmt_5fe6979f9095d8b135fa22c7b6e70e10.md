### Conventions Used in This Book

This study guide uses certain typographic styles in order to help you quickly identify important information and to avoid confusion over the meaning of words such as on-screen prompts.

In particular, look for the following styles:

*   *Italicized text* indicates key terms that are described at length for the first time in a chapter. These words are likely to appear in the searchable online glossary. (Italics are also used for emphasis.)
*   A `monospaced font` indicates the contents of a program or configuration files, messages displayed as a text-mode macOS/Linux shell prompt, filenames, text-mode command names, and Internet URLs.
