# **Exploration of Uniqueness** can be achieved via selecting and exploring the OOM data with high uniqueness. To identify these data, we can leverage the agreement and disagreement achieved by the optimal classifiers from various IM data. Our final goal is to optimize

$$\min_{h^O} KL(h^O(y|x_a^O) \| h^O(y|x_b^O)), \quad \text{where } x_a^O \in \mathcal{D}, x_b^O \in \mathcal{A}, \quad (6.11)$

in which $h_a^O$ and $h_b^O$ denote the optimal classifiers in two IM data $x_a^O$ and $x_b^O$, respectively, and $x_a^O$ and $x_b^O$ are selected from OOM data with modality disagreement $\mathcal{D}:=\{x^O : h_a^O(x^O) \neq h_b^O(x^O)\}$. Here we use two in-modalities for simplicity, but the conclusion can be extended to multiple modalities. Moreover, the data with agreement is considered anchor points that guide the exploration over those with disagreement.
