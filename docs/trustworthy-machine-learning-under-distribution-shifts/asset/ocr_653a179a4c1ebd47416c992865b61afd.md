# Parameter Sensitivity Analysis.

To analyze the different choices of α and ΔT, we set α to 0.1, 0.2, and 0.3 and vary ΔT to 100, 200, 300, 400, and 500. As shown in Figure 4.5, choosing α as α, and ΔT as 300 is the best. Moreover, a lower α would hinder the dynamic update of the mask, a higher α would cause incorporation of noises, thus both choices lead to a performance. On the other hand, ΔT controls the updating frequency. But too small ΔT and too large ΔT would correspondingly cause insufficient update and redundant update, further leading to degradation.
