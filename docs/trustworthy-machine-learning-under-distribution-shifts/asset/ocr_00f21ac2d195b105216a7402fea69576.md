# ## 2.4.4 Open-set DA

In open-set DA task, we follow [41] to validate on two DA benchmark datasets Office Amazon (A), Webcam (W), and DSLR (D). Office dataset contains three domains and each domain is composed of 31 classes. VisDA dataset contains two domains Synthetic and Real, and each domain consists of 12 classes. To create an open-set situation in Office, we construct it by sampling from the first 21 classes in alphabetical order. Then, the target dataset is sampled from all 31 classes.
