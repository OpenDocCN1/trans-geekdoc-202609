$\mathcal{A}$ denotes the updated anchor points which are realized by sorting the Euclidean distance: $\mathcal{A} := \text{SORT}(\{(g_i^o, y_i)\}_{i=1}^M, \min_{i \in\{1..N\}} \frac{1}{K} \sum_{k=1}^K \|z_i^o - v_{k,i}\|);$ where the first term computes the cross-entropy loss from the anchor points, and the second term calculates the KL divergence between the OOM data with modal disagreement and the anchor points.
