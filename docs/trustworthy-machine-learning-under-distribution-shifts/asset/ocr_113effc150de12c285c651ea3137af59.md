# 4.5.2 Experimental Setup

Evaluation Protocol. We follow the experimental setting of DomainBed [176] to evaluate OOD generalization performance. Specifically, DomainBed contains several benchmark datasets: CMMNIST [69] (60,000 images, 10 classes, and 3 domains), MNIST [211] (60,000 images, 10 classes, and 6 domains), PACS [177] (9,991 images, 7 classes, 4 domains), VLCS [212] (10,729 images, 5 classes, and 4 domains), OfficeHome [213] (15,588 images, 65 classes, and 4 domains), TerraIncognita [214] (24,788 images, 10 classes, and 4 domains), DomainNet [147] (586,575 images, 345 classes, and 6 domains), WILDS [215] (a testbed contains variant dataset with significant distribution shift, here we use two typical datasets: iWildCam and FMoW), ImageNet [104] (1000 images, here we use ImageNet for fine-tuning, and many of its variant for OOD evaluation, including: ImageNetV2 [216], ImageNetR [217], ImageNetA [218], ImageNetSketch [219], and ObjectNet [220]). For each benchmark dataset, we leave one out of the training dataset and use it as an OOD test dataset. Moreover, we use pre-trained ResNet-50 [3] as our backbone model and train it for 5,000 iterations on all datasets DomainNet, which requires 15,000 iterations to converge. The test accuracies generated by training models from the last step are provided. To avoid randomness, three independent trials are conducted.
