Our goal is to determine, at least visually by now, the sentiment of the preceding reviews. Let's begin with a quick summary of the word count:

```
library(ggplot2)
text_df %>%
  count(word, sort=T) %>%
  mutate(word = reorder(word, n)) %>%
  ggplot(aes(word, n)) +
  geom_col() +
  xlab(NULL) +
  coord_flip() +
  theme_bw()
```
