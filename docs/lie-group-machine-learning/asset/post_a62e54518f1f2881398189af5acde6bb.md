## 6.5 Summary

The idea of covering was introduced into the field of machine learning, and the simply connected covering algorithm and multiply connected covering algorithm for Lie group machine learning were presented. At the same time, the correlation learning algorithm was applied to drug molecule design to verify the effectiveness and superiority of the algorithm. The main contents of this chapter are as follows:

1. We studied the mathematical connotation of the mathematical properties of the Lie group, such as the whole nature, local properties, generating elements, group linear representation, and covering group.
2. The simply connected Lie group algorithm was applied to the model of drug structure-activity relationship. By changing the Lie group problem to solve its covering group problem, the efficiency of learning was improved effectively.
3. The multiply connected covering algorithm of Lie group machine learning was given for the complex learning model to solve the learning problems under multi-connected conditions.
4. Lie group machine learning was introduced into the field of drug molecular design and specifically applied to the structure-based relationship model and molecular docking model, yielding satisfactory results.
