# # Authors

Fanzhang Li is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the Soochow University. Dr. Li is the dean of the school of computer science and technology, Soochow University. He is director of the Network Security Engineering Laboratory in Jiangsu Province and is also the director of the Soochow Institute of Large Industrial Data. Dr. Li is the leader of the International Laboratory for Machine Learning and Brain Computing, Soochow University. He is the adjunct professor at Beijing Jiaotong University and the visiting professor at Yunnan Minzu University. Dr. Li is the Chinese Scientist of the Year for 2015. Dr. Li has undertaken more than 10 of the programs, including National Key Natural Science Foundation of China, general program, major program and other program. Dr. Li has published more than 200 papers, seven academic monographs, and four textbooks. He has twice won the second prize of the Provincial Science and Technology Award and has won the IEEE CS GRC Pioneer Award. Dr. Li is the lead proposer of dynamic fuzzy logic (DFL), Lie Group machine learning (LML), and the theory and method of multidimensional collaborative education.

Li Zhang (M'08) received the degree of B.Sc. in 1997 and the degree of Ph.D. in 2002 in electronic engineering from Xidian University, Xi'an, China. She is now a professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Soochow University, Suzhou, China. She was a post doctorate researcher at the Institute of Automation, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Shanghai, China, from 2003 to 2005. She worked as an associate professor at the Institute of Intelligent Information Processing, Xidian University, Xi'an, China, from 2005 to 2010. She was a visiting professor at Yuan Ze University, Taiwan, from February to May 2010. Her research interests have been in the areas of machine learning, pattern recognition, neural networks and intelligent information processing. She has published more than 100 papers in journals and conferences, and holds 23 patents. She is a member of the IEEE, a Senior Member of the China Computer Federation (CCF), and a Senior Member of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI).

Zhao Zhang received the degree of Ph.D. from the Department of Electronic Engineering (EE), City University of Hong Kong, in 2013. He is currently an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Soochow University, Suzhou, China. Dr. Zhang was a visiting research engineer at the National University of Singapore, from Feb to May 2012. He then visited the National Laboratory of Pattern Recognition (NLPR) at the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), from Sep to Dec 2012. His current research interests include data mining and machine learning, pattern recognition and image analysis. He has authored/co-authored more than 60 technical papers published at prestigious international journals and conferences, including IEEE TKDE (3), IEEE TIP (3), ACM TIST, IEEE TSP, IEEE TCBY, IEEE TII (2), IEEE TNNLS, Pattern Recognition (2), Neural Networks (2), ICDM, ACM ICMR, ICIP and ICR. Specifically, he has got 10 first-author regular papers published by the IEEE/ACM Transactions Journals.
