Dr. Zhang is now serving on the editorial boards of *Neurocomputing*, IET Image Processing, *Neural Processing Letters* (NPL), *Neural Computing and Applications* (NCA). He has also served as a senior program committee (SPC) member of PAKDD 2017, an area chair (AC) of the BMVC 2016/2015, a program committee (PC) member for several popular international conferences (such as SDM 2017/2016/2015, PAKDD 2016, IJCNN 2017/2015, CAIP 2015). He is an invited journal reviewer for the IEEE TNNLS, IEEE TIP, IEEE TKDE, IEEE TSP, IEEE TCYB, IEEE TSMC-B, IEEE TMM, IEEE TII, *Pattern Recognition*, and *Information Sciences*. He is a member of the IEEE and the ACM, a senior member of the China Computer Federation (CCF), and a senior member of the Chinese Association for Artificial Intelligence (CAAI).
