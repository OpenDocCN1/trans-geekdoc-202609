We prove that the method is the same, and we only prove the case of anti-inclusion. Obviously, $|\mathbb{L}(A)|<\infty$, and $\cap_{X \in A} X$ and $\mathbb{F}_{q}^{n}$ are the largest and smallest elements in $\mathbb{L}_{R}(A)$, respectively. Now, we prove that it is a lattice. Since $\mathbb{L}_{R}(A)$ is a partially ordered set, it is only necessary to prove that in the partially ordered set, $\mathbb{L}(A)$ is closed for intersection and union.
