## Algorithm 10.5: Training step of tensor field classification algorithm

**Input:** data set $X=\{X_1, X_2, \dots, X_M\}$, $X_i=<P_{i1}, P_{i2}, \dots, P_{iN}>$, i=1...M  
**Output:** Discriminant tensor $\Gamma$

**Process:**

- Discretise the continuous attribute values of X by using MDL/CHI2 algorithm, and calculate the range of the attribute values of X, $U_i = (U_P, D_P)$, i = 1 . . . N.
- Determine the dimension D=$\{l_1, l_2, \dots, l_N\}$ of each order of the tensor based on $U_i$, and N is the dimension number of X.
- Construct tensor field $\Phi(X)$ of X. Initialize $\forall T_i \in \Phi(X)$, i = 1 . . . M.
- Process $\Phi(X)$ by using higher-order singular value decomposition (HOSVD), and reconstruct $\Phi(X)$.
- Perform $\Phi(X)$ equalisation and calculate discriminant tensor $\Gamma \in R^{U_1 \times U_2 \times \dots \times U_N}.$
