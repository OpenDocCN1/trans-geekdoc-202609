We calculate the kernel tensor $S_i \in R^{K_1 \times \cdots \times K_N}$ and the affine transformation matrix $U^{(n)} \in R^{I_n \times K_n}$, and use storage space $M$ to save them:

$M_1 = I_1 \times K(1) \times \cdots \times I_N \times K(N) + m \times K(1) \times \cdots \times K(N)$. (10.28)

The storage space of the original $X$ is:

$M_2 = m \times I_1 \times \cdots \times I_N$. (10.29)

In the Algorithm 10.2, $M_1$ is smaller than $M_2$, which can be proved by the experimental result.
