# ## TYPES OF BLOCKCHAIN NETWORKS

Blockchain technology encompasses a diverse range of networks, each with its characteristics and applications. In this chapter, we will delve into the intricacies of various types of blockchain networks, including public, private, consortium, permissioned, permissionless, hybrid, and federated blockchains. By examining these types in detail, we can gain a comprehensive understanding of their strengths, weaknesses, and real-world use cases.

# ## PUBLIC, PRIVATE, AND CONSORTIUM BLOCKCHAINS

+   a) **Public Blockchains:** Public blockchains are open networks that allow anyone to participate in and validate transactions. These networks operate in a decentralized manner, with a distributed network of nodes collectively maintaining the blockchain's integrity. Public blockchains, such as Bitcoin and Ethereum, are known for their transparency, immutability, and resistance to censorship. They rely on consensus mechanisms like Proof of Work (PoW) or Proof of Stake (PoS) to secure the network and validate transactions. Public blockchains are widely used for applications like cryptocurrencies, decentralized applications (dApps), and decentralized finance (DeFi).
