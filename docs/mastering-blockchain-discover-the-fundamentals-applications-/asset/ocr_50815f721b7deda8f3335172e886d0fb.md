c) **The Chain of Blocks:** The chain of blocks, often referred to as the blockchain, is formed by linking blocks together in a sequential manner. Each block in the chain includes a reference to the previous block, creating a chronological order of transactions. This linking mechanism ensures the immutability and integrity of the data stored in the blockchain. Once a block is added to the blockchain, it becomes incredibly difficult to alter or modify previous blocks, providing a secure and tamper-resistant ledger of transactions.
