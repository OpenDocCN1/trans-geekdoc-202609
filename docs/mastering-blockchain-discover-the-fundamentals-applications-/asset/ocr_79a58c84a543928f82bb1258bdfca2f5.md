# c) **Consortium Governance:**

+   - **Collaborative Governance:** Consortium governance models involve a group of organizations coming together to govern and operate a blockchain network. These models are often implemented in private or permissioned blockchains where a limited number of trusted entities collaborate and share decision-making power.

+   - **Governance Frameworks:** Consortium governance frameworks establish rules, standards, and protocols for consortium members, outlining their roles, responsibilities, and decision-making processes. These frameworks aim to foster trust, consensus, and cooperation among participating organizations, enabling effective governance of the blockchain network.
