+   - **Privacy**: Techniques and mechanisms to protect the confidentiality of transactions and user identities.
+   - **Scalability**: The ability of a blockchain network to handle increasing transaction volumes without compromising performance.
+   - **Tokenization**: Representing real world assets or rights as digital tokens on the blockchain.
+   - **Interoperability**: The ability of different blockchain networks to communicate and share data seamlessly.
+   - **Governance**: The framework and processes for making decisions and managing the operation of a blockchain network.
+   - **Security**: Measures and protocols to protect blockchain networks from unauthorized access and attacks.
+   - **Transparency**: The visibility of transactions and data on the blockchain, providing trust and accountability.
+   - **Peer-to-Peer**: Direct interaction and exchange of information or value between network participants.
+   - **Fork**: A split or divergence in a blockchain network, resulting in two separate chains with different rules.
