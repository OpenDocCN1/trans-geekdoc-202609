+   - **Non-Fungible Tokens (NFTs):** Unique digital assets that represent ownership of a specific item or piece of content.
+   - **Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC):** Digital forms of national currencies issued by central banks.
+   - **Decentralized Finance (DeFi):** Applications and platforms that provide financial services using smart contracts and blockchain technology.
+   - **Consensus Algorithm:** The algorithm or protocol used to achieve agreement among network participants.
+   - **Zero-Knowledge Proof:** A cryptographic method that allows for the verification of information without revealing the actual data.
+   - **Sidechain:** A separate blockchain that is interoperable with the main blockchain, enabling scalability and specific use cases.
+   - **Hybrid Blockchain:** A combination of public and private blockchains, offering the benefits of both models.
+   - **Proof of Stake (PoS):** A consensus mechanism where the probability of validating blocks is proportional to the amount of cryptocurrency held.
