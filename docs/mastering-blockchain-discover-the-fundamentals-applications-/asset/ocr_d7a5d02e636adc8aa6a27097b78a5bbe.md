+   - **Cybersecurity and Trust**: Blockchain's distributed and decentralized nature provides inherent security benefits, but it is not immune to cybersecurity threats. The protection of blockchain networks and their underlying infrastructure requires robust security measures, including cryptographic techniques, secure key management, and secure coding practices.

# c) Ethical Considerations:

+   - **Transparency and Privacy**: Blockchain's transparency can conflict with the need for privacy in certain contexts. Striking a balance between transparency and privacy is crucial in blockchain applications, especially when dealing with sensitive personal data and confidential business information.
+   - **Trust and Trustlessness**: Blockchain aims to establish trust in a trustless environment. Ethical considerations arise in ensuring that trust is maintained among network participants, avoiding fraudulent activities, and promoting fair and transparent processes.
