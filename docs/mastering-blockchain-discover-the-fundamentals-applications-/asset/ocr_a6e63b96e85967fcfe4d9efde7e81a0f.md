+   - **Off-chain Governance:** Off-chain governance involves decision-making processes that occur outside the blockchain protocol. It relies on informal discussions, community coordination, and social consensus among network participants. Projects like Bitcoin and Ethereum rely on off-chain governance models.

+   - **DAO Structures:** Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) are entities governed by smart contracts and operated through blockchain technology. DAOs are designed to enable transparent, democratic, and decentralized decision-making by token holders. DAOs can govern fund allocation, project proposals, voting mechanisms, and membership rights, allowing stakeholders to participate in the organization's activities and shape its direction.
