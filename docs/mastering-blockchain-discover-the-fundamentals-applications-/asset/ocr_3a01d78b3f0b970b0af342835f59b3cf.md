# KEYWORDS IN BLOCKCHAIN

+   - **Blockchain**: A decentralized and transparent ledger that securely records and verifies transactions.
+   - **Cryptocurrency**: Digital or virtual currencies that utilize cryptography for secure transactions.
+   - **Smart Contracts**: Self-executing contracts with predefined rules and conditions encoded on the blockchain.
+   - **Decentralization**: The distribution of authority and decision-making power across a network.
+   - **Consensus**: The mechanism by which participants in a blockchain network agree on the validity of transactions.
+   - **Distributed Ledger**: A synchronized and shared record of transactions maintained across multiple nodes.
+   - **Immutable**: Unable to be changed or tampered with, ensuring the integrity of data on the blockchain.
+   - **Mining**: The process of validating and adding new transactions to the blockchain through computational effort.
+   - **Wallet**: Software or hardware used to store and manage digital assets securely.
