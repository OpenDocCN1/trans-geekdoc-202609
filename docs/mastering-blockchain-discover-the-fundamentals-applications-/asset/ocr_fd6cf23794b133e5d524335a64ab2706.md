+   - **Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO):** Organizations governed by smart contracts and run on the blockchain.
+   - **Token Economy:** The ecosystem created by the issuance and circulation of tokens on a blockchain network.
+   - **Cross-Chain Bridge:** A technology that enables the transfer of assets between different blockchain networks.
+   - **Plasma:** A scaling solution that creates a network of interconnected blockchains, offloading transactions from the main chain.
+   - **Sharding:** Dividing the blockchain network into smaller partitions to improve scalability and efficiency.
+   - **Zero-Knowledge Encryption:** Encrypting data on the blockchain without revealing the actual content.
+   - **Governance Token:** Tokens that give holders voting rights and influence over the development and direction of a blockchain project.
+   - **Smart Oracles:** External data feeds or services that provide real-world information to smart contracts.
+   - **Initial Coin Offering (ICO):** A fundraising method in which tokens or coins are sold to investors in exchange for funding.
