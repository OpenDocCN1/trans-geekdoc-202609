+   c) **Digital Signatures:** Digital signatures provide a way to verify the authenticity and integrity of data in the blockchain. They involve the use of the sender's private key to create a unique digital signature for a transaction or a piece of data.
+   d) **Signature Verification:** The recipient or any other party can verify the signature using the sender's public key. By decrypting the signature using the public key, they can confirm that the signature matches the original data, ensuring that the data has not been tampered with and that it originated from the expected sender.

Public and private key cryptography provides a powerful mechanism for secure communication and authentication without the need for shared secret keys. It enables participants in the blockchain network to transact securely and verify the integrity of data.
