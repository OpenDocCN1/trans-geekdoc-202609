+   - **Hard Fork:** A type of fork that results in a permanent divergence from the previous blockchain.
+   - **Plasma Chain:** A separate blockchain connected to the Ethereum mainnet that enables high speed and scalable transactions.
+   - **Stablecoin:** A type of cryptocurrency designed to maintain a stable value, often pegged to a fiat currency.
+   - **Layer 2 Solutions:** Technologies built on top of existing blockchains to improve scalability and throughput.
+   - **Cross-Chain Interoperability:** Enabling communication and transfer of assets between different blockchain networks.
+   - **Web3:** The vision of a decentralized and user-centric internet enabled by blockchain and other distributed technologies.
