b) **Verification and Efficiency:** Merkle trees provide an efficient way to verify the integrity of a transaction or a piece of data within a block. Instead of needing to traverse and hash every transaction in a block, one can compare the hash of a transaction with the Merkle root. If the hashes match, the integrity of the transaction is confirmed. This verification process allows for quick and secure validation without the need to access the entire block or the entire blockchain.

c) **Scalability and Light Client Validation:** Merkle trees also offer scalability benefits. In large blockchains, storing and transmitting the entire blockchain to every participant can be impractical. Instead, lightweight nodes can use Merkle proofs, which are compact sets of cryptographic proofs, to demonstrate the inclusion of a specific transaction in a block. These proofs allow participants to validate transactions efficiently without needing to access the entire block or the entire blockchain.

Merkle trees enhance the security, efficiency, and scalability of blockchain systems by providing a tamper-resistant and efficient way to verify the integrity of data within a block. They are an essential component of many blockchain protocols.
