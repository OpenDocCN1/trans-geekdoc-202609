# Theft Risk

Smartphones, tablets and laptops are tempting targets for thieves. Because of their size, these mobile devices are easily snatched, and thieves find them easy to sell on the black market. For this reason, it is worth taking extra care when out and about with your mobile devices.

+   Location: Before getting out your phone or tablet, take stock of your surroundings. Note who is about and whether it is safe to get your device out.

+   Unattended: Never leave a mobile device unattended, even for a few seconds. A phone left on a table can be easily grabbed, and never leave a mobile device in a car.

+   IMEI number: All smartphones and tablets have a 15-digit IMEI number, usually printed under the battery. Knowing this number enables your provider to block the device if it is lost or stolen (even if it has been given a new SIM).
