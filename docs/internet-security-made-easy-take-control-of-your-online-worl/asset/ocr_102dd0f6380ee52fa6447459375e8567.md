# ENCRYPTION KEYS AND IDENTIFIERS

Perhaps the most important form of security on a modern wireless router is the encryption key you enter when connecting to the router. Most manufacturers provide each router with a unique key, but is often a good idea to regularly change it.

# Hot Tip

Change your SSID in your router setting from the manufacturer's default, as this will prevent hackers from knowing what type of router you have, making it harder for them to hack into it.
