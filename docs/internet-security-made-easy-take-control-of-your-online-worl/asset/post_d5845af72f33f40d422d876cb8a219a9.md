## INTERNET SECURITY MADE EASY

It is an unfortunate truth that when we send an email or surf the net, most of us are unaware of just how much is occurring below the surface, of the risks we take when we enter cyberspace. From viruses and other malicious malware to hacking, identity theft and financial fraud; from embarrassing damage to one's reputation to serious dangers posed to children through social media, there are many threats against which we can take action.

Internet Security Made Easy aims to leave no stone unturned, from the necessity of a strong password and the means to obtain it, to the best antivirus software to install. With straightforward and digestible information, we provide you with multiple solutions and useful knowledge that you will be able to apply to your experiences online, regardless of whether you are already familiar with Internet security or a complete beginner. Beginning with an introduction to the World Wide Web and its risks, the book then covers a wide range of dangers to look out for, with specific sections on child Internet safety, followed by various software solutions and advanced troubleshooting.

The knowledge we provide is intended to get you to think outside of the box. You wouldn't leave your child alone with a stranger so why should online interaction be any different? Equally, many people overlook the fact that it is possible for your handheld devices to be hacked and even damaged by various viruses, so we cover that too. With practical step-by-step guides and helpful screenshots, Internet security really is made easy.

## INTRODUCTION

The Internet has changed our lives forever, but this communications revolution is not without its problems. As in ordinary life, there are viruses, hackers and identity fraudsters. This book is designed to help you safely navigate the online world and keep you secure from online threats.

### INTERNET SECURITY

Keeping safe online is important. As well as benefits, the Internet contains potentially harmful threats, from viruses and malware that can infect our computers, erasing our data or spying on our online activities, to hackers and ID fraudsters who want to gain access to our data for their own aims. Knowing how to defend yourself from these threats is increasingly crucial for keeping your network, computers, online accounts and family safe.

### The Importance of Protection

The Internet has become a vital tool for many of us, enabling us to socialize on social media, communicate by email, shop and do our banking online. Because of this reliance on the online world, keeping secure is vital to prevent unauthorized access to our online accounts and ensure our personal information does not fall into the wrong hands.
