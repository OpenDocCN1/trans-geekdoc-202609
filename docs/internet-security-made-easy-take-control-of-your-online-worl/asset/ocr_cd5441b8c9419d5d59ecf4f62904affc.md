# # Access For All

In what has to be one of the most magnanimous gestures in history, Tim Berners-Lee and the directors at CERN issued a statement in 1993 declaring that the World Wide Web was to become freely available for everybody to use. This move saw,the birth of the Internet as we know it today, which in a few short years has become arguably the most powerful communications tool ever created.

# # GROWTH OF THE WEB

Since the World Wide Web became freely available for everybody to use, its growth has been exponential, aided by all sorts of technologies and developments.
