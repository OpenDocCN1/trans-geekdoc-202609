+   - Viruses: Some viruses delete files as part of their programming.

> Hot Tip

> Most antivirus software can clean files, such as documents and pictures, but they may leave them in quarantine where you can recover them. However, program files are often harder to clean so should not be recovered.

# # Quarantined Items

The first place to check if you have lost a file is your antivirus quarantine. Normally, antivirus software tries to clean an infected file, but if it cannot, it places it in quarantine where it cannot do any more harm. However, before you can retrieve your file, you need to ensure it does not pose a threat as you could just be unleashing the virus back onto your system.
