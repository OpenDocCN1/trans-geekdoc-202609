# Stranger Danger

Before the Internet, it was easy to warn children about the dangers of talking to strangers. However, in the Internet era, with social media, chat rooms, forums and text messages, it can be all too easy for strangers to make contact. Furthermore, one of the great advantages of the World Wide Web is that your children can meet and chat with children from all over the world, which can be a positive experience, but which can also pose some perils.
