# HACKED ONLINE ACCOUNTS

Your computer is not the only thing that is at risk from hackers. Online accounts can be hacked too. This is done for a number of reasons.

+   - **Identity theft:** Gaining access to your online accounts is a way for identity fraudsters to obtain personal information about you.
+   - **Crime:** Criminals can steal your money if they hack into your bank or credit card account.
+   - **Spam, malware and phishing:** Taking over a social media account is a good way to spread spam and malware, or to fool people into handing over information because they think the message comes from a trusted source.

# An Extreme Example

In 2012, technology writer Mat Honan became the victim of online hackers who took over nearly all his online accounts. After taking advantage of a security flaw on an online retail site, Amazon, hackers were able to use the information they found to delete Mat's emails, take over his Google and Twitter accounts, as well as erase all data from his Apple devices.
