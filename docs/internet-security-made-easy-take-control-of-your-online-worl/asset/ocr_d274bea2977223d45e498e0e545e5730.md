# # OTHER ONLINE SECURITY MEASURES

As well as username and passwords, websites deploy a host of other security measures to help with the authentication process.

+   o **Memorable information**: When you sign up to a website or service, you may be asked to provide some memorable information such as your first school or mother’s maiden name.

+   o **IP address**: Some websites restrict you to logging in on specific computers and won’t allow access if your IP (Internet Protocol) address, the unique identifier of your computer, is different to the one registered to your account.
