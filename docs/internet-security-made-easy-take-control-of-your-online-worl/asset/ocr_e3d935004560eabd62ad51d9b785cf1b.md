+   - Wireless piggybacking: With so many Internet connections now wireless, it is possible for somebody to use your connection without you knowing it; they may even be downloading illegal files or hacking.

+   - Offensive content: There may be lost of Web content that you may not want see or want your children to see, such as pornographic or violent websites.
