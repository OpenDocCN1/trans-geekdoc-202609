# MONITORING WEB USE

One of the things parents find difficult is knowing where the line is between invading your child's privacy and sensible monitoring of their Internet use. Children will not appreciate you standing over their shoulder while they speak to their friends on social media, or accessing their accounts so you can read their posts, but there are other ways in which you can monitor their web use.

# Hot Tip

Spending too much time on the Internet is not good for anybody, let alone children. Some parents set limits as to how long their children can spend online each day.

# Page History

You may not feel comfortable snooping on your child's Facebook or Bebo page, but as a parent, it is sensible to ensure your children are not accessing inappropriate material, and one of the easiest ways to do this is to check their web history.

+   1. On your Internet browser, select options (this may be represented by three horizontal lines or a gear icon).

![](bbox=[0.49, 0.41, 0.97, 0.68])

Above: Web history in Chrome.

+   2. Select History.

+   3. You will be taken to a page of all the websites visited in chronological order (newest first). Click on the links if you are unsure what they are and want to check the content of the website.
