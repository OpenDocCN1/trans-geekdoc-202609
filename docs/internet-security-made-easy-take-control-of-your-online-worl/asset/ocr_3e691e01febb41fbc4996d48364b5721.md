# DAMAGED REPUTATIONS

We have already seen that things we post on the Internet can have long-lasting consequences. Images, comments and posts can stay there for ever. It is also very easy to copy and paste content from one site to another, so something that your child sends to just a few select friends could soon be available for anyone to see, even years later.
