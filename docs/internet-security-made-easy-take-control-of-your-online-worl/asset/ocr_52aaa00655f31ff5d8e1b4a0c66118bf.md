+   7. After the search, you should see the extension listed in the folder called: HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE \SOFTWARE\ Policies\ Google\Chrome\ Extension InstallForcelist.

+   8. Right click on the number to the left of the extension name, and from the menu, select Delete and confirm you want to delete it.
