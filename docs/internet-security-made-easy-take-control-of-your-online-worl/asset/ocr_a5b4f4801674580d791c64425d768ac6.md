# PREVENTING HACKERS

Prevention is better than cure, so ensure you are doing the following to minimize the risk of your machine being hacked:

+   - Firewall: Make sure you have a firewall installed and never turn it off; it is your first line of defence against hackers.

+   - Antivirus: Make sure your antivirus is always running and kept up to date.

+   - Updating: Keep your programs, especially your operating system, regularly updated.
