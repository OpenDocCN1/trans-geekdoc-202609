# # Twitter Security and Privacy

You can adjust several aspects of your account to protect your personal information.

+   - Login verification: Have Twitter send you an email or text whenever somebody attempts to log into your account.

+   - Password reset: Allows you to set Twitter to request additional information for resetting passwords.

+   - Photo tagging: As with Facebook, you can prevent people tagging you in photos.

+   - Tweet privacy: Choose to make your Tweets public, so anyone can see them, or private and visible only to your followers.

+   - Tweet location: Choose whether your location is included in your Tweets.

+   - Discoverability: Choose whether people can search for you on Twitter using your email address.

+   - Promoted content: Prevent Twitter from tailoring adverts specifically to you based on your Tweets and the people you follow.
