# Facebook Privacy

In the privacy, timeline and blocking menus, you can control who sees what in your timeline, prevent people from tagging images of you, as well as block people with whom you would rather not engage.

+   Who can see my stuff: You can choose whether your posts are public or for friends only, and even restrict things you have already posted.

+   Who can contact me: Filter messages sent to you and who can send you a friend request.

+   Who can look me up: Edit who can search for your profile on both Facebook and in search engines.
