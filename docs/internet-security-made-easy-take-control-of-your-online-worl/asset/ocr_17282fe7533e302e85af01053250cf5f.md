# Remote Wipe on iOS Devices

Organizing remote wipe on Apple devices is fairly simple, but you need to set up a few things first.

+   * iCloud: You need to set up an iCloud account so you can access Apple's online services.
+   * iOS 4.2 and earlier: For older Apple devices, you need to enable Push and Find My iPhone in the Settings app under Mail, Contacts, Calendars.

# Wiping Your iOS Device

+   1. Log into iCloud and click the Account button at the top of the screen.
+   2. Select the Find My iPhone, iPad, iPod Touch or Mac option once the page loads.
+   3. Select All Devices and choose the device you want to erase.
+   4. In the device's information window, select Erase.

> **Did You Know?**
> 
> If your phone or iPad is offline, it will erase as soon as it goes online again. You should receive a notification by email when it is erased, but for some older models, this can take as long as two hours.
