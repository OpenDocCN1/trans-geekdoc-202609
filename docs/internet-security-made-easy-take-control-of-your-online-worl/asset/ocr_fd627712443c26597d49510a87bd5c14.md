+   - Businesses: Some disreputable companies use malware to market products or get people to buy their software fixes.
+   - Governments: Even governments create malware to infiltrate the computers systems of another nation.

# How They Get In

Malicious software can get into a computer in a number of ways: some are hidden in programs and are activated when you install the program; some software developers create free programs just in order to install malware on people's machines; while other malware is disguised as genuine files and is activated when you open the file. Additionally, some malware lingers on malicious websites, infecting your Internet browser when you visit the site.

# Hot Tip

Whenever you install new software, especially free programs, always read the user agreement carefully and do a search on the Internet to see if other users have developed malware issues after installing.

# WHAT IS OUT THERE?

Malware is a broad term and includes all sorts of malicious software that can range from being minor annoyances to programs that can erase all your data, steal your files or disable your computer. Sometimes infection by malware is obvious because you may lose data or suffer continuous computer crashes. However, with some malware, you may not even know you have been infected.
