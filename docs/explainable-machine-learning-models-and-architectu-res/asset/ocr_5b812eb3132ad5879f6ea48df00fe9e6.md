# ## 7.2 Historical Background

Image forgery detection techniques (IFDT) for any type of forgeries have the goal of detecting forged regions with accuracy, improving the accuracy, regionalizing the forgery and making the time of detection as low as possible. IFDTs are categorized in two different types, as image processing and deep learning.

[5] discuss the use of CNN to classify different images categories they obtained from the ImageNet database. Mishra et al.
