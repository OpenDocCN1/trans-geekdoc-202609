# 6.15.1 MACHINE LEARNING APPLICATION IN DRUG DISCOVERY

ML approaches have played key role in the process of drug discovery in the recent times. It has minimized the high failure rate in drug development by leveraging the availability of high-quality data. There are multiple challenges in ML for drug development. One of the challenging and complex tasks in the process of drug discovery is to ensure drug safety. to analyze and interpret the available information of the known effects of the drugs and prediction of their side effects. Researchers from various reputed/institutions and of course, many pharmaceutical companies have been using ML to obtain relevant information from clinical data used in clinical trials. Analyzing and interpreting these data using ML in the context of drug discovery is an active area of research in recent times. Above all, the computational solution in drug discovery has helped significantly reduced the cost of introducing drugs to the market.
