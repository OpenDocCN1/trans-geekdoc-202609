# 7.4 WEARABLE HEALTH DEVICES

Healthcare technologies such as the IoT, also known as the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) and Wearable Health devices (WDs) play a crucial role by their immense capacity of generation and transmission of vital data [4,30]. Today there are many WDS available in the market, which accurately measure heartrate, exercise tracking, body temperature, female health and other vitals [31]. Some of the advanced WDs notify when they are suffering stress and provides to this users with a guided exercise to regulate the SpO2 – Oxygen level [32], where some other devices have the capabilities of generation and sharing of electrocardiogram known as ECG [33–35].
