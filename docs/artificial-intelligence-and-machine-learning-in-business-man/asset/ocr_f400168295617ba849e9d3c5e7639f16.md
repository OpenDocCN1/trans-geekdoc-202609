For evaluating the ML model, 4-fold cross-validation was used. Cross-validation is a procedure for evaluating ML models by training several ML models on subsets of the accessible input data and evaluating them on the complementary subset of the data. Use cross-validation to detect overfitting, failing to generalize a pattern. In k-fold cross-validation, you split the input data into k subsets of data (also known as folds). You train an ML model on all but one (k-1) of the subsets and evaluate the model on the subset that was not used for training. This process is repeated k times, with a distinct subset reserved for evaluation (and excluded from training) each time.
