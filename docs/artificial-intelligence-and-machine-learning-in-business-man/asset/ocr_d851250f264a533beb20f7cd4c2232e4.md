# 9.4 A SHORT INTRODUCTION TO AI AND MACHINE LEARNING

In 1950, Alan M Turing, in his paper ‘Computing Machinery and Intelligence’, mentions that ‘An important feature of learning a machine is that its teacher will be very ignorant of what is going on inside, although he may still be able to predict the pupil’s behaviour’. This is in clear contrast with normal procedure when using a machine to do computations one's object is to have a clear mental picture of the state of the machine at each moment in the computation’ [5]. The term “teacher” used by him the computer and the algorithm used for learning. It is important to note that he used three important entities: learning machine, teacher and pupil. He also mentioned the pupil’s behaviour is predictable to some extent. The goal of the Machine Learning Algorithms is to make a prediction as accurate as possible.

The term Artificial Intelligence was first introduced (coined) in 1956, by a group of scientists at a computer science conference in Dartmouth [6]. Their objective was to lay out a framework to better understand human intelligence. Based on their work, they identified the key factor for a machine to model human factors is Machine Learning.
