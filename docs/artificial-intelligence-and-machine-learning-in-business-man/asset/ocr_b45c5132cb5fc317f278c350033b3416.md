| 01 | Hansen et al. [5] | The methods used exponential smoothing, autoregressive integrated moving average (ARIMA), and partially adaptive estimation ARIMA models and improved Machine Learning techniques called SVM. | The objective of this study is to compare efficiency of SVM learning models with three proven models. The result show that SVM learning weakly dominate over models and achieved best results in eight of different data sets |
| 02 | Carbonneau et al. [6] | This study used the technique of neural network with advanced ML techniques, recurrent neural networks and SVM to forecast distorted demand (bullw effect). | They made a comparison of result with traditional models, i.e., naive forecasting, trending and linear regression. Their findings suggest that MLs techniques are superior to the traditional techniques |
| 03 | Choudhary and Garg [7] | The techniques used to forecast stock prices are used Hybrid Learning system based on GA and SVM. | Their findings show that hybrid GA-SVM outperform the standalone SVM system. The continuous technical analyst to gain from market strategy has raised the question against EMH |
| 04 | HamzaEebi et al. [1] | Iterative and direct method of ANN is used to forecast multi-period series data. The comparison is made with ARIMA and partial adaptive estimation technique. | Direct method is based on one step ahead on observed data is better than iterative method. The case of iterative method the forecasted date became input for next time period increases of the forecasted error. Overall, ANN method is better than other models used for comparison |
| 05 | Tsai and Wang [8] | This study used DT and ANN technique to forecast stock prices. | The DT + ANN and DT + DT models are used to forecast Taiwan stock market. The hybrid model (DT+ANN) give 77% forecasting accuracy is one of the best forecasting accuracy. |
| 06 | Ahmed et al. [9] | This study made comparison of MLP, BNN, RBF; generalized regression neural network (also called Kernel Regression), CART regression trees, SVR and Gaussian processes. These methods are used in conjunction with the ML forecasting models. | | The findings suggest that two models of MLP and GP are better performer to others used models.
