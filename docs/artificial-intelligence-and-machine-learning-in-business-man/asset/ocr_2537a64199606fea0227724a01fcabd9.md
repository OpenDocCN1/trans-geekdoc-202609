# 9.2.1 CONTENT DELIVERY PROCESS

The main task of content delivery is to present the learning material (text/audio/video) to the student, emulating a classroom environment. An experienced teacher in a classroom delivers the lessons in a face-to-face environment and he/she understands how well a student understands the subject by analyzing the response of the student while answering questions. The teacher can enhance and explain the subject to assist the students. If we can simulate this model, we could achieve an effective online learning environment. While in a classroom, the teacher faces a group of students with various capacities to learn, in an online there will be individual students and the content delivery should tailor the contents to each student. Hence it is necessary to assess each student separately.
