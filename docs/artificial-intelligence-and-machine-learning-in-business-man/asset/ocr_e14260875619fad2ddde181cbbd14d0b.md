# 1.5 MARKETING PLAN AND AI’S POTENTIAL

Applications of AI are very broad in nature. With the help of AI abilities trend to detect and extrapolate patterns in data, marketers can identify opportunities as well as threats, and act on them in real time. Following are the ways in which we can leverage AI’s potential across different stages of a marketing plan.

# Stage 1: Analyzing the Current Situation

This stage involves the analysis of macroenvironmental factors and future business environments by analyzing the current situation and understanding the business environment [6]. Marketers use SWOT (Strengths Weaknesses Opportunities Threats) analysis for getting the in-depth understating of the targeted market. With the help of AI assisted features like social listening, getting data-driven information on purchasing patterns, product demand and customer satisfaction can assess and estimate demand, market trend and respective customer. With the help of ML, an AI backed consumer research investigated the online consumer discussions and suggested that a brand should not just focus on itself while analyzing the online forums but other competitor brands [4]. This can bring an understanding of competitors’ behaviour and in gauging the target segments reactions.

# Stage 2: Understanding Markets and Customers

This stage involves the analysis of microenvironmental factors. Organizations tries to develop understanding related to consumer behaviour, factors affecting their loyalty, attitude, purchasing patterns, product category and also the market demand. AI can assist the marketers in processing the vast data available through their social media accounts, mobile activity, web searches and contact centre interactions [5]. For example, at the Consumers Electronics Show in Las Vegas, 2019, Neurodata Labs and Promobot (a robotics manufacturer) demonstrated a multimodal emotion detection, for the purpose of customer experience management [6]. This AI-enabled system uses a combination of eye movement, heart rate, voice, body gestures and facial expressions in order to determine customers’ emotional states [6]. Rosbank is using this technology in their call center, and collects information changes in voice volume, number of pauses in speech, conversion time etc. and converts it into customer satisfaction metric [7].
