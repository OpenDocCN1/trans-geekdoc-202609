# 1.4.2.1 Amazon Go (Caselet)

Amazon.com, Inc was founded by Jeff Bezos in 1994 under the name Cadabra (1994-95). It started as an online marketplace for books but later ventured into furnishing, apparel, food, jewellery, software etc. It is considered one of the Big technology companies (alongside Google, Apple and Facebook) and focuses on e-commerce, AI, digital streaming and cloud computing. In 2018 Amazon announced its two-day delivery service, Amazon Prime, which today has 10 million subscribers worldwide. Prime Video, Amazon Music, Audible and Twitch are subscribers which downloads are distributed, and audiobooks, videos and music are streamed. Amazon distributes various products and services through Fresh, Amazon Studios, Kindle Store.com has acquired around 40 subsidiaries including Zappos, Goodreads, Amazon Robotics, IMDb, Amazon Maritime, Ring, Whole Foods Markets etc.
Amazon opened its first store in 2018 in Seattle. It is a chain of convenience stores, with 26 stores across the US, and further then to open the United Kingdom as of 2020. Amazon Go is not just any grocery store; it is a store with 'Walk Out' technology, where the customers can simply enter the store, grab whatever they need and leave, eliminating checkout lines. In order to get started with the shopping, the shoppers need to have an Amazon account, smartphone and the Amazon Go App. As the shoppers need to scan a barcode within the app on their smartphone at the entrance turnstiles, keep their phones with them, and grab whatever they need before leaving the store. Amazon has applied the technology used by self-driving cars (AI, ML, Image recognition, array of sensors, deep learning, computer vision, and to create the shopping experience in Amazon Go. From the moment shoppers enter the store, they are recognized and tracked with the help of sensors and cameras installed all over the store. The technology automatically detects items taken from the shelves. It keeps a track of the products in the virtual cart, and once the shopping is complete and the shopper exits the store, Amazon charges them from their Amazon account and emails a receipt to the customer.

# 1.4.2.2 Technical Working of Amazon Go

Firstly, the data is acquired picked up several sensors in order to enable awareness of products which have been picked up from the aisle or the shelves. Some examples of the type of sensors are weight measurement, pressure detectors, dimensional measurements of the products. Secondly, deep learning algorithms are used to understand what product the customer picked or put back; which products in the virtual cart; how much time did the customer take to make a decision; which products are getting sold faster and where to place these products for easy access. And finally, when the customer places a transition point on exiting the store, a bill is generated for the items in the virtual cart, which is the result of the automated analysis done by intelligent machines working behind the curtain.

After the success of Amazon Go stores, many retailers showed interest in this technology which is an amalgam of complex system of sensors, cameras and software that tracks each and every activity and movement of the shoppers inside the store.
