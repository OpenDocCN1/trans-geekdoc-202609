# 15.7.2 SECURITY

Video surveillance is an old task of the safety domain. However, from the time that systems were monitored exclusively by humans to current supporting video analytics, with water passed under the bridge. Facility and licence plate recognition (LPR) can be applied to recognize people and vehicles in real-time and make appropriate decisions. For instance, it is possible to search for a suspect in real-time and in stored video footage or recognize unauthorized personnel and grant access to a secured facility. Crowd management is another crucial function of the security system. Cutting edge analytics tools can make a huge difference in places such as shopping malls, hospitals, stadiums and airports. These tools can provide an approximated count in real-time and can trigger alerts when a threshold is reached or surpassed. They can also analyze flow to detect movement in prohibited or unwanted directions. This is one of the advantages of these approaches: video content analysis systems are often trained to detect specific events, sometimes with a high degree of refinement. One one example is to detect fires as soon as possible. Or, in the case of airports, to raise an alert when someone walks against the intended direction for passengers or enters a forbidden area. Another great case is the real-time detection of unattended baggage in a public place. As traditional tasks like intruder detection, they will be performed robustly, because of algorithms which will filter motion caused by wind, rain, snow or animals. functionality offered by intelligent video analysis is growing day by day within the security domain, and this is on a trend that will continue within the future.
