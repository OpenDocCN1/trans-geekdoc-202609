# 6.15.4 MACHINE LEARNING IN PERSONALIZED HEALTHCARE

Internet of Things (IoT) in healthcare has made it increasingly possible to connect
a large number of people, things with smart sensors as wearable and medical
devices and environments. Patient vitals and various types of real-time data are
ccaptured by sensors and smart assets in IoT devices. Data analytics technologies,
such as ML, can be used to deliver value-based care to the people. For instance,
operational improvements enhance efficiencies that provide quality care at reduced
costs. Similarly, clinical improvements ensure quickly and relatively accurate
diagnoses. It also ensures more patient-centric, scientific determination of the best
therapeutic approach to support better health outcomes. ML uses dataset
to improve movement method and sickness forecast. Explanatory models
by ML are incorporated into various services applications. These
models typically break down the collected information from sensor gadgets and
different sources to recognize personal conduct standards and clinical states of the
patient.

# 6.15.5 MACHINE LEARNING IN OUTBREAK PREDICTION

Multiple outbreak prediction models are widely used by researchers in the recent
times to make most appropriate decisions and implement relevant measures to control
outbreak. For example, researchers are using some of the standard models such
asemiological and statistical models for prediction of COVID-19. Prediction
emerging from these models proved to be less robust and less accurate as it involves
h uncertainty and paucity of relevant data. Recently, many researchers are using
ML models to make long term prediction of this outbreak. Researchers have shown
ML-based models proved to be more robust compared to the alternative models
for this outbreak.
