Before modernization, manual labour by men and animals dominated travel. Mechanical energy from wind, water and fire was used for ages, such as sailing boats and water wheels. The steam engine invented by Thomas Savery in 1698 modified the design of steam engines making them more versatile for a variety of uses. James Watt is credited with the steam engines as his design was a breakthrough in the conversion of thermal energy which marked the dawn of an industrial revolution in Europe. By 1886, steam engines could generate 10,000 horsepower and were used to propel large steamships and long-haul steam locomotives [3]. By the turn of the century, gasoline engines had appeared, and the first automobile was built by Daimler [8].
