### CONCLUSION

The above literature review shows that ML play crucial roles in the domain of forecasting time series data. The use of eight individual models by Moghamad et al. [16] and eight families of Machine Learning called MLP, BNN, RBF, GRNN, also called Kernel-Nearest Neighbour Regression (KNN), CART Regression Trees (KART), SVR and Gaussian Processes (GP) by Makridakis et al. [15] show that Machine Learning has better prospects over traditional models. Chen et al. [13] and Chang et al. [12] also used two-stage procedure to forecast small samples in early hours of the business showed results over traditional models. The overall analysis of models and techniques used for the purpose of forecasting indicate that ML techniques will become the future tools in the field.
