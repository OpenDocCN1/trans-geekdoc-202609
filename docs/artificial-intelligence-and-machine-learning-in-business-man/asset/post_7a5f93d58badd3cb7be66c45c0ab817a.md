
# Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Business Management

## 15.6 APPLICATIONS

Some applications within the sector of video analytics are widely known to the public. One example is video surveillance, a task that has existed for about 50 years. In fact, the concept is simple: install cameras strategically to permit human operators to manage what happens in a room, or public space.

In practice, it is a task that is far away from simple. A single operator is mostly responsible for surveillance of more than one camera, and, as several studies have shown, increasing the camera counts to monitored adversely affects the operator's performance. In other words, whether or not an oversized amount of hardware is available out there and generating signals, a bottleneck is made when it's time to process these signals thanks to human limitations.

## 15.7 HEALTHCARE

Historically, healthcare institutions have invested a large sum of money in video surveillance solutions in making sure the security of their patients, staff and visitors, at levels that are often regulated by strict legislation. In fact, infant abduction and diversion are among the foremost common problems addressed by surveillance systems.

In addition to facilitating surveillance tasks, video analytics allows us to travel further, exploiting the data collected on to realize business goals. For instance, a video analytics solution could detect when a patient has not been checked according to the needs and alert. Analysis of patient and visitor traffic is extremely valuable in determining ways to shorten wait times while ensuring clear to the energy area.

At-home monitoring of older adults or people with health issues is another example of an application that gives excellent value. For example, falls are a significant reason for injury and death in older persons. Although personal medical devices can detect, they need to be worn and are frequently disregarded by the patron. A video analytics solution can analyze the signals of home cameras to identify in real-time if a person has fallen. With proper setup, such a system could also determine if an individual took a given medication once they were specified.

Mental health is another area where video analytics can make significant contributions. Systems that analyze facial expressions, body posture and gaze are developed to help clinicians within the evaluation of patients. Such a system is in a position to detect emotions from visual communications and micro-expressions, offering clinicians objective information that may confirm their hypotheses or give them new clues.

### 15.7.1 SMART CITIES/TRANSPORTATION

Video analytics has proven to be a fantastic help within the realm of transport, aiding within the event of smart cities. A rise in traffic, especially in urban areas, may end up in an increase in accidents and traffic jams if adequate traffic management measures do not seem to be taken. Intelligent video analysis solutions can play a vital role during this scenario.

## Semantic Data Extraction Using Video Analysis

Traffic analysts often want to adjust traffic light control systems dynamically and to look at traffic jams. It may also be useful in detecting dangerous situations in real-time as a vehicle stopped in an unauthorized space on the highway, someone driving in the wrong direction, a vehicle moving erratically or cars that are in an accident. In the case of an accident, these systems are helpful in collecting evidence in case of litigation.

Vehicle counting, or differentiating cars, trucks, buses, taxis and so on, generate high-value statistics to obtain insights about traffic. Installing speed cameras allows for precise control of drivers altogether. Automatic vehicle plate recognition identifies cars that commit an infraction or, because of real-time searching, spot a vehicle that has been stolen or employed in a criminal offence.

Instead of using sensors in parking zone, a wise parking system supported video analytics finds drivers a vacant spot by analyzing images from cameras. These are some examples of the contributions that video analysis technology can make to create safer cities that are more pleasant to live.

### 15.7.2 SECURITY

Video surveillance is an old task of the safety domain. However, from the time that systems were monitored exclusively by humans to current supporting video analytics, with water passed under the bridge. Facility and license plate recognition (LPR) can be applied to recognize people and vehicles in real-time and make appropriate decisions. For instance, it is possible to search for a suspect in real-time and in stored video footage or recognize unauthorized personnel and grant access to a secured facility. Crowd management is another crucial function of the security system. Cutting edge analytics tools can make a huge difference in places such as shopping malls, hospitals, stadiums and airports. These tools can provide an approximated count in real-time and can trigger alerts when a threshold is reached or surpassed. They can also analyze flow to detect movement in prohibited or unwanted directions. This is one of the advantages of these approaches: video content analysis systems are often trained to detect specific events, sometimes with a high degree of refinement. One example is to detect fires as soon as possible. Or, in the case of airports, to raise an alert when someone walks against the intended direction for passengers or enters a forbidden area. Another great case is the real-time detection of unattended baggage in a public place. As traditional tasks like intruder detection, they will be performed robustly, because of algorithms which will filter motion caused by wind, rain, snow or animals. The functionality offered by intelligent video analysis is growing day by day within the security domain, and this is on a trend that will continue within the future.

## 15.8 CONCLUSION

Video analytics solutions are helpful in our daily tasks. There is a wide range of sectors that can benefit from this technology, especially as the complication of potential applications has been flourishing in recent years. From smart cities to security controls in hospitals and airports to people tracking for retail and shopping centres, the field of video analytics enables processes that are synchronously more effective and less tedious for humans, and less expensive for companies.

Apart from the above applications, it can be used for several applications in different fields depending on the scenario to minimize losses and translate to big profits. The contributions of video analysis technology can help to build safer cities that are more pleasant to live. The functionality offered by intelligent video analysis is growing day by day within the security domain, and this is on a trend that will continue within the future.
