Expert committees should be available to guide employees throughout the process. Another important role is played by strong AI journalism. In order for end users, developers, companies and governments to understand the technical and ethical aspects of AI, responsible journalism will play a crucial role. Rather than fearmong and sensationalization, journalism in this field needs to be informative and wide enough to present different perspectives of the stakeholders.

The discussions of all the ethical issues pertaining to AI may seem to be painting a very gloomy picture of the technology for society. However, it has to be understood that every new technological advancement has faced similar concerns. Introduction of automobiles, to industrial machines, computers and IT software, such concerns have always existed and have been handled with deeper understanding of relationship between human and technology. AI technologies, too, will go through this learning curve where the stakeholders will come up with better solutions through continuous collaborations and knowledge sharing.
