# 12.2 ANALYZING PROMINENT STUDENTS

Hansen et al. [5] analyzed time series data with the help of exponential smoothing,
auto regressive moving average (ARIMA), partial adaptive ARIMA
models and ML models to capture the characteristics of the trends, cycles, seasonal
random components. The results show that support vector ML weakly dominates
other models and achieved best results in nine different data sets.

Carbonneau et al. [6] used advanced Machine Learning techniques including nei-
work, recurrent neural network and support vector machine for the purpose
forecast supply demand distorted by bullwhip effect. They made
comparison of results with traditional models, i.e., naive forecasting, and mov-
ing average and linear regression. Their findings suggest that Machine Learning
teiques are superior to the traditional techniques but the difference in forecasted
results is statistically significant. This means they are not providing a drastic
improvement over traditionally used techniques.

Choudhary and Garg [7] used hybrid ML based on Genetic Algorithm (GA) and
Support Vector Machine (SVM) to forecast stock market data. Their findings show
that GA-SVM outperforms the standalone SVM system. The continuous
success of technical analyst to gain from market strategies raised the question
against Efficient Market Hypothesis (EMH). In the current decade, there is increasing
momentum of the use of AI techniques for the purpose of forecasting successful
results. By these consequences, in the present scenario, EMH is used by researchers
as null hypothesis to forecast stock market data.

Tsai and Wang [8] tried to forecast stock prices of the Taiwan stock exchange
listed companies using hybrid ML techniques. The use of decision tree (DT) and
artificial neural network (ANN) had better predictive ability. The hybrid model
(DT+ANN) gave 77% forecasting accuracy. Their findings suggest that it is
based on biological nervous system like human brain has good forecasting ability
but lacking explanatory outputs. Their suggestion is to model stock
prices using SVMs, GA can be used to obtain better prediction.

Ahmed et al. [9] tried to forecast M3 data consisting of 3,003 daily, quarterly and
monthly time series. The data set was generated to organize a competition by the
International Journal of Forecasting [10]. The models used for comparison were mul-
tilayer percept, Bayesian neural network, Radial basis functions, generalized
regression neural network (also called Kernel Regression), Classification and
Regression Trees (CART) regression trees, support vector regression and Gaussian
processes. These methods are used in conjunction with the ML forecasting models.
The findings suggest that two models as multilayer perceptron (MLP) and Gaussian
Propagation (GP) are better performer to others used models.
