In this approach, data is fed to an algorithm and the machine tries to recognize the relationship between the input and the output. When the machine has stopped learning – in other words, it has learned optimally – the learned model can predict the value or class of new data points. For example, a system can be trained to differentiate between a kangaroo and a koala. By feeding the system with dozens of images of both animals, the system will learn about the features which distinguish each and improve its prediction. In unsupervised learning, data is analyzed without trying to make any predictions. It is focused on learning and understanding the underlying structural properties and associations between the observed data. This kind of learning can be used in detecting outliers, classifying and segmenting customers and the market. In reinforcement ML, the system does not have historical data to draw conclusions; instead, the algorithm learns by taking different actions and evaluating its successes and failures. Reinforcement learning is used by Facebook in advertising on it when it flighted for the first time. # 1.2 AI, ML AND DATA SCIENCE

We see AI, ML and data science use together, and they are considered to be lucrative career options today. Data science can be defined as a broad field of study which pertains to data systems and processes which are aimed at maintaining datas and deriving meaning from them. With the advent of technology and the Internet, today all organizations generate a large volume of data through their daily transactions, and it becomes problematic for these organizations to monitor, store, organize and extract important information from this data. Data scientists use a combination of tools, applications and principles to extract useful information from various random data clusters, and then use this to guide business processes to reach organization goals. The information extracted can be used to ongoing data trends in any field of business and is helpful in presenting business forecasts and setting courses of action based on insights and inferences made. The best example of ML is Netflix suggesting movies to the customers based on their movie-viewing behaviour and recommending books based on the past purchases of customers on the website. With the help of ML, marketers provide customers with customized content as well as other products that they may wish to purchase.

ML is a field of study that gives computers an ability to learn without being explicitly programmed, whereas data science deals with extracting knowledge from data. Deep learning is a big artificial neural network that mimics the network of neurons in a human brain. It is a subset of ML and is called “deep learning” because it uses deep neural networks for the learning.
