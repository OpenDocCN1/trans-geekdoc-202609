# Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Business Management

# 4.2.1 AI AND CUSTOMER SERVICE

“AI is interested in intelligent performance and behaviours by machines, computers or robots that are used to assist humans and businesses,” [9]. According to a report published by World Economic Forum (2018) in collaboration with Deloitte, about 76% of banking industry chief executive officers agree that AI is a priority because it is critical for providing smart customer service through its chatbots, personalized financial services, smart wallets, assisted customer support, blockchained payment ect. Banks require the adoption of AI and ML in order to deliver high level customer service, to retain the customers and understand the personal banking habits and needs of the customers to get a better customer experience. Through the use of AI, banks are using their customer experience and also increasing the number of customers. The big banks like SBI, PNB and HDFC are adopting AI tools such as chatbot, natural language processing (NLP) and Cognitive computing to offer personalized services to customers, round the clock support users, and to the analysis of transactions and loans ‘Personnetics’, one of the London based company using its chatbots, identify patterns in information and can do predictive analytics. Through this analysis it can guess what the next likely interaction from customers’ side AI. It not only help banks to provide innovative customer service, but also helps to determineworthiness, streamlining the lending process and improve customer experience for borrowers.

# 4.2.1.1 Chatbots

Chatbot is AI software which simplifies the interactions between user and machines in a natural language. It helps banks to enhance the customer experience, improve the customer engagement, magnify the operational efficiency and reduce the customer service cost. The Financial sector in India has seen tremendous investments into chatbots and AI to augment customer service,”[11]. AI-driven chatbots are widely adopted and are easy to deploy and can be used in various areas of activities like online customer support, phone interaction, answer product and company related queries. It generally deploys these 24/7 to engage and interact with customers. Renewed platforms of chatbots in 2020 include Mobile-money, Chatfuel and its Alive. Allahabad Bank is also developing and applying its own mobile chatbot named “Empower”.
