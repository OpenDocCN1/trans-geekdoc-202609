# 6.12 CANCER SCREENING AND MACHINE LEARNING

AI is provided with a tremendous scope for interventions in cancer screening and its
treatment. Every year, more than one million new cases are registered in India. It is
likely to increase with the changing lifestyle and increasing age of population in
India. Early detection and management are indispensable for a positive cure of
cancer. There is great need of qualitative pathology service for treating cancer which
is only available in some selected Indian cities. There are barely 2,000 pathologists
who have experience in oncology. There are less than 500 pathologists who could be
called expert oncologists. ML solutions could assist a general pathologist to
for a quality diagnosis which would bridge the gap of providing essential healthcare.
Quality annotated pathology datasets are much required to bring out a solution.
ITI Aayog is developing a national repository to solve the problem. It can be termed
“Digital Pathology”.
