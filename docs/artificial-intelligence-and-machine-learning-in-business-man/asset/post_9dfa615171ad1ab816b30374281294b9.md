## 5.1 INTRODUCTION

A credit is a plastic card consisting of a thin rectangular slab including a magnetic strip which is issued by the financial institution to its customers. These cards are provided to the customers so that they can buy items without paying cash or cheque. The financial institution pre-sets the limit of the cards before giving it to customers as per their monthly income and fraud is defined as wrong activity carried out by illegitimate persons by misrepresenting themselves for money or property gain. Therefore, credit card scams are nothing but gaining of confidential information like password, CVV number, etc. by the intruders. So, we need credit fraud detection technique is required to protect the cardholders from false activity.

India is on its way to becoming a developed country. To achieve this, the Government of India (GoI) has launched several initiatives, and one of these is the Digital India Campaign. The main intent of this initiative is to empower the nation through cashless payments using debit card, credit card, net banking, UPI. The government and RBI have focused immensely on digitalizing the economy. This is one of the important benefits of digital transaction is that it saves time. Customers no longer have to visit ATMs and stand in a queue to withdraw money. Whenever they want to make payment, they have to swipe the card and enter the PIN.
