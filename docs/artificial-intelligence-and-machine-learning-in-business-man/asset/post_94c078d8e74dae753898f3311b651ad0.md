## Artificial Intelligence in Marketing

## 1.5 MARKETING PLAN AND AI’S POTENTIAL

Applications of AI are very broad in nature. With the help of AI abilities trend to detect and extrapolate patterns in data, marketers can identify opportunities as well as threats, and act on them in real time. Following are the ways in which we can leverage AI’s potential across different stages of a marketing plan.

### Stage 1: Analyzing the Current Situation

This stage involves the analysis of macroenvironmental factors and future business environments by analyzing the current situation and understanding the business environment [6]. Marketers use SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) analysis for getting in-depth understanding of targeted market. With the help of AI assisted features like social listening, getting data-driven information on purchasing patterns and customer satisfaction can assess and estimate demand market trend and respective customer. With the help of ML, consumer research investigated online forums and suggested that brand should not just focus on itself while analyzing competitor brands this can bring an understanding of competitors’ reactions.

### Stage 2: Understanding Markets and Customers

This stage involves the analysis of microenvironmental factors. Organizations try to develop understanding related to consumer behaviour factors affecting their loyalty, attitude, purchasing patterns product category and market demand. AI can assist marketers in processing vast data available through social media accounts, mobile activity searches and contact interactions. For example, at Consumer Electronics Show in Las Vegas 2019, Data Labs and Promobot (a robotics manufacturer) demonstrated emotion detection for the purpose of customer experience [6]. AI-enabled system uses a combination of eye movement, heart rate voice gestures and facial expressions to determine customers’ emotional states. Rosbank is using this technology in call center and collects changes in voice volume speech conversion time [7].

### Stage 3: Segmenting, Targeting and Positioning

This stage involves extensive consumer research on certain criteria that may enable marketers to communicate messages precisely and develop products according to targeted customers’ requirements. AI can assist in predicting customers intent as well as gaining more insights through tailored promotions. For example, marketing algorithms named Albert were responsible for 40% of Harley Davidson's motorbike sales during 2016. Despite certain advantages of AI in segmenting and targeting, it can also lead to unintended price discrimination whom to offer products and services [9].

### Stage 4: Planning Objectives and Marketing Support

This stage involves development of short-term objectives, long-term goals, formulation of future strategies and customer service (Campbell et al., 2020). AI can assist marketers by integrating chatbots on websites, apps and social media channels to encourage sales (Wood, 2011). 24% of businesses in the US use AI-aided customer services and 54% are using ways in which customer services teams can use AI. In previous research it was found between 2019 and 2021 that the majority of customers prefer talking to a human over chatbot, AI can still provide back-end support to better match customers according to their specialization.

### Stage 5: Developing a Product Strategy

This stage involves development and selection of products to be offered by the brand involving decisions related to features, quality, design and customization of products. Insights on target customer positioning can be used by marketers for developing successful products. In this stage, AI can assist in product delivery and logistics [6]. For example, Samsung is taking AI assistance in understanding what their existing and potential customers are saying about them. This helps Samsung in improving their products and appropriately designing their campaigns. Likewise, Nike is also using AI-enabled clustering algorithms and data collected from various geographical locations through its apps to manage collection which products should be displayed together.

### Stage 6: Developing Pricing Strategy

This stage involves understanding target customers' price sensitivity, competitors' pricing strategies and accordingly deciding on how much to charge for their offering in order to maximize profits. In this stage AI can assist the firm in tracking buying trends and accordingly enabling dynamic pricing (e.g., surge pricing) and detecting related anomalies (e.g., non-profitable customers, cases of fraud and pricing errors) [12]. For example, Airbnb employees use AI and ML to suggest to customers in settings what pricing to set for each date by analyzing data on seasonal changes, location, special property attributes and local events [13].

### Stage 7: Developing Channels and Logistics Strategy

This stage involves developing strategies related to logistics or channels that include whole distribution channels, retail channels and direct distribution channels. AI can help managers in optimizing and predicting distribution, inventory, store display and layout. For example, Afresh uses AI-assisted management of stock and inventory [6], as does Browse a social commerce app that transforms posted pictures into shoppable content [14]. These types of platform technologies enable consumers to take pictures anywhere and make these items in the images shoppable.

### Stage 8: Developing Marketing Communication and Influence Strategy

In this stage a firm develops a communication strategy in order to create and enhance the brand's image in the customers mind by informing them about their offerings and benefits related to it. AI can help marketers in this stage by targeting advertisements to the right customers by analyzing their search history as real-time data obtained from the point of purchase. Brands with the help of AI can place interactive advertisements in the videos depending on the content of the videos [15]. For example, LEGO used interactive advertisements by training and feeding AI systems with the knowledge of wide range of LEGO products offerings [16].

### Stage 9: Planning Metrics and Implementation Control

The final stage involves monitoring the performance metrics of the previous development stages, identifying the problems and taking corrective actions in order to increase the efficiency. The most important aspect of this stage is identifying the anomalies in the metrics and deciding how to respond to them. In this stage AI can replace humans as it has the capability to collect and analyze vast amounts data obtained from real-time environment as well as historical data. AI can also be used in assessing the performance of online features and advertisements and continuously providing customers with relevant content [6].

In 2016 AI saw a 43% adoption rate which increased to 88% in 2019 as marketers realized the true potential of the AI that it helps them create better solutions for marketing related problems.
