The other aspect of privacy is when AI applications like computer vision, voice assistant and other monitoring devices/applications sneakly collect your data without you. In one example, researchers have done experiments that Amazon echo often starts recording in absence of official wake words [6]. These devices may collect your data, and use it in improving performance without any intention. You may say that these things were mentioned in the 'Terms & Conditions' which users accept; however, we know that no one ever reads these agreements. Tech companies have done excellent job of loading these agreements with complex jargon, that it is almost impossible for a regular user to interpret it. Rather than putting all the liability on users for maintaining their privacy, companies need to come up with solutions that can address this problem.

# 14.2.4 MISINFORMATION, DISINFORMATION AND FAKE NEWS

After the 2016 US elections, investigations in U.S. found presence of co-ordination media campaigns by a Russian "troll farm", the Internet Research Agency [7] (IRA). The focus of these campaigns was to create an atmosphere of anger and division in the country. IRA had hired round the clock who stole identities of real Americans to create fake profiles, with thousands of Americans to understand their behaviour, created fake information and posts and rumours, posted content that appropriately blends in American population without raising a doubt and were said to have such impact on the harmony and stability of atmosphere in the US around the elections [9].

For achieving these outcomes, the troll farm had invested millions of dollars and hours of human resource. Today, to achieve similar or more potent results the amount of human involvement and money troll farm would have to invest by lesser, thanks to advancements in AI technology. High powered language generators can create thousands of authentic posts by humans in no time. AI application can now allow you to create realistic pictures and videos of people who never existed. Deepfake technology allows anyone to create highly believable videos of people. These incidents have raised serious issues of misinformation, disinformation and fake issues on social media. Mis occurs when the inaccuracy is unintentional, while disinformation refers to intentionally misleading information.
