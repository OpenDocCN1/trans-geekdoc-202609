These have drastically improved our capability to process large amount of data generated in today's sensor-enabled world. Insights from this data have the potential to transform human lives for good. However, like any other technology, AI is bound to go through a learning curve where unintended outcomes might negatively affect the recipients. The community today is not just limited to scientists, developers and engineers, but also involves all the stakeholders who are in one or another impacted by these solutions. These includes citizens, industries, governments, policy makers, lawmakers and most other members society. In order to promote trustworthy, holistic and responsible development of this technology for greater good of society, it is important that all stakeholders are sensitized to various ethical aspects of this technology. This chapter aims to highlight such ethical issues and approaches that are currently being undertaken to address these issues.

# 14.2 ETHICAL ISSUES WITH AI APPLICATIONS

Ethical Issues with AI applications are shown below in Figure 14.1.
Despite being an immensely powerful and potentially game-changing technology, AI applications are raising serious ethical concerns about their use. The level of current AI application is not as advanced as give rise to a robot uprising and put human existence in danger. Such concerns may form an interesting plot of science movies but are not the immediate pressing issues for society. The immediate serious concerns are related to issues like power imbalance and societal divide that AI can create, labour issues that will be raised, privacy of users and serious threats to democracy by disinformation capabilities powered by AI tools.
