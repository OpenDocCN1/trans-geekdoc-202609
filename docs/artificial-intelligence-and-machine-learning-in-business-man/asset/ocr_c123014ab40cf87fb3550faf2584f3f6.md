# 13.2 RELATED WORK

Various Learning-based prediction models have been implemented by many researchers to enhance the accuracy of the model. The standard deviation of daily forex data in the logarithm rate over a statistical model [1], weighted average of daily high and low-forecast rates are empirically better than daily close price. Forecasting of forex [2] rates using ANN (i.e., Standard Back-propagation (SBP), Scaled Conjugate Gradient (SCG) and Backpropagation with Bayesian Regularization (BPR)) outperformed ARIMA model. Moving average of different technical indicators are used to develop and investigate estimation, where SCG gave the best output among three ANN. Analysis of financial markets [3] applying ANN in USD vs GBP predicted with low values of MSE and conjunction with other forms of technical & fundamental analysis. Short-term exchange rate predictions are generally more accurate [4] than long-term. Use of weekly, monthly and quarterly datasets can increase the accuracy in long-term prediction. In comparison of different frequencies of input [5], one week ahead is better than one day & month ahead because weekly data have appropriate fluctuation information of exchange rate. To improve performance of the model, many researchers [6,7] have developed hybrid model merging individual methods. To increase accuracy of ensemble model, each individual performance must be good. A hybrid ANN applying logistic regressive models [8], genetically optimized adaptive ANN [9] to evolve the structure and multilayer neural networks. The outcome of the proposed model succeeded in achieving significant improvement in accuracy for we ahead prediction. Another hybrid structure [10] consisting of two parallel systems (Least Mean Square (LMS) and Adaptive Functional Artificial Neural Network (FLANN)) provides more accurate and efficient result than individual LMS and FLANN. Return Genetic Programming [11] has ability to select the best possible feature, network architecture and connectivity pattern to decide whether to use a recurrent connection or a feed-forward connection. The accuracy of the model increased with number of feedback paths and improves the capabilities of the network. As exchange rate forecasting became popular, use of various ANN techniques increased. The newly developed Deep Belief technique [12,13] had some improvements and gave better results than typical forecasting methods (such as Feed-Forward Neural Network (FFNN)).
