## Preface

The focus of this book is to introduce Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) technologies in the context of Business Management. With the maturing of AI or ML in the field of business intelligence, this book examines examples of innovative AI beyond organizational limits. It follows the Predictive Modeling Toolkit for providing insight on how to use AI tools in the field of business. Taking a multidisciplinary approach for using AI, this book provides a comprehensive reference resource for undergraduate, graduate, business professionals, and related disciplines.

Nowadays, AI improves in the area of supply chain to track the product, to provide intelligence in industry field, to finance field etc. In this regard, the traditional approach of teaching and learning is not meeting the requirement of the policies of Industry 4.0. Here, we edited this book to minimize the gap between academia and industry.

The book is organized in 15 chapters:

Chapter 1, Artificial Intelligence in Marketing, discusses how AI help to the making people better understand the speech (recognition) by applying techniques like text mining, with latest advancement in programming it has made modelling of direct marketing responses and predicting churn using classification trees and machines.

Chapter 2, Consumer Insights through Retail Analytics, describes an in depth understanding on how Indian retail companies such as ‘Shoppers Stop’ and ‘Future Group’ are exclusive among customers’ and other transaction data to predict their future purchase behaviour and design product placement strategies.

Chapter 3, Multi-Agent Paradigm for B2C E-Commerce, discusses the business applications of KBS, CBR, ANN, GA and MAS along with negotiation, customer relationship management and customer orientation. From computational point of view, agent characteristics, multi-agent paradigm and its communication protocol also been discussed.
