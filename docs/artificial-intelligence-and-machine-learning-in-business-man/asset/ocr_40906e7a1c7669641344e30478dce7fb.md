# 6.17 CONCLUSION

ML is known as the most awaited applications of AI intelligence. It unfolds numerous ways to support the healthcare policy. It takes care of a large number of people by adopting preventive strategies. It revolves the patient-centred approach that covers patient advocacy and effective management of workforce and resources. The growing use of ML would offer tremendous facilities for India to provide appropriate healthcare system to a large section of population. It inspire the government in meeting the needs of common people in healthcare sector. In fact, ML is expected as a launching pad for making healthcare services more proactive – a welcome from “sick” care to “health” care.
