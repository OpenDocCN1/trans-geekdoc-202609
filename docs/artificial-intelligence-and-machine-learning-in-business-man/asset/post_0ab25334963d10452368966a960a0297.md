| Implantable technologies | The first implantable mobile commercially available | People are getting more closely connected with their devices and the devices are not just worn but also implanted into bodies, enabling communications, location and vital health monitoring. Cochlear implants, tooth implants and pacemakers are now examples. A biodegradable pill developed by Novartis has a biodegradable device which communicates with the mobile phone to inform on the drug kinetics. Google Glass is an example of how eyeglasses, eyewear/headsets can become intelligent and connect to the Internet. These glasses allow the user to manipulate an object in three dimensions. |
| New Interface of Vision | 10% of reading connected to the Internet | |
| Wearable Internet | 10% of people wear clothes connected to the Internet | The mobile technology is going closer than a phone by embedding a chip to connect to the articles and the owner. Useful for monitoring of infants or individuals. |
| IoT | 1 trillion sensors will be connected to the Internet | With hardware prices, it is economical to connect anything to the Internet. Intelligent sensors are available at competitive prices. The devices commented to the internet, we will be able to communicate with one other. |
| Smart Cities | The first city with least 50,000 people and no traffic lights | The smart cities connect the services, roads or streets to the internet. The smart cities manage their energy consumption, the flow of materials, logistics and project tasks. Singapore and Barcelona have already rolling out data-driven services, smart parking system and also the automatic trash collection. |
| Paradigm Shift Big Data and Decision making | Trigger Point The first government to replace census by big data sources | Governments may start to turn to big data technologies for automating their populist movements to deliver innovative services. | Robotics in Services | Robotic pharmacist | Robotics is 75% of modern automobile is done by robots. Robots in the service industry will be the next wave. |
| 3D Printing and Manufacturing | First 3D printed car in production | Additive manufacturing or 3D printing can create complex objects without equipment. The printer would be able to print multiple objects from various materials such as plastic, aluminum, ceramic or others. This would replace a host of equipment in factories making them smaller. |
