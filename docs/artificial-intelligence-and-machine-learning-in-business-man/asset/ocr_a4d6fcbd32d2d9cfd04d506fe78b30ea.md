Through the execution of AI bank is attempting to lessen the turnaround time (TAT), the bank has realized AI over 125+ cycles and intellectual mechanization around 90 cycles, which required tedious physical work. It is encountered that with the utilization of RPA there is obvious decrease in TAT, time spent in investment account opening has diminished by 90%, on current records by 92%, and on different cycles by 50% to 80%.

According Sameer Shetty, Head of digital banking (Axis Bank) fraud has declining due to the rise of authentication and security systems and he even stated that Axis has no digital banking identity fraud incidents, due to multilayered defence system that incorporates customer authentication and AI. Through the implementation of AI Axis' cross-referencing customers' identifying information with India's national identification number system, Axis verifies that the number customer presents is the same as they have in the national database in order to confirm their identity with the objective to reduce the fraud and anti-money-laundering. This instant verification system has made synthetic fraud nearly impossible, as the instant cross-referencing with government databases means that any made-up identification number will be discovered immediately. On the onboarding system is supplemented by an AI platform that looks for signs of account takeovers, like mismatched location data or unusual transactions.
