## 4.2.1 AI AND CUSTOMER SERVICE

“AI is interested in intelligent performance and behaviours by machines, computers or robots that are used to assist humans and businesses,” [9]. According to a report published by World Economic Forum (2018) in collaboration with Deloitte, about 76% of banking industry chief executive officers agree that AI is a priority because it is critical for providing smart customer service through its chatbots, personalized financial services, smart wallets, assisted customer support, blockchained payment ect. Banks require the adoption of AI and ML in order to deliver high-level customer service, to retain the customers and understand the personal banking habits and needs of the customers to get a better customer experience. Through the use of AI, banks are using their customer experience and also increasing the number of customers. The big banks like SBI, PNB and HDFC are adopting AI tools such as chatbot, natural language processing (NLP) and Cognitive computing to offer personalized services to customers, round the clock support users, and to the analysis of transactions and loans ‘Personnetics’, one of the London based company using its chatbots, identify patterns in information and can do predictive analytics. Through this analysis it can guess what the next likely interaction from customers’ side AI. It not only help banks to provide innovative customer service, but also helps to determine worthiness, streamlining the lending process and improve customer experience for borrowers.

### 4.2.1.1 Chatbots

Chatbot is AI software which simplifies the interactions between user and machines in a natural language. It helps banks to enhance the customer experience, improve the customer engagement, magnify the operational efficiency and reduce the customer service cost. The Financial sector in India has seen tremendous investments into chatbots and AI to augment customer service,”[11]. AI-driven chatbots are widely adopted and are easy to deploy and can be used in various areas of activities like online customer support, phone interaction, answer product and company related queries. Allahabad Bank is also developing and applying its own mobile chatbot named “Empower”.

### 4.2.1.2 AI and Personalized Banking

The banks using the existing data through digital processing, deliver meaningful and powerful personalized service to their customers. Through AI banks are focusing on understanding customer needs on the basis of their requirements, taste, moods and resources. This creates a win-win situation for both banks and customers, but it drives a competitive advantage to the first mover banks. AI truly shines when it comes to exploring new ways to provide additional benefits and comfort to individual users. These Apps, by tracking the personal data income, recurring expenses, spending habits and working experience, can suggest financial tips and optimized plans to customers. Some of the biggest US banks like Bank of America and Wells Fargo, through their mobile banking apps provide different services to their clients like sending reminders to pay electric bills, plan their financial transactions and connect with their banks in a streamlined way.

### 4.2.1.3 Smart Wallets

Digital wallets, otherwise known as smart wallets, refers to make digitization payment through AI application by one party to another party through digital currency for goods and services. In the current scenario digital payments is the future world payment technology. In order to reduce the dependence of physical cash payments and to increase the reach of money at higher level banks are implementing AI technology. Adoption of AI application in the banking industry promoted the use of smart wallets and digital wallets like Google, Apple, PayPal and others developed their payment gateways. Through the digital wallets banks are storing the user's payment information and passwords for making payment.

### 4.2.1.4 Voice Assisted Banking

Now a days of AI influence customers to get accustomed with voice command and touch screen banking service as a result of which voice assisted and physical presence banking are vanishing away. It helps to process queries to answer questions, provides the necessary information and helps the customers to get various banking services.

### 4.2.1.5 Robo Advice

For banks, regulations play an important role and AI also helps the banks in all regard. All collected data are carefully checked, organized and analyzed before forwarding for further decisions. Without the interference of AI, the entire work would be labour intensive which results in time consuming and resulted/prone to mistakes. This kind of solution provided by AI can be to some extent called as RPA. ### 4.2.1.6 AI Backed Blockchain for Expenditure Payments

It is a digital record of transaction where the individual records called blocks are linked together in single list called chain and it is for recommending transactions made with cryptocurrency as Bitcoin. Through the AI assisted blockchain the banks have replaced the expedite payments with sluggish payments. ### 4.2.2.1 Determining Credit Scoring and Lending Decisions

According to the International Data Corporation report, financial companies will spend $11 million to operate with AI and ML by 2020. By the estimate AI-based developments, including underwriting and AI credit scoring, financial sector will be to increase their GDP by as much as 10% in less than a decade (Volodymyr Soffininsky, Credit data scientist at Datacentrix). Many companies like Zestfinance and Lendo have developed their AI for credit scoring and the ML they are trying to determine the creditworthiness of individual customers or how likely a particular customer can be. These companies look at potential applicants on the basis of variables like Internet browsing histories, geolocation, social media accounts; ML turns all this data into credit score which are awarded by banks to determine the credit score of the individual customer. On the other hand, one of the start-up companies, Ustart, is using the modern science data to automate the loan process. This focuses on young adult's education. SAT score, GPT, job history; field of study and by ML predicts creditworthiness. Now a AI is to a better solution for banks to provide credit score on the basis of more data. AI takes into consideration many factors like income, employment status, work experience, credit history educational background on the basis of which bank grants loans to clients. ML provides best technological solution by developing the risk scoring models. These models predict forecast about the difficulty against credit supply to a particular client.
