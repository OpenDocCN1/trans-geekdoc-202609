Chang et al. [12] developed a grey silhouette coefficient to create an applicable hybrid model to forecast small samples. Before using the grey silhouette coefficient model of the small models are used to find the tendency of a real sequence. The findings shows that use of multimodal procedure reduce the predictive errors and improve forecasting results for small samples. Authors in this study claimed that popular forecasting techniques such as statistical methods, data mining and ANNs are incapable to accurately forecast the small samples.

Chen et al. [13] tried to predict movie goals based on weblogs and real time social media to better enhance income and expenditure. GA used for feature selection and five ML models, i.e., multiple linear regression (MLR), Decision tree regression (DTR), Random forest regression (RFR), support vector regression (SVR), and neural network regression (NNR) to predict weekly movie goals. The findings suggest that the predictive ability of the ML models are as high as linear models used for predictive work.

Yildiz et al. [14] did comparative analysis of regression and ML models to forecast electricity cost of local building to minimize environmental hazards and enhance economic benefit. The ANN and Bayesian Regression Backpropagation have lowest mean percentage error in case of modelling campus load than single building. While the regression model still better performed compared to adjusted ML models.

Makridakis et al. [15] applied eight families of Machine Learning models called MLP, Bayesian Neural Network (BNN), Radial Basis Functions (RBF), Generalized Regression Neural Network (GRNN), also called Kernel Regression, K-Nearest Neighbour Regression (KNN), CART Regression Trees (KART), SVR and Gaussian Processes (GP). The ranking of these models based on symmetric mean absolute percentage error (SMAPE), MLP was ranked one BNN, GP, GRNN, KNN, SVR, CART and BERT ranked. Along with this they used two models called recurrent neural network (RNN) and Long Short-Term Memory Network (LSTM). The comparison of top M3 models with six statistical methods (SES), Holt and Damped exponential smoothing and the fifth is combination of all three exponential smoothing method, the sixth model used called Theta method. The Theta method achieved superior result compared to M3 models. These findings show though MLS have superiority over traditionally used models but not significant.

Mohagham et al. [16] used a ML approach to find flood susceptibility of Haraz watershed (Mazandaran province in Iran). The eight models used by them are ANN, CART, Discriminant Analysis (FDA), Generalized Linear Model (GLM), Generalized Additive Model (GAM), Boosted Regression (BRT), Multivariate Adaptive Regression Splines (MARS), and Maximum Entropy (MaxEnt), and ensemble models such as Ensemble Model committee averaging (EMca), Ensembles Model confidence interval Infer (EMcinf), Ensemble Model confidence interval Superior (EMciup), Ensemble Model to estimate the coefficient of variation (EMcv), Ensemble Model to estimate the mean (EMmean), Ensemble Model to estimate the median (EMmedian), Ensemble Model based on weighted mean (EMmean). The model's performance is statistically shown by the statistics area under the receiver operating characteristics (AUROC). All the models used for forecasting are highly significant as per the statistics all models value is less than 0.05 (Sig = 0.000). Among individual models, BRT and GML are the best models among Ensemble Median and EMmean are the best models used for the purpose of forecasting. There is no significant difference between ML models and Ensemble models still they favoured ensemble models more useful in view of generalization ability and sustainability in predictive accuracy.

Oh Su [17] used time lags of meteorological variables on a wave forecasting model. Use of ML such as ANN and a hybrid model was used to forecast significant wave heights and periods with the combination of empirical orthogonal function and wavelet analysis in short called EOFNN. The use of wavelet analysis empowered the ANN model to deal with non-stationary data. This study also analyzed lag effect of methodological data. The findings show the highest level of accuracy subject to condition of used time.

Pathak [18] used hybrid ML models to forecast chaotic process. ML techniques promising result compared to the knowledge-based models used in the past. The combination of ML and knowledge-based technique fills the non-defined gap of old techniques.

Pechacek [19] used Machine Learning algorithm to develop military retention prediction. The objective of this study was to predict when individual service members would leave the military. A high level of accuracy was achieved by the model as predictive accuracy of the model up to one year was 88%, up to four years 80%, and for to longer periods predictive accuracy was 78%.

Shi et al [20] studied predictive accuracy of speed used for wind energy. The proposed forecasting models based on spatial temporal correlation (SC) theory and Wavelet Coherence Transformation Analysis (WCT). The results show that there is strong correlation between adjacent wind turbine and targeted wind turbine. This study used deep neural network models and compared same with traditional methods used for wind speed. The findings show that mean square error (RMSE), mean absolute error (MAE), mean absolute percentage error (MAPE) for wind prediction are lowest for ML models. The overall concluded hybrid models used for wind prediction are better than those used in the future.

Sun et al [21] used ML and Internet search index to forecast tourist arrivals in China, the same compared with Google Index and Baidu Index. The Kernel Extreme Learning Machine (KELM) with integrated tourist volume series of Baidu Index and Google Index gave a better result. The modelling of tourist data in the form of “tourist volume series + Baidu index + Google index” provided more accuracy and robust results. This completes the studies formed by [22,23].

Feizabadi [24] used ML techniques to forecast demand and supply chain performance. ARIMAX and Neural Network in their hybrid form used to forecast variance amplification emanating of demand and supply chain information from multi-stage supply chain distortion. The modelling with ML significantly improved the forecasted results.
