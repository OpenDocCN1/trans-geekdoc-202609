# 7.2 TEMPORAL DISPLACEMENT OF CARE

Healthcare providers can build value for themselves and individual patients who have autoimmune disease through IT and practicing the replacement of time where patients and physicians make interventions. The theory deals with when tasks and services are consumed by a group in the treatment of chronic disease, which also includes how an individual is being treated. The assumptions made by [16] are restricted to lifestyle chronic disorders and do not include infectious treatment or protocols to be followed in the treatment of acute diseases. However, there are some cases available in the present situation, where the theory stands true for acute and infectious conditions also. Patients with lifestyle disorders will be happy to engage and collaborate during their treatment period prescribed by the provider.
