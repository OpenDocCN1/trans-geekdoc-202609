# 14.2.2.1 What Kind of Jobs Are Most Likely to Be Impacted by the Threat of AI?

The key capability of AI/ML as a technology is to be able to predict events efficiently. AI applications for job application filtering to predict which candidate is mostly likely to perform well on the job, AI applications for medical diagnostic may try to predict if a tumour is likely to be cancerous or benign, autonomous driving vehicle to predict the next move of vehicles/pedestrian on the road and accordingly making decisions. These and many other examples of AI systems basically rely on the algorithm's efficiency to predict an outcome. The effect these systems will on the workforce depend on the importance of this prediction capability in the jobs. For jobs that are completely dependent on the prediction ability before forecasting, legal summary, email response by executive assistants, the threat of replacement would be strong. However, for jobs that are a mix of prediction and some other kind of skills like medicine, diagnostics or education where the decision-making skill, human touch of the practitioner is also important AI application may complement the existing jobs but not completely replace it [4].
