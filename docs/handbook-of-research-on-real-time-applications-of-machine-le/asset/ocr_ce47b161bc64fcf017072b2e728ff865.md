# # Data Visualization

Graphical representations of data, information, intermediate results, exploratory data analysis, signal representation are easy ways to understand inherent patterns, trends and even outliers. The visualization helps to extract latent correlations between various physical entities. Visualizations in predictive maintenance serves the following purposes:

+   • Deeper insights about the various physical quantities can be obtained by drilling into the data
+   • Higher degree of dynamicity
+   • Visualizations can effectively portray the results of various pre-processing, signal extraction and predictive models.
+   • They are time saving approach and are effective communication tools about predictive maintenance
