V = blake2b (T, U)

Finally, a signature will be generated using

ED25519, ζ = Sign (KI, V)

The ciphertexts T in Eq. (2), U in Eq. (3), and the signature ζ in Eq. (5) are ready to be uploaded into the database after the generation of the signature.

If the doctor wants to access the EHR of any patient, then the ciphertexts and the signature ζ in Eq. (5) are downloaded. If the ζ in Eq. (5) is valid, and the doctor possesses the required attributes used as attribute keys while encrypting, then they will be allowed to decrypt the U in Eq. (3) to obtain the Skey using which they can decrypt the EHR available in T Eq. (2).
