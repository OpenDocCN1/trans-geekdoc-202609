# MANPOWER AND HUMAN RESOURCE DEVELOPMENT: CYBER PHYSICAL SYSTEMS

Cyber-Physical Systems since become an important technology in the field of computing and information technology therefore this is the high time to introduce this field as an emerging technology within the subject IT and Computing. Even in allied subjects such as Communication Technology, Network Technology also this Cyber-Physical Systems is growing rapidly. Many universities internationally not only included Cyber-Physical Systems as a course or important area within their field of study but also as a specialization and full-fledged subject for making the society smarter, advanced and intelligent (Refer Fig: 3).
