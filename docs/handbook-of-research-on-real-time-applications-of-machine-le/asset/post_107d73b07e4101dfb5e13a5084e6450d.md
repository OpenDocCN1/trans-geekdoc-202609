### Congruence Graph Labelling

Labelling of graphs has been introduced by A. Rosa in 1966. Assignment of natural numbers to vertices and/or integers is referred as graph labelling. Congruence Graph labelling performs significant role in numerous sectors and can be implemented in multitudinous discipline including coding theory, X-ray, Psychology, crystallography, circuit design, communication networks, astronomy, radar, data security, secret sharing and data base management. ### Odd-Even Congruence Labelling

Suppose G = (V,E) be a graph with |V| = p and |E| = q is stated as an odd-even congruence graph, if there exist a vertex labelling function f: V → {1,3.....2k+1} for every vi in V and induces edge labelling g: E → {2,4,6,....2k} such that f(v_i) ≡ f(v_j) mod g(e_i) for every e_i = v_i v_j, where k = min{2|V|, 2|E|}.

A graph which admits odd even congruence labelling is stated as odd even congruence graph.
