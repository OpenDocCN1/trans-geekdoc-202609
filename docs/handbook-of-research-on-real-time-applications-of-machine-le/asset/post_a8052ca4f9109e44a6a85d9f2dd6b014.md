## LATEST ADVANCEMENT APPLICATION OF CYBER PHYSICAL SYSTEMS

In this section the latest advancement application of CPS are presented.

### Green Buildings

A physical building that eliminates negative influences, and can create positive impacts, on our climate and natural environment is considered a Green Building. CPS plays a vigorous protagonist in the arena of green building infrastructure. It expands the construction process and saves time of human efforts. CPS mainly focuses on environmental parameters such as humidity, temperature, water level, light, etc., for constructing a green building. The environmental data collected by various sensors that sense real-time hotness, moisture, and activate actuators is implanted within the construction to sense environmental variations. Overall, the functionality enhances energy efficiency and reduces energy consumption and emissions of greenhouse gases.

### Medical CPS

Medical cyber-physical systems (MCPS) are the network of different medical care devices for critical healthcare management. To achieve quality in large hospitals, MCPS are used. MCPS contains a WSN to collect diagnostic data, and for health monitoring and drug administration of patients.

CPS in the medical field or MCPS is used in many health industries such as hospitals, pharma industries, diagnosis centers, patient live assistant systems, etc. The complexity of MCPS depends on the particular application used for the health monitoring environment. CPS architecture requires a large amount of data to maintain patient health, such as patient disease history, historic data, and categories of diseases, etc. (Kritzinger et al., 2018).

### Smart Grid Structure

The simple word ‘grid’ represents an electric grid, transmission lines network, power substations, transformers, and other electricity supplier devices. A smart grid structure is an electric system that depends on data acquisition methods. Smart grid contains an advanced part of cyber-physical system. Electrical grid controls will be part of the Smart Grid, along with automation technology and a cyber-physical system that can respond digitally to quickly changing energy demand (Al-Ali, et al., 2018).

### Remote Humanoid Robots

Humanoid robots are used to help human beings in many fields such as entertainment, education, healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, etc. The present scenario nevertheless calls for human involvement in a self-reliant environment to complete tasks that might be better accomplished with human-in-the-loop. Humans, machines, and software program structures are required to interact and understand each other and work together in an effective and stable way (Kim et al., 2021).

### Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)

ITS is not only for controlling road traffic but also provides a safety mechanism for road accidents and productive infrastructure utilization. An intelligent transportation system (ITS) is an advanced technology in the transportation area that increases the quality of transportation management and monitors the movement of all vehicles using the Global Positioning System (GPS). Apart from smart traffic monitoring, the intelligent transportation system is also used in several areas such as real-time parking management, E-toll collection, ambulance notification, vehicle speed tracker and alert, real-time traffic light management, collision avoidance systems, etc.

### Aeronautic Applications

The major and critical application of CPS in the field of aeronautics. Aeronautic applications include real-time air traffic management, autopilot management, aircraft behavior, system health monitoring, and aircraft landing, etc.

### Manufacturing

In a manufacturing environment, cyber-physical systems are used to automate the manufacturing process and regulate the entire manufacturing process. Thus, production processes are improved and better data communication occurs between suppliers and clients. Smart production gives extraordinary visibility controls across the supply chain, resulting in improved goods traceability and security. The significance of IoT and CPS in the manufacturing industry is rapidly growing. Sensors are used to predict device wear and analyze loads. Analytics reduces maintenance costs and improves overall process performance (Grassi et al., 2020).

## SECURITY ISSUES OF CYBER PHYSICAL SYSTEM

In the digital transformation of the industrial revolution, a major challenge is security, which is faced by cyber-physical systems, and the most important element in this digital era is modern cyberspace. CPS security systems need to balance the three characteristics: confidentiality, availability, and integrity. The primary goal of confidentiality is to protect data by preventing information from being improperly disclosed. Availability is the concept that an authentic system is accessible by an authorized computer user at any time. Integrity is the protection of system data from unauthorized modification of information (Bakirtzis et al., 2021).

Cyber security can be achieved by a sound structure of information and specialized experts in the field of cyber-physical systems (Gupta et al., 2020). In this unit, we describe vulnerabilities of cyber-physical systems in Industry 4.0 by classifying attacks according to the following two target categories:

- Vulnerabilities in physical devices
- Vulnerabilities in intermediate networks

### Vulnerabilities in Physical Devices

Cyber-physical systems integrate with several physical devices with computation and communication. Physical devices include robotic arms, wireless sensors, robotic vehicles, automated machines, monitoring devices, etc. Several types of internal or external attacks could affect these physical devices, such as:

- **Physical attack**: In a physical attack, an unauthorized person gains physical access to physical devices to attack their structure to steal information or destroy all infrastructure.
- **Data injection attack**: A malicious program is injected into a computer network by an attacker, which corrupts all device information or sends confidential information to the attacker through the network. This type of attack is a major security problem in cyber-physical systems.
- **Side-Channel attacks (SCAs)**: Side-channel attacks steal confidential information from integrated circuits (ICs) or devices by analyzing physical parameters such as processing time, applied voltage, current flow, etc.
- **Time Delay attack**: A time delay separates two event occurrences in mechanical and electronic devices. A time delay attack identifies vulnerabilities in physical devices by analyzing how long the system takes to respond to different inputs.
- **Application layer attacks**: Application layer attacks, also called DDoS attacks (Distributed Denial of Service Attacks), are specially designed to disrupt application services so that a particular device application cannot deliver its services to users.
- **Zero-day attacks**: A zero-day attack is a software-related vulnerability recently exposed by an attacker to exploit a fault in a system that was unknown to the developer. The term derives from the number of days a software developer has been aware of the issue.
