The following algorithm is exploited for encryption and decryption.

# Encryption Algorithm

+   - **Step 1:** Consider any graph G.
+   - **Step 2:** Label the vertices and edges of G using the definition stated in 2.5
+   - **Step 3:** The edge weights of G is assigned to alphabets and they are interchanged, for example the first edge in G has 12 as its edge weight then it will be assigned to A, then A is replaced by the 12th alphabet L. Since each edge are labeled distinctly, all the alphabets will be interchanged distinctly.
+   - **Step 4:** Special characters also encrypted by extending the edges in G.

# Decryption Algorithm

+   - **Step 1:** The graph used in encryption is identified.
+   - **Step 2:** Using the edge labelling function, edge weights are reckoned and employed to swap the alphabets.
+   - **Step 3:** Cipher text is interpreted to acquire the earliest text.
