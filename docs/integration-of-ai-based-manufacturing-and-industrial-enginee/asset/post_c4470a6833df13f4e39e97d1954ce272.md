## 1.8 CONCLUSIONS

In conclusion, IoT and AI present both challenges and opportunities for industrial engineering. Industrial engineers must develop new data management strategies and cyber-security protocols to address the challenges posed by these technologies. At the same time, IoT and AI offer significant benefits, including real-time monitoring, predictive maintenance, and predictive quality control. The integration and implementation of these technologies are expected to shape the future of the industrial engineering field as they continue to advance.
