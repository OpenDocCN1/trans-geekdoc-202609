Filters come in a variety of forms, including transfer function, high-pass filter, quality factor, and BSF. An LPF is used to filter out high frequencies since it only permits low-frequency signals to pass through to its output. An LPF makes it simple to regulate the audio signal’s highest frequency range (Bhambri et al., 2020b). Only frequency components below a predetermined threshold are rejected by the LPF, which is a significant difference from an HPF. The output waveform of a precisely defined FIR filter of Nth order lasts for N plus one samples before zeroing off.
