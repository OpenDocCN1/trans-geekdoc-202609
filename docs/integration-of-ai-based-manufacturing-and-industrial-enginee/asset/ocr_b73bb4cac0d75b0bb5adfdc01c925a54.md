# 3.8 CONCLUSION

In the future, the role of both the Internet of Things (IoT) and Artificial Intelligence (AI) will be crucial. The increasing need for technologies in businesses and governments is fuelled by a variety of sources. Technologists, engineers, and scientists have already begun to implement it at various levels. In the digital marketing industry, IoT developments will empower marketers to design and execute successful marketing strategies. They establish a connection between consumers and marketers that enables them to fulfil one another’s demands.
