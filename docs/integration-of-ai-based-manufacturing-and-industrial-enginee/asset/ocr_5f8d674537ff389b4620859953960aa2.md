|--------|-------|---------|------------|--------|----------|---------|
| Frigui and Gader (2009) | GPR | LMS, EHD | KNN | EHD used fuzzy techniques to distinguish accurate detections from false alarms | Factors appear to be influenced by geography and the environment | Pd - 90% |
| Jin and Zhou (2010) | UWB SAR | SFFS | FHSSVM | Showed improvement in the performance of identifying anti-tank mines | Impossible to achieve a low FAR | Pd - 0.7 |
