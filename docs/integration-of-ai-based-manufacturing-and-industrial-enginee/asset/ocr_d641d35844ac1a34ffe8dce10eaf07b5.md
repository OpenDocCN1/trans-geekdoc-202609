D.G. Kendall (1953) and later A. Lee (1966) presented valuable representation for queuing models. The comprehensive representation can be articulated as

$(a / b / c) : (d / e / f),$

Where

+   - a: Arrival distribution
+   - b: Departure or service time distribution
+   - c: Number of parallel service channels in the system
+   - d: Service discipline
+   - e: Queuing discipline
+   - f: Max. No. of consumers permitted in the system

Notations for ‘a’ and ‘b’ notation

+   - M: Markovian (Poisson) arrival or departure

+   - GI: General independent arrival distribution
