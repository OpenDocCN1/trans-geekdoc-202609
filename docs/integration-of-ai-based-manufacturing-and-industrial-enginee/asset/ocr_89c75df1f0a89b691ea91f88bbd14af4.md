+   (iii) *LLVIP:*  
+   The LLVIP data set is a low-light vision visible-IR paired data set containing 33,672 frames (16 836 pairs), the majority of which are acquired in low-light environmental conditions, and all the frames are aligned perfectly in the spatial domain (Jia et al., 2021). All the frames are captured from a binocular camera which has both an IR and a visible light sensor. Each pair of frames is recognized and resized to maintain the same field in terms of view and dimensions. The high spatial alignment of frames makes them ideal for frame fusion and frame-to-frame translation.

+   (iv) *PTB-TIR:*  
+   For the evaluation of the IR pedestrian tracker, Liu et al. (2020) developed a thermal IR pedestrian tracking data set that was comprised of 60 manually annotated thermal sequences. For attribute-based analysis, each string included nine characteristic labels. Various attributes, such as thermal crossover, occlusion, background clutter, illumination variation, low-resolution and motion blur, have been taken into account (Figure 6.5).
