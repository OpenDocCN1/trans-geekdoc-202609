# What We Were Thinking

In the few weeks prior to when Maria and I planned the lesson on mitosis, we had engaged in some discussions about the big ideas that would hold all of the yearlong biology concepts together. We knew that, just as short-term memory can only hold seven or less ideas at one time and effectively use them to make new understandings, students need ten or fewer big ideas around which to integrate all of the concepts they will learn during the year. These big ideas are not topics, but rather overarching concepts where everything else studied becomes just another example of one of these big ideas. The sequence of the course is not organized around these, but some of these are embedded in every unit in the course.
