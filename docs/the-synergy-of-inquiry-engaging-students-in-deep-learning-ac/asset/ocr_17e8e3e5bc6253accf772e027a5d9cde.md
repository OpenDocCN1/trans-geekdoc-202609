# Guidelines for Creating Inquiry Teaching Lesson Plans (cont.)

# Understanding(s) [Large concepts]

Define what students will understand. Identify one new concept per day and never more than two.

# Students will know... [Sub-concepts]

List in a declarative sentence one or two relational understandings for each day of the lesson. Note which day of the lesson they relate to.

# Students will be able to... [Thinking Skills]

List one thinking skill that you will focus on and assess during each day of the activity.

# Students will start to regularly... [Habit of Mind]

List one habit of mind that you will focus on and assess during each day of the activity.
