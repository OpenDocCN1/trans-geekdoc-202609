# Materials Needed

List all the materials students (per group of four) will use in their explorations. Be specific about quantity and type of materials so that you or another teacher can gather the appropriate materials.

| Time | Procedure | Objective |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| 5 min | **Introduction**

Include an activator, for example, something that relates this to their own lives or a question previously created by students. This will not explain any of the concepts, but it sets the context for the challenge—the exploration. |  |
| Varies from 30–60 min | **Small Group Work Exploration**

Divide the class into groups of four students.

Start with students exploring some documents, artifacts, hands-on science or math investigations, or role-playing. Provide some mechanism in the beginning of the exploration to uncover students’ prior knowledge and naïve conceptions.

- Have getters retrieve materials. Ask the individual participants to make a prediction or explanation near the beginning about the exploration. Students need to share with others in their group their predictions and explanations. They then test their predictions and explanations with others in their small group. They try to see if the group can come to consensus.
- Sometimes teachers will stop all the groups while they are still engaged in their exploration if they see that most groups are missing a main idea that some other groups are approaching. They would have groups report out their thinking so far and lead a quick discussion before sending the groups back to finish their explorations. | Concept, skill, or habit of mind |
