Carolina asks each student to write down which of the boxes that they made in their group holds the most stuff. They then discuss the responses in their groups, explaining why they think their answer is correct and seeing if they can convince the others as to why theirs about how to decide which holds the most, such as fill it with beans or pennies and count the number it takes to fill them. These are all reasonable responses, but she asks students about the space between the beans and how will they measure that.
