# Detailed Learning Plan (cont.)

| Time | Procedure | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| Varies from 30–60 min | **Concept Clarification**

- When the teacher thinks that each group has had extensive time to create the concept or strategy or to hone the thinking skill, they call the class together as a whole. Reporters share each group's ideas, and the teacher leads a large class discussion that moves the participants to concept clarification.
- After this, students can search for what experts know about the concept and compare it to their naive conceptions. In order to accomplish this, they can listen to the teacher telling, read texts, or even do a web quest.
- Step-by-Step: For each of the preceding actions, be sure to note what students will be doing and creating, and what the teacher will be doing to facilitate this. Do this in great detail.
- Be sure that you have modifications for students with special needs, both in this facilitation of learning of concepts and skills, and in the assessments.

Be sure to include some synthesis homework that follows from what students did in class and leads to the starting of the next class. | Identify expected misconceptions and have these up front in your mind.
