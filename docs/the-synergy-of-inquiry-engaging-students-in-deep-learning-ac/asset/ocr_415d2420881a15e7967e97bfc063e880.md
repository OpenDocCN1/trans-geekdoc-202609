# What We Were Thinking

As we know, the most successful teachers reflect on their work on a daily basis. One of the most vital times is at the end of the day when the teacher sits down with the lesson plan and makes modifications based on paying careful attention to how well each student mastered the objectives. This is the perfect time to modify both the assessment and facilitation questions while the interaction with students is fresh in your mind. Every year, the questions become richer and more effective.
