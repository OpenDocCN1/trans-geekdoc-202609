The more I experience the power of inquiry, the more I realize that I cannot put up roadblocks or off-limit areas as to what can be questioned. If I am helping to shape the next generation of informed citizens and consumers, then, in the end, I *want* them to question authority and basic social structures in meaningful and productive ways.
