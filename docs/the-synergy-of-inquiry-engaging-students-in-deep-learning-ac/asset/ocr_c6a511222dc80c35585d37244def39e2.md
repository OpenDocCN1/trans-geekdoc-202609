The progression of topics needs to make sense to students and should not be in a random order. A good order of topics will make transitions fluid and will cut down on the need for constant new engagement, since one topic will raise questions that the next will answer.

Maria created a sequence of topics for the school year. Figure 2.2 shows this sequence starting with ecological issues, then some health issues, which led to a need to understand growth and repair, and homeostasis. This led students to look at the cellular level for answers. As they moved into asexual and sexual reproduction and a deeper understanding of health and nutrition, they began to need understandings that could only be answered at the molecular level.
