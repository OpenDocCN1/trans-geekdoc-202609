# Planning Process for the Mitosis Unit

As Maria and I developed the mitosis unit plan and lessons, we spent a lot of time talking about what students would be doing, how she would keep them engaged, and how she would stay aware of their growth in understanding the concepts. The protocol we developed has nine steps:

+   - **Step 1:** Identify five to ten big ideas for the whole course.
+   - **Step 2:** Identify the sequence of topics, concepts, skills, and social issues for the year.
+   - **Step 3:** Identify the main concepts to be learned in the lesson.
+   - **Step 4:** Identify the initial idea for the unaided student exploration.
+   - **Step 5:** Identify the applicable thinking skills and habits of mind.
