When the brain learns, it literally changes parts of itself. New nerve cells and networks are built. Knowledge is simply the way that these nerves and nerve networks are connected and how often they are used for taking actions. Learning is physically rewiring a place in the brain and how it is connected to other pieces of the brain. Because of modern imaging techniques, we can see these networks built. We can also see which ones are lit up when we are involved with certain kinds of learning and thinking, and we can use this knowledge to better engage students in learning.
