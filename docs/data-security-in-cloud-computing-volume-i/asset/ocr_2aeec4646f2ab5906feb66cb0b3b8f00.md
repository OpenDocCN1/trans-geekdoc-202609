to openly consider the possibility of internal data theft, the truth is that a
staggeringly high percentage of data thefts occur internally and are perpe-
trated by employees. When this is the case, it can actually be much safer to
keep sensitive information off-site. Given a developing competitive mar-
ketplace, most cloud suppliers concentrate on making services scalable
and practical, often preceding problems of reliability and resiliency.
