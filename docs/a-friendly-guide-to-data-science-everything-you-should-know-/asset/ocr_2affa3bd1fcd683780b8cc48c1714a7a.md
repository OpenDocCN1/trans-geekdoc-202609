This pie chart is obviously ridiculous, but the treemap is actually quite readable and intuitive and has the added bonus of grouping the states meaningfully. When you have numeric data with one or two categorical levels, consider a treemap. Stakeholders don't see them too often, but you'll have no trouble explaining it to them.

# Choropleth Map

Everyone has seen a choropleth map, but almost no one knows the name. It's simply a geographical map of voting with different areas colored to some values. The maps all the news stations create of the United States around elections, with states colored blue or red, are all choropleth maps. See the 2008 US presidential election map in Figure 18-34, showing county- and state-level voting.
