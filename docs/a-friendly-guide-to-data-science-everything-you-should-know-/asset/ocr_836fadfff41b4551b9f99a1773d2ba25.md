# Bernoulli Distribution

The Bernoulli distribution is a special case of the binomial, where only one trial occurs and has two possible outcomes, usually success and failure.

# Normal Distribution

Most people have heard of the normal distribution—a continuous distribution also called the bell curve or Gaussian curve—and would recognize it plotted with a peak similar to what we saw with the binomial distribution with a peak curving down to zero on either side of the center peak with a probability of 0.5. The *normal distribution* represents the expected theoretical shape we would see if we plotted all the values of something that has a natural mid high point with values getting lower as we move away from the middle. This would include things like exam scores, where most people do okay, some slightly worse or better, and fewer do really bad or really well. It is unimodal because it has only one peak. Many other things fit the normal distribution, including the height of adult men, ACT scores, birth weight, and measurement error.
