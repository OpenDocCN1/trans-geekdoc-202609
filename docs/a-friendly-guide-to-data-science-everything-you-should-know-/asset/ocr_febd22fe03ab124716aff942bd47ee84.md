> **Figure 3-7.** Six example samples from a table full of marbles, three with a biased picker and three with a fair picker

The broken picker can't hold the smaller marbles, so all the samples have only medium-sized and bigger marbles. Those samples don't look anything like what's on the table. The samples on the right, taken after the picker was fixed, look far more balanced.

It's also important to remember that humans can fairly easily eyeball balance in this scenario to a degree, but it's critical to pick more systematically, because humans don't have the big-picture precision needed.
