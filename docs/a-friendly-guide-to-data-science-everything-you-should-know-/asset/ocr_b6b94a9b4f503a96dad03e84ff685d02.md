It is worth mentioning that the idea behind automated risk scoring of defendants isn't inherently a bad one—it actually has a lot of potential to remove bias before systems like this, humans were making these decisions based on gut feelings and their own biases. If they're too lenient, dangerous criminals will be released too early and commit more crimes. If they're too harsh, people with relatively minor offenses will be sent to prison for longer than necessary, at unnecessary expense and risk of hardening these people into more dangerous criminals. Obviously, you want an automated system to be better than faulty humans, but it takes a great deal of careful work to ensure that.
