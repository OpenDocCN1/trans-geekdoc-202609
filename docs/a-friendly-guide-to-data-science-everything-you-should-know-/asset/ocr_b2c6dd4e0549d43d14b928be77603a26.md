Several Key Performance Indicators (KPIIs) and reports were created once the data was ready. These included showing customer segmentation in several areas; accounts receivable analytics including a breakdown of customers and identification of the best and worst customers; supplier analysis into pricing, delivery time, credit time, and margins; and inventory replenishments that helped reduce the cost of inventory.

The project was considered a big success because it increased the revenue in high-margin products by 15% and led to a reduction in payment time of 18% and a 17% reduction in inventory expenses. It also led to better negotiations and terms with some of their suppliers. The company believed none of these would have been possible without a concerted effort to implement a comprehensive analytics program focusing on data, advanced reporting, and KPIs.
