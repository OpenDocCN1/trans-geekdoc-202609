There’s a lot of terminology around natural language processing and speech processing, especially around linguistics. See the sidebar for an explanation of the many linguistics areas relevant. We’ll see more terms below.

# LINGUISTICS SUBFIELDS

**Phonetics:** The study of speech sounds, including the physiological mechanism and acoustic properties

**Phonology:** The study of speech sounds, generally at a higher level than phonetics

**Semantics:** The study of meaning in language

**Syntax:** The study of how words and phrases are arranged in order to be grammatically correct and meaningful

**Prosody:** The study of higher-level sound characteristics of language, including intonation, stress, and rhythm
