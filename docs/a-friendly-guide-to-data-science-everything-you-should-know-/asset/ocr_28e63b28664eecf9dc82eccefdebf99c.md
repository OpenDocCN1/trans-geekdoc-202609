# Background

Sandip Thanki is a physicist focusing on astronomy by training, earning a series of degrees culminating in a PhD in Physics and Astronomy. He studied stars and began his career as a professor. After becoming a department chair, one of his colleagues was a dean who worked with data a lot. He often pulled data for her, and ended working with it on his own as he became curious. He moved into another role that required even more work, and he loved it.
