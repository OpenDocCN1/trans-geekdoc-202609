# # More on Terminology

In the business world, everybody's excited about "AI," and "data science," terms that get thrown around a lot. It's interesting to look at how all of these terms are actually being used in the real world. Google has a tool called the Ngram Viewer that will display frequency percentages of any search terms found in their Google Books corpus, which has books published through 2022. Let's look at how popular the terms are, even though the last couple years is not ideal. We can use Google's Ngram Viewer to see the popularity in published books up through 2022, starting in 1960, shown in Figure 6-1.
