# CHAPTER 2 FIGURING OUT WHAT'S GOING ON IN THE DATA: DESCRIPTIVE STATISTICS

Primary Tools Used Currently: SQL and Excel mostly, occasionally Power BI and R

Future of Data Analysis and Data Science: Rapid change is going to continue, and things are getting more and more specialized.

What Makes a Good Data Analyst: Patience, good social skills, and curiosity. Because you're working with people from all sorts of different backgrounds and skill levels, it helps to know how to talk to people. Curiosity and a just-try attitude are important for learning and problem-solving. You can't let fear of the unknown hold you back.

Her Tip for Prospective Data Analysts and Data Scientists: This applies to everyone, but especially girls and women, don't let fear and competition get in the way of learning and achieving what you want. If people tell you that you can't learn something, prove them wrong.
