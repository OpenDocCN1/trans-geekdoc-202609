This was something that wouldn't really have been possible without the cloud, enabling people to watch the games and interact with others, as well as getting a lot of additional info and experience both during the games and outside them. They were so happy with the results that they continued offering much of this in future Australian Opens even post-COVID, with extensive statistics and visualizations on strokes, player rally analysis, and matchbeats.
