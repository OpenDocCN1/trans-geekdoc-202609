**Favorite Project:** One of Caitlin's projects was one she worked on at a startup incubator. The work specifically involved quantifying jobs and wages on the activities happening in the startups they funded as well as using predetermined models to determine total indirect economic impact. Put it a value to the question “what is the ROI that can be generated by directing tasks toward this org?”
