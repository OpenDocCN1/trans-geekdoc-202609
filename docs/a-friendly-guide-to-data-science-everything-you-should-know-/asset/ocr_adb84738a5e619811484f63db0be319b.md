To put this in this context, people have defined different levels of data. A famous diagram called the DIKW Pyramid in Figure 1-5 shows the progression of data from bottom to top as it becomes increasingly useful.

Figure 1-5. DIKW Pyramid

If the purpose of data is to help us become wiser about the world, then data analysis and data science are the main methods used to achieve that. Although the word information (or just info) is often used synonymously with data, there is an important distinction in this diagram: in this view, information is data that has meaning to it. Beyond that, meaning is clearly useful, but it takes a collection of information and understanding to become knowledge. And finally, knowledge doesn’t instantly become wisdom. It has to build up over time.
