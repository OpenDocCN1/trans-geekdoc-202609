**Future of Data Science:** The future of AI is great from a scientific perspective, but not necessarily for humanity. Now that we've put AI in the hands of regular people, we have to watch for the consequences. We have AI, and people are already inspired to go and do bad things with it. We need to pay attention and start focusing on training people, especially young people, to be responsible with it. Also, it's going to start feeding on itself, like AI training on AI-generated content. Companies and developers should be careful about what is released to the public.
