Figure 15-5 clearly shows an upward movement of the data. This is called the trend in time series analysis, a consistent general increase or decrease over a long period. We can also see seasonality, a pattern of regularly repeated increases or decreases, such as a boost in sales every June and December as we see in Figure 15-5.
