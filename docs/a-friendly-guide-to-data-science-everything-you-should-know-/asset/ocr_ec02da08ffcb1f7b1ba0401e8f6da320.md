# Bar Charts

The bar chart is another simple visualization, and because it is generally intuitive, it gets a lot in the media. We saw one type above in Figures 2-4, 2-5, and 2-6, but we were focused on understanding the distribution of ratings, and there's a lot more that a bar chart can show. It's used most with nominal data, often displaying counts of values per single variable. As we saw above, a histogram is a type of bar chart, but it is for continuous data and will be discussed separately below. The simplest bar charts show values of a single variable across the X-axis and usually counts or percentages on the Y-axis. The bars in bar charts can be shown vertically or horizontally, although vertically is most common.

If we look again at our video game dataset, we can see what the average rating is each year since 2015, which is shown in Figure 2-10.

> Figure 2-10. Simple bar chart with average video game ratings over nine years
