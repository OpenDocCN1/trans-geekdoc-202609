# Malware and Attacks

Computer viruses and worms are two well-known and feared types of malware, software that exists to do harm in some way. Other malware includes spyware, wiper malware, and ransomware. All of these can wreak havoc in business and even individuals' systems, whether physical or virtual.

Most of these attacks rely on social engineering to some extent, at least in the beginning, because someone needs to fall for the trick to get the malware into a company network. People are always the weakest link in any security program.

Anti-malware software is critical in most computer systems.

# Viruses and Worms

Computer viruses are software that gets installed on a computer or system and takes control of some of the software on the computer, or even the whole computer.
