It’s not surprising that lunch and dinner are the busiest times, and it’s also clear that they sort of mirror each other—lunch is busiest Monday through Friday but slow on the weekends, and dinner is less busy at the beginning of week but picks up later in the week. Afternoon sales are pretty steady, and the late-night crowd is buying more pizzas on the weekend than during the week.
