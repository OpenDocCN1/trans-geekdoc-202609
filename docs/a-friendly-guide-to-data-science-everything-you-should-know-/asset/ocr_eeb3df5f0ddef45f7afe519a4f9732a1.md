This was also seen in the Amazon recruiting tool, where they looked only at the people who were hired and didn't consider any of the ones who weren't hired were actually good. Always think about the data you're not seeing in a problem.

Another really interesting problem is Simpson's paradox, which occurs when trends appear in individual subsets of data are different, opposite, when the subsets are combined with each other. Sounds impossible, but it often crops up in real data. This is one reason it's important to slice and dice the data in different ways.
