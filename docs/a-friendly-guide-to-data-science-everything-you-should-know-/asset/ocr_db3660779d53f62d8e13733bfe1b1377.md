There are several steps to follow to select the right test(s):

+   1. Define the research question and null alternative hypotheses.

+   2. Determine the type of data you have (nominal, ordinal, or numeric) and, if numeric, it's normally distributed.

+   3. Determine if you have one sample or two or more and, if so, the samples are paired or independent.

+   4. Consider the possible tests based on #2 and #3 and look at the assumptions of each to see if your sample(s) meet(s) them.

+   5. Pick your test based on what assumptions your data meets.
