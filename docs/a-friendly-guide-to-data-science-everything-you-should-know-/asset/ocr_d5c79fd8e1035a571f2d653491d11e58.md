# Background

Saeed was an overachiever when younger and had many interests, although programming wasn't one of them. When it was time to go to college, he did well enough on Iran's tough entrance exam to pursue anything he wanted, his dad encouraged him to go into petroleum engineering, so that's what he did. He encountered programming again while working on his bachelor's and found that he really liked it. He did well on his degree and started a master's to avoid the mandatory military service he was facing. He got to do more interesting programming in his master's, including solving partial differential equations in Matlab. After completing the master's, he started a PhD in Canada, still in petroleum engineering, but his interests had definitely widened by that time. He continued programming during that degree, this time picking up Python, and also started hearing rumors about AI and machine learning—there was interest in ML in the oil and gas field, but nobody really knew how to use it yet.
