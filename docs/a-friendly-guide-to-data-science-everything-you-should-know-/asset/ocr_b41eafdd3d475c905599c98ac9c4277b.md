We can easily see that Nintendo releases many more games than EA does and generally Nintendo has a much higher proportion of the total games released by both together. There's one final type of bar chart that would make the specific proportion even clearer. a segmented bar chart shows the same data as a stacked bar chart but as literal proportions, where all the full bars sum to 100%.
