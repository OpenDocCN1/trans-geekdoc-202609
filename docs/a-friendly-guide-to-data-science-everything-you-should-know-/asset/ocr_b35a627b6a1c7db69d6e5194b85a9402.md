Note that although the right is a bit easier to read, the more lines we add, the harder it is to understand the values. It's easiest to comprehend the lowest series since it sits on the X-axis. Because of this, it's common to make the line chart values sum to 100%, which makes each value slightly easier to understand. Compare two charts in Figure 18-23, which show a simple stacked chart on the left and a chart on the right where each day sums to 100%.
