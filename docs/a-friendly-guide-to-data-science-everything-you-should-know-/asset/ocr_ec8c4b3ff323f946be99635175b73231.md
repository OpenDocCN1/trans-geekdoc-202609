In the next chapter, we’ll be talking about the idea of modern “data analytics,” another term that is used in different ways. But here, it means an initiative that involves all aspects of working with data to extract insights, from getting the data in the first place to making it ready to use and to performing business intelligence (primarily basic reporting), data analysis, and data science on it. I'll talk about each of those areas and how they all fit together (or not) at different organizations.
