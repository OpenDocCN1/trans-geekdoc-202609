# Example 2: Detecting Radiation More Effectively

Some other researchers did a similar study that showed that among many
techniques that can be used to achieve more accurate machine learning
results, quality feature engineering can bring the most improvement.² They
also found that feature engineering could also lead to better explainability
in the model itself. They were working with radiation detection in three
conditions: with obstructions, with background, and with neither
obruction nor background. Their model predicted the angle for the
direction the source radiation was located. They used a random forest
model and also did the normal data prep and machine learning training like
the researchers in the previous example. They ultimately found that feature
engineering improved the results in all three conditions by a large factor.

# Feature Engineering Techniques

last chapter, you're now ready to do the even more important work of
fine-tuning and improving your features. There are many techniques that
you may do at this point, but the first focus area is working with individual
features, transforming existing ones and making interaction or
other features.
