# Planning

One of the skills everyone in the work world needs to develop is the ability to plan, estimating effort and time. This is notoriously difficult, and two fallacies are primarily to blame. One is the hard-easy effect, which occurs when people overestimate their ability to complete hard tasks but underestimate their ability to complete easy tasks. This feeds into the planning fallacy, which is a belief people have that things will take them less time to complete than they really will. These two go hand in hand and really do come up all the time in the tech world. Providing estimates is fundamentally hard, and the only way to get good at it is to pay attention to your estimates and learn from how wrong they are.
