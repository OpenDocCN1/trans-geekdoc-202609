# Basic Probability

Most people know enough about probability to understand that it deals with the likelihood of specific things happening and helping us understand risk. But it goes beyond that, and aspects of probability underlie statistical techniques that data scientists use every day.

# Probability Theory

There are several concepts in probability theory that rely on specific terms that we'll define in this context.

An event in normal usage is just something that happens, but in probability it's defined in relation to a trial. A trial can be thought of as a single attempt, play, or roll of something that have some kind of result. For instance, in a dice rolling game, each play involves a player rolling their dice to generate a result. The trial is the roll of the dice, and the outcome—the combination of dice numbers—is called the event, the outcome or thing that happens.
