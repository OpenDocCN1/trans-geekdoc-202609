**[198](#12_ch003.html#fn198).** Automation also brings about an interesting gray area concerning the very nature of “managerial efforts.” What happens if tokens are sold for a DAO once (and only once) it is up and running, and operated by algorithmic governance? And suppose the objective of this DAO were solely to generate profits through automated trading rules for its token-holders. A judge may find herself in a gray area where it is not clear that the fourth prong of the *Howey* test is met, while it is clear that in this profit-only oriented case, the token should be treated as an investment security. There again, a clear determination of substance over form will undoubtedly be needed.
