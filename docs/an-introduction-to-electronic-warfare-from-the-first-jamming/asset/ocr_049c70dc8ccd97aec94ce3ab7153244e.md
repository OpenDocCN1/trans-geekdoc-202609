the working principles of a proximity fuze are covered in this chapter along with methods designed to jam proximity fuzes.

Since a missile is a lethal weapon, once it is detected, jamming techniques must be applied immediately. In this chapter, IR missile guidance principles and possible jamming methods are explained. Just like jamming a radar, the intelligence about missile’s guidance system is crucial for the jammer’s success. However, this knowledge is not always available. If the approaching missile’s guidance system is not known, all possible jamming techniques should be used. To jam an IR missile, both infrared jammers and flares (to be discussed in the next chapter) can be used. Obviously, the pilot may maneuver the aircraft to outsmart the missile (probably with the help of the sun), but this topic is beyond the scope of this book.
