# 3.10 Conclusion

The general rule of electronic warfare operations is to limit signal transmission. If enough information is obtained, the emitter should be kept silent because emitting signals can expose the emitter and even attract attacking weapons. Especially for new military radars, the signals must be kept secret. New radar can surprise the hostile aircraft and induce great damage. Intelligence collecting equipment are working all the time. If new radar signals were captured by a rival’s intelligence agents, the effect of the radar could be reduced drastically.

In order to protect oneself and act against the enemies in the meantime, the four layers of electronic warfare operations need to continue to improve. The field of electronic warfare is highly technical and keeps evolving constantly. For this reason, it can be a very interesting field for some researchers. In the following chapters, the operation of layers 2, 3, and 4 will be discussed in detail.
