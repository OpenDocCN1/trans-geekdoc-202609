By definition, a noise is a wideband signal covering a broad bandwidth. ^ 1 To limit the noise spectrum, a bandpass filter centered at the specific frequency can be used to filter out the portion of the noise outside the desired bandwidth generated by a noise generator, thus creating a band-limited noise.
