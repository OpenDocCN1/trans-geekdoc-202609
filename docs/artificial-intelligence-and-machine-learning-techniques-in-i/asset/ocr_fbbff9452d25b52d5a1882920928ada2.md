# **Saurabh Singh, PhD**
*Professor; School of Computing Science and Engineering at Galgotias University, Greater Noida, India.*

Saurabh Singh, PhD, is working as Professor in the School of Computing Science and Engineering, Galgotias University, Greater Noida India. Dr. Singh has more than 21 years of experience in teaching and research. He has received his PhD degree from Birla Institute of Technology, Ranchi. He has published over 40 research articles in various international reputed journals and conferences, written book chapters, and filed nine patents in various fields of engineering.

# **Abhishek Singhal, PhD**
*Associate Professor; Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Amity University, Noida, India*

Abhishek Singhal, PhD, is working as an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Amity University, Noida. He has received his PhD from Amity University, Noida, in the year 2018. He has more than 22 years of experience. He has published over 50 Scopus-indexed research articles in various internationally reputed journals and conferences.
