# Sample complexity and compression:

+   - In hybrid quantum-classical training schemes, generative models can compress structured data into low-parameter quantum circuits that still achieve good coverage over the target distribution.

# Provable hardness of classical simulation:

+   - Sampling from the output of specific QCBMs (e.g. instantaneous quantum polynomial-time or random circuits with certain depth and entanglement structure) is conjectured to be classically hard under plausible assumptions (e.g. anticoncration and average-case hardness).

This combination of expressivity, compactness, and complexity-theoretic hardness supports the view that quantum generative models could outperform classical ones in appropriate tasks, such as simulating quantum distributions or compressing large datasets in variational circuits.
