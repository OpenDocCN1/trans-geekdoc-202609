One of the key motivations for using quantum circuits is their **expressivity**, the ability to represent complex probability distributions compactly and accurately.

### 7.3.1 Defining expressivity

The expressivity of a quantum model refers to its ability to represent a wide and complex class of distributions. In the context of circuit-based models such as QCBMs, this notion is formalized via the induced distribution of a parameterized quantum state.
