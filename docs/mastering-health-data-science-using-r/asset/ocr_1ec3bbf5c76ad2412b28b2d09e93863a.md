# 1.4 RStudio Projects and RStudio Global Options

You have now had a basic tour of RStudio. Once you close RStudio, you have the option of whether to store your current R environment. We highly recommend that you update your RStudio options to not save your workspace on exiting or load it on starting. This ensures that you have a fresh environment every time you open RStudio and helps you to create fully reproducible code and avoid possible errors or confusion (Figure 1.4).

Now when you re-open RStudio, it opens the files you had open previously and has your history of commands. This may become confusing when you are working on different files.
