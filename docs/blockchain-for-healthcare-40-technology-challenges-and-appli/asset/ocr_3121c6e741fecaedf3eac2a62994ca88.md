# 3.8.19 Treatment Optimization

Targeted therapy and telemedicine are two factors for treatment improvement. In many countries, the quantity of medical patients has sharply increased, causing it to be more and more challenging for patients to receive immediate assistance from physicians or nursing personnel [56].

The technology used in telemedicine is viewed as a means of providing medical treatment that is both fair and economical [57].

There are security dangers in the transmission and logging of data exchanges as a result of the creation and widespread use of Internet of Things devices and other types of remote patient tracking systems. The blockchain's intelligent contract can be used to support security research and administration of medical devices because it is tamper-proof, anonymous, and transparent [37].

For instance, Medicalchain has introduced a smart contract that enables patients to allow doctors to virtually evaluate health cases and offer recommendations or second views [58].

Additionally, organizations for drug research and development can categorize the data with user approval to carry out drug-focused research and development, and the data can be transmitted safely using blockchain asymmetry cryptography encoding [59,60].
