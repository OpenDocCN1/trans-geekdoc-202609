+   1. Distributed ledger technology (DLT) (which Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies are built on) is a sort of blockchain. Get rid of centralized systems’ drawbacks, such as the requirement for more security and openness. Instead of keeping the information in one central location, DLT disperses it over a network of computers.

+   2. A private blockchain is a blockchain implementation that can handle secure data. A network that is either private or under the jurisdiction of a single organization. Miniature compared to public blockchains. The creator of a private blockchain is aware of all the nodes from the start.

+   3. Hybrid blockchains combine the best features of both public and private blockchains. Businesses can now establish permissionless public and permission-based private networks to control who has access to what information on the blockchain.
