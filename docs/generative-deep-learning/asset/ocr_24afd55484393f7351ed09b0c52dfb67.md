We can serialize the grid into a stream of tokens by moving first through the four voices, then along the timesteps in sequence. This produces a sequence of tokens S₁, A₁, T₁, B₁, S₂, A₂, T₂, B₂, ..., where the subscript denotes the timestep, as shown in Figure 11-12.
