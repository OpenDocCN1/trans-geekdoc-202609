All transition and stabilization phases last until the discriminator has been shown 800,000 real images. Note that even through the network is trained progressively, no layers are frozen. Throughout the training process, all layers remain fully trainable.

This process continues, growing the GAN from 4 × 4 images to 8 × 8, then 16 × 16, 32 × 32, and so on, until it reaches full resolution (1,024 × 1,024), as shown in Figure 10-5.
