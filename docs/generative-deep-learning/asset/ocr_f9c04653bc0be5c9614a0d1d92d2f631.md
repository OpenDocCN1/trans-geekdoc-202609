# Tokenization of Polyphonic Music

The Transformer we've been exploring in this section works well for single-line (monophonic) music, but could it be adapted to multiline (polyphonic) music?

The challenge lies in how to represent the different lines of music as a single sequence of tokens. In the previous section we decided to split the notes and durations of the notes into two distinct inputs and outputs of the network, but we also saw that we could have interleaved these tokens into a single stream. We can use the same idea to handle polyphonic music. Two different approaches will be introduced here: grid tokenization and event-based tokenization, as discussed in the 2018 paper “Music Transformer: Generating Music with Long-Term Structure.”¹
