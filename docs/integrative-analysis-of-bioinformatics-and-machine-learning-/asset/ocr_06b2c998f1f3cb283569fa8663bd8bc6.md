# Results

# Data Set Acquisition and Removal of the Batch Effect

After generating gene expression profiles of the TCGA and GSE31210 data sets, 60 CMGs were merged with those unwanted mutations. The TCGA set included 488 LUAD patients with 59 CMGs, and the GSE31210 dataset included 226 LUAD samples with 56 CMGs. Using SVA and limma were used to remove the batch effect of these two sets. A total of 585 CMGs were available for subsequent analyses. The flow chart for this study is presented in Figure 1.

# Patient Clustering Based on CMGs

According to the expression level of CMGs in patients with LUAD, unsupervised clustering analysis was performed to stratify patients into different clusters. As shown in Figure 2, and the C value, which identified the most optimal cluster, were 6 and 7 in the TCGA and GSE31210 data sets, respectively. The principal components similarity guided the clustering analysis, which showed that LUAD could be clearly classified into seven distinct clusters in the TCGA data set (Figure 2B) and into six clusters in the GSE31210 data set (Figure 2D).

# Determination of Time Stage Using the Estimate Algorithm

The ESTIMATE algorithm was used to calculate the immune score, stromal score, and tumor purity of LUAD sample in the TCGA and GSE31210 data sets. As shown in Supplemental Figure S1, clusters 2, 3, 5, 6 and 7 had relatively higher immune (Supplemental Figure S1A) and stromal (Supplemental Figure S1B) scores compared to clusters 1 and 4, which had higher tumor purity (Supplemental Figure S2A). In the GSE31210 data set, clusters 1 and 4 had relatively higher immune (Supplemental Figure S1C) and stromal (Supplemental Figure S1D) scores, whereas clusters 5 and 6 had relatively higher tumor purity (Supplemental Figure S2B).
