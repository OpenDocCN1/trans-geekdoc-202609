# Identification of Biomarkers of Hot Tumors

The TCGA and GSE31210 data sets were used to identify biomarkers from 58 CMGs after batch effect. In the TCGA data set, the Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator method screened 33 candidate CMGs (Figure 6, A and B) and the Support Vector–Recursive Feature Elimination identified 36 CMGs (Figure 6C). Overlapping CMGs from these two algorithms identified 32 coincident CM biomarkers. Fifteen diagnostic biomarkers were maintained in the TCGA, and the multivariable logistic analysis (Supplemental Figure S4).

Subsequently, biomarkers from the GSE31210 data set were merged with three CMGs (CD60, LT, and TNFS8) were overlapped and determined as final diagnostic biomarkers (Supplemental Figure S4). Supplemental Table S3 were analyzed at this phase.
