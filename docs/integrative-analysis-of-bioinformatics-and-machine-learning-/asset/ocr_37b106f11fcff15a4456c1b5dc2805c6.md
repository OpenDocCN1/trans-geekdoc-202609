Costulatory molecules are an indispensable signal for activating immune cells. However, the features of many costulatory molecules (CMGs) in lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) are poorly understood. This study systematically explored expression patterns of CMGs in the tumor microenvironment (TIME) of patients with LUAD. Their expression profiles were downloaded from the GEO database. Two subsets 'hot' and 'cold' were identified by K-clustering and estimated immune cells in malignant tumors using tissue expression. The 'hot' subset suggested higher activation in immune cells and enrichment in the immune cell receptor signaling pathway and adaptive response. Three CMGs (CD80, LTB, and TNFSF8) were screened as diagnostic markers by means of Absolute Signature Selection Operator and Survival Maximum Regression algorithms. The developed nomogram for predicting LUAD tissue status satisfied diagnostic accuracy in the Gene Atlas training cohort as well as GSE62110 and GSE13847. Immunohistochemistry staining of 16 specimens revealed an apparently positive correlation between expression of CMGs and pathologic response to immunotherapy. Thus, the independent prediction of status of LUAD patients with good predictive accuracy, which could serve as a test for identifying immunotherapy responders.
