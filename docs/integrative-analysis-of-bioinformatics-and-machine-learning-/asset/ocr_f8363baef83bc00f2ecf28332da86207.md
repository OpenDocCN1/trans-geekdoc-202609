In conclusion, an analysis of the characteristics and clinical value of costimomodulatory genes in patients with LUAD through a series of bioinformatics analyses identified robust signatures for patient stratification. CMGs (CD80, LTB, and TNFSF8) were identified as diagnostic markers through machine learning algorithms. On this basis, a novel signature was developed to improve predictions of the TIME status of LUAD, which showed good performance in external validation cohorts. The study provides new insights regarding the value of CMGs in the TIME status of patients with LUAD, which could serve as a tool for identifying candidates for immunotherapy.
