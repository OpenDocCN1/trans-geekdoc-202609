# Functional Annotation and Pathway Enrichment

To light on possible differences in the biological function and pathways enriched in hot and cold tumors, Gene Enrichment Analysis was performed using GSEA (version 4.0.1; http://www.msigdb.org/gsea/downloads.html). The Encyclopedia of Genes and CMGs in Gene C5 V7 and Gene Ontology in C5 V7 were used to perform the functional annotation and pathway enrichment.^34 A false discovery rate < 0.05 and P < 0.05 were considered significant.

# Patient Clustering Based on CMGs

The K-means algorithm, an unsupervised consensus clustering learning algorithm, was used to classify patients into different clusters with distinct CMG patterns in the TCGA and GSE12310 data sets. Before analysis, the factoextra package was used to find the optimal cluster numbers in these two data sets. Principal component analysis was conducted to show visual results of the clustering and to evaluate the clustering efficacy.

# Screen Diagnostic Biomarkers of Hot Tumors

Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator, the Support Vector-Recursive Feature Elimination, and logistic regression were used to screen diagnostic biomarkers of the hot immune type. Least Absolute and Selection regression with R package and the Vector Machine-Recursive Elimination algorithm was performed on the TCGA data set to use the caret package to show the number of candidate CMGs. From the aforementioned biomarkers were subjected to the multivariate logistic regression, CMGs with significant features were identified as diagnostic biomarkers in the TCGA. The same work was also conducted in the GSE12310 data set. Finally, diagnostic biomarkers in the TCGA and GSE12310 sets were overlapped to determine final CMG diagnostic biomarkers.

# Differentiation of Cold and Hot Tumors

The Estimation of Strom and Immune Cells in Malignant Lung Tumors Using Estimation Data (ESTIMATE) also calculate the immune score, stromal score, and tumor purity using the gene set.^34 With a high immune score represents a high level of immune infiltration that was identified as "hot". The ESTIMATE package was used to calculate the immune score, stromal score, and tumor purity in TCGA and GSE12310 data sets, where LUAD patients were divided into hot and cold tumors. The GSE180347 data set, with at least 50 lymphocytes per power field, were identified as hot.

# Construction and Validation of the Diagnostic Nomogram

Using the TCGA data set as a training set, the nomogram integrating CMG diagnostic biomarkers was established to predict the survival time for patients with LUAD. GSE12310 and GSE180347 were used as validation data sets to assess the performance of this diagnostic nomogram. The calibration and operating characteristics curves were plotted in all three sets. In addition, the area value of the nomogram and PD-L1 was compared, and the clinical value of the nomogram was evaluated via decision analysis in all three data sets.

# Immune Infiltration Analysis by TME

With per to 1000, the CIBERSORT algorithm was used to investigate the infiltration landscape of 22 immune cells in the TCGA and GSE12310 data sets.^35 The correlation between types of immune cells and CMG patterns between "hot" and "cold" tumors were compared.

A total of 16 postoperative specimens were collected from a cohort of LUAD patients treated with nejuvant immunotherapy between January 2018 and January 2022 at Sun-Sen University Center. Their response rates were acquired from the pathologic report of Sun Yat-Sen Cancer Center. The details of immunotherapy for 16 LUAD patients presented in Supplemental Table S2. The formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tumor sections were deparallelized and rehydrated with a graded series of ethanol and water mixtures. Sections were heated to boiling point in EDTA buffer (ph 9.0) for 5 min in phosphate-buffered saline and 0.1% Tween 20. Endogenous peroxidase activity was blocked with 3% H2O2 in methanol, followed by 1 x phosphate-buffered saline and 0.1% Tween 20. Four sections were incubated with a protein block (ZSJQ-Bio, Beijing, People's Republic of China) for 60 min and then incubated with primary antibodies CD48 (Rabbit, Abcam), CD67 (Rabbit, Abcam), and lymphotoxin (LTB) (Rabbit, Abcam), at 37°C for 1 hour. Tumor sections were washed with 1 x phosphate-buffered saline and 0.1% Tween 20 and incubated with goat anti-secondary antibody (ZSJQ-Bio) in methanol for 30 minutes. The quercetin sections were developed with 3,3'-diaminobenzidine hydrochloride for 15 to 60 minutes. Finally, the slides were dehydrated and mounted with neutral gel. The tumor sections were scanned by a digital pathology scanner (KF-, People's Republic of China) at 40x magnification.

All statistical and bioinformatics analyses were conducted using R (version 4.1). P < 0.05 was considered significant. The one-tailed test was used.

# Results

## Data Acquisition and Removal of Batch Effect

After generating gene expression profiles of the TCGA and GSE1210 data sets, SVA and limma were used to remove the batch effect of these two sets. The TCGA set included 488 patients with 59 CMGs, and the GSE1210 dataset included CMGs. The flow chart for this study is presented in Figure 1.

## Patient Clustering Based on CMGs

According to the expression level of CMGs in patients, unsupervised clustering analysis was performed to stratify patients into different clusters. As shown in Figure 2, the C value identified the most optimal cluster as 6 and 7 in the TCGA and GSE1210 data sets, respectively. Principal component analysis showed that LUAD could be clearly classified into seven clusters in the TCGA (2B) and into six clusters in the GSE1210 data set (2D).

## Determination of Stage Using Estimate Algorithm

The ESTIMATE algorithm was used to calculate the immune score, stromal score, and tumor purity of LUAD in the TCGA and GSE1210 data sets. As shown in Supplemental Figure S1, clusters 2, 3, 5, 6, and 7 had relatively higher immune (S1A) and stromal (S1B) scores compared to clusters 1 and 4, which had higher tumor purity (S2A). In the GSE1210 data set, clusters 1 and 4 had relatively higher immune (S1C) and stromal (S1D) scores, whereas clusters 5 and 6 had relatively higher tumor purity (S2B).

## Statistical Analysis

The expression profiles of LUAD patients in the TCGA and GSE1210 data sets were transformed following R analysis methods. SVM model based on radial kernel function was used to identify CMGs in immunotherapy. The Deconvolution test was performed to compare the operating characteristics between our genomic and PD-L1. The Mann-Whitney T and Kruskal-Wallis test were used to compare immune score, stromal score, and tumor purity among groups. Spearman analysis was used to determine the correlation between the pathologic response and the H-score.

In the TCGA cohort, the average score was 886.07, whereas the average immune score of 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 was -3437.2, 1258.8, 1266.6, 369.19, 2071.0, 1814.3, and 1881.9, respectively.
