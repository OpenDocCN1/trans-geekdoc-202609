# Discussion

With the development of immunotherapy and multi-omics analyses, emerging studies on molecular and cellular mechanisms within the intratum TIME have been intensively conducted, but most studies focused on immune cell phenotypes. Tumor cells usually deliver incorrect messages to immune cells by costimulatory molecule signaling and expression within the TIME further increasing immune evasion. As signals for activation of T cells, elaborations on costimulatory molecules in LUAD are still poorly understood. This study used an unsupervised clustering method for identifying

subtypes based on CMGs in LUAD, with significantly highlighted comprehensive characteristics and the diagnostic value of CMGs in LUAD cohorts. Moreover, an exploratory analysis in a real-world small sample via IHC revealed that the expression level of the three CMGs had an apparently positive correlation with the pathologic response to neoadjuvant immunotherapy in GSE9157 data, the SVM model using CMGs also showed high accuracy in predicting response to immunotherapy.
