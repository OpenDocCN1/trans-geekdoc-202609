## ADDING INVENTORY

In an e-commerce site that sells physical products, inventory management is an important feature. As you'll see later in this chapter, the inventory of an item is reduced when the item ships (you could alternatively choose to reduce the quantity on hand when the item sells). There needs to be a way to increase the inventory, too.

As I imagine it, the administrator might daily or weekly review the sales and the current inventory, then order more quantities of products to replenish the business's stock. When that shipment arrives, the added inventory needs to be reflected on the site.

To accomplish this, the administrator will be presented with a list of every product available for sale. Each will have a text input wherein the administrator enters the number just received (Figure 11.7).
