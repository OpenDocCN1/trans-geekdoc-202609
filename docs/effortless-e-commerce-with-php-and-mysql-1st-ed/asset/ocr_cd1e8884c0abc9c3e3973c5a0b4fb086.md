A final, obvious factor that was not listed earlier is cost. You'll need to consider the initial setup costs, the monthly fees, plus the individual transaction expenses. If you require features that come at an extra cost, factor those in, too.

> If the price of your transactions will be small, like less than $10 on average, find a payment provider that supports micro-payments, which means smaller transaction fees.

The development process occurs in phases. If each phase is approached deliberately and the end results are properly generated, you'll develop a great e-commerce site as efficiently as possible. If, on the other hand, you jump around, rush the process, skip steps, and make omissions, the whole procedure will take much longer, and the end result will be buggier.
