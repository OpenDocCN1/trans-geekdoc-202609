The other important thing to comment is the overall logic of pages, especially long pages. Often a script behaves differently under different circumstances. Variables passed from forms, errors, and other factors affect what portions of the script run. At the top of the page, you can indicate what factors affect the page's logic and then, as you reach different if blocks, explain where the conditions are coming from and what they mean.
