Compared with PARAFAC, PARAFAC2 is designed to deal with non‐trilinear data sets, while keeping uniqueness of the solutions as for PARAFAC. To do so, PARAFAC2 allows some freedom in the shape of the *k* slabs (`X[k]`) in the variable mode. To maintain the uniqueness of the solutions `X[k] X[k]^T` are forced to have the same structure for all *k*, i.e. `X[k] X[k]^T = A D[k] Φ D[k] A^T`. In this case the above equation is subject to `A[k]^T A[k] = Φ`. Consequently, `A[k]` can be modelled as `A[k] = P[k] A`, where `P[k] P[k]^T = I`.
