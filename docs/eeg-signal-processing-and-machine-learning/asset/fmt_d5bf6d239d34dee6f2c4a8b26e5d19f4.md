
The study of psychiatric disorders is probably the most challenging one due to similarity and overlaps among them and the complexity of brain function in both resting state and during the brain mental or motor activity and workload. Many of the techniques used for the analysis of psychiatric disorders are used for many other brain assessment applications. However, surprisingly, these methods involving machine learning applied to features such as connectivity, complexity, spectral power changes, or variations in the amplitude or latency of ERPs, perform very well for the classification of most psychiatric diseases from healthy individuals. The research in schizotypy is in its enfant stage and is currently under research.
