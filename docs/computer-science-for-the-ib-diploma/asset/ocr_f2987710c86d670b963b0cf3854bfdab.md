Normalization issues can encompass data duplication, missing data and a range of dependency concerns, including data dependencies, composite key dependencies, transitive dependencies and multi-valued dependencies. For example, a car manufacturer produces two colours (black and grey) of each model every year. The attributes Colour and ManufacturingYear are dependent on the field CarModel, but they are independent of each other. Therefore, they can be called “multi-valued dependencies” on the CarModel.

Multi-valued dependencies occur when two attributes in a table are independent of each other, but both depend on a third attribute. This is important for achieving fourth normal form (4NF), which addresses certain types of redundancy not handled by earlier normal forms.
