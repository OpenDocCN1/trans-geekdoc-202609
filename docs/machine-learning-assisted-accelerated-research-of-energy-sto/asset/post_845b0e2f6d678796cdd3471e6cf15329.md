To verify the feasibility of our method, the experimental validation was conducted by preparing the xBaTiO3-(1 − x)Bi(Zn2/3Ti1/3)O3 material system that was not included in the data set. The error range between the model's predicted values and the experimental values fell within an acceptable range, indicating that our method possesses certain practical utility.
