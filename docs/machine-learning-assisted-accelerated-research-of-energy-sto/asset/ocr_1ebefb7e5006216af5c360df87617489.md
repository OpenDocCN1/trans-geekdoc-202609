knowledge. Using this method, we have identified the key features that influenced the models and conducted a brief analysis. To verify the feasibility of our method, the experimental validation was conducted by preparing the xBaTiO3-(1 − x)Bi(Zn2/3Ti1/3)O3 material system that was not included in the data set. The error range between the model's predicted values and the experimental values fell within an acceptable range, indicating that our method possesses certain practical utility. We believe that our method can also be extended to other material systems such as linear dielectrics and antiferroelectrics. In addition, future endeavors will explore the integration of genetic programming methods to achieve multiobjective optimization.
