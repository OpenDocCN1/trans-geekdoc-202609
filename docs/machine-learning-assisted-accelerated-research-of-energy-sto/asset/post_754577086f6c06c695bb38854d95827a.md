From Figure 8a, it can be observed that the two most important features affecting the W_rec′ prediction model are E and ΔP, both of which are important parameters for measuring the energy storage performance of capacitors. As their values increase, a larger W_rec′ is obtained, which is consistent with our physical knowledge. Figure 8c,e visualizes the tendency of E values and ΔP values, as well as the corresponding SHAP values. It can be observed that as E values increase, ΔP values also increase, and similarly, the SHAP values of E also increase. When E values exceed approximately 120, the corresponding SHAP values are all positive, indicating that higher applied electric fields are more conducive to achieving higher energy storage densities. When ΔP values exceed 15, most of the corresponding SHAP values are also positive, suggesting that larger ΔP values are more likely to result in higher energy storage densities.

From Figure 8b, it can be observed that the two most important features affecting the η′ prediction model are DV and R. To better visualize the relationship between features and SHAP values, a SHAP dependence plot of DV and R for the LGBM η′ model was generated, as shown in Figure 8d. It can be observed that when the value of DV exceeds 1.81, it tends to drive the energy storage efficiency toward lower values. This implies that to obtain BaTiO3-based ceramic materials with high energy storage efficiency, it is necessary to control the value of the valence electron distance to be less than 1.81. As can be seen from Figure 8f, when the value of the ionic radius exceeds 2.55, it will also exert a negative influence on the η′ model prediction.

## CONCLUSIONS

In this research, ML models combined with different feature construction strategies were developed for predicting the energy storage density and efficiency of BT-BMO material systems, allowing us to explore the intricate relationships between material compositions, related properties, sintering parameters, and energy storage properties. Different feature construction strategies for predicting Wrec' and η' of BT-BMO ceramics were compared through rigorous analyses, and it turned out that features incorporating multidimensional useful information enable machine learning models to achieve superior performance. The establishment of the GBR regression model for Wrec' and the LGBM regression model for η' based on the strategy III exhibits high levels of accuracy. To enhance the credibility of the results, we have conducted a 10-fold cross-validation. The 10-fold r2 values for the GBR Wrec' model is 0.974, and for the LGBM η' model is 0.894. This demonstrates the outstanding predictive capability of these models, confirming their robustness and reliability in forecasting. To identify the most important features and further improve training efficiency, the wrapper method was used to find the optimal feature subsets for Wrec' and η' prediction models, respectively.
