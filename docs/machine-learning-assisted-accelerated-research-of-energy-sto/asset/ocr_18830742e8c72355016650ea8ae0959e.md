In our data set, some data involve the composite doping of Nb5+ along with other ions into the Ti site of BaTiO3. Notably, Ta5+ shares the same valence and ionic radius (0.64 Å) with Nb5+, yet there are limited reports on the energy storage properties of relaxor ferroelectric ceramic materials. Compared to Nb2O5 (≈3.4 eV), Ta2O5 possesses a wider band gap (≈4 eV), which can effectively reduce leakage current and energy loss, thereby enhancing the breakdown field strength and energy storage efficiency.44 The majority of the data in the data set that pertain to Nb5+ ions involve its combination with Zn2+, such as Zn2/3Nb1/3. (Zn2/3Ta1/3)O3. Therefore, this material system of BT-BZT was selected for experimental verification.
