# # Conclusion

The power of this particular principle comes not directly from its own content but instead, in the way that it helps us to remain compliant with the principle of encapsulation. If we're doing encapsulation correctly, then it is highly likely that we are honouring the *Tell, Don't Ask* principle. Conversely, if we are doing *Tell, Don't Ask* correctly, then we are equally likely to be doing encapsulation correctly too.

The key thing here though is that the *Tell, Don't Ask* principle is much more likely to lodge in the front of our coding minds and give us pause when we trip up in our code. Hence the analogy to an Ohio-class guided missile submarine. You may not see it, but you'll know it's there when it fires on you!
