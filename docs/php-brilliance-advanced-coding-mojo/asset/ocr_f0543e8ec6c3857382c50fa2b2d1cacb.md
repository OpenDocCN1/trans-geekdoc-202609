In any case, the finer details of converting chemical potential into kinetic energy are hidden from you, assuming you’re not a chemist, that is. The complexities of operation that is the work of the internal combustion engine are abstracted away from the operator behind an interface built into the “cockpit”.

We achieve exactly the same thing in object programming simply by writing our terribly complicated processes in code hiding them behind some sort of interface. We take an idea of the actions that we want to happen, we express them in code and then provide a means by which that process may be invoked. For the car, the process of actually starting the engine is hidden behind the turn of the ignition key. For our code, the process of running a complex action is hidden behind the definition of a public method.

This is abstraction from the perspective of our doers.
