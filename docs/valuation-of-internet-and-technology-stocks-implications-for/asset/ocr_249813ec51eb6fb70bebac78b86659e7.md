| **Rebates, discounts and other sales incentives** | These refer to certain sales incentives offered by Internet companies. For example, free one-month service, discount coupons offered by e-tailers, or rebates offered by ISPs in exchange for customers' agreement to subscribe to Internet service for three years. These costs are commonly included in marketing expense

Extract from the annual report of Barnesandnoble.com for the period ended 31 December 1999:

B&N.com has included in marketing expenses approximately $6 million, or 3% of sales, of coupon redemptions for the full year ended 1999. No coupons were redeemed in 1998. Had B&N.com treated coupon redemptions as a reduction to sales and marketing expense in 1999, gross margin would have decreased from 21.0% to 18.6% and marketing and sales expense as a percentage of sales would have decreased from 55.1% to 53.7%. | Such rebates and discounts should be considered a reduction of revenues |
