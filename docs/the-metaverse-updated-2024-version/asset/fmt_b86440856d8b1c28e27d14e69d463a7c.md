
Zuckerberg’s repositioning of Facebook was also aided, and perhaps implicitly encouraged, by his competitors. In March of 2021, the social gaming platform Roblox had completed the largest-ever initial public offering (IPO) in the history of video gaming, finishing its first trading day at a $45 billion valuation.^([4](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr4)) In its prospectus, Roblox declared itself a Metaverse company, while highlighting that its business plan “assumed” the “adoption of the Metaverse” which, in its view, was now “materializing” after 30 years of advances in computing power, high-bandwidth internet connections, and other related technologies.^([5](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr5)) Moreover, many Wall Street analysts had argued the commonalities between `Roblox` and “2D” social networks such as Facebook, Instagram, and Snapchat. At the time of its IPO, `Roblox` had more than 40 million daily users and nearly 200 million monthly, and estimated that three-quarters of Americans aged 9–12 used the platform on a regular basis.^([6](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr6)) When Facebook purchased Instagram in 2012, the image-sharing network had fewer than 30 million monthly users; by the end of 2021, it had more than a billion monthly users. When Snapchat rebuffed a Facebook acquisition offer in 2013, it had fewer than 25 million monthly users; by the end of 2021, it had more than 500 million.

In May 2021, two months after Roblox’s record-breaking IPO, Satya Nadella, the CEO of Microsoft, the world’s second-most-valuable public company, began to talk publicly about the enterprise Metaverse that Microsoft was in the process of building. At its annual Ignite conference later that year, Nadella boasted to developers, “I can’t overstate how much of a breakthrough \[the Metaverse\] is.”^([7](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr7)) May of 2021 also saw China’s largest company, the internet gaming giant Tencent, unveil its vision of the Metaverse, which it dubbed “Hyper Digital Reality.” In early August, Krafton, the maker of the blockbuster battle royale video game `PUBG`, completed its IPO, the second largest in South Korea’s history. Krafton’s investment bankers made sure to tell would-be investors that the company would also be a global leader in the enormously valuable and inevitable “Metaverse.”

August also saw Jensen Huang, CEO and founder of computing, semiconductor, and AI giant Nvidia, which also ranked among the 10 largest public companies in the world, declare that the Metaverse was the “next evolution of the internet,”^([8](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr8)) which he later said would have an economy larger than that of the physical world.^([9](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr9)) By the end of the month, Chinese internet giants Alibaba, the 13th-most-valuable public company in the world, and TikTok owner ByteDance, a start-up whose $500 billion valuation in private markets would have made it the 11th, both began to register various Metaverse trademarks and acquire promising VR- and 3D-related start-ups.

Throughout 2020 and 2021, the Metaverse had also become a popular theme among blockchain and crypto users, developers, and investors. To many in the community, blockchain technologies were needed to build the Metaverse—or at least to build one that protected the rights of individual users and developers, rather than the preferences of whichever groups or governments might own or control the Metaverse. Others argued that, at a minimum, the rise of virtual-only currencies with trillion-dollar market capitalizations, as well as virtual-only items worth tens of millions, demonstrated that society was ready for the Metaverse. In any case, crypto’s rising value was indisputable. In the 12 months leading up to Facebook’s name change, the combined value of all cryptocurrencies and tokens had surged from $400 billion to more than $2.5 trillion.^([10](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr10))

By the end of 2021, the term “Metaverse” had appeared in more than 277 filings made with the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), 86 of which preceded Facebook’s announcement.^([11](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr11)) By comparison, “Metaverse” had appeared in fewer than two dozen filings from January 2001 through October 2020, when Roblox filed for its IPO.^([12](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr12)) It took 2022 only 62 days to break 2021’s record. And it did so in its own record-breaking fashion.

## Trillions and Trillions

On January 18, 2022, Microsoft announced it would acquire Activision Blizzard, the largest independent video game publisher outside of China, at an enterprise value of $75 billion—making it the largest acquisition in big-tech history. Microsoft’s press release declared, “This acquisition will accelerate the growth in Microsoft’s gaming business across mobile, PC, console and cloud and will provide building blocks for the Metaverse.”^([13](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr13))

Over the following months, every institution seemed to be talking about the Metaverse, whether they made digital toys or physical ones, ran data centers or oil pipelines, crafted sandwiches or gave sermons,^([‡](#introduction.xhtml#footnote-062)) or steered global monetary policy, international peace, or worldwide police enforcement. In total, 2,934 SEC filings used “Metaverse” in 2022, more than ten times as many as in 2021.^([14](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr14))

Increased attention to the Metaverse was propelled by independent validations of the Metaverse’s market potential. The management consultancies Boston Consulting Group and McKinsey & Company estimated the Metaverse would contribute $1.3 trillion^([15](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr15)) and between $4 and $5 trillion^([16](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr16)) to global GDP by 2030, representing roughly 1.1%–4.2% of world GDP by the end of the decade and 4%–17% of growth in world GDP during it.^([17](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr17)) The investment bank Goldman Sachs projected between $2.5 and $12.5 trillion by 2030,^([18](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr18)) while Citi, another bank, and KPMG, a Big Four accounting firm, each predicted $13 trillion.^([19](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr19)) The investment bank Morgan Stanley estimated that the combined Metaverse economies of the United States and China would be worth $16 trillion annually, with other countries, markets, and territories generating trillions more.^([20](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr20))

Governments, too, weighed in. In fact, the response from many governments predated Facebook’s name change. In May of 2021, South Korea’s Ministry of Science and ICT (Information and Communications Technology) announced the South Korean “Metaverse Alliance,” spanning over 450 companies, including SK Telecom, Woori Bank, and Hyundai Motor. The following year, the ministry announced a $200 million initiative for the alliance intended to make South Korea the fifth-largest Metaverse market by 2026, ranking twice as high as it does in traditional GDP measure.^([21](#notes_split_000.xhtml#introfr21))
