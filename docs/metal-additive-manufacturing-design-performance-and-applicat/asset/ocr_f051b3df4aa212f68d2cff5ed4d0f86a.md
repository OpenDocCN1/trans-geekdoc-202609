Figure 9. Mean aspect ratio values for primary columnar and size of equiaxial $\beta$-Ti grains ($d_{av}$): Ti-6Al-4V-0 (a) and Ti-6Al-4V-1 (b) as-quenched samples. I/d—mean aspect ratio of primary columnar $\beta$-grains; $d_{av}$—mean size of equiaxed primary $\beta$-grains.

The quenching of the Ti-6Al-4V-0 samples allowed us to obtain more uniform aspect ratio values relating to the primary $\beta$-Ti grains (Figure 9a). The mean aspect ratio (I/d) values calculated for the bottom, middle, and top parts of the wall are 3.4 ± 1.1, 3.4 ± 1.1, and 3.6 ± 1.3, respectively (Figure 9a). For the as-quenched Ti-6Al-4V-1 samples, the average aspect ratio values did not change compared to those of the as-built ones, and they were 2.6 ± 0.5, 2.8 ± 0.7, and 3.4 ± 1.2 (Figure 9b).

According to TEM (Figure 10a,b,d,e)), the volume fraction of $\alpha'$-Ti increased significantly after quenching in both the Ti-6Al-4V-0 and Ti-6Al-4V-1 samples (Figure 10c,f). At the same time, the $\beta$-phase was partially retained between the $\alpha$-plates in the as-quenched Ti-6Al-4V-1 samples (Figure 10e), as confirmed by the corresponding XRD pattern (Figure 11).
