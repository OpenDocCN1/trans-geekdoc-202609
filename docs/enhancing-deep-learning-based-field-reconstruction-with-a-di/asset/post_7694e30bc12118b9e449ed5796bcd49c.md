# Result

We apply the general DSO framework for six benchmarks containing strong nonlinear dynamics over a wide range of spatiotemporal scales. The relative.r.m.s.e. $||q - \hat{q}||/q$ is used to evaluate the reconstruction performance, where $q$ and $\hat{q}$ denote the reconstructed field and-Baselines: six reconstruction models are used to verify the DSO, MLP, CNN, Fourier neural operator (FNO), and Senseler. Furthermore, DSO is compared with interpolation methods, namely, condition-based sampler (DSO), enhanced-clustering sampler (EES), and correlation-based sampler (CCS). These static methods optimize the bilinear field before interpolation. DWI is also considered as the baseline, which simultaneously optimizes the integrated field during placement. ISH is compared and serves as the baseline. The differential operator (US) and random sampler (RS) are also considered as baselines.

To investigate dependence of DSPO for periodic physical problem, we evaluated reconstructed performance of DSPO and the two baselines RS and US. The results are shown in Fig. 2. RS exhibits large variance, under varying numbers of sensors, DSPO consistently outperforms RS and US in all scenarios. (a) DSPO outperforms RS and US in all models. (b) DSPO shows comparable performance with MLP. Additionally, compared with U.S., DSPO outperforms PODN and Sensiver by 1-2 orders of magnitude. We also show the absolute errors of MLP, PODN, and CNN. Models using DSPO show significantly lower errors, especially in the flow direction. The optimized sensor distribution is shown in Fig. 2b. The advantage of optimized sensor placement consistently covers the wake area. More importantly, Fig. 2c also illustrates the importance of the rank of optimized placement. Removing sensors from the wake area results in a greater decrease in reconstruction performance. This result highlights the ability of DSPO to capture flow field characteristics and is more beneficial for flow field reconstruction. Comparisons of DSPO with more extensive SPO baselines are shown in Supplementary Table 1. DSPO provides lower reconstruction errors compared to other methods.
