comparisons between different fusion methods, AW-FPN improves by 2.91%, 1.63%, and 1.97% when using AFF (channel attention only) and by 4.09%, 2.06%, and 2.84% when using the proposed AWF (both channel and spatial attention), indicating that the attention-based fusion methods outperform the linear fusion methods and, that to generate non-linear fusion weights, it is better to use both channel and spatial attention rather than only using single channel attention. When CS²A-Net is used as the base detector, our ultimate AW-FPN further improves the AP by 3.96%, 0.38%, and 1.50%. Similar experimental results are obtained on the other two datasets. From Tables 8 and 9, for the SSDD and HRSC2016, the proposed AW-FPN achieves the most outstanding performance on RetinaNet-R and a considerable improvement on the advanced CS²A-Net, which proves the effectiveness of our approach.
