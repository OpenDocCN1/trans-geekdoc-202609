## 5. Conclusions

In this paper, we propose a novel self-supervised despeckling algorithm with an enhanced U-Net (SSEUNet). The proposed SSEUNet is composed of generation training pairs (GTP) module, enhanced U-Net (EUNet) and a self-supervised training loss function with a regularization loss. The proposed SSEUNet has the following advantages.
