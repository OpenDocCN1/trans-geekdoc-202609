# AO2 Level 4

The full level descriptor for Level 4 of AO2 is:

> > Ability to identify and analyse issues central to the question, showing some understanding of current debate and proposals for reform, OR to identify most of the relevant points of law in issue. Ability to develop clear arguments OR apply points of law clearly to a given factual situation, and reach a sensible conclusion.

# AO2 Level 5

The full level descriptor for this is:

> > Ability to identify correctly the relevant and important points of criticism, showing good understanding of current debate and proposals for reform, OR to identify all of the relevant points of law in issue. A high level of ability to develop arguments OR apply points of law accurately and pertinently to a given factual situation, and reach a cogent, logical and well-informed conclusion.

Notice that AO2 has alternatives depending on whether the question is an essay or involves applying law to factual situations (problem questions and dilemma board/evaluation questions). These are identified in the following comparison chart.

# AO2.4.3 Assessment Objective 3

For this there are only four levels. These are:

+   - Level 1 A limited attempt to present logical and coherent arguments
+   - Level 2 An adequate ability to present logical and coherent arguments
+   - Level 3 A good ability to present logical and coherent arguments
+   - Level 4 An accomplished presentation of logical and coherent arguments

All the Levels of Assessment are set out in full at the end of the Specification booklet issued by OCR. This booklet is online at www.ocr.org.uk.
