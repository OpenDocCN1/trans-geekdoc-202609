## Preface

This third edition has been updated. All the topics on the specification of the OCR Examining Board are included. It covers the general principles of criminal law: actus reus, mens rea, strict liability, attempts and general defences. It also covers the law on the offences of murder, voluntary manslaughter, involuntary manslaughter, assaults, theft, robbery and burglary. As well as explaining the present law, the text explores problem areas and proposals for reform in each topic.

The text is broken down with the use of many subheadings, diagrams and activities. There are also key facts charts and key cases charts to help students to revise topics. In this edition self-test questions have been added. These can be used in two ways. First, when doing a topic for the first time students can check their understanding working through the questions and referring back to the material. Secondly, the self-test questions can be used as a revision aid and answered without looking at the text.

After the chapters on substantive law, there are four appendices. The first contains extracts from Law Commission Reports. This material expands on topics in some chapters and gives the student experience of reading source material. The second explains the style of the examinations in Units G153 and G154. It also explains the assessment objectives and the levels of assessment used for marking exam answers. The third appendix gives students help with how to answer examination questions on Unit G153. The fourth contains tips on how to answer questions for Unit G154.

The law is stated as I believe it to be on 1 December 2011.

Jacqueline Martin
