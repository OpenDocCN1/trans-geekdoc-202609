# A4.4 Using the source materials

When you refer to the source material, this can be done by giving the source page and

line reference. Do not copy out large amounts of the source: this will earn little or no credit for you. The important points are showing an understanding of arguments in the source, being able to explain them in your own words and to use them in discussion.

Remember that linking your answer to the source material is important, especially in questions 1 and 2.
