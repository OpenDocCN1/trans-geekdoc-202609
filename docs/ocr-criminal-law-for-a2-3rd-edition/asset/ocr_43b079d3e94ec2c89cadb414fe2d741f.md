# 11.3 Problems in the law on robbery and burglary

# 11.3.1 The element of theft in robbery

Robbery requires a theft to be completed. However, there is a difference in the way that the law on theft is applied in robbery to the way the courts have applied it in theft cases. In theft cases it has been held that appropriation occurs at one point in time (see section 10.1.3).
