# Appendix 4

# Answering questions on Unit G154

The topics for this paper change each year.
OCR usually sends out the source material for the topics towards the end of the summer term for use in the examinations in January and June of the following year.

# A4.1 The case study question

Question 1 is always on a case in the source material. The question in the specimen material carries 16 marks, and you should spend about 18 minutes on this question; 12 marks are given for Assessment Objective 2 (analysis and evaluation) and 4 marks for AO3.

The source material on the case will provide a good starting point for your answer, but you must use the information in the source to analyse and evaluate.

Make sure that you focus on exactly what the question is asking. For example, a question may ask about the significance of a particular case to the development of the law in a specific area. One approach could be to state what the law was before the case, explain the problems in that law and from this show how the case developed the law.

# A4.2 The essay question

Question 2 can be on any area of criminal law in the Special Study Material. The question on the specimen paper is worth 34 marks, and you should

spend about 36 minutes on this question. Both AO1 and AO2 are tested in this question, with 16 marks available for AO1 and 14 marks for AO2.

For AO1, you will need to bring in extra knowledge, not merely rely on the source material. Remember that to get to Level 5 you need to show ‘wide-ranging accurate, detailed knowledge’.

For AO2, the level of analysis and evaluation is the important aspect. To get to Level 5 you must show the ability to identify correctly the relevant and important points of criticism and also show good understanding of current debate and proposals for reform.

There are also 4 marks for AO3.

# A4.3 The application question

There will usually be three mini-scenarios, each of which will raise different points of law. Overall there are 10 marks available for AO1 and 20 marks for AO2, with each mini-scenario being worth 10 marks. You should spend about 36 minutes on this question.

Some of the knowledge required will be in the source material, but there will be other knowledge needed, as there are 10 marks for AO1. So be sure you give knowledge of relevant extra cases and/or sections of Acts of Parliament.
