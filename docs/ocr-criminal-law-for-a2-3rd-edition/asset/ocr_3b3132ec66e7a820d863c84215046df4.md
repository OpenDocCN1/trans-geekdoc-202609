These Assessment Objectives are central to the marking of each question. When examiners mark, they have to decide a mark for each Assessment Objective for each question. They do this by considering the descriptors for each level of assessment.

The higher the level of assessment achieved the higher the final Grade which will be awarded.

# A2.4 Levels of assessment

# A2.4.1 Assessment Objective 1

There are five levels of assessment. Briefly these are:

+   - Level 1 Very limited knowledge
+   - Level 2 Limited knowledge
+   - Level 3 Adequate knowledge
+   - Level 4 Good, well-developed knowledge
+   - Level 5 Wide-ranging, accurate, detailed knowledge

From these brief descriptions you can see that it is important to know the law in each area as thoroughly as possible if you want a high mark.

Let’s consider Levels 4 and 5 in more detail and compare them to see what is needed for a top level answer.

# AO1 Level 4

The full descriptor for Level 4 of Assessment Objective 1 is:

> > Good, well-developed knowledge with a clear understanding of the relevant concepts and principles. Where appropriate, candidates will be able to elaborate by good citation to relevant statutes and case-law.

# AO1 Level 5

The full descriptor for Level 5 of Assessment Objective 1 is:

> > Wide-ranging, accurate, detailed knowledge with a clear and confident understanding of the relevant concepts and principles. Where appropriate, candidates will be able to elaborate with wide citation of relevant statutes and case-law.

# Comparison

The following table compares these two levels.

| AO1 Level 4 | AO1 Level 5 |
|---|---|
| Good knowledge | Wide-ranging knowledge |
| Well-developed knowledge | Accurate, detailed knowledge |
| Clear understanding | Clear and confident understanding |
| Good citation | Wide citation |

The important points to note are that to get to Level 5 it is essential to show knowledge that is wide-ranging (and, of course, relevant to the question). That knowledge also has to be accurate and detailed.

For both Levels 4 and 5 there must be citation of relevant cases and statutes. Again Level 5 is more demanding as there must be wide citation.

# A2.4.2 Assessment Objective 2

For AO2 there are also five levels of assessment. Briefly these are:

+   - Level 1 Ability to explain at least one of the simpler points central to the question
+   - Level 2 Ability to explain some of the more obvious points central to the question
+   - Level 3 Ability to analyse most of the more obvious points central to the question
+   - Level 4 Ability to identify and analyse issues central to the question, showing some understanding of current debate and proposals for reform
+   - Level 5 Ability to identify correctly the relevant and important points of criticism, showing good understanding of current debate and proposals for reform
