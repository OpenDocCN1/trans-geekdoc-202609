# OCR Criminal Law for A2

Published in partnership with OCR, *OCR Criminal Law for A2* is the textbook for you if you are taking the Criminal Law option on the OCR A2 Law specification. The book is a comprehensive, authoritative and accessible guide to the option.

This new edition is fully updated with important changes to the law including the Coroners and Justice Act 2009.

# Key features:

+   - Self-test questions in each chapter
+   - Examination practice and questions from past papers
+   - Scenarios and case studies to illustrate the law
+   - Endorsed by OCR for use with the OCR A2 Law specification.
