We can now start to train our learner! What is exactly passing into the architecture of our learner?

+   • We are passing in two fully connected layers with 1000 and 500 neurons.
+   • The ps means “*dropout*”. We are passing in two parameters – (notice, we have to pass in one value per layer!)
+   • The embed_p parameter we are passing in, handles dropout with the embedding layers
+   • y_range is the interval of our target predictions
+   • We are using expo_rmspe for the metric and
+   • MSELossFlat as a loss-function because we are doing regression

If we omit the loss-function, the tabular-learner tries to figure out what is happening and chooses a loss-function on its own. Yet with regression we have to declare the loss-function, otherwise we get an error.
