Now let’s move over to the topic of **style transfer**.

For style transfer we make use of a concept called *feature loss* or sometimes called *perception loss*. The central idea of this concept is to take care of the model’s input and output features and compare which ones are the same and which ones are different. To get the style of one image transferred to another, you simply adjust the different input-/output features.
