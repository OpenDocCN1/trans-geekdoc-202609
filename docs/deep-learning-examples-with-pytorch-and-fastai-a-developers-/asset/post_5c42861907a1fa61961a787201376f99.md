### Cross-Fold-Validation

Cross Fold Validation is a technique to split your data into n-parts for each training. During each round you just use n-1 parts for training and one part for validation. This asserts that you never use the same data for training and validation and you can use all of your training data for training because you do not have to do a permanent split into training and validation data.

This means that the same examples can be in the training-set in one epoch and the validation-set in another.
To show this we will use the Imagewoof training data. We will have a deeper look into imagewoof when we build our own model architectures
