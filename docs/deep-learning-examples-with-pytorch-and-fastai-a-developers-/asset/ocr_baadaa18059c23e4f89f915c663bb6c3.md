Now we have seen that our architecture works and returns good results. We can try to use a bigger model for transfer learning and restart using ResNet50.

A bigger model always needs more resources on training but doesn’t necessarily provide better results! A good idea is to start with a subset of your training data on a small model. Then you can do rapid prototyping and do not have to waste time while your model is training. If you think you have obtained good results, train your model on the complete training data and/or try a bigger model architecture.
