Let’s try this and scale our image points to e.g. 224 (the scale of imagenet).

```
_myScale_pnts(tensor_points, 224)
```

TensorPoint([[0.7857, 0.3393], [0.7857, 0.7857], [1.4554, 1.6875]])

The result represents what we have expected! Our tensors scaled to an image size of 224.
