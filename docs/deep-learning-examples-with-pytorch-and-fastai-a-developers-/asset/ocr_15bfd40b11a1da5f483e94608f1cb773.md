So our error rate went bad. After the first round of training we had an error rate of about 0.08 but now we got 0.11!

What happened here? This is because this time we had unfreezed all layers of the model before running the training loop and training such a big model is a way more difficult task than just retraining the last layer of a given model. Therefore we have to carefully decide on the training parameters.

Fastai provides a method called lr_find() that helps us in finding a good learning rate.

Let’s reload our weights and call lr_find() beforehand.
