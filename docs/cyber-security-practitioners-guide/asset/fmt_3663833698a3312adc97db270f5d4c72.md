
Therefore, an area for further research could be the development of a dataset of datasets, whereby datasets from verified sources with data of such nature could be collated to ease and perpetuate much needed analytical research into such behavioural trends online and on social media.

More detailed keyword analytics methods could also be used on a wider range of datasets, again, to provide more context and to build a more detailed results picture from the available raw data.
