In the context of evaluating performance in these applications this mathematical definition provides little insight (especially to the layperson, but even to mathematics professors!). Moreover, the nomenclature ROC is not particularly descriptive. To prove why AUC is actually the "probability that a randomly chosen individual from the positive class has a higher score than a randomly chosen person", consider scores $Sp$ and $Sn$ as our machine learning algorithms to members of the positive and negative classes, respectively. The statement above can be expressed as $p(S_P > S_N)$ i.e. what is the probability that the positive member receives a higher score than a negative distribution. Using the definitions in (12.4) and (12.5) can be written as the conditional probability

```
$p(S_P > S_N) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \int_{s}^{\infty} f_N(r)\cdot f_p(s)dsdr\quad (12.8)
```

which is equivalent to

```
$p(S_P > S_N) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_N(r)\int_{s}^{\infty} f_p(s)dsdr = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f_N(r)\cdot(1-F_p(r))dr,\quad (12.9)
```

which is identical to (12.7).

Using the term AUC, as we have done in this, is acceptable in technical situations but should be avoided when associations. Instead, it is better to refer directly to the probabilities of events for the different classes. Imagine, for example, the probability that an individual in the positive class is given a higher score than a person in the negative class is 70% (which was roughly the level observed in the previous example). This implies that

**Medical** 30% of cases where a person with cancer is compared to someone without, the wrong person will be selected for treatment.

**Personality** 30% of paired cases an advent to a more neurotic person will be shown to be a less neurotic person.

**Goals** 30% of paired cases the situation that was less likely to lead to a goal will be predicted to be a goal.

**Sentencing** In 30% of cases where a person who is going to commit a crime is compared to someone won't, the less likely to commit the crime will receive a harsher sentencing.

Clearly there are differences in the seriousness of such outcomes, that we must constantly be aware of when, such words should be used to describe the performance rather than simply reporting the AUC is 0.7.
