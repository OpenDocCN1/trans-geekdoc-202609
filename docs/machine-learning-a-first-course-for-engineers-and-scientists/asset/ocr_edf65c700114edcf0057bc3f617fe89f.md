Furthermore, and more importantly, if we want our classifier to generalize to new data, then it is not reasonable to assume that the length and energy of a song will give a complete picture of the artists style. Even, with the best possible model, there is some ambiguity about what artist has recorded a song if we only use these two inputs. This ambiguity is modeled as random noise. Also for the car stopping distance there could be a certain amount of random effects, not only in x but also in y. By using k=1 and adapting very well to the training data, the predictions will depend only on the interesting patterns in the problem, but also on the (more or less) random effects that has shaped the training data. We typically interpret this as overfitting.
With k-NN classifier can mitigate overfitting by increasing the regression of the neighborhood used to compute the prediction, which is increasing k. With, as k=20 the predictions are no longer based only on the closest neighbor, but a majority vote among the 20 neighbors.
