There are also problems of privacy preservation and accountability. The data contains sentences written in Internet chat groups by real-world people about other real-world people, and information later be tracked back to those individuals. It is something that Reddit will suddenly appear, in a slightly modified form, as a sentence written or spoken by a bot. These can also arise in medical applications where sensitive data is used to train models and may be revealed in some of the suggestions made by these. Nor are the problems limited to text, machine learning on video sequences is used to generate new, sequences, that can be difficult for viewers to distinguish from reality.

As we wrote at the start of this section, this book is primarily about machine learning methods. But what we see, now near the end of the book, that the limitations of our methods are also determined by having access to good data quality. In the case of about language and society this cannot be done without becoming aware of the culture we live in and its history. This includes centuries of oppression, acts of slavery and systemic racism. With all examples in this chapter, we can't hide behind neutrality, while a method might be purely computational, the data we put in it is shaped by this history.

We hope that this chapter will helped you start to think about some of the potential pitfalls in machine learning. We have emphasised throughout that the key starting point is awareness. Awareness that there is no equation for fairness; awareness that you can't be fair in all possible ways; awareness that it is easy to exaggerate performance (when you shouldn't); awareness of the hype around machine learning; awareness that technical jargon can obscure explanations of what your model does; awareness that data sets encode biases that machine learning methods don't understand; awareness that other engineers might fail to understand that they are not objective and neutral.

Being aware of a problem doesn't solve it, but it is certainly a good start.
