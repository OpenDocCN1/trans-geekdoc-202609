Digital asset 

Simply said, a digital asset is anything that exists in digital form and has the ability to be used (duplicate, a right to copy, reproduce, and modify). Documents, audio or visual information, photographs, and other related digital data, for example, are all termed digital assets.

## 5.5 NFTs vs. Cryptocurrency

The distinction between cryptocurrencies and non-fungible tokens must be clarified. Despite the fact that they both use blockchain technology, the fundamental differences between them might help us comprehend how NFTs work. The main distinction stems from the fact that cryptocurrency is fungible. You can, for example, exchange one Bitcoin for another. You cannot, however, do so with an NFT. A non-fungible token is linked to a single digital asset and cannot be exchanged for another. The major distinction between the NFT and cryptocurrency is that, unlike cryptocurrency, NFTs are one-of-a-kind representations of real-world goods that cannot be traded for one another. Cryptocurrencies can be traded for each other because their value will not depreciate. Cryptocurrencies and NFTs, on the other hand, are decentralized and governed by the communities that run them. Furthermore, cryptocurrencies and NFTs may be accessed via a digital public ledger, which makes all transactions transparent.

## 5.6 NFT vs. Bitcoin

While an NFT uses the same technology as a cryptocurrency, such as Bitcoin, the similarities end there. Bitcoin may be traded for one another and have the same value, i.e., one rupee is always worth another rupee, and one Bitcoin is always worth another Bitcoin, regardless of price variations against other similar currencies. NFTs aren't like other materials. Each NFT (virtual asset) has a digital signature that can't be copied exactly due to encoding.

## 5.7 The pros and cons of NFTs 

We have listed below some potential pros and cons: 

### 5.7.1 Pros of NFTs 

Some of the advantages of NFTs include: 

Security

NFTs can be traded in a safe and transparent environment. NFTs work as unchangeable digital signatures, giving collectors extraordinary trust. This assurance also extends to the art's future legacy. The blockchain protects digital works from censorship & physical degradation, but they must still be carefully stored. Blockchain technology also enables people to move large amounts of money around the world securely and in full view of the public. NFTs now guarantee legitimacy, uniqueness, and ownership of unique assets, just as blockchain technology built a trust protocol that enabled the establishment of digital currency.

Price appreciation

Physical valuables (such as art) have a long history of increasing in value, and digital art could follow suit.

Increased access to buyers and sellers

Buying as well as selling digital assets as NFTs gives you access to a much larger pool of buyers and sellers than before.

They give artists ownership of digital assets

When content creators develop a digital asset, an NFT allows them to not only 

demonstrate authenticity but also profit from it. This might represent a big money stream for the originator of memes, which are extensively shared.

They’re unique and collectible

Many people like the thrill of acquiring a one-of-a-kind or extremely rare object. Collectible content, especially digital goods, benefits from NFTs because they add an extra layer of authenticity.

They’re immutable

Non-fungible tokens can never be changed, erased, or replaced because they are blockchain-based. This is a valuable attribute to have when demonstrating the origin or authenticity of digital content.

Composability

One of the most exciting aspects of NFTs is that they may be thought of as interconnected Lego pieces. This means that designers can launch NFTs that capitalize on the success of previous NFTs. Any NFT project can be regarded as a module of a much broader, interconnected ecosystem because of its composability.

They can include smart contracts

Smart contracts are yet another exciting aspect of blockchain technology. They can basically hold instructions that are carried out when specific criteria are met. As a result, an NFT with a smart contract might provide artists with a portion of the profit if the NFT is sold in the future. Artists and producers can be paid in the future based on the use and resale of their work using "smart contracts," which are a series of coded commands incorporated into the blockchain.

### 5.7.2 Cons of NFTs

The disadvantages of NFTs include: 

They are speculative in nature

The big question is whether or not NFTs have any real value. Are they a good long-term investment or just a fad? It's difficult to say. Currently, the only criterion for valuing NFTs is their emotive quality.

Digital assets can be copied

The fact that someone possesses the NFT of a digital object does not rule out the possibility of copies. Art could be copied as well as pasted, GIFs can be shared hundreds of times, and videos can be shared throughout the internet. You don't have power over the asset just because you own the NFT; it's only proof of ownership.

Environmental costs

The environmental impact of blockchain-based cryptocurrencies like Ether and Bitcoin has received a lot of attention. Entering records onto a blockchain requires a lot of computing power. The long-term viability of blockchain-based assets is a major concern.

They can be stolen

Despite the fact that the technology behind NFTs is very safe, many of the exchanges and platforms are not. As a result, there have been a number of stories of NFTs being stolen as a result of cyber security breaches.

Decrease in value due to reduced demand

As many of the NFTs are immobile assets that don't generate any money on their own, subjective indicators such as buyer demand are used to determine their worth. Consequently, extremely high prices may not bear indefinitely, and NFTs may lose noticeable value.
