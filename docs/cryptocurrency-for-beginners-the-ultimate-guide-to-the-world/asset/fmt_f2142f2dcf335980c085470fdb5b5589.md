## 4.3 Cardano

Cardano is a decentralized cryptocurrency platform that is one of the world's first accessible and peer-reviewed networks. Output Data Hong Kong founded Cardano, headed by creator Charles Hoskinson, who's one of the numerous co-founders of Ether and has worked on Ethereum and Bitshares Classic. ## 4.4 Myths about Cryptocurrency

The universe of cryptocurrencies has flourished in recent years due to the asset's incredible profits. However, their meteoric growth, fueled by Bitcoin, has spawned a slew of myths that may or may not be genuine. Here are some typical cryptocurrency misconceptions.

Government can ban and stop cryptocurrencies.

It is feasible for governments to order financial institutions to cease dealing with cryptocurrency or any companies that deal with them, but it is practically hard to outright prohibit cryptocurrencies from existence. Cryptocurrency transactions are no more complicated than sending an email, and anybody who has internet access can do them with ease.

Governments have been apprehensive of the dangers involved with cryptocurrency because they want to exert more authority over anything that could be deemed a viable currency in the first place. However, a growing number of countries have shown a readiness to approach cryptocurrency with an inquisitive attitude in recent years.

Cryptocurrencies are used to support criminal, terrorist, and drug-related operations.
