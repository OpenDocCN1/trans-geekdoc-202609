
# 1.8 Decentralization

The movement of power and choice from a centralized entity to a dispersed network is referred to as decentralization in the blockchain. Decentralized networks aim to limit the amount of confidence that members must put in one another and prevent them from exerting control or power over others in ways that harm the network's performance.

## Why decentralization matters

The notion of decentralization is not new. Three basic network designs are often examined when developing a technological solution: centralized, distributed, and decentralized. While decentralized networks are often used in blockchain technology, a blockchain application cannot simply be classified as decentralized or not. Rather, decentralization should be extended to all components of a blockchain program on a sliding scale. Greater or fairer service may be accomplished by decentralizing resource utilization and access in an application. Decentralization has certain drawbacks, such as decreased transaction throughput, but the benefits of increased stability and levels of service outweigh the drawbacks.

## Benefits of decentralization

*   Optimizes Resource distribution

Decentralization may also aid in resource distribution optimization, ensuring that stated services are delivered with improved performance and reliability, as well as a lower risk of catastrophic failure.

*   Improves data reconciliation

Companies often share information with their partners. This data is then modified and kept in each party's silos, only to be resurfaced when it's time to transfer it downstream. Each time data is converted, the possibility of data loss or inaccurate data entering the workstream increases. Every entity gets access to a true, shared view of information thanks to decentralized data storage.

*   Provides a trustless environment

No one needs to recognize or trust anybody other in a decentralized public blockchain. In the format of a public ledger, each member of the network owns a duplicate of the same data. If a member's Ledger is tampered with or damaged in any manner, the rest of the network's members will reject it.

*   Reduces points of weakness

Decentralization may help to mitigate sources of vulnerability in systems when single actors are too reliant. Systemic failures might result from these flaws, such as inability to deliver expected service or ineffective service owing to resource depletion, recurrent outages, bottlenecks, a lack of appropriate incentives for effective service, or corruption.

## How decentralization compares

Wherever possible, decentralization should be used. Because it's a blockchain application, it doesn't have to be completely decentralized. Any blockchain solution's purpose is to provide what its customers need, which may or may not incorporate specific degrees of decentralization.

## Building blockchain applications leveraging the decentralization

Decentralization varies depending on the blockchain protocol, distributed application, Non-Governmental Organization, or any blockchain technology solution. The maturity of a solution, the time-proven dependability of its incentive systems and consensus procedures, and the capacity of the founders to strike the proper balance determine the adoption level. Many DAOs, for example, have components at various stages of decentralization: oracles may be partially decentralized, smart contracts may be fully centralized, and the governance framework for adjusting parameters may be community-driven and decentralized.

Decentralized cryptocurrency solutions are being researched and used by organizations of all types, sizes, and industries on a larger scale. Applications that offer quick international or emergency relief to persons in need without the involvement of a bank, government, or third-party agency are significant examples. Alternatively, programs that let users control their own user identity and data. Today, social media networks, businesses, and other groups sell this information for a profit, with no value to the user. It would be easier to make it egalitarian for everyone if it were decentralized.

To put it another way, blockchain decentralization is an information foundation that keeps a collection of materials and trades through a peer-to-peer network. It's possible that the "resource" isn't only cash or transaction records but also ownership, agreements, goods, and other information. Unlike previous peer-to-peer networks, a blockchain does not replicate the value that is sent.
