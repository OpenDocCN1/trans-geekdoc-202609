#### How Does Stellar Blockchain Work?

Like every blockchain technology, the Stellar network adds transactions to a shared and decentralized public ledger. The "Stellar Consensus Protocol," which is based on the Federal Byzantine Agreement (F.B.A.), is used by Stellar (S.C.P.). S.C.P. allows for faster transactions at cheaper costs by allowing everybody on the network to agree on the authenticity of a transaction in a matter of seconds. Each participant (referred to as a node) that assists in adding events to the global ledger chooses his or her mini-network of other trustworthy participants. Quorum Slices are the name for these small networks. As long as Consensus Slices overlap, the Stellar network can swiftly establish agreement on legitimate transactions and add them to the ledger.
