
Dr. Douglas Jackson and Barry K. Downey founded E-gold in 1996 as a private digital currency that allowed members of their website to transfer gold instantly using modern technology. E-gold differs from previous alternatives to federal currency in that it is entirely digital in nature, and transactions are completely irreversible. Despite the fact that the website was most likely started with good intentions, it quickly turned into a haven for criminals. Money launderers, identity thieves, fraudulent "financial" investors, and purchasers of child pornography all used E-gold because the gold transfers were irreversible. Moreover, the irreversibility of E-gold transactions meant that if one's account was hacked, there was no recourse. E-corporate gold's governance realized the website was being used for major illegal activities by 2006 and began blocking accounts. "E-gold operates legally but does not condone persons attempting to use e-gold for criminal activity," said CEO Douglas Jackson in a statement. E-gold has a long history of working with law enforcement agencies in the United States and around the world, providing data and investigative support in response to legitimate requests." In 2007, the US government ordered E-gold to block several E-gold accounts that were providing services similar to E-gold before indicting E-gold for a variety of criminal offenses. The main argument against E-gold was that it did not go far enough to prevent illegal activity by failing to implement a mechanism that prevented fraudulent users from simply creating new accounts as well as transferring their E-gold to them. E-gold was also unlicensed money transmitting business, despite the fact that it was not actually transferring money. Money-transmitting businesses in the United States are required by law to obtain a license, which includes keeping certain legal information on the users who create accounts. As a result, e-creators golds were found guilty of similar charges, including conspiracy. However, the legal ramifications for the company and corporate governance were minor, and E-gold was able to reach an agreement with the federal government, which allowed legitimate users to withdraw funds from their accounts, similar to how online poker players can do. E-gold is still operational today, but it no longer accepts new accounts or e-gold transfers from one user to another.

### 3.5.10 Hash-Cash

![](Images/image00210.jpeg)

Hash-cash was a forerunner to digital money. It was Satoshi's inspiration for Bitcoin's proof-of-work implementation. We must first deal with Hash-cash in order to comprehend Bitcoin's consensus mechanism and proof-of-work. Adam Back created hash-cash in 1997 as a way of preventing email spam. Email spam was an annoyance to anyone with an email account, even in 1997. This was due to a fundamental problem, according to Adam Back: all you had to do to email someone was send a few packets to a mail server. They made the decision to charge for emails. Sending an email was so cheap that it was practically free. Even though honest users only send a few emails per day, spammers can send tens of millions at little or no cost. Consider how much it would cost to send an email for a penny. This is fine for a regular user who has a few pennies to spare each day. Spammers, on the other hand, would have to pay millions of pennies, effectively ending their spam campaigns. You'll notice that this is a little backward: charging a penny per email presupposes the existence of digital pennies with which to pay. But there was no such solution, which is why we needed Bitcoin. So, how could Hashcash make spammers pay a virtual penny? The answer lies in Hashcash's core concept, which is now also present in Bitcoin: proof-of-work.

Hashcash's proof-of-work

The proof-of-work in Hashcash is pretty straightforward. The mail server first designates a difficulty level, which is a number that indicates how difficult the PoW must be. The default value is twenty. In Hashcash, the proof of work is to generate a message whose SHA-1 hash digest begins with 20 zeros in binary. How do you make a hash digest with a prefix that you specify? Remember that you shouldn't really be able to predict the output of a cryptographic hash function any better than chance. You don't have a choice but to force it. To solve this PoW, simply try different inputs till one of them has a sufficient number of 0s in front of it.

### 3.5.11 Ethereum

![Cryptocurrency prices today: Bitcoin gains over 4%, Ethereum surges more than 8% - Times of India](Images/image00211.jpeg)

Ethereum is a shared blockchain open-source framework and has its own crypto currency, Ether. As a forum for many other crypto currencies and for the application of decentralized intelligent contracts, ETH functions. In a 2013 white paper by Vitalik Buterin, Ethereum was first described. In summer 2014, Buterin secured support, along with other co-founders, for the project at a public online crowd sale and formally launched on July 30, 2015, the block chain. Ethereum itself is expected to become a digital forum for open apps that allows users worldwide to write and manage censorship, downtime and fraud-resistant apps.

History of Ethereum

Ethereum has eight co-founders. This is a large number for a crypto project. They first met on June 7, 2014, in Zug, Switzerland.

•       Russian-Canadian Vitalik Buterin wrote the initial white paper that first identified Ethereum in 2013 and still works on developing the network to this day. Just before ETH, Buterin co-founded & wrote for the Bitcoin Magazine news website.

•       British programmer Gavin Wood is possibly the second most influential co-founder of ETH.

•       Among the other co-founders of Ethereum are

•       Charles Hoskinson, who played the principal role in creating the Swiss-based Ethereum Foundation as well as its legal structure

•       Mihai Alise, who provided assistance in creating the Ethereum Foundation

•       Joseph Lubin, a Canadian entrepreneur who helped finance Ethereum during its early days

•       Amir Chetrit, who helped co-found Ethereum but walked away from it early into the development

Reasons behind the uniqueness of Ethereum 

Ethereum has pioneered the idea of a blockchain smart contract network. Smart contracts are computer systems that immediately perform the activities required to satisfy an arrangement between multiple parties on the internet. They were built to reduce the need for trusted intermediates between contractors, thereby lowering transaction costs while also increasing transaction efficiency. Ethereum’s principal breakthrough was developing a network that allowed it to conduct smart contracts using the blockchain, which further strengthens the already existing advantages of smart contract technology. Ethereum’s blockchain was built as a sort of one machine for the whole world. In addition to various smart contracts, Ethereum’s blockchain is able to host other crypto currencies, dubbed “tokens. In reality, this has been the most common use for the ETH platform so far.
