The seed requires certain conditions in order to germinate optimal moisture along with temperature conditions, thus constantly checking on the soil temperature and moisture requirements at several times [29]. Agriculture access is a starting point towards a better living, and agricultural tool development is the foundation for agricultural progress. Renuka Dhvale et al. The seed-spreading and digging robot will walk through several rows of soil, digging up, sowing seeds and covering the soil with a cover.
