# ## 1.3.2 Drones in agriculture

Agriculture is one of the major businesses that uses drones. Drones equipped with sensors and cameras are used for farm imaging, mapping, and surveying. There are drones that are both airborne and ground-based. Mobile robots called ground drones are used to assess areas. Unmanned aerial vehicles often known as aerial drones or unmanned aircraft systems are flying robots. Thanks to flight plans that are software-controlled and coordinated with sensors and GPS in their embedded systems, drones can be remotely commanded or flown automatically.

Drones equipped with sensors and cameras are used for farm imaging, mapping, and surveying. The drone data may be used to learn about crop health, irrigation, spraying, planting, the soil and field, plant counts, yield forecast, and much more. Drone surveys may be prearranged or drones may be brought and kept close to farms where they be maintained and recharged. Drones must be brought to nearby labs for data analysis after the surveys, which will enhance the usage of IoT in agriculture (Figure 1.16).
