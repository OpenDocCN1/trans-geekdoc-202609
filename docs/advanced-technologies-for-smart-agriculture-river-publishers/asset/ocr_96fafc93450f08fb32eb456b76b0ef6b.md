The primary goal of the government’s agricultural policy is to raise agricultural yield and productivity at a fair price. India’s agriculture strategy prioritizes modernizing the country’s agricultural industry. In this case, policy assistance involves the implementation of contemporary technology in agricultural operations as well as the use of enhanced commodities for agribusiness, like HYV seeds, fertilizers, and drip and sprinkler irrigation systems to ensure more cost-effective and efficient use of water. Farmers must utilize government initiatives to increase productivity and address climate change issues.
