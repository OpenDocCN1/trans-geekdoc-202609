Farmers and agriculture are regarded as the backbone of a country’s economic growth. Proper crop growth raises livelihood in the human era and creates the best yielding conditions for farmers. Skilled farmers cultivate crops more productively, increasing the likelihood of producing new breeds in agriculture. Many ideas in modern agriculture and science are taught to new technologies based on scientific principles to produce better crops.

Food products produced by agricultural industries are extremely beneficial in resolving many economic crises and crop wastage. The tomato crop is an example of such an agricultural crop that quickly reaches the wastage stage after yielding from agricultural farms.
