# 17.4 Conclusion

Smart, precision farming using IoT is the way of the future of agriculture. Of all, if they do not deal with global problems, none of these advances will be of any benefit. Future generations will benefit from wise farming techniques. The idea that a network of intelligent sensors, actuators, cameras, robots, drones, and other connected devices will provide agriculture with an incredible measure of influence and analytical decision-making has been propelled by the Internet of Things. This will pave the way for a bold ecosystem of innovation in the most established companies. Agricultural IoT and smart farming are establishing the foundation for a Third Green Revolution. The plant hybridization and genomics innovations were followed by the Third Green Revolution, which is now transforming agriculture. The Internet of Things (IoT), big data analytics, robots, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs or drones), and other data-driven analytics technologies are all utilized in this renaissance in precision agricultural machinery. This smart farming boom predicts a future with less agrotechnical use and greater overall performance. IoT technologies will lead to an improvement in food security by improving food monitoring. Additionally, it will improve the environment by, for instance, enabling more efficient irrigation use, purification, and supply optimization. Therefore, precision farming provides a chance of bringing about a more resilient and profitable kind of crop production built on a more accurate and resource-efficient strategy. The long-held ambition of humanity will, at last, be realized thanks to new farms. It will feed the next generation, which is predicted to number 9.6 billion by 2050.
