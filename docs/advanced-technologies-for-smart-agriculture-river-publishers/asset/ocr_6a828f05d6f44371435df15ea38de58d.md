Sample errors, a lack of data with acceptable spatial resolution, and the collecting and distributing pictures within the allowed time limit are only some of the real-world issues being faced by remote-sensing systems for farm management. Finally, many researchers in the field of agriculture have noted that a lack of data (or an insufficient amount of data) and the unreliability of that data are two of the most common challenges they face. Big data analytics in agriculture has significant hurdles due to agricultural data’s time sensitivity and heterogeneity.
