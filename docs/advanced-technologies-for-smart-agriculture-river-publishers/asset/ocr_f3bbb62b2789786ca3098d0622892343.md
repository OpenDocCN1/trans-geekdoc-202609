The researchers evaluated the first three big data [20] using low, medium and high indices based on a meta-analysis of 34 investigations. The papers were ranked from best to worst using an L, M and H scale. Exceptions to the rule where high velocity was required include projects dealing with weeds and animals, as a swift response is essential when evidence of diseases or weeds is discovered [21].
