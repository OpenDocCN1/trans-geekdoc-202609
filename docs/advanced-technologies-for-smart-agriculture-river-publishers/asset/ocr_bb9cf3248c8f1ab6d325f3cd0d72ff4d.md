+   5) Controlling pests:

Agrochemicals, germicides, and insecticides used in excess will disrupt the ecology. These approaches may aid in the rapid growth of plants or flora. Consumption of these items, on the other hand, may cause fatal and chronic disorders in humans. As a result, pest management employing IoT devices is beneficial in gaining control over the use of these manures. Pesticide usage monitoring, establishing the type of agricultural disease, and disease prediction at an early stage can all be aided by modern IoT systems.
