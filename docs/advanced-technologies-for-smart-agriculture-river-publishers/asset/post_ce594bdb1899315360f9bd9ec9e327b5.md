## Abstract

The creation, distribution, and marketing of food and other goods within the boundaries of a metropolitan area are broadly referred to as urban agriculture. Urban smart farms use aeroponics to grow fresh and healthy food in a cleaner, easier, and more effective manner. With 90% less land and 98% less water needed, our cutting-edge technology is the ideal option for farming at home or in an urban environment. The importance of greenhouse farming is also discussed in the chapter. A system is developed for agricultural field monitoring in IoT-based smart farming with the aid of sensors like light, humidity, temperature, soil moisture, etc. Farmers can keep an eye on the state of their fields from anywhere. Smart farming that is IoT-based is significantly extra effectual than traditional farming. MQTT and HTTP are used for the implementation of these models. Finally, supervising and scheduling the devices are discussed.
