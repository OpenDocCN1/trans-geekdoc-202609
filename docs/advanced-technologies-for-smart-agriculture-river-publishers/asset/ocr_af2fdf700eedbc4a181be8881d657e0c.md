# ## 2.2.5 TIVA C series

The TIVA TM4C Series TM4C123G pad analysis board (EK-TM4C123GXL) may serve as an affordable platform for ARM® CortexTM-M4F-based microcontrollers. For unique applications, the TIVA C Series pad has an RGB semiconductor diode and programmable user keys. The use of Booster Pack XL of the TIVA C Series TM4C123G pad is to extend the usefulness of the TIVA C Series pad by connecting various peripherals on numerous current Booster Pack add-on boards and upcoming goods.
