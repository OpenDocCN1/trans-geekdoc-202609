| Domain | Sources of big data | Methods for big data analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Weather and climate change | Data from weather stations, static information studies (climate and weather records), satellite remote sensing and geospatial information | Scalable vector machine (SVM) analysis, statistics-based analysis, cloud computing, GIS analysis and MapReduce analytics are all examples of machine-learning techniques |
| Land | Airborne remote sensing, radar data, geographical zone data, historical datasets associated with land characterization, crop phenology, temperature and rainfall, global tree cover maps, elevation and sensors in cameras | NDVI-related vegetation indices, SVM, k-means clustering, randomized trees and other machine-learning techniques |
| Research in animals | Camera sensors, ground sensors (for tracking grazing activity, food intake, temperature, milk production and so on, and historical data on animals and soils in terms of their physiological parameters) | Techniques of machine learning related to decision trees, Neural Networks, SVM |
| Crops | Metabolite sensors on the ground, satellite imagery and archived data | Vegetation indices (NDVI, Wavelet and Fourier transforms) and machine-learning techniques (SVM, K-means clustering) |
| Soil | Ground-based sensors (electrical conductivity, salinity and humidity) and photosensitive cameras | Machine learning (K-means clustering) |
| Weeds | Information based on historical (library of digitized plant and weed photos, plant-specific data), Remote sensing (plane and drones) | Image processing methods, ML methods and the NDVI |
| Food safety and availability | Data gathered through surveys, statistical data, GIS geographical data, remote sensing (synthetic aperture radar and historical information and databases (such as ENAR, CIALCA and rice crop growth figures) | Analytics based on statistics, simulation and modeling, network analysis, GIS spatial data analysis, image processing and machine learning (neural networks) |
| Investigations on the genetics of plants and animals | Geospatial information systems (GIS) and databases | Statistics |
| Farmer's decisions | Datasets containing historical information, weather stations, data based on the web, human-based sensors and remote sensing related to satellites and drones | Cloud-based systems, web-based services, mobile apps, statistical analysis, modeling, benchmarking, simulation, big data storage, message-oriented middleware |
| Farmer's insurance and finance | Web-based data, humans as sensors, historical information, weather stations | Cloud platforms, mobile applications, web services |
| Remote sensing | Data collected via planes, drones and satellites; web-based data, including maps and imaging; datasets containing historical information, including datasets of surface land containing images; MODIS surface reflectance datasets | Connected cloud-based platforms, statistical data analysis, GIS map-based spatial data analysis, image processing, NDVI-related vegetation indices, decision-making systems, big data storage, community- and web-based portals, mobile, computer vision, MapReduce-based analytics and AI-based applications |
