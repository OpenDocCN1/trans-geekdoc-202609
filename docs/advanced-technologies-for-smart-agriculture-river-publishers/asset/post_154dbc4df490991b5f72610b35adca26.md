This tensiometer sensor will work by measuring the soil moisture content by using a capacitive effect and will measure the water level in the soil horizon or the area where it is placed. Soil moisture sensors have two probes that measure the moisture content material throughout a vertical surface of the soil which usually spans several 30–120 cm. This soil sensor will intensively take care of monitoring the moisture content. These sorts of sensors should be placed or buried at the proper level from the top surface of the soil based on the crop and soil variety that is going to be sown in the agricultural field. To get a long duration of life and reduced reduction in the quality of the sensor, these are mostly preferred use which is built on plastic and PVC-based materials.
