# 3.5 Conclusion and Future Scope

The wide majority of recent agricultural developments produced through researchers were strongly associated with productivity and all other aspects of agriculture, to improve crop productivity, reduce and prepare for plant diseases, and promote mechanized and automated modernized agriculture and agro-industry. The principles, techniques, restrictions and algorithms of machine learning as well as deep learning are summarized elaborately in this paper.
