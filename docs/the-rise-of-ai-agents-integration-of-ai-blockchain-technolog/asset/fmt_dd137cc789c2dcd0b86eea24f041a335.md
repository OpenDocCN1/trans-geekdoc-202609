
### 9. What is the potential future development of AI blockchain oracles?
A. Reduction in AI capabilities
B. Predictive analysis using advanced machine learning models
C. Complete replacement of blockchain with AI
D. Removal of data privacy measures
**Answer: B**. Predictive analysis using advanced machine learning models. Future AI oracles are expected to demonstrate enhanced predictive capabilities, utilising advanced machine learning models for more sophisticated data analysis and decision-making.
