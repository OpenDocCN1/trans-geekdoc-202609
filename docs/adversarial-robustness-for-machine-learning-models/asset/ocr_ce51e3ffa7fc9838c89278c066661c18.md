on their analysis, they propose a theory-driven loss function for training
generalizable and robust neural networks against norm-bounded weight
perturbations. Their results offer fundamental insights for characterizing
the generalization and robustness of neural networks against weight per-
turbations. The adversarial robustness of joint perturbations to input and
weight spaces is studied in (Tsai et al., 2021b).

Weng et al. (2020) study the problem of weight quantization through
the lens of weight perturbations and certified robustness. They demon-
strate significant improvements on the generalization ability of quantized
networks through their robustness-aware quantization scheme.
