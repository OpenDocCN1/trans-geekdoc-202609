By no stretch of the imagination has the APT been a creature solely of Chinese making, although its activities do confirm a plurality of the ones identified by cybersecurity researchers. A European Union study identified APTs by name, just over half of were attributed to the People's Republic of China. Various APTs specialize in particular sectors. APT18 (attributed to China) pursues aerospace, defense, and engineering targets, all has also been associated with communications and media. APT15, also attributed to China, targets European government ministries; APT16 focuses on high tech, government, and financial areas in Japan and Taiwan. APT28's targets include NATO and various European militaries, while APT29 targets Western European governments and was connected to the hacks of the Democratic National Committee during the 2016 elections; analysts believe the "Snake" is a Russian entity dedicated to targeting the German government. North Korea's identified APT focus on South Korea, Japan, and Vietnam, as well as various industries such as manufacturing and aerospace that are seen as valuable by the regime. Iran's APTs have been associated with espionage against the United States as well as countries in the Middle East region and beyond, various industrial sectors including energy, telecommunications, and finance, and also a range of individual targets working in academia, journalism, and humanity rights⁴⁴
