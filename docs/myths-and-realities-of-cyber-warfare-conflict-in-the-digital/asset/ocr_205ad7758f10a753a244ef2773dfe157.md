In a globally connected landscape, products are rarely “sourced” in a single country. Instead, they are very likely to be assembled from components that have been built in another country, from materials that may have in turn been imported, and that the assembled product has been programmed with different software designed by different companies that may each have an international presence. The diverse origins of a single product can mean that several entities may have an opportunity to manipulate the functionality or fidelity of the end product.
