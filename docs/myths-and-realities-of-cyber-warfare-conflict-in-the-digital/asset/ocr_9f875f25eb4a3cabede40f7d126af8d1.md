Other factors sharpen the security challenges posed by lengthening software. Some trends such as BYOD (bring your own device) and the push to connect systems that had not been designed for connectivity with the broader world. Both trends aim to reduce costs of operation, respectively by shifting device acquisition and maintenance onto users and through more efficient monitoring of industrial or infrastructure systems.42 However, these moves also mean that more will be expected (or assumed) of users who are inexperienced in security and systems that people rely on for critical services will be more accessible than they had historically been to third parties.

Growth in the attack surface poses another area for security consideration. Schneider reminds us that “as the system gets more complex, the attack surface gets larger” and more complicated to defend, and “we are designing systems that are getting faster than our ability to secure them” is improving.
