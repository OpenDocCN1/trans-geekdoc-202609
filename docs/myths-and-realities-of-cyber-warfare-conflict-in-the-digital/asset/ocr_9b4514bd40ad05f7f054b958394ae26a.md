When propaganda works, it intends to do so because it plays on something factual or at least plausible and extrapolates from this project to normalize further ideas. In the absence of other factors, ISIS accountolders and Twitter employees could have played a game of account suspension merry-go-round endlessly. Ultimately what would change the online text would be changes on the ground. It is evident in adjustments in ISIS social media activity. As with other propaganda campaigns, ISIS worked to “seem disproportionately stronger than it was.” This was something it could plausibly accomplish when it was on the attack it could project and magnify its successes to make self-filling in the minds of others. Battlefield reversals can be covered by lies for so long. As its holdings on the ground in Iraq and Syria shrank, its previous pattern of calling on sympathizers to travel to the quasi-state became increasingly impractical; this coincides with an increase in ISIS using social media to encourage distant sympathizers to stage lone-wolf attacks in their own homeland rather than attempt a unsuccessful journey to a shrinking extremist pocket. 46 If propaganda builds on critical mass, ISIS fizzled as a state and its generating follow-on attacks forms a predictable but very different kind of approach than ISIS pursued when it possessed a possibly viable heartland for jihad.

# TWEETS FROM RUSSIA

Russia’s storied pedigree in information operations from the Cold War indicates an interest and aptitude for disinformation antecedented to the development of the cyber domain. This legacy, and subsequent activities that forensics has also traced to Russia, illustrate the relevance of social media as an avenue of information operations that have strategic goals but may be only indirectly related to violence.

Building is clearly more challenging and complex than tearing down. Even building an information image requires work in its construction and its preservation. This may help explain why ISIS was reportedly compelled to counter rumors that it practised female mutilation.47 although hardly squamish about inflicting pain through graphic violence, the terrorist group wanted to use its violence to propel a particular brand as part of a specific messaging campaign, and this could require counterintuitive cases of protecting its brand against disturbing rumors.
