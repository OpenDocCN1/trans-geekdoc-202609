The allusion to nuclear weapons appears to be a red herring. Even dire impact from disinformation campaigns cannot independently conjure the destructiveness and chaos inflicted through nuclear arms. A second aspect merits further note: the tone presumes that in weaponizing information, the steering wheel belongs in the hands of policy makers, even if the keyboards are set before trolls, patriotic hackers, hacktivists, or other citizens.
