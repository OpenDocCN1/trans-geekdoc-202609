### 3.4.4.3. Components of a CNN

Convolutional neural networks are made up of three layers: input and output, and likewise made up of several layers, each with its own set of functions, that take and process images as input. A general architecture diagram of CNN, with main components like layers, activation function, and key parameters. Layers of CNN are divided into three types: convolutional, pooling, and fully connected. Convolution and pooling, the first two layers, extract features; the third layer, fully connected, transmits those features to the output, like classification. The activation function is the nonlinear modification we apply to the input signal. This altered output is eventually transmitted as input to the next layer of neurons. Linear activation functions and non-linear activation functions such as Sigmoid and logistic functions, Hyperbolic Tangent function Tanh, ReLU contains subtypes Dying, Leaky and Parametric ReLU, Exponential Linear Unit (ELU), and Softmax are popular forms of activation functions [10].

CNN architectures. CNN architectures have many different shapes and sizes. These designs are utilized for the establishment of deep learning algorithms. Table 5 gives a summary of different CNN architectures with their pros and cons.

- LeNet-5: One of the most basic architectures is LeNet-5. It includes 2 convolutional layers and 3 fully-connected layers, thus it has “5” neural network names that are frequently obtained from the no. of convolutional and fully-connected layers. We recognize today’s average-pooling layer as a subsampling layer, which includes trainable weights (which is not how CNNs are created now). This design has roughly 60,000 different variables to take into consideration [11].
- AlexNet: AlexNet comprises eight layers, five of which are convolutional and three of which are fully connected. AlexNet just added a couple more layers to LeNet-5. AlexNet is a straightforward yet effective CNN design that includes both convolutional and pooling layers. Its top layer is then composed of layers that are fully connected. It makes use of the GPU for training and task execution, which is advantageous. AlexNet continues to be used as a foundation for deep neural network applications, most notably in computer vision and image processing [12].
- VGG Net: This architecture consists of a sequence of convolutional layers and pooling layers. Among the positives is that it has an excellent design that is great for benchmarking any activity. Additionally, VGG’s pre-trained networks are frequently used in a broad variety of applications. VGG-16 is composed of thirteen convolutional layers and three fully connected layers, carrying on AlexNet’s ReLU legacy. This network adds additional layers to AlexNet and employs lower size filters (2*2) and (3*3) than AlexNet. They also devised a system of deeper networks i.e. twice as deep as AlexNet [13].
