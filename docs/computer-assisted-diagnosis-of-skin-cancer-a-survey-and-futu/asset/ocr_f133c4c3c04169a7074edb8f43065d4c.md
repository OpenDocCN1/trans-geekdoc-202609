# 3.3. Segmentation techniques for analysis and diagnosis of skin lesion images

Segmentation is a critical phase in the diagnosis of skin lesions, it’s a crucial step in analyzing skin lesion images by isolating the unhealthy from the healthy, this method gets an area of interest. Recently, more emphasis has been placed on the segmentation process. Many segmentation methods or algorithms have been explored in the literature. These techniques address several shortcomings connected with typical medical division processes. However, no one strategy can be considered superior. Such solutions are only applicable to certain images and applications. Thresholding, region growth, merging and splitting, clustering, edge detection, and model-based image division are different techniques of segmentation. All segmentation techniques are based on the following important factors: levels of intensity, similarity, and discontinuity. This section goes over several strategies for segmenting skin lesions images handcrafted features-based approaches, such as threshold-based methods, are among these strategies, intelligence-based supervised segmentation, edge, and region-based methods. Deep learning and Artificial Neural Network algorithms are examples of intelligent-based systems.

# 3.3.1. Traditional intelligence based methods of segmentation

Artificial Intelligence-based systems can do an analysis of the image by reasoning, learning, and perceiving images in large datasets that already exist. Skin lesion segmentation may be accomplished in a variety of ways, both supervised and unsupervised, using existing datasets to train and learn the system. Intelligent-based methods of segmentation such as ANN, fuzzy C-means, and Genetic algorithms are commonly used and solutions have also incorporated deep learning technologies. +   • Threshold-based methods: Threshold-based methods can be used to create binary pictures from a grayscale image. Segmentation is used to create binary pictures from colored images. Segmentation is the process of assigning two or more classes to each pixel in the original image. When there are more than two classes, the outcome is usually a series of binary images. In image processing, thresholding is used to divide a picture into smaller segments, or junks, by defining their boundaries with at least one color or grey scale value. Eq. +   • Edge or boundary based segmentation methods: Consistency is essential to region-based methods. Assuming that all pixels in a region must be of the same gray level, these algorithms break the image into smaller subregions. The term "region-based technique" refers to methods that use comparable patterns in the intensity values of nearby pixels as a starting point. The algorithm's goal is to group areas based on their structural or functional roles, which is the cluster’s purpose.

+   • Region-based segmentation: A pixel must conform to certain established constraints in this segmentation process to be classified into related pixel regions. Based on particular criteria, an image is segmented into separate groups or regions of comparable pixels. Its core principle is that pixels nearby inside the same region have the same value.

+   • Laplacian of Gaussian Filter method: The Laplacian Filter is a second-order or second-derivative picture enhancing technique that draws attention to areas with rapid changes in intensity.
