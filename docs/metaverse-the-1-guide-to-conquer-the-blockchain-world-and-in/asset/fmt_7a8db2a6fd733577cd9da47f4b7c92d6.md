
Adjacency of land is a fundamental economic notion in Decentraland and other metaverses. Within finite geography, all metaverse parcels are contiguous at a fixed point. Due to the restricted availability of property, this causes scarcity. And scarcity allows property values to increase and decrease following universal supply and demand laws.

According to the Decentraland manifesto, a foundation is therefore constructed for "a social experience with an economy driven by the existing layers of land ownership and content distribution."

The property transactions that power the metaverse are made possible by NFTs. These tokens provide irrefutable ownership proof that is more secure than any land deed.
