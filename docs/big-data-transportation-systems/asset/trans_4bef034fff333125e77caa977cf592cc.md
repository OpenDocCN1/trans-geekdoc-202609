

# Application of Autonomous Driving Technology in Intelligent Transportation and Its Impact on Urban Traffic Flow

In the future, mature artificial intelligence will be able to analyze the surrounding environment and make decisions like a human. When analyzing driving strategies in specific scenarios, it does not need to re-analyze every time; instead, it can utilize the strategies previously processed by the vehicle for that scenario to control the car, or imitate the behavior of the vehicle ahead. This will have a disruptive impact on the automotive industry, and human life will also undergo significant changes.

Theoretically, autonomous vehicles will possess the "driving" capability through the integration of perception, decision-making, and execution systems. As Hod Lipson and Melba Kurman, authors of *Driverless: Intelligent Cars and the Road Ahead*, stated: "Although the technology is almost ready, the social environment for deploying this unique technology may not be prepared yet. For example, laws and regulations governing driverless vehicles are not yet in place. However, based on the advantages of driverless cars in terms of efficiency and safety, driverless cars are destined to increase, and the era of driverless cars will eventually arrive. We believe the development trend of 'driverless cars' is unstoppable."

## 12.3.5 Application of Autonomous Driving in the Field of Intelligent Transportation

Autonomous driving technology includes human-machine interaction technology, intelligent control systems, anti-lock braking systems, traction or stability control systems, etc. The following is a detailed analysis of the application of these technologies in the field of intelligent transportation:

### (1) Human-Machine Interaction Technology Ensuring Driving Safety
Intelligent transportation systems take human safety as the top priority. Vehicles and humans need to interact with each other to comprehensively guarantee human safety, which requires the use of human-machine interaction technology. Human-machine interaction involves not only the interaction between in-vehicle occupants and the car but also the interaction between external pedestrians and the car. Currently, research on human-machine interaction technology in the field of autonomous driving focuses more on the interaction between the vehicle and external pedestrians. By establishing a communication mechanism that enables efficient and convenient interaction between the car and people, efficient vehicle-road coordination can be achieved. In the long run, future autonomous vehicles will imitate human behavior patterns when making intelligent decisions.

Efficient and convenient human-machine interaction can bring users a better travel experience and has a very positive impact on enhancing the market promotion capability of autonomous vehicles. Without human-machine interaction, it is difficult for the car to fully understand human needs. Autonomous vehicles will analyze massive amounts of data in real-time for route planning, lane changing, obstacle avoidance, parking, etc.

### (2) Intelligent Control Systems Enhancing Travel Experience
The intelligent decision-making of a vehicle is an important indicator for measuring the performance of an autonomous car. Data obtained through sensors and other devices will be processed by processors, applying data models and intelligent algorithms to achieve optimal decisions. Of course, such intelligent algorithms should possess self-learning and self-improvement capabilities to meet the actual needs of autonomous vehicles in various driving scenarios. Automated control is the executor of decision commands, enabling the car to perform a series of behavioral actions, such as acceleration/deceleration, steering, obstacle avoidance, etc., which directly affects the human travel experience.

In many traffic accidents, those caused by human factors account for a considerable proportion. This is because humans feel fatigue and psychological fluctuations when stimulated by sound, images, or other external factors, leading to human errors that threaten personal and property safety. The intelligent control system based on autonomous driving technology can remain objective and calm for long periods without fatigue, effectively reducing human-caused traffic accidents.

### (3) Anti-lock Braking System Reducing Traffic Accidents
The Anti-lock Braking System (`ABS`) is one of the most widely used and relatively mature autonomous driving systems. Vehicles not equipped with `ABS` may experience tire lock-up during emergency braking, causing the vehicle to skid. Experienced drivers can avoid lock-up by pumping the brakes repeatedly, but most drivers are easily distracted within a few seconds, or even mistake the accelerator for the brake. Once equipped with `ABS`, the system can monitor tire status in real-time before the tires are about to lock up and take effective measures when an accident occurs, reducing the likelihood of traffic accidents.

### (4) Traction or Stability Control Systems Supporting Autonomous Vehicle Movement
The traction or stability control system is also a widely used technology in autonomous driving systems. Compared with `ABS`, this system is more complex. It can monitor road conditions, vehicle speed, driving direction, operating status of key components, and other information in real-time. Once it detects that the vehicle may lose control or roll over, it takes effective measures in a timely manner to ensure driving safety. In true autonomous driving, the car can efficiently and safely independently complete tasks assigned by passengers or the controller.

### (5) Interaction of Various Systems Truly Realizing Autonomous Driving
The traffic information collection system, information processing and analysis system, and information dissemination system are intelligent transportation systems closely related to autonomous driving technology. Among them:
- The traffic information collection system is built based on multiple systems such as the Global Positioning System (`GPS`) technology and infrared radar detection systems;
- The information processing and analysis system is usually applied in `GIS` application systems, expert systems, etc.;
- The information dissemination system highly relies on media technologies such as mobile internet and mobile phone terminals.

Developed countries such as the United States and Japan, leveraging their leading advantages in computer control, network technology, positioning and navigation, artificial intelligence, and other fields, took the lead in creating intelligent transportation systems with unmanned systems at the core. A large number of autonomous driving vehicle projects have emerged, and the costs in exploration and practice are relatively low.
