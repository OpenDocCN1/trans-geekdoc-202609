

## Integration of China's Cold Chain Logistics and Big Data Technology

On the other hand, China's current cold chain logistics system is not yet fully mature. During the logistics distribution process, the problems of high loss rates and low circulation rates have not been completely resolved. How to apply big data technology to cold chain logistics, achieve the integrated development of cold chain logistics, and thereby grow and strengthen the cold chain logistics industry has become an urgent task.

On September 10, 2016, the China Cold Chain Logistics Intelligent Cloud Platform was officially launched in Zhenjiang, marking the official market operation of the country's first intelligent cloud platform for cold chain logistics based on big data and IoT technologies, integrating functions such as online display and transaction, cold chain logistics orders, and cold chain warehousing.

This intelligent cloud platform enables intelligent monitoring of cold chain logistics, online order matching, and online order transactions. The platform effectively integrates upstream and downstream industry information to achieve full-process monitoring of cold chain transportation. The Zhejiang Data Monitoring Center can monitor temperature, humidity, GPS positioning, and other information of cold chain transportation 24 hours a day. Once an abnormality occurs during transportation, an alarm is received immediately and manual intervention is carried out at the first opportunity, greatly improving the safety of cold chain transportation.^([2](#ch07.xhtml#fn2))

Currently, many small and medium-sized cold chain logistics enterprises operate independently, with relatively scattered business models, poor product and logistics information flow, and numerous obstacles in information transmission. Compared with developed countries, China's level of big data technology application in the field of cold chain logistics is relatively low and still in its infancy.

Specifically, the lack of a data collection system is a serious problem in the application of big data technology in cold chain logistics. In addition, the market and government departments collect data on cold chain transportation of agricultural products relatively independently, relying heavily on traditional manual operations and lacking automatic collection means and methods, resulting in data that is not sufficiently accurate, objective, or timely.

Therefore, it is necessary to promote the combination of big data transportation technology and the cold chain logistics industry, so that logistics enterprises, cold chain product suppliers, consumers, and government regulatory departments can view transportation information, product information, consumer information, and weather information through big data.

The specific methods are as follows:

### (1) Establishing a Cold Chain Product Traceability System Using Big Data Technology

Products transported via cold chain are mostly agricultural products in a broad sense, including fresh products, fruits and vegetables, crops, etc. It is necessary for the cold chain industry to establish a big data platform for information traceability, use IoT technology to collect data sources, and establish an intelligent traceability system. This can form a complete intelligent information traceability network, ensuring the quality and safety of cold chain products during transportation.

For example, cold chain products should be equipped with location data, manufacturer data, production cycle data, distribution data, etc. In this way, big data can be used to manage the entire circulation process of cold chain products.

### (2) Comprehensively Coordinating Cold Chain Logistics Transportation Using Big Data Technology

Currently, many regions have adopted monitoring technologies such as IoT, GPS, and intelligent on-board terminals, and through the movement and processing of big data, have comprehensively improved cold chain logistics efficiency.

For example, establishing a vehicle identity authentication system can reduce the operational risks of cold chain distribution centers and third-party logistics companies. Another example is achieving full-process visual monitoring of cold chain distribution vehicles—from vehicle operating speed, product status, to driving routes and driver status—realizing full-process visual monitoring, thereby achieving reasonable vehicle scheduling, management, and supervision.

More importantly, the cold chain logistics transportation system can monitor vehicles in real-time via GPS to ensure the ability to check whether the vehicle is smoking and whether the temperature and humidity meet product storage and transportation requirements. Through the intelligent on-board system, technical parameters of refrigerated trucks (such as documents, drawings) can also be transmitted to the enterprise monitoring center in real-time. Both product consumers and enterprises can view relevant parameters via the Internet.

### (3) Ensuring Timely and Accurate Adjustment of Back-end Operational Strategies

The timely introduction of big data ensures that cold chain logistics enterprises can obtain integrated real-time data at any time when researching strategies, and obtain accurate and fast query results through the query and processing of unstructured data.

At the same time, in the cold chain logistics industry, the most important characteristic is the guarantee of timeliness. Due to the particularity of cold chain logistics distribution products, such as uneven supply sources, typical seasonality of various products, and bumper and lean production cycles, the costs and risks of cold chain logistics enterprises have increased significantly. Using big data technology, the operational strategies of cold chain logistics enterprises can be adjusted through existing data distribution patterns, providing support from both internal and external aspects. In addition, enterprises can obtain the most needed data in a timely manner to formulate precise deployment plans, including obtaining maintenance costs, failure frequencies, operational routes, etc.

The integration of existing cold chain logistics enterprises with cloud computing, big data, mobile Internet, and other technologies has become an inevitable trend. It can be assured that the application of big data technology in cold chain logistics has unlimited development potential. Big data transportation services and cold chain logistics will jointly usher in broad development prospects.

## 7.6 Big Data Transportation and Multimodal Transport

Multimodal transport refers to a logistics transportation process completed by the connection and transshipment of two or more modes of transport. With the development of the national economy, vigorously developing multimodal transport is a key task for comprehensive transportation development during the "13th Five-Year Plan" period. Strategically, it is an important focus for China to promote supply-side structural reform in transportation; from an enterprise perspective, it is an effective way to reduce logistics costs.

In the process of strategic innovation in multimodal transport development, various regions have currently shown an important leap toward big data transportation.

On May 19, 2017, the Zhengzhou Airport International Logistics Multimodal Transport Data Trading Service Platform was officially launched. The launch of this platform realized information sharing between domestic highway, railway, seaport, and other transport modes and the full-process transportation chain of international aviation and overseas land transport, guaranteeing the formation of information linkage in areas such as Zhengzhou Airport, the Pilot Free Trade Zone, and bonded parks.
