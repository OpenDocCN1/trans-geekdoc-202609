The application of big data transportation technology can comprehensively improve logistics efficiency by optimizing transport organization, help enterprises solve problems, and expand room for promotion.

Specifically, the impact of big data transportation technology on transport organization is reflected in the following aspects:

### 7.1.1 Transport Environment Prediction

In the past, logistics enterprises were accustomed to predicting the transport environment based on past experience, questionnaire surveys, or preliminary contacts—for example, finding cargo sources, designing optimal routes, and understanding traffic changes. However, in actual operations, by the time survey results were compiled, the transport environment had likely already changed.

In contrast, big data can help logistics enterprises comprehensively and accurately depict the transport environment and reflect environmental changes based on real and effective data, thereby forecasting various stages of product transportation, enabling transport organizers to formulate transport plans rationally.

### 7.1.2 Transport Node Location Selection

The issue of transport node location selection in transport organization requires logistics enterprises to minimize distribution costs while fully considering their own business characteristics, commodity attributes, and road conditions. This problem can be solved using the classification tree method in big data technology.

### 7.1.3 Transport Route Optimization

In transport organization, logistics enterprises can utilize big data to analyze commodity specifications and characteristics as well as customers' differentiated needs in terms of timeliness and cost. Then, with the aid of the fastest response mechanism, they can accurately reflect various factors affecting transport schemes—such as which transport scheme to choose, which cost model to adopt, and which transport route to select—thereby planning the most reasonable transport routes.

At the same time, enterprises can quickly analyze route and road conditions through real-time data received during the distribution process, provide early warnings for situations that may affect routes, accurately analyze transport process information, and make transport distribution management more intelligent.

### 7.1.4 Cargo Position Optimization

Reasonable arrangement of cargo positions is of great significance for improving the efficiency of transport route management. For transport lines with large cargo volumes and high delivery frequencies, cargo position optimization can directly bring improvements in operational efficiency and economic benefits.

In fact, which commodities can be placed in combination to increase sorting rates, and which commodities have short storage cycles and high transport requirements, can all be derived through big data association pattern analysis of the data correlation relationships of commodities during the transportation process, thereby planning transport cargo positions more rationally.

The most essential difference between modern logistics and traditional logistics lies in transport organization. Modern logistics is inseparable from the support of information technologies such as big data, which is reflected in the systematization, informatization, networking, and intelligence of transport organization.

Currently, limited by factors such as insufficient computer application penetration, weak awareness of big data application, and uneven employee quality, Chinese logistics enterprises have not yet fully integrated transport organization with functions such as packaging, warehousing, circulation processing, distribution, and information processing. Therefore, attaching importance to the role of big data in transport organization and using it to optimize logistics management decisions is of great significance.

### 7.2 Big Data Transportation and Rural Logistics

Compared with urban logistics, rural logistics involves more transport links. Rural logistics is usually implemented in three steps: warehouse sorting, regional transportation, and last-mile delivery. In fact, in the vast majority of rural areas, apart from last-mile delivery, other logistics links are similar to urban delivery. Last-mile delivery has become the most critical link determining logistics efficiency.

At the end of May 2016, at the 3rd China County-level E-commerce Summit, Xiong Jian, head of Cainiao Network's rural logistics, stated that Cainiao Network is using data systems to integrate the transport capacity of express logistics enterprises nationwide, building a denser and deeper delivery network in rural areas. Through the rural terminal logistics network, over 30% of rural parcels can achieve same-day delivery from county to village, and nearly 70% can achieve next-morning delivery to the village. Cainiao Network has built a terminal network covering over 320 counties and nearly 16,000 rural service stations nationwide.

Given issues in rural logistics such as unclear recipient addresses, route planning detached from reality, and excessive contingent factors, misdelivery and repeated delivery occur frequently, leading to increased delivery costs and decreased recipient satisfaction. Regarding the "last-mile" delivery dilemma in rural e-commerce logistics, although many logistics enterprises have attempted various special delivery models, they have failed to effectively improve efficiency.

In view of this, it is necessary to introduce big data transportation technology to accurately analyze the regional distribution status of rural logistics and the living conditions of recipients, propose targeted solutions, and effectively solve rural logistics problems.

### 7.2.1 Transport Service Innovation

The collection, collation, analysis, and application of big data can effectively integrate rural logistics social transport capacity, improve efficiency, and reduce costs. The key to integrating rural logistics resources lies in establishing a dedicated logistics information platform, which can be implemented through the following two models.

Leading enterprises take the lead in building a dedicated rural logistics information platform and are responsible for its operation. When other logistics enterprises or individuals access and apply it, their various business and shipping data will be recorded. Through massive data analysis, consumption habits, credit records, address accuracy, and path reliability in rural areas will be recorded and presented, providing a strong basis for subsequent customer screening.
