

# Precise Matching of Needs and Services: Big Data Applications in Transportation

Precise matching of needs and precise provision of services are important characteristics of big data. In the transportation sector, to achieve precision, both service providers and service recipients must possess precise data, so that needs and services can be perfectly matched. When data is sufficiently large and rich, quantified needs naturally emerge. At this point, providing precise needs-based services is no longer difficult.

### Passenger Perspective: From Anxiety to Composure

At 5:00 PM, Chen Daiyu (Editor's note: referred to as Chen Deyu later in the text, suspected to be the same person), who works near Lewan International, takes the bus home. "Everyone has a seat, closes their eyes for a rest, and arrives home quickly," Chen Daiyu said with a smile. But before July last year, Chen Daiyu's mood was far from relaxed when taking the bus home after work. "Waiting for the bus was very troublesome back then because the time was uncertain. Missing a bus wasted a lot of time. Taking a taxi here wasn't easy either, and it was expensive, so I was very anxious whenever I took the bus home after work."

**In July last year (2015), Guiyang launched its first "Customized Bus" route from the Provincial Museum on Beijing Road to Wudang Lewan International. Many office workers like Chen Daiyu who needed to commute between the two places felt greatly relieved. "Since the Customized Bus opened, buses depart at fixed points and times, going directly from home to work in one stop, saving time and improving work and life quality."** Subsequently, Guiyang Public Transport (Group) Co., Ltd. successively opened 4 more "Customized Bus" routes, including from the Provincial Government to Science City, and from the Municipal Administrative Center to the Memorial Tower.

In addition, ordinary citizens also began to enjoy the convenience of "Customized Bus". In 2015, students in Guiyang University Town also started taking "Customized Bus" on weekends and holidays.

In April 2016, the first "Customized Bus" route in University Town opened, and hundreds of students safely and quickly reached Jinyang Bus Station and Guiyang Railway Station via "Customized Bus". "Taking the 'Customized Bus' means we don't have to worry about squeezing onto the bus and missing the train home," university student Xiao Li said.

"'Customized Public Transport' is one of our 'Customized Bus' projects and also the earliest pilot project. Judging from current citizen feedback, acceptance is quite high, accumulating experience for our next step of promoting 'Customized Bus' citywide," said Liu Xianglong, Deputy Director of the Passenger Transport Division of Guiyang Municipal Road Transport Administration Bureau.

## Customized Bus Network Service Platform: Big Data-Driven Smart Scheduling

Liu Xianglong introduced that Customized Bus is an important component of public transport. Based on the civilian use of transportation big data, the Municipal Transportation Bureau built the Customized Bus Network Service Platform. The platform's operation supervision and application services are incorporated into the overall management of the Guiyang Low-Carbon Traffic Information Command Center. The data center relies on the construction of the Guiyang Municipal Data Resource Center, and relevant data is connected to the Guiyang Municipal Government Data Exchange and Sharing Platform.

Through the Internet and backend data processing and analysis, the platform provides basic functions such as market research, demand analysis, route pushing, price calculation, shift booking, online payment, publicity announcements, passenger interaction, vehicle management, group booking, and campus services for Customized Bus.

The Customized Bus Network Service Platform of the Guiyang Municipal Transportation Bureau has been under construction since last year and is now basically completed, soon to be put into use. "Residential areas, industrial parks, schools, and other public transport service blind spots with populations exceeding 30,000 are the key service areas for 'Customized Bus'. In addition, 'Customized Bus' can also make up for insufficient public transport capacity and meet the public's personalized travel needs."

The platform calculates optimal routes based on big data and users' personalized needs, shortening average travel time compared to traditional public transport. By providing services that are "not crowded, not expensive, and fast," it solves three major pain points and has become an effective means to alleviate peak-hour traffic congestion and improve travel experience.

## "Self-Processing" Data Push Enhances Law Enforcement Precision

"Self-processing" data push enhances the precision of law enforcement.

On May 18, Xia Xungui, a special administrator for the driver training industry of the Audit Detachment No. 2 of the Guiyang Municipal Transportation Administration Bureau, continuously received warning signals from the "Data Cage" on his mobile phone APP. "The reason is that a coach vehicle from a driving school in Yunyan District has exceeded the 'electronic fence' range for three days." Xia Xungui told reporters that at the end of 2015, the Guiyang Municipal Transportation Administration Bureau launched a new driver training timing system, which became one of the six sub-modules of the bureau's big data fusion platform for performance and efficiency. One of the important functions of this system is to monitor coach vehicle training grounds, forming an "electronic fence," and tracking the driving status of coach vehicles through a positioning system.

"In the past, we simply couldn't supervise thousands of coach vehicles across the city. Now with the big data fusion platform, real-time management of coach vehicles via big data is very convenient and effective. The platform detects abnormalities and automatically processes and proactively warns," Xia Xungui said.

On May 19, Xia Xungui went to this driving school to verify the operation of the coach vehicle warned by the platform. "This makes our law enforcement more precise and effective," Xia Xungui said.

In addition to alarms for coach vehicles exceeding electronic fences, the Guiyang Municipal Transportation Administration Bureau's big data fusion platform can also initiate self-processing warnings for various abnormal data such as vehicle operating permit expiration and rating assessment expiration. "Each type of abnormal data has different judgment standards. Abnormal data found must be processed in a timely manner. If not processed within the specified time, it will be reported to the supervising leader."
