## 9.4 Big Data Transportation and the "Belt and Road" International Forum

From May 14 to 15, 2017, the Belt and Road Forum for International Cooperation was held in Beijing. This was a major diplomatic event hosted by China, with significant significance for promoting international and regional cooperation. As the initiator and organizer of the "Belt and Road" Forum, China had to ensure the smooth convening of the forum. At the same time, this was the most important international event since the Beijing Olympics.

The forum also attracted numerous heads of state, government officials, and institutional representatives from countries around the world, as well as 29 foreign heads of state and government leaders, and 3 heads of important international organizations (UN Secretary-General, President of the International Committee of the Red Cross) attending the summit. Approximately 1,500 representatives from various sectors from over 130 countries attended the forum as official delegates. More than 4,000 journalists from around the world registered to cover the forum. The high specifications and far-reaching influence of the forum are evident.

For this important international forum, Beijing transportation faced challenges again after the 2008 Olympics. What role did big data transportation play for the "Belt and Road"?

In recent years, with the continuous development of big data technology, Beijing transportation has formed a relatively complete big data transportation model. Therefore, for the "Belt and Road" International Forum, these new technologies were widely applied. These technologies not only served the "Belt and Road" International Forum but also established a complete big data transportation network for Beijing. While ensuring the smooth convening of the international forum, they also brought tangible convenience to Beijing citizens, greatly improving Beijing's traffic conditions.

### 9.4.1 Traffic Restriction Measures Under Big Data

Implementing traffic control and restrictions for major conferences is an international convention, and the "Belt and Road" International Forum was no exception. In the past, traffic restrictions were often based on empirical judgment, with lagging enforcement, causing great inconvenience to the public's normal travel. However, for the "Belt and Road" International Forum, Beijing transportation departments used big data to conduct long-term information collection on key road sections. With the help of big data channels such as intelligent traffic lights and road monitoring equipment, relevant information was released in early May 2017, allowing the public to prepare in advance.

At the same time, restricted routes were optimized based on big data judgment. Instead of blocking most roads as in the past, only roads affecting the forum were restricted. This not only ensured the smooth progress of the forum but also guaranteed that the public's daily life and work were unaffected. Meanwhile, due to the proactive application of big data, the Beijing traffic management department also issued relevant detour suggestions, further ensuring residents' normal travel.

(1) **Traffic control measures were implemented on roads surrounding the Yanqi Lake International Convention Center in Huairou District.** From 0:00 on May 7 to the end of the event on May 15, from the intersection of Fanqi Road and Huidu Road in Huairou District (exclusive) via Fanqi Road, Yanqi Lake West Road to Baiyashanzhuang Bridge (exclusive), Yanqi Lake Road, Yanqi Lake South Road, vehicles other than those holding "Summit" special permits were prohibited from passing. Social vehicles and buses must detour via Yanqi Lake North Road; parking of motor vehicles is prohibited along the detour route.

From 0:00 on May 15 to the end of the event, from the east entrance of Yanqi Lake North Road via Yanqi Lake North Road to Shentangyu Road, vehicles other than those holding "Summit" special permits were prohibited from passing. Social vehicles and buses must detour via Jingjia Road.

(2) **Traffic control measures were implemented on roads surrounding the China National Convention Center in Chaoyang District.** From 0:00 on May 10 to the end of the event on May 15, within the enclosed area formed by Beichen East Bridge via North 4th Ring Road, Beichen West Road, Datun North Road, Beichen East Road to Beichen East Bridge (excluding the aforementioned roads), vehicles other than those holding "Summit" special permits were prohibited from passing. Social vehicles and buses could detour via Datun North Road, Datun Road Underpass, Huizhong Road Underpass, Beichen West Road, Beichen East Road.

From 6:00 on May 14 to the end of the event, vehicles other than those holding "Summit" special permits were prohibited from passing on Beichen East Road, Beichen West Road, Kehui South Road, Datun North Road, Datun Road Underpass, Huizhong Road Underpass, Minzu Yuan Road. Social vehicles and buses must detour via Anli Road, Jingzang Expressway, North 5th Ring Road, North 4th Ring Road.

Using big data for traffic restrictions is an important attempt by Beijing since the Olympics. Solving traffic problems based on data rather than experience is more effective, and this is the main direction for traffic control during major events in the future. While ensuring the normal operation of urban traffic, it can also show the world leaders a new look of Beijing, Beijing's development, and even the fact that China will become more mature.

### 9.4.2 Application of Beijing Intelligent Transportation

While implementing traffic restrictions with big data, a large number of new big data transportation applications emerged. Beijing transportation optimized from details, demonstrating new characteristics of Beijing transportation during the "Belt and Road" International Forum.

(1) **Handling traffic services via mobile phone.** In 2017, Beijing mobile big data transportation applications were fully rolled out. Citizens could not only swipe bus cards and subway cards via mobile phones but also intelligently pay fees, query road conditions, and bus arrival information through mobile apps. At the same time, software such as "Beijing Traffic APP" and "Customized Bus APP" have connected road traffic and aviation channels; traffic information is available at the click of a button, greatly enhancing the convenience of transportation operations.
