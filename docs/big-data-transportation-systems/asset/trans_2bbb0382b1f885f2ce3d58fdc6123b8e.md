
# Comprehensive Transportation Information Service System

The Shanghai World Expo launched a Comprehensive Transportation Information Service System. The system serves the vast number of visitors, covering integrated information on all modes of transportation. It highly centralizes information, allowing all traffic information to be queried through a single platform, avoiding the past problem of repeated queries, and greatly improving the efficiency of visitor travel and road traffic. In particular, the "One Network, One Hotline" service model enables visitors to obtain comprehensive traffic information with one click. Visitors can access Shanghai's traffic information through multiple Expo transportation websites, mobile communication services, the Expo hotline, dedicated traffic radio channels, etc. This not only brings convenience to visitors but also to frontline traffic commanders. They can quickly understand relevant road section information, thereby enabling precise traffic dispatching.

# Electronic Toll Collection System

In Shanghai, most road sections are toll roads. In the past, manual toll collection was inefficient and prone to causing traffic queues, directly affecting road passage. Before the Expo, all these sections were upgraded to Electronic Toll Collection (ETC) systems, which are more flexible and diverse. It not only enables fast electronic toll collection but can also combine various preferential schemes, making it deeply popular among car owners. With the help of the ETC system, traffic resources are effectively balanced, and vehicle speeds have increased significantly. At the same time, the costs of traffic operating companies have been reduced, thereby providing better services.

# Traffic Monitoring System

Similar to the Beijing Olympics, the Shanghai World Expo also launched a Big Data Traffic Monitoring System. After two years of technical improvements, the Expo's Big Data Traffic Monitoring System became more mature. For the first time, it established a Video Image Monitoring System, monitoring traffic flow at intersections, surrounding traffic flow, passenger flow, traffic security, and other aspects. All monitoring data is sent to the monitoring center as quickly as possible, covering all of Shanghai. Simultaneously, the monitoring system can issue emergency telephone alarms and dynamically release early warnings, providing better guidance for vehicles on the road. Vehicles can learn about sudden traffic incidents ahead in advance, thereby detouring to avoid obvious congestion.

# Decision Support System

During the Expo, the Shanghai Transportation Department launched an innovative "Traffic Decision Support System," relying on big data to solve problems of flow detection, guidance, and decision-making. The traditional manual mode has been completely eliminated, with all relevant work completed within the system. The system can build models to quickly find solutions, with efficiency far higher than the traditional manual mode. Because the system contains tens of thousands of traffic data models, it can handle almost all sudden traffic events and make accurate, scientific planning recommendations.

These four major systems constitute the foundation of the Shanghai World Expo's Big Data Traffic. The spatial scope of the four systems includes the Expo Park, surrounding traffic control zones, Expo expressways, comprehensive transportation transfer hubs, rail transit networks, Expo dedicated bus channels, Expo Park waterway channels, and other directly related areas. In addition, these big data technologies have broken through geographical restrictions, serving not only the Shanghai area but also radiating the Yangtze River Delta region, covering all spaces related to the Expo. Therefore, despite the record-high number of visitors to the Shanghai World Expo, orderly traffic operation showed no obvious omissions, providing a strong guarantee for the hosting of the Expo.

With the closing of the Shanghai World Expo, these big data platform systems continue to serve Shanghai and develop further. License plate recognition data, land use remote sensing image data, mobile communication data, etc., have now been fully implemented and applied in Shanghai. It can be seen that big data applications can guarantee major events, serve urban construction more extensively, and form a broader Big Data Traffic System, ensuring the coordinated advancement of urban traffic.

# 9.3 Big Data Traffic and the Hangzhou G20 Summit

From September 4 to 5, 2016, the G20 Summit was held in Hangzhou, China. Compared to the Olympics and the World Expo, the G20 Summit is an international economic cooperation forum where the heads of state of 20 major countries gathered in Hangzhou. It carries greater political and economic significance and is another important demonstration of China's comprehensive national strength and economic power. As an important barometer of national economic development, traffic also became a focus of world attention.

In 2016, internet development entered a new stage, different from 2008 and 2010. The concept of big data had been widely applied in the commercial field, and an increasing number of professional big data service companies emerged. Therefore, compared to the Beijing Olympics and the Shanghai World Expo, the Big Data Traffic for the G20 Summit was more mature, presenting characteristics of refinement and differentiation. Whether it was the Transportation Department, the Traffic Police Department, or even the Zhejiang Provincial Government, all listed Big Data Traffic as a top priority.

## 9.3.1 Government-Level Attention to Big Data Traffic

In May 2016, on the eve of the Hangzhou G20 Summit, Zhejiang Provincial Governor Li Qiang inspected the preparatory work for the G20 Summit, placing special emphasis on traffic issues. He pointed out that the construction of an interconnected modern comprehensive transportation system should be accelerated to support Hangzhou's future development. This provided clear guidance at the government level for Big Data Traffic, facilitating the rapid establishment of big data platforms.

Subsequently, Li Qiang made a special inspection of Big Data Traffic, particularly the Hangzhou G20 Summit Traffic Security Comprehensive Information Command Center. The center has fully realized big data access, integration of major transportation modes, and real-time monitoring and dispatching of full-domain traffic. Information on expressways, railways, civil aviation, etc., has also been incorporated into the platform. This platform is the core of guaranteeing G20 Summit traffic and played an important role in information collection and transmission.

## 9.3.2 Big Data Support for Road Traffic

In the Big Data Traffic application for the Hangzhou G20 Summit, professional big data companies were introduced, among which "Dameng Data" (Dameng Data) participated in the work of the Traffic Police Detachment. Dameng Data provided the Hangzhou Traffic Police Detachment with a comprehensive Traffic Big Data Platform, capable of real-time analysis of traffic dynamics and information assessment. It can quickly formulate traffic measures based on regional traffic characteristics, greatly guaranteeing the smoothness and safety of Hangzhou's traffic.
