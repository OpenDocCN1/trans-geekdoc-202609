# 11.1 What are cookies

At a high level, cookies are basically just data that a cookie is stored on a user’s com-\puter. When you visit a website that creates a cookie, it isn't actually storing
data on its own servers; The cookie file is saved on your computer. That is
why you can clear you cookies in almost every browser - you are deleting
local files.

What makes cookies unique from other files on your computer is that every
time you visit a website, your browser will automatically include the cookies
that the website has permission to access. This is really handy, because it means
that you don't need to type your password in every time you visit a web page.
