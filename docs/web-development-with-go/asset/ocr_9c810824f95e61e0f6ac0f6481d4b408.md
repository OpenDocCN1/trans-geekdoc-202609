# 10.1. Encryption functions are NOT hashing functions

At some point you may learn about encryption functions like AES and think they would be a good fit here, but I want to once again reiterate encryption and hashing are two different things, and state that encryption is not appropriate for an storing passwords.

The reason for this is pretty simple. When you encrypt a password it is reversible. Yes, you do need the encryption key to decrypt a password, but if someone has hacked your encryption key it isn't a stretch to imagine that they might be able to figure out your encryption key, and once they do they will be able to decrypt every password in your database.
