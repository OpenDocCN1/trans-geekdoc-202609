What is important is that, at the end of the day, the incentive to commit the crime is taken away from criminals and that cryptocurrency is not seen as a safe haven.

One of the most prominent Italian criminals, turned State’s witness, Gaspare Mutolo, one day told the Italian Parliamentary Anti-Mafia commission, “The worst feeling is when our money is taken away from us. People prefer to be put behind bars and keep their money than to stay free without the money. Money is the main thing.”** And this is true for cryptocurrency too.
