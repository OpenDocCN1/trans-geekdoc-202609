The secondary market conducts the exchanges of cryptocurrencies. Table 1.2 records the historical total market capitalizations of the overall cryptocurrency market value. It reflects the growing size of different cryptocurrency types, the growing supply of each type of cryptocurrency, and the growing fiat money supply by the central banks and governments across the world. On November 8, 2021, the total cap reached its all-time high of $3.08 trillion.
