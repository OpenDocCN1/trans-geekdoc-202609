There are two types of option contract structures: American and European. The American option contract is structured such that the buyer of the option can exercise his or her right to buy or sell the stock or cryptocurrency anytime from the day the contract is signed until expiration. The European option contract is structured such that the investor can only exercise the option on the expiration day. The investor can get out of both options anytime by selling his or her contract at the market premium until expiration. Due to their unique structure, American and European type options are traded at different premium prices. The valuation methods discussed later in the chapter take into consideration each of these structures.

When it comes to comparing the equity options market to the cryptocurrency options market, the differences are everywhere distinct. For starters, cryptocurrency options trade 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Another difference is that the contract size for cryptocurrencies can vary not only by the exchange, but also by the coin. The major cryptocurrency markets include Binance®, Deribit, OKEx®, BitMex®, and the traditional exchange of CME®. Most of these markets are not regulated in the United States and have varying levels of licensing and regulation from governments around the world. The largest exchange in the world, Deribit, offers contracts representing 1 BTC, with a minimum contract size of 0.1.

In addition, most of these cryptocurrency option contracts are structured as European types. Deribit uses cash settlement, and contracts expire at 4 AM ET Saturday. Deribit only accepts deposits and withdrawals in cryptocurrency. The CME Group offers Bitcoin derivative trading with rules similar to their other derivative markets; however, CME uses Bitcoin futures as the underlying asset, rather than Bitcoin itself.

Regarding the more than 10,000 cryptocurrencies that are in circulation, exchanges usually only offer derivative markets on the major ones, including Bitcoin and Ethereum. Since this is very new to the markets, the option trading volume can be very low at times. All cryptocurrency contracts use the blockchain technology, but decentralized finance (DeFi) allows individuals to enter cryptocurrency option contracts that settle by code rather than an exchange. DeFi allows countless possibilities for automation, such as algorithmic market making, but suffers from vulnerability to scams and hacks because of its reliance on code.
