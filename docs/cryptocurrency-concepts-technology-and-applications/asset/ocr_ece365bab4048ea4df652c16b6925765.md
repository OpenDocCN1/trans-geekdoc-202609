Further evidence of the importance of crypto was demonstrated by the White House's moving toward developing a policy approach on crypto. On March 9, 2022, President Biden signed an executive order on “Ensuring Responsible Development of Digital Assets.” The European Union, as of March 25, 2022, is also well on its way to passing the landmark regulatory framework Markets in Crypto Assets (MiCA) for governing crypto assets.
