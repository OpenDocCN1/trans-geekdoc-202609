# 4.1 Introduction

Central bank digital currency (CBDC) is part of the ongoing change and disruptive financial innovation occurring in the modern financial system. Historically, financial innovation lies within the realm of private sector agents such as regulated financial institutions and financial technology companies. They develop innovative ideas and create innovative financial products which the central bank or the regulator may approve or disallow. This means that the originator of disruptive financial innovations are primarily private sector agents, not the central bank.
