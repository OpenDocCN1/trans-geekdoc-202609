# 3.1 Corpus of Novels of the Spanish Silver Age: CoNSSA and CoNSSA-canon

The chronological criteria to filter authors do not refer to their lifespan, but to whether their works were published between 1880 and 1939. How does that affect the distribution of the different generations in the population? Is there a homogeneous distribution? Are there many authors who are either much younger or much older in comparison to most authors? To explore, the decades of the authors' birth and death are plotted in Figure 9.

Figure 9: Bar plot of birth of authors

![](bbox=[0.133, 0.318, 0.803, 0.538])

Figure 9 shows that the actual span of dates of birth represents a century, with a distribution skewed to the right with a median⁵ of 1881 and a variance, in Interquartile Range (IQR),⁶ of 28 years. That means that half of the authors were born between 1853 and 1939. The older authors are over 50 by the year 1880 (the oldest is José Selgas Carrasco, born in 1822), followed by Juan Valeria in 1924, while the youngest author was born in 1917 (Rafael García Serrano). It is surprising that no author in the 1920s got to publish their first novel before 1939.
