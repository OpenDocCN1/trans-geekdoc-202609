# 3.3.3 CORDE 1860–1960 + CoNSSA

From the complete CORDE, I extract a section between 1860 and 1960. This represents the boundaries of CoNSSA (1880 and 1939) as an additional margin of 20 years. There are several reasons for extending the chronological boundaries. First, the data is already available, so it is reasonable to work with more data rather than with less. Second, as discussed in Section 3.1.3, some authors of CoNSSA are representatives of former movements, so some could actually find more similarities in earlier publications. Third, some texts analyzed in CoNSSA might have created tendencies that were later practiced by other authors, which argues for an extension of the date of CORDE to some decades after the end of the COVID.

The section of the CORDE between 1860 and 1960 contains 6,587 different texts, including more than 81 million tokens, counting the typographical tokens amongst them. As mentioned lately, the works are divided into 19 different categories.
