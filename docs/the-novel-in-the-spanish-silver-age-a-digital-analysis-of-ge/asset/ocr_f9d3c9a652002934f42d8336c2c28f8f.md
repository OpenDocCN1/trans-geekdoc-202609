In this map, certain historical phenomena become evident: Madrid is the place where most authors are born, and even more, where they tend to their days. On the other side, other regions are clearly underrepresented, especially Catalonia. The geographical places of birth and death are two other examples that demonstrate almost nothing is balanced in a literary corpus, and that it is reasonable to expect that other sociological variables will behave similarly.²² What is the distribution of texts per author? Each author has two texts in median, mode of one, and an IQR of four. Half of the authors have between one and six texts in the corpus, being the most typical case a single work.
