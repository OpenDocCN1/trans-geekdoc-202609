The goal of this chapter is to predict whether a series of specific texts in CoNSSA can be labeled as novels or not. What is interpreted as a novel can differ from one project to the other. What CORDE labels novels could be different to that of the manual MLE, i.e. the labeling practices of these sources can differ. Although I consider the labeling of the MLE more accurate (since it is a manual written by experts in literature), I have the texts from CORDE, a general corpus. Using data and metadata exclusively from the RAE signify that I am assuming their understanding of the novel. Thus, to avoid this, I merge CORDE 1860–1960 + CoNSSA. resulting in a new corpus: “CORDE 1860–1960 + CoNSSA”. Since many texts are present in both corpora, I control the novels in both corpora by deleting the duplicates. In addition, I delete all the texts from the authors of the 14 genre-disputed texts, so that the algorithm cannot learn the statistics patterns of Antonio Machado or Juan Ramón Jiménez, for example. In other words, the authorial cue will not influence the results of the prediction. This merged corpus has a total of 6,800 texts (6,456 texts from 344 novels from CoNSSA).
