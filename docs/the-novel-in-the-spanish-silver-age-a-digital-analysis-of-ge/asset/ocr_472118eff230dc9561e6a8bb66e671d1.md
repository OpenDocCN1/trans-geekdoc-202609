To operationalize this prototypicality, I have used one of the possible operationalizations. However, at least two other operationalizations of prototypicality of genres are possible. The first is to adopt a more historical perspective and consider the center of the prototype of the works that published first or had a greater influence in later works (Henny-Kramer et al. 2018). The second is to obtain qualitative labels from annotators in they can express the subgenres to which the text belongs and how clearly the text belongs to this subgenre (discussed in Section 7.1.6). The option chosen is coherent with the cross-sectional perspective using several already existing sources.

The fourth observation of genres states that texts can be described by their specific internal features. For now, the model has only two types of instances: genre categories (expressed through the labels) and texts. Now, the third main element is added: internal features (introduced in Chapters 3.2 and 4.1, and evaluated in Chapter 6.1 and Section 7.1.5).
