# Acknowledgements

The fact that I am the only author of this work does not mean that I could have done it without the support of many. This can also be explained as a series of steps in life, and I thank God for those. I imagined I could be able to write a book of more than 400 pages about literature enjoying most of the process. God does move in mysterious ways.

There are two groups of people that have helped me through this process: the professional and the personal. These can be structured in concentric circles, moving closer towards them.

The largest groups of professionals I am thankful to, are two communities that have made many steps of this work possible. The first one are generous contributors of Stack Overflow,¹ from whom I have learned so much, from their questions and their answers (to me or to other people). The second one are the hardworking editors of epublic,² a source from which I have extracted many texts, labels, and summaries. Thanks to both communities.

The second circle is populated by researchers who have offered their time to teach me, debate, or give me personal feedback. I have learned a lot from the workshops of researchers such as Maciej Eder, Jan Rybicki, Mike Kestemont, Frank Fischer, Helena Bermúdez Sabel, and Pablo Ruiz Fabo. I have enjoyed the motivating exchange with Laura Hernández Lorenzo and Daniil Skorinkin, and appreciate deeply the support and collaboration with Elena Martínez Carro, María Concepción Jiménez Fernández, and Maria Teresa Santa María Fernández.
