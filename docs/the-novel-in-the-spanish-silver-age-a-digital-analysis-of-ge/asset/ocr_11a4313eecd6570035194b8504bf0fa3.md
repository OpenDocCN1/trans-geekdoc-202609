I manually map each semantic-label to the closest subject in Thema when one is found. From the 109 different semantic-labels, I locate Thema subjects for 61, leaving 47 without a standardized correspondence. This means that I manage to standardize 56.5% of the semantic labels.8

# ### 5.1.6 Analysis of Inter-Source Agreement

The fact that two sources employ the same semantic-label does not imply that they assign it to the same texts, that they consider the same extension of the category. Two manuals of Spanish literature might understand what an erotic novel could be in a completely different way, and therefore use the label for disjointed or partially overlapping sections of the corpus. Accepting the several sources is discussed in the research, for example, whether the labels of the covers are considered as subgenres for a further analysis (Bortrel 2001, 38–45). If this is the case, I expect the covers to assign dissimilar information to the rest of the sources.
