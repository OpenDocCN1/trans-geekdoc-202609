The horizontal axis: Now the data has a maximum value of 0.07. If this is multiplied by 100, it becomes the percentage of the text. For both texts, commas represent around 7% of all tokens of the text. In Sub-figure D, a perfect correlation between the total and the relative frequencies are observed, although the slope vary with the total length of the entire text.

The relative frequency is useful for comparing specific tokens in different texts. One of the disadvantages is that extreme skew makes some features much more prominent than others. While the majority of real data is close to zero, there are many outliers with high values. Besides, there is no definition of the range. In one text, the most frequent token could represent 10% of the text, while in another text it represents only 2%.
