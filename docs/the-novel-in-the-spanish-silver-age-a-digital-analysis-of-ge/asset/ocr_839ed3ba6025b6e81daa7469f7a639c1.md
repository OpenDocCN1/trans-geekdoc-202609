Another useful differentiation of any concept is the characteristics that constitute the concept, while the extension is the number of referents that belong to the symbol *extension and intention*. The intention is the number of referents... (Kabakate and Pusch 2011, 124; Pawlowski 1980, 53–54; Escandell Vidal 2012, 23–25). The intention of the symbol *novel* would be the characteristics of the concept 'novel', while the extension would be the number of instances in the world that are novels. A possible inductive analysis of genre would be a study of the intention of, for example, adventure novels (their shared features), by collecting the entire extension of adventure novels and analyzing their characteristics.
