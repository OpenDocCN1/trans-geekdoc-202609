Other information about the period is the span of the action over time, formalized in days. This field is the only one that has been originally encoded as interval, as a value with real numerical value and not only as an ordinal value which expresses its position in a sorted list. Besides its informational nature, this field has a further peculiarity. Most numerical characteristics of a text are originated in a process of counting: How many words are there in a text? For this numerical information, Statistics expects a Poisson distribution. In contrast to these examples, the chronological span is not a counting information and, therefore, there are no clear expectations about how the distribution of this field should be.
