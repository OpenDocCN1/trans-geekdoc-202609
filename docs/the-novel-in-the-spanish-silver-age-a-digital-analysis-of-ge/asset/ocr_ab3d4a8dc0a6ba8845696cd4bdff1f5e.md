Taxonomy, as a macro-model of the conceptual understanding of the genres similar to the species in biology, is surprisingly frequent, even in our authors like Schnee (Schaeffer 2006, 9). This conception was taken further by authors like Brunetieri, in 1880, proposed a Darwinian evolution of the genres, which they would compete against each other (see Petersen 1944, 121 or Schaeffer 2006, 33 for a critique). The idea that genres can be explained with animal simples is also present in Moretti's hypothesis that states genres tend to survive over a similar span of years as living beings.
