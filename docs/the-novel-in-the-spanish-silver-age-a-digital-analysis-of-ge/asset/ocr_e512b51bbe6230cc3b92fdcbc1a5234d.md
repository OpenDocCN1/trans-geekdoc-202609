The subgenre yields almost perfect classification (0.97 F-score). Because of its canonical status, this genre is not present in CoNSA-canon, and therefore its following description is only based on CoNSA.

The most specific features of this subgenre are clearly linguistics, with semantic fields being overrepresented such as the transmission of information (*intigar, predecir, citar, enviar, recado, teléfono, recado, verdad*), social relations (*saprobrar, corregir, atravesar, imponer*), danger (*baquetear, atravesas, travesis*), politics and society (*ley, politica, herada, moda*) and others (*elemento, perseverar*).

Literary features that are are related to the setting: The plots take to place in cities, trips through several continents, in boats, or in Spain, being typically realistic. However, some novels depict a non-existing world (especially the later works). The protagonists are normally adults from the middle class, the narrator is usually autodiegetic or the text is in epistolary form, and the ending is rather partially or clearly positive (with some neutral exceptions).
