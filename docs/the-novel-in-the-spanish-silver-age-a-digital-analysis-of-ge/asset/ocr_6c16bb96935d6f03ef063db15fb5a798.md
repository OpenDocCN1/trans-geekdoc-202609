The novel by Unamuno (on the right of Figure 103) is labeled as a war by all sources, and therefore is a good representative of the genre. This gives this edge of 100% or a proportion of one. In addition, third of the sources have also assigned the labels historical and modernist to this text. The text also belongs to these categories, but their connections are not so strong (33% or a proportion of 0.33). The quantification of the participation of a text in genre is expressed as a weight of the edges, which is visualized as thickness. The three novels are connected by the labels war and historical novel, and appear in the middle of the figure. The thickest edge in the graph is that connecting Paz en la guerra and war novel, because, as explained, all sources applied to this text. Zalacain el aventurero is connected to five subgenres in total, but its thickest edge is to the adventure novel, a label that half of the sources assigned to this text.
