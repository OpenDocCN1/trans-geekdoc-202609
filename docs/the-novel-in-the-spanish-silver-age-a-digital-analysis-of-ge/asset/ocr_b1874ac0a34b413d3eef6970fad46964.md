The same central position has literary fiction, a special case of the subgenre palette which been found in a single source: the publishers on Amazon (see Chapter 5.3). This subgenre has the lowest specificity of features of all subgenres (standard deviation of z-scores of o.28). It contains 190 novels in CoSSA, 94 in CoNSSA-canon. The novels are, surprisingly, highly canonized, with 1.64 pages in the manual on average. This explains a low classification result of 0.75 F1-score. The strongest feature of this subgenre would be a protagonist of lower classes of the society, followed at a distance by a setting in France, and surprisingly, that the story does not reflect the author's life. From the linguistic perspective: communication (criticar, discurso, oratoria, char), money and work (ajretar, bien, propiedad, encargar, función, pagar), royalty (soberano, rey, linaje), and other fields (familia and negations) are distinctive.
