More specifically, the texts labeled as dialogue novels rarely participate in any other genre, they tend to participate in this category. In addition, this atypical type of novel does share features with other novels, such as vocabulary about cognition, disorientation, or numerous divisions in text.⁴ Actually, the majority of the features of this category are exclusive, i.e. they are not shared with any other nodes – among others, the grammatical information about the first person, narrators in epistolary style, the em dash, the TEI element sp, or vocabulary about optics, to learn, to attenuate, to suppose, or to revenge.
