The biologic novel shows an eigenvector centrality of 0.11, and a relatively low specificity of features (standard deviation of z-scores of 0.50). The subgenre is populated by 30 novels (14 in CoNSSA-canon), relatively well canonized (1.44 pages in the manual MdLE, 2.12 for the cases in CoNSSA-canon). Its classification is relatively high (0.88 F-score).

The features overrepresented are literary ones, such as a close relation between the text and the author, ends that are either partially positive or partially negative (i.e., the ending is neither clearly positive, negative, or neutral), settings that mix several types (for example, city and town) countries, and a protagonist from the middle class.

The linguistic vocabulary represented in this category are about time (seguida, joven), education and childhood (collegio, resumen, seguro, memoria, pequeño), food (estómago, cerdo), positive vocabulary (benévolo, placer, dácil), others (tipo, espacio, silencio, mado, escaso), besides the presence of poems in the text.
