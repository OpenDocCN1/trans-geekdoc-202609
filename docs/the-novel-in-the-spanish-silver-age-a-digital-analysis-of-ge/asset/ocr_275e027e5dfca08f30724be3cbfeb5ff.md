# 7.2.4 Length of Novels

A further hypothesis is to observe whether the length of the text influences the classification results. As in the case of the number of texts, both outcomes are argue. On the one hand, longer novels contain more words, which means that their features are less assigned (contain fewer features assigned with zero), which should lead to a more exact classification. On the other hand, shorter novels could possibly be defined by a sharper set of features, while longer novels tend to participate in several genres blurring their relationship to each single genre. In this case, the x-axis of the scatter plot is the median length in tokens of the subgenre.
