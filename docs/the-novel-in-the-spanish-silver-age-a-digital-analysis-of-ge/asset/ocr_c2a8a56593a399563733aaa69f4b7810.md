This seems to show that works of authorized author are more likely to also be considered literary fiction, even when they are not treated with such detail by manuals like the MdLE.

To obtain a first glimpse of what characteristics are specific for this chapter, I explore metadata and linguistic information that is associated with the texts that have been assigned this label. The year of birth of the author, the year of publication, and the mean and standard deviation of the length of sentences correlate negatively with the frequency of assignment of the literary fiction label (details can be read in the Jupyter Notebook). However, the following aspects correlate positively with the literary fiction: the number of publishing houses that have published it, the number of chapters, and the number of interjections and instances of direct speech the work includes. This gives a first broad description about the works labeled as literary fiction: Works published in the first analyzed decades, written by the oldest generation and published again from several publishers.
