## 6.2.5 Comparison of Clusters and Evaluation with Metadata

The previous evaluation illustrates a series of tendencies across parameters with positive tendencies towards a clearly optimal combination of them. This means there is no perfect way of clustering the corpus to find subgenres, rather several ways. These different combinations of features could be pointing out the same or to several potential subgenres. In the next step, I apply the different values of the parameters that were most successful and cluster the corpus of novels several times. To be more specific, the features come from the mixed model containing lexical frequencies, TEI-tags, linguistic annotation, and ordered entities, as explained in Section 6.1.4. These features are transformed into four variants with the best facet grid: log, tf-idf, log10-scores, and tfidf-scores. The algorithms and the number of features have not shown a clear trend as to which of them work better, all of them are applied (except for 7,000 features). The algorithms are set to split the corpus into two clusters, and every combination of parameters is repeated ten times.

This setup produces more than 900 competing clusters: almost a thousand ways of organizing the corpus in binary groups. This represents nearly a thousand potential new subgenres. Of course, many of them can be very similar, or perhaps even identical. Possibly, there are actually two or three distinct basic clusters, and the remaining 900 show just little differences. To observe the similarity between these 900 competing models, I use a clustering method. However, instead of using the linguistic information of each text, the algorithm now turns into a binary feature of the clusters and this is the underlying data of the next step. This produces a dendrogram of meta-clusters, as observed in the following figure. In this representation, two exact clusters with two clusters that contain the same texts will be tightly grouped in the meta-cluster, while two clusters that sorted the texts in the meta-cluster will be far apart.
