In general, the best combination of parameters gives close to perfection. All the genres achieve their best F1-scores between 0.91 and 1.0, all being significant in comparison with the baseline of 0.5. Even when two standard deviations are considered, the results (the worst case, *Sociedad*, are still well above the baseline, such as close to 0.8). The categories with the best results are highly canonical, as plays both in verse and prose, literary prose texts, novels, letters, or technical texts about science, law, or religion. The cases with the lowest classification values in this evaluation are biographies and autobiographies, news, lyric prose, essays, and technical texts about history, society, and art. Some of these results can be explained. Plays, particularly in verse, are easily recognizable, and topics like science, law, and religion have a very particular vocabulary, especially if they are compared to more common themes such as society or art. Still, it is surprising that novels and essays are among the most difficult classes. In Chapter 7.2 it will be analyzed in depth the possible explanations for the variance of the classification in the ConSSA.
