These annotations do not have to agree on the tokenization. Consequently, the idea of standoff markup for identifying tokens and adding annotation in other elements does not seem ideal.

I choose to encode the annotation of each tool in parallel elements as TCF would do. In contrast to this format, I retain the text of the tokens in each element. For this, I customize the TEI schema of the project, creating the element `anno_orig` ('annotation') as a third option of the element choice, which normally contains `orig` ('origin') and `nrg` ('regularization') with their exact same properties. In the next example, the annotation contains a very simple paragraph in direct speech with only one sentence: “— ejemplo tú.” First, the primary text is to be found in the *orig* element. *several anno elements contain the annotation from several tools and flavors.* To keep the example short, I use the ellipsis to represent further attributes or elements:

```xml
<p>
<seg type="direct-speech">
<s>
<choice>
<orig>—Por ejemplo tú.</orig>
<anno source="freeling" type="tokens">
<w cligs:form="-" lemma="-\-" cligs:tag="Fg" cligs:pos="punctuation" type="hyphen"/>
<w cligs:form="Por" lemma="por" cligs:tag="SP" cligs:pos="adposition" type="preposition"/>
<w cligs:form="ejemplo" lemma="ejemplo" cligs:tag="NMSOO" cligs:pos="noun" cligs:gen="common" cligs:num="singular" cligs:wn="05820620-n"/>
<w cligs:form="tú" lemma="tú" cligs:tag="Pp2CSNo" cligs:pos="pronoun" type="personal" cligs:person="2" cligs:gen="common" cligs:num="singular" cligs:case="nominative"/>
<w cligs:form="." lemma="." cligs:tag="Fp" cligs:pos="punctuation" type="period"/>
</anno>
<anno source="freeling" type="multiwords">
<w cligs:form="-" lemma="-\-" tag="Fg" pos="ejemplo" cligs:tag="RG" cligs:pos="adverb" type="general" cligs:wn="00159040-r" cligs:wnlex="adv.all"/>
```

```xml
<w clogs-form="tú" lemma="tú" clogs-tag="PP2CSNo" clogs-pos="pronoun" type="personal" clogs-person="2" clogs-gen="common" clogs-num="singular" clogs-case="nominative"/>
<w clogs-form="." lemma="." clogs-tag="Fp" clogs-pos="punctuation" type="period"/>
<anno>
<anno source="dpde" type="dis-part" />
<anno source="refranario" type="dis-part" />
<choice>
<s>
<seg>
</p>
```

One can observe that the two annotations of the tool FreeLing agree on three tokens (“,” “tú”, “.”) but disagree on the words “por ejemplo”: Depending on a parameter in FreeLing, the tool will consider either a single lexical unit or two. A standoff markup identifying different layers of tokens and units would have been possible, but the extraction of specific features could have been more error-prone.

There are two disadvantages of this way of annotating. First is its verbosity: In the previous example, the information on three tokens is repeated. However, other solutions would have led to similar redundancies, only in different standoff elements or files. The second disadvantage is the opacity of one annotation towards the rest. In this format, the idiom recognized by FreeLing, “por ejemplo,” does not contain the information that it is composed of a preposition and a noun. If the sentence had contained proverbs, these would not be annotated with their semantic or grammatical characteristics. Of course, these aspects are irrelevant for the specific goal of this research about subgenres, but could be of importance in other projects with specific linguistic questions about these compositional units.
