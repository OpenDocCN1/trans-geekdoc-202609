Ródenas de Moya has defended the idea about the fatigue of the novel in the late years of the Spanish avant-garde (1998, 137; 2000, 67), which could explain the drop at the beginning of the 1930s.^12 The texts marked in the MdLe have been identified. The next step is to observe whether this manual assigns a further label to them, a subgenre, like erotic novel, adventure novel, or naturalist novel.^13 Out of 727, a subgenre label can be identified for 349 cases. In total, this manual uses 88 labels, the most frequent being novel erótica (erotic novel) with 38 works (5% of the population). This label is followed by five labels between thirty and eight works: *episodio nacional, histórica, naturalista, social, realista, de aventuras, y humorista*. The first of this group has been already discussed in the Chapter 2.1 as one of the one-author-labels, similar to historical novels, coined and employed by Galdós. The rest of the labels can be accepted as unproblematic types of novels. After this, there is a long tail of dozens labels with very few texts explaining them, and many of them are disputable: *de carne* ('about the flesh'), de la vida ('about life'), *sevillanas* ('from Seville'), sentimental, valenciana ('from Valencia'), *lírica, de transición* ('of transition'),

At the end of this long tail of the frequency of labels in the MdLe, 65 of the 88 labels are used for up to three texts. In fact, there are 36 labels that are assigned to a single novel: Either all these are extraordinary single works that functioned and ended a subgenre, or it is a too differentiated use of labels to categorize them. That means, the MdLe has a strong tendency to use very specific labels for few texts with a hand-full of exceptions like erotic, historical, or naturalist novel.

As I have shown, the several decades contain different numbers of novels. Now can be expected that the different subgenres should be equally distributed throughout the decades in the corpus. Consequently, they have the same or a similar number of erotic novels, in each decade. But, how is the original data of the population distributed? Does it depart from a balanced situation?
