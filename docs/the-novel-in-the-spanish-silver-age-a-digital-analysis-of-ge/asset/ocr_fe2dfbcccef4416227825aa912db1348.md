+   - Baza de Espadas, by Valle-Valle-Inclán, was not published as a book during its authors life, but only in magazines in 1932, his sole novel in that decade.

All these texts are marked explicitly as exceptions in their metadata and none of them are part of the CoNSSA-canon, which does not contain any exception. Thus, they can be automatically ignored or used for specific purposes.

The 14 works whose genre is unclear also belong exclusively to CoNSSA, and they contain some of the most influential works of this period, such as La lámpara maravilla y La media noche by Valle-Inclán, Platero y Diario de un poeta by Ramón Jiménez, De cancionero apocrifo and Juan de Mairena by Antonio Machado, or two editions of the Greguerías by Gómez de la Serna.

Some of them contain certain chapters belonging clearly to one genre, and others to others (such as poetry and prose, like in De cancionero apocrifo or de un poeta). The majority of them represent an intrinsic mix of genres across chapters. The main genre of these works will be analyzed in Chapter 3.3.

The number of novels of each author defined, I had to choose which novels of each author should be digitized for CoNSSA. In this step, I have just followed practical criteria about the digitized status, or the price of editions. Instead, I tried to define several criteria in order to increase the representativity for the entire production of the author. These criteria are the following:

+   - Preference for the most canonized texts of each author.
- Representation of the different periods, subgenres, and groups of texts by each author.
- Relative balance of decades over the complete population of novels.

The second criterion seeks to cover the different styles from one and the same author, especially as far as the prolific ones are concerned. Baroja wrote in different genres (adventures, dialogue, historical, sentimental), grouping them together in different series (La lucha por la vida, La raza, Las ciudades...), and his writing stretches over four decades of the analyzed period (1900-1939). The selected texts for the CoNSSA intend to represent all these facets.
