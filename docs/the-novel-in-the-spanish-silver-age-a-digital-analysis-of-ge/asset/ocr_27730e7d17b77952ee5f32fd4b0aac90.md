# 5.3.2 Description of Literary Fiction

The publishers assigned three labels related to literary fiction in Spanish to my corpus: ficción clásica (136 novels), ficción contemporánea (86), and novel y literatura (1). In general, there is a great overlap between these different labels, with the concept of the label 'classic' being especially frequent in the works published in the 1880s and the 1890s. This means the labels and the way they were allocated by the publishers do not correspond to different meanings, but are just a matter of chronological distribution: ficción clásica for the earlier works, ficción contemporánea for the later works.
