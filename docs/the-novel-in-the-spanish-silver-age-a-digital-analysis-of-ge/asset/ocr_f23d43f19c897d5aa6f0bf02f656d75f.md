# 3.2.11 Description of the Metadata of CoNSSA

In this final section, my goal is to describe the corpus in terms of metadata. By doing so, I want to achieve three different goals: Firstly, I show how already existing knowledge in digital metadata (coming from summaries or personal readings) can uncover broader pictures about literature. In other words, metadata is the first step to run distant analyses that are not explorations of uncertain tombs, but inductive descriptions of literary features. Second, I describe the general tendencies of novels of this period. These general patterns of the entire genre can be to compare specific characteristics of some subgenres: Are urban or contemporary settings of the erotic novel, or are they the most common values in all the novels of that time, regardless of the subgenre? Finally, I explore the most noticeable changes of specific literature phenomena over time: For example, are sad more common at the beginning of this period and therefore more typical in subgenres of the 19th century like in the realistic or naturalist novels?
