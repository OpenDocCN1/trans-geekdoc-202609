A number of the features, i.e. shared particularities, are distinctive for not only one, but for two or three novels. For example, the two novels at the bottom of the previous figure, *La media noche* and *Paz en la Guerra*, share many distinctive semantic fields about life, troops, fighting, peace, enemies. On the left, *Zalain el aventurero* shares vocabulary with projectiles in *La media noche*, on the right, it does not share any other vocabulary with *Paz en la guerra*. These three war novels have a single semantic field in common: vocabulary about war, which appears in the middle of the figure.

In these cases, the features can be more characteristic for one novel than for another. This difference is kept in the edges through the z-scores, which are visualized through the thickness of the edge. For example, the edge between the semantic feature of war (in the middle of the network) for the novels *La media noche* (z-score of 4.41) or *Paz en la guerra* (3.13) is thicker than between either of these novels and *Zalain el aventurero* (1.56).
