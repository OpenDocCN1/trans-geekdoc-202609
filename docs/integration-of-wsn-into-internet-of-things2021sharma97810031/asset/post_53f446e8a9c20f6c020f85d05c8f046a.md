Attacks and Preventions for CPS

There are many solutions for attacks against WSNs and IoT network [9]; therefore, considering the path, we are proposing a framework that is unified, meaning it can be fit anywhere, and scalable, meaning the scale of this framework can be scaled up or down as per convenience and need, at the same time. Our proposed framework takes considerations for constraints and scarce resources. The framework is shown in Figure 10.6. In this framework, some activities are performed

on each layer of CPS architecture. Correspondingly, these activities must employ security mechanisms, as shown in Figure 10.6, for every layer. This framework may overcome challenges and existing attacks at large; however, the main characteristic of the proposed model is a unified framework and scalable approach that can be deployed on an existing framework.

In Figure 10.6, each layer of architecture (on the left), its usages (on the right), and precautions offered by our framework (in the middle) are presented. The connection layer offers condition-based monitoring to the conversion layer which provides prognostic and health management to the cyber layer which in turn transfers data as the CPS. The data pulled by CPS offer the decision-making ability for the decision support system that is used by the resilient control system.

These activities are summarized below:

- Resilient Control System: this activity is performed on the configuration layer of CPS layer architecture, and it controls the CPS environment and also ensures resiliency in the entire CPS service architecture. Security mechanisms such as authorization must be employed to provide security solutions.

- Decision Support System: this activity is performed on the cognition layer of CPS layer architecture, and it primarily focuses on taking and executing decisions based on cognitive and intelligence determined by artificial intelligence and machine learning. Security mechanisms such as encipherment must be employed to provide security solutions.

- Cyber–Physical System: this activity is performed on the cyber layer of CPS layer architecture, and it focuses on obtaining data from the network and also it is responsible for sending the data further to decision support and execution and to layers above it. Security mechanisms such as end-to-end encryption (E2EE) must be employed to provide security solutions.

- Prognostic and Health Management: this activity is performed on the conversion layer of CPS layer architecture, and it focuses on the provision of diagnostic and prognostic capabilities, leveraging the increased availability of sensor data, etc. Security mechanisms such as integrity verification and E2EE must be employed to provide security solutions.

- Condition-Based Monitoring: this activity is performed on the connection layer of CPS layer architecture, and it focuses on monitoring logical executions enabling the CPS to perform automated decisions for executing industrial tasks. Security mechanisms such as mutual authentication must be employed to provide security solutions.

In our framework, the configuration layer is protected with the PoLP and proper authorization. The cognition layer has a guard of encipherment, whereas the cyber layer has E2EE. Integrity verification and E2EE protect the conversion layer, and mutual authentication is used for establishing secure connections in the connection layer.

The framework may be considered for deployment and here, we list down the attributes of the framework.

- Unified: the framework can be fitted into any system, even an existing system with ease as the requirements for this are simple.

- Scalable: the framework can be scaled up or down as per need.

- Best of Constraint Devices: the solutions described by the framework are resource-efficient, therefore best of resource-scarce and devices with constraints.

- Solution for Each Layer: the framework provides solutions for every layer of the CPS architecture.

## 10.6 CONCLUSION

With ever-advancing technologies comes convenience; however, they bring new challenges and risks as well. In this chapter, we discussed the advancements of WSNs, IoT network, and CPS that have impacted the working of society and identified their architecture for a deep dive into the Industry 4.0. We discussed the security paradigm of WSNs, IoT networks, and CPS. We also discussed the requirements of security, vulnerability, and challenges in all three different paradigms along with their solutions. The study further shows the relation and the evolution of these among paradigms evolving from WSNs to IoT networks and further toward CPS. Further, we also proposed a unified and scalable security framework for CPS that can be applied to existing CPS architecture. The framework suffices the problems in the existing domain and scalable in nature for the available constrained devices in the CPS network.
