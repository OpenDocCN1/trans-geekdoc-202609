But SKC fails to minimize the effects of the secret key exposure, whereas PKC satisfies this requirement. PKC is considered to be too computationally expensive for small devices. Recent studies showed that it is feasible to apply PKCs to small wireless devices with minimal resources by choosing public-key cryptographic algorithms [76,77].

Public-Key Infrastructure (PKI): This is an arrangement that binds public keys with respective users' identities utilizing public-key certificates issued by a certificate authority (CA). The significant components of PKI are the client, the CA, the registration authority, and the repository. To provide the services of PKI, these components and their functionalities must be mapped to the entities of WSNs. Most sensor networks will use a simple hierarchical PKI architecture, with only one root CA. Registration, initialization, key generation, certification, and certification retrieval are done in WSNs using this technique.

Identity-Based Cryptography (IBC): This allows a user's public key to be easily derived from its known identity information such as an email address or a cellular phone number by eliminating the need for public-key certificates [78]. ID-based schemes are more suitable for these WSN scenarios. Each sensor node that has its unique identification information, such as serial numbers, gets the corresponding private keys from the BS, which serves as the PKG. To authenticate each other, only the identity information should be exchanged without extra public-key data. IBC makes it possible to establish a session key without any interaction.

# 2.5 APPLICATIONS OF WIRELESS SENSOR NETWORK

WSNs are used in various sectors. It has a potential applications in domestic, environmental, and military use, among others. Different types of industries also utilize the WSN. In this section, both of these usage criteria are described briefly.

# 2.5.1 GENERAL APPLICATIONS OF WIRELESS SENSOR NETWORKS

The application of WSNs has increased with the scalability of infrastructure. During inception, WSNs were used in the military sector because of their small size and low cost. Moreover, due to their characteristics, they also have potential use in environmental areas. In weather stations, various wireless sensors are used, especially in harsh environments. Sensors can be deployed in underground caves and mines to detect harmful gases, depth, and air quality. This eventually helps the miners to go down in the cave safely and do their tasks. In the medical sector, sensors have tremendous use. Sensors can detect a patient's brain signals for treatment. Sensors can also be placed inside hospitals for administration purposes such as checking the doctors, nurses, and patients of their attendance. It can also be used to detect the heart rate of patients. This sensor is also mounted even in some smartphones. Thus they are more commonly available than earlier.
