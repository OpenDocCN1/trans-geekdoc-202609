# ## 8.3.1 DATA PERTAINING TO THE EQUIPMENT

The data from transformers, circuit breakers, switch gears, cables, and substations are equipment data that result from management and monitoring. All the data about pressure of oil, ground current, data release, nominal data, producer data, size data, assembly, chilling system, transformer model, fault recorder, report, source of fault, dielectric loss of transformer, index of polarization, resistance, insulation information, humidity content, load, short circuit current, and tap changed information are data that belong to the transformer [31]. Discharge, humidity content, and information about discharge conclude data about switchgear. A sag in voltage, signal waveform, and inclination of tower come under transmission line data. Temperature, discharge, and circulation current come under power cable data. All the information such as ID, type, setting, name, and status of pollution are a part of substantial data. Constraints data include information regarding current, minor voltage, capacity, model of the connection, manufacturer, cooling system, data about the release, operational data, recorder, source and action of faults, name of a substation, tripping details, SF_6 examination report, insulation resistance, mechanisms of control, production data of the used equipment, and quantity of circuit breakers.

Yan et al. [32] explored a novel idea for power equipment monitoring and found that RMT is suitable for modeling the massive datasets in this situation. Wu et al. [33] focused on the analysis and utilization of assembly big data in the manufacturing process and studied the key technologies of big data analysis and assembly scheduling optimization for complex equipment. Lai et al. [34], to recognize industrial equipment accurately in manufacturing systems, adopted the long short-term memory to analyze big data features and build a nonintrusive load monitoring system.

The major source of big data is represented by the equipment monitoring data. The data that energy management systems include certain toward voltage of the equipment, electricity, power, frequency, etc. Oil status or lubricant, ventilation, and transformer healthy operation can be represented by the equipment real-time data monitoring. Total harmonic distortion and flicker index are power quality calculations that are performed with voltage and current waveforms [35]. For monitoring renewable energy, remote telemetry units are used [36,37]. Digital protection data, fault description, previous disturbances, old faults, locations, trip numbers, and dips in the voltage records can all be sorted as a part of equipment diagnostic data.
