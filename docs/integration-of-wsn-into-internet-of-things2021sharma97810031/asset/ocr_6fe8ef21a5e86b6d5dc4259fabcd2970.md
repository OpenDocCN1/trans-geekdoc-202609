# 15.2.7 LAYER-INDEPENDENT CHALLENGES

There are a few challenges that circumvent all layer-dependent challenges and are crucial to almost every layer of the network. These include the following:

+   • Privacy: Privacy and authorization of data in WSNs and IoT are the cornerstones of the development of secure healthcare monitoring systems. The issue of privacy arises primarily owing to the two physical limitations: first, due to the limited memory and computational power available at the anode terminal of the networks which curtail the possibility of high-end algorithms and mechanisms to be used; and second, use of wireless data transmission in wireless sensor-based IoT network leaves loopholes and terminals being exposed to attacks from adversaries such as eavesdropping and tampering.
+   
+   In healthcare monitoring systems, privacy is particularly important. This becomes evident in a study where it addresses public mistrust against the use of sensor networks for healthcare-related data which would lead to the underutilization of these resources. Similarly, the concerns regarding the accessibility of health information of the user are also being legitimately raised. Another dichotomous topic that remains to be studied further regarding privacy and flexibility remains as to what degree of the public health information must be made available in the public domain in cases of health emergencies and what might be its implications over a person's social well-being, and what might be the necessary privacy safeguards.
+   
+   These concerns have been legitimized since even the existing privacy mechanisms that ensure encryption of data have been under fingerprint and timing-based snooping (FATS) attack. In such an attack, the attackers can monitor a user's ADL with only two pieces of information, i.e., the fingerprint of each radio message and the time stamp [10]. A few nations are more precautious than others and have enacted legal acts to protect the privacy of user's encrypted data such as US Federal law HIPAA 1996, while others recommend that public awareness surrounding privacy and its implications need to be emphasized further.

+   • Security: The security of the system has been interpreted in various ways. The US Department of Commerce defines security as a condition that results from the establishment and maintenance of protective measures that ensure a state of inviolability from hostile acts or influences. Security of the device and thereby of an individual can be under threat by exposing mere location using tracking capabilities of these networks [11].
