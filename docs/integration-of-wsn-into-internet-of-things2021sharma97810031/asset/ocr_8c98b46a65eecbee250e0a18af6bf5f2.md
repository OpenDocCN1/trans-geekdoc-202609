Therefore, before learning any further details about it, we must learn its history.

The first accepted work on AI was done by Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts in 1943, which drew inspiration from the human brain. They used the concept of Turing’s theory of computation which was given by Alan Turing in 1936 [10]. According to this theory, if an algorithm to a problem exists, it is guaranteed that a Turing machine will be able to solve it. Now, the question arises: What is a Turing machine? It is an abstract machine based on a mathematical model that captures the idea of computation. Despite being designed during the 1930s, today, after almost a century, it remains the central idea around which the concept of AI revolves. AI has led to the emergence of an immensely important subfield, machine learning (a term coined by Arthur Samuel in 1959), which was a pioneering step toward machine intelligence.

Now, let’s fast-forward to 2020. These days, most people across the globe own smartphones. We’ve all used the Google Assistant or Siri, depending upon whether we have an Android phone or iPhone, to know about the weather, news, and recipes and get answers to the questions we have. Google Assistant and Siri are both AI-powered virtual smart assistants that are capable of doing an incredibly large number of tasks digitally, ranging from answering the user’s queries to setting an alarm on the user’s commands, or from identifying different things using Google Lens to monetary transactions. Google Duplex, which is an extension of Google Assistant, enables us to book appointments, cabs, tickets, etc. To understand this, let’s say we are organizing a party next week and we need to book a celebration hall. Instead of wasting time going from place to place in search of a place that suits all our requirements, we can simply sit back at home and ask Google Duplex to do it for us [11]. Google Duplex shows us the list of all the banquet halls with their details, such as distance from your home, how many people it can accommodate, facilities provided by them, and even honest reviews from the people who have already been there. This helps us streamline our search and ultimately make the final decision with ease. Once we have decided on which hall we want to book, Google Assistant will ask us what time and date we want to book it, and the number of people that will be attending it. Then, it will take the necessary steps to reserve the hall. In case the hall is unavailable at that particular hour, it will show us the time and date of its availability. The user can then either look for an alternate banquet hall or change the date of their party. The assistant can also carry out the monetary exchange.
