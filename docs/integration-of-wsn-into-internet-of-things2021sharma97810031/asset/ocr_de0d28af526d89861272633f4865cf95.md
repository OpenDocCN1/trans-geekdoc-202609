# FIGURE 9.28 Simulation for identification of electrical charges.

The P and T signals are used to create and train the network, then the S signal is used to simulate the network. The resulting signal Y corresponds to the signal that the neural network returns as a result of the simulation. In other words, the S signal is the signal submitted to the network, and the Y signal is the signal returned by the network.

After completing the first program, the second program is executed to extract the coefficients of the tested signal, being an approximation coefficient and three detail coefficients. Thus, each signal can be represented by its coefficients of the wavelet transform.
