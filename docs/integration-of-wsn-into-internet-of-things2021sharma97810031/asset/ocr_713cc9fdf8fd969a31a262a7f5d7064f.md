# 15.5 CONCLUSION

With the advancements in information and communications technology (ICT) day in and day out, there is a growing demand for technology and systems that would make human life easier and more comfortable. This is not a new trend, yet the field of applications to which humans have applied WSNs and IoT has been unprecedented.
