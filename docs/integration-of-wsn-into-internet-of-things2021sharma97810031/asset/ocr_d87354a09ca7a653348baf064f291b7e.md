# ## 14.9 UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING IN MENTAL HEALTHCARE: CHALLENGES AND PROBLEMS

The invention of computer technology was a landmark incident in the history of discoveries. Man has invented numerous technologies, and all these technologies have contributed a lot to the development of mankind and its environment. During the past half century, technologies existing on earth have developed at a very fast pace and greatly contributed to the development of other technologies. But, if we talk about the pace of development of computer technology, it is the fastest-developing technology on earth, and not only this, computer technology has extensive intervention and access among other technologies, and it has completely revolutionized the development and advancement of other technologies. Computer technology is developing and emerging in its different formats, starting from supercomputing to ubiquitous computing. Ubiquitous computing is one of the forms of computing technology in which very small computing devices fitted with apps or software are developed in different shapes and sizes to insert them in a computing or noncomputing machine [27]. Ubiquitous computing has a profound effect on the way people interact and use computers and other machines. Ubiquitous computing envisions and develops a new world where embedded or inbuilt microprocessors, processors, computers, sensors, and digital units are abundantly available in humans, machines, and the environment to make them highly intelligent and automatic in their working. Ubiquitous computing helps its users to develop an environment that merges the portable, small-sized, and high-speed computing devices with other technologies as their embedded and integrated habitat. These embedded ubiquitous habitat devices work with a new style of functionality to provide specially demanded services for better and effective working. Insertion of ubiquitous devices in all the existing machines, equipment, gazettes, and tools is not a simple task; it brings forth problems and challenges [28]. The application of ubiquitous computing in healthcare, especially for the treatment and management of persons with psychological disorders, is a great challenge. Problems and challenges faced by the technologists in the development and application of ubiquitous computing in mental healthcare are as follows [29].

+   1. Discipline of mental health is very vast and comprehensive, so its mapping for the application of ubiquitous computing is a challenge.
+   2. Present potentials of ubiquitous computing are limited, so its application in desired devices with limitations is a challenge.
+   3. Development of ubiquitous devices of different sizes, shapes, speeds, and other technical features required for different application areas is difficult.
+   4. Application of ubiquitous computing in mental healthcare faces technical, social, economic, and legal challenges.
