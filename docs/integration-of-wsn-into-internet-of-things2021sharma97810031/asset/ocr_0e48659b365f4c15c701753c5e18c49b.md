The study has been conducted by using a qualitative method and a descriptive research design and by involving a secondary method of data collection. For a better generalization of findings, there is a need to conduct an empirical study based on the quantitative technique. The findings of the study will give a new and better insight to the researchers and professionals for comprehensive and better use of ubiquitous computing for the psychological well-being of persons with psychological disorders. There were relatively few studies available relating to the factors involved in the present research; this study has created new knowledge for effective management, evaluation, treatment, and rehabilitation of persons with psychological disorders.
