## 11.5 IoT CONCEPTUAL PROCESS FLOW

This section defines how IoT actually works. To know this, we need to understand the exact process flow, and every system has some hierarchy in order to follow the instructions and communication. IoT is the extension of traditional Internet services; therefore, IoT involves some additional processes for bringing a smart communication platform for multi-vendor devices and infrastructure-based application services so that the users can carry Internet services at a more feasible and comfortable state. Now, smart home, medical, chemical, and database services are easy to maintain, but it is always a crucial task for IoT to provide all the smart application services with security consideration, because there are many attackers in the network who are targeting the networking services; particularly, commercial banking services are facing real challenges for maintaining security. Because IoT is the mixture of different networking technologies, it is always difficult to protect applications and data from viruses and unauthorized users. IoT emerging various technical concerns with cloud computing, fog computing and edge computing infrastructures to provide more scalability, flexibility, and availability to IoT users.

The IoT process flow represents the role of sensors, actuators, preprocessors, and multi-vendor application service providers in some direction, with all the phases playing their role behind IoT and providing an abstract solution to their users by providing smart home and other day-to-day services in a user-friendly manner. IoT collectively gathers all the processes in a manner to eliminate background complexity to its users.
