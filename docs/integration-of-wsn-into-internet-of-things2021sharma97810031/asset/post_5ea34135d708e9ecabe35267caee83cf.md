## 8.6.3 CONTENT DELIVERY AND CACHE

The need for BDA to advance edge caching capability, which is considered as a promising approach to improve network efficiency and alleviate the high demand for the radio resource in future networks, is a present research field with a lot of opportunities.

- i. Delivery of content with allocated bandwidth

The QoS nowadays in a market as competitive as the mobile network industry is one service where no one wants to lag behind, concluding from the daily increase in various facilities like internet-based applications. Thus, it is of utmost importance that there is a correct allocation of bandwidth that ensures unhindered connections. Internet connectivity can be easily provided to the users, but video sharing on the network is still very expensive and of very high load. One such solution to the problem faced by BSs regarding the same was suggested by operating with the LTE system as the multicast techniques, but due to the complexity of this process, it was quickly discarded. Fan et al. [96] proposed a framework of a cluster of users who want to download the content and can do so by sharing the wireless channel of the BS. This idea allowed room for improvement by decreasing the load on BSs and also releasing their resources for better use. It also provides a better data rate for users who are in the connection cluster while at the same time delivers a great chance for users who are not joined in the cluster to enjoy the benefit of those assets.

- ii. Caching and tracking

The social media community has a massive user base, and similarly, the content shared among the community is also huge. Thus, when an interesting event occurs, it garners a vast majority of attention and a lot of information sharing. Viral data travel through a lot of servers in the network and therefore can easily end up exhausting the system. Such a problem can be handled with the help of monitoring the popular social media sites and recognizing the growth of the popular trends and content, performing different analyses on it and recognizing by which age-group is the content shared the most, which thus cache the data for the various BSs. The various models identified can provide great results by using big data on the required analysis, the result of which is the desired cache content [97].

- iii. 5G networks and caching

Recognizing and guessing the most viral content and storing it in cache can help the subscribers of the said cache system mobile network; the bandwidth saved here can help the network utilize it in other ways. Basteg et al. [97] predicted, by using machine learning and the ability of big data, an active method of caching by studying the approval rating distribution of the content in 5G networks. This tactic has been depicted to have a better user experience [98]. The data collected in raw form are analyzed with the help of various platforms, and the useful data are extracted from it for various purposes. The data are then analyzed for predicting the popularity surge of the content.

## 8.6.4 Optimization of Network

BDA is one of the state-of-the-art tools to optimize networks and transform them from merely being a blind tube that conveys data into a cognitive, conscious, and self-optimizing entity that can intelligently adapt according to the needs of its users.

- i. QoS in cellular networks

The property of handover and its success rate are very crucial components of cellular networks primarily because of the dependency of mobility and call continuity, particularly if the user is moving from one place to another. Shortcomings in such a service will put the operator in a very tricky situation because then the QoS is directly affected. Two methods were presented to handle these problems: self-configuration, related to the new cells, and self-optimization, pertaining to already-existing cells, handover process.

A neighbor cell list (NCL) is required when a BS is installed to arrange the new cells. First, the type of antenna, the locations of the cells (candidate), and the angle for directional cells are taken into account. Then, the cells that show the least amount of distance and most capacities for a load of traffic are selected and promoted as self-configuration. Self-optimization for current cells: with the help of a network management system, the data collected are processed with a key performance indicator (KPI) statistical measurement to check whether the handover was effective or not. Through the data collected, the cells with a value less than a preset amount are excluded from the NCL list, and the cells that have a value higher than the preset are considered for that slot.

- ii. Optimization of resource allocation

KPIs are elemental to gaining an overall better performance experience. Kiran et al. [99] proposed utilization of the user data (alarms, entries, and updates in the database) and network data. The main aim of this study is to provide feasible alternatives to the problems that arise when allocation of the radio resources to radio access network users is done. This is mainly carried out using BDA through recognizing the network and user behavior. First, the processing of data is done, which is obtained from the core and other cellular network sides. By comparing the levels of cellular KPIs to the preset value, binary values are developed on a regular interval. Then, the same process on a subscriber level is carried out. Finally, a pattern that is binary in nature is generated on a request. This creates a matrix that is updated and incorporated with various data according to their updating. This is done by then passing the data through PRB (pseudo random binary), which uses fuzzy binary pattern matching algorithm.

- iii. Using consumption prediction for network flexibility

Service category and the geographic locations of customers are two main factors for the analysis of consumption. The trends of consumption are usually termed as long term, seasonal, and short term. The association of the data (usage of services) generally termed as data of users with other different data (daily news, events, etc.) can help reach a more accurate estimate. With big data, they can get precise locations for planting nodes without affecting subscriber services.
