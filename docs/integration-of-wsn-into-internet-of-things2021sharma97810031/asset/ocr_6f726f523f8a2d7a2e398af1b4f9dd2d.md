# ### 7.1 INTRODUCTION

Wireless sensor networks (WSNs) along with artificial intelligence (AI) now allow
the farmers to analyze weather conditions, soil conditions, and water usage in the
field of smart agriculture and automate so many physical tasks and decision-making
activities. A WSN refers to a system that is formed of wired or wireless communica-
tions, sensors, and actuators capable of acquiring and transferring data to the Internet
