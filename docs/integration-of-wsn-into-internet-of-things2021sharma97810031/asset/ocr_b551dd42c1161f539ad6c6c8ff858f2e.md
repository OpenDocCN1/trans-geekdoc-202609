Another way to elucidate this technology is context-sensitive computing, which makes it possible to capture the situation through electronic devices, such as the movement of a person in space, which can be easily detected [4].

Its main idea refers to the use of mobile devices, their services, and resources, which are able to discover and connect to other mobile devices in the environment. Since the system for managing ubiquitous computing can be small, it is necessary that there is only a wireless network connection to a large-capacity computer. Through ubiquitous computing, houses can be controlled using ubiquitous technology, for example, turning on or off televisions and electronic equipment, activating lights, and monitoring bedridden people, through health care, and controlling the items in the pantry by the expiration date, among other uses [4,5].

The use of computers and the use of the Internet have, in recent decades, become an integral part of people’s way of life, and with this constant evolution, computer technologies, telecommunications, and different forms of connectivity have emerged. However, society is more dependent on the computational devices that emerge to generate facilities for day-to-day tasks, whether personal or organizational, but at the same time, through technology and ubiquitous computing, the improvement of Distributed Information Systems (SID) is reached [6].

With this advance, the creation of ubiquitous computing was induced, since several devices cooperate with each other; for example, the devices, objects, and computers share information of people, through the intelligent mobility of networks, and provide the user with instant access to new services in a transparent manner. Currently, communication technologies are able to provide services almost anywhere over the Internet; for example, smart TVs controlled by gestures and voice commands, smart-phones, and notebooks are already interacting with each other. Enabling the creation of smart homes, smart establishments, and intelligent vehicles, interacting with each other in such a subtle way almost imperceptible to users [6,7].

Thus, ubiquitous computing is considered a form of technology where mobile users, services, and resources encounter other users, services, and resources, which benefit from the advances in mobile computing and pervasive computing. The technology of ubiquitous computing, when applied, uses and manages the advantages of the concepts of mobile and pervasive computing; therefore, the responsibilities for the execution of the services used by the user are distributed among several devices, each one with its specific characteristics and functions [8].
