# ## 14.7 AUTHENTICATION AND EVALUATION OF APPS

As already discussed, the ubiquitous computing technology used to develop devices and apps is not authenticated because there is no regulatory body, guidelines, or rules for the authentication of these apps. There is an urgent need for the authentication of these systems, and each mental health app should go through a serious trial for its effectiveness. International standards should be developed for evaluating the effectiveness of these apps. There is a serious problem in the authentication and evaluation of these apps because of very quick changes and advancements in technology used for the development of these supports [26]. Before prescribing or using an app, the following facts should be kept in mind:

+   1. Always consult a well-qualified and trained professional for the prescription of such apps.
+   2. Check the validity and reliability of the app for what it is developed.
+   3. Understand the use of the app properly and seek all the information and guidelines necessary for its effective use.
+   4. Be in regular touch with your professional expert while using the app.

# ## 14.8 FUTURE PROSPECTS OF UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING IN MENTAL HEALTHCARE

Artificial intelligence is slowly penetrating all the minute possible activities of man, related to any walk of life or discipline of study. ICT in the form of ubiquitous computing is taking over all the fields for its development. ICT has already contributed a lot to the field of mental health, but still, there is a lot to be done. Some of the futuristic expectations from ubiquitous computing are the following:

+   - Effective intervention by ubiquitous computing for the identification, assessment, and understanding of psychological disorders.
+   - Effective use of ubiquitous computing for the treatment, management, and rehabilitation of a wider range of psychological disorders.
+   - Development of new therapeutic intervention blended with ubiquitous computing for the treatment of a majority of psychological disorders.
+   - Development of easily usable and portable ubiquitous devices as a support system for the mental health problem treatment.
+   - Effective use of virtual reality technology in place of face-to-face contact or remote counseling.
+   - Extensive use of ubiquitous computing in data collection, information development, communication, data storage, record keeping, and information sharing.
+   - Accomplishment of day-to-day activities by using ubiquitous technology.
