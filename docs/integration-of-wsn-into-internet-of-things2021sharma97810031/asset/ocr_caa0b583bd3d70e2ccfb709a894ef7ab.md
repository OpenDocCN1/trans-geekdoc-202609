# 13.5.7 BLOCKCHAIN

Blockchain is a distributed ledger technology that is decentralized and eliminates the need for a third party as their supervisor [23]. The main characteristics of blockchain are as follows:

+   • Distributed: It is a distributed technology that allows data/transactions to be distributed among all entities in the chain, instead of being copied or transferred.
+   • Decentralized: It means there is no need for a third party to manage the data and transactions because every entity in the chain has the exact copy of the ledger.
