The major contribution and finding of the work are on the overview of ubiquitous computing, which involves a diversity of concepts and technologies, although it is still a computational paradigm, as the technology is highly complex for the development of devices and applications with respect to the integration between mobility with systems and distributed and intelligent presence for the benefit of users. The need to develop new means of integration between users and computers, combining mobility with the technological features of pervasive computing, give a computing device on the move the ability to automatically configure its services more accurately and with minimal errors, for example. Ubiquitous computing in full relates that a person in the context of society does not actually need a computer (in the traditional sense of CPU, keyboard, mouse, or monitor) to perform tasks. But it will be there, inside TVs (or just screens), refrigerator, soda machines, water meters, or electricity, or in a lamp on the corner. In this sense, this technology can be considered as omni-present computing.
