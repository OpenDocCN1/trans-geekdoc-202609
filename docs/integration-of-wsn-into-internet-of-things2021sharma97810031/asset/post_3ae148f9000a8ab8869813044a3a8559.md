## 12.5.3 Infrastructure Problem

When a software programmer or a vendor presents their software, they do not have any idea about the weakness of their launched product, which allows the attackers to gather information about the infrastructure and then hack into it by the software. Such types of attacks are alarming and are called zero-day attacks, which is quite complex to preestablish with the conventional security techniques. Thus, a strong software infrastructure is required for the development of a proper security in the IoT system. In all the stages of the IoT system, protection must be inserted, starting from the hardware till the software, which will ensure vulnerability-free environment in the overall system.

## 12.5.4 Coexistence of Protocols and Wireless Technologies in IIoT

Nowadays, IIoT is attracting attention from both industry and academia. Therefore, for the information exchange to take place, it is necessary to have communication in IIoT so as to connect to a huge number of the heterogeneous devices, offer low-latency deterministic behavior, and provide adequate bandwidth for data transfer. Also, some of the industrial applications have some industrial requirements, availability, reliability, and restriction in timing. IIoT uses communication technologies, standards, and protocols. Usually wireless devices and systems are acquiring a lot of attention in contrast to the wired connections in past. Many challenges are being imposed by the wireless technology. Various protocols and wireless system’s coexistence are also a challenge to IIoT. Since there are different types of communication technologies, it is difficult to choose a communication protocol and technology which is most suitable to the given application. One communication protocol or a technology cannot provide all of the strength and features that can fit the requirements of different applications perfectly in IIoT. Thus, it is a huge challenge to select a communication protocol and technology.

## 12.5.5 Collaboration among Heterogeneous IIoT Systems

IoT can be characterized by the heterogeneity of devices that are part of a system, which are anticipated to present various capabilities from the communication and computational standpoints. Management of such high-level heterogeneity must be supported at protocol and architectural levels. IIoT system suite is a collaboration of various multivendor and heterogeneous technologies like robotics, industrial management, actuators, edge nodes, gateways, sensors, cloud/edge data sensors, cellular networks, and different wireless/wired communication, collaboration, and integration. Among these, multivendor and heterogeneous technologies of IIoT system are quite challenging. Various elements like sharing of resource, data privacy, synchronization, interoperability, and sharing of data make the collaboration and integration more complicated. For efficient and flexible methods for interoperability and collaboration, research and development are required.

## 12.5.6 TRUST IN IIOT SYSTEMS

User adaptation and acceptance are basically related to a technology's success and are greatly persuaded by consumer trust on the technologies. The fortunate distribution of systems based on IIoT with the help of commercial users is affected by the trust in IIoT systems. The IIoT systems are still at an early stage, and in the recent literature, the privacy and security of the system are highlighted as a major challenge. Privacy and security are linked with the trust of the user. Thus, weak privacy and security can affect the trust of users and discourage them from adopting the IIoT system. Therefore, customers' trust should be gained by the effective customer trust models for adaptation and deployment in the industry for the IIoT systems. Hence, for the successful acceptance of the IIoT systems, more research is required in the field of customer trust model.

## 12.5.7 IIOT PUBLIC SAFETY

Public safety is highly prioritized in case of a catastrophe and emergency situations in IIoT. In case of a catastrophe, the safety of industrial equipment and workers relies on the timely alert generation, event detection, notification of alerts, and site localization to the providers of emergency response service like fire department, units of disaster management, traffic police, ambulance, and the other agencies of law enforcement. However, the malfunctioning or absence of communication infrastructure can be a major issue in disaster areas. Also, communication and collaboration among different IIoT devices and the other systems of communication are a major issue. A few recent studies showed network architecture that is based on communication of public safety for future smart cities and IIoT by using edge computing, software defined networks (SDN), unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), and other technologies of advanced communication, for example, 4G/5G, long term evolution (LTE), etc. However, some efforts in research are required for designing the disaster-resilient, autonomous architectures that should allow efficient interaction in any normal situations. Also, such architectures are used to ensure the mechanism of disaster recovery in emergency circumstances for public safety.

## 12.5.8 REAL-TIME UPDATE ISSUE

Devices of IoT are rising quickly; hence, updating the software of IoT device and updating the firmware need to be properly analyzed. It is quite hard to apply updates and keep track of all the devices of IoT, since all devices are not that supportive of the air update. Also, it is required to apply manual update like real-time data consumption that is unmanageable for its users sometimes. Thus, the term “course of lifelong learning” has been introduced to guide the machines to persistently search for updates and to makes their firewall strong for the updated threats. Because of the dynamic nature of the IoT systems, each day a new electronic device and application is connected with the network, which results in new unspecified attacks. Thus, it is a difficult task for IoT security to adopt a real-time and intelligent ML algorithm for detecting the unknown attacks.

## 12.6 CONCLUSION

IIoT’s future is virtually a bit vast because of the development in the technology. IIoT system helps the industry to gather and analyze the data in the network by using IoT’s smart services. This also further improves the overall performance of the system. Although IIoT system makes life easier, smooth, and comfortable, it is somewhat lacking in the security aspect. The adequate security is not provided, which is quite essential for the development of IIoT. Therefore, for the better advancement in the security, a promising research must be addressed in the near future. Also, big analytics and ML help to overcome security issues that are faced by the IIoT systems. The chapter explores various types of attacks that cause hindrance to security in IoT and IIoT systems. Further, in order to overcome these security issues, the chapter comprehensively reviews the most prominent ML techniques that are applied to identify the smart threats and enable the development of a secure IoT infrastructure. Finally, the chapter highlights the most prominent challenges faced in IoT and IIoT scenarios.
