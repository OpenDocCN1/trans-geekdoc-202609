
## 14.7 AUTHENTICATION AND EVALUATION OF APPS

As already discussed, the ubiquitous computing technology used to develop devices and apps is not authenticated because there is no regulatory body, guidelines, or rules for the authentication of these apps. There is an urgent need for the authentication of these systems, and each mental health app should go through a serious trial for its effectiveness. International standards should be developed for evaluating the effectiveness of these apps. There is a serious problem in the authentication and evaluation of these apps because of very quick changes and advancements in technology used for the development of these supports [26]. Before prescribing or using an app, the following facts should be kept in mind:

- 1. Always consult a well-qualified and trained professional for the prescription of such apps.
- 2. Check the validity and reliability of the app for what it is developed.
- 3. Understand the use of the app properly and seek all the information and guidelines necessary for its effective use.
- 4. Be in regular touch with your professional expert while using the app.

## 14.9 UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING IN MENTAL HEALTHCARE: CHALLENGES AND PROBLEMS

The invention of computer technology was a landmark incident in the history of discoveries. Man has invented numerous technologies, and all these technologies have contributed a lot to the development of mankind and its environment. During the past half century, technologies existing on earth have developed at a very fast pace and greatly contributed to the development of other technologies. But, if we talk about the pace of development of computer technology, it is the fastest-developing technology on earth, and not only this, computer technology has extensive intervention and access among other technologies, and it has completely revolutionized the development and advancement of other technologies. Computer technology is developing and emerging in its different formats, starting from supercomputing to ubiquitous computing. Ubiquitous computing is one of the forms of computing technology in which very small computing devices fitted with apps or software are developed in different shapes and sizes to insert them in a computing or noncomputing machine [27]. Ubiquitous computing has a profound effect on the way people interact and use computers and other machines. Ubiquitous computing envisions and develops a new world where embedded or inbuilt microprocessors, processors, computers, sensors, and digital units are abundantly available in humans, machines, and the environment to make them highly intelligent and automatic in their working. Ubiquitous computing helps its users to develop an environment that merges the portable, small-sized, and high-speed computing devices with other technologies as their embedded and integrated habitat. These embedded ubiquitous habitat devices work with a new style of functionality to provide specially demanded services for better and effective working. Insertion of ubiquitous devices in all the existing machines, equipment, gadgets, and tools is not a simple task; it brings forth problems and challenges [28]. The application of ubiquitous computing in healthcare, especially for the treatment and management of persons with psychological disorders, is a great challenge. Problems and challenges faced by the technologists in the development and application of ubiquitous computing in mental healthcare are as follows [29].

- 1. Discipline of mental health is very vast and comprehensive, so its mapping for the application of ubiquitous computing is a challenge.
- 2. Present potentials of ubiquitous computing are limited, so its application in desired devices with limitations is a challenge.
- 3. Development of ubiquitous devices of different sizes, shapes, speeds, and other technical features required for different application areas is difficult.
- 4. Application of ubiquitous computing in mental healthcare faces technical, social, economic, and legal challenges.
- 5. Quick and frequent technical innovations and advancements in the field of ubiquitous computing give birth to the problem of validation because validation of ubiquitous devices in the field of mental healthcare is a lengthy process. Ubiquitous computing technology under validation may become obsolete before its validation because of the arrival of its next-generation technology.
- 6. Integration of microcomputers into physical objects and machines and their use for mental healthcare is a challenge.
- 7. Characteristics of ubiquitous devices such as embedding in the parent systems, small size, invisibility to the user, omnipresence, mobility, and portability are reasons for the challenge.
- 8. Advancement and innovations in the field of microelectronics and communications are a technical challenge for the development of ubiquitous computing in the field of mental healthcare.
- 9. Ubiquitous computing is a fast-developing technology, so its integration with the mental healthcare world is a difficult task.
- 10. Ubiquitous computing works on the technology of information and communication, whereas it is not possible to implement it as it is in mental healthcare.
- 11. Ubiquitous computing drastically changes the use of computers, so its use and operation becomes a challenge and needs specialized training.
- 12. Working of ubiquitous computing is based on different automatically run processes that are dependent on the user's instructions.
- 13. Security of ubiquitous devices and user's confidentiality is an issue.
- 14. Use of ubiquitous devices is suspected and doubtful because the user does not know whether it is working on his/her behalf or it also allows the intervention of outsiders. This is a kind of breach of the privacy of the user.
- 15. The huge volume of data generated by the ubiquitous devices is a problem.
- 16. The right of privacy of the user, that is, his/her data are not shared and utilized by others without their consent.
- 17. Environmental concerns in the use of ubiquitous computing are a serious problem.
- 18. Technology dependency of users and experts is a serious challenge.
- 19. Issues related to commerce and trades are also a challenge.
- 20. Development of ubiquitous computing and its impact is a problem.
