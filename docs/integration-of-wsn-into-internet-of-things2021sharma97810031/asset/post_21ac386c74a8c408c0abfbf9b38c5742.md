### 8.5.1 PLANNING AND DESIGNING THE VARIOUS APPLICATIONS

Whether improving urban energy use or conducting microgrid planning, both can be realized by big data via supporting the evolution toward renewable sources of production [76]. To achieve optimal results, appropriate planning that includes sales and operational data is required. The energy market generally tends to combine different sources of energies (wind and biomass). Big data allows policymakers to have selectivity while dealing with various energy sources. Huang et al. [77] depicted a design for an efficient power plant in which big data can be used. Their approach suggests that the collection of data such as roof material, inverter specifications, and maximum power point tracking units can help build a rooftop power plant. This system makes use of a self-adaptive harmony search. Big data and cloud computing can be used to manage the monitoring of market demands, data collection, etc. Circumstances such as hurricanes and storms can also be tackled with the help of big data applications in microgrids.

### 8.5.2 POWER GENERATION PREDICTION

By learning from the shortcomings between stock and capacity, a decision to use the applications of cloud computing with planning in microgrid big data was proposed [78]. Based on the data (consumer profiles, generation data, load data, and storage of historic weather) collected, power generation can be predicted. For data retrieval, HDFS is also used to perform processing on data. MapReduce is used for predicting and further processing. Grolinger et al. [79] proposed a pattern on energy consumption prediction that was deeply based on native knowledge with support vector regression of the big data. Daki et al. [80] also proposed a solution for matching the creation to the consumption in a school of engineering in Morocco, which is based on big data and applies to energy forecasting systems. Sporadic sources such as wind turbines can be of importance to improve the output of power of forecasting, and this can be done by big data. In Hebi, China, prediction of the yield of power of wind energy in microgrids, done for only a short time, used game-theoretical analysis of big data. Solar photovoltaic power forecasting system predicts photovoltaic energy accurately, which is built on big data and also does real-time processing of data in neural networks. This also estimates output power like the optimal operation of energies akin to solar and wind (renewable energy). Proper strengths can be added to the preparation of the power grid, which will make it hours even days ahead with weather forecasting, which would thus improve the scheduling of all the sources present in the grid.
