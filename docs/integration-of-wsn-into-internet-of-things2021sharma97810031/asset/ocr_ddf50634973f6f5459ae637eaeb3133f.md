# ## 2.3 WIRELESS SENSOR NETWORK SECURITY

Their low cost is one of the reasons sensors have become popular. For this reason, extra hardware resources and advanced protocols cannot be used in sensor nodes. This is a crucial factor in ensuring security. As mentioned in Section 2.2.1, low cost prevents installing new state-of-the-art hardware. For this reason, the sensors are not threat proof. These nodes and the networks are prone to data interception. Potential harmful instructions can be injected into the node for malicious activities. Sensor devices are not tamperproof for jamming attacks.

Moreover, advanced protocols cannot be installed due to their small size, low cost, and energy efficiency. Low power will cause potential in energy drains, and nodes will starve. The WSN node is highly resource constrained. It comprises a processor size of 4–8MHz, 4 KB of RAM, and 28 KB of memory. Besides, the 916 radiofrequency of a sensor node will cause potential limitation in the transmission capability. Though low power is one of the essential characteristics behind the adoption of WSNs, it is a significant vulnerability. Considering the above-mentioned limitations, various routing protocols that are optimized for energy efficiency for sensor nodes have been proposed. In this section, the WSN security requirements, data security of WSNs, multiple threats, and countermeasures are discussed.

# ### 2.3.1 Security Requirements for WSNs

A brief overview of the security requirements of the WSNs is given as follows [50]:

+   - Confidentiality: In the case of network security, confidentiality means that the information is “for your eyes only,” that is, the data have to be protected for the intended user. In WSNs, the confidentiality of the system means the data captured and processed by the node should not be leaked to any unauthorized nodes or stations. For this reason, data can be encrypted with secure encryption and a private key. Besides, secure routing protocols can be used for this.
+   - Integrity: Integrity is to ensure that the data do not get corrupted by any means over their lifetime. If the data get altered through their transit, get deleted accidentally by someone, or get modified by any unauthorized entity, then they become flawed, which is called breaching of integrity. Ensuring that information remains authentic, trustworthy, and consistent is integrity. In WSNs, whenever the data are sent to the BS or the user, they need to be unaltered; that is, integrity has to be protected.
+   - Authenticity: One of the essential properties of data for users is that they need to be authentic. Whenever two parties communicate, it is imperative that they have the trust of each other. For traditional data authenticity, message authentication code is used. In the case of WSNs, it is vital that the data that will be processed at the end need to be authentic.
