## 8.6 Cellular Network Design in Big Data Analysis

In recent studies on BDA for cellular network design, we find that the topic of the WSN field has received much critical attention. Mobile cellular networks have become both the generators and carriers of massive data. BDA can improve the performance of mobile cellular networks and maximize the revenue of operators.

### 8.6.1 Detection, Prediction of Failure, and Recouping

Mobile networks combine different communication technologies sharing the same infrastructure. Although each technology accomplishes different networking requirements, it also hinders the network operation. There are various challenges faced with failed handovers, optimization of networks, and detection of anomalies that occur in cellular networks.

- i. Data analysis for failed handover

  By conducting drive tests or network simulations, the analysis proposed that the most common problem experienced nowadays with call droppings can be appropriately tackled with various different methods, but the approach using big data gives more reasonable and efficient results. The BS application part is used to analyze the messages exchanged between mobile switching center and BS subsystem, further applying analysis with Hadoop. Locations where the disconnections (3G) occur are identified along with regular updates of locations where handover of intertechnology happens, and this is largely achieved by the use of the provided ID of the cell [91].

- ii. Network optimization of data-based long-term evolution (LTE)

  Combining all-around signaling, user environment information from wireless sources, and self-organized network technologies, optimization of the network at a full-scale level could be achieved. A platform based on signaling data was developed, which had the capability of optimizing the cellular network in the following three stages: first, by the mining of external data representation (XDR) keywords, which defines the indicators of network performance. The key information of the signaling is contained in XDR. The status of the complete signaling method can also be pinpointed by it. Second, by the discovery of the problem, the XDR-based performance indicator can be used to reflect network signaling plane status service establishment rate, drop rate, etc. Third, by proposing feasible solutions, optimization experience can be provided by pairing over identified and solved problems, which can thus verify a variety of other problems and therefore could be used to provide the required solution [45].

- iii. Anomaly detection

  The disruption that occurs in a cellular network is first felt by the user. Such a service can be identified by studying the call detail records (CDRs). These CDR files contain a wide array of information including the duration and ID and are generated when a call is placed. Karpepe et al. [92] proposed a CDR-based anomaly detection method that detects the anomalies. The CDR data are first collected and stored in the mediation department from the network nodes. Then, the collected CDR is distributed to its relevant department. Finally, the anomalies are found with the help of the Hadoop platform, and then these are pushed to the mediation department for appropriate processing.

- iv. Self-healing

  For troubleshooting, processing and maintenance databases can be used. However, the database volume is relatively large as it hosts data related to both ordinary and degraded intervals, thus making the data difficult to process. Therefore, a better-proposed approach that analyzes each BS separately by parallelizing this process independently works better. Furthermore, sleeping cells or outage cells can be detected by utilizing the neighboring BS measurements, hence measuring the consequences of this outage. The detection process depends on a very particular method involving resource output period, fault management, configuration management, and performance management [93].

- v. Failure prediction of cell site equipment

  By analyzing the trends of bandwidth in a single cell, equipment faults and infrastructure failures can be found. BDA becomes a major player in analyzing the large amount of data that are garnered here. The bandwidth received by the customer is collected over a period of time and then analyzed to understand the current streams.
