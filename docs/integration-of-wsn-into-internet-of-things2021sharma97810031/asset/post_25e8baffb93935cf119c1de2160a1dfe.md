## 14.5 UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING: REMEDIAL PROSPECT FOR MENTAL DISORDERS

Ubiquitous computing technology has opened new paradigms in support of data collection, record maintenance, information sharing, effective treatment, and management of mental health issues. Ubiquitous devices such as mobile phones, tablets, and other handheld equipment are assisting the community; persons with psychological disorders; medical professionals involved in the treatment and management of mental illnesses; and researchers to acquire, process, understand, and create information to further share it for monitoring, predicting, and improving the deteriorated psychological state of persons with mental health issues [22]. Ubiquitous computing devices connected with local/worldwide networking can be the simplest but very effective means for communicating the information to contact a crisis center of mental health provider professional on appropriate time to get desired help. Ubiquitous technology in the form of apps can be used in extremely sophisticated mobile phones, tablets, or palmtops [23]. These inbuilt apps of ubiquitous devices can be used as sensors to collect data and process it as useful information to identify, understand, and predict typical behavior patterns of persons with mental illness. If the inbuilt app of a ubiquitous device senses changes in the patterns of behavior of its user, it can record these changes, and simultaneously, these changes can be communicated to the professional expert before something untoward happens [24]. ICT, especially ubiquitous computing, is a very recent introduction. ICT and ubiquitous computing have started intervening in all the activities and processes of psychological disorder management. A number of software- and hardware-based supports have been developed to provide both virtual and physical aid for the treatment and management of mental health issues.

The newly introduced technology of ubiquitous computing in the field of mental health management has created a lot of excitement among technologists and mental health practitioners to exploit the huge range of opportunities of apps and device development for the treatment of psychological disorders. This excitement and desire for the exploitation of possibilities to develop apps for persons with psychological disorders and their support professionals have resulted in a type of outburst for app development. The market is already flooded with numerous ubiquitous apps and devices, and a lot many are waiting to enter or undergoing the process of development. But, ICT being a very recent entrant in the field of mental health illness, there are certain limitations as given below:

- Reliability and validity of newly developed apps and devices are not intact and appropriate.
- This technology is not authenticated, so there is a lot of ambiguity in its use.
- As this technology is to be used for the treatment and rehabilitation of human beings, it needs a regulatory body to properly evaluate it before use.
- No data are available for the use and effectiveness of this technology.

These limitations have not been considered as hurdles in the way of effective intervention by ubiquitous computing for the treatment and rehabilitation of persons with psychological disorders. A joint research work by the technologists and mental health professionals is trying to overcome these limitations and consequently pave the way for the following:

- 1. Ubiquitous computing-based assessment tools are being developed.
- 2. New ubiquitous technology-based devices for collecting, analyzing, and recording data and converting it into useful information for sharing and predicting the psychological states to monitor, treat, and manage the same.
- 3. ICT-based new psychological treatment strategies are under the process of development.
- 4. Ubiquitous computing-involved technologies for the identification of behavioral patterns, recording, sharing, monitoring, treatment, and rehabilitation of mental illness are under the process of development.
- 5. Traditional psychological treatments are being reformed and modified into a new set of ubiquitous computing-based treatment interventions.
- 6. Multidisciplinary treatment approach involving ICT as one of its major factors is emerging as a new strategy for treatment.
- 7. Ubiquitous computing-based devices are being developed to understand, describe, predict, and improve certain psychological processes such as memory, thinking, and learning.

## 14.6 CURRENT TRENDS OF UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING IN MENTAL HEALTH

Teams of information and computer technologists and mental health professionals are jointly working to develop software/hardware/apps to manage mental health issues; some of the very significant developments that can help in the treatment of mental health illnesses are as follows [25].

### 14.6.1 VIRTUAL REALITY DEVICES/SYSTEMS

Virtual is something that has a real existence but not a physical one. With the application of ICT, the use of software creates a similar effect which can generate realistic images, sounds, and sensations, but actually, it has nothing physical. Virtual reality-based headsets, devices, or systems fitted in a large-sized room can be created where a person using it is able to sense the images, hear the sounds, and feel the sensation of artificially created animated objects. With the help of virtual reality technology, such equipment is being developed, which is helpful in coping with depression, anxiety, and other disorders.

### 14.6.2 Mental Health Apps

Mental health monitoring and treatment apps are used by handheld devices such as mobile phones, tablets, or other portable and lightweight ubiquitous computing devices. These apps help to measure and monitor the patterns of mood, stress, anxiety, and depression in critical conditions. Because of their very small size and portability, their use is very easy and simple.

### 14.6.3 Illness and Self-Management Apps

Illness and self-management apps work by using their inbuilt instructions and information and give feedback on the basis of that to the user or experts for monitoring the behavior and providing treatment to the user. These apps are also helpful in tracking the location, position, state of working, breathing rates, blood pressure, pulse rate, etc., especially during the deteriorated state of mental health. Illness management apps are becoming user-friendly and helpful for the intervention and treatment of addiction, post-traumatic stress disorders, anxiety, insomnia, and depression.

### 14.6.4 Apps for Improving Psychological Processing and Training

By using ubiquitous computing technology, certain apps are being developed to improve an individual's psychological processing such as memory, thinking, learning, sensation, and higher cognitive processes. These apps are also helpful in the training of different psychological phenomena.

### 14.6.5 Data Collection and Symptom Recording Devices

Ubiquitous computing devices fitted with sensors have been developed to record the data relevant to a psychological disorder by sensing the body reactions generated by the disorder. Before the occurrence of a neurotic or psychotic disorder, such as mania, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and depression, changes in behavioral patterns start happening, and these changes can be sensed, recorded, and communicated to the mental health practitioners for remedial actions.
