# 9.7 CONCLUSIONS

As can be seen in this chapter, a database of domestic load current signals was created and a new methodology for the processing, identification, and disaggregation of loads was also proposed. The proposed methodology involves the extraction of coefficients by the wavelet transform and training based on neural networks. Due to the influence of one load on the waveform of the other, it was proposed to train the neural networks using a combination of individual loads activated automatically. The proposal is related to the concept of Smart Grid and allows fine monitoring of the use of equipment in homes, very important mainly due to drought problems and, therefore, low production of electricity.

The main contributions of this work are the profound bibliographic revision on techniques of load disaggregation, together with the creation of the database with the behavior of the main loads in homes, and consequently, the new algorithm proposed for the disaggregation of loads based on their measurement measures chain.

The database created and made available to the academic community represents an achievement in the area, since there is no such free and carefully obtained basis. It is hoped that this base will also be useful for other researchers so that they can go directly to the identification techniques. This database is composed of files in the “txt” format with samples of the currents of the measuring equipment.

The task of load disaggregation is complex and is aggravated in the presence of nonlinear loads, where the harmonics generated by one equipment modify the behavior of the signals measured in others. Specifically, one of the reasons for this is the lack of filters in the devices manufactured in Brazil, since the country’s legislation is not strict in this sense.

Therefore, the sum of the signals considering loads individually connected differs from the loads working simultaneously, that is, what could be used for the use of relays for the typical activation of home automation systems, but which will allow getting signals with the equipment connected individually and simultaneously. Thus, the analysis made in this work was static and transitional periods were not considered. The results found, from the created database, proved to be efficient, in all cases where the isolated and combined loads were recognized.
