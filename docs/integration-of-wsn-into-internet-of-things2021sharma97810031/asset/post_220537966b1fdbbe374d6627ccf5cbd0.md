
## 2.5.2 INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC APPLICATION OF WIRELESS SENSOR NETWORKS

In the previous sections, the characteristics of WSNs were considered. Their applications were also shown. WSNs have the potential for scalability. Industries are of several types, such as construction, apparel manufacture, metal object, car manufacture, weapon manufacturing industries. In today’s world, automation has replaced humans in many sectors. Automation needs data from various parts of the industrial machinery to run seamlessly. Information from the critical elements of the industry is necessary, for example, cracks inside machinery, production fault, wheel circle fault detection, etc.

In the case of structural engineering, there are awe-inspiring applications. To maintain proper metal bending in the industry, building fracture detection, accurate height detection, and stress and tension detection for structures can be done using sensors. Earlier, people used to follow complex mathematical formulas and tools for this kind of work. ## 2.6 WIRELESS SENSOR NETWORKS FOR FUTURE

The scalability and use of WSNs are increasing with time. The uses can be seen at home, office, research, industry, and various other levels. Every sector deals with data. Though, in this chapter, we have seen multiple attack methodologies and their proposed countermeasures, there can still be room for improvement [92]. Given the sensitivity of the usage factor, collected and processed data are a paramount concern.

Blockchain technology can be used in WSNs for protecting data confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity. However, the implementation of blockchain in WSNs, given the low power and ad hoc nature, can be a matter of open research issue. On the other hand, storing data in a blockchain can also ensure the verifiability of data to another party. Blockchain can also be used for infected nodes and malicious codes. In this case, smart contracts can also be of use. The use of WSNs in IoT networks is also increasing. Smart health applications are increasing day by day; mobile devices are also equipped with sensors to measure heart rates. Pacemakers are built for advanced heart treatment and use sensors. Sensors also have a network that can be exploited, given appropriate time and effort. Hence, the network can be made more secure using enhanced protocols and security updates. Moreover, machine learning is also being implemented in this case of improved energy efficiency.

The concept of a smart city is becoming popular day by day among scientists and researchers. In the smart city features, WSNs will be an essential tool. Using this network, a private communication network for a sustainable clean environment can be proposed where the nodes will collect data from the environment continuously. Depending on the data analysis and applying artificial intelligence, the ingredients in the air can be increased and decreased from artificial sources. If dust particles are increased, more effective filters can be designed to protect the environment. Sensors can also be used for harmful gas detection. This feature can be used for public health safety.

To summarize and conclude, it can be said that the WSN is one of the most crucial and flourishing sectors in modern IT. Understanding the basics of WSNs will facilitate leveraging the urgency and need for the use of WSNs. Moreover, security threats and attacks can be studied for further development of this technology. Though there exist some limitations in the technology itself, if efforts are made to understand security threats, novel techniques can be developed to leverage the security issues and challenges of WSNs.
