The second step is the procurement phase in which the data collected are amassed to procure and distinguish correct information for other phases. This contains three further divisional phases: the process of gathering data from various sources that create such raw information is the first phase. The sources used in microgrids here range from sensors to smart homes, etc. The second phase is the transmission of data, which usually represents the process of transporting data for storage and handling to a center of data, and this is realized by the use of communication arrangements. The third phase is to tackle the problems that occur with the presence of repeating and error-filled data, and, hence, preprocessing of data is applied. This compresses and reduces the repeating characters along with identifying and cleaning faults.

Storing of data with the purpose of further working and handling it is done at the third step. The fourth step is known as the analysis of data phase, which accelerates the inquiry of the gathered data with the help of various sculpting and review methods that aid in creating a comprehensive and confidential extract of all the raw information garnered from the phases before. This is a crucial phase in the processing of big data as its objective is to extract all the valuable information for future applications. This analysis can be described as expressive, prognostic, and strict based on the complexity of the study done.
