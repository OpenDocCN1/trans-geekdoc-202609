# ## 13.5.1 BUSINESS

In this sector, communication is key. The people concerned need to have complete knowledge about their products, know how to increase their sales, and have important information about the other companies in the same field. Instead of going through different sources that might not be authentic, a person can simply use a chatbot to gather all the necessary information [14]. Individual personalization of employees with AI-powered IoT devices helps them maintain a work–life balance that increases their efficiency of performance and thus the output of the task.

Introducing this technology in the human resources (HR) department of any company has the potential to bring about beneficial changes to the company. When the enormous amount of data managed by the HR department manually is managed by AI devices, they drastically reduce the chances of errors. There will also be less pressure on the workers because these trained machines can do the task quickly. In the hiring process of companies, using AI-powered robots for the screening process makes the process more transparent and reliable, as there is no chance of nepotism, and managers can be confident that only capable employees are being hired for the company. This is an example of computational intelligence working with data from various sensors.

# ## 13.5.2 EDUCATION

Education is the most powerful tool one can ever have. A literate person spreads positivity, leading to the development of a mentally healthy and content society. The intersection of technology with this field is exactly what is needed today. Smart education can be provided by equipping classrooms with smart screens and digital assistants.

Instead of using real specimens for dissection, augmented reality (AR) can give the experience and lessons equivalent to those of real dissections without the usage of real specimens [15]. It is an interactive way of learning, and the students are also under no pressure of spoiling specimens. Biology students who have done the dissection of real specimens can relate to this pretty well. It can bring about immense profits by ending the need to buy costly specimens for each student. This is especially profitable in medical science universities where many medical colleges have to arrange for human corpses for millions of students around the world—a difficult and expensive endeavor.

Another important application can be in correcting students’ examination answer sheets, evaluating marks, and making the final results. This reduces the teachers’ burden since it is all computer-generated. There are also no chances of any mistake in counting the marks or in evaluating the results.
