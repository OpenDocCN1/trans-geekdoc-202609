Size, shape, and speed are major concerns in ubiquitous computing equipment and have become a matter of great concern. Ubiquitous computing is the process of embedding smart, small, and speedy computer technologies or intelligent devices, like microprocessors, in the main fabric of the machines, equipment, gadgets, and tools of day-to-day work [6]. Ubiquitous computing technologies becomes the unidentifiable permanent artificial intelligent part of the parent machine/object and does not have its separate entity. The purpose behind the use of ubiquitous computing is to incorporate computer technology in all the spheres of day-to-day life activities and objects such as machines, equipment, clothes, pencils, papers, tools, toys, home, hospitals, and official utilities [7].

During the past 50 years, computer technology has passed through different stages of computing technology. The period from 1960 to 1980 was the era of mainframe computers used by big companies, organizations, and institutions. Later on, as a result of the development of small-sized, easily manageable, and affordable computers in the 1980s, computers reached the approach of the common man. Personal computers got an entry into many walks of work and the personal lives of human beings. In the next phase, computer technology brought about a remarkable development by introducing the mobile computing devices with high-speed networking capabilities, and the emergence of this new technology paved the way for anytime and anywhere facility of computing. The introduction of this new technology tremendously increased the computing power and created a paradigm shift in the use of computers by users. This newly emerged ICT replaced the team of trained engineers and professional computer operators with the common man. Presently, a common man is effectively and easily involved, throughout the day, in the operation of a multitude of sophisticated computational devices.

This revolutionary ICT development is moving toward a new era of more intelligent and interconnected machines/objects of human use in a much smarter environment, where highly sophisticated computer technology is effectively woven and embedded in the fabric of human environment to accomplish and execute the activities of different works of everyday life [5]. Emerging pervasive computing technologies are effectively working in many areas of human life, such as healthcare, sport, and education. Reduction in medical errors can be one of the major benefits of using ubiquitous computing technology. ## 14.3 UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING FOR HEALTHCARE

Technical intervention has completely revolutionized the healthcare sector; the medical field has witnessed a landmark change as a result of technological advancement [8–11]. The comparison of delivery of medical services over the past 50 years, with respect to the use of technology, shows a drastic change in its style of practice and effectiveness. Technology has helped the healthcare sector in improving to a great extent and resulted in effective and better health for human beings. Without the use of technology, the quality of healthcare services will decline severely. Medical technology is a constructive aid to the different dimensions of healthcare, such as understanding illness, informing people to protect themselves from diseases, diagnosing diseases, treating people, managing patients, mobilization of healthcare resources, managing healthcare organizations, developing healthcare for masses, and research and development in the medical field [12]. Different types of technologies have different types of interventions in healthcare practice. Technological support to the medical field started with the use of very simple tools, equipment, gadgets, machines, and other infrastructure. All the fields of engineering, starting from mechanical to nuclear, laser, and computer technology, have extensively contributed to the development of the medical field. Computer technology is considered as the most modern field of engineering development; the application of computer technology has a remarkable impact on healthcare, and this field has completely reformed the medical profession and practice. Computer technology has penetrated to the minutest activity of medical care [13].

Different types of computers, computing strategies, and computing networking have their application in the healthcare system. In modern times, management of health at both the international and national levels is a mega activity, whereas there may arise a need for the management of certain microscopic biological processes. Both of these extreme healthcare processes can easily be intervened by ICT. To address the need for healthcare management, all the computing technologies, starting from supercomputing to ubiquitous computing, have effective applications among various dimensions of medical care. This chapter will discuss the application of ubiquitous computing in the healthcare sector with special reference to its application in the management of psychological disorders.

Ubiquitous computing has possibilities of access, not only in the organizational, social, and personal spheres of healthcare, but also in health and illness, day-to-day healthcare activities, medical professionalism, and institutional management. Ubiquitous medical equipment, implants, tools, and devices have proved as a great aid in the treatment and monitoring of the medical states of patients. Ubiquitous computing has supported clinicians to seek and access clinical and other information for effective treatment of patients, both within and outside the clinical settings. Ubiquitous computing has the capability of reforming and redesigning clinical practices, empowering patients for their temporary/permanent impairments and disabilities, especially the bedridden, elderly, and disabled people [14]. Ubiquitous computing has significantly helped in developing the new roles and relationships between different levels of healthcare by enabling the healthcare providers to play an active and more useful role in the effective treatment both inside and outside the clinic. Healthcare informatics has also been revolutionized by supporting bio-medical informatics, maintaining the patient's record, telemedicine, treatment billing, and maintenance of administration among healthcare organizations. The introduction of ubiquitous computing has also opened new prospects and domains of evaluation, diagnosis, treatment, management, and rehabilitation of people with psychological disorders [15].

## 14.4 UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING IN THE MANAGEMENT OF MENTAL HEALTH PROBLEMS

There can be three possible models for dealing with illness at the national and international levels:

- 1. Treat people when they are sick by providing them desired healthcare facilities.
- 2. Vaccinate or medicate people in childhood so that they can be protected from diseases.
- 3. Educate and train people to live a typical lifestyle and maintain desired environmental conditions that are free from the possibilities of diseases.

Countries from all over the world are practicing all these three models of healthcare management by spending a huge percentage of their gross domestic product, but still, the basic minimum target of health forever and for all could not be achieved [16–19]. Following are the healthcare processes that need to be managed and executed for the effective maintenance of sound health and medical services:

- Identification of diseases and their characteristics.
- Development of investigative and diagnostic tools.
- Development of treatment, management, and precaution plans.
- Development of patient's care plan during the illness.
- Rehabilitation plan in case disease has resulted in partial or complete loss of body part or impairment of its function.
- Awareness about diseases and their prevention.

Accomplishment and management of the above-stated healthcare processes need the attention of professionals, from both medical and technological disciplines. If we critically examine and compare the contribution of medical and technological disciplines in the management of healthcare services, then technology has played a crucial role in the identification of diseases and their characteristics, development of investigative and diagnostic tools, treatment and management processes, patient's care plan during the illness, rehabilitation, and awareness about diseases and prevention [20]. If the support provided by technology to the medical services is withdrawn, then medical services will become completely crippled. The above-stated processes of healthcare management are compulsory factors of all the different disciplines of medical services, including the treatment of psychological disorders. So, for the diagnosis, treatment, management, and rehabilitation of people with psychological disorders, there is a need for technological intervention.

Even in the modern times of educational and scientific advancement, mental health issues are not considered as a health problem or medical illness; rather, they are considered as matters of shame, guilt, and disgust. People facing psychological disorders feel ashamed of sharing about their psychological problems and hesitate to consult for the treatment. If someone dares to do so, then people usually make a mockery of them and treat them strangely.
