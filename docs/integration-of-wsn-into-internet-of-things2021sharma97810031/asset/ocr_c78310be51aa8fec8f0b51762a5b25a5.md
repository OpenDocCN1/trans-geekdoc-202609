# 8.4.2 Acquisition of Data

Ceaselessly increasing big data and its manner of use of energy for scattered WSNs are two factors that are addressed in present problems. A dynamically growing cellular self-organizing map algorithm based on a self-organizing map is produced for choosing the head of groups. With the aid of the calculation of energy lost, the addition of new nodes becomes possible [63]. The QoS, ratio of packets, etc. are some of the features that enjoy better yields with this method. Portable sink–generated delay in networks (wireless) can be reduced by crossing paths of definite lengths, thus reducing the overall complexity produced by agility as suggested in another paper, with the use of the M-mobile method [52]. A structure fidelity data collection framework, which has its priority as maintaining the data security for its structural integrity, was suggested in another study with the sole purpose of reducing the count of actively participating nodes and reducing the organizational alteration [64]. This framework utilizes the alteration approach to examine the reliability metric of the image to understand the quality of inaccurate images.

Geng et al. [65] focused on evaluating the impact of multiple compression and serialization methods on big data platform performance and tried to choose optimal compression and serialization methods for the industrial data platform. Geng et al. [66] provided in their paper a hybrid smart grid (SG) communication architecture integrating fiber optic and Wi-Fi-based mesh networks, that is, fiber-wireless (Fi-Wi)-enhanced SG, and studied the problem of data acquisition under failures in Fi-Wi-enhanced SG.
