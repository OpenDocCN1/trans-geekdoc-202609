# 8.2.4 BENEFITS FROM BIG DATA ANALYSIS IN MICROGRIDS

The stability of a system and its reliability are two major features that are always prioritized when dealing with microgrids. These have been further divided into subfeatures ranging from the stability of voltage to detection of oscillation, etc. The new and ever-evolving budding applications of big data now provide an opportunity to search for new possibilities for the old and futile monitoring and detection methods [30]. The use of these resources and increased adeptness are two features that have been credited to be complementary to big data. They are of particular help when dealing with understanding the physical confines of the resources, authorizing and rectifying the models, and incorporating the renewable energy resources with the aid of provided tools. One such instance can be of the meters, which are smarter than traditional meters and have been mounted in houses and corporate buildings around the globe. This experiment permits detection of fraud, better predictions of power failure, comparatively easier process for billing, etc. This, however, requires a higher rate of sampling than other meters, among other shortcomings.

# 8.3 BIG DATA COLLECTION IN MICROGRIDS

The major advantage of microgrids is realized as the presence of a local power source very near to the location of the major energy consumption source, which in turn eradicates the need of transporting power over long distances. Management of data at these sources is done with the help of big data techniques, summarized in Table 8.1 presented below.

| Methods | Brief Summary |
|---|---|
| Data pertaining to equipment [31–37] | Data gathered from transformers, switchgear, power cables, and substations are gathered here. |
| Generation of distributed data [24,38] | Data from various sources—biogas, renewable, wind, and solar energy with their datasets. |
| Load side and storage battery data [39–41] | Data from battery systems and various other sources, including smart meters, cooling systems, and even customers. |
| Environmental data [42–44] | Precise and accurate predictions with faster results and better modeling while making use of present GIS. |
| WSN [45–51] | The best way to collect data, sensor, and sink nodes is used to collect all sorts of data. |
| Cloud storage and IoT [52–55] | Data collected from IoT sources and stored in the cloud while processed with big data analysis. |
