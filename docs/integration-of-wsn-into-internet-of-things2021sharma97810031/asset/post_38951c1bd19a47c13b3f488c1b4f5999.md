### 12.2.2.4 ZMQ Messaging Design Model

Meng et al. [16] proposed this design model of ZMQ messaging in which a flexible and generic messaging mechanism of machine to machine (M2M) is present among the machines for data sharing and notifications of event and command. Experimentation using the case study for food manufacturing production of quality inspection microwave sensor concludes that ZMQ technology is a promising tool for machine discovery and presence, machine connectivity, and messaging to accept data interaction and data access for the rich sensing IoT applications. This technique helps to solve heterogeneity problems and complex structure of applications of IIoT and also provides cross-platform capability which permits the implementation on different lightweight devices and powerful computers.

### 12.2.2.5 Synchronization Attacks

Yang et al. [17] proposed the two different types of time synchronization attacks known as timeslot template (TT) and absolute slot number (ASN), and two algorithms known as threshold filter (TOF) and Sec_ASN algorithm were proposed to deal with the two proposed attacks with IEEE802.15.4e based on stack of IIoT protocol, respectively. While the new nodes and network are connected, they may get some inaccurate ASN values in ASN attack. Whereas in a TT attack, the harmful node misleads the licit node for computing the error clock offset. Sec_ASN is an association of a method known as 2s + 1 and authentication. Authentication can be attained by two steps: one by verifying the sender’s data and then by inspecting the sent information for any tampering in the communication. In method 2s + 1, a node is chosen from the surrounding nodes as the time parent node for synchronizations. The TOF algorithm is used for the estimation of clock offset by applying the method of least squares with the variation between sending time of nodes and normal node time.

### 12.2.2.6 Robust Time Synchronization

Qiu et al. [18] proposed a scheme of robust time synchronization called R-Sync which removes the nodes that are isolated to reduce the energy consumption in the entire synchronization process and makes all nodes synchronized. With the help of two timers, the isolated nodes are pulled to connect synchronized networks. One of the timers is used for time synchronization with the help of two-way message exchange, while the other timer is used for the synchronization process which is present at the beginning. Node selection algorithm was also introduced by the authors for the utilization of balanced energy in the sensor nodes and for extending the course of the sensor networks. This algorithm is in contrast with the other three algorithms that already exist: spanning tree-based energy-efficient time synchronization, timing-sync protocol for sensor networks, and groupwise pair selection algorithm. This comparison proves that R-Sync algorithm has lower energy consumption, especially in large-scale and densely connected networks.

## 12.2.3 IoT's Enabling Technologies

The foundation of IIoT is established by allowing a huge plethora of technologies containing IoT, big data, virtual reality (VR), physical systems, human-to-machine (H2M), and artificial intelligence.

### 12.2.3.1 Internet of Things

In the light of the scenario of connected factory, the devices of IoT help in real-time actuation and collection of data. These devices help to track the assets of factory all over the globe. The entire process of finished goods is monitored using the IoT devices to reduce the manual system management and labor cost. In the thoroughly connected system of IIoT, the IoT devices are located across the facilities of the factory. However, the deployment, configuration, maintenance, and monitoring of devices are a hectic job and also require a technical staff who is highly qualified. Liu et al. [19] proposed Tornado, a blockchain system with high performance which is based on the corresponding algorithms and the space-structured ledger, so as to allow blockchain in IoT.

### 12.2.3.2 Blockchain Technology

It is one of the main technologies in IIoT. IoT allows devices in smart industries to generate a large quantity of data. These data that are produced by the IoT devices are then processed and analyzed for monitoring the performance, diagnosis, anomaly detection, asset monitoring, predictive maintenance of the devices, and the complete life cycle tracking of the product. However, sharing of data in IIoT system with all the entities is a difficult task. The distinctive features that make the blockchain technology suitable for IIoT are traceability, distributed nature, trust, security, survivability, tamper resistance, and inherit data provenance. The blockchain technology is also used for IoT devices' access control and firmware updates. Aderibole et al. [20] proposed a conceptual model of standard National Institute of Standards and Technology of smart grid domains concerning the main features of blockchain: incentive, trust, and decentralization. Also, this research helped in guiding and identifying the development of smart grid blockchain.

### 12.2.3.3 Cloud Computing

IIoT requires computing systems with high performance to control, examine, store, and process the massive data. Technologies of cloud computing allow network and storage services and compute the overall IIoT system facilities. All applications and connected devices are immediately interfaced with the back-end clouds. Models of these cloud services are built as public, private, or hybrid. The establishment of recruitment of technical staff and cloud services requires a high spend. Hence, the private cloud service model is not a feasible alternative for medium- and small-level enterprises. However, well-established and large multinational enterprises want deployment of the private clouds to confirm security, privacy, and safety and manage the industrial surveillance for competitive advantage. Shen et al. [21] presented a key agreement based on a novel block design by taking the supremacy of symmetric balanced incomplete block design. Also, the property of fault tolerance of the protocol allows data sharing in group with the help of cloud computing to withstand various key attacks.

### 12.2.3.4 Virtual and Augmented Reality

Augmented reality (AR) technologies assist industrial workers through some complex operations, for example, machineries disassembling/assembling, mission critical systems, and complex industrial products. AR technologies allow monitoring the machines and workers during the operations and simultaneously generate notifications or alert so as to decrease the amount of errors. A VR technology eases in envisioning of the reconfigurations and configurations of industrial modules and functions prior to the implementation in IIoT systems. The VR helps to cut off shut-down time and reduces the configuration times of industrial machines and plants. VR simulations are planned by taking into consideration the open standards, which is again done by taking into consideration the heterogeneity in IIoT systems and Cyber Physical Systems (CPS). Ping et al. [22] proposed a comparison among the AR and VR systems on the basis of depth perception performance by the use of an optical transparent head-mounted display which shows that AR has more precision than VR in depth estimation.

### 12.2.3.5 Machine Learning

ML methods are used for multilevel resources that help to register cross-level optimization and adapt the flexible optimization target. ML-based applications have two categories: problem-solving and classification. Classification includes the classification knowledge in analysis of the unknown input patterns. Its main purpose is to learn about the standard for classification. In problem-solving for the given target state, an action sequence is used to generate the target state from the current state. The main focus of ML is to acquire knowledge, which in turn improves the problem-solving efficiency in IIoT.

Some of the key learning natures of ML are synthesis rule, specialization, generalization, and generation. ML is befitting for configuration problems or parameter setting in nature. Multiobjective decision-making guarantees quality of security (QoS) of IIoT and also meets the various demands of real network state. Considering IIoT analysis, methods of ML can integrate filtering technology and multiagent theory to process the related data automatically. It expands the monitoring scope and decreases the quantity of transmitted data. Meanwhile, the security of IIoT environment is promoted on the network edge to block the harmful network data.
