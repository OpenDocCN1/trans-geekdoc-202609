+   i. Traffic analysis
+   Cellular networks that are relatively large usually perform monitoring of traffic and analysis with a large-capacity server that provides a high-performance rate to support the high requirements and good data rate links that are the needs of a vast network. In recent years, however, it has been noticed that the analysis on the above approach does not have full reach when faced with the size of today's abundant volume and rate of data [94]. To improve upon the said shortcomings, an advanced distributed computing platform was suggested that makes use of Hadoop, HBase, HDFS, and MapReduce. The ability to handle such vast amounts of data came in handy while operating on the hardware.

+   ii. Big data mobile internet operator
+   A telecom ecosystem that is mostly operator-centric was first widely noticed in China Unicom, with a staggering 250 million subscribers in 2012; it is their largest 3G WCDMA (wideband code division multiple access) mobile operator as highlighted in a research study [95]. The data that are usually generated by the internet mobile users can be understood and worked upon by the aforementioned platforms of big data. When storage in a distributed manner is concerned, the HDFS platform is usually chosen as the appropriate candidate; it provides various other features such as improving the performance, augmenting the process of storage, and quickening the speed of the transactions (database). This cluster had about 1.9 PB of data storage space and 188 nodes used for various purposes, ranging from performing statistical analysis on data to even storing and managing the data. The structured data that is present on the HDFS is usually processed with the help of 145k records per second writing rate and very large storage space of HBase, which acts as a large distributed storage and also a database.
