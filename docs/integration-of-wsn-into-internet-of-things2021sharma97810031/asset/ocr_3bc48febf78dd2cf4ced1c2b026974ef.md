# ## 11.7 LAYERED ARCHITECTURE OF IoT

It is always difficult to describe the architecture of those technologies that are a mixture of various things. As we have discussed in the introduction part of this chapter, many standards organizations define IoT in different ways because IoT supports dynamic things to get integrated and provide solutions from anywhere and anytime. IoT considers every direction of the need; therefore, we are not wrong if we say that IoT is the future of world. IoT has expanded the services of the Internet in a good manner. As defined in Ref. [8], the architecture of IoT we are considering various layers for communication along with its basic functioning and applications as shown in Table 11.2. All layers play their individual role to serve interoperability among the different technologies, scalability, efficient process management, security, and execution of IoT in different areas of applications.

# ## 11.7.1 APPLICATION LAYER

The role of the application layer in IoT is to serve the user interface-based smart services via smart devices such as smartphones and wearable smart communication devices. This layer consists of smart application domains such as smart projects for energy utilization and smart city, buildings, e-commerce businesses, commercial businesses, homes, cities, hospital and medical care units, and GPRS-based tracking and searching operations. The integration of applications with industries, cities, and businesses brings quality in services and growth in manufacturing and production; therefore, nowadays people of all age-groups like to use smart applications for on-demand services in their daily life's needs.
