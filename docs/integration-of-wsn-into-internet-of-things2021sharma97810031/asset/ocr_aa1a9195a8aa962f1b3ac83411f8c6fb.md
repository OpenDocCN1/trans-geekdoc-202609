# 15.3.2 GEOGRAPHIC OPPORTUNISTIC ROUTING BASED ON SELECTIVE AUTHENTICATION AGAINST DOS ATTACKS

The healthcare industry in the past two decades has witnessed multiple technological strides being made that have helped reduce the onus of inculcating expensive physiological data testing done in laboratories to be made by an unobtrusive, pervasive system of wearable sensors. These wearable systems of devices have made physiological monitoring of users and, hence, the diagnosis of ailments and diseases easier for medical staff in a more quantifiable and comprehensive manner. However, with these growing devices that rely heavily on wireless communication, the issue of privacy and security gained traction. Depending on the various forms of shortcomings of these systems, those with malicious intent have found multiple ways to attack and to lay hands on sensitive data procured by these devices. One of the foremost forms of attack is the DoS (denial of service) attack.

One of the most pivotal reasons attributed to the success of WSNs in the healthcare industry is wireless data delivery. However, until recently, the data delivery system was based on primordial multi-path routing strategies. The data transmission hence became susceptible, which was prone to link failures owing to signal fading or interferences. This led to the development of opportunistic routing where the path of data packets was not predetermined, but multiple data forwarders are allowed to overhear the packet transmission due to their shared network. Then, the data are routed by prioritizing the packet as directed by the sender of the data packet. This is further channeled through multiple data forwarders, and therefore, data delivery occurs uninterrupted as long as one data forwarder relays the information successfully. This is known to have minimal transmission contentions as arising in the multi-path routing method and improved performance. The routing method used in opportunistic routing is further coupled with geographic routing as it utilizes the dynamic nature of the WSNs and does not use a single established route or node for transmission, but rather uses multiple paths.
