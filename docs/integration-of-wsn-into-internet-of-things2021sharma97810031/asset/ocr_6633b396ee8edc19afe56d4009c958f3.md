In contrast to the previously mentioned reviews, this work proposes a holistic approach for applications of big data in microgrids. In this chapter, we talk about all of this while focusing on some aspects of introducing this approach to wireless sensor networks (WSNs). The field of WSNs is formed on the foundation of a grouping of sensors present in areas that are usually unreachable and connected in a random manner, thus forming information circulation networks [11,12]. The WSN generally finds its importance in fields where the processing of large data, recognition of functions, and implanting technology exist [13]. Big data is well on the road to become omnipresent in almost every field in sciences. Its major advantages are easy deployment, flexible compartmentalization, and heaps of various different possibilities it often open up for its users [14,15].

The major contribution of this work is a detailed comprehensive discussion on the recent advances in big data, microgrids, and WSNs. Apart from exploring the big data applications in microgrids, this work also presents an extensive analysis of optimization, deployment, and development. The major contribution of this work can be summarized as follows. The characteristics and requirements of big data have been discussed along with their tools and technologies in detail. The motivation for big data integration with microgrids and WSNs is discussed along with the architectural design of WSNs. Recently proposed WSNs and big data-based solutions to call dropping in cellular networks have been reviewed. Finally, current challenges faced are also presented in detail along with future research directions in the field.

Section 8.7 identifies the current challenges and future research scope of this field followed by a conclusion in Section 8.8.
