# 11.4.2 EMBEDDED SOFTWARE-BASED DEVICES

Sensors are devices already programmed with embedded software which help one to collect live data from various surrounding applications and data sources, and such data objects help IoT for information mining, decision-making, and pattern matching, and forecasting of various business decisions on time with more accuracy. At present IoT emerging various communication platforms of monitoring and tracking services with smartphones such as GPRS for location-based tracking operations, other wearable devices helps one to maintain and observe many health measurements like blood presser level, sugar level, heart rate pulse from time to time with alert notification for further treatment in the present and future; likewise, so many IoT-based smart applications and device-based services make people’s life more easier and comfortable.

# 11.4.3 COMMUNICATION MEDIA

To carry data from one place to another, communication media plays an important role for connectivity issues, through which data can be exchanged, transferred, and collected at cloud infrastructure. Communication media will connect all the surrounding sensor nodes from one end to the other to perform IoT operation, and there are many types of media, for example, mobile signals, Bluetooth frequency channels, Wi-Fi signals, and electromagnetic spectrum for wireless radio or microwave low- and high-range communication.

# 11.4.4 OPERATIONS ON DATA OBJECTS

After collecting data objects from multiple sources, various operations on data need to be performed to get the exact information for forecasting of weather and temperature management, and the collected data need to be mined so that our system can protect the life of many people from natural disasters such as earthquakes. To take these measures at run-time, the emerging dynamic services of IoT perform virtual operations on data objects for 24×7hours in a continuous manner.
