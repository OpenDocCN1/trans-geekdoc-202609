
Even the technology that aims to serve the people has faced several challenges of security, privacy, confidentiality, authentication and threats from attackers using this platform to steal sensitive information apart from the physical challenges of the system. This has been a major impediment to the growth and development of technology along with its adoption in the general populace. To overcome this hurdle, several upcoming solutions and countermeasures have been proposed in this chapter, including geographic opportunistic routing based on selective authentication against DoS attacks and a three-factor authentication protocol for ensuring privacy, confidentiality, and impersonation attacks. Although this chapter aims to bring out all the potential solutions and countermeasures, it is an impossible task to cover all of them.

An ecosystem of heterogeneous and homogeneous devices is being created that could help tap into the vast potential of remote diagnosis and prevention of disorders, ailments, and diseases at an early stage using the data being procured from sensors worn by the user. This would not only be a more cost-efficient, life-saving method to treat patients but also be an economic one that could potentially help reduce the burden of the number of patients being treated per doctor and hospital in-house resources but also allow more room for new patients to engage with medical staff regularly.

Along with using technology such as RFID tags, wireless body area network and other wireless sensors to be incorporated in tracking health implied that the technology being offered was matured over the years, and hence, they could be put into mass production easily, which is one of the key industrial aspects required for any industry, since these devices and components are not unique to the healthcare industry and the solutions thus being developed could be easily applied to other industries equally. Therefore, the onus of development of a more energy-efficient, computational resource-rich, and cost-efficient method remains the industry hurdle for an all-encompassing, pervasive healthcare system for the existing projects and future ventures as well.
