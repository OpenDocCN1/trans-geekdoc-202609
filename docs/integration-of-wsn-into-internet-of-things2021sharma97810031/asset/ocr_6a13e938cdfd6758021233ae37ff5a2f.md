The primary role of sensors is to collect all the contextual information from the environment where it exists; therefore, in IoT, various types of sensors are used for different tasks. According to the programming of the sensor, we can take various measurements such as temperature, pollution in air, humidity in the atmosphere, and water quality [9,10]. As per the need of application, some of the sensors may also have memory for the storage of measurements for the purpose of comparison, future research, and other scientific operations. The best feature of sensors is that they can record any measurement, and after collecting the measurement, it can converted into signals, so the receiving devices can get it in a proper way. Sometimes, applications need a group of sensors to perform any operation. In that case, many sensors are grouped together for any specific purpose, for example, body measurement sensors for observing real-time health condition and home appliance sensors for accessing, monitoring, and controlling smart home devices from some remote location.
