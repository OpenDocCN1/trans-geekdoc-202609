### 14.1 INTRODUCTION

Modern man has reached such a state where thinking about the existence of a world free from technology and materialistic aids seems to be very difficult. Technological and materialistic support has penetrated so deep into all the spheres of human life and affairs of day-to-day life that in case if it is withdrawn, then the availability, supply, and even the utilization of most basic human requirements will not at all be possible. If we look back, deep in the past, the attainment of man's capability to light and tame the fire and use it for personal purposes is considered as the origin of the evolutionary process of modern technological and materialistic development.

With the invention of the fire, wheel, waterwheel, pozzolana (bricks and mortar), compass, clock, steam engine, electricity, agricultural tools and equipment, vaccines, medicines, and surgery, the evolutionary process of modern technological and materialistic advancement progressed in the primitive times [1]. By the 17th century, the pace of technological development gradually started picking up, and later in the 18th and 19th centuries, a lot many inventions and discoveries contributed to a remarkable advancement in all the spheres of development. But still, the tempo of technological advancement and discoveries during this period was very slow, and it had been happening only once a while in years. The 20th century recorded the regular and frequent phenomenon of technological development, and in the later part of the 20th century, the occurrence of technological innovation, via means of new inventions and discoveries, in all the sectors of development became very frequent. The closure of the 20th and the beginning of the 21st century is known as an era of "techno-advancement explosion." Technological innovations in this period became the cut-throat competitor for the existing version of its own last generation of technology. The pace of advancement was so quick that in some of the cases, even the birth of a typical technology could not take place because of the arrival of another advanced innovation in the field [2]. In modern times, matching up with the pace of technological advancement has become very tedious and difficult.

During the last 20–25 years, advancement in technology has evolved in such a way that completely new and different types of technologies have come into existence. Computer and information technology is one such field that has just recently originated but developed to a very advanced stage. This technology has not only contributed significantly to its own development but also drastically influenced and intervened, nearly, among all other fields of technology and development. Computer technology is growing and developing at such a fast pace that it has created a kind of confusion about which version of computer technology is the latest. Before the arrival of any latest version of computer technology, its next generation is ready to reach the market. Innovation and advancement in computer technology are so fast and vast that even the experts in the discipline could not match up with the pace of advancement in the field. Computer technology has direct intervention among the rest of all the fields of technologies. As a result, the intervention of computer technology in other fields of technology and disciplines of study is multifold; it has totally changed the present working status of humans and machines. Computer technology has become an interface between humans and machines because it is quick, easy to use, and portable and can be blended with any other form of technology. The blending of computer technology with other technologies has improved the productivity, effectiveness, and efficiency of both humans and machines.

There are different types of computers such as supercomputer, mainframe, minicomputer, workstation, and personal computer that are used for different purposes in various disciplines of technology. Personal computers are further classified according to their design, position, and purpose of use: tower model, desktop model, notebook, laptop, subnotebook, handheld, palmtop, and personal digital assistant (PDA). For effective and appropriate use of computer technology, different fields, organizations, and disciplines need different types of computing techniques. Examples of such computing methodologies are cloud computing, grid computing, utility computing, distributed computing, cluster computing, farm computing, and ubiquitous computing. This chapter will discuss the ubiquitous computing [3].

### 14.2 UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING

Application and implementation of information and computer technology (ICT) has become so widespread and easy that it has possibilities of both direct and indirect intervention. ICT has become one of the major prerequisites for all the fields of human, environmental, space, and universe development. Use of ICT in understanding, describing, predicting, and advancement of different domains of human growth and development (such as physical, social, psychological, cultural, health, economic, scientific, technological, educational, environmental, and day-to-day life) has become a crucial factor [4]. Besides this, the application of ICT among different fields has further triggered the process of change and advancement, which in consequence can be addressed by the upcoming innovative versions of ICT inventions only. Upcoming versions of new ICTs always have ever-expanding and elevating effects on the respective field of implementation, which further demand their management, and to address this newly emerged state again, there is a need for generation of technology. ICT has the application of both of its formats, that is, software and hardware. Applicability of software is possible with the help of hardware computers only [5]. Different types of hardware formats of computers such as supercomputer, mainframe, minicomputer, workstation, and personal computer are available, which can be used for different purposes in different disciplines of technology. A variety of personal computers such as tower model, desktop model, notebook, laptop, subnotebook, handheld, palmtop, and PDA are available in different designs and sizes as per the need of their place and position and purpose of use. Cordless mobility of telephone and Internet technology in the past decade has further expanded the implementation, transformative capability, and use of ICT [5]. Digitalization of information and services has additionally simplified the possibility of mobility of ICT, which has given birth to a new trend of computing called ubiquitous computing.
