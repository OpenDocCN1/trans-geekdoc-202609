# 11.8.3 ENVIRONMENTAL AWARENESS APPLICATIONS

This domain aims to solve the problems related to the environment which is always around human beings. It considers smart green environment maintenance devices, smart disaster and security management, and smart pollution control system. Smart environmental software provides information about the future possibility of natural disasters such as cyclones and earthquakes in advance so that one can have enough time to get prepared and ready to face such disasters without losing life and resources. This domain also considers the smart water management system for societal needs; along with this, the IoT ecosystem provides various information and alarming notification for the quality of water and air and other measurements of purity. Smart eco-applications maintain various data sets for air pollution and water quality measurements based on specific areas such as residential, industrial, and hospital. The primary responsibility of such applications is to provide continuous monitoring of environmental natural needs and provide the best solution and notification from time to time to take measures in advance. Smart agricultural apps deal with the measurements of high production in less time. Actually, such smart agriculture applications guide one to implement new things according to the change in weather in a most appropriate way so that according to the demand, agribusiness can provide the best quality products to people. Using the specialized agribusiness applications, one can learn to use the chemical combination for producing vegetables in good quantity in less cost and time.

# 11.8.4 TECHNICAL SMART INTELLIGENT APPLICATIONS

To take care of the responsibility of various business operations efficiently, we have this domain of smart applications where IoT smart applications bring a most reliable way to do business of commercial things such as banking and investment using smartphone application, e-commerce services for online product marketing, business planning, and product delivery in a most efficient and transparent manner. Smart technical application tools help many businesses in decision-making regarding the manufacturing and marketing of different products, and analytical tools help in maintaining the ratio of demand to supply in a perfect manner so that businesses can plan their activities accordingly. Smart technical IoT applications bring a magical platform for the current business world in which anyone can start their own business by using various virtually available objects of the Internet. Cloud network provides virtual storage, applications, and infrastructure-based services in a more effective manner in very less cost. Information management, security, and retrieval are very challenging tasks for today’s business world, but smart IoT applications manage information with security in an open environment using different authentication and authorization mechanisms. IoT smart applications also take the responsibility of keeping high security and safety measures during and after operation; therefore, people can trust the services provided by different commercial applications. Nowadays India is moving toward digitalization, and various payment options such as Google Pay app and PhonePe app are most popular and used by many business platforms.
