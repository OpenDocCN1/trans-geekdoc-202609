This excitement and desire for the exploitation of possibilities to develop apps for persons with psychological disorders and their support professionals have resulted in a type of outburst for app development. The market is already flooded with numerous ubiquitous apps and devices, and a lot many are waiting to enter or undergoing the process of development. But, ICT being a very recent entrant in the field of mental health illness, there are certain limitations as given below:

+   - Reliability and validity of newly developed apps and devices are not intact and appropriate.
+   - This technology is not authenticated, so there is a lot of ambiguity in its use.
+   - As this technology is to be used for the treatment and rehabilitation of human beings, it needs a regulatory body to properly evaluate it before use.
+   - No data are available for the use and effectiveness of this technology.

These limitations have not been considered as hurdles in the way of effective intervention by ubiquitous computing for the treatment and rehabilitation of persons with psychological disorders. A joint research work by the technologists and mental health professionals is trying to overcome these limitations and consequently pave the way for the following:

+   1. Ubiquitous computing-based assessment tools are being developed.
+   2. New ubiquitous technology-based devices for collecting, analyzing, and recording data and converting it into useful information for sharing and predicting the psychological states to monitor, treat, and manage the same.
+   3. ICT-based new psychological treatment strategies are under the process of development.
+   4. Ubiquitous computing-involved technologies for the identification of behavioral patterns, recording, sharing, monitoring, treatment, and rehabilitation of mental illness are under the process of development.
+   5. Traditional psychological treatments are being reformed and modified into a new set of ubiquitous computing-based treatment interventions.
+   6. Multidisciplinary treatment approach involving ICT as one of its major factors is emerging as a new strategy for treatment.
+   7. Ubiquitous computing-based devices are being developed to understand, describe, predict, and improve certain psychological processes such as memory, thinking, and learning.
