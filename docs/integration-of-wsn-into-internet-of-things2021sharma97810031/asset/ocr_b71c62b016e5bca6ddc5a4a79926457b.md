# 13.7 CONCLUSION

As we have learned, AI, ML, and IoT are all superpower technologies independently, and their convergence leads to the development of such incredible applications. While it does require a lot of patience, hard work, and plenty of brainpower to develop such machines and complex algorithms and train them to make decisions on their own, the results are worth it. Developers should keep in mind the legal and privacy ethics while designing new applications and that they should not harm the other entities that are not directly a part of the system. Aligning all these technologies in such a way that it serves the purpose, gives better results, and addresses the monetary and privacy concerns of the users is an important requirement that should be addressed by the developers. In conclusion, IoT can be seen as an interconnected network of bodies whose brain is the AI that gets experience through ML techniques.
