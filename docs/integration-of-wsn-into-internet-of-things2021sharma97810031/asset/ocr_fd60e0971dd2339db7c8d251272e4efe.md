Size, shape, and speed are major concerns in ubiquitous computing equipment and have become a matter of great concern. Ubiquitous computing is the process of embedding smart, small, and speedy computer technologies or intelligent devices, like microprocessors, in the main fabric of the machines, equipment, gazettes, and tools of day-to-day work [6]. Ubiquitous computing technologies becomes the unidentifiable permanent artificial intelligent part of the parent machine/object and does not have its separate entity. The purpose behind the use of ubiquitous computing is to incorporate computer technology in all the spheres of day-to-day life activities and objects such as machines, equipment, clothes, pencils, papers, tools, toys, home, hospitals, and official utilities [7].

During the past 50years, computer technology has passed through different stages of computing technology. The period from 1960 to 1980 was the era of mainframe computers used by big companies, organizations, and institutions. Later on, as a result of the development of small-sized, easily manageable, and affordable computers in the 1980s, computers reached the approach of the common man. Personal computers got an entry into many walks of work and the personal lives of human beings. In the next phase, computer technology brought about a remarkable development by introducing the mobile computing devices with high-speed networking capabilities, and the emergence of this new technology paved the way for anytime and anywhere facility of computing. The introduction of this new technology tremendously increased the computing power and created a paradigm shift in the use of computers by users. This newly emerged ICT replaced the team of trained engineers and professional computer operators with the common man. Presently, a common man is effectively and easily involved, throughout the day, in the operation of a multitude of sophisticated computational devices.

This revolutionary ICT development is moving toward a new era of more intelligent and interconnected machines/objects of human use in a much smarter environment, where highly sophisticated computer technology is effectively woven and embedded in the fabric of human environment to accomplish and execute the activities of different works of everyday life [5]. Emerging pervasive computing technologies are effectively working in many areas of human life, such as healthcare, sport, and education. Reduction in medical errors can be one of the major benefits of using ubiquitous computing technology. Identification and blending of medicine and psychology can create better user-friendly healthcare systems.
