The clinical decision support system is another amazing advantage by which healthcare professionals provide clinical decision support to their patients [21]. It is a kind of targeted technology that aims at assisting caregivers in decision-making tasks at different stages in the process, including preventive care, diagnosis, treatment, regular monitoring, and follow-up procedures; it is a great example of the utilization of computational intelligence in the field of medicine. Biosensors are a boon to this industry. Interoperability is one of the biggest factors that can be optimized with the correct use of healthcare technologies. The different entities that are a part of the healthcare network can be connected and brought together in a common arena. This will help solve many issues. For example, hospitals may be connected to pharmaceutical companies from which they purchase medicines. Before the stock of medicines is gone, IoT sensors installed on the storage racks can send these data to the cloud where using ML techniques, it concludes that the stock needs to be replenished. Finally, using AI techniques the final decision is made that a new stock is required. A notification is sent to the pharmaceutical companies to send a new stock to the hospitals. This prevents hospitals from running out of medicine and is an example of WSNs in the field of medicine.

An example of these technologies in clinical health care is the recent development of smart watches for depression. The main aim is to improve the mental health of people which directly affects one’s cognitive skills, as well as physical health. It has been speculated that as the world is becoming more technology-enhanced and modern, cases of depression are increasing. Smart watches detect minute fluctuations in emotional states by observing the change in the rhythm of the heartbeat. These fluctuations are analyzed by ML algorithms and compared with the normal heart rate data of that person. The watch captures and records panic attack symptoms as well as stress symptoms of the person. Using AI techniques, the watch will be able to predict any future panic attack. This is an important step because stress and anxiety can cause heart disease. These watches in the future will be connected to healthcare centers. Suppose a person wearing such a device experiences an attack, their location is automatically sent to the healthcare center, which immediately sends help to the location. The data sharing is allowed to particular healthcare providers because the patient has consented to it. The data, as discussed at the beginning of this chapter, are collected and stored systematically in the cloud, so caregivers can easily retrieve them from there. Just like normal vacuum cleaners, it has brushes that clean the surface on which they are mounted.
