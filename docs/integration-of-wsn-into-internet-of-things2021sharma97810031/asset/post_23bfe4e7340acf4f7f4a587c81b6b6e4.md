Since IoT is a combination of multiple technologies, poor design of any integrated part of IoT may lead to high losses to any business or world. The following five factors are responsible for the technological challenges in IoT.

### 11.9.1.1 Security

At presently IoT devices, applications, and network are the essential parts of human life and many businesses, it is a common fact, when any technology is growing well and getting high popularity among many people always get hacked or attacked by someone, many vendors are making duplicate things and are trying to spread any virus to get valuable information for making any loss, such happening will lose trust of people and business worlds from IoT. Once the trust gets lost, then it is very difficult to get it again, since information is very important for everyone. Therefore, IoT should be technically sound, protected and should be secure enough to protect our network and its trust over IoT services as defined in Refs. [18,19].

### 11.9.1.2 Connectivity, Coordination, and Communication [CCC]

In the near future, the triple C factor of connectivity, coordination, and communication will be the measure challenges in IoT because accurate and proper connectivity with complete coordination and communication is the backbone of any Internet service platform. Now, IoT is participating in most of the businesses and services. One can imagine that the numbers of users are increasing exponentially and IoT is gaining trust from most of the people and business world for future services. IoT brings users satisfaction by offering centralized distributed connection, coordination, and communication services to manage billions of users, applications, and devices as a part of the IoT network, according to the present need of users IoT having enough capacity to management triple C factors without any challenge but it is always difficult to extend the same IoT structure of centralized distributed services for future because that time such single network will not be efficient as today and will get suffered by bottleneck issues due to high demand of IoT connection, coordination, and communication services.

### 11.9.1.3 Compatibility in Technologies

Compatibility issues among the different technologies will be the main area of research for future IoT because IoT is not a single structure or combination of similar technologies; instead, it is a heterogeneous kind of technological combinational structure, which provides heterogeneous services to its users and businesses. When one talks about the present IoT and its enabling technology, they can never consider it for future businesses and applications. The future of IoT demands heavy changes in the underlying hardware and software technology with a new definition of protocols to be used for communication. Therefore, such a system demands high financial investment and cost to upgrade and maintain the old version of IoT accordingly.

### 11.9.1.4 Protocols

Protocols are the set of rules and regulations for integrating multiple technologies and multi-vendor devices for managing information and process controlled and circulated through many sensors. The key responsibility of communication protocols is to carry the quality of communication services among multiple sensors to IoT users. Protocols are the lifeline for any communication system; therefore, protocol integration and management could be a big challenge in the future because technological convention and incorporation among multiple technologies is a very complex task for any IoT network [20].

### 11.9.1.5 Data Analytics

Data analytics is a very useful procedure for any IoT network. Sensors are continuously producing data and processing them for further operation. Such a huge amount of data is very difficult to manage and store for longer time. In this case, the data need to be mined for making it useful enough for the present and future of IoT operations, many applications of IoT perform intelligent decisions and actions against any process, based on the efficiency and accuracy of data analytics, data analytics tools helping sensors for making decision and accordingly perform further action to get result. It is a big challenge in front of all IoT designers and developers to invent such an efficient data analytics tool that can deal with a higher number of users who are using multidisciplinary IoT smart applications and a huge amount of data for producing accurate results. To do this, the IoT needs to integrate advanced data analytics tools with new technological concepts such as machine learning, deep learning, and data science techniques to ensure various operations based on data analysis.

## 11.9.2 Businesses and Application Challenges

Today, IoT is involved in maximum business applications as a prime service provider; therefore, we can say IoT is the lifeline of the present and future business class. Accordingly, IoT may cover the needs of billions of users through connected devices in the near future. Such involvement may lead to the following challenges in the business world:

### 11.9.2.1 Trust and Security

In Refs. [21,22], everyone knew that trust gains through security, as higher the security as higher the trust same practice is comes with theory where IoT gains complete trust of people and business world as it provide transparent and secure communication services across multiple platforms, but it is challenging to maintain trust and security at large scale to cover security measurement at every way of communication through IoT.

### 11.9.2.2 Costing

Budgeting plays an essential role in the success of every business, so participation of IoT and extension of business via IoT smart devices and applications demand high investment cost, which is not feasible for the future of IoT technology and its integration with business applications. Accordingly, extension of IoT network with a large number of people and businesses is very challenging for the growth of business in the future. To maintain costing issues, IoT developers need to design and develop low-cost devices, infrastructure, and communication system for managing the budget [23].

### 11.9.2.3 Interoperability

Interoperability is the core requirement of any IoT network, because IoT combines multiple standards together in parallel for providing real-time on-demand services via smart applications. Therefore, interoperability is essential for IoT which brings a communication platform where multi-vendor technologies and heterogeneous devices can cooperate and communicate with each other without losing its desired functioning, but in the future, IoT demands a high degree of interoperability because every time as users of IoT is growing as such similarly protocols, devices, infrastructure and technology also gets changed and upgraded rapidly.

## 11.9.3 Societal Challenges

IoT provides various types of smart and intelligent applications for serving numerous facilities to society in many aspects. According to the present scenario, IoT covers most of the things of societal needs, but it is very crucial and challenging to serve the best service on demand in the future. - Society needs a high degree of security considerations regarding the use of smartphone applications in which information should be safe from outside attackers.
- IoT technology gains popularity in very less time; therefore, it is always challenging for IoT to protect our society from outside attackers who break the security parameters by hacking some commercial applications for the purpose of transferring and withdrawing money.
