## 11.10 MERITS OF IoT

IoT is the magical era of communication world, and it serves many things in a very smooth manner. Therefore, IoT gains businesses’ and users’ trust in very less time. Most of the businesses and people are using IoT smart applications for different purposes in their daily life’s need. IoT spreads its services to many fields, so it is not easy to cover all the benefits of IoT, but we try to consider the key benefits of IoT in the following.

### 11.10.1 TECHNOLOGICAL ADVANCEMENT

IoT helps multiple technological units to design and develop new things with more advanced requirements such as mechanical design and manufacturing units, medical equipment design and developments, health care manufacturing units, and electronic smart device manufacturing units [24,25]. To do these things, IoT sensors are collecting data as information from many sources and produce a new idea based on the collected data for making more advanced and better development in the future. For example, car designers and manufacturers are collecting data from embedded car sensors to do necessary changes in the next version of a car.

### 11.10.2 EFFICIENT SERVICES AND RESULTS

IoT is a real-time output process technology which works on demand via different IoT platforms, since IoT having variety of sensors to collect, process and generate result for further process, defines its efficient working mechanism, such efficient performance of IoT attract many business platforms and users of the Internet to use IoT technology as an efficient service platform for many business classes.

### 11.10.3 TIME-SAVER

Time is very important for any business or user [26]. Since IoT smart applications can be accessible from anywhere and anytime, such a feature of IoT attracts its users by saving valuable time and helping the business world to achieve the desired objective on time.

### 11.10.4 Resource Utilization and Reducing Wastage

IoT supports heterogeneous technologies to process any task smartly, and the IoT infrastructure suggests to connect many types of living and non-living things to the Internet in a way that utilizes maximum network resources and reduces wastage. Such a feature of IoT defines that IoT initiates its work by offering a low-cost investment for a high result with proper utilization of resources.

### 11.10.5 Decision-Maker

IoT adopts many data analysis tools and techniques to find the best decision for future planning and analysis purposes. In many applications, such types of quality of decision-making feature brings IoT as best tools for many applications which deal with emergency and critical services for best planning and decision-making.

### 11.10.6 Accountability

This feature makes IoT a best service platform for the present and future of Internet services because accountability defines the strength of any business for finding problems, analyzing records, planning and structuring, and ensuring continuous availability of services, i.e., for 24×7 hours.

## 11.11 Demerits Of IoT

IoT is a most demanding and successful technology of today, but behind every success, there is a chain of problems that need to be resolved before a big loss in business and people’s life. The following are the main demerits of IoT:

### 11.11.1 Security Issues

As described in Refs. [27,28], the IoT technology offers services for confidential and non-confidential platforms for businesses and users under its own authentication and security control measurements, but sometimes such measures are not sufficient to maintain security. Users are performing banking operations via smartphone applications with login credentials that can be hacked and accessed by someone else; therefore, security measures and boundaries need to be strong enough for protecting and maintaining a high degree of security measures in IoT.

### 11.11.2 Privacy Issues

IoT applications deal with the user’s personal data, which are processed, exchanged, and stored in an open platform. The users of IoT do not have any information about it, and such private data can be accessed and misused by any third-party service provider; therefore, privacy measures are big issues with the IoT technology.

### 11.11.3 FLEXIBILITY ISSUES

The IoT architecture follows integration of various technologies and cooperation of different communication protocols; therefore, flexibility issues are measurement concerns with IoT technology.

### 11.11.4 COMPLEX MAINTENANCE AND DEPLOYMENT

The IoT architecture is quite complicated because integration of multiple technologies for incorporating active and non-active objects to provide services is not an easy task. The complex architecture may lead to high complexity in deployment and maintenance.

### 11.11.5 CHANCES OF POWER FAILURE AND SHUTDOWN

The IoT involving a group of tiny sensors object having low power backup for participating in process, in this case there is very high chances of power failure in between the process, if it does this may lead the complete system down.

## 11.12 CONCLUSION, FUTURE DEVELOPMENT, AND RESEARCH NEEDS FOR IoT

There is a very common fact that “nothing is complete in the world”. The same concept applies to the IoT technology. IoT serves numerous benefits for businesses and people, although limitations come around with technology, every invention and developments having its own risk and weaknesses, such risk, limitations and weaknesses motivate the developers and scientist to redesign and redeploy the old things and comes with revision as new version of technology with more suitable features.

In this section, we try to present a study regarding the upgradation of today’s version of IoT for the betterment of future of Internet and IoT platform. Here, we discuss the things that need to be considered for future development and research in the area of IoT technology. As we experienced in past researches that the old version of IoT is not considering for higher degree of confidentiality and security therefore the key responsibility of future developers are to redesign and redevelop new standards for IoT platform which may collaborate and carried out the security consideration to make IoT as most secured and trust worthy technology for future business class and people.

As defined in Refs. [29,30], technological development is growing, and we can imagine that in the near future, it might be possible that approximately many trillions of people are part of IoT, which may lead to a big challenge for developers as to how to develop applications that support such a huge number of users.

In the field of hardware devices and circuit development, one needs to develop and carry out the research in the field of nanotechnology because the integration of nanotechnology in IoT with advanced development may lead to big changes in the field of computation. Apart from this research, scientists need to do research for the development and design of chipsets with minimization of size and for empowering the feature of ultra-low-power circuits that resolve the limitation of power consumption.

Research needs to consider the weakness of sensor nodes and to create self-powering and intelligent sensors for making the IoT network stronger. In the field of computational advancement, research needs to focus on the development of IoT for incorporating with 5G network, development of unified multi-frequency communication protocols for long-range communication and to resolve the issues of interoperability with the development of new protocols for a wide network.

In the field of networking technology, research needs to upgrade the network according to IoT deployment with emerging services of self-configuring and self-repairing networking features. Similarly, in the field of software and development, one needs to design and develop new sensor-oriented and sensor-based intelligent, self-healing, self-configuring, and context-aware software [31]. Due to the consideration of various enabling technologies with IoT, there is a big challenge and requirement to carry out the research and development for various operational fields; for example, data and signaling technology, knowledge discovery, data science, big data, data analysis, and machine learning technologies need to be considered for future research and development in the field of “Internet of things”.
