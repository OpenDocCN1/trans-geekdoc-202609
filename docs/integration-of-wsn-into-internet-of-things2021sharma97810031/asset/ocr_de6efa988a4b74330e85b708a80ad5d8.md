# # 13.1 INTRODUCTION

Homo sapiens, the scientific term for modern humans, means “wise man” in Latin. Intelligence is the sole virtue that differentiates man from other entities and allows him to manifest absolute dominion over the rest of the species in the natural world [1]. Intelligence can be understood as the ability to reason, solve problems, think critically, understand and comprehend ideas, manipulate, communicate, predict, self-correct, and respond to stimuli of any kind. Some people even see it as an adaptation to natural selection wherein people started using their brains to solve simple problems to survive. In simple terms, intelligence can be perceived as “the ability of man to use his intellectual capabilities which is marked by the use of logic to solve problems”.


Artificial intelligence, abbreviated as “AI”, takes the intelligence of man to yet another level by allowing him not just to use his intelligence but also to design and create intelligent things [2]. It requires a thorough understanding of man’s behavior—perception of knowledge, its representation, retention in the brain, generation of its solution, emotional behavior, how a stimulus is perceived, processed, and finally transformed into response, and how a pattern is generated which helps man use his critical thinking ability to predict or plan. Incredible changes have taken place in the past two decades in the technology industry, with the tremendously rapid evolution of AI being the key reason behind it. AI encompasses all cognitive tasks that are carried out by non-living entities. It is a very interesting cognitive that is no doubt contributing spectacularly toward the building of intelligent entities that are expected to bring about the ultra-modernization of human lives by advancing all aspects of life—medical [3], sports [4], law [5], transport [6], business [7], and home automation [8], to name a few.


As will be explained in the chapter, the AI/ML-based IoT systems are semi-advancing all the fields of technology and society, and hence, learning about them at least in a generic fashion would help us grow even in our secluded niches. The potential this technology merger holds is not something one can and should look away from as it has proven its utility across all fields and industries. In the future, it will surely change from an add-on skill to a must-have in just a few years.
