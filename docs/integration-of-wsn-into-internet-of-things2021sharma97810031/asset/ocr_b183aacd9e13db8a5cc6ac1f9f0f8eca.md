# 11.8.2 Social Security and Control Applications

The primary objective of social security and control applications is to cover social needs and requirements of people who belong to a small group, a city, or a country. The IoT helps in many aspects of society to serve better security, traffic management, smart traveling, and collection of data in an easiest manner. At present, in various big cities of India, the smart-city project is going on which aims to provide better transportation, real-time on-demand traffic management, and smart parking services remotely, which saves time and cost and, more importantly, is very useful for maximum utilization of resources, for example, the best use of electricity by adopting the concept of smart street lightings that function smartly for on and off operations based on the weather condition; in this way, smart-city projects can save electricity and country resources. Using the GPRS system, people can find the best route using smart traffic control application to reach somewhere on time. Now, various online e-commerce business sites are delivering many products with home delivery facilities. The smart GPRS system makes this practice reliable, easier, and portable for the facility of society. Smart-city projects also work for collecting data of people based on different conditions and categories so that such data can be useful for more advanced and better services in the near future.
