# 11.4 MEANING OF “THINGS” IN THE INTERNET

When we talk about the things that exist in IoT, we can consider all the physical, connected and non-connected objects of the Internet. There are so many things that perform their individual role for the fulfillment of requirement of network, but in the case of IoT, the term “things” means objects in the form of sensors, which provide a seamless way for communication across multiple channels and enable the Internet to facilitate more services to its users, called IoT. All such things that provide connectivity across multiple platforms so consider user electronic devices, remote system, mobiles, smart home appliances, furniture, rooms and so many things will be considerable for exchanging data and enable all non-connected devices to be connected with the Internet for bringing effortless serviceable system with Internet services are called IoT.

The sensor node means that the object is embedded with some programming, electronic circuit, software, or integrated network system. Hardware is working with the aim of extending the Internet connectivity and services to a wider range; in other words, we can say that the IoT helps in extending the internetworking services for large-scale application areas.

Considering the security of things in IoT, the IoT improves Internet services with more security and safety. Now, confidential banking and commercial services are more safely available with multiple security checks, which make the commercial system trustworthy for people. The IoT provides confidential data accessing services with more portability and reliability, and IoT-based applications protect our data with cloud computing and big data management service architectures.

Many things used in the daily life of people are now smartly handled through IoT, such as maintaining the health system and monitoring and tracking things for the observation via virtual advancement in the Internet through various AI algorithms, data collection techniques, and machine learning mechanisms to provide on-time and on-demand medical and other required services at fingertips.
