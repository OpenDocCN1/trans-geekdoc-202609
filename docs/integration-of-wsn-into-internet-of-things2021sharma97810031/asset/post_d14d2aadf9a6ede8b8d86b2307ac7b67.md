
## 13.5 APPLICATIONS

The power of combining AI, ML, and IoT can be understood by the discussion that follows. Here, we have mentioned the real-world applications, given examples of a few already-existing systems, and used novel ideas to better explain how the intersection of these technologies can bring about drastic changes in the lifestyle of the common people.

### 13.5.1 BUSINESS

In this sector, communication is key. The people concerned need to have complete knowledge about their products, know how to increase their sales, and have important information about the other companies in the same field. Instead of going through different sources that might not be authentic, a person can simply use a chatbot to gather all the necessary information [14]. Individual personalization of employees with AI-powered IoT devices helps them maintain a work–life balance that increases their efficiency of performance and thus the output of the task.

Introducing this technology in the human resources (HR) department of any company has the potential to bring about beneficial changes to the company. When the enormous amount of data managed by the HR department manually is managed by AI devices, they drastically reduce the chances of errors. There will also be less pressure on the workers because these trained machines can do the task quickly. In the hiring process of companies, using AI-powered robots for the screening process makes the process more transparent and reliable, as there is no chance of nepotism, and managers can be confident that only capable employees are being hired for the company. This is an example of computational intelligence working with data from various sensors.

### 13.5.2 EDUCATION

Education is the most powerful tool one can ever have. A literate person spreads positivity, leading to the development of a mentally healthy and content society. The intersection of technology with this field is exactly what is needed today. Smart education can be provided by equipping classrooms with smart screens and digital assistants.

Instead of using real specimens for dissection, augmented reality (AR) can give the experience and lessons equivalent to those of real dissections without the usage of real specimens [15]. It is an interactive way of learning, and the students are also under no pressure of spoiling specimens. Biology students who have done the dissection of real specimens can relate to this pretty well. It can bring about immense profits by ending the need to buy costly specimens for each student. This is especially profitable in medical science universities where many medical colleges have to arrange for human corpses for millions of students around the world—a difficult and expensive endeavor.

Another important application can be in correcting students’ examination answer sheets, evaluating marks, and making the final results. This reduces the teachers’ burden since it is all computer-generated. There are also no chances of any mistake in counting the marks or in evaluating the results.

### 13.5.3 VEHICLES AND ROAD SAFETY

With the increasing number of vehicles on roads these days, road safety has become a concern. Tiredness and traffic jams cause drivers to ignore speed limits to finish a task as early as possible. This is a major cause of road accidents. This problem can be easily solved by empowering the vehicle with IoT, AI, and ML facilities [16]. Cars can be embedded with a Global Positioning System (GPS) facility that helps capture real-time data about their location, and sensors connected to the Internet that continuously send their data to the cloud. In a program called “AI for Road Safety”, which aims to maintain road safety, cars are fitted with cameras that capture the instantaneous behavior of the driver and send that information to the cloud. This is done by using emotion recognition, which uses deep learning techniques of ML to recognize the driver’s emotions. In case abnormal driving behavior is expected, notifications are sent to both the driver and the company’s executive concerned.

Another application is self-driving or autonomous cars [17]. These cars have already been tested by Toyota, Nissan, Tesla, Volvo, and a few other companies. They are provided with IoT sensors, actuators, a strong algorithm for deep learning, and AI techniques, which allow them to sense obstacles and quickly get out of the way. They are extremely beneficial for children, old people, and persons with disabilities.

### 13.5.4 MANUFACTURING INDUSTRIES AND PLANTS

IoT with AI and ML in industries has the potential to transform their outputs and aid them to yield better results. Machines such as smart meters continuously transmit data to the managers and field engineers who can monitor their jobs remotely from anywhere. Smart meters can also continuously record the consumption of resources and send the data to the storehouse, i.e., the cloud. Engineers monitor these data and manage and control any blunder effectively. Here, we can see that WSNs can be used on an industrial level and also lend a hand in saving energy.

The industries may also use connected tools and machinery that eliminate the chances of errors made while setting the parameters manually. The use of industrial wearables embedded with sensors such as RFID (radio frequency identification) by the workers drastically improves their safety and work efficiency because they are not prone to any hazardous situation anymore [18]. This greatly improves the quality of work done by them. These technologies may also be used to calculate the risk of working in different environments. If an environment is found to be too dangerous, it can be modified in such a way that it becomes safe to work there. Industrial wearables or smart personal protective equipment (PPE) is always connected to the supervisor’s machine. They can be informed of even the slightest problems on time, and the issue can be sorted out easily [19].

For example, QOOWEAR, a German company, manufactures smart thermal garments for the industry which can automatically change and adjust their temperature according to the surrounding temperature of the environment. ProGloves by Skoda are smart industrial gloves that help the worker identify the parts they are touching, thereby ensuring the use of correct parts. RealWear, a US-based company, has developed a smart wearable device that can be clipped onto the workers’ helmets. It has a camera that helps capture the workers’ steps and also has a built-in voice recognition facility they can use to easily communicate with the supervisor as and when needed. ### 13.5.5 Health Care

Despite great advances in the field of medical science, the workload keeps increasing manifold with each passing day because the population of the world is growing exponentially. Services are also costly, and many people around the globe are unable to access them. Another issue is the lack of proper coordination among the different sectors of the healthcare industry, which sometimes leads to compromises in the quality of caregiving service they provide. Such compromises should not be made in this industry. Thanks to the latest technologies, these problems will soon be solved when IoT backed up with the latest AI and ML techniques is merged with it. Such a healthcare service is known as e-health care or electronic health care. One advantage is that now records can be maintained digitally as electronic health records (EHR) [20], which are stored on the cloud and are accessible to anyone, anywhere, and anytime. Its biggest benefit is that a person need not worry about maintaining their health record files at home (though it is a good practice).
