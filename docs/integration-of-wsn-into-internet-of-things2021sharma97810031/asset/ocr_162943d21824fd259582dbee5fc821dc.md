MANETs are temporary setup networks having a number of characteristic features such as fast deployment, minimum cost, and robustness for mobility [4]. In today’s world, the wireless sensor networks (WSNs) are widely employed in various fields such as military applications, forest monitoring, industries, civilian scenarios, climate monitoring, and indoor living monitoring. There are countless advantages of WSNs due to which they have replaced the conventional networks [5]. The advantages of WSNs are its self-organization, deployment simplicity, expand transference range, versatile arrangement, and scalability. Due to the vast network, the issues of the network’s scalability get expanded. To solve problems related to scalability and energy effectiveness, a hierarchical architecture is introduced. In the hierarchical architecture [6], the whole networks get divided into several layers in which each node accomplished different functions. In WSNs, the very major issue is the energy conservation because the nodes are used in the environment, so a small change or replacement of batteries is not feasible as that disturbs the whole network’s environment [7]. The working functionality of each node helps to build a good network, so even if one node fails to perform its task, then it will disturb the stability of the genuine network. Hence, it is very important for the genuine network that all of its nodes need to work, so that the goal of the network is achieved [8]. The different methods that are used to upgrade the energy utilization rate of the network are clustering, methodical routing, and data accumulation. Today, advanced wireless sensors have been developed for the communications that are very small in size and have a low cost; all this is possible due to the advancement in the current wireless technologies. The wireless sensor nodes can be classified as either sensor nodes or sink nodes based on the different functionalities and performances of the sensors [9]. A ink node is also called the base station that is used for managing the data received on the network and performing data accumulation. The sensor nodes are employed for delivering the sensed information from the environment to other nodes. The wireless sensor nodes consist of battery, memory, transceiver, microsensors, and microprocessors [10]. The evolution of the wireless network can only be achieved when all the nodes of the network gather information for data processing and no node fails while processing of the data [11]. Different routing protocols such as PEGASIS, GAF, LEACH, HEED, and SPIN have been proposed for WSN-based applications [12]. As the WSNs are widely used in different areas, they are categorized into the tracking WSNs and monitoring WSNs. The tracking WSNs are used for tracing of animals, humans, vehicles, and objects [13]. The monitoring applications monitor the inside and outside environment, which comprises physical conditions, structural monitoring, seismic activity, and development [14]. In physical environment conditions, WSNs are also used for data collection. The environment conditions for data collection include the plain sensing field, underground, and undersea [15]. WSNs are also used for detecting air pollution and fires in forests. WSNs are also used in the health monitoring sector as they offer lost-cost devices that are used to assist the senior persons in their daily life.
