# 14.6.1 VIRTUAL REALITY DEVICES/SYSTEMS

Virtual is something that has a real existence but not a physical one. With the application of ICT, the use of software creates a similar effect which can generate realistic images, sounds, and sensations, but actually, it has nothing physical. Virtual reality-based headsets, devices, or systems fitted in a large-sized room can be created where a person using it is able to sense the images, hear the sounds, and feel the sensation of artificially created animated objects. With the help of virtual reality technology, such equipment is being developed, which is helpful in coping with depression, anxiety, and other disorders.

# 14.6.2 Mental Health Apps

Mental health monitoring and treatment apps are used by handheld devices such as mobile phones, tablets, or other portable and lightweight ubiquitous computing devices. These apps help to measure and monitor the patterns of mood, stress, anxiety, and depression in critical conditions. Because of their very small size and portability, their use is very easy and simple.

# 14.6.3 Illness and Self-Management Apps

Illness and self-management apps work by using their inbuilt instructions and information and give feedback on the basis of that to the user or experts for monitoring the behavior and providing treatment to the user. These apps are also helpful in tracking the location, position, state of working, breathing rates, blood pressure, pulse rate, etc., especially during the deteriorated state of mental health. Illness management apps are becoming user-friendly and helpful for the intervention and treatment of addiction, post-traumatic stress disorders, anxiety, insomnia, and depression.

# 14.6.4 Apps for Improving Psychological Processing and Training

By using ubiquitous computing technology, certain apps are being developed to improve an individual's psychological processing such as memory, thinking, learning, sensation, and higher cognitive processes. These apps are also helpful in the training of different psychological phenomena.

# 14.6.5 Data Collection and Symptom Recording Devices

Ubiquitous computing devices fitted with sensors have been developed to record the data relevant to a psychological disorder by sensing the body reactions generated by the disorder. Before the occurrence of a neurotic or psychotic disorder, such as mania, obsessive-compulsive disorder, and depression, changes in behavioral patterns start happening, and these changes can be sensed, recorded, and communicated to the mental health practitioners for remedial actions.
