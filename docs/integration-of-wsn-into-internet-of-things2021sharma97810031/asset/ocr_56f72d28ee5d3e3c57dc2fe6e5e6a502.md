# 13.5.5 Health Care

Despite great advances in the field of medical science, the workload keeps increasing manifold with each passing day because the population of the world is growing exponentially. Services are also costly, and many people around the globe are unable to access them. Another issue is the lack of proper coordination among the different sectors of the healthcare industry, which sometimes leads to compromises in the quality of caregiving service they provide. Such compromises should not be made in this industry. Thanks to the latest technologies, these problems will soon be solved when IoT backed up with the latest AI and ML techniques is merged with it. Such a healthcare service is known as e-health care or electronic health care. One advantage is that now records can be maintained digitally as electronic health records (EHR) [20], which are stored on the cloud and are accessible to anyone, anywhere, and anytime. Its biggest benefit is that a person need not worry about maintaining their health record files at home (though it is a good practice).
