# 11.9.2.2 Costing

Budgeting plays an essential role in the success of every business, so participation of IoT and extension of business via IoT smart devices and applications demand high investment cost, which is not feasible for the future of IoT technology and its integration with business applications. Accordingly, extension of IoT network with a large number of people and businesses is very challenging for the growth of business in the future. To maintain costing issues, IoT developers need to design and develop low-cost devices, infrastructure, and communication system for managing the budget [23].

# 11.9.2.3 Interoperability

Interoperability is the core requirement of any IoT network, because IoT combines multiple standards together in parallel for providing real-time on-demand services via smart applications. Therefore, interoperability is essential for IoT which brings a communication platform where multi-vendor technologies and heterogeneous devices can cooperate and communicate with each other without losing its desired functioning, but in the future, IoT demands a high degree of interoperability because every time as users of IoT is growing as such similarly protocols, devices, infrastructure and technology also gets changed and upgraded rapidly.
