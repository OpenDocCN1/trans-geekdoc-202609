

## 11.4 MEANING OF “THINGS” IN THE INTERNET

When we talk about the things that exist in IoT, we can consider all the physical, connected and non-connected objects of the Internet. There are so many things that perform their individual role for the fulfillment of requirement of network, but in the case of IoT, the term “things” means objects in the form of sensors, which provide a seamless way for communication across multiple channels and enable the Internet to facilitate more services to its users, called IoT. All such things that provide connectivity across multiple platforms so consider user electronic devices, remote system, mobiles, smart home appliances, furniture, rooms and so many things will be considerable for exchanging data and enable all non-connected devices to be connected with the Internet for bringing effortless serviceable system with Internet services are called IoT.

The sensor node means that the object is embedded with some programming, electronic circuit, software, or integrated network system. Hardware is working with the aim of extending the Internet connectivity and services to a wider range; in other words, we can say that the IoT helps in extending the internetworking services for large-scale application areas.

Considering the security of things in IoT, the IoT improves Internet services with more security and safety. Now, confidential banking and commercial services are more safely available with multiple security checks, which make the commercial system trustworthy for people. The IoT provides confidential data accessing services with more portability and reliability, and IoT-based applications protect our data with cloud computing and big data management service architectures.

Many things used in the daily life of people are now smartly handled through IoT, such as maintaining the health system and monitoring and tracking things for the observation via virtual advancement in the Internet through various AI algorithms, data collection techniques, and machine learning mechanisms to provide on-time and on-demand medical and other required services at fingertips. ### 11.4.1 Basic Elements of IoT

To present Internet services virtually, the IoT performs its basic operations with the help of some sensor-based devices. Circuits, traditional network nodes as router, switches, and embedded software-programmed devices such as smartphones, smart watches, smart TVs, and automatic washing machines all work together for the betterment and advancement of services via the Internet technology called IoT. The aim of this section is to introduce its readers about the basic elements of IoT and its utility with IoT.

### 11.4.2 EMBEDDED SOFTWARE-BASED DEVICES

Sensors are devices already programmed with embedded software which help one to collect live data from various surrounding applications and data sources, and such data objects help IoT for information mining, decision-making, and pattern matching, and forecasting of various business decisions on time with more accuracy. At present IoT emerging various communication platforms of monitoring and tracking services with smartphones such as GPRS for location-based tracking operations, other wearable devices helps one to maintain and observe many health measurements like blood pressure level, sugar level, heart rate pulse from time to time with alert notification for further treatment in the present and future; likewise, so many IoT-based smart applications and device-based services make people’s life easier and comfortable.

### 11.4.3 COMMUNICATION MEDIA

To carry data from one place to another, communication media plays an important role for connectivity issues, through which data can be exchanged, transferred, and collected at cloud infrastructure. Communication media will connect all the surrounding sensor nodes from one end to the other to perform IoT operation, and there are many types of media, for example, mobile signals, Bluetooth frequency channels, Wi-Fi signals, and electromagnetic spectrum for wireless radio or microwave low- and high-range communication.

### 11.4.4 OPERATIONS ON DATA OBJECTS

After collecting data objects from multiple sources, various operations on data need to be performed to get the exact information for forecasting of weather and temperature management, and the collected data need to be mined so that our system can protect the life of many people from natural disasters such as earthquakes. To take these measures at run-time, the emerging dynamic services of IoT perform virtual operations on data objects for 24×7 hours in a continuous manner.

### 11.4.5 Graphical User Interface (GUI)

GUI is the key element of IoT through which one can get the information needed whenever someone requires or demands. The availability of information is essential; therefore, we need a user-friendly interface that allows us to process the request and get information in the form of alert or notification for further processing. The best example for such services is the smart-city project maintained via an IoT-based communication system in which all the CCTV cameras are installed around all the main areas of the city, and for easy access of the data for a specific CCTV camera from Web servers using smartphones, one needs an interface.

In some cases, it may also be possible that the user has to go through multiple interfaces to perform a series of processes to achieve the goal. For example, if the user wants to change the setting of his/her refrigerator from any remote location, they can change it anytime by using a cloud-based mobile interface.

### 11.4.6 ACTUATORS

Sensors are essential in IoT. Similarly, actuators are also an important element of IoT. Both are opposite to each other. That is, sensors sense a signal to perform any action, while actuators act upon it. Sensors are always in an active position for detecting any change in the environment, while actuators wait for any alarming trigger to take any action.

To understand the need of sensors and actuators, we can go through an example of a smart air-conditioning system, where we can see that when the sensor senses that there is no person in the area, the actuator will act to stop the flow of air. Based on industry requirements, many applications demand actuators to perform turn-on and turn-off functions along with sensors to improve the result of the application at an advanced level.

### 11.4.7 PROTOCOLS

Protocols are always necessary for a communication system. Without protocols, the reliability, accuracy, security, and efficiency cannot be measured. Standard protocols play a crucial role in IoT; in other words, we can say “protocols are the set of rules and regulations through which a system can manage and control communication function to achieve its objective”.

Protocols help multiple physical objects of IoT to get connected and to transfer and exchange data with security and safety from one end to the other, so the key responsibility of protocols is to provide an effortless platform for communication using some secret language that allows two and more physical objects to connect with security.

For multiple Web and application-level services, the choice of selection of protocols depends on different functions, such as the speed of data transmission from one end to the other, the range of communication, the power consumption level, and operation efficiency. To measure all such factors, we have different types of communication protocols in IoT, for example, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Zigbee, and MQTT. The role of these protocols is to provide smooth communication with security and efficiency at every level of data transmission and data exchange in IoT.
