# 10.1 INTRODUCTION

Security, defined as a state of being free from danger or threat, is derived from the Latin word securus—which means “free from care.” It is defined by the standards put in place to secure something from any kind of danger it may face. Today, we use security in our day-to-day life, be it while emailing, instant messaging, cloud computing, interactions with Internet of Things (IoT) devices, or general interactions with protected domains over the internet.
