
### 8.7.2 Issues of Cybersecurity

The issues that arise due to breach of privacy, accessibility, reliability, verification, etc. in microgrids are usually termed under the umbrella of cybersecurity. To deal with these important and urgent issues, many approaches have been suggested [100]. Some of them include machine learning and vectors, which can collectively be used to tackle the security problems of big data. IoT is another great method that offers a lot of great services, but the lack of stable infrastructure in security and privacy restricts the access that it can provide to this sector [101].

### 8.7.3 Real-Time Data Handling

The existence of progressive analytics and the importance of processing in physical time are important issues, considering the increasing size of data that are generated [102]. This can be dealt with the help of approximate analysis. The estimated question can be used to produce estimated outcomes, also the validity of the outcomes and rejected nodes are both considered important aspects of making a successful analysis.

### 8.7.4 Issues with Deep Technology and Social Media

Machine learning along with big data can be used to solve critical analytical problems along with the help of correct statistical and analytical tools. The probabilities of the values present in data are considered, and statistical approaches are applied to constitute useful data. Similar to these issues, the problems of social media can also be rectified with similar frameworks [23]. Various distinct methods can be used to interlink social media to data from different statements to gain proper accuracy and different viewpoints.

### 8.7.5 Error Handling

The time of reaction for some applications such as identification of errors and transitory fluctuation finding is less than a few milliseconds. The fast and instant service that is expected for the smooth working of such a network is usually provided by cloud computing, but overcrowding of network, complex logic algorithms along with the gargantuan data that must be handled properly, oftentimes then not lead to dormancy [103]. A database that has its foundations in retention is an inappropriate approach for a task like this one, while a database called HANA could be used to develop and distribute power flow.

### 8.7.6 Visualizing the Data

The conception of graphs and various different charts can sometime conjure operators with very loud and unequivocal changes of different voltages and working frequency, thus concocting an effective method to not only depict the trends but also find them between the data that have their origin in multiple sources [104]. This is a difficult task and can be a source of further research; some of the important topics involve graphing algorithms, mining of data, etc.

## 8.8 CONCLUSION

Big data is a field that expands exponentially on a daily basis. Its reach and applications expand to various remote technologies with every new improvement introduced. It is believed that big data now is at a stage comparable to that of the internet in the 1990s, with the capacity to exceed far beyond what the dot net boom ever did. The applications of big data in WSNs require a very holistic blend of resources with an energy-conscious approach so as to overcome the present biggest challenges for big data. In this chapter, we discussed the possibilities of opportunities that big data provides in microgrids, along with a conclusive survey on various aspects of the integration of big data in various fields. Data generating sources for grids, various different applications along with their appropriate advanced tools, prospects involving error detection, maintaining the solidity of grid, and ideal design for power units, etc. are the backbone of this survey. Certain big data problems along with their proposed solutions approach have also been introduced, namely call dropping and data storing in real time. This flourishing field will continue to have a reasonable impact in many fields as more and more data continue to be gathered and processed; new and unique data will always demand novel and quick analysis from the foundations of big data processing.
