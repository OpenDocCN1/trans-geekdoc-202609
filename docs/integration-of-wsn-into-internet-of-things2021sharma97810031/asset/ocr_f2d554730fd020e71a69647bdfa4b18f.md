# ## 13.4 INTERNET OF THINGS

# ### 13.4.1 Meaning of IoT

Let us talk about your smartphone. What are the things it can do? It can be used to call, text, and stream any available content, be it songs, movies, books, etc. How does it carry out that task? Through the Internet, of course. Without the Internet, it is a device with limited capabilities. Connecting things to the Internet yields many amazing benefits, as we can clearly see for our electronic devices such as smartphones, laptops, and tablets. This also applies to everything else, from the electrical devices and appliances you own, or some other asset of yours, literally everything. The concept of IoT is pretty simple, i.e., to take everything and connect it to the Internet [13]. All the confusion about IoT is based on the fact that its definition and idea are so broad and loosely bound, giving rise to a multitude of examples and possibilities, making it difficult to grasp as one concept. To understand this, we should first know why connecting everything to the Internet matters.

# ### 13.4.2 Importance of IoT

Being connected to the Internet provides things with the ability to send or receive information, or do both. This ability gives things the tag of “smart”, for example, smartphones, smart TVs, and smart home, because they have exceeded their limitations of what they can do and how they do it by connecting to the Internet. Again, let us consider our smartphones. Now, we can listen to any song we want on our phones. It is not because we have all of them stored on the device, as we cannot store all the songs in the world. This is where the Internet comes to the rescue. How does it work? Because every song in the world is stored on some device that can send information (in this case, the song) when asked for it by a device such as our own phone; it exchanges information that the Internet provides us to allow us to listen to the song. This ability to exchange information is what the Internet provides us with. Thus, even a device as tiny as our smartphone can access all the potential of a high-capacity storage device or a supercomputer with just a tap. That is the power of things connected to the Internet, and hence, that is the power of the IoT. Everything that is being connected to the IoT or the Internet in general falls into three categories, all of which have their roles and benefits and also positively impact each other:

+   - Things that collect and send information
+   - Things that receive information and then act on it
+   - Things that can do both
+   - Things that can collect and send information:

These devices are called sensors. Just like our sensory organs that collect information from our surroundings and send it to our brain to process, these sensors also collect information from the environment. It could be temperature, humidity, moisture, light, wind speed, etc. Now, when we link these sensors to the Internet, they become a more useful tool.
