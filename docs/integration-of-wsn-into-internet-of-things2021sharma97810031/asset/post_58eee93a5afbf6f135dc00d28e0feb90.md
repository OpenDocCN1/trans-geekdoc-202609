### 12.1 INTRODUCTION

Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) is defined as a network of industrial systems that is highly connected and quite intelligent; also it is used to extract high production rate with efficient cost control by efficient management and real-time monitoring [1]. Internet of Things (IoT) is used to link the electrical devices with server and communication without any human intervention. The main idea of IIoT is to make good use of the IoT systems in industrial domain. They interact and perform real-time monitoring of the devices and real-time data analysis and collection, and also gather information about the entire event that took place in the system [2]. The use of IoT technology in industrial domain helps in the enhancement of security and network intelligence and also in industrial automation processes [3]. IIoT stores large data and then uses it later for the purpose of management of time, cost control, and predictive maintenance. IIoT comes under IoT, which needs a reliable, safe, and secure communication in the system. Although users can easily access their devices, the devices are more prone to attacks such as active and passive attacks [4].
