### 11.1.5 According to IETF

The Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF) is one of the most dedicated organizations having multiple vendors, designers, and scientists working together for the future of Internet technology, the evolution of the Internet has been invented from IETF which also offering any individual to participate and present his research for the advancement of networking technology, in the year of 2010, IETF defines the term IoT as “Internet” and “things” as the actual idea behind IoT is the communication environment which enables multiple electrical, electronic, and non-electrical/ non-electronic objects of network are participating to one another in seamless way to perform networking operations without additional efforts.

### 11.1.6 According to OASIS

The Organization for the Advancement of Structured Information Standards (OASIS) is one of the most nonprofit standards organizations for information and communication technology and is dedicated to the development and management of security standards for IoT, cloud computing, big data, and other management technologies. The OASIS is open to adapt new things from any individual, and the OASIS released a report in 2014 in which it defines IoT as “a system which connects the Internet to physical world elements via sensor-based tools and technology in the form of multiple available objects such as mobiles, watches, rooms, pens, laptop, buildings, and any home-based sensors and on, under, and outside considerable earth sensors” [6].

### 11.1.7 According to W3C

The World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) is dedicated to the development of Web standards. The W3C offers a user-friendly environment for its own staff members and outside public members to work together for the design and development of Web standards. It defines IoT as “Web of things (WoT)”, where Web is defined as how Web elements are connected with multiple active and non-active objectives of communication to facilitate the services at the application level so that the physical things can be represented via virtual representation to carry out the application-based Web services.
