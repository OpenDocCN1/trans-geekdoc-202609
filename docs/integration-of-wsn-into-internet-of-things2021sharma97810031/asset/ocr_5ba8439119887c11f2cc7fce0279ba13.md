# ### 13.5.3 VEHICLES AND ROAD SAFETY

With the increasing number of vehicles on roads these days, road safety has become a concern. Tiredness and traffic jams cause drivers to ignore speed limits to finish a task as early as possible. This is a major cause of road accidents. This problem can be easily solved by empowering the vehicle with IoT, AI, and ML facilities [16]. Cars can be embedded with a Global Positioning System (GPS) facility that helps capture real-time data about their location, and sensors connected to the Internet that continuously send their data to the cloud. In a program called “AI for Road Safety”, which aims to maintain road safety, cars are fitted with cameras that capture the instantaneous behavior of the driver and send that information to the cloud. This is done by using emotion recognition, which uses deep learning techniques of ML to recognize the driver’s emotions. In case abnormal driving behavior is expected, notifications are sent to both the driver and the company’s executive concerned.

Another application is self-driving or autonomous cars [17]. These cars have already been tested by Toyota, Nissan, Tesla, Volvo, and a few other companies. They are provided with IoT sensors, actuators, a strong algorithm for deep learning, and AI techniques, which allow them to sense obstacles and quickly get out of the way. They are extremely beneficial for children, old people, and persons with disabilities.

# ### 13.5.4 MANUFACTURING INDUSTRIES AND PLANTS

IoT with AI and ML in industries has the potential to transform their outputs and aid them to yield better results. Machines such as smart meters continuously transmit data to the managers and field engineers who can monitor their jobs remotely from anywhere. Smart meters can also continuously record the consumption of resources and send the data to the storehouse, i.e., the cloud. Engineers monitor these data and manage and control any blunder effectively. Here, we can see that WSNs can be used on an industrial level and also lend a hand in saving energy.

The industries may also use connected tools and machinery that eliminate the chances of errors made while setting the parameters manually. The use of industrial wearables embedded with sensors such as RFID (radio frequency identification) by the workers drastically improves their safety and work efficiency because they are not prone to any hazardous situation anymore [18]. This greatly improves the quality of work done by them. These technologies may also be used to calculate the risk of working in different environments. If an environment is found to be too dangerous, it can be modified in such a way that it becomes safe to work there. Industrial wearables or smart personal protective equipment (PPE) is always connected to the supervisor’s machine. They can be informed of even the slightest problems on time, and the issue can be sorted out easily [19].

For example, QOOWEAR, a German company, manufactures smart thermal garments for the industry which can automatically change and adjust their temperature according to the surrounding temperature of the environment. ProGloves by Skoda are smart industrial gloves that help the worker identify the parts they are touching, thereby ensuring the use of correct parts.
