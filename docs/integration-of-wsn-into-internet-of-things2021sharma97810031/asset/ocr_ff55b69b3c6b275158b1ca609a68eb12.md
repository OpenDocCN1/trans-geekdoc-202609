# 14.5 UBIQUITOUS COMPUTING: REMEDIAL PROSPECT FOR MENTAL DISORDERS

Ubiquitous computing technology has opened new paradigms in support of data collection, record maintenance, information sharing, effective treatment, and management of mental health issues. Ubiquitous devices such as mobile phones, tablets, and other handheld equipment are assisting the community; persons with psychological disorders; medical professionals involved in the treatment and management of mental illnesses; and researchers to acquire, process, understand, and create information to further share it for monitoring, predicting, and improving the deteriorated psychological state of persons with mental health issues [22]. Ubiquitous computing devices connected with local/worldwide networking can be the simplest but very effective means for communicating the information to contact a crisis center of mental health provider professional on appropriate time to get desired help. Ubiquitous technology in the form of apps can be used in extremely sophisticated mobile phones, tablets, or palmtops [23]. These inbuilt apps of ubiquitous devices can be used as sensors to collect data and process it as useful information to identify, understand, and predict typical behavior patterns of persons with mental illness. If the inbuilt app of a ubiquitous device senses changes in the patterns of behavior of its user, it can record these changes, and simultaneously, these changes can be communicated to the professional expert before something untoward happens [24]. ICT, especially ubiquitous computing, is a very recent introduction. ICT and ubiquitous computing have started intervening in all the activities and processes of psychological disorder management. A number of software- and hardware-based supports have been developed to provide both virtual and physical aid for the treatment and management of mental health issues.
