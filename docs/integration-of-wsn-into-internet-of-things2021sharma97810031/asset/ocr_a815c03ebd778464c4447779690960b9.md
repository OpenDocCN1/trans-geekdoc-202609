With the invention of the fire, wheel, waterwheel, pozzolana (bricks and mortar),
compass, clock, steam engine, electricity, agricultural tools and equipment, vaccines,
medicines, and surgery, the evolutionary process of modern technological and mate-
rialistic advancement progressed in the primitive times [1]. By the 17th century, the
pace of technological development gradually started picking up, and later in the 18th
and 19th centuries, a lot many inventions and discoveries contributed to a remarkable
advancement in all the spheres of development. But still, the tempo of technoologi-
cal advancement and discoveries during this period was very slow, and it had been
happening only once a while in years. The 20th century recorded the regular and
frequent phenomenon of technological development, and in the later part of the 20th
century, the occurrence of technological innovation, via means of new inventions
and discoveries, in all the sectors of development became very frequent. The closure
of the 20th and the beginning of the 21st century is known as an era of “techno-
advancement explosion.” Technological innovations in this period became the cut-
throat competitor for the existing version of its own last generation of technology.
The pace of advancement was so quick that in some of the cases, even the birth of a
typical technology could not take place because of the arrival of another advanced
innovation in the field [2]. In modern times, matching up with the pace of technologi-
cal advancement has become very tedious and difficult.

During the last 20–25 years, advancement in technology has evolved in such a
way that completely new and different types of technologies have come into exis-
tence. Computer and information technology is one such field that has just recently
originated but developed to a very advanced stage. This technology has not only
contributed significantly to its own development but also drastically influenced and
intervened, nearly, among all other fields of technology and development. Computer
technology is growing and developing at such a fast pace that it has created a kind
of confusion about which version of computer technology is the latest. Before the
arrival of any latest version of computer technology, its next generation is ready to
reach the market. Innovation and advancement in computer technology are so fast
and vast that even the experts in the discipline could not match up with the pace of
advancement in the field. Computer technology has direct intervention among the
rest of all the fields of technologies. As a result, the intervention of computer technol-
ogy in other fields of technology and disciplines of study is multifold; it has totally
changed the present working status of humans and machines. Computer technology
has become an interface between humans and machines because it is quick, easy to
use, and portable and can be blended with any other form of technology. The blending
of computer technology with other technologies has improved the productivity,
effectiveness, and efficiency of both humans and machines.
