### 2.1 INTRODUCTION

Wireless sensor networks (WSNs) are becoming increasingly important due to their flexibility of usage, installation, and modification. WSNs comprise sensor nodes connected wirelessly using specific protocols for communication purposes. This type of network falls under the ad hoc wireless network [1]. WSNs had a potential usage in the military at first. Later, they were integrated with other devices [2]. They have become one of the integral parts of home automation and industrial needs. In the industry, they are used to collect critical environment information for the decision-making process and data analysis. Nowadays, all the devices are connected, and they are being checked and observed continuously using sensor devices. These devices provide contextual information. Sensors are connected to the internet using various tools, for example, mobile devices, and Internet of Things (IoT) devices.

A considerable amount of data are collected using these sensors from the environment and components. Among these enormous data, there remains confidential, private, and sensitive information [3]. Before storing the sensor data to the storage devices in our workstation or data center, they have to travel through several nodes. With the rapid development of the Internet with its interconnected devices, including standalone sensors as well as embedded systems, data generation is on the rise [4].

Potential attackers target the network and its devices for information. Security threats are increasing and becoming more complex to determine and prevent. Due to this variety, veracity, and versatility of attack surfaces, the traditional security measures are becoming outdated. Increasingly advanced threats toward information technology (IT) infrastructures have made the security countermeasures more challenging as the organization has to protect itself against all the attacks. On the other hand, the attacker needs one successful attempt to breach the security.

Due to the widespread use of WSNs, researchers have actively engaged themselves in this arena. Security attacks are very crucial to any infrastructure that is connected to the internet. There have been a good number of previously published works related to WSN fundamentals, attacks, and countermeasures. For example, Dewal et al. [5] describe security issues, challenges, and defensive measures very briefly. Dhakne et al. [6] focused on a detailed analysis of attacks and countermeasures in various layers of WSNs. Moreover, attacks were classified in different categories for a concise representation. Similarly, Xiaomei et al. [7] provided a precise description of security threats and their countermeasures categorically, including various secure routing techniques. Patil et al. [8] and Chen et al. [9] focused explicitly on characteristics and potential attack scenarios of denial of service (DoS) attacks in WSNs, including their countermeasures, though Chen et al. expressly concentrated on IPv6 protocols.
