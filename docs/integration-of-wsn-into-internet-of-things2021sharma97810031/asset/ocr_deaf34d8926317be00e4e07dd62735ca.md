Such applications provide a good graphical user interface to farmers so that they can see the images and predict the problems. It leads to smart agriculture as it also suggests the optimal techniques and solutions to fix the reported problem. The implementation of deep conventional neural network to monitor cassava plants has already been made [11]. It identifies three types of crop diseases and two types of pest damages [12,13].

The sensor data and satellite data are analyzed to make future predictions and to build decision support systems. The most popular use of such predictions is associated with assess the weather and crop sustainability and to identify the pest and disease. The use of UAVs (drones) to gather information on vast acres of land within a short period is appropriate. With processing the data sensed, crop health can be improved, and cost of farming can be reduced [14]. The decision support system is also implemented in the “smart irrigation system.” It is a WSN-based smart system that monitors soil properties and weather conditions to regulate the irrigation and automatically sprinkles water on the crops [15]. Table 7.1 gives a summary of various field activities that can be supported by WSNs to enable smart agriculture.

In this way, our farmers are enabled to deal with natural disasters, climate conditions, and constant soil/crop monitoring with the intelligent information systems supported by WSNs in association with ML, IoT, and AI. Smart agriculture is a solution toward an improved and efficient agricultural dimension. The agricultural activities include a range of tasks from plowing the fields through monitoring the growth of crops to the sale of the products in an efficient way.

TABLE 7.1
Summary of Activities in Smart Agriculture

| Application Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| Crop water management [16] | Smart agriculture utilizes the IoT sensors to gather climate and soil parameters, and ML is used to make decisions. Actuators and relay are used to automatically on/off the water pump. In this way, constant monitoring of climate, soil, and crop enables the automated irrigation system. It ensures the proper water availability and in turn reduces the wastage of water resources. |
| Precision agriculture [17] | The weather and climate information must be accurate so that the crop damage due to disaster can be minimized. Smart agriculture updates the farmers with live data feed constantly and forecast the danger, if any. |
| Integrated pest management or control [18] | Smart agriculture constantly monitors the crops using sensors and a camera (via drones or satellites) and informs the farmers about when and how much pesticide is needed. |
| Food production and safety [19] | Smart agriculture allows to monitor the warehouse location, its temperature, facilities of shipping, and transportation to the storage warehouse and also records all the feed to the cloud and ensures the safety of the products. |
