### Stemming and Lemmatising

Stemming is the method of transforming inflected words to their word stem or the root form. A large number of similar origin words are converted to the root. E.g., words like” player”,” playing”,” play” are based on “play.” This benefits in achieving the training process with better accuracy. Lemmatising is the process of grouping together the inflected forms. `Stemmer` and `Lemmatizer` were imported from `nltk`.

*   ![Figure978-1-7998-7776-9.ch001.g36](i/ch001.g36.png)

### Applying Count Vectorizer

The purpose of using `Count Vectorizer` is to convert a string of words into a matrix of words. Column headers have the words themselves and the cell values signify the frequency of occurrence of the word(Vectorizer, 2021). Therefore, we passed the custom list of stop words performed earlier as the parameter with default values for ‘lowercase’ and ‘regular expression’.

## Step 5: Classifications Algorithms

To examine the performance of our algorithms, we split our balanced data set into two pieces (80/20). To avoid overfitting, we, therefore, used the k-fold cross-validation. This means that we separate our training data into k parts (folds) and then apply the model on k-1 folds before making predictions for the kth hold-out fold. Then repeat this method for every single fold and equate the resulting predictions.
