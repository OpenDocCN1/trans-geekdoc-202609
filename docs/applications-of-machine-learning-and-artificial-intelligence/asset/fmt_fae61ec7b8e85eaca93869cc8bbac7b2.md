
# 21st Century Learning and Instruction: DL-Backed ADDIE Integration

The modes and models of learning and instruction in the 21st century entered the remote learning era around a decade ago. As a result, this brings the challenge of successful integration and implementation of remote learning into existing systems and introduces the concept of eLearning management systems. On the other hand, Deep Learning (DL) has been impacting every aspect of human lives since the beginning of the 21st century. eLearning systems are no exception to this impact. Consequently, DL-backed ADDIE can add value to the modes and models of learning and instruction in the 21st century.

ADDIE can play a significant role in 21st-century modes and models of learning and instruction. Instructors and students are the key audiences in this model. Hence, the authors will advance the analysis considering the effect of ADDIE on them alongside the challenges involved in implementing ADDIE efficiently. The following sections and subsections highlight and explain ADDIE-aided instruction and learning modes and models, their impacts, and applications.

## Background

There are existing online learning services such as Moodle, Jenzabar, and Blackboard (Community M., 2021; Community J., 2021; Community B., 2021). These services provide basic online learning management services for institutions, instructors, and students (e.g., Fig 1, 2). Instructors can upload course materials, recorded class sessions, and discussion topics. Students can also view course materials and recorded sessions. As a result, these online learning services have become an integral part of the 21st-century remote learning model. Moreover, these eLearning management systems provide analytical services associated with learners' enrollment, engagement, and retention rates. For example, Florida Southern College manages recruitment and retention data with Jenzabar Analytics (Community J., 2021). Blackboard Analytics provides deep insights for various data analytics and predictions to increase effectiveness in enrollment management, recruitment and retention, instructional design, and identification of at-risk students (Community B., 2021).

However, these systems lack ADDIE-aided online learning modes and models, which is where the motivation for incorporating such ADDIE-aided services originates. Several studies support such incorporation, as suggested by (Muniasamy & Alasiry, 2020), (Engelbrecht, 2003), and (Martens, Gulikers, & Bastiaens, 2004). Inspired by the studies, applications, and implications of DL, the authors aim to provide guidelines for incorporating such services in this chapter.

ADDIE will incorporate DL services into existing remote learning systems. DL services should be consistent with the modes and models of the learning systems for better impact. Today's DL technologies have applications in almost all aspects of human lives (Deng & Yu, 2014; Zhou, Zhang, Liu, Qiu, & He, 2019; Mahdavifar & Ghorbani, 2019; Zhu, Xie, Han, & Guo, 2020). DL technologies can help advance data-driven analysis more efficiently than existing methods through different DL technologies such as Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN). Data-driven analysis can impact decisions associated with predicting weather, supply-chain relationships, stock prices, diseases, translating languages, etc.—almost all aspects of human lives!

## ADDIE Aided Instruction

The modes and models of instruction have been changing since the beginning of the 21st century due to the advancement of internet technology (Agarwal & Day, 2020; Banica, 2017; Hirschheim, 2005). The remote access service provided by this technology allows easy, 24/7 access to the eLearning system. DL-backed services on top of this remote access can have a significant impact on the system. The authors define ADDIE-aided instruction modes and models as instructions governed and supported by different DL-backed services included in ADDIE. The services included in ADDIE for instruction can be enhanced search results for different topics, student performance management, and data security management, which can impact the overall instruction modes and models as follows.

### Enhanced Search Result by the ADDIE

Everyone searches for specific information on the internet nowadays. Instructors are no exception. They need to search and summarize search results for analysis, discussion, and presentation. ADDIE can provide enhanced search results for any course-related topic by incorporating state-of-the-art deep learning-backed search engines into the online learning management system (e.g., Fig 1). This search engine can search different websites for a given topic to find the application and implication of the topic in different resources, websites, and domains. As a result, instructors along with students can have up-to-date information and knowledge of the same topic in different domains.

The technologies involved in the multidimensional search topic include the web crawler, which can scan or crawl through web pages to create an index of the data it is set to look for (NT, 2021). Finally, DL models on top of the indices can recommend the similarity of the search topic in different domains (Rahman, 2020). As a result, instructors can search and receive results for the topic of interest in different web pages and summarize the results on the course portal for students. This is very interesting and beneficial for students as they can understand the subject matter from different perspectives. For example, students from the Computer Science department can find the application and implications of different programming languages in different domains. Similarly, students from the Language department can learn different languages simply by looking at search results translated into the respective languages.
