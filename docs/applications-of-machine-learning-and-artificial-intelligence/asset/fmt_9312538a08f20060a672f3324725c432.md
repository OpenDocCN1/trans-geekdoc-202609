
# Mahbubur Rahman

Mahbubur Rahman is an Assistant Professor in the department of Computer Science at North American University, and has been working here since January 2020. He completed his Master’s in Computer Science from Texas Tech University in December 2010, and earned a PhD degree in Computer Science from the University of Houston in May 2015. He worked as a postdoctoral fellow at the University of Texas Medical Branch for two years after receiving his PhD degree. He then worked as a software engineer in California for two years.

Dr. Rahman has extensive research and development experience in the fields of machine learning, data mining, and algorithm optimization both within the industry and academia. During his industry research career, he developed a novel approach to extracting the distinguishable features from the unstructured data sets and did a multidimensional analysis on the structured data sets in order to find out interesting groups. Dr. Rahman has significantly contributed to academic research, as well. He introduced a data modeling-enabled machine learning algorithm that can enhance the predictability of the algorithms. His cluster-based prediction model developed at the postdoctoral research level is being used in different research projects. He is applying these deep learning models to optimize the algorithms, along with their conversions, to different programming environments. Dr. Rahman has published five journals and two conference papers so far in his field of research. He is very much interested in exploring the applications of machine learning and artificial intelligence in Education.

# Faruk Taban

Faruk Taban received his M.Sc. in Materials Engineering from the University of Southern California and Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Nevada. He has taught at the Western Nevada College and the University of Denver. Since 2017, he has been the Provost and VP for Academic Affairs at the North American University (NAU). In addition, Dr. Taban is currently serving as the Interim President of NAU.

# Yetkin Yildirim

Yetkin Yildirim is the Director of STEM Projects at Rice University School Mathematics Project (RUSMP). Dr. Yildirim received his Ph.D. in Civil Engineering in 2000 from the University of Texas at Austin and is registered by the State of Texas as a professional engineer. Dr. Yildirim served as the president of Harmony Public Schools between 1999 and 2002. Dr. Yildirim has also served as the Director of the Engineering Education Research Center (EERC), where he conducted research regarding new developments in model-based learning activities in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math (STEM) education. He served as the director of the Texas Pavement Preservation Center (TPPC) at the University of Texas at Austin for ten years and served as a faculty member in the civil engineering department at UT Austin for fifteen years.
