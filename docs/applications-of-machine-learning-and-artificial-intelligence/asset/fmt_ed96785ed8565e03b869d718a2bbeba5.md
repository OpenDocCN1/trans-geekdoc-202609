
Some learners require continuous assistance. However, it is not possible for the mentors. The DLA can play a vital role providing continuous assistance to the respective students. While assisting, the DLA can identify at-risk students and recommend corresponding measures to succeed to the students. In this way, the DLA can provide feedback related to the performance of the students to the enhanced student performance management system at the instructors’ end. The importance of the DLA has been recommended in several studies as (Brock & Smith, 2007), (Myles, Ferguson, & Hagiwara, 2007) (Chatterley & Chojecki, 2010).

### Digital mentoring assistance by the ADDIE

The digital mentoring assistance (DMA) can provide the students continuous mentoring service, that includes easy to understand summary of difficult concepts, visual representations, simulations of process, course contents translated into the students’ own language. The DL is bringing revolution in the natural language processing (e.g., `NLP`) (T. Young, 2018) (D. W. Otter, 2021). Similar types of DL backed NLP services can be integrated with the ADDIE for mentoring assistance in the student’s own language (e.g., Fig 6). The DMA can provide 24/7 mentoring assistance to the students. As a result, this can improve the overall performance of the students.

### Digital career assistance by the ADDIE

A successful career requires a successful training, mentoring. Hence, the 21st century modes and models of learning should have such career mentoring, assistance services. The digital career assistance (e.g., DCA) can provide 24/7 career related information, guidelines to the students. Each student has specific career goal. The DCA can be configured in such a way that it can provide student specific career objectives, suggestions, recommendations etc. The instructors can also get information on the demanding careers from the DCA and then design the course curriculum to meet the demand.

The DCA can be connected to the job portals (Job Search | Indeed, 2021) (LinkedIn, 2021) so that immediate notifications can be sent out to the students matching their interests, skills. The student career service in the institution can work in parallel with the DCA to provide more enhanced career assistance to the students.

The resume plays a significant role in successful job interviews. The DCA can have resume critique service integrated such that the students can receive necessary recommendations, critiques to update their resumes. The DL backed services in the DCA can classify good or bad resumes and then provide necessary recommendations to have a good resume (Cocarascu & Toni, 2018) (Sun & Vasarhelyi, 2018).

The DL based video analysis is an emerging field (Nishani & Çiço, 2017) and by incorporating such services, the ADDIE can be more beneficial to the future career endeavor of the students. The students can prepare recorded mock interview sessions and then upload the session to the DCA. The DCA can provide feedbacks on the recorded video sessions of the mock interviews by the students. The students can improve their interview skills depending on the feedback from the DCA. In this way, the ADDIE can contribute to the career advancement of the students in every other way.

## CHALLENGES ASSOCIATED WITH THE ADDIE

The ADDIE can provide invaluable services to the online learning platform as it has been explained above. The instructors, students and other respected professionals can benefit from such services. The implementation, maintenance of the ADDIE can create many opportunities in the respected fields contributing to the employment sectors as well. The educational personnel can work in the state-of-the-art learning technology system. The learners can also learn the application of such technologies in the 21st century modes and models of learning.

However, it has challenges as well. The challenges include proper implementation, management of the ADDIE. The implementation of the ADDIE requires expertise in the DL, software, hardware technologies. The DL requires enough data to be trained on. This data requires proper management by the database server, data center etc. The management of such services also requires expertise in the DL, cloud related technologies (Shi, 2021) (Zhang, Yan, Chen, & Li, 2018) (Bisong, 2019). Finally, the corresponding organizations need to allocate necessary funding for the development, deployment and maintenance of the ADDIE based eLearning management systems. The authors provided more in-depth analysis of the challenges and respective measures to resolve as below.

### Challenges associated with the enhanced search and DLA

The enhanced search service requires multiple data sources connected, processed. This is a complex process that requires searching engine design, development, and deployment in the ADDIE. Additionally, the cost associated with the incorporation of commercial web crawlers can limit the searching analysis (Mirtaheri, et al., 2014).

On the other hand, the DLA, and other associated agents (e.g., DMA, DCA etc.) receive inputs from the enhanced search engine and then display this to the learners. As a result, the enhanced search engine needs to have multiple connectivity features included to ensure concurrent access by the instructors, learners. All these challenges can be overcome by proper planning, accurate implementation, maintenance of the DL backed search engines following the available guidelines. The respected institutions can have discussions with the information technology department on the proper implementation, maintenance of such DL backed services in their systems.

### Challenges associated with the enhanced student performance management by the ADDIE

The enhanced student performance management system requires enough data to provide student specific recommendation, plagiarism analysis (Najafabadi, et al., 2015). As a result, the ADDIE needs to process, manage student record efficiently to provide such recommendations. Additional data base server needs to be incorporated to maintain, process such analysis. It might require additional budget to maintain such servers.

On the contrary, there are open-source servers that can be used to manage the student performance (Ven K., 2012) (Ven & Verelst, 2011) and other recommended services. Once the ADDIE can access to the student data in the server, then it can analyze and provide student specific recommendations. The DL services incorporated in the ADDIE can provide such recommendations.
