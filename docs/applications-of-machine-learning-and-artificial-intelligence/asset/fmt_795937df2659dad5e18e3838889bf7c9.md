
## Challenges Associated with Enhanced Data Security Management by the ADDIE

It is challenging to make the distributed system secured (Y Xin, 2018). The potential hackers and intruders can attempt to steal the identity of the students along with their financial credentials. However, appropriate security measures implemented in the eLearning system can reduce such cyberattacks. The security measures include strong password creation, multifactor authentication (Ometov, et al., 2018), and other necessary security service integration in the eLearning system.

The ADDIE services are data driven. The data requires protection against cyberattacks. The cyberattacks can occur anytime. Hence, 24/7 security service is also required to manage the security of the data (Singh, Jeong, & Park, 2020). The existing network and system administrators can be trained on the DL backed security services to integrate and manage such services in the eLearning system.

## Challenges Associated with Digital Assistance by the ADDIE

The authors explained different types of digital assistance by the ADDIE above. The most challenging part of such digital assistance is its acceptability to the target audience. The learners are used to receiving assistance from humans. The digital assistance by the ADDIE is a new concept. The concept can be made popular to the learners by providing 24/7 continuous efficient requested service. The educational professionals should receive proper guidelines on how to operate such digital assistances efficiently.

Another challenge might arise from the permission of using the data to provide the decisions from the ADDIE. Special protocols associated with the access privilege of the data might be required to be published and agreed by the interested personnel. This might require additional training and awareness in the respected individual. The multidimensional search engine by the ADDIE can be configured such that it can recommend relevant protocols and agreements for the audience to overcome such challenges.
