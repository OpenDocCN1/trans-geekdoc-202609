
## Enhanced student performance management by the ADDIE

The existing eLearning systems provide necessary course works (e.g., assignments, quizzes, discussions etc.) related with the courses. Some students perform better while others may not in the courses. Depending on the performance of the students in the courses, the students can be classified as outstanding or at-risk as well. The ADDIE can analyze the performance of the students by applying state-of-art DL classifier (Zhang S. a., February 2019) technology to classify the students in different classes and recommend corresponding actions (e.g., tutor service, deadline extension for the course works etc.) to the responsible personnel. As a result, the ADDIE can provide better student performance management system, that can impact the student success rate in the courses.

The DL technologies can be integrated with the recommendation systems, that can provide specific recommendation for specific students while analyzing their performances (Mu, 2018). The remote learning systems can provide the data related with the performance of the students to the ADDIE to design the recommendation systems. The DL backed performance monitoring systems have been gaining popularity in other disciplines as well (Sundaravadivel, Kesavan, Kesavan, Mohanty, & Kougianos, 2018) (Yolcu, Oztel, & Bunyak, 2019). Hence, it can impact on the 21^(st) century modes and models of the learning.

The instructors need to spend sometimes to grade the course works. It requires significant amount of time. The ADDIE can provide recommended grade for the submitted assignment by incorporating the natural language processing (e.g., NLP) service in the system. The NLP can be trained on the given rubrics for grading the assignments and then incorporated in the system to enhance the grading. It can provide better grade for the correct submission than the incorrect one. On the other hand, each institution has policies regarding the plagiarism. The ADDIE can provide the instructors DL backed plagiarism services (e.g. (Community T., 2021)) to handle such plagiarism related policies efficiently. As a result, the ADDIE can play an important role in the institutional policies, effective plans.

The instructors, students with the disabilities can get benefit from the services provided by the ADDIE as discussed above. The existing DL services can be customized to provide instructional assistance to the disabled individuals. As a result, the ADDIE can play important role for determining the institutional policies of the disability.

## Enhanced data security management by the ADDIE

The security breach can occur and create threats for the eLearning management systems as well. The ADDIE can analyze the existing network data traffics and detect possible cyber-attacks and take immediate necessary action. Today’s deep learning algorithms can provide inferential outcome, causal effects, while analyzing the data (Ke & Xie, 2009) (Batmaz, Yurekli, Bilge, & Kaleli, 2019). This technology can be integrated with the eLearning management system to analyze the network traffic, access and then, detect possible intruder, hacker, denial of service attack (Ferrag, Maglaras, Moschoyiannis, & Janicke, 2020) (Berman, Buczak, Chavis, & Corbett, 2019). As a result, the existing data in the system can be more secured.

The data is the center of analysis in the DL techniques. However, the data needs to be secured for various reasons as reducing the unprivileged accesses, cyberattacks, well-being of the organizations etc. The remote access feature of the 21st century modes and models of the learning also bring the security concerns. However, the DL backed security management system can get rid of such concerns.

The organizations suffer from multimillion to multibillion of dollars because of the security breach (Obderbeck, 2016). The remote learning system is not an exception to this cyberattack. There have been several attempts of cyberattacks in the educational institutions for the last several years (e.g., Fig 4). The attackers steal the identity of the employer (e.g., (Gandhi, et al., 2011), (Kwon, Liu, & Hwang, 2013)) of the institutions and then attempt to attack. The 21^(st) century mode and models of learning must incorporate the remote learning feature to provide an easy access of online learning to the respected professionals. The DL backed cyber security measures of the ADDIE can ensure such secured access, data management of the eLearning systems. The cybersecurity measures can analyze the traffic flow and identify potential threats there. The same measures can be used to identify potential attempts on the stealing of the identity of the educational personnel, students etc. The identity management service can be integrated with the DL backed identity service that can provide more secured access to the data (Arsenovic, Sladojevic, Anderla, & Stefanovic, 2017). As a result, the ADDIE can help to reduce the management cost associated with the security of the data.

## ADDIE Aided Learning

The instructors and the students are one of the two most important participants in the eLearning system. The authors have explained the impact of the ADDIE aided instruction on the participants above. The impact can be extended on the learning part as well. The authors define the ADDIE aided learning modes and models as the method, tools of learning that are governed, managed, supported by different services included in the ADDIE. It can be the enhanced search results associated with the students’ course topics, digital learning, mentoring, career assistances, that can improve the overall learning outcome for the students along with the career success. The authors have addressed the ADDIE aided learning impact, benefits in the following subsections.

### Digital learning assistance by the ADDIE

The digital learning assistance (e.g., DLA) can provide the students up-to-date information related with the course topics, homeworks, assignments. The latest information received, processed by the DLA at the student’s end can be organized by the DL backed searching services at the instructor’s end. The students can understand the core concepts along with the application of the concepts in different domains from the searching service provided by the DLA.

The DLA can have a visual representation on the eLearning system (e.g., Fig 5) as the Microsoft word document provided such assistance some years ago. Such visuals can not only provide the students course related helps but also provide psychological strength at the difficult times. The DLA ensures 24/7 assistance for the students. Amazon, Google, Microsoft have been building the digital assistants for several years to provide such 24/7 assistance to the customers (Maedche, et al., 2019). The authors are considering similar types of DLA for the 21^(st) century modes and models of learning for the learners here, that can be integral part of the students’ day-to-day learning activities.
