In this study, for EDA signals, the features proposed by Healey and Picard [5], amplitude (OM) and duration (OD) calculated from signal peaks and valleys, were extracted.

In the current study, EDA was measured at 15.5 Hz. Based on the calculation of mean OD ±3 standard deviation (SDOD), it was found that 99.2% of OD falls within the confidence interval. We found that the six-minute window size (approximately equal to 5580 samples) meets the accuracy requirements. In this study, one-minute sliding window was determined, which was approximately equal to 930 samples, and the moving average was 60 samples. To find the signal peaks and valleys, the “findpeaks” function in MATLAB (R2017) was used. The signal feature extraction allows us to extract EDA characteristics: minimum (min OD and OM), maximum (max OD and OM), mean (mean OD and OM), standard deviation (SD and SD OM), summation (sum of ODs and OMs), and the number of occurrences of duration and amplitude (nOD and nOM).
