# 4.2. Evaluation Metrics

There are various evaluation metrics in the literature to measure the discriminative performance of the FER model. In addition to widely used metrics for classification, such as accuracy, F1-score [23], area under the ROC curve (AUC) [24], and area under the precision-recall curve (AUC-PR) [25], two measures of inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's kappa [26] and Krippendorff's alpha [27]) are used in our work. In statistics, Cohen's kappa measures inter-rater reliability, which gives the same data.
