# ## 4. Future Work

Datasets developed for specific applications use passive methods to provoke emotions, such as IAPS, IADS, music videos, and film clips. Public databases, such as DEAP and SEED, use emotion elicitation through music videos and film clips, respectively. Few implement active methods for provoking emotions, such as video games and flight simulators.

Going forward, we expect the generation of datasets that use active elicitation because these techniques simulate "real life" events better, and are more effective at emotion induction. However, the implementation of such studies requires more complex experimental setup.

Furthermore, the study of individual emotions has been recently trending. Some works include fear detection, an algorithm has applications in phobia investigation, and other psychiatric disorders. It is worth mentioning that our survey found that negative emotions are more challenging to detect positive ones.

We did not find in the elicitation of the EEG-based emotion recognition of feelings that combine positive and negative effects at the same moment, instance, bittersweet feelings. These mixed emotions are interesting because they are also related to the study of high creative performance [141].

Feature extraction and selection are EEG-BCI system components, which are continuously evolving. They are designed based on a profound approach to understanding of the brain’s biology and physiology. The development of novel features is a topic that can contribute significantly to the improvement of emotion recognition systems. For instance, time-domain features are combined with frequency-frequency characteristics, channel location, and connectivity criteria. The development of novel feature extraction methods includes asymmetry discoveries in different functioning brain segments, new electrode locations that provide more information, connectivity models (between channels), and correlations needed for understanding functionality.

These evolving features contain EEG signals and their frequency bands are related to multiple functional and connectivity considerations. The study of the relationship between EEG and biological or psycho-emotional elements should imply going forward. Improved features could better capture individual emotion dynamics and also correlate characteristics across individuals and sessions.

A particularly interesting trend in feature extraction is to use deep neural networks. These systems receive raw data to avoid loss of information and take advantage of the neural networks functioning to obtain relevant features automatically.
