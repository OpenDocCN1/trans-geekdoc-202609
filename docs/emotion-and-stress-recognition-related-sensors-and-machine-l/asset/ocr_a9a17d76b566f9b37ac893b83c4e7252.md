The participants. However, to develop a stress management method for daily life, there is a need to validate the method out-of-laboratory, as well. Thus, we also chose the driving data set to assess stress out-of-laboratory. Although these data sets cannot represent all of the stress situations that can occur in everyday life, such as workload stress, stress anxiety, and so, we demonstrated an end-to-end architecture to detect mental stress for both in- and out-of-laboratory environments. However, there were limitations in this study. Although the two sensors used in the two data sets were individual, in view of generalization, the model needs to be validated by using ECG from different sensors, including other electrode configurations. We have other data sets, which were different from those using during training, to allow the model to detect specific type of stress sensor density. Even through bias or dependency remains, transfer learning from one data set to other may provide a solution to address the limited applicability in real-world scenarios. As mentioned above, ECGs during daily activities are necessary for considering other stress events, such as workload or anxiety, and we will apply it to multi-class problems or continuous level recognition. Additionally, we will investigate how physiological signals to train a neural network to overcome the limitations of the data set.
