Certainly, not only these factors have an impact on the predictability of a driving stress, for example, internal negative emotions and may affect the driver's state [24,49]. The distinction between these two groups of factors is a difficult scientific task. Based on this, future scientific questions are the improvement of stress detection methods, considering the personal characteristics and emotions of the driver.

Next factor that can affect the predictability of the stress state is stress measures. Villarro et al. [50] reported that EDA successfully detects various human emotions, but it is difficult to distinguish between, for example, the stress and the situation of being effort by a human. EDA sensor may recognize these two states as the same. Additionally, previous studies to detect the proposed various measures such as heart rate, blood pressure, muscle tension, etc [51,52]. In our paper, EDA was used as a significant measure of the stress level. It was confirmed that along with the most mentioned characteristics from previous studies, EDA can successfully be used to predict low/high stress during driving real time. Moreover, EDA is convenient and useful for long-term monitoring since this sensor requires no gel-type electrodes and contact points on skin unlike EEG and ECG sensors.

In the present study, classical statistics based on logistic regression analysis were developed. In turn, previous studies show that different features can be used for stress detection and emotional recognition. Raj et al [53] proposed a method to predict driving stress (high, and low) using EDA and ECG signals based on a Bayesian network framework with an accuracy range of 86-92%. Jahan et al [55] used several classifiers, including Bayesian Nets, decision tables, decision trees, support vector machines, regressions, and LogitBoost to predict unsafe driver behavior by a facial expression based on Kappa values (3). By comparing these with the methods proposed in our paper, it should be noted that elements of advanced deep learning can be considered in the future to increase the prediction ability of the developed models. The proposed models show can be extended to include more features or variables, but for other classifiers, there can be limitations due to algorithm inflexibility. Using presented models needs minimal efforts and simple devices. Additionally, it can be applied for science and industry. Otherwise, some advanced approaches can be more difficult. In general, the combination of developed and more advanced classifiers can result in offerings with improved accuracy and predictability.

Our study demonstrates and compares two developed models capable of a satisfactory accuracy to predict the state of the drivers based on physiological signals. Obtained results expand previous research and new findings can be found in traffic management, medicine, and design of electronic devices with physiological sensors.

## 4.2. Limitation of This Study and Future Research

The presented research is the result of a long-term study stress stress based on physiological signals. Even though developed classification models showed good results, there are a few limitations, which need to be addressed in the future. First, a shortcoming of this study is the number of participants, which was limited to one driver. The authors compensated for it by increasing the experimental period to one month to obtain the optimal number of driving datasets for analysis. Second, the temperature in the car was not regulated during the experiment. It may need to be controlled in future studies because this condition can possibly affect the psychological state and sweeping intensity of the driver. Third, the traffic flow theory [26] will be used in future research. From this theory, the speed of vehicles, traffic flow, density, road capacity, distance between vehicles will be considered to extend the developed methods. Fourth, the accuracies of models obtained can be improved by increasing their sensitivity to emotional states of the driver, such as anger, stress, etc., as discussed in previous studies [24,50]. Few additional observations will need to be considered in the future. For example, direct data shows that EDA are doubled in the morning routine (EDA=0). A higher EDA level was observed in the morning session on 12 January 2018, which was the coldest day with temperature of -14 C. Potentially, this indicates that weather conditions and time of day affect the driver's EDA signal, and further research can find many applications, including the development of wearable devices containing physiological sensors.

## 5. Conclusions

This study presented the findings on the level of driving stress using EDA signals. The study confirmed that EDA is a significant indicator of the psychological state and an effective tool to determine stress levels under actual driving conditions. Advantages of the developed method are that developed models have an ability to predict stress with over 80% accuracy. Developed models can also be used to classify stress in different traffic situations and different road types. The definition of traffic jam situation is one of the most important contributions of this study. One of the disadvantages of the proposed model is that traffic congestion concept needs to be defined and improved specifically. To make a better model in the future, the following points should be considered. First, researchers can collect more road information, such as lane-keeping status, as well as bio-signals instead of logistic regression. Researchers can identify interacting factors, such as in-vehicle temperature, and driver's attention. Obtained results can be used for practical application for monitoring the nervous system, smartphone/computer.
