images of children interacting with the mobile robot N-MARIA, achieving promising results
(85.77% accuracy) to locate face landmarks, as well to recognize (or) five emotions.
All children had a hopeful interaction with the robot, which demonstrated our system is useful to
stimulate emotions in children, and to trigger a profitable interaction with them. In future
work, this proposal will be integrated in N-MARIA, aiming to know the children's emotion and
making decisions based on emotions. Additionally, other methods will be explored for
tacking, in order to reduce the influence of head-pose during emotion recognition.
unsupervised methods for automatic label assignments and classifier learning will be evaluated in
database to obtain a robust recognition system for processing patterns of high uncertainty, such as facial
emotions and expressions.
