It is noting that PCA improved the performance of system for subset S06 (78.10% and ACC of 73.11% and Kappa of 65.09% for FNCA), as shown in Figure 8a,b. This improvement may be caused by the PCA of robustness for feature extraction under head-pose and imaging conditions [9]. Also, notice that for subset S22 the lowest performance was achieved by applying PCA (ACC of 49.27% and Kappa of 36.75%) and FNCA (ACC of 49.98% and Kappa of 37.78). However, higher values were achieved for a total of 14 children by applying PCA or FNCA, being the highest ACC 99.78 for subset S26. Labelling feature vector of emotions is a challenge, when labels are applied over periods of time, some delays between perception and manual label assignment may exist. Similarly, a visual stimulus may produce different facial emotions, thus the manual labeled procedure is subjective. Then, an automatic method for feature labeling may be suitable to obtain reliable labels and a classification model for LDA.

On the other hand, Figure 8 shows that four emotions (disgust, fear, happiness, and surprise) were successfully recognized by both PCA and FNCA, achieving TPR > 85%, whereas sadness was classified with less sensitivity (TPR of 74.69% and 76.15% for FNCA and PCA, respectively) by the proposed system. As a highlight, low values of FPR (≤5.40%) were obtained to recognize all studied emotions
