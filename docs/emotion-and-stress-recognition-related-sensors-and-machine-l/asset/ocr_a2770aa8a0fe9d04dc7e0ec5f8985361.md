where H_i: happiness score, C_i: cheerfulness score, A_i: anger score, S_i: sadness score, F_i: frustration score. Individual scores on the PSR can range from 0 to 30 with higher scores indicating higher stress. Stress ranging from 0-15 would be considered low stress, and stress ranging from 15-30 would be considered high stress. The division was made by adapting the three division of the PSR-14 class [54].
