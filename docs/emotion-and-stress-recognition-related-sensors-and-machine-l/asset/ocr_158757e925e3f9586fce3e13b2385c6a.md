# ## 4. Empirical Relaxation–Study 2

To validate the generalis of our models, we replicated the study with different
participants, using different users and different tasks. The study protocol was approved by the
University of Auckland Human Participants Committee.



The apparatus and data remained the same. The procedure was very similar, with
only the difference being between a single stress and a relaxation task. After each task,
participants were asked to answer the questionnaire from our study and to complete
a NASA Test. The data analysis remained the same. We recruited 11 participants (7 males and
fem), aged 22-34 (M = 26.4, SD = 3.17) with ethnicity: the inclusion/exclusion
criteria were similar to this study. We also used a different stress (Task 5: Mental Arithmetic
Test) and relaxation (Task 6: Nature video).



Participants were asked to complete 20 challenging maths questions based on fundamental
mathematics within 5 min. We created additional pressure by informing participant's performance
will be graded. Similar to previous tasks, an experimenter observed their performance and commented
on their performance.



To relax the user, we showed a 5 min nature video with soothing music. The video was different
then the one in Task 4.
