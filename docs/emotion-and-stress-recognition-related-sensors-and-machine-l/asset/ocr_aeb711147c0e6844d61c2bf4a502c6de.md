+   - Threat to validity: Threats to extend the generalization to other datasets of the results in this paper. Applying the proposed methods in paper to additional datasets will reduce this threat.

+   - Threats to validity: Threats to consider the appropriateness of the benchmark algorithms and evaluation metrics. For the most part, the proposed FER outperformed its counterparts on the same test dataset. The evaluation metrics used for validating methods (i.e., accuracy, F1-Score, Kappa score, AUC, and AUC-PR) are common in machine learning studies to evaluate the performance of classification models.

#  5. Conclusions

This paper proposed a facial expression recognition model based on deep learning and a novel weighted-cluster loss to mitigate the shortage of unbalanced data. SE-ResNet-50 model which is prepared for face identification is fine-tuned to recognize eight facial expressions in AffectNet data. This not only helps to save computing resource but also alleviate the shortage of training data used in the fine-tuning phase to handle the high imbalance in data distribution of AffectNet data. Multiple methods have been used to evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed model. Experimental results on the test set indicate that the proposed FER can outperform its counterpart models which use either weighted-softmax loss or center loss. However, the proposed model is built to recognize facial expressions on static image data, which may limit its applicability. Moreover, using generative adversarial networks (GAN) to generate more data for training models is considered as our future work.
