# 3.7. Validation of VR

Table 3 shows the content of the papers that presented analyses of the validation of VR in an
emotional research. 83.3% of the papers did not present any type of validation. Three papers
included direct comparisons of results between VR environments and the physical world [64,65,109],
and 3, in terms of the formats used, the emotional reactions evoked in 3D VRs, photos [109],
360° panoramas [64] and augmented reality [129]. Finally, another compared the influence of
imersion [121], the similarity of VR results with previous datasets [108] and one compared its results
with a previous version of the study performed in the real world [132].
