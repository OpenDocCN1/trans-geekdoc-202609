# 2.2. User Profile

The considered users (not real pilots) of both genders between 21 and 24 years old. Considering the 13 valid flights, nine were executed by male users and four were executed by female users.

Regarding the user experience in experimental context, one male user reported to have a more deep experience in flight simulator; the other male users reported to have experience with electronic devices and all female users reported to low experience in flight simulators and electronic devices.
