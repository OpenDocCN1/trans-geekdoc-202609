The validity of EDA as a measure of emotional arousal has been established in studies showing that EDA varies linearly with self-reported arousal when viewing emotional pictures [38]. Therefore, EDA is outstanding in behavioral medicine as a biomarker of individual characteristics of emotional response. EDA monitoring has been used for multiple applications, assessment of anxiety and stress, detection of orientation response, providing neurofeedback for epilepsy, and in addition, EDA can be very effective in discriminating patients with depression from healthy controls [39]. Specific patterns of electrodermal hyperactivity may be a reliable marker of a depressive state at population level, but must be carefully combined with other physiological and non-physiological indicators when used for preventive and diagnostic purposes.

EDA covers the variations that occur on the surface of the skin due to changes in sweat secretion. EDA signals are obtained by measuring the potential when a small constant current is applied between two electrodes (for example, chrome-silver electrodes). The skin usually responds to stress by producing an increase in sweat. The skin's conductivity increases. On the other hand, sweat production stops and skin conductivity is reduced when a person is subjected to a calm or neutral indifference. In this study, EDA is measured at the wrist, bearing in mind that wrist biosensors are widely adopted in conventional and commercial devices. The bracelets used excellent for attaching electrodes to the skin. Ideally, the proposed system should be further miniaturized to record EDA in the areas where the activity of the skin response (SCR) is pronounced, without intrusive or interfering with daily activities.

## 2.2. Data Acquisition and Empatica E4

The commercial Empatica E4 wristband has been used to carry out our experiment. The Empatica E4 bracelet is a device that allows the collection and measurement of physiological signals such as EDA, blood volume pressure, temperature and acceleration. This device has been used with good results in some previous works [14,41]. In this work, we have used only the EDA to study the possibility of determining whether significant differences occur when a participant is subjected to different musical stimuli.

An essential component of our protocol is to acquire, process and obtain a set of data that will be used for identification of the listener's arousal. Empatica E4 device must be firmly attached to the wrist so that the electrodes touch the skin correctly. Otherwise, if the device is not properly connected, the captured data are not valid to manifold artifacts.

## 2.3. Participants

40 participants, all from the region of Murcia, Spain, were recruited for the experiment. These volunteers were 23 women and 17 men with an average age of 65 (SD =6.3) and 68 (SD =5.1), The volunteers were all in good health and cognitive conditions to perform the measurement experiment. They were given two screening tests, the PROMS (Patient-Reported Outcomes Measurement System) diagnostic test and the YTM (Test Your Memory) for cognitive impairment. Those who scored above the cutoff point in depression and below in cognitive functioning did not participate in the study. In addition, participants were required to sign a consent form explaining the procedure and the risks that could arise from the study.

The experiment had been previously validated by the Ethics Committee of the Universidad de Castilla-La Mocha in accordance with the Helsinki Declaration.

## 2.4. Self-Assessment Manikin

One way of quantifying and subsequently relating the signals obtained from EDA to each of the different musical stimuli is by using a self-assessment manikin (SAM) questionnaire [23,24]. This questionnaire is widely used in psychology to measure the subjectively felt intensity of emotions to compare with the emotional annotation of the different physiological signals captured by electrophysiological devices [42–44]. The questionnaire consists of a series of manikans representing different values of valence, activation and dominance [45]. In this experiment the manikin for activation was used.

## 2.5. Music Stimuli

As mentioned above, in this experiment the key in provoking emotions is music. For this reason, eight music pieces have been specifically composed by a professional musician for this experiment. These compositions reflect some musical styles that older people heard when they were young (more than 30 years ago). This was the first time the participants heard each of these original pieces. All eight pieces are characterized by a same melody and eighty variations according to musical styles. The duration of each variation was 60 s. The musical genres used in this experiment and their repercussion in the region of Murcia are briefly described below. First, flamenco, has been widely disseminated on the radio and only through simple songs, is a deeply rooted genre in Spain. The most cheerful, folkloric and festive flamenco styles were adopted, such as the Fandango and the Pentera, relegating everything to the “jondo” singing to a secondary position [46]. Secondly, Spanish Folklore, mainly linked to moments of celebration, is characterized by its joyful and festive character. Also anchored in popularity, its simplicity and the repetition of melodic-rhythmic elements give off energy and vitality. It is linked to dancing as a couple, allowing one to enjoy the social atmosphere and to relate to the music and the corte.

On its side, Cuban evokes silent listening without movement or slow dancing in couples with direct physical contact. This musical genre has also been adopted by classical music and has been expanded mainly by the cinema and the radio to its sentimental character. Finally, jazz and rock’n roll imply a new way of listening and relating to music. The orchestration of this music adds instruments and sounds unknown in their culture was new to the participants. This music relies on simple and repetitive structures, as well as on melodic improvisation through instrumental or vocal solos. The design of this music is also new, in pairs but without physical contact, and with very rhythmic movements that sometimes are perceived as transgression.

## 2.6. Experimental Design

An appropriate experimental design is fundamental to achieving results. The E-Prime software has been chosen to create the basic design of the experiment. The software is the most widely used in the field of psychology for setting up experimental trials. In fact, E-Prime is a very robust software tool for our proper study, since it allows us to randomize and synchronize musical pieces that are played to the participants. Furthermore, it makes it possible to add the SAM questionnaire and to control/record different parameters that we will exploit the EDA signals acquired during music performance.

The design of the experiment has been carried out following the scheme shown in Figure 1. As it can be seen, the experiment has well-differentiated phases. In the first phase, the measuring instruments are placed on the participant. The EDA signals start to collect when the participant is prepared, which means he/she is in a neutral emotional state. To achieve this state, the participant remains silent looking at a black screen before the first piece of music. In second phase, the participant listens to all of the musical pieces, and when the reproduction of each of them is concluded, the person completes the SAM questionnaire. This process is carried out 8 times until the musical pieces have been played.

At the same time the experiment was being conducted, the EDA signals were continuously collected, making possible further segmentation, preprocessing and analysis of the signal.

As discussed, EDA has been measured by a non-invasive device. The E4 Empatica bracelet measures the skin conductance (SC) in the form of EDA signals. These measurements are composed of two signals: a first signal that varies wildly, called the driver or skin conductance (SCL), and a second that varies rapidly, the phase driver or skin conductance (SCR). The SCL signals establish the base level of the signal, while the SCR is directly associated with the activity of the sweat motor system.
