In order to examine if the differences of the SAM evaluations are statistically significant in the VAD-space between the sequences, further statistical analysis was carried out. To analyze the ratings for Group A and Group B, we conducted repeated measures ANOVA with the Factors Sequence and VAD (for Valence, Arousal, and Dominance, respectively). Post-hoc, Newman-Keels corrections were carried out to compare the means between the sequences. For Group A, the repeated measures revealed a significant effect of Sequence(F(5.185)=18.866, p < 0.001, n=32), VAD (F(2.274)=5.996, p < 0.001, n=62) and interaction (F(1.370)=30.748, p < 0.002, n=0.454). Post-hoc tests using Newman-Keels correction revealed differences (see Table A1 in the Appendix). For the Combined Group A + B, the repeated measures ANOVA revealed a significant effect of Sequence(F(5.280)=30.190, p < 0.001, n=0.353), VAD (F(2.112)=106.429, p < 0.001, n=0.774) and interaction (F(10.560)=51.405, p < 0.001, n=0.427). A subjective feedback evaluation was carried out in Direct Questions. After each sequence, the subjects were asked to answer Direct Questions related to the assessment of their perception. Four questions related to “Difficulty”, “Performance”, “Stress”, and “Motivation” were processed. With the help of the first sequence, subjects described how difficult the sequence was (easy = 1; difficult = 10). The second question is a personal performance assessment (performed well = 1; performed well = 10). For the first sequence, this “Performance” question was asked to answer the subjects’ interest (relaxed = 1; stressed = 10). The third question describes experienced stress level and the fourth question reflects the motivation of the participant (motivated = 1; motivated = 10).

In Figure 12 the results of the Direct Questions show for Group A. It can be seen, for the first question “Difficulty”, Overload has the highest rating (9.18), while Easy and Underload have the lowest (1.66 and 1.68, respectively). As for the second question “Performance”, the lowest rating is for Overload (2.13), while the highest were obtained for Easy and Underload (8.34 and 8.50). The “Interest” rating for the first sequence was 7.63.

Further, the third “Stress” question shows similar course as the first “Difficulty” question. The “Stress” ratings for the sequences Interest, Normal, and Frustration are in the range (5.13, 5.03 and 5.34, respectively), while Overload has the highest rating (7.00) and Easy and Underload the lowest (2.32 and 2.58). An interesting observation here, is the slightly increasing stress from Easy to Underload. A “Motivation” shows the highest rating for Interest (9.13), and the lowest ratings for Overload (7.13), Underload (7.55), and Frustration (7.11).
