The descriptor HOG can quickly and efficiently describe the local shape and appearance of objects by counting the orientations of gradients in a local position of the images. In this study, the images are divided into small cells, and for every cell the histogram of the gradient is calculated. This is done to represent spatial information of the face. For every image, in our study, 48 cells are constructed of every image by building a cell with 18 × 16 pixels. A horizontal gradient filter [-1 0] was applied with 59 orientation bins in the study. Final done was the concatenation of the HOG descriptors of each cell to form a HOG feature vector (size of 2832 (48 × 59)).
