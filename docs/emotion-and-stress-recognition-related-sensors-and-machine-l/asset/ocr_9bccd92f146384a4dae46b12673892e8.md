Last, model 10 (transferred model that was fine-tuned using the proposed joint weighted-soft loss) achieved better performance compared to model 9 (transferred model that was fine-tuned using joint center and weighted-soft loss) in all evaluation metrics (e.g., in terms of accuracy: 60.70% vs 59.60%, respectively). This shows that the proposed cluster effectively handles the limitations of center and weighted-soft loss by not only the imbalanced data into consideration but also simultaneously improving intra-class compactness and enlarging inter-class differences.
