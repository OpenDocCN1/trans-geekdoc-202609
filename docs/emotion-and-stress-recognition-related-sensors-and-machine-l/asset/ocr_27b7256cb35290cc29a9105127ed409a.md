Tackling the second problem of imbalanced data distribution in FER datasets, we propose a new loss function called the weighted-cluster loss, which integrates the advantages of the weighted-softmax approach and the auxiliary loss approach. First, weighted-cluster learns a class center for each emotion, which simultaneously reduces the intra-class variances and the inter-class differences. The proposed loss gives weights to each emotion class's loss terms based on their relative proportion of the total samples in the training dataset. In other words, weighted-cluster loss penalizes networks for misclassifying examples from minor classes while penalizing networks less for misclassifying from major classes. Furthermore, the training process is simple because weighted-cluster does not require preselected samples or triples.

Experiments were conducted to evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed method. In addition to widely used metrics for classification (F-score [23], area under the receiver operating characteristic [ROC] curve [AUC][24], and area under the precision-recall curve [AUC-PR [25]]), two metrics of inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's kappa [26] and Krippendorff's alpha [27]) are used to evaluate the methods. The experimental results on AffectNet dataset [5] show that our transfer learning-based model with weighted-cluster loss outperforms other methods that used weighted softmax-loss or center.
