The GSR scatter plot (Figure 5b) shows a positive correlation with $R^2=0.882$ for raw data and 0.896 for the moving averaged data. This means that 88.2% and 89.6%, respectively, of the data's total variance can be explained by a linear model. The cross-correlation plots (Figure 5c) show the highest cross-correlation for the as-is version at lag of 2 s, and the highest cross-correlation for the moving averaged version at lag of 1 s. In other words, the local trend of the E4 sensor is lagging 2 s and 1 s, "behind" the local trend of the VP sensor on average.
