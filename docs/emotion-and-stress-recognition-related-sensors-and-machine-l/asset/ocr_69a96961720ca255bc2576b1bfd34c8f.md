The validity of EDA as a measure of emotional arousal has been established in studies showing that EDA varies linearly with self-reported arousal when viewing emotional pictures [38]. Therefore, EDA is outstanding in behavioral medicine as a biomarker of individual characteristics of emotional response. EDA monitoring has been used for multiple applications, assessment of anxiety and stress, detection of orientation response, providing neurofeedback for epilepsy, and In addition, EDA can be very effective in discriminating patients with depression from healthy controls [39]. Specific patterns of electrodermal hyperactivity may be a reliable marker of a depressive state at population level, but must be carefully combined with other physiological and non-physiological indicators when used for preventive and diagnostic purposes.

EDA covers the variations that occur on the surface of the skin due to changes in sweat secretion. EDA signals are obtained by measuring the potential when a small constant current is applied between two electrodes (for example, chrome-silver electrodes). The skin usually responds to stress by producing an increase in sweat. The skin's conductivity increases. On the other hand, sweat production stops and skin conductivity is reduced when a person is subjected to a calm or neutral indifference. In this study, EDA is measured at the wrist, bearing in mind that wrist biosensors are widely adopted in conventional and commercial devices. The bracelets used excellent for attaching electrodes to the skin. Ideally, the proposed system should be further miniaturized to record EDA in the areas where the activity of the skin response (SCR) is pronounced, without intrusive or interfering with daily activities.

# 2.2. Data Acquisition and Empatica E4

The commercial Empatica E4 wristband has been used to carry out our experiment. The Empatica E4 bracelet is a device that allows the collection and measurement of physiological signals such as EDA, blood volume pressure, temperature and acceleration. This device has been used with good results in some previous works [14,41]. In this work, we have used only the EDA to study the possibility of determining whether significant differences occur when a participant is subjected to different musical stimuli.

An essential component of our protocol is to acquire, process and obtain a set of data that will be used for identification of the listener's arousal. Empatica E4 device must be firmly attached to the wrist so that the electrodes touch the skin correctly. Otherwise, if the device is not properly connected, the captured data are not valid to manifold artifacts.

# 2.3. Participants

40 participants, all from the region of Murcia, Spain, were recruited for the experiment. These volunteers were 23 women and 17 men with an average age of 65 (SD =6.3) and 68 (SD =5.1), The volunteers were all in good health and cognitive conditions to perform the measurement experiment. They were given two screening tests, the PROMS (Patient-Reported Outcomes Measurement System) diagnostic test and the YTM (Test Your Memory) for cognitive impairment. Those who scored above the cutoff point in depression and below in cognitive functioning did not participate in the study. In addition, participants were required to sign a consent form explaining the procedure and the risks that could arise from the study.

The experiment had been previously validated by the Ethics Committee of the Universidad de Castilla-La Mocha in accordance with the Helsinki Declaration.
