Regarding EEG, some studies showed that it is very difficult to find the user's specific region where the brain activity is sufficiently high to detect emotional states [32,33]. According to several studies, the prefrontal cortex or frontal lobe (near the front of the head) is more involved with cognition and decision making from emotional responses [34,35]. The 10-20 system or International 10-system system was the method used to describe and apply the location of scalp electrodes. This way, to better detect artifacts from the scalp, the electrodes were placed where recommended: Fp1 (channel 1), F3 (channel 2), C3 (channel 3), T7 (channel 4), Fp2 (channel 5), F4 (channel 6), C4 (channel 7) and T8 (channel 8). The EEG reference electrode (EEGR) was placed on the user's earlobe. It frequently aimed to use the beta rhythms (or band) in this experiment Figure 3 shows the electrodes used during experiment. Using the note of the frontal cortex to acquire EEG data, the beta rhythm (β-band) were considered i.e., brain signals between 10 and 30 Hz.
