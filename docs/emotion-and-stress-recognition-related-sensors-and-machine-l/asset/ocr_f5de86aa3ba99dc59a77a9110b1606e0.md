# ### 3.1. Experimental Procedure

Seventeen developing children, 9 boys and 8 girls (aged between 8 and 12 years)\participated in this study, who were recruited from elementary schools in Vitória-Brazil. All had
their parents' permission, signing of Terms of Free and Informed Consent. In addition,
child signed a Term of Assent, informing them of their participating. This was approved by
The Ethics Committee of Federal University of Espírito Santo (UFES)/Brazil, under number 1,121,638.
The experiments were conducted in a room with the children's environment, where the room
temperature was kept at 20 °C and 24 °C, using a constant luminous intensity, as was
Ref. [39].

A mobile robot (see Figure Ib), called N-MARIA (New-Mobile Autonomous Robot
for Interaction with Autism), but at UFES/Brazil to assist children during social relationship
habilitation, was used in our research. This robot has attached a camera to record facial
emotions during interaction. More details about N-MARIA are given in Section 3.2.1.
The experiment was conducted in three phases. First, N-MARIA was initially covered
in a room with a black sheet, except its attached camera, that was turned on to record visual
d thermal images of the face at 2 fps, for further processing. Afterwards,
tchild was invited to enter the room, and felt comfortably for the general activities
the experiment, being conducted to a relaxed state for a period of time of 10 minutes,nnin order to adapt her/her body to the temperature of the room, allowing her/her skin temperature
to stabilize for baseline records, according to similar studies carried out in Refs. [21,41]. Once
d completed the relaxation period, the child was placed in front of the covered room above
70 cm away from it, remaining in standing position. Immediately, recording of the face by the
camera system were carried out for a period of one minute with the robot covered,nobot uncovered, and three minutes of interaction with the robot. After, the child spent two minutes
answering a question about the experiment.
