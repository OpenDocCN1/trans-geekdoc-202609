Depending on the focus of the research question or modality, the recording sessions of 38 subjects or 19 subjects can be analyzed: For instance, when focusing on physiological reactions with no specific interest in facial EMG, all 57 subjects (Group A + Group B1) can be analyzed; while focusing on facial expression from video data, 38 subjects (Group A) can be analyzed.

+   * **Transtemporal analysis:** Our dataset allows temporal fusion and investigations of the changes and variations of the induction, reactions and recognition over time. This is possible with our second sample of 20 subjects who underwent three measurements at different times with one-week interval-between. The temporal analysis of the final dataset includes 19 subjects (Group B) out of the original 20 subjects, left after quality check. Further, the data analysis and evaluation of the subjective feedback and questionnaires demonstrate transferable and valid results.

+   * **Validated dataset:** The induction of the various emotional and cognitive loads states via six different sequences is validated through evaluation of subjective feedback acquired during the experiment. On one side, the reported SAM Ratings between the sequences and show significant differences between the relevant inductions (Tables 3 or 4 Tables A1a and A1b). Additionally, the SAM Ratings of Group B1 show a consistent result with the results of Group A (first measurements from both samples) with no statistically significant differences based on an ANOVA. On the other side, the results of the Direct Questions are compatible with the related induction state (i.e., the *Overload and Fusion* sequences have “Stress” answers rates, while the *Interest* sequence has “Motivation” answers etc.) and show significant differences between the relevant inductions (Tables 5 and 6 Tables A2 and A2b). Additionally, the Direct Questions ratings of Group B1 present similar result as the results of Group A for the four questions with no statistically significant differences based on an ANOVA. Finally, the evaluation and analysis of the various questionnaires, acquired from the subjects experimer, also show stable results.

+   * **High technical quality:** The technical quality of the data and related signals is also checked and demonstrated via different preliminary classifications on various subsets of the database (including: the video data [63], the gesture data [65], the audio data [66], the biophysical data [67], the speech and the biophysical data [68], and the multimodal data [69].

Overall, we created a dataset for various applications in the fields of affective computing and machine learning, including classifications, feature analysis, multimodal fusion or temporal investigations. The dataset includes multimodal data as well as various annotations and extracted labels. Limitations of this work include the relatively limited number of temporal data (57 measurements from 19 subjects) as well as the absence of electroencephalography (EEG) or electroencephalography (EEG) for brain and emotional event analysis, but cognitive reactions. Firstly, the experiment was conducted in a laboratory setting to be close to real life, and the next step will be to transfer our settings and findings into the eco-wild for real-life induction and recognition patterns.

Future work will involve numerical evaluations based on classification using machine learning for the dataset. Further, standardized, sets of feature extraction techniques for each emotional and cognitive state will be defined. A multimodal fusion analysis will be conducted to investigate the effect of each induction on the recognition rates of the different states. Further, a transtemporal analysis of the Group B data will be conducted, investigations related to the analysis of human-computer dialogs could be conducted, for instance to investigate the effects of computer feedbacks on human performance and the psychophysiological responses. Similarly, a gender analysis could also be conducted to investigate differences in the elicitation levels, emotional-cognitive psychophysiological responses or in the recognition rates and individual performance.
