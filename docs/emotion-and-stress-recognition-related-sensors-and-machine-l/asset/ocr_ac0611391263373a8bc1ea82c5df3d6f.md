Unfortunately, continuous target variables are rarely available for the datasets which is the reason regression models are often used for stress detection, and why stress detection based on classification models is an important topic to study in the.

Stress detection on personal training data was also studied using data from three persons. The results obtained using personal models were surprisingly bad. The situation was better when more personal data were used for training but there was a lot of variation in the recognition rates depending on how gathering sessions were divided for training and testing. This shows that biosignal have a lot of variation not only between the stress subjects but also the session gathered from the same person. Therefore, personal regression models should be able to constantly adapt to changes in human body. Due to this, for instance learning-based method should be experimented [21]. On the other hand, it was shown that subject-feature selection for user-independent model could be a better approach than using of personal training to personalize and improve regression models. It was noted that with subject-feature selection, the average detection rate improved 4%, and it was especially useful to reduce the variance in the recognition rates among the subjects.

Moreover, it can be that the recognition rates obtained in this article are not as high as the one's in other stress detection studies where the same features are used (17, 19). The reason for this can be due to messy data gathering protocol. In many other studies, the stress data are collected from contexts (giving public speech, performing arithmetic tasks) that are likely more stressful than driving a car. The dataset used in this study does have a difference between relaxed and stressed state making it harder to analyze. It also possible that the type of stress is different in these contexts. In fact, of the future work is to make experiments using different datasets in fact, to extend this study which include data from new types of stress (for instance electroencephalography (EEG)), to show that the proposed method is not dependent on the used sensor. The expected result is that the proposed method works similar to this if appropriate features are extracted from the user sensor. In fact, extracted features needs to be selected sensor-wise, and though the features should not be extracted from each sensor. In addition, the experimented datasets should contain not only from stress, but from other affect states to show that the proposed method is not dependent on the studied affect state. The final aim is to detect multiple affect states at some time, for example a person to be stressed and angry. This could be done by training an own regression model for each state.

Lastly, it should be noted when dealing with data from stress and affect states which is labeled by the subjects themselves, it should be noted that study by subjects do not necessarily know what they feel [22], while the preliminary results presented in this article are encouraging, more experiments with different datasets should be carried out to get a better understanding of how accurate the presented method actually is.
