The representation of the input sequences into their properties. The performed evaluation has shown that MHIs and OFs have the temporal aspect of the sequences integrated into their properties. The performed evaluation outperforms the one on MHIs in most cases. It has also shown that most of the interests framing MHIs are located at the end of the temporal axis of each sequence, while single MHIs extracted from entire sequences could also be used as input for deep architectures. Overall the performed experiments show that the difference between lower and higher pain levels remains a difficult endeavour.
