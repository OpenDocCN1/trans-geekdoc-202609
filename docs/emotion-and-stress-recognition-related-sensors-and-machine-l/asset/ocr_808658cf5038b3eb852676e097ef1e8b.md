# 2. Methods



The participant of the experiment was a healthy 35-year-old Korean male with a height of 176 cm and a weight of 67 kg. The driver was well equipped with the driving route and had driving experience of more than 15 years.



The Empatica E4 wristband (EDA sensor) was used for physiological signal collection in our experiment. The wristband [42] is a wearable and wireless device designed for comfortable, continuous, and real-time data acquisition in daily life. It contains three sensors: (1) PPG sensor that detects cardiovascular features as blood volume pulse and heart rate variability, (2) EDA sensor that measures an arousal of a sympathetic nervous system and deliver features related to stress, engagement, and excitement, and (3) three-axis accelerometer that captures motion-based activity. The wristband operates either in streaming mode for real-time data viewing on a mobile device, using Bluetooth Low Energy or in recording mode, using its internal memory.
