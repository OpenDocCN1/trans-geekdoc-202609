## Appendix C. Features from the EDA Signal

The structure used in order to extract the most relevant information from the EDA signal follows a similar structure to the first one described in the ECG signal and the TEB signal.

Figure A3 shows the measurements obtained from the EDA signal and the procedure to extract the features. In total, we obtained 62 time features and 42 frequency features. Taking into account that the EDA was registered in both the hand and the arm (as described above), we obtained 104 features from the arm EDA and 104 features from the hand EDA.

### Appendix C.1. Time Domain

We obtained four time domain measurements:

- EDA-Original: The 13 SSSPs and the baseline aforementioned parameter are calculated to the EDA-Original signal. The area is also calculated from measurement, using an approximated integral of the time segments through trapezoidal method with unit spacing.
- EDA-LF: The original signal is filtered with a 20-order low-pass FIR filter (LF block) with a cutoff of 0.2 Hz [41]. The 13 SSSPs and the baseline parameter were evaluated.
- EDA-HF: A complementary filter is used to obtain the high frequency components (20-order high-pass filter with a cutoff of 0.2Hz, and the same parameters as those from the FDA-LF measurement are evaluated over the obtained EDA-HF measurement.
- EDA-Processed: The work [26] shows the steps to process EDA signal, for Skin Conductance Response (SCR) detection. The process consists of removing the mean value, resampling to 20 Hz, time differentiating, and filtering with a 20-order Bartlett window. From the processed EDA measurement, typical parameters are extracted from the SSSPs and the baseline parameter, and also some specific parameters.
- Zero Crossing (ZC): Positive Z (PZC), and Negative Z (NZC) are interesting for the application at hand [26,41].
- Ratio or proportion of Negative Samples (PNS), evaluated as the quotient between the number of negative samples and the total number of samples [41].
- SCRs were analyzed the zero crossings in the processed EDA signal. The signal amplitude of the SCR occurrences and the number of occurrences in the analysis window were used as parameters [26,42,54,91]. SCRs were determined by first consecutive zero-crossings, from negative to positive and from positive to negative.
