As was stated above, four different activities were to be recognized: emotional activity, mental activity, physical activity, and neutral activity (resting).

In order to elicit the activity, we have used a segment documentary called "Earth" to induce Neutral Activity. In order to elicit emotional activity, we used a set of segments from several validated methods [61], "American History X" by Savoy Poitevin [62], "I am legend" (2007) by Warner Ross [63], "Life is beautiful" (1997) by Miramax [64,65] and Cannibal Holocaust (1980) by F.D. Cinematografo [66].

The designed recognition system had to detect a decision every 10 s, and each individual generated 28 time slots of each activity (the database is balanced). The total number of patterns (decisions) for this analysis was 4480, and each class is composed of 1120 patterns.
In this analysis, we have used four activities:

+   - Neutral activity, registered during the last 10 s of the first movie (the documentary). As each individual watched each movie twice, there are 280 s for each individual in the database
- Emotional activity, registered during the viewing of the last 70 s of the second and third movies (140 s); therefore, we obtained a total of 280 s per individual.
