**Abstract:** Over the past two decades, automatic facial emotion recognition has received enormous attention. This is due to the increase in the need for behavioral biometric systems and human-machine interaction where the facial emotion recognition and the intensity of emotion play vital roles. The existing works usually do not encode the intensity of the facial emotion and even involve modeling the multi-class facial behavior data jointly. Our work involves recognizing the emotion along with the intensities of emotions. The algorithms used in this study are Gabor filters, a Histogram of Oriented Gradients (HOG), and Local Binary Pattern (LBP) for feature extraction. We have used Support Vector Machine (SVM), Random Forest (RF), and Nearest Neighbor Algorithm (k). This attends emotion recognition and intensity estimation of each recognized emotion. This is a comparative study of classifiers for facial emotion recognition along with the intensity estimation of emotions for databases. The results verified that the comparative study could be further used in real-time behavioral emotion and intensity of emotion recognition.

**Keywords:** automatic facial emotion recognition; intensity of emotion recognition; behavioral biometric systems; machine learning
