Abstract: Over the past two decades, automatic facial emotion recognition has received enormous attention. This is due to the increase in behavioral biometric systems and human-machine interaction where facial recognition and intensity of emotion play vital roles. The existing works usually do not encode the intensity. Our work involves recognizing the emotion along with intensities. The algorithms used in this study are Gabor filters, Histogram of Oriented Gradients (HOG), and Local Binary Pattern (LBP) for feature extraction. We have used Support Vector Machine (SVM), Random Forest (RF), and Nearest Neighbor Algorithm (k).

Keywords: automatic facial emotion recognition; intensity of emotion; behavioral biometric systems; machine learning

One of the major components of behavioral biometrics is the recognition of facial emotion and its intensity [1–3].
