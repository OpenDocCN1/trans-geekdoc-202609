Aiming at the clarity of this work, other works should be considered and analyzed. However, it is not easy to make such a comparison due to the fact that (a) other works may combine other types of physiological signals and do not use only EDA, and (b) the reaction and response of EDA highly depend on the stimuli type, which showed better results when the stimuli is an autonomic one:

To our knowledge, this study shows for developing a subject-independent emotion recognition using only EDA with a promising recognition rate. It is also worthwhile noting that we were:

In the state-of-the-art, researchers in [22] tested a deep-learning model consists of RNN and CNN showed a Concordance Correlation Coefficient (CCC) [44] of 0.10 on the arousal dimension and 0.33 on the valence dimension based on EDA. Used AVEC 2016 dataset [23,24].
