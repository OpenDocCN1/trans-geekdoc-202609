# 6. Conclusions and Future Work

This work presented a solution to detect emotions from pilots in com (i.e., beginner users on a flight simulator), simulated flights. These flights were executed by the Flight Simulator Steam Engine (FSX-SE), using a Cessna 172SF aircraft. The users from the experiment were beginners in flights and they were trained before. A total of seven flight tasks were defined during take-off, climbing, navigation, descent, approach final and landing.

We considered three different data from the pilots' bodies: HR, GSR and EEG. They were acquired at the same time during the flight based on various sensors such as Enbio-NE8, Shimmer-3-GSR*, Med-Lab-Peer100 and Arduino.

After data acquisition, the processing was executed to correct abrupt changes of the data, to detrain, remove outliers, normalize the data and execute filtering and data sampling. The feature extraction was executed over the processed data where several features were extracted for the recognition phase. The ANN was used to recognize emotions using the extracted features such as the ANN inputs, based on traditional and Deep Learning techniques.
