# 3.2.1. Camera System and N-MARIA

The camera system composed of both visual and thermal cameras was attached to the head of the social robot, as shown in Figure 3. These two cameras were fixed so that both have the same field of view. To capture variations, a low-cost camera (Therm-App) was used, which has spatial resolution of 384 × 288 p, frame rate at 8.7 Hz and temperature sensitivity <0.07 °C. The normalization of the thermal images acquired to lower temperatures, and lighter pixels with brightness rate from 0 to 255, darker pixels to lower temperatures. Moreover, a C270 HD Webcam (Logitech) was used to obtain visual images in RGB format, with a resolution of 1.2 MP.
