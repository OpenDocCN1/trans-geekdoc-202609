## 5. Conclusions and Future

AUs are popularly used for measuring the intensity of facial expressions. Using an adequate amount of data is required for training and testing classifiers. Hence our research focused on that AU intensity is not always reliable and accurate in the case of spontaneous. This happens due to the ambiguity and dynamic nature of emotions. In our work, we presented a probabilistic model to calculate the ICC values and accuracies among the dynamic AU levels. Also, the accuracies for various algorithms (LBP; HOG, and Gabor) indicate that LBP achieves the highest accuracy in most cases. As a future work, in neural networks several hidden layers could be added to specially handle each case in the spontaneous intensity of emotion recognition with tilt and lift angle.
