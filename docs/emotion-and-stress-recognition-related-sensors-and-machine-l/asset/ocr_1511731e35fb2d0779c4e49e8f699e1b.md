* Approximate Entropy (AE), originally proposed in [85], and applied to physiological data in [39,42,86].

* Root Mean Square of Success Differences (RMSD), determined by calculating the square root of the mean squared difference between consecutive RR intervals [34,48,49,87]. The RMSD is the primary time domain used to estimate the high-frequency beat-to-beat variations that represent regulatory activity [48].

* Two parameters of the Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (DFA) [85]. These two parameters (DFA1 and DFA2) were used to quantify the presence or absence of fractal-like correlation properties of the heart period time series [39].

+   * Heart Rate (HR). It is measured as the number of pulses per unit of time, usually beats per minute (bpm). It is calculated as the inverse of the RR interval. It is obtained through the inverse of the RR interval. This parameter is highly important, as it is related to physical exercise, anxiety, sleep, food intake, and drugs. The increase or decrease on this is the speed of our body and mind [34,48]. The SSSFs and the baseline parameters were calculated from this

+   * Heart Variability (HRV), which has been widely used to extract information about the status of the autonomic nervous system and emotions [23]. The work [88] provides a review of this measurement. In addition, numerous studies reveal the importance of this parameter [23,35,36,39,41,42,45,48,50,51,89]. We decided to obtain the HRV as proposed in [26], where HRV is determined from a modified version of the HRV sampled at 256 Hz. Once the HRV is obtained, it is possible to extract various features, using the SSSFs and the baseline parameter.

Many other measures were found in the literature such as SDNN index, SDANN among [34]. However, we did not use these measurements because they require at least 5 minutes to be considered, since they are often calculated over a 24-h period.

# Appendix A.2. Frequency-Domain

The main group of measurements is evaluated in the frequency domain, through the Discrete Fourier Transform (DFT). The main measurements taken in this part were:

+   * Power Spectral Density (PSD) of the HRV signal is obtained using Welch's method [23,38,42]. Power Bands. From this PSD parameter, several frequency bands were considered: Very-Low frequency (PSD-VLF), from 0.0033–0.08 Hz; Low Frequency (PSD-LF) from 0.05–0.08 Hz; Very-Low and Low Frequency (PSD-VLF) from 0.0033–0.08 Hz, Mid Frequency (PSD-MF) from 0.08–0.15 Hz, High Frequency (PSD-HF) from 0.15–0.5 Hz. These values were established taking into account several papers such as [23,26,34,36–38–40].

The parameters from these spectral measurements were the SSSFs, the baseline parameter, and a set of specific parameters related to ratios between the average power for the different frequencies: HF/LF, LF/HF, MF/HF, (LF+MF)/HF, HF/TF, being TF the total power in all frequencies [23,43,44,46,47,49].
