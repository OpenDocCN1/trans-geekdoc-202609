# Appendix A.1. Time-Domain

A total of five measures were considered in the time-domain, directly or indirectly extracted from the QRS analysis:

+   - The unprocessed signal [53,55] and the R Amplitude (RA) (amplitude of the different R waves in each window). The SSSPs and the baseline parameters were calculated for these measurements.
- The interval between successive Rs (RR) (time lapsed between successive R waves) [35,49,80]. Apart from the SSSP and the baseline, some special features have been extracted from the RR measurement:
- Deep Breathing (DBD), calculated as the difference between the maximum RR and minimum RR in the window under study [81,82].
 Ratios maximum RR vs. minimum RR, that is, RRmax/RRmin and RRmin/RRmax under study.
- Logstandard of variation in the RR under study, calculated as the quotient between the DBD and the mean value of the RR in the window study. This measure is related to the function of parasympathetic nervous driving during spontaneous ventilation [84].
 Modal Value (MV), defined as the most frequent value in the RR intervals in the window study [40].
 Load Index (LI), based on the ratio between the number of occurrences of each Modal Value and DBD [40].
 NN50, determined as the number of successive RR interval pairs differing by more than 50 ms [40,42,48].
- pNN50, obtained dividing NN50 by the total number of RR intervals [40,42,48].
