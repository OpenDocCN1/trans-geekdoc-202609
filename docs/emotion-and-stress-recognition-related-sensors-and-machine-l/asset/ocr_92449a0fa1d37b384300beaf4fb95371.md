For the correlation analysis of same type parameters and exploratory plots, we keep the original scaling of individual time series to identify potential offsets of measurements. For the similarity analysis, however, we rescale the measurements of individual time series from minimum and maximum to 0 and 1 in order to compare similarity distances.

The data pre-processing was mainly carried out directly in the database using SQL and Java.
