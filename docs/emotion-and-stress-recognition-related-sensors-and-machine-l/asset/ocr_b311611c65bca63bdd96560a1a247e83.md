Majority of the work concentrates on the emotions depicted at the peak-level of the intensity, neglecting the emotions depicted at lower intensity levels as they are comparatively difficult to recognize. Measuring the low-level frame intensity is a challenge as the available databases lack the discriminative features.

Comprehensive standards were designed by Paul Ekman and Friesen to subdivide emotion into several AUs which popularly known as the Facial Action Coding Units (FACS). These categorizations are considered be a gold standard for emotion detection and recognition systems.
