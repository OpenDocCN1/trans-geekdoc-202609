# ## Materials and Methods

An experimental mobile interactive and multimodal emotional-cognitive load scenario was designed and implemented for the induction of cognitive and emotional states in an HCI setting. Based on this mobile and interactive scenario, multimodal data were acquired using the University of Ulm Multimodal Affective Corpus (uulmMAC). The basic concept of our cognitive load scenario follows a generic setup by Schüssel et al who proposed a gamified setup for the exploration of various aspects with potential influence on users' way of interaction [51]. The generic scheme is, however, an abstract foundation for HCI exploration with no specific field. The induction of emotional and cognitive states depends on various factors related to the specific nature of human reactions [52]. For our research focusing on emotional and cognitive states in-life HCI, the development of the current experiment required further developments with an in-depth analysis and re-implementation of the generic paradigm such to match the requirements of cognitive load and affective states. The main contributions include the design of the interaction sequences scheme including cognitive, emotional and neutral states (Section 2.2), the development of the emotional structure (Section 2.3) and the software implementation and platform embedding (Section 2.4). Furthermore, for the experimental data acquisition, we developed and implemented a technical infrastructure with multimodal systems for the experiment and recording setup (Section 2.5).

# ### 2.1. Participants and Consent

The uulmMAC dataset consists of two homogenous samples of 60 participants (30 females, 30 males; 17-27 years; mean age = 21.65 years, SD = 25) with a total of 100 recording sessions (N = 100) about 45 minutes each. The 60 subjects were recruited and distributed at the University of Ulm. The first sample includes 40 subjects who underwent one measurement, while the second sample consists of 20 subjects who underwent three measurements. The three different measurements were acquired at different times with one week of time-interval between. The second sample allows for instance the investigation of attentional research questions. While both subjects underwent exactly the same experiment they slightly differ modality acquisition: the first sample include facial electromyography (EMG) measurements, allowing better conditions for the analysis of facial expressions via data. Both subjects are evenly balanced between male and female. All subjects gave their informed consent for inclusion before they participated in the experiment and the study was approved by the Ethics Committee of the University of Ulm: Project C4 - SFB RRR62.

In summary, the original dataset of uulmMAC consists of 100 individual recording sessions: The first sample with 40 recording sessions (40 subjects x 1 measurement) and the second sample with 60 recording sessions (20 subjects x 3 measurements).
