# 4. Classification using a Convolutional Network–CNN

In this section, we present, the labelling of EDA signals, the design details of the proposed CNN for emotion classification, and the evaluation metrics and results.



First, raw data of EDA were scaled so that the distribution is centered around 0, with a standard deviation of 1.1. After normalization, these stated [34] valence and arousal are identified for emotion classification.

The valence of 1-5 were mapped to "negative" and 6-9 to "positive", respectively. The arousal scale of 1-5 was mapped to "passive" and 6-9 to "active", respectively.
