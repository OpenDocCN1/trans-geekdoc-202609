In Ref.[18] the authors proposed techniques for selecting ROI and classifying emotions using a FLIR A310 camera. To detect the face, the thermogram's temperatures between 32 °C to 36 °C were used to define the face. The ROIs were further calculated by proportions based on the head's width. All other points were considered background. For emotion classification, the system was calibrated using a baseline (neutral state) that contrasted the induced emotion by fuzzy algorithm, and thus calibrate the induced image. Using the baseline, the temperature is inferred by IF-THEN rules to classify the thermal images for the induced emotions: joy, disgust, anger, fear and sadness. Next, a top-down hierarchical classifier was used to analyze the emotion classification, reaching 89.9% of success rate.
