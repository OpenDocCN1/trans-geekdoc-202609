# Comparison of T7, T8 and in-ear EEG in arousal classification

**Figure 10.** Box plot comparison among arousal classification using single channel T7, T8, and in-ear EEG. Grey areas indicate proportions of classification accuracy above the median. Orange areas indicate proportions of classification accuracy below the median. X indicates the mean accuracy.

Overall four emotion classification accuracies were approximately 53.72% for in-ear EEG and 58.12% for T7 T8 EEG. Valence classification accuracies were 71.07% and 69.85% for in-ear and T7 T8 EEG, respectively. Arousal classification accuracies were 72.89% and 78.7% for in-ear and T7 T8 EEG, respectively. These comparable accuracies indicate that in-ear EEG has potential for emotion classification as T7 and T8 electrodes.



From the main results, in-ear EEG was verified to be highly correlated to the nearby T7 and T8 scalp signals (correlation between T8 and in-EEG was approximately 0.853). This was expected as the 10-20 system scalp positions of T7 and T8 are just above left and right ears, respectively. They are in close proximity to ear channels. The results also correspond to the finding in previous work [17,37].
