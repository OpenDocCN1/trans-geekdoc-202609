# ## Conclusions

In this paper, we presented a deep heatmap regression approach for facial landmark detection. We employed FC-DenseNets to extract dense feature maps with an explicit kernel convolution for early-stage facial shape prediction. Starting with a suitable shape, the detected shapes were refined to match the ground-truth shape during the last stage of the architecture. Our local appearance initialization subset served as a local detector of facial landmarks in the first stage and the dilated skip convolution subset was carefully designed to increase the performance of our dense prediction architecture and accurate spatial information by aggregating multi-scale contextual information for the sake of local prediction of the first subset. The proposed method achieved superior, or at least comparable performance to state-of-the-art methods for challenging datasets, including LFW, HELEN, 3009 and AFLW2000-3D.
