It is feasible to evaluate the autonomic nervous system (ANS) function through non-invasive physiological phenomena like the electrodermal (EDA) and heart rate variability (HRV). The final goal concerning all of the stress detection research is to find ways to notify the user about their stress levels and help them to manage it the social, economic, and health-related consequences.

Traditional approaches for measuring stress are taken either by a psychologist interviewing the subject to fill in particular questionnaires designed explicitly for self-reporting. Such interviews require the constant presence of a trained psychologist specialist. Requesting the subjects to fill out lists of questionnaires and stress reports are the most widely adopted protocols de-standard as. However, these methods are cumbersome and entirely manual. There are also other concerns and drawbacks regarding the interviews, stress-reports, and questionnaires due to the way that different subjects commonly behave. These reports are based on or influenced by personal feelings, tastes, or opinions, they are highly subjective. As an example, some people tend to respond to questions in a manner that may be viewed by others [6]. Some responders may feel that sharing their private psychological feelings may them to hide and share their real feelings under stress. In contradiction to that, there are numerous cases in which responses are exaggerated [7]. Gender differences also play an role in how men and women report and express their stress levels under daily stressors [8]. However, for everyday life, the stress-reports are the closest labels to the ground.
