Finally, considering the relevance of emotional Frustration and cognitive Overload in the emergence of stress, which was investigated in many studies [70–73], we believe that our uulMAC database on emotional and cognitive load states can also be used for affective computing and machine learning applications in the field of stress research. The well-developed TSST–Trier Stress Test [70] employs a mental arithmetic task to induce high cognitive load (besides a social-evaluative part based on public speaking task). The Stroop Color Test [71] employs a word-color task to induce high cognitive load and was further adopted by Choi et al to develop a wearable stress monitoring system. In a similar context, a multimodal dataset was recently collected under the SWELL project [73] to induce stress by manipulating the working conditions of the subjects through mail interruptions and time pressure. Based on these studies, we will investigate in our future work the application of our database to the field of stress recognition research and/or deep learning approaches can be applied to transfer features and classifiers created on the uulMAC dataset into the stress classification scenario.
