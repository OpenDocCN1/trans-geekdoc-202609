# 16.5 PRACTICAL ASPECTS OF GRAPHENE SYMFET TECHNOLOGY

Nowadays, hardware security has become a big concern for IP privacy and hardware Trojans [14]. The Trojans have triggered the researchers to design the applications with circuit protection and spiteful logic detection from different perspectives. In this chapter, emerging technologies are enhanced by their unique properties for hardware security domain applications. Circuit structures including power regulators, camouflaging gates, and polymorphic gates are measured to prove the high efficiency of nanowire FETs and graphene SymFETs for circuit protection and IP privacy prevention applications. Simulation results of the devices designate that extremely efficient and secure circuit design structures can be attained via the custom of emerging technologies. Overall, the knowledge of using nanoelectronics like graphene symmetric FET and silicon nanowire FET structures for security applications will be a new platform and interesting path of research for both electronic and security researchers [50–52].
