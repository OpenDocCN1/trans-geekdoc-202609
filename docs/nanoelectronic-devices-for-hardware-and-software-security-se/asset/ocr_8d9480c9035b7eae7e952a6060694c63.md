# 13.7 STUDY ON WAVEGUIDAVE BEND STRUCTURE AND ITS DESIGN

Frequently, a waveguide-based complete system realization requires bend structures. This is essential to mitigate the extra losses associated with degradation of the device or its interfacing circuit. It can affect the phase response of the whole assembly along with degradation. Hence, it should be modeled well, considering all fabrications intricacies. In this work, since multiple bend structures were designed (Figure 13.16), and simulated results were compared with the right-angled straight waveguide configuration. In the right-angled bend, surface area is nearer to the bending region, which basically increases the capacitance of transmission line; hence the characteristics impedance reduced. To maintain the impedance as constant, surface area should be kept equal to that of other portions of the waveguide, and this is done by making the chamfered bend with a radius equal to the lateral dimension of the waveguide. The result of the analysis is depicted in Figure 13.17. It is noticed that the matching profile of the waveguide structure is improved in the bending structure if the chamfering technique is used. Here, IL signifies the insertion loss and RL denotes the return loss parameters.
