#  16.4 GRAPHENE SYMMETRIC FET (SYMFET)

#  16.4.1 Fabrication of Graphene SymFET

The graphene h-BN graphene SymFET structure is shown in Figure 16.3. The graphene is obtained by chemical vapor deposition (CVD) and moved onto 90 nm-thick SiO2 on a silicon substrate. By patterning the graphene, Ni/Au contacts were deposited on this bottom layer of graphene. A Ti layer was deposited as a protection layer using e-beam evaporation. Hexagonal boron nitride was grown on the Ni by the CVD method. Ammonia was heated to the substrate with the presence of Ar/H2 gas and acts as an h-BN precursor. This process creates a multilayer (3–4) h-BN, and it was identified by the TEM characterization. Then the graphene was formed on the transferred h-BN.
