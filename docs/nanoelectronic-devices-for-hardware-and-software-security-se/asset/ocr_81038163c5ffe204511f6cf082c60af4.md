Polarization of optical field is considered in the gain saturation process, and polarization dependence is negligible in the XGM and XPS process of SOA. The electric field propagation in the z-axis is given by the standard wave equation:

$$\frac{\partial^2 E(z,t)}{\partial z^2} + n^2\frac{\partial^2 E(z,t)}{\partial t^2} = -\frac{1}{\varepsilon_0 c^2}\frac{\partial^2 P}{\partial t^2}$$ (9.1)

The relationship between polarization P and susceptibility $\chi$ is given as $P = \chi E$

$$\chi(N) = \frac{nc}{\omega}(\alpha - j)g(N)$ (9.2)

Carrier dependent density $g(N)$ is calculated by

$$\g(N) = \Gamma[\alpha(N-N_0)] - \alpha_p$$ (9.3)

Wave equation is given by

$$\frac{\partial E}{\partial z} = -\frac{1+j\alpha}{2}g(z,t)E(z,t)$ (9.4)

Integrating in z direction

$$\E(L,t) = E(0,t)\exp\left[\frac{1}{2}\int_0^L g(z,t)dz\right]$$ (9.5)

Carrier density rate equation is given as

$$\frac{dN}{dt} = \frac{I}{qV} - \frac{N}{\tau_s}\frac{g(n)}{\omega}|E(t,z)|^2$$ (9.6)
