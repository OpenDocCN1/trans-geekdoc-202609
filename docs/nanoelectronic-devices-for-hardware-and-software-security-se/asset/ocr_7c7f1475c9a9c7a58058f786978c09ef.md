The utilization of both types of BTBT (i.e., line and point tunneling) by the device stress increases the gate electric field from the top of the tunneling junction when the transistor is in the ON-state. As the band-to-band generation rate is strongly dependent on the electric field, the enhanced electric field enhances the tunneling current significantly.
