The reduction of gate length in CMOS devices creates gate leakage that causes the short barrier between source and channel. This phenomenon is known as the short channel effect (SCE). This undesired phenomenon can be suppressed by selecting new geometries with different working [5].

Device designs are not only facing problems in further reduction beyond 40 nm, but also it is difficult to overcome other effects, such as thermal noise due integration density problems of these devices [1]. The doped bulk materials change their properties when the device size shrinks beyond a limit, including avalanche breakdown, which may occur on applying the high electric field and increases the probability of device damage. The increased density of transistors is also responsible for thermal heat dissipation and noise, which results in a wrong output due to malfunctioning [1], [7]. As a result, researchers have explored new types of novel devices, including new materials and new geometries to operate at high frequencies.
