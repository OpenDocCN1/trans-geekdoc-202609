The tabulated values show that $E_b$ O-termination strengthens the stability of the GeNR structures (both AgGeNR and Zig cases) also suggest that the higher the concentration of O-atom at the edge of GeNRs, the more stable is the overall system. As a result, both O-AgGeNR-O and O-ZGeNR-O are reported to have high values of $E_b$ (viz. -26.07 eV, -13.65 eV, respectively). The electronic properties were tuned for AgGeNR and ZigNR based on the calculated bandgap using E-k diagrams. The tabulated values suggest that the band gap typically reduces the concentration of elemental O-atom at the edges. This typical behavior, on one hand, can be attributed to the symmetry of edge terminating elements. But also, on the other hand, since the charge induced at the edges of AgNRs from O-atom is much larger than the H-atom, the band edges appear near the Fermi level, resulting in much wider bandgap (for O-AgGeNR case). Hence, we can infer that O-functionalized AgNRs are semiconducting or metallic depending upon the site of O-termination. For ZGeNR, the case is quite straightforward, where all the terminations are metallic with almost zero bandgap. This implies that higher concentration of O-atoms at the edges of ZGeNR improves the metallicity of the structure to greater extent, which underscores its utility
