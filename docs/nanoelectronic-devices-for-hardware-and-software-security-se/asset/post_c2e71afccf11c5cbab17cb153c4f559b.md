# Editors

**Dr. Balwinder Raj (IEEE'2006)** has been an Associate Professor at National Institute of Technical Training and Research, Chandigarh, since 2019. Earlier, he worked as Assistant Professor at ABV-IIIT Gwalior (an autonomous institute established by Ministry of HRD). He received Best Teacher Award from Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE) New Delhi. His core areas of research are classical/non-classical semiconductor device modeling, nanoelectronics and their applications in hardware-aware security, sensors and circuit design, FinFET-based memory design, digital/analog VLSI design and FPGA implementation.

**Dr. Arun Kumar Singh** is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication, Punjab Engineering College (Deemed to be University), Chandigarh. Earlier to PEC, he had worked as Lecturer at Global Institute of Technology from July 2004 to 2006. His core research interests focus on novel, high-speed semiconductor nanodevices operating at room temperature, terahertz frequencies, and their application in energy harvesting.
