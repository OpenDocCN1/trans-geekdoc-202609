It is noteworthy to mention that varying the source doping concentration in one way may not necessarily affect directly the OFF-state leak currents in TFETs. Without changing the semiconductor material, the TFET ON-state current can also be enhanced using a technique called pocket doping, as depicted in Figure 2.5.

The pocket region depletes and leaves ionized positive charge. However, the pocket region does not alter the potential of the intrinsic region for a gate voltage as well as drain voltage. Large electric fields around the tunneling region tend to increase the generation rate, thereby enhancing the tunneling current. Concurrently, this extra field ensures that the current is raised directly after the inter-band tunneling is switched on. In consequence of this, the point subthreshold swing is lessened. Optimization of the pocket doping concentration, width, and activating of the dopants has been explored and established for the pocket TFET, where an average subthreshold slope of 66 mV/dec with ID$_{ON}$ = 1.4 µA/µm is attained [36].
