## 12.3.2 Analysis of Various Parameters of Nano-Structured Patch Antenna with Photonic Substrate

The design of the nano-structured honeycomb patch antenna is compact; if the radius of the slots is reduced, then it reduces the gain and bandwidth. To achieve the required gain and bandwidth, the maximum distance between the centers of the slots is evaluated for certain dimensional specifications. As this process was done, hole radius $r = 2 \mu m$ was determined as optimum value.

12.8 provides the return loss for the nano-structured honeycomb PhC patch antenna. The return loss of the antenna on PhC is -44.14 dB at 14.72 GHz for the $S_{11}$ parameter. The conventional patch antenna resonant frequency is obtained at 11.30 GHz, with the return loss of -35.44 dB. Therefore, the return loss is higher for resonant frequency for a nano-structured honeycomb patch antenna with PhC substrate.

12.9 shows the VSWR of the nano-structured honeycomb PhC substrate patch antenna. The VSWR of the antenna on PhC is 1.014 at 14.72 GHz. The VSWR of conventional antenna is 1.035 at resonant frequency 11.30 GHz. Therefore, the VSWR of the honeycomb patch antenna with PhC substrate is less compared to the conventional antenna.

12.10 shows the directivity of the honeycomb patch antenna; the directivity is 5.90 dB at resonant frequency 14.72 GHz, which is greater than the directivity of the conventional antenna.

12.11 shows the gain of the honeycomb patch antenna; the gain is 3.85 dB at resonant frequency 14.72 GHz, which is higher than the gain of the conventional antenna.

The designed nano-structured patch antenna along PhC substrate has obtained high gain and directivity when compared to the conventional antenna. Table 12.2 describes the comparison of resonant frequency, return loss, VSWR, and gain. From the table, the designed PhC antenna parameter is better than the conventional antenna.

The conventional honeycomb patch antenna resonates at 11.30 THz, with the antenna characteristics of -35.44 dB return loss, 5.11 dB directivity, and 3.5 dB of gain. When the conventional antenna is replaced by a PhC antenna with a square lattice structure, the characteristics are enhanced. PhC helps to diminish the sidelobes and improve the antenna efficiency by forbidding the electromagnetic waves that propagate into the substrate. The dielectric PhC acts as a frequency filter and restricts the waves from propagating into the substrate. The PhC antenna with THz frequency reduces data loss, due to which higher data rate and bandwidth are attained. The output of the antenna on PhC is better than the conventional antenna.
