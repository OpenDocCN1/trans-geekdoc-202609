# 5.6.2 Polymeric Memristors

The polymeric memristors are based on unique plastic materials. Single or a group of molecules are able to conduct and switch switches and memorize information using charge accumulation. Polymer-based resistive RAM samples information as the low or high conductance when an external electric field is applied. The conductance states can be read destructively. Because electric conductivity is the multiplication of charge volumetric concentration and ionic mobility, changes in either of these, or both, can cause changes in the element resistance states.
