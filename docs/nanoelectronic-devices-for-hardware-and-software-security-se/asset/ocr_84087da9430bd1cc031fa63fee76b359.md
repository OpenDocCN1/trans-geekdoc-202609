The conventional honeycomb patch antenna resonates at 11.30 THz, with the antenna characteristics of -35.44 dB return loss, 5.11 dB directivity, and 3.5 dB of gain. When the conventional antenna is replaced by a PhC antenna with a square lattice structure, the characteristics are enhanced. PhC helps to diminish the sidelobes and improve the antenna efficiency by forbidding the electromagnetic waves that propagate into the substrate. The dielectric PhC acts as a frequency filter and restricts the waves from propagating into the substrate. The PhC antenna with THz frequency reduces data loss, due to which higher data rate and bandwidth are attained. The output of the antenna on a PhC is better than the conventional antenna.
