## 15.5 CONCLUSIONS AND FUTURE SCOPE

### 15.5.1 Conclusions

The scaling issues in conventional electronics devices create the opportunity for novel devices to process the high speed data. The FTBR and TTJ are the types of novel-rectifiers analogous to a bridge rectifier. The high speed materials such as III-V heterostructures and graphene can generate the response at THz frequencies. The rectifiers can be used as voltage inverter and frequency doubler at zero temperature in addition to other applications as THz imaging, RFID.

### 15.5.2 Future Scope

Graphene-based rectifiers can be utilized as thermo-electric generators, which will respond to the change in temperature at interface. These thermo-electric generators can use the heat generated in circuitry and convert it into electrical signals. Graphene-based rectifiers can be integrated with antenna to perform energy harvesting using electromagnetic radiations. The generated DC output can be used to charge the mobile batteries. This will save and be used in future to perform digital operations such as logic gates, etc.
