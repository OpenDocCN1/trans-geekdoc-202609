# 3.1.7.6 Responsivity and Quantum Efficiency

In addition to response time and detectivity, the different parameters for evaluating photosensor efficiency are responsivity ($R_\phi$) and quantum efficiency ($Q_e$). The $Q$ gain defined as the number of charge carriers that pass for each photon absorbed between the electrodes and the responsivity of a photosensor. $R_\phi$ is defined as the photocurrent generated on the effective area of the sensors per unit of light power. The expression for $R_\phi$ and $Q_e$ is shown by equations 3.12 and 3.13, where $h$ represents the Planck constant, $q$ is the electron charge, and incident radiation wavelength is represented by $\lambda$ [47].

```
$Responsivity (R_\phi) = \frac{\text{Available Photocurrent},I_{ph}}{\text{Incident optical power},P_o} (3.12)$
```

```
$Quantum Efficiency (Q_e) = R_\phi \frac{h}{q\lambda} (3.13)$
```
