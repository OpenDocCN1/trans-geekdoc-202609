# 11.7 OBSERVATION

From the following simulations, different values have been measured/ calculated and they are provided in the Table 11.4.

From this observation, it has been concluded that a 4 to 2 encoder is consuming 11 cell structures to compare to a 4 to 2 priority encoder and octal to binary encoder, which are 17 and 42 cell count, respectively. Hence, the 4 to 2 encoder is having lower complexities by 54% and 35% than the 4 to 2 priority encoder and octal to binary encoder.
