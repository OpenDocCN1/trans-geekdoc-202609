The benchmarking of the vertical TFETs (point and line tunneling) is discussed in the previous section along with the transfer characteristic, which shows that the Ge Source DD Vert TFET possesses the steepest subthreshold slope in addition to the high ON-state current compared to the diverse line. Total capacitance-voltage curve of the LG-TFET, HTG-TFET, and the Ge Source DD Vert TFET is also reported in Figure 2.14b. The total gate capacitance comprises the Miller capacitances, including the gate to source capacitance and the gate to drain capacitance, which is the main contributor, as discussed in the previous sections. This indicate that the depletion capacitance at the Ge Source DD Vert TFET is highest among the structures. However, as the magnitudes are all in the same order (femto far) and only a small difference is observed, this will not induce several capacitance problems in circuit designing. This shows that with proper implementation and a well-designed TFET structure, implementing line tunneling can achieve a much better overall device performance.
