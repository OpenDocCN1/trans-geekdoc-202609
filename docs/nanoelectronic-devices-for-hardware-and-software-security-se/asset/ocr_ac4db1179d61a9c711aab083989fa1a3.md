3.14 shows the simulated voltage transfer characteristics with different dielectric constant value. The material used as a dielectric constant is SiO2 (k = 3.9), Si3N4 (k = 7.0), and HfO2 (k = 21), respectively. From the graph, it can be seen that the HfO2 material will show the maximum output voltage transfer characteristics from transition 0 to transition 1, with comparison to the Si3N4 and HfO2.
