# 2.7.1 Germanium Source with Overlap TFET

A germanium TFET employing both line and point tunneling is shown in Figure 2.8a. A gate sidewall spacer of silicon nitride (SiN₄) of 8 nm is used. An oxide thickness of 3 nm is used. The thickness of the silicon body is 70 nm, while the germanium source, which the gate region overlaps, is of 21 nm. In the On-state, an inversion surface layered electrons can be tunneled through the junction to the inverted layer of the germanium source to supply electrons and pass through the silicon channel region. The gate overlaps the source region, and this region is not sufficiently doped to allow the region to be inverted for acceptable gate voltages. In addition, appropriate functionality of transistor ensures the silicon body gate inversion threshold voltage is less than that of TBTBT, whose drain voltage could be coupled to the germanium source potential, splitting quasi-Fermi levels to activate TBTBT. At this point, the device possesses a to an off-state leakage current of 0.1 pA/µm for gate bias of 0.5 V and an ON current of 0.4 µA/µm. Hence an ON/OFF current ratio of the order 10⁶ at a low voltage operation of 0.5 V is achieved for the germanium source with overlap TFET [46].
