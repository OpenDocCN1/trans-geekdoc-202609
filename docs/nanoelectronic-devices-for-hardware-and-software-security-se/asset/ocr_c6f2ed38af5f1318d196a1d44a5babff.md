As stated earlier, the tunable optoelectronic properties of Hg,,Cd,Te semiconductor material makes a promising choice for the development of IR broadband photodetectors [21], [53], [59], [74], [75]. Several Hg,,Cd,Te-based high performance IR photodetectors with different device architectures, such as p–n [22], [68], p–i–n [74], UPD [62], and dual-band IR photodetector [23], [77] are being reported at cryogenic temperatures. Hg,,Cd,Te technology continues to be primarily for military- and civilian-related safety applications for the detection of a condition or an object [78]. Another common application is IR-sensitive motion detection for home-security systems. On the other hand, the ultrahigh mobility and higher temperature operation due to the lower thermo-generation rate of graphene led to the development of graphene/Hg,,Cd,Te-based p–n [39], [79], p–i–n [42], and barrier type [62], [63] IR photodetector architectures with superior performance that can be utilized for the detection of weak signal and security-related applications.
