# 2.9 CONCLUSION

In this chapter, we established a fundamental understanding of the TFET structure and its operating principle with some important electrical characteristics of TFETs. We looked into the physics behind the band to tunneling phenomenon. We listed several strategies researchers have explored to solve the low ON-state current problem as well as to improve various characteristics in TFET. Various TFET device structure implementations that utilize point and line tunneling as well both point and line tunneling were also discussed and discussed. The TFET structure that adopted vertical tunneling (line tunneling) possessed a better subthreshold swing as well as better overall performance. Among the vertical TFETs, the Ge Source DD Vert TFET has attained a better ON-state current of $2.76 \times 10^{-6}$ A/µm, a total gate capacitance of 7.73 fF/µm, and a point and average subthreshold swing of 7 mV/dec and 21.4 mV/dec, respectively. Finally, the mixed mode Ge Source DD Vert TFET was implemented, and the transient characteristic showed an average delay of 0.073 ps, which is significantly lower compared to the conventional TFET, which is crucial in TFET circuit performance. The Ge Source DD Vert Tunnel is indeed considered to be a feasible candidate for future ultra-low power and high-performance technology.
