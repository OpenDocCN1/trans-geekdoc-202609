Crop yield estimation and yield mapping are two essential processes in precision agriculture. This helps farmers use their available resources more effectively and gain access to more accurate representations of their fields. Farmers with accurate production estimates can more effectively manage their crop logistics, crop storage, and sales and account for losses.
