FIGURE 6.2 IoT based on smart healthcare devices.

+   * **Robotic Devices:** IoT-based small robotic devices help doctors during surgery.

+   * **Brain Swelling Sensors:** Such devices help surgeons check various parameters that are causing brain swelling and injuries by measuring pressure on the brain.

+   * **Smart Pills:** These travel inside the human body and collect information in the form of images and send them to devices like smart watches or wearable devices. With the help of the application, healthcare providers analyze gastrointestinal problems [23].

+   * **Remote Patient Monitoring Devices:** Remote devices collect patient data like blood pressure, sugar level, oxygen level and heart rate and share them remotely with healthcare providers.
