## INTRODUCTION

The application of artificial intelligence in the enterprise will profoundly change the way businesses work around the globe. Companies are beginning to incorporate AI into their business operations on daily bases with the target of saving money, boosting efficiency, generating insights and creating new markets for business expansion. There are AI-powered applications to customer service, maximize sales, optimize supply chains.
