# Levels of Explainability

# Global Explainability: Understanding the Whole Model

Global explainability delves into the inner workings of an AI model, providing a comprehensive understanding of its overall architecture, functionality, and decision-making processes. It’s concerned with explaining how the model behaves across a range of inputs, rather than focusing on specific instances. Through an analysis of the model’s framework, algorithms, and hyperparameters, global explainability provides a deeper understanding of the model’s capabilities, limitations, and prejudices. This level of transparency is crucial in comprehending how the model performs on unfamiliar data and in recognizing potential areas for enhancement.

# Local Explainability: Understanding Individual Predictions

Local explainability zooms in on individual predictions or decisions made by an AI system, providing a detailed understanding of the reasoning behind each outcome. It answers questions like “Why did the model classify this image as a cat?” or “Why was this loan application rejected?” By analyzing the model’s behavior at the instance level, local explainability identifies the most influential features and their contributions to the prediction. Having a clear understanding of how AI systems work is crucial in establishing confidence, particularly in critical situations where a single prediction can have major impacts.

# Benefits of Explainability

Facilitating User Understanding and Trust: Explainability helps users understand how and why decisions are made, promoting trust in the system. When individuals can comprehend the reasoning behind a decision, they are more likely to accept it and trust the artificial intelligence.

# Supporting Compliance with Regulations and Standards

As regulations around AI accountability and fairness evolve, explainability is becoming a key requirement. An example of this is the requirement set by the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for automated decision-making systems to provide explanations to those who are impacted [8].
