# # Continuous Monitoring for Bias Drift

Continuous monitoring for bias drift is essential to ensuring that AI models maintain fairness over time. **Bias drift** occurs when an AI model's behavior changes as a result of shifts in the data it processes, leading to biased outcomes for certain groups. This can happen as real-world environments evolve, making it crucial to track bias beyond the model's initial deployment.

To monitor bias drift, organizations must regularly evaluate key **fairness metrics**, such as demographic parity, equalized odds, and disparate impact. By assessing the model's performance across different demographic groups, deviations or unfair trends can be identified early. Automated tools can alert teams to significant changes in model outputs related to sensitive attributes like race, gender, or age.

Additionally, **continual auditing and retraining** are key to correcting bias drift. When drift is detected, retraining the model with more recent, representative data or adjusting for new societal or demographic shifts helps realign it with fairness standards. This ongoing process ensures that AI systems remain fair, accurate, and unbiased throughout their operational life.
