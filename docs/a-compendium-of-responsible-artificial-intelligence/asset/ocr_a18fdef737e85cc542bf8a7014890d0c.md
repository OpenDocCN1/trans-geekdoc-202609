# The Role of Human-in-the-loop Systems

One way to achieve this under the broad scope of responsible AI is by integrating cybernetic interface technologies like Human in The Loop (HITL), particularly in sectors like finance or short-term banking. The human-in-the-loop approach involves incorporating human agents into the AI decision-making process to ensure efficiency and accountability regarding ethical concerns. This is especially valuable in finance, where decisions significantly impact personal finances and the economy. Figure 3 illustrates the HITL concept.

Fig. 3 Overview of an HITL system

In finance, HITL systems enhance AI decision-making by incorporating human judgment and insights. This human element is crucial in extreme situations whereby the AI might lack comprehensive understanding of certain economic behaviours and predictions. For instance, in credit score assessment, AI can efficiently process vast amounts of data to determine credit eligibility. However, humans can add valuable context, such as, explaining a loan applicant's income. This combination leads to superior decision-making, where both approaches advocate for a balanced, moderate approach, acknowledging that intelligent machines are not as infallible as they are often perceived to be [65].

One of the key aspects of HITL systems is to relieve the bias and any potential shortcomings that may arise from the use of AI algorithms.
