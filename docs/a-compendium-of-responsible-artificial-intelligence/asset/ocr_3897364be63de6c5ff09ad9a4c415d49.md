# Examples of AI Regulation in Practice

# Notable Regulatory Actions and Their Impact

Notable regulatory actions in AI have significantly shaped the landscape of responsible governance, illustrating both the challenges and successes of implementing regulatory frameworks in practice. An important instance of this is the implementation of the AI Act by the European Union, which seeks to create a thorough set of rules for governing AI in all member countries. This significant law categorizes AI systems according to their potential risks, imposing strict mandates on high-risk uses like biometric recognition and essential infrastructure, while encouraging progress in lower-risk areas. The AI Act has established a model for how governing bodies can tackle the complex issues presented by AI advancements, prioritizing openness, responsibility, and security. A noteworthy regulatory measure is the algorithmic accountability legislation that has been implemented in various states in the United States. This legislation requires companies to perform evaluations on the effects of their algorithms and reveal any possible biases. This approach has led to greater awareness and scrutiny of AI systems, pushing organizations to adopt more ethical practices and improve algorithmic fairness. Additionally, the introduction of the Digital Services Act (DSA) by the EU seeks to regulate online platforms and address issues such as misinformation and harmful content, which directly impacts AI-driven content moderation. These regulatory actions highlight the importance of creating tailored regulations that address specific risks while supporting technological advancement. It can be understood from evaluating the results of these efforts that successful regulation of AI involves finding a middle ground between protecting the public's interests and promoting a climate that encourages innovation [17].
