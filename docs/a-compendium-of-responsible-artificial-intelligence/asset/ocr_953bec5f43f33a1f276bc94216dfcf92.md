# # 8 Responsible AI Governance and Regulation

Mohammad Israr¹, Saba Khan²*, Venya Gupta³, Abhishek Sharma³ and Devanshu Tiwari⁴

This chapter looks into the critical role of governance and regulation toward promoting Responsible AI through frameworks and practices that ensure the ethical and accountable development and deployment of artificial intelligence. It begins with the five key principles that define Responsible AI governance, focusing on transparency, fairness, and stakeholder engagement amidst the analysis of current and emerging regulatory landscapes at regional and sectoral levels. It addresses challenges related to existing policies that need to be overcome to keep pace with the rapid development of technology advocating for more adaptive regulatory approaches. The chapter includes case studies showcasing successful governance models that illustrate best practices in collaboration with governments, industry, and civil society. In also emphasizes the importance of international cooperation in standardizing common standards and guidelines in AI governance. Written to provide policymakers and practitioners with actionable insights it guides them through the complexities of AI governance to ensure AI technologies support societal values and lead to public good.
