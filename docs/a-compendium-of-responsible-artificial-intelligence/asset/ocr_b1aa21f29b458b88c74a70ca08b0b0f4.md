# A Call to Action for a More Responsible and Inclusive AI-driven Financial Future

As we come to our final thoughts on the issue of ethical AI in finance, we present a cry for the next evolution of financial services – AI Deep Dive, but this time more responsible and inclusive. However, moving toward that goal would be very hard unless many stakeholders, including technologists and ethicists, policymakers, and the finance community, work together to make AI’s positive aspects available to everyone while ethical principles are not compromised. The first ingredient in that call to action is a pledge for unceasing moral alertness. With the long-term progress of AI technologies, the control of its growth and the development of new technologies would have to be checked against an existing ethical framework. Ethical considerations should never be the last to be addressed in the production of AI. Instead, it would mean building ethical risk evaluation processes when creating AI and safeguarding those processes as the technology and its applications change. A second essential part of this call to action is to deepen the penetration of AI-enhanced financial services.

There is tremendous scope for AI to extend and make the availability of basic financial services to unhedged or underserved demographics more configurable. However, it will be crucial to customize the AI systems to accommodate the targeted audience. This includes language translation, culture, and the engagement of audiences with different levels of digital access. For users to be engaged and developers to provide relevant services, potential users will need to be aware of such varied needs for AI-based financial services to come to fruition. Also, there is a need for more collaborative efforts and dialogue between all stakeholders on AI-based finance. This entails provisions that enable communication between their practitioners (technology or IT), moralists or lawyers, financial advisers, lawmakers, and clientele. This collaboration is also expected to help frame better ethical practices in AI finance and create broader implementation strategies.
