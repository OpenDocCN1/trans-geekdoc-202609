# • The Importance of Financial Experts in AI Applications

The financial sector, including economists and analysts and those working in banks, are beneficial sources of insights. Their coverage of financial products, marketing approaches, and consumer trends is helpful when creating AI products applicable to finance. Financial experts assist with safeguarding the correct design of an AI system in the areas of financial risk, market risk, and regulatory constraints. Additionally, they work with technologist experts and ethical researchers to develop functional, profitable, and ethical AI technologies. The contribution of financial experts in the development of AI is addressed in several studies, including that of Michael Chui [37], which focuses on the role of AI in finance.

In the dispensation of AI in finance, the importance of collaborating with ethicists, technologists and business experts cannot be overemphasized. Collaboration across disciplines holds that AI systems will be designed holistically, taking ethics, technology, and economics into account. As AI continues to revolutionize the finance industry, it is essential to promote such collaboration so that this opportunity can be optimally utilized in an ethical and regulatory-compliant manner.
