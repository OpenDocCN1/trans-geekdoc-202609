# 2 Avatar Guard: Customizable, Secure, and Private Virtual Identities for Video Conferencing

R. Deepa1*, Raja Prathap V M2, Deepak Kumar Jain3, Sanjana Babu4,
Mohammad Shakeel4 and Harsha Vardh4

In today’s era of remote work and virtual meetings, ensuring privacy and security
in video conferencing is paramount. The rise in working from home, online
learning, and virtual socializing has significantly increased the use of video
conferencing apps. While these platforms have greatly improved our ability to
connect digitally, they have also raised privacy concerns. Many users are worried
about the potential for unauthorized access to their personal information and
invasion of their private spaces. Many traditional video conferencing platforms
often fall short in protecting information and personal identities. However, there is
a solution that aims to tackle these concerns: introducing AvatarGuard, a platform
offering secure and private virtual identities. AvatarGuard allows users to create
avatars that can represent them in the virtual world. Users can personalize their
avatars for video conferences enabling them to engage in meetings without
revealing their appearance or personal details. These avatars can be customized
to reflect the users’ comfort level and individuality, providing a sense of control
and personalization during meetings. What distinguishes this solution apart is its
advanced facial recognition technology and background replacement features
which together greatly enhance security and privacy during video conferences.
By substituting faces with avatars, users can maintain their anonymity and protect
their identity from unwanted exposure. Additionally, the platform provides
customization options for appearance and virtual backgrounds, allowing users to
express themselves freely during meetings. This level of personalization fosters
a collaborative meeting environment by promoting relaxation and enjoyment
among participants.
