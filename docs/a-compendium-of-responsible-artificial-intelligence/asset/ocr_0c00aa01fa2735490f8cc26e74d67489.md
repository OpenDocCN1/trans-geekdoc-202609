# ## Introduction

AI's coming has brought about a revolutionary epoch in different fields, with finance being one of the areas most affected. Traditionally known for its reliance on quantitative analysis and decision-making, finance found AI to be its trusted partner, prompting numerous innovations and improvements [1]. However, incorporating AI into this industry is not straightforward. The application of AI in finance goes beyond technological progress; it converges with ethical, social, and legal aspects, which call for a detailed understanding of what it implies [2].

AI occupies multiple positions in finance, including boosting customer service via chatbots and employing complicated algorithms for high-frequency trading (HFT). Such developments have made operations more efficient while at the same time opening new doors in terms of financial analysis and forecasting[3]. However, as more critical tasks like credit scoring and fraud detection are automated by AI systems, there is an outcry over issues like bias, transparency and accountability [4]. Ethical problems posed by the use of AI in finance are not just theoretical but have real impacts on individuals and companies. Therefore, solving these ethical matters assumes the utmost gravity as global financial institutions increasingly depend on AI.

# ## Brief Overview of the Rise of AI in Various Sectors

The widespread deployment of AI across nearly all sectors is a game changer in the technological landscape, leaving significant impacts in various areas, such as healthcare, education, and, notably, business. The penetrating ability of AI lies in its ability to process large volumes of data, standardize, and execute high-level processes, making it possible to destabilize former approaches in different areas [5]. AI is already becoming one of the most important facilitators of efficiency and creativity in business and industry. The commercial application of AI technology has enabled other forms of partnership and provision of services which has fundamentally altered the business ecosystem[5].

AI has revolutionized various aspects of healthcare in remarkable ways, including diagnosing diseases[6], patient care and e-health services. AI technology in healthcare cuts back on many administrative and business processes and enables better clinical decisions, as illustrated during the COVID-19 pandemic[7]. Also, the education sector has dramatically improved with the rise of AI inventions. Management education has mainly seen a spur of adoption of AI and virtual reality technologies in educational institutions post-pandemic. This integration has taught children the information and skills they need to thrive in a technologically driven world [8].

Consequently, the impact of AI in the finance sector mirrors its influence in other industries. In recent times and the years to come, AI applications in the banking sector are expected to enhance customer services and improve the efficiency of processes such as fraud detection and credit scoring.
