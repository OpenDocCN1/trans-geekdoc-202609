# Automatic Surgical Settings

The Da Vinci Surgical System is an example of responsible AI in healthcare. This robotic system is used in minimally invasive surgeries, assisting surgeons by accurately applying forces and reducing the risk of human error. The system ensures that the AI functions only as a supportive tool, and that the surgeon maintains complete self-restraint, ensuring patient safety and adhering to strict ethical standards in clinical practice
