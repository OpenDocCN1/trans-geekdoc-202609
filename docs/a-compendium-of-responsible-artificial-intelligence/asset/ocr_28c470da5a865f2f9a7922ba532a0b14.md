This begins with defining “AI for Good” and exploring various domains where AI can make a significant impact, including healthcare, education, poverty alleviation, and the environment. Applications include predictive analytics for disaster prevention and response, machine learning for wildlife conservation, and data-driven approaches to combating climate change. The chapter highlights best practices through case studies and success stories illustrating how governments, NGOs, and the private sector collaborate to promote AI for social good. # ## Introduction to AI for Good

Artificial Artificial Intelligence (AI) has the potential to be a powerful tool for addressing some of the most pressing global, social and environmental challenges [1]. “AI for Good” refers to the use of AI technologies to drive positive outcomes in areas such as healthcare, education, climate change, and social justice. By harnessing the capabilities of AI—such as data analysis, pattern recognition, and predictive modeling—organizations can tackle complex problems that were previously difficult to address. From enhancing disaster response efforts to optimizing resource management and improving access to quality healthcare, AI for Good focuses on applying technology in manners that are advantageous to the entirety of society. As the impact of AI grows, so does the need for ensuring that these technologies are developed responsibly and equitably, making sure that their benefits reach everyone, particularly marginalized communities.
