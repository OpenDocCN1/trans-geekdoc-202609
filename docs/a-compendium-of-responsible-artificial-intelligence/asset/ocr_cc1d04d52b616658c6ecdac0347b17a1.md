# Continuous Monitoring and Validation

Once an autonomous system is deployed, testing does not cease. Continuous monitoring and validation are essential to ensure that the gadget operates safely and ethically in real-world environments.

Autonomous structures frequently encounter new situations post-deployment that were not anticipated during development. Continuous monitoring allows developers to track system performance, identify anomalies, and gather feedback to enhance future iterations. This is especially crucial for AI-driven systems, where models might degrade over time or struggle to adapt to new environments.

Additionally, continuous validation ensures that the system remains aligned with evolving regulations, safety standards, and societal expectations. AI fashions may also require retraining to stay up-to-date with new datasets or to address newly identified ethical concerns, such as bias or fairness issues.
