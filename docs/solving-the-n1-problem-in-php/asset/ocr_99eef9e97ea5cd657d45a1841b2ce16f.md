# About the Author

Paul M. Jones is an internationally recognized PHP expert who has worked as everything from junior developer to VP of Engineering in all kinds of organizations (corporate, military, non-profit, educational, medical, and others). He blogs professionally at paul-m-jones.com and is a regular speaker at various PHP conferences.

Paul's latest open-source project is Aura for PHP¹. Previously, he was the architect behind the Solar Framework², and was the creator of the Savant template system³. He was a founding contributor to the Zend Framework¹, the DB, DB, Table, and View components, and has written a series of authoritative benchmarks⁵ on dynamic framework performance⁶.

Paul was one of the first members of the PEAR project⁷. He is a voting member of the PHP Framework Interoperability Group⁸, where he sheed the PSR-1⁹ “Coding Standard” and PSR-2¹⁰ “Coding Style” recommendations, and was the primary author on the PSR-4¹¹ “Autoloader” recommendation. He was also a member of the Zend PHP 5.3 Certification education advisory board¹³.

In a previous career, Paul was an operations intelligence specialist for the US Air Force. In his spare time, he enjoys putting .308 holes in targets at 400 yards.
