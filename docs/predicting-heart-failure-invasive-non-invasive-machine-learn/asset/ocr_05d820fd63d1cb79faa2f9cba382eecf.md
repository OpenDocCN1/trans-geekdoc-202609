# 11.2.4 Bioimpedance Monitoring and Heart Failure

Bioimpedance analysis is a leading and economically feasible method utilized for estimating body composition as well as health monitoring. Bioimpedance analysis estimates the total body fluid and aids in monitoring the fluid levels in the human body. Heart failure patients can benefit from the application of bioimpedance analysis, which is based on the electrical properties of the human body. It measures how well the body resists the flow of electrical signals when a small electric current is passed through it. The fats in the body have a high resistance against an electric current, while the water has low resistance. There are multiple types of bioimpedance analysis systems available, including systems which are focused on estimating the body composition of the lower body alone (leg-to-leg bioimpedance analysis), upper body alone (hand-held bioimpedance analysis), and the whole body (hand-to-foot bioimpedance analysis). A weak or small electric current is transmitted from the electrodes in the feet to the abdominal region via the leg. In addition, the electrodes in the hand are used to send weak electric signals from the hand to the abdomen and leg via the arm.
