# Table 3.1 (Continued)

| Biomarker | Description | Related CVD | Clinical Assessment | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Myeloperoxidase (MPO) | MPO is an enzyme that is involved with lipid peroxidation and the production of reactive oxidant species | Atherosclerosis, acute coronary syndrome, and coronary artery disease | MPO provides early phase diagnosis of coronary artery disease, and causes damage in atherosclerotic tissues | [18, 26, 29] |
| Micro RNAs (miRNAs) | miRNAs, as non-coding RNA sequences, participate in gene expression regulation by inhibiting protein translation | Coronary artery disease, acute MI, heart failure, and stroke | miRNAs can be released from protein complexes such as HDL, apoptotic bodies, exosomes, and microvesicles to peripheral blood; elevated blood levels of circulating miRNAs are associated with CVDs | [26, 31, 32] |
| Growth differentiation factor (GDF-15) | GDF-15 is secreted as a protection against stress factors such as oxidative stress, cellular ischemia, and myocardial overload | Atrial fibrillation, acute coronary syndrome, heart failure | Elevated GDF-15 levels indicate the renal injury associated with cardiovascular stress | [17, 18, 22, 29] |
| Galectin-3 (Gal-3) | Gal-3 is involved in inflammatory and fibrotic processes of the body | Hypertrophied hearts, heart failure, and outflow tract tachycardias | Higher Gal-3 levels indicate a higher risk of mortality in end-stage heart failure patients | [17, 24, 30] |
| Heart fatty acid-binding protein (H-FABP) | H-FABP is one of the fatty acid-binding proteins that is released from myocardial tissue during myocardial ischemia | Atherosclerosis, acute coronary syndrome, and MI | H-FABP is a significant early clinical biomarker to exclude MI probability | [17, 18, 30] |
| Tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α) | TNF-α is a proinflammatory cytokine and is involved in inflammatory pathways in cells | Atherosclerosis, arteriosclerosis, platelet activation, and vascular dysfunction | TNF-α is used for the prediction of postoperative complications | [17, 22, 26] |
| Interleukin-6 (IL-6) | IL-6 belongs to the immune cytokine family, and it is associated with CRP activation and acute phase inflammation | Atherothrombosis and obesity | IL-6 levels are used for the evaluation of cardiovascular risks of patients | [17, 18, 28] |
