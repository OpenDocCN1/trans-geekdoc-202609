# 13.4 Integrating Wearables Technology into the Healthcare System

Different commercially available wearable devices can monitor steps, heart rate, oxygen saturation, blood pressure, and electrocardiogram (ECG) signals, which can help the clinician in decision-making and clinical intervention. Wearable devices typically, however, focus on predicting HF or cardiac arrhythmia with the help of clinicians. There is a limited number of devices available that can be used to automatically detect a clinical abnormality of the patient reliably without human intervention. Most of such devices are implantable devices; however, these devices are very expensive and only a few of them are available on the market. Therefore, a safe, reliable, and effective automatic system like a wearable device to keep track of patients' cardiac health is very important.
