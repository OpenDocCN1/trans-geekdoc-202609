## 9.1.2.4 ML Applications in Clinical Prognosis

Predicting hospital readmissions or mortality in patients with HF is a huge challenge. Therefore, several studies have been conducted to evaluate the clinical prognosis of HF patients in terms of readmission to hospital and of mortality prediction. Accurate and early prediction can lead to adaptations in patient management in order to decrease the risk of adverse events.
