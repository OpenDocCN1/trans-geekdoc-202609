# ## 4.4.5 Discussion and Results

The electromagnetic radiation technique is a safe method of wireless energy transfer, depending on being near a magnetic field. However, the power received from the oscillator is not enough to energize the implant SoC chip.

Transferred power wirelessly from a gap of 5 mm (width of the skin) depends on the efficiency of the Tx coil and the Rx coil, the alignment required to power-up the biosensor.
