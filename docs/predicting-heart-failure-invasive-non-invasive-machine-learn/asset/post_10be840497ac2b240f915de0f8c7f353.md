## 7.6 Conclusion and Future Work

In this study, different machine learning algorithms were studied to enhance the early detection of heart diseases. Specifically, five classifiers were utilized to improve the accuracy of heart disease predictions. Moreover, an extensive experiment was performed using two data sets. For each data set, the classifiers were applied using leave-one-out cross-validation. Results demonstrated that all classification algorithms are accurate and can produce good predictions. However, it confirms that the NB and SVM were ranked first across all evaluation measures and in both data sets, followed by RF in second position. This can lead us to an important conclusion about the role of uncertainty in classifying data. SVM and NB have completely different mechanisms for building the relationship between inputs and output. In future work, we will study the impact of feature selection and weighting on the overall performance of classifiers.
