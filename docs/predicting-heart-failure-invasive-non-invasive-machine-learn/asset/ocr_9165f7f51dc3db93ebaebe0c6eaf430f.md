+   The role of mHealth apps for the self-care of heart failure patients was discussed in this review and it was found that the mHealth apps have the potential to educate the heart failure patients about the disease, symptoms, precautions, etc. However, most of the existing apps lack user-centered design. +   The existing commercially available devices or proposed devices in the literature have not addressed the security of patients’ data during transfer as well as storage for better privacy and security of the patients’ latest information, where security techniques like encryption and steganography can be employed.

+   A mix of invasive and non-invasive devices can be employed in the future to get the benefit from both. A hybrid model which combines both invasive and non-invasive techniques can be developed.

+   An effective, efficient, and interdisciplinary collaboration between doctors and engineers is required to develop safe, reliable, and long-term devices to effectively monitor heart failure.

# 11.6 Conclusion

Heart failure is causing an economic burden on the hospital system, due to constant admission and readmission of the patients. It also affects the patients’ quality of life. Currently, heart failure is detected using the symptoms and signs which appear late and are not reliable. Non-invasive and invasive methods are available to detect heart failure at early stages. In recent years, the number of research activities in the field of remote monitoring techniques for heart failure monitoring is increasing rapidly. Non-invasive methods are easy to use and safe, but accuracy and reliability are compromised. Invasive methods are accurate and reliable, but the safety is questionable. Invasive methods are available only in a limited number of cases. In this chapter, the commercially available invasive and non-invasive devices for heart failure monitoring are discussed. Discussion of the advantages and disadvantages of these devices was included. Finally, a future outlook section was added to mention possible future directions in this field.
