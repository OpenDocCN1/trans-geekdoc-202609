# 4.5.2 Design a Full-Wave Bridge Rectenna

The RF-DC rectifier design uses a full-wave bridge rectenna. The rectifier’s input impedance (Zin = 50 Ω) is constructed to maximize the electricity transmission and boost the signal reflected from the sensor. As indicated in subsection 4.5.1, the fractal antenna design operates at 2.45 GHz with an output impedance of 50 Ω [55]. Figure 4.31 presents the schematic configuration of the rectifier circuit.

The power-up pump configuration used in this circuit is the modified Dickson power-up pump. This circuit has been used extensively in other RF energy harvesting projects and closely resembles the form of the Cockcroft–Walton voltage multiplier.
