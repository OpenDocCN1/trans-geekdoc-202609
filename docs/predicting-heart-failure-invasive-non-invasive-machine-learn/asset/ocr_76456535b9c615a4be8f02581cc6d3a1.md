# Table 13.1 Summary of different wearable device studies' data sets on heart disease.

| Study | Device Design | Number of Patients with HF | Primary Outcome | Main Findings |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hershman et al. [62] | MyHeart Counts Study | 50,000 | Physical activity, sleep, and cardiovascular health data | Importance of physical activity and fitness for long-term cardiovascular health |
| Bogu et al. [5] | Fitbit-One (San Francisco, CA) | 106 | Heart rate and steps | An abnormal resting heart rate during the period of viral infection (7 days before the symptom onset and 21 days after) in 92% |

[10] | Fitbit-One (San Francisco, CA), smartwatch, Apple watches, and Garmin device | 5262 | Disease dates, as well as symptom dates, diagnosis dates, heart rate, and steps | Physiological and activity data from 32 individuals infected with COVID-19, identified from a cohort of nearly 5300 participants, and found that 26 of them (81%) had alterations in their heart rate, number of daily steps, or time asleep |
