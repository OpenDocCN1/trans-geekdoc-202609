# 12.4 ML and DL Algorithms for Prediction of HF

Application of ML on electrocardiographic (ECG) signals to classify disease states has become an extensive field of research, aiming at efficiently identifying and diagnosing changes in ECG test data, based on prior ECG training data knowledge acquired from healthy and diseased states.
