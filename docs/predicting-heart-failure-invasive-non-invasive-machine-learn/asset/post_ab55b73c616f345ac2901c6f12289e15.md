### 9.1.2.3 ML Applications in Severity Estimation

Due to the fact that HF is one of the main causes of hospitalization in western society and its increased mortality rate, it is becoming imperative to be able to assess the severity of HF at its early stages. HF might be asymptomatic in the early stages and can usually be expressed only by dyspnea or fatigue.
