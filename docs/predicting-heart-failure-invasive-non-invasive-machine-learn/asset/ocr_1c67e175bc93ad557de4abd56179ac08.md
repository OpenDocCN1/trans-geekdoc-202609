# ## 4.4 The High-Efficiency Technique to Wirelessly Power Up Implanted Sensors

Currently, there are many technologies to power-up and transfer data between the implanted biosensor and the external reader unit. We will present and discuss two solutions here: magnetic resonant coupling and ambient RF energy harvesting for power energizing and for data transmission in any implant biosensor.

# ## 4.4.1 Inductive Wireless Electricity Transfer and Data Transmission through a Common Inductive Coupling

In this technique, inductive energy transmission depends on electromagnetic resonant coupling. The scale of this technique very short, no more than a few millimeters, which presents a significant limitation to its feasible implementation. However, the analytical model of an inductive link structure can develop through coupled-mode theory (CMT). We utilize the CMT approach to propose multicoil inductive links, increasing the power transfer efficiency considerably at large coupling distances, skin absorption losses, and RF-DC rectifier losses. The multicoil inductive link has been studied from a circuit perspective for power transmission to energize biosensors.
