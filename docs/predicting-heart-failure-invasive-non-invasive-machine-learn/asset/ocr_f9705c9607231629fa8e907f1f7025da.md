The penetration depth of RF microwaves into the physical skin is a function of different frequencies, which shows that lower microwave frequencies ranging from 1 to 4 GHz have much better dermal penetration than high-frequency radiation above 4 GHz, as shown in Figure 4.22. In biological samples, it is a function of frequency. According to Thiele and Elsner’s Oxidants and Antioxidants in Skin Biology [52, see also 50, 51, 53, 54], the penetration depth of the most common microwave frequency of 9 GHz is less than 1 mm due to the high-water content of biological samples. According to the same study, the WiFi strength signal can infiltrate human skin up to 7 mm.

This research presents a novel solution to power up implant chips independent of a person’s trajectory and position.
