# 4.3.4 Laser Wireless Power Transmission

A laser is the best technique for transmitting electricity for a space energy beaming experiment. Investigation into the available laser technologies is presented [30, 31]. Laser energy transfer is performed by first converting DC into light using a laser source. These waves are transmitted wirelessly by laser light and then received and converted back into electricity, as shown in Figure 4.9. Historically, the main disadvantages with this technology were low efficiency of between 12 and 20% and safety concerns. However, the European Aeronautic Defense and Space Company (EDAS) successfully powered-up a robot with laser wireless power transfer.

We cannot use laser energy as a wireless charging solution for the implanted micro-chip, because the laser beam can pose a risk to the patient's skin.
