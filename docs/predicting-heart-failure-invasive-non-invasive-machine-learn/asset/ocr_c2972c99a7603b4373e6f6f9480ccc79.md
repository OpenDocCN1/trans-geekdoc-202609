# 13.7 Future Directions of Wearables for Early Prediction of HF

It is well known that an ECG of healthy individuals can have morphological variabilities/abnormalities at the baseline with variable prevalence. Similarly, patients following cardiac abnormalities can develop persistent abnormal ECG changes, which can be detected early if they are continuously monitored. This is where machine learning can play a really important role. Machine learning models can be used to detect heart abnormalities at an early stage, but the models should be made aware of how abnormalities might look and that they might be different for different patients. The authors are working on such a machine learning paradigm that can detect cardiac abnormalities for a specific patient early and can instruct the patient, along with their doctor and care-giver, to take precautionary actions (Figure 13.12).
