# 12.2 Search Strategy

The Google Scholar database was manually searched without language restriction for articles published in the 5 years from 2017 to 2021. The literature search was primarily focused on invasive and non-invasive devices (telemonitoring) that incorporated AI for HF diagnosis and prediction. The following keywords were used as search criteria: “(machine learning OR deep learning OR artificial intelligence OR telemonitoring OR invasive devices OR non-invasive devices OR commercial devices OR implantable devices) AND heart failure.”
