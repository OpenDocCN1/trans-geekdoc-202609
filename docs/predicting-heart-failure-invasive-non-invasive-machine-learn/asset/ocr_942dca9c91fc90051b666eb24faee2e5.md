The design and development of biosensors enabled with AI can be called the next-generation biosensors, and are a promising solution to the current problems in the detection and treatment of CVD. Moreover, AI also shows great potential in designing and crafting efficient wearable medical devices for the real-time checking of heart rate, rhythm, and thoracic fluid. Though there are currently several challenges to adopting AI in clinical practice, with consistent efforts in this direction, AI can bring a new dimension to more personalized and precise cardiac healthcare services in the next few years. The general understanding of AI techniques by physicians and clinicians will bring a drastic change in cardiovascular diagnosis and prognosis.
