## 4.4.3 Microchip Implant

The microchip implant underneath the skin consists of a receiver circuit section and data transmission via an induction link.

The receiver circuit consists of two blocks: a rectifier circuit and a data transmitter circuit through the magnetic field but with a frequency range greater than the frequency of the RF power circuit. ## 4.4.5 Discussion and Results

The electromagnetic radiation technique is a safe method of wireless energy transfer, depending on being near a magnetic field. However, the power received from the oscillator is not enough to energize the implant SoC chip.

Transferred power wirelessly from a gap of 5 mm (width of the skin) depends on the efficiency of the Tx coil and the Rx coil, the alignment required to power-up the biosensor. However, more work and study are needed to increase the electromagnetic radiation area so that the subcutaneous implant can harvest electricity anywhere in the external reader unit.
