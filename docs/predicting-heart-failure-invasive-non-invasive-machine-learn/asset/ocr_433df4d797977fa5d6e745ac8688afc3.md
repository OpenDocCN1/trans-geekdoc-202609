Unsupervised learning CNN-based ML algorithms have been validated to detect and predict arrhythmias. A recent study developed a CNN-based ML algorithm from 180,922 patients to predict the probability of developing atrial fibrillation from normal sinus rhythm by studying temporal ECGs from these patients [31]. The study split the patients into a training data set (126,526 patients with 454,789 ECGs), internal validation data set (18,116 patients with 64,340 ECGs), and testing data set (36,280 patients with 130,802 ECGs). The algorithm had an AUC of 0.87 (95 % CI: 0.86–0.88), a sensitivity of 79%, and a specificity of 79.5% [31, 36].

CNN has also been validated in detecting obstructive CAD from myocardial perfusion imaging (MPI), compared with total perfusion deficit (TPD). The study included 1638 patients undergoing stress 99mTc-sestamibi or tetrofosmin MPI in nine sites who also underwent invasive coronary angiogram within 6 months [37]. The CNN algorithm was trained using raw and quantified polar maps to evaluate and predict obstructive CAD using stratified 10-fold cross-validation [37]. The AUC for disease prediction by the CNN was higher than for TPD (per patient: 0.80 vs 0.78; per vessel: 0.76 vs 0.73; p < 0.01) [37].
