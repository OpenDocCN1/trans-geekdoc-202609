# 4.2 RELATED RESEARCH

During the last two decades, the ITSM-related frameworks have provided a better systematic approach to manage IT services in the fields of IT operation for continuous improvement, implementation, and design (Marrone, 2011). For example, different studies have concentrated on adopting ITSM as a ‘specific service-oriented best practice’. According to Winnifred (2009), 45% of US corporations are operating ITSM while 15% are using its usage. ITSM is somehow the quality service that tries to ensure customer needs and expectations are met at all times (Tan, 2009).

In ‘ITIL: What It Is And What Is Not’, Hank (2006) examined the measuring techniques of successful companies when implementing the ITIL best practice. He describes Service Support and Service Delivery explains its stress on an ITIL best practice that does not stand alone and could be successful when other practices. The authors define three significant tasks, which define appropriate goal setting through a Process Maturity Framework (PMF), rigorous auditing and reporting through a Quality Management System (QMS), Project Management and a Continuous Service Improvement Program (CSIP), to support ITIL usage. Furthermore, he provided more information about agile continuous and continuous improvement of the tactical and operational components, especially those focused on service quality by clients and users (Hank, 2006). After the other works on improving the efficiency of the ITSM field, there is a real-life example of a case study, which is focusing on the IT framework, and Service Strategy process of Steel Manufacturing Enterprise (SMC), is related to the subject area of this thesis. A manufacturing enterprise (Zhong, 2010) used the implementation of COBIT and ITIL best practices to implement and improve the ITSM framework. They introduced an approach to the service evaluation framework in SMC by providing indicators for different evaluation processes to improve the result from ITIL implementation and increase the changes in IT processes. They use different approaches to find the problem of minimizing the difficulty of business–IT alignment in importance within the IT community. Same Bartolino (2006) has suggested an IT Management by Business Objectives (MBO) method, which is a particular method to ensure business strategy objectives–IT alignment, by defining a new system for support ITSM. It is closely related to the IT component at the operational level and tactical level of theoretical model. In ‘E-government’: ITIL oriented Service Management Case Study’, Meziani (2010) developed service management self-assessment plans for the government agency to support continuous quality improvement of IT processes based on ITIL governance analysis methods concerning IT standards (Meziani and Saleh). Kumbara (2008) argues that the practical issues are based on standards and the management of IT services delivered by external or outsourced service providers. Here, the author’s purpose is to assist organizations to recognize the significance of having a mutual standard for managing IT services.
