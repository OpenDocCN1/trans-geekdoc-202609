# 1.1.9 EIS and Company Strategy

The information system must remain an element that enables the company to carry out and succeed in its strategy while respecting the security, integrity, accuracy, and traceability of data and information. Thus, by making the IS a strategic tool for achieving its strategy, it must be adequately supported by a backbone that corresponds to this strategy.
