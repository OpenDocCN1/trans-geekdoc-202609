By agile, we do not just mean 'fast', but it is a significant element. Agility in ITSM/ ITAM is more a measure of responsiveness, not just how fast IT technicians process tickets or changes in version. True agility includes the IT team that service demands while respecting optimized costs. In short, true ITSM/ITAM agility is achieved by implementing intelligent processes that work smarter and more efficiently (Sahid et al., 2021).

The implementation (or transition) of a DevOps development process (integration of development, operational terms, and processes) has proven to be a capable of reducing publication time. Automation Improved repetitive processes and a key element of Agile Development and Liberation. The DevOps approach is a philosophy, a way of thinking. It aims to establish closer and more effective collaboration between development and infrastructure teams enabling faster deployment while reducing operational friction.
