# ## 1.1.2 Information System Concept

Regardless of its size, purpose, or means, any human organization has an information system to support its internal activities and its exchanges with the outside world, as shown in Figure 1.3. Today, the information system is at the heart of the functioning of any organization. It reflects its image through the data it handles, and its efficiency determines its performance. In this way, information systems are associated with the notion of organization.

The information system is the company’s nervous system. As such, it is at the heart of its processes and an essential element of its strategy. On the other hand, the term system indicates a set of interacting elements and not a simple combination of its elements.
