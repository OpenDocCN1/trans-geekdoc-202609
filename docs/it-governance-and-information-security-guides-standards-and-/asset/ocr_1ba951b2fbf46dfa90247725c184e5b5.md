# 4.3.1 IT Service Management (ITSM)

Most ITSM organizations consider the Service Support process a difficult task. Difficulties are mainly due to the following reasons:

+   - IT organizations do not have a structured approach for measuring IT service and service management processes.
- Different tools exploited by IT Support Teams do not enable effective measurement.
- ITSM referral and standards do not provide practical guidelines and a road-map to measure the IT support process (Lahtela, 2010).
- Therefore, the IT Support processes such as ITIL V2/V3 or other reliable sources to increase IT quality of services. So, implementations of the Service Support process are chosen as a priority to support continuous improvement in ITSM.
