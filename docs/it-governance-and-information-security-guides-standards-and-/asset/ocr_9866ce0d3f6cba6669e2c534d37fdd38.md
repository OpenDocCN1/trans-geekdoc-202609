# 2.2.2 The Word of IT Governance Usage

The word 'usage' answers the question, 'What are the objectives of IT governance, what is its purpose?' It is a faceted characterization of governance objectives. This word has four facets: MINIMIZING RISKS, ATTENDING THE STATE OF ALIGNMENT, GETTING PERFORMANCE, and CREATING VALUE. All facets associated with this word helps to highlight the objectives of ITG.
