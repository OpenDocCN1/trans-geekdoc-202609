Case selection involved three key decisions. First, a single sector was chosen to eliminate possible confounds that might arise from investigating multiple sectors. The research involved a large Port agency in Morocco which was selected for a number of reasons: the agency is highly dependent on IT to support its core functions. IT governance is likely to be a significant concern to these organizations and the study therefore more relevant. The agency is generally more supportive of research and consequently likely to assist in this study.

### 3.7.1 Data Collection

A case study was conducted as a pilot project to identify relevant ITG practices in the organization. The capability maturity framework is implemented at a leading port organization in Morocco. The organization manages more than 30 ports and sites with more than 12,000 employees. The Information System department has 40 employees with different profiles. The purpose is to study IT governance practices. Figure 3.3 shows the ITG framework architecture proposed for eventual implementation in the organization.
