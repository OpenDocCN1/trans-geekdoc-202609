## 2.6 SUMMARY

This chapter proposed a faceted framework for the analysis of IT governance. This framework considers four perspectives, called words of the subject, use, system, and development of IT. Facets and their values detail these four perspectives. This framework has been applied to five known governance standards. The approaches do not have an overall vision of IT but piecemeal visions. The emphasis is on collections of good practices that are updated regularly. This work on state of the art has highlighted the need for research on the globalization of IT.
