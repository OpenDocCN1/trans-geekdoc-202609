Three groups of enterprise and IT-related goals were identified through the statistical analysis of the perceived ratings, presenting several points at which a priority list for each category could be formed. The purpose of the mapping Table 3.10 is to demonstrate how enterprise goals are supported, or translate into, IT-related goals. Subsequently, Table 3.11 contains the mapping table between the IT-related goals and how these are supported by IT processes, by part of the goals scale (ISACA, 2012). The results revealed that the required IT processes, a total of 28 IT processes, to support the perceived important-related goals for the MENA port organizations include the entire IT framework total of 16 processes, as perceived by the same sector. This validates the conceptual model for IT governance evaluation in organizations. This also allowed the triangulation of different data sources, adding to the credibility of the adapted framework.

# 3.7 Discussion

The majority of recent IT governance research has focused on accountability, structures, and mechanisms, yet little empirical data on the level of IT governance capability exists within organizations. To overcome this gap, this study employed the COBIT framework to evaluate and compare the results with other studies.

The adapted ITG framework contains six processes from COBIT used by previous studies. These are as follows:

- DSS05 Manage Security
- APO13 Manage Security
- EDM03 Ensure Risk Optimization
- EDM02 Ensure Benefits Delivery
- DSS02 Manage Service Requests and Incidents
- DSS04 Manage Continuity

The remaining ten IT processes:

- DSS04 Manage Continuity
- APO12 Manage Risk
- APO02 Manage Strategy
- EDM04 Ensure Resource Optimization
- EDM01 Ensure Governance Framework Setting and Maintenance
- DSS03 Manage Problems
- BAI06 Manage Changes
- APO06 Manage Budget and Costs
- DSS01 Monitor, Evaluate and Assess Performance and Conformance
- BAI09 Manage Assets
