# ABSTRACT

Presently Every nation places a high value on healthcare due to the rise in trauma instances. The ideal approach in this case is thought to be an Internet of Things (IoT) based health monitoring system. A novel approach using internet technology, the Internet of Things (IoT) is an increasing study area, mainly in the field of healthcare. The rising use of wearable sensors and cellphones has allowed these remote health care monitoring systems to grow at such a rapid rate. IoT health monitoring helps patients receive the appropriate care for their present level of health even when a physician is far away. The notion of the IoT and deep learning are combined in this study to present a systematic method to identify trauma instances more effectively. By utilizing some of the biological information from the patient’s body, such as temperature, heart rate, and other factors, this approach presents an overview of the IoT and is also used to monitor health status and identify symptoms in the human body. For fall detection using sensor nodes, the Deep Convolutional Neural Network (DCNN) analyses individuals’ motion and architecture. The suggested method may be put in place for less money but has significant potential to identify symptoms by giving patients and suspected cases the care they need and reacting to life-or-death circumstances. Metrics such as precision, recall, F-measure, and accuracy are utilized to evaluate the results when applying classification algorithms. When compared to techniques like the Decision Tree (DT), Support Vector Machine (SVM), and K Nearest Neighbour (k-NN), the findings show that it has been very successful.

# 1. Introduction

People are developing chronic illnesses at a significant pace in the expanding global population. Daily cigarette use, alcohol intake, excessive stress, lack of physical exercise, and other factors are the primary causes of this [1]. A crucial part of everyday living is healthcare. With the right care, health problems may be diagnosed diagonally and avoided at an early stage [2]. Using numerous diagnostic tools like CT, MRI, and PET, among others, it is simple to identify the anomalies that are present inside of us or under the skin. Additionally, early detection and prevention of certain rare disorders, such as heart problems and techniques are quite simple. The heart rate, body temperature, blood oxygen levels, etc., are crucial factors in trauma disorders. Patient monitoring systems allow doctors to keep an eye on multiple patients at once [3].

It is always a better idea to use remote health monitoring technologies to keep a check on these trauma victims in those locations where the outbreak is prevalent. The present solution is an Internet of Things-based health monitoring system [4]. Fig. 1 demonstrates the developing technology of the intersection of IoT and Machine Learning [5]. Intelligent services are being created to quickly analyze and apply massive amounts of data using machine learning to deliver easy user experiences and enhance system performance. These services gather data on human activities using a variety of sensors from IoT devices.
