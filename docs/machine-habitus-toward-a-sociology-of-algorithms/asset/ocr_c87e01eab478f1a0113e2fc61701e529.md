# Digital infrastructure

Since the labour market and the school system Bourdieu wrote about are now encapsulated by LinkedIn and Coursera, and most fields of the see see comparable form of platformization (Helmond 2015), it is important to consider another kind of structure too – that is, the digital infrastructures underlying techno-social fields.

The scholarly literature has similarly represented this second type of structure in topological and relational terms, as an ‘environment’, ‘assemblage’, ‘network’ or ‘cybers’ (see Plantin et al. 2018; Siles et al. 2020; Crawford and Jordan 2018; Nunes 1997). As a structural layer addition to and intertwined with social structure, digital infrastructures are powerfully wielded in the shaping of techno-social practice across information systems, platforms, apps and software more broadly (Plantin et al. 2018; Bucher and Helmond 2018; McKelvey 2018).
