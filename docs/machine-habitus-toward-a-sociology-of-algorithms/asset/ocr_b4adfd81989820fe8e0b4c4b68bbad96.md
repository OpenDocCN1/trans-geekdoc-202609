These were related by the YouTube algorithm based on what we labelled in the paper 'situational logic' of music reception, according to the duration and contextual function of the consumed music matter more than its subcultural or artistic value. Such a situational logic has been traditionally regarded as a taboo within artistic fields, long-dominated by an anti-functional, Kantian, high-art style of aesthetic appreciation (Bourdieu 1983, 1984, 1993). Thus, the fact that the recommender system put forward this alternative aesthetic perspective signalled an ongoing, computationally induced *transformation* of musical fields and reception practices.

I subsequently replicated this study on a different sample, composed of 14,865 related music videos uploaded by Italian YouTube accounts only (Airoldi 2021). This digitally encapsulated national field, dominated by domestic music, has allowed me to observe techno-social dynamics more closely.
