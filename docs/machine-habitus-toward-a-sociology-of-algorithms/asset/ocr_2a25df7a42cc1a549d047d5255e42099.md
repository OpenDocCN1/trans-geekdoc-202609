| Research direction | Key research questions | Selected example studies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follow machine creators | What culture is inscribed in the code as deus in machine? With design intentions? What kinds of data contexts are at the root of machine socialization, and encoded as machine habitus? | e.g. Benjamin 2019, Casilli 2019, Crawford and Joler 2018, Crawford and Paglen 2019, Delfanti 2019, Hallinan and Striphas 2016, A. Mackenzie 2018, and Ballatore 2020, Neyland 2019, Prey 2017, Tassebahji et al. 2021, Wilson 2018 |
| Follow the users | How do users understand and use algorithmic systems? In ways do we interact with them, and react to their outputs? What are the effects of computational authority on users’ practices and dispositional trajectories? | e.g. Brayne 2017, Bucher 2017, Christin 2020, Cotter 2019, Eubanks 2018, Gran, Booth and Bucher 2020, Klawitter and Hargittai 2018, D. Mackenzie 2018, Milan 2015, Noble 2018, Rosenblatt and Stark 2016, Ruckstein and Granroth 2020, Siles et al. 2020, Velkova and Kaun 2019 |
| Follow the medium | How do digital infrastructures encapsulate social fields? What affordances? What social agents inhabit them? How does the techno-social field unfold and change over time? | e.g. Aiordi 2021, Arvidsson et al. 2016, Beer 2013b, Bucher 2012b, et al. 2020, Helmond, Nieborg and van Vlist 2019, Light, Burgess and Dugay 2016, Marres and Gerlitz 2016, Rogers 2013, Venturini et al. 2018 |
| Follow the algorithm | What are machine habitus’ dispositions? How are they actual in practice, and how do they change? With consequences for techno-social fields and entangled agents? | e.g. Aiordi, Beraldo and Gandini 2019, Bessi and Ferrara 2014, Geiger 2017, Hargreaves et al. 2018, Jacobsen 2020, D. Mackenzie 2019, Neyland 2019, Rieder, Matamoros-Fernandez and Coromina 2018, Roth, Mazières and Menezes 2020, Tsvetkova and et al. 2017 |
