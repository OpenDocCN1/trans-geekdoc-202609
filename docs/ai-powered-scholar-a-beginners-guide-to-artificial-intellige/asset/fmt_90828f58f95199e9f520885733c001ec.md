
### Assumption reversal
This approach involves identifying the underlying assumptions or beliefs that shape currents and reflect on how the creative thinking technique influenced the ideation process.
Then, consider the following:
*   How did the creative thinking technique help you approach the research challenge in a new way?
*   What insights or ideas emerged from the AI-assisted ideation process that you might not have considered otherwise?
