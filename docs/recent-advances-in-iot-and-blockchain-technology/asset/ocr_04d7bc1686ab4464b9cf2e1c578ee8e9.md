Fig. (3). Sequence Diagram.

The states of the drug/medicine in the supply chain are: Discovered- The drug to be sold is first designed by the manufacturer, Approved- The drug will then be approved (by MVO). Produced- The drug is then produced by the manufacturer, For WholeSale- The drug is allowed to be bought by the wholesalers, ForRetail (owned by a wholesaler) - The drug is then sent to the retailers, For Sale (owned by a pharmacy)-the drug is put on sale by the retailer, Sold (owned by a client). The transaction (Fig. 4) will be tracked using smart contracts.
