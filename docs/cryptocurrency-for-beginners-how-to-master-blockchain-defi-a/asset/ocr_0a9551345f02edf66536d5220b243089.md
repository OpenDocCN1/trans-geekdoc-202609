# Differences

Bitcoin is a digital currency that may transmit money to other people. It functions in a manner that is quite similar to that of real-world cash. Within the Ethereum platform, Ether is utilized as a currency, but it may also be used for real-world transactions. Bitcoin transactions are done mechanically, which means you must complete them yourself when you want them completed. With Ether, you can perform transactions manually or automatically—they're programmed, which means they'll only happen if specific criteria are satisfied. A bitcoin transaction takes roughly 10 minutes to complete, which is the time it takes for a brick to also be digitally signed. It takes roughly 20 seconds to complete a transaction using Ether.

The number of bitcoins that may exist is limited to 21 million. By the year 2140, this figure is expected to be attained. Ether is anticipated to last a long time and be limited to 100 million units. Bitcoin is used for goods or services exchanges, whereas Ether uses blockchain technology to build a record that triggers a transaction when a specific condition is satisfied. Finally, the SHA-256 algorithm is used by Bitcoin, whereas the ethash method is used by Ethereum. 1 bitcoin is worth USD 8741.81 since May 2020, while 1 ether is worth $190.00.

# Similarities

At the moment, bitcoins and Ethereum are the most popular and valued cryptocurrencies. Both employ blockchain, in which events are put to a container known as a bit, and a series of blocks are formed in which information cannot be changed. The money is mined via a mechanism known as proof of work, which involves solving a mathematical challenge before a piece can be added to the network. Finally, bitcoins and Ether are both extensively utilized worldwide.
