Cryptocurrencies, like currency and the financial system, may be used for malicious reasons. The fallacy, on the other hand, is that cryptocurrencies are a haven for illegal conduct. Per a Chainalysis analysis, illegal activity accounted for $10 billion, or 1%, of all cryptocurrency the year before, down from nearly $20 billion, or 2%, in 2019.

Cryptocurrency transactions are also entirely anonymous, which is a fallacy. And also, are all transactions accessible just on a blockchain network, but government authorities are generally sophisticated enough to penetrate the cloak of anonymity provided by bitcoin transactions.
