# Mark to Market

In their quest to quit the invisible asset class and return to fiat money or other products, which many cyber purists sometimes despise, crypto holders' flight to protection or cash most often leads them to the dollar or the United States dollar. While price pegs operate well on the way into cryptocurrencies because investors influenced by their "animal spirits" who want to get in on a bullish wave are prepared to pay at a specified value or peg, they do not function well on the way out of cryptocurrencies. During the departure process, however, this marks to market characteristic causes a significant amount of downward price pressure, highlighting the negative consequences of illiquidity, limited exit options, and levels of participation in the asset. As more institutional investors join the market and additional markets & trading platforms arise, these challenges will be addressed. Meanwhile, market players should be aware of currency inconvertibility and the volatility of cryptocurrency, which might make high-frequency traders nervous. Suspension of disbelief is required to fully comprehend blockchain's potential. The suspension of the conventional economic yardstick is required to adequately understand the investing thesis of cryptocurrencies.
