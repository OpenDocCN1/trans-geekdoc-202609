## 2.3 Dogecoin

Dogecoin is a cryptocurrency similar to Bitcoin and Ethereum, but it's not the same as either of these prominent currencies. Dogecoin was named after a once-popular meme and was designed to some extent as a joke comment among crypto enthusiasts. Despite its strange genesis story, Dogecoin skyrocketed in popularity in 2021, becoming the sixth biggest cryptocurrency by market capitalization as of this writing.

![](img/9e732599f379d4f1dd55a1fd64d01531_45_0.png)

Dogecoin is no longer a joke, with its value skyrocketing by more than 5,000 percent in 2021. Tesla CEO Elon Musk is a supporter, calling Dogecoin his favorite cryptocurrency. Musk also dubbed Dogecoin the "people's cryptocurrency" and said an actual Dogecoin token would be planted on the moon.

### How Does Dogecoin Work?

Dogecoin is a virtual currency that, like Bitcoin and Ethereum, is based on blockchain technology. Blockchain is a decentralized digital ledger that keeps all transactions conducted with a decentralized digital currency in a distributed, secure manner. The Dogecoin blockchain record, which is regularly updated with all transactions in the cryptocurrency, is carried by all holders in an identical copy. The blockchain network of Dogecoin, like those of other cryptocurrencies, employs encryption to make all transactions safe.

Miners utilize computers to solve difficult mathematical formulas to process payments and store them on the Dogecoin blockchain, a mechanism known as "proof of work." Miners get more Dogecoin in return for handling transactions and maintaining the blockchain ledger, which they may then keep or sell on the market.

It is possible to make payments and purchases using Dogecoin; nevertheless, it is not a highly efficient store of currency. This is mostly because there is no limit on the amount of Dogecoin that may be generated by mining over the course of a lifetime, resulting in the cryptocurrency being intentionally extremely inflationary. Dogecoin miners are rewarded for their efforts by the blockchain, which creates millions of new Dogecoins every day, making it very difficult for speculative price increases in Dogecoin to last over time.

### The future

It's impossible to predict whatever the future brings for Dogecoin, much alone other cryptocurrencies. The polarization of thought in the cryptocurrency field is a major issue like pundits, bloggers, and YouTubers are either crypto haters or moon boys. Indeed, CoinMarketCap currently lists over 50 meme cryptocurrencies, demonstrating their ascent to popularity.

The inability to utilize it for transactions, like other cryptocurrencies, is the biggest impediment to its development. However, unlike other cryptocurrencies, meme coins have a unique drawback in that their utilization is dependent on people being drawn to the meme. If investors lose all interest in the meme, the price will plummet, rendering Dogecoin useless. One thing is certain: Dogecoin is now one of the riskiest and volatile cryptocurrencies on the market.

On decentralized exchanges, this cryptocurrency may be traded and transacted. The Ren Project has allowed it to access the decentralized financial network and operate on the Ether blockchain platform. This implies it's compatible with the Bitcoin market's burgeoning decentralized finance trend.

Due to their vulnerability to speculation-related events, growing competitive intensity, and absence of universal agreement as a collective class of assets, cryptocurrencies are very volatile by nature. ## Cons of Dogecoin

### Criticisms of Dogecoin

The news press and money managers had difficulty admitting Bitcoin as a serious currency. Experts have also said that it is an exceedingly dangerous investment due to pump-and-dump behavior and increased susceptibility to guesses and fads fueled by herd mentality. Others have referred to it as a pyramid scam.

David Gerard stated in a Foreign Policy piece from 2021 that there is no meaningful purpose for cryptocurrency other than trading. He went on to say that these "assets" had no applications. Individuals who aren't sure what they're doing and are just jumping on the bandwagon are often victims of personal finance and investing fads.

### Has No Supply Cap

One of Dogecoin's most significant drawbacks is the absence of a supply limit. Unlike Bitcoin and Cardano's Ada cryptocurrency, it is deflationary, which has a predetermined supply limit to generate scarcity and may raise its future value. Cryptocurrencies with no supply limit are poor inflation hedges.

Furthermore, the ever-increasing supply will need larger-scale blockchain mining operations in the future. Because this cryptocurrency relies on a proof-of-work consensus process, it will need to enlarge its pool of miners and their strong computers in the future to accommodate a rising number of transactions.

### Poor Technical Support

Despite its expanding network of users and supporters, Dogecoin's technical support is quite low. The project team is small compared to Bitcoin, Ether, and Cardano. Furthermore, unlike these Bitcoin industry heavyweights, the product plan is not as obvious.

Since 2015, no major technology advancements or improvements have occurred. It's also worth noting that it lacks a technologically distinct selling point. In comparison to the big players, this cryptocurrency's stated aims or ambitions and the whole blockchain platform are not as lofty.

## 2.4 Litecoin

In 2011, Charles Lee founded Litecoin (LTC), a peer-to-peer cryptocurrency. It is built on Bitcoin's source code and has many similarities. Litecoin was created for less expensive transactions and became more efficient in daily life. On the other hand, Bitcoin was primarily utilized as a long-term store of wealth. The coin limitation market cap of Litecoin is substantially greater than that of Bitcoin, and the feature extraction is faster. This implies transactions are quicker and less expensive. However, they are often smaller in size.

![](img/9e732599f379d4f1dd55a1fd64d01531_50_0.png)

Litecoin, like Bitcoin, is a digital currency. Litecoin is a virtual currency that uses blockchain technology to allow individuals and businesses to send money directly to each other. This ensures that all transactions are recorded in a public ledger, allowing the currency to run a decentralized payment system independent of government regulation or censorship.

### How does Litecoin work?

Litecoin is a digital currency created and transferred via a free software cryptographic system. It uses blockchain technology to keep track of all transactions in a decentralized, public ledger.
