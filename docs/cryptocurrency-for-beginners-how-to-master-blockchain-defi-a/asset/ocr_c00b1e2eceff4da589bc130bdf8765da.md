# Future of Ethereum

Many cryptocurrencies have seen remarkable growth in the last year. With a market valuation of about $900 billion, Bitcoin is still the most popular.

Ethereum, on the other hand, has been rapidly gaining popularity and closing the gap on its bigger rival. Ethereum has a market valuation of little over $450 billion, making it the second-highest popular cryptocurrency. # 2.3 Dogecoin

Doggie is a cryptocurrency similar to Bitcoin and Ethereum, but it's not the same as either of these prominent currencies. Dogecoin was named after a once-popular meme and was designed to some extent as a joke comment among crypto enthusiasts. Despite its strange genesis story, Dogecoin skyrocketed in popularity in 2021, becoming the sixth biggest cryptocurrency by market capitalization as of this writing.
