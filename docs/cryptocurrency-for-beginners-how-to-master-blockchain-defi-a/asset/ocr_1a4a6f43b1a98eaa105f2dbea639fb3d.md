# # 1.3 Understanding cryptocurrency

Bitcoin, and Ethereum are among the most well-known cryptocurrency, and you've undoubtedly heard of them or heard someone mention them to you. Cryptocurrencies are becoming more popular as payment options for online transactions. Becoming familiar with cryptocurrencies and understanding the risks associated with using them is essential before transforming real dollars, euros, pounds, or other conventional currencies into the symbol for Bitcoin, the most popular cryptocurrency.

When it comes to utilizing cryptocurrency, what are the dangers? Even though cryptocurrency is still a relatively new concept, the marketplace for such digital currencies is quite volatile. Because cryptocurrencies do not need the involvement of banks or other related parties to govern them, they are often uninsured and difficult to transfer into a form of fiat cash.
