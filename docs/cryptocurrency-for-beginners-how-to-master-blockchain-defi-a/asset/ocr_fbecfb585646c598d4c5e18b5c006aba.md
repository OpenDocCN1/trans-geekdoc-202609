# 3.5.15 Litecoin

![](bbox=[0.252, 0.122, 0.747, 0.375])

After Bitcoin, Litecoin was one of the first crypto coins, and the digital gold Bitcoin was branded as silver. With a bigger token and a revolutionary mining algorithm, Litecoin was a true breakthrough quicker than Bitcoin, better suited to be Bitcoin's smaller sibling. It encouraged the advent of many other crypto currencies that used its codebase but made it lighter, even more so. Dogecoin or Feathercoin are examples. Although after Bitcoin, Litecoin struggled to find a real use case and lost its second spot, it is still extensively pursued and traded, and if Bitcoin fails, it is hoarded as a replacement.
