# 6.6.3 Co investing in art and luxury collectibles

Any physical object that people may and do collect, from exquisite china to bottles of wine, rare manuscripts to vintage cars — and everything in between — is a collectible. These "tangible assets" are collected by collectors because they enjoy them. Investors hold on to them with the expectation that their value will rise over time. There are no guarantees, as with any investment, as to which would appreciate and which will not. Collectibles are a particularly unexpected group because of their diversity. When hyped-up things like Beanie Babies soar one year, then become nearly utterly useless the next, the most throw-away items are often the ones whose prices skyrocket. Interested collectors should learn about NFTs by creating their own crypto wallets and purchasing some lower-cost pieces. It helps to grasp the medium itself, just as it helps to understand how art is collected and invested in.
