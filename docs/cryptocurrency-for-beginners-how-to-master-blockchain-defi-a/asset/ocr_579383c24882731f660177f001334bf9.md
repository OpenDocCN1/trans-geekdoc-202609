# SmartCash

SmartCash is a zero-cash-based cryptocurrency that is community-driven. With the vision of attaining merchant adoption, it prioritizes privacy and quick transaction times. The project's defining feature is its aversion to a central authority, which has led to the project's foregoing of a core team. The currency is equivalent to Dash in that it aims to gain widespread adoption through a fast, private currency. Because of its use of master nodes and dependence on the Dash Foundation, Dash is perceived as a somewhat centralized currency. Foundations, core teams, and centralized mining are all concepts that SmartCash hopes to avoid. SmartCash, on the other hand, incorporates a number of decentralized governance as well as development features. It makes significant investments in community-based development. As a consequence, SmartCash has grown in popularity. SmartCash, while not as well-known as Dash as well as other private payment platforms like Zcash and Monero, offers a unique perspective on anonymous, untraceable, and usable cryptocurrency. Traditional cryptocurrencies, such as Bitcoin, incentivize only the miners while ignoring the other stakeholders who contribute to the project's upkeep, development, and promotion. SmartCash is a community-driven cryptocurrency that prioritizes development and community. A total of 29% of the block reward has been set aside to support SmartHive community proposals as well as the Hive Teams. SmartMining is a new feature in SmartCash that inhibits mining attacks.
