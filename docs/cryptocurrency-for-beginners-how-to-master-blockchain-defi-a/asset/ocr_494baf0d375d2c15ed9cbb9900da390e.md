# Types of risks in the cryptocurrency business

A cryptocurrency's distinguishing characteristic is that it is not produced by a central authority, making it potentially resistant to government intervention or manipulation. To comprehend the hazards associated with currency, one must also comprehend the system's characteristics upon which the new coin is built. All bitcoin transactions are recorded on a blockchain, a digital, decentralized public ledger.
