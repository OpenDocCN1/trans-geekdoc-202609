# 1.2.12 Issuance of credit by banks against gold coins

Early banks proposed a solution to this problem by storing these coins with a guarantee of return. These deposits were then used by the early banks to offer loans. Banks began paying interest to depositors to motivate more people to invest. Banknotes were introduced in this way. Banks gave depositors a slip that listed the amount as well as the weight of gold they had deposited. The government began printing the first currency notes to control the flow of these notes.
