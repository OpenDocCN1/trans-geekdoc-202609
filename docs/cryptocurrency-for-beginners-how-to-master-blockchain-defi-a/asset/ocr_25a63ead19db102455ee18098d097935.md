However, remember that purchasing individual cryptocurrencies is similar to purchasing individual equities on the stock market. By placing all of your investment in one asset, you expose yourself to more risk than by spreading your money out across hundreds and thousands of securities, such as you may do with a unit trust or a marketplace fund. Unfortunately, cryptocurrency money is scarce at the moment.
