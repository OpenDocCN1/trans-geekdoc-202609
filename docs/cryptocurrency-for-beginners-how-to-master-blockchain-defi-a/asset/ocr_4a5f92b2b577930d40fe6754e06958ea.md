As a number of banks have entered the Ripple network, Ripple has seen tremendous importance in the financial room. Ripple (XRP) is a real-time gross settlement (RTGS) system that functions as a money transfer, currency exchange, and remittance network. It was developed by Ripple Labs Inc. in the United States and validated by independent servers before being released in 2012. XRP is the name of the cryptocurrency that is traded (a cryptocurrency). XRP can be exchanged for almost any other currency, allowing users to avoid the long lines at banks.
