# Chapter 4: Myths about cryptocurrencies and Altcoins Generation 2

The universe of cryptocurrencies has flourished in recent years due to the asset's incredible profits. However, their meteoric growth, fueled by Bitcoin, has spawned a slew of myths that may or may not be genuine. Here are some typical cryptocurrency misconceptions. It is feasible for governments to order financial institutions to cease dealing with cryptocurrency or any companies that deal with them, but it is practically hard to outright prohibit cryptocurrencies from existence. Cryptocurrency transactions are no more complicated than sending an email, and anybody who has internet access can do them with ease.

Governments have been apprehensive of the dangers involved with cryptocurrency because they want to exert more authority over anything that could be deemed a viable currency in the first place. However, a growing number of countries have shown a readiness to approach cryptocurrency with an inquisitive attitude in recent years.

The application cases of blockchain, a technology that supports cryptocurrencies like bitcoin, have gotten a lot of attention. Any experts believe that blockchain has yet to establish any use case and that bitcoin is likely the only tool that has had some success. The fact is that blockchain technology is evolving at a breakneck speed. Smart contracts on the blockchain, for example, may hold a contract between such a lender and a house loan recipient. It has the potential to automate loan payments from the beneficiary's crypto wallet to the lender's account. From DeFi to NFT, blockchain technology offers a plethora of possibilities and applications.
