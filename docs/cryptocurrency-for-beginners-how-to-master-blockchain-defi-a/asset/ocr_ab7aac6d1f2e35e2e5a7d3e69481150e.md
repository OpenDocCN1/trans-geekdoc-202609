# Cyber Risks on All Sides

The area between both the computer and the chair is as crucial as the crypto custodian's cyber hygiene and defenses, just as cyber threats increase according to Moore's law. While the blockchain network has shown to be one of the most cyber-resistant developments to date, the companies that plug into it and, like other currencies, are often newcomers with inadequate cybersecurity standards and resources. In terms of traceability, transaction logging, and degrees of trust or fiduciary duty, not all currencies are born equal under this standard. Risks as basic as "strange disappearance" to as complicated as ransomware assaults and AI-powered bots combing the Net for weak connections and easy victims are diverse and rapid threats for this reason.

# Care, Custody, and Control

Despite its intangible and invisible crypto market and digital assets in general, care, custody, and control are the most pressing concerns confronting the business. A true standards battle is raging among crypto custodians about who is delivering the greatest norms of shareholder protection and plan to hold, much like the conventional banking sector's recurring issues of virtual and real security. This playbook of recommended security procedures is continually being produced based on many high-profile and high-value crypto heists. The richest crypto investors use cold storage devices housed in actual vaults and shelters to secure their intangible treasure. Not every cryptocurrency investor could afford this degree of protection, just as not every crypto investment is a threat, and most are subject to new care, custody, and control rules.
