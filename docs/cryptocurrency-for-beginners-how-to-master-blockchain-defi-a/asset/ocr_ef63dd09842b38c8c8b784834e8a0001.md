# Chapter 2: How to Invest in Cryptocurrencies and Altcoins

Every cryptocurrency that isn't Bitcoin is referred to as an altcoin. Altcoins are crypto coins or tokens that are not Bitcoin, such as Solana, Cardano, Polkadot, and Uniswap. Because Bitcoin is the most well-known and well-established cryptocurrency on the planet, some people refer to it as the "primary" cryptocurrency. Bitcoin has the largest market capitalization, or the actual amount of issued cryptocurrency compared to other cryptocurrencies. However, there are thousands of different cryptocurrencies, each with monetary value and applications. Blockchain technology, such as Ethereum, is a cutting-edge open-source platform that can facilitate financial transactions and smart contracts, decentralized apps (dApps), tokenized assets, and distributed financial services. Ethereum (ETH), the cryptocurrency that runs on the Ethereum platform, is the sec cryptocurrency by market capitalization at this writing and has been in that position for many years.
