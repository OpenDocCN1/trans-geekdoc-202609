Smart contracts are a decentralized instrument. Ethereum was the one that launched smart contracts into the world in the war against Ethereum vs. Bitcoin. With smart contracts, when a deal happens, you will set conditions. A smart contract is software that is running on the blockchain of Ethereum. This is a set of code (its functions) and data (its condition) stored in the Ethereum block chain at a certain address. Smart contracts are a form of an account of Ethereum. This ensures that they have a balance, and transfers can be transmitted through the network. They are, however, not operated by a person but rather distributed and run as programmed on the network. User accounts will then communicate with a smart contract by transactions that fulfill a smart contract-defined purpose. Smart contracts can define and automatically execute rules through a code, like a standard contract. All should write and deploy a smart contract to the network. You just need to know how to code and have enough ETH to deploy the contract in a smart contract language. Since smart Ethereum contracts are driven by the blockchain, developers may design applications that are never offline and therefore cannot be edited by third parties. One good way to think about smart contracts is to think about owning a home. Normally, third parties such as a lawyer and a broker would be needed. The possession of the house is immediately submitted after a prerequisite has been fulfilled with a smart contract.
