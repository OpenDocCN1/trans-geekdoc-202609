Bitcoin doesn’t and instead only relies on manual payment technology. It’s clear that there are advantages to using both Bitcoin and Ethereum. Bitcoin has smaller coin availability and is more liquid than Ethereum, but Ethereum has improved hardware and has more applications than Bitcoin would. Unlike several other block chain networks, Ethereum is programmable. This customizable functionality has allowed developers to solve problems ranging from digital identity and anonymity to corporate ownership and data protection.
