Given below is a chronological state of events related to Bitcoin.

# Initial Failure

Although cryptocurrencies were not legally mined until 2009, in previous years, there were other attempts to set up a digital currency. In 1998, two founders of cryptocurrencies, Wei Dai and Nick Szabo published ideas on the prospect of currency decentralization. Both of their proposals revolved around digital currencies as a means to replace conventional financial system inefficiencies, such as spending money on paper and coin currency formation. Although no proposal came to fruition, more than a decade later, both Dai and Szabo became early cryptocurrency adapters. The first true cryptocurrency’s creation, Bitcoin, was a result of these initial failures.
