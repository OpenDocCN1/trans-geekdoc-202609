# ## 1.2.10 Use of gold for diversifying investment

In general, gold is seen as a great way to diversify one's portfolio. Regardless of whether your concerns are connected to inflation, wealth protection, or a sinking US currency, gold has shown to be a profitable investment alternative that may add a diverse component to your investment portfolio. Gold, on the other hand, is unrelated to stocks, bonds, or real estate if your goal is just diversity.

# ## 1.2.11 Evolution of gold

Gold has evolved over time as a consequence of three primary transitions: first, it became the global standard as a medium of exchange; second, an international banking system was built around it, with paper money fully convertible into gold; and third, convertibility into gold ceased in 1971, and gold was replaced by a pure fiat money system. The benefits of gold, on the other hand, have not altered in the 40 years since the world switched to a fiat money system. Governments discourage the use of gold as money for their citizens, but they trade in it through their central banks and treasuries, according to the financial world. There are confirmed claims that the US Treasury holds 261 mm ounces of gold. In 2012, the Fed's assets were projected to reach 13.5 million ounces. India and China, through their central banks, have been buying gold at an alarming rate in recent months.
