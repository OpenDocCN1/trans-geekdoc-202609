# How Can You Mine Cryptocurrency?

Mining refers to the process of releasing new units of bitcoin into the world in return for confirming transactions. While it is theoretically conceivable for the common individual to mine cryptocurrencies, it is becoming more difficult with proof-of-work platforms like Bitcoin. Even as the Bitcoin community expands, it becomes more complex, requiring more processing power. It used to be possible for the typical customer to do just that, but it’s now prohibitively costly. There are just too many individuals who have optimized their equipment and technologies to outcompete.

Understand that mining Proof of Work cryptocurrency takes a lot of energy. According to estimates, Bitcoin farms use 0.21 percent of the world’s energy. As almost the same quantity of energy that Switzerland consumes in a year. According to estimates, most Bitcoin miners use 60 percent to 80 percent of their earnings to pay power expenses.

Although mining cryptocurrency in a proof-of-work system is unfeasible for the common individual, the evidence of stake model needs less high-powered computation since validators are picked randomly depending on the level people stake. However, it does need the possession of a cryptocurrency to participate. If you don’t have any crypto, you won’t stake anything.

# How to Use Cryptocurrency for Secure Purchases

Whether or whether you can conduct safe transactions using crypto depends on what you’re attempting to buy. In the United States, you may use a bitcoin debit card like BitPay to pay cryptocurrency at a shop that doesn’t accept it directly.
