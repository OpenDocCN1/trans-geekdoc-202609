It is possible to make payments and purchases using Dogecoin; nevertheless, it is not a highly efficient store of currency. This is mostly because there is no limit on the amount of Doge coins that may be generated by mining over the course of a lifetime, resulting in the cryptocurrency being intentionally extremely inflationary. Dogecoin miners are rewarded for their efforts by the blockchain, which creates millions of new Doges every day, making it very difficult for speculative price increases in Dogecoin to last over time.

# The future

It's impossible to predict whatever the future brings for Dogecoin, much alone other cryptocurrencies. The polarization of thought in the cryptocurrency field is a major issue like pundits, bloggers, and YouTubers are either crypto haters or moon boys'. Indeed, CoinMarketCap currently lists over 50 meme cryptocurrencies, demonstrating their ascent to popularity.

The inability to utilize it for transactions, like other cryptocurrencies, is the biggest impediment to its development. However, unlike other cryptocurrencies, meme coins have a unique drawback in that their utilization is dependent on people being drawn to the meme. If investors lose all interest in the meme, the price will plummet, rendering Dogecoin useless. One thing is certain: Dogecoin is now one of the riskiest and volatile cryptocurrencies on the market.
