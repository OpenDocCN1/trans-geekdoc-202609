Paypal buys Xoom, a digital money transfer firm, for $890 million. This enables the company to accelerate the handling of international payments.

# February 2017

PayPal launches its first peer-to-peer payment system. This helped the company establish a solid foothold in the emerging P2P marketplace.

# Present

The company has begun to delve deep into the lucrative but volatile cryptocurrency market, concentrating on the technology's underlying Blockchain platform and avoiding Bitcoin - at least for now. In addition, the company is expanding its presence in India, a key global market, by hiring 1,200 new company technology specialists to concentrate on mobile as well as other merging payment technologies in India. It also announces a partnership with Facebook (FB) - Get Facebook, Inc. Class A Report Marketplace, which company officials believe has the potential to outperform its long-time partner eBay in terms of financial performance.
