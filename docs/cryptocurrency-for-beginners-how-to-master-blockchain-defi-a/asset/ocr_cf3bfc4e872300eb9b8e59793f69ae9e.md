# **Discovering NFT**

While NFTs remain the "shiny new object" of the digital finance ecosystem, their utility and market capitalization are yet to be realized. NFTs must be represented and offered in mid-market settings, not simply in luxury, top-of-the-line opportunities if they are to fulfill their potential for general consumers. The normal shopper requires access to information as well as the capacity to search, compare, and shop. The discovery stage of a buyer's journey is crucial. And don't forget about the power of impulse buying. This will most likely lead to NFT marketplaces being more specialized based on certain use cases. OpenSea, one of the original NFT marketplaces, is seeing rapid growth. Trading volumes on OpenSea increased by more than 12,000 percent in the year 2021, with a record $150 million in NFT transactions in June alone. If that wasn't enough good news, their user base grew from 315 to 14,520 in just six months, propelling the company to a monthly NFT volume of $3 billion in August. All of this is wonderful news for the NFT as well as the digital finance industry as a whole, but a specialized marketplace can't move the sector forward as quickly as an internationally recognized juggernaut like Alibaba.
