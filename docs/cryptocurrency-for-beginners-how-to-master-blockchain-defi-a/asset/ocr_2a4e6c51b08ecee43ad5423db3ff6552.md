# Criticisms of Dogecoin

The news press and money managers had difficulty admitting bitcoin as a serious currency. Experts have also said that it is an exceedingly dangerous investment due to pump-and-dump behavior and increased susceptibility to guesses and fads fueled by herd mentality. Others have referred to it as a pyramid scam.

David Gerard stated in a Foreign Policy piece from 2021 that there is no meaningful purpose for cryptocurrency other than trading. He went on to say that these "assets" had no applications. Individuals who aren't sure what they're doing and are just jumping on the bandwagon are often victims of personal finance and investing fads.
