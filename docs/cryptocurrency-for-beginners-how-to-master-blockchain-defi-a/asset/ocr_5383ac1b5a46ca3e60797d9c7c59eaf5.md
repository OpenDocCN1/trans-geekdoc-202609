Distributed Ledger is a decentralized database that is administered by various people. Blockchain is a sort of distributed ledger technology in which information is recorded using a hash, which is an immutable electronic signature.

# The Properties of Distributed Ledger Technology

# Secure

Each record is encrypted separately.

# Immutable

Any records that have been verified are irrevocable and cannot be modified.

# Programmable

A blockchain may be programmed (Smart contracts, etc.)
