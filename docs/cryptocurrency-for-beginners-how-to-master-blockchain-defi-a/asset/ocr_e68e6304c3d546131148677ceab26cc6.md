# Disadvantages of Litecoin

The hazards associated with litecoin are comparable to those associated with other cryptocurrencies. As of the writing of this essay, the whole industry is just a dozen years old. And, as with any developing sector — especially one that isn't effectively controlled - the danger of losses is great. This is why:

# High volatility

The cryptocurrency market is very speculative, and as a result, it is quite volatile. The digital currency Litecoin was one of the top 10 currencies in the world in June 2021; as of Oct 2021, it has fallen to just outside the top 20. As a result of how quickly things may change, this tendency may not be a reliable predictor of what is to come. However, it is something to keep in mind while deciding whether to hold litecoin as an investment or as a means of preserving wealth.

# Regulatory holdups

Although Litecoin may theoretically be used for faster payments, the fact is that crypto's development as a universal payment method and legal trade is still in the early stages.

All of this is to imply that, although investing in Litecoin has certain advantages — for example, the currency has a lot of liquidity and has been around for a decade — it also has significant drawbacks.

# Pros

**Reliability:** When it comes to the core tech, Litecoin investors may rest comfortably knowing that the cryptocurrency is one of the oldest, having been developed as a fork of the Bitcoin network. LTC, like Bitcoin, is an open-source and decentralized cryptocurrency.
