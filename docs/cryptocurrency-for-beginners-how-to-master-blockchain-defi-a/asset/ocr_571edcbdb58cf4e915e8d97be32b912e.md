The following are some of the important factors in favor of the USDT:

# Value Stability

When you decide to purchase or sell other cryptocurrencies, you should be aware of their volatility. With Tether, this is not the case. Furthermore, several exchanges accept USDT rather than fiat money.

# Transaction Times

USDT transactions are completed in a matter of minutes. This is advantageous to everyone, especially traders who trade in minutes rather than days. Traditional banking procedures, on either hand, might take days to process USD deposits and withdrawals.
