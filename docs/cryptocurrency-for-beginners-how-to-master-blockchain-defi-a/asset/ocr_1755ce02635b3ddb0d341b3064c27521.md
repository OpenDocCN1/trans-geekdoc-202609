# 3.5.13 Tether

![](bbox=[0.273, 0.182, 0.722, 0.38])

Tether is a fiat-collateralized stable coin that allows users to transact with blockchain-based assets while avoiding price volatility. Tether is mainly issued on the Ethereum as well as Bitcoin blockchains and is pegged to US Dollars in bank accounts at a 1:1 ratio.

# History

Tether (USDT), formerly known as "Realcoin," is a cryptocurrency that aims to maintain a fixed 1:1 exchange rate with the US dollar. It was founded in July 2014 by Brock Pierce, Craig Sellars, and Reeve Collins. Built on Mastercoin (Omni), a protocol layer for Bitcoin that introduced the stablecoin concept to the world in 2012, Tether was one of the first stablecoins created. Tether is the most widely used stable coin today, having pioneered what is now known as a fiat-collateralized stablecoin model. Tether was created to address two critical issues with existing cryptocurrencies: high volatility as well as fiat currency-to-cryptocurrency convertibility.
