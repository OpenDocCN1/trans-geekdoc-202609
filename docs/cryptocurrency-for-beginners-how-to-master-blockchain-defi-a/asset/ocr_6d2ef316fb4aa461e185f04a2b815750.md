# 6.6 What’s next for NFTs?

NFT proponents do not believe the market’s latest slump is the end of the road. The market’s treatment of high-profile NFTs as speculative assets was clear when they sold for millions of dollars. The more pressing question for NFTs is their long-term worth, which is expected to be substantial. Ordinary people will spend more and more of their time — and thus money — in virtual environments as augmented & virtual reality technology improves.
