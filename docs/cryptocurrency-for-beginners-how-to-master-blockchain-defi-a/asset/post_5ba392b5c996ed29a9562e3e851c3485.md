## Invest in companies with crypto holdings

Consider this method to be a bitcoin investment with a twist. Certain publicly listed firms hold cryptocurrency. Because they're banking on succeeding, you can, too, with those corporations functioning as a safety net.

Examining a company's financial statements may disclose a lot of information: Tesla has had $1.31 million in digital content since about June 30, 2021. While Tesla's $1.31 billion investment has garnered a lot of media attention, it only accounts for roughly 2.4 percent of its overall assets. However, if those assets grow in price, as cryptocurrency is prone to doing, Tesla's stock price may also rise.

## Get ready for a cryptocurrency ETF

Although the Securities Commission has yet to authorize bitcoin exchange-traded funds, they are needed. A crypto ETF would work similarly to any other ETF, and only it would follow a cryptocurrency rather than a stock market index like the S&P 500. A bitcoin ETF, for example, would monitor the value of bitcoin.

Many efforts at ETFs have been made, with many of them being rejected. There are bitcoin ETFs that have been approved in other countries, and I believe that this is only a matter of time. ## 5.3 Common mistakes to avoid as investors

It takes luck, but more importantly, knowledge and accuracy, to invest in the correct cryptocurrency at the appropriate moment. The only people who constantly destroy the crowds enhance their investing strategies every day, one error after another.

Here's a rundown of frequent blunders to avoid while investing in the extremely unpredictable cryptocurrency market:

## You Don't Understand the Technology

The route will be dangerous if you don't comprehend the technology's basis. You'll be losing out on major chances unless you can appraise projects for yourself.

![](img/9e732599f379d4f1dd55a1fd64d01531_112_0.png)

## You Think Cryptocurrencies are Shares

Cryptocurrencies are not the same as equities because they are not shares. You don’t own any stock in the firm and don’t get any dividends. If a business issues a cryptocurrency, the business will likely profit or be purchased, with no benefits to you. Even if a firm is doing well, its currency might fall in value.

## You Overtrade

Some investors, typically novices, desire to make 20 deals every day. This is quite risky. Many of them eventually lose money due to fees or just because they make terrible deals and then sell more to make up for their losses. The truth is that there aren’t 20 excellent trade chances every day. Too much trading leads to poor decision-making. Overtrading might potentially raise your tax bill.

## You Chase Cheap Coins

Don’t waste your time chasing cheap pennies with fantasies of Lambos or private aircraft. Many inexperienced entrepreneurs in the crypto world purchase low-cost coins in the hopes of making large profits. This is a very typical pitfall. There are a number of elements that influence the price of a coin, including two major ones: the circulating supply and the coin’s real-world worth.

## You Research Poorly

The procedure will take some time. However, the more study you do, the better you’ll become at it. You can find each coin’s release thread and website on BitcoinTalk.org. Examine the coin’s economy, including its market capitalization, trade volume, value management, and total vs. circulating quantity. Compare and contrast the views of industry professionals.

## You Don't Diversify Your Portfolio.

Don't put all the eggs in one basket, as tempting as it may seem. Every seasoned investor diversifies his or her risk by investing in a range of assets. Having at least 5 cryptocurrencies is a smart idea.

## You Don't HODL Hard Enough.

Many investors are impatient and, as a result of their emotions, cut their losses early. The bitcoin market operates in cycles, with values fluctuating dramatically. If you purchase high, you'll have to wait for a whole new industry cycle to profit — that is, a new bear market followed by a new bull market, which may take well over a year. As a result, never, ever gamble with money you can't afford to lose!

## 5.4 Investment strategies

As more investors flocked to the cryptocurrency market in search of growth from investments that have the potential to expand in value over time, cryptocurrency assets such as Bitcoin and alternative cryptocurrencies were among the top-performing investment vehicles in 2021. Cryptocurrencies, on the other hand, are intrinsically risky investments that are subject to huge price movements. One of the numerous risk considerations that crypto traders must examine if they really want to make money investing in this burgeoning asset class, which currently has a total market capitalization of much more than $2 trillion, is the volatility of the cryptocurrency market. Investing in digital commodities is not all that unlike investing in conventional assets such as stocks and bonds, as previously stated. Listed below are some basic investing principles to remember as you manage your cryptocurrency holdings.

## Choose the right mix of storage.

There are a few options for storing your bitcoin in order to keep it secure. Warm or cold storage is used to store digital assets. A hot digital wallet is one that is saved online, while a cold digital wallet is one that is stored offline, generally on a hard disk. To stop hackers from obtaining access, experts recommend storing the bulk of your bitcoin in a cold wallet. It's helpful to keep some cryptocurrency in a hot wallet connected to the internet so crypto traders may swiftly enter and exit positions. According to Yubo Ruan, CEO and creator of Simultaneous Finance, a new decentralized borrowing and stake protocol on Polkadot, storing around 80% of long-term money in a cold wallet is a good crypto storage method. Then, the hot wallet may be utilized for short-term transactions.

## Harness volatility.

Because bitcoin is still a new asset, there is a lot of speculation and excitement around it, which may contribute to a lot of volatility. While significant price swings are often seen as a danger, everyday volatility is healthy and natural for the cryptocurrency world, and it may even be used to benefit. Greenberg demonstrates why volatility is really beneficial to savvy traders. However, in order to properly manage your volatility risk, you must first determine what sort of trader you are and how you will handle the market's price fluctuations. She emphasizes that it's essential to keep a careful eye on what's going on in the market as well as the traded item itself. This entails keeping up with the news and any connected blockchain developments, as well as previous charts, in order to spot developing trends.

## Take your gains often.

Crypto market traders, according to experts, should take profits often. Gains should be kept in your cryptocurrency wallet as a best practice. When it comes to making gains, crypto traders are often confronted with the risk that a cryptocurrency's value could fall or rise. Profit-taking on a regular basis helps smooth out this danger over time. "A lot of folks simply purchase and keep for an indeterminate period of time," Greenberg continues, "and they're at the whim of news, fads, and celebrity tweets." To properly define your profit-taking crypto trading strategy, it's vital to understand why you're joining a crypto trade in the first place, just as it is with any investment. You may specify your entrance and departure points this way.

## Prioritize liquidity.

When it comes to determining how to participate in the crypto market, liquidity is crucial. Because the market changes swiftly, crypto traders must be able to quickly enter and exit positions. This implies that there must be a demand for bitcoin so that market players may acquire at the best possible price and sell at a profit should they wish to sell any of their holdings. "You don't want to acquire an asset that has amazing potential but isn't being traded and is simply stagnating, then you're waiting on it and at the mercy of a market," explains Tally Greenberg, director of business at crypto hosting provider all nodes. Looking at a crypto asset's recent trade volume might be useful for determining liquidity. The amount of cryptocurrencies purchased and sold is referred to as trading volume, and it reflects the asset's overall popularity.

## Invest what you can afford.

Cryptocurrencies are highly speculative investments with a significant risk of loss. Invest just how much you can afford to give up in the crypto market, just as you would in regular investment. You cannot handle the danger of spending the money you are contemplating if you are unable to bear the whole loss of the crypto investment.
