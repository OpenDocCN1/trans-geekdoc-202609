# 3.5 History of Cryptocurrencies

Since the introduction of Bitcoin in 2009, the crypto currency world has grown by leaps and bounds all over the world. What began as an unproven concept is now widely regarded as the future of the currency. Thousands of other crypto currencies joined as well as failed prior to Bitcoin's advancement since the crypto exchange's opening. Although Bitcoin remains the most popular crypto currency by far and most similar crypto currencies have negligible non-speculative effects, other crypto currencies should be kept in mind by investors and consumers.
