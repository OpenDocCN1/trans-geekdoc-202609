EOS is a decentralized blockchain-based platform for developing, hosting, and running business applications or dApps. EOS was launched in June 2018 after Block. one raised $4.1 billion in cryptocurrency through an initial coin offering. EOS is the name of the open-source software developed by this company. On the network, EOS cryptocurrency tokens are used as a payment system.

# What is EOS?

EOS provides core functionality that enables companies and individuals to create blockchain-based applications in the same way that web-based applications are created. EOS provides secure access as well as authentication, permissions, data hosting, usage management, as well as dApp-to-Internet communication. EOS is backed by a web toolkit store that aims to make app development as painless as possible. EOS is made up of two main components: the EOS.IO software and EOS tokens. To use network resources and build and run DApps, a developer-only needs to hold EOS coins rather than spend them. Token holders who do not have any apps running can rent or allocate their bandwidth to other participants who do. EOS was founded by Dan Larimer, who is also the founder and creator of well-known platforms such as Bitshares and Steem. It is currently owned by the block. One company.
