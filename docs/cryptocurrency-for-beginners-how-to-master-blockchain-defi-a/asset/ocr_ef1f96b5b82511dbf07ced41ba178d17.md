Ethereum also varies by acting as a building framework for dApps/smart contracts, which enable it to submit tokens that reflect values. These values can be anything other than digital currency, rendering it different from Bitcoin.

# Their transactions

Bitcoin’s transactions are manual. On the other hand, Ethereum’s transactions are automatic and programmable.

# The speed of the transactions

Block time of Ethereum is only seconds (transaction speed). However, the block time of Bitcoin is minutes.

+   - Ether is built for the power transaction of the Ethereum Network – assume this to be gas.
+   - The value of real-world purchases is based on Bitcoin. So it is considered as currency.

# Their coin supply

Bitcoin is limited to only 21,000,000 coins. This generates supply and demand, which is ideal for a store of value. Ethereum, however, is not limited. The output of Ether is persistent. The supply of Ether will drop down a lot over time, though.

# Their ages

Ethereum is 6 years newer in contrast to Bitcoin. Moreover, it is further advanced.
