Hopeful entrepreneurs believe that cryptocurrencies have the potential to revolutionize payments by serving as a currency, facilitating, and storing value. Crypto has won a position in certain stock market investors' portfolios due to its potential function as a safe haven for funds.

Simply expressed, their theory is that bitcoin operates as "digital gold," with its value rising when the price of food rises dramatically (a.k.a. inflation). That's because cryptocurrencies' supply is constrained by design (much like actual gold, although by chance), and their improved utility over gold – except that it's simpler to acquire, transmit, and store – would imply that they're more valuable.

# Speculation

The price of a cryptocurrency, like the price of a financial asset, is determined by supply and demand. And other investors believe that the value of cryptocurrencies will climb as they become more popular, attracting more retail investors and investment firms. Speculators are crypto investors who gamble on such an event. They pay close attention to news that implies increased acceptance of cryptocurrency and elevated crypto-based initiatives, partially in the expectation that it would entice more investors to join in, raising the value of the assets.

# Decentralized finance

Decentralized finance, often known as DeFi, is a rapidly expanding segment of the cryptocurrency business whose potential has piqued the interest of a number of cryptocurrency enthusiasts. Its decentralized structure was one of the primary reasons for bitcoin's early popularity, and Defined takes that concept a step by further developing investment products that stand to gain from cryptocurrency.
