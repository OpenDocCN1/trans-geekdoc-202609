Digital money, or digital currency, is any form of money or payment that is only available in electronic form. Digital currency lacks a tangible form, like a bill, receipt, or coins. Electronic codes are used to account for and transmit it in machines. Payments are becoming more automated as automation becomes more prevalent, resulting in a decrease in the need for physical currency. Cryptocurrency, also widely recognized as crypto, is a type of digital currency that can be used as a form of payment. It employs cryptography to secure & verify transactions, as well as to regulate the creation of new digital currency units. Blockchain technology is a distributed ledger mandated by a distributed network of computers. Blockchain is at the heart of many cryptocurrencies.
