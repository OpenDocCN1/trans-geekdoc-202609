# **Cryptocurrencies vs. Traditional Currencies**

Consider the following scenario: you wish to thank a buddy who gave you lunch by transferring money with her or his account over the internet. There are several ways things may go wrong, including:

+   - There might be a technical problem with the financial institution, such as its systems being down or equipment not operating correctly.
+   - Your or your friend's account's transfer limitations may have been exceeded.
+   - Your or a friend's account might have been managed to hack example, a rejection attack or information theft could have occurred.
+   - A bank is the major point of collapse.

This is why bitcoin is the money of the future. Consider a comparable transaction between two persons who are using the bitcoin app. A message displays asking whether the user is certain he or she may be ready to send bitcoins. If yes, processing begins: the system verifies the user's identification, determines whether the client has sufficient funds to complete the payment, and so on. The payment is then transmitted, and the funds are deposited in the recipient's account. It just takes a few minutes for all of this to happen.
