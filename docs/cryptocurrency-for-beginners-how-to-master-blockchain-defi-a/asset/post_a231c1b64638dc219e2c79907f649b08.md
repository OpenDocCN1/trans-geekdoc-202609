## 5.3 How do NFTs work?

As compared to other cryptocurrencies, NFTs are not instantly exchangeable because none of the two identical NFTs exist despite belonging to the same platform, collection or game. You can consider them as festival passes. Each ticket contains information such as the purchaser's name, the date and the venue of the event. This information makes it difficult to exchange festival tickets.

### 5.3.1 How to buy NFTs

In principle, any digital image might be purchased as an NFT. However, especially if you're a newbie, there are a few things to bear in mind while acquiring one. You'll need to pick a marketplace to purchase from, a digital wallet to keep your cryptocurrency in, and the cryptocurrency you'll need to execute the transaction. Some of the most prominent NFT markets are OpenSea, Mintable, Nifty Gateway, and Rarible.

### 5.3.2 How to sell NFTs?

NFTs are also sold in marketplaces; however, the method varies by platform. Basically, you'll post your work to a marketplace and then follow the procedures to convert it to an NFT. You'll be able to enter details like a work description and pricing suggestions. The majority of NFTs are bought with ethereum, but they can also be acquired with other ERC-20 tokens like WAX and Flow.

### 5.3.3 How to create an NFT?

An NFT can be created by anyone. All you'll need is a digital wallet, a little ether, and access to an NFT marketplace where you can upload and convert your work into an NFT or crypto art. You might well be interested in producing NFTs for your work if you're an aspiring digital artist. Fortunately, there are various platforms that can assist you in getting started. The process is rather simple in general, and the various platforms will walk you through it. However, there may be a few things you should be aware of before you begin. NFTs are built on top of and supported by a specific blockchain. The Ethereum blockchain is now the most popular for non-fungible tokens. You'll need a bitcoin wallet and cryptocurrency to get started. Currently, ether is the most extensively utilized (ETH). On an NFT marketplace, you can generate and sell digital assets. The Ethereum-based OpenSea platform is very popular.

## 5.4 NFT and blockchain

There are several different types of blockchain technology available today. Some blockchains were created to cater to the needs of a small group of people with limited access to the network. So, where do NFTs fit into the blockchain world? A blockchain, remember, is a piece of software that acts as a distributed ledger across nodes in a communications network. Its immutability sets it apart from other online databases or trading platforms: we may trade digital assets peer to peer, and no one can change or erase those transactions without the agreement of a majority of the network. That's a huge bonus when it comes to the Internet. Each token in an NFT represents a one-of-a-kind item. You couldn't expect anyone to notice if you replaced an Andrew Wyeth painting with Mike Winkelmann's art in a contract. NFTs cover a wide range of unique assets, including birth and death certificates, property deeds, and the identities of Internet of Things objects. While the CryptoKitties (the first NFT craze) bubble may have burst, the excitement surrounding the auction of Beeple's work demonstrated the potential of NFTs. It's a field of value creation where your only limitations are your imagination, technical skill, and marketing, accounting, and legal backing.

## Digital asset

Simply said, a digital asset is anything that exists in digital form and has the ability to be used (duplicate, a right to copy, reproduce, and modify). Documents, audio or visual information, photographs, and other related digital data, for example, are all termed digital assets.

## 5.5 NFTs vs. Cryptocurrency

The distinction between cryptocurrencies and non-fungible tokens must be clarified. Despite the fact that they both use blockchain technology, the fundamental differences between them might help us comprehend how NFTs work. The main distinction stems from the fact that cryptocurrency is fungible. You can, for example, exchange one Bitcoin for another. You cannot, however, do so with an NFT. A non-fungible token is linked to a single digital asset and cannot be exchanged for another. The major distinction between the NFT and cryptocurrency is that, unlike cryptocurrency, NFTs are one-of-a-kind representations of real-world goods that cannot be traded for one another. Cryptocurrencies can be traded for each other because their value will not depreciate. Cryptocurrencies and NFTs, on the other hand, are decentralized and governed by the communities that run them. Furthermore, cryptocurrencies and NFTs may be accessed via a digital public ledger, which makes all transactions transparent.

## 5.6 NFT vs. Bitcoin

While an NFT uses the same technology as a cryptocurrency, such as Bitcoin, the similarities end there. Bitcoin may be traded for one another and have the same value, i.e., one rupee is always worth another rupee, and one Bitcoin is always worth another Bitcoin, regardless of price variations against other similar currencies. NFTs aren't like other materials. Each NFT (virtual asset) has a digital signature that can't be copied exactly due to encoding.

## 5.7 The pros and cons of NFTs

We have listed below some potential pros and cons:

### 5.7.1 Pros of NFTs

Some of the advantages of NFTs include:

### Security

NFTs can be traded in a safe and transparent environment. NFTs work as unchangeable digital signatures, giving collectors extraordinary trust. This assurance also extends to the art's future legacy. The blockchain protects digital works from censorship and physical degradation, but they must still be carefully stored. Blockchain technology also enables people to move large amounts of money around the world securely and in full view of the public. NFTs now guarantee legitimacy, uniqueness, and ownership of unique assets, just as blockchain technology built a trust protocol that enabled the establishment of digital currency.

### Price appreciation

Physical valuables (such as art) have a long history of increasing in value, and digital art could follow suit.

### Increased access to buyers and sellers

Buying as well as selling digital assets as NFTs gives you access to a much larger pool of buyers and sellers than before.

### They give artists ownership of digital assets

When content creators develop a digital asset, an NFT allows them to not only demonstrate authenticity but also profit from it. This might represent a big money stream for the originator of memes, which are extensively shared.

### They're unique and collectible

Many people like the thrill of acquiring a one-of-a-kind or extremely rare object. Collectible content, especially digital goods, benefits from NFTs
