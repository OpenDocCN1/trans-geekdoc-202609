Bitcoin Cash differs from Bitcoin in several ways, with the primary goal of creating sound money that is accessible to everyone. To begin with, compared to Bitcoin, Bitcoin Cash has a larger block size limit parameter. It can process up to two million transactions per day with its increased block size limit of 8MB. BCH's larger block size enables it to process more transactions per second. This means that payments will be processed more quickly, and fees will be reduced. Bitcoin Cash transaction signatures differ slightly because a brand-new SigHash type provides replay and wipeout protection as well as improved hardware wallet security. The problem of quadratic hashing is also solved. Bitcoin Cash also has a new feature called Emergency Difficulty Adjustment (EDA). This allows you to adjust the proof-of-work difficulty more quickly and effectively. This means miners can easily transition away from the legacy Bitcoin chain while ensuring that users are not affected by hash rate fluctuations. Bitcoin Cash lacks the "replace by fee" feature, and its development is decentralized, with multiple independent development teams providing software implementations. This means it can't be controlled by a single entity, making it immune to political or social upheavals. During its years of existence, the Bitcoin Cash network has seen a lot of infrastructure support, with more merchants, wallet providers, and exchanges announcing their growing support of the new cryptocurrency.
