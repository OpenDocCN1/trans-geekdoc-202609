# 6.4.6 KnownOrigin

KnownOrigin is an online marketplace where you can find and purchase rare digital artwork. Every piece of digital art on KnownOrigin is genuine and one-of-a-kind. Creators can utilize the site to show off their work and sell it to collectors that value authenticity. The Ethereum blockchain protects it. Creators can upload digital artwork to the KnownOrigin gallery as a jpg or Gif file, with all files stored on IPFS.

# 6.4.7 Mintable

Mintable is a two-sided marketplace for buying as well as selling NFTs which resembles eBay and is backed by Marc Benioff's Time Ventures and billionaire investor Mark Cuban. The site, which is built on the Ethereum and Zilliqa blockchains, is coupled with MetaMask, which allows buyers to create cryptocurrency wallets. Free "gasless" NFTs, short-run printing series, and regular transaction-based things are all options for creators. Buyers can purchase featured things or bid on auctions after creating profiles and loading their wallets, with winners notified via email.

# 6.4.8 Axie Infinity

Axie Infinity is a Pokemon-inspired video game in which players acquire cartoon creatures, combat other players, and establish farming kingdoms. It was created by the Vietnamese startup Sky Mavis. Between September 18 and October 18, 2021, Axie Infinity recorded an average of more than 23 million monthly players on their website. NFTs are used to encrypt characters and land plots. Collectors and players may purchase them on an internet marketplace, with some virtual property parcels reaching more than $20,000 and animals fetching hundreds of dollars.
