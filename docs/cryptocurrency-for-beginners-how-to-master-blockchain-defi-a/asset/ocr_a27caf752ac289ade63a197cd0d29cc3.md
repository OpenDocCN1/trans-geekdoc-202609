# 1.2 Benefits of Cryptocurrency

+   - In contrast to the price for transferring funds from a wallet app to a bank account, the transaction cost for cryptocurrencies is minimal to none.
+   - You may make purchases and withdrawals at any time of day or night, and there are no limitations on how much you can spend.
+   - And, unlike opening bank accounts, which need documents and other procedures, anybody may use cryptocurrencies.
+   - Cryptocurrency transactions are also quicker than wire transfers on a global scale. Money is transported from one location to another roughly half a day through wire transfers. Transactions using cryptocurrency are completed in seconds or minutes.
