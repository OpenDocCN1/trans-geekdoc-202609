## Reasons behind the uniqueness of Ethereum

Ethereum has pioneered the idea of a blockchain smart contract network. Smart contracts are computer systems that immediately perform the activities required to satisfy an arrangement between multiple parties on the internet. They were built to reduce the need for trusted intermediates between contractors, thereby lowering transaction costs while also increasing transaction efficiency. Ethereum’s principal breakthrough was developing a network that allowed it to conduct smart contracts using the blockchain, which further strengthens the already existing advantages of smart contract technology. Ethereum’s blockchain was built as a sort of one machine for the whole world. In addition to various smart contracts, Ethereum’s blockchain is able to host other crypto currencies, dubbed “tokens.” In reality, this has been the most common use for the ETH platform so far.

## Ethereum ecosystem

The Ethereum blockchain stack comprises multiple modules. At heart, there is the Ethereum blockchain operating on the peer-to-peer Ethereum network. Furthermore, there's an Ethereum client that operates on the nodes and links to the peer-to-peer Ethereum network from where blockchain can be downloaded and stored locally. It provides multiple functions, like mining and account management. A local copy of the blockchain is then synchronized periodically with the network. Another aspect is the web3.js library that enables contact with the geth client through the Remote Procedure Call (RPC) interface. A list of all high-level elements present in the Ethereum blockchain contains the following:

- Keys and addresses
- Accounts
- Transactions and messages
- Ether crypto currency/tokens
- The EVM
- Smart contracts

## Ethereum vs. Bitcoin

Ethereum and Bitcoin are entirely different from one another. They seem to have very different goals in mind.

In reality, other crypto currencies relate a lot more to Bitcoin and Ethereum than Ethereum and Bitcoin compared to each other. For instance, Litecoin is much more like Bitcoin than Ethereum is, and NEO is more like Ethereum than Bitcoin is.

### Comparing the Crypto currencies

When you glance at Ethereum vs. Bitcoin, you will see that their aims are widely divergent. Looking at their market figures, however, the gap is very minimal — Bitcoin's market capitalization stands at $147.3b in contrast to Ethereum's market capitalization of $84.2b.

Now take a look at a few of the functionality and facts of Bitcoin and Ethereum.

### Bitcoin Features

Listed below are the salient features of Bitcoin.

- Introduced in 2009, BTC was the very first crypto currency that made use of Blockchain technology
- Bitcoin is a digital money
- It seeks to address the challenges of global finance
- Bitcoin does not require a third party, like PayPal or a bank
- Developed as an alternative to regular currencies
- Used for manual purchases, including buying goods/services or collecting money
- It is highly liquid, which means you can quickly turn Bitcoin into cash
- It takes minutes to complete Bitcoin transactions

### Ethereum Features

Listed below are the salient features of Ethereum.

- Launched in 2015, Ethereum’s blockchain commonly recognized synonym is Blockchain 2.0
- Smart contracts program the transfers
- Its smart contracts remove the need for third parties in multiple structures, not just financial ones
- It offers a forum to develop smart-contract applications (known as dApps)
- It offers a programming language, named Solidity, to develop the dApps with
- Ether is the currency; it drives transfers on the Ethereum blockchain
- Ether is extremely liquid
- Ethereum’s transfers take seconds to complete

## Major differences

Let's look at the key differences between Ethereum and Bitcoin.

### Their concepts

When you think of Bitcoin, think about digital money. When you think of Ethereum, think about smart contracts.

### Their purposes

Bitcoin is a store of value, and a means to transfer money to others. Ethereum is also a means to give money to others, but only when such incidents happen.

Ethereum also varies by acting as a building framework for dApps/smart contracts, which enable it to submit tokens that reflect values. These values can be anything other than digital currency, rendering it different from Bitcoin.

### Their transactions

Bitcoin’s transactions are manual. On the other hand, Ethereum’s transactions are automatic and programmable.

### The speed of the transactions

Block time of Ethereum is only seconds (transaction speed). However, the block time of Bitcoin is minutes.

- Ether is built for the power transaction of the Ethereum Network – assume this to be gas.
- The value of real-world purchases is based on Bitcoin. So it is considered as currency.

### Their coin supply

Bitcoin is limited to only 21,000,000 coins. This generates supply and demand, which is ideal for a store of value. Ethereum, however, is not limited. The output of Ether is persistent. The supply of Ether will drop down a lot over time, though.

### Their ages

Ethereum is 6 years newer in contrast to Bitcoin. Moreover, it is further advanced.

### Smart contracts

Smart contracts are a decentralized instrument. Ethereum was the one that launched smart contracts into the world in the war against Ethereum vs. Bitcoin. With smart contracts, when a deal happens, you will set conditions. A smart contract is software that is running on the blockchain of Ethereum. This is a set of code (its functions) and data (its condition) stored in the Ethereum blockchain at a certain address. Smart contracts are a form of an account of Ethereum. This ensures that they have a balance, and transfers can be transmitted through the network. They are, however, not operated by a person but rather distributed and run as programmed on the network. User accounts will then communicate with a smart contract by transactions that fulfill a smart contract-defined purpose. Smart contracts can define and automatically execute rules through a code, like a standard contract. All should write and deploy a smart contract to the network. You just need to know how to code and have enough ETH to deploy the contract in a smart contract language. Since smart Ethereum contracts are driven by the blockchain, developers may design applications that are never offline and therefore cannot be edited by third parties. One good way to think about smart contracts is to think about owning a home. Normally, third parties such as a lawyer and a broker would be needed. The possession of the house is immediately submitted after a prerequisite has been fulfilled with a smart contract.
