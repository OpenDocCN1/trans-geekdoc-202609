# Wallets

Recall those private wallet keys we discussed earlier? Someone who isn't you might possibly use the key to obtain access to your cash unless it's carefully kept offline.

You should also be aware that two users who have access to the same wallet's private key have an equal right to cash. So it's a concern if your key slips into the hands of the wrong people via any way — online or offline. Because cryptos are decentralized, there is no 3rd authority to settle disputes or impose ownership, hence why ensuring sure you're the only one with access to your private key is crucial.
