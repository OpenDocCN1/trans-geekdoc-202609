# 6.4.10 Venly

Venky's peer-to-peer NFT marketplace, which is already in beta and has a user base of over 200,000 players, enables logged-in users to create, buy, and sell game characters, weapons, and collectibles without having to first obtain bitcoin. Users can buy and sell assets from blockchain games like The Sandbox, Ethermon, and Vulcan Verse by linking their accounts to digital wallets. The blockchain agnostic marketplace now accepts PayPal payments in US dollars thanks to a partnership with Polygon, a development framework for linking Ethereum-compatible blockchain networks and cryptocurrency exchanges like Binance.
