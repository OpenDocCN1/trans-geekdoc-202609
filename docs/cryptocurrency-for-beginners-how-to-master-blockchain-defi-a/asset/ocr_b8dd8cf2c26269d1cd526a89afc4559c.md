# 3.5.2 SmartHive & SmartVoting

![](bbox=[0.28, 0.215, 0.715, 0.445])

The SmartCash community says that a cryptocurrency with a "core" team is inefficient and corrupt in the long run. The fear is that the founders will place a higher priority on making money than creating an open and low-cost platform. With the rise of ICOs and increasing token allocations for founding teams, we've seen this play out. These central organizations can become bureaucratic, hard to react to, and prioritize conflicting goals. Conversely, SmartCash recommends a governance model based on the self-organization of ants and bees. The goal is to create a hive where people can work on specific projects in small groups. There's a case to be made that this disjointed approach will end up being less efficient than the Foundation/core team approach it seeks to replace. Self-governing teams, on the other hand, are thought to be more agile and productive than guided development, according to the community. Voting rights will be granted to all SMART token holders.
