While bitcoin has made a name for itself as a digital currency, many crypto

assets that may be used from money to a digital item or service.

# Why do cryptocurrencies have any value?

It's all about supply and demand, which is purposely constrained for many
large cryptos. A token's value will grow if it can attract users. As a result, if
there is a demand for bitcoin as a means of transferring financial value, its
value is likely to rise. Real-world assets, like property investment or a gallon
of gas, may also be linked to digital tokens, and their value can be derived
from them. The most high-profile crypto transactions, on the other hand,
have been the most bizarre, such as when someone bought $170,000 for just
a token depicting a digital cat.

# How do you buy cryptocurrency?

You'll likely want to select a crypto exchange since it's the most common
technique. These let you acquire cryptocurrency with your fiat money and
exchange between various forms of crypto. Digital currencies come in many
forms and sizes, so deciding which one to use isn't always easy. Make sure
your selected exchange enables you to buy the cryptocurrency tokens you're
interested in as well as convert between fiat currencies. You should also
consider the transaction costs charged by exchanges, if any, and make sure
you're okay paying them.
