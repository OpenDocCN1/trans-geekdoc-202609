# 2.1 Altcoins

Every cryptocurrency that isn't Bitcoin is referred to as an altcoin. Altcoins are crypto coins or tokens that are not Bitcoin, such as Solana, Cardano, Polkadot, and Uniswap. Because Bitcoin is the most well-known and well-established cryptocurrency on the planet, some people refer to it as the "primary" cryptocurrency. Bitcoin has the largest market capitalization, or the actual amount of issued cryptocurrency compared to other cryptocurrencies.
