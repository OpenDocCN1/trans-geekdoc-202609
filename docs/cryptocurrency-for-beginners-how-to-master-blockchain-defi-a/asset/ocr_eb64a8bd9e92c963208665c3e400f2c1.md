# Ethereum

Despite being a secondary consideration to Bitcoin in terms of market
capitalization, Eth, which accounts for roughly 20% of the global market, is
arguably on the verge of overtaking Bitcoin in terms of prominence. That's
because it serves a purpose other than just a store of money, with Ethereum
easily claiming the title of most widely used blockchain on the planet.
Ethereum's native currency, Ether (ETH), is one of the finest
cryptocurrencies to purchase since it supports smart contracts, nonfungible
tokens, and other cryptocurrencies. When it switches from the proof-of-
work to the proof-of-stake protocol, ETH will become significantly more
energy-efficient than Bitcoin, removing the far more energy-intensive
requirement to verify transactions on the blockchain via complicated
computing operations.
