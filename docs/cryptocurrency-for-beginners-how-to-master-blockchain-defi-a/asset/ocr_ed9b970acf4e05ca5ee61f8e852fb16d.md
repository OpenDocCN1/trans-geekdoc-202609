These accounts are necessary to be published, and the participants who do exchanges verify their balances by asking a large number of them in the second protocol. The participants also make sure the money supply isn't inflated. To become a server, you must post a bond, which is forfeited if the server is found to be dishonest. An alternative method of creating money is proposed, involving an auction in which participants bid on the solution of known-complexity computational problems. Regretfully, the B-Money project has remained stuck since the white paper was implemented.
