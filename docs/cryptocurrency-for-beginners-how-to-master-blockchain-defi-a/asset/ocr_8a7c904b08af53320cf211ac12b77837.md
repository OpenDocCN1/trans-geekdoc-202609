# They're immutable

Non-fungible tokens can never be changed, erased, or replaced because they are blockchain-based. This is a valuable attribute to have when demonstrating the origin or authenticity of digital content.

# Composability

One of the most exciting aspects of NFTs is that they may be thought of as interconnected Lego pieces. This means that designers can launch NFTs that capitalize on the success of previous NFTs. Any NFT project can be regarded as a module of a much broader, interconnected ecosystem because of its composability.

# They can include smart contracts

Smart contracts are yet another exciting aspect of blockchain technology. They can basically hold instructions that are carried out when specific criteria are met. As a result, an NFT with a smart contract might provide artists with a portion of the profit if the NFT is sold in the future. Artists and producers can be paid in the future based on the use and resale of their work using "smart contracts," which are a series of coded commands incorporated into the blockchain.
