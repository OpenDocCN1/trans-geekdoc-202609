# 2.1.2 Use of money as a medium of exchange

People used to barter to purchase the services and goods they wanted before the establishment of a means of trade, i.e., money. Two people, each owning certain commodities that the other desired, will enter into a trade agreement. However, early forms of bartering do not have the divisibility and transferability that ultimately makes trading successful.
