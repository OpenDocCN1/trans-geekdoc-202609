Tether, or USDT, is a cryptocurrency that has been linked to fiat money to ensure stability. Protocol Omni has created a cryptocurrency based on the Bitcoin network. Its value is 'tethered' to the value of the US dollar. The currency was created to solve three key issues:

+   - Enable Bitcoin stability
+   - Enable country's currency transfers
+   - Provide choices for verifying

# How does it work?

Tether Company is a Chinese company that acts as a 3rd person for the assets of this cryptocurrency. It maintains a fiat currency reserve equal to the quantity of USDT in circulation. A simple solution may reduce the difficulty of executing fiat and cryptocurrency audits. The audit's transparency or security will not be harmed as a result of its implementation.

USDT is based on Omni technology, a flexible platform for various currency and digital assets linked to the Bitcoin network. The idea is each USDT is valued at $1 and that you may exchange it for fiat cash at any moment.
