+   15. Remediation step requires careful discussion concerning the scan results, with the goal of satisfying both concerns of technical staff and the organization's business objectives?

+   C. Distribution of the results

+   D. Categorization of the results

+   - B is correct. Prioritization is the goal—that is, to discuss the results and decide on the next steps in remediation. Far too often results can be overwhelming if the focus is to "fix everything."

+   - A, C, and D are incorrect. A is incorrect, although validation is a correct step, it's generally a task done by an incorrect technical team, not shared among management. C is incorrect because distribution was already carefully decided upon and can be found in the statement of work. D is incorrect because categorization is not such a delicate discussion.
