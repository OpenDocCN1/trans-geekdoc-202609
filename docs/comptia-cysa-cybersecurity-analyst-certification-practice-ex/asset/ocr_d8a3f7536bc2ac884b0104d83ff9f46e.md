+   19. After the results of a vulnerability scan are prioritized into remediation steps, a company’s cybersecurity team began working on implementing these steps. All systems that were affected by the remediation team continued operating as expected, except for one. One server’s application stopped functioning, no longer able to reach other systems. The system owner could not figure out why or how the system just stopped working. What overall process seems most at fault?

+   A. Communication/change control

+   B. Patching/remediation

+   C. Business continuity

+   D. Systems administration

+   A is correct. Communication and practicing proper change control are very likely the cause for the unexpected application disconnect. Whether a previously open network port was closed or a service deemed unnecessary was shut down in the past. Change control, if done correctly, should have alerted the system owners to changes impacting the application.

+   B, C, and D are incorrect. B is incorrect because patching and remediation are too granular. Yes, remediation was the direct cause, but not the shortfall allowed remediation to become a problem. C is incorrect because business continuity details with resuming operations after a significant outage. D is incorrect because system administration wasn’t at fault but rather likely what helped the problem.
