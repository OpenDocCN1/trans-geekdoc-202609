Cacti, also a monitoring tool, is a free front end to a network logging and graphic tool based on MRTG. Unlike Bro, Cacti just monitors and makes nice graphs—again, far from functioning as SIEM. The packet capture and analysis tool tcpdump excels at capturing packets, but not at responding to or reporting findings in a particularly useful way.
