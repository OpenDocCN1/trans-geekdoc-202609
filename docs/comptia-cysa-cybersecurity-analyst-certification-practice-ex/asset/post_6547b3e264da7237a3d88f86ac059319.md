The steps on how to get a baseline are not covered in detail in this chapter, because it’s assumed that the baseline for your environment is already established and understood. This should be done before an incident occurs in order for you to have a point of comparison. So, on the assumption you have something to compare it to, you can diagnose your incident by what’s changed in your environment.

- C. Alert the CIO to the traffic spike.
- D. Sample the traffic using a packet analyzer.

- A. Processor consumption

- C. Memory consumption
- D. Network bandwidth consumption
