Of course, there are no guarantees or absolute job security. Adding a few letters to your name on your resume doesn't guarantee you are a rock star. # # What to Expect from the CySA+ Exam

To reap that sweet, professional karma we talked about, you must take the CySA+ exam. For bewarned: it isn't easy. (Why should it be?) Let's talk about what to expect when you take the CySA+ exam.

+   Bullet points:
* It is composed of up to 85 questions. (You might get fewer, doubtful.)
* The format is multiple choice, plus a “performance-based” questions (a few comments about those later).
* You'll have 165 minutes to complete the exam. (That's 2 hours and 45 minutes.)
* To pass, you need to score a 750 or higher, out of 900.

Some quick math shows you have roughly two minutes per question. Anyone who has ever taken such an exam knows that some questions will take you five seconds while others plague you for five minutes.

One warning I will give: do not be surprised by the performance-based questions if they come early. Do not panic about them taking all your time. After you handle the performance-based questions, the standard multiple-choice questions will seem to go by quickly.

Lastly, because the exam delivers only up to 85 questions, it is not possible to be questioned on every topic and subtopic in the Exam Objectives map. In this book, however, you can be assured that at least one question will touch each and every bullet point on the Objective map, to help you assess your knowledge and prepare for the exam.
