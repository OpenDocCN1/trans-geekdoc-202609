+   13. Which of the following provides a similar benefit to a chain of custody form?

+   A. Drive adapter

+   B. Tamper-proof seal

+   C. Cryptography tool

+   D. Drive and media cables

+   B is correct. Tamper-proof seals provide protection against unauthorized tampering. Similarly, a chain of custody form helps maintain the integrity of evidence by ensuring accountability.

+   A, C, and D are incorrect. A is incorrect because having a variety of drive adapters helps ensure a physical connection with the variety of drives and storage devices you might encounter. C is incorrect because cryptography tools can provide confidentiality through encryption. Although in this turn helps protect integrity, it's not a direct, analogous benefit. D is incorrect because drive and media cables provide a physical connection, similar to drive adapters.
