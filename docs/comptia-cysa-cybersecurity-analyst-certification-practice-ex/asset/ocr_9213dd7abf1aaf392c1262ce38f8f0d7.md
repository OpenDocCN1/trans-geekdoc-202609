+   3. Guidelines are to be drawn prior to any penetration testing can begin, and they determine the exact nature and scope of the testing. What are these guidelines called?

+   B. ROE (rules of engagement)

+   C. Scope and Invoicing document

+   D. Discovery

+   B is correct. Having the rules of engagement (ROE) is an absolute necessity before beginning any phase of a penetration test. The ROE determine what is within bounds, out of bounds, with whom to communicate, and so on. Most importantly, the approved rules of engagement function as your “get out of jail free” card, containing that without them, the penetration test is illegal.

+   A, C, and D are incorrect. A is incorrect because TTP does not reference a standard document, but instead refers to the general techniques and processes of conducting the test.
