Using the following scenario to answer Questions 5–7:
Tywin is the CEO of a factory where most employees are paid hourly. Employees must submit on all their expected hours for the week by Tuesday to get paid on Friday. Jon, the factory manager, shares with Tywin every week he hears the complaints from employees: “How do I know by Tuesday that my schedule won’t change by Friday?” Understanding the need for a quicker way to sum hours, Tywin remembers his neighbor’s son, Sam, has been taking development courses at a university for a few semesters. Therefore, Tywin offers Sam an opportunity to help.

After a few weeks of development, Sam launches a web application. Employees are told to authenticate and enter their hours using this application. The application submits the hours to the Finance department and tells employees how much they can get paid on Friday. Hours can be submitted as late as Thursday night. Jon reports to Tywin that employees are happy with the web application and it is to be the subject of a constant buzz around the break room.
