The BurstSampler provides more sophisticated limiting indicating how many messages are allowed per period. Additionally, a nextSampler can be used to indicate what sampler has to be invoked when the limit is reached. In 16.15, the sampler defines a burst of two messages every five seconds and then one sample every 90000000 iterations. After the first two entries, the burst limit is reached. Looking at the timestamp, we see that no more consecutive messages will be printed after five seconds.
