The nine activation functions compared in this study are the eight briefly mentioned in the Introduction (ReLU [16], Leaky ReLU [29], ELU [19], SELU [20], PReLU [14], APLU [21], SReLU [30], and MeLU [25]) and the method proposed here, GaLU.
