This can be considered as the *feature selection* step in machine learning terminology.

Next, multinomial logistic regression [54] was used to develop three different models based on the above features as the independent variables and the laterality labels—right mTLE, left mTLE, bilateral mTLE, and control—as the dependent variables. These models include:

+   1. Right vs. left mTLE,
2. Unilateral vs. bilateral mTLE, and
3. Right mTLE vs. Left mTLE vs. Bilateral mTLE vs. Control.
