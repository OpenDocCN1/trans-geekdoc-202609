# 2.1  BACKGROUND

Dermatology is a subject that relies on morphological features and the majority of diagnoses are based on visual pattern recognition. At present, the skin imaging technology is represented by dermoscopy, very high-frequency ultrasound, and reflectance confocal microscopy. Each method of skin imaging equipment has its own advantages and limitations. Dermatologists need to choose different imaging methods according to different conditions of skin lesions. The skin imaging technology has become a vitally important tool for clinical diagnosis of skin diseases
