# # Geeta Rani

Prof. Geeta Rani received Ph.D. in computer engineering from the University of Delhi, Delhi, India in 2017. She has teaching experience of more than 15 years. She is the chief editor of one book, has published more than 30 research articles, and more than 6 patents. She has acquired more than 17 copyrights on the software applications and inventions. Her research interests include machine learning, artificial intelligence, image processing and data analytics. Prof. Geeta has been a recipient of the ‘Women Scientist-IPR Fellowship’ in 2018 from the Department of Science and Technology, India.

# # Vijaypal Singh Dhaka

Prof. Vijaypal Singh Dhaka received Ph.D. in computer engineering from the Bhimrao Ambedkar University, Agra, India, in 2010. He has more than 18 years of industry and academic experience at various organizations of repute. He has more than 80 publications in journals of great repute and guided 11 research scholars to earn PhD. He has published more than 6 patents and acquired more than 15 copyrights on the software applications and inventions. He received “World Eminence Awards 2017” for leading research contribution in ICT for the Year 2016, at WS-4 in London on 15th Feb 2017.

# # Pradeep Kumar Tiwari

Prof. Pradeep Kumar Tiwari is an associate professor at Dr. Vishwanath Karad MIT World Peace University Pune. He has teaching experience of more than 10 years. He worked as controller of examination for an online education program at Manipal University Jaipur. He has published more than 60 articles in journals and conferences of repute. His research area is artificial intelligence and cloud computing.
