# Disadvantages

+   - As the magnification increases, the depth of focus reduces, potentially causing
+   the image to become out of focus.
+   - Capturing the best retina image while holding the lens by hand is challenging.
+   - Taking fundus photographs on a slit-lamp without adapters can be very difficult.

The following Table 2 gives the details of various imaging equipment.

| Eye Imaging Equipment | Machine/Lenses/System Required | Features | Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Digital retinal cameras | Topcon and Zeiss | Macular degeneration, choroid disturbances, and diabetic retinopathy, lesions, hard and soft exudates. | FFA, color fundus photography |
| OCT | OCT Zeiss Stratus, Cirrus | Age Macular Degeneration, Diabetic Retinopathy | Allows for cross-sectional imaging. Macular and optic nerve head imaging is the common application. |
| Scanning laser ophthalmoscope | HRA2 from Heidelberg Engineering | Structural changes in Glaucoma, Dense Cataract | For fundus autofluorescence, FFA, and ICG angiography. |
| Photo slit-lamp microscopy | Natural crystalline lens | Cornea and anterior eye assessment | The cornea, iris, conjunctiva, and lens are examples of anterior eye structures that can be photographed. |
| Corneal topography | Orbsan 2 | Corneal diseases, Irregular astigmatism, Postoperative cataract extraction | The cornea's thickness, refractive power, and shape are all measured. |
| Optic nerve head analyzer | HRT3, Heidelberg Engineering | Glaucoma | For accurate imaging of the optic nerve head. People suffering from glaucoma or those suspected of having glaucoma should use this medication. |
| External photography | digital Nikon SLR | Eye external structures like cornea, lashes, conjunctiva, eyelids, and sclera | Eyelids and other facial structures can be photographed using this technique. |
| Rostock corneal module | HRT | Keratitis, Dry Eye | All corneal layers are magnified at high magnification. Allows for the counting of endothelial cells. |
