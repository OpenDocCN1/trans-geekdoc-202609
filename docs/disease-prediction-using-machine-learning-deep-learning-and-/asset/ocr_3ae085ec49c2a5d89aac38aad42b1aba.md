# Functional Magnetic Resonance Imaging (fMRI)

The fMRI senses changes in the flow of blood that are connected to the neuronal activities in the brain. So, it aids in mapping behaviors to the corresponding regions of the brain, a process known as source localization. Any activity involving the brain causes an increase in blood flow to the area (Fig. 6). The BOLD contrast method is sensitive to hemodynamic responses. Differences in deoxyhemoglobin concentration in brain tissue can be seen as BOLD contrast intensities [16]. It has a low temporal resolution, but it has a high spatial resolution and can collect data from the deep parts of the brain that magnetic or electrical methods cannot.
