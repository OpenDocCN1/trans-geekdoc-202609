# Age-Related Macular Degeneration

Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD) is a condition that weakens the retina and causes a blur in the central vision, significantly impacting daily activities such as driving and reading. AMD primarily affects individuals aged 50 years or older, and it specifically targets the macula, a crucial part of the eye [5]. AMD can be classified into two types: dry AMD and wet AMD. Dry AMD occurs when areas of the macula thin out with age, and small clumps of a protein called drusen accumulate (this form is not curable). In contrast, wet AMD involves the growth of abnormal blood vessels under the macula, which leads to fluid leakage through the retina [32].
