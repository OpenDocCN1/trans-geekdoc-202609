Glaucoma can be classified into several types, including primary open-angle glaucoma, normal-tension glaucoma, closed-angle glaucoma, congenital glaucoma, and secondary glaucoma [39]. A brief overview of these glaucoma types is provided to enhance understanding.

According to the National Eye Institute (NEI) [40], open-angle glaucoma [41] is the most prevalent type of glaucoma. It is a slow process in which the eye fails to drain fluid properly, leading to an increase in eye pressure and subsequent damage to the optic nerve. At its initial stages, open-angle glaucoma is painless and does not cause noticeable changes in vision [42].
