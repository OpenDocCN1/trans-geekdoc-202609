The authors [3] used data from 7031 studies over the age of 65 gathered between 2001 and 2005 from the Korean National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (KNHANES) to develop a deep neural network (DNN) to predict dementia using health care and medical service data. Component-specific analysis (PCA) with small/large scale measurements is the proposed approach for anticipating and extracting significant background elements. They compared DNN/scaled PCA, the suggested technique, against five well-known machine learning algorithms. The proposed method achieves an AUC of 85.5 %, which is higher than that of existing algorithms.
