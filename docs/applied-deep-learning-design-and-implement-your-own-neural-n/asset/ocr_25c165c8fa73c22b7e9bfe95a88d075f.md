Standard Siamese CNN: In this, for similarity measurement of two images L2 distance metric is used.

L2_d(x, y) = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^{N} (x_i - y_i)^2}

We train CNN to make this small for similar or positive pairs, and for finding dissimilar of negative pairs, this L2 distance norm is large.
