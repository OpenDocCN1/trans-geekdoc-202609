# # Dual GAN

Image-to-image translations come under the computer vision and image processing field, where images from one domain are mapped into another domain. Image-to-image translations have a scope in numerous applications such as black and white to colored transfer, stylization, image segmentation, season transformations, sketches-to-photos, and so on. The previously-discussed models in this domain, that is, conditional GAN, have shown improvements in this field, but it requires labeled data on which the training of the model is conditioned. Now, the main issue with conditional GAN is the non-availability of labeled data and the difficulty in converting huge datasets with millions of images to labeled datasets. To overcome this need for labeled data for GAN training, Zili proposed a novel approach, Dual GAN, which is an unsupervised dual-learning framework inspired by the Dual-learning approach in natural language translations. In this dual-learning approach for natural language translation, two different language translators are trained simultaneously, for example, Chinese-to-Russian and Russian-to-Chinese. In this arrangement, one translation becomes the primal task, and the reverse translation becomes the dual-task. Now, while working in pairs under closed-loop forces both translators to learn through reinforcement learning.
