But how does the computer see the preceding image? To a computer, this image is actually an array of pixel-like any other image. And the pixels are in the form of numerical values, which represent different shades of different colors. The challenge here was to create or train the computer machines such that they can read and understand these digital images the same way humans do. This process of training the computer systems such that they can observe and understand the images is known as Computer Vision.
