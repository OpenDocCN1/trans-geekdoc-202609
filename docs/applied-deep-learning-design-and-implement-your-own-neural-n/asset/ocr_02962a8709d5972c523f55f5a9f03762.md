To check the performance of the two generators, there are two discriminators: DX and DY, that give their feedback on the generated images in the respective domains. Therefore, in addition to the cycle-consistency loss, the adversarial loss is still there to make sure that, along with the correct translations, the images are realistic too.

Table 8.7 shows the inputs, outputs, and aims of generator and discriminator networks in cycle-GAN:

| Cycle GAN | Input | Output | Aim |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Generator (GXY ) | Either a real image or generated image by (GY X) in domain X | New translated samples from X to Y | Realistic new samples in domain Y |
| Generator (GY X ) | Either a real image or generated image by (GXY ) in domain Y | New generated samples from Y to X | Realistic new samples in domain X |
| Discriminator (DX ) | Real and generated samples in domain X | Probability score to identifying real or fake samples in domain X | Correctly identify between real or fake samples in domain X |
| Discriminator (DY ) | Real and generated samples in domain Y | Probability score to identifying real or fake samples in domain Y | Correctly identify between real or fake samples in domain Y |
