In a feed-forward neural network, the transfer of data or information is one-sided only, that is, forward direction by transmitting through the input nodes, passing from the hidden nodes, and generating output at the output nodes or neurons.

They are also known as multi-layer perceptrons. **Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)** and **Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)** are special cases of feed-forward neural networks.
