## Neural network 1 (NN1)

The first neural network (NN1) is represented as follows:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
FM(NN1) & i_1 & i_2 & i_3 \\
o_1 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\
o_2 & 0 & 1 & 0 \\
o_3 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\
- & - & - & - \\
o_4 & 0 & 0 & 1 \\
o_5 & 1 & 0 & 0 \\
o_6 & 0 & 1 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
i_1 \\
i_2 \\
i_3
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 \\
0 \\
0 \\
- \\
0 \\
1 \\
0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

We will make a matrix as a food matrix that selects the next day drink on the basis of the previous day drink. The structure of this food matrix (FM) is the combination of the identity matrix

| | IM | i1 | i2 | i3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | o1 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| | o2 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| | o3 | 0 | 0 | 1 |

and permutation matrix

| | PM | i1 | i2 | i3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | o4 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| | o5 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| | o6 | 0 | 1 | 0 |

It is of size [6 x 3]. The first three rows of this food matrix is an identity matrix, and the rest are representing the permutation matrix. It will get multiplied with the input vector as a current-day drink of size [3x1], for e.g., hot coffee \(\begin{bmatrix} i_1 \\ i_2 \\ i_3 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\). It gives rise to [6x1] vector representing same drink (hot coffee) \(\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\) or next-day drink (Lime Juice) \(\begin{bmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{bmatrix}\) according to the round-robin schedule. The permutation matrix transforms current-day food into next-day food.

As per problem 3 of Table 6.1, the weather will also play a key factor in deciding whether same-day food will be served or next-day food. So in this RNN, the output will be dependent upon the previous day food (history) as well as the input vector as weather; that is, if it is sunny, then the same day (food | juice) will be served, and if it is rainy then the next day food will be served on the basis of round robin schedule. So for taking weather as input, we have to build a weather matrix (WM). If the input weather is sunny \(\begin{bmatrix} i_1 & 1 \\ i_2 & 0 \end{bmatrix}\) then same day food(drink) will be served else if it is rainy \(\begin{bmatrix} i_1 & 0 \\ i_2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\) then next day food will be served, as shown in table 6.2. There are two neural networks as NN1(FM) and NN2 (WM).
