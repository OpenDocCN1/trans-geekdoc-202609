## Training of GAN

After defining the complete frame for GAN, the next step is the training of the model.
GAN training basically consists of two parts: the first is training the discriminator,
and the second is training the generator. The model is trained on 10 and 20 epochs to
see the effects of epochs on the learning of the GAN. In the training of discriminator,
the aim is to maximize the objective function, and it is further a two-step process.
In the first step, the discriminator is trained on a batch of real samples from the
training set. In the forward pass, the loss, and in the backward pass, the gradient
is accumulated. Similarly, in the second step, loss and gradient are accumulated,
but this time for a batch of fake samples with the current generator. Now with the
gradient accumulated from both batches, the Adam optimizer is called for updating
the discriminator.

In the training of the generator, the goal is to minimize the objective function in
order to generate better fakes. The generator generates the new sample, which is
given to the discriminator for classification as real or fake, or we can say that aim is
to maximize log(D(G(z))). Based on the output, the optimizer is used to update the
generator's parameters.

```
# Training Loop
# Lists to keep track of progress
img_list = []
G_losses = []
D_losses = []
iters = 0
print("Starting Training Loop...")
# For each epoch
for epoch in range(epochs):
    # For each batch in the dataloader
    for i, data in enumerate(dataloader, 0):
        ############################################
        # (1) Update D network: maximize log(D(x)) + log(1 - D(G(z)))
        ############################################
        ## Train with all-real batch
        D_network.zero_grad()
        # Format batch
        real_cpu = data[0].to(device)
        b_size = real_cpu.size(0)
        label = torch.full((b_size,), real_label, device=device, dtype=torch.float)
        # Forward pass real batch through D
        output = D_network(real_cpu).view(-1)
        # Calculate loss on all-real batch
        errD_real = loss_function(output, label)
        # Calculate gradients for D in backward pass
        errD_real.backward()
        D_x = output.mean().item()
```
