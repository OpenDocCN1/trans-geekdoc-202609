# # Generative Adversarial Networks

# ## Introduction

**Generative adversarial networks (GAN)** are a revolutionary and innovative idea that has been researched for the past few years. Researchers have applied GANs to various domains, such as computer vision, time series synthesis, natural language processing, video generation, semantic segmentation, and so on, and achieved significant success in the respective area. GANs showed amazing results in computer vision, such as plausible image generation, facial attribute manipulations, image-to-image translations, and so on.
