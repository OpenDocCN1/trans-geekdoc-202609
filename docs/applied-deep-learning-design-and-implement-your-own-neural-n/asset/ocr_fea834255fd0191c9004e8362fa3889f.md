# Figure 4.175: Different types of model fitting

Model fitting is very important in deep learning. If the model is not correctly fitted, then it may not provide correct or accurate outputs on the test data.

Underfitting happens when a model can neither fit the training data nor work on the new test data. Under-fitting model even performs poorly on the training data itself. In figure 4.70, the under-fitting model is represented by a straight line, but it cannot even distinguish between the two types of classes.
