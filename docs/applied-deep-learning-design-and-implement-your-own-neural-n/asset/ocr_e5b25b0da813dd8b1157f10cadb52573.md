# Clustering applications

+   • **Planning of city**: Clustering can be used to make groups or clusters of houses and also to study the values of different groups depending on geographical and other related factors.
+   • **Libraries**: Clustering can be used to cluster different types of books on the basis of data and topics.
+   • **Biology**: Clustering can also be used in the classification of different types of flora and fauna.
+   • **Marketing**: Clustering can be used to illustrate and determine consumer sections for marketing purposes.
+   • **Insurance**: Clustering can be used to recognize the clients and their plans and recognize the deceptions.
+   • **Earthquake studies**: By means of learning the areas which are affected by earthquakes, we can find out the hazardous zones.

These are the most common applications of clustering algorithms.
