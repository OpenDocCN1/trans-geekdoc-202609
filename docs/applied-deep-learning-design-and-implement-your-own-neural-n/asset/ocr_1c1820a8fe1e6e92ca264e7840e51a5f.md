Intuition of Neural Networks

# ## Introduction

A neural network can be defined as a network consisting of a cluster of nodes or neurons. A neural network can be either biological, consisting of biological neurons, or artificial neural networks for solving problems related to Artificial Intelligence (AI). This chapter covers the basic concepts related to AI, such as neurons, neural networks, and feed-forward and backpropagation neural networks. In this chapter, we briefly describe the basic concepts of activation function along with their types with computational graphs techniques.
