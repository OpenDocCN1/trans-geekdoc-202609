From the preceding assumption, we can calculate the probability of getting four on the faces of a weighted pair of dice as follows:

$$
\frac{1}{8} \times \frac{1}{8} = \frac{1}{64}
$$

However, for the case of weighted dice, we cannot find the probability of other non-trivial elements because the probability of other faces is not known. If you observe the preceding two assumptions, the first assumption belongs to a statistical assumption because, in this case, by using the assumption, we can determine the probability of occurrence of any event.

For the second assumption case, it does not belong to the statistical assumption because here, we cannot determine the probability of the event by using the assumption.

# # Hyper planes

In geometry, a hyperplane can be defined as a subspace whose dimension of its ambient space is one more than that of its subspace. So accordingly, the hyperplane of the space is two-dimensional if the dimension of the corresponding space is three. The hyperplane can be used in any type of general space in which the concept of subspace dimensions is present.
