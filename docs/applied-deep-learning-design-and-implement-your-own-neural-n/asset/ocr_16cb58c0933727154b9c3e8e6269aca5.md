Output_{Size} with padding = \frac{227 + (2 \times 0) - 11}{4} + 1 = \frac{227 - 11}{4} + 1 = 55

# ZFNet

ZFNet evolved just after AlexNet in 2013. It was proposed by Zeiler and Fergus. It is similar to AlexNet instead of an 11*11 filter, and its filter size is 7*7 with stride 2 during convolution Layer 1. Furthermore, during convolutional Layers 3, 4, and 5, ZFNet uses 512, 1024, and 512 filters instead of 384, 384, and 256 in AlexNet.
