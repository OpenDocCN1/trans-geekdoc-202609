# Vanilla GAN

Vanilla GAN is the simplest type among the various GAN models. The previously-discussed introductory GAN architecture is basically a vanilla GAN model. In this model, both the discriminator and the generator use multi-layer perceptrons, competing against each other using cross-entropy loss function for training. All the other necessary information has already been discussed in the basics of GAN.

# Deep convolutional GAN

DCGAN is one of the most popular and successful GAN architecture, and that is why it is the first choice for the novice in this area, to begin with, and start a project. The DCGAN model was proposed to overcome some of the issues faced in the training of a GAN model. We have discussed the challenges faced in GAN; one of the main concerns is to have stable training. So, the authors suggested a few strategies for successfully training a high-quality generator. These are the main techniques the authors used in their model.
