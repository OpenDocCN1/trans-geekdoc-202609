# Identity mapping

Identity mapping ensures that the output of any multi-layer neural network is equal to its input. The neural network having identity mapping is also known as a replicator. Identity mapping simplifies the process of unsupervised learning, and also, the hidden layers of such a type of neural network can be used in data compression and detection of features.
