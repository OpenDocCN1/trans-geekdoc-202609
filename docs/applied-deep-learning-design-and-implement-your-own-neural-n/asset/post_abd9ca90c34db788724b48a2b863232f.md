
Differentiating, we get the following:

\(\nabla c(t-1) = \frac{\nabla E}{\nabla c(t-1)} = \Delta c(t) \times f(t)\)

Thus,

\(\nabla c(t-1) = \Delta c(t) \times f(t)\)

So, using the preceding equations, we can make learning all the gates of the cell unit of LSTM. Now, let us take a numerical example to understand the LSTM properly.

Now, we have got the output from the forward propagation. Now, let us discuss backpropagation for the same example. Now, let us calculate backward propagation for t = 1 timestamp.

To calculate the backward propagation. Let us calculate the mean square error first to find out the difference between the actual output and the predicted output.

MSE is given as follows:

$$ E = \frac{(x - x')^2}{2} $$

When we do the derivation of the preceding equation concerning x, we get the following:

$$ \delta_x E = x - x' $$

Now, suppose the actual value of the output of forward propagation at the time \( t = 0 \) is \( 1.25 \), that is, \( x^t = 1.25 \) and we have calculated it as \( 0.77 \).

Therefore,

\(\delta_x E = 0.77 - 1.25\)
\(\delta_x E = -0.48\)

And also change in output at \( t = 1 \) is \( 0 \) as there are no more future values of \( t \). So we can write

\(\delta Out(t = 1) = -0.48 + 0\)
\(\delta Out(t = 1) = -0.48\)

The change in the cell state is given as follows:

\(\delta State(t = 1) = \delta Out(t = 1) * o(1) * \left(1 - \tanh^2(state(t = 1))\right) + \delta(state = 2) * f(2)\)
\(\delta State(t = 1) = -0.48 * 0.85 * \left(1 - \tanh^2(1.52)\right) + 0 * 0\)
\(\delta State(t = 1) = -0.070\)

Now, \(\delta a(t = 1) = \delta State(t = 1) * i(1) * (1 - (a(1))^2\)

\(\delta a(t = 1) = -0.070 * 0.9 * (1 - (0.85)^2)\)

Therefore, \(\delta a(t = 1) = -0.02\)

Also, \(\delta i(t = 1) = \delta State(t = 1) * a(1) * i(1) * (1 - i(1))\)

\(\delta i(t = 1) = -0.070 * 0.85 * 0.9 * (1 - 0.9)\)

Therefore, \(\delta i(t = 1) = -0.001\)

Also, \(\delta f(t = 1) = \delta State(t = 1) * \delta State(t = 0) * f(1) * (1 - f(1))\)

\(\delta f(t = 1) = -0.070 * 0.78 * 0.88 * (1 - 0.88)\)

Therefore, \(\delta f(t = 1) = -0.006\)

And

\(\delta o(t = 1) = \delta out(t = 1) * \tanh(State(t = 1)) * o(1) * (1 - o(1))\)
\(\delta o(t = 1) = -0.48 * \tanh(1.52) * 0.85 * (1 - 0.85)\)

Therefore, \(\delta o(t = 1) = -0.05\)

Now, we know that

\(\delta x(t=1) = W^t * \delta gates(t=1)\)

Therefore,

\(\delta x(t=1) = \begin{bmatrix} 0.45 & 0.9 & 0.7 & 0.6 \\ 0.25 & 0.8 & 0.45 & 0.4 \end{bmatrix} * \begin{bmatrix} -0.002 \\ -0.001 \\ -0.006 \\ -0.05 \end{bmatrix}\)
\(\delta x(t=1) = \begin{bmatrix} -0.05 \\ -0.03 \end{bmatrix}\)

And also,

\(\delta out(0) = U^t * \delta gates(t=1)\)

\(\delta out(0) = [0.15 \quad 0.8 \quad 0.1 \quad 0.25] * \begin{bmatrix} -0.002 \\ -0.001 \\ -0.006 \\ -0.05 \end{bmatrix}\)

Therefore,

\(\delta out(0) = -0.02\)
