Now again, the IOU will be compared of this box with other boxes, and the boxes with high IOU will be suppressed.

Figure 5.37: Suppression of the boxes

Now, these steps are repeated again and again till the final bounding box is determined, as shown in figure 5.38:

This process is known as the non-max suppression technique.
