Then:

$$
\frac{\partial e}{\partial a} = \frac{\partial e}{\partial c} * \frac{\partial c}{\partial a}
$$
$$
\frac{\partial c}{\partial a} = 2 * 1 = 2
$$

If the value of $a$ is increased by $1$, that is, $a = 3$, then:

$$
c = a + b = 3 + 1 = 4
$$
$$
e = c * d = 4 * 2 = 8
$$

As we can see, earlier the value of $e$ was equal to 6. But if we change $a$ by $1$, we get a change of $2$ units in $e$.
