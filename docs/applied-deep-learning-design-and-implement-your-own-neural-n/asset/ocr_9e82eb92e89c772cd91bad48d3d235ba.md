The idea behind Cycle GAN is to complete a cycle, where an image, say from domain X is translated to some other domain Y and then back again to domain X. Now, the cycle is complete here, and therefore in an ideal situation, the (original image) and the (reconstructed image) should be the same. But if both images are not the same, then a loss value is calculated on pixel level with the aim to minimize this loss and to generate as realistic images as possible, as shown in figure 8.19:
