In DCGAN, the aim was to train a generator with the help of a discriminator. Finally, in the end, we get the trained generator, and the discriminator is discarded. But here, the focus is primarily on the discriminator. The goal of the training here is to have a semi-supervised multi-class classifier that can give results close to a supervised classifier. The generator helps the discriminator by providing extra fake sample information for enhancing its accuracy. Finally, the generator is discarded at the end of the training.
