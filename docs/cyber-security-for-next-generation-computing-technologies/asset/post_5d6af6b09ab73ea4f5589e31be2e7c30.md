## 15.4.2.7 Blockchain in Phishing Emails

Phishing emails, which try to persuade recipients to click on a link, open malicious file, or send email information to someone unaware of what is happening, are to blame for more than 90% of successful hacks and data breaches on critical infrastructure [48]. A decentralized blockchain-powered system will be advantageous for developing future smart grids. Blockchain-based emails could be used as an unchanging transaction record to demonstrate an author's identity and precise sender or recipient, de-anonymizing the source of phishing. This would improve the current non-repudiation capabilities. Even if the blockchain is compromised, it isn't easy to alter the records that are stored there [40].

## 15.4.2.8 Blockchain in Supply Chain Management

The benefits of blockchain that are typically cited include traceability, transparency, and immutability according to Chang and Chen [48]. They note that supply chains have relied on trust mechanisms based on centralized authorities may need help in accepting this. According to the authors, private (permissioned) blockchain may be better suited to supply chain due to its higher degree of centralization as an entity with a clear boundary and constituency. According to Pournader et al. [49], security is one of the main problems that international supply chains face. They go on to say that the potential for blockchain to improve inventory, transparency, and trust makes it relevant. The results support that sharing important information safely and reliably is essential for a strong and effective supply chain. In their discussion of challenges to blockchain adoption in supply chain risk management, Etemadi and Van Gelder [50] note that this particular use case is still in its infancy. The technology's immaturity, lack of trust and ability are a few mentioned obstacles. They also discovered that blockchain system's reliance on and need for trust in outside sources, such as oracles, presents a significant challenge. According to the authors, blockchain offers decentralized data security.

## Blockchain Security Measures to Combat Cyber Crime

No one can copy, alter, or pirate smart contracts. Blockchain has many benefits, but it also has many drawbacks. A few issues and difficulties must be overcome, each described. Organizations face these difficulties when attempting to solve some issues:

- The adoption of blockchain technology promises long-term benefits, but its initial implementation is expensive. Software and industries need to integrate blockchain into their systems, which might have classically developed for a particular organizational structure might also need specialized hardware.
- Lack of Skilled/Intelligent People: In addition to the cost of technology, operating it also requires a high level of intelligence. There aren't enough engineers who can handle it, which causes small or even medium-sized businesses to have high demands.
- Integration with Legacy System: Moving into the integration phase requires entirely different software. This will also require significant human resources, including time, effort, and money. Due to the high cost, some businesses are hesitant to adopt blockchain.
- Energy Consumption: Energy consumption is another difficult problem that must be considered [47]. It takes much energy to compute these difficult mathematical problems (including side-chain verifying). Such energy use does not seem sustainable in a place where climate change is a major factor. Thus businesses are looking for more environmentally friendly practices.
- Lack of Public Awareness: A lack of public awareness is another significant issue. Organizations using distributed ledger technology are dampening awareness. Therefore, raising public awareness is necessary before mainstreaming blockchain. Bitcoin and blockchain are two distinct concepts, and people must understand their differences to utilize this technology fully.
- Security: Blockchain technology is created in a way that makes access poses serious problems for the government.
