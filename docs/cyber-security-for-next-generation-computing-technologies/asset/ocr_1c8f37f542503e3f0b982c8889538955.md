# 5.3.4 COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF APT 29, CONTI, AND LAPUSS$

APT29, Conti and LAPUSS$ are important cyber threat actors. While the first two are unofficially affiliated with the Russian government, LAPUSS claims to be completely independent of any state group. The scope of attack also differs among the three.
APT29 primarily to attack government organizations based in Europe and belonging to NATO. Conti focuses on big game companies, high-income companies whose business is vital. LAPUSS targets big tech companies such as Samsung and NVIDIA. LAPUSS conducts large by unsophisticated attacks. They buy initial access on the darknet, using previously identified vulnerabilities to steal data and demand ransom for their non-disclosure.
Conti works similarly to a state-run group, using zero-day vulnerabilities and seeking to discretely promote themselves on their victim's IS. They use double extortion to maximize their chances of collecting the ransom and also install backdoors to resell access to malicious operators.
Finally, APT 29 uses much more sophisticated methods to penetrate highly protected systems and reach a maximum number of strategic points. Their objective is more related to espionage and destabilization than profit, even if part of their activities are dedicated to profit.
