## 9.11.2 BIFLOW SIMULATION RESULTS

Various figures present simulation results regarding biflow where accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and two scenarios are discussed in detail. Figure 9.15 shows simulation results related to the true positive and false positive rates. Six different classifiers were utilized for comparison with the hybrid model.

In Figure 9.16, the detection results of attacks and no attacks are presented. Specifically, it can be observed that 27.4% of the monitored areas are reported to have attacks, while 72.6% do not.
