# 11.10.2.1 Financial Gain

Financial gain is one of the most common motivations behind cyber threats [163]. Cyber criminals can use various tactics to gain access to financial information or funds, including phishing schemes, malware, and ransomware attack. Phishing schemes sending phony emails that seem to be from a trustworthy source, like a bank or credit card service trick people into revealing their financial information. Malware is a software class that harms or interferes with computers and can be used to steal information or financial data. Malware, known as ransomware, encrypts a victim's computer or requires payment to unlock them [120]. Cyber criminals also use tactics such as identity theft, credit card fraud, and bank fraud to gain access to financial information or funds.
