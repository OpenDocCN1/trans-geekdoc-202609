Because cyber crime is the most worrying factor and cannot be solved immediately, blockchain technology is helping federal agencies stop these crimes against people and society. Major corporations and the financial sector are frequently the targets of hackers looking to steal money and data. A recent report [30] states, "An account belonging to the Bangladesh Bank, the country’s central bank, was held with the Federal Reserve." hacked, resulting in a theft of $81 million that was held with the Federal Reserve.

Recently, several institutions, such as Netflix and Twitter, have faced multiple data breaches. This includes incidents like the WannaCry ransomware attack and significant events as the $81 million theft from Bangladesh Bank. To stop these serious attacks against the nation’s economy, blockchain is a better option to stop these kinds of activities because it assists agencies with monitoring.

The European Union supported a project with a budget of €5 million to prevent hackers and criminals using blockchain technology. "three-year project" is called Tools for Investigating Transactions in Underground Markets and involves 15 members working together. Interpol and other law enforcement organizations are involved in the project, which aims to create tools for disclosing traits typical of criminal transactions [31].

Banks are currently developing their fraud prevention system based on distributed ledger technology (also known as blockchain technology), which has multiple databases and helps to prevent cyber attacks. Today’s banking companies and other business institutions use cyber threats and attacks seriously. As a result, they have begun researching hybrid systems that can operate on the local, regional, and global levels [32].

Unlike blockchain, the first type of system protects customers’ privacy by keeping a personal record of all previous transactions while avoiding disclosing the specifics of each transaction. Afterward, the system must update and store all verified records, senders, and authenticated credentials. This kind of risk management system is in use by banks today to stop cyber attacks [33].
