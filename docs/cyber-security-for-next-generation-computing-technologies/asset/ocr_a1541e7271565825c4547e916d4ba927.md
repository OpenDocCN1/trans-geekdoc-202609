# About the Editors

Dr. Imam Ullah Khan is a visiting researcher at King’s College London, UK. Dr. Khan was a lecturer at different universities in Pakistan, including the Center for Emerging Sciences Engineering & Technology (CESET), Islamabad, Abdul Wali Khan University, Garden and Timergara Campus, and University of Swat. He did his PhD in Electronics from the Department of Electronic Engineering, Isra University, Islamabad Campus, School of Engineering & Applied Sciences (SEAS). He completed his MS degree in Electronic Engineering at the Department of Electronic Engineering, Isra University, Islamabad Campus, He obtained his undergraduate degree in Bachelor of Computer Science from Abdul Wali Khan Mardan, Pakistan. In addition, his master’s thesis was published as a book, Route Optimization with Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) (Lambert Academic Publishing, 2017), in Germany, which is a research scholar and has published research papers at the international level. More interestingly, he recently introduced a novel on routing protocol E-ANTHOCNET in the area of flying ad networks. His research includes network security system, intrusion detection, prevention, cryptography, optimization techniques, WSN, IoT, UAV, mobile ad networks (MANETS), flying ad networks, and machine learning. He has served at international conferences as a program committee member, including the EAI International Conference on Future Intelligent Technologies, Islamabad, Pakistan, and the 2nd International Conference on Networks and Distributed Systems, Amman, Jordan, June 26–27, 2018, and is currently working on the same level at the International Workshop on Computer Intelligence and Cybersecurity (CIEN’21) that will be in conjunction with the 12th International Conference on Ambient Networks and Technologies (IEEEUSN 2021), which was co-organized in November 1–4, 2021, in Leuven, Belgium. Research papers (including book chapters, peer-reviewed journal articles, and peer-reviewed conference manuscripts) and three edited books.

Dr. Mariya Ouaissa is currently a Professor in Cybersecurity and Networks at Cadi Ayyad with a practitioner and academic experience. She obtained a PhD in 2019 in Computer Science and Networks, at the Laboratory of Modelling of Mathematics and Computer Science from ENSAM-Moulay University Meknes, Morocco. She is a Networks and Telecom Engineer, graduating in 2013 from the National School of Applied Sciences, Khourib, Morocco. She is a co-founder and IT consultant at the IT Support and Consulting Center. She worked for the School of Technology of Meknes as Visiting Professor from 2013 to 2021. She is a member of the International Association of Engineers and International Association of Online Engineering, and since 2021, she is an ACM Professional Member. She is Expert Reviewer with the Academic Exchange Information Centre (AEIC) and Brand Ambassador with Bentham Science.
