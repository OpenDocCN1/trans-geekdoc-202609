# 2.2.2 STRONG ARTIAL INTELLIGENCE

Robots designed to mimic intellectual processes are often made with “strong” AI. They consume something in a specific pattern to simulate the brain works. However, the strong AI philosophy does not clearly distinguish between AI, which is a subset of an approach that includes imitating actions of the neural network and human judgment. Strong machine learning is a way of thinking, not a method, for creating, a novel approach to AI where biological things are compared to AI. Also, it claims that a machine can be trained to start acting like a human being, to be intelligent in every sense of the word, and to be able to have perspectives, views, and other intellectual areas traditionally associated with humans, though intelligence cannot be accurately described by humans. Also, it is difficult to establish a clear definition of success in the development of excellent machine learning. However, weak AI tries to develop intelligence centered on a particular job that would be a set of procedures that could be divided into manageable processes and established levels [8].
