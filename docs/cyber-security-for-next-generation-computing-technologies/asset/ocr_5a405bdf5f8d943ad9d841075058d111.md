# 11.2 LITERATURE STUDY

To increase the effectiveness and safety of transportation, ITSs depend on related technologies like sensors, communication networks, and cutting-edge data analytics. However, with the increased use of these systems, the risk of cyber attacks against them has also increased. Cyber attacks can have severe consequences, including disruptions to transportation services, loss of revenue, and endangerment of human lives. This literature review examines the different types of cyber attacks against ITSs and the associated impacts.
