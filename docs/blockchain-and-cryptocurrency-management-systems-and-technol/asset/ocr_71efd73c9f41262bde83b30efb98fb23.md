# 5.2  ENERGY AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

Energy plays a vital role in human lives and the growth of a nation, and energy shortage hinders both human and economic development (HuoJie et al., 2013). Energy consumption and economic development have a strong positive correlation with one another, as shown in Figure 5.2. A high rate of growth and development calls for higher per capita consumption of energy, and higher consumption of energy calls for the development of any nation.

According to Energy Outlook, 2019, the demand for energy at a global level is expected to increase by 30% to 35% in 2040 due to an increase in the standard of living of people and reduction in poverty (Wen et al., 2021), mostly in developed and developing countries like India and China, which are densely populated (Chen et al., 2021). This again calls for serious actions against climate change as more than 60% of energy demand is met through fossil fuels, resulting in the emission of Greenhouse Gases.
