# 8.2 ETHEREUM IS A DIGITAL ASSET

It is intangible and sellable online where the main value proposition is derived from its programmability through the Ethereum network. This digital asset is speculative and highly volatile with high risk. Ethereum is considered the Triple-Point Asset, constituting a store of value, capital assets, and consumable assets.
