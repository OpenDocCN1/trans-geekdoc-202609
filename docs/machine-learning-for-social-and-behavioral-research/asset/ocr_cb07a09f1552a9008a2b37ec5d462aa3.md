+   - The idea of cross-validation (CV) in SEM goes back to the 1980s (e.g., Cudeck & Browne, 1983). Most recently, CV has seen very little application in SEM, which can be partly attributed to its increased computational complexity and that traditional fit indices perform well or better in selecting models (see Jacobucci et al., 2016 as well as Li & Jacobucci, 2020 for application with regularization). Additionally, it isn't straightforward as to which fit index should be paired. It may be most appropriate to use CV (or bootstrapping) with the chi-square, although it can also be paired with fit indices such as RMSE.
