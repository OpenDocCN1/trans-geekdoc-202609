not involve the responses of people, but instead books, newspapers,
or other media.

+   - Stop words. Words as "a," "and," or "the" that are typically
removed from text prior to analysis.

+   - Tokenization. The process of splitting responses into individual
units, such as by character, word, or sentence.

+   - Stemming. Reducing individual words based on common stems. For
example, turning "take" and "taking" into "tak."

# # 11.2 Data

We focus on the analysis of the Professor Ratings data to show how
handle text to answer real research questions. The data with student
evaluation on college professors were scraped from an online website-
forming the site requirement. A tutorial on how to scrape online data can
 found in Landers, Brusso, Cavanaugh, and Collmus (2016). The whole
daset includes 27,939 evaluations on a total of 999 professors.
