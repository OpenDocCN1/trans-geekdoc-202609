# 11.4.3 Data Matrix Representation

Tokenization identifies the units of analysis. When a series of texts, collectively called a *corpus*, is available, we organize the texts systematically for further analysis. As an example, we use the five sentences previously detailed. Therefore, the text corpus consists of five units. After tokenization, we can use a document-term matrix (DTM) to represent the information in the text corpus. Other data representation methods are also available. A DTM is a matrix where the rows represent the documents, comments, sentences, or other bodies of words. The columns are the tokens, terms, or words used in the document.
