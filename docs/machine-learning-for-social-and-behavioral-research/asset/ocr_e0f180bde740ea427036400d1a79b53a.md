In this regard, the integrated completed likelihood (ICL) criterion can be used (Biernacki et al., 2000). A larger ICL indicates a better model.

The ICL for the stochastic block model with 1 to 15 blocks for the WeChat chat is portrayed in Figure 12.10. From the plot, the model with 10 blocks gives the largest ICL. However, the model leads to four blocks with one, two, four, and five students, respectively. An examination of the ICL reveals that the ICL from the model with seven blocks is close to the model with 10 blocks. However, again, the model with seven blocks leads to a block with only four students. Therefore, we finally decide to use the model with six blocks with 37, 9, 33, 28, 31, and 27 students, respectively.
