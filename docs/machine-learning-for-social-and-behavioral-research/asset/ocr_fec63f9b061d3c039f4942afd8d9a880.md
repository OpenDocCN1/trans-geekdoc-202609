In the previous section, we generated a word cloud for the top words used in all the comments. Using the sentiment lexicon, we can further identify the positive and negative words or phrases. Figure 11.17 shows the word clouds for the positive words on the left and for the negative words on the right. From this, we can see that the top used positive words in the comments are “easy,” “great,” “good,” “interesting,” and so on. The popular negative words include “hard,” “difficult,” “boring,” “horrible,” and so on. An interesting observation is that positive words are more concentrated where few words are used more frequently, but there are many more negative individual words that are used in the comments.
