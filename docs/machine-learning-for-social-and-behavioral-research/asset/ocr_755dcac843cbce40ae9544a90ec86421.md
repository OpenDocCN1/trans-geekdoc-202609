# 3.6.1 Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) Curves

The ROC curve is designed to use a collection of continuous data to derive a cutoff (threshold) that maximizes a balance between two continuous metrics. In the context of classification, this involves plotting both sensitivity and specificity (the false positive rate or 1 - specificity in practice) across the range of possible cutoffs, e.g., what is the sensitivity and specificity for a cutoff of 0.1, 0.5, and all up to 0.99. It is important to note that in our above discussion of deriving optimal cutoffs, these plots only display one metric, while the ROC curve denotes the impact of cutoffs on two metrics, sensitivity and specificity.
