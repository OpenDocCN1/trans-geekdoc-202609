# 11.9 Summary

Although there are probably more qualitative text than quantitative data, text data are relatively underutilized in social and behavioral research. In this chapter, we showed how one can extract and utilize information from text data. Through text analysis, one can gain insight into what otherwise might not be possible. For example, in the teaching evaluation, information on the gender of the professors was immediately available. However, we can still identify the gender of the professors based on the use of gender words such as he/she him in the comments of the students. Although it is simple, this example illustrates how to conduct basic text analysis—identify and extract important information such as individual words and quantify them such using frequency of the words.

Preprocessing in text analysis is more critical than in quantitative data cleaning. Preprocessing should be viewed as an isolated process but should be an integral part of the text data analysis. Preprocessing typically involves a variety of steps such as extracting text from unstructured, removing sensitive and confidential information, handling special characters and abbreviations, spell checking and correction, removing stop words, and word stemming. Not all analyses need all the steps and some analyses might need additional preprocessing.

Tokenization effectively break down the text to any unit for further analysis. Although it is most common to tokenize individual words, many other ways of tokenization are possible and can be used according to the purpose of the analysis. Tokenization is often combined and repeated with preprocessing to best process the data into a format that is ready to be analyzed.

After tokenization, we typically express the text data in a documentmatrix. Such a matrix includes all the information we need to extract from the text. Each element in the matrix is a statistic regarding the frequency of a token as such. The matrix can be visualized or analyzed in a variety of ways. For example, we can visualize the word frequency through a bar plot or a word cloud. A network plot can be used to display the word associations.
