# 11.5.3 n-gram Analysis

Previously, we focused on the analysis of individual words. Compared to an individual word, a group of words can provide additional information under certain conditions. For example, see the comment below. It starts with “not easy”. If the comment was broken into individual words and one looks at “easy” only, the phrase “not easy at all” would have the opposite meaning. Similarly, in the same comment, “never” was used to modify “know”. Therefore, for such situations, it is better to treat the two words together.

> not easy at all, yes she is a good teacher and explained material well enough. BUT there is a LOT of material and you never
