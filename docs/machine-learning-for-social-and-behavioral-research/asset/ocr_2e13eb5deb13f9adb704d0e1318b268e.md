To further investigate the nature of the associations with suicidal thoughts, we present the partial dependence plots for both of these variables in Figure 6.16. In the right panel, we see the limitations of partial dependence plots for binary predictors. We are limited to assessing whether it is a positive or negative relationship between the predictor and outcome. In our example, no on ADWRWRTH is associated with a drop in the probability of indicating yes on suicidal thoughts, which is in line with what we expect theoretically. In the left panel of Figure 6.16 we see a nonlinear relationship between ADPBINTF and suicidal thoughts, indicating that the transitions between the first response options are associated with increases in the probability of endorsing suicidal thoughts, while there is little differentiation between the two highest response options (a lot and extremely). The largest increase occurs for the transition between some (the third response option) and a lot (the fourth response option), which, given the wording of the response options, makes sense.
