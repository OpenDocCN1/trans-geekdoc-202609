# About the Authors

Ross Jacobucci, PhD, is Assistant Professor in Quantitative Psychology at the University of Notre Dame. His research is in the development and application of machine learning for clinical research, specifically suicide and nonsuicidal self-injury. He is also an active developer of open-source software for the R statistical environment, with five packages that implement some form of machine learning. For more information, see his website, rjacobucci.com.

Kevin J. Grimm, PhD, is Professor of Psychology at Arizona State University. Dr. Grimm's research interests include multivariate methods for the analysis of change, multiple group and latent class models for understanding divergent developmental processes, nonlinearity in development, machine learning techniques for psychological data, and mathematics reading ability development. His research has been funded by the National Science Foundation, the National Institute of Education, and the Institute of Education Sciences. Dr. Grimm received the Early Career Research Award from the Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology in 2017. He is an author of Growth Modeling: Structural Equation and Multilevel Perspectives, which received the inaugural Barbara Byrne Book Award from the Society of Multivariate Experimental in 2021. While at the University of California, Davis, Dr. Grimm received the Award for Excellence in Service to Graduate Students from the Graduate Student Association and was nominated for the Excellence in Education Award from the Associated Students of the University of California, Davis.
