# 8.5 Can Regularization Improve Generalizability in SEM?

In Chapter 4, we looked at how the use of regularization, whether it be ridge, lasso, or any generalization, can reduce the complexity of our models and produce model estimates that result in higher predictive performance in holdout sets. Extending this to our prediction of latent Grit factors, we have two avenues where we can explore the utility of regularization. The first is using regularized regression in the second step with predicting factor scores, and the second option is building regularization directly into our SEM model estimation. This latter approach has been referred to as regularized SEM (Jacobucci, Grimm, & McArdle, 2016) or penalized likelihood for SEM (Huang et al., 2017).
