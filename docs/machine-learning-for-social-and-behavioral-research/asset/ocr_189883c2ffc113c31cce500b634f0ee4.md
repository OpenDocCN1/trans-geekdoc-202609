# 3.5.1 k-Fold CV

By keeping the original sample intact, one can perform numerous variants of CV, with the most common being k-fold CV. This involves repeatedly splitting the sample into $k$ partitions (i.e., 5 or 10), running the model on $k-1$ combined partitions of the data, and then testing the model on the single partition that is held out.
