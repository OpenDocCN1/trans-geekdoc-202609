For illustration, we include four covariates—GPA, BMI, gender, and whether a student smokes or not—in the latent space model. The parameter estimates for the model are given in Table 12.9. In estimating the model, the Markov chain Monte Carlo method is used. We also compared several models with different latent dimensions and found that the one with two latent dimensions fitted the WeChat data best.

The results from the latent space model are similar to those from the ERGM. However, the estimates should be interpreted conditionally on the latent distance of the students in the latent space. Or we can interpret the results as if the effect of the latent space is controlled. In this model, students with the same gender and who both smoke are more likely to be WeChat friends.
