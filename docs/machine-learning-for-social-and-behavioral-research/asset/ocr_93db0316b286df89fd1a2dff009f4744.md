Changes in the interpretation of coefficients and model fit can be tricky to understand when switching from one outcome to another. We do not provide an extensive coverage of this and insist on both continuous and categorical (dichotomous) outcomes. In most applied texts, logistic regression receives considerably less coverage than linear regression. However, in machine learning, categorical outcomes are very common, in forms such as completed treatment or diagnosis status. Below, we provide a brief overview of logistic regression, followed by multiple examples that use a continuous outcome, and we end the chapter with an example categorical analysis.
