One of the drawbacks of partial dependence plots is extrapolation of the expected curve to parts of x with few or no data points. The addition of rug marks help indicate where the majority of data points are for the x variable, and what points may have little support by actual data. This is demonstrated in Figure 6.10, where the majority of the math scores range from 20 to 40, and the nonlinear component at higher levels of math is partially due to few actual data points.
