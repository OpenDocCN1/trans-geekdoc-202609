A standard practice at this point is to use the results from EFA to inform a CFA model. The use of CFA allows researchers to better incorporate the measurement model into more complex forms of SEM, with the added benefit of simplifying the factor loading matrix by not estimating a subset of parameters. Practically, the use of CFA entails the placement of Os into Table 7.2, which incurs some degree of model misfit, but is often negligible when the factor loadings from EFA are small (i.e., < 2).
