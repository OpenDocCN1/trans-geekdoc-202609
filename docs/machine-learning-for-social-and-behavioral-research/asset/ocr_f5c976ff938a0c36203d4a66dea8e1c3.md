# 2-gram

As for the individual word analysis, the extremely common two-word phrases as "he is" and "if you" might not be very informative for analysis. We can also remove those frequently used phrases on stop words. In doing so, if either of the two words appears in the stop word list, we remove the two word phrase.

From Figure 11.11, the three most frequent two-word phrases are "great teacher," "very helpful," and "extra credit." Each appeared more than 1,000 times in the comments. Note that one can combine phrases such as "best professor" and "best teacher" through stemming.
