# # Understanding Correlation

Correlation measures the strength and direction of a linear relationship between two variables. It is quantified by the correlation coefficient, which ranges from -1 to +1: - A coefficient of +1 indicates a perfect positive correlation, where one variable increases, so does the other. - A coefficient of -1 indicates a perfect negative correlation, where one variable increases, the other decreases. - A coefficient of 0 implies no linear relationship between the variables.

Imagine you are studying the relationship between the number of hours studied and the scores achieved in an exam by students in a Vancouver high school. If the correlation coefficient is close to +1, it indicates that more hours of study are associated with higher scores.
