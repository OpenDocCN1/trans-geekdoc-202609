## 4.1. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
The way humans express information is entirely unstructured. Human beings generally communicate in words and sentences rather than tables, lists, or excel sheets. Since humans write and speak unstructured, the complexity of understanding it by the machines and computers is very high. Natural Language Processing (NLP) deals with this problem. It helps computers and machines understand the unstructured text and vocals to retrieve meaningful information from it. You can consider Natural Language Processing (NLP) as a sub-field of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that helps computers interact with humans.

### Applications of NLP
1. Speech Recognition
2. Language Translation
3. Automated Question Answering
4. Automated Text Summary Generation
5. Chatbot
6. Spelling and Grammar check
7. Autosuggestions and Autocomplete
8. Email Spam detector
