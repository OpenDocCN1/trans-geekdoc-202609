# 8.2 Background and Preliminaries

The meaning of the term artificial is either duplicate or substitute of original. Here, artificial artificial intelligence (AI) is a technique that makes computers observe, contemplate, analyze, and behave like a human brain. AI is the research of analyzing human cognitive intelligence (thinking, learning, selecting, and working) for solving a particular problem. The objective of AI is to imitate intelligence in computers, such as problem solving, reasoning, and learning. A significant improvement is achieved in many areas of AI. It has several applications in real life, which are achieved by using fication ways, i.e. intelligent, cognitive, smart, and predictive. Specifically it is used in applications where data and information are crucial, i.e. digital health, preventive maintenance, by data analysis, life science, robotics, gaming system, speech recognition, vision system, handwriting recognition, human facial recognition, and so on. The issue with AI is that it is a broad concept and often interchanged with the other disciplines of AI, i.e. machine learning, cognitive computing, robotics, deep learning, and so on. It includes various sub-domains. Figure 8.1 illustrate some of its major sub-domains that come in its broader umbrella.
