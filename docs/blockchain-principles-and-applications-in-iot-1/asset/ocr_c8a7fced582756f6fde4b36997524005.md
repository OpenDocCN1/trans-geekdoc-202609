# 4.1.3 Digital Twins

In the production industry, a digital dual is basically a digital duplicate of a physical element that is comparable to the real counterpart. The digital duplicate may be updated in real time when the physical item alters in reaction to its environment, thanks to advancements such as the Internet of Things, artificial intelligence, and machine learning, among many others. Engineers may make changes or verify upgrades on the digital twin rather of the actual physical product, saving time and money.
