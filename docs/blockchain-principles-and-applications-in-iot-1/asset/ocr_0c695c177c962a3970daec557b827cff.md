# 4.1.8 Smart Contact Lenses

Smart contact lenses, which can gather patient information and address certain vision issues, are now the subject of extensive research in the United States. The Triggerfish intelligent contact lens, invented by the Swiss manufacturer Sensimed, is an intelligent contact lens that involves a detector implanted in a smooth silicone contact lens that detects small changes in an eye's volume, which can be an indication of glaucoma. Using wireless technology, the gadget sends data from the sensor to an adhesive antenna wrapped around each eye.
