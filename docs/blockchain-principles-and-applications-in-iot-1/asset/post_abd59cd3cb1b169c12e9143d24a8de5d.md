## 4.1 Introduction

Smartphones play an important part in the IoT, owing to the fact that IoT applications can be managed via an application. In order to provide the optimal condition for you when you get just back from a job, you may interact with your intelligent thermostat using your smartphone, for example. 

What is another plus? 

That does not imply that your smart speaker will engage in a conversation with you about the most important aspects of yesterday's night's big game. However, if your refrigerator detects that you are close to a supermarket, it may give you a warning on your cell phone that you are on milk and eggs, prompting you to replenish your supplies.

This IoT is intended to make the process easier in a variety of ways. Here seem to be a few illustrations:

- A smart bathroom measures that operate in conjunction with your running to send mail preparation suggestions to your computer or desktop, allowing you to maintain your fitness goals.
