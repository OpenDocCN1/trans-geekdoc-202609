Blockchain is a tamper-resistant, distributed immutable ledger that has proven to be the technology to overcome the limitations of centralized applications and move towards a decentralized system. Bitcoin was one of the first successful practical of the blockchain technology, attempting to develop a pure peer-to-peer electronic cash system that wouldn't require a trusted third party and also prevent the double-spending problem [11–14]. Following the success of Bitcoin, a number of cryptocurrency-based technologies followed including Ethereum, a blockchain implementation attempting to build a technology on which state machine concepts may be built [15–17], and Ripple, introducing a new low latency consensus algorithm and several more implementations.
