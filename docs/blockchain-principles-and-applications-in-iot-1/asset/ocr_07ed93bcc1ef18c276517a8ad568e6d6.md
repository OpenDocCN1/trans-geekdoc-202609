# 1.2.1.1 Sensors/Devices

Sensors or gadgets in the collection of particularly little data from the environment. Other than sensing objects, many sensors on a device can be stacked to execute a variety of activities [3]. A phone, for example, has multiple sensors, such as a GPS, accelerometer, and it also performs other jobs, such as perceiving its surroundings.

# 1.2.1.2 Connectivity

The data is subsequently delivered to a cloud infrastructure, although a transport medium is required. Sensors can interact with the cloud via cellular networks, satellite networks, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and other technologies.

# 1.2.1.3 Processing of Information

Once the data is obtained, the software processes it before sending it to the cloud.
