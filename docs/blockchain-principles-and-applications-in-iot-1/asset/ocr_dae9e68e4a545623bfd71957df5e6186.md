The model is made robust in terms of security with the help of the application of blockchain technology (secured storage) in IoT devices. Although the code is tested and found working, it is not tested in a real environment (connect a biometric access control IoT device with proper admin panel) due to lack of resources and the ongoing pandemic.

In the future, we can check the scalability of the architecture, cost, complexity, and efficiency comparisons with other existing technologies. Though real implementation can be challenging at the present, it is a promising research area and further research will solve many drawbacks and reduce complexities and costs.
