## 8.1 Introduction

Artificial intelligence is a computationally intensive, intelligent, self-analytic method, in which some ways automatically analyzes and deduces conclusions without manual interventions and interpretations. Almost every human is being directly or indirectly served by these highly specialized systems in their daily lives. To make a few, Google search, Google map, text to speech/speech to text, smart devices, and virtual assistants are AI-based applications. One can say that no domain untouched by artificial intelligence now. It is the technology that can predict (based on supervised/unsupervised learning), translate (using natural language processing), navigate the location, and last not least identify the fraudulent transactions also (using rule-based algorithms with AI). With all the above-mentioned applications, machine learning, internet of Things, and blockchain are some techniques that are in use. Since the 1950s, artificial intelligence (AI) has come a long way and nowadays has been applied in almost every aspect of science, engineering, management, and economics. Among all computational sciences, the healthcare field is gaining higher penetration of AI. Additionally, vast developments in medical diagnosis, prognosis, drug design, treatment, and for many other functionalities are observed.

AI is transforming the practice of medication and its administration in entire treatments. Algorithms of AI perform analysis on received data that helps doctors diagnose patients more accurately, makes predictions about the patient's future health, and recommends better treatments.

Now, the point of concern here is health-related data on which ML will apply. That data should be secure (immutable) and available (sharable) at the time of need (emergency case also). For such cases, the patient has to maintain the record of their health-related issues (documents) in a ready state. For such purposes, blockchain is the easiest way to provide ease to the patient and doctor as well. Here, the role of ML in blockchain in medical industries will be described.

## 8.2 Background and Preliminaries

The meaning of the term artificial is either duplicate or substitute of original. Here, artificial artificial intelligence (AI) is a technique that makes computers observe, contemplate, analyze, and behave like a human brain. AI is the research of analyzing human cognitive intelligence (thinking, learning, selecting, and working) for solving a particular problem. The objective of AI is to imitate intelligence in computers, such as problem solving, reasoning, and learning. A significant improvement is achieved in many areas of AI. It has several applications in real life, which are achieved by using various ways, i.e. intelligent, cognitive, smart, and predictive. Specifically it is used in applications where data and information are crucial, i.e. digital health, preventive maintenance, by data analysis, life science, robotics, gaming system, speech recognition, vision system, handwriting recognition, human facial recognition, and so on. The issue with AI is that it is a broad concept and often interchanged with the other disciplines of AI, i.e. machine learning, cognitive computing, robotics, deep learning, and so on. It includes various sub-domains. Figure 8.1 illustrate some of its major sub-domains that come in its broader umbrella.

Many researchers are working on the amalgamation of AI with other technologies.
