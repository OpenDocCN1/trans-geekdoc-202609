# BUSINESS DATA SCIENCE

Combining Machine Learning and Economics to Optimize, Automate, and Accelerate Business Decisions

## MATT TADDY

### PRAISE FOR *Business Data Science*

Matt Taddy has written a thorough, thoughtful book on the statistics that underlie the use of big data. This is a fantastic resource, chock full of real applications, techniques and insight. Unlike most machine learning texts, this book provides methods of extracting reliable understanding from data, addressing the problem that correlation is not causation.

—PRESTON McCAFFEE, former Chief Economist and Corporate Vice President for Microsoft; VP Chief Economist at Yahoo; Research Director at Google; and Professor and Executive Officer for the California Institute of Technology

Drawing on his experience from his days as a star teacher at Chicago Booth and his work leading data science teams at Microsoft and Amazon, Matt Taddy has written a masterful book for MBAs and engineers who want to be able to participate in the data driven decision processes at modern companies. Artfully weaving together the key concepts from modern statistics, machine learning algorithms and social science causal models into a colorful tapestry, he has written a highly accessible text that will let anyone see what all the buzz is about. It is likely to become the standard text in this area.

—GUIDO IMBENS, Professor of Economics at the Stanford Graduate School of Business, coauthor of *Causal Inference for Statistics, Social, and Biomedical Sciences*

*Business Data Science* stands out in a crowded field of data-science textbooks. It brings together topics from historically distinct disciplines, in order to tackle a basic business reality: accurate predictions are not an end in themselves, but a means to taking high-quality actions. Taddy’s presentation is crisp and readable, without sacrificing precision. It reaches the current state of the art in prediction, causation, and decision-making, without requiring a strong data-science background. I recommend it to anyone interested in putting these ideas to practice.

—JON MCAULIFFE, Cofounder and Chief Investment Officer at The Voleon Group

Matt Taddy is one of the best teachers I have ever seen; that ability to convey important ideas clearly comes shining through in this book. No one is better at combining insights from computer science, economics and statistics to improve how businesses use their data. Everyone should read this book.

—JENS LUDWIG, McCormick Foundation Professor of Social Service Administration, Law and Public Policy, and director of the Crime Lab at the University of Chicago

The most exciting data science book I have read in some time: current, modern, accessible, rigorous—and awesome.

—DIRK EDDELBUETTTEL, Quant, R-package author, Clinical Professor of Statistics at University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign

This book should be required reading for anyone interested in how data analytics can drive better decision-making.

—EMILY OSTER, Professor of Economics at Brown University, author of *Expecting Better* and *Crisbsheet*

Matt Taddy has a beautiful way of understanding and explaining complex ideas in data science. It is great to see that his perspective is now available to everyone through this book.

—JESSE SHAIRPO, George S. and Nancy B. Parker Professor of Economics at Brown University

*Business Data Science* explains the mathematical theory and practical methods that are important for solving data problems in modern business. Matt Taddy is a world leader in data science, and one with a rare perspective. His book reflects both his rigorous scholarly outlook and the wisdom of real business experience.

—DAVID BLEI, Professor of Computer Science and Statistics at Columbia University
