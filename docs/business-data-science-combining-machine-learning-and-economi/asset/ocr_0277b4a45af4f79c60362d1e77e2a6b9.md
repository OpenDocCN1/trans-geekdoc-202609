+   12. Beware of Excel making changes to your data (e.g., by automatically formatting numbers or by trimming rows). Data that has been corrupted and resaved by Excel is a common source of frustration when working through the examples in this book.

+   13. In general, it pays to keep your code and data organized. I *strongly* recommend that you use a version-control platform to keep track of your changes, collaborate with others, and share your work (especially important if you hope to get a job in data science). I and many others use GitHub.
