How close is the bootstrap-estimated sampling distribution to the truth? It depends upon how well your observed sample represents the population distribution. In general, *dimension matters*: you can bootstrap a low-dimensional statistic (like $\overline{\boldsymbol{X}}$) but should not expect to use resampling to learn a sampling distribution in more than two to three dimensions.
