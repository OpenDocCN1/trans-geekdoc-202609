Let’s start with a top-down interpretation exercise. Looking at factor scores for individual politicians, we find that the extremes of PC1 correspond to ideological extremes. That is, the PC1 direction doesn’t just capture party membership but also represents the spectrum of liberal–conservative beliefs within each party. For example, the most negative $v_{1l}$ correspond to far-right (very conservative) Republicans, and the largest positive values are all liberal democrats.
