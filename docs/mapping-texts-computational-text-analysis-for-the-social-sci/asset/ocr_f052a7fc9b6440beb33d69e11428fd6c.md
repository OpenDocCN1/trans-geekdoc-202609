Broadly, we discussed ways of tagging sub-document units using rules, dictionaries, and pretrained language models. We also explored several pretrained language models, such as those provided by Stanford’s OpenNLP, udpipe ’s annotation models for English and French, and spacy ’s language models. Regardless of what is tagged—gender, parts of speech, named-entities—these models rely on hand-annotated corpora to learn features that best predict those tags. As such, we’re relying on annotators’ interpretations. We can also train our own models by annotating tokens in our own corpora.28 When doing so, we recommend building on the best practices in corpus linguistics (e.g., McEnery and Hardie 2011, Kennedy 2014).
