Before removing more words, however, let's see if the issue is our _k_. While the topics should always pass the eye test with a deep reading of the documents with high probabilities per topic, we can also use some diagnostic statistics to help us locate the right _k_. We follow Roberts and colleagues (Roberts et al. 2014) and recommend two complementary statistics: *semantic coherence* and *exclusivity*.

**Semantic coherence** quantifies the extent high-probability terms in a topic occur in the same documents (Mimno et al. 2011).40 Topics with high-probability terms rarely co-occurring might signal a noisy topic.

40 A smoothing number —usually, 1—is added to avoid the logarithm of zero. While coherence typically ranges from 0 and below, this smoothing number allows for positive coherence in the limiting case where co-occurrence equals occurrence.
