tesseract converted the image of printed text into characters which R can understand. What about a picture of handwritten documents? The principles are the same; however, there is much more variation with handwriting. This requires fine-tuning tesseract on a handwriting sample. This also entails building a **labeled** dataset, where researchers manually associate handwriting samples with correct words (Rakshit et al. 2010).5 Similarly, if researchers use tesseract with a language not pretrained, they can train the engine using hand-labeled samples (Clausner et al. 2020).
