# ## 4. Findings

# ### 4.1 Digitalization

Luxury firms are considerably challenged by the organizational complexity of their supply chains. For instance, they must deal with numerous third parties with different profiles, locations (often overseas) and processing methods, making the digitalization of supply chains complicated. Additionally, they struggle to trace the components used in their production processes, as expressed by Interviewee 2: “I use so many types of software and processes, (. . .) so many suppliers, how can I suddenly digitalize all of my supply chain?” (I2). According to Interviewee 8, the COVID-19 pandemic saw the supply chain and logistics practices of luxury firms transform from direct deliveries to customers to sales from online-only stores and wholesalers. This shift complicated luxury firms’ supply chain management by forcing them to deal with worldwide online orders for a wider range of goods. “E-commerce and data management are essential for an efficient supply chain and to transform the luxury sectors’ supply chain ecosystem” (8). Moreover, online sales have drastically changed the seasonality of sales, making sales planning more complex. “Before we had a clear seasonality of sales linked to each season, which was similar each year; e-commerce has destabilized the sales seasonality, now it’s tough to anticipate sales levels!” (I8).

# ### 4.2 Supply chain and traceability dimensions in luxury

The increase in the range of luxury products and their worldwide distribution amidst growing demand has directly impacted firms’ value chain, making it opaque. While luxury brands could previously control the manufacturing process from raw materials to finished products, nowadays, these brands must outsource their non-iconic products to meet growing demand for luxury goods. They also lack long-term relationships with their raw materials suppliers, reinforcing the opacity of their overall ecosystem. In the luxury field, control of brand image is critical. Therefore, all the respondents raised the importance of the brand’s reputation, embodied through high product quality.
