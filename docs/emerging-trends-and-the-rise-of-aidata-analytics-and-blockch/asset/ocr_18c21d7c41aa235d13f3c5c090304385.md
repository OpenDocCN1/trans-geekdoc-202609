DIC_M is the DIC value obtained from each model and DIC_B is the DIC value associated with the best model. These weights are then normalized to sum to one over the regarded models. The normalized weights of mvn distribution, mvt distribution and mvGED are approximately equal to 0, 0 and 1. As evident, the weights of mvGED are more significant than the weights of other multivariate distributions, so for further discussion, the estimated model results based on the mvGED are only considered.

The posterior medians’ values mean standard error, based on the 95% confidence intervals reported. The significant value of posterior densities of the skewness parameters (\(\tilde{\gamma}_b\)) confirms asymmetry for all series. Moreover, \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) are positive, and the sum of \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) is less than one for each variable, which fulfills the necessary condition for DCC models. The significance of these coefficients implies that volatilities and shocks on specified return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies affect the level of volatility in that period. Although the effect innovations on volatilities are not the same for every time series, it depends on the magnitude and significance of \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) coefficients. Consequently, there is much uncertainty and risk in the markets of the series. It can be concluded that short-term volatilities (the stability of innovations) are more stable than long-run volatilities (stability of previous volatilities). Besides, comparing estimated values of \(\beta\) parameter reveal that RAF passes through more volatility from the previous day to the current day than other returns.

The results of the estimated Bayesian ADCC-MGARCH (1, 1) model indicate that the \(\alpha\) and \(b\) are significant, and that \(a + b\) is less than one. This ensures that conditional correlation matrix and accordingly conditional variance-covariance matrix (\(H_t\)) is positive definite. The positive sign of \(a\) implies that the conditional correlation for the future period will increase due to innovation to time series. \(b\) coefficient value indicates the conditional correlation of the previous period on the conditional correlation of the current period. \(b\) is also very close to one, which implies that the conditional correlation of the previous period and current period are close to one another. \(b\) is larger than \(a\), which indicates that the correlation between returns is affected more by innovations than previous values of return.

The estimated sequence parameter \(\delta\) indicates that the GED is appropriate for the error term. The estimates in Table 7 show that a CCC model hypothesis that \(a = b = 0\) can be rejected based on the marginal posterior distributions of \(\alpha\) and explains the justification of employing the DCC model.

Also, Frank and Hesse (2009) mention that CCC is not usually very realistic, especially during the financial crisis when correlations can change rapidly, and there is high volatility in time series. Therefore, they recommend that the DCC-MGARCH model is superior to CCC as correlations are dynamic.
