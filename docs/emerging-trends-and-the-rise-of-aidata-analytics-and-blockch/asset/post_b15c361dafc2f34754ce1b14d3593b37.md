## Appendix 1

### Details about attention layer

The LSTM model with an attention layer can be visualized in Figure A1. The weight of the attention layer and the overall output after applying the attention layer can be calculated by equations (A1) and (A2) given below-

```
$$a_i = \frac{e^{h_i^T h_t}}{\sum_{j=1}^t e^{h_j^T h_t}}$$ (A1)

$$h_a = \sum_{i=1}^t a_i h_i$$ (A2)
```

where ai’s are the weight of attention layer, hi’s are the hidden state output of ith LSTM cell in the sequence, $h_i^T$ is the hidden state transpose, h is the last LSTM cell’s hidden state output of the sequence, and $h_a$ is the overall output after applying attention layer weights.
