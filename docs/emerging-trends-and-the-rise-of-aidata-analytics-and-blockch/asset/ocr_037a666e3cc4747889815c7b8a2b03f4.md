# 5.3 Data analysis results

The first phase of analysis revealed that blockchain technology emerged as an infrastructure for value exchange via cryptocurrencies. The NLP analysis of the 1,851 abstracts show that the main topics investigated in the early stages of blockchain inception relates to fund security and confidence in cryptocurrencies. Nevertheless, blockchain matured to become a generic secure medium for data communication. Topics such as healthcare, supply chain, smart-grids were researched alongside blockchain with blockchain-based IoT ecosystems being the top investigated topic in 2019 and 2020. Figure A1 and Table A1 in Appendix depicts the most investigated topics related to blockchain overtime. The title analysis revealed similar patterns (see Appendix). IoT security remained the dominant area of blockchain research for 2019–2020 while security in general was the dominant area of blockchain research between 2016 and 2018. The keyword analysis also led to similar results, in which “security” has been the dominant topic for the years 2017, 2018 and 2019, while “IoT” is the dominant topic for the year 2020.

Based on our trend analysis (see results in Appendix), it is clear that over the past five years, “security” and “IoT” became the most researched topics alongside blockchain while “fawning” and “bitcoins” are less relevant. This substantiates the claim that blockchain is developing toward becoming a suitable security platform in different industries including energy, healthcare, and supply chain but most importantly for IoT ecosystems.
