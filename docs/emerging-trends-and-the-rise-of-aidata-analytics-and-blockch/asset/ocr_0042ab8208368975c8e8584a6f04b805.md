It uses scrypt as a proof of work scheme, making Litecoin mining more accessible for users than Bitcoin mining that requires advanced hardware systems. Litecoin also has a relatively large share in the crypto market, but it is still below Bitcoin. It is the seventh cryptocurrency by market cap.

Monero is another important cryptocurrency that is considered in this study. It was launched in 2014. The most essential and distinguishing feature of Monero is its privacy-oriented characteristics. Information like the identity of the trader and the amounts of transactions is anonymous. This feature is enabled by employing the concepts of ring signatures and stealth addresses. Along with anonymity, Monero has other useful features, like untraceable transaction history, equal and easy mining and fungibility, which means that two units of a currency can be substituted like gold. Monero's market cap reaches 1.1 billion dollars by May 2020.

The next cryptocurrency is Dash that is launched in January 2014 to become a medium for daily transactions. It is a fork of Bitcoin with some additional properties. Similar to Monero, Dash provides faster and more anonymous service users by offering two new services, including PrivateSend and InstantSend. As a result of these features, the transactions become untraceable. Dash coins are equal and fungible. Dash runs on a masternode network. Masternode is a specialized server that is responsible for PrivateSend and InstantSend services, as well as the governance of the system. Dash also has a different form of governance. It employs a self-funding model of governance as 10% of all the mining rewards go back to Dash. Dash is number 21 on the list of top cryptocurrencies by market capitalization.

The other cryptocurrency in this study is Peercoin that was launched in August 2012. Peercoin is designed based on the Bitcoin framework. The distinguishing feature of Peercoins is that it is the first cryptocurrency that employs a hybrid system of proof-of-stake and proof-of-work. This makes Peercoin a secure and energy-efficient cryptocurrency as using the proof-of-stake as a hashing method makes block generation more energy-efficient and more secure.

The Stellar is an open-source blockchain-based network that allows for fast, cross-border transactions between any pair of currencies. It is very similar to Ripple. The cryptocurrency of Stellar is called lumens. Stellar works under the Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP), under which transaction verification is carried out by a set of honest nodes instead of the whole network of nodes. It reduces the costs and time for a transaction significantly. The Stellar network automatically carries out the currency conversion at the best possible rates. By May 2020, Stellar is the 11 cryptocurrency by market capitalization.
