In contrast with the IoT-based IPF model, this model promotes an ecosystem that integrates all the participants, which provides a more general SCF model for all commercial banks. Blockchain later enables efficient digital transferring of the warehouse receipts in a reliable environment. Due to frequent transactions with upstream and downstream, the transmission of information penetrates each link in the supply chain operation. The in-time analysis further speeds up loan approval processes and dynamic IPF.
