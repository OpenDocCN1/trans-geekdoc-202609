However, these various transactions require a lot of traction, which poses a problem. The combined sharding mechanism has been proposed to address this issue, as it could improve the security, scalability and performance of blockchain-based systems (Abuidris et al., 2019); the blockchain can be split into small shards to store state and transaction history while significantly improving overall performance. However, there are still technical challenges involved in processing and controlling these vast amounts of data, so further research is needed to identify these issues. Due to issues with processing speed (performance), the applicable market is quite limited. Although blockchain can be applied to fields wherein processing speed is not a significant consideration, these technical issues can hinder adoption in markets that require fast response speeds, such as payments. As efficiency and usefulness may be necessary for users to adopt new technologies, future research may require quantitative study and data collection to investigate user acceptance of and experience with blockchain-based systems.

The issues of security and privacy in blockchain must always be closely scrutinized. Although there have already been studies in security and distributed system literature (Butun et al., 2016), more research needs to investigate potential solutions for transaction privacy in blockchain applications. Several methods have been proposed, including a privacy preserved blockchain system (Yang et al., 2019; Kong et al., 2019), a secure e-government system (Elisa et al., 2018) and a mechanism for improving anonymity and privacy (Zheng et al., 2018). Carefully reviewing the proposed and proven solutions while actively conducting research into usefulness and adoption will make it possible to overcome the challenges and limitations of adopting current blockchain technology and maximize the advantages of blockchain in government and public sector services.

## 5. Conclusions

This study presented the advantages and challenges of blockchain-based identity management systems used in public services and government sectors through a literature review and the introduction of an actual system design to be used in the public sector. The results of this study show that blockchain implementation can significantly improve transparency, accountability and reliability in the user control of one’s own data while reducing the time and cost associated with delivering public services and ultimately increasing administrative efficiency. However, it is not easy to implement such a technology, and introducing new technologies in the government field requires a complicated and time-consuming process. Therefore, interdisciplinary research must go beyond the usual technology-driven approach and explore the implications and understanding of the government adoption of new technologies. In particular, to encourage the introduction of blockchain-based identity management services in the public sector, the new pilot project demonstrated in this study provides practical benefits for both end-users and service providers, such as improvements in privacy, ease of use, data protection and transparency, as well as a reduction in cost.

Although there has been theoretical research into how the use of blockchain technology is necessary in the public sector, there is still a need for quantitative research based on user feedback examining practical usability, ethical issues and productivity. Future studies may need to conduct both qualitative and quantitative research into the benefits to all stakeholders. These also need to be accompanied by efforts and research into establishing standards that are recognized not only domestically but also internationally, as well as the design of standardizations that are compatible with each industry, such as logistics, distribution and health care.
