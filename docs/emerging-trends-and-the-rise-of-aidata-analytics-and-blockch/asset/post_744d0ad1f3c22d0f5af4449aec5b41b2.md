## Technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services

In this study, the return of four stock indices of technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial service is considered. The first is the S&P Kensho Digital Communities Index that evaluates the performance of companies focused on digital social networking services. These companies generally provide platforms connecting a user’s profile with another individual or group and unified communications. The purpose of these companies is to form communication and shape information-sharing platforms. The other index is the S&P Kensho Alternative Finance Index that measures the performance of companies focused on offering alternative financing and wealth management services. These services include improving the loan approval process in complexity and speed, improving algorithmic loan approval, directing lending platforms, like peer-to-peer lending platforms and microfinance institutions that use a peer-to-peer business model to provide flexible insurance plans and crowdfunding platforms to offer digital currencies and their related software and hardware, such as exchanges and wallets. The third index is the S&P Kensho Democratized Banking (DB) Index that assesses the performance of companies that provide innovative financial services, such as advance payments, transaction management, financing and wealth management. These companies aim to provide new financing and wealth management methods, like robo-advisors, crowdfunding and peer-to-peer lending. They also focus on offering distributed ledger technology related to financial services and future payment capabilities such as digital, real-time and direct payments. The last index is the S&P Kensho Future Payments Index, which assesses the performance of companies that aim to enable the next-generation transformation of payments infrastructure. These companies offer general-purpose platforms that allow consumers to use a digital balance within a system, often in multiple channels, such as mobile wallets and peer-to-peer platforms. They also provide services like real-time payments and transfers across consumer and merchant accounts. Furthermore, they provide products or services related to biometrically-enabled payments and transaction security, like tokenization, point-to-point encryption and end-to-end encryption (S&P-Dow-Jones, 2020a).

## Cryptocurrencies

In this section, we intend to present a brief description of the selected cryptocurrencies. We try to select different cryptocurrencies with particular characteristics and with relative market caps. Although plenty of cryptocurrencies are introduced over the past years, Bitcoin has remained the most dominant crypto in the market. It is a decentralized, peer-to-peer cryptocurrency system. It was started in 2009, and transactions are ordered and recorded in a distributed ledger called a blockchain. The network nodes, which are called miners, link the blocks (Casino et al., 2019). In this respect, each contains the previous block’s hash to create a blockchain (Crosby et al., 2016). Bitcoin has the highest market cap among cryptocurrencies.

Following Bitcoin, Ethereum is the second-largest cryptocurrency by market cap. In 2015, Ethereum used Ethereum blockchain technology which has different functionalities, such as developing platforms for decentralized payment networks and storing computer code. The main aim of Ethereum is to provide users with a smart contract, which is a contract written in code into blockchain. Currently, Ethereum is the second-largest cryptocurrency after Bitcoin.

Launched in 2012, Ripple is the third cryptocurrency by market cap. It is a global settlement network that offers instant and low-cost cross-border payments with end-to-end transparency. The cryptocurrency of Ripple is called XRP. Unlike Bitcoin, the XRP was premined, so there would be no new creation of XRP. The market supply of Ripple is changed by introducing or removing XRP from the market.

The other cryptocurrency selected in this study is Litecoin, which was launched in 2011. It is very much like Bitcoin, but it is faster, and its limited supply is four times that of Bitcoin.
