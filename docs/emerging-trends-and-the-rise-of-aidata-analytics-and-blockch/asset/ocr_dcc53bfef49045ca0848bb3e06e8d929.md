# Conclusions and implications

This study investigates the interconnection among eight cryptocurrencies and four stock indexes of technology companies that mostly operate on blockchain platforms by employing the Bayesian DCC-MGARCH. MGARCH have some advantages over the other methodologies in calculating the dynamic correlation between time series. The most significant advantage of multivariate GARCH is its ability to investigate the interactions among all time series returns and their volatilities, revealing the spillover effects. By applying this model, dynamic changes in conditional correlations between pairs of variables can be detected. We explore the co-movement between the cryptocurrencies and blockchain-based technology companies by considering the skewed form of multivariate distribution instead of conventional distributions (normal, t-student and generalized error distributions) within the Bayesian ADCC-MGARCH model.

The empirical results and sensitivity analyses showed that the model parameters are significant and robust to prior specification. It indicates that the correlations between time series are dynamic. The results also confirm that the correlation between cryptocurrencies and blockchain-based technology companies is consistently positive. Therefore, the neutral relationship between cryptocurrencies and technology stocks also is rejected. No studies specifically analyze the interconnection between cryptocurrencies and blockchain-based technology companies, but a few focuses only on the interaction between cryptocurrencies and technology companies and sectors, including Symitsi and Chalvatzis (2018) and Umar et al. (2021).
