Additionally, the returns of some other cryptocurrencies, like Bitcoin, Litecoin and Ethereum, positively correlate with the return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies for fewer days than other cryptocurrencies. Our significant finding was that investor sentiment could impact the price of both cryptocurrencies and stock prices of blockchain-based technology companies. Both cryptocurrencies and blockchain-based technology companies are applying blockchain technology, so the positive and negative sentiment of investors might affect both cryptocurrencies and blockchain-based technology companies in the same direction.

We also found that the main driver of cryptocurrencies and blockchain-based technology companies is technological innovation (Umar et al., 2021) that can simultaneously promote the popularity, applicability and capacity of cryptocurrencies and blockchain-based technology companies. Besides, improvements in cryptocurrencies can improve the performance of these companies. For instance, Monero is an open-source, privacy-oriented cryptocurrency that enables users to make anonymous and secure payments, and Ripple is a real-time global settlement network that provides instant and low-cost international payments. These two cryptocurrencies positively correlate with the return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies for about 100% of sampling days that can be related to their unique features. The same holds for Peercoin, Stellar and even Dash that offer fast, secure and anonymous payments. In contrast, Ethereum, Bitcoin and Litecoin positively correlate with the return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies for fewer days compared to other cryptocurrencies on average, which can be due to speculative intention in trading these cryptocurrencies. This idea is supported by several studies (Ali et al., 2014; Baur et al., 2018; Dyhrberg, 2016b; Glaser et al., 2014; Peetz and Mall, 2017; Yermack, 2013).

Blockchain-based technology companies demonstrate no remarkable differences. All the returns of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies have a positive correlation with cryptocurrencies for more than 70% of sampling on average. However, alternative finance and democratized banking has a positive correlation with cryptocurrencies for slightly more days in comparison to the indices of other blockchain-based technology companies that can be due to their focus on providing new platforms for payment or using the peer-to-peer platform for lending and microfinancing purposes that makes them more dependent on blockchain technology.

These results have several implications. First, as the return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies converges to the return of cryptocurrencies, there seems to be a significant connection between their development and advancement. In a way, developments in cryptocurrencies will benefit blockchain-based technology companies and vice versa. Second, the number of days that cryptocurrencies have a positive correlation with the return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies is different among various cryptocurrencies, and cryptocurrencies with more useful traits such as being fast and convenient, along with anonymity, have a positive correlation with the return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies for more days. As a result, policymakers and even investors should pay more attention to these cryptocurrencies. Third, based on their strategy, the blockchain-based technology companies can add cryptocurrencies to their portfolio as hedgers or diversifiers.

This paper investigated only the correlation between the price return of several cryptocurrencies and the return of four stock indices of technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services. Consequently, this paper has faced some limitations in generalizing the findings. Future studies can investigate the relationship and interaction between more cryptocurrencies and technology companies in other fields of science and technology.
