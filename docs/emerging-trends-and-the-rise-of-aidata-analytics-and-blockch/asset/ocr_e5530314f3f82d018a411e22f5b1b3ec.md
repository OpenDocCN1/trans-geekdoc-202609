# 3. Blockchain-based identity management systems

Identity management refers to “the management of individual identities, their authentication, authorization, roles and privileges within organization or across boundaries” (Mármol, 2010; Kumar and Bhardwaj, 2018). Identity management systems are not a new concept. However, considering the potential exposure of personal information, including name, age, address, employer, credit score and many other types of data, that can occur when information is passed through a network, identity management has recently become an extremely important issue, with the goal being to allow users to have complete control over their shared data. Governments play many roles, which range from issuing and maintaining individuals’ identities to identifying and providing identification sources; in identity management, government data management is centrally controlled to protect sensitive data.
