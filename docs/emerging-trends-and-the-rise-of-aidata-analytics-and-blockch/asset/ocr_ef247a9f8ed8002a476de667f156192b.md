For instance, Ripple (XRP) had a positive correlation with DB in the entire sampling period implying the vital role of the Ripple as a global end-to-end settlement network that enables cross-border payments settlements for DB. Besides, Monero had a positive correlation with the stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies for about 99.66% of sampling day that can be due to its unique characteristics, like high security and anonymity along with fungibility. On the other hand, the AF index that measures the performance of companies focused on offering alternative financing and wealth management services, such as providing peer-to-peer lending platforms and microfinancing institutions that use a peer-to-peer business model, has a positive dynamic correlation with cryptocurrencies for about 86% of the sampling days. This is the highest among other indexes of the technology companies that can be due to the importance of cryptocurrencies, especially those with unique and innovative characteristics like Ripple, Monero and Peercoin for their business.

It seems that the development in the cryptocurrency market can be beneficial to the performance of these companies. Besides, the growth and expansion of these companies can increase the demand for blockchain applications, like cryptocurrencies, and make them more prevalent in communities. However, Bitcoin, Ethereum and Litecoin have a positive dynamic conditional correlation with the return of blockchain-based technology companies' stock indices for fewer days compared to other cryptocurrencies.
