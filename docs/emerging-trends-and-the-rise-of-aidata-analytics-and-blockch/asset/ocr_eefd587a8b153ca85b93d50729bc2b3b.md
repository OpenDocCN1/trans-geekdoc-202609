| Research direction #3: evaluating effectiveness and ROI | **Evaluating the effectiveness of HRM-focused AI applications**<br>(1) Can the replacement of the manual method of measuring psychological capital with a logistic regression model save many resources and guarantee accuracy?<br>(2) Can the study of pioneering organizations in the field of HRM-focused robotic technology implementation help in gauging the effectiveness and subsequent of using robots?<br>(3) Can elaboration on the substantive characteristics and relevance of the focused HR problem(s) and demonstration of the domain success after implementing a technique be done?<br>(4) Can a comparison be made between traditional methods for decision making and a model comprising of a combination of AI techniques to facilitate decision making in different domains of HR?<br><br>**Return on Investment (ROI)**<br>(1) Is the substantial cost of development and implementation of AI techniques like ESs justified?<br>(2) Is the ROI of developing models and computer software rational when employees may not have the skills to access what is required? |
