So, the cryptocurrency market can be affected enormously and continuously by pathbreaking innovations and arising technologies that create new algorithms and produce modern, sophisticated mining hardware. However, cryptocurrencies are not the only application of blockchain. It is a well-known technology fintech, along with others such as significant data machine learning and cloud computing (Ji et al., 2020). On the other hand, the economies of payment systems and financial services are affected profoundly by introducing new cryptocurrencies and blockchain technologies with functionalities like providing faster and more secure payment services. These features can boost the performance of blockchain-based technology companies, especially those that overwhelmingly provide financial services. As a result of these unique features, an increasing number of firms have started to adapt to this technology (Ji et al., 2020).

There seems to be a dynamic connection and linkage between the price return of cryptocurrencies and the stock return of firms that use blockchain technology in two respects. The first justification is based on behavioral finance. This field of study investigates the effect of the behavioral and psychological features of market capital investors on the capital market outcome (Ji et al., 2020). Based on behavioral finance, if the investor sentiments caused by speculative needs and subjective performance influence the investment demand, asset prices will diverge from their value (Ji et al., 2020), which is also confirmed by empirical research (Ba and Zhu, 2016; Wei-qi and Xin-xin, 2014). Blockchain is developing so rapidly (Ji et al., 2020), so as the cryptocurrency (Allen and Bryant, 2019) can influence the general sentiment toward firms that use this technology (Ji et al., 2020) and cryptocurrencies. In addition, both these technology firms and cryptocurrencies are backed by blockchains. Any advancement or decline in blockchain technology will affect the performance and popularity of these firms, cryptocurrency and the sentiments of investors toward them that will ultimately result in changes in the price of cryptocurrencies. These companies and the cryptocurrency are linked and derived by technological innovation (Umar et al., 2021). Any innovation in blockchain technology can have a positive impact on the performance, popularity and capabilities of these companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform. For instance, new cryptocurrencies that use new consensus mechanisms and other new technology have unique and valuable characteristics. This can improve the applicability and productivity of companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform. On the other hand, the success of these companies can have positive impacts on the prevalence of cryptocurrencies.

Therefore, to examine the connection between the stock return of technology companies that mostly use blockchain platforms and the price return of several selected cryptocurrencies, including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Litecoin, Ripple, Monero, Dash, Stellar and Peercoin, the dynamic correlation between them is investigated by employing the BADCC M-GARCH (Bayesian asymmetric dynamic conditional correlation multivariate GARCH) model. The robustness of the results is also examined, and it is confirmed that the estimated coefficients are robust. Our results indicated that most price returns of cryptocurrencies were correlated positively with the return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies over most sampling days and vice versa. The results can have different implications. First, the positive correlation between the price return of cryptocurrencies and the return of stock indices of blockchain-based technology companies indicates a significant connection between their development and advancement, in a way that developments in cryptocurrencies will benefit blockchain-based technology companies and vice versa. Second, newly invented cryptocurrencies with unique characteristics were positively correlated with the index return of the stock of blockchain-based technology companies for more days which may be due to the higher applicability of those cryptocurrencies to blockchain-based technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services. Third, these results also have important implications for risk management and diversification for investors and investment managers.

This paper attempted to contribute to the existing literature on blockchain and cryptocurrency by investigating the connection between the price return of several selected cryptocurrencies and the stock return of technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services. As will be mentioned in the literature review, there have been no studies on this issue, and just two papers have investigated the relationship between cryptocurrencies and technology companies and sectors (see Symitsi and Chalvatzis, 2018; Umar et al., 2021). However, in this paper, the interaction between technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services and several cryptocurrencies, some of which have specific capabilities such as providing fast, secure and anonymous payment platforms, will be analyzed by employing BADCC-MGARCH model that has not been used in other related studies. Besides, we contribute to the existing literature on the BADCC-MGARCH model as we use the expected Akaike information criterion (EAIC), Bayesian information criterion (BIC), Deviance information criterion (DIC) and weighted Deviance Information Criterion (wDIC) to select the skewed-multivariate Generalized Error Distribution (mvGED) as the most optimal distribution for errors. Also, the robustness of the results of the model is investigated in three steps: (1) checking the robustness of the number of draws, (2) checking the robustness of the α and β coefficients in prior and (3) checking the robustness of the a and b coefficients in prior.

A brief introduction of the four technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services is presented in the following, after a concise overview of blockchain contributions to information systems. Then, a brief description of each selected cryptocurrency is presented. Then, in the literature review, first, the research that studies the role of blockchain in finance and the way it changes the financial sector will be mentioned briefly; then, the studies that analyze the different characteristics of cryptocurrencies will be reviewed, and then the literature on the relationship between cryptocurrencies and other various financial assets, like stock indices, gold prices, oil prices and commodities, will be reviewed. Then the literature on the factor that can impact the stock return of technology companies, especially those operating on blockchain platforms, will be presented. Finally, the studies that analyzed the effects of market sentiments on the return price of cryptocurrencies and stock returns will be presented.

## Blockchain contributions to information systems

As mentioned in the introduction, blockchain technology is constantly evolving. By the introduction of the third generation of cryptocurrency, it becomes more applicable technology with lots of applications in different sectors and industries, including Internet of Things (IoT), media, finance, healthcare, insurance, energy, real estate, manufacturing process, mining, retail, banking, information governance, supply chain and others (Berdik et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2020). Businesses and sectors are eager to implement innovative technology, like blockchain, within their information systems to benefit from its capabilities and applications (Berdik et al., 2021). Blockchain technology offers lots of benefits to information systems. For instance, integrating blockchain technology with information systems can reduce third-party role, increase interoperability and improve efficiency and security (Berdik et al., 2021). In the case of the healthcare sector, blockchain technology helps manage and store electronic health records (Kaur et al., 2018). Blockchain technology can also make message communication easier due to increased interoperability (Berdik et al., 2021). More accessible communication can also reduce duplication as the information can be updated more quickly.
