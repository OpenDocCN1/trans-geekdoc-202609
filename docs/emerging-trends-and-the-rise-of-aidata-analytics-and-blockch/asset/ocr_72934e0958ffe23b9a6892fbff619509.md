# 6. Discussion

Big Data Analytics Capability was found to have a positive association with all the direct and indirect relationships (mediations) of the proposed theoretical model. The H1a hypothesis (β = 0.353; p < 0.001) described the causal relationship of the path “Big Data Analytics Capability” and “Product Innovation Performance” was accepted because the firms understood that product innovation is necessary. This realization happened since they are in a constantly changing environment, and only by adding new technologies and tools – as is the case of Big Data and its analysis capacity – it is possible to improve Product Innovation Performance (Hooi and Abu, 2018). Big Data Analytics Capability can directly impact a firm’s Product Innovation Performance, strengthening the underlying process of dynamic innovation capabilities, specifically in firms that work with a high amount and diversity of data, which would be impossible to analyze and interpret another way differently (Wamba et al., 2017).

Using Big Data within the product innovation process, firms allow for greater accuracy in their research and development investments because there will be greater prior intelligence in knowing what will return to the organization. This intelligence will come from the larger amount and variety of information that the organization will have, allowing for more refinement in its investment strategy. The innovation capacities in the firms observed in this research indicate a dependence on the effort of the teams involved collectively and not on the individual effort in the use of Big Data. It is also essential that there is a cross-flow of knowledge between different people working in parallel on different aspects of a project (Koberg et al., 2003).

The H1b hypothesis (β = 0.600; p < 0.001), described as the causal relationship in “Big Data Analytics Capability” and “Process-oriented dynamic capabilities”, was accepted because organizations note that it will be a crucial competitive differentiator allowing the firms to stand out from the competition (Wixom et al., 2013). It was noted in the survey that the responding managers associated the Big Data Analytics Capability with the dynamic capabilities, as the organization is facing its competition. That is, the better the Big Data analytics applied, the better its differential is to the rest of the market (Wamba et al., 2017). In fast-paced environments, constantly changing conditions (unpredictable and volatile), existing modes of operation wear out quickly. Therefore, process-oriented dynamic capabilities are necessary to maintain competitiveness (Wilden and Gudergan, 2015). The role of Big Data Analytics Capability that operates as drivers of process-oriented dynamic capabilities has been noted in the literature (McAfee et al., 2012; Mikalef et al., 2019a, b; Singh and El-Kassar, 2018).

Regarding the H1c hypothesis (β = 0.740; p < 0.001), described as “Big Data Analytics Capability” and “Business Value”, it was accepted because the survey respondents note that there are significant gains to the firm when the Big Data tools are applied correctly. That is, the perception of managers and analysts is that the organization is ahead of its competition, enabling a better understanding of its customers and their relationship with them (Wamba et al., 2017). Big Data analysis is evident from firms’ growing investments, particularly those that operate in complex and fast-paced environments (Wang et al., 2016; Mikalef et al., 2019a).
