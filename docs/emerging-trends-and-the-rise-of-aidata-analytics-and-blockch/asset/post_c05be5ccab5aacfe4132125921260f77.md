
The micro-level research agenda of AI, data analytics and blockchain can be integrated within a macro-level overview that links to their disruptive effects throughout societies and economies globally. As organizations in all sectors progressively adopt more interactive, reliable, transparent and explainable AI, people will gain greater value from digital technologies in both their workplaces and lifestyles. Once AI becomes more tightly integrated with BDA, blockchain and other technologies such as the IoT, their innovative combinations may give rise to major industry disruptions. More exploratory research is necessary, especially through in-depth case studies, to uncover the potential for the emergence of these innovative business models. Hence, a macro-view of digital innovation must emerge and may eventually become a dominant force as digital transformation has become in the past few years.
