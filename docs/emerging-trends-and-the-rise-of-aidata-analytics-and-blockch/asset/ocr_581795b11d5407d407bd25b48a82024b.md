Evaluation Metrics: The efficiency of the deep learning models was evaluated using confusion matrix, Receiver Operating Characteristics (ROC) curve, Area under ROC curve score (AUC), F1-Score, Sensitivity, Precision, and Recall scores.

The confusion matrix is a table that shows the number of records correctly classified for each class.

The ROC curve is a graph plotted between True Positive Rates (TPR) vs False Positive Rates (FPR) at various threshold settings. The area under the ROC Curve (AUC) is the area under the Receiver operating characteristics (ROC) curve; sometimes, it is also referred to as AUC-ROC. AUC conveys the overall measurement of performance across all classes at various classification thresholds.

Precision is the number of correct positive results divided by the number of all positive results returned by the classifier. Sensitivity is calculated based on the number of correct positive results divided by the number of all relevant samples (all samples that should have been identified as positive). Sensitivity is also sometimes called as Recall. Specificity is the ratio of true negative to the sum of a true negative and false positive.

```
Precision = \frac{TP}{TP + FP} (1)

Sensitivity(Recall) = \frac{TP}{TP + FN} (2)

Specificity = \frac{TN}{TN + FP} (3)
```

where TP is True Positive which means that the observation is positive and is predicted to be positive as well, TN is True Negative where observation is negative and is predicted to be negative as well. FP is False Positive where observation is negative but is predicted to be positive, and FN is False Negative where observation is positive but is predicted to be negative.

F1 score is calculated by taking the harmonic mean or weighted average of precision and recall.

F1 = 2 \cdot \frac{\text{Precision} \times \text{Recall}}{\text{Precision} + \text{Recall}} (4)
