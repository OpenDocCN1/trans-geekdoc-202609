Second, our NLP method allowed us to automate the screening of articles in order to arrive at a more confined list to use in the literature review. For the analysis, we used the NLTK python package which allowed us to eliminate stop words (common words like am, this, are to ignore), word stemming (chopping off the derivational affixes of words to arrive at their base or root form), and tokenization (breaking sentences into groups of words) (see Navlani, 2019).
