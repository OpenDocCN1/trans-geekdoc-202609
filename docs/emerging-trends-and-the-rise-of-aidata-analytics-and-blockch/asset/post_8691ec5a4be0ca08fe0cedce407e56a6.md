## Consequential factors of Big Data's Analytics Capability: how firms use data in the competitive scenario

Luis Hernan Contreras Pinochet, Guilherme de Camargo Belli Amorim and Durval Lucas Júnior

Business Administration, Universidade Federal de São Paulo, Sao Paulo, Brazil, and Cesar Alexandre de Souza Business Administration, Universidade de São Paulo, Sao Paulo, Brazil

## Abstract

**Purpose** – The article's objective is to analyze the consequent factors of Big Data Analytics Capability for firms in the competitive scenario, using different analytical models.
**Design/methodology/approach** – The research had a quantitative approach, using a survey of data from firms located in the state of São Paulo – Brazil. Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) was used to validate the model.
**Findings** – The results reveal that all hypotheses were accepted. Business value was the construct that had the most explanatory power in the model. It is necessary to invest more in analytical tools, as well as people trained in the analysis of these models, in addition to a change of mindset, which will dictate the bias of the firm's strategic decision-making. The Big Data analysis is evident from firms' growing investments, particularly those that operate in complex and fast-paced environments.
**Practical implications** – The proposed theoretical model makes it possible to verify firms' analytical structure and whether they are better positioned to analyze customer data and information in real-time, generate insights and implement solutions to maintain and improve their market position.
**Originality/value** – The contribution of this article is to present a proposal to expand the research models in the literature that analyzed the direct and indirect relationship between “Big Data Analytics Capability” and “Product Innovation Performance”.

**Keywords** Big data analytics capability, Product innovation performance, Process-oriented dynamic capabilities, Business value
**Paper type** Research paper

## 1. Introduction

In a world with continuous technological changes, innovation is a determining factor for firms' economic growth and survival. Innovation has the ability to predict which technologies could potentially change an industry, create markets and increase accessibility and viability of the business. The organization must carefully analyze the environment to detect changes, disseminate knowledge and adjust according to what will create value for the customer. This implies adopting and managing new technologies such as: Big Data Analytics Capabilities (Hilbert and Lopez, 2011; Singh and El-Kassar, 2018; Wamba et al., 2017; Warner and Wäger, 2019).

Firms are increasingly under pressure to have a quick response and constant adaptation related to a highly connected and dynamic global environment. Analytical skills must be developed so that the results are a combination of management, learning and process reconfiguration processes (Bhimani and Willcocks, 2014). Organizations are creating or developing their products and services but adopting data analysis that can improve performance, productivity, agility and effectiveness within a perspective of structural change in organizations (Chang, 2017; Larson and Chang, 2016).

Big Data is an extensive set of data that needs special tools to analyze. Also, it has become an important decision-making component, potentially at all levels of the firm where management processes have had to change. This, as a result of the adoption of data-based strategies, led to changes in most business functions in firms (Mikalef et al., 2019a, b; Sena et al., 2019; Gupta et al., 2020).

From the management perspective, the relevance of Big Data tools in organizational restructuring processes that require a “new process” of organizations is clear, demanding a digital performance, from logistics to communication. Additionally, organizations interested in using Big Data Analytics Capability tools in their business should initially define their goals within a process. These goals should be aligned with those of the organization itself. This guarantees that the choice of tools and their attributes are the most suitable for its analysis (Aydiner et al., 2019; Subramaniam et al., 2019). For the advantages of Big Data Analytics Capability to be exploited, there is a growing need to implement intelligent systems and computational techniques that can reduce the cognitive load in accessing and processing large volumes of data in equipment with high computational capacity and analytical software (Birek et al., 2018; Dubey et al., 2019). Besides, the real potential of Big Data lies not in its Vs of Big Data but its affordance and knowledge that the firm has in different types of analytical methods (Markus, 2015).

The article's objective is to analyze the consequent factors of Big Data Analytics Capability for firms in the competitive scenario, using different analytical models. To this end, in this quantitative research, we conducted a survey (n = 141) in firms (leaders in developing innovation in their products and services) located in the state of São Paulo – Brazil.

The motivation for this study arose from the literature review to identify several other resources needed by a firm to create Big Data Analytics Capability and expected results with the use (Gupta and George, 2016; Mikalef et al., 2019a, b; Gupta et al., 2020).

The contribution of this article is to present a proposal to expand the research model based on the studies of the model of Hooi and Abu (2018) that analyzed the relationship between “Big Data Analytics Capability” and “Product Innovation Performance” also identifying other factors that affect the success of Big Data initiatives in firms (LaValle et al., 2011). The authors scrutinized the studies by Hannachi (2015) and Gunday et al. (2011) to improve the construct “Big Data Analytics Capability”; the studies by Wang et al. (2012) and Wamba et al. (2017) for the “Product Innovation Performance” construct, and for the “Process-oriented dynamic capabilities” construct formulated from Kim et al. (2011). Furthermore, the authors examined the relationship between Big Data Analytical Capability and “Business Value”, as observed in the literature by Akter et al. (2017).

The following sections of this paper expand on the observations noted above. In Section 2, we provide an overview of the literature on Big Data Analytics Capability and Big Data in Business and Product Innovation Performance, highlighting the main research conducted in the area and the existing gaps that this study intends to address. Next, we present in Section 3 the construction of the research hypothesis and research model. Section 4 outlines the research's general approach with a description of the study method and data collection. Section 5 presents the results and analyzes. Finally, in Section 6, the results are discussed. In Section 7, the research implications and conclusions are presented, which is subdivided into theoretical and practical implications highlighted, and the limitations and directions of future research.
