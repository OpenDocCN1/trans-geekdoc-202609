This paper attempted to contribute to the existing literature on blockchain and cryptocurrency by investigating the connection between the price return of several selected cryptocurrencies and the stock return of technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services. As will be mentioned in the literature review, there have been no studies on this issue, and just two papers have investigated the relationship between cryptocurrencies and technology companies and sectors (see Symitsi and Chalvatzis, 2018; Umar et al., 2021). However, in this paper, the interaction between technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services and several cryptocurrencies, some of which have specific capabilities such as providing fast, secure and anonymous payment platforms, will be analyzed by employing BADCC-MGARCH model that has not been used in other related studies. Besides, we contribute to the existing literature on the BADCC-MGARCH model as we use the expected Akaike information criterion (EAIC), Bayesian information criterion (BIC), Deviance information criterion (DIC) and weighted Deviance Information Criterion (wDIC) to select the skewed-multivariate Generalized Error Distribution (mvGED) as the most optimal distribution for errors. Also, the robustness of the results of the model is investigated in three steps: (1) checking the robustness of the number of draws, (2) checking the robustness of the α and β coefficients in prior and (3) checking the robustness of the a and b coefficients in prior.

A brief introduction of the four technology companies that mainly operate on the blockchain platform and provide financial services is presented in the follow-up, after a concise overview of blockchain contributions to information systems. Then, a brief description of each selected cryptocurrency is presented. Then, in the literature review, first, the research that studies the role of blockchain in finance and the way it changes the financial sector will be mentioned briefly; then, the studies that analyze the different characteristics of cryptocurrencies will be reviewed, and then the literature on the relationship between cryptocurrencies and other various financial assets, like stock indices, gold prices, oil prices and commodities, will be reviewed. Then the literature on the factor that can impact the stock return of technology companies, especially those operating on blockchain platforms, will be presented. Finally, the studies that analyzed the effects of market sentiments on the return price of cryptocurrencies and stock returns will be presented.

# Blockchain contributions to information systems

As mentioned in the introduction, blockchain technology is constantly evolving. By the introduction of the third generation of cryptocurrency, it becomes more applicable technology with lots of applications in different sectors and industries, including Internet of Things (IoT), media, finance, healthcare, insurance, energy, real estate, manufacturing process, mining, retail, banking, information governance, supply chain and others (Berdik et al., 2021; Chen et al., 2020). Businesses and sectors are eager to implement innovative technology, like blockchain, within their information systems to benefit from its capabilities and applications (Berdik et al., 2021). Blockchain technology offers lots of benefits to information systems. For instance, integrating blockchain technology with information systems can reduce their party role, increase interoperability and improve efficiency and security (Berdik et al., 2021). In the case of the healthcare sector, blockchain technology helps manage and store electronic health records (Kaur et al., 2018). Blockchain technology can also make message communication easier due to increased interoperability (Berdik et al., 2021). More accessible communication can also reduce duplicity as the information can be updated more quickly.
