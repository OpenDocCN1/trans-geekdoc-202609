# 3.1 Adoption of identity management systems in South Korea

South Korea is well known as a global leader in technological innovation, and it has been actively involved in the development of blockchain technology. Korea considers blockchain to be a core industry of the fourth industrial revolution, and it has proposed an initial market-forming technology strategy to secure competitiveness and lay a foundation for industrial revitalization (OECD, 2011). According to a UN e-government survey examining the wide-ranging state of digital governments in the 193 UN member states worldwide, South Korea is among the leading groups in terms of the scope and quality of its online services (UN, 2020).

As South Korea recently passed the “Electronic Signature Act Amendment,” which included the abolition of the existing public certificate system, there are growing expectations for its private certificate market, and blockchain-based decentralized identification and mobile identification are attracting increasing attention. At present, South Korea has recently launched the Decentralized Identity (DID) initiative and established the Decentralized Identity Alliance Korea Association, which has global influence (Table 4). The DID Alliance is an open industry association that has been established for Decentralized Identity services in an attempt to develop a standardized and interchangeable framework to ensure the reliability of digital identity verification (Jeong, 2019; Ledger Insights, 2020).

Blockchain platforms operate in a separate manner for each business combination, which can pose a challenge to future integration issues. In response to this, DID Alliance Korea actively collaborates with international companies and organizations to lead the search for global certifications related to Decentralized Identity.
