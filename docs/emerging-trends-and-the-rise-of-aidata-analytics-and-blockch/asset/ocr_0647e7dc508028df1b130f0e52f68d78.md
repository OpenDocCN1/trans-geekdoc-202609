# Consequential factors of Big Data's Analytics Capability: how firms use data in the competitive scenario

Luis Hernan Contreras Pinochet, Guilherme de Camargo Belli Amorim and Durval Lucas Júnior

Business Administration, Universidade Federal de São Paulo, Sao Paulo, Brazil, and Cesar Alexandre de Souza Business Administration, Universidade de São Paulo, Sao Paulo, Brazil

# Abstract

**Purpose** – The article’s objective is to analyze the consequent factors of Big Data Analytics Capability for firms in the competitive scenario, using different analytical models.
**Design/methodology/approach** – The research had a quantitative approach, using a survey of data from firms located in the state of São Paulo – Brazil. Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) was used to validate the model.
**Findings** – The results reveal that all hypotheses were accepted. Business value was the construct that had the most explanatory power in the model. It is necessary to invest more in analytical tools, as well as people trained in the analysis of these models, in addition to a change of mindset, which will dictate the bias of the firm’s strategic decision-making. The Big Data analysis is evident from firms’ growing investments, particularly those that operate in complex and fast-paced environments.
**Practical implications** – The proposed theoretical model makes it possible to verify firms’ analytical structure and whether they are better positioned to analyze customer data and information in real-time, generate insights and implement solutions to maintain and improve their market position.
**Originality/value** – The contribution of this article is to present a proposal to expand the research models in the literature that analyzed the direct and indirect relationship between “Big Data Analytics Capability” and “Product Innovation Performance”.

**Keywords** Big data analytics capability, Product innovation performance, Process-oriented dynamic capabilities, Business value
**Paper type** Research paper

# 1. Introduction

In a world with continuous technological changes, innovation is a determining factor for firms’ economic growth and survival. Innovation has the ability to predict which technologies could potentially change an industry, create markets and increase accessibility and viability of the business. The organization must carefully analyze the environment to detect changes, disseminate knowledge and adjust according to what will create value for the customer this implies adopting and managing new technologies such as: Big Data Analytics Capabilities (Hilbert and Lopez, 2011; Singh and El-Kassar, 2018; Wamba et al., 2017; Warner and Wäger, 2019).

Firms are increasingly under pressure to have a quick response and constant adaptation related to a highly connected and dynamic global environment. Analytical skills must be developed so that the results are a combination of management, learning and process reconfiguration processes (Bhimani and Willcocks, 2014). Organizations are creating or developing their products and services but adopting data analysis that can improve performance, productivity, agility and effectiveness within a perspective of structural change in organizations (Chang, 2017; Larson and Chang, 2016).
