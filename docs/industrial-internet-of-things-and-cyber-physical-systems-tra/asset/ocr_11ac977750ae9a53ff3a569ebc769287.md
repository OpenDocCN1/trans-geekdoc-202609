# Cloud-Based IoT Architecture for Green Buildings

![](bbox=[0.138, 0.125, 0.721, 0.441])

An integrated cloud enabled Internet of Things system has an open architecture which allows the building manager to look at all parameters of the building. The input parameters will be collected from electric meters, water sensors, thermal sensors, noise sensors, motion sensors. Data about occupants and their movement will be traced by respective sensors. Overall energy consumption past and present records will be measured. This data will be given as input to the intelligent systems which will analyze and predict the output. Once the analysis is done based on designed rules and potential the controllers are put into action. Actuators play the important role of execution. Thermostats, controllers and switches help utilize the feedback from the smart systems.
