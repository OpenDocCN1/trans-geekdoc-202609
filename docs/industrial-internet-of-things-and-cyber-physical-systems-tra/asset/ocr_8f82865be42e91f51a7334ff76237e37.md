# ABSTRACT

The internet usage for commercial and public services has significantly increased over these decades to where security of information is becoming a more important issue to society. At the same time, the number of network attacks in IoT is increasing. These include distributed denial of service (DDOS) attack, phishing, trojan, and others that will cause the network information to not be secure. With the revolution in Industry 4.0 and IoT being the main asset in the Fourth Industrial Revolution, many companies spend thousands or millions to protect their networks and servers.
