# MODULATION SCHEME

The use of Li-Fi depends on light communication as well as optical wireless communication (OWC) systems. The modulated signal that contains the data is used to switch the LED at the ideal frequencies. Various modulation schemes are available for Li-Fi which can communicate in an orderly manner even in the absence of light. It also helps to communicate in low-intensity area. Variation in light intensity as reported in the information signal.

+   1. Single Carrier Modulation (SCM): In this modulation scheme, microwave and optical wave is used as subcarrier and carrier respectively. It is worthy of a low and medium data rate. On-off keying (OOK), pulse-position modulation (PPM), and pulse-amplitude modulation (PAM) are the notable modulation technique used in SCM.
2. Multiple Carrier Modulation (MCM): The achievement of SCMs increases its spectral potential. This requires a complex process when SCM is brought into action at high data rates. The development of MCMs overcomes the loss of SCMs. OFDM is a popular variant of MCM. The Optical orthogonal frequency division multiplexing (O-OFDM) is the carrier use as a part in optical wireless communication (OWC).
