
## 2030.5-2013, Green Button / Energy Services Provider Interface (ESPI) / NAESB REQ 21 (NAESB) / Smart Energy Consumer Standards

The different platforms offered by IoT these days for retailers consist of three parts mainly: sensing, controlling, and metering. Data collection, pre-processing, and monitoring are done by different sensors to detect humidity, heat, pressure, noise, movements, light, and objects. Controllers are used to control the environment based on inputs from the sensors, which include switches, actuators, smart plugs, and thermostats. Metering is used to measure information precisely to keep track of consumption and changes in energy requirements. This includes electric meters, water meters, etc.

Current trends show the usage of Internet of Things devices such as battery-less energy harvesting switches and sensors (Cherry, EnOcean, ZF KNX modules), and low-cost Wi-Fi controllers (Particle, Ayla Networks, Exosite, Relay, FogHorn, Plat.One, Rivian, LoneStar, SAP, xPic, Microchip) (microcontrollers are preferred as they provide simplicity, security, and cost-effectiveness). Notable examples include Nest by Google, EchoBee, Netatmo, SimpliSafe, TenDril, Canary, Quby, iSmartAlarm, and many others. The idea is to ensure features like adaptability, flexibility, compatibility, low latency, interoperability, scalability, and feasibility using IoT-based systems (S. K. Viswanath, C. Yuen, W. Tushar, W.-T. Li, C.-K. Wen, K. Hu, C. Chen, and X. Liu, 2016).

### Gateway

It performs data conversion from device-specific data to cloud-specific data. Data from different sensors are connected via adapters that provide connectivity and compatibility on both sides. It ensures that data received in various formats (Wi-Fi, ZigBee, Bluetooth, 6LoWPAN) are converted into a standard format. For example, it transforms 6LoWPAN messages to IPv4 formats to be stored and processed further. Sensors/devices send data to gateways, which then backhaul the data to the cloud via a high-speed connection.

This allows sensors to talk within short distances, thus boosting battery life, which is critical for most applications and in turn enhances the life of each sensor/device. Sometimes the amount of data captured is huge, and it becomes very difficult at a later stage to segregate the data.

Nowadays, a lot of filtering and preprocessing is done prior to pushing data to the cloud, which helps in reducing overload and time. It also helps minimize overall requirements and save on storage costs as well. This is fruitful for lag-free operations for real-time applications. Overall performance will increase. ### Cloud Service

Cloud Service mainly consists of cloud service brokers that assimilate information from the gateway or other devices and do pre-processing before storing them in local databases in a specific format. Data analyzed can be displayed so as to monitor and check for errors in any device. Event Managers make rules and trigger alerts if faults are detected or in case of emergency. For example, based on data from a temperature sensor, it will check if the threshold level set is crossed to trigger an alert and process it further. The Control module will monitor the health of devices and display the current status via a mobile or web application. It identifies and monitors essential energy variables to calculate the heating and energy requirements to keep the building healthy and smartly assess the subsystems' behavior.

### Detailed Modeling – Based On Parameters

Consider parameters like occupancy, humidity, temperature, precipitation, PPM levels, appliances, positioning, ventilation, and other energy variables to design new models that optimize resource utilization and reduce overall costs in green buildings. These parameters are crucial to design and optimize.

- **Occupancy:** Prediction of occupants and their activity at different time intervals are essential to model the system. More false positives lead to inaccurate prediction, leading to giving wrong signals to control systems which will ruin the complete idea of comfort with technology.
- **Humidity, precipitation, and temperature:** It becomes the most critical parameter due to climate changing constantly throughout the year. External and internal conditions along with moisture ratio have to be precise.
- **Parts per million (PPM levels):** This also helps to predict and design the system. Parts per million (PPM level) depends on the area being urban or rural, densely or sparsely populated, and industrial or residential area. This leads to increased use of air purifiers and such systems, leading to a rise in cost and usage of energy resources. This impacts the environment.
- **Appliances:** Number of occupants as well as number of appliances have to be considered and calculated for accurate measurements. As the number of appliances keeps on increasing as the standard of living improves, the burden on power also increases. The amount of e-waste generated has also added to the list.
- **Positioning:** It becomes essential as it helps predict more accurately, and topology is a fixed parameter. Also, the position across the globe will have an impact on overall calculation.
- **Ventilation:** Will help to identify use of natural light and day lighting so that artificial resources are not utilized much.

Split the data available on the cloud and create new algorithms/methods to ease load on artificial resources and focus on renewable energy. Real-time data forecasting with timely feedback to Internet of Things devices will enhance the monitoring and control giving instant results. It will lead to proper scheduling of systems and mitigate man-made errors.

Lots of learning algorithms have been tested and applied on extensive data collected. Machine Learning as well as deep learning algorithms have been tried and tested by experts individually as well as in various combinations. From most of these papers, it can be concluded that best-known models like Support Vector Machine as well as Neural Networks (Giovanni Tardiolli, Ruth Kerrigan, Mike Oates, James O’Donnell, Donal Finn, 2015) fail to work for long-term and large predictions. Accuracy is least even in detailed building profiling. (Lai Wei, Wei Tian, Elisabete A Silva, Ruchi Choudhary, QingXinMeng & Song Yang, 2015) (Kadir Amasayi & Nora El-Gohany, 2016). Few researches show that deep extreme learning machine (DELM) are accurate and better than other neural networks for hourly and weekly energy consumption prediction. (Muhammad Fafaz and DoHyun Kim, 2018). It is also seen that deep auto-encoder performs better compared to a principal component analysis (Hinton, G.E.; Salakhutidinov, R.R, 2006). These researches are most challenging and depend on so many factors that their results cannot be considered uniform and effective. Also, the datasets used for time series predictions in these algorithms have less data than other researches in that domain. Trial and error prediction mechanisms prove effective only in small or controlled environments rather than open and large commercial spaces.

It will help scan all resources which could have integrated and cost-effective solutions. It can help predict retrofit candidates which will help cut costs. (Daniel E Marsaco, Constantine E.
