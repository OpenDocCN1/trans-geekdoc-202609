Badrinarayanan et al. [Bad17] proposed an encoder-decoder architecture for semantic labeling called SegNet, which showed promising semantic labeling results on benchmark datasets. As depicted in Figure 6.7, SegNet is composed of an encoder and a decoder network. The architecture of the encoder is topologically identical to the convolutional part of the VGG16 architecture, while the topology of the decoder is in essence the mirrored encoder. Note that the number of channels for the last convolutional layer is set to 6 in order to provide pixel-wise predictions for each category. Instead of applying deconvolutional layers to up-sample low resolution feature maps, the decoder uses max pooling indices to perform non-linear up-sampling.
