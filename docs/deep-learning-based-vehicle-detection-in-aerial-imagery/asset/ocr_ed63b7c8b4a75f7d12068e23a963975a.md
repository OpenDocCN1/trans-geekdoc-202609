As aerial imagery often exhibits higher GSDs, the detection performance of IMT Faster R-CNN and EMT Faster R-CNN are evaluated for various GSDs. Therefore, the image tiles used for training and testing are down-scaled by factor 2, 3, 4, and 5 yielding GSDs of 10 cm, 15 cm, 20 cm, and 25 cm, respectively. For each GSD, models are trained separately, whereby FCN-D16 is employed for the semantic labeling branch. The anchor box scales are adapted for each GSD, so that the respective anchor box areas are equivalent to the mean object dimensions. As shown in Table 6.10, EMT Faster R-CNN achieves the best AP for all GSDs. The gain in AP compared to the baseline Faster R-CNN increases with higher GSDs, which shows that additional semantic context information due to the proposed architecture boosts the detection performance even for tiny objects as in case of high GSDs.
