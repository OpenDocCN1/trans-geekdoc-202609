# xView

The xView datasets comprises 1127 images from WorldView-3 satellites with a GSD of 30 cm. The image dimensions are in the range between 700 and 4000 pixels. The images are acquired over different continents including Australia, Africa, Asia, Europe, Middle and South America and thus, include a large variety of different scenarios and objects (see Figure 4.5). GT annotations of 60 categories including buildings, vehicles and mini-scenes, e.g., shipping container lot, are provided in form of axis-aligned bounding boxes for 846 images. The most GT annotations either belong to category building or category small car because of their prevalence in densely populated areas.
