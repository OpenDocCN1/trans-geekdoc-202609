# ## 2.1 INTRODUCTION

Smart health monitoring frameworks provide services in many fields, such as hospitals, workplaces, and residences, to drastically reduce the cost of doctor visits while also improving overall patient care quality. Innovative healthcare services enable doctors to forecast, detect, diagnose, and treat diseases through intelligent remote handling and response to medical requests, reducing hospitalisation and supporting patients. Artificial intelligence (AI) can be used to prevent and control the spread of dangerous illnesses like as Ebola, avian influenza, and, most recently, the COVID-19 epidemic. Given the challenges, the development of effective, innovative healthcare services will surely promote the health of our society (Akmandon & Jha 2017). Intelligent healthcare systems integrate a variety of smart devices to meet the demands of many consumers. As a result, a vast volume of medical records created by smart devices must be accurately analysed.
