# 1.1 INTRODUCTION

In models, the algorithms are proposed and processed locally without dependence on the cloud, internet, etc. They use the data that they have generated themselves for training purposes, storing it locally and taking quick decisions when needed. Such techniques are model trains on various client devices. The model updates and the curated data are then sent to the remote server periodically. The implementation of such models saves backhauling costs and bandwidth requirements. In a field such as healthcare where even a second's delay can result in death, reduced latency is a major point to be considered. The analytics methods utilised by the edge-AI (edge-artificial intelligence) techniques to locally curate, analyse, and produce results, include advanced analytics methods like artificial intelligence and machine learning, edge computing techniques like machine vision, video analytics, and sensor fusion, and methods incorporating location intelligence. The hardware support consists of electronics and devices that enable edge computing.
