# 15.5.1 PROMOTES PREVENTIVE HEALTHCARE

There is promise of a more pro-active approach to healthcare with wearable technology. This is because wearables can be used to act before health issues become serious rather than reacting to them after they have already started to cause difficulties. Health abnormalities can be found in those who are already predisposed to them before they become concerns (Sabry & Tamer 2022).
