# 5.3 GLOBAL STATISTICS OF VISUAL IMPAIRMENT

# 5.3.1 VISUAL IMPAIRMENT AND BLINDNESS

According to the WHO, as of 8 October 2020, at least 1 billion people around the globe have a near or distant vision impairment which could have been prevented. The top leading causes of visual impairment and blindness currently are uncorrected refractive errors and cataracts. The report also found that most people who have visual impairment or blindness are over the age of 50 years.
