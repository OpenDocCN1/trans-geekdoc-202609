# ## 8.3.2 WORKING OF AAROGYA SETU

Aarogya Setu uses the technique of contact tracing as you go about your everyday activities to keep tabs on the persons you have or might have meet. Figure 8.3 shows the Aarogya Setu App's quick operation. You will be informed right away and will be given access to preventative medical care if any of your contacts subsequently test positive for COVID-19. Aarogya Setu will alert you and arrange for immediate medical attention if you have first- or second-degree contact with a COVID-19-infected person. The self-assessment test can also be used to determine infection risks.
