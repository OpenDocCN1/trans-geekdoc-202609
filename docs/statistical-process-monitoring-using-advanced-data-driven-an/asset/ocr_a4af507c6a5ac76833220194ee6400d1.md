In summary, the results indicate that the hybrid RNN-RBM model is an intuitive choice for modeling IMs multivariate time-series data. The RNN-RBM modeling approach has been combined with binary clustering algorithms to monitor IMs data of KAUST WWTP. RNN-RBM with OCSVM can adequately distinguish normal observations form abnormal IMs observations. This study shows that the combination of RNN-RBM and OCSVM results in better detection.
