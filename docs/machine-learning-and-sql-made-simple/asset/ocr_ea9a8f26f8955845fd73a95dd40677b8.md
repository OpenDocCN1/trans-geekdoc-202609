# 3. Decision Trees:

Decision trees are versatile algorithms that can be used for both classification and regression tasks. In regression, decision trees partition the feature space into regions and predict the average or mean value of the target variable for each region.

A decision tree consists of internal nodes, representing decisions based on feature values, and leaf nodes, representing the predicted target variable.

# Example:

Suppose we want to predict the sales of a product based on advertising expenditure and pricing. A decision tree regression model can split the feature space based on different advertising and pricing thresholds and assign the average sales value to each region. This allows us to understand which combinations of advertising and pricing lead to higher or lower sales.
