# 6. Applications of Neural Networks:

Neural networks have found wide-ranging applications in numerous domains. Some notable examples include:

+   - Computer Vision: Neural networks have achieved state-of-the-art results in tasks such as image classification, object detection, and image segmentation.
- Natural Language Processing: They are extensively used in tasks like sentiment analysis, machine translation, text generation, and question-answering systems.
- Speech Recognition: Neural networks play a crucial role in speech recognition systems, enabling accurate transcription and voice-controlled applications.
- Recommendation Systems: Neural networks power recommendation systems that suggest personalized content, products, or services to users based on their preferences and behavior.
- Financial Modeling: They are employed in various financial applications, including stock market prediction, credit risk assessment, and fraud detection.
