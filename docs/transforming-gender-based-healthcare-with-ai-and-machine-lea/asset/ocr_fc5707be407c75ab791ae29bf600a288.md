# ### 9.8.1 DECENTRALISED DATA MANAGEMENT

Blockchain’s decentralised nature opens up a new way to work with data in gender health. By distributing health data across a network of nodes, blockchain makes it less likely that someone will view or change it without permission. Every action is kept safe in a block, even ones that involve medical records, treatment plans, or personal information. This creates a data chain that can’t be changed (Zhang et al., 2018).
