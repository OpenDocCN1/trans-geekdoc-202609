The future of AI's role in gender-specific healthcare is very bright. It is important to strengthen the ethical and regulatory frameworks designed specifically for gender responsive healthcare. AI experts and healthcare professionals should collaborate to establish guidelines and standards that prioritize transparency, accountability and fairness when using AI driven gender-sensitive healthcare.
