# # Studies in Intelligent Systems and Cognitive Computing

Series Editors: Vijender Kumar Solanki, Vinit Kumar Gunjan and Mohammed Usman

This new book series will contain books on theory, applications, and design methods of Intelligent Systems and Intelligent Computing. Virtually all disciplines such as engineering, natural sciences, computer and information science, ICT, economics, business, e-commerce, environment, healthcare, life sciences will be covered.
