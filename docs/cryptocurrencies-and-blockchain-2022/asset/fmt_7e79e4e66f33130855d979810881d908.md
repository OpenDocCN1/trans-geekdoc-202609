
# Cryptocurrency Mining

## Transaction Confirmation & Network Protection

Confirmation of transactions is required. Protection against the introduction of misleading information into the network (fake transactions and blocks) and protection of the network against a variety of different forms of attacks are essential. Maintaining the decentralization of the network is essential.

As a result, the greater the number of mining devices in the network, the better the network's ability to defend itself against attacks. At the same time, the shutdown of a portion of the network's compute capacity will not result in a halt to transactions in the system. If at least one miner is actively mining, the network will continue to function.

## Mining Equipment: ASIC vs GPU

In the mining industry, there are many different kinds of mines. One of these options is starting a home farm.

Mining is currently carried out using specialized ASIC (application specific integrated circuit) or GPU (graphics processing unit) chips. ASIC stands for application-specific integrated circuit. It is a "soulless robot" that has been programmed to extract cryptocurrency coins. With regard to graphics processing unit mining, a typical farm consists of numerous video cards of the same brand, a powerful power supply, a motherboard with several PCI-Express slots, and a processor equipped with a cooling system. Coins operate on the basis of algorithms that are specific to each coin. These algorithms should be taken into consideration while selecting video cards.

### ASIC Mining

ASIC is simple to maintain and install, and it is also inexpensive. However, it is only designed to work with a specific set of coins, and you will not be able to change between them. If you choose to pay your electricity bills, you will need to mine successfully enough to cover the cost of the electronic equipment.

### GPU Mining

When it comes to GPU mining, unlike ASIC mining, you will be able to quickly and profitably sell your video card if you decide to discontinue mining at some point.

### Recommendation

From my own perspective, mining requires the use of both ASICs and GPUs. In the event that one of the devices fails, you can fall back on the second one.

## Cloud Mining

There is another option for those who do not want to get their hands dirty with the construction of a "money machine." You may mine from anywhere in the world by utilizing shared processing power. Cloud mining is the term used to describe this form of mining. Cloud mining, on the other hand, carries with it a number of risks. First and foremost, the payback period is deceptively short. Second, you have no control over your spending or profits. Even more importantly, none of the equipment is your own property.

When deciding whether or not to outsource mining to others, keep the following points in mind: you should not be offered an unrealistic profit forecast, and you should be given the option of contacting technical help. To learn more about these companies, I recommend that you read evaluations and keep an eye on the mining forums. I also recommend that you invest modest amounts of money for testing purposes before investing larger sums of money.

## Security Principles

In mining, like in trade, it is necessary to adhere to particular security regulations. The first rule of the so-called "mining club" is that you must not disclose the location of your fields to anyone. Is this a tidbit of information? You might believe that. Just remember that you are the only person who will be allowed access to the mining facility with your mining equipment.

The following are some more security principles:

*   Install a system to monitor indicators (temperature, humidity, etc.), as well as video surveillance and a fire suppression system, on the premises to ensure the safety of everyone.
*   Examine the electrical, power, and earthing systems.
*   Heat and humidity levels in the building should be monitored. Don't skimp on consumables, as doing so could result in a disappointing conclusion.
*   Mining is not for everyone, mostly because it requires the ability to negotiate with equipment providers, acquire and configure the equipment oneself, and then maintain and assure the equipment's security.

# ICO (Initial Coin Offering)

## Overview

An ICO (Initial Coin Offering) is the first time that tokens are offered for sale on the blockchain. It is a particular type of crowdsourcing model. Participants in an initial coin offering (ICO) contribute to the development of a project in exchange for future advantages. The initial coin offering (ICO) is frequently likened to the first public offering (IPO). However, unlike an initial public offering (IPO), participants in an initial coin offering (ICO) frequently do not obtain a stake in the firm and do not have the ability to influence internal management decisions.

## History & Notable Examples

It is thought that Mastercoin held the first initial coin offering (ICO) in June of 2013. The Bitcointalk forum announced the start of a crowdfunding campaign for the project on its website. As a result, the ICO Mastercoin raised more than 5,000 bitcoins during its initial coin offering.

However, the Ethereum Initial Coin Offering (ICO), which took place in 2014, was possibly one of the most powerful ICOs. The tokens were then auctioned for $0.30 each. Over the course of two years, their value on the exchange climbed to $12–$13, and they were already worth $390 in June of 2017.

## Risks & Scams

There are a plethora of initial coin offerings (ICOs) available these days. It seems like every day there are new ventures being announced, each with a positive outlook and a search for investors. The greater the number of such projects that are developed, the lower the likelihood that any of them will be successful. A large number of initial coin offerings (ICOs) are created solely for the purpose of defrauding investors. The number of such enterprises will only increase as more and more individuals become interested in generating quick money through initial coin offerings (ICOs).

## Bounty Programs

The developers of these firms have even discovered a way to gain the support of people who do not have the financial means to invest in initial coin offerings (ICOs). They were the ones who inspired the creation of the bounty program. It is an opportunity to obtain a portion of the project tokens without having to put up any of your own money. All that is required is for the project to become more widely known.

You may qualify for a bounty if you do any of the following:

*   Promote the project (through social media, email databases, and campaigns on the Bitcointalk forum) in order for potential investors to become aware of its existence.
*   Translate information regarding the ICO into different languages. Look for flaws in the project's design.
*   Create logos, pamphlets, and other informational materials (sometimes on a competitive basis).
*   Create mobile applications, wallets, and other software add-ons for smartphones and other mobile devices.
*   In the event that you possess the necessary abilities to perform at least one task from the list above, you have a good chance of earning a bounty.
