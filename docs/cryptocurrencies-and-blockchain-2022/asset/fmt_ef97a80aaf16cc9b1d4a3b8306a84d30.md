A very important challenge that can be solved by smart contracts in Ethereum is the question of how to make a bargain on the Internet when you don't have faith in the other side. What is the procedure?

A smart contract is a computerized transaction protocol that automates contract provisions. Most contracts allow one of the signatories to interpret the terms as he wishes. Smart contracts, on the other hand, are computer protocols designed to speed up, verify, or enforce contract negotiation and execution.

In this way, smart contracts enable the management of people's relationships on the Internet by allowing them to do the following:

*   Monitoring and executing agreements through the use of computer-controlled systems
*   Lowering the cost of execution
*   Contributing to the development of more dependable interpersonal relationships
*   Doing so without the assistance of a third party to carry out the conditions of the contract
*   A mathematical formulation as well as a clear logic of implementation are required
*   It is necessary to have an environment that enables complete automation of the execution
