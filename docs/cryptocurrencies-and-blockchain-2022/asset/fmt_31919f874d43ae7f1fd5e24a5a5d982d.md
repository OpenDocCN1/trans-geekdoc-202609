
# ICO Strategy and Investment Approaches

The following is the most important aspect of the ICO strategy, which I refer to as "startups." When it comes to funding initiatives, the cryptocurrency economy has created a very simple method for those who have unique and fascinating ideas to do so quickly and easily. They no longer have to pound the pavement, begging and persuading large corporations that investing in their idea is a profitable investment decision. These innovators and developers no longer have to do anything more than post a proposal on their website. Interested in their proposal? You can share the address of your wallet so that you can receive tokens if the idea proves to be a success. The risk is that you will have to put money aside right away in order to reap a potential benefit later on.

In the past, the rules of the game for initial coin offerings (ICOs) were rather straightforward. I give you one dollar, and you give me two tokens. Some initial coin offerings (ICOs) go to extreme lengths to attract investors.

Generally speaking, if you invest in an initial coin offering (ICO), it is possible that you will only be able to trade their tokens on Friday at midnight during the full moon, and only if you are a mermaid with a blue tail. Obviously, my story is a bit exaggerated, but it clearly illustrates the ambiguity of the rules that govern the majority of beginning businesses (ICOs).

It was not difficult to locate a grain of truth amid the diversity of ICOs when they first started half a year ago (since just a few of them appeared in a week and could be readily evaluated), but now new ICOs appear virtually every hour, and you don't have time to check them all.

What would I say to a newcomer who is tempted to try his luck with initial coin offerings (ICOs)?

First and foremost, you must have a firm grasp of the business area in which you intend to make a financial investment. If you are knowledgeable in the field, you can assess it and determine whether or not it has a chance of success. In addition, I recommend that you pay attention to what experienced investors and specialists have to say rather than what is posted on the ICO's official website. To the point that I would recommend that you avoid investing in any initial coin offering (ICO) that has not been featured in at least three professional analytical studies from acknowledged experts.

## PENNY STOCKS

When it comes to the stock market, the concept of penny stocks should be familiar to anyone who has even a passing interest in it. Generally speaking, these are shares of the corporation that no one wants or needs anymore. These shares, on the other hand, have one distinguishing feature: they are so inexpensive that nothing can prevent their value from skyrocketing tenfold. Such a spectacular leap can occur as a result of positive news, a small amount of market manipulation, or for any other reason.

So, what are the best ways to make use of this strategy? You purchase the most unusual and obscure coins available and then sit back and wait to see how the situation unfolds. During the course of the year, the value of some of your coins could rise. This is a situation in which you should sell your currency quickly. Suppose you invested in fifty coins. A rise in the price of even one super-cheap coin will be enough to make up for the loss of your whole investment portfolio.

This type of plan does not allow you to go deeply into the fundamentals of operating in the bitcoin industry. All that is required is that you purchase "trash" and sell it before the deadline. This method, on the other hand, is not within my purview because it resembles a casino. You don't need any special abilities or knowledge; all you need is luck. You have no control over what happens or how risks are managed because everything is dependent on good fortune.

## TRUST IN ME

This method is appropriate for you if you have no prior expertise in the bitcoin industry but have a substantial amount of money to invest. You must, however, be willing to take chances and give your money to people you do not know. Because the bitcoin business is not governed by any regulations, no one can ensure that a corporation or someone who has claimed to earn money for you would not take your money and flee to some exotic location.

There are a large number of businesses on the market that promise profit in exchange for confidence. Some companies, for example, provide mining contracts to those who wish to mine but do not have the financial resources to invest in all of the necessary equipment and space to do so. This is referred to as "cloud mining." Cryptocurrency funds are another sort of business entity. These businesses do not know how to mine, but they are skilled at predicting the value of promising coins.

The third type of mutual fund is an initial coin offering (ICO). These gentlemen spend their time researching and evaluating the various businesses on the market, in which they will subsequently invest your money. Finally, there is the option of trading your stock. When you delegate control of your money to someone else, they will perform the trading for you.

# CHAPTER 14. RISK AND MONEY MANAGEMENT

Money management is, without a doubt, one of the most significant topics in the world of investments. If you do not have a professional way of calculating the lot size, your account will be a non-starter no matter what trading strategy you choose. I am hopeful that we will be able to figure out how to quantify your risks by working together.

So, please take the time to carefully understand the basic guidelines of money management that I have established for the bitcoin market.

Ten percent of the risk is taken on in a single trade. That is, if you enter a single transaction, you should not take on more than 10% of the risk. If you make five transactions a day, you should divide your risk by five, which equals 10 percent of your total risk. Avoid making frequent transactions in an attempt to acquire more money. Paying commission fees to the exchange will cause you to lose a significant amount of money.
