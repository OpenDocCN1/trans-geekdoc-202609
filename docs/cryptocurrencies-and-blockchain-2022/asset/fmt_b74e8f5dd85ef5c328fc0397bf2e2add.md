In any other market, margin or leverage is an excellent instrument for increasing your profit margins. Leverage is used by almost all skilled traders. Nevertheless, as we previously stated, the Bitcoin market is extremely volatile, and so the use of leverage might literally kill you. What causes it to happen? Let's compare it to the stock market for a moment.

What if something happens, such as a 20 percent drop in Apple stock? What could be the cause? I don't believe anything of the sort will occur. As a result, it is relatively risk-free to trade Apple stock using leverage because the stock cannot abruptly fall by more than 10%. Meanwhile, you may always close your position in the stock market because trading is carefully regulated here, and you can keep track of all the events that are taking place in the market.

On the contrary, the cryptocurrency market is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Any development is feasible in this situation, including the most illogical and nonsensical, because the price can rise or fall by any percentage point. As a result, if you want to trade on margin in the Bitcoin market, you must use a leverage of 1:2. Trading with a larger degree of leverage will feel more like a game of Russian roulette. The majority of the time, if you trade with leverage of 1:14, your position will be completely annihilated.

Always proceed with caution: if you come across an exchange with a questionable interface or are offered 1:20 leverage, it is likely that this exchange is looking for marginal traders. It is on the lookout for traders who are greedy, crafty, and, as experience has shown, foolish, and who wish to raise their profits twenty-fold instantaneously. For this reason, I will tell you once more that if you decide to trade professionally, you should submit yourself for verification at the most reputable exchanges in the world. At the very least, you should open accounts with two of them.

When you are trading on margin, you should have two accounts: one for your money and another for the margin. Transferring money from your first account to a margin account and then trading from there is the best way to get started with leveraged trading.

In addition, you should be familiar with the following aspects of margin trading: Do you believe you will be compensated with money in exchange for nothing more than a "thank you"? Without a doubt, this is not the case. You will only be awarded money if you earn interest on it. You will pay more interest if, for example, you employ a leverage of 1:4 and trade not for the $10,000 you have but for $40,000, which means you will pay more in interest. It has the potential to grow at a rate of 1% to 2% per day. The interest accrues for a total of $30,000 in the situation under consideration. I'd like to remind you that you only have $10,000 in your account.

## Tips for Beginners

When trading on the stock market, look for assets whose value has dropped significantly. This is the primary technique I am using right now: purchasing undervalued assets or assets whose value has declined.

To close a trade, you should only place an order if you have managed to make a profit of more than 50% on your investment. You should close your position as soon as you have gained your 50% profit, because waiting for higher profits is extremely dangerous. I also do not propose accepting profits that are less than 10% of the whole investment. In my opinion, a minimum of 20 percent should be the minimum you strive for.

If you trade intraday, you can place sell orders with a profit margin of 30% or higher.

Don't forget that the exchange charges a commission fee for each transaction. The costs increase proportionally to the amount of trading you do. Furthermore, the greater your turnover, the lower your commission costs will be.

# Day Trading and Long-Term Position Trading

Focus on your job schedule on the cryptocurrency exchange for the time being, shall we?

Before you begin trading, you must make a decision on an extremely crucial topic, namely how much time you are willing to devote to the activity of trading. Of course, the most efficient method is to sit at a computer for 5–6 hours every day, continuously closing positions and keeping an eye on the overall situation. However, due to a shortage of time, the majority of people today opt for a different option. They swap one item a week with each other. On a particular day, such traders review all of the news for the week as well as any charts available, and then determine which positions to open or cancel based on their findings.

This is an option that many people choose, so here are some suggestions:

- Choose one certain day of the week that you will commit exclusively to trading and stick to it.
- On this day, go through your portfolio and see which currencies have gained in value and which have lost value.
- Keep an eye on the news.
- Determine your course of action based on current events and the composition of your portfolio. If the value of a particular asset has not increased, or has even decreased, then the position should be closed. If you see something that seems promising or that is becoming more affordable, get it. You should carry out these activities on each of the trade days that you have selected for the week in question. By the way, I propose that you use `Blockfolio` to make tracking your positions easier and less time-consuming.

To summarize, I recommend that you choose between two forms of trading: daily monitoring with limit sell orders or weekly monitoring with limit buy orders.

Meanwhile, certain cryptocurrency exchanges, such as `Bitfinex`, provide over-the-counter (OTC) trading services. Over The Counter (OTC) is an abbreviation that refers to a transaction that takes place over the counter. This is a decentralized market that does not have a central physical location where market participants exchange assets through the dealers' network of brokers and dealers.

For example, if you don't have time to keep up with the market and are totally committed to trading by placing orders, you can make use of the services provided by this market. Your collaboration will be conducted through the use of a mobile phone or an e-mail address. As a result, you tell a dealer that you, for example, want to purchase $250 worth of Bitcoin. After looking through the market, the dealer responds with an estimate of how much you should spend on this quantity of Bitcoins. This service will be provided at a cost by the dealer.
