## Chapter 1. Myths About Cryptocurrency and the Main Rules of the Cryptocurrency Market

Before we establish any goals for ourselves in the bitcoin business, let us first dispel some prevalent misconceptions about the cryptocurrency industry.

### Cryptocurrency Myths

My sincere confession: I was almost infected with skepticism about cryptocurrencies when I first started out on my path, as many people tried to scare me into believing that bitcoin was some sort of wildcat business, financial pyramid, or other such nonsense. Many people are discouraged from achieving their goals because of incorrect notions. Only a small minority of bitcoin adherents insist on independently verifying such claims rather than simply accepting them at face value.

I'm sure you've also heard claims that cryptocurrency will never be recognized by any country, and as a result, cryptocurrency will be obsolete in the near future. It is only necessary to recall the recent growth in the value of bitcoin in order to dispute this claim. Some governments have already recognized cryptocurrencies as a legitimate mode of payment, including the United States. As a result, what was formerly referred to as "entertainment for geeks" has turned into a very real industry for investment bankers and other organizations.

As a result, if you are still unable to generate income from cryptocurrencies, it is most likely due to the vast majority of the aforementioned comments made by pseudo-experts.

Let's go over some of the comments that cause many individuals to stop in their tracks:

1. It is far too hazardous. What if I make a financial investment and cryptocurrency is outlawed in all countries the next day?
2. I have no financial education, so I wouldn't know what I was getting myself into.
3. A friend, brother, or neighbor invested in cryptocurrency and lost everything;
4. The concept of cryptocurrencies is still too new and difficult to grasp at this point, so I'd rather wait a few years till everything becomes obvious.

All of these comments are nothing more than justifications for your inaction. You can keep making excuses while others all over the world have already established a business in the bitcoin market and are reaping the benefits of their efforts. Consider the following: what distinguishes me as being worse than these people? If you notice that you are not worse off than your friend, sibling, or neighbor who has already made money in the bitcoin market, you should consider the following question: why should I enter the world of cryptocurrency companies at this time?

### Why You Should Enter the Cryptocurrency Business World Now

As a result, I shall respond to this question point-by-point. To help you better comprehend why you should do something now, each of the arguments will begin with the phrase "right now."

First and foremost, there is relatively little competition in the market at the moment. Are you surprised by this? Yes, it is correct. Yes, there is a steady increase in interest in this topic, but in general, there is still a lot of rivalry in this field. This market is still considered "wild" and untamed.

The reason for this is that, as we have already indicated, the majority of people are wary of new trends and endeavors, which is understandable.

Second, you can earn substantial profits in this market at the moment. Even if cryptocurrency prices are still in the early stages of growth and development, you can still make a good amount of money. Profits can be made as long as risks are managed prudently and in a timely manner.

Third, there are a large number of freeloaders on the market at the moment. They come to the cryptocurrency market in order to make a quick profit without diving too deeply into the issue of cryptocurrencies. Typically, such individuals are recruited through pyramid schemes run by self-proclaimed "experts" who instruct others despite having little or no actual knowledge of the subject matter. I must acknowledge that the market favored such individuals in the beginning, when they stood a good chance of making a lot of money at the time they did so. However, in today's world, if you want to accomplish something similar in the bitcoin market, you will have to put forth significant work.
