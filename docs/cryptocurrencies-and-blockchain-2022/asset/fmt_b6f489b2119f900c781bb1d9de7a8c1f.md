### The Way of the Samurai

The first investment technique is referred to as "The Way of the Samurai" or "Just Buy Bitcoin." Consequently, to implement this technique, all you need to do is purchase Bitcoin.

All things considered, this method is sound. It is straightforward to put into action, and you will not be required to delve into the complexities of economic or technological principles.

This method has the potential to provide the greatest return. Despite this, there are hazards associated with this method, which I'll explain in more detail later.

### Follow the Money

In this case, I recommend that you purchase a particular amount of different coins from among the top ten greatest cryptocurrencies available today. To put it another way, you will acquire numerous market leaders. The coins, whose value will surge as a result of the failure of the other coins, will make up for the losses caused by the failure of the other coins.

This method, however, entails significant dangers. Nothing can shield you from the possibility that coins that have appeared to be constant in value thus far could face a significant decline in value the following year.

It's important to remember that, at the moment, there are no fundamental reasons to prevent Bitcoin from failing, which, for example, could be caused by the inability to survive the competition from other cryptocurrencies. For the simple reason that Bitcoin is one of the least technically sophisticated cryptocurrencies, this is a very plausible possibility.

Because of this, if you do not want to become a victim of the market, do not throw all of your eggs in one basket, as I did five years ago, says the author. ### Trading

Despite the fact that many people think of trading as being about analysis, patterns, and technical modeling, the trader's job is primarily about making a subjective judgement of what is happening in the market.

In addition to consuming a glass of whiskey (smile), competent traders should make predictions about which cryptocurrencies will gain in value in the market. Following that, traders must forecast when the optimum time to enter the market is likely to occur. What comes next? They should continue to make predictions about when coin prices will begin to plummet in value.

The most significant aspect of trading, on the other hand, is having a tremendous amount of expertise, as well as a little bit of luck. A trader must also perform all of the actions listed above on a consistent basis. This is an issue since things do not always turn out as planned, yet risks must be accepted on a consistent basis.

Therefore, in my opinion, trading is best suited to people who have an engineering mentality, who have a lot of free time, and who are also strong in their faith and values.
