Recurrent Neural Networks Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) are a type of deep learning model that excel at processing sequential and time-series data. They have loops within their architecture, allowing them to maintain a form of memory to capture information from previous inputs. RNNs are widely used in natural language processing, speech recognition, and handwriting recognition due to their ability to analyze sequences and make predictions based on context.

Generative Adversarial Networks Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) are a unique class of deep learning models that involve two neural networks, the generator and the discriminator, engaged in a competitive training process. The generator creates data samples trying to mimic a given dataset, while the discriminator aims to distinguish between real and generated data. Through this adversarial training, GANs can generate realistic data, making them valuable for tasks such as image generation, style transfer, and data augmentation.

Long Short-Term Memory Networks Long Short-Term Memory Networks (LSTMs) are a specialized type of recurrent neural network that addresses the vanishing gradient problem. LSTMs are well-suited for capturing long-term dependencies in sequential data due to their gating mechanisms, which enable the network to remember or forget information over extended sequences. LSTMs find applications in machine translation, speech recognition, and sentiment analysis, in which understanding context over long sequences is crucial.
