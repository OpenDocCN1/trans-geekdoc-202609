Incorporating homomorphic encryption is another powerful technique for preserving privacy when sharing data at the edge. This encryption scheme allows computations to be performed on encrypted data directly, without the need to decrypt it. Thus, sensitive information remains encrypted throughout the processing, ensuring that privacy is maintained at all stages.

In summary, a combination of differential privacy, federated learning, blockchain-based protocols, zero-knowledge proofs, and homomorphic encryption offers a robust toolkit for preserving privacy when sharing data at the edge. These techniques enable the development of advanced applications that leverage the benefits of edge computing while upholding the privacy and data security of individuals.
