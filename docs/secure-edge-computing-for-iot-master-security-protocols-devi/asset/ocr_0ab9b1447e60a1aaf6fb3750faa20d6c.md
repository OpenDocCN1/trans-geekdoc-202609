The primary emphasis of the end-to-end edge architecture outlined in this chapter is to ensure the security of the edge. Security, a complex field, typically centers on the fundamental principles of confidentiality, integrity, and availability.

In the context of cattle monitoring usage models, our focus is on security aspects that are aligned with various device management life cycles. Key capabilities crucial for securing the edge include Hardware Root of Trust and TPM 2.0 (Trusted Platform Module). This is followed by Identity and Access Management (IAM), Secure Edge Gateway, safeguarding data-at-rest, securing data-in-transit, ensuring data-in-use is protected, implementing data loss prevention, and establishing robust monitoring practices. While these objectives are broad, the subsequent section will delve deeper to provide more detailed insights.
