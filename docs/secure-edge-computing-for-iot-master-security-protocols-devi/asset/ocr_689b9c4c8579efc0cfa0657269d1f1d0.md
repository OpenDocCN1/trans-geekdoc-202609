In the context of edge security, the concept of zero-trust becomes even more critical. Edge devices often operate in untrusted or hostile environments, making it imperative to validate and authenticate every communication. Key management plays a pivotal role in implementing zero-trust principles by ensuring that only authorized entities have access to sensitive data and resources. This approach involves continuous verification of identities and the establishment of secure communication channels through effective key management practices.

In conclusion, key management in edge systems is a multifaceted challenge that requires innovative solutions tailored to the unique characteristics of decentralized computing. Lightweight cryptography decentralized key distribution mechanisms and a zero-trust security model are pivotal elements for safeguarding the integrity and confidentiality of data in the dynamic and distributed landscape of edge computing. As the edge ecosystem continues to evolve, the development of advanced and adaptive key management strategies will be instrumental in securing the future of edge computing.
