# # The Origin of the Term ‘Internet of Things’

In the 1990s, the term “Internet of Things” was coined by Kevin Ashton, a British technology pioneer, during his work at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. He envisioned a future in which everyday objects could be embedded with sensors, communicate with each other through the internet, collect data, and perform actions without human intervention. The concept gained traction, and with advancements in miniaturization and wireless communication, IoT began to take shape in various industries.
