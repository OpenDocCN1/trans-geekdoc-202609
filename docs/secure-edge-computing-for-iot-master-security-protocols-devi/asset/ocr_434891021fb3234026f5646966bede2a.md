Robust governance and oversight mechanisms are also pivotal in upholding ethical standards in edge AI. Governments, industry bodies, and academia need to collaborate to establish frameworks and regulations that promote responsible AI usage. Continuous monitoring, auditing, and regular updates to these guidelines are essential to keep pace with the rapidly evolving AI landscape.

Finally, promoting education and awareness about the implications and potential impacts of edge AI is key to fostering responsible use. Educating users, developers, and stakeholders about the ethical considerations, risks, and benefits associated with AI in edge scenarios can lead to a more informed and responsible approach to AI deployment, ultimately contributing to a more ethical and inclusive AI ecosystem.
