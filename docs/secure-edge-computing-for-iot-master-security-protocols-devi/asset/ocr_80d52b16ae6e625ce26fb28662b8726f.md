In a rapidly changing world, the fusion of real-time data processing and video analytics for surveillance systems at the edge stands as a testament to human ingenuity, reshaping the way we perceive and ensure security.

The following is a process flow for implementing video analytics at the edge for surveillance systems:

Select Suitable Hardware: Choose cameras or edge devices that are capable of performing video analytics tasks. These devices should have enough processing power, memory, and storage to handle the analytics workload.

Choose Video Analytics Algorithms: Decide on the specific video analytics algorithms that you want to implement at the edge. These could include motion detection, object detection, facial recognition, license plate recognition, people counting, and more. Choose algorithms that align with your surveillance goals.

Data Collection: Cameras capture video streams, which are then sent to the edge devices. Ensure that the cameras are properly placed to cover the desired surveillance areas effectively.

Pre-Processing: Before running analytics, perform pre-processing on the video data. This might involve tasks such as resizing, normalization, noise reduction, and converting the video into appropriate formats for analysis.
