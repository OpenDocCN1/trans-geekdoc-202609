Mitigating the risk of Mirai-like botnet attacks in edge computing requires a multi-faceted approach. To begin with, manufacturers of IoT devices must prioritize security in their designs, and implement measures to prevent default or easily guessable credentials. Regular security audits and updates should be mandatory for the entire lifecycle of these devices. On the side of operation, network segmentation and access control mechanisms must be enforced within the edge computing environment to minimize the potential impact of a compromised device. Furthermore, leveraging artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms at the edge can help in real-time threat detection and response, adding an extra layer of security to the ecosystem. Overall, the Mirai botnet attack served as a crucial lesson in fortifying the burgeoning realm of edge computing against evolving cyber threats.
