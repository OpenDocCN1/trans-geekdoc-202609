Privacy and Security Challenges: We will address the inherent privacy and security challenges posed by edge analytics, including data protection, encryption, and secure model deployment, and explore strategies to mitigate these concerns.

Edge-Analytics-Cloud Continuum: We will investigate the symbiotic relationship between edge, analytics, and cloud computing, and explore scenarios in which data is aggregated, refined, and correlated across these layers to create holistic insights.

Edge-Analytics Ecosystem: We will dive into the emerging ecosystem of tools, frameworks, and platforms that support edge analytics and machine learning deployments. We will also evaluate the trade-offs between edge-specific solutions and adapting traditional methodologies.

Case Studies and Applications: We will showcase real-world case studies highlighting the transformative potential of edge analytics and machine learning across diverse domains, such as retail, energy management, agriculture, and smart cities.

Ethical Considerations: We will explore the ethical implications related to edge analytics, including data ownership, algorithmic bias, and transparency. We will also discuss best practices for responsible deployment of edge-based machine learning systems.
