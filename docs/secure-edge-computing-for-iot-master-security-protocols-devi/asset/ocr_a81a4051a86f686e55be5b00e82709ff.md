# Runtime Application Self-Protection (RASP) at the Edge

Runtime Application Self-Protection (RASP) at the edge is a cutting-edge cybersecurity approach that takes real-time security to a new level. By deploying RASP technology at the edge of a network, organizations can protect their applications and data from threats, as close to the source as possible. This approach offers a proactive defense mechanism that actively monitors and safeguards applications while they are running, ensuring that any potential vulnerabilities or attacks are detected and mitigated in real-time. With RASP at the edge, security is not limited to a specific perimeter, but is distributed throughout the network, enhancing protection, and reducing the attack surface.

One of the key advantages of RASP at the edge is its ability to provide context-aware security. By inspecting and analyzing application behavior in real-time, it can distinguish between normal and malicious activities, adapting its protection measures accordingly. This contextual awareness allows RASP to provide tailored security, reducing false positives, and ensuring that legitimate users can access the applications without unnecessary friction. It is a dynamic and self-learning approach, constantly evolving to address emerging threats and vulnerabilities, making it a robust defense strategy for the ever-evolving threat landscape.
