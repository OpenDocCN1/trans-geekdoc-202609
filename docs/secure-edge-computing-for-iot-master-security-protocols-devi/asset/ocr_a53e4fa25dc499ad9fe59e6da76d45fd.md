# Application of AI at the Edge: Common Use Cases

Anomaly Detection: Anomaly detection is the art of uncovering the unexpected within a sea of normalcy. Like a vigilant sentinel, it scours through data to uncover patterns that deviate from the established norms. Whether it is detecting fraudulent transactions in financial data, identifying equipment malfunctions in industrial settings, or spotting unusual patterns in network traffic, anomaly detection acts as a guardian of integrity. Its algorithms dance between the lines of predictability and peculiarity, sifting out rare and often crucial occurrences that may otherwise remain concealed. In a world in which the unknown can be both a threat and an opportunity, anomaly detection stands as a vigilant gatekeeper to the realm of hidden possibilities.

Object Recognition: The world is a canvas painted with an intricate array of objects, each carrying its own significance and story. Object recognition, a triumph of artificial intelligence, grants to machines the power to appreciate this visual symphony. Like a curious explorer, it peers through the lens of data — be it images or videos — deciphering the contours, colors, and contexts that define objects. It is as if the machines, once blind to the beauty that surrounds us, are now equipped with virtual eyes. They recognize the wagging tail of a loyal companion, the vibrant petals of a rose, and the solemn silhouette of an ancient monument. Object recognition is not just about labeling, but a bridge between the human experience and the digital realm, allowing technology to interpret the visual poetry of our world.
