Common clustering algorithms include K-means, hierarchical clustering, and DBSCAN. Applications of clustering range from customer segmentation in marketing to image segmentation in computer vision.

Association: Association is another type of unsupervised learning in which the focus is on identifying relationships and patterns between variables in the data. It aims to discover rules that describe the co-occurrence of items within the dataset. An example of association mining is the Apriori algorithm, which is often used in market basket analysis to understand purchasing behavior and recommend related products to customers.

Dimensionality Reduction: Dimensionality reduction techniques are vital for simplifying and visualizing complex data by reducing the number of features or dimensions, while retaining the essential information. Principal Component Analysis (PCA) and t-SNE (t-distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding) are popular methods used for dimensionality reduction. These techniques are crucial for visualizing high-dimensional data and improving the efficiency of other machine learning algorithms.

Generative Modeling: Generative modeling involves learning the underlying structure of the data to generate new, similar data points. Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) and Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) are examples of generative models. VAEs learn a probabilistic mapping from the data space to a latent space, enabling the generation of new data points. GANs consist of two neural networks, a generator, and a discriminator, competing to produce realistic data.
