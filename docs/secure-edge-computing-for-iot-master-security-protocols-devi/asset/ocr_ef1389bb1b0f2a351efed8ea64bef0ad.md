# Water Quality Monitoring

IoT sensors in water bodies such as rivers and lakes continuously monitor water quality parameters such as pH, turbidity, and pollutants. This information aids in the early detection of pollution, and ensures the safety of water resources.

# Environmental Monitoring and Conservation

IoT devices are deployed in various environmental monitoring applications, to assess and safeguard natural resources. Sensors placed in forests, oceans, and wildlife habitats collect data on temperature, humidity, pollution levels, and animal behavior. This information aids researchers and conservationists in understanding ecosystem dynamics, tracking endangered species, and detecting environmental threats.
