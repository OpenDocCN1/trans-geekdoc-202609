# Introduction

This chapter serves as a compass for navigating the frontier of edge computing cybersecurity and cryptography, two inseparable guardians of the digital realm. Just as the edge of a network represents the periphery of connectivity, it also stands as the forefront of vulnerability. As we delve into the intricacies of securing decentralized computing environments, the symbiotic relationship between cutting-edge technologies and the cryptographic fortifications that safeguard them becomes abundantly clear.

As we explore the nuances of edge computing, the tapestry of cybersecurity unfolds. In a world in which cyber threats are as diverse as the devices we depend on, a robust security posture is non-negotiable. This chapter unravels the intricacies of edge-centric cybersecurity, examining the unique challenges posed by distributed systems, and unveiling innovative approaches to fortify the digital perimeter.

Amidst the digital tumult, cryptography emerges as the silent sentinel standing guard against malicious forces. From encrypting sensitive data in transit to securing communication channels at the edge, cryptographic algorithms form the backbone of a resilient defense. This section explores the cryptographic underpinnings of edge computing, shedding light on how these mathematical constructs enable secure transactions, data integrity, and confidentiality in the decentralized landscape.
