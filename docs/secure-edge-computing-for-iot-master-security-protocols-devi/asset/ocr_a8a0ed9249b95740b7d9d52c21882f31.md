# Case Studies and Applications

Our use case is Cow Health Tracking using Internet of Cows technology. The cows are located in West Africa, East Europe, and USA, and our company is hired to track and monitor the health of the cows, using ear tags. The ear tags are used to track the vital signs of the cow. Each location has 200 cows and 100 bulls, except for West Africa, which has 250 Cows and 50 Bulls.

The technical team is located in the USA, in Texas, where the USA farm is located. 350 ear tag IoT devices have been shipped to each location, and numbered by the manufacturer from the factory. The farm in West Africa is located in Southwest Nigeria, where internet traffic is not constantly stable, therefore a new edge solution has been crafted to mitigate the internet issue.
