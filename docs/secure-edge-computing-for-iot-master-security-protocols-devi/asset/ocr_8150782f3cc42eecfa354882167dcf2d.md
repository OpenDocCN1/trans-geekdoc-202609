# Trade-Offs between Accuracy and Efficiency

Trade-offs between accuracy and efficiency at the edge are a fundamental challenge in the realm of edge computing, in which computational capabilities are deployed closer to the data source. Achieving high accuracy often demands complex algorithms and deep learning models, leading to increased computational and energy requirements. However, in edge environments with limited resources such as power, processing capacity, and memory, striving for high accuracy can strain the efficiency and timeliness of the system.

Efficiency at the edge is paramount for real-time decision-making and responsiveness. Simplifying algorithms and using lightweight models can enhance efficiency, enabling faster processing and reduced energy consumption. However, this simplification often comes at the cost of reduced accuracy, making it essential to strike a balance.
