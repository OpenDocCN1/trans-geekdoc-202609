# The Mainstream Adoption of IoT

Throughout the early 2000s, advancements in wireless communication, sensor technology, and cloud computing propelled the growth of IoT applications. Industries, such as healthcare, transportation, and agriculture began to harness the power of IoT for improved efficiency and data-driven decision-making. Smart home devices, such as thermostats, lightbulbs, and security systems, also gained popularity, bringing IoT into people’s daily lives.
