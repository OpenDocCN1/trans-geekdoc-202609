# Sensors in IoT Architecture

Sensors are the fundamental components of Internet of Things (IoT) architecture, and play a crucial role in collecting real-world data. These devices are responsible for capturing various types of information, such as temperature, humidity, motion, light, and more, from the physical environment. The data collected by sensors serves as the foundation for making informed decisions and driving actionable insights in IoT applications.

# Types of Sensors:

Temperature Sensors: These measure ambient temperature, and are commonly used in climate control systems, weather monitoring, and industrial applications.

Humidity Sensors: These monitor the level of humidity in the environment, and find applications in agriculture, indoor climate control, and storage facilities.

Motion Sensors: These detect movement and orientation changes, and are utilized in security systems, home automation, and wearable devices.

Light Sensors: These measure the intensity of light, and are used in lighting automation, outdoor lighting, and photography.
