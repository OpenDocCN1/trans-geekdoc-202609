# Objectives

This chapter aims to unravel the intricate web of security challenges woven within the tapestry of IoT and edge computing. By examining the convergence of these transformative technologies, readers will gain a clear perspective on the vulnerabilities arising at the crossroads of data-generating devices and decentralized computing resources. The primary objective is to equip readers with a nuanced understanding of the threat landscape, enabling them to comprehend the potential risks posed to both individual privacy and systemic stability.

Through the exploration of real-world case studies and emerging scenarios of threat, the chapter will delve into the dynamic nature of security concerns in this domain. By the end of this chapter, readers will be able to discern the complexities of securing diverse IoT devices and the distributed computing fabric of the edge. Furthermore, a comprehensive analysis of cutting-edge security frameworks and encryption protocols will empower readers with practical insights to fortify IoT and edge computing deployments against cyber threats, ensuring data confidentiality, system resilience, and user trust. The ultimate goal is to foster an informed approach to security, enabling stakeholders to embark on the IoT and edge journey with confidence, knowing how to mitigate risks effectively.
