In this uncharted territory, a new era of cybersecurity must emerge, one that embraces flexibility, decentralization, and resilience. It is a frontier in which the evolution of security will be as fascinating as the technology it protects, shaping the future of connected systems, and the way in which we interact with the digital world at its periphery.
