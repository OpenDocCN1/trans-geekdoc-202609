# Introduction

In this chapter, we embark upon a journey through the ever-evolving
landscape of IoT and Edge computing security, navigating the intricate
interplay between cutting-edge technology and the imperative to
safeguard our data, devices, and digital lives. The network of
interconnected devices has seamlessly integrated itself into our homes,
cities, industries, and beyond. From smart thermostats that adjust our
living environments to wearable health monitors that track our vital signs,
IoT has brought unparalleled convenience to our daily lives. However, this
convenience is accompanied by an intricate web of security concerns.
How do we protect the troves of sensitive data generated by these
devices? How can we ensure the privacy of individuals as they interact
with smart gadgets that monitor their every move? These questions form
the heart of the IoT security dilemma, and we shall delve deep into their
answers.

Edge computing, the nimble counterpart to cloud computing,
complements the IoT by processing data closer to the source, reducing
latency and enhancing real-time decision-making. It empowers
autonomous vehicles to navigate busy streets, factories to optimize
production, and healthcare systems to provide instant diagnostics. Yet, as
we push the boundaries of computational capabilities to the edge, we must
also grapple with the challenges of securing distributed networks, on
which vulnerabilities lurk just beyond the horizon.
