
## AI-based infrastructures

`Augmented Reality`—technology that superimposes virtual images and information in the real world.
`Blockchain` is a distributed ledger technology that allows transactions and information to be recorded securely and immutably.
`Chatbots`—a technology capable of simulating a conversation, understanding, and automatically answering questions.
`Service and Social Robots`—a technology presented as mechanical devices capable of performing autonomously or semi-autonomously programmable tasks.
`Virtual Reality`—a technology that creates an immersive environment where users can interact realistically with virtual objects and people.
