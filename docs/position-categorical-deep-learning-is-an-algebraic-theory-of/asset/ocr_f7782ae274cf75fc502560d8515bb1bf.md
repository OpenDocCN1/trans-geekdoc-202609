**Example 1.3 (Folding RNN),** Consider the endofunctor $O \times -$ from Example H.4. Via strength $\sigma_{P,X}: P \times (O \times X) \to O \times P \times X$ we can form the 2-endofunctor $Para(O \times -): Para(Set) \to Para(Set)$. Then an unfolding recurrent neural network arises as its algebra.

More concretely, a coalgebra consists of the carrier $S$, and a parametric map $(P, (\text{cell}_o, \text{cell}_s)) \in Para(Set)(S \times O)$.
