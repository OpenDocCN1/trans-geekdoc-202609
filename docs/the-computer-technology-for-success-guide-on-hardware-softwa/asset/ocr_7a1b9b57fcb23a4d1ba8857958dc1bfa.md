# Information Technology and education:

The application of information & communication technology in education has significantly changed the traditional process of teaching and learning. Gone are the days of classrooms with blackboards. Today all educational institutions imparting knowledge by using various modern gadgets. Students with access to the internet have a whole world of research material at their disposal, which helps them grasp concepts efficiently and without wasting any time.

# Creation of new jobs:

The rising demand of IT professionals across businesses is creating new opportunities every day. Several emerging economies all over the world are now setting new records by strengthening their competence in this field. A greater number of people are showing interest in jobs like computer programming, system analysis, testing, software and hardware development and web application design.
