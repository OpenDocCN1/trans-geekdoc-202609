Construction of the single-cell sequencing platform suitable for high-resolution landscape construction, we built UATAC-seq technology. In UATAC-seq, we used a novo transposase to incorporate into the bounds of open genomic regions (Figure 1A). After end-split, the second round of DNA was handled via ligation and hybridization, followed by another round of ligation with a temperature-controlled transposase to add to both ends of reads (Figures 1A and 1B). We then used gap-fill and ligation to prepare the template and then PCR to incorporate the fourth round of barcodes and generate the sequencing library. The sequence quality is detailed in Table S1.
