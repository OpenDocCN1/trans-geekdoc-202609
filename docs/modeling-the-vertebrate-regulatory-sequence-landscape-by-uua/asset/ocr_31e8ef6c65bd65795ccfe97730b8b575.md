For Tn5 primers were dissolved in Annealing Buffer, 1 µL aliquots of Tn5_primerA and 1 µL of Tn5_primerB (see Table S1 for details) were incorporated into individual wells of 96-well free plates, generating a total of 192 unique barcodes distributed across two plates. The annealing reaction was performed as follows: denaturation at 95°C for 3 minutes, followed by a gradual temperature reduction at a controlled rate of -0.1°C/s through sequential incubation steps at 75°C for 15 minutes, 60°C for 10 minutes, 50°C for 10 minutes, 40°C for 10 minutes, and 25°C for 30 minutes. The annealed primer complexes were stored at 4°C until required for sequencing.

For Tn5 transposition assembly, 48 µL of Transpose Enzyme (2µg/mL), synthesized by Vazyme (cat.S1110-01) was combined with 18 µL of Coupling Buffer, and 12 µL of mixture were distributed to each of the annealed adapter plates. The 96-well plates were sealed and centrifuged at 300 x g for 5 minutes at 4°C. The plates were placed on a rotary mixer at 10 rpm and incubated at temperature (22-25°C) for 10 minutes. The reaction was stopped after 60 minutes to complete adapter, by adding 5 µL of Stop Buffer Vazyme Biotech Co., Ltd. which was added to each well. The resulting product, denoted as Tn5 Transposase Assembly (TEA), was stored at -20°C until one year.

# Sample Collection from Mouse, Chicken, Goose, Axolotl, and Zebrafish

Organs and tissues were collected from 5 mice, eight-week old female mouse, one-week male mouse, two-year-old female chicken, one-year-old female goose, three-year-old male mice, and 16-week-old zebrafish (4 females and 4 males).

For mouse tissue, the animals were first washed and subjected to anesthesia via intravenous injection of 1% sodium perobarbital for mobility. The tissues were dissected under perfusion with 20-30 µL of PBS-Buffered Saline (PBS). Organs were collected in the following sequence: stomach, spleen, pancreas, lymph nodes (nodal, abdominal aorta, carotid, and cervical nerves), thyroid, muscle, diaphragm, cerebellum, brainencephalon, brainstem, pituitary, tencephalon, thymus, heart, liver, kidney, adrenal, ovary and uterus (in females), bladder, prostate, and testis (in males), muscle (hind legs), and bone marrow was extracted from the femur and tibia. Residual muscle was removed from the bone site. The bone marrow shaft, which was then flushed with PBS/2mM EDTA using a 1 mL syringe with a 26 gauge needle. The marrow was collected in a tube.

The organs were then collected: aorta, brain, cerebellum, brainstem, cecum, cloaca, endocrine gland, glandular stomach, thymus, and pancreas. For zebrafish, fish were anesthetized with an interperitoneal injection of 1% sodium perobarbital and subjected to transfection with 20-30 µL of PBS. # Nuclease Inclusion from Tissues

For all tissues except bone marrow, samples were frozen with cold (0.8x PBS) dry ice-wrapped paper, and manually pulverized using mortar and pestle. The pulverized tissues were suspended in 1 mL of NIB and incubated for 1.5 to 3 minutes to allow lysis. For mouse, the cell pellet was directly resuspended in 1 mL of NIB and incubated for 2 minutes.
