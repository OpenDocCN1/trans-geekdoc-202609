# Nuclei Isolation from Cultured Cells

2T3 and 293T cells were cultured in Dulbeco's Medium (DMEM, ThermoFisher) with 10% FBS. Cells were harvested by trypsinization, ensuring that no more than 1 million cells were collected and resuspended in 1 mL of PBS. The cell suspension was centrifuged at 300 g for 5 minutes at 4 °C. The resulting pellet of 293T cells was reuspended in 1 mL of PBS and incubated for 5 minutes to release nuclei. The nuclei suspensions were then transferred into 15 mL centrifuge tubes with 5 mL of pre-washed WB, filtered through a 40 µm strainer, and centrifuged at 500 g for 5 minutes at 4 °C. The nuclei were washed with 1 mL of WB. After centrifugation, the nuclei were resuspended in an appropriate volume of PBS-Mg to achieve a concentration of 5 million nuclei per µL. A mixed cell population was prepared to confirm the rating of UATAC-seq, equal volumes of 2T3 and 293T nuclei suspensions, both at the same concentration, and combined and thoroughly.

# Tn5 Tagging of Open Chromatin (First round)

A tagmentation buffer was prepared by combining 5 µL of 2x buffer (10 mM MgCl2 and 20% dimethylformamide in 10 mM Tris-HCl, pH 7.5), 5 µL of PBS, 0.1 mL of detergent, and 0.1 mL of 10% Tween-20. A total of 11 mL of this was added to each well containing 100,0000 cells. The Tn5 tagging was initiated by adding 2 µL of ligated TTE. The wells were sealed and centrifuged at 300 g for 5 minutes at 4 °C to achieve complete homogenization of the reaction mixture. Next, 2 µL of nuclei-nucleus suspension (containing 10,000 nuclei per µL) was added to each well to initiate Tn5 tagging. The reactions were incubated at 55 °C for 30 minutes on a rotary mixer at 10 rpm to facilitate extension. To stop the reaction, 15 mL of 40 mM ATAC and PBS (0.5% Tween-20 in PBS) were added to each well. The reactions were incubated for 15 minutes. The supernatant was removed and the pellet was resuspended in 1 mL of PBS to remove any residual reagent mix. This washing step was repeated twice to ensure complete removal.

The scale of the tagmentation experiment, ensuring genome volumes and reaction numbers (types of TTE), can be proportionally scaled up or down according to the quantity and target yield of single-nuclei data required for subsequent analysis.

# Adapter Isolation (Second round)

For the second round of indexing, 1 µL of 10 mM Buffer (dissolved in 10 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.0) was dispensed into each well of 96-well plate using a multi-channel pipette. Then, 5 µL of 78 HA antibody (with 78 unique barcodes, dissolved in 10 mM Tris-HCl, pH 8.0) was added to each sequence. The wells were sealed and centrifuged at 300 g for 30 seconds to ensure thorough mixing. The wells were incubated using the antibody for 90 minutes at 37 °C (75 °C for 1 min; 60 °C for 2 min; 50 °C for 20 min; 25 °C for 15 min), and a final hold at 4 °C with a cooling rate of -0.1 ℃/min.

For the ligation step, the pellet was resuspended in a reaction-ready max buffer containing 38 µL of 0.1 U of DNA ligase (NEB), 768 µL of 5% PEG8000 and 498 µL of PBS. 3 µL of this mixture was aliquated into each well containing the annealed oligos, covering a total of 78 reactions. The eight plates were incubated at 16 °C for 2 hours using the cycling incubator. The reactions were then incubated at room temperature for 5 minutes. Next, 10 µL of cold PFBS was added to each well, and each was placed in a 5 mL centrifuge tube. The tube was centrifuged at 600 g for 5 min at 4 °C. Finally, the nuclei were washed twice with PFBS as described to remove any residual ligation reagents.

The scale of the ligation experiment, ensuring genome volumes and reaction numbers, can be proportionally based on the sample size and the desired single-nuclei data yield for downstream analysis.
