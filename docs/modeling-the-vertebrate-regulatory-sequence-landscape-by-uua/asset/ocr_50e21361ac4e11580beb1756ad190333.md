Important, diversity in the number of open regions, we found that some individual regions are conserved among vertebrates, with the size distribution centered at ~250 bp for most of the open regions. With 1 kb distance to the TSS, we observed two patterns of regions: with ~250 bp and the other ~900 bp for most of the vertebrates. The ~900 bp regions are located at the TSS of different genes (Figure S-D), suggesting that the motifs that contribute to the functional unit of these two types of CREs. Motif enrichment analysis indicates that the open regions are enriched with housekeeping motifs related to the core transcriptional program, including SP1. The ~250 bp regions in the promoters are enriched with CTCF and lineage-related motifs (Table S5). While the ~250 bp regions are within 1 kb of some other open regions in both size and enrichment (Figure S-E), the open regions are enriched with H3K4me3, while the ~250 bp regions are enriched with H3K41 (Figure S-F). It can be seen that there are two main classes of open regions in vertebrates: the ~900 bp regions are shared among the classical promoters, and the ~250 bp regions correspond to classical enhancers/repressors. Our results are in agreement with previous functional characterization of CREs using CRISPR-i.
