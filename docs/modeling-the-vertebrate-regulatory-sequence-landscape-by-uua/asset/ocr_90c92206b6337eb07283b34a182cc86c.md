# EXPERIMENTAL METHOD AND STUDY PARTICIPANT DETAILS

Animals All animals studied followed protocols by the Animal Ethics Committee of Zurich University. All experiments conform to the relevant standards of Zurich University Laboratory Animal.

For wild-type mice, one-week-old male and one-week-old female C57BL/ mice were obtained from Genepath Co., Ltd and maintained at Animal Center of Zurich in a Specific Pathogen Free (SPF) facility with individually caged.

For wild-zebrafish, 4-week-old male and 16-week-old female zebrafish rerio strain ABZ zebrafish were maintained at At Core Facilities, Zurich University School of Medicine.

For wild-type chickens, one-~2-year-old female (Gallus gallus) were obtained by whole genome mapping (genome mapping rate: 95.8% to Gallus gallus; 94.6% to Gallus gallus; 85.6% to Bambusicolor).

For wild-type geckos, one-~year-old leopard gecko (Eublepharis macularis) was obtained from Citizen Reptile Breeding Base. The strain was verified by whole genome mapping analyses (genome mapping rate: 97.33% to Eublepharis macularis; 63.92% to Coleryny eggs; 60.36% to Gekko japonicus; 61.96% to Paedoera picta; 58.97% to Hemidactylus; 60.63% to Leptodactylus later; 59.50% to Herpetonia binoi.

For wild-type axolotls, three-~1-year-old male (d Ambystoma mexicanum) was obtained from Jiteng Lab at Guang Provincial People's Hospital.

# Cell lines

3T3 and 293T cell lines were obtained from Stuart H. Orkin at Harvard Medical School. Cells were cultured in Dulbecco's Medium (DMEM, ThermoFisher) with 10% Fetal Serum (FBS, ThermoFisher) and 1% Penicillin-streptomycin (ThermoFisher).
