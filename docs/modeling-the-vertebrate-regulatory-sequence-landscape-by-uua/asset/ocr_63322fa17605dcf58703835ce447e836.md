Figure S4. Comparison of regulatory interactions in mouse and axolotl, related to Figure 4

(A) Histogram showing the number of potential regulatory interactions per gene in both mouse and axolotl. The right panel is an enlarged view of the left panel to zoom in smaller values. Only interactions with Cicero co-accessibility above 0.5 were included in the comparison analysis. Scale bars, 2 µm (inset). One of the 3D slices is shown.
(B) Nascent 60-min pulse RNA-seq occupies dechromatin space juxtaposed to Hoechst-stained chromatin.
(C) Boxplots showing the number of genes (left) and the number of UMIs (right) in axolotl and endothelial cells of scRNA seq dataset. Statistical significance for pairwise comparisons was determined using Wilcoxon rank-sum test.
(D) Scatter plot showing the difference in size distribution between cCREs and cCREs within a 1 kb distance to TSSs, with one around 250 bp while the other is around 900 bp for all vertices.
(E) Visualization of broad and narrow cCRE signals in UATAC-seq alongside chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP-seq) signals for H3K4me1 and H3K4me3 at gene regulatory regions, as indicated by color.
(F and G) Visualization of broad and narrow cCRE signals in UATAC-seq CUT&Tag signals for H3K4me1 and H3K4me3 in the Nr5a1 gene region for adrenal gland and pituitary gland samples
(H) A gene accessibility heatmap showing the divergence of expression mode in stromal lineage for five species, computed log2-transformed fold-change values, as indicated by the scale bar.
(I) Feature maps showing the gene accessibility of the mTOR pathway in stromal cells across five species. The color scale represents the log-transformed gene accessibility scores.
