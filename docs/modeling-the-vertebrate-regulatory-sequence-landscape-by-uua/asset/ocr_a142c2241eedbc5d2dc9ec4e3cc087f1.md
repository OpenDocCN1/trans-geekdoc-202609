# CUT&Tag

The assembly of pg-Trn5 was carried out following the manufacturer's protocol (Vazyme, cat no. SG601). Briefly, 20 µL of pg-Trn5 (500 ng/mL) was mixed with adapter oligonucleotides to generate an adapter-loaded complex. The assembled complex was subsequently diluted to a final volume of 180 µL using coupling buffer. The final product, referred to as TT-cte, was stored at -20°C, where it remains stable up to one year. And pituitary glands were isolated from three wild-type mice, with biological replicates designated as follows: after dissection, CTCF was collected in the nuclei of male mouse (WT1), H3K4me1 in a male mouse (WT2), and H3K4me3 in a male mouse (WT3). For nuclei, CTCF, H3K4me1, and H3K4me3 were assembled in male mouse WT_1, WT_2, and WT_3, respectively. Muscle tissue was harvested and homogenized in lysis buffer. The previously pulsed cells were then incubated in Nuclease Lysis Buffer B (containing 0.5% BSA, 0.05 mM NaCl, 1 mM KCl, 0.1 mM DTT, and 1x Roche Phosphatase Inhibitor in DPI) and incubated for 0.5 min. For CUT&Tag tagging (Abcam, cat no. ab28837), nuclei were washed with 1% formaldehyde for 2 minutes at room temperature, and the reaction was followed by adding glycine to a concentration of 50 nM. The mixture was then washed with Dution Buffer (containing: 0.5% Triton-X100, 0.5 mM NaCl, and 0.2% BSA in PBS) to a concentration of 1 microliter per nucleus using Dution Buffer and resuspended in 5 µL of NaCl at 10 mM KCl. For CoX-tagging (cat no. ab12502), 5 µL of MnCl2 were activated activated before using 5 µL of beads per test in Binding Buffer (10 mM KCl, 1 mM CaCl2, and 1 mM NaCl in 20 mM HEPES, pH 7.5) as the product material, followed by suspension in 5 µL of Binding Buffer. The activated beads were then striped on one ice. For each test, 5 µL of aliquot suspension was added to the CoX-beads and mixed by gentle incubation for 30 minutes at 4°C. The mixture was then washed twice with Dution Buffer (containing 0.5% Triton-X100, 0.5 mM NaCl, and 0.2% BSA in PBS) and incubated with the primary antibody overnight at 4°C. The beads were then placed on a magnet and the supernatant was discarded. The remaining beads were then incubated on a rotator at 4°C for 30 min, after which the mixture was finally incubated with the secondary antibody, the antibody-beads were washed twice and placed in 50 µL of 2nd Secondary antibody (1:1000, 0.5% BSA, and 1x Roche Phosphate Inhibitor in WB-pre mixed with anti-mouse IgG antibody (cat no. ab7602)). The mixture was then centrifuged at 1000 x g for 5 min, and the supernatant discarded. The beads were then resuspended in 50 µL of 2nd Secondary antibody (1:1000, 0.5% BSA, and 1x Roche Phosphate Inhibitor in WB-pre mixed with anti-mouse IgG antibody (cat no. ab7602)). The mixture was then incubated on a rotator at room temperature for 30 min. The beads were then placed on a magnet and the supernatant discarded. The beads were then resuspended in 50 µL of DNA elution buffer (10 mM KCl, 1 mM CaCl2, and 1 mM NaCl in 20 mM HEPES, pH 7.5). The DNA was then analyzed.

# Cohesatin Release Test

To evaluate the efficiency of UATAC-seq, we prepared a mixed nucleus population containing 2937 (human) and E74 (mouse) cells. Nuclei from different cell types were diluted to a solution of 5000 cells per µL. For the contamination test, 5 µL of 923 nuclei suspension was mixed with 20 µL nuclei suspension, to ensure homogeneity enrichment. The evaluation involved four rounds of incubation, comprising chromatin transposation of open chromatin, adapter ligation, adaptor switching and gapfilling, and fragment amplification, as described in previous sections. We performed a comprehensive indexing strategy using 96 Tn5 barcodes, density during cell cycle rates. We tested two densities: 125 nuclei per well and 750 nuclei per well. This setup resulted in library-coverage of approximately 1000 (125 nuclei/well) x 8 indexed-H Y beads and 6000 (nuclei/well) x 8 indexed-H Y beads, respectively. From each density, one sub-library was sequenced on the MGI DNNBQ-T platform.
