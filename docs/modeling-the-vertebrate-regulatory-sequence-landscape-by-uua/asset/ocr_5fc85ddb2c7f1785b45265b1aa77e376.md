Identifying CREs at global and lineage level
To identify CREs in diverse species, we aggregated the fragments from the upstream processing. Fragments with a quality score below 30 were filtered and not deduplicated, resulting in a collection of unique fragments. Further involved alignment based on diverse databases, yielding a merged fragment in BED format for calling. We used MACS3 (v3.0.0 beta 3, https://github.com/macs3-project/MACS) with the macs2-command. This was executed for each cell type and lineage with the following parameters: "-1 global" -g BEDPE -q 0.01 --mins 5 GLOBAL_ATAC_subset -B --callpeaks" for the mouse. For each lineage of species, a distinct barcode list was generated, and then called using the same parameters as the global level. We built a CCREs in a narrowPeak format, containing the anthenic ranges of accessible regions, peak sum counts and p-values and q-values.

Traditional ATAC-seq analysis naturally identifies peaks up to 500 bp, imposing artificial boundaries on regulatory elements. In contrast, the CCRE database developed here avoids the limitations of arbitrary peak selection, and of enrichment within each cell type reveals patterns across genomes.
