Motif enrichment analysis indicates that the open regions are enriched with housekeeping related to the core transcriptional program, including SP1. The ~250 bp regions in promoters are enriched with CTCF and lineage-related motifs (Table S5). While the ~250 bp regions are within 1 kb of some open regions in both size and enrichment (Figure S-E), the open regions are enriched with H3K4me3, while the ~250 bp regions are enriched with H3K41 (Figure S-F). It can be seen that there are two main classes of open regions in vertebrates: the ~900 bp regions are shared among classical promoters, and the ~250 bp regions correspond to classical enhancers/repressors. Our results are in agreement with previous characterization of CREs using CRISPR-i.
