The temperature-controlled adapter switching represents a key innovation in our protocol, preventing thermal regulation to enable robust in vitro adapter switching (Figure B). The bridge oligo (Tm 58°C) and Adapter I (A, Tm 56°C) were used; the bridge oligo fully dissociates, ensuring high-affinity binding for h-B oligonucleotides (at 10+ minutes). During the gradient phase, h-B preferentially binds to the genomic DNA 3' end, the adaptor region and the sequence within h-B, fully anneal to the DNA end. The gap-filling phase completes two steps: first, the 9-nucleotide gap between the two bases is filled and ligated to the nick to generate a continuous DNA strand, which serves as the template for subsequent primer ligation. T4 DNA ligase was used for strand ligation, and adapter B2 was incorporated into the primer-phase because it operates effectively at higher temperature. The temperature gradient allows for gap-ligation to occur at elevated temperatures necessary for gap repair, simultaneously optimizing ligation efficiency. This temperature-sensitive approach achieves near adapter replacement in the UIAAC-seq protocol. It ensures highly specific PCR linker addition and resolves the issue of self-tagging in hetero-adapter-ATAC-seq (Figure B).

# cRE Amplification (First round of indexing)

Using an 8-channel pipette, the gapfilling fragments from 96-well plates were pooled. Specifically, the 12 µL from each well was collected and transferred to 1.5 µL of Epuffer buffer. This resulted in a total of 9 new volumes to remove. 5µL of VHAN DNA Polymerase (V3, no. C44011) was added to each tube, and PCR was performed for full amplification. Each amplified product was diluted with 2µL of 2X HotStart Taq (BiosKystems, no. K2020) and 1µL of 5'-ATAC primers. The products were pooled on a 96-well plate. PCR was performed with mix of 2µL of 2X HotStart Taq (per well) with the following conditions: 5 min; 2 min at 90°C; 20 min at 65°C; 2 min at 72°C; 11-14 min at 90°C; 20 min at 65°C; 30 min at 72°C; 3 min at 80°C; and stop. To remove residual primers and fragments, the amplified products were purified with VHAN DNA Clean Buffer. Briefly, 25µL of Clean Beads were added to each well, and the supernatant was transferred to the mixture, and excess primers were removed by centrifugation. An additional Clean Bead was added to the mixture to capture the assembled strand. The scale of the fragment amplification setup, including required reagent volumes and numbers, can be properly adjusted based on the amount of nuclei required for a single experiment.

# Sequencing on Illumina DNSEQ-T

For NGS sequencing (PE1150.0, no. C940-002809-2), labeled with detection sequencing indexes were used in molar ratios to achieve a total of 40 ng per cell, with an expected output covering approximately 70,000 nuclei. The pooled libraries were sequenced using the VANHT Kit for NGS (Vamine, no. NM2201) to generate single-stranded cDNA, which was subsequently used for the sequencing of DNA Nanoblocks (DNA) in accordance with the manufacturer' guidelines. Following this, all sequences were sequenced on the DNSEQ-T platform using a custom sequencing + 19 cycles: Read 1: 24 cycles + 10 cycles + 19 cycles + 90 cycles; Read 2: 10 cycles + 30 cycles + 19 cycles + 91 cycles. Each chip runs can be performed simultaneously, with a single chip loading approximately 5000 reads, corresponding to data for around 70,000 nuclei.
