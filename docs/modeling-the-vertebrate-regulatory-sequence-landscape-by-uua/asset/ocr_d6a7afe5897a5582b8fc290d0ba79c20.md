# Axolotl peripheral blood mononuclear cells

The axolotl was anesthetized and euthanized after a defined volume of blood was collected from the heart using an EDTA-treated hematocrit capillary. The blood was transferred into an Eppendorf tube containing an equal volume of 50 mM EDTA-PBS. Cells were thawed five times. After centrifugation at 700 g for 5 minutes at 4 °C, the supernatant was removed, and the pellet was resuspended in 1 ml of distilled water and set aside for 3 minutes. Usually, 80 μl of PBS was added and mixed briefly by vortexing. The lysis step was repeated until the supernatant became clear. The supernatant was removed, and 1 ml of Resowl Park Minimal Medium (RIMP) 1640 medium was added to suspend the cells. The cell count was determined, and the cells were dispersed into a 6-well plate with a 10 mm cover at the top. The plate was placed in an incubator (37 °C, 5% CO2) for 2 hours. Non-adherent cells were carefully washed away with PBS. The cells were then incubated at 37 °C for 2.5 ml of RPMI-1640 medium containing 10% FBS per well.

# Mouse peritoneal/macrophage

The mouse was anesthetized and sterilized with 70% EtOH. The peritoneal abdomen was opened, and 6 ml of 1 x PBS was injected into the peritoneal cavity. The mouse abdomen was gently opened, and peritoneal fluid was collected and transferred into a tube (20 ml containing of 0.1-1 x PBS). The supernatant was centrifuged at 4 °C and 350 g for 5 minutes. After centrifugation, the supernatant was removed. The cells were resuspended in RPMI-1640 medium containing 10% FBS and seeded into a 6-well plate with a 10 mm cover at the top. Incubated at 37 °C for 2 hours. Non-adherent cells were carefully washed away with PBS.

# Chromatin staining and Super-resolution microscopy

Cells were incubated for an 1 hour with 5 ml medium containing 1 μM EGF. Fixation and permeabilization. EU cells were stained with α488-antibody (through a click reaction, chromatin was stained with Dchst-3342 (Baychst)). Super-resolution images were obtained using stimulated emission depletion (STED) or CellB STORM. Spherical aberration was minimized using optical immersion with diffraction correction. Data were collected at 2 nm with 0.5 power and 3-5 nm step size. Spherical aberration was minimized. Data quality was assessed with SimMack-101 where modulation-contrast-to-noise ratio (MNCR), spectral analysis, and, were reconstructed using Fourier plot, and chromatin intensity values were verified.

# Functional validation of vN3-GCA predicted target site

The Hupe2-HGb-64A-G cell line was established using homologous recombination with wild-type Hupe2 cells by DodeBio (Shanghai). Hgb-A sgRNA expression plasmid targeting the -68 region and single-strand oligononucleotide repeat Hgb-A mutation was generated by CRISPR/Cas9 by DodeBio (Shanghai). Detailed sequences were listed in Table S1. RNA, single-strand DNA, was transfected into Hupe2 cells using Electroporation (Lonza, V4XP). After 48 hours, a fraction of post-transformed pooled cells were collected and then reseeded into a 6-well plate at a density of 1 × 10^5 cells per well. The cells were collected and subjected to total RNA extraction. After cDNA sequencing, expression levels of Hgb1/2 were measured by qPCR using universal qPCR system (Heat, CF-G) and detected by qPCR (YaqGreen, Taq-92AM).

# Preprocessing of snATAC data

The FASTQ files generated by snATAC-seq were processed by the pipeline software Velvet (v1.6; https://github.com/met-bioinformatics/snatac-seq). Reads were removed if the base quality was less than 10 or if they had more than 30 cycles. Each cell was tagged with the name of the query. Using Dro-Seq tools each cell read contained four pairs of components: P7 index, BCCA barcode, BCD barcode and Tn5 barcode (128, 7, 8, and 192 indices, respectively). Barcodes were removed if their original sequence had an error caused by amplification. In. BAM files with faulty barcodes were removed by Tn5 barcodes and merged with tissue information. Fragments with duplicate reads were removed. Sometimes, references were realigned using Bowtie2 (v2.4.4) for mouse genome (mm39) and Danr111 for zebrafish (v0.7.5; https://github.com/deeptools/Tools4) to count for Tn5 insertion bias. Reads measured with a quality higher than 30 were then converted to counts. Nucleotide fragments ranging in length from 100 to 10000 were sequenced, generating one file for each tissue. For axolotl, due to the chromosome size, the coordinates of the GTF file and fragment files were sequenced to less than 2^20 bp to meet the length limit of at-atin index. Segmentation sites were located in intergenic region, where no gene existed between 50000 bp upstream or downstream (Table S3).
