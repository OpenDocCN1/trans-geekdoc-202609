Distance-based matrix methods use matrix as a data structure to conduct phylogenetic tree construction. The data is transformed into pairwise distances.

# 3.4.1 UNWEIGHTED PAIR GROUP METHOD WITH ARITHMETIC MEAN (UPGMA) – DISTANCE MATRIX METHOD

UPGMA is based on an agglomerative clustering technique. In this method, closely related clusters are progressively combined to form one cluster. Combining clusters carries on until all the clusters are combined to form a tree structure. Let us take an example of four nodes, namely a, b, c and d, as shown in Figure 3.6.

+   Step 2. Combine nodes b and c, as shown in Table 3.2, to form cluster bc.

