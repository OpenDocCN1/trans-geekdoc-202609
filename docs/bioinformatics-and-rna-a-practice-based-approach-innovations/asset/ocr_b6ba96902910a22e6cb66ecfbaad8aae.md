# ## 5.4.10 B-TYPE PSEUDOKNOT

A B-type pseudoknot is formed by base pairing between a bulge loop and a leftover unpaired single-stranded region of a secondary structure. Both of the bases in such a base pair are actually unpaired bases left during the formation of a secondary structure, as shown in Figure 5.26.
