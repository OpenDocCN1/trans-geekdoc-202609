# 5.2 BIOLOGICAL SIGNIFICANCE OF RNA PSEUDOKNOT

A pseudoknot was first discovered in the turnip yellow mosaic virus in 1982 [10]. Pseudoknots have many varied functions. One of the significant functions of a pseudoknot is frameshifting. It induces ribosomes into an alternative reading frame.
