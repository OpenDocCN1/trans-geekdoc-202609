
# Paper Wallets

Paper wallets are nothing but, as the name suggests, just papers with public and private keys printed. The keys can be published in the form of a QR code. They are considered a type of cold wallet since someone cannot access them online. Paper wallets are controversial since they are both secure, as only the paper holder can have the keys, but at the same time, if the paper is itself lost, then the keys can be compromised. Also, if the form is damaged, it can cause many issues. Bitaddress.org, Verge paper wallet generator, and Walletgenerator.net are the frequently used paper wallet generators that one can use.

# How to Choose a Crypto Wallet?

As we have seen, there are many different types of crypto wallets, and further, each type has wallets being offered by many other companies. This cannot be very clear to a person. So to better choose the correct crypto wallet, one should look at the following factors:

## Price

The price or the cost of the crypt wallet is the first factor to look into. Many online wallets are available on the web for free, but they are less secure; one could get them if one cannot afford a better wallet. Meanwhile, the hardware wallets from companies such as Ledger can cost a bit more, but they are very secure and should be used with some with an extensive portfolio.

## Security

Different crypto wallets offer varying amounts of protection. The entire reason behind getting a crypto wallet is to provide a secure gateway to your crypto assets. One should carefully assess the risks associated with the different wallets before selecting one. Hardware wallets generally offer more security than hot wallets; however, some are more secure than others, even between software wallets. If a person is risk-averse, they can purchase a software wallet.

## User Interface

The user interface is integral to the investor's experience. A beginner usually starts with an easier-to-use crypto wallet before getting more technical. Mobile wallets, just mobile applications, offer the most easy-to-use platforms. Paper wallets are also considered easy to use, especially for those not keen on using digital software.

## Flexibility and Compatibility

The compatibility and mobility offered by crypto wallets are also important factors. If one wants to use the wallet on the fly and access it from any computer or device, then an online wallet or even a mobile wallet is recommended. Desktop and hardware wallets are less mobile since the person can only carry them sometimes. A wallet is considered good if it can be used on multiple devices such as phones, desktops, laptops, and operating software like Windows, Mac, Linux, iOS, Android, etc.

## Currencies Offered

The number of cryptocurrencies the wallet supports must also be considered. If a person plans on having a portfolio containing many different cryptocurrencies, it is best to get a wallet supporting multiple currencies. However, if a person only invests in a single currency, they may get a dedicated wallet only made for that cryptocurrency.

## Backup

The backup to a wallet is necessary. There have been instances where one loses their purse due to its corruption and cannot access their crypto assets. This can be a cause of significant concern. Even in the case of backups, there are multiple prices that the wallet creators may charge.

## Multisig

Multisig is a new function available on specific crypto wallets. Multisig means that multiple people hold private keys to a particular crypto wallet. To make a transaction, all of the private keys are required. This makes the wallet much more secure since even if one of the private keys gets leaked or hacked, the transaction will still not go through. This is a feature that is ideal for family members and business partners.

## Transparency

A wallet provider must be as transparent as the technology to secure the crypto wallets. A transparent open-source-based wallet provider is usually the safest option.

## Privacy

The user’s privacy is of utmost importance; when choosing the wallet, one should see all the data being asked, especially during the KYC process, and ensure that the crypto wallets do not leak such data.

These are the significant factors that one must consider; however, a person may decide according to their choice depending on their constraints and what they do or do not want in their crypto wallet.

It is also possible to not have a specific cryptocurrency wallet for oneself. Most crypto exchange platforms have their wallets attached to the account; however, they are significantly more risky than a proper cryptocurrency wallet.

# Top Crypto Wallets In The Market

Now that we have seen the factors one must consider before deciding on the crypto wallet, let's look at the top crypto wallets available in the market.

## Ledger X

Ledger X is the top-of-the-line crypto wallet from the company Ledger. It is a hardware wallet. It can connect with devices through either Bluetooth or USB. It is considered the most secure crypto wallet but has a significant price tag. The user interface is straightforward to use. It can hold multiple cryptocurrencies. Although it is primarily a cold wallet, one can also use the Ledger Live software.

## Ledger S

Ledger S is the other cold wallet the French company offers. It comes at a price point that is slightly easier to digest for newer investors than Ledger X. Since it is cheaper, it has certain features, such as Bluetooth connectivity. However, one still has to use the Ledger Live software. It is an affordable yet secure crypto wallet that is suggested for beginners.

## Trezor

Trezor is another company that offers hardware or cold wallets. They also have two lines of wallets: the entry-level wallet is called the Trezor One, and the flagship model is called the Trezor Model T. The Model T comes with added functionalities and supports more currencies.
