## The Market Capitalization of Altcoins

The altcoin market has been booming in recent years; from 2017 to the present day, the total market cap of the altcoin space has increased by more than 100-fold. The altcoin market cap currently stands at 884 billion dollars, higher than Bitcoin's $665 billion. Let's look at some of the front runners in the altcoin space that account for a significant portion of this impressive market cap.

### Ethereum ($221 billion)

Ethereum is a cryptocurrency that draws inspiration from the original Bitcoin. While Bitcoin is used primarily as a store of value as a digital form of currency, the idea behind Ethereum is to decentralize applications and services from simple apps to more complex financial agreements. Ethereum uses the same technology as Bitcoin, wherein a blockchain uses a distributed, decentralized public ledger to decentralize the network, so it's not under the control of any single entity.

Ethereum lets you send cryptocurrency to anyone for a small fee and powers applications everyone can use. Ethereum boasts itself as "the world's programmable blockchain." It builds on Bitcoin's innovation, with some significant differences.

While both Ethereum and Bitcoin let you use digital money without payment providers or banks, Ethereum has the added advantage of being programmable, so you can also use it for many different use cases and business models. This also means Ethereum's application can go beyond fulfilling payments on a network. It can be a marketplace of financial services, games, and apps that can't censor or steal your data. Ethereum was conceived by Vitalik Butarin, Martin Wood, and other members in 2013 and released after a couple of years in 2015. Through the Ethereum project, they wanted to address different limitations present in Bitcoin and allow the execution of short programs in the Blockchain. Vitalik and the rest of the team sought better ways to build decentralized finance systems with apps running on the Blockchain.

At the heart of the programmable aspect of Ethereum lies smart contracts and dApps (Decentralized apps). A smart contract is a short program that can be deployed and run on a blockchain. It processes transactions sent to it by executing code and can also hold funds (Ether) on the blockchain ledger. These transactions can pass both funds and data.

Smart contracts can be built to perform all sorts of functions without a trusted third party or central authority. If you consider smart contracts as back-end code running in the blockchain, decentralized applications are the front-end or UI. They represent the visible layer connecting users or other applications with the smart contracts running in the blockchain. Eventually, entire decentralized autonomous organizations may exist in an ether economy.

Ethereum was trading at $2,913 as of Aug 2021 and $1,913 as of June 2023. Exactly years ago, it was trading at $390; that's more than a five times increase in market price.

As Ethereum's rally accelerates, many in the crypto industry predict that Ethereum could overtake Bitcoin as the world's biggest cryptocurrency by value. With the latest update to its blockchain, Ethereum is returning to a cryptocurrency bull market with fresh all-time highs on the horizon. The price boost, pushing ether tokens toward $3,000, has caused the combined crypto market cap to climb to almost $1.7 trillion.

### Tether ($83 billion)

Tether or USDT is a cryptocurrency pegged 1 to 1 with the US dollar. Tether is currently the most popular USD-pegged cryptocurrency in the world. According to its developers, the design of USDT is to be worth 1 USD, and the dollar will back each tether unit it issues into circulation in a one-to-one ratio. However, the price of USDT can be volatile, and it was seen trading at a >10% discount to USD in 2018.

Many consider Tether an optimal way for users to move money effectively without a centralized authority. Users can trade the currency actively and use it for international transactions in countries with capital regulations. The massive global payments market is a driving force for Tether's supply and demand movements.

Though the white paper states that one USDT should receive the backing of one USD, there are many questions about its legitimacy. The reserve level has been a mystery as rumors suggest that Tether's reserve is insufficient to back the amount of USDT. The last audit was in mid-2018 by a Washington law firm. The mistrust and speculations brought in a significant price movement in Oct 2018.

The credibility of Tether has always raised some concerns, especially regarding whether Tether's reserve has sufficient reserves. Despite such speculation, Tether remains at the top, holding ten times more market share than other USD stablecoins. Given the uncertainty surrounding its backing with USD, it will be interesting to see if Tether can maintain such dominance in the future.

### Binance Coin ($40 billion)

Binance Coin is the cryptocurrency issued by the Binance exchange, the largest cryptocurrency exchange in the world. Binance coin trades with the BNB symbol.

It was initially based on the Ethereum network but is now the native currency of Binance's blockchain, the Binance chain.

Binance uses one-fifth of its profits to repurchase BNB. They then permanently destroy this purchased currency every quarter in a process known as burning. This keeps inflation at bay and induces scarcity to drive up prices. The updated whitepaper states that BNB burns will now be based on its exchanges' trading volume.

Since Binance began burning BNB in Oct 2017, 10 token burns have occurred, with about 16.7 million BNB worth approximately $208 million burned as of January 2020.

Binance was initially intended as a utility token for discounted trading fees during its release in 2017. But now, its uses have expanded to various other applications, including payments for transaction fees, travel bookings, entertainment, online services, and financial services.
