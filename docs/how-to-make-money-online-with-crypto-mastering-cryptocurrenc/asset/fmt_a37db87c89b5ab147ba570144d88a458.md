## Binance

Binance has a market cap of more than $56 billion and ranks among the top cryptos in terms of market cap. Binance's story started with an `ICO` and has grown into one of the largest global cryptocurrency exchanges in terms of volume traded and market share by web traffic.

## Cardano ($12 billion)

Charles Hoskinson founded Cardano in 2017, two years after he helped develop the `Ethereum` blockchain technology. He also co-founded Internet Online Hong Kong (`IOHK`), the company that built the Cardano blockchain.

Cardano uses a timestamping methodology called `Proof of Stake` (`PoS`). `PoS` is the system by which the miners in the Cardano ecosystem validate and may block transactions. A stake is simply the number of coins (`ADA` coins) one holds, which also determines one's participation in creating new blocks.

Using knowledge gained from developing the `Ethereum` project, Hoskinson injected the idea of smart contracts into the Cardano project. Cardano sets itself apart in the crypto-verse in that it was conceived through peer-reviewed research. The research yielded unique features like unparalleled platform security, system sustainability, and a robust professional development community.

According to the project's website, `IOHK` created the Cardano blockchain to "restore trust to global systems" by availing a new standard in inclusive and open-source technology. Therefore, "changemakers, innovators, and visionaries" can leverage the technology to create a society that is "more secure, transparent, and fair."

`ADA` is the digital currency facilitating transactions among Cardano network participants. `ADA` ranks among the top ten tokens in terms of market cap. It enables peer-to-peer transactions in a decentralized, secure environment free from intermediaries. For this reason, transactions over the Cardano blockchain are fast and inexpensive.

Cardano's cryptocurrency claims to have legitimate advantages over fiat currency. The Cardano Foundation adopted a limit of `ADA`'s total supply at 45 billion coins. The idea is to avoid inflation and keep the coin's value in check. `ADA` is far better than a fiat currency in terms of transaction speed by peer-to-peer transactions. Fiat currencies require intermediaries like banks to enforce trust, leading to bottlenecks along the transaction chain.

## XRP ($30 billion)

Ripple is a cryptocurrency token equivalent to digital cash, purpose-built for payment networks and banks. `XRP` or Ripple is a native token on its Blockchain known as `RippleNet`. `RippleNet` is a payments system run by Ripple Labs and built on the `XRP Ledger`, decentralized and open-source blockchain technology. It boasts breakneck transaction speeds with an average transaction settlement speed of four seconds. Comparably, the transaction settlement time on the `Bitcoin` network is 3,600 seconds.

One of the areas where Ripple had an immediate impact was cross-border payments. The developers created `RippleNet` to streamline transactions. Consumers and businesses often suffer the inconveniences of converting between currencies and losses arising from exchange rate differences when transacting in fiat currency. `RippleNet` uses the `XRP` token as an intermediary to streamline fiat currency exchange without suffering slippage.

Moreover, digital currency facilitates faster settlement of transactions at speeds unseen in the traditional payments sectors. Due to the utilization of the peer-to-peer transaction system, users enjoy micro-fees where the average fee is less than $0.0003403. `RippleNet`'s competitors in the conventional payments sector, such as `VISA`, charge thousands of times higher for an equivalent transaction.

Ripple draws its strength from blockchain technology. Most blockchain platforms obtain consensus through `Proof of Work` (`PoW`) or `Proof of Stake` (`PoS`) mechanisms. The `PoW` system is slower and consumes more power, unlike the `PoS` system, which supports faster validation and approval of transactions while consuming less power.

However, blockchain platforms like `XRP Ledger` utilize another form of consensus, in this case, called the `Ripple Protocol Consensus Algorithm` (`RPCA`). Peace is how nodes in a blockchain network maintain agreement and correctness with each other. `RPCA` runs through the more than 150 nodes that make up the global `XRP` Community.

The robust consensus system is a significant reason behind Ripple's seven years of stability, enabling its developers to expand the product range of the ecosystem. For example, the developers have expanded `RippleNet`'s capacity to offer on-demand liquidity to its enterprise clients. This makes global payments faster and cheaper.

## Promising Altcoins

Some altcoins have immense potential to dominate future markets. We will now look at such altcoins whose market cap has been gradually increasing over the years.

### Basic Attention Token (BAT)

`Basic Attention Token` (`BAT`) is a utility token built on the `Ethereum` blockchain, which is the foundation for the `Brave` web browser platform. The `Brave` browser allows users to browse the internet anonymously and completely ad-free and then enables users to opt-in to watch ads in exchange for `BAT` tokens.

Publishers also get `BAT` when users watch or click on ads on their sites. Advertisers benefit from lower overhead costs for advertising and higher engagement from users. And users get increased privacy without sacrificing the benefits of tailored advertisements and can get `BAT` tokens. The `BAT` token is designed to be exchanged between users, advertisers, and publishers. It is based on the concept of user attention, which is critical to advertising companies operating in the digital age. `BAT` is an `ERC-20` token secured by a rigorously tested `proof-of-work` consensus algorithm supported by an extensive `Ethereum` miner network. This algorithm ensures that only valid transactions are confirmed, while the combined work of the `Ethereum` mining network ensures `BAT` transactions are irreversible once approved.

The developers of `BAT` seek to improve how advertising is done in the digital space. They see the current system as disadvantageous for users, publishers, and advertisers. Users are relentlessly targeted with ads and trackers, and they are subjected to malware that is becoming increasingly aggressive and powerful.

Conventional publishers have seen their ad revenues decline dramatically as monopoly platforms like `Google` and `Facebook` drive down the price of information while taking a hefty cut from the advertising revenue. In addition, bots and malware can inflict increasing damage each year, and platforms have suffered credibility issues. Moreover, advertisers need more accurate targeting methods and information to deliver their content sufficiently because platforms monopolize these functions.

The creators of `BAT` believe that tracking how users interact with digital advertising content and storing that information in a distributed blockchain ledger will enhance and improve the digital advertising experience for advertisers, publishers, and users. Users will experience fewer ads and content that is better tailored to them, while Publishers will find new sources of revenue. And advertisers will be able to target particular users better based on documented user content preferences and interests. They will also be able to avoid fraud more efficiently.

`Basic Attention Token` has a maximum total supply capped at 1.5 billion tokens. Almost all of this supply is currently in circulation. Hence, the `Basic Attention Token` can be considered almost entirely liquidated.

In its 2017 `ICO`, 1 billion `BAT` tokens were sold to investors, the remaining 200 million coins were locked in a development pool, and 300 million `BAT` was reserved for the user growth pool (`UGP`). As of November 2020, the development pool and `UGP` pool addresses are almost empty. `Basic Attention Token` can be bought and sold on every central cryptocurrency exchange platform and has excellent liquidity.
