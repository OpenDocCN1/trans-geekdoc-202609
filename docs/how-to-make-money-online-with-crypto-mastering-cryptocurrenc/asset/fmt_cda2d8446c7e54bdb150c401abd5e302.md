## Key Features of DeFi

### Immutability

One of the significant advantages DeFi offers is the immutability of financial data by reducing the possibility of any malicious data tampering. Data storage on Blockchain networks makes the records extremely secure, reducing the case of any unauthorized change in the papers. This, in turn, decreases the prospect of fraudulent practices reigning in this financial system. Also, smart contracts on the blockchain make the terms and conditions unchangeable by a single party due to the availability of activity regarding the intelligent agreements to all the concerned parties. This distinguishing feature of DeFi ensures a highly secure space and dialogue for exchanging financial services.

### Accessibility

As mentioned in the previous section, anyone can use DeFi financial systems with minimum prerequisites of a crypto wallet and an internet connection. This allows users across the world to relish the advantages offered by DeFi. Although such ledgers are permissionless in their essence, they might create many problems, like the proliferation of fraudulent activities or perpetrators disrupting the financial ecosystem set up by DeFi. Due to the same, it is pertinent for service seekers and givers on DeFi platforms to authenticate the other party by checking the Know-Your-Customer (KYC) requisites and, sometimes, Anti-money laundering (AML). Due to relevant strategies to prevent malpractices, accessibility still weighs more towards the side of benefits conferred.

### Flexibility and Autonomy

The permissionless feature of DeFi systems offers untamed flexibility and autonomy to the users. Again, this is because there is no regulating body to hinder the progression and evolution of services and their scope of DeFi systems. The users are not required to display compliance with a defined set of rules and regulations forced on them by external authorities. The flexibility offered to the software, for example, in the case of smart contracts, leads to autonomy. Once the interested parties have entered into intelligent agreements with mutual alignment, smart contracts are self-functional, requiring nothing from the involved parties except complying with the terms stated. Thus they can easily carry out their business without needing external authority, augmenting autonomy.

## Comparison Between DeFi and Traditional Financial Systems

Now that we know the characterizing features of the DeFi systems let's compare this system with the traditional financial methods and assess which system has more significant advantages.

The traditional financial systems include conventional banks and other financial institutions governed by a centralized authority, thus often used interchangeably with Centralized finance (CeFi). Centralized exchanges, part of the CeFi, jeopardize user funds, data security, and privacy as these centralized authorities manage both. However, these centralized exchanges allow various financial services like margin trading, borrowing, lending, etc. Also, CeFi effectively provides cross-chain services standing in strong contrast to DeFi. CeFi considers a multitude of chains for taking complete control of the funds, and only after that, it allows the trading of coins. Also, they have the feature of transfer of ownership that reinforces its simplicity in the case of cross-chain services. Additionally, CeFi systems also offer flexibility in working with fiat currency. Despite the advantages of CeFi or traditional financial methods, DeFi systems are gaining mainstream traction in the financial world due to their many benefits.

### Agents of Regulation of Service

The essence of traditional financial systems is the presence of a central authority. Conventional economic systems or CeFi (Centralized finance) operate under the aegis of an umbrella authority that lists the rules and regulations of different components constituting it. In the case of DeFi, as reiterated in the previous sections, intelligent contracts are responsible for the absolute management and regulation of financial services. These smart contracts are publicly available for audit by any user. Such a facility of contract audit is not available in the case of CeFi, as the operation of financial services needs to be made public for review by a more significant number of individuals. Critics might reason against this by implicating that the presence of intelligent contracts publicly can undermine the privacy of the parties involved. In this context, DeFi ensures that the public projection of the smart contracts is pseudonymous, which means that the identities and preliminaries of the parties involved are not involved.

Also, in the case of a financial institution acting as a platform for disseminating financial services, the culmination of financial services is delayed by the engagement of many people with different allocated responsibilities. Such a tedious and cumbersome process often drives the seekers of financial services away. Smart contracts, in the case of DeFi, ensure robustness in services with little to no human interventions in the process.

### Permissionless

CeFi and DeFi are also different regarding permissions required to process financial services. As outlined under the features of DeFi, the permissionless nature of DeFi helps economic activities to be carried out without much effort. The users can connect their existing wallets to their platform of DeFi service and engage in the requisite financial services. However, in the case of CeFi, with the requirements of KYC to avail services, the process becomes relatively longer.

### Operational Space

Another basic premise of the difference between traditional financial systems and DeFi is the operational platform. Most conventional economic systems need a physical space for their proper operation. It also requires human labor and services to keep disseminating their services in the physical area, thus necessitating a good amount spent on incentivizing human work. While DeFi is based on Distributed ledger technology (DLT) and runs effectively on blockchain networks. DeFi's entire operation employing technology and advanced software stacks eliminates the need for a formal setting in a physical space, on the one hand. It reduces the need for human labor, on the other.

### Currency

DeFi and CeFi also stand in contrast in terms of the characteristic flagship assets and their nature. For instance, DeFi, as we know, uses cryptocurrencies as all the transactions, in this case, occur on a blockchain network in a digital space. However, CeFi necessarily uses fiat currency. Thus the transactions occur with coins, paper notes, etc.

### Contract

In the case of DeFi, the interaction occurs directly between the interested parties, and the transactions subsequently take from peer to peer. Intelligent contracts support these transactions. In the context of traditional financial services, multiple points of contact or intermediaries often impede the smoothness of financial services.
