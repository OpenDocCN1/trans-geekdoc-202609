
# NFT Overview

## CryptoKitties

The cats in the game are all unique. Each cat has its color, size, name, and other characteristics which cannot be copied. The users own the cats and thus cannot be taken away by anyone, including the game's developers. The cats can also be used as collectibles, wherein people collect a specific type of cat and then display these collections. Depending on their attributes and rarity, a few different types of cats are in the game, such as Exclusive Cats, Fancy Cats, and Special Edition Cats.

## CryptoPunks

CryptoPunks are the other path setters in Non-fungible Tokens, along with CryptoKitties. Much like CryptoKitties, crypto punks were also introduced in 2017. Cryptopunks were created by the American development studio called Larva Labs. The cyberpunk movement inspired the creation of crypto punks. The crypto punks, in turn, inspired the ER-721 standard of Ethereum that is now used in most digital art NFTs.

Crypto punks are avatars that people can use to display pictures on various platforms. In total, 10,000 crypto punks were created by Larva Labs. Of these 10,000 crypto punks, 6039 are male, 3840 are female, 88 are zombies, 24 are apes, and 9 are aliens. Each of these crypto punks is unique and thus carries its value. The price of the crypto punk depends on the rarity; with the nine alien crypto punks being the rarest, they have been purchased at the highest prices till now.

Crypto punks regularly feature on the list of most expensive NFTs sold, with CryptoPunks #7804 selling for 7.6 million dollars, CryptoPunks #6965 selling for 1.6 million dollars, and CryptoPunks #4156 selling for 1.3 million dollars. Even mainstream companies have started purchasing punks, with Visa shelling out almost 176,000 dollars on a crypto punk in July 2021.

## Rare Pepes

Rare Pepes: Rare pipes were the first NFTs that were offered on the platform known as counterparty. Pepes are images of green-colored frogs that are edited in various ways. The Pepe images of different kinds have been converted to NFTs and then sold to the highest bidder. The 'rarest' of pipes, a picture of Homer Pepe, i.e., the famous TV character of Homer Simpson, edited to look like a Pepe, was brought for a price of 320,000 dollars in March 2021

## Everyday: The First 5000 Days and Other Beeple Arts

Everyday: The first 5000 days are the most expensive NFT to have been sold till now. It was sold for 69.3 million dollars or 42329 Ether during an auction by the auction house Christie's. It is a collage of five thousand images created by Beeple. It was purchased by Vignesh Sundaresan, who is now displaying it in a digital museum.

It is not only the most expensive digital art piece till now but also one of the most expensive pieces sold by a living artist. Beeple is the pseudonym of the American digital artist Mike Winkelmann, who has converted many of his digital pieces into NFTs and sold them. His artworks are some of the most in-demand NFTs in the crypto world, with most selling in minutes and charging exorbitant prices. Another one of his pieces, called Crossroads, a 10-second video, sold for approximately 6.6 million dollars in February 2021.

## Decentraland

Decentraland is a 3-D virtual reality world. In this world, the products, lands, and other amenities are sold as NFTs, connecting the virtual and real worlds. It was initially started by Argentinian developers in 2015. It slowly moved from a pixelated sphere to a 3-d world. Recently, the prices of virtual real estate have increased manifolds, with some of the prime real estate in decentral costing as much as 100,000 dollars. Real-world companies such as Sotheby's and Republic Realm are purchasing digital real estate in this world to use as digital offices and shopping places.

## Alchemy Toys

Alchemy Toys is a game based on the Binance Smart chain, developed by a German software company called Backupdev UG. It is being promoted as an amalgamation of non-fungible tokens, massively multiplayer online role-playing games, and decentralized finance. In this game, players can own unique and rare alchemy toys, which can then be combined to form newer toys or sold to the other players for the actual value. It is also possible to sacrifice or burn these toys. If a person offers all the different toys, they are rewarded with Binance coins.

## Pancake Swap

Pancake swap is also based on the Binance bright chain. On the platform, the users are provided with bunnies that are NFTs. The bunnies have no purpose other than being decorative, except that the users can also trade them for Cake coins, the cryptocurrency of the said platform.

## Gods Unchained

Gods Unchained is much like real-life card trading games. In the game, the players are given actual ownership of their cards which are tradeable NFTs. These cards, when sold, provide the players with cryptocurrencies and real value. The value of the cards depends on the rarity of these cards, with some costing upwards of ten thousand dollars. The game was created by Immutable, a development studio based in Australia specializing in blockchain games.

## Differences Between NFTs and Typical Cryptocurrencies

Before we look at how non-fungible tokens differ from the otherwise mainstream or typical cryptocurrencies such as Bitcoin, Ether, or Cardano, we must first understand their similarities and what connects them. NFTs and cryptocurrencies are, in essence, digital assets that can be sold or purchased on online platforms with little to no inherent or intrinsic value. Both NFTs and cryptocurrencies are based on blockchain or distributed ledger technologies. The transaction history in both cases is stored on blockchains and is permanent and immutable. NFTs, much like cryptocurrencies, are also in a grey area when it comes to regulation in most countries.

### Nature

The first point of difference that we see between NFTs and cryptocurrencies is their essential nature. When we talk about cryptocurrencies, we consider them, as the name suggests, as currencies, i.e., they are to be used as tools or money-like instruments that will be used for purchasing other things or assets. However, in some places and by some people, they are treated as investments that might grow in value and then be sold off. On the other hand, when we talk about NFTs, as earlier discussed, they are not currencies despite being tokens. NFTs are like assets in nature. They are not to be used for purchasing other stuff but rather as collectibles. While cryptocurrencies can be compared to money in real life, NFTs can be compared to works of Art and Luxury.

### Fungibility

The most crucial characteristic of NFTs that differentiates them from typical cryptocurrencies is the one that is in their name, i.e., fungibility. Fungibility is the ability of similar goods or assets to be interchangeable or replaced by an identical item. Fungibility is an essential feature of currency. It ensures that people can freely use cash anywhere, and there is no difference between one unit of money with another. Cryptocurrencies like fiat currencies are fungible. If you trade one ether for another, there is no difference between the two; you essentially have the same thing in hand. On the other hand, NFTs are not fungible; each one is unique and thus cannot be copied. If you exchange one NFT for another, you have something different now than you had earlier.

### Value Determinant

The way NFTs are determined is also different from how the value of cryptocurrencies is determined. Since cryptocurrencies have a very high volume of sales and exchange hands very frequently, thus the price of the cryptocurrencies is determined by the prevailing market forces, i.e., demand and supply. NFTs are unique, and each carries a different value; further, the price of the NFT is determined by what the buyer is willing to pay for the said product. In the case of NFTs, the market's supply side is constant.
