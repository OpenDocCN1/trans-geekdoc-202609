
# Hashed Health

Hashed Health is one of the most popular blockchain companies working in the health sector. It aims to provide distributed ledgers of records of patients and physicians. One of their projects will allow physicians to produce their licenses showing where they are allowed to practice. They are partnering with healthcare firms to provide distributed ledgers for improved transparency.

# Follow My Vote

Follow My Vote is a decentralized application platform that, as the name suggests, has been set up to follow the voice of every voter in a constituency; this provides transparency in the voting process and, at the same time, avoids voter fraud. It can offer services in all kinds of votes, within an organization, in the collegiate system, or even on a larger scale.

# Open Bazaar

Open Bazaar is an e-commerce platform based on blockchain technology. Unlike any other e-commerce platform, it acts as a peer-to-peer platform where one can sell anything they wish to sell and pay no fees as there is no central authority. The payments are made through cryptocurrencies, and transactions are recorded on blockchains. These purchases could be for anything ranging from art to everyday products.

# Consensys

Consensys is a blockchain software engineering company that provides end-to-end solutions to companies regarding blockchain technology. It has recently teamed up with Microsoft and Moët Hennesy Louis Vuitton to create blockchain software that will help them trace the luxury products produced by the company and its subsidiaries and provide for their authenticity. It also allows the customers to trace their products from design to purchase.

# How to choose a Blockchain? (For business owners)

Depending on the needs of a business or an organization, they may choose the blockchain. The decision to select the correct blockchain is dependent on the following factors:

## Adaptability

The first factor when adopting blockchain technology to your business is checking whether the blockchain is applicable in your field. Blockchain requires information on servers that are open to everyone, so it might not be suitable for dealing with sensitive data. Further, blockchain is not a cheap technology, at least not yet. Hiring blockchain specialists is expensive and might not be possible for smaller-scale firms.

## Scalability

Scalability refers to the number of transactions handled on a single blockchain platform. If the business requires a large number of transactions, then it may choose a platform accordingly. While some platforms, such as Bitcoin, offer `TPS` (Transactions per Seconds) of only about `7 TPS`, others, like Stellar, can go up to almost `1000 TPS`. The scalability of a blockchain is measured on three factors: speed, security, and decentralization.

## New vs. Existing

Organisations can decide that their needs require the use of blockchain technologies; they then have to choose whether they want to use an already existing blockchain platform or if they want to create their platform. Developing a new platform can be slightly more costly and time-consuming; however, it would be more personalized to the requirements.

## Public vs. Private

There are two significant kinds of blockchain platforms, either private or public. Both public and private networks have advantages and disadvantages; while public networks are more secure, they are slightly slower. Private networks have fewer nodes, so they process transactions much faster, but they need to be more secure since it is easier for hackers to access fewer nodes.

## Security

Security is of great concern when considering the blockchain platform, especially for firms dealing with sensitive information. One should look at the peer reviews of the forum they are choosing.

## Getting Started

Investing in cryptocurrency means buying them and keeping them stored. So, to invest in cryptocurrency, we need to research where to buy and store the coins. Cryptocurrency exchanges are the places cryptocurrency can be purchased. They are online platforms that allow exchanging coins with fiat currencies or other digital assets.

There are several cryptocurrency exchanges out there on the internet. It is necessary to choose the right one as it is where you will buy, sell, trade, and store digital assets. Some popular crypto exchanges include Coinbase, Gemini, Binance, Kucoin, Kraken, Bybit, and Bitfinex. Apart from being popular, they are the most reliable exchanges available. There are exchanges where you can buy cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum using your bank account or a debit card. These are fiat-to-crypto exchanges, which allow the buying and selling of cryptos using only fiat currencies. In other words, you cannot buy a cryptocurrency with the help of another cryptocurrency.

The best site to research cryptocurrencies and exchanges is [`https://coinmarketcap.com`](https://coinmarketcap.com); if you have yet to go to this site, go there now and set up an account to do some detailed research.

If you live in the US, you must approach one of the exchanges that can convert your USD to Crypto, like Gemini or Coinbase. But they may not support and hold all the Crypto you want to buy and save. Then you might have to use another cryptocurrency exchange that allows cryptocurrency trading with other cryptocurrencies; you have to use an exchange like Kucoin, Kraken, or Binance.

Some exchanges facilitate traders to buy and sell cryptocurrencies for other cryptocurrencies. Technically, you can trade pairs like `ETH/BTC` and `XRP/BTC` rather than just `BTC/USD` or `ETH/USD`. Thus, you need not involve traditional currencies in buying and selling cryptocurrency.
