### DeFi Software System Stack

The consolidation of multiple layers characterizes a software stack. These various layers, in turn, have components that have a definitive specific function to cater to in developing a DeFi system. The four layers comprise the DeFi Software System Stack:

#### Settlement Layer

The Settlement Layer is the first of the four layers constituting the DeFi software system. It can be comprehended as a base layer providing the platform for DeFi transactions. A settlement layer is paramount because it is the anchor of the financial ecosystem of DeFi. A public blockchain and cryptocurrency are their essential constituents for functioning. For example, Bitcoin and Ethereum are the quintessential representatives of the settlement layer.

#### Blockchain Protocol

These protocols are pertinent rules and regulations necessary to secure the reliable functioning of the system. Compliance with these rules allows operators in this industry to work unhindered. This layer ensures the conversion of the assets into cash in the DeFi ecosystem providing liquidity.

#### Application Layer

This layer simplifies the protocol mentioned above to ensure interface services, making the system and its services available to consumers. For example, this layer can allow borrowing services to be disseminated here. While there are different categorizations of the various layers, these layers mentioned above have been compressed to ensure the readers' understanding and avoid ambiguity.

Having understood the essential components of the DeFi ecosystem, let's understand the actual working process of these systems. Since the DeFi works on blockchain networks only thus, its working process is quite similar to that of blockchains. The DeFi blockchain ensures the security of financial services and transactions by issuing users encrypted keys. These keys enable the users to receive a unique identifier they can only access. This system has two essential keys: private and public keys.

These dual keys cater to different functions. For example, a public key is used by interested service seekers or peers to find the system's owner. The owner can use a private key to access and authorize any financial activity or transactions involving their funds. The private key is crucial as it acts as the owner's digital wallet through which the user can exchange cryptocurrencies. Only the owner can put the transaction into motion by authorizing the transaction with the private key. The transaction is set into action once the transaction request is verified by all the relevant parties involved. It is pertinent to mention that a unique ID accompanies every transaction to maintain high-security standards and prevent any malpractices on the platform. ## Key Features of DeFi

The central characterizing features of DeFi also fall in tandem with the benefits offered by this decentralized method of disseminating financial services across the globe. Most of these features are reinforced by DeFi services' blockchain network. From defining features like decentralization and permissionless nature of the systems to implicated qualities like increased transparency, immutability, etc., DeFi constitutes numerous characteristics. Some of the major ones have been outlined below:

### The Premise of Decentralization

The first and foremost feature of DeFi, as its constituent term indicates, is decentralization. As explained in the previous sections, decentralization means no centralized authority or intermediary to add to the tediousness and intricacies of the basic financial services process. In the absence of any such intermediaries, the seekers of such financial services can access these services without any constraints placed upon them. This feature is elaborated upon in the next part, which is permissionless and closely related to decentralization.

### Permissionless Nature DeFi Systems

"Decentralized finance" defines this feature, and including the word decentralized indicates the flagship feature being permissionless. Decentralization of financial services has allowed the seekers and receivers to interact on a platform that requires no oversight by an external centralized authority like a bank. It also reduces the dependency on banks and other intermediaries while seeking financial services easily accessible by DeFi platforms. This feature confers the advantage of being independent and user-friendly to the DeFi systems. So any individual can access these services with the minimal requisites of a crypto wallet and, of course, an internet connection. For example, Ethereum has its basis in a permissionless blockchain which has assisted the proliferation of this platform. Today, Ethereum is leading the DeFi industry.

The permissionless nature of DeFi's operation might even instigate doubts about security. The security of the operational activities on DeFi's systems is supplemented by intelligent contracts, which ensure that the terms and conditions of financial services are met, thus ensuring the security of the finances. Also, all the transactional activities are stored on the blockchain itself.

### The Premium Level of Transparency

Against the general notion that decentralization might decrease transparency, decentralization offers openness to a great extent. Since the distributed ledger encompasses all the transactional activities on the blockchain network, it provides scope for solidified clarity. Transparency extends to financial data and activities and applies to smart contracts. Transparency in the case of intelligent contracts expands the content of the review of its code by anyone. It ensures the presence of information for each individual.
