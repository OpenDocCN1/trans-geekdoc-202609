# Crypto Exchanges - What Are They?

One of the few issues one can face while investing in cryptocurrencies is that they are not available on your regular exchanges, and the banks usually do not offer the trade. So for this purpose, the investor needs to create an account on a separate platform dedicated to selling and purchasing cryptocurrencies. These platforms are essential for the trade of these coins. This platform is what we call a crypto exchange.

A crypto exchange is a platform where the people who want to sell their cryptocurrencies and those who wish to purchase cryptocurrencies can meet, and then the deal for the same can occur. Crypto exchanges charge a nominal fee for every purchase, which can vary from business to sale and from one cryptocurrency to another. In effect, they work similarly to the regular currency exchanges.

Different crypto exchanges can have different types of user interfaces and user experiences. Usually, they can be a bit confusing for a novice to understand; hence they also provide a beginner-friendly platform, but it usually charges a higher rate than usual.

On a crypto exchange, the trader or investor must set up an account, and only then can he start purchasing or selling cryptocurrencies. To set up this account, one has to go through the required process and fulfill the various regulations, such as KYC or AML. Once made, the bill functions like any other trading account, where you can store the coins or tokens and then make exchanges with the same.

## Payment Options

To make trades on a crypto exchange, one first needs to have funds in his account in business for which one can purchase the cryptocurrencies. You can get money in the report through different methods. Every crypto exchange allows for its payment methods, including Credit cards, debit cards, bank transfers, or PayPal. These methods use traditional fiat currencies for the purchase of cryptocurrencies. Thus you pay an exchange rate that the market has decided, i.e., you can convert your paper currency into a digital currency.

However, some crypto exchanges do not allow fiat currencies to purchase cryptocurrencies. On these exchanges, to buy a cryptocurrency, you need to exchange another cryptocurrency for it. Mostly this means that one has to use the usually common coins such as Bitcoin or Ethereum to buy the otherwise less common cryptocurrencies. The exchange decides the exchange rate for the same based on market forces.

The kind of payment options available on the crypto exchange is essential when deciding which crypto exchange one would choose for their trade.

## Types of Crypto Exchanges

As discussed above, the crypto exchange platforms function differently and have additional features. Every crypto exchange has its features, advantages, and disadvantages. The primary type of crypto exchanges are:

### Centralized Crypto Exchanges

As the name suggests, a centralized crypto exchange has a central authority figure. This authority acts as a third party between the seller and the buyer of the cryptocurrencies. These third parties are called exchange operators. The centralized crypto exchanges are used more commonly for trades since they are easier to use, and thus, even beginners can easily trade on these exchanges. Further, centralized exchanges also allow one to exchange fiat or traditional currencies for cryptocurrencies. However, since all their data is stored in a single space, they are more susceptible to hacks and data leaks and usually have significantly more regulations to be followed. Some of the common centralized crypto exchanges are Coinbase, Bittrex, and Kraken.

### Decentralized Crypto Exchanges

A decentralized crypto exchange is sometimes called a direct trading platform. In this kind of exchange, there are only two parties to a transaction, and no third party overlooks the trades; this system is also called a peer-to-peer exchange. In such a situation, the seller decides the price, not the third party. When cryptocurrencies were created, they aimed to have a decentralized currency system with no central oversight. Hence these exchanges carry the true essence of crypto exchanges. Although they are safer than centralized crypto exchanges since data is not stored in a single space in different servers worldwide, they are harder to use and are not recommended for beginners. Another issue with Decentralised crypto exchanges is that they currently do not allow the purchase of cryptocurrencies with fiat currencies. Some common decentralized crypto exchanges are IDEX, Waves DEX, and Stellar DEX.

### Hybrid Crypto Exchanges

Hybrid crypto exchanges have emerged and are said to be the future of crypto trading. They want to get the advantages of centralized and decentralized exchanges and reduce their disadvantages. Qurrex was the first hybrid crypto exchange.

## How to Choose a Crypto Exchange?

Choosing the correct crypto exchange is an integral part of the investment decision. The choice can depend on different factors, such as:

*   **Coins offered:** The most important thing to check before selecting a crypto exchange is what the business provides all coins. All currencies are not available on all platforms. Hence, going for a venue with more coins is advisable since that would allow one to quickly diversify their portfolio without signing up on multiple crypto exchanges.
*   **Fees:** The crypto exchanges vary in the quantum of fees they charge the traders on the platform. Generally, centralized systems charge a higher price than decentralized ones. Similarly, businesses with more straightforward beginner-friendly user interfaces and user experiences charge higher transaction fees marginally. People trading daily are affected by the prices more than those who only purchase the cryptocurrency once as a way to store value.
*   **Payment method:** The kind of payment methods offered by the exchanges can also impact the choice of crypto exchange. As already discussed, if one wants to pay for cryptocurrency using a fiat currency, they only have to choose from one of the centralized crypto exchanges. Further, if one only wants to use a specific payment method, such as bank transfers, they must select their crypto exchange accordingly.
*   **Type:** As we have seen, there are three different kinds of crypto exchanges; now, choosing the crypto exchange depends on the crypto exchange you want. A person might wish for a decentralized crypto exchange because of its additional security. In contrast, another might wish for an easier-to-use platform on centralized crypto exchanges.
*   **Country-based regulations:** Cryptocurrency regulations vary from country to country and territory to territory. Hence there is a possibility that some crypto exchanges do not work in certain regions. So before choosing a crypto exchange, one must see if it complies with their country's regulations.
*   **KYC:** The Know Your Customer regulations also depend on crypto exchange to crypto exchange. Some have slightly relaxed rules, while others can be stringent. The person can choose the business based on his privacy concerns.
