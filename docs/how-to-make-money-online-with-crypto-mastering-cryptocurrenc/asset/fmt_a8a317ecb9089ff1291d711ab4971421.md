
# DeFi vs Traditional Financial Systems

## Custodianship and Control

DeFi users have absolute custodianship of their funds and can choose when to make the transaction, with whom to make the transactions, and by how much. However, traditional financial systems do not offer such control over the funds and assets. The individual must cede their funds to the central authority in these systems. For example, banks substantiate this premise. Instead of being in control of the owners of the funds, the funds are actually under the power of the top central bank, and then the second bank follows.

## Innovativeness and Interoperability

The innovativeness also characterizes DeFi that it offers in adapting to the evolving standards of expectations of the users. The streak of innovation of these systems is also substantiated by the use cases, as outlined in the next section. With innovation at its core, these systems are getting into many industries. However, due to the centralized approach, the traditional finance ecosystem only offers rapid innovation and adaptation. Due to the centralization of the authority, any proposal for creation goes through a multitude of executives at different levels in the hierarchical structure of these authorities. Such a thorough hierarchical structure ensures that the central authority makes well-deliberated decisions. While such a rigorous approach for multiple checks makes the proposal relatively risk-proof, it often leads to delay or rejection of the proposal altogether. Due to evolving trends, such a time gap might make the proposals redundant.

## Accessibility and Reach

DeFi, being a digital finance system, is available and accessible to all individuals around the globe with the basic requirements of a stable internet connection and crypto wallet. Transactions between people sitting far apart on different continents can also successfully occur. These platforms work 24/7. At the same time, traditional financing systems are bound by time and space constraints. Due to the physical space requirements for the operation of maximum financial activities, these economical systems need to catch up to the evolved systems offered by DeFi. Additionally, due to the condition of human labor to put most financial services, activities, and transactions in motion, these systems need to be more useful in digitization.

## Current State and Challenges

The DeFi systems stand in strong contrast to the conventional financial systems. DeFi has more advantages in the financial world than traditional economic systems. The utility of DeFi systems is on an exponential rise, with more and more industries attempting to find the crossroads between DeFi and its application to complement their functionality. While the DeFi systems are visibly booming with the current digitization trends, they still have a long way to go. Despite the unprecedented proliferation of technology and increased accessibility of Internet services, only 65% of the world's population has Internet access, making it impossible for them to avail the benefits these systems offer. Thus, they continue to seek financial services from intermediaries under the umbrella of traditional economic methods.

Lack of reliable information provides concreteness to an individual's misconceptions about the working of DeFi platforms. They are falsely implicated in digital financial services as a hoax by magnifying and overdoing the risks. While it is imperative to warn the users of the dangers that might befall their digital journey in the finance world, it is essential to make them aware of the mitigation and manageableness related strategies corresponding to the risks. Additionally, many downsides of the DeFi systems have been discussed in the later sections, which contribute to tantalizing these platforms' reputation and trust quotient. Thus, even individuals accessing DeFi services refrain from venturing into these platforms. They instead turn to traditional financial service systems to receive economic benefits.

## Use Cases of DeFi

DeFi has taken the world by a whirlwind with its efficacy, resilience, immutability, and transparency in financial operations. It has set into motion many economic opportunities for its global user base. Due to the plethora of advantages DeFi offers, the scope of its application is already widening. It is expected to widen even further with the refinement of the system and the advancement of technology. DeFi is enabling the integration of digital finance services with multiple industries which initially didn't witness crossroads with these systems. It applies to conventional financial services like borrowing, lending, trading, marketplaces, etc. These economic systems have found application across varied sectors, from increasing the efficiency of traditional financial activities to introducing DeFi in unhackneyed industries. The use cases of this system are already growing at an unprecedented rate and are expected to follow an ascending graph line. A few prominent use cases have been delineated below.

### Borrowing and Lending

With DeFi's influence on systems of finance and transactions increasing, the loopholes in the traditional finance system are becoming all the more evident to the users. Traditional financial markets have proliferation in revenue generation by the interest they pay the lenders and the interest they charge from the borrowers. Such a system often discouraged the borrowers from approaching these markets due to rigid protocols for qualifying for a loan, including viable collateral and a hefty amount of interest charged. The digital ledger technology bolstered by DeFi opens opportunities for borrowers who need a loan and fail to procure it due to rigid requirements. With `smart contracts` in place, lending/borrowing loans and maintaining the terms of conditions outlined in the `smart contracts` smoothens the dual lending and borrowing processes. The dynamics structuring DeFi support peer-to-peer lending and borrowing. This use case is finding widespread application in the form of an increase in DeFi projects about this.

For instance, `Compound` is a DeFi protocol that stands out because it has an autonomous interest basis. `Compound` welcomes lenders to earn interest on various cryptocurrencies. It provides the lenders with tokens, which are then siphoned off to the borrowers at algorithmically decided autonomous interest rates. The `Compound` is highly efficient because the intelligent contract identifies the lenders and borrowers with concurring interests and lays out interest rates following the borrowed and lent assets ratio in the discussion. Another prominent example of such a project is `Pool Together`, which uses akin mechanisms in lending and borrowing money.

### Tokenization

One of the significant use cases of the DeFi system is in the context of tokenization. Tokenization refers to curating, issuing, regulating, and transferring digital assets on a blockchain network. Tokenization is highly pertinent because any form of investment can be converted into tokens for their storage on the blockchain. `Ethereum blockchain` successfully utilizes tokenization and has granted the world a secure and easy means of trading in the form of Ethereum-based tokens. Out of the many advantages conferred by the usage of tickets, the main benefits emerge as fractional aspects of these tokens and available options for their customization. Thus tokenization is one of the central features of the booming DeFi ecosystem due to its digital alternatives and efficacy in complementing trade.
