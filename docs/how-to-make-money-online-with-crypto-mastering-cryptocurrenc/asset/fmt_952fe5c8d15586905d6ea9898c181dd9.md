
# Blockchain Applications Across Industries

## Ride-Hailing Transformation

Ride-hailing companies like Uber and Lyft rule this sector now; however, these companies represent a central authority or a mediator through which the driver is connected to the passenger. With the introduction of companies that use blockchain technologies, companies will offer a direct peer-to-peer network for ride-hailing. This would mean any central authority is not controlling that driver and is free to create his own market space and provide services as he sees fit at their rates. It is also beneficial to the passenger as the cost of the rides will be lower since the intermediaries' commission is removed from the equation. Some blockchain ride-sharing apps, such as Arcade City and Drive, are already available. The users can also make payments through cryptocurrencies on these apps.

## Charity Transparency

How charitable institutions handle donations will also change if they adopt blockchain to track the money in the institution. This way, the donor can follow exactly where the donated money has been utilized and that the institution has not used it for other purposes. This provides transparency in charity which could encourage more people to donate since many do not do it because they think that charitable institutions are corrupt. Various companies are working in this sector, such as Bitgive Foundation, a Bitcoin-based charity, and Givetrack, a blockchain-based donation platform with a permanent record of global charitable transactions.

# Popular Blockchain Companies Making an Impact

## Leading Cryptocurrencies

### Bitcoin

Bitcoin is the original cryptocurrency and the first instrument to use blockchain technology commercially. It was started in 2008. Bitcoin is a peer-to-peer payment network through which you can pay another person without any central bank or authority. Bitcoin uses the blockchain technology underneath it to manage and keep track of the transactions. Bitcoin has popularized the concept of cryptocurrencies among the masses and currently has a market capitalization of around 950 billion dollars.

### Ethereum

Ethereum is one of the most famous blockchain companies and is credited with introducing the concept of smart contracts. Ethereum works in the financial services sector, allowing individuals to lend, borrow and save products via the Internet. It also runs ether, the second-largest cryptocurrency by market capitalization. Ethereum lets people conduct operations through its smart contracts.

### Sciencesoft

Established in 1989, the company serves non-IT enterprises and software product companies and is frequently approached for custom and platform-based development in CRM, Data Analysis, Collaboration & Knowledge Management, and Information Security. The company also offers end-to-end blockchain software and application development. The company serves midsize-large international organizations, including Walmart, IBM, Nestle, eBay, and NASA JPL.

### Ripple Labs

Established in 2012, the company works with the existing financial systems of its customers to improve and streamline their infrastructure. The company provides open-source code and developer tools to further the development of blockchain technology and is the only enterprise blockchain company with products for commercial use.

### R3

R3 is significant in providing distributed ledger technology to banks worldwide. It recently even partnered with the central bank of Switzerland to settle large cryptocurrency-based transactions between banks.

### Pledgecamp

Pledgecamp is a crowdfunding platform. It uses the transaction history and tracing feature of blockchains to allow the backers or investors in crowdfunding projects to see where the money they have put into a project is being spent. On this platform, the money is moved to an escrow account and slowly discharged to the company, and the records are always transparently available to the backers.

### Binance

Binance is one of the largest crypto exchanges in the world right now. Cryptocurrencies are not listed on traditional stock exchanges and are only available for purchase and sale through crypto interactions. The crypto exchanges typically charge a small fee for every transaction on their platform.

### Zweispace

Zweispace is a Japan-based company that has developed a blockchain-based automatic settlement procedure for wills and inheritance. The deceased's assets are passed on to the beneficiaries automatically on the confirmation of the trustee's death, thus avoiding the lengthy court battles that typically follow a will.

### Republic

Republic is an investment platform based on blockchain technology. One can invest in real estate, startups, and even cryptos through this platform. Republic is a secure platform where very particular companies are afforded the option of being listed; hence one can be assured of their investments through Republic.

### SALT

SALT is a blockchain platform that works as a bridge between cryptocurrencies and the real world. Users can leverage their crypto assets on SALT to procure loans in fiat currencies. The loanees can take loans for up to a period of 3 years.

### Drife

Drife is an Indian ride-hailing app that is currently operational only in the city of Bangalore. It is a blockchain-based app where the drivers and passengers are provided with a peer-to-peer platform on which they can decide the kind of service and price by themselves. This means that the app is not paid a commission for each ride. However, the passenger must pay a yearly fee for the use of the application.

### Winding Tree

Winding Tree helps hotels and airlines by providing a decentralized platform for making travel bookings. This reduces the commissions the intermediaries earn and directly connects the customers to the service providers. Winding Tree has already worked with airlines like Lufthansa, Air France, and Air Canada.

### Propy

Propy is a blockchain-based company that works in the real estate sector. It uses intelligent contracts created under blockchains to process purchases, sales, and real estate leases. Realtors can use it to streamline their process, making it easier to go through a deal and decreasing the time for each sale. This also helps to give a more customer-friendly experience.
