
# DeFi Use Cases: Stablecoins, Asset Management, Insurance, and More

## Cryptocurrency Volatility and Stablecoins

One of the significant downsides of cryptocurrency is the profound volatility that is widespread in the crypto ecosystem. Such fluctuations of prices that experience frequent ascent and descent in their value result in the reduction of the reliability of cryptocurrency. `Stablecoins` pose as a savior in this context. `Stablecoin` can be understood as a type of unique cryptocurrency because it is tied with an asset or bulk of them. These assets can be anything ranging from fiat to gold or even any other kind of cryptocurrency. Their consolidation with acquisitions offers stability in contrast to the volatility in the crypto market. They also provide the blockchain greater credibility as a reliable payment method. `Stablecoins` are now used for borrowing, lending, etc., in the `DeFi` ecosystem.

## Process of Asset Management

`DeFi` allows users to exercise greater control over their assets. With no central authority for regulation, decentralization helps the users gain absolute control of their assets. It enables the users to manage their assets according to their volition. It can involve buying, selling, lending, or transferring digital assets. By removing an intermediary, only the users can access and manage their assets in any way they deem perfect. For example, in many `DeFi` projects, including `Gnosis Safe`, `Argent` facilitates the users' complete control of their information and ensures safe data storage with the user only.

## Application in the Insurance Industry

Another major use case of `DeFi` is in the insurance industry. `DeFi` is revolutionizing the insurance industry by eliminating the significant limitations posed by the traditional handling of insurance systems. Such rules include unfathomable amounts of paperwork, the flawed claim handling process, time-consuming procedures, etc. The insurance industry also experiences setbacks due to the fraudulent practices of false claims. Blockchain's decentralized technology can verify the presented documents, ownership, policy registration-related details, and claim authenticity. With smart contracts in place, the process of financial exchange as a result of insurance becomes easier for both the seeker and the insurance company.

## Compliance Supported by the KYT Mechanism

Know-your-customer (`KYC`) protocols majorly guided compliance in countering-the-financing-of-terrorism and anti-money laundering (`AML`). However, `KYC` protocols often interfered with `DeFi`'s premise of privacy. It also did involve the external authority at one step or another contradicting the decentralization aspect of `DeFi`. Therefore, `DeFi` has introduced a relatively evolved and better Know-Your-Transaction (`KYT`) mechanism. This mechanism lays the ground for the focus to shift from the identity of the user to digital addresses and the transaction behavior of the users. Such a mechanism assesses the live behavior of transactions and maintains the users' privacy. This mechanism helps assess risk or crisis proliferating in the system, early detection of which can combat eventual large-scale frauds and siphoning of hefty amounts.

## Decentralized Exchanges

Decentralized exchanges (abbreviated as `DEXs`) refer to the transactions of cryptocurrency that occur `P2P` (peer-to-peer) without the involvement of any central authority, thus supporting the basic premise of `DeFi`. The assets in such exchanges are non-custodial to any external exchange platform. It allows users to interact, transact, and maintain control of their assets. The significant advantage conferred is in terms of mitigation of risks. Since the assets are not under the direct supervision of any external authority or platform, it reduces the chances of hacking, fraud, and price manipulation. Currently, many `DEXs` are used in the user-friendly and easily accessible `DeFi` space. The most popular ones are `Liquality`, `AirSwap`, `Mesa`, etc.

## Bolstered Digital Identity

Digital identity systems based on blockchain are coming to focus as viable and efficient identity systems. Mainly when amalgamated with the `DeFi` protocols, these identity systems can experience a boost in their efficiency in terms of immediate processing when required to avail of financial services. These systems are also changing the indicators of creditworthiness. According to the traditional finance systems, the means to authenticate the credibility of financial service seekers used to be their possession of assets (owned houses) and income level. However, the digital identity reinforced by `DeFi` and blockchain protocols authenticates the seekers by their transactional behavior, financial activities, online reputation, or any other attributes that deem practical and necessary. This helps financial service seekers overcome the hindrances from collateralization and receive requisite services based on indicators they are mindful of.

## Risk Mitigation and Management

The flagship features of `DeFi` highlight transparency and decentralization, which have, in turn, become highly crucial for mitigating risks arising on the respective platform. This is done by thorough data analysis that the users can engage in with the data repository provided to them. Following their data analysis and being well informed about the risks associated with and predicted, the users can make decisions of their volition. Such comprehensive frameworks help users know the risks and ways to mitigate them. It also keeps the users updated about the financial opportunities arising in the market. The proliferation of `DeFi` has brought tools like `DeFi Pulse` and `CoDeFi Data` to light, which are involved in the processes mentioned above.

## DeFi and Prediction Markets

Despite the critical views regarding the ethics of predicting and betting, these attract many people. The prediction markets allow the users to place bets or vote in favor or against the outcome of an event that hasn't occurred. Events under the umbrella of predictive use cases include predictions regarding sports events and election outcomes, to name a few. The prediction markets' functionality resonates with the traditional predictive needs regarding function only.

The amalgamation of blockchain with these markets eliminates the need for an intermediary. There are many contenders for the top spots in the list of `DeFi` prediction markets—for instance, `Augur`, `Gnosis`, `Polymarket`, `Predictit`, `FTX`, etc. `Augur` has emerged as the strongest contender on the list. This platform specializes in prediction and allows users to bet on political or economic events. `DeFi`-backed prediction markets were spotlighted in 2020 during the U.S. presidential elections due to the number of users involved in predicting results and the magnitude of bets in terms of values. `Augur` was reported to record the amount of about $8 million during these elections.

## Gaming and DeFi: The Wonder of No-Loss Games

An essential feature of `DeFi` is the composability that the system offers. This composability has motivated the product developers to design protocols of `DeFi` into the platform itself. The latest video games have options for in-app purchasing, which provokes the users to use the live currency to purchase characters, elements, or other aspects of the game. The developers in the field of gaming are integrating their area with the `DeFi` systems by incentivizing the playing users.

Curating reward models often do this to target the relish points of the users utilizing `DeFi` coins. For example, `PoolTogether` is a game that is based on the premise of no loss. The users are indicated to submit the `DAI` stablecoin, purchasing digital tickets. These stablecoins are pooled together and stashed away till the end of the month. At the end of every month, this stash again resurfaces to award one player who earned the interest, and the deposited amounts are returned to all the players.
