
# NFTs: Use Cases and Purchasing Guide

## Empowering Artists

However, it is not just famous artists like Beeple profiting from NFTs. Even smaller artists can convert their art into NFTs and thus monetize their work. Further, using smart contracts, it is also possible for the artist to attach a clause to the NFT that gives them a cut of the price every time an NFT is sold to protect their economic interest if their work gets popular and increases in price in the future. NFTs would also remove the need for mediators and other intermediaries between the artists and the art collectors. NFTs could finally bring the digital art world on par with the physical art world.

## Identification

Non-fungible tokens are also being used for providing proof of identity. NFTs can be programmed to contain unique information within them, and at the same time, we know that NFTs can only have a single owner at a time, and the data within an NFT cannot be mutated; thus, NFTs can be configured in such a way to issue identities, licenses, qualifications, etc. For example, suppose a person is to be given an identity card for club membership. In that case, the digital version of the card can be configured with an NFT to ensure that it is the original card shown upon entry. In case of identity theft, even if the culprit can copy the identification cards, they will not be able to copy the NFT that is attached to these cards.

## Collectibles

Another of the most common and popular uses of NFTs is currently in the collectibles sector. We know that NFTs are non-fungible and unique tokens, meaning that every NFT differs. Collectibles had been absent in digital form as they could easily be copied and duplicated, but this problem has been solved with NFTs. It is now possible to create an item in a finite amount. Thus even if a product is copied, its authenticity cannot be proven. This creates a form of exclusivity in the ownership of the said items. Some of the collectibles that are being produced with NFTs are:

### Trading Cards

For years, trading cards have been one of the most sought-after collectibles in the real world. Trading cards can be of many types, such as for sports like baseball or basketball trading cards or in the form of games such as Pokémon or Yu-Gi-Oh. The rarer cards have sometimes been sold for millions of dollars. Now we are seeing the start of digital trading card games like Gods Unchained.

### Avatars

CryptoPunks were one of the first NFTs and have become some of the most popular ones. The purchase of the limited edition alien CryptoPunks has regularly exceeded the value of a million dollars. These punks have no intrinsic value but are just being collected because of their rarity. One can use them as avatars or display pictures on various platforms.

### Original Copies

Just like the original documents of various products in the real world, such as comic books, novels, and album covers, are kept by collectors and worth thousands of dollars, the digital version of the audiobooks, comics, etc., are also collected by collectors. Not just books, but recently even the original copy of the first tweet by the founder and CEO of Twitter was sold for 2.9 million dollars to the highest bidder by using NFTs.

### Sports

Sports have also started venturing into the world of NFTs. NBA sold the first sport-based NFT. Using NBA Top Shot, collectors can purchase the original highlight of an image or a sequence of play by their favorite players during an NBA game. Formula 1 and boxing are both launching their own NFT collections.

### Domain Names

It is also possible to purchase and collect domain names. The ownership of the domain name is stored in the NFT. One of the common ways of buying and storing names is through the Ethereum Name Service.

## Finance

NFTs, though popularly used for their superficial purposes, can be helpful too. This provides the NFT holders with value for holding the NFT in itself. For example, if an NFT allows the holder to enter an exclusive pool of investors, the said NFT can be traded or sold for that right. Their holders can also stake NFTs to earn passive income. Some NFTs also double as discount coupons or combos, such as in the case of BAKE coupons. These financial NFTs can also have artwork attached to them, but their value comes from their use, not their art.

## Music and Video

Just as it is possible to attach an NFT to an image file or a GIF, it is also possible to link NFTs to audio and video files. By connecting the NFT to an audio file or album, musicians and video makers can start selling the original versions of their products. Just because a person holds the NFT to a song or video does not mean they have the copyright to it. It continues to be with the artist. Currently, the recording studios and other intermediaries earn from songs and music videos; using NFTs would diminish the need for these third parties, thereby increasing the income for the musicians. Kings of Leon was the first major rock band to release a digital version of their album; each album copy was uniquely numbered with NFTs.

## Real-World Assets

Real-world assets, such as shares, deeds, contracts, etc., could also use NFTs. Forgery and fraud are commonplace in the physical form. However, blockchain technology could prove its authenticity if these assets were given proper NFTs. In 2021, an NFT was created, which included the ownership of a property in California. Whoever owns the NFT also holds ownership of the said property. It could also help prove the ownership of valuable items such as jewelry.

## High Fashion

High fashion is another probable use case for NFTs. Most luxury fashion has no purpose other than the identity and exclusivity its ownership offers. This makes it a very logical use for NFTs. People can buy digital fashion products, such as digital sneakers or sunglasses, which are authenticated using NFTs. Big fashion houses have already started producing NFT-based digital products. A luxury virtual hoodie by Overpriced was sold for 19,000 pounds.

## Licensing

NFTs could be of great use in the software licensing sector. Firstly they would bring down piracy in the industry as it is tough to forge your ownership details when stored on NFTs. Secondly, NFTs allow people to sell their licenses directly to others who need them. So people won't be stuck with licenses or year-long memberships. They could go to an NFT marketplace and sell it to someone who needs it.

## How To Safely Buy NFTs And Where?

Non-fungible tokens, like other crypto tokens, are unavailable for sale and purchase on regular stock exchanges. Some specific platforms and businesses deal with NFTs. Some of them have been created specifically for this purpose. The process of purchasing an NFT is straightforward and can be done by following a few steps.

### Purchase Cryptocurrency

The first step in purchasing an NFT is cryptocurrency. NFTs are based on blockchains, each of which has its cryptocurrency. NFTs must be bought by paying for the value in the blockchain cryptocurrency. It is not possible to purchase NFTs directly using fiat currencies. They must be first converted to cryptocurrencies. If one wants to get an Ethereum blockchain-based NFT, one must purchase ether, and if it is a Binance Smart Chain-based NFT, one must purchase Binance coins. Cryptocurrency can be bought on various crypto exchanges like Binance, Coinbase, etc.

### Transfer Crypto to a Wallet

The next step is to transfer your ether or other cryptocurrencies to a wallet like MetaMask. These wallets store access to cryptocurrencies and provide security from hacking. It is also possible to directly use the crypto to pay for the NFT if it is bought on the identical blockchain, for example, if purchased on Binance.
