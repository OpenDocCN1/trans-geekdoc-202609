## INTRODUCTION

If you read my book *BlockChain Technology for Beginners*, you know that Bitcoin is the first scarce resource that can be digitally verified. As I ponder the creation of this book, my latest literary masterpiece, "How to Make Money Online with Crypto," I am struck by the sheer astonishment that such a lucrative opportunity exists in our lifetime. Let's seize the moment and make it count. We don't want to look back regretfully and wonder what could have been.

You might wonder who I am to author a book on generating income through Crypto. In 2012, I took the first step towards financial freedom by opening my first account with Coinbase. Although the report was created much earlier, it wasn't funded until later, in 2017. Please take advantage of potential gains more than I did. Invest wisely and avoid my mistake of missing out on purchasing Bitcoin at $100. Although early 2017 was still a great time to invest, I regret not taking advantage of the opportunity sooner. Learn from my experience and make informed investment decisions.

Even in 2017, I made mistakes such as buying more Ethereum than Bitcoin and missing out on the historical BTC bull run of 2017. In 2018 I tried to diversify my portfolio by investing in several ICOs (Initial Coin Offerings), similar to IPOs (Initial Public Offerings) but for the cryptocurrency world instead of stocks. In later years purchasing, selling, and constructing NFTs, I can help you quickly navigate that world of digital assets. With my good and bad experiences in DEFI projects, by participating in hundreds of them, I can help guide these areas as well.

I also invested in pyramid scheme coins, and owning too many Altcoins can be overwhelming, as I know it well. To make matters worse, having your wallet hacked and losing six figures worth of coins is a hard lesson I had to learn. That's why the most crucial aspect of making money in crypto is knowing how to keep your investments secure. This guide explores the best ways to protect your assets and ensure your peace of mind. In the dynamic world of Crypto finance, a lot has transpired over the last six (6) years. Our book delves into all of it, leaving no stone unturned.
