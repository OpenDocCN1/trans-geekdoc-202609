
# Transformative Applications of Blockchain Technology Across Industries

Banking is one of the biggest industries that blockchain technology will completely transform. Currently, banks store all data on centralized servers, which are vulnerable to data hacking and large-scale fund theft, resulting in billions of dollars in losses annually. Once banks start storing transaction history on the blockchain, they will gain additional security guarantees. Furthermore, another advantage for users or clients of these banks is that data will become transparent and accessible to everyone.

When such data is open to the public, financial violations will decrease, and banking failures that have occurred over the years (such as the Lehman Brothers case) will not recur.

Specific applications of blockchain in banking include:

- Cross-border payments will become faster.
- Loans will be easier to obtain through peer-to-peer lending rather than central banks.
- Clearing systems used by banks will become more efficient.
- Cryptocurrencies will serve as a parallel payment system alongside fiat currencies.

## Government Record Keeping

The record-keeping industry, especially the government sector, will benefit greatly from the advent of blockchain technology. Because data recorded in the blockchain features immutability, permanence, and timestamping, it will ensure that no false identity, ownership, death, birth, and property transfer certificates and records are issued. This will also make obtaining these certificates and information easier.

The data transparency provided by blockchain technology means everyone can view this data; it is no longer hidden. People can easily verify whether the government records provided to them are authentic. This will reduce welfare fraud, and government facilities can reach those in need more accurately.

## Healthcare

Data storage is one of the most significant problems facing the healthcare sector. Patients must repeatedly fill out various forms, documents, and records at different hospitals. Blockchain technology can quickly solve this problem. In addition, all hospitals can access patients' past medical histories and injury records from a transparent, authentic open source.

Globally, more and more doctors and medical professionals advocate for a universal healthcare system that digitally contains all patient information needed by doctors. This universal healthcare system can be implemented through blockchain technology.

## Media and Entertainment

With the emergence of online media platforms like Netflix, Amazon Prime, and YouTube, the media industry has undergone significant changes over the past decade. Using blockchain technology in this field can better track payments for purchases of these accounts and prevent multiple people from sharing a single paid account.

It will also help media companies track the release time, method, and true market response of products (such as songs, movies, etc.), avoiding data manipulation by promoters. Unique views or impressions will be counted via blockchain, not duplicate counts.

## Supply Chain

Once blockchain is finally adopted and integrated, the supply chain sector will be transformed. The focus on global product traceability makes it necessary for multinational corporations to track the origin and producer of every product. Consumers are increasingly concerned about not using products sourced from regions employing child labor or violating labor laws. Blockchain technology can record and track this data. Through it, everyone can access data to ensure the authenticity and validity of data provided by enterprises. Blockchain will also enhance supply chain streamlining. If producers can trace back to product manufacturers, waste can be reduced.

## Transportation

The transportation sector itself will be a huge beneficiary of blockchain technology adoption. In the aviation sector alone, we see fees paid at multiple checkpoints involving agents, insurance companies, passengers, and airlines. With blockchain, cost settlement will be much faster, thereby improving booking convenience. Additionally, since everyone can view booking numbers in a single central space, the efficiency of the overbooking process can be improved.

A blockchain system can be established to provide a single identity factor running through all different steps of boarding, reducing time costs for passengers and travel companies. Blockchain will also help reduce redundancy and discrepancies in data collection in the shipping and freight sectors.

## Agriculture

Introducing blockchain technology into the agricultural sector will significantly change how logistics are handled. First, it helps ensure that only high-quality, healthy crops are provided to consumers. If product quality is poor, the origin can also be traced. One of the most significant problems farmers face is delayed payments, which they need to purchase seeds for the next season; without funds, next season's production will be severely hit. Blockchain can make payments near-instantaneous, which will greatly help farmers.

## Retail

The retail sector can utilize blockchain technology in many ways, primarily for inventory tracking. This easily accessible data option will allow everyone to see inventory usage and rates, thereby rationally scheduling replenishment processes and saving retail enterprises stockpiling costs.

In addition, in the retail sector, blockchain will make it easier for customers to verify the authenticity, safety, and ethical sourcing of products provided by retail stores. A transparent supply chain visible to suppliers also helps resolve disputes between suppliers and retailers more quickly, saving legal costs.

## Insurance

In the insurance sector, blockchain applications aim to improve operational efficiency. A significant difference from other sectors is that technology application in this sector does not aim to create new products or services, but to reduce costs, increase service speed, and provide better customer service. Through transparent and permanent records, the industry will reduce insurance fraud or duplicate insurance. At the same time, it also helps the insured, enabling them to receive claims and payouts from insurance companies faster; delays would severely impact their lives. Smart contracts, as an application of blockchain technology, will also help reduce the time and cost of the underwriting process.
