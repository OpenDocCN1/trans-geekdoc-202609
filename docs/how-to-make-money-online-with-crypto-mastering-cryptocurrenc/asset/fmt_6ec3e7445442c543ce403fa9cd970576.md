# Chapter 1: The Cryptocurrency Revolution

## Understanding The Basics Of Cryptocurrency

Electronic virtual currencies originated way before the inception of the first-ever scalable crypto - Bitcoin. However, these currencies weren't termed cryptos because they do not have the cryptography tech embedded in them. But these are the very first attempts that eventually led to the invention of the currently used cryptocurrencies.

The revolution of digital currencies started in the Netherlands in the 1980s. A group of developers invented an electronic card system to avoid the thefts in Gas Stations that happen at night. Whoever wants to access Gas can use those cards, so the use of real money is eliminated.

The second attempt towards virtual currencies is made by 'David Chaum.' He is an American cryptographer and the inventor of Blinded Cash. The USP of this currency is that it is untraceable. That is, transactions made using this virtual currency cannot be tracked. Chaum used complex encryption techniques to achieve this feat. However, this crypto has failed because of various scalability issues.

We must know that eCash, B-Money, Bit Gold, and Hashcash are notable failed attempts in this space. eCash was the first conceptualized digital token currency to transfer between individuals. B Money was portrayed as an electronic cash system that is anonymous and secured at the same time. Bit Gold is considered the first digital asset with complete decentralized functionality. Bit Gold was pegged to the value of gold while moving away from centralization.

HashCash was one of the most successful digital currencies, getting everything in place except for scalability. It functions almost like Bitcoin and has a similar consensus algorithm too. This digital currency was truly ahead of its time, which could be one reason for its failure. Finally, in 2008, the whitepaper of Bitcoin was released, while in early 2009, the first ever Bitcoin block was mined, and the rest is history.

Cryptocurrency is a kind of digital money that exists only online. There is only an actual coin or bill if you utilize a service that enables you to exchange cryptocurrencies for physical tokens. Typically, you trade cryptocurrencies with another person online, through your phone or computer, without a third party's assistance, like a bank. Cryptocurrencies are unrelated to valuable things – they are irrelevant to anything of actual worth in the real world, which causes their value to fluctuate wildly, as you have probably seen.

In contrast to equities, bonds, artwork, real estate, or precious metals, cryptocurrencies have no intrinsic value or use. For instance, gold is a critical component of electronics and jewelry — and therefore has worth in addition to its scarcity. On the other hand, cryptocurrency costs money solely because someone else owns it and needs money to deliver it to you.

Cryptocurrencies address the following critical issues associated with the use of cash. There is a need for a centralized authority to control these objects' value, manufacturing, and authenticity. ### Cryptography

Cryptocurrency uses cryptography — the art of concealing and disclosing information — to guarantee the security of user data and the integrity of transactions. As the name implies, a cryptocurrency is digital or virtual money protected by encryption, making it hard to counterfeit or double-spend.

### Blockchain

A blockchain is a subset of Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT), a distributed database distributed among many operators (nodes, computing devices, etc.) Numerous cryptocurrencies are decentralized networks powered by blockchain technology—a distributed ledger maintained by a distributed network of computers.

This is the underlying technology that drives cryptocurrency. It is a digital ledger that validates transactions, accounts, and balances. Blockchain may be used for various applications outside financial transactions, including supply chain management, monitoring art ownership, and even digital collectibles.

A node is a word associated with blockchain technology that will be used throughout this essay. A node is a discrete component of a larger data structure known as a blockchain. Without nodes, the system would implode. Cryptography and blockchain technology assist cryptocurrencies in the creation of new coins, the enforcement of legal transactions, and the establishment of a secure system.

### Decentralized

A distinguishing characteristic of cryptocurrencies is that they are not issued by any central authority, which supposedly protects them against government intervention or manipulation. As shown by Bitcoin, decentralization implies that all authoritative source is spread evenly across all peers on a network, and there is no single point of failure. For example, to "hack" Bitcoin, someone must access at least 51% of the vast network of computers that operate Bitcoin, which is considered impossible.

### Peer-to-Peer

Without a broker, cryptocurrency may be transferred directly between two individuals. These transactions are made with minimal processing costs that benefit the network, allowing customers to avoid the high transaction fees associated with more conventional money transfer services.
