+   i. **Eigen Face Algorithm:** Eigenface is a prominent face recognition technique that employs principal component analysis (PCA) to identify characteristics from faces and is using them for classification [9].

+   o One of the key downsides of the Eigen algorithms is that it is aware of the differences in lighting and face expression. Specifically, Eigenface presumes that the light levels and facial gestures in the training data are comparable to those in the testing dataset. If there are large differences in lighting or face expression between both the training and testing datasets, the system may not perform effectively.
