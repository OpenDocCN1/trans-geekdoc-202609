# 13.5 Conclusion

This chapter discussed in detail various swarm-based optimization algorithms. In spite of their widespread utility, there are numerous challenges to overcome in swarm intelligence algorithms. We hope this chapter will encourage further study in this area and highlight the recent advancements along with their pros and cons. This chapter seeks to review everything from classical optimization to the most recent optimization algorithms as discovered by multiple web-search engines.
