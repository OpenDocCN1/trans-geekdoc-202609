# Abstract

Angiogenesis is one of the hall-mark in cancer which is initiated in preneoplastic lesions, from the preexisting vessel. New vasculature supports the tumor growth, progression, invasion, and metastasis. Hypoxia is one of main mediator of angiogenesis, in addition to the vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF), along with, basic fibroblast growth factor (bFGF), platelet-derived growth factor (PDGF) etc., which are secreted from tumor cells as well as from stromal cells. Ephrin EphRs, angiopoietin Tie, and Delta–Notch signalling pathways have crucial role in angiogenesis regulation. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has been approved various VEGF inhibitors for treatment of advanced cancer. However, acquired therapy resistance limits the benefit of anti-angiogenic therapy. This therapy resistance proved the limited knowledge of angiogenesis in cancer, creating the idea for its further deep understanding. Machine learning or artificial intelligence is one of the recently technologies that involves analytical strategies, which are currently applied to biomarker discovery, gene expression, and image understanding in digital pathology. With the availability of deep learning techniques, researchers are able to easily and accurately quantify morphological structures and are freed up to focus on advancing science. The current review article describing the angiogenesis, its role in cancer, mechanism, in addition to the machine learning approaches in angiogenesis understanding.

Keywords: Angiogenesis, cancer, resistance, deep learning, molecular mechanism
