# 7.3.4 Effectiveness of the Proposed Descriptors

The effectiveness of the proposed global and local shape descriptors is evaluated by considering the intra class and interclass variation among images. We consider four similar images of “bell” class (“bell-13”, “bell-14”, “bell-15”, and “bell-16”) and four dissimilar images (“elephant-6”, “camel-3”, “deer-6, and “dog-10”) from the MPEG-7 database given in Figure 7.3a. The efficacy of the local shape descriptor is presented in Figures 7.3b and 7.3c, which reveal insignificant variation among histogram features of images of similar class and significant variation in images of dissimilar classes. ZMs magnitude and phase coefficients discriminate contents of images of one class from the other effectively. It can be seen from Figure 7.3d and Figure 7.3f that the behavior of magnitude and phase coefficients is consistent for intra class images, whereas significant variation is observed among inter class images as presented in Figure 7.3e and Figure 7.3g, respectively. The phase coefficients are plotted after estimating the rotation angle and making phase correction using Eq. (7.4).
