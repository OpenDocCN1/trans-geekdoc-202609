$$S = \sum_{\tau=1}^{N} z_{\tau}$$ (18.6)

where S = sub-bands
The modulus of the co-efficient $z_{\tau}$ can be found by $|z_{\tau}|$. The equations for norm1 energy (mean) and norm2 energy (standard deviation) features are

$$e_1 = \sum_{\tau=1}^{N} |z_{\tau}|$$ (18.7)

$$e_2 = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{\tau=1}^{N} |z_{\tau}|^2}$$ (18.8)
