# 2.7.2 Face Detection and Identification in Real-World Situations

Face detection and identification have become crucial technology for real-world security and surveillance applications. However, various obstacles and restrictions must to be solved in order to assure accurate and dependable performance in these applications [14].

Encounter limitations and difficulties with detection and identification in practical situations: The main issues with face identification and recognition in real-world situations include variations in illumination, position, and occlusion. Faces can undergo significant changes due to changes in lighting, making it challenging to recognize and identify them. Posture adjustments can also produce considerable changes in the look of the face, making it difficult to compare it to a reference face. Face detection and recognition can also be hampered by occlusions such as hats, scarves, and sunglasses.

Techniques for adjusting to variations in stance, light, and occlusion: Many methods exist to address the problems of face detection and recognition in practical contexts. Techniques such as histogram equalization and contrast stretching can be used to equalize the image’s illumination when dealing with fluctuations in lighting. Techniques such as 3D face modelling and posture normalization can be used to turn the face into a canonical stance when dealing with pose variances. Techniques like as partial face recognition and multi-modal face recognition can be used to recognize faces even when parts of the face are blocked when dealing with occlusions.
