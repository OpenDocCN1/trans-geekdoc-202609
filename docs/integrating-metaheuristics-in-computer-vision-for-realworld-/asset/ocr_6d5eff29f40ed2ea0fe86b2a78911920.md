# ## 7.5.3.2 Evaluation of Image Retrieval Performance on Geometric and Photometric Transformed Databases

The P – R evaluation on the synthesized system is determined for geometric transformations i.e., rotation, scale, and translation as given in Figure 7.13a through 7.13c. It is perceived that all methods deliver upright performance under image rotation for the recall values up to 80%, although the performance of the devised approach is better than other methods. The improvement in the performance of the proposed system becomes more prominent when the recall assumes higher values closer to 100%. The trend for scale invariance shown in Figure 7.13b is similar for the proposed, optimal similarity, adjacent phase, and IZMD methods. However, the results of TCS and QLS is reduced in this test. The variation in P-R performance for all the methods for translation is very significant. The proposed system provides far better results than that provided by other methods. This is due to the local features of the proposed system as the histograms involve the computation from the centroid. Next, we evaluate the results of the proposed system over databases with photometric transformations. Figure 7.13d and Figure 7.13e present P–R curves for the noise and blur image databases, respectively, and have similar performance. We see that the projected solution shows its superiority over rest of the approaches. However, P-R performance for JPEG compressed images is similar for the proposed, optimal similarity and adjacent phase techniques, but it is reduced for IZMD followed by TCS and QLS as depicted in Figure 7.13f.
