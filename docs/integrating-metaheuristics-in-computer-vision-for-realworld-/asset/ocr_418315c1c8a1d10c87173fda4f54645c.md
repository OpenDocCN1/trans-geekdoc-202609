A dimensionality reduction technique called feature selection includes choosing a subset [16] of the most important characteristics from the initial collection of features. The goal of feature selection is to reduce the dimensionality of the data while retaining the essential information in order to improve the effectiveness of the learning algorithm and reduce the risk of overfitting. Filter methods, wrapper methods, and embedding methods are the three fundamental categories of feature selection techniques.

# 6.2.1 Filter Methods

The fundamental principle of supervised feature selection, which is widely used for classification issues, is the relationship or association between the feature and the class label.

Selecting pertinent features from a dataset using a filter is a common technique. According to this method, each feature's importance is assessed using a particular metric or criterion, such as correlation or mutual information, and features are sorted according to their scores. The best traits are then chosen for additional examination or modeling. We will go over the filter feature selection approach in detail, along with its benefits and drawbacks, in this part.

Filter feature selection is a widely used method in data science and machine learning because it is simple, rapid, and computationally efficient. No matter the amount or complexity of the dataset, it can be used. The main principle underlying filter feature selection is to assess each feature's importance independently of the others using its inherent qualities, such as its statistical distribution or its correlation with the target variable [8].

Correlation and mutual information are the most often utilized metrics in the selection of filter features. While reciprocal information reflects the amount of knowledge one variable has about the other, correlation measures the linear link between two variables. Higher ratings in both instances suggest greater significance. The chi-square, ANOVA, and Gini index are additional metrics that can be employed in the filter feature selection process.
