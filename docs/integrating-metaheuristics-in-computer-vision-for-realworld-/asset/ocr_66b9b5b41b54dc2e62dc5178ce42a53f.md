Modern methods yield an average of around 1000 points per image and rely heavily on the retrieval of local data. A graphic dictionary then describes each of these points (visual pattern characteristic of a portion of the image). Hence, a bag of visual words serves as an image’s representation (BoW). As part of the indexing process, all the words that are present in the database are compared to each visual word that appears in an image. Such strategies are, however, challenging to apply in a setting with many available photos due to their computational costs. The bag of word method uses to quantify local descriptor generated on the area of interest and extracted by an invariant detector.

By the search of textual records, the quantization indices of these descriptors are referred to as visual words. The frequency of appearance of visual words (histogram) serves as the representation for the image. This representation makes it possible to calculate image similarity quickly. Building the bags of words involves these three steps:

+   1. Identification of information from the image.
+   2. Quantification by comparing traits to nearby visual terms.
+   3. Producing a frequency histogram that will be used to assess the image’s overall global representation. So, it is possible to provide a compact representation of the images while still reducing the size of the vector descriptors of images by employing the bag of words method. The representational groups of the Bag of Features (BOF).

K-mean clustering is frequently used by defining a codebook for k visual words. The closest centroid is given to each local descriptor for dimension d from the picture. The histogram for all image descriptor assignments to visualized words creates the BOF representation. As a result, a normalised k-dimensional vector is generated. The histogram can be normalised in a number of ways. BOF vector is normalised by the Manhattan distance that are known in empirical distribution. Using Euclidean normalisation is an additional popular option. The quality of this representation has been altered in several ways. One of the most common is substituting soft quantization methods for k-means. The BOF representation’s key benefits are

+   1) Its compactness, or lower storage requirements, and
+   2) Speed of the Search.
