## 7.5 Experimental Study and Performance Evaluation

This section describes the analysis and observations carried out by our extensive set of experiments. It has been investigated by the recent studies that hybrid approaches to image retrieval system are being used for improving the retrieval accuracy. Therefore, we compare our system against two hybrid approaches given by Wei et al. and Qi et al. [25, 26], referred as TCS and QLS, respectively. We also compare our results with invariant Zernike moment descriptor (IZMD) [34], optimal similarity [35], and adjacent phase [36], where both ZMs magnitude and phase are used as features. In the studies by Revaud et al. [35] and Chen and Sun [36], the image matching performance is observed to be promising for geometric and photometric transformations; nevertheless, their performance degrades for subject database, where variations among sample intra-class samples exist. The algorithms are implemented in VC++ 9.0 for the proposed and the above mentioned approaches using the similar strategies as mentioned in their research papers. The following sub-sections describe the performance measure, database construction, and experiments results.

### 7.5.1 Precision and Recall (P-R)

We evaluate the performance of the image retrieval system using precision and recall as follows:

P = \frac{relevant images retrieved from the database}{total number of images retrieved} \times 100

R = \frac{relevant images retrieved from the database}{number of relevant images to query image in the database} \times 100 \quad (7.16)
