The position, different types of shape, and its sizes of the thyroid nodules are manifold depends majorly on the patient. Only the skilled resource person will diagnosis the thyroid nodules from lymph nodules correctly. At present, machine learning algorithm is used universally to perceive the thing. But the artifacts present in the echocardiography images are having serious issue also the edges present between the different tissues are vague [3]. So that, the obtained output details are not evident from any other tissues and the tissue recall precision is not up to the mark. Though, there are some webworks [3–5] implemented to improvise the content of image, still the recall precision is not good for the echocardiography images because of its rugged background. At the end, this complicates the skilled person to mark the ground truth point correctly to detect the large thyroid nodules from the ultrasound images.

Therefore, to overcome this difficulty simple realization of thyroid nodule in the ultrasound images based on L1-IDNN model is suggested. Basically, the suggested model consists of three major steps: preprocessing of thyroid images, Fusion, features extraction and thyroid nodules recognition. The major idea of this work is to enhance the image and remove the noise in the thyroid nodule, and to extract the features for fusion of thyroid images using hybrid pyramid algorithm [ 6]. At the end, the conjecture of the thyroid nodule will exactly classify the features in deep by the proposed L1-IDNN model.

# 19.2 Related Work

Zhou et al. [7] give a highly automatic and basic model called online transfer learning (OTL) for the differential diagnosis of benign and malignant thyroid nodules from ultrasound (US) images. Methods: The OTL method uses the hybrid algorithm model of transfer learning and online learning. Zhao et al. [8] proposed an automated diagnosis for the thyroid nodule image which combines the basic algorithm of convolutional neural networks (basic components like convolution, pooling, and fully-connected layer) and image texture features.

Shahroudnejad et al. [9] presents an end-to-end pipeline for thyroid nodule segmentation and classification technique. A residual dilated UNet (ResDUnet) model is proposed for thyroid nodule segmentation. The output of resDUnet is fed to two rule-based classifiers to differentiate the structure and echogenicity (ability of the sound to bounce from the surface) of the segmented nodule. Zheng et al. [10] reviews the study of deep learning method in the classification of benign and malignant thyroid nodules using ultrasound image, then gives the clear idea about the current research progress on the invasiveness of thyroid nodules.

Chen et al. [11] classify the work as two layers, i.e., the word vector and sentence presentation layer. Author used attention mechanism (the method is based on the covariance between the pixel values with all other pixel values) and Bi-Directional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) in every layer. And evaluation claims that the proposed algorithm is having better performance.
