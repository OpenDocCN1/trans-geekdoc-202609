# 5.2.1.3 Digital Twin in Drug Repurposing

Different stages regarding process development and advancement in the pharmaceutical manufacturing industry demonstrate the possibility of using Digital Twins to support smart manufacturing. The choice of a manufacturing route and its unit activities can be made much more quickly at the process design stage because of a digital twin’s ability to represent genuine components using a range of models. By removing the need for actual trials, digital twin simulations can be used to research process variability and forecast product quality, productivity, and process parameters, saving time and money [18]. Throughout the operational period, real-time process performance may be monitored and viewed, and the Digital Twins can continually assess the system to provide insights for control and process optimization. Real-time system monitoring and control utilizing ongoing data gathering from equipment, Process Analytical Technology (PAT), finished commodities, intermediate products, and an ongoing global modelling and data analysis platform are some important prerequisites for achieving smart manufacturing with Digital Twin. By utilizing strategies like Continuous Manufacturing (CM), Quality-by-Design (QbD), PAT and flowsheet modelling implementations, the pharmaceutical sector has made some progress
