$d_{BC}^{mag}(Q,T) = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{M} ||Z_i^Q| - |Z_i^T||}{\sum_{i=1}^{M} |Z_i^Q| + |Z_i^T|}$

$d^{phase}(Q,T) = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{i=1}^{M} \left[ \frac{\min(|\boldsymbol{\varphi}_i^Q - \boldsymbol{\varphi}_i^T|, 2\pi - |\boldsymbol{\varphi}_i^Q - \boldsymbol{\varphi}_i^T|)}{\pi} \right]^2$

where \(\boldsymbol{\varphi}_i^Q\) and \(\boldsymbol{\varphi}_i^T\) represent the ZMs phase coefficients of query and training images, respectively, and \(\boldsymbol{\varphi}_i^Q\) is the corrected phase of the query image, which is obtained after the estimation of rotation angle \(\phi\) and making phase correction. From above equations (7.13), (7.14), and (7.15), it is clear that \(d_{BC}^c(Q,T)\), \(d_{BC}^{mag}(Q,T)\), and \(d^{phase}(Q, T)\) are in the interval [0, 1]. In the above equation, \(w_c\), \(w_{mag}\) and \(w_{phase}\) are the weight factors for \(d_{BC}^c(Q,T)\), \(d_{BC}^{mag}(Q,T)\), and \(d^{phase}(Q, T)\), respectively. Therefore, in our experiments, for simplicity we assign equal weights to \(w_c\), \(w_{mag}\) and \(w_{phase}\) , i.e., \(w_c = w_{mag} = w_{phase} = 1/3\) to obtain equal contribution of all the features.
