# 6.3.1.3 Advantages

+   1) Lowering the dimensions of the data: One of PCA’s biggest benefits is its capacity to do so while still preserving the majority of the crucial details. It can be difficult to display data in high-dimensional databases, and performing any analysis on the data might be computationally expensive. By reducing the dimensionality of the data, PCA can facilitate visualization and analysis.
+   
+   2) Finding patterns in the data: Patterns that may be difficult to spot in the original high-dimensional dataset can be discovered using PCA. The most significant differences in the data are highlighted by translating the data into a lower-dimensional space in order to do this. This can aid in locating collections of observations that might be useful for additional investigation.
+   
+   3) Removing noise: A further benefit of PCA is its capacity to eliminate noise from the data. Noise in the data may be introduced by measurement errors or other sources. By converting the data into a lower-dimensional space with less noise, PCA can identify the sources of noise and eliminate them. This may aid in increasing the accuracy of later data analysis.
+   
+   4) Improving computational efficiency: PCA can be used to reduce the dimensions of a dataset, which can significantly improve computational efficiency. This is particularly important when dealing with large datasets that require extensive computations to analyze. By reducing the dimensions of the data, PCA can help in reducing the computational time required for analyzing the data.
