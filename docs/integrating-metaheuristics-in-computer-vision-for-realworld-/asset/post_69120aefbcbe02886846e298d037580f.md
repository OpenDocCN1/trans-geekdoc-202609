
## A Thyroid Nodule Detection Using L1-Norm Inception Deep Neural Network

Saranya G.
Department of Biomedical Engineering, Vel Tech Rangarajan Dr. Sagunthala R&D Institute of Science and Technology, Avadi, Chennai, India

### Abstract

In the current scenario, various computer-assist diagnosis models are available to detect the thyroid nodule. The complication with the model is to correctly identify whether the affected nodule is normal or abnormal. At present, echo (sound wave) is advised to do the initial and timely process to diagnosis the thyroid nodules. But, attaining an acceptable prognosis from ultrasound image hangs on the radiologist skill and further circumstance. Prodigious pressure is put on the automated version and for the safe mechanism to monitor the ultrasound images effectual. The Proposed work gives a stacking methodology for automatic thyroid nodule classification using L1-norm inception deep neural network (L1-IDNN). The medical ultrasound image is used to perform the fusion process, and it is carried out to strengthen the image by applying adaptive histogram equalization technique and variance stretching. The thyroid nodule images are fused with the help of hybrid pyramid fusion algorithm. The result claims that the initiated model provides preferable values in respect of accuracy compared to the normal machine learning technique.

**Keywords:** Thyroid nodules, ultrasonography, L1-IDNN, hybrid pyramid fusion, stacking method

But the artifacts present in the echocardiography images are having serious issue also the edges present between the different tissues are vague [3]. At the end, this complicates the skilled person to mark the ground truth point correctly to detect the large thyroid nodules from the ultrasound images.

Therefore, to overcome this difficulty simple realization of thyroid nodule in the ultrasound images based on L1-IDNN model is suggested. Basically, the suggested model consists of three major steps: preprocessing of thyroid images, Fusion, features extraction and thyroid nodules recognition. The major idea of this work is to enhance the image and remove the noise in the thyroid nodule, and to extract the features for fusion of thyroid images using hybrid pyramid algorithm [6]. At the end, the conjecture of the thyroid nodule will exactly classify the features in deep by the proposed L1-IDNN model.

### 19.2 Related Work

Zhou et al. [7] give a highly automatic and basic model called online transfer learning (OTL) for the differential diagnosis of benign and malignant thyroid nodules from ultrasound (US) images. Methods: The OTL method uses the hybrid algorithm model of transfer learning and online learning. Zhao et al. [8] proposed an automated diagnosis for the thyroid nodule image which combines the basic algorithm of convolutional neural networks (basic components like convolution, pooling, and fully-connected layer) and image texture features.

Shahroudnejad et al. [9] presents an end-to-end pipeline for thyroid nodule segmentation and classification technique. A residual dilated UNet (ResDUnet) model is proposed for thyroid nodule segmentation. The output of resDUnet is fed to two rule-based classifiers to differentiate the structure and echogenicity (ability of the sound to bounce from the surface) of the segmented nodule. Zheng et al. [10] reviews the study of deep learning method in the classification of benign and malignant thyroid nodules using ultrasound image, then gives the clear idea about the current research progress on the invasiveness of thyroid nodules.

Chen et al. [11] classify the work as two layers, i.e., the word vector and sentence presentation layer. Author used attention mechanism (the method is based on the covariance between the pixel values with all other pixel values) and Bi-Directional Long Short-Term Memory (Bi-LSTM) in every layer. And evaluation claims that the proposed algorithm is having better performance.

Avola et al. [12] targets on several modes of data which is generated by thyroid nodule ultrasonography. It acts as a model of CAD system by providing a classification output for the thyroid nodule into the benign and malignant process. Notably, the proposed system has the advantage of cues provided by an ensemble of resource person to guide the learning phase of a tightly connected convolutional neural network (DenseNet). The ensemble data is collected by various networks skilled on ImageNet, which includes AlexNet, ResNet, VGG, and so on.

Song et al. [13] proposed a hybrid multi-branch convolutional neural network locate on feature extraction to remove the features and to classify the thyroid nodule by ultrasound images. Initially, it is designed a backbone convolutional neural network to fetch splitted feature maps and a classification network used as a reference branch. Then, the result is added with feature removing branch in the network to perform multi-removing batch on feature maps, to reduce the impact on classification caused by the similarity of local features between benign and malignant thyroid nodule images. Finally, based on softmax prognosis of different branch feature maps, the network utilize a weighted cross-entropy loss function to train the proposed binary-classification network.

Chang et al. [14] performs data-driven analysis of expert behavior. An experimental model is organized based on belief rule base, which is essentially a white box for knowledge representation and uncertain reasoning. The model, with 2744 examination reports of thyroid nodules are included in the period from January 2012 to February 2019 that have been collected from a tertiary hospital located in Hefei, Anhui, China, experimental results are obtained from conducting missing validation, self-validation, and mutual validation.

Qin et al. [15] developed a method that is based on the mixture of conventional ultrasound and ultrasound elasticity images based on a convolutional neural network. It introduces well off feature data for the classification of benign and malignant thyroid nodules. Foremost, the conventional neural network model executes the training process in advance on ImageNet and transfers the information to the ultrasound image domain by learning network so that depth information may be removed and little trial can be processed.
