# # Advancement of ML in Smart House

# ## Abstract

The Internet of things is turning our houses into smart houses by automating our home appliances. The smart house system manages, secures and controls to have better management over the problems we face in our houses. The smart houses with IoT are still need Improvement for better efficiency. This can be achieved by the developing ML over house applications. Image processing gives intelligence to the house through which our smart house system can be improvised.

**Keywords:** Smart house, IoT, machine learning

# ## 12.1 Objective

The objective of this paper to give an outline of the smart house system and some of its construction, how IoT plays a roll to automate the house appliances and how to improve the efficiency of smart house system with developing ML in use of image processing. And discuss the scope of this system.

# ## 12.2 Introduction

Smart houses, as name suggest it is smartening our houses that it can manage itself in some of the work that needs human intervention. Let’s see a daily scenario of a weekdays, in morning we wake up and hurry up to school, college or office. In the morning we use lights in bathroom, fan in our living room, we use some electrical appliances such as microwave oven in kitchen, we may use a charger to charge the phone, etc. And it is human nature to forget to switch off the appliances and we would remember it when we are in our office or college. That’s when we feel bad when we can’t remember it. So, it is likely to be automated. So how are we going? to automate it? Well, we can use IoT to automate our house.
