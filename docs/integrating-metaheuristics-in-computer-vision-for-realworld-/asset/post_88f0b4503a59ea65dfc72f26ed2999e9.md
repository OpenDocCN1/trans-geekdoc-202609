## 4.4 Concluding Remarks and Future Scope

In the era of digitalization and e-commerce, digital fraud is becoming an increasingly pressing issue. Victims suffer significant losses, mental and emotional distress, and the misuse of banking credentials. To combat these frauds, measures must be strengthened. The proposed work compared various classification models and concluded that random forest was the best suited. The proposed work also aimed to take a step toward data processing and data analytics, which would help in effective and accurate identification of fraudulent data. This, in turn, would assist banks in enhancing their methodologies for protecting customer data and securing transactions.
