cooling performance but also the effects of ambient temperature and experimental protocol. Therefore, different designs based on the consideration of end-use scenarios is necessary for the improvement of PCGs. Moreover, the development of these types of PCGs requires the impact of human factors, including metabolic rates and physical activities, on individuals’ responses during the use of cooling garments under normal working conditions and should be studied in the future.
