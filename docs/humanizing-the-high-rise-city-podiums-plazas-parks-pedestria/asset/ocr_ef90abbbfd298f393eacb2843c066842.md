# MOTHER AND A BABY STATUE IN EAST NANJING ROAD, SHANGHAI

One of the iconic landmarks in Shanghai is the Mother with a Baby and Pram statue, located on East Nanjing Road, a bustling, pedestrian commercial street (Figure 14.10). Nanjing Road is divided into East Nanjing Road and West Nanjing Road. East Nanjing Road is a pedestrian street with many shops, restaurants, and entertainment venues (See Chapter 4). West Nanjing Road is a more upscale area that showcases modern architecture and luxury brands. Along the street, visitors can also find historical landmarks such as the Jing’an Temple (See Chapter 3). East Nanjing Road is one of Shanghai’s most well-liked and energetic shopping avenues. It travels around 1.5 kilometers, from the Bund to People’s Square. Along the route, guests may discover a variety of shops, eateries, cafés, and entertainment options to suit their interests and financial constraints.
