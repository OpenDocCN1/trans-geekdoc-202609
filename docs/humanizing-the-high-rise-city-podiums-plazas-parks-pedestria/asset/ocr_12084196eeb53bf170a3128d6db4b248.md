**The Garden Rhapsody**: Gardens by the Bay presents a distinctive light and sound extravaganza, harmonizing music with a captivating display of lights emanating from the iconic Supertrees. The show has different themes throughout the year, displaying Asia's rich and diverse cultures, opera romance, fantasy magic, and more. Some themes include Journey through Asia, Spring Blossoms, Opera in the Gardens, World of Fantasy, and Garden Rhapsody: STAR WARS Edition. The shows also feature local artists such as Aisyah Aziz, Joanna Dong, Linying, and Rani Singam, who perform renditions of songs from movies and Singaporean classics.

**The Avatar, The Experience**: A new attraction opened in Gardens by the Bay in Singapore in 2023. It is based on the popular sci-fi movie *Avatar*, directed by James Cameron. The attraction allows visitors to explore the lush and exotic world of Pandora, the fictional planet where the movie takes place. Visitors can interact with various elements of Pandora, such as the bioluminescent plants, the flying banshees, and the Na'vi people. The attraction also features a 4D immersive theater that simulates the experience of flying on a banshee through the floating mountains and waterfalls of Pandora. The Avatar is a unique and thrilling adventure showcasing nature's beauty and diversity.

Gardens by the Bay offers a variety of amenities to enhance the visitor experience, including restaurants, cafes, gift shops, and restrooms. Wheelchair accessibility and other facilities for disabled people are also available throughout the gardens. Gardens by the Bay is committed to sustainability and incorporates innovative environmental strategies in its design and operations. The Supertrees, for example, are not just decorative but also serve as vertical gardens and rainwater collectors (Rowe et al., 2019).
