The plaza offers various seating options, including steps, ledges, and stone benches (Figures 3.12 and 3.13). Movable chairs can be arranged at different spots and angles, creating a dynamic and inviting space for people to relax and socialize. During lunch hours, Chase Tower temporarily sets out tables and chairs along the edges of the lower levels to accommodate employees and visitors. The plaza offers many eatery options, from fast food to fine dining and catering to different tastes and budgets.
