# **Chapter 10: Elevated Parks**

Elevated parks refer to creating green spaces, gardens, recreational areas, or public parks on elevated structures, such as bridges, viaducts, highways, or other elevated infrastructure. This innovative approach allows urban areas to maximize limited space by repurposing elevated structures that might remain underutilized or solely dedicated to transportation. Chapter 10 argues that elevated parks can significantly humanize high-rise cities by providing much-needed green spaces, recreational areas, and communal gathering spots in otherwise densely built urban environments. Elevated parks enhance urban living by incorporating natural elements like plants, trees, and greenery into the landscape. This connection to nature relieves the concrete and steel of high-density areas, creating a refreshing and inviting atmosphere. The chapter uses the High Line in Manhattan, New York City, as a leading example to show the advantages and benefits of elevated parks. It also discusses challenges such as upkeep costs, fair access, visitor management, and possible gentrification impacts. In essence, elevated parks provide an innovative solution to balance the density of high-rise cities with the human need for open, natural spaces. By offering places to relax, connect, and experience nature, these parks enhance residents' overall quality of life and contribute to the humanization of densely built urban environments. In summary, Chapter 10 offers valuable lessons for urban planners, policymakers, and communities who want to use elevated parks as tools for urban renewal.

# **Chapter 11: Sky Parks and Gardens**

Sky parks and gardens are innovative ways of bringing nature to elevated urban spaces, such as rooftops and upper floors. Chapter 11 argues that sky parks and gardens are crucial in humanizing high-rise cities by introducing elements of nature, greenery, and outdoor spaces into urban environments often characterized by vertical density and limited ground-level open areas. These elevated green spaces create a more livable, balanced, and harmonious citiescape, offering numerous benefits to residents, visitors, and the overall urban ecosystem. Dense cities can feel overwhelming due to the constant hustle and bustle. Sky parks and gardens provide residents with an oasis of tranquility where they can escape the fast-paced urban environment and reconnect with nature. The presence of greenery and plants in sky parks and gardens offers a visual break from the concrete and steel of high-rise buildings, providing a calming and restorative atmosphere. The chapter presents real-world examples of sky parks and gardens from different cities, demonstrating the diverse approaches and designs used in creating these spaces. These examples inspire urban planners, architects, and developers to integrate nature into vertical urban landscapes. In short, Chapter 11 showcases how sky parks and gardens enhance the quality of life in high-rise cities by providing green spaces, promoting well-being, and fostering community interaction, showcasing how innovative design can create functional and emotionally enriching urban environments.
