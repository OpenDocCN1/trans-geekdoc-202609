By following these planning and design principles, integrating pedestrian networks, promenades, and streetscapes in a dense, high-rise city can transform urban areas into vibrant, safe, and enjoyable spaces that prioritize the needs and well-being of pedestrians and promote sustainable and inclusive communities. The following chapter discusses the roles of public art in humanizing the high-rise city.
