and boutiques, the coexistence of these features creates a rich and engaging environment catering to various interests and preferences.

+   **8. Public Seating and Gathering Spaces:** The provision of public seating and open spaces encourages people to gather, relax, and interact. This communal aspect contributes to a more humanized urban experience, providing opportunities for shared activities and social connections.

+   **9. Integration of Greenery:** Incorporating green elements within plazas, such as trees, plants, and landscaping, enhances the humanization of high-rise cities. Greenery adds a natural touch to urban spaces, promoting well-being and environmental sustainability.

+   **10. Collaborative Urban Planning:** Successful humanization requires collaborative urban planning involving designers, planners, policymakers, and the community. Times Square and East Nanjing Road emphasize the importance of collective vision in shaping vibrant, inclusive, and human-centric urban environments.
