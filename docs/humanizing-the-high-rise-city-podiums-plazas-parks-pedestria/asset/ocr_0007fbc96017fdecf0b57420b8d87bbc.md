**6.2** Shenzhen Central Park: A vibrant urban oasis offering relaxation, recreation, and cultural engadgement. The park provides a range of workout apparatus, sporting amenities (including tennis courts, soccer fields, and basketball courts), and a cultural plaza accessible to the public. It is a vital green space that enhances the well-being and quality of life for residents and visitors alike. *Source: Photographs by author.*

+   - **Landscape Design**: The park's landscape design combines natural elements, water features, gardens, and pathways to create a visually appealing and diverse environment for all to enjoy.
- **Eco-Friendly Practices**: Shenzhen Central Park promotes environmental stewardship through sustainable practices such as rainwater collection sys- tems, energy-efficient lighting, and native plant species.
- **Artistic Elements**: The park features sculptures and art installations that add aesthetic and cultural value to the space, making it an engaging and thought- provoking destination.
