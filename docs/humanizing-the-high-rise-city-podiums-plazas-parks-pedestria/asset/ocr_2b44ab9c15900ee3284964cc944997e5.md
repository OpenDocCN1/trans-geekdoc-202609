+   1. **Partnerships and Community Engagement:** Revamping aging urban infrastructure requires serious collaboration and an earnest commitment by local, state, and federal governments and community-based organizations, individuals, businesses, and institutions.

+   2. **Persistence:** River infrastructure projects take a long time to study, redesign, and implement. From vision to completion, the Chicago Riverwalk project took over 25 years. During a quarter century, there were moments of frustration and giving up. However, persistence and perseverance have helped keep this project's momentum (Kamin, 2022a). Even after completion, planning and design efforts to upgrade it continue.

+   3. **Urban Rooms:** The urban room concept (See Chapter 7) helps break vast spaces into smaller ones that fit human scale and senses. It also helps create multiple destinations along the river, offering visitors more choices and enriching their experiences with layered, diverse activities. As such, the “Urban Room” concept, although was proposed a century ago, retains its valuable applications to today's cities.

+   4. **Continuity and Connectivity:** While the urban room concept proved effective, some factors are needed to support it, including continuity and connectivity. A “pedestrian spine” that threads through these urban rooms will enable visitors to reach their destinations quickly. The Chicago Riverwalk serves as a “connective tissue” that seamlessly connects pedestrians with sidewalks, stairways, and elevators (for the handicapped) and links to neighboring activities, communities, and the city at large. Continuous public access and uninterrupted paths entice more people to walk along the river and visit urban rooms (Movahhadedeh et al., 2015).

+   5. **Right Location:** What has also contributed to the connectivity of the Chicago Riverwalk is being in the epicenter of Chicago Downtown. As such, in addition to providing amenities and entertaining facilities, it serves as a transport conduit connecting many different parts, activities, and downtown transportation nodes. It has supported a multimodal transport model, which Chicago has worked on for the last two decades.

+   6. **Intimacy with Water:** It would be more interesting if visitors could interact with the river's water. The Chicago Riverwalk allows visitors to do many activities, including fishing, canoeing, docking, boarding boats, sitting, relaxing, dining, drinking, picnicking, and feeding the ducks. It also has a water plaza that allows children to play with water. However, water quality is hoped to improve so visitors can touch the water with their hands, dip their feet, and even swim in the river.

+   7. **Extending Operation Hours:** The Chicago Riverwalk operates from 6 a.m. to 11 p.m. Since it enjoys a central location in the city and connects many activities downtown, it could benefit from extending its hours of operation.
