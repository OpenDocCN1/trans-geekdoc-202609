People's Park is a significant historical landmark in Shanghai. It was initially a horseracing track during the Shanghai International Settlement era in the early 20th century. After the Chinese Communist Party took power in 1949, the horseracing track was transformed into a public park, which opened to the public in 1952. It is an asset for Shanghai, combining community-oriented design, cultural diversity, environmental sustainability, and historical heritage. It provides a space for people to enjoy various activities, such as relaxing, playing, learning, and interacting, improving their well-being and happiness. People's Park is more than just a park, it is a humanizing force in the urban landscape of Shanghai (Wang et al., 2016); (Figure 5.6).

# HIGHLIGHTS

# Positive Aspects

+   - **Historical Significance**: People's Park holds historical importance as one of Shanghai's oldest parks, and its cultural heritage adds to its charm and identity.
- **Central Location**: Situated in the city's heart, the park's central location makes it easily accessible and a popular destination for various activities.
- **Variety of Amenities**: The park offers a range of amenities, including walkways, seating areas, gardens, and ponds, catering to different preferences and activities.
- **Botanical Garden**: The park's botanical garden houses a diverse collection of plants, offering a learning experience and a peaceful setting.
- **Cultural Elements**: The park features statues, sculptures, and other cultural elements that reflect the city's history and provide points of interest for visitors.
- **Recreational Activities**: People's Park hosts various recreational activities, such as dancing, Tai Chi, and cultural performances, contributing to its lively atmosphere.

# Areas for Improvement

+   - **Wayfinding and Signage**: Improved wayfinding elements and signage could enhance visitors' ability to navigate the park's expansive areas and amenities.
- **Equitable Access**: Ensuring that the park is accessible to individuals with disabilities is vital for creating an inclusive space that welcomes everyone.
- **Program Diversity**: Continuously diversifying the programming and events can cater to a broader range of interests and age groups, ensuring that there's always something for everyone.
