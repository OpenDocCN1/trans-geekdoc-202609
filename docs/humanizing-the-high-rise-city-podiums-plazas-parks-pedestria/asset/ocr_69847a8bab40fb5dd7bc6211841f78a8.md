# 6 City-Wide Parks

City-wide public parks are ample green spaces that cover urban areas and provide a variety of amenities for the entire city and its population. Within their sprawling spaces, a diverse spectrum of amenities and facilities is meticulously curated, catering to the multifaceted dimensions of recreation, education, and conservation. Unlike the localized nature of neighborhood parks, which intimately serve specific communities, city-wide parks are masterfully designed to bestow their manifold recreational, cultural, and environmental benefits upon a broader and more diverse population. Oversight of these monumental landscapes typically falls under the purview of local government entities or non-profit organizations, transforming them into comprehensive hubs of activity. These carefully crafted and diligently maintained sanctuaries are an enduring testament to a city's unwavering commitment to elevating the collective well-being and enhancing the quality of life for all its inhabitants (Al-Kodmany, 2018; Forsyth, 2021; Parakh et al., 2017; Talen, 2010).

City-wide and local public parks offer ample benefits (Refer to Chapter 5) but differ in scale and impact. Here are some differences (Tate, 2015; Scott & Jackson, 1996):

+   - City-wide parks are larger, serving a broader population and offering extensive recreational opportunities like sports fields, playgrounds, and cultural amenities. They foster social interactions and community by providing a common ground for residents to engage in activities and events. On the other hand, neighborhood parks are smaller and cater to local communities, offering accessible green spaces for relaxation, social interaction, and localized recreational activities.
- City-wide parks offer various leisure activities, such as hiking trails, sports facilities, picnic areas, and water bodies for boating or fishing. Neighborhood parks, on the other hand, provide localized opportunities for physical exercise, such as jogging paths, basketball courts, or playgrounds. Both types contribute to individuals' well-being and health by encouraging active lifestyles.
