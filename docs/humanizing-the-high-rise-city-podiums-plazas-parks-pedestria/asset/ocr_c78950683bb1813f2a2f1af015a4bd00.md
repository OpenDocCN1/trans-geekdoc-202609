# Plant and Tree Integration

Oasia Downtown is known for its extensive vertical greenery that covers a significant portion of the building's façade. The red aluminum mesh is a framework for climbing plants, allowing vegetation to grow and thrive. The building's facade incorporates specially designed planting niches accommodating diverse plant species. These niches provide a conducive environment for plants to flourish and contribute to the building's greenery. The sky gardens within Oasia Downtown feature a variety of plants, including trees, shrubs, flowering plants, and grasses. The vegetation is strategically placed to create a layered and visually pleasing landscape while providing shade, improving air quality, and reducing the building's carbon footprint. Further, integrating plants and trees in Oasia Downtown promotes natural ventilation by allowing air to flow through the building, reducing the reliance on mechanical cooling systems. The greenery helps to cool the surrounding areas and enhances thermal comfort for occupants (Figure 12.3).

A comprehensive maintenance plan supports the plant integration in Oasia Downtown. Regular care and upkeep, including watering, pruning, and monitoring for plant health, are carried out to ensure the longevity and vitality of the greenery. The building also employs sustainable practices such as rainwater harvesting and efficient irrigation systems to minimize water usage (Boby et al., 2019; Generalova et al., 2017).
