## BOOK OUTLINE: TRAVERSING THE MANUSCRIPT

### Chapter 2: Podiums

Chapter 2 examines how the base of a tall building, encompassing the podium and entrances, influences the perception and experience of the building. The chapter argues that the base is a crucial element in creating a positive and welcoming impression, rather than focusing only on the height and shape of the skyscraper. It examines real cases to identify common pitfalls and successful strategies in designing tall building bases, focusing on creating a human-scaled, inviting entrance for visitors and residents. The passage delves into the design principles and best practices for podiums and entrances, emphasizing the importance of openness, greenery, and varied forms to enhance tall buildings’ architectural appeal and urban integration. It introduces the concept of “sculpted podiums,” which generate visual interest and curiosity. The goal is to make the base of a building a point of arrival, offering a sense of hospitality and setting the tone for the overall building experience. In essence, Chapter 2 offers a valuable guide for crafting podiums and entrances that align with human-scale considerations.

### Chapter 3: Public Plazas

Public plazas are open spaces within cities that function as focal points for social interaction, community activities, and cultural events. Chapter 3 explores how urban plazas at the base of skyscrapers can improve the social and human aspects of high-rise living. It examines the benefits of well-designed plazas in high-rise projects and neighborhoods, highlighting their role in supporting a city’s economic, social, cultural, and political dimensions, fostering community interaction, cultural diversity, and civic participation, and providing insights for future design and management. The discussion aims to encourage the development of plazas that increase the city’s overall quality of life and well-being, providing residents and visitors with a healthier, more inclusive, and enjoyable urban experience. By prioritizing the creation of attractive and lively public spaces, Chapter 3 suggests that high-rise cities can cultivate a stronger sense of community, enhance social interaction, and enrich the overall human experience within these dynamic urban environments.

### Chapter 4: Streets as Plazas

“Streets as Plazas” is a concept that reimagines urban streets as more than just thoroughfares for vehicular traffic. It envisions streets as vibrant and active public spaces prioritizing pedestrians, social interaction, and community engagement. This approach seeks to transform streets into dynamic plazas that contribute to the overall livability and quality of urban environments. Chapter 4 explores how transforming streets into pedestrian plazas can enhance the urban experience in a high-rise city. It shows how pedestrian plazas can create vibrant public spaces for people to walk, socialize, and enjoy the city. The chapter discusses the environmental, social, and economic benefits of this urban design strategy. It also addresses the challenges and opportunities of implementing it in different contexts and scales. The chapter uses examples from around the world to demonstrate how pedestrian plazas can improve the quality of life in dense and vertical cityscapes. By prioritizing pedestrians, supporting sustainability, and building community, Chapter 4 suggests that streets as plazas can make the high-rise city more human-friendly and livable.

### Chapter 5: Public Parks

As urbanization and the proliferation of tall buildings continue, public parks become even more critical for providing green spaces, recreational opportunities, and a connection to nature within densely populated urban areas. Chapter 5 discusses how public parks can improve the quality of life in high-rise cities. Public parks offer many benefits for residential and commercial areas, such as improving the urban scenery, reducing air pollution, enhancing physical and mental health, and strengthening social bonds among residents. They also create green spaces, recreational amenities, and leisure activities, making high-rise cities more livable and appealing. The chapter examines the planning, upkeep, and integration of public parks in the urban context, highlighting successful park initiatives that use innovative park design strategies, such as green infrastructure, sustainable practices, and community involvement. The discussion urges urban planners, designers, and policymakers to prioritize establishing and conserving these essential green spaces, supporting health and well-being, and creating a sense of equilibrium and harmony in the urban setting. By incorporating public parks, high-rise cities can achieve a harmonious blend of nature and built form, enriching the human experience and promoting sustainable urban development.
