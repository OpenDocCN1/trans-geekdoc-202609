# Plant and Tree Integration

The selection of plant species for Bosco Verticale was carefully considered to ensure the vitality and sustainability of the vertical forest. The chosen plants include various trees, shrubs, and other vegetation suited to the local climate. The selection prioritizes species with unique root systems, adaptability to urban environments, and resistance to wind and pollution. The balconies of Bosco Verticale accommodate a different selection of plants, creating a diverse and visually dynamic facade. The two skyscrapers are adorned with various deciduous plants, such as beeches, yellow acacias, oaks, maples, ash trees, ferns, and ivy, in addition to the evergreen species. The trees are strategically placed at various heights, with taller trees on the lower floors to provide residents privacy and maximize visual impact. Light exposure, wind patterns, and artistic composition are some considerations that went into the intentional distribution of the plants throughout the balconies. The trees are supported by a complex system of structural elements and specially designed planters (Belcher et al., 2018; Boeri & Insulza, 2010). The ultimate configuration creates a lush and layered effect, resembling a natural forest ecosystem or a vertical forest (Figure 12.1).

The ongoing maintenance and care of the plants and trees in Bosco Verticale are critical to their longevity and vitality. A dedicated team of horticulturists and experts oversees the regular pruning, watering, and fertilization of the vegetation. Careful monitoring and management ensure that the greenery remains healthy and well-maintained.

# Awards and Recognition

Since its completion, Bosco Verticale has received several recognitions, certifications, and awards for its innovative and sustainable design, including:

+   - The 2014 International Highrise Award
- The 2015 Best Tall Building Worldwide by the Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat (CTBUH)
- The 2018 Emporis Skyscraper Award

Overall, the architectural design and plant integration in Bosco Verticale aims to create a harmonious and sustainable living environment. The towers' unique form, combined with the extensive presence of vegetation, contributes to the aesthetic appeal, ecological value, and overall well-being of the residents and the surrounding urban context.
