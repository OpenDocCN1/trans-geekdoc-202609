The choice of light-colored interior marble, complemented by granite, integrated stainless steel, and glass exterior elements, collectively reinforces the feeling of openness and transparency at the ground level. The sophisticated lighting system enhances the visual appeal of the lobby, contributing to a warm and welcoming atmosphere. This meticulous attention to design elements evokes a sense of hospitality, making the lobby not just a transitional space but a destination that invites people to linger and engage with the building’s interior (Goettsch, 2012).

In summary, the podium design of 111 South Wacker is characterized by a transparent and visually connected lobby, a strategic use of materials such as white marble, a circular ramp generating intricate patterns, and a sophisticated lighting system. Its sister building, Bentley Bay North Tower, is a 24-story residential condominium. Both towers were designed by Arquitectonica and completed in 2005. The Bentley Bay complex rises in sail-like elegance near the shore of Biscayne Bay.
