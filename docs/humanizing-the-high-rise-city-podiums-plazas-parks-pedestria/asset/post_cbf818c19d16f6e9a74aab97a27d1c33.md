
## Hangzhou Qianjiang CBD Sculpture Park

The park is easily accessible via public transportation for residents and visitors (Wu et al., 2018). The park, spanning 25 hectares (62 acres), is an open-air gallery that showcases a diverse collection of sculptures and art installations by local and international artists, blending nature and art. Hangzhou Qianjiang CBD Sculpture Park features diverse artworks in various styles, sizes, and materials, creating a captivating atmosphere amid lush greenery and winding pathways. This city-wide initiative aims to foster creativity and enrich public spaces, showcasing the city's dedication to enhancing public spaces.

The park features a beautifully designed landscape with lawns, gardens, and tree-lined pathways, blending lush greenery, vibrant flowers, and well-maintained plants for a serene and picturesque atmosphere. The park allows visitors to engage with nature through leisurely walks, idyllic picnics on the grass, and moments of tranquility in carefully designed spaces. Amid the urban surroundings, the park's blend of artistic installations and natural beauty creates a harmonious environment for artistic appreciation and relaxation, making it a distinctive and multifaceted destination within Hangzhou (Al-Kodmany & Ali, 2013).

The Hangzhou Qianjiang CBD Sculpture Park stands out for its emphasis on interactivity within its art installations. A notable feature is the incorporation of sculptures designed to encourage physical engagement or interaction with sound and light elements. This intentional design not only elevates the overall experience for visitors but also fosters a deeper and more personal connection with the artworks. The interactive nature of the sculptures brings a sense of playfulness and creativity to the park, catering to a diverse audience and making it a dynamic and engaging space where individuals of all ages can actively participate in the artistic environment (Al-Kodmany, 2017).

The Hangzhou Qianjiang CBD Sculpture Park's design integrates expansive plazas and open areas, serving as dynamic venues for cultural events, performances, and art exhibitions. These spaces provide a platform for local artists to showcase their talents and foster a sense of community by bringing people together to celebrate diverse art forms. The park's thoughtful landscaping, including gardens, walkways, and open spaces, invites visitors to explore and appreciate the diverse range of sculptures on display (Wu et al., 2018). With sculptures varying in style and material, the park creates a rich tapestry of artistic expressions, offering a harmonious blend of abstract and figurative pieces representing a spectrum of artistic perspectives (Figures 8.3, 8.4).

## KEY TAKEAWAYS: PLANNING AND DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Here are some recommendations on how sculpture parks can help humanize high-rise cities:

1. **Integrate Sculpture Parks in Urban Planning**:
   - Incorporate the concept of sculpture parks into urban development plans.
   - Designate suitable areas within the city to establish sculpture parks, ensuring accessibility and proximity to residential and commercial areas.
   - Integrate these parks into the urban fabric to connect art, nature, and the built environment seamlessly.

2. **Prioritize Public Access and Inclusivity**:
   - Ensure that sculpture parks are open and accessible to all members of the public.
   - Implement inclusive design practices, such as wheelchair accessibility, clear signage, and multilingual information, to accommodate diverse visitors.
   - Avoid imposing entrance fees, making the parks freely available to residents and visitors.

3. **Foster Community Engagement**:
   - Encourage community involvement and participation in sculpture parks.
   - Organize public art installations, exhibitions, workshops, and cultural events, encouraging interaction and dialogue between artists, residents, and visitors.
   - Establish partnerships with local schools, community organizations, and artists to foster a sense of ownership and inclusivity.

4. **Emphasize Local Artists and Culture**:
   - Showcase local artists' work and incorporate cultural elements in the sculpture parks.
   - Celebrate the city's unique identity by commissioning local artists to create site-specific artworks that reflect the community's history, heritage, and stories.
   - This approach enhances the connection between residents and the park, fostering pride and a sense of belonging.

5. **Provide Educational Opportunities**:
   - Develop educational programs within sculpture parks to promote art appreciation, creativity, and cultural understanding.
   - Offer guided tours, artist talks, and workshops for all ages.
   - Collaborate with schools and universities to incorporate the sculpture park into their curricula, facilitating art education and encouraging future generations to engage with public art.

6. **Embrace Sustainability**:
   - Design sculpture parks with sustainable principles in mind.
   - Incorporate green spaces, native plants, and sustainable landscaping techniques to enhance biodiversity and create an eco-friendly environment.
   - Utilize renewable energy sources for park lighting and implement environmentally responsible maintenance practices.

7. **Foster Collaboration with High-Rise Developments**:
   - Establish partnerships with developers of high-rise buildings to incorporate sculpture parks within or adjacent to their developments.
   - By integrating art and green spaces within high-rise complexes, residents can enjoy the benefits of urban density and sculpture parks' humanizing aspects.

8. **Ensure Maintenance and Safety**:
   - Regularly maintain the sculptures, greenery, and amenities within the park to ensure visitors' a safe and inviting environment.
   - Implement security measures, such as surveillance cameras and well-lit pathways, to enhance safety and encourage a sense of security within the park.

9. **Promote Public Transportation and Active Mobility**:
   - Support public transportation, cycling, and walking to access sculpture parks.
   - Establish convenient public transit connections and provide bike parking facilities to reduce reliance on private vehicles.
   - This approach helps alleviate traffic congestion and promotes a more sustainable and human-centric urban environment.
