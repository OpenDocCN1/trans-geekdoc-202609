
## 5.4

Pershing Square in downtown Los Angeles is a popular gathering spot for residents and visitors. Notable features include the iconic purple bell tower, sculptures, and public art installations. It hosts various events and programs, providing a lively atmosphere for residents and visitors to enjoy nature, art, history, and entertainment. *Source: Photographs by author.*

### HIGHLIGHTS

**Positive Aspects**
- **Central Location**: Situated in downtown Los Angeles, Pershing Square benefits from its central location, attracting residents and tourists seeking a green oasis within the urban fabric.
- **Historical Significance**: The park’s historical significance and role in the city’s development enhance its identity and charm.
- **Urban Retreat:** Amid the city’s urban environment, Pershing Square provides a green oasis where people can escape and unwind.
- **Accessibility:** The park’s accessibility via public transportation and proximity to various cultural and commercial hubs make it convenient for people from different walks of life.
- **Concerts and Events:** Pershing Square hosts various events, including concerts and outdoor performances, contributing to the cultural vibrancy of downtown Los Angeles.
- **Potential for Gathering:** The open layout and central lawn provide space for gatherings, picnics, and relaxation, allowing people to connect and enjoy outdoor activities.
- **Fountains and Water Elements:** Water features, including fountains and reflecting pools, enhance the park’s aesthetics and create a tranquil ambiance.
- **Public Art:** Pershing Square features sculptures and artistic installations that add visual interest and creativity to the public space.

**Areas for Improvement**
- **Design Issues:** The park’s design lacks a cohesive identity and clear layout, with previous redesigns not fully addressing user needs and desires.
- **Lack of Greenery:** Pershing Square’s landscaping features are commendable, but the overall green space is insufficient, requiring more trees, grass, and vegetation to offer a refreshing contrast to the urban landscape.
- **Homelessness and Safety Concerns:** The park is grappling with homelessness issues, potentially affecting its safety and cleanliness perception, necessitating a swift resolution to ensure a positive user experience.
- **Safety and Comfort:** Addressing safety concerns and creating a more comfortable environment through adequate lighting, visible security presence, and well-maintained facilities is essential for attracting diverse visitors.
- **Uninviting Atmosphere:** Pershing Square’s design and lack of maintenance have contributed to an unwelcoming atmosphere, potentially deterring people from spending time there.
- **Seating and Amenities:** Ensuring the availability of ample seating, shaded areas, and amenities such as public restrooms and drinking fountains can enhance visitors’ comfort and convenience.
- **Program Diversity:** Expanding the range of programming and events to cater to a broader spectrum of interests can attract a more diverse crowd and foster community engagement.
- **Underutilization:** Despite its potential as a central gathering space, Pershing Square is sometimes perceived as underutilized due to design issues, lack of programming, and other factors.

In summary, Pershing Square, located in downtown Los Angeles, offers a green oasis for residents and tourists seeking a retreat. With historical significance, it provides a convenient escape from the city’s urban environment. Accessible via public transportation and near major attractions, it serves as a vital community space and a cultural landmark within the urban landscape.

## LUJIAZUI CENTRAL GREEN SPACE, SHANGHAI

This sprawling green space provides a peaceful retreat within Lujiazui's bustling financial hub, surrounded by towering high-rise buildings. Also known as Lujiazui Park, its layout forms a large triangle shape and features a central sculpture, a white tent structure, and a prominent water element that adds a sense of tranquility to the surroundings. Visitors, including tourists and professionals working in the area, frequent the park, seeking respite from the urban chaos. The park covers an area of approximately 24 acres. Its design incorporates traditional Chinese and modern landscaping elements, creating a harmonious blend of natural and urban aesthetics (Zhang et al., 2019).

Park visitors are greeted with a tranquil and inviting atmosphere to explore the park's many paths that weave through verdant meadows. Throughout the park, clusters of trees form delightful groves that gracefully arch over dainty wooden benches, providing shade and creating intimate spaces for relaxation and contemplation. These groves offer moments of tranquility amid the vibrant cityscape. The park is a canvas of idyllic scenery and offers captivating views of Shanghai's iconic skyscrapers, such as the Jin Mao Tower, Shanghai World Financial Center, and Shanghai Tower. These impressive structures dominate the city's skyline, creating a fascinating contrast between the lush greenery of the park and the modern architectural marvels (Hu et al., 2022).

Lujiazui Central Green Space showcases humanizing aspects that contribute to the well-being and enjoyment of residents and visitors. It is a “green oasis” in the urban jungle. It provides a much-needed green space within the dense urban environment of Shanghai's Lujiazui financial district. It delivers a tranquil and calm retreat amid nature, offering a break from the nearby tall buildings and bustling streets. The verdant expanse provides a spacious and unobstructed environment that fosters social interaction, leisure, and participation in diverse pursuits. Picnics, outdoor games, and other leisure activities are welcome on the spacious grounds. The open layout promotes social interactions and creates opportunities for community engagement. Various trees, plants, flowers, and other vegetation adorn the park's landscaping. The landscape design enhances the space's visual appeal and creates a natural environment that promotes tranquility and well-being (Al-Kodmany & Ali, 2013).

The green space integrates water elements and reflection pools, creating a serene ambience for contemplation and relaxation amid the urban surroundings. The green space also includes well-maintained walking and cycling paths that allow visitors to explore and engage in physical activity. These paths promote active lifestyles and provide opportunities for exercise, leisurely walks, and enjoying the surrounding scenery. Furthermore, Lujiazui Central Green Space offers seating areas and shaded spots for visitors to rest, socialize, and enjoy the surroundings. Benches, loungers, and outdoor seating provide comfort and encourage people to spend time in the green space, fostering a sense of community and relaxation. The green space hosts cultural events, exhibitions, and performances, adding vibrancy and cultural enrichment to the area. These events attract visitors and create opportunities for social engagement, fostering a sense of connection and shared experiences. Lujiazui Central Green Space incorporates sculptures and statues such as the Angels and Tourists Sculptures (See Chapter 14).

Lujiazui Park is an urban haven that perfectly balances nature and the contemporary urban environment. It invites visitors to pause, unwind, and appreciate the beauty of the surroundings while providing a captivating backdrop of Shanghai's towering skyscrapers. Through its greenery, open space, recreational amenities, cultural offerings, and thoughtful design, Lujiazui Central Green Space humanizes the urban environment of Shanghai's Lujiazui district. It provides a place for relaxation, social interaction, and connection with nature, enhancing the quality of life for those living and working there (Figure 5.5).

### HIGHLIGHTS

**Positive Aspects**
- **Central Location:** Lujiazui Park’s location in the bustling financial district makes it easily accessible to office workers, residents, and tourists, offering a green retreat amid a busy urban environment.
- **Scenic Views:** The park provides breathtaking vistas of the renowned Shanghai skyline, including contemporary high-rise buildings and notable monuments that enhance the park’s distinctive ambiance.
- **Spacious Design:** The extensive design of Lujiazui Park offers abundant opportunities for outdoor recreation, leisure, and relaxation.
- **Water Features:** Including water features, such as ponds and fountains, enhances the park’s visual appeal and offers a serene atmosphere.
- **Variety of Spaces:** The park’s design incorporates a mix of open lawns, gardens, and water features, creating a diverse range of spaces for relaxation, leisure, and outdoor activities.
- **Green Environment:** Amid the urban environment, Lujiazui Park integrates a green environment with well-maintained lawns, trees, and landscaping.
