# OASIA DOWNTOWN, SINGAPORE

The Oasia Hotel Downtown is a lush tower of green in the middle of Singapore's congested Central Business District (CBD). It serves as a model for land use intensification in tropical cities. This “living tower” presents an alternative to the sleek, sealed skyscrapers that sprang out of the West. Oasia Downtown is a 27-story (190 meter) office building completed in 2016. It was designed by WOHA Architects (Boby et al., 2019; Generalova et al., 2017).

# *Architectural Design*

Oasia Downtown features a striking red aluminum mesh façade that spans the entire height of the building. This “vegetative exoskeleton” serves multiple purposes: a visual signature, an air filter, and a sunshade system. The building's design embraces a flowing and organic form, with curves and contours that break away from traditional rectangular structures. This design approach creates a sense of movement and fluidity, enhancing the overall aesthetics. Oasia Downtown incorporates several sky gardens distributed across various levels of the building. These lush green spaces are designed as open-air retreats for occupants, providing opportunities for relaxation, socializing, and connection with nature. At the top of the building is a spacious rooftop terrace offering panoramic views of the city skyline. This elevated outdoor space serves as a gathering area
