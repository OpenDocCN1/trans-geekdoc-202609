Notably, the steel pipes are arranged in a semi-vertical and random order, creating a visually striking contrast with the overall horizontality of the building. The regular protrusion of slabs on each floor further emphasizes the building’s horizontal lines, making the green walls a dynamic and eye-catching addition to the architectural composition.

Beyond their aesthetic appeal, these green walls serve a dual purpose. Firstly, they contribute to environmental sustainability by promoting air purification. The plants climbing the walls actively engage in the natural air filtration process, enhancing the overall air quality in the vicinity. Secondly, integrating greenery produces an interesting visual effect that significantly deviates from the traditional and often austere appearance of conventional parking garages (Al-Kodmany, 2017; Shulman et al., 2010).

By incorporating nature into the garage space, Bentley Bay South Tower addresses practical concerns related to air quality and transforms the parking facility’s visual experience. The juxtaposition of greenery against the structural elements of the garage creates a harmonious blend of nature and architecture, offering a aesthetically pleasing and environmentally conscious solution to parking infrastructure. In essence, the garage space becomes more than just a utilitarian structure; it becomes a visually engaging and environmentally responsive element that contributes to the overall appeal and sustainability of the Bentley Bay South Tower complex (Figure 2.18).
