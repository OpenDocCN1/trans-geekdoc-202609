## PETRONAS TOWERS PARK, KUALA LUMPUR

One of the most popular attractions in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, is the Petronas Towers Park, a lush green space surrounding the iconic twin skyscrapers. Completed in 1998, the Petronas Towers, often called the Petronas Twin Towers or KLCC Twin Towers, stand as an iconic pair of 88-story supertall skyscrapers in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Soaring 452 meters, these twin structures were the world's tallest buildings until 2004. Renowned for their sleek and modern design, they are connected by a skybridge, creating a distinct and recognizable silhouette on the Kuala Lumpur skyline. The towers, developed by Petronas, the Malaysian national oil company, symbolize the nation's economic prowess and architectural achievement. With a blend of Islamic motifs and cutting-edge architecture, the Petronas Towers remain a symbol of Malaysia's global significance and continue attracting visitors and admirers worldwide (Al-Kodmany & Ali, 2013; Bunnell, 1999).

The park surrounding the Petronas Towers, commonly known as KLCC Park, serves as a multifaceted urban oasis, providing diverse activities for visitors to Kuala Lumpur. It covers approximately 17 acres and offers a serene environment for various recreational pursuits, including jogging, cycling, and picnicking. Visitors can bask in the beauty of the lush greenery while enjoying the breathtaking backdrop of the Petronas Towers, which stand prominently as some of the world's tallest buildings. As day turns to night, the park transforms into a magical setting with a musical fountain show, captivating spectators with its enchanting display. Families with children can explore the dedicated playground and wading pool, adding to the park's appeal as a destination for leisure and family-friendly outings. Additionally, the park features a mosque, contributing to the diversity of amenities within this urban haven. KLCC Park, with its harmonious blend of nature and architectural splendor, stands as a testament to Kuala Lumpur's commitment to creating vibrant and inclusive public spaces (Robison, 1995).

Petronas Towers Park is a vibrant communal hub, fostering local and tourist connections. The expansive open spaces and inviting pathways create an atmosphere conducive to social interactions, providing a platform for people to gather, engage in conversations, and share their experiences. As a popular meeting point, the park facilitates connections and offers a shared space for individuals from diverse backgrounds. Beyond its everyday role, the park comes alive with festivals, performances, and cultural events celebrating the rich tapestry of Malaysian art, music, and customs. These cultural festivities contribute significantly to the humanizing essence of the park, promoting cultural exchange, nurturing a sense of community, and allowing visitors to immerse themselves in the vibrant local traditions. Petronas Towers Park stands as a landscape architectural marvel and a dynamic and inclusive space that reflects the spirit of Kuala Lumpur and its diverse community.

Petronas Towers Park also provides visitors with a variety of recreational opportunities. Yoga areas, jogging tracks, strolling paths, and outdoor exercise platforms are available. These facilities foster wellness and active lifestyles by offering opportunities for physical exercise and relaxation in an urban setting. The park offers seating areas and shaded spaces where visitors can rest, relax, and enjoy the surroundings. The availability of comfortable seating and shaded spots enhances the park's humanizing aspect by creating leisure, conversation, and observation spaces. The park incorporates water features, including fountains and reflective pools. These elements add a sense of tranquility and visual interest, creating a soothing ambiance and providing opportunities for contemplation. The play of water and the reflections of the surrounding architecture add to the park's aesthetic appeal. Finally, the park is designed to be accessible and well-integrated into the surrounding urban fabric (Al-Quadmany & Ali, 2013; Robison, 1995; Figure 5.7).

## KEY TAKEAWAYS: PLANNING AND DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Public parks can be crucial in humanizing high-rise cities by providing essential elements of nature, community, and recreation. Here are some guiding design principles that help to achieve that goal:

1. **Accessibility and Inclusivity:**
   - Ensure that parks are accessible to people of all ages, abilities, and backgrounds.
   - Incorporate ramps, elevators, and accessible pathways for equal enjoyment and participation.
   - Provide inclusive amenities such as seating, picnic areas, and playgrounds that cater to diverse needs.
2. **Greenery and Natural Elements:**
   - Integrate abundant greenery, trees, and plants within parks to create a sense of tranquility, connection with nature, and improved air quality.
   - Design lush gardens, walking paths, and green corridors that offer a respite from the surrounding urban environment.
3. **Varied Spaces and Activities:**
   - Design parks with active and passive spaces to cater to various recreational activities and preferences.
