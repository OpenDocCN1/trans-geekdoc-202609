10.4 In addition to seating choices, the High Line features vistas that overlook various parts of the vibrant cityscape of Manhattan, creating perfect spots to take photographs. 26th Street Viewing Spur (bottom). *Source: Photographs by author.*

+   - **The Wind Blows Where It Wishes:** Gabriel Chaile’s adobe sculpture, “The Wind Blowing Where It Wishes,” draws inspiration from biblical passages, natural phenomena, and pre-Columbian archeological ceramics for the High Line.
- **Column-Untitled No. 3:** Yu Ji’s Column-Untitled No. 3 features two twisting columns resembling the Equisetum fern family on the High Line, crafted from concrete and soap in a park.
- **Freedom’s Stand:** Faheem Majeed’s Freedom’s Stand, a sculpture on 30th Street, showcases Black newspapers’ contributions to the US over 200 years, featuring headlines, articles, photographs, and advertisements.
