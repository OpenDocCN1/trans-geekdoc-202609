In essence, the Chicago Riverwalk is a living canvas that captures the spirit of Chicago—an evolving city that celebrates its past, embraces the present, and shapes an inspiring future along the banks of its iconic river (Duis & Weese, 2022).

# Recreational Trail

The Recreational Trail segment of the Chicago Riverwalk, extending from Lake Shore Drive to Michigan Avenue, stands as the inaugural section of this iconic urban space. Constructed in the early 2000s, this eastern component connects the Chicago Loop with Lakefront Trail and Navy Pier, seamlessly intertwining with the cityscape along the Chicago River. As a docking point for boats offering educational and recreational tours, the Recreational Trail or Riverwalk Esplanade has become a hub of activity and leisure (Hill, 2019).

Among the most exciting tours is the Chicago River Architecture Cruise. It is a captivating and educational boat tour that navigates the iconic Chicago River, offering participants a unique perspective on the city's remarkable architectural landscape. This immersive experience provides a front-row seat to some of Chicago's most renowned architectural marvels, showcasing the city's rich history and innovative design.
