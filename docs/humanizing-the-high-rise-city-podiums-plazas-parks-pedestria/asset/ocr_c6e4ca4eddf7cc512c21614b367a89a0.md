# **Chapter 8: Sculpture Parks**

Sculpture parks are outdoor spaces designed to showcase sculptures and other three-dimensional artworks in a natural setting with the urban environment. These parks act as platforms for artists to exhibit their work on a larger scale while providing visitors with a dynamic and engaging experience outdoors. They combine art, nature, and architecture, providing a sensory-rich experience and inspiring creativity, contrasting with the monotonous skyscraper landscape of high-rise cities. Chapter 8 examines how sculpture parks can transform high-rise city life, emphasizing their role as innovative spaces that display artistic creativity and celebrate diverse forms of sculptures. It gives valuable insights and recommendations for urban designers, planners, and park managers, promoting the integration of sculpture as a vital part of public spaces, improving the human experience, and fostering a deeper appreciation for art, nature, and the built environment. In summary, Chapter 8 inspires and guides for creating captivating sculpture parks that enrich high-rise cities’ cultural, social, and visual vibrancy.

# **Chapter 9: Waterfront Parks**

Waterfront parks are public recreational spaces located alongside bodies of water, such as rivers, lakes, oceans, or other aquatic environments. These parks are designed to allow people to enjoy outdoor activities, relax, and appreciate natural and urban waterfront environments. They offer a range of amenities and attractions that capitalize on their proximity to water, creating a unique and often picturesque setting for recreation and leisure. Chapter 9 highlights the importance of waterfront parks in enhancing the humaneness of high-rise cities by balancing the vertical density of buildings with accessible green spaces, providing numerous benefits to residents and the urban environment. These parks create captivating environments that combine natural elements with social, sporting, and recreational activities, fostering lively spaces that serve residents and tourists. The availability of attractive waterfront parks boosts tourism, stimulates local businesses, creates jobs, and generates economic growth. Green infrastructure, ecological restoration, and flood mitigation strategies improve urban landscape resilience, support biodiversity, and restore natural habitats. The chapter uses the Chicago Riverfront as a powerful case study to demonstrate these concepts, highlighting the successful transformation of the riverfront into an engaging public space that incorporates natural elements, recreational amenities, cultural programming, and public art installations. In essence, Chapter 9 emphasizes the importance of waterfront parks in humanizing high-rise cities, enhancing livability, health, aesthetics, and cultural richness while balancing urban intensity and natural serenity.
