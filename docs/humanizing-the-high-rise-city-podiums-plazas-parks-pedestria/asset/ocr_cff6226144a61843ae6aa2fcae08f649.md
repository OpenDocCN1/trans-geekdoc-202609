**2. Concept Development:**

+   - Develop a clear concept and vision for the sky park or garden, considering the purpose, theme, desired user experience, and overall design objectives.
- Identify the unique selling points and desired features, such as seating areas, walking paths, recreational spaces, water features, or panoramic viewpoints.

**3. Site Analysis and Microclimate Considerations:**

+   - For outdoor parks, conduct a thorough site analysis to understand the microclimate and environmental conditions of the building’s location.
- Factors such as sunlight exposure, wind patterns, temperature variations, and noise levels should inform the design.
- Use this information to select appropriate plant species, design shading structures, and consider the need for wind mitigation measures.

**4. Landscape Design and Plant Selection:**

+   - Collaborate with landscape architects and horticulturists to design the green spaces within the building. Consider the chosen plant species’ aesthetics, functionality, and maintenance requirements.
- Select vegetation that can thrive in a specific microclimate and is easily maintained in an elevated environment.
- Integrate green walls, vertical gardens, hanging plants, or rooftop vegetation to maximize greenery and create a pleasant atmosphere (Tian & Jin, 2011).

**5. Accessibility and Circulation:**

+   - Ensure the sky park or garden is accessible to building occupants and visitors.
- Design safe and convenient access points, including elevators, staircases, or dedicated entrances.
- Consider universal design principles to ensure accessibility for people of all abilities.
- Create a coherent circulation design that effectively directs people through the different sections of the sky park or garden.

**6. Amenities and Functionality:**

+   - Incorporate amenities and functional spaces within the sky park or garden to enhance user experience.
- Include seating areas, gathering spaces, recreational facilities, and possibly food and beverage outlets.

**7. Sustainability Considerations:**

+   - Integrate sustainable design practices into the sky park or garden.
- Utilize green infrastructure elements such as rain gardens, living walls, or green roofs to manage stormwater runoff and improve insulation.
