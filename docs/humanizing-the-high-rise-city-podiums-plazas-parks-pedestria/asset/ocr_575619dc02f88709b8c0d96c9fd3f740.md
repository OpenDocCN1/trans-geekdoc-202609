Flower Dome at Gardens by the Bay. A botanical marvel, one of the world's largest glass greenhouses spanning 1.2 hectares, showcasing a diverse tapestry of flowers and plants from five continents in a stunning replication of the Mediterranean climate. *Source: Photographs by author.*

+   - **Supertree Grove:** Supertrees are visually appealing vertical gardens that support various plant species, provide shade, and cool the urban environment.
- **Flower Dome and Cloud Forest:** These two climate-controlled conservatories offer visitors the opportunity to experience different ecosystems, from Mediterranean landscapes to tropical rainforests.
- **Art and Culture:** Gardens by the Bay hosts various art installations, cultural events, and performances, enhancing the visitor experience and connecting nature with human creativity.
