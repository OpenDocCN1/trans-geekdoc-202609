In conclusion, Times Square serves as a testament to the idea that even amid towering skyscrapers, urban spaces can be designed to prioritize human experience. It is an example of creating functional and enriching environments, offering residents and visitors a place to connect, celebrate, and embrace the human side of the high-rise cityscape. However, while Times Square is a globally recognized and lively urban space, addressing aspects such as commercial dominance, crowd management, green spaces, community engagement, and local programming could contribute to a more balanced and humanized urban experience. A thoughtful reevaluation of these elements could enhance Times Square’s role as a genuinely inclusive and community-centric space within the high-rise cityscape (Banerjee, 2001; Berman, 2009; Sagaly, 2023b).

# EAST NANJING ROAD, SHANGHAI

East Nanjing Road (also known as Nanjing Road East) is one of Shanghai’s most famous and bustling commercial pedestrian streets. It is in the Huangpu District and stretches for approximately 1 mile (1.6 kilometers) from the Bund in the east to People’s Square in the west (See Chapter 5). East Nanjing Road is renowned for its vibrant atmosphere, diverse shopping options, cultural landmarks, and architectural charm. It is primarily a pedestrian street catering to the needs and preferences of walkers. Vehicular traffic is limited or prohibited, allowing pedestrians to freely explore the street and its numerous attractions (Al-Kodmany, 2018; Falkus, 2001).

East Nanjing Pedestrian Street is easily accessible by public transportation, including metro lines and buses. It is well connected to other parts of the city, allowing visitors to conveniently reach and explore the street (Istrate & Chen, 2022). During the colonial era, this once-dirt route became a major trade center, symbolizing Shanghai’s journey towards modernity and economic prosperity. Various architectural styles, spanning centuries and influenced by many cultures (e.g., Art Deco, Gothic, Neoclassical, and Modern), line the street. These architectural marvels enhance the street’s allure and provide a mesmerizing visual experience (Figure 4.5).

East Nanjing Road is renowned for its extensive shopping options, from luxury brands to local boutiques and department stores. It is a haven for shoppers, offering a wide array of fashion, electronics, jewelry, souvenirs, and traditional Chinese goods.
