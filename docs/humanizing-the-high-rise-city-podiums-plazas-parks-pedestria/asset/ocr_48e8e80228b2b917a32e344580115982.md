# # 3 Public Plazas

The challenge posed by social isolation in high-density living underscores the complexities of contemporary urban life, particularly in environments dominated by tall structures and dense populations. Amid towering buildings and bustling city life, residents may find themselves in a paradoxical situation—surrounded by numerous neighbors yet experiencing limited opportunities for casual interactions and community engagement. The vertical nature of high-rises often means residents pass each other in elevators or hallways without the chance for meaningful exchanges. The lack of communal spaces and the absence of designated areas for socializing can further contribute to a sense of isolation, where individuals may feel disconnected from their neighbors and the larger community. This challenge is exacerbated by the demands of modern lifestyles, with the fast pace and individualistic nature of urban living sometimes hindering the organic formation of social connections (Gehl, 1971, 2010; Marcus & Francis, 1998).

In response to the challenge of social isolation in high-density living, a thoughtful solution involves deliberately offering communal space and public plazas. These spaces catalyze casual interactions, providing residents with opportunities to connect organically. Promoting social events within these spaces, ranging from neighborhood gatherings to cultural celebrations, can further strengthen social bonds (Loukaito-Sideris & Banerjee, 1998). By creating spaces beyond the confines of individual residences and facilitating a sense of shared identity, high-density living can evolve into a socially enriching experience where residents feel connected, engaged, and part of a larger community fabric. As integral components of the urban fabric, public plazas contribute to the well-being of city dwellers by offering greenery, seating, and recreational opportunities, thereby mitigating the potential challenges posed by the vertical nature of high-rise living (Al-Kodmany, 2017; Whyte, 1980).

The following case studies highlight the roles of public plazas in humanizing the high-rise city.
