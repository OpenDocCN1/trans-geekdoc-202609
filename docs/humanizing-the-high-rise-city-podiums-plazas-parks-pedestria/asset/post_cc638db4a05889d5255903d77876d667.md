
## KEY TAKEAWAYS: PLANNING AND DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Applying the urban room concept to humanize dense, high-rise cities requires careful consideration of the urban fabric and the creation of well-designed spaces that enhance the human experience. Here are some recommendations for implementing the urban room design concept:

### 1. Analyze and Utilize Existing Spaces
- Conduct a thorough analysis of the city's urban fabric to identify existing spaces that can be transformed into urban rooms.
- Look for opportunities within plazas, squares, underutilized areas, or even spaces between high-rise buildings.
- Repurpose these spaces to create distinct urban rooms that offer unique experiences and foster a sense of place.

### 2. Design for Human Scale
- Ensure that every urban space is planned to have dimensions and proportions that are suitable for humans, therefore providing a pleasant and cozy atmosphere for pedestrians.
- Use appropriate materials, landscaping, and lighting to enhance the ambiance and create a welcoming atmosphere.
- Integrate gathering spaces, and pedestrian-friendly pathways to encourage social interaction and a sense of community.

### 3. Provide Functional Diversity
- Incorporate a variety of functions and activities within each urban room to cater to the needs and interests of different user groups.
- Consider including seating areas, green spaces, play areas, or performance spaces to create a dynamic and engaging environment.
- Balance active and passive spaces to accommodate both vibrant social activities and moments of tranquility.

### 4. Foster Connectivity
- Create a network of interconnected urban rooms throughout the city to encourage pedestrian movement and connectivity.
- Design pedestrian-friendly pathways and pedestrian bridges to link the urban rooms and facilitate ease of movement.
- Consider incorporating wayfinding signage and digital navigation tools to assist visitors in navigating the urban room network.

### 5. Emphasize Art and Culture
- Integrate public art installations, sculptures, or cultural elements within the urban rooms to enhance their aesthetic appeal and cultural significance.
- Collaborate with local artists and cultural organizations to incorporate site-specific artworks that reflect the identity and heritage of the city.

These artistic and cultural elements contribute to the humanization of the city by fostering creativity, stimulating the senses, and providing points of interest and conversation.

### 6. Ensure Accessibility and Inclusivity
- Design urban rooms to be accessible to people of all abilities.
- Implement universal design principles, including wheelchair ramps, elevators, and tactile pathways.
- Consider the diverse needs of different user groups, including families with strollers, elderly individuals, and individuals with disabilities, to ensure inclusivity and equal access to the urban rooms.

### 7. Engage the Community
- Involve the local community in designing and activating the urban rooms.
- Seek input from residents, businesses, and community organizations to understand their needs and aspirations.
- Organize community events, workshops, or art programs within the urban rooms to encourage community participation, ownership, and a sense of belonging.

### 8. Implement Sustainable Practices
- Integrate sustainable design principles into the urban rooms to promote environmental responsibility.
- Incorporate green infrastructure elements, such as rain gardens or green roofs, to manage stormwater runoff.
- Incorporate energy-efficient lighting and renewable energy sources.
- Preserve existing trees and integrate landscaping that supports biodiversity.

### 9. Promote Cultural Exchange
- Design urban rooms to serve as platforms for cultural exchange and interaction.
- Organize cultural events, performances, or festivals that celebrate the community's diversity.
- Create spaces where residents and visitors can share and appreciate different cultural expressions.

### 10. Facilitate Active Engagement
- Design urban rooms with interactive elements to encourage active engagement.
- Incorporate features like interactive art installations, pop-up markets, or temporary exhibits.
- Encourage community members to participate in shaping and evolving the character of the urban rooms.

By applying these recommendations, the urban room concept can help humanize dense, high-rise cities by creating inviting, pedestrian-friendly spaces that foster community engagement, artistic expression, and a sense of place. These urban rooms become transformative elements within the urban fabric, enhancing the overall livability and quality of life in dense, high-rise city environments. The subsequent chapter discusses the roles of sculpture parks in humanizing the high-rise city.

# 8 Sculpture Parks

High-rise cities often grapple with the challenge of architectural monotony, where uniform building designs dominate the skyline and contribute to a visual homogeneity that diminishes the uniqueness and identity of the place. The repetitive nature of architectural styles can erode a city's distinct character, transforming it into a landscape marked by indistinguishable structures. The monotony in urban environments hinders the creation of memorable landmarks and may impact the sense of place, making it difficult for residents and visitors to connect with the cityscape.

Sculpture parks disrupt the architectural regularity of tall cities by exhibiting three-dimensional artworks, countering the visual monotony of massive structures. The sculptures are strategically placed in green spaces, transforming the urban environment into a living gallery. Sculpture parks in high-rise cities enhance architectural grandeur and artistic expression, adding visual interest and variety to the cityscape, ranging from towering steel structures to delicate forms. They improve the surroundings’ aesthetic appeal and provide natural art experiences, serving as urban sanctuaries for relaxation, contemplation, and cultural enrichment amid vertical density (Eytres & Russell, 2006).

High-rise cities benefit significantly from the presence of sculpture parks for several compelling reasons (Refer to Chapter 14):

### 1. Urban Aesthetics and Contrast
- **Visual Appeal:** Concrete and steel structures can often dominate high-rise cityscapes. Sculpture parks introduce visually striking and contrasting elements, adding aesthetic diversity to the urban environment.
- **Humanizing Spaces:** Large-scale sculptures and greenery soften the hard lines of skyscrapers, creating a more welcoming and humanized atmosphere.

### 2. Balance and Harmony
- **Integration with Architecture:** Sculpture parks balance the vertical dominance of high-rises, fostering a harmonious coexistence between man-made structures and artistic expressions in a natural setting.
- **Creating Focal Points:** Sculptures act as focal points within the city, breaking the monotony of a skyline and giving residents and visitors landmarks to identify with.
