
## Public Plazas

A somber and moving homage to the terrible events of September 11, 2001, is the 9/11 Museum. Situated within the sacred premises of the 9/11 Memorial site, the museum is housed in a subterranean area that evokes a distinct and reflective ambiance. The gloomy atmosphere that greets visitors as they descend into the museum captures the seriousness of the historical event. Artifacts bearing witness to the destruction of that dreadful day are showcased in the displays, including pieces of the Twin Towers’ original pillars. Touching artifacts from the museum’s collection include broken telephone booths, a burned ambulance, and the melancholy remnants of helmets that firefighters and rescuers who responded to the unparalleled disaster left behind. The 9/11 Museum is a dignified and indispensable memorial for contemplation and recollection because each relic is a concrete reminder of the human cost and community resilience in the face of unfathomable misfortune (Parakh et al., 2017) (Figure 3.19).

9/11 Memorial Plaza is a testament to resilience, remembrance, and renewal. It provides a tranquil and reflective space where people can pay tribute to the past, appreciate the present, and look toward the future. With its thoughtful design, meaningful symbolism, and integration into the larger World Trade Center complex, the plaza is an essential and dynamic public space in the heart of New York City.

### HIGHLIGHTS

#### Positive Aspects

- **Symbolic Significance:** The World Trade Center Plaza holds profound symbolic importance as a memorial site, honoring the victims of the September 11, 2001, attacks.
- **Memorial Pools:** The plaza features two robust memorial pools in the footprints of the original Twin Towers, providing a space for reflection and remembrance. The design, cascading waterfalls, and voids evoke a sense of loss and pay tribute to the lives lost on 9/11.
- **Access to Transportation:** The plaza is well-connected to various forms of transportation, making it easily accessible to visitors.
- **Integration with Memorial Museum:** The plaza seamlessly connects with the 9/11 Memorial Museum, providing a comprehensive experience for visitors.
- **Landscaping:** The landscaping and greenery create an inviting atmosphere and a sense of healing.
- **Community Gathering:** The plaza serves as a gathering place for commemorative events, fostering a sense of unity and shared memory.
- **Educational Opportunities:** The plaza offers educational programs and resources that help visitors understand the impact of the events.
- **Public Engagement:** The plaza engages visitors emotionally and intellectually, sparking conversations about history, tragedy, and resilience.

#### Areas for Improvement

- **Sensitivity:** Due to its commemorative nature, some visitors may find the plaza emotionally overwhelming or difficult to visit.
- **Tourist Attraction:** The plaza's popularity as a tourist destination might occasionally lead to overcrowding, impacting the solemnity of the space.
- **Limited Amenities:** The plaza's design prioritizes its memorial function, resulting in limited seating, shading, and recreational amenities.
- **Weather Exposure:** Insufficient shading or indoor spaces could expose visitors to harsh weather conditions.
- **Lack of Active Use:** The emphasis on reflection and commemoration might limit the plaza's potential for active use.
- **Long Lines:** The popularity of the memorial pools might lead to long lines and waiting times for entry.
- **Maintenance:** The cascading waterfalls and reflecting pools require continuous maintenance to ensure proper functioning.

## UNION SQUARE, SAN FRANCISCO

Union Square is located in the bustling sociocommercial heart of San Francisco. The area surrounding the square is a convergence point for major hotels and department stores, creating a vibrant and dynamic atmosphere. Within this district, one can explore many upscale boutiques, diverse restaurants offering culinary delights, lively nightspots, and engaging galleries. These establishments are seamlessly integrated into the urban landscape, nestled between larger buildings, contributing to the lively and diverse character of the area. Union Square, occupying a sprawling 2.6-acre expanse, is framed by the intersecting streets of Geary, Powell, Post, and Stockton. Its name harks back to a significant historical context, as it was a site for pro-Union rallies during the American Civil War. This civic space has evolved into a central hub for commerce, leisure, and cultural happenings (Union Square).

At the heart of Union Square stands the impressive Dewey Monument, soaring to a height of 97 feet (30 meters). This monument is a tribute to Admiral George Dewey and commemorates his triumph in the Battle of Manila Bay during the Spanish-American War. The Dewey Monument adds a monumental and historical touch to the square, becoming a focal point that captures the city's history and role as a vibrant contemporary urban center. As visitors explore the square's surroundings, they are immersed in the bustling commerce and encounter this towering monument, a testament to the intersection of history and modernity in the heart of San Francisco (Bosselmann, 2012).

The plaza's ground is adorned with intricate paving featuring interlocking geometric shapes. The pattern incorporates granite of gray and beige colors, adding visual interest and texture to the space. There are seating areas with benches and vegetation. Meanwhile, the central space provides a focal point for gatherings, performances, and events, inviting people to sit, relax, and engage with one another. The plaza's design prioritizes pedestrians, with ample space for walking, sitting, and enjoying the surroundings. The absence of vehicular traffic enhances safety and encourages people to explore the area on foot. Union Square hosts various events throughout the year, such as concerts, art shows, and flower festivals (Union Square).

Union Square's combination of public art, seating, performance spaces, and seasonal elements ensures it remains a beloved destination for locals and tourists. While Union Square is not characterized by extensive greenery, it incorporates plantings strategically. Trees, flower beds, and shrubs are placed along the edges and corners, providing some natural elements that enhance the urban environment. The landscaping scheme incorporates seasonal plantings that change throughout the year. Further, the square is adorned with festive decorations and a giant Christmas tree during the holidays. Also, it features outdoor cafés and dining areas where visitors can enjoy meals or refreshments while experiencing the vibrant atmosphere (Bosselmann, 2012).

The south side of the square contains amphitheater-like steps that serve as both seating and a platform for performances and events. This design encourages interaction, observation, and engagement with various activities. The square has several public art installations and sculptures. These artworks add cultural and aesthetic value to the space, making it an engaging environment for locals and visitors. Union Square is illuminated by various types of lighting, including lamp-posts, ground-level lighting, and decorative elements. This ensures the square remains vibrant and usable even during the evening hours (Figure 3.20).

Overall, Union Square is a dynamic center of commerce, entertainment, and community, exemplifying the resilience and adaptability of urban ecosystems. The square's progression throughout history, starting with its first conversion into a communal area and now serving as a bustling center, mirrors the constantly shifting dynamics of an always-active metropolis. Union Square is significant for its architecture and economic strength and is a central hub for social and cultural activities, attracting various people to engage in its attractions. The combination of luxury retail stores with artistic displays and community activities demonstrates the square's capacity to blend contrasting components, resulting in an economically prosperous and socially interactive setting. With its extensive historical background and current significance, Union Square is the core of urban liveliness, serving as a miniature representation of San Francisco's lasting urban story.

### HIGHLIGHTS

#### Positive Aspects

- **Central Gathering Space:** Union Square is a vibrant and central gathering space for locals and visitors, fostering a sense of community and interaction.
- **Cultural Hub:** The square hosts various cultural events, festivals, and performances, adding to its dynamic and lively atmosphere.
