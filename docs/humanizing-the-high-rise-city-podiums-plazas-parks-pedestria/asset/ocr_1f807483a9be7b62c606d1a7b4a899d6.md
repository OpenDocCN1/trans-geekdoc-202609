Yerba Buena Gardens is a San Francisco public park with lush green spaces, trees, and well-maintained landscaping. It serves as a dynamic hub for the community, where various events and festivals take place, complemented by playgrounds, water features, and a memorial honoring Martin Luther King, Jr. Source: Photographs by author.

+   • Location: Situated in the downtown area, the park is easily accessible and acts as a gathering space for both residents and tourists, contributing to the urban vitality of the surrounding area.
• Design Variety: The park’s design incorporates open lawns, water features, playgrounds, and landscaped areas, catering to various preferences and activities.
• Public Amenities: Yerba Buena Gardens provides amenities such as restrooms, seating, and food options, enhancing the comfort and convenience of visitors.
