The Lujiazui Central Green Space in Shanghai, China, is a spacious, triangle-shaped park that blends traditional Chinese and modern landscaping elements, offering a peaceful retreat from high-rise buildings. It integrates attractive sculptures, a white tent structure, and an extended pond, promoting social interactions and cultural events. *Source: Photographs by author.*
