This sculpture captures the essence of these three heroic figures, representing their contributions to the American Revolution and the founding of the United States. This 11-foot-high bronze sculpture stands atop a six-foot-high granite base, creating a significant presence on Lower Wacker Drive, between Wabash and State Street, on the south side of the Chicago River (Figure 14.2). The bronze material used for the sculpture adds a sense of permanence and durability, underscoring the enduring legacy of these heroes and their impact on American history. The placement of the Heald Square Monument on Lower Wacker Drive allows it to be seen and appreciated by both pedestrians and motorists passing through the bustling area.
