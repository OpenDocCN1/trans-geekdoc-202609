The 9/11 Memorial Plaza's centerpiece is the Memorial Reflecting Pools, a serene atmosphere with cascading waterfalls inscribed with the names of 9/11 and 1993 World Trade Center bombing victims. The Memorial was inaugarated on September 11, 2011, marking a decade since the 9/11 attacks. Source: Photographs by author.

9/11 Memorial Plaza offers various public spaces and amenities for visitors. These include seating areas, walking paths, and open relaxation and social interaction spaces. The plaza also features several retail and dining establishments, providing convenience and options for visitors. Cultural concerts, public art exhibitions, memorial services, and other community events are all held at the plaza. These events contribute to the vibrancy and vitality of the space, fostering a sense of community and engagement among visitors. Moreover, the Memorial Plaza is adorned with almost 400 swamp white oak trees. This resilient tree species is indigenous to the locations of all three 9/11 crash sites: New York City, Arlington, Virginia, and Somerset County, Pennsylvania (Parakh et al., 2017).
