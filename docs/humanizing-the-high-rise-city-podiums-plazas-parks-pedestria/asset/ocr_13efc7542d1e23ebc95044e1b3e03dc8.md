The Westin Bund Center Shanghai podium design is a testament to thought-ful and refined architecture, focusing on creating a welcoming and harmonious environment for visitors. At the building’s entrance, a serene water feature takes center stage, adding a touch of tranquility and elegance to the space. This water feature acts as a visual focal point and contributes to a serene atmosphere, enhancing the overall experience for those approaching the entrance.

An exotic canopy crowns the entrance, providing both artistic charm and functional significance. This canopy adds a distinctive architectural feature and serves as a shelter, offering protection from the elements for those entering or exiting the building. The exotic design of the canopy contributes to the overall sense of elegance and sophistication, making a strong visual statement.

Abundant natural illumination is crucial in the entryway design, ensuring the space is well-lit and inviting. Natural light floods the area, creating a bright and airy ambience. This design choice enhances visibility and brings a sense of open-ness to the entrance, fostering a connection with the surrounding environment.

The minimalist and refined white color scheme of the podium aligns seamlessly with the architectural style of the West Bund public edifices. This design creates a sense of unity and continuity within the broader urban context, establishing a visual language that complements the surrounding structures.

In summary, the Westin Bund Center Shanghai podium design has a serene water feature, abundant natural illumination, lush foliage, and an exotic canopy. Combined with a minimalist white color scheme, these elements are meticu-lously tailored to accommodate the human scale, ensuring visitors’ welcoming and aesthetically pleasing experience (Figure 2.15).
