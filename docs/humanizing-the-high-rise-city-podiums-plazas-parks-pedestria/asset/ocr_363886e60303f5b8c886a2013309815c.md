# *Awards and Recognition*

The PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering in Singapore has received various recognitions and accolades for its innovative and environmental architecture, including:

+   - In 2019, the Council on Tall Buildings and Urban Habitat (CTBUH) recognized the project as one of the world's 50 Most Influential Tall Buildings of the Last 50 Years
- Green Hotel Award, 2019–2020
- SGBC-BCA Sustainability Leadership Award, 2019

Overall, PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering’s architectural design and plant integration create a unique and immersive experience for guests. The terraced gardens, sky gardens, and cascading waterfalls combine with the contemporary architectural style to provide a harmonious and sustainable environment that promotes relaxation, well-being, and a solid connection to nature.
