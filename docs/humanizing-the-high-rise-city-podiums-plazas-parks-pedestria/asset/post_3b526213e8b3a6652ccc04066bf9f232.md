### Areas for Improvement

- **Cost of Entry**: The high cost of entry to the Flower Dome and Cloud Forest conservatories may limit accessibility for specific groups.
- **Crowdedness**: Due to its popularity, Gardens by the Bay can become quite crowded, particularly during peak tourist seasons, which can detract from the tranquility of the experience.
- **Commercialization**: The presence of commercial outlets, restaurants, and souvenir shops within the park can diminish the natural and serene atmosphere the gardens aim to provide.
- **Artificial Environment**: The climate-controlled conservatories may be seen as an artificial environment, deviating from the natural outdoor experience that other gardens offer.
- **Energy Consumption**: The cooling and lighting systems required for the conservatories contribute to energy consumption, potentially raising concerns about the park's overall carbon footprint.
- **Environmental Impact**: The construction and maintenance of large structures, such as the Supertrees, can impact the surrounding ecosystem and the park's carbon footprint.
- **Accessibility**: While the park aims to be accessible, navigating the expansive grounds and uneven terrain may pose challenges for individuals with mobility issues.
- **Ongoing Maintenance**: The extensive infrastructure and diverse plantings require ongoing maintenance to ensure the park's long-term vitality.
