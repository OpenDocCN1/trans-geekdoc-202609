With each narrative, they transport their audiences into vibrant and dramatic landscapes, transcending the boundaries of time and space (Al-Kodmany, 2017).

Embedded in a rich storytelling tradition, these maestros pass down their tales and storytelling prowess to the younger generations, ensuring the continuity of this art form. They are not merely performers but custodians of cultural heritage, playing a pivotal role in shaping the cultural identity of East Nanjing Road. In this unique corner of the city, stories cease to be confined to the pages of books; instead, they come alive, resonating through the bustling night air. East Nanjing Road, with its storytellers, becomes more than a commercial thoroughfare—it evolves into a living tapestry of narratives, where the past and present intertwine, and the art of storytelling thrives as an integral and cherished aspect of the local culture (Figure 4.12).
