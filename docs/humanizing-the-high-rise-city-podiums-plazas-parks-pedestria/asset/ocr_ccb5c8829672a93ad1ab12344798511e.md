# Concourse Building, Singapore [1994]

The Concourse Building is a mixed-use complex in Singapore designed by Paul Rudolph. Completed in 1994, it comprises a 41-story office tower, a three-level retail podium, and nine stories of serviced apartments. The architectural design of the Concourse Building reflects Rudolph’s vision of a tropical skyscraper that responds to the climate and context of Singapore. The building uses solar shading, wide overhangs, communal gardens, and external balconies to create a comfortable and lush environment for the occupants.

The Concourse Building boasts an innovative design with an octagonal office tower supported by 12 massive pilots. This distinctive architectural feature provides structural support and contributes to the building’s dynamic and geometric expression.
