
## Public Plazas

The Burj Khalifa Plaza is beautifully illuminated at night, creating a captivating spectacle. The plaza and the Burj Khalifa are adorned with extensive lighting systems that highlight their architectural features, creating a mesmerizing visual experience for visitors. Overall, the Burj Khalifa Plaza is an appealing public space that complements the magnificence of the Burj Khalifa. With its striking design, open layout, lush landscaping, retail and dining options, and cultural events, the plaza offers a multifaceted experience for visitors, making it an attractive destination (Figure 3.23).

## HIGHLIGHTS

### Positive Aspects

- **Iconic Landmark:** The plaza’s location at the base of Burj Khalifa, the tallest building in the world, makes it an iconic destination.
- **Sense of Grandeur:** The vast open space and the towering presence of the Burj Khalifa evoke a sense of grandeur and opulence, providing a unique experience for visitors.
- **Spectacular Views:** The plaza offers breathtaking views of the surrounding tall buildings, including Burj Khalifa itself, allowing visitors to admire its impressive height and architectural intricacies up close.
- **Public Gathering Space:** The spacious plaza serves as a gathering point for events, celebrations, and community activities, fostering a sense of togetherness.
- **Night Illumination:** The plaza comes alive at night with carefully designed lighting that accentuates its architectural features and creates an enchanting ambiance.
- **Water Features:** Incorporating major water elements adds a sense of serenity and elegance to the plaza.
- **Retail and Dining:** The plaza’s proximity to the Dubai Mall provides visitors with numerous retail and dining options, enhancing the overall experience.
- **Public Transit Hub:** The plaza’s location near public transportation hubs facilitates easy access for residents and tourists.

### Areas for Improvement

- **Limited Shade:** The open design of the plaza lacks sufficient shaded areas, making it uncomfortable for visitors during the hot and sunny months.
- **Congestion:** Due to its popularity, the plaza can become overcrowded during peak hours, affecting the overall enjoyment of the space.
- **Lack of Greenery:** Despite its grandeur, the plaza’s landscaping regarding green spaces and natural elements is limited.
- **Cultural Sensitivity:** The modern and international design of Burj Khalifa and Mall might overshadow the local cultural context, which could be an area for improvement.
- **Historical Connection:** Enhancing the historical and cultural connection between the plaza and the surrounding area could enrich the visitor experience.

### KEY TAKEAWAYS: PLANNING AND DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Public plazas are crucial in humanizing high-rise cities by providing open, accessible, and vibrant spaces for people to gather, socialize, and engage with their surroundings. They balance the built environment and the needs for social connection, nature, and community, contributing to a more livable, inclusive, and human-centric urban environment.

Here are guiding design principles for public plazas:

1. **Accessibility and Connectivity:**
   - Ensure the plaza is easily accessible to pedestrians from various parts of the city.
   - Connect it to existing pedestrian networks and public transportation systems.
   - Encourage walkability and provide clear pathways accessible to people of all abilities.
   - Ensure that plazas are easily accessible to people of all abilities and diverse populations.
   - Design ramps, elevators, and other features that enable wheelchair access and accommodate individuals with mobility challenges.
   - Provide seating, signage, and amenities that cater to diverse needs, fostering inclusivity and equal enjoyment of the space.

2. **Human Scale and Proportion:**
   - Create a sense of intimacy and comfort by designing the plaza at a scale relatable to human proportions.
   - Consider using seating areas, shade structures, and landscaping elements to provide a more human-centric experience.

3. **Variety and Flexibility:**
   - Provide a range of activities and spaces within the plaza to accommodate diverse needs and interests.
   - Incorporate seating areas, green spaces, gathering spots, water features, art installations, and performance spaces.
   - Design plazas with adaptable and flexible spaces that can accommodate a variety of activities and events.
   - Include movable furniture, modular elements, and multipurpose areas that can be easily reconfigured to suit different uses and community needs, fostering flexibility in the dynamic programming of the plaza and encouraging a sense of ownership among users.

4. **Social Interaction and Community Engagement:**
   - Foster opportunities for social interaction and community engagement.
   - Design the plaza to encourage people to gather, interact, and participate in communal activities.
   - Include seating clusters, community gardens, play areas, or spaces for public events to encourage social connections.
   - Design plazas as vibrant social spaces that encourage social interaction, community engagement, and a sense of belonging.
   - Create seating arrangements, gathering areas, and amenities facilitating spontaneous conversations and gatherings.
   - Incorporate features like interactive installations, public art, and performance spaces to foster community engagement and cultural expression.

5. **Integration with Surroundings:**
   - Ensure that the plaza is designed to harmonize with the surrounding urban fabric.
   - Consider the architectural style, materials, and colors used in nearby buildings to create a cohesive and visually pleasing environment.
   - Integrate the plaza with adjacent public spaces, parks, or landmarks to enhance connectivity and continuity.

6. **Safety and Security:**
   - Prioritize the safety and security of plaza users.
   - Design the plaza with clear sightlines, well-lit areas, and open spaces to promote visibility and discourage crime.
   - Incorporate security measures such as surveillance cameras, emergency call boxes, and regular maintenance to create a safe and inviting atmosphere.

7. **Environmental Sustainability:**
   - Integrate sustainable design principles into the plaza's layout and features.
   - Incorporate green spaces, trees, and plants to provide shade, improve air quality, and mitigate the urban heat island effect.
   - Use sustainable materials, employ energy-efficient lighting, and incorporate rainwater harvesting or permeable surfaces to manage stormwater runoff.

8. **Cultural and Historical Context:**
   - Embrace the local culture and history when designing the plaza.
   - Consider incorporating local art, architecture, or cultural heritage elements to create a sense of place and identity.
   - Preserve significant historical features or landmarks that may already exist within or around the plaza.

9. **Greenery and Natural Elements:**
   - Integrate greenery, trees, and plants within the plaza design to provide shade, improve air quality, and create a sense of connection with nature.
   - Incorporate sustainable landscaping practices prioritizing native plant species and water-efficient irrigation systems.
   - The presence of greenery enhances aesthetics, promotes well-being, and provides a respite from the surrounding urban environment.

10. **Adaptability and Futureproofing:**
   - Design the plaza with the future in mind.
   - Anticipate potential changes in technology, urban development, and social needs.
   - Ensure that the plaza's design allows for adaptability and evolution over time so it remains relevant and functional for future generations.

Following these guiding design principles, public plazas can significantly humanize high-rise cities by providing spaces prioritizing people, social interaction, and a sense of community. The subsequent chapter discusses the roles of “Streets as Plazas” in humanizing the high-rise city.
