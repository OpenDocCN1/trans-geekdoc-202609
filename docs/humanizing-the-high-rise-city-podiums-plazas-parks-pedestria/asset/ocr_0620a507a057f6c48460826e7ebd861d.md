# HIGHLIGHTS

# Positive Aspects

+   - **Central Location:** The plaza is strategically positioned within Chicago's bustling Streeterveille neighborhood, offering convenient access for residents and visitors.
- **Urban Retreat:** Amid the surrounding high-rise buildings and urban environment, the John Hancock Center Plaza serves as a retreat where people can relax and unwind.
- **Landscaping:** The plaza combines greenery, trees, and shrubs, providing a natural ambiance within the concrete jungle.
- **Seating Areas:** Movable chairs allow visitors to find comfortable spots to rest, socialize, and enjoy the surroundings.
- **Water Feature:** A prominent water feature, often called "The Water Wall," adds a sense of serenity and visual interest to the plaza.
- **Views:** The plaza offers views of the John Hancock Center's distinctive architecture and cityscape.
- **Urban Experience:** The plaza encapsulates the urban experience, allowing people to gather, enjoy outdoor spaces, and immerse themselves in the city's energy.
