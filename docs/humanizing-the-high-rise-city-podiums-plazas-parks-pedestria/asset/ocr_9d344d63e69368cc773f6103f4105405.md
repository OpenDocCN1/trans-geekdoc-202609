The sculpture, created by artist Scott Reeder, challenges viewers to question the authenticity of information and constructed narratives that shape our understanding of the world. The sculpture, first installed in 2013 at Art Basel Miami Beach, was on loan to Chicago. It serves as a commentary on contemporary society’s complex interplay between reality, perception, and truth. The sculpture’s prominent placement in front of the Trump Tower, a symbol of power and influence, invited reflection on the role of media, politics, and public perception in shaping our collective reality. The decision to install the sculpture in such a prominent location reflects the city’s commitment to fostering dialogue and critical thinking among residents and visitors. # **THE LOVE SCULPTURE, NEW YORK CITY**

The Love Sculpture in New York City is a three-dimensional representation of the word “LOVE” in bold capital letters, arranged in a stacked and tilted manner (Figure 14.9). Created by American artist Robert Indiana (1928–2018) in 1970, the sculpture has captured locals’ and visitors’ hearts and imaginations. Its simple yet powerful message resonated with people from diverse backgrounds, cultures, and languages, transcending barriers and inspiring a sense of connection and harmony.
