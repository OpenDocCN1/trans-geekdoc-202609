# **Shimao International Plaza, Shanghai [2006]**

Shimao International Plaza is a skyscraper in the Huangpu District of Shanghai, China. It was designed by Ingenhoven Overdick and Partners, a German architectural firm specializing in ecological and sustainable buildings. Completed in 2006 after five years of construction, the building has a height of 333 meters and consists of 60 floors above ground. The building features a distinctive triangular shape that narrows as it rises, creating a sleek and elegant silhouette. The shape also reduces the wind load and optimizes the use of space. The facade comprises glass and aluminum panels, with horizontal louvers that provide shading and reduce energy consumption.

The podium design of Shimao International Plaza is conceived to craft a vibrant and interactive urban space that mirrors the vitality and diversity of the city. As one looks towards the apex of the podium, a captivating arrangement of glass panels unfolds, adopting a staggered pattern that imparts a pronounced sense of movement and rhythm. This deliberate arrangement contributes to the visual dynamism of the space and allows natural light to permeate the interior.

A prominent feature of the podium is the expansive space-frame canopy that extends from the structure's pinnacle, creating a distinctive roofline that stands in deliberate contrast to the tower's verticality. This canopy comprises triangular modules of varying sizes and angles, resulting in a dynamic and sculptural form. The irregular arrangement of these modules adds an artistic flair to the canopy, creating an intriguing interplay of light and shadow (Al-Kodmany, 2018; Binder, 2015).

Notably, the carved-in design of the podium serves a dual purpose. Beyond its aesthetic significance, this design element protects pedestrians, shielding them from the elements and ensuring a comfortable and sheltered experience within the urban space. The large, round columns supporting the upper floors contribute to the podium's structural integrity and serve as distinctive design elements that enhance the overall visual appeal.

In essence, the podium design of Shimao International Plaza is a thoughtful integration of artistic expression, functionality, and protection. By incorporating dynamic elements such as the staggered glass panels, the striking space-frame canopy, and the protective carved-in design, the podium creates an aesthetically engaging urban environment that is responsive to the practical needs of those who inhabit and traverse the space.
