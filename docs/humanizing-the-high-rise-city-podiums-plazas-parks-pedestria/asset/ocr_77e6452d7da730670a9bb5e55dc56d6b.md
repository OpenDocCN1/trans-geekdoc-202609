# 10.3

The High Line offers passersby numerous options to sit, relax, and enjoy the scenes. Each area provides a unique atmosphere, immersing visitors in nature, art, and history. 22nd Street Seating Steps (top) 10th Avenue Square & Overlook (middle). *Source: Photographs by author.*

![](bbox=[0.25, 0.11, 0.87, 0.72])

+   1. **What If They Bark?** Cosima von Bonin’s *What If They Bark?* project features anthropomorphic fish sculptures above the iconic 10th Avenue Square at the High Line.
2. **Old Tree:** Pamela Rosenkranz’s third High Line Plinth commission features *Old Tree*, a vibrant sculpture that animates historical archetypes, symbolizing the tree of life connecting heaven and earth.
3. **Painful Arc II (Shoulder-High):** *Painful Arc II*, a monument by Baseera Khan, features tablets inscribed with packaging labels and jotted notes from High Line staff members, who were interviewed to create the artwork.
