Public art installations, sculptures, and murals in cities’ streetscapes reflect their cultural identity. They create a diverse urban environment that encourages pride and community engagement. Well-designed streetscapes also enhance safety, accessibility, and business activity. They support economic vitality, job opportunities, and livability. Distinctive architectural features and street furniture offer memorable experiences for residents and visitors.

Street streetscapes humanize high-rise, dense cities by prioritizing pedestrians, incorporating green spaces, fostering social interactions, and promoting cultural expression. They create inclusive, engaging, and livable environments that enhance the quality of life and contribute to the overall well-being of urban communities.

# **Lujiazui, Shanghai**

As explained earlier, Lujiazui, Shanghai’s prominent financial hub, boasts a stunning skyline and modern architectural wonders. The district is well-connected by a comprehensive network of roads, bridges, tunnels, and public transit systems, facilitating smooth and convenient traffic and access for pedestrians and vehicles alike. Lujiazui also incorporates green spaces, such as gardens, parks, and tree-lined boulevards, offering relaxation, recreation, and respite from the urban hustle (Al-Kodmany & Ali, 2013).

Lujiazui provides a high-quality pedestrian network with amenities and facilities, such as comfortable seating, sanitary public restrooms, and sufficient trash disposal. Its streetscape epitomizes the modernity, innovation, and splendor of Shanghai’s urban landscape, blending impressive architectural achievements with well-designed public spaces for a visually pleasing and functional environment for residents, workers, and visitors (Figure 13.9).

# **Hangzhou CBD**

Hangzhou’s central business district boasts a modern, beautiful streetscape with spacious sidewalks, abundant greenery, and majestic trees. These features create a pleasant and attractive environment for pedestrians and visitors, enhancing the visual appeal and prioritizing pedestrian comfort and safety. The rich vegetation adds natural beauty, while the towering trees provide shade and cooling effects, making the area more comfortable during hot weather. Hangzhou’s dedication to integrating green spaces and natural elements in its streetscape shows a considerate approach to urban design and sustainability (Al-Kodmany, 2017). Human-centered streetscapes humanize Hangzhou’s central business district (Figure 13.10).
