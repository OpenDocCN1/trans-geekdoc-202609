The exterior of the podium is adorned with high-quality granite cladding, providing a refined and sophisticated appearance. This choice of material adds a touch of luxury to the structure and ensures durability and resilience against environmental factors.
Metal screens incorporated into the design serve both functional and decorative purposes. They contribute to the tower's overall visual appeal while also serving as a dynamic element that interacts with light and shadow, adding depth to the facade. The screens create an intricate play of patterns, enriching the architectural experience for onlookers.
Strategically positioned canopies further enhance the tower's welcoming ambiance. These canopies serve as sheltering elements and contribute to the tower's dynamic and sleek form. Their design introduces a sense of movement, adding a layer of sophistication to the entryways. Functionally, the canopies protect the entrances from the elements, ensuring a comfortable experience for visitors (Al-Kodmany, 2017; Binder, 2015).
The Jin Mao Tower's podium design is a harmonious blend of granite cladding, metal screens, and canopies. This thoughtful integration elevates the tower's aesthetics and creates a visually striking and welcoming environment. The dynamic form of the canopies adds movement to the design, making the Jin Mao Tower a standout architectural landmark in Shanghai (Figure 2.14).
