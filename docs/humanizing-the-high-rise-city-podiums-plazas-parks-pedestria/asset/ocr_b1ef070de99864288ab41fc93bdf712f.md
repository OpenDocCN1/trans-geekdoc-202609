While the Chicago Riverwalk is celebrated for its positive contributions to the city, there are also some social, economic, and environmental downsides associated with the development:

# Social

+   * The popularity of the Chicago Riverwalk has led to congestion during peak times, potentially diminishing the experience for visitors and residents.
* Accessibility challenges, especially for individuals with mobility issues, may limit inclusivity and equal enjoyment of the space.
* Chicago’s harsh winters can limit the usability of the Riverwalk during colder months, reducing the social benefits for a significant portion of the year.

# Economic

+   * Maintaining the Riverwalk’s infrastructure, landscaping, and amenities requires ongoing investment. Balancing maintenance costs against the economic benefits can be challenging, particularly during economic downturns.
* The economic benefits of the Riverwalk are not evenly distributed. Some businesses, particularly those with prime locations, may thrive while others struggle. This can lead to commercial exclusivity, with only certain businesses being economically viable in the area.
* The success of the Riverwalk might draw foot traffic away from other nearby businesses that do not directly benefit from the increased tourism, potentially impacting their revenue.

# Environmental

+   * The construction and maintenance of the Riverwalk infrastructure may have ecological consequences, such as alterations to the natural riverbank habitats and potential disruption to local wildlife.
* Increased human activity along the river may contribute to water pollution, with litter and runoff potentially affecting the quality of the Chicago River. Balancing development with environmental preservation is crucial for the long-term health of the river ecosystem.
* The Riverwalk’s amenities, lighting, and other features may contribute to increased energy consumption. Ensuring the sustainability of these elements and incorporating eco-friendly practices is essential to mitigate the environmental impact of the development.
