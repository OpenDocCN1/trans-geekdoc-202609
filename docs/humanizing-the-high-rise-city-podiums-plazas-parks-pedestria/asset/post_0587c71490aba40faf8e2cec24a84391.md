A fantastic way to support the local economy and savor the seasonal flavors of the region. In summer, the plaza also presents live music from different genres and styles. The concerts are free and open to the public and provide a great chance to enjoy the warm weather and the city skyline. The concerts highlight talented musicians from Chicago and beyond and create a festive mood for everyone. The outdoor plaza at 311 South Wacker Plaza is a dynamic and lively space that improves the quality of life for tenants and visitors alike. It is a place where people can unwind, socialize, and have fun in the city’s center (Saliga & Clarke, 1998).

One of the most striking features of 311 South Wacker Drive is its winter garden and lobby. The winter garden is a two-level glass-ceilinged space that spans five stories and is filled with greenery and a fountain. The fountain is adorned with a bronze sculpture by Raymond Kaskey called “Gem of the Lakes,” representing Chicago as a city of broad shoulders and engineering feats. The sculpture is a 12-foot-tall heroic figure wearing a cape, symbolizing the reversal of the Chicago River’s flow. The figure holds a shell that forms the fountain’s basin and is taken from the city seal. The sculpture looks over the garden from the Wacker entrance and is a tribute to Chicago’s history and culture. The winter garden and lobby of 311 South Wacker Drive are a welcoming oasis for visitors and tenants alike. The lobby connects to the one-acre plaza seamlessly (Pridmore & Larson, 2005).

### HIGHLIGHTS

#### Positive Aspects

- **Central Location:** Situated in the heart of Chicago’s financial district, the plaza commands a prime central location, turning it into a hub for local professionals and visitors and enhancing its vibrancy and foot traffic.
- **Architectural Integration:** The plaza is seamlessly integrated into the design of the 311 South Wacker Drive building, creating a harmonious and visually appealing connection between the indoor and outdoor spaces.
- **Ample Seating:** Abounding in seating options, ranging from benches to movable chairs, the plaza actively encourages individuals to come together, unwind, and appreciate the ambient surroundings, thereby nurturing a profound sense of community.
- **Greenery and Landscaping:** Despite being in an urban area, the plaza features well-maintained greenery and landscaping, adding a touch of nature to the environment, enhancing its visual appeal, and creating a more pleasant atmosphere.
- **Gathering Space:** The open layout of the plaza accommodates gatherings, events, and outdoor activities. It serves as a dynamic space for both spontaneous interactions and planned gatherings.
- **Social Interaction:** Designed to foster social interaction, the plaza has become a favored destination for people to meet, socialize, and network, creating a strong sense of community among its visitors.
- **Eatery Options:** The presence of outdoor seating and nearby cafes or restaurants makes the plaza an inviting spot for people to enjoy meals, fostering a lively atmosphere.
- **Public Programming:** Hosting music performances, art exhibitions, and public lectures, the plaza infuses a dynamic and cultural element into the space, drawing diverse audiences to participate in these engaging activities.
- **City Views:** Situated in a prime location, the plaza offers captivating views of the Chicago River and the surrounding cityscape, fostering a sense of connection to the larger urban environment and enhancing the overall experience.
- **Pedestrian-Friendly:** The plaza prioritizes pedestrians, offering a safe and comfortable environment for walking, promoting a pedestrian-friendly atmosphere.

#### Areas for Improvement

- **Weather Dependency:** The lack of weather protection can limit the plaza's usability during rain, extreme temperatures, or windy conditions.
- **Intense Urban Activity:** Being situated in a bustling area, the plaza might experience high noise levels and crowded conditions, affecting its tranquility.
- **Lack of Programming:** While events might occur, more varied and frequent programming could enhance its appeal.
- **Nighttime Safety:** Adequate lighting and security measures might be needed to ensure a safe evening environment.
- **Overcrowding:** During peak periods, the plaza might become overcrowded, diminishing the overall user experience.

## ROCKEFELLER CENTER PLAZA, NEW YORK CITY

Rockefeller Center Plaza is a public space in the heart of New York City, located between Fifth and Sixth Avenues and 49th and 50th Streets. It is part of the larger Rockefeller Center complex, which includes 19 buildings and a variety of attractions, such as the famous ice-skating rink, the Top of the Rock observation deck, and Radio City Music Hall. The plaza is also home to several artworks, such as the bronze Statue of Atlas, the Channel Gardens with seasonal displays of flowers and fountains, and the iconic Christmas tree lit yearly in a festive ceremony. Originally and best known for over 50 years as the RCA Building, it features a grand architectural design characterized by Art Deco influences and showcases a series of elegant buildings, including the GE Building (General Electric Building, now known as the Comcast Building), which stands as the complex's center-piece. The buildings are known for their distinctive limestone façades, intricate detailing, and iconic rooftop decorations (Fitzgerald, 2003; Weisman, 1951).

The Rink at Rockefeller Center is a popular ice-skating rink that operates in winter, attracting locals and tourists. The Channel Gardens is a prominent feature located in Rockefeller Center. It serves as an attractive pedestrian walkway connecting Fifth Avenue and Rockefeller Plaza. The Channel Gardens consists of a series of planters and fountains that run along the central axis of the Rockefeller Center. It undergoes seasonal transformations throughout the year, showcasing beautiful displays of flowers, plants, and decorations that reflect the changing seasons. Every year, a massive Christmas tree is erected in the plaza, attracting millions of visitors who come to see its stunning decorations. The tree is often adorned with thousands of colorful lights and a Swarovski crystal star. An underground network connects various buildings within Rockefeller Center and houses many shops, boutiques, restaurants, and cafes (Laurence, 2006; Fitzgerald, 2003).

The Statue of Prometheus in Rockefeller Center is a famous sculpture in the Lower Plaza of Rockefeller Center. Designed by the American sculptor Paul Manship, the statue depicts the Greek mythological figure Prometheus, known for stealing fire from the gods and giving it to humanity. The sculpture stands at the center of the Lower Plaza, directly in front of the iconic ice-skating rink. It was installed in 1934 and has become one of Rockefeller Center's most recognizable and enduring symbols. The statue is made of gilded bronze and stands approximately 18 feet tall. Prometheus is depicted holding a flame above his head, symbolizing the gift of fire. The sculpture is highly detailed, showcasing Manship's skills in sculpting dynamic and expressive forms. In action, the skating rink and the flow of skaters are often portrayed alongside Prometheus. Considering the historical backdrop of the 1930s, which included the Great Depression and the approaching World War II, as well as the emergence of 20th-century Modernism, this sculpture made of gilded, cast bronze gains substantial significance (Weisman, 1951; Figure 3.17).

The plaza serves as a venue for various cultural events and public gatherings throughout the year. These events include concerts, art installations, exhibitions, and seasonal festivals. The plaza's expansive space and iconic backdrop make it an ideal location for large-scale public celebrations and performances. The plaza is a popular destination for tourists and locals who enjoy its art, history, and entertainment options. Rockefeller Center Plaza is more than just a public space. It symbolizes New York City's culture, creativity, and vitality. It is a place where people can connect with each other and the world. It is a place where dreams are made and celebrated.
