The garden showcases plants from the Mediterranean and South Africa that are highly resistant to drought. Notable plant species include Strelitzia reginae (Bird of Paradise), Agapanthus (African Lily), Kniphofia (Red Hot Poker), and Agave attenuata (Lion's Tail). Gillespies, a landscape architecture firm based in London, designed the Sky Garden (Viñoly et al., 2015).

The following highlights the key aspects of the Sky Garden:

+   - **Serene Environment:** The Sky Garden features beautifully landscaped gardens with various plants and foliage. The interior space is beautifully landscaped with various lush plants, trees, and flowers, creating a serene environment reminiscent of a tropical garden. It is designed to provide visitors with a tranquil retreat where they can relax, socialize, and enjoy the beauty of nature (Reike, 2015; Viñoly et al., 2015). Within the Sky Garden, several designated areas serve different purposes. The main central area provides ample seating, allowing visitors to enjoy the panoramic views while sitting and admiring the surroundings. Some walkways and pathways guide visitors through the garden, providing opportunities to explore different sections and enjoy different cityscape perspectives. Overall, the garden is a leafy indoor space, a perfect place to get botanical inspiration while remaining indoors. It is helpful in a place like London, known for its rainy weather.

+   - **Floor-to-Ceiling Windows and Roof:** The indoor area of the Sky Garden features a stunning glass dome-like structure. The roof is designed with a unique shape, resembling an expansive greenhouse that allows natural light to flood the space. The Sky Garden also features floor-to-ceiling glass windows, providing unobstructed views of London. Visitors can enjoy stunning vistas of iconic landmarks such as the Shard, The Gherkin, St Paul's Cathedral, Tower Bridge, the River Thames, and more.

+   - **Observation Decks:** In addition to the indoor spaces, the Sky Garden offers an open-air terrace that allows visitors to step outside and experience the fresh air while enjoying the spectacular views. Visitors can marvel at the city's architectural wonders and capture stunning photographs of the London skyline. As visitors traverse the deck, they can immerse themselves in the dynamic energy of London, spotting famous landmarks and offering an unforgettable experience that uniquely invites visitors to connect with the city.

+   - **Restaurants and Bars:** The Sky Garden is not only a place of natural beauty but also offers various dining options. It is home to a range of restaurants and bars, allowing visitors to indulge in culinary delights while surrounded by the enchanting ambiance of the garden. To ensure a pleasant and comfortable experience for visitors, Sky Garden provides amenities such as restrooms, cloakroom facilities, and dedicated staff members who can assist and answer any queries.
