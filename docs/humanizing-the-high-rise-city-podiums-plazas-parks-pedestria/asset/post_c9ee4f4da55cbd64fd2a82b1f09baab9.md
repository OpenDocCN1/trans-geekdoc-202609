# HIGHLIGHTS

- **Positive Aspects**
  - **Pedestrian Focus:** East Nanjing Road encourages people to explore the area on foot by prioritizing pedestrians over vehicular traffic. This design choice fosters a sense of connection with the urban environment, allowing individuals to engage with the city on a human scale. It provides a break from the fast-paced vehicular environment commonly associated with high-rise cities.
  - **Community Activities:** Daily activities such as chess games, tai chi, martial arts, and communal dancing contribute to a sense of community and human interaction. These activities create a shared space where people of different ages and backgrounds can come together, fostering social bonds and a sense of belonging.
  - **Architectural Diversity:** East Nanjing Road showcases diverse architectural styles representing different historical periods. This architectural diversity breaks the monotony often associated with high-rise buildings. It provides a visually appealing environment that connects people to the city's history and heritage.
  - **Cultural Attractions:** The street is not just a commercial hub but also hosts cultural attractions, including museums, theaters, art galleries, and historical sites. These cultural elements add depth to the urban experience, allowing residents and visitors to engage with art, history, and local culture. This contributes to a more holistic and humanized urban environment.
  - **Nightlife and Festivities:** The vibrant nightlife, dance parties, and evening storytelling sessions contribute to the city's humanization. It transforms the street into a place of joy, entertainment, and shared experiences. Festive holiday decorations and themed displays add excitement, making the urban environment more welcoming and relatable.

- **Areas for Improvement**
  - **Crowded Atmosphere:** The lively atmosphere contributes to the dynamic urban experience. However, the street's popularity can lead to overcrowding, especially during peak hours. This may result in a less enjoyable experience for some visitors who prefer a quieter and more relaxed environment.
  - **Cultural Homogenization:** East Nanjing Road showcases a blend of architectural styles, reflecting different eras and influences. However, the heavy emphasis on international luxury brands and commercial enterprises might contribute to a sense of cultural homogenization. The street's identity could become more generic and less reflective of local culture.
  - **Limited Green Spaces:** East Nanjing Road is a pedestrian-friendly environment. However, the street might lack green spaces or areas for relaxation. The absence of such spaces could limit opportunities for people to unwind and connect with nature amid the urban hustle.
  - **Lack of Affordable Options:** The street offers many shopping options. However, the focus on luxury brands and high-end shopping might limit the inclusivity of the street. A lack of affordable options might make it less accessible to a broader socioeconomic demographic.

In summary, East Nanjing Road contributes to humanizing the high-rise city of Shanghai by offering a pedestrian-friendly, culturally rich environment that encourages community engagement, celebrates diversity, and provides spaces for shared experiences and cultural exploration. East Nanjing Pedestrian Street is a lively and dynamic destination in Shanghai, offering a blend of shopping, entertainment, dining, and cultural experiences. Its vibrant atmosphere makes it

# KEY TAKEAWAYS: LESSONS FROM TIMES SQUARE AND EAST NANJING ROAD AS STREETS AS PLAZAS

The examination of Times Square in New York City and East Nanjing Road in Shanghai as examples of streets transformed into plazas reveals crucial insights into humanizing high-rise cities:

1. **Pedestrian Priority:** Transforming streets into pedestrian-centric zones fosters a more intimate connection between people and their urban environment. By limiting vehicular traffic, spaces encourage exploration on foot, providing a break from the fast-paced nature of high-rise city life.
2. **Cultural Integration:** Successfully humanizing high-rise cities involves balancing modernity and cultural authenticity. Iconic billboards and diverse architectural styles in Times Square and Nanjing Road East, respectively, showcase this fusion, creating environments that resonate with locals and tourists.
3. **Inclusive Design:** Prioritizing accessibility ensures that urban spaces cater to everyone. Ramps, elevators, and features that enhance inclusivity contribute to the humanization of the city, making these spaces welcoming for people of all ages and abilities.
4. **Dynamic Atmosphere:** The strategic use of lighting and the creation of vibrant atmospheres, especially during nighttime, contribute to the humanization of high-rise cities. These elements transform urban spaces into inviting and visually stunning environments that enhance the overall experience.
5. **Cultural Events and Community Engagement:** Streets transformed into plazas serve as platforms for cultural events and community engagement. Whether through festivals, celebrations, or activities like dancing and story-telling, these spaces foster a sense of community and shared experiences, contributing to the humanization of the urban landscape.
6. **Adaptability and Flexibility:** The ability to adapt and reconfigure spaces to accommodate various activities ensures ongoing relevance. Both Times Square and East Nanjing Road demonstrate the importance of flexibility, allowing for seasonal events, performances, and other dynamic uses.
