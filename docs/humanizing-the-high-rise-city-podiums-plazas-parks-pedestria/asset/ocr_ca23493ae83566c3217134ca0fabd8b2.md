+   10.  **Continuously Evolve and Innovate:**
	*   Embrace innovation and adaptability within sculpture parks.
	*   Regularly introduce new sculptures and rotating exhibits to keep the park dynamic and engaging.
	*   Embrace technological advancements by incorporating interactive elements or digital art installations, creating a contemporary and forward-thinking experience.

By implementing these recommendations, sculpture parks can play a pivotal role in humanizing high-rise cities, fostering community engagement, celebrating local culture, and creating vibrant and inclusive public spaces that enhance the overall quality of urban life.
