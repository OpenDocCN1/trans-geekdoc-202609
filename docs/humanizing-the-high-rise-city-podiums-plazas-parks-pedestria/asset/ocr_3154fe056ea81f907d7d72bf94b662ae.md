+   1.  **Proportional Scale**: The podium should be designed with a scale appropriate for human interaction. By breaking down the overall mass of the building and incorporating horizontal and vertical elements, the podium can be visually and physically more accessible and relatable to pedestrians.
    
2.  **Visual Interest and Variability**: Introducing visual interest and variability in the design of podiums helps to break down the scale and monotony of the building. This can be achieved using varied materials, façade treatments, setbacks, and articulation, creating a visually appealing and dynamic streetscape.
    
3.  **Natural Elements and Biophilic Design**: Incorporating natural elements, such as landscaping, green walls, or vertical gardens, can enhance the connection to nature and improve the overall ambiance of the podium. Biophilic design principles can be applied to create a more human-centered and psychologically supportive environment.
    
4.  **Daylight and Views**: Maximizing access to natural daylight and views is essential for human-scaled podiums. Incorporating large windows, glass facades, and transparent spaces can allow for ample daylight penetration, while strategically located openings can provide views of the surrounding context, enhancing the visual experience.
    
5.  **Active Street Frontages**: Designing podiums with active street frontages is crucial to creating an engaging and vibrant environment. Incorporating ground-level retail spaces, cafes, restaurants, and other amenities can activate the street level, attracting people and fostering social interaction.
