+   **2. Complete Streets:**
* Embrace the concept of complete streets that accommodate various modes of transportation, including pedestrians, cyclists, and public transit.
* Design safe, convenient, and inclusive streets for all users.
* Provide dedicated cycling lanes, well-designed bus stops, and ample pedestrian crossings to promote multimodal connectivity.

+   **3. Human Scale and Proportions:**
* Design streetscapes and promenades with a human scale in mind.
* Consider the proportions of buildings, street furniture, lighting, and landscaping elements to create an inviting and comfortable environment.
* Enhance the visual appeal and character of the area by integrating architectural elements that complement the surrounding context.

+   **4. Active and Passive Spaces:**
* Incorporate a mix of active and passive spaces to cater to different activities and user preferences.
* Include gathering areas, plazas, seating, and green spaces for relaxation and social interaction.
* Integrate amenities such as water features, public art, and cultural installations to enhance the experience and create focal points.

+   **5. Landscaping and Greenery:**
* Integrate landscaping and greenery to create visually appealing and environmentally friendly streetscapes.
