# ## Podiums

The Henderson Metropolitan shopping mall podium exemplifies a successful architectural typology characterized by a clear horizontal division between the podium and the tower (Figure 2.26). This delineation adds to the building's visual appeal and facilitates a functional separation of different activities within the structure. In summary, the podium design of Henderson Metropolitan skillfully combines modern aesthetics, functionality, and a thoughtful spatial layout to create a vibrant and engaging urban space.

# **KK100, Shenzhen [2011]**

KK100 is a supertall skyscraper in Shenzhen, China, standing at 442 meters (1,450 feet) and housing 98 floors. TFP Farrelis, a British firm specializing in high-rise buildings, designed the tower. Completed in 2011, the building features a curved glass facade that tapers toward the top, creating a sleek and elegant profile. The building also has a distinctive crown that houses an observation deck, a garden, and several restaurants on the upper floors (Binder, 2015).

The podium design of KK100 is a masterful composition that adds to the iconic stature of one of the city's tallest buildings. The main entrance stands out with a distinctive feature—a large circular opening that serves as a focal point, framing the entrance visually strikingly. This circular design enhances the building's visibility from various angles and contributes to a sense of grandeur, elegance, and arrival for visitors.

Aesthetically, the entrance is a captivating element that plays a crucial role in the overall composition of the building.
