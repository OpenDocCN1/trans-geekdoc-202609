# Areas for Improvement

+   - **Accessibility**: Evaluate and enhance accessibility features to ensure the park
is easily navigable for all visitors, including those with mobility challenges.
- **Maintenance.** Regularly maintaining sculptures and landscaping is essen-
tial to preserve the park’s aesthetic appeal and ensure a positive visitor
experience.
- **Informational Signage**: Improve signage within the park to provide clear
information about the artworks, artists, and any interactive features, enhanc-
ing educational aspects for visitors.
