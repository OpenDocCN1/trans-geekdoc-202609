+   6. **Pedestrian Connectivity:** The design should prioritize pedestrian connectivity by providing clear and direct pathways and circulation routes. The podium should be seamlessly integrated with the surrounding urban fabric, connecting with sidewalks, public spaces, and transit hubs to encourage foot traffic.

+   7. **Safety and Accessibility:** Ensuring the safety and accessibility of the podium is paramount. Design elements such as well-lit walkways, clear signage, ramps, elevators, and universal accessibility features should be incorporated to cater to the needs of all individuals, including those with disabilities.

+   8. **Cultural Integration:** Infuse cultural elements into the podium's design to reflect the identity and context of the community. Art installations, cultural motifs, or historical references can create a sense of place and connection.

+   9. **Sustainability Practices:** Implement sustainable practices in the design of the podium, including energy-efficient technologies, green roofs, and water management systems. Ensure that the podium contributes positively to the environmental impact of the high-rise development, aligning with broader sustainability goals.

+   10. **Public Spaces and Plazas:** Public spaces and plazas near the podium provide opportunities for people to gather, rest, and engage with the surroundings. These spaces can feature seating areas, greenery, water features, and public art, enhancing the overall quality of the pedestrian experience.
