Canopies are incorporated into these crossings, serving a dual purpose. Firstly, they protect pedestrians from debris falling from the bridge deck above, enhancing safety and comfort.

Secondly, the canopies contribute to the aesthetic appeal of the Riverwalk by creating an architectural element that complements the surrounding environment. The choice of stainless-steel material for these under-bridges is not merely functional but adds an artistic dimension to the project. The reflective properties of stainless steel beautifully mirror the texture and color of the river's water, creating a dynamic interplay between the structure and its aquatic surroundings. Moreover, as pedestrians move through these spaces, their motions become part of this reflective spectacle, further enhancing the visual experience (Al-Kodmany, 2018; Kamin, 2022a, 2023).

Beyond their functional and aesthetic contributions, the under-bridges serve a crucial urban design purpose. These crossings delineate distinct sections by creating natural breaks along the Riverwalk's various areas, allowing visitors to transition seamlessly between different environments. This deliberate urban planning adds to the overall coherence and navigability of the Riverwalk, ensuring that it remains functional and visually captivating. In essence, the under-bridge crossings exemplify the project's commitment to creating a holistic and user-friendly experience, where practical considerations align seamlessly with architectural aesthetics, contributing to the success and continuity of the Chicago Riverwalk (Figure 9.10).
