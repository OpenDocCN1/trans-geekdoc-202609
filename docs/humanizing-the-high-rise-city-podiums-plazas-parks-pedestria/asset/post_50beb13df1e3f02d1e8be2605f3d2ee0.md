
## 1. Safety and Accessibility
The Sky Ring and elevated walkways prioritize pedestrian safety by providing a separate pathway away from vehicular traffic. This enhances the pedestrian experience by offering a safe, dedicated space for walking, jogging, or cycling. Lujiazui's Sky Ring and elevated walkways offer breathtaking skyline views, including iconic landmarks such as the Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower, and Jin Mao Tower. The elevated walkways in Lujiazui partially incorporate greenery and landscaping, bringing a touch of nature into the urban environment. Trees, plants, and gardens are strategically placed along the walkways, creating a visually appealing and refreshing atmosphere. Greenery contributes to a sense of well-being, tranquility, and connection with nature for pedestrians.

## 4. Public Seating and Rest Areas
The elevated walkways in Lujiazui feature public seating areas and rest spots, allowing pedestrians to take a break, socialize, or enjoy the surroundings. These areas contribute to a sense of community and offer comfortable, relaxing spaces while appreciating the views or interacting with fellow pedestrians.

Through these humanizing aspects, the Sky Ring and elevated walkways in Lujiazui enhance the pedestrian experience, providing shelter, safety, scenic views, greenery, and cultural enrichment. They create a more comfortable and engaging urban environment that prioritizes pedestrians' well-being and enjoyment as they navigate Lujiazui's vibrant district (Figures 13.6 and 13.7).

## STREETSAPES

Streetscapes are a street or road's visual and physical components, such as design, architecture, landscaping, and aesthetic appearance. They include the buildings, sidewalks, street furniture, vegetation, lighting, signage, and other elements that create the character and ambiance of a street. The streetscape is a key element of urban design and affects the overall appearance, functionality, and quality of a street and the experience of pedestrians, cyclists, and motorists who use the space.

Streetscapes play a vital role in transforming high-rise, dense cities into more human and lively places, as they promote dynamic, people-centered environments that prioritize pedestrians and enrich the urban experience. By incorporating green spaces, trees, and urban vegetation into the streetscape, the adverse impacts of urbanization, such as air pollution, heat island effect, and visual clutter, can be mitigated. These natural elements can also improve the aesthetic quality, mental health, and livability of the city, creating a sense of spaciousness and tranquility in high-density settings (Al-Kodmany, 2020).

Public art installations, sculptures, and murals in cities’ streetscapes reflect their cultural identity. They create a diverse urban environment that encourages pride and community engagement. Well-designed streetscapes also enhance safety, accessibility, and business activity. They support economic vitality, job opportunities, and livability. Distinctive architectural features and street furniture offer memorable experiences for residents and visitors.

Street streetscapes humanize high-rise, dense cities by prioritizing pedestrians, incorporating green spaces, fostering social interactions, and promoting cultural expression. They create inclusive, engaging, and livable environments that enhance the quality of life and contribute to the overall well-being of urban communities.
