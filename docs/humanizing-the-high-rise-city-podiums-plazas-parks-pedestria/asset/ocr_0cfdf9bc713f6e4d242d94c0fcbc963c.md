10-2  The High Line: It transformed a neglected elevated rail line into a green oasis that connects multiple neighborhoods in the Heart of New York City. 22nd Street Seating Steps (top); Donald Pels and Wendy Keys Gansvoort Woodland (next to bottom). Source: Photographs by author.

+   - **On Growth:** Kapwani Kiwanga’s sculpture, *On Growth*, features a fern encased in glass, referencing the historical botanic nurseries that significantly influenced modern plant science and horticulture.

+   - **Dancing About Architecture:** *Dancing About Architecture* features daily screenings of films by Gerard & Kelly, Young-jun Tak, and Clarissa Tossin, who interpret architecture through the human body.

+   - **Noah’s Ark in the Coachella Valley and Blowout:**Jessie Homer French’s paintings, inaugurated at the High Line–Moynihan Connector Billboard, urge for climate justice and highlight the detrimental effects of human negligence on wildlife and the environment.
