# 2. Promote Green Spaces and Parks.

+   - Incorporate green spaces, parks, and pocket gardens at various levels of the city to provide visual relief and spaces for relaxation.
- Design rooftops and terraces as green oases, integrating nature into the vertical realm.
- Prioritize using native plants, sustainable landscaping, and water features to enhance biodiversity.

# 3. Incorporate Public Plazaa and Gathering Spaces.

+   - Design public plazas and open spaces that invite social interaction, community events, and cultural gatherings.
- Provide seating, shading, and amenities to make these spaces comfortable and inviting.

# 4. Create Mixed-Use Spaces.

+   - Integrate residential, commercial, recreational, and cultural functions within the same neighborhood or building complex.
- Foster a vibrant 24/7 environment with various activities and functions catering to different ages and needs.

# 5. Prioritize Active Transportation:

+   - Design streetscapes encouraging cycling and walking, with dedicated bike lanes and safe crossings.
- Implement pedestrian-friendly and bike-sharing programs infrastructure.

# 6. Prioritize Walkability and Connectivity:

+   - Design pedestrian-friendly streetscapes and pathways that encourage walking and interaction.
- Create a network of well-connected sidewalks, pedestrian bridges, and pathways to enhance mobility.
- Ensure easy access to public transportation hubs and cycling lanes for sustainable commuting.

# 7. Embrace Vertical Landscaping:

+   - Integrate vertical gardens, living walls, and hanging planters on building facades to soften the visual impact of concrete structures.
- Utilize greenery to improve air quality, provide insulation, and create a sense of connection with nature.

# 8. Foster Inclusivity and Diversity:

+   - Create spaces accommodating people of all ages, abilities, and backgrounds.
- Embrace cultural diversity through public art, design elements, and programming celebrating different traditions.
