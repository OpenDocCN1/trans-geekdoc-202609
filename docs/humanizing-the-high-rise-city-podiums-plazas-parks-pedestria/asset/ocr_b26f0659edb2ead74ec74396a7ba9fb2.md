# MONUMENT WITH STANDING BEAST SCULPTURE, CHICAGO

“Monument with Standing Beast” Sculpture by Jean Dubuffet in Chicago is a monumental work of public art, a striking representation of the artist's imaginative vision. This impressive sculpture, constructed from fiberglass and painted in a white hue with black outlines, commands attention as it rises over 29 feet tall. It is situated prominently in front of the James R. Thompson Center captivates passersby with its powerful presence (Figure 14.5). Part of Dubuffet's Hour loupe series, the Standing Beast Sculpture showcases the artist's exploration of creating forms from doodles and scribbles. This series reflects Dubuffet's unconventional approach to art, challenging traditional notions of beauty and inviting viewers to engage with their imagination and curiosity. The sculpture's bold presence and unconventional form encourage viewers to question established norms and embrace new perspectives. Placed in a prominent public space, it promotes accessibility and encourages active engagement with the artwork. The sculpture's towering stature, unique design, and challenging nature defy conventional notions of beauty and art, encouraging viewers to embrace curiosity and engage in a dynamic dialogue with the sculpture and its surrounding environment. The 10-ton Jean Dubuffet sculpture that stood in front of the James R. Thompson Center since 1984 is being relocated after Google acquired the building (Herder, 2011).
