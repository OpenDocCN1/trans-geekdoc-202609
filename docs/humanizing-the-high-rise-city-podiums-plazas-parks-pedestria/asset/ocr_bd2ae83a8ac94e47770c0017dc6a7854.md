3.21 The Shanghai IFC Plaza provides ample public space for pedestrians, with a layout encouraging exploration through paved areas, wide walkways, stairways, and seating areas. The plaza is enhanced by the presence of an Apple Store designed with ultra-transparent glass, serving as the focal point, and attracting more people to the plaza. *Source: Photographs by author.*
