9/11 Memorial Plaza is seamlessly connected to the World Trade Center Transportation Hub, known as the Oculus. This architectural marvel serves as a transportation hub for various subway lines and PATH trains and provides underground access to nearby destinations, ensuring convenient connectivity for commuters and visitors. Over a million passengers are served weekly by the twelve subway lines, the World Trade Center PATH station, and dozens of stores in the Oculus. Along with monthly activities, including farmers' markets, art exhibits, musical performances, and retail sales, the main floor is busy with commuters, shoppers, and tourists (Figure 3.19).
