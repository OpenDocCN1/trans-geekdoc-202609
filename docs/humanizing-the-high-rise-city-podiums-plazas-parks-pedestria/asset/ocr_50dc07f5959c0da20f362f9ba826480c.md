Cloud Forest at Gardens by the Bay. A captivating conservatory featuring a 35-meter-tall mountain draped in lush vegetation and waterfall, providing an immersive journey into a tropical montane environment, highlighting the importance of biodiversity. *Source: Photographs by author.*

+   - **Recreational Spaces**: The park features wide open spaces, picnic areas, and walking paths, providing a tranquil environment for relaxation, exercise, and leisure activities.
- **Children's Garden**: The dedicated children's garden offers a range of interactive play zones and water features, catering to the younger generation and fostering a love for nature.
- **Educational Opportunities**: The park offers educational programs and guided tours that teach visitors about horticulture, conservation, and sustainable practices.
