Shenzhen's streets feature unique architectural elements, including bridges that provide shade and protection for pedestrians during the hot summer. They serve a dual purpose in Shenzhen's urban landscape: providing shade and enhancing the walking experience while promoting efficient urban mobility and connectivity.

The aesthetically pleasing architectural designs contribute to the visual appeal of Shenzhen's streetscape, blending functionality with innovative design concepts.
