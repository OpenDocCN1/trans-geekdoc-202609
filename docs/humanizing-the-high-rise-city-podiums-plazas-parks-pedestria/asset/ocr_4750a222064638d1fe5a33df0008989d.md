# Areas for Improvement

+   * **Sensitivity:** Due to its commemorative nature, some visitors may find the plaza emotionally overwhelming or difficult to visit.

+   * **Tourist Attraction:** The plaza's popularity as a tourist destination might occasionally lead to overcrowding, impacting the solemnity of the space.

+   * **Limited Amenities:** The plaza's design prioritizes its memorial function, resulting in limited seating, shading, and recreational amenities.

+   * **Weather Exposure:** Insufficient shading or indoor spaces could expose visitors to harsh weather conditions.

+   * **Lack of Active Use:** The emphasis on reflection and commemoration might limit the plaza's potential for active use.

+   * **Long Lines:** The popularity of the memorial pools might lead to long lines and waiting times for entry.

+   * **Maintenance:** The cascading waterfalls and reflecting pools require continuous maintenance to ensure proper functioning.

# UNION SQUARE, SAN FRANCISCO

Union Square is located in the bustling sociocommercial heart of San Francisco. The area surrounding the square is a convergence point for major hotels and department stores, creating a vibrant and dynamic atmosphere. Within this district, one can explore many upscale boutiques, diverse restaurants offering culinary delights, lively nightspots, and engaging galleries. These establishments are seamlessly integrated into the urban landscape, nestled between larger buildings, contributing to the lively and diverse character of the area. Union Square, occupying a sprawling 2.6-acre expanse, is framed by the intersecting streets of Geary, Powell, Post, and Stockton. Its name harks back to a significant historical context, as it was a site for pro-Union rallies during the American Civil War. This civic space has evolved into a central hub for commerce, leisure, and cultural happenings (Union Square).

At the heart of Union Square stands the impressive Dewey Monument, soaring to a height of 97 feet (30 meters). This monument is a tribute to Admiral George Dewey and commemorates his triumph in the Battle of Manila Bay during the Spanish-American War. The Dewey Monument adds a monumental and historical touch to the square, becoming a focal point that captures the city's history and role as a vibrant contemporary urban center. As visitors explore the square's surroundings, they are immersed in the bustling commerce and encounter this towering monument, a testament to the intersection of history and modernity in the heart of San Francisco (Bosselmann, 2012).

The pattern incorporates granite of gray and beige colors, adding visual interest and texture to the space. There are seating areas with benches and vegetation.
