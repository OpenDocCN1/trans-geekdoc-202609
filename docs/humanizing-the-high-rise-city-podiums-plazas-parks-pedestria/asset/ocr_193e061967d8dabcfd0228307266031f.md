+   • **Observer, Observed**: Julia Philips creates a custom bronze binocular sculpture in 26th Street Park, allowing visitors to interact with the sculpture, transforming the power dynamics of watching and being watched.

# *Membership*

Members contribute almost 90% of the High Line’s annual operating support. Friends of the High Line relies on its members to employ personnel responsible for maintaining the park’s cleanliness, tending to the plant life, and developing activities and artwork to captivate visitors. Members receive the biannual High Line Magazine, exclusive event invitations, discounts at local businesses, and other privileges.

# HIGHLIGHTS

# *Positive Aspects*

+   • **Green Space in an Urban Environment**: The High Line offers a much-needed green area in the middle of New York City’s congested, tall buildings. It is a linear park built on a former elevated railway track, bringing nature and greenery to an otherwise concrete-dominated environment. The park features a variety of plants, trees, and flowers, creating a serene and refreshing atmosphere for visitors.

+   • **Pedestrian-Friendly Space**: The High Line offers a pedestrian-friendly pathway above street level by repurposing the elevated railway tracks. It allows people to explore the city from a different perspective, away from the noise and traffic of the streets. The design of the High Line incorporates seating areas, gathering spaces, and overlooks, providing opportunities for relaxation and social interaction.

+   • **Cultural and Artistic Elements**: The High Line includes murals, sculptures, and public art installations. These creative elements enhance the city’s cultural fabric and add visual appeal. The park frequently hosts temporary art exhibitions, performances, and educational programs, enriching the cultural experience for visitors.
