# ACROS FUKUOKA PREFECTURAL INTERNATIONAL HALL, FUKUOKA

Located in the heart of Fukuoka, Japan, the ACROS Fukuoka Prefectural International Hall is a hub for international, cultural, and informational interaction. It aimed to provide an innovative solution to a prevalent urban issue: combining a developer’s desire for lucrative site use with the public’s need for open green space. By developing an innovative agro-urban model, Fukuoka’s strategy satisfies both objectives.
