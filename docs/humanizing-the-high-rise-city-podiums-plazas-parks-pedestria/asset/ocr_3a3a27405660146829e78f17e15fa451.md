+   8. **Collaboration and Stakeholder Engagement:**
- Engage with stakeholders, including building owners, architects, tenants, and landscape professionals, throughout the planning and design process.
- Collaborate with maintenance teams and horticultural experts to ensure a shared understanding of the requirements and responsibilities for maintaining the greenery-covered building.

+   9. **Environmental Considerations:**
- Assess the environmental impact of the greenery-covered building, considering factors such as biodiversity, air quality improvement, and carbon sequestration.
- Incorporate elements such as birdhouses, insect-friendly plants, and pollinator habitats to support local ecosystems.
- Consider using sustainable materials during construction and promote recycling and waste management practices.

+   10. **Educational and Social Engagement:**
- Utilize the greenery-covered building as an educational and social tool.
- Implement interpretive signage, guided tours, or interactive displays to inform and engage occupants and visitors.
- Promote community involvement through workshops, events, or gardening initiatives to foster a sense of ownership and connection with greenery.

Following these guidelines for planning and design, skyscrapers covered in vegetation can help humanize spaces and create a more sustainable and aesthetically pleasing cityscape. Incorporating nature into urban planning improves sustainability, offers residents a host of advantages, and produces a novel and uplifting environment.
