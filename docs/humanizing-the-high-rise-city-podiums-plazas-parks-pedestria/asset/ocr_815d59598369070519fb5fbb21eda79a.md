+   - Create pedestrian-friendly paths, bike lanes, and connections to public transportation to enhance accessibility.
- Integrate parks with surrounding amenities like libraries, community centers, and retail areas to create a seamless and integrated urban experience.

# 10. Maintenance and Management:

+   - Establish a robust maintenance and management plan to ensure the park’s ongoing care, cleanliness, and safety.
- Involve the community in stewardship programs, volunteer opportunities, and educational initiatives to foster a sense of ownership and pride among park users.

Following these guiding design principles, public parks can be crucial in humanizing dense, high-rise cities. They create inclusive and vibrant spaces that foster social interaction, cultural expression, and a connection with nature. These parks enhance the quality of life, promote well-being, and contribute to the overall livability of the urban environment. It’s important to note that specific design principles can vary depending on the park’s context, location, and intended purpose. Designers and planners should consider each space’s unique characteristics and needs to create inviting and engaging public spaces that serve their intended functions and enhance the human experience. The subsequent chapter discusses the roles of city-wide parks in humanizing the high-rise city.
