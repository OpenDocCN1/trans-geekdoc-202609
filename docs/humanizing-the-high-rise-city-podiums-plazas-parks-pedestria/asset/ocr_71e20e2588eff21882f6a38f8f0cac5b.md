Salesforce Park is a multifaceted park, including interconnected gardens and communal areas, providing a range of experiences for visitors. The prominent sections encompass the Main Plaza, Amphitheater, Bus Fountain, Children's Play Area, Walking Trail, and The Gondola, which offers sweeping vistas over the park and the adjacent city, as follows (AI-Kodmany, 2018a, 2018b; Salesforce Transit Center; Schorung, 2019).

+   1. **Main Plaza.** The central square of Salesforce Park has an oculus encircled by a cluster of bamboo trees. This expansive area is an ideal place for individuals to gather and engage in recreational activities. Moreover, visitors have the unique opportunity to stroll on the largest glass floor in the United States. After sunset, the main plaza organizes exclusive activities, including a music series and captivating fairytales. Throughout the day, there are engaging sessions of storytelling led by accomplished literary artists, invigorating exercise courses, and entertaining activities specifically designed for children.

+   2. **Amphitheater.** Situated near the westernmost part of the park, this amphitheater can accommodate up to 1,000 individuals on its verdant lawn. Additionally, it serves as an excellent venue for a diverse range of activities, such as complimentary yoga sessions, live performances, and outdoor film screenings beneath the night sky.

+   3. **Bus Fountain.** An engaging aspect of the park is its interactive permanent public artwork, a fountain designed expressively to synchronize with the arrivals and departures of the buses at the terminal below. This contemporary creation is constructed using almost 1,200 feet of white and gray granite, making it one of the longest water artworks in the world.

+   4. **Children's Play Area.** Situated amid the center lawn and the main plaza lies a designated children's play area, showcasing a climbing rope structure on a cushioned, safe surface. Following their energetic play in the play structure, children can borrow a book from the Children's Reading Room for spontaneous storytelling or select a board game from the game cart.

+   5. **Walking Trail.** The park's perimeter is a walking route extending for 0.5 miles. The path features 13 compact botanical gardens showcasing diverse flora and wildlife from various global areas.

+   6. **The Gondola.** This ascent to a rooftop park, while not the usual elevator or escalator experience, undoubtedly offers a more scenic and picturesque journey.

Salesforce Park hosts cultural and community events, including live music performances, art installations, and wellness activities, attracting residents and visitors. Accessibility is a crucial feature, with multiple entrances and elevators for easy access, including for those with mobility challenges. The park is integrated with the surrounding transportation infrastructure, connecting with the Salesforce Transit Center and nearby public transit options, making it easily accessible to individuals from all over the city.
