# **Henderson Metropolitan, Shanghai [2011]**

Henderson Metropolitan is located at 300 East Nanjing Road in the Huangpu district of Shanghai. It was completed in 2011 and covers an area of 35,450 square meters. It contains a mall featuring a variety of stores, restaurants, and entertainment options, catering to different tastes and budgets. Some of the critical tenants include Apple, Nike, Boy London, P.F. Chang's, and Sephora. The eastern façade features a large LED screen displaying advertisements and interactive contents (Denison & Ren, 2013).

The podium design of Henderson Metropolitan is a remarkable fusion of modern architecture, seamlessly blending functionality and aesthetics to create a dynamic and visually appealing space. The four-story structure serves as the foundation for a towering 24-story building housing offices and apartments while accommodating a diverse range of retail and entertainment spaces, such as a cinema, a supermarket, a food court, and a fitness center.

The podium's façade is composed of glass, metal, and wood panels, meticulously arranged to produce a dynamic and elegant appearance. This architectural approach not only contributes to the overall aesthetics of the building but also reflects a contemporary design ethos. The materials used in the façade add layers of texture and visual interest, creating a visually compelling exterior.

The large atrium that spans its interior is a vital feature of the podium. This design element serves a dual purpose by allowing natural light to permeate the space, creating a bright and inviting atmosphere, and providing natural ventilation. The integration of curved canopies undulating surfaces further enhances the sense of openness and connection within the interior, creating a visually stimulating environment for visitors.
