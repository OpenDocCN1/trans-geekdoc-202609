Here are some details of key features and characteristics of Gardens by the Bay (Flannery & Smith, 2015; Leet, 2013):

# SUPERTREES

Gardens by the Bay's Supertrees, soaring 25–50 meters, are a natural and technological marvel, featuring over 200 plant species in a breathtaking vertical garden setting. Their towering structures serve a dual purpose as breathtaking artistic installations and innovative environmental engines. These colossal structures act as rainwater collectors, efficiently harnessing and channeling rainwater to sustain the park's verdant landscapes. Integrating solar photovoltaic cells into their design, the Supertrees contribute to sustainable energy generation, illuminating the gardens with a spectacular display of lights during the evening (Gardens by the Bay).

Beyond their ecological functions, some Supertrees are interconnected by the OCBC Skyway, a 128-meter-long aerial walkway suspended 22 meters above the ground. This skywalk offers visitors an immersive experience, allowing them to traverse amid the towering Supertrees while enjoying panoramic views of the meticulously landscaped gardens below and the dynamic cityscape beyond the horizon. The OCBC Skyway, with its stunning vantage points and structural elegance, offers a unique perspective on the harmonious coexistence of nature and urbanity, making it an integral and enchanting part of the Gardens by the Bay experience. Perched atop the tallest “tree” in the SuperTree Grove, Supertree by IndoChine stands out as the exclusive “SuperTree” featuring dining and bar facilities (Al-Kodmany, 2018; Gardens by the Bay).
