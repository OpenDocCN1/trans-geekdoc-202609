The plaza also has seasonal decorations, such as corn stalks on lampposts in autumn. This area is very popular among visitors, who enjoy using it for various activities, such as chatting, eating, phoning, or relaxing. Next to the flower fountain is a brick path (differentiated from the concrete on the other paths) leading to a standing planter about four-and-a-half-feet tall. The planter has metal rings surrounding its bowl of flowers and contains a kaleidoscope, an excellent piece of interactive public art. To the south of the flower fountain is a wide path leading to an area of benches arranged across each other but staggered so as not to face one other directly (Al-Kodmany, 2017).

The "lawn plaza" contains a variety of sitting spaces, including benches, open grass, and planter edges. One of the most attractive seating options is the Adirondack chairs, which visitors use to lounge, converse, and enjoy the sunshine during lunch. With a sloped seat close to the ground, wide armrests, and a straight back, the Adirondack chair reinforces the perception that this is a garden-like space. Reading, napping, and resting are the most common activities. People place books, snacks, and cellphones on the chairs' wide arms, almost as personal tables. The chairs are unlocked at 8 a.m. every Monday through Friday and put away at 4 p.m.

Given the restaurants inside, the patio is intended as a space for people to eat, relax, and enjoy the incredible views of the plaza and surrounding buildings. Furthermore, the plaza integrates mature trees, particularly along its perimeters, which shield the lawn from the sun, excessive noise, movement, and the general chaos of the street, giving the area a sense of calm. Overall, the plaza offers a variety of seating options, including indoor dining, outdoor seating on the terrace, and a patio with a great view of the plaza and surrounding buildings. Therefore, the plaza is a great place to relax and enjoy the scenery while being protected from the hustle and bustle of the city (Figure 3.16).

The plaza is pristine because of the rigorous regulations forbidding smoking, dogs, rollerblading, skateboarding, alcohol, and loitering. These regulations are upheld by a sophisticated surveillance system and diligent security personnel who constantly watch over the plaza. The rules are displayed on signs at the entrances and on light posts along the pathways. In addition, the plaza is equipped with a substantial number of garbage receptacles, serving the purpose of upholding the cleanliness of the area and fostering an environment that encourages individuals to engage in activities such as sitting and relaxing on the ground. However, this maintenance comes at the cost of being continuously observed and controlled. Therefore, while the plaza is well-kept and tidy, visitors must comply with strict rules and face incessant surveillance and security (Al-Kodmany, 2017; Saliga & Clarke, 1998).
