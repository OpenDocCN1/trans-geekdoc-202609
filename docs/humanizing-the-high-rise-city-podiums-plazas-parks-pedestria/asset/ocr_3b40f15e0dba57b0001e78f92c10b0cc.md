The artwork “Man Riding a Dolphin” is derived from the Greek mythological narrative about the deity Poseidon’s pursuit of the affection of the goddess Amphitrite. Upon observing Amphitrite engaging in recreational activities alongside her sisters, Poseidon became utterly infatuated with her and promptly abducted her. Amphitrite expressed her reluctance to enter matrimony with Poseidon and consequently sought assistance from her father, beseeching him to conceal her. Because of the persuasive nature of Poseidon’s intelligent dolphin, it managed to locate her and successfully captivated her with its persuasive and eloquent communication. Subsequently, the dolphin escorted Poseidon to its sanctuary.
