# STATUE OF IRV KUPCINET, CHICAGO

The outdoor bronze statue of Irv Kupcinet, created by Preston Jackson in 2006, commemorates the influential journalist and writer in Chicago, Illinois. The statue is prominently located on the sidewalk of Wacker Drive near the intersection with Wabash Avenue, providing a significant presence step away from the picturesque Chicago Riverwalk (Figure 14.3).
