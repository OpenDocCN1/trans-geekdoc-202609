+   * **Events Space**: The Sky Garden can also be used as an events space. It has
hosted various events, from corporate functions to private celebrations, tak-
ing advantage of its one-of-a-kind setting.

* **Public Access**: Unlike traditional observation decks that typically charge an
entrance fee, the Sky Garden is unique in that it offers free public access.
Visitors can enjoy food, drink, and party options, wander, and stroll around the
exquisitely landscaped gardens and observation decks (Figure 11.5).

One of the main criticisms is the limited accessibility to the Sky Garden. Entry
is free, but visitors must book tickets in advance, and availability can be limited.
Some argue that this creates exclusivity and prevents spontaneous visits by
locals and tourists. Further, the Sky Garden can often get crowded, especially
during peak hours and tourist seasons (Reike, 2015).

The limited space and high visitor volume can diminish the experience and
make it difficult to appreciate the views and tranquility of the garden entirely.
Also, due to the all-glass envelope, thermal insulation is poor. The garden could
be too hot in summer or too cold in winter. The lack of adequate climate control
systems has been a common complaint among visitors.

Overall, the Sky Garden at 20 Fenchurch Street offers a unique and captivat-
ing experience for locals and tourists alike. It combines stunning architecture,
breathtaking views, and a lush botanical setting to create an urban oasis that
showcases the harmonious integration of nature within a tall building.
