# BOOK OUTLINE: TRAVERSING THE MANUSCRIPT

# Chapter 2: Podiums

Chapter 2 examines how the base of a tall building, encompassing the podium and entrances, influences the perception and experience of the building. The chapter argues that the base is a crucial element in creating a positive and welcoming impression, rather than focusing only on the height and shape of the skyscraper. It examines real cases to identify common pitfalls and successful strategies in designing tall building bases, focusing on creating a human-scaled, inviting entrance for visitors and residents. The passage delves into the design principles and best practices for podiums and entrances, emphasizing the importance of openness, greenery, and varied forms to enhance tall buildings’ architectural appeal and urban integration. It introduces the concept of “sculpted podiums,” which generate visual interest and curiosity. The goal is to make the base of a building a point of arrival, offering a sense of hospitality and setting the tone for the overall building experience. In essence, Chapter 2 offers a valuable guide for crafting podiums and entrances that align with human-scale considerations.
