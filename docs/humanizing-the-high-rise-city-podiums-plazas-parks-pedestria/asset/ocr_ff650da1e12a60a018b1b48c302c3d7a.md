Union Square in San Francisco, a popular urban oasis, features public art, seating, performance, and landscaping elements, enhancing the urban environment with natural beauty and seasonal decorations. It also contains spacious spaces for holding events, bringing in people, and humanizing downtown. *Source: Photographs by author.*
