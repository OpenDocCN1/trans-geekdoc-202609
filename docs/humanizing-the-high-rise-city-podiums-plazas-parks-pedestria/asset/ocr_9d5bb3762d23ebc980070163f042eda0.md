# 8. Sustainability Considerations:

+   • Implement sustainable design and management practices. To manage stormwater runoff, incorporate green infrastructure elements such as rain gardens, biofiltration systems, or green roofs.

• Use environmentally friendly materials and promote the park's waste management and recycling practices.

# 9. Community Programming:

# 10. Maintenance and Management:

+   • Develop a long-term maintenance and management plan to ensure the elevated park's sustainability and vitality.
• Consider partnerships with local community groups, non-profit organizations, or park management authorities for regular maintenance, cleaning, and programming initiatives.
