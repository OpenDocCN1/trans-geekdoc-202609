# Chapter 6: City-Wide Parks

City-wide parks serve as urban oases that provide extensive green spaces, recreational facilities, and cultural amenities, contributing to residents’ overall well-being and quality of life in densely populated urban areas. Chapter 6 explores how city-wide parks enhance the human experience in high-rise cities. These giant green spaces span urban areas and profoundly impact urban life’s social, economic, and environmental aspects. City-wide parks foster social interactions, community bonds, and economic activity by providing shared public spaces, job opportunities, and local business growth by attracting visitors. They function as urban lungs, reduce air pollution and heat island effects, and improve air quality while providing habitat for biodiversity and supporting plant and animal species. The chapter showcases successful city-wide park projects worldwide, analyzing design considerations, management strategies, and community engagement approaches that have transformed urban landscapes and enhanced human experiences. In short, Chapter 6 highlights the role of city-wide parks in promoting holistic urban development in high-rise cities, offering insights for planning, designing, and managing these spaces and emphasizing their importance in creating inclusive, prosperous, and ecologically balanced urban environments.

# Chapter 7: Urban-Rooms Parks

The “urban-room” park concept refers to a design approach in urban planning and landscape architecture where outdoor spaces within a larger urban context are designed to create a sense of enclosure, intimacy, and distinctiveness. Like rooms within a building that provide defined spaces for various activities, “urban rooms” are outdoor spaces that offer a more contained and immersive experience within the broader urban environment. Chapter 7 delves into the concept of urban rooms, which create a sense of enclosure and intimacy within large open spaces and parks while promoting various uses and activities. The chapter showcases Millennium Park in Chicago, which used the urban room concept to create different spaces within its more extensive area. The concept improves these parks’ social, cultural, economic, and environmental outcomes by creating smaller, enclosed spaces that host social interactions and cultural events. The urban room concept also enhances park walkability by reducing perceived distances between different attractions, creating a more pedestrian-friendly environment that motivates visitors to walk and experience the park on foot. This supports active lifestyles and increases the overall accessibility and usability of the parks. As such, the urban-room park concept aims to enhance the quality of urban life by providing diverse outdoor spaces that cater to different activities and moods.
