### Marina Bay Sands, Singapore

Marina Bay Sands is a world-renowned integrated resort in Singapore, designed by architect Moshe Safdie and owned by the Las Vegas Sands Corporation. With over 2,500 rooms and suites, the hotel rooms offer stunning views of the bay. Guests can access various facilities, including a fitness center, spa, swimming pool, and various entertainment options, including the Sands Theatre, Grand Theatre, ArtScience Museum, and The Shoppes at Marina Bay Sands. The casino, one of the largest and most luxurious in the world, offers a wide array of table games, slot machines, and electronic gaming machines. The resort offers extensive dining options (Safdie, 2011; Rowe et al., 2019).

The SkyPark is one of the most remarkable features of Marina Bay Sands. It is a 340-meter-long and 40-meter-wide structure that connects the resort’s three towers at a height of 200 meters. It resembles a giant surfboard resting on the towers, creating a striking contrast with the Singapore skyline. The SkyPark is more than just a visual spectacle. It is also a green sanctuary with landscaped gardens, jogging trails, and an infinity pool that offers breathtaking views of the city. The SkyPark also boasts fine dining and drinking options that cater to different tastes and preferences. It is a versatile venue that can host various events, such as concerts, exhibitions, and private parties. At night, visitors can enjoy the dazzling light and sound shows at Gardens by the Bay, which creates a mesmerizing ambiance (Bourg-Meyer et al., 2018). The resort’s unique architecture, as well as curved and cantilevered SkyPark, create a dramatic visual impact against the Singapore skyline (Figure 11.1).

Here are details of the key features of the SkyPark:

- **Observation Deck:** The observation deck at the SkyPark of Marina Bay Sands is a prominent feature that provides an unparalleled vantage point for guests and visitors to enjoy panoramic views of Singapore’s skyline. Visitors can gaze in every direction to enjoy the diverse and dynamic cityscape, including modern architectural marvels and iconic natural features. The Singapore River is a central element of the city, and from the SkyPark’s observation deck, one can have an unobstructed view of this historic waterway. The river has played a significant role in Singapore’s development and is bordered by a mix of historical and modern structures. Similarly, The Gardens by the Bay, a futuristic and award-winning garden featuring the iconic Supertree Grove, is a prominent element on the skyline (See Chapter 6). Visitors to the observation deck can enjoy an excellent view of this green oasis, which blends nature with cutting-edge design. Consequently, SkyPark’s observation deck is a popular spot for photography enthusiasts (Bourg-Meyer et al., 2018).

- **Restaurants and Bars:** The SkyPark has several upscale restaurants and bars offering various dining options. Guests can enjoy a range of cuisines while taking in the spectacular views from this elevated location. One of the most prominent establishments at SkyPark, CE LA VI offers a sophisticated dining experience with a menu that blends contemporary Asian cuisine with international flavors. Similarly, Sky on 57 is a fine-dining restaurant that combines classic French and Asian influences. The Flight Bar & Lounge is known for its extensive selection of cocktails and beverages. Lavo offers a distinctive dining experience, combining Italian cuisine and a lively atmosphere. The rooftop bar adds extra excitement, making it a popular choice for those looking for a vibrant social setting with views to match. A poolside bar offers refreshing drinks and light snacks for those enjoying exclusive access to the infinity pool. Guests can unwind by the pool while enjoying the cityscape that unfurls before them (Marina Bay Sands).

- **Event Spaces:** Events held at the SkyPark benefit from a sense of exclusivity. The elevated location ensures that attendees are treated to a one-of-a-kind experience, with the city lights and landmarks forming a mesmerizing backdrop that adds a touch of glamour and sophistication. The event spaces at the SkyPark are designed to be versatile, accommodating a variety of occasions such as corporate functions, product launches, weddings, private parties, and more. The flexibility of the spaces allows for creative event planning and customization. The SkyPark is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and technology to support various events (Marina Bay Sands). Whether it’s a corporate presentation, a live performance, or a grand celebration, the infrastructure ensures that events run smoothly and are memorable for attendees (Figure 11.2).

- **Infinity Pool:** The SkyPark at Marina Bay Sands boasts the world’s largest roof-top infinity pool, a remarkable feature that spans an impressive 150 meters in length. Perched on the edge of the three hotel towers, this infinity pool offers a breathtaking and boundless view of the cityscape. The pool’s edge seemingly disappears, creating a visual illusion that extends the water onto the horizon. This design not only adds to the overall beauty of the rooftop oasis but also enhances the sense of openness and connection to the expansive views (Bourg-Meyer et al., 2018). Swimmers at the pool's edge experience a unique sensation of floating above the skyline, creating an unforgettable and immersive experience (Figure 11.3).

The rooftop infinity pool at Marina Bay Sands has become an iconic symbol of luxury, leisure, and architectural innovation. It attracts visitors worldwide, drawn not only to its record-breaking dimensions but also to the unparalleled experience of swimming at such great heights with the stunning backdrop of Singapore’s skyline. The infinity pool's design is visually striking and functional, seamlessly blending with the architectural aesthetics of the SkyPark. The pool’s integration into the SkyPark reflects a harmonious balance between human-made and natural elements, contributing to the overall success of Marina Bay Sands as a world-class destination (Pomeroy, 2011).

- **Landscaped Gardens:** The pool and observation deck are accompanied by exquisitely designed gardens and relaxation spaces. These green spaces provide a tranquil setting amid the urban environment, creating a relaxing atmosphere for visitors. The gardens feature a variety of plant species, adding to the aesthetic appeal and creating a diverse and visually exciting landscape. The selection of plants include native and exotic species, enhancing the overall beauty of the outdoor spaces. Within the landscaped gardens are designated lounging areas where visitors can relax and unwind. These areas are furnished with comfortable seating, allowing guests to enjoy the natural surroundings, socialize, or simply take in the views.
