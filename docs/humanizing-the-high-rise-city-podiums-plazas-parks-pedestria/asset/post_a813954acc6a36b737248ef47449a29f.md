
## Public Plaza

The edge of the ancient Wusong River (now known as the Suzhou River), succumbed to the relentless assault of the river's waves and ultimately collapsed during a flood in 1216. Subsequently, the temple was relocated to the riverside of Bubbling Well Creek, where it currently stands at 1686 Nanjing Road W (Al-Kodmany & Ali, 2013; Al-Kodmany, 2017; Binder, 2015).

In 1972, the temple was reduced to ashes and ruins. Nevertheless, the reconstruction process commenced after 1984, resulting in the successive restoration of the Hall of Heavenly Kings and the Three-Sage Hall. The entire temple was ultimately made accessible to the public in 1990. The existing complex is aesthetically pleasing and creates a noticeable juxtaposition with the cluster of tall buildings that encircle it. The architectural design of Jing'an Temple incorporates Tibetan elements, resulting in a fusion of classical and contemporary styles. At nightfall, the temple is illuminated by a radiant light that imparts a golden hue.

The temple consists of the primary courtyard or a plaza, flanked by two pavilions, the main hall, and the entry hall. The primary entrance to the temple is in the southern section, and the entry hall serves as a passage from the bustling street to the serene courtyard of the holy compound. The plaza's design and landscaping scheme are carefully crafted to reflect the area's rich history, cater to various activities, and create a harmonious balance between tradition and modernity. The plaza's design complements the architectural style of the Jing'an Temple, featuring elements such as traditional Chinese roofs, intricate latticework, and decorative details that align with the temple's visual language. The plaza offers a spacious and open area for visitors to gather, relax, and engage in various cultural and spiritual activities. This open space caters to tourists and locals, making it a versatile public gathering place (Weishan, 2019).

The plaza is adorned with carefully selected plants, flowers, and trees that add greenery and soften the urban environment. The landscaping enhances the sensory experience and creates a soothing atmosphere. The Plaza features cultural installations, sculptures, and art pieces that resonate with the temple's history and spirituality. These elements contribute to the plaza's identity as a cultural destination. Designated areas within the plaza accommodate cultural performances, events, and outdoor exhibitions, fostering a sense of community engagement and artistic expression. Jing'an Temple Plaza serves as a serene urban retreat where visitors can escape the urban hustle, find solace, and connect with the temple's spiritual ambience (Figure 3.22).

The Hongwu Bell is a significant artifact kept within the Jing'an Temple. The bell is a substantial bronze artifact forged in the era of the Ming Dynasty (14th to 17th century) and is thought to have been the possession of Emperor Taizu. The bell was named after him since he was renamed as the Hongwu Emperor. The main hall has a significant Silver Statue of Gautama Buddha, weighing around 15 tons. The god is depicted seated on a pedestal, with the hands in a meditative posture.

## HIGHLIGHTS

### Positive Aspects

- **Cultural Significance**: The plaza centers the historic Jing'an Temple, a sacred site with deep cultural and religious significance. This connection to tradition and spirituality adds depth and cultural value to the space.
- **Harmonious Design:** The plaza's design harmoniously integrates modern aesthetics with traditional Chinese architectural elements, creating a seamless transition between the old and the new.
- **Versatility:** The spacious open area accommodates various activities, from leisurely strolls and contemplation to cultural events, performances, and festivals.
- **Cultural Engagement:** Cultural installations, sculptures, and art pieces within the plaza engage visitors with the area's history, heritage, and artistic expression.
- **Symbolism:** The plaza's layout and design symbolism reflect principles of harmony, balance, and cultural heritage, inviting visitors to connect on a deeper level.
- **Local and Tourist Attraction:** The plaza's dual role as a local gathering place and a tourist attraction contributes to a vibrant and diverse visitor mix.
- **Pedestrian-Friendly:** Pedestrian pathways and safety measures prioritize the comfort and well-being of those exploring the area on foot.
- **Place of Reflection:** The plaza's tranquil environment offers individuals a space for personal reflection, meditation, and mindfulness.

### Areas for Improvement

- **Lack of Shade:** The plaza's open design and limited tree canopy result in a lack of shaded areas, making it uncomfortable for visitors during hot and sunny days.
- **Limited Seating:** Despite its spaciousness, there may be a shortage of seating options, especially during peak hours, which could discourage longer stays and relaxation.
- **Congestion:** At times, the site's popularity might lead to congestion and crowding, diminishing the quality of the visitor experience.
- **Maintenance Challenges:** Keeping the plaza well-maintained and litter-free can be challenging, especially with a high volume of visitors.
- **Accessibility:** Although efforts have been made to ensure accessibility, there might be areas that are less accessible for people with mobility challenges.
- **Lack of Interpretation:** Without adequate signage or interpretation, visitors may not fully understand the historical and cultural significance of the site.
- **Public Restrooms:** Insufficient or inadequate restroom facilities can inconvenience visitors and deter them from spending more time in the plaza.

## BURJ KHALIFA PLAZA, DUBAI

Burj Khalifa Plaza is a prestigious commercial and residential complex in the heart of Dubai, UAE. The plaza consists of several buildings, including the iconic Burj Khalifa, the world's tallest skyscraper with a height of 828 meters (2717 feet) and 163 floors. It offers a variety of amenities and services, such as luxury hotels, restaurants, shopping malls, offices, apartments, and entertainment venues. Some of the attractions in the plaza are the Dubai Mall, the largest shopping mall in the world by total area; the Dubai Fountain, the world's most extensive choreographed fountain system; and the AtThe Top observation deck, one of the highest observation decks in the world, offering stunning panoramic views of the city. Burj Khalifa Plaza symbolizes Dubai's vision and ambition and is a testament to its engineering and architectural excellence. The Burj Khalifa Plaza is easily accessible through various transportation options. The Dubai Metro provides a direct connection to the plaza, and ample parking facilities are available for those arriving by car. Pedestrian pathways and moving walkways and bridges facilitate seamless movement to and from the plaza and surrounding areas.

The plaza seamlessly integrates with the impressive architecture of the Burj Khalifa, mirroring the sleek, and contemporary design inspired by Islamic motifs. Embracing a design aesthetic of grandeur, elegance, and modernity, the plaza boasts expansive paved areas, wide pedestrian walkways, and meticulously curated landscaping. Its layout facilitates easy navigation, making it accommodating for large crowds.

The Burj Khalifa Plaza showcases several art installations and sculptures, further enhancing its artistic ambiance. These art pieces contribute to the aesthetic value of the plaza and create focal points for visitors to appreciate. The Burj Khalifa Plaza is a venue for various cultural events and entertainment activities throughout the year. These can include music performances, art exhibitions, and festivals. The plaza's open space and state-of-the-art infrastructure make it an ideal setting for hosting such events and attracting a diverse audience.

The Dubai Fountain was designed by the team responsible for the famous Fountains of Bellagio in Las Vegas. It spans over 900 feet (275 meters) in length and is located on the artificial Burj Khalifa Lake. It has a total of 6,600 lights and 50 colored projectors, which create a stunning visual display. The fountain system consists of powerful water jets that shoot up to 500 feet (152 meters) in the air, accompanied by synchronized music and lights. The fountain has high-pressure water nozzles that can create various water formations and patterns. The Dubai Fountain performances are choreographed to various music, including Arabic, classical, contemporary, and world music. The water movements are synchronized with the music and are meticulously choreographed to create a captivating spectacle (Katrotydis & Mitchell, 2015).

The Dubai Fountain offers daily performances, usually every 30 minutes in the evenings. The performances commence at dusk and last until the late hours of the night. Visitors can enjoy the shows in several prime locations around the Dubai Fountain. The waterfront promenade of the Dubai Mall offers excellent vantage points. Additionally, restaurants and cafes with outdoor seating provide an up-close experience of the fountain performances. Visitors can find a spot along the waterfront or grab a table at a nearby restaurant to enjoy the show. The Dubai Fountain is a true marvel of engineering and entertainment, combining water, music, and light in a spectacular display. Its performances attract millions of visitors yearly, becoming an iconic symbol of Dubai's modernity and grandeur.
