# *Sky Ring and Elevated Walkways, Lujiazui, Shanghai*

Lujiazui, situated in the Pudong district of Shanghai, is a glaring symbol of the city's rapid transformation into a global financial and trade hub. This iconic financial district, often referred to as the “New Bund,” boasts a stunning skyline adorned with some of the world's tallest and most futuristic skyscrapers. At the forefront of Lujiazui's architectural ensemble is the Shanghai Tower, a remarkable feat of engineering, along with the Jin Mao Tower and the Shanghai World Financial Center (Al-Kodmany & Ali, 2013).

These structures define the cityscape, creating a modern and dynamic silhouette against the Huangpu River. Lujiazui is a business powerhouse and a cultural and recreational destination, featuring the Lujiazui Central Green Space and the Oriental Pearl Tower (See Chapter 5). Lujiazui continues to captivate visitors with its towering landmarks and vibrant city life as a testament to Shanghai's economic progress and urban sophistication.
