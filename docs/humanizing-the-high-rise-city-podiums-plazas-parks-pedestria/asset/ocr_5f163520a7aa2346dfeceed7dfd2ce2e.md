# Chapter 12: Greenery-Covered Tall Buildings

“Greenery-covered tall buildings” refer to skyscrapers and high-rise structures that incorporate extensive vegetation, plants, and green elements into their design, mainly in their exteriors. This concept goes beyond traditional architecture by integrating nature directly into the building’s facade, walls, balconies, and even rooftops. Greenery-covered tall buildings manifest the biophilic design trend, where nature is intentionally integrated into the built environment to improve human well-being and enhance the urban landscape. Chapter 12 argues that greenery-covered tall buildings are vital in humanizing high-rise cities by infusing nature, beauty, and environmental consciousness into the urban fabric. These innovative structures address the challenges of urban density and vertical growth while offering a range of benefits that enhance the quality of life for residents and visitors. Amid the concrete and steel of high-rise cities, greenery-covered buildings provide a direct and tangible connection to nature. The presence of plants, trees, and green spaces helps residents maintain a connection with the natural world, promoting well-being and reducing feelings of urban isolation. The chapter showcases pioneering examples of greenery-covered tall buildings, highlighting innovative designs and techniques for incorporating vegetation into the buildings’ exteriors and interiors. However, it acknowledges the challenges and downsides of greenery-covered tall buildings, such as maintenance costs and structural issues. Despite these challenges, greenery-covered tall buildings represent a step towards creating healthier, more sustainable high-rise cities. In summary, Chapter 12 highlights how greenery-covered tall buildings transform high-rise cities into livable, vibrant, and harmonious environments, enhancing well-being and promoting sustainable urban experiences.

# Chapter 13: Pedestrian Networks, Promenades, and Streetscapes

Chapter 13 discusses the role of pedestrian networks, promenades, and streetscapes in enhancing the quality of life in high-rise cities. These urban elements are vital for creating vibrant, accessible, livable urban environments. They facilitate social interaction, physical activity, environmental sustainability, and a strong urban identity. Pedestrian networks link different parts of the city and encourage walking and cycling as alternative modes of transportation. They increase walkability, connectivity, and health outcomes for residents and visitors. Promenades provide a variety of spaces for leisure, recreation, and cultural events, fostering relaxation, socialization, and engagement with the urban fabric. Streetscapes and streets’ design and aesthetics significantly impact the urban environment, enhancing the city’s attractiveness, creating a sense of place and identity, and improving livability. The chapter discusses the positive impacts of these elements, including increased physical activity, reduced congestion, improved accessibility, community-building through pedestrian-friendly environments, and safety measures like well-lit pathways and clear signage. In conclusion, Chapter 13 underscores the significance of pedestrian networks, promenades, and streetscapes in high-rise cities.
