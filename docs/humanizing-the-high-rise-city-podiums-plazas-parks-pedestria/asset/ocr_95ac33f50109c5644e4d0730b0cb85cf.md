+   3. **Encouraging Social Interaction:** Public art provides social interaction and community engagement spaces. Installations such as sculptures, interactive artworks, or performance spaces create opportunities for people to gather, connect, and share experiences. They catalyze conversation, bringing people together from different backgrounds and fostering a sense of community.

+   4. **Stimulating Cultural Expression:** Various forms of cultural expression are showcased and celebrated through public art. A city's history, traditions, and tales can be reflected in it, which can promote cultural variety and inclusivity. Artists in the community can find a voice and an audience through public art projects like murals, performances, or installations, which can enrich the city's cultural landscape.

+   5. **Activating Public Spaces:** Public art activates public spaces and revitalizes underutilized areas by creating a sense of place, transforming empty plazas, vacant walls, or neglected spaces, and inviting people to engage with their surroundings. It encourages people to spend time outdoors, promotes walkability, and activates areas that may go unnoticed.

+   6. **Provoking Thought and Dialogue:** Prompting thinking and conversation, public art has the power to disprove assumptions. Public discourse and understanding are sparked by artistic works that tackle political, social, or environmental concerns. A more involved and educated populace can result from their ability to stimulate critical thinking, increase empathy, and dispel myths.

+   7. **Enhancing Well-being and Quality of Life:** Public art can uplift spirits, inspire creativity, and improve the well-being of individuals in a dense urban environment. It offers respite, contemplation, and inspiration amid the hustle and bustle of city life. Public art also contributes to a sense of pride and ownership in the community, fostering a positive urban experience.

The following examples illustrate how public art can help humanize the high-rise city.
