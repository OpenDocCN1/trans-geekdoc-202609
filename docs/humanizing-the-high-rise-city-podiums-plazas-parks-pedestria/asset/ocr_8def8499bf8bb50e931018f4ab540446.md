Conservancy, a private, non-profit organization, oversees the management and maintenance of the park through a contractual agreement with the New York City Department of Parks and Recreation (Slavecek, 2009; Sutton & Anderson, 2016).

Central Park, a serene green oasis in Manhattan, offers residents and visitors a tranquil space to connect with nature, relax, and recharge, offering a much-needed escape from the densely built environment. The park is home to various attractions, including landscapes such as the Ramble and Lake, Hallett Nature Sanctuary, the Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis Reservoir, and Sheep Meadow; amusement attractions, including the Central Park Zoo, Central Park Carousel, and Wollman Rink; and formal spaces such as the Central Park Mall and Bethesda Terrace. Central Park offers recreational activities such as walking paths, bike lanes, sports fields, playgrounds, baseball and soccer fields, carousel, skating rinks, formal gardens, commemorative monuments, and concert and theater venues, contributing to the well-being and quality of life of its residents by promoting a healthier lifestyle and mitigating the negative impacts of sedentary urban living (Slavecek, 2009; Sutton & Anderson, 2016). Here are some of the park’s key elements (Central Park):

+   1.  **Great Lawn:** The Great Lawn, a 55-acre green pasture situated at the heart of Central Park, is renowned as one of the world’s most famous lawns and an excellent spot for leisure and picnicking.

+   2.  **Cherry Hill:** The Hill derives its name from the exquisite cherry blossom trees that flourish there in spring, but it is primarily renowned for its impressive 14-foot fountain.

+   3.  **Conservatory Garden:** Situated at the 5th Avenue and 105th Street intersection, Conservatory Garden is Central Park’s sole formal garden.

+   4.  **Conservatory Water:** Inspired by the model boat ponds of late 19th-century Paris, it offers children and adults the opportunity to enjoy the delight of sailing model boats.

+   5.  **Cherry Blossom Trees:** From April to May, the Kwanzan and Yoshino Cherry trees simultaneously blossom in Central Park.

+   6.  **Bethesda Terrace Arcade:** It is a vaulted indoor pathway located in the park’s heart, connecting the Mall to Bethesda Fountain and Central Park Lake.

+   7.  **Bethesda Terrace:** Regarded as an impressive architectural masterpiece, the Bethesda Terrace is situated at the 72nd Street and Cross Drive intersection, offering stunning panoramic views of the Central Park Lake and trees.

+   8.  **Bethesda Fountain:** Standing as one of New York’s largest fountains, Bethesda Fountain offers an ideal setting for people-watching, socializing with friends, or simply unwinding amidst the breathtaking vistas.

+   9.  **Belvedere Castle:** Derived from the Italian term “beautiful view,” Belvedere Castle offers park visitors sweeping vistas that encompass several of Central Park’s most exquisite landmarks.
