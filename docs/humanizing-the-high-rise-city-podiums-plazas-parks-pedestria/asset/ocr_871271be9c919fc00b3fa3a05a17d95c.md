# ■ Public Plazas

The Burj Khalifa Plaza is beautifully illuminated at night, creating a captivating spectacle. The plaza and the Burj Khalifa are adorned with extensive lighting systems that highlight their architectural features, creating a mesmerizing visual experience for visitors. Overall, the Burj Khalifa Plaza is an appealing public space that complements the magnificence of the Burj Khalifa. With its striking design, open layout, lush landscaping, retail and dining options, and cultural events, the plaza offers a multifaceted experience for visitors, making it an attractive destination (Figure 3.23).
