Petronas Towers Park in Kuala Lumpur is a green oasis adjacent to the Petronas Twin Towers. It offers recreational activities, cultural events, and festivals and is designed for accessibility. It complements the towers and enhances the city's livability, providing a welcoming environment for all. **Source:** Photographs by author.

# Areas for Improvement

+   - **Scale and Intimacy:** The park’s large scale and open design could lead to a lack of intimate and cozy relaxing spaces.
- **Greenery and Shade:** While the park features greenery, there's room for improvement by planting more trees and providing shaded areas. This would offer a respite from the tropical heat and provide a more comfortable environment.
- **Connectivity:** Improving pedestrian pathways and connections within the park can enhance accessibility and create a more seamless and user-friendly experience.
