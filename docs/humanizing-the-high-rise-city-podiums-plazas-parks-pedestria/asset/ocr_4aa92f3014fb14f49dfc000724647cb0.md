+   **8. Maintenance and Management:**
* Develop a maintenance and management plan to ensure the long-term sustainability and attractiveness of the sky park or garden.
* Consider the required maintenance tasks, including pruning, irrigation, and regular inspections.
* Collaborate with building management or external partners to establish ongoing maintenance responsibilities, schedules, and resources.

+   **9. Safety and Security:**
* Prioritize safety and security in the sky park or garden design. Implement appropriate lighting, signage, and wayfinding elements.
* Consider the installation of safety barriers or railings where necessary.
* Collaborate with building management and security teams to establish protocols and ensure the safety of users.

+   **10. Community Engagement and Programming:**
* Engage with building occupants and the surrounding community to foster a sense of ownership and encourage sky park or garden usage.
* Develop programming initiatives such as fitness classes, workshops, or cultural events to activate the space and promote social interaction.

By considering these factors and engaging with relevant experts and stakeholders, the planning and design of sky parks and gardens in tall buildings can create stunning and functional green spaces that enhance the well-being of occupants, promote sustainability, and contribute to the overall livability of urban environments.
