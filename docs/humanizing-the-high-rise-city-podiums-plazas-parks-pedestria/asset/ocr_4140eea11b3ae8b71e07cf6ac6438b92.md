14.13 Wu Ching-ju's "Terra Natura," a 15-meter copper sculpture in Shanghai, features 34 tons of faceless fairies gracefully hovering above the earth, symbolizing nature's purity and delicacy. *Source:* Photograph by author.

![](bbox=[0.12, 0.43, 0.74, 0.72])

14.14 Chen Yifei's bronze masterpiece, "The Tourists Sculpture," captures a captivating tableau in Lujiazui, Shanghai. In this evocative artwork, a group of individuals enthusiastically photographs the towering buildings, eloquently reflecting the city's dynamic interplay between traditional and modern elements. Crafted with meticulous detail, the sculpture freezes a moment in time and serves as a poignant commentary on the juxtaposition of Shanghai's rich heritage and rapid contemporary evolution. *Source:* Photograph by author.
