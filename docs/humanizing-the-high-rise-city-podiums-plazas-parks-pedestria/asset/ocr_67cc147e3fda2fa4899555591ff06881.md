East Nanjing Road buzzes with activity throughout the day. It is always filled with a lively atmosphere, bustling crowds, street performers, and vendors selling various street food and snacks. The vibrant ambiance creates a dynamic and energetic experience for visitors. It is a popular destination for festive celebrations, especially during holidays like Chinese New Year and Christmas. The street is adorned with festive decorations, dazzling lights, and themed displays, adding a touch of excitement and joy to the atmosphere (Yang & Xu, 2009). East Nanjing Road is a blend of history, commerce, and urban charm, making it a captivating destination attracting millions of visitors yearly (Figure 4.6).

Every morning, the vibrant scene on East Nanjing Road unfolds as a microcosm of Shanghai's diverse and dynamic culture. As dawn breaks, individuals from various walks of life converge on this iconic street, transforming it into a lively hub of communal activities. Amid the soothing ambiance, groups of people partake in ancient Chinese practices like tai chi and yoga; their collective movements create a serene symphony against the urban backdrop. The street becomes a canvas for displays of traditional Chinese martial arts and modern forms of physical activity. Enthusiasts showcase their progress in boxing or martial arts, blending the old and the new in a captivating demonstration of strength and skills (Figure 4.7).

Meanwhile, the air is filled with the rhythmic beats and harmonious melodies of those who choose to dance. In the enchanting morning of East Nanjing Road, a charming tradition unfolds as older residents gracefully engage in dance. These individuals find joy and connection through their favorite traditional Chinese music in this lively and communal setting.
