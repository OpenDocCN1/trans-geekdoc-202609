# PEOPLE’S PARK, SHANGHAI

People’s Park is a renowned public park in the heart of Shanghai, China. It is situated in the Huangpu District, adjacent to Nanjing Road, one of Shanghai’s most famous shopping streets (See Chapter 4). It spans Middle Xizang Road in the east to North Huangpi Road in the west and stretches from West Nanjing Road in the north to People’s Square in the south. The park has eight entrances, with the primary entrance on West Nanjing Road. Covering an area of approximately 100,000 square meters (25 acres); it is an important part of the downtown and is known for its beautiful green spaces, serene atmosphere, and various recreational activities, catering to visitors of all ages. Established in 1952, it holds significant historical and cultural value for the residents of Shanghai. It is a popular gathering place for locals, tourists, and families, offering many recreational activities and relaxing spaces (Wang et al., 2016).

People’s Park encompasses humanizing aspects that enhance visitors’ experience. It serves as a vibrant community gathering place, attracting people from all walks of life. The park offers ample open spaces, plazas, and seating areas, encouraging social interactions, community engagement, and a sense of belonging. It includes walking paths, jogging tracks, and designated areas for tai chi, dancing, and board games. These amenities promote active lifestyles, physical well-being, and opportunities for leisurely enjoyment. The park often hosts art exhibitions, performances, and cultural events, including music concerts, dance shows, and traditional performances.
