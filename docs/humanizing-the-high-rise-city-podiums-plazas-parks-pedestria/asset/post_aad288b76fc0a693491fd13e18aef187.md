## OASIA DOWNTOWN, SINGAPORE

The Oasia Hotel Downtown is a lush tower of green in the middle of Singapore's congested Central Business District (CBD). It serves as a model for land use intensification in tropical cities. This “living tower” presents an alternative to the sleek, sealed skyscrapers that sprang out of the West. Oasia Downtown is a 27-story (190 meter) office building completed in 2016. It was designed by WOHA Architects (Boby et al., 2019; Generalova et al., 2017).

### Architectural Design

Oasia Downtown features a striking red aluminum mesh façade that spans the entire height of the building. This “vegetative exoskeleton” serves multiple purposes: a visual signature, an air filter, and a sunshade system. The building's design embraces a flowing and organic form, with curves and contours that break away from traditional rectangular structures. This design approach creates a sense of movement and fluidity, enhancing the overall aesthetics. Oasia Downtown incorporates several sky gardens distributed across various levels of the building. These lush green spaces are designed as open-air retreats for occupants, providing opportunities for relaxation, socializing, and connection with nature. At the top of the building is a spacious rooftop terrace offering panoramic views of the city skyline. This elevated outdoor space serves as a gathering area and allows occupants to enjoy the surrounding greenery and urban vistas (Boby et al., 2019; Generalova et al., 2017).

### Plant and Tree Integration

Oasia Downtown is known for its extensive vertical greenery that covers a significant portion of the building's façade. The red aluminum mesh is a framework for climbing plants, allowing vegetation to grow and thrive. The building's facade incorporates specially designed planting niches accommodating diverse plant species. These niches provide a conducive environment for plants to flourish and contribute to the building's greenery. The sky gardens within Oasia Downtown feature a variety of plants, including trees, shrubs, flowering plants, and grasses. The vegetation is strategically placed to create a layered and visually pleasing landscape while providing shade, improving air quality, and reducing the building's carbon footprint. Further, integrating plants and trees in Oasia Downtown promotes natural ventilation by allowing air to flow through the building, reducing the reliance on mechanical cooling systems. The greenery helps to cool the surrounding areas and enhances thermal comfort for occupants (Figure 12.3).

A comprehensive maintenance plan supports the plant integration in Oasia Downtown. Regular care and upkeep, including watering, pruning, and monitoring for plant health, are carried out to ensure the longevity and vitality of the greenery. The building also employs sustainable practices such as rainwater harvesting and efficient irrigation systems to minimize water usage (Boby et al., 2019; Generalova et al., 2017).

### Awards and Recognition

The Oasia Downtown building has received outstanding awards for its innovative and green design. Some of them are:

- The 2016 Skyrise Greenery Award
- The 2016 CTBUH Urban Habitat Award
- The 2018 FIABCI Singapore Property Award

In general, the architectural design and incorporation of plants in Oasia Downtown result in a unique and environmentally conscious building that is prominent in Singapore's urban environment. The fusion of organic forms, vertical greenery, and sky gardens enhances the building's aesthetic appeal and promotes a sustainable and refreshing environment for occupants and the surrounding community.

## PARKROYAL COLLECTION PICKERING, SINGAPORE

The PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering, Singapore, is a remarkable example of architectural design and plant and tree integration in an urban setting. The hotel, designed by WOHA Architects, features a series of sky gardens cantilevered at every fourth level between the blocks of guest rooms. These gardens are filled with tropical plants and trees, creating a lush green landscape that contrasts with the concrete and glass facade of the building. The hotel also incorporates green walls, water features, and solar cells to enhance its environmental performance and aesthetic identity. The hotel’s design aims to create a “hotel-in-a-garden” that doubles the green-growing potential of its site and connects with the adjacent Hong Lim Park and the historic shophouse district. The PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering, Singapore, is an outstanding achievement of sustainable and green architecture in the city’s heart (Al-Kodmany, 2023; Hudson, 2020).

### Architectural Design

The most distinctive feature of PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering is its terraced garden architecture. The building comprises a series of stepped terraces that form lush green spaces at different levels. This design creates an impressive visual impact and offers a seamless connection between nature and the built environment. The hotel's architectural style is contemporary and sleek, characterized by clean lines, geometric shapes, and a blend of glass, steel, and concrete elements. The design harmoniously combines modern aesthetics with the natural surroundings. The building features corridors that wind through the terraced gardens, allowing guests to stroll amid the greenery and enjoy the scenic views. These passages create an immersive experience, allowing guests to engage with nature throughout the hotel (Hudson, 2020).

### Plant and Tree Integration

PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering showcases a remarkable integration of plants and trees throughout its design. More than 15,000 square meters of greenery envelop the hotel, creating a unique and immersive experience for guests. The hotel boasts over 50 species of plants, including ferns, creepers, and flowering climbers. These plants were carefully selected for their ability to thrive in a tropical climate and create a visually captivating and sustainable environment.

The hotel features sky gardens on various floors, offering supplementary verdant areas for guests to repose and rejuvenate. The gardens include meticulously designed landscaping, incorporating a diverse array of plant species, seating places, and water elements, all contributing to the overall atmosphere. The building’s terraced shape facilitates the incorporation of flowing waterfalls that traverse the green areas. The cascades provide a serene and natural element, amplifying the cooling effect and fostering tranquility.

Integrating plants and trees in PARKROYAL COLLECTION Pickering promotes natural ventilation throughout the hotel. The greenery helps filter the air, reduces heat, and improves overall indoor air quality, providing guests with a refreshing and comfortable environment. The hotel follows sustainable practices in maintaining its green spaces. Rainwater is collected and used for irrigation, minimizing water usage (Al-Kodmany, 2023; Hudson, 2020). The selection of plant species includes native and low-maintenance varieties, reducing the need for excessive watering and maintenance (Figures 12.4 and 12.5).
