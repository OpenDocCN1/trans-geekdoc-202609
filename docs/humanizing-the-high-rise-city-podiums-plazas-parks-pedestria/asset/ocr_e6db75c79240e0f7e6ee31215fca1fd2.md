# Sky Parks, Gardens, and Green Walls

+   * **Air Purification**: Plants in green walls help improve air quality by absorbing pollutants and releasing oxygen, contributing to a healthier indoor environment.
* **Sound Insulation**: The foliage of green walls can serve as a sound barrier, absorbing and diffusing noise and improving acoustic conditions.
* **Biophilic Connection**: Greenery promotes a connection to nature, linked to improved well-being and reduced stress.
* **Thermal Regulation**: Green walls can regulate indoor temperatures by providing insulation and reducing heat absorption.
* **Space Optimization**: Green walls are ideal for areas with limited horizontal space, allowing the integration of greenery in urban environments.
* **Branding and Identity**: Incorporating green walls can reflect a commitment to sustainability and environmental consciousness.
* **Biodiversity**: Depending on plant selection, green walls can support biodiversity by providing habitats for insects and tiny organisms.

Green walls can be found in various settings, including commercial buildings, offices, hotels, shopping centers, and public spaces, contributing to a harmonious blend of nature and architecture. See the example of the Shanghai Tower’s indoor spaces (Figure 11.7).
