# WRIGLEY SQUARE

Wrigley Square, situated northwest of Millennium Park, houses the Millennium 
Monument, symbolizing architectural history and philanthropy. Its circular col- 
nade pays homage to an earlier structure that once adorned Grant Park, invoking 
a sense of nostalgia through its Classical architectural style. The columns create 
a timeless ambiance reminiscent of ancient structures, establishing a connection 
to the past. Notably, the names of the generous donors who played a pivotal 
role in making Millennium Park a reality are inscribed on the colonnade, a lasting 
acknowledgment of their contributions.

At the heart of the monument, a tranquil fountain beckons visitors, provid- 
ing a serene space for relaxation and contemplation. Also, a large lawn before 
the monument adds to the scene's qualities of grandeur, elegance, and spa- 
ciousness (Figure 7.5). This calm and elegant setting starkly contrasts with the 
adjacent lively and famous Crown Fountain, offering a diverse range of experi- 
ces within the dynamic urban landscape of Millennium Park. The Millennium 
Monument becomes a focal point where history, architecture, and philanthropy 
converge to create a space of beauty and reflection within the bustling urban 
environment (Gilfoyle, 2006).
