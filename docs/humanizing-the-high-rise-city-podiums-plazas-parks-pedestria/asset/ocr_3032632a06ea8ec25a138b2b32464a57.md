| 6 | 10th Avenue Square | At 17th St. | The planting beds south of 10th Avenue Square display a variety of spring bulbs and moor grasses. |  
| 7 | Chelsea Grasslands | Between 18th St. and 19th St. | It is a winding trail with prairie grasses and perennials, showcasing Piet Oudolf’s appreciation for their visual qualities. It integrates salvias, coneflowers, black-eyed Susan, switchgrass, bluestem, Culver’s root, and wild bergamot. |  
| 8 | Chelsea Thicket | Between 21st St. and 22nd St. | The densely planted section evokes a woodland atmosphere with shrubs, trees, and wooden benches. It offers a noticeable, intimate, and shaded space for visitors to explore. |  
| 9 | 23rd Street Lawn and Seating Steps | At 23rd St. | It provides ample open space for recreational activities, picnics, and sunbathing. Adjacent to the lawn, the Seating Steps consist of tiered, amphitheater-like seating areas inviting visitors to sit, relax, and enjoy panoramic views of the cityscape. |  
| 10 | Meadow Walk | Between 23rd St. and 25th St. | A visual trail that features art galleries, warehouses, and buildings, with sun-loving perennials like tickseed, fern leaf yarrow, catmint, and thick Korean feather reed grass plantings. |  
| 11 | Flyover | Between 25th St. and 27th St. | The Flyover ascends slightly from the High Line, passing through a grove of serviceberry, sassafras, and magnolia trees. Historic warehouses on both sides provide shade, promoting the growth of fragile fern species and other forest groundcovers. A viewing spur, reminiscent of ads on the High Line, is located on the east side of the Flyover at 26th Street. |  
| 12 | Wildflower Field | Between 28th St. and 30th St. | It is a two-block path through a landscape of warm-season grasses, wildflowers, and perennials, tracing the pathway’s bend westward to the 30th Street Cut-Out of the High Line and a tiny forest. |
