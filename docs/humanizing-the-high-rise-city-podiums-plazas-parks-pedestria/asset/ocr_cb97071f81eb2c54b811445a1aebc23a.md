The rooftop infinity pool at Marina Bay Sands has become an iconic symbol of luxury, leisure, and architectural innovation. It attracts visitors worldwide, drawn not only to its record-breaking dimensions but also to the unparalleled experience of swimming at such great heights with the stunning backdrop of Singapore’s skyline. The infinity pool's design is visually striking and functional, seamlessly blending with the architectural aesthetics of the SkyPark. The pool’s integration into the SkyPark reflects a harmonious balance between human-made and natural elements, contributing to the overall success of Marina Bay Sands as a world-class destination (Pomeroy, 2011).

**Landscaped Gardens:** The pool and observation deck are accompanied by exquisitely designed gardens and relaxation spaces. These green spaces provide a tranquil setting amid the urban environment, creating a relaxing atmosphere for visitors. The gardens feature a variety of plant species, adding to the aesthetic appeal and creating a diverse and visually exciting landscape.

The selection of plants include native and exotic species, enhancing the overall beauty of the outdoor spaces. Within the landscaped gardens are designated lounging areas where visitors can relax and unwind. These areas are furnished with comfortable seating, allowing guests to enjoy the natural surroundings, socialize, or simply take in the views.
