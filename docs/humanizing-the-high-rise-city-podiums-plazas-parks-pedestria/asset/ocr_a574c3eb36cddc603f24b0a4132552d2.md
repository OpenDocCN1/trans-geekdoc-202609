# KEY TAKEAWAYS: PLANNING AND DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Public parks can be crucial in humanizing high-rise cities by providing essential elements of nature, community, and recreation. Here are some guiding design principles that help to achieve that goal:

+   1. **Accessibility and Inclusivity:**
• Ensure that parks are accessible to people of all ages, abilities, and backgrounds.
• Incorporate ramps, elevators, and accessible pathways for equal enjoyment and participation.
• Provide inclusive amenities such as seating, picnic areas, and playgrounds that cater to diverse needs.

+   2. **Greenery and Natural Elements:**
• Integrate abundant greenery, trees, and plants within parks to create a sense of tranquility, connection with nature, and improved air quality.
• Design lush gardens, walking paths, and green corridors that offer a respite from the surrounding urban environment.

+   3. **Varied Spaces and Activities:**
• Design parks with active and passive spaces to cater to various recreational activities and preferences.
