“Login denied. Please contact your Citizens Advice Bureau!” her watch informed her.

A feeling of nausea hit her like a fist to the stomach. She suspected what had happened. To

There was only one possible explanation: The Civic Trust Score dropped. Substantially.
A small drop meant minor inconveniences, such as not getting first flights or having to
wait a little longer for official documents. A low trust score was rare and meant that you
were classified as a threat to society. In dealing with these people was to keep
them away from public places such as the subway. The government restricted the financial
transactions of subjects with low Civic Trust Scores. They also began to monitor
y behavior on social media and even as far as to restrict content, such as
violent games. It became exponentially more difficult to increase your Civic Trust Score
lower it was. People with a very low score never recovered.

She could not think of reason why her score should have fallen. The score was based
on machine learning. The Civic Trust Score System worked like a well-oiled engine that ran
society. The performance of the Trust Score System was always closely monitored. Machine
learning had become much better since the beginning of the century. It had become so efficient
decisions made by the Trust System could never be disputed. An infallible
system.

She laughed in despair. Infallible system. If only. The system has rarely failed. But it
failed. She must be one of those special cases; an error of the system: from now on an outcast.
Nobody dared to question the system. It was too integrated into the government, into society\itself, to be. In the few remaining democratic countries it was forbidden to form
anti-democratic movements, not because where they were inherently malicious, but because they
would destabilize the current system. The same logic applied to the new more common
allocations. Critique in the algorithms was forbidden by the danger to the status
quo.

Algorithmic trust was the fabric of the social order. For the common good, rare false trust
scorings were tacitly accepted. Hundreds of other prediction systems and databases fed into
t score, making it impossible to know what caused the drop in her score. She felt like a big
dark hole was opening and under her. With horror she looked into the void.

Her tax affinity system was eventually integrated into the Civic Trust Score System, but she
never got to know it.
