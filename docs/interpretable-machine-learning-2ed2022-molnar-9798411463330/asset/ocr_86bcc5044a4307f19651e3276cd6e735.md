# 8.2 LOCAL SURROGATE (LIME)

Another really big problem is the instability of the explanations. In an article¹10 the authors showed that the explanations of two very close points varied greatly in a simulated setting. Also, in my experience, if you repeat the sampling process, then the explanations that come out can be different. Instability means that it is difficult to trust the explanations, and you should be very critical.

LIME explanations can be manipulated by the data scientist to hide biases¹11. The possibility of manipulation makes it more difficult to trust explanations generated with LIME.

Conclusion: Local surrogate models, with LIME as a concrete implementation, are very promising. But the method is still in development phase and many problems need to be solved before it can be safely applied.
