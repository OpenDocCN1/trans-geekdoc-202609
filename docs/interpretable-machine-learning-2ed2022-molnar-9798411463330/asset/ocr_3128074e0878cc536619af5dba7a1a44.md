# 9.1.4 Disadvantages

Many feature visualization images are not interpretable at all, but contain some
abstract features for which we have no words or mental concept. The display of feature
visualizations along with training data can help. The images still not reveal what the
eural network reacted to and only stating like "maybe there has to be yellow in the
images". For example, conv5_3 from VGG from ImageNet has 193 channels (out of 512)
that could not be matched with a human concept.

There are too many units to look at, even when "only" visualizing the activation channels.
for the Inception V1 architecture, there are already over 5000 channels from nine
convolutional layers. If you also want to show the negative activations plus a few from
training data that maximally or minimally activate the channel (let's say four, four
negative images), then you must already display more than 50 000 images. At least we
th to Network Dissection — that we do not investigate random directions.

Illusion of interpretability? The feature visualizations can convey the illusion that
understand what the neural network is doing. But do we really understand what is going on
in the neural network? Even if we look at hundreds or thousands of feature visualizations, we
cander the neural network. The channels interact in a complex way, positive and
negative activations are unrelated, multiple neurons learn features features do not have equivalent human concepts. We must not fall into thentrap of believing we fully understand neural networks because we believe that
nuron 39 in layer 7 is activated by daisies. Network Dissection showed that architectures
ResNet or inception have units that react to certain concepts. But the IoU is not
great and many units respond to the same concept and to no concept at all. The
channels are not completely disentangled and cannot interpret them.

For Network Dissection, you need datasets that are labeled on the pixel level with
the concepts. These datasets take a lot of effort to collect, since each pixel needs to be labeled,
which usually works by drawing segments over objects on the image.

Network Dissection only aligns human concepts with positive activations but not negative
activations seem to be linked to concepts. This might be fixed by looking at the lower quantile of
activations.
