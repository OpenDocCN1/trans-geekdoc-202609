But this model-based approach of "variance-explained" (also called ANOVA, which stands for Analysis Of Variance) works even if the other feature is categorical. The "variance-explained" lies always between 0 (no association) and 1 (temperature can be perfectly predicted from the other feature). We calculate the explained variance of temperature, humidity and wind speed with all the other features. The higher the explained variance (correlation), the more (potential) problems with plots.
