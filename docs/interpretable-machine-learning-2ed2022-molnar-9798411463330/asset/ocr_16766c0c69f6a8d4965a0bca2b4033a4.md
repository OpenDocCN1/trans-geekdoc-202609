The good side is that the additivity isolates the interpretation of an individual feature from all other features. That is possible because all the feature effects (= weight times value) in the equation are combined with a plus. On the bad side of things, the interpretation ignores the joint distribution of the features. Increasing one feature, but not changing another, can lead to unrealistic or at least unlikely data points. For increasing the number of rooms might be unrealistic without also increasing the size of a house.
