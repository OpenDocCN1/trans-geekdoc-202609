Agentic AI and Autonomous Threat Loops  

As artificial intelligence (AI) systems transition from passive assistants to autonomous agents, the complexity of their threat landscape expands significantly. Agentic AI introduces persistent memory, autonomous planning, dynamic tool integration, and continuous interaction with external environments—each of which represents a critical departure from the behavior of traditional predictive or generative models. These capabilities, while powerful, carry stateful vulnerabilities and deeply nested attack surfaces that defy conventional security assumptions. Understanding how attackers can manipulate agentic reasoning loops, environmental dependencies, and persistent context is essential to maintaining control over autonomous decision-making systems.
