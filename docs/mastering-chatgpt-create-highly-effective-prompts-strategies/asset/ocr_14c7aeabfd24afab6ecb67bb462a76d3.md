# **ChatGPT Explained**

ChatGPT, the invention of GPT, is a revolutionary, new technology as well as an AI-driven language model that uses artificial intelligence to create conversations so similar to those of us humans, that it's hard to tell the difference. Thanks to deep learning algorithms, ChatGPT has been trained on a large library of chat logs, granting it the ability to craft natural replies to your questions andprompts instantly.

The good thing about ChatGPT is that it can generate answers just like those made by people. In short, ChatGPT is a powerful, multi-purpose and valuable tool in generating human-like answers in chatbot applications.
