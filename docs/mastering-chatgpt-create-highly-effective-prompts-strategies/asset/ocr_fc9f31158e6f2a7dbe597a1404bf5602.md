# # Prompts to Get You Started Today

Sometimes the easiest way to use ChatGPT is simply just to give it instructions. Below is a list of potential prompts you can use in ChatGPT to achieve some impressive responses.

# ## Business Prompts

# ### Ad Copy

Prompt: "Generate a compelling advertisement for my product ____________."

# ### Client Profiles

Prompt: "Create the ideal client profile for a company that sells natural, vegan meals and desserts." You can ask a follow-up question such as, "create some additional ideal client profiles for this company."

# ### Cover letter

Prompt: "Generate a cover letter for the [Position Name] position at [Company Name], that highlight my qualifications [List them here] including [Number of Years] of experience in [Industry or field] and skills in [specific skills or qualifications]."
