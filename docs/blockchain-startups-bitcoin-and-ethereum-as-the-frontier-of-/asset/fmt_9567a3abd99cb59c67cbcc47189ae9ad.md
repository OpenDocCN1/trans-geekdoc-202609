This book shows you how to compete with banks. Bitcoin and Ethereum have features that empower entrepreneurs to disrupt finance. The functions of banks are now in the cross-hairs of open-source groups and software entrepreneurs. We look at what you can build for the future beyond traditional banking. Banks used to be protected from disruption by the nature of fiat money. The lack of competitors has made them slow to innovate. With Bitcoin and Ethereum, software entrepreneurs can spot opportunities to offer state-of-the-art financial services and soar above the status quo.
