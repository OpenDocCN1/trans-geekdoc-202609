# What is an intelligent military machine?

The term artificial intelligence (AI) was first used as a title for the conference held at Dartmouth College in 1955. Alan Turing’s (a mathematician and a pioneer in computer science) 1950 essay titled ‘Computing Machinery and Intelligence’ can also be taken as a signpost for the birth of the term AI.¹ AI is machines that can generate behaviour similar to humans, such as making choices and judgements. The rise of AI is part of the ‘Information Revolution.’ Information is a crucial ingential of material productivity in the era of Information Revolution. In this era, the generation of new knowledge is dependent on computing power. Basic services like banking, railways, transportation, water and energy distribution are becoming more and more dependent on information and communication technologies (ICT). The principal sources of energy include digital information, computer software and communications. The computer with its electronic brain is supplementing (might supplement in the future) human thinking. The integrated circuits and computer technology are the bedrock of AI, and these two technologies are continuously developing.AI is a combination of technologies which includes both hardware and software. So, an AI system includes hardware like mainframe computers, microchips, sensors integrated with software as programs, algorithms, etc. The fundamental building blocks of AI are learning algorithms which allow the machine to detect patterns in data, make decisions, adjust their behaviour relation to the environment. This learning process is called machine learning (ML) and its subset is deep learning (DL).We will discuss these two terms later in detail.
