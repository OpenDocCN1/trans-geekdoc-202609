One way to smoothen the MHC for controlling the AWS is to create cyborgs, which includes the best of humans and machines. A cyborg is a cybernetic organism. It is an organic life form that has integrated substantial elements of electronic technology to enhance its own capabilities.¹⁷⁷ In his book titled Future War (2017), Robert H. Latiff, a retired USAF majorgeneral, writes about the possible nature of the battlefield in 2050 in the following words:

Soldiers will look and be different. Technology will be employed, first externally, to give the soldier greater protection, greater situational awareness, and greater stamina... Function-enhancing drugs will become common. Soldiers’ bodies will be modified with exoskeletons to improve strengths, drugs are likely to be artificially enhanced to improve cognition or alter memory, and surgery to implant microelectronic neurological aids.⁷⁸⁸

Synthetic biology is a comparatively new area of research. It aims to design and construct novel biological pathways, organisms and devices, and restructuring of natural biological systems. Synthetic biology could provide the soldiers with new physiological functions and improved performance.⁷⁹
