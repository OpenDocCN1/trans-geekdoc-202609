Besides the India-China War of 1962, there have been many border violations, disregarding mutually agreed protocols. In recent years, China's intrusion in Doklam (June 2017), Galwan (June 2020) and the Arunachal border (December 2022) are cases in point. With the Chinese Navy's expanding in the Indian Ocean; their vessels are foraging for various purposes survey and research. Chinese ship, Yuan Wang Wi, entered the Indian Ocean ahead of India's planned missile test in the second week of November 2022. The Chinese vessel had the capability of tracking satellites and ballistic missiles and gathering intelligence. Before the entry of this ship, in September 2019, Chinese research and survey vessels were spotted in the Indian Ocean, south of Andaman and Nicobar Islands, in January 2021.

Pakistan is using drones to supply arms and explosives to terrorist groups in Jammu and Kashmir and Punjab. Many times these have been caught by the security forces. Along with countering these drones with precision engagement, there is a need to complement the effort with other countermeasures measures, such as the disruption of terrorist financing, information and cyber operations, plus security and counterterrorism assistance to states in the region, based on accurate real-time intelligence practicable. The government of India has initiated actions towards this end and its results are viable.

India is well known for its soft-skills power in the domain of IT. Microsoft CEO Satya Nadela has, in an interview with CNBC TV18 in 2023, said that all the world's software developers, India is number two. India can make best use of its talents to empower itself with AI-powered means, that can help the defence and security forces, as force-multipliers and enablers, to meet the challenges of conventional and irregular warfare. The Indian Navy has given a contract for manufacturing weaponised autonomous boats. Possibly, the navy will need autonomous systems powered by advanced-level technologies (like underwater drones) for naval warfare and protecting submarines and ships. What India needs is AI that can explain and make informed decisions, for both manned and unmanned systems, which are fully trustworthy and will not act outside of what they have been developed to.
