# AI, autonomy and autonomous systems

Cockelbergh, 'the hype surrounding AI has given rise to all kinds of speculations about the future of AI and indeed, of what it is to be human.'13 AI is (arguably) intelligence demonstrated by non-natural entities, or machines. B. Raphael defines AI as an idea and a process: 'of making things that would require human intelligence to drive man.'14 It would require human intelligence to drive a car from point A to B without hitting sidewalks at both locations. As such, any mechanical system that has the capacity to do this possesses some level of autonomy, and can be described as operating on AI.
