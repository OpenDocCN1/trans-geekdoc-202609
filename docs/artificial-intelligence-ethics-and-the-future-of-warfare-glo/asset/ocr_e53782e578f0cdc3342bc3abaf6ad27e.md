+   • The complexity of AI algorithms and the black-box nature of many AI systems make it difficult to understand and predict their behaviour, leading to concerns about accountability and transparency in military operations.

+   • AI technology is unpredictable, vulnerable to unique forms of manipulation and challenges human-machine interaction.

+   • Ensuring necessity for human presence on the battlefield alongside AI systems in some capacity is the principal restraining factor that will keep the technology from undermining warfare.

+   • LAWS will inevitably produce errors. These errors will be difficult to correct or prevent from reoccurring. It is difficult to take action without understanding how the weapon system behaves and why. Automation could create denial that an error has occurred.

+   • AI systems used in military operations are vulnerable to cyberattacks and hacking, which could compromise the security of military assets and put troops at risk.

+   • The deployment of LAWS might result in accidental and collateral damage risk causing fratricide, with large numbers of weapons turning on friendly forces and civilian casualties. This could be because of hacking, enemy behavioural manipulation, simple malfunctions, unexpected interactions with the environment or software errors.

+   • LAWS' errors would likely repeat with a consistent level of idiosyncratic interventions. Human error tends to be idiosyncratic and one-off, given a human operator's common sense, moral agency and capacity for near-time consequence management.

+   • Waging war is unethical. Because it spurs so much moral outrage among the population whom the country needs support, robot warfare has strategic disadvantages.

+   • Service members at every level lack the technical education and experience to employ AI.

+   • Current capabilities in the military sphere are far from perfect, and may have serious consequences.

+   • Inability to multitask. A fairly simple set of tasks is currently impossible for an AI system to accomplish.

The use of AI in warfare should be approached with caution, and the potential disadvantages should be carefully considered and addressed in the development and deployment of military technology.
