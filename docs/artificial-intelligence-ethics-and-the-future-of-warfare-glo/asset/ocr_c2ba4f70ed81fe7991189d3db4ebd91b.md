Probably stunde null (complete break with the past) never occurs in reality. Every revolutionary phenomenon incorporates elements of continuity within it. Both Military Revolutions and RMAs have occurred in history. In fact, Military Revolutions, RMAs and military evolution (slow gradual changes in halting stages) have occurred sequentially across the broad span of the last five millennia of human history. The emergence of the English longbow in the fourteenth century, which resulted in the decline of French feudal heavy cavalry, was a classic case of RMA.20 The rise of gunpowder war, which gave birth to the military fiscal state in early modern Europe (1500–1800), is an example of a Military Revolution. This Military Revolution gave birth to the cannon-equipped ocean-going navy and gunpowder infantry. Gunpowder infantry means drilled and disciplined infantry armed with handguns and supported by field (light) artillery in battles and with siege (heavy) artillery for conquering. This system, in turn allowed the Western European politics during the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries to colonise large swathes of Afro-Asia and the New World.21
