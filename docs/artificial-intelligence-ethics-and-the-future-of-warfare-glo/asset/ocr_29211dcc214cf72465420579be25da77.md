# Philosophy of status quo logic

Sentience is not the key challenge. The building blocks for generating a source bio-code are increasingly becoming available; the tipping point for viable integration will be reached sooner than later. The main challenge is to design the normative architecture for sentience. Sentience has been dissected using two main thought instruments: through qualia as a specific intersubjective experience that portrays several Western schools of philosophy¹¹ and through awareness as a continuous inter-relational process of cognition that infuses many Asian philosophies.

The need to understand human existence is at the core of philosophical traditions of the West. Rene Descartes is a key figure – if not the key figure – of the Scientific Revolution. Everything from logic, mathematics and geometry to philosophical treatises on reason, psychology and even physiology. His first principle of ‘Think, therefore I am’ in his 1637 Discourse on the Method is arguably the cornerstone of all Western philosophy anchored to mind-body dualism. This dualism undermines Karl Popper’s concept of empirical falsification that has informed the contemporary view of the relationship between knowledge, observation and objectivity. The concept of mind-body dualism of a binary separation between a thinker and his thinking and of an observer and his turns the ‘thought’ and ‘observed’ into a problematic.

Logically, a watertight case can be made that the act of thinking and observation cannot take place without pre-existing knowledge with conscious act and directed action possible without knowledge. In machine learning terms, it is akin to the pre-existence of a library of images logged in a sufficiently indexable manner for any act of observation to fulfil itself. As illustrated in Figure 6.2, machine learning software cannot observe a tree and turn it into an observation of a tree and categorise it unless it has a sufficiently dense catalogue of trees and non-trees to cross-reference and in the process identify a tree as a.
