Cyberoperations can have serious effects on the nuclear arsenal of a state. One example is the dising of Iran's refining operations at Natanz in 2010 by the Stuxnet virus. Stuxnet was delivered via a Universal Serial Bus (USB) thumb drive and it damaged the centrifuges used to refine uranium. Stuxnet found its way to the machine that controlled the Programmable Logic Controller attached to the motor control. The virus modified the instructions sent to motor, thus causing it to spin at different rates. Machinery rotating at high rates is extra sensitive to changes in the rotation rates, and centrifuges for refinement of radioactive uranium spin at 6,000 revolutions per minute. Thus, Stuxnet caused physical damage to the centrifuges in the Natanz plant. This attack was probably carried out jointly by American and Israeli intelligence agencies..^{53}

Integration of ML algorithms and other types of AI into cyberoperations offers several advantages for military operations in cyberspace. The use of ML increases the chance of discovering gaps in code that the AI could exploit autonomously, which leads to greater efficiency and speed of offensive cyberoperations. The same technology can also be used to strengthen defensive systems and ward off malware and other intrusions into networks. ML algorithms are also useful to scan and survey the online activities of a large number of individuals or autonomously prepare the digital battlefield by planting malware in an adversary's networks that might be activated remotely in case a war breaks out. The use of AI increases the potential impact of disinformation campaigns and other types of information warfare, making such campaigns more effective, scalable and widespread. The creation of deepfake audiovisual content, the detection of faults in the target population's social fabric to maximise a campaign's effect, the deployment of bots to artificially amplify subversive messaging directed at a target population and pursue automated agenda setting – particularly with more sophisticated bots that are programmed to credibly mimic real people's behaviour – are some the ways AI can be used. Finally, the automatic calculation of microtargeting in order to tailor content to a receptive audience can also be done effectively by AI..^{54}

Besides cyberspace, the use of AI in outspace is becoming a reality. Control of the space domain (the area above the altitude where atmospheric effects on orbit become negligible) is necessary for all domain military operations. Most of the space operations occur within the geosynchronous Earth Orbit (GEO), Highly Elliptical Orbit (HEO), Medium Earth Orbit (MEO) and Low Earth Orbit (LEO). GEO is for global communications, surveillance, observing large-scale weather patterns and warning about missile launches by the enemy. A spacecraft at HEO could loiter for a long time over a particular target, especially at high altitudes. M orbits between LEO and GEO. Spacecraft at LEO are relatively close to Earth. These spacecraft use less powerful transmitters for communication and achieve higher resolution imagery with similar-sized apertures compared to satellites in the HEO and GEO. However, spacecraft at LEO could remain over a particular target in the terrestrial plane for a short time. These spacecraft at various orbits (altitudes) constitute the orbital segment of space power. The terrestrial segment includes terrestrial-based radars and all the link segment connects the signals in the electromagnetic spectrum (EMS) and the nodes that connect the orbital segment to the terrestrial segment, as well as the individual terrestrial segments with each other.
