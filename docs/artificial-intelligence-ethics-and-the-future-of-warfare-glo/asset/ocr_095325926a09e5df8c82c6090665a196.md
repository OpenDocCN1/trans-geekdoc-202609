# AI-powered military robots

Almost eight decades ago, USSR first fielded military robots in the battlefield during the Second World War in the form of unmanned radio-controlled 'teletasks' by the Red Army used them during the Winter War against Finland. It was passed by the German Goliath, a tactical mine vehicle.¹¹ Robots from the point of view are of three types. Automatic robots—which respond in a mechanical way to external inputs. Automated robots—which execute commands in a chronological preprogrammed way with sensors that help sequence the action. They are mostly limited by algorithms that determine their rules of operations and behaviour, out of a fixed set of alternative actions, which makes them predictable. And the third type of robot is an autonomous robot which has minimum human interference other than possibly switching it on or off.
