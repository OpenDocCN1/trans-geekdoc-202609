There are various ethical issues associated with the use of this technology, the militaries have to grapple with it. Mainly, the technology is still not ripe for autonomous deployment. As such, humans would not like the machines to make the decision to kill a human. Hence, even with the autonomous weapon systems, there is a broad understanding that the human should always remain in the loop. AI allows the military leadership to get suggestions based on systematic assessment of vast data. It is found to make the process of decision making, from early stages of planning to actual operations, more effective and logical. AI-based weapon systems have great advantages and are currently proving to be force multipliers for the militaries. All in all, AI is expected to revolutionise military operations, and as technology matures, there would be endless possibilities for its usage in the military.
