## The modern contemporary war

It is useful to think of a contemporary 'modern' war as a system-of-systems it is composed of two interoperable pieces of Command, Control, Computers, Communications and Information (C4I) and Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance (ISR). A system-of-systems war plays out as a series of hybrid battles with manned assets and unmanned assets operating together in a complementary manner to combat a multidimensional hostile environment. The ongoing Russia-Ukraine conflict is a case in point. There are three key systems that are currently operating in a theatre of conflict, sometimes in conjunction with each other and at other times in isolation.

The first is an emerging complex of unmanned systems of different levels operating with each other. These range from intelligence gathering loitering drones operating together with small infantry-level handheld drones to lethal drones with stand-off missiles, with preconfigured commodity-level Kamikaze drones replacing misses due to cost and logistical considerations. The second are legacy assets like heavy and light tanks, bridge layers, portable radars and sensors electronic sniffing equipment, mine-clearing vehicles, trench digers, infantry vehicles, fighter aircraft and helicopters with air-to-air and air-to-ground roles that operate through a ground-, sea-, air-, and satellite-based command and control infrastructure.

The third system is in its infancy. It is composed of autonomous systems with executive functions. These include AI decision-making systems that provide everything from targeting decisions for air, sea and ground forces to big think 'how' to and with what' solutions for countering and suppressing offensive and defensive assets. Such systems have the inbuilt capability to execute their decisions independently, but are are currently supervised by a human layer with the final execution conducted through a human chain of command.

## Non-carbon entities of the future

The common foundation of all non-carbon entities of the future with key human characteristics will be a source-code (as a combination of logical artifacts and brain organs) that is self-learning, autonomously rewritable at the entity level by the entity itself each learning moment and can anticipate outcomes of multiple decisions within complex environments for achieving either specific purpose or a larger goal. Self-learning is a process connected to knowledge production and experiential wisdom. Knowledge and experience are integral parts of metacognition: A main indicator of sentience.

A non-carbon sentient entity of the future will use five metacognitive competences of Systems Thinking, Normative and Values Thinking, Anticipatory and Futures Thinking, Strategic Thinking and Self Awareness and Critical Thinking. Normative and Values Thinking is the most critical competency since it cross-sects and intersects all the other competencies and is necessary for designating decisions and outcomes as good, permissible and desirable or bad, inadmissible and undesirable. Ethics, Virtue and Morality are the key instruments of regulation for decision-making processes and all the executive functions connected to actions emerging from a decision.

For creating an all-purpose source-code, then, architecting a library of 'if' 'but' and 'or' statements that are coupled with a library of 'when' scenarios and 'then'-outcomes is an incredibly logarithmic problem set. The problem will set, require the design of a logarithmic scale of the widest range possible to accommodate all norms, including their contextual nuances, and degrees of application in different scenarios, over a wide range of values in a compact manner.

## Philosophy of status quo logic

Sentience is not the key challenge. The building blocks for generating a source bio-code are increasingly becoming available; the tipping point for viable integration will be reached sooner than later. The main challenge is to design the normative architecture for sentience. Sentience has been dissected using two main thought instruments: through qualia as a specific intersubjective experience that portrays several Western schools of philosophy¹ and through awareness as a continuous inter-relational process of cognition that infuses many Asian philosophies.

The need to understand human existence is at the core of philosophical traditions of the West. Rene Descartes is a key figure – if not the key figure – of the Scientific Revolution. Everything from logic, mathematics and geometry to philosophical treatises on reason, psychology and even physiology. His first principle of ‘Think, therefore I am’ in his 1637 Discourse on the Method is arguably the cornerstone of all Western philosophy anchored to mind-body dualism. This dualism undermines Karl Popper’s concept of empirical falsification that has informed the contemporary view of the relationship between knowledge, observation and objectivity.

Logically, a watertight case can be made that the act of thinking and observation cannot take place without pre-existing knowledge with conscious act and directed action possible without knowledge. In machine learning terms, it is akin to the pre-existence of a library of images logged in a sufficiently indexable manner for any act of observation to fulfil itself.
