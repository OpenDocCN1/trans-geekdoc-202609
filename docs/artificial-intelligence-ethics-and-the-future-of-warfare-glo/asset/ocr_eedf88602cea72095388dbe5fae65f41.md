In contrast to Panwar, Kausik Roy notes in Chapter 10 that the Indian armed forces are giving a lot of thought towards using semi-autonomous machines like UAVs, disposal ground robots, for various COIN tasks. The point is to be noted that the use of drones by the USA in Afghanistan and Pakistan has alienated the local population. Winning the ‘hearts and minds’ of the people living in the disturbed zone is a sine qua non for successful COIN. At present, the Indian COIN strategy is based on using a large number of infantry equipped with handheld weapons only. The use of UAVs and land mobile robots might change the equation for worse.

In Chapter 3, A.K. Sachdev focuses on a particular piece of military system: Aviation. He details the various ways in which AI-embedded second-generation aircraft is paving the way for the emergence of pilotless aircraft. Have human pilots reached a dead end? There are several advantages to using automated aircraft compared to a manned. Unlike a human pilot, the AI flying the aircraft will not get tired and will be able to make a steeper and faster turn and be able to fly longer and higher. The USA, Russia and China along with other major powers, are planning to have one manned aircraft six unmanned aircraft with the aid of AI. The UCAVs will function in this scenario as loyal wingmen. The point is to be noted between 2002 and 2010, there has been a 40-fold increase in the number of drones in the American armed forces. In 2010, the United Air Force (USAF) declared that it was training more drone operators than F-16 pilots.

Ash Heydar Pashakhanlou of the Swedish Defence University takes a more moderate view of the pilotless aerial vehicle (PAV). Pashakhanlou notes: ‘The central challenge for PAVs will probably be operations in complex and crowded environments, where human, but not pilot, involvement may be required in the absence of AGI. It will also likely take considerable time before the semi-autonomous PAVs dominate the skies. The day that happens, it will mark the end of pilots.’²⁸ So, PAV/UAV is an RMA in waiting. But UAV in combination with other AI embedded weapon systems is generating a new Military Revolution.
