In 2019, DARPA launched the Air Combat Evolution (ACE) program to adopt AI in individual and team aerial combat tactics and to develop Air Combat Maneuvering (ACM) algorithms for visual 1-vs-1, visual-2-vs-1 and 2-vs-2 engagements with a broad spectrum of performance. The AlphaDogfight fights mentioned earlier were a part of ACE which embraces manned and unmanned aerial combat. While ACE is aimed at developing AI software capable of close combat by unmanned platforms autonomously, Skyborg has many capabilities (Beyond Range [BVR] missiles and long-range sensors) in mind for loyal wingmen. However, as both programmes look at unmanned wingmen, they may be merged at some future point in time as the programmes mature.
