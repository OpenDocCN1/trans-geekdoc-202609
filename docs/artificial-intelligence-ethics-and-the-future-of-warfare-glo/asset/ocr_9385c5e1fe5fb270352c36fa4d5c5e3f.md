Bulletin messages in 7-Eleven stores read 'Warmoner Pelosi, get out of Taiwan.' There were also electronic billboards across Taiwan protesting the visit – one calling her an 'old witch.' There lies here an interesting comment on AI-enabled cyber weapons and future warfare. The first militarily significant and autonomous weapon system by AI will probably be deployed not on the physical battlefield but in cyberspace. AI-enabled cyber capabilities based on deep-learning algorithms will be easier and cheaper to develop than AI-enabled autonomous platforms, which require maneuvering to fight physical enemies. The USA will most likely be the first country to field an AI-enabled cyber weapon, but other nations, particularly China and Russia, are bound to follow suit.29
