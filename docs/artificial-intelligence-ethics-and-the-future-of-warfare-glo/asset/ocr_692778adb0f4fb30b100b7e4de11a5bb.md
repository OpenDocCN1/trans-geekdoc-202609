In a network-centric environment, AI assists soldiers to access and share information throughout the entire network in real-time. This, ISR are the key elements of battlefield management. The change in the battlefield scenario over the years has also led to changes in techniques of identification, surveillance and reconnaissance. Improvements in technologies in various spheres are presenting sensors with high sensitivity, small sizes and better visibility. Particularly AI is contributing significantly to the fields of ground, aerial and underwater ISR capabilities. Notably, UAVs are found using AI-based techniques for the purposes of intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance. This brings transparency to the battlefield and allowing dynamic visualisation of an almost actual (three-dimensional model) battlefield. AI is increasingly integrated in areas like internet search, military applications, terrain and atmospheric considerations and robotics. In strategic terms, AI has the potential to change the way armed forces execute their command, training, logistics, and force deployment. Various surveillance platforms, UAVs, Synthetic Aperture Radars, Airborne Early Warning Systems and Satellites continuously generates vast volumes of data and imagery, around the clock. Using such data, AI-based systems develop approaches to aid the battlefield commanders in identifying hidden patterns in the swaths of data with the highest precision and accuracy. It could be a standalone input given by an AI-based system or such information be used for augmenting the human ability to analyse and act.³⁷ In the near future, and other AI-based technologies are likely to become embedded in various other technologies and provide autonomous decision-making on behalf of military commanders. AI is expected to have a major role in ICT, including both traditional telecommunications as well as various communications-enabled applications.³⁸ AI also allows devising Intelligent Decision Support Systems, which actually leads to the transformation of human decision-making to machine-based decision-making or providing computational assistance to commanders to make decisions. C4ISR structures are also expected to be aided by blockchain technologies and the Internet of Military Things (IoMT).
