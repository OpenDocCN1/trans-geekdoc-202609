Further, the Indian Army is thinking of using loitering munitions fitted with precision-guided munitions in sub-conventional warfare. A loitering munition is a UAV which can engage explosive targets. These UAVs are fitted with high-resolution optical and infrared cameras which allow the target to locate, survive and track the target. This sort of UAV is able to hover over the target area for an extended period before striking, thus enabling the operator to decide what to target and when. The robust sensor-to-shooter communications will also allow a human-in-the-loop command system to continue. Loitering munitions are perfect for targeted assassinations of insurgent commanders and also for crowd control by taking out the leaders. In addition, urban centres, the loitering drones with non-lethal weapons could temporarily deactivate the hostile crowd. This will prevent non-combat fatalities as collateral damage in urban operations always causes a political hue and cry.79

# Conclusion

Technology is neutral. Both India and its non-state opponent are using AI-related technologies, and their use by both sides will increase in scope and frequency in the near future. The issue is which side will use it effectively. Complete autonomy for LAWS remains a chimera in the foreseeable future. At most, in the near future, we will see semi-autonomous intelligent weapon systems deployed for operations in combination with human soldiers and human-manned weapon platforms. The Indian armed forces need to update their joint and service-specific doctrines for conceptual blending of AWS with other conventional weapon systems by humans. Along with doctrine, training with and against AWS needs to be elaborated and implemented. Such amalgamation and deployment of AI-related technologies and non-AI technologies are necessary for effective force planning, force structuring and force deployment. For the time being, the officers of the Indian armed forces are comfortable using AI as a junior partner with the human commander in tactical and operational decision-making processes. However, in the future, there might be a demand to reduce the OODA loop and increase the tempo, more autonomy should be given to AWS.
