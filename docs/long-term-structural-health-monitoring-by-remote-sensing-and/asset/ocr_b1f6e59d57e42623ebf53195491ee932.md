# 2.4 The Frontier of Real-Time SHM: Proposed Online Hybrid Learning Methods

All the previously proposed methods are based on consideration of adequate training samples. In the SAR-aided SHM problems, since it is possible to access prior information (images) of a civil structure in a certain interval of time, the learning process of the previously proposed techniques are reasonable and reliable. This kind of SHM in machine learning perspective is called offline learning. It should be clarified that this approach is also valid for other kinds of SHM methods.

This section intends to suggest a new methodology under the idea of online learning for real-time SHM. In machine learning, online learning sets out to update a machine or computational model by new data. On this basis, the machine or model of interest can be developed by the previous information, in which case online learning improves it by receiving new training data. In some cases, an online learning strategy is considered to the initial step of a machine. For the applications of SHM, if the new information belongs to an undamaged structural state, the online learning process should be continued; otherwise, it should be stopped and the further investigation is based on the last updated model. In this regard, any new information treats as test data.

In this section, one attempts to propose two online learning methods in hybrid fashions. In other works, these methods (i.e., HMC-ODTL-EMD and SLS-ODTL-EMD) entail three parts of data augmentation, online data normalization, and feature discrimination. The first step of the proposed hybrid methods is concerned with the data augmentation. Hence, both the HMC and SLS techniques are considered to augment the small displacement data. Therefore, the major differences of these methods against the other proposed techniques are related to the process of online data normalization conducted by an ODTL algorithm and feature discrimination by EMD.

# 2.4.1 Online Deep Transfer Learning Paradigm

One of the main prerequisites of the DAE in the DTL framework is to use adequate (augmented) data. In other words, it is based on an offline learning strategy. In this section, the enhanced version of DTL under the idea of online learning is proposed and discussed. The proposed ODTL algorithm consists of two stages. The first stage is an online learning algorithm till the stopping condition terminates this algorithm. Accordingly, this stage declares that the new data utilized in the learning procedure does not belong to the undamaged structural state and it is probably associated with a damaged condition. To put it another way, the algorithm alarms the occurrence of an abnormal change. Hence, the second stage starts calling the last trained DAE from the first stage.
