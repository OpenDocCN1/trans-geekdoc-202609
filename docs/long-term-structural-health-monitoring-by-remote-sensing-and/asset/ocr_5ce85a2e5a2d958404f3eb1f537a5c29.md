To further investigate the EOC influences, Fig. 4.5 presents a statistical analysis via Box plot of the expanded displacements gained by the HMC and SLS techniques. This conclusion emphasizes the positive effect of data augmentation by this technique for better observation of the rate of EOCs. In contrast, it is seen in Fig. 4.5b that the SLS could not give the output as good as the HMC.
