Having implemented k iterations of the DTL algorithm, the trial (iteration) with the maximum accuracy value is selected as the optimum number of consecutive DAEs.

# 2.3.5 Feature Discrimination via Mahalanobis Distance

Using the augmented training and test samples, these are considered in a novelty detector for feature discrimination. In the context of SHM, the MD is a tried-and-tested novelty detector that was widely applied to SHM applications (Sarmadi and Karamodin 2020; Sarmadi et al. 2020, 2021, 2023b; Yeager et al. 2019; Sarmadi et al. ; Mosavi et al. 2012; Nguyen et al. 2014; George et al. 2018; Entezami et al. 2023 ). This is due to some great advantages such as simplicity, computational efficiency, and non-parametric behavior. In some conditions that the variability level is not considerable, this method yields reasonable results of feature discrimination with high detection accuracy. In this regard, Sarmadi (Sarmadi 2021) demonstrated that the MD-based novelty detection is a reliable method under low EOCs. However, it fails in providing satisfactory results in high variability conditions. In this case, data normalization is mandatory before applying the MD or other novelty detectors with poor performances in high EOCs.

The process of feature discrimination via the MD-based novelty detector is generally implemented in the baseline (training) and monitoring (testing) phases. The former requires the features of the undamaged state (i.e., training data) and the later needs some features of the undamaged condition (i.e., validation data) and all available features associated with the unknown (current) structural state. Assume that the augmented training matrix X comes from an undamaged condition of the structure with inconsiderable EOCs. In this case, it is not necessary to use a data normalization strategy similar to the proposed HMC-MD technique. Hence, the underlying goal is to utilize sample estimators for estimating the mean v_x in R^p and covariance S_x in R^{p x p} of X. It should be pointed out that as the augmented data obtained from either HMC or SLS conforms to the multivariate Gaussian/normal distribution, it can be ensured that the uses of the sample estimators are reasonable. With these descriptions, the MD for the ith column vector of X is expressed as follows:

d_MD(x_i) = (x_i - v_x)^T S_x^{-1} (x_i - v_x), forall i = 1, ..., n (2.38)
