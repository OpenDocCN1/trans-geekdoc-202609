# # Chapter 2
Advanced MIL Methods: Bridging SAR Images and Structural Health Monitoring

**Abstract** The classical SAR-aided SHM directly analyzes displacement data obtained from SAR images. Although the extraction of displacement responses from SAR images via various interferometric techniques is of paramount importance to remote sensing-based SHM, the direct use and analysis of SAR-based displacement responses for monitoring and damage assessment of complex and critical civil structures is not a reliable and effective approach, especially in long-term monitoring with various uncertainties and variability in measured data. On this basis, this chapter proposes various machine learning methods in terms of unsupervised learning for SHM under environmental and operational conditions and different types of structural displacement responses retrieved from SAR images in terms of their sizes. In case of a large set of displacement data, an innovative probabilistic unsupervised learning method is proposed. For a small set of displacement data, this chapter proposes two unsupervised hybrid learning methods and mainly consists of three parts of data augmentation for expanding the size of small displacement responses, data normalization for removing the effects of environmental and operational conditions, and anomaly detection.

**Keywords** Machine learning · Unsupervised learning · Hybrid learning · Online learning · Data normalization · Markov Chain Monte Carlo · Anomaly detection

# ## 2.1 Introduction

Most of the SHM programs via remote sensing are based on direct analyses of structural displacements retrieved from SAR images. Although this strategy is simple and efficient, it may not be reliable and effective for damage assessment of important and complex civil structures under EOCs (Behkamal et al. 2023a, b). To address the major limitations and shortcomings of this strategy, this book intends to propose more rigorous methods for health monitoring of civil structures, with the focus on bridges, by the same SAR images and displacement data used in the classical SAR-aided SHM technique.
