# 1.6 Chapter Summary

This chapter has aimed to present the introduction to health monitoring of civil structures by using the technology of remote sensing and SAR images. The main focus has been on the collapse phenomenon. After mentioning the advantages of the SAR-aided SHM by remote sensing, the major challenges and limitations in this strategy have been discussed in detail. For better explanation, the main motivations for conducting this book have been mentioned, which highly aid readers to better realize the aim and scope of this book. In the following, brief descriptions of all proposed methods have been presented. Eventually, the major innovations of this work have been disclosed properly. With these descriptions and literature reviews discussed in this chapter, one can conclude that the classical SAR-aided SHM techniques may noy provide reliable frameworks for damage assessment of large-scale civil structures by directly analyzing displacement data. For long-term monitoring programs via SAR images, it is essential to develop robust machine learning techniques for alleviating the negative effects of the EOCs.
