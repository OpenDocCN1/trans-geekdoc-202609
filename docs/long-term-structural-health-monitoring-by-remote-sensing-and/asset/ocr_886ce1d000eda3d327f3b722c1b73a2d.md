# About the Authors

**Prof. Alireza Entezami** has been an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering (DICA) at Politecnico di Milano, Italy, since November 2022. His current role also includes co-supervision of a research project granted by the European Space Agency (ESA), which employs data mining and machine learning techniques for monitoring the structural integrity of large infrastructures using earth observation.

Prior to joining the DICA department as a faculty member, he was a postdoctoral research fellowship selected by ESA, working in the DICA at Politecnico di Milano since May 2021. In April 2020, He received a Ph.D. in Structural, Seismic, and Geotechnical Engineering from Politecnico di Milano with *Cum Laude* degree. In addition, He holds a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering—Structure from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad with Summa *Cum Laude* degree. In September 2014, he obtained the M.Sc. in the same discipline from Ferdowsi University of Mashhad with *Summa Cum Laude* degree.

He has a history of securing substantial research funds and fostering collaborations across the academia and industry. Notably, he was awarded a three-year postdoctoral fellowship by the European Space Agency. He also won two medals for one of his inventions regarding a structural smart portable device dealing with structural health monitoring problem, at the IWIS exhibition, supported by the International Federation of Inventors’ Associations. Furthermore, the European Commission (EC), Horizon Europe Program has supported his research, selected by NGI Enrichers and the Agency for the Promotion of European Research (APRE), to implement his approved research proposal.

His research interests span from model-driven structural damage detection to data-driven structural health monitoring, with the focus on large civil infrastructures. Most of his research activities focus on artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistical pattern recognition, deep learning, image processing, big data analytics, information fusion, time series analysis, and remote sensing. His research has resulted in 41 international journal papers, 21 conference proceedings, 8 book chapters, and two book publications in Springer.
