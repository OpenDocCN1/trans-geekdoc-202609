One of the great advantages of the proposed probabilistic method is its capacity for developing various anomaly detectors based on different extreme value distributions. However, in this book, the proposed methodology is based on the semi-parametric concept of the EVT and the quantile modeling of the semi-parametric extreme value distribution.

For the SAR-aided SHM applications with a small number of images, leading to few displacement data, this book mainly concentrates on this challenge by proposing various offline and online hybrid learning methods; that is: (i) HMC-MD, (ii) HMC-DAE-MD, (iii) HMC-UTSL-MD, (iv) HMC-DTL-MD, (v) HMC-ODLT-EMD, and (vi) SLS-ODLT-EMD. # 2.2 Tackling Big Data Challenges: A Probabilistic Unsupervised Learning Method

The proposed probabilistic machine learning method called NNS-EVT aims to perform a long-term SHM process when the size of data is relatively large. The main elements of this method consist of a feature selection approach based on NNS and a novelty detector under the extreme value machine (EVM) and semi-parametric EVT. The feature selection approach is based on the nearest neighbor search for choosing the most appropriate and relevant samples, called representative features. For this reason, this step can be considered as a dimensionality reduction strategy and also a data normalization method. The second element of the proposed machine learning method is to propose a novelty detector for distinguishing normal samples, regarding the undamaged condition of the structure, from abnormal ones, associated with the possibly damaged state. In this method, a novel probabilistic method is proposed by considering the concepts of the EVT, semi-parametric extreme value distribution, and quantile modeling.
