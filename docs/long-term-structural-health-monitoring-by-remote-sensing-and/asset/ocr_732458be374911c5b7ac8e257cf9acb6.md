+   • Category II—Innovations for dealing with the challenge of novelty indices

**Category I**: Although HMC and SLS are well-known Markov Chain Monte Carlo algorithms for the generation of random samples, the first parts of the proposed hybrid learning methods exploit these algorithms for expanding the small displacement data. The important innovation of data augmentation via HMC and SLS is that this process not only expands the small set of displacements but also reveals the variability in extracted displacement data samples better than the original data. In this case, it is possible to better analyze the level of variability and decide whether data normalization is needed or it can be ignored it.

**Category II**: Without any doubt, one can declare that the great innovations of the proposed hybrid learning method are related to various novel data normalization algorithms. The central core of data normalization is based on deep and shallow AEs, especially DAEs. As the first innovation, the proposed hybrid learning technique called HMC-MD is developed by adding an undercomplete DAE with three hidden layers for data normalization. The key novel part of this process is to propose a hyperparameter optimization for finding the optimum neuron numbers of the three hidden layers of the undercomplete DAE with the focus on alleviating the negative effects of the EOCs. Although different hyperparameter optimization algorithms such as cross-validation are appilable, this book suggests a new algorithm based on an engineering justification; that is, the removal or reduction of the EOC influences. On this basis, the hyperparameters obtained from this algorithm allow the undercomplete DAE to exclusively address the challenge related to the EOCs. Concentrating on this challenge, this book proposes the other data normalization method based on the concept of teacher-student learning. Even though this concept is well-known, we develop two novel ANNs for creating teacher and student models in an unsupervised learning fashion. The key innovative part of the second data normalization method is related to proposing an undercomplete DAE with seven hidden layers as the teacher model and an overcomplete shallow AE as the student model. In other words, the proposed data normalization method creates **a novel hybrid ANN** strategy. The other innovation of this method is to propose a new hyperparameter optimization for selecting the neuron numbers of the seven hidden layers of the DAE and the encoding layer of the shallow AE. In the following, the other data normalization method is proposed by taking advantage of the idea of transfer learning (Yang et al. 2020; Weiss et al. 2016). Unlike the other well-known transfer learning algorithms, which mainly rely upon supervised deep neural networks in the configuration of convolutional neural networks (Dhillon and Verma 2020), this book suggests a novel deep transfer learning algorithm that is based on an individual ANN in terms of an undercomplete three-hidden-layer DAE. The key novel part of the third data normalization method is to design an iterative algorithm using consecutive DAEs. For this algorithm, the other hyperparameter optimization is suggested to find the optimum number of consecutive DAEs without any effort for selecting the neuron numbers. In order to take advantage of the capacity of online learning for real-time SHM, this book provides the other innovation in data normalization.
