
## 13.9 Human Rights

Having access to the web is increasingly evaluated to be a fundamental human right. The global organizations and national governments have formally begun to recognize its centrality for the right to speak freely, expression and information exchange. The next stage is to help confirm cybersecurity to be seen as a human right, also. The United Nations has formally proclaimed that the actions of governments cutting off web access are denying their residents' rights to free expression. However, this isn't adequate because nowadays we have standard web access which quickly suffers from cyber-threats, fearing our information to be hacked at any minute we feel powerless to stop it. An online rights advocacy group, prominently known as The Electronic Frontier Foundation⁵, called on all technology companies to "stand with regards to users," for defending their systems against interference by hackers and government surveillance.

Net neutrality is the rule that each point on the system can connect to some other point on the system, without discriminating based on source, destination or type of information and this principle is the principal reason for the success of the Internet. Net neutrality gives the capacity to the Internet to create new methods for human rights, for example, the freedom of expression and the right to send, receive and transmit data and additionally it is essential for innovation, modernization, competition and for the development of data.

Advantages:
- The major telecom companies can provide the ways but don't have any right to dictate how the public should rely on them.
- Net neutrality protects innovation, if larger enterprises like Google and Netflix⁶ could pay and get special treatment like faster speeds, more bandwidth, then new start-ups or small organizations would have great disadvantage.
- If net neutrality isn't in place then, large companies could give priority to TV networks and slow down the signals of others.
- It offers opportunity to each organization, from large enterprises to small start-ups to participate in it.

Disadvantages:
- Nowadays, large companies like Netflix and YouTube⁷ clog the pipes with tremendous amounts of data, here changes will put a limitation.
- Various companies like Google, Facebook provide services that allow individuals to make free approaches to the web that mobile companies have spent billions to build.

## 13.10 Cybersecurity Application in Our Life

### Mobiles
Company-provided or bring your own device (BYOD), customers with various devices and types will automatically select all of them effectively. Each device is carefully registered and linked to a customer and can be instantly reported lost or stolen. If reported, countermeasures are started and once found, can be effectively re-selected for continued use.

### Smart Cards
It can provide authentication, individual identification, information storage capacity, and application processing within organizations. A single contact/contactless smart card can be preset with various banking approvals, driver's license, medical benefit, public transport right, club memberships. It can also be used as an ID, a stored value cash card, a credit card, and a repository of personal information, for example, medical history or contact numbers. If it is lost or stolen, the card can be adequately replaced by the security personal identification number (PIN) [14].

### Virtual Private Systems (VPN)
It gives you web privacy and anonymity by creating a private network from a public Internet connection. While VPN delivers excellent network protection, it may only be used from a properly secured computing device. There are several reasons people around the world make use of VPNs: to cover location, gain access to work systems, even to prevent government censorship. A VPN is a commonly used service that keeps web browsing secure over public Wi-Fi hotspots. For example, OpenVPN⁸, Juniper⁹, Cisco, and so on.

### Signing of Documents
It provides e-signatures, time-stamps, approved collaborators, and encryption/decryption to contracts, reports, bills, approvals and financials in document formats like DOC, XLS, PDF, etc.

### Securing Email
Digital security prevents damage to email content, prevents exposure of email content with encryption, verifies message source, and provides flexible and secure communication [6].

### Strong Authentication
It provides strong authentication services by managing and verifying both device and user identities, allowing or permitting control over who indeed accesses your data, applications, information and advanced resources on your web, extranet or intranet assets [14].

### Protected Communication
It offers secure connections between SSL (Secure Sockets Layer)/TLS (Transport Layer Security) servers and SSL/TLS clients for internal private systems and the financial cost is less compared to purchasing broker-provided advanced verifications.

### Online Corroboration Facilities
The automated and strong validation service provides cybersecurity, provides secure connection requests whether clients are signing into a remote application or opening an account on a private device or local machine. It can process lakhs of requests every second.

### Code Signing
Digital security provides a technology known as code signing which ensures that the product development process is guaranteed and that the product running on servers, cloud and end users can't be changed or tampered with since it was verified by the validated creator.

### Time Stamping
It provides data about who is the author of an online record - who created it, approved it and the information about when this record was created or last updated, which are the most required information for the user for their online safety [14].

### Single Sign-on
It enables the authorized user to access various applications and servers simultaneously using master credentials and furthermore it stops all application logins by someone who gets access to work area or device which isn't authorized.
