Section 104(c)(1).
  - A “cyber threat indicator” means information that is “necessary to describe or identify” a collection of known threats, together with “malicious code” and procedures for exploiting a security vulnerability or making a legitimate user unwittingly enable such corruption. Section 102(6) [11].
  - A “defensive measure” is described as something that can be deployed on an information system which identifies, stops, or mitigates a known or anticipated cybersecurity threat or security vulnerability, and does not destroy, damage, or degrade the system or data. Section 102(7).
  - A “cybersecurity purpose” means the purpose of protecting an information system or data from a “cybersecurity threat” or “security vulnerability,” as defined by the law. Section 102(4) [11].

- **Review Individual Data before Sharing**: An entity that intends to share a cyber threat indicator must remove, or should clear any information “not specifically related to a cybersecurity purpose” that the entity “knows at the time of sharing.” Section 104(d)(2)(A), (B).
