The prediction goal for the Neural Network is to find a surface that approximates these points as much as possible.

Because of the high number of iterations (epochs=10000) and the high degree of non-linearity of the Neural Network (50 hidden neurons with non-linear logistic functions and 201 parameters), the Neural Network can produce a very flexible prediction surface.

Figure 9.5 shows that the fitted Neural Network workflow model generates a prediction surface that almost approximates the training data. This prediction surface bends in many ways to make the close approximation possible. This high flexibility of the prediction surface (i.e., the underlying prediction function) was made possible by the high number of hidden neurons leading to a total of 201 β parameters.
