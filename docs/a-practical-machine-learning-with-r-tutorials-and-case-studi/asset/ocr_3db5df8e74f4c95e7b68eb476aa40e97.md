**Step 4:** Two effective inputs — one for each hidden neuron are now plugged into non-linear functions (called *activation functions*) inside hidden neuron $H1$ and $H2$. The results are called the *activities* ($A_{H1}^\sigma$ and $A_{H2}^\sigma$) of the neurons $H1$ and $H2$. We will talk more about activation functions in a minute.

**Step 5:** The activities of the hidden neurons $A_{H1}^\sigma$ and $A_{H2}^\sigma$ are transmitted to the output neuron (O1), but before they arrive, their values are weighted by $eta_1$ and $eta_2$, respectively.

**Step 6:** Three values arrive at the @ sign in front of the output neuron (O1): the two weighted *activities* from the hidden neurons ($eta_1 A_{H1}^\sigma$ and $eta_2 A_{H2}^\sigma$) and the value from the bias neuron $B2$, which equals $eta_7$. These three values are aggregated (added up) to a single value, which is the value of the output neuron O1, and the predicted price for the related diamond.

In complex contexts, the output neuron O1 may also contain an activation function, but here what goes into the output neuron comes without any transformation ($O1 = f^\sigma(O1)$).

The above, in connection with Figure 9.1 provide a basic idea about how a Neural Network processes variables to generate a prediction for an outcome variable. If the Neural Network contains more input and hidden neurons, or more layers, the process is more complex, but the underlying idea is the same.
