Figure 4.2 Horizontal Decision Boundary for Acidity and Total Sulfur Dioxide Related to Wine Color

As Figure 4.2 shows, such a decision boundary would classify most white wines correctly. This is because most wines have an Acidity < 8 (below the decision boundary) and are therefore correctly classified as white. About half of the red wines have an Acidity > 8 (above the decision boundary) and would also be classified as red. However, the other half of red wines have an Acidity < 8 (below the decision boundary) and therefore falsely classified as white.

Note that in Figure 4.2 and the following Figures 4.3 and 4.4 we use the training data in the absence of testing data to evaluate predictive quality. This is not appropriate! However, since the purpose of this section is to visualize basic ideas, we ignore this fact for now.
