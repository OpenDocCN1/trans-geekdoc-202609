As expected, when the penalty $\lambda$ corresponds to hyper-parameter penalty in tidymodels increases, the sum of the absolute $\beta$ values decreases. You can also see that all $\beta$ decrease to zero when $\lambda$ increases enough. Before this happens to all $\beta$ values, some $\beta$ decrease to zero at a lower level, successfully eliminating the related predictor variable from the analysis.

Let us explain this with an example. When $\lambda = 500$, the parameters for the predictor $Sqft^3$ and $Sqft^4$ are already zero ($\beta = 0$ and $\beta = 0$), the values for $\beta_1$, $\beta_2$, and $\beta_3$ are -46058, 1171967 and -560318, respectively. You can confirm this in Figure 7.1 when you imagine a line at $\lambda = 500$.
