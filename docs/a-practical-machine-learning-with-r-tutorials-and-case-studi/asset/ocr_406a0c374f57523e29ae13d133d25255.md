$Price_i = -465058 \cdot Sqft_i + 1171967 \cdot Sqft_i^2 + 0 \cdot Sqft_i^3 + \n0 \cdot Sqft_i^4 + (-560318) \cdot Sqft_i^5 + 509945$\n$Price_i = -465058 \cdot Sqft_i + 1171967 \cdot Sqft_i^2 + (-560318) \cdot Sqft_i^5 + 509945$

The equation is simpler and has smaller $eta$ parameters than the related prediction equation for an unregularized model which explains why overfitting is less likely.
