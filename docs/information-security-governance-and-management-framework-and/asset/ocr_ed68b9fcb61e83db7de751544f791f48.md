A certain level of maturity in governance requires that IS be directed by leaders with sufficient power and authority, including decision-making, accountability, and allocating the necessary resources. In such an environment, security can contribute to company objectives by strategically aligning with the business, implementing adequate measures (controls) to maintain asset-associated risks at an acceptable level, and managing the allocated resources effectively.
