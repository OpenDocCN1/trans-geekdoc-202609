The following is an example of questions that can be used to spur discussions about the governance practices of an IS system. The action plan for each block is presented in the third column.

| Block | Questions | Action plan |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | Do we have a security strategy aligned with business objectives? <br> What is the level of management’s understanding of security issues? <br> How involved is the security executive manager in developing business strategies? | Review the security strategy based on discussions that will take place between business managers and the chief information security officer (CISO) <br> Align security initiatives with business objectives |
| Policies | Do our security policies and guidelines correspond to the needs of the business units? <br> Do we have the means to monitor the effective application of our guidelines? <br> Do policies mention the responsibility of all the employees and the consequences of neglect? <br> Have we established a documentary framework for internal regulations? <br> Who is responsible for proposing adaptations to policies and guidelines? <br> What is our security policy validation process? | Review the documentary framework of the policies and guidelines and provide better readability |
