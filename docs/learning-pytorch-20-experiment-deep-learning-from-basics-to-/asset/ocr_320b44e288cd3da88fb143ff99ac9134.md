It's important to be informed that CNNs are not necessarily "better" than RNNs or GRUs, but they serve different purposes. While CNNs excel at spatial processing (like images), RNNs and GRUs are designed for temporal processing (like time-series data or language). The best architecture often depends on the nature and structure of the data you're working with. That said, there is overlap in their capabilities and sometimes you'll see them used together, like using a CNN to extract features from an image, which are then fed into an RNN for image captioning.
