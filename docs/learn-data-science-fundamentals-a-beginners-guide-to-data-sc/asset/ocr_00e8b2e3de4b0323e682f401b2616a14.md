# Challenges and Limitations

Despite its advantages, quantum cryptography faces several challenges:

+   * Distance Limitations: Quantum signals can degrade over long distances, limiting the range of secure communication. Current implementations often require repeaters or trusted nodes to extend the distance.

+   * Cost and Complexity: The technology and infrastructure required for quantum cryptography can be expensive and complex to implement, making widespread adoption challenging.

+   * Integration with Existing Systems: Integrating quantum cryptography with existing classical cryptographic systems and communication protocols requires careful consideration and development.
