Generating descriptive statistics is one of the first steps in EDA. Descriptive statistics summarize the main characteristics of the dataset, providing a quick overview of its structure. Key measures include:

+   - Mean: The average value of a dataset.
- Median: The middle value when the data is sorted.
- Mode: The most frequently occurring value.
- Standard Deviation: A measure of the amount of variation or dispersion in the dataset.
- Minimum and Maximum Values: The smallest and largest values in the dataset.

These statistics help in understanding the central tendency and variability of the data, guiding further analysis.

# Data Visualization

Data visualization techniques are essential for gaining insights into the distribution, patterns, and relationships within the data. Common visualization methods include:

+   - Histograms: Display the distribution of a single continuous variable, showing the frequency of data points within specified ranges (bins).
- Box Plots: Summarize the distribution of a dataset through its quartiles, highlighting the median, interquartile range, and potential outliers.
- Scatter Plots: Visualize the relationship between two continuous variables, allowing analysts to identify correlations and trends.
- Bar Charts: Represent categorical data, showing the frequency or proportion of each category.
- Heat Maps: Use color gradients to represent the magnitude of values in a matrix format, useful for visualizing correlations between variables.
