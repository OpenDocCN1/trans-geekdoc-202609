# 9. Data Exploration

Diving into subsets of the data based on specific conditions or segments can uncover patterns and insights. Techniques include:

+   * Segmentation: Analyzing different groups within the dataset to identify unique trends or behaviors.

+   * Time Series Analysis: Exploring data collected over time to identify trends, seasonal patterns, or anomalies.

# 10. Hypothesis Generation

EDA often leads to the formulation of initial hypotheses about relationships, patterns, or potential causality in the data. This process involves:

+   * Observation: Noting interesting patterns or trends during the analysis that may warrant further investigation.

+   * Guiding Further Analysis: Initial hypotheses can guide subsequent analyses, helping to focus on specific relationships or variables of interest.
