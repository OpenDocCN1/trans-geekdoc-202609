2. Permissioned Blockchains: Private or permissioned blockchains, where access is restricted to authorized parties, can help align with GDPR requirements by allowing for more control over data processing and management.

3. Encryption and Hashing: Techniques like encryption and hashing can be used to protect personal data stored on the blockchain, while still allowing for necessary processing and verification.

4. Decentralized Identity Management: Decentralized identity management systems built on blockchain can enable users to control their digital identities and selectively share personal information, aligning with GDPR principles.

5. Regulatory Sandboxes: Regulatory sandboxes, which provide a controlled environment for testing innovative technologies, can help explore and develop blockchain solutions that balance data privacy and regulatory compliance.
