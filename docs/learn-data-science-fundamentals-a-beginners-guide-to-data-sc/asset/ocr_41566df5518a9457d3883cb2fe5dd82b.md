# 12.2 Types of Anomalies and Detection Techniques

Anomaly detection is a critical aspect of data analysis, focusing on identifying data points that deviate significantly from expected patterns. Understanding the different types of anomalies is essential for selecting appropriate detection techniques.

# Types of Anomalies

+   1. Point Anomalies:
    - **Definition**: Point anomalies, also known as outliers, are individual data points that stand out from the rest of the dataset. These anomalies can indicate errors, rare events, or significant deviations from normal behavior.
    - **Examples**: In a credit card transaction dataset, a single transaction of an unusually high amount compared to typical spending patterns would be considered a point anomaly.
    - **Detection Techniques**
        - Statistical Methods: Techniques like Z-scores and the Interquartile Range (IQR) can identify point anomalies by measuring how far a data point deviates from the mean or median.
        - **Visualization**: Boxplots and scatterplots can visually highlight outliers by showing data distributions and relationships between variables.

+   2. Contextual Anomalies:
    - **Definition**: Contextual anomalies are data points that are considered anomalous within a specific context but may appear normal in another. These anomalies depend on the surrounding conditions or time frames.
