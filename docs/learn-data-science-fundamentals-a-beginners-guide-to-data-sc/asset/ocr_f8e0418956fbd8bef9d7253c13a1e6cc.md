+   - Tokenization: The process of breaking down text into individual words or phrases (tokens) for further analysis.

+   - Part-of-Speech Tagging: Assigning grammatical categories (nouns, verbs, adjectives, etc.) to each token in the text, which helps in understanding the structure and meaning of sentences.

+   - Named Entity Recognition (NER): Identifying and classifying named entities in text, such as people, organizations, and locations.

+   - Dependency Parsing: Analyzing the grammatical structure of sentences to understand the relationships between words.

+   - Sentiment Analysis: As mentioned earlier, this involves determining the sentiment expressed in text, which can provide valuable insights into customer opinions and emotions.

# Applications of Text Mining and NLP

Text mining and NLP have a wide range of applications across various industries:

+   - Healthcare: Analyzing clinical notes, research papers, and patient feedback to extract valuable insights for improving patient care and outcomes.

+   - Finance: Monitoring news articles, social media, and reports to assess market sentiment and inform investment decisions.

+   - Marketing: Analyzing customer reviews and feedback to understand consumer preferences and improve products and services.

+   - Customer Support: Automating responses to customer inquiries and analyzing support tickets to identify common issues.

+   - Social Media Analysis: Monitoring social media platforms to gauge public sentiment and track brand reputation.
