While Bayesian statistics offers many advantages, it also faces some limitations and criticisms:

+   1. **Sensitivity to Prior Distributions**: The choice of prior distribution can significantly impact the results, especially when data is limited. Careful prior elicitation and sensitivity analysis are necessary to assess the robustness of the conclusions.

+   2. **Computational Complexity**: For complex models, Bayesian analysis can be computationally intensive and time-consuming, requiring advanced techniques and software.

+   3. **Subjectivity**: The use of prior distributions in Bayesian analysis can be seen as introducing subjectivity into the analysis, which may be a concern in some scientific fields that value objectivity.

+   4. **Difficulty in Assessing Model Fit**: Assessing the fit of Bayesian models can be more challenging than in frequentist approaches, as traditional goodness-of-fit measures may not be directly applicable.
