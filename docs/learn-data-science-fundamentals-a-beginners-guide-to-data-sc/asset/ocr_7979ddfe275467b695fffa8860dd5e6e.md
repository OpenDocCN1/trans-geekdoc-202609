+   Weather Forecasting: Analyzing historical weather data to predict future weather conditions and climate changes.

+   Manufacturing: Monitoring production processes and equipment performance to optimize operations and reduce downtime.

# Challenges in Time Series Analysis

While time series analysis is a powerful tool, it comes with challenges:

+   Non-Stationarity: Many time series exhibit non-stationary behavior, meaning their statistical properties change over time. Techniques like differencing and detraining may be necessary to achieve stationarity.

+   Complex Seasonality: Some time series may have multiple seasonal patterns or irregular cycles, making it difficult to model accurately.

+   Outliers: Extreme values can distort analysis and forecasts. Identifying and addressing outliers is crucial for reliable results.

# 4.4 Survival Analysis in Data Science

Survival analysis is a statistical approach used to analyze the expected duration of time until one or more events occur, such as death, failure, or churn. This method is particularly useful in various fields, including medicine, engineering, social sciences, and business, as it provides insights into the timing of events and identifies factors that influence these events.
