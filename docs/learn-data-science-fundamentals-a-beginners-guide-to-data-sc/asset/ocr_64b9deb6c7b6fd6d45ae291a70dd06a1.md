# 8. Monitoring and Maintenance

+   - Updating the Model: As data becomes available or business conditions change, the model may need to be retrained or updated to maintain its relevance.

+   - Feedback Loops: Establishing mechanisms for collecting feedback from users and stakeholders helps in refining the model and improving its performance.
