2. **Transactions**: Transactions are the basic unit of information recorded on the blockchain. They represent the exchange of value or data between participants.

3. **Blocks**: Blocks are containers that store multiple transactions. When a block is full, it is added to the chain, creating a permanent record of the transactions.

4. **Consensus Mechanism**: The consensus mechanism is the protocol used by the network to validate transactions and add new blocks to the blockchain. It ensures that all nodes agree on the state of the ledger.

5. **Smart Contracts**: Smart contracts are self-executing contracts with the terms of the agreement directly written into code. They automatically execute when certain conditions are met, enabling automated and trust transactions.

# Types of Blockchain Networks

+   1. **Public Blockchains**: Public blockchains are open to anyone and do not require permission to participate. They are decentralized and transparent, with no single entity controlling the network.

+   2. **Private Blockchains**: Private blockchains are controlled by a single organization or entity. They have restricted access and are often used within a company or consortium to streamline internal processes.

+   3. **Consortium Blockchains**: Consortium blockchains are semi-centralized, with multiple organizations collaborating to maintain the network. The consensus process is controlled by a pre-selected set of nodes.

+   4. **Hybrid Blockchains**: Hybrid blockchains combine elements of both public and private blockchains. They allow for a mix of public and private transactions, providing flexibility and customization.
