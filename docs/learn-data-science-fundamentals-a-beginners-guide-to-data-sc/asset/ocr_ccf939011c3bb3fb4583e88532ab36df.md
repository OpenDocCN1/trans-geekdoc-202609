+   5. Patient Identity Management: Blockchain can provide patients with secure and portable digital identities, allowing them to control and share their personal health information with authorized providers

+   6. Insurance Claims Processing: Blockchain can automate and streamline insurance claims processing, reducing fraud and improving efficiency
