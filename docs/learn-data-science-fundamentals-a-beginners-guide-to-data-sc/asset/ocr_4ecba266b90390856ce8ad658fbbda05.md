+   Interpretability: Understanding how deep learning models make decisions is often difficult due to their complex structure.

# 6.5 Ensemble Learning Methods

Ensemble learning is a powerful technique in machine learning that combines multiple models to improve predictive performance. By leveraging the strengths of various individual models, ensemble methods can often outperform any single model alone. Here are some of the most common ensemble learning methods:

# 1. Bagging (Bootstrap Aggregating)

Bagging involves training multiple models independently on different subsets of the training data, obtained through bootstrap sampling. The final prediction is made by aggregating the predictions of all the individual models, either by majority voting (for classification) or averaging (for regression). Bagging helps reduce overfitting and variance in the models. Random Forest is a popular example of a bagging algorithm.
