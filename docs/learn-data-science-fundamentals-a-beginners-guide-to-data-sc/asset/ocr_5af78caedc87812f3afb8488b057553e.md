+   2. **Transparency**: Transparency involves making AI systems understandable and explainable to users and stakeholders. Organizations should disclose how AI models are trained, the data used, and the decision-making processes involved. This fosters trust and accountability.

+   3. **Privacy and Security**: Protecting user data is paramount, AI systems must comply with data protection regulations and implement robust security measures to safeguard sensitive information. Organizations should prioritize user privacy and obtain informed consent for data usage.

+   4. **Accountability**: There should be clear lines of accountability for AI systems and their outcomes. Organizations must ensure that individuals are responsible for the development and deployment of AI technologies, and that mechanisms are in place to address any negative impacts.

+   5. **Inclusiveness**: AI systems should empower all individuals and engage diverse populations. This involves considering the needs and perspectives of various stakeholders, particularly marginalized groups, during the design and implementation of AI solutions.

+   6. **Robustness and Safety**: AI systems should be reliable and perform safely under various conditions. Organizations must conduct through testing and validation to ensure that AI technologies function as intended and do not cause harm.
