+   **Credit Card Fraud Detection:** Anomaly detection systems analyze spending patterns to identify fraudulent transactions. For example, if a credit card is used for a large purchase in a foreign country shortly after a local transaction, it may indicate fraudulent activity. By flagging such anomalies, banks can prevent unauthorized charges and alert customers

+   **Insurance Fraud Detection:** Anomaly detection is employed to identify suspicious claims that deviate from normal patterns. For instance, a sudden spike in claims for similar injuries or unusual claim amounts can indicate fraudulent behavior. By analyzing historical data, insurers can detect and investigate potential fraud more effectively
