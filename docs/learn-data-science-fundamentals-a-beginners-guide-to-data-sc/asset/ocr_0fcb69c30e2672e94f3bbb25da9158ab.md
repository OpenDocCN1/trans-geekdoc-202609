Blockchain technology has a wide range of applications beyond cryptocurrency, including:

+   1. Supply Chain Management: Tracking the movement of goods and ensuring transparency and traceability.

+   2. Identity Management: Secure storage and management of digital identities, reducing the risk of identity theft.

+   3. Voting Systems: Enabling secure and transparent voting processes, reducing the risk of fraud.

+   4. Healthcare Data Management: Secure storage and sharing of medical records while maintaining patient privacy.

+   5. Real Estate: Streamlining property transactions and record-keeping.

+   6. Energy Trading: Enabling peer-to-peer energy trading and distribution.

+   7. Decentralized Finance (DeFi): Building financial applications and services on top of blockchain networks.

As blockchain technology continues to evolve, new applications use cases are emerging, transforming various industries and revolutionizing the way we interact with digital information.

# 13.2 Smart Contracts and Decentralized Applications (DApps)

Smart contracts are self-executing contracts with the terms of the agreement directly written into code. They operate on blockchain networks, allowing for automated transactions without the need for intermediaries. Smart contracts are designed to execute automatically when predefined conditions are met, ensuring transparency, security, and efficiency in various applications.

# Key Features of Smart Contracts

+   1. Automation: Smart contracts automate the execution of agreements, reducing the need for manual intervention and minimizing errors associated with traditional contract execution.
