+   **Video Games**: RL techniques are widely used in video game environments, where agents learn to navigate and achieve objectives through trial and error. For example, Deep Q-Networks (DQN) have been employed to train agents to play Atari games, enabling them to learn optimal strategies without human intervention

+   **Real-Time Strategy Games**: In complex real-time strategy games, RL agents can learn to manage resources, build units, and engage in combat by optimizing their decision-making processes based on the evolving game state

+   **Multi-Agent Systems**: RL is applied in scenarios involving multiple agents, such as competitive or cooperative games. Agents learn to adapt their strategies based on the actions of other players, leading to dynamic and intelligent gameplay
