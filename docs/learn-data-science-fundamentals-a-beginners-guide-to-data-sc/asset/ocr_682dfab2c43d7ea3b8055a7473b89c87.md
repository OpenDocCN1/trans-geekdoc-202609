+   2. Open Source: Many DApps are open source, allowing developers to collaborate, contribute, and audit the code, fostering transparency and trust.

Blockchain technology, with its decentralized nature and cryptographic security features, offers unique opportunities for enhancing data privacy and security. However, the inherent transparency of blockchains also presents challenges in reconciling the technology with data protection regulations like the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
