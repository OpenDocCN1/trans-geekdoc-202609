# Chapter 2: AI and Psychology

In recent years, AI has been utilized in psychology to enhance our understanding of the human mind, through cognitive modeling, natural language processing and neural networks. These methods have the potential to revolutionize the field of psychology, providing new perspectives and insights into the workings of the human mind. In this essay, we will delve into the various ways in which AI is being used in psychology, and the potential implications of these advancements.
