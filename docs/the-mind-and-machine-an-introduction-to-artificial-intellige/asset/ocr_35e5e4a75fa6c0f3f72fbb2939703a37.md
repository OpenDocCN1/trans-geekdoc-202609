# Chapter 5: Cognitive Modelling

Cognitive modeling is the use of computational models to simulate and understand human cognitive processes. The goal of cognitive modeling is to create computational models that can replicate human behavior, and to use these models to generate predictions and explanations about human cognition. The field of cognitive modeling has seen significant advancements in recent years, with the incorporation of AI driving the development of new and innovative models. This essay will explore the use of AI in modeling cognitive processes, such as decision making, problem solving, and memory.
