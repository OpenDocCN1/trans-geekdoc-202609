Another important reference used throughout the course was the book "Deep Learning" by Yoshua Bengio, Ian Goodfellow, and Aaron Courville. This book provides a comprehensive introduction to deep learning, one of the most important subfields of AI. It covers everything from the basics of neural networks to the latest research in the field, including convolutional neural networks and recurrent neural networks. This book is considered a key reference for understanding the field of deep learning and its potential applications.
