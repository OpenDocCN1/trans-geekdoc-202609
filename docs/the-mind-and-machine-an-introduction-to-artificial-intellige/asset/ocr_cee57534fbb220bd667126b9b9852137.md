Neural networks, a type of machine learning algorithm modeled after the structure and function of the human brain, are also being used in psychology. Neural networks have been used to model a wide range of cognitive phenomena, including perception, attention, and memory. One example of a neural network model in psychology is the use of deep learning algorithms to analyze imaging data, such as fMRI scans, to understand patterns of brain activity associated with different cognitive processes.
