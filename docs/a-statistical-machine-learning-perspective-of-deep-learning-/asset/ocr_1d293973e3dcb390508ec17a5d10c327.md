+     - with implicit distributions and black-box NN models
+     - just swap the symbols $x$ and $z$

$z \sim p_{prior}(z)$
$x \sim f_{black-box}(z)$

$x \sim p_{data}(x)$
$z \sim f'_{black-box}(x)$
