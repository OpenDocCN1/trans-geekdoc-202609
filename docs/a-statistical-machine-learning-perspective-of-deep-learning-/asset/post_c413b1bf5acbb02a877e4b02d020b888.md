## Recap: conventional formulation of VAEs

- Objective:
  $$
  \max_{\theta,\eta} \mathcal{L}^{\text{vae}}_{\theta,\eta} = \mathbb{E}_{p_{data}(x)} \big[ \mathbb{E}_{\tilde{q}_\eta(z|x)} [\log \tilde{p}_\theta(x|z)] - \mathrm{KL}(\tilde{q}_\eta(z|x) \parallel \tilde{p}(z)) \big]
  $$
  - \(\tilde{p}(z)\): prior over \(z\)
  - \(\tilde{p}_\theta(x|z)\): generative model
  - \(\tilde{q}_\eta(z|x)\): inference model
  - Only uses real examples from \(p_{data}(x)\), lacks adversarial mechanism
- To align with GANs, let's introduce the real/fake indicator \(y\) and adversarial discriminator

## GANs vs VAEs side by side

|  | GANs (InfoGAN) | VAEs |
|---|---|---|
| Generative distribution | \(p_\theta(x\mid y)=\begin{cases}p_{g_\theta}(x)&y=0\\p_{data}(x)&y=1.\end{cases}\) | \(p_\theta(x\mid z,y)=\begin{cases}p_\theta(x\mid z)&y=0\\p_{data}(x)&y=1.\end{cases}\) |
| Discriminator distribution | \(q_\phi(y\mid x)\) | \(q_*(y\mid x)\), perfect, degenerated |
| \(z\)-inference model | \(q_\eta(z\mid x,y)\) of InfoGAN | \(q_\eta(z\mid x,y)\) |
| KLD to minimize | \(\min_\theta \mathrm{KL}\left(p_\theta(x\mid y)\parallel q^r(x\mid z,y)\right)\)<br>\(\sim \min_\theta \mathrm{KL}(P_\theta\parallel Q)\) | \(\min_\theta \mathrm{KL}\left(q_\eta(z\mid x,y)q_*(y\mid x)\parallel p_\theta(z,y\mid x)\right)\)<br>\(\sim \min_\theta \mathrm{KL}(Q\parallel P_\theta)\) |

## VAEs vs. Wake-sleep

- Wake-sleep algorithm
  - Wake: \(\max_{\theta} \mathbb{E}_{q_{\lambda}(h|x)p_{data}(x)} [\log p_{\theta}(x|h)]\)
  - Sleep: \(\max_{\lambda} \mathbb{E}_{p_{\theta}(x|h)p(h)} [\log q_{\lambda}(h|x)]\)
- Let \(h\) be \(z\), and \(\lambda\) be \(\eta\)
  - \(\Rightarrow \max_{\theta} \mathbb{E}_{q_{\eta}(z|x)p_{data}(x)} [\log p_{\theta}(x|z)]\), recovers VAE objective of optimizing \(\theta\)
- VAEs extend wake phase by also learning the inference model (\(\eta\))
  $$
  \max_{\theta,\eta} \mathcal{L}^{vae}_{\theta,\eta} = \mathbb{E}_{q_{\eta}(z|x)p_{data}(x)} [\log p_{\theta}(x|z)] - \mathbb{E}_{p_{data}(x)} [\mathrm{KL}(q_{\eta}(z|x)\parallel p(z))]
  $$
  - Minimize the KLD in the original variational free energy w.r.t. \(\eta\)
  - Stick to minimizing the wake-phase KLD w.r.t. both \(\theta\) and \(\eta\)
  - Do not involve sleep-phase objective
  - Recall: sleep phase minimizes the *reverse* KLD in the variational free energy

## GANs vs. Wake-sleep

- Wake-sleep algorithm
  - Wake: \(\max_\theta \mathbb{E}_{q_\lambda(h|x)p_{data}(x)} [\log p_\theta(x|h)]\)
  - Sleep: \(\max_\lambda \mathbb{E}_{p_\theta(x|h)p(h)} [\log q_\lambda(h|x)]\)
- Let \(h\) be \(y\), and \(\lambda\) be \(\phi\)
  - \(\Rightarrow \max_\phi \mathbb{E}_{p_\theta(x|y)p(y)} [\log q_\phi(y|x)]\), recovers GAN objective of optimizing \(\phi\)
- GANs extend sleep phase by also learning the generative model (\(\theta\))
  - Directly extending sleep phase: \(\max_\phi \mathcal{L}_\phi = \mathbb{E}_{p_\theta(x|y)p(y)} [\log q_\phi(y|x)]\)
  - GANs: \(\max_\theta \mathcal{L}_\theta = \mathbb{E}_{p_\theta(x|y)p(y)} [\log q_\phi^r(y|x)]\)
  - The only difference is replacing \(q_\phi\) with \(q_\phi^r\)
  - This is where the adversarial mechanism comes about!
  - GANs stick to minimizing the sleep-phase KLD
  - Do not involve the wake-phase objective

## Mutual exchanges of ideas: augment the loss functions

|  | GANs (InfoGAN) | VAEs |
|---|---|---|
| KLD to minimize | \(\min_{\theta} \mathrm{KL}\left(p_{\theta}(\boldsymbol{x}|y) \parallel q^{r}(\boldsymbol{x}|\mathbf{z},y)\right)\)<br>\(\sim \min_{\theta}\mathrm{KL}(P_{\theta} \parallel Q)\) | \(\min_{\theta}\mathrm{KL}\left(q_{\eta}(\mathbf{z}|\boldsymbol{x},y)q_{*}^{r}(y|\boldsymbol{x}) \parallel p_{\theta}(\mathbf{z},y|\boldsymbol{x})\right)\)<br>\(\sim \min_{\theta}\mathrm{KL}(Q \parallel P_{\theta})\) |

- Asymmetry of KLDs inspires combination of GANs and VAEs
  - GANs: \(\min_{\theta}\mathrm{KL}(P_{\theta}\parallel Q)\) tends to missing mode
  - VAEs: \(\min_{\theta}\mathrm{KL}(Q\parallel P_{\theta})\) tends to cover regions with small values of \(p_{data}\)

## AAVAE: adaptive data selection

![](img/cb490ed30bca4eda5fbba81c52a2c173_168_0.png)

$$
\max_{\theta,\eta} \mathcal{L}^{aavae}_{\theta,\eta} = \mathbb{E}_{p_{\theta_0}(x)} \left[ \mathbb{E}_{q_\eta(z|x,y) q_\phi^r(y|x)} [\log p_\theta(x|z,y)] - \mathrm{KL}(q_\eta(z|x,y) q_\phi^r(y|x) \parallel p(z|y) p(y)) \right]
$$

- An effective data selection mechanism:
  - Both generated samples and real examples are weighted by \(q_\phi^r(y=0|x) = q_\phi(y=1|x)\)
  - Only samples that resemble real data and fool the discriminator will be used for training
  - A real example receiving large weight \(q_\phi^r(y|x)\)
    - \(\Rightarrow\) Easily recognized by the discriminator as real
    - \(\Rightarrow\) Hard to be simulated from the generator
    - \(\Rightarrow\) Hard examples get larger weights

## Mutual exchanges of ideas

- AAVAE enhances VAEs with ideas from GANs
- We can also enhance GANs with ideas from VAEs
- VAEs maximize a variational lower bound of log likelihood
- Importance weighted VAE (IWAE) [Burda et al., 2016]
  - Maximizes a tighter lower bound through importance sampling
- The variational inference interpretation of GANs allows the importance weighting method to be straightforwardly applied to GANs
  - Just copy the derivations of IWAE side by side with little adaptions!

## Recap: Variational Inference

Maximize the variational lower bound \(\mathcal{L}(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\phi}; \boldsymbol{x})\), or equivalently, minimize free energy

$$
F(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\phi}; \boldsymbol{x}) = -\log p(\boldsymbol{x}) + \mathrm{KL}(q_{\phi}(\boldsymbol{z}|\boldsymbol{x}) \parallel p_{\theta}(\boldsymbol{z}|\boldsymbol{x}))
$$

- E-step: maximize \(\mathcal{L}\) w.r.t. \(\phi\) with \(\boldsymbol{\theta}\) fixed
  $$
  \max_{\phi}\mathcal{L}(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\phi}; \boldsymbol{x}) = \mathbb{E}_{q_{\phi}(\boldsymbol{z}|\boldsymbol{x})}[\log p_{\theta}(x|\boldsymbol{z})] - \mathrm{KL}(q_{\phi}(\boldsymbol{z}|x)\parallel p(z))
  $$
  - If closed-form solutions exist:
    $$
    q_{\phi}^*(z|x) \propto \exp[\log p_{\theta}(x,z)]
    $$
- M-step: maximize \(\mathcal{L}\) w.r.t. \(\boldsymbol{\theta}\) with \(\phi\) fixed
  $$
  \max_{\theta}\mathcal{L}(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \boldsymbol{\phi}; \boldsymbol{x}) = \mathbb{E}_{q_{\phi}(\boldsymbol{z}|\boldsymbol{x})}[\log p_{\theta}(x|\boldsymbol{z})] - \mathrm{KL}(q_{\phi}(\boldsymbol{z}|x)\parallel p(z))
  $$
