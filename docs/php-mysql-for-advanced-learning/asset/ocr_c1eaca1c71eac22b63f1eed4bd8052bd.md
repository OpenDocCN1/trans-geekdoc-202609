# # About Author

Hirdesh Bhardwaj, born on August 15, 1987, at Aligarh, India. He is well-known for his best website/ software scripting and has nominated for the Oracle magazine “TOP IT Youngsters of India” in 2008.

In 2005, He completed his school education and moved to Gurgaon, Haryana for the higher education. He completed Diploma in Software Engineering from NIIT, Gurgaon in 2008.and Msc.IT from Karnataka University in 2010.

Hirdesh Bhardwaj got interested in writing while a freshman in high school. He wrote a Hindi poem “21st Century”, later published in June 2009.

In 2009 he joined The Ardee Group, as a developer and IT head. Further he build Library Management System, Student management System, MLM System, Online Survey and many Applications using PHP/ Mysql.

Besides books, Hirdesh has about 250 scripts and tutorials will be publishing in upcoming years.

In 2009 He founded “Webs Jyoti”- Development & Training Organization. Webs Jyoti has developed about 350+ Websites and web applications.
