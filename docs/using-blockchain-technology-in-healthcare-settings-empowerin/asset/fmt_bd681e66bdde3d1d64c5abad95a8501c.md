## 12.1 Introduction to Halal health care and blockchain technology

### 12.1.1 Defining Halal health care

Halal health care represents a significant evolution in the healthcare sector, integrating Islamic teachings into various aspects of medical care and product development \[[1](#index_split_019.html#filepos1014824)\]. This innovative approach extends beyond the compliance with Islamic dietary laws, encompassing a broader spectrum of ethical, moral, and spiritual values rooted in Islam. It is about providing a healthcare system that is not only physically beneficial but also spiritually uplifting for Muslim patients. This holistic care model is particularly significant given the growing global Muslim population, which is actively seeking healthcare solutions that resonate with their religious and cultural values. Halal health care, therefore, plays a crucial role in meeting these specific needs, ensuring that medical treatments, pharmaceuticals, and healthcare practices do not contravene Islamic principles. \[[2](#index_split_019.html#filepos1015326)\] emphasizes the importance of this approach in modern health care, acknowledging the need for systems and solutions that are both innovative and faith aligned.

### 12.1.2 Unlocking the potential: The importance of blockchain technology

#### 12.1.2.1 Trust with immutability

Trust is the mutual reliance and expectation between partners that they can depend on one another to behave in a predictable and reasonable manner \[[2](#index_split_019.html#filepos1015326)\]. Trust is a fundamental attribute of blockchain technology \[[3](#index_split_019.html#filepos1015788)\]. Blockchain’s immutability fortifies the cornerstone of successful partnerships—trust. It ensures an unchangeable recording of transactions using cryptographic techniques and hash values, preventing the spread of false information and self-regulating agents’ behavior without requiring central authorities \[[4](#index_split_019.html#filepos1016247)\]. Immutability guarantees that once data is stored, it cannot be modified or altered, thereby enhancing security and data integrity.
