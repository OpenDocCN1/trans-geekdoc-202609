Using the expressions, in Table 2.21, we obtain thermal efficiency of Otto engines as

$$\eta_{\text{otto}} = 1 - \frac{Q_C}{Q_H} = 1 - \frac{T_4 - T_1}{T_3 - T_2} = 1 - \frac{1}{(V_1/V_2)^{\gamma-1}}. \tag{2.174}$$

**Table 2.21.** The four steps of one Otto-engine cycle with $\gamma = c_P / c_V$.

| Step | Process | $T$ | $V$, $Q$ |
|------|---------|-----|----------|
| ● 1 → 2 | isentropic compression | $T_1 \to T_2$ | $V_1 \to V_2$, $Q_{12}=0$<br>$T_1 = T_2 (V_2/V_1)^{\gamma-1}$ |
| ● 2 → 3 | isochoric heating | $T_2 \to T_3$ | $V_2 = V_3$, $Q_{23}=Q_H$<br>$Q_{23}=c_V (T_3 - T_2)$ |
| ● 3 → 4 | isentropic compression | $T_3 \to T_4$ | $V_3 \to V_4$, $Q_{34}=0$<br>$T_3 = T_4 (V_3/V_4)^{\gamma-1}$ |
| ● 4 → 1 | isochoric rejection of heat | $T_4 \to T_1$ | $V_4 = V_1$, $Q_{41}=Q_C$<br>$Q_{41}=c_V (T_4 - T_1)$ |
