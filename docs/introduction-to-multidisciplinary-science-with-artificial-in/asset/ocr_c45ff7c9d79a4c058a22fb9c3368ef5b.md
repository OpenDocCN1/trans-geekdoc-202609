electromagnetic background on calculation of electrical potential and magnetic fields around an inhomogeneous body like the brain.



The brain radius is about 0.25 m whereas the wavelength of electrochemical signals is about 500 m—that is more than 2000 times the brain radius. Therefore the use of Maxwell's quasi-static equations in Chapter 3 is justified. Also, there are two current densities involved in this problem.

The resulting current arises from the bioelectric activity of nerve and muscle cells due to the conversion of energy from chemical to electric form. This current is nonconservative and we denote it J^{(a)}=J^{(a)}(x,t), where x is the point at which the field is evaluated. It is responsible for the electric field, E=E(x,t), and the second current, σE, where σ is the electric conductivity of the brain, is the result of an applied field in a resistive material, such as the brain. So the total current is [see Chapter 5 of Ikelle (2023a) and Chapter 12 of Ikelle (2020)]

```
J = J^{(a)} + σE ⇌ J = J^{(a)} - σ∇ϕ ,
E=-∇ϕ
```

where ϕ=ϕ(x,t) is the scalar potential. We also used the fact that E=-∇ϕ. Using ∇·J=0 in Chapter 3, we can deduce that

```
∇·J^{(a)}=σ∇²ϕ ⇒ ϕ = -1/(4πσ)∫_V (1/|x|)∇·J^{(a)}dV
=1/(4πσ)∫_V J^{(a)}·∇(1/|x|)dV. (5.7)
```

The first equation here allows us to compute the electric potential under the assumption that brain conductivity is smooth, but it is not because of the various regions in the brain, as illustrated in Figure 5.1. To account the regions, we divide the brain into a finite number of homogeneous regions. We denote the boundaries of these regions as S_m. At these boundaries both the electric potential ϕ and the normal component of the current density must be continuous:

```
ϕ^{(-)}(S_m)=ϕ^{(+)}(S_m)
and
σ_m^{(-)}∇ϕ^{(-)}(S_m)·n_m=σ_m^{(+)}∇ϕ^{(+)}(S_m)·n_m, (5.8)
```

where the superscripts (−) and (+) represent the opposite sides of the boundary and n_m is the normal vector to S_m directed from the (−) region to the (+) one. If we define ψ=1/|x|, according to Green's theorem (Smyth, 1968), we have

```
∫_{S_m}[σ_m^{(-)}(ψ^{(-)}∇ϕ^{(-)}−ψ^{(-)}∇ϕ^{(-)})−σ_m^{(+)}(ψ^{(+)}∇ϕ^{(+)}−ψ^{(+)}∇ϕ^{(+)})]·n_m dS_m=∫_{V_m}[ψ∇·(σ_j∇ϕ)−ϕ∇·(σ_j∇ψ)]dV_m. (5.9)
```
