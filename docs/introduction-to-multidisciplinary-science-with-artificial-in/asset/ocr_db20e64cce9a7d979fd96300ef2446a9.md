Figure 6.3. (a) The overall structure of the Sun. (b) The restless Sun with t3 > t2 > t1 > t0. Snapshots of an eruption on the sun that produced a magnetic display known as coronal rain. Magnetic fields are invisible, but charged plasma is forced to move along the lines. This shows up brightly at the extreme ultraviolet wavelength of 30 nanometers, and outlines the fields as it slowly falls back to the solar surface. (b1) The pre-eruption state of the active region. (b2) and (b3) formation of the coronal loop and the expanding plasma structure. (b4) After eruption. Source: NASA images.
