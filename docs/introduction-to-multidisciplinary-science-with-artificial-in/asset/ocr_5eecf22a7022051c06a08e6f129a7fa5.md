The diesel-engine cycle operates on a cycle similar to the Otto cycle without a spark plug. The fuel is injected into the cylinder at the end of the compression stroke, thus avoiding spontaneous ignition of the fuel-air mixture during the compression stroke. The combustion occurs spontaneously without a spark plug along the constant pressure segment (2 → 3) after fuel injection.
