As illustrated in Figure 4.21, a one-qubit QFT is like a 90 degree rotation of one basis to another. For multi-qubit systems, the tensor products also include rotation gates (also known as controlled phase shift gates) in addition to the Hadamard gate. The rotation gates can be written as follows:

```
R_k = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & \exp(2\pi i / 2^k) \end{bmatrix} (4.115)
```

```
R_k |x_j⟩ = \exp(2\pi i x_j / 2^k ) |x_j⟩ . (4.116)
```
