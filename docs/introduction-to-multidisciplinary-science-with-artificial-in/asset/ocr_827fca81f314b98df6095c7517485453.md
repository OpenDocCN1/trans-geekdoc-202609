Because of the greenhouse effect, we have 0 < ε < 1, which means that the actual effective temperature of a given ε is greater than the temperature of the Earth without the atmosphere (i.e., warmer than 255 degrees Kelvin) and smaller than the temperature of the Earth with an opaque atmosphere (i.e., smaller than 300 degrees Kelvin).
