Let us now consider the non-causal action of the source. These integrations can be written as

$\nabla \cdot D(\mathbf{x}, t) = \int_{-\infty}^{t} \left[ \frac{\partial [\nabla \cdot D(\mathbf{x}, t')]}{\partial t'} \right] dt'$
