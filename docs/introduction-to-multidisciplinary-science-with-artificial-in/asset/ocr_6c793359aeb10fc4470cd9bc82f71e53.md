What are the differences between Navier-Stokes and MHD equations?

Maxwell equations concerns electromagnetic fields. Navier (or Navier Stokes) equations concern fluid flow. Magnetohydrodynamics (MHD) is a combination of the two which considers the electromagnetic and fluid behavior of a conductive fluid like a plasma. While Navier Stokes has 5 equations (density, momentum, energy), ideal MHD has 8 adding the time evolution of the magnetic field (a reduced form of Faraday's law).
