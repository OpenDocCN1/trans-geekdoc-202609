# 2.4.2. Bioheat Transfer Equations: Thermal Diagnostics and Therapies

**Physical model of the human body.** Before we dive into the heat-transfer equations and their solutions, it is useful to recall that a human body can be conceptualized as the multi-segmental model (Fiala, 1999), as illustrated in Figure 2.42. The segments are generally depicted as cylinders, except the head, which is described as a sphere. The segments representing shoulders, legs, thighs, arms, and forearms consist of, from inside to outside, bone, muscle, fat tissue, and skin layers. The head, thorax, and abdomen segments also include the brain, lungs, and viscera, respectively, as illustrated in Table 2.15. Because the head is described as a sphere, the layer radii are re-sized to keep the head volume constant.
