For an electric field of pure dipole (i.e., the monopole moment is zero) the dipole term becomes the leading-order term; i.e.,

```
Q = ∫_V ρ_E(x) dV(x) = 0 ⟹ φ_E ≈ (k / |x|²) p · i_r . (3.115)
```

For example, if the dipole is located at the origin and oriented along the z-axis (i.e., p = p_m i_z), (3.115) reduces to

```
φ_E = k_ε (p_m i_z · i_r) / |x|² = k_ε p_m cosθ / |x|² . (3.116)
```
