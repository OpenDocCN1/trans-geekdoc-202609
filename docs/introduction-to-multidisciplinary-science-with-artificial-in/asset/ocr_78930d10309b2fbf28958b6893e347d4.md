One may wonder about Titan’s atmosphere being flammable because of its hydrocarbon content. Carbon and hydrogen atoms are very stable molecules. One part carbon and four parts hydrogen (i.e., methane) is one of the most stable compounds we can make. Oxygen gases are the real cause of methane’s flammability on Earth. Oxygen is responsible for the burning of methane and other hydrocarbons as well as the rusting of metals and some rocks. Actually, nothing in our world would work without oxygen driving the reactions including most living things as described in Ikelle (2023a and 2023b). Titan is an entire other world. Titan’s atmosphere contains no oxygen gas, only tiny amounts of CO, CO₂, and H₂O. It is therefore impossible to start an inferno on Titan except to send a tank filled with oxygen into the planet. A nitrogen-methane atmosphere is equally stable as Earth’s nitrogen-oxygen atmosphere.
