# The properties and observables of accretion disks.

Infalling matter powers the central gravitating object with its gravitational energy. This process includes the gradual loss of angular momentum by infalling matter. This allows matter to progressively move inwards, toward the central gravitating object. Therefore, infalling matter on the disk follows a round path until it loses angular momentum before falling into the central object. As material falls into the central object, gravitational potential energy is released. This energy release heats the plasma. The hot plasma radiates away the energy, proving an observational signature of accretion. Note that black holes themselves do not shine, but accretion disks do.
