Let us briefly describe the key features of gravimeters. Modern gravimeters are designed to measure the free fall of a test mass (see Figure 1.26a). The free-fall motion is carried out in a vacuum to avoid air resistance (see Figure 1.26b). The parameters of this motion measured by components of the gravimeters are the pair of heights and times (z_i, t'_i) along the vertical axis, as illustrated in Figure 1.26, with i taking values from 0 to, say, 1,000 at a distance of about 20 cm and a time of about 0.2 seconds. Laser interferometers are used to determine (z_i, t'_i). The gravity is deduced from these pairs.
