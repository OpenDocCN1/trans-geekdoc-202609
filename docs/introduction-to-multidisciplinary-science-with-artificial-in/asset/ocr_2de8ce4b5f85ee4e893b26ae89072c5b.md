One additional weakness of current exoplanet search methods is their inability to detect exomons alongside exoplanets. # 6.6.2. Why is the Exoplanet Radius Distribution Bimodal?

Bimodal distributions have two peaks. In a continuous probability distribution, modes are peaks in the distribution. Exoplanet’s radii follow a bimodal distribution as shown in figure 6.55. The highest apex, known as the major mode, is at 1.3 r_earth and the lower, known as the minor mode, is at 13.6 r_earth. The first study with two peaks was made by Fulton et al. (2017).
