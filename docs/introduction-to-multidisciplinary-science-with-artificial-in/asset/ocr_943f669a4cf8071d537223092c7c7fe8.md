**Quiz:** Let us consider that B = [0,0,Bz)]^T and E = [Ex,0,0]^T. Verify that the drift velocity of the particle with charge q is v_E = [0,-Ex/Bz,0]^T, which means that this drift is perpendicular to both E and B. Verify also that

v_y = ± sin( (|q|Bz)/m + φ0 ) - Ex/Bz.  (6.34)

The particle guiding center drifts in the direction of -i2 (i.e., -y direction). So add an electric field (perpendicular to B) and the plasma starts to drift perpendicular to E and B (E × B drift).

Nonuniform magnetic fields have different effects on the dynamics of charged particles. For instance, a charged particle in a magnetic field with a nonzero gradient undergoes the so-called grad-B drift (Baumjohann and Treumann, 1997). Charged particles moving along curved magnetic field lines experience a centrifugal force perpendicular to the magnetic field. Hence charged particles experience drift in their motion. This kind of drift is known as curvature drift (Baumjohann and Treumann, 1997). There are many other drifts associated with the motion of charged particles in nonuniform magnetic fields. We will concentrate on grad-B drift and curvature drift.
