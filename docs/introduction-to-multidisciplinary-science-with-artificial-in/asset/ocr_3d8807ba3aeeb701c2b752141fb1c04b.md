In other words, most of our galaxy’s mass is invisible to us and even to the entire electromagnetic spectrum, as we know it today—that is, *neutrons and protons of which living things, Earth, and the stars are made of, do not account for most of the mass in our Milky Way, and in our entire Universe.* It is our objective here to prove the existence of this mass.
