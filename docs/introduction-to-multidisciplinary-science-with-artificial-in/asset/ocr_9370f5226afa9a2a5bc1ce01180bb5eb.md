The differences between the near and far sides of the Moon suggest that the Moon was formed from mixtures of matter of two totally different compositions. Perhaps, Earth may have shielded the near side from a significant amount of bombardments. As a result, their differences may be caused by materials deposited on the far side by colliding objects.
