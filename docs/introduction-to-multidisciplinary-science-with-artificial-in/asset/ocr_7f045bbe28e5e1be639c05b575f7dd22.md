# *Is Jupiter a brown dwarf star?*

Brown dwarfs are failed stars of mass less than 80 m_Jupiter but more than at least 13 m_Jupiter—that is, the mass of Jupiter is less than 0.075 m_Brown−dwarf. Jupiter is too small to be a brown dwarf star and even too small for a gas giant planet as described in the main text. Moreover, Jupiter is too cold to be a brown dwarf. In other words, if Jupiter were a brown dwarf, it would be hotter and brighter than it is now.
