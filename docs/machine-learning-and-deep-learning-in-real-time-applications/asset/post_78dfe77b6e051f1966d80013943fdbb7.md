# About the Contributors

**Mehul Mahrishi** is currently working as an Associate Professor in the faculty of computer Science & Engineering at Swami Kesvanand Institute of Technology, Management and Jaipur – India. He is a Professional Member of IEEE Delhi Section, Life Member of Institution of Engineers, India (L-IEI) and Life member of Indian contingent of BRICS that travelled to Moscow, Russia to attend Russian Energy Week-2018 and BRICS Youth Summit. He has published more than 20 research papers in national and international Journals including 19th SoCTA, 3rd ISDA, 3rd International National Conference on Machine Learning and Computing (IMCLC 2011), 3rd IACC 2013 and chapters in books. He has also participated in a range of forums by Infosys, TCS, and WIPRO-Mission 10 X, IBM etc. His research activities are twofold: the first research activity is to explore the developmental enhancements in applications in computer vision; the second major research theme focused on the emerging capabilities of parallel and cloud computing.

**Kamal Hiran** is an Innovative and Enthusiastic Academic Professional having more than 14 years of experience in Academic Administration, Teaching and Research experience in India, Europe, and Africa. The editor has worked on various academic positions as Lecturer, Sr. Lecturer, Asst. Professor, Associate Professor, Head IT, Head Academics, Founder, IEEE Liberia, IEEE Ghana & Professional Activity Chair, IEEE SB Coordinator. The editor has the tie-up with Academic-Industry collaboration as part of jobs such as Turnitin (Plagiarism Checker), Founder IEEE Student Branch, Ghana, and Oracle WDP. The editor has published and presented research papers in peer-reviewed journals as well as international conferences in India, Denmark, USA, Germany, Jordan, Ghana, and Ethiopia. Working as a Reviewer and Member in the Program Committee in the International Journals: Journal of Medical and Biological Engineering (JMBE), Springer, Germany, IJCA USA, IGI-Global USA, and IJERT, India.
