# Chapter 5

Employee's Attrition Prediction Using Machine Learning Approaches............................................... 121
Krishna Kumar Mohbey, Department of Computer Science, Central University of Rajasthan,
India

In any industry, attrition is a big problem, whether it is about employee attrition of an organization or customer attrition of an e-commerce site. If we can accurately predict which customer or employee leave their current company or organization, it will save much time, effort, and cost of the employee and help them to hire or acquire substitutes in advance, and it would not create a problem in the ongoing process of attrition. In this chapter, a comparative analysis between various machine learning approaches such as Naive Bayes, decision tree, random forest, and logistic regression is presented. The presented result will help us in identifying the behavior of employees who could be attracted over the next time. Experimental results reveal that the logistic regression approach can reach up to 86% accuracy over other machine learning approaches.

Computational biology is the research area that connects to the analysis of biological information. The selection of the subset of cancer-related genes is one amongst the most promising clinical research of gene expression data. Since a gene can take the role of various biological pathways that turn active only under specific experimental conditions, the stacked denoising auto-encoder(SDAE) and the genetic algorithm were combined to perform clustering of cancer genes from huge microarray gene expression data. The Genetic-SDAE proved superior to recently proposed clustering methods and better to determine the maximum similarity of a set of clusters of gene expression data with lower MSR and higher gene variance. This work also assesses the results with respect to the discovered genes and that the extracted clusters are supported by biological evidence, as enrichment of gene functions and biological processes.
