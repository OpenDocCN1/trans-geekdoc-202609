# Deep Learning in Engineering Education

Discriminative models are used for modelling dependency of unobserved (target) variable Y on observed variables X whereas the generative models are used for learning the probability distribution. The generative model learns the full relationship between input X (features) and label Y giving maximum flexibility at the time of testing. Discriminative models learn from the only X to predict Y using conditional probability. By using few assumptions these models can use existing data more efficiently. CNN, deep neural network and recurrent neural network are Discriminative models and DNN, restricted Boltzmann machine, and regularized autoencoders are generative models. Hybrid models are a combination of discriminative and generative models.

Also, the review of articles suggests that Deep Learning algorithm with other generalised algorithms may give better results in classification and prediction tasks. Through the survey, it is observed that hybrid models are more popular than plain DL algorithm based models (Vora & Iyer, A Survey of Inferences from Deep Learning Algorithms, 2017). In many applications dimensionality reduction is used to reduce the features and then DL algorithms are proved to improve accuracy.

# Educational Data Mining

EDM is a popular research area and an amount of research articles are available for study. These research articles identify the experimentation and algorithms used in EDM for performing various tasks. For the performance prediction, new techniques and ML algorithms have experimented. There are many factors or features which have a significant effect in predicting the performance of the students. These factors are classified as cognitive and non-cognitive factors. Non-cognitive factors play an important role in variety EDM goals.

Wattana & Ranbir (Punjumek & Rachberry, 2015,October) used various techniques like K-Nearest Neighbourhood, Naive Bayes, and Neural Network to classify the students'. The features considered were very many and attributes were related to marks of students.

Norlida, Usamah & Puaziah (Buniyamin, M., & Arshad, 2015, November) used Neuro Fuzzy algorithms to predict the performance of the engineering student. Here only 6 logistic parameters are used for prediction.

Camilo, Elizabeth & Fabio (Guarin, Guzmán, & González, 2015) used Decision tree and Bayesian Classifier for prediction of students' performance. Admission test score and academic information were used for prediction. In addition, socio-economic parameters were also used for prediction. The major stress was on the admission parameters.
