# BIOLOGICAL VALIDATION OF BICUSTERS

Biological validation can qualitatively evaluate the extraction of an algorithm to extract meaningful bicusters from a biological point of viewTang et al. (2002); Yang et al. (2011). If the discovered bicusters contain significant proportion of biologically similar genes, it proves that the clustering method produces biologically relevant results. The biological significance can be verified using Gene Ontology (GO) database. The enriched annotation terms associated with the gene list will give insight that investigators to understand the biological themes beyond the gene list. The GO project has developed three structured ontologies that describe gene products in their associated biological processes, cellular components and molecular functions in species- independent manner. The degree of enrichment is measured by p-values which use a cumulative hyper metric distribution to compute the probability of observing the number of genes from a particular GO category (function, process and component) within each bicuster. A bicuster is said to be over represented in a functional category if the p-value is below a certain threshold. The probability $p$ for finding at least $k$ genes from a particular category within a cluster of size $m$ is given in Equation (6.1).

```
$p = 1 - \sum_{r=0}^{k-1} \binom{\begin{matrix} M-G \ m-r \end{matrix}}{r} (1)
```

Where $G$ is the total number of genes within a category and $M$ is the total number of genes within the genome. The p-value is the probability that the genes are selected into the cluster by random. When the p-value is very small, it means that the majority of genes in a cluster appears in one category.
