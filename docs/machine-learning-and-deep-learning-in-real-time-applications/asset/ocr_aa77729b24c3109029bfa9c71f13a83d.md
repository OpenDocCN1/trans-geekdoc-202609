A two-dimensional gene expression matrix M with n columns and m rows, it consists of the expression level of m genes G = [G1, G2, ..., Gm] over a series of successive time-points or conditions C = [C1, C2, ..., Cn] during a biological process. Each element Mi denotes the expression level of the ith gene at the ti-point i or Experimental conditions. A bicluster is presented as a submatrix B(I, J) of matrix M, where I, J are subsets of genes G and C respectively (I⊆ G and J⊆C). A bicluster is encoded as a genetic chromosome that is represented by a fixed-size binary string collection of genes and experimental conditions. If a gene is available in a bicluster, the corresponding bit is set to 1, otherwise 0
