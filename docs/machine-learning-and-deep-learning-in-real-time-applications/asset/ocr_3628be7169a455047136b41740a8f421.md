# ## ABSTRACT

Computational biology is the research area that contributes to the analysis of biological information. The selection of the subset of cancer-related genes is one amongst the foremost clinical research of gene expression data. Since a gene can take the role of various biological pathways that in turn can be active only under specific experimental conditions, the stacked denoising auto-encoder(SDAE) and the genetic algorithm were combined to perform clustering of genes from high dimensional microarray gene expression data. The Genetic-SDAE proved superior to recently proposed methods and better to determine the maximum similarity of a set of clusters of gene expression data with lower MSR and higher gene variance. This work also assesses the results with respect to the discovered genes and that the extracted set of clusters are supported by biological evidence, such as enrichment of gene functions and biological processes.
