For the visible and hidden vectors pair $(	ilde{x}, \tilde{h})$, the probability assigned RBM approach is given in
Eq. (13), where $PR^F$ specifies the partition function as in Eq. (14).

```
$c(\tilde{x}, \tilde{h}) = \frac{e^{-EN(\tilde{x}, \tilde{h})}}{PR^F}$
```

```
$PR^F = \sum_{x,y} e^{-EN(x,y)}$
```

The DBN is trained using CD (Contrastive Divergence) (Goodfellow, Bengio, & Courville, 2016)
the steps of the CD training are as follows:

+   **Step 1**: Choose the $x$ training samples and brace it into visible neurons.
**Step 2**: Evaluate the feasibility of hidden neurons $c_y$ by identifying the product of $\widehat{W}$ weight matrix and\visible vector
**Step 3**: Examine the $y$ hidden states from $c_y$, probabilities.
**Step 4**: Evaluate the $x'$ exterior product of vectors and $d$, that is measured as a positive gradient $\varnothing^+ = x,c_y'$.
**Step 5**: Examine the reconstruction of $x'$ visible states from hidden states. Further, it is needed to
evaluate $y'$ hidden states from the reconstruction of $x'$.
**Step 6**: Evaluate the $x'$ and $y$'s exterior product, be it as a negative gradient $\varnothing^- = x',y'$.
**Step 7**: Define the updated weight as defined in Eq. (15), where $\eta$ indicates the learning rate.

```
$\Delta\tilde{W} = \eta(\varnothing^+ - \varnothing^-)$
```

+   **Step 8**: Update the weights with new values.

The following step defines the progression of DBN training with MLP training (normal) and RBM
training (pre-training)

+   **Step 1**: Initialize the DBN model with weights, biases, and further associated parameters, which are
randomly selected.
**Step 2**: Firstly, the initialization of RBM model is progressed with the input data that serves the potentials
in its visible neurons and gives the unsupervised learning.
**Step 3**: Here, input to the subsequent layer is subjected by potential sampling that processed in the
hidden neurons of the preceding layer. Further, it follows the unsupervised learning.
**Step 4**: The above-specified steps are continued for layers. Here, the pre-
training stage by RBM is processed until it reaches the MLP layer.
**Step 5**: MLP phase specifies the attained learning by supervised format and is continued until it attains
the target error.
