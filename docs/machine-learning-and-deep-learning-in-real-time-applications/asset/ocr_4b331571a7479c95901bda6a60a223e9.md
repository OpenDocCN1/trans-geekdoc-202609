# Employee's Attention Prediction Using Machine Learning Approaches

Timely delivery of service or product is the primary goal of any organization in recent days due to high competition in industries. If a talented employee leaves unexpectedly, the company is not able to complete the task at defined times. It may become the reason for the loss of that. Therefore, companies are interested in knowing the employee's attrition. They can make a proper substitute or arrangements earlier.

There may be various reasons for employee attrition, which include less salary, job satisfaction, personal reasons, or environmental issues if the employee terminates for any reason. It is known as involuntary attrition (Kaur & Vijay, 2016). On the other hand, voluntary attrition is known as the left of the employee by their side. This kind of attrition is loss for the company if he or she is a talented employee. In the present scenario, everyone wants a higher salary and job security. Therefore, employees leave immediately if they get a better chance in other places.

Various researchers have also studied the performance of different machine learning approaches (Ajit, 2016; Sikaroud et al., 2015). Kaur et al. (Kaur & Vijay, 2016) have discussed various reasons or factors that are involved in employee attrition. They have also investigated that talented employee replacement is a time-consuming and challenging. It is also a significant factor in loss in business.

Compensation is one solution to decreasing the attrition rate. Moncarz (Moncarz et al., 2009) have discussed how attrition can be decreased by providing better compensation.

Punnoose and Ajit (2016) have provided a comparative analysis of various machine learning approaches for employee turnover. Tree-based approaches are also used to predict employee attrition (Alaeo & Adeyum, 2013). Jantan et al. (Jantan et al., 2010) have compared tree-based methods with other traditional machine learning approaches. Radaieh and Nagi (Al-Radaieh & Al Nabi, 2012) used decision tree for attrition prediction. In their work, they have found that job title is a significant feature of attrition, whereas age is not a very important feature.

Saradhi & Palishkar, 2011 used various machine learning approaches for employee attrition prediction. They have taken a task of 1575 records with 22 features of employee and applied various classification approaches to predict attrition. They have found that SVM has higher accuracy, which is 84.12%.

Due to confidentiality and noisy HR data, sometimes prediction has higher accuracy. It is difficult to generalized predictions for organizations and employee roles (Zhao et al., 2018).

Previous studies presented accuracy as primary evaluation standard for attrition prediction. Various machine learning approaches are used and evaluated in different datasets. It is challenging to conclude which model is best for attrition prediction. The rate of employee attrition is always less than the employee who stays in the organization. Therefore, datasets are always imbalanced. Accuracy measures are not reliable for imbalanced datasets (Sexton et al., 2005; Sikaroud et al., 2015; Tzeng et al., 2004). So that it is desired to have an accurate model to enhance the prediction accuracy of the models. Which provides better results based on employees. Based on the accurate prediction results HR departments know the behavior of their employees
