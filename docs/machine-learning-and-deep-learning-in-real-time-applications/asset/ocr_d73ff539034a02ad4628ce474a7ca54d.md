# About the Contributors

**Mehul Mahrishi** is currently working as an Associate Professor in the faculty of computer Science & Engineering at the Swami Kesvanand Institute of Technology, Management and Gramoth, Jaipur – India. He is a Professional Member of IEEE Delhi Section, Life Member of Institution of Engineers, India (L-IEI) and Life member of Indian contingent of BRICS International Form that travelled to Moscow, Russia to attend Russian Energy Week-2018 and BRICS Youth Energy Summit. He has published more than 20 research papers in national and international Journals/conferences including 19th SoCTA, 3rd ISDA, 3rd International National Conference on Machine Learning and Computing (IMCLC 2011), 3rd IACC 2013 and chapters in books. He has also participated in a range of forums by Infosys, TCS, and WIPRO-Mission 10 X, IBM etc. His research activities are twofold: the first research activity is to explore the developmental enhancements in applications in computer vision; the second major research theme focused on the emerging capabilities of parallel and cloud computing. Mr. Mahrishi is rewarded at number of occasions in various domains including Recognition as an reviewer by Journal of Parallel and Distributed Computing (JPDC, Elsevier, and SCI & Sc Indexed), IEEE continuing education certification for “Cloud Computing Enable Technologies and Recognition for outstanding performance in Campus Connect Program by Infosys,
