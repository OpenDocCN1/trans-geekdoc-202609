
## PancakeSwap: Financial NFT's

DeFi platforms in Binance Smart Chain owe some of their success to smart NFT applications. NFT's, which include financial elements rather than purely for collection, are currently leading the way in NFT sales.

BSC's cutest, rabbit-themed DeFi project PancakeSwap has created its own special series of NFT characters for the collection. Each rabbit can be exchanged for a certain amount from the local token of the project `CAKE`. Similar to other buy-to-let assets, collectors can speculate on the value of these NFT's as the `CAKE` price fluctuates.

As we mentioned earlier, collections at BSC are maturing in terms of what they offer investors besides their artistic value. If you had previously taken part in the PancakeSwap draw, your ticket was actually a `BEP-721` NFT.

The `MEBs` that users use to buy their tickets are collected in a pool to form the reward fund. Each NFT ticket contains four numbers, which must match the numbers in the draw in the correct order. Part of the prize fund is burned and the remaining amount is divided among the winners. This is a great example of how NFT can be much more than collectibles.

### Why choose Binance Smart Chain for NFTs?

Instead of Ethereum, BSC has a large number of projects that use, create, and sell NFT. The scalability of the network and low transaction fees make BSC an attractive alternative to other blockchains. Because the BSC network is compatible with the Ethereum Virtual Machine (`EVM`), developers can easily transfer their DApp created on Ethereum to BSC.

From a user point of view, it is more cost-effective to buy and sell NFT using BSC. The high fees for Ethereum transactions make NFT very expensive for small investors and collectors.

Binance Smart Chain is rapidly growing the NFT ecosystem as more projects continue to benefit from the speed and affordability of this network. Whether you want to join the NFT wave or just trade with your collections, you can find everything you're looking for in the Binance Smart Chain.

## 5.3. Top NFT tokens in the market

### Beeple NFT's

You've probably heard of this NFT sale. Beeple NFT's are one of the few examples where an event from the crypto world attracts a wide audience. Digital artist Mike Winkelmann, better known as Beeple, turned the art world upside down by selling an NFT for `ETH` worth USD 69 million. The sale was made by Christie's auction house. Beeple, which has never sold more than $100 worth of print before, overnight became the world's highest valued living artist.

What could be behind such a high valuation of his work? Beeple already had a large fan base thanks to its series, which it created and uploaded every day. There hasn't been a day in the last 14 years when he hasn't done that. The work sold was called "Everydays - The First 5000 Days" and was a collage of artworks created in the first 5000 days of his project.

Beeple's sale marks a crucial milestone for the future of digital art and shows that NFT is not a hype that will fade over time.

### NBA Top Shot

As of March 2021, NBA Top Shot is one of the most successful NFT projects. According to Block's data dashboard, NBA Top Shot has the most users of all NFT projects. While this data doesn't take into account smaller and marginal NFT projects, for NBA Top Shot, this is a huge achievement.

In Dabber Labs' NBA Top Shot project, users can purchase packages of unique digital artworks, including famous NBA moments, as NFT.

Where could the success of the project come from? The impact of the NBA brand is undeniable. It is easier to create collectibles related to a game that many people are already interested in than to sell a digital pet. Another factor may be the user experience. The NBA top shot's failure to detect a blockchain product lowers the entry barrier for most people.

Have you ever tried to introduce a friend to the world of DeFi or yield farming and noticed that the products are very difficult for beginners to use? While we offer useful guides for products like `MetaMask`, there's a long way to go before the user experience becomes easy even for your grandmother.

NBA Top Shot avoids this by moving away from decentralization elements to improve the user experience. It's very possible that many users don't even realize that they're using a blockchain-based product, which in itself can be considered a success.

### CryptoPunks

CryptoPunks is one of the oldest NFT's on Ethereum. It has been in use for years and was receiving moderate attention until the NFT boom in 2021.

CryptoPunks is a project of Larva Labs. Ethereum offers 10,000 unique collections of "characters", and they are all different.

When the project was launched, these characters were available for free to anyone with an Ethereum wallet. In 2018, it was possible to buy a character for an average of 50-100 USD. But in 2021, prices have risen dramatically. As of March 2021, the most expensive piece to date has been sold for 4,200 `ETH`.

Getting it for free and selling it for millions of pounds doesn't seem like a bad investment. Although being the "first" purchaser of anything usually offers a price advantage, it is not possible to come across products that bring such a high profit in the future.

### Axie Infinity

Axie Infinity is a blockchain game where users can raise virtual pets called Axie. You can think of Axie Infinity as a combination of Pokémon and CryptoKitties. Players have these pets, control them and trade them with other players. Players can fight monsters together while in adventure mode and compete against each other in PvP (inter-player) competitions.

When we look at its technical infrastructure, the Axies use the `ERC-721` standard on Ethereum. The game also includes items and terrains, all of which are NFT.

Axie Infinity is one of the most popular NFT games on Ethereum. With the launch of the Rhonin side chain, high extortion charges are also expected to cease to be a problem for players. Want to know more about this game? You can read our detailed article about Axie Infinity.

### Decentraland

Decentraland is a blockchain-based virtual reality game. Players can develop and sell `LAND`, virtual terrain represented by an `ERC-721` token. Each part of `LAND` is unique, and `LAND` owners can determine for themselves what content to fill their land with. This can vary from a simple landscape to an interactive game.

Users can buy `LAND` with `MANA`, the platform's local token. In a sense, Decentraland has its own economy. Users can interact with each other in different ways and cash in on their activities. But unlike other similar virtual reality games like Second Life, Decentraland is in full control of a decentralized group of people.
