
# NFTs: From Early Foundations to the Flow Blockchain

## NFTs as Digital Art

This is how the art market discovered NFTs for itself. The property of a clear proof of ownership and the possibility to display this transparently are almost ideal for digital art. Some artists began experimenting with NFTs. Platforms for the trade in digital art such as [OpenSea](https://opensea.io/), [SuperRare](https://superrare.co/), [MakersPlace](https://makersplace.com/), or [Known Origin](https://knownorigin.io/) were founded and continuously expanded. So-called minting platforms such as [Digital Art](https://digitalartchain.com/), [Mintbase](https://mintbase.io/), and [Mintable](https://mintable.app/) integrated solutions to make it easy and fast to create NFTs for anyone without programming knowledge.

### Gaming and Virtual Worlds

The gaming industry around blockchain-based virtual reality also recognized the great potential of NFTs at an early stage. First and foremost are the Decentraland and Cryptovoxels projects, which enable the purchase and ownership of virtual land in the form of NFTs. In these virtual game worlds, new digital ecosystems with virtual cities and landscapes are created.

Another pioneer in the field of blockchain-based virtual reality is [Enjin](https://enjin.io/). This project allows gaming utensils in the form of NFTs to be easily transferred from one game to another.

### Domain Names

In addition to art and gaming, the domain name market has also adapted NFTs at an early stage to provide domains similar to ".com" with decentralized blockchain technology. The best-known service provider Ethereum Name Service was founded in May 2017 and connected to the most common NFT marketplaces in 2019. The number of registered “.eth" domains has grown steadily.

Another well-known service provider is Unstoppable Domains, which has issued the domain “.crypto", among other things.

Between 2018 and 2020, many important foundations were laid for future NFT mass applications. In the meantime, NFTs have penetrated into numerous other sectors of the economy. These include sports, film, music, leisure, finance, and collectibles. These new NFT ecosystems will be discussed again later.

## Disadvantages of Ethereum for NFTs

Even though Ethereum is the largest NFT platform, it is not always the first choice for every type of NFT. Ethereum is still struggling with the challenge of increasing capacity limits. For this reason, the creation and trading of NFTs sometimes incur extremely high fees. With a very valuable NFT like the Beeple artwork, $100 in fees may be insignificant. But for many smaller artists who want to sell their works for $100 to $200, such large amounts are a clear obstacle.

Against this background, several alternative blockchains have stepped up to cleverly close this market gap. Depending on the NFT use case, there is also a suitable NFT blockchain that promises faster and more cost-effective trading of NFTs.

### The Problems with Current Blockchains

Three problems occur again and again with known blockchains:

*   Cost to users
*   Duration of transactions
*   User experience for beginners & developers

And to solve these problems, the blockchains need to bet on better scalability. This is done either at the expense of the developers, as programming is much more difficult. Or by abandoning decentralization, as for example with Libra or EOS. Both solutions are suboptimal.

## FLOW: The Blockchain Built for NFTs

When CryptoKitties brought the Ethereum blockchain to a standstill in late 2017 due to an immense overload, developer Dapper Labs learned first-hand that blockchains of the current generation were not built for this demand.

Instead of simply finding another home for the decentralized app or waiting until the Ethereum scaling solutions are mature, Dapper decided to create a blockchain that the company and other developers can rely on.

The result is Flow, a blockchain specifically designed to support things like NFT collectibles and large-scale crypto games. Flow is a blockchain designed for comprehensive scaling without using sharding techniques that enable fast and cost-effective transactions that make sense for dApps like NFT marketplaces and crypto-infused video games.

As mentioned earlier, Flow comes from Dapper Labs, who decided to solve the problem of blockchain congestion directly by developing one provided for games and other interactive experiences. Dapper now uses Flow for all of its own projects, including NBA Top Shot, but is also open to other developers.

### How Does Flow Work?

Flow uses a pledge verification model where validators must deploy a certain number of `FLOW` tokens to participate in the network.

However, the way validation works is unique among blockchains in that Flow splits validation tasks into four separate node types: consensus, verification, execution, and capture. All four node types participate in the validation of each transaction.

According to Dapper, splitting tasks makes processing transactions more efficient than competing blockchains. This is an alternative option for sharding or distributing a blockchain's storage and compute requirements across numerous nodes. Flow does not use sharding. In this way, according to Dapper, Flow keeps transactions atomic, consistent, isolated, and permanent (ACID) and allows developers to build on each other's work.

Flow also features updatable smart contracts that allow smart contracts to be deployed in beta and then expanded or repaired before they are completed and made immutable.

Dapper Labs convinces with a diverse network. Well-known investors joined early and co-financed the development of the Flow Blockchain. These include the Warner Music Group and star investor Andreessen Horowitz. At the same time, they are already working intensively with the American basketball league NBA. The UFC or the game developer Ubisoft are also among the partners, who also include Coinbase and Samsung.

#### High Scalability

Among experts, the programming language is considered to be particularly developer-friendly. As a result, more and more developers are turning to the Flow Blockchain and working on optimization. At the same time, the commands in the network are broken down into individual tasks, which can then be processed in parallel. This increases the efficiency of the computing capacity; the network becomes more scalable.

Users of the Dapper Wallet can use both Ethereum and Flow and get access to both networks. Dapper's current users will be the first to gain access to the now released Flow network and be able to test applications and use the latest applications there.

#### SpoCKs Technique Instead of Consensus

Flow uses the new cryptographic approach Specialized Proof of Confidential Knowledge (SpoCKs). This technique was developed by the Flow team and published in a special technical documentation. With SpoCK, any number of developers of a third observation instance can prove that they each have access to the same confidential knowledge. There is no information about confidential content in the evidence and they act interactively.

Each SpoCKs is individually and specifically tailored to the developer and cannot be copied or forged by any other developer on the network. Basically, the technique asks the execution and verification nodes to prove their knowledge. This knowledge can only be obtained through the execution of all transactions assigned to them. And only when this has been clarified, they receive the reward.
