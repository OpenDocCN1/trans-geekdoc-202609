
# Genesis Cards and Gameplay

Genesis cards are currently purchased through the browser, but the game can be run as a desktop app. Developers are also considering integrating the game into mobile devices and tablets over time. Card packs will also be available for purchase with `Coinbase Wallet`, a mobile browser and wallet service from `Coinbase`, an investor in `Gods Unchained`.

Each package currently sold contains 5 genesis cards and has a total of 380 cards. Users who purchase cards on Presale will also get early access to the beta version. Each of these genesis cards is actually a non-fungible token created with the `ERC-721` protocol.

## What difference does blockchain technology make to the game?

As most of us know, traditional games are driven entirely by the companies that created them. Although players think they have valuable assets (characters, items, etc.) that they have spent a lot of time on in-game, this ownership status and value of assets may vary with a single decision by developers.

With many advantages such as immutable recording of transactions brought by blockchain, verifying ownership and tracking past transactions, players will be able to own assets just as they do in real life.

## How to play Gods Unchained?

In `Gods Unchained`, you play one-on-one battles against another player or computer. The goal of the game is to win by reducing the other side's life score to zero. At the beginning of each battle, players choose 30 cards from their collection and one God to fight together.

The game is completely free to play, and users do not lose any cards when they lose games. Cards can be under various categories, such as creatures, weapons, magic, or special god cards, and players also have God power that they can use once when it's their turn.

## Investment Perspective: Trading Card Games and NFTs

The whole concept behind `Gods Unchained` is that games don't have to be a silly pastime but can also become an investment case. Games like `Magic the Gathering`, in which tickets were sold for tens of thousands of US dollars, are the way to do that. And such digital games also exist on an NFT basis.

Trading Card Games fascinate many people. Presumably, this is the attractive mix of game and investment case: Should you really exchange a [Black Lotus](https://www.cardmarket.com/en/Magic/Products/Singles/Alpha/Black-Lotus) from the Alpha Edition for an [Ancestral Recall](https://www.cardmarket.com/en/Magic/Products/Singles/International-Edition/Ancestral-Recall) from the International Edition? Here it depends very much on whether you see the matter from an investment perspective or a strategic point of view. Even though the Black Lotus is worth almost a hundred times as much, it may make more sense for your own blue deck to have an Ancestral Recall. If you are still aware of the incredible complexity of `Magic` and the like, it is no wonder that such games have either been transferred to the digital age – see `Hearthstone` – or completely recreated.

Such trading card games can also build a bridge to cryptocurrencies: `MTGox` (now defunct) was originally planned as a trading exchange for trading cards. At the latest with the fall of `MTGox`, this bridge was torn down for the time being, but was rebuilt with projects such as `CryptoKitties`.

Sure, already regarding `CryptoKitties` an investment case can be discussed, but we would like to look at other games in this issue via the investment case Non-Fungible Token. Instead, we look at `Gods Unchained` as an example of an NFT-based game. The game has already been briefly watched in our series about Blockchain Gaming, but here we want to look less at the game than at the investment case.

## What should the investor look out for?

As soon as packs are available again, you should follow the announcements closely: Will there be something like the Mythical Cards that were released in the Genesis phase again? Some of these extremely rare cards were able to achieve very good prices on the markets. If there are such cards, you should take a close look at how they are distributed in the new packs. The Mythical Cards were distributed regardless of the type of pack. So the chances were the same; whether you bought a Rare Pack or a Shiny Legendary Pack. However, since the price of a Rare Pack was only a fraction of the price of a Shiny Legendary Pack, you could buy significantly more packs – and thus increase the probability of finding a Mythical card.

Why is this important? Because these cards can be worth a lot. The [Hyperion](https://blog.godsunchained.com/2019/02/11/the-62000-card/) card was sold in an auction for over $60,000. This makes the second most expensive card in a trading card game to be found at `Gods Unchained`.

### No new packs available? Cards are available in secondary markets

Without packs, the `Gods Unchained` investor is limited to buying individual cards in spe. How could he proceed here? Well, both on `Open Sea` and on the `Gods Unchained` platform directly, the marketplaces are visible. The cards can be sorted by rarity, price and technical details. Investors can now see if there are cards that are a bargain for their great rarity and/or playful use.

Accordingly, it is also worthwhile to study more closely which species are currently popular on decks. If you stock up on strategically suitable cards in good time, you can achieve good returns on the marketplaces.

Unfortunately, there are still no reliable statistics for the market from independent sources. On the one hand, one is dependent on the blog of `Gods Unchained`. It also makes sense to follow the community here.

Another, quite young way to make money is the so-called "forging". This is a kind of "silver plating" of existing cards. Through playful activity, players can acquire so-called "Flux". This can now be used to upgrade simple cards that you have twice to better variants.

# Session 5: Investing in NFTs

Before investing in NFTs, there are two main concepts you need to understand – `dApps` and `MetaMask`.

## 5.1 Which wallet do I need to use NFTs?

This depends on the network on which you want to create the tokens. Fortunately, most wallets today support `Ethereum` and the `Binance Smart Chain`, the two most commonly used blockchains for NFTs.

Find out which wallet the blockchain network on which your token is based requires. If it is an `Ethereum` token, you need a wallet that supports `Ethereum`. If the network is `Tezos`, you need a wallet that supports `Tezos`.

We recommend using `MetaMask` or `Trust Wallet`. These two crypto wallets support a variety of blockchains. However, still check again whether your wallet supports the blockchain on which you hold your tokens.

### What is the MetaMask Wallet?

`MetaMask` is an open-source wallet for `Ethereum` that has been on the market since 2014. The wallet is available as a browser add-on for `Google Chrome`, `Firefox`, `Edge` and `Brave` as well as for `iOS` and `Android`. In addition to the storage and management of tokens based on `Ethereum`, advanced functions such as staking or access to decentralized exchanges (`DEX`) are also easily possible even for beginners.

The rich applications with a simultaneously intuitive user interface are the reason why the `MetaMask` Wallet is still one of the most popular `Ethereum` wallets in 2021. With over 1 million users for `Android` alone, the platform is one of the most used `Ethereum` wallets. Even if hardware wallets are the most secure of all alternatives, the `MetaMask` Wallet can be worthwhile especially for those users who actively participate in the `Ethereum` network and really want to use the tokens.
