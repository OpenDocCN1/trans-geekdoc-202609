## NFTs: Solving Intellectual Property Problems

If we take into account the current uses of NFTs, we can say that they solve intellectual property problems first. While it is very easy to copy a work in a digital environment, NFTs solve this problem by revealing who owns the rights to the work. Because they are programmable digital assets, artists' share of the next sale can also be encoded inside the NFT. Thus, in the event of a sale, the relevant amount is automatically transferred to the artist's wallet. The digital nature allows users of all types to buy them from all over the world. As the customer base expands thanks to digitalization, the number of people who can reach the work and want to invest increases, demand increases, and prices rise accordingly. As demand increases, so does supply. Both more works and works at different price levels are coming to market. This increases accessibility for investors and art lovers and allows more works to be sold. The ecosystem is expanding.

### How can I be sure of the authenticity of NFTs?

In the cryptocurrency ecosystem, the reputation of individuals is highly valued. Artists who produce NFTs and want to put them up for sale go through the stream of verified profiles used by NFT marketplace applications to prove that their work is original. If you have the information of the wallet address of the artist concerned, which is shared publicly for the purpose of receiving payment, you have the chance to confirm the authenticity of the work. In addition, you can be sure of the authenticity of both the artist and their works by tracking the movements of the NFT from a blockchain monitoring application.

### Where are the files inside the NFT stored?

NFTs contain evidence of many file formats, such as pictures, audio, short videos, and documents. Because these files are difficult and costly to store on the blockchain, they are stored on a distributed file retention protocol called `IPFS`. Uploaded files are encrypted, broken into small pieces, and shared to drops on `IPFS`. Proof that the relevant file has been uploaded to `IPFS` is saved on the blockchain with the NFT.

### What are the future NFT use cases?

As experiences with NFTs increase, we see creative new business models being constructed. First, the digitization of physical assets (i.e., tokenization) can be adapted to a wide range of sectors. Last week, for example, a 3D virtual world-designed home in America sold for $500,000. We also know that in some games, land sales are made as NFTs. Another area where NFTs will have an impact is social media. Metrics such as the number of followers, views, and engagement that creators cash in on will become uncontrollable by centralized social media companies thanks to NFTs. Digitization of physical assets seems to cause us to redesign many of the processes we are accustomed to. We can consider a scenario in which governments issue bonds as NFTs in future uses and can trade them globally. Obviously, business models in the NFT world are limited only by your imagination.

## 3.3. Understanding Smart Contracts

Imagine you have to sell a house. This is a rather complex and daunting process. There are a large number of documents, communication with different companies and people, as well as a high level of various risks. Therefore, the absolute majority of home sellers want to find a real estate agent who deals with all the paperwork, markets the property, and acts as an intermediary when negotiations begin, overseeing the deal until it closes.

Moreover, an escrow service, which is especially useful in such transactions, provides assurance since the amounts in question are normally quite large and you cannot really trust the counterparties. However, after a successful deal, the seller and the buyer's agents will share about ten percent of the sale price as commissions. This means a fairly significant financial loss for the seller or buyer.

All these are situations where smart contracts can really revolutionize the entire industry in a convenient and effective way. Perhaps most importantly, a trust issue will be solved. Smart contracts work on the principle of "If-Then". In other words, the ownership of the house is transferred to the buyer only when the agreed amount of money is sent to the system.

They also work as escrow; that is, both the money and the property right are stored in the system and distributed to the participating parties at the same time. In addition, error-free delivery is guaranteed because the transaction has been witnessed and verified by hundreds of people. Since trust between the parties is no longer an issue, an intermediary is no longer needed. All functions performed by a real estate agent can be preprogrammed into a smart contract, saving both the seller and the buyer a significant amount of money at the same time.

### Decentralised Finance (DeFi)

DeFi can be defined as an ecosystem of financial applications running on a blockchain.

The promise of cryptocurrencies is to make money and payments universally and democratically accessible to everyone, wherever you are in the world.

Decentralized Finance (DeFi) or the Open Finance movement aims to take this promise one step further. So imagine that all the traditional financial services you use today (deposits, loans, commerce, insurance, and much more) are a global and open infrastructure that can be accessed by anyone with a smartphone and internet connection worldwide.

In the past 4 years, Decentralized Finance has become one of the fastest-growing areas in the blockchain and cryptocurrency world, both in terms of number of users and in terms of monetary value invested. The vast majority of this growth was built on the `Ethereum` blockchain.

#### Problems of Centralized Structures

DeFi is not really a new concept; this definition has emerged from individuals and institutions that have analyzed today's traditional financial structures for years in order to identify the problems found in traditional systems and to take steps towards a solution.

*   **The "Fault Tolerance" of central structures is low** (Need for Decentralization)
*   **Access barriers to National and International Alternative Financial Instruments are high** (Technology & Personal Finance Requirement)
*   **Intermediary and Third-party institutions risk endless control over our financial assets** (Need for Financial Freedom)
