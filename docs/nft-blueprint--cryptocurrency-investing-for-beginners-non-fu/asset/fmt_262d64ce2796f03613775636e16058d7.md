## Decentraland

Decentraland is a blockchain-based virtual reality game. Players can develop and sell `LAND`, virtual terrain represented by an `ERC-721` token. Each part of `LAND` is unique, and `LAND` owners can determine for themselves what content to fill their land with. This can vary from a simple landscape to an interactive game.

Users can buy `LAND` with `MANA`, the platform's local token. In a sense, Decentraland has its own economy. There are different ways for users to interact and cash in on their activities. But unlike other similar virtual reality games like Second Life, Decentraland is in full control of a decentralized group of people. Users develop and own land, artworks, and `NFTs`. Members are also participants in the platform's Decentralized Autonomous Organization (`DAO`).

Decentraland is a `DAO`, giving its community the power to participate in the governance of the project. Decentraland's local currency is `MANA`, and all in-game assets are on the Ethereum blockchain.

If you have played Second Life before and bought and sold cryptocurrency, then Decentraland may interest you. In just under five years, Decentraland has evolved from a simple 2D experiment into a larger 3D world. Decentraland's developers Esteban Ordano and Ari Meilich have created a virtual world that includes digital real estate parcels, items and other customizable assets. It is possible to buy all of these with Decentraland's `ERC-20` token `MANA`.

Decentralanders can buy `MANA` from various exchanges for crypto or fiat currency. `Erc-721` unique tokens represent Decentraland's unique assets, such as `LAND` properties and other collectible items.

### How Decentraland works

Decentraland is an online world that combines virtual reality with blockchain technology. Unlike many other online games, players have direct control over the rules of the online world. `DAO` tokens give owners the voting rights regarding the platform's organizational policies and in-game features. The organisational policies impact every aspect of Decentraland ranging from the types of items allowed to the investments to be made by the treasury of the `DAO`.

`NFTs`; clothes, items, and the game's virtual real estate represent in-game collections, including `LAND`. Users keep these tokens in their crypto wallets and sell them to other users in Decentraland marketplace. For example, you need Decentraland’s local currency `MANA` to buy yourself a new face mask.

Apart from buying and selling items and property, players can fill their personal space with games, events, and artworks where they can interact with others. There is also the option to cash in your `LAND`. Only the players get to decide what to do with their land.

Your destiny, success and ultimately your journey depend on you, your efforts and your imagination.

Decentraland has many uses, such as advertising and content creation. However, for new players who want to start using `NFT`, the entry barrier is quite high. Due to Ethereum gas charges, the cost of purchasing some cosmetic products almost doubles. Land fees are also in the thousands of dollars, making owning property too expensive for some players.

### What are LAND and MANA?

As we mentioned earlier, `MANA` is Decentraland's local currency. It not only has a digital currency function, but also gives everyone who owns `MANA` the power to vote in Decentraland `DAO`.

Users are required to translate their `MANA` into wrapped `MANA` (`wMANA`) and lock it into `DAO` to participate in `DAO` governance. Each `wMANA` represents one vote in governance motions. You can acquire `MANA` from the exchanges or by selling collectibles in Decentraland's Marketplace. The `MANA` in `DAO` is used to fund its decisions and operations.

`LAND` is a unique token that represents players-owned land in the community. Similar to `MANA`, Decentraland's governance protocol provides voting power.

But `LAND` does not need to be locked in the `DAO`, and each `LAND` token offers 2,000 votes per token. Players with multiple plots of land can combine them under a single `Estate` token. The `Estate` token gives a voting power equal to the number of lands it contains.

### What is Decentraland DAO?

One of Decentraland's interesting features is its focus on decentralization. As we mentioned earlier, Decentraland's land, digital assets and development are controlled by the player community.

The project uses open-source code to regulate rules as a Decentralized Autonomous Organization. Anyone who owns their `MANA` or owns `LAND` can prepare motions and vote on them. Decentraland `DAO` works on the `DAO` software solution Aragon and uses an Agent that can interact with Ethereum smart contracts.

### Uses of Decentraland

Decentraland's developers have considered the platform's potential for new uses in blockchain-based communities from the design stage. Whitepaper mentions the top five uses:

- **Applications**: Users get to create apps and 3D scenes with Decentraland's command language, enabling richer interactions.
- **Content Creation**: The neighbourhoods that emerged in Decentraland brought together people with similar ideas and led to the formation of organic communities.
- **Advertising**: Player traffic in neighbourhoods has led brands to buy space and place billboards to advertise.
- **Digital Collections**: Accumulated, created and bought and sold in Decentraland Marketplace, `NFT`'s give users ownership rights.
- **Social**: Communities on social media platforms, even offline groups, can experience more interactive ways to socialize with friends.

Like most games involving blockchain economies, you'll get a chance to make money here. Speculation is widespread, and some high-demand `LAND`'s can go up to very high prices.

### How to store MANA and LAND?

To fully interact with Decentraland, you need a crypto wallet that integrates into your browser. The development team recommends storing your `MANA` (`ERC-20`) and `LAND` (`ERC-721`) tokens in MetaMask.

Decentraland is quite unique among blockchain virtual reality platforms. Exploring this platform is completely free, so you can easily make your own decision by browsing this world. The development team of the project has made a small project quite mature compared to other crypto projects in five years.

## Axie Infinity (AXS)

To understand Axie Infinity, you can imagine a blockchain game that combines Pokémon and CryptoKitties. Axie Infinity is an `NFT` game and ecosystem built on the Ethereum blockchain. The local currency is an `ERC-20` token called the Axie Infinity Shard (`AXS`).

Following the concept pioneered by CryptoKitties, Axie Infinity is an Ethereum-based collectible that has been growing since 2018. Developed by Vietnam-based start-up Sky Mavis, the game started as an ambitious project and became a popular Ethereum game.

The token of the Axie Infinity ecosystem is the Axie Infinity Shard (`AXS`). `AXS` is an `ERC-20` governance token introduced in November 2020. `AXS` tokens can be purchased, traded, or earned by playing the game.

Other tokens related to Axie Infinity include game assets in the form of `ERC-721` tokens (for example, your Axie characters) and Small Love Potion (`SLP`), an `ERC-20` token.

### What is Axie Infinity?

In the Axie Infinity ecosystem, game characters are called Axies. These are unique tokens that you own and manage (`NFT`). You can keep `NFT` in your personal wallet, transfer it to other Ethereum addresses, or trade with other players using a blockchain-based marketplace. Apart from the Axies, the game also includes virtual terrains and items in the form of `ERC-721` tokens.
