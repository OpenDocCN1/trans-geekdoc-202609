
# How do you become a digital art dealer?

As in the DeFi ecosystem, a `MetaMask` wallet can help here. Likewise, connecting your own wallet to the art exchange `SuperRare` is reminiscent of the procedure you know from `Compound` or another DeFi platform: You have to allow a connection between your own wallet and the dApp via the menu item **Activity** and **Marketplace**, the digital art investor can now invest in a work of art. While the latter offers a general exhibition of his work, **Activity** can be interesting in order to react as quickly as possible to a bargain.

Why is this important? Because a large part of the works of art is auctioned off. There are also works of art that you can buy immediately. The above-mentioned work "AI Generated Nude Portrait #1", for example, is offered for sale for a whopping 850 ETH.

Since in these art auctions are partly good bargains to get hold of, we focus here on the offer of offers. The direct purchase of a work of art, which is offered for sale immediately, is similar anyway – you just have to click on "Buy Now". If an art connoisseur wants to offer an offer, he must click on "Place a Bid" and specify a sum that he would be willing to pay for the artwork.

The amount you would be willing to pay is actually transferred to a smart contract that acts as a trustee. However, the money is not lost (except for the necessary gas fees). As long as the seller has not accepted the offer, investors can withdraw it again. This is done in your own user profile under "Offers". This feature is also important to increase its supply.

After that, a waiting game begins: the art dealer has to wait until the offer is accepted.

## The needle in the haystack: How do I find a worthwhile investment?

The dream of the art dealer is to make a decent profit with these works of art. A few points that you can pay attention to in this regard are, in addition to your own taste:

- How rare are the artist's works of art? Someone who constantly creates new works of art, of course, also increases the offer. This does not have to be bad per se, but it can be reflected in the quality of his works.
- What is the average selling price? This size is important, as you can see from how the artist's works are received by the community.

The good thing about `SuperRare`: All this can be quickly found out via the platform. The two pages mentioned above – **Activity** and **Marketplace** – are a first starting point. Especially the first page, also gives interesting information about the community activity. You can find the top collectors and trending artists of the last week on the website. If an artist can be seen in these sections for several weeks, you should take a closer look at it. Likewise, you can see with the top collectors whether they have common preferences. Are there artists that can be seen in the collections of various top collectors? If so, you should take a closer look at this artist.

The individual artist pages are also very revealing. They can help to find a feeling for the monetary value of the works of art. How many works of art he publishes, how similar they are to each other and what the average selling price is, everything can be found on this page.

## Development of your own art gallery

Over time, the company's own collection of digital works of art will grow. Whether it is about another sale or "only" about the display of beautiful art, the question arises as to how to continue. Sure, on your own collection page on `SuperRare` you can see all the purchased works of art, but that's a bit thin. Many digital art dealers therefore exhibit their digital paintings in the virtual worlds. In both `Cryptovoxels` and `Decentraland`, both artists and art dealers have presented their works to the public.

# C. NFT Projects in Binance Smart Chain

Demand for NFTs continues to grow in `Binance Smart Chain (BSC)`. Its speed and low transaction fees make this blockchain very attractive to both users and developers.

In recent months, the world of smart contracts and unique tokens has witnessed numerous developments and innovations, especially in `Binance Smart Chain` and `Ethereum` blockchains. NFT's now offer much more than digital arts and property rights and are widely used in other applications.

At `BSC`, `Battle Pets`, `PancakeSwap`, and `BakerySwap` continue to push the boundaries of what an NFT can do. Both `Battle Pets` and `Bakery Swap` combine collections with Decentralized Finance (DeFi) staking for tokens of their own projects. `PancakeSwap` also works with NFT's that combine collectability, financial services and gamification.

A long way has come since `CryptoKitties`, and `BSC` has become a place where projects can benefit from their high performance and low fees to innovate NFT.

From `CryptoPunks` of the art world to collectible creatures in `Axie Infinity`, there are many NFT that you can buy and sell and trade. `Ethereum` has been the most common place for these activities to date, but now thousands of users and projects are switching to `Binance Smart Chain` due to their high performance and low fees.

Whether it's NFT exchanges, games or financial products, `BSC` is hosting ground-breaking new projects in what NFT can do. Let's talk about the most successful Decentralized Applications (DApps) in each field and examine why NFT is so successful in `BSC`.

## BakerySwap: Collectible NFT's

NFT marketplaces have long been used for people to buy and sell crypto artworks and collectibles. After storing NFT in a compatible wallet such as `Trust Wallet`, all you have to do is set a price and wait for the buyers to arrive.

While many exchanges are user-friendly, it's unlikely to say they're hosting exciting NFT developments. `BakerySwap`, on the other hand, runs things in a slightly different way.

As you can imagine, this Dapp's marketplace includes its standard art collection and feathered crypto monsters. The value for these NFT's depends on demand and possible uses. The value of a powerful monster is higher than that of a weak monster. Rare and in-demand artworks also have a higher price. You can see an example of an art NFT in `BakerySwap` below.

If you carefully review the NFT categories in `BakerySwap`, you can see that some interesting NFT is offered. These tokens offer much more than just the rights of a PNG image or song.

In addition, `BakerySwap` allows the creation of NFT Bakery Combos. These delicious snack-based NFT's are not only collectible items, they can also be used to farm `BAKE`, the platform's local token. While these NFT's have cute pictures, their real value comes from the financial advantages they offer.

Each NFT combination offers staking power and can be staked to gain `BAKE` tokens. The staking power of combinations is partially random. At the moment it is possible to reach much more than a few tables on sale from this NFT Supermarket.

## Battle Pets: Game NFT

The combination of NFT with games is another classic NFT usage area. Since the early days of `CryptoKitties`, gamification has been one of the main factors leading to the adoption of NFT's. There are currently many fighting feathered NFT creatures in the `BSC` that you can do much more than buy and sell and fight.

`Battle Pets` offers a Pokémon-style experience with buy-to-let NFT animals. This is a tried and tested formula, but this time it also contains elements of finance and games.

When we examine the combat mechanisms, we can see that `Battle Pets` is out of the usual way. Tournament and Siege involves directly staking NFT's to win farming rewards in `FRUIT` tokens, one of the platform's local cryptocurrencies.

You can also create weapons that offer true staking power, so you can get financial advantages as well as improving your game stats.

It's easy to sell the weapons and pets you've created in NFT marketplaces like `BakerySwap`, so the variety of existing NFT's also increases. NFT's can be exchanged for a certain amount of tokens, but you also need to destroy the item you have in the process.
