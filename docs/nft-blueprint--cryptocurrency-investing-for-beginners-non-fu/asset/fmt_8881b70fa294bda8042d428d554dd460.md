
# 2.8. How to Use Rarible to Program an NFT?

[Rarible](https://rarible.com/) is a platform you can use to buy and sell non-fungible tokens and turn your works into NFTs. Rarible is a digital marketplace known as non-fungible tokens (NFTs) where you can buy and sell digital assets on the Ethereum blockchain. Using Rarible, you can buy and sell NFTs and create and market an NFT for yourself.

Rarible mediates the purchase and sale of unique works as non-fungible tokens. You can also convert your own works to NFTs and put them up for sale on the platform. In this guide, you can find all the details about Rarible use, trading NFTs in Rarible, and creating NFTs in Rarible.

We also touched on other details you should know, such as the Rarible commission rate and cryptocurrency wallet usage.

## Rarible bidding

At Rarible, you can also bid on a digital asset at any price with the "bid" button.

After clicking on the bid button, you need to enter your bid amount as follows. For this process, the ETH in your wallet will be converted to WETH through the smart contract.

You can also see the amount of Rarible commission added on top of your offer amount in the "service fee" section.

After clicking on the "Place a bid" button, you will need to complete three steps in your wallet as follows to complete the bidding process.

The first transaction will convert the ETH in your wallet to WETH. The second transaction will allow the smart contract to use the amount in your wallet.

For the first two transactions, you will have to pay gas, i.e., transfer fee. By completing these transactions, you can bid for the NFT you are interested in.

## How to sell Rarible non-fungible tokens (NFT)?

If you already have a non-fungible token in your wallet, you can put it on sale on Rarible.

To do this, you first need to log in to MetaMask in your browser and then visit Rarible. You can also use the platform with other supported browsers and mobile cryptocurrency wallets.

After connecting your wallet to the platform, you can see the digital assets in your wallet from the "my items" page or the "my items" section below the user icon at the top right.

To put your asset on sale, you first need to click on the asset you want to put up for sale in my items. Then you can click on the "put on sale" button.

![Graphical user interface, application Description automatically generated](images/image-5.png)

After clicking on the "Put on sale" button, you can put your NFT on sale by setting a sales price or without setting a price as follows.

Either way, people can bid for the NFT you put up for sale. Click the "next step" button to put it up for sale without setting a price.

You can then put your NFT up for sale by "approve" and sign the sales process for the smart contract to access the NFT.

You will still have to pay a transfer fee for the Approve transaction, as with other transactions.

You can also put the NFT on sale at a certain price by pulling the sign opposite "instant sale price" to the right. Even if you set a price, people can bid for the NFT at any price.

In the following example, I set the price to 2 ETH. If the sale goes through, I'll have 1.95 ETH in my wallet. Rarible charges 2.5% commission from both sellers and buyers.

After clicking the "Next step" button, you can put the NFT up for sale by performing approve and sign on MetaMask or the wallet you are using.

## Rarible NFT creation: How to create a non-fungible token

In addition to buying and selling non-fungible tokens at Rarible, you can also create non-fungible tokens.

In this way, you can convert your work into a non-fungible token and put it up for sale on Rarible.

To create a non-fungible token at Rarible, the first thing you need to do is log into your MetaMask wallet and visit Rarible’s website.

Then you need to click on the "create" button at the top right to create an NFT.

After clicking the Create button, you need to determine whether to sell the work you will convert to NFT once or more.
