
# NBA Top Shot vs. Sorare

NBA Top Shot is currently considered the largest platform for collecting NFTs. However, there is no real game there, apart from individual and collection-based challenges. What is Sorare in contrast? This platform offers a real competition that is about achieving certain scores and competing against other players. If you also want to spend real money on the best team, you can not only win, but also collect rewards.

## How does the start work?

To get to know Sorare, players can first participate for free. Those who register on the website will receive some free player cards for this purpose. These cannot be sold and therefore have no value themselves. With a little luck, however, you can already win "real" cards in your first tournaments, which can actually be sold on the transfer market. In addition, the question "What is Sorare?" can be easily clarified in this way.

All in all, it's quite easy to explore the basics of the game itself and then enter the market. There you can then decide to buy trading cards. Although the prices are based on the cryptocurrency Ethereum, you can of course also simply pay by credit card. If you have bought a card at auction, it is entered into the Ethereum blockchain as a token. New tickets are offered for sale, alternatively you can also buy the tickets from other users if they are for sale.

## What does the future look like?

What is Sorare from an investor's point of view? In short: extremely interesting. In February 2021, a financing round raised more than 40 million euros. The money came from high-profile names, including Reddit co-founder Alexis Ohanian and US entrepreneur Gary Vaynerchuk. In addition, ex-professional footballer Oliver Bierhoff and French international Antoine Griezmann also took part. André Schürrle and Gerard Piqué had already invested before.

Sorare's growth is only likely to really pick up speed in the coming months. Among other things, there will be more teams and leagues, its own app and, above all, more users from all over the world. After all, football is a global sport – so the potential for Sorare is hardly limited.

## What Gives an NFT Its Value?

Although there are many answers to this question, we can list why NFT is valuable in general as follows.

### Uniqueness

Every stand-alone NFT is unique and unique. No one can be made other than the same smart contract with the same token number. This means that a manufactured token is single and solitary and cannot be produced or copied any other way. This is similar to the fact that the Guernica painting, which Picasso drew with his own hands, is priceless, although there are thousands of copies. This is confirmed by blockchain and can be seen by everyone.

NFT is produced with a royalty if the manufacturer wishes to do so. This copyright also means that the buyer has all commercial rights of the product, such as promotion and advertising, through the relevant blockchain. This means that it will also increase the commercial value of all kinds of products.

### Rarity

NFT's are rare because they are unique and cannot be duplicated. In addition to the knowledge that rare things are generally valuable, a very limited number of artists and producers have NFT, especially at this time. This is a good opportunity, especially in a constantly evolving virtual world where we cannot easily predict what will happen. If we make a very simple assumption that the values of the products offered for sale in the first place will be lower than in the future, it may also mean that we may have products that will be sold much more expensively in the future.

NFT's have no lifespan and are very available for purchase and sale, so they can be retained for years. In other words, translating a product that you don't want to end your life into an NFT can also mean attributing a partial infinity to it.

### Sellability

Because all NFT's are buy and sellable, they allow you to make all the products available for business. You can also download products if you use some brokerages. This can make NFT a virtual-only product.

### CryptoKitties

[CryptoKitties](https://www.cryptokitties.co/) was launched on November 28, 2017. Blockchain technology enables the exchange of players via a decentralized network. CryptoKitties is an online game in which users can collect and breed virtual cats. The cryptocurrency Ether (ETH) is used for transactions on the platform.

The developer behind the blockchain game is the venture studio Axiom Zen. The award-winning company is based in Vancouver, Canada, specializing in emerging technologies. The development team consists of blockchain experts and specialists in the field of cryptocurrencies. Products from Axiom Zen are used by well-known companies such as Adobe and Microsoft. Nasa's Jet Propulsion Laboratory also uses Axiom Zen developments.

The concept was first presented in October 2017 at ETH Waterloo, the world's largest Ethereum hackathon. Already on November 23, the beta version was presented to about 200 early users. The game's dApp (decentralized application) represented the smart contract with the third highest activity in the entire Ethereum network within a few hours. About 2 percent of all transactions on the Ethereum blockchain were accounted for by CryptoKitties.

#### CryptoKitties: Concept

CryptoKitties offers its players connected to each other in a blockchain network a versatile game concept with numerous possibilities. The focus is on the breeding of cats, which have a great variety in their appearance and character traits. The genome of the virtual cat parents is based on 256 bits, so that about 4 billion genetic variants can result from breeding.

Due to the decentralized structure, the gameplay depends entirely on the users and cannot be influenced by the development team. Team members who know the breeding algorithm are not allowed to participate in the game. The Ethereum founder Vitalik Buterin was involved in the design of a particularly exclusive CryptoKitty

#### Special features and outlook

Already at the presentation at the ETHWaterloo CryptoKitties enjoyed a high reception. Newcomers were also enthusiastic about the concept and the underlying blockchain technology.

One of the company's early supporters is Dmitry Buterin. The founder of BlockGeeks and father of Ethereum founder Vitalik Buterin was involved in the design of a particularly exclusive CryptoKitty. This virtual cat can be unlocked by players in the course of the game.

What sets CryptoKitties apart from other blockchain concepts is the absence of an Initial Coin Offering (ICO). Instead of this crowdfunding-like form of financing, CryptoKitties relies on a sustainable revenue concept. A small percentage of each transaction on CryptoKitties' marketplace is to be used to finance the project. However, players have the option of bypassing this small fee by interacting outside the regular marketplace.

As marketing director Elsa Wilk points out, this form of financing is a kind of ironic criticism of common ICOs of other cryptocurrency-based networks. She sees a revolutionary potential in blockchain technology that has yet to be brought to the consumer's heart through projects such as CryptoKitties.
