
# Sophia, the AI-Powered Humanoid Robot, Sells NFT Portrait for $688,888

Sophia, the artificial intelligence-powered humanoid robot from Hanson Robotics, recently sold an NFT portrait for $688,888. Sophia created the work with 31-year-old Italian digital artist Andrea Bonaceto. From Bonaceto's works, he combined elements of art history with his own physical drawings or paintings on various surfaces in a process that has been described many times as "iterative cycles of evolution". The artwork contains a 12-second MP4 file titled "Sophia Instantiation", showing the transformation of Bonaceto's portrait into a digital painting of Sophia, and a physical work of art painted by Sophia.

Currently, the majority of online games use centralized models. This means that all relevant data is stored in centralized servers completely controlled by game developers.

Typically, data consists of account information and server history. In this past, all in-game assets (such as collectibles, items, and virtual currency) collected by all events and players are saved and stored.

Because a single company owns the database, players don't own their accounts and items. In addition, central servers can have many limitations and vulnerabilities. For example:

- Server failures due to technical issues
- Hackers infiltrating the system
- Game closing
- Unfair prohibition of accounts
- Lack of transparency regarding game working principles and ratios
- Developers and administrators manipulating the gaming economy

This means that all the power of what happens lies with the gaming companies. However, thanks to blockchain and NFTs, this can be avoided.

#### How Does It Work?

As a blockchain-based system distributed database, it can be used to validate and secure all types of digital data, such as in-game history, digital items, and tokenized assets. The main idea is to take power from gaming companies and give it back to players.

In this way, each player can have full control over their accounts and digital assets and the freedom to buy and sell these assets at any time.

#### How NFTs Impact Online Gaming?

##### Real Property

As we mentioned earlier, blockchain-based games make it possible for users to have lasting ownership and full control over their in-game assets. Each entity is usually represented by unique, interchangeable tokens (non-fungible tokens-NFT), such as `ERC-721` tokens.

In-game cards, skins, equipment, and characters can be among these assets. However, all assets can be linked to blockchain tokens managed by a distributed network, regardless of type.

##### Decentralized Marketplace

Gaming companies have the power to manipulate drop rates and the economies of their games. They can also make in-game items unlocked or linked, making them non-tradeable.

By contrast, games built on top of blockchain networks allow decentralized marketplaces. This eliminates the need for trust among players, while resistance to censorship also mounts. All players can buy, sell, and trade their in-game assets peer-to-peer.

##### Facilitating In-Game Purchases

NFTs can significantly reduce transaction costs and speed up financial transactions. They can make it easier not only between players (peer-to-peer), but also in all types of payments between players and developers.

##### Game Multiverse

By linking in-game data and items to NFTs, players can transfer assets between different games. This allows players to reuse their digital assets while experiencing different games.

Because game items are represented using digital tokens, players can trade them in other game markets on the same blockchain.

##### Fair Playing Field

Depending on the application, blockchain enables open source, distributed and transparent game servers. In such cases, the working principles of the game can only be changed if the vast majority of the network votes in favour. Blockchain also thwarts hackers and fraudsters from disrupting the game because there is no single point of failure.
