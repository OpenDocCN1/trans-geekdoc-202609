
# DAPPs: Future Potential and Advantages

DAPPs have great potential to attract more and more users in the future. The advantages are obvious. Development is not dependent on large companies but is decentralized, and the community determines which functions are included in the respective DAPP and which are not. DAPPs can never be deleted due to decentralization. This means that a DAPP is available as long as the networked computers are online. The encryption and the peer-to-peer network also make it very secure against attacks by hackers. All data made available when using a DAPP is also quickly encrypted due to the underlying blockchain. New cryptocurrencies such as `Waves` or `Elrond` rely on the concept right from the start. As a result, anonymity is also guaranteed to a large extent.

Even if there is not yet a really large selection of different DAPPs, it can be assumed that this will change in the near future. Whether you are looking for a social media channel, want to use a social network, want to make sports bets, or simply want to play a game, there is already the right DAPP for each of these different possibilities. If blockchain technology continues to mature, it is not unlikely that in just a few years most users will do without central apps and will rather opt for DAPPs.

# 5.2. Where Can You Buy Popular NFTs?

Although NFTs have only exploded into the main DeFi scene recently, NFT marketplaces have been around for quite a while. Naturally, given Ethereum’s dominance of the DeFi sector, all the top NFT marketplaces are on Ethereum.

There are currently two Ethereum token standards for NFTs. First, there is the most widely used `ERC-721` standard, which makes the creation of individual tokens possible. For this, the token standard uses a new smart contract for each NFT.

In addition, there is the `ERC-1155` standard, which allows bundled transactions of NFTs and makes the theoretical creation of infinitely many tokens possible with a single smart contract. The `ERC-1155` standard is also known as the multi-token standard because it handles the storage and creation of various NFTs in a single smart contract. It is superior to other ERC standards in that it is cheaper and more effective.

If you want to trade NFTs, you have the choice from a growing number of marketplaces. Here are some of the most popular NFT marketplaces:

## NBA Top Shot

[NBA Top Shot](https://nbatopshot.com/) is an online company launched by Vancouver-based blockchain company Dapper Labs and supported by the NBA. It allows users to purchase a collection of digital basketball highlights and then show them to others. The NBA Top Shot concept is to publish and sell digital basketball trading cards. However, instead of static images of players, NBA Top Shot cards contain video clips from previous games. Each clip is a unique token. To acquire these so-called "Moments," real money must be spent buying digital packages containing random variations of these moments or spending money on the marketplace.

The process of buying these packages is similar to queuing to buy something in any store. Depending on when you click the link to buy, you have a dedicated place on a digital line, and if a product is still available when your turn arrives, you will buy a package. When packages are exhausted, they disappear indefinitely.

If all of this reads like a foreign language to you, there are two points to pay attention to: First, you are not alone. A common chorus of confusion among those outside the crypto community, hearing it for the first time, is often based on confusion and says something like, "I feel too old to understand this." Secondly, this new trend can certainly become much more complicated as it is linked to blockchain technology and speculative investment.

## OpenSea: Universal Marketplace for NFTs

[OpenSea](https://opensea.io/) is the largest trading venue for NFTs to date and allows trading in all types of NFTs. Therefore, on OpenSea, users can exchange, buy, and sell art, game items, collectibles, domain names, etc. The diversity of the offer outshines all other marketplaces. In addition, the platform has several auction functions and is fully integrated into the crypto infrastructure.

Although OpenSea is the largest NFT marketplace in the crypto space, the platform does not have its own token and currently serves exclusively as an interface for trading NFTs. For example, on OpenSea, NFTs from `Decentraland`, `SuperRare`, and `Enjin` can be traded.

## Decentraland: Marketplace in a Virtual World

[Decentraland](https://decentraland.org/) can best be described as a Minecraft-like virtual world where you can trade NFTs. In addition, users can acquire unique plots of land that they can freely develop. You can also play various games in the virtual world. Currently, there is a Halloween special where you can win boxes containing various items.

Within Decentraland, `MANA` holders can use the token as a means of payment for various NFTs. In addition, `MANA` gives users governance rights in Decentraland. This makes it possible for the community itself to vote on eligible NFT contracts, marketplace rules, and other operations in Decentraland.

## SuperRare: A Marketplace for Artists

[SuperRare](https://www.superrare.co/), as the name suggests, deals with super rare art. Therefore, it is an NFT marketplace that deals specifically with digital works of art. The platform stands out from other NFT marketplaces through social profiles, a mobile app, live auctions, and advanced payment options.

Via SuperRare, artists have already been able to sell art worth more than three million US dollars.

## Rarible: Get RARI Tokens for Trading NFTs

[Rarible](https://rarible.com/) enables the creation and trading of NFTs. In addition, the platform has its own governance token called `RARI`. This token makes it possible to participate in votes on important matters. Users can keep the generated NFTs, give them away, or sell and buy them on the marketplace. In addition, users of the Rarible platform receive `RARI` tokens as a reward for trading NFTs.

The interface allows NFT producers to set rules. This means, for example, that they receive a portion of the price paid for each subsequent transfer of ownership. In addition, the marketplace offers a clear user interface that is constantly evolving.

## Enjin Marketplace: A Marketplace for Gamers

The [Enjin Marketplace](https://enjin.io/) is the official marketplace for Enjin-based NFTs. Users can easily and quickly trade Non-Fungible Tokens through the website. In addition, Enjin, one of the first trading venues for NFTs, was released and is also considered the inventor of the `ERC-1155` standard. To buy NFTs, you need `ENJ` tokens. In addition, you need Ethereum so that transactions can be processed.

The Enjin platform is specifically aimed at gamers, with different items usable in multiple games. The Enjin marketplace has a considerable trading volume, lists thousands of items, and offers a clear web interface. According to DappReview, there are more than 1 billion `ERC-1155` items for which over a million US dollars have already been deposited to `ENJ`.
