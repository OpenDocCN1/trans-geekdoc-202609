## What is a Node?

As a process, nodes are an integral part of a blockchain.

Nodes use a client that performs the task of validating and executing transactions. They automatically receive a copy of that blockchain as soon as they connect to the blockchain network. All nodes are treated with a much different network structure than the internet network as we know it. As a function, each node voluntarily joins the network and takes the position of an administrator of that Blockchain network that it participates in.

Let's add that node joining the network have a chance to win or mine Bitcoin. All nodes try to solve numerical puzzles and get paid for them. As a result, the new block begins to form within 5-10 minutes.

## Blockchain and Decentralization

Blockchain, an innovative technology, was planned to be decentralized and independent of governments. In this case, it means that the decentralized network works directly from user to user.

The central system has one authority: all nodes must follow the commands of a single person. However, there is not a single authority in the decentralized system. There are several end nodes (blue). These depend on the secondary nodes (yellow) that are connected among themselves.

The most common use of blockchain is to represent cryptocurrency. To date, no tokens and cryptocurrencies have been created or controlled by a central authority. This helps cryptocurrencies move a few steps ahead of the price-based financial model and remain decentralized.

## Step 2: Qualifications and Processes in the Distributed Registry

In a system with more than one party, any data that is requested to be added to the system is expected to have a valid standard. Thus, the integrity of the system can be maintained. However, since distributed structures can consist of parties that do not know each other, it will be required to establish a structure that is bound by the rules accepted by the general system. These rules are determined during the design phase of systems that will serve a certain purpose and the rules may vary according to different needs. We call the structure and working process of these rules the "reconciliation structure”, which is specific to each application itself.

Within the systems constructed with a certain consensus structure, we also call the process of processing the rules that all stakeholders comply with the "reconciliation process".

When a limited number of individuals come together, they can shake hands and reach an agreement with an oral or written record. In order to achieve a consensus structure on a digital system, this must be guaranteed using software codes. That's where blockchain technology comes out and says, "I'm going to solve all these problems. I will have you save the data with the reconciliation structure (rules) set from the beginning. Then I'm going to distribute these records over the networks to a lot of locations. In the meantime, I will provide a credible reconciliation process that ensures that the data remains the same at all points. In fact, I will not only offer a solution where all users will encrypt their data."

At this point, Blockchain technology brings a different perspective to the way we use data that we can now easily create, update and even delete in the digital world; "I am a distributed data recording system" he says, adding: " A data saved in the solution I offer cannot be changed forever, thus creating a reliable structure."
