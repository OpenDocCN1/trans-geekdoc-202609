## 12.7 Independent Factor Analysis

Recall in our more general discussion of principal components earlier in the book, we surveyed the method of independent components analysis, which required components to not merely be uncorrelated but rather *independent*. `ICA` was featured as one method in the wider blind-source separation and manifold learning problem. Most applications of `ICA`, however, did not feature an error term and are regarded as *noiseless ICA*.
