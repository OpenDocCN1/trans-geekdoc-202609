# Concluding Remarks

This book has featured an overview of multivariate statistics and machine learning, well suited for an introductory text at the advanced undergraduate or beginning graduate level for students primarily in the social or natural sciences. The goal of the book has been to survey methods from these two merging and overlapping areas in the context of the new data science zeitgeist of the 21st century. The primary motivation behind the book was based on the premise that traditional statistics courses for specialists in science are *no longer enough* and should now incorporate features of machine learning as to emphasize the commonalities between *statistics*, *machine* *learning*, and *data science* more generally, within a scientific context of *research design* and *measurement*.

The book aimed to provide a bird’s eye “big picture” foundational view of a wide gamut of methods and techniques, building on a reasonable mix of requisite *theory*, *application*, and *computation* suitable for an introductory course with relatively minimal assumed prerequisites. The book placed great emphasis on unifying *themes* and *foundations* to understanding statistics and machine learning, demonstrating how many techniques and methods can be understood or conceptualized as special cases of wider and more inclusive approaches. A good scientist must understand the quantitative tools they use in addressing the richness of their data, for what one “finds” in their data can to a significant and often surprising extent be a function of what methodological or statistical “lens” was used to do the searching. *The strengths and limitations of the microscope are sometimes just as relevant in a scientific context as what is being observed under it*. It is hoped that this message has been well-received and that the reader has gained renewed awareness of how influential *context* is to the application of quantitative methods in what is essentially and most globally, *the art of generating narratives from experience*.

As also emphasized throughout the text, virtually all methods discussed have book-length treatments in their own right as well as often long and complex histories of development in professional journals and related publications. Hence, it should be recognized that our overview of these methods has simply *scratched the surface* into the richness and complexities of these data-analytic approaches. Still, it is hoped that the book has provided the student or professional a means to get into these techniques as a *starting point* and as a way to better understand and appreciate the *foundations* of the current quantitative landscape in which they practice their science.
