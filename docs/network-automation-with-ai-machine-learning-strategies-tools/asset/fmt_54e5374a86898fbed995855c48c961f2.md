## INTRODUCTION

This book, "Network Automation with AI and ML: Strategies, Tools, and Innovations", represents a comprehensive and detailed exploration of the revolutionary intersection between artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning (ML), and network automation. The primary goal of this work is to unveil how the application of AI and ML is transforming network infrastructure, from performance optimization to enhanced security, outlining a future where networks not only self-manage but also adapt and evolve intelligently.

By diving into the fundamentals of network automation, this book introduces basic concepts and key components, establishing a solid understanding of how AI and ML technologies can be applied to transform networks in ways previously unimaginable. Through an introduction to network-specific AI and ML, readers will be guided through the principles and fundamentals of these disruptive technologies, appreciating their potential for network applications.

We explore tools and platforms, such as Ansible and Nornir, that represent the cutting edge of network automation, showing how the integration of AI into these tools not only automates repetitive tasks but also brings intelligence and predictive capability to network management. We also discuss the importance of Software-Defined Networking (SDN) in the context of AI, highlighting how SDN serves as a crucial enabler for the implementation of AI solutions in networks.

A special focus is given to AI-enhanced network security, addressing how intelligent algorithms are revolutionizing threat detection and prevention. Predictive analytics, in turn, is explored as a powerful tool for anticipating failures and optimizing resources, illustrating the ability of AI and ML to not only react but predict and prevent network problems.
