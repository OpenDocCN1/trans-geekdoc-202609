The work was aimed to review whether the company should continue outsourcing transportation management or should go for insourcing or in-house transportation management. In this work, initially, theory and state of the art in outsourcing have been discussed. Afterward, research questions were formulated and research methodology was presented which combined both qualitative approach and quantitative approach. The qualitative study was conducted to get insight into problems being faced by the company. Whereas quantitative study i.e. cost analysis was performed to evaluate whether it is good to insource the operation of the company should keep going with outsourcing. The qualitative study and cost analysis both resulted in favor of insourcing. Finally, a concise discussion is provided regarding the selection of insourcing transportation management.
