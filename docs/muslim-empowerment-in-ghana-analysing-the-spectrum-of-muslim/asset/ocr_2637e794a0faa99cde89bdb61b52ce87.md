Interestingly, since the 2010s, a public debate about introducing Islamic social finance tools in Ghana, primarily in the form of Islamic banking to complement the conventional banking sector, gained momentum. Although no Islamic banks have so far been established in Ghana, Muslim philanthropists, groups and NGOs have introduced schemes for Islamic microfinance, Islamic investment and *waqf* as instruments for poverty alleviation and socio-economic improvement of local (Muslim) urban and rural communities. Future research will tell if these instruments will form the corner-stone of Muslim self-empowerment in Ghana.
