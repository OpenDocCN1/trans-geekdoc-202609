The Afro-Arab Microfinance started full operations in 2011, providing agribusiness loans, community banking, fast SME loans, business/personal loans and savings accounts. It is a Tier-2 microfinance company, licensed by the Bank of Ghana under the supervision of the Other Financial Institution Supervision Department, OFISD. The company was among the few Ghanaian microfinance institutions that survived the clean-up exercise of the Bank of Ghana in 2019.87 One year earlier, it had launched a specially designed product for mosque communities, and Islamic schools as well as Muslim and Zongo groups to save with the company and secure interest-free loans.
