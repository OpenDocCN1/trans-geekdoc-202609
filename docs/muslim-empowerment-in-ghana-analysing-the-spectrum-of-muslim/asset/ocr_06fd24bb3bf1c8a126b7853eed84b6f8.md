# "AZ-ZAKAT"

# THE 3RD PILLAR OF ISLAM

The Almighty Allah ordered every Muslim who possesses a certain amount of property to pay annually of this possessions the Zakat to the poor or to the other categories mentioned in the Holy Qur'an chapter 9 verse 60:

> "Zakat (Alms) are for the poor and the needy, and those employed to administer the funds. For those whose hearts have been recently reconciled to Islam for those in bondage and in debt, in the cause of Allah and for the wayfarer; a duty imposed by Allah and Allah is full of knowledge and wisdom".

# (1) GOLD, SILVER, MONEY AND GOODS:

The minimum amount of gold liable to payment of Zakat is 90 grams approximately, or its equivalent of money which shall be calculated annually according to the current price of gold locally and internationally.

As at Tuesday, October 4, 2005. The Daily Graphic financial, commodities and stock market (Page 33):

1 Oz of gold = $468.00. And UDS = ₵9,050.00
30grams = 1 Oz
90 grams /30 = 3 Oz = $468 x 3 = $1,404.00
= $1,404 x ₵9,050 = ₵12,706,200

Locally: due to impurity and the use of blades in weighting gold, the price is bound to be lower than the international prices. Combining the two prices in determining the minimal amount liable to payment of Zakat, one may say from ₵9,000,000 to ₵13,000 or ₵11,000,000.00 approximately.

The annual amount of Zakat fixed on money, gold, silver and goods of commerce is 2.5%
i.e.
2½/100 = 5/2 x 1/100 = 5/200 = 1/40

# EXAMPLE:

If you possess an amount of 13,000,000 in excess of your expenditure kept in your house or Bank for a full year. The amount due for Az-Zakat is 13,000,000 /40 = 325,000
