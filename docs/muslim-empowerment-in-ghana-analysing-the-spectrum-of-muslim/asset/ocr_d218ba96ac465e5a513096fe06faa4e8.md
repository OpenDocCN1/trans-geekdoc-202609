Alhaji Nurideen Salih, (retired) IEU Regional Manager in the Upper West Region, represents those scholars and activists who are critical of the institutionalisation of zakat in Ghana at any level. In his mind, the biggest problem connected with any zakat fund is the (potential) misappropriation of funds. None of the funds and initiatives that so far have been established in Ghana lack transparency and accountability. Therefore, Alhaji Nurideen Salih suggests leaving zakat aside when elaborating upon Muslim communities’ socio-economic empowerment and instead focusing on infaq, spending to please God without asking for any favour. There is no rule and no limit on infaq, he stresses, anybody can give any amount at any time.262
