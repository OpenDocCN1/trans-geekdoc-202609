# ZONGO TECH REVOLUTION

![](bbox=[0.12, 0.13, 0.85, 0.6])

The ZongoVation Hub is leading the Zongo Tech Revolution to train and develop 100,000 tech entrepreneurs and professionals from the Zongo communities in Ghana by the year 2030 in line with the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). The organization will establish centers across all 16 regions of Ghana in the pursuit of this agenda to make the Zongo communities the tech hub of Africa.
