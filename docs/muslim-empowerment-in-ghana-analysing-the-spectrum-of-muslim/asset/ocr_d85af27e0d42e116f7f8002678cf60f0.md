Although certainly existing, Iftar, Eid al-Fitr or Eid al-Adha (Qurban) donations organised by the Ahmadiyya Muslim Mission or Humanity First Ghana have left few traces. It seems these events have been annual although rarely noted on social media; among the few is a note on the Facebook page of Humanity First Ghana about a donation in Bolgatanga in late July 2021, the distribution of bread for Iftar in 2022, and Qurban meat and clothes in 2023.²⁵² The humanitarian relief campaign during the COVID-19 lockdown was among the few times when the Ghana chapter of Humanity First made headlines in Ghanaian news media.²⁵³ During previous years, humanitarian assistance Ahmadi organisations rarely made the headlines, with a few exceptions, such as the donation by the Ahmadiyya Youth Association to the Cape Coast School for the Deaf and Blind in 2019.²⁵⁴
