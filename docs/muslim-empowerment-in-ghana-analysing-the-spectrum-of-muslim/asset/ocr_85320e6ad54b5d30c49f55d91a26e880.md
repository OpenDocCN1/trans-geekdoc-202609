Some initiatives are funded by foreign donors, such as its SHS scholarship project launched in partnership with the Turkish INGO Human Development and Relief Organization in 2015.246 Its main social welfare project is the Ummah Support Fund, launched in 2016. The homepage does not give any information on the Ummah Support Fund apart from displaying a poster with a call to make a monthly donation of GHS 10 (USD 1,60) to the fund, “to support the sick, new reverts, students in need, and Muslims in urgent financial need.”247 It is not clear, however, if the support fund has been formalised or if it exists more on an ad hoc basis.
