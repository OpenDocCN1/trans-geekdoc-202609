Individuals with emotional or intellectual disabilities, in turn, belong to the most stigmatised persons in Ghanaian society. Hitherto, faith-based and traditional healers,178 including Muslim healers,179 have treated treat many if not most of them. For long, no Muslim NGOs had them on their agenda with the exception of the Kumasi-based Al-Bushra Foundation, a novel initiative to carter for the well-being of people with Down Syndrome.180
