# URBAN GARDENING PROJECT

# "My Garden , My Health, My Wealth Project."

This is an upscaled project in parts of Nima and fully in Maamobi that empowers local people and school children to grow fruits and vegetables in sacks filled with soil to make use of limited space while improving their nutritional value. The Technology is called Mutli-storey Gardens,Sponsored by the German Development Corporation and implemented by Hipsters of Nature and Mothers Club in Development
