In contrast to the first and second generations, the third generation of Muslim NGOs includes both dawatist, solidarity-based and secular ones. Founded during the 2010s, all of them are distinct NGOs of the ‘internet era’ and ‘Facebook generation’. Most importantly, their existence is a vivid indication of the emergence of Muslim middle-classes in Ghana, a phenomena hitherto not studied or discussed in academic research. A distinct marker of theirs is their independent position viz-a-viz foreign donors.
