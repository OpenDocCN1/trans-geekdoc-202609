There have been several attempts to broaden the community of the Zakat and Sadaqa Trust Fund donors outside the Muslim MPS and state employees. In 2016, the then President of Ghana, John Dramani Mahama, proposed to charge one percent of the total charge on Hajj on every pilgrim and transfer the proceeds to the Zakat and Sadaqa Trust Fund.
