*Muslim Global Relief* (MGR, established in 2000), in turn, began its work in Ghana in 2015, operating as a mere donor organisation. The MGR funded projects of its local partner organisation in the Upper East Region, the El-Ehsan Charitable Relief Foundation. In 2015, the MGR started its Water4Life Ghana project. This project sought to construct a hand-pumped well at Tes-Natinga village in Pusiga District, Upper East Region, and was implemented by its local partner organisation.538 It launched a fundraising campaign in 2016 for a water and sanitation project at Kuose-Jingiri, Upper East Region, implemented by its local partner.
