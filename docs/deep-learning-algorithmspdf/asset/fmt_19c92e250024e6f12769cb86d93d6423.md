After training our network, we use another 10,000 test data to test the accuracy of our network. We achieved a testing accuracy of 0.993, which indicates that the network only makes a mistake in 7 out of every 1000 digits.

We show the training curve in Figure 4. It can be observed that accuracy improves very quickly in the first 5000 iterations, then improves gradually until we reach approximately 99% accuracy on both the training set and testing set. No overfitting is observed.
