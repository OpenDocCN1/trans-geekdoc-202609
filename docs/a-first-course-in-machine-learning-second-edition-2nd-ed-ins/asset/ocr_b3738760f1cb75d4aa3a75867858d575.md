We also know that the posterior Dirichlet for a Dirichlet prior and multinomial likelihood has parameters equal to the prior parameters plus the observation counts. Therefore, defining $c_{nk}^{-i}$ as the count of the number of words (excluding the $i$th one) in the $n$th document currently assigned to topic $k$, we know that the term associated with the marginalisation of $\boldsymbol{\theta}_n$ is:

$$\frac{c_{nk}^{-i} + \alpha_k}{\sum_j c_{nj}^{-i} + \alpha_j}$$

and the term associated with the marginalisation of $\boldsymbol{\beta}_k$ is:

$$\frac{v_{kw} + \gamma_w}{\sum_{w'} v_{kw'} + \gamma_{w'}}$$

Multiplying the two together gives the update given in the text.
