Multiplying this by **w** gives:

$$x_nx_n^{\mathsf T}\mathbf{w}=\begin{bmatrix}w_0x_{n1}^2+w_1x_{n1}x_{n2}\\w_0x_{n2}x_{n1}+w_1x_{n2}^2\end{bmatrix}$$

Summing over the $N$ terms leads us to the matrix we derived previously.
