$\hat{y} = life \exp = b_0 + b_{Asia} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Asia}(x) + b_{Europe} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Europe}(x)\n$+ b_{NorthAmerica} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{NorthAmerica}(x) + b_{Oceania} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Oceania}(x)\n$+ b_{SouthAmerica} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{SouthAmerica}(x)\n$= 66.31 + 8.64 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Asia}(x) + 13.6 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Europe}(x)$\n$+ 9.98 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{NorthAmerica}(x) + 8.11 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Oceania}(x)$\n$+ 8.92 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{SouthAmerica}(x)$

Whoa! That looks daunting! Don't fret, however, as you understand what all the elements mean, dauntings simplify greatly. First, $\mathbb{1}_A(x)$ is what's known in mathematics as an "indicator function." It returns one of two possible values, 0 and 1, where

```
$$\mathbb{1}_A(x) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } x \text{ is in } A \ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
```

In a statistical modeling context, this is also known as a dummy variable. In our case, we consider the first such indicator variable $\mathbb{1}_{Amer}(x)$. This indicator returns 1 if a country is in the Asia, 0 otherwise

```
$$\mathbb{1}_{Amer}(x) = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } x \text{ is in Asia} \ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$
```

Second, $b_0$ corresponds to the intercept as before; in this case, it is the mean expectancy of all countries in Africa. Third, $b_{Asia}, b_{Europe}, b_{NorthAmerica}, b_{Oceania},$ and $b_{SouthAmerica}$ represent the 5 "offsets relative to the baseline for comparison" in the regression coefficients.

We put this all together and compute the fitted value $\hat{y} = life \exp$ for a country in Africa. Since the country is in Africa, all five indicator functions $\mathbb{1}_{Asia}(x), \mathbb{1}_{Europe}(x), \mathbb{1}_{NorthAmerica}(x), \mathbb{1}_{Oceania}(x),$ and $\mathbb{1}_{SouthAmerica}(x)$ will equal 0, and thus:

```
$$\hat{y} = b_0 + b_{Asia} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Asia}(x) + b_{Europe} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Europe}(x)\n$+ b_{NorthAmerica} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{NorthAmerica}(x) + b_{Oceania} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Oceania}(x)\n$+ b_{SouthAmerica} \cdot \mathbb{1}_{SouthAmerica}(x)\n$= 66.31 + 8.64 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Asia}(x) + 13.6 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Europe}(x)\n$+ 9.98 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{NorthAmerica}(x) + 8.11 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{Oceania}(x)\n$+ 8.92 \cdot \mathbb{1}_{SouthAmerica}(x)\n$= 66.31 + 8.64 \cdot 0 + 13.6 \cdot 0 + 9.98 \cdot 0 + 8.11 \cdot 0 + 8.92 \cdot 0\n$= 66.31$
```
