# About this book

This book was written using RStudio's bookdown29 package by Yihui Xie(Xie, 2024). This package simplifies the publishing of books by having all content written in R Markdown30. The bookdown/R code for all versions of ModernDive is available on GitHub:

+   - Latest online version The most up-to-date release:
  - Version 2.0 released on March 20, 2025 (source code31)
  - Available at https://moderndive.com/v2/
- Second print edition The CRC Press edition is what we are reading! It corresponds to Version 2.0. We welcomed Dr. Arturo Valdivdia as a co-author for his edition. His deep knowledge of statistics and superb teaching experience have been invaluable in improving the book. Here is a summary of what was updated from v1.0 to v2.0 (first print edition to second edition). Additional information about changes over the book time are available on our GitHub page here32
- Updated Datasets and Code: We replaced datasets (promotions, events, and pennies) with new ones (un_member_states_2024, specify_sample, and almonds). We adopted the nyflights23 package instead of nyflights13 and introduced the base R pipe (|>) of the tidytverse pipe (%>). We also incorporated envoy_flights and early_january_2023_weather in the moderndive package.
- Content Reorganization: We restructured sections in Chapters 7 and Chapter 10 for improved readability. We moved “Model Selection” from Chapter 6 to Chapter 10 and split it into two sections as suggestions.
