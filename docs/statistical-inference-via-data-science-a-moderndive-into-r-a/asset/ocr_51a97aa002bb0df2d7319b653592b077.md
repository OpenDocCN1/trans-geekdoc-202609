We can also study some of the characteristics of this bootstrap sample, such as its sample mean:

```
boot_sample |>
summarize(mean_weight = mean(weight))
```

```
# A tibble: 1 x 2
replicate mean_weight
<int>      dbl
1         3.702
```

By using summarize() and mean() on the bootstrap sample boot_sample, we determine that the mean weight is 3.702 grams. Recall that the mean of the original sample was found in the previous subsection as 3.682. So, the sample mean of the bootstrap sample is different from the sample mean of the original sample. This variation is induced by resampling with replacement, the method for finding the bootstrap sample. We can also compare the histogram of weights for the bootstrap sample with the histogram of weights for the original sample.
