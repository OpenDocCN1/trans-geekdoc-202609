As discussed in Section 2.4, since date is a notion of time and thus sequential ordering to it, a line graph would be a more appropriate visualization to use than a scatterplot. In other words, we should use a geom_line() instead of geom_point(). Recall that such plots are called time series plots.

```
ggplot(US_births_1999, aes(x = date, y = births)) +
geom_line() +
  labs(x = "Date",
      y = "Number of births",
      title = "US Births in 1999")
```
