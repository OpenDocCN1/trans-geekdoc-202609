# 10.4 The multiple linear regression model

We first comment on the plots with the response (total_cup_points) on the vertical axis. They are located in the last row of plots of the figure. When plotting total_cup_points against aroma (bottom row, leftmost plot) or total_cup_points against flavor (bottom row, second plot from the left), we observe a strong and positive linear relationship. The plot of total_cup_points against moisture_percentage (bottom row, third plot from the left) does not provide much information and it appears that these variables are not associated with any pair, but we observe an outlying for moisture_percentage around zero. The plot of total_cup_points versus continuous_of_origin (bottom row, four plots, from the left) shows four histograms, one for each factor level; the fourth group has a larger dispersion, even though the number of observations seems smaller. The associated boxplots connecting these two variables (fourth row, rightmost plot) suggest that the first-factor of "Africa" has a higher mean cup than the others. It is often useful to find any linear associations between numerical regressors as is the case for the scatterplot for aroma against flavor which suggests a strong positive linear association. By contrast, almost no relationship can be found when observing moisture_percentage against either aroma or flavor.

The function only produces these plots, but includes the correlation coefficient for any pair of numerical variables. In this example, the correlation coefficients support the findings using the scatterplot matrix. The correlations between total_cup_points and aroma (0.87) and total_cup_points and flavor (0.94) are positive and close to one, suggesting a strong positive linear association. Recall that the correlation coefficient is relevant if the association is approximately linear. The correlation between moisture_percentage and any other variable is close to zero, suggesting that moisture_percentage is likely not associated with any other variable (either response or regressor). Note also that the correlation between aroma and flavor (0.82) supports our conclusion of a strong positive association.
