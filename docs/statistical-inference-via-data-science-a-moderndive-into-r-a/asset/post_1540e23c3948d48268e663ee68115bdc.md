We've finished the last two scenarios of the “Scenarios of sampling for inference table, which we re-display in Table 10.17.

Table 10.17: Scenarios of sampling for inference

| Scenario | Population parameter | Notation | Point estimate | Symbol(s) |
|---|---|---|---|--|
| 1 | Population proportion | $p$ | Sample proportion | $\hat{p}$ |
| 2 | Population mean | $\mu$ | Sample mean | $\bar{x}$ or $\hat{\mu}$ |
| 3 | Difference in proportions | $p_1 - p_2$ | Difference in sample proportions | $\hat{p}_1 - \hat{p}_2$ |
