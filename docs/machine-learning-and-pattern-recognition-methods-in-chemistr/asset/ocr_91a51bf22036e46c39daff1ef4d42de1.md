# Preface

Human has always been looking for a way to do two things: to count and to measure. Humanity owes the knowledge of the definitions of coordinates, distance, and, in principle, the very existence of quantitative measurements in the sciences to the invaluable efforts of the French mathematician and philosopher René Descartes. In most cases, measuring requires a tool that can be used to characterize an object or thing. At first, an object is stimulated by a variable and then its response is recorded. In other words, all beings have a minimum of characteristics that are either common or unique to them. That is to say, measurement aims to recognize differences and similarities between individuals. Observations and measurements create a large amount of data designated to each member of a statistical population. Now, by projecting these features in a coordinate system, we create a space where each target population member occupies a specific position. Let us not forget that objects in such a space can be close or far apart. Their distinction or essentially defined in terms of distances. Depending on the nature of the discipline, for instance whether it is natural or social sciences, a study demands a particular interpretation of space, distance, and similarity in regard to the members, objects, or individuals of the target community. In experimental sciences such as chemistry, measurements and observations, and any monitoring and investigation of objects in space, are to uncover the existence of buried relationships, patterns, and distribution and to find a quantitative mathematical relation that relates the attributes and properties of objects to responses or sometimes their characteristics. The former leads to subjects such as pattern recognition, classification, and clustering, and the latter results in concepts such as regression and calibration.

Machine learning methods and artificial intelligence can perhaps be traced back to the work of the eminent English scientist Alan Turing. Then, tremendous advances in the development of new and powerful techniques in all scientific and technical disciplines have led to the provision of tools that have contributed significantly to the correct understanding of many phenomena.
