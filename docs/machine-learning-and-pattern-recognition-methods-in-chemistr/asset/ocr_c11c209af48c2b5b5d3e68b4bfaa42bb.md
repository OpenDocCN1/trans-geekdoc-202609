During the batch reaction, the concentration of ASA was measured at four different times, each with three replicates, using an offline HPLC method. The concentration values obtained by replicates at each time point were used for calculating the confidence interval with p=0.99, at that time. The individual concentration values of ASA and the confidence intervals are also shown in Fig. 1.8. As be seen in Fig. 1.8, the concentration band of ASA did not fall into the concentration intervals. The deviations were so great that if the reference values were implemented as the soft known-value constraints, none of the band profiles would be accepted. This evidence suggests that the HPLC results were highly biased. Accordingly, calculating the area of feasible solutions gives deeper insights into the problem.
