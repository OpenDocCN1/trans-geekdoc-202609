# 2.3 Near infrared hyperspectral imaging

Near infrared (NIR) hyperspectral images were collected using a Headwell Photonics NIR hyperspectral camera (Model 1002A-00371, Fitchburg, MA, USA). X-Control software was used to calibrate the camera settings, and Hyperspec Rev. A.11.13.1 software was used to set the acquisition parameters. Images were collected in the spectral resolution of 4.85 nm and 142 bands with an acquisition time of 100,000µs, a temperature of 260K°, and a focal distance of 30 cm. The spatial resolution was set to to 10µm. The light source was aimed at the sample at a 45° angle. Calibration was performed by taking a 0% reflectance measurement with the objective closed and 100% reflectance measurement with a Spectralon plate. The camera measures in line-scan modality. Scan length was adjusted as necessary to record all of the samples (Fig. 5.2).
