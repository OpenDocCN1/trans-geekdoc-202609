The results of calibration for the sensor array of mixtures and mixture (sensor elements), individually, are presented in Fig. 4.12. The results of PLS analysis confirm the assertion that a mixture of indicators in the aqueous solution is not sufficient for pH prediction by the PLS method. Fig. 4.12C and D demonstrates the result of experimental data analysis, for the mixture of indicators in micellar and aqueous solutions, respectively. The pre-predicted values of pH in the aqueous solution versus the measured pH by pH electrode are not in good agreement, particularly the pH values around 5.
