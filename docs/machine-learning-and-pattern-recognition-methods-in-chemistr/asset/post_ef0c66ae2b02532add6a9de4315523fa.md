All models (SIMCA with SNV; SIMCA with first derivative; PLS-DA with SNV; and PLS-DA with first derivative) were validated by random subset cross-validation and external validation (using 75% of the spectra for training and cross-validation, and 25% of the spectra for external validation). The datasets for training and external validation were randomly split.
