Another option is tuning of pKa values of available indicators (methyl orange, neutral red, and phenol red) by adding the surfactant to the indicator solution. Distribution of indicators between micellar and aqueous phases changes their transition interval and transition point and consequently their apparent dissociation constants. The shift in apparent constants depends on the surfactant concentration and distribution constants of different forms of indicator in aqueous and micellar phases.
