# 2. Materials and methods butadiene styrene (ABS) pellet

The polymer used was the acrylonitrile butadiene styrene (ABS). It was in the form of pellets of approximately 5mm in diameter. ABS was chosen from the list of polymers produced in the greatest quantities in Europe (Plastics Europe, 2020). Microparticles ranging in size from 100um to 2mm were produced from the pellet using mechanical abrasion.

The identity and size of ABS were confirmed using Raman microscopy. An Invia confocal Raman spectrometer coupled to a DMLM Leica microscope (Renishaw, Gloucestershire, UK) was used to measure the size and confirm the identity of the polymer. A still image of each microparticle on a glass plate was collected with $20×$ magnification. The still image collected of each individual microplastic particle was used to calculate the dimensions. A 785-nm excitation laser was used with 350mW output power and 150mW as 100% laser power.
