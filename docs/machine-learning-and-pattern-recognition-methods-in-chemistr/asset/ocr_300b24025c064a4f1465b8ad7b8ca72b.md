For obtaining the concentration profile of the analyte uniquely, the spectral subspace of the other components should be defined by the duality relation. Here, an example of such conditions is depicted for the simulated three-component system. Fig. 7.9A displays the concentration window of the components by horizontal lines along the time axis, and each line has the same color as the corresponding component in Fig. 7.8A. The blue (light gray in the printed version) component is considered as the analyte of interest, and two others are chosen as the interferents (green (gray in the printed version) and red (dark gray in the printed version) ones). The solid line rectangle shows the concentration window of the blue (light gray in the printed version), and the dashed line rectangle shows its absence window.
