(i) Compute the symmetrized matrix \(A^{\mathsf T}A\). We first compute the symmetrized matrix

\[
A^{\mathsf T}A=
\begin{bmatrix}2&-1\\2&1\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}2&2\\-1&1\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}5&3\\3&5\end{bmatrix}.
\tag{4.2}
\]

(ii) Find the right-singular vectors and singular values from \(A^{\mathsf T}A\). The characteristic polynomial of \(A^{\mathsf T}A\) is

\[
(5-\lambda)^2-9=\lambda^2-10\lambda+16=0.
\tag{4.3}
\]

This yields eigenvalues, sorted from largest absolute first, \(\lambda_1=8\) and \(\lambda_2=2\). The associated normalized eigenvectors are respectively

\[
v_1=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{bmatrix}
\quad\text{and}\quad
v_2=\begin{bmatrix}\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\end{bmatrix}.
\tag{4.4}
\]

We have thus obtained the right-singular orthogonal matrix

\[
V=[v_1,v_2]=
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\end{bmatrix}.
\tag{4.5}
\]

(iii) Determine the singular values. We obtain the two singular values from the square root of the eigenvalues \(\sigma_1=\sqrt{\lambda_1}=\sqrt{8}=2\sqrt{2}\) and \(\sigma_2=\sqrt{\lambda_2}=\sqrt{2}\). We construct the singular value diagonal matrix as

\[
\Sigma=
\begin{bmatrix}\sigma_1&0\\0&\sigma_2\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}2\sqrt{2}&0\\0&\sqrt{2}\end{bmatrix}.
\tag{4.6}
\]

(iv) Find the left-singular eigenvectors. We have to map the two eigenvectors \(v_1,v_2\) using \(A\). This yields two self-consistent equations that enable us to find orthogonal eigenvectors \(u_1,u_2\):

\[
Av_1=(\sigma_1u_1v_1^{\mathsf T})v_1=\sigma_1u_1(v_1^{\mathsf T}v_1)=\sigma_1u_1=
\begin{bmatrix}2\sqrt{2}\\0\end{bmatrix}.
\]

\[
Av_2=(\sigma_2u_2v_2^{\mathsf T})v_2=\sigma_2u_2(v_2^{\mathsf T}v_2)=\sigma_2u_2=
\begin{bmatrix}0\\\sqrt{2}\end{bmatrix}.
\]

We normalize the left-singular vectors by dividing them by their respective singular values and obtain \(u_1=\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}\) and \(u_2=\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}\), which yields

\[
U=[u_1,u_2]=
\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}.
\tag{4.7}
\]

(v) Assemble the left-/right-singular vectors and singular values. The SVD of \(A\) is

\[
A=U\Sigma V^{\mathsf T}=
\begin{bmatrix}1&0\\0&1\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}\sqrt{8}&0\\0&\sqrt{2}\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\\
\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}&-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}
\end{bmatrix}.
\]
