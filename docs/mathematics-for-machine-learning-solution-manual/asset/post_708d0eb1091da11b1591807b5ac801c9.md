
- a.  
  \[
  \frac{\mathrm{d}f}{\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{x}}
  = \underbrace{\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}}_{\in\mathbb{R}}

  \in \mathbb{R}^{1\times D}

  \[
  \frac{\partial f}{\partial z} = \frac{1}{1+z} = \frac{1}{1+\boldsymbol{x}^\top \boldsymbol{x}}

  \[

  \]
  \[
  \implies \frac{\mathrm{d}f}{\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{x}} = \frac{2\boldsymbol{x}^\top}{1+\boldsymbol{x}^\top \boldsymbol{x}}

- b.  
  \[
  \frac{\mathrm{d}f}{\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{x}}
  = \underbrace{\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}}_{\in\mathbb{R}^{E\times E}}

  \]

  \sin(\boldsymbol{z}) = \begin{bmatrix} \sin z_1 \\ \vdots \\ \sin z_E \end{bmatrix}

### 5.8 Compute the derivatives \(\mathrm{d}f/\mathrm{d}\boldsymbol{x}\) of the following functions. Describe your steps in detail.
