# 6.10  Derive the relationship in Section 6.5.2 in two ways:

+   a. By completing the square  
+   b. By expressing the Gaussian in its exponential family form

The *product* of two Gaussians $\mathcal{N}(x\mid a,A)\mathcal{N}(x\mid b,B)$ is an unnormalized Gaussian distribution $c\mathcal{N}(x\mid c,C)$ with

$$C=(A^{-1}+B^{-1})^{-1}$$
$$c=C(A^{-1}a+B^{-1}b)$$
$$c=(2\pi)^{-D/2}|A+B|^{-1/2}\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}(a-b)^\top(A+B)^{-1}(a-b)\right).$$

Note that the normalizing constant $c$ itself can be considered a (normalized) Gaussian distribution either in $a$ or in $b$ with an “inflated” covariance matrix $A+B$, i.e., $c=\mathcal{N}(a\mid b,A+B)=\mathcal{N}(b\mid a,A+B)$.

# (i) By completing the square, we obtain

$$\mathcal{N}(x\mid a,A)=(2\pi)^{-D/2}|A|^{-1/2}\exp\left[-\frac{1}{2}(x-a)^\top A^{-1}(x-a)\right],$$

$$\mathcal{N}(x\mid b,B)=(2\pi)^{-D/2}|B|^{-1/2}\exp\left[-\frac{1}{2}(x-b)^\top B^{-1}(x-b)\right].$$

For convenience, we consider the log of the product:

$$\log\left(\mathcal{N}(x\mid a,A)\mathcal{N}(x\mid b,B)\right)$$
$$=\log\mathcal{N}(x\mid a,A)+\log\mathcal{N}(x\mid b,B)$$
$$=-\frac{1}{2}\left[(x-a)^\top A^{-1}(x-a)+(x-b)^\top B^{-1}(x-b)\right]+\text{const}$$
$$=-\frac{1}{2}\left[x^\top A^{-1}x-2x^\top A^{-1}a+a^\top A^{-1}a+x^\top B^{-1}x-2x^\top B^{-1}b+b^\top B^{-1}b\right]+\text{const}$$
$$=-\frac{1}{2}\left[x^\top(A^{-1}+B^{-1})x-2x^\top(A^{-1}a+B^{-1}b)+a^\top A^{-1}a+b^\top B^{-1}b\right]+\text{const}$$
$$+\text{const}$$
$$=-\frac{1}{2}\left[(x-(A^{-1}+B^{-1})^{-1}(A^{-1}a+B^{-1}b))^\top(A^{-1}+B^{-1})\right.$$
$$\left.\cdot(x-(A^{-1}+B^{-1})^{-1}(A^{-1}a+B^{-1}b))\right]+\text{const}.$$

Thus, the corresponding product is $\mathcal{N}(x\mid c,C)$ with

$$C=(A^{-1}+B^{-1})^{-1} \tag{6.9}$$
$$c=(A^{-1}+B^{-1})^{-1}(A^{-1}a+B^{-1}b)=C(A^{-1}a+B^{-1}b). \tag{6.10}$$

# (ii) By expressing Gaussian in its exponential family form:

Recall from the Example 6.13 we get the exponential form of univariate Gaussian distribution, similarly we can get the exponential form of multivariate Gaussian distribution as

$$p(x\mid a,A)=\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}x^\top A^{-1}x+x^\top A^{-1}a-\frac{1}{2}a^\top A^{-1}a\right)$$
