The implication is that for accurate prediction and planning of policies to mitigate change, Australia's environmental planners should include a decision tree as one of the techniques for modeling and forecasting carbon emissions. Some ML algorithms could have performed better in terms of their computational efficiency; however, the combination and choice of parameters for such algorithms during the grid-search process could be computationally laborious in general and hence, to a marginal extent, could limit our results in those aspects. This study was for regression, future research could investigate the classification problems pertaining to Australia's carbon emissions.
