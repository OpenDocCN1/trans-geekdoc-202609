Based on the geographical area, the urban emissions causing air pollution have been analyzed and mapped by Asgari, Farnaghi, and Ghaemi (2017). Apache Spark was used to study Tehran from 2009 to 2013. The accuracy was accurately predicted by Naive Bayes and logistic regression algorithms. In the conclusion, the data was accurately forecast by Naive Bayes in comparison to other machine learning algorithms that were used to identify air quality groups. So strong results were obtained in this Apache Spark.

In Zhu, Cai, Yang, and Zhou (2018), optimization and regularization techniques were used to predict the air pollutant for the different days. The value air pollutants that were predicted in the paper were that of sulfur dioxide, particulate matter (PM$_{2.5}$), and ozone. From the two stations were used for prediction. For O$_{3}$ and $SO_{2}$ were predicted by one station and $O_{3}$ and PM$_{2.5}$ by the other. The data was modeled based on similarity, and for grouping, linear regression was used.
