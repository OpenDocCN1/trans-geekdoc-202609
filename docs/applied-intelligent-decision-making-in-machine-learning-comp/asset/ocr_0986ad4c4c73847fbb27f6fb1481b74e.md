An RNN is a mysterious kind of artificial neural network having a loop in it to allow information to persist. They are the same as normal neural networks, but have multiple copies of similar networks. In this chapter, the GRU network, a special type of recurrent network introduced by Kyngyun Cho et al. in 2014 [29], is implemented to predict the foreign currency exchange rates. GRU model works similarly to LSTM [30] but fewer gates and modified networks. It works exceptionally well on various problems and is now widely implemented.
