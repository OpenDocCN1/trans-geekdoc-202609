SLFA perform with 100% accuracy. DE performs 100% with KNN, and SVM performs 99.67% with ANN. In the case of the leukemia dataset, BSLA-PSO performs at 100% with all classifiers, whereas BSLA performs at 100% with KNN only. Other approaches perform much less well. With AML/AML and BSLA-PSO, BSLA performs at 100% with all classifiers, whereas DE performs at 100% with SVM. In the ADCA dataset, BSLA-PSO performs at 100% with all classifiers, but with the CNS dataset the performance of BSLA-PSO is better than all other feature selection approaches and achieves an accuracy of 91.89%, 90.45%, and 89.67% with KNN, ANN, and SVM, respectively. The datasets considered for this experiment were binary in nature, so in the future we will extend this work to multiclass classification problems.
