## 7.6 CONCLUSION

In this chapter, BSFLA-PSO is a novel hybrid algorithm based on combination of binary shuffled frog leaping algorithm and PSO. In the primary stage, 50 genes were identified after that BSFLA-PSO was implemented; classification was done with KNN. If we deeply analyze performance, we find that BSFLA-PSO provides outstanding performance with other learning, namely BSFLA, Basic PSO, DE, SFLA-PSO, wPSO, and SFLA. With AML/AML and BSFLA-PSO, BSFLA performs at 100% with all classifiers, whereas DE performs at 100%. In the ADCA dataset, BSFLA-PSO performs at 100% with all classifiers, but with CNS dataset the performance of BSFLA-PSO is better than all other feature selection approaches and achieves accuracy of 91.89%, 90.45%, and 89.67% with KNN, ANN, and SVM, respectively. The datasets considered for experiment were binary in nature, so in the future we will extend this work to multiclass classification.
