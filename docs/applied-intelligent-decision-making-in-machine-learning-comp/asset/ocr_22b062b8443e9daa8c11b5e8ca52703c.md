# 3.1 INTRODUCTION

Optical character recognition (OCR) for Indic languages has recently gained increasing popularity due to its high usage in many real-time applications. Most of the earliest works have shown higher recognition performance for languages like English, Japanese, and Chinese. However, the performance for Odia languages is still far from real-time demands. Odia is one of the constitutional languages of India, specifically used in states of Odisha, Jharkhand, and West Bengal. There are about 27 million people who use Odia as their basic language of communication. The major challenge in modeling an Odia OCR is the detection of characters with higher accuracy. Odia script contains more than 400 character classes that include vowels, consonants, matras (vowel allographs), compound characters
