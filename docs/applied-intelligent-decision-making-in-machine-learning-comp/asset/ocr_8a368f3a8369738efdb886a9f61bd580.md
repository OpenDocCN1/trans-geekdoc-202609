Higher values of the Silhouette Coefficient and the Calinski-Harabaz Index indicate the effectiveness of the clustering technique for the pollution dataset that was used. Thus, the values provided for each clustering technique and the comparison of these values suggest that the weighted ensemble cluster algorithm provides better results compared to the other methods. This algorithm has better cluster quality for all three measures with 0.75, 2369.59, and 0.56 index for the Silhouette method, the Calinski-Harabaz Index, and the DB Index respectively. It is better than the traditional cluster quality. Visualization of the results in a Geography of India reveals the severity of air pollution at various places. This can help analyze the degree of pollution at various places, for correct measures. One of the limitations of this model is that the results do not cover every area of the country, as the air quality data is unavailable for remote areas. This limitation can be overcome by extrapolating the results for surrounding areas. In conclusion, the weighted ensemble cluster algorithm is a highly effective technique to forecast air quality in any chosen location.
