## 10.4 CONCLUSION

This chapter has described the implementation of a special type of RNN, the GRU network for the exchange rate forecasting of five currencies (AUD, CAD, GBP, INR, and JPY). The GRU model reduces the complex structure of LSTM to two gated architectures having update and reset gates, and it also decreases the number of iterations needed to execute. This leads to fewer calculations which make the proposed model faster than LSTM. The performance measure of LSTM and GRU is carried out by applying different window sizes. For sliding window 7, the GRU model gives the best results for all five currencies; for sliding window sizes 10 and 13, LSTM performs better in some cases, with GRU results being better for the rest. Thus, by overall performance, GRU outperforms LSTM with greater accuracy and efficiency.
