FIGURE 4 Geological factors.

Geology shows the formation of the area and also the reason for the occurrence of slope-instability-related natural hazards. Distance to thrust, lineament density, and lithologies are all compactly developed main factors of geology (Figure 4.5). The Geological Survey of India (GSI) provided us with information about these factors to establish a geological map of East Sikkim.
