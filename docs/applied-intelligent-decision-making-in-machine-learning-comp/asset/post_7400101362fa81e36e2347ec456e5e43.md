## 2.1 INTRODUCTION

Machine learning (ML) applications are ubiquitous in the digital age. The ML methods are gaining attention due to their ability to solve complex problems and provide a decision support system from large volumes of data. Software packages are available for different ML methods in the form of various programming languages (e.g., Python, R, MATLAB). Sufficient online resources and publications are discussing the theoretical framework of ML methods and practical usage of the software modules in various platforms. However, to our knowledge, resources dealing with the concepts and mechanisms of the ML methods and the sequence of intermediate operations performed inside the algorithms with a working code are not available. The ML methods are usually presented as “blackbox” products providing no insight into the mechanism or allowing for modification to suit user needs. It will always be desirable to develop ML algorithms from fundamental principles, especially using open free software, to fit the fitting and analyzing requirements of the user. Therefore, it is proposed to focus on decoding the two common ML methods and developing working codes using open R software and demonstrate the applications.

Among the fields that successfully employ ML methods, modern agriculture is one of the sectors that produces an enormous amount of data from different element components, such as yield monitors, soil and fertilizer equipment.
