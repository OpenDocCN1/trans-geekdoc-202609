# Decoding Common Machine Learning Methods

Roundness = \frac{4A}{\pi a^2} (2.3)

Solidity = \frac{A}{A_C} (2.4)

Reciprocal aspect ratio = \frac{b}{a} (2.5)

Rectangularity = \frac{A}{W_{BB} \times W_{BB}} (2.6)

Feret major axis ratio = \frac{D_F}{a} (2.7)

Area ratio = \frac{A}{D_{minF} \times D_{minF}} (2.8)

Diameter range = D_F - D_{minF} (2.9)

Eccentricity = \sqrt{\frac{(b/2)^2}{(a/2)^2}} (2.10)

Area-perimeter ratio = \frac{A}{p^2} (2.11)

Hollowness = \frac{A_C - A}{A_C} \times 100 (2.12)

Feret aspect ratio = \frac{D_F}{D_{minF}} (2.13)

where $a$ is the major axis length (pixel); $b$ is the minor axis length (pixel); $A$ is the area (pixel$^2$); $p$ is the perimeter (pixel); $A_C$ is the convex area (pixel); $H_{BB}$ is the bounding box height (pixel); $W_{BB}$ is the bounding box width (pixel); $D_F$ is the Feret diameter, also called the maximum caliper diameter (pixel); and $D_{minF}$ is the mini-Feret diameter, also called the minimum caliper diameter (pixel).
