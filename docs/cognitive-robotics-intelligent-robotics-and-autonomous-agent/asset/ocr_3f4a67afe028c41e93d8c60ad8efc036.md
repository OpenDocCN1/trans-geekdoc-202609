By 32 weeks, nearly every part of the body is sensitive to heat, cold, pressure, and pain.

Auditory complete 18-24 weeks, voice hearing 25 weeks and recognition 27 weeks

Vision week 18: when the eyes are still closed, a baby's retina can detect a small amount of light filtering through a mother's tissue. By week 33, the pupils of the eye can now detect light and constrict and dilate, allowing your baby to see dim shapes. Studies shining a bright light on the belly of a woman at 37 weeks have shown a baby's heart rate speeding up in response, or the baby turning toward the light.
