# FIGURE 7.9 Ransomware

If a computer gets infected with ransomware, it may difficult or impossible to
open the files on that machine. This is one of the reasons it's so important to
constantly back up your data and encrypt it yourself; then, in the event that it
is stolen, you can tell the threat actors to keep your encrypted data because it
is useless to them, and you can just restore your backup. There are many
ways to back up either locally or to a cloud-based provider. It is important to
be prepared for a disaster like this.
