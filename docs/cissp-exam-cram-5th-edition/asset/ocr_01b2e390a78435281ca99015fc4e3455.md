DSL modems are always connected to the Internet; therefore, you do not
have to dial in to make a connection. As long as your computer is powered
on, it is connected to the Internet and is ready to transmit and receive data.
This is the primary security concern with DSL. An advantage of DSL is that
it maintains a more fixed speed than cable modems typically do. Table 5.9
provides details about the different DSL types.
