Crypters can use any encryption scheme, from AES or RSA to Blowfish, or they might use more basic obfuscation techniques, such as XOR obfuscation, Base64 encoding, or ROT 13. These techniques are used to conceal the contents of the executable program, making it undetectable by antivirus software and resistant to reverse-engineering efforts.
