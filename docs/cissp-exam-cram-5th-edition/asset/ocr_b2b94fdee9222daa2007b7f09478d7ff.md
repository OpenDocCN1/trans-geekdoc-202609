+   11. Formulas used for quantitative analysis include:
+   EF (exposure factor) = Percentage of an asset loss caused by an identified threat
+   SLE (single loss expectancy) = Asset value x Exposure factor
+   ARO (annualized rate of occurrence) = Estimated frequency a threat will occur within a year
+   ALE (annualized loss expectancy) = Single loss expectancy x Annualized rate of occurrence

+   12. Threat modeling techniques:

+   - **STRIDE:** Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information disclosure, Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege

+   - **PASTA:** Process for Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis

+   - **DREAD:** Disaster, Reproducibility, Exploitability, Affected Users, and Discoverability

+   13. Risk is dealt with in the following ways (which can be combined):

+   - **Risk reduction:** Implementing a countermeasure to alter or reduce the risk

+   - **Risk transference:** Purchasing insurance to transfer a portion or the entire potential cost of a loss to a third party

+   - **Risk acceptance:** Accepting the potential cost and loss of a risk

+   14. Security policies can be regulatory, advisory, or informative.

+   15. Senior management is ultimately responsible for the security of the organization.

+   16. Types of security documents:

+   - **Policies:** General statements produced by senior management

+   - **Standards:** Tactical documents that are more specific than policies
