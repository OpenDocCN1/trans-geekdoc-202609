Then it is transmitted over some medium (wire, coaxial, optical, or wireless) toward the target device, and then it travels back up the stack to the application. From the bottom of the stack, the seven layers of the OSI model are the physical, data link, network, transport, session, presentation, and application layers (see Figure 5.1). People often remember this order by using one of the many acronyms that have been thought up over the years. My favorite one is “Please Do Not Throw Sausage Pizza Away”:

+   - Please (physical—Layer 1)
+   - Do (data link—Layer 2)
+   - Not (network—Layer 3)
+   - Throw (transport—Layer 4)
+   - Sausage (session—Layer 5)
+   - Pizza (presentation—Layer 6)
+   - Away (application—Layer 7)
