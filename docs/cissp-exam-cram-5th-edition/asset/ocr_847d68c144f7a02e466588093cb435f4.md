**Defined** Defined processes and procedures are in place and used.

**Quantitatively managed** Data is collected and analyzed. A process improvement program is used.

**Optimizing** Continuous process improvement is in place and considered

**Note**

The five levels of the CMMI, as shown in Figure 9.5, have
