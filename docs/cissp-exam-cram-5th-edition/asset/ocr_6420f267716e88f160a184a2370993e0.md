71. Investigation types include, administrative, criminal, civil, and regulatory.

72. Clipping levels are the thresholds implemented for certain types of errors or mistakes that are allowed without alarm.

73. A duress code is used when an employee is being threatened. The duress code should be changed regularly and should be easy to remember.

74. The three main types of digital forensics are network forensics, software forensics, and hard drive (or computer) forensics.

75. The chain of custody examines who collected, transported, stored, controlled, and accessed evidence.

76. Common power anomalies:

+   - **Blackout:** Prolonged loss of power
+   - **Brownout:** Power degradation that is low and less than normal
+   - **Sag:** Momentary low voltage
+   - **Fault:** Momentary loss of power
+   - **Spike:** Momentary high voltage
+   - **Surge:** Prolonged high voltage
+   - **Noise:** Interference superimposed onto the power line
+   - **Transient:** Noise disturbances of a short duration
+   - **Inrush:** Initial surge of power at startup

77. Facility management requires review of the facility:

+   - **Proper construction and design:** Should give attention to walls, doors, ceilings, windows, flooring, HVAC, and fire detection and suppression. Must know where cut-off valves and switches are located.
+   - **Computing area:** Should be neither on the top floor nor in the basement but should be near the core of the building and offer protection on all six sides.
+   - **HVAC:** Should be separate for the data center and have positive pressurization to keep contaminants and smoke out of the facility.
