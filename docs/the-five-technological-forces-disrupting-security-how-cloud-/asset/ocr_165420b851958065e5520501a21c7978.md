One of the earliest customers for my company's cloud-based services was a McDonald's franchise owner who had five locations. He wanted to make sure each of his managers opened their respective stores by 4:30 a.m. Every morning, he sat in front of his browser with a cup of coffee and watch the activity log scroll before him. If a store had not been accessed on time, he was on the phone to find out why and fix the problem. Fast forward to the present. Owners can have this degree of insight into their operations, but they are no longer tethered to a desktop browser. This franchise owner can now enjoy this insight from his smartphone while still in bed or even traveling on vacation.

A related challenge for multifacity businesses is synchronization of data across multiple security systems. Prior to cloud-based systems, many businesses were saddled with having an independent security system installed at each physical location. The systems didn't share information with each other.

In practice, this meant that a person who had permission at one facility might not have permission at other facilities (unless their data were laboriously entered at each site again and again and again). The workload, duplication of data was error-prone, particularly when people left an organization and their data were not deleted from all systems. This is where mobile and the cloud systems behind it address this challenge. One of the most common events was that the security administrator or HR department dashing to the security system is employee departure, particularly a disgruntled one. Who wants the nightmare of trying to remove privileges from all sites, especially when those sites sit hundreds of miles apart? With a mobile application backed by a cloud-based security system, you can take care of it in a single swipe.
