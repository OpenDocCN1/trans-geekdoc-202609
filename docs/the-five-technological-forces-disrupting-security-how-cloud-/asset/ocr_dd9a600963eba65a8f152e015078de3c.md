The second will be composed of other devices—call alien sensors—that have a primary mission other than security whose exhaust creates valuable input for analytics. Corporate departments outside the security organization will be sponsors of these nonempty deployments, just as non-IT departments were the sponsors of rogue cloud services in the early days of SaaS. The challenge for security managers is to ensure that these alien IoT devices do not introduce new risks.

To really convinced that what's old and what's new, look no further than the advertising for home automation. The primary pitch is still "peace of mind," a phrase the industry has now used. Home automation adds many more IoT subsystems and has made them simpler to install and use, but safety is still the number consideration. That's why alarm and security companies pick the list of home insurers.

For the Industrial Internet of Things (IoT), our legacy of system integration serves us well. However, it presents new challenges and takes us into an arena with more competitors in the ring. IT and cabling companies in particular see IoT as a huge opportunity, from the SMB to the enterprise, and are jumping at the chance to expand their offerings. If the goal is for all of these devices to interact seamlessly, somewhat plays to their traditional strengths in IT.

# GROUND ZERO

The security industry sits at ground zero of this upheaval. To switch between gears and metas, we are in pole position for IoT adoption, given our history of creation, selling, and installing many of its precursors in both residential and commercial settings. That's why I say it's race to lose.

Not that we are losing, everyone is still in the first lap with many more to go. A sign of our strength, the largest group of consumer IoT devices deployed so far is for home automation and residential security. Market studies confirm that security is more important to buyers than any other feature of IoT in the home.

IoT spans both residential and commercial security installations and leverages industry incumbency in both. It's predicted to flourish in far greater numbers in consumer in commercial and industrial settings in residential and consumer.
