Risk management is the methodology to determine whether any of these approaches is worthwhile. Risk analysis is a part of many kinds of business intelligence: insurance, corporate investment, financial portfolio management, and of physical science. The fundamental principle is simple: if the probability of something bad happening times the cost it will inflict is greater than the cost to prevent it, then it is worth doing. This means that extremely low-probability events, even if they have dire consequences, are not likely to receive funding. At the other end of the scale, high-likelihood events will receive more investment, even if their individual occurrence is less costly.
