Commercial security applications of IoT technologies fit squarely into this industrial refinement of the basic IoT premise:

+   - **Intelligent**: Device intelligence in electronic security has been growing steadily. Complex cryptographic and public key infrastructure (PKI) functions are now performed routinely in small, smart devices like card readers used in high-assurance applications.
- **Interconnected**: The adoption of IP by the electronic security has driven much higher connectivity. While these interfaces were initially used over LAN, they paved the way for the Internet. Later, suppliers leveraged IP connectivity with the requisite firmware for connecting to cloud services.
- **Performance**: Performance increases have enabled new behaviors in intelligent devices, most notably in the field of video analytics, where complex algorithms and high-bandwidth data streams demand a certain minimum level of intelligence before they are actually useful.
- **Operating costs**: Cloud-connected intelligent devices are a striking example of reducing operating costs. They are essential to the TCO improvements attributed to SaaS services that manage enterprise security.

In short, yes, IoT matters a lot.
