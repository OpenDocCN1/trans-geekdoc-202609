# STANDARDS ORGANIZATIONS AND CONSORTIA

Open-standards organizations are defined by their inclusiveness (anyone can join) and adherence to a published set of procedural rules governing how standards are proposed, created, and subsequently modified. They are operated under the auspices of large standards bodies such as the American National Standards Institute (ANSI), the International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC), the International Organization for Standardization (ISO), and the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). They are generally open to any organization or individual who wishes to join, attend proceedings, or submit comments. There are no membership fees and no pay to play.

Standards consortia, though often mistaken for open standards organizations, are closed-membership groups that usually charge a fee and are free to determine their own procedural norms. They look very similar from the outside because their membership lists and scope of activities. They are nevertheless an important part of the standards landscape and can often move more quickly than formal standards organizations.

What follows is a brief overview of a number of open-standards organizations and consortia that are currently working on IoT standards to reveal the physical security industry.

# Security Industry Association Standards Committee

The open-standards organization closest to the interests of most security organizations is the Security Industry Association Standards Committee. (Full disclosure, I have served as the chairman of the SIA Standards Committee for the past 6 years.) The committee is an active ANSI Standards Development Organization (SDO) and so can have its specifications ratified as formal standards with both national and international organizations. The purpose of the group is to develop, discover, influence, and provide education on standards relevant to security applications. It is predominantly composed of members of the Security Industry Association, although its membership is open to any-one who wishes to participate.
