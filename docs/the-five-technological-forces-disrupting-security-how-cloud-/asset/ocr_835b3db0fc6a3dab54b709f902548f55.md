Anyone who has created a new account at a website will be familiar with the option to use one's own e-mail address or choose to leverage an existing identity at one of the providers just listed. The attraction of using, say, your Facebook ID is that you avoid creating another log-in and password combination that you will soon lose and/or forget. In this respect, the public has already voted with its keyboards and thumbs about who will be its favorite identity providers. We're even seeing B2B companies like Salesforce.com acting as identity providers for enterprise customers.
