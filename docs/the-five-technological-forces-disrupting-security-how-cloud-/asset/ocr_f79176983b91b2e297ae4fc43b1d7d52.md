# Mobile Starts With Cloud

The foundation of a mobile strategy is not actually a mobile strategy: it’s a cloud strategy. Over the long haul, mobile apps can only be delivered as a cloud service.

Attempting to deliver broadly available mobile services from a local server runs into problems out of the gate. The first compromise is that you must provide a hole in your firewall. This lets mobile applications—and pesky hackers—burrow through your company’s IT perimeter to reach the back-end servers that supply data and execute commands. This architectural compromise and the ensuing vulnerabilities arise because your mobile app can’t go to a single URL, which is normally the case for widely used mobile services. Your services originate from a different address than all the other companies using app. In effect, each end-user organization needs to have its own web service-up.

You are also counting on installers to get all the cyber provisions right and every time they do. Given the vast differences in network infrastructure from one client to the next and the limited network expertise of any individual installer, the odds of getting this right time are less than 100%. Serving your own mobile population from inside your corporate network also tightly binds your mobile applications to your IT infrastructure. If it changes (and it always does), your mobile solution may stop working or need to be reconfigured, along with every single application.

Who wants that headache?
