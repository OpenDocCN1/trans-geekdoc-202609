# THE EMERGENCE OF DATA ANALYTICS IN SECURITY

Physical security will follow the path of these leading industries as the cost of analytics to decline and the need for better information continues to grow. Providers in this new market will supply a variety of data products and services to security organizations, suppliers, system integrators, and external interests such as law enforcement and insurance.

The primary means of data aggregation for these new data services will be via cloud-based security applications. On-premise systems are unlikely to play a role because they cannot capture sufficient volumes or varieties of data from a single-user installation. The distribution of analytic products will be automated via the web or via professional service engagements for specialized needs.

These new data products will focus on a variety of analytics:

+   - Norms and benchmarks for individual families
- Industry and customer trend analysis
- Advisory services for customers
- Neighborhood, regional, and national statistics
