In most cases, high turbidity in water can cause increased water temperatures because suspended particles absorb heat and reduce the amount of light penetrating the water and high levels of suspended solids. Additionally, turbidity is an optical property that does not directly reflect the quantity or type of suspended solids (Kit et al., 2005).
