# Phytoremediation of Domestic Wastewater with the Internet of Things and Machine Learning Techniques

Phytoremediation of Domestic Wastewater with the Internet of Things and Machine Learning Techniques highlights the most recent advances in phytoremediation of wastewater utilizing phytoremediation methods for environmental sustainability and the experiences using phytoremediation methods for remediation of wastewater. It also examines the various interrelated disciplines relating to phytoremediation technologies and plots industry's best practices to share this technology widely, as well as the latest findings and strategies. It serves as a nexus between artificial intelligence, environmental sustainability and bioremediation for advanced students and practising professionals in the field.
