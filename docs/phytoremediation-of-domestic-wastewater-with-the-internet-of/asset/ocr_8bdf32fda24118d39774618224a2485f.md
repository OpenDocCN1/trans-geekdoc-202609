# 5.4.5 Method of Water Sample Collection

Sampling was conducted every 2days at 6-, 12- and 24-hour detention times for 14 days. The influent samples were collected directly from the discharge point of the secondary treatment, while the effluent samples were collected from each of the hydroponic tanks. The influent and effluent samples were collected aseptically in 250 mL bottles. The collected water samples were transferred to the environmental laboratory for water quality tests. In the second stage of the cultivation process, the influent and effluent water samples were collected every 2 days at 24 hours retention time. In some cases, the water samples were preserved in a refrigerator before laboratory analysis.
