# 2.3 PRELIMINARY TREATMENT OF WASTEWATER

In the preliminary phase of wastewater treatment, solid debris transported by sewers are removed. These solid debris be floating particles such as wood, feces, grains of rock or sand and rags. The sewage is passed through holes or chambers to remove floating debris, and at regular intervals, the built-up waste on the bars is scraped off (Jasim, 2020). Grits are removed by reducing the flow velocity to a point where the silt and grit settle, while the organic solids remain suspended (Abdel-Raouf et al., 2019).
