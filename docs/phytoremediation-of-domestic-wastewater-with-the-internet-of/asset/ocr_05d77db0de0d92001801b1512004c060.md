Despite the outliers indicated by other models, the overall results show the capability of ANFIS-M1 with regard to the spread of the graph. MLR generally provided the extent of the outliers, followed by ANFIS-M2, ANN-M1 and ANN-M2. The outliers observed in the ANN models can be attributed to the research by Abbas et al. (2017); Lee et al. (2018); Liu et al. (2013) and Pomeroy (2007), who reported that ANN models are not prone to over-fitting training which is the reason associated with the outlier. Additionally satisfactory performance obtained in the model combinations might be attributed to the direct relations established by the positive correlation between the observed turbidity (Turbi) and the other water quality parameters. These findings were similar to the results reported by Zhu and Heddam (2019).

Furthermore, a two-dimensional Taylor diagram was used to depict the pearson's evaluation for the best models. $R$-package was used in plotting the Taylor diagrams. The Taylor diagram summarizes and highlights statistical indices that included R, RMSE, and the standard deviation between the observed and predicted values (Ghorbani et al., 2018; Taylor, 2001). In addition, Taylor diagrams offers a means to graphically illustrate how exactly a pattern (or group of patterns) fits observations.
