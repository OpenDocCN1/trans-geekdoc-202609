# 7.4.4 TOTAL DISSOLVED SOLIDS (TDS) SENSOR

TDS sensor is used to measure the dissolved solids in water samples. TDS sensor can be connected to IoT systems for easy application (Anuradha et al., 2018). Additionally, TDS sensors accept input and output voltage ranging from 3.3–5.5V and 0–2.3V, respectively, making them suitable with 3.3 or 5 V control systems. The excitation source is an alternating current signal that prevents the probe from being polarized and extends the durability of the probe. TDS sensor has a measuring range of 0–1000ppm (Baldwin et al., 2019). TDS sensor is waterproof and can be used in hydroponic systems and wastewater treatment plants (WWTP) monitoring (Anuradha et al., 2018). The TDS sensor is shown in Figure 7.5.

# 7.5 LIQUID CRYSTAL DISPLAY (LCD)

LCD device is a flat panel that adjusts the light-modulating characteristics of liquid crystals and polarizers to display different characters and symbols. LCDs consist of 16-character and 2-line alphanumeric (16×2) LCD interface linked to the microcontroller, which enables the device to connect to E-block I/O ports. The data on the LCD is in serial format and can be powered by a 5 V source. The optimum source of 5 V is the E-block’s Multi programmer or 5 V fixed power source (Priyadharshini et al., 2018).
