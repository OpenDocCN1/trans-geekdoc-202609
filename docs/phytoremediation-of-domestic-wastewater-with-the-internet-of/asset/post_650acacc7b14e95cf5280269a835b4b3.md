## 6.2.9 STATISTICAL ANALYSIS

The readings of the physicochemical analysis were recorded in triplicate and the average were expressed as mean ± standard deviation. Additionally, Microsoft® Excel statistical package was used in calculating the mean and standard deviation. Analysis of variance (ANOVA) IBM® SPSS version 25 package was used in evaluating the significance of difference and t-test (SPSS, 2017). Equations 6.2 and 6.3 were used in calculating the dilution factor and percentage reduction efficiency (Valipour et al., 2015).

$$Dilution\ factor = \frac{Total\ volume\ of\ diluted\ sample}{Volume\ of\ wastewater\ sample} \quad (6.2)$$

$$Reduction\ efficiency(\%) = \frac{Ci - Ce}{Ci} \times 100 \quad (6.3)$$

Where Ci is the influent concentration and Ce is the effluent concentration.
