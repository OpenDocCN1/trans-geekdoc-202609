# 4.5.3.3 Description of E. crassipes

E. crassipes plants are composed of stalked rosette leaves, feathery roots, slender root stocks and flowers arranged in clusters of leaf axils. The leaves are broadly circular, gently curved, often undulating sides and have a diameter and height of 10–20cm and 0.5–1 m, respectively. Additionally, the leaves are dark green in colour and are borne on inflated petioles resembling bladders (Ecoport, 2011). The leaf veins are numerous, thick, longitudinal and densely packed. As the axial bud develops, leaves arise by rupturing a tabular leaf-like structure called a prophyll. Furthermore, the flowers have six petals with purplish-blue colour and yellow uppermost (Ecoport, 2011). The diagram of the plants displaying the flowers is presented in Figure 4.4.
