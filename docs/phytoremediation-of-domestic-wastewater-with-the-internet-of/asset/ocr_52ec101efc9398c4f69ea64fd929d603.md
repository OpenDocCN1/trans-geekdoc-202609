# 4.6 CONCLUSION

This chapter presents the significance of macrophrophy in the wastewater. The evaluation of aquatic weed plants can be beneficial in these processes as they have tremendous capacity to absorb contaminants from water and they can be easily cultivated and managed. High-valued compounds used as feedstock for the generation of a wide scope of products by aquatic plants, as they are a promising sustainable wellspring of supplements. Biomass obtained from phytoremediation can be utilized for biofuel and energy production, or for charcoal generation, fertilizer and food supplement.
