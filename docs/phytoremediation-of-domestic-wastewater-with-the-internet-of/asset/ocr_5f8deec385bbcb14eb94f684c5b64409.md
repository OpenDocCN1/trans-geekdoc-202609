# 5.3 NUTRIENT RECOVERY BY AQUATIC PLANTS

Tertiary treatment may be utilized to polish wastewater that has passed through the primary and secondary stages of conventional treatment methods. Tertiary treatment of effluents is a progression of extra strides following secondary treatment to further reduce the amount of phosphorus, nitrogen, metals, organics, phosphates and turbidity in wastewater. Summary, tertiary sewage is treated when the wastewater is used for drinking, agricultural and leisure purposes (Garba & Pepper, 2015). Besides, plants play a significant role in supplying oxygen through its roots, which aid the processes that lead to degradation of pollutants (Stewart et al., 2008). These processes include absorption of nutrients and heavy metals, denitrification and nitrification, and other biological processes in the treatment systems. Furthermore, absorption of inorganic and organic pollutants through volatilization is the essential process of nutrient uptake (Stewart et al., 2008). Thus, emergent, submerged and floating macrophytes have been used in reducing pollutants from different types of wastewater (Murad et al., 2014). This is due to the ability of the macrophytes to grow in the wastewater while accumulating and storing contaminants or nutrients in their roots and shoots through hydroponic cultivation of plants and transporting into polluted waters (Sharma & Gaur, 1995). Macrophytes accumulate substantial amount of pollutants: phosphates, nitrates, ammonia, heavy metals, reactive compounds and toxic compounds at low cost using energy eco-friendly approaches with limited waste disposal. They also produce large quantities of biomass roots through the use of renewable resources. Recently, the discovery of aquatic plants as phytoremediators and feedstock for the production of biofuels has led to its usage for gathering nutrients from the roots to the rhizosphere and to filter suspended solids. Several researchers maintain that macrophytes can discharge oxygen from roots to encompassing rhizosphere while providing aerobic conditions for nitrification occurrence.
