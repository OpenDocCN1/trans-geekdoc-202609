# 6.4 CONCLUSION

This chapter presented the outcome of research on the potential of selected macro-phytes in phytoremediation of wastewater. The results obtained from this research prove that the three aquatic plants were efficient in tertiary treatment of wastewater at different retention times. Thus, the lowering efficiency trend for the three systems following the pattern of 24 hours > 12 hours>6 hours. Furthermore, the results of this investigation suggested that the 6 hours retention time was insufficient for the test plants to absorb or break down the ammonia nitrogen contained in the influent samples. In most cases, the highest removal efficiencies were recorded on the 12th and 14th days. Therefore, it is clear from these findings that retention time is a crucial factor that influences and regulates the rate of nitrate uptake and assimilation by phytoremediation systems.
