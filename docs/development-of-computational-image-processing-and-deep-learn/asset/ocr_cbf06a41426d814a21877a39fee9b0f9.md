Increased model complexity and overfitting is tantamount to decreasing bias (error due to the used model’s incapability of capturing the underlying model) and increasing variance (error due to sensitivity to noise in the data). The opposite called underfitting hence typically shows high bias and low variance. This dilemma of finding the optimal model complexity is depicted in Figure 2.18. [186]
