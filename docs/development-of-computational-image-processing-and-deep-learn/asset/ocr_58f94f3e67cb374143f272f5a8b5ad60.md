# 2.4.1.4 Optimizing algorithms

Even though a variety of optimizing algorithms to minimize the loss function have been developed, only two will be shortly addressed in this section, which are the stochastic gradient descent (SGD) and the ADAM optimizer. The choice is based on the fact that these two perform well for common problems and are among the most widely used. Furthermore, of the three different networks implemented in this work, one works with SGD and the other two with ADAM.
