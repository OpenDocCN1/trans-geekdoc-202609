The higher reinforcement in 0°, i.e. flow direction, compared to 90° is just as recognizable as a drift of the highest stiffness values to about 5° instead of 0°, which was already seen in the CT evaluation. These observations apply more or less to both the CF and the GF material. The most noticeable difference between the two is that the ratio between the 0° and 90° directions differs. The CF material is about three times stiffer in the flow direction than at 90°, whereas the ratio for the GF material is only about two. This is due to the higher stiffness of the carbon fibers themselves.
