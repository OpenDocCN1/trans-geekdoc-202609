Both outer layers (considering the x direction) have higher values concerning the 33 component of the tensor, hence are oriented more towards the z-direction, which coincides with the findings of the evaluation in the other plane before. While they also show a slightly higher 22-component, thus are more oriented in y-direction (extrusion direction), the overall tensor behavior is very 11-component (x-direction) dominant. This again simplifies a geometric reconstruction, compared to similar studies where authors found a more significant orientation in y-direction, which will also be discussed in Section 5.
