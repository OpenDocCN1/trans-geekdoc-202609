\( m'_i = \sum_{j=0}^{1} p_j(z_j)^i, \quad i = 1, 2, 3; \) (2.85)

where \( p_0 \) and \( p_1 \) signify the fractions of below-threshold and above-threshold pixels, respectively. Preserving the first three moments of \( i \) in \( o \) means,

\( m'_i = m_i, \quad i = 1, 2, 3 \) (2.86)

has to hold. Furthermore,

\( p_0 + p_1 = 1. \) (2.87)

The last two equations can be translated into the following equation system

\( p_0 z_0^0 + p_1 z_1^0 = m_0, \)
\( p_0 z_0^1 + p_1 z_1^1 = m_1, \)
\( p_0 z_0^2 + p_1 z_1^2 = m_2, \)
\( p_0 z_0^3 + p_1 z_1^3 = m_3. \) (2.88)

\( m_i \) is calculated with the help of Equation (2.84) and \( m_0 \) is defined to be 1. Then, the equations are solved to obtain \( p_0 \) and \( p_1 \), which inevitably leads to choosing \( t \) such that

\( p_0 = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{z_j \le t} n_j. \) (2.89)

There might not exist an exact value for \( t \) fulfilling this condition, so the closest gray value should be chosen. [48]
