# KARLSRUHER INSTITUT FÜR TECHNOLOGIE (KIT)
SCHRIFTENREIHE DES INSTITUTS FÜR ANGEWANDTE MATERIALIEN

Discontinuously fiber reinforced polymers exhibit complex microstructures. Quantities to characterize the latter have been developed over time, such as the fiber volume content or fiber orientation distributions, which can be acquired through computed tomography images and subsequent image processing. This work deals with the development of both deterministic and AI based methods in this context, especially considering challenges of contrast and resolution with carbon fibers and scale-bridging issues.
