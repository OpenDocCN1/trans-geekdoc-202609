Nonetheless, Benkler was far from alone in to what he saw as a seemingly inexorable slide towards a ‘winner takes all star system’ (Lanier, 2014: 35), where an increasingly reduced number of corporations and individuals would see ever-higher profits in return for their growing domination of the Internet. Lanier suggested that a more equitable online economy would be possible, at the expense of redesigning the Internet to include ‘two-way links’ (Lanier, 2014: 218), which would put revenue and data capture within reach of every user rather than simply in the hands of those who already hold the financial and information advantage.
