# Abstract

The distributed ledger technology, often known as the blockchain, has attracted the attention of many people working in the financial industry, especially those who are active in payment, clearing, and settlement, because it promises to be more efficient and more resilient than the existing systems. Several new initiatives, partnerships, investment rounds, and white papers have been launched about distributed ledger technology through various public announcements and news pieces published during the last several months. Research or initiatives connected to **Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)** have also been announced by central banks as part of efforts to assist in the development of this technology in the commercial sector. These efforts are part of an effort to assist in the development of this technology in the commercial sector.
