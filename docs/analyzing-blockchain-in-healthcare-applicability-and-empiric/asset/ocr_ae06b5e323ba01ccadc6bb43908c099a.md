# Objectives

The chapter investigate a study specific goals to identify the use cases of Blockchain Technology in healthcare, sample applications that have been created for those use cases, challenges and restrictions facing blockchain-based healthcare applications, current development methods used for those applications, and potential future research areas.

# Synopsis of chapter

The successful diagnosis and treatment of issues through secure data exchange are just a couple of the beneficial ways that blockchain is changing conventional medical practices. Because of Covid-19, medical professionals and the government are having trouble preserving and logging private information about individuals. Real-time patient monitoring data, long-term electronic medical records, and sensor data make up the data-intensive field of modern healthcare.

As technology develops, blockchain in healthcare can handle numerous issues. A wide range of players, including general practitioners, hospitals, medical service providers, and insurance companies, must be able to access this data with ease. In order to ensure, the privacy of the patient, the information that is provided is under control, with whom it is shared, and how it is used, healthcare-related data must be validated, safely maintained, and when shared. In various industries, including healthcare, blockchain has become the go-to technology stack for distributed authentication, safe data storage, and automated data analysis.
