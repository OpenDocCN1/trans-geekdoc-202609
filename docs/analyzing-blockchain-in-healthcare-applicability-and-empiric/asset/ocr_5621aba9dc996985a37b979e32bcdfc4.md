# Conclusion

Medical devices are the next frontier in the digital revolution and IoT is a key component of the IoMT. Patient’s real-time data and a patient-motioning system provided by the underlying technologies have transformed the present healthcare system. However, because of the reliance on cloud storage, IoMT poses a significant issue regarding data storage management, security, and privacy. The vast amount of medical data produced by IoMT is now housed in a centralized database. On the other hand, centralization of sensitive patient information creates a single point of failure, privacy, and security concerns.

The security and privacy of IoMT users, remain key concerns. Medical equipment may be integrated with the IoT to provide better patient comfort, lower costs, faster hospital treatment, and more personalized medical care via the IoMT. But those with limited bandwidth can’t pay for it. All these issues must be resolved with more focus, exploration, and innovation in the concerned field for a safer and more secure future.
