# Conclusion

There are several areas in which the COVID-19 epidemic has had an impact, from healthcare and banking to politics, economics, and education. Post COVID-19 global management might benefit greatly from Blockchain Technology. As a result, a wide range of use cases may benefit from blockchain’s core capabilities, including contact tracking and disaster assistance, patient information sharing and e-government, supply chain management, and online education. However, before blockchain can be employed appropriately for these objectives, several obstacles, such as legal, security, privacy, latency, throughput, scalability, and resource consumption, must be handled. After being launched with bitcoin, blockchain has gone a long way in terms of development. Researchers worldwide are striving to discover the many uses of blockchain. Its use in the healthcare industry is particularly intriguing, given the need to tightly control and monitor data access. Patients’ privacy may be jeopardized by existing systems prone to data breaches. In addition to its immutability and successful sharing mechanism, blockchain offers a number of other advantages.

Most of the suggested methods for combining blockchain with healthcare to combat COVID-19 are now in the modeling phase. Furthermore, these suggestions focus on one or two of the many issues that COVID-19 presents, but a comprehensive solution is lacking. As a result of the coronavirus’s sudden exponential growth, massive amounts of data have been created every day. There is a vast scope of blockchain applications in mitigating and fighting the challenges similar to the COVID-19 Pandemic.

In the next chapter, the study seeks to understand how health facilities adopt blockchain networks to satisfy their patients without any quagmire.
