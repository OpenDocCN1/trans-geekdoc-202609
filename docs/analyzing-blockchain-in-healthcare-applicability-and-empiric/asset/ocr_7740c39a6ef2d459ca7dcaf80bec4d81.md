# ## Market organizational structure

It is possible that a DLT arrangement will affect the architecture of the financial sector. The structure is an improvement over the one that is already being used in specific applications; nonetheless, it does not significantly alter the way business activities are carried out. The distributed ledger technology, often known as DLT, can potentially eliminate the need for some middlemen in certain implementations, such as an agreement with no constraints. This change in corporate operations has the potential to disrupt the competitive equilibrium in financial markets and have an impact on the way they are designed. Additionally, it may attract new non-bank players that are not currently covered or envisioned by the regulatory systems in place.
