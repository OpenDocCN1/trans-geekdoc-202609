The recent issues and widespread Coronavirus (COVID-19) further highlight the necessity of establishing dependable, resilient, and robust patient and health care systems. These critical issues can be addressed by blockchain technology. The usage of blockchain in healthcare information systems could be a beneficial distributed technology that can handle data consistency issues, system compatibility, and the accessibility of medical records. It is difficult to acquire, comprehend, use, and share health data since it is difficult to standardize across systems. The data collected are from various sources and kept in centralized IT systems, making it difficult to manage and share them. Patients’ data collection takes a long time and necessitates many resources. Health care systems can gain a complete picture of their patients, better care and treatment, increase communication, and improve health outcomes when their data is managed correctly and safely retrieved.

# Synopsis of chapter

Better data exchange among healthcare practitioners facilitates more accurate diagnoses, more effective treatments, and the ability of healthcare organizations to provide cost-effective care. To secure data security and integrity, blockchain technology can allow different participants in the healthcare value chain to share access to their networks while tracking data provenance and any changes made.
