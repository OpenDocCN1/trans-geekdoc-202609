# Ease of use

According to scholars, perceived ease of use is defined as “the degree to which an individual has to intend for using a particular system or a person believes that using a particular system would be free of effort.” Thus, this intent is to heighten or influence the usage of IT in organizations, especially the health sector where health professionals are faced with challenges of keeping records and updates of the patient. This said the perceived ease of use of blockchain facilitates and ensures a successful convey of patient’s medical records. The TAM model in tandem with the ease of use will instigate health practitioners to use technology or IT freely without any quagmire.

# Usefulness

Innately, the perceived usefulness of the TAM is to spur workers, especially in the health sector to adopt the use of technology (blockchain) in making their work simple. Technology has reduced or eliminated the workload of health professionals,, especially in emerging economies where records of patients were misplaced or exchanged with other patients, thus making their work cumbersome. As technology evolves, adopting TAM in the health sector would ensure the personnel’s effectiveness and efficiency, which goes a long way to improve their performance and client satisfaction.

# Personal Health Records

In the health sector, blockchain networks encompass numerous “labs, hospitals, clinics, community health centers and so on.”. Health-facilities need to provide a blockchain system for the records of the patient (that is, via EHR/EMR interface); this helps in securing the data of the patient from being leaked. This robust system allows the easy tracking of the patient’s past history, diagnosis, and other vital information.
