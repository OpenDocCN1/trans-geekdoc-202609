In the conceptualization of the uses and gratification theory by scientists, the scholars categorized the social and psychological province of the mass media into five categories. Here is the list:

- Cognitive needs
- Affective needs
- Personal integrative needs
- Social integrative needs
- Tension release needs

## Cognitive needs

The cognitive needs of UGT are however related to acquiring information, comprehension, knowledge and so on. The UGT is of the firm assertion that the “choice of a media and specific content” to gratify a specific need is purely a discretion of the user and user-independent in choice”. The cognitive needs seek to influence users to evaluate and determine what kind of media to select its content in order to gratify their cognitive needs. For instance, this need informs people who have diverse needs and wants and why they choose to watch, engage or view any content on social media. This shows that different programs give different satisfaction to people and as well appeal differently.

## Affective needs

According to researchers the affective needs of the UGT relates to pleasure. Thus, people use different media for different purposes or parochial needs such as watching a particular telenovela aired on television. When people are glued to their media or television is the pleasure or satisfaction derived from the program consumed? For organizations to tailor marketing efforts to meet and satisfy the affective need of the consumer, the emotions of the consumer are very key as customers right touching will spur them to select and stick to a product or service. In the health sector, health personnel can rightly ‘touch the consumer’ by choosing a media that will appeal to and satisfy the patient as we live in a technological era where telemedicine is common.

## Personal identity or interactive needs

The personal identity or interactive need seeks to touch the innermost feeling of the individual. This need of the UGT is more concerned with the “self-esteem, personal stability, need for self-respect, self-confidence, integrity, and social status and others in the category”. In addendum, it suggests that people decide to use a particular social media platform of the self-prestige or social class (that is, the use of Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, and so on.). This call is very important to marketing organizations who intend to appeal to consumers with their adverts, firms can use influencers or stem on celebrity endorsement to meet and arouse the interest of the consumer in patronizing a product or service.

## Social interaction and integration needs

Researchers postulated that “the needs under social interaction related to interacting with family, friends, and relatives but rather with technological integration nowadays unlike two decades ago where there were no social media interactions.” In this day and age, people choose to use different media platforms to attain their desired interests, especially engaging or communicating with family, friends, or acquaintances. This need of the UGT seeks to bring people together to consume a product or service. Thus, marketers can use an appealing marketing strategy such as the stealth marketing approach to get more people from the same circle to consume a good or service. In the likes of the health industry, health practitioners can adopt this strategy to convince households to select their health facility over other health centers.

## Tension release needs (escapism)

The tension release need or escapism is said to permit people or users to escape certain hostile situations as the name depicts and this motivates them to take their minds off issues that bother them. The tension release needed from the UGT touches and provides antidotes to the issues of the user regarding a particular media to consume in order to gratify the need. In view of this, marketers can make room for users to either escape a particular advert targeting users.

## Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)

Evolved Technology Acceptance Model was first developed in the early 80s to ascertain why workers were nonchalant in using ITs that were at their disposal. In order for the workers to adopt the usage of IT, to first accrue their acceptance of IT which could be done by asking the individuals about the impending plans to use IT.

According to researchers “knowing the factors that shaped one’s intentions would allow the organization to manipulate those factors in order to promote acceptance, and thus heighten the usage of IT”. TAM study uncovered that only three factors were needed to elucidate, envisage, and seemingly control acceptance.

The frequently used model for technology adoption is the TAM. In this current chapter, the TAM model was also used to look into blockchain adoption. TAM has been used by several scientists to investigate blockchain adoption determinants and to expand models with external variables. For instance, took discomfort and fear into account whereas it enhanced TAM with suitability or complexities.

Interestingly, the TAM is of the view that an individual’s decision to embrace technology is influenced by the person’s intention. Thus, “the intention is jointly determined by attitude toward using the technology and perception of its usefulness”. It is imperative to note that the main components of the model are ease of use and usefulness.

### Ease of use

According to scholars, perceived ease of use is defined as “the degree to which an individual has to intend for using a particular system or a person believes that using a particular system would be free of effort.” Thus, this intent is to heighten or influence the usage of IT in organizations, especially the health sector where health professionals are faced with challenges of keeping records and updates of the patient. This said the perceived ease of use of blockchain facilitates and ensures a successful convey of patient’s medical records. The TAM model in tandem with the ease of use will instigate health practitioners to use technology or IT freely without any quagmire.

### Usefulness

Innately, the perceived usefulness of the TAM is to spur workers, especially in the health sector to adopt the use of technology (blockchain) in making their work simple. Technology has reduced or eliminated the workload of health professionals, especially in emerging economies where records of patients were misplaced or exchanged with other patients, thus making their work cumbersome. As technology evolves, adopting TAM in the health sector would ensure the personnel’s effectiveness and efficiency, which goes a long way to improve their performance and client satisfaction.
