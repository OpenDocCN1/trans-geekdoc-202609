# Factors embedded in theory

We have used major paradigms such as the Unified Theory of Acceptance & Use of Technology (UTAUT), Uses & Gratification Theory (UGT), and Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) to provide critical factors that influence the adoption of BC in the health sector.

# Unified theory of acceptance & use of technology

A Unified Theory of Acceptance & Use of Technology (UTAUT) was espoused. The theory is used widely in myriads of disciplines as it seeks to predict behavioral intention for using or adopting technology. The scholar illustrated or demonstrated this theory by researching customer purchase intentions, and this is, however predicted by different factors such as performance, effort expectancy, social influence, and so on. This study seeks to emphasize the preceding factors in relation to the health sector.

# The Performance Expectancy

With the Performance Expectancy (PE) of the UTAUT framework, users expect the performance of a technology to stimulate their desire to adopt and use the technology. Extant literature unravels the confirmation of influence by the perceived performance of behavioral intentions to adopt a particular media. Research evidently juxtaposed that “customer’s intention to adopt technology depends on how they perceive the usefulness of the technology.” The health industry can leverage this framework and select a particular media that will stimulate users to patronize their services.

# The Effort Expectancy (EE)

The effort anticipation of the unified theory of acceptance & use of technology is the expectation in the technology that emphasizes on the perception of the user pertaining to the kind of media or technology to accept. With this approach, the easier the technology usage, the more likely consumers will accept the technology. The use of blockchain or any technology will spur the patience of the health sector to adopt and use a particular media that will satisfy their needs and wants.
