# Consortium blockchain

A consortium blockchain is used by more than one company. For the member organizations, it serves as a public blockchain.

# Hybrid blockchain

Hybrid blockchains incorporate the best aspects of both private and public blockchains. Consensus is achieved by a small number of predetermined nodes, similar to a private blockchain, but transactions are available to everyone on the network, similar to a public blockchain.

# Use cases for the blockchain in healthcare

Using Blockchain Technology in healthcare is a smart idea. A prominent area of blockchain use is healthcare. Electronic Health Record (EHRs), remote patient monitoring, disease/pandemic management pharma supply chain, and health insurance are all potential uses for the blockchain in the field of medicine.

COVID-19 is an example of a pandemic crisis that can be efficiently managed utilizing the present healthcare infrastructure and Blockchain Technology for early detection and vaccine supply chain management.
