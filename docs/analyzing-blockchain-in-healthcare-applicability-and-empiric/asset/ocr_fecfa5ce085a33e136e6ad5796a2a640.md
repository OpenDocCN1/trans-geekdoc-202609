# Transparency

Transparency is something that the blockchain networks provide in a way that no other network does. In these types of networks, the ledger is distributed across all of the member nodes and may be accessed by any of them at any time.

Nugent et al. described a blockchain-based smart contracts system for medical clinical trials that were deployed on the Ethereum network. It is common for such studies to have their credibility called into question as a result of missing data, data loss as a result of being transmitted from one institution to another, or data discrediting as a result of data spying. Also, in the healthcare industry, there is a trade-off between giving openness and safeguarding the patient’s privacy due to the fact that the miner has access to a full ledger of all the information in the network.

# Autonomy

Since there’s no one central authority that governs the transactions, all of the member nodes of the blockchain are able to access the data and upload more data without any outside source keeping an eye on them. Because the patients may access the data on the network, and because they can also connect with other patients located anywhere in the world who may be afflicted with the same medical condition, this is a very beneficial resource. Patients will be able to exercise full control over their data and decide with whom they wish to share it thanks to the design, which guarantees this.

# Persistency

The blockchain network is able to swiftly verify all of the transactions, and it only permits the transactions that are legitimate to join the network. Once a transaction has been accepted into the network, the network does not let the transaction be rolled back or deleted. This quality makes it possible to make decisions more quickly and accurately, both of which are necessary for dealing with the COVID-19 scenarios. The management of the supply chain for COVID-19 vaccines may also benefit from the features of blockchain.

# Immutability

Once a record has been added to the blockchain network, it is impossible to take it out or erase it. This feature guarantees that a record of each and every transaction will be kept in the blockchain indefinitely. One of the benefits of using this strategy to combat the COVID-19 epidemic is that if a patient is sent to another doctor, the new physician will have access to the patient’s complete medical history.
