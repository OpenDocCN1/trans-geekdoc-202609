# Organized framework for logical investigation

This framework intends to assist central banks and other authorities in better understanding DLT arrangements for payment, clearing, and settlement operations by offering a systematic approach for analyzing the advantages and risks associated with using these arrangements. The structure is composed of the following four elements:

+   - **Scope**: Understanding the functionality, nature of service, and implementation factors of the arrangement
+   - **Efficiency**: Analyzing the arrangement’s implications for efficiency
+   - **Safety**: Analyzing the arrangement’s implications for safety
