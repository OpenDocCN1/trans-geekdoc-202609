# Barriers and Benefits of Blockchain Adoption in the Healthcare System

# Abstract

This chapter aims to unveil the benefits and barriers of Blockchain (BC) Technology in the health sector. BC is a computerized database of economic transactions that may record nearly everything of value and not only financial transactions. The chapter is basically literature review of BC in the health sector. It was found that BC technology allows secure, transparent, decentralized ledgers, smart contracts, and reliable networks for supply chain management that is environmentally friendly. The barriers found were storage barriers, cyber-attacks, technical flaws, and information security. It is concluded that though BC has some limitations, it is still critical for health care transformation.
