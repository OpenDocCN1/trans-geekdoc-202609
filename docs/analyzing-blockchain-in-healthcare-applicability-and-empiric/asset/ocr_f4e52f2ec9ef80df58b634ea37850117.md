A decentralized arrangement in which players contribute to maintaining and updating a shared ledger enables participants to share maintenance costs rather than having a single organization, such as an FMI, charge fees to participants. This is in contrast to a model in which the costs of maintaining the ledger are borne by a single organization. Participants in the distributed system keep the ledger updated. As a result of this division of labor, certain of the members running certain nodes in an arrangement could have to bear greater direct expenses.
