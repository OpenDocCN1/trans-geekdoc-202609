To do this, use contact tracing to keep tabs on those who have been in close vicinity to an infected person for a certain period of time.

All of a person’s closest friends and family members should be notified when they are diagnosed with COVID-19. Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) technology has been used to produce a number of mobile contact tracing apps. Close interactions between mobile phones and wearables and IoT devices may be recorded thanks to BLE. This strategy, however, raises the most serious issues about data security and privacy for consumers. As a result, consumers may lose control of their data since their data is kept and processed in a centralized cloud.

Data security and privacy problems may be addressed with the use of Blockchain Technology. Blockchain can provide a decentralized database alternative that enables users to maintain complete control over their data. Patients and users may set time-bound access to their data using smart contracts. In addition, pseudo-anonymity may be used to preserve patients’ privacy.

The blockchain-based system may allow the usage of a unique digital fingerprint (public key or hash) for each patient, instead of disclosing the patient’s genuine identity.
