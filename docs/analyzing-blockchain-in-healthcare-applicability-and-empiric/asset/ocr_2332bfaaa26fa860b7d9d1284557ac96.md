All transactions, as well as account balances, are recorded in ledgers. A blockchain is a distributed ledger that records transactions that have already taken place. When blockchain technology is used, the information about transactions is stored in blocks. Following the validation of a block, that block will then be linked to all of the ledger transactions that came before it.

One kind of distributed ledger is known as a blockchain, although not all of them use to block or chain transactions. There are several categories of distributed ledgers. One alternative is to use the more conventional accounting method, which entails solely altering the account balances.

More than only the ownership of assets could be recorded in a ledger depending on the circumstances. The provisions of a financial contract, or a copy of the contract itself, may be stored on a distributed ledger.

It is permissible to keep either the original contract or a copy. Certain DLTs make it possible to create “automated contract tools.” Users can automate their contractual commitments by embedding code on the ledger, which is made possible by these technologies. This includes the payment of interest and principal on specific dates, the collection or distribution of cash in response to defined events, and the automated termination of contracts according to the terms outlined in such contracts. This kind of agreement is referred to as a “smart contract” in the commercial world.

# # Making necessary corrections to the ledger

The fact that several nodes are collectively responsible for maintaining the ledger’s most recent version is one of the most noticeable aspects of DLT. These nodes can be dispersed throughout a broad number of places, institutions, and even nations where it is lawful to do so, as will be shown in the next section.

# # Validation, in addition to the overall consensus

In order to bring a synchronized distributed ledger up to date, an arrangement will typically use several protocols for communication between nodes and for facilitating consensus among nodes regarding the current state of the ledger as well as its historical record.

These protocols will be used to facilitate communication between nodes and will also be used to facilitate consensus among nodes. This is done to make upgrading process easier to complete.
