# Crucial aspect of the implementation

It is essential to distinguish between the myriad of aspects, each of which has the potential to influence either the formulation or the execution of the arrangement. In some agreements, just one or a few parties are involved. For others, adopting the technology throughout a whole industry is necessary to receive efficiency improvements or other advantages, some of which may be reliant on the network. There can be a need for a certain minimum number of participants for the sector to achieve greater productivity. Alterations to existing market norms and practices can also be necessary in order to implement the adoption. The realization of an arrangement could be affected by various issues, including those related to the environment, technology, and the economy.

+   • Variables in the environment, such as the rate at which institutions adopt new technologies, the size, structure, and practices of the market; regulatory and legal circumstances; and the degree to which industries coordinate their efforts.

+   • The development of technology and its compatibility with already existing processes and systems are factors that influence the adoption of technology.

+   • Economic considerations: Institutions and markets are more likely to promote initiatives that give chances to save money, earn more money, or do all these things simultaneously. There are a few approaches that are effective here.

There is widespread optimism that DLT may improve market efficiency. Design, usefulness, and resource requirements are all aspects of efficiency. In this sense, efficiency refers to the speed and cost of the whole asset transfer cycle and the degree to which the arrangement satisfies the market's requirements. When time and money are factors to consider, the importance of reconciliation, credit and liquidity management, and automation cannot be overstated.

# The whole processing time from start to finish

It is a common selling point for DLT because it enables quicker transaction resolution in a single arrangement. DLT has the potential to simplify workflows by lowering the barriers associated with information exchange. It is essential to keep in mind that potential improvements in end-to-end processing speed are being addressed at the ecosystem level (across the value chain), and transaction settlement within the infrastructure itself may be slower than expected. This is something that should be kept in mind at all times. Settlements using DLT could take more time than those using RTGS systems. It's possible that using DLT will make it more difficult, from a technical standpoint, to validate a transaction and reach an agreement than it would be using a centralized organization.
