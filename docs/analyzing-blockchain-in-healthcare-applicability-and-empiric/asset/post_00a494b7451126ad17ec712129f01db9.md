## Conclusion

Emerging technologies such as blockchain are being leveraged to give cutting-edge solutions in a variety of industries, including healthcare. A Blockchain network is being utilized effectively in the healthcare industry to hold for long time patient information and to facilitate a large amount of data transmission between hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, pharmacy businesses, and clinicians. In the medical specialized area, blockchain-based application solutions have the potential to precisely pinpoint important and possibly highly sensitive errors.

As a result, there exists a tremendous potential to improve the system for sharing medical data in the healthcare business in terms of efficiency, security, and accessibility. Utilizing this technology assists medical organizations in gaining insights and enhancing the examination of medical records.
