# Synopsis of blockchain

BC technology has gained a lot of interest around the world since it has the potential to improve healthcare management and sustainability goals. BC’s unrealized potential has been highlighted by a few active use cases, which include food, healthcare, and logistics supply chains. Improved efficiency, transparency, and traceability, as well as billions of dollars in corporate financial savings are all potential support health care issues. Despite its promise, Blockchain Technology has been unable to gain widespread adoption, regardless of being promoted for years. To analyze blockchain adoption hurdles, researchers used the technology-organization-environment framework and force field theories. A complete assessment of impediments to implementing BC technology to manage sustainable health is offered using several literature streams on technology, organizational practices, and sustainability.

For environmentally friendly supply chain management, BC technology enables safe, transparent, decentralized ledgers, smart contracts, and trustworthy networks.
