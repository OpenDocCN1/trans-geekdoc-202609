This study seeks to review the existing issues from a theoretical lens relating to the use of blockchain technology in the healthcare industry.

The only method to accomplish the objectives before blockchain was to create a powerful central authority to deliver these services, such as banks, states, and financial institutions. Each hospital or medical system acts as its own central authority to provide record-keeping and communication services in the area of health records.

Even though blockchain is a relatively new invention, it is already providing new perspectives on privacy, robustness, and the effectiveness of corporate processes, transforming the digital world. A blockchain is a decentralized archive that is managed by numerous user entities rather than being controlled by a single entity.

According to past research, “healthcare uses a blockchain network to store and share patient data across hospitals, diagnostic labs, pharmacy companies, and doctors.” In this technological era, people’s lives hinge on healthcare and this has become paramount for patients to be diagnosed and as well preserve their data (medical records) for future use. Medical information was initially painstakingly converted from paper records into electronic format. Using this approach provided various opportunities for data corruption and alteration. Therefore, it is essential to save healthcare data electronically.

On the other side, healthcare databases carry the risk of being permanently changed or obliterated, and information blocking is problematic. A data blockage happens if a person or other undesired entity obtains medical information that should only be accessible to patients or hospitals.

By tackling resource allocation and information blockade challenges, technology has the potential to enhance people’s quality of life. Timing is everything in cloud-based healthcare data sharing. Blockchain technology has potential uses in a variety of fields, including those related to strategy, organizations, economies, information, and technology.

This chapter also lists numerous prospective and actual applications of blockchain technology in the health periphery. The unified theory of acceptance & use of technology, uses & gratification concept, and Technology Acceptance Model (TAM), is a model that seeks to fathom how users feel about applying blockchain to their medical practice in the health sector.
