# # Blockchain

Blockchain is an immutable, decentralized database that will support maintaining assets and records transactions in a corporate network blockchain. Sometimes referred to as **Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT)**, employs decentralization and cryptographic hashing to make any digital asset's existence recognizable. The DLT idea transformed how we approach problems in our area. It made it possible for organizations to decentralize the function. Following the publication of the Bitcoin white paper in October 2008, blockchain became well-known as a distributed ledger technology. Since then, blockchain techniques have been widely applied throughout several IT sectors. The banking industry, supply chain, healthcare system, reputation system, and so on, are among the sectors where the blockchain's surge of innovation is going to transform how data is distributed. Blockchain is ideally developing as the norm for more effective decentralized business processes. Additionally, it advances governance and affirms all of the actions taken by the relevant parties within the blockchain, establishing the basis for a higher level of coordination. Because it minimizes fraud, eliminates risk, and increases the efficiency in a scalable way for a range of uses. Blockchain Technology is especially innovative and wide-ranging.
