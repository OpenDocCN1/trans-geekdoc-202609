# # Access to the configuration of the arrangement (unrestricted or restricted)

The arrangements do not impose any restrictions on the maximum number of participants that may take part in the event. Unrestricted arrangements are those that are planned and built in such a way that there are no limitations placed on who can access the arrangement or what roles nodes can play within the arrangement. This is because there are no restrictions placed on the planning and construction of the arrangement. In environments in which access is available to absolutely everyone, it is quite unlikely that the entities who are in charge of running the nodes are acquainted with one another.

It could be difficult to exercise control over such arrangements and the whole set of rules that are supposed to regulate interactions between nodes need to be written primarily “on-ledger” (encoded in the computer protocol).

On the other hand, limited platforms make it possible to oversee and monitor participants’ access to the arrangement. This is because they prevent unauthorized access. Due to limited access, the rules that govern interactions may be kept “off the ledger” if it becomes essential to do so.

These configurations map nodes to organizations in the same way that responsibilities are mapped from nodes to organizations. This responsibility comes in addition to entity A’s role as system operator. This is because entity A controls the univocal voting mechanism.
