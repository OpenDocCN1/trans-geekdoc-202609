# CHAPTER 7
Optimization of Blockchain Technology for Patient Satisfaction

# Abstract

The industrial revolution has fashioned new technologies such as blockchain networks. Blockchain (BC) Technology has the capability of securing data and ensuring transparency and equitable data management at health facilities. The Blockchain network has been applied in the healthcare periphery to heighten the security of health data. Thus, this study seeks to understand how health facilities adopt blockchain networks to satisfy their patients without any quagmire.

# Introduction

Industries have adopted new technologies such as blockchain networks to serve customers or curb industry challenges, and healthcare or health facilities are no exception. Blockchain has been used in various situations recently, including open creative businesses, manufacturing, healthcare, and so on. There are several common prerequisites for transactions in legal, medical, and other fields. Establishing the identities of the people involved in the transaction, upholding confidence, ensuring that transactions are accurately recorded and cannot be changed, and ensuring that the infrastructure in which transactions take place is stable are all requirements.
