# # Different categories of blockchain

Without a question, the blockchain has advanced significantly during the past ten years. The first blockchain variant was indeed the public blockchain, which was released publicly by Bitcoin. Nowadays, there are various forms of blockchain technology, each of which has a specific use and resolves a specific problem or group of problems.

In order to maximize the benefits, numerous businesses are employing them in their operations. While distributed technology eliminates the negative aspects of centralization, it also creates a number of new issues that must be addressed while using blockchain technology in various contexts.

For instance, the proof-of-work consensus mechanism was utilized with Bitcoin. The algorithm was inefficient because the nodes have to use energy to solve mathematical problems. It wasn't an issue at first, but the time and effort needed to solve those mathematical equations likewise increased, as the difficulty raised. The inefficiency discussed makes it unsuitable for any system that must always remain efficient, for instance; banks handle several transactions each day.
