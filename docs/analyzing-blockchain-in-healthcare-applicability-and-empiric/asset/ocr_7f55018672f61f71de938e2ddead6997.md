# ## Banking

Blockchain technology has the potential to be used in the computerization of a number of specialized facets of banking, including, client account consolidations and so on. It takes time to endorse a loan or derivative using traditional banking techniques because there are several back-end steps that require contract negotiations with several parties. Through smart contracts, Blockchain offers the necessary speed and transparency. Blockchain is already being tested by a number of institutions, and they are using services from corporate firms like *IBM*, *R3*, and *Microsoft*.

# ## Finance-payroll and settlement

Transactions involving public services require a series of steps, comprising legalizing the legitimacy of the transacting entity, authenticating the data given by the dealing parties, completing the transaction, and then providing the required service. The entire transaction is then recorded. This procedure is time-consuming. The Blockchain can shorten this period of time due to its structural authenticity and validity. Due to the fact that many events related to life-built pensions, such as receiving costs from current users, beneficiaries receiving their payments, and taxes on pensions, are fundamentally contract-controlled payments. The adoption of Ethereum has been proposed by *Sestof* as the algorithm should operate on self-regulatory contracts. Sestof presented a distributed network for an autonomous pension fund that is based on contracts and manages life-based pension funds directly without the use of third party.

**Taxation:** The government can use blockchain to collect taxes more efficiently and spend them more effectively. One area where blockchain may have a significant impact is taxation. Transparency, provenance, and traceability are four essential concepts in the blockchain which could be integrated to the requirements of taxation systems. Blockchain technology could be utilized for tax transactions, VAT, stamp fees, withholding tax, and so on. The responsibility of tax officials to collect taxes is removed by shifting taxation. Consumption of tax revenues is equally as crucial as the topic of tax provenance. However, digitizing the non-digital sellers that primarily respond on paper records would be one of the largest challenges.
