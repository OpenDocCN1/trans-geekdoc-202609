## Benefits of blockchain in healthcare

In the previous sections, we have also discussed the blockchain, health care, its types, capabilities and so on. Now, in this section we will discuss the benefits of blockchain in healthcare.

- **Decentralization:** Depending on the type of medical care, a decentralized management structure is necessary for healthcare because of the spread of stakeholders. Blockchain has the potential to become the decentralized health data management foundation that would allow all stakeholders without anybody acting as the global health data's governing body.

- **Improved data security and privacy:** Once the data is saved to the blockchain cannot be degraded, edited, or recovered, this property of blockchain significantly enhances the security of the health data kept on it. Each and every piece of health information on the blockchain is time-stamped, encrypted, and added in chronological sequence.

- **Health Data ownership:** Patients must be the owners of their data and in charge of how it is utilized. Patients should have the ability to notice instances of data misuse and require the confidence that their health information won’t be used improperly by third parties. Blockchain’s robust cryptographic protocols and clearly specified smart contracts help it to meet these needs.

- **Availability and robustness:** The availability of the health data saved on blockchain is ensured since records are duplicated across numerous nodes, making the system resistant to data loss, data corruption, and some security attacks on data availability.

- **Transparency and trust:** Blockchain end up in distributed healthcare applications because of its openness and transparency. This makes it easier for healthcare stakeholders to accept such applications.

- **Data verifiability:** The authenticity and integrity of the records kept on blockchain can be confirmed even without access to their plaintext. This capability is especially helpful in healthcare settings where record verification is necessary, such as in the processing of insurance claims and pharmaceutical supply chains.

## Research areas of blockchain with health care

The most significant research is highlighted in this area and is organised according to several use cases, including electronic medical records, remote patient monitoring, the pharmaceutical supply chain, and health insurance claims.

## Electronic Medical Records

The health data, which might be enhanced by the capacity to integrate disparate systems and improve the accuracy of **Electronic Health Records (EHRs)**, should be given priority in the effort to change healthcare. While the phrases **Electronic Medical Records (EMRs)** and Electronic Health Records are often used interchangeably, they have different meanings. EMRs are a more recent term for the paper charts kept by clinicians in their offices. EHRs, on the other hand, put a greater emphasis on a patient’s overall health by taking into account more than just the traditional clinical information gathered at a doctor’s office.

According to the mapping study, Blockchain Technology helps manage EHRs. A decentralized strategy to manage authorization, permissions and data exchange between healthcare stakeholders is proposed in this context by an EHR-related implementation. The Ethereum platform is used by *MedRec* to give patients knowledge and information about who can access their medical records.

*Fast Health Interoperability Records Blockchain (FHIRChain)* is a second application that incorporates EHR. It is a healthcare record management-focused blockchain-based platform for exchanging clinical data that is implemented using Ethereum. Patients can get solutions from FHIRChain that satisfy the ONC’s standards.

Similar to this, *MedShare* is an Ethereum application for systems that experience a lack of collaboration for exchanging data amongst cloud services due to the negative risks associated with disclosing the contents of confidential data. *MedBlock* and *BlockHIE* are two further blockchain-based EMR systems. MedBlock offers a method for searching records. The suggested method keeps track of the addresses of patient record blocks that are organized by department or healthcare provider. Each patient inventory has a link to the relevant blockchain record. A healthcare platform built on Blockchain Technology was presented by BlocHIE.

*BlocHIE* combines off-chain storage, in which data is kept in databases of external hospitals, and on-chain verification in order to continue taking advantage of current databases. Ethereum leverages smart contracts to achieve data privacy, security, access control, and EMR interoperability. This is another blockchain-based healthcare system.

*OmniPHR* is a distributed paradigm that keeps **Personal Health Records (PHR)** in a single, interoperable perspective. Additionally, OmniPHR evaluation might guarantee the distribution of PHR in a routing overlay network and the separation of PHR into data blocks.

The administration of electronic medical records is one of the prominent applications of blockchain in healthcare (EMRs). EMRs are used to create, store, and manage patient personal, medical, or health-related data electronically. They are also referred to as Electronic Health Records or **Personal Health Records (PHRs)**. In fact, 32% of the 65 publications that were selected for review addressed the EMR use case of blockchain, making it a substantial study topic in the literature. Some publications discuss how to make it easier for healthcare stakeholders, including providers, researchers, and insurers, to share patient-centric data.

According to the European General Blockchain, practical technology is widely recommended to create a healthcare platform that can provide patients control over how their data is shared, processed, or used. Sensitive personal data of patients may not be treated in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which prohibits such processing without the patient’s express consent.

In other evaluations, Guardtime is noted as a well-known example of how Blockchain Technology can be used to handle electronic medical data. Guardtime uses a platform built on Blockchain Technology that is being used by Healthbank, a Swiss digital health startup, to allow patients to fully own their data. The author talks about the Healthcoin project, which wants to create a global EMR system, and the Medicalchain project, whose blockchain-based platform would make it easier to share patient medical records across international healthcare facilities.

## Remote patient monitoring

Another illustration is the Gem Health Network (GHN), created by the US firm Gem using the Ethereum blockchain. The GHN makes it possible for many healthcare professionals to share access to the same data. Healthbank, a Swiss digital health startup, is making similar efforts, leveraging Blockchain Technology to give patients complete control over their data. The author discusses the Medicalchain project, whose blockchain-based platform would allow the exchange of patient medical records across international healthcare facilities, and the Healthcoin proposal, which aims to create a global EMR system. Some of the other businesses involved in various programs and projects are based on patient-centric EMRs enabled by Blockchain Technology. In this situation, researchers describe a use case in which data is transmitted from mobile devices to a blockchain-based application on Hyperledger Fabric.

The author uses a real-time patient monitoring application to show how Ethereum smart contracts may give automatic interventions in a secure setting. Other suggested ways highlight the enormous potential of the Internet of Things (IoT) in many fields, particularly how it is being heavily utilized in e-health. IoBHealth is a data-flow architecture that mixes the IoT and blockchain.

## Pharmaceutical supply chain

The pharmaceutical sector is another recognized use case for blockchain. Patients may suffer severe effects if they receive false or poor medications. It has been determined that blockchain technology has the power to solve this issue.
