## Factors embedded in theory

We have used major paradigms such as the Unified Theory of Acceptance & Use of Technology (UTAUT), Uses & Gratification Theory (UGT), and Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) to provide critical factors that influence the adoption of BC in the health sector.

## Unified theory of acceptance & use of technology

A Unified Theory of Acceptance & Use of Technology (UTAUT) was espoused. The theory is used widely in myriads of disciplines as it seeks to predict behavioral intention for using or adopting technology. The scholar illustrated or demonstrated this theory by researching customer purchase intentions, and this is, however predicted by different factors such as performance, effort expectancy, social influence, and so on. This study seeks to emphasize the preceding factors in relation to the health sector.

## The Performance Expectancy

With the Performance Expectancy (PE) of the UTAUT framework, users expect the performance of a technology to stimulate their desire to adopt and use the technology. Extant literature unravels the confirmation of influence by the perceived performance of behavioral intentions to adopt a particular media. Research evidently juxtaposed that “customer’s intention to adopt technology depends on how they perceive the usefulness of the technology.” The health industry can leverage this framework and select a particular media that will stimulate users to patronize their services.

## The Effort Expectancy (EE)

The effort anticipation of the unified theory of acceptance & use of technology is the expectation in the technology that emphasizes on the perception of the user pertaining to the kind of media or technology to accept. With this approach, the easier the technology usage, the more likely consumers will accept the technology. The use of blockchain or any technology will spur the patience of the health sector to adopt and use a particular media that will satisfy their needs and wants.

## The Social Influence

The likes experiment saw the significance of **Social Influence (SI)** in the acceptance or adoption of technology. The UTAUT framework seeks to fathom how SI shapes the purchase intentions of consumers. An individual is influenced by friends or close relatives to consume a certain product or service. In the health periphery, patients can be influenced by family or friends to seek medication from a certain health facility because of previous satisfaction. So, health professionals can leverage the social influence of the UTAUT framework to influence patient intentions to seek their services.

## Uses and Gratification Theory

*Katz, Haas, and Gurevitch* (1973) developed the **Uses and Gratification theory (UGT)** to determine why people will choose to consume a particular media that seeks to gratify their needs. The scholars are of the view that gratification is conceptualized as and when the socio-psychological need of an individual is attained through the consumption of said media.

Researchers further categorized the gratification of the UGT into two distinct parts (that is, *gratification sought* and *gratification obtained*). The gratification sought of the UGT is the conforming need for using a specific media, whereas the gratification obtained is the supposed result or added gratification. Research postulated that “_discovering gratifications obtained by individuals using a specific media can provide insights into the continuous usage of a given media._”

Researchers uncovered that gratification obtained from the use of a particular media varies depending on different personality types, levels of maturity, and individuals’ social backgrounds. The communication literature emphasizes the gratification obtained of the UGT hinges on the medium that the individual uses.

That said, social media provides a more diverse gratification than a magazine (newspaper), which gratifies another need than just listening to music or watching television. Since the conceptualization of the Uses and Gratification Theory, the theory has been used widely by scholars in understanding why an individual will select a particular media over the other in order to gratify his/her need.

The paradigm shift in mass communication has seen a swift use of personal computers, and the internet (such as social media, blockchain, AI, IoT and so on.) has revitalized the significance of the uses and gratification theory. Instinctively, research scholars have applied the uses and gratification theory to studies like “blockchain, big data, social media, Internet of Things (IoT), and modern ICT” platforms.
