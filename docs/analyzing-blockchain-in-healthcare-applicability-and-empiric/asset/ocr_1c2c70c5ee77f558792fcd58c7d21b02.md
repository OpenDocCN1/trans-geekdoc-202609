BC technology can help to avoid this, and it can even be used to create an internet marketplace where scientists can buy genomic data for research. # Benefits of BC adoption in the health care system

Disruptive BC technology has recently received a lot of attention and buzz. Organizations are now seriously considering implementing this technology because of its many potential advantages. Cost savings, traceability-transparency, and sustainability improvements have been cited as potential benefits. Blockchain aids in the elimination of information inequities that may deprive small businesses and farmers of social and financial opportunities. Blockchain can also contribute to social supply chain sustainability by reducing unethical, dishonest, and counterfeit activities.

BCs could offer a single transaction layer where organizations can submit and share data securely by retaining a common standard set of data on the BC and private encrypted linkages to independent data such as radiographic or other images. To battle the COVID-19 pandemic, new BC-enabled technologies are emerging, such as an identity management system in South Korea to allow contact tracking and a plan to support data and software code sharing for research purposes. Supply chain management for pharmaceuticals, medical supplies, and vaccination has also been utilized or proposed with BC.

Distributed ledger technology can track social and environmental events that may pose a threat to environmental ecological concerns and social issues such as others’ health and safety. This capacity can contribute to social, environmental, and economic sustainability.
