# Related works

The Internet of Medical Things Security Assessment Framework (IoMT-SAF) is recommended by Alsubaei et al. In contrast to IoMT security and privacy violations, a wide spectrum of cloud-based apps shares private medical information. Protective measures can only be put in place after they have been calculated. Inexperienced IoMT users, on the other hand, suffer challenges in choosing acceptable and strong security behavior during safety testing. Based on a novel ontological scenario, they have created an online technique for developing an IoMT-SAF that provides security estimations for the IoMT. The grandiosity, extensibility, and adaptation of IoMT-SAF to new clients, as well as its compliance with technological and medical standards, are some of its distinctive features. Medical Data Protection Access Control (MDPAC) was developed by Habib et al. to safeguard privacy-based medical data from internal threats in heterogeneous IoMT systems.

Information security is a critical concern that affects almost every facet of our daily lives. However, when it comes to medication, this is especially vital because of the dangers to one’s health and life. The security administrator may delegate user duties, roles, and actions to objects or services in the MDPAC model. The suggested paradigm ensures safe, confidential, and effective communication between doctors and patients. Forensics-by-design management system for blockchain-based medical devices was explored by Malamas et al.
