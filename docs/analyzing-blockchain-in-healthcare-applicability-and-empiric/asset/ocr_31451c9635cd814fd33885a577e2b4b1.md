# Blockchain (BC)

BC refers to the process by which new transactions are encapsulated in a data block and then appended to an existing chain of previous transactions. A Blockchain can be described as an organized, solid, incremental & digital block of cryptographically connected data.

This technology is a departure from the traditional model in which a single person or entity is responsible for all the system’s data. In terms of lowered costs, increased efficiency, and improved security, this technology has completely changed how supply chains are managed.

Patient satisfaction can be improved by using a blockchain-based technique for preserving health information, which meets all of the standard requirements for a healthcare record-keeping system while improving patient access to their records and strengthening security against privacy violations. BC has several advantages, as well.

Maintaining an average healthcare information system necessitates numerous processes, including but not limited to conducting backup storage services, having recovery mechanisms in place, and ensuring that fields have the most recent information.

This is where blockchain technology comes into play. There is no single point of failure in a blockchain. Hence there is no built-in method for backup. Additionally, each blockchain node has a copy of the same data.

As a result, the healthcare ecosystem is relieved of the stress caused by the volume of data exchanged between systems. Patients take control of their healthcare data by becoming the platform they own and control. All barriers to patients obtaining copies of their medical report records or sending these to other Health Providers (HP) have been eliminated by this measure.

No single institution can be looted or hacked to get vast numbers of patient records because data is maintained on a decentralized network. The patient’s private key is required to decrypt data stored in the blockchain. In addition to using tamper-proof timestamps and account IDs, the data is also added to the blockchain using mechanisms that can detect if the data has been altered.
