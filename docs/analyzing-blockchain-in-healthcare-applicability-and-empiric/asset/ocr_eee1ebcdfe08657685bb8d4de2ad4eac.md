# Blockchain faces several problems in the battle against COVID-19, including the following:

+   - A primary challenge implementing Blockchain Technology in the healthcare industry would be the reluctance of healthcare professionals to convert from paper records to sophisticated Blockchain Technology. The technical challenges they would encounter as a result of lack of competence and understanding of the new technology.
+   - When implementing a new blockchain network in the healthcare industry, legal and regulatory difficulties may arise. The sort of data transferred and kept on the blockchain network, privacy concerns, challenges to integrity, and copyright difficulties are only a few examples of these problems.
+   - In a short time span and with a vast network, it’s difficult to oversee all aspects of a project. As a result, the security of data may be compromised due to key management and the fear of the keys being exploited.
+   - A blockchain network connects the many stakeholders, each with a distinct database, to share information. In other words, interoperability challenges arise when attempting to manage data that is spread across many databases. In order to ensure a seamless operation, all parties must accept open standards.

When a person’s life is on the line, having too little power is just not an option. In order to ensure adequate and accurate patient and health data collecting, further measures must be undertaken.
+   - With the introduction of EHR and mobile recording devices, it is now possible to take readings on the go, resulting in more frequent updates to patient records. On the other hand, blockchain is unchangeable.
+   - It is thus necessary to create a new transaction each time there is a new reading, as the prior record cannot be changed. In any case, it’s still the same person. Scalability and storage difficulties can arise as a result.
+   - Finally, mining attacks, which are architecture-dependent and hence have different vulnerabilities, are the most detrimental problem and obstacle associated with the introduction of blockchain in COVID-19.
