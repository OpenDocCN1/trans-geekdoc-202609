# # Mobile healthcare

IoT applications in mobile healthcare might be utilized for emergencies or normal treatment. Apps connected to wearables track a user’s physical activity and health. Wearable IoT apps might deliver health data to a smartphone. The literature review identifies many research gaps. Before the organization matures and becomes successful, it must overcome several challenges, these issues are:

+   - **No standards:** Confidential remedies are offered. They lack established protocols to adapt technology and promote interoperability, preventing their adoption, and the problem remains. To handle end-user interactions with IoMT devices, blockchain technology, cloud computing, platform-agnostic and universal solutions are essential.

+   - **Programming abstractions:** Blockchain technology in IoMT opens the way for crucial medical applications. Blockchain-IoMT deployment is tough and requires multidisciplinary understanding. This includes managing IoMT devices and configuring blockchain to satisfy IoMT criteria, as well as distributing, storing, and processing IoMT data. In this light, it’s crucial to provide an abstraction layer that may hide this complexity and give developers new APIs and middleware to design decentralized and secure healthcare apps utilizing IoMT.

+   - **Restricted field of application:** Most published articles focus on remote patient monitoring and IoMT data administration, including data interchange and storage. Developing monitoring software to prevent the sale of counterfeit pharmaceuticals and medical errors is crucial. In this regard, using blockchain technology with IoMT may be an efficient method for administering the medicine supply chain and controlling medical personnel’s actions.

+   - Lack of technical specifics makes integrating blockchain with IoMT difficult. Most solutions lacked technical details. Researchers must describe how to integrate blockchain technology into IoMT.

# ## Conclusion

Due to the demanding security concerns in healthcare, research has concentrated on employing blockchain technology in IoMT. Most focused on authentication, secrecy, data integrity, and privacy. They offered various use cases, including RMP and medical data management. Our analysis and evaluation found that the various techniques for integrating blockchain into IoMT lack technical details, most of them couldn’t handle the massive amount of data streams provided by resource-constrained IoMT devices, and they didn’t advise any technical changes to the blockchain’s architecture.
