# Internet of Medical Things

In the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT), biosensors, communications, and medical equipment are all rolled into one. Health care facilities and equipment may be linked to one another, and vast quantities of data can be generated as the result. Medical experts may utilize this information to diagnose, identify, and facilitate therapies based on it. There are three levels to the Internet of Things: a device layer, a gateway layer, and a cloud layer. Using these layers, doctors and patients may communicate with one another. The medical sensors are used by IoMT devices to gather data from patients. Once the data has been processed by microprocessors, it is forwarded to the user terminal, where it is sent to the smart gateway by Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or Zigbee for further processing and relaying to the cloud.
