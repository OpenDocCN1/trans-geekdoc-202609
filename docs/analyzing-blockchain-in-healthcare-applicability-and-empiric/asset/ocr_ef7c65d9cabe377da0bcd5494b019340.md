# Synopsis of chapter

This study provides a methodology that may be used by central banks and other authorities to examine and evaluate the usability of this technology for payment, clearing, and settlement purposes. The findings of this study may be helpful to players in the market as well as other parties. The primary goal of the framework is to assist in understanding the applications of DLT. Also, in the process of doing so, identify both the potential and obstacles involved with using this technology as an essential component of the financial system. This objective will be accomplished through the use of a framework. Central banks and other parties interested in the subject matter will be better to judge the potential of the technology to increase the robustness and resilience of financial markets and produce operational benefits if they use this technique.

Recent developments would appear to show that DLT offers promise, yet, there is still a great way to go before this promise can conceivably be fulfilled. It is essential that the legal underpinnings of DLT arrangements be solid, that governance structures be robust, that technological solutions meet the sector's requirements, and that regulatory data controls be put into place. It is expected that improvements and gains in efficiency will be incremental rather than revolutionary.
