# Electronic Medical Records

The health data, which might be enhanced by the capacity to integrate disparate systems and improve the accuracy of **Electronic Health Records (EHRs)**, should be given priority in the effort to change healthcare. While the phrases **Electronic Medical Records (EMRs)** and Electronic Health Records are often used interchangeably, they have different meanings. EMRs are a more recent term for the paper charts kept by clinicians in their offices. EHRs, on the other hand, put a greater emphasis on a patient’s overall health by taking into account more than just the traditional clinical information gathered at a doctor’s office.

According to the mapping study, Blockchain Technology helps manage EHRs. A decentralized strategy to manage authorization, permissions and data exchange between healthcare stakeholders is proposed in this context by an EHR-related implementation. The Ethereum platform is used by *MedRec* to give patients knowledge and information about who can access their medical records.

*Fast Health Interoperability Records Blockchain (FHIRChain)* is a second application that incorporates EHR. It is a healthcare record management-focused blockchain-based platform for exchanging clinical data that is implemented using Ethereum. Patients can get solutions from FHIRChain that satisfy the ONC’s standards.

Similar to this, *MedShare* is an Ethereum application for systems that experience a lack of collaboration for exchanging data amongst cloud services due to the negative risks associated with disclosing the contents of confidential data. *MedBlock* and *BlockHIE* are two further blockchain-based EMR systems. MedBlock offers a method for searching records. The suggested method keeps track of the addresses of patient record blocks that are organized by department or healthcare provider. Each patient inventory has a link to the relevant blockchain record. A healthcare platform built on Blockchain Technology was presented by BlocHIE.

*BlocHIE* combines off-chain storage, in which data is kept in databases of external hospitals, and on-chain verification in order to continue taking advantage of current databases. Ethereum leverages smart contracts to achieve data privacy, security, access control, and EMR interoperability. This is another blockchain-based healthcare system.

*OmniPHR* is a distributed paradigm that keeps **Personal Health Records (PHR)** in a single, interoperable perspective. Additionally, OmniPHR evaluation might guarantee the distribution of PHR in a routing overlay network and the separation of PHR into data blocks.

The administration of electronic medical records is one of the prominent applications of blockchain in healthcare (EMRs). EMRs are used to create, store, and manage patient personal, medical, or health-related data electronically.
