# Benefits that might be derived from using the blockchain with COVID-19

Because Blockchain Technologies may be used in a variety of industries, they are rising in popularity.

Healthcare professionals are also looking at its possibilities, as are researchers. However, in 2011, a shared database for physicians, nurses, pharmacists, and other sector players was built using blockchain in the healthcare area. Because of COVID-19, several institutions and corporations throughout the globe have begun using blockchain in their solution space. This integration's primary goal is to bring together as many reliable and trustworthy data sources as possible. In the battle against COVID-19, blockchain's unique ability to continually verify data even in real-time is crucial.

Bell et al. ,McGhin et al. , and Kumar et al. have noted the following current healthcare issues:

# Nationwide interoperability

Legacy healthcare systems often store patient data in a centralized system in pieces that prevent simple data exchange among stakeholders.

# Healthcare data interchange

There must be some kind of safeguard in place for patient data that is shared across several parties.

# Supply chain management

Stock control and authenticity are major issues in healthcare since timely delivery and authenticity are critical to treatment effectiveness.

# Mobility

Mobile technology has made it increasingly likely that patients will demand that their medical records follow suit, which means they need to be accessible in real-time from any device, no matter where they are.

# Fraudulent transactions involving electronic funds transfer

During pandemics like COVID-19, double payments and insurance frauds pose a significant danger to the health care system.
