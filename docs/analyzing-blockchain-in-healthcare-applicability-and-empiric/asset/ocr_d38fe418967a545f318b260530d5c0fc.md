# Abstract

The last ten years have seen the emergence of **Blockchain Technology (BCT)**, which has increased interest in the healthcare industry. This systematic literature review aims to explore the possible pattern shift in healthcare with Blockchain Technology. Based on the findings, BCT is being used to develop cutting-edge therapies that are innovative and enhance the protocol for handling, disseminating, and processing clinical records and private medical data.

The chapter will induce a study’s specific goals to identify the use cases for Blockchain Technology in healthcare.
