# Remote patient monitoring

Another illustration is the Gem Health Network (GHN), created by the US firm Gem using the Ethereum blockchain. The GHN makes it possible for many healthcare professionals to share access to the same data. Healthbank, a Swiss digital health startup, is making similar efforts, leveraging Blockchain Technology to give patients complete control over their data. The author discusses the Medicalchain project, whose blockchain-based platform would allow the exchange of patient medical records across international healthcare facilities, and the Healthcoin proposal, which aims to create a global EMR system. Some of the other businesses involved in various programs and projects are based on patient-centric EMRs enabled by Blockchain Technology. In this situation, researchers describe a use case in which data is transmitted from mobile devices to a blockchain-based application on Hyperledger Fabric.

The author uses a real-time patient monitoring application to show how Ethereum smart contracts may give automatic interventions in a secure setting. Other suggested ways highlight the enormous potential of the Internet of Things (IoT) in many fields, particularly how it is being heavily utilized in e-health. IoBHealth, is a data-flow architecture that mixes the IoT and blockchain.

# Pharmaceutical supply chain

The pharmaceutical sector is another recognized use case for blockchain. Patients may suffer severe effects if they receive false or poor medications. It has been determined that blockchain technology has the power to solve this issue.
