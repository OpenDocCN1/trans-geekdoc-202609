# Predictive-Based Solution for COVID-19

# Abstract

All facets of human existence, numerous economic sectors, and regions of the globe have been badly impacted by the pandemic of COVID-19. To prevent the spread of the new coronavirus, human activity was paused for many months throughout the world for a long period of time. The side effects of those lockdowns can be seen on multiple fronts throughout the world. For example, in the form of economic and political turmoil in many parts of the world. In contrast to previous pandemics, technical innovations of the contemporary century have played a crucial role in protecting the globe from harm. COVID-19 pandemic has highlighted the issues of technological deficiencies and challenges of security and privacy, validating anonymity, user control over records while fully utilizing the available records, and so on. That can be encountered in an emergency or pandemic situation involving large numbers of people. The immutability, integrity, and correct authentication of documents needed by today’s digital economy make Blockchain Technology a possible answer. In pandemic conditions like the present COVID-19, blockchain has the potential to break through technological obstacles and maximize the use of existing resources and infrastructure.
