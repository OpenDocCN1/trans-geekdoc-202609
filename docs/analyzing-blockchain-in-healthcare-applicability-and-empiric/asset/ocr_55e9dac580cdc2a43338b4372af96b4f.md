Some strategies have been implemented to lessen the likelihood of such cyberattacks, including but not limited to:

# Virtual Private Network

These assaults may target company computers connected through home networks; however, Virtual Private Networks (VPN) may encrypt communications between those computers and the corporate network.

# Multi-factor authentication

This adds an extra level of safety to the system. In addition to the username and password, a one-time code sent to a mobile phone is needed to log in using multi-factor authentication.

**Awareness amongst employees:** In order to keep their workers safe, companies should provide seminars on cybersecurity procedures, such as, the significance of secure passwords.

# System updation

The most vulnerable systems are those that are no longer maintained and do not get security updates. A further layer of security may be added by updating outdated systems and firmware.

# Antivirus software

It is essential to keep antivirus databases up to date in order to combat the latest threats.

# Blockchain

Notably, the European Parliamentary Research Service has named blockchain as one of the 10 major technologies that may be used to combat COVID-19. The majority of the restrictions that are associated with centralized computing ecosystems may be eliminated thanks to Blockchain Technology, which offers a decentralized computing service architecture.
