# Introduction

COVID-19, a novel coronavirus illness designated by the World Health Organization (WHO), has brought the whole world to its knees. Illnesses like pneumonia have been spreading rapidly over the globe since it was first discovered in Wuhan at the end of 2019. Because of its quick spread, every nation faced danger and overwhelmed its medical facilities. In order to stop the transmission of the virus, social isolation and personal cleanliness were the only effective means of prevention. Keep your distance from people by at least one meter, stay away from public areas, and always wear a face mask, according to the WHO.
