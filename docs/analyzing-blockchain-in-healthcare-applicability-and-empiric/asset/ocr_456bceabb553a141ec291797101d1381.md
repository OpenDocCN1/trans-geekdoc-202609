In the conceptualization of the uses and gratification theory by scientists, the scholars categorized the social and psychological province of the mass media into five categories. Here is the list ;

+   - Cognitive needs
+   - Affective needs
+   - Personal integrative needs
+   - Social integrative needs
+   - Tension resealed needs

# Cognitive needs

The cognitive needs of UGT are however related to acquiring information, comprehension, knowledge and so on The UGT is of the firm accession that the “choice of a media and specific content” to gratify a specific need is purely a discretion of the user and user-independent in choice”. The cognitive needs seek to influence users to evaluate and determine what kind of media to select its content in order to gratify their cognitive needs. For instance, this need informs people who have diverse needs and wants and why they choose to watch, engage or view any content on social media. This shows that different programs give different satisfaction to people and as well appeal differently.

# Affective needs

According to researchers the affective needs of the UGT relates to pleasure. Thus, people use different media for different purposes or parochial needs such as watching a particular telenovela aired on television. When people are glued to their media or television is the pleasure or satisfaction derived from the program consumed? For organizations to tailor marketing efforts to meet and satisfy the affective need of the consumer, the emotions of the consumer are very key as customers right touching will spur them to select and stick to a product or service. In the health sector, health personnel can rightly ‘touch the consumer’ by choosing a media that will appeal to and satisfy the patient as we live in a technological era where telemedicine is common.

# Personal identity or interactive needs

The personal identity or interactive need seeks to touch the innermost feeling of the individual. This need of the UGT is more concerned with the “self-esteem, personal stability, need for self-respect, self-confidence, integrity, and social status and others in the category” In addendum, it suggests that people decide to use a particular social media platform of the self-prestige or social class (that is, the use of Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit, and so on.). This call is very important to marketing organizations
