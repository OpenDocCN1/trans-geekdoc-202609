# CHAPTER 3
Patient Engagement in Healthcare Using Technology

# Abstract

This study aims to look at the impact of Blockchain (BC) technology on patient engagement in the healthcare industry. The Technology, Environment, and Organization (TEO) model is examined in this chapter. The key features of BC are non-localization (decentralization), security, suitability, capacity, trust, finality, immutability, data traceability, and smart contracts. The barriers found were regulatory barriers, storage barriers, cyber-attacks, technical flaws, and information security. It is also observed that the benefits of BC are that BC ensures data integrity in real-time and BC technology can help alleviate some of the interoperability issues in healthcare.
