Unforgeability of blockchain ensures that an adversary cannot pose as the user may corrupt the network. To this end, unforgeability in blockchain inherent from the data integrity combined with digitally signed transactions was adopted and employed in several works.

## Traceability

From an audit standpoint, traceability is one of the most interesting qualities. Using the chain of blocks, you can go back in time and evaluate the transactions made on a certain date by exploring all the blocks that were generated on that date in the chain of blocks. To audit query operations, you would need to query the blockchain, which doesn’t save them. Because this query has not been registered in the chain, each node answers the information request delivered to it. Consequently, it is impossible to know all the requests made in all the nodes of a given system. The blockchain serves as a central repository for all of these consolidated transactions. The number of nodes that make up the blockchain network store the complete chain, which is constantly expanding in size. ## Privacy

In a public blockchain, the addresses are not linked to the identities of the people who manage each individual blockchain address, and this property is indicative of that. Having the public and private key pair to control your blockchain address is required to work on a public blockchain. A person’s identity cannot be tied to the address or the keys because the method that generates them doesn’t require any personal information. As a result, using this method of generating a blockchain address allows users to remain anonymous while doing so. The transactions are linked to an address and signed with a key that does not contain any information about the identity of the person who executed them.

There are several ways to generate a set of keys and an address for an entire blockchain, but the most common method is to use the software of a blockchain solution like Bitcoin or Ethereum to generate the keys and addresses (or to utilize a service provided by a company like an exchange house to do so).

There are some distributions that don’t have this feature since they don’t support the blockchain. The public blockchain’s privacy is one of its most important features, and it was a major factor in its early success. However, several countries have rejected the use of blockchain as a form of payment since some people have utilized this privacy to engage in unlawful activities.

## Transparency

The integrity of the document is safeguarded by keeping an audit trail of all modifications, and this builds confidence in the asset. Publishing the rules that define the blockchain’s operation is one way to bring about transparency in the cryptocurrency operation. A network of node and BC developers that adhere to the philosophy of open-source code is created by making the blockchain’s source code available to the public. For two persons who do not trust each other to carry out a transaction on the blockchain, they must have faith in the blockchain’s ability to function. An address is required to use the blockchain. This unique identifier is built from the public key and hash functions and has a defined size. An alphanumeric identification number with a maximum of 35 characters is generated for Bitcoin.

## BC for healthcare

Blockchain is a technology that is being designed to manage electronic data, which has the potential to support transparency and accountability. Here is the key critical list of the following application:

## Keeping tabs on outbreaks and diseases

The unique properties of BC can help with real-time disease reporting and disease pattern exploration, which can help locate the disease’s origin and transmission mechanisms.

## Assuring the availability and safekeeping of medical equipment

Pharmaceutical supply can be made more secure and traceable using BC. Monitoring labor costs and carbon emissions during supply chain production is also possible.

## Claims for medical care made through health insurance

It is impossible to alter the data on the BC at a later time for fraudulent purposes, making it ideal for usage in the claims processing industry.

## Keeping genetics under wraps

Genomic data theft has become a big problem as more and more companies deliver DNA sequencing to individuals. BC technology can help to avoid this, and it can even be used to create an internet marketplace where scientists can buy genomic data for research. This could lead to more secure sales and the elimination of costly intermediaries. Healthcare BC applications are still in their infancy. BC technology frameworks like Hyperledger Fabric and Ethereum are currently in use on a smaller scale. BC services could be adopted to improve the administration of healthcare data with increased BC security and systems that promote synchronized transactions.

## Benefits of BC adoption in the health care system

Disruptive BC technology has recently received a lot of attention and buzz. Organizations are now seriously considering implementing this technology because of its many potential advantages. Cost savings, traceability-transparency, and sustainability improvements have been cited as potential benefits. Blockchain aids in the elimination of information inequities that may deprive small businesses and farmers of social and financial opportunities. Blockchain can also contribute to social supply chain sustainability by reducing unethical, dishonest, and counterfeit activities.

BCs could offer a single transaction layer where organizations can submit and share data securely by retaining a common standard set of data on the BC and private encrypted linkages to independent data such as radiographic or other images. To battle the COVID-19 pandemic, new BC-enabled technologies are emerging, such as an identity management system in South Korea to allow contact tracking and a plan to support data and software code sharing for research purposes. Supply chain management for pharmaceuticals, medical supplies, and vaccination has also been utilized or proposed with BC.

Distributed ledger technology can track social and environmental events that may pose a threat to environmental ecological concerns and social issues such as others’ health and safety. This capacity can contribute to social, environmental, and economic sustainability.

The vast majority of recently published studies have as their primary focus just remote patient monitoring and medical/health data management, including data sharing and storage. Developing monitoring systems that prevent counterfeit pharmaceuticals and medical blunders is essential to ensuring the safety of patients. Doctors’ activities and drug supply chain management can be effectively monitored using BC technology in conjunction with medical technology.

## Barriers to BC adoption in the health care system

Despite the great promise that BC technology has, there are many roadblocks in the way to its widespread adoption. As a result of this constant communication, there is a substantial amount of overhead traffic. Bandwidth-constrained IoT devices can’t afford this.

In addition, distributed ledgers are difficult to search for specific phrases or information, as opposed to traditional databases. They are therefore of limited use as clinical and research data repositories. The user interfaces for BC-enabled ledgers are still in the early stages of development, limiting their deployment at a large scale. When BC is still a relatively new technology, new technological remedies may arise as the technology is used more frequently and in a wider variety of industries.

Complex cryptography in BC is resource-expensive, necessitating intensive processing and significant energy consumption, both of which medical devices with limited resources simply cannot afford to consume. As a result, such solutions cannot be implemented since standard protocols for adapting and promoting interoperability across heterogeneous technology are not defined. Medical devices, BC, cloud computing, and end-users all need to work together, therefore it’s critical that universal, platform-agnostic solutions are available.

In addition, while BC technology offers built-in protection against some forms of assault, it is vulnerable to others. Social engineering and zero-day attacks are both possible because of the BC code. Because of this, information security is extremely important, especially in the healthcare industry. Regulation is another concern. Regulatory agencies may find it difficult to trust BC and hence criticize it as a new governance technology that competes with other established institutions of capitalism and networks.
