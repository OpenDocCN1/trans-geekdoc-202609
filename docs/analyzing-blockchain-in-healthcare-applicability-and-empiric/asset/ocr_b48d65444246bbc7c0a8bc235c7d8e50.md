# The management system of information at hospitals

**Health Management Information System (HIMS)** is responsible for the collection and management of data including medical records as well as information on patients and physicians. The system delivers information that is up-to-date on appointments, hospital rooms, and other such topics. The hospital information management system offers the healthcare industry a number of important advantages, including the following:

+   - Simple document searches
+   - Remote access to patient’s medical data
+   - High levels of security
+   - Drug management
+   - Enhancement of the overall patient experience

Electronic health records are sometimes abbreviated as EHR. These types of systems provide information in real-time to authorized users in an instant and in a secure manner. Electronic health record systems do more than just save information about patients. They are also able to communicate data among many professionals, which may lead to improved treatment outcomes. In general, electronic health record systems provide a number of advantages to the medical community, both for patients and for their treating physicians. For instance, medical professionals have prompt access to the most recent results of a patient’s tests and they may also see an exhaustive account of the patient’s illness history. Concerning the patients, they are able to get in touch with their physicians at any time, which is particularly helpful for those who suffer from chronic conditions.
