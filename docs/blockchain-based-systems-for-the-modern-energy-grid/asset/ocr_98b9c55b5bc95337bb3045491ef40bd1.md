In various studies, they classified EV charging into three varieties: uncontrolled charging, delayed charging, and smart charging. Uncontrolled charging is the process in which charging stops only when the battery reaches full battery capacity. Delayed charging is where service providers use the responsibility to encourage EV owners to shift their EV load from peak to off-peak hours as per their convenience. Lastly, smart charging is a process of charging in which the EV charging station is limited by a mathematical formula. The conveyance framework can initiate smart charging.

Blockchain-enabled simply means that the end-user has a simple and secure payment system that uses digital currencies like bitcoin or Ether and can be paid with either a conventional wallet application or an RFID/NFC card (Aste et al., 2017). The following are the primary advantages of paying for a charging cycle using one of these ways:

+   - There is no need to pre-register with a local charging provider.
+   - There is no outside installment or charge card provider; thus, there are almost no costs.
+   - EV charging could become the standard, both locally and globally.
+   - The charging station may be operated by governments, organizations, and private individuals (P2P) on the same homogenous blockchain framework, thanks to the incorporated blockchain innovation.
+   - On a global scale, data collection (for example, energy charge per charging cycle) becomes a reality.
+   - The charging station is a claimed IoT Oracle for catching ecological information, such as air pollution, and is foreordained to be the choice of smart urban populations of the future (Huang et al., 2020).

Instructions for making a payment for a charging cycle.

# 12.4.3   Payment can be made in two ways

To begin with, the EV owner can use a wallet program such as Jaxx, Coinbase, or Free wallet. Clients scan a QR code shown on the charging station’s touchscreen and enter their required amount. When the transaction is confirmed, the touchscreen instructs the car owner to attach the charging cable to his or her vehicle. The charging process begins automatically.

The EV owner can also use an NFC card to pay. This card is known as the Ethan Card. The NFC card functions as a wallet with a wallet address. The customer can purchase a pre-loaded card with various amounts, such as $10, $25, $50, $100 USD, EUR, or other fiat currency.
