The introduction of blockchain technology for cryptographic money has given the world yet another venture. It provides a reliable record that keeps track of transactions in a safe manner. Blockchain is a technology that underpins bitcoin, Ether, and other common digital currencies. The blockchain has applications that are beyond the use of digital currency. Blockchain is a type of decentralized record, and not all decentralized records use block or chain swapping by default. This article proposes a blockchain-enabled P2P energy exchange and payment system for EVs (Mengelkamp et al., 2018). As the process outlined below shows, the outcomes of the system can provide a lot of time and resources to enter into these projects now, and they are less difficult and risky comparatively:

+   - Activate P2P EV energy trade and local energy balancing with DLT.
+   - Make it easier to pay at charging stations.
+   - EV owners get access to real-time pricing data.
+   - A mobile application is needed.
+   - Both EV and charging station owners must register in the mobile app.
+   - As a P2P exchange layer, the architecture uses an e-wallet and brilliant agreements on the public Ethereum Blockchain, including the Euro-based Mobility Token.
+   - P2P services allow EV and charging direct owners to lease their charging framework

We’ve proposed a blockchain-based platform for EV charging installment plans. The proposed framework is practical because it addresses the issue with EVs, which are deemed more environmentally friendly than vehicles based on petroleum derivatives. Due to the safe and reliable decentralized dataset supported by a blockchain, the suggested shared energy exchanging and charging installation framework for blockchain-based EVs can gain validity and broad applications in real-world transportation settings (Zhu et al., 2020).
