# **12.4   Electric vehicle charging**

Electric vehicles are expected to represent the next generation of mechanical power for transportation and progress. These vehicles have the ability to alter energy usage, age, and redirection. Electric vehicles can overcome the negative environmental impact of conventional vehicles (EV). Traditional vehicles emit a large amount of fossil fuel by-products that are released into the air and inhaled by the general public, making individuals immune to contamination and ozone-depleting substances. An electric car is a significant step forward in terms of making a significant contribution to the globe (Li et al., 2014; Tang & Zhang, 2016).
