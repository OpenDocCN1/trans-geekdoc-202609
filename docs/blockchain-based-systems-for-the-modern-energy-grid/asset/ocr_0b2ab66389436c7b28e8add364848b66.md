# **12.3.3 Aggregating distributed energy storage: a blockchain-based topology**

Electric vehicle, like power plants, will be used as decentralized energy storage systems capable of producing electricity in response to demand analysis. Many of these EV charging stations might help the localized distribution network by operating as a virtual power plant (VPP) in the region, and the blockchain could help secure and simplify the transactions. The EC aggregator may pool the available stored energy from several EV charging stations, letting them compete for power supply and alleviating stress on the local DT. (Dinh et al., 2017) and (Huang et al., 2020) refer Fig. 12.5.

As a result of the swapping of electricity units and monetary value, a huge number of transactions can take place. When the local transformer level is balancing the grid, the efficiency and availability of transactions in a secure way will enable optimal use of electricity stored in EVs through blockchain technology. This would also create a market that is both competitive and transparent. Each and every transaction will be informed to each player aggregator and grid operator, making participation simple and easy.
