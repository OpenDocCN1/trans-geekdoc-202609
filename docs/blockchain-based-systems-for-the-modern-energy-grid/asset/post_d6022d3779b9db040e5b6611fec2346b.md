### 12.4.1 Relevance and impacts

A distributed ledger that is shared across a public or private computing network is a catalog of data. The system eliminates the need for a central authority to validate transactions among different parties to avoid double-spending. Before being added to the ledger, each piece of data is mathematically encrypted and attached as a new "block" to the historical records chain, which is then validated by different consensus methods. Smart contracts are a set of conditions stored on the blockchain that activate transactions when the requirements are met and can also be encoded into the blockchain (Xiang et al., 2018).
