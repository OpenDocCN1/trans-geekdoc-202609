The Art of Prompting – Foundations of a Power User

Let’s begin with the foundation.

 

What Is Prompting, Really?

Prompting is the act of communicating with an AI model in a way that gets you               the best possible output.

It’s like giving instructions to a super-fast, super-smart intern:

If you’re vague, it’ll guess (and usually get it wrong).

If you’re specific, it’ll execute (often brilliantly).

A prompt isn’t just a command. It’s a creative collaboration.

And when done right, it feels magical.

 

Why Prompting Is the New Literacy

In the past, we learned to type. Then, to Google.

Now? We learn to prompt.

The way we ask questions is becoming more important than what we know.

Consider this:

●        A designer who prompts well can generate assets faster than a traditional design team.

●        A coach who builds prompt stacks can serve more clients and automate onboarding.

●        A creator who understands prompting can turn one idea into 15 pieces of content — instantly.

Prompting is the meta-skill of the digital age.

  And yes — you can learn it. Fast.

 

Prompt Power = Input Quality × Iteration Speed

 

The output you get from AI depends on two things:

●        How well you define the task (the input)

●        How quickly you improve it (the iteration)

 

A bad prompt = generic junk.

A good prompt = custom gold.

A great prompt = a repeatable system.

 

Let’s look at an example:

Weak Prompt:
" Write me a blog post about mindset ." 

Better Prompt:
"Write a 700-word blog post for millennials about building a growth mindset, with 3 examples, a motivational tone, and a tweetable summary at the end."  

Power Prompt: 
"Act as a motivational writer. Write a 700-word blog post for 25–35-year-olds struggling with self-doubt, focused on building a growth mindset. Structure: 1) relatable story intro, 2) 3 mindset shifts, 3) summary with a one-line takeaway. Make the tone vulnerable but empowering."

 

The third prompt gives AI a role, structure, audience, tone, and goal.

That’s prompting like a power user.

 

The Anatomy of a Great Prompt

Here’s a simple template to make any prompt more powerful:

●        🧠 ACT AS : Assign the AI a role or persona

●        🎯 TASK : Tell it what to create

●        👥 AUDIENCE : Say who it’s for

●        💬 TONE & FORMAT : Set the vibe or structure

●        📌 CONTEXT/EXAMPLES : (Optional but powerful)

 

When in doubt, over-inform the AI.

It works best with clear constraints.

Before you use AI for anything, run through this:

🧠 Wait — What’s the Difference Between a Prompt Stack and a Prompt Pack?

Let’s clear it up early so you can use them with confidence:

Term

Think of it like...

What It Does

Structure

Prompt                                   Stack

A recipe

Helps you complete one specific task using a series of prompts

Step-by-step sequence

      Prompt Pack

A cookbook

Gives you a full collection of prompt stacks for one goal or role

Multiple stacks + bonuses

 

🔹 Prompt Stack

A prompt stack is a mini-system.

Each prompt builds on the last — guiding you from idea to execution.

 

Example:

Want to write a blog post? A prompt stack might include:

  1. Define your reader
  2. Generate title options
  3. Outline the structure
  4. Write section drafts
  5. Add CTA and polish

 

🔸 Prompt Pack

A prompt pack is a bundle of multiple stacks and related prompts.

It’s designed to help you win in a category — like freelancing, coaching, or content creation.

 

Example:

The Freelancer’s Prompt Pack includes stacks for:

●        Building a bio

●        Pitching clients

●        Writing proposals

●        Automating follow-ups

 

Think of prompt stacks as the “how,” and prompt packs as the “what.”

Together? You become unstoppable.

Digital Products Prompt Pack – Create Once, Sell Forever

Welcome to your new favourite income stream: digital products.

Unlike service work, which trades time for money, digital products give you                             scale.

Create something once. Sell it a hundred — or a thousand — times.

No shipping. No inventory. No burnout.

Even better?

  You don’t need to be an expert, designer, or course creator.

  You just need the right idea, a simple system, and a few powerful prompts.

 

In this chapter, we’ll use prompt stacks to:

●        Find high-demand, low-effort product ideas

●        Outline and draft your product with AI

●        Name, package, and sell it

●        Automate your content creation

●        Start earning passively (or semi-passively) with confidence

 

Let’s turn your knowledge into income.

 

Prompt Stack #1: Idea Generator – Low Effort, High Demand

 

 

The best digital product ideas solve a small but annoying problem.

 

Start here:

Act as a digital creator coach. I want to launch a simple product under $25. 
Target audience: [describe your niche]. 
Prompt: Suggest 10 digital product ideas I can make in under a week using AI + no-code tools. For each, include: 
- Product title 
- What problem it solves 
- Format (e.g. Notion, Canva, PDF, etc.) 
- Monetization potential 

 

Follow-up prompt:

Rank these ideas based on effort to build vs. audience demand.

 

You now have a validated idea list tailored to your strengths.

 

Prompt Stack #2: Product Structure & Build Plan

Once you pick an idea, map it out.

 

Act as a product strategist. I’ve chosen [product idea]. 
Create a structure for it — outline 5 sections or modules. For each section, write a 1–2 sentence description. 
Format: [Notion / PDF / Carousel / Template Kit] 

 

Want a build plan too?

Now give me a simple 3-day build plan. Day-by-day tasks to complete this product from scratch using AI.

Now you’re not just dreaming — you’re executing.

 

Prompt Stack #3: Drafting with AI (Content + Voice)

Time to create your product content — fast.

 

Act as a product writer. Create the content for Section 1 of my product titled “[insert title].” 
Tone: [fun, actionable, empowering, minimalist, etc.] 
Audience: [entrepreneurs, creators, parents, etc.] 
Output: bullet points, short tips, or formatted text that’s skimmable and usable. 
Keep the word count under [X]. 

💡 Tip: Use this per section, then polish as you go. Add your personal insights, stories, and examples.

 

Prompt Stack #4: Product Name & Tagline

Naming paralysis? Prompt it away.

 

Act as a branding expert. Suggest 10 catchy names for a digital product that helps [audience] with [result]. 
Make the names short, clear, and benefit-driven. Include a one-line tagline or subtitle for each. 

Still stuck?

 

Give me names in these tones: clever, minimalist, emotional. 

You’ll know when it clicks.

 

Prompt Stack #5: Design Suggestions & Format Flow

Even if you’re not a designer, your product can still look amazing.

Act as a digital product designer. Suggest a layout for a Notion/Canva/PDF product based on my content outline. 
Include: 
- Section headers 
- Icons or visuals 
- Fonts to use 
- Color scheme 

Keep it clean and on-brand for [audience type].

 

Bonus:

Suggest where I can add callouts, quotes, or illustrations to improve visual flow. 

This turns your AI-generated content into a branded asset.

 

Prompt Stack #6: Sales Page Copy That Converts

Now let’s make it sell.

 

Act as a sales copywriter. Write a product sales page for “[product name]” that helps [audience] solve [pain point]. 
Include: 
1. Hook headline 
2. Problem section 
3. Features + benefits 
4. What’s inside 
5. Call-to-action 
Tone: friendly, no hype, clear value

 

Optional CTA prompt:

Write 3 CTAs I can use — soft, bold, and curious tones. 

Now you have copy for Gumroad, Shopify, Podia — wherever you host it.

 

Prompt Stack #7: Lead Magnet or Bonus Creation

Want to boost sales or grow your email list? Add a freebie.

 

Act as a marketing strategist. Based on my product “[product name],” suggest 3 lead magnets or bonuses I can offer. 
For each, describe: 


- What it is 
- How it connects to the main product 
- Why it adds value or increases conversions 

Now prompt to build one:

Now create the first draft of bonus #2 — make it light, useful, and quick to consume. 

Your $19 product now feels like a $49 steal.

 

Prompt Stack #8: Launch Content Engine

You’ve built it — now let’s tell the world.

 

Act as a content marketer. Create 5 short-form post ideas (Twitter/Instagram/LinkedIn) to promote “[product name]” to [audience]. 
Include a hook, body, and call-to-action in each. 
Make the tone [bold, educational, vulnerable, etc.]

Want a carousel idea?

 

Now turn Post #2 into a 5-slide Instagram carousel. Include slide headings and caption copy. 

Done. Share. Repeat.

Step 1: Get Client-Ready

 

Before you pitch, you need to sound professional — not desperate. Use these prompt stacks to build a winning portfolio, cover letters, and proposals.

 

🧠 Prompt Stack: Build Your Freelance Profile

Prompt 1: 
Act as a branding expert. Help me create a compelling freelancer bio for my [freelance skill — e.g., copywriting, graphic design, etc.], targeting [ideal clients]. Keep it under 200 words, with a confident and friendly tone. 

Prompt 2: 
Create a tagline and short elevator pitch I can use on my freelance profiles. It should sound expert-level but approachable. My top 3 skills are [skill1], [skill2], and [skill3]. 


Prompt 3: 
Give me 3 unique value propositions I can highlight to stand out from other freelancers in [industry]. Avoid generic phrases like 'hardworking' or 'attention to detail.'
 

 

🧠 Prompt Stack: Write Proposals That Win

Prompt 1: 
Act as a top-rated freelancer. Write a tailored proposal for this job description: [Paste client brief]. Keep it concise, show understanding of the brief, and suggest 1–2 improvements or ideas proactively. 

Prompt 2: 
Rewrite this proposal to be more client-focused and persuasive: [Paste your draft]. Add personality, clarity, and professionalism. 

Prompt 3: 
Give me a checklist of 5 things every winning proposal should include. Base it on top performers on Upwork/Fiverr. 

 

Step 2: Deliver High-Quality Work Fast

The real money isn’t in getting jobs — it’s in keeping clients. These prompts will help you overdeliver consistently.

 

🧠 Prompt Stack: Writing & Copy Projects

Prompt 1: 
You are an expert copywriter. Write a landing page copy for a [product/service] that highlights [key benefits] and ends with a strong CTA. 



Prompt 2: 
Summarize this long article into a crisp, engaging social post. Add relevant hashtags and a call to action: [Paste article]. 

Prompt 3: 
Create 5 email subject lines for this promo campaign. Keep them punchy, curiosity-driven, and under 50 characters. 
 

 

🧠 Prompt Stack: Design & Branding Projects

Prompt 1: 
Act as a creative director. Give me a color palette and mood board description for a brand targeting [audience] with a vibe of [emotion or aesthetic]. 



Prompt 2: 
Suggest 3 logo concepts for a company called [Name] that offers [product/service]. Keep it modern, clean, and scalable.

Prompt 3: 
Write a brand style guide (one-pager) that includes tone of voice, fonts, colors, and dos/don’ts for content creation.  

 

🧠 Prompt Stack: Virtual Assistant/Admin Projects

Prompt 1: 
Help me create a weekly email newsletter for [client type]. Suggest sections, tone, and write an example intro paragraph. 

Prompt 2: 
Build a social media content calendar for [industry] with 3 posts per week. Include themes, hooks, and CTA ideas.
 


Prompt 3: 
Create a client onboarding email that sounds professional but warm. It should thank them, explain next steps, and ask for any needed info.

You’re not just here to hustle — you’re here to scale. Use these prompts to create templates and automations for repeat tasks.

Chapter 4

Ebook Author’s Prompt Pack – Write, Format, Publish with AI

 

Writing an ebook used to feel like a mountain climb.

  Blank pages. Endless drafts. Design stress. Self-doubt.

But with AI and prompt stacking?

  You can go from idea to finished, formatted ebook in days, not months.

 

This chapter is for:

●        Coaches who want a digital lead magnet

●        Writers turning blogs into books

●        Creators selling ebooks on Gumroad or Amazon

●        Anyone with knowledge to share (and no time to waste)

Inside, you’ll get the prompt stacks to:

●        Plan your ebook’s content

●        Draft chapters lightning-fast

●        Edit, polish, and format

●        Create titles, covers, blurbs

●        Upload and launch like a pro

 

Prompt Stack #1: Define Your Big Idea & Reader Promise

 

Before you write, define the core transformation your book offers.

Act as an ebook strategist. I want to write a short ebook (5,000–10,000 words) for [audience]. 
Topic: [insert topic] 
Help me answer: 
– What is the core problem this ebook solves? 
– What transformation will the reader experience? 
– What makes this approach unique? 

Follow-up:

Now suggest 3 different angles or titles that focus on that transformation. 

You now have your why, who, and hook.

 

Prompt Stack #2: Chapter Outline Generator

Structure is clarity.

Let AI build the bones.

Create a 7–10 chapter outline for an ebook titled “[insert working title]” aimed at [audience]. 
Goal: Help the reader go from [starting point] to [desired result]. 
Include chapter titles + a 1–2 sentence description for each. 

 

Still refining?

Now group these chapters into 3 parts: Beginning, Middle, End. 
Add a one-sentence summary for each part. 

 

Now you’re ready to write with direction.

 

Prompt Stack #3: Chapter Drafts (That Don’t Sound Robotic)

Writing chapters with AI is like co-writing with a brilliant, fast ghostwriter.

 

Act as a non-fiction ebook writer. 
Write Chapter 1 of “[ebook title]” for [audience]. 
Tone: [friendly, practical, motivational, etc.] 
Structure: 
1. Hook or story intro 


2. Teaching or core content (3–4 sub-sections) 
3. Summary or takeaway 
Word count: ~1,000–1,200 

 

Tweak as you go:

Rewrite section 2 with more storytelling and less instruction. 
Make it sound like a real person sharing experience. 

You don’t need perfect — you need a solid first draft.

 

Prompt Stack #4: Edits, Voice, & Flow Polish

Let AI help you tighten your prose and unify your voice.

Improve clarity:

Edit this chapter for clarity and structure. 
Break long paragraphs. Use simpler language. Remove fluff.

Adjust tone:

Rewrite this chapter in a more [bold / compassionate / witty] voice. 
Keep the ideas, change the delivery. 

 

Add a quote or metaphor:

Add a relevant quote or metaphor in the intro to create emotional pull. 

Your raw draft now sounds sharp, human, and readable.

 

Prompt Stack #5: Title, Subtitle & Tagline

Titles sell. But coming up with a great one is hard — until you prompt it.

Act as a book marketer. Suggest 10 title + subtitle combinations for a non-fiction ebook that helps [audience] with [problem]. 
Make titles: 
– Clear, not clever 
– Benefit-driven 
– Skimmable 
Tone: [friendly / bold / empowering]

 

Follow-up:

Now give me one ultra-short tagline I can use on the cover or landing page. 

Test your favorites. Then lock it in.

 

Prompt Stack #6: Author Bio, Back Cover, and Amazon Description

Let’s polish your presence.

 

Author bio:

Write a short author bio (75–100 words) for someone who [your background/role]. 
Tone: approachable, credible, a touch of personality. 

 

Amazon description:

Write a book description for “[title]” that: 
– Hooks attention 
– Outlines what’s inside 


– Describes transformation 


– Ends with a CTA 
Limit: 200 words. Format with short paragraphs. 

 

Back cover blurb:

Now shorten that into a back cover blurb: 3 lines, 2 benefits, 1 bold sentence. 

Your ebook now sounds as pro as it is.

 

Prompt Stack #7: Formatting Guidance (Without the Headache)

You don’t need Vellum or fancy tools. You need structure.

Act as a layout guide. How should I format a Kindle ebook in Word or Google Docs? 
Include: 
– Font suggestions 
– Heading styles 
– Paragraph spacing 
– Page breaks 
– TOC setup 
Make it clean and Kindle-friendly. 

 

Need visuals?

Suggest a Canva template for a minimalist nonfiction cover. 
Recommend 2–3 visual themes or fonts that fit this book’s tone. 

Consistency = professionalism.

 

Prompt Stack #8: Launch Content & Promotion Plan

Let’s sell this thing.

Act as a digital launch strategist. 
Create a 7-day launch content plan for my ebook “[title]” for [audience]. 
Include: 
– 3 tweet ideas 
– 2 Instagram captions 
– 1 launch email 
– 1 “behind the scenes” story 
Tone: [educational / personal / hype-light] 

 

Want evergreen content?

Now turn that into 5 evergreen posts I can reuse monthly. 

 

Now you’re not just an author — you’re a content machine.

Bonus Chapter

Build Your Own Prompt Stack – The Framework Method

 

By now, you’ve seen how prompting can transform your workflow.

From ebooks and digital products…

  To freelance systems, content creation, SaaS ideas, and client work —

 

Prompting lets you turn clarity into action and ideas into income.

●        But here’s the ultimate unlock:

●        You don’t have to rely on pre-made prompts forever.

●        You can build your own stacks, tailored to your workflow, voice, and goals.

●        This chapter gives you the method.

●        We call it: The Framework Method.

●        It’s how power users create prompt stacks that grow with them — across tools, industries, and creative needs.

 

What Is a Prompt Stack, Really?

A prompt stack is more than one good prompt.

It’s a series of prompts , used in a specific order, that helps you:

●        Think

●        Write

●        Build

●        Refine

●        Launch

 

It mimics how you naturally work:

From brainstorming → planning → creating → polishing → sharing.

“One great prompt is helpful. A great prompt stack is unstoppable.”

— Lato Italic Callout

 

The 5-Part Prompt Stack Framework

Use this when you want to build your own stack for any task:

 

1. Define the Outcome

What do you want the end result to be?

A blog post? A Notion kit? A YouTube script? A product idea?

 

Prompt:

I want to use AI to help me [describe the end goal]. 
What kind of prompt stack should I use to get there? 

 

2. Break It Into Stages

Now reverse-engineer the process. What are the steps or layers?

Example for writing a course:

– Define audience

– Outline structure

– Write lessons

– Format content

– Create landing page

 

Prompt:

Break down the process of [insert task] into 3–5 stages. 
Then suggest one AI prompt I can use at each stage.  

 

3. Choose the Format & Tone

Will your output be a PDF? Carousel? Web copy? Tweet?

And what tone should it reflect — fun, professional, edgy, gentle?

 

Prompt:

Suggest the best format and tone for presenting this type of content to [audience type].

This keeps your stack aligned with your style and audience.

 

4. Iterate at Each Stage

 

Each part of the stack should allow for testing, rewriting, or scaling.

Add these inside your stack to make it flexible:

– Rewrite this in a more emotional/informative/short-form way. 
– Add 3 examples or use cases. 
– Turn this into a script/carousel/checklist. 

This makes your stack adaptable , not rigid.

 

5. Systematize & Save

Once it works, don’t start over next time.

Store your stack in Notion, Google Docs, or any prompt library.

Pro tip: Name your stacks.

 

Examples:

“Notion Launch Kit Generator”

“5-Minute Reel Script Builder”

“Ebook in a Weekend Stack”

This turns prompting into a system — not just a habit.

 

Example: Creating a Prompt Stack from Scratch

Let’s say you want to sell a Notion template.

Here’s how to build your custom stack using the Framework:

 

🧩 Outcome

Sell a Notion template for remote workers.

 

🔄 Stages

●        Brainstorm use case

●        Outline content

●        Fill in with sample data

●        Write sales copy

●        Create visuals

 

🛠️ Prompt Stack

1. “Act as a product strategist. What are 10 useful Notion templates remote workers need?” 
2. “Outline the sections of a Notion template called ‘Workday Clarity Planner.’” 


3. “Add sample fields and example content for each section.” 
4. “Write sales page copy for this template, including 3 bullet benefits.” 
5. “Suggest a visual mockup idea using Canva, and write a caption for launch.” 

Save that. Tweak it. Use it again.

Now you’ve got your custom prompt system .

✅ Build in options to revise, remix, or reformat

✅ Save the stack with a name you’ll remember
