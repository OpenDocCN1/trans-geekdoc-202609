CT scanners are also used to diagnose abnormalities in the brain and other cognitive impairment diseases like Alzheimer’s [1].

Figure 5.1 shows a block diagram of a computed tomography machine. The main components of a CT machine are:

+   - **Detectors:** An ionization chamber filled with xenon gas, sealed at both ends, and flanked by two conductors that make up a capacitor make up the detector.
+   - **DC voltage supply:** A high-DC voltage supply is applied to an X-ray tube.
+   - **X-ray tube:** The X-ray tube emits X-rays allowed to pass through the patient.
+   - **Computer machine:** This captures the rays passed through the patient and applies some mathematical formulation to generate the image slices.
