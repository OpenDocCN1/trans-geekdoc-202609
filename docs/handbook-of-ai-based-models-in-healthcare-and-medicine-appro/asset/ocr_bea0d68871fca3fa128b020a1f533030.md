# 14.9 CONCLUSION

In this research paper, we developed an efficient approach for the prediction of heart disease using random forest. Data mining plays an important role in the prediction of heart disease. Applying random forest has improved accuracy in the prediction of heart disease compared to other algorithms. Our proposed approach achieved an accuracy of 96.5% for the heart dataset. The introduction of chatbots serves as an important and quick decision-making tool for immediate solutions before seeking medical help.
