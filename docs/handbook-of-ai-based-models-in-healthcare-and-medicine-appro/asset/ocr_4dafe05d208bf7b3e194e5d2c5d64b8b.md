# FIGURE 10.2 Classification of diabetic retinopathy with abnormalities found on year-based timelines.

| Years-based Timescales | Phases of DR | Retinal Findings |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Normal Retina | No Diabetic Retinopathy |
| 3 to 5 | Stage 1 Mild Non Proliferative DR | Microaneurysms; Hemorrhages; Blood vessels with small bulges |
| 5 to 10 | Stage 2 Moderate Non Proliferative DR | Exudates; Cotton Wools; Growth of new blood vessels |
| 10 to 15 | Stage 3 Severe Non Proliferative DR | Blockages of blood vessels; Venous bleeding; Multiple hemorrhages |
| More than 15 | Proliferative DR | Blood leakages into Retina; Cloudy vision; Complete vision loss |
