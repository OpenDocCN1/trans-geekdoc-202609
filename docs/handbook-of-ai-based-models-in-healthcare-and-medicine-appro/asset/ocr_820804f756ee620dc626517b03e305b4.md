# 16.3.4.1 Working

The flow of questions in MayaMD is straightforward. After answering general questions, such as name, age, and gender, the symptom checker’s questionnaire starts (Figure 16.13).

You feed it a symptom, and it asks questions related to the symptom, then moves on to ask about the next symptom. This process is repeated until the patient enters “no” in the “Other symptoms?” question, after which it generates a result.
