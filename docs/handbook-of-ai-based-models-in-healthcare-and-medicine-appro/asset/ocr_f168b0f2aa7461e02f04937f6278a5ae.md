First, the generator generates a batch of images, then, the parameters of model G are fixed while a single iteration of gradient descent is performed on discriminator D and the gradient is propagated back through the discriminator. Next, model D's parameters are fixed, and the generator is trained for a single iteration of gradient descent. The generator begins with a noise input (z) with normal distribution and learns to map to G(z, θ_g) with objective function to achieve the highest D(G(z, θ_g)), given by Equation 6.1, where D learns to map input X to binary vector Y representing the label associated with the image, predicting the probability of given example X belonging to each of the classes with objective function. The overall objective function for GAN-MLC, also known as a min-max game with zero-sum, where the generator tries to minimize V and the discriminator tries to maximize it, is given as:

min_G max_D V(D,G) = E_{X~P_data(x)}[log D(x)] + E_{z~P_z(z)}[log(1 - D(G(z)))] (6.1)
