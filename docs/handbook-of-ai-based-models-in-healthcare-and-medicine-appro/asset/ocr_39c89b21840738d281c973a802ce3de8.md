The expert system market started to collapse in the late 1980s, as new research stopped finding its way into business. Once more, the AI trend ended in disappointment, and this sparked what was called the “second AI winter”. Since then, machine learning, a new approach to AI, has largely replaced knowledge-based systems. It aims to link factors to respond using complex nonlinear functions immediately trained from the existing data without expert suggestions. We are currently experiencing a third wave of AI boom, driven by technological advancements, which allows us to recognize signals across space and time.
