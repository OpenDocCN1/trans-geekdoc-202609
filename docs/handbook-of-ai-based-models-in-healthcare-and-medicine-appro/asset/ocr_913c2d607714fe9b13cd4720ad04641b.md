# 2.1.1 THE VIRTUAL BRANCH OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

The virtual branch of AI in medicine has two primary subfields: ML and DL, as shown in Figure 2.1. The mathematical techniques that use DL to enhance learning through experience serve as the virtual component. ML algorithms can be classified into three categories: (1) unsupervised (ability to detect patterns), (2) supervised (algorithms for categorization and prediction based on prior data), and (3) reinforcement learning (using a specific problem space as the basis for a method that uses a series of incentives and penalties). First, AI has expedited and continues to speed up genetic and molecular medical discoveries by offering ML algorithms and knowledge management.
