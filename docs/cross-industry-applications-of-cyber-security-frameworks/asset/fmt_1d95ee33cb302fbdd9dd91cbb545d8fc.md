
# Analysis of Cybercrime Data (2017–Mid-2019)

This section presents and discusses the results received from the analysis of cybercrime data collected from the Statista database from 2017 to mid-2019.

1.  **Funds Released and Utilized**: Details of funds released and utilized on cyber security by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) in India during this period.
2.  **CERT-In Incident Trends**: Month-wise trend of cyber security incidents reported to the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In).
3.  **Cybercrime against Children**: Analysis of different types of cybercrimes against children.
4.  **Cybercrime against Women**: Facts and figures of different types of cybercrimes against women.
5.  **Training Programs**: Status of different types of training programs across various states.

## Cybercrime against Children

There are different types of cybercrimes against children. Details of types such as **Cyber blackmailing/threatening** (Sec. 506, 503, 384 IPC R/W IT Act), **Fake profiling** (IT Act R/W IPC/SLL), **Cyber pornography/hosting and publishing obscene sexual materials depicting children** (Sec. 67B of IT Act R/W other IPC/SLL), **Cyber stalking/bullying** (Sec. 354D IPC r/w IT Act), and **Internet crimes through online games** (Sec. 305 IPC r/w IT Act) are shown in **Figure 4**. This figure presents a state-wise comparative analysis.

| Figure 4. State-wise comparison of different types of Cybercrime against children |
| --- |
| ![Figure978-1-6684-3448-2.ch001.f04](i/ch001.f04.png) |

---

Analysis depicts that the maximum number of cyber blackmailing/threatening cases were found in Maharashtra and Chhattisgarh. Maharashtra topped various crime categories against children, including cyber stalking/bullying, fake profiles, and internet crimes through online games. For cyber pornography/hosting and publishing obscene sexual materials depicting children, Himachal Pradesh ranked highest.
