instance, our current implementation does not prioritize cost-efficiency, leaving room for enhancing communication and computational speeds. Furthermore, it is not ideal for decentralized online learning scenarios involving general inequality constraints. Future efforts will concentrate on boosting the communication and computational efficiency of our method. Moreover, it would be compelling to expand our research to decentralized online optimization scenarios encompassing either equality or inequality constraints.
