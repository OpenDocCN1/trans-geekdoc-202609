# 6.5.3 Decentralized targets tracking

In this example, our primary focus revolves around a dynamic target tracking challenge, inspired by the work presented in [29], where a collaborative effort among sensors is essential to monitor several moving entities. Recognizing the inherent nature of real-world scenarios, wherein sensors exchange information in a unidirectional fashion, the ensuing communication network architecture is inherently asymmetric. These attributes align perfectly with the operational context of Algorithm 1. Consider a network comprising 5 interconnected nodes, characterized by its temporal and directional information flow. Specifically, each target, denoted as \( z_{l,t}^* = [z_{l,t}^{*,(1)}, z_{l,t}^{*,(2)}] \in \mathbb{R}^2, l \in \{1, 2, 3\} \), is portrayed in the following manner:

```
\( z_{l,t}^* = \begin{bmatrix} z_{l,t}^{*,(1)} \\ z_{l,t}^{*,(2)} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} z_{l,t}^{*,(1)} \\ \dot{z}_{l,t}^{*,(1)} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} v_l \sin(\gamma_l t + \rho_l) \\ \gamma_l v_l \cos(\gamma_l t + \rho_l) \end{bmatrix}, \)
```

where \( z_{l,t}^{*,(1)} \) and \( \dot{z}_{l,t}^{*,(1)} \) denote the location and speed of target \( l \) at time \( t \) respectively. \( v_l \) signifies the amplitude, \( \gamma_l \) indicates the angular frequency and \( \rho_l \) designates the phase of target \( l \). At instant \( t \), each node utilizes \( \phi_t^i = \psi^i z_t^* \) as an indicator to document its advancements in the tracking process, where \( z_t^* = [(z_{1,t}^*)^T, (z_{2,t}^*)^T, (z_{3,t}^*)^T]^T \) and \( \psi^i \in \mathbb{R}^{1 \times 6} \) denotes a randomly devised measurement vector. The nodes collectively strive to diminish the universal cost function \( f_t(x) = \sum_{i=1}^5 ||\psi^i x - \phi_t^i|| / 2 \)
