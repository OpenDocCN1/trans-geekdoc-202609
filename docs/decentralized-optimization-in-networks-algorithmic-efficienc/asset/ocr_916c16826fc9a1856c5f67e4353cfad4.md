# ## 5.5.3 Other directions

Algorithm 1 provides a hopeful launching pad for tackling the present obstacles in this domain. However, additional investigations are required to completely enhance the algorithm's efficiency. Below, we outline several research avenues that have the potential to further refine the efficiency of Algorithm 1, reserving the pertinent theoretical exploration for subsequent studies.

+   (i) Communication efficiency: Taking into account that Algorithm 1 necessitates every node in the network to engage in communication with its incoming neighbors for each instance of the assigned online learning assignment, it becomes evident that this communication approach demonstrates less resilience and possesses a comparatively subpar communication efficiency. Inspired by recent studies referenced in [2,21,53], we have the opportunity to incorporate asynchronous communication tactics into the algorithm. This integration serves to alleviate the communication burden, thereby achieving enhanced communication efficiency.

+   (ii) Computation efficiency: Real-world online learning implementations, whether in the medical or military domain, inherently entail the handling of extensive datasets. This poses significant challenges to the computational capabilities of the algorithms, especially considering the finite processing power of actual hardware. In this scenario, taking cues from references [57,58], we may utilize stochastic gradients as an alternative to the deterministic gradient used in Algorithm 1. Such a replacement notably lightens the computational load on individual nodes, without compromising the algorithm's convergence properties.

+   (iii) General constraints: Furthermore, by employing problem transformation techniques referenced in [3], Algorithm 1 possesses the capability to address widespread constrained (inequality) optimization challenges within directed networks.
