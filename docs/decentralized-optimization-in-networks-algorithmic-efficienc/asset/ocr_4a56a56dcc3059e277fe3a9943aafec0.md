The convergence speed follows a rate of \(\mathcal{O}(\hat{\lambda}^k)\), where \(0 < \hat{\lambda} < 1\). Through rigorous numerical simulations using various benchmark datasets, we have proven that our P-DVR algorithm exhibits remarkable computational efficiency and precise convergence. Nevertheless, it is important to note that P-DVR currently does not accommodate directed networks or those with compromised communication quality. Future explorations will focus on developing algorithms that can operate effectively within these networks. Additionally, we have plans to tackle multi-objective optimization problems using decentralized methods in our upcoming research.
