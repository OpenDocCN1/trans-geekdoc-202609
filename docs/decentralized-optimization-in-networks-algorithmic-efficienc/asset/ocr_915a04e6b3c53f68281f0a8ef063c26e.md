# 4.5 Conclusion

In this chapter, we have devised a new event-triggered decentralized stochastic gradient tracking approach, coined as ET-DSGT, by advancing the real-time decentralized stochastic gradient tracking technique. Agents only disseminate estimators to adjacent nodes during their designated trigger moments, indicating that ET-DSGT circumvents continuous communication, thereby enhancing communication effectiveness. We have demonstrated that, with a diminishing step-size, ET-DSGT can converge to the precise global minimum at a sublinear pace, subject to specific presumptions about the network and cost function. Furthermore, it has been established that, under certain circumstances, the duration between two triggering events exceeds the iteration gap. Consequently, the communication overhead of ET-DSGT is significantly lower compared to corresponding time-triggered methods. Simulation outcomes have confirmed the impressive capabilities of ET-DSGT. Our future efforts will be directed towards adapting the algorithm for constrained optimization challenges and directed networks.
