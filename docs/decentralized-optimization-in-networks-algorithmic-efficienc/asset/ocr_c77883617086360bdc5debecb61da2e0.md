# 6.6 Conclusion

In this chapter, we have crafted an efficient zeroth-order decentralized dual averaging methodology, termed ZO-DDA, tailored for optimizing privacy-sensitive online constrained optimization tasks across time-evolving directed networks. Instead of relying on precise subgradient computations, we harness a zeroth-order oracle. ZO-DDA employs specially crafted row-stochastic matrices to rescale the zeroth-order oracle with an auxiliary parameter, thereby mitigating the imbalance stemming from the dynamic directed network topology. Additionally, to uphold differential privacy, ZO-DDA incorporates perturbations infused with Laplace noise. Our theoretical investigations establish that ZO-DDA, under reasonable assumptions, attains a desired sublinear regret while safeguarding the differential privacy of each node’s loss function. We uncover the intricate balance between privacy preservation and optimization precision inherent in our algorithm. Through extensive simulations, we validate the efficacy of ZO-DDA. Looking ahead, we aim to enhance the communication and computational efficiency of our approach. Moreover, extending our findings to decentralized online optimization frameworks encompassing generalized (inequality or equality) constraints presents an intriguing direction for future research.
