# 2.4.3 Main results

To promote clarity and readability, we opt to substitute \(a_1\), \(a_2\), and \(a_3\) with \(\|J\|\), \(\|W\|\), and \(\|W - I_n\|\), respectively.

**Lemma 2.9.** Subject to the conditions of Assumptions 2.1–2.3, and provided that \(0 < \mathbf{1}_n^T \text{diag}(\alpha) \mathbf{1}_n < 2n\mu\), the subsequent linear inequality stands:

```
\(\mathbf{v}^{t+1} \preceq \mathbf{G}_{\hat{\alpha}, \beta} \mathbf{v}^t + \mathbf{b}^t,\)
(2.16)
```

where \(\mathbf{v}^t, \mathbf{b}^t \in \mathbb{R}^5\), \(\mathbf{G}_{\hat{\alpha}, \beta} \in \mathbb{R}^{5 \times 5}\), are outlined in the following manner:

```
\(\mathbf{v}^t = \begin{bmatrix} \|\theta^t - J^0\| \\ \|J\mathbf{J}^t - \mathbf{1}_n \otimes \theta^*\| \\ \|\theta^t - \theta^{t-1}\| \\ \|\mathbf{z}^t - J\mathbf{z}^t\| \\ \|\mathbf{z}^t - \mathbf{z}^{t-1}\| \end{bmatrix}, \quad \mathbf{b}^t = \begin{bmatrix} 3\beta \|\tilde{\xi}_\theta^t\| \\ 0 \\ 3\beta \|\tilde{\xi}_\theta^t\| \\ 3\beta \|\hat{\xi}\|\hat{\xi}\| + \|\tilde{\xi}_z^t\| \\ 3\beta \|\hat{\xi}\|\hat{\xi}\| + \|\tilde{\xi}_z^t\| \end{bmatrix},\)
```

```
\(\mathbf{G}_{\hat{\alpha}, \beta} = \begin{bmatrix} \sigma + \hat{\alpha}\hat{g}\sqrt{n} & \hat{\alpha}\hat{g}\sqrt{n} & \beta\sigma & \hat{\alpha} & \hat{\alpha}|s|\beta \\ \hat{\alpha}\hat{g} & \lambda & \beta a_1 & \hat{\alpha}a_1 & \hat{\alpha}|s|\beta a_1 \\ a_3 + \hat{\alpha}\hat{g}\sqrt{n} & \hat{\alpha}\hat{g}\sqrt{n} & \beta a_2 & \hat{\alpha} & \hat{\alpha}|s|\beta \\ \hat{g}(a_3 + \hat{\alpha}\hat{g}\sqrt{n}) & \hat{\alpha}\hat{g}^2\sqrt{n} & \beta\hat{g}a_2 & \sigma + \hat{\alpha}\hat{g} & \hat{\alpha}|s|\beta\hat{g} \\ \hat{g}(a_3 + \hat{\alpha}\hat{g}\sqrt{n}) & \hat{\alpha}\hat{g}^2\sqrt{n} & \beta\hat{g}a_2 & a_3 + \hat{\alpha}\hat{g} & \hat{\alpha}|s|\beta\hat{g} \end{bmatrix},\)
```

where \(\lambda = \max\{ |1 - \frac{\mathbf{1}_n^T \text{diag}(\alpha) \mathbf{1}_n}{nL}|, |1 - \frac{\mathbf{1}_n^T \text{diag}(\alpha) \mathbf{1}_n}{n\mu}| \}\). Let \(C = \hat{g}\sqrt{n}\gamma_1 + \hat{g}\sqrt{n}\gamma_2 + \hat{g}a_3\), where \(\gamma_1 = \frac{1-\sigma}{2}\), \(\gamma_2 = (\hat{g}(1-\sigma) + 2\hat{g}a_1a_3)L\).

_Proof._ Utilizing the results from Lemmas 2.4–2.8, deriving the linear inequality (2.16) becomes a straightforward task. \(\square\)

Drawing upon Lemmas 2.4–2.9, we proceed to delve into the rate of convergence of ET-PDA, as articulated in Theorem 2.1.

**Theorem 2.1.** Subject to the conditions of Assumptions 2.1–2.3, provided that the maximum step-size, denoted as \(\hat{\alpha}\), satisfies

```
\(0 < \hat{\alpha} < \min \left\{ \mu, \frac{\gamma_1(1 - \sigma)}{C}, \frac{a_3(1 - \sigma)}{2C} \right\}\)
(2.17)
```

and the momentum coefficient, \(\beta\), meets the requisite conditions

```
\(0 < \beta < \min \left\{ \frac{\gamma_1(1 - \sigma) - C\hat{\alpha}}{(\gamma_3\sigma + \gamma_5\hat{\alpha}|s|)}, \frac{a_3(1 - \sigma) - 2C\hat{\alpha}}{2(\gamma_3a_2 + \gamma_5\hat{\alpha}|s|)}, \frac{(2a_1a_3 + (1-\sigma))\hat{g}\hat{\alpha}}{2a_1(\gamma_3 + \gamma_5\hat{\alpha}|s|)} \right\},\)
(2.18)
```

where \(\gamma_3 = a_3\), \(\gamma_5 = (1+a_3)\hat{g}a_3\), subsequently, the spectral radius \(\rho(\mathbf{G}_{\hat{\alpha}, \beta})\) is less than 1, and the sequence \(\{\theta^t\}\) exhibits linear convergence towards \(\mathbf{1}_n \otimes \theta^*\) at a rate of \(O(\rho(\mathbf{G}_{\hat{\alpha}, \beta})^t)\).
