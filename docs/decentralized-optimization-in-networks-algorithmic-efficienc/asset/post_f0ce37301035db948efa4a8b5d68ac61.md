
## 8.3 Convergence guarantees

In this section, we analyze Algorithm 1 theoretically.

### 8.3.1 Auxiliary results

This subsection presents some auxiliary results relevant to directed networks, step-sizes, and the supermartingale convergence result.

**Lemma 8.1.** _[38] Let Assumption 8.2 hold and consider Algorithm 1. For any $t \ge 0$, there exist a normalized vector $c$ ($c^{\mathrm{T}}1_m = 1$), a constant $W > 0$, and a constant $0 < \lambda < 1$ satisfying:_

- a) $\lim_{t \to \infty} (B)^t = 1_m c^{\mathrm{T}}, |[z_i^t]_i - c_i| \le W (\lambda)^t, |[(B)^t]_{ji} - c_i| \le W (\lambda)^t, \forall i, j \in \mathcal{V};$
- b) there exists $\kappa > 1$ satisfying $\kappa^{-1} \le [z_i^t]_i \le 1, \forall i \in \mathcal{V}.$

_Proof._ The detailed proof is available in [38], thus it is omitted here.

Let the scalar $q$ satisfy $0 < q < 1/2$, and recall that $\gamma_i^t = 1 / \Gamma_i^t, \forall t \ge 1, i \in \mathcal{V}$. The conditions on the step-size $\gamma_i^t$ are established in the lemma below.

**Lemma 8.2.** _[43] For all $i \in \mathcal{V}$ and $t \ge \hat{t}$, where $\hat{t} = \hat{t}(q, m)$ is a sufficiently large positive integer, the step-size $\gamma_i^t$ satisfies a.s._

$$
\gamma_i^t \le \frac{2}{t\hat{\eta}}, \quad (\gamma_i^t)^2 \le \frac{4m^2}{t^2\hat{\eta}^2}, \quad |\gamma_i^t - 1/(t \eta_i)| \le \frac{2}{t^{3/2-q}\hat{\eta}^2} = \phi^t.
$$

_Proof._ The detailed proof is available in [43], thus it is omitted here.

Next, we present the well-known supermartingale convergence result [44] in the following lemma.

**Lemma 8.3.** _[44] Suppose the sequence of $\mathcal{F}^t$-measurable non-negative random variables $\{v^t\}, \{u^t\}, \{a^t\},$ and $\{b^t\}$ satisfy that for all $t \ge 0$._

$$
\mathbb{E}[v^{t+1}|\mathcal{F}^t] \le (1+a^t)v^t - u^t + b^t, \text{a.s.},
$$

where $\mathcal{F}^t$ is the collection of $v^0, \dots, v^t, u^0, \dots, u^t, a^0, \dots, a^t,$ and $b^0, \dots, b^t$. Assuming $\sum_{t=0}^\infty a^t < \infty$ and $\sum_{t=0}^\infty b^t < \infty$ a.s. hold, then, we have $\lim_{t \to \infty} v^t = v$ for a random variable $v \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ a.s., and $\sum_{t=0}^\infty u^t < \infty$ a.s.

_Proof._ The concrete proof can be found in [44], and therefore we omit it here.

### 8.3.2 Main results

We begin by demonstrating the asymptotic consensus of the proposed algorithm as stated in the following lemma.

**Lemma 8.4.** (Asymptotic consensus). Denote \(\bar{y}^t = \sum_{i=1}^m c_i y_i^t\) and consider Algorithm 1. Under Assumption 8.2, we obtain

$$
\lim_{t \to \infty} |y_i^t - \bar{y}^t| = 0, \text{ a.s., } \forall i \in \mathcal{V}.
$$

Proof. See Appendix 8.A.

Next, we present a crucial lemma that underpins the main results of this chapter.

**Lemma 8.5.** Consider Algorithm 1. Given Assumptions 8.1 and 8.2, and if \(0 < q < 1/2\), then, for all \(i \in \mathcal{V}\), \(v \in \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}\), and \(t \ge \hat{t}\), where \(\hat{t} = \hat{t}(q, m)\) is a sufficiently large positive integer, the following inequality holds:

$$
\sum_{i=1}^m c_i \mathbb{E} \left[ |y_i^{t+1} - v|^2 | \mathcal{F}^t \right] \le (1 + \phi^t \kappa) \sum_{i=1}^m c_i |y_i^t - v|^2 + \frac{2}{t} (Q(\bar{y}^t) - Q(v)) + \sum_{i=1}^m |\epsilon_i^t|^2 + C^2 \kappa^2 \hat{\eta} \sum_{i=1}^m |\gamma_i^t|^2 + 2C \kappa \hat{\eta} \sum_{i=1}^m \epsilon_i^t \gamma_i^t + 2 \sum_{i=1}^m |\epsilon_i^t| |v_i^t - v| + \frac{2C\kappa}{t} \sum_{i=1}^m |y_i^t - \bar{y}^t|^2 + \frac{2mk CW(\lambda)^t}{t} |\bar{y}^t - v| + \phi^t \kappa C^2 \hat{\eta}.
$$

Proof. See Appendix 8.B.

Utilizing the results from Lemmas 8.1 and 8.2, we establish the convergence of the proposed algorithm in the following theorem.

**Theorem 8.1.** Consider Algorithm 1. Under Assumptions 8.1 and 8.2, and if \(0 < q < 1/2\), then we have \(\lim_{t \to \infty} y_i^t = y^*\) a.s. and \(\lim_{t \to \infty} p_i^t = p_i^*\) a.s. for all \(i \in \mathcal{V}\), \(t \ge \hat{t}\), where \(\hat{t} = \hat{t}(q, m)\) is a sufficiently large positive integer.

Proof. Let \(v = y^*\) in Lemma 8.5. For any \(t \ge \hat{t}\), we have

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{i=1}^m c_i \mathbb{E} \left[ |y_i^{t+1} - y^*|^2 | \mathcal{F}^t \right] \le {}& (1 + \phi^t \kappa) \sum_{i=1}^m c_i |y_i^t - y^*|^2 + \frac{2}{t} (Q(\bar{y}^t) - Q(y^*)) \\
&+ C^2 \kappa^2 \hat{\eta} \sum_{i=1}^m |\gamma_i^t|^2 + 2 C \kappa \hat{\eta} \xi^t \sum_{i=1}^m \gamma_i^t + m (\hat{\xi}^t)^2 \\
&+ (2 \hat{\xi}^t + \frac{2 C \kappa}{t}) \sum_{i=1}^m |y_i^t - \bar{y}^t|^2 + \phi^t \kappa C^2 \hat{\eta} \\
&+ (\frac{2 m \kappa C W(\lambda)^t}{t} + 2 m \hat{\varepsilon}^t) |\bar{y}^t - y^*|.
\end{aligned}
$$

For simplicity, define

$$
v^t = \sum_{i=1}^m c_i |y_i^t - y^*|^2, \quad u^t = -\frac{2}{t}(Q(\bar{y}^t) - Q(y^*)), \quad a^t = \phi^t \kappa,
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
b^t = {}& C^2 \kappa^2 \hat{\eta} \sum_{i=1}^m |\gamma_i^t|^2 + \left(\frac{2 m \kappa C W(\lambda)^t}{t} + 2 m \hat{\varepsilon}^t\right) |\bar{y}^t - y^*| \\
&+ \left(2 \hat{\xi}^t + \frac{2 C \kappa}{t}\right) \sum_{i=1}^m |y_i^t - \bar{y}^t|^2 + 2 C \kappa \hat{\eta} \xi^t \sum_{i=1}^m \gamma_i^t \\
&+ \phi^t \kappa C^2 \hat{\eta} + m (\hat{\varepsilon}^t)^2.
\end{aligned}
$$

Then, (8.11) can be simply rewritten as \(\mathbb{E}[v^{t+1} | \mathcal{F}^t] \le (1 + a^t) v^t - u^t + b^t\), for all \(t \ge \hat{t}\). To apply Lemma 8.5 effectively, we need to verify that \(\sum_{t=0}^\infty a^t < \infty\) and \(\sum_{t=0}^\infty b^t < \infty\). Noting that \(0 < q < 1/2\), we have \(1 < (3/2 - q) < 3/2\). Thus we can further deduce that

$$
\sum_{t=0}^\infty a^t \le \frac{2 \kappa}{\hat{\eta}^2} \sum_{t=0}^\infty \frac{1}{t^{3/2-q}} < \infty.
$$

By Lemma 8.4, it follows from Proposition 3b in [38] that

$$
\sum_{t=0}^\infty (2 \hat{\xi}^t + \frac{2 C \kappa}{t}) \sum_{i=1}^m |y_i^t - \bar{y}^t|^2 < \infty.
$$

Next, if \(\hat{h}_i^t = 0, i \in \mathcal{V}\), we have that \(|\bar{y}^t - y^*| \le |\bar{y}^0 - y^*| + \sum_{l=0}^{t-1} \hat{\varepsilon}^l\). If \(\hat{h}_i^t = 1, i \in \mathcal{V}\), by Lemma 8.2, it is deduced that \(|\bar{y}^t - y^*| \le |\bar{y}^0 - y^*| + \sum_{l=0}^{t-1} \hat{\varepsilon}^l + 2 m C \kappa \ln(1+t) / \hat{\eta}\). Since \(\lim_{t \to \infty} \hat{\varepsilon}^t / (t^2) = 0\) and by Lemma 8.2, we obtain

$$
\sum_{t=0}^\infty (\frac{2 m \kappa C W(\lambda)^t}{t} + 2 m \hat{\varepsilon}^t) |\bar{y}^t - y^*| < \infty.
$$

Thus one has $\sum_{t=0}^\infty b^t < \infty$. All conditions of Lemma 8.3 are satisfied, so by Lemma 8.3, we obtain

$$
\lim_{t \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^m c_i |y_i^t - y^*|^2 = \theta \ge 0, \quad a.s., \tag{8.15}
$$

$$
\sum_{t=0}^\infty \frac{2}{t} (Q(\bar{y}^t) - Q(y^*)) < \infty, \quad a.s.. \tag{8.16}
$$

Due to $\sum_{t=0}^\infty (2/t) = \infty$, we further obtain

$$
\liminf_{t \to \infty} -Q(\bar{y}^t) = -Q(y^*), \quad a.s.. \tag{8.17}
$$

By the continuity of $-Q$, we have that the sequence $\{\bar{y}^t\}$ converges to $y^*$ a.s., i.e., $\lim_{t \to \infty} |\bar{y}^t - y^*| = 0$ a.s.. Now, it remains to show that $\theta = 0$, which by (8.15) will complete the proof. By the Cauchy–Schwarz inequality, we have

$$
\sum_{i=1}^m c_i |y_i^t - y^*|^2 \le 2 \sum_{i=1}^m c_i |y_i^t - \bar{y}^t|^2 + 2|\bar{y}^t - y^*|^2. \tag{8.18}
$$

Since $\lim_{t \to \infty} |y_i^t - \bar{y}^t| = 0$ a.s. and $\lim_{t \to \infty} |\bar{y}^t - y^*| = 0$ a.s., we achieve $\theta = \lim_{t \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^m c_i |y_i^t - y^*|^2 = 0$ a.s.. Then, from the strong duality between (8.1) and (8.3), it follows from (8.9) that $\lim_{t \to \infty} p_i^t = p_i^*$ a.s., $\forall i \in \mathcal{V}$. This completes the proof.

**Remark 8.4** (Effects of the Gradient Rescaling Technique). Theorem 8.1 shows that the algorithm we propose can almost surely reach the optimal solution of the EDP. This outcome, confirmed through simulation, indicates that the gradient rescaling method we use effectively handles the imbalance in directed networks. Without this technique, the algorithm would only converge to a vicinity of the optimal solution, as demonstrated in [62].

Denote $\hat{y}_i^t = (1/\alpha^t) \sum_{s=1}^t (y_i^s / s)$, $\forall i \in \mathcal{V}$, where $\alpha^t = \sum_{s=1}^t (1/s)$. We now provide the following error-bound analysis.

**Corollary 8.1.** Assuming the conditions of Theorem 8.1, we have

$$
\mathbb{E}[-Q(\hat{\mathcal{Y}}_i^t) + Q(y^*)] \le \frac{\Xi^t}{\alpha^t},
$$

where

$$
\Xi^t = \exp(\sum_{s=1}^t \phi^s \kappa) \cdot \mathbb{E}[\sum_{i=1}^m c_i |y_i^1 - y^*|^2] + C \mathbb{E}[\sum_{s=1}^t \frac{|y_i^s - \bar{y}^s|}{s}] + \exp(\sum_{s=1}^t \phi^s \kappa) \cdot \mathbb{E}[\sum_{s=1}^t b^s] < \infty.
$$
