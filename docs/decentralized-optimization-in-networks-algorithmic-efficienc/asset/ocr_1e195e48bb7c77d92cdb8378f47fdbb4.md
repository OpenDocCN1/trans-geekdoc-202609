# ### 3.1.2 Main contributions

In [26], the authors presented a decentralized algorithm, GT-CDGD, boasting a linear convergence rate, tailored for addressing decentralized optimization problems constrained within a closed convex set. Aiming to achieve elevated computational efficiency when dealing with extensive datasets, we leverage the powerful computational efficiency of the variance-reduction method to devise an innovative algorithm, namely P-DVR. This advanced algorithm offers both a linear convergence rate and remarkable computational efficiency. Our specific contributions are outlined below:

+   (i) A novel and innovative algorithm, termed as P-DVR, is introduced for addressing decentralized constrained optimization challenges involving closed convex set constraints. This P-DVR algorithm, which incorporates projected decentralized variance-reduction techniques, is specifically designed to tackle constrained optimization issues. Given a small enough constant step-size, the P-DVR demonstrates linear convergence towards the optimal solution, particularly when dealing with strongly convex and smooth local functions.

+   (ii) Distinct from the method presented in [26], known as GT-CDGD, the P-DVR algorithm possesses superior computational efficiency due to its incorporation of a variance-reduction method. In contrast to the GT-CDGD method, which lacks this variance-reduction technique, the P-DVR algorithm can theoretically boost computational efficiency per iteration by a factor of _n_, where _n_ represents the number of data items stored in each node.

+   (iii) Through numerical analysis, we validate the linear convergence of the P-DVR algorithm, exceptional computational efficiency, and overall efficacy by applying it to solve two distinct problems: a logistic regression-driven binary classification problem and an energy-based problem of acoustic source localization.
