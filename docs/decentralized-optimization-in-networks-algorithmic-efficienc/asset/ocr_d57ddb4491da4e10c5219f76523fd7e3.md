# # Preface

Along with the rapid development of information technology, human beings have moved from the information technology era to the data technology era, and gradually entered the ternary world of “human–machine–object” integration. In particular, with the integration of data-driven intelligent devices into the physical scene, the interaction of information acquisition, transmission and processing constitutes a complex multidimensional system, i.e., the networked control system, that deeply integrates communication, control, and optimization. This type of system is composed of many intelligent devices that collaborate with each other to efficiently complete the assigned tasks, thus solving large-scale complex problems that are difficult for a single device to handle. Currently, networked control systems have been enriched in many fields and brought great convenience to the life of human beings. With the advancement of wireless communication technology, microelectronics and microelectromechanical technology, and integrated perceptual computing system, the application scenarios of networked control systems are constantly expanding. With the advancement of wireless communication technologies and integrated perceptual computing systems, the application scenarios of networked control systems are constantly expanding. As with the rapid increase in computing power and storage capacity of smart devices, it has become a trend to deploy advanced optimization algorithms on decentralized smart devices in networked control systems. A typical example is the decentralized optimization framework: multiple smart devices in a network collaborate to solve a global optimization task through local computation and local communication. Decentralized optimization frameworks are more suitable for large-scale networked control systems because of their advantages over centralized optimization frameworks in terms of scalability, robustness, and privacy. As a hot research topic spanning multiple disciplines with wide applicability and great application value, decentralized optimization in networks lays an important foundation for promoting and leading the cutting-edge development of computer science, artificial intelligence, automatic control and other fields. As an emerging research hotspot and an important research field, decentralized optimization in networks has achieved substantial and meaningful research results in terms of algorithmic efficiency and privacy preservation, respectively, thereby providing effective approaches for solving related optimization problems. However, for the research of actual more complex optimization problems and higher algorithmic demands, the related theories and methods still face serious challenges. Hence, development of new theories and methods for efficient and security decentralized optimization in networks is urgently needed.
