
Let \(\bar{p}^t = \sum_{i=1}^n \pi_i p_i^t\), where \(\pi_i, i \in \mathcal{V}\), satisfies Lemma 1.1. Subsequently, the following lemma is also crucial.

**Lemma 1.4** (Asymptotic consensus). [32] Given that Assumption 1.2 holds, we turn our attention to the sequence \(p_i^t = \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t p_j^{t-1} + \xi_i^t, \forall i \in \mathcal{V}\). If \(\lim_{t \to \infty} \|\xi_i^t\| = 0\) a.s., then \(\lim_{t \to \infty} \|p_i^t - \bar{p}^t\| = 0\) a.s..

### 1.4.2 Main results

In this subsection, we establish a fundamental lemma that underpins our primary conclusions. For brevity in notation, we introduce \(\mathbb{E} [\cdot | \mathcal{F}^t] = \mathbb{E}_t, \forall t \ge 1\), in the subsequent analysis. Furthermore, we designate \(\eta_{\min} = \min_{i \in \mathcal{V}} \{\eta_i\}\) and \(\bar{\phi}^t = \sum_{i=1}^n \pi_i \phi_i^t\), where \(\phi_i^t = P_{\Xi_0}(\phi_i^t)\) and \(P_{\Xi_0}(\phi_i^t)\) is defined as the projection of \(\phi_i^t\) onto the convex set \(\Xi\). Notably, we denote \(\phi_i^t = \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t p_j^{t-1}\) for all \(t\).

**Lemma 1.5.** Assume that Assumptions 1.1 and 1.2 hold and consider algorithm (1.17). If \(0 < \epsilon < 1/2\), we can ascertain the existence of a threshold \(\hat{t} = \hat{t} (\epsilon, n)\) (large enough) such that for all \(t \ge \hat{t}\), \(v \in \Xi_0\) and \(i \in \mathcal{V}\), the following holds:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\sum_{i=1}^n \pi_i \mathbb{E}_{t-1} \left[ \| p_i^t - v \|^2 \right]
\le {}& (1 + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}}) \sum_{i=1}^n \pi_i \| p_i^{t-1} - v \|^2 - \frac{2}{t} \mathbb{E}_{t-1} \left[ b^{\text{T}} (\bar{\phi}^t - v) \right] \\
& - \eta_{\min} \beta \mathbb{E}_{t-1} \left[ \sum_{i=1}^n \pi_i \| \phi_i^t - \bar{\phi}^t \|^2 \right] \\
& + (\frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} + \frac{\alpha_2}{t^2} (1 + 4\nu + \tau)) \| b \|^2,
\end{aligned}
\]

where \(\tau > 1\) and \(\nu > \frac{\delta \tau D^2}{4(\tau - 1)}\) are two adjustable parameters for certain \(\delta > 0\), and \(\beta = \frac{\tau - 1}{\delta \tau D^2} - \frac{1}{4\nu} > 0\).

Proof.

- (i) \(i \in \{i^t, j^t\}\): Initially, it can be inferred from (1.17a) that for any \(v \in \Xi_0\) and \(i \in \{i^t, j^t\}\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\| p_i^t - v \|^2
&= \| h_i^t - \frac{[f_i (h_i^t)]_+}{\| \xi_i^t \|^2} \xi_i^t - v \|^2 \\
&= \| h_i^t - v \|^2 + \| \frac{[f_i (h_i^t)]_+}{\| \xi_i^t \|^2} \xi_i^t \|^2 - 2 \frac{[f_i (h_i^t)]_+}{\| \xi_i^t \|^2} (h_i^t - v)^{\text{T}} \xi_i^t
\end{aligned}
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
\le \|h_i^t - v\|^2 + \|\frac{[f_i(h_i^t)]_+}{\|\xi_i^t\|^2}\xi_i^t\|^2 - 2\frac{[f_i(h_i^t)]_+}{\|\xi_i^t\|^2} [f_i(h_i^t)]_+ \\
= \|h_i^t - v\|^2 - \frac{\|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+\|^2}{\|\xi_i^t\|^2}, \tag{1.18}
\end{aligned}
\]

where the inequality follows from the conditions that \([f_i(v)]_+ = 0\) and \((h_i^t - v)^T \xi_i^t \ge f_i(h_i^t) - f_i(v)\). Given that \(h_i^t = \varphi_i^t - \alpha_i^t b\) for \(i \in \{i^t, j^t\}\), where \(\varphi_i^t = \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t p_j^{t-1}\), we are able to deduce that

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|h_i^t - v\|^2 &= \|\varphi_i^t - \alpha_i^t b - v\|^2 \\
&= \|\varphi_i^t - v\|^2 - 2\alpha_i^t (b^T \varphi_i^t - b^T \phi_i + b^T \phi_i - b^T v) \\
&\quad + (\alpha_i^t)^2 \|b\|^2, \tag{1.19}
\end{aligned}
\]

for any \(\phi_i \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1}\), where \(\alpha_i^t = 1 / \Gamma_i^t\) for all \(i\) and \(t\). Note that \(b^T \varphi_i^t - b^T \phi_i + b^T \phi_i - b^T v \ge -\|b\| \|\varphi_i^t - \phi_i\| + b^T \phi_i - b^T v\), then we have

\[
\begin{aligned}
-2\alpha_i^t (b^T \varphi_i^t - b^T \phi_i + b^T \phi_i - b^T v)
&\le 2\alpha_i^t \|b\| \|\varphi_i^t - \phi_i\| - 2\alpha_i^t (b^T \phi_i - b^T v) \\
&\le 4\nu (\alpha_i^t)^2 \|b\|^2 + \frac{1}{4\nu} \|\varphi_i^t - \phi_i\|^2 - 2\alpha_i^t (b^T \phi_i - b^T v), \tag{1.20}
\end{aligned}
\]

where the second inequality is due to the fact that \(2\alpha_i^t \|b\| \|\varphi_i^t - \phi_i\| \le 4\nu (\alpha_i^t)^2 \|b\|^2 + (1/4\nu) \|\varphi_i^t - \phi_i\|^2\) for any \(\nu > 0\). Subsequently, utilizing both (1.18) and (1.20), we are able to deduce the following:

\[
\|p_i^t - v\|^2 \le \|\varphi_i^t - v\|^2 - 2\alpha_i^t (b^T \phi_i - b^T v) + \frac{1}{4\nu} \|\varphi_i^t - \phi_i\|^2
- \frac{\|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+\|^2}{\|\xi_i^t\|^2} + (\alpha_i^t)^2 (1 + 4\nu) \|b\|^2. \tag{1.21}
\]

Now, we attend to the step-size \(\alpha_i^t\) present in the aforementioned formula. Drawing upon Lemma 1.2, we arrive at the following conclusion:

\[
\begin{aligned}
-2\alpha_i^t b^T (\phi_i - v)
&= -2(\alpha_i^t - \frac{1}{t \eta_i} + \frac{1}{t \eta_i}) b^T (\phi_i - v) \\
&\le - \frac{2}{t \eta_i} b^T (\phi_i - v) + |\alpha_i^t - \frac{1}{t \eta_i}| \|\phi_i - v\|^2 + |\alpha_i^t - \frac{1}{t \eta_i}| \|b\|^2 \\
&\le \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|\phi_i - v\|^2 + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|b\|^2 - \frac{2}{t \eta_i} b^T (\phi_i - v). \tag{1.22}
\end{aligned}
\]

By incorporating (1.22) into (1.21), it yields that

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|p_i^t - v\|^2 \leq {}& \|\varphi_i^t - v\|^2 - \frac{\|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+\|^2}{\|\xi_i^t\|^2} - \frac{2}{t\eta_i} b^T(\phi_i - v) \\
& + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|\phi_i - v\|^2 + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|b\|^2 \\
& + \frac{1}{4\nu} \|\varphi_i^t - \phi_i\|^2 + (\alpha_i^t)^2(1+4\nu)\|b\|^2. \tag{1.23}
\end{aligned}
\]

Observing the convexity of \(\|\cdot\|^2\), we note that \(\|\varphi_i^t - v\|^2 = \|\sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2 = \|\sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t(p_j^{t-1} - v)\|^2 \leq \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t \|p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2\). Now, introducing the notation \(\phi_i = \varphi_i^t = P_{\Xi_0}(\varphi_i^t)\), where \(P_{\Xi_0}(\varphi_i^t)\) represents the projection of \(\varphi_i^t\) onto the convex set \(\Xi\), we can directly obtain

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|p_i^t - v\|^2
\leq {}& \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t \|p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2 - \frac{\|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+\|^2}{\|\xi_i^t\|^2} - \frac{2}{t\eta_i} b^T(\varphi_i^t - v) \\
& + (\alpha_i^t)^2(1+4\nu)\|b\|^2 + \frac{1}{4\nu} \|\varphi_i^t - \phi_i\|^2 \\
& + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|\phi_i^t - v\|^2 + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|b\|^2. \tag{1.24}
\end{aligned}
\]

Then, for the term \(\|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+\|^2\), it yields that

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+\|^2
&= \|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+ - [f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+ + [f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\|^2 \\
&\geq \|[f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\|^2 - 2|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+ - [f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+|\|[f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\| \\
&\geq \|[f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\|^2 - 2D\alpha_i^t \|b\|\|[f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\| \\
&\geq (1 - \frac{1}{\tau})\|[f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\|^2 - \tau D^2(\alpha_i^t)^2\|b\|^2, \tag{1.25}
\end{aligned}
\]

where the second and third inequalities follow from \(|[f_i(h_i^t)]_+ - [f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+| \leq D|h_i^t - \varphi_i^t|\) and \(2D\alpha_i^t\|b\|\|[f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\| \leq \tau D^2(\alpha_i^t)^2\|b\|^2 + (1/\tau)\|[f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\|^2\), respectively. Here, \(\tau\) represents a tunable parameter with a value greater than 1. Given the fact that \(\|\xi_i^t\| \leq D\) for all \(i \in \mathcal{V}\), we can deduce from (1.24) that

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|p_i^t - v\|^2
\leq {}& \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t \|p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2 - \frac{(\tau - 1)}{\tau D^2}\|[f_i(\varphi_i^t)]_+\|^2 \\
& - \frac{2}{t\eta_i} b^T(\varphi_i^t - v) + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|\phi_i^t - v\|^2 + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|b\|^2 \\
& + \frac{1}{4\nu} \|\phi_i^t - \bar{\phi}^t\|^2 + \frac{\alpha_2(1 + 4\nu + \tau)\|b\|^2}{t^2}. \tag{1.26}
\end{aligned}
\]

According to [42], there exists a finite positive constant \(\delta\) such that for any \(p \in \mathbb{S}_0\), the inequality \(\text{dist}^2(p, \mathbb{S}_0) \le \delta \|[f_i(p)]_+\|^2\) holds true. Based on this, we can further arrive at

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|p_i^t - v\|^2 \\
\le \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t \|p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2 - \frac{2}{t \eta_i} b^T (\phi_i^t - v) + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|b\|^2 \\
+ \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} \|\phi_i^t - v\|^2 + \frac{\alpha_2(1+4\nu+\tau)\|b\|^2}{t^2} \\
- \beta \|\phi_i^t - \bar{\phi}^t\|^2, \tag{1.27}
\end{aligned}
\]

where \(\beta\) is defined as \(\frac{\tau-1}{\delta \tau D^2} - \frac{1}{4\nu}\), and we assume that \(\nu\) satisfies that \(\nu > \frac{\delta \tau D^2}{4(\tau-1)}\) to ensure \(\beta > 0\). Due to \(v \in \mathbb{S}_0\), we can further obtain that \(\|\phi_i^t - v\|^2 = \|\sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t (p_j^{t-1} - v)\|^2 \le \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t \|p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2\). Consequently, we have that

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|p_i^t - v\|^2 + \beta \|\phi_i^t - \bar{\phi}^t\|^2 \\
\le (1 + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}}) \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t \|p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2 - \frac{2}{t \eta_i} b^T (\phi_i^t - v) \\
+ \frac{\alpha_3 \|b\|^2}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}} + \frac{\alpha_2(1+4\nu+\tau)\|b\|^2}{t^2}. \tag{1.28}
\end{aligned}
\]

- (ii) \(i \notin \{i^t, j^t\}\): Utilizing (1.17b), we can deduce that for any \(v \in \mathbb{S}_0\) and \(i \notin \{i^t, j^t\}\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|p_i^t - v\|^2 = \|\sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2 \\
\le (1 + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}}) \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t \|p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2. \tag{1.29}
\end{aligned}
\]

By integrating the two outcomes (1.28) and (1.29) and subsequently taking the conditional expectation with respect to \(\mathcal{F}^{t-1}\), it yields that

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{t-1} [ \|p_i^t - v\|^2 ] \\
\le (1 + \frac{\alpha_3}{t^{3/2-\epsilon}}) \mathbb{E}_{t-1} \left[ \sum_{j=1}^n W_{ij}^t \|p_j^{t-1} - v\|^2 \right].
\end{aligned}
\]
