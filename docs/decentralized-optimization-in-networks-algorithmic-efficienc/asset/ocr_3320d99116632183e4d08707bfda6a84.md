# ## 2.1.2 Statement of contributions

Inspired by the works of [39–44], this chapter proposes a decentralized accelerated algorithm with event-triggered parameter updates, referred to as ET-PDA. The main contributions of this chapter are summarized as:

+   (i) ET-PDA does not adhere to a single momentum strategy mentioned in [30–35]. Rather, it embraces a diverse array of momentum methods, tailored to specific parameter settings. Consequently, the momentum approach adopted varies in accordance with the parameter values, allowing each generator to garner greater insight from its neighbors, ultimately speeding up the convergence rate.

+   (ii) Given the significant costs and resources expended in continuous communication among generators, as documented in [11–20], ET-PDA employs an event-triggered approach instead of a time-triggered one [36–38]. This approach facilitates interaction between generators and their neighbors only when specific events occur, thus eliminating the need for real-time communication and ultimately enhancing overall efficiency.

+   (iii) Distinct from the centralized updating approach of the Lagrangian multiplier employed in [45], ET-PDA adopts a decentralized strategy. Assuming the strong convexity and smoothness of the local cost function are upheld, ET-PDA can attain a linear convergence rate, provided that the maximum step-size and momentum coefficient adhere to specific criteria. Additionally, ET-PDA mitigates the emergence of Zeno-like behavior.
