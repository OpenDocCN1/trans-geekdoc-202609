## 1.7 Conclusion

In this chapter, we introduce two innovative decentralized asynchronous methodologies aimed at resolving resource allocation challenges across directed networks. To mitigate the imbalance arising from directed networks in the dual-related domain, we adopt a fresh perspective by leveraging the epigraph approach. Additionally, we enhance communication efficiency by incorporating the gossip-based protocol and the random sleep protocol into our proposed algorithms. Our theoretical investigations confirm that these algorithms effectively allocate optimal resources for the given problem. Furthermore, we provide a theoretical analysis of the error bounds associated with these algorithms. To validate their performance, we conduct extensive simulations. Looking ahead, we intend to broaden the applicability of these algorithms to various real-world scenarios, encompassing random link failures, transmission losses, and latency. Additionally, exploring the integration of differential privacy strategies to safeguard information and prevent leakage represents a promising area for future research.
