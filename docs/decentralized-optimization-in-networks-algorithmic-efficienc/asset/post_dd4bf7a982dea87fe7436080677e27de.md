
By taking the expectation of the equation denoted by (3.23), we arrive at the subsequent result:

\(\mathbb{E}[\|y_{k+1} - A^\infty y_{k+1}\|^2]\)
\(\le \frac{1 + \sigma_2^2}{2}\mathbb{E}[\|Ay_k - A^\infty y_k\|^2] + \frac{2}{1 - \sigma_2^2}\mathbb{E}[\|g(s_{k+1}) - g(s_k)\|^2]. \quad (3.24)\)

To determine the limit of \(\mathbb{E}[\|y_{k+1} - A^\infty y_{k+1}\|^2]\), we must first set an upper limit for \(\mathbb{E}[\|g(s_{k+1}) - g(s_k)\|^2]\) as outlined below:

\(\mathbb{E}[\|g(s_{k+1}) - g(s_k)\|^2]\)
\(\le 2\mathbb{E}[\|g(s_{k+1}) - \nabla F(s_{k+1})\|^2] + 2\mathbb{E}[\|g(s_k) - \nabla F(s_k)\|^2]\)

By applying the updating principle expressed in (3.7a) and acknowledging that \(x^* = s^* - \eta \nabla f(s^*)\), we deduce that

\(- P_\Omega[1_m x^*]\|^2 + 4\|x_k - A^\infty x_k + A^\infty x_k - 1_m x^*\|^2\)

By utilizing the typical non-expansive characteristics of the projection operator, as denoted by (3.4), we can infer the subsequent conclusion from the inequality labeled as (3.26):

\(\|x_{k+1} - x_k\|^2\)
\(\le 4\|Ax_k - A^\infty x_k\|^2 + 4\|A^\infty x_k - 1_m x^*\|^2\)
\(+ 8\eta^2\|y_k - A^\infty y_k\|^2 + 8\eta^2\|A^\infty y_k - 1_m \nabla f(s^*)\|^2\)
\(+ 8\|x_k - A^\infty x_k\|^2 + 8\|A^\infty x_k - 1_m x^*\|^2. \quad (3.27)\)

\(\|x_{k+1} - x_k\|^2\)
\(\le (4\sigma_2^2 + 8)\|x_k - A^\infty x_k\|^2 + 12m\|\bar{x}_k - x^*\|^2\)
