# Privacy-preserving decentralized optimal economic dispatch algorithms with conditional noises

# 8.1 Introduction

EDP is a crucial and foundational issue in smart grid energy management [1]. The aim is to find the optimal power generation for all generators to minimize the total operational cost across the network while meeting load demands and adhering to each generator's capacity [2–5]. Consequently, EDP is essentially a constrained optimization problem extensively studied in recent years [6–8]. Many centralized methods have been developed to address EDP [8]. However, these centralized approaches are costly, susceptible to single points of failure, and lack robustness in new environments. With the integration of distributed generation equipment, energy storage, load equipment, and other components, modern smart grids are highly decentralized, making centralized EDP processing on a single central controller impractical [4]. Decentralized optimization control has been a classical topic, gaining renewed interest [9–11]. In decentralized optimization, each generator communicates only with its local in-neighbors over a connected network [11,12].

# 8.1.1 Literature review

Recently, considerable efforts have been devoted to developing decentralized solutions for EDP. The majority of decentralized discrete-time optimization methods for EDP in the literature rely on the consensus protocol [13–20]. For instance, a decentralized leader-following consensus algorithm was first proposed in [13] to solve EDP. However, the presence of a leader in [13] contradicts the core principle of full decentralization. To eliminate the leader role, a two-level incremental cost consensus algorithm and a fully decentralized algorithm using the “consensus+innovations” framework were proposed in [14] and [15], respectively. Furthermore, several efficient methods addressing real-world EDP factors such as transmission losses [16], private security [17], nonconvex conditions [18], supply–demand balance [19], and time delays [20] have emerged.
