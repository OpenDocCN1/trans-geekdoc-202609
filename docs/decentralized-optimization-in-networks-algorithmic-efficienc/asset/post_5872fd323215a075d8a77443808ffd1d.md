## 3.5.2 Decentralized energy-based source localization experiments

In this subsection, the issue of pinpointing the location of an acoustic source based on energy is tackled by P-DVR. Assuming there are _m_ sensors for localization scattered across a space, and a single acoustic source emitting energy, we can frame the problem of energy-driven acoustic source localization in this manner:


## 3.6 Conclusion

We have proposed a novel algorithm, named P-DVR, tailored for addressing complex decentralized optimization problems in extensive data environments. Our findings indicate that, with a suitably small and precise step-size, the P-DVR algorithm can converge linearly to the precise optimal solution when dealing with strongly convex and smooth local functions. The convergence speed follows a rate of \(\mathcal{O}(\hat{\lambda}^k)\), where \(0 < \hat{\lambda} < 1\). Through rigorous numerical simulations using various benchmark datasets, we have proven that our P-DVR algorithm exhibits remarkable computational efficiency and precise convergence. Nevertheless, it is important to note that P-DVR currently does not accommodate directed networks or those with compromised communication quality. Future explorations will focus on developing algorithms that can operate effectively within these networks. Additionally, we have plans to tackle multi-objective optimization problems using decentralized methods in our upcoming research.
