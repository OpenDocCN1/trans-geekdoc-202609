\sum_{t=1}^T \sum_{i=1}^n \langle \frac{\hat{\mathcal{G}}_t^i}{v_{t+1}^i}\theta_{t+1}^i + \vartheta_t^i\theta_t^i, \bar{x}_t \rangle

+ \frac{1}{2} \sum_{t=1}^T \alpha_{t-1}|| \sum_{i=1}^n (\frac{\hat{\mathcal{G}}_t^i}{v_{t+1}^i}\theta_{t+1}^i + \vartheta_t^i\theta_t^i) ||^2.\quad (6.29)
