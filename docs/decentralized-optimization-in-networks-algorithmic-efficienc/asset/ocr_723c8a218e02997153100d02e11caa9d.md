# 3.3 Algorithm construction

To decrease the margin of error introduced by the stochastic gradient algorithm, which can enhance computational efficiency, prior scholars have suggested the variance-reduction method (VR) [20–22]. To minimize the inaccuracy stemming from inadequate agreement among various nodes, past researchers have introduced a gradient tracking method (GT) [50,51]. Leveraging these two strategies, certain scholars have put forward the GT-VR algorithmic structure, noted for its high computational efficiency and precise linear convergence speed, particularly when the step-size is adequately small, as exemplified in [19]. To address optimization problems confined to a closed convex set, the researchers in [26] employed the projection operator to devise an algorithm called GT-CDGD. However, there is still considerable room for enhancing its computational efficiency. In order to enhance this efficiency, we integrate the projection operator with the variance-reduction method, presenting a new projected decentralized variance-reduction algorithm (P-DVR) tailored for constrained decentralized optimization problems. The iterative process at node i is exhibited below, with \(\eta > 0\) representing a fixed step-size:

```
\(x_{k+1}^i = P_{\Omega_0}[\sum_{j=1}^m a^{ij} x_k^j] - \eta y_k^i,\) (3.6a)

\(s_{k+1}^i = P_{\Omega_0}[x_{k+1}^i],\) (3.6b)

\(y_{k+1}^i = \sum_{j=1}^m a^{ij} y_k^j + g^i(s_{k+1}^i) - g^i(s_k^i).\) (3.6c)
```
