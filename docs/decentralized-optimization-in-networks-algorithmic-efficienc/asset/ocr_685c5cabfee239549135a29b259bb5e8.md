By leveraging the identity $(\pi^t)^\mathrm{T} = (\pi^{t+1})^\mathrm{T} \hat{W}^t$ as stated in Lemma 1.6 (iii), it suffices to demonstrate that

```
$$= \sum_{i=1}^n \pi_i^t || p_i^t - v ||^2 . \quad (1.48)$$
```

Then, we can establish the relationship $\sum_{i=1}^n \pi_i^{t+1} b^\mathrm{T} (\phi_i^t - v) = b^\mathrm{T} (\tilde{\phi}^t - v)$. By combining this result with Eqs.
