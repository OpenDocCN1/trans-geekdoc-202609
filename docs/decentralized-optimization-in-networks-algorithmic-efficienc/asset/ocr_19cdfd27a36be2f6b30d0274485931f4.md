+   (i) We develop and analyze a privacy-protected decentralized dual averaging push algorithm for general constrained optimization over time-varying directed networks. By using edge-based correlated perturbations, our algorithm surpasses differential-privacy-based decentralized algorithms [36,37] in convergence accuracy. Unlike related algorithms in [39,40] that use edge-based perturbations, our approach also leverages the push-sum averaging protocol to handle imbalances in time-varying directed networks. Additionally, compared to node-based perturbation methods [35,43], our algorithm offers more design flexibility with edge-based perturbations.

+   (ii) We provide a rigorous analysis of the convergence and privacy protection of our algorithm. It is demonstrated that our algorithm achieves the same \(\mathcal{O}(1/\sqrt{K})\) convergence rate (where \(K\) is the number of iterations) as those without privacy protection [25–28], under more relaxed initial conditions. This makes our approach more general than existing methods [25,26,28], which require zero initial conditions. Our algorithm thus does not necessitate the coordination of initial state estimates among network nodes, enhancing robustness and flexibility.

+   (iii) Our algorithm ensures that malicious nodes cannot deduce private information from their observations, thereby protecting node privacy. To the best of our knowledge, this is a novel decentralized algorithm that not only safeguards the privacy of normal nodes but also accurately solves a generally constrained optimization problem over time-varying directed networks under weaker initial conditions. Numerical examples further corroborate the performance of our algorithm.
