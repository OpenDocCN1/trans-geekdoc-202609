## 8.5.2 Example 2: IEEE 118-bus system

In this scenario, the proposed algorithm is employed to solve the Economic Dispatch Problem (EDP) on the IEEE 118-bus system [46] to further assess its performance. The IEEE 118-bus system, depicted in Fig. 8.7 [46], comprises 54 generator buses, along with a segment of load buses and transmission lines. Each generator i’s local cost function is quadratic, represented by \( J_i(p_i) = c_i p_i^2 + b_i p_i + a_i \), where the coefficients \( a_i \) range from 6.78 to 74.33, \( \{b_i\} \) between 8.3391 and 37.6968, and \( \{c_i\} \) spans 0.0024 to 0.0697, measured in $, $/MW, and $/MW^2, respectively [46]. ## 8.5.4 Example 4: performance comparisons of various algorithms on different systems

In this scenario, we compare the iterations and convergence time required for various algorithms to reach the residual \(\log(1/m) \sum_{i=1}^m \mathbb{E}[||y_i^t - y^*||] = -3\) across IEEE 14-bus, 57-bus, 118-bus, and 300-bus systems, with all related parameters available in [66]. The algorithms under consideration include Algorithm 1, the decentralized algorithm in [24] (Algorithm 1 without the random sleep strategy and conditional noises), and the centralized method in [42]. For consistency, other relevant conditions are set to \(\eta_i = 0.8\) and \(\hat{\xi}^t = 1/t^{2.1}\) for all \( i \) and \( t \ge 0 \). The comparison results, summarized in Table 8.2, confirm the following: (a) The proposed algorithm may require more iterations and time to achieve the expected results, an acceptable tradeoff for enhanced privacy protection and improved computational efficiency; (b) Incorporating conditional noises and the random sleep strategy into the proposed algorithm slightly reduces its convergence rate but does not compromise its accuracy in solving the EDP; (c) The proposed algorithm demonstrates adaptability to large-scale systems, showing promising robustness. The average computation time per generator per iteration is \(1.57 \times 10^{-5}\) for the IEEE 14-bus system, \(1.07 \times 10^{-5}\) for the IEEE 57-bus system, \(9.10 \times 10^{-6}\) for the IEEE 118-bus system, and \(8.82 \times 10^{-6}\) for the IEEE 300-bus system. These computation times may differ slightly from those reported in other works [27,63], primarily due to variations in computing power across different computers. Finally, while the results in Table 8.2 may have some uncertainty due to random factors, they do not impact the overall conclusions.
