# Containers

A container is a reinforced filling cabinet that can be used to store proprietary and sensitive information. The standards for classified containers are typically from a government. For example, the U.S. government lists a class 6 container (Figure 7.36) as approved for the storage of secret, top secret, and confidential information. The container must meet the protection requirements for 30 man-minutes against covert entry and 20 hours against surreptitious entry with no forced entry.
