# Blais de Vigenère

Blais de Vigenère, a Frenchman, developed the polyalphabetic cipher using a key word and 26 alphabets, each one offset by one place. This is shown in the following table. The top row of the table would be the plaintext values and the first column of the table the substitution alphabets.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|-----|---|

| B | B | C | D | E | F | G | H | I | J | K | L | M | ... | A |
| C | C | D | E | F | G | H | I | J | K | L | M | N | ... | B |
| D | D | E | F | G | H | I | J | K | L | M | N | O | ... | C |

| F | F | G | H | I | J | K | L | M | N | O | P | Q | ... | E |
