# The Early (Manual) Era

There is evidence of cryptographic-type operations going back thousands of years. In one case, there is an example in Egypt of one set of hieroglyphics that were encrypted with a simple substitution algorithm. The Spartans were known for the Spartan scytale – a method of transmitting a message by wrapping a leather belt around a tapered dowel.
