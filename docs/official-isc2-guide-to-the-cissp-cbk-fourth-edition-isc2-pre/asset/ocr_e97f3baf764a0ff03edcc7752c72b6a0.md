# Frame Relay

Frame relay is an economical alternative to circuit-switched networks and dedicated lines between networks that have significant idle time. Because frame relay uses packet-switching technology, organizations are charged for used bandwidth, which is less expensive than maintaining a dedicated line or the cost of a circuit that is based on the duration of the connection. The heart of a frame relay network is the frame relay cloud of switches on the provider’s premises. All frame relay customers share the resources in the cloud, which are assumed to be reliable, and do not require the intense error checking and correcting of X.25. This significantly increases the throughput over X.25. Devices within the cloud are considered data circuit-terminating equipment (DCE).
