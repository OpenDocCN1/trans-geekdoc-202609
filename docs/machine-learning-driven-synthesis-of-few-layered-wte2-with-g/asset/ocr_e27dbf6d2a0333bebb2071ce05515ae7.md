We have carried out a series of experiments with various R_Te/W values, in order to verify the information obtained from the ML analysis. Notably, the other four parameters are set within the optimal ranges as discussed above to ensure the successful synthesis of samples. Specifically, reaction temperature, ramp time, deposition time, and carrier gas flow rate are fixed at 750 °C, 20 min, 7 min, and 100 sccm Ar and 40 sccm H₂, respectively. The frequency distribution histograms of the length–width ratio, width, and length with corresponding optical images at different R_Te/W are presented in Figure 3D–G and Figure S5A. The gamma distribution fitting curve can well

fit all the frequency distribution plots. To further investigate the relationship between the R_Te/W and the size of as-obtained NRs, the mean value of the length–width ratio, length, and width are calculated together with the corresponding standard deviation (Figure 3F and Figure S5B). The length–width ratio shows a consistent increase from 12.5 to 77.7 as the R_Te/W increases from 1 to 20. Such intriguing geometrical evolution might be related to the concentration of Te, which will be detailed in the growth mechanism section. Within the error margin, the length of WTe₂ NRs increases from 15.7 to 49.2 μm (Figure S5B) along with the increase of R_Te/W, while the width shows the opposite trend (from 1.9 to 0.7 μm, Figure 3F).
