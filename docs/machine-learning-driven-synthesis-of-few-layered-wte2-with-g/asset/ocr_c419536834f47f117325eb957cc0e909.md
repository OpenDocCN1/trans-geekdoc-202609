Characterization of Few-Layered 1T′ WTe₂ NRs. The structure and quality of the synthesized few-layered WTe₂ NRs have been carefully examined, with results shown in Figure 4. The top and side views of the atomic structure of 1T′ WTe₂ NRs are presented in Figure 4A. Figure 4B shows the typical optical image of the few-layered WTe₂ NRs with a length of ~50 μm. The width and thickness were further determined by the atomic force microscopy (AFM) (Figure 4C). The width

of the WTe₂ NRs is ~0.8 μm, and the thickness is ~2.5 nm, indicating its triple-layer nature. The reproducible synthesis results (Figure S6) and corresponding statistical analysis (Figure S7) suggest the high reproducibility of the space-confined CVD method, attributed to its stable fluid field velocity distribution (Supporting Information Note 3, Figure S8, and Table S5).

Raman spectroscopy was carried out to investigate the quality of as-synthesized WTe₂ NRs, with the Raman spectrum displayed in Figure 4D.
