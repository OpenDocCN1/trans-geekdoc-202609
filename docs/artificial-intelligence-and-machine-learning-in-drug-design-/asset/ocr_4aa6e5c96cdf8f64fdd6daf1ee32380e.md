The virtual screening method comprises the technique in which the likely target-binding character of a set of chemicals (compounds) is evaluated by searching an extensive library of known chemical structures that bind to the specific target. Due to the less expensive and faster nature of virtual screening, it is more convenient in its use than the conventional and high-throughput screening methods [62–64].

# 15.4.1 Basic Steps Involved in the Implementation of AI Techniques in the Compound Screening Process

The AI method uses machine learning algorithms that enable the generation of statistical models by learning the specific pattern from large datasets. Then, the learning patterns are applied to predict the unknown data [65–67].

To perform molecular screening in the drug design process, the molecular data should be collected from the available databases and must be presented in a specific chemical format.
