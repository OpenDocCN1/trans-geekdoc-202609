# 12.3.4.1 Reinforcement Learning

Reinforcement learning is another subfield of AI that has the potential to improve drug treatment. Reinforcement learning is a type of machine learning that is inspired by the way humans learn from experience. It involves training a model to make decisions based on feedback received from the environment.

In the context of drug treatment, reinforcement learning can be used to identify the best dosage of a drug for a patient. This involves the patient's medical history, current condition, and other factors that may influence how they respond to a drug. The goal is to find the right balance between efficacy and side effects.
