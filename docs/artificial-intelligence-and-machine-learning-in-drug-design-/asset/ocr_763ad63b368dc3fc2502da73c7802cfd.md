Functional enrichment pathway study: This determines if a network or gene/protein group over-represents biological pathways or gene sets. Pathways or activities significantly associated to genes/proteins are revealed using enrichment analysis. Functional enrichment finds Gene Ontology and protein domains in networks. It identifies network component functional themes, molecular functions, and biological processes [11].

Network simulation biological networks simulate time and perturbation. ODEs and Boolean models simulate network dynamics, predict system behavior, and investigate genetic or environmental perturbations on network output.
