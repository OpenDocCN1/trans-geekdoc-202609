# Table 2.1 Glossary of key bioinformatics terms.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Genome | The complete set of genetic material in an organism, including all of its genes. |
| Proteome | The entire set of proteins that is, or can be, expressed by a genome, cell, tissue, or organism at a certain time. |
| Transcriptome | The total amount of all the RNA molecules expressed from the genes of an organism. |
| Sequence alignment | A method of arranging sequences of DNA, RNA, or protein to identify regions of similarity that may be a consequence of functional, structural, or evolutionary relationships between the sequences. |
