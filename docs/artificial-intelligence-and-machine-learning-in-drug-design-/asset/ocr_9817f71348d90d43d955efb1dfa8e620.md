# 7.4.1.1 Predictive Analytics and Precision Medicine

Precision medicine refers to a medical methodology that entails tailoring treatment plans for patients according to their unique circumstances, behaviors, and genetic makeup. The effectiveness of this specific procedure has been proven in accelerating the drug approval process and improving the success rates of clinical trials. The use of artificial intelligence (AI) to extract important information from vast medical data for diagnosis and treatment is a much-awaited advancement.
