
## Conclusion

The Internet has not turned anything into "virtual", but has simply provided everyone with a very versatile tool that has facilitated our real life with undoubted advantages in terms of cost and convenience. Today, with our computer or smartphone connected to the internet we can do things that previously required our physical presence (such as making a transfer, making endless queues to pay bills, booking a vacation, etc.). The Internet is real life and as in real life, we must take precautions. In real life, we know that we have to be careful of thieves and not to trust strangers. Even on the internet, we must take the same precautions; use complex passwords, do not click on equivocal banners, do not open unknown emails, do not click on suspicious links. This book aims to provide all possible precautions to surf the Internet safely.
