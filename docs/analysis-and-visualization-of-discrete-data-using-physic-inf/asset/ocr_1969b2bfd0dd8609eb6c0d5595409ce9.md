A value closer to 1 means that the regression model fits the data well, while a value closer to 0 means that the regression model does not fit the data well. The following equation calculates the coefficient of determination: $R^{2}=1-(S S E / S S T)$. In this equation, SSE represents the residual sum of squares (the sum of squares of the difference between the value predicted by the regression model and the observed value) and SST represents the sum of squares of hypotheses (sum of squares of the difference between the observed value and the mean value). The coefficient of determination is often used to evaluate the results of regression analysis. However, the coefficient of determination tends to increase in value as the number of explanatory variables is increased. Therefore, the complexity of the model must be taken into account.
