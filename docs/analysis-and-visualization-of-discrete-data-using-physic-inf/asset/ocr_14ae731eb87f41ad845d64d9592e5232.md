A new error term composed of partial differential terms, $MSE_{ip}$, is added to the loss function. By adding objective functions (errors), so-called multi-objective optimization is converted into optimization with a single objective function. The strategy of adding multiple errors to the loss function in the NN as described earlier is the same as that for the PINNs proposed by the research group of M. Raissi et al. [28].
