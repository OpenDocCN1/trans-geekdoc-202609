## 23.8 CONCLUSION

Time of service is the essence of good healthcare. Disparity in reach of healthcare facilities is an old reality of rural India. The progressive status of this gap has further widened by the emergence of COVID-19 pandemic. The disease burden of Indian rural areas is shifting fast. But the challenges are more confined to deficiency in structure, manpower and access. Structural advancement and systematic delivery are the fundamental changes imminent to upgrade healthcare delivery. It is not an overnight affair. Opportunity is there to fill the gaps. Therefore, AI integrated health approach can be a dependable solution. Further, it can provide legitimate benefit to the residents of rural India.

Technology cannot serve as a replacement for medical and paramount personnel. However, it can assist medical practitioners in expanding their reach to a larger number of patients in underserved regions where the availability of high-quality healthcare is still a challenge. The issue of staff shortage can be temporarily alleviated for urgent cases. The utilization of AI in conjunction with other technologies can render remote monitoring, chronic illness management and preventive care viable possibilities. Therefore, harnessing the untapped potential of AI can facilitate the development and prosperity of rural India. Otherwise, the healthcare administration will continue to be overshadowed by the financial burden of rural healthcare Smart Healthcare Systems.
