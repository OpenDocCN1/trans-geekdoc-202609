The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) include a number of major objectives, one of which is to achieve universal health care, as stated by Hogan et al. (2018). This indicates that by the year 2030, every person will have unfettered access to primary health care (PHC), which is also known as medical care. The World Health Organization (WHO) and the World Bank announced that “poverty caused by illness” is a result of the fact that 122 million people are considered to be “poor” (living on less than $3.10 per day) and 100 million people are recorded as “extremely poor” (living on less $1.90 per day) due to the costs associated with healthcare (World Health Organization-World Bank, 2017). This poverty caused by illness is result of medical expenses. Addressing the challenges mentioned above, employing government strategies, and including advanced and robust technology IoMT, achieving SDGs in specified time is possible.
