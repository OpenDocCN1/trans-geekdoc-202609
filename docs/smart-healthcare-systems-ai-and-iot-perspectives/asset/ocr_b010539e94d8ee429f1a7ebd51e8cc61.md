# 1.8.2 POTENTIAL IMPACT ON HEALTHCARE DELIVERY AND PATIENT OUTCOMES

The seamless synergy of AI and IoMT can streamline and optimize healthcare processes, leading to more efficient and personalized care. Real-time data collection through interconnected devices continuous patient monitoring, facilitating early detection of health issues and allowing timely interventions.
