# 23.5 SCOPE OF ALTERNATIVE APPROACH

Healthcare systems effectiveness is a function of resource allocation. This leads to high costs of operations and slow processing. Leveraging the support of AI and RPA, healthcare operation can achieve highest precision as it can automate many functions. More e-health is being used by developing countries to properly allocate budgets within their GDP, which may increase their range on e-health (Bambri and Gupta, 2018). Disease dynamics of rural India has a complex scenario as it is not only with infectious diseases. After the emergence of COVID-19, the situation is more critical. Advanced facilities and diagnostic services are the need of the hour (Gole and Khera, 2015). Moreover, poor accessibility and affordability of legitimate healthcare is a function of social disparity (Singh and Badaya).
