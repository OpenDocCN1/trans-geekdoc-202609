This transition could speed up clinical trials, measure the affinity between a biomolecule (like DNA or protein) and its ligand partner (like a medicine), help develop new pharmaceuticals and enzymes, and enable protein folding studies. The following are brief examples of possible uses. Fast DNA sequencing with a quantum computer allows for personalized treatment (Banchi et al., 2020). Using extensive modeling techniques can speed up drug development (Fedorov and Leonov, 2018). Quantum computing may help professionals develop real-time imaging systems with higher resolution and speed. It can also solve complex optimization problems like a radiation plan that will kill cells without harming healthy tissue. Quantum computing (QC) will enable molecular interaction analysis at the fundamental level, opening new avenues for drug development and medical research (Sayal et al., 2023a). Whole-genome sequencing and analysis are time-consuming, but may real-time computation, medical data security, chronic disease prediction, and drug discovery can revolutionize the healthcare system with QC (Ur Rasool et al., 2023).
Quantum computing, leveraging quantum mechanics, is transformative in computational chemistry and pharmaceutical research. It synergizes with advanced machine learning techniques and IoMT for data processing. Quantum biology, particularly quantum-mRNA technology, aligns with IoT's biological data (Rattan et al., 2005a). The internet of things (IoT), using biological networks for information transmission, presents ethical and safety challenges, underscores the need for robust security in IoMT. This paradigm shift emphasizes interconnectedness and data-driven value in IoMT and IoT ecosystems (Virolle et al., 2020; Gupta et al., 2023).

# 2.3.3 NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING FOR PATIENT RECORDS ANALYSIS

NLP is crucial in IoMT for enhancing patient outcome prediction, hospital triage systems, and early-stage chronic disease diagnostics. It processes unstructured medical data, aiding in communication through NLG and NLU, and is instrumental in chatbot interactions (Lin et al., 2019). CogStack and other tools facilitate data organization and clinical categorization (Shi et al., 2019). Despite the moves towards structured EHRs, NLP remains essential in IoMT, demonstrated by chatbots in apps like Health Tap and Babylon Health, and has been particularly valuable during the COVID-19 pandemic (Locke et al., 2021).
