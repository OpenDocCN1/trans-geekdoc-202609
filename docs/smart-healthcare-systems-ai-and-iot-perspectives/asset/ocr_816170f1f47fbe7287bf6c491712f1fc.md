# 9.3.1 GAIT ANALYSIS

Human movements are widely studied, and over the past years, numerous people died due to physical inactivity. Almost 100,000 deaths can be prevented annually if US citizens increase their physical activity by ten minutes each day. That is, gait analysis can play an important role. To keep track of physical activity, proper tracking, monitoring, and measurement are important. It is important to assess human locomotion quantitatively. The most commonly used human sensing is seen in the step tracker application. Sensors used in this application need to be highly precise, small in weight, user-friendly, and easy to use in all environments (Gupta et al., 2023).

# 9.3.2 PHOTOPLETHMOGRAPHY

Photoplethysmography (PPG) is used for both making biomedical and non-biomedical wearables. This signal is at the center of measuring heart rate variability. In the traditional method, heart rate variability is measured using HRV means of an electrocardiogram (ECG) signal. HRV data was used to measure the peak-to-peak time between cardiac cycles. HRV analytics can provide a wide range of information to the autonomous nervous system (Divedwi et al., 2022). However, there are limitations of acquiring ECG data collecting them at home as it requires proper positioning and orientation.

Trained medical staff need to properly position the electrodes in the right position to get proper results, which is sometimes quite unlikely and time-consuming.
