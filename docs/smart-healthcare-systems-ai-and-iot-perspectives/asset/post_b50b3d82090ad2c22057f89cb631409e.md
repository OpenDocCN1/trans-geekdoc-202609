## Smart Healthcare Systems

Diagnostic centres are using an AI, natural language and computer vision solution developed by Synapsica that may help to achieve better precision and high efficiency. Similar facilities could contribute to providing a greater level of service to the rural population of India. In rural areas, the risk of spreading sexually transmitted diseases can be reduced by using chatbot and voice assisted mechanisms where the patient can receive the right medical support when discussing their problems. Currently, the Indian rural sector is developing with artificial intelligence, particularly with machine learning. AI, which is the application of computer software with preprogrammed functions to replace human brains, has revolutionized the healthcare sector. However, these new technologies are revitalizing the infrastructure and lowering the one-time treatment expenses in the rural sector. The utilization of AI decreases the risk, accuracy in treatment increases which indirectly improves profit due to the reduction of total costs. New ML or other AI technologies are a solution for age-old rural health problems such as quality healthcare, inadequate ratio of skilled clinicians to patients (Bhambri et al., 2019). If these developments can be implemented in the Indian rural sector, the cost minimization can make a huge impact to the developing economy of India which stands approximately 2.1% of India's GDP for the healthcare sector. In India cost modelling, treatment variability and drug discovery are the healthcare sectors where the application of artificial intelligence is profitable by both quality and cost. But application of AI in healthcare involves few risks and challenges, like quality, governance, privacy, consent and ownership, etc. Therefore, prototype testing will signify the significance of AI oriented activity. Requisite training must be conducted at a feasible cost to increase the potential of the infrastructure. Also, other related issues like spontaneousness and high-speed network with dedicated server, are necessary for the successful implementation of AI in rural India which can increase the technological development costs. Hence, wishing and willful action is a prerequisite.
