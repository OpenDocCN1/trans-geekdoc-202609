# 8.3 VOICE SIGNAL ANALYSIS IN HEALTHCARE

Voice signal analysis in healthcare refers to the process of using advanced techniques and technology to extract valuable medical information from a person’s voice (Bambri and Gupta 2005). This field has gained significant attention due to its non-invasive, cost-effective, and patient-friendly approach to healthcare diagnostics (Sirisha and Reddy 2018). By analyzing the characteristics of a person’s voice, such as pitch, tone, speed, and even subtle changes imperceptible to the human ear, healthcare professionals and researchers can gather insights about various health conditions.
