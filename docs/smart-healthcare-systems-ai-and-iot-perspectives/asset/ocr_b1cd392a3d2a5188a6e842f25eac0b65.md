# 18.1.1 RETHINKING HEALTHCARE WITH DECISION SUPPORT SYSTEMS FOR PHYSICIANS

The rapid advances in technology have reshaped many facets of our day-to-day life, and healthcare is no exception. At the heart of this revolution, Physician Decision Support Systems (PDSS) have emerged as a beacon, bringing new ground in patient care and aiding clinicians in the decision-making process. This process will delve deeper into the DNA of PDSS, assessing what it brings to the table, and the indispensable impression it leaves in the realm of modern healthcare.
