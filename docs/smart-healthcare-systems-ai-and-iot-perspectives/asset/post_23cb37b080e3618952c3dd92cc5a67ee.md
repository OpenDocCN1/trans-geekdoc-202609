## 2.8 CONCLUSION

The AI-IoMT combination promises to revolutionize healthcare, providing individualized care through technologies like wearable devices, chatbots, and machine learning algorithms. This paper covers AI applications in various healthcare areas, the challenges in security, privacy, and algorithmic bias. It explores edge computing, 5G, deep learning, and quantum computing, emphasizing patient-centricity and flexibility for enhancing healthcare research, with a focus on ethical principles and collaborative frameworks.
