# TABLE 23.1
Framework of Digital Health Services in India

| Layers | Particulars |
| --- | --- |
| Layer-1 Infrastructure | Secure Health Network: Strong data link through high bandwidth network system. Application: Tele-health, Tele-radiology, etc. Health Cloud (H-cloud): Government Community Cloud (GCC) developed by MeiY. Application: Key data management Security and Privacy Operations Centre (SOC): Establishment of Operations Centre (POC) for 24/7 security surveillance. Application: Health Cloud and Health Network |
| Layer-2 Data Hubs | Unique Identifier (UHID): Specific details including demo, location, family, relationship, contact details of an individual. Application: Generation of medical records Electronic Health Record (EHR): General and aggregate digital health records. Application: Multiple users can exchange health data for the treatment Health Directories and Health Registers (HDRH): Master data of various entities which are subdivided as Faculty, Doctors, Nurses and Paramedical, Health Workers and Allied Professional Directory |
| Layer-3 Building Blocks | Anonymizer: Collect data from 'Health Locker' or other data sets, provides anonymized data to the users after removing identifiable information for better security, Consent Manager: The electronic consent framework by 'MeiY' to be used prior to use of health records. Health Locker: It helps to create longitudinal health record from the various links it provides and EHR to users. Health Information Exchange: Real-time data exchange mechanism is applied and subject to authorization and authentication for every exchange data. Health Analytics: Helps to decision making support in the areas of quality of care, quality of data, wellness, public health, fraud detection, policy making, etc. |
| Layer-4 GIS/Visualization | Take the data from the health analytics system and output as the queries near nearest hospital with specialty, disease incidence in a geographic area etc. |
| Layer-5 Access and delivery | Call Centers: Provide telephonic services to the residents of India. Health Portal: Multi language portal for digital health services. Social Media: Information, education and communication, etc. MyHealthApps: Several apps can be designed by open market.

Source: National Digital Health Blueprint, MOH and FW, GOI
