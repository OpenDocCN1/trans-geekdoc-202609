# 14.1.1 CONCEPT OF BIOSENSORS AND IOMT IN CONTEXT OF SMART HEALTHCARE

The concept of biosensors and IoMT form the bedrock of a transformative paradigm in the realm of smart healthcare. Biosensors, intricate devices designed to detect and quantify biological signals, represent a technological breakthrough in healthcare diagnostics. These devices, often employing sensitive elements with transducers, enable the real-time monitoring of physiological parameters. Whether embedded in wearable devices, implanted in the human body, or integrated into point-of-care diagnostic tools, biosensors empower individuals and healthcare professionals alike with continuous and personalized health insights (Irkam et al., 2023).

The synergy of biosensors and the IoMT lays the foundation for a dynamic and responsive smart healthcare system, wherein the proactive monitoring of health parameters and the swift transmission of data redefine the standards of patient care. This conceptual integration stands to revolutionize healthcare, offering a holistic approach to diagnostics and treatment that is data-driven, patient-centric, and technologically advanced (Johnson, 2020).
