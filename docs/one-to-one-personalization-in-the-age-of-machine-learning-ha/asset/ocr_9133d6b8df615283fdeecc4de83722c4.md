The company worked to synchronize its first-party customer loyalty data, behavioral data from its website, and third-party media exposure data (the next chapter will provide more detail on these data sources). As a result, the airline significantly reduced media spend while keeping conversions constant.

While the concepts are similar to the advertising section above, it is important enough to deserve its own section.
