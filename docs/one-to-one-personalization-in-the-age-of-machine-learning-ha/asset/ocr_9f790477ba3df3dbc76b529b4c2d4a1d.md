# WEBSITE

Many marketers view their website as their most important digital channel—and for good reason. Whether your goal is to drive online sales, generate leads or maximize page views, there are typically some key actions you want visitors to take while on your site (e.g., sign up, download, add to cart, make a purchase, etc.). As a result, many of your online marketing activities are focused on driving traffic to your website and encouraging people to take these steps. Given its importance, the website is often one of the first channels where marketers begin to apply personalization.

Marketers tend to think about personalization taking place in specific spots on a website, such as CTA buttons, product recommendations on product detail pages. or a personalized widget or two on the homepage.
