**Web Attributes:** These attributes are detected as soon as a person lands on a site. They are particularly valuable in helping you personalize an experience for first-time visitors who have not yet interacted with your site, although they can be used in many other situations as well. Some common web attributes include:

+   - Geolocation
+   - Source (such as search, email, social, paid ad, referring site, etc.)
+   - Industry
+   - Company
+   - Company size (revenue or employee count)
+   - Technology stack
+   - Time of day
+   - Browser type
+   - Device type
