# # BASE ALGORITHMS

Base algorithms are the foundation for delivering recommendations and individualized experiences and engagement. When choosing a base algorithm, you have the opportunity to tell the machine where to begin for selecting items to recommend. There are basic algorithms with which you might already be familiar, and more sophisticated algorithms that leverage advanced machine-learning principles.

# # BASIC ALGORITHMS

Basic algorithms generally rely on specific criteria or the “wisdom of the crowd.” Some of the most common are:

+   - **Trending:** Items are recommended based on what is most popular on the site during a specific time period.
+   - **Recently Published:** Items are recommended based on what has recently been added to the site.
+   - **Soon to Expire:** Recommended items are prioritized based on their upcoming expiration dates.
+   - **Co-Browse:** Items are recommended based on what other people on the site have also viewed.
+   - **Co-Buy:** Items are recommended based on what other people on the site have also purchased.
