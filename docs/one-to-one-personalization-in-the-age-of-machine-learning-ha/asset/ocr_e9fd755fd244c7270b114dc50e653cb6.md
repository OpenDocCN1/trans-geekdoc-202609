+   Target new campaigns and messages to customers/prospects that are included in direct mail campaigns across digital channels

How might companies identify whom to include in direct mail campaigns? A B2B company could mail an invitation for its annual event to known prospects who had engaged meaningfully on its site or spoken with a sales rep in the last six months. An e-commerce company could mail a promotion to shoppers who had made purchases in the past, but not bought anything in the last three months, and were interested in a particular category. Creative marketers can come up with an endless number of ideas, but the key to success with direct mail is data and segmentation (along with a healthy budget).
