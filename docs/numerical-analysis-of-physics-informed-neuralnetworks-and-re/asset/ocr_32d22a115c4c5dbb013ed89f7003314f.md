A more general result for linear Kolmogorov equations, including a more detailed expression for C and α, can be found in De Ryck and Mishra (2022a). A comparison with the corresponding result for the Navier–Stokes equations (Theorem 6.5) reveals the following.

+   - Just as for the Navier–Stokes equations, the total error scales with the square root of the training error, ℰ(θ) ∼ √ℰ_T(θ, S) when the training set is large enough.
+   - For the Navier–Stokes equations (with d = 2 or d = 3) we found that we need to choose N_int ≫ N_s ≫ N_t. However, in this case we find that far fewer training points in the interior are needed, as N_s ≫ N_t ≈ N_int is sufficient.

As a first example, Mishra and Molinaro (2022) consider the one-dimensional heat equation on the domain [−1, 1] with initial condition sin(πx) and final time T = 1. The results can be seen in Figure 6.2. As one can see, both the generalization and training error decrease with increasing number of quadrature points. In particular, the relationship ℰ(θ) ∼ √ℰ_T(θ, S) is (approximately) observed as well.
