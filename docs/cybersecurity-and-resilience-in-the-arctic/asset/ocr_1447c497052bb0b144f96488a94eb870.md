# 1.1.2. The history of Arctic Sea routes

The activity of the NEP (also referred as the Northern Sea Route or NSR) initially peaked under the USSR in 1987, when a total of 6.58 million tons of cargo was carried by 331 ships, over 1306 voyages. Comparing these volumes to the last decade, we see a large increase in the amount of activity through this region. In 2011, four ships sailed the entire length of the NEP, increasing in 2012 to 46, and then falling down to 19 in 2013 [6]. Since 2014 however, increases in cargo tonnage have been on the rise, with 2018 seeing a record-setting year for the NEP as 18 million tons of cargo travelled along Russia's coastline, a growth increase of 80% from 2017 [7].
