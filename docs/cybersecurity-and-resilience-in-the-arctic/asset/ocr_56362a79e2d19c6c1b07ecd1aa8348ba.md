It must be considered that many influence factors is cyclical in Arctic. For example, ice humming is depended on Moon phases, seal population is depended on polar bears

If estimations of system parameters carry out at equally probable intervals (it can be hours, days, weeks, months) probabilistic mathematical models with discrete time should be applied.

Linear random process with discrete time first appeared reviewed in [7]. Further, theory of linear random processes was developed in papers [8-12]. It is necessary to remark that currently linear random processes with continuous time are represented as autoregressive- moving average process with continuous time (CARMA).
