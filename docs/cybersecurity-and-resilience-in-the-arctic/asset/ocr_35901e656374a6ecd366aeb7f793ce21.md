The 2011-2020 Strategy aims to focus upon the relationship between Denmark and Greenland, with special consideration going towards the devolution of responsibility and channels of authority. Other issues prioritised in the document are the Northwest passage; globalization and trade; partnership with the European Union, Arctic Council and specifically the Kingdom of Denmark’s chairmanship from 2009-2022, and the boundaries of the Kingdom of Denmark’s continental shelf in the Arctic.

One important emphasis in the strategy is that on new and emerging economic developments and industrial activity in Arctic. Fishing as being traditionally prioritised, however, hydropower, mining, tourism, oil exploration, and of other minerals; exploration of off-shore fuels and other energy resources are viewed as critical to development in Greenland [43]. While attention is greatly paid towards these endeavours, shipping and transport on opening sea routes in the North are less prioritised. A strong need for integration in international trade is identified, however, it’s noted that for the exploitation and use of renewable marine resources requires high standards, indicating sustainable use of natural resources alongside elements of environmental protection.

# 6.5. Finnish Arctic Policy

The Arctic Policy for Finland can be viewed in “Finland’s Strategy for the Arctic Region”; adopted by the Finnish Cabinet Committee on the European Union, the strategy launched in June 2010 [52] and lists objectives in the following substantial sectors:

+   1. “Fragile Arctic Nature” (The Environment):
   a. Highlighting special features and risks of the arctic nature in international cooperation that require attention;
   b. Enabling strong support for Arctic research, helping to develop regional climate models that monitor the environment providing a more concrete basis for decision-making;
   c. Promote nuclear safety, particularly in the Kola Peninsula;

+   2. “Economic activities and know-how”:
   a. Bolstering the role Finland holds as an international expert and source for expert information related to northern issues and challenges;
   b. Utilizing Finnish expertise, specifically those related to winter shipping, transport and shipbuilding;
   c. Supplementing and enlarging the number of opportunities for Finnish enterprise to benefit from this expertise, in the context of current meta-project currently ongoing in the Barents Region.
