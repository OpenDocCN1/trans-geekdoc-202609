The issue of risk within resilience literature is also an area of contention. Many people define resilience as a function of threat and consider expected vulnerabilities and loss. Others take a threat-agent viewpoint and simply look at loss of performance due to disruption. The threat-agent approach also fail to standardize an approach to aggregating all potential disruption scenarios into a resilience metric.
Resilience quantification is also an area that lacks consensus. Metrics-based approaches are used in several papers. The metric(s) of performance include some form of network efficiency, such as cost, amount delivered, or time delay.
