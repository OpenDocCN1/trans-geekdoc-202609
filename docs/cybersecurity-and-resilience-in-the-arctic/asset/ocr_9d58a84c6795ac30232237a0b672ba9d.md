# Defining digital social resilience

Despite the attention and buzz that themes as fake news is in frontline news, definitions and conceptualization of digital social resilience are disproportionately in short supply. So far, digital resilience has been mostly associated with technical cybersecurity capabilities requisite to withstand cyber threats and electronic systems vulnerabilities related to national (e.g. Estonia 2007, WikiLeaks 2010, German Bundestag 2015) or corporate (e.g. point-of-sale system hacks of US retailers 2013, Ukraine electricity companies 2015) hack attacks, malware, ransomware, identity thefts and hardware systems failure, among others.
