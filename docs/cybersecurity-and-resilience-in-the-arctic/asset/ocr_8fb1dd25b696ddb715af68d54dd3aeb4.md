# 2.2 Coding Scheme

Upon acquiring the relevant set of 52 supply chain resilience modeling papers, the authors coded each piece across 5 domain areas, including “Resilience Characteristics,” “Supply Chain Network Representation,” “Transportation Network Representation,” “Command and Control Representation,” and “Disruptions Representation.” Within each domain, the coding scheme was used to assess papers based upon how they tackle a number of issues:

+   1. “Resilience Characteristics” classifies how the paper characterizes resilience. Papers were coded to which of the National Academy of Sciences phases of resilience were included in their methods and if they used any metrics to measure resilience, separate from base network characteristics. The National Academy of Sciences phases are “Plan,” “Absorb,” “Recover,” and “Adapt” (National Research Council 2012). “Calculate Resilience Metric” was used to indicate the usage of a numeric measure to identify a supply chain’s resilience, so long as that measure was not a simple characteristic of network geometry, such as betweenness, or length path. This restriction was put in place to ensure that resilience is being calculated for a working system, rather than a complex abstract.
