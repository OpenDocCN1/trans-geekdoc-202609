The U.S. Navy Strategic Outlook for the Arctic (2019) This chapter has examined the literature on resilience in the military context, integrating various definitions into a comprehensive, five-stage definition of resilience for military systems. Resilient military systems 1) have both utility and are prepared for disruptions 2) are able to absorb disruptions, repelling or isolating intrusions with minimal loss 3) are able to recover full functionality swiftly 4) adapt based on knowledge gained from the disruption, and 5) degrade gracefully by maintaining limited functionality as the product lifecycle comes to an end.

While this focuses on the importance of resilience for USN operations in the Arctic region, it has broader applications. Researching, investing in, and implementing resilient systems can help the U.S. Navy and NATO to maintain and grow their competitive advantage in all regions in which they operate.
