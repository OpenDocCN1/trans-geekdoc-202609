# What is consensus?

CONSENSUS (lat. Consensus - consent, unanimity, complexity, coordination), the presence of similar orientations in two or more individuals, allowing them to establish mutual understanding or interaction in any respect in any area of human activity.

A key solution in the blockchain is to reach an agreement that is beneficial to all participants in the network, which requires solving the problem of untrusted parties (e.g. traitors).

As a variant, it is necessary to consider the algorithm for the example of an ideal network in which the share of ownership is determined by the rules of network participants and consensus is considered if the total share of those who support the consensus (an action (transaction)) in the network is not less than $\delta$

$(\delta_0 - \delta_l) / \delta_l < 1 - \Delta \leq 0,5$

Where $\delta_0$ is the max weight for all network participants, $\delta_l$ is the total value of the ownership share, and $\Delta$ is between 50-100%. Weight is determined according to the laws of summation.
