Arctic Boundary defined by the Arctic Research and Policy Act (ARPA)

All United States and foreign territory north of the Arctic Circle and all United States north and west of the boundary formed by the Philippine,
Yukon, and Chukotka; all contiguous; including the Arctic Ocean and the Beaufort, Bering and Chukchi Seas, and the Canadian chain.
