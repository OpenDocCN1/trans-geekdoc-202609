BOX 1. The terms Arctic, Arctic Circle, and Far North, describe increasingly wide geographic range of lands, waters, and people. The Far North is meant as inclusive of areas and peoples that fall outside of the geographic Arctic and below the 10 °C is however but nevertheless defining social, ecological, and environment features with the Arctic (e.g., Fairbanks, USA and Rovaniemi, Finland), and are therefore included in this analysis.
