**Abstract.** To engineer a more defensible and survivable system, resiliency metrics must be current and provide relevant and practical measurement objectives to understand business impact from secure and resilient systems. Although there are several industry Information Communications Communications Technology (ICT) frameworks to measure resilience, each are proprietary in nature requiring specific input variables with different purposes and intent within specific industries, technologies, or regulatory guidelines. This paper will address resiliency and the metrics attributes that may be considered for visual and statistical tracking and measurement.

# 1. Introduction

The term resiliency has taken many meanings and definitions over the last several years with an even greater amount of confusion on how to apply metrics to measure and then assess the ‘resiliency’ of systems. Although there are several industry Information Communications Technology (ICT) frameworks to measure resilience, each are proprietary in nature requiring specific input variables with different purposes and intent within specific industries, technologies, or regulatory guidelines. Most lack generic adaptability and when attempting to apply them without specialized assistance from the owner. Without understanding how resiliency is applied and then measured, there is no way to know the ‘currency’ and value of ICT management practices, nor to update or improve resiliency to the organization. There is no way of knowing what services provide value and those that must be changed to ensure all services are equally secure and resilient. Another consideration is that hybrid threats exist the luck of resiliency and what data is potentially unprotected, or less protected within a system. This leads to data loss of information that could be considered noncompliance with regulatory requirements, and/or data misuse may result in loss of public and financial investor confidence, and reputational harm. Generic frameworks for the other hand would allow easy to understand, and repeatable practices to enable the effectiveness of resiliency to the people, process, and technology aspects of the organization.
