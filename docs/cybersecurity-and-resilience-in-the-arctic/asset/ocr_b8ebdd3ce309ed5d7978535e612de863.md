**Education and Awareness.** Raising education and awareness of possible resilience issues may appear as a futile effort, but it is important to limit the risks of threats.

**Increase paper-based (or any non-computer) redundancy solutions.** The UK Department of Health and Social Care System and National Health Service suggest that “Paper processes need to exist and be implemented in the event that the incident management system is impacted by a cyber attack” [51].
