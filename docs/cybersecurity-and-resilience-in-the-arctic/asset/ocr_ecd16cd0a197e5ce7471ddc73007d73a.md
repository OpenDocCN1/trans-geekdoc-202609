# 3. Results and Discussion

# 3.1 Bibliometrics

The initial Web of Science search shows a significant growth in publications on supply chain resilience over the decade examined. Publications start at 4 papers, in 2007, and quickly grow to 185, by 2016. It might seem that this could be attributed to growth in publication about supply chains in general over the same time frame. A topic search for “Supply Chain” shows a growth in publications from approximately 2,700 papers to 6,400 papers over the same years. The proportion of supply chain papers showing up under this search criteria increases more than 10-fold. This shows that interest in supply chain resilience is growing considerably, both in general and within the field of supply chains.
