# 3. Value of Metrics

As an ICT professional, it is important to understand why metrics are important and the value they provide to the organization. As ICT professionals typically view metrics as cumbersome and a nuisance, this section provides reasons and finer points of the importance of metrics. As a standard, metrics provide performance measurement for organizations to inform different audiences such as leadership, employees, and stockholders about the effectiveness of operations across the business. Operations indicators must be easy to repeat, and relevant indicators each and every time they are reported to any gauge and measure from timeframes, a metric for any should be as objective as possible and easy to understand. These measurements must be repeatable otherwise they cannot be compared from previous reports to show value in improvements or deficiencies. As a basic rule, a metric measurement must also accurately reflect the state of business objectives or goals starting at a base-rate and then filtering down to unique metrics typically referred to as attributes and key performance indicators (KPIs). For an objective, one could be develop key resiliency metrics with one attribute “time to recover systems after maine activity” within a specific and known time frame. Although a very basic operations metric, this metric would help leadership in understanding the complexity of systems maintenance and how those operations vary based on unknown factors such as patches or other complex, all within the concept of ‘resiliency’ to change.
