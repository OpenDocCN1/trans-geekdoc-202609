Future infrastructure and information communication systems need to be designed and adapt to this challenging environment. A large amount of communication capacity and new contact points will be needed in the future in order to satisfy the data transfer needs of users, businesses organizations, and governments in these areas. The regional operational of these northern areas require reliable communication connections as those provided by submarine optic communications systems and satellite systems. These new connections provide people in the area with real-time access to digital services in their home country as well as the ability to communicate with friends locally or elsewhere in the world.

One of the good options would be satellite communications, which would allow for the flexibility of services required in the Arctic. However, there are some challenges to implementing satellite communication in the Arctic region. One of the challenges is to cover the Polar area above 75°N and the area between 66°N and 75°N.
