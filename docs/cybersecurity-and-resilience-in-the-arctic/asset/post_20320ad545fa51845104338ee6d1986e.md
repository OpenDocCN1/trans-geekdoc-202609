The European Union’s policy towards the Arctic has been evolving since 2007. In the overall Arctic context, the EU is particularly important as a funder of research and as an actor strongly Arctic monitoring, observation, and surveillance, through rigorous research funding and the EU’s satellite missions: Galileo and Copernicus (Steien 2016).
