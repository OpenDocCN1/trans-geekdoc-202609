Technology – The Arctic region and energy

The most important segment in Arctic society is energy, Figure 11. All forms of functionality in the Arctic region and in any smart society and smart city are very much dependent on the supply of electricity. It is possible that smart grid, heating, cooling, and energy distribution systems collapse due to internal faults, but the failure can also be caused by a cyber-attack against these systems. Cyber-attacks have also been made against power stations, meaning that electricity-based systems in the distribution area of the power station are not working at any day, Figure 11. We use different fuels for homes, cars, ships, planes, etc. But it is not possible to pump fuel into a tank without electricity in many cases.
