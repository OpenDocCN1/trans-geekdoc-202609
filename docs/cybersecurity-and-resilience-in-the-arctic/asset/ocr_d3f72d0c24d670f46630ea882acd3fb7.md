In other words, the Navy, as a core component of the Joint Force, can maintain
and gain the competitive edge while optimizing its capabilities across key

“The U.S. Army in Multi-Domain Operations 2028” goes on to characterize the future operational environment as shaped by several interrelated characteristics:

Adversaries are contesting all domains, the EMS,

Adversaries are contesting all domains across a geographically expanded battlefield. Should the U.S. Navy and NATO seek to contest the threat posed by enemies such as China and Russia, they would benefit from being equipped to contest all domains and in this extended geographical battlefield. That battlefield— whether competition or conflict -- will almost certainly include the Arctic. Although the Arctic has long been a peaceful region rarely subject to the geopolitical order which governs the rest of the world, this could change. Competition below the threshold of armed conflict is more likely than full-scale. The goal of preventing armed conflict can be assisted through the addressing of competition. The Arctic may very well become another region via Russia, China, and other adversaries seek to press their advantages against NATO, the West, and the United States.
