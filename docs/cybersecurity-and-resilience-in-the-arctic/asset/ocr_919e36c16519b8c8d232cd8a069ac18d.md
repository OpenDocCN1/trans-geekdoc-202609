# 6. Conclusion

The topic of cyber risk in the maritime sector is becoming more and more critical. Cyberattacks against 4.0 ships and shipping companies may have a devastating impact on worldwide commerce. We have briefly discussed the features of a methodology to assess and manage cyber risk for safety on a vessel and presented a possible solution with its supporting tools. More experience in this field is required to evaluate alternative solutions and choose the most promising ones.
