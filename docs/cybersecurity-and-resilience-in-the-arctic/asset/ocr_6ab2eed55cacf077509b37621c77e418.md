A value of "Agent Based" means that multiple autonomous agents, potentially separately from the supply chain nodes, follow mathematical, potentially probabilistic, rules to make decisions. A value of "Optimization" means that the whole supply chain, or each independently owned supply chain, runs on a global optimization algorithm. +   5. "Disruptions Representation" describes the set of disturbances that a supply chain will be simulated as facing. The possible values are "Case Study," "Set List,"MonteCarlo," "Targeted," and "None/Other." A value of "Case Study" shows that the circumstances and/or disturbances of the model will be based upon contemporary or historical situation(s). A value of "Set List" means that the model is subjected to a finite and predefined list of disturbances, which are not explicitly derived from contemporary or historic situations. A value of "Monte Carlo" means that disturbances will be modeled with random variable(s). A value of "Targeted" means that disturbances are being generated to include "Targeted as a classifier meant with a mathematical model meant to maximize supply chain disruption. It was decided to include a value before the initial review, due to specific sponsor interest. A value of "None/Other" shows that either no disturbances were modeled, or that the method was used differently enough from the other models used in the prior categories to be classified separately, but not common enough to get its own category.
