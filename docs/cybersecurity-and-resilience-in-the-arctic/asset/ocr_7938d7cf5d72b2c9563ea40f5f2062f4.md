- In 2019, a highly targeted attack by criminals on the Norway-based aluminum giantorsk Hydro forced a disruption in production in Europe and the US. The ransomware penetrated the company networks, exploiting the operating system's Active Directory of the company, and then synced up encryption across Hydro's geographical regions. Mainauxite and aluminum production processes were suffered while other parts, such as primary metal and rolled products, were stopped or switched to manual operations (Fiveash, 2019).
