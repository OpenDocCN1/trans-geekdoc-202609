**Abstract.** All services and most traditional services are now totally dependent on the digital environment. Few users are aware of the revolutionary nature of modern technology. Every day we use real-time access to digital services in our home country and social media (SoMe) to communicate with friends locally or elsewhere in the world. We can communicate in real time with text messages or even through real-time video feeds. People have the choice of millions of videos to watch anytime anywhere. Modern communications systems satellites and submarine cables system connect data centers and data networks of different continents, enabling real-time communications throughout the world. We can order different goods from all over the world, the pay electronically and have the goods delivered to our door. Companies use the same channels of communication for daily communications, sending invitations to tender and through banks in real time.

As a result of these developments, people and systems produce huge amounts of data needs to be processed and stored. However, the solutions for all of these new services are not yet in line with international standards and their connections to telecommunications and service networks are very diverse. Technically outdated solutions and new technologies are often used simultaneously. Future information and communication systems need to be designed and adapt to this challenging business environment where security and cybersecurity are constantly present. Each function has its own service and communication needs depending on the user group. These groups include design and maintenance staff, financial staff, telecom operators, service provider staff, service providers and administrators, indigenous peoples. migrate technology has a great strategy for society as submarine optic systems, and the same has remained poorly understood among the general population. This is especially true if we were to talk about the communication systems in the Arctic, which are a tempting target for hackers and state actors who seek to use undersea cables and nets that connect the continents to each other or to different parts of the Arctic communities.

**Keywords.** The Arctic, communication, cybercrime, submarine cables, hackers.
