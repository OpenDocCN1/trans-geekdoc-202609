Par and emergency enforcement resources are limited by the small number of individuals that are full Arctic residents. Additionally, local governments have to centralize and cut back resources to save money. For example, in Finland many the search and rescue response teams were recently centralized. This has taken a toll on local communities. Current rescue teams can be over 120 kilometers (~75 miles) away, making it difficult to provide prompt support and services in the case of an emergency. Recent policies have tried to focus economic endeavours in tourism long-term sustainability in order to minimize any negative impacts on the environment, local and traditional ways of life and culture heritage.[41].
