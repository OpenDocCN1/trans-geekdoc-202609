# Technology – Mobility in the Arctic region

Figure 11 illustrates the various transport opportunities in the Arctic region, including public transport (trains, buses, air transport, etc.). The citizens of the Arctic region need to know when various transport systems can be used, when the vehicles arrive and depart, where one can buy tickets, etc. These services must also be available to the citizens of the Arctic region today. Such public transport is even more important than in most regions the distances in the Arctic are so great and place to place is not easy. This means that a very good communication system is necessary to provide such services to the people there.

# Technology – the communication systems of the Arctic region (virtualization)

In the smart societies and cities of the future, the amount of information will increase exponentially, as people use their smart devices not only to send messages but also to send real-time videos, to watch movies, and so on which means that energy consumptions also increase exponentially. Everything will be done in real time. Therefore a great volume of storage space will be used, which in turn means more data. Retrieving data from different networks also involves high real-time requirements, and so data centers should be as close as possible to the users. Figure 16 shows the smart societies and cities of the future for one another. There is a high-level architectural description of this smart society, with large and data centers that are placed as close as possible to the users.

Similar structures will be used in the Arctic region, in Europe, and in Asia. Arctic, European, and Asian future smart societies and cities will be connected to each other by both old and new. Figure 17, submarine optical cables systems, fixed network systems, and satellite systems [24].

The Arctic region communication systems and their routes will be of great interest to hackers, terrorists, cyber-attackers, and state actors, as there will be a high volume of information transmitted, from Europe to Asia and vice versa [25].
