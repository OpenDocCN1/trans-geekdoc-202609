# 7. Additional Research Needed

Additional research is needed to advance resiliency links to the engineering practices
 for ICT systems. This hybrid approach would allow a broader view of how systems
engineering is leveraged to provide a more defensible, survivable, and resilient
system. It would also advance the necessary resiliency metrics beyond the data found in
this paper in providing relevant and practical measurement objectives to understand the
business impact of securing and providing resilient ICT systems.

# 8. Summary

Although there are several ICT frameworks to measure resiliency, each are proprietary
in requiring specific input variables with different purposes and intent within

generic framework that offers an easy to understand, common, and repeatable practices
to creating metrics along with additional research to advance resiliency
measurement techniques.
