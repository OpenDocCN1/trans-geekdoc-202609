Communications and creation have been intimately tied to life; not only because of the relevant concepts and ideas, but because processes of communication, and coordinated inevitably involve production of artifacts. Archaeological evidence suggests that human ancestors developed and used tools at least 3 million years ago (Thompson, 2015); the tasks, materials, fabrication techniques, and use all processes related to the respective locations. Millions of years later (290,000 - 700,000 years ago), art appeared on the scene (Corneau & Sutton, 2016); a phenomenon that accelerated social development, as it facilitated communication of ideas and imagination of things that could be (Harari, 2015).
