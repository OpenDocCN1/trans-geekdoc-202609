
# Cyber Security and Resilience of Smart Grid Infrastructure

**Abstract.** Smart Grids are an emerging technology promising significant changes in the economy and social sphere, transformation from a consumer of electricity to producing, sharing, and storing. With that, due to extreme conditions, cybersecurity is one of many challenges. This paper summarizes our previous work and findings from a working group at NATO Workshop on Governance for Cybersecurity and Resilience in the Arctic as it pertains to critical infrastructure. It aims to introduce some areas of smart grid security and forensics, and some aspects of developing a cyberforensic framework based on smart grid network data are also discussed.

**Keywords.** Cyber security, smart grid, infrastructure, forensics, blockchain.

## 1. Introduction

Historically, many communities and industries relied on diesel as their main energy and heat source. In recent years, there has emerged a tendency in energy security to transform from diesel-powered communities to renewable energy or wind-powered communities. Innovative technologies and modernized energy systems will provide alternative energy sources for communities and industries that have limited energy options. Related to this trend, the Arctic area is characterized by the following components:
- Warning (cooling)
- Smart grid and micro grid
- Smart metering
- Energy distribution

The majority of Arctic regions have begun to transform from being simply consumers of electricity to also producing and storing energy themselves. Table 1 summarizes information about electricity production by countries in the Arctic region.
