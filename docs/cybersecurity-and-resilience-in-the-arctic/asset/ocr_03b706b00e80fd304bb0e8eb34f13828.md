# A. Hummelholm/Threat Characterization for Various Layers of Infrastructure

# Section I. Objective and grouping of the paper

This study defines functional segments that can be used as a guide when outlining the necessary Arctic infrastructure, services, and architectures that form the Arctic Region Ecosystem. The architectural descriptions can be as accurate as needed, depending on the service needs of the region and the specific requirements of the service. Different architectures are also needed to develop the services as well as for cyber threat assessments. The Arctic Ecosystem architectures are defined, the dependencies, risks, vulnerabilities, and cyber threats can then be explored. This research will also look for dependencies on various activities and services that can be identified by risks and to carry out analyses. In addition, there are several vulnerabilities in various functions and services from which we can further identify and examine cyber threats. This study also seeks to define a model to facilitate threat assessments and the comparison of threats and to facilitate the analysis of threats in the Arctic Region. Recognizing the technical design criteria, we also need to take into account different type of threats as natural threats, accidental threats, and cyber-attacks. The results obtained through the model will facilitate the design and implementation of architectural solutions. The implemented model can help better assess the likelihood of future attacks on Arctic infrastructure, submarine optic cable systems, satellite systems, as well as threats to services in this region. Chapter 2 describes the ecosystems and future operating environments of the Arctic region, the submarine optic cables communications, mobile networks, satellite networks, high altitude platform systems (HAPS) networks, and data centers at a general level. Chapter 3 describes the natural threats, accidental threats, cyber-threats, dependency analyses, risks, risk analyses, and the models which can be used to make threat analyses for the Arctic region's infrastructure, submarine optic communications networks, and the network infrastructure and services they provide. Chapter 4 deals with the making and modelling of threat analyses, and Chapter 5 describes conclusions and areas for future work.
