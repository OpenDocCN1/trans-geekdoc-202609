# 6.6. Icelandic Arctic Policy

In September 2009 the Icelandic Ministry of Foreign Affairs published Iceland’s official position in regard to Arctic issues in the report titled “Island á norðluðum”—“Iceland in the High North)”. This report was followed up by a “A Parliamentary Resolution on Iceland’s Arctic Policy” which was adopted by the Icelandic Parliament, Althingi, in March 2011 [53]. From the report and parliamentary resolution, and six key highlights can be taken, alongside identification of twelve principles.
