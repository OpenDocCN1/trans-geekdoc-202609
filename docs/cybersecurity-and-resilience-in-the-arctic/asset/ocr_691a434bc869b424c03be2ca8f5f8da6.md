Over 90% of authors are from academic institutions and all but four papers were authored exclusively by academics. Many of these authors claim connection with industry in their papers (e.g. in data acquisition), yet direct authorship was separate from only one paper. Government perspective is even more lacking. Among the reviewed academic literature, university collaboration with government was more rare than academic collaboration, as government employees were credited in only two papers. While the reviewed authors were geographically diverse, over 80% of the reviewed authors came from Very High Development economies, including the EU, OECD, Russia, Taiwan and Iran (HDI > 0.8) (Jahan et al. 2016). Developing countries, with High or Medium Human Development, were represented by China, including Hong Kong, India, and Iran (HDI > 0.6) (Jahan et al. 2016). This suggests a limit in perspectives from authors whose economies and infrastructure are under develoveloped or developing.
