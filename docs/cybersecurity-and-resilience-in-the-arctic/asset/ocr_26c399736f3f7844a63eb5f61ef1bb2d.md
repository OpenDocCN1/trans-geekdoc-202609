Arctic communities will find the formula for their own resilience, but need
external help. As the Stockholm Resilience Center suggests (Arctic Council, 2016), the
new paradigm should foster local context and self-organization. The most useful
istance may come in the form of resources that catalyze community-based
collaboration, information sharing capabilities, and mentoring from entities with
common interests. In essence, the dynamic must shift from directive programs toward
entrepreneurial development in a newly empowered world.
