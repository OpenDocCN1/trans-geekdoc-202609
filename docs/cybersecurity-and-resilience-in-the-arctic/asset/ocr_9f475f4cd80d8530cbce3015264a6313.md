Supply chain performance and resilience are the product of the interactions of multiple networks, none of which failsafe or entirely controllable. A proper understanding of supply chain resilience will, therefore, require the ability to account for these effects. The field of supply chain literature must take steps to explicitly acknowledge and model this network inter-dependencies if the profession is to be able to accurately understand, measure, and improve supply chain resilience.
