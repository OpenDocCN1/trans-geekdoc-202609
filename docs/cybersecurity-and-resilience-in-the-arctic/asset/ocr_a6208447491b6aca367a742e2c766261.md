However, even with urgent incentives to develop capacity for operations and transport in the Arctic, Western nations remain fixed in a 19th mindset regarding how best to protect valuable and sensitive assets in a harsh and contested environment. Risk-based approaches of hardening vessels and equipment to natural hazards as well as mankind attack the shifting reality of systemic risk in the Arctic region. Such strategies work well for thoroughly understood and relatively simple hazards, yet fail to properly protect critical infrastructure and complex systems against compounding threats that leverage single points of failure and generate dispersed system losses.
