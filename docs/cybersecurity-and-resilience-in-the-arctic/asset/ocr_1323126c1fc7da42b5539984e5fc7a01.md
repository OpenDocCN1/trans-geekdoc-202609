The Arctic region has cities, villages, research stations, and satellite earth stations that need various services; these infrastructures must be connected to the communications systems in one or another. This infrastructure also requires electricity, water, smart lighting systems, waste systems, trains, trains, buses, airplanes, and ships. **Figure 11.** One of the large systems is logistics, which is needed on both the ground regions and in the sea portions of the region.
