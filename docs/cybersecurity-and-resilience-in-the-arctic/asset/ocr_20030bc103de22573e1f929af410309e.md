# 3.3.3. Geopolitical disruptions

Designing resilient under- and above-water systems and resilient cyber systems will go
a long way toward preparing for attacks from both enemy countries and non-
state actors, such as terrorists and pirates. Additionally, the Navy might focus on
increasing the resilience of its battle ships. One approach is to utilize control algorithms
that rapidly respond to "dynamic changes in conditions" in order to enable these systems
to continue attack even damaged by maintaining the "functionalities of the
eléct system, the chiller system, and the communication system on-board the"
183–184]. More research into the resilience of Navy battles, and all USN
vessels, is urgently needed. Many USN vessels are nearing the end of their lifecycle,
not degrade, and are unable to adapt or be based on recent
teological gains. In order to fulfill its objectives, maintain its superior strength, and
operate-effectively, the USN could design future vessels with resilience in mind
from the outset.
