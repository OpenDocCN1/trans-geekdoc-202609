# 2.3.2. Comparing Russian and U.S. Capabilities in the Arctic

The DoD Arctic Strategy (2018) identifies the “Joint Force’s eroding competitive edge against China and Russia” as the “central problem” which the DoD must address [16]. Wherever is the erosion of U.S. military advantages more evident than in the Arctic. In 2007, Russia planted its flag on the seabed under the North Pole, the United States would have been incapable of doing so [132]. As late as 2017, the United States had only “two barely icebreakers, no surface ships with the double hulls necessary to conduct polar operations” [132]. The United States has the strongest Navy of any NATO nation [186]; accordingly, this means that NATO as a whole is also well-equipped for Arctic operations.
