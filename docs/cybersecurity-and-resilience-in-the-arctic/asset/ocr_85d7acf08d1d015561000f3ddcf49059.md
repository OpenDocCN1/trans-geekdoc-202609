# 5. CONCLUSION

Although harsh conditions are hallmarks of the Arctic, the decreasing predictability and rapid transformations have place no historical analogy. Understanding and ultimately coping with uncertain and rapid change in the Arctic requires a process that maintains the integrity of diverse information threads while synthesizing a systems-level synthesis of relevant knowledge [40]. This enables stakeholders in the Far North to regain control despite system transformations by offering a path forward that both accepts and changes more resilient, desirable outcomes.
