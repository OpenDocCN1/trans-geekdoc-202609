But the representation of risks suppose the experienced operator of the system. He have to make the right decision based on complex risks representation as a set of numbers. It's not always possible.

National Academy of Sciences of Ukraine a different approach was proposed for solution of the problem. It based on colorimetry methods. The colorimetry methods are widely used in color television. And there was born an idea to formalize the level of risk using RGB color model.

It is necessary to remark that there are 256 values of each color in RGB model. There are 768 colors at all. There are 100 values to asses risk for each part of information security paradigm. There are 300 marks at all.

The first 23 (0 to 27) and the last 23 (228 to 255) are out of selection because it is more difficult to find the difference between the first 23 “shades of black” and the last 23 variants of RGB.

If we don't like to find difference between shades of grey we can use even and uneven values of colors.
