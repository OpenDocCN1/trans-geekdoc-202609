+   - ECDS (Electronic Chart Display System) supports the pre-planned routing of the ship (Deloitte 2017). It shows data to pre-planned routes, distance to run, isolated dangers; however, the computer used for ECDS may be the target of malware to replace the original chart with an erroneous one. This may result in the rerouting of the ship with a non-ideal route.
- GMDSS (Global Maritime Distress and Safety System) is used to broadcast the distress messages related to collision, flooding, sinking, fire explosion, etc.(CML 2015). It uses satellite links to exchange messages. Broadcasted messages consist of the ship's type, size, and MMSI number.
- Signals and data of GNSS (Global Navigation Satellite System) provide the correct position of the ship (CML 2015). GNSS data and signals can be spoofed, causing an attack on the ECDS system as well.
As mentioned above, the STPA method can be used to analyze the risks in the CyberShip framework is a risk and hazard analysis technique approach (Leesson 2011). It focuses on individual component failure as well as complex system interactions including humans in the loop. Systems fail when the safety or security constraints are violated. The focus is to enforce and security constraints on system behavior to defend the system from cyber attacks. The STPA analysis consists of five steps:

+   1. System identification and description: System goals, the boundaries of the system, and information flow between components of the system have to be specified.
