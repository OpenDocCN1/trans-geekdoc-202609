## Section 5. Conclusions and future work

The system to be built in the Arctic region is technically complicated and many new technical solutions will be needed to meet the required transmission rates, usability, and criteria. This places considerable demands on the management and control of the system as well as its maintenance. The long lifecycle of submarine optical cables, about 25 years, should be taken into account when developing submarine cable systems. Changes in social structures, which take very quickly also affect the implementations and operating models these changes must also be considered of developing submarine cable systems. The current powerful digitalization trend increases the range of services offered and facilitates their use. These developments also have a strong impact on the service chains for the provided services, including subcontractors with their subcontracting chains, the hardware providers, the service providers, and operating models in every part of the service chain on every continent.

And in the future, modern communications will connect data centers and different continents, enabling real-time communication throughout the world. This type of communications is made possible by undersea optical cable and satellite systems, which we use for daily communications. Because submarine cables systems have a large strategic impact on our society, they are also very tempting for hackers, cyber-attackers, terrorists, and malevolent state actors. They seek to gain access to the information that is transmitted through the networks on the continents are connected to each other by undersea cables. When considering security in these systems, we must take into account the development of upcoming technologies, which means there are more challenges ahead of us. In addition, changes in cable technology due to dispersion phenomena provide their own challenges in detecting intrusion into the cable system. As a result, we must exert the utmost care in their design.

## Future work

Because communication systems are so critical and will be used by a number of different countries, organizations, and people for their own purposes, it is essential to study the key issues affecting the functioning of such a system. In relation to cyber security, the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) needs to be investigated and its potential to protect submarine optical cables and satellite systems needs to be clarified in order to better protect such systems against malware and cyber-attacks.

- In relation to safety, Artificial Intelligence (AI) use needs to be investigated and its possibilities to protect submarine optical cable systems and satellite systems, in order to better protect from malware and cyberattacks.
- The use of OTDR should be investigated as it is used for searching for faults and can also be used for tapping via cable connections.
- We need to research the protection mechanisms for submarine optical cable systems, protecting submarine optical cable between continents is very important.
- Another study area would be different encryption systems, such as quantum encryption and/or Layer 1-2 encryption systems, for both submarine optical cable systems and satellite systems.
- An additional research area would be the virtualization of telecommunications networks and Data Centers, and what we need to consider when it comes to ensuring the cyber-security of services offered in the Arctic region in this kind of virtualized network.
