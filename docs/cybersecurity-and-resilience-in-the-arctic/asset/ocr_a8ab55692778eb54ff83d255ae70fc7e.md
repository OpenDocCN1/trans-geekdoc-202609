People who are working inside the buildings are using the same connections and systems of those IoT devices and sensors at the same time. When we look for security issues impacting these types of systems there are many security and cyber challenges. In those challenges are not only a question of the size of the building, but it is also coming to everybody's home, where there are new types of IoT devices and sensors installed. When people use medical devices in their home, there are many opportunities for interference between those devices and the home systems because they are using same frequencies. Nowadays, many patients are sent home with various sensors, so that healthcare staff can track their conditions. There are many different types of vulnerabilities in these systems, such that hackers and cyber-attackers can come to make attacks into different systems in the surrounding cities and society. One challenge will be in the future coming-energy buildings, because mobile communications systems do not work properly from outside to inside and versa. If we consider the Arctic regions, this situation is even more challenging because the connections are not as good and a patient's vital information must be seen by the hospital database in real time. The same situational also pertains to different types of critical systems in the Arctic region.
