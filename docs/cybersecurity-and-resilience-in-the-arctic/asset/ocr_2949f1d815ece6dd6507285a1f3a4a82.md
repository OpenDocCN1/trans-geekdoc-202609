# 3. Natural threats, accidental threats and cyber threats

Infrastructure related threats

Quite infrastructure management and control systems, like electric power stations, big buildings, and homes, are connected to the Internet. This kind of management and control system forms its own service network in the Internet, but security protections there may inadequate protection technology and this gives hackers and cyber-attackers ample opportunities to attack it. Many of the service operators of the real estate automation systems use the Internet to control their management systems to the real estate automation systems remotely, which are their responsibility. Vulnerabilities are more likely to help attackers to penetrate systems and thus cause harm to the systems which are used every day.

Long-distance electric power transmission lines are controlled by radio systems, which also involve security challenges. These control systems are also quite easy to disturb and provide an easy way for hackers to get inside the power supply management system to interfere with functions or to block the operation altogether.

# Communications System Threats

The communication systems in the Arctic are interconnected in one way or another in a international communications network, and this also allows connections to the continent's communications. These connections have been made, the Arctic communication systems are also targeted by hackers and cyber-attackers in a similar way to the rest of the world. Mobile technologies like 2G/3G/4G are used in the Arctic.

# The Threats Associated with Submarine Optical Cable Systems in the Arctic Region

In addition to the technical design criteria of the submarine optical cable systems, we also need to take into account the different types of threats that can occur, such as natural threats, accidental threats, and cyber or malicious threats. In this context we are looking mainly for threats against the submarine optical cable systems.
