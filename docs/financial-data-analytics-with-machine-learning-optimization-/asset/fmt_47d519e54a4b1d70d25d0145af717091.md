## 9.11 RECOMMENDER SYSTEM

A recommender system, commonly encountered in `Meta`, `Amazon`, and `Netflix`, helps predict the “rating” or “preference” that a subscriber would give to an item. It has become increasingly popular in recent years, and is utilized in a variety of areas including movies, music, news, books, research articles, search queries, social tags, and even groceries. Recommender systems can be used in marketing science research and business informatics to formulate a better business solution to maximize companies' profit. There are also recommender systems for entertainment enterprises, restaurants, garments, financial services, insurance products, romantic partner finding (online dating), and `X` (formerly `Twitter`) pages.

In the rest of this section, we shall provide the basic connection between the theory behind one of the commonly used recommender systems and PCA. To motivate this, imagine that we found a startup which provides online movie viewing. The company has attracted `i = 1, 2, ..., m` subscribers, and has also acquired `j = 1, 2, ..., n` movies. Each subscriber is invited to rate a movie after viewing with `r = 1, 2, ..., 5` (a continuum rating is certainly allowed) such that `r = 1` means the worst and `r = 5` means the best.
