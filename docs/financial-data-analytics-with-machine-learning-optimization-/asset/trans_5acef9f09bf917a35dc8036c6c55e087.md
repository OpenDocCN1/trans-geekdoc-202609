In the more general case beyond normal distribution, the method of `rejection sampling`, also known as the `acceptance-rejection method`, is commonly adopted; this method essentially dates back to the needle experiment by French mathematician `Comte de Buffon` in estimating the circumference-diameter ratio ![pi](images/c05-i0055.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>&#960;</mi></mrow></math>. We here only elaborate the case of univariate distribution, while the multivariate case is analogous.
