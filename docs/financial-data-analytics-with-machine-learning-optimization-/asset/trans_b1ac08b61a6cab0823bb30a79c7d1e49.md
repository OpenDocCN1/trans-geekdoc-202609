

By [Proposition 6.1](#c06.xhtml#c06-fea-0016)(iii), `N₂(s₁, s₂) < ∞` for every couple of `(s₁, s₂) ∈ 𝒮 × 𝒮`. Following a similar continuity argument to that in the proof for [Theorem 6.2](#c06.xhtml#c06-sec-0016), we can establish the existence of a finite collection of product open sets `{𝒮(sᵢ) × 𝒮(sⱼ′) : (sᵢ, sⱼ′) ∈ 𝒮(sᵢ) × 𝒮(sⱼ′)}` covering `𝒮 × 𝒮` such that `min_{(s, s′) ∈ 𝒮(sᵢ) × 𝒮(sⱼ′)} μ^(N₂(sᵢ, sⱼ′))(s | s′) > 0`. Denote the set of all these pairs `(sᵢ, sⱼ′)` by `𝒷 ⊆ 𝒮 × 𝒮`.
