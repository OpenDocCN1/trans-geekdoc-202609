
These considerations lead us to define upper tail comonotonicity (cf. [\[26\]](#c08.xhtml#c08-bib-0026)) as follows. A random vector `x` with a copula `C` and a survival copula `Ĉ` is said to be *upper tail comonotonic* if for any `w₁, …, w_d ∈ [0, ∞)`,

```
lim_{u ↓ 0} Ĉ(uw₁, …, uw_d) / u = min{w₁, …, w_d}.
```

Such a copula `Ĉ` is then said to be an *upper tail comonotonic copula*.

Upper tail comonotonicity is closely connected to the asymptotic additivity of Value-at-Risk. Consider a portfolio of `d` loss variables `x₁, …, x_d`. We aim to examine the Value-at-Risk of the aggregate loss `s = x₁ + … + x_d`. Suppose that for some `1 ≤ m ≤ d`, `x₁, …, x_m ∈ MDA(Φ_α)`

1. `x_j ∈ MDA(Φ_{α′})`⁶ with `α′ > α`;

2. `x_j ∈ MDA(Λ)`⁶;

3. `x_j` has a finite endpoint.

Under the above setting, our recent work [\[11\]](#c08.xhtml#c08-bib-0011) establishes that Value-at-Risk is asymptotically additive for such portfolios:

```
V@R_p(s) ≍ ∑_{i=1}^{d} V@R_p(x_i)  p ↑ 1.
```

Moreover, there are two other scenarios where such asymptotic additivity of Value-at-Risk remains true:

1. The non-negative random variables `x₁, …, x_m` are in `MDA(Λ)`⁶ with infinite endpoints and they are upper tail comonotonic, while other non-negative `x_{m+1}, …, x_d` have finite endpoints.

2. All the non-negative random variables `x₁, …, x_d` have finite endpoints and they are upper tail comonotonic.

## (VI) Archimedean Copulae

Archimedean copulae form an associative class of copulae. Explicit formulae are readily available for most commonly used Archimedean copulae, while this is not the case for other classes of copulae such as the commonly used Gaussian copula. Besides, Archimedean copulae can be easily used to model dependent risks in an arbitrarily high dimension, which is another reason of their popularity in practice.
