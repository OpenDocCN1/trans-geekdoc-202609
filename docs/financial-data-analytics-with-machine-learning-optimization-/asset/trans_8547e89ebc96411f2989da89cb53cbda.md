

Since `dk` and `(x^k Ω_k^⊥)^⊥ = Ω_k (x^k)^⊥` are both vectors, `dk x^k Ω_k^⊥` is a rank‐1 matrix, by virtue of the Eckart–Young–Mirsky Theorem for the Frobenius norm (Theorem 9.1), we can apply SVD to `E_k Ω_k^⊥` to get the closest rank‐1 matrix (in Frobenius norm) that approximates `E_k Ω_k^⊥`. Therefore the solution `dk` can be taken as the first column of `U`, and `x^k Ω_k^⊥` is the scalar product of `σ1` (the largest singular value in `Σ`) and the first column of `V`, where `E_k Ω_k^⊥ = U Σ V^⊥`. The steps above are then implemented iteratively for `k` from 1 to `K` in this codebook update stage. Then we move to the next epoch of the sparse coding step and then the codebook update step again and again until convergence. Notice that convergence is guaranteed by the fact that MSE is monotonically non‐increasing at each iteration.
