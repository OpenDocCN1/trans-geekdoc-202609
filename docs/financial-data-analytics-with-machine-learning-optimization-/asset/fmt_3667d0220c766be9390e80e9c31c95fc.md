
7. Since the sample mean of `uᵢ` is taken to be zero, the variance of `uᵢ` can be estimated by the sample mean of the squared returns `uᵢ²`'s. For these squared returns, some positive autocorrelation exists, meaning `Corr(uᵢ₊ₖ², uᵢ²) > 0` for small values of `k`.

8. A generic stochastic model for the return is `uᵢ = μᵢ + σᵢzᵢ`, where `zᵢ`'s are iid according to a distribution with a mean 0 and variance 1. A special case of this is `uᵢ = μδt + σ√(δt)zᵢ`, where `zᵢ`'s follow iid `N(0, 1)`, which is the celebrated Black–Scholes–Merton model.
