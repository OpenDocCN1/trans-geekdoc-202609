## 1.4 COMMON PROBABILITY DISTRIBUTIONS

In this section, we shall review some basic probability distributions learnt in elementary statistical courses. Readers may refer to [\[10](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0010), [13\]](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0013) for more a comprehensive treatment of this topic, and [\[1](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0001), [14](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0014), [17\]](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0017) for a more advanced discussion, and on tackling block matrices in particular. These distributions are important and serve as the building blocks for constructing and deriving the null distributions of many test statistics. Throughout the book, “iid” stands for “*independent and identically distributed*”, referring to an underlying family of random variables.
