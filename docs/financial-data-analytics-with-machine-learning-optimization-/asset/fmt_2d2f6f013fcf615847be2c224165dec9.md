If `A` is a real symmetric matrix, and `λᵢ` and `λⱼ` are two distinct eigenvalues of `A`, then the corresponding eigenvectors `hᵢ` and `hⱼ` are orthogonal; indeed, by definition, `Ahᵢ = λᵢhᵢ` and `Ahⱼ = λⱼhⱼ`, and therefore `hⱼᵀAhᵢ = λᵢhⱼᵀhᵢ` and `hᵢᵀAhⱼ = λⱼhᵢᵀhⱼ`. By the symmetry of `A`, `hⱼᵀAhᵢ = hᵢᵀAhⱼ`, hence `(λᵢ - λⱼ)hⱼᵀhᵢ = 0`. As `λᵢ ≠ λⱼ`, we must have `hⱼᵀhᵢ = 0`.
