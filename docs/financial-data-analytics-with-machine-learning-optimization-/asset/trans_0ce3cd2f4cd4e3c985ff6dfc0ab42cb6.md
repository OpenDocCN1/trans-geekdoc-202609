### 6.1.2 Application to Handwriting Recognition

In this subsection, we discuss an immediate application of the EM algorithm to the unsupervised learning of recognizing handwritten Arabic numbers. This can help us easily identify handwritten numerical figures found on cheques and business documents. The training dataset is a collection of labeled black-and-white images of handwritten digits from MNIST
