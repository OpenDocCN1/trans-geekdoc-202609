
We construct an artificial “trading horizon” of length of `T + 1` days starting from every trading day, and from now on, we call this artificial “trading horizon” an *examination window* to avoid ambiguity. Clearly, for a chosen trading day `s`, it is enclosed in a total of `T + 1` different examination windows, such that the `(j + 1)`-th examination window spans from day `s - T + j` to day `s + j`, for `j = 0, ..., T`. In the following, we explain what it meant by the *action period* in each examination window.
