## *5.4 GREEKS AND THEIR APPROXIMATIONS

Various sensitivities of derivatives (or portfolios) prices ![upper V](images/c05-i0510.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>V</mi></mrow></math>'s with respect to different model parameters, such as time ![t](images/c05-i0511.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>t</mi></mrow></math>, risk-free interest rate ![r](images/c05-i0512.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>r</mi></mrow></math>, current asset price ![s](images/c05-i0513.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>s</mi></mrow></math> and volatility ![sigma](images/c05-i0514.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>&#963;</mi></mrow></math>, are called *Greek letters* or simply *Greeks* for short: see [Table 5.6](#c05.xhtml#c05-tbl-0006) for some common examples.

For example, a fund manager may want to trade a portfolio that is insensitive to the (small) change of the current price of the underlying asset, that is, a portfolio with a value ![upper V](images/c05-i0520.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>V</mi></mrow></math> such that ![normal upper Delta equals StartFraction partial-differential upper V Over partial-differential upper S EndFraction equals 0](images/c05-i0521.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mi mathvariant="normal">&#916;</mi><mo>=</mo><mfrac><mrow><mi>&#8706;</mi><mi>V</mi></mrow><mrow><mi>&#8706;</mi><mi>S</mi></mrow></mfrac><mo>=</mo><mn>0</mn></math>. A portfolio with such a property is called a *delta neutral portfolio*. For instance, a *delta-neutral portfolio* can be formed by selling one European option and holding ![normal upper Delta equals StartFraction partial-differential upper V left-parenthesis normal upper S Subscript t Baseline comma t right-parenthesis Over partial-differential upper S EndFraction](images/c05-i0522.png)<math display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mi mathvariant="normal">&#916;</mi><mo>=</mo><mfrac><mrow><mi>&#8706;</mi><mi>V</mi><mo stretchy="false">(</mo><msub><mi mathvariant="normal">S</mi><mi>t</mi></msub><mo>,</mo><mi>t</mi><mo stretchy="false">)</mo></mrow><mrow><mi>&#8706;</mi><mi>S</mi></mrow></mfrac></math> amount of stocks, and this hedging portfolio will remain *riskless* throughout the period under consideration.
