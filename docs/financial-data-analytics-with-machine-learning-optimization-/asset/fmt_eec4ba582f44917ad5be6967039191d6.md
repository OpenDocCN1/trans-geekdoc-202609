
As discussed in [Chapter 8](#c08.xhtml), copulae can be used to model the general dependence structure of underlying feature variables. While many copulae are readily available for use, `CIBer` focuses on utilizing the comonotonic copula. A natural question for readers to raise at this point would be: why choose comonotonic copula over other alternatives? To address this, let us provide a comprehensive simulation study to justify the use of comonotonic copula as first choice.

We investigate the robustness of comonotonicity as a modelling copula in `CIBer`, by comparing `CIBer` against three other Archimedean copula‐based classifiers, namely Gumbel, Frank, and Clayton copulae.
