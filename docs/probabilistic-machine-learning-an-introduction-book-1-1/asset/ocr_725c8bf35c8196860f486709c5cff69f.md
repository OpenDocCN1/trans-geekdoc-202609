One small annoyance with using the IG(ã, b̃) distribution is that the strength of the prior is encoded in both ã and b̃. Therefore, in the Bayesian statistics literature it is common to use an alternative parameterization of the IG distribution, known as the (scaled) inverse chi-squared distribution:

χ⁻²(σ² | ν̃, τ̃²) = IG(σ² | ν̃/2, ν̃ τ̃²/2) ∝ (σ²)^{-ν̃/2 -1} exp(- ν̃ τ̃² / (2σ²))  (7.79)
