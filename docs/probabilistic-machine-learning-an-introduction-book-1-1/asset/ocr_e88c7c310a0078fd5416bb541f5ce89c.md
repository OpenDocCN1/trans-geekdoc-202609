The use of residual blocks allows us to train very deep models. For example, [He+16a] trained a 152 layer ResNet on ImageNet. The reason this is possible is that gradient can flow directly from the output to earlier layers, via the skip connections, for reasons explained in Sec. 13.4.4.

In [He+16b], they showed how a small modification of the above scheme allows us to train models with up to 1001 layers. The key insight is that the signal on the skip connections is still being attenuated due to the use of the nonlinear activation function after the addition step, x_{l+1} = φ(x_l + F(x_l)). They showed that the following kind of residual block works better:

x_{l+1} = x_l + F'_l(x_l) (14.18)
