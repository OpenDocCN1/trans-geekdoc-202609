Fig. 3.24(a) shows two value types: Surname and Cityname. Even if these appear as top-level types in an ORM schema diagram, it is possible that they overlap, as shown in the Euler diagram of Fig. 3.24(b). Here, the character strings “Denver” and “Columbus” are both surnames and city names. Although subtype relationships between value types are rarely displayed, they may exist implicitly. In rare cases where the supertype plays a role of interest, we might display the connections explicitly, as in Fig. 3.24(c).

Step 3 of the design procedure begins with a check to see if some entity types should be combined. At this stage we are concerned only with primitive entity types, not entity subtypes. So, if you spot some entity types that do overlap, you should combine them into a single entity type, or at least introduce a common supertype (see Chapter 6).
