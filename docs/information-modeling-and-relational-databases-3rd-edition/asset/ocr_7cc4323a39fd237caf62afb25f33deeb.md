Extensive discussions of HCI design are provided by Shneiderman (1992) and Dix et al. (1993). Extensive documentation on Microsoft products and technologies is accessible at https://learn.microsoft.com/en-gb/docs/. Enter “user interface guidelines” in the search field for Microsoft’s recommend UI guidelines.

Specifications for RDF, RDFS, and OWL may be found online at www.w3.org. For OWL 2, see https://www.w3.org/TR/owl-overview/. A standard reference on description logics is Baader et al. (2003). For discussion of mapping ORM to OWL and description logics, see Keet (2007). For an example of an ORM-based ontology tool, see Trogl et al. (2006). Our discussion of ontological modeling and the semantic web is largely drawn from a 15-part series of articles by Halpin on ontological modeling (see www.orm.net/resources). This series includes many OWL examples in Turtle syntax as well Manchester syntax.

For a comparison of metamodels for ORM, Barker ER, and UML, see Halpin (2004b). A metamodel for UML 2 is contained in the online UML specifications accessible at https://www.omg.org/spec/UML. Click on the abstract syntax sections of the most recent version to see diagrams for various parts of the metamodel.
