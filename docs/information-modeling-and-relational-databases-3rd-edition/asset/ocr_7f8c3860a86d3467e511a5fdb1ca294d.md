# 3.6 Summary

The following criteria are desirable characteristics for any language used for conceptual modeling: expressibility, clarity, simplicity, orthogonality, semantic stability, semantic relevance, validation mechanisms, abstraction mechanisms, and formal foundation.

ORM was designed with these criteria in mind. In particular, its omission of the attribute concept from base models leads to greater stability and simplicity while facilitating validation through verbalization and population.

With large-scale business domains, the UoD is divided into convenient modules, the CSDP is applied to each, and the resulting subschemas are integrated into the global conceptual schema. The CSDP itself has seven steps, of which the first three were discussed in this chapter.
