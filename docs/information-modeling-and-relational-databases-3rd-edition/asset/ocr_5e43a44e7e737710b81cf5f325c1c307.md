Of the table operations covered, only five are primitive (i.e., cannot be defined in terms of the other operations). While some choice exists as to which to consider primitive, the following list is usual: U; -; X; selection; and projection. The other three (n, ><, and +=) may be defined in terms of these five operations (the proof is left as an exercise).
