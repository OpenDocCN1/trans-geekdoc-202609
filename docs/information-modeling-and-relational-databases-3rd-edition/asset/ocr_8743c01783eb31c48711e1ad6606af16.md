Some of the tours have a fixed sequence of sites to visit, as shown in the figure. Here the circled numbers are stop numbers, indicating the order in which the tour bus stops at a site. No tour may visit the same site more than once. Sites are primarily identified at a site code (e.g., “MW”), but also have a unique name (e.g., “Movie World”). Although site codes are not displayed in the figure, they are recorded in the information system.

A site may have zero or more facilities of interest. In the figure, these facilities are displayed as icons. In the information system, however, each facility is instead identified primarily by a facility code (e.g., “DN”) and has a unique name (e.g., “Dining”). The list of facility codes and names may include facilities not yet provided by any tour site (e.g., Horseback Riding). The code, name, and facilities of other potential tour sites may be recorded even if those sites are not yet included on a tour. For example, we might record “SB” and “South Bank” for Brisbane’s magnificent South Bank.
