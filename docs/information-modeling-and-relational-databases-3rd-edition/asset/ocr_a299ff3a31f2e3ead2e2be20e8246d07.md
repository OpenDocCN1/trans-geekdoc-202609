Exclusive unaries provide the simplest case of this. For example, suppose that workers in a company hold at most one position (manager, clerk, or secretary). This may be rephrased using exclusive unaries, as shown in Fig. 14.7, assuming appropriate translations between the predicates. For implementation, the binary version is usually preferred (e.g., its relational schema simplifies both updates to a worker's position, and schema updates to the list of allowable positions). The larger the number of unaries, the worse the unary solution becomes (why?).
