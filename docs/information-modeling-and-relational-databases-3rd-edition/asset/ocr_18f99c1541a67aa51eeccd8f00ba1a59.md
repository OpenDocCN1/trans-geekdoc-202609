To illustrate some of these points, we can look at an example process model. Fig. 15.40 illustrates a simple complaints process using BPMN. After recording the complaint, the client is contacted to acknowledge receipt and the relevant department is contacted to assess the complaint. After assessment, the client is either compensated in some way or a standard letter is sent to explain the resolution. Details of the case are filed in case of any future query, and the process terminates.
