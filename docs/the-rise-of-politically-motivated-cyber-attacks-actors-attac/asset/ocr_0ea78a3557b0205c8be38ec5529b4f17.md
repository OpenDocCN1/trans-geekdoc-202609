Over the years, the units have conducted several high-profile attacks with a limited level of operational security and secrecy. A part of the operations of the units is to create uncertainties about their capabilities and capacities. The publicity the groups are getting is a part of the game to establish Russia as a leading cyberpower (NCSC, 2018; US Congress, 2021).
