# 01 The Business of AI: Strategies for Leaders – London Business School

This 10-week online course from London Business School empowers executives and senior managers to unlock the true business value of artificial intelligence. Designed and delivered by world-class faculty, the program guides leaders through the strategic operational, and organizational challenges of AI adoption, equipping them to drive transformation and sustainable growth.

Unlike many technology-focused AI courses, this program centers on value creation and strategic implementation within real business contexts. Participants benefit from LBS’s global reputation, peer network of international leaders, and a hands-on approach that culminates in the development of a tailored AI implementation plan for their own organization.

# Who Should Take This Course

+   - C-suite executives and senior leaders seeking to drive business transformation with AI
+   - Mid-to-senior-level managers responsible for functional or business unit performance
+   - Business heads, strategists, and innovation leaders aiming to leverage AI for competitive advantage
+   - Consultants and advisors guiding clients through digital transformation

# Key Takeaways for Learners

+   - Gain a solid understanding of AI and machine learning advantages and limitations in business
+   - Learn to identify, evaluate, and communicate AI opportunities for your organization
+   - Develop frameworks for addressing data infrastructure and talent management challenges
+   - Explore the strategic impact of generative AI and other emerging technologies
+   - Build and present a comprehensive AI implementation plan tailored to your business needs

# Prerequisites and Learner Background

+   - No coding or deep technical expertise required
+   - Designed for professionals with 8+ years of management or leadership experience
+   - Ideal for those comfortable with business strategy, digital initiatives, or organizational change
+   - Curiosity about technology, data, and innovation is beneficial

# Benefits to the Business

+   - Enables leaders to make smart, informed decisions about AI investments and strategy
+   - Equips teams to identify and pursue automation and optimization opportunities
+   - Reduces risk by clarifying ethical, data, and talent considerations in AI adoption
+   - Supports innovation and competitiveness through exposure to global best practices and case studies

# Course Structure and Content Review

+   - Duration: 10 weeks, 4–5 hours per week
+   - Format: Self-paced online learning with pre-recorded faculty lectures, curated content, assignments, and select live sessions
+   - Support: Dedicated Learning Manager and access to a global peer network
