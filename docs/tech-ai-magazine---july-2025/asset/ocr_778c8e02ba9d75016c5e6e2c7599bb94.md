# Essential Packing Categories

# CLOTHING (Layered for Variable Weather)

+   **Base Layers (Moisture-wicking):**
+   * 2-3 thermal tops (merino or synthetic)
+   * 2 thermal leggings or long underwear

+   **Mid Layers (Insulation):**
+   * 2 fleece or wool sweaters
+   * 1 lightweight insulated jacket (down/synthetic)

+   **Outer Layers (Wind/Waterproof):**
+   * 1 waterproof shell jacket with hood
+   * 1 pair of waterproof hiking pants
+   * 1 warm parka (especially for Northern Finland)

+   **Everyday Wear:**
+   * 3-4 long-sleeve shirts or turtlenecks
+   * 2 pairs of jeans or warm pants
+   * 1-2 casual outfits for restaurants/museums
+   * 1 set of clothes for indoor lounging (e.g., leggings, joggers)

+   **Undergarments & Socks:**
+   * 7-10 pairs of underwear
+   * 4-5 pairs of wool or thermal socks
+   * Sleepwear (warm)

# FOOTWEAR

+   * Waterproof hiking boots (already broken in)
+   * Warm insulated boots (for colder nights/Northern lights viewing)
+   * Light shoes or sneakers (for indoor use or casual days)
+   * Flip-flops or sandals (for sauna use)

# SPECIALIZED GEAR

# For Hiking:

+   * Trekking poles (optional but helpful)
+   * Trail map or GPS (offline navigation apps like Maps.me or Gaia GPS)
+   * Headlamp with extra batteries (daylight is limited)
+   * Microspikes (optional; for slippery trails or early snow in Lapland)
+   * Reusable rain cover for backpack

# For Northern Lights Viewing:

+   * Tripod (for long-exposure photography)
+   * Remote shutter or timer (avoid camera shake)
+   * Extra camera batteries (they drain faster in cold)
+   * Red light flashlight (preserves night vision)
+   * Thermal blanket or sitting pad (while waiting in the cold)

# TOILETRIES & HEALTH

+   * Travel-sized shampoo, conditioner, soap
+   * Toothbrush, toothpaste, floss
+   * Moisturizer & lip balm (cold, dry air)
+   * Sunscreen (snow glare or sunny days)
+   * Basic first-aid kit (bandages, ibuprofen, blister plasters)
+   * Personal medication (with prescriptions)
+   * Hand sanitizer & tissues

# TECH & DOCUMENTS

+   * Smartphone with offline maps
+   * Power bank (cold drains batteries quickly)
+   * Plug adapter (Finland uses Type F plugs, 230V)
+   * Charging cables
+   * Travel documents: passport, visa (if needed), travel insurance
+   * Copies of bookings (digital & printed)
+   * Debit/credit cards and small amount of cash (Euros)
