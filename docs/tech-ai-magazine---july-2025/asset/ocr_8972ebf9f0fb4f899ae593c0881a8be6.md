# # 03 AI for Business Innovation: Beyond Generative AI – Imperial College Business School

Visit Website

AI for Business Innovation: Beyond Generative AI is a 6-week, fully online executive program from Imperial College Business School designed to empower business leaders to strategically integrate AI, including Gen AI, into their organizations. Delivered by renowned Imperial faculty, the course equips participants with practical frameworks, real-world applications, and the strategic mindset needed to drive innovation and operational excellence in the AI era.

![](bbox=[0.5, 0.22, 0.945, 0.395])

This program stands out for its blend of cutting-edge academic insight and business-focused practicality, led by Professor Christopher L. Tucci, a recognized expert in digital strategy and innovation. The curriculum is uniquely structured to move beyond AI theory, emphasizing hands-on application, strategic integration, and ethical considerations, all while leveraging Imperial's global reputation for business technology and entrepreneurship.

+   ### Key Takeaways for Learners
+   - Understand the fundamentals and business applications of AI and generative AI.
+   - Learn to identify, prioritize, and evaluate AI opportunities for ROI and scalability.
+   - Develop frameworks for ethical, legal, and governance considerations in AI adoption.
+   - Gain exposure to real-world AI use cases and practical implementation strategies.
+   - Build an actionable roadmap for AI-driven innovation in your organization.

+   ### Who Should Take This Course
+   - Mid-to-senior level executives in strategy, operations, innovation, IT, marketing, or product management.
+   - Business leaders planning or undergoing digital or AI transformation.
+   - Consultants and advisors seeking practical, business-centric AI knowledge.
+   - Investors and entrepreneurs exploring AI-driven ventures.

+   ### Benefits to the Business
+   - Enables leaders to embed AI readiness and innovation into business models
+   - Equips teams to identify automation and optimization opportunities
+   - Reduces risk through a deeper understanding of AI governance and ethical best practices
+   - Supports sustainable growth and competitiveness by showcasing successful AI use cases and trends

+   ### Prerequisites and Learner Background
+   - No coding or deep technical background required.
+   - Ideal for professionals comfortable with business frameworks, strategic planning, or digital initiatives.
+   - Curiosity about data, technology disruption, or innovation is helpful.
