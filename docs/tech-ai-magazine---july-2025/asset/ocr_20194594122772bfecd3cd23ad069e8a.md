The course equips participants with actionable strategies to harness AI for business growth, blending academic rigor with real-world applications across industries and enterprise functions.

This program stands out for its focus on bridging the gap between business strategy and technical execution, empowering participants to act as effective liaisons between business leaders and data scientists. Led by renowned Kellogg faculty, the course emphasizes responsible AI, ethical considerations, and organizational readiness, providing a holistic framework for driving successful AI initiatives.
