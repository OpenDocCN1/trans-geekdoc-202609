# # 01. Notion AI

Based on the well-loved workspace platform, Notion AI integrates smart ideas and automation right into your existing workflows. It can assist in generating content, summarizing files, organizing tasks, and even building databases, which can be an asset for teams looking to distill their information into one location and simplify daily routines. Its simplicity of use within a widely adopted package for productivity makes it much more appealing.

![](bbox=[0.37, 0.112, 0.928, 0.3])

# why is this relevant:

Notion has progressed from being a basic note-taking application to become a one-stop productivity center. With Notion AI, it can now provide context-aware automation, making static documents dynamized resources. For instance, it can:

+   - Auto-generate project briefs from scattered notes.
+   - Summarize lengthy research into bullet points for execs.
+   - Build relational databases from natural language prompts (e.g., "Create a CRM tracker for our sales pipeline").

> **Useful For**
> Remote teams, knowledge-heavy roles (e.g., consulting, legal), and startups scaling documentation.

# # 02. Gumloop

Gumloop offers a modern approach to workflow automation by combining the flexiblity of Zapier (a platform for automation, integration, and AI orchestration manage workflows) with the intelligence of large language models. Businesses can connect any major LLM (such as GPT-4 or Claude) to internal tools and automate tasks without writing code. Notably, Gumloop provides built-in access to premium AI models, a clean interface, and continuous agents that monitor and act on new data in real time and ideal for sales, research, and admin automation.
