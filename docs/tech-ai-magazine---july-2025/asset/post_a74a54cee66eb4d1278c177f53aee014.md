## 6 Best AI's to Watch This Month

![](img/e32591b2b2e7d31a7d021e1ef3c31ee9_7_0.png)

Artificial intelligence has evolved from buzzwords to business needs. Today's AI tools are transforming how companies operate, automating everything from routine tasks to complex code generation. From doing mundane tasks to writing sophisticated code, AI software is becoming the norm for teams looking to stay in the game. What are they worth looking into? They're not just thought experiments; they're real-world, sophisticated, and already creating buzz in their respective niches. Let's take a look.

### 01. Notion AI

Based on the well-loved workspace platform, Notion AI integrates smart ideas and automation right into your existing workflows. It can assist in generating content, summarizing files, organizing tasks, and even building databases, which can be an asset for teams looking to distill their information into one location and simplify daily routines. Its simplicity of use within a widely adopted package for productivity makes it much more appealing.

![](img/e32591b2b2e7d31a7d021e1ef3c31ee9_8_0.png)

### why is this relevant:

Notion has progressed from being a basic note-taking application to become a one-stop productivity center. With Notion AI, it can now provide context-aware automation, making static documents dynamized resources. For instance, it can:

- Auto-generate project briefs from scattered notes.
- Summarize lengthy research into bullet points for execs.
- Build relational databases from natural language prompts (e.g., "Create a CRM tracker for our sales pipeline").

> **Useful For**
> Remote teams, knowledge-heavy roles (e.g., consulting, legal), and startups scaling documentation.

### 02. Gumloop

Gumloop offers a modern approach to workflow automation by combining the flexibility of Zapier (a platform for automation, integration, and AI orchestration manage workflows) with the intelligence of large language models. Businesses can connect any major LLM (such as GPT-4 or Claude) to internal tools and automate tasks without writing code. Notably, Gumloop provides built-in access to premium AI models, a clean interface, and continuous agents that monitor and act on new data in real time and ideal for sales, research, and admin automation.

![](img/e32591b2b2e7d31a7d021e1ef3c31ee9_8_1.png)

### why is this relevant:

Gumloop bridges the gap between Zapier's connectivity and LLM intelligence. Unlike traditional automation tools, it lets users:

- Deploy AI agents to monitor CRM updates and trigger personalized follow-ups.
- Auto-extract insights from support tickets using Claude or GPT-4.
- Build real-time dashboards that analyze sales calls and flag churn risks.

**Useful For**
Revenue operations (RevOps), customer support, and data-driven teams.

### 03. Tabnine

Website

![](img/e32591b2b2e7d31a7d021e1ef3c31ee9_9_0.png)

Tabnine is built for developers who prioritize both productivity and security. Tabnine works seamlessly with popular IDEs, providing real-time code completion, automated code generation from natural-language comments, and adaptive learning personal coding styles. Tabnine's SOC-2 compliance guarantees high privacy and security standards, making it perfect for enterprise environments.

### why is this relevant:

While GitHub Copilot garners headlines, Tabnine concentrates on enterprise-level security (SOC-2 compliant) and customized code recommendations. It:

- Learns user coding styles to minimize context-switching.
- Produces boilerplate code from comments (e.g., “Create a React form with validation”).
- Works with private repos without exposing sensitive information.

**Useful For**
Fintech, healthcare, and regulated industries where code privacy is critical.

### 04. GetGenerative.ai

GetGenerative.ai focuses on leveraging generative AI to streamline various aspects of the software development lifecycle, particularly within enterprise settings. Users have noted its ability to quickly generate design proposals, user stories, and epics, significantly reducing manual effort in project planning and development. Its focus on practical application for complex enterprise software initiatives makes it stand out as a valuable tool for development teams.

![](img/e32591b2b2e7d31a7d021e1ef3c31ee9_10_0.png)

### why is this relevant:

This tool tackles the planning and deployment dilemma in software projects by:

- Auto-generating user stories from stakeholder interviews.
- Converting wireframes into functional specs (e.g., “Turn this Figma design into React components”).
- Flagging scope gaps in Agile sprints using historical project data.

> Useful For
> Product managers, enterprise DevOps, and agencies juggling complex client projects.

### 05. Saleshandy

Saleshandy is an AI-powered cold outreach software. It automates and personalizes email campaigns using AI to discover potential leads and tailor messages according to an Ideal Customer Profile (ICP). The software seeks to maximize the effectiveness of lead generation, especially for sales agencies and small to medium enterprises seeking to increase reach.

![](img/e32591b2b2e7d31a7d021e1ef3c31ee9_10_1.png)

### why is this relevant:

Saleshandy's AI does more than generic email sequences. It:

- Screens LinkedIn profiles to customize messaging (e.g., “Reference their recent post on sustainability”).
- Uses historical engagement data to predict the likelihood of responding.
- A/B tests subject lines in real-time with GPT-4.

Useful For B2B sales teams, recruiters, and bootstrapped startups.

### 06. Krisp

Online conferencing is an everyday part of modern business, and background noise can be a big distraction. Krisp provides AI noise cancellation, as well as transcription, note-taking, and recording. Installed on multiple platforms, it is meant to make online meetings more productive by providing clean audio and full documentation, so participants can pay attention to what is being said, not background noise.

### why is this relevant:

Krisp isn’t just noise cancellation; it’s a meeting intelligence platform. Beyond crystal-clear audio, it:

- Generates searchable transcripts with speaker attribution.
- Flags action items (e.g., “Joel to send proposal by Friday”).
- Integrates with Zoom/Teams to auto-join and record calls.

![](img/e32591b2b2e7d31a7d021e1ef3c31ee9_11_0.png)

Useful For Consultants, async-first companies, and anyone drowning in back-to-back calls.

### Final thoughts

These solutions represent a snapshot of the evolving AI landscape, offering practical solutions to a range of business problems. The question for businesses in 2025 isn’t whether or not to adopt AI, but pairings of which will unlock their own potential. As AI continues to evolve, its application to everyday business processes promises further efficiencies along with new avenues for growth.
