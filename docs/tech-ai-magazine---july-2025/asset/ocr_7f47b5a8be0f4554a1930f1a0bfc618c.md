# # 05 Artificial Intelligence for Business – Wharton Executive Education, University of Pennsylvania

The “Artificial Intelligence for Business” program from Wharton Executive Education offers a comprehensive exploration of how AI, big data, machine learning, and generative AI can be strategically deployed within an organization. This course, designed by Professor Kartik Hosanagar of the Wharton AI & Analytics Initiative, provides real-life examples and case studies to help business professionals understand and apply AI effectively.

What sets this program apart is its focus on the practical deployment of AI, moving beyond theoretical concepts to address how businesses can truly leverage AI for competitive advantage. It delves into the ethics and risks of AI, and how to design robust governance frameworks for proper implementation, which is crucial for responsible AI adoption.

# ## Key Takeaways for Learners

+   - Develop a fundamental understanding of big data, AI, machine learning, and generative AI.
+   - Learn to differentiate between various machine learning applications and their business relevance.
+   - Understand how to strategically implement AI initiatives within an organization.
+   - Explore the ethical implications and risks associated with AI in business management.
+   - Gain insights into the transformative potential of generative AI for productivity and innovation.

# ## Who Should Take This Course

+   - Business leaders and executives seeking to gain a strategic edge through AI.
+   - Managers and professionals responsible for understanding and applying AI in their roles.
+   - Individuals interested in the potential of generative AI for creativity and innovation.
+   - Those looking to develop a portfolio approach to AI projects within their firms.

# ## Benefits to the Business

+   - Equips decision-makers with the tools to implement AI-driven solutions.
+   - Enhances decision-making processes through AI-powered insights.
+   - Supports digital transformation by clarifying how AI can be integrated into existing strategies.
+   - Fosters responsible AI practices through a focus on ethics and governance.

# ## Prerequisites and Learner Background

+   - No specific technical background or coding skills are required.
+   - Ideal for professionals across various industries who want to understand AI’s business impact.
+   - A willingness to engage with real-world business scenarios and case studies.
