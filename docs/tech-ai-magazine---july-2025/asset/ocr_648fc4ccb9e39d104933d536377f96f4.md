# 1. Ecovacs Deebot X9 Pro Omni Robot Vacuum

The Deebot X9 Pro Omni redefines what a flagship robot cleaner can do. It marries powerful BLAST suction designed to deepen airflow beyond typical models with a self-washing OZMO roller mop, all coordinated through intelligent sensors and AI logic. Add to that an automatic docking station that washes, dries, and even refills its own mop and dustbin, and you’ve got a self-sufficient cleaning system that adapts to both carpets and hard floors with impressive finesse.

# 2. Wyze Bulb Cam Outdoor Security Camera

This clever gadget takes the hassle out of outdoor security. It merges a bright 800-lumen LED bulb with a 2K security camera, fitting neatly into any standard E26 light socket. By turning your porch light into a camera, Wyze delivers smart surveillance without new wiring or battery hassles. The automations, motion-triggered lighting, and integration with Alexa and Google Home make it a seamless addition to your smart home setup.
