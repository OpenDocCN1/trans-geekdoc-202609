Blockchain has been the most hyped technology in the last decade. Though blockchain technology is being overhyped, it has the potential to disrupt many existing industries. Start-ups, moreover, and even governments are already experimenting with blockchain-based applications.

Blockchain has crept into our daily life as we are bombarded with social media, web portals and advertisements. As a result, nearly everyone is talking about cryptocurrency today, most people still do not understand blockchain, the technology that powers cryptocurrencies. Therefore, I have written this book with hope of empowering everyone. This book is a comprehensive guide covering fundamental and advanced topics such as:

+   - Storing your cryptocurrencies security in crypto wallets
- Smart contracts
- dApps
- Enterprise blockchain frameworks like Hyperledger and Corda
- How to conduct ICO and IPO
- DeFi
- Blockchain for financial services
- Blockchain for supply chain management

Building a digital government with blockchain also covers technical topics such as:
- Improving scalability with Plasma
- Storing blockchain data on IPFS
- Writing smart contracts with Solidity
- Developing DApps
-Developing ERC-20 tokens
- Setting up a private Ethereum blockchain
- Creating a multisig wallet
- Creating an automotive supply chain management blockchain platform with Hyperledger Fabric
