# 4.2.3 Centralized Wallet

Centralized wallets, also called escrow wallets, are divided into two types:

+   - **Online wallet**: Generally designed as a web page, while keeping your private key. Such wallets have a higher risk, because storing your private key is equivalent to storing the cryptocurrency assets under your private key. One example is Coinbase.

+   - **Exchange Wallet**: Each exchange will also provide the wallet address corresponding to the cryptocurrency. This type of wallet keeps the private keys of the users, funds can only be withdrawn by the exchange mechanism. Examples are Binance, Huobi, Luno and more.
