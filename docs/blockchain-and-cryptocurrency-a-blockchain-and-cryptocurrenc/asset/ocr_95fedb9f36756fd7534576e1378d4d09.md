One of the most famous Ethereum-based DApps is CryptoKitties (https://www.cryptokitties.co/). CryptoKitties is a game centered around breedable, collectible, and adorable creatures that are known as CryptoKitties. Each cat is unique and is 100% owned by an owner; it cannot be replicated, taken away, or destroyed. The game is so popular that it congested the Ethereum Mainnet in December 2017, causing it to reach an all-time high in number of transactions and slowing it down the network significantly.
