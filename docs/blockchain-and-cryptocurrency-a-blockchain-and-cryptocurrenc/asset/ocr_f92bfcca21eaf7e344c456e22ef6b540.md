# 11.1 National Digital ID Blockchain Net

The first step in creating a digital government is to create a
National Digital ID Blockchain Net. I propose a National Digital ID
Blockchain Net that implements Ethereum Parity's Proof of
Authority (PoA) Consensus Protocol, hosted on the National
Registration Department (NRD) cloud server. This should be a
private permissioned blockchain.
