# 8.5 Over-the-Air Results

The first over-the-air tests were performed during the IEEE Conference on Dynamic Spectrum Access Networks (DySPAN) in April of 2007 in Dublin, Ireland [1]. At the conference, a number of participating companies and research labs set up and ran their cognitive radio and dynamic spectrum access equipment specifically licensed by Ireland’s Communicationsulators (COMREG). We set up two of our radio nodes at the conference using the GNU Radio SDR platform with USRP RF ends, a PSD sensor, and the WSGA optimizer.
