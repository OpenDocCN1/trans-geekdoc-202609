Figure 5.9 shows a solution space for bit error rate and power. The optimization goals are to produce a waveform with low BER and low transmit power. Figure 5.9 shows five solutions, labeled $A$ through $E$, resulting from the availability of three different modulations, BPSK, 8PSK, and QAM16, under SNR conditions of 0 to 12 dB. With the two objectives listed, solutions $A$, $B$, and $C$ fall on the Pareto front any improvement in BER would cause an increase in transmit power. Solutions $D$ and $E$, in this case, be selected based on the optimization criteria, but not all represent certain properties not specified in the optimization function definition. For instance, BER and power are objectives, the quality of service would suffer from a higher BER than a higher transmit power, the choice is more narrow between solutions $B$ and $C$ and depends on what property ranges higher; the 1.5 dB increase would the decrease in BER from $10^{-5}$ to $2 	imes 10^{-7}$? If a third objective such as throughput were added to the optimization problem, solutions $D$ and $E$ are now contenders since they dominate the BPSK modulation scheme in this objective. Again, selection from this set is based on quality of service goals by how much importance is given to data rate, power consumption, and BER.
