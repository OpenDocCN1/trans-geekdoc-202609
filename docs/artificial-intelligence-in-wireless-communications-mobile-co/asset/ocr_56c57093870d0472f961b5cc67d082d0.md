# # 1 Introduction to Cognitive Radio

The current methods of communications are becoming less relevant under today’s growing demand for and reliance on constant connectivity. Of decreasing relevance to customers are the models of a single radio to perform a single task. The expansion of wireless access points among coffee shops, airports, malls, and other public arenas is opening up opportunities for new services and applications. In the data market, new technologies like the IEEE 802.16 WiMAX standard are being deployed, and mobile phone companies are offering services to customers for connectivity over their networks without reliance on a WiFi access point. With current mobile phone access increasing worldwide and the Third Generation Partnership Project (3GPP) Long Term Evolution (LTE) project rapidly developing to a full standard, mobile phone companies are seriously competing in the wireless data market.

It is in these choices that we see for more intelligent decisionmaking from our devices. And this concept does not stop with the choice between standards; the complexity of the standards themselves is growing. While WiFi devices manage their very few choices based on simple connection quality metrics, WiMAX and LTE include many waveform choices with highly complex interactions and consequences to the device behavior.
