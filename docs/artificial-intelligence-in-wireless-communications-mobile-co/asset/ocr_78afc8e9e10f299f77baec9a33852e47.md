# Definitions

$f_e$ = center frequency of waveform (Hz)
$I(f)$ = interference spectral power density at frequency $f$ from interference map
$B$ = bandwidth calculated as in Section 4.2.2 (Hz)

The interference power is calculated over a given bandwidth in (4.16). From a practical standpoint, the interference spectral power density, $I(f)$, will probably be measured over a set bandwidth by the receiver, anyway. So (4.16) would be reduced to a summation of the interference power over an observed bandwidth, such as during a spectrum sweep period of the cognitive radio.

```
$I_{pwr} = 10\log_{10} \left(\frac{1}{B} \int_{f_e-B/2}^{f_e+B/2} I(f)df\right) \quad (dBm) \quad (4.16)$
```
