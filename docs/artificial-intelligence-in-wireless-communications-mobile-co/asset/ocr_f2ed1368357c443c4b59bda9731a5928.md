# 2.2 Cognitive Engine Design

In order to better explain the operation of a cognitive engine, we now expand the discussion by introducing some specifics of our implementation.

To develop the cognitive capabilities of Figure 2.2, Figure 2.3 presents an architecture of the cognitive engine. It includes a central component called the cognitive controller that acts as the system kernel and scheduler to handle the input/output and timing of the other attached components. The other major components include:

**Sensors:** collect radio/environmental data.

**Optimizer:** given an objective and environment, create an optimized waveform.
