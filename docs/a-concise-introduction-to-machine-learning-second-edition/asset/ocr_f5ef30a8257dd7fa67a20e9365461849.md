# Foreword

In the panic and excitement around the advent of chat bots based on generative AI, it can be difficult to understand how to react. But this technology is not really artificial intelligence; it is a reflection of our natural intelligence delivered by machine learning. It is an emulation of our intelligence. The field of machine learning has its fundamental roots in the technologies of the Second World War when radar detection of planes. In that domain operators struggled to separate the responses they received into signal and the noise. Today's reader may also be forgiven for feeling they face the same challenge when it comes to understand AI.

So how should we proceed? One way of cutting the noise is to return to first principles. The character of the noise can be exposed when we remind ourselves of mathematical and statistical foundations of the methods, and we use those principles to support us when we are being distracted by the shifters, grifters, and changers that emerge in the wake of such exciting new technology.

Artificial intelligence technologies will be transformed for our society, but not in the manner that the confident proclaimers of utopia and dystopia suggest. The changes we expect require careful understanding from both the societal and technical perspectives. This is where Anita's Concise Introduction to Machine Learning comes in. With comprehensive coverage of the mathematical principles of machine learning, Anita's book provides the foundation of principled choices that students need to make good decisions when we the noise noise the signal. This foundation is critical to ensuring that we develop beneficial aspects of the technology while curtailing the damaging outcomes. This can only occur through comprehensive understanding of the capabilities and limitations of these techniques.

Welcome to Anita's world: a concise introduction to Machine Learning that builds on solid foundations, practical real-world experience and a long experience of sharing these principles with students.
