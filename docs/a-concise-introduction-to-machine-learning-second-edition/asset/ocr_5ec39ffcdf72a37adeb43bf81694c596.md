# Acknowledgments

Special thanks are due to my PhD supervisor Professor Mike Powell who sadly passed away in April 2015. From him I learnt that one should strive for understanding and simplicity. I more often saw him working through an algorithm with pen than sitting at a computer. He wanted to know why a particular algorithm was successful. When machine learning systems reach greater and greater complexity, it is especially important to continue to strive for understanding.

Of course, I need to thank all the students within the MPhil in Scientific Computing at the University of Cambridge, UK, as well as other learners from across the world whose questions and quest to further their understanding improved this book. I am thankful to Mani Sarkar, whose insightful comments helped inform the second edition.

I would also like to express my gratitude to Cambridge University and the staff and fellows at Selwyn College for creating such a wonderful atmosphere in which to learn and teach.

This book would not have been written without the support of many people in my private life, foremost my parents Helmut Marichise Faul, who sadly passed away in 2019 and 2021. They instilled a love for knowledge and understanding in me from an early age, and that the most important question is “why”?
