The Wishart distribution $W(\Psi, \nu)$ is the conjugate prior for the covariance matrix $\mathbf{\Sigma}$ of a multivariate normal distribution with a known mean. The least informative choice for $\nu$ in the prior is $\nu=d$. We can assume that the mean of this distribution is zero, we can recreate the data at zero by subtracting the mean from all samples. Let $D = \{\v_1, \dots, \v_N\}$ be draws from $N(\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{\Sigma})$. The likelihood of this data is given by

```
$$\prod_{n=1}^N \sqrt{(2\pi)^d|\mathbf{\Sigma}|} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\v_n^T \mathbf{\Sigma}^{-1} \v_n\right) (2\pi)^{-ND/2}|\mathbf{\Sigma}|^{-N/2} \exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=1}^N \v_n^T \mathbf{\Sigma}^{-1} \v_n\right)$
```
