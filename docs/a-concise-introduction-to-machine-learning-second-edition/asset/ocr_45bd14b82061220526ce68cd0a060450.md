Firstly, in Figure 9.4a it be seen that the activations of the training data are separated. It is a two-dimensional space, where there are two hidden neurons. This space becomes close to the space spanned by the first principal components of the training data, because the data points are most easily separated and this is the goal of the encoder. The transfer function emphasizes this separation, which we see in Figure 9.4b.
