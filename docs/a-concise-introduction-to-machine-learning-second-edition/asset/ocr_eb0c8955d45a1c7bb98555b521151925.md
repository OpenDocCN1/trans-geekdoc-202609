# Preface

Machine Learning is known under many other names such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Pattern Recognition, Data Mining, Data Assimilation, and Big Data, to list but a few. It developed in many areas of science, for example physics, engineering, computer science, and mathematics in parallel and independently. Optical Character Recognition (OCR), search engines, computer vision, Natural Language Processing (NLP), discovery, fraud detection, robotics, data prediction, and drug discovery, makes it sometimes difficult to find a solution for a particular problem in the future, simply because of the fact that different words and phrases are used for the concept.

This book aims to alleviate this. A common concept, but known in sciences under different names, is described using the common language. Readers will find the index useful to find a particular topic as it is known to them. The index is comprehensive, making it easy to find the required information. Hopefully, this will prove the book useful as a reference and make it essential on the bookshelves of anybody Machine Learning techniques.

Often, in teaching Machine Learning, the emphasis is on the questions "What?” and "How?”, and the question "Why?” is neglected. In my opinion, this is the most important question. If it is understood why an algorithm is successful it can be properly applied. On the other hand, if it is not understood why it arrived at its results, these results cannot be trusted. A more recent reason, is the necessary processing power, and energy consumption, of very large models such as Large Language Models (LLMs). Here algorithmic improvements can reduce the size and cost. These are only possible, if the inner workings are understood.

Algorithms are often taught by side by showing the similarities and differences between them. This book addresses this by introducing the commonalities. Most techniques to find an approximate model modeling the data in a finite, low-dimensional space. They differ in how a solution to this approximate model is chosen. This approach helps to keep the book concise while still giving a thorough and in-depth treatment. In some places, where further detail was felt to be out of the scope of this book, the reader is referred to further reading.
