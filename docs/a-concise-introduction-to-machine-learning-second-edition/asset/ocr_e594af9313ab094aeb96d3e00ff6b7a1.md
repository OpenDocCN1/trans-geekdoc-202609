# Exercises

**Exercise 7.1.** *The following data samples are given:*

```
$$\begin{pmatrix} 2 & 3 & 5 & 7 \ -1 & 3 & 4 & 6 \ 1 & 1 & 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
```

By inspection, how can the dimensionality be reduced? After doing so, find the sample mean. Centre the reduced data on the origin. Calculate the sample covariance of the reduced, centred data. Once you have calculated the sample covariance, find the first principal component.

**Exercise 7.2.** *Continuing from Exercise 7.1, we take a probabilistic view. State the three-dimensional sample mean and the $3\times3$ sample covariance and calculate the assumed noise variance. What should the squared length of the eigenvector be? Calculate this for the largest eigenvalue. State the covariance matrix of the assumed distribution the data samples are drawn from.*
