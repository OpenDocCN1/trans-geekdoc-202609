Instead of thinking about failures and successes, it is more convenient to think about a change of state. λ gives the rate of change over a time interval, and the exponential distribution describes the probability of how much time states changes. Constant rate of change is a crucial assumption not satisfied by real-world processes. For example, the rate of emergency calls on Friday and Saturday nights and staffing levels need to be adjusted compared to other times. The exponential distribution is inappropriate to model the overall lifetime of organisms or technical devices, where the event of death or failure depends on the lifetime so far. If the rate is constant, it is used in queuing theory.
