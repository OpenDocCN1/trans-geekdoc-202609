These are not just spectral colours, but also non-spectral ones. Magentonis an extraordinary colour. It is a mixture of the primary colours red and
blue which lie far apart in the colour spectrum. It occurs when nature the
green component of white light is absorbed, since it is the complementary
colour to green. In fact, magenta did not exist as a dye until 1859, when it
invented by François-Emmanuel Verguin (Lyon), Chambers Nicolson and
George Mau (London). It is an aniline dye, and was named after the battle
of Magenta. It caused a fashion craze with those who could afford it, showing
of magenta clothes some of which can be seen in the Victoria and Albert
Museum in London, England.

Different animals and insects have different numbers of colour types.
The following table gives an overview.

| Chromacity | Types of colour cells | Rough number of colours perceived | Examples |
|---|---|---|--|
| Monochromy | 1 | 100 | Marine mammals |
| Dichromacy | 2 | 10,000 | Most terrestrial mammals |
| Trichromacy | 3 | 10 million | Humans, great apes, some insects |
| Tetrachromy | 4 | 100 million | Most reptiles, amphibians, birds and insects, rarely humans |
| Pentachromacy | 5 | 10 billion | Some insects (butterflies), some birds (pigeons). |
