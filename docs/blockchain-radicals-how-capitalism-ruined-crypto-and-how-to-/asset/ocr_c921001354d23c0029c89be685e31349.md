The commercial begins with the question, “What size is your carbon footprint?”, after which a series of people (likely actors) explain what they believe the term “carbon footprint” means, guiltily implying that their lifestyle choices were hurting the environment. The last third of the commercial includes the line, “We can all do more to emit less”, along with a description of how BP was working on projects to reduce its own emissions.
