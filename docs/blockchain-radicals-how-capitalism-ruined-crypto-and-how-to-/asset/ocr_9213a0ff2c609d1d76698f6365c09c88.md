Some of the acceleration towards the acceptance of cryptocurrency in the sex industry has been caused by the passing of the Fight Online Sex Trafficking Act (FOSTA) and Stop Enabling Sex Traffickers Act (SESTA) in the United States in April 2018. While the legislation was meant to counter sex trafficking through online platforms, advocacy groups have pointed out that it is disproportionately affected sex workers and is essentially an Internet censorship bill. The legislation has made it more difficult for online platforms to stay in business because they require teams of lawyers to evaluate multiple scenarios of risk at the state and federal level. This has meant that some have gone out of business, forcing many sex workers back into more dangerous forms of work like prostitution, which includes a much higher risk of human trafficking.
