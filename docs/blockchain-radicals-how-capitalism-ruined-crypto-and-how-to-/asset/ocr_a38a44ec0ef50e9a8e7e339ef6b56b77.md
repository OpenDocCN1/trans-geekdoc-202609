What this example shows that the use of smart contracts does not necessarily remove the human element in the way that Szabo may have envisioned. In fact, they can be created in ways that increase the ability for humans to interact with each other over digital space. The decision to reject the second Doge of Venice image did not come from shared knowledge that was encoded on the blockchain — it was the social factor and shared understanding of the community that led to the creation of the precedent. At the same time, this flies in the faces of critics and gatekeepers who declare confidently that smart contracts have no ability or place in dispute resolution, governance or social coordination.
