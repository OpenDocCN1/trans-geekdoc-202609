Since the spending of the bitcoin collected can be tracked on the bitcoin blockchain, there is evidence that at least 3,500 BTC has been spent, having gone to the known address of BitPay, a bitcoin payment processing service, likely to convert it into fiat currency like US Dollars or Euros.19

While the original financial blockade eventually ended, WikiLeaks never removed the option to donate via bitcoin, something which came handy when a new financial blockade was enforced in 2017. In fact, they have since expanded to accept other popular and privacy-oriented cryptocurrencies. The choice of continuing to accept bitcoin donations ended up being not only a smart move for circumventing blockades, but also as an investment. According to Assange in 2017,20 WikiLeaks’ donated bitcoin generated a 50,000% return. He thanked the US government for pressing them to make the move, as each action taken against WikiLeaks it gave the organisation a boost in bitcoin donations.21
