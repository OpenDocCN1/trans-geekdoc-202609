Putting that aside, these examples, and the many more to come, show some of the advantages of using smart contracts. Smart contracts give predictability and confidence when using templates that are tried and audited for bugs. They can automate many of the same functions of various types of agreements that people are likely to make with each other without involving more parties. They do not need to try to foresee every single possible outcome and implement structures that take into account the complex relations that are inherent to agreements between humans. They are certainly not needed or perfect for all types of agreements, but that's OK. Just because code is not law does not make smart contracts not useful. And it is with this grounded understanding we can begin taking a closer look at their more complex applications.
