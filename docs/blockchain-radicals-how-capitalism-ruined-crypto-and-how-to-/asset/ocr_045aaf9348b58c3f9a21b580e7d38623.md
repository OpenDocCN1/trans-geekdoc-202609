To be clear, at this point I quite regret adopting the term “smart contracts”. I should have called them something more boring and technical, perhaps something like “persistent scripts”. I do think that persistent scripts controlling assets compete with the legal system on some margins, but so do locks on doors. So IMO it’s wrong to equate them with a specific philosophy of law privatization.
