So let's walk through the entire process of the creation of one block on the Bitcoin blockchain. Users who are connected to the Bitcoin network sign transactions with their private key to send to the receiver's public address along with a market determined transaction fee. These transactions are then requested to the P2P network of miners who are competing to create hashes with a predetermined amount of zeroes in the front using the requested transactions, the hash of the previous block and a "nonce". When a miner is able to create a block meeting the criteria, it serves as a proof of work to other miners that they won the competition for that block, and the winning miner receives newly minted bitcoin as well as the transaction fees paid by users whose transactions are included in the block, and the process repeats itself.
