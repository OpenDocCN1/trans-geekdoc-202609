Throughout the last two sections of this book, with a few exceptions, I’ve focused on projects built on the Ethereum blockchain. That’s because the newest innovations in the cryptocurrency space that I’m interested in started there — it was the first to utilise smart contracts, and its native cryptocurrency, ether, is second in market cap size to Bitcoin. This, however, should not be taken as an endorsement of all the projects built on Ethereum, or even the exact protocol design of Ethereum. For anyone who has used the Ethereum Mainnet during 2020, one of the issues Ethereum has had is with scaling its transactions. Making a transaction with a smart contract on the Ethereum Mainnet can at times be be prohibitively expensive, to the degree that the only activities that take place on it are very high-value.
