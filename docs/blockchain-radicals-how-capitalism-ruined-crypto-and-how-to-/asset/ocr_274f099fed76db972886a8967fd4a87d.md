On paper, most “Western” countries are liberal capitalist democracies where every citizen has the right to vote, and therefore theoretically the outcomes of elections and the legislations that are passed into law are democratically determined. In practice, however, this could not be further from the truth. In many countries, but particularly in the United States of America, bribery is essentially legal, it’s simply referred to as “lobbying”.
