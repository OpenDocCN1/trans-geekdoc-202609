Solvent use: While preparing colloids, it is admitted that organic solvents and stabilizing agents are toxic and thus need to be handled with concern due to their impact on the environment and safety of humans.

# 3.5.2 CVD

High purity and uniformity: CVD techniques mentioned create highly pure and uniform QDs, which are crucial for the use where the required optical properties are well defined.

Scalability: CVD method is appropriate for mass production where QDs require high quality for commercial use especially in industrial applications.

Complex equipment requirements: CVD presents the need to use specialized and costly instruments, and hence, start-up expenses are likely to be higher than in the other syntheses.

Limited size control: This is because, although CVD processes can achieve good coverage and size uniformity, it is easier to lose control over the size of the QD structures to be formed, and therefore, are useful only in size control related imaging applications.

# 3.5.3 MBE

Atomic-level precision: MBE gives the best control over the growth and dimensions of QD layers and enables the synthesis of highly qualified QDs with appropriate electronic and optical features.

High uniformity: The method gives exceptionally small and monodisperse QDs, which are desired for reproducible applications.

High cost and complexity: MBE is often complicated and not cheap, which means that used equipment is not available for all sorts of research fields. The process also has a need for vacuum environment and stringent control over the deposition parameters.

Slow deposition rates: MBE has a weak deposition rate compared to other PVD techniques, particularly sputtering, and this makes processing time longer and throughput lower.

The most widely used methods among green techniques for the synthesis of metal and metal oxide nanoparticles are the hydrothermal and solvothermal syntheses.

Simple and cost-effective: Hydrothermal and solvothermal are more straightforward processes, without the need for costly precursors and dangerous conditions to synthesize QDs using water solvents.
