# 4.2.2 Recent breakthrough in image processing: noise reduction and artifact removal

However, CT imaging has some limitations; these are noise and artifacts, which distort image and lead to inaccurate diagnosis. To overcome these difficulties, sophisticated methods of surface image analysis are used. IR method reduces the visibility of random noise and gives better image quality to demonstrate the anatomical details. It is acknowledged that certain artifact correction techniques such as metal artifact reduction and beam-hardening correction are employed to compensate for the impacts of metal implants and other conflicting imaging sources. These enhancements enhance the analysis of CT images and decrease the rate of re-scans.

# 4.2.3 Methods of increasing the contrast of CT pictures

Sharpening technique is necessary for segmentation of tissues and perception of pathologies in CT scans. Iodine-based contrasts are given intravenously to outline blood vessels, organs, and tumors for ease of identification. Further techniques increasing the contrast and the detailed view of CT images include the contrast enhancement of the images as well as the equalization of histograms. Multiphase imaging techniques and dynamic contrast-enhanced CT contribute further information regarding tissue flow and function in tissue characterization and diagnostic paradigms.

# 4.2.4 Artificial intelligence in automated computerized tomography pictures analysis

CT imaging was getting increasingly connected with Machine Learning and AI so as to increase the speed of diagnosis and accuracy of results. One subclass of deep learning algorithms, specifically the CNNs, is fed large amounts of data in order to allow them to learn and detect and sort anomalies in the CT scans on their own. These cognitive instruments enhance the work of a radiologist by identifying potential issue areas, performing measurements autonomously and providing for decisions. Through enhancing the capability of analyzing the data obtained from CT images, use of machine learning in CT imaging is likely to enhance specific treatment, quicken the process of diagnosis, and reduce human error.
