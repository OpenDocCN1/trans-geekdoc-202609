# 4.4.2 Clinical applications and benefits

Use of real-time monitoring systems in clinical environments offers several major advantages. For instance, in Intensive Care Units, the said systems are used to track at one time the vital signs and imaging data of patients and notify the appropriate healthcare personnel about any abnormalities that erupt to severe conditions like acute respiratory distress. In the context of operations, it helps to track the outcomes of actions and recognize adverse reactions as soon as possible. The feature of giving feedbacks and alerts in real-time helps medical teams to be more responsive, and increases patient’s safety.
