# 6.2 Enhancing treatment planning

Processes associated with medical imaging play a vital role in shaping treatment plans by providing detailed and accurate diagnostic information. This precision enables clinicians to formulate more tailored and effective treatment strategies for individual patients. As depicted in Figure 11, advancements in imaging technologies combined with sophisticated image analysis and processing techniques have significantly improved the ability to diagnose, monitor, and prevent diseases across various medical specialties. These innovations contribute to enhanced targeting of therapeutic interventions and a more proactive approach to patient care.
