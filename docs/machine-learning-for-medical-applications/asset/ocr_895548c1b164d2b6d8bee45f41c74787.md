Other traditional assessment techniques were histopathology, laboratory assessment, blood test, and MRIs, while imaging like X-ray, CT scan, and MRI though are important in assessing the overall health of the patient. These techniques often faced limitations regarding its sensitivity, specificity, and the ability to handle large amount of data proficiently.

AI has pioneered and revolutionized the enhancement of conventional diagnostic tools through advanced data processing and pattern recognition capabilities. As illustrated in Figure 2, machine learning (ML) techniques applied to medical imaging have significantly improved diagnostic accuracy by detecting anomalies that might be missed by the human eye. Deep learning (DL) models, in particular, trained on vast and diverse datasets, can recognize subtle patterns in images that are often imperceptible to human observers. Radiology has been a major beneficiary of these advancements, as AI systems now assist clinicians by offering diagnostic suggestions and highlighting potential issues based on prior image inputs streamlining the workflow and improving clinical decision-making.
