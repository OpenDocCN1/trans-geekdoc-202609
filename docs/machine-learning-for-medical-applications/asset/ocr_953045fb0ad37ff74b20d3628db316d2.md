Automatic segmentation methods also reduce the employment of picture analysis, which can be inconsistent and time-consuming. Automation disburses work flow initiatives, standardizes work, and makes it possible for healthcare specialists to devote their time on result analysis and clinical decision-making.

In general, medical image segmentation has paved way for precision medicine; solutions are conceived based on various imaging parameters that benefit the patients through a personalized approach in the management of health. The continual improvement and advancements in segmentation technology help in the improvement of the medical imaging making its importance prominent in the current healthcare.

# 2.4 Segmentation tasks and challenges

Many different processes are included in medical image segmentation, all aiming to identify specific structures within medical images. This task was particularly challenging due to the similar intensity values and variations in tissue appearance, yet it was essential for understanding brain structure and detecting abnormalities.
