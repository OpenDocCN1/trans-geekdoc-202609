# 3.3 Chemical vapor deposition

CVD is another common method of QD synthesis as the technique provides a good opportunity to obtain high-quality nanoparticles with a defined size, shape, and composition. The process in this method includes the condensation of vaporized chemical precursors onto a substrate and subsequent reaction to form solid QDs. The CVD process has certain advantages and particular features that make it appropriate for QD formation.

# 3.3.1 Growth of gallium arsine as a foundation of chemical vapor deposition

CVD process is initiated by the supply of gaseous precursors into a reaction tube. Such precursors are often represented by metal-organic compounds gases containing the elements necessary for QD formation. On heating, the precursors decompose on the substrate surface to form the QD colloids. Deposited material sticks to the substrate and then is subjected to further treatment for its intended size and characteristics.

# 3.3.2 Chemical vapor deposition (CVD) advantages

The advantages of using CVD with respect to the synthesis of QDs are the following. The first advantage is achieving high purity and uniformity of the subsequently obtained QDs. The CVD chamber provides an enclosed atmosphere where deposition conditions, such as temperature, pressure, and precursor flow can be regulated in order to obtain reproducible QD characteristics. Further, it is also established that CVD can produce QDs of a narrow size distribution and well-defined geometries, which are important for the predetermined optical and electronic characteristics of the compound.

Various types of CVD techniques are employed for QD synthesis, including:

+   - **Metal-organic chemical vapor deposition (MOCVD):** In MOCVD, metal-organic precursors are introduced to grow self-assembled QDs on a substrate. This technique is especially suitable for realizing II-VI and III-V semiconductor QDs including the CdSe and InP materials. This method provides a highly accurate means of controlling QD size and composition since the ratio of metal to organic components can be varied.
