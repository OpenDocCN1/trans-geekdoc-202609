AI can also contribute to the creation of POC diagnosable devices for everyday use. These devices can be controlled through different algorithms and elegant user interfaces and can help the user to perform the tests without a lot of mistakes, and the results can be easily understood and applied in the future. This is especially helpful when working in facilities where the clients’ caretakers have diverse levels of accreditation. Through the introduction of AI into POC devices, it is possible to offer sophisticated diagnostic analytical tools to more players in the market, since these processes can be done by a simple push of a button.

This advance can also enable POC diagnostics to have continuous monitoring and capability to integrate data in real time, coupled with higher accuracy, portability and, ease of use due to AI integration. Smart and mobile diagnostics tools also maintain constant track of biomarkers, monitoring systems, and other essential health aspects, characteristic of a patient. This feature is especially valuable when the illness enters chronic stages and care givers wish to keep track of the performance of the illness and the reaction of the body to treatment. Through constantly updating trend and anomaly detection from AI analysis of data, appropriate actions can be taken immediately and for individual patients, therapies can further be adjusted on time.

AI equally participates in data integration and management of PoC diagnostics. AI systems are unique in their ability to provide a more holistic view of a patient’s health, the data from which could include EHR’s and other tests. This synchronized treatment delivery increases the capacity to tracks and modify patients’ statuses over time and can thereby enhance information-gathering. AI analysis of data provides a means of making more accurate diagnoses and increases the demands for a more focused treatment plan; Data analysis also helps in uncovering relationships and connections that otherwise remain unnoticed, given the individual results of a test.

Despite these advancements, there remain some barriers toward the increasing adoption of AI-driven POC diagnostics. For technologies to be safe and effective, some issues such as data protection, authorization from the government, and need-to-prove effective experiments must be considered. Thus, to eliminate these challenges and bring full-scale AI implementation for POC diagnostics, further research is required.
