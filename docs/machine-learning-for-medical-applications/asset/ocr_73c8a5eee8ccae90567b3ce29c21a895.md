# 4.1.3 Constraints of X-ray imaging and their resolution

However, there are certain drawbacks that constrain use of X-ray imaging; these are being addressed by modern technology as follows. Some of the issues include high radiation, which is a potential danger, especially when a number of images are taken. There is a radiation protection strategy, and reducing the dose and best imaging techniques try to tailor the correct balance between dose rate and diagnostic quality. X-rays also have poor contrast for soft tissues compared to bones; these X-rays also have very harmonized density. To counter this, there are methods including dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry and the use of contrast agents, which help in the visualization of soft tissues. Also, the interpretation of pictures on an X-ray can be somewhat of a challenge, as this requires knowledge. Machine learning algorithms are being created to assist radiologists in localizing minor manifestations and enhancing the assessment’s accuracy.
