# 5.3 Increased accuracy and precision in medical devices

Besides, due to the 3D printing applied to the production of medical products and services, improved exactness and consistency in medical device production can be regarded as one of the most apparent advantages of 3D printing technology. This advancement is becoming astounding in redesigning the medical device industry in many ways, including its production cycle, with serious impacts on patient care and results.

Enhanced customization: Personalized medical gadgets are designed and developed using 3D printing with a level of accuracy never seen before, specific to each patient. Instead, the doctors develop gadgets that finely suit their body features by considering numerous images of the patient's body, particularly CT and MRI. Such levels of customization ensure increased functional and morphological compatibility of prostheses, implants, and other medical instruments, enhancing the patients' comfort and effectiveness in general.

Enhanced precision in geometry: By virtue of the layer-by-layer additive manufacturing concept of 3D printing, complex and detailed steric geometries, which otherwise could not be easily achieved in a normal manufacturing process where the material is cut with a disposable tool, can be created. This capability is indispensable for creating devices as intricate as the implants and tools used in surgeries that require precise fabrication to determine measurements and strength in order to function in the human body without risk of failure.

Reduction of manufacturing tolerances: In many manufacturing processes, there are commonplace methods of manufacturing that result in techniques that are “take-away” based, thus causing variation in a particular part's accuracy. 3D printing constructs an object from a model, making it less prone to mistakes and narrow manufacturing fraternization. This precision is important for certain devices that require precise compliance with current standards and perform successfully in medical applications.
