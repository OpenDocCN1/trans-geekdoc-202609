# 5.4.2.1 Characteristics and applications

High resolution and detail: CT imaging provides high spatial resolution and delineation of both cortical and trabecular bone as well as soft tissues. It is therefore of great use in diagnosis of disorders involving assessment of trauma, tumor, and internal hemorrhages.

Cross-sectional imaging: Cross-sectional studies are superior for counting and localizing the abnormality, and overall, the extent of disease involvement. CT is often applied in oncology to stage the tumor and in trauma surgery to assess the patient.

Limitations: Compared with the conventional radiography, CT imaging uses a higher level of X-ray, which exposes the patient to the risk of radiation-induced effects especially when the scans are repeated often. As a result of these concerns, new methods and strategies for the reduction of radiation doses are routinely being discovered.

# 5.4.3 Ultrasound imaging

Piezoelectric crystals transmit high-frequency sound waves to aid in the formation of picture images of internal organs. Waves generated by an ultrasound transducer are reflected from tissues and converted into pictures displayed on a monitor.

# 5.4.3.1 Characteristics and applications

Real-time imaging: Ultrasound allows for the initial imaging in real time and subsequently facilitates dynamic evaluation of organs and structures. It is common in cardiology to determine patients’ heart functioning and in obstetrics to monitor the growth of the fetus.

Nonionizing radiation: Ultrasound is not as dangerous as its counterparts such as CT that create ionizing radiation. Due to this, the medication is safer for some patient groups, including newborns and women who are expecting.

Limitations: A major concern with the modality is that it has low penetration and cannot easily image through bone, which restricts its uses and some areas of the body cannot be imaged. There are two factors that affect the quality of images: the operator’s expertise and the patient’s mobility.

# 5.4.4 Magnetic resonance imaging

MRI was another method that is used to produce picture images of the body by using magnetic fields and a radiofrequency wave. MRI is particularly exceptional in imaging soft tissues and holds a high contrast compared to X-ray and CT.
