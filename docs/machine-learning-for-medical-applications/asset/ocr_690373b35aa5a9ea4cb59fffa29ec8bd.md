The generator generates visuals that increase in realism because of this adversarial process. Thus, GANs find use in style transfer, data augmentation, and the generation of high-resolution images, among others.

There was also another family of generative models, which focuses on learning a low-dimensional representation of the data and is called VAEs. VAEs encode the input pictures into a latent space with an encoder network and decode images from the latent space using a decoder network. Due to this, VAEs are capable of delivering various good formation pictures of a learned latent distribution that is probabilistic. Applications of VAEs include in-painting, picture de-noising, and generating new samples of a dataset that are generated from the learned distribution.

Codifying this idea, the style transfer approaches based on generative models integrate the content of an image with the appearance of another. Convolutional networks are widely used in neural style transfer since they allow the generation of synthetic pictures that combine the appearance of a reference picture with the structural properties of a content picture. Recent developments in this area have allowed the crafting of visually impressive images that resemble the distinct artistic look of certain art movements.
