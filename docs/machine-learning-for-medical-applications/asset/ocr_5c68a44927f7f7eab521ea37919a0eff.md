# Physical stability:

Other issues that need be considered include the physical stability of the functional groups. Even the surface changes can have a critical dependence on physical characteristics like temperature, pH, and concentration of the ions. Altering these conditions results in aggregation of functional groups, which are responsible for the interaction with target molecules and system performance. It is important to devise strategies of functionalization that would make the materials more physically stable, yet maintain functional activity.
