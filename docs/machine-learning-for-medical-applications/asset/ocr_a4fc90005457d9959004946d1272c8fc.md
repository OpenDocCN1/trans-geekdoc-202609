Selection of appropriate algorithms: Lastly, the type of registration method to use depends on certain attributes of the images as well as the domain of the clinic, a registration issue that can be highly dependent on context and situation.

Image registration is an important step in medical image analysis by which images from various sources or taken at different times are realigned for comprehensive analysis in diagnosis. New methods of registration remain a critical factor influencing the improvements of the registration methodology and thus the clinical practice of medical imaging.

# 5.5 Feature extraction and analysis

Feature extraction and analysis form part of the processing of medical images to convert raw data from medical images into usable information for diagnosis and monitoring of diseases and development of treatment plans. This process concerns the successful detection and measurement of differences or similarities of features or structures in medical images for analysis and evaluation.

The main task of this section is to describe the means of detecting and extracting the set of features that are relevant to the classification problem.

Feature extraction on the other hand involves extracting particular attributes or patterns desired for analysis from medical images. Several techniques are employed in this process, including:

Texture analysis: In a picture, the spatial arrangement of pixel intensities is measured by the texture features. Textural information is generalizable and textural features can be captured using some preprocessors such as Gabor filters, local binary patterns (LBP), and gray-level co-occurrence matrix (GLCM) to distinguish between different types of tissues and highlight abnormalities.

Shape Analysis: Shape features refer to the characteristics of geometric appearances of stiff structures in any given image. Contour detection, for instance, boundary extraction, and even region properties work to quantify certain parameters including the area, perimeter, and geometric shape. These features are particularly useful for splitting or arranging the respective teaching model into segments based on anatomical structures or lesions.

Intensity-based features: Features based on intensity measure the distribution as well as the variation of pixel intensity within an image. Histogram analysis, mean, variance, and skewness of pixel intensity are some of the methods used in linear analysis used to give information on image contrast and brightness that may hold information on pathological changes.

Edge detection: In feature detection, there are the Sobel, Canny, and Prewitt operators, which are used to limit the edges and contours of structures within an image. Such features facilitate delineating areas of interest with higher contrast that leads to improving the identification of structural features.
