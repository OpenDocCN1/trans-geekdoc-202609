On the other hand, SPECT imaging utilizes gamma-emitting radiotracers to generate functional data, and the data on the anomalies involving the bones, brain, and heart perfusion are provided. Besides anatomical imaging studies, PET and SPECT present other useful diagnostic information to assist in therapy and offer a more complete picture of the patients’ condition.

This chapter discusses some of the challenges in MIP.

Medical image processing has greatly developed but at the same time there are several challenges that influence efficiency and reliability of these approaches. Another factor that may be considered is that medical photographs contain information about a patient that has to be protected from leaks and unauthorized access [19]. Further, for precise analysis and diagnosis, proper image quality and homogeneity of the various imaging techniques as well as the systems are important [20]. The appearance of inconsistencies in the images for acquisition, levels of noise, and artifacts can also influence the overall approach taken for feature extraction [21]. Additionally, the integration of MIP with telemedicine and the diagnosis in other locations increases the problem area with issues related to the protection of the processed data, as well as the technical requirements for telecommunications channels.

Another important consideration is the future direction and innovation that may emerge from the implementation of this framework. These are worth exploring in several key areas. There is little doubt that further advancements and subsequent technological revolutions could have a profound impact on the future of medical image processing (MIP) [22]. It is predicted that the integration of emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) will significantly enhance the functions of medical image analysis by improving the effectiveness of various diagnostic and treatment methods [23].

In healthcare and patient management, the application of AI in predictive analytics, anomaly detection, and image segmentation is expected to lead to more accurate and timely clinical decisions [24]. Moreover, continuous advancements in radiotracers, imaging systems, optics, and mathematical algorithms will open new opportunities for innovation in MIP [25]. The integration of these technologies has the potential to transform clinical practice, improve patient care, and advance the development of personalized medicine.
