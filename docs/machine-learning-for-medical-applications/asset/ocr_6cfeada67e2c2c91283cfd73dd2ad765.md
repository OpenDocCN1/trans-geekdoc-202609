Environmental monitoring:

In environmental monitoring functional QDs are applied for sensing of pollutants. QDs, particularly their surface, can be modified in a way that enhances the selectivity and sensitivity to certain environmental toxins. Long-lasting, resistant to environmental effects, elemental and chemical coatings that provide epigenetic stability of monitored objects are essential for ensuring high performance of handling devices and obtaining accurate data.

# 4.5.2 Challenges and considerations

Stability and aggregation:

The problems that functionalized as well as surface-coated QDs can encounter include stability and aggregation. Reliability of the above methods of surface modification is determined by the environmental conditions and type of coating to be applied. It is critical to maintain QD performance, keeping in mind that functional groups must not degrade and the coatings must be hard.

Toxicity and biocompatibility:

Since modifications like functionalization of the polymer surface and coating of the material can improve biocompatibility, the selection of the material and the method of its application should be discussed thoroughly to exclude toxicity. It is important to test the toxicity of QDs and their properties after the surface modification for biological application.

Cost and complexity:

The functionalization of QDs and the coating process bring about some extra costs and difficulties in fabrication. Thus, the search for cost-effective and scalable techniques for producing the surface modifications still remains an essential question for QDs and their further commercial use.
