# 6.3.3 How to assess disease progress in autoimmune diseases

MIP is also used to track the evolution of autoimmune diseases, including rheumatoid arthritis and lupus. Ultrasonography and MRI are employed to evaluate joint inflammation, synovial enlargement, and bony abrasion. These imaging findings help clinicians assess disease activity and degradation of joints and monitor the impact of DMARDs and other treatments. AI developed imaging that include and promote dynamic contrast-enhanced MRI, which enhances changes in tissue perfusion and inflammation are effective for all autoimmune diseases.

MIP in relation to monitoring disease progression has a few challenges. Fluency in usage of different imaging protocols, patient motion during scans, and differences in image acquisition parameters do influence scan measurements and their interpretations. In this respect, proper imaging protocols and creation of stable image analysis techniques are critical to obtain accurate assessment of disease evolution. There are better and unbiased ways of monitoring diseases through imaging data integration, high-resolution imaging etc., to mention a few, which enhance the sensitivity and accuracy of the monitoring mechanisms. Moreover, it is concluded that the extent of automation and reportedly accurate analysis of illness progression should be enhanced with the help of techniques used in artificial intelligence as well as machine learning.

MIP greatly influences practice by offering vital endpoints for tracking disease progression. Using new technologies in imaging and analysis, they continue to chart the progression of diseases, observing reaction to treatments, and deciding on necessary actions regarding patients. New developments in imaging systems and computing techniques are constantly enhancing the disease surveillance systems and goals in this area, thus improving patient’s health and promoting the development of personalized medicine.

# 6.4 Personalized medicine and patient-specific treatment

# 6.4.1 Purpose of imaging in personalized treatment

Thus, medical imaging has become one of the most important elements of individualized therapy because it allows obtaining the overall picture of each patient’s anatomic and physiologic environment. MRI, CT, PET, and SPECT high-resolution images are useful in presenting information in organ or tissue activity, composition, and pathology. The use of such imaging results in developing treatment plans for the individual patients, which is made possible through commonalities with other parameters like gene type and medical history. It ensures that medical applications are uniquely tailored across patients’ unique needs, thus enhancing quality healthcare.
