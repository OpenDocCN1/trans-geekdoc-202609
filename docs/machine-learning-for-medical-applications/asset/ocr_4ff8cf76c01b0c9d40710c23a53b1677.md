# 6.1.1 Enhancements in image segmentation and analysis

Optimal division into the affected structures and lesions is important for adequate diagnosis and treatment plan. Recent developments in image analysis offer such features as the fully or partly automated analysis and segmentation of structures like organs, tumors, or blood vessels. Application of the thresholding, edge detection, and region growing methods offers precise definition of regions of interest. Higher degree of segmentation improves accuracy of measurements of the size, volume, and distribution of the tumor that is vital for cancer staging, presurgical planning, and evaluation of the treatment outcomes.

The convergence of comprehensive machine learning and artificial intelligence Medical imaging has been revolutionized by machine learning and artificial intelligence, which has given new advanced tools for pattern identification and prediction. In AI, big data is employed to teach CNNs and deep learning models to differentiate complex patterns and disorders in medical images. These algorithms enable conventional radiologists to actually recognize and subcategorize these abnormalities to a level of precision. For example, AI has been proved useful in identifying signs of early warning symptoms of diseases such as diabetic retinopathy and breast cancer many times more accurately and faster than conventional methods.

# 6.1.2 Prevention of minimization of diagnostic errors

Misdiagnoses which could be in the form of an incorrect positive diagnosis or an incorrect negative diagnosis will cause an impact on outcomes. These errors are minimized by MIP methods, which offer better analysis in imaging data. Computer-aided systems of image analysis have less inter-operator variability and are therefore less error prone than human methods. Measurable parameters analysis and pattern recognition provide for more accurate evaluation of imaging data which, in turn, decreases the possibility of inaccurate interpretation and raises diagnostic efficacy.
