# 6.5.1 Adapting interventions for the person

To deliver tailored treatments based on a patient’s unique anatomy, the precise delineation of body structures and diseased regions facilitated by segmentation plays a vital role. As illustrated in Figure 15, in oncology, for example, accurate tumor segmentation supports the creation of personalized radiation therapy plans that effectively target malignant tissues while sparing healthy surrounding structures. This precision not only improves treatment efficacy but also minimizes side effects, ultimately enhancing patient care and increasing the overall success rate of therapeutic interventions.

# 6.5.2 Role in precision oncology

Accurate segmentation is pertinent in precision oncology to decide the patterns of tumors and assess reactions to treatment. Medical picture segmentation allows derivation of quantitative info on tumor volume, shape, texture, and others, which can be used to predict the patient’s prognosis and to guide treatment decisions. These quantitative imaging biomarkers provide valuable knowledge regarding the activity of a tumor and can thus select individualized drugs that effectively treat people’s specific cancers.
