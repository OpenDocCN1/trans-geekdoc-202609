These networks struggle in a zero sum proposition. Improving the performance and generalization of models, this augmentation addresses a problem known as lack of annotated data. The practicality of segmentation has been enhanced by GAN-based techniques, notably in a scenario where there was no pre-labeled data set available for use.

# 4.3.4 Transfer learning and pretrained model integration

Transfer learning is a strong approach that calls for the use of pretrained models on Big Data to enhance segmentation. Scientists can seamlessly adapt models trained on general imaging data such as ImageNet for use in small medical-specific image databases via fine-tuning. It was a shortcut to the introduction and application of DL models in clinics since there was no need for enormous quantities of training data and computer performance.

# 4.3.5 Challenges and opportunities in deep learning

Nevertheless, there are still several challenges that are preventing DL-based segmentation techniques for expanding their applications. Due to the continuous requirement of large, high-quality annotated datasets, the process of model building and generalization suffers a lot. In addition, since clinical trust and utilization of DL models require understanding the decision-making of these complex networks, the models themselves are somewhat opaque. To overcome those difficulties, investing further efforts in improving DL system's transparency and monitoring, enhancing the data utilization efficiency, and providing better model designs are suggested.

DL-based methods have significantly redirected the focus of the medical image segmentation techniques offering an opportunity for better and faster segmentation results with greater precision. It also shows that there was a potential for further developments of the theories, based on the constant development of the research area and technologies.
