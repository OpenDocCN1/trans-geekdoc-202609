The shape of the collector also has paramount influence on the orientation and disposition of electrospun nanofiber. Because collectors can come in various forms, for example as flat plates and rotating drums, certain collector orientations can affect the flow of the deposited fibers and their orientation. For instance, rotating collectors can cause fiber stretching and alignment and hence result in enhanced mechanical properties of the fibers. The position of the collector, relative to the spinneret, also influences fiber orientation and morphology, as also the transverse and longitudinal distances at which the collector is positioned with respect to the spinneret. One of the key requirements to obtain uniform fiber deposits and reduce defects is the uniformity of the collector surfaces.

Post-spinning treatments, which are also able to change the properties of the electrospun nanofibers, include heat treatment, chemical cross-linkage, and plasma treatment. Heat treatment helps in enhancing the crystalline structure of the polymers and residual solvents to enhance the strength and stability of the fibers. Covalent bonding can also be made to occur between polymer chains through a process called chemical cross-linking; this enhances the utility of the fiber and its life span. Plasma modification enhances the biocompatibility of the nanofibers’ bioactive molecules to attach on their surface. Since it is essential to attain specific fiber characteristics without a doubt on their properties, such treatments should be controlled.

# 2.5 Advances in electrospinning techniques

The technique of electrospinning has been developed since its discovery and new technologies are implemented to increase the accuracy in creating the nanofiber matrix. Newer methods in electrospinning have been defined to overcome these drawbacks of electrospinning, including poor control over fiber properties, scalability, and ability to create structures of multiple fibers. These developments are to increase the performance, multifunctionality, and variety of applications of electrospun nanofibers in order to meet the increasing needs of different sectors such as biomedical engineering, electronics, environmental engineering, and others.

Three significant progresses have been made in electrospinning. Identification of more than one needle electrospinning system: Seven-point probes system allows the creation of several nanofiber streams from one system. This technique improves throughput and lowers processing time, which makes it very ideal for business with massive operations. Multineedle electrospinning also enables the use of more than one type of polymer, as well as the formation of nanofibers of different dimensions and sizes within a single step, making the created materials significantly more useful.
