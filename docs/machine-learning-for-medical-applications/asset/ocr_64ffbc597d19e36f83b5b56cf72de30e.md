# 6.3.4 Enhancing personalized medicine

The exact targeting is incorporated into disease tracking for individual patient treatment plans, which underlies the concept of solving the problems of personalized medicine. Through detailed segmentation data, the treatment approaches can be tailored to specific illness characteristics and progression related with the illness. In the end, individual treatment approach results in benefits such as high treatment effectiveness and a lower probability of side effects in patients.

Precise bout delineation is essential to assess disease’s evolution, response of treatments, relapse, chronicity, behavior over time, and to promote progress in the field of individualized medicine. Segmentation helps improve patient care services and manifests itself in staying abreast with alterations in disease characteristics due to correct definition of the myriad segments in the healthcare industry.

# 6.4 Reducing manual effort and subjectivity

Automated segmentation techniques complement and/or replace manual segmentation skews in medical imaging as central to decreasing manual work and improving standardized diagnostic methods.

# 6.4.1 Organizing work to minimize repetitive activities

Automatic edge detection assists in reducing the effort involved in manually defining regions of interest and the anatomical structures and pathological tissue regions of interests. This automation is beneficial because it saves time in the interpretation of the medical images leading to more time for other aspects of patent’s care. These algorithms help speed up the imaging workflow and overall throughput and decrease the amount of work that must be done by radiologists as well as other healthcare specialists.

# 6.4.2 Minimizing human error

Manual segmentation was used in this study, which introduced certain limitations such as operator fatigue, subjective interpretation, and variability in techniques. These human factors can affect the consistency and accuracy of the results. In contrast, automated segmentation methods mitigate these issues by applying consistent algorithms across all images. This reduces the likelihood of human error and enhances the reproducibility of imaging findings – an important aspect of diagnostic accuracy, treatment planning, and disease follow-up.
