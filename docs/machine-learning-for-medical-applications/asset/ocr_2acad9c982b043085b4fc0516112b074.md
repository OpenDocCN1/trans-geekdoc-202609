Quantum confinement makes the electrical characteristics of QDs considerably different from conventional electronics and helps in a number of optical and electronic applications. These properties are then used in medical imaging to provide high-resolution images with flexible emission wavelengths suitable for the identification and imaging of biological structures. These electronic properties must be understood and controlled through size and surface modifications in order to enhance the performances of QDs for wider uses in ultrahigh-resolution imaging systems.

# 2.5 Comparison with traditional fluorescent materials

The use of QDs has an important advantage over the use of traditional fluorescent materials in a variety of practices, especially medical imaging practices. This comparison is aimed at shedding light on the major differences in terms of properties and efficiency between QDs and conventional fluorescent dyes.
