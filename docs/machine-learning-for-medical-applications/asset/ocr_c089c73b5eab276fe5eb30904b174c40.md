# 2.5.4 Applications and developments

Patch-based CNNs have found applications in various domains, including medical imaging, where they are used for tasks such as tumor detection and organ segmentation. In these contexts, the patch-based approach enables the analysis of small regions, which is helpful, especially when high point annotations are needed. The advancements in recent years have attempted to solve some of the problems associated with the use of patch-based approaches, including overlapping patches and multiscale analysis. These innovations are intended to enhance the stability of segmentation results and provide a more accurate reflection of the objective data, while still maintaining the computational advantages of the patch-based approaches.
