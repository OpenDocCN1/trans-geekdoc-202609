# 5.5.1 Conformity in making automobiles

One of the best cases is the application of multimodal anomaly detection in the quality control of automobiles. In this context, visual inspection systems are integrated with high-resolution cameras, together with sensors from production equipment. Individual pictures of varying resolution are processed to distinguish surface roughness inaccuracies; yet other sensors offer information concerning machinery and surroundings. Combining such variables of data acquisition allows the system to detect some defects that cannot be easily observed from a visual standpoint, such as those due to machine maladjustment in working parameters. Such an approach has proven to reduce defect rates and enhance the overall quality of products within the organization.

# 5.5.2 Predictive maintenance in the aerospace industry

In aerospace, multimodal anomaly detection has been used in the areas of predictive maintenance of the aircraft engine. When data from several temperature sensors, vibration, and acoustic sensors are analyzed collectively, with the historical maintenance reports and working parameters, one can arrive at a better picture about the health of an engine. A variety of data from these multiple sources are processed by machine learning models to identify valuable patterns that disclose early warning signs of impending failures such as vibration irregularities fluctuations. I have found out that this approach makes it easier to predict the maintenance needs quicker and accurately, lessening the cases of unscheduled downtimes and improving flight safety cases.

# 5.5.3 Structural improvements for enhancing processes in semiconductor production

The multimodal anomaly detection approach has also been employed as a way of improving operations in manufacturing new semiconductors. In this sector, data from other sources such as optical inspection system, environmental sensors, and process logs are used in the observation of the manufacturing of semiconductor wafers. Incoming and outgoing surface defect inspection data is used, while environmental data includes data such as temperature and humidity, which affect product quality.
