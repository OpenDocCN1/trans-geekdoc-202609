# 3.5 Pros and cons of different synthesis methods

The synthesis of QDs involves the following methods, and each of the methods has got its merits and demerits. Learning every one of these methods and their relative advantages and disadvantages is critical to define which technique is better suited depending on the properties and/or applications required for the synthesis of a specific type of QDs.

# 3.5.1 Colloidal synthesis

Versatility and control: QDs obtained by colloidal synthesis have strong size-, shape-, and composition-dependent properties that can be easily tuned by varying the reaction conditions like temperature, concentration of precursors, and reaction time. This versatility makes it possible to synthesize QDs with certain optical characteristics.

Scalability: The overall synthesis process of colloids is not too cumbersome and hence makes it possible to synthesize QDs on a large scale for use in different applications.

Surface functionalization: This approach also permits the alteration of QD surfaces with ligands and coatings, which help stabilize the QDs, as well as improves solubility and compatibility with biological systems.

Complexity of reaction conditions: The problem of achieving uniform size and composition with a desired reaction is a matter of strict control of the reaction conditions and can be difficult with batch-to-batch variability.
