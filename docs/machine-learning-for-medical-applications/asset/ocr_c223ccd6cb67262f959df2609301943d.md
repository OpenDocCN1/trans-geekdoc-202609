# 6.2 Antimicrobial electrospun nanofibers for infection control

Wound infections have always been seen to complicate the treatment process as well as the healing of wounds, as most of them are characterized by worsening outcomes, longer length of stay, and increased cost of treatment. Due to their high surface area, combined with modifiable properties, electrospun nanofibers are a feasible choice in wound care infection control. These advanced fabrics can provide local and long-term antibacterial activity due to the impregnation of antimicrobial agents into the nanofiber structure. This reduces the likelihood of infections and increases the rate of wound healing.

Appliances incorporating antimicrobial electrospun nanofibers employ multipronged strategies to inhibit microbial adhesion and growth. These approaches often involve embedding antimicrobial agents such as silver nanoparticles, antibiotics, and essential oils within the nanofiber scaffold. Silver nanoparticles, for instance, demonstrate strong antimicrobial properties by releasing silver ions that penetrate bacterial cells, rupture the cell membrane, and disrupt essential metabolic activities. Antibiotics can be incorporated to target specific bacterial processes, while essential oils provide broad-spectrum antimicrobial effects. Together, these mechanisms create a synergistic and holistic antimicrobial environment at the wound site, enhancing protection and supporting healing.

The synthesis of antimicrobial electrospun nanofibers uses several methods to incorporate and release antimicrobial agents. Techniques like disposing the polymer with other agents during preparation and infusing an antimicrobial agent onto the electrospun fibers are typical. For incorporation of the antimicrobial agents in the nanofiber matrix, antimicrobial agents are incorporated into the polymer solution before electrospinning, to control distribution. Surface modification techniques include extruding antimicrobial agents onto shaped nanofibers, which can provide homogeneous treatments. Such fabrication methods are vital, whereby ultimate antimicrobial potential and entrenched release of the active agents are considered.
