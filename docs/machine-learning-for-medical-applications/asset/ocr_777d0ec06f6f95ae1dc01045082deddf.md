Another landmark work was ResNet, introduced by He and colleagues in 2015. To enable the training of much deeper neural networks without suffering from performance degradation, they introduced residual connections. This innovation set new benchmarks across multiple computer vision challenges and made it possible to train networks with hundreds of layers.

These milestones together signify the fast pace of growth of deep learning and prove that the field has the ability to learn and progress enormously.
