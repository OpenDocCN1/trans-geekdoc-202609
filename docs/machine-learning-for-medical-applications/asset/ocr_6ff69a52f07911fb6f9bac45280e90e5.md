# 3.3 AI’s role in data processing

AI is needed for the development of data processing in the healthcare field since it immensely enhances the function of diagnostic tools. The increased complexity and volume of healthcare information from genomic data, imaging, and Electronic Medical Records present these important challenges that sometimes cannot be handled by basic data processing techniques. These issues are best addressed by AI technologies, particularly, ML as well as DL, wherein the algorithms perform complex data analysis with homogenous data.

Big data is a repository for patterns and relationships, which are the things ML algorithms do best. Big data is incorporated here to train these algorithms to look for intricate and hard-to-decipher data that indicate some diseases. For instance, ML models in medical imaging can consider hundreds of photos to diagnose and classify issues. Specifically, early illness identification and individualizing therapy require considerable reliance on diagnostic precision and stability that learning and iterative improvement are made possible by these models.

These skills are enriched by a subgenre of ML called DL that employs multilayer neural networks to perform a more complex analytical work. DL models have been remarkably effective in the healthcare industry in the context of identifying patterns of different medical images such as CT and MRI, as well as X-ray images. These algorithms promote making accurate and specific imaging-based diagnoses, since discovery of miniscule abnormalities may not be noticed by a human radiologist. There is a tendency to apply DL algorithm to genomic data and analyze genetic components leading to the illness and come up with better and individual-specific recommendations for treatment.

AI function in data processing was previously defined as including the integration and analysis of several data sources. Most commonly, data relating to healthcare is gathered from several sources, including test results, wearable devices, and patient-reported data. Such paradigms are processed and joined by AI technologies to provide a comprehensive overview of patients’ condition, for example, the multisectoral view and long-term prognoses of the patient’s health condition based on an integration of information from wearable sensors and electronic records.

With the help of NLP and complex data mining, AI is an additional innovation that also enhances data analysis. AI is conducive for identifying regularities and associations that govern therapeutic decisions data mining, a technique of retrieving useful information from significant databases. To support the AI system in extracting relevant information from unstructured data and then integrating it with structured data for more precise diagnostic and decision-making results, NLP makes it possible to analyze the unstructured data and medical literature.
