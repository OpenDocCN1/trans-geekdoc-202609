# 6.3.2 CVD

Equipment costs: The cost of obtaining CVD equipment at the start is expensive, and expansion of the process entails a huge investment in state-of-the-art equipment and facilities. Due to the inherent complexity of CVD systems, maintenance and operation are equally complicated and can only be provided by experts.

Deposition rate and throughput: The deposition rate in continues vapor deposition processes can be low, which affects the throughput and scalability. To achieve a high production rate, it is sometimes difficult to simultaneously ensure the manufacturing of high-quality QDs.

Batch and continuous CVD systems: Thus, developing more advanced batch systems for high volume can be done. By increasing the deposition parameters to an optimum level and buying high throughput systems, there can be increased rates of production.

# 6.3.3 MBE

High costs and complexity: MBE systems are sophisticated and costly, and therefore not well-suited to high-volume manufacturing. The requirement for higher vacuum and the control of various deposition parameters again adds complexity and cost to the production process.

Deposition speed: It should be noted that slow deposition rates are characteristic of MBE and this makes scaling up the production of QDs difficult due to long processing times.

Innovative MBE designs: Increasing the deposition speed and decreasing the cost of equipment are two useful ways to make MBE more scalable; new MBE designs and techniques should be produced for these purposes. Extension of MBE methods and materials to more applications can also be addressed by the identification of even cheaper methods and materials.

# 6.3.4 Hydrothermal and solvothermal synthesis

Scalability of reaction vessels: Hydrothermal and solvothermal syntheses are performed at high temperatures and pressures, and large, mechanically resistant reactors are applied. It can be difficult to scale these vessels up while still staying safely and efficiently designed.

Consistency and uniformity: Lack of uniformity in large-scale synthesis of QDs by hydrothermal methods is one of the challenges in large-area assembly.
