A particularly notable contribution by Alex Krizhevsky, Ilya Sutskever, and Geoffrey Hinton dramatically outperformed earlier approaches in the ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge (ILSVRC) by significantly reducing the classification error rate. The major innovations introduced by AlexNet are illustrated in Figure 7, which highlights its core components and architectural design.

Deep convolutional architecture: The AlexNet has the architecture of a deep CNN with eight layers, of which five are convolutional layers and three are fully connected layers. This depth also made it possible for the network to derive hierarchical feature representations from the raw picture data.

ReLU: Due to the problem of vanishing gradients in previous activation functions, the introduction of ReLU activation functions boosted network performance, accelerated the training phase, and imposed nonlinearity.

Overlapping pooling: Instead of employing fully connected layers, the feature map dimensions were reduced, but important spatial information was maintained by using overlapping max-pooling.
