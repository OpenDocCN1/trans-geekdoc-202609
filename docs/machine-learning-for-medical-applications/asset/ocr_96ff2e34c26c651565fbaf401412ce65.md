A number of examples are presented below to describe how organizations have been able to implement predictive maintenance in their operations. For instance, in manufacturing plants, there are systems that have been developed in order to monitor the health of most crucial equipment-like motors, pumps and compressors. When used together with prior maintenance records, such systems have proven highly effective in enhancing the earlier identification of failed equipment, the affordability of maintenance, and ultimately the least amount of time lost to equipment breakdowns. Likewise in the energy sector, Predictive maintenance has been adopted to help check on turbines and generators to increase reliability and availability of electric power in the market.
