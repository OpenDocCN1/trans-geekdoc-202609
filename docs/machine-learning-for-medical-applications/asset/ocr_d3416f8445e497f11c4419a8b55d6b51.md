# 6.2 AI in personalized medicine

The upright shift of paradigm regarding the diagnosis and treatment of diseases is the use of the AI in the concept of personalized medicine. In the field of personalized medicine, AI is applied for the enhancement of accuracy and efficiency of the therapy being offered by adopting specific therapy that would suit the patient. The integration of advanced intelligence into customized medicine is massive for pharmaceutical advancement in patient treatment and for the healthcare industry as a whole.

AI remains central to the development of personalized medicine where patterns are sought from huge intertwined datasets. For recommendation of the diagnosis and treatment, the information from genetic profile, EHRs, and clinical trial are analyzed using ML algorithms. This feature allows the development of personalized therapy due to genes, medical history, and life preferences of the patient.

Genomic research was one of the main areas where AI can significantly impact personalized medicine. AI systems are capable of searching for genetic markers associated with specific disorders in genomes, enabling accurate risk assessment and personalized treatment plans. For example, AI systems analyze complex genetic codes to predict a person's susceptibility to genetic diseases, helping to guide prevention strategies and the development of individualized drug treatments. This application of AI facilitates the design of precision medicine tailored to treat genetic disorders.

AI also plays a transformative role in drug development and personalized medication management by accounting for the fact that drug responses vary among individuals. This approach, known as pharmacogenomics, helps to minimize the risk of adverse drug reactions and enables the selection of optimal drugs and dosages for each patient. By integrating clinical trial data with broader, real-world datasets, AI facilitates the design of highly personalized treatment regimens, improving both efficacy and safety for diverse patient populations.
