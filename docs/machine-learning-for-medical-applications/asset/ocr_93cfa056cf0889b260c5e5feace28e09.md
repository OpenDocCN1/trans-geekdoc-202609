# 6.1.3 Help identify the disease early on

Disease diagnosis at an early stage is paramount for management and better patient outcome. Using sophisticated techniques of analyzing images, changes that signify emergence of disease, often before the physicians start noticing symptoms can be pointed out. For example, the details in better imaging systems and in more improved image processing techniques can show cancerous detection at its initial stages or other early and pre-clinical conditions in the brain, which may include neurodegenerative diseases. In a way, MIP helps to identify diseases earlier and thus better outcomes are possible for patients.

# 6.1.4 Championing personalized medicine

Personalized treatment is the use of treatment based on individual patient characteristics, thus making it very unique. MIP offers the analysis of the morphological and pathophysiological condition of each patient, which means more individual approach in treatment. Imaging modalities together with image quantification provides features suitable to evaluate patient variability in terms of disease manifestation and treatment. The suitability of treatments in combination with matching client's characteristics increases the efficiency of treatments and minimizes the potential negative outcomes.

**In effect, allowing longitudinal monitoring depends on the achievement of certain conditions that make these results possible.**

Maintenance of disease and therapeutic efficacy is crucial in chronic disorders; this could only be achieved through longitudinal evaluations. MIP enables the evaluation of such serial imaging studies to monitor the temporal changes that occur. Proc am techniques, for example, image registration and change detection allow one to align and compare images obtained at different time points. This capability can be useful for ongoing assessment of how a disease is progressing, or how therapy is working, or how the therapeutic management strategies may need to change.

The advancements in MIP had taken a toll on the clinical front where positive changes on diagnostic effectiveness have been witnessed. As a result, more accurate, precise, and timely diagnosis was made possible from improvements in visualization, segmentation, and analysis of medical images and superimposition of advanced AI technologies. These advancements enhance patient care and treatment and increase efficacy of the treatments as well as development of individualized medicine, which re-emphasize the importance of image processing in contemporary practice.
