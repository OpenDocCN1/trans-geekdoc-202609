Organizing of training data for healthcare decision-making, differential diagnosis, analysis of individual variations for treatment, and population-genetic analysis are all to be incorporated into the AI of advanced models for customized medicine and treatment for chronic diseases. Remote tracking of a patient’s performance by AI can continuously illustrate the progress of an illness as well as the effectiveness of a therapy. Smart clothing and telemetry devices that can track multiple aspects of patient’s health: heart rate and blood sugar, for example, allow direct intervention and control over the patient’s treatment. This semi structured approach to disease management enhances patient-centered care since it offers timely inputs and allows modification of care in accordance with the need of the sick person.

There seems to be great prospects in the future use of AI in such areas, especially in this area of medicine. For instance, it is believed that increased incorporation of superior algorithms into the framework of AI as well as availability of improved data integration tools would dramatically improve the precision and efficiency of personal healthcare regime.
