# 3.5.3 Overview – guidelines and principles for the use of artificial intelligence-based tools

Meanwhile, the legal landscape of AI-based tools, in particular, medical imaging, is becoming a rapidly growing profession. The criteria for approval and regulation of such diagnostic tools are just being set by regulatory authorities such as the EMA and the FDA. All of these recommendations concern the need for validation, post-market oversight, and performance evaluation to ensure that safety and effectiveness of AI technologies in healthcare are fully realized. Before AI systems are included into routine practice, they have to meet specific criteria relating to their reliability, reliability, and evidence-based information that meets the rules mentioned above.

Examination of the ethical question that arises during the process of applying artificial intelligence

But compliance with the law and protection of patients’ information are only part of the ethical debate concerning the use of AI in interpretation of medical imagery. Questions that need to be answered are: appropriate use of AI, consent form, the question of how AI will change the clinical decision-making process. Ethical use of AI in healthcare depends on patient self-governance, communication about the use of AI systems in patient care, and how AI suggestions affect patient’s outcomes. Further, questions include the case of overdependence on such systems in clinical practice and how future AI systems might erode clinical skills and the necessity for constant human interaction with the technology.

It was for this reason that it was important to refer to the above outstanding academic and ethical considerations so as to understand how best to develop and apply ML algorithms in various types of medical image analysis. The development and application of such technologies require data privacy, fairness, transparency, and compliance with the rules regulating the activities of healthcare organizations to support the key objectives of contemporary healthcare strategies.
