# 5.1.2 Role of sensors and detectors

Imaging signals also require sensors and detectors to pick as well as translate signals into images in the digital platform. X-ray and CT imaging utilize detectors that can be categorized as either film or digital; however, digital methods present benefits such as fast image production and flexible image processing. In MRI, detectors are incorporated in the radio frequency coil antenna that receives the signals produced in tissues due to applied magnetic fields. Ultrasound scan as a technology uses piezoelectric transducers, which transform sound waves into electric impulses. The type of sensors and detectors differ with the resolution, sensitivity, and quality of the images collected during the tests.

# 5.1.3 Effectiveness of some parameters of acquisition on image quality

Image acquisition factors such as duration of exposure, radiation dose, and image resolution have a significant effect on picture quality. Examples of quantitative factors that influence the trade-off between radiation dose and image quality in X-ray and CT are tube current, voltage, and slice thickness. Factors influencing contrast and resolution of the images may include field strength, echo time (TE), and repetition time (TR). In ultrasonography, image definition and diagnostic information depend on several factors such as depth of scanning, gain controls, and transducer frequency. It is important to calibrate these values accurately in order to get optimum picture quality at minimum risk to the patients.

# 5.1.4 Methodologies for enhancing the process of image capture

We discuss a range of approaches to make the image acquisition as optimal as possible in terms of image quality and diagnosis. In X-ray and CT imaging, modernization like, modulation of the patient X-ray dose and application of iterative reconstruction algorithms decrease radiation risks while acceptable image quality is provided. In MRI, methods including parallel imaging and the use of advanced pulse sequence can help achieve high-resolution images in shorter amount of time.
