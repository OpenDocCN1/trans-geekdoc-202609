# 6.4 Methods for assessing data precision and consistency

Statistical measures are fundamental for assessing data precision, providing insights into the variability and reliability of measurements. Common statistical techniques include calculating standard deviation, variance, and coefficient of variation. These metrics quantify the dispersion of data points around the mean, offering a measure of precision. For scaffold research, high precision is indicated by low variability in repeated measurements of scaffold properties. Statistics assist in defining relational errors and eliminate possible mistakes while ensuring consistent and replicable experiments.

A reserve of data is crucial when it comes to testing the reliability and validity of a model for forecasting.
