# 2.3 Methods for ensuring consistency in degradation rate measurements

To ensure consistency in degradation rate measurements of biodegradable scaffolds, it was essential to implement standardized measurement protocols. This involves defining uniform procedures for preparing and testing scaffolds to minimize variability between different experiments. Particularly recommended are descriptions regarding the choice of scaffold materials, their handling and preparation, the size of the scaffold samples used, and preconditioning steps. Furthermore, environmental factors like temperature and humidity settings in the fluid must also be kept constant and can have their standard protocols throughout the study.
