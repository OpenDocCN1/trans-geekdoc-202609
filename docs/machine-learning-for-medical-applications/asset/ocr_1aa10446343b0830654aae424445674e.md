# 3.1.2 Manipulating differences in images resolution and its formats

Different techniques may preserve image resolution and/or format, which define how finer details and accuracy of the information captured are affected. Images at higher resolution give better details about the scene but when it comes to image processing or analysis more computational power is needed. On the other hand, coarser images as a result of low resolution will prove difficult to analyze by virtue of containing less detail. To ensure that an algorithm can provide accurate results, it has to be designed to correct the image for different qualities, meaning that the methods of image processing must be scalable depending on the quality of the image; while different formats and resolutions might lead to distortion, it is necessary to make algorithms adapt to a number of imaging conditions.

# 3.2 Scalability and data volume

Medical image analysis is a diagnostic procedure highly affected by the scalability and data volume management problems. Solving these problems is crucial to maintaining the quality and availability of medical imaging services as imaging equipment changes and the volume of information generated increases.
