# 3.4.2 Growth control and precision

Molecular Beam Epitaxy (MBE) offers distinct advantages in the precise control of quantum dot (QD) formation parameters. As shown in Figure 5, the size and composition of QDs can be finely tuned by adjusting the atomic beam flux, substrate temperature, and deposition rate. This high degree of control during the growth process enables the fabrication of QDs with tailored optical properties and minimal size dispersion, which is crucial for applications such as medical imaging. The selection of elemental composition and materials in MBE is often guided by practical considerations including thermal conductivity, mechanical stability, and compatibility with integrated systems for purposes such as ventilation, lighting, heating, and sound insulation.
