# 6.2.2 Personalized treatment approaches

Thus, segmentation aids the formulation of appropriate management strategies for each patient by producing precise descriptions of their anatomy and disease states. For example, in oncology, tumor boundaries enable selection of appropriate therapy cycles to address tumor pathologic type and its anatomic location. In cardiovascular interventions, structural resolution of the heart allows for tweaking of procedures like congenital heart disease. In addition, through input of patient data to the treatment plan, segmentation optimizes therapeutic tactics for problem-solving.

# 6.2.3 Interoperability with complex overview techniques

Optimal segmentation facilitates the application of complicated therapeutic technologies, including image-facilitated procedure and less invasive treatments. In the interventional applications, correct delineation of the regions of interest is crucial for accurate positioning of tools and equipment, thereby increasing the success rate of the procedure and lowering the danger inherent in it. Here, segmentation helps use of robotic systems and navigational tools in operations and also gives immediate feedback on the process.

# 6.2.4 Supervision and modifying management

Segmentation also helps oversee the process of treatment and calculate possible necessary alterations. With help of segmentation, clinicians are able to evaluate progress after treatment of outcomes and identify complications if any. A continuation of such monitoring helps in early modification of patient care plans so that implemented interventions are useful and relevant.

This paper aims to stress the significance of segmentation in nursing care planning and management, treatment planning in particular. Segmentation makes it easier to prepare for surgery, design accurate treatments, plan radiotherapy, develop individualized treatment programs, adapt new advanced treatments, and monitor progression. Such contributions enhance significantly the quality of patients’ treatment and results in clinical settings.
