# 3.2 Mechanical properties

The mechanical properties of electrospun nanofibers have significant influence on the preformation of different applications. Of most importance is biomedical employment due to the fact that the material is strong and flexible as well as long lasting. The size of the nanofiber, type of polymer, and the electrospinning parameters influence the mentioned characteristics. Understanding and controlling these mechanical properties are critical for tailoring nanofibers for the specific applications that include protective clothing, medical dressings, and beds for tissue regeneration.

Tensile strength is one of the mechanical properties that characterizes the strength of electrospun nanofibers to hold stretch without failure. Both the inherent strength of the polymer as well as the radius of the fiber proves to have a profound effect on this property. For this reason, in comparison to wider fibers possessing lesser SAVR, increased resistance to deformation prevents the fibers from bending, and slimmer fiberglass possesses greater tensile strengths. The last essential characteristic to consider is the ability of fibers, in other words, the elasticity of fibers to recover their initial form after deformation.
