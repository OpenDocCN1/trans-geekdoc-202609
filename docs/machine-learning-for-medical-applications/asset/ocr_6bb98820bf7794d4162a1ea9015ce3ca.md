# AI-enhanced diagnostic materials improving sensitivity for disease detection and diagnostics

**Abstract:** The application of artificial intelligence (AI) has changed the process of diagnosing a disease since it has the ability to fuse with diagnostic materials, thus enhancing high sensitivity and precision in medical practice. In this chapter, the author depicts AI-assisted diagnostic technologies with a focus on the significant change on the accuracy and productivity of diseases identification. We delve deeper into some of these key breakthrough areas like real-time data analytics, biosensors, and other advanced AI manufacturing algorithms. The chapter puts a lot of focus on AI when it comes to addressing important aspects, among them being; increased diagnostic sensitivity, increased capacity for handling big data, and real-time monitoring. In addition, it discusses the existing and emerging ethical issues and research prospects concerning AI in diagnostics with regard to data privacy, algorithmic and data bias, and policy. Everything about AI was researched and reviewed for academics, professionals in this field, and lawmakers, who wish to understand and tap into the potential of having AI become a dependable healthcare solution and better diagnostic tool.

**Keywords:** artificial intelligence, diagnostic sensitivity, biosensors, machine learning, data privacy, real-time monitoring
