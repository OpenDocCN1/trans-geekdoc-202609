## 6.4 Innovations in generative models for image synthesis

Drawing special attention to generative models as an effective approach, deep learning was most apparent in picture generation. These models provide a number of modern computer vision services and capabilities in generating brand new pictures of data that seem absolutely genuine.

But later in the development of GANs by Ian Goodfellow and others, generative modeling has advanced significantly. A GAN is usually comprised of two neural networks such as a discriminator and a generator, which are trained in opposition to each other. It plays an important role in evaluating the truth of the artificially perceivable pictures created by the generator. The generator generates visuals that increase in realism because of this adversarial process. Thus, GANs find use in style transfer, data augmentation, and the generation of high-resolution images, among others.

There was also another family of generative models, which focuses on learning a low-dimensional representation of the data and is called VAEs. VAEs encode the input pictures into a latent space with an encoder network and decode images from the latent space using a decoder network. Due to this, VAEs are capable of delivering various good formation pictures of a learned latent distribution that is probabilistic. Applications of VAEs include in-painting, picture denoising, and generating new samples of a dataset that are generated from the learned distribution.

Codifying this idea, the style transfer approaches based on generative models integrate the content of an image with the appearance of another. Convolutional networks are widely used in neural style transfer since they allow the generation of synthetic pictures that combine the appearance of a reference picture with the structural properties of a content picture. Recent developments in this area have allowed the crafting of visually impressive images that resemble the distinct artistic look of certain art movements.

Generally, picture super-resolution aims at developing clear and detailed pictures from low-quality ones as the input data. Conditional generative models are variants of GANs and generative VAEs that utilize other data at the time of generation. For example, deep learning models such as conditional GANs help create an image from a given set of conditions of the image to produce. This makes it possible to synthesize some images in a more controlled and selective way, increasing the application field of image-to-image translation and generation of new and specific content.

Generative models for new image synthesis contribute to the further development of potential computer vision applications. The models enable the creation of accurate and high-quality imagery. These developments enhance not only the production of visual content but also a set of computer vision applications, including data enhancement and artistry.

## 6.5 Applications in real-time computer vision systems

Real-time computer vision systems employ the use of deep learning to perform an initial analysis of observed visual data and provide causal action and reaction. In the field of AI, deep learning has brought a revolutionary change to these systems in many ways and has expanded the existing fields and applications, and situations in which such systems are relevant.

Deep learning has shifted the dynamics of autonomous driving due to its ability to make real-time sensing and decisions. In these systems, deep learning models are employed to analyze camera and sensor data with a view to detect and identify objects such as vehicles, individuals on the road, and road signs. This capability is very important for orientation, avoidance of collisions with other vehicles, and for safe operation in unclear conditions on roads. Deep learning improves advanced driver-assistance systems and fully autonomous vehicles, which is one of its greatest benefits.
