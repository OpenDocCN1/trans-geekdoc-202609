# 5.1.4 Issues in MRI segmentation

Nonetheless, MRI has many valuable attributes in segmentation task; several complications are observed in this domain. Listed among the main issues was noise and artifacts, because they often impair image definition and segmentation. These include movement by the patient and the internal organs, susceptibility effects, and variation in the chemical shift. There was also the issue of differences in image acquisition and variability in patient health states that affects the segmentation results. Solving these challenges often involves complicated preprocessing as well as strong segmentation models to help in producing the right results.

# 5.1.5 Recent improvements and change

With development in the MRI, there is strong evidence of better picture quality, scan duration, and resolution. Technologies like high-field MRI scanners, better coils, and parallel imaging methods have improved efficiencies in getting better resolute image. In addition, the expansions of both machine learning and DL have indicated value in enhancing the segmentation and analysis of MRI. Such refinements also help extend the applicability of MRI procedures as well as fine-tune their diagnosing abilities concerning a vast variety of diseases.

MRI continues to be as one of the main methods of creating images in medicine and is lauded for providing both high contrast and detail for soft tissue. These features along with continuous development make clinical biochemistry an invaluable diagnostic aid and research tool for modern medicine.

# 5.2 Computed tomography

CT is an old imaging technique that involves taking many simple X-ray images from different orientations and assembling them in layers to produce whole body picture. This method is very effective in numerous various therapeutic applications since it is analyzed for a comprehensive view of internal structures.
