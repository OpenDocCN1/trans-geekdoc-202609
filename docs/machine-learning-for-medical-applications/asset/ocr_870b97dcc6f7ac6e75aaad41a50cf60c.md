The clinical practice is a field where various applications can be described to help better understand the patients’ conditions.

Integrated imaging strategies are more and more used in different clinical processes. The utilization of PET/CT is common in oncology as it reconstructed both metabolism of tumor and its anatomical distribution in staging, treatment planning, and evaluation. MRI and fMRI are widely employed in neurology in combination to assess the structural and functional changes of the brain in patients with neurological diseases. Likewise in cardiology, integrating MRI and echocardiography offers precise analysis of the heart structure and function in evaluating cardiovascular disease.

# 5.5.3 Challenges and considerations

Several difficulties arise in the integration of multimodal imaging data. Variation in spatial resolution, contrast, and the method and protocol of acquisition between the modalities can sometimes make the registration and fusion tasks challenging. Multiple modal data has added complexity implying that it needs sophistical computational treatment and methods in order to get integrated rightly to derive correct meaning. Some of these challenges include data management and storage, as multisource and multistep capture and construction of multimedia data require sound solutions for acquiring, organizing, storing, and retrieving Big Data.
