Ability to produce various materials: These methods can be used to synthesize most kinds of QD materials including those that are challenging to synthesize in other methods.

Limited size control: The two main criteria, size and uniformity are difficult to control accurately, which in turn cause fluctuations in optical properties of the QDs.

Safety concerns: While using high pressure and temperature, these methods result in a number of risks; mishaps maybe prevented by practicing adequate safety measures and using protective gear.

# ### 3.5.4 Laser ablation

High purity: Laser ablation techniques synthesize QDs with good purity because they use a technique that directly removes material from the target in a controlled manner.

Flexibility in materials: It enables one to synthesize QDs out of any recoverable material, no matter how complex when using other methodologies.

Low yield and scalability: Laser ablation in aprotic solvents offers the disadvantage of relatively small production of QDs when compared to other approaches for mass production.

Complexity of equipment: The apparatus used for laser ablation is costly and intricate, which makes it slightly restricted for some uses.

Consequently, depending on the application, each synthesis method for QDs exhibits unique advantages and disadvantages. The selection of the method depends on the features that are required in the QDs, the level of production, and available tools.
