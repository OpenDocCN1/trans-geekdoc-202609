# 4.4 AI’s role in enhancing specificity

Some of the aspects include sensitivity in diagnosing patients, low chances of false negatives, and specificity in diagnosing patients, where the test has the least chance of giving a wrong impression that the patient has the disease. Getting the specificity as high as possible is important in the diagnosis of the disease so that those who are negative by the tests are truly negative, healthy, and devoid of the disease because such tests and their results induce anxiety.

In a number of ways, it has become possible to enhance diagnostic specificity with the help of AI. One of the core breakthroughs is attributed to AI’s ability to provide virtually perfect analysis of complex data. Many diagnostic data in the form of genetic markers, as well as diagnostic images, are most accurate to be analyzed with the help of modern ML tools, including DL. Due to these features, it is possible to obtain constantly improving high specific algorithms suitable for learning small differences between normal and pathological conditions using large database, which is necessary for increasing the diagnostic reliability.
