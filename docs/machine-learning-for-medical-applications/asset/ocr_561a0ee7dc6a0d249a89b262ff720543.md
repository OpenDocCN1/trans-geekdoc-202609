There are few variations of the CNN-RNN mix model depending on how the spatial and temporal parts are combined. In two-stream CNN-RNN model, the CNN and RNN are two independent streams: the CNN takes spatial information in each frame and the RNN takes the temporal part. These two networks yield their outputs in the final layers for classifying as to whether the invoked input data demonstrate normal behavior. In the other model, known as joint architecture CNN-RNN, the output of the CNN can be directly incorporated into the RNN at each time step, allowing for spatial and temporal information to be processed at the same time.
