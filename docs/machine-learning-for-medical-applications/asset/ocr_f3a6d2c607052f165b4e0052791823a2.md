# ### 2.2.3 Third and fourth waves of AI development for modern AI (2000s–2010s)

The availability of new algorithms of ML, known as DL, since the early 2000s helped churn the field. Of course, the field of image identification received an ample shot in the arm when AlexNet posted an unprecedented performance in the 2012 ImageNet Challenge by demonstrating the viability of such DL procedures. CNNs were also invented in this period and were popularly used in processing and diagnosis in the medical fields.

# ### 2.2.4 Implementation of AI in healthcare applications (2010–present)

During 2010s, growth of applications using AI in healthcare system was noticeable in more applications in the fields of genomics, and predictive and diagnostic imaging. Two examples of technologies cleared for use are AI algorithms for breast cancer and diabetic retinopathy, which show the future ability of AI in improving diagnostic performance. AI’s usefulness in enhancing the quality of patient management and clinical decision-making was put to evidence by the development of AI-based e-Hope and patient analytics systems.

# ### 2.2.5 Explainability in artificial intelligence: a timeline (from 2020s)

The two concepts of explainable AI and ethical issues have received much attention in the past few years to address the concerns over transparency and responsibility in AI systems in the context of the healthcare sector. Continuous efforts in maintaining that AI technologies are applied appropriately and beneficially was demonstrated by the creation of guidelines for the appropriate use of AI and the techniques that are being newly introduced for enhancing the explainability of AI systems. These advancements are critical in order to ensure that the use of AI-based diagnostics in practice is reliable and effectively integrated.

They represent the progressive process in AI advancement and its applicability to diagnostic equipment. All of these steps have contributed to further evolve the abilities of AI and usher in the space needed for disease diagnosis and healthcare advancement.
