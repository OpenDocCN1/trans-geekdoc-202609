# 5.2 Loss functions and optimization algorithms

So, loss functions, which are also known as objective functions, are critical in training deep learning models because they provide a measure of discrepancy between the goal values and the expected outcomes. Figure 16 shows different loss functions and optimization algorithms in the deep learning system, describing how they are used in the training and enhancement of pointing models. The choice of loss function has significant influence on the efficacy and performance of a model.

Cross-entropy loss, often used in classification problems, gives rise to the measure of divergence between the real class distribution and the expected probabilistic distribution. This type of loss function provides an entirely unique gradient indication to shift the model parameters during training and is very useful for multiclass classification problems.

MSE is one of the most famous measures employed in cases involving regression issues. It is designed to estimate the average of the square of the residuals between the actual and the predicted values.
