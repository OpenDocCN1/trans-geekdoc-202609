# #### 4.4.5 Transformers for segmentation

Originally deployed in natural language processing, transformers are increasingly achieving impressive performance in medical image segmentation. The self-attention feature of the transformers allows the model to take into account the global dependencies and the interaction between some parts of the image. These abilities are utilized by architectures including vision transformers (ViTs) and segmentation variants to enhance management of structures of various complexities in images. Proposing transformers into segmentation models is a recent practice and would enhance the advancement of segmentation and generalization as well.

These DL architectures are a range of models that have been developed to improve the medical image segmentation. Each of them brings value adding to its strengths – from feature extraction and localization to context awareness and attention. These enhancements are a result of continuing evolution of these models and the development of new techniques that increase segmentation performance, which in turn improves clinical output and research.
