# 3.4 Applications of U-Net beyond biomedical imaging

Developed for biomedical image segmentation, U-Net has shown great flexibility and accuracy in numerous applications outside the biomedical field. The model's encoder-decoder build illustrated that this architecture can handle many forms of segmentation across different domains with the incorporation of symmetric skip connections. This flexibility was due to the fact that U-Net was able to capture the big picture context as well as a high level of detail of the image and was therefore suited for many other segmentation problems outside the healthcare domain.

# 3.4.1 Satellite as well as aerial image segmentation

In the field of remote sensing, there has been successful application of U-Net for the purpose of segmenting satellite and aerial images. The model was useful in the identification of various land cover types, detection of changes in vegetation cover, and extraction of geographic features, which has been used in environmental monitoring, urban planning, and disaster management. The study attributed the successes in the use of U-Net to its capability of processing high-resolution images and extracting spatial information for accurate classification of different features on the land, and offering solutions on pertinent issues affecting urban and rural development.

# 3.4.2 Self-driving cars and road environment understanding

The automotive industry has utilized U-Net for segmentation problems connected with autonomous driving. This work, proposed by the authors, shows that the architecture of the network permits the segmenting of road scenes and shows the accurate location of lanes, vehicles, pedestrians, and traffic signs. With the help of the images collected by cameras installed on the vehicles, U-Net helps to create new generations of the ADAS and autonomous navigation systems. This ability to distinguish many components of a road, with precision, helps make real-time decisions and improves the safety and operation of self-driving cars.

# 3.4.3 Industrial and manufacturing applications

In industries, U-Net has been used in quality assurance and inspection problems. The model's strength in segmenting these manufactured part images allows for the discovery of defects such as cracks and dents on the surface of the manufactured product.
