# 2.4 Key technological innovations

# 2.4.1 New developments in imaging hardware and sensor devices

There are different stages in the development and progress of medical image hardware; major changes in the kind and quality of images have led to improvements in diagnosis. These early imaging systems had poorly developed sensors or detectors that created limitations in sensitivity and/or resolution that hampered the work of a diagnostic system. The latest detectors have been developed from high-resolution and various innovative sensor technologies, which make the images clearer. Technologies that include digital radiography applied in this facility to replace the conventional film-based technologies with digital sensors have helped to improve image quality, reduce radiation exposure, and hence improve the efficiency of the imaging process. In addition, the design of the solid-state detectors and photostimulable phosphor plates has also enabled improved image acquisition and processing.

# 2.4.2 Use of GPUs to support image processing endeavors

Graphics processing units (GPUs) have been successfully introduced into MI processing pipelines and their addition has enhanced computational performance many fold. Originally developed for graphics computations, these GPUs have a processing structure amenable to the computations needed in medical imaging; parallel. Their use has allowed for increased throughput in terms of imaging data volumes that required processing, tasks such as image reconstruction, enhancement, and analysis. Technology advancement in GPU has helped in the realization of complicated algorithms such as those used in deep learning with regard to the acceleration of the medical image analysis.
