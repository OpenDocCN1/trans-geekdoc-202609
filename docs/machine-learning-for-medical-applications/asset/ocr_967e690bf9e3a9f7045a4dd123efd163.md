The random forests comprise one more ensemble method that accumulates the decision trees to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of the anomaly detection process. A random forest generally builds many decision trees on a random sample of the data sets and combines the individual forecast through voting. Random forests are powerful for processing big data with lots of features and low risk of overfitting. They are very effective for use in anomaly detection scenarios when data has many features that not be easily modeled by a single construct. Random forests can be used to detect anomalies since the various trees capture different decision boundaries across the trees to boost the detection capability.
