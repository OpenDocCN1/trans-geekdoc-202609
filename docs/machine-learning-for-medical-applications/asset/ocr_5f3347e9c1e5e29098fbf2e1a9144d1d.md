# 3.2.4 Recurrent U-Net: adding recurrent layers

The proposed technique, recurrent U-Net, improves the original architecture by adding Recurrent Neural Network layers into the U-Net structure. These recurrent layers are used with the idea to reflect time-related dependencies and the contextual information in multiple iterations, which enhances the model’s potential for the multi-segmentation problems. Recurrent U-Net is most effective for the cases when images are sequenced, for example in dynamic medical imaging and temporal data analysis, wherein temporal connection and context are essential for segmentation.

# 3.2.5 U-Net3D: toward the extension of U-Net for volumetric data

U-Net builds upon its older version by extending the architecture for 3D medical scan and other volumetric data. In this manner, by extending the 2D convolutions to 3D, U-Net3D permits the segmentation of volumetric protagonists, characterized by spatial coherency in three dimensions. This makes this extension useful to overcome the challenges of the conventional methods of differentiating complex 3D anatomical structures and covers a wider aspect of volumetric datasets. U-Net3D was designed for volumetric data, and its improvement in this area makes it suitable for use in tasks such as medical image segmentation.
