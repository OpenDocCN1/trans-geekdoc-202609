# 6.5.5 Training data and annotation requirements

The training of transformer-based models is specifically dependent upon the existence of such massive, high-quality annotated data. Deep learning-based transformers needs big amount of data for capturing the sophisticated structures and dependency. Small set of annotated data in some domains hamper the working and application of models. Possible solutions can be a concentrated restoration of semisupervised and unsupervised learning methods, also as information augmentation procedures and the utilization of transfer learning from pretrained models, to minimize the dependence on enormous labeled datasets.

Despite their successes, DeepLab models face challenges related to computational demands and fine detail preservation, highlighting the need for ongoing refinement and optimization.
