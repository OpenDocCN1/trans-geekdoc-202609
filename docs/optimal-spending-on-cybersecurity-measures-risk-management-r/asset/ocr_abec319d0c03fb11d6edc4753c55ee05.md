# ## Operational risk management

Operational risk is defined by the Bank of International Settlements (BIS) as “the risk of loss resulting from inadequate or failed internal processes, people and systems or from external events (strategic and reputational risk is not included in this definition for the purpose of a minimum regulatory operational risk capital charge)” (Basel Committee on Banking Supervision, 2001). Legal risk is included in this definition. Operational risk is therefore the sum of operating, information systems, compliance and legal risk.
