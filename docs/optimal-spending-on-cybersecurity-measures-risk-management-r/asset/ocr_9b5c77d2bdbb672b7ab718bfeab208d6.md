# Likelihood rating scale

Table 7.4 shows the quantitative scale for the impact rating based on a score of 1–5.

Table 7.4 A sample likelihood rating scale (Kissoon, 2020).

| Likelihood | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| Almost Certain 5 | • Almost certain.<br>• Minimum of once in 6 months.<br>• Greater than 90% chance of occurrence over the life of the asset or project. |
| Likely 4 | • Likely.<br>• Minimum of once in 1 year.<br>• 60% to 90% chance of occurrence over the life of the asset or project. |
| Possible 3 | • Possible.<br>• Minimum of once in 5 years.<br>• 30% to 60% chance of occurrence over the life of the asset or project. |
| Unlikely 2 | • Unlikely.<br>• Minimum of once in 10 years.<br>• 10% to 30% chance of occurrence over the life of the asset or project. |
| Rare 1 | • Rare.<br>• Minimum of once in 20 years.<br>• <10% chance of occurrence over the life of the asset or project. |
