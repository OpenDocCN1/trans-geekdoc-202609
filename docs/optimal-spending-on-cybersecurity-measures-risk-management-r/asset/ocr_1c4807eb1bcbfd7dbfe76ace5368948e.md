SAD refers to the information on a payment card used for authentication at the time of a purchase, including data from the full magnetic strip, card security code (CSC, CVV2, CID, CAV2) and PIN. ABC University has outsourced the transaction processing of payment card data to a third-party service provider, Moneris Solutions. The university's vendor is responsible for compliance with regulatory and legal statutes, as well as protecting such information from dissemination to unauthorized parties.
