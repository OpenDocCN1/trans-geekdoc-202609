+   1 Agile and lean refer to teams iterating, with short development cycles and fast feedback. Agile focuses on culture and is agnostic in regard to the tools used.
+   2 DevOps refers to how engineering organizations collaborate using cross-functional teams. DevOps starts with culture and drives towards tooling.
+   3 System reliability engineering (SRE) refers to how engineering organizations automate, entrusting highly scaled operations to people with a software engineering mindset. SRE starts with tooling and drives towards culture.
+   4 DevOps variants (such as “SecDevOps”) involve the insertion or addition of another organization/practice early in the SDLC, and the prevalence of these different types of DevOps speaks to the increasing integration of functions in modern organizations.

DevOps incorporates many variations on the theme. However, most observers would agree that the following capabilities are common to virtually all DevOps cultures: (1) collaboration, (2) automation, (3) continuous integration, (4) continuous delivery, (5) continuous testing, (6) continuous monitoring and (7) rapid remediation.
