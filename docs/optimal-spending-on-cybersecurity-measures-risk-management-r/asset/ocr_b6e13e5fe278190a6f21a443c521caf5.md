# Collaboration

Development and IT operations work together, and DevOps extends far beyond the IT organization due to the need for collaboration, which extends to all stakeholders in the delivery of software.

> > "The foundation of DevOps success is how well teams and individuals collaborate across the enterprise to get things done more rapidly, efficiently and effectively" – Tony Bradley, “Scaling Collaboration in DevOps”, DevOps.com.

# Automation

DevOps relies heavily on automation and tools and on toolchains to automate large parts of the end-to-end software development and deployment process.
