# ABSTRACT

The photovoltaics (PVs) as clean power generation has been highly penetrated in distribution networks, which motivates the network towards highly complex operation status. In this paper, an optimal real-time Voltage/Var control (ORT-VVC) is proposed to reduce power losses and mitigate fluctuations by regulating the reactive power of the PV inverters. The ORT-VVC proposed using drop-based multi-agent reinforcement learning (DC-MARL) is established. The structure is constructed in distribution network with network partition and the multi-agents. Then, the control model is formulated as a Q-var drop function with parameters, to optimize the control parameters instead of minimizing reactive power output directly, which aims to improve the real-time VVC performance for distribution network. The DC-MARL model is established and numerical simulations are performed on the IEEE 123-bus test system to demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method.

# 1. Introduction

The distribution network develops towards a active pattern with increasing penetration of renewable energy sources (RES) [1], which is environment friendly. According to the international energy agency, the grid capacity will increase nearly twice from 2022 to 2027. The penetration of renewable energy like wind and turbine (WT) is increasingly intermittent and uncertain, which leads to frequent voltage fluctuations in distribution network [2]. The Voltage/Var control (VVC) is realized by controlling reactive power output to mitigate voltage fluctuations and reduce power losses of level [3], 4]. As for the control of distributed resources, e.g., PV inverters provide new means for VVC implementation [5], 6].

For the VVC of distributed resources, various methods have been proposed. From the point of view of control method, they fall into three categories: centralized control, local control, and distributed control [7], 8). The centralized control requires a central coordinator and system for information storage, communication, and interaction [9], 10]. However, the distribution network facilities are relatively backward, and the network operator doesn't have the benefit of global communication. The required calculation amount is large, it is difficult to realize the optimal real-time control for distribution network especially with the high-penetration of RES [11]. The local control can make decisions based on local information and does not have high requirements for communication in the distribution network. Meanwhile, the local control faces communication delays and data delays [12], 13). It is true that the local control can regulate the voltage within a reasonable range, but without coordination between local agents, the voltage regulation capability of the devices cannot be fully utilized. The distributed control doesn't achieve a better result in terms of reduction, robustness and scalability enhancement, compared with the centralized methods. The distributed control can be realized by the distributed algorithm, which utilizes the local information and communication to improve the performance of the network.

In other words, it follows the physical law of power system operation. As for the VVC of the distribution network, the model-based method can be realized with renewable VVC droop control.

The method proposed in this paper is different from the model-based method which has been used in VVC of distribution network and attracts lots of attention [27–21]. The reinforcement learning (DRL) is a data-driven method, that is based on the Markov Game. The DRL generator is realized through online training and combines with well-trained neural networks (DNNs). The decisions can be determined in milliseconds. In other words, the DRL can act in real-time with high optimum searching. Thus, the DRL method can optimize the operation of distribution network without variation. In fact, a multi-agent reinforcement learning (MADRL) method is proposed to optimize the static variance $Q$ reactive power for managing the bus loads, which can be decentralized and executed independently. In this paper, the multi-agent reinforcement learning (MADRL) method is implemented as a model and control. The droop parameters are optimized by the MADRL, realizing the optimal real-time VVC for distribution network. The main contributions are as follows:

- An ORT-VVC method is proposed to mitigate voltage fluctuation and reduce power losses. The ORT-VVC is implemented via a model and control. The PAC-DRL method is implemented together with the Q-V droop control, the relationship between voltage and reactive power with the optimum searching and online ability of MADRL for distribution network with high penetration of RES.
- A novelty penalty function is proposed to improve convergence rate during training. The particular define function is replaced by a linear penalty function which equals 0 when the training time of one episode is finished. With this method, the episode occurs more frequently and the convergence rate will be reduced.
