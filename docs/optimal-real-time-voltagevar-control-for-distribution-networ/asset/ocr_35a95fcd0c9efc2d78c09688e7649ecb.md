# 6 Conclusion

This paper proposes a DC-MADRAL for real-time VVC control of distribution networks. The UVC uses certain rules to reduce power losses and voltage fluctuation, in which PV inverters are connected in a distributed framework. Along with a Q-driven control model with variable parameters, the partially observed Markov decision model is employed to collect optimal strategy. Through various methods, the proposed approach combines model and data driven together for enhancing stability in the power system network.
