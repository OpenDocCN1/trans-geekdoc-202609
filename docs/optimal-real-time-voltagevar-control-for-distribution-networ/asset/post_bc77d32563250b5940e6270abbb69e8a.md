## 5.3. DC-MADRL online test performance

With utilizing MADRL, the agents share the critic network and select the actions via their actor network during the training process, and during the execution, each agent determines the optimal action based on the local observation and trained actor network. After the training, the DC-MADRL performance is verified through the test data. The test set is constructed with the random sample of 120/480 scenarios, then the remaining 360 scenarios are used for training. The online decision speed of the proposed DC-MADRL is 19.63ms, which implies that it meets the real-time VCC requirement.

Aiming at verifying the effectiveness of the proposed method compared with the traditional Q-V drop control (QVDTC) (28), the drop control single-agent deep reinforcement learning (DC-SAD) is constructed. The TVQDC has a fixed drop curve and parameters, which is not adaptable to changing environments. The proposed method is fully data-driven, so it is easier to compare with the data-driven method. The DC-SAD is used to verify the accuracy of proposed distributed VCC scheme, as the only difference between DC-SAD and DC is distributed method. Then, the comparison results of the online test performance for these four methods are shown in Table 2.

As seen in Fig. 8, the proposed reinforcement has a better convergence performance than employing the different punishment function. During the training process for the proposed linear punishment function, the convergence rate is improved by 42% compared to the quadratic function. With the linear punishment function, the training process of DC-MADRL is shown in Fig. 9. As seen in Fig. 9, the value of the distributed network is regulated within the allowable scope as (0.95, 1.03). However, the power losses also converge to 0.008 after 2360 episodes training. Therefore, as shown in Fig. 8 and Fig. 9, the proposed method has converged with the proposed linear punishment function.

From Table 2, it can be seen that comparison of model based TVQDC and data-driven methods, the power losses are reduced more than the data-driven methods, which results from the TVQDC is DC-MADRAL has the average level to 1.0pu than TDVC, which can also give much less losses than the latter, which includes the joint of data-driven model and has better ability in loss reduction. For the DC-SAD and DC-MADRAL, the average and power losses are nearly the same for both methods, which have the high accuracy of distributed optimization, that proposed method can regulate voltage in time and effectively reduce power losses for the distribution network.
