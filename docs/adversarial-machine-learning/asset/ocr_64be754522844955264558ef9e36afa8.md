The denominator in the above expression is strictly positive since \(\alpha_\tau^{opt} > 0, \alpha_{\tau+1}^{opt} > 0\) and \(\mu_{\tau-1}^{(\alpha^{opt})} \ge 0\). Further, from assumption (B.4), we have that \(\alpha_\tau^{opt} > \alpha_{\tau+1}^{opt} > 0\), and hence, the above numerator is strictly less than zero.\(^2\) Thus, we have \(\Delta_{\alpha^{opt},\alpha'} = D(\alpha^{opt}) - D(\alpha') < 0\), from which we conclude that \(D(\alpha^*) = D(\alpha^{opt}) < D(\alpha')\). This contradicts assumption (B.3) that \(\alpha^*\) is optimal, thus showing that any sequence with a sub-sequence of its non-zero elements that is not monotonically non-decreasing is non-optimal. Hence, every sub-sequence of the non-zero elements of any optimal attack sequence must be monotonically non-decreasing.
