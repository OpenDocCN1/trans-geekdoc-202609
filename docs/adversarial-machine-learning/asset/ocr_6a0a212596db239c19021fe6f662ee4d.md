**COROLLARY 7.18** Consider a run of Algorithms 7.1 and 7.4 with \( \hat{D} \in \mathfrak{N} \), \( C > 0 \), convex loss, and translation-invariant kernel. Denote by \( \hat{f}^* \) and \( \tilde{f} \) the classifiers parametrized by weight vectors \( \hat{\mathbf{w}} \) and \( \tilde{\mathbf{w}} \), respectively, where these vectors are related by \( \hat{\mathbf{w}} = \tilde{\mathbf{w}} + \boldsymbol{\mu} \) with \( \boldsymbol{\mu} \sim^{iid} \text{Laplace}(\mathbf{0}, \lambda) \) in Algorithm 7.4. For any \( \epsilon > 0 \) and \( \delta \in (0, 1) \), if \( 0 < \lambda \le \min \left\{ \frac{\epsilon}{2^4 \log_e 2\sqrt{D}}, \frac{\epsilon \sqrt{D}}{8 \log_e \frac{2}{\delta}} \right\} \) then \( \text{Pr} \left( \|\hat{f}^* - \tilde{f}\|_\infty \le \frac{\epsilon}{2} \right) \ge 1 - \frac{\delta}{2} \).

**Proof** As in the proof of Theorem 7.11 we can use the Chernoff trick to show that, for an Erlang \( 2\hat{D} \)-distributed random variable \( X \), the choice of \( t = (2\lambda)^{-1} \), and for any \( \epsilon > 0 \)
\[\text{Pr} \left( \|\hat{f}^* - \tilde{f}\|_\infty > \epsilon/2 \right) \le \frac{\text{E} [e^{tX}]}{e^{\epsilon t \sqrt{\hat{D}}/2}} \\
\le (1 - \lambda t)^{-2\hat{D}} e^{-\epsilon t \sqrt{\hat{D}}/2} \\
= 2^{2\hat{D}} e^{-\epsilon \sqrt{\hat{D}}/(4\lambda)} \\
= \exp \left( \hat{D} \log_e 4 - \epsilon \sqrt{\hat{D}}/(4\lambda) \right) . \]


Provided that \( \lambda \le \epsilon / \left( 2^4 \log_e 2\sqrt{\hat{D}} \right) \) this is bounded by \( \exp \left( -\epsilon \sqrt{\hat{D}}/(8\lambda) \right) \). Moreover, if \( \lambda \le \epsilon \sqrt{\hat{D}} / \left( 8 \log_e \frac{2}{\delta} \right) \), then the claim follows.

To relate \( f^* \) and \( \tilde{f} \), we exploit smoothness of regularized ERM with respect to small changes in the RKHS itself. We begin with a technical lemma that we will use to exploit the convexity of the regularized empirical risk functional; it shows a kind of converse to Remark 7.14 relating that functions with risks that are close in value will also be close in proximity.

**LEMMA 7.19** Let \( R \) be a functional on Hilbert space \( \mathcal{H} \) satisfying \( R[f] \ge R[f^*] + \frac{a}{2} \|f - f^*\|_{\mathcal{H}}^2 \) for some \( a > 0 \), \( f^* \in \mathcal{H} \), and all \( f \in \mathcal{H} \). Then \( R[f] \le R[f^*] + \epsilon \) implies \( \|f - f^*\|_{\mathcal{H}} \le \sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon}{a}} \), for all \( \epsilon > 0, f \in \mathcal{H} \).

**Proof** By assumption and the antecedent
\[\|f - f^*\|_{\mathcal{H}}^2 \le \frac{2}{a} (R[f] - R[f^*]) \\
\le \frac{2}{a} (R[f^*] + \epsilon - R[f * ]) \\
= \frac{2\epsilon}{a} . \]
Taking square roots of both sides yields the result.
