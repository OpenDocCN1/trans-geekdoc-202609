# 6.7.3 CLINICAL ASPECTS OF CURCUMIN

Past research studies have shown the potent role of curcumin to treat AD where curcumin directly comes into contact with various signaling molecules, including apoptotic proteins, certain pro-inflammatory cytokines, phosphorylase kinase. All these studies give solid evidence that shows the efficacy of curcumin in the clinical field trials [56]. Until now, approximately nine human trials have been done related to curcumin in the case of AD interventions that include diagnosis, therapy, and prevention (Table 6.1).
