AI will boost the identification and diagnosis of COVID-19, since there is an immediate need to have reliable and low diagnostic tests because many countries at present do not have the necessary tests or tools to differentiate COVID-19 from the “common flu.” Certain AI algorithms could be used as a primary screening method for suspected infections, to send the high-risk patients to a laboratory for further examination and tests and finally to isolate and quarantine them if required.
