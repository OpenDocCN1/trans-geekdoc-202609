# 5.8 GENES LINKED WITH ALZHEIMER DISEASE

Several numbers of different genes, including PSEN-1, APP, and PSEN-2, play a potent role in the progression of this devastating disease. Mutation in these genes results in the generation of amyloid plaques whose accumulation outside the neurons causes hindrance in cell signaling pathways (Tables 5.1 and 5.2).
