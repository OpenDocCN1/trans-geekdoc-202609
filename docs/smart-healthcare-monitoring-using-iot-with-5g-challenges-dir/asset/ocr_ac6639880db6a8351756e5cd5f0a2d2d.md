# 9.9 THE LAYOUT OF THE SMART HOSPITAL SYSTEM: DESIGNING THE FUTURE HEALTHCARE

In this framework, the patient’s platform can send alarming signals to the nurse and doctors, which will be seen as a vibration in the devices. In addition, with the help of the sensors, the patient’s health can be monitored in real time.

In the critical case, the nurse itself can send alarming signals to the doctor in real-time, so that the patient can be treated immediately. The information sent by the sensors will be stored in the cloud and will also help in the effective examination of the patient, including the patient’s health history and earlier diagnosis.
