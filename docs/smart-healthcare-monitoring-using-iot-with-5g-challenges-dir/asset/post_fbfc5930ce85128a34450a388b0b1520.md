## 5.23 CONCLUSION

Alzheimer’s is an incurable insidious disease that usually affects the people from all over the world because of the strong reduction in mortality rate and improvement in medical science that results in high life expectancy and ultimately increases the risk of Alzheimer’s that is associated with mental illness or dementia.
