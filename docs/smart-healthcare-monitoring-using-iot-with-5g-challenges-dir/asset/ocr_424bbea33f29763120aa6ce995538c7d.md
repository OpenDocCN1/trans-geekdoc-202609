# 9.8 PATIENT’S PLATFORM

In any health-related system, it is necessary to have a patient’s platform. In the patient’s platform, the patients with the less critical situation and those who have no attendant can easily take care of themselves as they easily call the nurses whenever they needed. But for the patients with a more critical situation and who are not in their consciousness, their attendees/relatives can use this platform to call nurses whenever needed.
