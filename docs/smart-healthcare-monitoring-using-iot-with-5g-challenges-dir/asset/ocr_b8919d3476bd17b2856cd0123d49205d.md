Pace et al. [11] proposed INTER-Health framework aims to impose an interactive IoT framework for the decentralized smartphone tracking of the lifestyle of people to prevent health problems emerging from nutrition and physical activity disorders. This screening process can be delegated from the health center to the homes of the tracked individuals, and assisted by the use of on-body physical activity sensors during mobility.

Inderpreet et al. [12] presented the structure of the sensors to easily demonstrate how the sensors perform in the healthcare sector.
