+   - Malignant tumor

A tumor which does not affect the healthy tissues, does not spread to non-adjacent tissues, and does not grow unexpectedly known as benign tumor. Moles are one of its examples, while a pre-malignant tumor is a pre-carcinogenic stage and, can be identified as a cancer, if not treated.

Malignant neoplasm is also a kind of tumor that worsens with time and the death of the infected individual is eventually the outcome. Within the skull, tumor cells are present and develop within the skull, which is considered as the primary tumor.

Incipient brain tumors are malignant brain tumors. Outside a skull, the tumor emerges and reaches to region of the skull called secondary tumor. One of the examples of secondary tumors are metastatic tumors. In the skull, the tumor takes its place and interferes with the brain’s normal functioning.
