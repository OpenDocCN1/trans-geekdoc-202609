## 15. Elaborate on the role of procedural abstraction within your program.

By encapsulating the algorithm within a procedure, I have significantly reduced the complexity of my program. This simplification allows me to reuse the algorithm at multiple points in my program without the need for redundant code. For example, when I need to calculate the average score for 15 students, I can simply call the procedure 15 times. Without this procedural approach, I would have to replicate the entire code 15 times instead of just once.

Furthermore, the use of procedures ensures that errors associated with the `scrambleWord` functionality are localized within the procedure itself. Without procedures, I would need to inspect the entire program to pinpoint the source of any errors related to this functionality.
