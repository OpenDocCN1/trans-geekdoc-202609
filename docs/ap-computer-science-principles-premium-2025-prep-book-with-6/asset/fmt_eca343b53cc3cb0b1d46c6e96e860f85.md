### [49](#09_Diagnostic.xhtml#dia-49). (A)

2/3 on one computer could equal 0.66666666666667. However, 2/3 on another computer could equal 0.667. There is no common protocol among computers for the number of decimal places for a real number. In an overflow error, 2/3 on one computer will not equal 2/3 on a second computer.
