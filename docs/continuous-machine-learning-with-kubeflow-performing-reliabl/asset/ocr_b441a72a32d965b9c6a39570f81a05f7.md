**Mean Absolute** The average of the absolute differences between the actual and predictions values. It tells how wrong the predictions were. It also measures the magnitude error, and gives an idea direction, so a value of o indicates no error or perfect predictions.

**Root Mean Square** The R^2 (or R Squared) metric talks about an indication of the goodness of fit for our predictions with respect to the original values. In statistical terminology, it is also known as the coefficient of determination. Its value lies in between o and 1 for no-fit and perfect fit respectively.
