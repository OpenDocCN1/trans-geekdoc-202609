
# Chapter 9: Statistics and Probability

Statistics and probability form the backbone of data science. In this chapter, you will explore the key concepts needed to analyze and interpret data effectively. You will start with sampling techniques and descriptive statistics to identify patterns and understand your data. Then, you will dive into inferential statistics and hypothesis testing to validate claims about populations based on sample data. Finally, you will study probability theory, which plays an essential role in understanding and developing machine learning algorithms.

# Chapter 10: Introducing ML

In this chapter, you will learn how to frame a problem and determine whether machine learning is the right solution. Discover how ML approaches differ from traditional methods and how to choose the appropriate model for a given business problem. You will also gain insights into the different types of ML systems and learn how to address common challenges when implementing ML algorithms.

# Chapter 11: Training Machine Learning Models

Dive deeper into machine learning by exploring supervised and unsupervised learning techniques. This chapter begins with the distinction between supervised and unsupervised learning. You will work with popular supervised learning models like linear regression, logistic regression, and decision trees. Then, you will explore unsupervised learning concepts, focusing on clustering techniques like k-means. The chapter also highlights the advantages and limitations of these methods, helping you choose the best approach for your data.

# Chapter 12: Building a Career in Data Science

As data science continues to thrive, so do career opportunities in the field. This chapter guides you through the key steps to build and grow your career in data science. The chapter also offers tips for navigating the job search, understanding role requirements, and enhancing your communication skills to stand out as a data scientist. Curiosity and continuous learning are the ultimate keys to success in this dynamic field.

# Chapter 1: The Data Science Landscape

## Introduction

This chapter teaches how to approach a data problem, what to check, and what questions to ask when solving a business problem. You will know the process of analyzing a problem by setting each element apart to solve it, and that will help you to understand why problem framing leads to determining your product's success. Depending upon the type of business requirements, all data applications fall into four types of business analytics—descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive.

## Structure

This chapter will cover the following topics:

- Why should everybody understand data science
- Understanding the business problem
- Process of approaching your data
  - Questions to ask about your data
  - Checking reproducibility and consistency
  - Making hypotheses and looking for evidence
- Analytical skills you need
- Types of business analytics
  - Descriptive analytics
  - Diagnostic analytics
  - Predictive analytics
  - Prescriptive analytics

## Objectives

After completing this chapter, you will have a solid grasp of the data science ecosystem and learn how to tap into the vast amounts of available data to address and solve real-world business problems effectively. You will gain foundational knowledge of approaching any data problem and attain the fundamental skills to develop into a data-driven professional.

## The need to understand data science

Understanding data science is becoming more vital for all individuals because we live in an age where data is far and wide. With quality data skills, we could bring more power to organizational success.

Let me introduce you to Vedh, a content writer who is passionate about helping professionals build their personal brands and has built up a following of around 7,000 followers on LinkedIn. He has been sharing his knowledge on the domain (insights) on LinkedIn for the past three years. Despite the considerable effort, Vedh has struggled to gain traction on the platform for the past few months. He has been posting regularly; on the other hand, his posts are not getting the same level of engagement that they used to. As a result, Vedh feels balked and wonders if he can do anything to boost his engagement rates and reach more people with his content.

Over time, Vedh attends a data science workshop where he learns about the power of data analytics in strengthening content performance. Intrigued, Vedh decides to try it and ends up realizing that by analyzing his LinkedIn posts, he can gain valuable insights into what is working and what is not and leverage data for his content strategy.

Excited by this contemporary insight, Vedh starts reviewing his posts in fine points. He commences by tracking the engagement rates on each post, including the number of likes, comments, and shares. He proceeds by categorizing his posts by topic, format, and length and tracking the engagement rates in each category.

Within a brief timeframe of analysis, Vedh notices some exciting trends. First, he realizes that his posts on a particular topic get more engagement than others and that his longer-form posts perform better than his shorter posts. Upon closer inspection, he realizes that the posts that performed better were the ones that included more personal anecdotes and stories. Moreover, posts with images or videos get more engagement than text-only posts. With this knowledge, Vedh decides to incorporate more stories into his future posts.
