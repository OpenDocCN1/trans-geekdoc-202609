## 14.12 Drug discovery using ML

Drug discovery and manufacturing is one of the main applications of ML. In the current state, companies are finding the process of discovering a drug a tedious and time-consuming task. Discovery of a drug requires a lot of predictive power with great accuracy as any complications in the final drug might lead to serious conditions of a patient. At present, there are not many technologies with the computational ability to easily coin out a drug. ML is highly useful in this sector as its predictive capability is unmatched among all the available technologies. Unsupervised learning is used to find out hidden patterns that help in taking better decisions. Governments spend millions of dollars every year in search of finding a new drug for improved treatment or for better results. This technology can significantly cut down the expenditure for discovery of drugs.

To discover a drug, initially the companies go on search for a compound that cures the disease by reacting with the target molecules of the body, but in most of the cases, the compound tends to react with other molecules that are not the target and this might result in life-threatening circumstances. Therefore, companies find it difficult to discover a drug for a particular disease due to lack of predictive methods. ML uses its computational and predictive power to find the drug without any dangerous tests.

The drug discovery process is initiated by extracting information from previous tests from different sources and analyzing it. The procedure involves back-and-forth switching between induction and deduction. This results in identification of an accurate hit. The initial steps involve searching for compounds with decent biological activity. This activity is calculated based on the degree of interaction of the compound with the required enzyme or organism. A hit is the earliest compound that shows good amount of activity with a specific target. Generally, hits are found during the screening of chemical data and compounds like plants, fungi, and bacteria. The second step of this process involves the detection of a lead molecule. Any compound that is showing promising potential for the discovery of a new drug that can be used for treating a disease is known as a lead. These hits are used for prediction of disease and the safety of using a compound. After the identification of a lead compound, modifications are done to structure and to obtain desirable compounds with high clinical benefit which deal no harm.

Hits are chemically modified to increase their activity against specific targets and to eradicate any undesirable side effects during the generation process of lead molecules. The compounds formed after these chemical modifications of hits are known as analogues and the entire process is called hit expansion. For this process of hit expansion, medical experts make use of well-known chemical techniques. Experts emphasize on a particular sequence of reactions to c building blocks together that generate the analogues quickly which increase the efficiency. Any compound containing functional group or atom that can effectively interact with an active site of the target is called a building block. The target contains a particular region called the active site that reacts and combines with the compound through interaction forces. In this way, the initial chemical processes take place for the development of a drug.

Finding an enhanced version of an existing drug is relatively easier when compared to the strenuous task of discovering an entirely new drug. This happens because the chemical space consists of compounds of the order of 10^(60). The firms implement ML techniques that refine this space, making it smaller and easier for the algorithms to find a required compound. These ML techniques are useful in all the stages of drug discovery. They help in identification of biological targets, improving efficiency of discovery, analyzing available data, and making better decisions that are vital for the development of the drug. By eliminating human intervention and confusion, ML techniques remove any kind of anomalies or uncertainties helping to provide an improved development of a drug. ML has also proven to considerably increase the efficiency in predicting the routes for molecules to react and providing the final drug with required properties and characteristics.
