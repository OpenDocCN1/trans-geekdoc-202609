
Incorporating ML for the discovery of a drug has been very beneficial for R&D department. All the past issues like lack of computational power and resources for analysis for enormous datasets, extremely tedious screening of compound with less error, and possible toxic side effects have been completely eliminated with the help of ML. The finance department has also significantly improved after the use of ML as it cuts down the prices of analyzing huge datasets, predicting the right target, and reduces the time required for the accurate development of a new drug.

ML has taken the process of drug discovery to a whole new level. This is done by integration of genomics information, biological characteristics, and the ability to track targets efficiently. IBM Watson is one of these technologies and it has already discovered five new RNA-binding proteins that are used to detect a neurodegenerative disease called amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. ML algorithms operate on the chemical space for the process of development of new drug molecules. Since the chemical space contains a very wide range of compounds, it serves as a platform to increase the probability of finding the required organic molecule. ML algorithms along with predictive analysis tools make the detection target molecules easier and increase the safety by helping in the association of molecules with their target. They optimize the compound to eradicate any type of side effects and increase the quality of desired attributes. ML techniques also considerably decrease the attrition rates and R&D expenditure by only selecting the compounds needed for analysis and excluding analysis of any unnecessary compounds.

The available data is utilized by diverse techniques for selecting profiles of small molecules based on their structural biology. Wherever the structural data is not sufficient, ML techniques come in handy. Algorithms based on data, disease, biological attributes, or structure are used. Before implementing an ML technique for the development of a drug, it should be validated. The techniques are validated to boost up the success rate in drug discovery on application. Synthesis of the selected molecules is the most essential part in the process of development of a drug. Therefore, ML plays a crucial part as it can prioritize the molecules based on the synthesis and can create tools that make the process easier.

The most popular method used by scientists for the synthesis of drug-like compounds is retrosynthesis. The initial step in retrosynthesis involves analyzing the targets extensively and slowly break them down into smaller molecules or building blocks that are capable of being made easily. The next step involves identification of reactions that have the ability of converting these smaller fragments into required targets. This step is very difficult as it is impossible for humans to comprehend all the chemical reactions present and to pick the most suitable reaction. Here, ML techniques are highly beneficial as they can predict the reactions accurately avoiding wastage of time and resources for analyzing irrelevant data. Any kind of failure is totally avoided with the help of these algorithms. Some voids still occur due to unpredictable causes and misunderstanding of the reaction mechanism. As of now, numerous computer-aided organic compound synthesis systems are assisting experts in selecting the mode of synthesis. There is a new ML technology called `3N-MCTS` (Monte Carlo Tree Search) that filters out the less important building blocks and picks the best reactions for the targets. The retrosynthesis systems fall behind this technology as it is relatively faster and more efficient. It further eliminates any unnecessary stages and provides viable synthesis routes quicker. This technology might be faster but it is not very flexible and cannot be applied to every aspect of drug development.

Image-based screening is also a recent technology that is used for various applications in both preclinical experiments and drug development. It excels in categorizing small molecules based on their structure and does it efficiently. It is used to detect specific target molecules on the basis of their characteristics. High-throughput imaging is capable of segregating cancer cells to form a group of similar molecules that have similar mechanism of reaction. For this image-based profiling, supervised learning is generally preferred as it is better in classifying single cells into smaller fragments. The training data needs to be refined with the help of predefined parameters for supervised learning techniques. The result and performance of the screening depend on the quality of data.

The major challenge in incorporating ML in drug development is ensuring drug safety. Knowing its effects on humans and foreseeing its side effects is a very complicated task and takes a lot of resources. Active researches are going on to understand the entire effects of a drug and making sure that it is safe to use. Drug development involves clinical trials. These clinical trials are very expensive. The costs of these clinical trials can be reduced by using the knowledge obtained from past clinical trials in the initial stages of drug development. For this ML is employed to utilize the available data from previous research experiments so that it can analyze and use it to predict the exact use and its side effects. ML algorithm should analyze the data from clinical trials that should support the existing biological data. By implementing these in the discovery process of a drug, better outcomes are possible with improved clinical experiments that eventually lead to better treatment and therapies with very less side effects ([Scheeder et al., 2018](#bodymatter-014.xhtml#b_9783110648195-014_ref_013)).

## 14.13 Conclusion and future work

Healthcare sector is growing exponentially every year as millions of patients keep admitting in hospital every second around the world. Implementing a futuristic technology like ML can help this industry in all aspects. It has a great potential in drastically lowering the expenditure on medical industry and provide improved treatment with better care. Majority of companies have already taken steps to incorporate such a beneficial technology to make treatment more patient-oriented.

Implementing ML algorithms significantly increases the communication between doctor and patient, the amount of time a doctor spends with a patient, and speed of treatment and recovery. They are also highly useful in predicting a disease beforehand accurately, discovery of a drug, and organized management of health records and patient data. They considerably reduce the workload of doctors by performing all the mundane tasks of a doctor at inhuman speeds. The increased availability of electronic health data is utilized by ML for training and learning to improve its precision in all of its tasks. It helps the medical experts in understanding the exact risks and side effects of a new medicine. ML opens up a very wide range of possibilities in healthcare out of which all of them can highly benefit us. It is one of those technologies that can permanently change the medical industry, and all major companies must take an initiative to make use of this technology.
