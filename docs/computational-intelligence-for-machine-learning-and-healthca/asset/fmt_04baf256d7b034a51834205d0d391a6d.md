
The wrapper-based approach has implemented two FS mechanisms that use a variant of PSO with digital pheromone concept. Feed-forward neural network, back-propagation neural network and recurrent neural network are used in the classification process. The proposed novel approaches have given closely accurate results in minimum time ([Vanaja and Mukherjee, 2018](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_ref_046)). The performance comparison uses the existing FS techniques like pair-wise correlation, Eigen-centrality, latent, relief, and PSO where they have been used as a wrapper and compared with the proposed PSODP and PSO + PSODP techniques. The graphs in [Figures 15.7](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_fig_007) and [15.8](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_fig_008) that uses the proposed two novel approaches have shown that for both breast cancer and chronic kidney disease data sets, they have performed comparatively better than the existing approaches.

## 15.11 Conclusion and future work

The chapter has contributed to reviewing some of the recent state-of-the-art research works in evolutionary approaches of FS. It is analyzed that in the past 5 years, there are reasonable FS approaches used with ACO and GP techniques. It is also noticed that there is number of research contributions for FS mechanism that have been used with GA and PSO. Owing to the importance of FS tasks in the machine learning cycle, it is believed that the research progression using evolutionary approaches of the search paradigm will be exponential in the mere future arena of computational intelligence. In particular, the contributions made in the health informatics domain have taken more importance when compared to other industries or applications. Thus, the interest of the chapter proposal has extended in explaining the implementation results that have been obtained through filter and wrapper approaches of FS using a variant of PSO to build an efficient clinical decision support system. As future plan, there are extended ideas to survey all the possible evolutionary approaches used for the FS process with regard to different time sensitive and time insensitive applications.
