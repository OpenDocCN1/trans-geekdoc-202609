
In any machine learning technique, `FS` mechanism plays an important role to provide the model, good generalization ability, shorter training time, and increased accuracy and also helps in simplifying the model for the better interpretation for users ([Cai et al., 2018](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_ref_005)).

## 15.3 General types of feature selection and their differences

`FS` techniques are in general classified as filter, wrapper, and embedded methods ([Chandrashekar and Sahin, 2014](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_ref_007)).

### 15.3.1 Filter-based FS methods

The filter-based `FS` approach applies some statistical methods like information gain, correlation coefficient, or others to assign a numeric score to each feature based on which feature ranking will be performed. This feature ranking can be used either to use the features or not, based on the requirement of the application.

As shown in [Figure 15.2](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_fig_002), the selection of features is independent of any machine learning algorithm ([https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2016/12/introduction-to-feature-selection-methods-with-an-example-or-how-to-select-the-right-variables/](https://www.analyticsvidhya.com/blog/2016/12/introduction-to-feature-selection-methods-with-an-example-or-how-to-select-the-right-variables/)).

![Figure 15.2: Filter-based feature selection approach.](graphic/converted/b_9783110648195-015_fig_002.png)

Figure 15.2: Filter-based feature selection approach.

### 15.3.2 Wrapper-based FS Methods

In the wrapper-based `FS` method, a feature subset is generated and is given as input to a learning model. Based on the output of the previous model, features can be added or removed; or a new feature subset can be formed based on some evaluation criteria. The problem is essentially reduced to a search problem. The flow of the method is shown in [Figure 15.3](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_fig_003).

![Figure 15.3: Wrapper-based feature selection approach.](graphic/converted/b_9783110648195-015_fig_003.png)

Figure 15.3: Wrapper-based feature selection approach.

### 15.3.3 Hybrid and embedded FS methods

The hybrid method is a mixture of filter and wrapper methods. Algorithms in embedded methods have an in-built `FS` mechanism. The most common examples are `Lasso` and `Ridge` regression techniques where they perform L1 and L2 regularization, respectively, to reduce overfitting.

### 15.3.4 Difference between filter-based and wrapper-based FS methods

While it is possible to use hybrid method that combines the characteristics of filter and wrapper, yet there are internal differences between filter-based and wrapper-based `FS`.

Such differences are mentioned in [Table 15.1](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_tab_001) concerning some specific characteristics.

Table 15.1:Difference between filter and wrapper FS method.

| S.No. || Characteristics || Filter-based FS method || Wrapper-based FS method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. || Methodology || They measure the relevance of features by using their correlation with the dependent variable || They measure the usefulness of a subset of features by training a model on it. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2. || Speed || Faster (as they do not involve training the model) || Comparatively slow |
| 3. || Computational cost || Computationally inexpensive || Computationally expensive |
| 4. || Evaluation methods used || Statistical methods || Cross-validation techniques |
| 5. || Accuracy || Inaccurate (Fails to find the best subset of features) || Accurate (Provides the best subset of features) |

## 15.5 Filter-based evolutionary methods of feature selection

The following subsections explain the recent research progresses in filter-based mechanism executed in combination with `GA`, `GP`, `PSO`, and `ACO` evolutionary approaches.

### 15.5.1 FB-GA

The initialization and the operations in the evolutionary approach of genetic algorithms are modified by introducing the concept of grouping the individuals as tribes. It is ensured that the features selected in each tribe follow Gaussian distribution. The exploration ability of individual tribes is compared to determine the competing tribes that are allowed to produce more individuals to search their respective solution space effectively. The proposed algorithm is compared with many existing `FS` approaches and is experimentally determined to produce accurate pattern classification ([Ma and Xia, 2017](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_ref_028)). `FS` that provides maximum discriminating ability using a fine level of information is proposed. A binary genetic algorithm is used with rough set concept for the above-mentioned feature granulation ([Dong et al., 2018](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_ref_014)).

### 15.5.2 FB-GP

The genetic programming approach based on parsimony pressure is used for figure-ground segmentation as a preprocessing step in the image processing task. The experimental results of the proposed work are compared with many existing `FS` mechanisms and have shown improvement in F1-score ([Liang et al., 2016](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_ref_025)). A new `FS` method is proposed to perform symbolic regression for high-dimensional data. The main objective of the system is to show improvement in the generalization ability using the regression task ([Chen et al., 2017](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_ref_008)). Automatic `FS` using image descriptor is proposed. `GP` has been utilized to synthesize a descriptor. Experiments have been conducted for classifying the image texture with different rotational degrees and compared with domain expert knowledge to assess the proposed system effectiveness ([Al-Sahaf et al., 2016](#bodymatter-015.xhtml#b_9783110648195-015_ref_003)).
