
In this chapter, we discussed the language barrier in healthcare services. Importance and necessity of speech technology for removing the language barrier has been presented here. We identify the challenges associated with the medical domain in reference to technology building for automatic communication. A brief review of S2S systems existent in different domains has been covered here. The analysis of the review helped to find a way to overcome the language barrier with the help of speech technology and lead to the development of the S2S system in healthcare. Proceeding further we discuss here the S2S translation system for Hindi to English and vice versa in healthcare services. Each component of the system is separately discussed and a pipeline architecture of ASR-MT-TTS is described to reduce the language barrier. The performance of the subcomponents is analyzed separately. It shows that our system performed fairly well and further improvements can be obtained by working on the shortcomings of each component. The ASR can be made more noise-robust, and MT performance can be enhanced by utilizing several hypotheses for the input text. It is evident that in future the use of speech technology will become essential to break the language barrier, especially in healthcare sector.

## 4.7 Future scope

In future the primary aim is to enhance the accuracy of the existing system. Speech-enabled services are data-centric; hence, we need to increase the size of speech corpus for increasing the accuracy percentage. Apart from this, integration of noise removal algorithm in the existing system will also help to improve the system performance. India is a country of several languages. Extending the voice-enabled services to the people of other languages will ease their way to global healthcare services. We intend to develop multilingual speech translation system in healthcare sector that would cover more Indian languages along with languages of other countries where better healthcare services are available. The corpus needs to cover variations of accents due to the social and economic background of speakers to increase system robustness.

Nowadays, use of mobile phones is expanding rapidly and has covered most of rural India also. Extending the system for deployment on mobile phones will provide a worthwhile service in the healthcare sector for rural areas.
