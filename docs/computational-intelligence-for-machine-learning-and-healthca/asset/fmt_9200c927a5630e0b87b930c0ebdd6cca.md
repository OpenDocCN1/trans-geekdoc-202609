### 5.4.5 ML in personalized healthcare

A new healthcare approach is personalized healthcare or `PH` that improves the traditional healthcare system by mainly focusing on Internet of things sensor devices, web-based or social media information, and patient electronic health records collected patients’ data. Then by `PH`, an `AI` technique was applied on collected data that helps in improving the clinical intervention, disease progression, prediction of diseases, and patient self-management. Then to make analytic models, `ML` techniques that were used were integrated into different clinical decision support system and healthcare service applications. From sensor devices, a collected data was analyzed using these models, and behavioral pattern of patients along with their clinical conditions was identified through other sources.

For classification purpose, either supervised or unsupervised `ML` was used. Sometimes the decisions made by humans can be wrong due to involvement of emotion; on the other hand, computers do not have emotions. So, people are biased in decision making on the basis of their perception toward political, religious, and world. In the available dataset used for training purpose, these factors can create a problem as it was used for training it to make decision. Due to all these factors, the Esurance of dataset that was used for `ML` becomes challenging. Overcoming these will result in enhancement of personal healthcare. [Ahamed et al. (2018)](#bodymatter-005.xhtml#b_9783110648195-005_ref_001) have worked on `ML` models that were used in recent research and identified its reliability issues and proposed an improvement. For continuous monitoring of everyone health by using biomedical devices and prediction of potential health hazards, a `PH` plays a crucial role. And if these hazards were not treated on time, then it can become dangerous. [Mr. Walinjkar et al. (2017)](#bodymatter-005.xhtml#b_9783110648195-005_ref_034) have mainly focused on constant monitoring of `ECG` signal of an individual; for this purpose, a `3-lead` `ECG` kit was used and arrhythmia was detected by performing real-time analysis. This enables the prediction of heart risk and at `ML` phase a classification accuracy was needed to be dealt with as a feature extraction method was developed. This method was able to increase the classification accuracy. For development of prediction models, classification, and learning development, an arrhythmia database (`MIT-BIH`, `Physionet`) was used. The proposed models were tested and its results show that an accuracy of 99.4% was achieved using `k-NN` classifier model along with the cross-entropy error of 7.6 and on test data there was 1.2 of misclassification error.
