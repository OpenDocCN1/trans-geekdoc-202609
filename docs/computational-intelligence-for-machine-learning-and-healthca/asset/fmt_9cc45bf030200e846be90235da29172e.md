## 5.6 Current and future directions of deep learning in healthcare and medical domains

The current and future applications of deep learning in healthcare are evident and can be easily observed. Some of them are:

* In healthcare industry, a transfer of knowledge taken from heterogeneous, complex, and high-dimensional biomedical information is a challenging task. In modern world of medical science, there is abundant amount of data like data of sensor text, electronic records, imaging, etch that has been solved to much extension with the use of deep learning algorithms. A complex or unstructured example can be represented successfully with the use of deep learning, and an efficient and new paradigm is obtained using latest deep learning technology that is able to obtain an end-to-end learning model for complex types of data.

* In advance clinical research, a promising result is obtained using electronic health records and it also better informs the clinical decision-making skills. With modern electronic health records, a predictive modeling can be prevented with a practice of summarizing and representation. The use of alternative feature learning strategy has proved to be successful in patients who have achieved better results using electronic health record data. From various studies done by different researchers, it can be said that use of deep learning framework arguments in clinical environment decision systems gives improved results.

* In thrombosis, use of urokinase and plasminogen activator streptokinase and therapy can no longer be an approach as approved by the Food and Drug Administration. Instead of it, a deep learning comes into existence for the treatment of acute peripheral arterial thrombosis and embolism along with acute coronary thrombosis.

* There are some measures and outcomes of deep learning trained algorithms. In detection, a reference diabetic retinopathy sensitive and specificity nature of the algorithm can be defined as worst or moderate one. Because of this reason, two development set-selected operating points are used in deep learning trained algorithms. In this the first operating point handles high specificity and another one handles high sensitivity that results in better results.

* For identification of relevant information from patient records and charts, a deep learning NN can be used. As a scalable and accurate prediction can be created for variety of clinical resource sceneries, a deep learning approach can be used.

* It is also been used for analyzing medical images and in future it can also be used in medical segmentation, classification, registration, and various other tasks. Deep learning has been used in medical fields such as neural, digital pathology, pulmonary, and retinal.
