
Big data is the processing of massive volume of complex data. It has become a popular trend across the world. It is used to find patterns, analyze, manage the data, and help make better decisions. This technology has been burgeoning in many sectors in the past few years. It involves storing, analyzing, discovering hidden patterns, and intelligently managing datasets by gathering deep insights that help in better functioning of the system. It deals with datasets of the size of petabytes. It analyzes and strategically arranges structured, semistructured, and unstructured datasets. They are incorporated in companies to handle enormous chunks of data both economically and strategically.

Healthcare is a data-rich industry with data flowing in from health records, prescriptions, examinations, personal info, and readings. All this data keeps increasing the amount of data in the field at a tremendous rate. As big data deals with collecting, assessing, and managing data, it can hugely take advantage of the data readily available in the medical field. In the modern technological era, data is being constantly generated from various sources and they have reached a point where the present technologies are struggling to handle it. It is not easy to handle such huge chunks of data as it requires machines with very high computational power. Big data techniques can be utilized here to organize and manage this data efficiently and cost-effectively.

With the ever-increasing costs of treatment, it has become quite difficult for the lower class to cope up with the expenses of the procedures and medications. The costs of treatment can also be cut down to some extent by introducing big data in the medical sector. All the mental and social factors of the person are studied for the treatment instead of just considering the symptoms and providing treatment with the help of big data, leading to better care and efficient treatment. Personalized care is given to patients having a history of medical conditions in the past as big data takes entire data into consideration for organizing and making decisions. Big data can help in faster drug discovery due to its high computational ability. It develops a better relationship between doctor and patient as it analyzes and gathers insights from appointment data, referrals, and prescriptions. As it deals with the management and organization of health data, it goes through the entire blocks of huge data and recognizes hidden patterns, which can be very essential for better marketing strategies. It is extremely helpful in predicting accurate outcomes quickly, which leads to faster and better patient care and satisfaction. Big data eventually helps in the entire growth of the hospital in terms of care and economically by providing efficient and personalized care.

## Challenges

Prioritizing and assigning importance to data are the major challenges faced by big data. Since the available data is so huge, the analysis becomes difficult to judge if the insights are essential or not, due to which some irrelevant data points are prioritized. This makes it mandatory for the firm to check if the algorithms are efficient enough and have a good ability to sort data before adopting them.

Access to data is another challenge faced by big data. Since the data is very sensitive and it is transferred to various organizations for management, it needs to be secure and particular steps must be taken to ensure that this data does not get misplaced or fall into the wrong hands.

Noisy data is a major issue when it comes to big data. Since the efficiency of organization and management of data depends on the quality of data itself, the data must be checked thoroughly and any kind of noise or inconsistency should be removed. Patients must be assigned with a unique variable so that it can be easy to identify and match them. Without a unique identifier it becomes a tedious task to match or find a specific patient. Heterogeneous data also has chances of causing issues during the analysis of data, so any kind of mismatches and irregularity should be avoided ([Murdoch Travis et al., 2013](#bodymatter-014.xhtml#b_9783110648195-014_ref_007)).

The activity of the medical expert must be recorded under any circumstances as any missing data might cause unwanted anomalies and result in unclear conclusions. As predictions and management depends on the data, every single activity must be recorded and stored for better functioning of the system. Big data is analyzing the entire gigantic data to recover hidden patterns and to arrange them smartly to get useful insights. For this to take place all the data from the past must be present in digital form to be analyzed, and converting these old written files into digital data is a tedious and time-consuming task. Big data might provide a new and better approach to handle data generated by hospitals and medical centers but not all companies have enough capital to implement it.

Addressing these issues will help the big data work fluidly and will produce economical advantage to the firm. It also results in accurate disease prediction, better patient treatment and care, and improved usage of available electronic data, and gives a more holistic approach to the medical procedures ([Basel et al., 2013](#bodymatter-014.xhtml#b_9783110648195-014_ref_001)).

### 14.11.1 Applications of ML using big data in healthcare

In this modern technological era, most of the companies have started digitalizing all the health data. This data is vast as it includes past health records, diagnosis, medical imaging, and rest of the patient-related data. With the help of electronic health records, doctors have easy access to patients’ medical history. These include any kind of past allergies, medications, and results of various tests that are made available to the doctor effortlessly. The treatment can thus be done in lesser time due to the provided previous test results that are used by the algorithm. Using electronic records, doctors have found a significant rise in treatment as it removes any irregularities caused by bad handwriting of doctors. A substantial decrease in time required for the treatment is also noticed because using electronic records eradicates the need for unnecessary tests, as the past test results are readily available. All these eventually resulted in reduced number of errors made in prescribed medication and dose.

Electronic records are also essential for doctors in setting automatic reminders for appointments, to remind about vaccination, and to prevent unusual issues. They are proven to provide better care by facilitating reminders for periodic check-ups and easy communication between doctors and patients. They diminish the amount of paperwork required by providing electric approval and billing. These records are further passed on to the ML firms that employ this information to enable faster retrieval of data and gain important insights. They are also used to predict the onset of an epidemic outbreak helping the public to take preventive measures sooner.

Electronic health records also reduce the ever-increasing costs of treatment by finding clever insights among the data it stores. The benefits from health insurance schemes are also improved considerably by incorporating electronic health records in the medical field. Electronic health records also shine in the management area by reducing the amount of time required for billing and prevent any kind of confusion.
