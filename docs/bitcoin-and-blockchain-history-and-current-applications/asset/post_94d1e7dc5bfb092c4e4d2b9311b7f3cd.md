## 6.2 ASTONISHING WORLDWIDE FINANCIAL CRISIS OF 2007–2008

The most devastating financial emergency in the last 10–15 years is the crisis of 2007–2008 [5]. This was the biggest fiscal tragedy that caused immense despair after the Great Depression of 1929–1930. The 2008 crisis started in 2007 by a calamity in the subprime mortgage market in the United States and advanced to a complete global banking disaster through the failure of the investment bank Lehman Brothers on 15 September 2008 [6].

Huge bailouts of financial institutions and additional easing of financial and economic rules remained working to avert a conceivable ruin of the global monetary system. This predicament stayed, however shadowed by a worldwide commercial slump, the Excessive Decline. The European debt crisis, a predicament in the banking system of the European countries by means of the euro, followed later [7].
