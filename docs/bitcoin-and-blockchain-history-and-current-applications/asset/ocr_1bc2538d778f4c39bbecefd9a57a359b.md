# 6.3.8 ETHEREUM AND ICOs (2016)

After the theft of Bitcoins, another cryptocurrency platform originated in 2016 with much expectations, called the “Ethereum.” The cryptocurrency used in this platform was called ether which can easily be used by blockchain-based smart agreements and apps. Ethereum’s influx was noticeable by the appearance of Initial Coin Offerings (ICOs). An Initial Coin Offering (ICO) is the cryptocurrency industry’s equivalent to an Initial Public Offering. ICOs act as a way to raise funds, where a company looking to raise money to create a new coin, app, or service launches an ICO. Interested investors can buy into the offering and receive a new cryptocurrency token issued by the company. This token may have some utility in using the product or service the company is offering, or it may just represent a stake in the company or project [15–18].
