# 2.2.3 Low cost

Although the ticket of the high-speed railway is much higher than the ordinary train ticket, from the overall economic value of society and the value of time, the high-speed railway saves the travel time, improves the economic value of the unit time, and reduces the cost of travel for consumers, as described in the following news.

The development of high-speed railway has also livened up the economy and commerce in the areas surrounding the big cities. Mr. Chen, who works in the financial industry, is processing emails in the waiting lobby with two cell phones and a laptop. Because of his job, he has to commute between Beijing and Wuqing of Tianjin many times a week. Beijing and Wuqing are about 90 kilometers apart, and it takes more than an hour to drive without traffic jams, while the high-speed railway takes only 24 minutes. “Transportation saves both time and labor. Driving a car is quite troublesome, especially in Beijing. When you drive to Beijing, you may have traffic jams. There’s no risk of traffic jams by taking railway, so you don’t have to delay your work. If we drive a car, the cost will be too high. In fact, the fuel fee and the bridge fee are far beyond the high-speed railway ticket. I only spend more than 70 yuan back and forth. If we drive, the cost must be higher than that”.4
