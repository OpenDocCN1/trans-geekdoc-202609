Secondly, in the case of a generally smooth road, where the proportion of unmanned vehicles is 40%, the unmanned driving vehicles will have the greatest impact on road capacity, and this impact will become weaker and weaker as the proportion of the unmanned driving vehicles continues to increase. Lastly, on the roads that are highly prone to congestion, the roadway capacity is strongest when the proportion of the unmanned driving vehicles reaches 45%. As the percentage increases, the passing capacity of the road will decrease. The reason for this phenomenon is that the maximum capacity of the road prevents the traffic from growing, and for these types of road sections, the traffic authorities can dedicate the unmanned driving lanes to alleviate the traffic pressure.
