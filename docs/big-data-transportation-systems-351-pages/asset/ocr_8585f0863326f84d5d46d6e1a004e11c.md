# 6.1.1 Definition of the management requirements of expressway big data

Only when the demand is clear can we have a definite target. To establish the expressway big data management platform, its demand points mainly focus on the following aspects:

+   (1) **Purpose of information sharing.** The basic and common data in expressway traffic information are sorted out to form a unified sharing standard so as to support different business applications.

+   (2) **Purpose of information summary.** The business data generated in the expressway traffic management is summarized to realize the transparency and scientificity of the business management of the superior and subordinate in the management organization.
