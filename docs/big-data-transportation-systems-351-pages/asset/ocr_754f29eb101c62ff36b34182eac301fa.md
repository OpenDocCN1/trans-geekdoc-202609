In essence, electronic navigation map is a kind of spatial data system based on computer system and it has the characteristics of rich and diverse data content, complex data relationship, and information sharing. At the same time, electronic navigation map itself is a kind of geographic data system, which has most functions of geographic data system. Therefore, electronic navigation map can display digital signal (including digital map, remote sensing digital image and collected digital data) and analog signal on computer screen through Internet.

In fact, due to the combination of the visualization function, data query and analysis function, multimedia technology and virtual reality technology of digital mapping technology, the electronic navigation map has won the widespread interest of traffic management departments since its emergence. With GPS navigation, BeiDou navigation, and other applications, the electronic navigation map is constantly providing data information for big data transportation management system.
