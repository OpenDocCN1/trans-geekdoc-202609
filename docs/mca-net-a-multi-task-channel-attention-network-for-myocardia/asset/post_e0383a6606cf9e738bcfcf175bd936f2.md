### 6.3. Further analysis

- **Time and Resource**: To demonstrate that our method is relatively economical in terms of time and resources compared to other methods. We first count the average time overhead for each method to complete one epoch and one fold for each task. For the MCA-net multi-task model, each task would complete at the same time. For the comparison methods, we execute three tasks in parallel and choose the training time of the last finished one as the benchmark for comparison. Then, we further compare the resource overheads of the models by considering the sum of trainable params required for each task. From Tables 8 and 9, we can see that MFB-CBRNN requires the least trainable params, but has a relatively long training time, especially the time overhead of one fold on the PTB dataset. In contrast, ML-Net has a relatively low time overhead on both datasets but requires far more trainable params than the other methods. Both ML-ResNet and our method, in relative terms, balance the time and resource overheads better, but our method achieves better classification performance on each task than ML-ResNet. In the future, we will further optimize our model to reduce the overhead of the model while ensuring high classification accuracy.

- **Qualitative results**: We use 5-fold and 10-fold cross validation on the PTB and PTBXL datasets to obtain more reliable results, respectively. In this case, each fold is used in turn as the test set and the remaining data as the training set. Therefore, for further qualitative analysis of the experimental results, we detail the performance results and the corresponding number of records and patients for each fold, as shown in Figs. 6 and 7. We can see that MCA-net achieves good overall performance on the MI detection task, with an accuracy of over 95% in most folds. On the PTB dataset, there is some variation in the results across folds, which may be influenced by patient specificity, as the number of patients involved in each fold is limited. - **Limitations**: Although our method demonstrates some advantages for each task, it still has some limitations. Firstly, the model suffers from under-learning on some of the labels due to data imbalance, leading to a performance bottleneck in the model. For example, as shown in Fig. 8 (Since the PTBXL dataset is a multi-label dataset, we only give the confusion matrix for each task on the PTB dataset), the classification accuracy of ALMI was significantly lower than that of the other labels in MI location task. Secondly, on the MI location task, we can find that ALMI and AMI as well as IMI and ILMI are easily confused. This may be because the proximity of infarct locations usually leads to similar ECG waveform abnormalities, thus making it more difficult for our model to distinguish them which indicates that our method needs further improvement in its ability to identify the location of adjacent infarcts.

## 7. Conclusion

In clinical practice, cardiologists can make a preliminary diagnosis of MI by analyzing abnormal ECG signals, but it is very difficult to give a rapid and accurate diagnosis. Therefore, providing an automatic computer-aided method can effectively reduce the burden on healthcare professionals while providing reliable support for the prognostic assessment of patients.

In this paper, we design a multi-task channel attention network named MCA-net. It uses multiple independent channels to more comprehensively extract valid features from each lead. Each channel includes multiple stacked convolutional layers which contain SE modules to better assign feature weights. We also add a multi-task framework to additionally take into account the shared and complementary information between the MI detection and location tasks to further improve the MCA-net performance. The experimental results show that our model has state-of-the-art performance.

Although our method demonstrates some advantages for each task, it still has some limitations. Firstly, the model suffers from under-learning on some of the labels due to data imbalance. Secondly, MCA-net is prone to confusion when differentiating the relevant labels. To address the above issues, in the future, we will consider collecting more clinical data while further improving our MCA-net with the help of data augmentation such as contrastive learning. Moreover, considering the multi-view nature of the 12-lead ECGs, more effective lead fusion methods can be considered from a multi-view fusion perspective to obtain a richer feature representation.
