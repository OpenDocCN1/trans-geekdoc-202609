
# About Authors

## Prof. Mrunalini P. Moon

Prof. Mrunalini P. Moon is a distinguished Professor with over 18 years of experience in the field of teaching. Born and raised in Nagpur, she developed an early passion for the subject, which laid the foundation for a remarkable career. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Electronics Engineering from Priyadarshini College of Engineering, Nagpur (Nagpur University) and a Master's degree in Electronics (Computer) from Government College of Engineering, Pune (Pune University).

Over the years, she has worked with prestigious organizations such as Nagpur Institute of Technology, Nagpur and G.H. Raisoni Institute of Engineering & Technology, Nagpur, where she made significant contributions to the subject and initiatives.

Prof. Mrunalini P. Moon is widely recognized for her expertise in AI & ML, Data Structure, Microprocessors & Microcontrollers, C, C++, Python, IoT, and Networking. She has published numerous papers and articles in esteemed national and international conferences and journals. Her research and insights have been instrumental in advancing our understanding of these topics. In addition to her professional achievements, she has shared her knowledge and experience at conferences, workshops, and in classrooms around the world.

This book reflects the Author's deep commitment to the subject and serves as a comprehensive guide for anyone looking to delve into Data Science. With a blend of practical insights and theoretical knowledge, she provides readers with invaluable tools and perspectives to enhance their understanding and application of Data Science. She currently resides in Nagpur with her family and continues to inspire and mentor the next generation of professionals in the field, fostering a community of innovation and excellence.
