Intelligent environments: How to give learners the appropriate material at the appropriate time and in the appropriate manner presents a significant difficulty, especially when many users are present in such settings and judgments made by one user may be influenced by those of other users. The difficulty lies in ways to increase human learning capacity while attempting to make learning efficient and simple.

+   6. Pedagogical impact of learning technologies: A significant issue is that technology should put emphasis on the viewpoints of learners themselves and the frontiers of education in order to include besides grades, such as motivation, self-assurance, fact, contentment, and achieving professional goals. The fact that the same technology may perform differently in various economic, social, and cultural contexts presents another barrier to the effective pedagogical use of technology in education. As a result, a “one-size-fits-all” strategy would not be practical and technology should be customizable and adaptable.
