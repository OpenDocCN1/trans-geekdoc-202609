# 7.7 IIoT Start-ups in India

India is making its best effort to leverage IoT and artificial intelligence to promote the culture of innovation on par with the developed countries. The increased attention and sustained efforts of the Government of India, under the Make in India project, have defined new horizons of business and innovation. The promotional avenues and financial support through MSMEs (micro, small and medium-sized enterprises) have triggered the establishment of small start-ups. India has a galaxy of 1,500 IoT start-ups, which are intended to enhance the potential of IoT and its applications perspectives. This section highlights few such IoT start-ups.

+   - Stellaps Technologies: Stellaps is one of the leading IIoT firms in India that have gained enormous growth by offering top-notch services. This Bangalore-based company is primarily focused on data collection and machine learning. The SmartMooT platform collects data, using sensors built into animal wearables, milking systems, milk chilling machinery, and milk procurement processes. The gathered data is sent to the Stellaps SmartMooT™ Cloud Service Delivery Platform (SDP), wherein the StellapsSmartMooT™ suite of applications extract and analyse the information before sending the results of the analytics and data science to stakeholders via low-end and sophisticated mobile devices [22]] (www.stellaps.com).
