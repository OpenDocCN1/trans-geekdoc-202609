Another interesting example in a creative field was the first AI-scripted movie, where screenplay was generated using sci-fi scripts as the training set. The effect, where professional actors enact the AI-generated scenes, is quite hilarious and unexpected. The movie, *Sunprasp*, was placed in the top ten at the annual Sci-Fi London Film Festival in one of the categories [15].

While in the field of games, AlphaGo, developed by Google DeepMind, defeated Mr. Lee Sedol, with 18 world titles at professional Go, by a 4-1 victory in Seoul, South Korea, in March 2016. The viewership for this game reached 200 million widely. During the games AlphaGo played several inventive moves, several of which – including a specific number 37 in game – were surprising for the defending champion. Afterwards Lee Sedol said, "I thought AlphaGo was based on probability calculation and it was merely a machine. But when I saw this, I changed my mind. Surely, AlphaGo is *creative* [16].

Recently, the company OpenAI announced the Generative Pre-trained Transformer 3 (GPT-3) language model [17]. Trained on trillions of words from the internet, it was the latest NLP model to date. GPT-3 is aimed at natural language answering of questions, but can also translate between languages and coherently improved text. It has been successfully used for many tasks from copywriting to automatic generation of computer code. Recently, OpenAI announced a program called DALL-E 2 in the beta testing that can create realistic images and art from a natural language description (e.g., "Teddy bears shopping for groceries as a one-line drawing")

Similarly, in the field of music, there are many projects that include Sony Computer Science Laboratories’ AI-assisted music production program called Flow Machines. DeepMind, an AI owned by Google, has created software that can generate music by listening to recordings [18]. At this point we are not aware of a similar project for Indian classical or pop music.

Mathematics is an area where creativity is thought to be a preserve of the few individuals with natural (or God-given) gifts. Here too AI has been used for discovery and to generate mathematical theorems. Google has an open source project called DeepHOL that has proved more than 1,200 theorems it had not previously seen [19]. Among the AI-based discoveries are a new formula for pi, and some results in number theory.
