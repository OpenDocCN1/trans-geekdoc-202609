# 13.5.7 Managing Repetitive Tasks

Businesses are using AI to increase the productivity of their employees. One of the major benefits of AI for employees is handling repetitive processes in the organization, which frees the employees to focus on creative and complex solutions. One of the examples of the repetitive processes is the chatbots. An AI-operated chatbot helps in daily interactions with customers and offer fast and accurate support to customers.

# 13.5.8 Minimizing Errors

AI solves complex problems consistently and also speeds up the process which is unmatched by human intelligence. But AI cannot replace human intelligence, it needs the right problem to solve, the data is required, in order to design the solutions for the problem and develop the right process for AI to adapt, learn through errors and produce better results. AI also provides more time for the employees to handle reduced, based on the large amount of data that connects the symptoms of illness and the cause. AI strengthens the decision of the doctors and removes the error, based on machine learning and the pattern recognition algorithm.

Undoubtedly, healthcare and other businesses around the world are leveraging AI to produce important leaps in error management.
