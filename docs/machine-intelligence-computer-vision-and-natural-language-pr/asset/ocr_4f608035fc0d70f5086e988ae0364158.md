+   - Alexa from Amazon: Alexa and the Echo were introduced worldwide by Amazon in 2014, starting the smart speaker revolution (Figure 2,7). Alexa can be found in the Echo, Echo Show, Echo Spot, and Echo Buds headphones (Amazon’s variation of Apple’s AirPods). Alexa was ahead of the curve in terms of smart home device integration, including cameras, door locks, sound systems, lighting, and thermostats. Users can ask Alexa to reorder their garbage bags, and it will simply order them from Amazon. In fact, Alexa can order millions of things from Amazon without ever having to lift a finger, giving an advantage over its competitors.
