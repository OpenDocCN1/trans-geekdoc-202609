# 16.8.2 Privacy

Privacy is one of the most sensitive issues in data science. In fact, the public's current privacy concerns regarding surveillance cameras, financial transactions, and email communication are nowadays difficult to detect due to the cross-reference of larger datasets, quasi-identifier is the best example for this. While genomics-based privacy has some distinct characteristics compared to data science-based privacy issues, the fact is that the genome is crossing across generations and is very important to protect. Moreover, revealing genomic data is more dangerous than leaking other kinds of data. Once a person's data or their relative variants are revealed or leaked, they may not be able to take them back. Finally, genomic data are far larger in size than several other kinds of personal data; for example, the genome contains more confidential information than a social security number or credit card.
