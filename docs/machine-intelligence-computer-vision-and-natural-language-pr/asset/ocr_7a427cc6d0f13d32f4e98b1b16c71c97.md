This value of k is the time gap considered and is called the lag. A lag 1 autocorrelation (i.e., k=1 in the above) is the correlation between values that are one time apart. More generally, a lag k autocorrelation is the correlation between values that are k periods apart. The graph of ACF is shown in Figure 8.3 ACF is a way to measure the linear relationship between an observation at time t and the observations at previous times. If we assume an AR(k) model, then we may wish to only measure the association between yt and yt-k and filter the linear influence of the random variables that lie in between (i.e., yt-1,yt-2,...,yt-(k-1)yt-1,yt-2,...,yt-(k-1)), which requires a transformation of the time series. By calculating the autocorrelation of the transformed time series we obtain the partial autocorrelation function (PACF). The graph of PACF is shown in Figure 8.4.
