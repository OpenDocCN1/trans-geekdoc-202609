# 13.1.6 How Intelligent Agents Work

Intelligent agents work through three components: sensors, actuators and effectors. An overview of these components can give a clear idea of how the intelligent agents work.

+   - Sensors: Sensors detect the changes in the environment. The details are sent through other devices. In AI the environment is observed through the sensor agent.
- Actuators – Actuators convert the energy into motion and perform the role of moving and controlling a system. Examples are motors, rails and gears.
- Effectors: The effectors affect the environment. Examples are fingers, legs, wheels, display screens and arms.

The sensor agent uses AI to make decisions based on the observation through experience of past tasks. Then action is activated through actuators. Future decisions are based on history and past actions.
