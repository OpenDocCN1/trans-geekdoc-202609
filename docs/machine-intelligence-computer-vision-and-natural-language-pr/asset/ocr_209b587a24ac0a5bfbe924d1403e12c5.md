# 5.1 Introduction

# 5.1.1 Self-Driving Vehicles

Self-driving vehicles are cars or trucks without human drivers to securely operate the vehicle. Conjoint referred to as autonomous or driverless cars, they combine sensors and computer code to regulate, navigate, and drive the vehicle.

# 5.1.2 Levels of Autonomy

Different cars are capable of various levels of self-driving, and area units are usually represented by researchers on a scale of 0–5 (Figure 5.1):

+   Level 0: All main systems units are controlled by humans.
Level 1: Certain systems, like control or automatic braking, are also controlled by the vehicle, one at a time.
