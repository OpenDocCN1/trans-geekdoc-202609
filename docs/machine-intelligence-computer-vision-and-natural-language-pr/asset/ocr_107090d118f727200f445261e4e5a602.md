# 14.3.2 Data Pre-Processing

The extracted tweets are subjected to pre-processing to eliminate inconsistencies. Python programming is adopted to carry out pre-processing of the retrieved tweets. Initially, the necessary packages using numpy, pandas, re, nltk, spacy and string were imported. To maintain a state of uniformity among the tweets, all the tweets were converted to lower case. Punctuation was removed. Some of the most commonly used words in English like “a,” “but,” “is” are considered as stop words. Stop words that are not necessary from an analysis point of view. Stop words in the considered dataset are eliminated. It is quite important to obtain the root of a word to logically analyze the words. Lemmatization is an important aspect of NLP reduces a word to its root form. The nltk package of Python plays a great role in lemmatization of the considered tweets. Hyperlinks that are not necessary are also scraped out.

Emoticons and emojis that convey the emotional feelings of a person in a conversation play an important role in opinion mining. Tweets contain lots of emotions and emojis. The emotions and emojis are converted to their respective meanings. A number of HTML tags in the dataset are also eliminated since they are of no significance for analysis. A short of forms, such as Tyl (“Talk to you later”) are used by users of social media sites like Twitter and Facebook. The short forms or chat words like Tty are converted to the appropriate text form. Duplicate tweets in the dataset are eliminated. Removal of duplicate tweets resulted in a total of 498 tweets.
