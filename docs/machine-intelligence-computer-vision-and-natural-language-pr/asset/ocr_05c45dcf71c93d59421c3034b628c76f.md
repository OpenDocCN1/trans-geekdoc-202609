**Radius** The radius is the bone of the forearm on the thumb side of the wrist; the radius is lateral to the ulna. The radius is wedge-shaped and angular and articulates with the capitulum of the radius.
The radius also allows for rotation of the forearm in the context of articulation around an axis of rotation. The ulna rotates around the centre or distal axis of the radius by pivot, alone the forearm.
