### 7.10 Applications of IoT

IoT has a spectrum of applications in healthcare, agriculture, etc.

- Smart Homes: The integration of IoT manages traffic flows, electricity, and entertainment.
- Smart City: Use of IoT-enabled information and communication technologies manages traffic flows, electricity distribution.
- Farming: IoT-based drones monitor environmental conditions, soil moisture, and nutrients.
- Retail Business: IoT-based sensors track consumer behavior and product modification.
- Wearable Devices: Fitness bands and smart watches track calories, heart rate, and oxygen saturation.
- Traffic monitoring: IoT-based systems manage traffic flows, arrival times, and destinations.
- Fleet management: IoT sensors inform drivers of conditions, functionality, and requirements.
- Water supply: Sensors coupled with software monitor water quality, usage, and reporting.

## IIOT Applications and Services

### 7.11 Use-Cases in Industrial IoT

#### 7.11.1 Predictive Maintenance

Periodic maintenance of assets using IoT-based cameras and data analytics predicts likely breakdown before it occurs. These IoT-enabled systems transform the maintenance process into something dynamic and automatic. Predictive maintenance increases safety and extends the lifetime of equipment, while avoiding accidents and hazards.

#### 7.11.2 Smart Metering

A smart meter is an internet-enabled device that detects the amount of energy, water, or natural gas used by a building or residence. Conventional meters track only total consumption whereas smart meters detect the quantum of resource consumption along with the time-stamp. Industries use smart meters to track consumer usage as well as fix price as per usage. Moreover, this approach is helpful in hazardous industries like mining and with widely dispersed equipment or heavy reliance on environmental conditions. To avoid squandering resources or overloading energy-generating equipment, the energy sector relies on smart metering and remote monitoring.
