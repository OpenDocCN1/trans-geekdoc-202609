# 7.5 Industrial IoT (IIoT)

The existing and emerging opportunities for industrial IoT applications have propelled the usage of IoT in business, operations and manufacturing sectors in order to maximize productivity and profit as well to reduce costs. The industrial IoT (IIoT) is a network of equipment, machinery, and sensors interconnected via the internet, in order to continuously gather and process data for better monitoring, operational, and business solutions.

# 7.5.1 Applications of IoT

The scope and influence of IoT are increasing over time. In the data-driven era, the IoT sectors serves as one of the primary sources of data for production and business management. The scope section outlines the role of IoT applications and impact in the industry sector [19]:

+   - Equipment management and monitoring: IoT can remotely control many production plants across various geographical regions by using digital devices and software. The goal of gathering and utilizing historical data is to improve the process/product and to create an environment for information-driven decision-making.
