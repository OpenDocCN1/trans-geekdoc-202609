+   Industry 5.0 generates large amounts of data through interconnected devices, digital transactions, and customer interactions, raising issues about data privacy and regulatory compliance.

+   Compliance with evolving regulatory requirements requires ongoing monitoring and adaptation of banking systems and processes, adding complexity and cost to operations.
