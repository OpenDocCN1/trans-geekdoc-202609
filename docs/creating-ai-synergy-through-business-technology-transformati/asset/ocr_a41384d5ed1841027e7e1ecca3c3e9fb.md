+   6. **Job Displacement:** The rapid advancement of AI and automation technologies presents a considerable threat to employment, particularly in fields that rely on repetitive and routine tasks. Automation is increasingly replacing jobs in manufacturing, retail, customer service, and even certain professional domains like legal research or medical diagnostics. While AI can lead to the creation of new job opportunities, the transition period can be challenging, necessitating the retraining and upsk of many workers. If not managed properly, widespread job displacement can have serious economic and social repercussions, leading to higher unemployment rates and increased social inequality.
