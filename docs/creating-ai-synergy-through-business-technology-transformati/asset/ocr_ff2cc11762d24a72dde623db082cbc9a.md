DeFi platforms enable peer-to-peer lending, decentralized exchanges, yield farming, and DeFi provides financial services without the need for traditional intermediaries as banks. DeFi provides opportunities for financial inclusion, transparency, and innovation but also poses challenges related to regulation, security, and scalability.

CBDCs provide benefits as faster settlement times, lower transaction costs, and increased financial inclusion. Several countries are discussing or piloting CBDC projects with potential implications for monetary policy, financial stability, and the future of banking.
