Despite few researchers argued that AI has limited applicability in HRM processes (Chitrao et al., 2022) and that availability of these technologies is not well known to recruiters, many other researchers believed it has the potential to shape the future of HR practices (Pan & Roe, 2023); Vedapradha et al., 2023), Ekuma, K. A more intriguing fact in the literature is that AI-based applications are regarded as one of the most most advance development in HRM technologies.

Although it is still in the infancy stage, 2019, and has a limited applicability to teach and innovative firms (Albert, 2019), the use of AI in the field of HR has witnessed a rapid growth (Pan & Roe, 2023) (Davenport et al., 2020). And the impact of the digitization, in particular AI, on different HR functions has becomes a phenomenon of interest for HR professionals and practitioners (Pandita, 2019) The presence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) effect on human resource represents represents a paradigm shift on various HR practices from planning, recruiting talents to retention. By all means, this has not only transformed traditional human resources practices but has formerly offered both opportunities as well as concerns in multifaceted for HR professionals.

Indeed, literature indicates many contributing to keenness among organizations to integrate AI technologies into their processes and functions. For instance, AI technologies have the potential to make HR tasks faster than before. Also, these technologies ensure more accurate HR processes and better alignment with organizational goals. For instance, with the help of AI tools, organizations spend less time to scan resumes and quickly identify the best suitable candidate. The secret behind is undoubtedly the use of algorithms as a enabler to save time and efforts undoubtedly reduce cost per hire.
