These different forms of data can contribute to the physical identification of a person. Consequently, these data are personal and sensitive. as such, it must be protected as it is generated. Minimal data collection is required [27].
The security of the technology of any mobile payment system – mobile payment at a POS, mobile payment platform, independent mobile payment system, and direct billing by the operator – is essential. User data is at the heart of the digital economy. It is also subject to cyber-attacks.
