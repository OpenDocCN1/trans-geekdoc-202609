The results of AlMamun et al. show that an attacker cannot access the personal information of a citizen and any unauthorized access attempt is denied instantly, ensuring the privacy of private data. Same as AlMamun, Sin and Naing [22] also proposed a digital identity management system to decrease duplicate identity documents. Het et al. [23] proposed the use of blockchain technology for digital identity management systems as a solution for digital identity in Myanmar. Their proposed system enabled to identify and control illegal duplication of passports. Since, a valid passport can be issued only to one person (except passport loss, renewal, and expired) and is impossible for a person to apply for more than one passport type in the decentralized system. Dong et al. [24] proposed a solution for digital identity management and securing the information of banking customers. They proposed BBM which is a blockchain-based self-sovereign identity system for open banking. Self-sovereign identity (SSI) is a model for managing digital identities in which an individual or business has ownership over the ability to control their accounts and personal data. With an SSI approach, the users have control over their personal information is kept and used⁴. Their proposed model provides a secure way for open customers who hesitate in sharing their personal information with third-party service providers. Their model makes it possible to have a reliable and secure connection between open customers and third-party service providers by allowing the customers to manage and control their own identity and data. Li et al. [25] also used blockchain technology in the context of court trials and evidence management. They proposed a blockchain-based event management scheme LE-chain which supervises the flow of evidence and all of the court data as votes and trial results, They use short rand signatures that anonymously authenticate the identities of witnesses to protect their privacy. Noack and Kubieck [26] discussed the introduction of digital identity for online authentication in Germany. Germany started the process of electronic-ID in the 1990’s incorporating e-governments and e-commerce where they implemented a legislation on e-signatures that was supposed to be used for the online authentication of citizens. This was then replaced by e-ID card in 2009 which was considered a radical innovation that followed two-sided authentication to the citizens.

Healthcare has also been very important area where the identity of the citizens is crucial. Many researchers discovered the importance of digital identity in health care and how it could be used to secure the patients' data and improve their experience. Christen [27] proposed the use of blockchain-based digital platform to serve as a secure data repository while Bhuyan et al. [28] proposed a blockchain-based solution for managing and sharing the patients' healthcare care across different medical centers/hospitals in a secure way. Other researchers have also proposed the effective and secure management and sharing of healthcare data among different stakeholders.
