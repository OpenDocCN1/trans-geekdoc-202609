# 3.3.1.2 Smart Cities

The idea is to ensure comfort, savings, and security in the city as much as possible. Making everything connected is a good way to transmit information as quickly as possible and make decision-making efficient and instantly. This is why IoT is a major contributor to the creation of smart cities as stated by Alavi et al. [3] and Qian et al. [56]. In fact, IoT can provide the following services:

+   - Traffic control: Receiving data from sensors and cars to adjust traffic lights in real-time reduces congestion.
- Garbage collection: By creating scheduled pick-up in need compared to a preplanned schedule by the distribution of smart garbage across the city that can send data to waste management companies.
- E-governance: Making mobile driver's license and ID with digital credentials simplifies and accelerates access to the city and local government services.

Barcelona Gascó-Hernandez [24] is considered one of the world's most advanced smart cities. It has an IoT project aiming to create a single operating system for all smart devices and services in the city. It senses all over, for example, water storage tanks and water supply lines, enabling it to predict domestic and industrial usage requirements.

# 3.3.1.3 Healthcare

Monitoring a person's health condition using wearable devices benefits the health sector. Many wearable devices are being created using different types of sensors to keep track of personal health conditions and provide warning alerts in case they detect an abnormal or abnormal indicator; they could suggest prescriptions to their user [55].

In an experiment by Sooyev et al. [66], they used a stress recognition application using smartphones to measure the stress level of a college student. They understand college student stress and their academic performance by tracking the student's location during a full day with GPA score, human interaction via audio sensors, physical activities, and sleep rest amount.

IoT applications can also create a record of all the person's medical details called an Electronic Health Record (EHR). It could contain blood pressure, allergies, sugar in the blood, and many more [18].
