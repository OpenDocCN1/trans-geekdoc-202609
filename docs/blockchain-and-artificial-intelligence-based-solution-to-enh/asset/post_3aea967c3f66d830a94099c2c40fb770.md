# 3.1 INTRODUCTION

One of the objectives of smart spaces and systems is improving society and human life quality, in terms of comfort, efficiency, or productivity. The Internet of Things (IoT) has developed into a backbone innovation for smart environments providing the mentioned features. In a market research, the evaluation that IoT systems could reach a potential economic impact of $11 trillion by the year 2025 compared to the actual $4 trillion, which represents approximately 11% of the world economy Institute [33]. IoT systems are becoming one of the most developed fields in computer science [74], and it's a significant contributor to the economy. Real-time data processing and analysis greatly benefit multiple fields and infrastructures, especially for healthcare systems, antivirus and autonomous vehicle technology, smart cities, etc. Privacy and security are critical elements in any real-world environment established by an IoT system. All of these made a target of cyberattacks due to their security leak compared conventional computing. The report has shown an increase in attacks targeting IoT devices by 600% in the year 2017 Semantic [65]. IoT devices, despite their importance, major security updates, have insecure features, and insufficient data protection. For example, the Mirai Botnet, first introduced in 2016, was able to reach almost 400,000 devices simultaneously, leading to a total of $100 million in damage and shutting down significant platforms such as Netflix, GitHub, and Reddit for multiple months [48]. IoT devices don't have the enough computational or hardware resources to use complex security features such as computers or smartphones [2]. Those systems can use a security layer in their implementation that provide the required security against cyberattacks, such as firewalls, antivirus software, etc. Hence, it is crucial to focus on securing these systems. They are becoming increasingly Security Corporate Violation to Privacy by cyberattacks due to their security vulnerabilities. Moreover, companies are using IoT to access users’ data to create profiling that will help them for personal reasons. This creates a breach of their right to privacy. In this, Big Data represent financial benefits; that is why we see their tremendous growth since every organization tries to incorporate them in their business model for their benefit to collect an amount of data.

# 3.1.1 PRIVACY ISSUES REVISITED

As the Internet is taking over our daily lives, most of the world’s data, such as videos, social information, and many more, is constantly published and shared over the global network; privacy is becoming a major target and a right that keeps fading over time raises prime concerns [12]. Moreover, the rise of IoT devices connected to the Internet and collecting extensive data carries a comprehensive risk to users’ privacy. An entity has the right to determine the amount of data it is willing to provide with a system or others. Still, despite this, many labeled data of a person can be collected without the person consent using IoT devices. Having control of personal data can be becoming difficult, if not impossible, especially when, in multiple cases, that is necessary for the device' proper function. Especially the identification of an individual and their behavioral patterns is a growing concern. As IoT devices are increasingly used in all fields of daily life, such as the healthcare sector [40], a great amount of considered private information is collected by sensors and for analysis study reasons, all potentially without adequate accountability, transparency, security, or meaningful consent. It is quite to say that consumers are infringing their privacy without noticing. As a wearable, smart applications, and Wi-Fi increase demand and replace “old” devices. Consumers are not capable of purchasing devices that won’t collect their data and track them. So, it becomes necessary to discuss the implications of the IoT and the need to integrate privacy and safeguards into creating and implementing smart environment components.
