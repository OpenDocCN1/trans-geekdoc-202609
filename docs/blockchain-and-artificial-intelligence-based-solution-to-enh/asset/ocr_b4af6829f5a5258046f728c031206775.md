# 5.3.7 OBLIGATIONS IN PROVINCES TO STORE PERSONAL DATA

It should be highlighted that additionally to the fact that the Ontario’s act regarding the public sector does require personal information on Canadian territory, other provinces have legislated obligations such as British Columbia88 and Nova Scotia.89 Alberta imposes an obligation of informing when the data is stored outside of Canada.
