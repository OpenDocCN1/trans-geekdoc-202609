# 3.2.1.3 Bluetooth

Since its creation in 1998, Bluetooth has seen and provided considerable technological advancement. Its contribution to device-to-device communication has led to its adaptation by multiple industries. There is no coincidence that his technology is widespread and found in most devices such as phones, smartwatches, PCs, glasses, earphones, shoes, and more. So it is safe to say that it represents the go-to wireless connectivity for wearable gadgets and other devices. It uses UHF radio waves, known as short radio wave, with radio bands ranging from 2402 GHz to 2480 GHz and building a Personal Area Network (PAN), enabling a master-connectivity [58]. This technology provided the reality of IoT and its wide range of use. With the adaptation of Bluetooth low energy (BLE) it is found in battery-powered devices, most sensory devices that offered reduced power consumption, reduced latency, and cost compared to the classic Bluetooth while maintaining a decent communication range, more opportunities and benefits were provided to the field of IoT [62]. Since BLE can help conserve the device's energy when it is not in use, it can quickly pair and reconnect with devices in less than six milliseconds. With product selling four billion this year and its advantages, experts believe that Bluetooth will be a significant factor in the trillion-dollar IoT market.

# 3.2.2.1 Cloud Computing

Due to the rapid growth of data, storage issues, processing, and analyzing large amounts of data, cloud computing is becoming essential to IoT. Cloud computing enables users to perform computing tasks using services provided over the Internet [19]. The “Cloud” is a centralized system that helps in transferring and delivering data to centers over the Internet, also making access to a large amount of data and programs accessible and fast. Moreover, Cloud computing allows economic solutions since it doesn't require on-site storage, processing, and analytics infrastructure. And as we do, we live in a significant data era nowadays, and scalability is a must. Cloud computing can provide that, meaning with business and data growth, technological, and analytical capabilities can also. Researchers saw an opportunity in the cloud for IoT systems. Their mutual combination enabled a vast amount of data collection by the IoT devices and powerful processing for those data streams and their monitoring using the cloud [20].
