## 1.2 LACK OF USABILITY OF DIGITAL IDENTITIES

Reuse of digital identities has great potential to reduce data replication and environmental footprint of data. However, digital identities are specified and used rarely in various applications and this fact will create costs for organizations, lack of interoperability, and usability challenges for users. Thus, it is necessary to identify challenges and success factors for reuse of digital identities, to optimize technical challenges and usability. Given the challenges and risks in digital identity systems, several researches proposed the use of blockchain technology as solution in different capacities.

## 1.3 BACKGROUND

Blockchain is a digital ledger of economic transactions continually updated by several users with focus on maintaining the integrity of data. It is a continuous chain of blocks that originated with the introduction of bitcoin [15]. Blockchain has transformed the way of doing business transactions with security and integrity. Business organizations have been gaining competitive advantage over their competitors by leveraging this technology. A typical database consists of two types of records, where blocks are responsible for transactions [16].

Blockchain was initially introduced with the emergence of cryptocurrency for providing a reliable source of digital transactions. It provides an environment for transferring money to users through peer-to-peer data sharing [17]. However, blockchain is not the only technology that is now widely used in various domains such as finance, health, smart cities, and government organizations [18].

Blockchain is a decentralized system and does not need a third-party trusted authority. Rather, it adopts the consensus mechanism in order to guarantee the reliability of data and transactions. Current blockchain has four major components:
- Pow (Proof of Work)
- Pos (Proof of Stake)
- PBFT (Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance)
- Dpos (Delegated Proof of Stake)

Blockchain provides security and privacy in that it provides a way of digital identity that consumers feel more comfortable in sharing their data. This self-sovereign identity is based on two principles, i.e., consent and control. Consent is the permission among individuals and organizations defining which information could be used. Having said the world is digitally connected today and every transaction of data can be perceived within seconds, however still exists the question of security of data and transactions. Due to many features of security, data integrity, and anonymity, blockchain has significance in digital identity [20]. With the rapid transformation of business processes and their emerging requirements to meet the challenging markets, businesses have started adopting ICT-based solutions where the concept of digital identity is no exception. The use of digital citizen identity has not only made business processes efficient and effective but also has provided a way of security and privacy of personal data and information. Several researches have been conducted on digital identity where they discussed its usage, its benefits in different application areas, and the prospective that it has.

Blockchain is an emerging technology that is going to lead the modern world with its revolutionary technology features such as security and transparency. Many researchers have proposed the use of blockchain technology in digital identity to make it more secure and for avoiding the violation of privacy. AlMamun et al. [21] proposed a system on blockchain-based citizen identity using bio-information. They implemented their proposed digital identity system using Ethereum smart contract. Smart contracts are a type of Ethereum account; this means they have a balance and can send transactions over the network. However, they are not controlled by a user, instead they are deployed to the network and run as programmed. The results of AlMamun et al. show that an attacker cannot access the personal information of a citizen and any unauthorized access attempt is denied instantly, ensuring the privacy of private data.

Same as AlMamun, Sin and Naing [22] also proposed a digital identity management system to decrease duplicate identity documents. Het et al. [23] proposed the use of blockchain technology for digital identity management systems as a solution for digital identity in Myanmar. Their proposed system enabled to identify and control illegal duplication of passports. Since a valid passport can be issued only to one person (except passport loss, renewal, and expired) and is impossible for a person to apply for more than one passport type in the decentralized system, Dong et al. [24] proposed a solution for digital identity management and securing the information of banking customers. They proposed BBM which is a blockchain-based self-sovereign identity system for open banking. Self-sovereign identity (SSI) is a model for managing digital identities in which an individual or business has ownership over the ability to control their accounts and personal data. With an SSI approach, the users have control over their personal information is kept and used. Their proposed model provides a secure way for open customers who hesitate in sharing their personal information with third-party providers. Their model makes it possible to have a reliable and secure connection between open customers and third-party providers by allowing the customers to manage and control their identity. Li et al. [25] also used blockchain technology in the context of court trials and evidence management. They proposed a blockchain-based event management scheme LE-chain which supervises the flow of evidence and all of the court data as votes; they use digital signatures that anonymously authenticate the identities of witnesses to protect their privacy.

Healthcare has also been very important area where the identity of the citizens is crucial. Many researchers discovered the importance of digital identity in health care and how it could be used to secure the patients' data and improve their experience. Christen [27] proposed the use of blockchain-based digital platform to serve as a secure data repository while Bhuyan et al. [28] proposed a blockchain-based solution for managing and sharing the patients' healthcare care across different medical centers/hospitals in a secure way. Other researchers have also proposed the effective and secure management and sharing of healthcare data among different stakeholders.
