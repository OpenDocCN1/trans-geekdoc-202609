# 5.2.2 ROLE OF AI

The presence of AI in proctoring software is somewhat of a novelty. During
the 2010s, proctoring software was highly dependent on human intervention, notably
human proctors who would supervise students in real time. In hopes to
improve precision and efficiency of online proctoring, today's software implements
artificial intelligence software.⁵⁶ Today,s proctoring software now require less
human proctor in order to identify exam takers and detect cheating instances
to their algorithmic dependence.⁷⁷
