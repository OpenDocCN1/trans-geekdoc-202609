## 3.2.1.3 Bluetooth

Since its creation in 1998, Bluetooth has seen and provided considerable technological advancement. Its contribution to device-to-device communication has led to its adaptation by multiple industries. There is no coincidence that this technology is widespread and found in most devices such as phones, smartwatches, PCs, glasses, earphones, shoes, and more. So it is safe to say that it represents the go-to wireless connectivity for wearable gadgets and other devices. It uses UHF radio waves, known as short radio wave, with radio bands ranging from 2402 MHz to 2480 MHz and building a Personal Area Network (PAN), enabling a master-connectivity [58]. This technology provided the reality of IoT and its wide range of use. With the adaptation of Bluetooth low energy (BLE) it is found in battery-powered devices, most sensory devices that offered reduced power consumption, reduced latency, and cost compared to the classic Bluetooth while maintaining a decent communication range, more opportunities and benefits were provided to the field of IoT [62]. Since BLE can help conserve the device's energy when it is not in use, it can quickly pair and reconnect with devices in less than six milliseconds. With product selling four billion this year and its advantages, experts believe that Bluetooth will be a significant factor in the trillion-dollar IoT market.

## 3.2.3.2 5G

This network presents the fifth-generation wireless technology. It can provide higher speed, lower latency, and greater capacity than previous LTE networks. The 4G network is responsive 400x faster than processor, with predicted speeds of up to 10 Gbps. Furthermore, 5G networks will have even lower latency than 4G LTE, with data transmission less than five milliseconds. Finally, its capacity to connect devices at once is considered to deliver up 1.000x more than 4G, creating a productive ground for IoT development [46]. It will become possible to involve thousands of sensors in humans of devices to act and operate automatically. For example, Smart homes and cities will make a massive step into the future with 5G; using multiple connected devices, AI will be taken to place it has never been before from houses that give personalized suggestions that maximize environmental impact to connected vehicles for police linked to traffic lights. This high-speed, high-capacity, low-latency application of 5G will impact each sector. As a result, it will create an excellent and massive IoT ecosystem where networks can serve billions of connected devices [13].
