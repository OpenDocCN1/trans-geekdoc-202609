Machine learning (ML), a technique used in AI can monitor IoT devices and network activity to detect behavior that’s out of the ordinary in real-time and provide protective measures instantly [29]. Models that adapt daily to keep track of new and upcoming threats make them ideal for protecting complex threats. A network using ML systems can instantly detect threats before breaking the network via IoT devices. Moreover, given many IoT devices, ML could identify external devices to retrieve or send data when they are hidden inside the network from a security perspective. We do that by constantly scanning and comparing historical network behavior. Seeing an unusual increase in network traffic at a particular location, they could provide an alert or act on it [32].
