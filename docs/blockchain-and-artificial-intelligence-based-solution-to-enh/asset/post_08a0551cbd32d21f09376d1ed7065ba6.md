## OUR SENSITIVE DATA

According to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), when the processing of a personal data can represent a significant risk to fundamental rights and freedoms, it is sensitive data. Consequently, all security measures must be considered. Without limitation, the sensitive nature may concern data related to:

- ethnicity
- fingerprints
- biometrics
- financial
- education
- sexual preference
- social media
- expression of opinion (political, religious, and union)
- vulnerable persons
- minors

The collection of data by IoT can concern only sensitive data but another category of sensitive data.

## OUR PRIVATE LIFE

As Philippe Aries and Georges Duby have shown in Histoire de la vie privée:15 privacy is a concept as old as the world and manifests itself in different cultures. However, the meaning we refer to originates from an article that was published in 1890 by Samuel Warren and Louis Brandeis in the Harvard Law Review. It "the right to be alone."16 In other words, the right not to be disturbed by another, without permission. It is also the right to be able to exercise control over the use, and even storage of the data one produces. The web giants have wanted to Eric Schmidt, CEO of Google, said this: "If you have something that you don't want anyone to know, maybe you shouldn't be it in the first place."17 According to Schmidt, you have nothing to hide.
