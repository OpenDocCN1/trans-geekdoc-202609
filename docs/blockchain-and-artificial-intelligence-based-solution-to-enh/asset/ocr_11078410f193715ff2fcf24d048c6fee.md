# 1.6 PROPOSED SOLUTION

# 1.6.1 ARCHITECTURE OF THE PROPOSED SOLUTION

We propose in Figure 1.1 the architecture of a secure digital identity application. We define three main necessary layers. Our goal is (1) to ensure citizen data privacy and get it protected with a secure blockchain network, (2) to give to the citizen control of his identity and data, and (3) to allow a quick recovery of his identity.

As concrete example, we present in Figure 1.2 a use case of the renewal of the health card 2.

Figure 1.2 shows three actors, who is the citizen, the trusted authorities, and two main government services, that are, Health insurance service and financial service. The financial service is used by the blockchain module to verify and validate the digital identity of the citizen who requests the renewal of his health card without accessing to his personal financial information. This verification requests the appropriation of the citizen himself who has to authorize the verification of his identity through another service.
