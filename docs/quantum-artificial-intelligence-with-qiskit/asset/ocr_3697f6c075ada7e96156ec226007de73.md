# 6.3.4 No Solution

For the Boolean formula

> $\phi = (x_1 \leftrightarrow x_2) \wedge (
eg x_1 \wedge x_2) \wedge \neg x_3$$

no solution exists, the formula can never evaluate to value true. We change our circuit correspondingly with

```
#First And 2 is zero
qc.x(0)
qc.ccx(0,1,2)
qc.x(0)
qc.barrier()
#Result in 2
```
