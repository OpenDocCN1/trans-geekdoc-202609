# ### 3.7.3 OR Operation

The OR operation is represented by the unitary mapping according to the De Morgan's laws

$x_1 \vee x_2 = \neg(x_1 \wedge \neg x_2)$
$((I_2 \otimes X) \cdot CCX \cdot (X \otimes X \otimes I_1)) \cdot [xy0] = xy(x \vee y)$.

```
x = QuantumRegister(1, 'x')
y = QuantumRegister(1, 'y')
r = QuantumRegister(1, 'result')
qc = QuantumCircuit(x,y,r)
#Preparation
qc.x(x)
qc.x(y)
qc.barrier()
#Or Operation according to De Morgan's law
qc.x(x)
qc.x(y)
qc.x(r)
qc.ccx(x,y,r)
qc.x(x)
qc.x(y)
qc.draw()
```
