## 16.2. Level 2

The probability of observing $q_{21} = 00$ is $\sqrt{0.1}$

> $\sqrt{0.3}\cdot|00\rangle + \sqrt{0.7}\cdot|01\rangle$

and the probability of observing $q_{21} = 01$ is $\sqrt{0.3}$. both values divide the probability of observing $q_2 = 0 = \sqrt{0.3}$. To define the angle $\theta_{00}$, we normalize the value $\sqrt{0.1}$ by division of the probability of observing $q_2 = 0$

$$\theta_{00} = 1.91063 = \arccos\sqrt{\frac{0.1}{0.3}} \quad (16.4)$$

The rotation generates a superposition

$RY_{\theta_{00}}(\sqrt{0.3}\cdot|0\rangle + \sqrt{0.1}\cdot|00\rangle + \sqrt{0.2}\cdot|01\rangle) \quad (16.5)$

## Amplitude Encoding ■ 225

The probability of observing $q_2q_1 = 10$ is $\sqrt{0.4}$;

$\sqrt{0.1 \cdot |100\rangle} + \sqrt{0.3 \cdot |101\rangle}$

and the probability of observing $q_2q_1 = 11$ is $\sqrt{0.3}$ since both values divide the probability of observing $q_2 = \sqrt{0.7}$. To define the angle $\theta_{10}$ we normalize the value $\sqrt{0.4}$ by the division of the probability of observing $q_2 = 1$

$$\theta_{10} = 1.42745 = 2 \cdot \arccos\left(\frac{\sqrt{0.4}}{\sqrt{0.7}}\right) \quad (16.6)$$

The rotation generates a superpositions

$RY_{\sqrt{0.7} \cdot |1|} = \sqrt{0.4} \cdot |10| + \sqrt{0.3} \cdot |11|} \quad (16.7)$

Applying both two rotation gates that are controlled by the qubit $q_2$ generates a superposition (see Figure 16.1)

$RY_{\sqrt{0.3} \cdot |00|} + RY_{\sqrt{0.7} \cdot |01|} = \sqrt{0.1} \cdot |00| + \sqrt{0.2} \cdot |01| + \sqrt{0.4} \cdot |10| + \sqrt{0.3} \cdot |11|} \quad (16.8)$

The level of 1 and 2 decomposition is represented as (see Figure 16.3). Since the circuit only the two most important qubits (the left) $q_1q_0$ corresponds to the qubits $q_2q_1$ in the final circuit of three qubits.

## 16.3 COMBINING STATES

Instead of representing the binary tree by multi-control gates, we can use controlled SWAP operators with simple rotation gates [8].

### 16.3.1 Level 2

The binary tree at level two is represented by three qubits, to each qubit a rotation gate is applied. To the qubit 1 we apply the rotation $\theta_{01} = 1.98231$, to the qubit 0 we apply the rotation $\theta_{10} = 1.2745$, and to qubit 2 we apply the rotation $\theta_{00} = 1.9106$. The value of the qubit 1 controls the SWAP operation and the measured tensor product of qubit 2 represents the required distribution of the two qubits representing four states (see Figure 16.5)

## 16.3.2 Level 3

The binary tree at level three is represented by seven qubits, to each qubit a rotation gate is applied. To the qubit 3 we apply the rotation $\theta_{011} = 1.98231$, to the qubit 1 we apply the rotation $\theta_{101} = 1.2745$, and to qubit 5 we apply the rotation $\theta_{000} = 1.9106$. The value of the qubit 3 controls the SWAP operation. For the level three we apply the additional four rotations, we apply the rotation $\theta_{110} = 1.23096$ to the qubit 0, $\theta_{100} = 2.0944$ to the qubit 2, $\theta_{010} = 1.0472$ to the qubit 4, and $\theta_{000} = 1.98231$ to the qubit 6. The subtrees are merged with the results of level one by the controlled SWAP operation by qubit 3.
