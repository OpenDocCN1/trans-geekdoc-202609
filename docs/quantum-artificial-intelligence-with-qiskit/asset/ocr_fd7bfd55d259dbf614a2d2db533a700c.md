Quantum supremacy means that programmable quantum devices can solve a problem that no classical computer can solve in any feasible amount of time. Quantum computers will dominate when enough qubits are available. Such gains in computing power could change every aspect of our lives in the future.

Even without quantum supremacy, the quantum computing approach leads to easier formulation of certain algorithms, such as certain feature maps that are difficult to handle. Quantum computing creates a new way of thinking, making it possible to perform calculations that could not be considered before. The face of the world could be changed forever.
