A quantum register of length $m$ represents $m$ qubits in a Hilbert space of dimension $n=2^m$. A state in an $n$-dimensional Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_n$ is defined by an orthonormal basis

```
$|x_1\rangle, |x_2\rangle, \dots, |x_n\rangle$
```

and is represented as a unit-length vector

```
$\omega_1 \cdot |x_1\rangle + \omega_2 \cdot |x_2\rangle + \dots + \omega_n \cdot |x_n\rangle$
```

that determines the probability of distribution of the states. Each dimension corresponds to a possible combination. The state is in a basis state $|x_i\rangle$ with a probability $|x_i|^22$.

The compound system of the Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_n$ and a $w$-dimensional Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_w$ defined by an orthonormal basis $|y_1\rangle, |y_2\rangle, \dots, |y_w\rangle$ is defined by the tensor product

```
$\mathcal{H}_{n-w} = \mathcal{H}_n \otimes \mathcal{H}_w \\quad (2.18)$
```

According to this definition we can define operator on two qubits

```
$H \cdot (\omega_0 \cdot |0\rangle + \omega_1 \cdot |1\rangle) \otimes H \cdot (\omega_0 \cdot |0\rangle + \omega_1 \cdot |1\rangle) = \\
```
