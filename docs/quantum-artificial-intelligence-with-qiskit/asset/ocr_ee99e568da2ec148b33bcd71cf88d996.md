```

If we set $	heta = 0.x_m \cdots x_1$, we can rewrite the equation as

```
$|y\rangle = F_m \cdot |x\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \sum_{y \in B^m} e^{2\pi i \cdot y \cdot \theta} \cdot |y\rangle = \n= \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot \left(|0\rangle + e^{2\pi i \cdot (\theta^{m-1})} \cdot |1\rangle\right) \otimes \left(|0\rangle + e^{2\pi i \cdot (\theta^{m-2})} \cdot |1\rangle\right) \otimes \cdots \\n\quad\otimes \left(|0\rangle + e^{2\pi i \cdot (\theta^{2})} \cdot |1\rangle\right).$$
```

For m qubits, we define $C_{j+1}U^2$ in the following way. For $j \in {0, 1, 2, \cdots, m-1\}$, the control qubit $j+1$ of the $m$ qubits is set, $C_{j+1}U^2$ is applied to the target $|u\rangle$, otherwise not. The first step of the algorithm is

```
$|0^{\otimes m}\rangle|u\rangle$$
```

with $|u\rangle$ being the eigenvector of $U$. In the first step of the algorithm we build a superposition of $m$ controls

```
$H_m \cdot |0^{\otimes m}\rangle|u\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \sum_{x \in B^m} |x\rangle|u\rangle$$
```

```
$= \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot \left(|0\rangle + |1\rangle\right) \otimes \cdots \otimes \left(|0\rangle + |1\rangle\right) \cdot |u\rangle.$$$$
```

In the second step we apply $C_{j+1}U^2$ operators to the target $|u\rangle$

```
$\prod_{j=0}^{m-1} C_{j+1}U^2 \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot \left(|0\rangle + |1\rangle\right) \otimes \left(|0\rangle + |1\rangle\right) \otimes \cdots \otimes \left(|0\rangle + |1\rangle\right) \cdot |u\rangle = \\n= \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot \left(|0\rangle + e^{2\pi i \cdot (\theta^{m-1})} \cdot |1\rangle\right) \otimes \cdots \\n\quad\otimes \left(|0\rangle + e^{2\pi i \cdot (\theta^{2})} \cdot |1\rangle\right) \cdot |u\rangle.$$$$
```

In the third step we apply inverse QFT to the $m$ controls

```
$F_m \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \sum_{y \in B^m} e^{2\pi i \cdot y \cdot \theta} \cdot |y\rangle = \\n= F_m \cdot \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \sum_{y \in B^m} e^{-2\pi i \cdot y \cdot \theta} \cdot |y\rangle = |x\rangle|u\rangle.$$$$
```

In the fourth step we measure the first register composed of $m$ control qubits and estimate $\theta$

```
$$\theta = 0.x_m \cdots x_2x_1 = \frac{x}{n}=\frac{x}{2^m}.$$
