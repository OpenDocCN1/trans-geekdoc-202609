The probability of observing $q_{21} = 00$ is $\sqrt{0.1}$

> $\sqrt{0.3}\cdot|00angle + \sqrt{0.7}\cdot|01angle$

and the probability of observing $q_{21} = 01$ is both values divide the probability of observing $q_2 = 0 = \sqrt{0.3}$. To define the angle $	heta_{00}$, we normalize the value $\sqrt{0.1}$ by division of the probability of observing $q_2 = 0$

$$\theta_{00} = 1.91063 = \arccos\sqrt{\frac{0.1}{0.3}}$ $\quad (16.4)$

The rotation generates a superposition

$RY_{\theta_{00}}(\sqrt{0.3}\cdot|0angle + \sqrt{0.1}\cdot|00angle + \sqrt{0.2}\cdot|01angle)$ $\quad (16.5)$
