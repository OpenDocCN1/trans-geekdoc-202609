**Figure 15.9** Four superposition state corresponding to the four units of the associative memory. The qubits 0 to 3 represent the query vector $\chi_q = (1, 0, 0, 1)$, the qubits 4 to 7 to the associative memory, the qubits 8 to 11 represent the count, and the qubits 12 and 13 are the index qubits, whereas the control qubit 14 is zero. Note that the units are counted in the reverse order by the index qubits: 11 first unit, 10 for the second unit, and 00 for the fourth unit.

The architecture is described by 15 qubits. The qubits 0 to 3 represent the query vector, qubits 4 to 7 to the associative memory, the qubits 8 to 11 represent the count and the qubits 12 and 13 are the index qubits, the qubit 14 is the control qubit. The count operation is done by the cex Gate (see Figures 15.8 and 15.10).
