# 3.7 QUANTUM CIRCUITS

The four bit conjunction $x \wedge y \wedge z \wedge v$ requires three quantum Toffoli gates $CCX$ and three additional qubits that are zero. With the input state

```
$|xangle|yangle|zangle|vangle$
```

First Toffoli gate

```
$$(CCX \cdot |xangle|yangle) \otimes (I_4 \cdot |zangle|vangle) = |xangle|x \wedge yangle|zangle|vangle|$
```

Second Toffoli gate

```
$$(I_2 \cdot |xangle) \otimes (CCX \cdot |x \wedge yangle|zangle) \otimes (I_2 \cdot |vangle) = = |xangle|x \wedge yangle|x \wedge y \wedge zangle|vangle).$
```

Third quantum Toffoli gate

```
$$(I_4 \cdot |zangle|x \wedge yangle|zangle) \otimes (CCX \cdot |x \wedge y \wedge zangle|vangle) = |xangle|x \wedge yangle|x \wedge y \wedge zangle|x \wedge y \wedge z \wedge vangle.$
```

The circuit corresponds to the following unitary mapping

with the result

```
$|xangle|x \wedge yangle|x \wedge y \wedge zangle|vangle|x \wedge y \wedge z \wedge vangle$
```

We define a quantum circuit with seven qubits and name them using the QuantumRegister(size, name), with size is number of qubits to include in the register and the name of the register as appears in the drawing of the quantum circuit. We create the register $x, y, z, v$, two auxiliary registers and a register $r$ with the name result and generate the quantum circuit with the QuantumCircuit(x, y, z, v, r). We initialize the qubits $x, y, z, v$ to one with the gate $X$. We note the order of the qubits to make it clear. After the initialization we use the qc.barrier(), it separates the representation in the circuit. It is not a gate and use three quantum Toffoli gates, also called the $CCX$ gate (CCNOT - controlled not gate) and the final result or the operation is represented in the quantum qubit $r$. Qiskit uses the little endian notation, which stores the least-significant byte by the smallest address. Qubits are represented from the most significant bit (MSB) on the left to the least significant (LSB) on the right (little-endian). This is similar as binary numbers or to bitstring representation on classical computers, in our example we represent $x$ by the first qubit, $y$ by the second qubit and on representing the state $|r, aux, v, z, y, x>$
