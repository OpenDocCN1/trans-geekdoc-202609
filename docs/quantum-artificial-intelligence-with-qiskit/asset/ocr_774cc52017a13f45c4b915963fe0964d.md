Figure 15.8. The quantum circuit that produces the sleep phase. 0 to 3 represent the vector, the qubits 4 to 7 the associative memory, the qubits 8 to 11 represent the count, and the qubits 12 and 13 are the index qubits whereas the qubit 14 is the control qubit.

```
$$\frac{1}{2\cdot\sqrt{2}} \cdot |0\rangle \left(\\sum_{j=1}^{4} (c_{4c_3c_2c_j}) \cdot (w_4w_3w_2w_j)\right) \otimes |1001\rangle +$
```

```
$$\frac{1}{2\cdot\sqrt{2}} \cdot |1\rangle \left(\(\sum_{j=1}^{4} (c_{4c_3c_2c_j}) \cdot (w_4w_3w_2w_j)\right) \otimes |1001\rangle \quad (15.15)$
```

Applying controlled phase operation with $N=2$ since two ones are present in the vector count and $count_j \le 2$

```
$$\frac{1}{2\cdot\sqrt{2}} \cdot |0\rangle \left(\sum_{j=1}^{4} e^{-i\frac{\pi\cdot count_j}{2}} \cdot (c_{4c_3c_2c_j}) \cdot (w_4w_3w_2w_j)\right) \otimes |1001\rangle +$
```

```
$$\frac{1}{2\cdot\sqrt{2}} \cdot |1\rangle \left(\sum_{j=1}^{4} e^{-i\frac{\pi\cdot count_j}{2}} \cdot (c_{4c_3c_2c_j}) \cdot (w_4w_3w_2w_j)\right) \otimes |1001\rangle \quad (15.16)$
```

and using the Hadamard gate to the control qubit we get

```
$$\left(\sum_{j=1}^{4} \frac{1}{2} \cdot \cos\left(\frac{\pi\cdot count_j}{2}\right)\right) \cdot |0\rangle \cdot (c_{4c_3c_2c_j}) \cdot (w_4w_3w_2w_j) \otimes |1001\rangle$
```

```
$$\left(\sum_{j=1}^{4} \frac{1}{2} \cdot i\sin\left(\frac{\pi\cdot count_j}{2}\right)\right) \cdot |1\rangle \cdot (c_{4c_3c_2c_j}) \cdot (w_4w_3w_2w_j) \otimes |1001\rangle \quad (15.17)$
```
