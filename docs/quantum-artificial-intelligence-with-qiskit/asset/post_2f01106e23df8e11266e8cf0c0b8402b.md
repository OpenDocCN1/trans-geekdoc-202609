## 5.3.2 Circuit Representation

Grover’s amplification (for $m \geq 2$) is based on $G_m=-Q_z$. The unitary operator reverses the sign of $|0\rangle$

$$\Lambda_m \cdot |0\rangle = -|0\rangle$$

and for $|x\rangle \neq |0\rangle$

$$\Lambda_m \cdot |x\rangle = -|x\rangle$$

Then we can write

$$G_m=-Q_z=-I_m+2 \cdot P_m=2 \cdot P_m-I_m=(H_m \cdot \Lambda_m \cdot H_m) \quad (5.17)$$

with

$$H_m \cdot \Lambda_m \cdot H_m=\begin{pmatrix} 1-\frac{2}{n} & \dots & \dots & \dots \\ \dots & \frac{2}{n} & \dots & \frac{2}{n} \\ \dots & \dots & \dots & \dots \\ \dots & \frac{2}{n} & \dots & 1-\frac{2}{n} \end{pmatrix} \quad (5.18)$$

## 5.5 DECOMPOSITION

With the Grover's amplification we have $G_m = -(H_m \cdot \Lambda_m \cdot H_m)$. How we decompose $\Lambda_m$ by quantum gates? We know that for

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$$

and

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$$

For three qubits we can define $\Lambda_3$ accordingly with

$X_3 = X \otimes X \otimes X$
$H_0 = I \otimes I \otimes H$
$\Lambda_3 = X_3 \cdot H_0 \cdot CCX \cdot H_0 \cdot X_3$

and for minus sign operation $RZ(2\pi)_3 = RZ(2\pi) \otimes RZ(2\pi) \otimes RZ(2\pi)$ it follows and we can represent it by the following circuit

## 5.6 QISKIT EXAMPLES

In the next step we apply Grover's amplification to a marked state of three qubits. Our solution corresponds to the Boolean formula

$-x \wedge y \wedge \neg z$

for which it evaluates true, which is the case for $x = 0$, $y = 1$, and $z = 0$. In this case the state determined by the oracle function is $o(010) = 1$ with the solution encoded by $(-1)^{(x)}$. The unitary operator $T$

$$T = (X \otimes I \otimes X \otimes I) \cdot MCX \cdot (X \otimes I \otimes X \otimes I)$$

represents the oracle function $o(x)$ which determines if the configuration is the configuration

$$T \cdot |x\rangle = |o(x) \oplus c\rangle$$

with

$$H_0 = I \otimes I \otimes I \otimes H$$

$$H_0 \cdot T \cdot H_4 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{8}} \sum_{x \in B^3} (-1)^{o(x)} \cdot |x\rangle$$

the value of the function $o(x)$ is encoded by $(-1)^{o(x)}$, see well Figure 5.2.
