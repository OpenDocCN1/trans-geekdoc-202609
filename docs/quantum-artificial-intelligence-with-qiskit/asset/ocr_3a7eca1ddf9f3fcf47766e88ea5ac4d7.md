# 5.2 HOUSEHOLDER REFLECTION

The Householder reflection reflects one $|xangle$ to its negative and leaves invariant the orthogonal complement of it. It is described by the Householder matrix $Q_x$ with $||x|| = 1$ representing $m$ qubits with $n=2^m$ and the projection matrix $P$

$P = |xangle\langle x|

and

$Q_x = I_m - 2 \cdot P \quad (5.4)$

Suppose $P_m$ is generated by the normalized vector $|xangle$ indicating the direction of the bisecting line,

$|xangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot |x_1angle + \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot |x_2angle + \dots + \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \cdot |x_nangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \quad (5.5)$

then the projection matrix $P_m$ is

$P_m = |xangle\langle x| = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \ 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \ 1 & 1 & \dots & 1 \end{pmatrix} \quad (5.6)$

The projection matrix $P_m$ computes for each dimension the mean value

$\begin{pmatrix} \frac{x_1}{\sqrt{n}} \ \frac{x_2}{\sqrt{n}} \ \vdots \ \frac{x_n}{\sqrt{n}} \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} \frac{1}{n} & \frac{1}{n} & \dots & \frac{1}{n} \ \frac{1}{n} & \frac{1}{n} & \dots & \frac{1}{n} \ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \ \frac{1}{n} & \frac{1}{n} & \dots & \frac{1}{n} \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \ x_2 \ \vdots \ x_n \end{pmatrix} \ \quad (5.7)$

and the Householder reflection

$Q_x = I_m - 2 \cdot P_m. \quad (5.8)$

computes the following mapping,

$x_i^{new} = x_i^{old} - 2 \cdot \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n x_i^{old}}{n} \ \quad (5.9)$

# 5.3 GROVER's AMPLIFICATION

Grover's amplification is based on $-Q_x$. It is a unitary operator with

$G_m := -Q_x - I_m + 2 \cdot P_m = 2 \cdot P_m - I_m \ \quad (5.10)$
