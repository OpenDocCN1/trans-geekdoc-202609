$\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \cdot |1angle \otimes (e^{-i\frac{\pi}{3}} \cdot |1angle \otimes |0angle \otimes e^{-i\frac{\pi}{3}} \cdot |1angle) = \quad (15.11)$

$\frac{e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}}}{\sqrt{2}}|0101angle + \frac{e^{-i\frac{\pi}{3}}}{\sqrt{2}}|1101angle$

If we apply a Hadamard gate to the control qubit [111] we get

$(H \otimes I \otimes I \otimes I) \cdot \frac{e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}}}{\sqrt{2}}|0101angle + \frac{e^{-i\frac{\pi}{3}}}{\sqrt{2}}|1101angle) =$

$\frac{e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}} + e^{-i\frac{\pi}{3}}}{2}|0101angle + i \cdot \frac{e^{i\frac{\pi}{3}} - e^{-i\frac{\pi}{3}}}{2}|1101angle = \quad $

$\cos\left(\frac{\pi \cdot 2}{2 \cdot 3}\right)|0101angle + i \cdot \sin\left(\frac{\pi \cdot 2}{2 \cdot 3}\right);$

$\cos\left(\frac{\pi \cdot 2}{2 \cdot 3}\right)|0angle + i \cdot \sin\left(\frac{\pi \cdot 2}{2 \cdot 3}\right)|1\rangle = \quad (15.12)$

The probability of measuring the control qubit |0⟩ is

$p(|0\rangle) = p(|0101\rangle) = \left(\cos\left(\frac{\pi \cdot 2}{2 \cdot 3}\right)\right)^2 = 0.25$

and the probability of measuring the control qubit |1⟩ is

$p(|1\rangle) = p(|1101\rangle) = \left(\sin\left(\frac{\pi \cdot 2}{2 \cdot 3}\right)\right)^2 = 0.75$
