Two marked state results after two iterations are indicated in the histogram of Figure 8.3. The two solutions correspond to the path descriptor [1100] and the symmetric path descriptor [0011].

The solution of the path descriptor [1100] corresponds to:

+   - [11]: $p_{21} \rightarrow p_{12}$, working memory: $cdcab \rightarrow cdcba$
- [00]: $p_{54} \rightarrow p_{45}$, working memory: $cdcba \rightarrow dccba$

The solution of the path descriptor [0011] corresponds to:

+   - [00]: $p_{54} \rightarrow p_{45}$, working memory: $cdcab \rightarrow dccab$
- [11]: $p_{21} \rightarrow p_{12}$, working memory: $cdcab \rightarrow dccab$
