# Preface

Quantum artificial intelligence (QAI) is a new interdisciplinary research field that combines quantum computing with artificial intelligence (AI). It aims to use the unique properties of quantum computers, which leverage quantum mechanical effects (such as superposition and entanglement) to enhance the capabilities of AI systems. In QAI, progress is being made quickly. What was a scientific dream years ago is becoming more real, and now you (by reading this book) are at the forefront of the revolution QAI.

Quantum algorithms for AI have been proposed, including a quantum tree search algorithm and a quantum production system that will be demonstrated by qiskit simulation by step. Qiskit is an open-source software development kit (SDK) for working with quantum computers at the level of circuits and algorithms [21], IBM Quantum, https://quantum-computing.ibm.com/. It provides tools for creating and manipulating quantum programs and running them on prototype quantum devices on IBM Quantum Experience or simulators on a local computer. It follows the quantum circuit model for universal quantum computing and can be used for any quantum hardware that follows this model. Qiskit is based on Python and you can find all information about it at https://qiskit.org.

This book provides tools for creating and making quantum programs and running them on prototype quantum devices or simulators on a local computer, such as a simple laptop. It follows the quantum circuit model for universal quantum computing and can be used with any quantum hardware that follows this model. You can download all the examples (Jupyter notebooks) from the book https://github.com/andrejwieissert/qai.1
