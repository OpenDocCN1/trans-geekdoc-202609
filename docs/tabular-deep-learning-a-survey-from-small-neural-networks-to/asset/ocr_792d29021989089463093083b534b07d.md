Interpretability. We want to emphasize that interpretability is an important aspect of model. Historically, there has been a trade-off between performance and interpretability. Today, however, several models have evolved that incorporate interpretability into the design of the architecture and are completely competitive. Attention-based methods, graph-based methods may bridge the gap between accuracy and transparency. If you choose to use a model that is not interpretable, performance will suffer in respect to these models, considering potential biases and differences. Benchmarks should also be used for evaluating model interpretability. Not all models provide the same performance. The lack of ground truth does not prevent a thorough investigation. In general, there is a need for standardization regarding interpretability methods and their evaluation. Thus, several open questions remain for future study.
