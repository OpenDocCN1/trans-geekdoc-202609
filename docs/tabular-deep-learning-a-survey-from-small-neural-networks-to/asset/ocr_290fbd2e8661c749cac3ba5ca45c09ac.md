# 14. Conclusions

Tabular data remain dominant in real-world applications (finance, healthcare, cybersecurity, ecommerce). However, this remains the last domain where neural models have not achieved dominance over traditional machine learning. This stems from several challenges that make it complex to design a winning architecture. Our various models have attempted to address the limitations of neural networks in the tabular domain, significantly narrowing the gap between neural and traditional models. To no doubt, unlike computer vision, tabular data is difficult to process. Furthermore, large-scale preprocessing techniques that generalize across different datasets are one of the most interesting areas of research. At the same time the dominance of small/medium datasets is often explored. Since these datasets come from sensitive fields, there is a strong interest in terms of their interpretability. Additionally, unsupervised and undersupervised learning for tabular data remains underrepresented, presenting a promising direction for future updates of this field. As such, the tabular domain is still evolving, and there remains room for improvement and research. Some open questions remain, such as better inductive biases, efficiency and interpretability, which may be intriguing research questions for future studies.
