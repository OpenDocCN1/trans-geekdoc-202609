Privacy preservation. Synthetic data was used to produce an anonymized dataset for privacy, but without simultaneously considering task or model performance. The Synthetic data utilizes a Gaussian Copula distribution to learn the distribution of relational data and thus is able to generate new data [292]. Bayesian networks have been used to generate tabular data without too high computational cost [398]. GANs have been used extensively for this purpose. PGAN and DCTGAN are used here to avoid recurring the same examples [332]; PATE-GAN uses the discriminator as an ensemble of Teacher models (PATE) [162]; it is a method based on differential privacy to ensure that the output of a model does not reveal information about any specific individual's data (multiple models are trained on sensitive subsets and each has access to a certain subset, the predictions of the teachers are aggregated via "voting") [259]. Table-GAN [261] attempts to generate tables that are similar to the original table while preserving the statistical properties of the original table while ensuring privacy. There are some models that are specific to particular domains, such as MedGAN to generate anonymized patient data [10]. In fact they can be directly used or pretrained for other algorithms. # 10. Interpretability

Explainable learning (XAI) is a sub-branch of artificial intelligence that is becoming increasingly important. The purpose of XAI is to make machine learning model transparent, especially when it to models that are considered black boxes, like neural networks. XAI is now a component of choosing a model, especially when it comes to a sensitive domain such as finance or healthcare. To date, there is no clear definition or taxonomy of XAI. This discussion is beyond the scope of this study. In short, we can see that XAI methods fall into two groups: methods that are inherent by definition or design (of structure or training), and the latter represents methods that are applied to already trained models.
