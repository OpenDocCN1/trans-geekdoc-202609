Therefore, the shipping industry is a very relevant domain that needs attention, especially when a new wave of technological advancements that can ease and dissipate the inherent troubles facing the industry looms on the horizon. This book and the subject the authors are trying to tackle focuses on understanding the impact of specific technologies that are disruptive in nature on the shipping industry, including related issues of global supply chain management.

A force majeure, such as pandemics, natural calamities, and geopolitical instabilities, could affect the global supply chain, especially in the future. With an opaque supply chain, overpowered by middlemen, and covered with heaps of paperwork, acts as a hindrance to the efficient functioning of the global shipping industry. This can be addressed by employing innovation afforded by novel technologies, such as blockchain and artificial intelligence. The authors, through the book, have focussed on two technologies, namely blockchain and artificial intelligence. Analyzing the numerous technical aspects, its importance, and applications, the authors have furthered this thought, to study an ecosystem, that has integrated the two technologies. Furthermore, by exploring this possibility of an integrated ecosystem, the shipping industry is brought into focus, eyeballing at the possible applications it has on the industry.
