For centuries the world has witnessed technology as a fundamental agent for social change. They offer numerous possibilities to innovate, design, and develop new scientific and technological ideas to bring in a change in civilian, commercial, and strategic domains.

Renowned Harvard Business School professor, Clayton Christensen, debated about disruptive technology and innovation during the 1990s. During the last few decades, the world has witnessed the rise of novel technologies such as artificial intelligence, blockchain, the Internet of Things (IoT), robotics, 3D printing, and many more that have trodden a new path of innovation, although still in its nascent stages. These technologies have showcased the potential applicability it has in many sectors. It has been predicted that technologies like these have the potential and capability to disrupt the existing technology setup bringing in a sea change in human activities for the future. They could bring in lifestyle changes, improve various existing commercial practices, and scientifically impact the security apparatus. Disruptive technologies tend to be new and aren’t incremental improvements in existing technologies. Hence, it is critical to study the possible impact of such technologies on the existing industrial setup. It is necessary to undertake such a study for every major sector of the industry.
