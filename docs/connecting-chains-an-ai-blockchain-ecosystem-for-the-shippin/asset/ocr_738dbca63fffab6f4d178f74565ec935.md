Before joining SSIS he was a Senior Research Associate at the Society for Policy Studies (SPS), New Delhi from June 2013 to July 2016. At the SPS, he has demonstrated his ability to organize conferences and roundtables in concert with diplomatic missions and other stakeholders. Prior to joining the SPS, he was a Research Analyst with Socio-Political Analysis and Research Kendra (SPARK), Mumbai. At SPARK, he provided research inputs to members of the Maharashtra legislature for the preparation of private member bills. He has done his Masters in the International Relations from the South Asian University, New Delhi, established by South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation. In 2004, he graduated with a Bachelors of Computer Applications from Tilak Maharashtra Vidyapeeth.
