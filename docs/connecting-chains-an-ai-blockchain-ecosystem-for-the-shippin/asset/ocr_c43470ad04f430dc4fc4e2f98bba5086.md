# Foreword

Expressly owing to the COVID-19 crisis, various states in the world have understood the importance of living together for their survival and future progress. At the same time, the crisis, as worst as the COVID-19 pandemic, that can destroy the entire human race, if the appropriate cure is not found in time, has not stopped humanity from playing dirty power politics. However, broadly, humans have understood that for their safe existence, they need to ensure to retain and enhance their ability to innovate and experiment. This process of experimentation would only allow them to find solutions for various difficulties, including major crises like the pandemics.

One of the influential technologies which have been dominating the global technology spectrum since the second half of the 20th century is the Information Technologies (IT). The COVID-19 pandemic and resultant interruptions have conclusively changed the approach towards technology adaption in general and digital technology adoption in particular, both for consumers as well as corporates. More importantly, several governments have also understood that various offshoots of IT, as well as IT-based applications, can play a significant role in governance alike, and technology is likely to play a vital role in recovery, as well.
