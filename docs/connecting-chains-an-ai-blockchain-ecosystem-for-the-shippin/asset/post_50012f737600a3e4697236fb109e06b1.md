In a world where various technological advancements have influenced the way humans interact on a global scale, a few disruptive technologies have cleared the path for a new chapter in global affairs. Among these technologies is the blockchain technology which is a distributed ledger technology (DLT) and artificial intelligence. Global logistics, which remains to be the backbone of the entire global supply chain, describes the importance of ensuring the optimization of the shipping industry challenges. However, the shipping industry is going through some challenges that force the industry to reconsider their business model. The shipping industry has a lack of visibility due to the opaqueness created by the complex structure of the industry, with numerous agents and intermediaries, filing heaps of paperwork, which continues to plague the smooth and efficient functioning of the shipping industry. Through this book, we are focusing on these aspects of the evolved technologies and their applications in the global shipping industry. Emerging and disruptive technologies such as blockchain and AI, has been implemented in parts, within the shipping industry, however, due to certain challenges, a fully-functional implementation has not taken place. This book would be exploring how the integration of blockchain technology and artificial intelligence would impact the shipping sector and examines the benefits of an integrated ecosystem for the industry.

## About the Authors

Ronnie Ninan is enrolled in the Young India Fellowship from Ashoka University, Sonipat, India. His areas of interests are science and technology policy, defence policy, and supply chain management. He has been an active contributor to various online platforms. He has worked as a research intern in the Centre on Strategic Technologies at Manohar Parrikar Institute for Defence Studies and Analyses (MP-IDSA), New Delhi. Prior to this he worked as a management intern at the French container transportation and shipping company, CMA-CGM, at their Mumbai head office. He holds a Master's degree in International Studies from the Symbiosis School of International Studies, which is an institute under the Symbiosis International University, Pune, India. He has graduated with a Bachelors in Physics from St. Xavier's College (Autonomous), Mumbai, India.

Aniket Bhavthankar joined SSIS in August 2016. His areas of interest include Indian strategic thought, maritime domain, South Asia, the geopolitics of the Arctic region. He has written articles on diverse subjects ranging from the strategic interests of India in South Asia to the emerging flux in maritime affairs in the Indian Ocean and the Arctic. He is an active contributor to on-line platforms, South Asia Monitor and The Indian Diaspora, on issues related to the Indian diaspora, maritime affairs and issues pertaining to the Indian foreign policy in South Asia and Africa. Before joining SSIS he was a Senior Research Associate at the Society for Policy Studies (SPS), New Delhi from June 2013 to July 2016. At the SPS, he has demonstrated his ability to organize conferences and roundtables in concert with diplomatic missions and other stakeholders. Prior to joining the SPS, he was a Research Analyst with Socio-Political Analysis and Research Kendra (SPARK), Mumbai. At SPARK, he provided research inputs to members of the Maharashtra legislature for the preparation of private member bills. He has done his Masters in the International Relations from the South Asian University, New Delhi, established by South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation. In 2004, he graduated with a Bachelors of Computer Applications from Tilak Maharashtra Vidyapeeth.
