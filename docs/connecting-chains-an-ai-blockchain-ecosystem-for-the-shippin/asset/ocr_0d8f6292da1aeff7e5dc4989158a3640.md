# About the Authors

Ronnie Ninan is enrolled in the Young India Fellowship from Ashoka University, Sonipat, India. His areas of interests are science and technology policy, defence policy, and supply chain management. He has been an active contributor to various online platforms. He has worked as a research intern in the Centre on Strategic Technologies at Manohar Parrikar Institute for Defence Studies and Analyses (MP-IDSA), New Delhi. Prior to this he worked as a management intern at the French container transportation and shipping company, CMA-CGM, at their Mumbai head office. He holds a Master's degree in International Studies from the Symbiosis School of International Studies, which is an institute under the Symbiosis International University, Pune, India. He has graduated with a Bachelors in Physics from St. Xavier's College (Autonomous), Mumbai, India.
