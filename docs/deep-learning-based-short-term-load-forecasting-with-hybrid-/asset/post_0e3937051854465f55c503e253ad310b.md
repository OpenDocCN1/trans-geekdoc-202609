# ABSTRACT

- Keywords:
- Deep learning
- Hybrid feature selection
- Long term load memory
- Power system
- Recurrent neural network

The reliable and economic operation of the power system rely on an accurate prediction of short load. In this paper, a deep learning Based Short Term Memory (LSTM) with hybrid feature selection RMR-HFS-LSTM is compared. The objective of this study is to reduce the error of load prediction. The RMR-HFS is a combination of filter and wrapper feature selection introduced for selecting optimal subset of features. The information theory based mutual information filter selection is utilized to reduce dimensionality of diversity by finding and eliminating irrelevant features. The selected features of feature selection is tested using Recurrent Feature Elimination (RFE). The result shows LSTM improves the accuracy and load data using uncertainty experiments. The performance of the proposed method is conducted on European weather and load data using comparison against Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) and Recursive Neural Network (RNN). The results show that the proposed RMR-HFS-LSTM outperforms other models.

# 1. Introduction

The electricity load forecasting an important research area in the competitive, economic business due to an imbalance of electricity power demand and construction. Load forecasting is a prime task to provide power in system for making decisions on trading market, real time smart dispatch, smart grid automation and control [1]. The load forecasting should result in accurate outcome for achieving the stable and an efficient function of the power system. The electricity demand is highly fluctuating for satisfying electricity demand, so it should be generated when a demand for it. If the demand is known in advance, all the operational requirements for the generation of electricity may be met. The power system has several objectives, to provide the stable and safe power as per requirement at the right time, to save the operational cost, to maintain the maintenance cost, and the reliability of the power system and balance the power generation with the dynamic changes in load [5,2]. Over estimation or under estimation leads to complex financial implications to the power system [7].

The uncertain and volatile nature of the load and the existence of the incomplete, non-homogeneous, redundant and irrelevant information in the load data makes it difficult to achieve an accurate forecast. Weather information has high impact on the load demand. The prediction of load considering the seasonal and weather information yield an accurate result [9,10]. The forecasting can be done at an hourly, daily, monthly and yearly basis. The load demand correlation with external factors such as humidity, temperature, wind speed, pressure, snowfall, cloud and the usage of home appliances by the ends [12]. In learning process, good quality of input is and only the relevant information is fed as input to the learning and prediction process. The feature selection is one of the dimensionality reduction technique which selects the relevant features from the list of original features based on the statistical characteristics of the features [13]. So, it retains the originality of the features.

On the other hand, the wrapper feature selection selects subsets of features based on the learning model [15,14]. While the feature selection techniques the non-informative, irrelevant and redundant information from the input data provided to the learning process can lead to the better performance of the classifier.

The statistical and artificial intelligence based machine learning methods were utilized by researchers in the past. Now the deep learning methods are used for achieving efficiency with minimum effort. The deep learning based model has an ability of learning complexities, and spatial and temporal dependencies of the series data by stacking number of layers between input and output layers. For example, neural networks can handle non-linear mapping of series data. But, it suffers from the vanishing and the exploding gradients problem. The neural network utilizes backpropagation for generating the derivative [21]. While the gradients are small the learning becomes slow, and when they are large it creates the exploding gradients problem. So the use of RNN LSTM solves these problems by introducing gating mechanisms. The identification of importance of important feature, optimal lag and any minimum number of layers will guarantee the reduction of an overfitting issue. LSTM can capture large volume of high-dimensional data by considering multiple features.

The proposed RMR-LSTM deep learning prediction model consists of preliminary data processing, hybrid feature selection and deep prediction.

- The pre-processing stage handles the hourly time series electricity load and the associated weather data, by replacing the missing values with the past data, and reducing the data using min-max normalization.

The hybrid feature selection finds the relevant features by using of clustering, and wrapper feature selection. The RLEf and mutual information feature selection identifies the features by removing the irrelevant and non-informative features with statistical characteristics. The approach reduces redundant and tunes the features selected by the feature selection SVR as the feature evaluator and finds the optimal subset of features.

- The deep prediction phase predicts the load by using LSTM. The proposed deep learning model RMR-LSTM is compared against RNN and LSTM in terms of MAPE and RMSE metrics.
