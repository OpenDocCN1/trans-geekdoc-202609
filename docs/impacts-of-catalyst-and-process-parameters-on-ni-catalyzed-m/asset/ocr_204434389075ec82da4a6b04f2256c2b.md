Firstly, the Boudouard reaction (2CO <=> CO2 + C) may be favored under high CO concentrations, which will decrease the relative concentration of CO and thus increase the syngas ratio [51]. Secondly, catalysts that are particularly prone to direct methane decomposition (CH4 <=> 2 H2 + C) will produce additional H2 as a result of this side reaction [52]. Thus, although the possibility of producing syngas with
