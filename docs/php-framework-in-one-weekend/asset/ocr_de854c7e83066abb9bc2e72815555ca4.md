# About the author

I wrote and delivered my first course in PHP at the University of Sheffield in 2001. After a brief stint working at a record label, I co-founded a digital music startup and was CTO until 2007 when I went on to start Ideation Networks. As a Drupal specialist, I provided software development and training for Drupal agencies, large Drupal deployments and flagship Drupal websites. Most recently, this has included helping developers switch from Drupal 7 to Drupal 8, and teaching modern PHP.
