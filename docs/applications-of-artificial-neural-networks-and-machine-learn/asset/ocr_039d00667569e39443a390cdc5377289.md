# 15.3 Optimal Design of Towers

In this chapter the genetic algorithm is used for optimal design. For optimization of small towers one can use the force method together with energy approach as described in [8]. Using energy, force method and genetic algorithms result in eliminating the need for the formation of the inverse of matrices. For large scale towers such a mixed method can be slow and occasional leads to local optima. In order to speed up the process of analysis, here a neural network is utilized. Also the genetic algorithm employed, is migration genetic algorithm used which is more suitable than simple GA for large scale problems. For towers with small number of elements the first method is used and for the towers with high number of members the second approach is employed. In the following the formulation for both methods are presented.

First a short introduction is provided for the energy methods. The nonlinear behaviour of the material is modeled as ε = f(σ) or σ = g(ε) representing the stress strain relationship. The strain energy, complementary strain energy and total potential energy can be represented as:

```
U = ∬ g(ε)dεdV, Uᶜ = ∬ f(σ)dσdV (15.7)
V = U - P′u (15.8)
```

where U is the strain energy, Uᶜ is the complementary strain energy, V is the total potential energy, P is the applied load vector and u is the nodal displacements vector. Considering the first Castigliano's theorem for an elastic (linear or non-linear) system the potential energy is minimum for each system in equilibrium. Similarly in the second theorem, if the internal forces satisfy the compatibility, then the complementary energy becomes minimum. In general, U corresponds to the stiffness method and Uᶜ corresponds to the flexibility approach. Ion the first method we look for the displacements while in the latter we seek the unknown forces. In a statically indeterminate structure once the unknown forces are calculated the remaining member forces can easily be obtained. The use of Uᶜ corresponds to smaller number of unknowns and in what follows the force method will be employed.
