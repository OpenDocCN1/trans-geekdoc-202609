# 11.2 Nonlinear Dynamic Analysis and Damage Assessment

In order to estimate the seismic damage of the structures quantitatively, an appropriate damage assessment method is needed. For this purpose, several damage indices have been presented by researchers. Park and Ang [14] damage index is one of the most suitable indices for evaluating the seismic damage of concrete structures. This index is defined as follows:

D.I. PA = \frac{\delta_m}{\delta_u} + \frac{\beta}{\delta_u F_y} \int dE (11.1)

In the above equation:

+   - D.I. PA Park and Ang Damage Index
+   - \delta_m Maximum deformation of structure during an earthquake
+   - \delta_u Ultimate deformation capacity of structure under monotonic loading
+   - \beta Dimensionless strength reduction parameter
+   - \int dE Hysteretic energy absorbed by structure during response history

Park and Ang damage index is calibrated as follow:

+   - 0 \le D.I. PA < 0.4 Represents repairable damage
+   - 0.4 \le D.I. PA < 1.0 Represents damage beyond repair
+   - 1.0 \le D.I. PA Represents total collapse of structure.

In order to perform dynamic analyses, IDARC program is employed. This program is capable of calculating the Park and Ang damage index for structures. Park and Ang damage index can be obtained either for a structural element (local damage index), or for the entire structure (global damage index). In this research, the global damage indices are calculated for the structures.
