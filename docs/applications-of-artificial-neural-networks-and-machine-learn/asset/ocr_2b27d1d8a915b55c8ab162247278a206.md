Thus, the developed models can predict the target values of the shear strength of HSC slender beams without web reinforcement with acceptable accuracy and less error than the available design codes over a wide range of input variables.

To have a deeper understanding of the errors in design codes, the discrepancy (DR) between measured and predicted shear strength as an error indicator was plotted against the most effective parameters including longitudinal steel ratio (\(\rho\)), \(f'_c\), \(d\) and \(a/d\) for testing data set in Fig. 22.3. The results of Eurocode-2 [30], CEP-FIP [31] and AS 3600-2009 [32], which had more acceptable accuracy, were compared with those of GMDH and MARS models in this aspect. The best linear regression is also fitted to prediction values of mentioned models. Errors of an appropriate model should be independent or less sensitive to the variation of the input parameters involved in that phenomenon. Otherwise, it can be interpreted that those input parameters either aren’t correctly incorporated or should be included in that model [39]. As shown in Fig. 22.3, the errors of these design codes are sensitive to change of \(\rho\) as if their prediction varied from overestimation status to underestimation status as \(\rho\) increases. It can be interpreted that this parameter is not correctly involved in their equations. On the other hand, the errors of GMDH and MARS models are completely independent of this parameter.

According to this figure, the errors of all models are almost independent of \(f'_c\) and \(d\) parameters. Thus, it can be expected that these parameters probably well-defined in the mentioned models. It should be noted that the errors of Eurocode-2 [30] to variation of \(d\) parameter is more than other models. Also the error sensitivity of GMDH and MARS models to variations of \(d\) and \(f'_c\) is less than the three design codes. Figure 22.3 also demonstrates the errors of mentioned models as a function of \(a/d\) parameter. It can be seen that the predictions of design codes varied from underestimation status to overestimation status as \(a/d\) increases and this inclination in CEB-FIP [31] model is less than the other two equations.
