+   • Among the existing equations in literature, there is a good agreement between measured and predicted maximum shear strength using AS 3600-2009 equation. However, a notable number of its predictions were categorized in unsafe region based on Collins criteria. In contrast, the CEB-FIP model represented the safest predictions according to this criterion while its accuracy is remarkably limited.

+   • The newly proposed GMDH and MARS models outperformed the existing design equations in terms of both accuracy and safety. The errors of developed GMDH and MARS models also showed a symmetrical and predictable behavior. The MARS model also outperformed GMDH and MNLR models in both accuracy and safety aspects.

+   • The results of statistical measures show that the proposed MARS model outperformed the other design equations in literature. The proposed models yield an R² = 0.94 and RMSE = 0.18 kN, this represented a 65.5% improvement in RMSE and 88% improvement in term of R² in respect to AS 3600-2009 equation as the best equation among the othr design equations.
