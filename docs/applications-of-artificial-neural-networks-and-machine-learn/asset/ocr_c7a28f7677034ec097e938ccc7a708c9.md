# 13.4.2 Example 2: Three-Bay, Three-Story Frame

The second example, as illustrated in Fig. 13.3, is more complicated than Example 1. In this example, the collapse mechanism and load factor of a three-story, three-bay frame are investigated.

Similar to example 1, the comparison of the real mechanism and the obtained one is presented (Fig. 13.4). The collapse load factors for both methods are equal to 1.6 however, the efficiency of the proposed method is about 0.007 s in comparison to 1501.44 s of the evolutionary methods.

Due to the similarity of the collapse mechanisms and load factors of the evolutionary and the proposed method and the obvious differences between their computational costs, it is possible to claim that the new proposed algorithm is considerable, either in accuracy or efficiency.
