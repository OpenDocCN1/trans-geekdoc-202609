# 14.7 Concluding Remarks

A hybrid Graph-Neural-Genetic method is presented for nodal ordering of the graph models of FEMs resulting in their nodal ordering with small Frms for the corresponding equations. This method selects the coefficients of the graph parameters for priority function in a systematic manner. The potentials of graph theory, artificial neural networks and the genetic algorithm are incorporated in a simple ordering algorithm. Future progress in neural networks and genetic algorithms will enhance of the present approach.
