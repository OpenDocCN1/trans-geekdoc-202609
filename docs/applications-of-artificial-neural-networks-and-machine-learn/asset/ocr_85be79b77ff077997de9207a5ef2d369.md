# 16.2 Damage Index

In order to quantitatively estimate the seismic damage of the structural buildings, an appropriate damage assessment method is needed. Among different existing damage indices that have been proposed by researchers, the Park and Ang [13] damage index is selected due to its frequent use in the literature, for evaluating the seismic damage imparted to the concrete MRF structures.
