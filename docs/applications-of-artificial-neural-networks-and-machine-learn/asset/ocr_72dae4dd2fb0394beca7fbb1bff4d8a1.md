# Preface

In today's world, which has witnessed unprecedented advances in technology and computer science, artificial intelligence has emerged as a top field captivating global attention. Often referred to as AI, this technology stands apart from other disciplines as it aims to design machines and systems that exhibit intelligence, learn autonomously, and make decisions akin to humans. To comprehend the impact of this innovation, one must delve into the workings of artificial intelligence, trace its historical evolution from inception to present day, and explore its diverse applications in domains like medicine, transportation, broadcasting, and marketing. Artificial intelligence introduces a transformative element to our reality, fostering significant breakthroughs and innovations.

The aim of the present book is to provide different applications of Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) in various problems of material science, structural optimization, and optimal analysis in 22 chapters. In Chap. 1, the main definitions, concepts, and objectives of the present book are provided. Chapter 2 introduces buckling resistance prediction of high-strength steel columns using metaheuristic-trained artificial neural networks. Chapter 3 provides the use of artificial neural networks and metaheuristic algorithms to optimize the compressive strength of concrete followed by Chap. 4, where efficient neural networks are trained for design of double-layer grids. Analysis of double-layer barrel vaults using different neural networks is presented in Chap. 5. In Chap. 6, the BP and RBF neural networks for predicting displacements and design of Schwedler domes. In Chap. 7, a neurocomputing strategy is presented which combines data processing capabilities of neural networks and numerical structural optimization. In Chap. 8, the applications of two artificial neural networks, namely, the backpropagation neural net and the improved counter propagation neural net are described for the analysis and design of large-scale space structures. A hybrid enhanced colliding bodies optimization and artificial neural network algorithm is presented for shear strength of partially grouted masonry walls in Chap. 9. Shape optimization of arch dams with frequency constraints by enhanced charged system search algorithm and neural network is performed in Chap. 10.
