Wavefront reduction using graphs, neural networks, and genetic algorithm is discussed in Chap. 14. Optimal design of transmission towers using genetic algorithm and neural networks is the subject of Chap. 15. Estimating the vulnerability of the concrete moment resisting frame structures using artificial neural networks is presented in Chap. 16. In subsequent Chap. 17, a hybrid graph-neural method is presented for domain decomposition. GMDH-based prediction of shear strength of FRP-RC beams with and without stirrups is discussed in Chap. 18. Efficient training of two ANNs using four metaheuristic algorithms for predicting the FRP Strength is provided in Chap. 19. New predictive models for prediction of bond strength between FRP reinforcements externally glued on masonry units are provided in Chap. 20. Kernel extreme learning machine application in prediction of bond strength between EBR FRP and concrete substrate is discussed in Chap. 21. Development of predictive models for shear strength of HSC slender beams without web reinforcement using machine-learning-based techniques is the subject matter of Chap. 22.
