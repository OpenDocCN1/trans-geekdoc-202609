**Example 3** In this example a 72-bar space truss is considered as shown in Fig. 15.6. Details of this structure is provided in Table 15.7. The aim is to optimize once the cross sections of the members, and then to optimize the sizing of the cross sections and the geometry simultaneously. Considering the dimension of the problem, here the second method is employed. The number of subpopulations is taken as 4 and the number of each generation is selected as 30. In this problem, using the second method, only 14% of the total number of analyses are performed directly by the analysis program and the remaining are obtained from the neural network. The results of the sizing optimization are compared to the other references in Table 15.8.
