It can easily be seen that selecting a larger domain of search causes a reduction in the accuracy of the trained ANNs which in turn affects the results of neuro- optimizer. However, the associated error of the objective function for training pairs above 200 is less than 7.1%. When the number of design variables is high, it is appropriate to increase the domain of search and evaluate the response of the neuro-optimizer and then refine the results by decreasing the domain of search about the design variables. Furthermore, the error can be reduced either by increasing the number of training pairs in the process of training the ANNs or by selecting a smaller value for the Kohonen units classifier, D, which results in larger size ANNs.
