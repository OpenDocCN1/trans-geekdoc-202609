# 1.6.20 Generative Adversarial Networks

Generative adversarial networks are a large family of binary networks built from Generator and Discriminator. The generator is responsible for generating synthetic data. In fact, its job is to generate new data that looks real and is similar to real data. The discriminator plays a major role in data evaluation. This network tries to distinguish between the data generated by the generator network and the real data. The training goals of this network is to be able to distinguish between two types of data.

The main goal in adversarial generative networks is to program the generative network to produce data that looks real. This process gradually improves the quality of the data produced by the generator network. This concept was proposed by Goodfellow and others and is known as one of the most widely used and effective methods in the field of artificial intelligence.
