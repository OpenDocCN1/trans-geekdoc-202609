# 7.6 Concluding Remarks

A neurocomputing strategy is presented for structural optimization. In spite of the fact that neurocomputing modeling belongs more to a class of qualitative than quantitative analysis tools, satisfactory accuracy of response predictions is obtained. An improved CPN is implemented, and two ANNs are trained based on this neural net, one for constraints and the other for gradients.
