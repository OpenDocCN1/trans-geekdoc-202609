# 1.2 Types of Artificial Intelligence

# 1.2.1 Evolutionary Computation

Evolutionary computing (EC) is a subfield of artificial intelligence that uses an iterative process, often inspired by biological mechanisms of evolution, to evolve a population of solutions to achieve a desired goal. EC has been applied in the field of civil engineering for decades, mainly serving as an effective method for solving complex optimization problems [3].

# 1.2.2 Swarm Intelligence

A meta-initiative based on swarm intelligence, which simulates a population of simple individuals that evolve their solutions by interacting with each other and with the environment, has shown promising performance on many difficult problems and in recent years, they have become a very active research field [3].
