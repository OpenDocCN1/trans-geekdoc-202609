# 1.2.3 Fuzzy Systems

The term “fuzzy” means vague and unclear. In normal life, we sometimes face situations where we do not know which is the right or wrong decision and the correct action is hidden from our eyes. At this point, “fuzzy logic” offers a unique and valuable proposition. In this way, the amount of uncertainty can be determined for each situation. For this reason, fuzzy logic is sometimes called doubtful logic because its results are created with doubt. Fuzzy logic has helped to solve many decision- making problems today, so that most of the time, it produces the best decision based on the inputs. Fuzzy logic is based on human decision-making and can be seen as a development of “Aristotelian logic” or “Boolean logic”. Today, based on fuzzy logic, “fuzzy numbers”, “fuzzy calculations” and even fuzzy statistical methods such as fuzzy clustering have been created. Devices and computers have been produced that have far better performance than similar devices with Boolean logic. The concept and study of fuzzy logic began in 1920, but the term fuzzy logic was first used by Professor Lotfi Asgarzadeh (1921–2017) in 1965 at Berkeley University.

# 1.2.4 Expert System

An expert system relies on human experts based on the existing knowledge of the startup knowledge system. The expert system creates the first and most effective system in the field of artificial intelligence research. The expert system is widely used in roads and bridges, construction engineering, geotechnical engineering, underground engineering, disaster prevention projects, materials engineering, geological exploration and petrochemical industries, etc. A system expert is in a specific domain and has relevant knowledge and experience in system programming. The expert system simulates the human expert’s problem-solving thinking process and reaches or approaches the level of experts.

# 1.2.5 Chaos Theory

Chaos theory is a branch of mathematics that investigates the behavior of dynamic systems that are very sensitive to initial conditions. Chaos is an interdisciplinary theory that states: in the randomness of complex chaotic systems, there are basic patterns, constant feedback loops, repetition, self-similarity, fractals, and self- organization. According to the definition, the word Chaos means “a state of complete confusion and disorder; complete lack of organization or order”. Chaos theory is an interesting contrast. The science of predicting the behavior of “inherently unpredictable” systems. In fact, chaos theory is a mathematical tool that allows us to derive beautiful structures from chaos.
