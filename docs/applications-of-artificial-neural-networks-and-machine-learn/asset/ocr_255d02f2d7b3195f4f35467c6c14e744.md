In Ref. [25] this problem is studied once again, however, a different code of practice is employed. The volume of the tower is obtained as 5.41e5 mm$^3$ in Ref. [24]. Here we want to vary the geometry as well as the cross sections. For simplicity considering the form of the tower from construction point of view, we assume the node 29 to be able to move in X and Y directions. The amount of these displacements are considered to be identical in both directions, and the coordinates are limited to 700–1300. The coordinates of the node 77 are limited to 2000–5000. In order to preserve the symmetry, the displacements of the nodes 8, 27 and 26 are specified with respect to those of node 29. The displacements of nodes 76, 75, 74 with respect to 77 are considered such that the symmetry is preserved. The remaining nodes move proportional to the position of these nodes in such a manner that the general form of the tower does not change. As an example, the direction of the nodes 29 and 27 and the corresponding nodes should stay on one straight line.
