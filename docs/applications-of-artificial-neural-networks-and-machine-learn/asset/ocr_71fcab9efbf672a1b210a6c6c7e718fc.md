This study aims to develop machine learning-based models that can predict the ultimate strength of I- and H-shaped sections under compressive loading with consideration of initial imperfection accurately and reliably. Four ML methods, namely Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO), Colliding Body Optimization (CBO), and their developed version by applying Genetic Algorithm (GA), Particle Swarm Optimization–Genetic Algorithm (PSO-GA), and Colliding Body Optimization–Genetic Algorithm (CBO-GA), are implemented in this study to train ANNs efficiently. Firstly, a finite element method (FEM) model was created using ABAQUS software for proof of concept in FEM Modeling; then, a database consisting of 114 FEM specimens was created, and their statistical characteristics were analyzed. Secondly, the fundamentals of the four adopted ML methods were briefly presented to illustrate how they solve complex regression problems. Thirdly, a model training procedure was implemented to obtain the optimized and trained models. Finally, the performance of the trained ML models was evaluated and compared.

# ## 2.2 Numerical Modeling and Verification

The development of a numerical model was conducted using the finite element software ABAQUS (Version 2019-22.11.51) in order to confirm the viability and reliability of the FEM models used in this study compared with the experimental data provided by Tankova et al. In order to have a consistent support behavior and to create a more realistic simulation, an initial imperfection was implemented in the model.
