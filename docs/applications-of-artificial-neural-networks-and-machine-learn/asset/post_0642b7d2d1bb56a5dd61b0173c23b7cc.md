## 15.5 Concluding Remarks

Optimization of transmission towers plays an important role in the cost of transferring the electrical power and its distribution plays due to high number of such towers. These structures perform similar to space trusses under three types of vertical, horizontal and longitudinal loadings. The genetic algorithm used for optimization is a migration GA. Here, the energy method is combined with the force method to reduce the design unknowns and also to remove the need for the operation corresponding to matrix inversion. In towers with small number of elements this algorithm can easily

![](img/4048998719320644ee88964326fd7995_316_0.png)

Fig. 15.9 The geometry of a 244-bar transmission tower and its nodal numbering

be employed, however, in the towers encountered in practice which contain many elements, this reduces the speed of the computation. Employing neural networks, part of the computational load is devoted to the network. The examples presented in this chapter show the important role of the neural networks in reducing the computational time. Similar method can be developed for frame structures where the formation of efficient statical basis is feasible [26].
