Using information from the arterial biology of people with chronic hepatitis C, an AI model was built for predicting the outcomes of interferon-ribavirin, a combination treatment; 48,940 B-mode US pictures from chronic patients with hepatitis C were used as training data for four machine learning models, which resulted in the creation of an innovative AI model that predicted the capacity of direct-acting antiviral medicines to treat patients.

To anticipate issues caused by liver cirrhosis, AI may be applied [17]. It used laboratory results and US as training data for AI models to distinguish between different incidences based on pressure changes between the vein known as the portal and hepatitis vein. It created a neural network prediction model that took into account the level of platelets, lymphoma, and blood vessel width to forecast the emergence of varices [18]. With the use of clinical data from 3,972 individuals, AI was able to predict the development of esophageal varices in patients with liver cirrhosis brought on by hepatitis C virus (HCV) with a 68.9% accuracy.
