# 2.1 INTRODUCTION

Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to a method for teaching a computer, a computer-controlled machine, or software with the facility to think in the same manner as intelligent people do. AI is achieved by first researching in what the human intelligence works, as well as what method people learn, choose, and function while trying to resolve a problem, besides applying the outcomes towards the growth of intelligent software as structures. AI is a science and technology that combines disciplines such as computer science, biochemistry, neuroscience, linguistics, mathematics and engineering to produce intelligent machines. The advances in computer capabilities related to human cognition, such as thinking, learning, and problem-solving, is a primary focus of AI.

One or more of the key areas may contribute to the development of an integrated system. The applications of AI include entertainment, natural language processing (NLP), decision support systems, imaging systems, voice recognition, detection, and autonomous robots as shown in Figure 2.1. The inspiration for artificial neural networks comes from human intelligence’s neural networks. The human brain is very intricate. Scientists and engineers studied the brain care-fully and devised an architecture that would work in the digital world of binary computers. Figure 2.2 depicts such typical building.
