# 6.2 AI APPLICATIONS IN CYBERSECURITY

AI cybersecurity systems can deliver superior real-time detection and response for recognized and new threats, reduce time in cyber defense, and produce novel solutions beyond the capability of present humans. These capabilities are made possible through automation and machine learning. Tools based on generative AI alter the method in which cyber threats are designed and carried out. Because these models can generate text and speech that sounds like humans, they can be used to automate the production of malicious content, such as social engineering attacks, phishing emails, and other forms of harmful content.
