The conventional methods of cybersecurity, such as rule-based systems and signature-based detection, are no longer adequate to tackle the complexity and breadth of new threats since they are reliant on patterns and signatures. As a direct consequence of this, there is an increasing awareness of the importance of utilizing AI in the process of strengthening cybersecurity. This chapter explored the many facets of AI's role in cybersecurity and emphasized its potential to revolutionize how we protect our digital environment.
