## DISCUSSION

The results of the research are quite interesting, as we explored the effectiveness of various ML algorithms in a balanced dataset. The use of voting classifier is commendable, as it allows for ensemble approach to decision-making, which can improve accuracy. The incorporation of undersampling, oversampling, and SMOTE techniques is also a good practice, as it ensures the dataset used for training is representative of the actual distribution. This approach helps mitigate class imbalance, a common challenge in many ML applications. Notably, this study found that the extra classifier led to the highest accuracy. This shows that adding classifiers could increase the performance of ML models in real-world applications. Overall, this underscores the importance of carefully selecting ML algorithms and ensuring balanced datasets.

## CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK

Using various supervised classifiers, we attempted to provide an accurate diagnosis for patients with early detection of liver disease. After careful analysis of patient information and implementing algorithms, we found that the extra classifier achieved the highest accuracy score, followed closely by CatBoost. We also tested voting classifier of AdaBoost, extra tree, and CatBoost, which demonstrated its effectiveness in improving overall performance. Our work has significant implications for healthcare development, particularly in low-income countries where access to medical resources is limited. Moving forward, there is still scope for further research in this area, including additional algorithms and refining the prediction model to improve accuracy. Overall, this work represents an important step toward developing a more reliable and effective diagnostic tool for liver disease.
