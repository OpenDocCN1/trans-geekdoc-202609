The laboratory setting limits complicates gene testing to detect medication resistance or infection in the pathogen Mycobacterium. TB is an infectious disease that transmits from one to person another directly and is a leading cause of morbidity and death around the globe. Anti-TB medications can effectively treat TB patients, and the drug regimen, dosage, and length of the treatment period depend on the exact state of the infection in the body, whether it is a drug-resistant strain or not, and any coexisting conditions. The majority of anti-TB drugs may be harmful to the liver and cause hepatitis as a side effect.
