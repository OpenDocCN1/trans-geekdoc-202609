# 10.5.1 AI in diabetes disease

Whether using ML or DL, there are several ways to create algorithms to recognize the patterns of diabetes. ML is a technique in which people train computers to gather patterns of characteristics from data to a particular task. Only the characteristics that the machine has been taught to recognize may be detected using this method. As ML-based systems can predict when trained levels of diabetes retinopathy (DR) will occur [8]. Conversely, DL is a type of learning in which an algorithm is developed to recognize the best predictive qualities from sizable datasets that have been labeled with specific details. Artificial neural networks (ANNs) are the foundation of deep learning, which mimics how the human brain operates and discovers directly from data. # 10.5.2 Future AI prediction in diabetes

Prediction and risk stratification may the fourth category of AI applications in the diagnosis and management of diabetes. In preemptive medicine, this group may be used to precisely identify members of the general public who are extremely likely to develop a certain disease at the pre-illness stage [8]. Thus, by setting up health care for these individuals at a very early stage, this technique might ultimately eradicate the incidence of diabetes.
