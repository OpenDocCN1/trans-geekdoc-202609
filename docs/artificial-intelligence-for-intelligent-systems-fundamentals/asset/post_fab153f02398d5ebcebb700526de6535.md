## 4.1 INTRODUCTION

An enormous increase in data collection is observed around the world. This flood of data, also known as big data, has forced the development of more robust methods for analysis. The purpose of this chapter is to shed light on the importance of artificial intelligence (AI) in data mining and distributed processing. Following are the ways of AI in big data and distributed processing.

### 4.1.1 Applications of artificial intelligence in big data

The integration of AI and big data has dramatically altered how businesses analyze and visualize data. Distributed processing methods are already being revolutionized by AI, and this trend will only accelerate as more businesses adopt AI-driven strategies.

### 4.1.2 Improving the effectiveness of data processing

Today's data present significant problems for conventional processing techniques due to its sheer amount, variation, and rapidity. However, AI algorithms can automate and speed up several steps in the data processing pipeline [1]. Pre-processing and cleaning data using AI techniques like machine learning (ML) and natural language processing (NLP) can reduce noise and improve data quality. In addition, AI algorithms allow for effective indexing and querying, which leads to quicker and more precise retrieval.

### 4.1.3 Making possible cutting-edge analytics

The goal of "big data mining" is to discover actionable insights hidden in massive datasets. AI algorithms excel at managing intricate data processing jobs, which opens new avenues for enterprises [2]. For instance, ML algorithms can quickly and accurately identify trends and outliers, forecast results, and recommend next steps. Analytics powered by AI allows businesses to make evidence-based decisions, enhance existing operations, and identify untapped opportunities.

### 4.1.4 Enabling on-demand information processing

Real-time data processing is essential for many applications in the age of quickly changing data streams, such as banking, health care, and internet of Things (IoT). The processing of massive amounts of data relies on AI methods like stream mining and deep learning (DL). With the help of these algorithms, businesses will be able to detect and respond to unusual or unexpected patterns in data real time. Proactive decision-making, minimizing hazards, and productivity are all aided by AI-powered real-time data processing [3].

### 4.1.5 Processing in a distributed manner

Robust mechanisms for processing and interpreting data in parallel are necessary since big data is commonly stored across multiple systems. Distributed computing frameworks and parallel processing algorithms are examples of AI technologies that improve the scalability and performance of data-intensive applications. With AI, businesses may use distributed computing to efficiently process big datasets by splitting up processing duties among several nodes or clusters [4].

### 4.1.6 Data extraction and visualization automation

Extraction and representation of data manually can be laborious and prone to mistakes. Extraction made possible by AI methods would significantly improve the efficiency with both structured and unstructured data may be extracted from a wide variety of sources [5].
