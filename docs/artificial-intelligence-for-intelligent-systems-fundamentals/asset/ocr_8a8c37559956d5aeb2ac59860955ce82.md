ResNet-50, DenseNet-121, and VGG19 are models that have not previously been employed as part of an ensemble model. When merging various basic learning models for final MRI image classification, the usefulness of the ensemble approaches, as majority voting and stacking, is investigated for their efficiency. Regard correction rates for Alzheimer's detection, the ensemble classifier produces the best results, with the precision of the proposed model being 96%, recall being 95%, F-score being 94%, and overall accuracy of the model being 90.15%. The results also show that the ensemble classifier outperforms the single learning model by a margin, suggesting that the proposed approach is both effective and efficient. In the future, we plan to incorporate multimodalities into the ensemble model, as well as improve the overall performance of the model.
