Earlier, we calculate the weighted sum $\Sigma a_j w_j$ of the inputs $a_j$ and the weights $w_j$ (also known as an activation value); $a_j$ represents either the input data values or the outputs of other neurons (i.e., if the neuron is part of a neural network), $w_j$ indicates the strength of the inputs or, alternatively, the strength of connections among neurons in the network.
