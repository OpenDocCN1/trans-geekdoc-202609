# I.I.1 Brief overview of artificial intelligence and its capabilities

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the emulation of human intellect in computers that are designed to carry out tasks like learning, decision-making, problem-solving and that normally need human intelligence. It's a vast field that includes a number of subfields, including robotics, computer vision, natural language processing, and machine learning.

Figure 1.1 illustrates the relationships between AI and their specific applications, providing a clear overview of the diverse capabilities of AI. The graph structure and node styles are used to represent the hierarchy and associations within the AI field, aiding in understanding the various domains and applications encompassed by AI. Recent years have witnessed tremendous progress in processing power, the availability of big datasets, and innovations in algorithmic techniques. Machine learning, which enables systems to learn from data and improve performance over time, is one of the core skills of AI. Large datasets can be analyzed by machine learning algorithms to find trends, forecast outcomes, and reveal new information. Deep learning, a branch of machine learning that uses neural networks to teach them to recognize hierarchical data representations, is one example. In domains like speech recognition, picture identification, and natural language processing, deep learning has shown impressive performance [1]. Another important feature that focuses on the communication between computers and human languages is natural language processing, or NLP. Machines can now comprehend, interpret, and produce human language to NLP approaches. NLP is used in a variety of contexts, including sentiment analysis tools, language translation systems, and virtual assistants like Siri and Alexa [2].
