# 10.9.2 Future detection in skin disease

Recently, dermatological AI has advanced quickly, but there are still a number of pressing issues that need to be resolved. These include the following. First, the volume of data now available on skin diseases is still minimal, hospitals do not share much information with one another, and skin imaging standards and quality vary. High-quality picture data is difficult to obtain, which makes study findings suspect. Second, it is difficult to find people with complicated AI and medical skills together. Close collaboration with multidisciplinary experts in computer science, biomedicine, and medicine is essential. Dermatology deals with a wide variety of illnesses.
