## 2.5 CONCLUSION AND FUTURE WORK

The present study utilized machine learning algorithms to accurately diagnose hepatitis C. We examined and evaluated different models' classification accuracy, precision, and recall. By analyzing relevant scholarly papers and comparing them with other algorithms, we determined the most effective methods for diagnosing hepatitis C. The empirical results demonstrated that machine learning significantly enhances hepatitis C diagnosis. Specifically, the extra trees model achieved an impressive accuracy rate of 99% after implementing undersampling. These remarkable results prove that these models can diagnose hepatitis C accurately.

It is crucial to note that machine learning in medicine offers several advantages for detecting the global health threat posed by hepatitis C. The high accuracy levels achieved by these models enable prevention and ultimately improve patient outcomes. This study contributes substantially to the growing research on machine learning in healthcare. The authors emphasize the significance of advanced computational methods for diagnosis. These findings underscore the importance of continuous research and development in improving the accuracy and efficacy of diagnostic technologies. Overall, this study demonstrates that machine learning techniques can effectively improve diagnosis in future studies, investigating novel features and data sources, such as genetic data and patient demographics, which could yield significant findings for developing more comprehensive diagnostic models. Furthermore, implementing more in-depth studies including diverse and representative datasets can yield a better understanding of the versatility of models and their practical significance in real-world scenarios.
