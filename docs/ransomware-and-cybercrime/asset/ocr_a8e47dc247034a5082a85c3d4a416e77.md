So back to Deterrence Theory: many experts have applied the theoretical framework of deterrence theory to cyberspace as cyber deterrence. While both typical warfare and cyberwarfare share some characteristics, such as the offensive advantage, given the difficulty and costs of defence, significant differences exist.

The emergence of deterrence theory in the military dates to the 1920s/30s when the first flight bombers were considered unstoppable by defensive measures. Then, strategists thought that large-scale attacks on one’s cities could only be prevented if the other side feared (were deterred) counterattacks of similar or even greater magnitude.
