# Foreword

My name is Dr Vladas Leonas, I am an Adjunct Professor at Torrens University and I have over 45 years of experience in technology and over 20 years of experience in cybersecurity. I have often been concerned and even frustrated by the lack of understanding and knowledge, and therefore the lack of action to secure corporate businesses and even government entities, and, in my own way, have been vocal to get my views across.

I met the author, Andy Jenkinson about 2 years ago and immediately identified with his position, with what he stood for and the way he tried to simplify the messages and oversights. Andy is unquestionably a thought leader that not only Talks the Talk with unbridled volume, but like no one else as I have witnessed, Walks the Walk.

I took great pleasure in reviewing Andy’s first book, “Stuxnet to Sunburst” and even with all my years of experience there was a plethora of information that I was unaware of and that resonated with me. When Andy asked me to write a foreword for his second book Ransomware & Cybercrime, I was delighted to do so.
