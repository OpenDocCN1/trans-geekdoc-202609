The FCA is a financial regulatory body that operates independently of the UK government and is financed by charging fees to members of the Financial Services Industry. The FCA regulates financial firms providing services to consumers and is responsible for maintaining the integrity of the UK’s financial market. The FCA was formed on 1 April 2013 after being the FSA previously. The FCA, Prudential Regulator, and Bank of England are intrinsically linked and work closely together to ensure monetary policies, financial stability, and regulations are upheld.
