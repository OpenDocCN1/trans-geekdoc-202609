When the first taxis came into being in the early 1900s, anyone could become a taxi driver, such as ex-convicts and people with questionable habits and traits. It was not until 1907, when Harry N. At that time, only the well-heeled could afford taxis; however, Allen’s new taxis ceased opaque charging to make taxis more affordable.
