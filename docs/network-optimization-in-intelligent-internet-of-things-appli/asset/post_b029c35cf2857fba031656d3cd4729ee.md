# 5.1.6 INTEGRATION OF IOT WITH AUTOMATED VEHICLES

IoT paves the road for AVs by enabling enhanced transportation efficiency, a greater driving experience, and advanced vehicle management capabilities. This will examine how automotive IoT solutions will bring automobiles up to date.

By 2025, 470 million linked vehicles will be on highways all across the world, according to a Deloitte study. Using cloud-connected telematics systems, automotive IoT solutions can contribute to safer roadways. The stakeholders may monitor their fleets in real time and receive alerts regarding potential safety hazards, from fleet management to vehicle diagnostics [9].

Automobile IoT technologies also enable vehicles to communicate with their surroundings. This includes automated smart parking systems that use cameras and sensors to detect vacant spaces and lane-centering driving systems.

The Internet of Things is crucial for the market forecast for connected vehicles. Market research Gartner projects that in 2020, IoT goods and services suppliers will increase their revenue by more than $300 billion [11]. - Wi-Fi capabilities using 3G/4G/5G technology
- Car-to-car communication
- Vehicle information system
