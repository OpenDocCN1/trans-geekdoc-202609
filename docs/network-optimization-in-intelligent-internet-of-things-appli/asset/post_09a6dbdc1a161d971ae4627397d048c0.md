
The following open issues and future research directions are noted regarding green IoT:

- Designing a green Internet of Things should be done while considering how much energy the entire system consumes, as long as service goals are met and performance standards are met (QE).
- Acknowledging the features of distinct IoT users and the facilities de-rendered them essential.
- Real power usage modelling of IoT system elements is rendered (such as WSN, core networking, embedded circuit, and CC).
- Well, with the ubiquitous use of sensors, it is possible to envision a standardised sensor as a service (SNaaS), wherein users get and management over their personal IoT.
- Within the sense of SNaaS, looking into energy-saving system architecture, energy-saving service composition techniques, circumstance and situational consciousness relating to consumers and devices (such as learning and predicting), power-efficient WSN governance, and power-cloud monitoring is crucial.
