# 3.5.1 GNSS-BASED TOLLING SYSTEM

GNSS stands for Global Navigation Satellite System. India has a functional NETC system known as FASTag. This system was provided by the Indian Highways Management Company Ltd. (HMCL) and is now in use. RFID technology, with more 720 electronic toll plazas located nationwide, and On-Board-Units (OBU) installed in cars are the system's foundation. FASTag was first released in 2016, and its usage mandatory in February 2021. By March 2021, the number of users reached 30 million. At nationwide electronic toll plazas, drivers without most automobiles in India use FASTag to pay their tolls. Wait, drivers without most automobiles in India use FASTag to pay their tolls.

# 3.5.1.1 Working of GNSS-based Tolling System

Numerous different uses are possible for GNSS. The amount of dependability that is required can be used to classify these applications into one of the three categories [11]:

+   - Deemed secure: Applications are deemed secure when an inaccuracy has the possibility of causing bodily damage or fatalities. Applying GNSS-based solutions in air transport is a notable example of the application of this subject.
- Responsibility: Mistakes in responsibility systems can have severe economic and legal repercussions. One example is the use of GNSS in tolling systems, where failure would not cause any actual injury but would result in financial damages.
- No liability: This module includes apps that do not fit into the other groups. For instance, the use of GNSS for navigational reasons in the modern day is included in this category.
