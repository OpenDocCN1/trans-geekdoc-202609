When a miner discovers a nonce, selected randomly, that results in the hash of the complete block falling below the specified nBits, the miner shares the block with the entire network. Once other miners authenticate and approve the block, it becomes the most recent addition to the blockchain. Subsequently, the mining process proceeds for the subsequent block.
