Deep learning (DL) approaches the potential to accurately forecast incoming assaults that have not been recognized by capitalizing on the information gained from previous cases. This capacity to predict future assaults is especially significant since new attacks often take the form of modifications of existing ones. It is vital to replace secure device communication with security-based intelligence made possible by DL methods to guarantee the efficacy and safety of IoT systems.
