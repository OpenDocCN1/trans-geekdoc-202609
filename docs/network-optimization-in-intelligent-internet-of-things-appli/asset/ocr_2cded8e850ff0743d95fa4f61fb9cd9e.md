Pheromone trails are updated once all ants have built their solutions. While fresh pheromone trails are deposited dependent on the caliber of the solutions discovered by the ants, the current pheromone trails progressively disappear to prevent premature convergence. The algorithm iterates until a termination condition is satisfied, which could be reached after a predetermined number of iterations or by arriving at a satisfactory solution. # 7.3.4 NEURAL NETWORKS

There are several ways that neural networks can be applied to enhance the functionality of IoT systems. First, predictive maintenance of IoT devices using neural networks can help decrease downtime and increase overall reliability. Neural networks can forecast when a device is likely to fail and plan repair before the failure occurs by analyzing data. In IoT systems, neural networks can also be used to optimize energy utilization. Neural networks can find ways to minimize energy usage by looking for patterns in energy consumption data. This can aid in lowering running expenses and enhancing the long-term viability of IoT systems. The detection of anomalies is another use for neural networks which can find unusual patterns that might point to a security breach or other system issue by examining data from various sensors. Data breaches and other security events may be avoided as a result. In IoT networks, neural networks can forecast traffic flows and manage network resources by evaluating past traffic flows, helping to avoid congestion and guarantee the network's smooth operation.
