# 18.2 LITERATURE REVIEW

Wi-Fi jammers are devices that disrupt or block communication signals, including Wi-Fi networks. They operate by emitting radio frequency signals in the same frequency band as the Wi-Fi signal, causing interference and preventing devices within range from connecting to the network.
