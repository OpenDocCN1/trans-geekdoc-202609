# 3.3.1.3 Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR)

ANPR, also known as Automatic Number Plate Recognition, is an image processing technology used to capture images and extract license plate information from them. This technology converts the extracted license plate into machine-readable formats, such as text strings, making it easier to process and store in a database. ANPR is widely used for various purposes, including vehicle identification, monitoring, and enforcement. By automating the recognition and tracking of license plate data, ANPR technology originated in 1976 when it was invented by the UK police. The first practical implementation of ANPR was a pilot system around 1979, specifically for monitoring traffic. Initially, ANPR was primarily used in policing and security applications. However, over time, it has progressed and changed along with other Information and Communications Technologies (ICTs). As a result, there has been a significant surge in new methods and groundbreaking applications of ANPR across various research fields.
