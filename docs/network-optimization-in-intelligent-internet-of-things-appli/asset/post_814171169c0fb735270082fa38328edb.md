We have also proposed a novel architecture that provides data confidentiality and authentication to the IoT communication framework from cyberattacks. However, many recent cyberattacks have also appeared with the growth of LWC techniques. In addition, LWC techniques are based on mathematical structures that are not secure with futuristic computers.
