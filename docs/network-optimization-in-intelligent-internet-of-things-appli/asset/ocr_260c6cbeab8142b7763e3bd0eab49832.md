# 9.1.1 Healthcare 4.0 AND INDUSTRY 4.0

Healthcare 1.0–4.0 follows industry 1.0–4.0. Healthcare 1.0 increased quickly between 1970 and 1990 to increase productivity by automating and minimizing paperwork. Healthcare 2.0, introduced around 2000, overcomes Healthcare 1.0’s limitations of standalone systems by integrating health IT systems and clinical imaging integration to produce health data. Between 2006 and 2015, Healthcare 3.0 emerged with electronic health records (EHRs), wearables, and implants, where EHRs enabled genetic data storage [8, 9].
