# 2.9.1 RELIABILITY AND CONFIDENTIALITY OF 6G FOR INTELLIGENT IoT

6G technology incorporation into IoT networks offers increased dependability, extremely low latency, and broad coverage. Security issues are brought about by the wide variety of IoT devices and access techniques, including higher attack risks during technology transfers.
