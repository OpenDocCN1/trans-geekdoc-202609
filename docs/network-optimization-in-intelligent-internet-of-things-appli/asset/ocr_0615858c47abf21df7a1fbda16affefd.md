# ## 18. INTRODUCTION

A Wi-Fi jammer is a device that interferes with transmissions to prevent communication between devices and routers [1]. It sends deauthentication packets to the router, resulting in the connection loss between the router and connected devices. Jammers are undetectable, causing users to encounter side effects like poor signal coverage. Wi-Fi jammer is a device that can disconnect all devices connected to the Wi-Fi IoT devices within a particular area for a specific time. Wi-Fi jammers can be seen as a threat to IoT. Hackers can easily use them to interfere with the connection between the user and the Internet, accessing the user's personal data and information [2]. For this reason, using jammers has been limited to certain sections of society. Jammers are also banned in some countries, and safety measures are taken.

The application of jamming devices was first seen in the armed forces and military-technical devices [3]. This fundamental goal was to prevent the successful transmission of information from the receiver to the sender and vice versa. Wi-Fi jammers have been used by organizations to prevent the leak of official data [3]. However, the high cost of Wi-Fi jammers makes it difficult for some organizations and individuals to use and buy them. Many countries have banned the production, sale, and use of jammers as they are also perceived as a threat to civilians for leaving private data. One of the repercussions of jammers is that they can refrain first responders or civilians from calling for help.

A plethora of data transmissions occur daily, and they need to be secured from security breaches such as data theft. The motivation for this work is to prevent the leakage of official data at places such as the armored.

Advanced jammers can disable specific channels while leaving the frequencies used for communications unaffected in case of emergency [4].
