# 2.1 INTRODUCTION

The wireless communication landscape is undergoing an extraordinary transformation, teetering on the cusp of ushering in a new era of limitless possibilities and unparalleled connectivity. Within this rapidly evolving milieu, the concept of the sixth-generation (6G) wireless communication networks emerges as a visionary leap poised to redefine the landscape of customer services and applications through the transformative lens of the Internet of Things (IoT). The fusion of 6G and IoT is a beacon of enormous promise, offering us to lead towards a future inhabited by fully intelligent and autonomous systems as our linked world continues to weave a complex tapestry of innovation. In this chapter, we embark on an immersive journey into the dynamic crossroads where 6G and IoT intersect, delving into the fertile grounds of emerging opportunities that this symbiotic relationship brings to the forefront. From a comprehensive survey, we will illuminate the foundational technologies, exploring their catalytic role in empowering the IoT ecosystem. From the dawn of edge intelligence and the reconvis of intelligent surfaces to the boundless realms of space-air-ground-under communications, terahertz networks, and the bedrock of ultra-reliable and low-latency communications, we will carefully unveil the intricate tapestry of 6G technologies that are poised to shape the trajectory of future IoT networks and applications. What sets this chapter apart is its departure from surface-level discussions. We plunge into the heart of IoT domains, where the impact of 6G's integration is set to reverberate most profoundly. We venture into the domains of Healthcare Internet of Things, Vehicle Internet of Things and Autonomous driving, Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), Satellite Internet of Things, and Industrial Internet of Things (IoT). By doing so, we unveil the transformative role that 6G is poised to play, envisioning the metamorphosis it will facilitate for each facet of our interconnected world. As we navigate this uncharted terrain, facing challenges that beck innovative solutions.
