# ## 14.4 BLOCKCHAIN

# ### 14.4.1 OVERVIEW

Blockchain technology (BCT) was first proposed by Satoshi Nakamoto [4]. In recent years, blockchain technology has gained momentum as a rising innovation in academic and industrial spheres. Blockchain provides common distributed immutable ledger technology [12]. Blockchain technology, without the involvement of the central trusted party, can record and verify transactions between participants. To overcome the disadvantage of centralized databases, decentralized architectures are used for peer-to-peer (P2P) communications among the nodes. Within blockchain technology, a ledger utilizes public cryptography to uphold an expanding series of information blocks. Every individual block within a blockchain is composed of a sequence of blocks, each holding a timestamp, random number, and certain transactions from the preceding block. The block is appended to the blockchain by the mining process. The miners performing the mining process add the new block to the blockchain. To create a block, designated nodes may engage in solving a computationally intensive cryptographic challenge called proof of work (PoW). The miner that successfully solves this challenge is responsible for adding the new block to the blockchain.

The new block is added by securing SHA 256 [12]. Based on the data from its parent block, the hash of the parent block is connected and retained within the child block. The hash value is generated according to the content of the block; any changes in the block will change its hash value.
