## 6.1.4 GREEN INTERNET OF THINGS

In terms of creating a green IoT and intelligent society, energy usage becomes state-of-the-art. Power savings represent the IoT to minimize (MIN) the greenhouse gases (GHG) and carbon dioxide (CO₂) pollution of sensing, devices, apps, and activities to create a SUST TECH world.
