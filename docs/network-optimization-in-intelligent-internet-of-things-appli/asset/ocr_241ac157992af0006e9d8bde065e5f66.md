A gateway device is essential to the healthcare industry's ability to integrate and communicate with multiple IoT systems and devices while utilising EC capabilities. The gateway device serves as a focal point for integrating and linking IoT devices into the healthcare ecosystem. It supports various communication protocols and standards, enabling the connection between gadgets, including wearables, monitoring equipment, and smart medical equipment. The gateway device is also used to gather data in real time from various IoT systems, sensors, and devices. By filtering and preparing data at the edge, it minimizes the quantity of information that must be sent to the cloud or central servers. This less latency while maximising network bandwidth.
