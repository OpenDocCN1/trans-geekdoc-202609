Many investigators have conducted surveys on IoT security to give a clear picture of its weaknesses and lay the groundwork for further inquiry. Most IoT security assessments have left DL applications out in the cold. The studies examined network security, application security, access control, authentication, and other security-related issues in IoT systems.

Tariq et al. [1] thoroughly discuss anomalies and security. We categorize and analyse IoT's multilayer security vulnerabilities, including connection, communication, and management protocols. The author investigated IoT security and set security objectives to evaluate IoT solutions.

Kumar [2] examined industrial IoT system challenges and opportunities. They discussed energy efficiency, resource optimization, waste reduction, and environmental mitigation. Bhachawat et al. [3] studied machine learning and DL for Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease. Model updates improved degenerative disease classification. Mohanshan et al. [4] identified risks and mitigation measures from the literature.

Ray [5] analyze Web3's innovations, impacts, and DApps. Das et al. [6] created the radio propagation environment without pilots in RS. Ghiasi et al. [7] explained system security cybersecurity. IoT intrusion-detection systems were divided into four categories by Heidari et al. [8]; signature-based, anomaly-based, and hybrid. Sarker et al. [19] advised cybersecurity and IoT researchers.

IoT communication was emphasized by Gormüz et al. [20] after examining problems and security. Zarpelão et al. [21] investigated IoT infiltration.
Weber [18] examined the privacy and security of the IoT framework using legal and regulatory issues. Roman et al. [22] explored distributed IoT security and privacy. Researchers explored IoT security and privacy. Ransomware and IoT security [23].

Xiao et al. [24] reviewed IoT data privacy and DL methods. Computing and communication overhead, backup security, and state monitoring hampered IoT adoption. Data mining and machine learning have also been studied [25, 26] for their potential use in detecting cyber-attacks. Data mining and machine learning handled cybersecurity, abuse, and anomalies [25]. Unlike previous surveys, ours delves further into machine learning and DL to secure IoT. In this chapter, we look at the state of DL techniques for IoT security and discuss the challenges and opportunities they lie ahead. This study provides a crucial road map for researchers to shift their focus from enabling secure communication between IoT components to adopting intelligent ways that ensure end-to-end IoT security.
