# About the Editors

Payal Khurana Batra, PhD, is an assistant professor in the Department of Computer Science Engineering and Information Technology at RJIT, Jaypee Institute of Information Technology, Noida, India. She earned her B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering from Kurukhetra University, Kurukshetra, India, in 2004 and her M.E. in Information Technology from Panjab University, Chandigarh, India, in 2009. She earned her PhD from Jaypee Institute of Information Technology, Noida, India, in 2017. She has more than ten years of teaching experience. She has patents to her name in the domain of IoT. She has several publications in international journals and journals indexed in SCI and Scopus. She is an active researcher in WSNs, IoT, soft computing techniques.

Pawan Singh Mehta, PhD,(Senior Member, IEEE) is an assistant professor at Delhi Technological University, New Delhi. He earned his PhD in Computer Engineering from Jamia Millia Islamia and M.Tech. (Hons) in Computer Science and Engineering from the Center for Development of Advanced Computing (CDAC). He completed a Bachelor of Engineering from RJIT, Gwalior. He has authored 14 years of Teaching and Research Experience. He has authored more than 50 publications in international journals and conferences indexed in SCIE, Scopus, ESCI, and Web of Science. He is an active reviewer in international journals indexed in IEEE/ACM Transactions, SCIE, and Scopus. He was awarded the Research Excellence Award in 2022, 2023, and 2024. He is a Senior Member of IEEE, ACM, Fellow of IETE, and Life member of CSI and ISTE. He has published two books on the internet of things and edited a book for GATE. His research interests include wireless sensor networks, internet of things, quantum computing, computing, cryptography and network security, and artificial intelligence.

Sandeep Tanwar(Senior Member, IEEE) is a professor in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at the Institute of Technology, Nimra, India. Tanwar is a visiting Professor at Jan Wyzykowski University in Poznan, Poland, and the University of Pitesi in Romania. Tanwar's research interests include blockchain technology, wireless sensor networks, fog computing, smart grids, and IoT. He has authored 2 books and edited 13 books and more than 200 technical papers, including top journals and conferences, such as IEEE TNSE, TVT, TII, WCM, Networks, ICC, GLOBCOME, and INFCOM. Tanwar initiated the research field of blockchain technology adoption in various verticals in 2017. His h-index is 44. Tanwar actively serves his research communities in multiple roles. He is currently serving in the editorial boards of Physical Communication, Computer Communications, International Journal of Communication System, and Security and Privacy. He was awarded the best research papers from IEEE GLOBCOME 2018, IEEE ICC 2019, and Springer ICRIC-2019.
