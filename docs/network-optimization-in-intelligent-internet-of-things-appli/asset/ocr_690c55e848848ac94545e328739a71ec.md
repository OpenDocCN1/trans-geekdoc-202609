The authors in this article [3] discuss the state-of-the-art methods addressing core challenges in IoT. They reviewed ML and deep learning methods for anomaly detection. These disputes use unexplained trends in data and the behaviour of devices that may occur due to unexpected noises, attacks, and anomalies. The algorithms must be able to capture large high-dimensional data streams flowing continuously in available limited memory space. They define the taxonomy of anomaly detection, covering the techniques, nature of data, types of anomalies, unsupervised anomaly detection methods, evaluation criteria, etc. Most techniques used in the literature on publicly available datasets. In this article [4], the authors have addressed the challenges caused by the exponential growth of IoT networks using mutual information to select the best features and deep neural networks. It presents the comprehensive analysis of deep learning models and their variants on the IoT-Bot 2020 dataset. These different models include Recurrent Neural Network (RNN), Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU), and Convolutional Neural Network (CNN). The proposed model improved the accuracy by 0.57%–2.6% and reduced the false alarm rate by 0.23%–7.98%.
