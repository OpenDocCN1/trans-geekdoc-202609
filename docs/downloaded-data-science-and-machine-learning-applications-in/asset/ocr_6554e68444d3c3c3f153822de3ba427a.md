The BO-ET model was successfully deployed on Well C to predict Vs and Vp from the wireline logs. The sensitivity towards fluid presence was adequately predicted for all three formations confirming the robustness of the model. This result demonstrates the appropriateness of the model for predicting Vs and Vp in similar formation types.
