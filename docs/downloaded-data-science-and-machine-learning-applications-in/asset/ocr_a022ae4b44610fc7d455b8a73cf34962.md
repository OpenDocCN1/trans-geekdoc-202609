Looking forward, we can expect advancements in artificial intelligence and generative machine learning models to improve our ability to analyse subsurface data, extract meaningful insights, and make decisions. However, as with any rapidly evolving field, there will be challenges along the way. It will be essential to continue to develop and refine algorithms to ensure that the models are accurate and reliable and to address the ethical implications of using these technologies in subsurface engineering. I hope this book will serve as a valuable resource for researchers, engineers, and decision-makers in the energy industry and inspire continued innovation and progress in the dynamic field of subsurface engineering.
