As aforementioned, virtual flow metering includes physics-based and data-driven methods. When it comes to the plunger lift application, since the process is extremely transient, the application of physics-based methods is extremely complex and unviable (Akhiiartdinov et al., 2020). Regarding data-driven models, Andrianov (2018) (GarcAa et al., 2010) and Shoeibi Omrani et al. (2018) (Loh and Omrani, 2018) demonstrated the application of ANNs to simulate the transient behaviour of severe slugging and liquid-loaded gas wells. On the other hand, Akhiiartdinov et al. (2020) have attempted to model a VFM on plunger lifted wells. In this study, the objective was to optimize the “on” and “off” periods of the control valve, which serve as parameters for building the response surface.
