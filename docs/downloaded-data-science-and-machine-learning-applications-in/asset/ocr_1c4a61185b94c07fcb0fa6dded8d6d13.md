# 3.3.3  Desired Flow Rates

The desired rate is the rate to achieve optimum production based on technical and economic factors. This is usually a choice of management. In this work, technical consideration was the basis to determine the desired flow rate. Desired flow rate is required for gas lift. A sensitivity plot was obtained by varying some parameters such as reservoir pressure, water cut and total gas-oil ratio for each well.
