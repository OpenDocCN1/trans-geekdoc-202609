# 1. Introduction

The interpretation of seismic data is a critical aspect of geological exploration. It enables geologists and engineers to identify and delineate subsurface structures such as faults, reservoirs, and geological formations. However, traditional approaches to seismic interpretation, such as manual picking and horizon tracking, are laborious and time-consuming, particularly in areas with complex geology and numerous faults.
