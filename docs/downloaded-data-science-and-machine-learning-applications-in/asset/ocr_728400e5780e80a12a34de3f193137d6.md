# 2.2 Geochemical Reactions of CO₂-LSWAG

Carbonates with SO₄²⁻ and carboxylate ion exchange interpolation was carried out on sets of relative permeabilities. Equations (7.2) and (7.3) show the partitioning of CO₂ between oil and water in the reservoir (Dang et al., 2014).

The aqueous reactions considered in CO₂ LSWAG modelling are:

CO₂(g) → CO₂(aq) (7.1)
CO₂(aq) + H₂O ↔ H⁺ + HCO₃⁻ (7.2)
H⁺ + OH⁻ ↔ H₂O (7.3)
CaCH₃COO⁺ ↔ CH₃COO⁻ + Ca²⁺ (7.4)
CaSO₄ ↔ Ca²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ (7.5)
MgSO₄ ↔ Mg²⁺ + SO₄²⁻ (7.6)

The mineral reaction considered in CO₂-LSWAG modelling for calcite and dolomite are:

CaCO₃ + H⁺ ↔ Ca²⁺ + HCO₃⁻ (7.7)
CaMg(CO₃)₂ + 2H⁺ ↔ Ca²⁺ + 2HCO₃⁻ + Mg²⁺ (7.8)

The ion exchange considered in CO₂-LSWAG modelling is given as:
