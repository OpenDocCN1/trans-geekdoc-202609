There are a few to choose from, but the two most fundamental processes are known as the _waterfall process_ and the _spiral process_. Both of these processes have the same basic stages (or phases) and elements in common. The difference lies in the order and manner in which they are approached. Both processes share the following four consecutive phases:

+   1. Specification
+   2. Design
+   3. Build
+   4. Test
