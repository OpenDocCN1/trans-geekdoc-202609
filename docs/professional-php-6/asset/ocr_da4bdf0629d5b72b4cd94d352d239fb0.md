# The Spike

Before continuing, consider how you’ll display what you need to show on the screen. Go back and eyeball Figure 19-8 again. Note that something will have to take care of the subtotaling, not to mention the order. Something must associate human names with what exists in the database.

In addition, remember that “something” ultimately stops with you, and that anything that makes your life easier can’t be a bad thing. Clearly, hard-coding the HTML will be a maintenance nightmare.
