The attribute types are indicated after the colon. In this simple example, all the attribute types are
strings. The minus sign (–) says that the attributes are private members of the class. The plus sign (+) in
front of the operations indicates that they are public. If an operation returns a value, its type is shown
after the colon. Again, in this example, they are all strings.
