When load testing, endeavor to use another server (or, ideally, servers) to act as clients connecting to the live Web server(s). This ensures that the load test software itself is not a burden on your Web server. In addition, always try to use the live server running in a staging mode or, if not feasible, an accurate recreation of the live environment and configuration on another server, to ensure that the results are representative.

It can often be helpful to present the results of your tests (assuming that they are positive) in a nontechnical manner to your client. It will help the client plan for future expansion against his or her own commercial or operational objectives.
