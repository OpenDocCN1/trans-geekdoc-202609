# Preface

In the dynamic landscape of today’s global economy, the integration of technology, business intelligence, and innovation has become pivotal for entrepreneurs seeking to navigate and thrive in the ever-evolving business environment. As the world witnesses a digital revolution, marked by the widespread application of artificial intelligence and the continuous emergence of smart technologies, entrepreneurs find themselves at the forefront of transformative change.

This edited reference book, Applying Business Intelligence and Innovation to Entrepreneurship, is a collaborative effort by Kannapat Kankaew, Parinya Nakpathom, Alhuda Chnitphattana, Krittipat Pitchayadejanant, and Siwaporn Kunnapapdeelert, along with various authors from around the world. It is designed to provide insights into the intricate interplay between technology, business intelligence, and innovation in entrepreneurship across diverse sectors.

In this era of the innovation economy, where technological advancements propel commerce forward, entrepreneurs play a vital role in shaping and redefining industries. From agricultural products to fast-moving consumer goods, from the hospitality industry to cultural and indigenous products, entrepreneurship is a catalyst for creativity and circular economies within communities and societies.

This book delves into the multifaceted realm of entrepreneurial business management, emphasizing the symbiotic relationship between technology and business intelligence. It explores how entrepreneurs can leverage digitalization for sustainability, innovation, and a competitive edge in various sectors. Notably, the book addresses the challenges faced by entrepreneurs in today’s dynamic business environment and provides valuable insights into successful business strategies.

Our aim is to cater to a diverse audience, including academicians, researchers, undergraduate/postgraduate/doctoral students, and professionals in both private and public enterprises. The content covers a spectrum of topics, ranging from traditional entrepreneurship and management to cutting-edge areas like Fintech startups, cryptocurrency in business, community startups, and the integration of local wisdom into innovative practices.
