The recommended topics span the breadth of entrepreneurship, including the adoption of business intelligence by small and medium enterprises, innovation in social enterprise management, the dynamics of the hospitality and tourism industry, and the influence of digital marketing and marketing communication. In this book, the chapters unfold a comprehensive exploration of the dynamic interplay between technology, business intelligence, and innovation in the entrepreneurial landscape. From a systematic literature review on the influence of business intelligence on entrepreneurial orientation to an in-depth analysis of the role of technology business incubators in startup development, each chapter offers valuable insights and practical perspectives. The disruptive force of blockchain technology, the pivotal role of supply chain and operations management, the transformative impact of Neuromarketing in the digital era, and the revolutionary influence of cryptocurrencies and fintech startups are among the diverse topics covered. Additionally, the chapters delve into the adoption of business intelligence by small and medium-sized enterprises, the significance of design thinking and creativity in entrepreneurial innovation, and the exploration of environmental, social, and governance performance in corporate boards. With a wide-ranging scope, this collection is a valuable resource for academicians, researchers, students, and professionals seeking a nuanced understanding of the ever-evolving landscape of entrepreneurship in the digital age.

Chapter 1 conducts an extensive systematic literature review, employing the PRISMA framework, to delve into the influence of business intelligence (BI) on entrepreneurial orientation (EO) within ventures. By analyzing articles from notable databases, the study uncovers a scarcity of research in this domain, emphasizing the need for further exploration. With “Business intelligence” and “Entrepreneurial orientation” as keywords, 23 articles are scrutinized, providing a foundation to identify gaps for future researchers in the dynamic intersection of BI and entrepreneurship.

Focusing on the role of technology business incubators (TBIs), Chapter 2 explores their impact on startup effectiveness and overall development. Through a comprehensive literature review and empirical analysis of Gujarat’s entrepreneurial ecosystem, the chapter establishes a framework connecting TBI support to operational effectiveness. It offers insights into the challenges faced by startups from ideation to commercialization, presenting a valuable resource for entrepreneurs and policymakers aiming to enhance the existing support systems.
