# ## Findings

It is clear that the requirements and success factors for adopting a business intelligence system are numerous and diverse, as a result of its connection to all parts of the organization. These are primarily organizational requirements, centered around the design of the operational business intelligence system, in addition to requirements related to management, control, and coordination. They are embodied in the method of integrating the business intelligence system into the organizational structure to ensure vertical and horizontal flow of data and information to enhance coordination levels. In addition to technical requirements, which are concentrated in a high level of maturity of information technology and communication within the enterprise. Moreover, a crucial requirement is the skills that individuals responsible for designing and operating the business intelligence system must possess.

This chapter dealt with the requirements of adopting Business Intelligence in small and medium-sized industrial enterprises. It tries to analyse the main reasons behind adopting Business Intelligence successfully, specifically in light of the characteristics of such enterprises, like ilimited resources, the simplicity of its organizational framework, and its limited market share.
