- **6. Creativity:** Entrepreneurs are seen as creator by the start-up fathers. Entrepreneurs are extremely creative problem solvers. Idea generation, thinking, innovation, invention. Innovation-skill is the ability to brainstorm and invent marketable and sellable goods and services that possess economic value and subject to scarcity. Creativity is fabricating into existence that which does not yet exist for benefits of human wellness and wellbeing. Creativity is the process of transforming certain input (tangible/intangible) into a desired output for purposes of achieving beneficial needs. In entrepreneurship, creativity must have an economic value. The fact that goods and services possess economic value means it has pricing concept and could be subject to scarcity. Thus, in this context, goods and services could be product innovation, process innovation, market innovation, idea innovation, technological innovation and administrative innovation (Ovharhe et al., 2021; Manohar, Paul, Strong & Mittal, 2023). However, the skills needed for creativity and innovation process are affective skill, cognitive skill and psychomotor skill. Hence, innovation-skill and creativity can be idea, experience, tangible and intangible.

- **7. Leading the Way and Networking:** Entrepreneurial leadership is very interesting features for the achievement of greatness. Entrepreneurs are potential leaders which enable them to initiate, discover and sight what the future holds. Entrepreneurs are also known for their branching network in building bond in relationship, partnership and customership. This leading tendency aids entrepreneurs for planning, organizing, coordinating, motivating, directing and controlling. Leading the way by entrepreneurs create an orientation for pace setting. All teams and intrapreneurs will be able to dive into the vision, mission, core values and propositions. The journey would be easily defined and spelt out on goals to achieve (Lim, 2023). Entrepreneurs need networking to build solid and foundation relationship to pursuit their business. Networking is of necessity in fundraising and crowd funding especially in social entrepreneurship (Mandrinos & Lim, 2023).

- **8. Focus on Purpose, Vision and Mission:** Entrepreneurs should always ask the questions that will boost up the stream of focus. The first question is ‘Why am I here’ or ‘What am I doing here’? This will answer the question of purpose. Secondly; ‘What does the future holds for me’ or ‘What am I seeing in the future from here now’? This answer the vision parity. Thirdly; ‘What is my course line of action to achieve my entrepreneurial journey’ or ‘What path am I threading on to arrive at my entrepreneurial dreams’? No distraction is allowed when focusing on the purpose, vision and mission. Any distraction may lead to a failure in the enterprise mission which will be aborted. This will lead to a bleeding vision and unfertile purpose at the edge. Proper focusing enable entrepreneurs to development credible risk culture, creativity and innovation skills on learning how to drive at the right path of action and targeting best goals (Ratten, 2023).

- **9. Resiliency and Building the Bridge:** Entrepreneurs should have the slogan of forward ever and backwards never in the business stream. Letting the stream of cash-flow circulating in the short-term and long-term in the entrepreneurial journey is the dream of every entrepreneurs. Entrepreneur function is to build the stream of consistent wealth creation and value creation in every business opportunity. But sometime business owners should always protect and secured their bridge at all cost (Blessley & Mudambi, 2022). A broken bridge causes a lot to repairs and maintain. Resilience strategies that will aid like competitive advantage should be of great concern to every entrepreneur as they want to sustain and survive in business. Developing fighting strategy is good, but developing resiliency and building bridges should not be taken for granted by entrepreneurs. Building solid bridges for customer, client, vendor, government, legal framework, community, institutions and stakeholders at large is of necessity. More so, this strengthens and builds strategic bonds for relationship, partnership and customership.

- **10. Communication Skills:** Liaison with all stakeholders is a vital business strategy for start-up. Whether it is upwards communication (from bottom-top), downward communication (top-bottom) or diagonal communication (cross-sectional) the entrepreneur must learn how to input information and get feedback. Communication also could be external virtue that must be maintained for free flow and better understanding of business. Communication is the forces that bind all entity to oneness.

Communicating the purpose, vision and mission to the organization human resource is the credibility of success and consistency in the business function. Nevertheless, effective and efficient communication to clients and customer revealed the longevity of the enterprise in the turbulent times. Thus, effective and efficient communication is a competitive advantage to the enterprise. Entrepreneurs need to learn how to generate information for effective communication. Information should be from the confident source, reliable, precise, accurate, authentic, trusted, valid, verifiable, comprehensive and meaningful. If entrepreneurs should be accurate and eloquent with information virtue they will maintain credible communication skills. The business world needs good communicators for interface with employees, management, community, external environment (government, agencies and task) vendor, customer relations and the entire supply chain management system (Yadav et al., 2023).

## 3. CONCEPT OF BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

The fundamental aim of business intelligence is to actualize precise, accurate and confident decision making. This is while authors argue that business intelligence is correlated to competitive intelligence and decision support system because the large amount of accurate data that has become more accessible allows business intelligence to synchronize (Huang, Savita & Zhong-jie, 2022).

Business intelligence combines analytic, data mining, data visualization, data tools, data infrastructure and best practices to help management and entrepreneurs make more data-driven decision in the short-term and long-term (Horn, 2023). Business intelligence is also software that suggests business data and presents it in user-friendly views such as report, dashboard, chart, graph, metrics and framework. However, business intelligence could be well analyzed in entrepreneurship by integrating it with competitive intelligence and decision support system (Zhou, San, Liu & Kozyrkov, 2023).

Holori, Ahmadi, Khanmohammadi, Ranjbar and Kordlouie (2023) averred that business intelligence is a fundamental tool that assist management to evaluate business information on return on investment, return on equity, return on asset, return on capital employed, net present value, cost-benefit analysis and break-even analysis. By using the Structural Equation Model, business intelligence could yield tremendous output that boost enterprise performance in the small medium enterprise, large corporation, multinationals and conglomerates. Entrepreneur decision making should be authenticated and optimistic with the intervention of business intelligence.
