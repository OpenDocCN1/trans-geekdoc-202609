Businesses that strategically design and optimize their supply chains gain an edge. A managed supply chain ensures operations, timely deliveries, and cost efficiency setting businesses apart from others in the market. This competitive advantage goes beyond cost reduction; it also involves creating an agile structure that can quickly adapt to changing market conditions. This phenomenon aligns with resource-based views and dynamic capabilities theory. The resource-based view suggests that competitive advantage stems from valuable resources possessed by firms. Efficient OSCM then becomes a resource that’s not easily replicable, by competitors. Dynamic capabilities theory emphasizes a company’s capacity to effectively integrate, develop, and adapt both external competencies to navigate rapidly changing environments. Within this context, operational supply chain management (OSCM) serves as a capability that empowers entrepreneurs to continuously adapt and innovate thereby contributing to the attainment of competitive advantage (Tönnissen & Teuteborg 2020).

**Enhancing Operational Efficiency and Cost Reduction:** For ventures aiming to optimize resource utilization and minimize costs, operational efficiency stands as a metric. OSCM plays a role, in enhancing efficiency by streamlining processes reducing waste, and optimizing resource allocation. The implications extend beyond cost savings; they encompass the establishment of an agile operational framework that bolsters overall adaptability for the business. From a perspective, the relationship between OSCM and operational efficiency aligns with principles derived from systems theory and efficiency theories. Systems theory asserts that organizations operate as systems with interconnected components that rely on one another for functioning. By implementing OSCM practices entrepreneurs effectively optimize the system by ensuring each component works harmoniously together. Efficiency theories emphasize the significance of minimizing resource wastage while maximizing output. Through the integration of OSCM practices, entrepreneurs adhere to these principles of efficiency ensuring optimal resource utilization (Inman & Green 2018).

**Prioritizing Customer Satisfaction and Fostering Loyalty:** Within the ecosystem prioritizing customer satisfaction is crucial, for establishing a customer base. OSCM plays a role, in ensuring that products are available and delivered on time and consistently of quality. The impact of OSCM on customer satisfaction aligns with the Service Profit Chain theory, which suggests that satisfied customers result in increased loyalty, repeat business, and positive word of mouth. Efficient OSCM contributes to a seamless supply chain enhancing customer experiences and fostering brand loyalty (Mahmoud et al., 2018). This relationship is emphasized by the principles of customer relationship management (CRM) and service quality theories. CRM highlights the significance of managing and nurturing long-term customer relationships for profitability.
