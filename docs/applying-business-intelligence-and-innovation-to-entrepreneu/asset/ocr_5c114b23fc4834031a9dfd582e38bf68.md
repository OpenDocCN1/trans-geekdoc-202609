Preventing disease by maintaining health (preventive) and healing (curative) is the command of Allah ﷻ in the Holy Quran. Moreover, Allah ﷻ, through the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ (may the peace and blessing of Allah ﷻ be upon him), teaches men how to maintain health and treat disease through the Sunnah (the Way of the Prophet). There is a quote that refers to the Hadith At-Tirmidzi said that Had not the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ himself said, ‘O Servant of God, use the medicine because God hath not created a pain without a remedy for it.” Every Muslim is obliged to travel to find a cure for the disease or maintain health while hoping for the pleasure of Allah ﷻ to provide healing. Offering a different approach derived from the Holy Quran and Hadith, Muslim health tourism poses a proximity between religion, tourism, and health (Bafadhal, 2021). This means that although the travelling purpose of medical travellers is for health treatment, their primary concerns are similar to other Muslim tourists in the spiritual-related journey in which ritualised practice is incredibly significant (Teerakunpisut & Kongpiam, 2023).

Bafadhal (2022) defines Muslim health tourism as “a worship through sincere trips by Muslims to leave their origin place towards a certain health care provider as an effort to prevent or cure a disease, in order to find improvement in the quality of physical, psychological, psychosocial, and spiritual aspects according to the Quran and Hadith.” There are several combinations in this term, for example, “halal healthcare tourism” and “Islamic health tourism.”

Muslim health tourism consists of two principal subsets, namely Muslim wellness tourism and Muslim medical tourism, which have different subjects, purposes, and objects. Regarding tourists who travel, Muslim wellness tourism tends to be done by people who are in good condition or healthy. However, they want to prevent disease and improve their health (preventive) (Bafadhal, 2017). Muslim wellness tourism is “a worship in a sincere trip by Muslims, leaving their origin place to certain health care providers to prevent diseases (preventive) as to get the optimised well-being and resilience with the blessing of Allah ﷻ” (Bafadhal, 2017). This term is often used mutually with other terms, such as “Halal wellness and spa.”
