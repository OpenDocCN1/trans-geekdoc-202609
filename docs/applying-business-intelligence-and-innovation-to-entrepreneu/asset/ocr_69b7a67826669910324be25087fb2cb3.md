# BENEFITS AND POSITIVES OF BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE FOR INDUSTRIAL SMAL AND MEDUIM SIZED ENTERPRISE

The term Business Intelligence indicates the combination of data, information, technologies, and tools used for multidimensional data collection and analysis to improve decision-making processes. Business Intelligence systems primarily aim to achieve many benefits for industrial smal and meduim sized enterprise management, especially when employed correctly. The benefits can be summarized as follows:

+   - Providing necessary information and knowledge within the enterprise, contributing to improved decision-making, performance, and operational efficiency.
+   - Offering a wide range of essential information in management operations when needed, providing flexibility to external environmental changes and quick responsiveness to customers. (Grossmann & Rinderle-ma, 2015, p. 2)
