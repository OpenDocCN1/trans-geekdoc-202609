**Satya Ranjan Acharya** is a Professor and Director, of the Department of Entrepreneurship Education, Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India. He is a Co-Chair of the Post Graduate Programmes Executive Committee. An academician in the areas of Entrepreneurship Education, Dr Satya Ranjan Acharya holds expertise in training budding entrepreneurs as well as grooming developed entrepreneurs. His mentoring activities beyond institutions and classrooms are commendable. Through the “Hastakala Setu Yojana” project, he is mentoring over 20,000 traditional artisans of Gujarat, India in providing training, and exposure to market and financial linkages across 33 districts of Gujarat. He continuously engages with his incubated startups at the incubation centre to ensure startups’ desired growth and sustainability. He is known for teaching courses in New Enterprise Creation and Entrepreneurial Finance and is also a supervisor of FPM students. He conducts and organizes various management development and faculty development programs on entrepreneurship teaching and startup incubation for the Govt of Gujarat and the Ministry of Education, Government of India. He received the National Teacher’s Award, on 5th September 2023 from Hon President Smt. Droupadi Murmu, at New Delhi.
