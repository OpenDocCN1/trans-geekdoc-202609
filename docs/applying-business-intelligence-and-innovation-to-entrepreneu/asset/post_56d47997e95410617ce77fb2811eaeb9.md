
## Effects of Transactional Leadership on Environmental Sustainability

Transactional leadership has been the subject of extensive research concerning its impact on organizational performance and employee behavior. The findings of Kafetzopoulos and Gotzamani (2022) indicate that this leadership style has a positive influence on talent work motivation and performance. However, Khan and Jantan (2022) discovered that transactional leadership has adverse effects on innovative work behavior. Moreover, Chuan's (2022) research revealed that the use of this leadership style results in negative consequences for non-financial performance in the multi-level marketing industry. These studies emphasize the importance of considering the pros and cons of transactional leadership carefully. Additionally, Tanveer et al. (2021) explored the impact of environmental sustainability and financial resource utilization on firms' financial performance, indicating the mediating role of leadership styles, including transactional leadership. Additionally, some authors discovered that transactional leadership enhanced the link between stress and unfavourable conduct, lessening the environment's impact on workplaces (Yao et al., 2014).

The references provided in this chapter collectively suggest that the effects of transactional leadership on environmental sustainability and organizational performance are complex, with both positive and negative impacts. The influence of transactional leadership on employee behavior, talent motivation, and organizational performance varies across different contexts and industries.

## METHODOLOGY

### Sample and Measures

To measure these relationships, the structural equation model is used for the path model of hypotheses. Production-based nitrogen emissions (kg/capita) data was gathered from the Sustainable Development Report 2023 (www.sdgindex.org). Reactive nitrogen is released while manufacturing goods imported or used locally. Reactive nitrogen is associated with emissions to the atmosphere of ammonia, nitrogen oxides, nitrous oxide, and reactive nitrogen that may be exported to aquatic environments. These pollutants can harm human health and the environment (Sustainable et al., 2023).

Environmental protection expenditure by domains, 2013-2021, is gathered from TurkStat, Environmental Protection Expenditure Statistics, 2021, from TUIK (Turkish Statistical Institute) (TUIK, 2023a). A leadership survey is gathered from the highest manager of each textile company from the Thrace region in Turkey. The textile industry has the fourth highest share by sales value of the four highest industries in Turkey (TUIK, 2023b).

Due to its strategic location at the crossroads of several regions, including the Middle East, Asia, North Africa, Eastern Europe, and Russia, the Turkish textile industry is significant (Kaya et al., 2019). The industry primarily focuses on labor and exports, with small and medium-sized businesses making up more than 90% of the sector (SMEs) (Divrik & Baykal, 2023). However, the sector needs help with environmental sustainability, energy management, nitrogen emissions, energy consumption, and pricing. However, the sector needs help concerning environmental sustainability, energy management, and nitrogen emissions, as well as energy consumption and pricing (Alkaya & Demirer, 2015; Divrik & Baykal, 2023; Hasanbeigi & Price, 2012). Despite experiencing periods of recession, the Turkish textile industry’s economic significance is seen in its contribution to the country’s economy (Bilgin et al., 2011; Duran & Temiz Dinç, 2016). Global occurrences like the COVID-19 epidemic have also affected the sector, causing changes in manufacturing and economic problems (Sutrisno, 2022). Analysis has been done on the sector’s development, productivity, and competitiveness, which reflects how dynamic the Turkish textile industry is (Başkol, 2018; Karabag et al., 2014) (Karabag et al., 2013; Başkol, 2018; Alvan & Ghosh, 2010). In addition, research and concern have focused on the industry’s effects on the environment, waste management, and energy conservation, underscoring the necessity of sustainable practices and technological developments (Gümüş & Akbal, 2011; Qingfen et al., 2023; Tunc et al., 2016). The textile industry’s importance to the Turkish economy and beyond is further highlighted by its position in trade, intra-industry dynamics, and effects on other sectors like tourism and agriculture (Jain, 2022; Permata Sari et al., 2019; Tokel et al., 2022).

Moreover, the Thrace Region is the locomotive region of our country’s textile and textile product manufacturing, mainly concentrated in the city of Tekirdağ. According to the 2018 SSI database, 488 businesses in the Thrace Region are active in the textiles industry, and 56,799 people are employed within the industry (Invest in Trakya-Tekirdağ, Edirne Kırklareli, 2023). The online survey was sent to 472 textile manufacturing firms; 341 returned, but 322 were found suitable for the analysis. Turkish validated scale by Demir and Okan (2008) of transformational and transactional leadership survey is used for the research. An important statistical metric that considers a regression model’s number of predictors and offers a more precise evaluation of the model’s goodness of fit is the adjusted R-squared (R2). It is especially crucial in regression analysis since it modifies the R-squared value according to the number of predictors, providing a more accurate picture of the percentage of the dependent variable’s variance that can be accounted for by the independent variables. The adjusted R-squared value is significant because it can handle the possible problem of overfitting in regression models with several predictors. The adjusted R-squared provides a more realistic assessment of the model’s explanatory power by considering the impact of adding predictors and penalizing the inclusion of irrelevant variables, in contrast to the standard R-squared, which tends to increase with more predictors. When used for research and data analysis, a higher adjusted R-squared value means that the model minimizes the risk of overfitting while accounting for the significant factors and better fitting the data. This is essential to guarantee the validity and dependability of regression models, particularly when drawing conclusions or forecasts from the model’s output. Thus, to evaluate the reliability and validity of regression models, researchers and analysts can benefit greatly from the modified R-squared, which eventually enhances the accuracy and robustness of statistical studies.
