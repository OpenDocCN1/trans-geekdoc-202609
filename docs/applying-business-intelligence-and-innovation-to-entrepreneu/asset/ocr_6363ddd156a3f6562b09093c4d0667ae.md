# CONCLUSION

Despite the evolution of natural entrepreneurs, data shows that incubators have improved and increased their function as training, communication, and advisory hubs for business owners in all sectors of expertise rather than as traditional business centers with offices. Incubators have prioritized raising the success rate of innovation by supporting entrepreneurs in overcoming their youth and young challenges, in addition to the diversity of perspectives and evolution of the models and services supplied (Dettwiler et al., 2006; Mireftekhari, 2017; Schwartz, 2013). Monitoring new technological developments has a significant impact and may help support long-term knowledge and uncertainties expected as a result of lower levels of outsourcing technologies when choosing sales and marketing, where increased levels of technology exploration are expected to slow the pace of outsourcing research and development activities and increase the cost of entry for new firms (Koza et al., 1998; Koza & Lewin, 1998; Lavie & Rosenkopf, 2005, 2006b, 2006a).
