
## About the Contributors

### Kannapat Kankaew
Kannapat Kankaew is currently an Assistant Professor at Burapha University International College. He received Doctor of Public Administration majoring in Human Resources Management. Before his 12 years as an academic professional, he worked in the aviation industry for over 10 years. He has published and participated in International Conferences, reputed journals. He also published chapters in edited books, being a books editor, and examiner for Ph.D. and Master students in both national and international institutions. And he was dedicated as an editorial board, associate editor, and reviewer for various domestic and international journals.

### Parinya Nakpathom
Parinya Nakpathom is Dean of Burapha University International College and Instructor of Holistic Health and Wellness Management. He earned Bachelor’s Degree in Arts (Thai) from Burapha University in 2005, followed by a Master’s Degree in International Hospitality and Hotel Management from the University of Western Sydney, Australia in 2008. In 2018, he has completed his Doctoral Degree in Management from Silpakorn University, Thailand. Since 2012, he has been contributing to academia as a lecturer at Burapha University International College. In 2021, he was appointed as an assistant Professor in Business Administration. His research interests encompass Tourist, Hospitality, Service, and Tourist attraction. Over the years, he has had the privilege of being a guest speaker more than 100 times in public organizations, private companies, and universities, both in Thailand and abroad.

### Krittipat Pitchayadejanant
Krittipat Pitchayadejanant is a lecturer in Logistics Management, Burapha University International College. His research interests are in business data analysis, especially advanced statistical analysis applied to hospitality and tourism, logistics management, and marketing. ### Satya Ranjan Acharya
Satya Ranjan Acharya is a Professor and Director of the Department of Entrepreneurship Education, Entrepreneurship Development Institute of India. He is a Co-Chair of the Post Graduate Programmes Executive Committee. An academician in the areas of Entrepreneurship Education, Dr Satya Ranjan Acharya holds expertise in training budding entrepreneurs as well as grooming developed entrepreneurs. His mentoring activities beyond institutions and classrooms are commendable. Through the “Hastakala Setu Yojana” project, he is mentoring over 20,000 traditional artisans of Gujarat, India in providing training, and exposure to market and financial linkages across 33 districts of Gujarat. He continuously engages with his incubated startups at the incubation centre to ensure startups’ desired growth and sustainability. He is known for teaching courses in New Enterprise Creation and Entrepreneurial Finance and is also a supervisor of FPM students. He conducts and organizes various management development and faculty development programs on entrepreneurship teaching and startup incubation for the Govt of Gujarat and the Ministry of Education, Government of India. He received the National Teacher’s Award, on 5th September 2023 from Hon President Smt. Droupadi Murmu, at New Delhi.

### Khairul Anuar Mohd Ali
Khairul Anuar Mohd Ali is a Professor at the UKM-Graduate School of Business. He received his first degree in Bachelor Business Administration from Washington State University, USA, his Master in Business Administration from Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia and his PhD from Universiti Utara Malaysia. His current research interests are in the area of quality management, supply chain management, performance measurement, service quality and customer satisfaction. His current research project are on the development of Customer Satisfaction Index and Mathematical Modeling for Performance Measurement of Malaysian Local Authorities, both funded by relevant Research Grants. Professor Khairul's research works have appeared in various international refereed journals such as Total Quality Management & Business Excellence Journal, Quality & Quantity International Journal, Social Indicators Research Journal, International Journal of Services and Operations Management, International Journal of Supply Chain Management, Test Engineering and Management, LogForum Scientific Journal of Logistics, Management Science Letters, Interdisciplinary Journal of Information, Knowledge, and Management, International Review of Business Research Papers, Journal of Global Business Management, Pakistan Journal of Nutrition, The Indonesian Management & Accounting Research, Jurnal Sains Malaysiana and Jurnal Pengurusán.

### Yamini Chandra
Yamini Chandra is an organizational psychologist, entrepreneurship educator, professor, and mentor with thirteen years of experience in teaching, training, and research in entrepreneurship theory and practice, corporate ethics, organizational behavior, organizational psychology, and human resource management. She is actively involved in accelerating the learning process by developing peer-collaborative work. Dr. Chandra closely works with student entrepreneurs, mapping their competencies for behavioral nurturing and enhancing knowledge, skills, abilities, values, and habits through coaching and mentoring start-ups, some of which were also carried out as ventures. She also has exposure to working with government grant projects and has also published papers in reputed national and international journals, such as in ABDC, Scopus, and Web of Science, etc. Recently Dr. Chandra received ‘an outstanding paper award by Asian Education and Development Studies - Literati Award Winners 2022’ by Emerald Publishing Ltd.
