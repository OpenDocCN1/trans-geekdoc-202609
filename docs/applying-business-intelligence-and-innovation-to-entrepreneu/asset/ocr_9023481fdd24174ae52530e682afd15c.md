+   **6. Creativity:** Entrepreneurs are seen as creator by the start-up fathers. Entrepreneurs are extremely creative problem solvers. Idea generation, thinking, innovation, invention. Innovation-skill is the ability to brainstorm and invent marketable and sellable goods and services that possess economic value and subject to scarcity. Creativity is fabricating into existence that which does not yet exists for benefits of human wellness and wellbeing. Creativity is the process of transforming certain input (tangible/intangible) into a desired output for purposes of achieving beneficial needs. In entrepreneurship, creativity must have an economic value. The fact that goods and services possess economic value means it has pricing concept and could be subject to scarcity. Thus, in this context, goods and services could be product innovation, process innovation, market innovation, idea innovation, technological innovation and administrative innovation (Ovharhe et al., 2021; Manohar, Paul, Strong & Mittal, 2023). However, the skills needed for creativity and innovation process are affective skill, cognitive skill and psychomotor skill. Hence, innovation-skill and creativity can be idea, experience, tangible and intangible.

+   **7. Leading the Way and Networking:** Entrepreneurial leadership is very interesting features for the achievement of greatness. Entrepreneurs are potential leaders which enable them to initiate, discover and sight what the future holds. Entrepreneurs are also known for their branching network in building bond in relationship, partnership and customership. This leading tendency aids entrepreneurs for planning, organizing, coordinating, motivating, directing and controlling. Leading the way by entrepreneurs create an orientation for pace setting. All teams and intrapreneurs will be able to dive into the vision, mission, core values and propositions. The journey would be easily defined and spelt out on goals to achieve (Lim, 2023). Entrepreneurs need networking to build solid and foundation relationship to pursuit their business. Networking is of necessity in fundraising and crowd funding especially in social entrepreneurship (Mandrinos & Lim, 2023).

+   **8. Focus on Purpose, Vision and Mission:** Entrepreneurs should always ask the questions that will boost up the stream of focus. The first question is ‘Why am I here’ or ‘What am I doing here’? This will answer the question of purpose. Secondly; ‘What does the future holds for me’ or ‘What am I seeing in the future from here now’? This answer the vision parity. Thirdly; ‘What is my course line of action to achieve my entrepreneurial journey’ or ‘What path am I threading on to arrive at my entrepreneurial dreams’?
