## 4. DEXTERITY OF INNOVATION ON ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND MANAGEMENT

The field of entrepreneurship is the iota of innovation. The entrepreneurs are the steering that drives innovation into high depth of competitiveness in the business world. Every firm’s innovative management team is dependent to entrepreneurship orchestration to push and pull the trigger of the market forces competitive parity string. Innovation in the plight of entrepreneurship can be categories to the two instrumentations which regarded as the simplex innovation and complex innovation. The simplex innovation can also be referred to as ‘Content Innovation’. Hence, the complex innovation is perceived as Context Innovation. Both are the anchor of the instrumental strings that push and pull the resultant outcome of innovation in the competitive market environment because the business intelligence of an enterprise is revealed on its ability, capability and capacity of innovation (Reeves & Whitaker, 2022)

The simplex innovation can also be called the ‘content innovation’ which are instrumental strings of component that are unique concept such as the product innovation, process innovation, service innovation, administrative innovation, market innovation and technology innovation. Whereas, the complex innovation can also be seen as the ‘context innovation’ consist of digital innovation, sustainable innovation and transformative innovation. The instrumental components are drawn to augment the simplex innovation or content innovation. It is applied on specific task as hybrid or aggregate entrepreneur. For example, the digital innovation can be use by the entrepreneurs to promote data science, analytic, processing, report and feedback loop which is in liaison with the digital entrepreneurship. Though, with the advent of business competitiveness digital entrepreneurship is skyrocketing towards massive total adoption of artificial intelligence, fintech startup and crypto-currency and blockchain (Anderbjork & Martell, 2021). While, the sustainable innovation embraces the triple P’s concept on people, planet and profit that is in alignment with green entrepreneurship. Basically, the sustainable innovation pursuit the Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) seventeen fundamentals focus of the United Nation. Moreover, the entrepreneurs are always struggling for cost-leadership, price-leadership, and market share leadership; the truth is that innovation makes the difference. Thus, the nexus of innovation is centered on content innovation and context innovation that boost entrepreneur in the market survival operationally and strategically.

## 4.1. Innovation

Entrepreneurship deals with the art and science of creation, idea generation, invention of the future to the present and innovation of tangible and intangible beneficial resources to mankind. Oyìnlola et al., (2023) argued strongly that the business process can only be achieved through innovation. Ovharhe (2023a,b) opined that innovation is the practical implementation of corporate and beneficial ideas that result in the introduction of new tangible and intangible or improvement in offering goods or services that have value and treasure. He further categorized innovation into simplex and complex innovation. The simplex for example is the product, process, service, administrative, market, digital, social and lot more. While the complex are transformation, systematic, sustainable and other form of hybrid and multi-dimensional innovation. Innovation is the new life blood flow of entrepreneurship, meaning if there is no innovation, there is no concept of entrepreneurs that will be in existence. The passion, vision, mission and fusion propelling force of entrepreneurship are pivoted on innovation (Ovharhe, 2023; Kanyi, Wanjau & Kyalo, 2023).

La Verne (2023) as supported by Drucker pointed out that innovation is the driving force behind progress in any industrial transformation and systematic process. It is the process of developing new and better ways of doing things, from developing new tangible and intangible ideas implementation to improving existing ones. Innovation can be sparked by a variety of factors, including new technologies, changing customer needs, and emerging market trends in the business world, institutions and society at large. Though, entrepreneurship is the process of turning innovative idea into sustainable and successful business enterprise. It involves identifying a market need, developing a product, process, technology, structure and service that meet need, and then creating a business model that allows the product or service to be sold and scaled. Entrepreneurs are the individuals who take on the risks and challenges of starting a new business, and they are the ones who bring new products and services to market.

Jain (2023) said innovation is the process of bringing about new ideas, methods, products, services, or solutions into valuable existence that have a significant positive impact and value. It involves transforming creative concepts into tangible outcomes that improve efficiency, and effectiveness, or address unmet needs.

Innovation is not limited to technological advancements and encompasses novel approaches to problem-solving, processes, organizational practices, or business model innovations. At its core, innovation involves challenging the status quo, thinking outside the box, and taking calculated risks to drive progress and achieve breakthrough outcomes (Ntandoyenkosi, 2023).

Innovation is driven by a combination of factors, including curiosity, creativity, and the desire for improvement. It requires a mindset that embraces change, welcomes ideation, and encourages experimentation. Innovation can occur in various contexts, such as business, science, technology, social sectors, or public services. It can lead to economic growth, social progress, improved quality of life, and sustainable development (Kanyi, Wanjau & Kyalo, 2023).

Jain (2023) later portray that innovation can take various forms, and different types of innovation serve as discussed beneath.

- Product Innovation: This involves developing new or improved products or services. It can include innovations in functionality, features, design, performance, or packaging. It aims to create value for customers by addressing their needs, solving problems, or introducing novel and desirable offerings.

- Process Innovation: This is improving the efficiency, effectiveness, or quality of internal processes within an organization. It involves rethinking and redesigning workflows, technologies, and systems to streamline operations, reduce costs, enhance productivity, or improve the delivery of products or services. Process innovation often leads to increased operational efficiency and competitive advantage.

- Service Innovation: This anchor developing new or improved services, delivery methods, or customer experiences. It focuses on enhancing the value and satisfaction customers derive from service interactions. Service innovation can include innovations in service design, customization, accessibility, convenience, or personalization. It aims to meet evolving customer expectations and create a competitive edge.

- Sustainable Innovation: This orchestrates developing and implementing new products, services, technologies, or business models that have a positive environmental, social, and economic impact. It involves finding creative and efficient solutions to address pressing challenges, such as climate change, resource depletion, pollution, inequality, and poverty.

- Social Innovation: This fine-tuned the development and implementation of novel solutions to address social, cultural, economic, or environmental challenges. It involves the creation and adoption of new ideas, products, services, or approaches that result in positive societal impact and sustainable change.
