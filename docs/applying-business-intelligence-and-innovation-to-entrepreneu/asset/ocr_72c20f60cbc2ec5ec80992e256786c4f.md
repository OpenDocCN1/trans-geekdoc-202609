# Challenges and Opportunities in OSCM for Entrepreneurs

While there are advantages, to operating in the field of Operations and Supply Chain Management (OSCM) entrepreneurs face challenges along the way. These challenges include disruptions in the supply chain unpredictable changes in demand and evolving market dynamics (Castillo, 2018). Additionally, with global supply chains becoming increasingly complex entrepreneurs must have an understanding of factors, regulatory shifts, and environmental considerations. However, these challenges also present opportunities for innovation. Entrepreneurs who can proactively tackle these obstacles and take advantage of emerging trends can gain an edge (Elkington and Hartigan 2008). For instance, incorporating sustainability practices into the supply chain is not only a choice but also a strategic move since environmentally conscious consumers are driving market preferences. The emergence of e-commerce and the digitalization of business operations open up avenues for exploration (Elkington and Hartigan, 2008). The rise of platforms has reshaped traditional supply chain dynamics through, consumer models. Entrepreneurs can leverage these changes to build responsive supply chains that meet evolving consumer expectations (Scherbakov and Silkina 2021).
