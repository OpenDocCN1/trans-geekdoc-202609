# SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY, INNOVATION, AND ENTREPRENEURSHIP POLICY FRAMEWORKS

The activities of its stakeholders are directed by specific policy support in order to create an environment that can promote innovations. The push toward technology-driven activities has been the focus of worldwide science, technology, and innovation policy in the previous two decades due to the rise in the number of technology entrepreneurs. The bulk of policy tools attempts to lower regulatory barriers to entrepreneurship due to the high risk involved and the longer gestation period in the technology-driven entrepreneurial process. It entails aiding in the form of improved access to money, information, and training while fostering an entrepreneurial culture. The support systems and fresh stakeholders that are available to technology-driven entrepreneurs have changed the entrepreneurial environment. The regional economies have been more significantly impacted by technological entrepreneurship Global Startup Ecosystem Report (2023). Due to this, regional economic strategies have been altered to incorporate entrepreneurial activities.

Adding the entrepreneurial economy model to the current science, technology, and innovation policies will cause volatility. Innovation is required in the setting of competitive dynamics in place of assurance and customization is required in place of expertise. The science, technology, and innovation policies, up until recently, were centered on the work being done in the specialist fields. However, as the economy shifts toward unpredictability, the entrepreneurial stakeholders must adapt and customize in order to master the art of flexibility (Nicotra et al., 2018; Romano et al., 2014). Interventions in policy must be adjusted with the necessary indicators.
