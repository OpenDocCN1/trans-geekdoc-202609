+   **Market Intelligence.** This is a surveillance gathering about the product folio or market mix (product, price, promotion and place) use to ascertain the market share, customer relationship management, market analysis and market structure (Jėna, 2019).

+   **Innovation Intelligence:** Innovativeness is the centre of business intelligence assurance and excellence. The key concept of competitive intelligence and business intelligence must be interface with product innovation, administrative innovation, process innovation and technology innovation as concepts that trigger transformation in the enterprise growth and development. This approach is suitable in enterprise that deals with consignment inventory system (Ahunanya, Ovharhe, Emenike & Otto, 2022a,b; Ovharhe, Ahunanya & Woko, 2022).

+   **Sales Intelligence.** The forecasting of sales trend in the enterprise is a vital data analysis. Currently, big data, machine learning, data virtualization and data science can assist in computing sales quotas, sales territories and sales metric for judgment credibility and authentication. The data board shows competitive bench marking of the business failure and success. (Rahchamani & All, 2019).

+   **Procurement and Supply Chain Intelligence.** This is about satisfying the client from the forward and backward integration. The procurement on enterprise resource planning and supply chain is very relevant for the sustainability, survival and success of any business (Effa, Ovharhe, Ezirim & Igwe, 2023a,b; Handfield., 2023).

+   **ESG Intelligence:** This is intelligence tool which is an industry agnostic business intelligence platform, used to assist companies across all sectors monitor and report their Environmental, Social and Governance performance. It could exist in form of extranet. This is more application of strategic intelligence to analyze environmental data generation (Maune, 2022).

Generally, competitive intelligence focus on operational information and tactical analysis from various systematic sources such as news media, customer feedback and competitive parity analysis, professional experts in specific business, trade shows and conferences, symposium, government records, public filings, indexes and corporate dossier (Artur, 2020). Furthermore, competitive intelligence focuses on collecting, processing and analyzing sensitive data about your competition and client feedback to gain an advantage or leadership edge in the enterprise (Zighed et al., 2023).
