Majority of management decisions entrepreneurs need in start-up operations are programmed in the enterprise because of its implication on daily operational activities. On the contrary, non-programmed decisions deals with functions that are exceptional or nonrecurring to management, and they are often made under strategic conditions which involve so much ambiguity that specific procedures or programs are not available. These are done by the strategic (top level) management like the chief executive officer (CEO), general management (GM), director general (DG), chief medical director [CMD (in hospitals)], chairman medical advisory committee [CMAC (in hospitals)], among others (Dewar et al., 2022). Therefore, managers who must make non-programmed decisions rely on tough judgment, cognitive innovativeness, brain storming, creativity and strategic intuition (Reeves & Whitaker, 2022).

Decision making is an operational and strategic process of initiating ideas, design framework and analyzing basic alternatives with possible remedies to make a best choice from selective outcomes. Most decisions are made in response to a problem that requires high level of intelligence. These might be in need of sales intelligence, market intelligence and production intelligence (Ovharhe & Odegeli, 2024).

Zhou et al., (2023) posit chaotic processes that fathom entrepreneurs and management to invoke concept of decision support system being buttress by business intelligence applications and complex innovations. Decision support system is a computerized program used to support determinations, unbiased sound judgments and analytic courses of action in a business enterprise.

Decision support system (DSS) is synchronized information system that supports enterprise decision-making functions in merchandising activities. DSSs serve the management, operations and planning levels of an enterprise which help entrepreneurship and managerial decision making about remedies to problems solving that may be rapidly changing and not easily specified in advance (i.e., structural, unstructured and semi-structured decision problems). Decision support systems can be either fully computerized or human-powered, or a combination of both (Kozyrkov, 2023).

While academics have perceived DSS as a tool to support decision making processes, DSS users it as decision intelligence on a dashboard tool to facilitate enterprise processes (O’Callaghan, 2023). DSS include any systematic phenomena that might boost decision making and some DSS include a decision-making software component.
