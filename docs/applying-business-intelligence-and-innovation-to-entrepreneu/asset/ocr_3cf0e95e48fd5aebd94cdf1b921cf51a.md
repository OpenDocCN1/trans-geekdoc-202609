Ovharhe (2022, 2023) identified some common characteristics of entrepreneurs (champiopreneurs), which include:

+   1. **Charisma:** Most entrepreneurs have potentials that are like inborn talent which is very difficult for people to practice or imitate. Charisma is special gifts, skills and competence entrepreneurs possess that are priceless which makes them to be a resource and treasureable talent (i.e. singing, dancing, see the unseen by the ordinary) (Ovharhe, Woko & Ogolo, 2021). Also, these may be attributed to individuals like King David, Samson, Chinwe Achebe, Celine Dion, J. P Morgan, Rocky Feller, Joanne Rowling and Michael Jackson. We have charisma leaders in business like Nicholas Tesla, Elon Mask, Bill Gate, Steve Job etc (Ovharhe, Okolo, Woko & Igbokwe, 2022).

+   2. **Spirit of Enthusiasm with Burning Passion:** Strong consistence zeal that catches fire within individuals is factors that make them to stand and not discourage in turbulence and competitive time in the business world. Passion is the driver and inspiration to any entrepreneur, because in the times of high storm and dynamism in the business environment, the vital tool that is needed is the spirit of passion. Passion is the rhythm that every entrepreneur curtails to sustain, survive and succeed in business.
