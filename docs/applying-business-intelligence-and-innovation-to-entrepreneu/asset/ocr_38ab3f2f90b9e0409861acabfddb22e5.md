# THE THEORETICAL FOUNDATIONS OF DESIGN THINKING IN ENTREPRENEURIAL INNOVATION

The idea and practise of “design thinking,” which has roots in many fields including industrial design, engineering, and architecture, has developed into a crucial strategy in the entrepreneurial world (Johannsson-Sköldberg, 2013). Its rise to prominence is ascribed to its human-centric approach to problem-solving, which has a profound resonance with the opportunities and challenges faced by entrepreneurs in today’s fast-paced business climate. In this thorough investigation, we will delve into the theoretical underpinnings of design thinking and how it fits with the entrepreneurial process, offering insights into how it enables businesspeople to innovate, create, and succeed. Design thinking is an all-encompassing strategy that draws influence from various fields of study. Its roots can be seen in the innovative and iterative methods used in industrial design, where the demands and experiences of users are crucial to the development of products.
