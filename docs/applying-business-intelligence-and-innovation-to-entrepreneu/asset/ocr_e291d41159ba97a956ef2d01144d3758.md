# Effects of Transactional Leadership on Environmental Sustainability

Transactional leadership has been the subject of extensive research concerning its impact on organizational performance and employee behavior. The findings of Kafetzopoulos and Gotzamani (2022) indicate that this leadership style has a positive influence on talent work motivation and performance. However, Khan and Jantan (2022) discovered that transactional leadership has adverse effects on innovative work behavior. Moreover, Chuan's (2022) research revealed that the use of this leadership style results in negative consequences for non-financial performance in the multi-level marketing industry. These studies emphasize the importance of considering the pros and cons of transactional leadership carefully.Additionally, Tanveer et al.Tanveer et al. (2021) explored the impact of environmental sustainability and financial resource utilization on firms' financial performance, indicating the mediating role of leadership styles, including transactional leadership. Additionally, some authors discovered that transactional leadership enhanced the link between stress and unfavourable conduct, lessening the environment's impact on workplaces (Yao et al., 2014).

The references provided in this chapter collectively suggest that the effects of transactional leadership on environmental sustainability and organizational performance are complex, with both positive and negative impacts. The influence of transactional leadership on employee behavior, talent motivation, and organizational performance varies across different contexts and industries.
