# # Human Requirements for Adopting Business Intelligence

The human requirements for Business Intelligence encompass a diverse set of skills that should be possessed by business analysts, the individuals forming the technical team responsible for designing and operating the enterprise’s Business Intelligence system. Small and medium-sized industrial enterprises often face limitations in having individuals with high-level technical skills due to their limited human resources. Additionally, Business Intelligence, business analysis, and data analysis are considered highly specialized and precise fields, making training individuals in these areas a significant challenge for management.

Bert Brijds identifies five types of skills that should be present in a business analyst:

+   * **Data Knowledge:** Business analysts should master linking data quality to master data management, starting from data profiling to analyzing data proportions. This requires in-depth knowledge of processes responsible for creating, using, or modifying data.

+   * **Application Knowledge:** involves proficiency in working with Business Intelligence applications capturing real events and transactions within the organization, such as invoices, purchase and sales orders, etc. Familiarity with Customer Relationship Management (CRM), Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP), sales channels, and Online Analytical Processing (OLAP) is crucial as they are primary data sources.

+   * **Process Knowledge:** acquiring knowledge related to the nature of the organization’s processes is one of the most critical skills for business analysts. Understanding business processes is vital as they are significant sources of data.
