# **Women and Minority Entrepreneurship**

Research explores the experiences and challenges faced by women and minority entrepreneurs. It aims to understand the factors that influence their success and identify ways to promote diversity and inclusion in entrepreneurship.

# **International Entrepreneurship**

Cross-border entrepreneurship is an evolving field of study. Research investigates the motivations and challenges of international expansion, including market entry strategies, cultural considerations, and the role of technology in globalization.

# **Technology and Digital Entrepreneurship**

The digital transformation has brought technology-driven entrepreneurship to the forefront. Research examines the impact of digitalization on business models, e-commerce, and emerging trends in areas such as blockchain, artificial intelligence, and e-health.

# **Entrepreneurship Policy and Regulation**

The regulatory environment plays a critical role in entrepreneurship. Research evaluates the effectiveness of policies and regulations in supporting or hindering entrepreneurial activities, with a focus on areas like taxation, intellectual property, and business registration.

# **Family Business and Succession**

Research explores the unique challenges and opportunities associated with family-owned businesses. Studies investigate succession planning, governance structures, and the long-term sustainability of these enterprises.

# **Failure and Resilience**

Failure is an inherent part of entrepreneurship, and research acknowledges its role in the learning process. Studies examine the factors contributing to business failure and the strategies entrepreneurs employ to recover and thrive.
