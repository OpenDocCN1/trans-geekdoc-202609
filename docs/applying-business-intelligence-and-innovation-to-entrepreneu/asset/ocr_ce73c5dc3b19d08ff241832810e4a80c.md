+   2. Authority-Compliance Management (9.1): This type of leadership style dramatized where the leader of the enterprise is focused more concerned about productivity rather than the emotional and social premium of employee. The leaders in this sense do not care for about individual lifestyle pattern. This is a game of hiring and firing kind of leadership if anyone messes up with the strict rules and fundamental policy of the organization. The leader talks more about man-hour and instrumentations (i.e. device, machine, material, finance) on productivity (Northouse, 2018); Yadav et al., 2023). The leader attitude is; ‘it’s cool out there down town’, if you don’t like the job and leadership pattern you quit. This kind of job may not be favorable for intrapreneurs and entrepreneur that have huge competition because there might be lack of business intelligence, innovation, creativity and business opportunities, perhaps their opposition might use it as attacked against them. Entrepreneurs with this attitude which is a starter might not survive in the long-term. This kind of leadership style is for lean startup or SME’s, but favorable where the entrepreneur want to sustain is business growth with huge compensation and enormous fringe benefits with bonus tighten to the job functions, despise the autocratic nature of the leadership style the enterprise might succeed (Alqahtani & Uslay, 2023).

+   3. Impoverished Management (1,1): This pattern of leadership is the unexpected in any workplace because it ruins the entire enterprise from the roof to the foundation. It poses negation attributes in all ramifications because the concern for people is very poor and the concern for productivity is much obscured. It is a type of leadership style which occurs where there are high needs for distributive or decentralized forms on management. The task, responsibility and job function is specified to certain work scope without much interference from the centre. This can occurs in holdings and subsidiaries, religious organizations and large conglomerate with multi-nationals. This is good for leader where they may be community disturbance, turbulence, hazards, pandemic and inconsistency in exchange rate, inflation rate and interest rate (Helmold, 2021; Ovharhe et al., 2021). This kind of leadership style does not follow the steam of any entrepreneurship pattern, talk less of champiopreneurship.
