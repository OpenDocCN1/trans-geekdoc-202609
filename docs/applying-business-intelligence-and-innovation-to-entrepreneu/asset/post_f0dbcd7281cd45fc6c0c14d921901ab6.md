- 2. **Imitating Entrepreneurs:** The imitating entrepreneurs are those who immediately copy the new inventions made by the innovative entrepreneurs. These do not make any innovations by themselves; they just imitate the technology, processes, methods pioneered by others. These entrepreneurs are found in the places where there is a lack of resources or industrial base due to which no new innovations could be made. Thus, they are suitable for the underdeveloped regions where they can imitate the combinations of inventions already well established in the developed regions, in order to bring a boom in their industry.

- 3. **Fabian Entrepreneurs:** Mediocrity and skeptics are the order of this entrepreneur status. They are very mild and careful; do not want to be seen making mistakes. At first they are not initiative and innovation, but spend time to study the trend of motion on event before they strike their actions in the business place. They follow the success rate of others in the invention and change transformation before they operate theirs. They strongly believe in concept testing and test marketing of their product or services and were very sure of the success and survival rate before they launch attack on the market (Ratten, 2023).

- 4. **Drone Entrepreneurs:** These are more mediocrity entrepreneurs that do not want to adopt and implement change in the business environment because of their conservative nature. They strongly choreograph the rhythm of ‘whatever will be, will be’ in their business because they are afraid to make loss. Also A.B. - 5. **Nichers:** These are entrepreneurs with passion but do not have the required resources or managerial acumen to capture the market. But, they decided to start from a very low scale in the business ladder trying to wait for the appropriate opportunity to grow. You see them starting with roadside business before owning a business platform.

- 6. **Followers:** They are very strong and ready to climb to the business top at any given opportunity. They may be looking for connections on the business climax and political structure. They are always scanning in the business environment for any slight opportunity to launch their attack. Their target is not the market nichers rather the challengers and leaders on top the ladder in the business environment. They are not satisfied with where they are, but any mistake by those at the top, they will take over.

- 7. Challengers (counter entrepreneurs): These are the guerillas and leopards in the business environment. Their arrows are targeted to be in the control of the market share so they can occupy the first position to be the market leaders in the enterprise. Their strategy is to grab some market share, territories and sales quotas from the ‘Followers’, then proceed to launch full scale attack on the market leaders. Their first priority is not profit maximization, but to gain in control of the leadership of the market. This is where the key drama of the competitive parity orchestrates.

- 8. Leaders: This is where the eagle and lion exist in the business climax. They are ready to soar to any high altitude with enthusiasm, ruggedness and competitiveness to occupy the leaders ladder at all cost. They seek to lobby and manipulate all agency, legal and political structure, client, customer, vendor among others. They dream like the Ultrapreneurs and Extrapreneurs with resources to avail their plight.

## 2.1.2. Characteristics of Entrepreneur

Talent, innovation-skills and potentials are never enough to attain or experience sustainability, survival and success in entrepreneurs’ field of endeavors or interest and passion. A lot of entrepreneurs possess these traits but do not have success and never become champiopreneurs in their various fields. This is because they lack certain character which was never exhibited in the field of specialty.

Every entrepreneur possesses trait, feature and attributes that make them to be seen as champion in their environment. This is the reason they are recognized as ‘champiopreneur’ when they attain or experience sustainability, survival and success. Just like the conventional entrepreneur, champiopreneur are characterized with charisma, spirit of enthusiasm with burning passion, visionary on focus, risk culture, creator, learning, leading the way, optimistic and opportunist of changing the future, resiliency, building the bridge and idea generator (Blessley & Mudambi, 2022; Ovharhe, 2022; 2023b, 2023c). This also means infant that are potential champion should be characterized with these attributes. Infant possesses all these characteristics as inert model wired and locked within them (Ovharhe & Woko, 2024a,b).

Ovharhe (2022, 2023) identified some common characteristics of entrepreneurs (champiopreneurs), which include:

- 1. **Charisma:** Most entrepreneurs have potentials that are like inborn talent which is very difficult for people to practice or imitate. Charisma is special gifts, skills and competence entrepreneurs possess that are priceless which makes them to be a resource and treasureable talent (i.e. singing, dancing, see the unseen by the ordinary) (Ovharhe, Woko & Ogolo, 2021). Also, these may be attributed to individuals like King David, Samson, Chinwe Achebe, Celine Dion, J.P. Morgan, Rockefeller, Joanne Rowling and Michael Jackson. We have charisma leaders in business like Nikola Tesla, Elon Musk, Bill Gates, Steve Jobs etc (Ovharhe, Okolo, Woko & Igbokwe, 2022).

- 2. **Spirit of Enthusiasm with Burning Passion:** Strong consistency zeal that catches fire within individuals are factors that make them to stand and not discourage in turbulence and competitive time in the business world. Passion is the driver and inspiration to any entrepreneur, because in the times of high storm and dynamism in the business environment, the vital tool that is needed is the spirit of passion. Passion is the rhythm that every entrepreneur curtails to sustain, survive and succeed in business. Therapeutic passion is highly a stimulating and motivating concept which enables entrepreneurs in tough times to withstand every catastrophe and risk in times of business turbulence especially during post covid-19 pandemic (Ovharhe, 2021, 2022a, 2023b). Passion is the architect of vision, mission and fusion for entrepreneurs to achieve their entrepreneurial journey. Passion is the pivoting fusion on innovation, creativity and perseverance (Duckworth; Biraglia & Kadile, in Ovharhe, 2023b). Passion is the anchor of entrepreneurial alert that makes entrepreneur to burn with fusion which fuels the vision and mission not to die (Nordström, Sirén, Thorgren & Vincent; O'Keefe, Dweck & Walton in Ovharhe, 2023a).

- 3. **Optimistic and Opportunist of Changing the Future**: Entrepreneur must always have a positive mindset no matter the storms. Entrepreneurs should debunk fears at all times. They should be adaptive to change, flexibility and consistency with the mission of the enterprise. This is only how the future can be changed. Changing the future is ability to invent the unseen to be seen with added benefits to the society at large (Ratten, 2023).

- 4. **Learning, Unlearning and Relearning**: Grossly, learning can be viewed in the prospect of entrepreneurship as the apt of the acquisition, storage, retention, utilization and dissemination of knowledge that transforms to a change in behavior which yields input to projected output (Ovharhe, Woko & Ogolo, 2022; Ratten, 2023). Entrepreneurial learning is generated from education, training and development programs. Learning could also be formal, semi-formal and informal. The key factor is change and transformation. All learning could lead to transformation on positive, zero or negative learning. But, the benefit is unlearning and relearning with positive result intentions. Relearning occurs when the entrepreneurs learn new strategies, tactics, patterns and approach of achieving the dream, vision and mission in the entrepreneurial journey. Whereas, unlearning is the total abandonment of the knowledge because it's obsolete, no link and supply chain of ideas.

- 5. **Risk Culture**: Entrepreneurs have the ability to take on a significant amount of risk with the goal of profiting from it. Risk culture is a set of standards, conformity, norms, attitudes and behaviors related to awareness, alertness, avoidance, tolerance and absorption of risks. It shapes management and employees' day-to-day decisions and has an impact on the risks they take. The plight of every therapeutic entrepreneur is the ability to manage risk (Ovharhe, 2022a). This is the necessity of risk culture to the sense of humor that enables entrepreneurs to develop risk alert and risk awareness (Chibiuke & Ovharhe, 2022). More so, risk culture is disciplinary value system with caution to deal and handle risk in the enterprise environment.
