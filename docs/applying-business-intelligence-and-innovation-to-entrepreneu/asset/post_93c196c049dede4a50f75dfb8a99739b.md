### b. Tactical Intelligence

These are intermediate or functional issues and concern for department level challenges: agile factors (changes to new technology and artificial intelligence), intermittent cash flow analysis on inflow and outflow. New idea generation, market development, forecasting, product development, leasing and procurement of sophisticated devices, machinery and equipment, sales quotas, launching of new prototype, test marketing, concept testing, expansion of market share, sales territories, service quality delivery etcetera (Lim, 2023).

Tactical intelligence is the middle field and heart of combat intelligence of creative and innovative information required for decision support system in the competitive parity which orchestrates sustainability, survival and success in the business enterprise. Britannica (2024) pointed out that tactical intelligence primarily respond to the plans of the business enterprise need to executes for positive resultant outcome in the competitive parity by employing the most appropriate actions.

Tactical intelligence is specific information about a target to accomplish mission and corporate goals on departmental or functional level in the business enterprise, such as in production, telecommunication, purchasing and supply, logistics, marketing and finance. Tactical intelligence is medium-term or intermittent period which gives credible knowledge store, knowledge memory and knowledge dissemination of business enterprise at all parameters. It is the fundamental based of the decision support system of the business enterprise (Wakuthii, 2024).

Tactical intelligence is focused on support to marketplace operations at the functional level of the business enterprise where the competitive parity is tense. Tactical intelligence is in liaison with the units or group level of the business enterprise to smoothen and swing into action for revenue generation, frequency of patronage, return on asset, return on investment and return on equity, customer satisfaction and feedback (ScienceDirect, 2024). This is the reasons most conglomerates, large corporation, multinationals sees tactical intelligence as secret and mysteries intelligence, because of the roles in secretly generated wisdom beyond the limits of formal reasoning that makes uncertain estimates less to be certainty, and consequently generates political, business, economical, strategic and operational advantages in the market competition. ### c. Strategic Intelligence

These are the remedies to long-term bottlenecks and pitfalls that surround the business enterprise. It is anchored by top leadership functions of the enterprise as highest executive order. The challenges can be merger and acquisition, reconstruction, take-over, conglomerate diversification, vertical integration, horizontal integration, forward integration, backward integration, enterprise resource planning, ESG intelligence, strategic intelligence, supply chain management system, bench-marking, pension and gratuity, building of new industries, franchising, licensing, consignment inventory system and automation/robotic/artificial intelligence (Alqahtani & Uslay, 2023).

Britannica (2024) postulate that strategic intelligence is long-term information and decision making which is used to formulate policy and plans at interfacing the global networking. Strategic intelligence is the outlook for the future prospect of the business enterprise which authenticates the business excellence, climate change, artificial intelligence, machine learning, data virtualization, big data analytics, data science, leadership and champion entrepreneurship (World Economic Forum, 2024, Ovhare & Woko, 2024a). Entrepreneurs are more concern about the integration of strategic intelligence outlook on innovation skills, creativity, customer feedback, new business opportunities, growth and development (Emergent Risk International, 2024).

### 3.2 Decision Support System

Entrepreneurs cannot function effectively and efficiently without conceptualized decision making to accomplish her corporate goals. The enterprise depends on its long-term and short-term decision making to regulate statutory framework for flow of fiduciary responsibilities and oriented task (Kozyrkov, 2023). In managerial expression, the short-term decision making can also be regarded as operational or programmable decisions, while the long-term decision making can be view as strategic or non-programmable decisions. Programmed decisions are functions conducted by operational (low level) management like the supervisors and foremen which can be utilized by entrepreneurs in the short-term as repetitive and routine merchandising activities to solve through clear-cut mechanical procedures, such as applying the formidable rules and sound judgment to find the best solution and possible remedies within the specific available time frame (O’Callaghan, 2023). Majority of management decisions entrepreneurs need in start-up operations are programmed in the enterprise because of its implication on daily operational activities. On the contrary, non-programmed decisions deals with functions that are exceptional or nonrecurring to management, and they are often made under strategic conditions which involve so much ambiguity that specific procedures or programs are not available. These are done by the strategic (top level) management like the chief executive officer (CEO), general management (GM), director general (DG), chief medical director [CMD (in hospitals)], chairman medical advisory committee [CMAC (in hospitals)], among others (Dewar et al., 2022). Therefore, managers who must make non-programmed decisions rely on tough judgment, cognitive innovativeness, brain storming, creativity and strategic intuition (Reeves & Whitaker, 2022).

Decision making is an operational and strategic process of initiating ideas, design framework and analyzing basic alternatives with possible remedies to make a best choice from selective outcomes. Most decisions are made in response to a problem that requires high level of intelligence. These might be in need of sales intelligence, market intelligence and production intelligence (Ovharhe & Odegeli, 2024).

Zhou et al., (2023) posit chaotic processes that fathom entrepreneurs and management to invoke concept of decision support system being buttress by business intelligence applications and complex innovations. Decision support system is a computerized program used to support determinations, unbiased sound judgments and analytic courses of action in a business enterprise.

Decision support system (DSS) is synchronized information system that supports enterprise decision-making functions in merchandising activities. DSSs serve the management, operations and planning levels of an enterprise which help entrepreneurship and managerial decision making about remedies to problems solving that may be rapidly changing and not easily specified in advance (i.e., structural, unstructured and semi-structured decision problems). Decision support systems can be either fully computerized or human-powered, or a combination of both (Kozyrkov, 2023).

While academics have perceived DSS as a tool to support decision making processes, DSS users it as decision intelligence on a dashboard tool to facilitate enterprise processes (O’Callaghan, 2023).
