+   7. Challengers (counter entrepreneurs): These are the guerillas and leopards in the business environment. Their arrows are targeted to be in the control of the market share so they can occupy the first position to be the market leaders in the enterprise. Their strategy is to grab some market share, territories and sales quotas from the ‘Followers’, then proceed to lunch full scale attack on the market leaders. Their first priority is not profit maximization, but to gain in control of the leadership of the market. This is where the key drama of the completive parity orchestrates.

+   8. Leaders: This is where the eagle and lion exist in the business climax. There are ready to sow to any high altitude with enthusiasm, ruggedness and competitiveness to occupy the leaders ladder at all cost. They seeks to lobby and manipulate all agency, legal and politic structure, client, customer, vendor among others. They dream like the Ultrapreneurs and Extrapreneurs with resources to avail their plight.

# 2.1.2. Characteristics of Entrepreneur

Talent, innovation-skills and potentials are never enough to attain or experience sustainability, survival and success in entrepreneurs’ field of endeavors’ or interest and passion. A lot of entrepreneurs possess these traits but do not have success and never becomes champiopreneurs in their various fields. This is because they lack certain character which was never exhibited in the field of specialty.

Every entrepreneur possesses trait, feature and attributes that make them to be seen as champion in their environment. This is the reasons they are recognized as ‘champiopreneur’ when they attain or experience sustainability, survival and success. Just like the conventional entrepreneur, champiopreneur are characterized with charisma, spirit of enthusiasm with burning passion, visionary on focus, risk culture, creator, learning, leading the way, optimistic and opportunist of changing the future, resiliency, building the bridge and idea generator (Blessley & Mudambi, 2022; Ovhare, 2022; 2023b, 2023c). This also means infant that are potential champion should be characterized with these attributes. Infant possesses all these characteristics as inert model wired and locked within them (Ovharhe & Woko, 2024a,b).
