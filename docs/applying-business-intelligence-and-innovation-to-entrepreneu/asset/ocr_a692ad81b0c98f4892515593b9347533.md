+   9. **Resiliency and Building the Bridge:** Entrepreneurs should have the slogan of forward ever and backwards never in the business stream. Letting the stream of cash-flow circulating in the short-term and long-term in the entrepreneurial journey is the dream of every entrepreneurs. Entrepreneur function is to build the stream of consistent wealth creation and value creation in every business opportunity. But sometime business owners should always protect and secured their bridge at all cost (Blessley & Mudambi, 2022). A broken bridge causes a lot to repairs and maintain. Resilience strategies that will aid like competitive advantage should be of great concern to every entrepreneur as they want to sustain and survive in business. Developing fighting strategy is good, but developing resiliency and building bridges should not be taken for granted by entrepreneurs. Building solid bridges for customer, client, vendor, government, legal framework, community, institutions and stakeholders at large is of necessity. More so, this strengthens and builds strategic bonds for relationship, partnership and customership.

+   10. **Communication Skills:** Liaison with all stakeholders is a vital business strategy for start-up. Whether is upwards communication (from bottom-top), downward communication (top-bottom) or diagonal communication (cross-sectional) the entrepreneur must learn how to input information and get feedback. Communication also could be external virtue that must be maintained for free flow and better understanding of business. Communication is the forces that bind all entity to oneness.

Communicating the purpose, vision and mission to the organization human resource is the credibility of success and consistency in the business function. Nevertheless, effective and efficient communication to clients and customer revealed the longevity of the enterprise in the turbulence times. Thus, effective and efficient communication is a competitive advantage to the enterprise. Entrepreneurs need to learn how to generate information for effective communication. Information should be from the confident source, reliable, precise, accurate, authentic, trusted, valid, verifiable, comprehensive and meaningful. If entrepreneurs should be accurate and eloquent with information virtue they will maintain credible communication skills. The business world needs good communicators for interface with employees, management, community, external environment (government, agencies and task) vendor, customer relations and the entire supply chain management system (Yadav et al., 2023).
