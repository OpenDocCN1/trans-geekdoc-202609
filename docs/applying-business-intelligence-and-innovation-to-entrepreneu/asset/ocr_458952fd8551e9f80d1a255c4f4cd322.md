The relationship, between innovation and EO is quite strong. This study describes the effects of BI and provide insights into how BI technologies contribute to fostering innovation generating ideas and developing products and services that benefit society as a whole. The findings from this research can be helpful for policymakers, business support groups and incubators in creating an environment that effectively integrates BI. This could potentially create an atmosphere for small businesses. The knowledge gained from research may also have an impact on programs by shaping the development of curricula that equip aspiring entrepreneurs and students, with the skills and knowledge to effectively utilize BI in their endeavours. Although not much research has been made on the influence of business intelligence on entrepreneurial orientation so this systematic literature review will give us an insight view of the possible gaps that can be covered by the upcoming researchers in this field.

# LIMITATIONS

In comparison to more established fields, these emerging fields may not have garnered as much attention from researchers as they should have. Only four databases have been used for this study, upcoming researches can other databases as well for their future studies. The 23 papers show that, in contrast to the allied themes of BI, relatively few of the studies have examined the direct influence of BI on EO. Articles published between 2010-2023 have only been reviewed. Time constraint is another limitation of this study.
