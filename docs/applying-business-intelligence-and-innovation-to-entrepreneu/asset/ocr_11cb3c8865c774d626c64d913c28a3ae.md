# 3. CONCEPT OF BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

The fundamental aim of business intelligence is to actualize precise, accurate and confident decision making. This is while authors argue that business intelligence is correlated to competitive intelligence and decision support system because the large amount of accurate data that has become more accessible allows business intelligence to synchronize (Huang, Savita & Zhong-jie, 2022).

Business intelligence combines analytic, data mining, data visualization, data tools, data infrastructure and best practices to help management and entrepreneurs makes more data-driven decision in the short-term and long-term (Horn, 2023). Business intelligence is also software that suggests business data and presents it in user-friendly views such as report, dashboard, chat, graph, metrics and framework. However, business intelligence could be well analyzed in entrepreneurship by integrating it with competitive intelligence and decision support system (Zhou, San, Liu & Kozyrkov, 2023).

Holori, Ahmadi, Khanmohammadi, Ranjbar and Kordlouie (2023) averred that business intelligence is a fundamental tool that assist management to evaluated business information on return on investment, return on equity, return on asset, return on capital employed, net present value, cost-benefit analysis and break-even analysis. By using the Structural Equation Model, business intelligence could yield tremendous output that boost enterprise performance in the small medium enterprise, large corporation, multinationals and conglomerates. Entrepreneur decision making should be authenticated and optimistic with the intervention of business intelligence.

# 3.1. Competitive Intelligence

Competitive intelligence can be diagrammatized with the phrase ‘know your enemy’ (Baskin, 2023). Competitive intelligence is not only utilized in profit-for-making business is also adopted by the military (for defense intelligence), medical personnel (for diagnostics, clinical findings and result analytic and information storage), institution (for tracking bench marking growth and comparative analysis on activity based trend) (Cekuls, 2022). This is the essence it is also regarded as corporate intelligence because of the aid to generate data, environmental scanning, scientific systematic investigation of facts and speed in decision making (Taranu, 2023; Cloutier, 2013).
