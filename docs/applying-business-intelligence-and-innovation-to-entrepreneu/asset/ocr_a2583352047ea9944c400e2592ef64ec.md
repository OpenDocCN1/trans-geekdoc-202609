# 2. ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND MANAGEMENT

Prima facie in the 18th century the Irish economist, Richard Cantillon dive into the term “entrepreneur”. Cantillon sees an entrepreneur as the person who buys factors services at certain prices with a view to selling its product at uncertain prices. But, Joseph Schumpeter deemed to be the father of entrepreneurship as argued by most scholars, came up with a paradigm shift with theories and concept to develop a modern features and attributes. Schumpeter averred that entrepreneurship is a process of creation of both intangible and tangible resource to generate wealth. His emphasizes was on the word ‘creation’. This is while the creative ability of every entrepreneur is to innovate, brain storm, invent and network from the past to the presence and future. This means entrepreneurs are creator of resources to satisfied human livelihood in the short-term and long-term. Being a creator makes entrepreneurs to becomes a ‘Champion’

In 2024 Eric Beato from Babson College, the renowned Entrepreneurship Institute with championopreneurship trait, laid emphasis on resilience and allship interface with championopreneur and leaders on applying business intelligence, creativity and innovation to achieve enterprise excellence. Breato (2024) and Ovharhe (2024) inclined that these factorial elements are expected to augment vital proceedings in empowering, proliferating and advancing infant-childhood, women and youth entrepreneurs beyond now and SDG towards championopreneurship. But, for entrepreneurs to be experience on sustainability, survival and success in the enterprise, there ought to be completive edge, business intelligence and competitive advantage. For these to be achieved certain planning, organizing, directing, motivating, staffing and control measures need to be in place. This will enable the entrepreneur to set metrics on targeted goals, strategies, policy, procedure, budget, implementation, monitoring and feedback.

This led to the synchronization between entrepreneurship and management, just as Schumpeter rings the bell of Entrepreneurship, Peter F. This represents that actual characteristic of entrepreneur as been a risk bearer. In the same vein the term is still believes to be go or enter (Entre), between (Prendre).
