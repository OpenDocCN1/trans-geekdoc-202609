According to another study’s authors (Oesterreich et al., 2022), future theory development should be guided by their increased grasp of the mechanisms resulting in big data analytics (BDA)-induced economic value. Prioritising the sociotechnical aspect of BDA is important for managers and decision-makers starting new BDA initiatives. The significance of social and technological elements in generating economic value from BDA is investigated in this research using a meta-analysis of 107 individual studies.

A bibliometric study (Rialti et al., 2019) has revealed that organisational performance and decision-making processes are greatly impacted by big data and BDA systems. Managers can utilise the forecasts generated by BDA systems regarding future trends to decide on sales, revenues, and production needs. Real-time consumer analytics from BDA enable managers to respond quickly to changing customer preferences. Managers can be better equipped to make decisions that improve organisational efficiency in the production and supply chain by using insights from BDA. BDA systems may provide managers with information about sales, manufacturing, and supply, allowing them to react quickly to changes and make wise choices. Also, enhancing the quality of information and facilitating its efficient exchange across supply chain networks makes an enterprise more capable of quickly and effectively responding to interruptions (Hyun et al., 2023).
