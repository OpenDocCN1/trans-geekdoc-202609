# SIGNIFICANCE OF ENTREPRENEURSHIP

The value of entrepreneurs is not limited to starting new companies. Entrepreneurship is a key factor in the expansion of the economy, the creation of new products, and the general progress of society (Blinova, 2022). Here, we’ll take a close look at some of its most salient features:

**Economic Growth and Job Creation:** Entrepreneurs are vital for economic growth and job creation. When people start enterprises, they not only employ people directly but also stimulate the growth of related sectors. Small and medium-sized firms (SMEs), which comprise the bulk of entrepreneurial initiatives, are often the principal producers of new jobs in an economy (Perrini and Vurro 2006).

**Innovation and Technological Advancement:** Entrepreneurs are frequently the first to introduce groundbreaking new technologies. They push innovation in technology forward by constantly looking for better ways to do things and fresh approaches to old issues. This quest results in the creation of cutting-edge products and services that advance numerous industries, such as medicine, computing, and green power (Janjić and Radenović 2019).

**Competition and Market Dynamism:** Market rivalry is boosted by entrepreneurial efforts. They provide a threat to established businesses and industries, which typically results in better goods, services, and support for consumers. Consumers may benefit from more options and more activity in the market as a result of increased competition (Sarkar et al., 2001).

**Economic Flexibility and Resilience:** The entrepreneurial spirit gives the economy more adaptability and stability. New businesses are better able to respond quickly to market shifts and structural shifts by testing out new markets and adjusting their business strategies (Thurik, 2009).

**Social Change and Community Development:** The drive to make a difference in the world is a driving force for many businesses. Entrepreneurship with a social mission seeks to improve society in areas such as health, education, and the environment. This approach can lead to considerable improvements in quality of life and community cohesion (Sarkar et al., 2001).

**Global Impact and Connectivity:** As a result of globalization, today’s business owners may make an international difference. They connect markets, stimulate international trade, and contribute to global innovation networks, making the globe more integrated (Fayolle, 2007).

**Personal Fulfillment and Skill Development:** People can follow their visions and find success by starting their businesses. Entrepreneurship is great for developing skills like critical thinking, innovation, leadership, and perseverance (Fayolle, 2007).
