A framework for ecosystem support based on the Babson Entrepreneurship Ecosystem Project seeks to develop world-class entrepreneurs (Isenberg, 2010) Babson Business Entrepreneurship focuses on six key areas that are essential for creating a strong business case at the national level, including a corporate governance culture characterized by social trends and success stories, a financial infrastructure that is accessible to entrepreneurs, and a high level of staff due to the quality of the educational systems used and the character of the staff. # GUJARAT ENTREPRENEURIAL ECOSYSTEM

Gujarat is one of those ecosystems where the state government has pledged to an enormous number of startups right from the seed stage by providing support with early-stage grants. Through student startup interventions at several technology and non-technology universities, Gujarat made systematic efforts at its pre-incubation layer. Gujarat’s ecosystem has been significantly shaped by ecosystem actors from the non-government sector, particularly from the perspectives of mentoring, early investment, and incubation. In the most recent Gujarat Budget, more than 200 crores were distributed to several departments of the Government of Gujarat to stimulate entrepreneurship. The state government has launched several programs to direct these financial resources.
