# a. Operational Intelligence

These are short-term challenges that occur within the period of one year which deals with day to day functions. They are problems encountered with the foremen or line operations that deal with intrapreneurs for executorships of the enterprise activities. These challenges include inventory control, repairs and maintenance, payment of allowances, customer satisfaction, market-cost pricing, product mix or company port-folio analysis, capital formation and fund raising, hiring and firing, fintech startup, crypto-currency, blockchain, creativity and sustainable innovation etcetera (Corsaro & D’Amico, 2022).
