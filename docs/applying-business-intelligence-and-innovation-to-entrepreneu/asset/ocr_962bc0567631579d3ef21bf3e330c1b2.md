Operations and supply chain management play a crucial role in the implementation phase. Entrepreneurs have the responsibility of ensuring the timely and accurate delivery of their products or services while keeping expenses to a minimum without sacrificing quality (Kumar, 2008). Logistics, stock control, vendor relations, and customer support are all part of this. In today's fast-paced business climate, the capacity to adapt and respond to supply chain difficulties is crucial (Korpysa et al., 2020). Human resource management expertise is also crucial for entrepreneurs. Successful businesses require leaders who can build and motivate teams, create positive work environments, and keep employees happy and productive (Hora and Dutta 2013). Business owners need to be aware of how technological developments affect their strategies, processes, and potential customer bases. Legal considerations also represent an integral aspect of the entrepreneurial scope (Rao C.H., 1971). Corporate governance, compliance, intellectual property rights, and employment law are just a few of the many areas of law that entrepreneurs must understand to run a successful business (Fosso Wamba et al., 2022). Mainly, entrepreneurship is multifaceted and calls for knowledge and abilities from many fields. It calls for a unique set of skills, including imagination, planning, skill, and flexibility. The job of the entrepreneur, facing new obstacles and taking advantage of possibilities in a dynamic global market, is always shifting as the business world develops.

# Importance of Entrepreneurship

+   **1 Economic Growth:** Entrepreneurs stimulate economic growth by creating jobs, introducing innovations, and boosting productivity. They play a critical role in generating new wealth and advancing economic development (Jengeta et al., 2021).

+   **2 Innovation and Technological Advancement:** Entrepreneurs often lead the way in technological advancements and innovation. They bring new products and services to the market, fostering a culture of innovation (Jacobs and Chase 2018).

+   **3 Social Change:** Through their ventures, entrepreneurs can address societal challenges and contribute to social change. They often drive progress in areas like sustainability, healthcare, and education (Jacobs and Chase 2018).

+   **4 Personal Fulfillment:** Entrepreneurship provides individuals with the opportunity to pursue their passions, achieve personal growth, and realize their creative potential (Bosma, 2013).

+   **5 Global Impact:** Entrepreneurs can have a global impact by creating solutions that transcend borders. This is especially relevant in the age of globalization and digital connectivity (Perrini and Vurro 2006).
