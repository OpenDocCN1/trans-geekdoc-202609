Additionally, the natural characteristics of small and medium-sized industrial enterprises, associated with the simplicity of their organizational structure, contribute more to activating the organizational agility feature through the adoption of Information and Communication Technology (ICT) and Business Intelligence (BI). Therefore, organizational requirements related to management, control, and coordination impose the granting of full authority to all individuals constituting each process, ensuring the addition of value to production during the planning, execution, and control stages. This includes granting complete permissions for utilizing the data and information provided by the Business Intelligence system to guarantee its employment in decision-making processes and avoiding secondary problems resulting from the absence of crucial information instantly. These requirements are strongly connected to achieving organizational agility, which imposes a type of coordination and harmony in obtaining crucial data and information from inside and outside the organization. These pertain to the cost and quality of products and their distribution to a specific number of managers, executives, and individuals for their use in decision-making.

# Technical Requirements for Adopting Business Intelligence

The technical requirements for Business Intelligence systems serve as their underlying infrastructure, enabling enterprise management to acquire, handle, manage, and use data and information in the areas of business analysis and decision-making. These requirements vary with Information Technology and Communication (ITC) assets but are closely integrated with them. The technical requirements for the successful adoption of Business Intelligence in small and medium-sized industrial enterprises differ from larger organizations. This is primarily due to the lower maturity level of Information Technology and Communication practices in small and medium-sized enterprises in general. This can be attributed to various factors such as limited resources, lower levels of expertise among individuals in the field of Information Technology and Communication, and a lack of a clear vision from management regarding the utilization of Information Technology and Communication in creating value.

Therefore, technical success requirements can be categorized into two main groups:

+   - Requirements associated with the maturity of Information Technology and Communication practices (ITC).
+   - Rechnical requirements specific to the Business Intelligence system itself.
