Star products give the enterprise huge competitive advantage over the competition because their threats are like arrow in the market environment. They give hope and pride to the enterprise with good corporate image and strong perceived good will. Entrepreneurs uses stars product to boost their reputation and corporate identity with business world (Alqahtani & Uslay, 2023).

Entrepreneur seeks to be called a champion. The championship entrepreneurs profile is all about becoming the market leader. The struggle and fight encountered by the champiopreneur to win the market share, sales territories and sales quotas is enveloped in capturing the business units to be the star in the marketplace. Star product needs a lot of advertisement which in the process publicized the enterprise other products or business units (Yadav, Tripathi, Tripathi, Ghosal, Kumar, Mandal & Singh, 2023). - 2. **Cash Cows:** Cash Cows identify products and services of the enterprise or business units that possesses extensive profane market share in the competitive mature stage. Whereas there is slow market growth but less investment capital or seed capital unlike the Star business units (Lim, 2023). Hence, Cash cow seems to generate rapid inflow of income, with tremendous net cash flow in the enterprise. The cash cow products are the bedrock or strength based of the enterprise. With the horn of the cash cow its can harm and attacked another product or business units that seems to be threat in the entire product portfolio of the competition. When the cash cow is close to the end of the maturity stage or decline stage of the business cycle, an aggressive product rejuvenization strategy should be integrated to be the champiopreneurship (Ovharhe et al., 2022).

Entrepreneurs use the horn of cash cow business units to protect and support offsprings of new product launched into the market industry. The cash cow can be use to feed problem children or wild cat (question mark) product to develop to star and cash cow before they can develop their horn to be self-defendant in the business fight. This is because the horn of the cash cow is very aggressive and dangerous in the business world during combat; it is like fighting in battle of the guerilla warfare. Every entrepreneur that wants to be champion leaders needs the horn to be alert to survive in the business world.

- 3. **Question Marks:** There are product or business units in enterprise portfolio that are behaving like wild cat or problem children. This is range of business unit which are tagged with questions marks. The enterprise is in tough dilemma phrase on what to do or how to cope with them. The problem is that entrepreneur and intrapreneur are always faces with doubt and worries about what to do with them. The questions every entrepreneur or intrapreneurs ask are that; should we stay here with it? Should we go forwards or retrench? This is because the question marks (business units) have very low relative competitive market share with responses to high business growth rate. The question mark or wild cat product required huge investment capital to sustain and survive her for success to expand the market share intensively (Ratten, 2023). This can calls for the champiopreneurship for sustainability, survival and success to holds the business units to illuminate the market.

Entrepreneurs need more attention and market research to uphold the business units of the questions mark. This is while it is also called problem children business units or product. Problem child is perceived to be very sick, needs diagnostic intervention and immunization for survival. Entrepreneurs are worried for how long to continued this intervention because it’s a drain of human, material, financial and physical resources. But the good news is that most entrepreneurial journey and dreams begin with the question mark product before they start nurturing is for growth before building extensive market share in the business environment. If entrepreneurs and intrapreneurs ignore the problem children (questions mark) they will just be looking and behaving like wild cat and eventually turn to dogs, which might be a total loss or failure for the entrepreneur. Hence the lean startup principles need to be in place before commencement by champiopreneurs (Ovharhe, 2023a,b,c).

- 4. Dogs: The cannibalistic products or vampire business units that drain the life cycle of the enterprise as proscribe to be ‘dogs’. When a product drains the organizational life cycle it can eventually sink the boat of the enterprise in the deep ocean without safety instrumental tools. Dogs are vampire products because they feed on the flesh and new blood of the enterprise portfolio. Entrepreneur should be very careful in dealing with this kind of business units (dogs) that make them to have low market share, high debt leverage, poor customer feedback/relation and no patronage (Ovharhe et al., 2022). Dogs are epileptic and paralytic products that do feed on their host until they drain it to death. Dogs are business unit with weak market shares and market growth that do not generate cash and attract capital formation for investment venture in the business environment. It is advisable to invoke the pruning of the product line when any business units are perceived to be dogs. This calls for retrenchment or take a shot at the dogs. Hence, such livelihood of the cycle of dogs should not exist because it is at the expense of the enterprise that if such product will survive which required huge cash flow expenditure, it is only favorable to the competition at the expense of the enterprise. Dogs are usually from factors of dying product and bad company name. Thus a lot need to be done before saving the life of the product because the competitor might use it as competitive advantage to attack the enterprise weakness. Sometimes what need to be done is do nothing strategy to await an entrepreneurship opportunities that will yield momentum in short-term. But for some exceptional and strategic reasons some dogs product life are sustain by some enterprise, cooperation or conglomerate (Mandrinos & Lim, 2023).

## B. Blake-Mouton's Managerial Grid Theory

The managerial grid is an exceptional instrumental strategic tool use to appraised leadership competence and concern in management of people and productivity in the workplace. This is very vital for the intrapreneur and entrepreneur relationship evaluation on how the enterprise can move forward for growth especially for lean start-up and growth. The managerial grid Theory was developed R. R. Blake and J. S. Mouton which helps leaders and entrepreneurs in the enterprise to optimized leadership style best fit for business intelligence, creativity and innovation (Blake & Mouton, 1964).
