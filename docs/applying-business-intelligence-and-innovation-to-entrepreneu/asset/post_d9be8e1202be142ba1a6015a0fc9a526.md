This leadership style pattern could be advisable when the employee are self-starter, self-motivated and self-monitored because of self–oriented competence and goal driven individuals attitude at the workplace. But such kinds of employee are difficult to see in the workplace; even those that possess such attitude may not be interested in high social lifestyle (Helmold, 2021; Ovharhe & Chukwuemeka, 2023).

- 2. Authority-Compliance Management (9,1): This type of leadership style dramatized where the leader of the enterprise is focused more concerned about productivity rather than the emotional and social premium of employee. The leaders in this sense do not care for about individual lifestyle pattern. This is a game of hiring and firing kind of leadership if anyone messes up with the strict rules and fundamental policy of the organization. The leader talks more about man-hour and instrumentations (i.e. device, machine, material, finance) on productivity (Northouse, 2018; Yadav et al., 2023). The leader attitude is; ‘it’s cool out there down town’, if you don’t like the job and leadership pattern you quit. This kind of job may not be favorable for intrapreneurs and entrepreneur that have huge competition because there might be lack of business intelligence, innovation, creativity and business opportunities, perhaps their opposition might use it as an attack against them. Entrepreneurs with this attitude which is a starter might not survive in the long-term. This kind of leadership style is for lean startup or SMEs, but favorable where the entrepreneur want to sustain its business growth with huge compensation and enormous fringe benefits with bonus tied to the job functions, despite the autocratic nature of the leadership style the enterprise might succeed (Alqahtani & Uslay, 2023).

- 3. Impoverished Management (1,1): This pattern of leadership is the unexpected in any workplace because it ruins the entire enterprise from the roof to the foundation. It poses negative attributes in all ramifications because the concern for people is very poor and the concern for productivity is much obscured. It is a type of leadership style which occurs where there are high needs for distributive or decentralized forms on management. The task, responsibility and job function is specified to certain work scope without much interference from the centre. This can occur in holdings and subsidiaries, religious organizations and large conglomerates with multinationals. This is good for leader where they may be community disturbance, turbulence, hazards, pandemic and inconsistency in exchange rate, inflation rate and interest rate (Helmold, 2021; Ovharhe et al., 2021). This kind of leadership style does not follow the stream of any entrepreneurship pattern, talk less of championreneurship.

- 4. Middle-of-the-road Management (5,5): This illustrates where there are mediocrity concerns on the management of an enterprise in terms of employee welfare and productivity. The leadership perception is on boomerang and fictitious profit been generated from another market environment, hence the leaders seem to display the attribute of 50-50 ratio on concern for people and productivity. It is done sometimes just to keep the ball rolling in the business enterprise. Much energy is not enacted because the portfolio in another sales quotas and sales territories is very high. Thus, they will averagely concerned about the market and employee in the place of 50-50 provided they just keep generating wealth for the stakeholders (Northouse, 2018; Yadav et al., 2023). This somehow happens to entrepreneurs that discovered new business unit with high profit market and net cash flow. The income stream seems to be very beneficial. This is practice of most serial extrapreneurs, ultrapreneurs and extrapreneurs (Ovharhe, 2023).

- 5. Team Management (9,9): this is the best posited leadership that orchestrate high proficiency of concern for people and concern for productivity. Here, there will be high spirit of motivation to accomplish task with target with pursuit of business opportunities, business intelligence, creativity and innovation (Helmold, 2021). The entrepreneurial dreams and journey will experience upward smooth slope movement despite the turbulence, hazard, pandemic and recklessness in the business environment. There will be upward impression management and affective commitment, job satisfaction. Employee satisfaction will be experiential compared to employee turnover, intention to stay, normative commitment with enterprise. Many attributes of organizational citizenship behavior will be experienced (altruism, civic virtue, sportsmanship, courtesy and conscientiousness) (Ovharhe & Chukwuemeka, 2023).

This type of leadership style promotes team spirit, cohesiveness standard, norms and industrial harmony among workforce. Grievances that resulted to strike and industrial dispute will be handled amicably. The concern for the employee to meet up target is at large; also the compensation and benefit of the employees are in the heart of the management at all times. The competition will be afraid to the arrow and threat on the business portfolio because they will see the corporate image and identity of the enterprise (Ratten, 2023).

The managerial grid matrix indicated the model of paradigm shift among managers, entrepreneurs, intrapreneurs, social entrepreneurs, serial entrepreneurs, ultrapreneurs and extrapreneur is their mode of operation towards the concern of workforce and productivity. Though, the managerial grid does not cover vast aspect of management. This dealt most with concern for workforce and productivity whereby it calls for business intelligence, creativity and innovation with ability to grab business opportunities. But, the grid can be used as a paradigm shift of extension to gross function of leadership style (Islam, 2023).

## 7. CONCLUSION

Entrepreneurship is the engine house of any merchandising activities and propeller of any nation's economic growth and development. Entrepreneurs determine the flow of new blood stream in any enterprise. Whereas, management is the coordinating force that binds all the activities to ensure there is effectiveness and efficiency. This means the managerial strategies will be able to fine-tune the plans, organizing, directing, motivating and control of the input resource and projected output to work in achievable concept.

Every entrepreneur need to learn how to navigate with potentials and capabilities on the right track where there are valuable treasures of business opportunities. The right track is the valuable path on the vision and mission to tread on which can accomplish the entrepreneurial journey and dreams. This will enable the entrepreneur to experience new enterprise creation successfully through the application of business intelligence (competitive intelligence, decision support system), creativity and innovation.

The business intelligence was adopted to enhance the return on asset (ROA), return on investment (ROI), return on capital employed (ROCE) and return on equity (ROE). The business intelligence is anchored with strategic intelligence, tactical intelligence, operational intelligence and decision support system that give the enterprise competitive advantage and championship edge in the market structure. However, the enterprise growth and development cannot be achieved without these concepts that give new blood stream flow in the enterprise that is fathomed as creativity, innovation and entrepreneurship opportunities. This is the store house of idea generation, networking and brainstorming that enables entrepreneurship to be on the leading edge rather than bleeding edge over the artificial intelligence science, data virtualization, machine learning and big data emergence.

Entrepreneurship opportunities, creativity and innovation are fruition to the management of any enterprise in the short-term and long-term in her merchandising activities. With the application of entrepreneurship innovation skills, the managerial policy, decision making and budgetary control implementation, the enterprise will experience business excellence in sustainability, survival and success. This calls for a championreneurship approach which possesses ability, capability and capacity to control turbulence, post-pandemic and competitive parity in the business world. Championreneurship is the key to enterprise growth and development.
