+   - Identifying business processes to be improved using the data and information provided by the business intelligence system.

During field visits to many small and medium-sized industrial enterprises in the industrial area of Setif, Algeria, extensive interviews were conducted with 32 managers and executives (CIO, CFO, COO, and CEO). Over a period of two months, 14 detailed questions were asked about activities that could be significantly improved using business intelligence systems. These questions were selected based on their connection to the enterprise’s strategy, as well as their contribution to creating benefit. The interviews led to the following results:

+   - Using business intelligence contributes to predicting sales trends and discovering new markets.
+   - Relying on business intelligence leads to significant improvements in the distribution process to stores.
+   - Using business intelligence facilitates the measurement and improvement of customer satisfaction.
+   - Business intelligence can be used to achieve substantial improvements in supply chain management.
+   - Using business intelligence helps improve productivity across the enterprise.
+   - Business intelligence contributes to eliminating non-value-added activities.
+   - Business intelligence can be used to improve indirect cost allocation on products.
+   - Business intelligence can be used to improve inventory management.
+   - Business intelligence can be used to improve coordination between different functional departments.
+   - Business intelligence can be used to maintain a high level of competitive awareness.
