# Figure 2. Works on blockchain technology on entrepreneurship

![](bbox=[0.142, 0.13, 0.863, 0.375])

+   • **Transaction Initiation:**
+   
+   An entrepreneur initiates a transaction by creating a digital record of it. This record can represent various types of transactions, including financial payments, the transfer of digital assets, the creation of a smart contract, or the logging of supply chain data.

+   • **Digital Signatures:**
+   
+   Transactions on the blockchain are secured using digital signatures. The entrepreneur signs the transaction with their private key to prove ownership and authorization. The recipient, in turn, can verify the transaction’s authenticity using the entrepreneur’s public key.

+   • **Validation and Consensus:**
+   
+   Once a transaction is initiated, it is broadcast to the network of nodes. These nodes collectively validate the transaction to ensure it follows the rules and adheres to the consensus mechanism (e.g., proof-of-work or proof-of-stake). Consensus mechanisms vary depending on the blockchain platform.
