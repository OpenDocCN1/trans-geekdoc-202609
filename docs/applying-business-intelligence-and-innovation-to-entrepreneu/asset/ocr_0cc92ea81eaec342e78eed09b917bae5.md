# RESEARCH METHODODLGY

Through a “PRISMA 2020” systematic review of existing literature, this study aims to identify how business intelligence can be influencing entrepreneurial orientation among the ventures that need to be addressed for future research agendas.

# Identification

In order to identify the resources, the first stage entails filtering out journals from the various databases, such as Science Direct, Emerald Insight, Ebscohost, and JStor, since they are some of the main chosen databases for evaluation and financing in Indian social science research. Utilizing keywords such as “Business Intelligence” AND “Entrepreneurship Orientation” will allow to search the four database journals for the resource. This study includes only English-language research papers, review articles, early-cite publications, and peer-reviewed articles that were published between 2010 and 2023.

# Screening

In order to find relevant articles, keywords are used to search for resources. The screening process led to the retrieval of 306 articles (59 from Science Direct, 235 from Emerald Insight, 4 from EBSCOhost, and 8 from JStor). A duplicate article was identified during the compilation process and removed. 37 articles were sorted after reading the abstracts and the titles, and 269 were discarded. There were 13 articles that had no full-text available, as well.
