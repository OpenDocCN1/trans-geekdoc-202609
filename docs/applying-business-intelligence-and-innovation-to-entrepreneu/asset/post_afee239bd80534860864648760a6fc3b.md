
The fast growth of health tourism has been attributed to creating a health and prevention boom era expected to usher in new economic power. Offering a different approach derived from the Sharia, Muslim health tourism poses a proximity between religion, tourism, and health. Amid the urgency to understand Muslim tourists as a potential market with such unique characteristics and specific needs, it is unfortunate that no model describes the decision-making behaviour when selecting a Muslim-friendly health destination. As a result, the author suggests a Muslim health tourist behaviour model. This study also highlighted Sharia salons and spas as one of the forms of Muslim health tourism business. It's becoming increasingly clear that for the Muslim health tourism business to truly understand its market, going back to basics is the most innovative approach.

## Preface

In the dynamic landscape of today’s global economy, the integration of technology, business intelligence, and innovation has become pivotal for entrepreneurs seeking to navigate and thrive in the ever-evolving business environment. As the world witnesses a digital revolution, marked by the widespread application of artificial intelligence and the continuous emergence of smart technologies, entrepreneurs find themselves at the forefront of transformative change.

This edited reference book, Applying Business Intelligence and Innovation to Entrepreneurship, is a collaborative effort by Kannapat Kankaew, Parinya Nakpathom, Alhuda Chnitphattana, Krittipat Pitchayadejanant, and Siwaporn Kunnapapdeelert, along with various authors from around the world. It is designed to provide insights into the intricate interplay between technology, business intelligence, and innovation in entrepreneurship across diverse sectors.

In this era of the innovation economy, where technological advancements propel commerce forward, entrepreneurs play a vital role in shaping and redefining industries. From agricultural products to fast-moving consumer goods, from the hospitality industry to cultural and indigenous products, entrepreneurship is a catalyst for creativity and circular economies within communities and societies.

This book delves into the multifaceted realm of entrepreneurial business management, emphasizing the symbiotic relationship between technology and business intelligence. It explores how entrepreneurs can leverage digitalization for sustainability, innovation, and a competitive edge in various sectors. Notably, the book addresses the challenges faced by entrepreneurs in today’s dynamic business environment and provides valuable insights into successful business strategies.

Our aim is to cater to a diverse audience, including academicians, researchers, undergraduate/postgraduate/doctoral students, and professionals in both private and public enterprises. The content covers a spectrum of topics, ranging from traditional entrepreneurship and management to cutting-edge areas like Fintech startups, cryptocurrency in business, community startups, and the integration of local wisdom into innovative practices.

The recommended topics span the breadth of entrepreneurship, including the adoption of business intelligence by small and medium enterprises, innovation in social enterprise management, the dynamics of the hospitality and tourism industry, and the influence of digital marketing and marketing communication. Additionally, the book explores emerging trends such as delivery services, operation and supply chain management, data science for business, blockchain in entrepreneurship, technology management, and sustainable innovation.

In this book, the chapters unfold a comprehensive exploration of the dynamic interplay between technology, business intelligence, and innovation in the entrepreneurial landscape. From a systematic literature review on the influence of business intelligence on entrepreneurial orientation to an in-depth analysis of the role of technology business incubators in startup development, each chapter offers valuable insights and practical perspectives. The disruptive force of blockchain technology, the pivotal role of supply chain and operations management, the transformative impact of Neuromarketing in the digital era, and the revolutionary influence of cryptocurrencies and fintech startups are among the diverse topics covered. Additionally, the chapters delve into the adoption of business intelligence by small and medium-sized enterprises, the significance of design thinking and creativity in entrepreneurial innovation, and the exploration of environmental, social, and governance performance in corporate boards. With a wide-ranging scope, this collection is a valuable resource for academicians, researchers, students, and professionals seeking a nuanced understanding of the ever-evolving landscape of entrepreneurship in the digital age.

Chapter 1 conducts an extensive systematic literature review, employing the PRISMA framework, to delve into the influence of business intelligence (BI) on entrepreneurial orientation (EO) within ventures. By analyzing articles from notable databases, the study uncovers a scarcity of research in this domain, emphasizing the need for further exploration. With "Business intelligence" and "Entrepreneurial orientation" as keywords, 23 articles are scrutinized, providing a foundation to identify gaps for future researchers in the dynamic intersection of BI and entrepreneurship.

Focusing on the role of technology business incubators (TBIs), Chapter 2 explores their impact on startup effectiveness and overall development. Through a comprehensive literature review and empirical analysis of Gujarat’s entrepreneurial ecosystem, the chapter establishes a framework connecting TBI support to operational effectiveness. It offers insights into the challenges faced by startups from ideation to commercialization, presenting a valuable resource for entrepreneurs and policymakers aiming to enhance the existing support systems.

Investigating the disruptive force of blockchain technology, Chapter 3 explores its potential to reshape entrepreneurship across various industries. From supply chain management to decentralized finance, the paper examines the symbiotic relationship between blockchain and entrepreneurship. It sheds light on opportunities for innovation, challenges, and the transformative role of blockchain in startups, emphasizing the need for entrepreneurs to navigate the evolving landscape strategically.

Chapter 4 delves into the critical objectives of supply chain and operations management, underscoring their significance for entrepreneurial ventures’ success and sustainability. Exploring how effective management in these areas enhances competitiveness and customer satisfaction, the chapter also addresses emerging trends. Entrepreneurs are guided toward adopting best practices in supply chain and operations management for long-term success in today’s dynamic business environment.

Examining Neuromarketing as a digital marketing strategy, Chapter 5 explores its role in evolving marketing communication. Rooted in neuroscience, it reveals how Neuromarketing integrates with digital channels, emphasizing personalization and emotional engagement. The chapter highlights the significance of personalized content, ethical considerations, and the transformative impact of Neuromarketing in decoding consumer behavior in the digital marketing landscape.

Chapter 6 traces the revolutionary impact of cryptocurrencies and fintech startups on business models and entrepreneurship. Exploring their development and disruptive influence on the financial services industry, the chapter investigates how companies embrace cryptocurrencies, conduct ICOs, and integrate them into investment portfolios. It also addresses technological challenges and regulatory issues, providing insights into the dynamic merger of fintech and cryptocurrency solutions.

Focused on small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), Chapter 7 addresses the requirements for adopting business intelligence systems. Emphasizing the evolving communication technologies and the importance of business intelligence for sustainable competitive advantage, the chapter analyzes technical, human, and organizational requirements for SMEs in the industrial region of Setif, Algeria.

Exploring the nexus of design thinking and creativity in entrepreneurial innovation, Chapter 8 highlights their significance in problem-solving and idea generation. Through case studies and empirical evidence, it demonstrates how these concepts foster innovation in entrepreneurial ventures. The chapter emphasizes the need for a creative mindset and the application of design thinking principles in navigating the challenges of innovation in the contemporary business environment.

Chapter 9 employs a championrepreneurship approach to explore the influence of business intelligence and innovation on entrepreneurship and management. Focusing on competitive intelligence and decision support systems, it orchestrates championrepreneurship as a concept aligning entrepreneurship and management for prospective entrepreneurs. The study underscores the synchronization of entrepreneurship and management through the intensive application of business intelligence and innovation.
