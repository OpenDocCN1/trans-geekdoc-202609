The managerial grid Theory was developed R. R. Blake and J. S. Mouton which helps leaders and entrepreneurs in the enterprise to optimized leadership style best fit for business intelligence, creativity and innovation (Blake & Mouton, 1964).

As shown in the Figure above, the managerial grid is enveloped on two dimensions, firstly; Concern for People secondly; Concern for Production. The managerial grid managers intrapreneurs and entrepreneur to identify best fit pattern of five basic leadership style; (a) Country Club Management, (b) Authority-Compliance Management, (c) Impoverished Management, (d) Middle-of-the-road Management, and (5) Team Management.

+   1. Country Club Management (1,9): This demonstrates how leaders are more concerned for the people rather than the job functions.
