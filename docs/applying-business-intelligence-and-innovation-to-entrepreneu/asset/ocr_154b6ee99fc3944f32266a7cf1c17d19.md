**Sustainable and Ethical Practices;** In an era where sustainability and ethical considerations are fundamental, in business practices, the implications of OSCM extend into responsibility. The connection between these practices and corporate social responsibility (CSR) theories as ethical supply chain management frameworks is supported by scientific research (Mani et al., 2020). CSR theories emphasize the significance of businesses contributing to societal well-being beyond profit making (Brin & Nehme 2019). By implementing practices in operations and supply chain management that consider social impacts entrepreneurs position themselves as responsible corporate citizens. Ethical supply chain management frameworks, such as the UN Global Compact offer guidance to businesses in ensuring practices throughout their supply chains. Entrepreneurs who incorporate these frameworks into their strategies contribute to ethical entrepreneurship.
