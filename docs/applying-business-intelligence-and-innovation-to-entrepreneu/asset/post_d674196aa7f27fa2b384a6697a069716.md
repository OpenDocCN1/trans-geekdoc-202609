Supply chain management relies heavily on the OPP. In the context of the supply chain, this term designates the stage at which products are tailored to individual buyers. ### Lean Principles and Waste Reduction
Lean Principles and Waste Reduction focus on streamlining operations by eliminating non-value-adding activities, thereby enhancing efficiency and productivity. This approach emphasizes continuous improvement, waste minimization in processes, and optimizing resource utilization, leading to cost savings and improved customer value.

### Logistics and Distribution
Logistics and Distribution in supply chain management involve the coordinated planning and execution of transporting goods from suppliers to customers. This process encompasses warehousing, inventory management, transportation, and order fulfillment, all aimed at ensuring timely and cost-effective delivery of products to meet customer demands.

Collaboration and Partnerships in supply chain management are about building and maintaining effective relationships with suppliers, distributors, and other stakeholders.
