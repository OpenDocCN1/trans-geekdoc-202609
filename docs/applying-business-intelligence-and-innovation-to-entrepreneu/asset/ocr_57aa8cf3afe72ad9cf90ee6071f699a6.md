Islam does not disregard innovation. Some terms refer to innovation in Islam. Islah, tajdid and ijtihad are good innovations, while bidaah is an innovation in religion that can be good or bad. In line with that, before the Muslim health tourism business can return to accomplishment and compliance in its fundamental framework, it can return outside the market competition arena. It requires a back-to-basics approach to innovation, where Muslim health tourism businesses return to focusing on perfecting the fundamental areas of their frameworks, i.e. Sharia as Islamic law.
