# ## UNDERSTANDING OPERATION AND SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT: NAVIGATING THE ENTREPRENEURIAL LANDSCAPE

In the world of entrepreneurship, the delicate balance between Operation and Supply Chain Management (OSCM) is crucial for achieving long-term success. As entrepreneurs endeavor to establish their presence in markets it becomes essential to have a comprehension of how operations and supply chains operate. In this examination, we will embark on a voyage to uncover the complexities of OSCM shedding light on its significance, within the entrepreneurial landscape.

# ## Defining Operation and Supply Chain Management

Operations management involves the art and science of overseeing processes that transform inputs, into products or services (Russell and Taylor 2019). This encompasses a range of activities, including planning, production managing inventory ensuring quality control, and optimizing processes. In a context efficient, operations play a role in delivering high-quality products or services promptly which contributes to customer satisfaction and loyalty (Traynor et al., 2022). In addition to operations management supply chain management plays a role in coordinating the flow of materials, information, and resources from the stages of production to the ultimate delivery to customers. Supply chains are networks that connect suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, and retailers (Russell and Taylor 2019). It requires coordination for operation. A managed supply chain is an advantage, for entrepreneurs as it enables cost-effectiveness and responsiveness to market demands.
