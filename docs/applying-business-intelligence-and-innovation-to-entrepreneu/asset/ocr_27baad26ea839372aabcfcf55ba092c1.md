The nature of BI systems in small and medium-sized industrial enterprises differs significantly from that in large enterprises. Rao, Mansingh, and McNaughton argue that in the recent past, most researchers and specialists had a misconception that BI was only suitable for large resource-intensive enterprises. However, this has completely changed at present, as small and medium-sized industrial enterprises possess inherent organizational agility, which can only be enhanced by successfully adopting BI. (Rao-Graham, McNaughton, & Mansingh, 2020, p. XIII) This constitutes a considerable research gap, necessitating deeper theoretical and field research into the requirements for the successful adoption of BI by small and medium-sized industrial enterprises, taking into account their distinctive characteristics. That is, the need for methodical specification and detailing of the requirements for the successful adoption of BI, primarily associated with the requirements for establishing an operational BI system, the technical integration requirements of the BI system with operational processes, in addition to the level of maturity of information and communication technology (ICT) practices and the technical requirements for establishing a BI system. Also, the human requirements related to the training and qualification of individuals responsible for establishing and operating BI.
