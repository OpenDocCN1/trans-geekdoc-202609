Ovharge (2023c) asserted from the marketing lecture of Prof A. B. Akpan (2018) which coined from Murray et al., revealed that human needs are categorized into forty (40). He argued that there are twelve (12) physiological needs, while twenty eight (28) psychological needs. This posits that human psychological needs are more than human physiological needs. Physiological represent the tangible (product) needs, whereas psychological are the intangible (services) needs. Champiopreneur are the innovator, inventor and creator of the intangible and tangible needs for client or customer satisfaction. Champiopreneur can be disabilities, dwarf, unstable or deformed, but their innovative skills and creativity coupled with business intelligence changes the game of their condition or impairment (i.e. Helen Keller, Steve Wonder, Peter Dinklage; Hollywood, Nick Juvicic, Cobham Asuquo, Akin & Pawpaw in Nollywood among others).

These are champiopreneurs that uses their disability as competitive advantage which give them edge in the competitive parity. Their condition gives them entrepreneurship opportunities as leading edge instead of bleeding edge because of the application of business intelligence, creativity and innovation. They were able to display their entrepreneurship and managerial innovation skills to conquer their business world and satisfied the needs of clients by unlocking business intelligence, creativity and innovation skills they possess (Ovharge, 2023b; Seth, 2023; OECD, 2023).
