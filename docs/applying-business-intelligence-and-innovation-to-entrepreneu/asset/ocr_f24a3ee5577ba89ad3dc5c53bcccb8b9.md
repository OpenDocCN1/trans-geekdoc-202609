+   2. **Imitating Entrepreneurs:** The imitating entrepreneurs are those who immediately copy the new inventions made by the innovative entrepreneurs. These do not make any innovations by themselves; they just imitate the technology, processes, methods pioneered by others. These entrepreneurs are found in the places where there is a lack of resources or industrial base due to which no new innovations could be made. Thus, they are suitable for the underdeveloped regions where they can imitate the combinations of inventions already well established in the developed regions, in order to bring a boom in their industry.

+   3. **Fabian Entrepreneurs:** Mediocrity and skeptics are the order of this entrepreneur status. They are very mild and careful; do not want to be seen making mistakes. At first they are not initiative and innovation, but spend time to study the trend of motion on event before they strike their actions in the business place. They follow the success rate of others in the invention and change transformation before they operate theirs. They strongly believe in concept testing and test marketing of their product or services and were very sure of the success and survival rate before they lunch attack on the market (Ratten, 2023).

+   4. **Drone Entrepreneurs:** These are more mediocrity entrepreneurs that do not want to adopt and implement change in the business environment because of their conservative nature. +   5. **Nichers:** These are entrepreneurs with passion but do not have the required resources or managerial acumen to capture the market. But, they decided to start from a very low scale in the business ladder trying to wait for the appropriate opportunity to grow. You see them starting with road side business before owing a business platform.

+   6. **Followers:** They are very strong and ready to climb to the business top at any given opportunity. They may be looking for connections on the business climax and political structure. They are always scanning in the business environment for any slight opportunity to lunch their attack. Their target in not the market nichers’ rather the challengers and leaders on top the ladder in the business environment. They are not satisfied with where they are, but any mistake by those at the top, they will take over.
