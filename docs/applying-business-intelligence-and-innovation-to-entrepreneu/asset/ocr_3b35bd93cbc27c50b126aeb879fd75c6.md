However, Islam does not forbid wearing jewellery, clothes, fragrance, make-up, or even improving their visual appearance for health reasons or beautifying themselves properly to appear beautiful in front of her husband. The Prophet Muhammad was once asked, “What about a people who like to wear nice clothes and sandals?” He replied, “Verily, Allah is beautiful, and He loves beauty. Arrogance means rejecting the truth and looking down on people” (Hadith narrated Muslim). The Hadith, as mentioned, Allah is beautiful; of course, His creation is also excellent. For the beauty of His beauty, Allah also loves beauty; in another sense, beauty is above the best form.

Abu Hurairah said that the Prophet Muhammad said, “Whoever has hair, let one take care of it” (Hadith narrated Abu Dawud). The Prophet Muhammad also did not like seeing messy, long, and untidied hair. Wa’il ibn Hijr said, “I came to the Prophet and had long hair. When the Messenger of Allah saw me, he said, ‘Evil, evil.’ I then returned and cut them off. I then came to him in the morning. He said (to me), ‘I did not intend to do evil to you; this is much better.’” (Hadith narrated Abu Dawud). For women, the Prophet Muhammad forbade them from trimming their hair. Furthermore, Abdullah ibn Umar, the Prophet Muhammad said, “There is nothing for women to shave (her hair), she can only cut it (shorten)” (Hadith narrated Abu Dawood).

Related to hair polish, Abu Hurairah was once asked, “Did the Prophet Muhammad polish his hair? He replied, “Yes” (Hadith narrated At-Tirmidzi). The colour advised is red or yellow/gold. Dyeing the hair black is forbidden, as it is feared for Tadlis (deception), like an older woman polishing her hair to attract a person proposing to her or wanting to marry her or an older man. Hence, the hair looks greyish to attract the woman he wants to marry. Such polishes belong to deceit and lies. Ibn Majah narrated the Hadith of Ummu Salamah that Prophet Muhammad , the Messenger of Allah, masked his body, starting from the Aurat by using blossom, then to the whole body.

For the women, the Prophet ordered to arrange and braid their hair in the Hadith of Al-Bukhari, narrated the history of “Umm Athiyah Umm Jahl that she said, and we put the hair of the daughter of the Prophet Muhammad in three braids, two at the sides and one at the front.”. If Islam allows men and women to adorn themselves as a whole, it means Islam maintains the nature of women and femininity. This includes allowing women to use or wear something forbidden for men, such as silk and gold (Hadith narrated At-Tirmidzi). Thus, in general, women are allowed to adorn themselves. In addition, styling the hair for the husband’s happiness is halal. However, it becomes haram if the purpose is to show it off to non-mahram (all those forbidden to marry due to heredity, breastfeeding, and marriage in Islamic law).
