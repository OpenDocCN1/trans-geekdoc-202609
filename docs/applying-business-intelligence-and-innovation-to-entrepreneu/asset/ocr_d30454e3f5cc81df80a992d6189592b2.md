+   4. Middle-of-the-road Management (5,5): This illustrates where there are mediocrity concerns on the management of an enterprise in terms of employee welfare and productivity. The leadership perception is on boomerang and fictitious profit been generated from another market environment, hence the leaders seems to display the attribute of 50-50 ratio on concern for people and productivity. It is done sometimes just to keep the ball rolling in the business enterprise. Much energy is not enacted because the portfolio in another sales quotas and sales territories is very high. Thus, they will averagely concern about the market and employee in the place of 50-50 provided they just keep generating wealth for the stakeholders (Northouse, 2018; Yadav et al., 2023). This somehow happens to entrepreneurs that discovered new business unit with high profit market and net cash flow. The income stream seems to be very beneficial. This is practice of most serial extrprepreneurs, ultrapreneurs and extrapreneurs (Ovharhe, 2023).

+   5. Team Management (9,9): this is the best posited leadership that orchestrate high proficiency of concern for people and concern for productivity. Here, there will be high spirit of motivation to accomplish task with target with pursuit of business opportunities, business intelligence, creativity and innovation (Helmold, 2021). The entrepreneurial dreams and journey will experience upwards smooth slope movement despite the turbulence, hazard, pandemic and recklessness in the business environment. There will be upward impression management and affective commitment, job satisfaction. Employee satisfaction will be experiential compare to employee turnover, intention to stay, normative commitment with enterprise. Mush attributes of organizational citizenship behavior will be experience (altruism, civic virtue sportsmanship courtesy and conscientiousness) (Ovharhe & Chukwuemeka, 2023).

The competition will be afraid to the arrow and threat on the business portfolio because they will see the corporate image and identify of the enterprise (Ratten, 2023).
