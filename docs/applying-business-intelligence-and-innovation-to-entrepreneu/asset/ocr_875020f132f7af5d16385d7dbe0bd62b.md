# DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSION

Initial analysis of this study shows that transformational Leadership is not significant, and transactional leadership style is partially significant in sustainability. Even if the studies have shown that transformational and transactional leadership approaches positively impact the environmental aspects of firms (Changar & Atan, 2021; Tanveer et al., 2021), our results differ from the literature.

According to a study conducted by Kafetzopoulos and Gotzamani in 2022, there is a significant correlation between transactional leadership and sustainable organizational performance. While the study does not specify the exact environmental factors involved, it suggests that transactional leadership can have a positive effect on sustainability outcomes. In contrast, leaders who focus on environmental transformation can indirectly influence their organizations, as noted by Robertson and Carleton in 2018. Moreover, one study found that the degree of influence of transformational leadership on employees’ sustainable performance depends on organizational members’ citizenship behavior. This research supports this study’s results that in the textile sector in Turkey, transformational leaders have a harmonious passion for the environment; however, influencing employees’ voluntary pro-environmental behavior could not be changed easily.
