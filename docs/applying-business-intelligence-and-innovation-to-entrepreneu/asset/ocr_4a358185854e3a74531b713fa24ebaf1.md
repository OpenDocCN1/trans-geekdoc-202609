Since larger boards positively influence ESG performance, firm managers should select both male and female directors to increase board size. Contrary, our study suggests evidence to support the effect of executive compensation but not for all geographic areas as a strategic tool that demonstrates the firm’s strong commitment toward ESG performance.

Our study did not investigate the CRS committee, the emission score or environmental innovation, but future research could go in this direction. Recent empirical evidence for non-financial firms shows that a CSR committee is more effective in improving a firm’s corporate social responsibility when it is characterized by a larger proportion of independent directors, by a female chair and a smaller size.
