Based on the results of these interviews, various organizational requirements that ensure the successful adoption of business intelligence can be categorized into two main groups:

+   - Requirements associated with establishing an operational business intelligence system, which focuses on providing data from internal activities.

+   - Requirements associated with management and control, aiming to provide comprehensive coordination between the parts of the organizational structure and achieve organizational effectiveness.

# Organizational Requirements Associated With Establishing an Operational Business Intelligence System

When adopting a business intelligence system, it will necessarily include all operational activities and processes and all managerial levels of the organization, including suppliers, customers, and public data for citizens outside the organizational structure but in continuous relation with it. (Howson, Successful Business Intelligence: unlock the value of BI and big data, 2014, p. 4) In line with this approach, the operational system of the organization consists of a set of internal activities and processes that transform available resources into products and services directed to the market. At each step of these processes, users enter a set of data into the operating systems, which are then captured and used in the business intelligence system. Operational systems, also known as transaction processing systems or source systems and also referred to as Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems, are the main source of quantitative data related to the organization. Various activities are practiced, involving the input of a considerable amount of data into these systems, covering many areas, such as:

+   - Manufacturing system: Entering a specific product production order into the manufacturing system, recording the quantity and quality of raw materials used, etc.

+   - Sales system: Entering customer purchase orders, along with all details.

+   - Supply Chain System: Executing orders once the product is available and entering shipping details.

+   - Accounting system: Recording all cash and financial transactions, such as customer invoices, etc. (Howson, Successful Business Intelligence: Secrets to making BI a killer app, 2008, p. 22)
