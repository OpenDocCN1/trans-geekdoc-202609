Technology Business Incubators (TBIs) support the early stage and development of startups by offering various technological, managerial, and other administrative support. National Business Incubation Association (NBIA), has identified main goal of an incubator is to create profitable, self-sustaining businesses. The goal of the incubator is to help aspiring business owners get off the ground. A business consultant can help bridge the gap that exists in many areas from mentoring to funding support. By offering entrepreneurs fundamental training, the chance to launch a firm, and in certain circumstances, the chance to interact with other potential future investors in the company, incubator programs fill the gap (Burns & Jim, 1996).

TBIs aim to use technology, resources, and first-hand experience to foster entrepreneurial talent, speed up the growth of the art industry, and ultimately speed up the selling of technology (Hackett & Dilts, 2004a, 2004b). They collaborate closely with colleges, research facilities, and technological parks. Technology business incubators are an endeavor to offer a supportive environment for technology-based businesses, new product and service development for the technology sector, and new market delivery.

# Technology Business Incubation: Gujarat

Gujarat is making significant investments in creating a solid ecosystem for fostering innovation and startups to promote job growth and drive economic expansion. As per the information from GoG, the state has an ambitious goal to build about 200,000 square meters of incubator space, construct about 100 incubators, and support at least 2,000 companies over the next few years. To date, Gujarat has given 175 businesses financial support totaling INR 220 million (IJSD 3 million) during the last two years. By 2021, the government had assisted 2,000 startups. 26 firms in the state are being sponsored by High-Net-Worth Individuals in addition to the 175 startups with government funding.
