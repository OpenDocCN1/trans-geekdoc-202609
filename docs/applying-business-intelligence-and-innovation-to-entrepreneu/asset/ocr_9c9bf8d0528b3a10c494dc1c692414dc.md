# THE CONNECTION BETWEEN BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE (BI) AND ENTREPRENEURIAL ORIENTATION (EO)

Since entrepreneurial orientation and business intelligence are relatively new topics in the academic arena, there aren’t many empirical studies that demonstrate their direct correlation. A study (Caseiro & Coelho, 2018) explored among various firms located in different European countries and stated that how business intelligence (BI), entrepreneurial orientation (EO), and startup competitiveness are related to one another. It does this by looking at how EO influences BI’s impact on startup competitiveness as well as how BI directly influences it. Because business intelligence entails using information to make better decisions, which can affect a firm’s entrepreneurial orientation, the study predicts a favourable association between EO and BI. The competitiveness of a business, which is impacted by EO, might benefit from BI techniques and attributes. It is recommended that practitioners increase the knowledge of how business intelligence (BI) affects organisational elements and think about including BI methods into startup training and information sessions.
