Muslim health tourist behavior model is formed through three stages. First is the pre-decision stage, taking the form of needs recognition. When Muslims are ill, they may decide to take corrective action through Muslim medical tourism. Meanwhile, when Muslims are in good health and want to improve their health to avoid diseases, they can choose to perform preventive actions through Muslim wellness tourism.

Second, it is the decision stage. This stage starts with (a) *iyyah* (worship intention), in which every Muslim must see and do any activities as a form of worship to Allah \(^\S\). Hence, it is necessary to straighten the intention to worship Allah \(^\S\) in the form of health tourism. Then, it goes to (b) evaluation of *Maslahah* (profit here and in the afterlife) by selecting products of good utility. At the same time, the products do not violate Islamic law for them to be blessed by Allah \(^\S\).
