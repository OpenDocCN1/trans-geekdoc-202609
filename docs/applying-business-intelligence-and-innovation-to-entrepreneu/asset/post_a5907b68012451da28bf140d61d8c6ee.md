## 5. BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND INNOVATION MODERATING ROLE ON ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND MANAGEMENT

Business intelligence is synonymous with competitive intelligence and decision support system in practical implementation phrases. Competitive intelligence is structured on strategic intelligence, tactical intelligence and operational intelligence. Strategic intelligence focus on long-term activities of the enterprise such as mergers, reconstruction, takeover, diversification, new product development, launching a new product, make or buy decision etcetera. Strategic decision is convey by the top management of the enterprise such as the managing director, general manager, director general etc. In the same vein, tactical intelligence is centered on the middle, functional and intermediate management of the enterprise. This is anchors on medium term corporate affairs of the enterprise management that deals with sales on revenue generation, production forecasting, market territories, market quotas, supply chain management system and telecoms choice decision (Ahunanya et al., 2022a,b) This is conveying by the accounting management, production manager, marketing manager and sales manager. Sustainably, the operational intelligence is the daily activities and operation of the enterprise. This is short-term operations which involve payment of monthly salaries, buying and procurement of utilities, repairs, maintenance and frequency of patronage, time of patronage and volume of patronage, profitability, return on investment, break even analysis and lots more. More so the operational intelligence is the foremen, supervisors and low executive of the enterprise.

Business intelligence is needed at all level of management for her to achieve short-term goals and long-term goals because innovations occur at all ramifications. Business intelligence is the added competitive advantage of entrepreneurs. In the essence, expressing the integration of entrepreneurship and management could be seemed as a CHAMPIOPRENEUR.

### 5.1. Concept of Championpreneurship

The world is rule by champions in all ramifications of industries and organizations, be it in sport, military, academic, entertainment, movie, news, academic, technology, engineering, music, manufacturing, logistics, online business, crypto etcetera. The operational system of championpreneur’s performance in its enterprise merchandising function is regarded as champiopreneurship. The term champiopreneurship is embrace to business intelligence, innovation skills and creativity in respective field. The application of business intelligence depends on the enterprise ability to recognize and grabs the available business opportunities (Ovharhe & Woko, 2024a). When business opportunity is recognized, pursue and utilize, there will be need to apply competitive intelligence and decision support system to authenticate the process to achieved optimum goals. The success of every enterprise to achieved goals lies on the entrepreneurship to adopt the spirit of championship known as ‘champiopreneurship’.

Champiopreneurship is coined from the prefix (champion) and suffix (entrepreneur). The simple algebraic expression:

```
Champiopreneur= Champion + Entrepreneur
Let, Champion=Champio (prefix from Medieval Latin)
Where, Entrepreneur= Preneur (suffix from Middle Age French)
Thus, Champio-preneur= Champio + Preneur
Therefore= Champio-preneur= Champiopreneur (synchronization of compound word)
However, Champiopreneurship= Championship + Entrepreneurship
Hence, Champiopreneurship= Ship (Champio + Preneur)
```

Furthermore, Champiopreneur ≡ Champiopreneurship

The term ‘Champion’ emanates from the Latin word ‘campionem’ meaning gladiator, fighter.” Also, means to fight or grab for an opportunity. Champions do not just fight, there must be an opportunities to take advantage (Vocabulary.com, 2024). For business opportunities to be accomplish, champion should have insight of competitive advantage before the swing into action, Where entrepreneurial champion sense an opportunity, it shows there is a need for grabbers, chasers and achievers which creates room for entrepreneurs to become Champiopreneur. Champiopreneurs are equipped with innovation skills and creativity to grab business opportunities by application of business intelligence (competitive intelligence and decision support system) to achieve long-term goals and short-term goals in the business world. This posits that a champiopreneur has the ability, capacity and capability to defeat opponent and win in the marketplace (Collin, 2024).
