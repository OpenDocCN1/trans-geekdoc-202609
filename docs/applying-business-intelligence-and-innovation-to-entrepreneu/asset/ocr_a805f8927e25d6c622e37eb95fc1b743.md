# Significance of OSCM in Entrepreneurship

In the world of entrepreneurship, where being quick and adaptable is crucial, operations
and supply chain management (OSCM) plays a role, in multiple ways. Firstly, it acts
as a tool to control costs by helping entrepreneurs identify inefficiencies in their
operations and streamline processes thus reducing expenses. Secondly, effective
OSCM contributes to improving the quality of products or services which’s essential
for customer satisfaction and loyalty – both critical factors for long-term business
success (Seyedghorban et al., 2022). Furthermore, OSCM is closely connected
to innovation (Shibin et al., 2022). To stay ahead in markets entrepreneurs must
constantly strive to enhance and innovate their operations and supply chains (Shibin
et al., 2022). This may involve embracing technologies exploring sourcing strategies
or implementing sustainable practices. An entrepreneur’s ability to adapt and
innovate within the realm of OSCM can be a differentiating factor, in an evolving
business landscape.
