Blockchain technology has emerged as a disruptive force with the potential to reshape entrepreneurship and business ventures across various industries. Its decentralized, immutable ledger system offers a foundation for innovative solutions, fostering transparency, security, and efficiency. Entrepreneurs are harnessing blockchain for a myriad of applications, from supply chain management to tokenized assets and decentralized finance (DeFi). This chapter delves into the symbiotic relationship between blockchain and entrepreneurship, exploring the transformative role of blockchain in startups, raising capital through initial coin offerings (ICOs), and streamlining processes through smart contracts. A comprehensive understanding of blockchain's impact on entrepreneurship is crucial for navigating this evolving landscape, fostering new business models, and realizing the full potential of blockchain technology.
