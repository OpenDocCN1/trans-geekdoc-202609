+   3. **Optimistic and Opportunist of Changing the Future**: Entrepreneur must always have a positive mindset no matter the storms. Entrepreneurs should debunk fears at all time. They should be adaptive to change, flexibility and consistency with the mission of the enterprise. This is only how the future can be change. Changing the future is ability to invent the unseen to be seen with added benefits to the society at large (Ratten, 2023).

+   4. **Learning, Unlearning and Relearning**: Grossly, learning can be view in the prospect of entrepreneurship as the apt of the acquisition, storage, retention, utilization and dissemination of knowledge that transforms to a change in behavior which yield input to projected output (Ovharhe, Woko & Ogolo, 2022; Ratten, 2023). Entrepreneurial learning is generated from education, training and development programs. Learning could also be formal, semi-formal and formal. The key factor is change and transformation. All learning could lead to transformation on positive, zero or negative learning. But, the benefit is unlearning and relearning with positive result intentions. Relearning occurs when the entrepreneurs learn new strategies, tactics, patterns and approach of achieving the dream, vision and mission in the entrepreneurial journey. Whereas, unlearning is the total abandonment of the knowledge because it's obsolete, no link and supply chain of ideas.

+   5. **Risk Culture**: Entrepreneurs have the ability to take on a significant amount of risk with the goal of profiting from it. Risk culture is a set of standards, conformity, norms, attitudes and behaviors' related to awareness, alertness, avoidance, tolerance and absorption of risks. It shapes management and employees' day-to-day decisions and has an impact on the risks they take. The plight of every therapeutic entrepreneur is the ability to manage risk (Ovharhe, 2022a). This is the necessity of risk culture to the sense of humor that enables entrepreneurs to develop risk alert and risk awareness (Chibiuke & Ovharhe, 2022). More so, risk culture is disciplinary value system with caution to deal and handle risk in the enterprise environment.
