## THE FUTURE OF DESIGN THINKING IN ENTREPRENEURSHIP

As we stand at the verge of a new era in entrepreneurship, the role of design thinking is experiencing a fundamental transition. In this final session, we will cast our attention into the future and investigate how design thinking is poised to revolutionise the entrepreneurial landscape. With expanding technology, worldwide reach, the significance of continuing education, and the enduring worth of its principles, design thinking is ready to accelerate innovation, drive creativity, and map a road to success in the ever-evolving world of entrepreneurship.

The future of design thinking in entrepreneurship is inextricably related to the rapid evolution of technology (Clark, 2008). New frontiers like blockchain, virtual reality (VR), and artificial intelligence (AI) are poised to change sectors and offer entrepreneurs unprecedented opportunity (Johansson-Sköldberg, 2013). How might design thinking combine with these cutting-edge technologies to drive innovation and challenge existing markets?

- Blockchain and Design Thinking: Blockchain technology, known for its security and transparency, is already altering industries such as finance, supply chain management, and healthcare. Entrepreneurs may utilise design thinking to discover pain spots within these industries and develop blockchain-based solutions that boost security, traceability, and trust. Design thinking approaches may influence the user-centric design of blockchain apps, ensuring they are intuitive and fit real-world needs.

- Virtual Reality (VR) and Immersive Experiences: VR has the ability to transform user experiences across numerous sectors, from entertainment and education to healthcare and real estate. Entrepreneurs may employ design thinking to develop immersive and user-centered VR experiences. By empathizing with customers and understanding their aspirations for engaging and intuitive interfaces, entrepreneurs may pioneer breakthrough applications in the VR realm.

- Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Personalization: AI-driven technologies are becoming increasingly integrated into our daily lives. Entrepreneurs can utilize design thinking to harness AI's capabilities for personalized user experiences. Through empathetic research and iterative prototyping, entrepreneurs can develop AI-driven solutions that anticipate user preferences, enhance decision-making, and provide tailored recommendations.

The convergence of design thinking and emerging technologies presents an exciting frontier for entrepreneurial innovation (Huq, 2017). By integrating user-centric design principles with the capabilities of blockchain, VR, and AI, entrepreneurs can unlock new avenues for disruption and create solutions that resonate with the evolving needs of a tech-savvy market. In an increasingly interconnected world, the relevance of design thinking extends beyond geographical boundaries. Its guiding principles of empathy, problem definition, ideation, and collaboration are universal and adaptable to diverse cultural contexts. As we peer into the future, it becomes evident that design thinking has the potential to transcend borders and play a pivotal role in the global entrepreneurship scene.

- Cross-Cultural Innovation: Design thinking’s emphasis on empathy positions entrepreneurs to navigate cultural nuances and develop solutions that resonate with international audiences. By conducting cross-cultural empathy interviews and understanding the unique needs and preferences of different markets, entrepreneurs can create products and services that bridge cultural divides.

- Global Collaborations: In a world where interdisciplinary teamwork and collaboration know no borders, design thinking fosters a collaborative spirit that transcends geographic constraints. Entrepreneurs can assemble diverse, international teams that bring a rich tapestry of perspectives and expertise to tackle global challenges. This collaborative approach not only drives innovation but also opens doors to international markets.

- Adaptability to Local Contexts: Design thinking’s iterative nature aligns with the need for adaptability in global entrepreneurship. Entrepreneurs can use design thinking methodologies to continuously iterate on their offerings, adapting to changing market dynamics and local preferences. This flexibility ensures that entrepreneurial ventures remain relevant and competitive on a global scale.

The future of design thinking in entrepreneurship is characterized by its ability to unite cultures, foster collaboration, and enable entrepreneurs to create solutions that transcend borders (Dunne, 2006). As the world becomes increasingly interconnected, design thinking’s universal principles will continue to be a driving force behind global entrepreneurial success. In the dynamic field of design thinking, staying ahead of the curve is essential for entrepreneurs seeking to maintain a competitive edge.

The landscape is continually evolving, with new methodologies, tools, and trends emerging regularly. In this future-focused context, the importance of continuous education for business owners cannot be overstated.

- Mastering New Tools and Techniques: Design thinking is constantly evolving, with new tools and techniques being developed to enhance the innovation process. Entrepreneurs who invest in continuous education can stay abreast of these advancements and incorporate them into their entrepreneurial toolkits. Whether it’s mastering the latest prototyping software or adopting cutting-edge research methods, ongoing learning is a pathway to innovation.

- Adapting to Changing Trends: Entrepreneurship is subject to market trends and consumer preferences that evolve over time (Finkle, 2001). By engaging in continuous education, entrepreneurs can stay attuned to these shifts and adapt their strategies accordingly. For example, understanding the latest trends in sustainability or digital transformation can inform product development and market positioning.

- Fostering a Culture of Learning: Beyond individual education, entrepreneurs can cultivate a culture of continuous learning within their organizations (Syam, 2018). Encouraging team members to pursue professional development and fostering an environment where experimentation and learning are celebrated can drive innovation and resilience in the face of change.

The future of design thinking in entrepreneurship is connected with the commitment to continuing education and adaptation (Liedtka, 2011).
