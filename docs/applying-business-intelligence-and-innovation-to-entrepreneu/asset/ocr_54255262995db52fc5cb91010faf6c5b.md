This culture not only undermines the dignity of women but also leads to the exploitation of men against women. Islam prohibits *Tabarruj* because it can eliminate shame among women. The order of covering aurat and make-up within reasonable limits in Islam has specific purposes, among others, to maintain women’s dignity.

As the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ said, “Faith consists of sixty to seventy branches, the best of which is to declare *Lāllāhallallāh* (there is no God, but Allah ﷻ) and the least of which is to remove something harmful from the road, and modesty is a branch of faith” (Hadith narrated Al-Bukhari). Examples of *Tabarruj* are shaving or tattooing the eyebrows, applying too much powder, applying eye shadow, applying lipstick with striking colours, applying excessive makeup, wearing nail polish, showing off the jewellery, undressing and opening aurat, such as hair, neck, calvess, thighs, and others in public; such actions fall into the category of *Tabarruj*. Instead, Islam teaches its people to adorn themselves within reasonable limits, covering the aurat, graceful, and within specific contexts. For example, women adorn themselves before worshipping Allah ﷻ or beautify themselves in the presence of the husband or *Mahram*.

This can lead to cultural transformation that will ultimately affect the various sectors of public life. In public perception, the spa and salon business is identical to the negative stigma (Narendran, 2011). Young (1973) suggests that tourism may give rise to undesirable activity, such as prostitution, sex trafficking, and HIV, and penetration of foreign cultures that can negatively impact local communities. Undeniably, there are many transgender people in salons and spas, erotic massage parlours, homosexual salons, and prostitution disguised as the health and other fitness business. Despite promoting themselves as massage, spa, and sauna with exclusive prices and services, some salons and spas facilitate prostitution and other hedonism. It has led to a negative image and stigma for conventional salons and spas, raising doubts and worries for Muslims about using salon and spa services.
