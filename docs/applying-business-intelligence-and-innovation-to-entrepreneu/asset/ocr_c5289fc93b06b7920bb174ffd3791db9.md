What can be noticed regarding this process as a whole is the necessity for high-level coordination to ensure a smooth horizontal flow of data and information between all steps constituting multifunctional activities. This coordination has proven challenging to achieve within vertical organizational structures due to limited communication tools and mechanisms. Here precisely emerges the effective role of the Business Intelligence (BI) system in achieving the instantaneous and continuous transfer of data and information among individuals responsible for the process. Referring to Figure (5), we can see that the Business Intelligence system ensures the provision of crucial data and information to the team responsible for the process of designing new products. This team extends beyond the limits of a single function or department to include various functional departments. The process is virtually formed (while maintaining the type of adopted organization), including representatives from the marketing department, research and development department, and the production department. This helps reduce the risks resulting from handling work between departments and the possibility of distorting information related to product characteristics.
