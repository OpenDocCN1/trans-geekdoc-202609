Lean Principles and Waste Reduction focus on streamlining operations by eliminating non-value-adding activities, thereby enhancing efficiency and productivity. This process encompasses warehousing, inventory management, transportation, and order fulfillment, all aimed at ensuring timely and cost-effective delivery of products to meet customer demands.
