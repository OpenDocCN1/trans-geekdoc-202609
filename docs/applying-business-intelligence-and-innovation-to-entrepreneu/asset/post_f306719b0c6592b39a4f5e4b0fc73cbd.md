## 3.1.1. Elements of Competitive Intelligence

Know your enemy in entrepreneurship and managerial functions calls for serious issues and concern in the business world because of the recklessness, turbulence, dynamism and competitiveness in the market environment. Certain elementary tools are needed for competitive intelligence to design dashboard, metric and framework to mitigate any negativism in the business environment.

Applying organizational entrepreneurship paradigm, Kühn (2006) and Handfield et al., (2023) supported the following elements of competitive intelligence; viz:

- **Sector Intelligence**: It entails specialized data gathering, processing and analytics should be structure by expert in specific field of entrepreneurship such as in agriculture, research and development, construction, ICT, artificial intelligence, real estate management, manufacturing, sales and distribution, logistic and telecoms (Rahchamani & All, 2019).

- **Market Intelligence**: This is a surveillance gathering about the product folio or market mix (product, price, promotion and place) use to ascertain the market share, customer relationship management, market analysis and market structure (Jena, 2019).

- **Innovation Intelligence**: Innovativeness is the centre of business intelligence assurance and excellence. The key concept of competitive intelligence and business intelligence must be interface with product innovation, administrative innovation, process innovation and technology innovation as concepts that trigger transformation in the enterprise growth and development. This approach is suitable in enterprise that deals with consignment inventory system (Ahunanya, Ovharhe, Emenike & Otto, 2022a,b; Ovharhe, Ahunanya & Woko, 2022).

- **Sales Intelligence**: The forecasting of sales trend in the enterprise is a vital data analysis. Currently, big data, machine learning, data virtualization and data science can assist in computing sales quotas, sales territories and sales metric for judgment credibility and authentication. The data board shows competitive benchmarking of the business failure and success. (Rahchamani & All, 2019).

- **Procurement and Supply Chain Intelligence**: This is about satisfying the client from the forward and backward integration. The procurement on enterprise resource planning and supply chain is very relevant for the sustainability, survival and success of any business (Effa, Ovharhe, Ezirim & Igwe, 2023a,b; Handfield, 2023).

- **ESG Intelligence**: This is intelligence tool which is an industry agnostic business intelligence platform, used to assist companies across all sectors monitor and report their Environmental, Social and Governance performance. It could exist in form of extranet. This is more application of strategic intelligence to analyze environmental data generation (Maune, 2022).

Generally, competitive intelligence focus on operational information and tactical analysis from various systematic sources such as news media, customer feedback and competitive parity analysis, professional experts in specific business, trade shows and conferences, symposium, government records, public filings, indexes and corporate dossier (Artur, 2020). Furthermore, competitive intelligence focuses on collecting, processing and analyzing sensitive data about your competition and client feedback to gain an advantage or leadership edge in the enterprise (Zighed et al., 2023). Competitive intelligence is a process where enterprise scan, generate, gather and process information about competitors and competitive business environment, ideally using it in their policy formulation, decision-making and planning process, with the corporate goal of adjusting activities to improve performance (Cloutier, 2013).

## 3.1.2. Level of Competitive Intelligence

Challenges in business growth vary on certain factors that need interventions with competitive intelligence. Certain factors are environment issues, type of business and services, season of the business or services, capital formation and human resource constraint. Though, be it SMEs or large enterprise all have their own peculiar challenges. Also certain challenges in business are exigencies, hazards; security, pandemic, exchange rate, inflation rate and interest rate and lots more are critical issues and bottlenecks that have resultant effect on business growth. But, certain challenges could be mitigated by the application of Boston Matrix, Ansoff Matrix, SWOT Analysis, Entrepreneurship Profile, Customer Profile and Managerial Grid (Ovharhe, 2024a,b).

Ovharhe and Igbokwe (2021) identify certain pitfall and bottleneck that faces an enterprise as challenges. These can be operational challenges, tactical challenges and strategic challenges; they are daily activities that flow with the activities of work. They are so enormous based on the specific field such as engineering, production, audit, account, telecoms, and administration etcetera. However, in entrepreneurship the following itemized below could be actualized.

- **a. Operational Intelligence**

These are short-term challenges that occur within the period of one year which deals with day to day functions. They are problems encountered with the foremen or line operations that deal with intrapreneurs for executorships of the enterprise activities. These challenges include inventory control, repairs and maintenance, payment of allowances, customer satisfaction, market-cost pricing, product mix or company portfolio analysis, capital formation and fund raising, hiring and firing, fintech startup, crypto-currency, blockchain, creativity and sustainable innovation etcetera (Corsaro & D’Amico, 2022). For smart motion to be activated in the business enterprise, the short-term intelligence is of basic necessity to actualized goals and objectives in the mission.
