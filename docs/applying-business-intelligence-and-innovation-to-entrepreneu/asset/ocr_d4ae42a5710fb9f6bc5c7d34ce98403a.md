**Mitigating Risks and Building Resilience:** Entrepreneurial ventures inherently face risks including supply chain disruptions and market uncertainties. OSCM serves as a tool for identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks. The impact of Operations and Supply Chain Management (OSCM), on risk management is in line with the principles of resilience theory and risk management frameworks. Resilience theory emphasizes a system’s ability to absorb shocks adapt and reorganize to maintain functionality. Effective OSCM practices contribute to resilience by enabling responses to disruptions ensuring continuity and minimizing risks. From a perspective risk management framework like the COSO Enterprise Risk Management framework provides an approach to identify and mitigate risks. Efficient OSCM aligns with these frameworks offering entrepreneurs a way to evaluate and address supply chain risks. This not only protects businesses from disruptions but also contributes to long-term sustainability.

**Innovation and Adaptability:** Innovation and adaptability are also implications of OSCM for ventures operating in dynamic markets. OSCM becomes a catalyst for improvement allowing businesses to adapt to emerging trends seize opportunities and stay ahead in an ever-changing marketplace (Kim & Marcouiller 2018). The scientific foundation of this relationship lies in innovation theories and organizational learning principles. Innovation theories suggest that businesses must continuously innovate to remain competitive. When viewed as a capability OSCM aligns with this idea by fostering an environment of ongoing improvement. Organizational learning theories highlight the importance of adaptability and knowledge acquisition, for success. OSCM, as a way to learn from market feedback and adapt operations aligns with these principles ensuring that businesses adapt to changing environments.

**Expanding Globally and Reaching Markets;** Entrepreneurial ventures aiming for expansion face challenges regarding logistics, customs, and distribution (Moshood et al., 2021). The impact of OSCM on reaching global markets aligns with business theories and strategies for market entry. International business theories emphasize the importance of adaptability and responsiveness to markets. Efficient OSCM practices equip entrepreneurs with the tools needed to navigate these challenges and ensure that supply chains are tailored to the demands of markets. Market entry strategies such as exporting, licensing, and strategic alliances are influenced by the efficiency of supply chain operations. From a perspective, the connection between OSCM and global expansion is in line with transaction cost economics which suggests that the choice of market entry mode is influenced by transaction efficiency. Effective OSCM reduces transaction costs associated with operations making international expansion more viable for ventures.
