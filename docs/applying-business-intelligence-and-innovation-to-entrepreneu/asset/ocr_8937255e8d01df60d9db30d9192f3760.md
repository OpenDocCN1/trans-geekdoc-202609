A leadership survey is gathered from the highest manager of each textile company from the Thrace region in Turkey. The textile industry has the fourth highest share by sales value of the four highest industries in Turkey (TUIK, 2023b).

Due to its strategic location at the crossroads of several regions, including the Middle East, Asia, North Africa, Eastern Europe, and Russia, the Turkish textile industry is significant (Kaya et al., 2019). The industry primarily focuses on labor and exports, with small and medium-sized businesses making up more than 90% of the sector (SMEs)(Divrik & Baykal, 2023). However, the sector needs help with environmental sustainability, energy management, nitrogen emissions, energy consumption, and pricing. However, the sector needs help concerning environmental sustainability, energy management, and nitrogen emissions, as well as energy consumption and pricing (Alkaya & Demirer, 2015; Divrik & Baykal, 2023; Hasanbeigi & Price, 2012). Despite experiencing periods of recession, the Turkish textile industry’s economic significance is seen in its contribution to the country’s economy (Bilgin et al., 2011; Duran & Temiz Dinç, 2016). Global occurrences like the COVID-19 epidemic have also affected the sector, causing changes in manufacturing and economic problems (Sutrisno, 2022). Analysis has been done on the sector’s development, productivity, and competitiveness, which reflects how dynamic the Turkish textile industry is (Başkol, 2018; Karabag et al., 2014) (Karabag et al., 2013; Başkol, 2018; Alvan & Ghosh, 2010). In addition, research and concern have focused on the industry’s effects on the environment, waste management, and energy conservation, underscoring the necessity of sustainable practices and technological developments (Gümüş & Akbal, 2011; Qingfen et al., 2023; Tunc et al., 2016). The textile industry’s importance to the Turkish economy and beyond is further highlighted by its position in trade, intra-industry dynamics, and effects on other sectors like tourism and agriculture (Jain, 2022; Permata Sari et al., 2019; Tokel et al., 2022).

Moreover, the Thrace Region is the locomotive region of our country’s textile and textile product manufacturing, mainly concentrated in the city of Tekirdağ. According to the 2018 SSI database, 488 businesses in the Thrace Region are active in the textiles industry, and 56,799 people are employed within the industry (Invest in Trakya-Tekirdağ, Edirne Kırklareli, 2023). The online survey was sent to 472 textile manufacturing firms; 341 returned, but 322 were found suitable for the analysis.
