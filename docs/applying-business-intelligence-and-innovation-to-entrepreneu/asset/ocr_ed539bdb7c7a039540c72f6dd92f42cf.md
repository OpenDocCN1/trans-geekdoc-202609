Along with initiatives and support from the government, various corporate institutions, trade associations, and academia have come together for the same. It is very interesting to note that many states have scaled up institutional mechanisms based on the Gujarat ecosystem’s lessons learned. Pre-incubation and university startup ideas in Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, and across the nation through AICTE have drawn a lot of inspiration from Gujarat-driven academic projects. While the Gujarat startup ecosystem is envisioning modern mechanisms and institutions to cater to knowledge-driven startup needs as well, it has already acted quite early in the 1980s by institutionalizing its processes through entities like the Centre for Entrepreneurship Development (CED), Industrial Extension Bureau (iNDEXTb), and others.

If we look at Figure 1, the Ahmedabad district has the most DST-approved TBIs which comes to 21, 8 of which are supported by both commercial and public educational institutions. These TBIs also oversee NI, and it should be emphasized that 28 of the 33 NI they oversee—which are recognized under the Gujarat Industrial Policy-2015’s startup program—were established in academic institutions. The research, project, and incubation activities taking place across Gujarat’s academic institutions and universities are the foundation of the supply side of the technology and innovation value chain of the Gujarat ecosystem.
