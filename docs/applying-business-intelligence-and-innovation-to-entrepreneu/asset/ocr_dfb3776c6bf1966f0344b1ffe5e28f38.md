# A. BCG Matrix Analysis

The prolific strategic firms in the USA came up with a tactical model to manage the product portfolio of enterprise. The tactical model is known as Boston Consulting Group (BCG) Matrix. BCG's founder, Bruce Henderson, popularized the concept in his essay _The Product Portfolio_, in 1970. The champion entrepreneur BCG's Alan Zakon created a trophy in this plight. While Reeves & Moose (2023) breathe a fresh breathe on the matrix in collaboration with the team.

The matrix is a simplex graphical instrumental tools use to cross-examine and evaluate the product portfolio of the different enterprise product and services the firm is willing to offer for sales in the competitive parity. This is expressed in simplex form called the strategic business units (SBU) in synchronization of the market share and business growth of the enterprise. It shows relatively how the market share expands or contrasts in alignment with the business growth rate. It is perceived by the management as two dimensional analyses among the market share and business growth of the SBU's (Strategic Business Unit).

The expression can be seen as stated beneath as stipulated per annum comparison of base and preceding.

```
Relative Market Share = Strategic Business Unit (SBU) Sales leading in the competitive parity.
Market Growth Rate = Industry sales per annum now - Industry sales per annum last year.
```

The BCG matrix has four compartment cells which can easily be understood in the horizontal axis expression and vertical axis expression with the strategic business unit (SBU).

The four compartment cells of the BCG matrix are the Stars, Cash Cows, Question Marks and Dogs as illustrated in Figure 3.
