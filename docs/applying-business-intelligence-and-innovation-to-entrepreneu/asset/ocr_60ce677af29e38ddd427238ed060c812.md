Entrepreneurs who embrace advancements not only improve their operational efficiency but also establish themselves as industry pioneers. The field of Operations and Supply Chain Management (OSCM) becomes a platform, for problem-solving, where innovation's not just a competitive advantage but a powerful catalyst that propels businesses toward future success (Caiado et al., 2023).

# # DEFINITION, SCOPE, AND IMPORTANCE OF ENTREPRENEURSHIP

# ## Definition of Entrepreneurship

Starting and running a firm from scratch is at the heart of what entrepreneurs do. Finding a profitable business model requires figuring out what customers want, coming up with novel ways to meet those wants, and sticking with the process until the end. This procedure usually calls for a healthy dose of bravery, ingenuity, and the capacity to adjust to shifting market conditions (Diandra and Azmy 2020).

# ## Scope of Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship covers a broad and ever-changing terrain, which is reflective of the fluidity of business and innovation. At its heart, it is the process of ideation through which business owners come up with, evaluate, and ultimately implement new business concepts (Jacobs and Chase 2018). This creative phase is vital as it creates the framework for what the firm will become. The next crucial step is to build a business model, in which the business's operations, income streams, and value creation for consumers and stakeholders are mapped out. This framework is the road map that brought from idea to execution (Nandan, 2013). Conducting research into potential customers is also essential for any entrepreneur. It entails learning about the market, finding your ideal clientele, and checking out the competition (Sharma, 2021). This research is crucial for establishing the viability of the business concept and developing effective expansion plans. Market research is an integral part of financial planning. Entrepreneurs need to create in-depth financial plans that detail everything from the sources of capital to the expected revenue and expenses. Effective financial management is crucial for the viability and growth of the company (Blundel et al., 2017). The entrepreneurial vision becomes a reality through the implementation of the company plan. This phase calls for precise planning, clear communication, and methodical execution of strategies. To achieve success, entrepreneurs need to master the challenges of running a firm every day (Mohanty, 2005).
