The term “business intelligence” is quite extensive. Covers a range of approaches, processes and technological resources that are used to gather, store and analyse data in order to support decision making (Wanda & Stian, 2015). The field of intelligence research provides an opportunity to explore advancements in collecting and analysing data, managing information making decisions and enhancing capabilities. Intelligence focuses on extracting value from data, information and the dynamic nature of organizations (López-Robles et al., 2019). Business Intelligence (BI) is a valuable tool that has to be created and utilised for information collecting, analysis, and distribution to enable improved decision-making (Caseiro & Coelho, 2018). To provide information and insights, for decision making strategic intelligence combines various types of intelligence such as business intelligence, competitive intelligence and knowledge management.
