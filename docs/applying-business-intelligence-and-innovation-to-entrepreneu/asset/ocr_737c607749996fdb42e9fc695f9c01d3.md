A study demonstrates how the interaction between digital technology and entrepreneurs’ everyday activities shapes the nature of their business. The qualities and dynamics of the surrounding environment influence how various digital tools are used, and their use is interrelated. The digital revolution in entrepreneurship is not the product of deliberate design, but rather an evolutionary, practice-based phenomena. The results have important ramifications for software development companies that cater to entrepreneurs, universities that are interested in entrepreneurial education, and entrepreneurs who want to comprehend the dynamics of their working practices (Corvello et al., 2022). One way to find various strategic levers for encouraging innovation and taking advantage of technology opportunities is through the strategic integration of data-driven orientation, skills upgrading, and innovation development (Visvizi et al., 2022).

On the other hand, the methods, procedures, and choices used throughout the decision-making process that result in a new market entrance are referred to as entrepreneurial orientation (EO) (Lumpkin & Dess, 1996; Covin & Miller, 2014). EO is crucial to a firm’s entrepreneurial process, which is in identifying opportunities, innovation processes, and opportunity exploitation. It is an effective way to handle competitive risks and minimise competitive pressures (Y. C. Chen et al., 2012). Proactiveness, inventiveness, and risk-taking comprise the three components that make up entrepreneurial orientation as a combined construct (Covin & Miller, 2014). The concept of entrepreneurial orientation centres on an organization’s procedures, leadership perspectives, and enterprising actions, encompassing the generation of novel ideas via digital transformation. Businesses may increase the speed and accuracy of business intelligence operations by digitising data collecting and analysis processes and minimising manual labour. Therefore, it is concluded in a study that digitization improves business intelligence via streamlining procedures related to data collecting, analysis, and decision-making; it also boosts productivity and gives researchers access to insightful information from a variety of sources (Ismail, 2023). When it comes to leveraging information systems (IS) to identify chances for innovation, EO which entails actions that bring about change in the company or market is a crucial component. With autonomy, managers may think through more possibilities and alternatives, which improves their capacity to spot chances when utilising IS. Using IS to identify possibilities also requires creativity and taking calculated risks. Decision support systems (DSS) may give managers the information and understanding they need to spot possible areas for innovation. Examples of DSS include business intelligence tools and analytics (Roberts et al., 2016).
