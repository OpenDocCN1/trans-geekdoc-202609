# 5. BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE AND INNOVATION MODERATING ROLE ON ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND MANAGEMENT

Business intelligence is synonymous with competitive intelligence and decision support system in practical implementation phrases. Competitive intelligence is structured on strategic intelligence, tactical intelligence and operational intelligence. Strategic intelligence focus on long-term activities of the enterprise such as mergers, reconstruction, takeover, diversification, new product development, launching a new product, make or buy decision etcetera. Strategic decision is convey by the top management of the enterprise such as the managing director, general manager, director general etc. In the same vein, tactical intelligence is centered on the middle, functional and intermediate management of the enterprise. This is anchors on medium term corporate affairs of the enterprise management that deals with sales on revenue generation, production forecasting, market territories, market quotas, supply chain management system and telecoms choice decision (Ahunanya et al., 2022a,b) This is conveying by the accounting management, production manager, marketing manager and sales manager. Sustainably, the operational intelligence is the daily activities and operation of the enterprise. This is short-term operations which involve payment of monthly salaries, buying and procurement of utilities, repairs, maintenance and frequency of patronage, time of patronage and volume of patronage, profitability, return on investment, break even analysis and lots more. More so the operational intelligence is the foremen, supervisors and low executive of the enterprise.

Business intelligence is needed at all level of management for her to achieve short-term goals and long-term goals because innovations occur at all ramifications. Business intelligence is the added competitive advantage of entrepreneurs. In the essence, expressing the integration of entrepreneurship and management could be seemed as a CHAMPIOPRENEUR.
