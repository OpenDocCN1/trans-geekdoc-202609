**Manifolds.** We train on the hourly 2m surface temperature measurements in Kelvin from 1971 to 2020, a subset of the ERA5 [118] climate reanalysis. The measurements are then monthly averaged by the hour of day, giving a total of 14.4k temporal measurements in polar coordinates. The measurements are evenly spaced between \(\lambda \in [\pi/2, -\pi/2]\) and \(\phi \in [0, 2\pi)\) with step-sizes \([0.25^\circ, 0.25^\circ]\). The train, validation and test split contain 10k, 2.2k, and 2.2k samples, respectively.

**3D-Shapes.** For 3D shape super-resolution, we utilize a subset of the ShapeNet Core [38] dataset. We use the voxelized solid representation of the first 4 categories, namely Tables, Cars, Chairs and Airplanes. The dataset is split into training, validation, and test sets with 45k, 3728, and 3728 samples, respectively.

**Audio.** For the audio data, we use 100-hour clean subset of the LibriSpeech [204] dataset. From this dataset, we extract the first 12k samples, trimming each sample to 5 seconds and discarding those shorter than this length. The processed data is divided into training, validation, and test sets, consisting of 10k, 1k, and 1k samples, respectively.

**Video.** We use the Adobe240 [254] dataset containing 133 videos recorded at 720p resolution and 240 frames per second, with video durations between 1 and 10 seconds. For data preprocessing, we split the videos into 1-second segments and rotate any portrait-oriented videos to landscape. The first 100 videos are allocated for training, 16 for validation, and 17 for testing.

**LiDAR.** We train on LiDAR data from the KITTI-360 dataset [164]. We disregard the global scene context and consider each vehicle at each timeframe as an independent sample. Following the methodology in [298], we define training, validation, and test splits of 20k, 2.5k, and 2.5k samples, respectively, by temporally down-sampling frames in the driving sequences. We retrieve our spatial Functa by dividing the scans into angular sectors.
