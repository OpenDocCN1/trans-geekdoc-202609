# # Hierarchical and Searchable Representations for Vision Transformers

# ## Abstract

Transformer-based models have achieved remarkable success in various domains, but their high computational cost restricts their application to large-scale inputs. To address this, we propose a novel tree-based encoder-decoder architecture that reduces self-attention complexity in the encoder to $O(N_E)$ and cross-attention complexity in the decoder to $O(N_D + \ln \sqrt{N_E})$, where $N_E$ is number of input encoder tokens, and $N_D$ is the number of decoder tokens. Our model first constructs a hierarchical tree representation of the input. Subsequently, a decoder parses this tree, selecting relevant nodes for cross-attention.
