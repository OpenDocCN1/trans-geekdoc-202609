Previous work has demonstrated the potential of INRs for SR by sampling the fitted INR at a higher resolution than the training data [226, 237]. However, this approach yields only marginal improvements over basic interpolation techniques, primarily because standard INRs lack explicit spatial context in their representations.

To address this limitation, we leverage Spatial Functa [16], an approach that augments INRs with spatial information. Spatial Functa compute individual INR representations for spatially arranged subsets of the input data. These localized representations are then fed as tokens into a transformer backbone, which captures spatial dependencies and interactions between different regions. The transformer then produces a residual that is applied to the original INR, enhancing its capacity to generate high-quality, high-resolution outputs.

Our contributions can be summarized as follows:

+   - We propose FunctaFormer, a novel domain-agnostic super-resolution architecture that leverages Spatial Functa and transformer models to achieve competitive performance across multiple data modalities, including images, audio, videos, 3D shapes, manifold data, and LiDAR scans.
+   - We conduct extensive ablation studies to optimize the sampling process of high-resolution outputs from Spatial Functa, resulting in efficient SR performance.
+   - We explore various configurations of the transformer backbone, particularly focusing on the optimal attention window size for the SR task in conjunction with the chosen Spatial Functa representations.

The codebase is made publicly available at https://anonymous.4open.science/r/functaformer/.
