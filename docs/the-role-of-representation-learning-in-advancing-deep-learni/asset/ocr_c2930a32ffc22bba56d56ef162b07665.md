As Table 5.11 shows, FunctaFormer significantly outperforms the baseline interpolation methods. While the domain-specific models achieve higher performance in most cases, this is to be expected since they are designed for the particular data modality. FunctaFormer, with its more general architecture, shows remarkable versatility, achieving solid performance in all domains and even outperforming the specialized model for 3D shapes. This adaptability allows FunctaFormer to be applied to novel domains such as data on manifold, where no previous super-resolution work exists. The strong performance on manifold data indicates that FunctaFormer is able to exploit the structure of the manifold for improved super-resolution quality. The largest performance gap exists for LiDAR, where the domain-specific model Tulip [298] significantly outperforms FunctaFormer. We hypothesize that, due to the sparse and irregular structure of the LiDAR scenes, more advanced training methods and post-processing steps could yield a large performance improvement.
