# Conclusion

In this thesis, we have carried out a comprehensive exploration of representation learning, traversing diverse domains and challenges within the field of deep learning. From active learning to the intricacies of disentangled representations, functional representations, transformer scaling and reasoning, we have delved into the core mechanisms that drive robust and intelligent AI systems. Our investigations have not only led to novel techniques and architectures, but have also highlighted the strengths and limitations of current state-of-the-art models, particularly in the area of reasoning. The introduction of new benchmarks and the analysis of scaling behavior on complex tasks have provided valuable insights that pave the way for future research aimed at improving the efficiency, scalability, and logical coherence of AI systems. As the field of AI continues its rapid evolution, the pursuit of more robust, interpretable, and generalizable intermediate representations within models remains a critical endeavor. The contributions of this thesis serve as a stepping stone to a future where AI systems can effectively tackle increasingly complex challenges and contribute to a deeper understanding of the world around us.
