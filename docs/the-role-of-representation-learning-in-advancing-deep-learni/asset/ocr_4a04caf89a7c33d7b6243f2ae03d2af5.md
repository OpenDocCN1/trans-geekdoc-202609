With the adaptation of the Rectified Linear Unit (ReLU) activation function [197], numerous studies in the field of Implicit Neural Representations (INR) have employed ReLU as their nonlinearity [207, 189, 96, 97, 190]. However, the authors of [245] laid the groundwork for this work by utilizing a simple sinusoidal activation function, demonstrating that such networks can accurately reconstruct various data modalities with low error and faster convergence speed.
