# 5.5 Conclusion

In this chapter, we introduced FunctaFormer, a versatile and domain-agnostic super-resolution architecture leveraging INRs to provide high-quality upsampling. Through various ablation studies and experiments, we demonstrated the effectiveness of spatial Functa and the adapted Swin Transformer backbone in achieving competitive super-resolution results across a variety of data modalities. While specialized models continue to excel within specific domains, FunctaFormer showcases strong performance, even outperforming in certain cases, such as 3D shape super-resolution.

Our findings underscore the potential of INRs and transformer-based architectures in unifying super-resolution approaches across heterogeneous data types.
