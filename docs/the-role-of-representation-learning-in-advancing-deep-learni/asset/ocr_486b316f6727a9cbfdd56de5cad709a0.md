$P_Q, P_K$ and $P_V$ as well as all feed-forward linear layer weights are initialized with values drawn of a truncated normal distribution parameterized as in eq. (C.3), where $a$ and $b$ are the lower- and upper-bound of the distribution and $\varphi$ is the probability density function of a standard normal distribution, with $\Phi$ being its cumulative distribution function. We set $\mu = 0, \sigma = 0.02, a = -2$ and $b = 2$ for all experiments. Biases are set to zero. Convolutional layers of all dimensionalities are initialized with PyTorch's default initialization scheme, which is uniformly drawn of $\mathcal{U}[-\sqrt{k}, \sqrt{k}]$, where $k = (c_{in} \cdot \prod_i d_{kernel}^{(i)})^{-1}$, $c_{in}$ are the input channels and $d_{kernel}^{(i)}$ is the i'th dimension of the kernel.

```
\( f(x \mid \mu, \sigma, a, b) = \frac{1}{\sigma} \cdot \frac{\varphi(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma})}{\Phi(\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}) - \Phi(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma})} \)
```

# C.3 Data

**Images.** For the image modality, we select the well-known DF2K dataset, which combines the DIV2K [6] and FlickR2K [165] datasets. DIV2K consists of 800 high-resolution training images, 100 validation images, and 100 test images. FlickR2K contains over 2560 training images. For our training set, we use the 800 training images from DIV2K along with the 2560 training images from FlickR2K. For ablation studies, we utilize the 100 validation images from DIV2K, splitting them into two halves: the first half for validation and the second half for testing, as the 100 DIV2K test images are not available for download. For comparison against current state-of-the-art methods on super-resolution tasks, we utilized the SET5 [19], SET14 [299], Urban100 [126], Manga109 [186], and BSD100 [184] image sets. These datasets are standard benchmarks in single-image super-resolution. Instead of resizing the entire dataset, we take random crops of size $120 \times 120 \times 3$ during training and validation. In terms of data augmentation, we add random horizontal flip the images with a probability of $p = 0.5$. To ensure a fair comparison with previous works, such as those by [65, 163, 124], we padded the test images to a size divisible by the SIREN slicing dimensions and FunctaFormer window sizes. Before evaluation, the images are cropped back to their original sizes, and we do not exclude the border pixels in our evaluation.
