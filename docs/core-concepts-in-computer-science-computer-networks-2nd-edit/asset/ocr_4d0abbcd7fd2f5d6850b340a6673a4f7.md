The Control field identifies sequencing and/or commands/responses. There are three types:

| Bits | 7 | 1 | 7 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---||
| Information Transfer (I) | Send sequence no. | 0 | Receive sequence no. | P/F |
| Bits | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 7 | 1 |
| Supervisory (S) | 0 | 0 | 0 | S | 0 | 1 | Receive sequence no. | P/F |
| Unnumbered (U) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | | |
| M | M | P/F | M | M | 1 | | |

P/F - Poll/Final
M - Modifier function bit
S - Supervisory function bit
