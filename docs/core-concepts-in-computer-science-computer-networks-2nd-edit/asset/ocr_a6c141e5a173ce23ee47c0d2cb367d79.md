# REFERENCES

+   1. ADSL, VDSL, and multicarrier modulation by John A. C. Bingham. Wiley, 2000. Another broadband book.

+   2. Behrouz A. Forouzan, “Data communication and Networking”, Fourth Edition, Tata McGraw – Hill, 2011.

+   3. Fax, Modem, and Text for IP Telephony by David Hanes and Gonzalo Salgueiro. Cisco Press, 2008. A comprehensive guide to sending non-voice data over telephone lines. Chapter 1 is a great introduction to how modems work, including a brief history, a look at the components of a modem, and an analysis of exactly what happens during a modem call.

+   4. James F. Kurose, Keith W. Ross, “Computer Networking – A Top-Down Approach Featuring the Internet”, Fifth Edition, Pearson Education, 2009.

+   5. Nader. F. Mir, “Computer and Communication Networks”, Pearson Prentice Hall Publishers, 2010.

+   6. Next-Generation Modems by Gilbert Held. Wiley, 2000. A detailed technical guide to DSL and cable modems.

+   7. Ying-Dar Lin, Ren-Hung Hwang, Fred Baker, “Computer Networks: An Open Source Approach”, Mc G Hill Publisher, 2011.
