The device, which converts digital signal into analog, and the reverse, is known as modem.

# Characteristics of Digital and Analog Signals

Digital and analog signals are two distinct types of signals used in various electronic communication systems and devices. Each has its unique characteristics, advantages, and applications. Let's explore the key characteristics of digital and analog signals:

# Digital Signal:

+   1. **Discrete Values**: Digital signals are represented by discrete values, typically in the form of binary digits (0 and 1). These discrete values make it easier to detect and correct errors during transmission.

+   2. **Noise Immunity**: Digital signals are more immune to noise and interference during transmission, as long as the noise levels are below a certain threshold. This is because digital signals only need to be distinguished between two states (0 and 1).
