# Preface

Computer networks have become incredibly important in our modern, technology-driven world. With the rapid advancements in today’s digital age, these networks play a vital role in connecting various devices and facilitating seamless communication. The ubiquitous internet that we use on a daily basis to the localized networks within our offices, having a solid grasp of the significance of computer networks is essential for anyone seeking to be well-informed. By understanding how these networks operate, individuals can work effectively.

A computer network refers to a system comprised of various interconnected devices like computers, servers, routers, and switches. These devices communicate with each other by exchanging data through wired or wireless connections. The primary purpose of these networks is to enable the seamless sharing of information and resources, promoting enhanced productivity and collaboration among individuals and organizations. Computer networks have various functions that contribute to their overall usefulness. One of the primary purposes is facilitating data sharing among connected devices. This means that files and information can be effortlessly transferred between devices, promoting effective communication and enabling centralized storage and backup of crucial data. By providing a centralized platform for data exchange, computer networks enhance efficiency and productivity in various settings. These networks not only streamline communication but also simplify the process of accessing and safeguarding important information.

Computer networks play a significant role in our daily lives, particularly in providing internet access. This access has become an essential part of our routine. Through the utilization of network infrastructure, individuals are able to conveniently and effortlessly explore the vast expanse of the web, exchange emails, enjoying streaming videos, and partake in other activities online. It is important to recognize that without the existence of computer networks, the abundance of information available on the internet would remain out of reach for us, greatly limiting our ability to tap into the wealth of knowledge
