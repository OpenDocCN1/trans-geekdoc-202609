Point-to-point networks provide a straightforward and efficient method of communication between two devices with dedicated bandwidth and low latency. They are well-suited for scenarios that require secure and reliable connections between specific endpoints. However, they may not be practical for larger networks due to the limitations of individual connections, making them more suitable for connecting remote locations or establishing dedicated links within a network.

# Personal Area Network

Some examples of devices that are used in a PAN are personal computers, printers, fax machines, telephones, PDAs, scanners, and even video game consoles.
