# Wireless Pen Testing

Wireless hacking was briefly discussed in a Chapter 12, “General Hacking Techniques”; however, in this chapter we will delve into details. In some cases, there may be a small amount of repeated information, but that is because that information is important.

The first issue is to define what wireless means. You are probably thinking of the wireless router in your home or office. This operates using radio waves usually at either 2.4 GHz or 5.0 GHz. However, wireless can also be achieved with infrared, Bluetooth, and cellular. All of these constitute wireless communications. We will begin by examining the basics of each of these.
