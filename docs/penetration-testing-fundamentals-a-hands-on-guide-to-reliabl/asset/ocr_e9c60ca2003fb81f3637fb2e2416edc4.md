# Conferences

In any field of endeavor, it is critical to keep informed. You have to know what the latest trends are, and you need to make connections in the community. With penetration testing, you are in effect living in two worlds. One world is the professional IT world, with a combination of corporate IT personnel and academics. The other world is the hacking community.

For hacking, the oldest and most respected conference is still DEF CON. While writing this book I presented a workshop at DEF CON 25. That should indicate to you how old this conference is (25 years as of this writing). That was my second presentation at DEF CON, and my impression is the same.
