Despite the widespread applications of machine learning fields (MLFFs) in solids and small molecules, there is a notable gap in applying MLFFs to simulate liquid electrolytes—a critical component of commercial lithium-ion batteries. We introduce ByteDance Artificial Intelligence Molecular simulation Booster BAMBO, a predictive framework for molecular dynamics, with a demonstration of its capability in the context of liquid electrolytes for lithium batteries. We design a physics-inspired graph equivariant transformer architecture as the backbone of BAMBO to learn from quantum mechanical simulations. Additionally, we introduce an ensemble knowledge distillation approach and apply it to MLFFs to reduce the fluctuation of observations from molecular dynamics simulations. Finally, we propose a density alignment algorithm alongside BAMBO which demonstrates state-of-the-art accuracy in predicting key electrolyte properties such as density, viscosity and ionic conductivity across various solvents and salts.
