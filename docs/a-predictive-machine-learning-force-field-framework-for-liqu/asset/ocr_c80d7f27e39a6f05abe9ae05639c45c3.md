# Initial training of BAMBOO

We use the GET architecture and structure to generate different interactions, using a loss function combining energy, force, viral and stress to train the neural networks in BAMBOO. Specifically, the charge is trained by BAMBOO is by four aspects: first is to train the neural network, force and charge and other with the electrostatic potential from charge density from electric potential; the third is to train the density, a macroscopic observable from charge densities by DFT $D$; and the fourth is to align the equilibrium structures.

In this work, we build neural networks in BAMBOO using PyTorch, and use the AdamMax optimizer at a learning rate of $10^{-2}$. In the training, the weight decay is set to 0.0001, while the learning rate decreases at a rate of $10^{-4}$. The batch size is 128. The model consists of three layers with an embedding dimension of 64. It features 16 attention heads, uses a cut-off of 0.5 Å per message passing layer and includes the fully connected networks with two layers for predicting energy and force.
