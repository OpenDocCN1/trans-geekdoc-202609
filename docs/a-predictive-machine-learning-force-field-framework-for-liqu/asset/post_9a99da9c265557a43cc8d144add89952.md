## Solution structures and atomic charges

In addition to the accurate predictions of macroscopic properties, BAMEO to explicitly predict atomic charges enables it to provide additional insights about solution structures in liquids. Here we present the Li- and O- charge-histograms derived from the three Li environments simulated at 0.5 m, 3.7 m, and salt concentration per kilogram. The weight ratio of Li to DMC, 1:49, remains consistent across all environments. The histograms of all three FST are presented in Supplementary Figures 15 and 16.
