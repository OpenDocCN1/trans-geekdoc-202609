The data in Table 3 is from high quality air quality meteorological monitoring data collected by 12 national air quality monitoring stations from January 2019 to December 2020. We divided the data into each season and measured the correlations using the Pearson correlation coefficient method. The correlation coefficients for each of the seasons were averaged by year to obtain the final correlation coefficient and use to sensitivity analysis.
Analysis of the most important variables showed that the meteorological variables were positively correlated with $PM_{25}$ in winter more than in the other seasons.
