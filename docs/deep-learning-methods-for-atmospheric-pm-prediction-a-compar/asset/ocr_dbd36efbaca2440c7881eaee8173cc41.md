# 2. Materials and methods

# 2.1. Research area and data

Beijing, located in the northern part of North China, the economic, social, and cultural center of China (39.90°N, 116.40°E); is bordered to the north by the Yan Mountains and the Taihang Mountains to the west so that the topography is high in the northwest and flat in the east. As the main base of heavy industry in China, Beijing conducts large amount of coal burning every year. The special geological and climatic conditions of Beijing limiting the location and dispersion of pollutants contribute to the concentration of pollutants in Beijing. In this study, hourly air quality data and meteorological data measured at 12 atmospheric air quality monitoring stations from January 1, 2013, to January 1, 2017, were obtained from the China Meteorological Data Network (http://data.cmc.gov.cn).
