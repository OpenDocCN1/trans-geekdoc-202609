## 4. Conclusions

In this study, we developed a Transformer-based method for predicting PM2.5 over a long period and compared its performance with the CNN-LSTM-attention model in terms of RMSE, MAE, and R² in order to determine how the model can capture the relationship between pollution factors and meteorological conditions. Using the historical data acquired in Beijing from 2013 to 2017 we used the results.
