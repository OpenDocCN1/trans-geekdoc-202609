#### 1. Public Procurement

When it comes to government activities, public procurement or matters related to government contracts is the largest marketplace for various levels of government spending. There are several reasons why this specific government process has become prone to a high level of corruption both in high-income countries as well as in developing countries. I need to add here that there are certain limitations to this use case. It is impossible for a blockchain platform to capture all aspects of human interaction.
