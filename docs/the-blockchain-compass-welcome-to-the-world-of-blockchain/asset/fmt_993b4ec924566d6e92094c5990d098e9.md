
Of course, the processes of commercial and financial markets will be redefined by these digitized instruments and this will lead to the creation of a new era where people enjoy value at every point. Here are some of the business benefits digital instruments offer:

i. **Programmable capabilities:** They provide codes that handle issues of compliance, governance, identity (`KYC/AML` attributes), data privacy, and system incentives. They also enable features that handle stakeholder involvement (such as voting) to be added to the assets.

ii. **Open the door to new products and markets:** They open the door to new products such as rapid transfers that are secure and scalable, tokenized micro-economies, fractionalized ownership of real-world assets, etc.

iii. **Help to streamline processes:** Part of the benefits of an increased level of automation is an increase in operational efficiency. Other excellent benefits of automation include the enablement of real-time settlement, audit as well as reporting, a reduction in processing time and the level of error and delays associated with various processes. It also helps to reduce the number of steps and third parties needed to get the same levels of confidence in existing centralized processes.

iv. **Fosters Data Authenticity and Scarcity:** Digitization does not only ensure data integrity but also enables asset provenance and provides a complete history of transactions all in one shared source of truth.

v. **Market reactivity:** When compared to standardized securities, digital securities give room for more customization and can easily be issued in shorter timeframes. With digitization, we now have the resources to develop unique digital financial instruments perfectly designed to suit the demand of investors.

## Capital Market and Blockchain Technology

The capital markets industry is witnessing an outstanding transformation in several areas, especially in business dynamics. This is as a result of the disruptions by new techs, regulations as well as modified economics of core areas of the business. Digitization has transformed various industries and the capital market is not left out as the industry has experienced dramatic changes to the prevailing mindset. After the 2008 crisis, many companies spent almost a decade trying to stabilize but it was not long before they were faced with expectations of new methods of business transactions courtesy of the digital revolution.

The implication of these new expectations also translates to making changes to the existing norms in the industry especially with challenges such as:

*   The cost of collateral (which is very high) after implementing regulations such as `Basel III` and `Dodd-Frank`.
*   The settlement cycles - instruments such as leveraged loans can take several weeks to settle trades.
*   Inefficiencies in various processes such as reconciliation.
*   The activities of intermediaries in asset management, payments, and several others often result in high transaction costs.

The emergence of blockchain technology also has several implications for the capital markets as they are now faced with another phase of disruptions. But it should be a welcome development because the adoption of distributed ledger technology in the capital markets will help solve some of the biggest challenges facing the industry. The capital markets can benefit immensely from the impact of blockchain technology, especially with the elimination or reduction of the roles that intermediaries play. Let's examine the various use cases of Blockchain in the capital markets.

### Trade Settlements in Real-Time

As a result of the absence of a mechanism that can carry out real-time monitoring of the positions of different financial instruments, trading parties will always experience an inherent trade default risk. Those who currently absorb these default risks are clearinghouses that also act as intermediaries. But the trade settlement cycle timelines have been extended as a result of intermediaries. A blockchain-powered system ensures that the moment a trade gets executed on an exchange, every crucial information about the trade will be passed to a smart contract and it will be maintained on a private or permissioned blockchain network.

Also, the smart contract will synchronize with the ledger positions of all the instruments that are maintained on the blockchain. It also carries out a real-time check to ascertain if the traded instruments are available. Check the illustration below to get a clearer picture of the entire process.

Of course, it is impossible to tamper with the rules already written on smart contracts. So, this guarantees transparency and trust for all trading entities and eventually leads to the settlement of trades in real-time.

Things like counterparty risks (exchange risk, credit risk and several others) will drastically reduce due to near-real-time settlements courtesy of blockchain technology. Also, it will help to eliminate challenges in communication, reconciliation and errors associated with settlement.

### The Tri-Party Collateral Management

To help in the management of collateral and counterparty exposure, financial institutions require the services of tri-party agents. What works with the present traditional system only enables an end-of-the-day view of collateral positions which causes higher collateral deposits. Eventually, the available collateral is not utilized optimally and this dramatically increases the cost of funding. Blockchain has an excellent solution to the collateral management issue.

For the blockchain solution, the allocation logic is coded on a smart contract and the contract has rules that are strictly meant to help regulate mechanisms – Wrong Way Risk, Eligibility Check, and Concentration Limit. Due to their volatile nature, privacy rules such as credit support annex terms and conditions will not be on-chain. Instead, they will be off-chain – on a private rules engine. The smart contract will communicate with the long boxes as well as segregated account positions before completing the allocation.

The distributed ledger serves as the platform where the allocated collateral positions will be maintained on a real-time basis and all stakeholders can easily view them.
