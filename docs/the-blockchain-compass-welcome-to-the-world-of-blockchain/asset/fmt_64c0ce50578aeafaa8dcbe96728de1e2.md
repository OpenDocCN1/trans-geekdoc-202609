
## 5. Trust

Since the metadata used for communication in blockchain-based systems is maintained in the digital ledger, data authenticity verification is carried out via several nodes. In the context of digital identities, this decentralization is extremely useful, especially in situations where national identifiers are often used across several agencies.

Here is a summary of what users are allowed to do with Self-Sovereign Identity:

*   Users can control their identities.
*   They can easily access and update their information (in some cases, there may be a need for third-party verifications with some claims).
*   They can transport their data to other organizations or jurisdictions in case they relocate.
*   Erase the identity if they need to.
*   Select the specific information they want to keep private.

Any system that can provide these services must put certain things in place. They must:

*   Ensure that identities have a long shelf life – forever is the best.
*   Use extremely powerful algorithms that will help validate users' identities as well as claims.
*   Protect the rights of users.
*   Ensure interoperability.
*   Ensure data sharing is strictly based on user consent.

### Merits of Decentralized Identity

Government regulations like the EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) have for several years made efforts to strengthen identity standards, especially those that need modern identity solutions. Governments are increasingly exploring ways to leverage blockchain technology to provide identities to individuals who are yet to be identified and also protect the personally identifiable data of their citizens. So, in what ways can blockchain be beneficial to decentralized digital identity? It offers three key benefits that will help governments and organizations achieve their goal of creating decentralized identities.

#### Decentralized Public Key Infrastructure (DPKI)

At the core of decentralized identity is **DPKI**, and digital ledger technology enables **DPKI** simply by establishing a trusted and tamper-proof medium suitable for the distribution of the asymmetric verification as well as the encryption keys of the identity holders. With **DPKI**, it would be possible for anyone to create or anchor cryptographic keys on the distributed ledger in a manner that is tamper-proof and chronologically ordered. The purpose of these keys is to grant other individuals access to verify digital signatures or even encrypt data to any given identity holder.

Initially, traditional certificate authorities were responsible for providing users with digital certificates. However, with distributed ledger technology, a centralized Certificate Authority (**CA**) is no longer required. Apart from being indispensable to decentralized identity, **DPKI** is also an enabler for other use cases – verifiable credentials (**VC**). When people mention the phrase verifiable credentials (**VCs**), they often talk about digital credentials that are accompanied by cryptographic proofs.

#### Decentralized Storage

Generally, digital identities that are stored on distributed ledgers are quite safer than the ones anchored on various centralized servers. When blockchains that are cryptographically secure, such as the Ethereum blockchain, are combined with distributed data storage systems such as **OrbitDB** or **InterPlanetary FileSystem**, then we can disintermediate centralized data storage systems already in existence and still maintain trust and data integrity.

One of the advantages of decentralized storage solutions that are designed to be tamper-proof is that they significantly lower the ability of an entity to gain unauthorized data access just to exploit or even make money from users' confidential information. Undoubtedly, one of the core aspects of secure identity data management is decentralized storage. Credentials in a decentralized framework are often stored on the devices of users like their tablets, smartphones, desktops, or private identity stores can also store them. These private identity stores are usually known as identity hubs, and excellent examples include **3Box** or **uPort's TrustGraph**.

Identities are regarded as self-sovereign when they are mainly under the user's control. What this also means is that the user is capable of controlling access to their data without being bothered that their access may be revoked. When a user's data is under their control, then the information will be more interoperable, and this enables a user to utilize the data on several platforms for multiple purposes and not limited to just a single platform.

#### Manageability and Control

The organizations providing the identity in centralized identity systems are mainly in charge of securing the identity data. This is not the case in a decentralized identity setup because it will now become the user's responsibility to ensure the security of the identity data. With the decentralized identity framework, the user is free to implement their specific security measures, and they can also outsource the task to an external service like a password-manager application or digital bank vault. Since decentralized identity solutions that are powered by blockchain technology compel hackers to attack individual data stores, they tend to be discouraged from doing so since it is quite expensive and often unprofitable.

### Decentralized Digital Identity and its Use Cases

All around us, we can see several sectors or industries that will benefit immensely from decentralized digital identity. In fact, some of these sectors are beginning to use blockchain technology. Let's take a look at some of the use cases of decentralized digital identity.

#### eCommerce

To prevent cases of fraud in eCommerce, you would require proper identification. One of the most common types of fraud issues in the eCommerce industry is that of fraudulent product selling. Most times, buyers are prepared to pay good money just to purchase authentic products. Unfortunately, they end up getting something entirely different from what was advertised on the eCommerce website. One of the reasons why this happens is as a result of the absence of appropriate protocols while signing up on any given eCommerce platform. There is a need for adequate verification to help safeguard the eCommerce sector, and decentralized identity can serve as the right foundation for this purpose.

#### Banking

Already, banks are at the forefront in terms of sectors that are making use of decentralized digital identity. Of course, you would agree with me that the banking sector handles a lot of issues related to user identity like repetitive identity thefts, **KYC** protocols, false identifications, etc. By making use of decentralized digital identity, banks can enjoy an easy solution to their identity-based issues. With this solution, users would not have to deal with so many issues whenever they want to open an account. All that is required is to give the bank permission to access your identity and also verify your credentials.

#### Government

One of the biggest sectors for decentralized identity foundation companies is the government sector. Governments around the world are responsible for issuing their citizens with official identification documents, but the entire process needs to be upgraded to meet current demands and standards. This is because it is often a slow process and one that is full of mistakes. One of the challenges of government-issued identification documents is that citizens lack full ownership access to their identity. This explains why government services need decentralized digital identity since it would drastically cut down on corruption, paperwork, and provide adequate security.
