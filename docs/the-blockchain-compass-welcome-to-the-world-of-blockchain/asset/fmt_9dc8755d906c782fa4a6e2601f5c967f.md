
# AI and Blockchain: A Powerful Combination

What we are facing now is the rapid development of several progressive technologies and things are really moving fast. We are in the era of artificial intelligence, distributed ledger technology, the Internet of Things, and several others all happening at the same time. While blockchain is transforming our perception regarding the internet's capabilities and enabling us to transparently and dramatically increase the speed of internet transactions, AI is also opening new doors to several aspects of business.

AI is already optimizing routine work and shifting the focus of human efforts toward more complex and important tasks. In case you are not sure what artificial intelligence means, it is often used to denote computers that execute their tasks where human contribution is required. The technologies that make this possible include deep learning, machine learning and neural networks. So, how can we benefit from the combination of AI and blockchain?

## Blockchain Offers Us a Better Explanation of AI Actions than Humans

Humans are capable of creating artificial neural networks and we can also educate the computer algorithms to increase the scope of what they can do based on our experience with assistance from machine learning algorithms. However, one issue that has raised concerns around the world is the fact that developers lack the ability to determine the actions of AI and they cannot even explain how it thinks. Also, the AI systems that operate complex decision trees are just like black boxes for human intelligence.

The summary of this explanation is that we lack a deep understanding of how artificial intelligence thinks. The primary reason why we are unable to understand how artificial intelligence thinks is that we cannot imagine the capability of the computer to analyze enormous amount of data. The average memory of a computer contains far more information than that of the most intelligent individuals that have ever existed. The machine's memory also needs to define the level of importance of each piece of information.

Well, we have what it takes to create an algorithm that can educate the computer to do this, but we also cannot ascertain how this kind of algorithm will develop.

When every decision of the AI system is recorded in a distributed ledger like blockchain, we can get an extensive database that will provide us with sufficient information needed to check all the decisions that AI will take and also explain their logic. Blockchain will also ensure the security of the data because it is not possible to falsify the information stored in the blockchain.

## AI can Increase Blockchain's Efficiency

Those who validate blockchain transactions are miners who utilize their computing power as they combine different characters to guess the right one before being rewarded, especially with proof of work consensus. Although many miners are competing to answer the puzzle first and get the reward, only one miner will get the reward. So, what happens to the energy other miners used in the process? It will all be wasted. We are likely going to see a future where AI can be trained by machine learning algorithms to guess the code intelligently rather than waste energy and time in guessing the right one. This will eventually increase the speed of the validation process and drastically reduce cost.

It is also important to talk about the size of data which is one of the challenges of using blockchain in our daily lives. We record every piece of information in the blockchain and every node on the network also has a copy of the data. However, the chain will become heavier as the number of blocks increase and this is where AI comes in. We can actually optimize the data storing techniques of blockchain with machine learning algorithms.

The truth is that the function of decentralized AI will depend on a parallel computing system that comprises various independent nodes that are positioned in different cities around the globe. This distributed network will ensure optimum usage of all its computational power to quickly analyze huge data sets. The dataset will be divided into smaller units that can be analyzed by different nodes and the final results will be combined in an integral database. A dominant company can control this worldwide database and every member of the network will have access to the information.

We can eventually train the advanced AI algorithms with this immense data. The combination of the two technologies will undoubtedly revolutionize how we conduct business and there are many possibilities. As blockchain technology continues to mature alongside AI, we are likely going to witness better use cases and other ways their integration can be useful.

## A Highly Siloed Blockchain Ecosystem

Blockchain was designed to be a decentralized technology, however, this does not imply that individual blockchain projects can communicate properly with other blockchain networks. Presently, we now have many public and private blockchain networks but they all have different features such as hashing algorithms, consensus models, types of transactions, etc. all focused on a specific area. This issue is even worsened by the fact that different networks and financial institutions all utilize totally different blockchain versions, governance rules and regulatory frameworks.

The outcome of all these differences is a collection of unconnected blockchain ecosystems where each network functions alongside the other ones but is siloed from others. This has prevented the blockchain industry from attaining its full potential. According to a ConsenSys research paper, *“If the existing blockchain networks continue to operate separately without communicating with each other, then we would have a scattered collection of siloed blockchains that are assisted by a weak network of nodes and prone to centralization, attack, and manipulation.”*
