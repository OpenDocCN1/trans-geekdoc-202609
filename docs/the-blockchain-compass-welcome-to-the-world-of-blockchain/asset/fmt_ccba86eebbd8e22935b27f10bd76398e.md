
# Blockchain in Capital Markets and Asset Management

## Capital Markets Adoption

One of the largest financial exchanges in the world is the Australian Securities Exchange (ASX) and it is actually ranked 16th in the world. Interestingly, the exchange is working toward adopting a distributed ledger technology-based alternative to replace its current CHESS clearing and settlement system. They have developed the system in collaboration with a blockchain consultancy known as Digital Asset Holdings. Bear in mind that ASX has a stake in the company.

Presently, ASX has scheduled an Industry Test Environment (ITE) to support adequate industry-wide testing before the platform will go live. Another effort to leverage the benefits of blockchain for the capital markets is a project that is even bigger than the first one. It is a partnership that will lead to the movement of the $11 trillion Trade Information Warehouse of the Depository Trust and Clearing Corporation (DTCC) to Axoni's Axcore blockchain – a New York startup.

The largest financial infrastructure in the world is owned by DTCC. The company handles securities transactions worth about $1.6 quadrillion each year. Also, the Trade Information Warehouse of the company services almost all global derivatives dealers around the world in addition to about 2,500 buy-side firms. The seriousness of DTCC in championing the integration of blockchain into capital markets is a strong indication that the industry is departing from its traditional systems that has been in use for decades.

Here is a summary of what blockchain will do when the capital markets adopt the technology:

*   It will help eradicate the single point of failure courtesy of its decentralized structure.
*   With the digitization of workflows and processes, there will be a significant reduction in operational risks of human error, counterparty risk and risks of fraud.
*   It will foster the streamlining process of the activities of the capital markets and this will help to decrease settlement times and costs.

## Blockchain and Asset Management

![Image](Image00037.jpg)

We have discussed how blockchain has disrupted several industries and several ways it can transform the way we do things. Distributed ledger technology has already transformed the way customers purchase various goods and services online and offline. Also, fundraising for companies has been disrupted by blockchain with the emergence of ICO. But one area that the technology can significantly disrupt is the way we manage our funds – asset management. Firms that adopt distributed ledger technology will enjoy amazing rewards because blockchain can transform the asset management value chain.

Despite the fact that the technology is still new, it has what it takes to transform and extend the business value chains of asset management businesses. Another reason why asset management firms need to take blockchain seriously is that we are still in the early stages of the development of distributed ledger technology and this signals opportunity.

Instead of providing clients with mere products, asset managers can manage data and offer their clients long-term solutions courtesy of blockchain. There are various ways the technology can assist managers in increasing their revenues while cutting costs. With blockchain technology, there will not be a need for intermediaries to assist with processing, verifying and safeguarding financial assets.

### 1. Compliance with KYC/AML Regulations

It is the duty of every financial institution to carry out proper checks on the information of their clients and understand the purpose of their business transactions. With digital identification that is stored on a distributed ledger, companies can automate the process of creating and verifying new accounts. It can also facilitate speedy compliance verification and make the process of monitoring transactions easy and efficient via the distributed ledger. However, one of the challenges assets management firms may face has to do with the cost of upgrading their centralized infrastructure.

During the process of client onboarding (preparing a client to make use of any given platform as well as products), several steps must be taken. The first thing is to confirm that clients are exactly who they claim to be. Unfortunately, it is becoming more difficult to efficiently verify people's identity, especially with the increasing cases of data breaches, malware attacks and hacking. However, distributed ledger technology can significantly reduce or eradicate most of these issues. In fact, blockchain's major application for asset management firms has to do with identity management and data security.

### 2. Distribution of Funds

When it comes to allocating capital gains as well as income from funds to investors, distributed ledger technology can also help in several ways. There will no longer be a need to distribute and transfer agent functions since transactions can now be recorded on the blockchain. Also, it would be possible to implement fund characteristics as well as terms of execution into a smart contract. This will significantly improve the efficiency and speed of the distribution process.

### 3. Smart Contracts

Clients can start placing orders after the confirmation of their identity and their account has been properly set up. Blockchain can actually receive transaction requests and then activate a smart contract with the specific terms and conditions of the transactions. Then it will also automate the processes of approval and clearing calculations. Once a client's request satisfies all the stipulated conditions in the smart contract, then the blockchain will transmit documentation securely and even secure funding for the new investment position via payment channels that are automated. All this will be executed within a few minutes, unlike the traditional system that takes asset managers several hours and, in some cases, days to complete.

### 4. Foster Operational Efficiency

In an industry where many companies invest hundreds of millions just to lower the execution time for trades from milliseconds to microseconds, one factor that is extremely important is data transmission speed. Faster data transmission times does not just mean reducing the time required for processing orders, it also means several other things. It means managers (this could be artificial intelligence and human beings) will be informed of breaking news that may affect the portfolio of their clients and can help them in dealing with challenges as they arise.

Several factors like the emergence of fintech mobile apps, online platforms that offer zero-commission securities trading and also the increased adoption of mobile payments, will increasingly expose the capital markets and eventually attract millions of investors from around the world to asset management. If asset management firms must handle the increased volume as well as the complex nature of requests and orders, then data transmission speed must be increased.

### 5. Regulatory Compliance

In terms of management reporting, investor communication and regulatory compliance; the secure, transparent and meticulous way that distributed ledger technology compiles transaction data will be extremely useful. Of course, asset managers are aware of the benefits offered by blockchain in streamlining and automating reporting and monitoring functions. In their quest to properly comply with information security rules, anti-money laundering regulations as well as privacy laws, they can significantly lower cost, enhance productivity and cut down on their risk of fraudulent activities and errors simply by leveraging blockchain technology instead of depending on people.
