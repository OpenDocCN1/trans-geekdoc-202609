
# Integrating Blockchain with Legacy Systems

One of the challenges that corporate organizations have faced is integrating blockchain with legacy systems. For companies that desire to use blockchain, they are required to carry out a total restructure of their previous system or come up with a solution on how to integrate both technologies. But the challenge with this integration is the lack of skilled developers since most companies are unable to access the required blockchain talent that would help accomplish this process.

Of course, they can manage the situation by employing the services of an external party; however, the majority of solutions in the blockchain space require that interested companies invest a lot of resources and time to make a successful transition.

Another factor discouraging most organizations is the high incidence of data breaches and loss during the transition to distributed ledger technology. Most companies are often very reluctant and reserved when it comes to modifying their database, and they have good reason for this approach. They are trying to avoid the risk of data corruption or loss which could negatively affect their operations. The blockchain arena is also working on solutions that would allow legacy systems to connect to a blockchain backend.

An excellent example of such a solution is `Modex Blockchain Database`. The product was created to assist individuals who lack any remarkable knowledge of the technology to enjoy the numerous benefits of distributed ledger technology and also eliminate any obstacles they may face as a result of the loss of sensitive information.

## Distributed Ledger Technology is Still Immature

Blockchain and DLT in general is still an immature technology, and this also explains why it has several implementation challenges. This brings us to some of the technical challenges facing blockchain technology.

### Scalability

Scalability is undoubtedly among the most difficult hurdles facing the blockchain space. You can see it as simply a lack of scale; what it means is that the rate at which transactions are processed on distributed ledgers compared to traditional platforms is limited. Presently, most legacy companies process thousands of transactions each second, but most top blockchain networks are still far behind. For private blockchains, scalability is not often an issue since trusted parties are involved in the activities of the nodes in such networks, and this is an excellent business strategy. The top blockchain networks such as `Bitcoin` and `Ethereum` have not been able to handle the level of transactions on their network effectively because of the structure of their networks.

There is a limit to the number of transactions that we can process on-chain, and this issue of lack of scale is a major challenge for organizations that require networks that can facilitate high transaction throughput while maintaining low latency. So, what are the available blockchain solutions to the issue of scalability?

#### 1. Off-Chain Solutions

Many blockchain platforms feel that off-chain solutions will boost the transaction speed of blockchain networks while decentralization and security should also be maximized on the digital ledger. In case you do not understand what off-chain scaling solutions mean, it has to do with solutions that permit the execution of transactions without over-burdening the blockchain. So, any protocol that makes use of off-chain scaling solutions permits users on their platform to send and receive funds while the details of the transaction do not appear in the main chain. Currently, many blockchain projects are exploring various off-chain solutions in a bid to resolve the issue of scalability, and we shall look at some of them.

#### 2. Accelerated Chips

`Skynet Core` is a forerunner in accelerated chips, and this solution could increase the speed of confirmation as well as transaction times. The project is focused on dealing with the challenges of the adoption of blockchain technology as well as the functionality of the Internet of Things (`IoT`). One of the core goals of `Skynet` is to provide an end-to-end system, and this includes an extremely scalable `IoT` blockchain network and a blockchain `IoT` chip that is license-free known as `Skynet Core`.

The project intends to provide billions of blockchain chips that are license-free, and they will be deployed to devices around the world that will connect through the company's blockchain network. This license-free blockchain chip is intended to replace the `CPU` we all use presently, and it has a core that is optimized for distributed ledger technology and `IoT`. With the hardware, `Skynet Core` devices can provide adequate protection from theft of digital currencies and easily run blockchain networks with high throughput.

#### 3. Sidechains

One of the most promising solutions for the issue of scalability is the use of side chains. It is simply a separate blockchain but not a standalone platform because it is somehow pegged to the main chain. So, both the sidechain and main chain are interoperable – assets can freely flow from one chain to the other. The primary reason for creating side chains is to cut down on the load that a blockchain network is having. Achieving this involves sending transactions through all the linked sidechains and also adding the final state of the transaction on the main blockchain. This mechanism helps to relieve the main blockchain of the burden of processing numerous transactions.

To ensure the transfer of funds, there are a variety of side chain solutions. For instance, some solutions move data from the main chain to a special address while a matching amount will also be issued on the sidechain. Another option is sending the funds to a custodian who will in turn exchange the deposited funds for funds on the sidechain.

#### 4. Sharding

`Ethereum` is one of the blockchain networks that is exploring the use of sharding as a scaling solution. Sharding simply has to do with spreading the computing and storage needs from just a blockchain network to single nodes. So, networks that adopt sharding split their blockchain into several separate areas known as shards. The network will assign to each of the shards a small group of nodes that they will maintain.

This implies that the nodes on the network will not process the transactional load of the entire network. All that each node is expected to do is to maintain the information that pertains to its shards or partition. The use of sharding eliminates the need for every single node in a blockchain network to be involved in a transaction.

#### 5. Multi-Layered Structure

This is also one of the solutions that blockchain projects are working on to help upgrade scale. It refers to the isolation of two separate aspects of blockchain technology – transaction processing and data storage. `Cardano` is one of the blockchain networks working on this solution. Commonly regarded as a third-generation blockchain, the project is a decentralized and open-source blockchain project that has a layered structure. Its structure comprises two key elements: `Cardano Computational Layer (CCL)` and `Cardano Settlement Layer (CSL)`.

The two-layered architecture of the project makes `Cardano` very unique since the majority of existing blockchain networks operate with a single layer, which eventually leads to network congestion, reduces the speed of transactions, and leads to spikes in fees. `Cardano`'s settlement layer handles transaction confirmation as well as the flow of the native coin. On the other hand, the computational layer of the network is responsible for maintaining the network's security. Also, it handles the deployment of smart contracts, and the core developers programmed it to recognize the identity of the data.
