
# Interoperability: A Major Challenge in Blockchain

Interoperability is one big challenge facing the blockchain space. There are over 6,500 projects making use of different blockchain platforms (most of which are standalone platforms) and solutions that have different coding languages, protocols, privacy measures and consensus mechanisms. The blockchain space is undoubtedly in a state of disarray due to the existence of many different networks and the absence of a universal standard that would permit each of these networks to communicate properly.

Since there is no uniformity across various blockchain protocols, basic processes such as security lack consistency. This renders the mass adoption of the technology a very difficult one. One of the things that could help enterprises to validate proofs of concept, collaborate on application development and share blockchain solutions is the establishment of industry-wide standards for different blockchain protocols. It will also help in making the integration of blockchain platforms with existing systems an easy task. The good news is that many blockchain projects are already offering interoperability among various blockchain networks.

## Major Interoperability Projects

Presently, most blockchain networks allow the creation of sidechains and as I mentioned before, sidechains are simply blockchains running alongside the main blockchain. But the number of interoperability projects in the blockchain space has increased recently. This is in response to the need for different blockchains to communicate with one another and increase the potential of the technology. We shall look at three examples of great interoperability projects.

### 1. Polkadot

This project is a high-profile multi-chain technology that is working toward improving interoperability in blockchain. Part of the core goals of Polkadot is to facilitate the transfer of smart contract data through different blockchain platforms. The project's ecosystem has multiple "parachains" that are simply individual blockchains. These parachains are different in terms of their characteristics, however, they still belong to the Polkadot ecosystem.

Transactions in the Polkadot blockchain are distributed over a wide area considering the network's number of chains. What serves as a connector of all the parachains is a relay chain and the goal of the Polkadot blockchain project is to enable public networks, private chains as well as oracles and permissionless interfaces to connect seamlessly. The team behind the interoperability project wants to enable an internet where independent blockchain solutions can leverage Polkadot's relay chain to exchange information.

### 2. Cosmos Blockchain

This blockchain interoperability project is one of the interesting ones in the blockchain arena and it runs on the fault tolerance protocol known as Tendermint Byzantine. One of the goals of the project is to be the hub of several projects. Its structure is made up of various independent blockchains (known as Zones) that are attached to a central blockchain called the "Hub." These Zones are plugged into the Cosmos Network and can communicate with one another courtesy of the Cosmos Hub. Also, it is possible to connect new ones.

One striking feature of the Cosmos Network interoperability project is that it allows zones to retain their consensus mechanism. What powers every zone, in this case, is the Tendermint Core which provides enhanced performance in addition to its secure and consistent consensus engine. Courtesy of the IBC connection, the Cosmos blockchain can easily connect various zones from public to private projects.

### 3. Blockchain Industrial Alliance (BIA)

There is also an increasing number of blockchain projects coming together to grant their blockchains the permission to communicate with one another. One excellent example is BIA which is an alliance formed by three blockchain projects (AION, WANChain, and ICON) to allow their blockchains to communicate with one another. The primary goal of this alliance is to deal with the issue of blockchain isolation. Members of the team have a common purpose of promoting interconnectivity between various isolated blockchains.

Their major area of focus is on research into interchain transactions as well as communication. They will also focus on creating common industry standards and every participating blockchain in BIA is working toward connecting blockchain protocols.

Before we can see the implementation of blockchain technology on a larger scale, major issues facing the existing blockchain platforms that we have discussed must be resolved. It is quite interesting to know that within a short time, blockchain has experienced rapid improvement in its structure and features. This is a relatively young technology and the launching of new projects that are attempting to proffer solutions to these issues is a sign that blockchain indeed has the potential to disrupt industries. These barriers will certainly be resolved sooner than most of us think.
