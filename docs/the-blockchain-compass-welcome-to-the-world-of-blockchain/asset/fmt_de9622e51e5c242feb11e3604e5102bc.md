
People are looking at ways to invest their funds in places where they can get the best returns while having possession of assets from different locations around the globe and not the ones their local financial markets have for them. Governments around the world should not censor themselves out of this amazing and historic movement that will eventually transform the global financial structure and render it more efficient. This movement is inevitable just like other forms of progress that we have ever made like the switch from the barter system to the use of coins, gold-backed money and fiat currency.

Every smart government will not hesitate to adopt DeFi as well as permissionless blockchains in addition to non-financial applications. This struggle is one that we cannot avoid just like every other remarkable change we have witnessed before. Most governments banning digital assets will eventually experience several pains before they discover that this movement is one they cannot oppose. But this fight may be well justified for reserve currency incumbents such as Japan, the European Union, the United States and of course China. It is crucial for new stablecoin projects to provide strong differentiation to the new CBDCs that are soon going to erupt.

There is minimal human intervention when it has to do with truly decentralized mechanics because they are purely based on algorithms. Some key stakeholders may argue that it is not possible to halt the operations of an app after its deployment to a blockchain network such as Ethereum. Well, if governments are interested in hampering the development and maintenance of such apps, it is possible. Governments can still go after their operators, founders as well as very active community members.

In fact, the well-known regulator, crypto-friendly and pro-government Brian Brooks had previously made straightforward comments along such lines. The impact of COVID-19 has further increased the drive for digital versions of some fiat currencies and this will undoubtedly strengthen the argument that governments may desire to hamper the development of stablecoins to protect their CBDCs. This also means that some projects in countries like the US such as Maker DAO may be at risk. Of course, governments may not go after such projects, but they are likely going to enforce certain control over their team, founders as well as the decentralized community.

What this also implies is that for a stablecoin to survive such regulatory clampdowns, it has to be as independent as possible from all forms of human intervention. Although the pseudonymity of leaders of a team is secondary, it is still very essential since it will help channel focus on the technology itself. We have already seen some interesting experiments in this direction with the emergence of governance-minimized approaches adopted by teams at Gambit, Lien as well as Basis Cash. Most stablecoins are presently pegged to the value of the global currency – the U.S dollar in different ways.

As long as they are collateral or algorithmic-based, this is not critical – based on the fact that governments may attempt to sanction, control or even freeze assets. On the other hand, for assets that are backed by US dollars and a basket of global fiat currencies, either directly or indirectly, this means a significant level of vulnerability. Remember, the US Federal Reserve is in charge of all USD-denominated bank accounts. What happens when there is a drastic change in the value of the US dollars (this may take place for a short time or even in several steps in line with history and macroeconomic theory)?

Of course, such assets are likely to become unstable and this explains why in the long run, a crypto-native stablecoin will be a better option. Presently, several projects like Float, Lien, Reflexer, etc., are all backed by ETH which is a decentralized asset. In fact, Float and Reflexer are actually leaving a strict peg to the USD and instead, they are working toward ensuring the stability and purchasing power of their tokens.

Please take note that these musings are purely a long-term view and not what we may expect soon – though things may change faster than we can imagine.

But one thing is certain; stablecoins that are backed by the US dollar remain the go-to source of liquidity in the blockchain and cryptocurrency space and will retain this position for a while. However, with the increasing tension between private stablecoins and CBDCs, state actors may eventually emerge to exploit the fiat dependencies of most of such projects. This scenario is inevitable because no government would sit back and watch as privately owned digital assets replace their national currencies. *“It would be a smart move to be prepared and participate in most of the emerging alternative experiments instead of just waiting to see what happens next.”* In terms of impact and size, this is certainly the biggest sector of DeFi.

## Blockchain Technology Can Help with Vaccine Tracking and Distribution

Before we witnessed the COVID-19 pandemic, the general perception of governments and most organizations regarding the use of distributed ledger technology such as blockchain for vaccine traceability was that the technology was not yet matured enough for immediate use. However, at the initial stage people were not aware of the critical nature of accountability in vaccine distribution from the first to the last dose.

### Factors Behind the Increased Use of DLT in Vaccine Distribution

When it comes to the distribution of goods, we have several stakeholders who are involved to varying degrees. This further leads to several issues in provenance, and authenticity, especially within several industries such as the pharmaceutical, wine and diamonds industries. We have earlier discussed the use of blockchain in supply chain management. The use of blockchain technology in vaccine distribution is closely related to the pharmaceutical supply chain use case which we earlier talked about. Governments and healthcare institutions have invested a lot of funds and efforts in testing, producing and distributing billions of vaccines.

Unfortunately, the COVID-19 vaccination distribution efforts are quite complex and highly scrutinized. Among the things that have posed significant challenges is the scale and volume of the rollout of the doses. Perhaps one of the greatest reasons why a digital ledger is essential is the speed at which the distribution of these vaccines must take place around the globe. The easiest and most effective way to create and also access a single record of all activities for all parties involved is to leverage blockchain technology. This will certainly build trust not just in the vaccine but also in the process.

While the first billion doses of the vaccine have been administered, we have already recorded thousands of counterfeit vials that authorities have seized. Predictions from experts point to an increase in the number of counterfeit vaccines which will eventually enter the supply chain. This prediction has motivated government officials in various locations to invest more resources toward investigating, managing and intercepting any fake supply.

### Blockchain in Vaccine Distribution

Several factors have made experts to forecast that there may likely be an increase in the rapid adoption of blockchain technology. Some of these factors include the magnitude and time pressure of COVID-19 vaccine rollout along with the increasing cases of counterfeit vaccines that are flooding the market. Many stakeholders now believe that distributed ledger technologies like blockchain may be what governments and healthcare organizations require to prove the authenticity of vaccines which will restore confidence and trust in the vaccine supply chain.
