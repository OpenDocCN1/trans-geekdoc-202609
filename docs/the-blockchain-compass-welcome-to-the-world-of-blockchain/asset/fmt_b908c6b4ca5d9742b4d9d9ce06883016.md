
# Banks can leverage blockchain's ability to manage, approve and instantaneously record any transaction

Banks can leverage blockchain's ability to manage, approve and instantaneously record any transaction, unlike the traditional processes that involve slow and manual authentication and verification processes as well as slow data sharing workflows most lenders are meant to depend on. Another benefit that blockchain technology brings to the lending industry is that of flexibility. It has opened the door for peer-to-peer loans as well as a variety of loan structures.

## Some Blockchain Projects in the Banking Industry

More blockchain projects are entering the banking and lending space as DeFi has seen a significant increase in popularity over the years. We can look at a few top projects in this space and what they intend to bring into the banking sector.

### Paxos

This project is actually the first blockchain-enabled trust leveraging DLT for settling assets and payments at the same time. With similar trust powers just like traditional banks, the token for the project (`PAX`) is an Ethereum-based stablecoin that is pegged to $1 and it enables instant settlement in digital transactions. There are many stablecoins in the crypto space and Paxos is among the few stablecoins that have received the approval of the strictest and toughest regulators – the New York Department of Financial Services.

Details of a Deloitte's study shows that on an annual basis, factors such as increased cyberattacks, rising costs and cost of global payment processing cost the finance sector billions of dollars. With decentralized ledger technology, it is possible to improve existing banking methods and upgrade loan offerings, credit monitoring, international payments and fundraising. Also, in the same study, banks have been encouraged to embrace amazing solutions from blockchain such as immutable smart contracts, dispute settlement technology and real-time finance tracking to have an edge over traditional banking.

Other blockchain projects in the lending space include `BlockFi`, `Binance`, `Celsius`, `CoinLoom` and `YOUHODLER`. Some of these lending platforms do not require users to have a minimum amount and one good example of such projects is `Celsius`. Also, some focus mainly on offering high returns on digital currencies while other ones are more interested in returns on stablecoins.

## Decentralized Finance (DeFi) and the Financial Sector

You can see DeFi as a global financial system that was designed specifically for the internet age. It is simply an excellent alternative to a system that is tightly controlled, opaque and largely depends on old infrastructure and processes that are several decades old. With DeFi, you now have control as well as visibility over your funds as well as exposure to global markets, and excellent alternatives to your banking options and local currency.

All that people need to gain access to DeFi products from any location around the globe is an internet connection and of course, many smartphone users can access the internet. Presently, several billions of dollars worth of digital currencies have been injected into various DeFi applications and this amount has continued to increase every day.

### Understanding DeFi

DeFi is an umbrella term meant for various financial products and services. All the services on DeFi applications that are slow and often affected by human error in traditional financial institutions are now fully automated and safer. They are not handled by centralized institutions and humans, but by code that we all can scrutinize easily. The booming crypto economy allows anyone to lend, borrow earn interest, buy, sell, and do so many things. Many organizations are now paying their employees with digital currencies and many people have taken and paid out million-dollar loans without personal identification. This is an amazing development in the finance industry.

Generally, DeFi makes use of smart contracts and digital currencies to render services without third parties. Financial institutions in our world act as guarantors of various transactions but this grants them power over your funds since your money always flows through them. But DeFi has successfully replaced financial institutions with smart contracts for every transaction that takes place. Smart contracts can hold users' funds and send or refund the money under specific conditions. Once a smart contract is live, no party can modify the terms; instead, it will run strictly as it was programmed.

### Why DeFi?

You can easily identify the potential of DeFi by looking at the problems facing the traditional finance industry. First, due to identification issues, some individuals are unable to create a bank account or make use of various financial services. Also, some of these financial services can hinder people from getting paid due to several bottlenecks. When people lack access to financial services, they may not really be employable. Centralized institutions and governments can also shut down markets at will which will affect the users. Money transfers often take several days as a result of various human processes.

The presence of intermediary institutions that want to get a cut of people's money adds a premium to financial services. The list of issues associated with traditional finance is much. #### Things you can do with DeFi

For every financial service out there, there is a decentralized alternative to it. Ethereum and other smart contract platforms offer the chance for developers and entrepreneurs to develop unique financial products that are entirely new and the list of DeFi services has continued to increase.

i. **Global money transfer services:** DeFi Makes it quite easy to send funds to any location. It's as simple as sending an email from your phone or laptop and the recipient will get the funds within minutes.

ii. **Stream money globally:** With platforms like the Ethereum blockchain, you can stream money. This feature enables you to pay your employees their wages within seconds and also allow them to access their funds whenever they require it. Apart from streaming `ETH`, you can also stream other stablecoins if you are concerned about the volatility of the cryptocurrency. Several amazing alternatives suit a wide range of customer needs.

iii. **DeFi offers Stablecoins:** One of the aspects of blockchain that has drawn the attention of governments around the world is stablecoins. They were created to help cushion the effect of the volatile crypto market. The volatility of cryptocurrencies affects most financial products and services but the perfect DeFi solution to this issue is the creation of stablecoins. The value of stablecoins is often pegged to other valuable assets and currencies. For instance, one `USDT` is pegged to $1 while the value of others like `USDC` or `DAI` remains within a few cents of a dollar. This also provides users the chance to earn and a good number of individuals in Latin America, as well as some African countries like Nigeria, are protecting their savings due to uncertainty in the value of fiat currencies in their countries.

iv. **Borrowing:** Decentralized providers offer two varieties for borrowing funds:

a. Pool-based systems or platforms where individuals who want to lend provide liquidity (funds) to the pool. Then people can borrow from the liquidity provided while those who provide liquidity earn a commission.

b. Peer-to-peer systems where a borrower can borrow funds directly from any lender of their choice.
