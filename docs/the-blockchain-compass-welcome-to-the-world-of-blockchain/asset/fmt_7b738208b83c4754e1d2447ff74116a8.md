
# Decentralized Digital Identity: Use Cases and Platforms

## Gaming Industry
You would agree with me that the gaming industry is a huge one and one of the things that the gaming industry deals with is the identity of gamers. Unfortunately, the industry has not been able to provide adequate security for gamers in most cases. Another point is the fact that most of these game apps also have in-app purchasing features. This further increases the need for adequate security since such apps will demand credit card numbers. Decentralized digital identity can provide the decentralized identity foundation with regulations that will shape the entire process thereby eliminating the cases of theft of credit card details.

## Healthcare
The truth is that most healthcare institutions have not been able to properly secure the personal and health information of their patients. Consequently, most of these data are used for private research while other healthcare institutions access their patients' sensitive information without the necessary approval of the owners. With a decentralized identity foundation, patients can now enjoy full control and privacy they deserve and healthcare institutions can protect all data related to their patient's health.

## Ideal Enterprise Platforms for Decentralized Identity

The blockchain space already has several amazing enterprise blockchain platforms that are perfect for managing a decentralized identity management platform. They provide excellent services for various identity issues.

### Hyperledger
This is an enterprise blockchain project with several projects already running on this platform. Hyperledger actually offers great projects that are appropriate for decentralized identity management and one of such projects is called `Hyperledger Indy`. This is a distributed ledger that works primarily with digital identity. The distributed ledger also comes with other features like libraries, components and tools that are required to create your decentralized identity management system.

In addition to this, it is also possible to use your identity on different platforms. One of the projects Hyperledger launched is known as `Aries` which graduated from incubation to active status earlier in 2021. The project serves as a toolkit and it is just like you creating your platform with various features on the module. Features of `Aries` include:

*   High-level performance and increased scalability.
*   Offers permissioned blockchain platforms as well as open-source toolkits.
*   Data availability is strictly on an "only need-to-know basis."
*   Provides increased safety measurements to ensure the safety of sensitive information.
*   Provides query languages to help search the ledger.
*   Offers a plug and play modular structure.

### Ethereum
Perhaps, the next most popular blockchain network after Bitcoin is `Ethereum` and the project has witnessed increased adoption over the years. The brain behind the project alongside other core developers is `Vitalik Buterin` who during the bullish cryptocurrency market became the youngest crypto billionaire when `Ether` hit its all-time high. The purpose of the `Ethereum` blockchain is for enterprise-grade usage, but it is permissionless. This could lead to several challenges, especially for decentralized identity management systems since privacy is the primary need for most users. However, `Ethereum` offers a private version for enterprises via `Enterprise Ethereum Alliance`. With the private version of its platform, anyone can develop a secure decentralized identity management system. The striking features you will get to enjoy from this platform are:

*   Open-source platform.
*   Constant updates from the public version.
*   Users will enjoy authoritative support from `Enterprise Ethereum Alliance (EEA)` for different kinds of projects on the `Ethereum` platform. This is useful for completing guidelines and standards.
*   The platform provides several standards that would make it straightforward for anyone to develop platforms of different types.
*   The `Enterprise Ethereum Alliance` also offers governmental support for all the projects developed under them. What this means is that if you discover that another organization developed several attractive features you require, `EEA` will provide you with the same features too.

## Projects in the Digital Identity Space

Already, several real-life digital identity solutions have emerged over the years and more are being developed. Here are some of the popular digital identity companies working on this unique identity model structure:

### Civic
This project is a personal identity verification process that offers digital identities courtesy of distributed ledger technology. When you make use of this platform, it would be possible to not just create your virtual identity but also store other information too. But before you can store your information on the digital identity solution, you need to verify all your credentials. Those who desire to grow their business on these digital identity companies can use any of the four different solutions:

*   Reusable `KYC` which helps to manage all forms of `KYC` requirements.
*   A "passwordless" login.
*   Secure ID platform that allows users to access any website via a secured identity.
*   Automated retail grants users access to smart vending machines.

There are individual ID codes for all forms of advisory, investment and business relationships.

### Sovrin
Another popular project that offers users Self-Sovereign Identity is `Sovrin`. The non-profit organization works with decentralized digital identity solutions to enable users enjoy trust, control and digital identities that are easy to use. The project boasts of offering a new standard for digital identities and users can obtain a digital copy of their IDs as well as their driver's license. The project is a metasystem that gives individuals and organizations the opportunity to use any application without the issue of their identities bothering them.

### uPort
With the `uPort` project, it is possible to enjoy full freedom of control over your identity. The project is built on the `Ethereum` blockchain which as earlier mentioned is among the top platforms for decentralized digital identities. On the `uPort` platform, users simply need to create their digital identity solutions. It is possible to either request or send credentials, sign transactions, manage their data and keys and perform several others tasks. This platform offers users credentials – an ecosystem of identity credentials. You can easily demand for other users' credentials with your app but this is subject to their approval – whether they are prepared to share with you or not. Also, the platform's developer's tool comes in the form of a modular structure which is not difficult to use. This type of digital identity company also offers you access to several protocols and messaging options.

## Conclusion
As our world goes through an outstanding digital transformation era, we are gradually seeing the merger of the physical and digital world into a single reality. Undoubtedly, there is now an urgent need for great ways to maintain our digital identities which is also on the increase. As many projects enter the decentralized digital identity space, those that will stand out are the ones that have security and privacy in all aspects. Decentralized digital identities grant users complete control of their identity rights while making it easy to manage as well. In the future, we are likely going to witness an increase in the number of decentralized identities.
