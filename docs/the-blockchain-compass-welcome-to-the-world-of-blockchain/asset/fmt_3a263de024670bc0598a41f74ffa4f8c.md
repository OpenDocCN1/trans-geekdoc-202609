
# Blockchain Applications in Government

## 1. Land Registry

With land registries that are based on distributed ledger technology, it is possible to have a decentralized, secure, immutable and publicly verifiable record system that would allow people to prove their land rights. The qualities that blockchain provides to this sector will not just increase the resilience of land ownership but significantly lower the opportunity for the manipulation of land rights for selfish interests. But blockchain alone may not be sufficient to formalize property ownership in countries with incorrect, nonexistent or incomplete land registries. Countries in this category may have to gather, clean and digitize their land ownership information before enjoying the benefits of a blockchain-based land title registry.

## 2. Blockchain and Smart Cities

Smart cities leverage the combination of information technology and data to integrate and manage infrastructures (business, social, physical infrastructures) to streamline services to residents while ensuring that the available resources are optimally and efficiently utilized. Governments around the globe can render innovative solutions and services to their citizens as well as local municipalities by combining IoT, distributed ledger technology and cloud computing.

Governments can establish a secure infrastructure that can help in managing various functions by using blockchain. With blockchain-powered interoperable infrastructure, smart city functions and services can exceed current projections. Presently, ConsenSys has handled projects toward the realization of smart city initiatives in countries like Dubai. The Smart Dubai initiative which is headed by Sheikh Mohammad bin Rashid Al Maktoum is focused on ensuring that Dubai becomes the happiest city in existence courtesy of IoT, AI and blockchain technology.

## 3. Central Banking

One of the activities that governments around the globe engage in repeatedly is real-time gross settlement. It involves settling interbank payments in central bank records and since blockchain facilitates a remarkable increase in network resilience and transaction volume, central banks can process real-time gross settlement faster and securely. Later in chapter 16, we shall look at central bank digital currencies and how they can change the way government handles money.

## 4. Voting

Regardless of your beliefs, one thing that most people agree with is that attempts to influence voting systems has been an age-long practice and it remains one of the greatest issues countries are dealing with. Most people attempt to trick their way into power as a result of the nature of the political environment. There have been increasing concerns regarding the integrity of voter registration, voter turnout, election security and poll accessibility.

These issues have motivated governments to explore the viability of a blockchain-based voting platform to boost participation and faith in the democratic process. Blockchain features can support voting by fostering immutability and transparency all through the election process. The decentralized, encrypted, transparent and immutable qualities of distributed ledger technology like blockchain can drastically reduce cases of election tampering while boosting poll accessibility.

Horizon State is a project that was established mainly to meet the needs of organizations and individuals who are fed up with the costly and slow paper ballots as well as the insecure electronic voting services. The project's secure digital ballot box provides a smart solution to the challenges facing the existing voting procedures and it is also cost-effective.

All that a participant requires to cast votes from their laptop or smartphone is Horizon's digital token (HST). The votes are instantly logged into an immutable blockchain which will serve as a reliable database for verifying election results. With this proposed system, it would be extremely hard to record errors, manipulate or tamper with the results. Apart from being useful for voting, the platform is very useful for other purposes. In environments where authority and resources are shared, it will be useful for decision-making and can motivate people to participate.

Another issue that is common with voting is voter apathy – a situation where voters fail to cast their votes. This issue increased recently but with projects like Horizon, anyone can conveniently cast their votes from their smartphone. People who are scared of assault or intimidation in voting centers can confidently cast their votes.

## 5. Blockchain and Validation of Credential

Blockchain-based systems for credential validation is another excellent use case of the technology in government processes. Individuals can enjoy more control of access to their data by storing their academic and professional certificates on an encrypted identity wallet courtesy of blockchain technology. This system can also help schools, employers and universities to easily validate “attestations” for work and courses achieved by employees and students.

## 6. Vaccine Distribution

A blockchain-based distribution network for vaccines will ensure that manufacturers of the vaccine monitor its application proactively for possible challenges and enjoy improved recall management. Also, distributed ledger technology can offer distributors real-time visibility as well as increased capacity to attend to possible disruptions in supply chain. Dispensers are not left out as blockchain can help them improve inventory management and effectively monitor the safety of the vaccines. With blockchain, people can trust the vaccines more and this will in turn help boost global economy.

## 7. Tracking Student Loans and Grants with Blockchain

Governments can program smart contracts to help manage loans and grant applications. It can also be useful for dispensing loans and for monitoring compliance with all the terms and conditions of the loan. With distributed ledger technology, it would be possible for government agencies to automate performance tracking which will, in turn, engender real-time data as well as increased compliance and security.

## 8. Tax Collection

Of course, the tax collection process will experience a remarkable transformation courtesy of smart contracts. With smart contracts, governments can easily streamline the entire process of tax collection by matching tax data with income transactions and calculating social security and tax deductions. It would be possible to automatically transfer net salary as well as tax payments to each recipient and this leads to increased speed, efficiency and security of the process.

In our quest to deal with malicious actors, more blockchain use cases will certainly emerge. With the discovery of more use cases, the uniqueness and untapped potential of blockchain to deal with corruption in government should not discourage policymakers from deploying blockchain to the public sphere. The technology has the potential to make government processes efficient, fast, secure and transparent.
