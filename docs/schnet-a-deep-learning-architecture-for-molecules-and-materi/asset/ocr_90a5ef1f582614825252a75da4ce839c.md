# E. Application to molecular dynamics of C$_{20}$-furanide

After demonstrating our SchNet on the MD benchmark set, we performed a method selection using a ML-driven MD simulation of C$_{20}$-furanide. This mid-sized molecule has a complex potential field that requires to be described with vibrational normal modes and degrees of freedom. Here we use SchNet to perform analysis of basic properties of PES of C$_{20}$ when including quantum effects. The reference data we generated by running molecular MD at 300 K DFT in the generalized gradient approximation (GGA) level of theory used the Perdew-Burke$^{39}$ exchange-correlation functional and the Tkachenchenko-Schffler$^{40}$ TS method. Further details about the simulations can be found in Appendix B.
By training SchNet on DFT data at the PBE+vdW levels, we reduced the simulation time by orders of magnitude from 11 hours using 32 CPU cores to 10 ms using NVIDIA GTX1080. This allows us to perform long MD simulations with DFT accuracy at low computational cost, which is making this feasible.
