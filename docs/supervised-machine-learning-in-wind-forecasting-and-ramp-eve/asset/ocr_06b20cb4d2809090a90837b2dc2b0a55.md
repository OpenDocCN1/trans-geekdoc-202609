# 4.5 $\epsilon$-twin support vector regression

Derived from a twin support vector machine, Shao et al. [17] propose a novel regressor, $\epsilon$-twin support vector regression ($\epsilon$-TSVR), which determines the pair of $\epsilon$-insensitive functions by solving two convex optimization problems. In terms of the objective function to be minimized, $\epsilon$-TSVR considers an added regularization term that solves the ill-conditioning problem of $Q^TQ$ as described in the previous section. The formulation of primal objective functions for $\epsilon$-TSVR is

```
\(\begin{aligned}
\min \quad & \frac{1}{2} C_3 (w_1^T w_1 + b_1^2) + \frac{1}{2} \xi^{*^T} \xi^* + C_1 e^T \xi, \\
& \text{s.t. } Y - (Xw_1 + e b_1) = \xi^*, \\
& Y - (Xw_1 + e b_1) \geq -e \epsilon_1 - \xi, \xi \geq 0, \\
\min \quad & \frac{1}{2} C_4 (w_2^T w_2 + b_2^2) + \frac{1}{2} \eta^{*^T} \eta^* + C_2 e^T \eta, \\
& \text{s.t. } Y - (Xw_2 + e b_2) = \eta^*, \\
& Y - (Xw_2 + e b_2) \geq -e \epsilon_2 - \eta, \eta \geq 0.
\end{aligned}\)

```

In this optimization problem, $C_1, C_2, \epsilon_1, \epsilon_2$ are the hyperparameters that determine the regression performance. The Lagrangian function for these two primal problems can be written as

```
\(\begin{aligned}
L(w_1, b_1, \xi, \alpha, \beta) = \frac{1}{2} & (Y - (Xw_1 + e b_1))^T (Y - (Xw_1 + e b_1)) \\
& + \frac{1}{2} C_3 (w_1^T w_1 + b_1^2) + C_1 e^T \xi \\
& - \alpha^T (Y - (Xw_1 + e b_1) + e \epsilon_1 + \xi) - \beta^T \xi, \\
\end{aligned}\)

(4.28)
```

where $\alpha = (\alpha_1, \alpha_2, \dots, \alpha_n)$ and $\beta = (\beta_1, \beta_2, \dots, \beta_n)$ are the Lagrangian multipliers. To obtain the dual of the stated primal objective functions, KKT conditions are given by

```
\(\left\{\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial L}{\partial w_1} = 0 \Rightarrow & -X^T(Y - Xw_1 - e b_2 - e \epsilon_1) + X^T \alpha + C_3 w_1 = 0, \\
\frac{\partial L}{\partial b_1} = 0 \Rightarrow & -e^T(Y - Xw_1 - e \epsilon_1 - e b_2) + e^T \alpha + C_3 b_1 = 0, \\
\frac{\partial L}{\partial \xi} = 0 \Rightarrow & C_1 e^T - \alpha - \beta = 0, \\
\frac{\partial L}{\partial \alpha} = 0 \Rightarrow & Y - (Xw_1 + e b_1) \geq -e \epsilon - \xi, \xi \geq 0,
\end{aligned}\right.\)
```
