A GRA-based framework is used to study wind forecasting in the presence of wind wakes. Benchmark wake models like Jensen’s and Frandsen’s models are tested against a novel bilateral Gaussian wake model. The proposed wake model is based on Gaussian variation of Jensen’s and Frandsen’s models. The model is tested for two datasets D1 and D2 considering single-wake and multiple-wake scenarios. The proposed model outperforms Jensen’s and Frandsen’s models. Further, forecasting is carried out where the wind speed time-series is decomposed into approximate and detail signals using the Daubechies wavelet with a five-level decomposition. The wavelet transform removes the noise components present in the series, and SVR is used to forecast wind speed. GRA is used as an important tool to identify the upstream turbines significantly contributing to wake effect. Results reveal that for both wind farm layouts, GRA improves the forecast accuracy.
