When you are setting up your home WiFi, you need to set it up with a password. Many routers come with a sticker on the side of the box that will give you access to that network. Make sure that you change it! Do not stay with the default password because it is entirely too easy to be hacked for someone to be gain access to your network.

Also, when the machine asks which encryption you want, it is best to choose WPA-2.

It is wise to attempt and try and use WEP and WPA. The encryptions actually have a flaw that will give you the password to your network within a few seconds.

At the time that your home router is set up, it will ask you if you want to hide the SSID. When you click yes, your WiFi will be hid from others, but it will force your devices to constantly search for the network that they are connected to. Your devices will connect, but in the time that is spent searching for the network, it will open up your device to connecting to a strange network.
