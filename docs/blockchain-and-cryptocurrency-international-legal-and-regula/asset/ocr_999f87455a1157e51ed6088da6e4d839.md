# The Metaverse

12.1 There is a great deal of confusion about what the Metaverse is and what it can actually do. In order to properly understand the meaning of a Metaverse, it will assist if one first appreciates its technological characteristics. The Metaverse is a form of internet application, what some might term an extension of our existing internet version Web 2.0 and it combines a number of new or emerging technologies. This combination of technology, that might be termed multi-technology, sees a convergence of artificial intelligence, blockchain, spatial-temporal consistency, cybersecurity and privacy. There are in fact a multitude of technological combinations and possibilities but the fundamental common technologies or common denominators of the Metaverse comprise of at least the above. It is important to note that Artificial Intelligence (AI) Algorithms, for example machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning and the like, are essential to connect a virtual world and real world. AI’s components including information, algorithms and computing power must play an important role in establishing and developing a Metaverse. It is by using AI that a Metaverse can create social and other economic activities that are further than the boundaries of what we experience in our actual, real world. Using computer vision, intelligent voice, language processing and other technologies users will be given the same visual and auditory sensations as in the real world.
