# NATURAL RESOURCES INDUSTRY

**1.45** Blockchain technology will likely have an impact across almost every aspect of the natural resources industry, and **Chapter 10** is devoted to studying the potential impact on natural resources. The potential benefits range from real time data registry as gas and oil come out of the ground, or electricity is produced, to the possibility of peer-to-peer energy selling, disintermediation and the creation of a smart energy grid. These benefits are often, and first by the Energy Web Foundation, grouped under the headings of:

+   - decarbonisation;
+   - decentralisation;
+   - digitisation; and
+   - democratisation.^9^

**1.46** These four interconnected categories are often defined as the long-term aims of blockchain implementation in the natural resources industry, and we discuss the possibilities as well as the regulation that is needed to help address the world’s natural resource problems. Those problems are usually grouped in to the ‘trilemma’ comprising:

+   - security, given the World’s finite resources;
+   - decarbonisation; and
+   - affordability.

The war in Ukraine has brought these concerns in to sharp focus, especially the need for affordable oil and gas. **Chapter 10** examines the position through the blockchain prism and considers whether its application might be able to resolve or mitigate the concerns around our energy and natural resource needs.
