# 6.71 Global regulation: the rest of the world

6.71 Blockchain-based applications may also require authorisation. In each case the provider must ascertain in advance whether authorisation may be required under the Stock Exchange Act or the Financial Market Infrastructure Act which may well be apply where cryptoasset trading platforms are in operation.

6.72 FINMA may investigate where it discovers an activity involving virtual currencies or other blockchain based applications is being carried out without the requisite authorisation; any contravention of licensing requirements under supervisory law is a criminal offence and consequently a criminal prosecution may follow such a breach.
