4.51 French regulations mandate that crypto firms be registered. The French PACTE law was introduced in 2019, regulating the crypto sector by three basic principles and apply to all crypto firms operating in France, including crypto-to-crypto exchanges and trading platforms.

+   1. Legal status. Crypto firms are legally defined as digital asset service providers, or DASPs (PSAN in France).
+   
+   2. AML/CFT obligations. The AML/CFT obligations mandated by French regulators are based on the principles of the EU's Fifth AML Directive, which France implemented in January 2020.
+   
+   3. Mandatory registration. Any crypto firm operating in France or working with French clients must register with the Financial Markets Authority (AMF), a French financial regulator.

# 4.52 In addition there exists:

+   - A ban on anonymous transactions. This has effectively banned anonymity on the French crypto market, as even small crypto transactions must be accompanied by KYC.
+   
+   - Expanded KYC obligations. This includes crypto-to-crypto exchanges and trading platforms.
+   
+   - Expanded registration requirements. From now on, French crypto-to-crypto services and trading platforms are obliged to register managers and beneficial owners with the Financial Markets Authority (AMF).
+   
+   - Expanded AML/CFT obligations:
+     - identification and verification of customers and beneficial owners;
+     - authentication of documents;
+     - identification of the purpose and intended nature of business relations;
+     - ongoing monitoring;
+     - screening (sanctions, watch lists).
+   
+   - Enhanced due diligence for higher-risk business relationships.
+   
+   - Third-party CDD/KYC.
