# **3.82** *Regulatory and legal challenges*

**3.82** The unique issues facing lawyers advising on arrangements based on smart contracts will require them to consider matters such as the ownership of data, IP ownership, jurisdiction and dispute resolution.

**3.83** The Law Commission Report on Smart Contracts^33^ was recently released (November 2021) and suggested that Smart Contracts do have validity in UK jurisdiction. This was in part due to policy concerns about making London the cryptoasset trading hub of the world or at least Europe to pivot its previous status as a financial centre and in part due to the increase in their use and subsequent high value of civil litigation.

**3.84** In our experience the best way to deal with the type of issues set out above, is to draft a term sheet, or term text which sits alongside the smart contract and is given to the smart contract creator. This ‘text’ does not have to be long or have the complexity of a traditional contract but even where smart contracts are at their most valuable in commonplace situations, it is worth having the issues outlined above, fully addressed.
