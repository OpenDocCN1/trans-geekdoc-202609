8.57 On 9 November 2020, ENISA published Guidelines on Securing the IoT Supply Chain55 to complement Baseline Security Recommendations for IoT. The report is designed to address the entire lifespan of IoT product development by offering security measures for each step, and builds on the 2019 Good Practices for Security of IoT – Secure Software Development Lifecycle.56 ENISA's Good Practices for Security of IoT introduces good practices for IoT security, with a particular focus on software development guidelines for secure IoT products and services throughout their lifetime. ENISA suggest that establishing secure development guidelines across the IoT ecosystem, is a fundamental building block for IoT security, and the study tackles aspect for achieving security by design, a key recommendation that was highlighted in the ENISA Baseline Security Recommendations.

8.58 Compliance with the Baseline, Good Practises for Security of IoT, and Guidelines on Securing the IoT Supply Chain represent best practice, worthy of ‘high’ certification, rather than the ‘basic’ level requirements under the Cybersecurity Act. Those working within the IoT sector, and who wish to trade within the EU, should consider an audit of their current position against these recommendations.
