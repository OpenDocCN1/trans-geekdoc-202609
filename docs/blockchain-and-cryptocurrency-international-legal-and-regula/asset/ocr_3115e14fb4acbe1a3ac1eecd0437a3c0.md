# THE METAVERSE'S REGULATORY FRAMEWORK

# Contract

12.7 When one looks at the laws and regulations that will likely govern any Metaverse the starting point has to be contract law. The reason for this is quite simple; there are already a number of Metaverses and each is owned and controlled by its company or organisational owner. It is clear to the authors that whatever Metaverse one seeks to enter the first proceeding step must be to agree to its terms. Those can be terms of service or terms of use or end user licence agreement but whatever badge is given to that document it essentially sets out the contractual position between the user and the Metaverse's owner. These contracts are intended to be legally binding documents that set out in detail the rights, duties and expectations of those who use the Metaverse and the terms and conditions will govern, in some fine detail, exactly what is and what is not possible. Those contractual terms may not only deal with the concept of ownership in the Metaverse but may also extend to other aspects such as Intellectual Property rights and the powers and decisions of the owners in the context of the Metaverse.

12.8 We will look at this in a little more detail later in this chapter but, safe to say, the usual and expected position is that these Metaverse terms of use will put the owner in a position of strength over the user. Moreover, a user not only has very little bargaining position, if any bargaining position at all at the point of entry but most users will not even read the contractual documentation before scrolling down and clicking their acceptance.
