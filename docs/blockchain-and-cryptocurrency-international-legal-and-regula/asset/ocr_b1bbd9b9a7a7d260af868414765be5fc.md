**7.8** The characteristics of the project are commonly compiled in a White Paper which is generally unregulated. Whether or not this White Paper meets the requirements for a ‘regulated prospectus’ it will include technical information, business information, and information regarding the team; and is a measure of the project’s credibility. In this sense, ICOs can be compared to other more traditional forms of financing, such as initial public offerings, venture capital (VC) and crowdfunding. However, ICOs have most commonly been used as a source of capital for tech-based start-up companies which allow start-ups to avoid regulatory compliance and intermediaries such as venture capitalists, banks and stock exchanges. ICOs have unique characteristics that differentiate them from other types of financing and make them more suitable for financing innovative projects that offer information goods. The use of blockchain solutions for financing new ventures has several advantages:

+   - the creation and distribution of ‘democratised’ tokens allows newly created ventures to obtain funding at significantly lower discount rates compared to VC financing;<sup>12</sup>
+   - there are increased returns for entrepreneurs’ and investors willing to utilise ICOs;<sup>13</sup> and
+   - there is no necessity for third party involvement, such as investment banks. This reduces the investment thresholds.<sup>14</sup>
