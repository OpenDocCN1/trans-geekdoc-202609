# Copyrights

12.24 Another area that will be a fierce battleground in the Metaverse is copyright. Whilst there are differences in copyright law and its protection around the world there are certainly some common themes and the author's view is that copyright will be severely stress tested given the immersive nature of the Metaverse and potential tension it will likely cause between the platform owners and users especially in the commercial war that will likely wage between participants to generate influence and win business. Copyright protection in the major global jurisdictions extends to protect original works of authorship that are fixed in a tangible form of expression. Copyrights, which are literally the right to copy, are exclusive rights granted to the author(s) or creator(s) of an original work including the right to copy, distribute and adapt that work. Copyright essentially protects the expression of what may be termed the fixation of an idea and not the idea itself. It is thus the transformation of the idea into a creation that is afforded protection. Copyright laws also set out those categories of works that are copyrightable, ie those that are capable of being afforded copyright protection and there can be no doubt that the Metaverse will produce many works that are copyrightable. This is not to say that every work created in the Metaverse will be copyrightable but certainly works that include artistic images or graphics or software or music or other creative form, for example an avatar, will be copyrightable.
