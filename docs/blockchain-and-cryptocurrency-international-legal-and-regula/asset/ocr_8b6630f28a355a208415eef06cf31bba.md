# Russia

# 6.66

> 'On the 22nd of May 2018, the Russian Parliament approved the first reading of new regulation for the cryptocurrency. The law will regulate the ICOs and cryptocurrencies defining both cryptocurrencies and tokens as property, as well as specifying the rules for interacting with crypto and blockchain-related technologies such as smart contracts and mining. Moreover, the new laws affirm that the Bank of Russia may restrict the number of crypto transactions for anyone who is not a qualified investor.' (Zakharova & Ali, 2018).

6.67 Yet despite that proposed regulation the current position in Russia, at the time of writing, is that there is something of a civil war between the Bank of Russia which has decided it wishes to severely restrict cryptocurrency such that it is effectively banned and the Finance Ministry which seeks to pursue regulation. These opposing views resulted in a stalemate. The Bank of Russia looks to China and India for its position whereas the Finance Ministry condemns an approach of prohibition and declares ‘if we ban cryptocurrencies then we need to ban the internet. We don’t use the methods that China uses’ (Anton Siluanov, Finance Minister, 16 February 2022). The Bank of Russia however appears firm in its view that cryptocurrencies should not be taken into the regulatory field. There is, until further notice, an uncertain for future for cryptocurrency.
