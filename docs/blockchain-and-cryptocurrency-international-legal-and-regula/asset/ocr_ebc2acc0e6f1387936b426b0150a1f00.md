**1.52** The Metaverse will also be subject to myriad regulation and laws due to the sum of its many parts and the commercial possibilities it promises to fulfil. Commerce in the Metaverse will fall to be caught by regulatory frameworks and the chapter additionally details the sorts of behaviours and activities that will be caught by those frameworks. The Metaverse will capture an immense range of business and personal involvement in a novel virtual setting and we consequently examine regulation around fintech, privacy and personal data, intellectual property, financial crime, cybersecurity, cybercrime and cyberspace. In terms of horizon scanning, we look at the UK’s Online Harms Bill and its potential impact on service providers who participate in the Metaverse.

**1.53** The book closes on an examination of the jurisdictional conundrum that was brought into sharp focus by the internet and which, with the advent of Web 3 and the Metaverse will become a more complicated and pressing issue. The Metaverse will be an immersive virtual world and the questions around whose jurisdictions and laws can be prescribed and enforced are going to be critical to its success. Nation states have discovered that the rule of law is central to a jurisdiction’s attractiveness and a major factor in commercial success; can the Metaverse adequately assure its Metizens that the rule of law is there to provide protection? Read on and decide.
