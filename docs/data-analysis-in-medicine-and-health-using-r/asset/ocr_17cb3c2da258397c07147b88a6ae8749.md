These data come from patients who were admitted to a tertiary hospital due to acute stroke. They were treated in the ward and the status (dead or alive) were recorded. The variables are:

+   - doa: date of admission
- dod: date of discharge
- status: event at discharge (alive or dead)
- sex: male or female
- dm: diabetes (yes or no)
- gcs: Glasgow Coma Scale (value from 3 to 15)
- sbp: Systolic blood pressure (mmHg)
- dbp: Diastolic blood pressure (mmHg)
- wbc: White cell count
- time2: days in ward
- stroke_type: stroke (Ischemic stroke or Hemorrhagic stroke)
- referral_from :patient was referred from a hospital or from a hospital
