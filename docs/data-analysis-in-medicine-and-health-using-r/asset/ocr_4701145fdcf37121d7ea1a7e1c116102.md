It turns out that the interaction term between res_inf and ghq12 is significant. We may include this interaction term in the final model, but this might complicate our interpretation as we can no longer interpret coefficients. The p-value of the interaction term is close to the commonly used significance level of 0.05, we may choose to ignore this interaction. But keep in mind that the decision is yours, the analyst. Having said, if the purpose of modeling is mainly for prediction, the issue is less severe because we are more concerned with the predicted values than with the clinical interpretation of the result.
