# II.10 Semi-Parametric Models in Survival Analysis

One advantage of time-to-event data (from a cohort study) is the ability to estimate the hazard or risk to develop the event (outcome). However, the challenge in the cohort study is the presence of censoring. Censoring can happen due to

+   - patients leave the study (loss to follow-up) randomly
- patients do not experience the event at the termination of the study
