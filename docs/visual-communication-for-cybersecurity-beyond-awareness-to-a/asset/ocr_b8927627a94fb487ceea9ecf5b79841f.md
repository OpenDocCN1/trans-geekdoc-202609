This illustration is part of a larger infographic on social engineering. The infographic displays data as isotypes: pictograms that represent quantities, not by size of the pictogram but by a greater number of the same-sized pictogram.

Isotypes come from the theories of Otto Neurath (1882–1945), a Viennese philosopher, economist and social scientist. He invented this system to pictorially organise statistics.
