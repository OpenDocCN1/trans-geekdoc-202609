When it comes to online security your personality type may not inform your behavior, but it does reveal how you rationalize your bad password habits.

# ## TYPE A

Bad password behavior in Type A personalities stems from a desire to feel in control even though passwords, they don't believe they are actually at risk because of how well organized system are and proactive efforts.

+   - **CONTROL** 35% Retake because they want to remember all passwords
+   - **DETAIL-ORIENTED** 49% Have a personal "system" for remembering passwords

# ## TYPE B

Type B personalities rationalize their bad behavior by comparing it to actions they view as more risky, making them bigger targets for hackers. This embraces them to maintain their casual, laid back attitude toward password security.

+   - **NONCHALANT** 45% Believe your accounts aren't valuable enough to make them worth a hacker's time
