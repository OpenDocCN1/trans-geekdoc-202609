# Introducing the psychology of passwords

This infographic shows the results of a survey by LastPass and Lab42. The survey about attitudes and behaviours around password security found that although people know what safe passwords should be, they tend to ignore this knowledge in favour of using easy-to-remember passwords, because the fear of forgetting is stronger than the fear of being hacked. Furthermore, the personality traits that normally define us seem to have little bearing on our poor behaviour but do help people rationalize it.
