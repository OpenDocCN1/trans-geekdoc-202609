# 16. Visual activities

Cybersecurity professionals work in teams on many occasions. This section shows some examples of visual work forms that teams can use in meetings to solve problems, to generate new ideas, or to design new processes or systems. Visual collaboration techniques are helpful during such occasions. There are countless techniques for many different situations and for different audiences.
