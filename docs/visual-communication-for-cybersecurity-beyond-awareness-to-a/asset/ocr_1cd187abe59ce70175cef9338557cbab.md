# # Is your C-Suite Prepared for the Cyber Future?

J. Lowe and S. Duffy, Electric Energy Online

Printed with permission from Electric Energy Online

This picture is used to explain that C-suite leaders in energy should look beyond just IT and operations to enhance their organization’s security posture. The temple is a metaphor where five pillars and the three-layered foundation hold up the roof that is ‘trust’. If one of the pillars is unstable, the roof might collapse.
