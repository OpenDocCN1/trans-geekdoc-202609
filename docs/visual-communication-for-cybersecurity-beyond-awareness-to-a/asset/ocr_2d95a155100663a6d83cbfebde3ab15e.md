# A taxonomy of information risks in Healthcare

Nicole van Deursen, created with Flourish
Based on: 25
A taxonomy is a system for naming and organising things into groups that share similar characteristics. Taxonomies are often organised hierarchically, with different levels of groups and sub-groups. This visualization of a taxonomy of information risks in healthcare shows the categories (threat, method, weakness, event and damage) that together describe a risk scenario, and their sub-categories.
