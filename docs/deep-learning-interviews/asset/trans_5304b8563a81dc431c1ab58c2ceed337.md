\frac{d}{dx}s(x) = \frac{d}{dx}((1 + e^{-x})^{-1}) \quad (8.56)

$$
\frac{d}{dx}s(x) = -1((1 + e^{-x})^{(-2)})(0 + e^{-x}(\frac{d}{dx}(-x))) \quad (8.59)
$$
