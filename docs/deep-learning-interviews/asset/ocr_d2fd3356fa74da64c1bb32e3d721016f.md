The Kullback-Leibler divergence , which was discussed thoroughly in chap 4 is a measure of how different two probability distribution are. As noted, the KL divergence of the probability distributions P, Q on a set X is defined as shown in Equation 8.11 .

```
$$D_{KL}(P||Q)=\sum_{x\in X}P(x)log\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\tag{8.11}$$
```

Note however that since KL divergence is a non-symmetric information theoretical measure of distance of P from Q, then it is not strictly a distance metric. During the past years, various KL based distance measures (rather than divergence based) have been introduced in the literature generalizing this measure .
