```
$E[X] := \sum_{k \in K} k P(X=k)$.            (3.12)
```

```
$\text{Var}[X] := E[(X - E[X])^2]$.            (3.13)
```

Note: The variance of $X$ may also be denoted by $\sigma_X^2$, while $\sigma_X$ itself denotes the standard deviation of $X$.
