$P(H|C)=\frac{P(H\cap C)}{P(C)}$ 
$=\frac{P(C|H)P(H)}{P(C|H)P(H)+P(C|M)P(M)}$ (3.43) 
$=\frac{\frac{1}{20}\cdot\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{1}{20}\cdot\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{15}\cdot\frac{1}{2}}$ 
$\approx 0.42.$
