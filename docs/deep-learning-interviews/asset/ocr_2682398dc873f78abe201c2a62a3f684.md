*Denoting $s$ and $f$ as success and failure respectively, we know that the likelihood is:*

$$p(\boldsymbol{x_1}, \dots, x_n|\theta) = \theta^s(1 - \theta)^f$$
(3.83)

*With the following parameters $\alpha = \beta = 1$ the beta distribution acts like Uniform prior:*

$$\theta \sim \text{Beta}(\alpha, \beta), \text{ given } \alpha = \beta = 1$$
(3.84)

*Hence, the prior density is:*

$$p(\theta) = \frac{1}{B(\alpha, \beta)}\theta^{\alpha-1}(1 - \theta)^{\beta-1}$$
(3.85)

*Therefore the posterior is:*

$$\begin{align*}
p(\theta|x_1, \dots, x_n) & \propto p(\boldsymbol{x_1}, \dots, x_n|\theta)\ p(\theta) \\
& \propto \theta^s(1 - \theta)^f\theta^{\alpha-1}(1 - \theta)^{\beta-1} \\
& = \theta^{\alpha+s-1}(1 - \theta)^{\beta+f-1}
\end{align*}
(3.86)
