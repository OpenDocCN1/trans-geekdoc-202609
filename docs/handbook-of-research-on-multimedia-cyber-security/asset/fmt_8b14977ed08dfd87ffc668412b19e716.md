
# Introduction

In global internet usage, increasing multimedia messages, which include video, audio, images, and text documents, on the web raised a lot of consequences related to copyright. For copyright protection, authentication purposes, and forgery detection, digital watermarking is the robust way in social network content protection. In this technique, private information is embedded inside the multimedia content like images and video. The protected content embedded inside multimedia content is called watermark-enabled information. To make the process of watermarking more effective, the content is encrypted before embedding into the image. Basically, the digital watermarking embedding process is implemented in two different domains called spatial and frequency domains. In spatial domain digital watermarking, the watermark information is embedded in the least significant bit of the original image on the basis of the bit plane selected and on the basis of the pixels of the image; embedding and detection are performed.
