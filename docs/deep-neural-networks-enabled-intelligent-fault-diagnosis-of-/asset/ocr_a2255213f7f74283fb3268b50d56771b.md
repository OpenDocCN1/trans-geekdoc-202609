Raw vibration signals from the planetary gearbox are divided into training data and testing data in appropriate proportions. Samples are obtained in an overlapping way from the above training or testing data, respectively. The data splitting way is depicted in Figure 10.2. To utilize time and frequency-domain information synchronously, this chapter adopts SET to convert 1D vibration signal to 2D TF map, which carries more potential fault information and enhances the robustness than raw signal.
