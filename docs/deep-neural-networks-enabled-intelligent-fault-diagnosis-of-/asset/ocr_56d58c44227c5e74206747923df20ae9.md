It seems that the representation of these three hyper-networks remains the same level. However, the subsequent experiment shows that there are indeed significant differences between them.

After the training phase, some Bayesian differentiable search strategy is used to optimize the sampling groups, and 2000 architectures are sampled, respectively corresponding to three hyper-networks. Figure 8.8 shows the kernel density estimation of the validation accuracy for the sampled 2000 architectures. It can be seen that there are significant differences between their distributions although the accuracy levels of the three hyper-networks are comparable in the training phase. Table 8.3 describes the maximum and median of the validation accuracy of the sampled architectures. In the case of 0 NEL, path-dropout can improve the maximum accuracy by 19.3% on the basis of path-dropout, and warmup can further improve the maximum accuracy by 6.08% to significantly improve the representation of hyper-network by enhancing the competitiveness of the parametric operators and alleviating the co-adaptation problem.
