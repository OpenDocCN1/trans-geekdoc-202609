# CONCLUSION

In this chapter, an SAE-based DNN approach has been developed for induction motor fault diagnosis. This approach can learn features directly from raw data, which are discriminative over different working conditions of the induction motors. When the denoising coding is integrated with the SAE, it improves the robustness of the learned features and the stability of the DNN against disturbance. The dropout technique integrated into the network classifier design reduces the overfitting in the training and improves the performance of the DNN for induction motor fault classification. Experimental study and a detailed analysis have verified the effectiveness of the SAE-based DNN for induction fault diagnosis.
