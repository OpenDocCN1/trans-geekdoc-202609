# EXPERIMENTAL STUDIES

In this section, we test two datasets to verify the performance of different UDTL methods for comparative studies.

+   Case Western Reserve University (CWRU) dataset: The CWRU dataset provided by Case Western University Bearing Data Center (Case School of Engineering 2021) is one of the most famous open-source datasets in IFD and has been used by tremendous published papers. Following other papers, this also uses the drive bearing fault data whose sampling frequency is to 12 kHz and ten bearing conditions are listed in Table 7.2. In Table 7.2, one normal bearing (NA) and three fault types including inner fault (IF), ball fault (BF) and outer fault (OF) are classified into ten categories (one health state and nine fault states) according to different fault sizes.

+   Besides, as CWRU consists of four motor loads corresponding to operating speeds. For the transfer learning, this section considers these working conditions as tasks including 0, 1, 2, and 3. For example, 0→1 means that the source domain with a motor load of 0 HP transfers to the target domain with a motor load of 1 HP. In total, there are transfer learning tasks.

+   Paderborn University (PU) dataset: The PU dataset acquired from Paderborn University is a bearing dataset (Lessmeier et al. 2016) which consists of artificially induced and real measures. The sampling frequency is to 64 kHz. By changing the rotating speed of the drive system, the radial force onto the bearing and the load torque on the drive train, the PU dataset consists of four operating conditions as shown in Table 7.4. Thirteen bearings with real caused by accelerated lifetime times (Lessmeier et al. 2016) are used to study transfer learning tasks among different working conditions (twenty experiments were performed on each bearing code, and each experiment sustained four seconds). The categorization information is presented in Table 7.5 (the meaning of contents is explained in (Lessmeier et al. 2016)). In total, there are 12 transfer learning settings.
