Also, the Kappa coefficient is regarded as a statistic of the classification conformance testing (Huang, Nie, and Luo 2019). To further analyze the recognition conformance of the proposed method on each state, the Kappa coefficient (Eugenio and Glass 2004), here $k$, is also used as an evaluation metric in this chapter. It is defined as:

```
$k = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{M} X_{test,correct}^{(i)} \sum_{i=1}^{M} X_{test,actual}^{(i)} 	imes X_{test,predicted}^{(i)}}{\sum_{i=1}^{M} (X_{test,actual}^{(i)})^2} \quad (10.18)$
```

where $X_{test,actual}^{(i)}$ is the actual number of the $i$-th category test sample and $X_{test,predicted}^{(i)}$ is the predicted number of parameters of the model are mainly located on the C-layer and FC-layer operations, and the number of parameters (Num) (Chen et al. 2021) is related to the diagnostic time. Thus, the model's trainable Num is used to measure its complexity.
