Data Description In this chapter, three diagnostic subtasks of planetary gearbox, including the fault diagnosis of bearing, gear, and bearing-gear mixture, are carried out. The first two tasks can be regarded as the 5-pattern guessing game, and the last one can be deemed a 9-pattern guessing game. Each subtask is carried out in three speed-load operating conditions, i.e., 20_0, 30_2, 40_0. Thus, there are nine diagnostic tasks in single-work conditions. In each operating condition, vibration signals of each state are put into 2000 samples in the way depicted in Figure 10.2, where the first 1000 samples are used to train and the last 1000 samples are used to test, and each sample contains 1280 data points. Thus, the first two tasks have 10,000 samples, and the last task has 18,000 samples.
