Constructing the Sampling Distributions Group
In the proposed BDAS, each point $\alpha(i=1,2\cdots8; j=1,2\cdots14)$ of the sampling matrix $A$ is extended as a distribution, then the sampling distributions group $A$ is obtained. For a validation dataset $D_{out}=(x,y)$ and a trained hyper-network $H(w^*)=\argmin_w L_{train}(w)$, the variational posterior $q(\Lambda|\theta)$ is used to approximate the true Bayesian posterior $P(\Lambda|D_{out},H(w^*))$.
