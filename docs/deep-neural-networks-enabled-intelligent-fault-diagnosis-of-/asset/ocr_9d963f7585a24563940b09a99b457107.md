Hyper-parameters Effect on Diagnosis Result

Node Change of FC1 Layer An appropriate model should have little model complexity as possible while ensuring a certain recognition accuracy. In the designed model described in Figure 10.3, its trainable Num is mainly located in the FC1 layer. The effect of the node number of the FC1 layer on the recognition rate is analyzed. The number of FC1-layer nodes is 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024, respectively. Besides the above changes, parameters’ setting is the same as described in Section 10.4. To reliably analyze the performance of different FC1 layers, all experiments are independently carried 5 times in each step. Results of different FC1 layers on the previously mentioned tasks are shown in Figures 10.9 and 10.10. Besides, their trainable Num is also compared in Figure 10.11. Figure 10.9(a), OA on each diagnostic test reaches 99.5%. It shows that the main body of the designed model is reasonable. When the number of FC1-layer is 128, this model has slightly better overall diagnostic performance and stability, especially in B40_0, G30_2, and C30_2 situations. At this point, the OA of the model in the situations is 99.94%, 99.92%, 99.89%, respectively. The average OA of the model on nine datasets reaches 99.9%. From Figure 10.9, it can also be found that the effect of different FC1 layers on B20_0, B30_0, and G40_0 datasets is significant.
