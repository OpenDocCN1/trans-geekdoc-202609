# CONCLUSION

To improve the safety and reliability of rotating machinery transmission systems, this chapter transforms planetary gearbox fault diagnosis as a “guessing game” and presents a new fault recognition method integrating SET and DRL. Moreover, this method is validated under different load conditions on a DDS test platform. Results prove that this approach has better diagnostic accuracy and stability on multiple diagnostic tasks. Even in multi-work conditions, the proposed method realizes OA over 99.5%. Importantly, it provides a reference for highly accurate and intelligent fault diagnosis for planetary gearbox from a view of DRL.
