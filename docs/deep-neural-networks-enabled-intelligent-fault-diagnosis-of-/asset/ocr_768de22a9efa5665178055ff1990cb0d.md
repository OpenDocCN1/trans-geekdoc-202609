Uncertainty quantification plays a critical role in assessing the safety of DL models construction, optimization, and decision-making processes. Bayesian networks (Gal and Gahramani 2016) and ensemble learning techniques (Lakshminarayanan, Pritzel, and Blundell 2017) are widely used uncertainty quantification methods, and their effectiveness has been verified by different kinds of applications, such as self-driving car, etc. Thus, uncertainty as an auxiliary term can be used to further correct some inappropriate properties or results during the transfer learning. For example, the prediction uncertainty is explicitly estimated during training to rectify the pseudo labeling for UDTL of semantic segmentation (Zheng and Yang 2021).

# CONCLUSION

This chapter constructs a taxonomy and provides a brief review of UDTL-based IFD according to different tasks of UDTL. Two publicly available datasets are gathered to perform a comparative analysis of different UDTL-based IFD methods from several perspectives. Based on the systematically comparative study, results of different methods indicate that the assumption of joint distributions and adversarial training are two helpful techniques for promoting the accuracy. Meanwhile, different input types often define what might also be important to improve the accuracy. The stability and robustness of UDTL-based IFD need to be taken seriously. To sum up, it may be useful for scholars to think ahead of these results before developing new models.
