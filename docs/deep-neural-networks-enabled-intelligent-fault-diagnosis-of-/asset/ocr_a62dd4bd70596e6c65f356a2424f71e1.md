# Intra-Temporal Relation Mining

Vibration signals are essentially a type of time series. Their temporal relations can also reveal different fault modes. Therefore, exploring the intra-temporal relation of unlabeled data is effective to extract more discriminative representations. By motivated sentence prediction (NSP), which is a pre-trained self-supervised task in BERT (Devlin et al. 2018; Fan et al. 2021), a temporal relation prediction is adopted to relate the relationship of different segments in a sample as supervision. Given given samples $[x_i | x_i \in R^{1 \times L}]$, we split each sample into two parts $x_i^{past} \in R^{T \times L}$ and $x_i^{future} \in R^{(L-T) \times L}$, where the former T points refers to its past condition and the latter L points denote its future condition, as shown in Figure 9.3(b). Based on prior knowledge that the future of a signal owns similar sequential characteristics to its past, we randomly select a past segment as the anchor $x_i^{anc}$.
