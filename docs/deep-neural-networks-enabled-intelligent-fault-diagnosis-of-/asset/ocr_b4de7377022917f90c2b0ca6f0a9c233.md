# CONCLUSION

In this work, we have proposed a one-shot NAS method to automatically design domain matching diagnostic model. As a rethinking exercise, our method solves two drawbacks of DARTS, the unfairness of hyper-network and the overconflicting result. The warmup and path-dropout are used to obtain an intuitively fair hyper-network, and variational Bayesian inference is introduced into the differentiable search strategy to estimate the uncertainty of model matching and architectures. Our approach achieves state-of-the-art performance on the aeroengine bevel gear fault diagnosis with different NEL cases.
