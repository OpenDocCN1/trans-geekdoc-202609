+   3. Encoder-decoder-accuracy training: To train the components, a custom loss consisting of decoder_loss, accuracy_loss, legal_loss, and distance_loss is used:

custom_loss = wDist*(decoder_loss) + wA*(accr_loss) + wL*legal_loss
+ WDist*distance_loss

where legal_loss is the MSE of the network reconstruction, accuracy_loss is the MSE of the accuracy prediction, legal_loss is binary cross-entropy of legal network prediction, and distance_loss is the distance error of the Siamese network.

Weights were determined experimentally, and the learned components were evaluated for consistency of the embedding space, a reconstruction architecture, and precision of accuracy and legal network predictions. After training, the encoder-decoder network achieved a reconstruction accuracy over legal networks of 99%.

To validate the spatial consistency of the decoder, the locations of legal and illegal network descriptions were evaluated and compared to the legal predictions. Fig. 12 shows the results where blue points indicate valid networks, while red dots indicate embedding points that do not correspond to legal network configurations. The shaded region indicates the area for which the system predicts illegal legal networks.

This shows that the system is able to organize the embedding space coherently and accurately demarcate the area of legal network embeddings with a legal prediction accuracy of 98% on validation data.

To evaluate the accuracy prediction, Fig. 13 shows the learned predictions for a specific training problem (FV 5).

The accuracy prediction here achieved a mean square error of 4.5 × 10⁻⁵, on validation data across all training programs, indicating its ability to learn to predict accuracy across a range of problems. Note that the system is only trained on a small region of the embedding space for illegal networks, and thus accuracy predictions in illegal areas are hard to interpret.
