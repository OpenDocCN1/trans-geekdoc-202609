## 3 Intelligencism

We find ourselves confronted with a significant philosophical dilemma in a time when technological advancement is rising and machine intelligence teeters on the verge of surpassing human capability. From this chasm, the idea of “Intelligencism” emerges, reflecting the philosophical underpinings of notable philosophers like Socrates, Kant, Hegel, and Heidegger. It illustrates how our society is becoming more dependent on intelligent machines while struggling with a loss of understanding of these systems. The increasing complexity of intelligent robots creates a paradox that our development carries run the risk of losing human autonomy as we go further into this unexplored region. This paradox necessitates a thorough revaluation of our notions of intelligence, their social functions, and the moral implications of handing decision-making to machines. We suggest using ANT to investigate the complexities of Intelligencism in order to address this complicated problem [8, 25]. According to this viewpoint, both humans and non-human things, such as smart robots, are participants in a complex network that interact and share one another in a changing ecosystem. Human and AI coexist in a complicated and dynamic interdependence where each influences and interacts with the other. Although this dynamic has enormous potential for development and advancement, we must exercise caution giving computers too much control can have unfavorable effects that could impair other aspects of intelligence. This is the fine line we walk as we go to investigate and push the boundaries of knowledge.

### 3.1 Anthroposia

Anthroposia, which is described as a state of being human, considers the ongoing conflict over control between human and artificial intelligence. It raises questions about authority, control, and accountability in a time where technology is advancing quickly, such as with LLM AI [3]. The idea of anthroposia can be elaborated upon and reinterpreted using the ANT principles to take into account the complex web of interactions between human and artificial intelligence. According to ANT [8],

"actors" or "actants," which can be either human or non-human, play important roles within networks and influence outcomes through their interactions. These actors in anthropos include people, AI systems, as well as the moral standards, societal expectations, and technological frameworks that direct their interactions.

By building anthropos, one can investigate how the power disparity between human and AI presents in a variety of areas, including decision-making, privacy, employment, and government [10]. In a world where intelligent robots are increasingly pervasive in daily life, it becomes crucial to take the distribution of power and control into. Tensions are emerging in the current surge of AI-based intelligence about the accountability for the decisions made by machines, the implications of such acts, and the possible effects on both persons and society. Additionally, arguments about the necessity of human agency and the protection of human autonomy arise in the context of human-machine connections. As technology develops, it is essential to maintain a balance where humans retain control over important decision-making processes and make sure that machines only supply rather than completely replace humans.

The philosophical investigation of anthropos invites us to evaluate technology's effects on our reality, our interactions with other people, and our shared obligation to promote an inclusive and compassionate society [26]. The ultimate objective is to change machine intelligence into one that can be seen as "being human," or anthropocentric. By taking into account anthropos, we can navigate the complex dynamics of human-machine interaction and work towards a harmonious integration of technology and human intelligence.

By expanding on anthropos from a Heideggerian perspective, it is also feasible to comprehend the difficulties and opportunities that result from the preservation of local worlds and the prevailing cultural paradigm of technology [26]. It promotes consideration on the ways that technology can either strengthen or weaken our relationship with commonplace objects, the development of regional comforts, and our capacity to trace ourselves. Drawing on ANT's concept of "immutable mobiles," [8] which refers to network components with strong irreversibility features, might also represent the ethical standards and principles that control the behavior of AI systems despite their developing capabilities and social circumstances. It implies that can eventually reach a breaking point where the capacity for loyalty is irreplaceable.

Anthropos provides a framework for comprehending and navigating the dynamic world of interaction between humans and machines and the intelligence that both provide. In pitting humans against robots, this investigation aims to shed light on the nuances of their connection and pinpoint possible avenues for both parties to advance. As technology advances, it is crucial that we work towards harmony, balance, and the preservation of our fundamental human nature—the essence of anthropos.

### 3.2 Telopolarity

Telopolarity includes the possible effects of utilizing both human and artificial intelligence. It addresses both the positive and negative effects of these intelligence interactions, as well as the difficulties in determining the consequences for personal and social life that come along with the greater use of AI in the equation.

Heidegger's perspective highlights how technology drives us to view things through the lens of predictability and attainability by putting human-technology interaction in the spotlight. It fosters an endless thirst for advancement by making the world flexible to our planning, arrangements, and manipulations [27]. However, when the human mind struggles with the pervasiveness of technology, such a worldview may obscure the necessity for introspection and other strategies. ANT breeds this discussion to take into account how non-human actors, may influence the potential results of technological advancement. All participants in a system are interrelated, according to ANT, which presents telolarity as a complex interaction between human and non-human components. However, we may neglect the behavior or autonomy of sophisticated AI systems because its main attention is on network connections.

The entire impact of technological breakthroughs, particularly those involving AI-based devices, is frequently not until after they have gained popular use. At that point, technology has typically been thoroughly ingrained in our society norms, making its removal or correction extremely difficult. This emphasizes the necessity of thoughtful, anticipatory reflection on the possible effects of new technologies before they are incorporated into society. Heidegger suggests the concept of Besinnung, which translates as “sensibility,” to counteract the pervasive impact of technology [28]. We are better prepared to handle the intricacies of technological breakthroughs, thanks to our increased understanding of their ramifications and means. We may call the meditative that technology encourages us by encouraging sensitivity, which keeps us up to the world around us.

Although Heidegger's method and ANT have virtues of their own, they are insufficient for foretelling the effects of artificial intelligence, particularly in circumstances when AI becomes self-sustaining and acts independently of human considerations. Heidegger's theory, which mainly concentrates on how people interact with technology, not fully account for the autonomous behavior of artificial intelligence. Similar to ANT, sophisticated AI systems' emergent behavior or potential autonomy may be overlooked by ANT's focused on networked interactions. Currently, self-creating capabilities are not present in AI systems. The current status of AI is a product of human design, reliance on human oversight, adherence to human legal systems, and human-based ethical considerations. The code, data, and algorithms that humans used to create the AI systems ultimately dictate how they behave, even in a hypothetical situation in AI develops autonomy.
