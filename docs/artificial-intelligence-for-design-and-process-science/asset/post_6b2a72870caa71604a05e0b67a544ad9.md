## 6.1 Empowering Business Value: Implementing PRIMETHOD and P3Tech in Research Institutions and Corporate Initiatives

A business value process was defined employing PRIMETHOD, P3Tech Initial, Management, and Process in the research facilities of the institutions described in the previous section of this chapter. The TELESP project initiative enabled the development of its BVLL through the application of its main characteristics shown in Fig. 15. With regard to the banking sector, fifteen employees from BB management and operational team underwent training to establish a BVLL on credit recovery. Additionally, the Institute of Exact and Technological Sciences of PUC-PR utilized the PRIMETHOD and P3Tech methodology and architecture to establish the inaugural Web-based digital ecosystem called LabP Network. This platform was designed for developing and simulation processes, as well as facilitating distance education for the creation of BVLLs. This initiative gave rise to the university’s inaugural business incubator, which concentrated on software development and forged partnerships with private sector entities like Siemens. In collaboration with AEB and the Brazilian Management Center of the Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation (CGEE), a BVLL was established, involving key institutions within the Brazilian Aerospace Sector's science, technology, and innovation (S&T&I) ecosystem.

The implementation utilized PRIMETHOD and P3Tech methodologies to model and simulate a multifaceted socioeconomic system, encompassing technical-productive modalities, inter-organizational dynamics, and technological cooperation, influenced by cultural and competitive factors. Recognized as a strategic priority by the Brazilian government, this initiative aimed to develop an inter-organizational cooperation network to promote Brazil's development in nanotechnology innovation applied to payloads and satellites. At CEF, the BVLL for Business Development was created to facilitate the exchange of expertise in strategic business management, engaging C-level executives. As a result of this initiative, knowledge and practices were gained by the Brazilian Ministry of Planning. Moreover, UNASP utilized P3Tech Machine and while offering an MBA program specializing in BVLL. Furthermore, in collaboration with Embrapa Instrumentation, a BVLL was created to facilitate the dissemination of outcomes related to the coordination, management, and operation of agents within a research and innovation network focused on aerial pesticide application. Assisted by the Ministry of Human Rights, a BVLL focused on urban development for longevity was initiated. It was utilized for training public administrators across 15 Brazilian cities, encompassing all states in the country. The initiative yielded 15 process models emphasizing value, aimed at enhancing regional longevity. The Brazilian Federal State is providing courses in PRIMETHOD and P3Tech Process for its staff, have outlined and developed over two hundred process business values.

Also, the following Human Resources were trained by each respective organization mentioned above:

- TELESP: the initiative engaged the Operational Planning Department, comprising 380 staff members across five divisions: engineers, IT technicians, systems analysts, programmers, and administrators. These teams collaborated closely with the C-suite, influencing corporate decisions at the highest level.
- BB: fifteen employees from management and operational team to establish a new credit recovery department.
- PUC-PR: over 100 undergraduate and graduate students from diverse academic backgrounds participated, with R&D managers from research institutes. Additionally, the LabP Network reached approximately 1500 individuals.
- CEF: more C-level staff members for business development.
- UNASP: 66 individuals, with 2 holding a PhD and 58 with undergraduate degrees in various areas of concentration.
- UAB: six individuals enrolled in the Multidisciplinary Program.
- TAMUC: three individuals enrolled in the Master Program in Computer Science.
- Brazilian Federal Senate: 61 individuals, with 2 holding an MBA and 59 with undergraduate degrees in various areas of concentration.
- AEB: three individuals with a master's degree. Also, top executives from leading institutions in the aerospace sector as Department of Aerospace Science and Technology (DCTA), National Institute for Space Research (INPE), Institute of Applied Economic Research (IPEA), Support Program for Scientific and Technological Development (PADCT), and University of Brasilia (UnB).
- Embrapa Instrumentation: two researchers, with a doctoral degree and the other with a master's degree.
- MDH: 47 public managers from 24 cities.
- Information Environment: 25 individuals, including 8 with a PhD, 7 with a master's degree, and 10 with undergraduate degrees in various areas of concentration.
