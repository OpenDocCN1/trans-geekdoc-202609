The core elements of this architecture involve:

+   1. Defining specifications, interfaces, transformations, measurements, and interoperability of business values
2. Modeling the relationships between initial, intermediary, and final values in a business network
3. Ensuring synchronization to trigger the necessary transitions through protocols accessing human, technological, environmental, and data resources, according to the axioms or references required by the real world
4. Identifying potential repercussions of altering business values
5. Spotting inefficiencies in differential processes to boost value enhancement
6. Aligning business values and legacy systems (human, technology, data) with the required business values

The challenges of synchronicity and connecting interfaces in interoperating legacy systems across organizations necessitate P3Tech to be a versatile multipatform development environment equipped with editing tools, interface construction tools, and runtime classes that amalgam into a comprehensive ecosystem. This technology should cater to a broad spectrum of business solutions, from stand-alone departmental to complex distributed systems aimed at addressing enterprise challenges over long distances [54]. Additionally, it should serve as a robust tool for resolving interoperability issues and critical missions across various platforms and operating systems.
