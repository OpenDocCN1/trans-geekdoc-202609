# The Importance of Transdisciplinary Thinking

Transdisciplinary approaches involve a rich interplay of technology and diverse fields of expertise, such as computer science, biology, economics, and social sciences. Collaborative environments where specialists from different disciplines work together can lead to innovative solutions that do not emerge within the silos of individual disciplines. AI can process and analyze the vast amounts of data that such collaborations generate, identifying patterns and insights that are not obvious. This broad integrated perspective helps in developing more robust, adaptable, and effective solutions.

# Human Responsibility

While much attention is focused on developing ethical AI, human responsibility has far more significant implications. The blunt fact is that we have become an irresponsible society on a global level. Perverse societal incentives drive deeply damaging acts, such as the burning of the Amazonian rainforest or dumping of toxic waste in the poorest parts of the ocean by multinational companies. In many countries, consumers carefully sort recyclables, only to have these plastics burned or dumped into the ocean, which has contributed to the formation of the Great Pacific Garbage Patch, a vast area of floating debris twice as large as the state of Texas.

The previous examples highlight that today's tough problems do not fall under the jurisdiction of any single country or region, which makes them the responsibility of all humanity. As these problems are complex and subject to dramatic changes over time, a solution space that also changes, in response to conditions, is ideal. For instance, on a small scale the Kapiʻolani Medical Center for Women & Children in Honolulu, Hawaii, is a responsive solution space. Care teams are transdisciplinary, composed of members from various specialties and areas of expertise, and combining conventional medical treatments with alternative therapies. The medical center is supported by cutting-edge technology and equipment, and both the teams and technology can be upgraded over time in response to need.
