```
$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2me}\left(\frac{d^2R}{\dr^2\|+\frac{2}{r}\frac{dR}{\dr}\right)+\left(\frac{\hbar^2}{2\mu r^2}l(l+1)-\frac{Ze^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0}}\right)R=ER \quad (22)$
```

where $\mu=\frac{m_{electron}m_{nucleus}}{m_{electron}+m_{nucleus}}$, which is approximately equal to $m_{electron}$. Therefore, when $l=0$, for s orbitals, solving. (22) acquires:

```
$E=-\frac{Z^2\mu e^4}{32\epsilon_0^2\hbar^2}\frac{1}{n^2}=-13.6\frac{Z^2}{n^2} \quad (23)$
```

where $Z$ is the effective nuclear charge, which is the same as Eq. (19) when $Z=1$, indicating that both theories are in agreement at H atom.
