# BVLL Experimentation: Results from Collaborative Projects in the USA and Brazil Since 1972

Several experiments utilizing BVLL with their PRIMETHOD and P3Tech architectures have been conducted in the USA and Brazil since 1972 through collaborative agreements with various institutions worldwide.

The experiences outlined in the timeline span over several decades showcase a remarkable journey of academic and professional growth, innovation, and leadership worldwide. Noteworthy achievements during this period include contributions to the development of the first Brazilian computer alongside the Ministry of the Navy and pioneering work in graph clustering algorithms for cancer analysis and taxonomy of plants.

Subsequent years delve into transdisciplinary research, particularly in Soil Value for Experimental Agriculture Development at EMBRAPA, the most representative agriculture research center in Brazil. This was followed by groundbreaking work in Software Engineering, including involvement in the Star Wars Program and seminal research on Software Evolution Management, and leadership in computer science and management science.

The impact of these experiences extended into the realm of national and international projects, notably as agriculture and systems integration. Projects as the Paulista Software Laboratory, the Telesec Project, and the Federal State’s Living Factory underscored a commitment to cutting-edge technology implementation and management, earning international recognition and certification.
