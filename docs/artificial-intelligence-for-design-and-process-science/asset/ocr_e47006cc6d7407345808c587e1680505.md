# Foreword

It is indeed a great pleasure to write a foreword for this book dedicated to Professor Murat Tanik and Professor Bernd Kraemer, my two great friends. This book, the result of a one-day workshop in Hawaii in January 2024, touches upon a variety of topics yet consistently highlights three crucial concepts: artificial intelligence (AI), transdisciplinary thinking, and human responsibility. The convergence of these concepts creates a prime solution space for solving the truly tough economic, environmental, and social planetary problems.

The leveraged power of transdisciplinary thinking, when supported and augmented by AI, increases the likelihood that humanity will develop solutions to the numerous existential crises currently looming. A deep commitment to human responsibility can guide the development of those solutions along moral and ethical pathways.

# The Proper Use of AI

With massive processing power, continuous learning, and access to large datasets, AI can elevate rather than replace humans in problem-solving and decision-making. In arenas where human judgment and ethical considerations are paramount, with healthcare and public policy, AI enhancement can prevent problems such as cognitive bias and decision fatigue. AI should remain in a supportive role bounded by ethical guardrails, which involves creating and using AI systems that are fair, transparent, and accountable. This includes considerations like avoiding bias in AI algorithms, ensuring AI decisions are explainable, and respecting user privacy.
