# 3.2 AI-Human co-creativity

Computational co-creative systems represent a burgeoning field where human users collaborate with AI agents to produce creative works [36]. These systems, particularly in design, allow users to move beyond their initial ideas and overcome design fixing by working with an AI agent on open-ended creative tasks throughout the design process. While some research in computational creativity focuses on generative, co-creative systems emphasize how systems implementing creativity can collaborate with humans on tasks. For instance, sketching is a common technique used in creativity tasks where designers generate and refine ideas. In the realm of design creativity, AI has proven valuable in assisting designers with ideation and overcoming issues.

Co-creative systems hold significant promise for enhancing human creativity across various domains by fostering and encouraging creatives in a human-AI co-creative sketching system, reporting the outcomes of a user study that analyzed user responses to the Creative Sketching Partner’s (CSP) sketches.

The authors developed an AI model to facilitate the mapping process between visual and conceptual spaces, aiding users in making connections between these spaces to generate fresh ideas for their design tasks.
