# Discussion

From Socrates and other ancient thinkers through modern philosophers like Kant and Hegel explores the philosophical and historical roots of intellectualism. Heidegger's concept of technology as framing is explored in this chapter, along with the effects of technology on human cognition [32]. According to Intelligenticism, it is crucial to think about the future interactions between intelligent machines and machine intelligence develops and its philosophical consequences when the two either merge or need to coexist in a harmonious mode. The study highlights the importance of standards to differentiate the outputs of AI and the ethical implications of AI use for IS professionals. 

With the rise of LLMs and impressive developments in AI, the Intelligenticism ideology proposes a new way of understanding intellectualism. The study's focus on the overlap between AI and IS shows that these ideas are ready for widespread adoption. It stresses the importance of keeping things in perspective and anticipating the advantages of technology without letting them cloud our wider picture. Our goal is to get IS scholars to take a fresh philosophical stance as a means of coping with AI's complicated new world. This will help us move toward a day when we can design our future with an eye toward the moral and social consequences of our technological choices. It prompts serious reflection on the nature of intelligence, place in human society, and the moral consequences of giving robots autonomy.

The information systems (IS) field is in a prime position to absorb and incorporate Intelligenticism's ideas and principles as a philosophical framework to help individuals adapt to the ever-evolving world of machine intelligence as AI's influence grows through the ICT industry [1]. Policymakers and AI practitioners, including entrepreneurs, need a firm grasp of four key concepts: the degree to which machines exhibit human-like intelligence (anthroposis), the identification of potential conflicts in outcomes (teleolarity), and an appreciation of the significance of philosophical interpretation of time (temporal fusion), to stop machine intelligence or allow it to continue unchecked. We must stress the importance of openness and responsibility in AI systems because of the danger they pose to our moral and ethical standards towards one another. The outcomes of AI systems also need to be rigorously evaluated.

What makes the aforementioned study so important is the fresh philosophical perspective it offers over Intelligenticism. For those analyzing the development and appearance of intelligence in modern artificial agents, the conceptual framework provided by this is invaluable. The report encourages further research into the effects of AI in order to better understand such intricate systems. The study suggests that the inclusion of the Intelligenticism construct may encourage new, more holistic views of intelligence that take into account the role of AI. Possible unintended consequences of this include the generation of innovative hypotheses and the formulation of more complex research topics. Overall, how this chapter shows 172, F. J. Costello and K. C. Lee, how important it is to keep an open mind when thinking about AI in the context of the broader technological landscape, appreciating its benefits while remaining vigilant against the possibility that it could cloud our perception of the world.

The findings of this study demand that our ideas about intelligence is, how it contributes to society, and the moral consequences of granting such power to computers be completely rethought. We hope this study will stimulate society becoming increasingly reliant on intelligent technologies, while our understanding of these systems continues to erode. However, we recognize that the scale of our research had to be limited. To go extensively into this topic and acquire a firm grasp of it, a nuanced approach is required, perhaps adopting a scientific methodology such as critical realism. Understanding Intelligentism, its possible generative mechanisms, and its ties to both IS and the academic community will be facilitated through in-depth studies of AI.
