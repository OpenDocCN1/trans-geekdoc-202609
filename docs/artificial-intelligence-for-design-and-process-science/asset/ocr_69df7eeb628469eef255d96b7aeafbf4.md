# The Platform-Specific Manufacturing Model (PSM) transforms production planning decisions for PIM models into control commands for production processes on the factory floor to fulfill the control and monitoring needs of different types of machines and equipment. This type of conversion structures the actual interface between the digital and the physical world, resulting in improved production performance, increased uptime, improved resource availability, asset management, and operational speed. Based on factory automation and control systems, PSM includes device control, intelligent devices, work centers, robots, machines, programmable logic controllers, and monitoring sensors as carbon emission sensors, temperature, and vibration sensors.

To ensure efficiency, high quality, and high throughput, devices typically use different communication protocols that run independently. For these components to work properly, coordination, synchronization, and access to resources must be effectively managed. To take advantage of the Factory of Things, manufacturers need to manage the communication barriers between these field devices and connect them to a cloud-based Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) implementation, which offers significant advantages for product design parameters. This requires rapid application development, global access to data, and integrated predictive analytics capabilities.

**Feedback loop** Product manufacturability depend on functional and nonfunctional design parameters. This requires a feedback loop in MDM. As shown in Fig. 1, there is a feedback loop in product design and manufacturing process design, manufacturing, and ultimately product delivery and maintenance. Data from the product conception/ideation and design process (and the subsequent use of the DTs and the corresponding physical product manufacturing) is captured and continuously updated, triggering a continuous cycle between the digital and physical domains.

MDM feedback loops provide not only product improvements but also the ability to recognize, improve, and modify production lines, processes, and machines. Real-time operational data can also be collected and analyzed to optimize production processes and predict demand, downtime, and future value. During implementation and deployment on the PSM platform, feedback drives product product optimization through quality assurance testing, ensuring that product defects are identified and fixed as they are discovered.
