In another study [39], a Turing test was conducted to assess DALL-E’s ability to achieve combinatorial creativity in comparison to human designers. To compare the performance of DALL-E with human notice designers, two datasets were prepared for evaluation: one for machines and one for humans. Both DALL-E and human designers were given the same textual prompts containing design ideas as input, and they generated images corresponding to these prompts as output. After selections were made, an equal amount of data was saved for both human and datasets. Subsequently, three tests were conducted: a Turing test, a computational test, and an expert test. The results indicated that DALL-E could achieve combinatorial creativity at a level comparable to that of notice designers, shedding light on the distinctions between computer and human creativity.

In a review [40], various creative machines applied to the AUT were examined. The reviewed studies suggest that systems based on large language models (LLMs) perform at or slightly above human levels in terms of originality and novelty. Similarities were observed between human and LLM model data in the AUT, such as output order effects and a negative correlation between originality and utility. However, it was concluded that despite generating novel and useful responses, systems like GPT-3 and similar ones do not exhibit creativity because they agency and operate solely on algorithms. The performance of GPT-based chatbots in the AUT seeks to improve with the increasing power of the chatbots from GPT-3 to GPT-4.

The study [24] investigated the correlation between creativity and the origin of creativity by analyzing paintings produced by AI and humans across various artistic genres. Through a mathematical approach employing entropy analysis, utilizing Shannon entropy and a complexity measure based on multi-scale entropy, the findings reveal differences between AI and human art not only in the schematic representation but also in color changes. The AI struggles more with representing painting styles lacking well-defined objects and with handling color changes involving pixels similarity intensity. The study drew two main conclusions:

+   (1) Paintings generated by AI exhibit significantly higher standard deviation values for features compared to human works. (2) Both features are smaller for AI-generated drawings to human works.

In a separate study [38], both human participants and GPT-4 were tasked responding to the AUT, Consequences Task, and Divergent Associations Task. Human and artificial participants were recruited using GPT-4. Responses were evaluated for fluency, originality, and elaboration. The study revealed that AI consistently displayed greater creativity across all measures of divergent fluency, AI exhibited higher levels of originality and elaboration. Overall, GPT-4 demonstrated higher levels of originality and elaboration than humans across all divergent fluency.
