Its integration with value-based processes yielded remarkable transformations. In June 1998, a team trained in the PRIMEMETHOD methodology spearheaded the implementation of the first integrated P3Tech environment at Teleunicações de São Paulo S.A. (TELESP), certified by the Society for Design and Process Science (SDPS), as shown in Fig. 13.

This initiative gave birth to the Telesp Software Factory and introduced the living laboratory concept, revolutionizing the Brazilian largest telecommunications community since its privatization. The factory became a pioneering force, capable of specifying, simulating, and generating software products in real time, setting the stage for the Fábrica de Software Paulista endorsed by São Paulo State Governor, Mário Covas.

This achievement marked the inception of a community comprising scientists, entrepreneurs, and value-based process experts through the BVLL network, as illustrated in Fig. 14.

Their vision is bold: to leverage science and technology for integrated development, reducing risks, ensuring business evolution, enhancing workers' quality of life, and fostering a more equitable, inclusive, and cooperative society, both in Brazil and globally.

In more recent years, the focus shifted toward the coordination and operation of projects for the implementation, development, and training of living laboratories in Brazil and abroad.
