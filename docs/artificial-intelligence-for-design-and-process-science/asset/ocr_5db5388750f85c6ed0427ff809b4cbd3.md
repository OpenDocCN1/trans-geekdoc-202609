# Acknowledgment

In honor of Professors Bernd Krämer and Murat M. Tanik—Life Fellows of SDPS—for their lifetime contribution and achievement in the field of design and process science.

This book appears on the occasion of Prof. Bernd Krämer and Prof. Murat M. Tanik being named Life Fellows of the Society for Design and Process Sciences (SDPS) in January 2024, at a special event held at the AI in Design and Process Science (AIDPS) during the 57th Hawaii International Conference in System Sciences (HICSS). To appreciate their work and honor them for their contribution and achievement in the field of design and process science, a wide range of scientists, engineers, educators, scholars, and practitioners, both from SDPS and from the international research community, dedicated chapters to this commemorative edition.
