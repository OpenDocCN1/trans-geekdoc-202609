# 2 Related Work

Chatbots are powered by AI are a big help to education [6]. These interactive agents provide prompt answers and are great resources for both educators and students. AI chatbots are beneficial to education because they can help with a variety of tasks, provide prompt feedback, and tailor personalized instructions. Research and development, teaching, administration, assessment, and advisory are among the areas where chatbot technology is being used in a rapid fashion in education [7]. It is noteworthy that the majority of chatbot applications are in teaching and learning domain (66%), the remainder going to development (19%), administration (5%), evaluation (6%), and advisory (4) [6]. These highlight how chatbots can effectively change how education is delivered and made accessible.
