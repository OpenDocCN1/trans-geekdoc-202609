# Two Creativity Tests: AUT and TTCT

The Alternate Uses Task (AUT) and the Torrance Test of Creative Thinking (TTCT) are represented in the following sections. These creativity tests have been widely employed in the literature as they are well-documented, structured, and straightforward. These tests have also been used in the field of AI and creativity as benchmarks.

# 2.1 Alternate Uses Task

The Alternate Uses Task (AUT), pioneered by J.P. Guilford in 1967 [28], is a prominent psychological assessment tool utilized to gauge divergents from participants based on specified criteria as outlined by Torrance. Similarly, the figural component, TTCT-Figure, also exists in two versions, featuring tasks involving drawing creation, completion of unfinished drawings, and development of new drawings from parallel lines. For example, participants may be asked to creatively finish a partially drawn image, with evaluations based on predetermined criteria [14, 35].
