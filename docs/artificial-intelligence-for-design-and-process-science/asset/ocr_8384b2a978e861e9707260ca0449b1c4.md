# ## Introduction

The concept of big data encompasses a diverse array of applications, including the management of large datasets, social media analytics, advanced data management capabilities, real-time data processing, and data mining, among others [1].

In the context of business processes with a focus on environmental, social, and governance (ESG) principles and sustainable development goals (SDGs) [2, 3], understanding the semantics of Legacy Data and its alignment with expected values and outcomes is crucial for navigating the complexities of the globally interconnected economy. Also, there is a growing need to leverage artificial intelligence (AI) to comprehend the semantics of Legacy Data and synchronize it with expected values [4].

This chapter introduces an AI framework for a business value-centric living (BVLL), utilizing a context-dependent methodology and information communication technology (ICT) to drive semantic modeling for big data in alignment with ESG and SDG frameworks [5–13].

Within this framework, Legacy Data undergoes AI-driven transformation and integration, ensuring its relevance and quality for analysis [14]. The AI algorithms employed on understanding the nuances of data semantics, enabling more insightful and accurate analytics [15]. The living laboratory fosters an environment where AI technologies can be experimented with and refined in realworld scenarios, maximizing their value to the business and contributing to sustainable development goals [16–20].

Organizations, industries, and society at large are seeking methods, techniques, and tools to leverage Big Data for meaningful insights and value creation in alignment with ESG and SDG principles [21]. AI technologies such as machine learning, natural language processing, and knowledge representation play a pivotal role in this endeavor, organizations to extract insights from massive datasets, uncover patterns, and make data-driven decisions based on the value chain [7, 8, 22].

Furthermore, the framework recognizes the contextual nature of data analysis, understanding that approaches may vary based on specific business circumstances and societal impacts [23].
