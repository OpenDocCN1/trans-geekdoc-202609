# 3.1 Anthroposia

Anthroposia, which is described as a state of being human, considers the ongoing conflict over control between human and artificial intelligence. It raises questions about authority, control, control, and accountability in a time where technology is advancing quickly, such as with LLM AI [3]. The idea of anthroposia can be elaborated upon and reinterpreted using the ANT principles to take into account the complex web of interactions between human and artificial intelligence.
