To explore these intricate considerations, we shall well begin with the definitions to understand the connections between creativity and design to better understand design creativity. As mentioned before, creativity is defined as the ability to develop useful, novel, and surprising ideas [10-12, 61]. Design aims at changing existing situations into desired ones [62]. Design is nonlinear and complex [63] and lies at the heart of creativity [64-67]. On the one hand, by definition, creativity is a measure of the process of design, either intentional or unconscious. On the other hand, it is important to note that not all existing ones are inherently creative; differing only in their specific context. Design creativity typically denotes a scenario where the resulting product implies creativity. Design creativity is inherently unpredictable; yet it may seem—yet it happens. It is noteworthy that different designers may provide various solutions to the same design problem, and even the same designer generate diverse solutions for the same problem over time [10]. Creativity may spontaneously emerge even if one does not intend to create a creative design, whereas creative design just may not come out matter how hard one tries. Moreover, it is conceivable that a designer may lack creativity, while the product demonstrates creativity attributes, and conversely, a designer may exhibit creativity, while the resulting product does not [16]. Concerning what was covered in this chapter, future studies are recommended to focus on AI-powered design to creativity and design creativity. Although there exist several papers on “AI and creativity,” deep explorations are required to associate complex and abstract concepts of design creativity with AI-related ones. In addition, for the differences between creativity and design creativity, researchers should focus on AI-based design creativity studies as there are a relatively limited number of studies in the field.

In conclusion, the exploration of AI in creativity is still in its early stages, and there is a need to adopt advanced and updated AI methods to further enrich this area of research. Although the literature indicates some applications of AI in creativity, other types of AI-powered research, such as machine-machine creativity, AI in teams, and machine-machine creativity, will soon emerge due to the advancement of AI methods. Generative neural networks, for example, have the potential to be employed in creativity and design creativity applications. In this chapter, we aimed to cover the most pivotal topics in the realm of AI applications in creativity and design creativity. We covered artificial creativity versus human creativity, creativity assessment using AI, human-AI co-creation, and AI in design creativity.
