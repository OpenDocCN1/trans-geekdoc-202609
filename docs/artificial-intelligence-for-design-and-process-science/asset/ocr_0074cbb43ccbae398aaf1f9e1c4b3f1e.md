# 5.1 Hydrogen Energy Spectrum

To understand the energy spectrum of hydrogen atom, the structure of the atom needs to be known. Hydrogen atoms are mainly composed of a nucleus and electron (s) orbiting around the nucleus. The electrons rotate in different levels with respect to its energy. The lowest orbit is n=1, mₛ=½ n is the principal quantum number and mₛ is the spin quantum number¹.
