# # A Revolutionary Artificial Intelligence Framework for a Business Value-Centric Living Laboratory

Cristiane Chaves Gattaz, Fuad Gattaz Sobrinho, Joaquim Roberto Neves Campos, Oscar Ivan Palma Pacheco, and Quymá da S Viana

**Abstract** The convergence of big data and artificial intelligence (AI) technologies have revolutionized the landscape of business processes, necessitating a profound understanding of Legacy Data semantics and its synchronization with expected values, highlighting the application of environmental, social, and governance (ESG) principles and sustainable development goals (SDGs) in the global economy. This chapter introduces an AI framework for a business value-centric living laboratory (BVLL), leveraging semantic modeling for big data analytics. Through AI-driven transformation and integration of Legacy Data, organizations extract meaningful insights and drive decision-making in alignment with human transformation objectives. The framework emphasizes the contextual nature of data analysis, recognizing the importance of AI technologies such as machine learning and natural language processing in unlocking potential sustainability. By exploring the synergy of AI and semantic modeling, businesses can navigate complex global landscapes, enhance operational and governance efficiency, and drive economic prosperity while contributing to environmental and social sustainability. This chapter presents the PRIMETHOD methodology and P3Tech architecture model, showcasing how AI integration revolutionizes process management, value realization, and operational excellence in line with ESG and SDG principles.
