# 2 Exploring the Synergy of Artificial Intelligence and Semantic Modeling for Maximizing Business Value in Complex Global Landscapes

In today's fast-paced business environment, organizations face various challenges such as low quality, productivity issues, high costs, and inefficiencies [34]. Recognizing the need for improvement, executives are turning to data analytics to gain deeper insights into business behavior [16, 35].

However despite the buzz around data analytics, obtaining accurate information about organizational performance requires complex thinking and transdisciplinary approaches that consider the impact on environmental, social, and governance factors and sustainable development goals [16, 35]. This involves developing advanced analytical models to understand and solve complex business problems.
AI further emphasizes the importance of advanced data analysis. With technologies requiring massive datasets and high processing capabilities [36], organizations must develop sophisticated models to ensure AI systems can interpret data effectively and achieve desired outcomes.

Despite the potential benefits, many organizations struggle to adopt advanced analytical techniques [5].
