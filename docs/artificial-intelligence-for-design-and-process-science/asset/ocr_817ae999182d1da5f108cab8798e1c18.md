Creativity outoubtedly stands as one of humanity's most complex and advanced cognitive tasks [10–12], engaging both mental and physical abilities [6, 13]. It operates as a recursive, complex, and nonlinear cognitive process, encompassing tasks like problem understanding, idea generation, and idea evolution [14–16]. Despite numerous theoretical investigations, there are unexplored facets of creativity, including assessment methods and human-machine collaboration, which necessitate exploration [17, 18]. These uncharted research presents opportunities for advancement through the application of AI [19–22].

The progress in artificial intelligence (AI) has empowered researchers to investigate processes like cognitive tasks, including creativity. AI tools have been developed into highly sophisticated instruments capable of surpassing programmed functionalities [23, 24]. This suggests that complex algorithms exhibit a form of "creativity.\Nonetheless, artificial creativity may diverge from human creativity across various domains. This suggests that exploring AI in creativity is of great importance.

Although AI has extensive use and discussion across various disciplines, the application in the reality of creativity remains relatively understudied, leaving questions unanswered and prompting ongoing research. Some of these questions include the following:

+   1. How can AI be leveraged to augment human creativity?
2. How can AI effectively evaluate a creative product or solution?
3. In what ways AI and humans collaborate in creativity tasks?
4. Is there a potential for AI to surpass human creativity?

These inquiries have been and continue to be focal points within the field of creativity. They serve as the driving force behind our exploration of AI’s applications in creativity. Several studies compare human and artificial creativity concerning several aspects [9, 19, 21–23, 25–27]. Our main aim in this chapter is to address the aforementioned questions to the fullest extent possible based on the existing literature.

In this chapter, we explore AI applications in creativity going over several aspects such as (1) AI as a creative tool, (2) AI-human co-creation, (3) creativity assessment by AI tools, and (4) AI creativity versus human creativity. We also provide some information regarding creativity tests and assessments.
