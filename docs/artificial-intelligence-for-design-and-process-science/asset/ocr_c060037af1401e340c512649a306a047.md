# 6.1 Empowering Business Value: Implementing PRIMETHOD and P3Tech in Research Institutions and Corporate Initiatives

A business value process was defined employing PRIMETHOD, P3Tech Initial, Management, and Process in the research facilities of the institutions described in the previous section of this chapter. The TELESP project initiative enabled the development of its BVLL through the application of its main characteristics shown in Fig. 15. With regard to the banking sector, fifteen employees from BB management and operational team underwent training to establish a BVLL on credit recovery. Additionally, the Institute of Exact and Technological Sciences of PUC-PR utilized the PRIMETHOD and P3Tech methodology and architecture to establish the inaugural Web-based digital ecosystem called LabP Network. This platform was designed for developing and simulation processes, as well as facilitating distance education for the creation of BVLLs. This initiative gave rise to the university’s inaugural business incubator, which concentrated on software development and forged partnerships with private sector entities like Siemens.
