# 4 Discussion

Creativity spans various disciplines and intersects with numerous fields. With the advancement of AI and computational machines, AI has found applications in various creativity studies. While much of the research has focused on creativity assessment, human-AI co-creativity, and the comparison between artificial and human creativity, there is a notable gap in exploring other aspects, such as simulating creativity using AI based on neurophysiological findings.

Existing literature predominantly enhances generic or artistic forms of creativity. However, there is a pressing need for further investigation into the challenges and limitations of machine learning and deep generative models, particularly in domains like design creativity, constraint handling, and simultaneous modeling of function and functional performance.

While many studies have primarily targeted tasks like AUT and TTCT, there is a lack of exploration in other creativity tasks. Although most studies suggest that AI outperforms human creativity levels in AUT and TTCT, it is crucial to consider additional factors like validity, appropriateness, and usefulness to comprehensively compare human and AI creativity.

In this domain, various AI methods and models have been employed, yet there is room for exploration and comparison with other AI methods. Choosing the appropriate AI tailored to specific creativity applications is essential, considering each method's unique capabilities and potential limitations. Future researchers should prioritize understanding AI methods, with workshops on prompt engineering being particularly beneficial.

Moreover, the social and psychological implications of AI application in creativity warrant attention. Future studies should investigate how AI impacts students' problem-identification capacity, convergent thinking, and long-term creativity when integrated into educational settings.

We would like to shed light on the aspect that has been less discussed in the literature. While several studies have applied AI or explored it in the realm of creativity, there are just a few studies that addressed the considerations of AI applications in design creativity. Investigating design creativity presents significant challenges due to its multifaceted nature, involving nonlinear cognitive processes and various tasks such as divergent and convergent thinking, perception, and memory retrieval.

Considering the points mentioned above, AI can be employed to investigate or model the intricate processes of design creativity. However, there is a necessity to understand the differences between creativity, design, and design creativity
