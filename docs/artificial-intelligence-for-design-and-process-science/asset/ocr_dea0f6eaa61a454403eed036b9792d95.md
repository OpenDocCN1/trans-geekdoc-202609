# 7 Summary and Future Prospects

This chapter presents a novel digital twin approach that provides a set of high-level abstract knowledge types that can be assembled and programmed via a purpose-built meta-programming language to formally represent, assemble, cross-correlate, and process knowledge regarding all aspects of a customized product, services, production processes, and quality assurance. By leveraging digital twins, the manufacturing model and its programming environment can help manufacturers to (i) gain insights to improve digital models; (ii) capture, aggregate, and analyze operational data; and (iii) improve product quality and production system efficiency.

Current and future work concentrates on extending blueprints with AI and machine learning techniques to improve situational awareness and enable validation of product designs, processes, and analysis of performance characteristics, critically identify more opportunities for product innovation. This will help DTS to recreate real-world conditions that can be experimented in a virtual environment. We expect to explore AI- and machine learning-based customization algorithms to automatically generate multiple alternatives for customization requirements.

Future work will also consider AI- and machine learning-based planning and scheduling algorithms to improve manufacturing process synchronization, transparency, and realize the utilization and delivery times of customized products by providing track-and-trace visibility across the entire production chain. Through the product development phase, the designer will be able to review and validate these manufacturing process plans by walking through the complete production sequence and directly visualize the corresponding product assembly, including associated tooling and resources.
