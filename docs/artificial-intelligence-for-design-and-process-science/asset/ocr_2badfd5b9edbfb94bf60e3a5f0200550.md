Figure 15 shows eight policy trajectories generated by the policy from random start networks for one of the test problems.

These trajectories show that the policy manages to transform random networks into high-performing networks that match or exceed the performance of the best of the 300 initial random networks even for problems that were never seen during training. The policy cannot follow a straight line but has to traverse accuracy “valleys,” effectively leaving local maxima in favor of better, globally optimal networks.
