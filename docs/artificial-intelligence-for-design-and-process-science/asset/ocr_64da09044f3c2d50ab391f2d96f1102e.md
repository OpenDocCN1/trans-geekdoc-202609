# 6 Dissemination

To maximize the impact of the AI4SG project, we devised a strategic dissemination plan aimed at broadening the project's scope beyond our immediate academic and geographic confines. Central to this strategy was a project website, designed to showcase the "AI for Social Good" projects undertaken by students across CSU campuses, available at https://www.aifsocialgood.org/. This site not only highlights the innovative solutions that students have developed to address societal challenges through AI but also serve to inspire educators, stakeholders, and investors about the potential of AI for social improvement.

Further amplifying our outreach, we organized a virtual symposium in collaboration with the CSU Chancellor's Office and STEM-NET, designed to foster disciplinary and cross-institutional dialogues about AI's role in societal transformation. The First Annual AI for Social Good Innovation Symposium brought students, faculty, industry partners, and community stakeholders, facilitating a rich exchange of ideas and highlighting career opportunities in the field of AI. It attracted about 150 attendances in May 2023. Through these initiatives, our dissemination effort not only showcased the practical outcomes of the AI4SG project but also established a foundation for ongoing collaboration and innovation in the application of AI for Social Good.

# 7 Conclusion

In this study, we introduced the AI for Social Good (AI4SG) project, which aims to offer an innovative educational pedagogy to impart AI knowledge to undergraduate students from diverse academic disciplines, with a focus on societal betterment. Students were instructed to generate a multifaceted understanding of AI applications through case studies, practical hands-on labs, and the prototyping of AI-driven solutions for local societal challenges. The AI4SG curriculum was implemented among three universities between Fall 2022 and Spring 2023 semesters, encompassing 514 students from three distinct disciplines.
