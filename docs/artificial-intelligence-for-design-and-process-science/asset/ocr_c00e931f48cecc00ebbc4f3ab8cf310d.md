Intellectualism is a school of thought that emphasizes rational thought, and human intelligence. Notable thinkers like Socrates, Plato, and Kant, who highlighted the value of human reason and knowledge in comprehending and influencing the universe, have an influence on it. The “transcendental idealism” approach, which Kant popularized, sees human mind as an active maker of knowledge rather than a passive receiver. To understand, navigating the environment using created models, or “Erkenntnis”, is what Kant defines as intellectualism [13].

In reaction to the rapid development of intelligent robots, a new philosophical idea known as Intelligenticism has developed. It provides a new viewpoint for navigating the shifting terrain of AI. By considering the place of AI-powered robots in our intellectual framework, this idea goes beyond the confines of conventional human-centric intellectualism. Intelligenticism pushes us to develop a thorough grasp of these complex systems while exercising caution when giving computers the ability to make decisions. It highlights how crucial it is to strike a balance between AI’s potential and human intelligence. It also emphasizes the need to reconsider how we perceive intelligence, its role in society, and the moral ramifications of empowering artificial intelligence. In essence, Intelligenticism expands the definition of intellectualism to take into account the function and significance of AI in our philosophical framework.

Due to the widespread use of AI and its increasing dominance in information and communication technologies (ICT), the IS discipline is in a unique position to adopt the concepts discussed in this [1, 2, 12]. We believe that this study will serve as a springboard for a deeper and more reflective investigation of the function of AI and the complex relationships that will influence humankind’s destiny. We intend to advance a future in which people and machines coexist peacefully by incorporating Intelligenticism into the IS field as a starting philosophical framework to help traverse the fast-evolving world of AI powered by contemporary AI.
