Over a period of time, your costs to host your models can increase, and you would need to manage them proactively. You will need to identify how models are being spent across different environments, such as retraining and posting, as well as across different business units, owners, environments projects, purpose, and so on.

You can implement AWS tagging to keep a tab on these expenses. For example, you can tag AWS instances as retraining or hosting depending upon the purpose for which they are used.

You can then run billing reports based on the tags and understand how these models are used across these dimensions. This visibility will help you control costs by working with various stakeholders.

The visibility of costs using these tags can help not only control the costs but also plan for future expenses based on the cost of running the models and the benefits from them.
