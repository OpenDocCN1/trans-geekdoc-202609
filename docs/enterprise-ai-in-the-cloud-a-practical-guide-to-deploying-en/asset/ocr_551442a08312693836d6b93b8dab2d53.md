This section discusses some validation metrics for classification problems, such as accuracy, precision, recall, and F1 score, as shown in Figure 17.10.

> **TIP** No single metric will tell you the whole story. While accuracy may be very impressive, it can be misleading if your classes are imbalanced. That's why you need to look at other metrics such as precision, recall, and F1 score.

# Accuracy

Accuracy is the percentage of the correctly predicted labels out of all predictions calculated as follows:

Accuracy = Number of correct predictions / Total number of predictions
