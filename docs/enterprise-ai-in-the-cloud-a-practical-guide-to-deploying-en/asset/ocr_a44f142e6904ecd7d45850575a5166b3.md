# Conduct Organizational Readiness Assessment

Conducting an organizational readiness assessment will help you understand the organization's willingness, desire, and ability to cope with change and put measures in place to address any gaps, such as skills training, communication, sponsorship, and changes to the change acceleration.

# Create a Case for Change

The case for change is a document that outlines the benefits that the organization will gain through this AI initiative. You should create it after the stakeholder assessment and use it in various communications to get buy-in from the employees. It should be clear, concise, and persuasive to the audience and be documented with data.
