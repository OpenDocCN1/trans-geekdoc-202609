# # Developing an AI Transformation Plan

The AI transformation plan is a strategic plan focusing on taking the organization forward in adopting AI at the enterprise level.

Unlike an AI portfolio, an AI transformation plan goes beyond individual AI initiatives and instead looks at the organization as a whole and contains a clear roadmap to increase the maturity levels of the organization in various areas such as culture, processes, and infrastructure. It takes into consideration that for AI to succeed and to be sustained in the long term, it needs to be integrated into the other parts of the organization, and therefore its focus is around enabling that integration.
