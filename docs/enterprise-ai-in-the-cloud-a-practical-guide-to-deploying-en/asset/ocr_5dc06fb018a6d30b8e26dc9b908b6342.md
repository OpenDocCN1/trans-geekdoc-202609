+   > **Root mean squared error:** As the name suggests, it is the square root of the MSE.

+   > **R-squared:** Also known as the coefficient of determination and is the proportion of the variation in the dependent variable that is predictable from the independent variable(s).

# ## Clustering Metrics

Adjusted Rand index and silhouette coefficient are two clustering metrics.

+   > **Adjusted Rand index (ARI)** measures the similarity between two data clusters.

+   > **Silhouette coefficient** identifies a model that has better-defined clusters. It provides a visual way to assess clusters.

# ## Ranking Metrics

Precision@k and mean average precision are two metrics used to measure rankings.

+   > **Precision@k** measures how many of the first k recommendations are in the set of true positive items. True positive is when the actual and predicted values are the same.

+   > **Mean average P(MAP)** is used to measure the ranking of the recommended items.

**TIP** _Set reasonable ranges for metrics, and when the performance falls out of range, trigger a review or retraining process._
