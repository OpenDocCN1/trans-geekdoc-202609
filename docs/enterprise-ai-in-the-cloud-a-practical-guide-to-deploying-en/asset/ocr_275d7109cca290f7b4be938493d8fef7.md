# SUMMARY

The key takeaways from this chapter underscore the necessity for organizations to take AI seriously to gain a competitive edge. This means elevating machine learning projects from mere PoC pet projects into large-scale, enterprise-wide transformative efforts. To achieve this, you need to adopt a more systematic AI-first strategy and methodology, the primary focus of this book.

This chapter also delved into the transformative power of cloud computing, enabling many use cases previously unattainable and providing a pathway to thrilling consumer experiences.
