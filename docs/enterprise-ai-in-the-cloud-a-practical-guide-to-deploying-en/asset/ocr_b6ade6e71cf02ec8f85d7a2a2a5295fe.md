Within classification are binary classification or multiclass classification:

+   - **Binary classification:** The outcome can be one of two values, such as whether the email is spam.
+   - **Multiclass classification:** You can have more than two outcomes, such as when you're trying to classify a set of animal images. For example, it could be a cat, dog, horse, and so on.

Another example of a classification problem involves an Amazon fulfillment center. There are millions of boxes. They have hundreds of items in each box, and some items may be missing or damaged. You can use machine learning to identify which boxes are damaged and then send warehouse representatives to address the issue. Note that classification can also be further subdivided into multilabel classification, imbalanced classification, ensemble methods, support vector machines, KNN, and so on. Logistic regression, SVM, and decision tress are types of binary classification, and neural networks, random forests, and naïve Bayes multivariate are types of multiclass classification.
