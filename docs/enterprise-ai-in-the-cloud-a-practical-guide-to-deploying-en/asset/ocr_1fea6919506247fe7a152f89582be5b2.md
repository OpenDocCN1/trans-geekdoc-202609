# SUMMARY

This chapter explained the importance of Responsible AI to ensure AI systems are designed and deployed with ethical, social, and human-centric principles in mind. Users should be able to understand AI decisions, and the systems should eliminate bias and prioritize human values. These systems must behave consistently and reliably and should be scalable to large amounts of data. That means you must ensure adherence to data privacy regulations, implement robust security and testing, and involve stakeholders during design.

In the next chapter, we will discuss developing the AI strategy, the roadmap, and getting alignment from various stakeholders.
