# # HANDS-ON EXERCISE FOR CULTURAL EVOLUTION AND ORG REDESIGN TO ADOPT AI

This exercise gives you tangible experience in dealing with org change management practices in the context of AI adoption.

| TASK DESCRIPTION | TASK OWNERS | DELIVERABLES |
|---|---|---|
| **Assess Cultural Tensions**<br>Identify cultural tensions using surveys, interviews, etc.<br>You can use Figure 11.3 to identify different categories of tension. | HR department, organizational design team | Cultural report |
| **Develop a Cultural Evolution Program**<br>Create a strategy to evolve culture focusing on innovation, alignment, risk-taking, resource-building, collaboration.<br>Refer to Figures 11.4 and 11.5 for the steps, phases, and tasks. | Senior management, HR department | Cultural evolution program and strategy document |
| **Create an Organizational Redesign Plan**<br>Assess current organizational structure for agility and innovation. Develop redesign plan for implementing AI. | Senior management, operations team | Organizational redesign plan |
