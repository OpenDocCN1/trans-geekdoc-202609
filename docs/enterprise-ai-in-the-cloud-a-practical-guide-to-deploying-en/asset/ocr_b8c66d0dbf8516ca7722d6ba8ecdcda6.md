# Regression Metrics

Consider these regression metrics:

+   Mean absolute error (MAE): This gives an idea of how wrong the predictions were and is calculated by taking the average of the absolute differences between the predicted and actual values.

+   Mean squared error: This is similar to MAE except that the differences are squared before averaging, so they accentuate the more significant errors more than the MAE.
