# Ensuring Identical Performance Across Training and Production

Synchronizing the architecture across environments alone isn't enough. You must also ensure the model performs identically across the training and production environments. You can achieve this validation through the use of AWS SageMaker Model Monitor. If you find a difference in the model's accuracy between production and training environments, you can start troubleshooting the cause and try to address the issue.

# Looking for Bias in Training and Production

SageMaker Clarify can also detect bias either during training or in production. SageMaker Clarify can help you understand how these models predict using a feature attribution approach. It also keeps monitoring the model in production and looks for any bias or feature attribution drift.

# Generating Governance Reports

You can use the governance reports generated by SageMaker Clarify to share with your stakeholders, such as audit teams, regulators, risk and compliance teams, and other business stakeholders.
