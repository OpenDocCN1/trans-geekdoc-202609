Remember that machine learning helps you identify patterns in your dataset to predict outcomes. This feature/capability can be used to improve existing processes while also enabling new opportunities. Processes that are well suited to AI/ML are those

+   > Where decisions are based on data
+   > Where decisions happen repeatedly, either thousands or millions of times
+   > That use software to automate
+   > That are slow
