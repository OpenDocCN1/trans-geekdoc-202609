# **Becoming Agile and Scalable by Moving Data Centers Into the Cloud**

How did they do it?

+   > They moved their eight data centers to the cloud.
+   > They built an 11,000-member technology team over eight years.
+   > They instituted training plans and programs for the AWS cloud.

In the process, they were able to reap the following benefits:

+   > They reduced the environment build time from three months to three minutes.
+   > They had a high-performing agile and scalable infrastructure, which helped them go to market with their products much earlier, from months to days.
+   > They were even able to release applications that took months and weeks to more than once a day.
