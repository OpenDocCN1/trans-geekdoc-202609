> **BEST PRACTICE TIP** A robust AI system should perform consistently even when the data constantly changes, especially in an enterprise setting, where many data sources are feeding the model. If the system produces unreliable insights, users will lose trust and confidence when using the system. You need to test the system for any surprising attacks or situations.

# Collaborative AI

The idea behind collaborative AI is to leverage the strengths of both AI and humans. While humans have creative thinking, leadership, judgment, and emotional and social skills, robots can crunch large amounts of data and help humans with physical labor, information gathering, speed, and routine tasks such as customer service. By combining the strengths of both humans and AI, you can build better systems to improve business processes.
