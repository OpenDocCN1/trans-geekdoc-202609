Internet Protocol Security, a suite of protocols that provide a cryptographic layer to both IPv4 and IPv6

**IPT**  
Integrated Product Team, a customer-focused group that focuses on the entire lifecycle of a project

Internet Protocol version 4, commonly called IP. It is the fundamental protocol of the Internet

Internet Protocol version 6, the successor to IPv4, featuring far larger address space, simpler routing, and simpler address assignment

**ISDN**

**ITSEC**  
Information Technology Security Evaluation Criteria, the first successful international evaluation model
