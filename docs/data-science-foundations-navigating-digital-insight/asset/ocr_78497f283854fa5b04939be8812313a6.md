Pure means that most of the items in a group belong to the same category (e.g. all ‘Yes’ or all ‘No’). There are different ways of calculating how pure things are; one of the common methods is using entropy and information gain. Entropy measures uncertainty, and information gain tells us how much we reduce uncertainty by splitting:

```
$H = -\sum_{C} p(c) \log_2 p(c)$
```

To determine the best split, decision trees rely on a mathematical concept called information gain.

$H_{attr}$ is the entropy

```
$H_{attr} = -\sum_{V} p(v) \sum_{C} p(c|v) \log_2 p(c|v)$
```

where $c$ is the outcome, $v$ is the attribute and $p$ is the probability of it occurring.

```
$InfoGain_{attr} = H - H_{attr}$
```
