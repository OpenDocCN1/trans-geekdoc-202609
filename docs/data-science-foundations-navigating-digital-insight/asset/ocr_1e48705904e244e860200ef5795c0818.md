# The formula

The simplest ARIMA method is known as the Box–Jenkins model. It is a combination of the linear trend and the moving average.

$Y_t = \delta + \phi_1 Y_{t-1} + \phi_2 Y_{t-2} + ... + \phi_p Y_{t-p}$
