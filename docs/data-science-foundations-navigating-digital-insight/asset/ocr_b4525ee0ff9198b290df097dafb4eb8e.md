If $p \le \alpha$: Reject the null hypothesis ($H_0$). This means there's enough evidence to support the alternative hypothesis ($H_\alpha$).

If $p > \alpha$: Fail to reject the null hypothesis. This means there's not enough evidence to support $H_\alpha$.

Example of hypothesis testing: Is a coin fair? Let's say you flip a coin 20 times and get 17 heads. You suspect the coin might be biased.

State the hypotheses:

$H_0$: The coin is fair ($P(\text{Heads}) = 0.5$).

$H_\alpha$: The coin is biased ($P(\text{Heads}) \neq= 0.5$).

Choose a significance level:

$\alpha = 0.05$.

Collect data and calculate the test statistic:

Number of heads = 17.

Total flips = 20.
