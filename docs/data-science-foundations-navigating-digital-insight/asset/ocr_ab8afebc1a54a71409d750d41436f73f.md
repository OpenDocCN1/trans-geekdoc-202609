Predictive analytics gives you a glimpse into the future, but it doesn’t tell you what to do with that information.

# PRESCRIPTIVE ANALYTICS: DECIDING WHAT TO DO

Prescriptive analytics is the final and most advanced stage. It’s not just about understanding the past or predicting the future – it helps you to decide **what actions to take** to achieve the best outcome. Prescriptive models combine data, predictions and optimisations to recommend the best course of action.

Think of prescriptive analytics as answering the question: what should we do about it?
