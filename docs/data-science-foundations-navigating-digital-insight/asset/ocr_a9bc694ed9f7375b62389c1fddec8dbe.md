Correlation is one of the most over-used terms when talking about data and statistics. Journalists will quite often use the correlation of two data sets to build a story when it helps to justify that it is it is someone’s fault for something happening.

Let's try an example of looking at how time spent on computer games affects student grades.

We can gather data on students by asking for their test grades at school and also asking how many hours a week they spend gaming. The data may look like this:

| Student | Test score | Hours gaming |
|---|---|---|
| A | 45 | 5 |
| B | 95 | 1.5 |
| C | 63 | 3.5 |
| D | 28 | 7.5 |
| E | 78 | 2.5 |
