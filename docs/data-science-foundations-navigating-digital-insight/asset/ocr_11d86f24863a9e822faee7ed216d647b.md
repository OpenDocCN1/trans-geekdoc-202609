# NEURAL NETWORKS

Neural networks is a branch of ML, and is fundamental to AI. They look to model how the brain works, with different connections between neurons firing in response to inputs, leading to outputs. If you see a ball coming towards you (input), you decide (neurons firing) whether to duck or catch it (the output).
