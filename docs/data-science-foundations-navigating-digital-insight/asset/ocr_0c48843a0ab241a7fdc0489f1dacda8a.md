# BIAS OR SKEWED DATA?

He was referring to the way that we can make numbers show a dismay that may not be the correct picture. There are lots of words used when describing data ‘appears’ to have been influenced. Many people bandy about the terms ‘skewed’ and ‘biased’ – and also correlation – but we will talk about that later in the book.
