# # DATA SCIENCE FOUNDATIONS
Navigating digital insight
Stephen Mariadas and Ian Huke

While data science is powering most modern organisations its technical intricacies often remain confined to a few.

Data Science Foundations provides foundational knowledge for newcomers to the field, building their confidence and understanding. This will enable effective project constructions through informed decision-making and action. In an accessible manner, this book provides explanations of concepts and methods in layman's terms supported by examples. It includes a holistic view of data science covering the technical, ethical and delivery challenges illustrated by case studies.

Aligned with industry-recognised qualifications, this is the definitive guide for aspiring data scientists, providing a solid foundation in data analysis techniques.

+   * Written for the beginner: providing clear explanations in layman's terms
* Explanations supported by practical examples
* Content aligned to data analysis qualifications
* Covers the technical, ethical and delivery challenges

A must-read companion for anyone eager to dive into data science.
