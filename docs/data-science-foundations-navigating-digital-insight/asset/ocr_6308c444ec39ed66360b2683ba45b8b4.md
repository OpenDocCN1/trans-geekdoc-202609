Even for a simple data science project, the audience can often be segmented. It is worth considering four potential segments, consisting of:

+   - **Technical**: This audience will want technical detail of the models used, and understands the technical language.
- **Managerial**: This audience wants the key insights expressed in simple non-technical terms, with the ability to drill into detail that is relevant to them.
- **Executive**: This audience wants the key insights expressed in simple non-technical terms.
- **Team**: Like the executive audience, this one wants key insights expressed in simple non-technical terms. However, the scope of information provided might be more limited.

This audience segmentation is a generalisation, providing a useful starting point. How might this play out in practice?
