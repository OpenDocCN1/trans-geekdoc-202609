The types of data we use can be given official-sounding names. Let's start at the top and look at the two main types of data which people tend to use:

+   * Quantitative data defines an amount of something. It is almost always numbers and is the most common form of data that people tend to think of in their reports, scorecards, dashboards. Humans are trained to work with numbers so, when we get data that doesn't fit this form, we try and make it quantitative just so that we can use traditional methods like percentages or totals (e.g., how many customers said we were 'good').
