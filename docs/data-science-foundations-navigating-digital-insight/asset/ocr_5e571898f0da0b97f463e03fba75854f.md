# What is an area chart?

Area charts are like line charts, but the area beneath the line is filled in, emphasising the magnitude of change over time. They’re great for visualising cumulative data (Figure 11.6).

# When to use area charts

+   - **Showing trends over time:** They are useful for illustrating trends and comparing multiple quantities over time.
- **Visualising part-to-whole relationships:** They are ideal for showing how individual parts contribute to a whole over time
