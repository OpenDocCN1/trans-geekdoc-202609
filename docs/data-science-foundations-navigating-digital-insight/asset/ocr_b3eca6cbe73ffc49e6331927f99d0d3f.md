When to use box and whisker plots

+   - **Comparing distributions:** The are useful for comparing the distribution of data across different categories.
- **Identifying outliers:** They are ideal for spotting outliers in your data set.

Remember: Box plots are especially helpful when you have multiple data sets to compare, as they provide a clear summary of each distribution.
