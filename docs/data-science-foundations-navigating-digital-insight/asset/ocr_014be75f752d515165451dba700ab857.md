Simplify, simplify, simplify

When it comes to visualisations, less is often more. Avoid clutter by focusing on the most important data points and removing unnecessary elements. Simplicity not only enhances clarity but also makes your visualisations more aesthetically pleasing.

Use colour wisely

Colour can be a powerful tool in your visualisation arsenal, but it must be used thoughtfully. Stick to a cohesive colour scheme and use colour to highlight key information rather than overwhelm the viewer.

+   - **Consistent colours:** Maintain consistency across visualisations for easy interpretation. Lots of organisations have a ‘theme’ or ‘palette’ of colours to use.
- **Contrasting colours:** Use contrast to draw attention to important data points.
