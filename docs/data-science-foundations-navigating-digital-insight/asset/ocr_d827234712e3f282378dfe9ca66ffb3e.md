# UNDERSTANDING THE HYPOTHESIS

What is a hypothesis?

In data science, a hypothesis is a formal statement proposing a potential explanation or relationship between variables. It serves as a foundation for testing assumptions and drawing conclusions based on data-driven evidence. As a science, there are clear rules on how we should propose – and test – our hypothesis.
