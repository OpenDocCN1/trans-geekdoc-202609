**Trend:** A pattern of changes over time.

**Unsupervised learning:** Where the model learns by looking for patterns in unlabelled data.

**User experience honeycomb:** A framework showing the seven principles of user experience.

**Variability:** The inconsistency in data – one of the 5Vs.

**Variance:** The average of the squared differences from the mean – it shows how much the data varies.

**Variety:** A concept of Big Data highlighting the diverse range of data generated.

**Velocity:** A concept of Big Data highlighting the speed at which data is created and processed.

**Veracity:** The quality of data – one of the 5Vs.

**Volume:** A concept of Big Data highlighting the vast amounts of data being created.

**Waterfall:** A linear method of project delivery where activities are cascaded, one activity being completed before another starts.
