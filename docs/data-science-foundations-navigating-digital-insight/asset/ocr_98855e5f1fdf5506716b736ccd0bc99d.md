If you think about it, the whole data analysis lifecycle contributes to a good user experience. The usefulness, desirability and credibility of the communication are dependent upon the framing of the problem, the data sourcing and preparation and the methods applied. The design of the communication also plays a part in whether these principles are met. Design is similarly an important factor in whether the knowledge shared is useful, findable and accessible. How the communication is stored and shared also plays a role in whether the knowledge is findable and accessible.
