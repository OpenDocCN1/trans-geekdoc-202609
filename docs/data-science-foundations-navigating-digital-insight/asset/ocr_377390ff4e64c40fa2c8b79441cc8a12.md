Once your model is ready, interpret the results and share them with stakeholders in a way they can easily understand. Remember: the main goal is to solve the business problem.

Example:

Instead of saying ‘Our model has 85 per cent accuracy’, say ‘Our model predicts 85 per cent of customers at risk of churning can be identified, allowing us to target them with retention offers.

Tips:

+   - Use clear visuals such as charts and graphs.
- Focus on actionable insights (e.g. ‘Offer discounts to customers likely to churn’).

Pitfall to avoid:

+   - Too much technical jargon. Business stakeholders may not understand complex statistical terms.
