ARIMA works best when your data does not have any trends (also known as seasonality). We are therefore aiming to make the data stationary.

To make data stationary (i.e. remove the trend), we calculate the difference between consecutive values. This is called ‘differencing’.

| Week | Mobile phones | First difference (change) |
|---|---|--|
| 1 | 500 | – |
| 2 | 520 | 520 – 500 = 20 |
| 3 | 530 | 530 – 520 = 10 |
| 4 | 570 | 570 – 530 = 40 |
| 5 | 580 | 580 – 570 = 10 |
| 6 | 600 | 600 – 580 = 20 |
| 7 | 630 | 630 – 600 = 30 |
