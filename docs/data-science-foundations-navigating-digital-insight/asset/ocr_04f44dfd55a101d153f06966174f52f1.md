# Skewed data

We have seen in Chapter 6 that skewed data is where the data is unevenly distributed around the mean. A model's output can be affected by how skewed the data is within it. If this is the case, steps can be taken to normalise the data, leading to an even distribution either side of the mean. This even distribution is referred to as normal distribution or Gaussian distribution. Statistical tests that assume normalised distribution are called parametric tests, those that don't are nonparametric tests.
