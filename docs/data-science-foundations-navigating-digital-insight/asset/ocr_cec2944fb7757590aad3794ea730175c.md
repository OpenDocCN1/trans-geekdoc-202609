To accurately collect data, Steve tried several different methods, using drinks in a range of containers. Eventually, he settled on filling and pressurising containers himself. In doing this, he had to source specialist equipment and learn how to use it.

Once a robust data set had been collected, Steve looked to understand the relationships between the data. He initially started out using a simple linear regression model. When this didn't reveal any substantive relationships, he investigated other models. This included multivariate and polynomial regression – to no avail, the hypothesis remained unproven.

Steve is very conscious of the old saying ‘garbage, garbage out’. He recognises the importance of data quality and the challenges of measuring it, including the tolerances of the machines used for this.
