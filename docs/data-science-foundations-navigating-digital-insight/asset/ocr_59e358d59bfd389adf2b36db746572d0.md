+   **Bias** is one of the most popular terms to use when people ‘feel’ that something is unfair or not accurate. Bias can happen when your sample or method of sampling causes the results to lean a certain way. For example, if you only ask customers who buy mobile phones if they would like to buy headphones. Your results might be biased because you’ve left those out who are less likely to buy headphones. Bias can lead to incorrect conclusions because your sample doesn’t truly represent the population, and making sure you know the best way of sampling is the easiest way of avoiding bias.
