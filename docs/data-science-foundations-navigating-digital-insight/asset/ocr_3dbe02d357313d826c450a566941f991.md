# PRINCIPLES OF USER EXPERIENCE

Communication can be made in many forms, including written, visual and oral. Often, it is a combination of these – for example, a presentation. In this case, the presentation materials will normally be made up of words and images, with a voiceover by the presenter. In this instance, there is an opportunity to explain and react to an audience. Where a written or visual report is produced and made available to the audience, the opportunity to explain further does not exist. It is important to understand the communication channel to ensure that you tailor your communication to deliver a good user experience.
