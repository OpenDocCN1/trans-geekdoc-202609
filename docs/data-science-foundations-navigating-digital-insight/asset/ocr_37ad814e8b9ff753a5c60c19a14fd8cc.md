Let us consider a project to help recruitment, looking to identify good candidates who would be good at a job. Potential data sources could well be biased. If we used that data, could it prejudice or harm future applicants for the job? If so, would it seem unethical to use the data. We could also ask ourselves whether people giving data to apply for a job would be happy to have their data used to predict whether future applicants would be successful in that role. They might have signed a consent clause, but is this really consenting to the use of the data in this way? This might mean the data’s use in this way isn’t unlawful. In fact, both questions might make us consider the use of the data both unethical and unlawful.
