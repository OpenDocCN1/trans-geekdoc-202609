Let’s calculate everything for the rule ‘battery pack → case’:

+   1. Support
   1. Support(battery pack) = 4/5 = 0.8.
   2. Support(case) = 4/5 = 0.8.
   3. Support(battery pack, case) = 3/5 = 0.6.

+   2. Confidence
   1. Confidence = Support(battery pack, case) / Support(battery pack).
   2. Confidence = 0.6 / 0.8 = 0.75 (75 per cent).
   3. Interpretation: When people buy battery pack, 75 per cent also buy case.

+   3. Lift
   1. Lift = Support (battery pack, case) / Support (case) × Support (battery pack)
   2. Lift = 0.6 / 0.64 = 0.9375

Key points:

+   1. Lift = 1: Items are independent.
   2. Lift > 1: Items are complementary.
   Example: chips and salsa are quite commonly bought together and might have lift = 3.0.
   3. Lift < 1: Items are substitutes.
