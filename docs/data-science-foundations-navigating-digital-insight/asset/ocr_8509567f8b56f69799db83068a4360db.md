# Figure 2.6 Communication plan for phone project

| Sponsor | A weekly meeting will take place with the sponsor where they will be given an update on progress, and any key issues will be raised with them |
| Regulator | The compliance team will be updated at the monthly compliance board meeting |
| Employees (Early project) | HR will be asked to inform the project of any questions raised in relation to phones. |
| Employees (Late project: once they are aware it will impact them) | Updates will be provided weekly on the intranet and team leaders will communicate the proposed changes in team meetings. |

One final point on stakeholders: they will have different levels of understanding of the problem and data science. It is important to understand this and adapt your language and explanations to reflect this.
