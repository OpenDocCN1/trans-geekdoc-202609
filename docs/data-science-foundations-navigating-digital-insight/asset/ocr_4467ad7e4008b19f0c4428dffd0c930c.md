At this point it is worth revisiting the stakeholder needs. They are likely to be different from a one-off investigation. A key question is how frequently they need the report and when it needs to be received. Also, the scope of the information required might change. In this instance, the problem will need to be reframed. There might also be benefit in automating the process, both to speed up its production and to increase trust by reducing errors.

# PRACTICAL TIPS

+   - Understand your audience and their needs.
- Consider all aspects of the user experience.
- Ask what knowledge can be derived from the information obtained from your models.
- Thinking about the domain should assist in generating knowledge from information.
- Consider who or what the subject of the story should be.
- Plan the story you want to tell and how you will envisage it.
- Beware of telling a story people want to hear, rather than the one that should be told.
