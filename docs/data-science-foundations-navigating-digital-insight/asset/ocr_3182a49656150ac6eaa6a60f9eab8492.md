In today’s rapidly evolving world, data science stands as a beacon of innovation and insight. This book is designed to introduce you to the fascinating realm of data science, guiding you through the essential techniques and models that define the field. More importantly, it aims to show you how to harness the power of data science to achieve success within your organisation. This book is unique in the way that it covers all the practical concepts of getting a data science project up and running, without the need for deep technical knowledge.

The book’s structure mirrors the data analysis lifecycle, a framework that while seemingly straightforward, is often oversimplified in educational settings. It correctly highlights the iterative nature of this process, reminding us that real-world data science is rarely a linear progression. This emphasis on iteration and adaptability is crucial for anyone working with data, where experimentation and refinement are the name of the game.
