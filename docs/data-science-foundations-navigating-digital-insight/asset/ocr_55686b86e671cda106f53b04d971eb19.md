# RECOMMENDATIONS

Recommendations will often flow from investigating our problem and testing our hypothesis. However, it is very easy to make recommendations to address something our investigation has revealed that are not backed up by the hypothesis testing or other evidence.

Going back to our contact centre example, we recommend that contact time is increased to improve customer satisfaction. However, we don't know that this is the case from the analysis we have undertaken. Yes, satisfaction is forecast to continue to fall, and yes there is correlation between contact time and satisfaction. That doesn't mean that shorter contact time is the cause of lower customer satisfaction. It is a theory, hence the proposal of A/B testing to develop understanding.

When putting forward recommendations, be clear as to which can be evidenced and which are only theories.
