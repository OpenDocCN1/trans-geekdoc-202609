# When to use bar charts

+   - **Comparing categories**: They are ideal for showing comparisons between discrete categories, such as sales across different regions.
- **Display counts**: They are useful when illustrating the frequency of different values in a data set.

Remember: Keep your bar chart simple by limiting the number of categories and using consistent colour schemes to avoid overwhelming your audience.
