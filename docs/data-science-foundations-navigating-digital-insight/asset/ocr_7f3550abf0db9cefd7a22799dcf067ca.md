# RISK MITIGATION STRATEGIES

Follow policy

Where your organisation has a policy in place, follow it. This means you need to know where the policies are, what is in them and who to ask if something isn’t clear. Just keep in mind that the policy might not properly reflect the legal and regulatory standards to which you will be held.

Data classification

Where your organisation has a data classification policy, understand the classification that applies to the data you plan to use and apply the appropriate classification to the data sets you create.
