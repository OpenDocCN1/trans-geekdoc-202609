What is linear regression?

Imagine you're trying to predict how much your plant will grow
ased on how much water you give it. Linear regression is like
drawing a straight line through a bunch of data points to show
the relationship between these two things. It helps us to
understand how one thing changes when another thing
changes.

Linear regression models the relationship between
continuous target variable and one or more predictor variables
