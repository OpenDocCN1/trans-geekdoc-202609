The technique to move categorical data in one field into binary data in many fields is called ‘one-hot encoding’.

# PROPERTIES OF DATA SETS

As well as the data having properties, data sets can also have properties. Two properties often encountered are patterns over time and skewness.

Patterns over time

When looking for patterns over time, the data might have a trend. It could be an upwards trend (values increasing over time) or a downwards trend (values decreasing over time). A pattern over time could also be cyclical, for example retail sales peaking in the run-up to Christmas every year.
