We will take a step back from the end of the world as an AI risk and focus on the more technical risks relating to AI outputs. The main risks can be summarised as:

+   - **Bias**: Does the AI produce outputs that are in some way biased?
- **Hallucination**: Has the AI made something up, so the output is in some way incorrect?
- **Transparency**: Can you understand how the AI has reached the outcome it has?
- **Privacy**: Do you understand, and are you happy with, the data you have provided is being used?

In using AI within your work, it is important to understand your organisation’s policy in relation to AI. This will be designed to help mitigate these risks.
