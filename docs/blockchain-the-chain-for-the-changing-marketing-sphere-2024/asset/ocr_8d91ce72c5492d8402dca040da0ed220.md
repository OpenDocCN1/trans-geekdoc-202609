# Verification of Ad Impressions and Clicks

Blockchain technology is the best solution to the issues that advertising now faces, everything points to it having a significant impact on the Internet advertising industry. Smart contracts will be used in a blockchain-based advertising system to track ads and only pay for verified impressions as specified in the contract.

# Decrease Advertising Fraud

Every other digital marketer is undoubtedly aware of the widespread advertising fraud carried out to generate quicker and more often clicks. However, end-to-end encryption made available by blockchain technology makes it feasible to generate an unchangeable record of transactions that eliminates the risk of fraud and unauthorized activity.

# Maintain Data Integrity and Privacy

With blockchain transactions, users may now manage their data. They will be the owner of the data and be able to stop others from getting and abusing it thanks to private and public keys. One can manage when and how a third party can access data that they own by storing it on the blockchain.

# Effect of Blockchain on Marketing

Blockchain technology has major potential for disruption, and its effects on the marketplace are well known. Rapid innovations in blockchain technology have effectively ushered in significant shifts in the industry with benefits in use scenarios like capital management, monitoring, and compliance with regulations. For various industries, blockchain technology has opened up new productivity opportunities, although in its infancy. On this are a few significant possibilities that blockchain technology may affect business marketing.
