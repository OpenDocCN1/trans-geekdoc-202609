The potential for blockchain technology seems to be quite promising, and the advancements that can be accomplished by acquiring the technology will be unimaginable. ## The Impact of Blockchain on the Financial Sector

Blockchain technology now has widespread interest. We are continually inundated with stories that describe how technology will alter the world. Although it may change many facets of our life, we have a strong hunch that the changes will take place in spheres that we will only indirectly be affected by. That is to say, the deployment of invisible operations, or what organizations consider to be their back-office tasks, will be significantly altered as a result of blockchain technology. The “marriage” of technology and finance is referred to as “financial technology” (sometimes spelled “FinTech”) in certain circles. When technology and money are brought together, a “chemical reaction” takes place, which results in the creation of a multiplier impact that is far more significant than the combination of the two separately.

Blockchain technology is regarded as a vital tool in the financial sector, and it has the potential to bring about exciting developments in areas such as internet development, economic growth, and financial innovation. The financial industry urgently requires a digital transformation as well as the implementation of measures that will ensure the effective use of Blockchain technology. As a result, the purpose of the present study is to evaluate the moderating impact that information technology alignment plays in addition to the mediating function that Blockchain adoption plays between new digital process innovation and strategy innovation, as well as financial performance. Over the next ten years, blockchain technology is anticipated to have a substantial influence on e-commerce. If one of the initial key elements of usage of the innovation was making payments and worldwide transfers, then the recent flurry of approaches - and the participation of household names in those initiatives - is a clear indication that blockchain technology is likely to expand and have a profound effect on the economy beyond just the payment services industry.

The expansion of technology has resulted in substantial shifts in the business world, especially within the financial sector. A technology development known as blockchain has been developed to bring confidence and transparency to financial transactions. Blockchain technology can improve operational efficacy, raise levels of transparency, lessen exposure to risk by tying up fewer assets during transactions, and cut down on expenses, such as those associated with the transaction fees incurred in the financial industry. Several applications may be of use to the financial sector, including smart contracts, which is one of the most significant uses of blockchain technology; however, there are other possibilities as well. It is too soon to tell what precisely blockchain technology will make possible given it is still in its early stages of development; yet, the technology has the potential to cause a significant disruption to the present financial system. The most far-fetched theory is that distributed ledger technology would eventually render banks obsolete. The inability of professional market players to satisfy the prerequisites for innovation is the root cause of the objective obstacles that stand in the way of the implementation of blockchain technology within the financial industry. In addition to challenges that are solely technical (such as a lack of dependability, scalability, performance, etc.), there are also implementation hurdles, which are risks that are characteristic of every invention. This involves problems with the law, the wisdom of significant decision-makers concerning innovation, and the risk of judgments in the technological sphere becoming obsolete.

## Blockchain Applications

Blockchain is being investigated extensively across businesses, and numerous use cases are being tested. While the majority of use cases include numerous stakeholders in the value chain or at the industry level, some are particular to a single organization, such as loyalty and Know Your Customer (KYC) processes. Though financial services companies are emerging as the frontrunners in developing Blockchain use cases, other industries are catching up.

## The Sector of Banking and Financial Services

The Financial Services industry is seeing an increase in the number of Blockchain-based use cases that have the potential to improve operational efficiencies and client experience. Financial services majors are experimenting with “Cross-border remittances,” “Post-trade settlements,” “Trade Finance,” and even “Loyalty programs” applications.

Even though financial players were the first to investigate this Distributed Ledger Technology, non-financial players have been paying attention and seeking methods to capitalize on the benefits that Blockchain provides. ## Conclusion

While blockchain began as a proposal to build a virtual currency system (Bitcoin), it has expanded into a broad technology used to record, share, and exploit data with increased convenience, security, and anonymity. Its marketing applications have enormous potential, ranging from improved transaction and customer data recording to better connecting market participants, quantifying intangible assets and entities, and more efficient segmentation and marketing.

Blockchain technology is likely one of the most popular notions today. We have begun to digitize our assets as digitalization has become a common (and even required) phenomenon. The transformation of facts into ones and zeros (data) that can be saved, transferred, and evaluated in a computer context is referred to as digitalization. We have begun to look into ways to safeguard all of the assets that we can digitize (turn into data) using this technology. As a result, blockchain technology was initially designed to ensure the security of digital assets (i.e., cryptocurrency).
