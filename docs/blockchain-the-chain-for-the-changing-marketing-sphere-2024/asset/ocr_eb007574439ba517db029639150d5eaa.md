# Blockchain Technology and Cryptocurrencies

Blockchain technology and cryptocurrencies (like “Bitcoin”) are briefly covered in this section of the FinTech Handbook (a protocol for a peer-to-peer electronic cash system) (Table 1).

Cryptocurrency serves as a means of commerce, a store of value, and a standard of measurement. Cryptocurrencies are utilized as proxies for the worth of other assets despite their low intrinsic value. One of the earliest digital currencies, Bitcoin debuted in 2009. It’s digital money (a means of exchange) that also has the potential to be traded on the open market (how much is it trading for).
