# ## Significant Security Transactions

The environment for mobile faces some trustworthiness challenges, which blockchain might assist in. How to ensure that one will receive whatever one paid for is one of the top worries that internet shoppers have. Who might assist us if the order is not ever arrived? Blockchain can significantly increase the security of digital purchases by providing a level of confidence and transparency. Blockchain operates through an open electronic system that enables users to track each activity on its shared database as it takes place. Without the consent of all other network users, no individual would be able to add or cancel any operations. This implies that since the parameters of a contract are available to all parties participating in the entire process, blockchain tackles among the most common intimacy issues with electronic payments. As a result, both individuals and companies would no more be concerned that one side may break a contract.
