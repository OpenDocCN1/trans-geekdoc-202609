The work meets the needs of the agriculture supply chain business, and the Agri-SCM-BIoT system (Bhat et al., 2022) makes use of multiple blockchains. It is made up of a group of logically distinct blockchains that are integrated into various parts of the agri-supply chain network. Where edge computing nodes and IoT gateways act as member nodes of the blockchain, each local area blockchain segment can have its engagement terms, access control policies, and blockchain ledger. Each edge-level blockchain organization can represent distinct IoT agriculture business verticals. By keeping sensitive information, such as banking information, away from the potential security risk nodes, it can use the multiple-tiered architecture of blockchain in the agri- supply chain to reduce the security attack surface and prevent potential security risks in the supply chain. Private blockchains are used for the deployments of local area blockchains.

For time-sensitive applications and transactions, data is stored on the IoT edge layer, where the edge blockchain tier is in charge of processing tasks that need to be completed closer to it. It avoids the problem of the blockchain’s growing ledger size. Only the most recent transaction hash values are stored on the core blockchain. It also transfers data from the lower edge tiers to the cloud computing layer over the IoT blockchain architecture.
