# Conclusion

The relevance, method, application scenarios, challenges, and mitigation strategies of blockchain technology for the digital transformation of organizations are briefly reviewed in this study. (1) Blockchain technology has a favorable effect on businesses’ digital transformation, particularly through distributed data ledgers, asymmetric cryptography, decentralization, and consensus mechanisms, among other things. (2) The existing literature provides more references on the relationship between blockchain technology and enterprise informatization, but most of the articles stay in the stage of theoretical explanation, and the significance of their relationship has not been tested by empirical studies; and (3) When it comes to counter-matter, there is often little discussion about the technical details and principles behind the mechanism. Additionally, the associated risks and constraints are often overlooked.

How should the legal framework and particular technological requirements for blockchain technology be established? Further research on these problems is required.
