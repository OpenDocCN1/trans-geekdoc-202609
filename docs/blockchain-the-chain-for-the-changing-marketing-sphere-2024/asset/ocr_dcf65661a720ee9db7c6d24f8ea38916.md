# Applications of Blockchain to Address Supply Chain Problems

The SCM’s first significant use of blockchain technology is in smart contracts. The supply chain activities would become more automated, transparent, secure, and efficient as a result. Blockchain technology would have a greater positive influence on international transactions. Second, applications of blockchain technology could enhance information security and sharing. Data storage using blockchain-based systems would guarantee quick access and strong security because altering the data would be impossible without reporting it as a result.

Thirdly, the traceability of cargo and the required waybills may be considerably improved by blockchain technology, which would reduce scams, interruptions, corruption, and related costs. Blockchain technology’s lack of trust also helps to resolve issues with trust that commonly develop between the parties participating in an SCM transaction. In general, blockchain can help to address the key problems the SCM is now having and can boost the competitiveness of the present business models in the sector.

# Transportation and Mobility

During the COVID-19 epidemic, the transportation and mobility industry was severely impacted. It might be one of the industries most impacted by the crisis. This led to a change in mobility behaviors and transportation modes all around the world. Research on passenger mobility in the EU states that an automobile is the most popular form of transportation. However, the market for shared transportation is growing rapidly and might surpass $751 billion by 2030. Some problems and difficulties have also been brought about by the shift in patterns. Many of them crossover with the logistics and supply chain sectors because of their commonalities.
