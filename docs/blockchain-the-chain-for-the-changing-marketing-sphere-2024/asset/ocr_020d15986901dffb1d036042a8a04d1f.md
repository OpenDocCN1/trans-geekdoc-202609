# Significance of the Study

The sharing economy notion has developed over the last ten years, and at the moment we use shared services like Ola, Uber, Oyo, etc. Blockchain technology is already revolutionising society in a few specific cases. Theoretically, buyers should be able to learn about a laptop’s location, current value, and condition if they register it on the Blockchain and choose to sell it on Olx. Imagine that a client purchases your laptop and asks for insurance. In such case, he can quickly share the blockchain data with the insurance company, including “what it is,” “how much it costs”, “what condition it’s in”, etc., and acquire insurance as a result. When items are kept around for a long period without being used, renting them out is less expensive than retaining them. In the coming years, blockchain will increase the efficiency and security of the sharing economy. Although there has been a slow uptake of blockchain technology, numerous countries have gradually adopted regulations that support it and virtual currencies. India is gradually embracing blockchain technology with the introduction of the new programme “National Digital Health Mission (NDHM)” as well as the adoption of a brand-new education policy. A database for quick deployment is what the government is attempting to give.
