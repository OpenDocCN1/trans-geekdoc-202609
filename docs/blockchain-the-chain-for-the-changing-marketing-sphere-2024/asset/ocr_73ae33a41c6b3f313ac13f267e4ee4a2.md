# Increased Trust

As a member of a blockchain-enabled members-only network, you can be confident that you are receiving accurate and timely data, and that your sensitive blockchain records will be shared only with network members to whom you have explicitly granted access.

# Increased Security

Because confirmed transactions are permanently recorded, all network participants must agree on data veracity. No one, not even the system administrator, may erase a transaction.

# More Efficiencies

With a distributed ledger shared by network participants, time-consuming record reconciliations are avoided. A smart contract is a set of rules that may be written on the blockchain and implemented automatically to speed up transactions.
