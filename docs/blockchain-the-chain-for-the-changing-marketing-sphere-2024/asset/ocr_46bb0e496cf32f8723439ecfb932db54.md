These exchanges show the transmission of an asset, which could be something tangible or immaterial. The selection of data, including who, what, when, where, and how much, as well as the state, can be recorded in the data block. The blocks that are placed before and after one another are connected. These blocks build a chain of data as an asset moves from one place to another or as management changes. The blocks guarantee the precise time and order of operations, and they safely link together to prevent any block from changing or a block from being inserted between two known blocks. In an unbreakable chain, such as the blockchain, several operations are forbidden. Every new block strengthens the validity of the previous block and, consequently, of the blockchain as a whole. As a result, the blockchain acquires its essential irreversibility feature and becomes corrupted. # # Digital Marketing

Internet, social networks, portable devices, browsers, as well as other channels are all used in digital marketing to reach out to customers. A specific user group segment is the target of participatory digital marketing. Digital marketing mixes marketing with client feedback or a two-way conversation with the customer (Rabby et al., 2022). It involves paid tweets, mail advertisements, and Google results advertisements and is becoming more popular. Digital marketing can take action on portable devices, via video games, or through smartphone applications, although internet marketing occurs online. In digital marketing, individuals who get relevant advertisements are frequently alluded to as recipients while advertisers are frequently alluded to as suppliers.

Digital marketing is one sector of the economy that is changing quickly. With the advent of blockchain technology, digital marketing strategies will take on a new spin that will promote openness and provide firms with the potential to start to be innovative with their operations. Blockchain technology is meant to be incorporated into marketing as just a mechanism for companies to trace the transfer of data and assure their authenticity. Blockchain technology facilitates monitoring and security, which enables us to respond more rapidly to problems, find precise answers, and give clients a superior internet presence. Blockchain is the digital marketing sector's finest co-conspirator whenever it pertains to information security and openness.
