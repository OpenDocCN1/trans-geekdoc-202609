Technically, digital data is recorded and transmitted via a relatively new technology called the blockchain. Blockchain in digital marketing offers financial and data transfers with accessibility, security, and openness (Erragcha & Babay (2021). Consumers may gain from better digital marketing, while businesses may profit from spending less money on expensive digital advertising efforts. With the use of this recently developed technology, consumers may now own and sell their data directly to marketers and advertising.

Today, the market is driven by data. Blockchain technology can give data owners back control over the advertising data they use (Urban et al., 2019). Direct data interchange between customers and brands is developing as a result. High-quality surveillance used to monitor user activity leads to increased consumer data usability and confidence.

# History and Working of Blockchain

The year 2008 saw the creation of the first decentralized blockchain by Satoshi Nakamoto, a person or set of individuals. Nakamoto greatly improved the architecture by timestamping blocks without requiring them to be validated by a third party. To limit the rate at which blocks are added to the blockchain, he also established a hard level. The next year, Nakamoto put the creation into use as a key component of the cryptocurrency Bitcoin, serving as the channel's main public record for all transactions. The terms ‘block’ and ‘chain’ were used separately in Satoshi Nakamoto’s first paper, but by 2016, the term “blockchain” had become more common. As it happens, each activity is recorded as a “block” of data (Singh, 2021; Singh, Bansal, and Sharma 2022).
