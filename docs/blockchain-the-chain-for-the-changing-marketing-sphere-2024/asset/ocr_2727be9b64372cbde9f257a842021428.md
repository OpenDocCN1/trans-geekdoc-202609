The Indian Government is also actively involved in measures to develop the BC eco-system, and more interfaces and coordination are needed with eco-systems, technologists who had experience in designing and developing BCs in other countries and the applications they have adopted at their end may help us in exchange of protocols and enable re-engineer the process quickly and come out with products in a lesser time than was earlier possible. Hence as with any other technologies, BC will also greatly benefit collaboration and cooperation across borders.

Certain additional points of merit – which may help us to scale up the process faster and permeate across sectors of the BC products could be as under:

+   • Academic – Industry interface has to increase – the Dept of CS and IT in reputed institutions to work closely with industry and try and develop the necessary BC products for sectors.
+   • The government should encourage subsidized funding to BC startups.
+   • Improve and enhance the scale-up process in the BC eco-system.
+   • Collaborate and Cooperative with other scientists, engineers, and technologists and economies in other geographies e.g., Russia, Germany, Ukraine, Romania, Japan, USA, Israel, etc. who have a high talent pool of experts in BC technologies. A symbiotic arrangement with these economies will help enhance the Indian BC ecosystem.
+   • Practical applications to be made a part of course curriculum in the education system and enable people to develop technologies, preferably using Open-source technologies and Open-source applications of AI.
+   • The future of BC lies in the hands of the developer community and therefore closer interaction between them and the IT industry to warranted now more than ever before.
+   • Competitions at the national level similar to hackathons or cyber-attack competitions could be encouraged to enable technologists to come out with stronger BC technologies and cost-effectively enable ready deployment across industries.
