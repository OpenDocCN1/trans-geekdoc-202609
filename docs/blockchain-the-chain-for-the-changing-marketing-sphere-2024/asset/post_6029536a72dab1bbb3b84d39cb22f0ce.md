## Realtime Examples of Companies Using Blockchain Technology

### Banking Sector

In the last few decades as technology has advanced scams, frauds, and threats have also taken advantage of the technology. There have been so many fraudulent activities going on via phone calls, the internet, e-mails, messages, etc. With one touch the hackers try to steal the money of the common public. But bankers provide safety and security to the consumers' financial transactions and interests. They effectively provide a global platform to help clients transfer money as an alternative to physical cash. Bitcoin cryptocurrency has been popular in recent years, it can be replaced in place of currencies that are currently put in use. Despite its increasing popularity, cryptocurrency is currently only accepted by a limited number of businesses. This is mainly due to its complex nature, which has resulted in a lack of basic knowledge among the general public regarding cryptocurrency and its uses. Nevertheless, cryptocurrency has the potential to pave the way for the digitalization of transactions, as demonstrated by Figures 1 and 2 (Howell, 2022).

### IBM

It uses blockchain technology to analyze the demand supply and advertising chain. Their goal is to eliminate unnecessary expenses on third parties or intermediaries. And they also use this technology to keep an eye on the fraudulent activities taking place, like exposing fake data, advertisements, etc., which improves their goodwill (Singh, 2021; Singh, Bansal, and Sharma 2022).

### The Telecom Sectors

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) is slowly integrating blockchain technology to combat the rise of fake messages, spam, hoax calls, and fraudulent activities that have become prevalent since the advent of mobile phones. This technology helps filter out unregistered fake callers and messages. As blockchain technology is often used in conjunction with Artificial Intelligence (AI), it can effectively address the weaknesses of organizations, according to Daley (2022).

### SEBI

The Securities Exchange Board of India (SEBI), in its earlier stages they have faced many fraudulent activities, and third-party intrusion, later they broke down into listed and unlisted companies to avoid these activities which made a huge difference and it brought transparency among the stock and securities. After the incorporation of blockchain technology, they can bring more transparency to their record-keeping, and monitoring of securities.

## Conclusion

Despite the advancements in technology, there are still numerous instances of fraudulent activities taking place online. Unfortunately, many unsuspecting customers fall victim to these scams. It's important to note that technology itself cannot be held responsible for these occurrences. However, companies and banks have taken steps to strengthen their safety and security measures. They are constantly reminding consumers through ads on social media, television, and other platforms not to trust fake ads or messages. Therefore, consumers need to remain vigilant and thoroughly check any offers or benefits provided by these technologies before taking any action.

## Introduction

Information and communication technologies (ICTs) have significantly influenced the restructuring of virtual business models that have occurred in the current digital era. Blockchain was the most revolutionary and turbulent technology ever found, and it has significantly changed business structures and opened new opportunities across a variety of industries. Blocks are lengthening lists of data, a part of distributed ledger technology blockchains are securely associated with one another using encryption. The transfer of data, either way, is typically represented as a Merkle tree, where data nodes are represented by leaves, and each block also includes a date and the previous block's cryptographic hash. The timestamp proves that the transaction data was there now the block was generated. Each block references the preceding ones because each block includes information about the block preceding it. As a result, the blocks function as a chain. Blockchain transactions are therefore irreversible once they are recorded because doing so would require undoing all blocks after it.
