# Introduction

In today’s era of Web 3.0 where users retrieve and share information through personalized searches; and results are based on the user’s behavior learned by the browser. Hence, Web 3.0 has upgraded the traditional internet to a more open, connected, and intelligent internet.

Web 3.0 has made the Internet artificially intelligent, which means, unlike Web 2.0 internet doesn’t give search results that a business wants to showcase to the customers, but it shows the result that the customer wants from a business. The best example is Google search, earlier, based on our keywords Google used to show the best relevant results. However, now Google considers our - past keywords, click-on search results, preferences, field of interest, influencers, online connections, how we connect with others, and more. And based on the compiled information, Google shows the most accurate results.

Social media platforms are websites and mobile applications-based online tools that enable people to create & share content (like data, pictures, audio, and videos) quickly and interact with new people in real time. It has made communication easier than ever before.

According to McKinsey 2018, Indians spend 17 hours per week on social media websites like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, and others; which is the highest in the world. As per a report by Statista 2017, because of growing digitalization, access to cheap Android smartphones, and a 4G network, more than 462 million people across India are using the internet. And about 250 million, of these, are active social media users. Facebook and YouTube accounted for the largest penetration, both at 30 percent each. Among all, Facebook and TikTok have more than 200 million active users each in India.

With such a big number, social media is indeed a rich source of content, for individuals and communities. This entices a huge surge of brands from FMCG, Electronics, Mobile handsets, FMCG, Grooming. Handicrafts, and the Entertainment Industry to social media for promoting their products and services.
