# **Blockchain Technology in the Entertainment Industry**

Through distributed ledger technology and smart contracts, blockchain technology has already been used to share material more fairly. As a result, the revenue can be automatically allocated under the predetermined contractual arrangement, which is advantageous to all parties. In 2021, funding for blockchain companies in the film and television industries increased by 713% year over year, totaling $25.2 billion. The only expected growth is in the north.

Decentralized media libraries powered by blockchain technology have also begun to emerge, and in recent years, many blockchain startups and enterprises have been bought by bigger corporations. For instance, the blockchain startup Media chain was purchased by Spotify. The gaming industry could benefit from more transparent competition and prize distribution thanks to blockchain technology. The use of cryptocurrency has increased in the betting industry as well. On the other hand, the development of NFT and Metaverse concepts as well as associated digital assets has been significantly aided by blockchain.

# **Information and Communication Technology (ITC)**

The introduction of ICT has altered all facets of business and society, as has been said countless times, but we feel compelled to repeat it. By the end of 2022, the ICT market is expected to reach a value of $5.5 trillion worldwide. Accelerated ICT adoption during the COVID-19 pandemic was observed in multiple sectors, and this helped to fuel trends like remote work that are now commonplace across many businesses. 5G, web 3.0, AI, and the metaverse have opened exciting new possibilities, but they have also prompted many concerns and issues. The roadmaps for digital transformation that businesses and government organizations have presented will decide how the economy and society advance and change over the next ten years.
