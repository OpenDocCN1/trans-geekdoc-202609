With the advent of the internet, consumers’ interests are increasing in purchasing products via online social media platforms. It also helps to connect domestic as well as international business. It helps in marketing communication as social media platforms reduce the gap between domestic and international business communication (Costa e Silva and Elo, 2018).

Market research reports suggested that companies use social media platforms for building good customer relationships. As for any company, it is important to build brand image and social media platforms are an important part of the promotion mix of business.

There has been a shift in how people communicate with each other and with businesses as social networking sites act as a platform for targeting customers and transforming the market into a technology platform with content that is according to users (Lorenzo-Romero et al., 2014). To promote brands and for sharing information regarding brand content social media channels are used and results indicate that the inclusion of corporate brand names, functional and emotional appeals, and information search cues has increased the popularity of brand content (Swani et al., 2017).

It has been identified that there are three factors for a company or brand to use social media platforms and these are; to share information with other planners easily and quickly through chat or discussion boards, to communicate queries and solutions to problems and opinions with other meeting planners and to get feedback from people after the meeting (Lee, Tyrrell and Erdem, 2018).

It was also stated that there was no direct relation between the size of the company and their frequency to use social media as a platform but at the same time reasons for using social media as a platform are different because small and medium size firms mainly use it for customer services while large size company uses it for brand building (Olivas-Lujan et al., 2013).

The business focuses on increasing its business exposure and brand awareness through the use of social media platforms and creates a large pool of client and provide proper access to employees of the organization (Heetal, 2017; Wizigetal, 2012).

Information communication is an important aspect of making customers satisfied. It also gave importance to this issue by highlighting the expectation of consumers through continuous availability of information and timeliness to be an important aspect when securing customer satisfaction.
