## Big Data Analytics : Case Study 1

Speaking of big data analytics, it helps a company to understand the behaviors and demands of its customers. It gradually helps the company to make adequate changes in their services to promote their brands even further. Analytics tools are highly significant since they help businesses to collect information regarding their real-time audiences. The number of users opting for online means is on a hike at the moment and hence is significantly increasing. Big Data Analytics helps a company to explore more about its audience and ensures an opportunity to segment its markets accordingly. It plays a significant role in segmentation since with the data that are collected, the demands of the audiences can be found out and hence the company might make necessary changes to a particular offer and ensure that it is the best fit for a particular segmentation of audience that the company has chosen. Big Data Analytics can be used as a means to track the traffic online thereby getting enough reach to sing out to the audience segments. The collected data can further be used as insights that will help the company in the long run. Data Analytics is highly useful as it helps business companies to adequately measure the effectiveness of their business and marketing plans.

The tools of segmentation and web analytics play a significant role in analyzing the big data that is collected. Speaking of Starbucks, they use their online website as a tool for analytics. They keep every single data of their customers starting from what they order frequently to what they might like in the future. The data that are collected and stored as insights are pretty large and hence need to be analyzed perfectly. It further helps Starbucks in building tools for the making of hypotheses, and identifying patterns of what their segmented audience might like thereby extracting the most valuable insights from the big data. It eventually helps Starbucks to understand the demographics of their audience thereby providing them with an opportunity of segmenting their customer base on the grounds of data insights. Big data Analytics further help Starbucks to identify the metrics to look out for and the customer properties to consider as a basis upon which they might segment their audience groups. Moreover, it portrays a clear view of what should be the ideal differentiation models to use while segmenting the base. It hence helps in the marketing optimizations of Starbucks by providing them with the idea of what could be the high-converting or best-performing audience segments.

Speaking of customer satisfaction, the Big Data Analytics tools used by Starbucks help them to build an active and loyal customer base who believe in their company. Starbucks provides two types of views in their business: qualitative and quantitative. It helps them to arrange their big data thereby analyzing them perfectly to provide their best to the audiences. This eventually creates a happy and loyal customer base.

Identification of new spots for growth and prosperity is understood by business organizations through the assessment of Big Data Analytics. Smarter and better businesses are to be conducted by taking the opportunities by assessing Big Data Analytics (Ghani et al., 2019; Gupta et al. 2023). The analysis of Big Data and the assessment of them by an organization, specifically, a business organization, helps them to gain valuable insights and pieces of data from their real-time customers. This process of assessment and undertaken, then this method helps business organizations to tend to understand the behavioral changes and the shifting diverse tastes of their customers. Big Data Analytics provide such valuable insights to business organizations and these pieces of data help them to improve their services and production efficiency towards their customers. This would eventually help a business organization or business enterprise to retain their existing customers and further to attract new customers. Customer segmentation is specifically done by an organization after extracting the data by assessing Big Data Analytics and segmenting their customer bases accordingly for better effectiveness and efficiency. After the process of customer segmentation comes the next step of customer satisfaction, which is one of the most crucial steps for an organization to retain and attract more customers. Customer satisfaction is also achieved by these business organizations and enterprises by assessing and extracting data about their customers from Big Data Analytics. Analyzing Big Data gives them information about how to improve and focus on segments for which the existing customers could be made happy.
