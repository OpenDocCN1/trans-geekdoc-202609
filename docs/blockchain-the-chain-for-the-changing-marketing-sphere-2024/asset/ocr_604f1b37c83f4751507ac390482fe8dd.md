# Conclusion

Despite the advancements in technology, there are still numerous instances of fraudulent activities taking place online. Unfortunately, many unsuspecting customers fall victim to these scams. It's important to note that technology itself cannot be held responsible for these occurrences. However, companies and banks have taken steps to strengthen their safety and security measures. They are constantly reminding consumers through ads on social media, television, and other platforms not to trust fake ads or messages. Therefore, consumers need to remain vigilant and thoroughly check any offers or benefits provided by these technologies before taking any action.
