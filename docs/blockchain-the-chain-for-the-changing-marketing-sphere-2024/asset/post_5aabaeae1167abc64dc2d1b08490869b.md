After going through a review of the literature, the researcher proposed the following hypothesis

- *H01: There is no significant impact of social media on business operations.*
- *H02: There is no significant impact of age on using social media for business.*

## Research Methodology

For examining the hypothesis proposed, a survey questionnaire was developed. The data were collected from 70 online customers who are using online social media for business purposes by using stratified random sampling in which strata were formed according to the age group of respondents. In total 100 questionnaires were distributed directly to the consumers. A total of 70 usable surveys were collected from consumers who are using online social media for business purposes in Haridwar. The consumers, who are not using the online social media platform for business purposes, were excluded from the study. To reduce response bias, users were asked not to reveal their names in the survey.

Based on these surveys, the following research question has been set to present the impact of social media on businesses.

- *Research Question 1: Does presence on social media impact a business? If yes, how much it contributes to total sales?*
- *Research Question 2: What benefits does a business derive from social media platforms?*
- *Research Question 3: Which social medium is best for doing business?*
