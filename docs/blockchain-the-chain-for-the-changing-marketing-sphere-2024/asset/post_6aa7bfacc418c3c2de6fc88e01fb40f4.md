## Cybersecurity

According to estimates, each person created roughly 1.7 MB of data every second in 2020. Every single day, that amounts to more than 2.5 quintillion bytes of data. We have a lot of data and information on hand, making a security breach possible. According to the estimate, among the significant sources of breaches include human mistakes (95%), hacking (45%), phishing (22%), and malware (17%). Businesses spend more than $75 billion each year trying to recover from ransomware attacks.

### Problems in the Cybersecurity Sector

The Internet and digital technology are crucial to today’s enterprises. On the one hand, businesses and consumers benefit from the faster internet access brought on by the rollout of 5G. On the other hand, the increasing rates bring with them more serious cybersecurity risks. They give many concerning pieces of evidence in a recent edition of Oxford University Press’ Journal of Cybersecurity. Businesses face many cybersecurity issues, including the failure to acknowledge threats, unaware employees, security problems caused by remote work or operator mistakes, targeted cybercrime, the slow introduction of security fixes, and a lack of attention to security problems because they are seen as IT problems rather than financial ones.

It’s crucial to remember that businesses other than SMEs, which invest a lot of money in defense against assaults, are also concerned about cybersecurity. In October 2016, for instance, a huge attack on Dyn, one of the largest DNS (domain name service) providers, caused the interruption of important online businesses including Spotify, Netflix, and Twitter. A similar attack on Amazon Web Services occurred in February 2020. Blockchain technology has solutions that would improve cybersecurity in such a situation.

### Blockchain Technology in Cybersecurity

Decentralization and a consensus-driven, trustless framework, two essential characteristics of blockchain technology, make it inherently secure, watchful, and resistant to breaches. Blockchain uses automated data storage to solve the main cyber security issue, human mistakes. Blockchain system nodes automatically compare the data and warn of any information that is falsified.

Since data cannot be altered, blockchain-based solutions are secure. The hackers have an edge because they typically use their distribution with caution.

It is possible to beat hackers at their own game by implementing blockchain technology and decentralizing digital assets and security infrastructure. Decentralizing DNS records, for instance, can aid in thwarting DDoS assaults. Blockchain technology enables the verification of security patches, firmware updates, and installers before their execution. This would eliminate any malware alterations or other dangers that unintentionally entered the protection system. Additionally, using encryption and blockchain integration to confirm and verify the system access procedure would prevent any unwanted intrusion. DAC. ## Supply Chain Management (SCM)

SCM is quite complex and involves a lot of data. It’s mostly because paper-based record-keeping is still widely used, notably in the marine industry, which makes up a sizable portion of the global supply chain. There are still significant digital transformation efforts needed for the supply chain management sector. The COVID-19 pandemic’s recent supply chain interruptions have cost businesses in the sector a lot of money. However, it has also presented itself as a major potential, as noted by several international corporations (McKinsey, Deloitte, EY).

### Serious Issues with Supply Chain Management Right Now

Currently, there are significant data storage and exchange bottlenecks in supply chain management (SCM). In particular, the strict nature of the personnel involved in the operational process and the smaller suppliers in the corporate structure. Security is cited as the main reason for not going towards digitalization. It results in significant losses for many industry stakeholders. Delays in shipments, lost paper documents, corruption, and so on are a few of the key obstacles that must be removed.

Cybersecurity, increasing tariffs or transaction fees, inflation, sluggish cash flow, and other key SCM challenges have lately come to light. The same research also emphasizes the increased requirement for management and supervision over the alteration in supply as well as the elimination of redundancy.

However, blockchain technology is the least adopted of all the possible alternatives when it comes to overcoming problems. It suggests a lack of knowledge about the advantages of blockchain technology applications in SCM.

### Applications of Blockchain to Address Supply Chain Problems

The SCM’s first significant use of blockchain technology is in smart contracts. The supply chain activities would become more automated, transparent, secure, and efficient as a result. Blockchain technology would have a greater positive influence on international transactions. Second, applications of blockchain technology could enhance information security and sharing. Data storage using blockchain-based systems would guarantee quick access and strong security because altering the data would be impossible without reporting it as a result.

Thirdly, the traceability of cargo and the required waybills may be considerably improved by blockchain technology, which would reduce scams, interruptions, corruption, and related costs. Blockchain technology’s lack of trust also helps to resolve issues with trust that commonly develop between the parties participating in an SCM transaction. In general, blockchain can help to address the key problems the SCM is now having and can boost the competitiveness of the present business models in the sector.

## Transportation and Mobility

During the COVID-19 epidemic, the transportation and mobility industry was severely impacted. It might be one of the industries most impacted by the crisis. This led to a change in mobility behaviors and transportation modes all around the world. Research on passenger mobility in the EU states that an automobile is the most popular form of transportation. However, the market for shared transportation is growing rapidly and might surpass $751 billion by 2030. Some problems and difficulties have also been brought about by the shift in patterns. Many of them crossover with the logistics and supply chain sectors because of their commonalities.

### Blockchain Can Help with Today’s Transportation and Mobility Problems

Blockchain can help with today's transportation and mobility problems. Autonomous mobility is growing in popularity and necessitates networked vehicle-to-vehicle communication. This brings up the security issue and the requirement to protect the vehicles. Encryption based on blockchain technology can help protect these. It would be quite difficult to hack. The need to track car mileage is a pressing one. There have been several reports of odometer tampering. This problem can be resolved by smart odometers built on blockchain and connected to GPS. This problem could be solved by using GPS data saved on a blockchain’s distributed network for tamper-proof data storage.

Individuals to interface (I2I) directly assist the third party is necessary to support the growth of shared mobility. Blockchain-based solutions can make it easier for such an I2I interaction to take place between such linked devices or automobiles. Finally, BC technology has a significant role to play in terms of vehicle insurance, as was mentioned above in the insurance section. It would be helpful to have a tamper-proof odometer in addition to an information system for cars that are based on a blockchain network.
