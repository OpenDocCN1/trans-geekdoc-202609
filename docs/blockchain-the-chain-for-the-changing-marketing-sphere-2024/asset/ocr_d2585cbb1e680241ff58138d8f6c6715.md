# Introduction

In the current environment, it is essential to have an understanding of blockchain technology and the value it provides to successfully execute Industry 4.0. Some industries could potentially benefit from blockchain technology, such as applications for financial transactions, in which blockchains can give confidence. Problems with fiat currencies and other foreign currencies are not taken into consideration, and regulated supply transactions are permitted. When it comes to Industry 4.0, both the product itself and the identification element of its assembly can be linked to other sections of the Blockchain. It serves as a reminder of the situations in which having the capacity to identify goods with the problem could be advantageous. In this case, blockchain technology will be used to safeguard all of the information about a product, including its sub-assemblies, parts, sales routes, and so on. It lowers the costs involved and the amount of time the supply chain is disrupted for recovery at any point. The information that was recently obtained by cameras and sensors has the potential to be utilized in the construction of the Blockchain network. It grants us access to more information than a single person could amass in such a short amount of time (Chang et al., 2020; Tang & Veelenturf, 2019; Ferreira et al., 2020).
