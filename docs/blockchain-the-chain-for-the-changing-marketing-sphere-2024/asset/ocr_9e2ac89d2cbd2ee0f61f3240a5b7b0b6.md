# Abstract

Employees must be the most essential assets of a company. However, for a company to achieve success in the long run, it is important to sustain customers and provide them with the best services/products based on their demands. This is where customer segmentation comes in. For a company to deliver its best, the target audience must be selected and divided into groups. This practice is known as customer segmentation. In this paper, various advantages of customer segmentation have been discussed along with certain strategies to make it better. Lastly, a relationship between customer segmentation and satisfaction has been established.
