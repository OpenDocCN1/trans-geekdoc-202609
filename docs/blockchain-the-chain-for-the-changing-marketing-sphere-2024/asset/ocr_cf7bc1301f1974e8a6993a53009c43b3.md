# Abstract

Blockchain technology is a disruptive breakthrough built on information and computing technology that is still growing very quickly. This fundamental disruptive technology enables a shared digital history and record updating between parties who may not necessarily trust each other while avoiding the need for a central trusted agent for coordination. From its beginnings in cryptocurrency, blockchain is already finding use cases across a variety of industries, such as banking, law, education, healthcare, supply chains, and manufacturing. More than ever, marketers must learn how to use new technology and deploy them quickly.

However, despite the advent of blockchain technology more than ten years ago, academic marketing researchers have paid very little attention. Because of the distinctive characteristics of blockchain—transparency, decentralization, and immutability—it can foster a safer, customer-focused, and open marketplace for both individuals and companies.
