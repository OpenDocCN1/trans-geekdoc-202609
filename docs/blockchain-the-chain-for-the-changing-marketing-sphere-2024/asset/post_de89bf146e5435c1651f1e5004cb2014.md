## The Application Scenarios of Blockchain in the Digital Transformation of Enterprises

As well as applications in safe storage, access control, and auditing in the fields of intelligent medicine, intelligent transportation, the smart city, and supply chain management, Yang et al. (2020) analyzed the typical application framework of blockchain technology in data sharing scenarios. By establishing a technical inspection model and applying blockchain technology to the digital landscape of the marine industry, Yang (2019) concluded that the use of and further advancement of blockchain technology can have a significant influence on sectors associated with shipping. Emphasizing the need to further investigate the issue of data security, especially data security in the manufacturing industry, Pal (2021) noted that blockchain technology can make the processing of transactional data in the manufacturing field more intelligent and flexible through appropriate fusion with the Internet of Things technology. According to Lu et al. (2021), the “blockchain+ agricultural internet of things” model, “blockchain+ agricultural supply chain” model, “blockchain+ agricultural data sharing model,” etc., are examples of applications of blockchain technology in agricultural data management. Zhang (2020) looked at how blockchain technology may be used for several business and trade-related issues, including letters of credit, cross-border payment, supply chain financing, digital logistics, commodities traceability, and digital customs and management.

By creating an asymmetric consortium blockchain system, Fu et al. (2021) facilitated the effective and timely execution of cross-border transactions. They created a new smart contract to lessen each node’s opportunity loss and improved the method for distributing profits. They then used the system in actual cross-border transaction situations in Shenzhen and Hong Kong to confirm its efficacy and viability. According to Zhang et al. (2019), Country Garden Holdings and Ant Financial should work together to develop application scenarios such as a blockchain-based e-invoicing platform, a platform for tracking agricultural products, and a platform for exchanging interest and credit rights. According to Nie et al. (2017), the issue of Internet financial risk control may be solved by advancing blockchain technology to the “dual-channel” credit reference technology of “decentralized+ centralized” based on big data. According to Guo (2019), the Chinese insurance sector has entered an age dominated by digital technologies including big data and blockchain. The digital transformation of insurance firms has the potential to improve the synergy between them and linked businesses, spur innovation in insurance products, optimize customer experience scenarios, and change the way insurance organizations operate. Large-scale banks like J.P. Morgan, HSBC, and Bank of America, among others, have successfully used blockchain technology, and now they are looking for technical innovation. This technology demonstrated great efficacy, economy, and directness and also examined the method by which banks used blockchain technology to improve transaction security and banking safety. The uses of blockchain technology in commercial banks, supply chain finance, cross-border payments, risk management, and the capital market were enumerated. Chen et al. (2021) developed an e-commerce payment platform security algorithm, improved the standard payment procedure, and used blockchain finance to optimize the safe payment method (Singh, 2021; Singh, Bansal, and Sharma 2022).

## Constraints on the Application of Blockchain in the Digital Transformation of Enterprises

CAICT (2021) noted that the complexity of cross-border trade financial business itself can bring, including the enforceability of smart contracts, standardization, and security of technology, etc., is due to the delay in the unveiling of regulatory laws on blockchain technology and the absence of a unified technical standard for blockchain applications. It was required to create a framework for blockchain regulation and standardize the technological requirements. The benefits of blockchain technology in reducing transaction costs, reducing counterparty risk, and decentralizing contract enforcement, among other things. It also had issues with safety, technological dangers, and risks from governmental regulations. Blockchain technology, according to Zhang et al. (2020), has certain flaws, including tamper-proofness, a poor fault tolerance rate, a low consensus efficiency, and a small data capacity, which limit the effectiveness of an enterprise’s digital transformation. According to Gong et al. (2021), blockchain technology is still in its developmental stage, with limited empirical research and data available. More research is needed to assess its impact on marketing and business administration, as well as to address challenges such as scalability, privacy and security concerns, software defects, cyber-attacks, high investment costs, and the absence of legal regulations. Given its relative youth and immaturity, many experts stress the importance of establishing an international technical standard for blockchain technology.

## Countermeasures

To increase the data transparency of SMEs in logistics, money flow, and information flow, Lu et al. (2021) proposed applying the blockchain-distributed ledger to all nodes of company production and operation and adopting “blockchain+ credit reference” technology. According to Yang et al. (2020), building an intellectual property data platform, a trading platform, and a valuation platform were all effective ways to boost the value of SMEs. These steps included determining the developmental stage of businesses using the “blockchain+ big data” model, assessing their needs for financing and financial stability, and identifying the SMEs’ developmental stage. Tang (2018) used the digital transformation of SMEs in Germany as an example to suggest that the nation should create a unified framework for digital transformation, develop a mechanism for Internet regulation, strengthen trust and security in data processing, place a premium on and increase investment in R&D, and offer opportunities for vocational training. Zhang (2021) asserts that SMEs need to strengthen their capabilities, nurture their talents in digital management, technology, and business, and use benchmark enterprises to guide their use of key industry businesses as a point of breakthrough for digital transformation. They should also develop an open and shared industrial Internet platform.

## Conclusion

The relevance, method, application scenarios, challenges, and mitigation strategies of blockchain technology for the digital transformation of organizations are briefly reviewed in this study. (1) Blockchain technology has a favorable effect on businesses’ digital transformation, particularly through distributed data ledgers, asymmetric cryptography, decentralization, and consensus mechanisms, among other things. (2) The existing literature provides more references on the relationship between blockchain technology and enterprise informatization, but most of the articles stay in the stage of theoretical explanation, and the significance of their relationship has not been tested by empirical studies; and (3) When it comes to counter-matter, there is often little discussion about the technical details and principles behind the mechanism. Additionally, the associated risks and constraints are often overlooked.

According to recent studies, the following matters require more attention:

- 1) The security of citizens’ information throughout the digital transformation of businesses needs to be given top priority in the “Dazhizhiyun” age. Enterprises’ use of data not only increases marketing precision but also exposes customer privacy. Can blockchain be enhanced in terms of enhancing data security and standardizing technology? How should the legal framework and particular technological requirements for blockchain technology be established? Further research on these problems is required.

- 2) More research is needed to confirm how blockchain impacts businesses during their digital transformation. It is important to understand that this transformation is an ongoing process, and it is worth investigating whether blockchain plays a consistent role at various stages of the digital revolution. Empirical data can help shed light on these questions and guide future research.
