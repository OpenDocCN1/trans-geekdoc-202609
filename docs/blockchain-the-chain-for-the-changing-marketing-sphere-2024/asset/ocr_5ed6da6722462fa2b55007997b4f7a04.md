A lightweight privacy-preserving mechanism for a healthcare blockchain system is included in the framework (Fu, Wang, & Cai, 2020). To encrypt the original EMRs, an interleaving encoder is utilized. It was mostly made up of two main parts: recovery and use of EMRs and the creation and storage of shares of EMRs (Figure 12). The primary participants in the initial EMRs are the patient and medical professional. An authorized data user must first search the EMR’s index on the blockchain to locate all the nodes that store the EMR’s shares before attempting to explore an EMR. The interleaving decoder will decode the recovered subsections of the EMR after the subsections of the EMR have been reconstructed, and the original EMR R will be obtained. After that, authorized customers can process these recovered EMRs for various purposes. Utilizing the indexes of the stored EMR shares, blocks that are then chained together to form a blockchain is created. An EMR can be recovered by authorized data users or institutions requesting at least t shares from the blockchain nodes.
