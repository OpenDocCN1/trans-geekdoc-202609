# # Changing the Phase of Economy and Industries through Blockchain

Blockchain’s transparent consensus mechanism enables users to confirm the legitimacy of transactions and the changes or additions made to data during the process. Although the financial industry was the primary emphasis of Blockchain technology, manufacturing, agriculture, healthcare, supply chains, and logistics are showing much more promise. Blockchain technology supports new organizational structures, labor processes, and business models. Hierarchical organizations are giving way to self-organizing economies as the paradigm. Blockchain has created a wide range of new opportunities for the convenient and reliable direct transfer of wealth between participants. Workflow execution will be utilized with smart contracts to automate regulatory workflows. It will be simpler than ever to report and monitor the necessary data, verify compliance, and complete approval processes.

Blockchain technology is now appearing in all spheres of society and business. Blockchain, for instance, can streamline company procedures while producing secure, reliable records of agreements and transactions in the financial sector. With the automated execution of real-time financial transactions, a worldwide consortium of over 80 institutional players has been created to develop proof-of-concept and prototype financial systems. Additionally, a Blockchain-enabled ecosystem can make it possible for an end-to-end service to reduce supply chain disruptions caused by the appearance of fraudulent goods, such as in the case of food supply chains. By combining supply chain management with an Internet of Things (IoT) solution that simplifies machine-to-machine communication, value can be moved through the process quickly and safely.
