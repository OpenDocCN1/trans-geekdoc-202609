## Conclusion

Social media platforms act as a host for promoting business and are useful in various fields of business-like food, welfare, and lifestyle. For creating brand value an entrepreneur uses social media platforms to keep in touch with customers and remind customers through flash-up advertisements.

Social media platforms have successfully served their entrepreneur in reaching a large number of customers. It enables entrepreneurs to share content that customers want to know about products or services and also convey the message they want their potential customers to know.

As well-structured content act as a promotional message for the business and builds a brand image for the same so social media act as a bridge to convey it.

Businesses that are continuously promoting their products and services online with good content and message have a better image in the eyes of customers and it helps them to increase the visibility, and viability of the product.

As in the era of Web 3.0 online presence has become vital for business today. Social media has improved delivery structure and small leaders are using such media to improve their business promotional techniques and sustain in this competitive era.

The researcher here also identifies that social media platforms are a good medium for doing business. There are several types of platforms through which people can conduct their business operations successfully such as Unacademy, YouTube channel, Meesho app, Facebook, WhatsApp, and many more. Through these platforms, they can do different types of business such as providing education, commercial purpose, and technological sectors.

So our H01 is rejected because there is a significant impact of social media for operating a business whereas H02 is accepted as there is no significant impact of age on using social media platforms for business as the different age groups of person use social for conducting their business and majority of the person earn 30-50% profit via a social media platform.
