# Abstract

E-commerce refers to the buying and selling of goods and services, and the transfer of funds or data via computer networks. There are different types of e-commerce transactions, including business-to-consumer, business-to-business, consumer-to-business, and consumer-to-consumer. E-commerce has been around since the early days of computer technology, but it has become more prevalent in the last two decades with the advent of new technologies. One popular form of e-commerce is online retail shopping, or “e-tail,” which allows customers to purchase everyday items online.

This chapter provides an overview of blockchain technology, including how it works and its various applications. With real-life examples, the chapter explores the usefulness of blockchain technology for both organizations and the general public.
