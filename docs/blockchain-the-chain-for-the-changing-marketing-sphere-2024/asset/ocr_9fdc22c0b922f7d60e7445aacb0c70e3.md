# Big Data Analytics : Case Study 2

Business organizations get huge assistance from the use of Big Data Analytics and assessing the data stored in them, to undertake certain crucial methods within the business organization. Identification of new spots for growth and prosperity is understood by business organizations through the assessment of Big Data Analytics. Smarter and better businesses are to be conducted by taking the opportunities by assessing Big Data Analytics (Ghani et al., 2019; Gupta et al. 2023). The analysis of Big Data and the assessment of them by an organization, specifically, a business organization, helps them to gain valuable insights and pieces of data from their real-time customers. This process of assessment and undertaken, then this method helps business organizations to tend to understand the behavioral changes and the shifting diverse tastes of their customers. Big Data Analytics provide such valuable insights to business organizations and these pieces of data help them to improve their services and production efficiency towards their customers. This would eventually help a business organization or business enterprise to retain their existing customers and further to attract new customers. Customer segmentation is specifically done by an organization after extracting the data by assessing Big Data Analytics and segmenting their customer bases accordingly for better effectiveness and efficiency. After the process of customer segmentation comes the next step of customer satisfaction, which is one of the most crucial steps for an organization to retain and attract more customers. Customer satisfaction is also achieved by these business organizations and enterprises by assessing and extracting data about their customers from Big Data Analytics. Analyzing Big Data gives them information about how to improve and focus on segments for which the existing customers could be made happy.
