Chen et al., (2019) explored the application of Fabric technology in certain business sectors by fusing consortium blockchain technology with hyper-ledger fabric and using the forfeiting industry as an example. They also introduced a novel blockchain information-sharing scheme that incorporated homomorphic encryption to address security concerns. Chen et al., (2021) noted that cross-border blockchain service platforms can increase payment efficiency by providing equal access to information for all users in the supply chain, which eliminates the need for banks to rely on intermediary companies to obtain actual information and saves time, money, and human resources. These platforms share data with banks and other financial institutions, including enterprise loan information, capital flow information, credit information, and logistics information, sourced from various government departments such as the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, tax authorities, and logistics offices.

# The Application Scenarios of Blockchain in the Digital Transformation of Enterprises

As well as applications in safe storage, access control, and auditing in the fields of intelligent medicine, intelligent transportation, the smart city, and supply chain management, Yang et al., (2020) analyzed the typical application framework of blockchain technology in data sharing scenarios. By establishing a technical inspection model and applying blockchain technology to the digital landscape of the marine industry, Yang (2019) concluded that the use of and further advancement of blockchain technology can have a significant influence on sectors associated with shipping. Emphasizing the need to further investigate the issue of data security, especially data security in the manufacturing industry, Pal (2021) noted that blockchain technology can make the processing of transactional data in the manufacturing field more intelligent and flexible through appropriate fusion with the Internet of Things technology.
