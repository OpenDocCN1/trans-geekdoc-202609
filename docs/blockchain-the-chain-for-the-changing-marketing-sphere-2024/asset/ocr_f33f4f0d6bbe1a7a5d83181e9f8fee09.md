# Abstract

The application of blockchain-based tech to internet advertising is currently in its inception. As a result of new technological developments, the ongoing relationship between a brand and its customers has altered, making it possible to target a larger but more specialized audience to increase consumer confidence and brand recognition. Blockchain-based technology now extends beyond simply tracking digital money. Telecommunication companies, shipping companies, retailers, care providers, and media companies all regularly use blockchain for a wide range of jobs. Automation has already had a huge impact on how firms currently run. However, it has the power to change how customers use their gadgets and make purchases on e-commerce websites. The proposed chapter will go through potential uses of blockchain in marketing as well as the benefits and drawbacks of putting this ground-breaking technology into practice. The Internet currently enables marketing organizations to create virtual platforms, contemporary trade platforms, and market appetite. Blockchain terminal offers helpful e-commerce platforms, lower transaction processing costs, a perjury system, an effective paying structure, and delegated administration to challenge the hegemony of enormous organizations.
