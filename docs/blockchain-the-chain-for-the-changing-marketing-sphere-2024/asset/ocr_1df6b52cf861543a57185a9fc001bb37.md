# Abstract

The purpose of this paper is to investigate the impact of social media on businesses. Specifically, it aims to explore the relationship between social media and the operation of business giving light to the age group of people. The data were collected from 70 online customers who are using online social media for business purposes by using stratified random sampling. It has been identified that there is an impact of using social media platforms for operating a business whereas the age factor does not impact the usage of social media platforms. As today different age group of persons operates their business operations on social media. The findings of this research help the entrepreneurs to develop a deeper insight into product marketing and better operation of business using social media platforms.
