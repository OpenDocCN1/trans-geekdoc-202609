# Introduction

The process by which a business enterprise, company, or organization divides its customer bases into similar, relevant, and different groups is known as the method of customer segmentation. The division of such measures of the customer groups is done by the business organizations to put more emphasis and focus on their customer bases as well as their customers. Business organizations present all over the world, have an organizational goal of expanding their business and increasing their sales. To do so or attain their organizational goals, they have to know the tastes and demands of their customers and assess what is needed by their various customer bases. The bigger the business enterprise, company, or business organization would be, the bigger would its business bases, and the bigger would be the emphasis and assessment of its customer demands. In order to know the customer bases, business organizations assess and analyze the preferences, tastes, and various demands of their customers. One of the first principles and strategies of a business company or business organization is to identify their customers and put more focus on attaining them. Customer segmentation is done by using the tactic or strategy of customer segmentation management. This method helps business companies and business organizations to divide their customers into separate distinctive groups. The division is done by the business organizations based on the similar and relevant tastes and demands of their customers and segments them into similar groups. The marketing departments and the executive departments of a business company or business organization implement the process of customer segmentation within their business organization. These departments have the authority of implementing customer segmentation because eventually, it helps the business organizations to assess their customer behaviors and get information about who is buying or using their products and services.
