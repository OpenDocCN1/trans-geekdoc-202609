## Blockchain and the Digital Transformation of Businesses: A Review of the Literature and Future Research

Jaskiran Kaur*

Lovely Professional University, Phagwara, Punjab, India

### Abstract

The digital economy has grown rapidly in the era of Dazhiyiyun (big data, artificial intelligence, mobile Internet, and cloud computing), and based on technical supremacy, the digital transformation of firms has become a hot subject in the sector. The significance, mechanism, application scenarios, restrictions, and countermeasures of blockchain technology for the digital transformation of organizations are picked out from the literature on the digital transformation of enterprises in this study. The majority of the literature suggests that blockchain technology aids in the digital transformation of businesses, but there is a dearth of evidence and actual studies to back this up. It is also discovered that there is no systematic theoretical framework and experience summary, the dynamics of the digital transformation of organizations are not taken into consideration, and the evolution of firms after digital transformation is understudied.

### Introduction

Big data, artificial intelligence, cloud computing, blockchain, and other technologies are developing quickly, and this is reflected in the rapid pace, broad reach, and profound effect of India's digital economy. According to the White Paper on the Development of the Chinese Digital Economy in 2021, the overall size of the Chinese digital economy in 2020 was 39.2 trillion yuan or 38.6% of GDP, and it was taking up more and more space in the country's economy. The 14th Five-Year Plan for the Development of the Digital Economy also included proposals to implement the comprehensive “Shangyun Yongshu Fuzhi” service (i.e., put on the cloud, use data, and render intelligence), speed up enterprise digital transformation and upgrade, and make it easier to rationally apply blockchain and other technologies in banking, securities, insurance, and other fields. Since 2017, Postal Savings Bank, Merchants Bank, Ant Financial, Tencent, and other businesses have also used blockchain technology in asset custody, cross-border RMB clearing, letter of credit settlement, SME finance, and other areas. Thus, enabling businesses to undergo a digital transformation through technology has become a general trend and hot topic in the sector. How can blockchain technology aid businesses in becoming digital? What are the limitations of the transformation process? Are there viable alternatives for digital transformation? These are all crucial issues that require resolution. This piece presents a summary of the importance, mechanism, and application scenarios of blockchain technology for enterprise digital transformation. It also explores the connection between blockchain technology and digital transformation, offering a future research direction to enable enterprises to transform in an information-based manner. Existing literature has been organized to provide insights into the constraints and elevation schemes of blockchain technology for digital transformation.

### The Significance of Applying Blockchain Technology to the Digital Transformation of Enterprises

Many academics concentrate on the technical benefits of blockchain and its beneficial practical application to the digital transformation of businesses. Huang (2019) claims that the blockchain’s asymmetric encryption technology can guarantee the security and dependability of business data, help business data become assets, speed up the flow and encashment of data, allow enterprises to preserve and trace data to its source, and realize its manageability and controllability. According to Lu et al. (2021), the decentralized, disintermediation, collective maintenance, and reliable database are four benefits of blockchain technology that can be used to create a financial blacklist and whitelist to improve the effectiveness of the current financial credit reference system. According to Yang (2019), the implementation of the “blockchain+ big data” finance model was beneficial for lowering financing risks, increasing the readiness to provide money, and improving the effectiveness of resource allocation. On-chain digital value transfer technologies, such as exclusive chain ledgers, shared chain ledgers, certificate management, and SaaS layer services, have been noted by Tan and Wu (2018) as having the potential to streamline current processes, lower business risks, and enhance data accuracy. According to Chen et al. (2019), government and business investments in digitization can make it easier for blockchain applications to improve the effectiveness of the internal capital market for businesses. Financial institutions used blockchain technology to increase the cost paid by small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to falsify credit information and price differentials. Silva and Santos (2022) claimed that blockchain technology can improve the efficiency and transparency of the supply chain, and the decentralized structure offered invariable properties to all parts of the supply chain. Cryptography also guaranteed security. CAICT (2021) asserted that the creation of a cross-border trade financial blockchain platform can resolve the issue of the veracity of cross-border trade background, improve the service effectiveness of cross-border trade settlement of commercial banks, and increase the security of cross-border trade finance business of commercial banks, using the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area as an example. According to Fang and Hu (2017), blockchain increased the transaction efficiency of banks and trade financing businesses and made the entire trade financing channel more unhindered by enabling all parties of the industrial chain to clearly understand the flow of goods, capital, and information. According to a recent analysis by PWC, blockchain might help the insurance business save $5 billion to $10 billion in costs, or 15% to 20% of its overall cost (Zhao, 2020). And according to the Gartner Group’s projection, the market value of blockchain will be around $176 billion in 2025 and $3.1 trillion in 2030. Additionally, the Bank of Spain thinks blockchain technology would help them save $20 billion in accounting expenses by 2022. All of those statistics show how important blockchain is to company digitalization.
