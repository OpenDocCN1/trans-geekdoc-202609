# The Significance of Applying Blockchain Technology to
the Digital Transformation of Enterprises

Many academics concentrate on the technical benefits of blockchain and its beneficial practical application to the digital transformation of businesses. Huang (2019) claims that the blockchain’s asymmetric encryption technology can guarantee the security and dependability of business data, help business data become assets, speed up the flow and encashment of data, allow enterprises to preserve and trace data to its source, and realize its manageability and controllability. According to Lu et al., (2021), the decentralized, disintermediation, collective maintenance, and reliable database are four benefits of blockchain technology that can be used to create a financial blacklist and whitelist to improve the effectiveness of the current financial credit reference system. According to Yang (2019), the implementation of the “blockchain+ big data” finance model was beneficial for lowering financing risks, increasing the readiness to provide money, and improving the effectiveness of resource allocation. On-chain digital value transfer technologies, such as exclusive chain ledgers, shared chain ledgers, certificate management, and SaaS layer services, have been noted by Tan and Wu (2018) as having the potential to streamline current processes, lower business risks, and enhance data accuracy. According to Chen et al., (2019), government and business investments in digitization can make it easier for blockchain applications to improve the effectiveness of the internal capital market for businesses.
