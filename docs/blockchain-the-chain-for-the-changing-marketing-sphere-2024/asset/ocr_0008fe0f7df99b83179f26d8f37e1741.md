# Abstract

An up-and-coming digital technology that will play a role in the advancements made by Industry 4.0 is blockchain. Blockchain may be used by organizations of all sizes to improve their security, privacy, and data transparency. The term “Industry 4.0” describes the integration of several cutting-edge manufacturing methods that accelerate the pace at which enterprises may achieve their objectives. Research on the potential for substantial disruptions brought on by a wide range of Industry 4.0 technologies has been conducted in recent years. These technologies include Artificial Intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), Big Data, and Blockchain. The industrial and supply chain sectors, among others, benefit greatly from these technological advancements. Blockchain has garnered a lot of interest because of its potential to enhance the business climate of supply chains and factories.
