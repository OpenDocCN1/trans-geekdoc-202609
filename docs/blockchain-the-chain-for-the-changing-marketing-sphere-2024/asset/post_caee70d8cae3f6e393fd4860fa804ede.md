## Impact/Application of Blockchain in Supply Chain

Blockchain innovations are having a big impact on the global banking industry, but less is known about how emerging technology may affect other industries (Figure 1). Although actual research in this area is sparse, the supply chain business is a significant and extremely promising application scenario for blockchain to enable transparency among untrustworthy stakeholders as well as increased efficiency and traceability throughout manufacturing and distribution operations (Scully & Hößig, 2019).

Market conditions have become more dynamic and demanding, resulting in a challenging competitive environment. To adapt to this changing environment, supply chains have become increasingly reliant on collaboration, integration, flexibility, and trust among stakeholders. As a result, the importance of new technological applications in supply chains has grown even more. Modern specialized apps must be installed to better supply chain flow control. Blockchain technology has shown to be an essential component of today’s competitive landscape. In today’s dynamic business world, companies should invest in blockchain technology to respond rapidly to changing market conditions and needs (Meidute-Kavaliauskiene et al., 2021).

In today’s competitive business environment, supply chain management is widely regarded as a critical tool for cost control and economic performance enhancement. However, as obstacles such as increased supply chain complexity, transparency, and flexibility requirements emerge, task-specific challenges and supply management methodologies must be modernized if firms and industrial sectors are to remain competitive (Helo and Hao, 2019).

Changes in demand and product portfolios make it difficult to synchronize the supply chain’s supply and demand plans because they are scattered over many various locations (Heidary et al., 2021). It is critical to prevent product damage during the transportation of sensitive products from one company to another until they reach the end consumers. Tampering with products during transportation, delays, fraud, identity verification, a lack of data management, and a lack of accuracy and integrity of data are all prevalent concerns in traditional supply chain management. Many modern corporations have built identification systems to preserve and maintain their global supremacy and retain the right to educate their suppliers. Otherwise, they will have to rely on and follow major regulatory firms or middlemen.

The impact of blockchain technology on supply chain flexibility, transparency, and trust has been studied in this study. Because blockchain technology is new and unknown to businesses, it is viewed with skepticism. As a result, businesses may regard their investments in this technology as high-risk. An attempt is made to reveal some of the benefits that the technology can give to business. Transparency, flexibility and trust has been chosen by many researchers as dependent variables since these are the critical issues for supply chains. Hence, it has been discovered by many researchers that use of blockchain technology has a substantial impact on supply chain transparency, flexibility and trust.

According to the relevant literature, organizations can boost customer satisfaction and, as a result, customer loyalty by providing more transparent supply chain operations. Furthermore, supply chain openness can increase supply chain cooperation by improving confidence among organizations. Furthermore, supply chain flexibility can be an important tool for organizations seeking to acquire a lasting competitive edge. Blockchain, according to the research findings, is an essential and valuable technology in all three aspects.

## Blockchain's Impact/Application in Digital Platforms

Airbnb is one example of the so-called platform economy, which connects apartment hunters with apartment owners via an Internet platform. Despite having a turnover of more than 3.6 billion USD in 2018, Airbnb does not own any apartments (Hook, 2018). However, many platform economy business models are founded on powerful monopolies or oligopolies. This is why many stakeholders (such as publishers, advertisers, and users) seek more balanced ecosystems. Is it possible for a new disruptive technology to change this?

Blockchain technology has been garnering increasing media attention (Nofer, 2017). The number of cryptocurrency users has surged from 5 million in 2015 to 44 million in 2019. (Statista, 2020). Bitcoin is the most popular cryptocurrency, with a current market valuation of 174 billion EUR, putting it at the top of the German DAX30. This begs the question, what exactly is blockchain technology?

Marketers are being challenged to comprehend and adopt new technologies at a faster rate than ever before. Although blockchain technology has been around for over a decade, academic scholars in marketing have paid very little attention to it. Given blockchain’s distinct characteristics (transparent, decentralized, and immutable), it has the potential to build a more secure, customer-centric, and open marketplace for consumers and enterprises.

The application of blockchain technology in digital marketing is still in its infancy. Blockchain technology’s effects on digital marketing include (but are not limited to) strengthening digital marketing security, reducing click fraud, increasing trust and transparency, and developing loyalty programs.

Blockchain technology is based on peer-to-peer communication, which encourages disintermediation, or the removal of intermediaries who process and filter data streams and add cost. By producing immutable and shared data records, blockchain technology can also help to improve data quality and facilitate data access. From the perspective of the consumer, blockchain technology has the potential to fundamentally transform customer relationships by increasing data and information availability, as well as privacy and security. It also permits new sorts of consumer loyalty programs, which may aid in the provision of value.

## Blockchain's Impact on Cryptocurrency

Blockchain technology is incredibly novel for all industries and organizations to implement. In the business sector, the primary components of the system are the manufacturer, production, retailer, and consumer. Thus, in the future, we might contemplate creating and implementing a single blockchain arrangement that can be used for any company, regardless of whether it is a land, food store network, clothes production network, automobile industry, medicines organization, or any other business, making it less demanding to convey the blockchain arrange.

The first commercially viable use of blockchain technology is cryptocurrency, which has the potential to serve as the primary energy source for the worldwide capital transfer network. The use of blockchain protocols, which have novel characteristics, has the potential to bring about a revolution in the online gaming sector. Blockchain technology enables permanent records, is very secure, offers a layer of anonymity in addition to transparency, does away with the need for middlemen, incorporates validation and verification processes, and enables transactions that can be shown to be fair and trustworthy. Blockchain technology and cryptocurrencies are yet to achieve widespread adoption, and they may continue to be too hard for the majority of consumers and companies to employ. At the moment, a very high degree of technical expertise is required to operate with blockchain.

Cryptocurrencies are exclusive to the digital realm. “digital currency” is often favored over “virtual currency” for this reason and because of the balanced connotations associated with it. When referring to money that is maintained in a “digital” or “electronic register,” “virtual” has a negative connotation since it signifies something that is “apparently real” but not “real.”
