This step of looking after customer satisfaction is needed and applied to business firms belonging to and operating in every market and industry that exists. Customer satisfaction is a very crucial step along with customer segmentation because no matter the innovativeness and pricing of an organization, unhappy customers are going to go away. The step or method of customer satisfaction can be undertaken and done by carefully listening to the demands and voices of various customers. Poll votes, polling booths, focus groups, and customer surveys are all part of determining the satisfactory point and measures of their customers and the customer bases present all across the globe. The experience of the customers using the products and services of an organization, the pricing set by the organization, and the launched products by the organization are all part of determining factors. These factors are all considered and evaluated as well as assessed to gain and acquire valuable detailed insights about the customer behaviors of the customer bases of a business organization (El-Adly, 2019; Dahiya 2023; Bansal 2023). These detailed insights are further used by the business organization to tailor the needs and expectations of their customers to provide them with satisfaction with the organizational services and products. A business organization also emphasizes and focuses on these measures and determining factors and tries to exceed their expectation as well to provide even more customer satisfaction to their customer bases.
