# R. Vennila*, PhD and Reshma Sultana, PhD

School of Commerce Studies, Jain Deemed to be University, Bangalore, India

# Abstract

Blockchain is one of the emerging technologies for accessing and sharing transactional data across a vast peer-to-peer network, where non-trusting users can communicate with each other without an intermediary in a verifiable manner. The fundamentals of blockchain, its uses, kinds, and operation are reviewed in this essay. Security, privacy, and the Consensus processes of this technology are also significant and cause for concern, and they are the primary drivers behind this novel technique. Anonymity, integrity, scalability, and transparency are a few of the key benefits of blockchain technology. A variety of technical, social, economic, and regulatory hurdles make large-scale integration of blockchain complicated. The article describes blockchain technology, how it affects the Indian economy, and its role in shifting the economy to a digital economy.

Keywords: block chain, digital economy, digital transformation
