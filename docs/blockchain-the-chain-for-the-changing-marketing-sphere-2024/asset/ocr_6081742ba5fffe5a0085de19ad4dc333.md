Blockchain technology is regarded as a vital tool in the financial sector, and it has the potential to bring about exciting developments in areas such as internet development, economic growth, and financial innovation. The financial industry urgently requires a digital transformation as well as the implementation of measures that will ensure the effective use of Blockchain technology. As a result, the purpose of the present study is to evaluate the moderating impact that information technology alignment plays in addition to the mediating function that Blockchain adoption plays between new digital process innovation and strategy innovation, as well as financial performance. Over the next ten years, blockchain technology is anticipated to have a substantial influence on e-commerce. If one of the initial key elements of usage of the innovation was making payments and worldwide transfers, then the recent flurry of approaches - and the participation of household names in those initiatives - is a clear indication that blockchain technology is likely to expand and have a profound effect on the economy beyond just the payment services industry.

The expansion of technology has resulted in substantial shifts in the business world, especially within the financial sector. A technology development known as blockchain has been developed to bring confidence and transparency to financial transactions. Blockchain technology can improve operational efficacy, raise levels of transparency, lessen exposure to risk by tying up fewer assets during transactions, and cut down on expenses, such as those associated with the transaction fees incurred in the financial industry. Several applications may be of use to the financial sector, including smart contracts, which is one of the most significant uses of blockchain technology; however, there are other possibilities as well. It is too soon to tell what precisely blockchain technology will make possible given it is still in its early stages of development; yet, the technology has the potential to cause a significant disruption to the present financial system. The most far-fetched theory is that distributed ledger technology would eventually render banks obsolete. The inability of professional market players to satisfy the prerequisites for innovation is the root cause of the objective obstacles that stand in the way of the implementation of blockchain technology within the financial industry.
