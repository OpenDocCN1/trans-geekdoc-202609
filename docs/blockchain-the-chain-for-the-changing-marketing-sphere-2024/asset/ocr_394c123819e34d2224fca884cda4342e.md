Satoshi Nakamoto presumably created a protocol for a decentralized electronic currency system after the 2008 banking sector collapse. The blockchain distributed ledger system was built on top of this protocol. Blockchain may be thought of as a global spreadsheet or ledger. Because it lacks a centralized database and runs on computers donated by volunteers all around the globe, it is decentralized. The distributed nature of a blockchain means that it may be seen by anybody at any time, rather than being restricted to a single entity. If you want to keep your digital transactions safe, you may use a blockchain, which is encrypted and relies on a system of public and private keys. To send money to another person or organization safely and reliably, without going via a bank or other financial institution, one may use a blockchain.

# Importance of Block Chain in the Context of Industry 4.0.

Using a distributed ledger to record “agreement/transaction” among an invited group promotes trade and business. The duplicated records among several users may verify the synchronized agreement or transaction.

Bitcoin’s rise has shown Blockchain applications since 2008. This popular coin solves the decentralized trust issue. Transactions are permanent, verifiable, and untraceable. A centralized bank is no longer needed as each agreement, method, task, and transaction has a digital record and signature that can be detected, verified, archived, and shared. Blocks that contain only valid transactions are gathered and sealed forever.
