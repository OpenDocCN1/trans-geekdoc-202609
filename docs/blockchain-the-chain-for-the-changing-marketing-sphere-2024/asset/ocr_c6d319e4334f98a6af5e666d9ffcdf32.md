Business organizations undertake this method of customer segmentation to increase their sales and profit margins. This also helps them in greater response from their customers who are divided into different segments and helps them retain their customers by emphasizing and meeting their demands. This process of customer segmentation is also done by various business organizations present all over the world to maintain and manage relations between their customers and their organization. The process of customer segmentation in a way helps different business organizations in various ways. Business organizations get help in designing their marketing plans and sales strategies based on their assessment and implementation of customer segmentation. The brand image, value, and brand loyalty of an organization or business company are also increased along with customer satisfaction of that business organization. These are all done under the various needs and demands of their segmented customer bases. However, customer segmentation is considered the first step towards customer satisfaction by all the business organizations and business companies in the industry. The method of customer segmentation that is undertaken by various indifferent business organizations as a part of their marketing strategies gives them certain perks. Business organizations get to know and acquire the ability to understand different patterns and several different differences among their customer bases (An et al., 2018; Taneja et al., 2023a, 2023b, 2023c, 2023d, 2023e 2023f).
