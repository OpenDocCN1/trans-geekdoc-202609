# Role of Blockchain in Digital Marketing

Blockchain technology has attracted significant attention and proven to be effective in several different industries through documented use cases. For instance, there are several well-known implementations for blockchain in the financial, medical, rental properties, and management of supply chain sectors. As a result, marketing is one of the probable uses for blockchain.

# Enhancing Digital Ad Campaigns and Marketing

Blockchain enables the connection between retailers and advertisers, enabling data transparency, clicks, and engagements, reducing fraud, and doing away with the middlemen. It can be challenging to enhance because digital advertising campaign data is occasionally unreliable. Blockchain makes it possible to track marketing initiatives in real time and collect reliable information.

Blockchain is being successfully used by leading brands like Unilever to increase the effectiveness of digital advertising. The technology will enter full production after which it and IBM will introduce a Blockchain advertising solution.

# Prevention of Fraud

A blockchain network permits the tracking of recognized digital outlets and compares the registry with tested advertising practices to prevent fraud. Only authorized stakeholders can securely access these measurements. Fraud is likely to occur in places like digital advertising, bots, click farms, and ghost websites. Engaging a third party can raise the risk of human error, cybersecurity breaches, and data loss. To reduce fraud in its digital advertising efforts, major businesses like Toyota have been using Blockchain applications in digital marketing. The volume of website visitors has increased over time, they have noticed.
