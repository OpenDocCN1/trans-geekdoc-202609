A very popular form of attack used to interrupt business operations is the Denial-of-Service (DoS) attack. It is designed to deny legitimate users’ access to computing and related services by flooding the host/targeted server with fake requests from numerous compromised devices (also known as zombies) on the Internet. The flood of incoming messages, connection requests, or malformed data packets forces the target server to slow down or crash and thereby deny service to legitimate users.
