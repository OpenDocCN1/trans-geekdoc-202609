Establishing a culture of preparedness is also well documented in emergency and disaster management literature.33 Numerous studies on disaster preparation and recovery have examined the critical success factors and made recommendations on how best to prepare for the different types of possible calamities. Enhancing general awareness, conducting vulnerability assessments, and having in place well-rehearsed action plans are the distinctive characteristics of organizations that have a strong culture of preparedness.34
