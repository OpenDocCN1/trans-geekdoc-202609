Thus, there are several elements to a comprehensive approach to preparedness—from identifying the critical assets to coming up with a risk-based protection prioritization scheme, implementing multi-factor authentication measures, managing access privileges, encrypting data, following reliable backup methods, securing networks and ports, engaging in customized and immersive training exercises, deploying sophisticated detection capabilities, and formulating and rehearsing business continuity and disaster recovery plans. Doing them well can be a challenging and overwhelming endeavor. Without a deep-rooted conviction and belief in the value of being thoroughly prepared, organizations cannot achieve and sustain a high level of preparedness.
