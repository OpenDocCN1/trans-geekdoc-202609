| **Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act** | Passed in 1999, this regulation is both an information security and privacy law.<br><br>The law applies to financial institutions, and that includes banks, insurance companies, securities firms, nonbank mortgage lenders, auto dealers, and tax preparers. | Employees must be subjected to background checks, especially those who are going to have access to customer information.<br><br>New employees must sign a confidentiality pledge.<br><br>Access to private information should be limited on a “Need to know” basis.<br><br>Strong passwords must be used, and they should be changed frequently.<br><br>Computer screens should lock after a certain specified period of inactivity.<br><br>Security policies must be in place for devices and data encryption.<br><br>Employees should go through security training when they join the company and on a periodic basis. They should be regularly reminded of the company’s security policies. | Violators could be fined in excess of $1 million.<br><br>The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) insurance could be terminated. |
