# The Cyberattack Epidemic

Research and survey reports reveal a continual rise in the frequency and severity of cyberattacks. No country and no industry is being spared; small and large organizations are being targeted; both public and private infrastructures are under attack (Table 1). The United States has been experiencing, on average, 130 large-scale targeted breaches per year and the number is growing by 27% every year. In 2017, the average number of breaches per country was reported to be 24,089. It is predicted that “cybercrimes will cost the world $6 trillion annually by 2021, up from $3 trillion in 2015.”¹ The forces fueling the cyberattack epidemic and the nature and extent of its impact are discussed in the following sections.
