| Consumer Privacy Protection Act of 2017 | Designed to ensure the privacy and security of sensitive personal information, to prevent and mitigate identity theft, to provide notice of security breaches involving sensitive personal information, and to enhance law enforcement assistance and other protections against security breaches, fraudulent access, and misuse of personal information. It will apply to organizations that collect, use, access, transmit, store, or dispose of sensitive personally identifiable information of 10,000 or more U.S. citizens during any 12-month period. | Internal and external penetration testing at least annually<br>Control testing once every three years<br>Annual security incident response plan testing<br>Annual enterprise technology risk assessment | Civil penalty fines will not exceed $5 million unless the violation is found to be willful or intentional, in which an additional $5 million can be imposed. |
