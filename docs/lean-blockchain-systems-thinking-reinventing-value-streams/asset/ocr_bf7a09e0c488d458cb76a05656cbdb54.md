# Examples of Tokens That Are Not on a Blockchain

Although we may not be aware of them, tokens are already widely used in our daily lives. We use physical and internet-based tokens already for proof of ownership, identity, or access, and they play an important role in simplifying processes and facilitating transactions in various domains.

These examples of physical and internet-based tokens assist in many of the principles of Lean methodology, streamlining operations and improving organizational efficiency. In this section, we provide examples of tokens that are already used in our daily lives.

# Physical Money

Physical money is the most obvious example of a token. The money itself does not have value; it is what it represents which is important. Money functions as a tangible representation of the intangible concept of value, enabling economic transactions and serving a critical purpose within an economy. As a token, it embodies consensus about value and facilitates the exchange of goods and services within a society.

# Debit Card

A debit card can be viewed as a token due to its role in representing access to financial resources and as a means of authentication in financial transactions. You don't have to have physical money in your pocket to make a purchase. The debit card is a token of your ability to exchange money.
