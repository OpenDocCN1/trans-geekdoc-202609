# 2.5 Perfection

Perfection is an ongoing pursuit of continuous improvement and excellence in all aspects of operations. It involves striving for perfection in processes, products, and systems by continuously eliminating waste, improving efficiency, and innovating to meet changing customer needs. Perfection is a long-term goal that encourages a culture of continuous learning and improvement within an organization.
