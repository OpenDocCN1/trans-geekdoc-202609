# Balancing Tools: Accounting

To better understand the opportunities to improve balancing, we need to better understand the principles of accounting and its evolution. This journey takes us from:

+   1. The physical ages of stone, bronze and iron with trust in single-entry accounting
2. The industrial age with trust in double-entry accounting
3. The internet age, double-entry accounting with trust in third parties (that synchronize our balances in separate ledgers)
4. Finally, into the age of the internet of trust (blockchain), with trust in computer code protocols that provide a decentralized consensus on the truth: triple-entry accounting
