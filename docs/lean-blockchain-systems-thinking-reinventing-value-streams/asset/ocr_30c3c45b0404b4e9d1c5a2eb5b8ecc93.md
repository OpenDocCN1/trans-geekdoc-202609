Let's have a look at a simple blockchain-enabled healthcare use case following procedures with smart contract implementation and tokenization:

# 1. How Does This Work?

See Figure 7.2 for a typical healthcare transactional process.

+   - Tokenized representation: Step 1

The client's digital wallet contains tokens representing their allocated hours of care from the insurer, acting as verifiable proof of entitlement to health-care services.

+   - Automated verification: Step 2

When the care is provided, the caretaker updates the service completion status within the blockchain network.

+   - Automatic payment: Step 3

Smart contracts trigger automatic payments from the insurer to the health-care agency directly from the client's wallet, synchronizing token balances immediately with the provided care hours.

# 2. Benefits

+   - Client empowerment: Clients have control via their digital wallets, improving convenience and trust in the healthcare process.
