# Introduction

In this chapter, we explore Lean Kaizen, Lean Kaikaku, and Lean Kakushin to understand their pivotal roles in driving transformative change.
We will use as an example the transformative change that occurred in business and society as a result of the “World-Wide Web” internet during the 1990s and early 2000s.
