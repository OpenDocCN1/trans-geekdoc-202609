# 2. Critical Success Factors

In Lean methodology, the CSFs are the pivotal elements crucial for the effectiveness and efficiency of a process or system. By integrating these success factors, organizations can effectively implement Lean principles and realize sustainable improvements in efficiency, quality, and customer satisfaction.

To keep the analysis of this process simple, we have chosen five CSFs that are important in every process. See Figure 4.11.

+   - **Quality**
Quality is a fundamental success factor in every process. It emphasizes delivering products or services that may or exceed customer needs and expectations. It involves striving for perfection with **transparency**, **accountability**, effective resource management, and ensuring consistency in output. Functionality and features work as expected and accuracy of information are examples of quality CSFs.

+   - **Reliability**
Reliability focuses on the consistency and predictability of processes. It's about creating systems and workflows **you can trust** to produce the expected results consistently, without unexpected errors or variations. The use of **standards** plays an important role in reliability.
