# Introduction

In the previous Cause and Effect chapter, we exposed the inherent problems
of our traditional organizational structures (TRAD-ORG), exposing them to
generating waste, restricting the flow of value, and providing poor quality
outoutcomes by design. To assist in resolving such problems, we present our
adapted DMAIC scheme for Lean Blockchain System Thinking.
See Figure 10.1.

Our thesis is that Blockchain technology and Systems Thinking, supple-
enting existing Lean principles, will help to improve our organizational
structures through improved collaboration, cooperation, and communica-
t, resulting in improved flow of products, information, and payments (PIP)
