# Lean Blockchain Systems Thinking

We'll Also Assist in Preventing You from Getting FOMO!

FOMO is an acronym for “Fear of Missing Out”; the fear of not being included in something (such as an interesting or enjoyable activity) that others are experiencing.

# Control by design
