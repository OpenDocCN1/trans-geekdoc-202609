## 5.3.1.4 Training

It is practicable to embark on the training after gaining abundant dataset and constructing the requisite DL framework. The training enables the surrogate model to acquire the capability to approximate the object operator involved in the multiphysics systems. As the leading motivation for the network is to force the output as ground truth generated by the software, a suitable loss function is critical. Here, the mean square error is the definitive selection under the hypothesis of the maximum likelihood. During the training procedure, the two frameworks are trained separately. The input module is trained to map the explicit boundaries to the corresponding physics quantity A, while the Fourier module is aimed at utilizing the directly related field A the indirectly field B. After training, these two networks are assembled for multiphysics prediction.
