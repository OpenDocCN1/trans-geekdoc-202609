# 5.3.2. Comparison

In this part, experiments are performed to highlight the strength of the developed Fourier network. The predicted accuracy is compared to two conventional networks at different resolutions. To end this, the calculation of temperature in the first experiments are investigated by employing the fully connected network (FCN) the convolutional neural network (CNN). The average mean square errors on the results with a resolution ranging from $16^2$ to $128^2$ are collected. It is noted that the benchmark of the three network is the computational costs of an adequately dense grid. It is lucid that the MSE of the cascaded Fourier framework is more than 10 times lower than FCN and CNN. Additionally, as the conventional CNN or FNN focuses on the data fitting while the Fourier network aims at operator learning, the average MSE is nearly independent of the mesh density.
