These networks are able to handle the intractability of problems, so that are practical in applications. Nevertheless, the existing works have a few defects inevitably. For instance, the aforementioned PINN has low computational efficiency and generalization ability for one network is merely applicable for specific case. If boundaries or the geometries are altered, the network has been retrained. For the DeepM&Net (on the basis of the DeepOne), it is fairly cumbersome to train the framework, it is hard to be applied in reality.

In practical scenes, nowadays have requirements for precision and real time. For example, to monitor the state of electronic devices, it is essential to acquire the electrical potential and temperature field on the basis of the boundaries to accurately tune the future electricity. Beside, in the heat-flow analysis in an expensive equipment, it is anticipated to infer the temperature and flow rate via the measurement on the chassis, to assess the working condition in real time. However, it is nearly impossible for conventional approaches or equation-based DL frameworks (i.e.: PINN) for their learning process. Additionally, the existing supervised learning networks (i.e.: CNN and FCN) Have calculation errors apparently increasing with grid density, hindering it to acquire accurate solutions. For operator learning networks, however, the state-of the art work such as the DeepM&Net has complicated training procedure, it is inconvenient for practice applications. In view of this, a novel framework based on cascaded Fourier net is proposed to compute the multiphysics fields. The developed network has features listed below:

+   1. Real-time: A mature model can acquire the physics fields in real time after being fully trained.

+   2. High accuracy: The operator-based learning strategy yields a high independence of the resolution, and is thus applicable to the dense grids.

+   3. High flexibility: The DL network is adapted to diverse multiphysics fields, while the training process is concise.
