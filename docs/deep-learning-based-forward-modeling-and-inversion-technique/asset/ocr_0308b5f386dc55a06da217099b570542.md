# 4.3.2 3D Heat Conduction Model

The physical model of 3D IHC in this section is an industrial product made of alloy, which is made up of a cuboid and a quadrangle. On the top, a cavity is truncated by an ellipsoid. As displayed in Figure 4.6, the main part of the geometry is denoted by $\Omega$ and the outer surface is termed as $\partial\Omega$. Similarly to the 2D case, an inner heat source is located inside $\Omega$, while temperature recorders are placed at the outer surface $\partial\Omega$.

To predict the problem, the heat density at r is regarded as a function $P$. Here, the thermal conductivity, thermal capacity, and density of the artifact are $k$, $C_p$ and $\rho$. Here the heat conduction equation in the model can be depicted as

```
$$\rho C_p\frac{\partial T}{\partial t}=\nabla\cdot(k\nabla T)+P. \quad (4.20)$
```

All the surfaces $\partial\Omega$ of the model comply with the convective boundary conditions

```
$$-k\frac{\partial T}{\partial n}=h(T - T_0), \quad (4.21)$
```
