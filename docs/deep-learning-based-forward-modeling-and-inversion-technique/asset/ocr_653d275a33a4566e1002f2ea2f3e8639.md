However, the average error on the whole dataset only measures the performance of the framework from a holistic perspective and may inappropriate for uneven error distributions. For instance, few samples with large errors increase the average error by a large margin. In this scheme, statistical analysis is conducted on the results.

Here, the violin plot is introduced in Figure 2.29 to depict the distributions of the relative error on the three datasets. The plot is composed of two symmetric density curves whose width corresponds to the approximate frequency of data points in each domain. The peaks, valleys, and tails of each dataset's density curve can be pellucidly exhibited.
