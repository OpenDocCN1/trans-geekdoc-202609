# 2.3.1 Heat Conduction Model

At the beginning of this section, it is necessary to briefly introduce the three-dimensional heat conduction model. The calculation area is a cube with a side length of 3.2 m. For ease of computation, the whole solving domain is divided into 32 × 32 × 32 subgrids, and the volume of each is 0.001 $m^3$. It is assumed that the thermophysical parameters (heat capacity, thermal conductivity, density) and the temperature in each sub grid are the same.
