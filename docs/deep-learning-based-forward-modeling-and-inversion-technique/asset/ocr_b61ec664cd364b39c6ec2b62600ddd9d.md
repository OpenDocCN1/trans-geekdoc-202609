# 2.3.2.1 Thermophysical Parameters

The thermophysical parameters of the material include three items: the thermal conductivity $k$, the constant heat capacity $C_p$ and the density $ho$. The thermal conductivity is defined as the heat transferred by the unit temperature gradient through the heat conduction area unit time, while the heat capacity is defined as the heat absorbed or released by the temperature change of 1 K per unit mass under constant pressure. Although the three parameters vary with temperature, such changes can often be ignored in a certain temperature range.
