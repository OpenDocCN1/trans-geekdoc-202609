In contrast, the goal of operator learning is to learn the mapping between infinite dimensional function spaces, which can resolve the entire family of partial differential equations without retraining, thus saving computing resources. Recently, operator learning in PDE solution is an emerging research direction with vigorous development. Several representative examples are selected in this section.

In 2021, Amkur et al. [69] approximated the infinite-dimensional mapping by learning nonlinear functions and a category of integral operators. It is noted that the kernel integration is calculated by the message passing mechanism of the graph networks, which can be implanted to diverse levels of resolution.
