Compared with the traditional convolution network, the proposed work is appropriately modified to adapt to complex three-dimensional problems. First of all, the mean pooling rather than the common max pooling is utilized to implement the downsampling. This is because the average pooling concentrates more on the overall thermophysical parameters, while the maximum pooling tends to focus on feature textures (e.g., interfaces of two mediums).
