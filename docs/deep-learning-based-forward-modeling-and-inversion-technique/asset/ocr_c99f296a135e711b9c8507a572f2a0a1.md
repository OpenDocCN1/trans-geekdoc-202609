# 5.2 Graph Neural Networks

Graph Neural Networks (GNN) are a series of algorithms aiming at utilizing DL frameworks to process graph data. As is known to all, the traditional deep networks are inappropriate for graph-structured data since it is not based on a regular grid. The G was first proposed in 2005 and 2008 by Scarselli et al. [28], which are intended for node- and graph-oriented tasks, respectively. Nowadays, with the exuberant development of the DL techniques, more efforts are dedicated to the GNNs. The classical reals involved in GNNs include recommendation systems [29], computer vision [30], natural language processing [31], anomaly detection [32], drug discovery [35], and so on.
