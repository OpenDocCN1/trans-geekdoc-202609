Higher in the GO hierarchy, there are broader terms; for example, in the BP category, terms such as biological regulation and cellular process exist. As we move the hierarchy, the terms become more specific and narrower in scope, and regulation of gene expression or protein localization to organelle more specific terms within the BP category. Each term has parent terms and child terms.

The hierarchical nature of the GO allows researchers to perform analyses at different levels of specificity. A common need is to collapse similar terms to a higher, more general term that captures them all. In this recipe, we'll look at a couple of ways of automating with the topGO package. Specifically, topGO implements the hierarchy-aware methods like elim and parent_child, as described in Alexa et al. 2006 (Bioinformatics 22:1660) and Grossman et al. 2007 (Bioinformatics 23:3024).
