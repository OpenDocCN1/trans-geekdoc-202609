Where, \( V_k \) is the k\(^\text{th}\) column of matrix \( V \)

\( V = G^H (GG^H)^{-1} \) (11.7)

Consequently, with zero forcing precoding, the signal to noise ratio for user k can be expressed as,

\( r_k = \frac{P \Lambda_K}{k \sigma^2} (g_k w_k)(g_k w_k)^H \) (11.8)

Where, \( g_k \) is the k\(^\text{th}\) row of \( G \) and

\( \Lambda_K = [\Lambda \wedge^H]_{k,k} \) (11.9)

Here, we assume that the channel state information is perfectly obtained by the base station and thus \( \Lambda \) is assumed to be a constant in the following which can be obtained by water filling algorithm.

Using above notations, we can express the data rate for user k as,

\( r_k = E [log_2 (1 + r_k)] \) (11.10)

The multiuser capacity can be achieved by,

\( C = E \sum_k log_2 \left( 1 + \frac{P \Lambda_K}{k \sigma^2} [GG^H]_{k,k} \right) \) (11.11)
