We have the following lower bound using metric entropy with respect to the Hellinger distance, which is analogous to Corollary 12.20.

**Corollary 12.25**  _If for some $C>0$ and $\epsilon>0$,_

$$C^{-1}\epsilon^{-q}\leq \ln M(\epsilon,\mathcal{F},H_{D_X})\leq C\epsilon^{-q}.$$

_Assume that (12.2) holds. Then we have_

$$r_n(\mathcal{P},Q_H)\geq C'n^{-2/(2+q)}$$

_for some $C'>0$._

We also have the following upper bound using the Gibbs algorithm on sieves.

**Proposition 12.26**  _Assume that the distribution of $X$ is $D_X$. Let $\mathcal{F}_n$ be an $\epsilon$ packing subset of $\mathcal{F}$ under the $H_{D_X}$ metric with $M(\epsilon,\mathcal{F},H_{D_X})$ members. Assume that $p_D\in\mathcal{F}$ and (12.2) holds. Let $\hat q$ be a Gibbs distribution on $\mathcal{F}_n$ defined as_

$$\hat q(p\mid S_n)\propto q_0(p)\exp\left(0.5\sum_{i=1}^{n}\ln p(Y_i\mid X_i)\right),$$

_where $q_0(p)$ is the uniform distribution on $\mathcal{F}_n$. Then_

$$\mathbb{E}_{S_n\sim D^n}\mathbb{E}_{p\sim\hat q}Q_H(p,\mathcal{D})\leq c_F\epsilon^2+\frac{2\ln M(\epsilon,\mathcal{F},H_{D_X})}{n}.$$

_Proof_  Let $\Delta(\mathcal{F}_n)$ denote the set of probability distributions on $\mathcal{F}_n$. We obtain

$$\begin{aligned}
\mathbb{E}_{S_n\sim D^n}\mathbb{E}_{p\sim\hat q}Q_H(p\mid\mathcal{D})
&\leq \inf_{q\in\Delta(\mathcal{F}_n)}\left[\mathbb{E}_{p\sim q}\mathbb{E}_{X\sim D_X}\mathrm{KL}(p_D(\cdot\mid X)\Vert p(\cdot\mid X))+\frac{2\mathrm{KL}(q\Vert q_0)}{n}\right]\\
&\leq \inf_{q\in\Delta(\mathcal{F}_n)}\left[c_F\mathbb{E}_{p\sim q}H_{D_X}(p_D\Vert p)^2+\frac{2\ln M(\epsilon,\mathcal{F},H_{D_X})}{n}\right].
\end{aligned}$$

The first inequality used Corollary 10.26 with $\alpha=0.5$. The second inequality used (12.2) and $\mathrm{KL}(q\Vert q_0)\leq\ln M(\epsilon,\mathcal{F},H_{D_X})$. Since $\mathcal{F}_n$ is also an $\epsilon$-cover of $\mathcal{F}_n$ in $H_{D_X}$, we obtain the desired bound.

Under the condition of Corollary 12.25, we note that

$$\inf_{\epsilon>0}O\left(\epsilon^2+\frac{1}{n}\ln M(\epsilon,\mathcal{F},H_{D_X})\right)=O\left(n^{-2/(2+q)}\right).$$

Therefore the upper bound of Proposition 12.26 matches the lower bound of Corollary 12.25.
