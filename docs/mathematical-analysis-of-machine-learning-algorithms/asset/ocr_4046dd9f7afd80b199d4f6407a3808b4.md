formula. Inequality (a) used $\left(\widetilde{\Sigma}^{(i-1)}\right)^{-1} \geq \Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_{i-1})^{-1}$ and $\eta z/(1+\eta z)$ is an increasing function of $z>0$ to bound the first term, and used $\Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_i) \geq (1-\eta)\Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_{i-1})$ to bound the second term. The last inequality used the definition of $m$ and $\dim(\cdot)$.

By summing over $i=1$ to $i=n$, we obtain

$$n\frac{\eta g_m(X_m)}{1+\eta g_m(X_m)} \leq n\frac{\eta}{1-\eta}\dim(\lambda,\psi(\cdot))+\ln\left|\frac{\Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_n)}{\Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_0)}\right|.$$

The algorithm implies that $g_m(X_m)\geq (1-\eta)\gamma_m$. We can replace $g_m(X_m)$ by $(1-\eta)\gamma_m$ to obtain the desired result.

**Example 9.40**  If $\mathcal{H}$ is a (finite) $d$-dimensional space and $\|\psi(X)\|_{\mathcal{H}}\leq B$, then

$$\ln\left|\frac{\Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_n)}{\Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_0)}\right|\leq d\ln\operatorname{trace}\left(\frac{\Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_n)}{d\Sigma_{\lambda}(\pi_0)}\right)\leq d\ln\left(1+\frac{B^2}{\lambda d}\right).$$

The first inequality used the inequality of arithmetic and geometric means (AM-GM). Since $\dim(\lambda,\psi(\cdot)\leq d$, we have

$$\frac{\gamma_m}{1+\eta(1-\eta)\gamma_m}\leq\frac{d}{(1-\eta)^2}+\frac{d\ln(1+B^2/(\lambda d))}{\eta(1-\eta)n}.$$

By taking $\eta=0.25/d$ and $n=O(d\ln(1+B^2/(\lambda d)))$ in Theorem 9.39, we obtain a bound of $\gamma_m=O(d)$, which matches Theorem 9.38 up to a constant.
