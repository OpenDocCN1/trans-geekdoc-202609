$$\forall w\in\Omega:\quad \phi(w,\mathcal{S}'_n)\le \phi(w,\mathcal{S}_n)+2\epsilon+\sqrt{\frac{2\ln(2N/\delta_1)}{n}}.$$

The standard additive Chernoff bound implies that for all $w\in\Omega$, with probability at least $1-\delta_2$,

$$\phi(w,\mathcal{D})\le \phi(w,\mathcal{S}'_n)+\sqrt{\frac{\ln(1/\delta_2)}{2n}}.$$

Therefore in Lemma 4.11, we can take symbols as defined, together with $\delta_1=2\delta/3$ and $\delta_2=\delta/3$ to obtain the desired bound.

Similarly, we consider $\mathcal{F}=\{f(z)=\phi(w,z):w\in\Omega\}$. Given $S_n$, we consider an $\epsilon$-$L_1(S_n)$ cover $\mathcal{F}_\epsilon(S_n)$ of $\mathcal{F}$, of size no more $N=N_1(\epsilon,\mathcal{G},n)$. We assume that $f(Z_i)\in[0,1]$ for all $f\in\mathcal{F}_\epsilon(S_n)$. From Corollary 2.27 and the union bound, we obtain the following uniform convergence result over $\mathcal{F}_\epsilon(S_n)$. With probability at least $1-\delta$,

$$\forall f\in\mathcal{F}_\epsilon(S_n):\quad f(\sigma,S_n)\le\sqrt{\frac{2\sum_{Z\in S_n}f(Z)^2\ln(N/\delta)}{n^2}}$$
$$\le \gamma'\frac{1}{n}\sum_{Z\in S_n}f(Z)+\frac{\ln(N/\delta)}{2\gamma'n}.$$

The first inequality used Corollary 2.27. The second inequality used $\sqrt{2ab}\le \gamma'a+b/(2\gamma')$ and $f(Z)^2\le f(Z)$. Since for all $f\in\mathcal{F}$, we can find $f'\in\mathcal{F}_\epsilon(S_n)$ so that $\frac{1}{n}\sum_{Z\in S_n}|f(Z)-f'(Z)|\le\epsilon$. It follows that

$$f(\sigma,S_n)\le f'(\sigma,S_n)+\epsilon$$
$$\le \gamma'\frac{1}{n}\sum_{Z\in S_n}f'(Z)+\frac{\ln(N/\delta)}{2\gamma'n}+\epsilon$$
$$\le \gamma'\frac{1}{n}\sum_{Z\in S_n}f(Z)+\frac{\ln(N/\delta)}{2\gamma'n}+(1+\gamma')\epsilon.$$

Now, with $\psi(f,S_n)=\tilde{\psi}(f,S_n)=\gamma'\frac{1}{n}\sum_{Z\in S_n}f(Z)$, we obtain from Lemma 4.8 the following uniform convergence result. With probability at least $1-\delta_1$ over $(S_n,S'_n)\sim\mathcal{D}^{2n}$,

$$\forall w\in\Omega:\quad (1-\gamma')\phi(w,S'_n)\le (1+\gamma')\phi(w,S_n)+2(1+\gamma')\epsilon+\frac{\ln(2N/\delta_1)}{\gamma'n}.$$

Let $\gamma'=\gamma/(2-\gamma)$, then it is easy to check algebraically that $(1-\gamma')/(1+\gamma')=1-\gamma$ and $1/(\gamma'(1+\gamma'))=(2-\gamma)^2/(2\gamma)$. We thus obtain

$$\forall w\in\Omega:\quad (1-\gamma)\phi(w,S'_n)\le \phi(w,S_n)+2\epsilon+\frac{(2-\gamma)^2\ln(2N/\delta_1)}{2\gamma n}. \tag{4.1}$$
