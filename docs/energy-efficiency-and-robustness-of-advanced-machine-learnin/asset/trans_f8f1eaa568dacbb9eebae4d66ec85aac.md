**The following key observations can be derived from our experiments:**

1. For Example 3, both the DeepCaps and the ResNet correctly classify the input image (`[Figure 4.13a](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_13)`) as “truck” (S9), and for both networks, the target class is “ship” (S8). As shown in `[Figure 4.14a](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_14)`, *the DeepCaps is fooled by the attack*. After 6 iterations of the attack, the image is classified as “ship” with a probability of 0.421. Increasing the number of iterations increases the gap between the probabilities, thus making the attack more robust. After 11 iterations, the distance has overcome `*D*[*M**A**X*]` = 520, and the adversarial example generated at this point is shown in `[Figure 4.13b](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_13)`. Similarly, *the attack also fools the ResNet*. After 5 iterations, it is classified as “ship” with a probability of 0.376. At the iteration 8 (see `[Figure 4.13c](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_13)`), the probability associated to the class “ship” is 0.433, while the distance has overcome `*D*[*M**A**X*]` = 520.

2. Example 4 (`[Figure 4.13d](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_13)`) is correctly classified as a “bird” by the DeepCaps, with a probability equal to 0.847. The second highest probability, which will be the attack's target class, is associated with the class “horse” (S7). After 9 iterations, the distance overcomes `*D*[*M**A**X*]` = 450, while the image shown in `[Figure 4.13e](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_13)` is still correctly classified as a “bird” by the DeepCaps with a probability equal to 0.524. The yellow bars in `[Figure 4.14c](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_14)` show that the image in `[Figure 4.13f](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_13)` is classified as a “horse” with a probability equal to 0.365, but the distance is way beyond `*D*[*M**A**X*]`. Hence, *only a perceptible noise can mislead the DeepCaps*.

3. The Example 4 (`[Figure 4.13d](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_13)`) is correctly classified as a “bird” by the ResNet, with a probability equal to 0.910. The target class is “deer” (S4). After 9 iterations, *the probability of the target class has overcome the initial one*. As shown in `[Figure 4.14d](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_14)`, the ResNet classifies the image in `[Figure 4.13g](#chapter4.xhtml#fig4_13)` as a “deer” with a probability equal to 0.483. At this point, the distance has also reached `*D*[*M**A**X*]` = 450.
