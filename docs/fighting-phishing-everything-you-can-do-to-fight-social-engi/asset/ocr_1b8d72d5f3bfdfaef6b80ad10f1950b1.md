# Definitions

This section defines various related definitions that may appear in the policy or SAT-related program documentation.

+   **BEC** BEC is short for business email compromise, which is also known as CEO fraud. Malicious social engineering scams where the perpetrator tries to trick someone or a business into making a payment they should not otherwise make.

+   **Cybercrime** The term cybercrime (or computer crime) encompasses a broad range of potentially illegal activities. In our context, it means crimes that target computer networks, devices, operating systems, applications, and their users.

+   **Endpoint** Another word for the workstation that is used by an end user in an organization. Refers to a computer or device at the end of a network connection.

+   **Exploit** An exploit (in French, meaning “achievement”) is (usually malicious) software that takes advantage of a bug, glitch, or vulnerability in other code to cause unintended or unanticipated behavior to occur and to gain control of a computer system.
