| Level | Name | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Basic Compliance | • Bare minimum of training<br>• Limited metrics<br>• “Check the box” |
| 2 | Security Awareness Foundation | • At least annual and onboarding training<br>• Occasional phishing simulations<br>• Focus on a variety of content |
| 3 | Programmatic Security Awareness & Behavior | • Intentional awareness program with integrated tools<br>• Quarterly training with simulated phishing<br>• Focus on security-aware behaviors |
| 4 | Security Behavior Management | • Continuous training across varied delivery methods and audiences<br>• Heavy use of integrated tools to inform training strategy<br>• Program focused on real behavior change |
| 5 | Sustainable Security Culture | • Program that intentionally measures, shapes, and reinforces security culture<br>• Multiple methods of behavior-based encouragement<br>• Security values woven through the fabric of the entire organization |
