Base64 Encoding Base64 is another encoding system that allows English characters and binary data to be encoded as Base64 characters. Like ASCII, you can find the Base64 encoding chart by searching for the “base64 encoding chart” on the Internet. Figure 13-21 shows a partial representation of the Base64 conversion table. Back in the early days of the Internet, when everything was text-based (instead of graphic), Base64 encoding was the only way to send binary data and files. Well, URLs can be Base64-encoded.
