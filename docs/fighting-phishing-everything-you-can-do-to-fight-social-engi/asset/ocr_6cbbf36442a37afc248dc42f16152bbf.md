# Romance Scams

Another common scam on social media and dating sites is romance scams. With romance scams, the scammer pretends to be someone they aren’t and attempts to get the victim to fall in love with them to then commit financial fraud. The scammers are often very successful and tens of thousands of romance scam victims have lost their entire life savings and even committed fraud and crime themselves to send money to their online “lovers.”

Because I've written a few articles on romance scams and how to help victims over the years, I frequently get emails from loved ones trying to help a victim get out of the hold of a romance scammer. Sometimes, the victims themselves write me, suspecting that their virtual loved one may not be who they say they are, and they ask for my help in how to confirm their suspicion. If you're writing a stranger to confirm your “love connection,” you should probably reevaluate the strength of that love and hold off on sending more money.

I once had a woman claim to me that Yanni, the famous musician, was in love with her, but that he needed her to send him money because his wife, actress Linda Evans, whom he was soon to divorce, supposedly controlled his money. But if the victim sent him money, Yanni could somehow buy his way out, divorce Linda Evans, and then marry the victim.
