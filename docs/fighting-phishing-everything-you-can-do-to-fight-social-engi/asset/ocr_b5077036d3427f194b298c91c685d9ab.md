# Policy Goal

The goal of this policy is to significantly reduce Acme’s cybersecurity risk due to participant actions and decisions when faced with social engineering threats, by using security awareness training and education. Participants should be able to better recognize cybersecurity risks and understand how to treat them.

# Control Mapping
