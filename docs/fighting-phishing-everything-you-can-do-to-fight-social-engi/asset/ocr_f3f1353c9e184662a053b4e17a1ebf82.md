# FIGURE 15-1 Real-world examples of disjointed email addresses.

Legitimate emails are going to have email addresses and Friendly Names that match.

It is also very suspicious if the DNS domain in any included URLs doesn’t match the DNS domain of the originating email address. Figure 15-2 shows an example of that.

In Figure 15-2, the email address comes from the DNS domain lilac.plala.or.jp, but when I hovered over the URL linked to the Keep same Password button, it tied to indiatimes .com. Most legitimate emails will have email addresses and URLs from the same domain. Any email having a disjointed email address and URL domains should be considered suspicious.
