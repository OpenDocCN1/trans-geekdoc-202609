Although he would use any known exploit to achieve his goal, he was notorious for social engineering. Kevin was eventually arrested and spent time in jail. Upon his release, Kevin turned his life around, doing legal professional penetration testing and computer security consulting, and he was a co-owner at KnowBe4.
