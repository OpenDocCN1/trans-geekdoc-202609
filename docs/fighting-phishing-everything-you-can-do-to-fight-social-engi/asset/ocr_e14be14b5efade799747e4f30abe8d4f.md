# Responsibilities

Responsibilities are the state of accepting or assigning accountability. A good security culture makes each individual accountable for particular actions and makes them feel like an integral and beloved part of the organization. Employees who feel responsible are more likely to make appropriate decisions when faced with unexpected or misunderstood scenarios.

All of these personal dimensions can help an organization have a good security culture or if done poorly cause the opposite. Business culture leader, John R. Childress (www.johnrchildress.com) says, “You get the culture you ignore.”
