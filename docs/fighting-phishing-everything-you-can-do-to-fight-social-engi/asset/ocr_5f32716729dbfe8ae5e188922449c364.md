# Simulated Phishing Campaigns

Acme uses simulated phishing exercises to gauge an employee’s understanding of the training and their fitness against particular types of high-risk threats. One to two simulated phishing exercises will be sent per week without previous notice to participants. Simulated phishing exercises will have a “pass/fail” component. Any participant who clicks on a link, downloads an attached document, or follows instructions in a simulated phishing exercise will be deemed as having failed the exercise. Any participant simply opening a phishing email or message or listening to a message will not have been deemed to fail the exercise. Any participant reporting a suspected phishing exercise to IT Security or the Help Desk and not clicking on any links, downloading any files, or providing any credentials will be considered as having “passed” the exercise. Any participant simply deleting and not reporting a suspected simulated phish will not reported as passing the test, but will be reported as a “soft fail.” Two or more soft fails count as a full regular failure rating.

Clicking on links, downloading documents, or providing login credentials to simulated phishing content may result in the end user immediately learning they failed a simulated phishing exercise.
