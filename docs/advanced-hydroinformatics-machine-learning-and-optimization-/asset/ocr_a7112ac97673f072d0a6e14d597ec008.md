Table 8.5 and Figure 8.9 present the performance metrics and scatter plots between the predicted and observed hydrograph for different forecast lead times (3, 6, 9, and 12 hr). These results show a diminish in the model performance as the lead time increases, especially for the RMSE, the NRMSE, NSE, and KGE values. The Mean Error (ME) exhibits better results for the naïve reference model than the MLP-ANN model for lead time larger than 2 hr. These results basically means that the average model error in the naïve reference model is lower than the MLP-ANN models, but the magnitude of the error is not measured in the mean error formula.
