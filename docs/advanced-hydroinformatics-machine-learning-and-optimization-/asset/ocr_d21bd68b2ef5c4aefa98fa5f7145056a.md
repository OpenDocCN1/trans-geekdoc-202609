# 7.4.1. Selecting Model Inputs and Data Preparation

Based on the correlation analyses and the usefulness of rainfall as one of the predictors, a number of trials were conducted to determine the most suitable combination of predictors and their lags. Models were set up to initially run using the inputs of rainfall (referred to as R) ([R_{t-1}, R_t]), water level (referred to as WL) (combinations of [WL_{t-1}, WL_t], [WL_{t-2}, WL_{t-1}, WL_t], and [WL_{t-3}, WL_{t-2}, WL_{t-1}, WL_t]), and discharge (referred to as FLOW), where applicable (combinations of [FLOW_t], and [FLOW_{t-1}, FLOW_t]. The dependent variable was [WL_{t+1}]. Similar to the correlation analyses, the tests using the WL inputs provided the best results and were used as a basis for further experiments. For the stations not influenced by a water-control structure, the rainfall inputs were added to the combinations of WL inputs initially tested, resulting in the combinations [R_{t-1}, R_t, WL_{t-1}, WL_t], [R_{t-1}, R_t, WL_{t-2}, WL_{t-1}, WL_t], and [R_{t-1}, R_t, WL_{t-3}, WL_{t-2}, WL_{t-1}, WL_t]. The test for the stations not influenced by a water-control structure were conducted first, and then the combination that provided the best model performance was used for tests for the stations influenced by a water-control structure, adding the additional input(s) of discharge. The combinations tested here were [R_{t-1}, R_t, WL_{t-2}, WL_{t-1}, WL_t, FLOW_t] and [R_{t-1}, R_t, WL_{t-2}, WL_{t-1}, WL_t, FLOW_{t-1}, FLOW_t]. Table 7.1 shows the improvement of model performance at the majority of lead times with the addition of the rainfall inputs. This improvement is observed in the RMSE values in Table 7.1. All tests were conducted using the TDNN model architecture.

Additional experiments were conducted with inputs, such as the 7 day moving average of rainfall and change in water level, but rendered no improvement in model performance and were not considered further. The final combination of inputs was decided based on the results of the tests.
