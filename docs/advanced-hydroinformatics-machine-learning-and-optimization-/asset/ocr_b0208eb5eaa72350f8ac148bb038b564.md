For example, Malekmohammadi et al. (2009) built a hydrodynamic model for Bakhtiari and Dez River-Reservoir systems in Iran, used to derive optimal solutions from historical floods using genetic algorithm. When current observation comes, similar events as well as corresponding optimal solutions would be found by the K nearest neighbor (K-NN) algorithm. However, such research that considers real-time operation is mostly related to reservoir operation or water distribution systems operations, while studies that are considering flood storage area management are absent. To our knowledge, this study is the first one to study both real-time operation and robust optimization of flood storage areas for developing operation strategies.

This chapter proposes robust optimization strategies and near-real-time operation method for operating multiple gates of the flood storage areas considered. Before the start of strategy development, uncertainty of inflow was analyzed, which was realized by sampling synthetic hydrographs, generating 100 possible inflow hydrographs with the characteristic of different severity levels. In order to produce robust strategies, the method of ROPAR was used and the probability of inundation of the considered storage areas was derived. For developing potential real-time operation method, these sampled hydrographs and corresponding optimal solutions were taken as pre-exiting set of solutions to be considered during actual strategy selection and extraction. In order to identify the most similar inflow conditions, four methods were proposed and tested. The testing was carried out by three amplified hydrographs (considered actual for real-time operations), which were derived by a standard approach taken in China to represent hydrographs with different return periods. Finally, the robust strategies and real-time decisions were compared so that the suitability of the different methods could be assessed. The analysis of storage area operation could hopefully provide suggestions for local administration with the aim of reducing flood risk in Bengbu City while simultaneously lowering storage area damage.
