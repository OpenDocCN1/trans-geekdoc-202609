The maximum number of iterations and the training rate are set as 100 and 0.01, respectively, here. The sigmoid activation function is selected to compute the output of each neuron. The default values suggested by Beale et al. (2010) are employed in the training of weak learners for other parameters. In our study, 10 weak predictors were trained and then assembled into a strong predictor. In order to make sure that SM meets the demand of the accuracy in our study, we use the hold-out cross validation to validate the training results. In this study, when the *ER* value in SM-I is less than 10% and the *RMSE* value in SM-II is less than 5% of the crucial range, the training of the two-stage surrogate model is completed.
