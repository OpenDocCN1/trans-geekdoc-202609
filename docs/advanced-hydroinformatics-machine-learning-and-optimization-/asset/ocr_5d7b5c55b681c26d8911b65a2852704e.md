The determination of the parameters and bounds for the chosen distributions was based on the available data (the hydrograph of 2007), with final selection as follows:

```
Q_{b1} = N(597,25); Q_{b2} = N(1340,25); (14.7)
Q_{p1} = Q_{p2} = U(6850,7950); (14.8)
```

where, N is the notation of normal distribution; U is the notation of uniform distribution; other terms are same as in equation (14.6). The period of hydrographs is the same as the historical record, from 24 June to 8 September.

The sampling method used in this study is Latin Hypercube Sampling (LHS) (Swiler & Wyss, 2004), which is able to sample parameters more diversely with the same quantity of sampling numbers compared with some frequently used methods such as Monte Carlo Sampling (Kapelan et al., 2005; Kang et al., 2009; Jung et al., 2013). Because each experiment of multi-objective optimization with one hydrograph would take nearly 1 day, considering time saving, the number of samples needs to be relatively small but still large enough to cover the uncertainty spectrum. In this study, it was set at 100 samples.

After the LHS, an additional operation was performed in which the flood volume of each hydrograph was modified to a value that is approximately the same as the available real hydrographs (from 2007). This was done by changing the value of the skewness parameter. This operation was introduced mainly because in the analysis the same flood volume needs to be distributed over the four storage areas and the main river. Too varying patterns (including variation of flood volume) result in some unrealistic hydrograph shapes and introduce difficulties in analyzing the effect of the synthetic hydrograph parameters to results (Zhou, 2019). That means the uncertainty studied in this research is restricted to the condition that the flood volume of all sampled hydrographs during the considered period is the same (a restriction that could be addressed in future research).

These 100 sampled hydrographs were used to develop robust strategies and in the similarity selection method for real-time operation, as will be elaborated in the subsequent sections. However, the performance assessment of the so produced strategies in different conditions needs to be carried out with hydrographs that are different from sampled ones and with different characteristics of severity.
