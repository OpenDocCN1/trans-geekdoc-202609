The parameter set with the best performance in the 10 sets mentioned above is denoted as P_SM and the compromise parameter set given by VIC is denoted as P_VIC. Simulated daily streamflow compared with observation in the LJR basin and the XJR basin during the calibration and validation periods are displayed in Figure 3.11 and Figure 3.12, respectively. The streamflow simulated with P_SM and P_VIC is compared with the observation, respectively. The left panels of Figure 3.11 and Figure 3.12 are the hydrographs of the study station in the two study areas, and the right panels show the empirical CDFs of the simulated streamflow and the observation in each station. The simulated streamflow with P_VIC was used as the baseline. It illustrates that the empirical CDF distributions of the simulation of P_SM coincides well with those of P_VIC. Compared with the empirical CDF distributions of the observations, the empirical CDF distributions of the simulations of P_SM also show good performances in the two study areas.

Figure 3.13 and Figure 3.14 show the annual average runoff depth calculated with P_SM and P_VIC (the left map and the middle map) and the difference of them (P_SM minus P_VIC, the right map), respectively. As shown in Figure 3.13a, the runoff depths of P_SM and P_VIC are almost the same and most grid cells in the map of P_SM-P_VIC are around 0. Figure 3.13b and Figure 3.14 are similar to Figure 3.13a. The figures indicate that the results of the parameter sets acquired by SM agree well with those of the parameter sets acquired by the VIC model.
