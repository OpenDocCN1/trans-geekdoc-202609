Previous studies have investigated optimal strategies for storage area gates operation in this study area. Jonoski et al. (2018) identified optimal solutions using a coupled multi-objective optimization algorithm and a hydrodynamic model, while Jonoski et al. (2019) updated the objective function formulations and the geometric setting of hydrodynamic model (to include 2-D analysis of the flooding) in order to make the coupled simulation-optimization model more realistic. These previous studies were both based on a particular flood event of 2007, from 24 June to 7 September. The research presented here builds on these existing studies, and further develops storage area operation strategies by taking uncertainty of inflow into consideration.

When it comes to dealing with flooding uncertainty, two types of methods are often considered. One effective way is to develop robust solutions that are able to cope with a large spectrum of uncertain conditions, still resulting in relatively small or manageable impact. Another approach is to make real-time decisions regarding strategy of actions (e.g., gate operations of flood storage areas) when certain flood-generating inflow is forecasted, which could guarantee that the selected strategy would be effective or even optimal for the given situation.
