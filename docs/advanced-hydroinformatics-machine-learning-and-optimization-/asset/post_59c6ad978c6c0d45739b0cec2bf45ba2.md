## 8.7. Operational MLP-ANN Forecast Model

The trained MLP-ANNs models can be used with real-time observation as guidance for the flood situation in Grande de San Miguel catchment. Many forecast systems can be used as an environmental decision support system in order to organize the complex process of data acquisition in real time and link the model with these hydrometeorological data.

According to Werner et al. (2013) two categories of forecast systems can be defined: a model-centric and a modular approach. The former basically means that any change in the model or in the data used may require a redesign and redevelopment of the system. The latter is a more flexible approach, which can adapt to changing needs in modeling requirement and data transformation. After training and validating the MLP-ANN, it was decided to place the developed MLP-ANN models in operational mode using a model-centric approach since the Ministry of Environment and Natural Resources of El Salvador does not have any modular forecast platform (e.g., Delft-FEWS). The conceptual design of the forecast system is shown in Figure 8.11 where all the real-time rainfall and water level in the Grande de San Miguel catchment are imported from MARN Database using a MATLAB routine.
