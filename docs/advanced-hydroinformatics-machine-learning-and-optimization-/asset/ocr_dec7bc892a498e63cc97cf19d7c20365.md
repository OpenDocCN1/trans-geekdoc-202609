The LJR is located on the upper reach of the Qiantang River basin in Zhejiang Province in the southeastern China on the west coast of the Pacific Ocean. The LJR basin covers an area of about 1.8 × 10⁴ km² controlled by the Lanxi station. The basin is a humid region with annual average precipitation of about 1,600 mm and annual average temperature about 17°C, dominated by the maritime air masses in summer and the continental air masses in winter. Rainfall in the basin is concentrated at the turn of spring and summer and the high flow occurs during the plum rains from May to July. Besides the plum rains, the typhoon in summer also influences the hydrologic regime in the LJR basin, sometimes resulting in catastrophic floods.

The XJR is located in a humid region in southern China, about 856 km long, which is one of the largest tributaries of the Yangtze River. The river flows into the Dongting Lake, the second largest freshwater lake in middle China. The area of the basin above the Xiangtan station is about 8.2 × 10⁴ km².
