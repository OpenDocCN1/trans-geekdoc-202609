# 9.5.4. Other Data-Driven Models: Least Median Square, Model Tree (M5P), Pace Regression, and Linear Regressions

Four different DDM were tested, these being (1) least median square, (2) model tree (M5P), (3) pace regression, and (4) linear regressions. Results can be visualized in Figures 9.14 and 9.15. The charts are divided into the training, validation, and testing sets for all decomposition levels. It can be seen that for different DDMs, data preprocessing with wavelet analysis is always improving the model skills, however, only for certain decomposition levels. This is an indicator that wavelet subseries can play an important role in the modeling process, reflected by the improved results that must be cautiously found in the decomposition space at the expense of greater computational resources. Among models, the coupled wavelet-M5P (DDM 2) seems to consistently provide the best results and is quite comparable with the performance of the coupled WANN model done in the previous section. In second are the methods (3) and (4) with wavelet as preprocessor, presenting similar performances in all data sets. Finally, method (1) had the lowest performance within the studied models, clearly showing that the problem to solve cannot be assumed with linear behavior and is negligibly improved by the wavelet subseries.
