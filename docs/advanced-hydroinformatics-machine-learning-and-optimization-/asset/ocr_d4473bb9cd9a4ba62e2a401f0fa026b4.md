Next, we employ the stochastic calibration procedure to fit the model on the basis of synthetic series of 2,000 yr length by also employing SEEAS, and then we use the full sample of observations for validation. The generation of synthetic data is conducted for the rainfall and runoff, by using the recently introduced anySim package (Tsoukalas et al., 2020), which is in accordance with the specifications discussed in section 2.3. Regarding the PET inputs, we simply apply the mean monthly values of the historical sample. We remark that the use of a surrogate-based approach within the search procedure, which is subject to the computationally expensive hydrological model as a result of the long synthetic data, strongly facilitates the derivation of good solutions in reasonable time. The optimized parameter values against the synthetic rainfall-runoff data are quite different with respect to the ones derived by calibrating against the half of historical data, namely S_max = 112 mm, κ = 0.220, λ = 0.533, and μ = 0.511. Under this premise, the model exhibits slightly better performance over the full historical data, since the NSE value is 0.775.
