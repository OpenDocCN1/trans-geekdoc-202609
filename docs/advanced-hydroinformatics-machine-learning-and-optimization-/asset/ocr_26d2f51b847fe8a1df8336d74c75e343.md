Concerning the localization of precipitation objects within the Jubones basin, centroid occurrence appeared to be unaffected by any physical attribute that could be derived for the basin (i.e., altitude, land use, etc.). It is interesting that no hotspot of precipitation occurrence was detected for the Jubones basin (see Fig. 6.7). # 6.4.3. Classification of Precipitation Events Associated With Extreme Hydrologic Events

The combination of duration and extension thresholds of 7 hr and 50 km² served to define four precipitation classes. We determined 24 extreme hydrological events for the LSE precipitation class, 5 for the LLE, 7 for the SEE, and 10 for the SLE. Figure 6.8 depicts the visual discrimination between precipitation classes, from which it is apparent that the majority of extreme hydrological events occurred as a result of short duration and spatial local (LSE) precipitation events, and long duration and spatially extensive events (SLE).
