## 8.1 INTRODUCTION

### 8.1.1 Internet of Things (IoT)

Internet-connected devices are replacing or integrating other technologies, making the system faster, more efficient, and smarter. These are only possible with an IoT network and devices. At the moment, IoT technology is being adopted in every industry sector as well as in everyday life. IoT is a combination of two technologies, the internet and smart devices, and its importance makes it a considerable future technology of the digital world [[1]](#chapter8.xhtml#ref8_1). IoT is being used as a key tool for the development of smart cities, in which all resources are connected to the internet through smart devices for better services. A continued evaluation of the IoT technology is required to understand the shared information of the user and their devices, as well as the architecture and advanced technology for that, so the information can be transmitted seamlessly [[1]](#chapter8.xhtml#ref8_1).
