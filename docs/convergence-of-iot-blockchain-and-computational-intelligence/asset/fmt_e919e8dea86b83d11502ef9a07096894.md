
### 3. System for alerting emergency vehicles

An in-vehicle e-call is generated manually by the occupant(s) of the vehicle, automatically by the activation of the in-vehicle sensors following an accident, or both. The electronic call sends voice and data simultaneously to the closest emergency location (usually the public safety response point). The voice contact enables the occupants of the vehicle(s) to speak with the qualified e-call operator while also providing the e-call operator with the minimal amount of information necessary for them to take the necessary action, such as the vehicle's identity, the details of the accident, the date of the incident, the precise location, and the path of travel.
