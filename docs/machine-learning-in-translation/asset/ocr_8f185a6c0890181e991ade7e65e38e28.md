The second line of thought involves two different activities in MT assessment: evaluation and editing. These two areas can be a source of confusion among practitioners, as evaluation can be a side product of MT editing, including both post-editing and editing while translating (as shown in interactive MT). When editing is involved, 'quality' can be defined according to how much post-editing effort is needed. There is a difference between reference translations completed with and without the influence of MT.
