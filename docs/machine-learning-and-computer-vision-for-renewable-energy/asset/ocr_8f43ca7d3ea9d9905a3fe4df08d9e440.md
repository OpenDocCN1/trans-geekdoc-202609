# CONCLUSION

Green energy offers a more ecologically friendly substitute for many of the energy sources available now, suggesting that it is a part of the world’s future. These readily replenishable energy sources are good for the environment, help create jobs, and seem to be economically viable in the long run. In actuality, fossil fuels are not a sustainable energy source and should be gradually phased out of our energy mix. We can build diverse green energy sources that don’t harm the environment in which we all live, and thereby produce a truly sustainable energy supply in the future. It is imperative to use renewable energy for preserving, enhancing, and maintaining the state of the environment globally. Complete transition from fossil fuel-based, non-renewable energy to renewable energy is necessary. They come with a host of safety advantages that are good for the environment and the people who use renewable resources. That is only possible if certain problems are resolved. These challenges include the public’s acceptability, government rules, political pressures, old infrastructure, expensive plant building costs, surplus capacity, and corporate dominance.
