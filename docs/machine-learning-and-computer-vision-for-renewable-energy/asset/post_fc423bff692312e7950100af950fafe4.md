### 8. CONCLUSION

In this paper a general analytical survey was done on publicly available OCR based handwritten datasets. Starting from NIST and MNIST other popular available datasets are discussed which are important to develop the automated systems of optical character recognition. Handwritten characters are read properly and the computerized softcopy will be prepared based on that. These dataset are also important for feature extraction and classification of images of different languages like English, Devanagari, Bengali, Arabic etc. Future computing will heavily rely on handwritten character recognition. It's essential to have handwritten character recognition since it may be used to automate a variety of tasks, including bank receipts, which might be laborious. In order to achieve optimal efficiency, we can apply a spellchecker to the output and apply cleaning algorithms to the input photos to improve accuracy. Separate characters provide good accuracy, although differing writing styles have an impact on word recognition. Since the classifier must deal with a finite number of variations, good accuracy is seen in cases when the word scope is restricted to fixed numbers. In this research we effectively use unsupervised feature learning for a deployable application to identify numbers in natural settings. Although the primary benefits are demonstrated of learning representations over manually created ones, we have also discovered that there is still tremendous space for development of computer performance on our recently introduced tasks greatly trails human performance.
