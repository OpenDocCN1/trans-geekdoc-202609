# Projected Primary Energy Consumption in India

The demand for energy is significantly influenced by a nation’s population and rate of expansion. As of January 2019, India has 1.368 billion people living in it, making it the second most populous nation in the world (TWI-Global, n.d.). It makes up about 17.74 percent of the world’s population and grows at a rate of 1.18 percent every year. By the end of 2020, 2030, 2040, and 2050, the population of the nation is projected to be 1.383 billion, 1.512 billion, 1.605 billion, and 1.658 billion, respectively. The world’s largest immigration source each year is India; in fact, the population of several of its states exceeds that of several other countries.

By 2040, India will have the fastest-growing energy consumption of any large nation. The majority of this demand will be met by coal, with renewable energy coming in second. By 2020, renewable energy has become the second most significant domestic power generation source, surpassing both gas and then oil.
