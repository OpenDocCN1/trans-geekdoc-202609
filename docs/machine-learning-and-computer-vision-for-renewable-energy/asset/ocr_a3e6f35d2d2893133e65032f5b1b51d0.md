# About the Contributors

**Jayecta Majumdar** earned her Doctorate in Philosophy in 2022 in Computer Science and Engineering. She is currently employed as an Associate Professor in the department of Computer Science and Engineering, Haldia Institute of Technology, Haldia, West Bengal, India. She has more than 15 years of teaching. In numerous national and international journals, conferences, and book chapters, she has published research articles/papers. She is currently conducting research in the fields of Cryptography & Network Security, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Artificial Intelligence.

**Katleho Makatjane** is a trained quantitative business and risk analyst with more than four years of experience. His research interest is in data mining with applications to business and finance. In particular, he specializes in Statistical methods used for quantitative business and risk analysis, financial engineering and extreme value theory. He also specializes in the area of causal inference in business and finance.

**Ntebo Moroke** earned her Ph.D in Statistics from North West University in 2014. She joined the faculty in 2002 as a junior lecturer. Her areas of research are on using Multivariate and Time Series Data Analysis methods to analyse real-world high-dimensional data. She has published a number of papers in accredited peer-reviewed journals as well as book chapters. Her ability to execute research has garnered her numerous accolades in the field of statistic.

**Anshit Mukherjee** is the student pursuing BTech in computer sciences and Engineering from Abacus Institute of Engineering and Management.

**Neeta Nathani** is a Professor and Head of the Department in Electronics and Communication Engineering in Gyan Ganga Institute of Technology and Sciences, Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh. She received the Graduate degree in Electronics and Telecommunication and M.Tech. in Digital Communication from Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya, Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. She has completed her Ph.D. in Wireless Communication under faculty of Electronics Engineering from Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University, Nagpur, Maharashtra. She is a member of CSI, IETE and IEEE. She has organised many national and international conferences and seminar. She received Outstanding Paper Award on “Assessment of Quasi-Permanently Vacant Channels in Mobile Communication Bands for Cognitive Radio” in the 15th IEEE International Conference on Advanced Communications Technology (ICACT-2013) in Phoenix Park, South Korea. With a teaching experience of over 17 years, she has international and national paper publications to her credit.
