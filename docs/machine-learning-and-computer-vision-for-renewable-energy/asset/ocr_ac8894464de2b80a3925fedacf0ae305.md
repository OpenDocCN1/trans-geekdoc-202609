The nonlinear trend for the hourly contracted electricity demand time series data shows irregular cycles instead of regular seasonality. The peaks are sometimes smaller or larger. Thus, modelling this time series data requires the inclusion of the lag features to allow our forecasters to react dynamically to the changing conditions. This study includes lag variables as explanatory variables to allow varying amounts of the recent history into the forecast. The results of the extracted nonlinear trend are shown in Figure 7.
