
## About the Contributors

**Jayecta Majumdar** earned her Doctorate in Philosophy in 2022 in Computer Science and Engineering. She is currently employed as an Associate Professor in the department of Computer Science and Engineering, Haldia Institute of Technology, Haldia, West Bengal, India. She has more than 15 years of teaching. In numerous national and international journals, conferences, and book chapters, she has published research articles/papers. She is currently conducting research in the fields of Cryptography & Network Security, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, and Artificial Intelligence.

**Katleho Makatjane** is a trained quantitative business and risk analyst with more than four years of experience. His research interest is in data mining with applications to business and finance. In particular, he specializes in Statistical methods used for quantitative business and risk analysis, financial engineering and extreme value theory. He also specializes in the area of causal inference in business and finance.

**Ntebo Moroke** earned her Ph.D in Statistics from North West University in 2014. She joined the faculty in 2002 as a junior lecturer. Her areas of research are on using Multivariate and Time Series Data Analysis methods to analyse real-world high-dimensional data. She has published a number of papers in accredited peer-reviewed journals as well as book chapters. Her ability to execute research has garnered her numerous accolades in the field of statistics.

**Anshit Mukherjee** is the student pursuing BTech in computer sciences and Engineering from Abacus Institute of Engineering and Management.

**Neeta Nathani** is a Professor and Head of the Department in Electronics and Communication Engineering in Gyan Ganga Institute of Technology and Sciences, Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh. She received the Graduate degree in Electronics and Telecommunication and M.Tech. in Digital Communication from Rajiv Gandhi Proudyogiki Vishwavidyalaya, Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh. She has completed her Ph.D. in Wireless Communication under faculty of Electronics Engineering from Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University, Nagpur, Maharashtra. She is a member of CSI, IETE and IEEE. She has organised many national and international conferences and seminars. She received Outstanding Paper Award on “Assessment of Quasi-Permanently Vacant Channels in Mobile Communication Bands for Cognitive Radio” in the 15th IEEE International Conference on Advanced Communications Technology (ICACT-2013) in Phoenix Park, South Korea. With a teaching experience of over 17 years, she has international and national paper publications to her credit.

**Sergiy Plankovskyy** is originally from Volodymyr-Volynsky, Ukraine. He has 35 years of international experience in the research and development of processing with intense energy fluxes (mainly technologies using plasma, laser, and detonation sources). After being Head of the Faculty of Aircraft Engineering at the National Aerospace University “Kharkiv Aviation Institute”, he is now Head of the School of Energy, Information and Transport Infrastructure at the O.M. Beketov National University of Urban Economy in Kharkiv, Ukraine. He has over 200 refereed publications and has supervised 11 Ph.D. students to successful completion. His research interests cover innovation technologies, including the methods of manufacturing for the digital industry. He is a member of the Expert Council on Mechanical Engineering of the National Agency for Higher Education Quality Assurance (NAQA), Ukraine.

**Vladyslav Pliuhin** is the Head of the Department of Urban Electrical Energy Supply and Consumption Systems at the O.M. Beketov National University of Urban Economy in Kharkiv (Ukraine). He received his Ph.D. degree in Electrical Machines and Apparatus from Donetsk National University (Ukraine) in 2004 and a D.Sc. degree in Electrical Machines and Apparatus from National Technical University “Kharkiv Polytechnic Institute” (Ukraine) in 2016. He had an international traineeship as a Post-Doctorate at the Czech Technical University in Prague (Czech Republic) in 2012. He is an expert of the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine for examining research and development projects of the thematic direct “Energy and Energy Efficiency”, and a member of the Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine Council for examining Ph.D. and D.Sc. thesis. His research activities are mainly focused on Electrical Machines Design and Optimization, CAD Software Development, Energy Efficiency, Machine Learning, and Digital Twins.

**Pampa Saha** is working as assistant professor in Brainware University. She is working in research also.

**Modisane Bennett Seitshiro** graduated PhD in Business Mathematics and Informatics under the centre for Business Mathematics and Informatics at the North-West University, South Africa in 2020. He was permanently employed by Standard Bank Group, Corporate and Investment Banking - Market Risk for 4 years as a Quantitative Analyst and Market Risk Manager for 2 years until 2012. In 2013, Modisane started a permanent lecturing position in the Department of Mathematical Statistics, North-West University. Dr Seitshiro is currently a Senior Lecturer at the Centre for Business Mathematics and Informatics at the North-West University. His research interests are Quantitative Risk Management, Statistical Learning and Financial Time Series Analysis. He is involved with teaching and learning, industry related postgraduate supervision and projects. He is passionate about enhancing the discipline of Mathematical Sciences in South Africa, especially in breaching the gap between industry and academic research.

**Yevgen Tsegelynk** is a Deputy Head (Research) at the School of Energy, Information and Transport Infrastructure of the O.M. Beketov National University of Urban Economy in Kharkiv (Ukraine). He received his Ph.D. degree in Aerospace Engineering from the National Aerospace University “Kharkiv Aviation Institute” (Ukraine) in 2010. **Diteboho Xaba** is an emerging researcher and lecturer at the University of South Africa (Unisa) in Pretoria, South Africa. He pursued a Ph.D. in Operations Research and Statistics because he was eager to learn more about the relationship between data and decision-making. His doctoral research explored innovative approaches to modelling BRICS exchange rates using MRS-GARCH models with various estimation algorithms, a critical area in operations research. He is a researcher who has written and published academic journal papers as well as presented research papers at national and international conferences. He has published a book chapter from his Ph.D. work, which is being published by Springer Nature. Dr. Xaba is interested in research fields such as operations research, statistics, and applied econometrics. Dr. Xaba made contributions to statistics and applied econometrics by developing models that captured the nonlinear dynamics of economic variables. His works, published in reputable journals, demonstrated an understanding of economic systems, incorporating behavioural factors, and addressing challenges posed by volatile markets.
