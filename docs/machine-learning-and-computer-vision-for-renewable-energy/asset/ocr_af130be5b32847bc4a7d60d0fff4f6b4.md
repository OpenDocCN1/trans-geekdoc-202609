# Discussion, Recommendation, and Conclusion

Precise long-term predictions for electricity demand are vital for strategic planning and decision-making within power utility companies and governmental bodies that oversees energy security. These forecasts impact key aspects such as determining the appropriate generation capacity, maintenance of scheduling, selection of an optimal energy mix, and planning future expansions of infrastructure. According to Conteh et al. (2023), forecasts ensure that resources are efficiently allocated, preventing either excess capacity or shortages. Moreover, the forecasts guide economic planning, influence policy formulation, and facilitate the implementation efficiency initiatives for energy. Overall, these predictions play a fundamental role in fostering a reliable, sustainable, and economically sound energy supply (Aziz et al., 2023).
