The cause of this decline might be the inevitable result of the chaos inside Eskom, as its fleet of mostly aging power stations is hit by maintenance problems, sabotage, and corruption. It is worth revealing that Minerals and Energy (1998) predicted that Eskom would run out of electrical power reserves by 2007 unless action is taken to prevent it. To improve power supply and reliability, it was recommended that Eskom be restructured into separate electricity generation and power transmission businesses. Despite these warnings and requests by Eskom to be allowed to increase capacity, the national government took no action. Hence the current decline in electricity demand (Phaahla, 2023). Due to a reduction in electricity production, imported electricity went up markedly in 2014 by 18.6% compared with 2013.
