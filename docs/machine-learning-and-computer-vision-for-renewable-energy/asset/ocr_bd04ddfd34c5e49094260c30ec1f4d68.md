# ABSTRACT

Green energy deployment in any country has the primary goal of assisting economic development, improving energy security, increasing energy access, and combating global climate change. Sustainable development can be achieved through utilizing renewable energy sources and ensuring inhabitants’ access to affordable, reliable, and modern electricity. The term “green energy” refers to energy produced by natural resources such as sunshine, wind, and water. Environmentally hazardous mining and drilling operations are typically avoided by green providers. The advancement of technologies such as automation, additive manufacturing, artificial intelligence (AI), and others can facilitate the shift to a cleaner future by improving environmental quality and controlling and monitoring infrastructures through the replacement of outdated equipment with more advanced options.
