Renewable energy sources such as heat, light, and wind are infinite, but their power must be conserved for long-term usage. Smart grids, which are managed by IoT infrastructures, are used in smart city environments for optimal power management and distribution. As a result, the integration of grid management with IoT uses information and communication technologies to improve power management operational efficiency in smart cities. Carbon dioxide (CO2) emissions must be reduced in order to achieve sustainable development goals, protect the environment, address climate change, and preserve biodiversity.
