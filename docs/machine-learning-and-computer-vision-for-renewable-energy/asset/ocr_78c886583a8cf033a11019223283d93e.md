# UTILIZING MACHINE LEARNING FOR PREDICTING RENEWABLE ENERGY GENERATION

Renewable energy is an environmentally advantageous and sustainable energy source. The utilisation of carbon-neutral technology is of utmost importance in the efforts to address climate change and has emerged as a significant alternative to fossil fuels. The utilisation of energy often presents a diverse array of attributes, leading to inherent uncertainty. The core phases of machine learning are acquiring and preparing data, identifying and extracting features, selecting a model, and validating the model. For this inquiry, a total of 40 recent papers were collected.

The pie chart seen in Figure 1 illustrates the distribution of seven distinct categories of renewable energy. The literature of this study reveals that solar and wind combined constitute around 50 percent of the sectors, whereas the remaining five sectors collectively contribute less than five percent. It is the purpose of this section to examine the various machine learning approaches used for predicting renewable energy.
