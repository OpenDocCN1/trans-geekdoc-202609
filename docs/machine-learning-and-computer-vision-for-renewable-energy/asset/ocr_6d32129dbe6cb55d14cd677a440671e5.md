+   1. **Turbine Generator Systems for Heat Recovery:** The finest turbine generator for you will have heat recovery power applications such as geothermal, solar thermal, ocean thermal energy conversion (OTEC), waste heat from engines, and industrial operations.
+   
+   2. **Wind Turbines and Compressors for Low Flow:** Many completely novel and extremely effective axial and radial impeller designs are now being development for application in both industrial and urban environments. As little as 5 mph of wind will be needed to start up and reach full efficiency with these little wind turbines. Turbine wind turbine technology is also being developed to lessen noise pollution and bird mortality. It is especially critical for offshore areas along migratory flight pathways to develop new blade forms that are safe for birds. A compressor is powered by a wind turbine in an additional wind-power cycle. There are occasions when compressed air is used to run a turbine generator (or another use).
+   
+   3. **Solar Turbines:** More recent and advanced solar technologies promise to use solar energy more effectively, efficiently, and practically for thermal applications such as absorption chillers or for electrical generation by using concentrated solar heat (at 400°F or higher) to power a steam or gas turbine. Increasing turbine and pump efficiency to reduce installed cost per watt is the main turbomachinery problem across all solar technologies and applications.
+   
+   4. **Hydroelectric Turbines:** Another fantastic potential is to develop innovative hydroelectric designs that can provide steady, predictable electricity from a range of low-head, low-power river flows that can vary greatly by location and season without the need for dams. Even sluggish moving streams can be used to power a highly efficient hydrokinetic turbine, which has an energy density 850 times that of wind. Low-flow underground streams and falling water are projected to benefit from the same turbo-green technologies. Underwater streams in ocean estuaries are a very reliable source of energy for driving a hydrazine turbine.
