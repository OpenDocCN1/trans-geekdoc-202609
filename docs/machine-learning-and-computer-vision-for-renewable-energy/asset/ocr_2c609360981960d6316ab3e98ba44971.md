Besides this, economically feasible open pond based microalgae cultivation has shown a considerable amount of biohydrogen production (1,239 kg/ h) with suitable market price ($10 kg-1) (Gholkar et al., 2021). One has to keep an eye on storage cost by lowering the production cost in case of direct biophotolysis. This can be further managed by controlling the conversion efficiency and biomass outcome (Jiménez-Llanos et al., 2020).

Design of certain bioreactors demands specific manufacturing materials as well as nutrients, which usually cover majority of expenditure. This may lead into high biohydrogen production cost (Show et al., 2018).

Microalgae biomass based biohydrogen production cost through supercritical and thermal gasification is still unfavorable as the cost seems to be fourfold higher than the already existing natural gas driven hydrogen production (Kumar et al., 2019).

High production costs are also caused by problems including differential proton gradients in thylakoid, disturbances in light capture, and inefficient photosynthetic processes (Eroglu et al., 2016).

If one effectively compare the capital cost for direct biophotolysis with photofermentation and dark fermentation, then it seems quite clear that the later one is economically unfavorable (Dincer et al., 2015; Bolatkhan et al., 2019; Peng et al., 2019; Limongi et al., 2021; Wang et al., 2021).

However, synthesis of biohydrogen characteristically costs more than other fuel alternates. Forthcoming research should concentrate on detailed assessment of amenity and operation perspective, budget of raw ingredients and operation process, light, meteorological conditions, and site-conditions, mixing of biomass, and product solidarity. Recycling waste metabolites and biomass can increase economic viability.

Traditional researches regarding AI comprises of data representation, structured planning, methodical learning, natural language processing, portraying perception, and aid for robotics.
