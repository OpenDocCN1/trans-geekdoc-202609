Present situation of mother earth positively supports to build environmentally-friendly and sustainable strategies for autonomous energy systems as was shown in a DL algorithms based supply-demand forecasting model system to mimic an independent hydrogen blended renewable grid (Hwangbo et al., 2019).

AI methods like non-dominated sorting genetic algorithm (NSGA) has also become popular for system optimization in case of short term hydrogen energy storage to fulfil the load demand (Beshr et al., 2018). However, the particle swarm optimization algorithm(PSO) is basically a simple and efficient swarm intelligence based technique which has commendably unveil the optimal proportion and energy designing of hydrogen storage system (HassanzadehFard et al., 2020). Improved ones can optimize the hydrogen fuel cell system for techno-economic, environmental and socio-political goals (Maleki et al., 2016; Eriksson et al., 2019). Here, artificial swarm optimization (ABSO) algorithm has proven its efficacy to optimize life span value and loss of power-supply probability in hydrogen based systems. Besides this, simulated annealing (SA) can be implemented to achieve an improved outcome in comparison to the outdated response surface methodology (RSM) system(Maleki et al., 2018). Cost optimization can be attained by employing a discrete harmony search algorithm (DHS) for successful size amelioration of fuel cell storage devices (Maleki et al., 2015). Additionally, a hybrid algorithms for optimization (HRES) has been applied to achieve minimum lifecycle cost for storage system and it portrayed acceptable robustness with lower index value in comparison to other methods (Zhang et al., 2018).

Currently, green hydrogen production (GHP) systems from water manufactories has focused on machine learning (ML) dependentforecasting and optimization of scaling up issues. They applied different relevant models to forecast and optimize the proton exchange membrane and dark fermentation methodologies to unveil the scaling up issues confirming a sustainable hydrogen economy (Kabir et al., 2023). Moreover, researchers from the KEPCO International Nuclear Graduate School have used a hydrogen economic evaluation programme to compute the manufacturing cost employing two types of small modular reactors evolved by the Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute and NuScale Power, LLC. They have unveiled the critical parameters like heat consumption and electricity rating that potentially influence production cost from a ML-based approach (Kim et al., 2022).
