There is a barrier presented by the restricted openness and interpretability of machine learning and deep learning models. There is a significant lack of transparency in a number of models, which makes it difficult to completely comprehend the fundamental concepts that are responsible for the predictive capabilities of these models. When it comes to the prediction of renewable energy utilizing machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) techniques, there are a lot of prospects for future research. It is necessary to conduct additional study in order to improve the creation of models that are capable of properly managing multiple sources of renewable energy simultaneously. The creation of models that are capable of efficiently managing the inherent uncertainty and variance that comes with sources of clean energy is another area of concentration that ought to be given priority. It would be helpful to incorporate weather information, network data, and other outside sources of information in order to increase the accuracy of forecasts for renewable energy.
