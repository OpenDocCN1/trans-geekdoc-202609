Chapter 8 deals with Green energy deployment in any country has the primary goal of assisting economic development, improving energy security, increasing energy access, and combating global climate change. Sustainable development can be achieved through utilizing renewable energy sources and ensuring inhabitants’ access to affordable, reliable, and modern electricity. The term “green energy” refers to energy produced by natural resources such as sunshine, wind, and water. Environmentally hazardous mining and drilling operations are typically avoided by green providers.

Chapter 10 deals with Blue Wind Energy, a pioneering concept, amalgamates offshore wind energy generation with cutting-edge technologies. There is a dire need to address the growing energy demands of urbanization while mitigating the environmental impact of conventional energy sources. Urban areas are substantial energy consumers, and traditional methods are both unsustainable and environmentally damaging. Blue Wind Energy, alongside AI and IoT integration, presents a compelling solution, but the challenge is in effectively implementing and optimizing these technologies to ensure sustainable, efficient, and eco-friendly energy management in cities.
