Within this chapter, we employ the enhanced GBDT-KF algorithm designed for time series forecasting. Our application focuses on predicting future wind power generation in South Africa, providing insights into the 24-hour wind power generation cycle. By incorporating a Kalman filter to eliminate the noise from the initial time series, we elevate the performance of the conventional GBDT. Notably, the GBDT-KF algorithm boasts a relatively low level of complexity when compared to neural network approaches like LSTM. This methodological choice underscores the efficiency and effectiveness of the algorithm in handling wind power generation time series forecasting.

# Future Research Directions

The practical application of our findings enables service providers to anticipate network congestion locations and times, allowing for proactive measures. This capability empowers them to address potential issues before they occur. Subsequent efforts should focus on incorporating additional factors into the time series prediction process for a more comprehensive analysis. Additionally, our future endeavours involve exploring alternative nonlinear filters with more intricate structures, supplementing the Kalman filter in the pre-processing stage. This approach aims to enhance the versatility and effectiveness of our predictive modelling framework.
