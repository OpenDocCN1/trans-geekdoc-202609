# Electricity Production Prediction by Microsoft Azure

In Moradzadeh et al. (2021) authors shows selected applications of machine learning and deep learning based on their learning, structure, mode of operation, and application in the load forecasting of power systems. To analyze the condition of power systems they used different prediction models based on linear regression, classification, and clustering. To analyze the behavior of power systems, advanced metering, and condition monitoring devices and systems are utilized, which generate a huge amount of data. This chapter is useful to understand practical application of ML in power energy field and males links with energy consumption and development of industry and technology systems.

In Kirubakaran et al. (2023) the hybrid electricity production was investigated to show how produce and use electrical energy from many sources as part of an integrated setup. This study optimizes geographic locations with various wind and sun renewable energy input potentials. Convolutional neural networks with long short-term memory are designed to maximize electricity production while consumer load, demand, and weather conditions are considering. This paper is close to current authors work and beside of all describes in details application of neural networks algorithms. Almost the same work but with more details in datasets creation and optimization metrics determinations is studied in Yang (2022). The main outcome of this research can support decision optimization for an ML based decision support system. The proposed system is very promising for potential applications in wind (power) energy management. This study optimizes three distinct geographic locations with various wind and sun renewable energy input potentials. Convolutional neural networks with long short-term memory are designed to maximize electricity production while taking consumer load, demand, and weather conditions into account. Finally, in Balal et al. (2023) was shown a study that utilizes various machine learning models to predict solar photovoltaic (PV) power generation in Lubbock, Texas. The developed machine learning models describes how to aid solar PV investors in streamlining their processes and improving their planning for the production of solar energy.

Despite the wide range of works devoted to the topic of machine learning, the analysis of trends in the consumption of electricity networks within the country has not been performed or has not been publicly announced. The authors of this work have already carried out research on the implementation of machine learning in the field of electrical engineering (Pliuhin et al., 2018; Pliuhin et al., 2019a; Pliuhin et al., 2019b; Sukhonos et al., 2023). The practical use of machine learning technology Azure Machine Learning (AML) is also embodied in the research works of master's scientists and in the topics of master's works of students of O.M. Beketov National University of Urban Economy in Kharkiv. The goal of the work performed by the students together with teachers was to develop an innovative approach to the optimization of electric machines and power industry facilities. In addition, a team of university researchers (consisting of two professors, two master's students, and two first-year graduate students) took part in Microsoft's worldwide competition “The Next AI Guardians” (Microsoft, 2020) with a project to introduce standard calculation methods into the database blocks of Microsoft AML Studio own modules, which are capable of fully automatic design of electrical machines with their further optimization by means of machine learning.
