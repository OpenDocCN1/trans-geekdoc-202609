## Prediction and Forecasting Performance of Hourly Electricity Demand Load

To train and test our base learners, we fixed the batch size to 32 and increased the number of epochs by a ratio of 0.2. Three criteria are used to perform a comparative analysis exercise which is done to find the model that best mimics the forecasting errors. These are namely mean absolute error (MAE), symmetric mean absolute percentage error (SMAPE), Mean Forecast Error (MFE), and mean square error for both in-sample and out-of-sample forecasting performance. The empirical results are shown in Table 3 from the perspectives of level forecasting and directional forecasting. Looking at the results reported in Table 3, the in-sample forecasts of the RNN give a small error when epochs are set at 50 this is because, out of the four metrics used, three have minimum values at this value of 50. However, in the out-of-sample forecasting, the model performed better when the epochs were increased to 250. The CNN has the minimum error when epochs are set at 250 in the in-sample forecasting but in out-of-sample forecasting, the model has minimum error when the epochs are at 10. The same analogy can be used for the LSTM. It is worth noting that our stacking ensemble did improve in our combined forecasting model. This indicates that as the number of epochs increases, both the base learners and meta-model improve the performance. Deep learning has become a prominent source in several sectors due to its superior performance, as far as we know. Thus, deep learning also produced positive results in this domain, as Table 3 illustrates. More recently, deep learning techniques have produced improved results, but in the process of modeling energy patterns, they are missing some crucial information, which indicates that model performance is not as good as it could be. We made this assumption throughout the literature review and developed an ensemble model to account for every feature, effortlessly model the energy pattern, and identify both spatial and spatiotemporal cues. We, therefore, suggest an ensemble deep learning technique and secure good results in comparison to other baseline methods because the performance of deep learning in the forecasting of time series issues is the inspiration for the construction of the ensemble framework.
