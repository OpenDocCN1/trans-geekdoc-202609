# 4.11 Scene Text

The KAIST scene text dataset consists of 3000 photos taken in various settings. This includes scenes both inside and outside in a variety of lighting settings such as bright artificial lights, nighttime and clear days. Either a high-resolution digital camera or a low-resolution cell phone camera was used to take the pictures. Text recognition on any shaped scene-text can be benchmarked using the Scene Text dataset. Natural photos with arbitrary shapes for scene text require models to do text recognition. With the help of about one million high-quality word annotations, Scene Text enables end-to-end reasoning to be applied to downstream activities like image captioning and visual question answering. Every image has had its size adjusted to 640x480. Language used to collect the scene text is used to categorize the KAIST scene text database (Jung et al., 2011).
