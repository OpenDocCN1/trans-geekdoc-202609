# Probabilistic Forecasting of Hourly Wind Power Load in South Africa

# CONCLUSION

This chapter used the enhanced gradient-boosted decision tree algorithm, based on the Kalman filter technique, to predict and forecast short-term hourly load wind power time series in South Africa. We study the potential of using all of the series for prediction rather than selecting the series from certain parts of the hierarchy. Furthermore, we see the hierarchical structure as a defining feature of the data generation process, and use it to instantaneously produce forecasts for each level of the hierarchy. This integrated technique employs the Kalman filter to directly handle hourly demand load dependencies such as products of complementarity and energy substitution, which are typically neglected. The results of this chapter showed our enhanced GBDT-KF algorithm is the perfect algorithm to predict short-term hourly wind power load in South Africa with prediction power of 92% in the training data and 90% on the test data.
