# Preface

The shortcomings of conventional methods for predicting renewable energy are becoming more and more obvious as the globe struggles to meet the pressing need for sustainable energy alternatives. More than ever, there is a need for more precise forecasts in the areas of net load forecasting, line loss forecasts, and the smooth integration of hybrid solar and battery storage systems. Advanced Artificial Intelligence approaches are developing as a potential solution to address this difficulty and have the potential to completely transform the renewable energy industry. In order to address the critical problems facing renewable energy systems, Machine Learning and Computer Vision for Renewable Energy offers a thorough examination of AI modelling, analysis, performance prediction, and control techniques.

The book focuses on the potentially revolutionary applications of Computer Vision in the field of Renewable Energy, moving from the challenges of energy prediction to the opportunities presented by cutting-edge technology. In the fight to improve sustainability in all sectors of the economy, Computer Vision technology comes out on top, gathering priceless information from digital images and videos. Better energy management, the ability to forecast variables influencing renewable energy, and the optimization of sustainability all benefit from this data. Readers will become thoroughly versed in the Artificial Intelligence techniques and computer vision methodologies that are essential to solving the problems that renewable energy systems encounter, including researchers, academics, and students. The field of Machine Learning and Computer Vision for Renewable Energy presents itself as the driving force behind this transition.

In addition to discussing the energy industry's current issues, the book provides information on how to create a more sustainable future. The goal is made evident by highlighting advances in Computer Vision and Artificial Intelligence: to propel social growth via innovation, research, and technological achievements in the field of Renewable Energy. This book will be an invaluable resource for academic researchers, educators, college students, and business executives who are interested in the convergence of digital transformation and renewable energy to navigate the opportunities and difficulties that lie ahead. This book, which offers a wide range of suggested readings, is a monument to the rapidly changing fields of artificial intelligence and computer vision, helping to shape the future of sustainable energy for future generations.

The following research topics are well covered in this book:

+   • AI Techniques in Renewable Energy
+   • Computer Vision in Renewable Energy
+   • Regression Methods in Computer Vision for Renewable Energy
+   • Solar Power Prediction with AI and Vision
+   • Technologies for Renewable Energy Sources
+   • Trends in Renewable Energy Research and Applications
