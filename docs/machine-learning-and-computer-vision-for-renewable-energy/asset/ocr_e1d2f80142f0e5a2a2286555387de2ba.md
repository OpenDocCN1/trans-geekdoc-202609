In a recent study, (Shivam et al.,2020) suggested utilizing Artificial Neural Networks (ANNs) to predict the optimal thickness of ARC coatings based on solar cell-generated voltage and current. The NN-based approach exhibited accuracy and increased speed compared to FDTD simulations in predicting optical absorption concerning incident light wavelength and cell geometry. ANTUM solar cells based on p-type Czochralski Silicon (Cz-Si) wafer. Their objectives were to understand the global hourly time trends of solar cell efficiency in a 1-week dataset and the impact of different features of solar cell production on the temporal evolution of performance. The training data, collected from 500,000 solar cells through inline measurements over one week, encompassed 329 features grouped using hierarchical clustering. The ML model demonstrated high prediction accuracy for solar cell efficiency over time, indicating its potential application in real production lines.
