# Applications of Renewable Energy Sources and Sustainable Innovations

renewable energy market is becoming more favorable over time, more has to be done to promote the wider usage of these sources. Focusing on building the technology and infrastructure needed to capture renewable energy sources, such wind turbines, dams, and levees, is essential. It is also essential to keep the marine and aquatic environments clean and healthy if you want to get the most out of hydropower. The market for these technologies will expand and public acceptance of them will increase with the encouragement and promotion of solar panel installation in residential buildings. The development and utilization of renewable energy must be given top priority, particularly in light of the increasing awareness of the harmful environmental effects linked to the industrial process that uses fossil fuels. The need for sustainable energy development is growing rapidly on a worldwide scale. Widespread usage of renewable energy is necessary to attain sustainability in the energy sectors of both developed and developing countries.
