# LITERATURE REVIEWS

The research trends in the identification of renewable source of energy using the machine learning principle has been of great demand. Number of research works has been performed producing overall good outcomes. Many research work are going on right now producing many different directions of new possibilities with robust future prospects. Some of the work and their discussions along with their score on the achieved accuracy values has been covered in the following part of the section.

(Zhu et al.,2020) employed ML techniques to analyze key variables affecting the performance of Cu(In1–x Gax)Se2 (CIGS) solar cells and the underlying correlations. They selected 15 input features, including CGI, GGI ratio, CIGS layer thickness, highest substrate temperature during fabrication, fabrication method, alkali treatment, substrate type, among others. Random Forest (RF) performed the best in predicting CIGS solar cell efficiency. According to RF results, alkali post-deposition treatment (PDT), CIGS thickness, buffer layer thickness, CGI, and GGI gradient were identified as the most significant features influencing device performance. The study recommended a co-evaporation fabrication method for CIGS with CdS as the buffer layer. It also suggested an ideal CIGS thickness of 2-2.3 micrometers, a substrate temperature range of 540-600oC, and CGI and GGI ratios adjusted within specified ranges for highly efficient CIGS solar cells. (Salman et al.,2020) developed an Artificial Neural Network (ANN)-based ML model to extract Cu doping profiles resulting from the diffusion annealing and cool-down process in the fabrication sequence of CdTe solar cells. Using a dataset generated by the PVRD-FASP solver, they aimed to mimic the Cu diffusion process with the ANN ML model.
