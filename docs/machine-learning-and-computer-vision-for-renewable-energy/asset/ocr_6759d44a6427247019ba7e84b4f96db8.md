# CONCLUSION

The renewable source of energy in the current paradigm of energy crisis has become the great relief
path towards finding direction of sources. As the natural source of energy are gradually dwindling with
time and increasing number of populations, the thought for alternative means has been realized. The
renewable source of energy can fulfill the demands and easily overcome such problem of shortfall. Out
of many different renewable sources of energies, the solar power energy has become the great and reli-
able source of energy and can easily become the alternative mean for all other sources of energies. The
source of such energy has been found with the help of different models. The machine learning model
out of them has gained much potential response in assessing the source of such energies using different
types of optimizing algorithms. The detailed analytical approach has been done in the specified chapter.
The regression analysis has also been done to develop a predictive model for precision prediction of the
types of different energy sources. The concept of such machine learning modeling can be extended also
in future to tap some potential energy sources other than the conventional ones with greater possibility
of usage and utilization.
