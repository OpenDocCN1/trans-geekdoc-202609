Addressing this challenge, the increased utilization of sustainable energy sources, particularly solar and wind power, presents a financially viable trajectory in alignment with the climate objectives outlined in the Paris Agreement. Nevertheless, the pace at which renewable energy adoption has progressed has struggled to keep pace with the continually expanding energy demands, resulting in a limited share of total energy derived from renewable sources (Singh, 2023). Consequently, expediting the shift to sustainable energy sources becomes imperative. Facilitating this transition necessitates the development of energy technologies, infrastructure, and policies that support and encourage the capture, storage, conversion, and effective management of renewable energy (Dai et al., 2016).

The careful selection of acceptable materials, particularly those used in photovoltaic applications, is critical in the pursuit of sustainable energy solutions. Following that, these selected materials must be synthesized with high yield and quality to match the standards for devices such as solar panels. The typical timescale for the materials discovery process is 15-20 years, implying plenty of room for improvement. Furthermore, the devices must be refined for durability and repeatability in order to fit easily into energy systems such as solar farms. To maintain economic viability, this integration demands effective control of energy use and generation patterns (Elavarasan et al., 2020).

# 4. SUSTAINABLE URBANIZATION AND ENERGY DEMAND-IMPORTANCE OF TRANSITIONING TO GREEN ENERGY SOURCES

As the severity of global warming and climate change increases, as evidenced by frequent and drastic changes in the natural environment affecting humans and other living beings around the world, international organizations such as the United Nations Organization, the G20, the G8, and individual nations become more aware of the environmental consequences of extensive fossil fuel consumption. This understanding emphasizes the economic and societal implications of climate change, particularly global warming (Zwane et al., 2022). The United Nations, notably through its climate change-focused environmental program, has continually worked to encourage greater international agreement. This agreement focuses on the need to meet an overall global carbon reduction target within the period given by the Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC). The goal is to gain international consensus that each country should have some responsibility for attaining the carbon emission reduction objective (Mohammadian, 2022).

Renewable energy and energy efficiency stand out as critical components of the transition to a low-consumption, low-carbon society. To make this transition easier, government policies should prioritize achieving region-specific objectives for renewable energy generation and energy efficiency. However, these objectives range between metropolitan regions, and there is a lack of comprehensive understanding of the link between energy transition and urbanization levels (Amran et al., 2020). The emphasis on growing and safeguarding energy in the current global context is around increasing energy producing capacity and, ideally, adding cleaner and more environmentally friendly energy sources into the growth process. Through a complex system, advancement and planning procedures frequently cause changes in the landscape and have a human influence on the surroundings (Hainsch et al., 2022). Resource extraction and land management operations in the building of energy infrastructure, as well as human monitoring of these systems, can have long-term and often unexpected implications. Despite increased interest in alternative energy sources, many developing countries continue to rely largely on fossil fuels for energy generation (Atalay et al., 2016).
