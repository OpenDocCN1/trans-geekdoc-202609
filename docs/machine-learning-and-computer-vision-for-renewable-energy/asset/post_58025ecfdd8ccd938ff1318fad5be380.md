## 2.2. Grid Integration

Integrating energy from renewable sources into existing power grids poses technical and operational challenges. Figure 8 shows the smart grid. Research is focused on evolving smart grid technologies, enhancing grid reliability, and optimizing the arrangement of various power sources. Addressing the disparity between power supply and demand demands innovative solutions in power forecasting and grid management (Croisier et al., 2008) (Lathi et al., 2020) (Rego et al., 2012).

Grid integration is a critical facet of the transition to a sustainable and low-carbon power system. As the globe strives to reduce its dependence on hydrocarbon deposits, energy from renewable sources such as solar and wind play a pivotal role. However, merging these variable and irregular sources into the existing power grid poses various challenges. This review surveys the key issues associated with grid integration of renewable energy, explains various strategies to address these challenges, and provides insights into the future prospects of a seamlessly integrated renewable energy grid.

## 2.3. Environmental Impact

While renewable energy is mainly considered environmentally friendly, some technologies can have material impacts. Hydropower projects may disrupt ecosystems, and the use of bioenergy raises concerns about land use and biodiversity. Research focuses on reducing these impacts through sustainable practices, habitat restoration, and the development of environmentally friendly technologies (Fosket et al., 2009) (Chambers et al., 2013) (Mikkelsen et al., 2015).

Renewable energy sources have emerged as a promising solution to address the environmental challenges posed by conventional hydrocarbon deposits. This paper provides an in-depth analysis of the environmental impact of various renewable energy technologies, including solar, wind, hydropower, and geothermal energy. By examining each technology’s lifecycle, resource extraction, production, operation, and decommissioning, we aim to present a comprehensive understanding of their environmental interactions.
