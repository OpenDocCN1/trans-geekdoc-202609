Besides this, economically feasible open pond based microalgae cultivation has shown a considerable amount of biohydrogen production (1,239 kg/h) with suitable market price ($10 kg-1) (Gholkar et al., 2021). One has to keep an eye on storage cost by lowering the production cost in case of direct biophotolysis. Design of certain bioreactors demands specific manufacturing materials as well as nutrients, which usually cover majority of expenditure. This may lead into high biohydrogen production cost (Show et al., 2018).

Microalgae biomass based biohydrogen production cost through supercritical and thermal gasification is still unfavorable as the cost seems to be fourfold higher than the already existing natural gas driven hydrogen production (Kumar et al., 2019).

High production costs are also caused by problems including differential proton gradients in thylakoid, disturbances in light capture, and inefficient photosynthetic processes (Eroglu et al., 2016).

If one effectively compare the capital cost for direct biophotolysis with photofermentation and dark fermentation, then it seems quite clear that the latter one is economically unfavorable (Dincer et al., 2015; Bolatkhan et al., 2019; Peng et al., 2019; Limongi et al., 2021; Wang et al., 2021).

However, synthesis of biohydrogen characteristically costs more than other fuel alternates. Forthcoming research should concentrate on detailed assessment of amenity and operation perspective, budget of raw ingredients and operation process, light, meteorological conditions, and site-conditions, mixing of biomass, and product solidarity. Recycling waste metabolites and biomass can increase economic viability.

## Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning-Assisted Hydrogen-Based Systems

Artificial intelligence (AI) has drawn peer attention over the last few years and successfully been applied in industry arena together with other phases. Traditional researches regarding AI comprises of data representation, structured planning, methodical learning, natural language processing, portraying perception, and aid for robotics. In principle, the sole aim is to develop general intelligence that can potentially complete any duty as human can perform. Present generation has adapted and amalgamated a vast range of problem solving mathematical optimization techniques along with formal logic and different network modelling systems depending on statistics as well as operation research. On the other hand, Machine learning (ML) emphasizes on the practice of data and algorithms to replicate the humanized mode of learning in an improved and accurate manner.

The extensive usage of AI in current industrial sectors has potentially changed the scenario in relation to high production cost and performance accuracy. A major improvement has been observed specifically in hydrogen refining and filling system which has radically changed the modus operandi regarding prediction, planning and control. AI based systems thus well divided as swarm-biological, non-swarm biological and physico-chemical heuristic algorithms for sound implication in real time situation. Whereas machine learning algorithms has improvised artificial neural network following its simplification capability (Xiao et al., 2020; Xiao et al., 2021; Ahmad et al., 2021; Rangel-Martinez et al., 2021).

In 2021, a hydrogen manufacturing setup prediction prototype buttressed by photovoltaic technology has been established with the help of agnostic deep learning (DL) and artificial neural network (ANN) models based on insolation value dataset (Mert et al., 2021). Proper planning can steadily help us to minimize the hydrogen storage systems by implementing DL algorithm (Shams et al., 2021). Present situation of mother earth positively supports to build environmentally-friendly and sustainable strategies for autonomous energy systems as was shown in a DL algorithms based supply-demand forecasting model system to mimic an independent hydrogen blended renewable grid (Hwangbo et al., 2019).

AI methods like non-dominated sorting genetic algorithm (NSGA) has also become popular for system optimization in case of short term hydrogen energy storage to fulfil the load demand (Beshir et al., 2018). However, the particle swarm optimization algorithm (PSO) is basically a simple and efficient swarm intelligence based technique which has commendably unveil the optimal proportion and energy designing of hydrogen storage system (Hassanzadeh Fard et al., 2020). Improved ones can optimize the hydrogen fuel cell system for techno-economic, environmental and socio-political goals (Maleki et al., 2016; Eriksson et al., 2019). Here, artificial swarm optimization (ABSO) algorithm has proven its efficacy to optimize life span value and loss of power-supply probability in hydrogen based systems. Besides this, simulated annealing (SA) can be implemented to achieve an improved outcome in comparison to the outdated response surface methodology (RSM) system (Maleki et al., 2018). Cost optimization can be attained by employing a discrete harmony search algorithm (DHS) for successful size amelioration of fuel cell storage devices (Maleki et al., 2015). Additionally, a hybrid algorithms for optimization (HRES) has been applied to achieve minimum lifecycle cost for storage system and it portrayed acceptable robustness with lower index value in comparison to other methods (Zhang et al., 2018).

Currently, green hydrogen production (GHP) systems from water manufactories has focused on machine learning (ML) dependent forecasting and optimization of scaling up issues. They applied different relevant models to forecast and optimize the proton exchange membrane and dark fermentation methodologies to unveil the scaling up issues confirming a sustainable hydrogen economy (Kabir et al., 2023). Moreover, researchers from the KEPCO International Nuclear Graduate School have used a hydrogen economic evaluation programme to compute the manufacturing cost employing two types of small modular reactors evolved by the Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute and NuScale Power, LLC. They have unveiled the critical parameters like heat consumption and electricity rating that potentially influence production cost from a ML-based approach (Kim et al., 2022).

## CONCLUSION

Hydrogen is a environment-friendly transportation and vehicular fuel as it forms only water in the time of combustion. The State of California acknowledged significant effects on environment that has resulted into the severe wildfire in 2020. The state then realized the value of immediate implementation strategy of zero-carbon electric system that can potentially be achieved by 2030. Right now electrolysis process is the leading contributor of clean hydrogen as biohydrogen based system is still not feasible for mass-scale level. The cost and output of the current biohydrogen technologies, however, is still lagging behind those of the traditional methods of manufacturing hydrogen. Additional research initiatives are of utmost importance that can potentially elevate the process optimization, product yield and economic stand up. Genetic engineering can also help us to enhance microbial properties. Besides this, specifically designed bioreactors can greatly improve the same. Also, the lacuna in latest economic assessment remains as majority of information were carried out few years back. Therefore, systematic economic studies should be conducted in order to minimize the cost regarding biohydrogen storage and transportation. Finally, Artificial intelligence plays a crucial role in simulating renewable energy systems. Artificial Intelligence techniques have the potential to enhance energy generation and supply chain variable prediction by enabling more intelligent planning, management, and control. The renewable energy systems can be optimized to have a more appropriate size or configuration by using a variety of Artificial Intelligence optimization algorithms. Important factors that influence the cost of producing green hydrogen can be accurately anticipated using machine learning-based methods as well.
