Equation (14) describes the characteristics of the sound field for the radial coordinate (propagation direction), which is a classic Bessel equation, and its solution is a combination of two Hankel functions. Since the time factor is chosen as \(e^{i\omega t}\), the first type of Hankel function represents diffuse waves and the second type of Hankel function represents convergent waves. To satisfy the infinite radiation condition, the latter does not exist. Therefore, the solution of Equation (14) can be written as

```
\(p(x, r) = \sum_n A_n \Phi_n(k_{zn}x_0) \Phi_n(k_{zn}x) H_n^{(1)}(k_m r)\)
```

where \(k_{zn}\) is the vertical wave number, \(x_0\) is the depth of a sound source, and the coefficient \(A_n\) is decided by the sound source and boundary conditions. Furthermore, the expression of the nth order normal mode can be separated as follows
