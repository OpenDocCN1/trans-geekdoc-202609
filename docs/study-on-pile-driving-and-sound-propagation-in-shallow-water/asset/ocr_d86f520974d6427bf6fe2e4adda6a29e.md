Substituting Equation (6) into Equation (1) gives

```
\frac{\partial^2 u(x)}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\mu}{R} \frac{\partial w(x)}{\partial x} + \frac{\omega^2 \rho (1 - \mu^2)}{E} u(x) = 0 (7a)

```

where \( C_0 \) is an abbreviation for ease of writing and be expressed as

```
C_0 = \frac{1}{\mu R} - \frac{\omega \rho c_f (1 - \mu^2) R}{\mu n h E \sqrt{1 - (\frac{\omega}{c_p})}} - \frac{\omega^2 \rho (1 - \mu^2) R}{\mu E} (8)
```

To get the exact solution of displacements of the pile, we apply several boundary conditions on the top and of the pile

