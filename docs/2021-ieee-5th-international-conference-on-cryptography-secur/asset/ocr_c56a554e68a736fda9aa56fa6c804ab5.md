Abstract—Recommendation system is widely used to predict users’ interests and provide targeted products for them, which effectively facilitates users in the era of big data where information overload problem is prevalent. Unfortunately, massive data closely related to users’ privacy is in high demand to produce more accurate predictions. In this case, the collection and transmission of such data is communication costly; to process and analyze such data is of high possibility to compromise users’ privacy. In this paper, we propose a privacy-preserving recommendation mechanism based on blockchain, which well addresses these problems. Leveraging the inherent advantages of blockchain, we establish a completely distributed model mitigating the risk of privacy disclosure caused by central data storage. Moreover, we combine Inter-Planetary File System with blockchain to greatly improve the communication efficiency. We also introduce local sensitive hashing and local differential privacy into proposed mechanism to reduce the computation load and provide a strong privacy guarantee. The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed mechanism shows better performance on privacy preservation while maintaining desirable recommendation accuracy when compared with the baseline.

Keywords—big data; blockchain; privacy; recommendation; local sensitive hashing; local differential privacy

# I. INTRODUCTION

Recommendation system is proposed as a promising technology to address the information overload in the era of big data. However, massive users’ data need to be collected to improve the quality of recommendations, which brings a number of privacy issues. Efforts from legal aspects have been made to preserve users’ privacy. For example, the European parliament unanimously passed the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) [1], which came into effect in May 2018. After that, the California government of the United States also established the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) [2], which took effect in January 2020, to ensure the privacy rights and interests of consumers. Recommendation system, having heavy demand for users’ data, is of vital significance to preserve users’ privacy in the society where privacy is widely concerned.

At present, many approaches to achieve privacy preservation are based on centralized framework under the distributed environment [3, 8]. To this end, users’ data still needs to be collected and stored in the central server. In this work, we

propose a completely distributed recommendation mechanism leveraging the inherent decentralized features of blockchain. In this framework, users’ raw data are preserved locally to well reduce the risk of privacy disclosure. In addition, we apply local differential privacy technology to further minimize this disclosure.

More specifically, we followed the basic framework in [4] to implement the minimum hash algorithm based on Jaccard similarity and involve CRC32 verification algorithm for data clustering, making offline computation more efficient and secure. Moreover, we use local differential privacy on the users’ sides by adding a small perturbation to the data via the random response mechanism [5], which can further enhance the system’s ability to preserve users’ privacy. To the best of knowledge, this paper is the first to combine blockchain technology, recommendation algorithm, and differential privacy for the purpose of privacy preservation. Considering that the recommendation accuracy will be degraded due to the parameters in the clustering process and the perturbation process, we conduct experiments to demonstrate how to strike a balance. Possible privacy attacks based on the proposed system are analyzed and discussed in this paper.
