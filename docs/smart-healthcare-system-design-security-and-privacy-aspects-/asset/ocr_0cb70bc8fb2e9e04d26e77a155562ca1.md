Unauthorized access to data and equipment raises concerns regard-
ing patient safety in the healthcare system. Data confidentiality is one of
the key aspects of protection that must be taken into consideration when
sharing data. In addition, it is important to minimise high computation
overhead caused by existing cryptographic security algorithms and to
withstand different attacks such as Brute Force, Eavesdropping, Man-
in-the-Middle, etc. This creates a need for reliable, effective and lightweight
security algorithms to be created. It is therefore important to build light-
weight algorithms to monitor and manage data protection across different
layers of the IoT architecture.
