More images is better. We use 4,800 images, but 48,000 would have been better, and 4.8 million would have been even better. However, there is a real cost associated with finding and annotating images, and for most projects a few hundred images or at most few few images per class may be all you afford. Use what you've got—you can retrain your model at a later date once you've collected more training data. Data is king in machine learning, and who has the most of it usually ends up with a better model.

Create ML is super easy to use, but lets you tweak only a few aspects of the training process. It is also currently limited to image and text classification models.

Turi Create gives you more task-focused models to customize, and lets you get more hands-on with the training process. It's almost as easy to use as Create ML, but you write Python. The next chapter gets you started with some useful tools, so you can train a model with Turi Create. Then, in Chapter 5, "Digging Deeper Into Turi," you'll get a closer look at the training process, and learn more about the building blocks of neural networks.
