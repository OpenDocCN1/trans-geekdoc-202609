You didn't specify a validation set as part of the Model;Parameters, so the classifier spends 5% of the training data for that purpose. It spends a good bit of time keeping the reviews and converting them to training features. After that process completes, it starts training a MaxEnt model (on more than that), performing multiple training iterations until it reports a training accuracy close to 100%.

    print("Unknown error evaluating model")
}
