vectors filled with all zeros for their hidden cell states. They still work, correctly passing states between timesteps internally, but they cannot pass their states out of the layer, which is what we need for a sort of work around this, where you train your decoder to ignore the initial cell use and use the LSTM’s instead of their hidden states – remember that the output is identical to the hidden state – but means you are forced to throw away half of the information from your LSTM. Or to put it another, you need to train models twice as large as you want in order to pass the same amount of information from the encoder to the decoder.

So until these issues are resolved, it's a better idea to create bidirectional models like you did here, using multiple recurrent layers explicitly.
