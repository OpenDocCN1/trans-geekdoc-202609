The logic for interpreting the Vision results lives inside the show(predictions:) method. This simply loops through the VNRecognizedObjectObservation instances, converts the predicted coordinates to screen coordinates, and shows rectangles for the detected objects using the BoundingBoxClass.

+   - The VNRecognizedObjectObservation class has a labels of familiar VNClassificationObservation instances, sorted from highest probability to lowest, telling the app simply grabs the most likely classes for the object inside the bounding box. From the list, as this is the best prediction, and puts its identifier and confidence into the label's.
- There is also a boxShadow property, a CGRect that tells you where in the image is located. This uses normalized coordinates again, but with the origin of the CGRect in the lower-left corner. It's a little awkward, but it's how Vision does things. In order to draw a rectangle around the object, you need to transform the normalized coordinates to screen coordinates and also flip the y-axis.
