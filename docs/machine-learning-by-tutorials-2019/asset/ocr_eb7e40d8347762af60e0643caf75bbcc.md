The training accuracy is now 90%! This means on the training of 4582 examples it only gets 10%, as opposed to 20% (when the training accuracy was about 80%).

That pretty good, but remember that you shouldn't put too faith in the training accuracy by itself. More important is the validation accuracy. As you can see, this briefly went up and then down again.

The sweet spot for this model seems to be around 150 iterations where it gets a validation accuracy of 63.7%. If you train for longer, the validation accuracy starts to drop and the model becomes worse, then the training accuracy will slowly keep improving. A classic sign of overfitting.

Overfitting has a bad risk, and it's certainly an issue you'll run when you start training your own models. Overfitting isn't necessarily a bad thing to experience, as it means your model still has capacity to learn more. It's just learning the wrong things, and techniques such as regularization will help you to stay on the right path. (More about regularization later in this chapter.)

Unfortunately, Turi Create does not let you select the iteration of the model with the best validation accuracy, only the last one, and you'll have to train again with max_iterations=150, to get the best result.
