
*   **In-app payments:** It is not uncommon for social media platforms to offer varied services to their user base to increase engagement levels and value propositions. Few social media platforms have pioneered the inclusion of in-app payment systems to initiate financial transactions without leaving the social media application. This has resulted not only in an increased customer base but also in improved customer engagement and retention time. However, not all such applications run safely and are prone to data leaks and identity thefts; thus, many high-profile social media sites have withdrawn such services. Blockchain technology addresses such issues and provides an effective solution. Fundamental transactions can be supported through `smart contracts` apart from offering financial transactions through the `Bitcoin` cryptocurrency. Organizations like `Steemit` and `SocialX` are introducing several in-app offerings for their customer base. `Steemit` rewards its native currency to the customer base for contributing to social interaction and influence. `SocialX` users earn `SOCX` tokens by contributing videos and images in the social network and utilize those to avail services and products.

*   **Compensation and rewards:** The fundamental principle for the growth of the social economy is inclusive and collective contributions. Well-known social media enterprises ignored real social and economic growth by leaving social users aside and concentrating on sole benefit by earning and saving the bigger pie of the revenue. Only a few influencers get a fraction of the revenue. For example, `YouTube` provides the influencer a portion of the targeted ads revenue over the content contribution. Such models are unfair and most beneficial to a single entity. Blockchain technology solves this problem, and whosoever contributes to the social network using valid, authentic, and approved transactions gets rewards. The days when the content creator is unpaid for creative contributions are over. The `smart contracts` embedded in the process will be able to process compensation and rewards to the contributor immediately once the contributions are acceptable and available on the network. `Steemit` and `Sapien` are new-age blockchain-based organizations that provide such offerings to the user base. `Foresting` is a lifestyle social media platform on the blockchain which offers its community base a compensation policy considering their content creation, sharing/activities, rewarding, and use of rewards over the network.

*   **Confirming the legitimacy of the content:** Media credibility and truth are questionable due to issues related to the quality of information circulated and published on social media platforms. In a research study done by `MIT`, it was found that false news and information reach the social community faster and have deeper impressions. It is somewhat challenging to identify the source of the credibility of the data. But blockchain provides a robust solution to fake and misinformation. All information on the blockchain is considered as transactions, and since transactions are time-stamped and traceable, it becomes easy to trace the origin and source of the data.

    Moreover, the credibility of the information can also be established. The social network `Flixxo` is a decentralized, peer-to-peer video sharing blockchain-based platform. Users in the platform community get rewarded for sharing quality legal video through `Flixx` tokens. Users in the platform need to support the proof of evidence or information about the legality of the video, and once approved by the participants, the video is available on the network. The creator can set up the `Flixx` for watching and sharing the video. Similarly, advertisers can place some `Flixx` for introducing targeted video whenever a user in the platform watches them. Users in the community need to stake the tokens to upload the video on the platform.

*   **Information accessibility:** Conventional social media enterprises not only drive social interactions but are also able to control the information shown to users. Such information can also be restricted and limited to access by regulatory agencies. For example, access to `Wikipedia` is limited to specific communities and geographical locations. Considering blockchain as a platform to store and disseminate information, it offers an unprecedented choice to the user to access the data as long as a node is up in the network. There is no way that anyone can put a restriction or impose any form of censorship exclusively on information access. `Everipedia` is a blockchain-based content distribution platform to support information sharing and community consensus. In contrast to the centralized `Wikipedia`, `Everipedia` endorses the mission of universal information as a peer-to-peer resource.

*   **Verified marketplaces:** The social marketplace suffers from the inability to provide value to customers, as they are often cluttered with goods and services that are not personalized or relevant to the customers. Social interactions in the social network over the blockchain network will enable and empower varied stakeholders such as users, merchants, vendors, and service providers to accept and release their information to provide a better, significant marketplace. Blockchain social platforms like `Indorse` provide a better career marketplace for their users. The participating user can claim a particular set of skills which will be `Indorsed` by peer participants. Participants get rewards and showcase the indorsed claims to recruiters, which becomes prominent in the career solution offerings.

*   **User participation motivation:** Social media platforms need to offer continuous innovation and features to the social community so that they always feel motivated to be part of the community. One way is to compensate and reward each user for providing contributions to the community. By including `smart contracts` capable of transferring financial values for recorded contributions, platforms can tie values with the popularity of the social community. Users will be motivated to contribute more as they become eligible for more significant monetization processes. `YouNow`, an application offered through the `Props` project, allows social users to contribute to the community and share their rewards across a variety of applications over the platform.

*   **Identities:** Social media platforms are affected by the contributions of fake identities and bots, which are significantly hampering social community growth. It is essential to verify the true identity of contributions to avoid unnecessary circulation, distribution, and population of misinformation and information overload. Blockchain can solve the problem of fake identities. The social community can vote and agree on the consensus for the legitimate identity of the contributor and thus, at the very first place, avoid and eliminate contributions from fake identities. The blockchain network will also be able to reprimand the user involving the forfeiture of staked tokens in case contributions are not legitimate or fake identities are concerned. The platform provided by `Civic` offers the tools to control and protect identities.
