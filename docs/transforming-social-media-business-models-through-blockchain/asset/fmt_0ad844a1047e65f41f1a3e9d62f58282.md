## 5. Blockchain-based Social Media Enterprise

Blockchain provides an excellent opportunity for social media enterprises to transform their business model by incorporating inclusive and collective growth. Readers will get the chance in this chapter to explore potential business use cases impacting the social community and the business. Also, emerging blockchain-based social enterprises are covered to understand their strategic advantage and business model for new-age social media business.

### Introduction

In this chapter, we will investigate several case studies showing the implementation of blockchain in emerging social media enterprises. Further, we will cover and discuss the role of technology in bringing success to the social media business. However, social media enterprises are still in the nascent stage of embracing blockchain. Thus, use cases are still developing. The underlying rationale for selecting emerging social media enterprises is twofold. Firstly, to describe the characteristics and solutions of blockchain-based social media enterprises already covered in previous chapters. Secondly, most emerging blockchain-based social media enterprises have already reached the market and thus show the potential of the technology in redefining social media platforms. Each business use case is supported by a brief description of the social media enterprise, highlighting the main characteristics of the application or software. Further, information related to key stakeholders and motivations behind the offerings is covered. Also, time to market and user perception indicators are discussed to measure the feasibility of the proposed solution.

### Blockchain Use Cases in Practice

Blockchain is shaping social media enterprises, and several potential use cases exist to mitigate the risks and limitations of conventional social media applications.

*   **User is no longer a product:** Social media platforms offer services to customers so they can build a revenue empire by collecting customer data and sharing it with potential advertisers. Moreover, the targeted advertising industry flourishes on such accounts, and monetization benefits the service provider acting as an intermediary. The decentralized nature of blockchain enables and empowers social users to take ownership of their data and contributions; as such, they are no longer a product but can now be part of the revenue. Blockchain social media organizations like `Sapien` and `Killi` are solving this issue. `Sapien` provides control, access, and ownership to all users who participate and contribute to its network. `Killi` compensates social users for their contributions. In both cases, users are not treated as a product; instead, they contribute to the social economy.

*   **Effective content control:** Manipulation of social user emotions by leveraging data is not uncommon, and several stories have recorded such events. One potential reason triggering such events is the sole authority over data utilization, as social user data is accessible to a central governing authority. With blockchain, no single entity can dictate content, making manipulative content on the network easy to eliminate. There will be more effective control over content on social networks due to the inherent decentralized nature of the network. Organizations like `DTube` are solving this issue. It is a decentralized video distribution platform based on the `STEEM` blockchain. The purpose is to eliminate the central authority and reduce control over content distribution. `STEEM` is a decentralized, blockchain-based public content system. It supports community building by promoting social interactions based on a rewards system. The platform rewards community participants for making quality contributions. `STEEM` is a liquid currency and can be bought or sold on exchanges.

*   **Improved security:** Social media platforms can go far to reach and breach social interactions to govern social user behaviors. Several allegations have been reported against `Facebook` for accessing social user data without explicit permission. Moreover, less secured communication channels over social networks raise many potential data leaks and identity theft issues. Blockchain technology provides a convenient and robust security layer for the exchange of messages or communications. Organizations like `SoMee` are working in this direction to provide a secured social communication network. It is a platform based on a decentralized blockchain to cryptographically secure user data.

*   **Expanding and protecting freedom of speech:** In the democratic culture of expression, the technological dominance of controlling speech and expression is somewhat detrimental to societal growth. Many incidents of blocking and controlling access to social media sites have been attempted. Even though popular sites promise to encrypt communication messages to avoid identity issues, they become identifiable due to the presence of metadata with the message. Blockchain provides an effective solution to this problem, as metadata is not tied to a single identity and is distributed across the network, thus providing true anonymity. An organization like `Civil` has based its solution on a decentralized blockchain network. It supports freedom of speech and offers services to small outlets and journalists. The `Civil` community governs the `Civil` registry. Community members approve newsrooms joining the civil society. `Civil` tokens can be used to raise issues against any newsrooms.
