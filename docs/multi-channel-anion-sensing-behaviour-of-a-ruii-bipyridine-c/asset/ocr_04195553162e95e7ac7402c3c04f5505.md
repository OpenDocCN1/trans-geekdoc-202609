# 3.3. Anion sensing

We carried out the analysis in acetonitrile optical channels and spectroscopic tools. We measured therein the lifetimes of salts complexes of F⁻, Br⁻, I⁻, AO⁻, CN⁻, SCN and $₄. The absorption spectrum of the complex changes upon addition of different anions with their visual colour change. Substantial shift is observed in case of basic anions (F⁻, AO⁻ and CN⁻), while the position remains almost unchanged after other anions. Essentially, the complex acts as sensor for F⁻ and CN⁻ which is due to strong hydrogen bonding. The red-shift in the MLCT bands is due to the presence of NH protons in acetonitrile which are deprotonated in presence of excess anions. In-line with the absorption spectrum, the emission remains practically unchanged except for the red-shift.

The emission properties follow step-by-step addition of anions as shown in Fig. 3c and d (ESI). Spectroscopic quenching of emission intensity increases with considerable magnitude and a corresponding red-shift of the emission peak is observed in the case of the anions. In the first step, however without further quenching occurs without the addition of excess anions.
