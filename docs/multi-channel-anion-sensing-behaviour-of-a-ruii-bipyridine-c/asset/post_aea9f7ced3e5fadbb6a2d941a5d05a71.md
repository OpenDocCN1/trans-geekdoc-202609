## 3.4. Electrochemical monitoring

The CV and SWV traces of the complex reversible Ru³⁺/Ru²⁺ at E₁ = 0.2–0.94 V and two stepwise reversible waves at -1.46 and -1.78 V due to reduction of two bulky moieties (Fig. 5 (S2)).
