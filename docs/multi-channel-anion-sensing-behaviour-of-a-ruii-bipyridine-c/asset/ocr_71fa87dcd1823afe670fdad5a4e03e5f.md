# 3.6. Operations

In the previous section, we observed quite a large degree of change in the absorption and emission spectra as well as electrochemical behaviour of the complex and species anionic. The retention of the complex is also possible upon treating with water. Furthermore, photo-induced decomposition of the photo-redox complex observed by anions followed by reconstruction of the original form is fully reversible and can be repeated many times. Here we will study the spectral and electrochemical properties upon successive addition of anions and for fabrication of multiple logic complexes.

# 3.6.1. Combinational logic and keylock lock

Combinational logic is basically like Boolean operations ("OR", "AND", and "NOT”). However, the luminescence respect at 685 nm and absorbance at 545 nm are treated as the outputs in presence of F' (input 1) and F'' (input 2). Emission quenching below the threshold and absorbance enhancement above demonstrate the influence of F', showing the "OFF" state (0) and "ON" state (1). The emission and absorption maxima throughout wavelengths upon addition of inputs (F' and H') leads to the notion of combination of IMPLICATION and INHIBIT H' (Fig. 8c and S3).
