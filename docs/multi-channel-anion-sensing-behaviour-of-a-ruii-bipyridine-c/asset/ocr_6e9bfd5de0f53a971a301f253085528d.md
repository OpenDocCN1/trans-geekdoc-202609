# 3.1. Synthesis and characterization

Synthesis and characterization of the ligand (Hpbzb) and the Ru(II) complex was followed by our previously reported method [40]. Recrystallization was carried out in MeCN-water medium of flows of 0.1 M HCl to retain the pyrazole NH proton within the complex.

# 3.2. Solvent absorption and emission spectral behaviour

The absorption and emission spectrum of the complex is acquired in few selected solvents and associated spectral parameters are provided in Table 1. Figure 1 shows the measured emission spectrum of the complex. The strong absorption in the UV-Vis domain is evident. The position of the peak varies between 480 and 500 nm, where the higher energy bands (443–458 nm) can be attributed to $\pi$–$\pi^*$ transitions of the ligand. The peak between 332–368 nm arises from ligand-to-metal charge transfer in acetonitrile, while the bands at $\sim$310–290 nm are due to to- $x$–$\pi^*$ transitions.

The resolution of the emission band at maximum intensity (FWHM), which varies between 676 and 722 nm, depends on the nature of the solvent. The quantum yield ($\phi$) varies in the range of 9.0–8.0–$10^{-3}$
