# ABSTRACT

- Keywords:
- Ruthenium

- Adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference system

Multi-channel anion sensing of a Ru(II) complex of the form [(bpy)Ru(Hpbz)]Cl(CO₄)₂ (bpy = 2,2'-bipyridine and Hpbz = 3,5-benzothiazol-2-yl-pyrazole) has been carried out in this work and analyzed through multiple machine learning tools. Essentially one NH proton on its outer sphere interaction with anions yields considerable changes in photochemical properties. The sensing aspect of the complex is spectrophotometrically investigated in absorption-spectrum spectroscopy as well as SVCM wavelength monitoring. In absence of anions, the complex remains and represents the "off-state" of the system. Essentially the complex acts as an anion-induced molecular switch. The absorption of anions is employed to identify different types of Boolean and Fuzzy logic (FL) functions. Additionally, we implemented different deep learning tools such as artificial neural networks (ANNs) and adaptive neuro-fuzzy inference system (ANFIS) to evaluate the full sensing aspect of the complex. The three models are compared all with the experimental results.
