In case of the phase 1, belonging to 1950s as well as 1960s, the technology of the computers generally tends to have an inclusion of mainly the extensive mainframes of the provided computers that were supposedly no associated or not connected machines, or in the other words: the stand-alone machine.

Certain repercussions associated with the morality of the process of computation of all the concerns related to the society as well as morality, at this specified facet that can be viewed in the form of the various questions that in today’s time, are being linked or connected with the artificial intelligence (AI).

During this phase, the other concern was the fear linked with “Big Brother,” as it is a centralized government that could collect substantial amount of information regarding its citizens. This information is stored in large databases indefinitely in the form of electronic records.

Having a consideration of the phase 2, belonging to the 1970s as well as 1980s, the first and the foremost step for this is to initiate with the viewing up of the idea of merging the devices related to communication as well as various machines for the process of computation or calculation, presenting with a progress to the epoch belonging to the information or the computer networks.

Within this specified facet, various personal computers, mainframes, microcomputers as well as the minicomputers could all get associated or connected with the help of the networks of the computer that has been held privately, in the form of the LANs (i.e., local area networks) as well as WANs (i.e., wide area networks).

Information could now be readily exchanged between databases, which were accessible to networked computers.

The beginning of internet technology enhances amongst different other things, anonymity, concerns linked with free speech, trust, authority, and so on, that evolve during this phase of computing technology.

In addition to this, there pertain varied questions, which are of greater concern, related to the personal information, possessing private character or the personal one that can be simply accessible on the cyberspace.
