# 7.5.2. Infiltration and Beyond

The autonomous agents are used to gather or acquire information, and these agents are used to remember the data that is acquired during the whole process of reconnaissance and this information which consists of different vulnerabilities is used to plan an attack. This process is called as the infiltration. The major strategy that is used to plan an attack or infiltration is by the method of using “trees.”

Trees are the different structures that are used in signifying certain problems in AI. These trees in artificial agents are used to design different strategies in the process of the cyber-attack. These trees are used as models for the future attacks that are planned. These trees act as representatives to plan the future acts of infiltration and successfully execute the present process of infiltration. The process of representation of the inner surrounding and the different vulnerabilities would enable the autonomous agents to protect and combat against the defenders. This process would make the autonomous agents more “persistent” and such threats to these autonomous agents are called as the “advanced persistent threats” (APTs).

The proper knowledge and representation of the possible threats would make the autonomous agents understand the appropriate defensive strategies and enable them to prepare themselves accordingly and be successful in executing the attack.

The intelligent autonomous agents would acquire information, understand the possible countermeasures and act accordingly. The whole process of understanding different strategies and preparing against these strategies and being counteractive would enable the autonomous agents to be stronger and use different techniques of AI to protect the information and successfully carry forward their objective.

# 7.5.3. Swarms

Swarms are called as the decentralized agents that are independently programmed and can execute different sets of commands. These decentralized agents though have distributed functions and diverse roles they are called “artificial flocks” and can communicate amongst each other to execute a task effectively. The network that is formed by a set of decentralized agents is very strong and is extremely difficult to combat, and they should be handled both independently and collectively to protect a system against a set of decentralized agents.
