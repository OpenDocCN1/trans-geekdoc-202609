Among the most discussed issues regarding the IT industry, the ones related to information communication technologies, it has generally come up on the top. The uncontrolled expansion of the ICT and its overwhelming use that support the technology structure across industries have made some notable impacts on the social existence of people which makes it a subject that gets enough attention from the ethical point of view.

The problems regarding this have been discussed in such a manner that the confusion regarding it has had varied impacts in society. The various issues that the use of ICT gives birth to are a breach of privacy of a person or an organization, taking over the rights of property, pricking the various social rights of the people, and the accountability that sets up due to the situations that take place after the use of ICTs.

The issues that have been mentioned above have given rise to other related problems such as unemployment, improper working conditions and degrading a person’s individuality. However, the actions and measures that must have been taken up to resolve these issues and problems have not been up to the mark. As Fielden has stated in 2004, the field of IT serves as a center point and is employed across almost all the industrial verticals like commercial sector, governmental organizations, pharmaceutical sector, education, entertainment business, industrial setups, thus concerning the whole society in a specific way.

The benefits of IT industry do not need an extra introduction. But, just like the other evolving sectors, the use of IT also carries with itself certain flaws and implications that affect the society in some way or the other. It contributes to the issues related to ethics in three main ways, which may also be termed as ethical issue. They are:

- Personal Privacy;
- Access Right; and
- Harmful Actions.

When the subject is about the personal privacy, IT may help in exchanging large amounts of information from anyone or from any place across the globe and at any point of time. This kind of exchange gives rise to an increased risk of having the data leaked and having the privacy of an entity breached. This makes it essential for the humans to take steps in order to maintain the privacy and honor the quality of data that concerns different people. This incorporates the measures needed to make sure that the data remains accurate, and additionally securing it from being accessed by an unauthorized entity or getting the information disclosed accidentally to the people who are not meant to have it.

The other part of the issues regarding ethics is the right to access the content.

The matter of security in the digital space has shifted from being one of the low priorities to that of a highly prioritized one on the board of agenda of the different institutions and organizations in charge. This has happened because of the sudden rise in the online market of the international trade.

The subject matter has been even more in the focus of late owing to the digital breach of the security at some significant organizations such as the Los Alamos National Laboratories and NASA in the United States. In the recent times, there have been numerous incidents of the illegal breach attempts by the hackers into the administrative systems of the US government. The connections on the internet network cannot be a cent percent vulnerability proof unless the policies and strategies regarding the security of digital platforms are thoroughly established and practiced.

## 4.2. FOUNDATIONAL ISSUES IN INFORMATION ETHICS

There are specific questions that are pointed at the uniqueness of information ethics (IE). There have been numerous arguments given by the researchers and various other people who have studied about the subject in favor of IE and its study claiming the field to hold theoretical importance. Following this kind of research and study, the digital world and its utilization lead to certain obstacles that are meta-ethical, epistemic, and ethical in nature. These hurdles authorize solving the issues in the form of unified classes that need specific training and perfection.

On the one hand, where the researchers and publishers contend that IE holds its uniqueness in a theoretical and essential way, the other way around they tend to be divided on the way they consider it to be distinct.

### 4.2.1. Interpreting the Uniqueness Thesis

The uniqueness thesis is interpreted in different ways. In a first way, the people may put a point forward that the ethics regarding computers is different from others in a way that some of the activities that the digital world involves, requires the ethical qualities that may not be borne in any other kind of sector.

### 4.2.2. The Meta-Ethical Thesis

There are acts in computer ethics that cannot adequately be characterized by the traditional concepts of obligatory, permissible, good, and supererogatory. The meta-ethical thesis, then, makes the very strong claim that the very meta-ethical foundation for general and applied ethicals is that there seems to be no way to mince that impression in any current system. It is not plenty to just state that deontological theories, utilitarian theories, care theories, rights-based theories, all provide the incorrect outcome; one must validate this statement by reference to some rationally believable concept or code.

The property theory also faces problems. While supporters of this theory typically have faith in that computers possess ethical standing (i.e., are allocated an ethical responsibility of esteem), none of the unique assets of computers have any noticeable ethical significance with respect to the subject of ethical standing since these assets seem to have only instrumental value (i.e., value as a means to an end) and lack the intrinsic value (i.e., value as an end-in-itself) thought to be worthy of ethical admiration or accountability.
