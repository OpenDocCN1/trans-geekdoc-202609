# 5.3.4. Information Ethics from the Point of View of Systems and Technology

There are a lot of concepts connected to the IE of the information society that have risen from the related areas like digital and computer ethics. Broad availability of information in digital forms has generated many concrete issues concerning ownership of digital records and digital information.

The various aspects related to IE are categorized into different types (Zwass, 2010). They are:

+   - Privacy;
+   - Accuracy;
+   - Property;
+   - Access.

These are the fundamental concepts that are used in the design, expansion, and utilization of information systems.

The right of the people to secure the information about their personal lives from unlicensed access is called as privacy. Privacy is considered by different scientists of IE as a basic aspect of social science (Cibangu, 2012).

The issues related to the regulation of clicks are generally connected to the misuse of information with an intent to do marketing. For an instance, the ACM code of ethical conduct is known to make experts to value privacy as a fundamental rule, and many industries are reviewing these issues as an aspect if self-regulation. The correct and accurate data is connected to the collecting of information of high standards and maintain privacy and security of information systems. The accurate information must to totally mistake free with all the relevant content with comprehensive information.

The right of possession of information must generally be obtained by following the law. Therefore, intellectual property is an integral component of ethics as it deals with irrelevant possession of creative content by a person or an organization. The expansion of internet services, however, has forged an inviting environment for infringement of this right.
