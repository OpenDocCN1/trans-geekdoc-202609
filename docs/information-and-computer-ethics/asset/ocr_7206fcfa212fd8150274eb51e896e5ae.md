The persistent problems raised up by Information and communications
technology contain the attack of individual and trade privacy, individual,
and societal rights, intellectual property rights, values preservation and
responsibility for the values and moments arising from the use of Information
and communications technology, etc. There are issues that thrown up main
challenges in the area of employment, working conditions and individuality.
However, some areas still not have developed and require more progress
associated with ICT.

IT plays an important role in various sectors like healthcare sector,
finance, commerce, government, education, media, and entertainment and
society at large. IT is one of the most challenging effects and can create
some negative impacts on the society.
