# ## 8.3. ETHICAL ISSUES IN INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

The business and organization will be more successful only with the high level of technology. For Example, goods, and services can be exchanged or moved with the relevant information can give profit to the business.

Nowadays, Information is necessary to move the business ahead, and IT play a major role in the business, and there can be also sharing of data with the help of software, telecommunication, computer hardware and digital electronics for making the business successful.

According to the experts, ethical attentions for Information and Communications Technology associated issues first looked under the topic “information ethics (IE)” in the Annual Review of Information Science and Technology in 1992. This advises that there is an ethical program related with the use of Information and Communications Technology. Organizations and Individuals then need to be ethically delicate as they organize ICT on their actions and procedures.

The impact of Information and Communications Technology on human relationship has been tremendous. Information and Communications Technology has helped to enhance and improve family relationship like everyone is now connected with the mobile phones, laptops, palmtops, viral conferencing, etc. but some of them believe that Information and Communications Technology has separated family and friends with each other.

Information and Communications Technology has allowed new friendship and relationships in virtual communities, and it is important to know that such relationships are genuine. This technology has brought new and different jobs over the internet, but these changes and opportunities are also endangered by problems of unemployment from computer replacing humans.

A wide range of new laws, regulations, rules, and practices are therefore needed if society is to manage these workplace and other changes and development brought about by Information and Communications Technology.
