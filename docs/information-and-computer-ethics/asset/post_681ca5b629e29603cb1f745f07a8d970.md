## 5.2. DEFINING THE FRAMEWORK OF INFORMATION ETHICS

### 5.2.1. Definition

IE is defined as the ethics of several fields that produce data and generate information along with distribution of knowledge. These fields are library and information science, education, technologies, censorship, privacy, research reviewing and others. This definition of IE was given by Robert Hauptmann, who is the editor of the Journal of IE magazine that is being published from the year 1992.

### 5.2.2. Important Aspects of Information Ethics

There are two important features that need to be considered when defining the IE and they are:

- Information; and
- Ethics.

Information refers to the information behavior, information system, and general information in various contexts. Ethics refers to the moral and ethical values that are related to the information. However, there is need to understand the specifications of the framework of both aspects so that they help in regulating the way these affect the content and concretization of the actual life examples.

To acquire a universal approach towards IE in one required frame, theories related to information science should be acknowledged properly, and researchers should also look into the way these theories have represented information. One way to tackle this is by identifying the different levels of interaction between ethics and information (individual, group, organizational, societal).

The information is defined as a framework which brings together cognitive, biological, social, physical, and technological components of information environment when an individual aspires to gain meaning out of something. Information is constructed socially in concrete, real-life instances of information behavior.

Each information has its own context, and the context is a circumstance or a situation which gets created and has a meaning to that one or relatable situation or an event. Since context is dynamic, it is contingent on personal characteristics, social circumstances and information space.

There are two different approaches that are used to define IE. These methods are:

- Microethical; and
- Macroethical.

Various researchers differentiate between morals as ethics applied in praxis, and morals as a moral practice. There is a distinction between theoretical reflection on the one hand, and concrete information behavior and information systems on the other.

However, there is need to use an interdisciplinary approach with the merger of different branches like the information science, philosophy, new media studies, as the subject is too complex to understand by just studying a single field.

### 5.2.3. Microethical Approach

As per the definition stated by Microethics, IE is an indispensable part of human information behavior, and wherein there are several factors and contexts related to IE which are represented by information behavior.

Microethics defines the IE as a process with several stages of human activity of utilization of information that is categorized by the human adaptation to information environment. The factors that regulate IE are the human being, the different sources of data and the various products that have been made by utilizing information and producing information.

With the help of a context, it becomes easy to summarize the ownership of information, authorized use of information, and information activities within the information environment.

### 5.2.4. Macroethical Approach

The Macroethical approach is more inclined towards the application of norms of ethical nature. This helps in digging into that information that are based on societal and global levels. At a global level, IE takes the shape of information ecology. The macroethical framework deals with the issues that are related to information society, particularly its digital divide, environmental ethics (man as part of information environment), and the ecological approach to the life cycle of information.

It is a social framework for the moral values of information use and the interconnectedness of the social with the technological. Common discourse that probes information existence is formulated here, and issues of coexistence of information objects and man in the information environment are examined.

The microethical and macroethical approach enable in defining the field of IE as an idea based on the information behavior. IE is a kind of information behavior of the creators, users, and information experts where there is a value system that is based on the choice making and problem-solving in the field of utilization of information.
