The autonomous agents are categorized into two types based on their architecture. They are:

+   - Monolithic Agents; and
+   - Decentralized Agents.

Monolithic agents: Monolithic agents are those autonomous agents that are made from one source of information and the functionalities of the monolithic agents are limited. For example, monolithic agents are any date file or software libraries that have different information.

Decentralized agents or Decentralized Intelligent Agents: Decentralized agents are called as decentralized intelligent agents and as per their name they refer to the systems where the intelligence is distributed among many different elements. Though the intelligence is distributed these elements function in coordination with one another and are called as the “artificial flocks” by Craig Reynolds because of the demonstrated similarities in the way they live or function.

The decentralized agent is known to more resistant to the efforts related to deactivating and the impacts of counteroffensives. Therefore, for an example, a botnet is a network of various agents in the place of the conventional malware software which would be the central point of control that would be deactivated.
