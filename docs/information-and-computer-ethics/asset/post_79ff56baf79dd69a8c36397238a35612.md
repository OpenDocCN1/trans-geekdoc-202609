## 5.4. ETHICAL APPROACHES TO SYSTEM DESIGN

Even at the broadest of the levels, the ethics can get implemented or applied, in accordance with the entire approach, to the structure of the provided system. Nevertheless, such approach might tend to differ dramatically in terms of their respective potentiality so that they could be able to cope with the issues related to morality or ethics.

Varied conventional approaches towards the growth or development along with the structure of the systems, for instance: the process of analyzing or assessing the systems that has been designed along with the various other structured procedures or SSADM, which is also known as the most known procedure for the development in various countries possessing major concentration on the issues that are technical in nature in comparison to the issues belonging to the human beings.

It generally tends to consider various facets of technology belonging to the development or the growth of the pertaining system by the process of segregating the development of the system within minor tasks. It possesses the involvement of the varied characterization of the levels or the stages for example: assessment or the analysis of the needs, provided specification of the requirements, alternatives for the technical systems, design or the structure of physics, feasibility, varied substitutes for the business systems along with the logical designs.

All of the provided levels generally tend to have an inclusion of the enumeration of the provided steps as well as all of those steps, thus, involves certain enumeration of the tasks, which are required to be performed.

At the bottom level, SSADM includes around 230 tasks. Amongst the 230 tasks, not all are executed in a specific implementation. The stage definition informs the developer regarding the preceding products that are fed into the next stage and what are the products that will be created by the particular stage.

SSADM tends to put an effort in describing three perspective regarding the pertaining system as to how all the items of the data present within the system, would be able to sweep away from it, how the provided items of the data are interrelated with one another, how all of the items within the data tend to transit over the time period (Kendall and Kendall, 1999).

Whereas the pertaining 230 tasks, which have been composing the SSADM in the recent times does not tend to particularly possess a concern over the issues related to the ethics or morality, it can be apprehended that the various steps or the stages or the provided tasks belonging to SSADM that possess the potentiality of getting restructured so that they could assist them in doing likewise.

The substitute approach for this is the model of CATWOE, which helps in identifying or recognizing the various customers, processes of conversion, the respective environment, view or the perception of the world, actors along with the owners.

As it clearly comprises different roles of people, it can support the ethical concerns mainly if augmented by the building of an ethical conflict web (Wood-Harper et al., 1996).

The provided approach, then, tries to have an identification or the recognition of who all are doing what and for whom are they are doing it, to whom all are they accountable, what all expectations or the assumptions have been made related to it, as well as within what environment this process is occurring.

Limitations related to the approach of CATWOE are not at all similar to that of the approach related to SSADM, as it never provides with the information as to how one should be building a specific system. In addition to it, it generally tends to make assumption regarding the varied workers along with the managers that they usually discuss their respective issues in open.

The basic notion belonging to the ecologies of the pertaining information (Davenport and Prusak, 1997) has been considered as a novel approach in accordance with the development of the provided system, which considers the technology in terms of the notion of the various individuals, practices along with the institutes or the organization, which is being related to the utilization of the system.

This approach is based on analogies with ecology such as information diversity (species diversity), information environment evolution (biological evolution), emphasis on observation and description, and a focus on people and information behavior. The emphasis on people and the method by which they utilize the information must be an enhancement over the conventional approaches; however, it lacks the particular emphasis on the ethics described by Wood-Harper et al. (1996).

But, in accordance with any of the procedure related to the structure of the systems, wherein there is an obligation that there should not be much of concentration on the ways as to how all the pertaining individuals tend to make utilization of the provided information, but also on the manner in the present people tend to utilize the systems or the provided information in a wrong manner, and thus, clearly or tactically providing with an indication regarding the ways of utilizing the systems and how they shouldn’t be.
