+   - The attacker may not be in total control of non-state actors in cyberspace operations, even though he commands what targets and methods should be employed during the cyber-attack, as there is little control over undesirable behavior, and it is difficult to end ongoing attacks.

+   - Attacks may extend beyond the desired scope and size. The attacker may accidentally hit targets that were not planned and cause undesired collateral damage. Excessively keen associates of cyber cities, not limited by the limitations that govern military organizations, could opt to target civilian targets without any thought of possible consequences.

+   - Common infrastructures like Internet backbone connections, may get overloaded which would adversely impact the attacker’s network and resources if attack non-state actors are engaged.
