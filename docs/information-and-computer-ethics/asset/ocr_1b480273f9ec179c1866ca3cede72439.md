+   - The factual information available to the agent regarding the condition that agent is endeavoring to navigate;
+   - the condition in which the interaction happens.

There is an additional literature that evaluates basic arguments like Floridi's with the expectation of growing the concept of automated moral reasoning; hence a person can speak of various automated moral technologies from very modest applications all the way to complete moral agents with the rights as well as responsibilities alike with humans.

While scholars identify that in some time developing IT would be identified as an artificial moral agent, there are enough theoretical arguments in recommending that automated moral reasoning is an ultimate possibility and thus it is a suitable area of study for those who are interested in the moral effects of information technologies.
