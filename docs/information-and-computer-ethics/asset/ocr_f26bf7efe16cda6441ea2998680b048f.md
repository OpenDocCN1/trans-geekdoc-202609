# 8.7. CHALLENGES AND ISSUES RELATED TO CYBERCRIME

Cybercrime series across a variety of activities. Midway along the spectrum lie transaction-based crimes such as money laundering, fraud, trafficking in child pornography, digital piracy, money laundering, and counterfeiting.
