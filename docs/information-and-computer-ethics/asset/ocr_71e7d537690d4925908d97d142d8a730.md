# 4.11.1. Privacy

Privacy is now something which is most commonly hacked by people through different sources. There are several questions which need to be answered before understanding the concept of privacy. Some of them are:

+   - What information should one be required to disclose about one’s self to others?
+   - Under what circumstances an individual must disclose about the information without the consent of the other person?
+   - What information should one be able to keep strictly to one’s self?

Today more than ever cautious citizens must be asking these questions.

Two kinds of situations pose a threat to the privacy of the people. The first of them is the rapid development of IT that too with an improved intensity for surveillance, correspondences, digitization, storage, and retrieval. The other one, which is a more profound form of threat, is the value of the information that has gone up in the process of taking important decisions. It is amazing how the value of information, for the people who construct policies, has gone up. These people crave for this information, even in the case that it indulges with the privacy of some other people.
