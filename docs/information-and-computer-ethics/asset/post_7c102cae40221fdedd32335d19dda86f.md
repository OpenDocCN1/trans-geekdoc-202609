## 3.9.1. Will Computer Ethics ‘Disappear’ in the Future?

Johnson recommended that the area of computer ethics may or should disappear in the coming future. She assumes that whatever we are considering as issues in computer ethics may become well integrated into the “ordinary ethics” issues.

Johnson has been considered as very cautious while pointing out or indicating that the issues or the problems are not going to get mitigated on their own, instead they are not even going to get manipulated or posed similar to those of the problems or issues related to the ethics belonging to the information along with the communication technologies.

She argued that the computer technology tends to mend or affect the actions performed by the human beings in varied ways in which it has never been implemented or executed. Nevertheless, in case a novel execution or the application has been established within the process of moral to Johnson through a demand regarding the two pertaining ways with which she could be able to understand or interpret the claim made by her regarding the banishment of the computer ethics:

- As an autonomous discipline, computer ethics will disappear; and
- As an aspect of applied ethics, computer ethics will disappear.

In addition to challenges posed to Johnson’s prediction by Gotterbarn and Marturano, others would also likely disagree with Johnson.

It would surely appear that questions related to AI comprising moral responsibility will endure to rise in the coming future and that these AI related questions would benefit arguably from the analysis by computer ethicists.

Rather, it is believed that a better knowledge of how computer technology has emerged during the last fifty years can aid to recognize more clearly some of the issues and challenges that people will face in the future when they attain a state of more comprehensive ‘technological convergence.’

In considering the impact that computing has had for ethics during the last half of the twentieth century, we could view it from the vantage point of four distinct stages or phases.

In case of the phase 1, belonging to 1950s as well as 1960s, the technology of the computers generally tends to have an inclusion of mainly the extensive mainframes of the provided computers that were supposedly not associated or not connected machines, or in the other words: the stand-alone machine.

Certain repercussions associated with the morality of the process of computation of all the concerns related to the society as well as morality, at this specified facet that can be viewed in the form of the various questions that in today’s time, are being linked or connected with the artificial intelligence (AI).

During this phase, the other concern was the fear linked with “Big Brother,” as it is a centralized government that could collect substantial amount of information regarding its citizens. This information is stored in large databases indefinitely in the form of electronic records.

Having a consideration of the phase 2, belonging to the 1970s as well as 1980s, the first and the foremost step for this is to initiate with the viewing up of the idea of merging the devices related to communication as well as various machines for the process of computation or calculation, presenting with a progress to the epoch belonging to the information or the computer networks.

Within this specified facet, various personal computers, mainframes, microcomputers as well as the minicomputers could all get associated or connected with the help of the networks of the computer that has been held privately, in the form of the LANs (i.e., local area networks) as well as WANs (i.e., wide area networks).

Information could now be readily exchanged between databases, which were accessible to networked computers.

The beginning of internet technology enhances amongst different other things, anonymity, concerns linked with free speech, trust, authority, and so on, that evolve during this phase of computing technology.

In addition to this, there pertain varied questions, which are of greater concern, related to the personal information, possessing private character or the personal one that can be simply accessible on the cyberspace.

At present, we are on the threshold of what could be called Phase 4, in which we are beginning to experience an unprecedented level of convergence of technologies. At the phase 3 the interface to and the metaphor of, computing technology is yet same as the previous phases such as screen, keyboard, etc. and the computers are yet considered as something peripheral to the people that is, they are external devices that humans can use to interact.

As it is quite known that the computers have become nearly omnipresent in today’s world, and, thus, too much of extent a part of what the individuals are as the human beings, feasibly, it can be said the people are on the verge of the novel, as well as debatable extensively considerable epoch of the process of merging.

Certainly, it may turn out to be problematic to separate out certain aspects of biology from technology in the near future.

Because of the situation, it is envisaged that in Phase 4, it may seem that a view that fits with Johnson’s conception (described above), where computer ethics as a separate field of study will go away. In the exact opposite to this, it has been assumed that there will be an extensive requirement for both facet of the ethics belonging to the information as well as the communication technologies along with the philosophers so that they could be able to do research on that specified track, in comparison to the previous times.
