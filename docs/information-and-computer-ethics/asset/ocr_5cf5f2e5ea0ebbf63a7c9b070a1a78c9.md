The second area where the ethical dilemma is faced involves acts that are carried out in the interest of a certain social cause or of a political party. “Hacktivism” is the term coined to represent this area of conflict since it signifies the convergence of hacking with activism.

If the attack is planned with the motive of causing significant harm to civilians and cause widespread terror, they are termed as “cyberterrorism.” It is a term coined to signify the confluence of cyber-attacks with terrorism. Cyberterrorism can be unambiguously labeled as heinous and immoral; however, hacktivism raises ethical dilemmas. For instance: can a cyber-attack be considered as ethical when the hackers take down a website which is mainly used to trade child pornography, or a cyber-attack which supports terrorist operations or performing attacks on stolen credit card numbers trafficking.
