# 7.2. ETHICS FOR THE DIGITAL AGE: WHERE ARE THE MORAL SPECS?

The digital systems, institutions, infrastructures, and applications must be developed in accordance with the moral values so that the ethics play an important role in the digital world. The VSD perspective is an important aspect and is not restricted to technology development but is a part of different fields of engineering, medicine, science, and development.

The ethics is an important subject in recent times and if any technology that is not approved or does not meet the ethical standards would face difficulties in terms of approval, acceptance, and development. Therefore, the different technologies that are being developed in recent times keep in view the different ethical and moral constraints and are developed accordingly. The sudden shift in technology-centric world towards ethical aspects can be termed as “The Design Turn in Applied Ethics” (Van den Hoven et al., 2017).
