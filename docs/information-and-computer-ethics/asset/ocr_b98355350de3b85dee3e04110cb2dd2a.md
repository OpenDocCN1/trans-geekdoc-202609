The important elements regarding the autonomous agents are as follows:

The main aspect of these independent agents is that they should have inner surroundings that represent their functioning which is called as the “belief state.” Based on their internal surroundings these autonomous agents are classified into different types by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig in their reference book for artificial intelligence (AI). The classification is based on the environment of these robots, based on which these autonomous agents are classified into following types. They are:

+   - Simple reflex dependent autonomous agents- these are not dependent on the inner environment;
+   - Model dependent autonomous agents;
+   - Goal dependent autonomous agents;
+   - Utility dependent autonomous agents.

The agents that are based on the function and utilization are known to the illustrations of their internal surroundings that would act as the precursors to guess the actions of the agents and the measure of the utility like the platform to outlet the different states of the environment.
