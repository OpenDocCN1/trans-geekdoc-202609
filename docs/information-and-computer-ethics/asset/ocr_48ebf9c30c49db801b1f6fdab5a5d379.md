# 5.3. THEORETICAL APPROACHES OF INFORMATION ETHICS

IE is examined both at the level of theory and at the level of applied IE. In approaches to information science of the last twenty years, information science is dominated by the concepts of intellectual freedom, freedom of speech and press, and free access to information (particularly in electronic environment). The theoretical approach of information science enables to differentiate among the socio-philosophical and system-based approaches of IE.

# 5.3.1. Socio-Philosophical Perspective on Information Ethics

The socio-philosophical perspective of IE denotes that the study of general ethics. The different socio-philosophical classes of information science are epistemology, ontology, and ethics. In Ancient Greek tragedy, “parrhesia “means freedom of speech and personal devotion to truth. This concept has recently been incorporated into research concerning IE and the ethical use of information.

There are several novel understandings in the network-based electronic environments which state that different new types of information behavior are exhibited and generated when the new media is being utilized Capurro (2005).
