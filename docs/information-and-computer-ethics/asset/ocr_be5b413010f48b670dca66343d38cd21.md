This gives rise to a model called as the RPT model (Resource, target, and product model)

+   - **Information as resource:** The use of information in IE where information is valuable resource.
+   - **Information as product:** The generation of information by IE is the use of information as a product.
+   - **Information as target:** The environment of information is affected by the IE.

**Ethics of information as a resource:** Ethics of information as a resource can be described as those moral values which are incorporated when there is a presence or absence of information resources in a secured form. Moral values concern accessibility, safety, reliability, and exactness of information resources. The focus lies on safeguarding the anonymity, supporting a fair treatment towards an information and its resource and evaluating the disinterested evaluation.
