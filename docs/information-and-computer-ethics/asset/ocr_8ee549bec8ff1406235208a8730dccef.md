# 1.4.3. A Cybernetic View of Human Nature

Wiener’s emphasized on the physical anatomy of humans and also the immense potential that they hold for developing themselves and innovate new processes. Wiener often compared humans to lesser evolved organisms to explain his view that humans are capable of remarkable achievements.

Cybernetics takes the view that the configuration of the machine or of the organism is an indicator of the competence in the execution of tasks that can be expected from it. The fact that the mechanical rigidity of the insect is such as to limit its intelligence while the mechanical fluidity of the human being provides for his almost indefinite intellectual expansion is highly relevant to the point of view of this book.
