# 8.6.1. Digital Divide

The digital divide poses a serious problem today. A new breed of haves’ and ‘have nots’ are being created, between those that have access and can use a computer and the Internet, and those that do not have such access. There are clearly serious ethical implications here. Those that do not have such access may well be discriminated against, feel ‘socially excluded’ and miss out on many life opportunities.

Cloud Computing: Cloud Computing has raised many challenges for IT professionals as it also is having the ability to connect large numbers of computers on a single network.
