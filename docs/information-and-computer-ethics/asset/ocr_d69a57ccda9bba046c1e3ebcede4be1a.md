Wieners’ works are based on human nature and an ideal society. From his works, we can derive that different cultures that are characterized by various practices, languages, traditions, and religions can provide the required platform for supporting a decent human life. Wiener supported the view that “variety and possibility belong to the very structure of the human organism,” hence he could welcome the existence of a broad diversity of cultures in the world to maximize the possibility of choice and creative action.

According to Weiner, a society should support an enabling environment where a man can maximize their potential as sophisticated beings that can process information, make choices and be responsible for their own lives. To establish such a social order, freedom, equality, and human compassion should be there in the society. Ethical relativists opine that there can be no universal foundation for ethics since diverse laws, ethics, and norms exist in the worldwide.

Wiener’s response was that as per his work on human life, humans can adopt various cultures and traditions while still observing “the great principles of justice.” These principles lay the foundation for ethics across diverse cultures.
