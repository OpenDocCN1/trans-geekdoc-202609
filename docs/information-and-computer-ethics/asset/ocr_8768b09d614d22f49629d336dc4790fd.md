IE is such a comprehensive topic that it also covers that discipline which involves evaluating human behavior, their values and the way they make their choices (whether they are rational or irrational) apart from following just the principles related to philosophy and ethics. IE is also related to other dimensions of information which involve internet, biology, politics, business, and ethics of information systems, media, and medicine.

In the case of IE, different kinds of studies are involved which could be normative, descriptive, general, analytical, applied or special in nature. Each professional (especially scientists) is required to oblige to fulfill its moral responsibilities, and these responsibilities are supposed to be followed by a required professional code. The main divisions of IE are:

+   * IE of agents;
+   * IE of users; and
+   * IE of makers.
