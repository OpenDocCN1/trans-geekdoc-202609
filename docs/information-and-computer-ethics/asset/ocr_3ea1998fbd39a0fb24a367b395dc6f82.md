# 4.10. COMPUTER CRIME

When some of the estimates were made, about seventy-three million people for the United States had their records disclosed accidentally. Talking about one of the cases that caught attention, there happened a theft at the house of a professional at the U.S. Department of Veteran Affairs, in which a computer was stolen that had in store, data of about twenty-six million members of the U.S. military involving both – the ones who were serving and the others that had retired from the services. Though, in a consequent action, the computer was brought back, with a claim that as the thieves did not know the intensity of the machine they had taken, the data was safe.

Several illegal activities have been witnessed on computers since quite a long time which include, hacking, fraud, harassment, and embezzlement. There are also various ethical dilemmas, such as whether copying such files is as bad as stealing something.
