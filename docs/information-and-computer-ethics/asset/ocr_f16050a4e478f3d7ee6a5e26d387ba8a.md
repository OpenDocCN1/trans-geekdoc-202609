# 5.3.3. Information Ecology Perspective of Information Ethics

According to Floridi in 2010, “The ecological approach to IE perceives information both as a method and an entity of environment and the world.” If any object of the information environment may be considered an information object, it may also be taken into consideration a global view of the entire life cycle of information belonging to ecological IE.

There is an ontological approach that has been made by Capurro and Floridi about the concept of IE. There are two concepts proposed by them, and they are:

+   • Environmental Approach proposed by Floridi; and
+   • Digital ontology proposed by Capurro.

These two models differ in the fundamental concepts which formed the basis for this classification.

**Environmental Approach:** The view of IE as an environmental model which states that there is connection between the value obtained from the information of an object to the existence of the object. There is a need for the objects of information to grow; hence, the contamination of the infosphere is the major component of the IE.

However, the environmental model proposed by Floridi does not concentrate on the social concerns of IE which is a major drawback.

**Digital Ontology:** The IE of a digital objects with respect to their interaction with the surrounding environment and their respective social, intercultural, and moral values is called as Digital Ontology. The IE is related to the principles of ecological, political, economic, and cultural domains (Capurro, 2005).
