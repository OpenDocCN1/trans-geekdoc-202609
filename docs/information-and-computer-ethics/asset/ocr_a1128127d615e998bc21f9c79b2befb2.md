Autonomous agents are classified into different types based on their function, topology, and architecture. The important categories of the autonomous agents are the monolithic agents and decentralized agents. Monolithic agents are single pieces of software such as the data files, whereas the decentralized agents involve the division of power among different smaller agents which function collectively.

The important risk to the autonomous agents is the cyberwarfare, and the cyberwarfare is a preplanned and executed attack to get access to the data and information.
