# 4.6. KEY TECHNOLOGY TRENDS THAT RAISE ETHICAL ISSUES

The ethical issues have been born due to the use of IT for a long time. However, the extended use of IT has been increasing the worries regarding ethics, disturbed the social things that currently exist and has resulted in making some of the laws regarding the society redundant and severely damaged.

The intensity of the digital world that has been increasing by twice as much every one and a half year has made it easier for most of the companies to utilize these digital systems for the production processes that form the most important part of manufacturing. As a result, the dependence of the several entities on such systems, and the risk of getting vulnerable to the errors it commits and the improper and bad quality of information has increased.

The regulations and legal frameworks that have existed have not been able to adjust to this over-reliance. The standards that make sure that the data systems remain accurate and have an increased reliability, have not been accepted globally by all and neither have they been enforced on them. The latest advances in the techniques for storage of information and the costs that have been coming down with great pace have been the reason for the ever-increasing databases on various people including the employees, customers, and possible consumers, which have been kept and updated by several private and public institutions.

These techniques and innovations have regularly been violating the privacy of several entities and have made it cheaper and more effective to do so. The big firms find it extremely inexpensive to use the storage systems encompassing terabytes of data, for storing the individual identities of people, mainly their customers.

The innovations in data analytics and its techniques that concerns a huge amount of information is another trend in technology that increases the worries regarding the ethics. This happens due to the fact that the companies and government agencies are capable of figuring out extremely private information about the people and that too in a detailed way.
