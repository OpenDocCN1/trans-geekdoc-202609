# 5.2. DEFINING THE FRAMEWORK OF INFORMATION ETHICS

# 5.2.1. Definition

IE is defined as the ethics of several fields that produce data and generate information along with distribution of knowledge. These fields are library and information science, education, technologies, censorship, privacy, research reviewing and others. This definition of IE was given by Robert Hauptmann, who is the editor of the Journal of IE magazine that is being published from the year 1992.

# 5.2.2. Important Aspects of Information Ethics

There are two important features that need to be considered when defining the IE and they are:

+   - Information; and
+   - Ethics.

Information refers to the information behavior, information system, and general information in various contexts. Ethics refers to the moral and ethical values that are related to the information. However, there is need to understand the specifications of the framework of both aspects so that they help in regulating the way these affect the content and concretization of the actual life examples.

To acquire a universal approach towards IE in one required frame, theories related to information science should be acknowledged properly, and researchers should also look into the way these theories have represented information. One way to tackle this is by identifying the different levels of interaction between ethics and information (individual, group, organizational, societal).
