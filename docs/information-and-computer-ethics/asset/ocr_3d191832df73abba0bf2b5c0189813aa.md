# 2.2.2. Moral Values in Information Recording

Power is the control of information, and in the economy of information, it might be the eventual form of political power. Today, we are living in a world which is full of data and the technology to produce, record, store a huge amount of data has rapidly developed.

Here the primary and moral concern is that when the data is collected, stored, and eventually accessed, it is dynamic that this be done in a way that reason can be observed as fair and also in the best interests of every party involved.

Varied social institutions which conventionally used to practice these powers are the things viz: the pertaining religious organizations, various institutions, officials within the healthcare sector, libraries, several agencies of the government, banks as well as the corporations.

These kinds of entities have easy access to the information that is stored that further provides them a fixed amount of power over their constituencies and customers.
