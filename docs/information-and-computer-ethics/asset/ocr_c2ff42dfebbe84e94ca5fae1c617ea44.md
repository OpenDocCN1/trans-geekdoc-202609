This is a prime example of the mindset of why, for the delivery of software, code of ethics is required in the tech companies to assure their aims are good willed while delivering the product. As one identical code of ethics like the Hippocratic Oath in the medical profession can be a resolution for the software industry, it is also important to teach delivery teams how to ask the right questions when considering the ramifications for emerging innovations. The ethics question may be different for different types of organizations as well, leaning more toward an individual, organizational view rather than a global one.

The organizations can use various values that are commitment, courage, focus, openness, and respect. They provide help to guide the decision-making and increase team dynamics (Figure 5.3).

+   **Commitment:** In the case of IE, commitment could be described as a form of trust an individual or a team have towards the agile approach and its values. Generally, it is believed that people working in the same team adopt similar approach towards a problem, but by making the commitment explicit as an individual, team, and organization, they all start from the same baseline. This might create a situation where one team member would not follow the information ethics.

+   **Courage:** It is the courage that motivates an individual to speak up and call out unethical behavior by management that can influence work being produced. Various organizations usually believe that the bad decision will ultimately disappear if you don’t say anything.
