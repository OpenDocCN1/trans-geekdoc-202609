## 5.7. GLOBALIZATION AND COMPUTER ETHICS

Today, computer ethics is progressively evolving into a wider and also more significant field that might practically be known as “global IE.” The international networks such as the Internet and mainly the world-wide-web are linking the people all around the world.

Krystyna Gorniak-Kocikowska in her paper, “The Computer Revolution and the Problem of Global Ethics,” for the first time in history that, “efforts to develop mutually agreed standards of conduct, and efforts to advance and defend human values, are being made in a truly global context.”

So, for the first time in the history of the earth, ethics, and values will be debated and transformed in a context that is not limited to a geographic region, or constrained by a specific religion or culture. This may very well be one of the most important social developments in history. Consider just a few of the global issues:

- **Global Laws:** If the users of computers in the US wants to secure their freedom of speech on the internet. Around two hundred countries are interconnected by the internet; thus the constitution of the US is just a “local law” on the web; it really does not apply to the rest of the world.

- **Global Cyber business:** The world is quite close to have the technology that can give electronic privacy as well as security on the internet enough to conduct the transactions of international business safely. Once the technology at its right place, there will be a robust enlargement of international cyberbusiness.

- **Global Education:** If inexpensive access to the global information network is provided to rich and poor alike -- to poverty-stricken people in ghettos, to poor nations in the “third world,” etc.—for the first time in history, nearly everyone on earth will have access to daily news from a free press; to texts, artworks and documents from inordinate libraries as well as museums of the world; to religious, political, and social practices of peoples all over the place.

What will be the influence of this abrupt and deep “global education” upon isolated communities, political dictatorships, religious practices, coherent cultures, etc.? As great universities of the world begin to offer degrees and knowledge modules via the internet, will “lesser” universities be damaged or even forced out of business?

## 5.8. FACTORS AND VALUE PRINCIPLES OF INFORMATION ETHICS

There is a phenomenon called the ethical tension which originates from the professionals and information institutions that are at work due to the large volume of written and unwritten rules and institutionalized and individual professional principles. It can be described through an example of a corporate world where ethics play a key role in determining an individual’s future in that company. This is so because it talks about the effects of an employer’s ethical code, ethical codes of professional companies, customer pressure regarding provision of services, personal ethical convictions, and ethical, societal norms (Rubin and Froelich 2009).

Apart from the corporate world, ethical factors have a huge impact on the library and information studies. There are the factors of social usefulness, survival, social responsibility, and respect of man’s personality.

The concept of IE is slowly becoming a part of university education in many programs of library and information science, and professional organizations, such as American library association (ALA), Association for information science and technology (ASIST) and Association for Library and Information Science Education (ALISE).

## 5.9. EXAMPLES OF ETHICAL DILEMMAS WHEN USING AND PROCESSING INFORMATION

The main example of IE is citation ethics. This is related particularly to ease of access and use or abuse of electronic information. Issues of plagiarism have therefore been more closely monitored in student works, while citation ethics is included in courses of information literacy.

The different instances include matters of defense of intellectual property with the help of novel methods of information utilization in digital science, which is called as “Creative Commons.” A state of issue pertains due to the stress that accumulates when a situation related to the right to information and freedom of speech arises, particularly as regards censorship and filtering of information. Restrictions may apply particularly to dissemination of malignant information (These are that information which might not be suitable for the kids to go through or would be inappropriate for them to understand.).

Use of information systems and technologies is another main concern that gets originated when encountered by an individual. For example, digital divide, provision of electronic information services in governmental areas, cybernetic crime. Maximum number of solutions to ethical dilemmas is contingent on existing regulations (laws, codes) on the one hand, and on the formation of information policies and information literacy that underpin man’s information behavior on the other.

The best-suited method for understanding the matters related to IE is information ecology (Capurro, 2005; Floridi, 2010). It has now become an indispensable part of interpretation of relationships between man, information, and technologies, placing emphasis on the positive values of the cultivation of information environment and services for advancement of knowledge in society.

For example, the technologies established by the companies like Volkswagen and YouTube have as well as continue to have a reflective influence on the society. For more than ten years, YouTube has formed the way people make, share, and consume the content of video. Likewise, it is quite hard to imagine a world deprived of Volkswagen cars, as since the early 20th century, the company has been a mainstay.

There is a reason why incidents leave an impact on the society. For example, incidents like “YouTube’s controversial recommended videos incident” and Volkswagen’s emission scandal were few to name that had a long-lasting impact on the society and especially on the kids (a major point of concern for the parents).
