As per the consequentialist approach, actions that lead to bad consequences can be termed as wrong. Whereas, deontological approaches assume that the moral duties of an individual have to be viewed independently of the consequence of their actions, whether good or bad.

Mostly, ethical principles and legislations are interconnected with each other. But it is found that it is not possible for a legislation to function as a substitute for morality. It is for this reason that individuals and corporations are always required to consider not only the legality but also the morality of their actions. Ethical analysis of privacy and security issues in the field of Information Technology (IT) took place primarily in computer ethics that emerged in the late 1980s.

The aim of moral analysis is to provide clear knowledge and bring an equilibrium between several rights, interests, values.
