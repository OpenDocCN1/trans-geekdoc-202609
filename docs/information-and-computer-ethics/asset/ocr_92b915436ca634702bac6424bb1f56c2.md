# 7.2.2. Responsible Innovation

The innovations and creations team of Google called as the Google X has developed a novel product called as the Google Glass. The Google glass has voice-controlled access to internet and augmented reality features. The Google glass was taken for testing in the year 2014; however, there were a lot of privacy, safety, and security concerns, because of which the Google glass was stopped in the year 2015.

Google glass was discontinued because of the easily accessibility to take pictures via the Google lens which was not accepted by a number of people. The Google lens was an innovative project which gave the spectacles/glasses a small camera to take pictures while checking out people. The easy access to data and the ability of the Google lens to take pictures of a everything people see, gave a number of security and privacy concerns because of which the project was discontinued. The Google glass was an innovative project that was started by Google; however, the major reason for the failure of this project was the ethical and moral considerations that Google failed to consider.
