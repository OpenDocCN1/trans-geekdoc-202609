There are two major aspects in the field of Autonomous Intelligence called as the ‘Strong’ and ‘weak.’ The strong in autonomous intelligence refers to the development of technologies which are better than human intelligence and can-do aspects better than human beings, whereas the weak refers to the beings with limited capabilities which are not better than human intelligence in any way. The examples of weak autonomous intelligent agents are computer vision systems, game-playing software, and others.

There are a lot of debates on the strong vs. weak concept, but the importance of all of them is the philosophical debate regarding which of them is better.
