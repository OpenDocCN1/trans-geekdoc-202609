definition for the autonomous agents, which is as follows “An autonomous agent is a system situated within and a part of an environment that senses that environment and acts on it, over time, in pursuit of its own agenda and to effect what it senses in the future.”

The major aspects regarding the autonomous agents which are part of this definition are as follows (Figure 7.1):

+   - The autonomous intelligent agent is completely and stringently related to the surroundings.
+   - The autonomous agent that is present away from the respective surroundings is known to have no use or function, or it is not considered to be an autonomous agent.
+   - There are proper sensors that are used to communicate with the surroundings.
+   - There are proper actuators along with the sensors that are used to interact with surroundings and are known to function in a way that can create some impact on their surroundings.
+   - The autonomous agent’s function with a defined motto or aim which is called as ‘agenda’ by the Franklin and Graesser.
+   - The autonomous agents that are made to be used in the warfare are called as the targets.
+   - The functions of a completely autonomous agent are established over a period so that they have a sustainability related to an assigned function.
+   - There is certain feature of an autonomous agent that are not mentioned here; however, they can be understood and comprehended from the knowledge that is gained from the aspects mentioned here.
