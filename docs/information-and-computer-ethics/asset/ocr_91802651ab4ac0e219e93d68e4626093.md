Simultaneously, there is a lot of emphasis on the ethical context of the application of computing technologies in IS. Nevertheless, one can argue that there is a substantial overlap in the themes of interest between computer ethics and ethics-related IS research.

Thus, in order to provide with the summarization of this, one might present with the opinion that the ethics belonging to the computers tend to play an essential part of the dissertation of the various references for the research of the IS being related with the ethics or morality in the way that most of the important tracts of their respective interest can be discovered within both of them. This does not really make a robust statement regarding the way of the relationship between the two.

A further possibility would be to see both streams of research reflect the public interest in important social concerns. For the purposes of this paper, the details of the mechanisms facilitating it are of secondary importance.

Although the ground for this turn was prepared by those working in computer ethics, it is worth emphasizing the new discourse and its advantages in comparison to (traditional) computer ethics.
