The organizations can use various values that are commitment, courage, focus, openness, and respect. They provide help to guide the decision-making and increase team dynamics (Figure 5.3).

- **Commitment:** In the case of IE, commitment could be described as a form of trust an individual or a team have towards the agile approach and its values. Generally, it is believed that people working in the same team adopt similar approach towards a problem, but by making the commitment explicit as an individual, team, and organization, they all start from the same baseline. This might create a situation where one team member would not follow the information ethics.

- **Courage:** It is the courage that motivates an individual to speak up and call out unethical behavior by management that can influence work being produced. Various organizations usually believe that the bad decision will ultimately disappear if you don’t say anything.

It is important to motivate people in such a way that they have transparency within the team as this would help them in achieving their respective goals ethically. It is not just the leaders’ responsibility to encourage the team to follow the ethical line, but everyone must have that sense of responsibility towards the work they do and should follow an ethical line to accomplish the goal.

- **Focus:** Instead of working independently, teams are more effective when they collaborate on small sets of customer-driven outcomes. Therefore, company leaders should empower their teams to tackle the most important tasks first as they work toward a final product. Concentrating on a task at a particular point of time assures that the teams are pushing the best resources only for the production of final product any organization can be honored of.

- **Openness:** A leader must not encourage commitment and courage, but should also help the team to be open amongst the team members. This will build trust on each other and would help them take the track with complete positivity while achieving the required goal. Openness within the team helps in creating transparency and would give space and scope to everyone to develop their skills by their own, the phrase “fail fast” is often used to describe agile approaches, but actually, it is “learn fast.” Openness is a prerequisite to that mantra.

- **Respect:** Above all, leaders of the company must respect the diverse backgrounds, variety of skills and experience of their team members. Giving respect to the team members and presuming they have ideal intentions can easily lead to the conversations which can be utilized to resolve the conflicts present in an organization and enhances the performance of the team. When people feel respected and know they are being heard, they can fully support company decisions, even if the decision was not their preference.

### 5.10. CONCLUSION

IE is a big challenge to maintain in the age where people are getting used to the advancements that are taking place in the technological world. The focus, however, is generally towards theoretical aspects of IE and identification of basic socio-philosophical and system- and technology-based approaches to and taxonomies of IE. The main challenges of researchers are more inclined towards the development of theories of information behavior and information theories.

With regard to the near future, issues of importance are regulations concerning intellectual property, ownership, plagiarism, negative phenomena and spam dissemination on the one hand, and issues of securing equality of access, such as digital divide, and censorship, on the other.

The latest replicas of IE are known to upsurge receptivity of social and ethical standards of information behavior and information practice, that enable to discover the right equilibrium between information privileges and responsibilities, penalties, and rules of information behavior, and approachability, frankness, and reliability of information.
