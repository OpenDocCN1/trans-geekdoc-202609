Information Societies has its share of moral problems.

+   - Firstly, the information should be credible and also meet the required quality standard.
+   - Appropriate control should be there over the Internet.
+   - Data processing should be done responsibly.
+   - Software should conform to the accepted standards.
+   - Personal data should be protected, and privacy should be maintained.
+   - There are also problems concerning the power and dominance of commercial parties.
+   - Information should be accessible to everyone, who wants to access. Also, information should be distributed on a fair basis.

In the twenty-first century, technology has taken over our lives. Children and young adults are the ones whom technology has influenced the most. Technology and its contents have a prolonged effect on them and have shaped their ethical values and behavioral growth.

As further technology advances, new concerns will keep arising. This issue is often discussed in public debates and attract much public attention. There is a requirement for stringent regulations because the effect technology has on lives is far-reaching. So, it is very important to have a code of conduct in place, to highlight these issues. Computer and IE have tried to do just that, in the past decades.

The features of ICTs are such that we do not have direct solutions to the ethical issues that keep arising. We cannot ignore or detach technology from our lives and have no choice, but to face all the complications and issues.

+   - ICTs have a universal presence and are more prevalent than our most common technical appliances. Common household artifacts and another day-to-day appliances nowadays can be called computers they are often connected through wireless network technologies. Gradually, more ordinary objects of our day-to-day life, are coming under the garb of the Internet of Things that eventually interconnects with the Internet of People. It is not easy to quantify or assess the role of technology and infrastructure on routine tasks. Their dependence on technology continues to grow but is not perceived much as technology tends to blend in the background.
