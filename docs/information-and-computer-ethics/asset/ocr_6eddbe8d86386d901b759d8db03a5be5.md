At the end of the 20th century, the term web 2.0 started to surface, and it basically referred to the advanced method that the global web was being utilized as a way for sharing of information, collaboration, and a change in the approach of web designers to comprise user-centered experiences on the websites.

Whilst, the real structure of the world wide web, in the year 1989 by the creator of it, has always been the one which has been included in accordance with the norms or the context of meeting other people and thus, cooperating with them online, wherein the users ultimately agreed to entirely utilize such potential by the year 2004, at the time when the first web 2.0 conference got held by O'Reilly Media.

The new kind of “augmented reality” is usually enabled by the fact that various people carry GPS facilitated smartphones and various other portable systems with them upon which they can easily run different applications that let them to interact with their neighbor and also with their computers at the same point of time, possibly looking at an item through a camera in their system. These technologies basically come up with their own conduct of issues and challenges.
