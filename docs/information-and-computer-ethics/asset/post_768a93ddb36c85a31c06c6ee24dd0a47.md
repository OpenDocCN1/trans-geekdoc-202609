Cyberwarfare is the important term of recent times as there is a lot of research, discussions, and arguments that are being carried out in the recent times. - A variety of political aspects and stakeholders that are involved in these cyber attacks;
- The accessibility to information;
- The privacy settings that are maintained by different organizations regarding the data.

The concept of cyberwarfare is mostly focusing on the defense rather than the attack. There is a need to understand the different stages of cyberwarfare to prepare well for the cyberwarfare. ### 7.5.1. Reconnaissance

Reconnaissance is an important aspect of any cyber-attack as the reconnaissance refers to that stage of cyber-attack which involves research, identification, and selection of the target.

Reconnaissance is the start to most belligerent processes and is called the initial stage of a cyber-attack as it offers a perfect environment for the disposition of autonomous agents. The disposition of agents happens in three directions:

- Automatic discovery of technical vulnerabilities in target systems or networks;
- The process of collecting information about the technical vulnerabilities like the specifications of the assembly and topology of different operating systems and applications; and
- The process of acquiring information about different users, and different personal security credentials of the users that would give access to the cyberattacks.

The process of identification of the weakness and the possible susceptibilities in the target network to strategize the attack is an important stage of planning a cyber-attack. The cyber-attacks are researched, planned, and executed by skilled professionals who are pioneers in the areas of cyber-attack.

The software autonomous agent is known to reconnoiter the target, identify, and differentiate the susceptibilities and strategize a method to exploit these susceptibilities. Although complete automation of exploit development is not extensively accessible, there are some scenarios that are utilized for the incorporation of agents with that competence.

The important stages of the cyber-attack are as follows:

- **Reconnaissance:** Reconnaissance involves the process of researching about the objects, identification of the objects and selection of the targets.
- **Weaponization:** The process of the weaponization involves the combination of the different malware to the researched targets.
- **Delivery:** The process of delivering or sending the malware or viruses to the identified and selected targets by the emails, messages, pen drives, different attachments, and other routes.
- **Exploitation:** The delivered viruses or malware start executing the harmful set of operations that are programmed in the malware.
- **Installation:** The process of exploitation becomes effective with the successful installation of the harmful malware which would give them access to the data of the target continuously.
- **Command and Control:** The installed malware not only provides the existing information, but also gives access to the users which would enable outsiders to establish a command on the system and control the target's activities.
- **Actions on objective:** The attackers would plan and execute the attack with a set of objectives which are successfully achieved with the attack. These objectives would vary according to the requirements of the attackers, and they could be things like destroy the data, access the data or replace the data, gain control over the network or corrupt a software and any more.

The process of planning, executing, and achieving the desired result by a cyber-attack would create damage that could not be measured in magnitude. The whole process of cyber-attack and its damage cannot be measured; therefore, an important strategy could be defending and designing defensive strategies into the network rather than being susceptible technologies. There are different types of protections that can ensure data privacy and security in the network.

- The autonomous agents or the software agents are mostly given training or a set of instructions to attack a particular network.
- The next stage would be the process of acquiring information that is needed to target a network.
- Beyond the information gathering stage is the stage of infiltration, wherein the different autonomous agents would attack a network based on a set of instructions that were programmed into the agent prior to the attack.
- The decentralized agents are executed to plan a perfect attack where the same action is divided into different sets of packets and each agent is programmed differently to execute the attack.
- The fundamental motto of this type of action is the collection of data that would be directed to the command and control structure for further processing. The different technical and other information regarding the susceptibilities is gathered, and this information is used to execute the attack (Figure 7.4).

### 7.5.2. Infiltration and Beyond

The autonomous agents are used to gather or acquire information, and these agents are used to remember the data that is acquired during the whole process of reconnaissance and this information which consists of different vulnerabilities is used to plan an attack. This process is called infiltration. The major strategy that is used to plan an attack or infiltration is by the method of using “trees.”

Trees are the different structures that are used in signifying certain problems in AI. These trees in artificial agents are used to design different strategies in the process of the cyber-attack. These trees are used as models for the future attacks that are planned. These trees act as representatives to plan the future acts of infiltration and successfully execute the present process of infiltration. The process of representation of the inner surroundings and the different vulnerabilities would enable the autonomous agents to protect and combat against the defenders. This process would make the autonomous agents more “persistent” and such threats to these autonomous agents are called “advanced persistent threats” (APTs).

The proper knowledge and representation of the possible threats would make the autonomous agents understand the appropriate defensive strategies and enable them to prepare themselves accordingly and be successful in executing the attack.

The intelligent autonomous agents would acquire information, understand the possible countermeasures and act accordingly. The whole process of understanding different strategies and preparing against these strategies and being counteractive would enable the autonomous agents to be stronger and use different techniques of AI to protect the information and successfully carry forward their objective.

### 7.5.3. Swarms

Swarms are called decentralized agents that are independently programmed and can execute different sets of commands. These decentralized agents, though they have distributed functions and diverse roles, are called “artificial flocks” and can communicate amongst each other to execute a task effectively. The network that is formed by a set of decentralized agents is very strong and is extremely difficult to combat, and they should be handled both independently and collectively to protect a system against a set of decentralized agents.
