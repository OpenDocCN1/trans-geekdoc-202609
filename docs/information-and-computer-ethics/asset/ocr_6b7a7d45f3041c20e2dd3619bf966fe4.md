The major concerns of IE are related to the moral responsibility of makers and users of information and of information professionals. This is so because these two roles could be easily interchange their roles and responsibilities with the help of technology. New knowledge concerning human information behavior makes it equally important now to build up a theoretical frame of IE.

As stated by Froelich in 2004, other than the roles and responsibilities, there are other dimensions related to IE, which are; ethics of information use, ethics of information systems and technologies, and ethics of information society and information strategies. Based on these dimensions several researchers believe that information is that discipline which helps in creating a well-structured framework for interconnectedness of different value aspects of work with information.

It is IE that help in understanding the importance of moral values and also their criticality. Moral values here are referred to the information that is used by an individual for any purpose.
