# ## 2.6.3. Robotics and Moral Values

Information technologies have not been gratified to restricted to software implementations and virtual worlds. These technologies are directly interacting with the generation by the application of robotics. Robotics is an evolving technology, but it has produced a huge number of uses that has significant moral implications.

Various technologies like medical robotics, personal robotics, military robotics and the world of sex robots are some of the existent applications of robotics that express the moral commitments.

The launch of semi or fully autonomous machines in the public life will not be that easy. Autonomous machines are those that take decisions with little or almost no intervention of human.

The military robotics has recognized to be one of the most ethical and charged application of robotics. These days these machines are basically operated remotely (telerobots) or semi-autonomous, but over time these machines are possible to become progressively autonomous because of the requirements of modern warfare.

In the first ten years of war in the 21<sup>st</sup> century, the robotic weaponry has been absorbed in various killings of soldiers as well as noncombatants and alone this fact is of profound moral concern. Gerhard Dabringer has done various interviews with ethicists and technologists related to the inferences of automated warfare.
