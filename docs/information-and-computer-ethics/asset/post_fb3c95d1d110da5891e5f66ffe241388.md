### 5.3.2. RPT Model (Resource, Target, and Product Model)

This gives rise to a model called the RPT model (Resource, target, and product model)

- **Information as resource:** The use of information in IE where information is valuable resource.
- **Information as product:** The generation of information by IE is the use of information as a product.
- **Information as target:** The environment of information is affected by the IE.

**Ethics of information as a resource:** Ethics of information as a resource can be described as those moral values which are incorporated when there is a presence or absence of information resources in a secured form. Moral values concern accessibility, safety, reliability, and exactness of information resources. The focus lies on safeguarding the anonymity, supporting a fair treatment towards an information and its resource and evaluating the disinterested evaluation.

**Ethics of information as a product:** Ethics of information as a product can be described as a social agent which act as a producer of an information. The production lies within the restrictions and possibilities of the information environment. Responsibility concerning creation is the most important value, as is compliance with rules (legislative), or issues such as plagiarism, propaganda, advertisement, and misinformation.

**Ethics of information as target:** Ethics of information as target is related to the impact that is made after the moral judgment on the information environment. An example for the same could be; Disturbing the information environment intentionally which would also comprise activities which would be unethical or unacceptable such as “hacking” (unauthorized access to information systems), infringement of privacy, and piracy on the one hand, and securing of private ownership, freedom of speech, censorship, filtering, and content control on the other.

### 5.3.3. Information Ecology Perspective of Information Ethics

According to Floridi in 2010, “The ecological approach to IE perceives information both as a method and an entity of environment and the world.” If any object of the information environment may be considered an information object, it may also be taken into consideration a global view of the entire life cycle of information belonging to ecological IE.

There is an ontological approach that has been made by Capurro and Floridi about the concept of IE. There are two concepts proposed by them, and they are:

- Environmental Approach proposed by Floridi; and
- Digital ontology proposed by Capurro.

These two models differ in the fundamental concepts which formed the basis for this classification.

**Environmental Approach:** The view of IE as an environmental model which states that there is connection between the value obtained from the information of an object to the existence of the object. There is a need for the objects of information to grow; hence, the contamination of the infosphere is the major component of the IE.

However, the environmental model proposed by Floridi does not concentrate on the social concerns of IE which is a major drawback.

**Digital Ontology:** The IE of a digital objects with respect to their interaction with the surrounding environment and their respective social, intercultural, and moral values is called Digital Ontology. The IE is related to the principles of ecological, political, economic, and cultural domains (Capurro, 2005).

Therefore, it is necessary that IE should be applicable to those issues that are intercultural in nature with respect to the digital divide. They could also be applicable in a scenario where a change in behavior is witnessed and a situation where moral values of life become affected by developmental changes of the infosphere and internet.

### 5.3.4. Information Ethics from the Point of View of Systems and Technology

There are a lot of concepts connected to the IE of the information society that have risen from the related areas like digital and computer ethics. Broad availability of information in digital forms has generated many concrete issues concerning ownership of digital records and digital information.

The various aspects related to IE are categorized into different types (Zwass, 2010). They are:

- Privacy;
- Accuracy;
- Property;
- Access.

These are the fundamental concepts that are used in the design, expansion, and utilization of information systems.

The right of the people to secure the information about their personal lives from unlicensed access is called privacy. Privacy is considered by different scientists of IE as a basic aspect of social science (Cibangu, 2012).

The issues related to the regulation of clicks are generally connected to the misuse of information with an intent to do marketing. For instance, the ACM code of ethical conduct is known to make experts to value privacy as a fundamental rule, and many industries are reviewing these issues as an aspect of self-regulation. The correct and accurate data is connected to the collecting of information of high standards and maintain privacy and security of information systems. The accurate information must be totally mistake-free with all the relevant content with comprehensive information.

The right of possession of information must generally be obtained by following the law. Therefore, intellectual property is an integral component of ethics as it deals with illegal possession of creative content by a person or an organization. The expansion of internet services, however, has forged an inviting environment for infringement of this right.
