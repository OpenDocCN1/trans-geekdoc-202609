Gorniak’s hypothesis reiterated the same. However, Gorniak did not foresee that IE would pave the way for concepts that would be applicable globally. She projected that a global ethics theory would develop with time because the internet is universal and consequently the ethics conversation centering around it also has a global character.

Gorniak was accurate in her to prophesy. Global network, especially the internet, has been fundamental in connecting human beings worldwide. Computer ethics appears to be progressing into an expansive and even more significant field today. It might be rightly termed as “global IE.” For the first time in history, steps are being taken on a global level, to formulate mutually agreed standards of conduct. Also, the need for securing human values and advancing them is being felt worldwide.
