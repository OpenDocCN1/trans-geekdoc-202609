Information Ethics (IE) is defined as a current alternative to the traditional ethical theory to account for the moral phenomena. It is the subject of additional research to explore how it can be made to tolerate upon the practical issues and problems in Information and Communication Technologies (ICTs) and also to validate that it has a descriptive and justificatory surplus as related to the traditional ethical normative theories. Reconstructed concepts are evolved, for example, contestatory democracy, privacy as data protection, justice as fairness, function as high-level architectural principles for the design and framework of information systems and ICTs applications. The chapter deals with the branches of ethics. Mainly, it describes the normative ethical theories. Future projections are also described in the latter part of this chapter.
