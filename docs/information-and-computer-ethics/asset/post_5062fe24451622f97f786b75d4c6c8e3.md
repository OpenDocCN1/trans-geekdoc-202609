### 8.4.1. International Efforts on Legislations

The government department are very much aware of their people, and existing laws are likely to be unenforceable against such crimes in many countries around the world. The growing threat to individuals is beginning to claim attention in national and international community. The limited amount of security from the legal aspect indicates that the organizations and administrations should depend completely on the technologies available to them to guard themselves against the people who may spread incorrect information or the ones who handle the information in a very unhealthy and irresponsible way by either stealing it or destroying an important part of it.

### 8.4.2. Encryption Technology to Minimize Harmful Actions on Internet

During the time of World War II, it was very normal to use jumbled messages that were encrypted in secret patterns so that the enemy would not be able to interpret the messages even if they get hold of it in some way. Today on the Internet, these scrambled messages are quite popular for protecting and defending the credit card numbers and private information from enemy hackers. The mathematical method that is commonly found on the internet today that uses jumbling words to encode a message is known as ‘encryption.’

Data can be converted in several forms such as e-mail, transactions, files, web information transmissions, etc. The encryption is generally used in internet shopping, stock trading, and online banking transactions, in ATMs and in electronic business-to-business transactions.

The data transmissions that happen on the homepage use a widely known method to encrypt the data in order to secure it, which is called Secure Sockets Layer (SSL). This kind of encryption is easy to be traced back to its source of the web page that begins with “https:” instead of the previously and commonly used “http.” There is one more indication that informs about the use of SSL, in which there can be seen a gold lock on the status bar of the Web Browser.

Another indication is the golden outline that forms around the lock of the Security toolbar button. The most widely known methods to encrypt the important files and messages on the email are DES (Data Encryption Standard), RSA (Rivest Shamir and Adleman), and PGP (Pretty Good Privacy).

Each employee has a key method to encrypt data, which requires the use of two keys, a public key and a private key. The sender encrypts the email using the receiver’s public key to encrypt an email message, which is widely known and can be obtained from an Internet public-key server or company. The email message is then sent in a locked, unreadable format.

In such a case the one who is receiving the message makes use of the key that they hold in private and which is a secret one, only used to decode the message. Some time ago, a new Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) is a kind of security feature taken into consideration by the government of the United States. An encryption technique known as Rijndael is created by two coders from Belgium and is made in such a way that it proves to be a much safer option than the one that was being used earlier and can be operated on more than one hardware platform and in association with different software.

This method of coding makes use of very little memory and makes for the defensive mechanism against various kinds of attacks in the digital world. This kind of technique is quite significant, and it even grows more when the data goes through the systems that are shared or the networks that lack basic security features so as to provoke people to breach into the data networks illegally. In such cases the data which is in the category of being sensitive needs to be encrypted. One more kind of technique for encryption is known as “Digital ID.”

Email is the method of exchanging messages between people using computers or electronic devices and people use this source to share some important information which can be confidential and important to another user and ensure that messages sent cannot be stopped and read by anyone other than the future receiver.

By using “digital IDs” in MS Outlook Express, senders can prove their identity in electronic transactions in a way like showing driver’s license when people cash a check. Like encryption technique, digital ID is composed of a “public key,” a “private key,” and a “digital signature.”

The techniques discussed above are used to transact data in private modes and in secrecy in order to disable people from indulging in unethical deeds. Moreover, these techniques may also be employed to avoid any unauthorized changes in the encrypted messages on the digital networks. There is one more kind of security system that is usually used to protect the computer networks known by the name of firewalls.

The firewall of the internet can be referred to as a system that commands the accessibility of information between the digital networks. The internet can be taken as a huge network of different digital networks that should be kept away from each other’s territory.

This internet firewall provides help to fulfill two important functions. Firstly, it regulates the accessibility of the external networks to the internet systems. Secondly, it makes sure that only a controllable amount of information can be transmitted to the external world from the internal network. Thus, it contributes towards regulating and developing the policies for control of accessibility for the organizations.

Such strategies should address a growing number of worldwide community issues as these issues can be solved when the society provide people with jobs that are challenging, fulfilling, and interesting and the businesses and governments deal with possible exploitation of poor countries by wealthy countries and multinational conglomerates.

Many more ethical issues directly need the attention of governments, educational institutions, businesses, public, and private individuals worldwide. Strategies and Guidelines should be applied so that global information can be exploited in a socially and ethically sensitive way for future benefit and applications.
