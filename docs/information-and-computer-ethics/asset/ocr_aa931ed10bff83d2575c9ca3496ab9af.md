# 3.3.4. Character-Based Theory

Many of the researchers have observed that the various theories based on the repercussions as well as the deontology are basically act-centered, and thus, they tend to label them as the specified acts, being positive or negative, correct or wrong.

Being a dissimilarity to this, the ethics or the morality which is based on the character, is considered to be agent-centered that means that the righteousness is not just pertaining within the act but within that specified person, wherein the researchers tend to label or observe them if the agent is a great or honorable person in accordance with their respective habits as well as by their character.

In the theory of Kant, a person doing good acts morally by habit is not considered as good morally, as the moral goodness needs the moral agent to resolve what should be done in accordance with the reason.

Having a consideration of an instance: there pertained a eulogy regarding a lady, some years back, in one of the provided communities belonging to the church, who had been addressed as being the most honorable person as she was good to everyone, habitually, in spite of judging them for who they are.
