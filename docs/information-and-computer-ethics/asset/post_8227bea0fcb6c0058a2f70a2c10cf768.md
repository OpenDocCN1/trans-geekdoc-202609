## 8.7. CHALLENGES AND ISSUES RELATED TO CYBERCRIME

Cybercrime spans across a variety of activities. At one end are crimes that involve fundamental openings of personal or corporate privacy, such as attacks on the integrity of information held in numerical collections, stores, and the use of criminally gained digital information and data to blackmail a firm or individual.

Midway along the spectrum lie transaction-based crimes such as money laundering, fraud, trafficking in child pornography, digital piracy, money laundering, and counterfeiting. It is difficult to find the criminals who has done crime over the internet. The person who is expert or have knowledge in this field can only check what has gone wrong.
