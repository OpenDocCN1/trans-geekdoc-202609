## 2.2.4. Moral Values in Organizing and Synthesizing Information

Additionally, to the storing and communicating the data and information, various information technologies systematize the organization of information and mechanically authoring or synthesizing or acting on modern information. Norbert Wiener first established a theory of automated information synthesis which he then called Cybernetics (Wiener, 1954).

Wiener comprehended that a machine could be planned to collect the information regarding the world, descend logical inferences regarding that information which would indicate positive actions, which the machine could ultimately implement, all without any direct input from a human agent.

Wiener rapidly saw that if his cybernetics vision was apprehended, there would be incredible moral concerns elevated by such machines and he sketched some of them in his book, The Human Use of Human Beings.

Wiener has argued that, whilst such types or forms of technology might have had great impacts on the moral, it was still viable to be enterprising as well as providing with the guidance regarding technology in varied manner that would be helping in having an increment within the abilities of the moral reasoning, belonging to both the machines as well as the human beings.

Here is a condition where a part of IT was making decisions regarding the prospect of immoral activity taking place that gives rise to a small amount of damage to the person that it was trying to aid. Gradually, machines make significant life-changing financial decisions related to people without many mistakes from human agents.

Whether or not, a person will be provided with a credit card, the price that he has to pay for insurance, mortgage loan, etc., is usually determined by a machine. For example, if a person applies for a credit card, the machine will then look for few data points such as credit record, salary, the economic condition of the area, etc. and then evaluate the probability that a person will default on the credit card.

Such possibility will either pass the plethora of embracement or not and thus, helps in determining if one is being provided with the card or not. The machine holds the potential of learning to make great judgments, provided with the outcomes regarding the prior decisions it has already made.
