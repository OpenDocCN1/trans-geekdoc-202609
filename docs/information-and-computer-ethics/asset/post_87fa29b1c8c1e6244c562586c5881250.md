## 7.2. ETHICS FOR THE DIGITAL AGE: WHERE ARE THE MORAL SPECS?

The digital systems, institutions, infrastructures, and applications must be developed in accordance with the moral values so that ethics play an important role in the digital world. The VSD perspective is an important aspect and is not restricted to technology development but is a part of different fields of engineering, medicine, science, and development.

Ethics is an important subject in recent times and if any technology is not approved or does not meet the ethical standards, it would face difficulties in terms of approval, acceptance, and development. Therefore, the different technologies that are being developed in recent times keep in view the different ethical and moral constraints and are developed accordingly. The sudden shift in the technology-centric world towards ethical aspects can be termed as “The Design Turn in Applied Ethics” (Van den Hoven et al., 2017).

### 7.2.1. Value Sensitive Design (VSD)

There were underpasses with low-level for cars to pass and prevent buses from passing through, that were designed by a prominent architect called Robert Moses who was an urban planner. This was a main proposal to encourage private transport especially in the area of Jones Beach. This raised a lot of social and political controversies as it prevented the poor and largely colored population to access the Jones Beach as they mostly traveled by public transport. This low-level underpass was criticized for acting as borders between the wealthy and poor. The major assumption here is that technology is not unbiased with respect to values.

According to the VSD, the process of development of technologies is based directly and systematically on the realization, or suppression, of particular configurations of social, ethical, and political values.

The idea and concept of the incorporation of social and moral values into the development of new technologies started in Stanford in the 1970s, where ethics was the main subject of focus in the study of computer science. It was a central subject of study in Computer Science. This concept of value design later was incorporated into different areas and is called the VSD.

VSD is a key area of research, and there are different research groups that are working in this area. The concept of VSD was initially suggested by Batya Friedman (2001) and followed by him there are a number of people who suggested different theories based on the concept of values and these theories are ‘Values in Design’ at University of California (Bowker; Gregory) at Irvine and NYU and ‘Values for Design’.

There are a lot of methods or approaches for the values in design, and some of the important methods are as follows:

- The primary claim about the values in design is that there are different values that must be understood, analyzed, and incorporated into the existing and upcoming technologies.
- The process of understanding, analyzing, and the embedding of different ethical, moral, and value aspects into technology would pave the way for the novel or future users to use the technology effectively.
- The secondary claim is that values and choices have direct and tangible effects on those factors that are directly or indirectly involved.

Digital technologies are those that are present as computer interfaces, apps, menus, and webpages, search engines that give examples of selective architectures regarding how different people choose, act, and think. The third claim is that the mindful and clear thought process regarding the values is more important to make morally correct inventions. Churchill stated before the House of Commons that: “first we shape our dwellings, and then our dwellings start to shape us.”

The important fundamentals that design the lives of human beings and society are technology and innovation. There must be adequate thought put into understanding the role of technology and the different impact on human beings in terms of technology.

- The important and final claim for value-design is that the moral requirements that must be expressed in the initial stages of the procedure at the beginning of design and development would make a major difference to the value design.
- This approach is comparatively easy and gives rise to a theory called the “Collingridge dilemma,” and this theory postulates that “early in the process of development of a technology, the degrees of freedom for design are significant, but information that could inform design is relatively scarce, while later on in the development of the technology, as information starts to become available, the degrees of freedom in design have diminished.”

### 7.2.2. Responsible Innovation

The innovations and creations team of Google called Google X has developed a novel product called Google Glass. Google Glass has voice-controlled access to internet and augmented reality features. Google Glass was taken for testing in 2014; however, there were a lot of privacy, safety, and security concerns, because of which Google Glass was stopped in 2015.

Google Glass was discontinued because of the easy access to take pictures via the Google Lens which was not accepted by a number of people. The Google Lens was an innovative project which gave the spectacles/glasses a small camera to take pictures while checking out people. The easy access to data and the ability of the Google Lens to take pictures of everything people see gave a number of security and privacy concerns because of which the project was discontinued. Google Glass was an innovative project that was started by Google; however, the major reason for the failure of this project was the ethical and moral considerations that Google failed to consider.
