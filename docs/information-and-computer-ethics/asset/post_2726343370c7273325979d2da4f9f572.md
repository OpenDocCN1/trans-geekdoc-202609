Following Mason, James Parrish recommends four key policies that a social media user must follow so as to assure ethical concern for the privacy of others:

- While sharing the information on social networking sites, it is mandatory to consider the confidentiality of the personal information of a particular individual as well as the privacy of others’ information who may connect to the information that is being shared.
- While sharing information on social networking sites, it is the major concern of an individual who wants to share information, to validate the correctness of the information before sharing it with others.
- A user of social networking site should never post any information related to themselves that in future they might want to retract. Additionally, the user should not post any information that is the product of the mind of any other individual, as once the information is shared on these sites, it will be very difficult to retract.
- It is the major concern of every user of the social networking site to validate the authenticity of a person before permitting the person access to the information that is shared.

These systems are not framed to openly invade on the privacy of the individual. ### 2.4.2. Online Games and Worlds

The very first moral effect one comes across when anticipating online games is the propensity for these games to represent sexism and violence. There are various news stories that claim a kind of cause and effect relationship between real violence and violence in computer games.

The assertion that the violence happening within the video games possesses a causal connection or link with the occurrence of the actual violence has robustly been evaluated by the various social scientists. However, many of the other scientists have argued that as such connection or relationship is being considered as tenuous at best, and that the actual problem at hand is the impact of such games on the moral character of the respective person.

The connection they possess is that the people who tend to design might be realizing that they are making up with the space wherein the people are being intended to spend larger parts of their lives and thus, gets entangled in those actual social as well as economic activities and thus, the designers are required to have a hold of the moral responsibilities, which are almost equivalent to those who might be writing a constitution of politics.

As per Purcell, there is a very small obligation to egalitarianism or democracy by those who develop and own online games, and it requires to be discussed if a greater number of people spend time living in the virtual societies.
