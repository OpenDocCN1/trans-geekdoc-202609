### 3.2.1. Meta-Ethics

The Meta-Ethics generally takes into consideration the behavior or the nature of the moral reasoning as well as the integrities. Meta-ethics is one of the fundamental branches of philosophy, which examines the nature of morality in general, and what justifies moral judgments. The three provided questions that have been scrutinized by the meta-ethics are being provided below:

### 3.2.2. Normative Ethics

In the category of normative ethics, the major focus lies on the process of determination of the basic notion related to the ethical behavior. Various theories based on the normative ethics tend to search or discover so that they could be able to present with the action guides; certain methods for responding to the pragmatic questions. The moral theories of Kant and Bentham are examples of normative theories that seek to provide guidelines for determining a specific course of moral action.

Certain studies based on the normative ethics provides with the problems related to how an individual is required to spend their living along with the way to perform. A theory related to normative ethics belonging to the great life generally tends to probe regarding all the pertaining needs as well as demands of the human beings so that they could be able to lead a great life.

The theory based on this study related to correct actions basically possess their major focus on searching out for the required actions that are ethically conventional in accordance with the set standard.

Alternatively, normative ethics tries to provide a system consisting of principles, procedures, and rules that can be used for determining what a person should or should not do.

A typical approach of “normative ethics” is to define “norms” as reasons to have faith in and to feel. Wherein, ultimately the theory related to the justice for the society has been considered as a trial for seeking the way as to how the society is required to get designed or structured, as well as how to disperse or distribute the goods of society related to the power as well as independence, within the pertaining society.
