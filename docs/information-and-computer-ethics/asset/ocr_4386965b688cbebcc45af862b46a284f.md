Information technology (IT) is defined as an essential part of the practices and institutions of postindustrial society. Also, it is a source of hard moral questions, and hence it is a probing as well as a relevant field of moral theory. The chapter talks about IT and moral methodology of computer ethics, the challenges involved in IT. IT is a universal technology. This is not just regarding the computers, networks or other electronic devices. It is also regarding the information that everyone should be careful of what the IT has. The chapter further describes the future concerns of IT in the context of acceleration of change, artificial intelligence (AI), artificial life (ALife) and robotics.
