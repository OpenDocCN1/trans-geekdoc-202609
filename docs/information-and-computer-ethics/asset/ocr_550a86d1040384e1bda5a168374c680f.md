There are a number of computer technologies that have come up in the recent times such as the tablets, laptops, and smartphones along with multiple novel software, applications, user interfaces and other default settings that form different our ‘choice architectures’ (Thaler and Sunstein, 2010) and our ‘extended minds’ (Clark and Chalmers, 1998). The different uncertainties and ethical aspects are challenged by a set of operations that control the programs that are executed.

There are several thought processes and research in different moral values and ethical considerations that need to incorporate into different technologies. The whole process of research and development is focusing on different ethical and moral aspects as there are a lot of novel and beautiful innovations that were withdrawn, just because they were not able to meet the ethical and moral constraints. Therefore, the computer ethics and moral considerations are an important aspect of any novel technology, and these are integral aspects of any research and development.

The different aspects of research and development are making major studies into different moral and ethical aspects that are pertaining to certain technologies. The ethics and moral values of every technology are dynamic, and they change based on different aspects such as the geography, region, climate, and culture. Therefore, there is need for adequate research for different moral and ethical aspects that can change the whole process of development of a technology.

There are two different aspects of the moral considerations that must be made in the field of information technology (IT), and they are:

+   - Initial factor being “a value turns in engineering design”; and
+   - Second factor that is “a design turns in thinking about values.”

The computers were initially introduced in the mid-twentieth century with the focus on technology, and not much thought was given to them:

+   - The different applications of the computer in the real life;
+   - The use of computers in varied aspects of life;
+   - The impact that computers would have on the social, organizational, and political aspects of the society.

Computers were novel and interesting technological machines which have answers to a variety of problems.
