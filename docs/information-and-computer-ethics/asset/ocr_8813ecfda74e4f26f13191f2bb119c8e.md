# PREFACE

# The aim of the book

The ethical issues in the ever-expanding world of technology are being noted with concern and this book aims at spreading the message of ethical use of the informational technology in all its dimensions.

# Why a “Handbook of Information and Computer Ethics”?

Digitalization and its sporadic growth in various spheres are perhaps one of the biggest boons of the twentieth century. No sphere in life remains untouched by this advent. However, as the digital world expands, it brings with it an added number of threats which make it incumbent for the users to constantly bear in mind the ethical perspectives of the vast amounts of information available. Ethics is the cornerstone in the entire workplace and this becomes all the more relevant in the digital world. Whereas it is essential to follow the ethical norms whilst exploring the digital world, it is becoming the main area of concern when one uses or designs information on a digital platform. This book caters for the ethical requirements that need to be borne in mind while working on digital technology.

It is essential for all users of IT to be thoroughly familiar with the concepts of information ethics and as to how this knowledge can better arm them while they utilize this important source of information in its myriad forms. It is important to understand the relationship between ethics and information technology and be familiar with the development of computer ethics.

This book covers the important aspect of moral technology with respect to computer ethics along with an understanding of the moral challenges that are paramount while developing IT systems and other technologies including a robots and virtual games. The normative theories of computer ethics find a detailed discussion in the book whereby it deals with the various branches of ethics as also how information technology gets affected by globalization.

The digital world cannot remain immune to the criminal activities of the current times. The book takes a look at activities like plagiarism, hacking and cybercrime that have become rampant in this field.
