# 2.8. INFORMATIONAL ORGANISMS AS MORAL AGENTS

The inclusive literature that argues in the service of the outlook of utilizing IT to develop not so moral agents is that of Luciano Floridi (1999, 2002, 2003, 2010b, 2011b), and Floridi with Jeff W. Sanders (2003).

Floridi (2010) in his researches had come up with several observations which tend to explain that, issues elevated by the ethical effects of information technologies strain all the traditional moral theories. To get rid of such rough phases, it has also been debated that what is required is a wider philosophy of information (2002).

After this move, Floridi (2003) claimed that information is an authentic environment of its own. It has its own fundamental values which are in few ways related to the natural environment and in other ways completely foreign. In either way, the consequence is that information is a thing which is worthy of moral concern on its own.
