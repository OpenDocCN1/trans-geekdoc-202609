
## 8.3. ETHICAL ISSUES IN INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY

The business and organization will be more successful only with the high level of technology. For example, goods and services can be exchanged or moved with the relevant information can give profit to the business.

Nowadays, information is necessary to move the business ahead, and IT play a major role in the business, and there can be also sharing of data with the help of software, telecommunication, computer hardware and digital electronics for making the business successful.

According to the experts, ethical attentions for Information and Communications Technology associated issues first looked under the topic “information ethics (IE)” in the Annual Review of Information Science and Technology in 1992. This advises that there is an ethical program related with the use of Information and Communications Technology. Organizations and Individuals then need to be ethically delicate as they organize ICT on their actions and procedures.

The impact of Information and Communications Technology on human relationship has been tremendous. Information and Communications Technology has helped to enhance and improve family relationship like everyone is now connected with the mobile phones, laptops, palmtops, video conferencing, etc. but some of them believe that Information and Communications Technology has separated family and friends from each other.

Information and Communications Technology has allowed new friendship and relationships in virtual communities, and it is important to know that such relationships are genuine. This technology has brought new and different jobs over the internet, but these changes and opportunities are also endangered by problems of unemployment from computer replacing humans.

A wide range of new laws, regulations, rules, and practices are therefore needed if society is to manage these workplace and other changes and development brought about by Information and Communications Technology. Thus, the society need to consider the following ethical and social challenges related to Information and Communications Technology use:

- Recognition for personal and corporate ethics associated with Information and Communications Technology.
- striking a balance between ethical, economic, and technological as well as political considerations.
- Intellectual property rights issue (trademarks, patents, copyright, and trade secrets).
- Non-violation of privacy and associated rights amidst electronic information data mining.
- The opportunity to commit crime with ICT (computer crime).
- Legal issues and limitations.
- Consequence of using ICT.
- Professional responsibilities (Kallman and Grillo, 1996).

Both individuals and organizations are using the information and communication technology which had major impacts on the society along with the use of its enabling technologies. Sometimes, IT experts don’t able to find the solution, and they got confused that how to solve the queries of the users but they try to experiment and resolve it.

The persistent problems raised up by Information and communications technology contain the attack of individual and trade privacy, individual, and societal rights, intellectual property rights, values preservation and responsibility for the values and moments arising from the use of Information and communications technology, etc. There are issues that thrown up main challenges in the area of employment, working conditions and individuality. However, some areas still not have developed and require more progress associated with ICT.

IT plays an important role in various sectors like healthcare sector, finance, commerce, government, education, media, and entertainment and society at large. IT is one of the most challenging effects and can create some negative impacts on the society.

There are problems which has been raised, and it is linked with ethics. The problems can be harmful actions, personal privacy, and access right which are the part of the ethical issues.

IT Sector are allowing the data exchange and high level of information to other countries and different locations in the world and this problem needs to be solved because it is a case of personal privacy.

Sometimes, it is necessary to have the information as it will help the government in find the criminals and terrorists. The information should be used in a right way, and the information should not be shared with so many people. It has now become responsibility and challenge for everyone to maintain the privacy and integrity of data regarding individuals.

The authority should take control of the personal information of the individual, and nobody has the right to disclose any information without any reason. It is necessary to create PIN or password so that the information will not get disclosed.

Another problem which is a part of ethical issues is access right. The government has allowed to use the internet websites, but they are concerned with the untrusted applications which are still working in the internet as there are number of hackers who are involved in these things. They want to remove quickly and want to allow legal websites to the people.

In USA, the highly technological laboratories such as NASA and Los Alamos National Laboratories has tried to search different things and making people aware for the future. The computer hackers have tried to hack the information from the military computers and US government offices so that they have to increase their computer security and build new applications so that they find the hackers who are illegal doing the wrong things.

Loss of property, loss of information and property damage and unwanted environmental impacts are all become a part of computer ethics which is considered as negative consequences.

There can be an issue when the virus take place in the computer system. The virus can make harm to the important files which can also include the loss of data in the system. ### 8.3.1. Plagiarism

Plagiarism can be defined as an amount of work which is copied from others, and the user submits the same work in its own way which it has copied. The individual follows the regular practice of copying the work which save the time and effort of the individual. This is considered as a highly unethical practice because the internet provides information of all types and it is easy to do.

Education: Education can be known for its online learning and students can use some tools to learn new things, but this can also distract them from studying because they also get some wastage materials on the internet. The Internet has had a variety of effects, some of which are quite disturbing.

Education is important in every aspects and computer play a major role in the education as it helps in developing the technical skills of the persons. The computer labs have increased in the schools, colleges, and universities for teaching more and more students. Teaching more students at one time can save efforts and time for the teachers, but they should make sure that they will teach with an interest. It has become important for the individuals to do work practically so that they will develop their skills which will help them in future.

Teachers also find it easy that the students prefer to research their papers with the help of internet rather than issuing book from library. In a related effect, the Internet has brought plagiarism into the computer era in two distinct senses.

The students may find simple and easy while working on the systems as they have electronic texts which they can copy and paste and it saves time of the individual.

There are lot of sources which are available in the internet so the students can just download the important papers which are used as a free source for the students. They don't need to go to purchase the papers as the papers are mainly available on the internet.

Teachers can mail the students easily, so the students get updated about all the things that have studied in the class or for future purposes. Teachers now have access to databases of electronically submitted papers and can easily compare their own students' papers against a vast archive of sources. Even a simple online search can sometimes find where one particularly well-turned phrase originally appeared.
