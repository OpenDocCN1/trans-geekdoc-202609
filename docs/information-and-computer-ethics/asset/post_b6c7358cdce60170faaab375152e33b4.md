### 4.8.1. Information and Copyright in Education

That one sector that has gained a lot of importance in the market in the past few years due to the advancement in technology and the internet is the Commerce industry. But, in the case of education sector, the picture is not similar. There are more computers in the classroom than ever before, but there is scant evidence that they enhance the learning of basic skills in reading, writing, and arithmetic.

Having reachability to digital information is easy; it has also become apparent that most students now see libraries as antiquated institutions better used for their computer terminals than for their book collections.

Education has become easy for students who have better hands-on acquiring information in ethical as well as in an unethical manner. In a related effect, the Internet has brought plagiarism into the computer era in two distinct senses.

- Electronic texts have made students’ lives easy as they can easily, “cut, copy, and paste” without giving content their own perspectives. They prepare a set of completely plagiarized reports which add nothing to their knowledge.

- For students, it is easy to purchase a test paper on a website, or they even get the opportunity to copy paste the assignment they have received for their internal examinations. They even commission original term papers for a fixed fee. Ironically, what the Internet gives, it also takes away.

The process of copying a software in illegitimate ways is a matter of serious concern and as per the projections, about half of the programs that run on the computers of various individuals, are copies that have resulted from piracy. The programmers spend a lot of time in designing various software and programs, using a code that is finely detailed and in expanded form and hence, it needs to be secured.

Although, some of the people may put a point forward that piracy, to some extent, should be legalized as it can result in bringing up more and more people who are familiar with the knowledge of computers and digital world. But, as far as the major industries and organizations are concerned, this situation of piracy is a subject of grave concern and may cause severe damage to a company’s profits and reputation.
