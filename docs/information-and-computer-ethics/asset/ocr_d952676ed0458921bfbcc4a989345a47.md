The health care sector would have seen major changes with respect to the maintenance of patient files and would have achieved better data security with respect to the patient data; however, the moral and ethical aspects have stopped the innovation in these areas.

These innovations would have been a huge revolutionary success if the engineers would have considered the moral and privacy aspects and combined these aspects into their innovations. The process of considering the moral and ethical aspects and giving them due importance while making an innovation is an important aspect of any innovation.

The major take-home point from these examples is that there is need to consider the moral and ethical values must be given importance as the “nonfunctional requirements,” at an initial stage of the development of a project. The moral and ethical values are called as the nonfunctional requirements and the storage, capacity, speed, bandwidth, compliance with technical standards and protocols are called as the “functional requirements.”

The smart meter and the electronic patient record system are undoubtedly the best innovative designs; however, any idea or design is considered to be a true innovation only if it has the capacity to fulfill all the requirements and enter into the market effectively. Both these projects failed because of lack of proper value sensitive thinking.

Value sensitive thinking is a process of understanding the different aspects of a design such as the efficiency, privacy, sustainability, and safety. A prior evaluation of all these factors and the impact of the innovation into the new market would save a lot of time, energy, and resources. If the engineers have carefully analyzed and understood these aspects, they should have integrated them into their innovations which would have made these innovations a great success.

The smart meter is not made available because of privacy and security concerns, but it can be redesigned by installing privacy and security features. There is a lot of debate and several oppositions regarding the redesigning of the smart meter, among which the initial one is regarding the moralism and the second one is regarding the relativism.
