## 7.3. AUTONOMOUS AGENTS

### 7.3.1. Characteristics of Autonomous Agents

There are two major aspects in the field of Autonomous Intelligence called as the ‘Strong’ and ‘weak.’ The strong in autonomous intelligence refers to the development of technologies which are better than human intelligence and can-do aspects better than human beings, whereas the weak refers to the beings with limited capabilities which are not better than human intelligence in any way. The examples of weak autonomous intelligent agents are computer vision systems, game-playing software, and others.

There are a lot of debates on the strong vs. weak concept, but the importance of all of them is the philosophical debate regarding which of them is better. However, the important of all the autonomous intelligent agents is that they are not part of the strong autonomous intelligence but are mostly these are technologies and applications that imitate natural intelligence.

The autonomous agents are designed to extremely smart and have a very high intellect, and they can be either in the form of software or this software that are integrated into a physical system called as the robots. The major change in the operation of the softbots and the software is dependent on the type of surroundings that these softbots must function. The environment in which these softbots interact, and function is called as the cyberspace. Softbots are capable of communicating to the human beings from their virtual world, and they are interesting aspects of technology.

The software autonomous agents and the softbots/robots do not have many differences when compared to one another. The important aspect of any softbot or robot is that they have an inbuilt software system which is called as the firmware which would enable the robot function according to the designed capabilities.

The complete understanding of the autonomous agents gives us a comprehension that these are predesigned software programs. Autonomous agents are unique in their own way, as every computer program is designed and programmed differently, therefore each autonomous agent is different. The difference between each autonomous agent make them unique, and these autonomous agents are independent in their own way.

Autonomous agents have a wide range of applications because of their uniqueness and independent functioning; as a result, they are widely used in cyberwarfare. The autonomous agents have been defined differently by a number of people; however, Franklin and Graesser gave the most standard definition for the autonomous agents, which is as follows “An autonomous agent is a system situated within and a part of an environment that senses that environment and acts on it, over time, in pursuit of its own agenda and to effect what it senses in the future.”

The major aspects regarding the autonomous agents which are part of this definition are as follows (Figure 7.1):

- The autonomous intelligent agent is completely and stringently related to the surroundings.
- The autonomous agent that is present away from the respective surroundings is known to have no use or function, or it is not considered to be an autonomous agent.
- There are proper sensors that are used to communicate with the surroundings.
- There are proper actuators along with the sensors that are used to interact with surroundings and are known to function in a way that can create some impact on their surroundings.
- The autonomous agent’s function with a defined motto or aim which is called as ‘agenda’ by the Franklin and Graesser.
- The autonomous agents that are made to be used in the warfare are called as the targets.
- The functions of a completely autonomous agent are established over a period so that they have a sustainability related to an assigned function.
- There is certain feature of an autonomous agent that are not mentioned here; however, they can be understood and comprehended from the knowledge that is gained from the aspects mentioned here.
