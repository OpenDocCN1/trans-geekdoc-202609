# 4.8.2. Information and Copyright in Piracy

The process of copying a software in illegitimate ways is a matter of serious concern and as per the projections, about half of the programs that run on the computers of various individuals, are copies that have resulted from piracy. The programmers spend a lot of time in designing various software and programs, using a code that is finely detailed and in expanded form and hence, it needs to be secured.

Although, some of the people may put a point forward that piracy, to some extent, should be legalized as it can result in bringing up more and more people who are familiar with the knowledge of computers and digital world. But, as far as the major industries and organizations are concerned, this situation of piracy is a subject of grave concern and may cause severe damage to a company’s profits and reputation.

# 4.8.3. Information and Copyright in File Sharing

College students have been at the leading edge of the growing awareness of the centrality of intellectual property in a digital age.

The characteristics of the recording and movie firms to not be able to avoid the sharing of file has resulted in them designing new forms and highly secured form of protection on the compact disks and digital video drives. In a certain incident that raised a lot of controversy, Sony Corporation had brought CDs to the market in 2005 that were protected against piracy. But it had a code that acted like a computer virus when inserted in the computer system. This kind of software provided a better gateway for the hackers to get into the users’ computer sets and take control of their systems.
