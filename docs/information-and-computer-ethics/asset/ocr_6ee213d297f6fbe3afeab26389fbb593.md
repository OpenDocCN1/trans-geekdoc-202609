Susan and Michael Anderson have gathered a lot of articles related to this topic in their book, *Machine Ethics* (2011), and also, Rocchi Luppicini has a section of his anthology dedicated to this topic in the *Handbook of Research on Technoethics* (2009).

# 2.7.1. Information Technology as a Model for Moral Discovery

Patrick Grim has been a great follower of the notion that philosophy must apply different information technologies so as to illustrate and automate philosophical thought experiments.

Also, Peter Danielson (1998) has extensively written on this topic with his book *Modeling Rationality, Morality, and Evolution* with a lot of initial research in the computational theory of morality focused on utilizing computer models in order to explain the evolution of cooperation between simple software AI or ALife agents.
