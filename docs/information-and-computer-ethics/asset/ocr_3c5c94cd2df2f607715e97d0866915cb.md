According to the VSD the process of development of technologies is as follows directly and systematically on the realization, or suppression, of particular configurations of social, ethical, and political values.

The idea and concept of the incorporation of social and moral values into the development of new technologies started in Stanford in the year 1970s, where ethics was the main subject of focus in the study of computer science. Where it was a central subject of study in Computer Science. This concept of value design later was incorporated into different areas and is called as the VSD.

VSD is a key area of research, and there are different research groups that are working in this area. The concept of VSD was initially suggest by Batya Friedman (2001) and followed by him there are a number of people who suggested different theories based on the concept of values and these theories are ‘Values in Design’at University of California (Bowker; Gregory) at Irvine and NYU and ‘Values for Design’.
