## Information and Computer Ethics

The ethical issues in the ever-expanding world of technology are being noted with concern and this book aims at spreading the message of ethical use of the informational technology in all its dimensions.

Digitalization and its sporadic growth in various spheres are perhaps one of the biggest boons of the twentieth century. No sphere in life remains untouched by this advent. However, as the digital world expands, it brings with it an added number of threats which make it incumbent for the users to constantly bear in mind the ethical perspectives of the vast amounts of information available. Ethics is the cornerstone in the entire workplace and this becomes all the more relevant in the digital world. Whereas it is essential to follow the ethical norms whilst exploring the digital world, it is becoming the main area of concern when one uses or designs information on a digital platform. This book caters for the ethical requirements that need to be borne in mind while working on digital technology.

It is essential for all users of IT to be thoroughly familiar with the concepts of information ethics and as to how this knowledge can better arm them while they utilize this important source of information in its myriad forms. It is important to understand the relationship between ethics and information technology and be familiar with the development of computer ethics.

This book covers the important aspect of moral technology with respect to computer ethics along with an understanding of the moral challenges that are paramount while developing IT systems and other technologies including robots and virtual games. The normative theories of computer ethics find a detailed discussion in the book whereby it deals with the various branches of ethics as also how information technology gets affected by globalization.

The digital world cannot remain immune to the criminal activities of the current times. The book takes a look at activities like plagiarism, hacking and cybercrime that have become rampant in this field. The international standards lay down to deal with the challenges facing the ethical concerns in the development of informational technology form an important part of the book. One man’s morals may be another man’s norms. The book brings out the ethical dilemmas faced by the designers of information technology. Cyber space cannot remain immune from the conflicts of the nations. The book explores these and talks about the various existing ACM code of ethics that the IT professionals need to follow while designing international codes and software. The requirement for a legal framework to ensure that information technology is morally ethically correct and binding finds a detailed discussion in the book.

A large number of autonomous bodies have made a foray into the social media channels and have gone on to strengthen their presence. The book delves upon the need for these channels to be diligent and follow the ethical codes while dealing with the data of their users. The book covers the various challenges faced in implementation of the regulations that exist and culminates with providing information on the existing malicious software that can pose a challenge to the user. The handbook shall be beneficial equally to novices and experts in the field as it guides them through the sensitive issue of morals and ethics involved in the optimum and efficient use of IT.
