
# Broker vs Exchange Comparison

| Feature | Broker | Exchange |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Liquidity | Deep | Average |
| Market Depth | Could be high | Limited |
| Customer Service | Experienced | Limited |
| Features | For the more experienced trader | For average users |
| Fiat Currency | Yes | Yes/No |
| Number of Supported Cryptocurrencies | Low | High |
| Spread | Tight | Wide |
| Safety of Funds | Protected | Semi-protected |
| Regulated | Yes | Location-dependent |

So, which one do you choose? I cannot recommend that you use either a broker or an exchange. Each suits different clients, and which one you choose is dependent on your trade purpose and requirements.

If you are trading in large amounts of currency, choose a broker service. They are regulated and audited, so your money is safer, and they store your funds in their bank accounts. If they are subject to a hack attack, clients have the chance of being compensated. Professional traders also get access to a range of technical tools. Brokers are also ideal for traders who want to get involved in mid or short-term margin trading as they can take full advantage of price fluctuations.

However, exchanges come with higher risks, leading to potentially higher gains. Even though their fees are higher, they offer a wide range of trading pairs and even offer access to some of the newer altcoins that are highly volatile and have the potential for a significant profit. Exchanges are ideal for those making physical cryptocurrency investments—they own the assets directly—who are looking to buy and wait for mid to the long-term for the prices to rise.

The only recommendation I can give you is to ensure you do thorough research before deciding and always tailor your recommendation to your requirements. Nothing stops you from using an exchange for some trades and a broker for others.

# Top Five Cryptocurrency Brokers

Because all brokers are different, you need to research which one/s suits your requirements as an investor or trader. The following are five of the very best brokers available today:

## eToro

eToro allows you to trade in cryptocurrencies without paying a commission, but you do have to pay a spread based on what crypto you are trading. For example, if you trade in Bitcoin, there's a markup of 75 basis points, but for the newer, less well-known cryptocurrencies, that goes up to 500 basis points, equivalent to 5%. eToro offers a feature called `CopyTrader`, allowing traders to follow top traders on the eToro platform and copy their trades. However, do plenty of research first—although you get the gains they make, you also get their losses too.

*   **Commission** - $0 and a spread markup depending on the traded cryptocurrency
*   **Account Minimum** - $50

## TD Ameritrade

One of the best full-service traders, TD Ameritrade offers standard products like bonds and stocks and now offers Bitcoin futures. However, you cannot trade directly in BTC, and if you want to get involved in futures trading, you need to meet the account minimum.

*   **Commission** - $2.25 per contract
*   **Futures Account Minimum** - $25,000

## Binance.US

Binance is one of the more specialized platforms, allowing the purchase and sale of digital currency. This includes Ethereum, Bitcoin, and other large, well-known ones and lots of smaller ones—around 60 in total. The commission is low, and the more you trade, the cheaper it gets. Trading fees start at 0.1% of the trade value, which equates to $10 per $10,000 traded, and from there, they depend on how much you have traded over the last 30 days.

*   **Commission** - 0.1% or less of the transaction value
*   **Account Minimum** - $0

## Coinbase

Coinbase is a specialized platform focused on cryptocurrency and direct trading with more than a hundred supported cryptocurrencies. You also have the choice of storing your cryptocurrency in a vault that offers time-delayed withdrawals, providing extra protection. However, they do have a heavy commission structure. The markup spread is 0.5%, and then a transaction fee is added on top, depending on how much is transacted and where the funds came from. It is cheaper to join their Pro platform.

## Interactive Brokers

Interactive Brokers allows direct trades in four cryptocurrencies for an incredibly low commission. You can also buy Ethereum and Bitcoin futures instead of the cryptocurrencies. Interactive Brokers also allows Bitcoin futures to be purchased on the Chicago Mercantile Exchange. Bitcoin contracts cost $11.02 and allow five coins, while Ethereum contracts cost $9.02 for 50 coins. They also provide a full range of investment offerings, allowing you to purchase pretty much anything that trades on any exchange.

*   **Commission** - 0.12 to 0.18% of the trade's value and $10.02 per futures contract
*   **Account Minimum** - $0

# Best Crypto Exchanges

And these are some of the best cryptocurrency exchanges you can find online:

## Crypto.com

One of the biggest exchanges, it is also one of the fastest-growing and, although its base is in Hong Kong, it offers plenty of support for customers in the USA. It offers access to over 90 crypt coins and tokens worldwide and 50 for US-based customers. They also offer excellent crypto savings rates and interest is paid weekly, and they offer a debit card for your account. However, there is no desktop platform at the moment; it is purely app-based. The fee structure is also quite complicated, and customer support is somewhat limited.

## Voyager

Voyager's popular platform was one of the first crypto exchanges to be publicly traded. Like Crypto.com, they are app-based rather than desktop and provide a trading structure free of commission. Most of the biggest cryptocurrencies are supported, and interest on assets is paid at an attractive rate. One of the biggest pluses is that when you have traded $100 in cryptocurrency, you can earn $25 in BTC. The biggest downside is that withdrawals can take a day or more.

## BlockFi

A crypto investment platform, BlockFi lets you lend your holdings and earn interest on them. And rather than selling your crypto tokens and coins, you can borrow against them, using them as collateral. They sometimes offer a bonus of up to $250 when you deposit 25 and leave it where it is for a set period. They don't charge any commission or fees and are based in the USA. BlockFi is fully regulated, ensuring a level of protection. However, they don't support many different cryptos, and you can only make a limited number of free withdrawals before they start charging you.

## Binance

While Binance isn't quite so simple to use as some on this list, it is one of the best platforms for cryptos like Neo and Cardano. They are also one of the best-known and most robust trading exchanges. Their mobile app is not terribly easy to use, but they have a web platform. There are also two versions—`Binance.com`, which is international and has many tokens and coins for investment purposes, and `Binance.us`, the US-based version, which is somewhat more limited in what they offer and isn't available in all US states. Customer support is a little limited, but their transaction times are fast compared to others.

## Kraken

Kraken was one of the first crypt exchanges, and they offer a decent selection of tokens and coins for trading and investment purposes, along with margin trading. However, consider that Kraken is a real exchange and may not be as user-friendly as other top platforms. It is one of a limited number of platforms that trade in DOGE and some of the other riskier tokens. Again, US-based customers have a much smaller selection of tokens and coins than international customers.
