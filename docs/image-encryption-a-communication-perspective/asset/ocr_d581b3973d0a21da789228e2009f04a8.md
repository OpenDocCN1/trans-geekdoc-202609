The assumption made that the signal samples are mutually independent and uncorrelated is not true when oversampling is applied. Also, this expression is not accurate for a small number of subcarriers because the Gaussian assumption does not hold in this case [156–159]. It was shown that the PAPR of an oversampled signal for N subcarriers is approximated by the distribution for αN subcarriers without oversampling, where α is larger than 1 [160]. In other words, the effect of oversampling is approximated by adding a certain number of extra signal samples.

The CDF of the PAPR of an oversampled signal is thus given by

$P(PAPR \le z) \approx (1 - e^{-z})^{\alpha N}$ (8.23)
