In general, there are two types of cryptosystems: (1) symmetric- (private-) key cryptosystems and (2) asymmetric- (public-) key cryptosystems. Most people have chosen to call the first group simply symmetric-key cryptosystems, and the popular name for the second group is just public-key cryptosystems.

# 2.3.3 Classification According to Percentage of Encrypted Data

With respect to the amount of encrypted data, the encryption can be divided into full encryption and partial encryption (also called selective encryption) according to the percentage of the data encrypted.
