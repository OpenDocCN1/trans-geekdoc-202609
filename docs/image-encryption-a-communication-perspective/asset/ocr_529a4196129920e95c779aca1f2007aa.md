One of the important factors in examining the encrypted image is visual inspection. The Cameraman image has been encrypted with the AES and the RC6 algorithms in the ECB, CBC, CFB, and OFB modes and the ECB mode with preprocessing by the proposed network for comparison purposes. The results of these experiments are shown in Figures 6.9 and 6.10, respectively. The encryption in the ECB mode with the proposed network solves the problem.
