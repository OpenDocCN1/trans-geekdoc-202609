# 7.5 Communication Channels

# 7.5.1 Additive White Gaussian Noise Channel

For an additive white Gaussian noise (AWGN) channel, the received signal $r(t)$ is expressed as [141]

$r(t) = s(t) + n(t)$

(7.22)

where $s(t)$ is the transmitted signal, $r(t)$ is the received signal, and $n(t)$ is the AWGN with power spectral density given by [141]

$\Phi(f) = N_0/2$ [W/Hz]

(7.23)

where $N_0$ is a constant that is often called the noise power density.
