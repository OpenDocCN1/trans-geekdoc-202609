The effect of noise on the chaotic encryption with different modes of operation and block sizes was also studied, and the results are given in Figure 5.7. It is clear from this figure that all modes of operation have approximately the same performance in the presence of noise, and the performance is improved at high SNR values. It is also clear that the block size has no effect on the noise immunity of the algorithm in all implementation modes.
