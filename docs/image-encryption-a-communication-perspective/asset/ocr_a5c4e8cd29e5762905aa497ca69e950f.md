Figures 9.13 to 9.15 illustrate the variation of the PSNR with the $E_b/N_0$ for the FFT-OFDM with $G = 10, 16, 32$, with different encryption algorithms in a frequency-selective channel. Without pilot symbol estimation (PSE), it was shown that fading disrupts the PSNR performance of FFT-OFDM systems regardless of the encryption algorithm. However, Figures 9.16 to 9.18 show that, with PSE, the PSNR performances of all systems were better than those without PSE.

The DES, AES, and RC6 provide lower PSNR performance for the FFT-OFDM system than the chaotic algorithm because these algorithms have a diffusion mechanism, which highly reduces the noise immunity. On the other hand, the chaotic algorithm is more robust to noise.
