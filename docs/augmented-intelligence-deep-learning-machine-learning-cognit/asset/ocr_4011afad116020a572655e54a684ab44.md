# 8.3.1. What is Computational Intelligence (CI)

Computational Intelligence (CI) is the concept, design, practicality, and improvement of computational paradigms influenced by biology and language. Traditionally, the three pillars of CI are neural networks, fuzzy systems, and evolutionary calculations. However, over time, many natural factors have stimulated the development of calculation standards. Therefore, CI is a developing area and added value; three main elements include computational models such as environmental intelligence, artificial life, cultural learning, artificial endocrine network, social reasoning, and artificial hormone network. CI plays a major role in creating a successful intellectual system, game, and cognitive development system.
