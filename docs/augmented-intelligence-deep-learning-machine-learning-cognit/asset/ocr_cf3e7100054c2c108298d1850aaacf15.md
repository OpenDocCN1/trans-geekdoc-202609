The formula used to convert frequency to mel is given by Equation (5a):

$m = 2595 * log_{10}\left(1 + \frac{f}{700}\right)$

(5a)

The sum of energies in each mel filter is taken and passed through discrete cosine transform (DCT). The DCT extracts the most important information and peaks from the audio signal. The peaks represent the gist of the information [15]. This complete process creates a new spectrum known as Mel cepstrum as shown in Fig. (4.13).

Typically, the first 13 coefficient obtained from the Mel frequency spectrogram is known as MFCC. It contains the most useful information of the audio signal as it can be feed into machine and deep learning algorithms consisting of recurrent neural networks (RNNs) or other memory networks for audio and signal analysis. It is one of important and state of the art tool to extract information from the audio signal.
