Indirect Reputation (IDR): Indirect reputation is the product of the normalized recommendation reputation value of recommender with recommendation value for DH. IDR of particular recommender X is given by equation 6.7.

IDR_X = Σ_{i=1}^n (WX_i / Σ WX) * Rec_{(X_i-DH)}  (6.7)

WX_i: Weight of recommender ‘i’ of ‘X’ recommender group given by its F-Score

Where x∈ { R, F, N, S } i = Recommender i of group X

Rec_(Xi-DH): recommendation value given by recommender i of group X.

Total indirect reputation (TIDR) is the weighted sum of reputations obtained from four groups of recommenders who had past transactional history with DH. TIDR is given by equation 6.8.

TIDR = α IDR_R + β IDR_F + γ IDR_N + δ IDR_S  (6.8)

where α > β > γ > δ such that α+β+γ+δ=1.

α, β, γ,δ: weight parameters to Relative, Friend, Neighbour and Stranger group respectively

α: weight of Relative recommender group

β: weight of Friend recommender group
