## 2.10. BIOMIMETIC ROBOT RESEARCH

A variety of important robotics have been developed, including actuators and sensors that are not just proprioceptive, tactile, and visual. The ARTS robot is made up of 25 DOFs, 2 visual sensors, 39 sensors, and 3D image stabilization, and has a head, arm, and hand. It contains a multinetwork architecture that is biologically inspired and incorporates progressive and functional learning for motion analysis. A 5-digit biomimetic hand prosthesis is their latest biomechatronic cyber hand (Fig. 2.20), which includes a motor plus embedded biomimetic force and proprioceptive sensors for each digit. Although the number of actuators is controlled, the conformability of the hand and the integrated closed-loop control offer the artificial hand excellent gripping features. This instrument is meant for use by amputees and is thus operated by an interface with the nerves in the remaining arm of the patient.
