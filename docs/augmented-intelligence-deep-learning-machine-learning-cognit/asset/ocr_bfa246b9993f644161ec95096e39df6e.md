# 2.6. BCI EEG SENSORS

Almost all BCI studies require the use of skull-fixed Ag or Au disk electrodes with non-invasive sensors to allow the installation of the EEG electrodes using a certain type of head cap setup. Over the last two decades, there have been little advances in developing these instruments to easily and conveniently position them on the skull of a BCI patient. Head caps have been designed to support the calculation and positioning of the “International 10–20 grid system” EEG electrodes from 64 to 256.
