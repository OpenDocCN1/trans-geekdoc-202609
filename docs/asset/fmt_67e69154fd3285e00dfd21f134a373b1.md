
## `Insurance and Claims Adjudication`
Insurance and claims organisations face fragmented IT systems and data held in silos. These often create inefficiencies, delays, higher than necessary costs and fraudulent activity. Data volumes are increasing, especially with the large amounts of data being generated by IoT that make it necessary to sift through large datasets. Through blockchain, the verification of claims/payments data from third parties can be automated, and insurance companies can easily verify transactions. As a result, the records will show only one version of the truth (Cummings, 2018). The security of blockchain can provide assurance that there is no duplication of data and smart contracts ensure a better customer engagement cycle.

## `Intellectual Property and Media`
Intellectual Property (IP) has faced many challenges, especially with the availability of digitalised content which can be shared with minimal hassle through avenues like the Internet. The cost of software piracy in the US in 2015 was reported at over $9 billion (manufacuring.net, 2016). If IP rights are registered with blockchain technology (in the case of patents, for example), it will be much easier to verify who owns them. Any infringement can be challenged, and doubts can be counteracted by the use of smart contracts in which terms of engagement are set out.

### 6.6.Conclusions
The benefits of blockchain look to be clear and straightforward; blockchain technology can help to secure and protect data in many industry sectors. The defined properties of blockchain include the enhanced security due to encryption, the immutability that prevents any tampering, the auditability which depends on the use of chronological time-stamping/smart contracts and the transparency that allows precise tracking. In this chapter, the importance of securing data, especially in the sectors under heavy cyber attack such as the healthcare or energy sector, was discussed.

如果我们将`blockchain`视为一种能够与其他技术协同工作的机制，那么它有潜力在保护关键数据方面提供额外的安全层。这可以与推出策略/合规政策、培训、保持补丁更新、入侵检测与防御系统（IDPS）等实践协同部署。这些措施旨在进一步保护个人数据的隐私，并通过加密等方法增强安全性，以防传统方法因高级网络攻击和针对性数据窃取手段而失效。此外，随着物联网（IoT）的近期兴起及其为许多组织带来的附加价值，考虑如何处理和保护当今庞大的数据量以及保持其安全至关重要。当前投入使用的数十亿台智能可穿戴设备可能包含个人敏感数据，应通过传统安全措施与区块链相结合的方式予以保护。

区块链本身的安全性颇具趣味性。对机器学习和算法的进一步研究可能有助于保护区块链，因为机器学习和算法能够通过行为监控和剖析收集的数据来检测入侵并识别模式（Xu, 2016）。这将有助于加强对恶意攻击者的防御，并在该方法被人工智能识别时阻止其成功。区块链的更广泛采用也将帮助保护用户免受恶意活动的侵害，因为在开发防范系统方面将投入更多努力。当然，区块链有许多应用场景，可与传统网络安全机制协同工作。目前，区块链是一项强大且可靠的安全选项，是一种有用的机制，能够在众多收集敏感和个人数据的行业提供支持，尤其是那些最易受到网络攻击的行业部门。
