
# Big Data Transportation and Logistics

## 7.1 Transport Organization Optimization

In transport organization, logistics enterprises can utilize big data to analyze commodity specifications and characteristics as well as customers' differentiated needs in terms of timeliness and cost. Then, with the aid of the fastest response mechanism, they can accurately reflect various factors affecting transport schemes—such as which transport scheme to choose, which cost model to adopt, and which transport route to select—thereby planning the most reasonable transport routes.

At the same time, enterprises can quickly analyze route and road conditions through real-time data received during the distribution process, provide early warnings for situations that may affect routes, accurately analyze transport process information, and make transport distribution management more intelligent.

### 7.1.4 Cargo Position Optimization

Reasonable arrangement of cargo positions is of great significance for improving the efficiency of transport route management. For transport lines with large cargo volumes and high delivery frequencies, cargo position optimization can directly bring improvements in operational efficiency and economic benefits.

In fact, which commodities can be placed in combination to increase sorting rates, and which commodities have short storage cycles and high transport requirements, can all be derived through big data association pattern analysis of the data correlation relationships of commodities during the transportation process, thereby planning transport cargo positions more rationally.

The most essential difference between modern logistics and traditional logistics lies in transport organization. Modern logistics is inseparable from the support of information technologies such as big data, which is reflected in the systematization, informatization, networking, and intelligence of transport organization.

Currently, limited by factors such as insufficient computer application penetration, weak awareness of big data application, and uneven employee quality, Chinese logistics enterprises have not yet fully integrated transport organization with functions such as packaging, warehousing, circulation processing, distribution, and information processing. Therefore, attaching importance to the role of big data in transport organization and using it to optimize logistics management decisions is of great significance.

## 7.2 Big Data Transportation and Rural Logistics

Compared with urban logistics, rural logistics involves more transport links. Rural logistics is usually implemented in three steps: warehouse sorting, regional transportation, and last-mile delivery. In fact, in the vast majority of rural areas, apart from last-mile delivery, other logistics links are similar to urban delivery. Last-mile delivery has become the most critical link determining logistics efficiency.

At the end of May 2016, at the 3rd China County-level E-commerce Summit, Xiong Jian, head of Cainiao Network's rural logistics, stated that Cainiao Network is using data systems to integrate the transport capacity of express logistics enterprises nationwide, building a denser and deeper delivery network in rural areas. Through the rural terminal logistics network, over 30% of rural parcels can achieve same-day delivery from county to village, and nearly 70% can achieve next-morning delivery to the village. Cainiao Network has built a terminal network covering over 320 counties and nearly 16,000 rural service stations nationwide.

Given issues in rural logistics such as unclear recipient addresses, route planning detached from reality, and excessive contingent factors, misdelivery and repeated delivery occur frequently, leading to increased delivery costs and decreased recipient satisfaction. Regarding the "last-mile" delivery dilemma in rural e-commerce logistics, although many logistics enterprises have attempted various special delivery models, they have failed to effectively improve efficiency.

In view of this, it is necessary to introduce big data transportation technology to accurately analyze the regional distribution status of rural logistics and the living conditions of recipients, propose targeted solutions, and effectively solve rural logistics problems.

### 7.2.1 Transport Service Innovation

The collection, collation, analysis, and application of big data can effectively integrate rural logistics social transport capacity, improve efficiency, and reduce costs. The key to integrating rural logistics resources lies in establishing a dedicated logistics information platform, which can be implemented through the following two models.

Leading enterprises take the lead in building a dedicated rural logistics information platform and are responsible for its operation. When other logistics enterprises or individuals access and apply it, their various business and shipping data will be recorded. Through massive data analysis, consumption habits, credit records, address accuracy, and path reliability in rural areas will be recorded and presented, providing a strong basis for subsequent customer screening.

#### Build a Special Transport Capacity Ecosystem

By installing `GPS` devices on rural freight vehicles, logistics enterprises can master data from thousands of vehicles. Although these vehicles vary in function and model, they can constitute the foundation of rural logistics transport capacity. In addition, large logistics enterprises can establish private information platforms to master and integrate their own information, thereby providing personalized and professional logistics information services. When the above platforms interconnect, a logistics transport capacity system covering a certain regional rural market can be formed. For example, facing the rural market, if an enterprise has insufficient self-owned capacity, it can obtain capacity from social resources; similarly, social capacity can actively serve enterprises in need based on its own characteristics. Especially under the effect of big data technology, vehicle and cargo source information can be guaranteed to be timely and reliable, which is beneficial to the "last kilometer" of rural market freight transactions.

### 7.2.2 Solving the "Last Kilometer" Transport Capacity Shortage

Big data can solve the problem of insufficient "last kilometer" transport capacity in rural logistics distribution. Logistics enterprises can face the public and rural workers, students, grassroots government staff, or long-distance freight drivers, taxi drivers, etc., by developing open information systems through mobile applications to undertake logistics distribution tasks. Thus, enterprises can obtain "temporary capacity" and manage it effectively, thereby relieving the pressure on self-owned capacity.

This crowdsourcing model based on big data is particularly suitable for promotion in populous rural areas and mountainous regions. Of course, facing the management of such a large amount of mobile logistics transport resources, the massive data processing capability of big data technology must be possessed to complete it.

### 7.2.3 Accelerating Rural Logistics Information Network Construction

The application of big data technology in the field of rural logistics is inseparable from a strong rural logistics information network. This task cannot be completed by a single enterprise and must be coordinated and promoted step by step by relevant government departments.

On the one hand, it is necessary to establish and improve agricultural product information centers and establish a responsive agricultural product information network to ensure that big data can be discovered and transmitted in a timely manner. Especially when the whole rural information network is still under construction, the infrastructure for agricultural product big data collection must be further improved.

On the other hand, it is necessary to cultivate rural logistics professional talents. Through the cooperation of enterprises, government departments, and scientific research institutions, utilizing various educational resources, through training of different forms and levels, improve the quality awareness and industry awareness of logistics service and management personnel, and comprehensively improve the level of rural big data information services through information technology to meet the talent needs of rural logistics services.

## 7.3 Big Data Transportation and Urban Distribution

Currently, Chinese urban logistics lacks overall system planning, and urban logistics distribution routes are chaotic. In addition, urban driving restrictions, traffic congestion, and diversified customer needs lead to relatively low current urban logistics efficiency. Fortunately, with the rapid development of the Internet and information technology, big data transportation will be able to change the traditional urban logistics industry.

On the morning of December 16, the first public logistics information platform in Yiwu City, "Dunda Distribution", was officially put into operation. `Dunda Distribution` is an urban public logistics information platform independently developed by `Yiwu Dunda Supply Chain Management Co., Ltd.` The platform supports functions such as online ordering, online order receiving, online settlement, and online query, realizing information management of the entire process from delivery to signing. It implements unified scheduling of distribution vehicles with clear operating trajectories, fully guaranteeing the timeliness and safety of cargo distribution. At the same time, it reasonably integrates social vehicles to achieve shared distribution, improving vehicle utilization and actual load factor.^(1)

The lack of overall planning in urban logistics leads to repeated investment and huge waste of logistics facilities by different enterprises, restricting the effective utilization and integration of resources. At the same time, each logistics enterprise plans logistics centers and distribution routes based on its own interests and does not share logistics data, making it impossible to form an optimal distribution system at the macro transport level. In addition, the development of mobile Internet has made urban logistics customer needs tend toward customization. Many customers hope to grasp logistics information from multiple aspects and angles at any time and control the whole process.

In view of these situations, big data technology should be applied to solve them from the following perspectives.

### 7.3.1 Government-led Establishment of Urban Logistics Resource Sharing Platform

Under the leadership of competent government departments, build an urban logistics resource sharing platform. The government should effectively allocate local logistics resources and implement monitoring during the urban distribution process. This can not only achieve local economic growth but also fully utilize the big data obtained in logistics distribution to achieve overall planning and management of urban logistics and even more industries. Of course, for the government, directly seeking cooperation with urban logistics enterprises and completing the extraction, collection, and management of logistics big data is a challenging goal.

### 7.3.2 Industry Alliance Building Sharing Platform

Under government leadership, an urban logistics distribution industry alliance involving logistics companies and enterprises can be established. Because logistics industry enterprises master first-hand information on urban logistics, the industry alliance building an information resource sharing platform can solve the problem of logistics big data sources. At the same time, the industry alliance stores all data and information in the same database, facilitating instant sharing and information exchange between logistics nodes, avoiding problems such as traffic congestion, chaotic logistics operations, and low customer satisfaction.

### 7.3.3 Attaching Importance to Urban Logistics Warehousing Platform Construction

To build an urban distribution resource sharing platform, it is necessary to use big data to support the urban logistics warehousing platform. The urban logistics warehousing platform is an important factor affecting urban distribution efficiency. Its foundation includes warehousing centers, scheduling centers, settlement centers, etc. Through this platform, comprehensive analysis is conducted on all human resources, material resources, goods flow, distribution speed, distribution costs, and all distribution channels, thereby calculating the optimal site selection and distribution plan and optimizing resource allocation (see Figure 7.1).

In short, the best way to solve urban distribution problems is: with big data traffic technology led by government departments, participated by logistics enterprises, and technically supported by professional companies as the core, to build an urban distribution information sharing platform.

## 7.4 Big Data Traffic and Express Logistics

Currently, express logistics has penetrated every corner of people's lives. It is no exaggeration to say that the quality of express service often directly affects our life experience. Facing the continuous upgrading of the e-commerce industry, express logistics enterprises also need to actively transform and upgrade, using big data technology to shift from homogeneous competition to diversified competition.

On May 31, 2017, the Internet dispute between `SF Express` and `Cainiao Network` under `Alibaba` became public. `Cainiao Network` issued a statement saying it received a notice from `SF Express` about suspending the `Fengchao` data interface. Subsequently, `SF Express` successively closed the data interface of `Cainiao Fengchao` self-pickup cabinets and the full-link return logistics information of the `Taobao` platform. On June 1, `Alibaba` announced a suggestion for merchants to suspend using `SF Express` for delivery.

However, the statement released by `SF Express` was completely different. `SF Express` stated: In May, `Cainiao Network` stopped cooperation with `Fengchao` due to data security reasons and took down the `Fengchao` interface information at 0:00 on June 1, after which the `Alibaba Group` platform removed `SF Express` from logistics options.

Why did e-commerce and express giants conflict over data? In fact, the outbreak of commercial contradictions reflects that in the era of big data traffic, Chinese express logistics enterprises are very likely to complete industry integration and promotion inside and outside the industry by virtue of data resource value.

In fact, although the business volume of Chinese express service enterprises has grown rapidly, crises lurk beneath the seemingly good situation. Due to the competition mode shifting from scale war to price war, coupled with high labor costs, the gross profit margin of express business has begun to decline significantly; at the same time, they face competition from cross-border e-commerce enterprises such as `JD.com` and `Suning`. Therefore, express enterprises must use big data resource allocation as soon as possible to expand competitive advantages and enhance market influence.

### 7.4.1 Application of Electronic Waybills in Express Logistics

Electronic waybills are an efficient and environmentally friendly information waybill. Unlike traditional express three-part or four-part forms, electronic waybills are printed by computers, carry QR code identifiers, and have a self-adhesive design for easy pasting and tearing.

With electronic waybills, the express system can easily identify, process, and deliver individual parcels among massive packages. Through the data flow supported by electronic waybills, the enterprise's own electronic waybill system can automatically connect all data information of senders, express enterprises, recipients, trunk lines, and branch lines. Relying on massive big data connections, enterprises can optimize express links.

Currently, mainstream domestic express enterprises have basically completed the popularization of electronic waybills, indicating that big data technology has become the standard configuration for express logistics enterprises.
