
## Network Architecture Selection

The choice of network architecture depends on the degree of decentralization, the amount of information made public, and the fee per transaction. When partial decentralization is required, permissioned networks are preferred. If maximum decentralization is needed, public permissionless blockchains are better. When using a public network, all information related to the transaction is accessible, enabling real-time election monitoring. However, if specific information needs to be kept confidential from the public, a private blockchain network would be more suitable. All public blockchain networks require transaction fees, which act as a security measure to prevent Denial-of-Service (DoS) attacks.

### 9.2.3.2 Consensus Algorithm

A consensus algorithm refers to a mechanism that maintains coordination between users or machines, i.e., creates a fault-tolerant system, by ensuring that all agents in the system agree on a specific point of truth even when some agents fail. Consensus algorithms are divided into two categories: competitive and non-competitive. Competitive algorithms include Proof of Work, Proof of Stake, and Delegated Proof of Stake. Non-competitive algorithms include the Paxos algorithm, the Raft algorithm, and the Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT) algorithm.

### 9.2.3.3 Anonymity

By default, a blockchain network is public in nature, allowing users to access transaction details. However, it must be noted that in the case of online voting, the secrecy of the vote cast by the voter must be ensured. Therefore, the vote should not be linked to the voter who cast it; hence, the anonymity of the vote given by the user must be guaranteed. In certain cases, including parliamentary voting and stakeholder voting, a secret ballot is not necessarily required.

## 9.3 Related Work

Extensive work has been completed in the domain of utilizing blockchain for e-voting; some of the related works are as follows.

Francesco et al. [[16]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_16) built a "Crypto Voting System" using a dual-chain blockchain, a one-way pegged sidechain (acting as an extension of the blockchain), and the Shamir Secret Sharing method. This method further enabled the creation of new features by avoiding writing data on the main chain. The eligible voter registry and voting operation records are stored in the first sidechain, while the vote count is recorded in the second sidechain.

Khan et al. [[17]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_17) created an efficient e-voting scheme using the MultiChain platform, while utilizing the concepts of transparency and cryptography to achieve an effective solution.

To determine which voting systems meet criteria such as reliability, anonymity, and security, Garg et al. [[18]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_18) conducted a literature review of various techniques and solutions for the e-voting problem and provided a comparative study. However, they concluded that most systems require better authentication techniques to verify user identity.

Kshetri and Voas [[19]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_19) attempted to evaluate the pros and cons of BEV (Blockchain-Enabled Voting) and listed the advantages and challenges faced by the approach. The study noted that the approach provides trust, accuracy, and security; the main issue is that public accessibility is hindered due to its complexity and fluctuations in broadband connectivity.

Yavuz et al. [[20]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_20) implemented an e-voting system on the Ethereum platform using smart contracts to keep the system secure and transparent, and examined the time required for a voter's vote to be recorded, thereby judging system efficiency, but the method is limited to small-scale elections.

Khan et al. [[21]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_21) attempted to find the scalability constraints of e-voting systems by determining the impact of parameters such as block size, voting speed considered, and population, and examining their impact on system efficiency. A scenario where all parameters are optimal to achieve maximum efficiency is unlikely to occur; experiments show that different adjustments when changing these parameters lead to high performance in one parameter at the expense of others.

Widayanti et al. [[22]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_22) built a holistic e-voting system using three independent models—an integration model, a voting model, and a revocation model—the system uses the concept of Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC). The system is designed to prevent vote forgery and satisfy other parameters. The main issue is that the ECC technique used is vulnerable to quantum computer attacks.

Chaudhari [[23]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_23) used the concept of smart contracts as shown in reference [[20]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_20), but focused more on privacy protection. The method includes creating smart contracts, user authentication and verification, applying Proof of Work (PoW) and Proof of Vote (PoV) algorithms, and handling faulty nodes. It examines the probability of failure and smart contract efficiency, finding that the PoW method reports a higher probability of failure compared to the PoV method.

Yousif et al. [[24]](#chapter9.xhtml#ref9_24) provided a comparative study of privacy requirements and security requirements in e-voting systems by comparing various methods based on specific parameters including eligibility, anonymity, reliability, transparency, fairness, robustness, etc.

此外，必须注意到隐私和安全在电子投票系统中均至关重要，且基于不同的方法，满足这两项需求的能力已得到提升。同时也必须指出，在验证已投选票真实性、匿名性、投票速度及其可访问性等方面，仍需做更多工作。

Hjálmarsson 等人 [25] 利用以太坊和 Solidity 算法测试了一种电子投票系统，以使用智能合约并结合 HTML、CSS 和 JavaScript 以及 NodeJS 来提高安全性、降低构建成本，结果发现透明性、安全性和可审计性均得到了良好满足。

Thuy 等人 [26] 提出了另一种基于区块链的电子投票系统方案，该方案最小化了中心化概念，增强了公平性概念。该过程涉及创建所用的界面，随后创建应用服务器和选票监管机构，以及以太坊节点和选票合约；进一步发现该模型有助于降低成本并改善用户体验。

Vivek 等人 [27] 基于多链平台创建了另一种基于区块链的去中心化投票系统，该系统解决了 Sawtooth 框架的问题，确保了系统的可扩展性、投票的公平性以及小规模下的用户匿名性。

Jafar 等人 [28] 创建了一个基于多链平台的投票系统，重点在于改进文献 [27] 中提到的安全性，以及选票的机密性和完整性。该系统通过创建后端和节点来实现，指定存储容量、选民身份验证等要素，最后进行投票。

Haibo [29] 利用 ECC（椭圆曲线密码学）概念来保障电子投票系统的安全，该系统通过使用私钥生成投票区块签名并使用公钥进行验证的过程来工作。

Khan 等人 [30] 最终基于区块链概念检查了基于应用程序的交易延展性攻击的可能性。为了得出结论——即当诚实发送的交易与可延展交易之间的时间间隔非常短时，攻击机会显著，该文尝试解释可能导致该攻击的情况，并检查各种条件以查看若对应用程序实施攻击是否存在任何显著副作用。
