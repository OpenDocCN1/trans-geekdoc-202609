
Let's take a closer look at how Bitcoin transactions function. You are now aware that the blockchain stores records of all legitimate Bitcoin transactions, however some readers may be thinking: Ok, cool, transactions are recorded on the blockchain... What about my real Bitcoins? Where are they kept, and how do I make a transaction? That's an excellent question!

The fundamental notions of the blockchain are critical for understanding how and why Bitcoin works, but they don't truly answer the question of how to add blocks to the chain.
