# Chapter 10: Big Data Transportation and Traffic Management

Traffic management is a major topic in the transportation field; where there is traffic, there is a need for traffic management. It involves all aspects of the traffic process, including the management of major traffic facilities and traffic information, as well as the management of technological changes, management models, and organizational models. Comprehensive management requires powerful real-time response and analysis capabilities, and big data happens to meet these requirements.

## 10.1 Status Diagnosis of Big Data Transportation

The diagnosis of traffic status by big data relies on the collection of various types of information, followed by multi-dimensional data analysis, and finally forms complete data with the assistance of historical archives and current road conditions. Compared with the early development of big data, the status diagnosis at that time was too vague and difficult to understand. Today, big data diagnosis forms complete reports and presents them in intuitive forms such as charts, which can be effectively applied. Therefore, more and more cities are integrating transportation big data to effectively build complete big data diagnosis systems and improve operational capabilities.

Taking Nanjing as an example. As early as 2012, Nanjing had launched the informatization construction of transportation big data. The core projects at that time were "Two Cards and One Center", namely the Citizen Card, Vehicle Card, and Government Cloud Computing Center. With the continuous upgrade of big data since 2013, these core projects were integrated into "Two Networks and One Platform", namely the LTE Government Dedicated Network, the Internet of Things, and the Citizen Comprehensive Service Platform, effectively improving the status diagnosis capability of big data and realizing the effective interconnection of various types of information.

Especially for the public and service platforms, this platform not only provides relevant data and suggestions to public transportation departments but is also open to citizens. Through real-time road conditions, citizens can obtain specific information on various stations and road sections, obtain optimal route suggestions in advance, and ensure smooth road traffic. This status diagnosis is the future development trend of traffic management, which can not only make data more accurate but also effectively reduce traffic supervision and operation costs.

In terms of technical means, how does big data transportation carry out these status diagnoses?

### 10.1.1 Operation of the Information Collection Module

To perform status diagnosis, data collection must first be carried out; this is the foundation of big data operation. Currently, there are two information collection modes: static mode and dynamic mode.

In most cities, static information collection has been put into use. The characteristic of this method is that when vehicles pass through video monitoring equipment or induction loops fixed on roads, corresponding data is quickly captured, thereby realizing vehicle information collection. For example, the thousands of information collectors installed on Beijing's expressways and main roads belong to static collection equipment. Such equipment is low-cost, fixed installation is simple to maintain, but its disadvantage lies in limited flexibility. Once vehicles avoid the monitoring range, it will lead to data collection failure. Therefore, the number of static information collection devices is often large.

Compared with the disadvantages of static information collection, dynamic information collection equipment is more flexible. For example, collection equipment installed on law enforcement vehicles and regulatory vehicles collects data through magnetic frequency, photoelectric, weight sensors, and other methods, which can effectively collect the real-time operating speed of vehicles. The data is in a flowing state, so the prediction of congestion is more accurate.

Accurate data is a prerequisite for traffic management and the basis for big data to make accurate judgments. Therefore, a "static + dynamic" combination mode should be formed to ensure the accuracy of data sampling.

Intelligent buses are a typical representative of the "static + dynamic" information collection combination mode. The new intelligent bus system can continuously collect the driving conditions of surrounding vehicles during operation and quickly judge the road conditions ahead. At the same time, it can also perform complete static data collection on bus stops and traffic intersections, providing data support for the next step of traffic management. Therefore, promoting the use of intelligent buses is not only for low-carbon life but also for urban road optimization.

### 10.1.2 Application of the Big Data Analysis Module

After the big data information collection is completed, relevant information will enter the big data platform for the next step of analysis. Currently, third-party big data companies are developing rapidly, and different brands of big data transportation products have different analysis modes. But their commonality is: effectively summarizing information from different channels and establishing a traffic information data model. Subsequently, these data form images and videos, and through the deep analysis of the big data platform's classification, valuable information data is obtained.

The more complete the big data platform, the higher the accuracy of this analysis. Even if some data is fuzzy, the big data platform will also effectively perform mathematical modeling through historical archives, built-in databases, and other methods to improve the level of data analysis.

Currently, most third-party big data enterprises serving the transportation sector have established complete analysis modules. Internet giants such as `Baidu` and `Alibaba` are also continuously advancing into related fields, creating a flourishing landscape. Local transportation departments should preferably choose to cooperate with enterprises possessing rich market experience. The first reason is to safeguard the security of the big data platform; the second is to leverage complete databases to manage regional transportation. For example, as of 2014, the `Alibaba` big data brand had stored 100 PB of data content, equivalent to 40,000 Seattle Central Libraries and 58 billion books. Particularly for new district transportation development, utilizing data files from other regions can supplement data for the area, providing more rational suggestions for road and network planning.
