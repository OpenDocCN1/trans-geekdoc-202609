# 11.4 Benefits, Opportunities, and Challenges of Physics-Based Digital Twin in Novel Markets

The benefits, opportunities, and challenges of the DTs are clearly divided between two groups: customers and manufacturers. In practice, the introduction of DTs is on the shoulders of manufacturers, and therefore the benefits for them must be foreseen. For manufacturers, the use of DTs can produce many benefits. Above all, manufacturers benefit from the fact that they can accumulate their product knowledge and that DTs can offer them a competitive advantage over customers who prefer personalized services and user experiences. In some cases, public authorities may also be interested in exploiting data generated by DTs, but this poses a challenge to the protection of customers’ privacy. The opportunities related to DTs are endless; however, the approach to a given industry and product varies significantly, and quantifying the business value requires a case-specific approach.
