The above list can provide important guidance for researchers who would be interested in considering research topics not yet fully explored. It is also wholeheartedly recommended to focus more on a human factor in digital twin investigation, especially that – as Petzhold et al. (2020) mentioned – it leads to a new role of humans in the increasing digitization of the world: “the role of operator changes from that of a manual worker to that of a supervisor and problem solver.”
