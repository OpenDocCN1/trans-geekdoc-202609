# 21.2 Artificial Intelligence in the AECO-FM Industry

Adoption of technologies in the AECO-FM industry is slower than in other industries because productivity is not as stable as in other industries and investment in technology is somewhat less (Akinosho et al., 2020). Another reason is that the construction professionals are mostly on the move. One of the challenges faced in the AECO-FM industry is that most industry contributors accept a culture of resistance to digital technologies. This has caused the construction industry to be one of the least digitized industries. Lack of digitization has caused problems such as low productivity, cost overrun, schedule overrun, scope creep, and poor quality (Abioye et al., 2021), leading to a poor decision-making process (Delgado and Oyedele, 2021).
