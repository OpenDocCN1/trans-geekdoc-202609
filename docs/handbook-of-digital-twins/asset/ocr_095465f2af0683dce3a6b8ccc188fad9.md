# 13.4 Discussion

In this section, we reflect on the architecture and its application presented
in the previous section. We clarify design and engineering challenges that
popped up in the course of application and may need further research.

Since the proposed architecture consists of one or multiple SID layers and
one or multiple SBD layers, an organization dealing with various value-added
processes may need further structures to organize the process diagrams.
When the proposed architecture can be created for each value-chain process
of an organization, communication across processes is likely to occur and
some SIDs and SBDs might be needed to show the involvement of particular
roles or tasks in various value chains.
