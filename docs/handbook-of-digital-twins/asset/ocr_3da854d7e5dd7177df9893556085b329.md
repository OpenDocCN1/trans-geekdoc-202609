For each finger of the pneumatic gripper, there are one L-TENG, consisting of one channel, and one T-TENG, consisting of four channels. There are a total of 15 channels for grasping data collection, including the contact area, contact position, and bending angle information. The excellent flexibility and compatibility with the soft robotic manipulator during the grasping process enable the TENG-based sensing network to feedback a 3D sensory space. Compared with a single sensor, this sensing network could reveal more information, such as the bending speed, collision vibration, contact sequence, and other characters with respect to time.
