In the second part, a digital twin model is constructed based on the DBN to handle the diagnosis and prognosis of the fatigue damage state in complex structures, accounting for multiple uncertainties. Several crack length measurements in the experiment are considered observations and used to update the digital twin. Results show that the uncertainty of the digital twin is decreased eventually, which further improves the prognosis accuracy of the damage state.

It is expected that the methodology can be easily extended to more realistic and complex aircraft structures, such as the non-planar growth of crack surfaces under mixed mode loading. Furthermore, by utilizing the capability of the digital twin, it would be helpful to achieve the CBM.
