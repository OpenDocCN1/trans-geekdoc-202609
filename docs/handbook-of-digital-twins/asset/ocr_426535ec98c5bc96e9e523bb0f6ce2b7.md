# 21.2.1 Building Information Modeling

BIM is a technology that can create, share, and use nD information throughout the project and building lifecycle. Condensable project information throughout its lifecycle flows from individual professionals, teams, and organizations to the BIM model, which is a digitalization mediator for the built environment. A BIM model is a digital representation of a building. It can be integrated with multidimensional information as a means of creating and sharing knowledge (Ozturk, 2020). The promise of BIM is to increase efficiency in construction projects by reducing time and cost through its potential such as clear and fast analysis, multipurpose service visualizations, and increased cooperation among all stakeholders. Nowadays, BIM is widely used for performance improvement, reliable predictions, design improvement, and the ability to integrate endless amounts of data, (Chen and Tang, 2019).
