# # Editor Biographies

**Dr. Jyotismita Chaki** is an Associate Professor at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Vellore Institute of Technology, India. Holding a PhD in Engineering from Jadavpur University, Kolkata, her research focuses on Computer Vision, Image Processing, Pattern Recognition, Medical Imaging, Artificial Intelligence, and Machine Learning. With a prolific publication record, Dr. Chaki has authored over 50 international conference and journal papers and contributed to more than ten books as author or editor. In 2024, she was presented among “TOP 2% Scientists in the World”. Her editorial roles currently include Editor of Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence (Elsevier), Section Editor of PeerJ Computer Science, and Associate Editor for Computer and Electrical Engineering, Array, and Machine Learning with Applications journals (Elsevier). She is a Senior Member of IEEE.

**Marcin Wozniak** received an MSc degree in applied mathematics, a PhD degree in computational intelligence, a DSc degree in computational intelligence, and Full Professor honours from the President of Poland. He is currently a Full Professor with the Faculty of Applied Mathematics, Silesian University of Technology.

He is a Scientific Supervisor in editions of “The Diamond Grant” and “The Best of the Best” programs for highly talented students from the Polish Ministry of Science and Higher Education. He participated in various scientific projects (as Lead Investigator, Scientific Investigator, Manager, Participant, and Advisor) at Polish, Italian, and Lithuanian universities and projects with applied results in IT industry both funded by the National Centre for Research and Development and abroad. He was a Visiting Researcher with universities in Italy, Sweden, and Germany.
