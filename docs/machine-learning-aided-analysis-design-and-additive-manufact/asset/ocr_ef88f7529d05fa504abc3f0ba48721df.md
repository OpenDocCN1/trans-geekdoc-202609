In recent years, functionally graded (FGP) material has been increasingly applied in aerospace, medical devices, mechanical engineering, and other fields due to its unique advantages. Such material refers to a functionally graded material made of metal foam with pores, and its properties are affected by the type of pore distribution and the distribution gradient [1–3]. Compared with conventional homogeneous materials, FGP material has the advantages of low weight, high strength, excellent impact resistance, good thermal and acoustic insulation properties, and so on, which makes FGP materials ideal for structural elements such as beams and shells. Driven by the reality of engineering applications, the research on the free and forced vibration characteristics of FGP beams is a popular and valuable research topic currently.

At present, the mainstream modeling and solution methods of FGP beam structure include finite element method, differential quadrature method (DQM), and Ritz method. Shafafi and Kazemi [6] used the generalized DQM to solve the buckling characteristics of a 2D FGP beam with variable section.
