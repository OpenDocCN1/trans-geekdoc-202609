Duan et al. [55] investigated the effects of cell regularity on the mechanical properties and deformation mechanisms of Voronoi foams (Fig. 14.14). Experimental results indicated that random foams have lower collapse stresses and plateau stresses over Kelvin foams. This is because random foams showed localized deformations and cell-wall tearing failures while Kelvin foams exhibited layered deformations and no tearing failure. To decouple the deformation and failure mechanisms affecting the Voronoi foam properties, further numerical investigations without introducing artificial stresses were carried out. It found that random foams have collapse but similar plateau stresses compared to Kelvin foams. Therefore it can be concluded that random foams have randomly distributed localized deformations and thus can always mitigate the collapse stress but not lower the plateau stress compared to Kelvin foams because of their cell-wall tearing failures.
