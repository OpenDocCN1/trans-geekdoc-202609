# # 11.6 Conclusion

# ## 11.6.1 Summary and conclusions

For the chapter, a freshly developed stochastic analysis of FGP structures is thoroughly investigated. As an expansion of the classical FSDT, different conditions of spatially dependent and independent uncertainties are implemented for one complex uncertainty governing equation, a freshly developed framework, as the MLA-SSA, has been established for the evaluation of advanced FGP structures. For, the population NN and X-SVR are adopted to calculate the probabilistic features of the defects on small amount of training resources. Consequently, by using these techniques, more robust and efficient safety evaluation could be made for real-life composite applications. In the numerical investigations, by implementing the proposed framework in practical composite structures, the accuracy, effectiveness, and applicability for the MLA-SSA method were clearly demonstrated for the FGP structures.
