# 12.3 Stochastic free vibration analysis

# 12.3.1 Free vibration analysis of functionally graded porous plate

For the free vibration analysis, typically as a multidegree-of-freedom system, the governing equation for the free vibration analysis of FGP plate is presented as:

```
$$\mathbf{M} + \mathbf{K} = \mathbf{C} = 0, \quad \ddot{\mathbf{u}}(t) = d^2\mathbf{u}(t)/dt^2$ (12.5)
```

where $\mathbfddot{u}(t)$ is the acceleration vector; $\mathbf{u}$ is the deflection vector; $\mathbf{M}$ and $\mathbf{K}$ are the global mass and stiffness matrices of the FGP plate, which is assembled from individual element mass matrices $\mathbf{M}^e$ and stiffness matrices $\mathbf{K}^e$ through

```
$$\mathbf{M}^e = \int_{V_e} \rho N dV, \quad \mathbf{K}^e = \int_{V_e} \mathbf{B}^T\mathbf{DB} dV$ (12.6)
```

where $e$ is the individual element within the FGP plate; $ho$ is the mass density of the FGP plate; $\mathbf{N}$ is the deflection interpolation matrix for an element; $\mathbf{B}$ is the strain deflection compatibility matrix for an element; $\mathbf{D}$ is the elasticity stiffness matrix; $V_e$ is the volume of an element.
