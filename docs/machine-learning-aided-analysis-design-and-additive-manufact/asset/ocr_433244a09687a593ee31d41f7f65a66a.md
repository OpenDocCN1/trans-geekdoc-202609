The third recommended application is the large-scale concrete structure for either structural or nonstructural purposes. Here two realized examples are proposed and described. The first sample is the bicycle bridge in Gertmert, the Netherlands which consists of six individual extrusion-based 3D-printed structural segments. Each segment had two straight edges that acted as the top and bottom of the bridge. For the interior of the segment, the bottle-shape pattern was designed as to form a hollow-structure concrete part, whose function was mainly to transfer the shear force (see Fig. 13.17). The density of the infill pattern (bottle-like shape) for each segment was carefully designed by the structural engineer to ensure the printed bridge had adequate structural performance while achieving the lightweight structure construction at the same time.
