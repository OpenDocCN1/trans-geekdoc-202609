To build a Voronoi foam with randomness, a subsequent nucleus is accepted only if its distance to any existing nucleus is greater than a minimum distance $\delta$. The minimum distance $\delta$ must be less than $d_0$, otherwise, it is impossible to place the number of $N$ nuclei in this $V_0$ space. The cell regularity of Voronoi foams is defined as:

> $R = \frac{\delta}{d_0}. \quad (14.2)$

The nuclei generation process continues until N nuclei are included in $V_0$. After, all nuclei grow isotropically at a constant rate until they touch each other to form a Voronoi foam. The cell regularities $R=1.0$ and $R=0.0$ represent the regular Kelvin foam and fully random foam, respectively, providing the upper and lower boundaries of the regularity of a foam material.
