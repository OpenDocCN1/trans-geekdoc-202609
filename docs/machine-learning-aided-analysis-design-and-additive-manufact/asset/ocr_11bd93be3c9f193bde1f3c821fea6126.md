Duan et al. [85] built the cell-sized foam (SGF) and the cell-wall-graded foam (TGF) using the Voronoi method and FFF technique (Fig. 14,23). Experimental results revealed that SGFs deformed continuously to higher density regions and hence possessed a gradually increasing plateau stage in the stress-strain curve, TGFs showed distinct plateau stages because of their three layers with different cell-wall thicknesses. The correlation was verified by experimental results and indicated that TGFs with adapters layers also possessed a gradually increasing plateau stage just like SGFs. It means the strength of each layer in a graded foam depends on its local density rather than cell morphology.
