#  3.3.2.2 Parametric investigation

With purpose to qualitatively analyze the influence on structural transient response caused by porosity coefficient and distributions, Figs. 3.11–3.16 show the displacement of FGP quadrilateral, elliptical, and truncated circular plates by regulating the porosity coefficients and the pulse loads. All the structural edges are clamped, the pulse width $t_p$ and analysis time are 0.01 s.

From Figs 3.11 to 3.16, it can be found that the amplitudes of displacements are in proportion to the value of $\epsilon_0$. The reason for this is that larger porosity leads to smaller structural stiffness, making FGP plates more sensitive to excitations. In the meanwhile, the displacements with Type 2 distribution are larger than those with Type 1 distribution, symmetric distribution benefits the vibration of FGP plates, the conclusion is in accordance with the parametric analysis for free vibration.
