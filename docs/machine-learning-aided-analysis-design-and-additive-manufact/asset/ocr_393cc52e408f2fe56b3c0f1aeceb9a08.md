Fig.4.20 shows frequency response with 1:1 internal resonance of the FG circular cylindrical shell. To illustrate the problem clearly, the linear responses of driven and companion modes neglecting the nonlinear effect are also shown by the red line. The linear response curve of the driven mode shows one peak when the excitation frequency is equal to the natural frequency of the mode ($m=1, n=5$), while that of the companion mode is zero. Compared with the linear response, the nonlinear response curve has one more solution (branch 2) due to the 1:1 internal resonance between the driven and companion modes. The emergence of branch 2 is due to participation of both modes $Z_{1,s}(t)$ and $Z_{1,s}(t)$.
