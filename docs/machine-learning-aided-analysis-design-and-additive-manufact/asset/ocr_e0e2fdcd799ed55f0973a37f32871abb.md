the recent research progress on additively manufactured cellular solids is reviewed. Firstly, the manufacturing process of additive manufacturing commonly used in the preparation of cellular solids is briefly introduced. Powder fusion, material extrusion, and vat photopolymerization are discussed. Secondly, studies of cellular solids are classified into five groups: open-cell foam, closed-cell foam, 2D honeycomb, 3D lattice, and gradient cellular solids. Representative research and its significance in each class have been summarized. Finally, based on the current research, the authors point out major scientific issues in future work. Potential works include complementing the advantages of additive manufacturing and traditional manufacturing, additive manufacturing composite components or structures with cellular solids as the core material, as well as introducing 4D and intelligent manufacturing into additively manufactured cellular solids.
