#  4.5.2 Results and discussion

In the following, the nonlinear forced vibration with 1:1 internal resonance of the FG porous circular cylindrical shell is investigated. Unless otherwise stated, the geometric and material parameters are the same as those in Section 4.4.3. The supplementary parameters are as follows:

$$\epsilon = 0.0053, f_d = 0.0015.$

According to Section 4.4.3, the fundamental natural frequency corresponds to the mode $(m=1, n=5)$. When the excitation is equal to the natural frequency of the mode $(m=1, n=5)$, the driven mode $Z_s(t)$ is excited. The 1:1 resonance can occur between the driven and companion modes because they have the same natural frequencies.

In order to calculate the nonlinear frequency response of the shell accurately and efficiently, the convergence study should be executed to determine the number of modes. Table 4.9 lists different combinations of generalized coordinates. GC2 and GC3 take asymmetric modes and modes having $2 \times n$ circumferential axes into account.

Fig. 4.18 compares the nonlinear frequency responses of the FG circular cylindrical shell with different combinations of generalized coordinates. It can be seen that the frequency of GC2 and GC3 are almost coincident. Therefore the GC combination is accurate enough to calculate the nonlinear frequency response of FG circular cylindrical shells.
