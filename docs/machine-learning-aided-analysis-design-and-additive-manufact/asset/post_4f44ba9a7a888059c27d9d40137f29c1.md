### 1.1 Porous materials

Natural porous media, such as rocks, timbers, and biological bones, have inspired researchers and engineers to design lightweight forms by introducing cells into the originally solid substances, leading to the development of artificial porous materials [1,2]. Not all the composites with internal pores should be called porous materials, as the porosity here does not refer to undesired defects during fabrication or usage. Instead, evenly or unevenly distributed pores are manually generated inside the solid matrix to achieve superior material performance.
