# 7.6.2 Validation and accuracy of the probabilistic buckling analysis

In this study, the probabilistic information of random material properties is given in Table 7.4.

To demonstrate the applicability and effectiveness of the proposed method, the probability density functions (PDFs) and cumulative density functions (CDFs) of the present method are verified by QMCS, as shown in Fig. 7.3, 500, 1000, 3000, and 10,000 sampling points are generated for stochastic variables by Latin hypercube sampling (LHS) method. The means of normalized first loads and time consuming of different boundary conditions are compared in Table 7.5. As can be seen, the proposed method is not only timesaving but also sharp to the large overestimation. Apparently, the results of QMCS-3000 match very well with the proposed method while the time cost is nearly two times of the proposed method; therefore QMCS-3000 is chosen for comparison in the following sections. Thus the proposed method can give accurate results within a short period of time.
