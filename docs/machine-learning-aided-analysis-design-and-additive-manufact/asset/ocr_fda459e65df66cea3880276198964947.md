FG porous nanocomposite beams with different porosity and reinforcement distributions, Yang et al. [14] examined the uniaxial, biaial, and shear buckling loads of FG GPLs reinforced plates within the framework of first-order shear deformation theory. The vibration and buckling behaviors of FGGR nanocomposite cylindrical shell were studied by Dong et al. [15,16] with the consideration of spinning motion.

However, the defects and imperfections of nanostructures, manufacturing errors, as well as geometrical randomness significantly affect the mechanical behaviors and impede engineering practices of FGGR porous materials. It is true that all the above-mentioned studies achieved great success using deterministic analysis, while these results are underpinned by predetermined metric properties and the design metric between nano-sized reinforcements and metal matrix [17]. As summarized by researchers, the common uncertainties in FGGR materials can be classified into three categories [18–21]: (1) the random distributions of FGGR materials [19]; (2) material uncertainties due to manufacturing defects [22,23]; and (3) the assumed mechanisms from nanoscale to macroscale by using effective material properties. Ghashemi et al. [24] studied the influence of effective material via metamodel approach. Kumar et al. [25] reported that the consideration of stochastic material properties is necessary in the design of such structures. Roux et al. [26] conducted the buckling analysis of nano-reinforced cylindrical shells in the micromechanical model. The stochastic eigen problem of beam-type structures was proposed by Gupta and Arun [27] using perturbation method. Therefore the presence of uncertainty, unpredictability, and randomness of the FGGR porous materials on structures is hardly avoidable and ignoring these effects would compromise the safety of structures.

The influences of stochastic material geometrical parameters are not studied by literature comprehensively. In this work, the structural buckling performance of FGGR porous materials by considering stochastic material properties and random distributions of internal pores and nanofibers is analyzed. The noncommutative Chebyshev metamodel (CMM) is implemented on deterministic analysis from discrete singular convolution (DSC) method. By doing this, the analytical solutions can be easily solved and transformed into stochastic analysis in the system.
