## 7.7 Conclusion

The present study proposed the probabilistic stability analysis of FGGR porous beams using stochastic porosity distributions, GPL reinforced reinforcements, and random material properties. The DSC method was utilized to solve the governing equations of analytical forms, and the non-intensive CMM was proposed to finish the statistics. Two-step validation was conducted to verify the proposed method, followed by a detailed study about influences of material properties, porosity distributions, GPL patterns, and different boundary conditions on structural behaviors. The following can be summarized:

- The stochastic behaviors due to defects and imperfections of nanostructures, manufacturing errors, as well as geometrical randomness significantly affect the mechanical behaviors and impede engineering practices of FGGR materials, especially the porosity distribution, porosity coefficient $\alpha_c$, and GPL fraction weight.
- Symmetric porosity distribution in the case has the largest values of buckling loads, while symmetric porosity distribution in the middle is the least.
- For different boundary conditions, there is no overlapping phenomenon, which means boundary conditions play a significant role over material properties.
