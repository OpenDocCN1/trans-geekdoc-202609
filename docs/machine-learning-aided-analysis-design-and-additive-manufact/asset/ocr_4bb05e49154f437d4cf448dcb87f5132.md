Fig. 2.7, the steady-state displacement response changes of FGP beams with different slenderness ratios are investigated. As, the larger the slenderness ratio, the thicker the beam, the higher the frequency of the steady-state displacement response peaks. In Figs. 2.8 and 2.9, the variation of steady-state displacement response for FGP circular beams is similar to that of straight beams, which is affected by porosity coefficient and slenderness ratio. It can be found from the comparison between Figs. 2.6 and 2.8 that the effect of porosity coefficient on the position of the steady-state displacement response peak of the circular beam is more significant.
