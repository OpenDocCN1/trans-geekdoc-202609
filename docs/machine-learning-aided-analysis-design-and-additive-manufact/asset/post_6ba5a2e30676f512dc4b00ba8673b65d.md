## 12.6 Conclusion

In this chapter, the stochastic vibration analysis of FGP plate structures has been investigated using different machine learning algorithms. To obtain the inherent relationship between the random inputs and structural outputs (i.e., stochastic eigenvalues), the kernel-based GPR method and the X-SVR method are presented with the basis function kernel and the Gegenbauer polynomial. By calculating a limited number of finite element analysis as training data, the corresponding regression models can be established and predict the detailed statistical information (i.e., mean, standard deviation) and probability profiles (i.e., PDF and CDF) of any order structural frequency in an efficient number.

To illustrate the robustness of the proposed framework, one verification case and two practical FGP plate cases are implemented with different types of random properties. Observed from the results, only a small amount of training resources are required from the X-SVR method to efficiently predict the potential natural frequencies of FGP plate compared with the GPR approach. Moreover, the machine learning aided uncertainty quantification techniques exhibit great computational cost advantages over the classical MCS method, which could be referred to by the real-life engineering fields.
