# 3.3.1.1 Convergence and verification

To test the validity and accuracy of the current CDQM for vibration prediction of FGP plates with complex edges, a general and valid scheme is to first present convergence examples and then compare out numerical results with this method and the other methods, which have been validated.

Tables 3.2 and 3.3 demonstrate the natural frequency of FGP quadrilateral and truncated circular plate with respect to the truncation number of Chebyshev polynomials of first kind. Three boundary conditions including classic and elastic constraints are applied, the material type is selected as FGM, porosity distribution $p_0=0.2$. Geometric properties are as for quadrilateral plate, $L_1=1$ m, $L_2=0.8$ m, $h=0.9$ m, $\alpha_1=80^{\circ}$, $\alpha_2=75^{\circ}$, $h=0.1$ m; for elliptical plate, $a=2$ m, $b=1$ m, $h=0.1$ m; for truncated circular plate, $r=0.4$ m, $d=0.25$ m, $h=1$ m., frequencies of three FGP plates behave stably with increasing the truncation number, regardless of the boundary conditions and mode numbers, providing the correctness of this method. Comprehensively demonstrate the computational stability and cost, truncation numbers M and N are both set to 16 in the following works of this chapter.
