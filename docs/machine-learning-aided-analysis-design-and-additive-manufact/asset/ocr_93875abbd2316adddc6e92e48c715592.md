# 17.2.6 Energy and electronic applications

Porous structures exhibit an excellent behavior in heat exchange, electrical conductivity, and high electromagnetic interference (EMI) shielding effectiveness. Forte [47] developed a micro-scale porous foam heat exchanger and completed testing for cryogenic applications; numerical models were created in FLUENT 6.3 at the same time (Fig. 17.17). The model shows a great agreement with the experimental data when fluid velocities were between 0 and 0.5 m/s. A novel Ni/MnO2 air electrode (Fig. 17.18) by the design of the oxygen transfer route inside Ni foam was fabricated by Xu [48]. It shows excellent properties like time efficiency, ease of preparation, and high activity and stability, which makes it possible to be applied in long-lifetime metal-air batteries and other electrochemical devices.
