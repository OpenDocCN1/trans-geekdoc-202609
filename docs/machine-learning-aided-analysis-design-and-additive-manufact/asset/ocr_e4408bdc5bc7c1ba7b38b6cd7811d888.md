# 16.4.1 The interaction between foam and tube in uniform foam-filled tube

The internal interaction between the cell wall of the foam and the thin-walled structure of the tube is largely monitored during the compression experiment. Therefore a numerical study is performed to reflect the deformation mechanism of interest. The action between the thin-walled tube and foam at the mesoscopic scale is shown in **Fig. 16.10**. A quarter of the model is hidden for a better view of the internal deformation. It can be seen that the buckling and supporting effect of FFT have undergone concentrated deformation as indicated by the red dotted box in Fig. 16.10. There is an obvious interaction between the extrusion of the buckled tube and the support of denied foam. Fig. 16.10B and C correspond to the extrusion of buckled thin-walled tubes near the ending and the supporting, respectively. Part of infilled foam is even sandwiched by the buckled thin-walled tube. **Fig. 16.10D** corresponds to the support of densified foam materials to thin-walled tubes. The foam materials plastic hinges after being compacted and then supports thewalled tubes. These interactions enhance the energy-absorbing potential of FFT, making FFT better in load-carrying capacity through the sum of tube and foam alone.
