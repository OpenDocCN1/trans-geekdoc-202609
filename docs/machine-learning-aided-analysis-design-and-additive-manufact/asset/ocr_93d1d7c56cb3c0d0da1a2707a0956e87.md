The fabrication process is briefly described herein. Firstly, the concrete slab was designed using computational tools to achieve geometrical complexity and structural integrity with the use of fewer materials. After that, the formwork required for casting concrete was fabricated using a BJ printing machine. The slab was subdivided into 181 segments for assembly, hence a total of 181 formworks were evacuated through the BJ method. Then the printed formworks were taken out of the printing machine which were cleaned and prepared for the casting of slab segments. At the stage of completion, the slab segments were lifted away from the formworks and transported to the construction site for further assembly. Readers interested in the Smart Slab are recommended to refer to [83,84] for more information.
