# 2.4 Conclusion

Based on the Timoshenko beam theory and spectral-Chebyshev method, a unified analysis of FGP straight and curved beams is established to deal with the free and forced vibration behaviors of FGP straight and curved beams under general boundary conditions. Using typical straight beams, circular-, and elliptical-curved beams as examples, we compared the results of free and forced vibration characteristics, which were solved using the spectral-Chebyshev model proposed, with the published literature and finite element results. Our findings indicated good convergence between these various methods. Our results were positively evaluated in terms of convergence, reliability and accuracy. In addition, the computational efficiency of the method has been verified to be high. The following conclusions can be drawn from a large number of parametric studies.

+   1. Comparing the two porosity types, the peaks of the state-steady and transient displacement responses of the FGP beams with the symmetrically distributed porosity are smaller than those of the beam with the asymmetrically distributed porosity, which can improve the mechanical properties of the FGP beam, especially for the elliptical beam.
2. As the porosity coefficient increases, the fundamental frequency of the FGP beam decreases, while the peak of displacement response increases. This phenomenon is more pronounced when calculating the vibration characteristics with non-symmetric porosity distributions.
3. The slenderness ratio is decisive for the free and forced vibration characteristics due to its significant effect on the stiffness of the FGP beam. Thicker beams have large natural frequencies and smaller displacement response peaks.
