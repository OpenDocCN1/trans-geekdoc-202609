some numerical examples are carried out to validate the convergence and accuracy of CDQM first; then, the effects on structural behavior caused by the porosity meter and distributions are comprehensively analyzed, and some innovative results are obtained. In addition, several boundary conditions including classic and elastic boundary constraints are adopted to increase the richness of data.
