Wang et al [46] used different base materials including polyurethane, polyamideide 12, and polyamide to produce addively manufactured open-cell foams. They studied the macro mechanical properties and deformation mechanism of species under quasi-static loading. The Voronoi diagram is a typical method to generate random cells, whose process is similar to the physical growth of traditional foams. In the Voronoi diagram, a 3D finite space with $V_0$ volume is first established, the set of $N$ nuclei is created in this space by one. If $N$ nuclei distribute in the $V_0$ space uniformly, a fully regular Kelvin foam (also tetrakaidecahedral foam) will be created, and the distance between adjacent nuclei in a Kelvin foam is given by:
