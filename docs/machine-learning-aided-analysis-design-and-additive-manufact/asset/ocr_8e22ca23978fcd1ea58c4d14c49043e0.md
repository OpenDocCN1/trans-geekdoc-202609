From Fig. 15.21, the graded foam M1-NG and uniform foam U8 have similar densification stage because of similar overall relative densities. As foams are compressed into the densification stage, their mechanical properties only depend on the overall relative density rather than the internal structure.

In conclusion, mechanical properties of graded foams are related to those of uniform foams. At the elastic stage, graded and uniform foams have the same modulus if their overall relative densities are identical. At the plateau stage, graded foams exhibit multiple plateau stresses of their composition at each uniform layer. At the densification stage, graded foams have similar densification stages to uniform foams with a similar overall relative density. Hence, the stress—strain curves of graded foams can be predicted by those of uniform foams with different relative densities.
