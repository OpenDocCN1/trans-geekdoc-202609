As an advanced material, FGP material has demonstrated great potential for applications in many fields, like as aerospace engineering, civil engineering, automotive engineering, and other engineering sectors as mentioned above. Many researches have been conducted numerical and experimental studies to gain a deeper understanding in this area; however, there are still many challenges and problems left to be solved for further applications in engineering fields:

The multiobjective optimization design methods are in great need to satisfy requirements and make the porous structure more competitive.

- Micromechanics models

There are several micromechanical models to connect the micro-scale properties to macroscopic mechanical properties, such as the rule of mixture, shear theory [53], Halpin-Tsai model, Eshelby's theory, and Mori-Tanaka model. These simplified models are based on specific assumptions and do not satisfy the actual demand of applications. For example, the influences of temperature, nanoreinforced materials, distribution between filler and matrix are not thoroughly considered in these models. Comprehensive experimental study and molecular simulations are needed to establish accurate and reliable micromechanics models.

- Defects of FGP structures

Although advanced additive manufacturing has been widely acknowledged for manufacturing scenarios that require flexibility, small-batch production, mass-customization applications, and functionally enhanced components, due to the uncertainty, unpredictability, and randomness in system parameters at different levels, the manufacture of FGPs using AM technology will unavoidably increase the level of variability from sources. Structural failure accidents caused by manufacturing defects are constantly reported in recent news (3adeept.com, 2021 and fabbaloc.com, 2019), which cost billions of dollars in damage and loss of life annually (Automation.com, 2021). Despite its practical importance, however, little research has been conducted to assess the effects of uncertainties in designing lightweight structures. The resulting lack of understanding the influence of uncertainties may result in the practical application of novel material-structural forms, thus limiting the full exploration of advanced manufacturing technologies. Therefore it is of significant importance to consider the imperfection of FGP materials and quantify or detect the defects or vacancies at an early stage of designing FGP structures.

## 17.4 Summary and concluding remarks

The applications of composite structures made of FGP materials have been comprehensively summarized in a wide range of engineering fields, which clearly shows that FGP materials have broad application prospects in the future. New applications for FGP materials are emerging across civil engineering for protective layers, space engineering for lightweight aircraft, biomedical engineering for implants and scaffolds, etc., attracts billions of dollars investment from industry and academia. Nano-reinforced FGP materials further promote the development of new generation lightweight structures.
