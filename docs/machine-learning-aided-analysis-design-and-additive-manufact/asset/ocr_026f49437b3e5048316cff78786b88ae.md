# 12.5.2 Functionally graded porous plate example

The first example involves a FGP plate structure and the structural layout is shown in Fig. 12.3. The thickness of the FGP plate is 5 cm with material properties changing from bottom to top surfaces. The metal and ceramic components of the plate are made of aluminum (Al) and zirconia (ZrO). The four edges of the plate are clamped, and the SFV analysis is considered. Calculating through FEM method, the convergence of different mesh conditions is discussed for the structure, and the most converged mesh size is selected as $h_{max}=0.35$cm.

For this example, two different types of porosity distributions are investigated, marked as Case A and Case B. For both cases, the uncertainty information is focusing on the Young's modulus, mass density, and Poisson's ratio at top and bottom of the FGP plate. The variational input data of the FGP plate is listed in Table 12.3.

In order to construct the stochastic vibration modes, convergence analysis using different sample sizes (i.c., 10, 20, 50, 100) must be determined. For instance, focusing on the first natural frequency of the plate for Case A, the accuracy measures of $R^2$ and root-mean-square-error (RMSE) under different training sizes of the two methods are plotted in Fig. 12.4. From the results, the training sample size for Case A and Case B has been selected as 150 and 100 for GPR and X-SVR methods.

To illustrate the different regression performances of the GPR and X-SVR machine learning algorithms, a comparative test of the two methods are provided for Case A and Case B.
