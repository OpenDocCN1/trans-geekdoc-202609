# 13.3 Manufacturing techniques

# 13.3.1 3D printing of composite materials

The TPMS topologies were discovered in the last century, but the manufacturing of TPMS-like structures has been challenging due to the limitation of conventional fabrication methods. With the recent advances in AM techniques, it is now feasible to fabricate complex TPMS-structures with high accuracy. This section presents the current manufacturing techniques for the fabrication of TPMS-based structures. The examples of TPMS lattices fabricated by different AM techniques are presented in Fig. 13.5.

Powder bed fusion (PBF) uses a laser or electron beam to selectively melt a metallic or polymeric powder to form the desired 3D structures. The PBF techniques can be categorized into selective laser sintering (SLS), selective laser melting (SLM), and selective electron beam melting (SEBM) according to energy sources and processes.
