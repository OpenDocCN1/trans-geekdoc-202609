## 1.5 Conclusions

This chapter first briefs the definition of artificial porous materials and clarifies the compositions (cellular frame morphologies, base materials, and fluid phases) geometrical classifications (foams, honeycombs, and lattices). The application potential of various foams is then disclosed. FG porous materials are subsequently explained and the corresponding fabrication methods are discussed. Moreover, FG porous structures, including beam, plate, arch, shell, tube, and frame, their mechanical analyses, are concisely reviewed. All the chapters in this book are also viewed and structured in a way to clearly reveal the important aspects of this innovative composite, from analysis and design to manufacturing and application. The details of each section are summarized to elucidate the linkage between different chapters, contributing to an easy understanding of this book.
