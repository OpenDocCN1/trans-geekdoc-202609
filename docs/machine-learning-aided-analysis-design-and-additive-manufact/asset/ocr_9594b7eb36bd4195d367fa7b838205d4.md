In this section a unified model of FGP straight and curved beams is established, the main purpose of which is to study the free and forced vibration characteristics of FGP beams with various material and geometric parameters. A series of numerical examples are provided to test the convergence, reliability, and accuracy of the formulations in the previous section. Three classical boundary conditions are considered, including simply supported, and free support, which are denoted by symbols of C, S, F, respectively.
