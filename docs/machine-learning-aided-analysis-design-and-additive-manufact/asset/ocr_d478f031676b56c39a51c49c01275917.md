Structural failure accidents caused by manufacturing defects are constantly reported in recent news (3adeept.com, 2021 and fabbaloc.com, 2019), which cost billions of dollars in damage and loss of life annually (Automation.com, 2021). Despite its practical importance, however, little research has been conducted to assess the effects of uncertainties in designing lightweight structures. The resulting lack of understanding the influence of uncertainties may result in the practical application of novel material-structural forms, thus limiting the full exploration of advanced manufacturing technologies. Therefore it is of significant importance to consider the imperfection of FGP materials and quantify or detect the defects or vacancies at an early stage of designing FGP structures.

# 17.4 Summary and concluding remarks

The applications of composite structures made of FGP materials have been comprehensively summarized in a wide range of engineering fields, which clearly shows that FGP materials have broad application prospects in the future. New applications for FGP materials are emerging across civil engineering for protective layers, space engineering for lightweight aircraft, biomedical engineering for implants and scaffolds, etc., attracts billions of dollars investment from industry and academia. Nano-reinforced FGP materials further promote the development of new generation lightweight structures.
