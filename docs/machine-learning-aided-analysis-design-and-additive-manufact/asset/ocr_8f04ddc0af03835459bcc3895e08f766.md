# 2. Digital fabrication of lightweight concrete structure

This subsection discusses the application of different digital concrete fabrication techniques in the field of fabricating the new generation of lightweight concrete structures, such as the porous structure and other structures with high geometric complexity.

As for the form-free 3DCP method, research has shown the feasibility of 3D printing lightweight concrete prisms with hollow structures. Wang et al. [77] utilized a self-developed printing system to print lightweight concrete blocks (150 × 150 × 150 mm) and beams (100 × 100 × 400 mm) with different hollow patterns, including cellular, triangular, truss, and lattice patterns.
