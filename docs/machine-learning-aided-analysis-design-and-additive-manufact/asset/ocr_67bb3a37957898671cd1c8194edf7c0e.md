# **1. Solid/network phase TPMS lattices

Lattice structures based on solid/network phase TPMS topologies have been comprehensively studied for their mechanical properties, with most research focusing on quasi-static compression [88–92]. Table 13.1 provides a summary of compressive properties of solid/network phase TPMS lattices fitted by the Ashby–Gibson model. Overall, the fitting experiments for elastic modulus indicate that solid/network phase TPMS lattices show a bending or mixing of deformation under uniaxial compression. A detail of advanced fitting techniques to evaluate the effective moduli of TPMS structures can be found in [93].
