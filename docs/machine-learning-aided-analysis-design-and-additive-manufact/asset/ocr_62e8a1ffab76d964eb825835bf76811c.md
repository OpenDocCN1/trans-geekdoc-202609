# ## Nomenclature

+   - $E(z)$ elastic modulus along the material thickness coordinate
- $ho(z)$ mass density along the material thickness coordinate
- $G(z)$ shear modulus along the material thickness coordinate
- $e_0$ the porosity
- $e_m$ the porosity coefficient for mass density
- $R_x$ the principal radius along the x-axis
- $R_y$ the principal radius along the y-axis
- $u$ the displacement on the middle surface in the direction of the x-axis
- $v$ the displacement on the middle surface in the direction of the y-axis
- $w$ the displacement on the middle surface in the direction of thickness
- $U$ the displacement of any point on the shell along the x-axis
- $V$ the displacement of any point on the shell along the y-axis
- $W$ the displacement of any point on the shell along the z-axis
- $\varphi_{xy}$ the rotation variables of the FGP shell
- $\epsilon_x$ the normal strain in the x-axis
- $\epsilon_y$ the normal strain in the y-axis
- $\gamma_{xy}$ the shear strain
- $\gamma_{yz}$ the transverse shear strain of the y-z coordinate
- $\gamma_{zg}$ the transverse shear strain of the y-z coordinate
- $\epsilon^b$ the normal strain on the midplane
- $\epsilon_b$ the normal strain on the midplane
- $\gamma_{xb}$ the transverse shear strain on the midplane
- $\gamma_{yb}$ the transverse shear strain on the midplane
- $\gamma_{gb}$ the shear strain on the midplane
- $\kappa_x$ the curvature changes on the midplane
- $\kappa_y$ the curvature changes on the midplane
- $Q_{ij}$ the material stiffness coefficients
- $A_{ij}$ the extensional stiffness coefficients
