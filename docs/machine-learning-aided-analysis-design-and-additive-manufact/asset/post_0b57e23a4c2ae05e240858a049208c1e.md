## 6.6 Conclusions

In research, buckling behavior and nonlinear free and forced vibration response of bidirectional and unidirectional porous beams under dynamic vertical load and constant axial load are investigated. Relatively thick beam is considered, which is based on the third-order shear deformation beam theory. Hinged-hinged boundary conditions are used. The nonlinear governing equations are derived using Hamilton's principle considering buckling load and natural frequency of porous beams are presented. First, using the Galerkin method, the set of closed-form solutions for the critical loading and partial differential governing equations are transformed to a set of nonlinear ODEs. Subsequently, the HBM shows that the axial load has a significant effect on the fundamental natural frequency of porous beams. Results show that a porous beam with porosity type B3 has the highest buckling load in comparison to the other bidirectional and unidirectional porosity types. However, a porous beam with porosity type B1 has the highest fundamental natural frequency. Furthermore, at a constant frequency excitation, amplitude of vibration is smaller for porosity type B1 in comparison to the rest of porosity types. Results revealed that the third-order porosity distribution can improve critical buckling load by around 61% and fundamental natural frequency by around 64% in comparison to the first order porosity distribution for porosity type B1.
