The parameters of RVEs are computed with metal foam characteristics, and the following approach is merely suitable for high-porosity foams. The estimated RVE internal cell number $N_{cell}$ is linked to the foam average cell size $\varnothing_{cell}$ which comes from the manufacturers, via

```
$N_{cell} \approx (H/\varnothing_{cell})^2$
```

where $H$ denotes the height/width of square RVEs.
The total cell length $L_{cell}$ differs based on the morphology of each RVE and is essential in the determination of the cell thickness (uniform for all the cell walls)

```
$t_{cell}=S_{RVE}\mu/L_{cell}$
```

of which $S_{RVE}=H^2$ and $\mu$ is the relative density.
Following construction of RVE models, FE simulations are performed in ANSYS to evaluate their structural behaviors by meshing the geometry with Smart Size (1) (fine mesh, ANSYS automatic sizing) and beam element (BEAM188). All of the out-of-plane displacements are restricted, since 2D homogenization is carried out in this study to focus on in-plane deformations. It has been demonstrated that 2D modeling is feasible for porous foam analysis [31,32] and closed-cell metal foam simulations [15,53,34]. PBCs are applied to the related RVE nodes along left/right edges which share the same vertical coordinates, while fixed supports are imposed across bottom edges. A uniform in-plane vertical displacement ($0.01 \times H$), moving and zero horizontal movement are prescribed on the top edges

```
$E = F/(0.01H_{cell})$
```

that is assumed to be the Young's modulus of the effectiveness of metal foams.
A validation case is conducted to confirm the effectiveness of the proposed RVE modeling to mimic the foam materials in reality. We use the specimen from an existing paper [35] ALPORAS closed-cell aluminum foams: specimen size $50\times50\times30$ mm$^3$; average cell size 2.88 mm, cell wall materials: Young's modulus 61.7 GPa, metal density 2700 kg/$m^3$; Poisson's ratio 0.3; relative density 8.52% $\pm0.74\%$. The proposed RVE model is shown in Fig. 10.2 with external size $30\times30$ mm$^2$, cell number $N_{cell}=109$ (Eq. (10.11)), relative density 8.52%, and the same cell wall material properties as those from the experimental specimen. The predicted compressive modulus $E=1.103$ GPa (Eq. (10.3)) is within the suggested range 1.0–1.2 GPa [35], verifying the validity of the proposed method. Please note that the RVE external sizes do not evidently impact the overall stiffness when sufficient cells are enclosed [17].
