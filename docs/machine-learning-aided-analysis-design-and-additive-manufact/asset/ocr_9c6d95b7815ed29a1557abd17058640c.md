# 14.2.3 Vat photopolymerization

Vat photopolymerization generally aims at base materials of photosensitive resins. Under ultraviolet light, the resin is polymerized rapidly to complete the manufacturing-photopolymerization. Stereolithography (SLA) is the most representative technology in vat photopolymerization. As shown in Fig. 14.8, the system is composed of a liquid photopolymer, fabrication platform, laser source, and scanner system. The manufacturing process is similar to the SLS shown in Fig. 14.5. The difference is that the powder base material is replaced by the liquid resin base. The big-advantage of SLA is that it can produce high-resolution (submicron-level) material. However, two disadvantages of SLA exist, where the strength of the base material is low, and the residual photinitiator and uncured resin are of cytotoxicity.
