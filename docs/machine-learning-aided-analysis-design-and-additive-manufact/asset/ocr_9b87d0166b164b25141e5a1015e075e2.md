# 3.2.3 Spectral Chebyshev method

In this chapter, the energy energies of FGP plates with complex curved edges are related to displacement fields and the computational strains and stresses in the square area the computational domain by a one-to-one mapping method [29,40] as shown in Fig. 3.2. Then the strain and kinematic equations are expanded with the Chebyshev polynomials of first kind. As a result, the problems of structural vibration can be easily solved.

A typical coordinate transformation with $5 \times 5$ mapping points used herein is exhibited in Fig. 3.3, the corresponding shape functions are:

```
$x = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{m} N_{ij}(\xi, \eta)x_{ij}, \quad y = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{m} N_{ij}(\xi, \eta)y_{ij} \quad (3.14)$
```

where $x_{ij}$ and $y_{ij}$ are coordinates values of the mapping points in the $x$- $y$ coordinate system, $i$ and $j$ denote the sequence number of mapping points in the $x$ and $y$ directions, separately; $m$ and $n$ are the total number of mapping points in the $x$ and $y$ directions. $N_{ij}$ represents the basis function of the mapping method, the formulation is:

```
$N_{ij}(\xi, \eta) = L_{i}^{m}(\xi)L_{j}^{n}(\eta) \quad (3.15)$
```

```
$L_{i}^{m}(\xi) = \prod_{\rho \neq i}^{m} \frac{\xi - \xi_{\rho}}{\xi_i - \xi_{\rho}}, L_{j}^{n}(\eta) = \prod_{\rho \neq j}^{n} \frac{\eta - \eta_{\rho}}{\eta_j - \eta_{\rho}} \quad (3.16)$
```
