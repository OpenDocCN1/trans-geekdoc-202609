Fig. 6.6 shows the frequency response curves for the porous beam with 50% porosity considering types B1, U1, and B3. In this case there is no axial load on the beam. Results reveal that at a certain frequency excitation, the amplitude of vibration is smaller for porosity type B1 in comparison to the other types of porosity (U1 and B3). It can also be said that porous beam with porosity type B1 needs higher excitation frequency to reach the same amount as the other two types. Thus it can be concluded that a beam with voids at its center has the lowest vibration among considered porosity distributions. As an example, at constant amplitude of vibration $(\epsilon_{max}=n=2)$, the excitation frequency for type B1 is 17.6% and 25.1% higher than U1 and B3, respectively.
