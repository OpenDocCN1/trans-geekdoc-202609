# # 7.7 Conclusion

The present study proposed the probabilistic stability analysis of FGGR porous beams by using stochastic porosity distributions, GPL reinforced reinforcements, and random material properties. The DSC method was utilized to solve the governing equations of analytical forms, and the nonintensive CMM was proposed to finish the statistical statistics of buckling analysis. Two-step validation was conducted to verify the proposed method, followed by a detailed study about the influences of material properties, porosity distributions, GPL patterns, and different boundary conditions on structural buckling behaviors were investigated. The following can be summarized:

+   1. The stochastic behaviors, due to defects and imperfections of nanostructures, manufacturing errors, as well as geometrical randomness significantly affect the mechanical behaviors and impede engineering practices of FGGR porous materials, especially the porosity distribution, porosity coefficient $\alpha_c$, and GPL fraction weight wt.%
2. Symmetric porosity distribution (st in the case) has the largest values of buckling loads, while symmetric porosity distribution (st in the middle) is the least one.
3. For different boundary conditions, there is no obvious overlapping phenomenon, which means boundary conditions play a significant role over material properties.
