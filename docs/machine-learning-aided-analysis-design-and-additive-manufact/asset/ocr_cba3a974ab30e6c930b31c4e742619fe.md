As a result, FGP plates have been widely utilized in engineering areas requiring lightweight, extreme temperature, high speed and strong impact resistance, as bullet trains, space equipment, and advanced industries [1–3]. In order to promote the mechanical dynamic characteristics, the foundation and key are exploring the vibration mechanism of FGP plates. Since the shapes of FGP plates are always irregular, it is meaningful in theoretical and engineering fields to develop a unified approach to analyzing the vibration behaviors of FGP plates with complex edges.

A number of institutions have developed methods to predict the vibration characteristics of FGP structures. Barati et al. [9] studied electro-mechanical vibrational behavior of functionally graded piezoelectric plates via a fixed four-variable plate theory for the first time. CuongLe et al. [10] performed a three-dimensional (3D) numerical solution to the free vibration and buckling of FGP structures, showed that porosity of the FGP structures has influence on free vibration and buckling response. With the work of the first deformation theory (FSDT), Ebrahim et al. [11] analyzed the vibration wave of FGP cylinders with regard to parameters containing porosity coefficient, length-thickness ratio, radius-to-thickness ratio, circumferential wave number, and properties of elastic foundation. FSDT analyzed by Parsani et al. [12] for prediction of the free vibrations of FGP plates in contact with fluid, side-to-thickness ratio, length-to-width (aspect) ratio were analyzed. Foroutan et al. [13] developed a formulation of a fully nonlinear refined plate theory in a total Lagrangian approach to estimate the postbuckling and deflection of a sandwich FGP plate. Gao et al. [14] studied the characteristics of FGP sandwich plates with viscoelastic square-celled core. Gong [15] developed a cell-vertex finite method for analyzing FGP structures with consideration of thermal environment. Results show that the distribution of the porosity has a weak influence on the temperature and the deformation, but obviously affects the thermal stress. Hung et al. [16] presented an analytical approach for nonlinear buckling and postbuckling of FGP plate shells of which thickness varies. Taking three kinds of porosity distributions are investigated, and the corresponding results show that the parameters of porosity distribution, geometric, and foundation make remarkable effects on the mechanical behavior of the FGP toroidal shell. Kumar et al. [17] investigated the static and dynamic behavior of the exponential FGP material under the thermal-electromechanical coupled loads. The motion equations are deated by means of FSDT and Hamilton's principle. The accuracy of their method is validated compared to the existing literature.
