# ## 11.1 Introduction

An advanced composite material with good performance in mechanical properties and functions, functionally graded materials (FGMs) are used in engineering fields, especially in areas such as mechanical and aviation [1]. In recent years, with the widespread usage of lightweight porous materials, more and more scholars have gradually focused their research on the properties of functionally graded porous (FGP) materials [2]. People’s understanding of FGPs becomes more precise with the deepening of research.
While many potentials exist for the advanced composites, there are still many difficulties in the understanding of actual performance of materials under different conditions. For example, there is always a discrepancy between the real phase distribution of produced FGP structures and designed one owing to the inherent factors of uncertainty in the construction process [3]. Based on which the inevitable discrepancy results in actual material properties, evaluating the fundamental mechanical property (i.e., static response) of FGP structures. It is of great importance to assess the static analysis of FGP structures under various situations for the structural safety and maintenance purposes.
Recently, and more researchers have focused on the FGP porous structure analysis and have gotten many achievements. Li et al. [4] investigated the buckling analysis of FGP arches with graphene platelets against uniform loads. Chen et al. [5] used the Timoshenko beam to study the bending performance of FGP beam under transverse pressure. Zhao et al. [6] considered the linear fiber vibration of FGP rectangular, annular plates by using the first-order shear deformation theory (FSDT) and Rayleigh–Ritz approach. In literature, different mechanical problems have been examined for the FGP beams, plates and shells, most of them concentrate on the deterministic structural analysis of FGP materials.
