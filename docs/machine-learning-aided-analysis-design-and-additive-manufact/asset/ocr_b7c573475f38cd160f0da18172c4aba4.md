As shown in Fig. 4.2, three types of porosity distribution are considered, including nonuniform PG porosity distributions (PD-A, PD-B) and a uniform porosity distribution (PD-U). It can be seen that the largest pores are located on the mid-plane for PD-A on the inner and outer surfaces for PD-B. The material properties of the three porosity distributions are described by Eqs. (4.1)–(4.3).
