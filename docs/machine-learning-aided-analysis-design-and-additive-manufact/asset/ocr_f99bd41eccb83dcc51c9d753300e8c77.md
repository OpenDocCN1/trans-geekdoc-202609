# 17.1 Introduction

Foams or cellular solids are materials that obtain the desired properties by utilizing voids [1] that can be categorized into stochastic structure and nonstochastic structure, as shown in Fig. 17.1.

Cellular structures consisting of foams, honeycombs and lattices are widely used in industries such as aerospace engineering, civil engineering, automotive engineering, and biomedical engineering due to their excellent impact energy absorption, high specific strength, low thermal conductivity, and other special characteristics. Take metal foam as an example, the applications can vary from automotive engineering to biomedical engineering, which attracts billions of dollars investment from industry and academia (see Fig: 17.2).
