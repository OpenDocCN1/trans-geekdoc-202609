## 5.4 Conclusion

In this chapter, the free and forced vibration model of a FGP shallow shell resting on an elastic foundation under arbitrary boundary conditions is established by adopting a spectral Chebyshev method and FSDT. The convergence and validity of the spectral Chebyshev models are verified by implementing a series of convergence tests and comparative studies. In addition, the effects of key factors, such as porosity, elastic foundation, boundary conditions, and load types, on vibration behaviors of FGP shallow shells are discussed. The main conclusions are summarized as follows:

- 1. The ratio of thickness to radius has a great influence on both the steady-state response and the transient response. Also, with the increase of the ratio of thickness to radius, the vibration responses of FGP shallow shells can be suppressed.

- 2. The change of porosity coefficient can affect the dynamic characteristics. Therefore the forced vibration performances of FGP shallow shells can be adjusted by selecting the porosity coefficient.

- 3. The transient responses of FGP shallow shells are closely related to the load type. Also, for the same parameters' configuration, the time-history amplitude is the largest for the rectangular pulse excitation and the smallest for the triangular pulse among the four selected excitations.

- 4. Vibration resonances of FGP hollow shells are sensitive to elastic parameters. Specifically, changes in elastic parameters within a certain range can lead to the offset of resonance peaks for the steady-state responses, and cause the vibration amplitude and oscillation period of the transient responses to be changed.
