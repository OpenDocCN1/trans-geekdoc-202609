Lists, lots of papers about FGP structures have been published [20–28].

The aforementioned papers are almost concerned with certain structure; however, in order to develop adequate approaches for vibration solution of structures with various shapes, some researchers conducted a series of theoretical explorations and published comprehensive achievements. Liu et al. [29] utilized the FSDT and Chebyshev differential method (CDQM) to model plates with curved edges. By introducing the one-to-one mapping technology into CDQM, the structural displacement fields can be expressed in terms of Chebyshev polynomials nowadays. Abundant comparisons among the results with CDQM and results obtained by experiment, FEM, and existing methods show the accuracy and efficiency of CDQM. Zhong et al. [26] applied to clarify the free vibration mechanism for plates with curved edges considering irregular hole and curved corner. Tornabebe al. [30–36] utilized and developed isogeometric and Generalized Differential Quadrature methods to investigate structures of complex edges and corners. In order to overcome the difficulty of isogeometric method on with Dirichlet boundary conditions, Liu et al. [37] developed new functions for an isogeometric approach to predict the vibration of thin plates. By presenting a method that transforms the tensor product on the basis of triangular plates to area coordinates, the singularity problem at edges eliminated. Based on the above, Xue et al. [22] investigated the free vibration of FGP structures including square plates, circular plates, and rectangular plates with a hole by employing the isogeometric analysis (IGA) and FSDT.
