Compared with FGP straight beams, the research on FGP curved beams has only paid more and more attention recently proposed a general theoretical model for FGP straight and curved beams considering a variety of elastic boundaries, which is suitable for parametric studies. Sayyad et al. [31] provided an analytical solution to solve the free vibration characteristics of FGP circular beam under supported boundary conditions, and obtained better accuracy. Functionally graphened nanoplatelets-reinforced composites have received increasing attention due to the obvious improvement of the static and dynamic vibration characteristics of the arch [32–36]. Li et al. [37,38] investigated the linear and nonlinear stabilization mechanisms of arches, which are composed of FGP graphene nano-reinforced composites, and provided a series of parametric analysis for buckling loads. Furthermore, a temperature field was added to model the effect of temperature parameters on the bearing capacity of the arch [39]. On the basis of triangular shear deformation theory, functionally reinforced curved beams. Ganapati et al. [40] investigated the vibration and dynamic properties of functionally grained piezoelectric materials with pores, Su et al. [41] focused on the effect of porous material properties on the transient response of deep curved beams.

Summarizing the existing research, the following conclusions can be drawn: in the published literature, research on the vibration characteristics of FGP straight beams has been very rich. For the FGP curved beam, as a general structure, there are related studies, especially for the dynamics of elliptical-curved beam. At the same time, there is a lack of a unified model that can solve multiple beam structures at the same time, which is convenient for simulating practical engineering applications. In addition, improving the computational efficiency of the model is still a research direction needs attention. Therefore, the primary purpose is to develop a unified analytical model for FGP straight and curved beams considering both symmetric and non-symmetric porosity distribution forms. The motion equations of FGP straight and curved beams are deduced using the spectral-Chebyshev method and Timoshhenko beam theory, then the vibration response is solved by the Newmark–β method. On this basis, the reliability and validity of the model are verified by comparison with the results of literature and finite element software, with the free vibration and forced vibration characteristics of three structural forms of FGP straight beam, circular-curved beam, and elliptical-curved beam are studied.
