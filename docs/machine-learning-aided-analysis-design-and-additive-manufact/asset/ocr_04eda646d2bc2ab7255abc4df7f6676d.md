Therefore, the chapter is concerned with a unified theoretical model combining FSDT and CDQM for investigation on vibration characteristics of FGP plates with porosity distributions. According to the FSDT, the energy equations for plates are obtained first. In the meantime, by introducing the Lagrange transformation technique, plate with curved edges is mapped onto a square plate. In other words, the structural displacement fields can be expressed in terms of Chebyshev polynomials in computational domain, and the vibration problem can be solved by energy equations. Sets of numerical examples are carried out to verify the convergence, correctness, and versatility of this method. Ultimately, the contributions of porosity coefficient and distributions on mechanical behaviors of structures are discussed in detail.

# 3.2.1 Establishment of the model

As shown in Fig. 3.1, a general geometric model of FGP plate with arbitrary curved geometry is established, an orthogonal coordinate system (x, y, z) is established in the middle plane. The boundary edges of the plates are restrained by a set of linear springs ($k_x, k_y, k_z$) and a set of rotational springs ($K_x, K_y, K_z$), and general boundary conditions containing classic and elastic boundary conditions can be easily achieved by regulating the spring stiffness [38,39]. The symmetric and asymmetric porosity distribution considered in this chapter are named Type 1 and Type 2, respectively.
