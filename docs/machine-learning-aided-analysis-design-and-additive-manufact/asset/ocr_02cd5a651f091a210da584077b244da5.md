# ## 15.2 Specimen preparation

# ### 15.2.1 Modeling of uniform foams

The Voronoi model is well suited for real foams because its generation process is similar to the physical foaming process. Therefore it is used to build uniform foams [43]. Uniform foams with different cell sizes, ranging from 5 to 10 mm, are shown in Fig. 15.2. Specimens are marked by U5–U10, where U represents uniform foams followed by their average cell size. For instance, “U5” represents uniform foams with a cell size of 5 mm. All foams used in the quasi-static test are cubic with the side length of 50 mm. In the dynamic test, the specimen adhered to the impact bar is accelerated in the air gun, which is difficult for the high-speed camera to capture a specific plane of foams. Therefore in the dynamic test, the cross-section of the specimen is set as a cell with a diameter of 50 mm, and the height is the same as that used in the quasi-static test. At least five cells are included in all directions to avoid the size effect [45]. For each cell size, we build three specimens with different mesostructural geometries to support the reproducibility of experimental results.
