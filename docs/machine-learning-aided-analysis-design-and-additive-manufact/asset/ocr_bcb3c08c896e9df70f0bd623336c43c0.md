Extension stress at the upper beam face is higher than the lower face because at the upper face, the beam is ceramic- whereas, at the lower face, it is metal-rich. It is observed that the neutral point with zero axial stress is moved upward towards the middle position. This is due to the inhomogeneous distributions of the FG beam (p=5).

Fig. 8.10A and B plots distributions of the non-dimensional shear stress σxk through the thickness and versus the nominal length of the FG beam, respectively, for (L/h=5) and (p=5). These figures reveal that the shear stress reaches its maximum value at the ends but with opposite signs. It is canceled in the middle of the beam on the neutral plane and the lower and upper faces. As a result, the conditions of nonshearing on both the lower and upper faces are satisfied.
