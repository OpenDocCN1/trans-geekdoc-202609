# 7.6.3 The influence of different porosity types

The influences of different porosity types on buckling behaviors are discussed in this section, as seen in Fig. 7.4. As can be seen, T1P has the greatest mean values of buckling times while T4P has the least one. T2P1 and T4P1 are almost the same. The uncertainty of different porosity distributions has a strong influence on buckling loads. Taking first buckling loads (FBLs) of T1P as an example, there is an 18%–45% error range, which leads to discrepancies between theoretical simulations and experimental results.

# 7.6.4 The influence of different graphene platelets distribution pattern

The difference of GPL dispersion patterns is presented here, as shown in Fig. 7.5. It can be seen that P1 beams possess the highest mean values of buckling loads while P3 has the least values. This is because the reinforcements dispersions closer to the top or bottom layers lead to stiffer beams than those near the mid-plane.
