# 2.3.1 Convergence study

Considering that the computational accuracy of the present model is strongly related to the truncation number of the Chebyshev polynomial, the convergence of the proposed calculation method with respect to the truncation number $N$ that can satisfy both the computational accuracy and the computational efficiency is determined accordingly. Convergence study for the first three dimensionless frequency parameters of FGP straight beam, circular- and elliptical-curved beam with distribution Type I and the porosity coefficient of 0.5 is carried out in Table 2.1. As the truncated number increases from 10 to 50, the dimensionless frequencies of straight beams, circular, and elliptical beams all converge rapidly, and the convergence speed of elliptical beams is slower than others. The present method has excellent convergence for the three types of beam structure. When the truncated number $N$ is greater than 30, the dimensionless frequency converges to a certain value. Therefore the truncated number $N$ will be taken as 30 in this following research.
