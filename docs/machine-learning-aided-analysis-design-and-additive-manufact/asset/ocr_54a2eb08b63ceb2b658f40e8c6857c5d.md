The reliability and accuracy of these methods been verified by researchers, but there is still room for improvement in their computational efficiency.

FGP beam is one of the structures that have been extensively and deeply studied. Based on the premise that the porosity is related to the thickness, Jamshid and Arghovani [10] found that the distribution of pores with few sides and large centers enable most FGP beams to have good performance. Chen et al. [11] conducted a parametric study of the established limited FGP beam model, and also discovered that gradient porosity affects critical buckling load and frequency. Ebrahim and Zia [12] took the FGP Timoshenko beams as the research object to study its vibration characteristics, and explored the influence of factors such as material volume fraction and porosity volume. The analysis of bending [13–15], buckling [16,17], dynamic vibration characteristics are the important research results of the FGP beams. Pennya [20] proposed a method that can solve the bending response of FGP beams in hot and humid environments. Chen et al. [21] came to the conclusion that both the pore distribution and graphene platelets have huge influence on the nonlinear free vibration and postbuckling characteristics of the multilayer FGP graphene-reinforced composite beams. A parametric analysis of the elastic buckling of FGP beam was carried out by Harsha et al. [22], where the porosity parameters of the material were the focus of the study. Nguyen et al. [23] proposed a bivariate shear deformation theory for the prediction of buckling and vibration characteristics of symmetric porous beams and carried out a parametric analysis of the porosity coefficient and density type. Chen et al. [24] took the theoretical analysis of its low-velocity impact response, and found that small incursion impact loads would cause larger deflection of the beam. In addition, researchers have provided numerous discussions and analyses on the nonlinear behavior of FGP beam [25]. Chen et al. [26] applied a DQN-based Galerkin program to establish a theoretical analysis of vibration for FGP beams with imperfections, and classified the nonlinear resonance behavior of FGP beams. According to the method, Fouadi et al. [27] developed a solution for analyzing nonlinear bending characteristics of FGP beams. Based on the statistical linearization method and Euler beam theory, Raskheni and Ghadiri [28] studied the nonlinear random vibration of FGP nanobeam under elastic boundary conditions, and verified the reliability of their method by comparing the results with the perturbation method. Chen et al. [29] took the FGP sandwich beam as the research object and analyzed the influence of material and geometric parameters on the nonlinear free vibration characteristics under the clamped and simply supported boundary conditions.
