**Figure 4.10** Frequency responses for misspan ($\eta=1/2$, $	heta=0$) of the functionally graded porous shell with different damping ratios ($\epsilon_i=0.2$); (A) C-C; (B) S-S; (C) E-E.

The effect of the damping on the vibration response of the FG porous shell under radial harmonic excitation is illustrated in *Fig. 4.10*. Different boundary conditions including C-S, S-E, and E-E are considered, $k_s=10^3$ is adopted as the E-E boundary condition in this discussion. As shown in Fig. 4.10, the amplitudes of the resonance decrease due to the existence of damping attenuation. Doubling the damping cannot lead to the doubling of the amplitude attenuation. That is, as the value of the damping increases, the effect of the damping on the resonant response decreases. Another observation is that the damping has no effect on the response in the nonresonant region.
