
$$\bar{V}_e = \mathbf{V}_e \bullet \text{diag}(\mathbf{J})$$

The symbol $\bullet$ denotes the Hadamard product. Based on the previous works, the transient response of FGP plate can be solved with the formulation as:

$$\mathbf{M}\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{C}_R\mathbf{u} + \mathbf{K}\mathbf{u} = \mathbf{F}$$

where $\mathbf{u}$, $\dot{\mathbf{u}}$ and $\ddot{\mathbf{u}}$ express the displacement, velocity, and acceleration vector, respectively; $\mathbf{C}_R$ is the structural damping matrix defined by

$$\mathbf{C}_R = \beta_1\mathbf{M} + \beta_2\mathbf{K}$$

where $\beta_1$ and $\beta_2$ denote the Rayleigh damping coefficients, which are given by

$$\beta_1 = 2\left(\frac{\omega_2^2}{\omega_3^2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{\omega_1^2}-\frac{1}{\omega_2^2}\right), \beta_2 = 4\left(\frac{\omega_2}{\omega_3}\right)\left(\frac{1}{\omega_1\omega_2}\right)$$

in which, $\gamma$ and $\beta$ represent the attenuation coefficients. The Newmark-$\beta$ method is applied to solve the transient dynamic equation, and the correlation of $\mathbf{u}$, $\dot{\mathbf{u}}$ and $\ddot{\mathbf{u}}$ are obtained.

$$\begin{cases} \ddot{\mathbf{u}}_{i+1} = a_0\ddot{\mathbf{u}}_{i+1} + a_1\dot{\mathbf{u}}_i + a_2\mathbf{u}_i \\ \dot{\mathbf{u}}_{i+1} = a_3\ddot{\mathbf{u}}_{i+1} + a_4\dot{\mathbf{u}}_i + a_5\mathbf{u}_i \end{cases}$$

where

$a_0 = \frac{1}{\Delta t^2}, a_1 = \frac{1}{\Delta t}, a_3 = \frac{1}{\Delta t}-1, a_5 = \frac{\Delta t}{2}-1, \quad a_2 = -0.5, a_4 = 0.5$

By substituting Eq. (3.45) into one, one obtained

$$\mathbf{M}\ddot{\mathbf{u}}_{i+1} + \mathbf{C}_R\dot{\mathbf{u}}_{i+1} + \mathbf{K}\mathbf{u}}_{i+1} = \mathbf{F}_{i+1}$$

In the end, the displacement, acceleration and velocity at time $t_{i+1}$ can be obtained.
