The key information of each section and chapter is summarized in Fig. 1-6, in terms of the research subjects and material forms. Section I and Chapter 1 are written to provide an easy and profound understanding of the whole book by elaborating the general concepts of porous materials, FG porosities, and the composite structural forms. The related fabrication and mechanical analysis are briefed as needed.

The following 16 chapters are divided into four sections. Section II contains seven chapters, where the first four aim at the fixed and forced vibrations of FG porous straight and curved beams (Chapter 2), quadrilateral plates and shells (Chapter 3), cylindrical shells (Chapter 4), and elastic foundations. Symmetric and asymmetric material gradations are discussed and compared to those of uniform/random geometries on the vibration of porous beams and shells. Chapter 6 evaluates the improved beam buckling and vibration responses brought by the high-order unidirectional and bidirectional open-cell voids. Chapter 7 investigates the impacts of stochastic material properties on probabilistic stability analysis of FG graphene-reinforced beams. Chapter 8 suggests a bending theory considering the thickness effect when evaluating the bending behavior of FG beams, which can be effective in the examination of porous composite components with graded materials. These seven chapters cover the deterministic and nondeterministic, buckling, vibration, and bending studies of FG (porous) structures based on the beam, plate, and shell theories.

Section III consists of four chapters with a focus on the adoption of machine learning in the calculation of FG porous structural performance. According to the connection between geometric features and mechanical responses, Chapter 9 uses a machine-learning-based framework for accelerated characterization and pattern generation of noncellular architectural materials. Chapter 10 develops a convolutional neural network model to directly predict the metal modulus from the images of FE representative volume elements, and conducts a structural uncertainty analysis on the bending of FG porous to examine the influence of uncertainty predictions. Chapters 11 and 12 carry out the machine learned static and free vibration analyses of FG porous plates, by proposing an interface to solve the uncertainty problem. These four chapters use different strategies incorporating machine learned techniques into the assessment of porous composites, offering innovative solutions to facilitate the traditional mechanical computations.

Following the analysis and design of composite forms with graded porosities, Section IV provides insights into their efficient fabrications via 3D printing and includes four chapters. Chapter 13 reviews the recent advances in the performance evaluation of FG porous structures containing periodic minimal surfaces, and introduces the additive manufacturing techniques of composite materials and the digital fabrication of lightweight concrete structures.
