# 5.2.2 Description of the shallow shells

```
z = \frac{x^2}{2R_x} + \frac{xy}{R_xR_y} + \frac{y^2}{2R_y} (5.4)
```

where $R_x$ and $R_y$ are the principal curvature radii along the x-axis and y-axis of the rectangular coordinate system, $R_{xy}$ is the coefficient describing the twist of the rectangular shell, as shown in Fig. 5.2. Here, $a$ and $b$ are the length, width of shallow shell. Since there are many forms of shallow shells corresponding to different radius. For the simplicity of the analysis, this mainly part considers the case with constant radius. With $R_x, R_y$ are constant, Eq. (5.4) degenerates into a quadratic surface whose projection in the $xy$-plane is a rectangular plane. By adjusting the values of $R_x$ and $R_y$, it is obvious that we can obtain different kinds of shallow shells including tilted plates, spherical shells, cylindrical shells, and hyperbolic parabolic shells, as shown in Fig. 5.2B–D.
