Similar to solid/network phase TPMS lattices, both unit cell topology and base property contribute to the deformation mechanism of matrix phase TPMS lattices. Abueida et al. [52] investigated the deformation mechanism of matrix phase TPMS lattices under compression. A by-layer collapse is observed for polymeric and Neovius lattices, while IWP lattice deforms uniformly across vertical layers [6]. Meanwhile, the deformations of matrix phase TPMS lattices are found to be identical at low and high relative densities. Al-Kenan et al. [88] reported that shear bands were formed in matrix phase gyroid lattice matrix of maraging steel. They studied other matrix phase TPMS lattices and found a limited effect on the failure mechanism, while shear and double shear bands were observed for other TPMS topologies (Fig. 13.5B). However, a uniform deformation across vertical layers was observed for the IWP lattice. Zhang et al. [40] also found that the deformation mechanism of matrix phase TPMS lattices made of stainless steel depends on their topology, while the relative density of the lattice had a very limited contribution.
