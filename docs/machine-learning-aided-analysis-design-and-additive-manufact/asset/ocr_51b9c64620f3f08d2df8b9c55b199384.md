# 8.2.3 Calculation of energies

+   - Strain energy:

```
$$\begin{aligned} \delta U &= \int_{h_0}^{h_1} \left[ \frac{h}{2} \delta(\sigma_{\varepsilon_x} + \tau_{xz}\gamma_{zx}) + \sigma_{\varepsilon_z}) \right] dz \ dydx \ &= \int_{h_0}^{h_1} \left( \mathcal{N}_x \frac{\partial \tilde{B}u_x}{\partial\varepsilon_x} - \mathcal{M}_x \frac{\partial^2 \tilde{B}u_x}{\partial\varepsilon_x^2} - \mathcal{M}'_x \frac{\partial^2 \tilde{B}u_x}{\partial\varepsilon_z^2} + 2\mathcal{M}_{xz}\frac{\partial^2 \tilde{B}u_x}{\partial\varepsilon_x\partial\varepsilon_z}} + \mathcal{N}_z \frac{\partial \tilde{B}\phi}{\partial\varepsilon_z} \right) dx \end{aligned} $$
```

where, $\mathcal{N}_x, \mathcal{M}_x, \mathcal{M}'_x, \mathcal{M}_{xz},$ and $\mathcal{N}_z$ are the stress resultants, specified as:

```
$$\begin{aligned} \mathcal{N}_x &= \int_{-h_0/2}^{h_1/2} z(z)\sigma_x bdz \ \mathcal{M}_x &= \int_{-h_0/2}^{h_1/2} z^2\tau(z)bdz \ \mathcal{N}_z &= \int_{-h_0/2}^{h_1/2} \sigma_z \frac{dy(z)}{z} \end{aligned} $$
```

By using Eq. (8.7), the stress resultants given in Eq. (8.9) can be expressed as:

```
$$\begin{aligned} \mathcal{N}_x &= \begin{bmatrix} \mathcal{A} & \mathcal{B} & \mathcal{C} & 0 \ \mathcal{D} & \mathcal{E} & \mathcal{F} & 0 \ \mathcal{G} & \mathcal{H} & \mathcal{I} & 0 \ 0 & 0 & 0 & \mathcal{A}_s \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial \tilde{U}w_b}{\partial\varepsilon_x} \ \frac{\partial^2 \tilde{B}w_x}{\partial\varepsilon_x^2} \end{bmatrix} \end{aligned} $$
```

($\mathcal{A}, \mathcal{B}, \mathcal{C}, \mathcal{D}, \mathcal{E}, \mathcal{F}, \mathcal{G}, \mathcal{H}, \mathcal{I}, \mathcal{A}_s$) are the FG stiffness expressed, and are given in Appendix A.
