The interaction includes the extrusion of the buckled tube to foam and the support of densified foam to the tube. Since more foam material is involved in the interaction, FFTs with higher-density infiltrated foams possess higher compressive force and more obvious hardening. In addition, although GF and UF possess similar densities, FFT shows an increasing compressive strength rather than a plateau like UFFT. A predictive model including three terms (tube, foam, and their interaction) is proposed to predict the compressive force of GFFT. The strength of foam varies with increasing strain in the predictive model. Good agreement is achieved between experimental data and the model for GFT.
