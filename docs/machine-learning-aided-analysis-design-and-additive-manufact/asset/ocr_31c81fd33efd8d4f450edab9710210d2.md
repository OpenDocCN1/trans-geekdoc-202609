In this chapter, the free and forced vibration model of a FGP shallow shell resting on an elastic foundation under arbitrary boundary conditions is established by adopting a spectral Chebyshev method and FSDT. The convergence and validity of the spectral Chebyshev models are verified by implementing a series of convergence tests and comparative studies. In addition, the effects of key factors, such as porosity, elastic foundation, boundary conditions, and load types, on vibration behaviors of FGP shallow shells are discussed.
