In this chapter, we investigate the spatial heterogeneity and resulting mechanical properties of nonuniform unit squared cells. Our baseline geometry includes $3 	imes 3$ and $4 	imes 4$ square-shaped cellular materials with cross infills as reinforcement architected material. We discuss the complexity of geometric design with $3 	imes 3$ units and then used $4 	imes 4$ units as a demonstration for ML. An ML-based framework is developed to predict mechanical responses to nonuniform geometries.
