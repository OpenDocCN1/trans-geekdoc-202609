The ultimate regression performance of the S-XVR model depends on the kernel selected, and a new type of feature mapping kernel function, namely the Dirichlet feature mapping is proposed, which is defined recursively as

$$\varphi_0^d(X) = \begin{cases} x_d, & \chi_d \in X, \ d=1, 2, \dots, n \\ \Gamma(\sum_{k=1}^n \alpha_k) \prod_{k=1}^n \chi_k^{\alpha_k-1} \times (1-\sum_{k=1}^n \chi_k)^{\alpha_n-1}, , & d=n+1 \end{cases}$$ (11.39)

where $d \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ represents the degree of Dirichlet mapping function and the overall input data dimension is increased by 1 based on equation $\varphi_{d+1}^*(X)$.

## 11.5 Investigation of results

### 11.5.1 Convergence and validation

Since there is limited open literature on the topic of stochastic linear elastic analysis for the FGP plate, the proposed MLA-SSA framework is validated first with the pure functionally graded plate results without porosity distribution.

For a given FGM plate with thickness $h$, the material properties of the composite is varied according to the power law as follows

$$E(z) = E_m + (E_c-E_m)(\frac{z}{h})^n \\ \rho(z) = \rho_m + (\rho_c-\rho_m)(\frac{z}{h})^n \\ v(z) = v_m + (v_c-v_m)(\frac{z}{h})^n$$ (11.40)

Where $E_c, E_m, \rho_c, \rho_m, v_c, v_m$ are the different phases of Young’s modulus, mass density and Poisson’s ratio at bottom/top surfaces of the FGM plate; $n$ is the gradient coefficient. The detailed values of the material properties are listed in Table 11.1.

To validate the static analysis of the proposed framework, the deflection of a square FGM structure with $a/h=5$, against the static loading $P$ under simply supported (SSSS) and clamped (CCCC) boundaries are considered as shown in Fig. 11.3. The converged finite element method (FEM) mesh with $50 \times 50$ elements is adopted. Tables 11.2 and 11.3 list the normalized central displacement of the composite from the MLA-SSA framework under two boundary conditions.

In comparison with other published literatures [11–13], the results calculated from the machine learning framework show good agreement with the benchmark values. Therefore observing from the tables, the accuracy of the MLA-SSA framework for static linear elastic analysis can be guaranteed.
