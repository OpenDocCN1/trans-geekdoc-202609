```

```

$R_{ho}$ is the vector of $R_{ho}(\varphi)$ at the Gauss–Lobatto discrete points. According to the above formulations obtained by the spectral-Chebyshev method, the translation and rotation displacement functions in Eq. (2.5) are rewritten as:

```
$\begin{bmatrix} u \ v \ \theta \end{bmatrix} = e^{-j\varphi} \mathbf{C} \begin{bmatrix} c \ d \ e \end{bmatrix} = e^{-j\varphi} \mathbf{C}q$
```

where the coefficients $c$, $d$, and $e$ are the coordinates in the three directions, respectively, while $q$ denotes the generalized coordinate. Similarly, the strain, kinetic, boundary springs, and external load energy expressions can be reconstructed into matrix form.

The whole energy equation of FGP curved beam subjected to external load can be expressed as:

```
$L = T - (U_e + U_{\theta}) + W_f$
```

By substituting this energy equation into the Lagrange equation, the expression in matrix form can be obtained as:

```
$(\mathbf{M}\omega^2 - \mathbf{K} - \mathbf{K}_s)\mathbf{q} = \mathbf{F}$
```
