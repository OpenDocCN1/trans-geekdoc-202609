**Figure 4.21** Frequency response of the functionally graded porous circular cylindrical shell with different porosity distributions (---, stable; ---, unstable state; $e_1=0.5$).

Frequency response curves of the FG porous circular cylindrical shell for various kinds of porosity distribution are demonstrated in Fig. 4.21. Among these three types of porosity distribution, results show that the PD-A shell has the strongest nonlinear vibration characteristics, the PD-B shell has the weakest nonlinear characteristics. For comparison, the resonance region of the PD-B shell is the smallest, while that of the PD-A shell is the largest. It is noted that porosity distribution has little effect on the peak value of the frequency responses but mainly affects the resonance region of the shell.

Fig. 4.22 shows frequency response curves of the FG porous circular cylindrical shell for different porosity coefficients. For the PD-A shell, the larger porosity coefficient leads to the stronger softening-spring characteristics. For the PD-B shell, however, the case is opposite. Besides, the porosity coefficient has little effect on the nonlinear vibration of the PD-U shell.
