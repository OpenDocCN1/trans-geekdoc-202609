in $E_{GPL}$ denote the Young's modulus of GPLs.

The mass density and Poisson's ratio of FGGER beams are expressed as:

```
$$\rho_{max} = \rho_{GPL}^{GPL} + \rho_{m}(1 - V_{f}^{GPL})\n v_{max} = v_{GPL}^{GPL} + v_{m}(1 - V_{f}^{GPL})$$ (7.7)$
```

where $ho_{m}$ and $v_{m}$ are the mass density and Poisson's ratios of matrix and GPLs, respectively. The relationship of volume fraction for these two components is $V^{GPL} + V^{m} = 1$.

To compare different of porosity distributions and GPL patterns, mass $M$ and geometry properties keep constant, therefore the volume of GPLs $V_{GPL}$ can be written as:

```
$V_{GPL} = \frac{V_{f}^{GPL}}{\rho_{max}}, M = \int_{-h/2}^{h/2} \rho(z)dz$$ (7.8)$
```

Furthermore, the volume fraction of GPLs is determined by the rule of mixture, which is expressed as:

```
$V_{GPL} = \frac{V_{f}^{GPL}}{V_{GPL} + V_{m}} = \frac{\Delta_{GPL}}{\Delta_{GPL} + (1 - \rho_{GPL}/\rho_{m})}$$ (7.9)$
```

in which $\Delta_{GPL}$ is the weight fraction of reinforcement inside the matrix matrix, also marked as wt.% in some studies. By using Eq.(7.9) into Eq.(7.8), and then combining with Eq. (7.4), the parameters $c_{s1}, c_{s2}, c_{s3}$ and $c_{s4}$ of porous nanocomposite sites for different combinations of porosity distributions and dispersions can be derived from:

```
$c_{s1} = \int_{-h/2}^{h/2} [1 - \cos(\frac{\pi z}{h})] \frac{\rho(z)}{\rho_{max}}dz\nc c_{s2} = \int_{-h/2}^{h/2} [1 - \cos(\frac{\pi z}{2h})] \frac{\rho(z)}{\rho_{max}}dz\nc c_{s3} = \int_{-h/2}^{h/2} [1 - \cos(\frac{\pi z}{3/2})] \frac{\rho(z)}{\rho_{max}}dz\nc c_{s4} = \int_{-h/2}^{h/2} \frac{\rho(z)}{\rho_{max}}dz$ \n\Delta_{GPL}^{h/2}^{h/2} = \frac{\int_{-h/2}^{h/2} (1 - e_{m}(\rho(z)))dz}{\Delta_{GPL} + (1 - \rho_{GPL}/\rho_{m})}$$ (7.10)$
```
