To describe the geometry of straight beam, circular-curved beam, and elliptical-curved beam accurately and uniformly, a set of geometric parameters are introduced: the curvature radius $R_c$, the thickness $h$, and the width $b$ of the beam section. The curvature radius $R_c(\varphi)$ of elliptical beam shown in Fig. 2.1 is given as:

$$R_c(\varphi) = \frac{a_e b_e^2}{\sqrt{a_e^2\sin^2\varphi+b_e^2\cos^2\varphi}}$

where $a_e$ and $b_e$ denote the long and short semiaxis of ellipse. It is worth noting that $a_e=b_e=R_c$ a circular-curved beam is obtained as Fig. 2.1B. Furthermore, as the value of $R_c$ increases to infinity, the straight beam will be degenerated from the curved beam in Fig. 2.1C.
