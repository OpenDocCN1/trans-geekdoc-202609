# Nomenclature

+   $k_{0x}, k_{0y}$ Linear boundary springs
$K_x, K_y$ Rotational boundary springs
$u_v, v_w$ The linear displacement components on the middle side of the FGP plate
$\Phi$ The rotation component of the FGP plate about y axis
$\Phi_y$ The thickness of the structure
$L_1$ The length of bottom edge of the quadrilateral plate
$L_2$ The length of right edge of the quadrilateral plate
$L_3$ The length of top edge of the quadrilateral plate
$L_4$ The length of left edge of the quadrilateral plate
$\alpha_1$ The interior angle between $L_4$ and $L_1$
$\alpha_2$ The interior angle between $L_1$ and $L_2$
$\alpha_3$ The interior angle between $L_2$ and $L_3$
$\alpha_4$ The interior angle between $L_3$ and $L_4$
$a$ The maximum diameter of elliptical plate
$b$ The minimum diameter of elliptical plate
$r$ The distance from the center to the truncated circular plate
$N_x$ Truncation number of Chebyshev polynomials of first kind in x axis
$N_y$ Truncation number of Chebyshev polynomials of first kind in y axis
$E$ Elastic modulus
$G$ Shear modulus
$ho$ Mass density
$ho_0$ The porosity coefficient
$ho_m$ The porosity coefficient for mass density
$\varepsilon_x$ The normal strain in x axis
$\varepsilon_y$ The normal strain corresponding to y axis
