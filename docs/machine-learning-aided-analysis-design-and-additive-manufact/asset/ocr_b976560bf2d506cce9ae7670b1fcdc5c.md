Yang et al. [87] produced graded Gyroid-type TPMS lattice structures using laser PDF. Fig. 14.20. The Young’s modulus and yield strength of these specimens could be easily tailored and predicted according to specific structures. Han et al. [88] found the potential of graded scaffolds for bone implant applications. As shown in Fig. 14.27, they designed the functionally graded porous scaffolds (FGPS) to replicate the bone and mimic the localization change in stiffness. FGPS with a wide range of graded volume fractions were manufactured by SLM. They adjusted the graded volume fraction of titanium FGPS to tailor the elastic modulus and yield strength to be comparable to those of the cancellous bone. This work could be used to guide the design and manufacture of graded scaffolds for bone implant applications.
