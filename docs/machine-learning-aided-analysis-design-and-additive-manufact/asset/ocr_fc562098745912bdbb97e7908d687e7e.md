The aim of this research is to investigate the effects of higher porosity distributions (unidirectional and bidirectional) on the buckling, free, and forced vibration of porous beams. For this purpose, a mathematical model is developed using the Hamilton principle considering the third-order deformation theory and Karman geometrical nonlinearity. The Galerkin technique and harmonic balance method (HBM) are utilized to obtain a closed-form approximate solution. Correctness and accuracy of the presented methodology are verified with the literature. Subsequently, a thorough investigation is presented to discover the effect of the bidirectional and unidirectional porosity distributions on the critical buckling load and free vibration response of porous beams. It is found that bidirectional porosity distribution (BPD) has better buckling and vibration performance and these performances can even be improved if the high-order porosity distributions are used.
