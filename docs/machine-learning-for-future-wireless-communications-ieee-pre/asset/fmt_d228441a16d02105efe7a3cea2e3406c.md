## 11.5 Simulation

For the simulation, we consider ![images](images/c11-i0323.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>K</mi><mo>&#8712;</mo><mo stretchy="false">&#123;</mo><mn>3</mn><mo>,</mo><mn>4</mn><mo>,</mo><mn>5</mn><mo stretchy="false">&#125;</mo></mrow></math> users and a single base station with ![images](images/c11-i0324.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>M</mi><mo>=</mo><mn>3</mn></mrow></math> antennas. The modulation scheme is quadrature phase‐shift keying (`QPSK`). The simulation parameters are shown in [Table 11.1](#c11.xhtml#c11-tbl-0001). The average user performance is shown in terms of the averaged gray coded bit error rate (`BER`). We simulate two Rayleigh channel blocks, each consisting of ![images](images/c11-i0325.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><msub><mi>T</mi><mtext>block</mtext></msub><mo>=</mo><mn>500</mn></mrow></math> complex channel symbols, and we average results over 100 channel realizations. To show the online learning progress of Eq. ([11.10](#c11.xhtml#c11-disp-0018)), we evaluate the detection performance for every 100 training samples.

In the following, we denote our proposed method by `PLAF`. [Figure 11.3](#c11.xhtml#c11-fig-0003) compares the performance of `PLAF` against the minimum mean squared error (MMSE) receive filter with symbol‐level successive interference cancellation (SIC), commonly referred to as the symbol‐level `MMSE-SIC` receiver. We assume perfect channel knowledge for the `MMSE-SIC`, which is an unrealistic assumption in practice, but it highlights the advantages of the proposed method (which does not require channel knowledge explicitly). To demonstrate that `PLAF` has a high resolution, we are interested in the number of users that we can detect, with a certain average user BER, for a fixed number of antennas at the base station. [Figure 11.3](#c11.xhtml#c11-fig-0003) shows that `PLAF` is able to support a larger number of users than the `MMSE-SIC`‐based receiver.
