### 16.2.2 Opportunistic Relaying

Because of the constraints on power supply, cost, and hardware size of antenna arrays at sub‐![images](images/c16-i0023.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mn>6</mn><mtext>GHz</mtext></mrow></math> frequency bands, it is difficult for a mobile terminal to obtain the benefits of `MIMO` technology. Alternatively, taking advantage of the broadcast nature of radio signals, a technique called `cooperative relaying` (Sendonaris et al., [2003](#c16.xhtml#c16-bib-0034)), which can alleviate these constraints by forming a virtual antenna array using multiple single‐antenna terminals, draws much attention from researchers.
