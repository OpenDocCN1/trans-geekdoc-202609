The one‐versus‐none classifier will become non‐separable when the feature space realizations cannot lead to the deterministic class outcomes. Hence ![images](images/c09-i0179.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>C</mi></mrow></math> acts as the parameter to optimize the performance of the classifier and minimize the probability of choosing the wrong MCS class.
