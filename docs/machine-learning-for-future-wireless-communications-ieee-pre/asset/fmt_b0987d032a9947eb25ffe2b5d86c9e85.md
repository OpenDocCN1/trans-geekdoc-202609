It can be observed that adding more terms does not guarantee better `NMSE`: on the contrary, the `NMSE` starts degrading when the parameter identification is ill‐conditioned due to overparametrization, and consequently the estimated parameters take high power values, as depicted [Figure 17.10](#c17.xhtml#c17-fig-0010)‐left.
