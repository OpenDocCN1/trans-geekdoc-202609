### 17.2.4.3 Elastic Net

The elastic net was proposed in Zou and Hastie ([2005](#c17.xhtml#c17-bib-0130)) to overcome the LASSO limitations of selecting at most ![images](images/c17-i0078.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow></math> components (or basis functions) when the number of components ![images](images/c17-i0079.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>M</mi></mrow></math> is bigger than the number of observations ![images](images/c17-i0080.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>N</mi></mrow></math> (i.e. ![images](images/c17-i0081.png)<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML" display="inline" overflow="scroll"><mrow><mi>M</mi><mo>&#62;</mo><mi>N</mi></mrow></math>), and of selecting only one component from a group of highly correlated components.
