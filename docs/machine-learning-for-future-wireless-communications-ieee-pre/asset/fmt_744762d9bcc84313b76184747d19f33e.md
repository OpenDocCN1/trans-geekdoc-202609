Ultimately, we aim to find an optimal coordination policy, i.e. one that maximizes the sum‐rate of the network while satisfying the coordination budget.
### 3.2.2 Problem Formulation
Here, we first show that a pure model‐based approach to optimize beamforming/ combining, association, and coordination is computationally intractable, using the models developed in [Section 3.2](#c03.xhtml#c03-sec-0008). Moreover, after reformulating the problem as a multi‐armed bandit, we highlight the massive signaling overhead of a pure data‐driven method.
