### Payment

Just like the payment scheme for online advertisement, i.e. cost per click Moon and Kwon ([2011](#c19.xhtml#c19-bib-0033)), the FANs are assumed to charge by usage amounts or downloads. To motivate more UTs to download files from them, and thus earn more revenue, the FANs set their prices as an inverse demand function Lã et al. ([2016](#c19.xhtml#c19-bib-0023)).
