In the field of image processing, a filter learns the spatial dependencies in a multi‐channel image by applying the convolution operation on each of the two‐dimensional local patches. For position sequences, in order to acquire the spatial dependency in a short period of time, the filters need to slide in the time dimension, which is known as 1‐D convolution.
