## 16.3 The Impact of Outdated CSI

From a practical point of view, the CSI at the time of selecting transmission parameters may substantially differ from the CSI at the instant of using the selected parameters to transmit. Utilizing an outdated version of the CSI rather than the actual CSI may severely deteriorate system performance. This section first provides a mathematical model to quantify the inaccuracy of outdated CSI and then uses the `ORS` system as an example to illustrate the impact of outdated CSI on the performance of adaptive transmission systems.
