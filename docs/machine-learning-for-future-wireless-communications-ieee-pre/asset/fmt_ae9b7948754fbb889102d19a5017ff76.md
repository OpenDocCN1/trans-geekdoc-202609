### 1.3.2 Distributed Learning, Game‐Theoretic, and Matching Approaches

In this subsection, we discuss related works that use game‐theoretic models, distributed optimization and learning, and matching techniques, and that were proposed in past and recent years for related models. Very recent developments based on deep learning will be discussed later.

**Aloha‐Based Protocols and Cross‐Layer Optimization**. Aloha‐based protocols have been widely used for spectrum access primarily because of their easy implementation and random nature. Related work on Aloha‐based protocols can be found in (Pountourakis and Sykas, [1992](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0071); MacKenzie and Wicker, [2001](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0059); Jin and Kesidis, [2002](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0046); Shen and Li, [2002](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0073); Altman et al., [2004](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0003); Bai and Zhang, [2006](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0011); Menache and Shimkin, [2008](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0062); To and Choi, [2010](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0076); Cohen et al., [2012, 2013](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0028); Cohen and Leshem, [2013, 2016](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0024); Wu et al., [2013](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0088)) for fully connected networks, where all users interfere with each other, and in (Kar et al., [2004](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0048); Wang and Kar, [2006](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0085); Baccelli et al., [2006](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0009); Kauffmann et al., [2007](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0049); Baccelli et al., [2009](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0010); Gupta and Stolyar, [2012](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0038); Chen and Huang, [2013](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0018); Hou and Gupta, [2014](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0043); Cohen et al., [2015, 2017](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0031)) for spatially connected networks, where each user interferes with its neighbors only. Opportunistic Aloha schemes that use cross‐layer techniques, in which the design of the medium access control (MAC) layer is integrated with physical layer (PHY) channel information to improve spectral efficiency, have been studied under both the single‐channel (Bai and Zhang, [2006](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0011); Menache and Shimkin, [2008](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0062); Baccelli et al., [2009](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0010)) and multi‐channel (Bai and Zhang, [2006](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0011); To and Choi, [2010](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0076); Cohen et al., [2012, 2013](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0028); Cohen and Leshem, [2013, 2016](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0024)) cases. Other recent related studies considered opportunistic carrier sensing in a cross‐layer design to exploit the channel diversity to optimize a certain objective (e.g. maximizing the data rate, minimizing the invested energy) (Cohen and Leshem, [2009, 2010b,a, 2011](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0022); Leshem et al., [2012](#c01.xhtml#c01-bib-0051)).
