# NET NEUTRALITY

We've seen that packet scheduling mechanisms (e.g., priority traffic scheduling disciplines such a strict priority, and WFFQ) can be used to provide different levels of service to different "classes" of traffic. The definition of what precisely constitutes a "class" of traffic is up to an ISP to decide, but could be potentially based on any set of fields in the IP datagram header.
