**Abstract—Nowadays**, searching for parking is tedious and causing stress. Also, parking traffic leads to congestion, air pollution, and illegal parking in parking areas because the driver chooses the most nearest parking. Intelligent Transportation System (ITS) mainly focuses on roadside traffic and is generally considered. However, parking a vehicle consumes a major part of time. In this article, we have proposed Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Blockchain (BC) integrated architecture to predict parking availability and pricing rates based on vehicle type and time (i.e., demand). Our system provides various parking services, where vehicle type is connected to the BC Network, using smart contracts for management of driver's preferences and requirements. We have ensured security and privacy and provide performance evaluation and analysis of the proposed model.

**Index Terms**—Artificial intelligence, blockchain, parking pricing prediction, ethereum, IEEE

# A. Motivation

Centralized AV services carry security, privacy and latency issues. Following are the main motivation for this survey paper:

- 1. Current parking systems take considerably long time for predicting occupancy and rate of parking.
- 2. Static pricing does not cope with the market condition because it can be higher or lower than the optimal value. While dynamic pricing generally changes according to the demand each up to the optimal value (i.e., price with higher demand and lower price with lower demand).
- 3. Dynamic pricing also solves congestion issues by varying prices between the distance parking and location. PG system predicts parking slots availability so that users can occupy parking with basic rules and suitable pricing.
- 4. Private BC enhances security and data integrity by restricting access and authentication to the BN. Also, the user needs to perform the pre-decided task to join the BN, thus the malicious user has fewer to harm the AV system.
