Upon analyzing these results (Figure 4A), basic rules or conclusions about this reaction were hard to be drawn. For instance, it was difficult to summarize how substituents with different electronic characteristics on o-alkylaryl ArBF,K would affect the overall yield. Examples in Figure 4B revealed that synergistic effect of all variables instead single factor determine the reaction outcome. The electron density on the aromatic ring decreases with substitution from the left to right and from top to bottom.
