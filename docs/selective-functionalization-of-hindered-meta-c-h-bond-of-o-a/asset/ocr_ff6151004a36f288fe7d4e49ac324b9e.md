# Reaction scope

With the optimized conditions in hand, we began to explore the scope of o-alkylaryl ketones and potassium nitrilylfluoroborate (ArBF,K) via HTE (Figure 3). Thanks to the ¹H-NMR shift of the CH-aryl group, we identified the reaction yield via high-throughput method. Therefore, the HTE enabled scope exploration on the tangent C-H functionalization, i.e., photo-induced carboxylation/carbonyl group decoration. A substituted ketones array of structurally diverse substrates, consisting of 24 ketones (1a-14) and 43 ArBF (3a-32z), was selected to use micro-scale reactions using HTE (Figure 3).

Subsequently, a dataset with 1032 reaction data were analyzed (Figure 4a). It was that over 70% of the reactions gave desired products, which demonstrated that our strategy was generally applicable for a wide range of substrates. Generally, o-tolyl ketones (1a-u) afforded the corresponding products in notable yield with many ArBF,K. Substrates such as fluoro (1c, 1g, 1n), and chloro (1d and 1h), and tert-butyl (1f and 1p) on the aromatic ring were loaded. According to the literature, compounds with substantialic steric hindrance at the benzyl position were challenging.²⁶ To our surprise, substrates with Me, Et, Bn, and Ome at the benzyl position (1q-1t) were also amenable to this process. In the case of o-tolyl ketone (1u), such products were afforded much. It appears that o-phenyl ketones (1y) yielded high yield or no product. 41% overall yield of using tandem C(sp³)-H activation/C(sp²)-H arylation was obtained when it reacted with p-OMePhBF,K (3j). In the C-H carboxylation step, aryl alkyl ketones (1v, 1x) were generally considered to have lower reactivity than bromophenyl ketones; however, many reactions gave good overall yield. Prediction of C(sp³)-H bonds with two adjacent substituents was extremely challenging due to the steric hindrance. Substituted o-phenyl ketones (1e, 1p) and para-substituted o-tolyl methyl ketone (1w) gave low yield or no product; however, p-OMePhBF,K (3j) still gave 23% overall yield with para-chloro o-tolyl ketone (1p). In terms of the substrate scope of ArBF,K, electron-donating or electron-drawing groups at ortho-, meta-, or para-positions were possible in this reaction (3a-3ze).
