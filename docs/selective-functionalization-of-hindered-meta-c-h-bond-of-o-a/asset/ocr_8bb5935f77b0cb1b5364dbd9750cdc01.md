# RESULTS AND DISCUSSION

# Reaction development

Our studies began at the optimization of the photo-induced C(sp³)–H carbonylation
(Figure 2A)²²⁴. Considering the solubility of CO2 and relatively low boiling,
point dimethylformamide (DMF) was selected as solvent instead of dimethyl sulf-
oxide (DMSO). Then, bases were evaluated to improve the carbonylation yield.
Addition of KHCO3, base compound (2) was favored in excellent yield.
With KHPO4 and DMF saturated by CO2 in advance, C(sp³)–H carbonylation also
proceeded successfully (90% yield in 40 min).
