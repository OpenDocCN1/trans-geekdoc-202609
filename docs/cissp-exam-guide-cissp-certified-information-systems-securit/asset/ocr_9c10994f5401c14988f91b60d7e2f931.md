# CODE AND ETHICS SUBSCRIPTION:

Another important thing which you need to do after successfully passing your exam is to subscribe to the code and ethics of the (ISC)² community. Doing so would help you avail your certification for the CISSP.

# THE FEE STRUCTURE FOR THE EXAMINATION:

The CISSP examination has a fee of 699 US dollars. This fee is included in the training fee.

Although for the international students due to the change in currency, the fee may vary a little. These details are going to be provided by the Pearson VUE at the time of registration for the exam. Taxes are to be added to the fee as well.

The training fee for the international students and those who are training online is 500 US dollars and 1875 AED.
