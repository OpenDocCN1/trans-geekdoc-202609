### EXAM PATTERN:

### 考试模式：

CISSP examination is a 6-hour long exam which is one of the first prerequisites to complete in order to become a certified cybersecurity professional.
CISSP 考试是一场持续6小时的考试，是成为认证网络安全专业人士的首要先决条件之一。

### TIME DURATION OF THE EXAMINATION:

### 考试时间：

The 6-hour long paper has around 250 questions that are conducted from the 8 domains of the (ISC)² course curriculum. The questions are multiple-choice question types. 6 hours are given to solve these 250 multiple-choice questions. The course contents are the 8 domains of cybersecurity which are needed to be thoroughly revised and well prepared for the examination.
这份持续6小时的试卷包含约250道题目，涉及 (ISC)² 课程大纲的8个领域。题目为选择题。给出6小时来解答这250道选择题。课程内容是网络安全的8个领域，需要彻底复习并充分准备考试。

### 通过考试的标准：

The examination is worth 1000 points. Out of these 1000 points you need to score at least 700 to pass the examination. Passing score for the examination is 70 percent.
