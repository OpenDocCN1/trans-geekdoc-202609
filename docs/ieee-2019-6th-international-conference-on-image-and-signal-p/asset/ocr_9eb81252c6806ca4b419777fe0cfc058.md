In Fig. 4, we study the effect of different values of association bias on the coverage probability, considering both Co-DU and De-DU with RFA scheme. The lower set of plots shows that the coverage probability monotonically decreases with the increase in $T_s$. This means that under based MPR, if no proper interference management scheme is employed, users prefer to associate with SBSs with lower SINR. Here overloading using based MPR strategy is suboptimal with larger $T_s$ since all the users associate to SBSs. The upper set of plots shows that optimal coverage probability is achieved with RFA scheme without interference. The RFA scheme coverage can be not affected by the optimal coverage of the De-DU as the Co-DU, since users prefer to associate with DL and SBS in UL direction.
