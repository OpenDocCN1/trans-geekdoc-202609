# ## VII. CONCLUSION

In this paper we consider a two-tier HetCNet models, and statistically measure the coverage performance with the joint effect of RFA employment and De-DUA. We observe the effect of association and RFA employment on the coverage, while considering De-DUA. Numerical results clearly indicate that the coverage performance improves of RFA employment increases the MCI on offloaded users.
