## VII. CONCLUSION

In this paper we consider a two-tier HetCNet models, and statistically measure the coverage performance with the joint effect of RFA employment and De-DUA. We observe the effect of association and RFA employment on the coverage, while considering De-DUA. Numerical results clearly indicate that the coverage performance improves of RFA employment increases the MCI on offloaded users. It is also observed, as SBS density increases, number of users are associated with MBS and SBS in DL directions. By selecting appropriate values of $R^{(i)}$ coverage range and association bias, the coverage performance can be significantly improved.
