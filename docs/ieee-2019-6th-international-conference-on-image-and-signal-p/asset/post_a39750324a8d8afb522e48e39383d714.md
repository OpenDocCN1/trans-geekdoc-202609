# III. STATISTICS WITH DECOUPLED UPLINK ASSOCIATION

In this section we analyze the SINR distribution and define downlink and uplink-based association rules. Performance of the proposed model is analyzed.

## A. INTERFERENCES WITH DECOUPLED UPLINK ASSOCIATION

GIVEN UE $R^{(0)}$, $\tau \in \{in, out\}$

+ 1) INTERFERENCE WITH DISTRIBUTED DECOUPLED UPLINK ASSOCIATION $I_{R^{(0)},dec}$ (WHICH IS (1))
When a UE in $R^{(0)}$ is associated with vBS in both DL and UL, it experiences the interference from vBSs located in $R^{(0)}$ in DL direction (from the serving vBS) and from users associated with vBS, $v, \tau \in \{M, S\}$ and $v \neq \tau$.

+ 2) INTERFERENCE WITH DISTRIBUTED DECOUPLED UPLINK ASSOCIATION $I_{R^{(0)},\tau,dec}$ (WHICH IS (2))
If a UE in $R^{(0)}$ is associated with vBS in both DL and UL in DL direction, it receives interference from all vBSs (except the serving vBS in $R^{(0)}$), and interference from uEs in $R^{(0)}$. However, in UL transmission, the UE receives interference from all uEs in $R^{(0)}$ and as vBS where $v, s \in \{M, S\}$ and $v \neq \tau$.
