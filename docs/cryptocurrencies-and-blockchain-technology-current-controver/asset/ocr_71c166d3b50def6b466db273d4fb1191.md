9 A holder of electronic central bank money faces no counterparty risk because a central bank can print its own liabilities. In contrast, commercial demand deposits are a promise to pay out cash (central bank money) on demand and promise might not be fulfilled. However, central bank money is not immune to financial disaster. Historically, hyperinflation has impoverished many households that have held a large part of their wealth in the form of central bank currency (Berensen and Shan, 2016).

12 The Chicago Plan was developed during the Great Depression by many leading US economists. It advocated a major monetary reform including 100 percent reserve balances to back commercial bank deposits (see Benes and Kumhof, 2012). A system with 100 percent reserve requirements is essentially identical to a system where commercial banks are forced to hold all deposits in accounts that are separated from their own balance sheet.
