# Deflation Spiral

The situation in which a currency keeps increasing in relative value when compared to the goods one can purchase for it is called a deflationary spiral.

When this happens, all the users of currency are inherently encouraged to save and stockpile it because the chances of the currency doubling in value in a short timespan are great. If there's a chance for you to to get a car for one bitcoin today, or two cars for one bitcoin next week, you certainly won't spend the bitcoin, will you?
