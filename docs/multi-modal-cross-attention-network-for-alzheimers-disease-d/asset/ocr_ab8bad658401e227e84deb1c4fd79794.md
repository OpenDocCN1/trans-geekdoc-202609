# 3.2. Overview of the proposed framework

In this paper, we propose a novel framework MCAD to tackle the problem of AD diagnosis using multi-modal data. In Fig. 2, the proposed framework MCAD contains three main parts: feature extraction including imaging encoder and CSF encoder (Section 3.3), multi-interaction (MI) module to manage the interactions between imaging and CSF and classification module (Section 3.4) to perform information reduction and age diagnosis (Section 3.5). MCAD consists of two strategies implemented for imaging and CSF modalities, respectively are learned with two features in the feature extraction module. Based on these fine-grained representations for images blocks and CSF components, the interaction module is designed for exploiting the information across modalities for AD diagnosis. After the attended intended imaging and CSF features, the multi-modal features are used to decide the status in the classification task in terms of AD diagnosis and modality alignment. Specifically, the modality alignment is introduced to align the inconsistent feature distributions of imaging and CSF biomarkers, which can further reduce the inter-modality gap at feature-level to improve the diagnostic performance.
