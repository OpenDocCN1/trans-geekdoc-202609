# 4.3 EXAMPLES OF OVERCOMING SMART ENERGY CHALLENGES

# 4.3.1 Gorona del Viento Wind-Hydro at El Hier Canary ISLAND IN SPAIN

One of the challenges of Smart Energy is the current cost of storage using lithium ion batteries. A Smart project to overcome this limitation has been done by Endesa Gorona (https://www.endesa.com/en/projects/all-projects/energy-transition/renewables/el-hierro-renewable-sustainability) at El Hierro Canary Island Spain. They built a farm with enough capacity to make 100% of electricity demand of the island. The surplus wind energy which is not consumed by the Island's population is used to pump water from a lower reservoir to a higher reservoir. This water acts as energy medium that is later used to generate power using hydro power turbines when wind production is low.
