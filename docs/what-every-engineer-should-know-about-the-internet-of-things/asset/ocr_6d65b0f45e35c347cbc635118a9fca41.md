If we consider that each of these smart environments can be considered an independent system that operates to other external and independent systems, it is easier to understand why smart cities are frequently called systems of systems (Cavalcale et al., 2017), i.e., a collection of multiple independent systems that operate to achieve common established goals. Expanding this notion, we reach the interoperability level, in which several systems start to become to form the smart city architecture, such as the entire network formed by the smart homes connected to the smart hospitals via the emergency service. Hence, we observe the form of the city being outlined by the multiple interoperability links established among the constituent systems.
