# 1.6 IoT DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

IoT system development requires a comprehensive and reliable approach that encompasses the development processes (e.g., requirements engineering, design, construction, and testing), the people involved in the development (i.e., qualification through certification and licensure), and the use of appropriately certified tools. Application governance entities set the standards for the appropriate qualifications, certifications, and standards involved in the development of these systems. For example, in the United States, there are governing agencies for avionics (the Federal Aviation Administration), medical devices (Federal Drug Administration), and for transportation of vehicles and infrastructure (Department of Transportation), to just name a few. For other types of systems, other governing bodies, professional organizations, and standards boards help to ensure process discipline.

Whether an IoT system is a critical one developed under the discipline of an appropriate regulating or guiding agency or an informal one with no particular rules, it is imperative that the system be built with a structured, disciplined approach.
