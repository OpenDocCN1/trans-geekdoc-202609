# # ABOUT IDG BOOKS WORLDWIDE

Welcome to the world of IDG Books Worldwide.

IDG Books Worldwide, Inc., is a subsidiary of International Data Group, the world's largest publisher of computer-related information and the leading global provider of information services on information technology. IDG was founded more than 30 years ago by Patrick J McGGovern and now employs more than 9,000 people worldwide. IDG publishes more than 200 computer publications in 75 countries. More than 90 million people read one or more IDG publications each.

Launched in 1990, IDG Books quickly became No. 1 publisher of best-selling computer books in the United States. We are proud to have received eight awards from the Computer Association in recognition of editorial excellence and three from Computer First' Annual Readers' Choice Awards. Our ...Forummies series has more than 50 million copies in print with 33 languages. IDG Books Worldwide, through a unique approach to publishing technical business books, has become the first choice for millions of readers around the world who want to learn how to manage their business.

Our mission is simple: every of our books is to bring extra value and skill-building instructions to the readers. Our books are written by experts who understand and care about our readers. The knowledge base of editorial staff comes from years of experience in publishing, education, and journalism. Our experience we use to publish books to carry us into the new millennium. In short, we care about books, so we attract the best people. We demand excellence in interior design, of icons, and illustrations. And we use an efficient process of authoring, editing, and publishing our books, we can spend more time ensuring content and less time on the technicalities of making books.

You can count on us to deliver high-quality books at competitive prices on topics you want to read about. At IDG Books Worldwide, we continue in the tradition of delivering quality for more than 30 years. You'll find no better book than one from IDG Books Worldwide.
