# About the Authors

Jay Greenspan made his life as a technical consultant and editor before finding his way into Wired's Webmonkey. There he learned everything he knows about Web technology and gained an appreciation for electronic music, the color orange, and “cute top.” He now makes his life as a writer and consultant. He will neither confirm nor deny the rumors that he once worked for a prime-time show.

Brad Lugar can remember when computers were as big as refrigerators and oldtimers into the machine room and call them “mini.” He learned more than anyone really should about database systems by working for Relational Technology incre CA for many years. After an interregnum he got a job with Wired. He would still like to know when the future is going to get here, but has a sneaking suspicion he already knows.
