# 9 Challenges in Real-world Implementation

# 9.1 Model Generalization, Integration, and Clinician Adoption

While the device holds great promise for transforming healthcare, its implementation in real-world settings remains a challenging endeavor. Establishing reliable and scalable systems in controlled environments involves overcoming numerous significant barriers during the transition.

A primary challenge in ML is achieving model generalization. A model trained on one group's data may face difficulties when deployed due to variations in demographics, devices, protocols, and data access formats across different environments. To address this issue, models should be trained on multi-institutional datasets and externally validated. Techniques such as transfer learning, domain adaptation, and continual learning are key approaches to improving generalization and flexibility.

Integration into current clinical workflows is another essential barrier. Without addressing the inclusion of ZOGN or the operational burdens in ML solutions, healthcare professionals must adapt to how they already work. Seamless interoperability is an important function of EHR systems, along with intuitive user interfaces and automated indices embedded in clinician dashboards. Providing clear purposes, preserving transparency, and demonstrating tangible enhancements in care are crucial for maximizing benefits and fostering engagement in efficiency. Training programs that enhance digital literacy and provide ongoing support are essential to promote adoption.
