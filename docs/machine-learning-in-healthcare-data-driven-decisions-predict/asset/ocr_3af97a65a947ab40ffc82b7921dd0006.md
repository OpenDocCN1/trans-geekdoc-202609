\( F_{avg} = \text{AvgPool}(F_{cam}), F_{max} = \text{MaxPool}(F_{cam}) \)

**Step 9:** Concatenate and convolve with a \( 7 \times 7 \) kernel to generate spatial attention:

\( F_{spatial} = \sigma(\text{Conv}_{7 \times 7}([F_{avg}; F_{max}])) \)

**Step 10:** Multiply the channel-attended feature map with the spatial attention map:

\( F_{attn} = F_{cam} \otimes F_{spatial} \)

**Step 11:** Fuse the attended features with the original multi-scale output:

\( F_{final} = F_{multi} + F_{attn} \)

**Step 12:** Apply global average pooling on \( F_{final} \) to obtain a 1D feature vector:

\( f = \text{GAP}(F_{final}) \)

**Step 13:** Apply dropout to prevent overfitting and encourage generalization:

\( f_{drop} = \text{Dropout}(f) \)

**Step 14:** Feed the dropout-regularized features into a fully connected layer with Softmax activation:

\( y = \text{Softmax}(W_{cls} \cdot f_{drop} + b_{cls}) \)

**Step 15:** Output the final probability vector \( y \in \mathbb{R}^{14} \) corresponding to the 14 thoracic disease classes.
