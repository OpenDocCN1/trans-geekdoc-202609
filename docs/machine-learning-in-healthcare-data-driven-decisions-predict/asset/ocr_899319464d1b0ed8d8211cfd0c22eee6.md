Evolutionary multi-objective optimization techniques have been proposed for deploying Internet of Things (IoT) services in fog-enabled healthcare networks. These techniques optimize service placement and resource usage to improve latency and reliability. Although the approach improves network efficiency, its direct impact on clinical decision-making requires further investigation [15].

Blockchain-based healthcare frameworks, empowered by mixed multi-agent reinforcement learning, offer promising solutions for secure, decentralized health data management. These frameworks optimize data sharing policies while ensuring privacy and compliance with regulations. Challenges in scalability and integration with existing healthcare IT infrastructure persist [16].

Adaptive multi-objective resource allocation frameworks using DRL address optimization in edge-cloud workflows for healthcare applications. These systems dynamically allocate computational resources to balance latency, energy consumption, and data privacy. While promising, their deployment depends on seamless integration with heterogeneous healthcare devices and networks [17].

Studies on multimodal affective computing in healthcare investigate the use of machine learning to enhance emotional intelligence in artificial intelligence systems. These approaches aim to improve patient engagement and mental health monitoring by analyzing physiological and behavioral data streams. Optimization challenges include balancing accuracy with computational cost and data privacy concerns [18].

Reinforcement learning frameworks tailored for healthcare operations management explore methodological advances and emerging trends. These systems optimize scheduling, resource allocation, and patient flow management by balancing competing operational objectives. The studies emphasize the importance of explainability and integration with clinical workflows to drive adoption [19].

Finally, exploring the Pareto front of multi-objective COVID-19 mitigation policies using reinforcement learning highlights the potential for AI-driven public health decision support. These studies identify trade-offs between public health outcomes and economic impacts, demonstrating how reinforcement learning can support adaptive and transparent policymaking. The complexity of societal dynamics and data availability challenges remain significant hurdles [20].

This review illustrates the broad and growing landscape of multi-objective optimization and reinforcement learning research in healthcare. While existing methods have demonstrated promising results, persistent challenges include scalability to real-world clinical environments, data limitations, and the need for interpretable, clinically relevant outputs. These insights provide the foundation and motivation for the development of novel hybrid frameworks that can dynamically balance multiple healthcare objectives in real-time, paving the way for more adaptive, personalized, and effective healthcare systems.
