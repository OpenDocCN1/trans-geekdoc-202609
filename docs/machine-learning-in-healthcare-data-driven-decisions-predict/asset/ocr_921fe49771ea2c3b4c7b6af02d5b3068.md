Adaptive Workload Management: Real-time stress detection would dynamically reschedule tasks, disable digital notifications, or reschedule lower-priority meetings during periods of increased stress.

Leadership and Productivity Analysis: High-end systems offer insight into leadership success, team satisfaction, and the physiological impact of remote or hybrid work.

# 3.3 Education and Academic Environments

Student Support Systems: Stress monitors on smartwatches or fitness trackers monitor students during exam hours or long study sessions and send them personalized tips or reminders to teachers.

E-Learning Adaptation: Platforms can use detected stress levels to adjust content challenge, switch to more interactive modes, or send break reminders.

Counseling Integration: Universities are employing AI-powered chatbots and digital check-ins to direct vulnerable students to mental health professionals based on stress profiling.

# 3.4 Safe Driving and Transport

Driver Monitoring Systems (DMS): ML-driven DMS in intelligent cars identify driver fatigue, stress, or distraction with the help of EEG, facial recognition, and HR, triggering alerts or enabling autonomous modes.

Aviation and Marine Stress Monitoring: These high-risk sectors employ stress detection devices in pilots and captains of vessels to reduce human error by controlling fatigue and facilitating decisions in real time.

Logistics and Fleet Safety: Trucking firms use wearables to monitor the physiological condition of long-haul drivers to avoid accidents caused by stress-related cognitive impairment [13,14].

# 3.5 Ambient Assisted Living and Smart Homes

Emotionally Intelligent Environments: Intelligent integrated systems regulate lighting, sound, or aroma based on levels of stress measured to induce relaxation or focus.

Elderly Monitoring: Constant stress tracking among the elderly assists caregivers in managing loneliness, dementia, or emotional relapse.

Home Rehabilitation: Intelligent rehabilitation programs can modify daily and environmental parameters for trauma patients according to psychological stress reaction.
