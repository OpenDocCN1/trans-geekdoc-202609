Compared to the past, global perspectives play a bigger role because challenges such as pandemics, diseases that last a lifetime, and aging societies are common in many countries. # 10 Conclusion

Appropriate use of healthcare data can improve healthcare for patients, make care delivery more efficient, and boost research. Despite the problems related to organizations, data quality, people resisting change, and difficulty in interpreting data, care providers still have options for advancing with real-time analytics, AI, and training their staff. Applying advanced models and AI tools encourages doctors to manage patients’ conditions more effectively and also ensures that healthcare professionals understand how to use them. Moreover, it is necessary to ensure that privacy guidelines and data access policies can work internationally to promote the equal distribution of data. As technology and knowledge grow, it is vital that medical systems from different countries use a collaborative strategy when making use of good data. As a result of this, experts believe, healthcare professionals will make better decisions, support healthier populations, and provide flexible solutions adequate for the needs of communities in various parts of the world.
