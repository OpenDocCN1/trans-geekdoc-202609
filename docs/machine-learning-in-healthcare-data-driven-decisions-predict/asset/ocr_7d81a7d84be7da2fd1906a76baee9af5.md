# Figure 2: Diverse applications of machine learning across the healthcare ecosystem.

ML techniques open powerful possibilities for disease surveillance, outbreak prediction, and public health interventions. ML models observe data from EHRs, laboratory reports, social media, and environmental sensors to give early signals for disease outbreaks, track their geographic spread, and predict trends onward. Such information would allow public health officials to make the best possible decisions concerning resource allocation, vaccination campaigns, and other interventions to minimize the impact of outbreaks [13]. ML-based approaches have been proposed to study influenza and other infectious diseases based on Google searches and social media trends. In addition, ML can be applied to identify populations at high risk for specific health conditions, thus allowing leveraging on public health programs/interventions targeting such cohorts to address health disparities and improve population health outcomes. ML could also analyze social determinants of health data to provide insight into nonclinical factors impacting health, thereby allowing for more inclusive public health strategies with optimal effectiveness.
