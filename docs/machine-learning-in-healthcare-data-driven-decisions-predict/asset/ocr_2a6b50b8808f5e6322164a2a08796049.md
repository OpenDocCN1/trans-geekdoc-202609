Collecting real-time data within these networks ensures that the healthcare providers have access to information for proactive decision-making.

Nowadays, disease tracking implementation is technologically more advanced, as it incorporates various sources such as electronic health records (EHRs), social media data, and geographical information [5]. Such multidimensional approach allows understanding disease dynamics, transmission patterns, and the ways in which social factors affect health outcomes. Sophisticated network models can capture the links that are actually present and give healthcare experts a way to make data-driven decisions when it comes to disease monitoring, interventions, and so on [6].

The field of network theory in healthcare has grown even greater with the advent of devices and Internet of Things (IoT) [7]. These devices produce large volumes of data that can be used to create networks that change, depending on real-time health parameters. These networks allow healthcare givers to track patients, identify indications of diseases, and personalize treatment interventions for precision healthcare [8].

To address the dynamic environment of disease monitoring, we introduce an emerging network-based framework for disease monitoring [9]. This framework uses statistical techniques, real-time data streams, and social network analysis to build an interactive system that can track diseases. Findings from the framework enable understanding of how diseases are transmitted by individuals, healthcare facilities, as well as geographic locations. This proposed framework relies on the use of graph theory to represent relationships within healthcare systems, enabling identification of nodes and patterns for disease transmission. Statistical models presented in this chapter improve the modeling functions of being able to detect possible outbreaks and allowing for directed interventions [10, 11]. More so, this framework focuses on the privacy and security of data to ensure compliance with healthcare regulations as well as ensuring that sensitive patient information is protected. This complicated network-based approach is representing a change in disease tracking by providing a data-driven strategy, for healthcare surveillance. It has the potential to transform how healthcare professionals understand, monitor, and react to diseases in various populations by leveraging power of networks.

Social networks are complex in nature and have high levels of heterogeneity in it. In social networks, some nodes have large numbers of edges (high degree), while some nodes have only a countable number of edges (low degree). Using social network, we can analyze and study complex systems like Covid-19 epidemics, airline transportation networks, railway transportation networks, computer viruses spreading network, Ebola epidemics, HIV epidemics, vehicular network, and spread of diseases under immunization [12].
