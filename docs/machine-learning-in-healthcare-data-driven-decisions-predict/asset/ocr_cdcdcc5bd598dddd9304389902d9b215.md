source heterogeneity, model performance is contingent on having high-resolution social media data in near real-time as well as mobility data. Future research can attempt to enhance the process of data collection, employing more effective data imputation and more effective algorithms in handling noisy or missing data. The other feature that requires improvement is scalability. While EpiCastNet performs well under a controlled environment, scaling it to cover enormous geographic areas or the entire globe is difficult from the point of view of computational complexity and processing real-time data. More efficient architectures, perhaps using edge computing or distributed learning, can be utilized to improve the scalability of the model and process high-volume real-time data at a quicker pace.

Further, EpiCastNet can be enhanced by incorporating other more advanced methods of interpretability and explainability so that public health decision-makers have a better idea of how predictions are made. This will enhance trust and enable more informed decision-making. Finally, research into privacy issues of real-time monitoring and development of privacy-protection methods (e.g., federated learning) will be critical to guaranteeing that sensitive medical data are treated appropriately as the model is deployed in real-world settings.
