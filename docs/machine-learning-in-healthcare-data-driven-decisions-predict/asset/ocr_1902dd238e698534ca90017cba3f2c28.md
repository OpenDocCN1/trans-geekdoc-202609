Dynamic convergence was ensured by the inclusion of a Bayesian-tuned adaptive learning scheduler, whereas interpretability and transparency were encouraged through the use of SHAP, which provided clinically useful explanations. Outcome of experimental validation on the combined NIH ChestX-ray14 and MIMIC-III datasets verified that HAD-Net outperforms state-of-the-art models on accuracy, F1-score, and AUROC. In addition, the utility of the model in real, evidence-based healthcare is exemplified by its ability to identify clinically significant biomarkers. This study fills the gap between state-of-the-art AI and robust clinical application by demonstrating the transformative ability of smart multimodal systems to enable active and personalized patient care.
