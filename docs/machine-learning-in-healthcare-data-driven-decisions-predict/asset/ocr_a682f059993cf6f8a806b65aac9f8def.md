Further advancing multi-objective optimization, a reinforcement learning method is introduced for dynamic multi-objective optimization problems, utilizing an approach that blends deep reinforcement learning (DRL) with evolutionary computation. The framework dynamically adapts to changing objectives and environmental conditions, enabling continuous optimization across multiple conflicting goals. Experimental results indicate improved convergence speed and solution diversity compared to traditional multi-objective evolutionary algorithms, suggesting suitability for healthcare applications requiring ongoing adaptation, such as personalized treatment planning [3].

In another contribution, an intelligent decision-making scheme employing DRL in dynamic multi-objective environments addresses the challenge of learning optimal policies where objectives evolve over time. This doctoral research presents a model that dynamically adjusts policy learning rates and reward functions to balance multiple healthcare objectives effectively. Despite showing promising results in simulation environments, the approach requires extensive training data and computational resources, which could limit deployment in real-world healthcare systems [4].

Adaptive scheduling and resource optimization in multi-departmental hospitals are tackled using reinforcement learning frameworks designed to manage healthcare staff and resources efficiently. This approach incorporates multi-objective optimization to balance operational efficiency, patient wait times, and staff workload. The reinforcement learning agent learns to optimize scheduling policies dynamically based on real-time hospital data, achieving improved resource utilization and patient throughput. Nevertheless, the framework’s reliance on accurate and timely data remains a critical bottleneck, especially in resource-constrained settings [5].

An evolutionary game-theoretic approach, combined with deep learning, is proposed to support health decision-making in critical environments. This method models interactions among healthcare agents as games with multiple objectives, such as maximizing patient survival rates and minimizing treatment costs. The integration with deep learning enables prediction and adaptation to evolving conditions, facilitating robust decision support in emergency and intensive care contexts. However, the complexity of modeling multi-agent interactions and the interpretability of results require further investigation for clinical adoption [6].

The control of infectious diseases through multi-objective model-based reinforcement learning has been explored to optimize public health interventions. The framework balances objectives such as reducing infection rates, minimizing economic disruption, and maximizing healthcare system resilience. Using simulation-based experiments, the study shows the effectiveness of reinforcement learning policies in identifying Pareto-optimal intervention strategies. Challenges include uncertainty in disease transmission models and the translation of simulation results into actionable policies [7].
