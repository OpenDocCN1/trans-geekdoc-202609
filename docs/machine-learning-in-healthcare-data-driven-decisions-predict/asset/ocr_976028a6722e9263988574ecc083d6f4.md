# 3 Methodology

Stroke continues to be a major cause of morbidity and mortality globally, and early risk stratification and the prediction of functional outcome are essential in enhancing patient care and long-term rehabilitation. Standard prognostic approaches are usually inadequate in describing the intricate relationship between imaging biomarkers, clinical markers, and comorbidities that determine stroke outcomes. To solve this challenge, we introduce a new multimodal ML system that combines high-resolution neuroimaging, structured clinical information, and domain-informed handcrafted features for accurate prediction of poststroke risk and functional recovery. Taking advantage of the dense ISLES dataset, our method integrates a 3D CNN to learn spatial lesion features from MRI images, a GNN to represent clinical similarity between patients, and a LightGBM-based conventional model to retain interpretable clinical knowledge. These diverse data representations are combined into a single latent space and optimized through a multitask learning approach to predict stroke severity risk levels and 90-day functional outcomes on the mRS simultaneously. This DL model hybrid provides an optimistic direction for personalized stroke management with data-driven, explainable, and precise clinical decision support.
