This retrospective data analysis used clinical histories of 1,516 women suspected of having OC, collected at six healthcare centers in Sari City from 2015 to 2019. Six ML models were applied to develop predictive models for OC risk prediction: XGBoost, RF, J48, SVM, KNN, and ANN. # 2.3 AI-Driven Precision Diagnosis in Breast Cancer

Advancements in AI and ML have significantly impacted precision medicine, particularly for BC diagnosis and therapy. From vast genomic, imaging, and clinical databases, AI algorithms make precise tumor detection, identification, and predictive value of outcomes compared to traditional diagnostic processes. ML methods, specifically DL systems, reveal sophisticated patterns in multimodal data and enable the generation of personalized and directed treatment protocols. Applicability of AI to BC care is discussed by this review with examples of cutting-edge solutions, overcoming significant challenges to deployment in the clinic and achieving a perspective of future directions in research. Through comparison of data-driven approaches, it recognizes their contribution toward enabling early diagnosis, supporting clinical decisions, and ultimately benefiting patients [20].

# 2.4 Colorectal Chemotherapy Toxicity Prediction Model

ML algorithms reveal patterns within data through the identification of feature correlations and predictive insight into intricate clinical situations. The research pursued building a predictive model for recognizing colorectal cancer patients who are more likely to develop chemotherapy-induced toxicity and distinguishing the characteristics responsible for inducing the risk of side effects of treatment. Using a dataset of 95 pre-treatment health features from 74 patients, we have trained RF models with appropriate preprocessing to achieve a compromise between interpretability and predictive performance. The model successfully detected the relative contribution of numerical and categorical variables toward predicting toxicity outcome. The findings highlight the promise of integrating AI into colorectal cancer management, offering a personalized approach to anticipate and prevent treatment toxicities, advancing the boundary of precision medicine [21].
