# Analyzing the Impact of Social Network on Epidemiological Spread in the Healthcare Sector

Abstract: The emergence of complex network-based frameworks has significantly evidenced the development in health monitoring in the most complicated terrain of disease tracking. This shift in paradigm brings together sophisticated network theory, live data feeds, and social network analytics to develop a flexible system that is comprehensive. By exploring the complex network of connections between individuals, healthcare facilities, and locations within a geographical area, this framework utilizes graph theoretic concepts to depict relations as well as identify central individual elements (nodes/vertices) in disease spread. Predictive modeling gets enhanced by complex network concepts and statistical techniques that allows early outbreak detection and targeted intervention. The ultimate aim of this study is toward tracking of diseases through a complex network framework by analyzing the disease spread patterns in an egocentric network. This provides insightful findings for future healthcare surveillance and enables healthcare providers to contain the disease spread by exploiting social network analytics.

Keywords: disease spread detection, complex network, healthcare, statistical analysis, social network analytics
