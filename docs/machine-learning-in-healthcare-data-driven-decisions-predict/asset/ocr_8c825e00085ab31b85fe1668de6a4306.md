The requirement for a more holistic, interpretable, and universally applicable method of healthcare analytics is the motivation behind the development of the HAD-Net architecture. The complementariness between structured (such as EHR) and unstructured (such as imaging) data is often underleveraged in current models, resulting in disjointed clinical insights. HAD-Net addresses this by employing multimodal learning mechanisms that involve self-attention in order to model high-level dependencies between modalities, GNNs for relational learning between patients, and BiLSTM for EHR dynamics over time. HAD-Net is robust and trustworthy, as the Bayesian-tuned adaptive learning scheduler enhances optimization efficiency, and SHAP ensures predictions are interpretable to medical professionals.
