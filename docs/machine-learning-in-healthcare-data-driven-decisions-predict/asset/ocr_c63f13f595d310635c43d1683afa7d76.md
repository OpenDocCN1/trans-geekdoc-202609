Figure 4: Performance analysis – TNPE – ROC-AUC.

As scored with the ROC-AUC metric analysis in Figure 4, a measure of how well a model can discriminate between positive and negative classes across all probability thresholds, the TNPE achieves a greater value of 0.963. Such outstanding performance guarantees TNPE's better discriminative power to separate instances of depression and anxiety from non-instances with accuracy, a highly desirable characteristic in clinical prediction environments where misclassification is of serious nature. On the other hand, MLP returns a decent ROC-AUC score of 0.905, followed by GBM at 0.899 and kNN at 0.893, with each of these possessing fairly decent classification performances yet not quite equaling TNPE's optimized ensemble approach. AdaBoost at 0.892 lags behind kNN, though competitive yet possessing less discriminatory ability. By contrast, NB at 0.842 and DT at 0.865 share comparably lower ROC-AUC values that signal their diminished ability to always be able to discriminate between states of mind and noncases. That failing is particularly unfortunate in medical diagnosis applications, when very high specificity and sensitivity are crucial to make sure clinical judgments are trustworthy, while preventing pernicious mistakes of diagnosis.
