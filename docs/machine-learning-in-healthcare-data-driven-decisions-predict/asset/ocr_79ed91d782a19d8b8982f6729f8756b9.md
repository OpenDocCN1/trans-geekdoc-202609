# 2.3 Feature Engineering in Disease Diagnosis

Feature engineering is selecting, transforming, or creating additional features from raw data to rehabilitate or improve performance on ML models. Feature engineering, therefore, plays an important role in obtaining the information that is clinically meaningful and relevant from intricate medical data in disease diagnosis [11].

Medical Image Analysis: Radiomics: It is the extraction of quantitative characteristics of medical images, like size, shape, texture, and intensity of tumor tissue, which may provide significant and valuable disease profile and prognosis information.

Deep Feature Extraction: It is an automatic learning of relevant features using pre-trained deep networks from medical images.

Genomics: Single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs): These are genetic variations that can be identified to associate the risk of having a disease.

Gene Expression Analysis: It is the studies about the activity levels of genes that can provide insights into disease mechanisms and drug targets [12].

Electronic Health Records (EHR): Clinical codes: These are information retrieved through a standard coding system, like ICD-10, which codes for diagnosis, procedure, and medications.

Temporal Features: These are event sequences and time interval analysis of medical events capable of detecting the evolution pattern of diseases.

Natural Language Processing (NLP): It is the extracting of structured information from unstructured text through techniques such as named entity recognition and sentiment analysis in clinical notes and discharge summaries [13].
