## 5 Discussion

Real-time prediction of disease epidemic by EpiCastNet is a step forward from the traditional models like ARIMA, LSTM, and Prophet. The fusion of spatiotemporal data with GNNs and TCNs provides a robust and dynamic model to identify the spatial and temporal pattern of epidemic spread. The adaptive learning online module allows EpiCastNet to adjust to shifting trends in disease, helping in early warning systems for intervention at the correct time. Handling heterogeneity and uncertainty within the data may be the biggest challenge that faces disease forecasting models. EpiCastNet tackles such a problem by integrating data from different sources such as healthcare records, social media, and environmental data to provide a greater and timelier set of data. The multisource fusion enhances the model and enables it to respond optimally to changes in public health status. In addition, spatial precision and anticipation time offered by EpiCastNet are particularly valuable for public health practitioners who need to respond quickly in the case of new emerging outbreaks. The model's performance remains reliant as much on input data availability and quality, particularly from nontraditional sources such as social media and mobility data. Promising as it is, it must deploy the model into live environments with a keen eye to ethics and privacy, particularly where sensitive health data is being accessed. It will adopt techniques such as federated learning and differential privacy to make this simple, but more is required to do it safely and ethically at scale.

## 6 Conclusion

EpiCastNet is advancement in real-time public health surveillance. Combining spatio-temporal modeling, adaptive online learning, and multisource data fusion, it delivers highly accurate and real-time prediction of disease outbreaks. Through the use of the combination of GNN and TCNs, the model can understand the complexity of the spread of the epidemic and deliver both spatial and temporal accuracy compared to the standard models like ARIMA and LSTM. The ability to forecast outbreaks 6.2 days in advance and identify outbreaks with IoU of 0.72 makes EpiCastNet an extremely useful tool for health authorities as it enables them to intervene preventively in advance and enable resource allocation to be maximized. Additionally, the model's high AQI of 0.80 indicates its ability to provide timely and accurate warning at a very low false alarm rate. Though the model does well on test cases, it is challenging to employ in practice due to concerns of data quality, scalability, and constant updating to stay responsive to evolving patterns of illness. Nevertheless, EpiCastNet offers a good pathway toward next-generation epidemic forecasting systems that are predictive, accurate, adaptive, and interpretable and can play a role to support global health systems to adapt to improved health crises in the future.
