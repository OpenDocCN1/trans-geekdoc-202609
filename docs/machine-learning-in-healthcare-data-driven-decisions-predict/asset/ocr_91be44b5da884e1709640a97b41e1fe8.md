# 3 Methodology

The study is a comprehensive, data-driven ML model for prediction and early diagnosis of oncological pathology and precursors. Owing to the complexity and multidimensionality of data related to oncology, the study includes heterogeneous data like clinical reports, imaging data, genomic data, and biomarker datasets. The study involves routine data preprocessing, advanced FS methods and usage of diverse ML models for diverse data. In order to ensure a strong model that enhances prediction performance and reduces bias, ensemble learning method is used with blending of conventional ML methods, deep models, and recurrent networks for the study of longitudinal data. Utilizing multimodal integration of data as well as hybrid modeling techniques, the proposed approach aims to augment early detection capabilities and facilitate data-driven clinical decision-making in cancer.
