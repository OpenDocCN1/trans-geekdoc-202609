## 4 Proposed Algorithm

To facilitate accurate and personalized treatment suggestions, we introduce CareRec-Net, a patient-oriented treatment suggestion system integrating knowledge-enhanced attention with deep sequence models. The model is designed to abstract domain relationships between medical concepts, learn temporal patterns from longitudinal EHR data, and provide explanations that are evidence-based and interpretable. The architecture supports improved representation learning of unstructured and structured clinical information by integrating an attention-augmented knowledge graph embedding module (AKGEM) together with a bidirectional long short-term memory (BiLSTM) network for temporal learning.

Let \( X = \{x_1, x_2, \dots, x_T\} \) be the sequence of patient visit records over \( T \) time steps, where each \( x_t \in R^d \) represents a \( d \)-dimensional feature vector including vitals, lab results, medications, and diagnosis embeddings extracted for time step \( t \).

### Step 1: Temporal Modeling using BiLSTM

To capture the sequential patterns in a patient's health evolution, we employ a BiLSTM network. The LSTM cell is defined as:

$$\begin{aligned} f_t &= \sigma(W_f \cdot [h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_f)i_t \quad (1) \\ &= \sigma(W_i \cdot [h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_i)C_t^- \quad (2) \\ &= \tanh(W_c \cdot [h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_c)C_t \quad (3) \\ &= f_t * C_{t-1} + i_t * C_t o_t \quad (4) \\ &= \sigma(W_o \cdot [h_{t-1}, x_t] + b_o)h_t \quad (5) \\ &= o_t * \tanh(C_t) \quad (6) \end{aligned}$$

where $f_t, i_t, o_t$: forget, input, and output gates respectively, $C_t$: cell state at time t, $h_t$: hidden state at time t, $\sigma$: sigmoid activation function, $W_f, W_i, W_c, W_o$: weight matrices, $b_f, b_i, b_c, b_o$: biases.

The forward and backward hidden states are concatenated as:

$$ h_t \leftrightarrow = [\overrightarrow{h_t}; h_t \leftarrow] \quad (7) $$

This produces a context-rich representation of the patient's temporal progression.

### Step 2: Medical Knowledge Graph Construction

To integrate domain-specific relationships, we build a medical knowledge graph $G = (V, E)$ where

- $V = \text{set of clinical concepts (diagnoses, medications, procedures)}$
- $E = \text{set of relationships (e.g., "treats," "associated\_with," "contraindicates")}.$

Each concept $v_i \in V$ is embedded into a vector space using TransE, which enforces that

$$ v_i + r_{ij} \approx v_j \quad (8) $$

for each triplet $(v_i, r_{ij}, v_j) \in E$, where $r_{ij}$ is the relation embedding. The objective function minimized is:

$$\begin{aligned} L_{KG} &= \sum_{(h,r,t) \in T} [||h + r - t||_2^2] \\ &+ \sum_{(h',r,t') \in T'} [\max(0, \gamma + ||h + r - t||_2^2 - ||h' + r - t'||_2^2)] \quad (9) \end{aligned}$$

where $T$ represents the true triplets, $T^{'}$ the corrupted triplets, and $\gamma$ the margin hyper-parameter.

### Step 3: Knowledge-Attentive Feature Fusion

For each time step t, the temporal representation h_t is augmented with knowledge graph-derived embeddings e_t using an attention mechanism:

```
a_t = softmax(h_t^T W_a e_t) (10)
z_t = a_t \cdot e_t + (1 - a_t) \cdot h_t (11)
```

Here, W_a is a trainable weight matrix. The final fused feature z_t captures both patient dynamics and medical knowledge context.

### Step 4: Treatment Recommendation via Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)

The concatenated patient representation over all time steps is passed through a fully connected network to predict the treatment class:

```
Z = [z_1, z_2, ..., z_T] \in R^{T \times d} (12)
y = softmax(W_out \cdot ReLU(W_fc \cdot Flatten(Z) + b_fc) + b_out) (13)
```

where W_fc, W_out: learned weights, b_fc, b_out: biases, \(\hat{y} \in R^k\): probability distribution over k treatment categories

The model is trained using categorical cross-entropy loss:

```
L_{CE} = - \sum_{i=1}^k y_i \log (\hat{y}_i) (14)
```

where y_i \in {0, 1} is the ground truth label.

As shown in Figure 1, the CareRecNet framework demonstrates a significant advancement over traditional treatment recommendation systems by incorporating several key innovations that enhance its accuracy, adaptability, and clinical relevance. Through the incorporation of a BiLSTM model that replicates the health path of the patient over time, CareRecNet gains temporal consciousness, one of its strongest features. This allows the framework to capture cyclic patterns and fluctuations in lab results and vital signs typically lost to static models. The ability of the model to provide safe and appropriate decisions is enhanced by including clinically pertinent information, like drug-disease relationships and contraindications. Through an attentional mechanism that emphasizes the most significant features, such as recent alterations in lab values or required diagnoses, CareRecNet puts the focus on individualizing and interpretability. This explainability can enhance clinician trust and medical audits.
