# 4.1 Identifying High-Risk Patients Using ML

Patient risk stratification involves categorizing individuals based on their likelihood of experiencing specific adverse events, such as hospitalization, disease progression, or complications. Figure 4, Illustrate the ML algorithms are particularly well-suited for this task, as they can analyze large and complex datasets to uncover hidden risk factors and patterns that may not be apparent through traditional methods. Logistic regression, decision trees, SVMs, and gradient boosting machines are commonly used for risk prediction. More recently, deep learning models have been applied to longitudinal patient data to capture temporal patterns that indicate deteriorating health conditions. One notable application is predicting the risk of readmission within 30 days post-discharge, which has significant implications for both patient care and healthcare costs. By identifying high-risk patients early, healthcare providers can implement targeted interventions such as follow-up calls, medication reconciliation, and personalized discharge planning to prevent avoidable readmissions and improve overall outcomes [14].
