# 2 Related Study

The integration of multi-objective optimization and reinforcement learning techniques in healthcare has gained substantial traction in recent years. This trend is driven by the increasing complexity of healthcare systems, where decision-making must balance multiple, often conflicting objectives such as maximizing diagnostic accuracy, optimizing treatment effectiveness, minimizing adverse effects, and efficiently utilizing resources. Recent literature explores diverse methodologies ranging from systematic reviews of machine learning-powered healthcare management systems to cutting-edge reinforcement learning frameworks designed for adaptive and dynamic healthcare environments.
