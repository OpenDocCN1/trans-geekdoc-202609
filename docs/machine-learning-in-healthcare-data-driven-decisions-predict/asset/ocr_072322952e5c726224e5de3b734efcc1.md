# # 6 Applications of Data-Driven Approaches in Healthcare Delivery

By digitizing healthcare, methods that involve analyzing data have been incorporated into daily clinic and hospital routines. All the data available from various electronic and medical sources can now be used as a useful tool to improve management, delivery, and quality of healthcare. Now that intelligent algorithms are being used in healthcare, we are seeing improvements in patient care, healthcare resources, and more.

# ## 6.1 Enhancing Diagnostic Accuracy and Treatment Planning

In healthcare, using data-based methods often increases the accuracy of diagnosis. Using the usual methods to diagnose a condition can be influenced by a doctor's opinion and, at times, fail to be accurate. Using AI algorithms and evidence-based models with clinical information, doctors, and nurses can now detect hidden trends that the human eye may miss, making it less likely there will be errors during diagnoses. There have been promising outcomes using ML techniques after training on extensive medical records in radiology, pathology, and dermatology. For instance, using CNNs, it is now common to distinguish malignant tumors and benign tumors as well as expert radiologists do [43]. Furthermore, some DSSs look at a patient's history, labs, and clinical guidance to recommend the best care plan. They allow for the same practices to be used by health professionals and increase consistency in medical care.
