# 5 Operational Efficiency and Public Health Applications

ML impacts many areas of life in healthcare besides direct patient care, such as healthcare operations and public health. Healthcare operations are very complex and resource-consuming, and ML can eliminate many bottlenecks in the whole administrative and logistical process. For instance, predicting when patients may not show up for an appointment enables clinicians to optimize scheduling and minimize wasted resources. Specifically, ML can analyze any historical appointment data and patient demographic and environmental factors to identify patients most likely not to show up and intervene proactively with reminder calls or offer flexibility with scheduling.

One more sector where ML will be of great aid is in optimizing the allocation of hospital resources, staffing, bed management, and equipment utilization. As per predicting patient admissions, length of stay, and demand for specific services, the above predictions can greatly benefit hospital administrators in making good decisions on resource allocation to be put where they are needed in time. This ensures reduced waiting periods, improved flow of patients, and better use of costly resources, such as those experienced in predicting the demand for ICU beds, which can help hospitals manage their capacity better [12].

Fraud detection in healthcare is a big challenge. It accounts for losses in annual billions and is where ML comes in handy because these AI techniques, especially the anomaly detection algorithms, can easily and quickly identify suspicious patterns in billing data, claims submissions, and provider behavior that may indicate fraudulent illegalities. So, by simply analyzing copious amounts of transaction data, the ML models can suspect a few claims and flag them for follow-up and investigation; such capability will help healthcare payers and government agencies combat fraud and abuse.
