# 6 Conclusion

EpiCastNet is advancement in real-time public health surveillance. Combining spatio-temporal modeling, adaptive online learning, and multisource data fusion, it delivers highly accurate and real-time prediction of disease outbreaks. Through the use of the combination of GNN and TCNs, the model can understand the complexity of the spread of the epidemic and deliver both spatial and temporal accuracy compared to the standard models like ARIMA and LSTM. The ability to forecast outbreaks 6.2 days in advance and identify outbreaks with IoU of 0.72 makes EpiCastNet an extremely useful tool for health authorities as it enables them to intervene preventively in advance and enable resource allocation to be maximized. Additionally, the model's high AQI of 0.80 indicates its ability to provide timely and accurate warning at a very low false alarm rate. Though the model does well on test cases, it is challenging to employ in practice due to concerns of data quality, scalability, and constant updating to stay responsive to evolving patterns of illness. Nevertheless, EpiCastNet offers a good pathway toward next-generation epidemic forecasting systems that are predictive, accurate, adaptive, and interpretable and can play a role to support global health systems to adapt to improved health crises in the future.
