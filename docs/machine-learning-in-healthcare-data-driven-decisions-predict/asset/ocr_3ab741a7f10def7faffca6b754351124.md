$$ \min_{w, b, \xi} \frac{1}{2} ||w||^2 + C \sum_{i=1}^m \xi_i $$

Subject to

$$ y_i(W^T x_i + b) \ge 1 - \xi_i, \xi_i \ge 0 $$

In eq. (14), C is the regularization parameter.

SVM uses a kernel \( K(x_i, x_j) \) to translate input characteristics into a higher-dimensional space for nonlinear data without directly calculating the transformation. The common kernels are as follows:

$$ K(x, z) = x^T z \, (\text{linear}) $$

$$ K(x, z) = (X^T z + 1)^d \, (\text{polynomial}) $$

$$ K(x, z) = \exp \left( - \frac{||x - z||^2}{2\sigma^2} \right) \, (\text{RBF}) $$

After training, a new input x is classified as follows:

$$ f(x) = \text{sign} \left( \sum_{i=1}^m \alpha_i y_i K(x_i, x) + b \right) $$

In equation \((\alpha_i)\) are the legend support vector weights.
