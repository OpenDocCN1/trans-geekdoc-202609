To preserve temporal trends, the continuous IoMT streams were segmented into 5-min fixed windows. Mean and first-order derivatives (e.g., heart rate change rate) were computed. A Savitzky-Golay filter was used to eliminate high-frequency noise and preserve signal trends and reduce the impact of noise on real-time sensor readings. ICD-9 diagnosis codes were also label encoded to make them compatible with multiclass classification models. Input data was tidy, well-structured, and optimized for precise diagnostic prediction and dynamic patient tracking because of our powerful preparation pipeline.
