**Precision** – The ratio of accurate positive predictions to all predicted positives for a given class. Shows how well the model can prevent false positives [21].

**Recall (Sensitivity)** – The ratio of the true positives to the actual positives. Indicates the capacity of the model to capture all instances that are relevant.

**F1-score** – The harmonic mean of precision and recall. Particularly valuable with imbalanced datasets where certain stress levels can be underrepresented.

**Confusion Matrix** – A tabular representation that shows the correct and incorrect predictions made by the classifier, broken down by class. It helps to identify specific areas of weakness, such as confusion between stress levels 2 and 3.
