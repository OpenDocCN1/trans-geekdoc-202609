# 2.9 Robust Ensemble Breast Cancer Classifier

BC is a prevalent and life-threatening condition evidencing the importance of accurate and efficient methods of diagnosis to aid the treatment of patients. DL and ML have been major contributors to optimizing the detection of BC in terms of enhanced accuracy, automation, and support in the diagnostic process. Yet, most existing approaches remain plagued by problems related to scalability and performance in general. In order to address these limitations, this study presents a reliable hybrid detection approach that combines a pretrained deep ResNet50V2 model with ML ensemble-based classifiers. The DL component provides the functionality to extract discriminative and descriptive features of breast histopathology images, while the ML ensemble provides the functionality of model explainability as well as better generalization. The suggested approach was applied on a publicly available dataset of IDC histopathological images consisting of examples of different sizes. Experimental outcomes verify that the hybrid method is superior to many state-of-the-art methods by far, with an accuracy rate of 95%. Out of the experimented classifiers, the light gradient boosting machine was most powerful in combination with ResNet50V2. The hybrid process is a high-performance, robust BC diagnostic tool that can potentially be used in real-world clinical situations. It facilitates clinicians to make speedy and high-confidence decisions [26].

Convergence of AI, DL, and ML into oncology has transformed cancer diagnosis, prognosis, and tailoring therapy. The series of studies discussed as a whole provides the scope and magnitude of AI-driven approaches to different cancers most importantly breast, ovarian, colorectal, and lung cancers. From genomics-based predictive models to clinical risk stratification and advanced image analysis, these AI platforms are increasingly proving their value in everyday clinical practice. Most importantly, holistic approaches like BGWO_SA_Ens in BC biomarker discovery and the hybrid CNN-GRU in IDC prediction both take advantage of biological intuition and computational effectiveness to achieve higher accuracy and predictive robustness. Similarly, ensemble methods ranging from classical ML stacks to deep ensemble networks that ensemble architectures like ResNet50, InceptionV3, and VGG16 all best individual models, a testament to hybridization's power in fault-tolerant diagnostic feature design.

In addition, application of FS techniques (e.g., chi-square and PPI analysis), image processing techniques (e.g., CLAHE, MedSAM, and YOLOv5), and statistical testing (e.g., t-tests and Borda count) assure scientific validity and clinical transibility of such models. Achievements like near-perfect F1-scores (e.g., 0.981), AUC scores > 0.95, and consistent cross-validation accuracy scores per dataset and cancer type assure practicality of such models.
