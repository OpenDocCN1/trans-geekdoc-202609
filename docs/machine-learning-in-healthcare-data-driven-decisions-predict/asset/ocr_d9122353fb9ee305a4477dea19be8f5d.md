Also, predictive scheduling helps allocate the right number of equipment and facilities, based on expected needs by patients, focused on avoiding bottlenecks and having the resources readily available. Operations dealing with supply chain become crucial and imperative in healthcare facilities. A hospital requires sufficient supplies of medications, medical equipment, and consumables, without overstocking a hospital facility. This ties up valuable resources and incurs unnecessary costs. Predictive analytics, powered by ML models, can help assess the demand for diverse supplies based on the number of patients, seasonal trends, and historical usage data. Hospitals will thus maintain the required inventory at all times, thereby reducing wastage and non-availability. Proper management of the supply chain would then lead to cost reduction, improved cash flow, and avoidance of interruptions in services. By applying ML to such operational challenges, overheads would be lowered and resource utilization improved, while patient care is enhanced. More dependable staffing, better resource management, and optimized inventory systems permit healthcare institutions to utilize time and conduct more efficient care options, which all lead to improved patient outcomes.

# 3.1 Much More Applications of Machine Learning in Operation Efficiency

Examples of more specialized roles of ML in operational efficiency and resource management are described next.

Improving Bed Management: This can be accomplished by capturing input variables such as patient admission, length of stay, and discharge patterns, through automated bed allocation. This brings about an increase or decrease in a patient’s throughput, reduce waiting time, and lower occupancy in the correction of bed utilization.

Predicting No-Shows and Cancellations: ML model-trained prediction for missing an appointment might lead the staff to proactively reschedule or allow those slots available for other patients. So, it reduces wasted resources and schedules appointments in an efficient way.

Streamlining Claims Processing: Insurance claim processing is automated by ML processing of claims, which reduces manual effort, speeds the payment process, and detects claims as fraud.

Improving Supply Chain Management: Modeling of inventory levels by ML predicts demand for specific medicines and supplies, automates procurement activity, minimizes waste, lowers the cost of storage, and ensures the right inventory of essentials is in place.
