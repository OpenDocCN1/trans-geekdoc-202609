LR + Manual Feature Selection: The method utilizing LR with hand FS performed worst among the methods under testing. With accuracy standing at 75%, it lags a very long distance from other models with regard to predictiveness. Metrics of 72%, 70%, and 71% on precision, recall, and F1-score, respectively, illustrate that the method performs bad on marking correct positives with accuracy as well as in weighting obtaining true positives versus the generation of false positives. It is due to the reason that logistic regression is simple and manual FS cannot capture subtle, nonlinear interrelations of the data.

RF with RFE does reasonably well with an accuracy of 82%. It is quite an improvement from the LR-based approach, and precision 80%, recall 78%, and F1-score 79% are achieved. It implies that although RF, as a method of ensembling, is more capable to handle high-dimensional and complex data, it is behind DL-based techniques. RFE facilitates enhanced model efficiency through the selection and retention of the most suitable features, yet its standalone function is limited to detecting complex patterns of data vis-a-vis hybrid or DL techniques.

DL based on CNN which is applied in image-based detection fares exceptionally with 85% performance rate. Precise and recall performances of 83% and 82% and F1-score of 82% reflect that CNN handles the discovery of image-related properties and features available in the activity of classification successfully. Though this is a better method than elementary ML algorithms, its effectiveness is still less than the proposed hybrid methodology. This suggests that though CNNs are very good at handling image data, using them along with other models that can process other forms of data structures could lead to even greater levels of predictive performance.
