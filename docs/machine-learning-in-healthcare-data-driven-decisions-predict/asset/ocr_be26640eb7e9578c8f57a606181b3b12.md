# # AGBO-CP: An Adaptive Gradient Boosted Optimization Framework for Enhanced Clinical Prediction Accuracy

**Abstract:** Machine learning (ML) in recent years has emerged as a disruptive technology in healthcare to enable automated diagnoses, personalized therapy, and predictive analytics. Traditional ML models suffer from some of the disadvantages like overfitting, lack of good generalizability across patient groups, and poor tuning of parameters that compromise clinical accuracy and reliability. We propose a new optimization-based paradigm, adaptive gradient boosted optimization for clinical prediction (AGBO-CP), to mitigate these challenges. To enhance model robustness and explainability, this approach integrates ensemble learning with a dynamic hyperparameter adjustment process, leveraging Bayesian optimization and evolutionary algorithms. AGBO-CP is better than standard models such as random forest (92.4%) and support vector machine (89.1%), with diagnostic accuracy of 96.8% when utilized on benchmark health datasets such as MIMIC-III and UCI Heart Disease. Moreover, the proposed method demonstrated increased sensitivity (95.9%), specificity (94.7%), and area under the curve (97.3%), suggesting prospects of real-world application in clinical decision support systems.

**Keywords:** adaptive optimization, AGBO-CP framework, clinical decision support, healthcare machine learning, diagnostic performance, hyperparameter tuning
