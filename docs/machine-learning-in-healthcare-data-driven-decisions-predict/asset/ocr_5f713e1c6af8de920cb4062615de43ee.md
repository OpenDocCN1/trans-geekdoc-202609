+   7. Resilience to Attacks and Failures: Complex networks exhibit resilience against random failures but are susceptible to focused attacks on crucial nodes, often known as hubs. This feature has impact on the design and security of the diverse system.

+   8. Dynamics and emergent phenomena: Complex networks often exhibit dynamic processes, including information dissemination, epidemic propagation, synchronization, and phase transitions. Emergent phenomena, characterized by the emergence of global features, resulting from the interactions of individual components, are frequently seen.

# 3.2 Complex Network in Healthcare Sector

In terms of epidemiological spread, a complex network refers to a system where people, healthcare institutions, and communities are interrelated in complicated ways that impact the transmission and management of infectious illnesses. Gaining insights into the organization and behavior of these networks is essential for developing efficient approaches to control and reduce the transmission of illnesses.

Some essential elements of complex networks in terms of the illness spread in the healthcare industry:

+   1. Graph components: nodes and edges:

+   a) Nodes: Nodes represent various things such as people, healthcare personnel, healthcare institutions, and communities within the network. These entities may either serve as carriers of a disease or be possible targets for intervention.

+   b) Edges: They reflect the connections or interactions between nodes, serving as channels for the transmission of illnesses, for instance, the dynamics between patients and healthcare professionals, or among members within a community.

+   2. Network Structure: The network structure may exhibit heterogeneity, with some nodes acting as hubs with a larger number of connections, while others have less connections. This indicates that certain persons or regions may have a greater impact on the spread of diseases.

+   3. Transmission Dynamics: The manner in which infectious diseases spread within a network is affected by elements such as patterns of contact, persons' movement, and their behavior in seeking treatment. Collecting insight into these processes is crucial for accurately forecasting and managing outbreaks.

+   4. Super-spreaders: It is important to identify super-spreaders, who are the people who have an unusually large influence on the transmission of diseases. Within the healthcare industry, this might include individuals afflicted with highly transmissible illnesses or healthcare professionals who often interact with patients [21, 22].
