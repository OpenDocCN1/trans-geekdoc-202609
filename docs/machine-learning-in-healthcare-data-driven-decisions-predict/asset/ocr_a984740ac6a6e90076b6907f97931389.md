Model Interpretability: The black-box nature of advanced ML and DL models can impede clinical adoption, highlighting the need for explainable AI. Future work will need to develop strong, generalizable models, fuse multimodal data streams, improve interpretability, and account for privacy and ethics.

# 3 Applications in Multimodal Stress Detection

ML-based stress detection systems have gained increasing real-world utilization in a wide range of domains. With the use of physiological and behavioral signals gathered from wearable sensors and mobile devices, these systems allow for early interventions, enhance mental healthcare, and enhance performance in high-stakes settings. The following presents the major areas in which stress detection models are being successfully used:

# 3.1 Health and Mental Well-Being

Remote patient monitoring: ML-based stress monitoring allows patients suffering from anxiety disorders, PTSD, depression, or chronic pain to be monitored in real-time. Clinicians are able to preemptively adjust treatment procedures by trend analysis and early warning [11,12].

Digital therapeutics: These mHealth AI-based apps such as Wysa, Youper, and Woebot use stress detection algorithms to personalize interventions such as cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), guided meditation, or breathing exercises.

Chronic Disease Management: Stress is a causative factor in diseases like diabetes, cardiovascular disease, and autoimmune disorders. Predictive models assist in clinical stabilization management by identifying deterioration of symptoms or complications caused by stress [12,13].

Telehealth Integration: Telehealth-enabled wearables offer visualization of stress trends to enable physicians to make evidence-based mental health decisions even for remote consultations.

# 3.2 Corporate and Workplace Wellness

Employee Assistance Programs (EAPs): Organizations are incorporating stress analytics into well-being initiatives. Stress indices enable collective stress to assist HR departments in finding departments most vulnerable to burnout.
