# ## 2.6 Behavioral Signal Integration-Based Approaches

Whereas moment-to-moment information comes from physiological signals, behavioral indicators such as sleep patterns, movement rhythm, eye movement, and snoring provide longitudinal information regarding stress trends. The researchers designed a multimodal stress detection system incorporating physiological signals (HR, EDA) together with behavioral patterns (sleep duration, movement abnormalities, and activity level). Through GBM, their system registered an _F_<sub>1</sub>-score of 91.8%. The research proved that including behavioral data helps minimize false positives by providing context – e.g., distinguishing between an increase in HR during exercise vs. emotional stress [7]. The study utilized actigraphy data and smartphone log sensor data to assess daily behavior patterns. Using XGBoost on ambient noise, screen time, and physical activity features, they attained 88% accuracy. In their work, behavioral drift within days can be an early warning sign of burnout or chronic stress.

# ## 2.7 Deep Learning and Hybrid Architectures

Deep learning structures have unlocked new potential in the modeling of nonlinear and high-dimensional stress signals. They are capable of learning short-term fluctuations as well as long-term dependencies within multimodal streams of data. The study introduced a hybrid CNN-LSTM model that is trained on combined EEG, EDA, and accelerometer data from the AMIGOS dataset. CNN layers were employed to learn spatial features, and LSTM learned temporal dependencies [8]. The model had 94.6% accuracy and 93.8% recall. Their result indicated that hierarchical combination of signals improves the stability of predictions, particularly with noisy or missing data.

Authors presented a multi-head attention-based transformer network to detect stress from physiological time-series data. Their approach enhanced explainability by determining which components of the signal (e.g., HR spikes or EDA troughs) were primarily responsible for the stress classification outcome.
