(I) Fitbit Sense: A commercially available smartwatch allows individuals to regularly monitor their health-related information, such as heart rate, body temperature, and oxygen level. This device is used to collect physiological data in the LifeSnaps dataset.

(II) SEMA3: SEMA3 is a mobile application installed on the participants’ smartphones to facilitate regular surveys. Two surveys are conducted: (a) context and mood survey and (b) step goal survey.

(III) Surveys: The researchers conduct surveys to gather subjects’ psychological and demographic data for ground truth. The demographics, PAR-Q [25], IPIP [26–28], BREQ-2 [29, 30], S-STAI [31], and PANAS [32] are surveys included in the LifeSnaps dataset.
