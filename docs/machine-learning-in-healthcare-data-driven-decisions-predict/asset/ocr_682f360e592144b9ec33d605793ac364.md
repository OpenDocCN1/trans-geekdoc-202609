# 7 Conclusion

The advanced complex network-based framework proposed in this study signifies a state-of-the-art solution to deal with challenges arising from changing landscapes when it comes to disease tracking. This framework leverages statistical techniques, real-time data streams, and SNA as an adaptable approach to understand and counter the spread of diseases. By exploiting graph theoretic concepts, we obtained the mapping between interconnected healthcare systems, identifying nodes and patterns of transmission. Our proposed approach not only elevates the precision of predictive modeling but also delivers practical insights to enable medical professionals in taking preventative actions. Thus, this innovative framework can be regarded as an indication of development that has the potential to transform healthcare surveillance and contribute toward global disease spread network monitoring.
