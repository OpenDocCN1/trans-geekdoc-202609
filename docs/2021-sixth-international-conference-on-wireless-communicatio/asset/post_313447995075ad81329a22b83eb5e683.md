## V. CONCLUSION

In this paper, we propose a detailed indoor localization technique wherein the augmented fingerprinting technique is used. The RSSI-based fingerprinting database is collected from the four ANs with the aid of the UN mounted on a line follower robot, which goes around to collect the RSSI values in the area which is divided into grids. Because of the compact indoor location affected by multipath propagation and interference, huge fluctuations in the RSSI values are observed. To mitigate this drawback and to accurately estimate the location of the grids, Machine learning classifiers are employed after appropriate augmented data is obtained. Following augmentation, the data increases threefold from 1180 to 3200 rows, which increases the accuracy of localization from 61% to 96%. Five different Machine Learning Classifiers were employed to compare the metrics. Among these, Random forest classifier turned out to be the most accurate with 96% accuracy and also the lowest error at 0.04. For future work, we suggest that the readers devise a method of increasing any minimal fingerprinting dataset substantially more, which can be utilized by CNNs for localization.
