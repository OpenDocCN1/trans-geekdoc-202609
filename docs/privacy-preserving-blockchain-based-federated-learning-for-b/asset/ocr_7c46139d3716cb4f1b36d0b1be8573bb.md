```
\( F_l(w) = \frac{1}{|D_i|} \sum_{j \in D_i} f_j(w; x_i, y_i) \) (2)
```

A global loss function \( f_j(w; x_i, y_i) \) minimizes the weights for multiple hospitals in a dataset \( D \). In order to handle the statistical heterogeneity among participating clients, a proximal term is added as a local subproblem to the each client optimization so as to effectively limit the impact of divergent variable local updates as:

```
\( F_i(w; w^t) = \frac{1}{|D_i|} \sum_{j \in D_i} f_j(w; x_i, y_i) + \frac{\mu}{2} \|w - w^t\|^2, \) (3)
```

where \( w^t \) is the latest global parameter update and \( \mu \) is a scaled parameter for the proximal term.

The global model function \( F(w) \) is defined as the difference between the estimated and actual values for each hospital, represented as \( f_j(w; x_i, y_i) \).

```
\( F(w) = \frac{1}{|M_I|} \sum_{l \in I} u_l \cdot F_l(w) \)
\( = \frac{1}{|M_I|} \sum_{l \in I} \sum_{j \in D_i} u_l \cdot \frac{f_j(w; x_i, y_i)}{|D_i|} \) (4)
```

where \( i \) represents a sample \( (x_i, y_i) \) from the gallery \( I = 1, 2, \dots, n \), and \( n_i \) denotes the number of individual dataset models of hospitals. In our proposed training method, we improved the model's accuracy through an iterative process of minimizing the loss function associated with the global model.

# 2.2. Background of blockchain

Training an effective AI model for Industry 4.0 requires collecting data from multiple sources without compromising privacy. For authenticating users, a blockchain network is essential. Several challenges persist in federated learning on a central server, such as insufficient incentives and the risk of poisoning attacks. To address these, some authors, such as Lu et al. [39] and Qu et al. [40], have utilized blockchain to secure federated learning data. Choi and Pokhrel [41] designed a technique to safeguard the privacy of federated learning using blockchain. However, a major issue with previous studies is their failure to include encryption and masking techniques for securing model parameters. Therefore, we combine federated learning with a blockchain distributed ledger for secure data sharing and to update the global AI model with data from various hospitals. The primary advantage of blockchain technology is its ability to collect data models from different nodes and aggregate local and global models. Smart contracts are used to upload the weights and update the models. The architcture we propose seamlessly combines the strengths of blockchain technology with the capabilities of federated learning, resulting in a fully decentralized system with enhanced security measures. This decentralized approach not only improves the precision of the model but also cultivates a higher level of trust among the participating entities.
