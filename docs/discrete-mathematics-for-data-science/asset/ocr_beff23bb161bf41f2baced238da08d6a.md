# Abstract

Data scientists spend much of their time generating statistical reports and dealing with probabilities.

This chapter provides an introduction to probability theory, including joint probabilities and conditional probabilities. Probability is a measurement or estimate of the likelihood of some result, on a scale from 0 to 1. A probability can be calculated theoretically in some situations, but in other situations we can only estimate a probability based on the frequency of an event's occurrence in the past relative to a known finite set of possible outcomes.

This chapter also cover descriptive statistics, including measures of variation and measures of a data value's position within a set. Related to measures of position are methods for normalizing and standardizing data. The are important processes in data analysis.
