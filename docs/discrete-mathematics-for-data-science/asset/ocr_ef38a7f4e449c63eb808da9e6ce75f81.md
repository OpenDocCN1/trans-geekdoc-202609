The appeal of a circular queue in data science and in computer science is that it can be used to automatically re-allocate memory space. Consider a signal processor that takes analog sound samples. Meta-data representing that sound sample, such as decibel level and frequency, is temporarily stored in buffers of a certain length, say 32 bytes.9 Each sample requires minimal temporary register storage, such as a circular queue, before it is directed to a permanent storage medium or transformed back into analog for amplification through audio speakers.
