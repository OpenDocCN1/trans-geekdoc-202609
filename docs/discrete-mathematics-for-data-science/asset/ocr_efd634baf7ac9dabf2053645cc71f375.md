Each operator in the expression is the root of a two node subtree. The arrangement is the same for infix, prefix, and postfix expressions.

To assist visualizing the respective operations of the full expression (A - B) + (C × D), let us substitute each sub expression with the symbols I and II.

I = (A - B) and II = (C × D)

Thus:

(A - B) + (C × D) = I + II

Or, in postfix:

A B - C D × = II +
