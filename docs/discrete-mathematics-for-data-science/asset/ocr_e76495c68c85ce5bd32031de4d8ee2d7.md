We can use this correspondence as a tool for base conversion. Starting with base 10, extend the positional correspondence as follows:

| 10⁴ | 10³ | 10² | 10¹ | 10⁰ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10000 | 1000 | 100 | 100 | 1 |
| × | × | × | × | × |
| 1 | 0 | 2 | 3 | 6 |

Notice the pattern:

10236 = 1 × 10000 + 0 × 1000 + 2 × 100 + 3 × 10 + 1 × 6
10236 = 10000 + 0 + 200 + 30 + 6
