| dec | hex | bin | BCD |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | B | 1011 | 0001 0001 |
| 12 | C | 1100 | 0001 0010 |
| 13 | D | 1101 | 0001 0011 |
| 14 | E | 1110 | 0001 0100 |
| 15 | F | 1111 | 0001 0101 |

BCD readily converts binary to hexadecimal with 4-bit groupings
of the hexadecimal digits 0 to F. This is sometimes referred to as
grouping.

For example, bcd0011010101 when segmented into 4-bit groups has
the following hexadecimal association:

```
0x3     0x5
0b0011  0b0101
```

Checking 0x3 and 0x5 in this particular BCD lookup table, we can
see that binary is 00110101 and BCD is 00000011 00000101.

Consider the conversion of 0x9C to BCD. In binary we get
10011100, while in BCD we get 00011001 000110010. Therefore, it
is important to know what kind of binary scheme is implemented
by a computer system for such low level processing.
