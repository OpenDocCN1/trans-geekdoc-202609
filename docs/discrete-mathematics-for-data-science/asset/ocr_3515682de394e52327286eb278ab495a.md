To improve the likelihood of resembling a population, a sampling of population data must be done with sufficient randomness. This requires invoking an adequate random number generating function until the sampled data approximates a known parameter of the population data. Otherwise, if sampling is not done at random, then the data may only represent some narrow aspects or segments of the population, and our estimates will be too far off, as seen in Figure 21.6.
