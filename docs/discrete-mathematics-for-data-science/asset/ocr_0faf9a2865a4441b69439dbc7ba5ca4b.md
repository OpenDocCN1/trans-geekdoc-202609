# ## Abstract

Being able to distinguish between simple and complex data types is an important basic skill for data science.

There are generally two types of data. There is a single number that, by itself needs no interpretation. It is _atomic_ in that it is irreducible and we can refer to it as a singular object or element. Atomic elements may also be referred to as foundational or _primitive_ elements. Conversely, there are complex data that are the organization of a collection of both primitive and other complex elements, as well as functions. Such element organizations may be referred to as _abstract data types_ or simply _abstractions_ and are often symbolized by a name.
