The f-test statistic for a regression model is computed as MSR/MSE, the mean of the squared residuals relative to the mean of the squared errors. This is a measure of how much of the variation in the dependent variable is explained by the model, and is used primarily when more than one independent variable is involved. If the model includes only one independent variable, then R2, the coefficient of determination, is used to measure the amount of variation attributed to the model, as discussed earlier.
