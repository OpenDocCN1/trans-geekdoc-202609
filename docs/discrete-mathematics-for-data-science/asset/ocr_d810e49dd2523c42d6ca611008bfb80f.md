into unsorted and sorted sections. To get a sort in descending order, we begin by scanning the unsorted elements for the maximum value, and then swapping that value with the one in the first index. An index counter is then incremented to mark the second index, where the next highest value will be placed. The process is repeated for all indices up to and including the index counter.

The following is a trace of the steps required for a selection sort to put the sequence “5, 4, 6, 3, 7” in descending order.

| Iteration | Unsorted | Maximum | Sorted List |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 5 4 6 3 7 | 7 | 7 |
| 1 | 5 4 6 3 | 6 | 7 6 |
| 2 | 5 4 3 | 5 | 7 6 5 |
| 3 | 4 3 | 4 | 7 6 5 4 |
| 4 | 3 | 3 | 7 6 5 4 3 |
