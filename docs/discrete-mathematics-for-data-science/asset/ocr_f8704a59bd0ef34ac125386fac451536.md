# Abstract

A good understanding of digital logic can assist the data scientist
in optimizing high-performance computing environments. The
skill of being able to think through digital logic is essentially
problem-solving leverage for breaking complex problems down
into simple components of binary logic.

Boolean logic is the conceptual basis for digital logic, which
takes a binary input, such as 0 or 1, representing true or false,
and outputs a binary result. Only a few rules of digital logic are
needed for complex decision making by general purpose
computers.

Arithmetic operations such as addition, subtraction,
multiplication and division, as well as Boolean logic, may be
applied to various representations of binary numbers.
