Unnecessary costs arise from processing data dimensions that do not add value to the model, such as when a model is overly fit to noise, a situation called _overfitting_. Additionally, generating estimates with irrelevant data may be costly, as when they form the erroneous basis of business decisions.

# ## 23.1 HIGHLY DIMENSIONAL DATA

Computing with huge amounts of redundant data, as hidden in _Big Data_, can lead to a significant waste of computing time and resources. To use computing resources efficiently, it is best to identify and minimize redundant data dimensions through appropriate pre-processing of data. Simply put, not all conceivable influences of a forecast variable need be in its predictive model. For example, if automobile sales are of interest, there are myriad attributes of cars for which we can collect data, including make, model, color, power train, etc. There are also economic conditions that may be considered, such as personal income, the unemployment rate, the stock market, etc. The collection of such data for analysis comprises the dimensions of a larger data set. Furthermore, these dimensions will expand as the data scientist applies certain transformative techniques to the original dimensions.

_Data dimension terminology:_

+   - Dimensions: Also referred to as features, factors, fields, predictors, manifolds, variables, columns.
+   - Observations: Also referred to as samples, records, and feature vectors.
