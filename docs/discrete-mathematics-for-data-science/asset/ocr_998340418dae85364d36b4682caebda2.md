M : IF (Y₂ > Y₁ AND Y₃ < Y₂ AND Y₄ > Y₃ AND Y₅ < Y₄)

To ensure that a pattern matching classifier works, the conditions for classification should allow for some deviation. In that way, less exact W patterns can be matched. For example:

M : IF ((Y₂ > Y₁ AND Y₃ < Y₂ AND Y₄ > Y₃ AND Y₅ < Y₄) OR ((Y₂ + 0.01) > Y1 AND Y₃ < (Y₂ + 0.01) AND (Y₄ + 0.01) > Y₃ AND Y₅ < (Y₄ + 0.01)))

More sophisticated classifiers can entail additional dimensions, perhaps even accounting for the scale of patterns.
