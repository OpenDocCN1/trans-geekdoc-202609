# 19.1.4 Joint Probability

Joint probability is the statistical likelihood that multiple events will occur, whether at the same time or in some succession.

The joint probability of two events, $A$ and $B$, may be represented either as $P(A \cap B)$ or $P(A, B)$, and is read “the probability that $A$ and $B$ both occur.”

The computation for the joint probability of events $A$ and $B$ may be given by:

$P(A, B) = P(A) \times P(B \mid A)$

Where $P(A)$ is the probability that event $A$ occurs, and $P(B \mid A)$ is the probability of event $B$ on the condition that event $A$ occurs.
