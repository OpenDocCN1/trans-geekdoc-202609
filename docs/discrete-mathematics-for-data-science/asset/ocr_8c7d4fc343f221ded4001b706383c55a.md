Unlike the logical conjunction, a logical disjunction uses an OR instead of AND. For example:

*Daisy likes bones or Daisy barks.*

Symbolically, that statement can be represented as:

$p \lor q$

Or, in postfix notation:

$p q$ OR

The use of disjunction is not exclusive, as both statements may be true. OR means one or the other, or both.
