# Predicting 30-Day Reintervention RISK after Endoscopic Sleeve Gastroplasty using Machine Learning

# Methods and Cohort

**Dataset**: 3,583 patients from the MBSAQIP database (2016-2021)

**Method**: LASSO penalized regression for predictor selection, multivariate analysis for ESG-RS generation

![](bbox=[0.143, 0.545, 0.256, 0.706])

# Results

**11 variables selected, 5 key factors influencing reintervention risk:

+   - History of foregut surgery
- Preoperative AC use
- Female gender
- ASA score >= 2
- Hypertension

**Model performance**: AUC = 0.74 (95% CI: 0.70, 0.78)

**Optimal cut point**: 67.9 (high risk vs. low risk); Sensitivity: 0.76, Specificity: 0.71

# Conclusion

The ESG-RS aids clinicians in preoperative risk stratification and helps identify patients at a higher risk of reintervention, enabling better decision-making and patient management.
