# Ethical statement:

The present study did not require institutional review board oversight because the Metabolic and Bariat Surgery Accreditation Quality Improvement Program (MBSAQIP) database is identified, and freely accessible to MBSAQIP participant centers. It does not identify hospitals, health care providers, or patients.

Patient Consent Statement: The MBSAQIP database lacks specific patient identifiers. Hence, patient consent was not required.
