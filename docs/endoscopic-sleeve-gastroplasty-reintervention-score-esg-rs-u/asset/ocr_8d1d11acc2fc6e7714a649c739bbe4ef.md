# Results

A total of 3583 patients were included in the present analysis. The demographic and clinical
characteristics are shown in Table 1. The 30-day endoscopic reintervention rate for ESG cases was 3.3%,
even out of the 26 predictors were retained by LASSO regression for predicting 30-day reintervention,
with a mean lambda of 0.003, including eleven variables were selected by LASSO, including the history
of foregut surgery, preoperative anticoagulation (AC) use, female gender, long term steroid use, history of
percutaneous coronary intervention, ASA score >=2,hypertension, preoperative GERD diagnosis, history
de deep venous thrombosis, preoperative albumin, and hematocrit (table 2)

After multivariable logistic regression, five variables were statistically significant, including the history
of foregut surgery (OR 4.98, 95% CI 3.08–8.06, P < 0.001), preoperative AC use (OR 2.22, 95% CI
1.10–4.51, P = 0.02), female gender (OR 0.44; 95% CI 0.29–0.68; P = 0.01), ASA score >=2(OR 1.62,
95% CI 1.03–2.52, P = 0.03), and hypertension (OR 1.63, 95% CI 1.11–2.40, P = 0.01)(Supplementary
file 2).
