# Abbreviations

**ESG:** Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty

**ESGRS:** Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty reintervention scoring system

**MBSAQIP:** Metabolic and Bariatric Surgery Accreditation Quality Improvement Program

**LASSO:** Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator

**IVC:** Inferior vena cava

**DVT:** Deep vein thrombosis

**AUC:** A rea under the curve

**AEs:** Adverse events

**BMI:** Body mass index

**BC:** Bias-corrected

**SE:** Standard error

**LSG:** Laparoscopic gastrectomy

**SG:** Sleeve gastrectomy

**ASA:** American Society of Anesthesiologists Classification

**GERD:** Gastroesophageal reflux disease

**PCI:** Percutaneous intervention

**IQR:** Interquartile range

**MI:** Myocardial infarction
