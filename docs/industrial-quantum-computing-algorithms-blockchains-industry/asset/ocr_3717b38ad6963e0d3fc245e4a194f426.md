# 6.6.5 Health logistics optimization

The management of health systems from a logistical point of view is extremely complex, with countless interdependencies between the entities involved, its optimization being a continuous challenge and substantial resource-consuming.

Quantum computers could help manage and optimize the logistics systems of health systems in terms of transport routes for medicines and equipment, as well as in programming and allocating the necessary resources updated in real time, which translates into reduced expenses.

By developing dedicated algorithms, stocks of medicines in healthcare facilities can be managed, or simulations can be carried out to anticipate the level of future demand, as to avoid shortages or surpluses of medicines.

Among the high costs in medical logistics are those related to transportation, and quantum computers could plan medical transport routes, correlated with other factors such as response time, traffic restrictions, or resource availability. Given the high computing power and the possibility of analyzing multiple variables simultaneously, quantum computers could adapt and update in real time the data needed to man-age.

By optimizing appointments or medical procedures, depending on the availability of medical staff, treatments, and equipment, waiting times for patients and the opt-imal of resources can be reduced.

The data collected in hospitals can be analyzed in correlation with the resources used, the benefits obtained and detailed reports can help develop future strategies to increase resource efficiency and optimize health spending.
