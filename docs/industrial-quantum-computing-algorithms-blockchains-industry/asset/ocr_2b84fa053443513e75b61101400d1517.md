**c) Quantum support vector machines (QSVMs):** These are support vector machines
(SVMs) that identify the best hyperplane for grouping data points into discrete categories
using quantum techniques [9]. QSVMs enable quantum computers to solve complex prob-
lems than traditional computers by executing several operations concurrently
using quantum speedup and quantum parallelism. The goal of QML is to use machine
learning algorithms on quantum computers, leveraging concepts like entanglement and
superposition to solve issues more quickly. SVM is a machine learning methodology that
performs well in classification, even if the SVM kernel method may occasionally be slug-
ish and error. Using proper quantum feature mapping, this technique seeks
to compare the speed and accuracy of QSVMs with those of classical SVM classification [14].
