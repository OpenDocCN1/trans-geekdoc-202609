# 11.5.7 Quantum convolutional neural networks

It is a kind of QNN that one can observe in some convolutional neural networks (CNN), which is a highly successful model in traditional ML. As discussed, QNNs, the qubits and the entanglement principle of quantum mechanics are applied in order to improve and increase the training speed of classical networks as well as solve computational difficulties. Utilizing the pattern recognition ability of CNN, it performs additional tasks like image recognition and natural language processing.

# 11.5.8 Quantumvolitional neural networks

Quantumvolitional neural networks is a term that is used when we explore the intersection of quantum computing and ML, specifically for the task of image recognition. It is a combination of "quantum" and "convolutional neural networks". It aims to use the unique properties of quantum mechanics to enhance the capabilities of traditional CNNs for image processing.

This helps in faster training times for complex image recognition tasks compared to classical CNNs, and the use of superposition entanglement in QNNs might lead to more efficient and rich feature extraction from images, potentially improving the recognition accuracies.

# 11.5.9 Quantum recurrent neural networks

This is a dynamic branch of research where quantum principles are finding their way through recurrent neural networks, which are an essential part of classical ML.

**Recurrent neural networks (RNNs)** are an influential class of neural networks that can tackle any input data in sequence, including text, speech, and time series data. They use previous input information processing by building a kind of logical model, which they use to discern patterns and make informed decisions in every specific situation. QRNNs try to take advantage of quantum generalized communication to outperform classical RNNs.
