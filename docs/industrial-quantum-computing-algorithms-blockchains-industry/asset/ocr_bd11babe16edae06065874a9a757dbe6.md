# 12.5.2 Operational disruptions and system vulnerabilities

# 12.5.2.1 Denial-of-service (DoS) attacks

Uninterrupted digital systems are essential for care and management in health organizations. DoS attacks can degrade the availability of critical systems, leading to operational outages that pose safety risks.

# 12.5.2.2 Exploiting weaknesses in connected medical devices

Different vulnerabilities would arise because of the continual introduction of new IoT devices and medical equipment connected to the market. Medical devices may give rise to malfunctions or manipulation, creating risks for patient health and safety concerning cyber-attacks.

# 12.5.3 Regulatory compliance and data protection

# 12.5.3.1 HIPAA compliance challenges

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) places numerous restrictions on the confidentiality of patient data. In most cases, compliance with these standards is a never-ending process, even in the presence of changing cyberthreats for healthcare providers.

# 12.5.3.2 International data protection standards

The demand to meet several foreign data protection standards is a challenge with the globalization of health systems. Different regulatory regimes and compliance with the various privacy standards require proper reflection.
