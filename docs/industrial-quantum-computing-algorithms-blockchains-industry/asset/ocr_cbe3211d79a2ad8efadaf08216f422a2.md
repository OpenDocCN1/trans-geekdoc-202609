In this chapter, it is shown how quantum advancement and automation technologies may take an impact on the business processes and how automation and quantum would define both threats and chances for the business. Upon closing the gap between the manufacturing companies and the knowledge of the quantum-amplified automation technology, we strive to make a concise presentation and provide a discussion on basic principles, the quantum model construction, the practical applications, and a real-life example that displays the enormous potential of the automation performance enhancement. As the process of our exploration and discovery invites, so does the collaboration between business automation and quantum computing that revolutionizes manufacturing in new ways. An intellectual journey becomes known as technology and artificial intelligence (AI) integrate, and new possibilities arise. The next part of the chapter entails the evolution of industrial automation, the basics of quantum computing, the integration of quantum technology in industrial environments, challenges, and solutions based on the scientific evidence available, we suggest suggestions, future directions, and vision. Through this, we try to provide information on the difficulties as well as opportunities that the technology brings, affecting the business. Industrial automation is the primary element of the modern manufacturing, which is an indelible flow of efficiency, accuracy, and progress. This technology segment has made a lot of progress from developing CNC machines to robotics and smart control systems. Nevertheless, the economics of quantum computing along with the classical computing approach is still a new field that is developing and will deal with the problems of the classical model. The collocation of industrial automation and quantum computing is a milestone to find new solutions to achieve better productivity, spend less money, and maintain a higher quality. However, quantum computing will still overcome the computational power and resolution limits by using the laws of quantum mechanics on the opposite end. As mentioned by Smith [1], industrial automation changes the shape of the production process this way, making it more efficient and precise. Automation technology in the production process has a vital role in the design of assembly lines, supply chains, and quality processes for different sectors. Thus, Smith says that automation not only adds to the intensity of competition among markets but also adds innovation. When the quantum computing phenomenon is contemplated, it stands at the intersection of physics, mathematics, and computer science, and is expected to show under a dramatic change in computational implementation [2]. Just as a classical computer includes a binary object to compute information, quantum computers process information using quantum bits or qubits. The superposition principle and entanglement underlie the power of qubits to execute exponentially more computations needed to solve issues which in turn cannot be solved with the traditional algorithms. The combination of quantum computing and automation systems leads to the possibility, for the first time, to have the entire system with the overall efficiency, intelligence, and flexibility.
