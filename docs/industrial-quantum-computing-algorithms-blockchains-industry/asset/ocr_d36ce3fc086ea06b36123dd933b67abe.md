Quantum computers can analyze genomic, medical, and environmental data to create tailored treatment plans. This can greatly increase medical treatment accuracy and efficiency, improving patient health. Quantum computing could improve healthcare logistics and operations [19].

Quantum algorithms can optimize hospital resource allocation, scheduling, and supply chain management by analyzing amounts of data. This can improve operations, times, and healthcare. Quantum computing can also transform medical imaging and diagnosis. Scientists use quantum algorithms to improve MRI and CT scan precision and clarity. This can help the doctors spot diseases and anomalies early, improving patient outcomes. Quantum computing can also improve genomics and personalized genomes. Quantum computers can interpret massive genetic data, helping researchers grasp the complex human genome [24].

This can help understand genetic diseases, develop precise drugs, and create personalized treatments. Quantum computing offers many intriguing healthcare applications. As the field continues to evolve, it is expected that quantum computing will catalyze groundbreaking discoveries and innovations, leading to improved patient care, better outcomes, and a healthier population.
