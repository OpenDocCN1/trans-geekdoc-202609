# 15.4.1 Effects on materials science and drug discovery

Quantum algorithms can predict chemical characteristics and model molecular interactions to speed up drug discovery. These methods let materials create new materials with specific properties, advancing electronics, catalysis, and energy storage. Classical methods are still important in drug development and materials research, although they may struggle to predict complex molecular features. # 15.4.2 Analysing chemical data with quantum machine learning

Quantum machine learning models use quantum states to represent and process chemical data. These models can depict intricate quantum correlations and entanglement, enabling more precise chemical system descriptions. Classical Machine Learning (CML) models analyze chemical data using classical methods. They can analyze enormous datasets, however they may miss quantum events in chemical systems.
