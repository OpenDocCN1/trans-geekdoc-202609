# Chapter 13 Introduction to quantum computing and society

**Abstract:** Quantum computing is nowadays a very popular topic in terms of technology, enabling individuals to solve various types of computational problems. Quantum computing is a multidisciplinary area consisting of Information Technology, mathematics, and physics that use quantum theory/mechanism. Classical computer systems use binary number (0 or 1), whereas quantum computers use quantum bits that represent both 0 and 1 together. Quantum computing is used for optimization problems. Quantum computing improves machine learning by training them quickly. Quantum computing has an impact on sectors of healthcare, etc.

**Keywords:** Quantum computing, quantum theory/mechanism, quantum bits, AI models, applications.

## 13.1 Introduction to quantum computing

Our world is inherently governed by quantum mechanics. Technologies built on the foundation of quantum mechanics have led to faster algorithms, new cryptography, and ways to communicate. The area of quantum information processing, which includes quantum computers, classical communications, wireless, and games, seeks to use quantum mechanical methods to obtain information and its applications. Physical processes change, rather than changes in the perception of information and actual computation. In quantum computing, we use quantum effects to make faster, more efficient, or even possible, which would be impossible with a conventional computer. Quantum computation is a combination of quantum mechanics, information theory, and computer science. This field offers secure data transfer as an increase in computing speed over classical computers.

Quantum computation enables several problems to be solved efficiently; some problems that would take longer than the age of the universe for a standard computer can be solved or completed in two days by a quantum computer. Unlike traditional computing, which is deterministic, quantum computing explores the probabilistic and unpredictable nature of the physical world. This developing field has great potential for solving tough issues and advancing various industries, from cryptography to drug discovery and more.

Despite being immensely useful, quantum computing is still at an initial stage, and building quantum computers remains a crucial challenge. Quantum computers are delicately dependent on their surroundings and can easily make mistakes due to interference and other issues. Consequently, quantum computers need specialized equipments to function, and their effectiveness is constrained by the number of qubits that can be reliably managed.

The modern world runs quickly and depends more on automation and information technology. The internet equivalents of manual operations have mostly replaced them: emails have replaced letters, photos have been replaced by JPEG files, and papers have been replaced with Word documents. Technology is vital to industry, and innovations are occurring at an exponential rate. One of these innovations is quantum computing, which has the potential to alter information processing.

In brief, quantum computing is a current era that could completely revolutionize how we deal with information. Quantum computers can resolve complex problems more quickly and accurately by applying the principles of quantum physics to perform computations that are not feasible for classical computers. For a detailed account of quantum mechanics in this article, its main ideas and applications are also discussed. Quantum mechanics is a type of computing that manipulates information using entanglement and superposition, examples of quantum mechanical phenomena. This depends on the principles of quantum mechanics, which define the behavior of energy and matter at small scales like atoms and particles. In conventional computing, information is processed in bits, which are denoted by either 0 or 1 at the same time, known as superposition. This unique characteristic enables quantum mechanics to execute some calculations more efficiently than standard computers.
