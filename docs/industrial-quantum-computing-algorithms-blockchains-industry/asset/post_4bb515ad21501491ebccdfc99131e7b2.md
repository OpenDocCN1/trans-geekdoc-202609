## 10.4.3 Use of AI in education

AI has had a significant impact on schooling. AI tools, such as digital instructors and interactive systems, have significantly impacted educational opportunities. AI improved education by making it easy for students to learn and stay engaged; reducing reliance on rote learning and increasing educators' ability to personalize curricula; augmenting matter access; enabling more efficient assessments and diagnostics; and assisting in the scaling of education initiatives [79]. The emergence of digital tutors is one way in AI has been able to alter education. Digital tutors, like human tutors, can provide students with personalized training and feedback. Nevertheless, they are able to handle information at a quicker and more effective rate, enabling them to adapt and change education to the pace and level of students. This enhances student involvement by enabling them to progress through the course material at their own speed. Wellhammer and Dolata [80] suggest that digital tutors could integrate multimedia and digital education to enhance students' involvement with the subject material. AI has also enhanced education by providing students with personalized guidance. Platforms like Knewton use AI algorithms to customize students' learning experiences based on their individual needs. This guarantees that students get individualized instruction that suits their unique learning preferences. Moreover, AI can analyze student data to identify areas of learning deficiency and customize education accurately. This helps improve the learning experience for students with different levels and skills [81]. AI has also improved access to academic material, enabling students to access educational resources regardless of where they are or what they have.

AI-powered systems provide users with access to current libraries and pertinent information. This has been particularly advantageous for individuals residing in areas with limited access to educational resources. Moreover, AI has increased the efficiency of assessment and diagnosis. AI-powered assessment and course materials have the ability to quickly evaluate student progress and offer feedback on areas needing improvement. This enables quick detection of areas needing additional education or resources, avoiding wasting resources on topics mastered by students. Although AI has greatly benefited education, it has also been linked to a rise in poverty and financial inequality within the education sector. This is because AI technology is becoming more prevalent, limiting opportunities for low-income students and individuals with limited resources. AI-driven tools come with a high price tag, posing a barrier to quality education for a large number of underprivileged students. Additionally, AI has been linked to a decrease in available teaching positions due to automation, leading to worries about a rise in job loss for teachers. These examples of poverty and inequality serve as a clear warning that AI technology to improve educational chances, should not result in increased marginalization [31, 82]. Poverty is frequently linked to a family's limited opportunity to receive a formal education. According to the World Bank, over 39% of the world's poorest people do not have access to education. Two factors restrict households' ability to access education: the cost of education and the quality of higher education institutions. Multiple organizations have a limit on the number of individuals they can host. AI could improve the academic performance of underprivileged kids by personalized learning strategies to boost student engagement and crafting individualized education plans for each student [83].

## 10.4.4 Women’s empowerment and income inequality

Empowering women has the potential to effectively decrease income inequality. Enhancing women's access to education and training, healthcare, financial resources, and legal standing are all measures that can be implemented to empower them and increase their economic opportunities and influence. Gender discrimination often limits women's ability to access economic resources, leading to an imbalance in the economy [84]. Enhancing women's financial independence can increase their earning capacity and household earnings, resulting in a decrease in income inequality. Women are more inclined to use their income to support their family and community, thus potentially contributing to economic growth in these regions [85]. In order to decrease income and access to resources, governments should offer women the same economic opportunities, including credit and financial tools. Implementing laws and policies that address discrimination based on gender can play a significant role in promoting equal economic opportunities for everyone. To increase inequality, it is necessary to take additional steps along improving women's access to economic means [86]. Governments can decrease income inequality by encouraging the fair distribution of resources and wealth through progressive taxes and welfare programs. Investing in education and enhancing skills can increase economic mobility and create new opportunities for women [52]. Moreover, efforts at generating employment opportunities and increasing wages for women can help reduce the wealth gap. Ultimately, the involvement of women in leadership roles may aid in lessening economic inequality. This also helps decrease employment discrimination and increase opportunities for women to engage in economic activities. By implementing these measures, the gap in income can be reduced and a fairer society can be created for all individuals [87].

## 10.5 Conclusion and recommendations

This paper assessed how AI and automation affect income inequality in BRICS. It was discovered that BRICS nations, AI and automation have resulted in enhanced efficiency and cost savings, but also in heightened income inequality by replacing low-wage and low-skill positions. This resulted in reductions in wages and salaries in the manufacturing and service sectors. AI and automation in the retail sector in India have resulted in the removal of low-paying jobs, leading to a rise in income inequality. In Brazil, automation improved agricultural productivity while displacing paid workers and widening wealth gaps. From 1992 to 2006, automation in Russia was also linked to a rise in income inequality. AI and automation have increased efficiency in the financial industry, but this has resulted in a widening income gap in South Africa by displacing jobs that require lower skills and pay. Also, AI systems support rural communities in multiple ways, such as identifying crop diseases and pests, providing personalized teaching, and evaluating and forecasting crop yields. Ultimately, although AI has enhanced education by promoting greater engagement, learning access and evaluations, it has also exacerbated poverty and financial inequalities within the educational sphere. Ultimately, AI and automation could lessen income inequality within the BRICS nations; however, numerous consequences must be taken into account. Although AI and automation are generally beneficial for BRICS countries, they can also bring about negative consequences like job loss, lower wages, and widening economic inequality. When utilizing AI, it is important to take into account poverty and economic inequality, which can exacerbate the marginalization of vulnerable communities. The utilization of AI and automation should focus on enhancing productivity and efficiency while also considering the impact on economic inequality within the BRICS nations. In conclusion, it is clear that AI and automation have played a major role in shaping the BRICS communities.

AI technology has been utilized in various sectors such as manufacturing, service industries, banking, retail, agriculture, and financial services. While AI and automation have improved efficiency and saved costs, they have also been linked to a rise in income inequality and poverty. Automation has led to the replacement of jobs that require little skill and pay low wages, which has led to a rise in economic inequality, while AI has surpassed human decision-making. Moreover, AI has been employed to address poverty and food insecurity, and to offer educational opportunities to disadvantaged individuals. Enhancing women's economic empowerment could potentially raise a woman's income and earnings, ultimately reducing income inequality. Additionally, women tend to reinvest their earnings back into their families and communities, contributing to greater economic growth in these regions. Governments can also offer women equal economic opportunities and access to resources like credit, resulting in higher economic development in those regions.
