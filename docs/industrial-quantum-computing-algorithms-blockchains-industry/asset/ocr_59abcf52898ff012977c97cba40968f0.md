As of 2014, hospitals produced more than 50 petabytes of data per year, including medical records, laboratory tests, medical images, sensor data records, genomics, operational and financial data, for health systems' management bodies. However, according to studies, 97% of this information is unused or summarily analyzed, being mainly stored on different systems [10].

Such a large volume of data is impossible for humans to analyze and process in an efficient way and with positive results. Therefore, dedicated software solutions have been developed, which can replace human work and automate analysis and processes by providing centralized results.
