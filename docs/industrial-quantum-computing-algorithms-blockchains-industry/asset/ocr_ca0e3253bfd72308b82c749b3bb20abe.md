# 1.3.1 Healthcare

Quantum computing has the potential to revolutionize healthcare by accelerating the development of vaccinations and drugs, facilitating early patient diagnoses, and customizing treatment plans. As shown with the coronavirus vaccine, existing methods can only expedite the lengthy medication development process to a certain extent [24]. Researchers have to build new molecules to see how they interact with one another, which adds time to the process. However, with the help of quantum computers, they can create virtual molecules to test their interactions. With quantum computers, researchers can accurately model even the smallest molecules.
