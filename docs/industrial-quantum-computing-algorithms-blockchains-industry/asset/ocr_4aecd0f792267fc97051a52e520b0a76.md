# 13.1.1 Algorithms

Certainly, Shor's algorithm and Grover's algorithm are among the major quantum algorithms. One way to think of the Shor algorithm is as a quantum method for integer factoring, which is widely regarded as one of the most amazing developments in quantum research. Mathematician Peter Shor's 1994 discovery of the Shor algorithm, as shown in Figure 13.3, is one of the most remarkable instances of how quantum systems might surpass traditional mechanics in some fields.

To factorize a big number, Shor's method makes use of qubits' ability to exist in superpositions, or many states at once, and entanglement, or the ability to be impacted by another qubit regardless of how far away they are. Because ATAN can factor big numbers into their prime factors, it can circumvent public key cryptystems that are widely used, such as RSA encryption. It creates a common ground for numerous encryption systems by effectively factoring huge integers.
