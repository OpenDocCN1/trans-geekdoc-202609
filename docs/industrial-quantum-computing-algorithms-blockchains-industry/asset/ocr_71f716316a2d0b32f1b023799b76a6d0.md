# 13.8 Quantum computing revolutionizes industry and society

The combination of quantum mechanics into various industries is set to have a profound influence on how we work, live, and engage with digital media. Quantum computing's impacts are huge and far-reaching – helping scientists develop complex AI formulas toha public health services with tailor-made medical solutions [17].

Drug discovery, materials science, finance, and logistics are some of the fields that quantum computing will revolutionize in the industry sector [8]. It leads to faster analysis of molecular structures and subsequently improved designs for new drugs and materials that have desired traits. The power that comes with quantum computing enables us to make better choices when it comes to investment while simultaneously mitigating negative outcomes. Quantum computing can also be used by supply chain and logistics management of companies to speed up processes, reduce-costs, and increase productivity.

Quantum computing will greatly benefit society, particularly in the areas of cybersecurity, climate modeling, and AI [10]. Quantum cryptography provides a high level of security that protects data and prevents its unlawful access by strangers. With such an application, participants in climate studies can build quantum models that have less uncertainty, hence enabling them to have a better understanding and subsequently combat environmental problems. Quantum computing also increases the capabilities of AI algorithms, resulting in breakthroughs in machine learning and pattern recognition.
