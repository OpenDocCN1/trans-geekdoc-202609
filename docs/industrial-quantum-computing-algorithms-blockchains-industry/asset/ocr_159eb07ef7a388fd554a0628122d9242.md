Introduction to quantum computing and its revolution in industry and society

**Abstract:** Quantum computing is nowadays a very popular topic in terms of technology, enabling individuals to solve various types of computational problems. Quantum computing is a multidisciplinary area consisting of Information Technology, mathematics, and physics that use quantum theory/mechanism. Classical computer systems use the binary number system (0 or 1), whereas quantum computers use quantum bits that represent both 0 and 1 together. Quantum computing is used in solving optimization problems. Quantum computing improves machine learning algorithms by efficiently analyzing complex data. Quantum computers can improve AI by training them quickly. Quantum computing has an impact on the sectors of healthcare, security, etc., and each has its own applications.

**Keywords:** Quantum computing, quantum theory/mechanism, quantum bits (Q-bits), AI models, algorithms, applications

# ## 13.1 Introduction to quantum computing

Our world is inherently governed by quantum mechanics. Technologies built on the foundation of quantum mechanics have led to faster algorithms, new cryptography, and ways to communicate. The area of quantum information processing, which includes quantum computers, cryptography, classical communications, wireless, and games, seeks to use quantum mechanical methods to obtain information and its applications. Physical processes change, rather than changes in the perception of information and actual computation. In quantum computing, we use quantum effects to make faster, more efficient, or even possible, which would be impossible with a conventional computer. Quantum computation is a combination of quantum mechanics, mathematics, information theory, and computer science. This field offers secure data transfer as an increase in computing speed over classical computers
