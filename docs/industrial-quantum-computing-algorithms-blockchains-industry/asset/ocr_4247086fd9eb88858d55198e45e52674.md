# 6.6.7 Quantum encryption for health data security

Digitalization in healthcare has multiple advantages related to access to information, reduced costs, analyses, reports, and statistics, but this comes with increased risks regarding IT security and the protection of collected and stored data.

The security of medical data is an extremely important issue, given the sensitive nature of stored data, and it is vital that it is protected and its access is very well regulated and managed.

Current trends in data use are to be accessible via computer networks and mobile devices, which means high security risks and pressure on IT security systems. It is obvious that things get complicated when staff who have access to health data personal mobile devices, do not have the necessary level of protection and to third parties may have access, and data transmissions can be intercepted, or devices lost or stolen.

In addition to limited and well-regulated access to medical data, data encryption measures are needed, when transmitting it over secure communication networks like VPN tunneling.

The management of all these aspects is very, even sometimes, for IT staff, especially when the rules are not followed exactly, and it leads to situations where medical information is transmitted by email, messaging, or free communication applications which they are exposed to an extremely high level of data protection risk.

In health systems, quantum computers could provide a superior level of protection, through quantum encryption that is considered impossible to penetrate with conventional algorithms.
