## 12.4.1.2 Smart medical devices

IoT integration in medical devices such as glucose monitors, blood pressure cuffs, and insulin pumps allow for automatic data collection and transmission to medical care specialists. Smart inhalers and medication dispensers aid in monitoring, which can reinforce the development of medication adherence.

## 12.4.1.3 Asset tracking and management

IoT allows the monitoring of medical equipment devices and is available when needed to streamline inventory management. Using RTLS helps find medical assets by reducing the time to search for critical equipment.

## 12.4.1.4 Enhanced operational efficiency

IoT technologies do to advance hospital operations because they monitor and control resource utilization. A principal advantage of automated frameworks lies in controlling temperature, lighting, and other environmental factors to produce optimal conditions for providing patients with care and comfort.

## 12.4.1.5 Data analytics and predictive maintenance

Significant experiences for good decision-making can be extracted from data generated by the IoT. Predictive medical equipment maintenance prevents time losses by detecting problems before they become devastating.

## 12.4.1.6 Telemedicine and remote consultations

IoT is indispensable to telemedicine since it allows remote consultations through connected devices. Healthcare services delivered over the internet can inform from IoT devices, offering a comprehensive view of one’s health status during telemedicine sessions. IoT in healthcare can improve patient outcomes, cut costs, and redefine conventional healthcare delivery models.

## 12.4.2 Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML)

AI and ML are bringing positive changes to the healthcare industry by providing novel approaches to problems. These technologies can potentially enhance patient outcomes, improve diagnoses, simplify administrative procedures, and contribute to medical research. Following is a list of important uses of AI and ML in the field:

### 12.4.2.1 Screening and diagnostic procedures

Risk stratification is made feasible in healthcare using AI and ML models that analyze patient data. This makes it possible to implement preventive treatments and care plans. At the same time, early indicators of diseases that can be identified by predictive analytics make healthcare simpler before situations become severe.

### 12.4.2.2 Probabilistic analytics and risk evaluation

In the healthcare industry, ML and AI models analyze patient data to identify individuals according to their susceptibility. In addition to facilitating proactive mediation and individualized care plans, predictive analytics enable medical service providers to detect diseases early and intervene.

### 12.4.2.3 Drug discovery

AI accelerates the process of drug discovery by analyzing biological data to identify targets. Simultaneously, ML models predict the appropriateness of drugs, potentially accelerating the development of remedies for different illnesses.

### 12.4.2.4 Personalized medicine

In medicine, AI advances treatment by processing patient information, which may include genetics, to build individualized plans based on data. This helps to enhance the accuracy of treatment. In genomic medicine, ML algorithms are utilized to analyze genetic data and detect disease-associated markers and make recommendations for unique treatment processes based on the outcomes.

### 12.4.2.5 Clinical decision support systems

AI in medicine facilitates the progress of treatments by analyzing patient data, including genetics, to develop personalized strategies. This contributes to improving the precision of treatment. ML algorithms are employed in genomic medicine to assess data, identify markers linked to diseases, and provide recommendations for tailored treatment approaches based on the analyses.

### 12.4.2.6 Natural language processing (NLP)

In medical processes, natural language processing (NLP) adds value to clinical documentation by gaining insights from unstructured notes, improving the accuracy of electronic health record (EHR) systems. Additionally, chatbots empower patients by answering questions, providing healthcare knowledge, and enabling communication between patients and doctors.

### 12.4.2.7 Fraud detection and healthcare management

In medical processes, ML algorithms manage claims, identify fraudulent claims, and ensure insurance processing accuracy. Simultaneously, AI is used to modernize hospital processes to provide efficient resource allocation and proper management of patient flow. Although AI and ML implementation in the health sector has various benefits, issues such as data privacy, ethical considerations and compliance must be considered.

## 12.4.3 Blockchain in healthcare

Healthcare services have been impacted by the development of blockchain technology, which is a solution because of its transparency, security, and efficiency associated with handling data. Here are key aspects of how blockchain is transforming healthcare:

### 12.4.3.1 Data security and integrity

Blockchain technology operates through a decentralized and distributed ledger that ensures the maintenance of health data, preventing anyone from tampering with or removing any information already recorded on it. Cryptographic security in blockchain involves measures to prevent unauthorized access and alteration, as each block has a unique identifier (hash) that links to the previous block, forming a chain of data that is both secure and transparent.

### 12.4.3.2 Interoperability and data sharing

Blockchain helps ensure harmonious and interoperable patient records, making such data available to all health providers in real time. Second, it enables patients, with a consent management feature, to have more control of their health information by giving only limited permissions for data access. It facilitates privacy protection and GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) compliance.

### 12.4.3.3 Supply chain management

Two uses of blockchain-based technology are so crucial that the entire system is shown to be indispensable for healthcare safety. In the first place, it offers drug traceability throughout the entirety of the pharma supply chain, which guarantees the genuineness of the drug and reduces the likelihood of counterfeit medicine. The second benefit is that it assists in tracking the movements of medical equipment and serves as an instrument for ensuring that materials that are either forged or substandard are not utilized.

### 12.4.3.4 Smart contracts in healthcare

In the realm of medicine, smart contracts that are powered by blockchain technology offer a double advantage. They contribute to the automation of claim processing, billing, and insurance payouts, eliminating the demand for mediators and reducing the likelihood of errors occurring because of their presence. Regulatory compliance can be achieved seamlessly through these contracts because they can be designed to take action automatically following the fulfillment of predetermined requirements.

### 12.4.3.5 Clinical trials and research

The integration of blockchain technology has revolutionized clinical research, yielding a twofold impact. Initially, it enhances the transparency and accessibility of clinical trial data by establishing a secure and transparent platform for collecting and sharing trial data. This platform is safeguarded from unauthorized modifications, maintaining the reliability and precision of research findings. Furthermore, it establishes a secure and verifiable framework that incentivizes users to contribute their sample data for research objectives, fostering increased collaboration among researchers.

### 12.4.3.6 Credentialing and identity management

Blockchains provide dual advantages in the management of healthcare. Firstly, they ensure secure individual identification by regulating the credentials of healthcare professionals and restricting access to authorized personnel for important information about patients. Furthermore, the technology enhances the efficiency of administrative duties, particularly in verifying credentials, resulting in time and resource savings for these patients.

### 12.4.3.7 Decentralized healthcare apps (DCH-Apps)

Blockchain focuses on the patient by using decentralized apps that let patients see their health information and have a say in making decisions. This technology also introduces the idea of data monetization, which lets people exchange their knowledge with study participants and healthcare providers while owning the data. This opens the door to new ways of making money off data.
