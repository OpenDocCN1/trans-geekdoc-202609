# 11.5.2 Quantum optimization problems

The problem of optimizing computation using qubits is a computing task that seeks to find the most perfect solution for a given problem using algorithms from the quantum information framework. In complex situations, there is a need for optimization of at least a couple of objective functions while operating within the given constraints. To deal with problems more effectively than classical optimization problems, quantum optimization problems utilize unique properties of quantum computing, as superposition, entanglement, and particle parallelism. Even though the fact that quantum computers solve optimization problems is promising, they are still in the first stage of development, and researchers continue to spend a lot of effort to fully implement their capacity to optimize practical applications.

# 11.5.3 Quantum approximate optimization algorithm(QAOA)

QAOA (quantum approximate optimization algorithm) is an approach that searches for the solution to a combinatorial optimization problem. This algorithm is based on PQCs, which adapts the adiabatic evolution from an initial Hamiltonian.

The QAOA is a promising approach to achieving a quantum advantage in the NISQ era (noisy intermediate-scale quantum) by iteratively adjusting parameters to optimize a desired outcome derived from the quantum circuit's output.
