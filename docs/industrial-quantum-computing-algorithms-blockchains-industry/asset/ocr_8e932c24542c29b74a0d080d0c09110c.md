The progression of healthcare is slow and methodical, largely due to the inherent complexities of biological systems and ethical constraints. Many of these constraints are not technical, and many regulations still haunt the healthcare industry long after their technical justification has ceased. There are also difficulties in organizations being able to collect different data types and use it for optimizations. Even if all privacy concerns are eliminated, and security can guarantee that data cannot be tampered with, it is not clear that corporate interests will align with individual interests for optimizing healthcare for individuals [38].
