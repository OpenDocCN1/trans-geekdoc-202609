- Hardware limitations: Hardware constraints are also an issue for quantum computers. The sophisticated gear required for quantum computing has not yet reached its full potential. Therefore, qubit connection, gate integrity, and coherence time limit quantum computer performance. Hardware upgrades are required to surpass these restrictions and make more powerful quantum computers possible.

- Algorithm development: Furthermore, the creation of quantum algorithms is in high demand. Although several quantum algorithms have been created, further algorithms that may use the distinct features of quantum computing are still required. Doing so calls for an in-depth familiarity with quantum physics and the skill to convert this knowledge into workable algorithms [30–34].

- Software development: Finally, specific programming languages, tools, and software development are needed for quantum computing. The programming paradigms used in conventional and quantum computing are drastically different, making to develop appropriate tools and languages difficult.

These obstacles are substantial, but the potential rewards of quantum computing are enormous; therefore, several academics and businesses are slaying away to solve them. Research and development efforts using this technology are expanding at an exponential rate. The race to construct the most robust and effective quantum computer is heating up, with major tech companies like IBM, Google, and Microsoft pouring millions into the field. Experts will have more time to develop this technology safely and intelligently to avoid hazards and use it for the good of our planet, even if it will take time to enhance the capabilities of these quantum machines.

## 1.6.2 Quantum computing limitations

From a technical and social point of view, quantum computing has drawbacks [24–27]. The three most important of them are:

- Environmental sensitivity and quantum error correction pose significant obstacles.

- National security is a major issue with post-quantum encryption.

- AI driven by quantum mechanics may have unexpected results.

## 1.7 Conclusion

This chapter primarily delves into the core ideas of quantum computing, significant achievements, and the many applications that might revolutionize civilization. Quantum computing has both positive and negative effects on society, we have covered in our discussion of its effects. Thanks to the ever-expanding realm of quantum computing, new hardware, algorithms, and applications are on the horizon. These developments will transform our perception of the world, spur innovation in many sectors, and make previously impossible tasks seem simpler.

As soon as quantum computing comes, technology will reach its peak. While we continue looking into this cutting-edge technology, we must think about how it will change things, help us solve problems, and ensure everyone will benefit. Due to the potential enormous influence that quantum computing may have on our current way of life, the development of this technology has proceed with utmost caution and an emphasis on ethical concerns. Given that quantum computing has the potential to bypass existing encryption methods, privacy and security of information are key concerns.

As quantum computing becomes more prevalent, it will be necessary for people, businesses, and governments to take steps to protect sensitive information. An effective approach is to create and execute strong security policies and secure networks of communication that can withstand attacks from quantum computers. Quantum computing is a constantly being researched by various organizations, including businesses, educational institutions, and government agencies. Understanding how to effectively communicate with a wide variety of people is necessary to facilitate the advancement and acceptance of quantum innovation. To define rules for responsible advancement and discovery, it is necessary to thoroughly consider the ethical, social, and economic ramifications associated with the progression of technology.
