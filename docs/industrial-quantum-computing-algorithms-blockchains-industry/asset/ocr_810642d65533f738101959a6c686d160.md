# 15.4.1.3 Impact on chemical data analysis

When it comes to systems with strong quantum effects, chemical data can be better and more thoroughly interpreted by Quantum Machine Learning (QML) models. They have the potential to shed light on molecule interactions and characteristics by revealing previously unseen patterns in chemical datasets. Data processing continues to make heavy use of CML models because of their adaptability. Discovery, materials science, and other fields are benefiting from CML approaches that are better evaluating complicated datasets towards ongoing research and improvement. It is possible that quantum-enhanced MD simulations might speed up the discovery of new materials, improve our understanding of biological processes, and allow us to build molecular systems with desired properties. This comprehensive study highlights the ground-breaking methods that are enhancing molecular dynamics and driving innovation in many technical and scientific fields through the use of quantum potential.
