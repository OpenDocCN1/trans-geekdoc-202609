# Nirdesh Jain and Sakshi Kathuria

# Chapter 7
Industrial automation and quantum computing

**Abstract:** Industrial automation, a cornerstone of modern manufacturing, is undergoing a transformative phase with the integration of quantum computing technologies. This paper explores the intersection of industrial automation and quantum computing, examining how quantum computing principles enhance traditional automation paradigms. Quantum computing's ability to process vast datasets and solve complex optimization problems enables unparalleled advancements in industrial control systems, predictive maintenance, and supply chain management.

**Keywords:** Industrial automation, Quantum computing, Manufacturing, Optimization, Control systems, Predictive maintenance, Supply chain management

# 7.1 Introduction

Industrial automation is a key component of modern manufacturing, which manifests in the relentless pursuit of efficiency, precision, and new practices. Over time, the field has progressed far beyond the use of numerical control (CNC) to advanced robotics and intelligent algorithms. The economics of quantum computing is currently an important part of our computational science and has the potential to alleviate the drawbacks of the classical computing paradigm by offering moderate gains in quality at a minimal investment investment. The fusion of industrial automation and quantum computing stands at the core of the future of manufacturing technology. Industrial automation is the search for ideals and the practice of optimizing business and technological processes to improve terms of service. The use of quantum computing in automation processes is the beginning of the era, where a new process variable with new values is created that are hyper-efficient, intelligent, and flexible.
