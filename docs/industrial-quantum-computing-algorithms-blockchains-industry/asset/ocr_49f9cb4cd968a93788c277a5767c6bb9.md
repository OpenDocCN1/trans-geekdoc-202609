# Chapter 4
Highlighting major issues with quantum computing in healthcare

**Abstract:** The rapid advancement of quantum technology has spurred rivalry between academics and corporations to produce quantum computing technologies. Quantum computers and annealers can now manage several qubits, according to a recent research. Quantum computing is increasing rapidly and attracting interest. Many are researching and writing about it. Quantum computing does things normal computers cannot. We do not know how it will influence healthcare. This chapter is a debut to quantum computing and healthcare applications. Quantum computing can enhance medical imaging, pharmaceutical safety, and personalization. It can improve patient experience and healthcare system efficiency. This chapter explores these subjects and explains quantum computing in healthcare. We wish to inspire more researchers to study quantum computing, comprehend the present status of research, discover possibilities, and overcome hurdles to construct improved healthcare.

**Keywords:** Quantum communications, quantum computing, quantum network, quantum computing, high-performance computing, quantum machine learning, health care
