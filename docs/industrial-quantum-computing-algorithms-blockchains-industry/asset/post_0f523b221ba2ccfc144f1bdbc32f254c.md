
## 1.2.1 Quantum computing elements

The key elements of quantum computing are as follows (Figure 1.1):

![](img/003d10cd18c2ce02e1f86a5992a8292b_14_0.png)

**Figure 1.1:** Key elements of quantum computing.

- **Quantum bits** are the basic building blocks of quantum computing, often known as qubits. In contrast to traditional bits, qubits can only take on the value 0 or 1, qubits can hold onto both states simultaneously. Quantum computers may increase their processing capacity exponentially by processing huge volumes of data in parallel, thanks to their special characteristics [14].
- **Quantum superposition** allows qubits to exist in different states simultaneously. It is a principle of quantum physics. This effect gives quantum computers a significant advantage over conventional computers for solving problems, as they may perform computations on all conceivable combinations of qubits concurrently [15].
- **Quantum entanglement** is an crucial feature of quantum physics. It occurs when two physically distant qubits' states become interdependent. The instantaneous communication between qubits made by their interconnection considerably increases the power and efficiency of quantum computing [16].

## 1.2.2 Comparison with traditional computer

The key comparison is as follows:

- **Distinctions in computational capability:** Traditional computers use binary logic to function, where each bit represents a value of 0 or 1. Due to their sequential processing capability, these computers are slow and unable to handle complicated tasks. However, quantum computers use quantum mechanical principles to process data in parallel, dramatically improving computing capability. This opens the door for quantum computers to solve issues that conventional computers find impossible [16, 17].
- **Substantial advancements in quantum computing:** The advancement of quantum physics in the early twentieth century laid the groundwork for quantum computing. However, the area did not come together until Richard Feynman put the concept of a quantum computer forward in the 1980s. A lot happened since, moving the research forward and making quantum computing a viable possibility [18].
- **Notable accomplishments and discoveries:** The first quantum computer, IBM Q, was unveiled in 2016, showcasing IBM’s position as a leader in quantum research. IBM Q was made available to academics and companies to explore quantum computing and create new applications. The availability of quantum computing was greatly enhanced by this [19].
- "Quantum supremacy" was proclaimed by Google in 2019 after its quantum computer, Sycamore, completed a computation that would take a traditional supercomputer thousands of years. This achievement shows that those quantum computers may potentially resolve issues that were previously believed to be intractable by Preskill [20].
- **Improvements in quality**, error correction, and scalability are only a few examples of the noteworthy strides in creating quantum computing hardware within the last decade. These developments have pushed us one step closer to constructing large-scale, operational quantum computers that can compete or surpass traditional computers in several tasks. ## 1.3 Quantum computing applications

All sectors will benefit from quantum computing ability to computations and inventions previously impossible with classical computers. There are several ways in which society may profit from quantum computers. For example, they could revolutionize transportation, speed up the development of medications and vaccinations, and help with investment choices. However, one of their drawbacks is that quantum computers may be able to crack existing encryption [22]. The following is a compilation of the opinions of industry professionals about the potential societal effects of quantum computing. Figure 1.2 presents the application of quantum computing [23].

![](img/003d10cd18c2ce02e1f86a5992a8292b_18_0.png)

Figure 1.2: Application of quantum computing.

### 1.3.1 Healthcare

Quantum computing has the potential to revolutionize healthcare by accelerating the development of vaccinations and drugs, facilitating early patient diagnoses, and customizing treatment plans. As shown with the coronavirus vaccine, existing methods can only expedite the lengthy development process to a certain extent [24]. With the help of quantum computers, researchers can build molecules to see how they interact. With quantum computers, researchers can accurately model even the smallest molecules.

### 1.3.2 Business and finance

Several studies have shown that, at least shortly, quantum computers will be most useful to the financial industry. The capacity to predict stock market outcomes that were previously unpredictable and numerous to compute is the primary advantage of quantum computing for the financial sector. Quantum computers will be able to perform more precise risk assessments under numerous probable situations, which is something that investors are increasingly seeking. Better lending judgments will also be possible by using quantum computers regarding loans and portfolios [25].

### 1.3.3 Climate prediction

It is also possible that quantum computers might forecast the weather. That "currently, a classical computer might take more time than it takes the weather to evolve to predict the weather" is based on an interview with an MIT professor that advised PBS. The transportation sector, the food production industry, and many others feel the effects of this enhanced climate forecasting. Directly or indirectly, weather impacts around 30% of GDP in the USA. Better weather prediction would also allow more time to be ready for calamities.

### 1.3.4 Travel and transportation

The transportation and travel industries gain much from integrating artificial intelligence (AI) and quantum computing in areas such as air traffic management, autonomous vehicle development, and traffic signal optimization. It might be a while before we see the benefits of quantum computers in transportation, but they could completely change the game. Congestion will be reduced, and freight will be delivered more quickly with the help of quantum computers because of their ability to swiftly determine the best traffic routes. Enhanced efficiency can potentially raise profits for businesses like FedEx and Amazon by as much as 600% [27].

Autonomous cars, which rely on artificial intelligence for training, may also benefit from using quantum computers. Training artificial intelligence algorithms take weeks or months on the best computers in the world, but might change with the advent of quantum computers. Media and entertainment, consumer products, and insurance are just a few other sectors that will feel the societal effects of quantum computers. On the other hand, if businesses are not ready, quantum computers might severely compromise their privacy and cybersecurity [28].
