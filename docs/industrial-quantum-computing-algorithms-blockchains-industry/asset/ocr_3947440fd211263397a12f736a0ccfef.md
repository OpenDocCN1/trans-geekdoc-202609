Research indicates that the use of automated technologies and AI could potentially lead to a rise in inequality. Automation can affect different industries in a varied manner, leading to higher job displacement and increased competition for individuals in lower-income households [32]. Moreover, studies indicate that a significant portion of employees might not have the necessary abilities and background to effectively transition to a different career with the rise of automation and AI [33]. This could lead to lower earnings growth and limited opportunities for individuals in lower-income families, especially older people [34]. There is a belief that automated technologies and AI could lead to “skill-biased technological change,” potentially harming those most impacted by automation [35]. Skill-biased technological advancement might lead to job opportunities for individuals with lower wages, consequently heightening wage disparity [36]. Moreover, automation has been shown to hinder lower-income families from escaping poverty because of inadequate earnings [37]. This could lead to extended instability for low-income families and significantly contribute to income inequality.

Inquality has grown on a worldwide scale in the past few decades, creating concerns about the welfare of people and communities in numerous areas of the globe. Promoting entrepreneurship is a suggested strategy for increasing economic opportunities and reducing inequality. Studies have demonstrated that entrepreneurship has diverse impacts on income inequality, and the existing research is crucial for policymakers seeking to address these disparities. Multiple research papers have indicated that entrepreneurship can help individuals escape poverty. For instance, Si and Ahlstrom [38] found that individuals who lost their own businesses were in comparison to individuals who remained employed or unemployed. Like-Waziriiri and Chivandi [39] explored a link between starting new businesses and decreasing poverty, showing that people can earn more money than before even with limited resources to establish a company. Entrepreneurship plays a vital role in reducing disparities among gender and social classes. Research shows that people from diverse backgrounds encounter many obstacles when to establish a business, including issues like inadequate funding and limited social connections [38]. However, if they manage to conquer these challenges, their success could eradicate fairness. Research shows that female entrepreneurs, can profit from entrepreneurship and make higher salaries than in conventional job markets [40]. Moreover, entrepreneurship contribute to decreasing overall income inequality by promoting the redistribution of wealth across various economic levels. Similarly, Morris and Santos [41] stated that increased entrepreneurial activity has narrowed the income gap, benefiting individuals with lower income levels.
