Each healthcare provider has implemented its own applications dedicated to collecting and processing information about patients. Often, these applications are not standardized, being created according to their own requirements and needs, compatibility sometimes only in terms of generating reports for authorities.
This leads to a digitalisation of data, making it difficult to communicate between different providers, requiring additional data processing, a significant consumption of resources, and for significant amounts of data this is unproductive.

The advancement of current technology allows relatively easy data collection, as well as processing and creating useful reports that can be used to provide better care services.
The use of information collected at the level of entities active in the field of health is countless and may include predictions and planning, telemedicine, electronic health records, real-time alerts, optimization of expenses and logistics, prevention, risk analysis, medical imaging analysis, and development of new therapies (Figure 6.2).
