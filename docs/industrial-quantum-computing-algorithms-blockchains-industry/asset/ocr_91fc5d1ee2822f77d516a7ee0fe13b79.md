# 1.1 Background and rationale

To completely comprehend the growing significance of quantum computing as transitions from concepts to realities, one must grasp the science and history of the technology. This type of quantum computing surpasses the average performance of traditional processors using quantum physics and “qubits” to perform complex computations at a faster rate [3]. Beyond processing massive volumes of data, quantum computing can fundamentally alter industries and social norms. The realization of broad ramifications drives research on the effects and implications of quantum-computing. The integration of quantum technology presents unique opportunities and challenges that need to be considered in daily life.

A new “race to the moon” has begun, in which countries take great delight and enormous resources into developing quantum computers. For example, beginning in 2018, the European Commission intends to invest heavily over the next two decades on a €11 billion quantum computing flagship effort. Adding to the €550 million invested in individual projects, this is a follow-up investment to position Europe at the forefront of what is being called the second quantum revolution. Now is the time to take this technology to the next level and construct actual quantum computers. This will allow us to use their theoretical advantages over classical’s Neumann computers in real-world applications. The original quantum revolution began in the early 1900s with the work of Planck, Bohr, and Einstein, which led to a theoretical understanding of the behavior of light and matter at extremely small scales [4].

The need for very low operating temperatures close to 0 °C is a common issue with most modern quantum devices. Thus, quantum computing as a cloud service seems to be the most viable commercial model for bringing this technology to consumers. On the other hand, when communicating with a cloud-based quantum computer via an Internet connection that other quantum computers may decode, the dependability of findings becomes an urgent concern.
