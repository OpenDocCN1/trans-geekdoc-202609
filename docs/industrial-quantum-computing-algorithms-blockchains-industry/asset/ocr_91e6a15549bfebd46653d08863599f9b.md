# 12.4.2 Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) applications

AI and ML are bringing positive changes in the healthcare industry by providing novel approaches to various problems. These technologies can potentially enhance patient outcomes, improve diagnoses, simplify administrative procedures, and contribute to medical research [13]. The Following is a list of important uses of AI and ML in the medical field:

# 12.4.2.1 Screening and diagnostic procedures

Risk stratification is made feasible in healthcare using AI and ML models that analyze patient data. This makes it possible to implement preemptive treatments and personalized care plans. At the same time, early indicators of diseases that can be identified by predictive analytics make healthcare intervention simpler because it can be carried out before situations become severe.

# 12.4.2.2 Probabilistic analytics and risk evaluation

In the healthcare industry, ML and AI models analyze patient data to identify and classify individuals according to their disease susceptibility. In addition to facilitating proactive mediation and individualized care plans, predictive analytics enable medical service providers to detect diseases early and intervene promptly before conditions deteriorate.

# 12.4.2.3 Drug discovery and improvement

AI exped the process of drug discovery by analyzing biological data to identify potential targets. Simultaneously, ML models predict the presumed appropriateness of drugs for new applications, potentially accelerating the development of remedies for different illnesses.

# 12.4.2.4 Personalized medicine
