Understanding the structure of proteins, which are essential for life, can help understand their function. Determining these structures means a huge amount of work, given that at this points of over 100,000 unique proteins have been determined, and the actual number which reaches billions [17].

There are studies that show the efficiency of using quantum computers depends greatly on the technique used to perform the calculations. So a group of researchers performed resource estimation for several active spaces of the drug Ibrutinib, using two techniques: trotterization with a complete Hamiltonian and qubitization using a truncated Hamiltonian. Using the latter technique, it has been estimated that calculating ground state energy in an active space (42e, 42o) will take over 1,000 years using trotterization, which is reduced to about 7.6 days using the rare quantization approach (assuming a 0.01% physical error rate and code cycle) [18].

# 6.6.2 Optimization of personalized treatment

Another extremely important area where quantum computers can be extremely useful is for personalized treatments, by conducting complex simulations to determine how a patient will respond to certain treatments, depending on the molecular and genetic profile.

Personalized treatments are also applied at this time, especially in oncological diseases, which multidisciplinary teams of specialists are involved, and the treatment is performed personalized according to certain criteria. The difference is that most of the criteria apply in determining these treatments are focused on the category of disease, type of organ, affected, tumor biology, or condition of the patient, not necessarily the molecular and genetic characteristics of patients.

Thus, through the extremely high power of data processing, quantum computers could simulate how drugs interact with specific molecules in the patients' bodies, being able to customize their dosage and combination in order to obtain the best response and eliminate side effects as much as possible.
