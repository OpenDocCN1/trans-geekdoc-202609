# 7.7.6 Global collaboration and information sharing

# 7.7.6.1 Quantum enablement

Developing business automation as a global force for positive change requires collaboration and information sharing of the unseen. Global collaboration, collaborative research, and open platforms will facilitate the exchange of ideas, best practices, and lessons learned across fields and disciplines [20]. By fostering a culture of collaboration and integration, the global community can come together to harness the transformative potential of quantum computing to solve complex and increasingly complex problems at the level of human knowledge.

In conclusion, the future of industrial automation infused with quantum computing is promising but is hampered by the complexity and uncertainty inherent in technological innovations. By grasping modern thoughts, collaborating inventively, and seizing unused opportunities, we will unquestionably explore the unfamiliar waters of computerization driven by quantum advancements.

# 7.8 Conclusion

Manufacturers can anticipate substantial gains in productivity, creativity, and security as quantum innovations appear and spread. Combining mechanical automation and quantum computing is, in essence, a paradigm shift that requires a thorough evaluation of the possible consequences on generation quality rather than merely a technological advance. Comprehending this connection enables businesses to effectively address present issues related to efficiency, knowledge, and capability, paving the way for a future where automation represents not just technology but also human creativity and aspiration.
