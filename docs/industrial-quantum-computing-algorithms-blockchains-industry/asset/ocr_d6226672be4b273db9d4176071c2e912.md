Although technology offers many benefits, it can also be used by companies to take advantage of workers [11]. For instance, the creation of AI-powered robots that can handle customer service has been linked to a decrease in human income and employment stability. Additionally, automation and AI could result in decreased opportunities for individuals from disadvantaged backgrounds that are qualified. The implementation of automation and AI has the potential to widen current gaps in the job market, particularly between the wealthy and the disadvantaged, leading to higher levels of income inequality. The rise of research studies focused on exploring the effects of automation and AI on income inequality is driven by the possibility of exacerbating the situation [12].

These research findings all agree that the implementation of such technologies has the potential to greatly alter job market dynamics in the upcoming years, as worsen current disparities in income distribution. The World Economic Forum (WEF) predicts that as many as 75 million jobs could be lost by 2022 due to automation and AI [12]. Enterprises across the globe have extensively used automation and AI in recent years due to advancements in technology. Automation and AI are seen as key drivers of the current technological revolution and digital transformation due to their efficiency and cost reduction. Consequently, the introduction of automation and AI in the workforce has led to both beneficial and detrimental effects on income inequality. It has been linked to elevated levels of unemployment, a drop in wages, and job insecurity. On the other hand, advancements in AI models and technologies, in addition to growth in the services sector and gig economy, have created chances for entrepreneurship, resulting in a reduction in income inequality [13]. In recent years, entrepreneurship has flourished and has the potential to lessen the gap between rich and poor individuals [13]. Additionally, the shift to the digital economy has opened up more chances for access to information and assets, which has resulted in numerous opportunities to reduce income inequality.
