# 7.6.1 Fundamentals of supply chain optimization

Making a supply chain work superior needs a plan that brings together all the operations and individuals included within the supply chain. They ought to work together to reach iterative targets saving money, delivering faster, managing inventory better, and improving product quality. The main point of making a supply chain superior is to manage all parts of the supply chain, from acquiring raw materials and making products to distribute and convey them, utilizing information to make smart decisions, point-by-point investigation, and strategies to find the most excellent arrangements.

# 7.6.1.2 Methods and processes

Every method and procedure helps the supply chain and is designed to manage specific problems and goals in products. Linear programming, integer programming, and mixed-integer programming techniques can be used to develop and solve optimization models that facilitate efficient decision-making in all areas of the supply chain, including inventory management, production planning, and transportation.

Additionally, optimization algorithms such as genetic algorithms, simulated annealing, and particle swarm optimization provide heuristics for solving combinatorial optimization problems that vary with real-world constraints and uncertainty in the product.

# 7.6.1.3 Technology enablers

The emergence of technologies such as AI, ML and big data analytics has changed the landscape of good products, causing organizations to spend a lot of money on information and good ideas. Understanding and making informed decisions, forecasting, and custom analysis lead to risk management, demand forecasting, and resource planning, while process monitoring provides visibility, traceability, and efficient operation across connected devices.
