# 4.2 Utilizing quantum computing in the healthcare sector

Quantum computing is a promising new discipline of computer science that might revolutionize medical research, diagnosis, and therapy. By using quantum physics, quantum computing has unmatched promise in healthcare across many applications. Quantum bits, entanglement, superposition, and interference give it an edge over classical computing in speed, parallelism, and quantum annealing. Quantum computing offers an interest to solve some of healthcare's biggest problems.
