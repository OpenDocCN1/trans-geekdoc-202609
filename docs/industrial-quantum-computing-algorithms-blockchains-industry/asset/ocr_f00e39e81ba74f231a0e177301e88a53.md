# 7.2 Evolution of industrial automation

The current technology automation of business is undergoing change, which is subject to advances in technology, designs, and operating excellence. The history of industrial automation started with manufacturing that would likely take 15 h by few minutes by the technology kicked off by Henry Ford at the beginning of the new millennium. Furthermore, by birth of CNC machines in the 1950s has started to form the working system of today. According to Smith [1], the upcoming CNC devices introduced a significant change in production not only by precise but also by digital information. The last part of the decade represented the advent of the programmable logic controller, which was lead of industrial automation systems with their program filters, reliability, and physical robustness. Industrial process automation also includes the integration of robotics in the manufacturing process. The latest generations of robots have been designed and equipped with multi-functional scanners, actuators, and controllers, thus highly capable of moving from only mechanical operations to more complex assembling, material handling, and quality control [4]. Firstly, Internet of Things (IoT) technology is a new era of connectivity, decision-making, and predictive maintenance that has to be a fix of business automation problems. IoT makes it possible for dynamic workflow management through sensing, actuation, and communication technology intrinsic to the manufacturing processes [5]. The researchers' and developers' discoveries of AI and machine learning (ML) were the main factors caused business automation technology to develop so quickly. AI-based algorithms are designed for predictive analysis. Change management and automated decision-making systems are necessary for optimal production lines, eliminating downtime and flexibility of business [6]. Business automation has continued to grow by the inventions made in robotics, industrial IoT, AI, and data analytics fields. As digital transformation changes the shape in the realm, the lines between physical machines and virtual ones are slowly vanishing, creating smart factories that have interactive, intelligent, and flexible production systems. In general, development of industrial automation can be described as an innovative journey characterized by after change by technology and the value attached to competition for superior performance. The mechanization industry has extended incredibly since its initiation because of specialized improvements, budgetary necessities, and ceaseless development in fabricating strategies.
