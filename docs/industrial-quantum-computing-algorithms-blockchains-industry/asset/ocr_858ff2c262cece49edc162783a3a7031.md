# 12.4.2.5 Clinical decision support systems

AI in medicine facilitates the progress of treatments by analyzing patient data, including genetics, to develop personalized treatment strategies. This contributes to improving the precision of the treatment. ML algorithms are employed in genomic medicine to assess genetic data. These algorithms identify markers linked to diseases and provide recommendations for tailored treatment approaches based on the results of these analyses.

# 12.4.2.6 Natural language processing (NLP)

In medical processes, natural language processing (NLP) adds value to clinical documentation by gaining insights from unstructured notes, improving the accuracy and efficiency of electronic health record (EHR) systems. Additionally, chatbots empower patients by answering questions, providing healthcare knowledge, and enabling communication between patients and doctors.

# 12.4.2.7 Fraud detection and healthcare management

In medical processes, ML algorithms majority manage claims, identify fraudulent claims, eliminate healthcare scams, and ensure insurance processing accuracy. Simultaneously, AI is used to modernize hospital processes to provide efficient resource allocation and proper management of patient flow. Although AI and ML implementation in the health sector has various benefits, issues such as data privacy, ethical considerations, and compliance requirement must be considered.
