# # 5.8 Strategies for securing 6G IoT networks

Keeping IoT networks secure in 6G technology is a must to protect data privacy and keep all devices connected safely [10]. With the scale and complexity of IoT growing globally, a blended strategy approach is vital to deal with threats and vulnerabilities adequately as advance [13].

# ## 5.8.1 Encryption and authentication mechanisms

It has accelerated the connections that we have with our family and friends but increased our fragile emotional states, leading to several mental disorders. Implementing robust encryption protocols and authentication mechanisms is becoming vital for data in motion and at-rest security [12]. By the Gartner report, 2025, using the current trends, 75% of IoT-generated data will be processed at places away from traditional IT data centers, thereby underscoring the value of encryption to secure data transmission over already distributed environments.
