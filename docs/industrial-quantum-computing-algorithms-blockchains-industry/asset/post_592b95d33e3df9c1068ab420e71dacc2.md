## 4.5 Conclusion

Quantum computing has revolutionized computational systems, bringing unprecedented speed, efficiency, and reliability. This comprehensive literature review aims to leverage quantum computing's potential in healthcare applications [48–52]. We exhaustively explore the range of healthcare scenarios that might benefit from quantum computing. We discuss the complex requirements needed to create quantum-powered healthcare solutions and classify the present quantum computing architectures for healthcare systems.
