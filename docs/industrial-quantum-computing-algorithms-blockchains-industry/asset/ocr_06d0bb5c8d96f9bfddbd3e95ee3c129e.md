# 12.5.1 Threats to patient data privacy

# 12.5.1.1 Ransomware attacks on healthcare systems

The healthcare industry is one of the biggest victims of ransomware, as patient information will be locked, and attackers will demand that the data be released after they have been paid. These attacks impede the work of medical personnel, diminish the quality of clinical care, and result in significant economic costs.
