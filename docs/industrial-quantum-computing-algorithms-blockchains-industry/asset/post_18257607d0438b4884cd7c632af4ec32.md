## 7.5.10 Quantum security concerns

Integrating quantum technologies into industrial systems raises additional security problems. While quantum computing has the potential to defeat present encryption systems, there is still time to develop quantum-resistant encryption techniques. To assess the potential hazards and benefits of quantum technologies in corporate separation, cybersecurity measures must be carefully considered, as well as quantum-secure encryption techniques must be developed [9].

As a result, scientists, engineers, and lawmakers must thoroughly examine the challenges and issues associated with incorporating quantum computing into business automation. Solving these difficulties will require collaboration in quantum hardware development, algorithm innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, cost management, and security enhancement. As quantum technology evolves, addressing these difficulties will be critical to realizing the potential of quantum-enhanced industrial automation and ushering in a new age of production.

## 7.6.2 Production planning and resource planning

Did you know that quantum technology is revolutionizing the way we manufacture car parts? Gupta and Singh [11] conducted a study where they used quantum annealing, a type of quantum computing, to improve production planning and resource allocation. They turned scheduling challenges into a special type of problem called QUBO and quantum techniques to solve it. The result is significant improvements in efficiency, resource use, and production times. In other words, these quantum-powered algorithms are like a superpower for manufacturers, giving them the ability to adapt in real time and optimize on the fly.
