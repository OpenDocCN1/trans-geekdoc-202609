# 10.4.3 Use of AI in education

AI has had a significant impact on schooling. AI tools, such as digital instructors and interactive systems, have significantly impacted educational opportunities. AI improved education by making it easy for students to learn and stay engaged; reducing reliance on route learning and increasing educators' ability to personalize curricula; augmenting matter access; enabling more efficient assessments and diagnostics; and assisting in the scaling of education initiatives [79]. The emergence of digital tutors is one way in AI has been able to alter education. Digital tutors, like human tutors, can provide students with personalized training and feedback. Nevertheless, they are able to handle information at a quicker and more effective rate, enabling them to adapt and change education to the pace and level of students. This enhances student involvement by enabling them to progress through the course material at their own speed. Wellhammer and Dolata [80] suggest that digital tutors could integrate multimedia and digital education to enhance students' involvement with the subject material. AI has also enhanced education by providing students with personalized guidance. Platforms like Knewton use AI algorithms to customize students' learning experiences based on their individual needs. This guarantees that students get individualized instruction that suits their unique learning preferences. Moreover, AI can analyze student data to identify areas of learning deficiency and customize education accurately. This helps improve the learning experience for students with different levels and skills [81]. AI has also improved access to academic material, enabling students to access educational resources regardless of where they are or what they have.

AI-powered systems provide users with access to current libraries and pertinent information. This has been particularly advantageous for individuals residing in areas with limited access to educational resources. Moreover, AI has increased the efficiency of assessment and diagnosis. AI-powered assessment and course materials have the ability to quickly evaluate student progress and offer feedback on areas needing improvement. This enables quick detection of areas needing additional education or resources, avoiding wasting resources on topics mastered by students. Although AI has greatly benefited education, it has also been linked to a rise in poverty and financial inequality within the education sector.
