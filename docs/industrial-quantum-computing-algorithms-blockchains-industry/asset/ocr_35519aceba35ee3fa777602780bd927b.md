# 12.4.3.5 Clinical trials and research

The integration of blockchain technology has revolutionized clinical research, yielding a twofold impact. Initially, it enhances the transparency and accessibility of clinical trial data by establishing a secure and transparent platform for collecting and sharing trial data. This platform is safeguarded from unauthorized modifications, maintaining the reliability and precision of research findings. Furthermore, it establishes a secure and verifiable framework that incentivizes users to contribute their sample data for research objectives, fostering increased collaboration among researchers.

# 12.4.3.6 Credentialing and identity management

Blockchains provide dual advantages in the management of healthcare. Firstly, they ensure secure individual identification by regulating the credentials of healthcare professionals and restricting access to authorized personnel for important information about patients. Furthermore, the technology enhances the efficiency of administrative duties, particularly in verifying credentials, resulting in time and resource savings for these patients.

# 12.4.3.7 Decentralized healthcare apps (DCH-Apps)

Blockchain focuses on the patient by using decentralized apps that let patients see their health information and have a say in making decisions. This technology also introduces the idea of data monetization, which lets people exchange their knowledge with study participants and healthcare providers while owning the data. This opens the door to new ways of making money off data. There are many good things about blockchain, but it is not widely used in the healthcare business because of unclear regulations, difficult technical integration, and the requirement for interoperability. However, new information and test programs show the demand for blockchain to inspire change is spreading.

In medical care, Industry 5.0 innovations are described by how they link and transfer information between different parts of the ecosystem: medical care. Using these kinds of technologies will make healthcare more efficient, improve the level of care for patients, and raise the standards of care. As healthcare systems start to live Industry 5.0 principles, the human-intelligent technologies synergy will only continue toward a new age of patient-centered and technology-innovated care.
