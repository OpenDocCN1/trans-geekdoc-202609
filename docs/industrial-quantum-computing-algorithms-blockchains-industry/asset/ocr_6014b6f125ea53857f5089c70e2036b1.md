# 3.3.1 Types of quantum machine learning

There are different types of QML.

**a) Quantum neural networks (QNNs):** In both science and industry, neural networks have achieved great success. With the latest developments in quantum technology, the goal is to build QNNs that can complete entire quantum learning tasks [13]. More sophisticated than standard artificial neural network (ANN), QNN combines ANNs with quantum computing [12]. The basic building blocks of quantum information, qubits and quantum logic gates, are used by these neural networks to operate. **Quantum phenomena like superposition and entanglement are used by QNNs, enabling qubits to exhibit correlation with one another and exist in several states simultaneously.** When compared to conventional neural networks, QNNs have faster and more efficient learning [9]. Large dataset management, function approximation, and computer gaming are applications of QNN. Among other things, QNN algorithms are utilized to simulate automated control systems, associative memory devices, and social networks. Figure 3.4 demonstrates that even for two-input one-output qubit perceptions, our QNNs' quantum circuit architecture enables universal quantum computing. Especially noteworthy is the discovery of a QNN made of four-level qubits that commute inside each layer.
