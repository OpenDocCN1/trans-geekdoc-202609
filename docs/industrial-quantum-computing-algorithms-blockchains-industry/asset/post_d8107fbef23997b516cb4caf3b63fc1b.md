## 5.7 Security and privacy issues of 6G technologies

6G, the next big thing in connectivity, may bring the global community to a crossroads between security and privacy concerns [10, 13]. Here's a deep dive into these challenges, armed with relevant global statistics.

### 5.7.1 Artificial intelligence

Algorithms, like people, are biased and vulnerable to manipulation, threatening billions worldwide [27].
**Global AI market size:** Likely to come down to $1.56 trillion in 2023 (Statista).
**Bias in AI algorithms:** A McKinsey report from 2022 showed that most global companies (83%) experienced one or more types of AI bias [28].
**Manipulating AI models:** A 2021 study on image recognition models by Carnegie Mellon University showed that the models could be easily fooled, with success rates as high as 99.6%.
Such numbers draw a picture we would rather do without – a world where thousands of users of 6G powered by AI might face discrimination or misinformation [25]. Efficient data governance and stringent security protocols are equally important in mitigating risk [38].

### 5.7.2 Molecular communication

**Estimated number of connected devices by 2030:** Over half of global Internet traffic will come from 50 billion (Statista).

**Molecular communication research funding:** More than €15 million is being spent by the European Union on projects dealing with these issues.

**Eavesdropping on molecular signals:** A 2022 study by MIT revealed that intercepting and analyzing molecular communication is possible.

Because billions of different devices can transmit information beyond molecules in 6G, the possibility of the outside listener to sensitive health information in the body is now a global issue [30]. Privacy should be the main consideration when designing secure communication protocols and encryption in this domain [31].

### 5.7.3 Quantum communication

**Global quantum technology market size:** It is expected to grow as high as $45.5 billion by 2025 (Grand View Research).

**Vulnerability to “man-in-the-middle” attacks:** Recent research showed that vulnerability of quantum communication systems was successfully exploited.

**Disruption of fragile quantum states:** A study from ETH Zurich, which found quantum communication signal disruption due to environmental conditions, was published in 2021.

With quantum-resistant encryption being highly impregnable, it urges to address the issues of a fast-growing global blockchain market. Quantum key distribution protocols and sophisticated error correction methods are a must to protect these revolutions [32].

### 5.7.4 Blockchain

**The number of global blockchain users** is 46 million (source: what Statista states).

**Value of the global blockchain market:** In 2022, it was worth $4.8 billion (Grand View Research).

**51% attacks:** Unlike the Ethereum Classic blockchain, which was successfully attacked in 2022, securing its vulnerability.

**Sybil attacks:** The study of University of California, Berkeley, 2020 proved that the Sybil attack was the most lethal threat for 80% of the proof-of-stake blockchains.

With millions of users and billions of dollars invested in it, this problem must be solved. As such, securing systems like 51% and Sybil attacks are prioritized [33]. Impart robust consensus mechanisms [34], identity verification protocols, and regular security audits to avert tampering with the distributed ledger [22].

### 5.7.5 THz technology (THz)

**Global 5G market size:** A market value of $131.9 billion in 2022, according to Market Research Future.

**THz frequencies used in 6G:** With the anticipation of achieving higher speed than 5G.

**Limited research on health effects:** The long-term health effects of exposure to terahertz rays have not been fully studied.

The need for research on health risks as 6G possibilities in THz emerge become necessary with 5g global propagation [3]. In addition to that, the potential of THz waves to see through clothing causes privacy concerns, raising the need to adopt responsible deployment strategies33].

### 5.7.6 Visible light communication (VLC)

**Global indoor location services market size:** The projection is to amount to $55.8 billion by 2027 (as stated by Market Research Future).

**Visible light communication (VLC)'s potential for indoor applications:** Lots of examples are a house, a hospital, and retail places.

**Susceptibility to interference and eavesdropping:** VLC signals can be tapped, thus raising a serious privacy concern.

To safely exploit 6G in vertical location locations, VLC in 6G poses considerable opportunities, but we should also take care of security gaps. Rigorous modulation methods, encryption protocols, and network segmentation approaches can be used to prevent interference and eavesdropping [37].
