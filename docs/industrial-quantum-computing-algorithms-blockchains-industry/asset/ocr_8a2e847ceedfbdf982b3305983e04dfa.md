#  5.3 Unique cybersecurity threats in 6G IoT

Similarly, 6G allow faster access speeds and innovation that we could not imagine yet, but it may put us at a greater risk of cyber threats. The threats, as opposed to those that torment current networks, evolved from the unique fabric of revolutionary technologies of 6G [11]. It is important to realize these unique weaknesses and find ways to prevent them because tomorrow’s interconnected world would not be secure without them.
