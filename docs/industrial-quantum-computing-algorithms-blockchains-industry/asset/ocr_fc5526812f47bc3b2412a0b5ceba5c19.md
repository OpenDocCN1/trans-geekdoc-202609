# 10.2.3 Impact of women's empowerment on income inequality

In the current economy, the problem of income inequality is increasingly noticeable and significant. Finkelstein and Harding [48] have linked income inequality to increased poverty rates, persistent racial and gender discrimination, deteriorating mental health, decreased overall well-being, and reduced economic growth. Women are equally affected by income inequality, facing challenges like gender disparity, poverty levels, and systematic discrimination in the workplace [49]. Empowering women has become an important strategy in addressing economic inequality [50].
