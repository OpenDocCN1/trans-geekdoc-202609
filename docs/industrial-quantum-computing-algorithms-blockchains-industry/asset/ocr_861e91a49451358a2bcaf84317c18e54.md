# 1.7 Conclusion

This chapter primarily delves into the core ideas of quantum computing, significant achievements, and the many applications that might revolutionize civilization. Quantum computing has both positive and negative effects on society, we have covered in our discussion of its effects. Thanks to the ever-expanding realm of quantum computing, new hardware, algorithms, and applications are on the horizon. These developments will transform our perception of the world, spur innovation in many sectors, and make previously impossible tasks seem simpler.

As soon as quantum computing comes, technology will reach its peak. While we continue looking into this cutting-edge technology, we must think about how it will change things, help us solve problems, and ensure everyone will benefit. Due to the potential enormous influence that quantum computing may have on our current way of life, the development of this technology has proceed with utmost caution and an emphasis on ethical concerns. Given that quantum computing has the potential to bypass existing encryption methods, privacy and security of information are key concerns.

As quantum computing becomes more prevalent, it will be necessary for people, businesses, and governments to take steps to protect sensitive information. An effective approach is to create and execute strong security policies and secure networks of communication that can withstand attacks from quantum computers. Quantum computing is a constantly being researched by various organizations, including businesses, educational institutions, and government agencies. Understanding how to effectively communicate with a wide variety of people is necessary to facilitate the advancement and acceptance of quantum innovation. A wide variety of individuals are included in this category, such as professionals, legislators, business leaders, representatives of social organizations, and officials from the government. To define rules for responsible advancement and discovery, it is necessary to thoroughly consider the ethical, social, and economic ramifications associated with the progression of technology.
