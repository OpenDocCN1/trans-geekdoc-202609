+   - **Protecting data online:** The future of encryption and personal data protection may be affected by quantum computing. Data encryption while in use, both at rest and in transit, might be aided by QMCs, which could be useful in light of the ever-changing cybersecurity scenario by Group [39].
- **Optimal routing and traffic:** An efficiently functioning supply chain depends on well-designed transportation routes. The most challenging aspect of this planning process is effectively using all pertinent real-time data, including traffic patterns and weather predictions. QMCs are very advantageous in this context. They can analyze the data instantaneously and concurrently optimize the travel plans of a whole fleet of vehicles.
- **The production process:** Better, more lifelike testing and prototyping can be done using QMCs. In the industrial sector, this can potentially improve designs with less testing and lower cost of prototyping [40].
- **Studying chemicals and drugs:** With the help of QMCs, we may improve our knowledge of molecular structure by building more accurate simulations of atomic interactions. The development of new goods and medications, as well as the field of chemical and medicinal research, can be significantly affected by this.
