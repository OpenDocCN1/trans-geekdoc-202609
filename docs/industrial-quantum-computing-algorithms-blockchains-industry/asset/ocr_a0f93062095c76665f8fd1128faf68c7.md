+   **Substantial advancements in quantum computing:** The advancement of quantum physics in the early twentieth century laid the groundwork for quantum computing. However, the area did not come together until Richard Feynman put the concept of a quantum computer forward in the 1980s. A lot happened since, moving the research forward and making quantum computing a viable possibility [18].

+   **Notable accomplishments and discoveries:** The first quantum computer, IBM Q, was unveiled in 2016, showcasing IBM’s position as a leader in quantum research. IBM Q was made available to academics and companies to explore quantum computing and create new applications. The availability of quantum computing was greatly enhanced by this [19].

+   "Quantum supremacy" was proclaimed by Google in 2019 after its quantum computer, Sycamore, completed a computation that would take a traditional supercomputer thousands of years. This achievement shows that those quantum computers may potentially resolve issues that were previously believed to be intractable by Preskill [20].

+   **Improvements in quality**, error correction, and scalability are only a few examples of the noteworthy strides in creating quantum computing hardware within the last decade. These developments have pushed us one step closer to constructing large-scale, operational quantum computers that can compete or surpass traditional computers in several tasks.
