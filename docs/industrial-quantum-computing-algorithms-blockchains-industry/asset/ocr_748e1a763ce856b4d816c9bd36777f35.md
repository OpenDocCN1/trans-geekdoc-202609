Encouraging income-generating activities and increasing financial autonomy are vital strategies for empowering women to mitigate economic disparities. Research shows that providing women access to microfinance directly impacts their capacity to generate income, enhance earnings from current businesses, and improve economic stability [52]. This holds particularly true for females in developing countries, who have limited opportunities for education, resources, and credit [53].

Empowering women can also reduce income inequality by increasing their educational opportunities. Education plays a crucial role in an individual's overall welfare and has the potential to greatly influence their earnings. Research has demonstrated that education enhances women's outcomes in the labor market, less poverty, and stimulates economic growth [54]. Women's ability to obtain education, especially at secondary and postgraduate levels, increases their chances of securing well-paying jobs and narrowing the wage disparity between men and women. Ultimately, the empowerment of women contributes to achieving social and economic equity by reducing gender discrimination in the workforce. Research suggests that providing women with equal opportunities in the labor force and eliminating gender discrimination can positively affect income levels and decrease income inequality [55]. In addition, providing women with economic empowerment can result in improved economic opportunities for all women, ultimately enhancing household welfare [56].

# 10.3 Methodology

This research study mainly depends on desktop research to assess the influence of AI and automation on income inequality. The research utilized noninteractive methods to objectively evaluate the impact of AI and automation on income inequality in BRICS countries. One of the methods employed includes assessments of peer-reviewed journals, publications, and authoritative documents regarding AI and digital financial inclusion. In simpler terms, content analysis is a research method that identifies specific words, topics, or concepts in a qualitative data text or data set. The study used content analysis to examine specific words, themes, or concepts that underpinned its arguments and conclusions, measuring their presence, importance, and relationships.
