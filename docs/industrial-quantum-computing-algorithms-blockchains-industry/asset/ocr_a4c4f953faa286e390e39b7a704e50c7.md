# 3.3 Quantum machine learning

Machine learning has made a wide range of cutting-edge applications possible in social and scientific domains, especially in deep networks that use thepropagation method. Speech recognition and handwriting recognition together with sophisticated applications in scientific research are widely used successful examples [9]. The theory and practice of machine learning training algorithms have advanced significantly, but they still need a lot of computing power. We must prepare for the possibility that development may slow down in the future as Moore’s law losing traction. New and intriguing opportunities are emerging with the impending release of quantum computing devices, which use the principles of quantum physics to circumvent the constraints of conventional computation. Research employing quantum computing systems is in its early stages regarding quantum machine learning (QML). Quantum applications include quantum measurement, adaptive quantum computing, and complex system modeling may benefit from traditional machine learning. Conventional machine learning processes can be accelerated by quantum algorithms. Learning difficulties may be solved using quantum data utilizing quantum computing devices [13].
