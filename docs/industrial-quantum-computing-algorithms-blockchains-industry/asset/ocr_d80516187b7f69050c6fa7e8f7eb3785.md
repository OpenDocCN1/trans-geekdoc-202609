# 1.3.2 Business and finance

Several studies have shown that, at least shortly, quantum computers will be most useful to the financial industry. The capacity to predict stock market outcomes that were previously too unpredictable and numerous to compute is the primary advantage of quantum computing for the financial sector. Quantum computers will be able to perform more precise risk assessments under numerous probable situations, which is something that investors are increasingly seeking. Better lending judgments will also be possible by using quantum computers' more accurate credit computations regarding loans and portfolios [25].

# 1.3.3 Climate prediction

It is also possible that quantum computers might forecast the weather. That "currently, a classical computer might take more time than it takes the weather to evolve to predict the weather" is based on an interview with an MIT professor that advised PBS. The transportation sector, the food production industry, and many others feel the effects of this enhanced climate forecasting. Directly or indirectly, weather impacts around around 30% of GDP in the USA. Better weather prediction would also allow more time to be ready for calamities [26].

# 1.3.4 Travel and transportation

The transportation and travel industries gain much from integrating artificial intelligence (AI) and quantum computing in areas such as air traffic management, autonomous vehicle development, and traffic signal optimization. It might be a while before we see the benefits of quantum computers in transportation, but they could completely change the game. Congestion will be reduced, and freight will be delivered more quickly with the help of quantum computers because of their ability to swiftly determine the best traffic routes. Enhanced efficiency can potentially raise profits for businesses like FedEx and Amazon by as much as 600% [27].

Autonomous cars, which rely on artificial intelligence for training, may also benefit from using quantum computers. Training artificial intelligence algorithms take weeks or months on the best computers in the world, but might change with the advent of quantum computers. Media and entertainment, consumer products, and insurance are just a few other sectors that will feel the societal effects of quantum computers. On the other hand, if businesses are not ready, quantum computers might severely compromise their privacy and cybersecurity [28].
