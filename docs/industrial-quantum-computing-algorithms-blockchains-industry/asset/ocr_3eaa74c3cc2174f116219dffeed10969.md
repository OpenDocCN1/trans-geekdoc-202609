# 15.6.4 Effects of correcting drug discovery models

When calculation errors are corrected, quantum simulations are more reliable. Reliability is essential for drug development predictions of molecular attributes like affinities and reaction pathways. Error correction reduces quantum noise and faults, improving simulations. Predicting molecular interactions accurately allows for more confident therapy selection. Error correction approaches enable larger and more sophisticated chemical system simulations by minimizing errors and improving quantum computing. Expanding the simulation to cover additional chemical regions helps find new medication possibilities.

Error correction can be used with qubits and gate operations before they occur. Efficient resource makes quantum drug discovery simulations more scalable and ensures computational resource allocation.
