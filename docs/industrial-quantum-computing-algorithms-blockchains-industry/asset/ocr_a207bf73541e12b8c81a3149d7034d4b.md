# 1.3.9 Environment and climate models

By simulating complex natural systems more accurately, quantum computing has the potential to radically alter climate modeling and environmental studies. Researchers can create more effective solutions to environmental problems and planet preservation if they have a deeper knowledge of the elements that cause climate change and how our ecosystems function [34, 35].

# 1.3.10 Streamlining logistics and the supply chain

Outperforming conventional computers in solving complicated optimization issues, quantum computing may aid in optimizing supply chains and logistical operations. Businesses and industries may see cost reduction, improved resource allocation, and improved efficiency [35].

# 1.4 Societal impacts

There has been a proactive effort to build legal, regulatory, ethical, and cybersecurity initiatives to handle the digital trust issues that quantum computing brings, even if it is expected to have far-reaching beneficial effects on society, the economy, and several businesses [36]. Quantum computing's rapid evolution is revealing its societal effects. Quantum computing may transform how we access and handle massive volumes of data, whether for cybersecurity or traffic optimization. Quantum computing might revolutionize our lives, but it carries severe moral and ethical issues. Quantum computing experts worry about people abusing quantum computing [37, 38].

Quantum computing may revolutionize data storage, analysis, and use. Traditional computing issues are now easier to solve using this method. Despite this, many experts feel this technology might radically change privacy rules. Quantum computing may make data storage safe. Classical computing stores data centrally, subjecting it to hacking and other attacks. Quantum computing can secure data in several places, making access it almost difficult. This may preserve privacy and greatly prevent data breaches [38, 39].

Quantum computing may also improve encryption. These algorithms may secure sensitive data like medical or financial information. This may help protect users from unauthorized parties. Quantum computing has the potential to provide novel authentication methods. These tactics will enable us to restrict access to sensitive information to authorized users.
