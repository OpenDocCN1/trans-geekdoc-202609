## 13.8 Quantum computing revolutionizes industry and society

The combination of quantum mechanics into various industries is set to have a profound influence on how we work, live, and engage with digital media. Quantum computing's impacts are huge and far-reaching – helping scientists develop complex AI formulas to public health services with tailor-made medical solutions [17].

Drug discovery, materials science, finance, and logistics are some of the fields that quantum computing will revolutionize in the industry sector [8]. It leads to faster analysis of molecular structures and subsequently improved designs for new drugs and materials that have desired traits. The power that comes with quantum computing enables us to make better choices when it comes to investment while simultaneously mitigating negative outcomes. Quantum computing can also be used by supply chain and logistics management of companies to speed up processes, reduce-costs, and increase productivity.

Quantum computing will greatly benefit society, particularly in the areas of cybersecurity, climate modeling, and AI [10]. Quantum cryptography provides a high level of security that protects data and prevents its unlawful access by strangers. With such an application, participants in climate studies can build quantum models that have less uncertainty, hence enabling them to have a better understanding and subsequently combat environmental problems. Quantum computing also increases the capabilities of AI algorithms, resulting in breakthroughs in machine learning and pattern recognition.

### 13.8.1 The impact of quantum computing on business, industry, and society

Due to the increasing acceptance of quantum computing in different fields, how we actively work and engage with modern digital media will drastically transform. Quantum computing carries with it various consequences, as developing high-AI algorithms and revolutionizing healthcare using personalized medicine.

Basically, quantum computing would bring innovation in the domains of medicine development, finance, logistics, and other materials. Quantum algorithms can enable quick analysis of molecular structures, leading to a more efficient process for discovering new drugs or developing innovative materials. Capitalizing on the massive processing power, quantum computing would enable financial companies to better manage investment portfolios as well as minimize exposure to risks. Further, supply chain and logistics management of companies can use quantum algorithms to optimize processes, reducing costs and increasing efficiency.

Quantum mechanics would have a huge effect on society in areas like AI, cybersecurity, and climate modeling. Data is protected from possible intrusions by the extremely methods provided by quantum cryptography [9]. Quantum simulations are a valuable tool for climate scientists to develop more accurate models that will help with environmental concerns' comprehension and mitigation. Furthermore, the capabilities of AI systems are enhanced by quantum computing, leading to advances in pattern recognition and machine learning.

Although quantum computing has numerous applications, its constraints also halt its application for real-world use. This is where the quantum coins:
