# 13.5 Emergence of quantum physics

The base of quantum physics can be said to have emerged in the early twentieth century. The most advanced mechanics in quantum computing were discovered by well-known scientists like Max Planck, Albert Einstein, and Niels Bohr, who formed the groundwork for the development of quantum mechanics.

Richard Feynman introduced the idea of using quantum phenomena to substantially improve classical computing in 1982. David Deutsch evolved the theoretical basis for quantum computing between 1984 and 1985, building on this idea. The breakthrough came in 1994 when Peter Shor introduced Shor's algorithm, which demonstrated the potential power of quantum computing by efficiently factoring large numbers using quantum polynomial time.
