# Chapter 10
The impact of AI and automation on income inequality in BRICS countries and the role of structural factors and women’s empowerment

**Abstract:** This study evaluated the impact of artificial intelligence (AI) and automation on inequality in the BRICS countries (Brazil, Russia, India, China, and South Africa). The study thoroughly examined the moderating role of structural factors and women’s empowerment on the effect of AI and automation using desktop research that included 75 journal articles, 35 reports, and 45 key documents. It was discovered that automation boosts productivity and decreases costs, it also has a detrimental impact on inequality since it eliminates low-wage, low-skill jobs, which results in job losses and salary reductions in the manufacturing and service sectors. By increasing access to education and presenting equitable opportunities in the workplace, women’s economic empowerment may contribute to the reduction of economic disparity brought by automation. Governments should promote an equitable distribution of wealth and resources through progressive taxes and welfare policies in order to reduce economic inequality. While considering financial inequality and poverty, AI and automation should be used to boost economic mobility and eliminate poverty.

**Keywords:** Artificial intelligence, automation, income inequality, structural factors, women's empowerment

## 10.1 Introduction

Income inequality is an issue that has been around for decades, long before the emergence of automation and artificial intelligence (AI). In fact, the main source of inequality is the uneven distribution of income, where the rich earn more than the poor. This phenomenon occurs due to disparities in earnings, socioeconomic class, employment opportunities, education level, and other factors [1]. Despite being inevitable, income inequality is an issue that could be remediated by improving resources available, empowering individuals, and respecting one's identity, through a combination of public and private initiatives [2]. Nonetheless, automation and AI are regarded as two of the most innovative technologies of the present time, with the potential to greatly enhance economic output [3]. The practice of delegating normal and repetitive work to machinery and systems that are controlled by computers and other electronic devices is referred to as automation [4]. Automation boosts efficiency, lowers costs, and eliminates the need for trained labor. AI, on the other hand, is the creation of computer systems that can “think” and interact with their surroundings [5]. AI has the ability to transform the way businesses function, particularly via boosting decision-making efficiency and accuracy, as well as AI-powered data analysis. It appears evident that both positive and negative effects on income inequality will be caused by the influence of automation and AI. Automation and AI have the potential to increase production and operational efficiency. As a result, organizations could see increased profits, employees may receive higher compensation, and increased prosperity could be enhanced. Conversely, automation and AI may have negative effects, as job insecurity, reduced salaries, and a decrease in labor value [6]. These impacts could widen the current disparity between the wealthy and the poor, worsening income inequality.

Three main issues primarily contribute to the worsening of inequality. Initially, automation and AI may result in a higher number of people losing their jobs. This is a result of automation, which can decrease the need for workers and thus lead to job cuts [7]. Additionally, the introduction of automation and AI in work settings could lead to decreased salaries and job uncertainty, as employers may opt for lower wages owing to reduced labor expenses [8]. Thirdly, according to Li and Wang [9], technology may lessen the advantages of human capital by discounting labor.
