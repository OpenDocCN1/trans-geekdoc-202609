# 6.6 Application areas of quantum technology in health

# 6.6.1 Molecular simulation and drug design

The design of medicines is a process that consumes extremely high resources, both in terms of human resources (highly qualified researchers and specialists) and finance, but especially in terms of time, the creation of a new product is being possible in many years of research.

It is estimated that the total number of drug-like molecules is between $10^{23}$ and $10^{60}$ [15]. So far, only about $10^6$ of these substances have been synthesized, which means that there is an enormous space to explore, and quantum computers can make a difference in this.

With the development of quantum algorithms and machine learning models for creating new drugs, medicinal chemistry could advance enormously, with quantum computers offering infinitely computing power than conventional computers.

Quantum computers can simulate how molecules will behave so as to allow understanding of the molecular interactions required when designing drugs, which would lead to the creation of new drugs with high efficiency.
