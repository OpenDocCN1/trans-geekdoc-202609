# Chapter 9
Quantum computing in machine learning:
an an overview

**Abstract:** Quantum technologies is a buzz word spinning around different fields, from chemistry to computer science and agriculture, and also expanding into the medical field. The term combine computations and concepts of machine learning. Among these, quantum machine learning has gained prominence, particularly in healthcare applications, by using its effective data processing and classification techniques. This field uses the concepts of quantum mechanics, where qubits are used to increase the speed of calculations compared to normal computers. It also super-position and entanglement to enhance data processing, providing the capability to perform many calculations at once. The regular enhancement in quantum machines has led to a fierce race in academia, industry, and research. This article covers the basics of quantum computing, quantum machine learning, and its techniques, which provide valuable insights about this emerging technology.

**Keywords:** Quantum Computing, Qubits, Superposition, Optimization, QAML, Quantum Support Vector Machine, Entanglement

# 9.1 Introduction

Quantum computing is an emerging technology that can perform numerous calculations simultaneously, leading to better performance for certain tasks. According to some current research, it has been shown that quantum machines can perform better than regular computers in specific situations, which also creates curiosity in the research community. This has led researchers to dive into how quantum computing could be combined with machine learning (ML), resulting in a term called "quantum machine learning" (QML). Researchers are making progress in this field by making small prototypes. They have also shown interest in "quantum neural networks" (QNNs), which is an advancement in the concept of neural networks in ML today. The main motive of all these researches is to build powerful models that can learn and perform faster, by combining quantum computing with ML.
