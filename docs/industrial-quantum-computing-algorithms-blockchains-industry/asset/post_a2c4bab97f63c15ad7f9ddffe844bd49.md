## Chapter 1
## Quantum computing in society: impacts and implications

**Abstract:** Recently, there has been a lot of interest in and funding for quantum computing in the academic and business communities. The “new race to the moon” is no greater task than constructing a workable quantum computer. Since this technology can crack any encryption methods used today, it is of great interest to national authorities, academics, and sellers of future quantum technologies. If this technology were to mature, it may have far-reaching and disruptive consequences for our society. However, it is quite probable that many areas of quantum computing will benefit from quantum computing. This chapter lays out our plans for how software-programmable quantum computers might revolutionize scientific computing in the future. This leads us to believe that quantum computers, similar to graphics processing units and coprocessor cards, will be integrated into hybrid-accelerated computing platforms shortly. Specifically, we discuss how quantum algorithms could revolutionize the field of applied mathematics. Lastly, we illustrate the potential societal effect of quantum-accelerated computational science with many cases.

**Keywords:** Quantum computing, Cryptography, Quantum bits, Quantum Supremacy, Healthcare, Economy, Business

## 1.2 Quantum computing

In quantum computing, which is grounded in the principles of quantum physics, data is represented and processed using qubits, which are quantum bits, rather than conventional bits. Because of this, quantum computers can factor in huge numbers and other specific kinds of computations far more quickly than conventional computers. Number theory and cryptography are two areas of mathematics that stand to benefit from the advent of quantum computing [12].
