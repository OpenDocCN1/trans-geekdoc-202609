The present fourth industrial revolution, known as Industry 4.0, focuses on utilizing the Internet of things (IoT), machine learning (ML), and artificial intelligence (AI) to achieve digital transformation in smart, linked manufacturing ecosystems. Industry 4.0 raises concerns over potential job displacement due to extensive automation [15–17].

Industry 5.0 is regarded as the subsequent stage of development in which human cooperation will be reintroduced to establish an environmentally friendly production system that can be maintained over time [11]. Elon Musk, an entrepreneur and CEO of Tesla Inc., has acknowledged the drawbacks of automation in highly automated factories. The new age within the contemporary healthcare world has appeared using a convergence of quantum computing and digital technologies as unconventional networks, productivity, and innovation are applied. Industry 5.0, the latest innovation regarding industrial technologies, has emerged as a reform force that will change the healthcare system’s landscape. The digital age brings tremendous opportunities and challenges as healthcare systems become more dependent on connected networks of smart devices that rely upon AI to deliver patient care. These include the need to harden healthcare systems against increasing cyberthreats and breaches that threaten patient information, operational integrity, and, ultimately, patient mobility [6–8, 14].

The key role of Industry 5.0 technologies, such as quantum computing, IoT, AI, and blockchain, is shaping the digital transformation for healthcare organizations. It is more about comprehending the great array of cybersecurity risks brought on by this digital ecosystem and crucially, deciphering a unique approach to implementing technological advancement [1, 18].

From IoT devices with remote prompting to the impressive capabilities of AI-enabled statistical analysis in cyberthreat detection and blockchain technology’s unchangeable, quantum-secure promises for health transactions, each of these advancements offers significant benefits as technology progresses towards enhanced cybersecurity [21].

The tour will encompass an examination of the present cybersecurity challenges in the healthcare sector, as well as provide insights into innovative advancements that act as vigilant guardians against cyberthreats. Integrating cybersecurity structures, encoding, decentralized security scenarios, cooperative efforts, and developing quantum technologies among the public and commercial sectors will serve as essential foundations for enhancing the resilience of healthcare systems against rising threats.
