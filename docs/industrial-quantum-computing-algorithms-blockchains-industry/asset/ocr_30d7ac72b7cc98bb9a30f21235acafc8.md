# 12.5.4 Shortage of cybersecurity talent training

# 12.5.4.1 Cybersecurity workforce shortage

The shortage of professionals who can implement and sustain a comprehensive security program is greater than required in the healthcare industry. This weakness can erode proper identification and cyberthreat mitigation.

# 12.5.4.2 Staff training and awareness

Human error is also a major cause of cyber-attacks. It is important to ensure healthcare providers are trained and understand the principles before cybersecurity practices to prevent unintentional security breaches.

Addressing cybersecurity problems in the healthcare industry requires a holistic approach, combining technical solutions with robust policies and protocols, ongoing staff training, and outside collaboration. As the digital transformation in healthcare continues, it is essential to maintain a proactive and adaptive cybersecurity stance that ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of patient data.
