# 9.4 Application of QML in various fields

Nowadays, QML has a wide range of applications in different industries. Here, we have discussed some areas where QML is making an impact:

+   i. **Healthcare and medical research:** Quantum computing with ML plays an important role in enhancing the biomedical field. Various algorithms are used for drug discovery, increasing diagnostic accuracy, and improving early disease detection. Furthermore, it improves the processing of complex biomedical datasets, which increases the quality and accuracy of clinical research. Additionally, it provides accurate prediction of the toxicity in compounds, which enhances drug safety validations [9].
ii. **Optimization:** Quantum computing is also useful in optimization. Quantum algorithms can find optimal solutions that are fast and more efficient. It also improves resource allocation, classification, and scheduling [10].
iii. **Finance and investment:** Quantum computing is used in the banking and financial areas for portfolio optimization, risk management, and fraud detection, such as credit card fraud. It helps in minimizing risk and providing efficiency and accuracy in these domains [11].
iv. **Energy and materials science:** Quantum computing is very helpful in environmental science, particularly in the field of molecular modeling. Algorithms of quantum computing can improve the efficiency of renewable energy systems by allocating proper resources [12].
v. **Quantum chemistry and material optimization:** Quantum systems are used to simulate chemical reactions and the nature of materials at the atomic level precisely. QML algorithms are used to examine large datasets of material properties, which identify the ideal compositions and structures for applications.
vi. **ML and artificial intelligence (AI):** There are many quantum computing algorithms that are used to enhance ML by analyzing datasets more accurately. Algorithms of quantum computing are used in the ML and AI fields include QSVM and quantum principal analysis. These algorithms help in enhancing the fields of image processing and natural language processing [9].
net. **Cryptography:** Quantum computers improve upon the old-style cryptographic algorithms such as RSA and ECC. They solve problems more competently than traditional computers, such as factorization of numbers and discrete logarithms. Quantum-resistant cryptographic algorithms are being developed to ensure the security of data in the quantum era [9].
. **Environmental modeling and climate change: Climate modeling:** QML algorithms enhance climate models by integrating illustrations of physical processes more precisely, such as changing aspects of the atmosphere. These models improve the predictions of weather patterns, climate events, and long-term climate trends.
