## 4.2 Utilizing quantum computing in the healthcare sector

Quantum computing is a promising new discipline of computer science that might revolutionize medical research, diagnosis, and therapy. By using quantum physics, quantum computing has unmatched promise in healthcare across many applications. Quantum bits, entanglement, superposition, and interference give it an edge over classical computing in speed, parallelism, and quantum annealing. Quantum computing offers an interest to solve some of healthcare's biggest problems. Quantum computing speeds up chemical simulations, helping drug development researchers find and optimize novel drug possibilities [5–6]. Quantum machine learning speeds up biological dataset processing and improves prediction models, enabling customized therapy. Quantum computing's capacity to handle large datasets and complicated data dimensions can help us comprehend disease genetics in genomics and biomarker detection. Quantum-enhanced imaging might improve medical imaging by detecting diseases early and more precisely. Quantum computing may improve supply optimization, patient scheduling, and medicine dose, improving healthcare delivery. However, healthcare quantum integration issues must be acknowledged. Hardware limits, mistake correction, and ethics are these obstacles. These concerns show that quantum computing in healthcare is still young.
