Quantum algorithms could simulate in real time the exact doses of drugs needed to treat patients, so that the therapy is at optimal concentrations and especially according to the individual reaction of each patient to that treatment.

Currently, there are companies offering individual, low-cost genetic analysis, which includes reports on hereditary risks to diseases or the detection of genetic mutations that may alter health in the future.

Through genetic, metabolic, or molecular analysis, quantum computers can simulate how the patient will respond to certain treatments or can quickly modify the treatments administered according to the evolution of the disease in each patient, optimizing the therapy in real time.

Quantum computers can perform detailed analyses of biomarkers for each patient and determine whether a particular treatment might have a positive or negative response.

Quantum computers can accelerate the analysis of genetic data to identify variants that may be associated with diseases, and quantum algorithms can perform comparative analyses of genetic data extremely quickly and accurately to provide information on the risk of hereditary diseases.

# 6.6.3 Assistance in diagnosis and medical imaging

For both medical staff and patients, establishing the correct diagnosis as soon as possible for medical conditions is essential in order to prescribe the necessary treatment and medical evaluations along the way.

Quantum computers could provide fast and accurate diagnostic solutions based on analyzing medical data by performing complex operations in parallel.

Through dedicated diagnostic algorithms, quantum computers can analyze large volumes of medical databases to contribute to the early diagnosis of diseases, greatly reducing the time needed to establish diagnosis.

With high quantum computing speeds, quantum computers can evaluate and accelerate machine learning models in diagnostics, increasing the performance and accuracy of the diagnoses provided.

Every year, millions of X-rays are performed in hospitals around the world and in medical clinics. Medical imaging is essential in establishing diagnoses, and it is impossible for doctors to conduct detailed research in the history of databases to find diagnostic patterns.

Quantum computers, in addition to algorithms to remove erroneous images, which are in a high percentage, could quickly provide results obtained from the entire database to provide diagnostic assistance.

Also, quantum simulations can improve magnetic resonance image processing, which by segmenting and analyzing details can provide more accurate results.
