The generative models, which belong to a solid construction, can be used for the detection of complex patterns situated in a high-dimensional space. Every layer in the model adds the ability of the neural network to create instances that are similar to the trend it follows. The neural network does this by learning the data on a higher level of complexity. However, realizing precise probability within the complex models can sometimes only be possible by utilizing methods like Markov chain Monte Carlo (MCMC), which is tedious.

Quantum computers will play a vital role in this matter. In place of MCMC, their potential to tackle the same probability distributions may be much faster. Even though various limitations in quantum technologies exist, they may be merged with conventional computers to build "hybrid architectures," which implies real-world applications can be possible in the future. Such approaches take advantage of both classical and quantum computing; hence, are not hampered by all the difficulties of the available quantum hardware.
