# 10.5 Conclusion and recommendations

This paper assessed how AI and automation affect income inequality in BRICS. It was discovered that BRICS nations, AI and automation have resulted in enhanced efficiency and cost savings, but also in heightened income inequality by replacing low-wage and low-skill positions. This resulted in reductions in wages and salaries in the manufacturing and service sectors. AI and automation in the retail sector in India have resulted in the removal of low-paying jobs, leading to a rise in income inequality. In Brazil, automation improved agricultural productivity while displacing paid workers and widening wealth gaps. From 1992 to 2006, automation in Russia was also linked to a rise in income inequality. AI and automation have increased efficiency in the financial industry, but this has resulted in a widening income gap in South Africa by displacing jobs that require lower skills and pay. Also, AI systems support rural communities in multiple ways, such as identifying crop diseases and pests, providing personalized teaching, and evaluating and forecasting crop yields. Ultimately, although AI has enhanced education by promoting greater engagement, learning access and evaluations, it has also exacerbated poverty and financial inequalities within the educational sphere. Ultimately, AI and automation could lessen income inequality within the BRICS nations; however, numerous consequences must be taken into account. Although AI and automation are generally beneficial for BRICS countries, they can also bring about negative consequences like job loss, lower wages, and widening economic inequality. When utilizing AI, it is important to take into account poverty and economic inequality, which can exacerbate the marginalization of vulnerable communities. The utilization of AI and automation should focus on enhancing productivity and efficiency while also considering the impact on economic inequality within the BRICS nations. In conclusion, it is clear that AI and automation have played a major role in shaping the BRICS communities.

AI technology has been utilized in various sectors such as manufacturing, service industries, banking, retail, agriculture, and financial services. While AI and automation have improved efficiency and saved costs, they have also been linked to a rise in income inequality and poverty. Automation has led to the replacement of jobs that require little skill and pay low wages, which has led to a rise in economic inequality, while AI has surpassed human decision-making. Moreover, AI has been employed to address poverty and food insecurity, and to offer educational opportunities to disadvantaged individuals.
