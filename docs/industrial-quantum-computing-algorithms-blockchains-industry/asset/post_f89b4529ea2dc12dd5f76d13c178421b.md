# 13.2 Working mechanism of quantum computing

Quantum mechanics is an extremely specialized field that requires proficiency in all these fields: [16] quantum mechanics, information technologies, and electronics or electrical engineering.

- These are some elements that work on quantum computing:
- Quantum bits (qubits): Qubits can exist in both states concurrently; they differ from standard bits, which can only have a value of 0 or 1. Quantum computing may handle information in fundamentally different ways thanks to these special techniques.
- Quantum gates: Quantum gates are features applied to qubits that permit state modification. Although they are similar to traditional logic gates, the nature of quantum physics causes some significant differences. Quantum gates can operate on qubits in superposition, unlike conventional gates.
- Quantum circuits: These are combinations of sequences of gates that work with qubits or quantum bits, just like traditional circuits. However, because of the entanglement property, quantum circuits, in contrast to classical circuits, can function on different qubits at once.
- Quantum algorithms: These algorithms outperform classical algorithms in problem solving by taking advantage of the special qualities of qubits and quantum gates. Quantum algorithms can achieve previously unheard-of-levels of speed and efficiency in computing operations.
- Quantum hardware: This hardware realization of a quantum computer is referred to as quantum hardware. In present, there are multiple varieties of quantum hardware, such as geometric quantum bits, ion traps, and conductors.
