# 5.1.2 Beyond the numbers

Mobile technology has transformed everything we do, from our daily activities to how we work and interact with our environment. From the overview of 1G to the path-breaking innovation of 5G, it has been a remarkable manifestation of the genius of man and his contribution to the progress of our connected world. As progress, 6G + technologies [21] will bring us more exciting possibilities, and the boundaries in our connected world will forever disappear [2].
