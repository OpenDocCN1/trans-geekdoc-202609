# # Chapter 1
# Quantum computing in society: impacts and implications

**Abstract:** Recently, there has been a lot of interest in and funding for quantum computing in the academic and business communities. The “new race to the moon” is no greater task than constructing a workable quantum computer. Since this technology can crack any encryption methods used today, it is of great interest to national authorities, academics, and sellers of future quantum technologies. If this technology were to mature, it may have far-reaching and disruptive consequences for our society. However, it is quite probable that many areas of quantum computing will benefit from quantum computing. This chapter lays out our plans for software-programmable quantum computers might revolutionize scientific computing in the future. This leads us to believe that quantum computers, similar to graphics processing units and coprocessor cards, will be integrated into hybrid-accelerated computing platforms shortly. Specifically, we discuss how quantum algorithms could revolutionize the field of applied mathematics. Lastly, we illustrate the potential societal effect of quantum-accelerated computational science with many cases.

**Keywords:** Quantum computing, Cryptography, Quantum bits, Quantum Supremacy, Healthcare, Economy, Business

# ## 1.1 Introduction

Quantum computing is a novel and prominent field based on quantum physics and information technology. It might transform many aspects of the society, as noted by Wynn and Jones [1]. This chapter explores how revolutionary innovation has changed our civilization. Quantum computing offers exponential improvements to many things, which might revolutionize our lives and businesses.
