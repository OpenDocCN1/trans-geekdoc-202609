Mathematics, physics, and computer science are all combined in the topic of quantum computing It solves complicated issues more quickly than conventional computers by using quantum physics. Tasks like machine learning, optimization and mimicking physical systems might be beneficial for these computers. They may be useful in the future for financial portfolio optimization or chemical process simulation, tasks that are now beyond the capabilities of the most potent supercomputers [1].

Quantum mechanics or computing is the new field which studies the principles of quantum mechanics to execute calculations at a speed unimaginable using classical computers. The qubit is the quantum alternative to the binary bit, around the very concept of quantum computing is built. Although classical bits can only exist as either a 0 or 1, qubits can be in a superposition at the same time [2]. Such a feature is the basis for data parallelism, enabling quantum computers to simultaneously process large volumes of information at an incredible rate, resulting in an exponential enhancement in the computation capacity for a large number of varying problems. In such situations, the states of two qubits that have been entangled a single entity. Such an event permits quantum mechanics or computing to make calculations that could not have been done with the old standard ones due to high computational complexity [14].
