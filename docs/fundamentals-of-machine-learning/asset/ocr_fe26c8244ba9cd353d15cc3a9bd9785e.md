We will specifically explore how networks with many layers of neurons have advanced the capabilities of machine learning considerably, which is known as **deep learning**.

An example of a network type called a convolutional network is illustrated in Fig.1C. It is common that such networks have tens and hundreds of layers. Deep networks are a form of high-dimensional non-linear fitting, and preventing overfitting is a very important component in deep learning. Deep networks have many free parameters, and large data sets (big data) were important for the recent progress in this area, in combination with other techniques to prevent overfitting such as technique called dropout that we will discuss later. In general, one can think about preventing overfitting by restricting the range of parameters. While relying on big data is one simple solution, making complex systems work with a limited amount of training data is the much more interesting challenge.
