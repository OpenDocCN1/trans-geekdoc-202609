This example gives us the opportunity to revisit representations. In Chapter 8 we mentioned the bag of words representation. This representation consists of a large vector of the vocabulary with each component of this vector indicating the number of times this word occurs in the text. We want to represent the text itself as a sequence of words, which is a much better way to approach natural language processing (NLP). With a one-hot representation of a letter, we would have a high-dimensional yet very sparse representation. A sequence would then be a sequence of high-dimensional space vectors. Another way of word representations would be every word a unique identifier. While this helps with the dimensionality of the representation, this does not capture relations between words.
