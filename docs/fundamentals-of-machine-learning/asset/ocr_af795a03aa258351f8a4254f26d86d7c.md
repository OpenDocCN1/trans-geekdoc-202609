$e_{c_2} = \left(1/2 - 1/2 \ \text{erf}\left(\frac{XD}{\sqrt{2}\sigma}\right)\right) p(c_2). \quad (8.12)$

Thus, in the case illustrated in Fig. 8.1 with equivalent priors $p(c_1) = p(c_2) = 1/2$, unit variance $\sigma^2 = 1$, and a separation of $\mu = 2$, the best achievable accuracy is

$$\text{Acc} = (1 - \text{erf}(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}})/\2 \approx 0.84. \quad (8.13)$

As can be, the maximal possible accuracy of a classifier is not always 100 per cent.
