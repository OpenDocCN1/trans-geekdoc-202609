These data look more noisy than the previous data, although we know that this is not really noise butter, rather, unknown factors. The point of including this is basically that it does not really matter if this randomness may come from an *irreducible indeterminacy*; that is, true randomness that cannot be penetrated by further knowledge, or this noise might represent *epistemological limitations* as the lack of knowledge of hidden processes or limitations in observing states directly.
