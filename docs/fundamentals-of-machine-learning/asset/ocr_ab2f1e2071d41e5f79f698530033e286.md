In particular, it gets away from tabular methods which somewhat alleviate the curse of dimensionality as we can now deal with a finite set of parameters in an infinite (continuous) state space. Also, while we use a simple model here, we can combine this with the advancements in deep learning.

# 10.4.2 Deep Q-learning

At the time of the TD-Gammon, the MLP with one hidden layer has been the state of the art, elaborate models with more hidden layers have been difficult to train. However, deep learning has made major progress over several factors, including faster computers with specialized processors such as GPUs, larger databases with lots of training example, the rediscovery of convolutional networks, and better regularization techniques. The combination of deep learning with reinforcement learning has recently made major breakthroughs in AI. These breakthroughs have been demonstrated by playing Atari games and winning the chessboard game Go against a grandmaster by the deep learning system AlphaGo by DeepMind. The Atari games are a great example of learning directly from sensory data in an environment that is much more complex than typical low-dimensional environments to RL systems have been applied. Mastering the game is relevant as it has been considered one of the most challenging examples for AI and was thought to require deep intuition by the players. Before AlphaGo, computer versions of games have only been able to play on an advanced level.
