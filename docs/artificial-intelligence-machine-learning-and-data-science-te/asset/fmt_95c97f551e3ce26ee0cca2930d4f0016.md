## 10.1 Introduction

The photo-voltaic (PV) framework has become one of the most successful resources of renewable energy in recent years, due to technological advances. The PV device creates nonlinear properties. The major challenge in the PV system is therefore the technique of maximum power point tracking (MPPT) under standardized and increasing weather states. Partial shading conditions (PSC) is triggering multiple peaks in a P-V curve. This chapter presents a comparative study of two popular MPPT algorithms, namely Incremental Conductance (INC) and Perturb & Observe (P&O), under different operating conditions for application towards green energy for smart cities. The complete PV architecture is conceived using modeling/Simulink with a boost converter and MPPT algorithms. Under standard experiments and various meteorological conditions, both the suggested MPPT solutions have been tested to prove their efficiency by looking at their maximum power monitoring across the maximum power point.
