# ## Intelligent manufacturing system safety strategies: generating Lockout/Tagout sheets by Machine Learning – a case study

*Victor Delpa*, Kevin Chapron**, Jean-Pierre Kenné*, Lucas Hof*
*École de Technologie Supérieure, Montréal, Canada
(e-mail: victor.delpa1@etsmtl.ca, lucas.hof@etsmtl.ca)
** CONFORMAT, Chicicoomi, Canada
*(e-mail: kevin.chapron@conformat.com)

**Abstract:** Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) is a procedure that considerably reduces the risk of work-related accidents by isolating and securing the energy sources of industrial equipment. In the Industry 4.0 era, safety procedures must also evolve towards more intelligent procedures supported by technologies such as machine learning or the Internet of Things. The LOTO procedures indicate how to perform the security. Sheets, manually generated, can be generated automatically to optimize the LOTO procedure. This paper proposes a methodology to achieve an automatic generation of sheets. The first step extracts information from the LOTO sheets provided by the industrial partner and in a next step develop text mining methods and word similarity search. Several options are evaluated, which aims to generate sheets from the previously obtained data. Future steps of this research will be presented. In practice, an automatic generation of sheets would enable to implement the LOTO procedure for an an industrial device, which would reduce the risk of accidents.

#  1. INTRODUCTION

Workplace accidents that can have severe human and material consequences. In the industrial environment, the frequency of accidents should be minimized with modern methods and precautions. Manufacturing environments where accidents can occur are very varied. Workers in manufacturing are at the highest risk for injury and deaths (Bulazcelli et al., 2008). The National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health has published guidelines for energy control management and service. The procedures proposed in these guidelines are mandatory since 1988 when the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (Occupational Safety and Health Administration 1989) promoted the Hazard Energy Control (Lockout/Tagout) Standard. The standard requires employers to lock out and disconnecting equipment. Lockout/tagout is the locking of the energy isolating device (hydraulic, electrical, pneumatic, etc.) in order to
