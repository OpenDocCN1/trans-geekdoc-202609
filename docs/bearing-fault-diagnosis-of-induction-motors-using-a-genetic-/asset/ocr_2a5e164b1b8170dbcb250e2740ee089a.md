Here, sensitivity indicates how appropriately the model is detecting events in the positive class, whereas specificity measures how precise is the assignment to the positive class. Most of the time, sensitivity and precision are reported pairwise because type I errors are calculated by sensitivity and type II errors by precision. Finally, the F-measure is the harmonic mean of sensitivity and precision.
