## 1 Introduction

Agriculture plays an essential role in modern society and still represents the main source of livelihood for most people in low-income and developing countries. According to data provided by the World Bank[⁷](#fn7)

In this context, Distributed Ledger Technologies (DLTs)—such as blockchains and smart contracts—are gaining increasing attention as they come with the promise of boosting the overall sector and promoting inclusive market participation ([Natanelov et al., 2022](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_48)). In particular, the application of DLTs is expected to bring significant efficiency gains throughout the value chain by improving transparency and traceability ([Tripoli and Schmidhuber, 2020](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_70)) and redefining trust ([Powell et al., 2023](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_50)). Additional emerging technologies such as IoT, AI, and cloud computing— just to name some—are already being used to automatically collect food information along all stages of the supply chain, from production to distribution ([Feng et al., 2020](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_24); [Mondal et al., 2019](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_44); [Powell et al., 2023](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_50); [Tian, 2016](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_68), [2017](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_67)). Traditional supply chain traceability systems are mostly centralised and “rely on a single information point to store, transmit, and share the traceability information” ([Feng et al., 2020](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_24), p. 2), therefore, the data is vulnerable to tampering ([Powell et al., 2022](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_51); Tian, [2017](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_67)) and difficult for small farmers, consumers and other stakeholders to access ([Xiong et al., 2020](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_75)). As a distributed database system allowing for secure, transparent, and immutable recording of data, the application of DLTs is regarded as a solution to reinforce transparency, build trust across the supply chain ([Feng et al., 2020](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_24); [Tripoli and Schmidhuber, 2020](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_70)), and ultimately improve traceability and quality control ([Rocha et al., 2021](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_54); [Tripoli and Schmidhuber, 2018](#ch3.xhtml#ref3_71)).
