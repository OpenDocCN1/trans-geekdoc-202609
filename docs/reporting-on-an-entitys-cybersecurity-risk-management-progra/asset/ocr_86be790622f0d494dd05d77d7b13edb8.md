In 2018, the AICPA plans to introduce a new examination service, SOC for ven-dor supply chains, and a related attestation guide to provide application guid-ance to practitioners engaged to examine and report on system-level controls in the supply chain. The purpose of such an examination is to enable entities to better understand and manage external risks, including cybersecurity risk, related to their vendors and distribution networks.
