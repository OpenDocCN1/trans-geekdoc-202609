# 3.3. Blockchain technology

Blockchain is described as a distributed and digital ledger system that gives the concept of transparency and user privacy. The concept of Blockchain was introduced for the financial and business industry at first but now due to its features, it is being applied to other sectors as well where security has now become an issue (Rawat and Sah 2012). Blockchain technology is dependent on the value-to-value architecture where the data (in form of blocks) is joined using cryptography technique (chain). Figure 3.2 shows the publication classification of Blockchain in healthcare from 2017 to 2022.
