Blockchain has swiftly gained notoriety because of its ability to establish a massive, worldwide shared ledger ability to record everything of value, which was originally created as part of the technology that underpins Bitcoin. A Blockchain is a digital, public distributed log that keeps precise replicas from each of the channel’s users’ computers. New entries can be made by any party, and prior entries can be reviewed.

In a Blockchain, activities are recorded and transmitted one after another (hence it is named as “Blockchain”). Because previous transactions cannot be erased or falsified, encryption protects the linkages among blocks and its contents.
