## The Role of Smart Contracts and Blockchain Technology in Healthcare and Other Use Cases

Newly emerging technologies have encouraged every sector to adopt these technologies to increase their productivity. The traditional techniques have been replaced by digital techniques to overcome the limitations faced by the people. One of the emerging technologies is Blockchain technology, which offers multiple benefits to business enterprises. The smart contract is a Blockchain-based technology that overcomes the limitation experienced in traditional contracts. This chapter explains the concept of a smart contract and its role in different sectors in detail. The research begins with the introduction of smart contracts, moving forward to its challenges, the real-time companies using smart contracts, with some past historic attacks. Overall, this chapter's research consists of deeper knowledge about smart contracts, their platform and real-time applications.

### 10.1. Introduction

The evolution of technologies has unboxed tremendously easier and simpler applications. These applications have turned the traditional approaches into smarter ones. A similar example is a Blockchain-based technology known as “smart contracts” (Rouhani and Deters 2018). Emerging technologies have reduced the effort needed and saved a lot of time. The term smart contract came into existence to overcome the limitations faced in traditional contracts. A traditional contract is defined as a bunch of agreed-upon terms between two parties that are validated and signed by the two parties. Smart contracts replace the issues that occurred in traditional contracts. There are a few similarities as well as dissimilarities between smart contracts and traditional contracts (Toshev & Boteva 2020). The traditional contract systems are enforced by the law and agreed upon thoroughly by both parties. But the major difference is that traditional contract is based on natural human language, whereas smart contracts are based on digitally signed laws (IBM n.d.; Infosys n.d.; Investopedia 2022). There are multiple differentiating factors. These differences are as follows.

#### 10.1.1. Comparison between traditional contracts and smart contracts

##### 10.1.1.1. Automated contract

It takes more time to prepare, draft and formulate a traditional contract. Usually, it takes around one to three days to prepare the contract. Smart contracts need more time to code the contract. Smart contracts are time-consuming to develop but once deployed the contracts are automated. There are ready-made platforms present like Ethereum, Hyperledger Fabric, etc., that automate contracts.

##### 10.1.1.2. Contract remittance and execution

In traditional contracts, some obligations are applied to the parties to pay the overdue within the allocated time as an organizational effort. Whereas in smart contracts, the remittance of the contract is executed automatically once the contract is fulfilled. These values and statements are recorded in the form of code (Toshev & Boteva 2020).

##### 10.1.1.3. Low cost

In traditional contracts, the procedure fee is slightly higher as lawyers, contract makers and other third parties are involved. Smart contracts preferably require less cost.

##### 10.1.1.4. Data security and confidentiality

Traditional methods may not maintain the security and confidentiality of the data. Traditional contracts require paperwork that opens the door for entering threats. However, smart contracts enable a strict path for data security and confidentiality. They use Blockchain-based cryptographic protection to maintain the confidentiality and integrity of data.

##### 10.1.1.5. Physical presence

In traditional contracts, the two parties must be present physically; without their presence, the contract cannot be signed and it is impossible to proceed. In this ever-changing world, smart contracts are signed digitally anywhere and the contracts can be accessed anytime.
