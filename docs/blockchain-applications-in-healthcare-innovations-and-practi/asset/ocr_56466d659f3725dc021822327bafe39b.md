The tracing of Covid-19-positive cases in the community was the primary requirement of the authorities to keep the outbreak of the virus in check. With knowing or having the records of people who were tested positive with Covid-19 and their close contacts, we could identify the “hotspot” areas and try to prevent the further spread of the virus. The technology that was being used was not giving assurance in preserving privacy with the centralized design of the technology giving the government the ability to do mass surveillance. Now Blockchain having a decentralized system is being used to provide better protection for government documents. Similarly, immunity passports, also known as immunity certifications or recovery certificates, given to the positive patients after they have fully recovered from the infection and Covid-19 diagnoses, make use of Blockchain technology in storing and sharing patient’s information to only authorized users. For accessing sensitive documents, smart contracts are also being used in the form of an extra layer. The general properties of the Blockchain, which includes the applications for remote monitoring, tracking status and identity verification, are now being used.

# 3.7. Conclusion

The growing demand for health has led to the development of hospitals and medical institutions further leading to an increase in medical data and the need for managing it. Thus, we have discussed Blockchain technology in association with the medical sector. Health records including the personal details of the patients are to be kept confidential. These records are saved in “medical clouds” but can be misused by the other parties, for example by deleting the original data or modifying it. The problems related to the healthcare industry and how they can hamper technological advancements in the industry preventing it from growing have also been discussed in this chapter. Blockchain due to its various properties can be a solution to these problems, and Blockchain-based systems seem to exhibit higher robustness, scalability and immunity that allows secure data sharing and data privacy. Blockchain technology has now gained great attention in many countries and companies all over the world.
