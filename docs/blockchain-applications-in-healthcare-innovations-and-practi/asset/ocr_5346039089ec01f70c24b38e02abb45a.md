# 8.4.2. Interoperability

Medical records have traditionally been disconnected and stored in service provider silos. This is due to fact that electronic health records (EHRs) were designed episode by episode, without taking into consideration the linkages needed for lifetime records or many service providers, and hence lack linkages, common designs and standards. There is no system in place for sharing medical data with stakeholders or even patients. As a result, the patient's regular proven records cannot be modified into such at other periods if competing health services remain accessible. Both patients and medical care providers benefit greatly from interoperability. Population health projects are made possible by data interoperability, as to help with these large-scale initiatives, a chain may also be used to collect massive volumes of health information.
