Antibiotic resistance is becoming a growing issue for people all over the world, even though the excessive use of these life-saving treatments stimulates the creation of antibiotic-resistant superbugs. Multidrug-resistant bacteria may wreak havoc in hospitals, killing thousands of people every year. The United States health service spends $5 billion each year and puts the lives of over 30,000 individuals in jeopardy. Data from electronic health records could help detect infection patterns and identify people who are at risk, before they display symptoms. The use of machine learning and artificial intelligence to drive these analytics will improve their accuracy and provide health firms with easier, more accurate notifications.
