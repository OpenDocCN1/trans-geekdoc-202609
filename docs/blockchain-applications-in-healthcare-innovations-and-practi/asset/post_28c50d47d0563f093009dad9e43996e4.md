## 3.4. Blockchain applications that can be useful for treating the medical sector problems

### 3.4.1. Smart contracts

Employing smart contracts, the user can form or create agreements between the buyer and the seller, by using Blockchain technology. Smart contracts, stored in Blockchain network, encrypt and convert the transaction into legal documents for legal services. These smart contracts consist of the scripts that are present in the Blockchain network, and they can be traced or identified by their unique address. By means of these smart contracts, we can establish a fair exchange among the parties while having minimum communication between them, in a decentralized manner. Thus, by this, the user can form their document without the need of a legal representative thus removing the challenges including both financial and time-wise, and making a better transfer of the resources or transaction.

### 3.4.2. Fraud detection

It is the process that verifies the document or the data in the system to check if there is any kind of tampering with information or malicious conduct, for example, the false comments and ratings which are given in the online review system in the form of bad-mouthing as well as the scams happening in the financial industry in the form of loan applications, etc. The idea of fraud detection can also be applied in the concept of crowdfunding where we have a bunch of people who are investing or buying stocks from the company. Blockchain technology can be used as a low-cost platform for registering shares and stocks and thus makes transactions quite secure, effective, efficient and easier.

### 3.4.3. Identity verification

The government system uses passports and the fingerprints of an individual to identify the person but Blockchain technology can provide an alternative to these identification methods (Khanna et al. 2020). Blockchains can identify the user, for example, using Blockchain in notarizing marriages, birth certificates and business contracts. For a person can prove their existence at a point in time, Blockchain would use its “distributed ledger” property and is further verified by a group of people by distributed nature of the Blockchain technology. For example, we can use Blockchain technology to validate marriages, birth certificates and business contracts.

## 3.5. Examples of healthcare platforms using Blockchain

### 3.5.1. Data sharing using Gem Health Network

As discussed earlier, the healthcare sector contains the records of patients and stores them in a centralized database, which can lead to issues such as data security and interoperability. If a patient needs the records when going through a specialized treatment either in another hospital, interstate or overseas, then the sharing of data becomes a problem. Every time a person sees another doctor, there is a need to form a new set of records and update several existing records, changing the communication protocols between the doctors, and also there is IT interference which is very time-consuming. Hence, there has been a solution given out for the problem by the Gem Health Network that uses Ethereum Blockchain Technology and creates a shared network infrastructure. This network helps different professionals in the healthcare sector to access the same information and records of the patients, unlike the centralized database. Access to the latest treatment information (which is transparent and relevant) is only given to the authorized users. This helps in reducing the risk of neglecting a person’s health issues due to misinformation or outdated health records.

For example, we have “Estonia, who has collaborated with Guardtime that uses Blockchain technology. Thus, all the medical professionals, insurance companies and citizens can have access to the health records and treatments that have been performed in Estonia with the help of this technology, indicating a way in which Blockchain can be applied similarly as stated above".

### 3.5.2. MeDshare

MeDshare is a platform that is designed for sharing the data and records amongst medical professionals who store their data and records in a not so trustworthy environment. This system looks after the basic requirements in the medical sector such as maintaining the patient’s privacy and risk of malicious activities on the medical records. MeDshare system uses the Blockchain technology providing data management, auditing and data provenance in the cloud-based environment among the companies. The system is designed in such a way that smart contracts and access control mechanisms are used to keep a track of data and to retract any malicious entity on the discovery of any kind of misbehavior in the system. The main aim of MeDshare is to provide the cloud providers (that store the medical records) with the capability of data management, auditing and data sharing to the medical industry without leaking the private information of the patient. This system is divided into four layers.

The first layer consists of the arrangements that are made for the users who are allowed to access the medical records.

The second layer (data query layer) consists of certain queries for accessing, processing, forwarding and responding to the requests. Here, the processes are broken down into two components – the first component being the system consisting of queries that are in control for generating requests, and the second component being the trigger that deciphers the transactions to and from the smart contracts.

The third layer (data structuring and provenance layer) involves processing of requests for accessing the information or data from the existing database amidst various existents such as smart contracts, processing, authenticators, smart contracts, consensus nodes and Blockchain systems.

The fourth layer (existing database infrastructure) comprises the database that is executed by individuals to complete their tasks.

### 3.5.3. OmniPHR

OmniPHR is a model to handle the health records and information of the patients, giving them a clear view of the records stored across multiple health providers. It helps in keeping data up to date when it is stored in another system or modified by another provider. OmniPHR addresses the difference between the EHR and PHR. EHRs are set up according to the government standards that help to have uniform records over the country; this makes the records accurate and up to date. The EHRs are updated on a regular basis by medical professionals without the involvement of patients, unlike PHR in which the patients keep a track of their records. Here, OmniPHR creates a framework that helps in addressing this problem where the patients can have a comprehensive look at their records alongside the medical community that can have the desired accuracy of the records. It makes use of Blockchain technology with the focus of unifying data that has been distributed over several organizations. PHR would be stored in an organized way hierarchically, encrypted and distributed in data blocks present on the network. The user can access the OmniPHR records from anywhere – home, work or hospital. Some of the cloud computing features that are used, scalability, elasticity, middleware, encryption protocols, open standards, etc., are included in the model. The data flow of the model is data provided to open standards, and then it is changed by middleware technologies and encrypted and authorized by privacy and security modules before storing it in the series of repositories.

## 3.6. Blockchain during the Covid-19 pandemic

In today’s world, the one which we refer to as the “global village”, there has been a massive rise in health diseases and health issues, due to the sedentary lifestyle of the people and many factors including ecological and environmental factors. Recently, the Covid-19 virus has been wreaking havoc all across the globe since December 2019 leading to more than 54 million people affected and over 1,300,000 deaths. This pandemic has overburdened healthcare systems all over the world. Because of its significant features which include decentralization, immutability, transparency and auditability, Blockchain has turned out to be very useful in technology.

> European Parliament Research Service considers the Blockchain technology to be one of the top 10 vital technologies to mitigate Covid-19 challenges.

The applications of the Blockchain technology used to mitigate Covid-19 challenges have been discussed in the paper by Abd-alrazaq et al. (2020). The paper jots down 10 cases of the Blockchain technology to tackle the situations that have emerged due to the coronavirus, out of which six studies discuss contact tracing, i.e. the process that traces down those who were in close contact with the people who tested positive with Covid-19, and the remaining four studies discuss the application of immunity passports, which will show that a person is free from the disease
