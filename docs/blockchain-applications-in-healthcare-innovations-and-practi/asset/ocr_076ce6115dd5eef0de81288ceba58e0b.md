When a block is formed, it is ser to all of the platform's peers for approval. Miners use complex algorithms to solve the extremely difficult mathematical problem of producing an approved hash using a nonce. Because the nonce is only 32 bits long and the hash is 256 bits long, there are roughly 4 billion nonce-hash permutations to mine before finding the proper one. Miners are believed to have uncovered the “golden nonce” when this happens, and the block is included in the chain. When a block is mined correctly, the update is authorized by all servers on the platform, and the miner is rewarded economically. Following acceptance, a current chain is modified with the newly created block. After being added to the chain, the update is disseminated around the system and the action is completed. _Public Blockchain_: it is a distributed ledger scheme with no permissions. Everyone who has an Internet connectivity can join the Blockchain network. A user (also known as a node) who is a member of the public Blockchain is allowed to get the data, validate transactions and mine for the next block. The sharing of cryptocurrency is one of the most popular applications of shared Blockchains. The majority of the time, public Blockchain is safe, if users adhere to safety protocols. However, if consumers do not adhere to the security protocols, it can be dangerous. Bitcoin, Ethereum and Litecoin are some of the most well-known examples (Andreev et al. 2018; Aggarwal et al. 2019).
