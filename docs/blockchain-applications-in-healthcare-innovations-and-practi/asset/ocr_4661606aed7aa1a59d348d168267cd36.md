Finding an appropriate SDLC model is a very challenging task. An efficient SDLC model aids in the improvement of software product quality while reducing cost and time. Kneuper (2017) discussed various software development life-cycle models and provided a detailed overview of these models’ development. This paper describes how SDLC affects the rise of software processes. According to the author, an effective life-cycle model resolves the productivity and scalability problem in software development time. The author also discussed a new type of life-cycle model known as Kanban and Devops. This new life-cycle model helps to decrease the process time and improve the quality of the software process. Ragnarath et al. (2010) presented various life-cycle models and gave the comparative analysis between them. According to the authors, incident management, change management and release management are the key issues of any SDLC models. To resolve these issues, the authors have proposed a hypothetical model called SDLC model 2010. This model is based on the three-dimensional model concept, which has transformed the conventional SDLC model’s concept of user–developer interaction into a three-dimensional model that includes developers, users and owners. Bhuvaneswari and Prabaharan (2013) discussed various SDLC models and gave suggestions, in particular situations, about what SDLC is best used in the developing project. They discussed the advantages and disadvantages of various models, such as the waterfall model, prototyping model, agile model, spiral model, iterative model, V model, rapid application development model and Big Bang model.

Despa et al. (2014) discussed various software development characteristics and gave the advantages and disadvantages of the various software development models. According to the authors, in any software development method, a proper development phase is the main aspect of the success of the software. It provides a formal software development methodology that helps in the innovation of the software industry. This paper shows the comparative study of different development methodologies and highlights their main characteristics. Dora and Dubey (2013) and Barjtya et al. (2017) discussed various software development life-cycle models in detail, such as the waterfall model, spiral model, agile model and V model. In these papers, the authors discussed some advantages and disadvantages of the above models. They also provided a comparative study between the agile model and other existing models. The authors said that the agile model helps to increase productivity and performance, and decrease time complexity and risk analysis. The authors also presented an overview of the agile model and discussed different agile development methods such as feature-driven development, extreme programming and crystal methods, etc.
