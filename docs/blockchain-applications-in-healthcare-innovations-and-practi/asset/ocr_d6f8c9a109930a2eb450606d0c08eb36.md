# 11.3. Application of Blockchain in the healthcare industry

Let us talk about the various use cases of Blockchain in the healthcare industry. Starting with the pharmaceutical industry, security and stability are given the highest priority. Blockchain implementation can improvise supply chain management monitoring and recording by reducing the scope for human error and delays. By keeping track of the products to prevent the flow of falsified drugs in the market, which can lead up to $200 billion loss, the products can be verified. Many big companies have already taken advantage of this technology to have more effective solutions. Some of these companies are Blockpharma, Chronicles and Modum. The proposed solution is the creation of a sensor that keeps track of the physical conditions that the product goes through. These records are permanently stored on the Blockchain for future reference.

DNA data, also called genomic data, is an entirely new market that requires Blockchain security. The secure exchange of genomic data can be empowered with the assistance of a Blockchain organization. As this is a developing business sector, there are many use cases that can emerge from assortment, stockpiling and examination using Blockchain. This market alone could cost billions of dollars. What makes Blockchain a fitting choice here is its decentralization, permanence and transparency properties. Clinical records and data are significant and straightforward, and approved clients have continuous access to the latest treatment data (Mettler 2016). In addition, there are data avoidance methods that give protection, traceability and respectability to the common data. Furthermore, patients are generally mindful of who is accessing their data (Yue et al. 2016).
