# 5.8.3. Improving medical record keeping with Blockchain

phrOS (phrOS – Healthcare Blockchain Operating System) was recently launched by Taipei Medical University Hospital and Digital Treasury Corporation (DTCO) (see DTCO n.d.). It attempts to promote openness among medical organizations by storing all of a patient’s medical records on a Blockchain. It contains photos as well as numerous pieces of information on a patient’s condition. A smartphone app allows clinicians and patients to view the information. It also improves the security of medical data by using Decentralized Ledger Technology (DLT). The open framework allows for autonomous and secure solutions, making smart health a reality. It generates an anonymized digital identity for patients to aid in patient verification and identification, as well as patient data recording and querying, covering in treatment and medication planning. Smart contracts can be used in clinical trials for protocol changes, trial enrollment delays and so on. Patient-authorized claims, including timestamps and medical records, as well as all authorized files, have digital signatures that can be evaluated at any time without the need for third-party services to resolve conflicts.
