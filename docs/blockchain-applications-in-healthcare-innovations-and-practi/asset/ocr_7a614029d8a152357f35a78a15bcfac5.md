# 10.7.2. Fizzy AXA

The French Airline AXA built flight insurance over the Blockchain platform. AXA ideated a simple way that benefits a wide range of travel insurers. It is difficult to work with airline companies that are crediting compensations for late flights. Whether it is flight travel insurance or the reason for the delay of a flight, it is the biggest challenge to get compensation without any delay. Fizzy AXA was initiated to resolve this problem. It outsources specific conditions that can be applied for compensations. Fizzy says that if a flight is delayed for more than two hours, this information is loaded onto the app, then the desired compensation is credited directly to the bank account. This contemporary feature is possible only because of smart contracts. Here, the use of smart contracts is made by setting some parametric insurance, where users are not allowed to take compensation for the total loss, rather than compensating the traditional loss. This flight insurance is based on a triggering action, which is the flight being delayed for two hours. This condition is only available for flights between the U.S and Paris, even with an extended plan till 2018. To avail these products, it is mandatory to pay a subscription fee (PolySwarm 2018).

# 10.7.3. Etherparty

Etherparty is one of the leading startups. They are referred to as “The Smart Makers”. This tech startup permits users to build their smart contracts on the platform. Etherparty contains the wizard of contracts and holds around 500 pre-registered users who can access uncountable templates of contracts. It has designed the templates elegantly, so that users do not need prior knowledge about smart programming to create a contract. The users have the flexibility to create several kinds of contracts, such as peer-to-peer escrow contracts, tokens, real estate agreements, Supply Chain contractor’s agreements and additionally, any other contract based on any domain. Etherparty assures the company about the quality of the product. It ensures that the company adopts the technologies easily, escalating processes and mitigating risk (PolySwarm 2018).
