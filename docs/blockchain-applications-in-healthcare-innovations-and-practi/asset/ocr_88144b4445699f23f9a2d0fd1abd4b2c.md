# 1.4. Development of Blockchain: A state of art

The Blockchain is a distributed chain of blocks that keeps track of all transactions across several computers around the world, prevents its alteration and, after that, is recorded in such a manner that Blockchain improves data security (Kuo et al. 2017) while also making data interchange more cost-effective and public. It also assigns third parties for whom the primary function is to provide a certificate aspect between transactions and trust (like as in banks and notaries).
