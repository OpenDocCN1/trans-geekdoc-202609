+   4) compile and run the code;

+   5) finally, test the code to check whether the contract is working fine.

# 10.6. Real-time use cases of smart contracts

Smart contracts have multiple use cases in various industries, but the ultimate use cases where the role of a smart contract is reflected are mentioned below.

# 10.6.1. Smart contracts and insurance

For insurance companies, it is difficult to manage several contracts conducted between the customers and the company. These contracts contain terms and conditions that need to be signed by both parties. The core requirements focus on the insurance claim, reimbursement of expenses and renewal of insurance. These industries are looking for an automation solution that reduces paperwork, invites events and activates the agreement. Smart contracts in insurance industries handle the entire procedure of insurance, beginning from the claim of policy or executing automated actions as per the agreement. Smart contracts settle the payout efficiently. These insurance contracts are hard-coded ones that cannot be altered later due to strict security (Grosso 2019; ONIXX 2018).

# 10.6.2. Smart contracts in an electric vehicle

Sooner or later, smart contracts will be used in the transport or automotive sectors. Due to high rates of fuel, electric vehicles will be preferred most. These electric vehicles are associated with IoT sensors, and smart contracts so that the recharge of electric vehicles becomes faster. Smart contracts provide flexibility to business processes so that the users can securely make the payments to recharge their electric vehicles (Grosso 2019).

# 10.6.3. Smart contracts in the energy sector

The flow of energy depends on the act’s decentralization, decarbonization and digitalization. The energy consumers, as well as the energy producers need turning energies that can upgrade more resources. Therefore, energy sectors are even looking for advancement to secure their resources and make them faster. Smart contracts play a vital role in defining the distributed energies as per the agreed terms. Smart contracts even facilitate easy integrations with IoT devices (Grosso 2019).
