# 10.2. Ethereum: Generation Two of Blockchain technology

Blockchain (Khanna et al. 2021; Sah et al. 2021) developers have thought of creating a simpler interface for smart contracts compared to existing ones. The newly emerged second generation of Blockchain (Khanna et al. 2020a, 2020b), known as Ethereum, was evolved to ideate assets that would add more benefits to Blockchain technology. Initially, to define Ethereum, it is a decentralized open-source platform that enables smart contract functionality. After Bitcoin, the second-largest evolution of cryptocurrencies in the market is Ethereum. Its evolution took place in 2013 by the programmer Vitalik Buterin. This Blockchain technology had a boost in 2014, with a supply of 72 million coins in the beginning. This platform permits the developers to build and operate tasks that users rely on for interaction (Corporate Finance Institute n.d.; NTU 2020).

Ethereum is defined as the public open-source Blockchain platform that enables the functionality of smart contracts and is another leading platform in the cryptocurrency market with a secured environment (Corporate Finance Institute n.d.; NTU 2020). Ethereum holds specifically two accounts, one is an externally owned account and the other is a contract account. The externally owned account can be controlled with the help of a private key.

# 10.2.1. History of Ethereum

The founder of Ethereum, Vitalik Buterin, was first a Bitcoin programmer in 2011; he initially worked for Bitcoin Magazine. Later, he realized some other platforms that can bring a distinct view towards Blockchain technologies. In 2013, Ethereum was established as a general scripting language (NTU 2020; Corporate Finance Institute n.d.). In 2014, the team at Ethereum launched a campaign where they sold Ethers to check how it affects the world. At the same time, they raised 80 million dollars (Corporate Finance Institute n.d.; NTU 2020).
