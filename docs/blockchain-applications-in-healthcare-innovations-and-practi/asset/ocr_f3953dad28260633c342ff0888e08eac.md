The primary purpose is to apply Blockchain technology to EHRs by addressing its key properties in order to provide tamper-proof, distributed, irrevocable, transparent, reliable, and open to scrutiny remote medical care. Furthermore, the chapter discussed the potential benefits of Blockchain technology in medical systems. It also includes a discussion of the current Blockchain-based electronic health records that doctors use to store health data. The chapter finishes with a list of challenges that must be solved in order to improve the electronic health record system through the use of upcoming Blockchain-based networks.
