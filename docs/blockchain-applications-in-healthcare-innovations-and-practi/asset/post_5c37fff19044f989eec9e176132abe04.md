Many research organizations are trying to develop a software system with the latest technology to increase our trust and secure data exchanges. Blockchain technology is a system that has the above features. A Blockchain is defined as a distributed digital ledger. It contains several blocks. These blocks are interlinked by hash cryptography in a peer-to-peer network. Every block of the Blockchain contains a block number, time stamp, nonce, data in the form of Merkle root and hash of the previous block, as shown in Figure 9.2. Every block is immutable, so there is no possibility of malicious attacks in the future. This technology was firstly introduced by Haber and Stornetta in 1991, and the first application was introduced by Satoshi Nakamoto in 2008 in the form of cryptocurrency for peer-to-peer transactions, known as Bitcoin (Nakamoto 2009). In Blockchain, data can be stored as a block that contains any type of data such as text, video and any kind of transaction. When a new block wants to join this Blockchain, its information is broadcast over the entire peer network, where miners are ready to mine this new block. After mining, a valid block is added to the Blockchain. Each Blockchain contains a set of rules known as a consensus algorithm (PoW, PoS). With the help of a consensus algorithm, the miners validate the new block and decide which block is added to or discarded from the Blockchain. This technology is becoming more popular because of its features, which include security, decentralization, immutability and transparency. Blockchain has the potential to solve many problems related to healthcare applications, such as a lack of disability of transition, trust and security for the patient record. It increases the trust without any intermediaries and provides a new business model. Due to its potential, Blockchain technology has attracted the healthcare industry to develop a healthcare application using it. It can be used in different fields of healthcare such as supply chain management of medical goods, patient record management, pharmaceutical research, telemedicine, etc. (Katuwal et al. 2018; De Aguiar et al. 2020). In Blockchain technology, data is stored in immutable form and cannot be deleted or altered. Hence, using Blockchain technology in healthcare software increases the trust, reliability, traceability and security of the patient record.

## 9.2. Literature review

Every new technology has helped to increase human productivity, performance and its prosperity. Many industries have been attracted due to this new technology, and the healthcare industry is one of them. Technology has brought about massive changes in the healthcare industry today. Due to this, users can now choose the best medical devices according to their requirement. The increases in demand for software related to healthcare applications provide many benefits to patients and doctors. With the help of these applications, the hospital can easily create a database related to the patient's medical records and treatment records; the doctors can easily study diseases and diagnose them quickly and accurately. Using healthcare software and its applications, we can easily perform various activities, such as blood testing, brain testing, body scanning, Covid-19 testing, etc. Remote consultation and different mobile apps have also led to improving patient care and giving better results for the patient. The main aim of implementing healthcare applications is to provide a lower cost, minimize error, improve patient experiences, easily update their records and also trust these sensitive patient records. However, technology innovation and the high expectations of the patient create new challenges for the medical organization, such as quality, privacy, security and trust of the patient data. Hence, the user searches for better-quality products and services.

Healthcare applications always demand the best technology, methodology and a software development model to resolve the above problem. A proper software development cycle model is essential for any software to become successful. An SDLC model is a software engineering process used to design, develop, test and deploy any software system. In other words, we can say that SDLC is considered as a framework that provides a sequence of activities for the system developer, as well as system designer. SDLC contains different phases that are essential for developing any software system, such as requirement analysis, design, coding and testing. Different SDLC models are suitable for specific projects that depend on some conditions such as which organization uses it, the stability of the requirement, the risk factor, budget and time duration of the project (Rastogi 2015; Gajalaksmi 2016). It gives perspective characteristics that provide suggestions of how to develop a software system.

Finding an appropriate SDLC model is a very challenging task. An efficient SDLC model aids in the improvement of software product quality while reducing cost and time. Kneuper (2017) discussed various software development life-cycle models and provided a detailed overview of these models’ development. This paper describes how SDLC affects the rise of software processes. According to the author, an effective life-cycle model resolves the productivity and scalability problem in software development time. The author also discussed a new type of life-cycle model known as Kanban and Devops. This new life-cycle model helps to decrease the process time and improve the quality of the software process. Ragnarath et al. (2010) presented various life-cycle models and gave the comparative analysis between them. According to the authors, incident management, change management and release management are the key issues of any SDLC models. To resolve these issues, the authors have proposed a hypothetical model called SDLC model 2010. This model is based on the three-dimensional model concept, which has transformed the conventional SDLC model’s concept of user–developer interaction into a three-dimensional model that includes developers, users and owners. Bhuvaneswari and Prabaharan (2013) discussed various SDLC models and gave suggestions, in particular situations, about what SDLC is best used in the developing project. They discussed the advantages and disadvantages of various models, such as the waterfall model, prototyping model, agile model, spiral model, iterative model, V model, rapid application development model and Big Bang model.

Despa et al. (2014) discussed various software development characteristics and gave the advantages and disadvantages of the various software development models. According to the authors, in any software development method, a proper development phase is the main aspect of the success of the software. It provides a formal software development methodology that helps in the innovation of the software industry. This paper shows the comparative study of different development methodologies and highlights their main characteristics. Dora and Dubey (2013) and Barjtya et al. (2017) discussed various software development life-cycle models in detail, such as the waterfall model, spiral model, agile model and V model. In these papers, the authors discussed some advantages and disadvantages of the above models. They also provided a comparative study between the agile model and other existing models. The authors said that the agile model helps to increase productivity and performance, and decrease time complexity and risk analysis. The authors also presented an overview of the agile model and discussed different agile development methods such as feature-driven development, extreme programming and crystal methods, etc.
