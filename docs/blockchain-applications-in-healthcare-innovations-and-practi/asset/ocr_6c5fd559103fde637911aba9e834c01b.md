# 4.6. Discussion

Worldwide, healthcare systems are under tremendous stress due to the pandemic, which has resulted in supply chain disruptions, counterfeit medical products, health data management and security, among other issues. New and emerging technologies, such as Blockchain, the technology behind cryptocurrencies, can address these issues (Saranya and Murugan 2021). This study will identify the challenges that healthcare sectors will face in implementing a BC system. The three challenges identified are mainly the technical challenges, social challenges and organizational challenges. TC stands for technical challenges faced by the BC adopters. These challenges have been identified in previous studies (Sadhy and Sadhya 2018; Onik et al. 2019; Pandey and Litoriya 2020; Saranya and Murugan 2021). SC stands for the social challenges faced by the BC adopters. These challenges and results have been identified in previous studies (Shah et al. 2018; Shahnaz et al. 2019; Siyal et al. 2019; Wamba and Queiroz 2020; Wong et al. 2020). OC stands for organizational challenges faced by the BC adopters. These challenges and results have been identified in previous studies (Zhang et al. 2018; Zhao et al. 2019; Wong et al. 2020; Yaqoob et al. 2021). Blockchain adoption in healthcare faces several challenges, including technical issues and ownership concerns (Kant Pal et al. 2021). Technological challenges such as processing speed and massive data duplication will be resolved as technology develops (Zhang et al. 2018). As a result of its benefits in payment integrity, data interoperability and security, Blockchain can transform the healthcare ecosystem. A time-consuming and labor-intensive pharmaceutical research process, for example, would be transformed into a more efficient, data-driven one (Tanwar et al. 2020). Assigning patient control, ownership and access to patient data using a Blockchain solution would make this possible.

As part of the study, healthcare companies will be asked to describe their challenges when implementing BC in their systems. Many industrial sectors use BC to improve transparency and tracking. After completing a structured literature review, a list of issues facing healthcare organizations was compiled for the research project. An Indian hospital is currently working on a survey-based research questionnaire. For data analysis and modeling, exploratory factor analysis and structural equation modeling are used. As a result, more challenges can be identified by extending this study into other fields.
