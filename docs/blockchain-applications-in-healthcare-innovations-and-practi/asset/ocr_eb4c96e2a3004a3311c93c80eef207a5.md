With the better and advanced models storage, tracking and management of payment systems and others, a significant change can be brought in the various industries and services. However, other than the decentralized storage property, Blockchain technology offers much more valuable propositions such as much more advanced and innovative investment models and crypto-economic currencies, radically new types of advanced models for governance which can generate further development in the age where digital platforms and business interactions have multi-stakeholders.
