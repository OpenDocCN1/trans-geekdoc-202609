EHRs are set up according to the government standards that help to have uniform records over the country; this makes the records accurate and up to date. The EHRs are updated on a regular basis by medical professionals without the involvement of patients, unlike PHR in which the patients keep a track of their records. Here, OmniPHR creates a framework that helps in addressing this problem where the patients can have a comprehensive look at their records alongside the medical community that can have the desired accuracy of the records. It makes use of Blockchain technology with the focus of unifying data that has been distributed over several organizations. PHR would be stored in an organized way hierarchically, encrypted and distributed in data blocks present on the network. The user can access the OmniPHR records from anywhere – home, work or hospital. Some of the cloud computing features that are used, scalability, elasticity, middleware, encryption protocols, open standards, etc., are included in the model. The data flow of the model is data provided to open standards, and then it is changed by middleware technologies and encrypted and authorized by privacy and security modules before storing it in the series of repositories.

# 3.6. Blockchain during the Covid-19 pandemic

In today’s world, the one which we refer to as the “global village”, there has been a massive rise in health diseases and health issues, due to the sedentary lifestyle of the people and many factors including ecological and environmental factors. Recently, the Covid-19 virus has been wreaking havoc all across the globe since December 2019 leading to more than 54 million people affected and over 1300,000 deaths. This pandemic has overburdened healthcare systems all over the world. Because of its significant features which include decentralization, immutability, transparency and auditability, Blockchain has turned out to be very useful in technology.

> European Parliament Research Service considers the Blockchain technology to be one of the top 10 vital technologies to mitigate Covid-19 challenges.

The applications of the Blockchain technology used to mitigate Covid-19 challenges have been discussed in the paper by Abd-alrazaq et al. (2020). The paper jots down 10 cases of the Blockchain technology to tackle the situations that have emerged due to the coronavirus, out of which six studies discuss contact tracing, i.e. the process that traces down those who were in close contact with the people who tested positive with Covid-19, and the remaining four studies discuss the application of immunity passports, which will show that a person is free from the disease
