# 7.5.3. Advantages of using differential privacy in Blockchain

Now as we have already explained the concept of differential privacy, it will be easier to understand how it is useful in Blockchain. The fundamental features of Blockchain, which make it the next generation of secured storage, and using differential privacy in Blockchain will enhance the privacy of Blockchain. Some examples are given below:

+   - protection of sender and receiver addresses from punitive and feather forking attacks, and linking attacks;
+   - protecting network-wide privacy from a deanonymization attack;
+   - preserving data during consensus;
+   - protection from a trinity attack.

# 7.6. Privacy by Design

Privacy by Design and Privacy by Default are frequently used terminologies, especially since new privacy regulations started coming into the picture. Privacy by Design aims to integrate privacy requirements and controls in all new systems where personal information is in scope. This means Privacy by Design recommends that from the very start of a product/system development, privacy requirements shall be considered and shall be integrated in the design and architecture of the system, so that when a system is developed, all privacy controls are embedded in the system. On the other hand, Privacy by Default talks about post development, i.e. privacy settings shall be enabled by default once a product/system is deployed in production.

The concept of Privacy by Design was first discussed by the former Canadian Information and Privacy Commissioner Ann Cavoukian, as she created several principles as foundation of Privacy by Design. The purpose of these principles is to make privacy integral to priorities of the organization, project objectives and design process. The seven principles of Privacy by Design are discussed (Kurtz et al. 2018; Romanou 2018; Wahlstrom et al. 2020) in Figure 7.13.

From the Blockchain point of view, many of these principles can be easily followed because of the fundamental features of the Blockchain. However, it is highly recommended while creating any system/application using Blockchain to first list all privacy requirements and then create a map that will be automatically sufficed by Blockchain features, the one that needs to be covered in consensus algorithms and smart contracts, and finally the one that needs addon security/privacy features to be integrated into Blockchain to enhance its security and privacy capabilities such as differential privacy and proxy re-encryption.
