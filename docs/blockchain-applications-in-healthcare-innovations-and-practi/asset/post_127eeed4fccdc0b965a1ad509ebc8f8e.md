Because Blockchain operates on a confirmation interchange, all statements are easily known, and the contract is then implemented after the network has agreed. Since there is no centralized entity in the Blockchain design, there is a lower risk of mistakes or cheating.

## 4) Management of the supply chain

The design of Blockchain-based contracts can benefit healthcare businesses by tracking supply–demand cycles through supply chain management. The entire management includes monitoring how the transaction is happening, checking for the success of the contract and reporting if any transactions or delays are incorrect.

Connectivity is a pledge by the chain, which is frequently realized using complex APIs to create EHR compatibility and secure data storage.

The Blockchain networks, which are shared with approved service providers in a safe and standardized manner, are well known for removing the burden of data reconciliation from Blockchains. Other complex activities addressed by the Blockchain, namely through interoperability, include medicine supply management, fraud eradication, clinical studies or revenue cycle management transformation.

## 1.8. Open issues related to Blockchain

There are varieties of issues related to Blockchain associated with its applications. Some of these are mentioned below.

### Blockchain is incorporated with an environmental expenditure

Encryption is the mechanism that Blockchain uses to provide security and get consensus from a distributed network. The actual meaning is just to demonstrate if a person has the power to write down just onto the chain's intricate procedures which should be executed, each of which will require a massive amount of processing power. After all, of course, it comes with a cost. Using the most widely used cryptocurrency as an example, it was estimated that last year the computing expected to maintain the running system used the same amount of energy as that of 159 countries.

Hence, it requires sophisticated and computationally costly protection. Smaller Blockchain technologies that are mostly used privately by a corporation to record and maintain commercial activity will use a portion of those sophisticated Blockchains. Nonetheless, this is a significant aspect, as are the natural ramifications, because energy prices cannot be overlooked.

### A dangerous environment is created by a lack of regulation

An unregulated environment is usually a big stumbling block for the value-based ledger systems due to the increasing risk of data hacking at various levels of processing. That may be due to the larger number of networks available, highly unstable market scenarios or due to the lack of government oversight, scams and market manipulation. There is always the risk that perhaps the swap or Internet block in which the user maintains their tokens/valuable information would be subject to hacking attempts, closed down by government bodies attributable to deceptive dealings, as well as disappear entirely. Furthermore, this really is typically the result of a global absence of government regulation. The benefits are difficult for end-users to comprehend due to their complexity.

Although the anticipated revolutionary applications of Blockchain are easy and safer to implement along with the concepts like encrypted and shared ledgering, it requires a lot of time and learning before the “person in the street” will realize how Blockchain technologies are always so useful.

It is no surprise the first Blockchain technology, cryptocurrency, rose to prominence in the aftermath of the 2008 economic trouble, when the media and public opinion expressed broad unhappiness and rising suspicion of existing financial institutions and instruments. Is there really a desire to demolish savings programs and reconstruct them from the ground up 10 years later, with no immediate threat of a repeat? Who knows what is going to happen next? Of course, the past recession was mostly unanticipated, and world events may fuel the need for changes, yet still they do, Blockchain may be a difficult sell for just a number of people.

### Blockchains can indeed be sluggish or inconvenient to use

Blockchain transactions (Zhou et al. 2020) could be processed quickly because of their intricacy and encryption and flexible architecture, especially compared to “conventional” methods that can take many hours, showing that the concept has faults. In general, the approach could be used for Blockchain networks that are used for purposes other than storing data, such as inside interactions in an IoT setting, documenting payments and many more. Even as computer access and publishing to a network grows, these chains – which are essentially simply computer files – may become bulky and complex. Ideally, this is really a problem that could be remedied with developments in engineering and computing speed.

Therefore, it is believed that, although these difficulties may offer substantial challenges, Blockchain technology would certainly develop in the near future. And besides, technical progress, like the natural world, has a method of navigating itself its own across intentionally erected hurdles.

## 1.9. Future trends of Blockchain

Blockchain technology is approaching the stage of maturity with the great enhancement of new start-ups. Furthermore, the Blockchain could also guarantee integrity and non-modification of the entered medical record, which in turn may have repercussions both for keeping track of the outcomes and better documentation of medical errors. However, the tracking of the outcomes in the pharma supply chain also aims to guarantee the integrity of the medicines and other pharmaceutical products. The creation and updation of the directories of healthcare providers is also the support-receiving field of the Blockchain. However, working for the energy requirements of the Blockchain is also one of the prime concerns nowadays along with favorable speed and scalability.
