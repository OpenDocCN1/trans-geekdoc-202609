On-chain storage is considerably neither economically nor precisely feasible as most of the Blockchain benefits can be used for off-chain storage solutions. The hash of a small bit of data, for example, can be placed in the chain. Because a hash is very modest in size, the cost of storage will be low as well. Smart contracts and distributed hash tables (DHT) are comprised of dual solutions that are examined in this regard. In this instance, the data is also lost, and there is no way to restore it. # 6.4.3. Trust issues in the context of health information exchange (HIE)

The aspect of trust is critical in situations when consumers and sellers are separated based on Internet-based environments (Carvalho et al. 2016). Most of the HIE networks tend to share patient’s health information electronically with most of the care providers who require data so that care coordination and patient safety can be implemented accurately. HIE programs contain mechanisms that are allowed to share most health information without any patient direct control and supervision. In this situation, many security issues and privacy risks may arise, and the patient’s trust is very important as per HIE. The reactions obtained from patients can be thoroughly predicted based on the faith in the technology to seek assurance over the HIE networks without threatening personal health information or mishandling sensitive medical records before disclosing the information.
