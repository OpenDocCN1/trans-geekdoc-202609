MedBlock also ensures that EHRs are not manipulated with or used for malicious reasons. Since information transmission of Covid-19 patients was crucial in the pandemic, the proposed system used a 6G network channel of communication, which reduced information transmission latency (10–100 sec) and reliability (99.99999%). The layers of MedBlock are as follows: (i) data collection and aggregation, (ii) AI, (iii) Blockchain and (iv) accessibility. The data collected and aggregated by the preceding layer is sent into the AI layer. The AI makes decisions based on the data provided. The Artificial Neural Network (ANN) receives the patient's Covid-19 laboratory and CT-Scan results. It functions as a binary classifier based on the input parameters. The ANN model is used to determine whether the patient is positive or negative for Covid-19. The EHR, which can be either positive or negative, is an input to the layer that follows.
