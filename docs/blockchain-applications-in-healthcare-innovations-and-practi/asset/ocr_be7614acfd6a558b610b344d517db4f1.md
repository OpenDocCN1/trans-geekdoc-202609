The users’ expectations are growing day by day, and the cost of a complex system is continuously rising due to this expectation. Hence, the developer feels pressure to provide more effective healthcare applications at a lower cost, with better quality, and safe and secure health services for the patient record. This is the reason the developer is shifting to develop healthcare software applications with specific advanced technology such as Blockchain that solves the problem related to healthcare applications. Healthcare software delivers secure data and enhances trust for both the doctor and the patient. Carroll et al. (2018) discussed the various challenges of the software engineering that is used in the healthcare industries, such as designing, dynamic processing, testing and deployment. According to the authors, software engineering plays an important role in many sectors, and healthcare application is one of them. In this paper, a new theory of software engineering related to healthcare software is introduced by the authors. They pointed out that better planning and balancing efforts deliver a safe and quality healthcare application. Lalband et al. (2019) discussed various modern software life-cycle models and represented how to implement smart healthcare applications using this model. According to the authors, systematic SDLC phases help to provide a high-quality healthcare application. Hence, healthcare always needs the best method and software development model. They also discussed the research challenges of software engineering for constructing a smart healthcare application.

Angraal et al. (2017), Bell et al. (2018), Katuwal et al. (2018), Ben Fekih et al. (2020) and De Aguiar et al. (2020) presented a survey of healthcare applications based on Blockchain. They discussed many applications in detail, such as electronic media records, pharmaceutical supply chain management, remote patient monitoring, health insurance claims, billing management, etc. They also discussed the limitation of healthcare applications based on Blockchain. They also pointed out some techniques that enhance the automation of medical services. According to the authors, Blockchain technology in healthcare applications can solve scalability, security and privacy issues. Blockchain technology removes the dependency of several mediators, decreases cost, and maintains trust and transparency. Siyal et al. (2019), Hasselgren et al. (2020) and Zhang and Boulos (2020) discussed the development of different healthcare applications using Blockchain technology.
