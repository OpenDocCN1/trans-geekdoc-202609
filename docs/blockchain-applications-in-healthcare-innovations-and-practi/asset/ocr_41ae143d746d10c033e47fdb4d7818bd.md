*Auditability:* a software public register stores all events in a Blockchain network and verifies them with a digital timestamp. As a result, any networking node may be visited to examine and follow past records.

# 5.2.5. Applications of Blockchain technology

The Blockchain platform has a use in almost every sector. It is worth remembering that Bitcoin is not the same as Blockchain; rather, it is one of the more common uses of the technology (Memori et al. 2019). Some of the general applications of Blockchain technology are as follows.

*Healthcare:* healthcare can be transformed by distributed ledger technologies (McGhin et al. 2019). Blockchain will also help with drug tracking and clinical information safeguards. Drug piracy is a major problem in the healthcare companies. A fake drug is a drug or pharmacist item which is made and sold with the aim of deceiving its source, legitimacy or effectiveness. A counterfeit drug may have inadequate active chemicals, or nothing at all, and may be metabolized improperly in the body. According to the Health Research Funding Agency (Glass 2014), counterfeit pharmaceuticals account for 10–30% of all legal drugs in underdeveloped nations. Another big challenge for the healthcare sector is maintaining patient data confidentiality. Because all interactions are logged on a Blockchain, it has the potential to be a sustainable response to this issue. Unchangeable actions are added to the distributed ledger and electronically time-stamped, allowing for the tracking of an item’s progression and making things tamper-proof by securing the information. Since each patient is different in terms of physical characteristics, a treatment plan for a common condition differs based on the circumstances. As a result, in order to get personalized care, it is important to give a link to the patient’s symptoms. Medical data, on the contrary, is vulnerable and requires a stable sharing network.
