The paper by Ferrer-Gomila et al. (2019) provides an operational hybrid platform using a consensus mechanism that breaks the problem of partaking the sensitive information of the digital forensic medical evidence. The mechanism records the transparent history of the case and prevents the modification of the data by unauthorized users. Firstly, the digital evidence is encrypted before storing them in an online file storage system that is present in the Blockchain network. A dynamic agreement tool is applied to entertain the data in the Blockchain. Cryptography ensures secured access to digital data stored on various Blockchain nodes.

The complex nature of the modern healthcare system has made them costlier. The cost of the healthcare system can be reduced by befitting the application of insurance companies, amending record management and by using Blockchain technology as discussed in Tanwar et al. (2020). Breakthroughs in Blockchain technology have guided and helped to evolve transactions involving insurance billings, smart contracts and medical records. Using Blockchain in healthcare has many advantages, one of them being the capability to reform the interoperability of the data (healthcare and prescription), device tracking, etc. To prescribe the medication, it is important to know the patient’s medical history, and Blockchain has helped to improve the medical sector framework in this case. Here, many solutions are explored to expand the boundaries of the healthcare system using Blockchain. To liquidate the Hyper-Ledger-based EHR sharing in the system that employs chain code, for correcting data accessibility among healthcare providers, helping in the stimulation of the environment, the Access Control Policy Algorithm is examined. The proposed scheme uses Blockchain technology to improve efficiency and security instead of the traditional system that uses a client-server architecture.

The paper by Abd-alrazaq et al. (2020) discusses the Covid-19 situation currently in the world and how we can use Blockchain technology to mitigate the challenges that are being faced due to the virus. The paper shows various properties of the Blockchain that are generally used in the healthcare sector and the applications that can be used to help government authorities to face the challenges due to the virus. In the fight against the Covid-19 virus, Blockchain technologies are expected to play a significant role.
