# 1.6.1. Challenges faced by Blockchain

The Blockchain in healthcare serves for multiple issues including data security, care regularity issues, coordination and interoperability, since this industry has a variety of data inputs on a regular basis such as medical records, test results, prescriptions and much more. Thus, the potential benefits are as follows.

# Scalability

The first and most important issue is adoption and scalability. Although the transaction network is able to process hundreds of transactions per second without fail, there might be an unusual lag in processing.

# Interoperability

The second major issue that must be solved is interoperability, which is frequently cited as one of the main reasons why it is yet to implement the technologies. Because they are unable to send and receive data from some other Blockchain-based systems, many Blockchains create silos and do not interact with other peer groups.

To address this, a number of projects have been established to address the issue. For example, the Smart Bridges design is used to bridge the communication gap between Ark’s networks. The technology aims to provide worldwide compatibility and global communication and transmission.

# Consumption of energy

The solid evidence technique is used by the technology to verify transactions and ensure that they are trustworthy enough to be added to a system. This technique necessitates a substantial amount of computer power in order to solve complicated mathematical riddles, process, validate and more importantly, protect the entire system.

To solve this problem, Ethereum’s co-founder came up with the solution of changing proof-of-work to proof-of-stake. Participants do not have to answer complex puzzles when this approach is used, which saves gobs of energy.

# Talent deficit

The requirement of experts is growing steadily, yet finding high-quality people is typically considered as a significant obstacle when considering the digital technology deployment.
