# ### 6.3.4. Shared ledger

Various members in the distributed network will tend to record all of the digital transactions in a shared ledger by implementing various algorithms for evaluating and verifying the proposed transactions. In the process for every novel, transaction is supplementary over the collective ledger when the majority of the network participants accept the transaction which is valid. Modifications in the collective ledger are replicated over all the duplicates of Blockchain within an acceptable amount of time in most of the situations that occur. By definition, every transaction is immutable and combined with the ledger, which cannot be further modified or withdrawn, as every member of the network has a widespread and clear model of the Blockchain and no specific person has the ability to damage or modify the data in this technology.

# ### 6.3.5. Digital transactions

In a Blockchain, any kind of information or digital quality can be recorded, and the network that outfits the Blockchain defines the kind of information controlled in the transaction to ensure the validity and correctness of data that is encrypted and numerically contracted. Transactions are organized into distinct blocks, where each of the blocks comprises a cryptographic hash that is prior to the block in the Blockchain, as blocks are added in a linear as well as chronological order.
