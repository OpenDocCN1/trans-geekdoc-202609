# 8.3. Use of Blockchain in healthcare systems

Blockchain networks may help with data privacy, diversity and transaction cost reduction. They can also help with the exactness of medication, bettering patient care with results, as well as integrating cross-border health records. This may be advantageous in an emergency since the hospital can contact data right away rather than having to acquire individual patient information (Woods 2018; Kumar Singh et al. 2020).
