# 8.1.1. Introduction to AI

Expert systems have also lately made headlines in the medical field, creating a heated discussion about whether AI doctors will ever be able to replace human doctors. Computers will not replace human doctors immediately, but AI will surely help physicians create good clinical decisions and may replace human judgment in some areas of healthcare (e.g. radiology). Recent successful AI applications in healthcare were made possible by the increased availability of healthcare data or the massive development in advanced analytics algorithms. Effective AI tools can extract clinically significant knowledge contained in vast amounts of data, assisting clinical decision-making (Al-Megren et al. 2018; Grishin et al. 2019; Han et al. 2019; Alsalamah and Nuzzolese 2020; Efremova and Teichmann 2020). The advantages of AI have been extensively studied in the scholarly literature. AI can use effective methods that can “learn” features from a big amount of healthcare data
