## 5.2.5. Applications of Blockchain technology

The Blockchain platform has a use in almost every sector. It is worth remembering that Bitcoin is not the same as Blockchain; rather, it is one of the more common uses of the technology (Memori et al. 2019). Some of the general applications of Blockchain technology are as follows.

- Healthcare: healthcare can be transformed by distributed ledger technologies (McGhin et al. 2019). Blockchain will also help with drug tracking and clinical information safeguards. Drug piracy is a major problem in the healthcare companies. A fake drug is a drug or pharmacist item which is made and sold with the aim of deceiving its source, legitimacy or effectiveness. A counterfeit drug may have inadequate active chemicals, or nothing at all, and may be metabolized improperly in the body. According to the Health Research Funding Agency (Glass 2014), counterfeit pharmaceuticals account for 10–30% of all legal drugs in underdeveloped nations. Another big challenge for the healthcare sector is maintaining patient data confidentiality. Because all interactions are logged on a Blockchain, it has the potential to be a sustainable response to this issue. Unchangeable actions are added to the distributed ledger and electronically time-stamped, allowing for the tracking of an item’s progression and making things tamper-proof by securing the information. Since each patient is different in terms of physical characteristics, a treatment plan for a common condition differs based on the circumstances. As a result, in order to get personalized care, it is important to give a link to the patient’s symptoms. Medical data, on the contrary, is vulnerable and requires a stable sharing network. Blockchain technology, with its irreversible ledger, could provide a framework for the unification of patient history among multiple medical institutions, as well as data security measures. Blockchain is capable of providing a stable and open system for storing digital medical records, resulting in better patient care and lower treatment costs.

- Energy Industry: one of the most extensively used Blockchain solutions in the energy business is microgrids. A microgrid is a locally integrated and regulated network of sources of electric power with the goal of increasing the efficiency and stability of energy generation and consumption. One of the most important benefits of microgrid technology is that it enables consumers and other electric power customers, like retailers, to not only access the electricity they need but also to sell and manufacture surplus energy to the grid. In microgrids, it is used to simplify, register and verify power sale and purchasing transactions. Similarly, at larger scales, Blockchain can be used to allow electricity sharing in smart grids. Blockchain can be used to facilitate safe and privacy-preserving usage control and electricity sharing without the requirements of the centralized person (Saxena et al. 2021).

- Stock Market: interoperability, confidence and openness are all problems that fragmented business processes face. Blockchain technology may be able to address these issues. It will improve the stock exchange’s efficiency by decentralizing and automating it. Blockchain will further cut costs by replacing intermediaries and speeding up contract settlements. Furthermore, the technology has the potential to be useful in contract clearance and settlement, as well as easing the tedious paperwork associated with trading and legal ownership transition and ensuring a safe post-trade mechanism.

- Voting: Blockchain technology has several applications to solve issues that a traditional database may have. Voting is an example of such a dilemma. It was recently announced that a major voting machine maker in the United States had implemented remote control tools on some of their machines. When counting the totals, this program provided for the modification of votes. As a result of incidents like this, there is a loss of confidence in the election system of America: “Exclusive poll: The majority of people anticipate international meddling in the midterm elections”. Because an elector’s ledger is the same as the one used to count the votes, Blockchain can address this problem by creating a public ledger that ensures votes are recorded.

- Insurance: Blockchain will be used to facilitate exchanges between clients, insurers and health insurers in the insurance business. Blockchain technology might be used by insurance companies to acquire and record contracts, file and evaluate complaints and pay premium operations. Smart contracts will simplify a variety of insurance plans, lowering management costs dramatically (Gatteschi et al. 2018).

Writing insurance claims, for example, comes with a heavy administrative expense. Owing to misunderstandings and misinterpretations of the terminology, the management of lawsuits may be a rather complicated procedure throughout several instances. By making insurance plans structured in more precise if-then partnerships, smart contracts will avoid these issues. These policies allow the implementation of terms to be automated by digital protocols. Insurance firms will lower the quality of their insurance premiums and become more attractive as a result of this cut, attracting more buyers.

## 5.3. Blockchain in healthcare

The Blockchain technology was created for its most well-known applications in economics and cryptocurrency, but its applications are increasing in different domains, including in biomedicine. Through stabilizing and protecting the data collection in which consumers can communicate through various forms of transactions, medication, biotechnology, telehealth, tele-monitoring, e-health, genetics and customized healthcare apps have all demonstrated promise for Blockchain technology. The sections that follow discuss specific domains of healthcare and medical care wherein Blockchain technology holds enormous potential.

- Clinical research: in clinical trials, a variety of concerns such as data protection, integrity of data, sharing of data, patient enrollment and so on can occur. Blockchain, as the next internet generation, has the potential to solve these issues. With the assistance of Blockchain technologies, healthcare experts are trying to resolve these problems. Permissioned Ethereum is a Blockchain platform with smart contract features that is used in conjunction with systems for data computing centered in clinics. The major purpose of these studies is to overcome the patient enrollment conundrum.

- Fraud detection: one of the most significant uses of Blockchain systems in the medical business is the control of pharmaceutical medication supply chains. Supply management is a key topic in many businesses, but it is especially vital in healthcare due to its increasing complexity. As a result, every disturbance in the supply chain of healthcare affects a patient's health. As a result of the many moving parts and individuals involved, supply chains are unstable and contain gaps for malicious assaults. Blockchains offer a safe and stable mechanism to eradicate this issue and, in some situations, avoid fraud from occurring.
