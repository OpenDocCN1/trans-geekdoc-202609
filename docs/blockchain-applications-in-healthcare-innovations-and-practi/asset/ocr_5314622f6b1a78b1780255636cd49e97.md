AI has a variety of uses in the medical industry. The healthcare system in the United States is in serious need of an overhaul. From chronic diseases and cancer to radiography and quality assurance, there is an almost limitless opportunity to use technology to deliver increasingly precise, efficient and impactful treatments at precisely the correct time during a client's treatment. Artificial intelligence is positioned to be the spark that enables changes throughout the whole system as payment methods grow, clients demand more out of healthcare providers or the availability of data continues to rise.
