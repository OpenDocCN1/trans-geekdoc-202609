Now let us discuss the famous term “connected health” (Papageorgiou et al. 2018). With the increase in automation and digitalization of healthcare services, this is not only patient-centric but is also used across the healthcare industry. In the whole healthcare industry, there are various entities that are dependent on each other. The digitalization has been boon for all the entities as now they can connect with each other, the data collected/generated at one entity can be shared with other entity very easily, hence it is called “connected health”, where the goal is to achieve a smart health paradigm.
