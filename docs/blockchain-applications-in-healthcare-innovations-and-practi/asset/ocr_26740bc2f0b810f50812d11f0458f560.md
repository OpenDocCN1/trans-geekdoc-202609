Blockchain-based network for keeping, maintaining and transferring electronic medical information for cancer patients should be developed. To view, handle and archive secure patient data, they used permissioned Blockchain technology. These proposed solutions will be used to incorporate Blockchain technologies in healthcare practices for maintaining the safety and confidentiality of information of patient and background. Perhaps the explanation for the rapid adoption of Blockchain technologies in medicine around the world is a clear promise to patients that using this technology can make their medical data permanent and unaltered. Any access or modification initiative may be easily branded and identified through the Blockchain. This is valuable not only for medical integrity, but it also flags some fraudulent activities, such as wholesale bribery or record adulteration. Furthermore, posting and reviewing authorized medicinal service records would be much easier. When a patient visits, the rest of the patient’s suppliers are likely to notice it right away. Pharmaceutical errors, hypersensitivities and pharmaceutical solutions may be seamlessly related across all Blockchain records using suitable patient-caring algorithms, removing the need for time-consuming prescriptions agreement forms. As a result, the application of Blockchain technology would improve treatment accessibility, medical record management, quick clinical proof verification, greater monitoring and more effective healthcare management.

Al-Karaki et al. (2019) have presented a DASS-CARE that is a Blockchain-based system that enables open connectivity to health centers, particularly medical information, that is scalable and secure. The proposed system would significantly simplify the process of real-time access and notifications without jeopardizing patient data protection, integrity or confidentiality.

Vora et al. (2019) developed a system based on Blockchain for EHR storage and maintenance that is both effective and safe. The suggested system also enables customers, providers and other organizations to connect to medical data in a secure and convenient manner while maintaining patient confidentiality.

Dagher et al. (2018) developed a system based on Blockchain for patients, insurers and third parties to offer secure, accessible and dependable connectivity to health data while protecting patients’ personal information.
