Blockchain technology is increasing in popularity as it is a novel process of managing the business along with the commercial transactions by dynamically sharing the ledgers. Blockchain can make it easier to record economic transactions among parties that are involved directly or indirectly. In addition, because Blockchain is based on a peer-to-peer network of distinct nodes, it must eradicate the need for intermediary transactions and direct third-party control over a company’s operations. The majority of Blockchain research has focused on the technological aspects of cryptocurrency. Blockchain technology, on the contrary, has a broader and deeper set of applications than virtual currencies, and can be used for more than just various economic transactions. The interest in Blockchain has risen by attracting a lot of attention and financing from a number of efforts across multiple industries. Blockchain technology is transforming almost all of the sectors and healthcare. Because of its foundation technology, one of the most acceptable processes of Blockchain is the development of trust in coordination and coordinating communication across networked businesses (such as hospitals).
