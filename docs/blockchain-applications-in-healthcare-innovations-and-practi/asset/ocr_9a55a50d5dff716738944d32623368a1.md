Newly emerging technologies have encouraged every sector to adopt these technologies to increase their productivity. The smart contract is a Blockchain-based technology that overcomes the limitation experienced in traditional contracts. This chapter explains the concept of a smart contract and its role in different sectors in detail. The research begins with the introduction of smart contracts, moving forward to its challenges, the real-time companies using smart contracts, with some past historic attacks. Overall, this chapter's research consists of deeper knowledge about smart contracts, their platform and real-time applications.

# 10.1. Introduction

The evolution of technologies has unboxed tremendously easier and simpler applications. These applications have turned the traditional approaches into smarter ones. A similar example is a Blockchain-based technology known as “smart contracts” (Rouhani and Deters 2018). Emerging technologies have reduced the effort needed and saved a lot of time. The term smart contract came into existence to overcome the limitations faced in traditional contracts. A traditional contract is defined as a bunch of agreed-upon terms between two parties that are validated and signed by the two parties. Smart contracts replace the issues that occurred in traditional contracts.
