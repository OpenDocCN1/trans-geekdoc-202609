

**Principle 1 Proactive not reactive — preventative not remedial**
- Anticipate privacy issues and risks before they happen
- Develop a culture of privacy awareness

**Principle 2 Lead with privacy as the default setting**
- Personal data shall always be automatically protected in a system
- No added actions required by user

**Principle 3 Embed privacy into design**
- Privacy controls shall not be added on to the system, but be embedded in the system
- Data Protection shall be core function of any system

**Principle 4 Retain full functionality (positive-sum, not zero-sum)**
- A win-win situation, no tradeoff
- No compromise or tradeoff to achieve both privacy and security

**Principle 5 Ensure end-to-end security**
- Data Security throughout its lifecycle
- From data creation/collection till data disposal

**Principle 6 Maintain visibility and transparency — keep it open**
- Ensuring data visibility and transparency to the owner
- Stakeholders shall have the visibility after independent verification

**Principle 7 Respect user privacy — keep it user-centric**
- Provide strong privacy defaults to the user
- Appropriate Privacy Notices

## 7.7. Conclusion

Blockchain technology has been considered a game changer and that is why industries like banking are adopting it for faster transactions, cost reduction, improving security and record keeping. In the healthcare industry, Blockchain can become a valuable generating factor for healthcare entities because it can allow different healthcare entities to share their networks without compromising data security and integrity yet will still allow for them to track the source and the changes. With increased digitalization in the healthcare domain, trust has gained importance along with security and Blockchain because of its fundamental principles which provide trust, security and privacy. With the involvement of IoT in healthcare, the interdependency has increased following the need of security during communication, which has also increased. Now, the need of the hour is more research on developing prototypes of healthcare applications based on the type of services using Blockchain as backbone. This will help in scaling Blockchain in the healthcare domain.

## Decentralized Smart Healthcare Systems Using Blockchain and AI

Patients, developers, vendors and legislators all face substantial challenges as a result of increased data availability and rapid innovations of intelligent machines in healthcare. Simple face photographs or videos are being transformed into effective records for predictive modeling, transforming whatever statistics that are more about people into medical sciences, courtesy of a unique, deep mastering or transferable learning algorithms. Patients now have little control over who has access to their medical records and are ignorant of the overall cost of the data they possess. Such revolutionary Blockchain generations will get society further to the idea that each patient record will be able to communicate changes to an open-source, community-wide relied-on ledger that is available and understood across enterprises with guaranteed integrity. This paper describes a Blockchain-enabled fragmented personal fitness facts ecosystem that enables unique drug discovery, biomarker development and preventative healthcare procedures, as well as secured personal records using Blockchain and deep learning technologies to connect users and providers of personal records with medical records.
