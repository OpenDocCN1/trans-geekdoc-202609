The decentralized storage feature of the Blockchain can be very useful for better security and authentication of the data stored in the systems (Khanna et al. 2020a). Decentralized storage is the process in which there is a breakdown of the records in the storage from the single server to numerous servers by the means of a Blockchain ledger. # 3.3.1.3. Authentication

Blockchain by the means of its decentralized feature makes sure the authentication of the patient record that is present in the blocks of the Blockchain. The authentication process is achieved by a private key which in turn is connected to a public key to initialize the operation of creation, modification and checking of the information in the Blockchain. In the software, a Bitcoin wallet is associated with the Bitcoin address and stores these keys. By the use of the cryptographic scheme, the software applications which are usually used for bitcoin and cryptocurrency can be determined for authentication purposes.

# 3.4.1. Smart contracts

Employing smart contracts, the user can form or create agreements between the buyer and the seller, by using Blockchain technology. Smart contracts, stored in Blockchain network, encrypt and convert the transaction into legal documents for legal services. These smart contracts consist of the scripts that are present in the Blockchain network, and they can be traced or identified by their unique address. By means of these smart contracts, we can establish a fair exchange among the parties while having minimum communication between them, in a decentralized manner. Thus, by this, the user can form their document without the need of a legal representative thus removing the challenges including both financial and time-wise, and making a better transfer of the resources or transaction.
