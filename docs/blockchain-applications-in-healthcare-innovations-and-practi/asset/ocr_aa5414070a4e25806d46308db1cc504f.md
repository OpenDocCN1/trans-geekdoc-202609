# 10.6.4. Intellectual property rights

Smart contracts play a vital role in several sectors. One of these sectors is intellectual property rights. Using smart contracts, artists, music directors and television shows can easily protect their content and apply copyrights over them. For instance, a music director deals with a party that needs to reform their song. In that case, the music director can create certain protocols, paying high royalties, and protecting information about the project. These clauses can be set together using smart contracts. Thus, smart contracts protect intellectual property rights (Grasso 2019; ONIXX 2018).

# 10.6.5. Stock trading

The market fluctuates every moment; hence, the traders are looking for some autonomous transactions that cost less and are less time-consuming. Smart contracts contribute in every sector just to automate the task. Similarly, smart contracts even help to record every movement of the stock or follow it once it is transferred to its owner. The traders can buy or sell their stocks quickly because the smart contracts update the transactions. Before updating the transactions, it records and validates the transactions automatically (IONIXX 2018).
