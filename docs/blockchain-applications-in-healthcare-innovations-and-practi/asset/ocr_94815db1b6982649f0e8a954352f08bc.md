In recent years, the healthcare industry has started adopting new technology that increases the quality of care and fast recovery rate from the disease. Upcoming new technologies such as cloud computing, AI, Blockchain and IoT are being used in the medical field, which provide more benefits for developing healthcare applications. Healthcare IT solutions promise to improve interoperability between patient and doctor, reduce the overall cost of healthcare delivery and increase the accessibility of patient records according to their requirement. The main focus of this chapter is to identify the factor that affects healthcare quality. Many healthcare policies provide the best software practices and design principles to secure applications. When developing any healthcare software system, cost, time, effort, trust and security are the main factors that affect the development process. An effective SDLC helps to reduce these kinds of difficulties. SDLC is defined as a tool based on the project context and is helpful for producing high-quality software. A better life-cycle improves the quality of care for the patient. It decreases manpower hours, treatment costs and supports easy accesses to patient information in a timely manner. In this chapter, we have discussed the life-cycles of software development through the component-based software engineering approach. Further, we discuss how to create a Blockchain-based repository that stores software components in the form of blocks and discuss how Blockchain technology helps to secure the health-related data of a patient in healthcare applications and increase the trust in the patient record. The proposed component-based software development life-cycle with Blockchain ensures that our data is secured and also improves the quality of healthcare applications.
