# 10.3. Smart contracts

In 1996, a smart contract was defined as a digital contract structured in the form of lines of code and executed in the same way. It is a set of protocols formed between parties to fulfill the desired promises. The smart contract executes the contract, ensuring no malicious contents as well as unpredictable circumstances. It reduces the usage of third parties, which results in low transactional cost, and lowers potential collusions and countered risk. Smart contracts came into existence due to Blockchain technologies (Wang et al. 2019). They are in the form of general-purpose programming. These lines of code and the agreements are stored over distributed and decentralized technology termed as Blockchain.

The speciality of smart contracts is that they are reliable and trustworthy, and permit a faster flow of procedure without any involvement or interference from third parties, or the legal systems. Smart contracts are stored over Blockchain technology that runs on preferred conditions (Wang et al. 2019). Smart contracts are solely focused on automating the execution of the agreement without any loss of time or certain involvement. Users develop their code and form a Turing complete program on the Blockchain network.

Smart contracts are developed on the Ethereum platform. The execution of text on Ethereum consists of the heap, stack and a determined memory over the chain. Once the development of the contract is accomplished, the next step is to deploy the contract. After the deployment of the contract, the contents of the contract cannot be changed or altered, even by the contract creator. The execution of a contract requires a set of operations and specified functions to be carried out. Once the set of functions is carried out, then the program can be finished by the gas depletion. # 10.3.1. How smart contracts work

Smart contracts follow a stipulated model and are entirely executed on the Blockchain. A contract is transformed into lines of code that are then executed under certain conditions. A wide range of networks execute the actions over the set of protocols that are verified and validated. The actions related to smart contracts are conducted and executed. These actions involve vehicle registration, notifying the users, raising a ticket and transferring funds to relevant parties. These chunks of actions, once executed, are updated on Blockchain, and mark the transaction as completed. To dive deeper, a smart contract is more than a general script or piece of code that is stored on a decentralized Blockchain network. It maintains transparency between the two parties.
