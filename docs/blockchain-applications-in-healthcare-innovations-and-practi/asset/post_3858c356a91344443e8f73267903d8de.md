## 10.8. Challenges

Building smart contracts gives rise to multiple benefits, but simultaneously there are some challenges to be addressed while using smart contracts based on Blockchain technology:

- Confidentiality: although Ethereum promises to deliver transparency, enterprises still hesitate to store their contracts publicly because of competitive strategies. Ethereum does not support private smart contract Blockchain platforms that can harm confidentiality.

- Inception to human error: the smart contracts are formatted in lines of code, but there may be a possibility that the terms and conditions related to transactional data may be affected because of missed code. This can decrease accuracy and reliability.

- Threats through bugs or errors: in 2016, because of a vulnerability in the smart contracts of the DAO, 50 million ETH was siphoned off by hackers. This resulted in the fork of ETH into ETC (Ethereum Classic).

- Predictability: smart contracts automate depending on the event that is triggered. Depending on the parameters set, the smart contracts perform certain actions. For instance, let us consider, the stock price's multiplication factor fluctuates, which is not in the control of the smart contract; this may result in certainly unplanned contract code executions.

- Correctness: one of the major challenges faced while using smart contracts is that once the smart contract is developed and deployed, it cannot be altered. Any error can be exploited.

- Scams: as Blockchain is a newly emerging technology, it may be vulnerable to malicious attacks and threats. Therefore, these vulnerable attacks can be initiated through scams or honeypots.

## 10.9. Historical attacks and issues with smart contracts

Previously, smart contracts were prone to various security issues and attacks. The most discussed attacks are the DAO attack and the Parity Wallet hacks. These were the two historical attacks that took place, impacting smart contracts negatively. DAO is a smart contract that permits people to exchange their tokens and win rewards. In 2016, DAO gathered a fund of 150 million USD. Participants can buy tokens and vote over proposals, receiving rewards. Later, it was affected by several threats that created loopholes and removed funds. The hacker attacked the smart contracts, which gave way for vulnerability to enter in the form of bugs or errors in the code (Sayeed et al. 2019).

Another historical popular attack took place at Parity Multisig Wallet. This was discovered when the hacker had an opportunity to make the wallet vulnerable with version 1.5+. This flaw allowed the hacker to remit more than 30 million USD, which is around 150 million Ether. Once the Parity Wallet initiated a smart contract, two hacks took place consecutively. The attacker executed a script that used bugs. Due to this, two hacks were initiated; in the first one, the attacker initiated a call to update the status of the wallet. This means the attacker can act as an owner and remove all of the funds from the wallet (Sayeed et al. 2019).

## 10.10. Conclusion

Smart contracts are hassle-free digital contracts that do not have any intervention from third-party lawyers or legal enforcement. This seamless technology can be built using the Ethereum Blockchain platform, which provides security through cryptographic and encryption algorithms. A cyber-attack cannot directly hit a single record, as Blockchain is a chain of records that is connected with a global supercomputer network. This chapter describes the use cases, benefits and challenges of smart contracts. Smart contracts even have multiple benefits that can minimize all the disadvantages faced while dealing with traditional contracts. But since the technology acts as a boon and even a curse, smart contracts are similarly leading to cyber threats and cyber-attacks, which can create loopholes and vulnerabilities that can cause harm. In this chapter, we have mentioned the six ultimate companies that had implemented smart contracts on a real-time basis. The results indicate that the companies experienced a positive impact using smart contracts. Smart contracts do not involve the intervention of a middleman or any legal person. Instead, smart contracts reduced the transactional cost and manual work. Implementing smart contracts takes time, but once the smart contracts are deployed, they automatically fulfill the set of promises mentioned in the smart contract. Apart from the numerous benefits and features of smart contracts, the developers may come across some other challenges. To sum up, smart contracts have several advantages, but simultaneously need to answer some challenging questions. Still, if businesses prefer smart contracts with strict security protocols, then it is one of the best-recommended Blockchain-based technologies.
