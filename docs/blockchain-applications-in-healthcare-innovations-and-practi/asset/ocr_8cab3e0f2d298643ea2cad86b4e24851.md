Based on the proposed health Blockchain outlined in this study, most of the healthcare contributors and institutions, along with medical researchers, enlighten their research by imparting huge volumes of genetic, nutritional, lifestyle, environmental or healthcare data with guaranteed confidentiality and anonymity. HIE networks need to reassure patients with medical records that they are kept secure during the interchange process as per the privacy policies that emphasize sensitive health information to be used only in their treatment towards better health and disease prevention. Before creating an industry-ready solution, more work is required in strategic areas, as there are increased costs and incentives to implement data security and intellectual property preservation.
