In terms of revenue and employment, healthcare is now one of India’s largest industries. Healthcare encompasses hospitals, doctors, clinical trials, outsourcing, telemedicine, tourism, health insurance and medical equipment. The Indian healthcare industry is growing as the country’s population, services and public and private spending all increase (Rout et al. 2019). The Indian healthcare system is divided into two major components: public and private (Mukherjee et al. 2022a). The government provides a small number of secondary and tertiary healthcare institutions in major cities. It is concerned with providing basic healthcare in rural areas through primary healthcare centers (PHCs). The private sector provides most high school, tertiary and quaternary care in metro and level I and level II cities.
