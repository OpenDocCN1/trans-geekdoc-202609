There are multiple differentiating factors. These differences are as follows.

# 10.1.1. Comparison between traditional contracts and smart contracts

# 10.1.1.1. Automated contract

It takes more time to prepare, draft and formulate a traditional contract. Usually, it takes around one to three days to prepare the contract. Smart contracts need more time to code the contract. Smart contracts are time-consuming to develop but once deployed the contracts are automated. There are ready-made platforms present like Ethereum, Hyperledger Fabric, etc., that automate contracts.

# 10.1.1.2. Contract remittance and execution

In traditional contracts, some obligations are applied to the parties to pay the overdue within the allocated time as an organizational effort. Whereas in smart contracts, the remittance of the contract is executed automatically once the contract is fulfilled. These values and statements are recorded in the form of code (Toshev & Boteva 2020).

# 10.1.1.3. Low cost

In traditional contracts, the procedure fee is slightly higher as lawyers, contract makers and other third parties are involved. Smart contracts preferably require less cost.

# 10.1.1.4. Data security and confidentiality

Traditional methods may not maintain the security and confidentiality of the data. Traditional contracts require paperwork that opens the door for entering threats. However, smart contracts enable a strict path for data security and confidentiality. They use Blockchain-based cryptographic protection to maintain the confidentiality and integrity of data.

# 10.1.1.5. Physical presence

In traditional contracts, the two parties must be present physically; without their presence, the contract cannot be signed and it is impossible to proceed. In this ever-changing world, smart contracts are signed digitally anywhere and the contracts can be accessed anytime.
