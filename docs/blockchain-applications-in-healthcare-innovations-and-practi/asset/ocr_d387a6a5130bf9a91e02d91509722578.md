Any basic operations (such as polling, supply chain management and asset ownership) may be performed using a private Blockchain. Multichain and Hyperledger projects are the examples of private Blockchains (i.e. fabric and sawtooth) (Aggarwal et al. 2019).

Consortium Blockchain: it is a somewhat decentralized Blockchain network operated by a number of entities. This sort of Blockchain enables several organizations to collaborate and serve as the regulator for mining and knowledge exchange. Energy Web Foundation and R3 are two examples of this kind of Blockchain (Aggarwal et al. 2019).

Hybrid Blockchain: it is a hybrid Blockchain network that incorporates private and public Blockchain platforms. In this case, the functionality of all Blockchains is used. Subscribers on the hybrid network may keep records of who has access to which Blockchain information. Just a few of the Blockchain’s selected documents are allowed to be made available, while the others are kept private in the private network. Users should, however, publish this on the public Blockchain for authentication. Public Blockchains enhance hashing and include additional nodes in the validation process. This improves the Blockchain network’s visibility and trust. The hybrid Blockchain “Dragonchain” is an example (Aggarwal et al. 2019).

# 5.2.3. Smart contracts

Szabo coined the word “smart contract” in the mid-1990s, defining it as “a collection of guarantees, defined in electronic format, including rules under which the parties deliver on trust” (Jani 2020). It is a part of Blockchain-based software that helps to enable, implement and monitor contractual arrangements. Its primary objective is to carry out the provisions of an agreement as soon as possible unless certain criteria are satisfied. As a result, smart contracts provide lower settlement costs than conventional schemes, which count on a trustworthy third party to implement and process the terms of a contract. In the literature, several common meanings of a smart contract have been explored. Code that is registered, checked and implemented on a Blockchain is what smart contract code refers to. Smart legal contracts will almost certainly be a hybrid between smart contract code and conventional legal vocabulary. Consider the case of a product seller who enters into a smart legal deal with a manufacturer. The payment terms should be written in code and executed immediately when the product is delivered.
