# 2.2.2. Applications of smart contracts

# 2.2.2.1. Finance

Smart contracts and Blockchain enhance transparency and trust among players, while reducing infrastructure, transaction and administrative expenses. The following are a few examples of smart contract applications in finance (Ojetunde et al. 2019).

+   - Security: the security sector entails complicated operations that are time consuming, wasteful, inconvenient and risky. Smart contracts can avoid the use of middlemen in the securities custody chain. The centralized clearance process necessitates time-consuming operations, as well as complicated external and internal reconciliation.

+   - Insurance: every year, the insurance industry invests millions of dollars in claim processing while losing millions to fraudulent claims. They could be used to automate insurance claims, validation and payment, boosting claim processing speed while reducing fraud and minimizing potential risks (Gatteschi et al. 2018).
