Smart contracts are hassle-free digital contracts that do not have any intervention from third-party lawyers or legal enforcement. This seamless technology can be built using the Ethereum Blockchain platform, which provides security through cryptographic and encryption algorithms. A cyber-attack cannot directly hit a single record, as Blockchain is a chain of records that is connected with a global supercomputer network. This chapter describes the use cases, benefits and challenges of smart contracts. Smart contracts even have multiple benefits that can minimize all the disadvantages faced while dealing with traditional contracts. But since the technology acts as a boom and even a curse, smart contracts are similarly leading to cyber threats and cyber-attacks, which can create loopholes and vulnerabilities that can cause harm. The results indicate that the companies experienced a positive impact using smart contracts. Smart contracts do not involve the intervention of a middleman or any legal person. Instead, smart contracts reduced the transactional cost and manual work. Implementing smart contracts takes time, but once the smart contracts are deployed, they automatically fulfill the set of promises mentioned in the smart contract. Apart from the numerous benefits and features of smart contracts, the developers may come across some other challenges. To sum up, smart contracts have several advantages, but simultaneously need to answer some challenging questions.
