## 3.7. Conclusion

The growing demand for health has led to the development of hospitals and medical institutions further leading to an increase in medical data and the need for managing it. Thus, we have discussed Blockchain technology in association with the medical sector. Health records including the personal details of the patients are to be kept confidential. These records are saved in “medical clouds” but can be misused by the other parties, for example by deleting the original data or modifying it. The problems related to the healthcare industry and how they can hamper technological advancements in the industry preventing it from growing have also been discussed in this chapter. Blockchain due to its various properties can be a solution to these problems, and Blockchain-based systems seem to exhibit higher robustness, scalability and immunity that allows secure data sharing and data privacy. Blockchain technology has now gained great attention in many countries and companies all over the world.

## Analyzing and Modeling the Challenges Faced by the Healthcare Sector in the Adoption Process of Blockchain Technologies

Using Blockchain technology, information can be recorded and shared in a community. This study aims to identify the challenges healthcare organizations face in adopting Blockchain technology to improve patient care. Three hypotheses are being proposed by taking three independent variables and one dependent variable. Target populations are mainly the IT staff working in the hospitals. The respondents were selected from the employees who had IT information about their individual healthcare firms’ present and future tasks. A total of 536 questionnaires were sent for a survey, out of which only 350 valid responses are being counted as the rest are mostly unanswered or partially filled. The findings showed that the proposed hypotheses are being accepted. This study will help Blockchain providers and the healthcare industry to understand the problems and find a probable solution.
