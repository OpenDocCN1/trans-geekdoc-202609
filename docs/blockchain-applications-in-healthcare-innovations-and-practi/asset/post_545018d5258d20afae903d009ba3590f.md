## Blockchain as an Effective Technology in Maintaining Electronic Health Record Systems

Blockchain technology is one of the most important innovations in the professional world today. It is a distributed network that is made up of an organized list of nodes and connections, with nodes responsible for storing information and chains connecting them. It is the chain of blocks that constitutes the data and helps in maintaining a trust among the individuals. The Blockchain technology is moving on the path of constant change and revolution. The increased use of Blockchain technologies in recent years has compelled experts and academicians to find innovative approaches to implement Blockchain in various fields. Healthcare is the sector that has seen the most growth in terms of data and sales. Security has become increasingly important as the number of electronic health records (EHRs) grows. There has been a push to use Blockchain technologies to make EHRs safer. The increase in the usage of Blockchain technology provides many new application opportunities that include the area of healthcare. EHRs are the most promising use of Blockchains. It can be defined as the health information of the patients that are stored digitally over the network. Blockchain technology has the potential to revolutionize healthcare by putting the user at the center of the clinical system and improving health information transparency, privacy and confidentiality. This chapter not only covers the introduction to Blockchain technology and its background concepts, but it also covers the importance of Blockchain in the healthcare sector. The model for EHR on the basis Blockchain is also proposed. This chapter concludes with the various research challenges in the domain of Blockchain along with a case study.

### 5.1. Introduction

One of the most precious resources is data. With cyber criminals becoming more skilled at stealing sensitive data, cybersecurity has become one of the most pressing global challenges, necessitating cyber protection. Various methods have been suggested in the past to meet the security needs of various organizations, but security attacks have no limits, resulting in the techniques being either compromised or rendered useless. Blockchain technology is the most recent solution to safeguard useful records. It is described as a distributed ledger that holds an ordered list of various items connected by connections known as chains (Katal et al. 2021b). The content included in these blocks, which relate to financial operations, is only accessible to subscribers. An intricate chain of self-managed encryption keys maintains user approval: each authorized user gives a time-sensitive secret identifier that autoblocks it if the decryption timer runs out (Zhang et al. 2018). Blockchain is a modern platform that allows for more autonomous computing. It is based on the principle of public ledger, which acts as an archive for previous transactions surrounding these agents. It is regularly audited by groups of agents (selection is done according to different policies based on the software domain) who audit it on a regular basis. The results of each audit are saved in a block and presented to the entire network. In a cryptographically linked row, blocks are sequentially added to the ledger. Blockchain technology allows data to be shared while maintaining confidentiality. The following are a few of the reasons why Blockchain technology is so common and is being used in various domains:

- When opposed to conventional banks that work on operating hours and rules, Blockchain technology allows for faster transaction settlements. On the contrary, it has no such limitations. It allows for quicker contract settlements.

- Since Blockchain stores data and prevents it from being corrupted, it removes human error. Accuracy is ensured when records are checked as they move from one node to the next.

- Data objectivity is a product of Blockchain technologies. It not only guarantees data confidentiality but also warns users against any data change. If an organization's data is hacked, it cannot be exploited, ensuring a balance between confidentiality and governance.

- In the event that anomalies are discovered, they can still be traced back to their source, which simplifies inquiries and the execution of necessary acts. As a result, quality assurance is achieved.
