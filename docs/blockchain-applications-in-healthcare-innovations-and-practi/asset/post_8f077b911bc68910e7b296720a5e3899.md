This is why the developer is attracted to another approach for constructing the software, and this advanced approach is based on the adapting nature. CBSE is an advanced approach where software is developed by selecting appropriate reusable components. The traditional approach does not focus on the reuse of software. The software is always built from scratch. Whereas, in CBSE, developers write code once and can reuse component code multiple times when needed. Therefore, the development cost and time for building large and complex software is minimized using this approach.

Chaudron et al. (2005) discussed the component-based development process and its life-cycle. According to the authors, the software development life-cycle model of components is different from the conventional life-cycle model. It contains two life-cycles that work simultaneously. They also discussed three other types of component-based development processes, such as product line development, architecture-driven component development and product line development. In this paper, the authors gave a comparative study between these three component-based development processes. With the help of a case study that is related to the software industry, it presents these three types of development processes, and uses different technologies and methods as needed. Capretz et al. (2004) discussed the life-cycle models based on component-based development and discussed various reusability issues that occur during any software product development. They discussed component-based process models in detail. This process model helps to increase reusability, evolution and productivity during the software development time. The authors also discussed some reusability techniques such as composition and inheritance that help to increase the software’s productivity.

Fahmi and Choi (2008) discussed some life-cycle models based on component-based software development (CBSD), such as the Y model and V model. They also discussed some issues during the software development process, such as the CBSE life-cycle-related issue, the CBSE management issue, and trust and reliability on the component issue. The authors gave valuable suggestions to resolve the above issue effectively. Negi et al. (2015) and Gulia (2017) discussed various component-based software engineering models. They also compared all component models according to various parameters, such as domain analysis, component selection technique, risk analysis and component evolution for developing any software. According to the authors, in SDLC, a systematic arrangement of phases helps deliver high-quality software in good time. The authors also discussed the advantages and disadvantages of these different process models. Talukder and Rahman (2015) presented a comparative study between the different CBSD life-cycle models. They proposed a new life-cycle model called CROM (customer requirement-oriented CBSE life-cycle model). The proposed model handles the issue related to customers. It helps identify actual customer problems in developing software systems and maintain all customer demands through feedback analysis. This model increases the understanding between the customer and vendor for developing any software.

Khan et al. (2012) presented various characteristics of CBSD life-cycle models and proposed a new model called an improved model for reuse. This new model encompasses both component-based and software development processes. In this paper, the authors provided a comparative analysis between existing models with their proposed model and discussed their pros and cons. Garg and Jha (2019) compared various component-based life-cycle models and discussed a new proposed model that makes the component selection procedure easier. This proposed model provides the best-fit mechanism for the selection of any existing components. It contains three types of repositories, namely a black repository, white repository and gray repository. To construct any software, a developer can go to each repository in parallel and select appropriate components. Kaushik and Dulawat (2012) and Kumar and Bhatia (2014) discussed comparative analysis between the traditional life-cycle model and CBSD life-cycle models and described their strengths and weaknesses. According to the authors, the traditional approach only has limited requirements with limited features, but the component-based approach requirement changes frequently and supports design flexibility and reusability. According to the authors, component reusability is an emerging topic of the software engineering community. Reusability saves time and effort and reduces the development time and cost of software.

CBSE proposes an attractive approach that can be used to build any software application. This approach supports flexibility of design, changes requirements rapidly, improves quality and performance, and easily detects a fault in implementation (Nautiyal et al. 2012). Hence, due to these features, people get a better-quality product within time and budget. Due to the above facility, many IT industries are adopting this technology to develop healthcare software. In many healthcare applications, component-based approach is treated as a service-oriented approach that handles the complex environment software and gives a better analysis of the patient record (Chu 2005). Healthcare applications always insist on adopting best practices and software development, a process model that helps to improve the quality of care and fast recovery rate from the disease. Healthcare IT solutions help to improve interoperability between the doctor and patient, reduce the overall costs of healthcare delivery and increase the accessibility of patient records according to their requirement.

McMurtry et al. (2013) discussed the impact of the component-based software development life-cycle model in the healthcare application. The authors also discussed various factors such as requirement analysis, design, risk analysis and select components for developing the healthcare application. In this paper, using an effective SDLC model, the authors designed and implemented a home health component with the help of pedagogical tools and this component can be used everywhere. According to the authors this home health software package provides the facility of regional hospital services.

The users’ expectations are growing day by day, and the cost of a complex system is continuously rising due to this expectation. Hence, the developer feels pressure to provide more effective healthcare applications at a lower cost, with better quality, and safe and secure health services for the patient record. This is the reason the developer is shifting to develop healthcare software applications with specific advanced technology such as Blockchain that solves the problem related to healthcare applications. According to the authors, software engineering plays an important role in many sectors, and healthcare application is one of them. In this paper, a new theory of software engineering related to healthcare software is introduced by the authors. They pointed out that better planning and balancing efforts deliver a safe and quality healthcare application. Lalband et al. (2019) discussed various modern software life-cycle models and represented how to implement smart healthcare applications using this model. According to the authors, systematic SDLC phases help to provide a high-quality healthcare application. Hence, healthcare always needs the best method and software development model. They also discussed the research challenges of software engineering for constructing a smart healthcare application.

Angraal et al. (2017), Bell et al. (2018), Katuwal et al. (2018), Ben Fekih et al. (2020) and De Aguiar et al. (2020) presented a survey of healthcare applications based on Blockchain. They discussed many applications in detail, such as electronic media records, pharmaceutical supply chain management, remote patient monitoring, health insurance claims, billing management, etc. They also discussed the limitation of healthcare applications based on Blockchain. They also pointed out some techniques that enhance the automation of medical services. According to the authors, Blockchain technology in healthcare applications can solve scalability, security and privacy issues. Blockchain technology removes the dependency of several mediators, decreases cost, and maintains trust and transparency. Siyal et al. (2019), Hasselgren et al. (2020) and Zhang and Boulos (2020) discussed the development of different healthcare applications using Blockchain technology.
