# 7.4.1. HIPAA, HITRUST, HITECH and GDPR (Shu and Jahankhani 2017; Magyar 2018; Jin et al. 2019)

HIPAA is a US regulated act which is an abbreviation for the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. HIPAA came into force in 1996 and since then there have been the following four major amendments:

+   - the Security Rule Amendment of 2003:
+     - technical safeguards,
+     - physical safeguards,
+     - administrative safeguards;
+   - the Privacy Rule Amendment of 2003;
+   - the Breach Notification Rule of 2009;
+   - the Final Omnibus Rule of 2013.

In this section, we will cover the first three amendments (except physical and administrative, as that is not relevant to this chapter) because we are here trying to understand how Blockchain as a technology is helpful in compliance with regulatory requirements while the fourth amendment is related to business associates affected by HIPAA.
