# 9.3.1.4. Spiral model

The spiral model is one of the most famous models of SDLC, as shown in Figure 9.7. The spiral model looks like a spiral with many loops. Each loop presents as a phase of a software process, such as determining objectives, identifying and resolving risks, development and testing, and planning the next iteration (Kumar et al. 2014). To develop the large and complex software, the spiral model is used in a real-time application. This model is helpful for reducing the risk factor of the project because it applies a risk reduction mechanism for analyzing risk at any stage. That is why it is known as a risk-driven process model. This model is used to build a high-quality product.

# 9.3.1.5. Agile model

The agile model is based on the iterative development approach (Barjtya et al. 2017). In the agile model, the whole software project is divided into smaller iterations and each iteration is considered as a frame, as shown in Figure 9.8. This division helps us to minimize the risk factor of the project and reduces time and cost.
