# Case 2: HOW MICROBIOME CENTRE CLOSES THE KNOWLEDGE GAP

The health of your intestines largely determines how you feel. If the bacterial collection in the intestines (the micro-organism) is not balanced, this may lead to various medical complaints. Nevertheless, the knowledge about this was limited until a few years ago and was anything but useful.
