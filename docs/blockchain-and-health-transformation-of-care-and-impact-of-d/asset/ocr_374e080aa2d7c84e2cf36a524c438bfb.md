# Only needed a money system

Nobel Prize winner in economic sciences in 1976 Milton Friedman, saw back in 1999 (Gobry 2014) that the internet would play a major role in reducing the role of government in our economic and social life. According to Friedman (Gobry 2014), only a reliable electronic money system was lacking that allows people to make money mutations without knowing the fellow operators. Such a system then resembles cash transactions with anonymity. According to many, Bitcoin has delivered on this promise. The internet already makes it possible to transfer information quickly and cheaply without paper and without intermediaries. Blockchain offers the same benefits for transferring values. The internet is used to transfer word and image; Blockchain, transactions.
