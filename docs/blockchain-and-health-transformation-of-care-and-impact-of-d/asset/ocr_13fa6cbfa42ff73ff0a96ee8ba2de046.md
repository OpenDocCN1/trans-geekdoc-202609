Small healthcare institutions will increasingly come into the picture if their (administrative) accountability is not in order. For large healthcare institutions, more and more attention is being paid to monitoring and protecting data. The approach is based on an (integrated) connection between care content, organizational form and IT infrastructure.The PlatformVmZ was established to shape healthcare much more from this perspective.

# PlatformVmZ structure & services

**Top Level Entities:**
- Care institution: Hiring, Subcontractor, Settlement, Payments, Ensuring quality, Dashboard for control
- Healthcare office: Contract, Statement, Payments, Ensuring quality, Dashboard for control
- Healthcare insurer: Statement, Payments, Healthcare purchasing, Payment agreement, Ensuring quality, Dashboard for control
- Municipality: Statement, Payments, Procurement, Ensuring quality, Dashboard for control

**PlatformVmZ Internal Structure:**
- Stichting Verbinden-met-Zorg WTZi (100% ownership -> Verbinden-met-Flex BV)
- VerbindenBV (50% ownership -> CuraVitel Bale BV (real estate & healthcare))
- Connection: contract services e.g. software
- Labels: - VmZAdvies, - Verbinden met Studenten, - De Start-Up Professional

**Bottom Level Service Flow:**
- Left Path: Care request, Choice of care provider, Client portal -> Client
- Middle Path: Care content (ECD), Ensuring independence, Support, Care administration -> Self employed professional
- Right Path: Consultancy, Beheer 'on demand', Virtueel hoofdkantoor -> Care institution

**Relationships:**
- Client <-> matching <-> Self employed professional
- Self employed professional <-> hiring <-> Care institution
- Care institution <-> students working while studying <-> MBO / HBO
- General practitioner, Hospital <-> Safe data transfer <-> Self employed professional / Care institution
