# The Simplest of Linear Models

Let me explain by showing some simple data in Figure 6.4.

| Number of Cats Owned | Likelihood I'll sneeze in your home |
|---|---|
| 0 | 3% |
| 1 | 20% |
| 2 | 36% |
| 3 | 45% |
| 4 | 67% |
| 5 | 80% |

Figure 6.4: Cat ownership versus me sneezing

In the pictured table, you have the number of cats in a house in the first column and the likelihood that I'll sneeze inside that house in the second column. No cats? Three percent of the time I sneeze anyway just because. Five cats? Well, then my sneezing is just about guaranteed—and probably more than just random dust or allergies.
