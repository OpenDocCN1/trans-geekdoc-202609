These two points are 8 – 4 = 4 feet apart in the horizontal direction. They’re 4 – 2 = 2 feet apart in the vertical direction. By the Pythagorean theorem then, the squared distance between these two points is 4² + 2² = 16 + 4 = 20 feet. So, the distance between them is the square root of 20, which is approximately 4.47 feet (see Figure 4.14).
