Now, if you want to multiply a de-acidification binary variable times the amount of juice you've ordered, what are the values that product can take on? There are a number of distinct possibilities:

+   • If both the indicator and the product purchase amount are 0, their product is 0.
+   • If you order some juice but decide to not reduce the acid, the product is still 0.
+   • If you order some juice and choose to reduce, the product is merely the amount of juice ordered.
