Our primary performance metric is the area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC). We think that a high true positive rate is crucial for medical diagnostics of non-life-threatening terminal diseases, such as the majority of neurodegenerative diseases, in order to identify all Alzheimer's patients as soon as possible. However, since we don't want to mistakenly identify a healthy adult as mentally ill and start medical treatment, we also want to make sure that the false positive rate is as low as possible. Therefore, AUC appeared to be the perfect option for a performance metric. The following table 1 shows the different classifiers with training score and testing score.
