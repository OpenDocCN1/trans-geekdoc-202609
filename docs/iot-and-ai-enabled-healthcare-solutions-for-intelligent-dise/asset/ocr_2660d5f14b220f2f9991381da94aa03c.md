# 2.1 Difficulties with Feature Selection

Feature selection is the fundamental problem in machine Learning. The main goal of feature selection challenge is to reduce the feature set’s dimension while maintaining efficacy and accuracy. To categorize the datasets, numerous techniques have been developed. However, metaheuristic algorithms have prompted a lot of curiosity in the treatment of numerous optimisation issues. The two inconsistent standards that are displayed by metaheuristic algorithms are the exploitation of the seek space and the exploration of the best possible outcome. Not every FS problem can be solved in the study’s domain using a metaheuristic-based approach. Execution of metaheuristic algorithms can be enhanced by offsetting exploration and exploitation of the search space, though improvements or alterations can be prepared to initiate a most recent version. Most of our efforts to build a model that predicts using hybridization techniques for resolving subsets of issues by minimizing the number of weakly essential and unnecessary features are motivated by this stimulus. In reality, a subset that is ideal is most likely to include only significantly relevant features.
