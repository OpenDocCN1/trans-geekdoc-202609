**Magnetic field:** MRI is commonly used to determine tumour size, identify new tumours in the breast, brain, and other regions of the body, and screen for tumours in humans. A screening MRI, in addition to yearly mammograms, is recommended for high-risk individuals. However, MRI can yield false positive results, prompting additional testing or biopsies. As a result, it is rarely advised as a screening procedure for people who are at medium risk of disease.
