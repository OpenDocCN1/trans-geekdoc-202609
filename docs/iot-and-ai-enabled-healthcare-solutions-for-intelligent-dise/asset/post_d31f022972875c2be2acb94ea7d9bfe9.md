### 5.1 Data Collection

Data collection is critical in building an effective machine-learning model for diabetes prediction.

- 1. Retinal Fundus Photography: Data were collected from publicly available databases such as the Messidor dataset and the Kaggle Diabetic Retinopathy dataset.
