The optic nerve is the main organ affected by glaucoma; it is a vital component that relays visual information from the eye to the brain. Intraocular pressure, or IOP, is the internal pressure inside the eye and can affect the optic nerve’s health. One important risk factor for optic nerve injury in glaucoma patients is elevated intraocular pressure.
