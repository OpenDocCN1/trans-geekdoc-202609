# 7.2 Clear and Intuitive Navigation

After applying user-centered design principles, a clear and intuitive navigation structure is created to allow users to easily access and explore different types of health data. Various navigation elements, such as menus, tabs, and breadcrumbs, are used to enhance usability. Visual cues and feedback are incorporated to indicate the user’s current location and status within the interface.
