Using that logic CDOs began gobbling up the BBB and the other lower-rated tranches of mortgage-backed securities. Between 2003 and 2007, $4 trillion in mortgage-backed securities were created, and Wall Street issued nearly $700 billion in CDOs that included mortgage-backed securities as collateral.

With ready buyers for their product, mortgage securitisers continued to demand loans for their pools, and hundreds of billions of dollars flooded the mortgage market.
