# 5.4 GRID METHODS FOR VANILLA OPTIONS

# 5.4.1 Introduction

In Section 5.3, we discussed the use of binomial lattice methods for valuing both European and American options. The lattice methods we have described the advantage that they are fairly easy to implement and can value simple options, such as vanilla puts and calls, *reasonably* accurately. The use of up- and down-jump probabilities at the lattice nodes is also an appealing feature, they are directly related to the stochastic process which is being modelled. However, lattice techniques have the following drawbacks:

+   - They require small time steps to ensure numerical stability.
- There is little control over where the lattice nodes are located. This can lead to poor accuracy when valuing certain types of options, for example, those with barriers at particular asset prices.
