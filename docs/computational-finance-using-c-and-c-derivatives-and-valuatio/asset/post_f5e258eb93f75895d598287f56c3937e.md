## 5.4.6 The Knockout Call Option

The purpose of this section is to provide an example which illustrates the benefits to be gained from using the log transformed Black-Scholes equation and also a nonuniform grid. The problem we will consider is the European double knockout option with strike $E$ and expiry date $T$. This is a barrier option with an upper barrier at $B_U$ and a lower barrier at $B_L$; during the life of the option, the asset price goes above the upper barrier or below the lower barrier, then the option becomes worthless. If, on the other hand, the asset price stays between the barriers, then the option has value $\max(S_T - E)$, where $S_T$ is the asset price at time $T$. This problem has been previously investigated by Boyle and Tian (1998), henceforth referred to as BT, which used explicit finite-difference method.
