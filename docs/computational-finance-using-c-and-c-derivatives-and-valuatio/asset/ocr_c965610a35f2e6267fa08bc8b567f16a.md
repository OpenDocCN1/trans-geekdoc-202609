# # Glossary

The notation used is as follows:

+   - **GBM** Geometric Brownian motion.

+   - **BM** Brownian motion.

+   - "$W_t$ Brownian motion at time $t$ - the term may be non-zero."

+   - $ho$ The correlation coefficient.

+   - $\sigma$ The volatility. Since assets are assumed to follow GBM it is computed as the generalised standard deviation of the $n$ continuously compounded returns.

$N_1(a)$ The univariate cumulative normal distribution. It gives the cumulative probability, in a standardized univariate normal distribution, that the variable $x_1$ satisfied $x_1 \leq a$.

$N_2(a,b,ho)$ The bivariate cumulative normal distribution. It gives the cumulative probability, in a standardized bivariate normal distribution, that the variables $x_1$ and $x_2$ satisfy $x_1 \leq a$ and $x_2 \leq b$ when with correlation between $x_1$ and $x_2$ is $ho$.

$r$ The free interest rate.

$q$ The continuously compounded dividend yield.

$S_t$ The asset price at time $t$.

$I_{nn}$ The $n$ by $n$ unit matrix.

$\Lambda(\mu,\sigma^2)$ A lognormal distribution with parameturs $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$. If $y = \log(x)$ and $y \sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ then the distribution for $x = e^y$ is $\Lambda(\mu,\sigma^2)$. We have $E[x] = \exp(\mu + \frac{\sigma^2}{2})$ and $Var[x] = \exp(2\mu + \sigma^2)(\exp(\sigma^2) - 1)$.

$DF(t,T)$ The discount factor between times $t$ and $T$, where $T \geq t$. The price of a non-defaultable coupon bond that matures at $T$ is the expected value of $DF(t,T)$. In this book we assume that interest rates are deterministic and thus $DF(t,T)$ is the value of a non-defaultable coupon bond maturing at $T$.
