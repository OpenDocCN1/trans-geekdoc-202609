The problem we will consider is the European double knockout call option with strike $E$ and expiry date $T$. This is a barrier option with an upper barrier at $B_U$ and a lower barrier at $B_L$; during the life of the option, the asset price either goes above the upper barrier or below the lower barrier, then the option becomes worthless. If, on the other hand, the asset price stays between the barriers, then the option has value max$(S_T - E)$, where $S_T$ is the asset price at time $T$.

This problem has been previously investigated by Boyle and Tian (1998), henceforth referred to as BT, which used explicit finite-difference method
