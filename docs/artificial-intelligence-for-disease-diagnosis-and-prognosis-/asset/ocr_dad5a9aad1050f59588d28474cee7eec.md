# 5.2 IMAGE ACQUISITION AND DATASETS

Getting access to the sufficient number of high-quality images is essential to evaluate the performance of skin disease recognition systems. Existence of the large amount of labeled data is even considered as the primary requirement for the training the reliable deep learning-based algorithms with high performance. Generally, skin disease images are acquired in two ways (shown in Figure 5.4): (a) Using dermoscope for dermoscopic images and (b) using normal digital cameras for clinical images. Each of these methods has their own advantages and disadvantages. Dermoscopy is a non-invasive imaging technique widely used in dermatology.
