Compared to existing techniques, the integrated system can categorize with less parameters and achieve maximum prediction accuracy. This strategy can lower complexity of the prediction methods as well as minimize computational load. To achieve higher classification rates, most weighted features as well as innovative techniques can be used. In conclusion, our preliminary reports suggest the use of accelerometers as an acceptable diagnostic tool in PD. In the end, the hybrid methodology supports early PD detection, this one will benefit all of us.
