External validation of AS classification

In the temporal validation the AS classifier was used to study 263 consecutive TTEs acquired at Tufts Medical Center with independently verified AS grade as assigned by a board certified echocardiographer. For the diagnostic screening task of identifying AS (all grades) the AUROC was 0.95. In this external validation cohort the prevalence of AS was 14.5%. The sensitivity was 94.7% and the specificity was 78.7%. Positive predictive value (PPV) was 42.9% and negative predictive value (NPV) was 98.9% (**Figure 5**).
