# De-identification

Leveraging known region locations that are encoded within the DICOM storage format, property software was created to automatically identify the image burn region that contains protected health information (PHI). By excluding these imaging regions from the data copy, images were reliably de-identified. A 10% samples of the included de-identified images was manually reviewed to confirm no PHI was included.

# Limited View labels

The study setup followed the cognitive steps involved in diagnosing AS by echocardiography (Figure 1), specifically view recognition followed by view interpretation. We collected expert annotations of a limited number of view types with two goals in mind; 1) to evaluate and validate automated view classification networks and 2) prioritized views are used in subsequent AS diagnostic models. Labels were assigned to examples of the parasternal long axis (PLAX), parasternal short axis at the level of the aortic valve (PSAX AoV).
