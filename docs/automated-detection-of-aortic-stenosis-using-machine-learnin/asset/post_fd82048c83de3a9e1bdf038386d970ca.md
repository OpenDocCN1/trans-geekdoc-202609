## Table 1. Aortic Stenosis Reference Labels

| Reference AS Severity | Grading Thresholds |
| :--- | :--- |
| Severe | valve area of < 1.0 cm², peak velocity > 4.0 m/s, or mean gradient > 40 mmHg |
| Moderate | valve area of < 1.0 cm², peak velocity < 4.0 m/s or mean gradient < 40 mmHg and LVOT derived stroke volume < 35 mL/m2 |
| Mild | valve area of 1.0-1.5 cm², peak velocity 3.0-4.0 m/s, or mean gradient 20-40 mmHg; valve area of < 1.5 cm², peak velocity 2.6-2.9 m/s, or mean gradient <20 mmHg |

**Table 1. AS Reference Labels.** Aortic severity was assigned using an integrative approach consistent with current American Society of Echocardiography Guidelines. The Severe AS reference label includes both high gradient and low gradient subtypes. AS severity was pulled from the echocardiogram report as assigned by the clinical reader. As part of routine care an additional label of ‘Mild to Moderate’ was assigned when hemodynamic profiles overlap the ‘Mild’ and ‘Moderate’ classes. Valve area represents the continuity derived valve area. LVOT is the left outflow tract diameter. mmHg is millimeters of mercury, m/s is meters per second.
