In example (B) clearly $ar{d} > 0$, but as the variances are high, we are not confident if $ar{d} > 0$ is due to a change in noise model or due to inherent randomness in the learning process (randomness in training set sampling, stochastic gradient descent, etc.). So, when we are able to reject $H_0$, we can say with high confidence that the decrease in performance is due to a change in the noise model (for example, a change from SLN to Veto+SLN).
