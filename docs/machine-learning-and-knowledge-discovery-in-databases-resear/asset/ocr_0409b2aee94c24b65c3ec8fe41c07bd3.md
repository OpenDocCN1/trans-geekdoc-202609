**Entropy-Optimized Graph Node Pruning Mechanism.** To enhance efficiency and reduce redundancy, we perform entropy-optimized graph node pruning on both the local and global graphs. (1) In the local graph, $\mathcal{G}_i$, a large number of street view images nodes $SV_{ij}$ are present, with each node represented as $oldsymbol{sv}_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in}}$. Here, $j = 1, 2, ..., |\mathcal{M}_i|$. To assess the informativeness of each node $SV_{ij}$, we first normalize its feature vector as $oldsymbol{sv}_{ij,f}^{	ext{norm}} = \frac{|oldsymbol{sv}_{ij,f}|}{\sum_{f=1}^{d_{in}} |oldsymbol{sv}_{ij,f}|}$ to ensure proportional scaling. We then compute its entropy $H(SV_{ij})$ and sampling probability $P(SV_{ij})$ as:

$H(SV_{ij}) = - \sum_{f=1}^{d_{in}} oldsymbol{sv}_{ij,f}^{	ext{norm}} \log(oldsymbol{sv}_{ij,f}^{	ext{norm}}), \quad P(SV_{ij}) = H(SV_{ij}) / \sum_{j=1}^{|SV_i|} H(SV_{ij}). \quad (3)$

Then existence of node $SV_{ij}$ follows a Bernoulli distribution, $SV_{ij} \sim 	ext{Bern}(P(SV_{ij}))$. Nodes with higher entropy values, and consequently higher $P(SV_{ij})$, are more likely to be retained, while low-entropy nodes are pruned. The retained nodes are selected based on the sampling ratio $\varepsilon$. The sampled street view node representations, where each is $oldsymbol{sv}_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in}}$, are averaged to derive an embedding $oldsymbol{sv}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in}}$ for region $U_i$. Similarly, an embedding for the road network is obtained as $oldsymbol{rn}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in}}$.

**(2)** In the global graph, $\mathcal{G}$, a large number of fine-grained street view images nodes and road network elements nodes are present in the region $U_i$. Applying the same pruning strategy as in the local graph, we retain high-entropy nodes and average their representations to obtain the final embeddings $\overline{oldsymbol{sv}}_i, \overline{oldsymbol{rn}}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in}}$ for region $U_i$.

**Modality-Aware Attention Mechanism.** To effectively capture the varying contributions of different modalities, we apply a modality-aware attention mechanism in both the local and global graphs. (1) In the local graph, $\mathcal{G}_i$ of region $U_i$, modality representations are denoted as $oldsymbol{m}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in}}$, where $oldsymbol{m} \in \{u, oldsymbol{p}, oldsymbol{tf}, oldsymbol{rs}, oldsymbol{sv}, oldsymbol{rn}\}$. Since different modalities contribute unequally to the final region representation, an attention weight vector $oldsymbol{w}_m \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in}}$ is learned for each modality to capture its importance. The modality-specific representation is computed as $oldsymbol{m}_i^{attn} = \sigma(oldsymbol{w}_m \odot oldsymbol{m}_i)$, where $\odot$ denotes element-wise multiplication and $\sigma$ is an activation function. To refine the information, a linear transformation [17] is applied, followed by an autoencoder [1] with a hidden dimension of $d_{hid}$, producing the final local modality representation $oldsymbol{m}_i^{final} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{out}}$.

**(2)** In the global graph $\mathcal{G}$, modality representations in region $U_i$ are given by $\overline{oldsymbol{m}}_i \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{in}}$, where $oldsymbol{m} \in \{u, oldsymbol{p}, oldsymbol{tf}, oldsymbol{rs}, oldsymbol{sv}, oldsymbol{rn}\}$. Applying the same modality-aware attention mechanism, we derive the final global modality representation as $\overline{oldsymbol{m}}_i^{final} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{out}}$.

**Local-Global Region Representation.** We compute coarse-grained, fine-grained, and overall local region representations $oldsymbol{H}_i^{\mathcal{CM}}, oldsymbol{H}_i^{\mathcal{FM}}, oldsymbol{H}_i^{local} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{out}}$ and global representation $oldsymbol{H}_i^{global} \in \mathbb{R}^{d_{out}}$ as described below:

$oldsymbol{H}_i^{\mathcal{X}} = \text{Mean} \left( \{ \boldsymbol{m}_i^{final} \mid \boldsymbol{m} \in \mathcal{X} \} \right), \quad \mathcal{X} \in \{ \mathcal{CM}, \mathcal{FM}, local \}, \\ \mathcal{CM} = \{ \boldsymbol{p}, \boldsymbol{tf}, \boldsymbol{rs} \}, \quad \mathcal{FM} = \{ \boldsymbol{sv}, \boldsymbol{rn} \}, \quad local = \{ u, \boldsymbol{p}, \boldsymbol{tf}, \boldsymbol{rs}, \boldsymbol{sv}, \boldsymbol{rn} \}, \quad (4) \\ \boldsymbol{H}_i^{global} = \text{Mean} \left( \{ \overline{\boldsymbol{m}}_i^{final} \mid \boldsymbol{m} \in \{ u, \boldsymbol{p}, \boldsymbol{tf}, \boldsymbol{rs}, \boldsymbol{sv}, \boldsymbol{rn} \} \} \right).$
