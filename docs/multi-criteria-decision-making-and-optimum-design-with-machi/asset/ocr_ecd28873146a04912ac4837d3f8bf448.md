Affairs Program of the Battelle Research Center developed DEMATEL between 1973 and 1976 to analyze and solve complex problems (Fontela & Gabus, 1974, 1976).

By creating structural models, DEMATEL helps groups make decisions and solve significant changes (Wu & Lee, 2007; Tseng, 2009). Since each criterion in a decision might affect any other, the DEMATEL technique is used to structure MCD problems via pairwise comparison. DEMATEL was developed using matrices and math theory and divides components into cause-and-effect groups. The technique was designed with the assumption that scientific research methods might help with understanding a specific problem, a cluster of interconnected difficulties, and a hierarchical structure of practical solutions (Jeng & Tzeng, 2012). Significant issues are conceptually difficult to solve; DEMATEL breaks down large problems into smaller ones and analyzes their relationships using collective data.

In the context of this study, the interaction between tourist destination decision and social media users is complex, and improvements should be developed towards the most important aspects. DEMATEL matrices or graphs show the textual relationships between system elements, with numerals representing influence or network strength (Chen-Yi et al., 2007).
