## 23.5 RESULTS AND DISCUSSION

The ranks in Table 23.5 show that applicant D2 earned the highest PCI and thereby the highest rank; the final rank order was D2 > D4 > D1 > D3, with D1 and D3 having a tie score. Based on the ranking, applicant D2 should receive the best math professor award.
