## 19.1 INTRODUCTION

Researchers analyzing digital data use supervised and unsupervised learning experiments with different digital data. For instance, Ana et al. The results from this chapter will be useful for determining the best-suited algorithms for machine learning (ML; Dipo et al., 2024) based on multiple parameters such as deviation from real value, mean scale prediction, the accuracy of the model, and computation performance of algorithms. ## 19.3 CLASSIFICATION

In information mining and AI, classification refers to allocating a pre-indicated class to obscure information that is given to the model. A learning model is built on the relationship correlation, the indicator characteristics, and the estimation of the objective. The challenge is to perfectly predict the class based on its findings on the previous data (Vaccaro et al., 2021).

### 19.3.2 OPTIMIZATION

All ML calculations have a fixed strategy of learning that relies on estimating their boundaries. When boundaries are dissimilar, the grouping fluctuates for each situation. The test in AI is to locate the most appropriate boundary estimations of the calculations for meeting the objective. There are a few enhancement procedures like genetic algorithms, particle swarm optimization, and search methods.
