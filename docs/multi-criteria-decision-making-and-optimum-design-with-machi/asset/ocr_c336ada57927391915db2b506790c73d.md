TPU has many properties, including elasticity, transparency, and resistance to oil, grease, and abrasion. It can be formulated as different materials, from soft and flexible elastomers to rigid plastics with high modulus. TPU’s flexibility and durability make it suitable for various applications such as medical devices, sports equipment, robotics, and functional prototypes.
