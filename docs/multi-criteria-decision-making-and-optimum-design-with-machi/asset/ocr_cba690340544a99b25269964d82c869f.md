# 7.5 CONCLUSION

We studied the unnotched impact toughness of GF-reinforced PBT/PA6 composites with seven GF ratios: 0%, 3%, 6%, 9%, 12%, 15%, and 18%. GF demonstrably increased the toughness in all cases, although the difference between the highest toughness for sample PBT/PA6/18%GF and the lowest, for PBT/PA6/0%GF, was only 15.95%. This result opens a new research direction for improving the toughness and other mechanical properties of PBT/PA6 composite by reinforcing with 18% GF. Factories and plastic manufacturers can increase the toughness of recycled PBT/PA6 blends by reinforcing them with GF, which can help with addressing environmental pollution.
