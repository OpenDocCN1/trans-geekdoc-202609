# 5.2.4 ISOLATED VERTEX

A vertex $\mathcal{U}_i \in \mathbb{V}$ in an IFG $\mathbb{G} = (\mathbb{V}, \mathbb{E})$ is said to be an isolated vertex if $\mu_2 \left(V_i, V_jight) = 0$ and $\gamma_2 \left(V_i, V_jight) = 0$.

# 5.2.5 CARDINALITY OF AN IFG

Let $\mathbb{G} = (\mathbb{V}, \mathbb{E})$ be a intuitionistic fuzzy graph. The cardinality of $\mathbb{G}$ is defined as

```
$|\mathbb{G}| = \sum_{V_i \in \mathbb{V}} \frac{1+T_{V_i}-F_{V_i}}{2} + \sum_{V_i,V_j \in \mathbb{E}} \frac{1+T_{V_{ij}}-F_{V_{ij}}}{2}$
```

# 5.2.6 DOMINATING SET

Let $\mathbb{G} = (\mathbb{V}, \mathbb{E})$ be an IFG and $\mathcal{U}_i$ and $\mathcal{V}_j \in \mathbb{V}$. Then that $\mathcal{U}_i$ dominates $\mathcal{V}_j$ if there exists a strong arc between them. A subset $\mathcal{V}_d \subseteq \mathbb{V}$ is said to be a dominating set in $\mathbb{G}$ if for every $\mathcal{V}_j \in \mathbb{V} - \mathcal{V}_d$, there exists $\mathcal{V}_j \in \mathcal{V}_d$ dominates $\mathcal{U}_i$.

# 5.3 MAXIMAL PRODUCT OF THREE INTUITIONISTIC FUZZY GRAPHS

In this section, we discuss the max product of three IFGs:

```
$\mathcal{N}_1' = \left(\sigma_{V_i}^1, \sigma_{V_i}^2, \sigma_{V_i}^3ight), \left(\mu_{V_i}^1, \mu_{V_i}^2, \mu_{V_i}^3ight)$,
$\mathcal{N}_2' = \left(\sigma_{V_j}^1, \sigma_{V_j}^2, \sigma_{V_j}^3ight), \left(\mu_{V_j}^1, \mu_{V_j}^2, \mu_{V_j}^3ight)$, and
$\mathcal{N}_3' = \left(\sigma_{V_k}^1, \sigma_{V_k}^2, \sigma_{V_k}^3ight), \left(\mu_{V_k}^1, \mu_{V_k}^2, \mu_{V_k}^3ight)$
```

be three IFGs. The product of the three graphs is defined as $\mathcal{N}_i' = (A_1, B_1); \mathcal{N}_2' = (A_2, B_2)$; and $\mathcal{N}_3' = (A_3, B_3)$ denoted by $\mathcal{N}_1' 	imes \mathcal{N}_2' 	imes \mathcal{N}_3'$ as follows:

+   (i) $(v', m', o') \in \mathbb{V}_1' 	imes \mathbb{V}_2' 	imes \mathbb{V}_3'
$(O', S', O') = O_1' * O_2' * O_3'((l', m', q)) = v \vee \{O_1'(l'), O_2'(m'), O_3'(o')\}$
$(T', S', T') = T_1' * T_2' * T_3'((l', m', r)) = \wedge \{T_1'(l'), T_2'(m'), T_3'(r')\}$
$(R', S', R') = R_1' * R_2' * R_3'((l', m', s)) = \wedge \{R_1'(l'), R_2'(m'), R_3'(s')\}$,
(ii) $v_p \in \mathbb{V}_1', v_q \in \mathbb{V}_2'$ and $l' \in \mathbb{E}_1'$
$(O', S', O') = O_1' * O_2' * O_3'((p, q, r)) = v \vee \{O_1'(p), O_2'(q), O_3'(r')\}$
$(T', S', T') = T_1' * T_2' * T_3'((p, q, r)) = \wedge \{T_1'(p), T_2'(q), T_3'(r')\}$
$(R', S', R') = R_1' * R_2' * R_3'((l, q, r)) = \wedge \{R_1'(l), R_2'(q), R_3'(r')\}$,
(iii) $v_p \in \mathbb{V}_1', v_q \in \mathbb{V}_2'$ and $m' \in \mathbb{E}_3'$
$(O', S', O') = O_1' * O_2' * O_3'((p, q, l')) = v \vee \{O_1'(p), O_2'(q), O_3'(l')\}$
$(T', S', T') = T_1' * T_2' * T_3'((p, q, m')) = \wedge \{T_1'(p), T_2'(q), T_3'(m')\}$
