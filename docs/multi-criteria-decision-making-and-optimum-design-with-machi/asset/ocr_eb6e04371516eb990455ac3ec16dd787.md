# 5.2 PRELIMINARIES

# 5.2.1 INTUITIONISTIC FUZZY GRAPHS

An intuitionistic fuzzy graph (IFG), $\mathbb{G} = (V, E)$ where $V=\{v_1, v_2...v_n\}$ is a finite vertex set that

+   (i) $\mu: V \rightarrow [0,1]$ and $\gamma_1: V \rightarrow [0,1]$ denote membership and nondeterministic membership, and $0 \leq \mu(v) + \gamma_1(v) \leq 1$ for every $v_i \in \mathbb{V}$.

+   (ii) $E \subseteq V \times V$ where $\mu_2: V \times V \rightarrow [0,1]; \gamma_2: V \times V \rightarrow [0,1]$ are such that $\mu_2(\{v_i, v_j\}) \leq \min{\mu(v_i), \mu(v_j)}\}; \gamma_2(\{v_i, v_j\}) \geq \max{\gamma_1(v_i), \gamma_1(v_j)}\ and where $0 \leq \mu_2(\{v_i, v_j\}) + \gamma_2(\{v_i, v_j\}) \leq 1\forall(v_i, v_j) \in \mathbb{E}$.

# 5.2.2 STRONG ARC

An arc $(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{V})$ is said to be strong

# 5.2.3 IFG DEGREES

The neighbourhood of $\mathcal{U}_i$ is denoted by $N(\\mathcal{U}_i)=\{\mathcal{V}_j \in V/(\mathcal{U}_i, \mathcal{V}_j)\}$ representing a strong arc.
