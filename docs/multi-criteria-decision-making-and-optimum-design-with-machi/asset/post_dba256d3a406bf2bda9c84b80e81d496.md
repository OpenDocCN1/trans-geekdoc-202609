## 20.1 INTRODUCTION

Efficient sorting algorithms serve as the backbone of various data processing tasks, significantly impacting the overall computational efficiency (Satish et al., 2009). Sorting algorithms, such as Quick Sort and Merge Sort, have been extensively studied and are well established for large data sets. However, there exists a unique challenge in the realm of sorting: data sets of intermediate sizes.
