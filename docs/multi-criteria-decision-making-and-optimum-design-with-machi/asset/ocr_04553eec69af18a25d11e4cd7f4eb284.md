# 7.2.2 MEASUREMENT METHODS

First, we injection molded and produced samples. Next, we selected five random samples to measure the unnotched Izod toughness according to the ASTM D2566 standard. The sample in Figure 7.1 is 63.5 ± 12.7 ± 0.2 mm. To measure impact toughness, we used the Tiusens OTS04 impact toughness tester in Figure 7.2, including digital controller 104; this controller allows quick and accurate calculations and meets most experimental needs. The primary impact energy of the hammer is 2.82/2 J with a standard height of 610 mm; the corresponding impact speed is 3.46 m/s. However, the aerodynamic design allows lowering the height of the hammer, meeting the need for impact testing smaller than 2.82 J, which the impact energy can easily vary up to 25 J using different load sets. The machine has a solid structure with speeds of 3.14 m/s and 2.91 m/s, respectively. mounted on the hammer, the impact design that ensures impact accuracy and energy loss caused by wind.
