## 11.3.2 THEOREM

Let G be the regular IFG. Then we have

$M(G) = c^2 \sum_{i=1}^n [T_s(u_i) + F_s(u_i)], \forall u_i \in S \text{ where } \sum_{v \neq u_i} T_s(v, u) = c, \sum_{v \neq u_i} F_s(v, u) = c$

Proof: Given the degree of the definition of each points, $d[v] = [d_r(v), d_r(v)]$

$d(v) = \left[ \sum_{v \neq u} T_s(v, u), \sum_{v \neq u} F_s(v, u) \right]$.

On the other hand, for regular intuitionistic fuzzy graph, we know that $\sum_{v \neq u} T_s(v, u) = c, \sum_{v \neq u} F_s(v, u) = c$

$d(v) = [d_r(v), d_r(v)] = (c, c)$

Now by applying the formula in the first Zagreb index, we will get the results.

## 11.4 ALGORITHMS FOR HARMONIC INDEX AND RANDIĆ INDEX IN INTITIONISTIC FUZZY GRAPH

Let $G$ be an IFG with membership and non-membership values of vertices $S$ and edges $T$.

**Input:** vertex set, edge set, and degrees for $G$
**Output:** $H(G)=$ Harmonic index

$H(G) = \frac{1}{2}\left[\sum_{(u_i)\in T(G)}\frac{1}{(T_s(u_i),F_s(u_i))d(u_i)+(T_s(u_i),F_s(u_i))d(v_i)}\right]$

- **Step 1:** sum of product of vertex and the degrees of adjacent vertices
- **Step 2:** Input step 1 value.
- **Step 3:** Divide step 2 by 2.

**Input:** vertex set, edge set and degrees for $G$
**Output:** $R(G)=$ Randić index

$R(G)=\frac{1}{2}\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{1}{\sqrt{(T_s(u_i),F_s(u_i))d(u_i)\cdot(T_s(u_i),F_s(u_i))d(v_i)}}\right]$

- **Step 1:** Find the product of the vertex and the degrees of each vertex.
- **Step 2:** Sum the products of the adjacent vertex from step 1.
- **Step 3:** Take the root of the step 2 value.
- **Step 4:** Enter the step 3 value.
- **Step 5:** Divide step 3 value by 2.

These procedures also give the remaining topological indices for IFGs.
