## INTRODUCTION

Global development has led to severe energy crises, environmental pollution, and de-forestation and natural resource depletion. Electric vehicles (EVs) are crucial for sustainable development. Electric vehicle charging stations (EVCS) serve as the backbone of EV industry, promoting sustainable energy and energy penetration. However, the optimal location of EVCS is a complex challenge that must balance economic, social, and environmental dimensions to address rising CO2 emissions and atmospheric pollution.

This paper utilizes a neutrosophic TOPSIS approach for the selection of EVCS, considering uncertain and incomplete factors.

## 1.2 PRELIMINARIES

Neutrosophic TOPSIS operates as a compensatory method and assumes total aggregation among criteria. The determination of weights and scores primarily involves pairwise comparisons between available options.

### 1.2.1 FUZZY SET

A fuzzy set $\tilde{A}$ in discourse X is defined by a membership function $\mu_{\tilde{A}}(x)$ that associates each element $x$ in X with a real number in the interval [0, 1], where $\mu_{\tilde{A}}$ represents the degree of membership of $x$ in $\mu(x)$, with a higher value indicating a stronger association. The membership function quantifies the degree to which elements in the universe X belong to the $\tilde{A}$. When $\mu_{A}(x)$ is closer to unity, it signifies a higher degree of membership for $x$ in $\tilde{A}$.

### 1.2.2 NEUTROSOPHIC TOPSIS

Neutrosophic TOPSIS is an extension of the classical TOPSIS method that incorporates neutrosophic set theory. TOPSIS is a decision-making technique used for ranking alternatives based on their proximity to an ideal solution. Neutrosophic set theory is an extension of classical set theory that allows for the representation of indeterminacy, uncertainty, and inconsistency. In Neutrosophic TOPSIS, the decision matrix and the criteria weights are expressed in the form of neutrosophic numbers.
