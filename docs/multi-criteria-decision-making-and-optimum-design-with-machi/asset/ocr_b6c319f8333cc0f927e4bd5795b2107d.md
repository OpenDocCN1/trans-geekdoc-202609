# 3.4.1 DEFINING THE PROBLEM

The optimization problem is the following: Find $x = [P, Q, V_c, f_z, a_p]$ to minimize $\{R_a, F_c\}$, and maximize $[MRR]$ with the constraint in Equation (3.18):

```
\begin{cases} 1 \le P \le 5 \ 50 \le Q \le 150 \ 60 \le V_z \le 240 \ 0.02 \le f_z \le 0.10 \ 0.1 \le a_p \le 0.9 \end{cases}
```

where P (bar) is the MQL air pressure, Q (ml/h) is the MQL oil flow rate, $V_c$ (m/min) is the cutting speed, $f_z$ (mm/tooth) is the feed per tooth, and $a_p$ (mm) is the depth of the cut.

# 3.4.2 INTEGRATING SVR AND NSGA-II WITH ENTROPY-WEIGHTED TOPSIS

As we have noted, for this study we employed a hybrid approach of combining SVR and NSGA-II with entropy-weighted TOPSIS to determine the ideal technological parameters for milling Ti-6Al-4V alloy with minimal lubrication. We utilized SVR to establish a regression model linking $R_a$ (Figure 3.12); (Figure 3.13); and cutting parameters $P, Q, V_c, f_z$, and $a_p$. This model formed the foundation for multi-objective optimization via NSGA-II.

The outcomes of multi-objective optimization obtained through NSGA-II in a Pareto set, which this Pareto represents a collection of feasible optimal values.
