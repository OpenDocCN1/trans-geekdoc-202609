# Multi-Criteria Decision-Making with Machine Learning

For Chapter 4, Trieu Khoa Nguyen et al. conducted a deep study on selecting 3D printing parameters to optimize tensile strength using the Taguchi-based response surface method. By systematically investigating the influence of various printing parameters, the authors offered valuable insights into optimizing the mechanical properties of 3D-printed components. However, challenges persist in accurately predicting the relationships between printing parameters and mechanical properties due to the complex nature of additive manufacturing processes.

Annamalai Meenakshi et al. introduced an enhanced MCDM optimization approach using the method to optimize critical project management networks by integrating intuitionistic fuzzy graphs. While this methodology shows promise in network efficiency, further research is needed to validate its effectiveness in practical applications.

In Chapter 6, Pham et al. investigated the optimization of surface roughness in H13 steel machined by wire discharge machining. By exploring the effects of discharge time on surface roughness, the authors offered valuable insights into optimizing machining parameters for improved surface finish. However, challenges remain in balancing competing objectives and optimizing processes for complex materials.

Cong Nguyen et al. examined the impact toughness of a PBT/PA6 composite reinforced with glass fibers in Chapter 7. Experimental analysis, highlighted the potential of glass fiber reinforcement for enhancing the mechanical properties of polymer composites. However, optimizing the reinforcement process to achieve desired mechanical properties poses challenges due to the complex interactions between materials and processing parameters.

In Chapter 8, Truong and fellow researchers explored the effect of chamber temperature on the flexural strength of thermoplastic polyurethane via fusion deposition modeling methodology. This underscores the importance of temperature control in achieving desired mechanical properties in 3D-printed parts. However, challenges persist in optimizing printing parameters for specific materials and applications.

Ganesh Khekare et al. focused on enhancing underwater imagery using MCDM and machine learning in Chapter 9. By employing advanced image methodologies, the authors were aiming to improve the interpretability of underwater images for relation. However, challenges remain in mitigating environmental factors and ensuring the reliability of underwater imaging systems.

In Chapter 10, Mousmi Karmakar and Subhaj Bhattacharya awarded select optimal sites for electric vehicle charging stations using analytic hierarchy and the VIKOR. By integrating MCDM with hybrid ranking, the authors offer a systematic approach to selecting optimal sites, but challenges persist in considering uncertainty and qualitative aspects of site criteria.

J. Sengamal and V. Gomathi introduced optimal indices on topological fuzzy graphs in Chapter 11. By defining various topological indices, the authors advance graph applications in computer science, but challenges remain in developing robust algorithms and applications for intuitionistic fuzzy graphs.
