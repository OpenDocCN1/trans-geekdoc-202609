# 7.3.2 THE SAMPLE MICROSTRUCTURES

After measurement, we took samples to capture the microscopic structures at the fracture surfaces for observation (Figure 7.5).

Figure 7.6 shows the microstructures of the PBT/PA6/GF samples with each GF ratio: 0, 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, and 18% GF. Observing sample PBT/PA6/0%GF, we see small round dots that are PA6 plastic components evenly dispersed on the surface of the PBT substructure. Next, the PBT/PA6/9%GF sample shows cylindrical fibers that are the GF component; at this time, the small round dots gradually disappear, and the PBT/PA6 mixture becomes smoother. We finally observe that the samples with higher proportions of GF show increasingly even distribution on the PBT/PA6 substrate.
