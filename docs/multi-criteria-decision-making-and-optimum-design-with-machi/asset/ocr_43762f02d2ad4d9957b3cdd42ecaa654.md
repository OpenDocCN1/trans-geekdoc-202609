# Optimizing Surface Roughness of H13 Steel Machined by Wire EDM

Figure 6.4 presents the influence of EDM parameters on the surface roughness of H13 steel using a main effects plot for means. The optimal values based on the high-point in the graphs are T_{on} = 6 µs, T_{off} = 14 µs, and Ra = 1.91 µm. The regression equation was Equation (6.2):

```
Ra = 1.360 + 0.1171 × T_{on} + 0.0071 × T_{off}
```

The equation indicates that the ratio of T_{on} is 0.1171, which is considerably higher than the 0.0071 for T_{off}. That is, T_{on} has a stronger influence on surface roughness T_{off}, which is consistent with the results in Table 6.4. In Table 6.7, the F values corroborate this strong influence of T_{on}.
