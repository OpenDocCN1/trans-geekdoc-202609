# ## 15.1 INTRODUCTION

Computers can learn on their own, without being taught, to carry out tasks, according to the core idea of machine learning. Given its capacity to automatically identify patterns in data and predict future outcomes, machine learning has become increasingly demanding for making judgments in a variety of areas, including financial trading and medical diagnosis.

To resolve ambiguous circumstances, Fuzzy theory [11] is essential. After the fuzzy set was extended, a new set known as intuitionistic fuzzy sets [9] was developed. Smandache developed the general idea of neutrosophic sets and later later scholars exposed as Pythagorean, bipolar [2, 5], and Fermate [7] neutrosophic sets. Broumei measured the Hausdorff distance between two neutrosophic sets [2, 3], and digital neutrosophic topological spaces were first suggested by Nirmada Devi [4, 5, 6]. Incomplete and ambiguous data in MCDM [10] challenges exposed the variable of approximate reasoning and pushed the decision-making process to new advancements and innovation.

The theory of neutrosophic logics extends to include number theory, operations research, algebra, topology, probability, and numeric measures. Field neuro neutrosophic theory is exposed in a wide range of fields including image processing, large-scale image and multimedia processing, machine learning, big data analytics, deep learning, visualization, feature learning, classification, regression, and clustering, virtual reality, heterogeneous data mining multimodal sensor data, sensor networks networks astronomy, space sciences, bioinformatics and medical analytics, retrieval of medical images, brain-machine interfaces, medical signal analysis, and large-scale health care data.
