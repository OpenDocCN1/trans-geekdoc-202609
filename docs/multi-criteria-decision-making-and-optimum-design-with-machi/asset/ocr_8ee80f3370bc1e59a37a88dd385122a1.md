# 13.6.5 MODEL VALIDATION AND EVALUATION AND ETHICS AND BIAS CONCERNS

Ensuring the reliability and accuracy of ML models is paramount in decision support. Rigorous validation techniques are essential for assessing the model's performance and robustness. Cross-validation involves splitting the data into training and testing subsets, preventing models from being overly tailored to specific points (Albari et al., 2023). Additionally, benchmarking against existing methods helps establish the model's value relative to existing decision-making approaches. ML models can inadvertently inherit biases in the training data, perpetuating unfair or discriminatory outcomes. Addressing these concerns requires ongoing vigilance and effort (Siddiki et al., 2023). Practitioners must conduct thorough audits to identify and mitigate biases, apply techniques fairness-aware, and design reward functions in reinforcement learning scenarios that promote fairness across different groups and demographics (Sun et al., 2023).

# 13.6.6 DECISION-MAKER INVOLVEMENT AND TRANSPARENT COMMUNICATION

Collaborating with domain experts and experts throughout the modeling process is essential for building effective ML models. These stakeholders possess critical domain knowledge, which helps shape the problem definition, data selection, feature engineering, and model evaluation (Arunagiri et al., 2023). Involvement from beyond ensures that the models capture relevant insights and criteria accurately, enhancing the model's practicality and real-world applicability. Transparent communication of model results, limitations, and uncertainties is crucial to fostering trust and understanding among decision-makers and stakeholders (Habib et al., 2023). Clarifying how the model generates predictions, the rationale behind its decisions, and communication highlights the model's value while acknowledging its limitations and constraints (Belmecher et al., 2023).
