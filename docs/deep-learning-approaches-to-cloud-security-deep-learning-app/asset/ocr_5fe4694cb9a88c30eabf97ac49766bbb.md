# 4.2 Study of Bi-Facial Solar Panel

Bifarious (i.e., Bi-facial) solar panels Figure 4.2, are one of the most effective and efficient sources of solar power generation. They have solar PV cells in the front side of the panel, as well as on the back. This construction of PV placement generates effective power generation is small sizes.

An important feature of these panels is that they can be placed in small areas yet can deliver two times the power of conventional panels.
