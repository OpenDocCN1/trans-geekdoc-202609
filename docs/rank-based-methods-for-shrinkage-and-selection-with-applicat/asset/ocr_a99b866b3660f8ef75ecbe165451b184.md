**Theorem 5.3** Under the local alternatives \(K_{(n)}\), the asymptotic distributional bias, and ADL\(_2\)-risk of the ridge R-estimator, \(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{ridgeR}}(\chi)\), are respectively given by

\(\text{ADB}(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{ridgeR}}(\chi)) = \begin{pmatrix} \mathbf{0}_{p_1} \\ -\frac{\kappa}{1+\kappa}\boldsymbol{\mathcal{S}}_2 \end{pmatrix}\)

and

\(\text{ADL}_2\text{-risk}(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{ridgeR}}(\chi)) = \eta^2 \left[ \text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[1]}) + \frac{1}{(1+\kappa)^2} \left( \text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]}) + \kappa^2 \frac{\boldsymbol{\delta}_2^\top \boldsymbol{\delta}_2}{\eta^2} \right) \right]\)

where \(\mathbf{D}_{22} = \text{Diag}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[1]}, \mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})\).

The lower bound of ADL\(_2\)-risk is

\(\text{ADL}_2^*\text{-risk}(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{ridgeR}}(\chi)) = \eta^2 \left[ \text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[1]}) + \frac{1}{(1+\kappa)^2} \left( \text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]}) + \kappa^2 \Delta^2 \text{Ch}_{\min}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]}) \right) \right]. \quad (5.2.20)\)

The optimum value of \(\kappa\) for the lower bound given in Eq. (5.2.20) for the ADL\(_2\)-risk(\(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{ridgeR}}(\chi)\)) is

\(\kappa^* = \frac{\text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})}{\Delta^2 \text{Ch}_{\min}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})}.\)

Hence,

\(\text{ADL}_2\text{-risk}(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{ridgeR}}(\kappa^*)) = \eta^2 \left[ \text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[1]}) + \frac{\text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})\Delta^2 \text{Ch}_{\min}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})}{\text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]}) + \Delta^2 \text{Ch}_{\min}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})} \right].\)

As a result, the lower bound ADL\(_2\)-risk efficiency of \(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{ridgeR}}(\chi)\) is given by

\(\text{ADRE}(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{ridgeR}}(\kappa^*) : \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_n^{\text{UR}}) = \left( 1 + \frac{\text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})}{\text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[1]})} \right) \times \left( 1 + \frac{\text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})\Delta^2 \text{Ch}_{\min}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]})}{\text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[1])}(\text{tr}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[1]}) + \Delta^2 \text{Ch}_{\min}(\mathbf{D}_{22}^{[2]}))} \right)^{-1}\)

which is similar to Eq. (5.2.19).
