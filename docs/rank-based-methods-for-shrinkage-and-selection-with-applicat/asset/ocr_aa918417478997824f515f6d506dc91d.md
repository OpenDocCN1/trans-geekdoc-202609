**Theorem 9.2** Under \(K_{(n)}\) and the regularized conditions stated in Theorem 9.1,
we have

+   (i) \[
+   \lim_{n \to \infty} P \left( \mathscr{L}_n \le \lambda^2 \middle| K_{(n)} \right) = \mathcal{H}_{P_x}(\lambda^2, \Delta^2), \quad \Delta^2 = \sigma_\phi^2 \left( \delta_\phi^\top \Sigma_{22.1} \delta_2 \right)
+   \]

+   (ii) \[
+   \lim_{n \to \infty} P \left( \sqrt{n} \left( \hat{\rho}_{1n}^{\text{UR}} - \rho_1 \right) \le \boldsymbol{x} \middle| K_{(n)} \right) = \Phi_{p_1}(\boldsymbol{x}; \mathbf{0}, \eta^2 \Sigma_{11.2}^{-1})
+   \]

+   (iii) \[
+   \lim_{n \to \infty} P \left( \sqrt{n} \left( \hat{\rho}_{1n}^{\text{RR}} - \rho_1 \right) \le \boldsymbol{x} \middle| K_{(n)} \right) = \Phi_{p_1}(\boldsymbol{x} + \Sigma_{11}^{-1} \Sigma_{12} \delta_2; \mathbf{0}, \eta^2 \Sigma_{11}^{-1})
+   \]

+   (iv) \[
+   \lim_{n \to \infty} P \left( \sqrt{n} (\hat{\rho}_{1n}^{\text{PTR}}(\lambda) - \rho_1) \le \boldsymbol{x} \middle| K_{(n)} \right) = \\
+   \mathcal{H}_{P_{x_2}}(\lambda^2, \Delta^2) G_{p_1}(\boldsymbol{x} + \Sigma_{11}^{-1} \Sigma_{12} \delta_2, \mathbf{0}, \eta^2 \Sigma_{11}^{-1}) \\
+   + \int_{E(\delta_2)} \Phi_{p_1}(\boldsymbol{x} - \boldsymbol{D}_{12} \boldsymbol{D}_{22}^{-1} \boldsymbol{z}; \mathbf{0}, \eta^2 \boldsymbol{D}_{11.2}) d\Phi_{p_2}(\boldsymbol{z}, \mathbf{0}, \eta^2 \boldsymbol{D}_{22})
+   \]

where \(\Phi_\nu(\cdot)\) is the multivariate normal c.d.f. \(\boldsymbol{D} = \Sigma^{-1}\) and the \(D_{ij}\) and \(D_{ii \cdot j}\) are
defined in the usual way. Further,

\(E(\delta_2) = \{ \boldsymbol{Z} : \eta^2 (\boldsymbol{Z} + \delta_2)^\top \Sigma_{22.1} (\boldsymbol{Z} + \delta_2) \}.\)

Finally,

+   (v) \[
+   \sqrt{n} \left( \hat{\rho}_{1n}^{\text{SSR}} - \rho_1 \right) \xrightarrow{d} \boldsymbol{D}_1 \boldsymbol{U} + \frac{c \eta^2 \Sigma_{11}^{-1} \Sigma_{12} (\boldsymbol{D}_2 \boldsymbol{U} + \delta_2)}{(\boldsymbol{D}_2 \boldsymbol{U} + \delta_2)^\top \Sigma_{22.1} (\boldsymbol{D}_2 \boldsymbol{U} + \delta_2)}
+   \]

where \(\boldsymbol{U} \sim \mathcal{N}_p(\mathbf{0}, \eta^2 \Sigma)\) and \(\boldsymbol{D} = \begin{pmatrix} \boldsymbol{D}_1 \\ \boldsymbol{D}_2 \end{pmatrix}\).

Using linearity results imply that

\(\hat{\rho}_{1n}^{\text{RR}} = \hat{\rho}_{1n}^{\text{UR}} + \Sigma_{11}^{-1} \Sigma_{12} \hat{\rho}_{2n}^{\text{UR}} + o_p(n^{-\frac{1}{2}})\) (9.4.2)

\(\mathscr{L}_n = \frac{1}{\eta^2} \left( \hat{\rho}_{2n}^{\text{UR}} \right)^\top \Sigma_{22.1} \hat{\rho}_{2n}^{\text{UR}} + o_p(1).\) (9.4.3)

Therefore,

\(\sqrt{n} \left( \left( \hat{\rho}_{1n}^{\text{UR}} - \rho_1 \right)^\top, (\hat{c}_{2n}^{\text{UR}} - n^{-\frac{1}{2}} \delta_2)^\top \right)^\top \xrightarrow{d} \mathcal{N}_p(\mathbf{0}, \eta^2 \begin{pmatrix} \Sigma_{11.2}^{-1} & \mathbf{0} \\ \mathbf{0} & \Sigma_{22 \cdot 1}^{-1} \end{pmatrix}).\) (9.4.4)
