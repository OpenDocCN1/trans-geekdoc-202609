# Case 2. When the null hypothesis does not hold.

The risk difference \(\text{ADL}_2\text{-risk}(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{SSLiRuR+}}(d)) - \text{ADL}_2\text{-risk}(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{PTLiRuR}}(d))\) will be non-positive when

\(\eta^T F_d^T F_d \eta \ge \frac{f_3(\Delta^2, d, \alpha)}{g_2(\Delta^2, \alpha)}\) (8.5.3)

where

\(f_3(\Delta^2, d, \alpha) = \eta^2 \text{tr}(F_d A F_d^T) \{ \mathcal{H}_{q+2}(\chi_{q,\alpha}^2; \Delta^2) - (q-2)X(\Delta^2) + a_1 \} -2(1-d)\eta^T F_d^T (C + I_p)^{-1} \beta \{ (q-2) E(\chi_{q+2}^{-2}(\Delta^2)) - \mathcal{H}_{q+2}(\chi_{q,\alpha}^2; \Delta^2) - a_3 \}\)

and

\(g_2(\Delta^2, \alpha) = Z(\alpha, \Delta^2) - (q-2)Y(\Delta^2) + a_2 + 2a_3.\)

Also,

+   \(a_1 = \mathbb{E} \left[ \left( 1 - (q-2)\chi_{q+2}^{-2}(\Delta^2) \right)^2 I \left( \chi_{q+2}^2(\Delta^2) \le q-2 \right) \right] \\ a_2 = \mathbb{E} \left[ \left( 1 - (q-4)\chi_{q+4}^{-2}(\Delta^2) \right)^2 I \left( \chi_{q+4}^2(\Delta^2) \le q-4 \right) \right] \\ a_3 = \mathbb{E} \left[ \left( (q-2)\chi_{q+2}^{-2}(\Delta^2) - 1 \right) I \left( \chi_{q+2}^2(\Delta^2) \le q-2 \right) \right].\)

Since \(\Delta^2 > 0\), assume that both the numerator and the denominator of Eq. (8.5.3) are positive or negative, respectively. Then \(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{SSLiRuR+}}(d)\) dominates \(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{PTLiRuR}}(d, \alpha)\) when

\(\Delta^2 \le \Delta_3^2(\Delta^2, d, \alpha) = \frac{f_3(\Delta^2, d, \alpha)}{\text{Ch}_{\max}[(F_d^T F_d) C^{-1}] \times g_2(\Delta^2, \alpha)},\)

and \(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{PTLiRuR}}(d, \alpha)\) dominates \(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{SSLiRuR+}}(d)\) when

\(\Delta^2 > \Delta_4^2(\Delta^2, d, \alpha) = \frac{f_3(\Delta^2, d, \alpha)}{\text{Ch}_{\min}[(F_d^T F_d) C^{-1}] \times g_2(\Delta^2, \alpha)}.\)

Now, we consider the risk difference of PRLRE and PTLRE as a function of the eigenvalues and define

\(d_2(\Delta^2, \alpha) = \frac{f_4(\Delta^2, \alpha)}{g_3(\Delta^2, \alpha)},\)

where

\(f_4(\Delta^2, \alpha) = \max_i \{ \lambda_i q_{ii} P_1 + \lambda_i \eta_i^{*2} P_2 + \theta_i \eta_i^{*} P_3 \}\)
