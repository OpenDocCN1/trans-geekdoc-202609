Further, under \(K_{(n)}\)

\(\left( \sqrt{n}(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\widehat{s}_1}^{\text{ridge}\mathbb{R}} (\gamma_n) - \boldsymbol{\beta}_{\widehat{s}_1}^*)^\top, \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\widehat{s}_2}^{\text{ridge}\mathbb{R}} (\gamma_n) - n^{-\frac{1}{2}} \boldsymbol{\delta}_{\widehat{s}_2}^*)^\top \right)^\top = n^{-\frac{1}{2}} \gamma^{-1} (\boldsymbol{\phi}, f) \boldsymbol{C}_n^{-1} \boldsymbol{L}_n (\boldsymbol{\beta}) + o_p(1) \xrightarrow{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{N}_{p_{1n}+p_{2n}} (\boldsymbol{0}, \eta^2 \boldsymbol{\gamma}^{-1}).\)

Now, by definition, \(\boldsymbol{D}_{11} = \boldsymbol{C}_{11}^{-1} \boldsymbol{C}_{12} \boldsymbol{D}_{21}\) and \(\boldsymbol{D}_{12} + \boldsymbol{C}_{11}^{-1} \boldsymbol{C}_{12} \boldsymbol{D}_{22} = \boldsymbol{0}\) and we may write \(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{1n}^{\text{RR}} = \boldsymbol{L}_1 \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\widehat{s}_1}^{\text{ridge}\mathbb{R}} (\gamma_n)\) and \(\mathcal{L}_n = \left[ \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\widehat{s}_3}^{\text{ridge}\mathbb{R}} (\gamma_n) \right]^\top \boldsymbol{L}_2 \left[ \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\widehat{s}_3}^{\text{ridge}\mathbb{R}} (\gamma_n) \right] + o_p(1)\), where \(\boldsymbol{L}_1 \boldsymbol{C}^{-1} \boldsymbol{L}_2 = \boldsymbol{0}\). Hence, using Eq. (10.7.4), we conclude \(\sqrt{n}(\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\widehat{s}_1}^{\text{ridge}\mathbb{R}} (\gamma_n) - \boldsymbol{\beta}_{\widehat{s}_1}^*)\) and \(\mathcal{L}_n\) are asymptotically independent, while the joint distribution of \((\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}}_{\widehat{s}_2}^{\text{ridge}\mathbb{R}} (\gamma_n) - n^{-\frac{1}{2}} \boldsymbol{\delta}_{\widehat{s}_2}^*)\) and \(\mathcal{L}_n\) can be obtained by integrating over the appropriate subspace.
