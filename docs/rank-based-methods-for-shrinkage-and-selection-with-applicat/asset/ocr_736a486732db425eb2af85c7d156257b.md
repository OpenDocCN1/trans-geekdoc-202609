$$X_t = \rho_1 X_{t-1} + ... + \rho_{p_1} X_{t-p_1} + \varepsilon_t, \quad 1 \le t \le n, p_1 \ge 1. \quad (9.1.4)$$

Our primary interest in this chapter is to develop the theory of R-estimation of various types, beginning with selection of subsets and ending with shrinkage R-estimation.

# ## 9.2 R-estimation of $\rho$ for the AR($p$)-Model

In this section, we introduce a class of R-estimators of $\rho$ for the AR($p$) model of Eq. (9.1.1) and discuss their large sample properties. Let $Y_t = (X_t, ..., X_{t-p+1})^\top, 1 \le t \le n$, and $R_t(b)$ be the rank of $X_t - b^\top Y_{t-1}$ among $\{X_j - b^\top Y_{j-1}, 1 \le j \le n\}$. Set $R_i(b) = 0$ for $i \le 0$. Let $\phi$ be a non-decreasing function from $[0, 1]$ to the real line and define $L_n(b) = (L_1(b), ..., L_p(b))^\top$, where

$$L_s(b) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{n}} \sum_{t=s+1}^n X_{t-s} \phi \left( \frac{R_t(b)}{n+1} \right), \quad 1 \le t \le p, \ b \in \mathbb{R}^p. \quad (9.2.1)$$

The class of rank statistics, $L_n(b)$, one for each $\phi$, is analogous to a class of similar rank statistics in linear regression models where one replaces the weights $\{X_{t-j}\}$ by appropriate design points.

Thus, for the R-estimation of $\rho$, we apply Jaeckel (1972) to the AR($p$)-model. Accordingly, we set

$$a_n(t) = \phi \left( \frac{t}{n+1} \right), \quad 1 \le t \le n,$$

and $Z_{(t)}(b)$ is the $t$th largest residual among $\{X_k - b^\top Y_{k-1}, 1 \le k \le n\}$, and

$$D_n(b) = \sum_{t=1}^n a_n(t) Z_{(t)}(b), \quad b \in \mathbb{R}^p. \quad (9.2.2)$$

If $\sum_{t=1}^n a_n(t) = 0$, then $D_n(b)$ may be shown to be convex on $\mathbb{R}^p$ with its derivative \(-n^{-\frac{1}{2}} L_n(b)\). Thus, a minimum of $D_n(b)$ exists.

Let us now define the R-estimator $\hat{\rho}_n^{UR}$ of $\rho$ by the relation

$$\inf_{b \in \mathbb{R}^p} \| L_n(b) \| = \left\| L_n(\hat{\rho}_n^{UR}) \right\| . \quad (9.2.3)$$
