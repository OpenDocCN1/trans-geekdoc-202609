# ## 3.6 Saleh-type R-estimators

In this section, we sequence through a series of R-estimators that eventually lead to the positive-rule Saleh-type R-estimator which is a LASSO-type of estimator. This is used in conjunction with the ridge-type R-estimator to produce a naive elastic net (or nENet-type) R-estimator. The theorems will be presented without proof to improve readability but they are extensions of the proofs given in earlier sections.

# ### 3.6.1 Hard-Threshold R-estimator of \(\theta\)

Inspired by the PTR estimator, we can effect a simplification by introducing a tuning parameter, \(\lambda^2\), to play the role of \(\chi_1^2(\alpha)\). Then we adjust \(\lambda\) until we achieve the minimum \(\text{ADL}_2\)-risk. For this purpose, we now define hard threshold R-estimator of \(\theta\) when one suspects \(\theta\) may be 0, as

\(\hat{\theta}_n^{\text{HTR}}(\lambda) = \hat{\theta}_n^{\text{UR}} I(\mathcal{L}_n > \lambda^2) \quad (3.6.1)\)

where \(I(\cdot)\) is an indicator function taking on two possible values, 0 or 1, depending on the threshold value, \(\mathcal{L}_n\), as defined earlier in Eq. (3.5.7). This HTR estimator should be considered as identical in form to PTR.
