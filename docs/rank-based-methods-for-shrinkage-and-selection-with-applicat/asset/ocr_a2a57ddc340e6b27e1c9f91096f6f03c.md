= -L_{n_j}(\theta^*_j, \beta^*_j). \quad (5.2.5)

We use the following quantities for our expressions of ADB and ADL$_2$-risk of the R-estimators,

\(\begin{aligned} A_\phi^2 &= \int_0^1 \phi^2(u)du, \quad & A_\psi^2 = \int_0^1 \psi^2(u)du, \\ \psi(u) &= -\frac{f'_0(F_0^{-1}(u))}{f_0(F^{-1}(u))}, \end{aligned}\)

and

\(\gamma(\phi, \psi) = \int_0^1 \psi(u)\phi(u)du.\)

Also, let

\(\begin{aligned} A_{n_j} &= \frac{1}{n_j-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n_j} (a_{n_j}^+(i) - \bar{a}_j^+)^2, \\ \bar{a}_j^+ &= \frac{1}{n_j} \sum_{i=1}^{n_j} a^+(i), \\ A_n^2 &= \sum_{j=1}^p (n_j-1) A_{n_j}^2. \end{aligned}\)

Note that for a given $x_j$,

\(L_{n_j}(\theta^*_j, \beta^*_j) \searrow \beta^*_j \text{ for } \beta^*_j \in \mathbb{R},\)

and

\(T_{n_j}(\theta^*_j, \beta^*_j) \searrow \theta^*_j \text{ for a fixed } \beta^*_j.\)

Under the model of Eq. (5.1.2) with $\theta^* = \beta^* = 0$, both $T_{n_j}(0, 0)$ and $L_{n_j}(0, 0)$ have symmetric distributions about $(0, 0)$. Then, as \(n \to \infty\), we have

\(\begin{pmatrix} T_{n_j}(0, 0) \\ L_{n_j}(0, 0) \end{pmatrix} \xrightarrow{\mathcal{D}} \mathcal{N}_2 \left( \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}, A_\phi^2 \chi_j^{-1} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix} \right). \quad (5.2.6)\)

Now, from Section 3.2.1, the R-estimation of \((\theta^*_j, \beta^*_j)^\top\) is given by

\(\hat{\beta}_j^{*\text{UR}} = \frac{1}{2}(\hat{\beta}_j^{*(\text{U})} + \hat{\beta}_j^{*(\text{L})}) \quad (5.2.7)\)
