= \left( \frac{\eta}{\sqrt{\kappa_j Q_j}} (\text{sgn}(Z_j)(|Z_j| - \lambda)) \bigg| j=1, \dots, p \right)^\top \quad (5.4.1)

where

Z_j = \sqrt{n} x_j Q_j \hat{\beta}_j^{\text{UR}} / \eta.

Using Eq. (5.4.1), we have the following theorem.

**Theorem 5.7** Under the local alternatives, \(K_{(n)}\), the asymptotic distributional bias, and the lower bound of ADL$_2$-risk of the Saleh-type R-estimator, \(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{SR}}(\lambda)\), are respectively given by

\text{ADB}(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{SR}}(\lambda)) = \left( -\frac{\eta}{\sqrt{\kappa_j Q_j}} |2\Phi(\Delta_j) - 1| \bigg| j=1, \dots, p \right)^\top \quad (5.4.2)

and

\text{ADL}_2\text{-risk}(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{SR}}(\lambda)) = \eta^2 \sum_{j=1}^p \frac{1}{\kappa_j Q_j} \left[ 1 + \lambda^2 - 2\lambda \sqrt{\frac{2}{n}} \exp \left( - \frac{\Delta_j^2}{2} \right) \right].

For \lambda_{\text{opt}} = \sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}, where \Delta_j^2 = 0, we have

\text{ADL}_2\text{-risk}(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{SR}}(\lambda_{\text{opt}})) = \eta^2 \sum_{j=1}^p \frac{1}{\kappa_j Q_j} \left[ 1 - \frac{2}{\pi} \left( 2 \exp \left\{ - \frac{\Delta_j^2}{2} \right\} - 1 \right) \right].

Hence,

\text{ADRE}(\hat{\beta}_n^{\text{SR}}(\lambda_{\text{opt}}) : \hat{\beta}_n^{\text{UR}}) = \frac{\sum_{j=1}^p \frac{1}{\kappa_j Q_j}}{\sum_{j=1}^p \frac{1}{\kappa_j Q_j} \left[ 1 - \frac{2}{\pi} \left\{ 2 \exp \left\{ - \frac{\Delta_\alpha^2}{2} \right\} - 1 \right\} \right]}.

If \Delta_j = 0, \forall j, then it is equal to (\pi - 2)/\pi and as \Delta_\alpha \to \infty, \forall \alpha, then it equals (\pi + 2)/\pi, crossing the surface at (2 \ln 2, \dots, 2 \ln 2)^\top.
