where \(\lambda_2\) is the ridge tuning parameter, and in expanded form, we have

```
\(D^{R\text{-}ridge}(\beta) = \sum_{i=1}^n (y_i - x_i^\top \beta) a_n(R_{n_i}(\beta)) + \lambda_2 \beta^\top \beta.\quad (2.4.13)\)
```

The penalized rank estimates, \(\hat{\beta}_n^{R\text{-}ridge}\), are obtained by minimizing Eq. (2.4.13). All properties of LS-ridge regression described above hold equally for R-ridge regression.
