What if PC1 sent a packet to PC3? PC1 would address the packet with the IPv4 address of PC3 but would forward the packet to its default gateway, which is the G0/0/0 interface of R1. The router accepts the packet and accesses its routing table to determine that G0/0/1 is the appropriate exit interface based on the destination address. R1 then forwards the packet out the appropriate interface to reach PC3, as shown in Figure 10-3.
