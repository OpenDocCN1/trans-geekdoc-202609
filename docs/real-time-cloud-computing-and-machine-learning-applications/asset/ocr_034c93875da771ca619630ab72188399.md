+   (ii) **Private cloud:** Businesses normally own and operate a private cloud for internal usage. When the need for compliance, security, and mitigation of other risk factors are a priority, a private cloud model is the most appropriate choice. The main benefit of a private cloud is that it provides the business with full control of the types of services it wants to offer to its users. All the costing models, policies, access control, and definition of the collection of services as well as VMs to be used in the private cloud, can be defined by the business without any third party involvement. Figure 2.5 (a) and (b) show two private cloud models. The first one is an on-premise private cloud in which the private organization owns a private cloud located on its premises and also manages the private cloud. In the second case the private organization owns the private cloud outsources its management to a third party and is not necessarily located on the company's premises.
