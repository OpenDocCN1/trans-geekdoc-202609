+   (iv) Cybersecurity – The internet connected world of today is witnessing unprecedented developments in cybersecurity protection. A very large volume of data is being produced and stored due to the widespread deployment of thriving technologies as cloud computing and IoT. Although by the use of big data analytics, this huge amount of data could be used in several ways to enhance the productivity of businesses, they also open the door to several cybersecurity and privacy issues. One of the main challenges is real-time identification of malicious patterns in big data. Consequently, several AI powered techniques such as machine learning, deep learning, neural networks, inferential statistics and big data analytics have emerged as potential solutions to the new cybersecurity threats. State of the art firewall systems are now using AI-based cybersecurity analytics to perform real-time intrusion detection, classification of encrypted traffic, detection of malicious software, etc. The optimization of algorithm design and minimization of cryptanalysis time in differential search, AI can provide to researchers in the field of cryptography. An interesting scheme in designing an automatic intelligent security solution was recently developed using the concept of generative adversary network. However, AI can also be wrongly utilized by hackers to create AI powered security attacks that could jeopardize the security of internet users and even trigger an uncontrollable spread of intelligent computer viruses. This situation will give rise to an AI based cybersecurity warfare with hackers using AI to launch attacks and cybersecurity professionals using AI to counteract these attacks [32].
