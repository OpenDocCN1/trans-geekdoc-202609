# 6.1. OVERVIEW OF MACHINE LEARNING ALGORITHMS FOR WEATHER FORECASTING

# 6.1.1. Introduction

Weather forecasting, which refers to the prediction of the state of the atmosphere at a future time and a specific location, has always profoundly affected many aspects of human livelihood [1, 2]. The most popular method currently being used for weather forecasting is the use of physical models to simulate and predict meteorological dynamics known as Numerical Weather Prediction (NWP). In NWP, the atmosphere is modelled as a fluid and numerical solution of atmospheric hydro thermodynamic equations are solved to provide the future state of the atmosphere. The accuracy of the predictions depends on the initial conditions used. However, NWP may not be reliable due to the instability of these differential equations under perturbations and uncertainties in the initial measurements of the atmospheric conditions [3].
