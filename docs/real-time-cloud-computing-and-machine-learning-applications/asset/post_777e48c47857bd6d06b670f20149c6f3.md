Physical layer signal processing – The signal processing blocks of a mobile communication system includes several components such as modulation, channel coding, channel estimation, equalization, Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiplexing (OFDM) and Multiple Input Output (MIMO) based antenna diversity schemes. ML algorithms as Deep Neural Networks (DNN), Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN) can be effectively employed to enhance the encoding and decoding of codes such as Polar codes and Low Density Parity Check codes in 5G systems. Additionally, supervised learning schemes can be used to enhance MIMO detectors, Neural Networks can effectively be employed in channel equalization and reinforcement learning can be used in adaptive modulation and coding [27].

- b) Coverage and capacity enhancement – To enhance the coverage and data rates, the application of machine learning in beam-forming, has become a crucial technique adopted by next generation communication systems. Multiple beams are generated simultaneously by a base station with multiple antennas [28, 29]. Since the quality of a beam serving a user can degrade, another beam from the same BS or a neighboring BS that can also serve the user, has to be chosen. The selection of the best beam to serve the user can be made using machine learning. In order to minimize the energy consumption and interference levels, machine learning algorithms can also adaptively enable or disable the beams based on traffic profiles and subscriber profiles. Machine learning coupled with data analytics and holographic beam forming [30] can extend the coverage as per requirements to enable the use of different usage patterns by performing adaptive traffic redirection and allowing dynamic access to adjacent cells.

Massive MIMO is an important technique in 5G communications. For massive MIMO channels, the beam-forming mechanism must satisfy two conditions. Firstly it should adapt to the cell user distribution and secondly maintain the interference from broadcast channels of adjacent cells, to a minimum. When these two conditions are met, the communication system is able to obtain maximum benefits from massive MIMO sites, satisfy the coverage requirements and optimize the user experience.

A crucial factor that controls the beam-forming effect for a massive MIMO site is the weight setting. The weight setting of the massive MIMO site has a significant impact on enhancing coverage quality and efficiency in different coverage areas and scenarios. The weight setting and adjustment can be made based on current requirement or past knowledge but this will require several adjustments before obtaining one is close to the optimum. In this context, An AI system can be used to automatically obtain the optimal weight settings by learning from past data to identify the relationship between radio quality and optimal weights. In addition to intra-cell optimization, AI can also help in optimizing the coverage by considering interference from multiple massive MIMO sites. This will lead to a global optimization problem that will require advanced learning algorithms [28, 29, 30].

- c) **Network management and optimization** – The current methodology to manage and optimize cellular networks may use of “models.” For the design of the physical-layer to the deployment of network infrastructure, the same methodology based on “models” is employed. However, the model-centric approach is inadequate for designing next generation networks which will consist of several and different radio access techniques, to be deployed in ultra-dense configurations, and have to service a wide spectrum of heterogeneous applications and requirements. Going beyond a model-centric approach and leveraging on the use of big data in network design has become important to deal with the wide spectrum of requirements that they have to meet. In more specific terms, the new challenges faced by networks such as 5G, it has become mandatory to use AI and ML techniques so to fully exploit the benefits of big data analytics in the design and optimization of 5G networks. AI can create a complete functional layout of the devices inside the network by analysing the sets of data generated from various sources as wireless channel measurements, sensor values, and captured images. It can be exploited to optimize various functions, such as fault monitoring and user tracking. Such functions such as fault detection and monitoring can be optimized throughout the network by the use of AI. Moreover, by monitoring the conditions of the wireless channels and user traffic over the network in real-time, it is possible to perform AI based resource management online and autonomously. These AI powered optimization schemes are capable to perform more effectively and provide enhanced network decision making [31]. AI and machine learning algorithms, can perform satisfactory predictions on the utilization of network resources, traffic characteristics and economic growth of the organization in a specific region. Such predictions are vital for network planning and implantation. In order to make such predictions, AI algorithms to be fed with multidimensional data such as geographical information, historical performance indicators as well as cross-domain data. Challenges such as blind spot identification and removal, coordinated network planning and capacity expansion in 4G/5G networks, can be addressed more effectively by feeding appropriate measurements on multi-mode coverage and interference to AI algorithms. As a result, regional network capacity planning can be made to approach the optimal theoretical bound, while at the same time minimizing network planning and deployment costs for the operators [31].

- (iv) Cybersecurity – The internet connected world of today is witnessing unprecedented developments in cybersecurity protection. A very large volume of data is being produced and stored due to the widespread deployment of thriving technologies as cloud computing and IoT. Although by the use of big data analytics, this huge amount of data could be used in several ways to enhance the productivity of businesses, they also open the door to several cybersecurity and privacy issues. One of the main challenges is real-time identification of malicious patterns in big data. Consequently, several AI powered techniques such as machine learning, deep learning, neural networks, inferential statistics and big data analytics have emerged as potential solutions to the new cybersecurity threats. State of the art firewall systems are now using AI-based cybersecurity analytics to perform real-time intrusion detection, classification of encrypted traffic, detection of malicious software, etc. The optimization of algorithm design and minimization of cryptanalysis time in differential search, AI can provide to researchers in the field of cryptography. An interesting scheme in designing an automatic intelligent security solution was recently developed using the concept of generative adversary network. However, AI can also be wrongly utilized by hackers to create AI powered security attacks that could jeopardize the security of internet users and even trigger an uncontrollable spread of intelligent computer viruses. This situation will give rise to an AI based cybersecurity warfare with hackers using AI to launch attacks and cybersecurity professionals using AI to counteract these attacks [32].
