+   Generative design- AI can assist manufacturers in the product design phase. An AI algorithm, commonly referred to as generative design software, can be used to analyze all feasible schemas of a solution, from a precise design brief which is fed to the algorithm as input. The brief can define limitations and specificities of the material types, manufacturing techniques, timeliness factors and financial constraints. Machine learning can be used to test the solution set generated by the algorithm. More information on the designs which worked best and those which failed can be obtained during the testing phase, to subsequently come up with an optimal solution [15].

+   Market adaptation/supply chain – The application of AI to Industry 4.0 is not restricted to only the manufacturing processes. Deep AI can be employed across the full spectrum of the Industry 4.0 ecosystem. One example of this is the use of AI in optimizing the supply chain of manufacturing operations. In this way, AI can allow manufacturers to provide a better response to market fluctuations. Several factors such as demand trends sorted according date, location, socio-economic characteristics, macroeconomic performance, political stability, weather forecasts etc., can be used by an algorithm to generate predictions of the market demand. This information can be used in a groundbreaking way by manufacturers to make improved financial decisions with regards to the organisation's goals as well as optimization of inventory control, staff deployment, energy efficiency and processing of raw materials [26].

+   Mobile communications – The diversity of service requirements, applications, devices, and networks have led to the evolution of Next-generation wireless networks such as 5G and beyond into very complex systems. Available resources such as power, spectrum, and infrastructures have to be used in an optimum way by Mobile Network Operators (MNOs). Major challenges have been created in wireless communications by the rising demands for capacity, coverage, latency, efficiency flexibility, compatibility, quality of experience and silence convergence. One of the most effective ways of dealing with these challenges is to make use of AI and deep neural algorithms. Deep ML algorithms can be applied to several components of a mobile communication system such as optimization of system architecture, physical and cross-layer signal processing, radio air interface and protocol design, beam-forming and antenna setup, network coding and slicing, cell acquisition and handover, scheduling and rate adaptation, access control, smart proactive caching, modulation and coding as well as adaptive resource allocations.
