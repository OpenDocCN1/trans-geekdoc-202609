+   c) **Network management and optimization** – The current methodology to manage and optimize cellular networks may use of “models.” For the design of the physical-layer to the deployment of network infrastructure, the same methodology based on “models” is employed. However, the model-centric approach is inadequate for designing next generation networks which will consist of several and different radio access techniques, to be deployed in ultra-dense configurations, and have to service a wide spectrum of heterogeneous applications and requirements. Going beyond a model-centric approach and leveraging on the use of big data in network design has become important to deal with the wide spectrum of requirements that they have to meet. In more specific terms, the new challenges faced by networks such as 5G, it has become mandatory to use AI and ML techniques so to fully exploit the benefits of big data analytics in the design and optimization of 5G networks. AI can create a complete functional layout of the devices inside the network by analysing the sets of data generated from various sources as wireless channel measurements, sensor values, and captured images. It can be exploited to optimize various functions, such as fault monitoring and user tracking. Such functions such as fault detection and monitoring can be optimized throughout the network by the use of AI. Moreover, by monitoring the conditions of the wireless channels and user traffic over the network in real-time, it is possible to perform AI based resource management online and autonomously. These AI powered optimization schemes are capable to perform more effectively and provide enhanced network decision making [31]. AI and machine learning algorithms, can perform satisfactory predictions on the utilization of network resources, traffic characteristics and economic growth of the organization in a specific region. Such predictions are vital for network planning and implantation. In order to make such predictions, AI algorithms to be fed with multidimensional data such as geographical information, historical performance indicators as well as cross-domain data. Challenges such as blind spot identification and removal, coordinated network planning and capacity expansion in 4G/5G networks, can be addressed more effectively by feeding appropriate measurements on multi-mode coverage and interference to AI algorithms.
