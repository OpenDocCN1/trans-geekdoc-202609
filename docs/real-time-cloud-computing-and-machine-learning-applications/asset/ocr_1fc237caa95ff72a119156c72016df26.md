A Hadoop-based ARIMA algorithm for mining weather data to provide effective weather forecasts [10]. The algorithm provided an average of 10% error for relative humidity and 20% error for vapour pressure predictions. For multistep predictions, as the number of steps increased, the overall error also increased indicating bugs in the multistep prediction algorithm and further improvement needed.

ARIMA has also been been used in [11] to develop models describing weather parameters such as relative humidity, precipitation, and temperature using a dataset consisting of 20 years of statistics. In [12], the authors used data collected from the meteorological station of Hejiang airport to fit an ARIMA model for temperature, rainfall, and wind speed. A variance of 1.87 was obtained meaning the forecasts can be considered reliable. However, fitting the model for rainfall gave a variance of 2230 which is close to the actual values, the precision of the forecasts for the next 7 months seemed questionable due to its large variance. The model was unable to fit data for wind speed since the data proved to be white noise. The ARIMA model was compared to Neural Networks in [13] predicting the daily rainfall for Mauritius. The authors found that the ANN performed better with an average MAPE of 1.46% compared to the SARIMA model with a MAPE of 3.02.

In [14], the non-parametric KNN algorithm with re-sampling technique was used for simultaneous prediction of daily radiation, maximum and minimum temperature, and precipitation for multiple locations in Georgia. The authors found that the KNN implementation was also able to reproduce the targeted pattern for the next year based on the observed historical weather data. The authors in [15] used KNN to predict the output of photovoltaic generation systems to determine their reliability. The algorithm used numerical weather and solar irradiance prediction data to implement a weather classification process and a physical model of PV units. In [16], the authors devised a KNN approach to classify historical data into specific periods and k different spans are then used to predict up to 17 weather parameters. A comparative study of Moving Average for pre-processing Rainfall Time series was developed. The data was then passed through an Evolving Neural Network Classifier to obtain a 1-month forecast for the Bandung area, Indonesia. The lowest MAPE observed was 15.66% from centered MA with an accuracy of 84.34% for a 1-month forecast. Weather forecasting using Artificial Neural Networks was applied in [17–19]. In [20], the authors evaluated the use of Recurrent Neural Network to predict the temperature for the Netherlands. They also investigated the effects that different hyperparameters have on the network's output. Furthermore, in [21] an ANN trained by Fireworks Algorithm was used to predict the daily mean temperature based on several measured weather parameters obtained from the Meteorological Station, located in Bangkok.
