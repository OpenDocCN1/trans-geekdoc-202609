# 1.1.2.2. Cloud as an Enabler for Industry 4.0

Industry 4.0 is a digitalization process that has changed the core nature of the organisations associated mainly with the manufacturing sector, by totally distorting the value and production chain. The massive adoption of digital technologies and an autonomous information management system are the base of companies that integrate the Internet of Things (IoT) in their processes to conduct their business operations. Industry 4.0 is therefore a novel high-tech intelligent framework which is powered by the cloud and exhibits the characteristics of ubiquity and pervasiveness. It is not a purely machine and equipment-centered paradigm. Industry reports around the world mention that Cloud technology is a critical enabler of the Industrial Revolution 4.0. Research commissioned Oracle found that 60% of businesses see an integrated enterprise Cloud Platform as the route to unlocking the potential of disruptive technologies such as robotics or artificial intelligence at the heart of Industry 4.0. This is due to the scale and the connection backbone of Industry 4.0. It is the main enabler for the development of a pioneering, effective and efficient production strategy that combines sensors, AI and robotics. The downtime of production equipment is reduced to a minimum via the use of machine learning techniques, which also enhance the flow of materials in the manufacturing process. Enterprises adopting Industry 4.0 are also able to bring about the digital transformation process faster than the cloud [7, 8].

# 1.1.2.3. Cloud Radio Access Networks (C-RAN)

In access networks that are based on emerging standards such as 5G, C-RAN is a central cloud computing architectural framework. Real-time virtualization functionalities, collaborative radio system support and massive deployment are some of the functions which are enabled by C-RAN. It becomes possible to manage complex features such resource slicing, statistical multiplexing, energy efficiency, and high capacity, thanks to the advanced mobile network architecture provided by C-RAN. The main state-of-the-art technologies that enable the realization of C-RAN are Software-Defined Networking (SDN) and the Network Function Virtualization (NFV). By using the same infrastructure with different access technologies, operators can achieve a more rapid deployment of mobile networks in C-RAN. To this end, the deployment cost is reduced, the network resources are effectively utilized, and the maintenance cost is low.
