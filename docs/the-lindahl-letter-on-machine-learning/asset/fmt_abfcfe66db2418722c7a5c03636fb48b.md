
Based on time, we can only spend a few moments on the compendium of KPIs and how important that is to the overall execution of your machine learning strategy. You must be able to present the results of machine learning within the organization in terms of ROI and other key drivers that show criticality to the business. Honestly, you must be able to think about your machine learning dashboard as something that is so simple to consume that in 30 seconds the overall status is consumable and intuitive to understand. That is good reporting. It takes time and a lot of hard work to pull off, but it is impactful when you do get to this point in the execution. I personally begin with the end in mind as part of my strategy, and while people are executing, I work to figure out how to compile the measures and how to tell the story. Sometimes the teams directly involved in the work do not have the time to dig in and dashboard as they are building and turning models. Getting the data sorted and ready to use alone is a major milestone in your machine learning journey. Pairing that with a dashboard at the same time can feel overwhelming to a team.

Everything about figuring out how to use machine learning in your organization must come down to a strategic plan. You need to figure out how to visualize that strategic plan in action, which probably will involve having a compendium of KPIs that show the value within delivering your machine learning solution. Beginning with that outcome in mind is important. It helps to frame the entire solution being operationalized. At a strategic level, the things being operationalized by teams must contribute to specific budget-based outcomes. That inherently connects expenditure to benefit throughout the organization in a measurable way.

From ideation to being operationalized, machine learning benefits must be mapped to a KPI. All those things wrapped up together in a compendium help present a picture of what strategic planning is contributing to specific action. All that gets defined in the form of clear and understandable results. Bringing those examples together within a compendium is the right way to ensure the organization has a clear view of what is going on. Understanding how the elements of the compendium of KPIs work together is what helps people visualize the strategic plan the organization is executing. It paints a very clear picture of the strategy and whether that strategy is being operationalized correctly.
