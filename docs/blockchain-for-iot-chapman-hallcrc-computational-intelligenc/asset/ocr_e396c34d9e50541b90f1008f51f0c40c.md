# 10.2.1 Blockchain-Inspired RFID-Based Information Architecture for Food Supply Chain

The authors in [20] proposed a secured and transparent food supply chain using IoT and Blockchain. This method uses radio-frequency identification (RFID)-based sensor and Blockchain at the physical and cyber layers, respectively. Here a management hub is used to cater to the need of data storage and computation tasks, and through this the IoT devices can interact with the Blockchain. With RFID and blockchain technology used in the supply chain, the food product is visible to different entities in the chain.
