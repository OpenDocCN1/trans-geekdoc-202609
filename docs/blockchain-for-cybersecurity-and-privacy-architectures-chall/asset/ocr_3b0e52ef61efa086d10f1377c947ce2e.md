# 312 Blockchain for Cybersecurity and Privacy

In RETPAY, the contract conditions related to the retention payment for partial completion are coded as a smart contract. The retention payments for each work are also embedded in the smart contract. The smart contract is deployed on the Ethereum blockchain, which is the most widely used blockchain supporting smart contracts (Li et al., 2017). Once completion of a work is confirmed by the contractor and client, RETPAY transfers the amount of the works completed from client's account (wallet address) and stores the partial payment on the contractor's account (wallet address) on the blockchain.

RETPAY consists of two modules. The first module is ad-software that was developed to capture data from Microsoft Project 2019, using the C# language and Visual Studio 2019 platform. Microsoft Project software is commonly used for management in numerous domains, including the construction sector. The purpose of the first module is to enable the contractors to use their existing project data and software for preparing the list of works completed, to facilitate the data exchange among the proposed system. In the add-on, the contractor selects the first dates, and then presses on the ‘Prepare List of Works Completed’, as shown works’ unique IDs and their completion dates to a TXT file, as shown in Figure 13.3. Once the button is pressed, the ad-on exports the list of works completed and their completion dates.

The second module of RETPAY is a Dapp consisting of two parts: the web part and the smart contract part. The web part is developed using the PHP Laravel 5 Framework in HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript in the frontend. The smart contract is developed in Remix IDE, using the Solidity 0.5.2 language.
