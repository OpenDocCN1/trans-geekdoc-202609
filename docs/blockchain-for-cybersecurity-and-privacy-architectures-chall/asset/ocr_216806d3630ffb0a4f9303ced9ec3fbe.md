Enigma [5,17] is the nearest to our methodology in that it utilizes blockchain for access control and employs the sharing of off-chain stored data. However, the information stored by Enigma reaches the logs within the block, addressing the notable scalability issues. In addition, their framework is not suited for Internet of Things (IoT) stream information.

Christian Esposito et al. [18] proposed a blockchain-based Electronic Medical Records (EMR), Electronic Health Records (EHR), and Personal Healthcare Records (PHR) ecosystem that allows us to acquire a worldwide view of the patient's medical history in an effective, objective, and lasting.

For maintaining the integrity of datasets, they proposed a reference integrity metric (RIM) that is maintained by blockchain. In this methodology, there is a central hub that just keeps references of member repositories, where the datasets are really away and disseminated. There is another chain of block that maintains member information (such as address and policy) and RIM of datasets, that the blockchain guarantees data integrity [19].
