As shown in Figure 3.6, when administrators log into the site, they will be able to view a report page containing the various courses and each individual student's status. In the example of the Introduction to Blockchain, there are several tabs under the Introduction to Blockchain: assigned learners, completed learners, learners in progress, instructors, and training time. On the top right are tabs to add ‘USERS & PROGRESS’ allows the administrators to view the various users and their progress. The ‘FILES’ tab allows the administrators to add and remove file for students to view. Last is the ‘RULES & PATH’ tab for courses.

At the bottom of the Administrator reporting page is progress overview of the course, broken down by day, month, and year to allow administrators to view completion of the course.

Our objective is to increase the future IT professionals' awareness and prepare them for the emergent of the blockchain revolution and Web 3.0. Thus, creating a comprehensive hands-on lab will bridge the gap between rapid advancement of technology and the classroom.
