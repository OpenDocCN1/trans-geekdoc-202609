# 13.5 DISCUSSION OF THE DESIGN DECISIONS
AND SECURITY EVALUATION

The architectures of smart contract applications have been manifested in various
ways in time. Initially, it was not in a smart contract, but simply, there were
assets to be used for digital transfer. Later, digital assets were taken more seriously
and were commonly used for the payment of digital goods such as the payment of
content stream. The basic architecture of smart contract appeared later, with only
one party such as landlord remote control of locks passed upon. Next, smart
multiparty contracts arose. The proposed system is an example of a multiparty smart
contract architecture. It runs on a distributed environment, namely blockchain. In its
current, it fulfills a single requirement/use case, the retention payment as a part
of project management. As it is already demonstrated in the case study,
retention payment is commonly used in the construction domain, but it is neces-
sarily specifically for this domain. It is seen in all domains where commonly project-
based tasks are end with possible partial payments.

Relying on the blockchain network, it is a distributed application and has the inher-
advantages and disadvantages. The most well-known risk to blockchain applica-
s are 51% vulnerability. It occurs when one malicious person/group fights the 51%
of the blockchain community (Li, Jiang, Chen, Luo, & Wen, 2017). In general, this
risk is mainly towards the integrity of blockchain data. Being able to change integrity\may result in secondary blockchain risks, such as double spending and criminal
activities. The authors believe that the nature of the retention payment would result
in the interest of a closed group of related users rather than a vast amount of blockchain users. Thus, having the possibility of 51% risk for this specific application is
very low. Another risk for blockchain applications is the risk of security of private
keys for blockchain. This may end confidentiality problems for both users
data. This, storage of private keys, is depicted more during the description
of use of wallets for this application.

The current design relies on the use of web wallets. Other types of wallets are
desktop wallets, phone wallets, brain wallets, and hardware wallets. Although the
web-based wallets are not secure as other types of wallets, the ease of use and
free (for majority of the web-based wallets), compared with paid hardware wallets,
make them convenient to be used in such a prototype system.

MetaMask is in RETPAY prototype design and implementation. The use
of MetaMask to store the wallet has some advantages. MetaMask is known to be a
secure web wallet. It is open-source; thus, its code is to be evaluated by a large
community of users, currently reaching 1 million active user population. It also allows
connection of hardware wallets such as Ledger (Gentil, Martins, & Sousa, 2017)
