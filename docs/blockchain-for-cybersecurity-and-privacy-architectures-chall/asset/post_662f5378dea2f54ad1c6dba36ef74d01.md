## Blockchain for Cybersecurity and Privacy

### 10. Introduction
The inception of blockchain technology has created new opportunities for businesses to provide high-quality products and services. Healthcare is one of those sectors that can bring potential benefits while integrating blockchain technology to provide. Larson et al.[1] report that blockchain offers numerous short- and long-term benefits for businesses.

According to Merritt [2], the principles of blockchain technology, such as high transparency, instant access to information, decentralized services, and convenience, are the major benefits that blockchain technology can bring to healthcare. Initially, blockchain technology was limited to financial institutions; however, such have been realized by other sectors. Various researchers have attempted to understand it, and they have theoretically conceptualized and proposed frameworks pertaining to the management of operations for non-financial sectors and academic institutions [3]. Furthermore, governments have been planning and developing the use of blockchain in the public sector as well [4].

### 10.2 Background and Motivation
Electronic health records existed before the emergence and diffusion of blockchain technology in various sectors. However, they have admitted security and accessibility issues due to vulnerability of digital systems [6,7]. It is believed that the blockchain technology, by its inherent characteristics, has the potentials to overcome healthcare compliance issues in security and accessibility. It can be used to share information with large network of healthcare institutions in an efficient and safer manner (Minn and Koo [8]). Nevertheless, all aspects of blockchain can only be achieved through the development of a blockchain-based data sharing framework that can be implemented within the healthcare sector.

### 10.3 Current State-of-the-Art
The current state of blockchain-based frameworks in healthcare focuses on:
- Smart contracts
- Data interoperability
- Security-based access control

### 10.4 Advantages and Disadvantages
The analysis of transaction throughput in a blockchain network reveals:

Advantages:
- Attribute-based access control
- Role-based access control
- Context-aware access control

Disadvantages:
- Loopholes in blockchain technology
- Data ownership concerns

### 10.5 Open Issues
The open issues regarding blockchain-based frameworks include:
- Data access through blockchain
- Data ownership
- Data breaches in healthcare
