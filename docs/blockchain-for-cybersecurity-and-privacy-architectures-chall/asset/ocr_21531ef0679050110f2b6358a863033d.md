The processes for RESCHAIN are as follows:

+   * Step 1: Stakeholder requests for initiating a block of the query (property request) with his/her wallet id.
* Step 2: The stakeholder's wallet is verified.
* Step 3: Stakeholders participate in consensus for property validation.
* Step 4: Once the decision is to reach, the status of the property is added in RESCHAIN. We have also considered that each property should have a unique ID for distinguishing among various properties.
