# 6.6 APPLICATIONS

An Interchain environment, from a distance, might seem to be offering a dynamic and scalable ecosystem, but the ecosystem needs to be built. To implement such a framework, it needs public participation to enhance real-world business, and also the project needs the support of the authorities, as well as the regulators. To make sense of the implementation of the Interchain framework, we are using an example of a supply-chain model in a car factory and the related business model:

+   - The car manufacturer has its blockchain.
- Every node of the factory is connected through a trusted and preconfigured network.
- Identities are valid and resolvable via their domain.
