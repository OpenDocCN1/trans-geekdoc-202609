# 3.2 BACKGROUND

There will be an exponential growth in the adoption of blockchain applications, with the most considerable increase in the financial services and significant growth in many other industries such as consumer products, technology, healthcare, and the public sector. Some sectors have been affected by blockchain application are finance, Internet of Things (IoT), social services, security, and privacy. This chapter will examine the works of each of these industries and the examination of how developing a new hands-on laboratory in academia will significantly enhance our students' development. The need for blockchain application is provide data security through linking transactions using cryptography, enhancing transparency and integrity. Typical operations are done using a centralized client-server infrastructure, where computers, commonly referred to as nodes, are connected to one central authority, whereas blockchain applications are a decentralized or distributed ledger with no single point of failure. There is no central authority and all nodes are connected, making it a secure form.
