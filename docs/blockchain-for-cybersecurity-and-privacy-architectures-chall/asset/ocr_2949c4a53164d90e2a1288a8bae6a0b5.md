# Foreword

Blockchain has moved beyond hype to real-world implementation in a broad range of industries (eg, finance, supply chain management, and Internet of Things) and applications (eg, cybersecurity and digital forensics), partly evidenced by its evolution over the last decade or so. At the time of writing this foreword, we are already in the fourth generation of blockchain (also known as Blockchain 4.0) and inching toward the fifth generation of blockchain.

Interest in blockchain among the academic community is also reflected by the growing number of conferences and initiatives, as well as this book titled Blockchain for Cybersecurity: Architectures, Challenges and Applications.

This book focuses on the applications of blockchain in cybersecurity, privacy, and digital forensics, for different industries sectors as Internet of Things and healthcare. Specifically, the book comprises five sections, each covering a different theme of associated research challenges. In Section I, the reader is introduced to the blockchain architectures and a number of associated challenges of how blockchain can be utilized for cybersecurity and privacy in the IoT and facilitating payment system security and privacy. The next two sections provide a more in-depth discussion of IoT and healthcare sectors. The fourth section then discusses the potential of blockchain in cybersecurity and privacy. The last section, perhaps my favorite due to my professional biases, covers the potential utility of blockchain in digital forensic applications.

In summary, this book is a timely contribution to the literature and a quality addition to the reader's bookshelf and library.

Kim-Kwang Raymond Choo
The University of Texas at San Antonio
