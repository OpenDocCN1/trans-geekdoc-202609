# Towards Secure Internet of Things

Blockchain is a new technology entirely based on decentralization principles and secure cryptographic primitives. The secure cryptographic primitives allow the users to store the data on the immutable tamper-proof ledger of the blockchain, where it is nearly impossible or very difficult to alter the data. This tamper-proof state, coupled with the decentralization control mechanism, can leverage the state of security of IoT network to make it more secure. Blockchain technology has high computational overhead, excessive bandwidth consumption, and computation delay (Deogoglu et al. 2019). As such, the integration of blockchain technology to IoT network is not an easy task, it comes with various challenges such as security, storage overhead, interoperability, scalability, data privacy, and legal issues (Reyna et al. 2018). Blockchain ensures a high level of security and robustness against privacy issues, but a suitable trade-off has to be carefully defined while integrating the technology in IoT network.
