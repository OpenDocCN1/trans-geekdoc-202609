# 16.3 Proposed Concept

RESCHAIN is a blockchain-based solution to avoid frauds in real estate environment. It maintains decentralized and transparent ledger of property information, which is accessed by different stakeholders with proper credentials. The real estate business and information system deal with various stakeholders, who have their own set of tasks and responsibilities. In the present scenario, the stakeholders of real estate are not able to monitor the development or transaction processes directly online, only manual vigilance is in. Therefore, it will be much useful when blockchain provides a mean of real estate status.
