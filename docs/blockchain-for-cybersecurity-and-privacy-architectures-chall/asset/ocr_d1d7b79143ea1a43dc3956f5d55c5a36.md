# A Comparative Study of Distributed Ledger Technologies

The blockchain involves the consumption of a lot of effort, resources, and energy. When the complex computational tasks are carried out on high-processing machines, increased electricity consumption is inevitable. The estimated data so far is 24 terawatt per year.
Hashgraph provides a fusion of speed, security, and scalability, which is significant to attain the highest level of security in the DLT. It has importance especially because of asynchronous BFT (Figures 2.4 through 2.19).
