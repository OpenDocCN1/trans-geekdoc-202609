# 5.1 INTRODUCTION

### 5.1.1 BACKGROUND 

The shift of computing paradigm closer to the end devices has enabled Internet of Things (IoT) to create a wide-open network. This open network has enabled diverse sensitive applications such as smart cities (Awad et al. 2019), transportation (Battula et al. 2019), healthcare (Yang and Xu 2018), and disaster-related services (Uijwal et al. 2019). This open network facilitates the addition of devices into the information space, where users not only consume the services but also contribute different kinds of resources. Moreover, the resources of IoT devices can currently be accessed by other devices as well network is based on centralized principles, which are prone to cyberattacks (Abonmah 2015).
