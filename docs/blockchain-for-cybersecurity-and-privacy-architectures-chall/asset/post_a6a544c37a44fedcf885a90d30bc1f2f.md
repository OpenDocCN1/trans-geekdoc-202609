# 1.1 INTRODUCTION

Blockchain technology (BT), which is the underlying technology of Bitcoin, has emerged with a number of potential applications. In less than a decade, BT has seen investment by many companies, stimulated the establishment of consortia, and raised $3.1 billion in total venture capital. The cryptocurrency market created by BT is estimated to reach a total market capitalization of $143 billion.

Given that BT is a cutting-edge technology with many promises, there are concerns about its robustness. If such authority exists in a system, tampering with blockchain or interrupting the broadcast of its contents is possible with collision between the most powerful entities. There have been many reported cyberattacks, and several cybersecurity vulnerabilities have been identified in blockchain implementations. A recent example is the Litecoin network, which shows that attackers are able to break the privacy and anonymity of Bitcoin (BTC) users by sending tiny amounts of coins to their personal wallets.
