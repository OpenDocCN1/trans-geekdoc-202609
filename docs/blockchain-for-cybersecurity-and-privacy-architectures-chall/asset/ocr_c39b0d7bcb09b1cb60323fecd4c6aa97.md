# 6.2.2 CLOUD COMPUTING

Cloud computing is a recent trend in information technology that coming from end-user devices to the World Wide Web (Ghoae-Arani, Souri, Baker, & Hussien, 2019), while the maintenance and resource management are centrally done and are no longer a user’s concern (Atori et al., 2012).

The National Institute of Standard and Technology (NIST) has reported aspects of cloud computing as a model for enabling ubiquitous and on-demand network access to a shared pool of configurable resources (Mell & Grace, 2011). The report has enumerated networks, servers, storage, application, and services as a resource. It has also highlighted agile provisioning and minimal management effort of the solution.
