# 15.5.2 BENEFITS OF FORENSIC READINESS

+   Fast Investigation: Data and transactional records stored in blockchain platform ease job of forensic examiners. Blockchain records are more trustworthy than the data and records stored in traditional databases. Significant time is saved in verifying the authenticity of the record. Overall, it also saves the cost of investigation.

+   Prevention from Insiders Attack: Organization is vulnerable from their own employees. Once employees are aware that the transaction records and activities are recorded in blockchain, they are deterred from carrying out any mischievous activities for the fear of being caught.

+   Cost Reduction for Regulatory Compliance: Most of the organizations work under some regulatory authority. Regulatory authorities always need authenticated compliance reports from the organizations. Digitizing the process of regulatory compliance on blockchain would make the process smooth and trustworthy. It will significantly save the audit time and prevent the organization to indulge in fraudulent activities.

+   Good Governance: Implementation of information system management policies such as forensics readiness garner goodwill for the organization. It increases the trust of all stakeholders and significantly helps the regulatory authority and law enforcement agencies in case of any incident.
