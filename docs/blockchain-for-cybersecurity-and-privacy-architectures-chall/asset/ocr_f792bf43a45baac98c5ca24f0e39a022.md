Public vs. Private vs. Federated Blockchain

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Security | Anonymous | Known participants | Known participants |
| | Consensus mechanism | Pre-approved | Preapproved |
| | Proof of work/proof of stake | Voting/multiparty consensus | Voting/multiparty consensus |
| Transaction speed | Slow | Medium and Fast | Medium and fast |
