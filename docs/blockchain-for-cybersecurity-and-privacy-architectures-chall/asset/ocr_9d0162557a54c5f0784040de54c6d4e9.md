Vora et al. [31] proposed another framework pertaining to the electronic data interchange systems based on blockchain technology. The main aim of this work was to strike a balance between privacy and accessibility to electronic records to improve the feasibility of blockchain-based sharing systems for the healthcare industry. The authors also provided an architecture of blockchain-based data sharing systems based on Ethereum blockchain.

Shen et [32] considered the issues pertaining to the already-established frameworks and data sharing mechanism in the healthcare industry. The efficiency issues and other security and privacy concerns were investigated, and a robust blockchain-based data sharing framework named 'Medchain' was proposed, which involves the amalgamation of blockchain, the structured P2P networking technique, and the design to overcome the efficiency issues. The framework is based on the session-based healthcare sharing mechanism that attempts to provide higher security assurance to the healthcare institutions and patients. The blockchain structure of the 'Medchain' framework is based on the digest chain system.
