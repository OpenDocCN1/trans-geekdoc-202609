# 8.1 INTRODUCTION

According to the International Organization of Motor Vehicle Manufacturers (OICA), over 70 million vehicles were produced in 2016. The number of vehicle sales grew from 50 million in 2015 to 78 million in 2018. This number is still increasing, leading to a corresponding increase in the number of vehicles on roads. Therefore, roads' safety and efficiency, as well as passengers' entertainment, are becoming one of the significant interests for researchers and developers. With the advancement in communication technologies and vehicle industry, the paradigm of the intelligent transportation system (ITS) appeared, which smart vehicles have been integrated with vehicle-to-vehicle/infrastructure-based vehicle hoc communications to exchange road information and critical events with one another.
