Currently, an emerging area where CAs have potential to help some shortages and unmet needs in the health sector, especially in healthcare services delivery because of their abilities to (i) imitate conversation with humans through natural language, (ii) be available any time, anywhere and (iii) provide an almost immediate response [26]. This field is still a nascent period of investigation, and why most studied documents have been published since 2005 and rarely evaluate efficacy or safety. Nonetheless, CAs have been used with success in industry for dissemination of news, offers and promotions, and in the commerce for customer service [31, 51].

Despite the fact that the literature on CAs for healthcare services delivery is scarce, most of the research in this area is on education and counseling, which are used to support patients and clinicians in specific processes. Moreover, some research evaluates CAs for rehabilitation, mental health, physical training and palliative care, according to the amount of information found. Overall, the published studies show that CAs can make interaction more engaging, although many all studies have measured user experience by linking the level of acceptance and comfort with the participation of CAs. In some studies, the Likert Scale was the methodology to measure people's opinions and attitudes [54]. In other cases, Acceptability E-Scale (AES) was used for assessing the level of acceptability of the CAs from patients [49]. However, regardless of the scale used, participants expressed comfort in interacting with CAs and were very receptive to them [57].

Although there is a good perception of CAs, their potential largely depends on whether these technologies are accessible to the populations that could benefit from them. Therefore, most CAs rely on types of technology including Apps delivered via mobile device [9], web [53], or computer [16, 21]. Short Message Service (SMS) [50] and telephone [77]. The second one is mobile devices which represent a great advantage because they are omnipresent and the user can be reached by the CAs at all times and in all places [10], with the limit that in many cases an Internet connection is needed.
