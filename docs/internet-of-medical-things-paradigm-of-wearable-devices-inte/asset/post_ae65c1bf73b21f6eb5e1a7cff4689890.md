Characterization must be performed for the tissue-mimicking material using different techniques. The Specific Absorption Rate (SAR) studies should be performed before proceeding with the in vitro testing of the system. Besides tissues, the radiation of the implantable antenna should pass through the skin to reach the collector outside the human body so the characterization of the material (a mix of Triton-X114, propylene butyl ether, and deionized water can be used as skin mimicking material), which possess the same properties as human skin can be performed. After materials characterization, studies in animal models like such must be performed.

Recently, various researchers have proposed different implantable antenna designs for MICS/ISM bands [25–30]. In [25], a low-profile Coplanar Waveguide (CPW)-V-shaped implantable antenna on FR-4 substrate was proposed. This antenna was reported for ISM biological applications and can be positioned on various tissues such as skin, fat and muscles. IE3D software was used to simulate the antenna design. In [26], a dual-band antenna for implantable neuro-microsystem was reported. The operating frequencies of the antenna were 433.9 and 542.4 MHz. The SAR study of the antenna was performed by the authors using the solution which matches the properties of the tissues. In [27], the authors proposed a dipole, patch and inverted-F-antennas to help paralyzed patients. The device was designed such that it can be placed in the buccal space of the mouth to issue commands using the user-centric gestures of the tongue. This implantable antenna has a compact design enclosed in an arc-shaped device, better bandwidth and gain in comparison to other implantable antennas reported. In [28], planar inverted-F and dipole antennas working at ISM band were reported. These identifies part of the device placed in the mouth of paralyzed patients, who identified the signals generated by the voluntary movement of the tongue. The various activities like the movement of the wheelchair, operation of mobile phone or computer device were carried out. In [29], a compact CPW-fed dual spiral antenna was proposed for implantable applications. The antenna operates in ISM band and was tested using human models. It possesses higher gain, low return loss with good impedance matching. In [30], the authors presented the design of an ultra-compact spiral coiled implantable antenna with wireless transfer capacity. The power was transferred from an external transmitter device and an efficiency of 47.2% at a frequency of 39.86 MHz was obtained.

### 5.4.2.1 Design Requirements of Ingestible Antennas

In order to perform the design, the following aspect must be taken into account:

- The bandwidth should be high so that high-definition images can be transmitted.
- The efficiency should be high.
- As the antenna is surrounded by human body, which may affect and change performance.

### 5.4.3 Wearable Antennas

These types of antennas are useful for people of all ages and occupations (such as firefighters, military personnel and paramedics) for monitoring purposes.
