# 5.4.1 IMPLANTABLE ANTENNAS

These antennas are implanted inside the body for constant monitoring of different body parameters such as blood glucose level, brain activity, cardiac activity, etc. [13, 14]. These antennas must be designed by using some biocompatible material or should be enclosed in some biocompatible material to avoid any adverse reaction and its rejection by the body.
