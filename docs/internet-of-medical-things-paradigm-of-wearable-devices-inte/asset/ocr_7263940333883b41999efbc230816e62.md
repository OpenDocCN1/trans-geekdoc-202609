Furthermore, given the current situation with a global pandemic, the promotion of programs that allow people to exercise at home will make it possible to combat the mental and physical consequences of the COVID-19 quarantine [28].

# 7.3.5 Palliative Care

The WHO estimates that more than 20 million people need palliative care or about one of them who have cancer, and the rest suffer from degenerative diseases or life-threatening illnesses [40]. That is, the purpose of palliative care is not only to relieve pain, but also to alleviate the physical, psychosocial and emotional suffering of patients with serious illnesses in their advanced stages and to help the families of these people care for their loved ones [42].

In view of the above, palliative care is an emerging field in which CAs could intervene to relieve the suffering and worries of those with terminal diseases who do not respond to curative treatments. The current evidence is supported by two studies, which are shown in Table 7.6.
