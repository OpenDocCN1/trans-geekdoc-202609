# 5.4.2.1 Design Requirements of Ingestible Antennas

In order to perform the design of an ingestible antenna, the following aspect must be taken into account,

+   a. The antenna bandwidth should be high so that high-definition images and data of GI tract (for example temperature, oxygen concentration, pH, etc.) can be transmitted.
b. The antenna efficiency should be high to consume less power and transmit data at higher rates.
c. As WCE is fabricated inside the human body where body fluids offer a conductive environment, which may affect and change the antenna performance. So, all these factors must be taken into consideration while proposing the antenna design.
