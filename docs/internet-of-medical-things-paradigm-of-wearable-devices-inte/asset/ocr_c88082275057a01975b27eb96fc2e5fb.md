**FIGURE 6.2** Experimental setup. Participant was instrumented on their dominant side with an IMU placed on the dorsal side of their foot. Likewise, with four more IMUs located on each thigh and shank with different electrodes located on the tibialis anterior, rectus femoris, biceps, and gastrocnemius.

The volunteer was asked to participate in a short study consisting of one session with four stages. Each stage had the same exercises: high knees, jumping jacks, squats, and short runs. The difference between the steps was the duration of each task, which was progressively increasing, i.e., the corresponding time of exercise was 30 s (first step), 45 s (second step), 60 s (third step), and 75 s (fourth step).

In order to assess the fatigue, at the end of each stage, four parameters were measured: (i) Blood lactate level, (ii) a perceptive scale, the Borg CR10, both variables gave an indication of the fatigue level, (iii) EMG signals, and (iv) spatiotemporal parameters. Regarding the measurement of blood lactate, a blood sample was taken from the participant's earlobe with a lancet. The blood was collected with a new test strip, and finally, the strip was inserted into the Lactate Pro2 (Arkray, Japan) to measure the blood lactate level. On the other hand, the Borg CR10 scale was obtained by asking the volunteer how tired he felt according to the scale (Figure 6.1). We indicated to the participant that they should only concentrate the sensation of total effort and not on the shortness of breath or muscle pain.
