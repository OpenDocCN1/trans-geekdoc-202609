# Blockchain-Based IoT for Personalized Pharmaceuticals

# 4.4.5 CRYPTOPHARMACEUTICALS

Applying information-rich models sterilizing every dosage unit, check after production, and check-out of the product while the patient is administering the prescribed dosage unit. Such models will allow scanning the product by a smart-link to the IoT and to the health system, whereas AI is adding to the management and doses adjustments [3, 34]. Such digital products are specific and customized for every patient enabling the blockchain-based health system [35] (Figure 4.1).

Cryptophaceuticals is a blockchain principle for pharmaceutical dosage units where every recent ledger includes the distinctive information for a certain product, revealed as a hash by the producer in the check-in and by the patient in the check-out; hence, every ledger includes a cryptographic hash of the preceding ledger cascading all the ledgers in a non-modifiable manner Norfeldt proposed incorporating pharmaceutical products into an IoT-based health system allowing a technological framework for merging ML-driven diagnosis with the patient’s data, involving point-of-care sensor data and health records, and eventually producing a safe chain of serialized individualized products. Programming platforms have been used to create an open-source code with the freely available Android and Apple MedBlockchain application where have been engineered so that every screen shows four distinct user views: manufacture, patient monitoring, and blockchain allowing information in every workflow level according to whether the user is a healthcare provider, patient, health system, or producer of the medicinal product. However, the latest data on the influences of data breaches suggests that cybersecurity will be among the highly significant challenges to the future health system [37].
