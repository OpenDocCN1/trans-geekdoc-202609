One of its main advantages is that it encompasses advanced digital signal processing algorithms [38] that offer high quality results in cardiology diagnosis and telediagn at low cost. It is devoted to reach patients located in rural areas of Mexico.

The medical briefcase encompasses an ECG, a Holter (ambulatory ECG), a pulse oximeter, a digital thermometer, and a sphygomanometer (blood pressure meter). Its architecture is shown in Figure 1.5.

The medical briefcase enables medical access to patients, physicians, and clinics in rural communities or remote locations. Collected data in these locations are uploaded to a cloud server-based medical portal, which is ensured by an Internet provider. Data is to be analyzed by specialists at a central clinic or hospital located in cities. The idea is to assist patients with specialized diagnosis without the barriers of distance.

A number of systems for telemonitoring of physiological signals have been developed and can be found in the literature: heart signals [39, 40], brain signals [41, 42], respiratory activity [43, 44], and blood parameters [45, 46], among others. All of them are devoted to perform a real-time follow-up of the patient health status to ensure a prompt medical response.
