## 6.5.3 ANAEROBIC STUDY

Aiming to show the implementation of IMU measurements in anaerobic exercises for estimating fatigue, this section of the chapter presents the results obtained in a study carried out with a healthy male volunteer (age: 23 years, weight: 68 Kg and height: 174 cm) who ride on a cycle ergometer during five minutes. Considering that he a healthy subject, the maximum heart rate can be obtained with the Tanaka's formula (equation 6.2, 191 BPM approximately).

### 6.5.3.1 Experimental Anaerobic Procedure

The following procedure is based on one high-intensity training implemented in cardiac rehabilitation [28]. First, the volunteer was instructed in a 5-minute warm-up, composed of stretching activities focused on the lower limbs. Afterwards,

A Zephyr HxM BT sensor was placed on the subject's chest with an elastic band, for measuring his heart rate during the whole test, by a Bluetooth communication channel with a sampling frequency of 1 Hz. Besides, a Shimmer3 IMU Unit was located on the thigh of the subject's dominant leg (the right one) with an elastic band. The three accelerometers, three gyroscopes, and the EMG module of the Shimmer were set to provide their data with a sampling rate of 512 Hz, through a Bluetooth communication channel. Finally, three external electrodes were attached to the corner, two on the biceps femoris and one to the external protuberance of the femur, as the reference of the EMG signals.
