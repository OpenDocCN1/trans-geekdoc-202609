However, despite the promising role it can play in supporting people, literature discusses how to develop and demonstrates its long-term effectiveness is scarce [30]. Although in most of the literature, users report being fully satisfied with Cas, they do not necessarily translate into improvements in clinical outcome (e.g., a significant reduction in a clinical measure of depression).

This chapter is organized into five sections, addressing a systematic review of the medical applications in which the integration of wearable devices and Cas creates a new way to healthcare delivery through interactive and personalized support that can be carried out in both hospital and home conditions. Also, it provides an overview of their efficacy and related-out issues like inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria and data extraction in early experiments or pilot studies.
