# 3.3 WEARABLE DEVICES IN THE MEDICAL FIELD

WHDs have rapidly increased in various biomedical applications through digital health such as tracking, recording and monitoring. The main aim of these devices is to improve the health of people. The wearable devices are smart watches, glasses and armbands. The first implantable heart device was created in 1960 as a implanted pacemaker for arrhythmia patients. The Implantable Cardio Defibrillators (ICDs) and implantable devices in the deep brain were created to treat millions of patients [25, 26].
