Directions to the destination are translated to actuator commands, which are transmitted via radiofrequency (RF) to an electronic module that the user carries with him/her. Hence, users perceive directions as vibrations. A comprehensive study on tactile-force perception [55] has shown that this information is easy to understand and that it actually has the potential to point directions to blind people.
