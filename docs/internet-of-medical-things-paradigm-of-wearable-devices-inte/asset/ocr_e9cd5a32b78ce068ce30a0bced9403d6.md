# Preface

In Chapter 1, “Fundamentals and Paradigms in the Internet of Things,” contributed by Claudia L. Garzón-Castro and Ramiro Velázquez, they discuss new technologies that have permeated all activities in daily life, leading to many objects being connected and communicated through a network in one way or another. This connection of multiple components and devices through the Internet is known as the Internet of Things (IoT). The IoT helps to collect data from devices in real time, devices with backend systems, ensure interoperability and operate diverse applications. Like most technological advancements, the aim of IoT is to offer the user comfort in various areas while being time and cost effective. IoT has been used in several applications including, but not limited to, remote monitoring and telemetry for predictive maintenance. This diversity of applications has allowed the use of IoT in various fields of action such as environmental and urban infrastructure control, mass production, health, transport systems, and the energy industry, among others. This chapter presents a holistic vision of the fundamentals of IoT with the aim of providing a comprehensive overview to readers interested in the topic. The chapter addresses the most relevant concepts involved in IoT such as architectures, sensors, actuators, communication protocols, and user interfaces. Finally, some IoT-based applications for healthcare were provided. In this sense, it can be concluded that IoT can be successfully used in services associated with patient monitoring, risky situations detection, and telemedicine, among other applications.

Chapter 2 is titled, “Internet of Medical Things: Current and Future Trends.” Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) establishes the necessary scenario for medical devices and applications to evolve through information technology. In this chapter, the authors, Lucia Alonso Virgen, Miguel A. Sanchez Valdes, Fernando López Hernandez, and J. Javier Granados, analyze the most relevant and future trends, referred to as IoMT. The objective of IoMT is to offer a theoretical framework that allows to initiate new lines of research focused on offering methods and/or carefully analyzing the benefits, methods of implementation and the evaluation of each one of the existing systems.
