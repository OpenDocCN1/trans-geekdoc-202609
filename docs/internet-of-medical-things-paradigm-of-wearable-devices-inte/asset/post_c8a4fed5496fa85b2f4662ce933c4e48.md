1.1 INTRODUCTION

The term Internet of Things (IoT) was used in 1999 by the British technology Kevin Ashton, who worked at Procter & Gamble (P&G). This new paradigm emerged as the solution to the lack of information P&G had in order to determine when and where its most popular products sold out in stores. The solution proposed by Ashton was to include sensors in their products so they would be able to track and count everything in order to greatly reduce waste, losses and overall costs.
