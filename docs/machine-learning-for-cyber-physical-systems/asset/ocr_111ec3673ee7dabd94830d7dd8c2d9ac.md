The accuracy of the quality prognosis is mainly driven by the accuracy of the regression model. Therefore, a sufficiently large database is necessary. In the future, we plan to increase the data volume for a higher prognosis accuracy. The simulated data should be as close as possible to reality. Typically, the parameters of the press-hardening cycles follow a normal distribution and most of these cycles produce accepted parts. However, in the AutoForm software, a mesh is placed over the boundaries of the process parameters and the parameter variations are evenly distributed over the mesh. How this affects the regression model remains to be investigated. A validation of the ML approach with FEM simulations is under way. Once the extended simulation based regression and optimization approach ways we will move on to experimental data and other more complex production lines.
