### Three steps to applying blockchain in your business

The process of applying the blockchain to your business should take three primary steps:

1.  **The blockchain suitability test**
    Identifying exactly how your business could make the blockchain technology part of its processes and systems.
2.  **The blockchain readiness test**
    Finding out what your business already has as well as what you would need to acquire to make the process easier. At this stage, in other words, you take stock of your capacity and needs.
3.  **The blockchain level or type test**
    Identifying the exact part of the blockchain technology to use as every business has a particular element that is most appropriate for it. At this stage, you want to be laser accurate on how you can acquire what you need with the least cost and effort.

### Business blockchain suitability

The first part of the workbook is designed to help you figure out whether your business is suitable to use the blockchain technology. Nearly every business on the planet can, in one way or another, find some use for the technology. However, the benefit is not the same for each, and some businesses find critical uses for the technology. In contrast, others use it for what could be described as "cosmetic functions"―the only practical application is not at the core of the business operations. For example, here is a case of applying the technology to change the core processes and systems of a business: a gold trading company creates a digital token on the blockchain to represent the actual gold held in their vaults and make it easy for it to be traded. However, a hotel chain brand choosing to host its website on top of a blockchain like Ethereum or NEO is not applying the technology at the core of its operations.

Broadly speaking, the blockchain can help your business achieve at least one of three goals: fix a problem faced by your business, improve efficiency (such as cutting costs), and support innovation (such as creating new products and experience for your customers).

#### Fixing problems

Businesses face persistent problems all the time, some of which you are forced to accommodate as part and parcel of doing business. For example, as a small business, you might find it a little costly and demanding to set up a rewards points system. You would have to worry about protecting the user data you collect for the purpose, which distracts you from your core operations. The blockchain makes it possible for you to create and issue tokens easily to your customers without needing to collect their data, and more importantly, without having to use the services of a third party.

#### Improving efficiency

The technology achieves efficiency through cutting costs, hastening processes, and improving security for data and systems. For example, if it takes several days to move value from one corner of the globe to another, the technology has proven that it can significantly reduce that time to minutes and even seconds. Besides, it cuts the costs involved, in some cases (through particular protocols), to zero.

#### Supporting innovation

In regard to innovation, the technology offers you the opportunity and capacity to create new products and experiences for your customers in several ways. For example, using smart contracts and the micropayment capacity, you could automate certain transactions, opening up opportunities that did not exist before. For example, online magazines could use the technology to allow readers to pay to read per article. This would bring in more readers who have no need or desire for a monthly subscription when all they want is to read a single article.

### Business blockchain readiness

This is the second step in the process of onboarding and making the blockchain technology part of your systems as a business or an institution. This stage analyses your business readiness or capacity to make the technology a part of your systems.

In particular, you need to determine whether you have the necessary talent and skill in your team. Also determine how much it would cost you to put in place the blockchain system, and whether you have such funds. Most importantly, justify the financial cost and whether it can be recouped.
