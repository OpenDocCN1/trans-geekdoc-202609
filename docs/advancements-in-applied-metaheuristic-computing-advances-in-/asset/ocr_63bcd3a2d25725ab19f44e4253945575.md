**Supply Chain:** A system of organizations, people, activities, information, and resources involved in moving a product or service from supplier to customer.

**Volunteered Geographic Information (VGI):** The harnessing of tools to create, assemble, and disseminate geographic data provided voluntarily by individuals (Goodchild, 2007).
