

Accuracy = \frac{tp+tn}{tp + fp + fn + tn} (5)

Precision = \frac{tp}{tp + fp} (6)

Recall = \frac{tp}{tp + fn} (7)

F-Measure = 2 * \frac{Precision * Recall}{Precision + Recall} (8)

MCC = \frac{(tp * tn) - (fp * fn)}{\sqrt{(tp + fp) * (tp + fn) * (tn + fp) * (tn + fn)}}

Matthews Correlation Coefficient (MCC) = \frac{(tp*tn) - (fp*fn)}{\sqrt{(tp + fp) * (tp + fn) * (tn + fp) * (tn + fn)}} (9)

FM-Index = \sqrt{Precision * Recall}

Fowlkes-Mallows index (FM index) = \sqrt{Precision*Recall} (10)

## CONCLUSION

Water quality is a critical vital issue that directly affects the economy and health. It is extremely significant to guarantee safe drinking water supply for decent public health. Subsequently, accurate water quality prediction becomes a challenging issue. In the present work, an effective multi-objective GA supported the NN model has been conducted for predicting the drinking water quality. Water samples from different municipal areas in both sides of the Hooghly River (West Bengal, India) have been collected. The collected samples have been used in the present work to classify them into two different classes, namely ‘Safe’ and ‘Unsafe’ in accordance with the Indian standards (IS 10500:2012).
