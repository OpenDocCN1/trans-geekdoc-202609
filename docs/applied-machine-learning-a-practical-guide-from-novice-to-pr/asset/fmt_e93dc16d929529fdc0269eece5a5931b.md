![](OEBPS/image_rsrc52C.jpg)

# Interpretation of the r2_score for Polynomial Regression Model

The R2 (pronounced "R-squared") score, also referred to as the coefficient of determination, is a statistical metric that quantifies the proportion of the variance in the dependent variable that can be explained by the independent variable(s).

An R2 score of 0.851227 indicates that approximately 85.12% of the variance in the dependent variable is predictable from the independent variable(s) in the model. In other words, the model explains about 85.12% of the variability in the data. This suggests that the model fits the data quite well, as a high R2 score indicates that the observed outcomes are well-represented by the model predictions.
