combination offers a specific approach to contribute to the CIA triad; i.e., by using federated learning, we manage to provide privacy and confidentiality to the data during training. At the same time, the blockchain offers traceability, integrity, and transparency. Those attributes also answer to the GDPR principals and CCPA regulations.
