# 14.4.8 Classification and Regression Trees (CART)

Table 14.7 shows the performance results of our CART algorithm in predicting bank failure on the training set. The algorithm’s level of accuracy on the training dataset was 82.8%. The best tune, or complexity, parameter of our optimal model was 0.068. The Kappa statistic was 75%, envisaging that our classifier was effective, as also shown with the Kappa SD of 0.07 in the classification of bank categories. On the test dataset, the algorithm achieved an accuracy level of 92.5% and a kappa of 88.72%. The algorithm only misclassified two instances as moderate and one as satisfactory.
