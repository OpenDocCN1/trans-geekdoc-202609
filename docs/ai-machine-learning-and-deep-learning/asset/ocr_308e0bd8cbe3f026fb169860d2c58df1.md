# Spatial Transformation Attack
(1.4.12)

In contrast to the boundary attack and the HopSkipJump attack that are based on bounded adversarial perturbations, the spatial transformation attack [4] is a black-box “semantic” adversarial attack that reveals a small random transformation, such as a small rotation on an image, and can easily fool a state-of-the-art image classifier. This attack really questions the robustness of these state-of-the-art image classifiers. Furthermore, the primary disadvantage of the ZOO attack is the need to probe the classifiers thousands of times before the adversarial examples are found [27]. This is not a case for spatial transformation attacks. Engstrom et al. [4] showed that the worst-of-10 ( is able to reduce the model accuracy significantly with just 10 queries. Basically, it rotates or translates a natural image slightly to cause misclassification. This attack reveals the vulnerability of the current state-of-the-art ML models.
