Pattanaik et al. continued to provide three types of attacks by adding perturbations to the states [28]. The first is adding random noise to the DRL states to mislead the victim agent to select a suboptimal action. The second attack is gradient based (GB), similar to the FGSM mentioned with regard to the policy attack. The third attack is also gradient-based but improves the performance of the FGSM attack. Instead of using a gradient-based attack, the authors use a stochastic gradient descent (SGD) attack.
