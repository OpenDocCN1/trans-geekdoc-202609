# 13.6.5 IDS for Internet of Vehicles (IoV)

To provide a safe driving security for IoV, VANET is more essential for secure vehicle-to-vehicle communication. Different types of sensors and embedded controls are available in the vehicle to gather information such as traffic conditions, parking facilities, road accidents, etc. This information is communicated among nearby vehicles in order to have a comfortable journey. These communications are sometimes compromised by attackers that affect the whole network. MLP based on neural network plays a major role in identifying such intrusions and attacks in the IoV network [35]. This security can also be achieved using the deep belief network of deep learning [36, 38, 43].
