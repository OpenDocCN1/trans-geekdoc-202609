Each hidden layer in a particular subnetwork acts as a visible layer for the next, thus improvising the training method due to its pretraining and fine-training. DBN does require labeled data, as in a deep neural network, but it works with unsupervised data, and the weights are updated and fine-tuned by back-propagation. This helps many in the industry that lack large amounts of data. Also, DBN gains good insight from the data and, when combined with CNN, produces higher accuracy. This method also performs better in malicious URLs identification [33], as well as in detection of security breaches in the IoT [34].
