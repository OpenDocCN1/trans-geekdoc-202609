therefore ensures that

$\mathcal{C}_{(\mathfrak{X},d),n} \leq 4\left(n+1\right)^{-1/N} \leq 4\left[\begin{matrix} 4R \\ r \end{matrix}\right]^N^{-1/N} = 4\left[\begin{matrix} 4R \\ r \end{matrix}\right]^{-1} = \frac{r}{R}$  (12.132)

This and Lemma 12.2.22 demonstrate that

$\mathcal{C}_{(\mathfrak{X},d),n} = R\mathcal{C}_{(\mathfrak{X},\frac{r}{R}),n} \leq R\left[\begin{matrix} r \\ R \end{matrix}\right] = r$.  (12.133)

Lemma 12.2.13 hence ensures that

$\mathcal{C}^{(\mathfrak{X},d),r} \leq n \leq \left[\begin{matrix} 4R \\ r \end{matrix}\right]^N = \left[\begin{matrix} 4R \\ r \end{matrix}\right]^{\dim(V)}$  (12.134)

(cf. Definition 4.3.2). The proof of Proposition 12.2.23 is thus complete. \quad $\square$

**Proposition 12.2.24.** *Let $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $a \in \mathbb{R}$, $b \in (a, \infty)$, $r \in (0, \infty)$ and let $\delta$: $([a,b]^d \times [a,b]^d) \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $x, y \in [a,b]^d$ that $\delta(x, y) = \|x - y\|_\infty$ (cf. Definition 3.3.4). Then*

$\mathcal{C}^{([a,b]^d,r)} \leq \left(\left[\begin{matrix} b-a \\ 2r \end{matrix}\right]\right)^d \leq \begin{cases} 1 & : r \geq \frac{(b-a)}{2} \\ \left(\begin{matrix} b-a \\ r \end{matrix}\right)^d & : r < \frac{(b-a)}{2} \end{cases}$  (12.135)

(cf. Definitions 4.2.6 and 4.3.2).

*Proof of Proposition 12.2.24.* Throughout this proof, let $\mathfrak{N} \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ satisfy

$\mathfrak{N} = \left[\begin{matrix} b-a \\ 2r \end{matrix}\right]$,  (12.136)

for every $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $i \in \{1, 2, \dots, N\}$ let $g_{N,i} \in [a,b]$ be given by

$g_{N,i} = a + \frac{(i-1/2)(b-a)}{N}$  (12.137)

and let $A \subseteq [a,b]^d$ be given by

$A = \{g_{\mathfrak{N},1}, g_{\mathfrak{N},2}, \dots, g_{\mathfrak{N},\mathfrak{N}}\}^d$  (12.138)

(cf. Definition 4.2.6). Observe that it holds for all $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $i \in \{1, 2, \dots, N\}$, $x \in [a + \frac{(i-1)(b-a)}{N}, g_{N,i}]$ that

$|x - g_{N,i}| = a + \frac{(i-1/2)(b-a)}{N} - x \leq a + \frac{(i-1/2)(b-a)}{N} - \left(a + \frac{(i-1)(b-a)}{N}\right) = \frac{b-a}{2N}$.  (12.139)

In addition, note that it holds for all $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $i \in \{1, 2, \dots, N\}$, $x \in [g_{N,i}, a + \frac{i(b-a)}{N}]$ that

$|x - g_{N,i}| = x - \left(a + \frac{(i-1/2)(b-a)}{N}\right) \leq a + \frac{i(b-a)}{N} - \left(a + \frac{(i-1/2)(b-a)}{N}\right) = \frac{b-a}{2N}$.  (12.140)
