**Proof of Lemma 7.2.11**. First, note that (7.59) and Lemma 5.6.4 prove that for all $\theta,x \in \mathbb{R}^{9}$ it holds that

The assumption that $\mathbb{E}\left[\left\|X_{1,1}\right\|_{2}\right]<\infty$ hence implies that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{9}$ it holds that

$\mathbb{E}\left[\left\|\left(\nabla_{\theta}\ell\right)\left(\theta,X_{1,1}\right)\right\|_{2}\right]=\mathbb{E}\left[\left\|\theta-X_{1,1}\right\|_{2}\right] \leq\|\theta\|_{2}+\mathbb{E}\left[\left\|X_{1,1}\right\|_{2}\right]<\infty.$
