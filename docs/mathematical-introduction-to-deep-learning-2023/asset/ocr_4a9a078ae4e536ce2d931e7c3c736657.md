and let $\pmb{\mathfrak{L}}: C^{1,2}([0,T] \times \mathbb{R}^{d}, \mathbb{R}) \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $v = (v(t,x))_{(t,x) \in [0,T] \times \mathbb{R}^{d}} \in C^{1,2}([0,T] \times \mathbb{R}^{d}, \mathbb{R})$ that

```
$$\pmb{\mathfrak{L}}(v) = \mathbb{E}[|v(0, \mathcal{X}) - g(\mathcal{X})|^{2} + |\big(\tfrac{\partial v}{\partial t}\big)(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{X}) - (\Delta_{x}v)(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{X}) - f(v(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{X}))|^{2}]. \tag{16.12}$$
```

Observe that Theorem 16.1.1 assures that the unknown function $u$ satisfies

```
$$\pmb{\mathfrak{L}}(u) = 0 \tag{16.13}$$
```

and is thus a minimizer of the optimization problem associated to (16.12). Motivated by this, we consider aim to find approximations of $u$ by computing approximate minimizers of the function $\pmb{\mathfrak{L}}: C^{1,2}([0,T] \times \mathbb{R}^{d}, \mathbb{R}) \rightarrow [0, \infty]$. Due to its infinite dimensionality this optimization problem is however not yet amenable to numerical computations.

For this reason, in the second step, we reduce this finite dimensional stochastic optimization problem to a finite dimensional stochastic optimization problem involving *ANNs*. Specifically, let $a: \mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable, let $h \in \mathbb{N}$, $l_{1}, l_{2}, \ldots, l_{h}$, $\mathfrak{d} \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfy $\mathfrak{d} = l_{1}(d+2) + [\sum_{k=2}^{h}l_{k}(l_{k-1} + 1)] + l_{h} + 1$, and let $\mathcal{L}: \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}} \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ that

```
$$\begin{align*} \mathcal{L}(\theta) &= \pmb{\mathfrak{L}}\big(\mathcal{N}^{\theta, d+1}_{\mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{1}}, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{2}}, \ldots, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{h}}, \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{R}}}\big) \\ &= \mathbb{E}\Big[\big|\mathcal{N}^{\theta, d+1}_{\mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{1}}, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{2}}, \ldots, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{h}}, \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{R}}}(0, \mathcal{X}) - g(\mathcal{X})\big|^{2} \\ &\quad+ \big(\tfrac{\partial \mathcal{N}^{\theta, d+1}_{\mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{1}}, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{2}}, \ldots, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{h}}, \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{R}}}}{\partial t}(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{X}) - (\Delta_{x}\mathcal{N}^{\theta, d+1}_{\mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{1}}, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{2}}, \ldots, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{h}}, \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{R}}})(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{X}) \ &\quad- f\big(\mathcal{N}^{\theta, d+1}_{\mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{1}}, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{2}}, \ldots, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{h}}, \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{R}}}(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{X})\big)\big)^{2}\Big] \tag{16.14} \end{align*}$$
```

(cf. Definitions 1.1.3 and 1.2.1). We can now compute an approximate minimizer of the function $\pmb{\mathfrak{L}}$ by computing an approximate minimizer $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ of the function $\mathcal{L}$ and employing the realization $\mathcal{N}^{\vartheta, d+1}_{\mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{1}}, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{2}}, \ldots, \mathfrak{A}_{a, l_{h}}, \operatorname{id}_{\mathbb{R}}}$ of the *ANN* associated to this approximate minimizer as an approximate minimizer of $\pmb{\mathfrak{L}}$.

The third and last step of this derivation is to approximately compute such an approximate minimizer of $\mathcal{L}$ by means of *SGD*-type optimization methods. We now sketch this in the case of the plain-vanilla *SGD* optimization method (cf. Definition 7.2.1). Let $\xi \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$, $J \in \mathbb{N}$, $(\gamma_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq [0, \infty)$, for every $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $j \in \{1,2,\ldots,J\}$ let $\mathfrak{T}_{n,j}: \Omega \rightarrow [0,T]$ and $\mathfrak{X}_{n,j}: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{d}$ be random variables, assume for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $j \in \{1,2,\ldots,J\}$, $A \in \mathcal{B}([0,T])$, $B \in \mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^{d})$ that

```
$$\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{T} \in A) = \mathbb{P}(\mathfrak{T}_{n,j} \in A) \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbb{P}(\mathcal{X} \in B) = \mathbb{P}(\mathfrak{X}_{n,j} \in B), \tag{16.15}$$
```
