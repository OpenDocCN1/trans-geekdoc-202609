Induction therefore proves (2.92). This establishes item (i). Note (2.79), item (v) in Proposition 2.1.2, item (i), and the fact that $\mathcal{I}(\mathbb{I})=\mathcal{O}(\mathbb{I})$ ensure that

$\begin{align*}\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbb{N}}(\mathcal{E}_{L,\mathbb{I}}(\Phi)) &= \mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbb{N}}([\mathbb{I}^{*}(L-L(\Phi))] \bullet \Phi)\\&\in C(\mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{I}(\Phi)},\mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{O}(\mathbb{I})}) = C(\mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{I}(\Phi)},\mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{I}(\mathbb{I})}) = C(\mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{I}(\Phi)},\mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{O}(\Phi)})\end{align*}$

and

$\begin{align*}\forall x \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathcal{I}(\Phi)}: \; (\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbb{N}}(\mathcal{E}_{L,\mathbb{I}}(\Phi)))(x) &= (\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbb{N}}([\mathbb{I}^{*}(L-L(\Phi))] \bullet \Phi))(x)\& = (\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbb{N}}(\Phi))(x).\end{align*}$

This establishes item (ii). The proof of Lemma 2.2.10 is thus complete.

**Lemma 2.2.11** (Architectures of extensions of fully-connected feedforward *ANNs*). Let $d,\mathbb{I},L,\mathfrak{L}\in\mathbb{N}$, $l_{0},l_{1},\ldots,l_{L-1}\in\mathbb{N}$, $\Phi,\Psi\in\mathbf{N}$ satisfy

$\begin{align*}\mathfrak{L}\geq L, \quad \mathcal{D}(\Phi)=(l_{0},l_{1},\ldots,l_{L-1},d), \quad \text{and} \quad \mathcal{D}(\Psi)=(d,\mathbb{I},d)\end{align*}$

(cf. *Definition* 1.3.1). Then $\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathfrak{L},\Psi}(\Phi))\in\mathbb{N}^{\mathfrak{L}+1}$ and

$\begin{align*}\mathcal{D}(\mathcal{E}_{\mathfrak{L},\Psi}(\Phi)) = \begin{cases}(l_{0},l_{1},\ldots,l_{L-1},d) & \text{: } \mathfrak{L}=L\\(l_{0},l_{1},\ldots,l_{L-1},\mathbb{I},\mathbb{I},\ldots,\mathbb{I},d) & \text{: } \mathfrak{L}>L\end{cases}\end{align*}$

(cf. *Definition* 2.2.8).

*Proof of Lemma 2.2.11.* Observe that item (i) in *Lemma* 2.2.9 demonstrates that

$\begin{align*}\mathcal{H}(\Psi^{\bullet(\mathfrak{L}-L)}) &= \mathfrak{L}-L, \quad \quad \mathcal{D}(\Psi^{\bullet(\mathfrak{L}-L)}) \in \mathbb{N}^{\mathfrak{L}-L+2}\end{align*}$

and

$\begin{align*}\mathcal{D}(\Psi^{\bullet(\mathfrak{L}-L)}) = \begin{cases}(d,d) & \text{: } \mathfrak{L}=L\\(d,\mathbb{I},\mathbb{I},\ldots,\mathbb{I},d) & \text{: } \mathfrak{L}>L\end{cases}\end{align*}$

(cf. *Definition* 2.1.6). Combining this with *Proposition* 2.1.2 establishes that

$\begin{align*}\mathcal{H}((\Psi^{\bullet(\mathfrak{L}-L)} \bullet \Phi)) &= \mathcal{H}(\Psi^{\bullet(\mathfrak{L}-L)})+\mathcal{H}(\Phi) = (\mathfrak{L}-L)+L-1 = \mathfrak{L}-1,\end{align*}$

$\begin{align*}\mathcal{D}((\Psi^{\bullet(\mathfrak{L}-L)} \bullet \Phi)) \in \mathbb{N}^{\mathfrak{L}+1},\end{align*}$

and

$\begin{align*}\mathcal{D}(((\Psi^{\bullet(\mathfrak{L}-L)} \bullet \Phi)) = \begin{cases}(l_{0},l_{1},\ldots,l_{L-1},d) & \text{: } \mathfrak{L}=L\\(l_{0},l_{1},\ldots,l_{L-1},\mathbb{I},\mathbb{I},\ldots,\mathbb{I},d) & \text{: } \mathfrak{L}>L\end{cases}\end{align*}$

This and (2.79) establish (2.98). The proof of Lemma 2.2.11 is thus complete.
