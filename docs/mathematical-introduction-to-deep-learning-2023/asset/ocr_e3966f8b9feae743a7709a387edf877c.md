# ### 9.12.2 Abstract local convergence results for GD processes

Proposition 9.12.4. Let $\boldsymbol{d} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mathbf{c} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\varepsilon, L, \mathfrak{C} \in (0, \infty)$, $\alpha \in (0, 1)$, $\gamma \in (0, L^{-1}]$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d}$, let $\mathbb{B} \subset \mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d}$ satisfy $\mathbb{B} = \{\theta \in \mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d} : \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 < \varepsilon\}$, let $\mathscr{L} \in C(\mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d}, \mathbb{R})$ satisfy $\mathscr{L}|_\mathbb{B} \in C^1(\mathbb{B}, \mathbb{R})$, let $\mathfrak{G} : \mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d} \to \mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d}$ satisfy for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ that $\mathfrak{G}(\theta) = (\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta)$, assume $\mathfrak{G}(\vartheta) = 0$, assume for all $\theta_1, \theta_2 \in \mathbb{B}$ that

$$\|\mathfrak{G}(\theta_1) - \mathfrak{G}(\theta_2)\|_2 \leq L \|\theta_1 - \theta_2\|_2,\tag{9.216}$$

let $\Theta : \mathbb{N}_0 \to \mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d}$ satisfy for all $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ that $\Theta_{n+1} = \Theta_n - \gamma \mathfrak{G}(\Theta_n)$, and assume for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ that

$$\begin{aligned}
\left|\mathscr{L}(\theta) - \mathscr{L}(\vartheta)\right|^\alpha &\leq \mathbf{c} \|\mathfrak{G}(\theta)\|_2, \quad \mathbf{c} = \left|\mathscr{L}(\Theta_0) - \mathscr{L}(\vartheta)\right|, \quad 2 \mathfrak{C} (1-\alpha)^{-1} \mathbf{c}^{1-\alpha} + \|\Theta_0 - \vartheta\|_2 < \frac{\varepsilon}{\gamma L + 1},
\end{aligned}\tag{9.217}$$

and $\inf_{n \in \{m \in \mathbb{N}_0 : \forall k \in \mathbb{N}_0 \cap [0, m] : \Theta_k \in \mathbb{B}\}} \mathscr{L}(\Theta_n) \geq \mathscr{L}(\vartheta)$ (cf. Definition 3.9.4). Then there exists $\psi \in \mathscr{L}^{\leftarrow -1}\left(\{\mathscr{L}(\vartheta)\}\right) \cap \mathfrak{G}^{-1}\left(\{0\}\right) \cap \mathbb{B}$ such that

+   * (i) it holds for all $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ that $\Theta_n \in \mathbb{B}$,

+   * (ii) it holds for all $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ that $0 \leq \mathscr{L}(\Theta_n) - \mathscr{L}(\psi) \leq 2 \mathfrak{C} \mathbf{c}^2 \mathfrak{C}^{\leftarrow -1}_{\{0\}}(\mathbf{c}) + \mathbf{c}^{2\alpha} n^\gamma + 2 \mathfrak{C} \mathbf{c}^{\leftarrow -1}$, and

+   * (iii) it holds for all $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ that

$$\begin{aligned}
\|\Theta_n - \psi\|_2 &\leq \sum_{k=n}^{\infty} \|\Theta_{k+1} - \Theta_k\|_2 \leq 2 \mathfrak{C} (1-\alpha)^{-1} \left[\mathscr{L}(\Theta_n) - \mathscr{L}(\psi)\right]^{1-\alpha} \\
&\leq 2^{2-\alpha} \mathfrak{C}^{3-2\alpha} \mathfrak{C}^{\leftarrow -2\alpha}_{\{0\}} (1-\alpha)^{-1} \mathfrak{C}^{\leftarrow -1}_{\{0\}}(\mathbf{c}) + \mathbf{c}^{2\alpha} n^\gamma + 2 \mathfrak{C} \mathbf{c}^{\leftarrow -1}\left[\mathbf{c}^{2\alpha} n^\gamma\right]^{\alpha - 1}.
\end{aligned}\tag{9.218}$$

Proof of Proposition 9.12.4. Throughout this proof, let $T \in \mathbb{N}_0 \cup \{\infty\}$ satisfy

$$T = \inf\left(\left\{n \in \mathbb{N}_0 : \Theta_n \notin \mathbb{B}\right\} \cup \{\infty\}\right),\tag{9.219}$$

let $\mathtt{L} : \mathbb{N}_0 \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ that $\mathtt{L}(n) = \mathscr{L}(\Theta_n) - \mathscr{L}(\vartheta)$, and let $\tau \in \mathbb{N}_0 \cup \{\infty\}$ satisfy

$$\tau = \inf\left(\left\{n \in \mathbb{N}_0 \cap [0, T) : \mathtt{L}(n) = 0\right\} \cup \{T\}\right).\tag{9.220}$$

Observe that the assumption that $\mathfrak{G}(\vartheta) = 0$ implies for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ that

$$\gamma \|\mathfrak{G}(\theta)\|_2 = \gamma \|\mathfrak{G}(\theta) - \mathfrak{G}(\vartheta)\|_2 \leq \gamma L \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2.\tag{9.221}$$

This, the fact that $\|\Theta_0 - \vartheta\|_2 < \varepsilon$, and the fact that

$$\begin{aligned}
\|\Theta_1 - \vartheta\|_2 &\leq \|\Theta_1 - \Theta_0\|_2 + \|\Theta_0 - \vartheta\|_2 = \gamma \|\mathfrak{G}(\Theta_0)\|_2 + \|\Theta_0 - \vartheta\|_2 \\
&\leq \gamma (L + 1) \|\Theta_0 - \vartheta\|_2 < \varepsilon
\end{aligned}\tag{9.222}$$
