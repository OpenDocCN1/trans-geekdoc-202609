(iii) it holds for all $f, g \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R})$ that
$$\mathbb{E}\big[|f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]|^{2}\big] = \mathbb{E}\big[|g(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]|^{2}\big] + \mathbf{r}(f) - \mathbf{r}(g). \tag{14.4}$$

Proof of Lemma 14.1.1. First, note that (14.1) shows that for all $f \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R})$ it holds that
$$\begin{aligned} \mathbf{r}(f) = \mathbb{E}\big[|f(X) - Y|^{2}\big] & = \mathbb{E}\big[|f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]) + (\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - Y)|^{2}\big] 
& = \mathbb{E}\big[|f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]|^{2}\big] + 2\mathbb{E}\Big[\big(f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]\big)\big(\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - Y\big)\Big] \tag{14.5} 
& \quad + \mathbb{E}\big[|\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - Y|^{2}\big] \end{aligned}$$

Furthermore, observe that the tower rule demonstrates that for all $f \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R})$ it holds that
$$\begin{aligned} & \mathbb{E}\Big[\big(f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]\big)\big(\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - Y\big)\Big] 
& \quad = \mathbb{E}\Big[\mathbb{E}\Big[\big(f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]\big)\big(\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - Y\big)\Big|X\Big]\Big] \tag{14.6} 
& \quad = \mathbb{E}\Big[\big(f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]\big)\mathbb{E}\Big[\big(\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - Y\big)\Big|X\Big]\Big] 
& \quad = \mathbb{E}\Big[\big(f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]\big)\Big(\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]\big)\Big] = 0. \end{aligned}$$

Combining this with (14.5) establishes that for all $f \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R})$ it holds that
$$\mathbf{r}(f) = \mathbb{E}\Big[|f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]|^{2}\Big] + \mathbb{E}\Big[|\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - Y|^{2}\Big]. \tag{14.7}$$

This implies that for all $f, g \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R})$ it holds that
$$\mathbf{r}(f) - \mathbf{r}(g) = \mathbb{E}\Big[|f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]|^{2}\Big] - \mathbb{E}\Big[|g(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]|^{2}\Big]. \tag{14.8}$$

Therefore, we obtain that for all $f, g \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R})$ it holds that
$$\mathbb{E}\Big[|f(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]|^{2}\Big] = \mathbb{E}\Big[|g(X) - \mathbb{E}[Y|X]|^{2}\Big] + \mathbf{r}(f) - \mathbf{r}(g). \tag{14.9}$$

Combining this with (14.7) and (14.8) proves items (i), (ii), and (iii). The proof of Lemma 14.1.1 is thus complete. ∎

# 14.1.1 Risk minimization for measurable functions

Proposition 14.1.2. Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ be a probability space, let $(S, \mathcal{S})$ be a measurable space, let $X: \Omega \rightarrow S$ and $Y: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be random variables, assume $\mathbb{E}[Y^{2}] < \infty$, let $\mathcal{E}: \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R}) \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $f \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R})$ that
$$\mathcal{E}(f) = \mathbb{E}\big[|f(X) - Y|^{2}\big]. \tag{14.10}$$
Then
$$\begin{aligned} \big\{& f \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R}): \mathcal{E}(f) = \inf_{g \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R})}\mathcal{E}(g)\big\} 
= \{& f \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R}): \mathcal{E}(f) = \mathbb{E}\Big[|\mathbb{E}[Y|X] - Y|^{2}\Big]\big\} \tag{14.11} 
= \{& f \in \mathcal{L}^{2}(\mathbb{P}_{X}; \mathbb{R}): f(X) = \mathbb{E}[Y|X]\ \mathbb{P}\text{-}a.s.\big\}. \end{aligned}$$
