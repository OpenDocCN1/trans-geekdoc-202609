**Lemma 2.4.2.** *Let* $m,n\in\mathbb{N}$. *Then*

*($cf.$ Definitions 1.3.4, 1.5.5, *and* 2.3.1)*. *This proves items* $(ii)$ *and* $(iii)$. *The proof of Lemma 2.4.2 is thus complete.* $\square$
