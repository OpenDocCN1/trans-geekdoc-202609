*(ii)* it holds that $\{ \theta \in \mathbb{B}: \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \inf_{w \in \mathbb{B}} \mathcal{L}(w) \} = \{ \vartheta \}$, and

*(iii)* it holds that $(\nabla \mathcal{L})(\vartheta) = 0$.

Proof of *Lemma 5.6.8*. Throughout this proof, let $B$ be the set given by

$B = \{ w \in \mathbb{R}^3: \| w - \vartheta \|_2 < r \}$.  (5.129)

Note that (5.128) implies that for all $v \in \mathbb{R}^3$ with $\| v \|_2 \le r$ it holds that

$\langle \langle \nabla \mathcal{L} \rangle (\vartheta + v), v \rangle \ge c \| v \|_2^2$.  (5.130)

The fundamental theorem of calculus hence demonstrates that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ it holds that

$\begin{aligned} \mathcal{L}(\theta) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta) &= [\mathcal{L}(\vartheta + t(\theta - \vartheta))]_{t=0}^{t=1} \\ &= \int_0^1 \mathcal{L}'(\vartheta + t(\theta - \vartheta))(\theta - \vartheta) \, \mathrm{d}t \\ &= \int_0^1 \langle \langle \nabla \mathcal{L} \rangle (\vartheta + t(\theta - \vartheta)), t(\theta - \vartheta) \rangle \frac{1}{t} \, \mathrm{d}t \\ &\ge \int_0^1 c \| t(\theta - \vartheta) \|_2^2 \frac{1}{t} \, \mathrm{d}t = c \| \theta - \vartheta \|_2^2 \left[ \int_0^1 t \, \mathrm{d}t \right] = \tfrac{c}{2} \| \theta - \vartheta \|_2^2 . \end{aligned}  (5.131)

This proves item *(i)*. Next observe that (5.131) ensures that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B} \setminus \{ \vartheta \}$ it holds that

$\mathcal{L}(\theta) \ge \mathcal{L}(\vartheta) + \tfrac{c}{2} \| \theta - \vartheta \|_2^2 > \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)$.  (5.132)

Hence, we obtain for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B} \setminus \{ \vartheta \}$ that

$\inf_{w \in \mathbb{B}} \mathcal{L}(w) = \mathcal{L}(\vartheta) < \mathcal{L}(\theta)$.  (5.133)

This establishes item *(ii)*. It thus remains thus remains to prove item *(iii)*. For this observe that item *(ii)* ensures that

$\{ \theta \in B: \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \inf_{w \in B} \mathcal{L}(w) \} = \{ \vartheta \}$.  (5.134)

Combining this, the fact that $B$ is open, and Lemma 5.6.7 (applied with $\mathfrak{d} \curvearrowright \mathfrak{d}$, $\mathcal{O} \curvearrowright B$, $\vartheta \curvearrowright \vartheta$, $\mathcal{L} \curvearrowright \mathcal{L}|_B$ in the notation of Lemma 5.6.7) assures that $(\nabla \mathcal{L})(\vartheta) = 0$. This establishes item *(iii)*. The proof of Lemma 5.6.8 is thus complete. ∎
