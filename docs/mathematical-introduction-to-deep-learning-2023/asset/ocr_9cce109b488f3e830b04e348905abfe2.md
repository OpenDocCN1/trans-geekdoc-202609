(ii) it holds that $\Omega\ni\eta\mapsto\sup_{x\in E}|Z_{x}(\eta)-\mathbb{E}[Z_{x}]|\in[0,\infty]$ is $\mathcal{F}/\mathcal{B}([0,\infty])$-measurable.

Proof of Lemma 12.3.5. Observe that the assumption that for all $x, y \in E$ it holds that $|Z_{x}-Z_{y}|\leq L\delta(x, y)$ implies that for all $x, y \in E, \eta \in \Omega$ it holds that

$
\begin{align*}
\left|\left(Z_{x}(\eta)-\mathbb{E}[Z_{x}]-\left(Z_{y}(\eta)-\mathbb{E}[Z_{y}]ight)\right| & =\left|\left(Z_{x}(\eta)-Z_{y}(\eta)ight)+\left(\mathbb{E}[Z_{y}]-\mathbb{E}[Z_{x}]\right)\right|\\
& \leq\left|Z_{x}(\eta)-Z_{y}(\eta)ight|+\left|\mathbb{E}[Z_{x}]-\mathbb{E}[Z_{y}]\right|\\
& \leq L \delta(x, y)+\left|\mathbb{E}[Z_{x}]-\mathbb{E}[Z_{y}]\right|\\
& = L \delta(x, y)+\left|\mathbb{E}[Z_{x}-Z_{y}]\right|\\
& \leq L \delta(x, y)+\mathbb{E}\left[\left|Z_{x}-Z_{y}\right|\right]\\
& \leq L \delta(x, y)+L \delta(x, y)=2 L \delta(x, y).
\end{align*}

This ensures item (i). Note that item (i) shows that for all $\eta \in \Omega$ it holds that $E \ni x \mapsto\left|Z_{x}(\eta)-\mathbb{E}[Z_{x}]\right|\in \mathbb{R}$ is continuous. Combining this and the assumption that $E$ is separable with Lemma 12.3.2 proves item (ii). The proof of Lemma 12.3.5 is thus complete.
