# 1.6.2 Vectorized description of simple fully-connected RNNs

**Definition 1.6.3** (Vectorized description of simple fully-connected $RNN$ nodes). Let $\mathbf{r}, \mathbf{n}, \mathbf{i} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{(\mathbf{r}+\mathbf{i}+1) \times (\mathbf{i}+1)\mathbf{n}}$ and let $\Psi_1: \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i} \to \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i}$ and $\Psi_2: \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{n} \to \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{n}$ be functions. Then we call $r$ the realization function of the simple fully-connected $RNN$ node with parameter vector $\theta$ and activation functions $\Psi_1$ and $\Psi_2$ (we call $r$ the realization of the simple fully-connected $RNN$ node with parameter vector $\theta$ and activations $\Psi_1$ and $\Psi_2$) if and only if it holds that $r: \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{r} \times \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i} \to \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{n} \times \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i}$ is the function from $\mathbb{R}^\mathbf{r} \times \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i}$ to $\mathbb{R}^\mathbf{n} \times \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i}$ which satisfies for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{r}$, $\dot{x} \in \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i}$ that

$$ r(x, \dot{x}) = \left( \left( \Psi_2 \circ \mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{n}, \mathbf{i}}^{\theta, (\mathbf{r}+\mathbf{i}+1)\mathbf{i}} \circ \Psi_1 \circ \mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{r}+\mathbf{i}}^{\theta_{\dot{x}, \mathbf{i}}} \right) (x, \dot{x}), \left( \Psi_1 \circ \mathcal{A}_{\mathbf{i}, \mathbf{r}+\mathbf{i}}^{\theta_{\dot{x}, \mathbf{i}}} \right) (x, \dot{x}) \right) \tag{1.64} $$

(cf. Definition 1.1.1).

**Definition 1.6.4** (Vectorized description of simple fully-connected $RNNs$). Let $\mathbf{r}, \mathbf{n}, \mathbf{i}, T \in \mathbb{N}$, $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{(\mathbf{r}+\mathbf{i}+1)\mathbf{i}+(\mathbf{i}+1)\mathbf{n}}$, $\mathbb{I} \in \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i}$ and let $\Psi_1: \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i} \to \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i}$ and $\Psi_2: \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{n} \to \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{n}$ be functions. Then we call $R$ the realization function of the $T$-step unrolled simple fully-connected $RNN$ with parameter vector $\theta$, activation functions $\Psi_1$ and $\Psi_2$, and initial information $\mathbb{I}$ (we call $R$ the realization of the $T$-step unrolled simple fully-connected $RNN$ with parameter vector $\theta$, activations $\Psi_1$ and $\Psi_2$, and initial information $\mathbb{I}$) if and only if there exists $r: \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{r} \times \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i} \to \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{n} \times \mathbb{R}^\mathbf{i}$ such that

+   (i) it holds that $r$ is the realization of the simple fully-connected $RNN$ node with parameters $\theta$ and activations $\Psi_1$ and $\Psi_2$ and

+   (ii) it holds that

$$ R = \mathfrak{R}_{r, T, \mathbb{I}} \tag{1.65} $$

(cf. Definitions 1.6.1 and 1.6.3).
