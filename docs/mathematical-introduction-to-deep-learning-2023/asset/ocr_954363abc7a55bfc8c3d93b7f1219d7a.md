# # Chapter 2
# ANN calculus

In this chapter we review certain operations that can be performed on the set of fully-connected feedforward $\mathbf{ANNs}$ such as compositions (see Section 2.1), parallelizations (see Section 2.2), scalar multiplications (see Section 2.3), and sums (see Section 2.4) and thereby review an appropriate calculus for fully-connected feedforward $\mathbf{ANNs}$The operations and the calculus for fully-connected feedforward $\mathbf{ANNs}$ presented in this chapter will be used in Chapters 3 and 4 to establish certain $\mathbf{ANN}$ approximation results.

In the literature such operations on $\mathbf{ANNs}$ and such kind of calculus on $\mathbf{ANNs}$ has been used in many research articles such as [128, 159, 180, 181, 184, 228, 321, 329, 333] and the references therein. The specific presentation of this chapter is based on Grohs et al. [180, 181].

# ## 2.1 Compositions of fully-connected feedforward $\mathbf{ANNs}$

# ### 2.1.1 Compositions of fully-connected feedforward $\mathbf{ANNs}$

**Definition 2.1.1 (Composition of $\mathbf{ANNs}$)**. We denote by

```
$$( \bullet)(\cdot) \bullet(\cdot): \{ (\Phi, \Psi) \in \mathbf{N} \times \mathbf{N} : \mathcal{I}(\Phi) = \mathcal{O}(\Psi) \} \rightarrow \mathbf{N} \tag{2.1}$$
```

the function which satisfies for all $\Phi, \Psi \in \mathbf{N}$, $k \in \{ 1, 2, \ldots, \mathcal{L}(\Phi) + \mathcal{L}(\Psi) - 1 \}$ with $\mathcal{I}(\Phi) = \mathcal{O}(\Psi)$ that $\mathcal{L}(\Phi \bullet \Psi) = \mathcal{L}(\Phi) + \mathcal{L}(\Psi) - 1$ and

```
$$(\mathcal{W}_{k, \Phi \bullet \Psi}, \mathcal{B}_{k, \Phi \bullet \Psi}) = \begin{cases} (\mathcal{W}_{k, \Psi}, \mathcal{B}_{k, \Psi}) & : k < \mathcal{L}(\Psi) \\ (\mathcal{W}_{1, \Phi}\mathcal{W}_{\mathcal{L}(\Psi), \Psi}, \mathcal{W}_{1, \Phi}\mathcal{B}_{\mathcal{L}(\Psi), \Psi} + \mathcal{B}_{1, \Phi}) & : k = \mathcal{L}(\Psi) \\ (\mathcal{W}_{k - \mathcal{L}(\Psi) + 1, \Phi}, \mathcal{B}_{k - \mathcal{L}(\Psi) + 1, \Phi}) & : k > \mathcal{L}(\Psi) \end{cases} \tag{2.2}$$
```

(cf. Definition 1.3.1).
