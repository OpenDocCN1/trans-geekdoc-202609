Lemma **7.2.6**, the fact that $X_{n,j}: \Omega \to S$, $j \in \{1, 2, \ldots, J_n\}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, are i.i.d. random variables, and the fact that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $j \in \{1, 2, \ldots, J_n\}$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that

$\mathbb{E}\bigl[\|\| (\nabla_{\vartheta} \ell)(\theta, X_{n,j}) \|\|\bigr] = \mathbb{E}\bigl[\|\| (\nabla_{\vartheta} \ell)(\theta, X_{1,1}) \|\|\bigr] < \infty \qquad\qquad (7.47)$

hence establish that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that

$\begin{gathered}\phi_n(\vartheta) \geq \left(\frac{\gamma_n}{J_n}\right)^2 \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} \mathbb{E}\left[\left\|(\nabla_{\vartheta}\ell)(\theta, X_{n,j}) - \mathbb{E}\left[(\nabla_{\vartheta}\ell)(\theta, X_{n,j})\right] \right\|^2\right] \\[6pt] = \left(\frac{\gamma_n}{J_n}\right)^2 \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} \mathbb{E}\left[\left\|(\nabla_{\vartheta}\ell)(\theta, X_{1,1}) - \mathbb{E}\left[(\nabla_{\vartheta}\ell)(\theta, X_{1,1})\right] \right\|^2\right] \\[6pt] = \left(\frac{\gamma_n}{J_n}\right)^2 \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} \mathcal{V}(\theta) = \left(\frac{\gamma_n}{J_n}\right)^2 \left[J_n \mathcal{V}(\theta)\right] = \left(\frac{\gamma_n}{J_n}\right)^2 \mathcal{V}(\theta). \end{gathered} \qquad (7.48)$

Furthermore, observe that **(7.42)**, **(7.44)**, the fact that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ it holds that $\Theta_{n-1}$ and $(X_{n,j})_{j \in \{1,2,\ldots,J_n\}}$ are independent random variables, and Lemma **7.2.7** prove that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that

$\begin{gathered}\mathbb{E}\bigl[\|\| \Theta_n - \vartheta \|\|^2\bigr] = \mathbb{E}\left[\left\|\left| \Theta_{n-1} - \frac{\gamma_n}{J_n}\left[\sum_{j=1}^{J_n} (\nabla_{\vartheta}\ell)(\Theta_{n-1}, X_{n,j})\right] - \vartheta \right\|\right]^2\bigr] \\[6pt] = \mathbb{E}\bigl[\phi_n(\Theta_{n-1})\bigr]. \end{gathered} \qquad (7.49)$

Combining this with **(7.48)** implies that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that

$\begin{aligned}\mathbb{E}\bigl[\|\| \Theta_n - \vartheta \|\|^2\bigr] &\geq \mathbb{E}\left[\left(\frac{\gamma_n}{J_n}\right)^2 \mathcal{V}(\Theta_{n-1})\right] = \left(\frac{\gamma_n}{J_n}\right)^2 \mathbb{E}\bigl[\mathcal{V}(\Theta_{n-1})\bigr]. \end{aligned} \qquad (7.50)$

This establishes **(7.43)**. The proof of Lemma **7.2.9** is thus complete.
