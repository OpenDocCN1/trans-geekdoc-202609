This, (5.161), and (5.168) demonstrate that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\mathbf{t} < n$ it holds that

$$\begin{align*}\liminf_{s / \uparrow \tau_n} \|\Theta_s \| &= \liminf_{s / \uparrow \tau_n} \|\Theta_s \| = \liminf_{s / \uparrow \tau_n} \| \Theta^{(n)}_s \| \\
&= -\frac{1}{(n-\mathbf{i})} + \liminf_{s / \uparrow \tau_n} \left[ \left\| \Theta^{(n)}_s \right\| + \frac{1}{(n-\mathbf{i})} \right] \\
&= -\frac{1}{(n-\mathbf{i})} + \liminf_{s / \uparrow \tau_n} \left[ \left\| \Theta^{(n)}_s \right\| + \frac{1}{(n-\mathbf{s})} \right] = \infty.\tag{5.174}\end{align*}$$

Therefore, we obtain that

$$\liminf_{s / \uparrow t} \|\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_s + s \| = \infty.\tag{5.175}$$

Next note that for all $\mathbf{i} \in (0, \infty)$, $\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}} \in C([0, \mathbf{i}], \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta})$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $t \in [0, \min\{\mathbf{\hat{t}}, n\})$ with $\liminf_{s / \uparrow t} \|\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_s + s \| = \infty$ and $\forall s \in [0, \mathbf{i})$: $\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_s = \xi + \int_0^s \mathcal{D}(\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_u) \, du$ it holds that

$$\begin{align*}\liminf_{s / \min\{\mathbf{\hat{t}}, n\}} \left[ \left\| \mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_s \right\| + \frac{1}{(n-\mathbf{s})} \right] &= \infty \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_t = \xi + \int_0^t \mathcal{D}(\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_s) \, \mathrm{d}s.\tag{5.176}\end{align*}$$

This and (5.161) prove that for all $\mathbf{i} \in (0, \infty)$, $\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}} \in C([0, \mathbf{i}], \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta})$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\liminf_{t / \uparrow t} \|\mathbf{\hat{t}}_t + t \| = \infty$ and $\forall t \in [0, \mathbf{i})$: $\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_t = \xi + \int_0^t \mathcal{D}(\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_s) \, ds$ it holds that

$$\begin{align*}\min\{\mathbf{\hat{t}}, n\} &= \tau_n \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_{[0, \tau_n)} = \Theta^{(n)}.\tag{5.177}\end{align*}$$

Combining (5.169) and (5.175) hence assures that for all $\mathbf{i} \in (0, \infty)$, $\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}} \in C([0, \mathbf{i}], \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta})$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$ with $\liminf_{t / \uparrow t} \|\mathbf{\hat{t}}_t + t \| = \infty$ and $\forall t \in [0, \mathbf{i})$: $\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_t = \xi + \int_0^t \mathcal{D}(\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_s) \, ds$ it holds that

$$\begin{align*}\min\{\mathbf{\hat{t}}, n\} = \tau_n &= \min\{\mathbf{\hat{t}}, n\} \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_{[0, \tau_n)} = \Theta^{(n)} = \mathbf{\Theta}|_{[0, \tau_n)}.\tag{5.178}\end{align*}$$

This and (5.167) show that for all $\mathbf{i} \in (0, \infty)$, $\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}} \in C([0, \mathbf{i}], \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta})$ with $\liminf_{t / \uparrow t} \|\mathbf{\hat{t}}_t + t \| = \infty$ and $\forall t \in [0, \mathbf{i})$: $\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_t = \xi + \int_0^t \mathcal{D}(\mathbf{\hat{\Theta}}_s) \, ds$ it holds that

$$\begin{align*}\mathbf{\hat{t}} &= \mathbf{t} \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{\hat{\Theta}} = \mathbf{\Theta}.\tag{5.179}\end{align*}$$

Combining this, (5.169), and (5.175) completes the proof of Lemma 5.7.3.

*(cf. Definitions 1.4.7 and 3.3.4). Then*
