This and the fact that $[0, T] \times \mathbb{R}^d \ni (t, x) \mapsto (\operatorname{Hess}_x u)(t, x) \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ is continuous ensure that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that

$$\begin{aligned}\limsup_{t \downarrow 0} & \left| \frac{u(t, x)-u(0, x)}{t}-\frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Trace}\left(B B^{*}(\operatorname{Hess}_x u)(0, x)\right)\right| \\
& \leq \limsup_{t \downarrow 0} \left[ \frac{1}{t} \int_0^t \left| \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Trace}\left(B B^{*}(\operatorname{Hess}_x u)(s, x)\right)-\frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Trace}\left(B B^{*}(\operatorname{Hess}_x u)(0, x)\right) \right| \mathrm{d} s\right] \\
& \leq \limsup_{t \downarrow 0} \left[ \sup _{s \in[0, t]} \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Trace}\left(B B^{*}\left((\operatorname{Hess}_x u)(s, x)-(\operatorname{Hess}_x u)(0, x)\right)\right)\right]=0 .\end{aligned} \tag{17.31}$$

Item **(I)** therefore shows that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that $[0, T] \ni t \mapsto u(t, x) \in \mathbb{R}$ is differentiable. Combining this with (17.31) and (17.28) ensures that for all $t \in[0, T], x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that

$$\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\right)(t, x)=\frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Trace}\left(B B^{*}(\operatorname{Hess}_x u)(t, x)\right) . \tag{17.32}$$

This and the fact that $[0, T] \times \mathbb{R}^d \ni(t, x) \mapsto\left(\operatorname{Hess}_x u\right)(t, x) \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ is continuous establish item **(i)**. Note that (17.32) proves item **(ii)**. The proof of Proposition 17.3.2 is thus complete.
