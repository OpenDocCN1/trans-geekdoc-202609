This and item (VI) ensure that

$$\|\boldsymbol{\theta}\|_{\infty} \leq \text{max}\{1, L, \text{max}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} \|\boldsymbol{\mathfrak{f}}_{\boldsymbol{k}}\|_{\infty}, 2\text{max}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} |f(\boldsymbol{f}_{\boldsymbol{k}})|\} \tag{4.187} .$$

Moreover, note that (4.186), Lemma 4.4.2, and item (VIII) imply that

$$\begin{aligned} \sup_{x \in E} |f(x) - \mathscr{A}^{\boldsymbol{\theta}, (\boldsymbol{l}_0, 1, \dots, \boldsymbol{l}_{\boldsymbol{L}})}_{\infty, \infty}(x)| &= \sup_{x \in E} |f(x) - \mathscr{A}^{T(\boldsymbol{\Phi}), \mathscr{D}(\boldsymbol{\Phi})}_{\infty, \infty}(x)| \\
&= \sup_{x \in E} |f(x) - (\mathscr{R}_{\boldsymbol{r}}^{\boldsymbol{N}}(\boldsymbol{\Phi}))'(x)| \tag{4.188} \\
&\leq 2L \left[\sup_{x \in E} \left(\text{inf}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} \|x - \boldsymbol{f}_{\boldsymbol{k}}\|_1\right)\right] \end{aligned}$$

(cf. Definition 4.4.1). The proof of Corollary 4.4.9 is thus complete.

Corollary 4.4.10. Let $\boldsymbol{d}, \boldsymbol{K}, \boldsymbol{L} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\boldsymbol{l} = (\boldsymbol{l}_0, 1, \dots, \boldsymbol{l}_{\boldsymbol{L}}) \in \mathbb{N}^{\boldsymbol{L}+1}$, $L \in [0, \infty)$, $\boldsymbol{u} \in [-\infty, \infty)$, $v \in (u, \infty]$ satisfy that

$$\boldsymbol{L} \geq [\log_2 K] + 2, \quad \boldsymbol{l}_0 = \boldsymbol{d}, \quad \boldsymbol{l}_{\boldsymbol{L}} = 1, \quad \boldsymbol{l}_1 \geq 2\boldsymbol{d}K, \quad \text{and} \quad \boldsymbol{d} \geq \sum_{i=1}^{\boldsymbol{L}} \boldsymbol{l}_i(\boldsymbol{l}_{i-1}+1), \tag{4.189}$$

assume for all $i \in \mathbb{N} \cap \{1, \boldsymbol{L}\}$ that $\boldsymbol{l}_i \geq 3[\frac{K}{2^{i-1}}]$, let $E \subseteq \mathbb{R}^d$ be a set, let $\boldsymbol{f}_1, \boldsymbol{f}_2, \dots, \boldsymbol{f}_K \in E$, and let $f: E \to ([u, v] \cap \mathbb{R})$ satisfy for all $x, y \in E$ that $|f(x) - f(y)| \leq L\|x - y\|_1$ (cf. Definitions 3.3.4 and 4.2.6). Then there exists $\boldsymbol{\theta} \in \mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d}$ such that

$$\|\boldsymbol{\theta}\|_{\infty} \leq \text{max}\{1, L, \text{max}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} \|\boldsymbol{\mathfrak{f}}_{\boldsymbol{k}}\|_{\infty}; 2\text{max}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} |f(\boldsymbol{f}_{\boldsymbol{k}})|\} \tag{4.190}$$

and

$$\sup_{x \in E} |f(x) - \mathscr{A}_{u, v}^{\boldsymbol{\theta}, 1}(x)| \leq 2L \left[\sup_{x \in E} \left(\text{inf}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} \|x - \boldsymbol{f}_{\boldsymbol{k}}\|_1\right)\right]. \tag{4.191}$$

(cf. Definition 4.4.1).

Proof of Corollary 4.4.10. Observe that Corollary 4.4.9 demonstrates that there exists $\boldsymbol{\theta} \in \mathbb{R}^\boldsymbol{d}$ such that

$$\|\boldsymbol{\theta}\|_{\infty} \leq \text{max}\{1, L, \text{max}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} \|\boldsymbol{\mathfrak{f}}_{\boldsymbol{k}}\|_{\infty}; 2\text{max}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} |f(\boldsymbol{f}_{\boldsymbol{k}})|\} \tag{4.192}$$

and

$$\sup_{x \in E} |f(x) - \mathscr{A}_{u, v}^{\boldsymbol{\theta}, 1}(x)| \leq 2L \left[\sup_{x \in E} \left(\text{inf}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} \|x - \boldsymbol{f}_{\boldsymbol{k}}\|_1\right)\right]. \tag{4.193}$$

Furthermore, note that the assumption that $f(E) \subseteq [u, v]$ proves that for all $x \in E$ it holds that

$$f(x) = \boldsymbol{c}_{u, v}(f(x)) \tag{4.194}$$

(cf. Definitions 1.2.9 and 4.4.1). The fact that for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ it holds that $|\boldsymbol{c}_{u, v}(x) - \boldsymbol{c}_{u, v}(y)| \leq |x - y|$ and (4.193) hence establish that

$$\begin{aligned} \sup_{x \in E} & |f(x) - \mathscr{A}_{u, v}^{\boldsymbol{\theta}, 1}(x)| = \sup_{x \in E} |\boldsymbol{c}_{u, v}(f(x)) - \boldsymbol{c}_{u, v}(\mathscr{A}_{\infty, \infty}^{\boldsymbol{\theta}, 1}(x))| \tag{4.195} \\
&\leq \sup_{x \in E} |f(x) - \mathscr{A}_{\infty, \infty}^{\boldsymbol{\theta}, 1}(x)| \leq 2L \left[\sup_{x \in E} \left(\text{inf}_{\boldsymbol{k} \in \{1,2,\dots,K\}} \|x - \boldsymbol{f}_{\boldsymbol{k}}\|_1\right)\right]. \end{aligned}$$

The proof of Corollary 4.4.10 is thus complete.
