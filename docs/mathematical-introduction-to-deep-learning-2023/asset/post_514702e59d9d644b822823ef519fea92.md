Proof of Lemma 4.3.5. Note that the assumption that for all $x, y \in [a,b]^d$ it holds that $|f(x) - f(y)| \le L \|x - y\|_1$ assures that $L \ge 0$. Next observe that Lemma 2.3.2 assures that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that

$ (\mathcal{R}_v^\mathbf{N}(\mathbf{F}))(x) = f((a+b)/2, (a+b)/2, \dots, (a+b)/2). \qquad (4.93) $

The fact that for all $x \in [a,b]$ it holds that $|x - (a+b)/2| \le (b-a)/2$ and the assumption that for all $x, y \in [a,b]^d$ it holds that $|f(x) - f(y)| \le L \|x - y\|_1$ hence ensure that for all $x = (x_1, x_2, \dots, x_d) \in [a,b]^d$ it holds that

$\begin{align*} &| (\mathcal{R}_v^\mathbf{N}(\mathbf{F}))(x) - f(x)| = | f((a+b)/2, (a+b)/2, \dots, (a+b)/2) - f(x)| \\ &\quad\le L \|((a+b)/2, (a+b)/2, \dots, (a+b)/2) - x\|_1 \\ &\quad= L \sum_{i=1}^d |(a+b)/2 - x_i| \le \sum_{i=1}^d \frac{L(b-a)}{2} = \frac{d L (b-a)}{2}. \end{align*} \qquad (4.94) $

This and the fact that $\|\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{F})\|_{\infty} = |f((a+b)/2, (a+b)/2, \dots, (a+b)/2)| \le \sup_{x \in [a,b]^d} |f(x)|$ complete the proof of Lemma 4.3.5.
