Moreover, note that the fact that $\max \{1, L, |a|, |b|\} \le c$ and $(b-a)^2 \le (|a|+|b|)^2 \le 2(a^2+b^2)$ shows that

$9L^2(b-a)^2 \le 18c^2(a^2+b^2) \le 18c^2(c^2+c^2) = 36c^4$.   (15.40)

In addition, observe that the fact that $B \ge c \ge 1$, the fact that $M \ge 1$, and Lemma **15.1.4** prove that $\ln(3MBc) \le \frac{23B}{18}\ln(cM)$. This, (15.40), the fact that $(v-u) \le 2\max\{ |u|, |v| \} = \max\{2|u|, 2|v|\} \le c \le B$, and the fact that $B \ge 1$ demonstrate that for all $p \in (0, \infty)$ it holds that

$\frac{9d^2L^2(b-a)^2}{A^{2/a}} + \frac{4(v-u)\mathbf{L}(|\mathbf{L}|_{\infty} + \mathbf{L})c^{L+1}\max\{1, p\}}{\mathbf{K}|\mathbf{L}^{-1}(|\mathbf{L}|_{\infty}+1)^{-2}}$
$+ \frac{18\max\{1, (v-u)^2\} \mathbf{L}(|\mathbf{L}|_{\infty} + 1)^2\max\{p, \ln(3MBc)\}}{\sqrt{M}}$
$\le \frac{36d^2c^4}{A^{2/a}} + \frac{4\mathbf{L}(|\mathbf{L}|_{\infty} + 1)^2c^{L+2}\max\{1, p\}}{\mathbf{K}|\mathbf{L}^{-1}(|\mathbf{L}|_{\infty}+1)^{-2}}$
$+ \frac{23B^3\mathbf{L}(|\mathbf{L}|_{\infty}+1)^2\max\{p, \ln(ecM)\}}{\sqrt{M}}.   (15.41)$

Combining this with (15.39) implies (15.33). The proof of Theorem **15.1.5** is thus complete.

# Corollary **15.1.6**.

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ be a probability space, let $M, d \in \mathbb{N}$, $a, u \in \mathbb{R}$, $b \in (a, \infty)$, $v \in (u, \infty)$, for every $j \in \mathbb{N}$ let $X_j\colon \Omega \to [a, b]^d$ and $Y_j\colon \Omega \to [u, v]$ be random variables, assume that $(X_j, Y_j)$, $j \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}$, are $i.i.d.$, let $\mathbf{d}, \mathbf{L} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mathbf{1} = (\mathbf{1}_0, \mathbf{1}_1, \dots, \mathbf{1_L}) \in \mathbb{N}^{L+1}$, assume

$\mathbf{l}_0 = d, \quad \mathbf{l_L} = 1, \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{d} \ge \sum_{i=1}^{\mathbf{L}} \mathbf{l}_i (\mathbf{l}_{i-1} + 1),   (15.42)$

let $\mathfrak{R}\colon \mathbb{R}^d \times \Omega \to [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ that

$\mathfrak{R}(\theta) = \frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} [\mathcal{N}_{a, u}^{\theta, j}(X_j) - Y_j]^2],   (15.43)$

let $\mathcal{E}\colon [a, b]^d \to [u, v]$ satisfy $\mathbb{P}$-a.s. that

$\mathcal{E}(X_1) = \mathbb{E}[Y_1|X_1],   (15.44)$

let $L \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $x, y \in [a, b]^d$ that $|\mathcal{E}(x) - \mathcal{E}(y)| \le L\|x-y\|_1$, let $K \in \mathbb{N}$, $c \in \max\{1, L, |a|, |b|, 2|u|, 2|v|\}$, $B \in [c, \infty)$, for every $k, n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ let $\Theta_{k,n}\colon \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ be a random variable, assume $\bigcup_{k=1}^{\infty} \Theta_{k,0}(\Omega) \subseteq [-B, B]^d$, assume that $\Theta_{k,0}$, $k \in \{1, 2, \dots, K\}$, are $i.i.d.$, assume that $\Theta_{1,0}$ is continuously uniformly distributed on $[-c, c]^d$, let $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mathbf{T} \subseteq \{0, 1, \dots, N\}$ satisfy $0 \in \mathbf{T}$, let $\mathbf{k}\colon \Omega \to (\mathbb{N}_0)^2$ be a random variable, and assume for all $\omega \in \Omega$ that

and $\mathfrak{R}(\Theta_{\mathbf{k}(\omega)}(\omega)) = \min_{(k,n) \in \{1, \dots, K\} \times \mathbf{T}, \|\Theta_{k,n}(\omega)\|_{\infty} \le B} \mathfrak{R}(\Theta_{k,n}(\omega))   (15.46)$
