## 12.1.6 A strengthened Hoeffding's inequality

Lemma 12.1.22. Let $f,g: (0, \infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $x \in (0, \infty)$ that $f(x) = 2 \exp(-2x)$ and $g(x) = \frac{1}{4x}$. Then

(ii) it holds that $g(\frac{1}{2}) = \frac{1}{2} < \frac{2}{3} < \frac{2}{e} = f(\frac{1}{2})$.

## 12.2 Covering number estimates

### 12.2.1 Entropy quantities

#### 12.2.1.1 Covering radii (Outer entropy numbers)

**Definition 12.2.1** (Covering radii). Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space and let $n \in \mathbb{N}$. Then we denote by $\mathcal{C}_{(X,d), n} \in [0, \infty]$ (we denote by $\mathcal{C}_{X, n} \in [0, \infty]$ the extended real number given by

$\mathcal{C}_{(X,d), n} = \inf \left\{ r \in [0, \infty] : \left[ \exists A \subseteq X : (|A| \le n) \wedge (\forall x \in X : \exists a \in A : d(a, x) \le r) \right] \right\}$

and we call $\mathcal{C}_{(X,d), n}$ the $n$-covering radius of $(X, d)$ (we call $\mathcal{C}_{X, r}$ the $n$-covering radius of $X$).

**Lemma 12.2.2.** Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space, let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $r \in [0, \infty)$, assume $X \neq \emptyset$, and let $A \subseteq X$ satisfy $|A| \le n$ and $\forall x \in X : \exists a \in A : d(a, x) \le r$. Then there exist $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \in X$ such that

$X \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^n \{v \in X : d(x_i, v) \le r\}$.

Proof of Lemma 12.2.2. Note that the assumption that $X \neq \emptyset$ and the assumption that $|A| \le n$ imply that there exist $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \in X$ which satisfy $A \subseteq \{x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n\}$. This and the assumption that $\forall x \in X : \exists a \in A : d(a, x) \le r$ ensure that

$X \subseteq \bigcup_{a \in A} \{v \in X : d(a, v) \le r\} \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} \{v \in X : d(x_i, v) \le r\}. \tag{12.70}$

The proof of Lemma 12.2.2 is thus complete.

**Lemma 12.2.3.** Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space, let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $r \in [0, \infty]$, $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \in X$ satisfy $X \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} \{v \in X : d(x_i, v) \le r\}$. Then there exists $A \subseteq X$ such that $|A| \le n$ and

$\forall x \in X : \exists a \in A : d(a, x) \le r. \tag{12.71}$

Proof of Lemma 12.2.3. Throughout this proof, let $A = \{x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n\}$. Note that the assumption that $X \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} \{v \in X : d(x_i, v) \le r\}$ implies that for all $v \in X$ there exists $i \in \{1, 2, \ldots, n\}$ such that $d(x_i, v) \le r$. Hence, we obtain that

$\forall x \in X : \exists a \in A : d(a, x) \le r. \tag{12.72}$

The proof of Lemma 12.2.3 is thus complete.

**Lemma 12.2.4.** Let $(X, d)$ be a metric space, let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $r \in [0, \infty]$, and assume $X \neq \emptyset$. Then the following two statements are equivalent:

-   (i) There exists $A \subseteq X$ such that $|A| \le n$ and $\forall x \in X : \exists a \in A : d(a, x) \le r$.
-   (ii) There exist $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \in X$ such that $X \subseteq \bigcup_{i=1}^{n} \{v \in X : d(x_i, v) \le r\}$.

Proof of Lemma 12.2.4. Note that Lemma 12.2.2 and Lemma 12.2.3 prove that $((i) \leftrightarrow (ii))$. The proof of Lemma 12.2.4 is thus complete.

$\mathcal{C}_{(X,d),n} = \begin{cases} 0 & : X = \emptyset \\ \inf(\{r \in [0,\infty) : (\exists x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n \in X : X \subseteq \bigcup_{m=1}^{n} \{v \in X : d(x_m, v) \le r\})\} \cup \{\infty\}) & : X \neq \emptyset \end{cases}$

(cf. Definition 12.2.1). $\tag{12.73}$

Proof of Lemma 12.2.5. Throughout this proof, assume without loss of generality that $X \neq \emptyset$ and let $a \in X$. Note that the assumption that $d$ is a metric implies that for all $x \in X$ it holds that $d(a, x) \le \infty$. Combining this with Lemma 12.2.4 proves (12.73). This completes the proof of Lemma 12.2.5.
