**Definition 9.1.2** (**Standard KL functions**). *Let $\varrho \in \mathbb{N}$ and let $\mathcal{L}\colon \mathbb{R}^{\varrho} \to \mathbb{R}$ be differentiable. Then we say that $\mathcal{L}$ is a standard KL function if and only if for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}$ there exist $\varepsilon, \mathfrak{c} \in (0, \infty)$, $\alpha \in (0,1)$ such that for all $v \in \{v \in \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}\colon \|v - \theta\|_2 < \varepsilon\}$ it holds that*

$\qquad|\mathcal{L}(\theta) - \mathcal{L}(v)|^\alpha \le \mathfrak{c}\,\| (\nabla \mathcal{L})(v)\|_2 \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad (9.2)$

*(cf. Definition 3.3.4).*

# **9.2 Convergence analysis using standard KL functions (regular regime)**

**Proposition 9.2.1.** *Let $\varrho \in \mathbb{N}$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}$, $\mathfrak{c},\varepsilon \in (0, \infty)$, $\alpha \in (0,1)$, $\mathcal{L} \in C^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{\varrho}, \mathbb{R})$, let* $O \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}$ *satisfy*

$\qquad O = \{\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}\colon \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 < \varepsilon \}\setminus \{\vartheta\}$ *and* $\quad \mathfrak{c} = \mathfrak{C}^{2} [ \sup_{\theta \in O} | \mathcal{L}(\theta) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta) | ]^{2 - 2\alpha}, \qquad (9.3)$

*assume for all* $\theta \in O$ *that* $\mathcal{L}(\theta) > \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)$ *and*

$\qquad \qquad \qquad \quad |\mathcal{L}(\theta) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)|^\alpha \le \mathfrak{C} \| (\nabla \mathcal{L})(\theta)\|_2, \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad (9.4)$

*and let* $\Theta \in C([0, \infty), O)$ *satisfy for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that*

$\qquad \qquad \qquad \quad \Theta_{t} = \Theta_{0} - \int_{0}^{t} (\nabla \mathcal{L})(\Theta_{s})\,\mathrm{d}s \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad (9.5)$

*(cf. Definition 3.3.4).*

*Then there exists* $\psi \in \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}$ *such that*

+   *(i) it holds that* $\mathcal{L}(\psi) = \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)$,

+   *(ii) it holds for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that*

$\qquad \qquad 0 \le \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t} - \mathcal{L}(\psi) \le [ (\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{0}) - \mathcal{L}(\psi) )^{-1} + \mathfrak{c}^{-1} t ]^{-1}, \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad (9.6)$

+   *and*

+   *(iii) it holds for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that*

$\qquad \qquad \| \Theta_{t} - \psi \|_2 \le \int_{t}^{\infty} \| (\nabla \mathcal{L})(\Theta_{s}) \|_2\,\mathrm{d}s \\[1mm]
\qquad \qquad \quad \le \mathfrak{C} (1 - \alpha)^{-1} [ \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{0}) - \mathcal{L}(\psi) ]^{1 - \alpha} \\[1mm]
\qquad \qquad \quad \le \mathfrak{C} (1 - \alpha)^{-1} [ (\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{0}) - \mathcal{L}(\psi) )^{-1} + \mathfrak{c}^{-1} t ]^{\alpha - 1} \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad (9.7)$
