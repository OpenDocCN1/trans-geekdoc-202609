holds that

```
\begin{align} \|\nabla \mathscr{L}(\theta)\|_2 = \ & \underset{w \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d},\|w\|_2 = 1}{\text{sup}} \left[\mathscr{L}'(\theta)(w)\right] \\[1ex] = \ & \underset{w \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d},\|w\|_2 = 1}{\text{sup}} \left[\lim_{h \downarrow 0} \tfrac{1}{h} \big( \mathscr{L}(\theta + hw) - \mathscr{L}(\theta) \big)\right] \\[1ex] \leq \ & \underset{w \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d},\|w\|_2 = 1}{\text{sup}} \left[\liminf_{h \downarrow 0} \big\{ \tfrac{1}{h} \max \big\{ \|\theta + hw - \vartheta\|_2, \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 \big\} \|\theta + hw - \theta\|_2 \big\} \right] \\[1ex] = \ & \underset{w \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d},\|w\|_2 = 1}{\text{sup}} \left[\liminf_{h \downarrow 0} \Big\{ L \max \big\{ \|\theta + hw - \vartheta\|_2, \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 \big\} \tfrac{1}{h} \|hw\|_2 \Big\} \right] \\[1ex] = \ & \underset{w \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d},\|w\|_2 = 1}{\text{sup}} \left[ \liminf_{h \downarrow 0} \Big\{ L \max \big\{ \|\theta + hw - \vartheta\|_2, \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 \big\} \Big\} \right] \\[1ex] = \ & \underset{w \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d},\|w\|_2 = 1}{\text{sup}} \Big[ L \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 \Big] = L \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2. \end{align}
```

The fact that the function $\mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d} \ni v \mapsto (\nabla \mathscr{L})(v) \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d}$ is continuous therefore establishes **\(6.159\)** . The proof of Lemma **\(6.1.24\)** is thus complete.

> **Corollary 6.1.25.** Let $\mathfrak{d} \in \mathbb{N}$ , $r \in (0, \infty)$ , $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d}$ , $\mathbb{B} = \{w \in \mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d} : \|w - \vartheta\|_2 \leq r\}$ , $\mathscr{L} \in C^1(\mathbb{R}^\mathfrak{d}, \mathbb{R})$ (cf. Definition 3.3.4). Then the following four statements are equivalent: *{(i)}* There exist $c, L \in (0, \infty)$ such that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ it holds that \begin{equation} \langle \theta - \vartheta, (\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta) \rangle \geq c \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2^2 \quad \text{and} \quad \| (\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta) \|_2 \leq L \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 . \end{equation} *{(ii)}* There exist $\gamma \in (0, \infty)$ , $\varepsilon \in (0,1)$ such that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ it holds that \begin{equation} \|\theta - \gamma(\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta) - \vartheta\|_2 \leq \varepsilon \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 . \end{equation} *{(iii)}* There exists $c \in (0, \infty)$ such that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ it holds that \begin{equation} \langle \theta - \vartheta, (\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta) \rangle \geq c \max \big\{ \|\theta - \vartheta\|_2^2, \|(\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta)\|_2^2 \big\} . \end{equation} *{(iv)}* There exist $c, L \in (0, \infty)$ such that for all $v, w \in \mathbb{B}$ , $s, t \in [0,1]$ with $s \leq t$ it holds that \begin{equation} \mathscr{L}\big( \vartheta + t(v - \vartheta) \big) - \mathscr{L}\big( \vartheta + s(v - \vartheta) \big) \geq c(t^2 - s^2) \|v - \vartheta\|_2^2 \end{equation} and \begin{equation} |\mathscr{L}(v) - \mathscr{L}(w)| \leq L \max \big\{ \|v - \vartheta\|_2, \|w - \vartheta\|_2 \big\} \|v - w\|_2 \end{equation} (cf. Definition 1.4.7).

*Proof of Corollary 6.1.25.* Note that items {(ii)} and {(iii)} in Lemma **\(6.1.8\)** prove that {(i)} $\rightarrow$ {(ii)}. Observe that Lemma **\(6.1.19\)** demonstrates that {(ii)} $\rightarrow$ {(iii)}. Note that Lemma **\(6.1.20\)** establishes that {(iii)} $\rightarrow$ {(i)}. Observe that Lemma **\(6.1.21\)** and Lemma **\(6.1.23\)** show that {(i)} $\rightarrow$ {(iv)}. Note that Lemma **\(6.1.22\)** and Lemma **\(6.1.24\)** establish that {(iv)} $\rightarrow$ {(i)}. The proof of Corollary **\(6.1.25\)** is thus complete.
