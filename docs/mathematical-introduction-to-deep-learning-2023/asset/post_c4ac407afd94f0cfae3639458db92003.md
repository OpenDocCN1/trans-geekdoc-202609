
## 9.11 Convergence analysis for solutions of GF ODEs

### 9.11.1 Abstract local convergence results for GF processes

**Lemma 9.11.1.** Let $\mathfrak{d} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Theta \in C([0, \infty), \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}})$, $\mathcal{L} \in C^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}, \mathbb{R})$, let $\mathscr{F} : \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}} \to \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ *satisfy for all* $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ *that* $\mathscr{F}(\theta) = (\nabla \mathcal{L})(\theta)$, *and assume for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that* $\Theta_{t} = \Theta_{0} - \int_{0}^{t}\mathscr{F}(\Theta_{s}) \, \mathrm{d}s$. *Then it holds for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that*

$$
\mathcal{L}\bigl(\Theta_{t}\bigr) = \mathcal{L}\bigl(\Theta_{0}\bigr) - \int_{0}^{t}\bigl\lVert \mathscr{F}\bigl(\Theta_{s}\bigr) \bigr\rVert_{2}^{2} \, \mathrm{d}s \tag{9.124}
$$

*(cf. Definition 3.3.4).*

**Proof of Lemma 9.11.1.** Note that Lemma 5.2.3 *implies* (9.124). *This completes the proof of Lemma 9.11.1.* $\square$

**Proposition 9.11.2.** Let $\mathfrak{d} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$, $\mathfrak{c} \in \mathbb{R}$, $\mathfrak{C}, \varepsilon \in (0, \infty)$, $\alpha \in (0, 1)$, $\Theta \in C([0, \infty), \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}})$, $\mathcal{L} \in C(\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}, \mathbb{R})$, let $\mathscr{F} : \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}} \to \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ *be* $\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}})/\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}})$- *measurable, assume for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that*

$$
\mathcal{L}\bigl(\Theta_{t}\bigr) = \mathcal{L}\bigl(\Theta_{0}\bigr) - \int_{0}^{t}\bigl\lVert \mathscr{F}\bigl(\Theta_{s}\bigr) \bigr\rVert_{2}^{2} \, \mathrm{d}s \qquad \text{and} \qquad \Theta_{t} = \Theta_{0} - \int_{0}^{t}\mathscr{F}\bigl(\Theta_{s}\bigr) \, \mathrm{d}s, \tag{9.125}
$$

*and assume for all* $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ *with* $\lVert \theta - \vartheta \rVert_{2} < \varepsilon$ *that*

$$
\bigl| \mathcal{L}(\theta) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta) \bigr|^{\alpha} \leq \mathfrak{C} \bigl\lVert \mathscr{F}(\theta) \bigr\rVert_{2}, \qquad \mathfrak{c} = \bigl| \mathcal{L}\bigl(\Theta_{0}\bigr) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta) \bigr|, \qquad \mathfrak{C}(1-\alpha)^{-1}\mathfrak{c}^{1-\alpha} + \bigl\lVert \Theta_{0} - \vartheta \bigr\rVert_{2} < \varepsilon, \tag{9.126}
$$

*and* $\inf_{t \in \{s \in [0, \infty) : \forall r \in [0, s] : \lVert \Theta_{r} - \vartheta \rVert_{2} < \varepsilon \} \} \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) \geq \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)$ *(cf. Definition 3.3.4). Then there exists* $\psi \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ *such that*

- *(i) it holds that* $\mathcal{L}(\psi) = \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)$
- *(ii) it holds for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that* $\lVert \Theta_{t} - \psi \rVert_{2} < \varepsilon$,
- *(iii) it holds for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that* $0 \leq \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) - \mathcal{L}(\psi) \leq \mathfrak{C}^{2}\mathfrak{c}^{2}(\lVert_{[0]}(\mathfrak{c}) + \mathfrak{C}^{2}\varepsilon + \mathfrak{c}^{2\alpha}t)^{-1}$*, *and*
- *(iv) it holds for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that*

$$
\begin{aligned}
\bigl\lVert \Theta_{t} - \psi \bigr\rVert_{2} & \leq \int_{t}^{\infty}\bigl\lVert \mathscr{F}\bigl(\Theta_{s}\bigr) \bigr\rVert_{2} \, \mathrm{d}s \leq \mathfrak{C}(1-\alpha)^{-1}\bigl[\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) - \mathcal{L}(\psi)\bigr]^{1-\alpha} \\[5pt]
& \leq \mathfrak{C}^{3-2\alpha}\mathfrak{c}^{2-2\alpha}(1-\alpha)^{-1}(\lVert_{[0]}(\mathfrak{c}) + \mathfrak{C}^{2}\varepsilon + \mathfrak{c}^{2\alpha}t)^{\alpha-1}.
\end{aligned} \tag{9.127}
$$

**Proof of Proposition 9.11.2.** Throughout this proof, let $\mathbf{L} : [0, \infty) \to \mathbb{R}$ *satisfy for all* $t \in [0, \infty)$ *that*

$$
\mathbf{L}(t) = \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta), \tag{9.128}
$$

let $\mathbb{B} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{9}$ satisfy

$$
\mathbb{B}=\{\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{9}: \|\vartheta - \vartheta\|_{2} \leq \varepsilon\}, \tag{9.129}
$$

let $T \in [0,\infty]$ satisfy

$$
T=\inf(\{t \in [0,\infty): \Theta_{t} \notin \mathbb{B}\} \cup \{\infty\}), \tag{9.130}
$$

let $\tau \in [0,T]$ satisfy

$$
\tau=\inf(\{t \in [0,T]: \mathbf{L}(t)=0\} \cup \{T\}), \tag{9.131}
$$

let $\pmb{\mathscr{G}} = (\pmb{\mathscr{G}}_{t})_{t \in [0,\infty)}: [0,\infty) \to [0,\infty)$ satisfy for all $t \in [0,\infty)$ that $\pmb{\mathscr{G}}_{t} = \int_{t}^{\infty} \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_{s}) \|_{2} \, \mathrm{d}s$, and let $\mathfrak{D} \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\mathfrak{D} = \mathfrak{C}^{2}\mathfrak{C}^{(2-2\alpha)}$. In the first step of our proof of items **(i)**, **(ii)**, **(iii)**, and **(iv)** we show that for all $t \in [0,\infty)$ it holds that

$$
\Theta_{t} \in \mathbb{B}. \tag{9.132}
$$

For this we observe that **(9.126)**, the triangle inequality, and the assumption that for all $t \in [0,\infty)$ it holds that $\Theta_{t} = \Theta_{0} - \int_{0}^{t} \mathscr{G}(\Theta_{s}) \, \mathrm{d}s$ imply that for all $t \in [0,\infty)$ it holds that

$$
\begin{aligned}
\| \Theta_{t} - \vartheta \|_{2} & \leq \| \Theta_{t} - \Theta_{0} \|_{2} + \| \Theta_{0} - \vartheta \|_{2} \leq \left\| \int_{0}^{t} \mathscr{G}(\Theta_{s}) \, \mathrm{d}s \right\|_{2} + \| \Theta_{0} - \vartheta \|_{2} \\
& \leq \int_{0}^{t} \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_{s}) \|_{2} \, \mathrm{d}s + \| \Theta_{0} - \vartheta \|_{2} < \int_{0}^{t} \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_{s}) \|_{2} \, \mathrm{d}s - \mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{0}) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)^{1 - \alpha} + \varepsilon.
\end{aligned} \tag{9.133}
$$

To establish **(9.132)**, it is thus sufficient to prove that $\int_{0}^{T} \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_{s}) \|_{2} \, \mathrm{d}s \leq \mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{0}) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)^{1 - \alpha}$. We will accomplish this by employing an appropriate differential inequality for a fractional power of the function **L** in **(9.128)** (see **(9.138)** below for details). For this we need several technical preparations. More formally, note that **(9.128)** and the assumption that for all $t \in [0,\infty)$ it holds that

$$
\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) = \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{0}) - \int_{0}^{t} \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_{s}) \|_{2}^{2} \, \mathrm{d}s \tag{9.134}
$$

demonstrate that for almost all $t \in [0,\infty)$ it holds that **L** is differentiable at $t$ and satisfies

$$
\mathbf{L}'(t) = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}(\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t})) = -\| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_{t}) \|_{2}^{2}. \tag{9.135}
$$

Furthermore, observe that the assumption that $\inf_{t \in \{s \in [0,\infty): \; \forall r \in [0,s]: \; \|\Theta_{r} - \vartheta\|_{2} \leq \varepsilon\}} \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) \geq \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)$ ensures that for all $t \in [0,T)$ it holds that

$$
\mathbf{L}(t) \geq 0. \tag{9.136}
$$

Combining this with **(9.126)**, **(9.128)**, and **(9.131)** establishes that for all $t \in [0,\tau)$ it holds that

$$
0 < [\mathbf{L}(t)]^{\alpha} = |\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)|^{\alpha} \leq \mathfrak{C}\|\mathscr{G}(\Theta_{t})\|_{2}. \tag{9.137}
$$

The chain rule and (9.135) hence prove that for almost all $t \in [0, \tau)$ it holds that

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\left( [\mathbf{L}(t)]^{1-\alpha} \right) & = (1-\alpha)[\mathbf{L}(t)]^{1-\alpha}-\alpha \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_t) \|_2^{2} \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\left( [\mathbf{L}(t)]^{1-\alpha} \right) \\
& \leq -(1-\alpha)\mathfrak{C}^{-1}\| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_t) \|_2^{4} \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_t) \|_2^{2} = -\mathfrak{C}^{-1}(1-\alpha)\| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_t)\|_2.
\end{aligned} \tag{9.138}
$$

Moreover, note that (9.134) shows that $[0, \infty) \ni t \mapsto \mathbf{L}(t) \in \mathbb{R}$ is absolutely continuous. This and the fact that for all $r \in (0, \infty)$ it holds that $[r, \infty) \ni y \mapsto y^{1-\alpha} \in \mathbb{R}$ is Lipschitz continuous imply that for all $t \in [0, \tau)$ it holds that $[0, t] \ni s \mapsto \big[\mathbf{L}(s)\big]^{1-\alpha} \in \mathbb{R}$ is absolutely continuous. Combining this with (9.138) demonstrates that for all $s,t \in [0,\tau)$ with $s \leq t$ it holds that

$$
\int_s^t \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_u) \|_2 \, \mathrm{d}u \leq \mathfrak{C}(1-\alpha)^{-1}\big( [\mathbf{L}(t)]^{1-\alpha} - [\mathbf{L}(s)]^{1-\alpha} \big) \leq \mathfrak{C}(1-\alpha)^{-1}[\mathbf{L}(s)]^{1-\alpha}. \tag{9.139}
$$

In the next step we observe that (9.134) ensures that $[0, \infty) \ni t \mapsto \mathcal{L}(\Theta_t) \in \mathbb{R}$ is non-increasing. This and (9.128) establish that $\mathbf{L}$ is non-increasing. Combining (9.131) and (9.136) therefore proves that for all $t \in [\tau, T)$ it holds that $\mathbf{L}(t)=0$. Hence, we obtain that for all $t \in (\tau, T)$ it holds that

$$
\mathbf{L}^{\prime}(t) = 0. \tag{9.140}
$$

This and (9.135) show that for almost all $t \in (\tau, T)$ it holds that

$$
\mathscr{G}(\Theta_t) = 0. \tag{9.141}
$$

Combining this with (9.139) implies that for all $s,t \in [0, T)$ with $s \leq t$ it holds that

$$
\int_s^t \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_u) \|_2 \, \mathrm{d}u \leq \mathfrak{C}(1-\alpha)^{-1}[\mathbf{L}(s)]^{1-\alpha}. \tag{9.142}
$$

Therefore, we obtain that for all $t \in [0, T)$ it holds that

$$
\int_0^t \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_u) \|_2 \, \mathrm{d}u \leq \mathfrak{C}(1-\alpha)^{-1}[\mathbf{L}(0)]^{1-\alpha}. \tag{9.143}
$$

In addition, note that (9.126) demonstrates that $\Theta_0 \in \mathbb{B}$. Combining this with (9.130) ensures that $T > 0$. This, (9.143), and (9.126) establish that

$$
\int_0^T \| \mathscr{G}(\Theta_u) \|_2 \, \mathrm{d}u \leq \mathfrak{C}(1-\alpha)^{-1}[\mathbf{L}(0)]^{1-\alpha} < \varepsilon < \infty. \tag{9.144}
$$

Combining (9.130) and (9.133) hence proves that

$$
T = \infty. \tag{9.145}
$$
