**Lemma 6.1.22.** Let $\mathfrak{d} \in \mathbb{N}$, $c \in (0, \infty)$, $r \in (0, \infty)$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$, $\mathbb{B} = \{w \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}: \|w - \vartheta\|_2 \leq r\}$, $\mathscr{L} \in C^1(\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}, \mathbb{R})$ satisfy for all $v \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$, $s, t \in [0, 1]$ with $\|v\|_2 \leq r$ and $s \leq t$ that

$$\mathscr{L}(\vartheta + tv) - \mathscr{L}(\vartheta + sv) \geq \mathscr{L}(c^2 - s^2)\|v\|_2^2 \tag{6.150}$$

(cf. *Definition 3.3.4*). Then it holds for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ that

$$(\theta - \vartheta, (\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta)) \geq 2c\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2^2 \tag{6.151}$$

(cf. *Definition 1.4.7*).

*Proof of Lemma 6.1.22.* Observe that (6.150) ensures that for all $s \in (0, r] \cap \mathbb{R}$, $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}} \backslash \{\vartheta\}$ with $\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 < s$ it holds that

$\begin{aligned} &(\theta - \vartheta, (\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta)) = \mathscr{L}'(\theta)(\theta - \vartheta) = \underset{h \searrow 0}{\text{lim}} \big[\tfrac{1}{h}[\mathscr{L}(\theta + h(\theta - \vartheta)) - \mathscr{L}(\theta)]\big] \\ &= \underset{h \searrow 0}{\text{lim}} \big(\tfrac{1}{h}\big[\mathscr{L}\big(\vartheta + \frac{\|\theta + h\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2}{s}(\theta - \vartheta)\big) - \big. \\ &\quad - \mathscr{L}\big(\vartheta + \frac{\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2}{s}\big(\frac{s}{\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2}(\theta - \vartheta)\big)\big)\big]\big) \\ &\geq \underset{h \searrow 0}{\text{lim sup}} \big(\tfrac{c}{h}\big[\big(\frac{\|\theta + h\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2}{s}\big)^2 - \big[\tfrac{\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2}{s}\big]^2\big]\big\|\tfrac{s}{\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2}(\theta - \vartheta)\big\|_2^2\big) \tag{6.152} \\ &= c\big[\underset{h \searrow 0}{\text{lim sup}}\big(\frac{\|\theta + h\|^2 - 1}{h}\big)\big]\big[\big\|\frac{\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2}{s}\big\|^2\big]\big\|\tfrac{s}{\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2}(\theta - \vartheta)\big\|_2^2 \\ &= c\big[\underset{h \searrow 0}{\text{lim sup}}\big(\frac{2h + h^2}{h}\big)\big]\big\|\theta - \vartheta\big\|_2^2 \\ &= c\big[\underset{h \searrow 0}{\text{lim sup}}(2 + h)\big]\big\|\theta - \vartheta\big\|_2^2 = 2c\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2^2\end{aligned}$

(cf. *Definition 1.4.7*). Hence, we obtain that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}} \backslash \{\vartheta\}$ with $\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 < r$ it holds that

$$(\theta - \vartheta, (\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta)) \geq 2c\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2^2. \tag{6.153}$$

Combining this with the fact that the function

$$\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}} \ni v \mapsto (\nabla \mathscr{L})(v) \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}} \tag{6.154}$$

is continuous establishes (6.151). The proof of *Lemma 6.1.22* is thus complete. $\Box$

**Lemma 6.1.23.** Let $\mathfrak{d} \in \mathbb{N}$, $L \in (0, \infty)$, $r \in (0, \infty)$, $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$, $\mathbb{B} = \{w \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}: \|w - \vartheta\|_2 \leq r\}$, $\mathscr{L} \in C^1(\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}, \mathbb{R})$ satisfy for all $\theta \in \mathbb{B}$ that

$$\|(\nabla \mathscr{L})(\theta)\|_2 \leq L\|\theta - \vartheta\|_2 \tag{6.155}$$

(cf. *Definition 3.3.4*). Then it holds for all $v, w \in \mathbb{B}$ that

$$|\mathscr{L}(v) - \mathscr{L}(w)| \leq L\max\{\|v - \vartheta\|_2, \|w - \vartheta\|_2\}\|v - w\|_2. \tag{6.156}$$
