This establishes that for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}^p$, $\varepsilon \in (0, \infty)$ and all $z = (z_1, z_2): \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}^p \times \mathbb{R}^p$ with $\lim \sup_{k \to \infty} (\|z_1(k) - x\|_2 + \|z_2(k) - y\|_2) = 0$ and $\forall k \in \mathbb{N}: z_2(k) \in (\mathcal{D}\mathcal{L})(z_1(k))$ it holds that

$y \in \overline{\bigcup_{v \in \{w \in \mathbb{R}^n : \|x - w\|_2 < \varepsilon\}} (\mathcal{D}\mathcal{L})(v)}.$ (9.297)

This and (9.284) show that for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}^p$ and all $z = (z_1, z_2): \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{R}^p \times \mathbb{R}^p$ with $\lim \sup_{k \to \infty} (\|z_1(k) - x\|_2 + \|z_2(k) - y\|_2) = 0$ and $\forall k \in \mathbb{N}: z_2(k) \in (\mathcal{D}\mathcal{L})(z_1(k))$ it holds that

$y \in (\mathcal{D}\mathcal{L})(x),$ (9.298)

Combining this with (9.293) proves item (i). Note that (9.289) implies that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^p$, $y \in (\mathcal{D}\mathcal{L})(x)$ it holds that

$\left[ \forall k \in \mathbb{N}: \left(Z_2^x,y(k) \in (\mathcal{D}\mathcal{L})(Z_1^{x,y}(k)) \right) \right] \wedge \left[ \lim \sup_{k \to \infty} \left(\|Z_1^{x,y}(k) - x\|_2 + \|Z_2^{x,y}(k) - y\|_2 \right) = 0 \right]$ (9.299)

(cf. Definitions 3.3.4 and 9.15.1). Combining this with item (i) establishes item (ii). Observe that the fact that for all $a \in \mathbb{R}$ it holds that $-a \leq |a|$ demonstrates that for all $x \in \{y \in \mathbb{R}^p : \mathcal{L} \text{ is differentiable at } y\}$ it holds that

$\lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\langle \mathcal{L}(x + h) - \mathcal{L}(x) - \langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x), h \rangle}{\|h\|_2} \geq \left| -\lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\langle \mathcal{L}(x + h) - \mathcal{L}(x) - \langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x), h \rangle}{\|h\|_2} \right|$ (9.300)

$\geq \left[ -\lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\left| \mathcal{L}(x + h) - \mathcal{L}(x) - \langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x), h \rangle \right|}{\|h\|_2} \right] = 0$

(cf. Definition 1.4.7). This demonstrates that for all $x \in \{y \in \mathbb{R}^p : \mathcal{L} \text{ is differentiable at } y\}$ it holds that

$(\nabla \mathcal{L})(x) \in (\mathcal{D}\mathcal{L})(x).$ (9.301)

Moreover, note that for all $v \in \mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\}$ it holds that

$\lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\langle v, h \rangle}{\|h\|_2} = \sup_{\varepsilon \in (0, \infty)} \inf_{h \in \{w \in \mathbb{R}^p : \|w\|_2 \leq \varepsilon\}} \frac{\langle v, h \rangle}{\|h\|_2}$

$\leq \sup_{\varepsilon \in (0, \infty)} \frac{\langle v, -\varepsilon \|v\| \frac{v}{\|v\|} \rangle}{\left| -\varepsilon \|v\| \frac{v}{\|v\|} \right|_2} = \sup_{\varepsilon \in (0, \infty)} \langle v, -\varepsilon \|v\|^{-1}_2 v \rangle = -\|v\|_2 < 0.$ (9.302)

Hence, we obtain for all $x \in \{y \in \mathbb{R}^p : \mathcal{L} \text{ is differentiable at } y\}$, $w \in (\mathcal{D}\mathcal{L})(x)$ that

$0 \leq \lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\left( \mathcal{L}(x + h) - \mathcal{L}(x) - \langle w, h \rangle \right)}{\|h\|_2}$

$= \lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\left( \mathcal{L}(x + h) - \mathcal{L}(x) - \langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x), h \rangle - \langle w - (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x), h \rangle \right)}{\|h\|_2}$

$\leq \lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\left| \mathcal{L}(x + h) - \mathcal{L}(x) - \langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x), h \rangle + \langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x) - w, h \rangle \right|}{\|h\|_2}$

$\leq \left[ \lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x) - w, h \rangle}{\|h\|_2} \right] + \left[ \lim \sup_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\left| \mathcal{L}(x + h) - \mathcal{L}(x) - \langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x), h \rangle \right|}{\|h\|_2} \right]$

$= \lim \inf_{\substack{\mathbb{R}^p \setminus \{0\} \ni h \to 0}} \frac{\langle (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x) - w, h \rangle}{\|h\|_2} \leq -\| (\nabla \mathcal{L})(x) - w \|_2.$ (9.303)
