**Proposition 2.2.3** (Realizations of parallelizations of fully-connected feedforward $ANNs$). *Let $a \in C(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R})$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Phi = (\Phi_1, \dots, \Phi_n) \in \mathbf{N}^n$ satisfy $\mathcal{L}(\Phi_1) = \mathcal{L}(\Phi_2) = \dots = \mathcal{L}(\Phi_n)$ (cf. Definition 1.3.1). Then*

+   * (i) it holds that

$$\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbf{N}}(\mathbf{P}_{n}(\Phi)) \in C(\mathbb{R}[\sum_{j=1}^n \mathcal{I}(\Phi_j)], \mathbb{R}[\sum_{j=1}^n \mathcal{O}(\Phi_j)]) \tag{2.49}$$

$$\begin{align*} &\left(\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbf{N}}(\mathbf{P}_{n}(\Phi))\right)(x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n) \\\ &= \left((\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbf{N}}(\Phi_1))(x_1), (\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbf{N}}(\Phi_2))(x_2), \dots, (\mathcal{R}_{a}^{\mathbf{N}}(\Phi_n))(x_n)\right) \in \mathbb{R}[\sum_{j=1}^n \mathcal{O}(\Phi_j)] \end{align*} \tag{2.50}$$
