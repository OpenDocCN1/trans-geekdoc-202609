This book grew out of a series of lectures held by the authors at ETH Zurich, University of Münster, and the Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. We thank all of our aforementioned co-authors for very fruitful collaborations. Special thanks are due to Timo Welti for his permission to integrate slightly modified extracts of the article [230] into this book. We also thank Lukas Gonon, Timo Kräger, Siyu Liang, and Joshua Lee Padget for several insightful discussions and useful suggestions.
