Item $(I I I)$ hence establishes that for all $t \in(0, T], x \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$ it holds that

$\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\right)(t, x)=\frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Trace}\left(B B^{*}\left(\operatorname{Hess}_{x} u\right)(t, x)\right)$.    (17.28)

The fundamental theorem of calculus therefore proves that for all $t, s \in(0, T], x \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$ it holds that

$u(t, x)-u(s, x)=\int_{s}^{t}\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\right)(r, x) \mathrm{d} r=\int_{s}^{t} \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Trace}\left(B B^{*}\left(\operatorname{Hess}_{x} u\right)(r, x)\right) \mathrm{d} r$.    (17.29)

The fact that $[0, T] \times \mathbb{R}^{d} \ni(t, x) \mapsto\left(\operatorname{Hess}_{x} u\right)(t, x) \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times d}$ is continuous hence implies for all $t \in(0, T], x \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$ that

$\begin{aligned} \frac{u(t, x)-u(0, x)}{t} & =\underset{s \searrow 0}{\lim}\left[\frac{u(t, x)-u(s, x)}{t}\right]=\frac{1}{t} \int_{0}^{t} \frac{1}{2} \operatorname{Trace}\left(B B^{*}\left(\operatorname{Hess}_{x} u\right)(r, x)\right) \mathrm{d} r.\end{aligned}$   (17.30)
