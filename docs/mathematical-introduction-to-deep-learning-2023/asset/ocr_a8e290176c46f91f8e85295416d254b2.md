In this section we introduce the stochastic version of the Nesterov accelerated GD optimization method from Section 6.4 (cf. [302, 387]).

# **Definition 7.5.1** (Nesterov accelerated SGD optimization method). Let \( \mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{N} \), \( (\gamma_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq [0, \infty) \), \( (J_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq \mathbb{N} \), \( (\alpha_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq [0, 1] \), let \( (\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P}) \) be a probability space, let \( (S, \mathcal{S}) \) be a measurable space, let \( \xi : \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{d} \) be a random variable, for every \( n \in \mathbb{N} \), \( j \in \{1, 2, \ldots, J_{n}\} \) let \( X_{n, j} : \Omega \rightarrow S \) be a random variable, and let \( \ell = (\ell(\theta, x))_{(\theta, x) \in \mathbb{R}^{d} \times S} : \mathbb{R}^{d} \times S \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \) and \( \boldsymbol{g} = (\boldsymbol{g}_{1}, \ldots, \boldsymbol{g}_{d}) : \mathbb{R}^{d} \times S \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{d} \) satisfy for all \( U \in \{V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{d} : V \text{ is open}\} \), \( x \in S \), \( \theta \in U \) with \( (U \ni \psi \mapsto \ell(\psi, x) \in \mathbb{R}) \in C^{1}(U, \mathbb{R}) \) that

\(\begin{gathered}\boldsymbol{g}(\theta, x)=(\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, x).\end{gathered}\tag{7.116}\)

Then we say that \( \Theta \) is the Nesterov accelerated SGD process on \( (\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P}), (S, \mathcal{S}) \) for the loss function \( \ell \) with generalized gradient \( \boldsymbol{g} \), learning rates \( (\gamma_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \), batch sizes \( (J_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \), momentum decay factors \( (\alpha_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \), initial value \( \xi \), and data \( (X_{n, j})_{(n, j) \in \{(k, \ell) \in \mathbb{N}^{2} : \ell \leq J_{k}\}} \) if and only if \( \Theta : \mathbb{N}_{0} \times \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{d} \) is the function from \( \mathbb{N}_{0} \times \Omega \) to \( \mathbb{R}^{d} \) that satisfies that there exists \( \mathbf{m} : \mathbb{N}_{0} \times \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{d} \) such that for all \( n \in \mathbb{N} \) it holds that

\(\begin{gathered}\Theta_{0}=\xi,\quad\mathbf{m}_{0}=0,\\\mathbf{m}_{n}=\alpha_{n} \mathbf{m}_{n-1}+\left(1-\alpha_{n}\right)\left[\frac{1}{J_{n}} \sum_{j=1}^{J_{n}} \boldsymbol{g}\left(\Theta_{n-1}-\gamma_{n} \alpha_{n} \mathbf{m}_{n-1}, X_{n, j}\right)\right],\\\text { and }\quad\Theta_{n}=\Theta_{n-1}-\gamma_{n} \mathbf{m}_{n} .\end{gathered}\tag{7.117}\tag{7.118}\tag{7.119}\)
