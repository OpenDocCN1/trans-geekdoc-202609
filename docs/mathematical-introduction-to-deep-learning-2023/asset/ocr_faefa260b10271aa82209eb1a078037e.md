# ## 9.13 On the analyticity of realization functions of ANNs

Proposition 9.13.1 (Compositions of analytic functions). Let $l, m, n \in \mathbb{N}$, let $U \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{l}$ and $V \subseteq \mathbb{R}^{m}$ be open, let $f: U \to \mathbb{R}^{m}$ and $g: V \to \mathbb{R}^{n}$ be analytic, and assume $f(U) \subseteq V$ (cf. Definition 9.7.1). Then

$$U \ni u \mapsto g(f(u)) \in \mathbb{R}^{n} \tag{9.260}$$
is analytic.

Proof of Proposition 9.13.1. Observe that Faà di Bruno’s formula (cf., for instance, Fraenkel [134]) establishes that $f \circ g$ is analytic (cf. also, for example, Krantz & Parks [254, Proposition 2.8]). The proof of Proposition 9.13.1 is thus complete. $\square$

Lemma 9.13.2. Let $\mathbf{0}_{1}, \mathbf{0}_{2}, l_{1}, l_{2} \in \mathbb{N}$, for every $k \in \{1, 2\}$ let $F_{k}: \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{0}_{k}} \to \mathbb{R}^{l_{k}}$ be analytic, and let $f: \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{0}_{1}} \times \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{0}_{2}} \to \mathbb{R}^{l_{1}} \times \mathbb{R}^{l_{2}}$ satisfy for all $x_{1} \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{0}_{1}}$, $x_{2} \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{0}_{2}}$ that

$$f(x_{1}, x_{2}) = (F_{1}(x_{1}), F_{2}(x_{2})) \tag{9.261}$$
(cf. Definition 9.7.1). Then $f$ is analytic.
