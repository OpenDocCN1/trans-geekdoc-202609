This and (11.38) ensure that for all $k \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}$ with $e_k \notin \bigcup_{i \in \mathbb{N} \setminus \{0, k\}} \{e_i\}$ it holds that

$P^{-1}(\{e_k\}) = \left\{x \in E: \min \left\{l \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}: d(x, e_l) = \min_{u \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}} d(x, e_u) \right\} = k\right\}.$

Combining (11.40) with the fact that $D: E \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^n$ is $\mathcal{B}(E)/\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^n)$-measurable hence establishes that for all $k \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}$ with $e_k \notin \bigcup_{i \in \mathbb{N} \setminus \{0, k\}} \{e_i\}$ it holds that

$P^{-1}(\{e_k\})$\n$= \left\{x \in E: \min \left\{l \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}: d(x, e_l) = \min_{u \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}} d(x, e_u) \right\} = k\right\}$\n$= \left\{x \in E: \min \left\{l \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}: D_l(x) = \min_{u \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}} D_u(x) \right\} = k\right\}$\n$= \left\{x \in E: \begin{array}{c} \forall l \in \mathbb{N} \cap \{0, k\}: D_k(x) < D_l(x) \text{ and} \\ \forall l \in \{1, 2, \dots, n\}: D_k(x) \leq D_l(x) \end{array} \right\}$\n$= \left[ \underset{\substack{l=1 \\ \cup \mathcal{B}(E)}}{\bigcap_{l=1}^{k-1}} \left\{x \in E: D_k(x) < D_l(x)\right\} \right] \cap \left[ \underset{\substack{l=1 \\ \cup \mathcal{B}(E)}}{\bigcap_{l=1}^{n}} \left\{x \in E: D_k(x) \leq D_l(x)\right\} \right] \in \mathcal{B}(E).$

Therefore, we obtain that for all $f \in \{e_1, e_2, \dots, e_n\}$ it holds that

$P^{-1}(\{f\}) \in \mathcal{B}(E).$

Hence, we obtain that for all $A \subseteq E$ it holds that

$P^{-1}(A) = P^{-1}(A \cap \{e_1, e_2, \dots, e_n\}) = \bigcup_{f \in A \cap \{e_1, e_2, \dots, e_n\}} P^{-1}(\{f\}) \in \mathcal{B}(E).$

This proves item (ii). The proof of Lemma 11.2.5 is thus complete.
