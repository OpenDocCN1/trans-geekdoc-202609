*Proof of Corollary 13.1.6*. Note that Proposition 13.1.5 and Lemma 13.1.4 show that

```
$$ \begin{split}
\begin{align*}
\bigg(\mathbb E \bigg[\bigg\| \frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^M X_j \bigg\| - \mathbb E \bigg[ \frac{1}{M}\sum_{j=1}^M X_j \bigg] \bigg\|_2^p \bigg)^{1/p} 
\leq \frac{2\delta_p}{\sqrt M}\bigg[ \frac{1}{M} \max_{j\in\{1,2,...,M\}} \big(\mathbb E \big[\|X_j\| - \mathbb E[X_j]\|_2^2\big]^{2/p} \big)\bigg]^{1/2} 
\leq \frac{2\delta_p\sqrt{p-1}}{\sqrt M}\bigg[ \frac{1}{M} \max_{j\in\{1,2,...,M\}} \big(\mathbb E \big[\|X_j\| - \mathbb E[X_j]\|_2^2\big]^{2/p} \big)\bigg]^{1/2}.
\end{align*}
$$
```

(cf. Definition 13.1.3). The proof of Corollary 13.1.6 is thus complete.

# **13.2 Uniform strong error estimates for random fields**

+   Lemma 13.2.1. *Let* $(E, \delta)$ *be a separable metric space, let* $N \in \mathbb N$, $r_1, r_2, \ldots, r_N \in [0, \infty)$, $z_1, z_2, \ldots, z_N \in E$ *satisfy*

```
$$E \subseteq \bigcup_{n=1}^N \{ x \in E: \delta(x, z_n) \leq r_n\}, \tag{13.12}$$
```

+   *let* $(\Omega, \mathcal F, \mathbb P)$ *be a probability space, for every* $x \in E$ *let* $Z_x \colon \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb R$ *be a random variable, let* $L \in [0, \infty)$ *satisfy for all* $x, y \in E$ *that* $|Z_x - Z_y| \leq L \delta(x, y)$, *and let* $p \in [0, \infty)$. *Then*

```
$$ \mathbb E\big[\sup_{x\in E}|Z_x|^p\big] \leq \sum_{n=1}^N \mathbb E\big[(Lr_n + |Z_{z_n}|)^p\big] \tag{13.13}$$
```

(cf. Lemma 12.3.2).

*Proof of Lemma 13.2.1*. Throughout this proof, for every $n \in \{1, 2, \ldots, N\}$ let

```

```

Observe that (13.12) and (13.14) prove that

```
$$E \subseteq \bigcup_{n=1}^N B_n \quad \text{and} \quad E \supseteq \bigcup_{n=1}^N B_n. \tag{13.15}$$
```
