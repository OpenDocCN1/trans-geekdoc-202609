# Lemma 4.3.3. Let $(E,\delta)$ be a metric space and let $r \in [0, \infty]$. Then

$$\mathcal{C}_{(E,\delta), r} = \begin{cases} \emptyset & : X = \emptyset \\ \inf \left( \left\{ n \in \mathbb{N} : \left( \exists x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n \in E : \right. \right. \right. \left. \left. E \subseteq \bigcup_{m=1}^n \{ v \in E : d(x_m, v) \leq r \} \right) \right\} \cup \{ \infty \} \right) & : X \neq \emptyset \end{cases} \tag{4.83}$$

(cf. Definition 4.3.2).

Proof of Lemma 4.3.3. Throughout this proof, assume without loss of generality that $E \neq \emptyset$. Observe that Lemma 12.2.4 establishes (4.83). The proof of Lemma 4.3.3 is thus complete. $\Box$

# Lemma 4.3.4. Definition 3.3.4). Then it holds for all $p \in [1, \infty)$ that

$$\mathcal{C}_{([a,b]^d,\delta_p),r} \leq \left( \frac{d^{1/p}(b-a)}{2r} \right)^d \leq \begin{cases} 1 & : r \geq \frac{d^{(b-a)/2}}{r} \\ \left( \frac{d^{(b-a)}}{r} \right)^d & : r < \frac{d^{(b-a)/2}}{r}. \end{cases} \tag{4.84}$$

(cf. Definitions 4.2.6 and 4.3.2).
