and let $\Theta: \mathbb{N}_0 \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ be the stochastic process which satisfies for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ that

$$\Theta_0 = \xi \quad \quad \text{ and } \quad \quad \Theta_n = \Theta_{n-1} - \frac{\gamma}{n^\nu} (\nabla_{\vartheta} \mathcal{L}) (\Theta_{n-1}, X_n). \tag{7.98}$$

*Then*

+   (i) *there exists a unique* $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ *which satisfies that* $\{\theta \in \mathbb{R}^d: \mathcal{L}(\theta) = \inf_{w \in \mathbb{R}^d} \mathcal{L}(w)\} = \{\vartheta\}$,

+   (ii) *for every* $\varepsilon \in (0, \infty)$ *there exist* $c_0, c_1 \in (0, \infty)$ *such that for all* $n \in \mathbb{N}$ *it holds that*

$$c_0 n^{-r_{\varepsilon,0}} \le \big( \mathbb{E} \big[ \| \Theta_n - \vartheta \|_2^2 \big] \big)^{1/2} \le c_1 n^{-r_{\varepsilon,1}}, \tag{7.99}$$

*and*

+   (iii) *for every* $\varepsilon \in (0, \infty)$ *there exist* $e_0, e_1 \in (0, \infty)$ *such that for all* $n \in \mathbb{N}$ *it holds that*

$$e_0 n^{-2r_{\varepsilon,0}} \le \mathbb{E} [ \mathcal{L} (\Theta_n) ] - \mathcal{L} (\vartheta) \le e_1 n^{-2r_{\varepsilon,1}}. \tag{7.100}$$

*Proof of Theorem 7.2.15. Note that Jentzen et al. [229, Theorem 1.1] establishes items* (i), *(ii), and* (iii). *The proof of Theorem 7.2.15 is thus complete.* \hfill $\square$
