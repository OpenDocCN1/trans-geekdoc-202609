Proof of Lemma 6.1.23. Observe that (6.155), the fundamental theorem of calculus, and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality assure that for all \(v, w \in \mathbb{B}\) it holds that

```
\begin{align*} \left|\mathscr{L}(v)-\mathscr{L}(w)\right| &= \left|[\mathscr{L}(w + h(v - w))]_{h=0}^{h=1}\right| \\[4pt] &= \left|\int_0^1 \mathscr{L}'(w + h(v - w))\langle v - w \rangle \,\mathrm{d}h\right| \\[4pt] &= \left|\int_0^1 \langle \langle\nabla \mathscr{L}\rangle (w + h(v - w)), v - w \rangle \,\mathrm{d}h\right| \\[4pt] &\le \int_0^1 \|\langle\nabla \mathscr{L}\rangle (hv + (1 - h)w)\|_2\|v - w\|_2 \,\mathrm{d}h \\[4pt] &\le \int_0^1 L\|hv + (1 - h)w - \vartheta\|_2\|v - w\|_2 \,\mathrm{d}h \tag{6.157}\\[4pt] &\le \int_0^1 L(h\|v - \vartheta\|_2 + (1 - h)\|w - \vartheta\|_2)\|v - w\|_2 \,\mathrm{d}h \\[4pt] &= L\|v - w\|_2 \left[\int_0^1 (h\|v - \vartheta\|_2 + h\|w - \vartheta\|_2) \,\mathrm{d}h\right] \\[4pt] &= L\big(\|v - \vartheta\|_2 + \|w - \vartheta\|_2\big)\|v - w\|_2\left[\int_0^1 h \,\mathrm{d}h\right] \\[4pt] &\le L\max\{\|v - \vartheta\|_2, \|w - \vartheta\|_2\}\|v - w\|_2 \end{align*}
```

(cf. Definition 1.4.7). The proof of Lemma 6.1.23 is thus complete.  \square

