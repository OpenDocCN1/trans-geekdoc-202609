
*Proof of Lemma 9.4.1.* Note that (9.31) and the triangle inequality ensure that for all $\vartheta \in U$ it holds that

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
&\| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) \|_2 \\
&= c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) + [ (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) - (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) ] \|_2 \\
&\leq c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2 + c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) - (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2 \leq c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2 + \frac{c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) \|_2}{2}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}

Hence, we obtain for all $\vartheta \in U$ that

\begin{equation*}
\frac{c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) \|_2}{2} \leq c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2.
\end{equation*}

Combining this with (9.31) establishes that for all $\vartheta \in U$ it holds that

\begin{equation*}
| \mathscr{L} (\theta) - \mathscr{L} (v) |^{\alpha} \leq \frac{c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) \|_2}{2} \leq c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2.
\end{equation*}

The proof of Lemma 9.4.1 is thus complete. $\square$

*Corollary 9.4.2* (Standard KL inequality around non-critical points). *Let* $\mathbf{d} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mathscr{L} \in C^1(\mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{d}}, \mathbb{R})$, $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{d}}$, $c, \alpha \in (0, \infty)$ *satisfy* $(\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) \neq 0$. *Then there exists* $\varepsilon \in (0,1)$ *such that for all* $\vartheta \in \{ v \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{d}}: \| v - \theta \|_2 < \varepsilon \}$ *it holds that* $| \mathscr{L} (\theta) - \mathscr{L} (v) |^{\alpha} \leq c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2$ $(cf.$ *Definition 3.3.4$)$.

*Proof of Corollary 9.4.2.* *Observe that the assumption that* $\mathscr{L} \in C^1(\mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{d}}, \mathbb{R})$ *ensures that* $\lim \sup_{\varepsilon \searrow 0} (\sup_{\vartheta \in \{ v \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{d}}: \| v - \theta \|_2 < \varepsilon \}} \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) - (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2) = 0$ $(cf.$ *Definition 3.3.4$). *Combining this and the fact that* $c > 0$ *with the fact that* $\mathscr{L}$ *is continuous demonstrates that* $\lim \sup_{\varepsilon \searrow 0} (\sup_{\vartheta \in \{ v \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{d}}: \| v - \theta \|_2 < \varepsilon \}} \max \{ | \mathscr{L} (\theta) - \mathscr{L} (v) |^{\alpha}, c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) - (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2 \}) = 0$. *The fact that* $c > 0$ *and the fact that* $\| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) \|_2 > 0$ *therefore prove that there exists* $\varepsilon \in (0,1)$ *which satisfies* $\sup_{\vartheta \in \{ v \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{d}}: \| v - \theta \|_2 < \varepsilon \}} \max \{ | \mathscr{L} (\theta) - \mathscr{L} (v) |^{\alpha}, c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) - (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2 \} < \frac{c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) \|_2}{2}$ *Note that (9.39) ensures that for all* $\vartheta \in \{ v \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbf{d}}: \| v - \theta \|_2 < \varepsilon \}$ *it holds that* $\max \{ | \mathscr{L} (\theta) - \mathscr{L} (v) |^{\alpha}, c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) - (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (v) \|_2 \} \leq \frac{c \| (\nabla \mathscr{L}) (\theta) \|_2}{2}$. *This and Lemma 9.4.1 establish (9.36). The proof of Corollary 9.4.2 is thus complete.* $\square$

## 9.5 Standard KL inequalities with increased exponents

**Lemma 9.5.1** (Standard **KL** inequalities with increased exponents). Let $\mathfrak{d}\in\mathbb{N}$, let $U\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ be a set, let $\vartheta\in U$, $\mathfrak{c},\alpha\in(0,\infty)$, let $\mathcal{L}\colon U\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ and $\mathcal{G}\colon U\rightarrow\mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $\vartheta\in U$ that

$$|\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\alpha} \leq \mathfrak{c}|\mathcal{G}(\vartheta')|, \tag{9.41}$$

and let $\beta\in(\alpha,\infty)$, $\mathfrak{C}\in\mathbb{R}$ satisfy $\mathfrak{C}=\mathfrak{c}\big(\sup_{\vartheta\in U}|\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\beta-\alpha}\big)$. Then it holds for all $\vartheta\in U$ that

$$|\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\beta} \leq \mathfrak{C}|\mathcal{G}(\vartheta')|. \tag{9.42}$$

*Proof of Lemma 9.5.1.* Observe that (9.41) shows that for all $\vartheta\in U$ it holds that

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
	|\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\beta}
	&= |\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\alpha} |
	\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\beta-\alpha}
	\leq \mathfrak{c}|\mathcal{G}(\vartheta')| |
	\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\beta-\alpha} \\
	&= \left(\mathfrak{c}|\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\beta-\alpha}\right)
	|\mathcal{G}(\vartheta')| \leq \mathfrak{C}|\mathcal{G}(\vartheta')|. 
\end{aligned} \tag{9.43}
\end{equation*}

This establishes (9.42). The proof of Lemma 9.5.1 is thus complete. $\square$

**Corollary 9.5.2** (Standard **KL** inequalities with increased exponents). Let $\mathfrak{d}\in\mathbb{N}$, $\mathcal{L}\in C^1(\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}},\mathbb{R})$, $\vartheta\in\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$, $\varepsilon,\mathfrak{c},\alpha\in(0,\infty)$, $\beta\in[\alpha,\infty)$ satisfy for all $\vartheta'\in\{v\in\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}:\|v-\vartheta\|_2<\varepsilon\}$ that

$$|\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\alpha} \leq \mathfrak{c}\|\nabla\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')\|_2 \tag{9.44}$$

(*cf. Definition 3.3.4*). Then there exists $\mathfrak{C}\in(0,\infty)$ such that for all $\vartheta'\in\{v\in\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}:\|v-\vartheta\|_2<\varepsilon\}$ it holds that

$$|\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')|^{\beta} \leq \mathfrak{C}\|\nabla\mathcal{L}(\vartheta')\|_2. \tag{9.45}$$

*Proof of Corollary 9.5.2.* Note that Lemma 9.5.1 establishes (9.45). The proof of Corollary 9.5.2 is thus complete. $\square$

## 9.6 Standard KL inequalities for one-dimensional polynomials

**Corollary 9.6.1** (Reparametrization). Let $\xi\in\mathbb{R}$, $N\in\mathbb{N}$, $p\in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R},\mathbb{R})$ satisfy for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$ that $p^{(N+1)}(x)=0$ and let $\beta_0,\beta_1,\ldots,\beta_N\in\mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $n\in\{0,1,\ldots,N\}$ that $\beta_n=\frac{p^{(n)}(\xi)}{n!}$. Then it holds for all $x\in\mathbb{R}$ that

$$p(x) = \sum_{n=0}^N \beta_n (x-\xi)^n. \tag{9.46}$$

*Proof of Corollary 9.6.1.* Observe that Theorem 6.1.3 establishes (9.46). The proof of Corollary 9.6.1 is thus complete. $\square$

**Corollary 9.6.2** (Quantitative standard **KL** inequalities for non-constant one-dimensional polynomials). *Let* $\xi \in \mathbb{R}$, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R})$ *satisfy for all* $x \in \mathbb{R}$ *that* $p^{(N+1)}(x) = 0$, *let* $\beta_0, \beta_1, \ldots, \beta_N \in \mathbb{R}$ *satisfy for all* $n \in \{0, 1, \ldots, N\}$ *that* $\beta_n = \frac{p^{(n)}(\xi)}{n!}$, *and let* $m \in \{1, 2, \ldots, N\}$, $\alpha \in [0, 1]$, $c, \varepsilon \in \mathbb{R}$ *satisfy*

$$|\beta_m| > 0 = \sum_{n=1}^{m-1}|\beta_n|, \qquad \alpha \geq 1-m^{-1}, \qquad c = 2\Big[\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{|\beta_n|^{\alpha}}{|\beta_m m|}\Big], \qquad (9.47)$$

*and* $\varepsilon = \frac{1}{2}[\sum_{n=1}^{N}\frac{|\beta_n|^m|}{|\beta_m m|}]^{-1}$. *Then it holds for all* $x \in [\xi - \varepsilon, \xi + \varepsilon]$ *that*

$$|p(x) - p(\xi)|^{\alpha} \leq c |p'(x)|. \qquad (9.48)$$

*Proof of Corollary* **9.6.2**. *Note that Corollary* **9.6.1** *ensures that for all* $x \in \mathbb{R}$ *it holds that*

$$p(x) - p(\xi) = \sum_{n=1}^{N}\beta_n(x-\xi)^n. \qquad (9.49)$$

Hence, we obtain for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ that

$$p'(x) = \sum_{n=1}^{N}\beta_n n (x-\xi)^{n-1} \qquad (9.50)$$

Therefore, we obtain for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ that

$$p(x)-p(\xi) = \sum_{n=m}^{N}\beta_n(x-\xi)^n \quad \text{ and } \quad p'(x) = \sum_{n=m}^{N}\beta_n n (x-\xi)^{n-1}. \quad (9.51)$$

Hence, we obtain for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ that

$$|p(x)-p(\xi)|^{\alpha} \leq \sum_{n=m}^{N}\big(|\beta_n|^{\alpha}|x-\xi|^{n\alpha}\big). \qquad (9.52)$$

The fact that for all $n \in \{m, m+1, \ldots, N\}$, $x \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|x - \xi| \leq 1$ it holds that $|x - \xi|^{n\alpha} \leq |x - \xi|^{n(1-m^{-1})} \leq |x - \xi|^{\alpha(1-m^{-1})} = |x - \xi|^{m-1}$ therefore implies that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|x - \xi| \leq 1$ it holds that

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned} 
|p(x) - p(\xi)|^{\alpha} &\leq \sum_{n=m}^{N}|\beta_n|^{\alpha}|x-\xi|^{n\alpha} \leq \sum_{n=m}^{N}|\beta_n|^{\alpha}|x-\xi|^{m-1} \\ 
&= |x-\xi|^{m-1}\Big[\sum_{n=m}^{N}|\beta_n|^{\alpha}\Big] = |x-\xi|^{m-1}\Big[\sum_{n=1}^{N}|\beta_n|^{\alpha}\Big]. 
\end{aligned} \quad (9.53)
\end{equation*}

Hence, we obtain for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|x - \xi| \leq 1$ that

$$|p(x) - p(\xi)|^{\alpha} \leq |x - \xi|^{m-1}\Big[\sum_{n=1}^{N}|\beta_n|^{\alpha}\Big] = \frac{\varepsilon}{2}|x - \xi|^{m-1}|\beta_m m|. \qquad (9.54)$$

Furthermore, observe that (9.51) ensures that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ with $|x - \xi| \leq 1$ it holds that

\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned} 
|p'(x)| & = \Big|\sum_{n=m}^{N}\beta_n n (x - \xi)^{n-1}\Big| \geq \Big|\sum_{n=m}^{N}\beta_n m| |x - \xi|^{m-1} - \Big[\sum_{n=m+1}^{N}\beta_n n (x - \xi)^{n-1}\Big] \Big| \\ 
&\geq \Big|\Big[\sum_{n=m}^{N}|\beta_m m| |x - \xi|^{m-1}\Big] - \Big[\sum_{n=m+1}^{N}|\beta_n n| |x - \xi|^{m-1}\Big] \Big| \quad \quad (9.55) \\ 
&= |x - \xi|^{m-1}\Big|\sum_{n=m}^{N}\beta_n m\Big| - |x - \xi|^{m-1}\Big[\sum_{n=m+1}^{N}|\beta_n n|\Big]. 
\end{aligned}
\end{equation*}
