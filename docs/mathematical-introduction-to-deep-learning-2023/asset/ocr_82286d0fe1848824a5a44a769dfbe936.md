(ii) it holds for all $t \in [0, T]$ that $\|\Theta_t - \vartheta\|_{2} \leq e^{-ct}\|\xi - \vartheta\|_2$, and

(iii) it holds for all $t \in [0, T]$ that

$0 \leq \frac{5}{2}\|\Theta_t - \vartheta\|_2^2 \leq \mathcal{L}(\Theta_t) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta).$ (5.139)

**Proof of Proposition 5.7.1.** Throughout this proof, let $V: \mathbb{R}^{9} \to [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{9}$ that $V(\theta)=\|\theta - \vartheta\|_{2}^{2}$, let $\epsilon: [0, T] \to [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $t \in [0, T]$ that $\epsilon(t)=\|\Theta_t - \vartheta\|_{2}=V(\Theta_t)$, and let $\tau \in [0, T]$ be the real number given by

$\tau=\inf(\{t \in [0, T]: \Theta_t \notin \mathbb{B}\} \cup \{T\})=\inf(\{t \in [0, T]: \epsilon(t)>r^{2}\} \cup \{T\}).$ (5.140)

Note that (5.138) and item (ii) in Lemma 5.6.8 establish item (i). Next observe that Lemma 5.6.4 implies that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{9}$ it holds that $V \in C^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{9}, [0, \infty))$ and

$(\nabla V)(\theta)=2(\theta - \vartheta).$ (5.141)

Moreover, observe that the fundamental theorem of calculus (see, for example, Coleman [85, Theorem 3.9]) and the fact that $\mathbb{R}^{9} \ni v \mapsto (\nabla \mathcal{L})(v) \in \mathbb{R}^{9}$ and $\Theta: [0, T] \to \mathbb{R}^{9}$ are continuous functions ensure that for all $t \in [0, T]$ it holds that $\Theta \in C^{1}([0, T], \mathbb{R}^{9})$ and

$\frac{d}{d t}(\Theta_t)=- (\nabla \mathcal{L})(\Theta_t).$ (5.142)

Combining (5.138) and (5.141) hence demonstrates that for all $t \in [0, \tau]$ it holds that $\epsilon \in C^{1}([0, T], [0, \infty))$ and

$\begin{aligned}\epsilon'(t)= & \frac{d}{d t}(V(\Theta_t))=V^{\prime}(\Theta_t)(\frac{d}{d t}(\Theta_t)) \\ = & \langle(\nabla V)(\Theta_t), \frac{d}{d t}(\Theta_t)\rangle \\ = & \langle 2(\Theta_t - \vartheta), - (\nabla \mathcal{L})(\Theta_t)\rangle \\ = & -2\langle(\Theta_t - \vartheta), (\nabla \mathcal{L})(\Theta_t)\rangle \\ \leq & -2c\|\Theta_t - \vartheta\|_2^2=-2c\epsilon(t).\end{aligned}$ (5.143)

The Gronwall inequality, for instance, in Lemma 5.6.1 therefore implies that for all $t \in [0, \tau]$ it holds that

$\epsilon(t) \leq \epsilon(0)e^{-2ct}.$ (5.144)

Hence, we obtain for all $t \in [0, \tau]$ that

$\|\Theta_t - \vartheta\|_2=\sqrt{\epsilon(t)} \leq \sqrt{\epsilon(0)e^{-ct}}=\|\Theta_0 - \vartheta\|_2e^{-ct}=\|\xi - \vartheta\|_2e^{-ct}.$ (5.145)

In the next step we prove that

$\tau>0.$ (5.146)
