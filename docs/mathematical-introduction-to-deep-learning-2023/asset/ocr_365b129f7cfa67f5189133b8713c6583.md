# 2.4.3 Sums of fully-connected feedforward ANNs

**Definition 2.4.10** (Sums of fully-connected feedforward $ANNs$ with the same length). Let $m \in \mathbb{Z}$, $n \in \{m, m+1, \ldots\}$, $\Phi_m, \Phi_{m+1}, \ldots, \Phi_n \in \mathbf{N}$ satisfy for all $k \in \{m, m+1, \ldots, n\}$ that

$$\mathcal{L}(\Phi_k) = \mathcal{L}(\Phi_m), \qquad \mathcal{I}(\Phi_k) = \mathcal{I}(\Phi_m), \qquad \text{and} \qquad \mathcal{O}(\Phi_k) = \mathcal{O}(\Phi_m) \tag{2.151}$$
(cf. Definition 1.3.1). Then we denote by $\bigoplus_{k=m}^n \Phi_k \in \mathbf{N}$ (we denote by $\Phi_m \oplus \Phi_{m+1} \oplus \cdots \oplus \Phi_n \in \mathbf{N}$) the *fully-connected feedforward ANN* given by

$$\bigoplus_{k=m}^n \Phi_k = \left( \mathcal{SO}_{\mathcal{O}(\Phi_m), n-m+1} \bullet \left[ \mathbf{P}_{n-m+1}(\Phi_m, \Phi_{m+1}, \ldots, \Phi_n) \right] \bullet \mathbb{T}_{\mathcal{I}(\Phi_m), n-m+1} \right) \in \mathbf{N} \tag{2.152}$$
(cf. Definitions 1.3.2, 2.1.1, 2.2.1, 2.4.1, and 2.4.6).

**Lemma 2.4.11** (Realizations of sums of fully-connected feedforward $ANNs$). Let $m \in \mathbb{Z}$, $n \in \{m, m+1, \ldots\}$, $\Phi_m, \Phi_{m+1}, \ldots, \Phi_n \in \mathbf{N}$ satisfy for all $k \in \{m, m+1, \ldots, n\}$ that

$$\mathcal{L}(\Phi_k) = \mathcal{L}(\Phi_m), \qquad \mathcal{I}(\Phi_k) = \mathcal{I}(\Phi_m), \qquad \text{and} \qquad \mathcal{O}(\Phi_k) = \mathcal{O}(\Phi_m) \tag{2.153}$$
(cf. Definition 1.3.1). *Then*

+      (i) it holds that $\mathcal{L}\left(\bigoplus_{k=m}^n \Phi_k\right) = \mathcal{L}(\Phi_m)$,   (ii) it holds that
$$\mathcal{D}\left( \bigoplus_{k=m}^n \Phi_k \right) = \left( \mathcal{I}(\Phi_m), \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_1(\Phi_k), \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_2(\Phi_k), \ldots, \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_{\mathcal{H}(\Phi_m)}(\Phi_k), \mathcal{O}(\Phi_m) \right), \tag{2.154}$$
and   (iii) it holds for all $a \in \mathcal{C}(\mathbb{R}^\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R})$ that
$$\mathcal{R}_a^\mathbf{N}\left( \bigoplus_{k=m}^n \Phi_k \right) = \sum_{k=m}^n \left( \mathcal{R}_a^\mathbf{N}(\Phi_k) \right) \tag{2.155}$$
(cf. Definitions 1.3.4 and 2.4.10).

*Proof of Lemma 2.4.11*. First, observe that Lemma 2.2.2 implies that

$$\begin{aligned} &\mathcal{D}(\mathbf{P}_{n-m+1}(\Phi_m, \Phi_{m+1}, \ldots, \Phi_n)) \
= & \left( \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_0(\Phi_k), \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_1(\Phi_k), \ldots, \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_{\mathcal{L}(\Phi_m)-1}(\Phi_k), \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_{\mathcal{L}(\Phi_m)}(\Phi_k) \right) \
= & \left( (n-m+1) \mathcal{I}(\Phi_m), \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_1(\Phi_k), \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_2(\Phi_k), \ldots, \sum_{k=m}^n \mathbb{D}_{\mathcal{L}(\Phi_m)-1}(\Phi_k), \right. \
& \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \left. (n-m+1) \mathcal{O}(\Phi_m) \right) \end{aligned} \tag{2.156}$$
