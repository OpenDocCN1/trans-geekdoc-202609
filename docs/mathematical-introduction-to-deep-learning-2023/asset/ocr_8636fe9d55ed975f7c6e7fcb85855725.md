This implies that for all \(x \in \mathbb{R}\) it holds that

\[ \lambda x = \left(\frac{b-x}{b-a}\right) \lambda a + \left[1-\left(\frac{b-x}{b-a}\right)\right] \lambda b. \] (12.33)

The fact that \(\mathbb{R} \ni x \mapsto e^x \in \mathbb{R}\) is convex hence demonstrates that for all \(x \in [a, b]\) it holds that

\[ e^{\lambda x} = \exp \left(\left(\frac{b-x}{b-a}\right) \lambda a + \left[1-\left(\frac{b-x}{b-a}\right)\right] \lambda b\right) \leq \left(\frac{b-x}{b-a}\right) e^{\lambda a} + \left[1-\left(\frac{b-x}{b-a}\right)\right] e^{\lambda b}. \] (12.34)

The assumption that \(\mathbb{E}[X] = 0\) therefore assures that

\[ \mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda X}\right] \leq \left(\frac{b}{b-a}\right) e^{\lambda a} + \left[1-\left(\frac{b}{b-a}\right)\right] e^{\lambda b}. \] (12.35)

Combining this with the fact that

\[ \begin{align*} \frac{b}{b-a} & = 1-\left[1-\left(\frac{b}{(b-a)}\right)\right] \\ & = 1-\left[\left(\frac{(b-a)}{(b-a)}\right)-\left(\frac{b}{(b-a)}\right)\right] \(12.36)\ & = 1-\left[\frac{-a}{(b-a)}\right] = 1-p \end{align*}

demonstrates that

\[ \begin{align*} \mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda X}\right] & \leq \left(\frac{b}{b-a}\right) e^{\lambda a} + \left[1-\left(\frac{b}{b-a}\right)\right] e^{\lambda b} \\ & = (1-p) e^{\lambda a} + [1-(1-p)] e^{\lambda b} \\ & = (1-p) e^{\lambda a} + p e^{\lambda b} \(12.37)\ & = \left[(1-p) + p e^{\lambda (b-a)}\right] e^{\lambda a}. \end{align*}

Moreover, note that the assumption that \(p = \frac{-a}{(b-a)}\) shows that \(p(b-a) = -a\). Hence, we obtain that \(a = -p(b-a)\). This and \(\textbf{(12.37)}\) assure that

\[ \begin{align*} \mathbb{E}\left[e^{\lambda X}\right] & \leq \left[(1-p) + p e^{\lambda (b-a)}\right] e^{-p\lambda (b-a)} = \exp \left(\ln \left(\left[\left(1-p\right) + p e^{\lambda (b-a)}\right] e^{-p\lambda (b-a)}\right)\right) \(12.38)\ & = \exp \left(\ln \left(\left(1-p\right) + p e^{\lambda (b-a)}\right) - p\lambda (b-a)\right) = \exp \left(\phi(\lambda (b-a))\right). \end{align*}

The proof of Lemma \(\textbf{12.1.14}\) is thus complete. \(\square\)
