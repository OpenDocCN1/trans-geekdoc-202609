# 11.3. Strong convergence rates for the optimization error involving ANNs

(i) it holds that $\mathscr{R}$ is a $\boldsymbol{\mathcal{B}}([−B, B]^{\mathbf{d}} \otimes \mathcal{F})/\mathcal{B}([0, \infty))$-measurable function and

(ii) it holds for all $\theta \in [−B, B]^{\mathbf{d}}, p \in (0, \infty)$ that

$$
\begin{align*}
& \left( \mathbb{E}\left[ \min_{k \in \{1, 2, \ldots, K\}} | \mathscr{R}(\Theta_k) - \mathscr{R}({\theta}^{[p]}) |^p \right] \right)^{1/p} \\
& \quad \leq \frac{4(v-u)b\mathbf{L}(\|\|\|_\infty + 1)^L B^L \mathbf{v}\max\{1, p/6\}}{K^{1/6}} \\
& \quad \leq \frac{4(v-u)b\mathbf{L}(\|\|\|_\infty + 1)^L B^L \max\{1, p\}}{K^{\mathbf{L}^{-1}(\|\|\|_{\infty + 1} - 2)}}
\end{align*}
$$

Proof of Corollary 11.3.9. Throughout this proof, let $L = 2(v−u)b\mathbf{L}(\|\|\|_\infty + 1)^L B^{L−1}$, let $P: [−B, B]^{\mathbf{d}} \to [−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}}$ satisfy for all $\theta = (\theta_1, \theta_2, \ldots, \theta_{\mathbf{d}}) \in [−B, B]^{\mathbf{d}}$ that $P(\theta) = (\theta_1, \theta_2, \ldots, \theta_{\mathbf{v}})$, and let $R: [−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}} \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $\theta \in [−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}}, \omega \in \Omega$ that

$$
R(\theta, \omega) = \frac{1}{M}\left[ \sum_{j=1}^{M} |\mathcal{N}^{\theta_1}_{u, v}(X_j(\omega)) - Y_j(\omega)|^2 \right].
$$

Observe that the fact that $\forall \theta \in [−B, B]^{\mathbf{d}}: \mathcal{N}^{\theta_1}_{u, v} = \mathcal{N}^{P(\theta)}_{u, v}$ establishes that for all $\theta \in [−B, B]^{\mathbf{d}}, \omega \in \Omega$ it holds that

$$
\begin{align*}
\mathscr{R}(\theta, \omega) = & \frac{1}{M}\left[ \sum_{j=1}^{M} |\mathcal{N}^{\theta_1}_{u, v}(X_j(\omega)) - Y_j(\omega)|^2 \right] \\
= & \frac{1}{M}\left[ \sum_{j=1}^{M} |\mathcal{N}^{P(\theta)}_{u, v}(X_j(\omega)) - Y_j(\omega)|^2 \right] = R(P(\theta), \omega).
\end{align*}
$$

Furthermore, note that Lemma 11.3.8 (applied with $\mathbf{d} \hookrightarrow \mathbf{v}, \mathscr{R} \hookrightarrow (([−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}} \times \Omega) \ni (\theta, \omega) \mapsto R(\theta, \omega) \in [0, \infty))$ in the notation of Lemma 11.3.8) shows that for all $\theta, \theta' \in [−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}}, \omega \in \Omega$ it holds that

$$
|R(\theta, \omega) − R(\theta', \omega)| \leq 2(v−u)b\mathbf{L}(\|\|\|_\infty + 1)^L B^{L−1} \|\theta − \theta'\|_\infty = L \|\theta − \theta'\|_\infty.
$$

Moreover, observe that the assumption that $X_j, j \in \{1, 2, \ldots, M\}$, and $Y_j, j \in \{1, 2, \ldots, M\}$, are random variables demonstrates that $R: [−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}} \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ is a random field. This, (11.114), the fact that $P \circ \Theta_k: \Omega \to [−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}}, k \in \{1, 2, \ldots, K\}$, are i.i.d. random variables, the fact that $P \circ \Theta_1$ is continuously uniformly distributed on $[−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}}$, and Proposition 11.2.7 (applied with $\mathbf{d} \hookrightarrow \mathbf{v}, \alpha \hookrightarrow −B, \beta \hookrightarrow B, \mathscr{R} \hookrightarrow R, (\Theta_k)_{k \in \{1, 2, \ldots, K\}} \hookrightarrow (P \circ \Theta_k)_{k \in \{1, 2, \ldots, K\}}$ in the notation of Proposition 11.2.7) imply that for all $\theta \in [−B, B]^{\mathbf{d}}, p \in (0, \infty)$ it holds that $R$ is $(\mathcal{B}([−B, B]^{\mathbf{v}} \otimes \mathcal{F})/\mathcal{B}([\mathbb{R}])$-measurable and

$$
\begin{align*}
& \left( \mathbb{E}\left[ \min_{k \in \{1, 2, \ldots, K\}} | R(P(\Theta_k)) - R(P(\theta)) |^p \right] \right)^{1/p} \\
& \quad \leq \frac{L(2B)\max\{1, (p/6)^{1/6}\}}{K^{1/6}} = \frac{4(v−u)b\mathbf{L}(\|\|\|_\infty + 1)^L B^L \max\{1, (p/6)^{1/6}\}}{K^{1/6}}.
\end{align*}
$$
