
Therefore, we obtain that

\begin{equation}
X \subseteq \left[ \bigcup_{m=1}^{n} \{ v \in X : d(x_i, v) \le 2r \} \right].
\tag{12.101}
\end{equation}

Combining this and Lemma **12.2.5** shows that $\mathcal{C}_{(X,d),n} \le 2r$ (cf. Definition **12.2.1**). The proof of Lemma **12.2.15** is thus complete.

## 12.2.3.4 Equivalence of covering and packing radii

**Corollary 12.2.16.** *Let* $(X, d)$ *be a metric space and let* $n \in \mathbb{N}$. *Then* $\mathcal{P}_{(X,d),n} \le \mathcal{C}_{(X,d),n} \le 2\mathcal{P}_{(X,d),n}$ *(cf. Definitions* **12.2.1** *and* **12.2.6***)*.

*Proof of Corollary* **12.2.16**. *Observe that Lemma* **12.2.10** *and Lemma* **12.2.15** *establish that* $\mathcal{P}_{(X,d),n} \le \mathcal{C}_{(X,d),n} \le 2\mathcal{P}_{(X,d),n}$ *(cf. Definitions* **12.2.1** *and* **12.2.6***)* *. The proof of Corollary **12.2.16** is thus complete.

## 12.2.4 Inequalities for entropy quantities in finite dimensional vector spaces

### 12.2.4.1 Measures induced by Lebesgue–Borel measures

**Lemma 12.2.17.** *Let* $(V, \lVert \cdot \rVert)$ *be a normed vector space, let* $N \in \mathbb{N}$, *let* $b_1, b_2, \dots, b_N \in V$ *be a Hamel-basis of* $V$, *let* $\lambda : \mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^N) \to [0, \infty]$ *be the Lebesgue–Borel measure on* $\mathbb{R}^N$, *let* $\Phi : \mathbb{R}^N \to V$ *satisfy for all* $r = (r_1, r_2, \dots, r_N) \in \mathbb{R}^N$ *that* $\Phi(r) = r_1b_1 + r_2b_2 + \dots + r_Nb_N$, *and let* $\nu : \mathcal{B}(V) \to [0, \infty]$ *satisfy for all* $A \in \mathcal{B}(V)$ *that*

\begin{equation}
\nu(A) = \lambda(\Phi^{-1}(A)).
\tag{12.102}
\end{equation}

Then

- (i) *it holds that* $\Phi$ *is linear*,
- (ii) *it holds for all* $r = (r_1, r_2, \dots, r_N) \in \mathbb{R}^N$ *that* $\lVert \Phi(r) \rVert \le \left[ \sum_{n=1}^{N} \lVert b_n \rVert^2 \right]^{1/2} \left[ \sum_{n=1}^{N} \lVert r_n \rVert^2 \right]^{1/2}$,
- (iii) *it holds that* $\Phi \in \mathcal{C}(\mathbb{R}^N, V)$,
- (iv) *it holds that* $\Phi$ *is bijective*,
- (v) *it holds that* $(V, \mathcal{B}(V), \nu)$ *is a measure space*,
- (vi) *it holds for all* $r \in (0, \infty)$, $v \in V$, $A \in \mathcal{B}(V)$ *that* $\nu(\{ (ra + v) \in V : a \in A \}) = r^N \nu(A)$,
- (vii) *it holds for all* $r \in (0, \infty)$ *that* $\nu(\{ v \in V : \lVert v \rVert \le r \}) = r^N \nu(\{ v \in V : \lVert v \rVert \le 1 \})$, *and*
- (viii) *it holds that* $\nu(\{v \in V: \lVert v \rVert \leq 1\}) > 0$.

*Proof of Lemma 12.2.17.* Note that for all $r=(r_{1}, r_{2}, \ldots, r_{N}), s=(s_{1}, s_{2}, \ldots, s_{N}) \in \mathbb{R}^{N}$, $\rho \in \mathbb{R}$ it holds that

$\Phi(\rho r + s) = (\rho r_{1} + s_{1})b_{1} + (\rho r_{2} + s_{2})b_{2} + \cdots + (\rho r_{N} + s_{N})b_{N} = \rho \Phi(r) + \Phi(s). \qquad (12.103)$

This establishes item **(i)**. Next observe that Hölder's inequality shows that for all $r=(r_{1}, r_{2}, \ldots, r_{N}) \in \mathbb{R}^{N}$ it holds that

$\|\Phi(r)\| = \|r_{1}b_{1} + r_{2}b_{2} + \cdots + r_{N}b_{N}\| \leq \sum_{n=1}^{N} |r_{n}| \|b_{n}\| \leq \left[\sum_{n=1}^{N} \|b_{n}\|^{2}\right]^{1/2} \left[\sum_{n=1}^{N} |r_{n}|^{2}\right]^{1/2}. \qquad (12.104)$

This establishes item **(ii)**. Moreover, note that item **(ii)** proves item **(iii)**. Furthermore, observe that the assumption that $b_{1}, b_{2}, \ldots, b_{N} \in V$ is a Hamel-basis of $V$ establishes item **(iv)**. Next note that **(12.102)** and item **(iii)** prove item **(v)**. In addition, observe that the integral transformation theorem shows that for all $r \in (0, \infty)$, $v \in \mathbb{R}^{N}$, $A \in \mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^{N})$ it holds that

$\begin{aligned} \lambda(\{(ra + v) \in \mathbb{R}^{N}: a \in A\}) &= \lambda(\{ra \in \mathbb{R}^{N}: a \in A\}) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}} \mathbb{1}_{\{ra \in \mathbb{R}^{N}: a \in A\}}(x) \, \mathrm{d}x \\ &= \int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}} \mathbb{1}_{A}(\tfrac{x}{r}) \, \mathrm{d}x = r^{N} \int_{\mathbb{R}^{N}} \mathbb{1}_{A}(x) \, \mathrm{d}x = r^{N} \lambda(A). \qquad (12.105) \end{aligned}$

Combining item **(i)** and item **(iv)** hence demonstrates that for all $r \in (0, \infty)$, $v \in V$, $A \in \mathcal{B}(V)$ it holds that

$\begin{aligned} \nu(\{(ra + v) \in V: a \in A\}) &= \lambda(\Phi^{-1}(\{(ra + v) \in V: a \in A\})) \\ &= \lambda(\{\Phi^{-1}(ra + v) \in \mathbb{R}^{N}: a \in A\}) \\ &= \lambda(\{[r \Phi^{-1}(a) + \Phi^{-1}(v)] \in \mathbb{R}^{N}: a \in A\}) \\ &= \lambda(\{[ra + \Phi^{-1}(v)] \in \mathbb{R}^{N}: a \in \Phi^{-1}(A)\}) \\ &= r^{N} \lambda(\Phi^{-1}(A)) = r^{N} \nu(A). \qquad (12.106) \end{aligned}$

This establishes item **(vi)**. Hence, we obtain that for all $r \in (0, \infty)$ it holds that

$\begin{aligned} \nu(\{v \in V: \|v\| \leq r\}) &= \nu(\{rv \in V: \|v\| \leq 1\}) \\ &= r^{N} \nu(\{v \in V: \|v\| \leq 1\}) = r^{N} \nu(X). \qquad (12.107) \end{aligned}$

This establishes item **(vii)**. Furthermore, observe that **(12.107)** demonstrates that

$\begin{aligned} \infty = \lambda(\mathbb{R}^{N}) &= \nu(V) = \limsup_{r \to \infty} \big[\nu(\{v \in V: \|v\| \leq r\})\big] \\ &= \limsup_{r \to \infty} \big[r^{N} \nu(\{v \in V: \|v\| \leq 1\})\big]. \qquad (12.108) \end{aligned}$

Hence, we obtain that $\nu(\{v \in V : \|v\| \leq 1\}) \neq 0$. This establishes item **(viii)**. The proof of Lemma 12.2.17 is thus complete. $\square$

### 12.2.4.2 Upper bounds for packing radii

**Lemma 12.2.18.** *Let $(V, \|\cdot\|)$ be a normed vector space, let $X = \{v \in V : \|v\| \leq 1\}$, let $d : X \times X \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $v, w \in X$ that $d(v, w) = \|v - w\|$, and let $n, N \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfy $N = \dim(V)$. Then*

$\mathcal{P}_{(X, d), n} \leq 2(n+1)^{-1/N}$                                                                                     (12.109)

*(cf. Definition 12.2.6).*

*Proof of Lemma 12.2.18.* Throughout this proof, assume without loss of generality that $\mathcal{P}_{(X, d), n} > 0$, let $\rho \in [0, \mathcal{P}_{(X, d), n})$, let $\lambda : \mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^N) \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ be the Lebesgue-Borel measure on $\mathbb{R}^N$, let $b_1, b_2, \dots, b_N \in V$ be a Hamel-basis of $V$, let $\Phi : \mathbb{R}^N \rightarrow V$ satisfy for all $r = (r_1, r_2, \dots, r_N) \in \mathbb{R}^N$ that

$\Phi(r) = r_1b_1 + r_2b_2 + \dots + r_Nb_N,                                                                       (12.110)$

and let $\nu : \mathcal{B}(V) \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $A \in \mathcal{B}(V)$ that

$\nu(A) = \lambda(\Phi^{-1}(A))                                                                                             (12.111)$

*(cf. Definition 12.2.6)*. Observe that Lemma 12.2.11 ensures that $\rho < \mathcal{P}_{(X, d), n} \leq 1$. Moreover, note that (12.75) shows that there exist $x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{n+1} \in X$ which satisfy

$\min_{i,j \in \{1, 2, \dots, n+1\}, i \neq j} |x_i - x_j| = \min_{i,j \in \{1, 2, \dots, n+1\}, i \neq j} d(x_i, x_j) > 2\rho.             (12.112)$

Observe that (12.112) ensures that for all $i, j \in \{1, 2, \dots, n+1\}$ with $i \neq j$ it holds that

$\{v \in V : \|x_i - v\| \leq \rho\} \cap \{v \in V : \|x_j - v\| \leq \rho\} = \emptyset.                                             (12.113)$

Moreover, note that (12.112) and the fact that $\rho < 1$ show that for all $j \in \{1, 2, \dots, n+1\}$, $w \in \{v \in X : d(x_j, v) \leq \rho\}$ it holds that

$\|w\| \leq \|w - x_j\| + \|x_j\| \leq \rho + 1 \leq 2.                                                                    (12.114)$

Therefore, we obtain that for all $j \in \{1, 2, \dots, n+1\}$ it holds that

$\{v \in V : \|v - x_j\| \leq \rho\} \subseteq \{v \in V : \|v\| \leq 2\}.                                                        (12.115)$

Next observe that Lemma 12.2.17 ensures that $(V, \mathcal{B}(V), \nu)$ is a measure space. Combining this and (12.113) with (12.115) proves that

$\begin{align*} \sum_{j=1}^{n+1} \nu(\{v \in V : \|v - x_j\| \leq \rho\}) &= \nu\bigg(\bigcup_{j=1}^{n+1} \{v \in V : \|v - x_j\| \leq \rho\} \bigg) \\ &\leq \nu(\{v \in V : \|v\| \leq 2\}). \end{align*}$                                                                                          (12.116)
