**Lemma 7.2.9.** *Let* $\varnothing \in \mathbb{N}$, $\{\gamma_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq (0, \infty)$, $\{J_n\}_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq \mathbb{N}$, *let* $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle : \mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R}^d \to \mathbb{R}$ *be a scalar product, let* $\| \cdot \| : \mathbb{R}^d \to [0, \infty)$ *satisfy for all* $v \in \mathbb{R}^d$ *that*

$\|v\| = \sqrt{\langle v, v \rangle},$ **(7.40)**

*let* $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ *be a probability space, let* $\xi : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ *be a random variable, let* $(S, \mathcal{S})$ *be a measurable space, let* $X_{n,j} : \Omega \to S$, $j \in \{1, 2, \dots, J_n\}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, *be i.i.d. random variables, assume that* $\xi$ *and* $\{X_{n,j}\}_{n \in \mathbb{N}, j \in \{1, \dots, J_n\}}$ *are independent, let* $\ell = (\ell(\theta, x))_{(\theta, x) \in \mathbb{R}^d \times S} : \mathbb{R}^d \times S \to \mathbb{R}$ *be* $\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^d) \otimes \mathcal{S} / \mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R})$-*measurable, assume for all* $x \in S$ *that* $(\mathbb{R}^d \ni \theta \mapsto \ell(\theta, x)) \in \mathbb{R}) \in C^1(\mathbb{R}^d, \mathbb{R})$, *assume for all* $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ *that* $\mathbb{E}[\| (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{1,1}) \|] < \infty$ *(cf. Lemma 7.2.8), let* $\mathcal{V} : \mathbb{R}^d \to [0, \infty]$ *satisfy for all* $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ *that*

$\mathcal{V}(\theta) = \mathbb{E}[\| (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{1,1}) - \mathbb{E}[ (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{1,1}) ] \|^2]$, **(7.41)**

*and let* $\Theta : \mathbb{N}_0 \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ *be the stochastic process which satisfies for all* $n \in \mathbb{N}$ *that*

$\Theta_0 = \xi \quad \text{ and } \quad \Theta_n = \Theta_{n-1} - \gamma_n \left[ \frac{1}{J_n} \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} (\nabla \theta \ell)(\Theta_{n-1}, X_{n,j}) \right]$. **(7.42)**

*Then it holds for all* $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $v \in \mathbb{R}^d$ *that*

$(\mathbb{E}[\|\| \Theta_n - v \|\|^2])^{1/2} \geq \left[ \frac{\gamma_n}{(J_n)^{1/2}} \right] (\mathbb{E}[\mathcal{V}(\Theta_{n-1})])^{1/2}$. **(7.43)**

*Proof of Lemma 7.2.9.* *Throughout this proof, for every* $n \in \mathbb{N}$ *let* $\phi_n : \mathbb{R}^d \to [0, \infty]$ *satisfy for all* $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ *that*

$\phi_n(\theta) = \mathbb{E}\left[ \left\|\| \theta - \frac{\gamma_n}{J_n} \left[ \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{n,j}) \right] - v \right\|\|^2 \right]$. **(7.44)**

*Note that Lemma 7.2.3 shows that for all* $v \in \mathbb{R}^d$ *and all random variables* $Z : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ *with* $\mathbb{E}[\|\| Z \|\|] < \infty$ *it holds that*

$\mathbb{E}[\|\| Z - v \|^2] = \mathbb{E}[\|\| Z - \mathbb{E}[Z] \|^2] + \|\| \mathbb{E}[Z] - v \|^2 \geq \mathbb{E}[\|\| Z - \mathbb{E}[Z] \|^2]$. **(7.45)**

*Therefore, we obtain for all* $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^d$ *that*

$\begin{aligned}
\phi_n(\theta) &= \mathbb{E}\left[ \left\|\| \frac{\gamma_n}{J_n} \left[ \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{n,j}) \right] - (\theta - v) \right\|\|^2 \right] \\
&= \mathbb{E}\left[ \frac{\gamma_n}{J_n} \left[ \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{n,j}) \right] - \mathbb{E}\left[ \frac{\gamma_n}{J_n} \left[ \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{n,j}) \right] \right\|\|^2 \right] \\
&= \frac{(\gamma_n)^2}{(J_n)^2} \mathbb{E}\left[ \left\|\| \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} \left( (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{n,j}) - \mathbb{E}[ (\nabla \theta \ell)(\theta, X_{n,j}) ] \right) \right\|\|^2 \right].
\end{aligned}$ **(7.46)**
