Hence, we obtain that

$$\sup_{x \in E}|Z_{x}| = \sup_{\mathbb{P}_{x \in (\cup_{n=1}^{N} B_{n})}}|Z_{x}| = \max_{n \in \{1, \ldots, N\}}\sup_{x \in B_{n}}|Z_{x}|. \qquad (13.16)$$

Therefore, we obtain that

$$\begin{aligned}\mathbb{E}\left[\sup_{x \in E}|Z_{x}|^{p}\right] &= \mathbb{E}\left[\max_{n \in \{1, \ldots, N\}}\sup_{x \in B_{n}}|Z_{x}|^{p}\right] \\& \leq \mathbb{E}\left[\sum_{n=1}^{N}\sup_{x \in B_{n}}|Z_{x}|^{p}\right] = \sum_{n=1}^{N}\mathbb{E}\left[\sup_{x \in B_{n}}|Z_{x}|^{p}\right].\end{aligned} \qquad (13.17)$$

(cf. Lemma 12.3.2). Furthermore, note that the assumption that for all $x, y \in E$ it holds that $|Z_{x} - Z_{y}| \leq L \delta(x, y)$ demonstrates that for all $n \in \{1, 2, \ldots, N\}$, $x \in B_{n}$ it holds that

$$\begin{aligned}|Z_{x}| \leq |Z_{x} - Z_{z_{n}}| + |Z_{z_{n}}| & \leq |Z_{x} - Z_{z_{n}}| + |Z_{z_{n}}| \leq L \delta(x, z_{n}) + |Z_{z_{n}}| \\ & \leq L r_{n} + |Z_{z_{n}}|.\end{aligned} \qquad (13.18)$$

This and (13.17) establish that

$$\mathbb{E}\left[\sup_{x \in E}|Z_{x}|^{p}\right] \leq \sum_{n=1}^{N}\mathbb{E}\left[(L r_{n} + |Z_{z_{n}}|)^{p}\right]. \qquad (13.19)$$

The proof of Lemma 13.2.1 is thus complete. $\square$

# **Lemma 13.2.2.**

Let $(E, \delta)$ be a non-empty separable metric space, let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ be a probability space, for every $x \in E$ let $Z_{x} \colon \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a random variable, let $L \in (0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $x, y \in E$ that $|Z_{x} - Z_{y}| \leq L \delta(x, y)$, and let $p, r \in (0, \infty)$. Then

$$\mathbb{E}\left[\sup_{x \in E}|Z_{x}|^{p}\right] \leq \mathcal{C}^{(E, \delta), r}\sup_{x \in E}\mathbb{E}\left[(L r + |Z_{x}|)^{p}\right] \qquad (13.20)$$

(cf. Definition 4.3.2 and Lemma 12.3.2).

**Proof of Lemma 13.2.2.** Throughout this proof, assume without loss of generality that $\mathcal{C}^{(E, \delta), r} < \infty$, let $N = \mathcal{C}^{(E, \delta), r}$, and let $z_{1}, z_{2}, \ldots, z_{N} \in E$ satisfy

$$E \subseteq \bigcup_{n=1}^{N}\{x \in E \colon \delta(x, z_{n}) \leq r\} \qquad (13.21)$$

(cf. Definition 4.3.2). Observe that Lemma 13.2.1 (applied with $r_{1} \sim r, r_{2} \sim r, \ldots, r_{N} \sim r$ in the notation of Lemma 13.2.1) implies that

$$\begin{aligned}\mathbb{E}\left[\sup_{x \in E}|Z_{x}|^{p}\right] & \leq \sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb{E}\left[(L r + |Z_{z_{i}}|)^{p}\right] \\ & \leq \sum_{i=1}^{N}\sup_{x \in E}\mathbb{E}\left[(L r + |Z_{x}|)^{p}\right] = N \sup_{x \in E}\mathbb{E}\left[(L r + |Z_{x}|)^{p}\right].\end{aligned} \qquad (13.22)$$

(cf. Lemma 12.3.2). The proof of Lemma 13.2.2 is thus complete. $\square$
