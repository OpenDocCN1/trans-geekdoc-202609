# 7.2.2 Non-convergence of SGD for not appropriately decaying learning rates

In this section we present two results that, roughly speaking, motivate that the sequence of learning rates of the SGD optimization method should be chosen such that they converge to zero (see Corollary 7.2.10 below) but not too fast (see Lemma 7.2.13 below).

# 7.2.2.1 Bias-variance decomposition of the mean square error

Lemma 7.2.3 (Bias-variance decomposition of the mean square error). Let \(d \in \mathbb{N}\), \(\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^d\), let \(\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle: \mathbb{R}^d \times \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\) be a scalar product, let \(\|\cdot\|: \mathbb{R}^d \rightarrow [0,\infty)\) satisfy for all \(v \in \mathbb{R}^d\) that

\begin{equation}\|\vartheta\|=\sqrt{\langle\vartheta,\vartheta\rangle} \tag{7.17}\end{equation}

let \((\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})\) be a probability space, and let \(Z:\Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d\) be a random variable with \(\mathbb{E}\|Z\|<\infty\). Then

\begin{equation}\mathbb{E}\left[\|Z-\vartheta\|^2\right]=\mathbb{E}\left[\|Z-\mathbb{E}[Z]\|^2\right]+\|\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\|^2. \tag{7.18}\end{equation}

Proof of Lemma 7.2.3. Observe that the assumption that \(\mathbb{E}\|Z\|<\infty\) and the Cauchy-Schwarz inequality demonstrate that

\begin{align}\mathbb{E}\left[\langle Z-\mathbb{E}[Z], \mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\rangle\right]& \leqslant \mathbb{E}\left[\|Z-\mathbb{E}[Z]\|\|\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\|\right] \notag\\ & \leqslant \left(\mathbb{E}\|Z\|+\|\mathbb{E}[Z]\|\right)\|\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\|<\infty. \tag{7.19}\end{align}

The linearity of the expectation hence ensures that

\begin{align}\mathbb{E}\left[\|Z-\vartheta\|^2\right] & =\mathbb{E}\left[\left\|(Z-\mathbb{E}[Z])+(\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta)\right\|^2\right] \notag\\ & =\mathbb{E}\left[\|Z-\mathbb{E}[Z]\|^2+2\langle Z-\mathbb{E}[Z],\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\rangle+\|\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\|^2\right] \notag\\ & =\mathbb{E}\left[\|Z-\mathbb{E}[Z]\|^2\right]+2\langle\mathbb{E}[Z]-\mathbb{E}[Z],\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\rangle+\|\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\|^2 \notag\\ & =\mathbb{E}\left[\|Z-\mathbb{E}[Z]\|^2\right]+\|\mathbb{E}[Z]-\vartheta\|^2. \tag{7.20}\end{align}

The proof of Lemma 7.2.3 is thus complete.
