**Corollary 9.6.4** (Qualitative standard **KL** inequalities for general one-dimensional polynomials). *Let* $\xi \in \mathbb{R}$, $N \in \mathbb{N}$, $p \in C^{\infty}(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R})$ *satisfy for all* $x \in \mathbb{R}$ *that* $p^{(N)}(x) = 0$. *Then there exist* $\varepsilon, c \in (0, \infty)$, $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ *such that for all* $x \in [\xi - \varepsilon, \xi + \varepsilon]$ *it holds that*

$$|p(x) - p(\xi)|^{\alpha} \leq c|p'(x)|. \tag{9.63}$$

*Proof of Corollary 9.6.4*. *Note that Corollary 9.6.3 establishes (9.63). The proof of Corollary 9.6.4 is thus complete.* $\Box$

**Corollary 9.6.5**. *Let* $\mathscr{L} : \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$ *be a polynomial. Then* $\mathscr{L}$ *is a standard* **KL** *function* (cf. Definition 9.1.2).

*Proof of Corollary 9.6.5*. *Observe that (9.2) and Corollary 9.6.4 establish that* $\mathscr{L}$ *is a standard* **KL** *function* (cf. Definition 9.1.2). *The proof of Corollary 9.6.5 is thus complete.* $\Box$
