
## 3.3.2 Convergence rates for the approximation error

**Definition 3.3.4** (Quasi vector norms). *We denote by* $\|\cdot\|_{p} : (\bigcup_{d=1}^{\infty} \mathbb{R}^d) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}, \, p \in (0, \infty]$, *the functions which satisfy for all $p \in (0, \infty)$, $d \in \mathbb{N}$, $\theta = (\theta_1, \dots, \theta_d) \in \mathbb{R}^d$ that*

$$ \|\theta\|_p = \left[\sum_{i=1}^d |\theta_i|^p\right]^{1/p} \quad \text{and} \quad \|\theta\|_{\infty} = \max_{i \in \{1, \dots, d\}} |\theta_i|. \tag{3.90} $$

**Corollary 3.3.5** (Implicit one-dimensional ANN approximations with prescribed error tolerances and explicit parameter bounds). *Let $\varepsilon \in (0, \infty)$, $L \in [0, \infty)$, $a \in \mathbb{R}$, $b \in [a, \infty)$ and let $f : [a, b] \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $x, y \in [a, b]$ that*

$$ |f(x) - f(y)| \le L|x - y|. \tag{3.91} $$

*Then there exists* $\mathbf{F} \in \mathbb{N}$ *such that*

-   (i) it holds that $\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{r}}^{\mathbf{N}}(\mathbf{F}) \in C(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R})$,
-   (ii) it holds that $\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{F}) = 1$,
-   (iii) it holds that $\mathbb{D}_1(\mathbf{F}) \le L(b - a)\varepsilon^{-1} + 2$,
-   (iv) it holds for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ that $|(\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{r}}^{\mathbf{N}}(\mathbf{F}))(x) - (\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{r}}^{\mathbf{N}}(\mathbf{F}))(y)| \le L|x - y|$,
-   (v) it holds that $\sup_{x \in [a, b]} |(\mathcal{R}_{\mathbf{r}}^{\mathbf{N}}(\mathbf{F}))(x) - f(x)| \le \varepsilon$,
-   (vi) it holds that $\mathcal{P}(\mathbf{F}) = 3(\mathbb{D}_1(\mathbf{F})) + 1 \le 3L(b - a)\varepsilon^{-1} + 7$, and
-   (vii) it holds that $\|\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{F})\|_{\infty} \le \max\{1, |a|, |b|, 2L, |f(a)|\}$

(cf. Definitions 1.2.4, 1.3.1, 1.3.4, 1.3.5, and 3.3.4).

Proof of Corollary 3.3.5. Throughout this proof, assume without loss of generality that $a < b$, let $K \in \mathbb{N}_0 \cap [\frac{L(b-a)}{\varepsilon}, \frac{L(b-a)}{\varepsilon} + 1)$, $\mathbf{f}_0, \mathbf{f}_1, \dots, \mathbf{f}_K \in [a, b]$, $c_0, c_1, \dots, c_K \in \mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $k \in \{0, 1, \dots, K\}$ that

$$ \mathbf{f}_k = a + \frac{k(b - a)}{\max\{K, 1\}} \quad \text{and} \quad c_k = \frac{K(f(\mathbf{f}_{\min\{k+1, K\}}) - 2f(\mathbf{f}_k) + f(\mathbf{f}_{\max\{k-1, 0\}}))}{(b - a)}, \tag{3.92} $$

and let $\mathbf{F} \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfy

$$ \mathbf{F} = \mathbf{A}_{1, f(\mathbf{f}_0)} \bullet \left( \bigoplus_{k=0}^{K} (\mathbf{c}_k \otimes (\mathbf{1} \bullet \mathbf{A}_{1 - \mathbf{f}_k})) \right). \tag{3.93} $$

(cf. Definitions 1.3.1, 2.1.1, 2.3.1, 2.3.4, 2.4.10, and 3.2.1). Observe that Corollary 3.3.3 demonstrates that

-   (I) it holds that $\mathcal{D}(\mathbf{F}) = (1, K + 1, 1)$,
-   (II) it holds that $\mathcal{R}^{\mathbb{N}}_{\mathfrak{r}}(\mathbf{F}) \in C(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R})$,
-   (III) it holds for all $x, y \in \mathbb{R}$ that $|(\mathcal{R}^{\mathbb{N}}_{\mathfrak{r}}(\mathbf{F}))(x) - (\mathcal{R}^{\mathbb{N}}_{\mathfrak{r}}(\mathbf{F}))(y)| \leq L|x - y|$,
-   (IV) it holds that $\sup_{x \in [a,b]} |(\mathcal{R}^{\mathbb{N}}_{\mathfrak{r}}(\mathbf{F}))(x) - f(x)| \leq \varepsilon$, and
-   (V) it holds that $\mathcal{P}(\mathbf{F}) = 3K + 4$.

(cf. Definitions 1.2.4 and 1.3.4). This establishes items (i), (iv), and (v). Note that item (I) and the fact that

$$ K \leq 1 + \frac{L(b - a)}{\varepsilon} $$

prove items (ii) and (iii). Observe that item (ii) and items (I) and (V) show that

$$ \mathcal{P}(\mathbf{F}) = 3K + 4 = 3(K + 1) + 1 = 3(\mathbb{D}_1(\mathbf{F})) + 1 \leq \frac{3L(b - a)}{\varepsilon} + 7. $$

This proves item (vi). Note that Lemma 3.2.4 ensures that for all $k \in \{0, 1, \ldots, K\}$ it holds that

$$ c_{k} \circledast (\mathbf{1}_{1} \bullet \mathbf{A}_{1, -\mathfrak{r}_{k}}) = ((1, -\mathfrak{r}_{k}), (c_{k}, 0)). $$

Combining this with (2.152), (2.143), (2.134), and (2.2) implies that

$$ \begin{array}{rcl} \mathbf{F} & = & \left( \left( \left( \begin{array}{c} 1 \\ 1 \\ \vdots \\ 1 \end{array} \right) , \left( \begin{array}{c} -\mathfrak{r}_{0} \\ -\mathfrak{r}_{1} \\ \vdots \\ -\mathfrak{r}_{K} \end{array} \right) \right), ((c_{0} \ c_{1} \ \cdots \ c_{K}), f(\mathfrak{r}_{0})) \right) \\ & & \in (\mathbb{R}^{(K+1) \times 1} \times \mathbb{R}^{K+1}) \times (\mathbb{R}^{1 \times (K+1)} \times \mathbb{R}) \end{array} $$

Lemma 1.3.8 hence demonstrates that

$$ \|\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{F})\|_{\infty} = \max\{|\mathfrak{f}_{0}|, |\mathfrak{f}_{1}|, \ldots, |\mathfrak{f}_{K}|, |c_{0}|, |c_{1}|, \ldots, |c_{K}|, |f(\mathfrak{r}_{0})|, 1\} $$

(cf. Definitions 1.3.5 and 3.3.4). Furthermore, observe that the assumption that for all $x, y \in [a, b]$ it holds that

$$ |f(x) - f(y)| \leq L|x - y| $$

and the fact that for all $k \in \mathbb{N} \cap (0, K + 1)$ it holds that

$$ \mathfrak{r}_{k} - \mathfrak{r}_{k-1} = \frac{(b - a)}{\max\{K, 1\}} $$

establish that for all $k \in \{0, 1, \dots, K\}$ it holds that

$$ \begin{align} |c_k| &\le \frac{K(|f(\mathfrak{f}_{\min\{k+1,K\}}) - f(\mathfrak{f}_k)| + |f(\mathfrak{f}_{\max\{k-1,0\}}) - f(\mathfrak{f}_k)|}{(b-a)} \\ &\le \frac{KL(|\mathfrak{f}_{\min\{k+1,K\}} - \mathfrak{f}_k| + |\mathfrak{f}_{\max\{k-1,0\}} - \mathfrak{f}_k|)}{(b-a)} \\ &\le \frac{2KL(b-a)[\max\{K,1\}]^{-1}}{(b-a)} \le 2L. \end{align}\tag{3.101} $$

This and $(3.98)$ prove item $\mathbf{(vii)}$. The proof of Corollary $\mathbf{3.3.5}$ is thus complete. $\square$

**Corollary 3.3.6** *(Implicit one-dimensional ANN approximations with prescribed error tolerances and asymptotic parameter bounds).* *Let* $L, a \in \mathbb{R}$, *$b \in [a, \infty)$* *and let* $f: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ *satisfy for all* $x, y \in [a, b]$ *that*

$$ |f(x) - f(y)| \le L|x - y|.\tag{3.102} $$

*Then there exist* $\mathfrak{C} \in \mathbb{R}$ *such that for all* $\varepsilon \in (0, 1]$ *there exists* $\mathbf{F} \in \mathbf{N}$ *such that*

$$ \mathcal{R}^{\mathbf{N}}_{\mathfrak{t}}(\mathbf{F}) \in \mathcal{C}(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R}), \quad \sup_{x \in [a,b]}(|\mathcal{R}^{\mathbf{N}}_{\mathfrak{t}}(\mathbf{F})(x) - f(x)|) \le \varepsilon, \quad \mathcal{H}(\mathbf{F}) = 1,\tag{3.103} $$
$$ \|\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{F})\|_\infty \le \max\{1, |a|, |b|, 2L, |f(a)|\}, \quad \text{and} \quad \mathcal{P}(\mathbf{F}) \le \mathfrak{C}\varepsilon^{-1}\tag{3.104} $$

*(cf. Definitions* $\mathbf{1.2.4}$*,* $\mathbf{1.3.1}$*,* $\mathbf{1.3.4}$*,* $\mathbf{1.3.5}$*, and* $\mathbf{3.3.4}$*).*

*Proof of Corollary* $\mathbf{3.3.6}$. *Throughout this proof, assume without loss of generality that* $a < b$ *and let*

$$ \mathfrak{C} = 3L(b-a) + 7.\tag{3.105} $$

Note that the assumption that $a < b$ shows that $L \ge 0$. Furthermore, observe that $(3.105)$ ensures that for all $\varepsilon \in (0, 1]$ it holds that

$$ 3L(b-a)\varepsilon^{-1} + 7 \le 3L(b-a)\varepsilon^{-1} + 7\varepsilon^{-1} = \mathfrak{C}\varepsilon^{-1}.\tag{3.106} $$

This and Corollary $\mathbf{3.3.5}$ imply that for all $\varepsilon \in (0, 1]$ there exists $\mathbf{F} \in \mathbf{N}$ such that

$$ \mathcal{R}^{\mathbf{N}}_{\mathfrak{t}}(\mathbf{F}) \in \mathcal{C}(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R}), \quad \sup_{x \in [a,b]}(|\mathcal{R}^{\mathbf{N}}_{\mathfrak{t}}(\mathbf{F})(x) - f(x)|) \le \varepsilon, \quad \mathcal{H}(\mathbf{F}) = 1,\tag{3.107} $$
$$ \|\mathcal{T}(\mathbf{F})\|_\infty \le \max\{1, |a|, |b|, 2L, |f(a)|\}, \quad \text{and} \quad \mathcal{P}(\mathbf{F}) \le 3L(b-a)\varepsilon^{-1} + 7 \le \mathfrak{C}\varepsilon^{-1}\tag{3.108} $$

(cf. Definitions $\mathbf{1.2.4}$, $\mathbf{1.3.1}$, $\mathbf{1.3.4}$, $\mathbf{1.3.5}$, and $\mathbf{3.3.4}$). The proof of Corollary $\mathbf{3.3.6}$ is thus complete. $\square$

**Corollary 3.3.7** (Implicit one-dimensional ANN approximations with prescribed error tolerances and asymptotic parameter bounds). Let $L, a \in \mathbb{R}$, $b \in [a, \infty)$ and let $f: [a, b] \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $x, y \in [a, b]$ that

$$ |f(x) - f(y)| \leq L|x - y|. \quad \tag{3.109} $$

Then there exist $\mathfrak{C} \in \mathbb{R}$ such that for all $\varepsilon \in (0, 1]$ there exists $\mathbf{F} \in \mathbf{N}$ such that

$$ \mathcal{R}_{\tau}^{\mathbf{N}}(\mathbf{F}) \in C(\mathbb{R}, \mathbb{R}), \quad \sup_{x \in [a, b]} \big| (\mathcal{R}_{\tau}^{\mathbf{N}}(\mathbf{F}))(x) - f(x) \big| \leq \varepsilon, \quad \text{and} \quad \mathcal{P}(\mathbf{F}) \leq \mathfrak{C}\varepsilon^{-1}. \tag{3.110} $$

(cf. Definitions 1.2.4, 1.3.1, and 1.3.4).

Proof of Corollary 3.3.7. Note that Corollary 3.3.6 establishes (3.110). The proof of Corollary 3.3.7 is thus complete. $\square$
