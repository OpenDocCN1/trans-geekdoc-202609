### Lemma 7.2.11 (Lower bound for the SGD optimization method)

Let $\mathfrak{D} \in \mathbb{N}$, $(\gamma_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq (0, \infty)$, $(J_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq \mathbb{N}$, let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \bb{P})$ be a probability space, let $\xi: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}}$ be a random variable, let $X_{n,j}: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}}$, $j \in \{1, 2, \ldots, J_n\}$, $n \in \mathbb{N}$, be i.i.d. random variables with $\bb{E}\bigl[\|X_{1,1}\|_2\bigr] < \infty$, assume that $\xi$ and $(X_{n,j})_{(n,j) \in \{(k, \ell) \in \mathbb{N}^2: \ell \leq J_k\}}$ are independent, let $\ell = (\ell(\theta, x))_{(\theta, x) \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}} \times \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}}}: \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}} \times \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}} \to \mathbb{R}$ satisfy for all $\theta, x \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}}$ that

$\ell(\theta, x) = \frac{1}{2}\|\theta - x\|_2^2,$ (7.59)

and let $\Theta: \mathbb{N}_0 \times \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}}$ be the stochastic process which satisfies for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ that

$\Theta_0 = \xi$ 
and 
$\Theta_n = \Theta_{n-1} - \gamma_n \left[\frac{1}{J_n} \sum_{j=1}^{J_n} (\nabla_\theta \ell^\epsilon)(\Theta_{n-1}, X_{n,j})\right].$ (7.60)

Then

$\bb{E}\left[\left\|\nabla_{\theta}\ell\right)(\theta,X_{1,1})\right\|_{2}\right]<\infty,$

(ii) it holds for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}}$ that

$\bb{E}\left[\left\|\left(\nabla_{\theta}\ell\right)(\theta,X_{1,1})-\bb{E}\left[\left(\nabla_{\theta}\ell\right)(\theta,X_{1,1})\right]\right\|_{2}^{2}\right]=\bb{E}\left[\left\|X_{1,1}-\bb{E}\left[X_{1,1}\right]\right\|_{2}^{2}\right],$

The assumption that $\bb{E}\left[\left\|X_{1,1}\right\|_{2}\right]<\infty$ hence implies that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{D}}$ it holds that

### 7.2.2.3 Non-convergence of GD for summable learning rates

In the next auxiliary result, Lemma 7.2.12 below, we recall a well known lower bound for the natural logarithm.

### Lemma 7.2.12 (A lower bound for the natural logarithm)

It holds for all $x \in(0, \infty)$ that

$\ln(x) \geq \frac{(x-1)}{x}.$

**Proof of Lemma 7.2.12**. First, observe that the fundamental theorem of calculus ensures that for all $x \in [1, \infty)$ it holds that

$\ln(x) = \ln(x) - \ln(1) = \int_{1}^{x} \frac{1}{t} \mathrm{d}t \geq \int_{1}^{x} \frac{1}{x} \mathrm{d}t = \frac{(x - 1)}{x}$. (7.68)

Furthermore, note that the fundamental theorem of calculus shows that for all $x \in (0, 1]$ it holds that

$\begin{aligned} \ln(x) = \ln(x) - \ln(1) = -(\ln(1) - \ln(x)) &= -\left[ \int_{x}^{1} \frac{1}{t} \mathrm{d}t \right] \\ &= \int_{x}^{1} \left(-\frac{1}{t}\right) \mathrm{d}t \geq \int_{x}^{1} \left(-\frac{1}{x}\right) \mathrm{d}t = (1 - x)\left(-\frac{1}{x}\right) = \frac{(x - 1)}{x}. \end{aligned}$ (7.69)

This and (7.68) prove (7.67). The proof of Lemma 7.2.12 is thus complete. $\square$

### Lemma 7.2.13 (GD fails to converge for a summable sequence of learning rates)

Let $\vartheta \in \mathbb{N}$, $\vartheta' \in \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta}$, $\xi \in \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta} \setminus \{\vartheta\}$, $\alpha \in (0, \infty)$, $(\gamma_n)_{n \in \mathbb{N}} \subseteq [0, \infty) \setminus \{1/\alpha\}$ *satisfy* $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \gamma_n < \infty$, let $\mathscr{L} : \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta} \to \mathbb{R}$ *satisfy for all* $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta}$ *that*

$\mathscr{L}(\vartheta) = \frac{\alpha}{2}\|\vartheta - \vartheta\|_2^2$, (7.70)

*and let* $\Theta : \mathbb{N}_0 \to \mathbb{R}^{\vartheta}$ *satisfy for all* $n \in \mathbb{N}$ *that* $\Theta_0 = \xi$ *and*

$\Theta_n = \Theta_{n-1} - \gamma_n (\nabla \mathscr{L})(\Theta_{n-1})$. (7.71)

*Then*

-   (i) *it holds for all* $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ *that*

$\Theta_n - \vartheta = \left[ \prod_{k=1}^{n} (1 - \gamma_k \alpha) \right] (\xi - \vartheta)$, (7.72)

-   (ii) *it holds that*

$\liminf_{n \to \infty} \left[ \prod_{k=1}^{n} |1 - \gamma_k \alpha| \right] > 0$, (7.73)

-   (iii) *it holds that*

$\liminf_{n \to \infty} \| \Theta_n - \vartheta \|_2 > 0$. (7.74)

**Proof of Lemma 7.2.13.** Throughout this proof, let $m \in \mathbb{N}$ satisfy for all $k \in \mathbb{N} \cap [m, \infty)$ that $\gamma_k < 1/(2\alpha)$. Observe that Lemma 5.6.4 implies that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^2$ it holds that

$$(\nabla\mathcal{L})(\theta) = \alpha\left(\frac{n}{2}(\theta - \vartheta)\right) = \alpha(\theta - \vartheta).$$

Therefore, we obtain for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ that

$$\begin{aligned} \Theta_n - \vartheta &= \Theta_{n-1} - \gamma_n(\nabla\mathcal{L})(\Theta_{n-1}) - \vartheta \\ &= \Theta_{n-1} - \gamma_n\alpha(\Theta_{n-1} - \vartheta) - \vartheta \\ &= (1 - \gamma_n\alpha)(\Theta_{n-1} - \vartheta). \end{aligned}$$

Induction hence demonstrates that for all $n \in \mathbb{N}$ it holds that

$$\Theta_n - \vartheta = \left[\prod_{k=1}^{n}\left(1 - \gamma_k\alpha\right)\right](\Theta_0 - \vartheta),$$

This and the assumption that $\Theta_0 = \xi$ establish item **(i)**. Note that the fact that for all $k \in \mathbb{N}$ it holds that $\gamma_k\alpha \neq 1$ ensures that

$$\prod_{k=1}^{m-1}[1 - \gamma_k\alpha] > 0.$$

Moreover, note that the fact that for all $k \in \mathbb{N} \cap [m, \infty)$ it holds that $\gamma_k\alpha \in [0, 1/2)$ assures that for all $k \in \mathbb{N} \cap [m, \infty)$ it holds that

$$(1 - \gamma_k\alpha) \in (1/2, 1].$$

This, Lemma 7.2.12, and the assumption that $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\gamma_n < \infty$ show that for all $n \in \mathbb{N} \cap [m, \infty)$ it holds that

$$\begin{aligned} &\ln\left(\prod_{k=m}^{n}|1 - \gamma_k\alpha|\right) = \sum_{k=m}^{n}\ln(1 - \gamma_k\alpha) \\ &\geq \sum_{k=m}^{n}\frac{(1 - \gamma_k\alpha) - 1}{(1 - \gamma_k\alpha)} = \sum_{k=m}^{n}\left[-\frac{\gamma_k\alpha}{(1 - \gamma_k\alpha)}\right] \\ &\geq \sum_{k=m}^{n}\left[-\frac{\gamma_k\alpha}{(\frac{1}{2})}\right] = -2\alpha\left[\sum_{k=m}^{n}\gamma_k\right] \geq -2\alpha\left[\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\gamma_k\right] > -\infty. \end{aligned}$$

Combining this with (7.78) proves that for all $n \in \mathbb{N} \cap [m, \infty)$ it holds that

$$\begin{aligned} \prod_{k=1}^{n}|1 - \gamma_k\alpha| &= \left[\prod_{k=1}^{m-1}|1 - \gamma_k\alpha|\right]\exp\left(\ln\left(\prod_{k=m}^{n}|1 - \gamma_k\alpha|\right)\right) \\ &\geq \left[\prod_{k=1}^{m-1}|1 - \gamma_k\alpha|\right]\exp\left(-2\alpha\left[\sum_{k=1}^{\infty}\gamma_k\right]\right) > 0. \end{aligned}$$
