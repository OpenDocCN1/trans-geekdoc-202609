This and \((7.124)\) establish that for all \( n \in \mathbb { N }\) it holds that

```
\begin{align*} \Psi _ {n} & = \Psi _ {n-1} - \frac {\delta _ {n+1}\alpha _ {n+1}(1-\alpha _ {n})\boldsymbol {m} _ {n}}{1-\alpha _ {n+1}} - \delta _ {n} \left [ \frac {1}{J _ {n}}\sum _ {j = 1} ^ {J _ {n}} \boldsymbol { \mathcal { G }} \left( \Psi _ {n-1}, X _ {n, j} \right) \right] \\ & = \Psi _ {n-1} - \delta _ {n+1} \beta _ {n+1} \boldsymbol {m} _ {n} - \delta _ {n} \left [ \frac {1}{J _ {n}}\sum _ {j = 1} ^ {J _ {n}} \boldsymbol { \mathcal { G }} \left( \Psi _ {n-1}, X _ {n, j} \right) \right].\end{align*}
```

Combining this with \((7.121)\), \((7.125)\), and \((7.130)\) proves item \((ii)\). The proof of Lemma \(7.5.2\) is thus complete.

# Definition 7.5.3 (Simplified Nesterov accelerated SGD optimization method). Let \(\boldsymbol {d}\) \( \in \mathbb {N}\), \((\gamma _ {n})_{n \in \mathbb {N}} \subseteq [0, \infty)\), \((J _ {n})_{n \in \mathbb {N}} \subseteq \mathbb {N}\), \((\alpha _ {n})_{n \in \mathbb {N}} \subseteq [0, \infty)\), let \((\Omega, \mathcal { F}, \mathbb {P})\) be a probability space, let \((S, \mathcal { S })\) be a measurable space, let \(\xi: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb {R} ^ {\mathbb {d}}\) be a random variable, for every \(n \in \mathbb {N}\), \(j \in\) \({1, 2, \(\cdots\), \(J_{n}\)}, let \(X_{n,j}: \Omega \rightarrow S\) be a random variable, and let \(\mathcal {L} = (\ell (\theta, x))_{(\theta, x) \in \mathbb {R}^{\mathbb{d}} \times \mathbb{S}}: \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{d}} \times S \rightarrow \mathbb{R}\) and \(\boldsymbol {g} = (\boldsymbol {g}_{1}, \cdots, \boldsymbol {g}_{\mathbb{d}}): \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{d}} \times S \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{d}}\) satisfy for all \(x \in S\), \(\theta \in \{v \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{d}}: \ell (\theta, x)\) is differentiable at \(v\}\) that

```
\( \boldsymbol {g} (\theta, x) = (\nabla _ {\boldsymbol {\theta}} \ell) (\theta, x).\)   (7.133)
```

Then we say that \(\Theta\) is the simplified Nesterov accelerated SGD process on \(((\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P}), (S, \mathcal{S}))\) for the loss function \(\ell\) with generalized gradient \(\boldsymbol{g}\), learning rates \((\gamma_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\), batch sizes \((J_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\), momentum decay factors \((\alpha_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\), initial value \(\xi\), and data \((X_{n,j})_{(n,j) \in \{ (k,l) \in \mathbb{N}^{2}: l \leqslant J_{k} \}}\) (we say that \(\Theta\) is the simplified Nesterov accelerated SGD process for the loss function \(\ell\) with learning rates \((\gamma_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\), batch sizes \((J_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\), momentum decay rates \((\alpha_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\), initial value \(\xi\), and data \((X_{n,j})_{(n,j) \in \{ (k,l) \in \mathbb{N}^{2}: l \leqslant J_{k} \}}\) if and only if \(\Theta: \mathbb{N}_{0} \times \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{d}}\) is the function from \(\mathbb{N}_{0} \times \Omega\) to \(\mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{d}}\) which satisfies that there exists \(\boldsymbol{m}: \mathbb{N}_{0} \times \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{\mathbb{d}}\) such that for all \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) it holds that

```
\(\Theta _ {0} = \xi, \quad \boldsymbol {m} _ {0} = \mathbf {0},\)   (7.134)  \( \boldsymbol {m} _ {n} = \alpha _ {n} \boldsymbol {m} _ {n-1} + \frac {1}{J _ {n}} \sum _ {j = 1} ^ {J _ {n}} \boldsymbol {g} \left( \Theta _ {n-1}, X _ {n, j} \right), \)   (7.135)  and \(\Theta _ {n} = \Theta _ {n-1} - \gamma _ {n} \alpha _ {n} \boldsymbol {m} _ {n} - \gamma _ {n} \left[ \frac {1}{J _ {n}} \sum _ {j = 1} ^ {J _ {n}} \boldsymbol {g} \left( \Theta _ {n-1}, X _ {n, j} \right) \right].\)   (7.136)
```

The simplified Nesterov accelerated SGD optimization method as described in Definition \(7.5.3\) is implemented in PyTorch in the form of the torch.optim.SGD optimizer with the `nesterov=True` option.
