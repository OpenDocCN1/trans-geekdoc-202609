
*Proof of Proposition 9.11.4.* Observe that (9.166) implies that $[0, \infty) \ni t \mapsto \mathscr{L}(\Theta_t) \in \mathbb{R}$ is non-increasing. Therefore, we obtain that there exists $\mathbf{m} \in [-\infty, \infty)$ which satisfies

$$
\mathbf{m}=\operatorname*{lim sup}_{t \rightarrow \infty} \mathscr{L}\left(\Theta_t\right)=\operatorname*{lim inf}_{t \rightarrow \infty} \mathscr{L}\left(\Theta_t\right)=\operatorname*{inf}_{t \in[0, \infty)} \mathscr{L}(\Theta_t) . \quad (9.168)
$$

Furthermore, note that the assumption that $\lim \inf _{t \rightarrow \infty}\left\|\Theta_t\right\|_2<\infty$ demonstrates that there exist $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^9$ and $\delta=\left(\delta_n\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}: \mathbb{N} \rightarrow[0, \infty)$ which satisfy

$$
\lim \inf _{n \rightarrow \infty} \delta_n=\infty \quad \text { and } \quad \lim _{n \rightarrow \infty}\left\|\Theta_{\delta_n}-\vartheta\right\|_2=0 . \quad (9.169)
$$

Observe that (9.168), (9.169), and the fact that $\mathscr{L}$ is continuous ensure that

$$
\mathscr{L}(\vartheta)=\mathbf{m} \in \mathbb{R} \quad \text { and } \quad \forall t \in[0, \infty): \mathscr{L}\left(\Theta_t\right) \geq \mathscr{L}(\vartheta) . \quad (9.170)
$$

Next let $\varepsilon, \mathbf{c} \in(0, \infty), \alpha \in(0,1)$ satisfy for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^9$ with $\|\theta-\vartheta\|_2<\varepsilon$ that

$$
\left|\mathscr{L}(\theta)-\mathscr{L}(\vartheta)\right|^\alpha \leq \mathbf{c}\left\|\mathscr{G}(\theta)\right\|_2 . \quad (9.171)
$$

Note that (9.169) and the fact that $\mathscr{L}$ is continuous demonstrate that there exist $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\mathbf{c} \in[0,1]$ which satisfy

$$
\mathbf{c}=\left|\mathscr{L}\left(\Theta_{\delta_n}\right)-\mathscr{L}(\vartheta)\right| \quad \text { and } \quad \mathbf{c}\left(1-\alpha\right)^{-1} \mathbf{c}^{1-\alpha}+\left\|\Theta_{\delta_n}-\vartheta\right\|_2<\varepsilon . \quad (9.172)
$$

Next let $\Phi: [0, \infty) \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^9$ satisfy for all $t \in[0, \infty)$ that

$$
\Phi_t=\Theta_{\delta_n+t} . \quad (9.173)
$$

Observe that (9.166), (9.170), and (9.173) establish that for all $t \in[0, \infty)$ it holds that

$$
\begin{gathered}
\mathscr{L}\left(\Phi_t\right)=\mathscr{L}\left(\Phi_0\right)-\int_0^t\left\|\mathscr{G}\left(\Phi_s\right)\right\|_2^2 \mathrm{~d} s, \quad \Phi_t=\Phi_0-\int_0^t \mathscr{G}\left(\Phi_s\right) \mathrm{d} s, \\
\text { and } \mathscr{L}\left(\Phi_t\right) \geq \mathscr{L}(\vartheta) .
\end{gathered} \quad (9.174)
$$

Combining this with (9.171), (9.172), (9.173), and Corollary 9.11.3 (applied with $\Theta \wedge \Phi$ in the notation of Corollary 9.11.3) establishes that there exists $\psi \in \mathbb{R}^9$ which satisfies for all $t \in[0, \infty)$ that

$$
0 \leq \mathscr{L}\left(\Phi_t\right)-\mathscr{L}(\psi) \leq\left(1+\mathbf{c}^{-2} t\right)^{-1}, \quad \left\|\Phi_t-\psi\right\|_2 \leq \mathbf{c}(1-\alpha)^{-1}\left(1+\mathbf{c}^{-2} t\right)^{\alpha-1}, \quad (9.175)
$$

and $\mathscr{L}(\psi)=\mathscr{L}(\vartheta)$. Note that (9.173) and (9.175) show for all $t \in[0, \infty)$ that $0 \leq$ $\mathscr{L}\left(\Theta_{\delta_n+t}\right)-\mathscr{L}(\psi) \leq\left(1+\mathbf{c}^{-2} t\right)^{-1}$ and $\left\|\Theta_{\delta_n+t}-\psi\right\|_2 \leq \mathbf{c}(1-\alpha)^{-1}\left(1+\mathbf{c}^{-2} t\right)^{\alpha-1}$. Hence, we obtain for all $\tau \in\left[\delta_n, \infty\right), t \in[\tau, \infty)$ that

$$
\begin{gathered}
0 \leq \mathscr{L}\left(\Theta_t\right)-\mathscr{L}(\psi) \leq\left(1+\mathbf{c}^{-2}\left(t-\delta_n\right)\right)^{-1}=\left(1+\mathbf{c}^{-2}(t-\tau)+\mathbf{c}^{-2}\left(\tau-\delta_n\right)\right)^{-1} \\
\leq\left(1+\mathbf{c}^{-2}(t-\tau)\right)^{-1}
\end{gathered} \quad (9.176)
$$

and

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left\|\Theta_{t} - \psi\right\|_{2} &\leq \mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}(1 + \mathfrak{C}^{-2}(t - \delta_{n}))^{\alpha - 1} \\
&= \left[\left[\mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\right]^{\frac{1}{\alpha - 1}}(1 + \mathfrak{C}^{-2}(t - \delta_{n}))\right]^{\alpha - 1} \\
&= \left[\left[\mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\right]^{\frac{1}{\alpha - 1}}\left[1 + \mathfrak{C}^{-2}(\tau - \delta_{n})\right] + \left[\left(\mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\right]^{\frac{1}{1 - \alpha}}\mathfrak{C}^{2}\right]^{-1}(t - \tau)\right]^{\alpha - 1} .
\end{aligned} \quad (9.177)
$$

Next let $\mathfrak{C}$, $\tau \in (0, \infty)$ satisfy

$$
\mathfrak{C} = \max\{\mathfrak{C}^{2}, \left[\mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\right]^{\frac{1}{1 - \alpha}}\mathfrak{C}^{2}\} \quad \text{and} \quad \tau = \delta_{n} + \mathfrak{C}^{2}\left[\mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\right]^{\frac{1}{1 - \alpha}} . \quad (9.178)
$$

Observe that (9.176), (9.177), and (9.178) demonstrate for all $t \in [\tau, \infty)$ that

$$
0 \leq \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) - \mathcal{L}(\psi) \leq (1 + \mathfrak{C}^{-2}(t - \tau))^{-1} \leq (1 + \mathfrak{C}^{-1}(t - \tau))^{-1} \quad (9.179)
$$

and

$$
\begin{aligned}
\left\|\Theta_{t} - \psi\right\|_{2} &\leq \left[\left[\mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\right]^{\frac{1}{\alpha - 1}}\left[1 + \mathfrak{C}^{-2}(\tau - \delta_{n})\right] + \mathfrak{C}^{-1}(t - \tau)\right]^{\alpha - 1} \\
&= \left[\left[\mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\right]^{\frac{1}{\alpha - 1}}\left[1 + \left[\mathfrak{C}(1 - \alpha)^{-1}\right]^{\frac{1}{1 - \alpha}}\right] + \mathfrak{C}^{-1}(t - \tau)\right]^{\alpha - 1} \\
&\leq \left[1 + \mathfrak{C}^{-1}(t - \tau)\right]^{\alpha - 1} .
\end{aligned} \quad (9.180)
$$

The proof of Proposition 9.11.4 is thus complete.

### **Corollary 9.11.5.**

Let $\varrho \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Theta \in C([0, \infty), \mathbb{R}^{\varrho})$, $\mathcal{L} \in C(\mathbb{R}^{\varrho}, \mathbb{R})$, let $\mathfrak{G}: \mathbb{R}^{\varrho} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}$ be $\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^{\varrho})/\mathcal{B}(\mathbb{R}^{\varrho})$-measurable, assume that for all $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}$ there exist $\varepsilon, \mathfrak{C} \in (0, \infty)$, $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ such that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}$ with $\|\theta - \vartheta\|_{2} < \varepsilon$ it holds that

$$
|\mathcal{L}(\theta) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta)|^{\alpha} \leq \mathfrak{C}\|\mathfrak{G}(\vartheta)\|_{2}, \quad (9.181)
$$

assumes for all $t \in [0, \infty)$ that

$$
\mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) = \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{0}) - \int_{0}^{t}\|\mathfrak{G}(\Theta_{s})\|_{2}^{2}\mathrm{d}s \quad \text{and} \quad \Theta_{t} = \Theta_{0} - \int_{0}^{t}\mathfrak{G}(\Theta_{s})\mathrm{d}s, \quad (9.182)
$$

and assume $\liminf_{t \rightarrow \infty}\|\Theta_{t}\|_{2} < \infty$ (cf. Definition 3.3.4). Then there exist $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\varrho}$, $\mathfrak{C}$, $\beta \in (0, \infty)$ which satisfy for all $t \in [0, \infty)$ that

$$
\|\Theta_{t} - \vartheta\|_{2} \leq \mathfrak{C}(1 + t)^{-\beta} \quad \text{and} \quad 0 \leq \mathcal{L}(\Theta_{t}) - \mathcal{L}(\vartheta) \leq \mathfrak{C}(1 + t)^{-1} . \quad (9.183)
$$

*Proof of Corollary 9.11.5.* Note that Proposition 9.11.4 demonstrates that there exist $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$, $\mathfrak{C}, \tau, \beta \in (0, \infty)$ which satisfy for all $t \in [\tau, \infty)$ that

$$
\|\Theta_{t}-\vartheta\|_{2} \leq\left(1+\mathfrak{C}(t-\tau)\right)^{-\beta}\quad \mathrm{and} \quad 0 \leq \mathcal{L}\left(\Theta_{t}\right)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta) \leq\left(1+\mathfrak{C}(t-\tau)\right)^{-1}. \quad (9.184)
$$

In the following let $\mathcal{K} \in (0, \infty)$ satisfy

$$
\mathcal{K}=\max \left\{1+\tau,\left(1+\tau\right)^{\beta}, \mathfrak{C} \tau^{-1}, \mathfrak{C} \tau^{-\beta},\left(1+\tau\right)^{\beta}\left(\sup _{s \in[0, \tau]}\left\|\Theta_{s}-\vartheta\right\|_{2}\right),(1+\tau)\left(\mathcal{L}\left(\Theta_{0}\right)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta)\right)\right\}. \quad (9.185)
$$

Observe that (9.184), (9.185), and the fact that $[0, \infty) \ni t \mapsto \mathcal{L}\left(\Theta_{t}\right) \in \mathbb{R}$ is non-increasing prove for all $t \in[0, \tau]$ that

$$
\|\Theta_{t}-\vartheta\|_{2} \leq \sup _{s \in[0, \tau]}\left\|\Theta_{s}-\vartheta\right\|_{2} \leq \mathcal{K}\left(1+\tau\right)^{-\beta} \leq \mathcal{K}\left(1+t\right)^{-\beta} \quad (9.186)
$$

and

$$
0 \leq \mathcal{L}\left(\Theta_{t}\right)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta) \leq \mathcal{L}\left(\Theta_{0}\right)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta) \leq \mathcal{K}\left(1+\tau^{-1}\right) \leq \mathcal{K}\left(1+t^{-1}\right). \quad (9.187)
$$

Furthermore, note that (9.184) and (9.185) imply for all $t \in[\tau, \infty)$ that

$$
\begin{gathered}
\|\Theta_{t}-\vartheta\|_{2} \leq\left(1+\mathfrak{C}(t-\tau)\right)^{-\beta}=\mathcal{K}\left(\mathfrak{C}^{1 / \beta}+\mathfrak{C}^{1 / \beta} \mathfrak{C}(t-\tau)\right)^{-\beta} \\
\leq \mathcal{K}\left(\mathfrak{C}^{1 / \beta}+t-\tau\right)^{-\beta} \leq \mathcal{K}\left(1+t\right)^{-\beta} .
\end{gathered} \quad (9.188)
$$

Moreover, observe that (9.184) and (9.185) demonstrate for all $t \in[\tau, \infty)$ that

$$
0 \leq \mathcal{L}\left(\Theta_{t}\right)-\mathcal{L}(\vartheta) \leq \mathcal{K}\left(\mathcal{K}+\mathfrak{C} \mathfrak{C}(t-\tau)\right)^{-1} \leq \mathcal{K}\left(\mathcal{K}-\tau+t\right)^{-1} \leq \mathcal{K}\left(1+t^{-1}\right). \quad (9.189)
$$

The proof of Corollary 9.11.5 is thus complete. $\square$

### **Corollary 9.11.6.**

*Let* $\mathfrak{d} \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Theta \in C([0, \infty), \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}})$, $\mathcal{L} \in C^{1}\left(\mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}, \mathbb{R}\right)$, *assume that for all* $\vartheta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ *there exist* $\varepsilon, \mathfrak{C} \in (0, \infty)$, $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ *such that for all* $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^{\mathfrak{d}}$ *with* $\|\theta-\vartheta\|_{2} < \varepsilon$ *it holds that*
