### Corollary 12.3.10.
Let $\mathfrak{d}, M, L \in \mathbb{N}$, $u \in \mathbb{R}$, $v \in (u, \infty)$, $R \in [1, \infty)$, $\varepsilon, b \in (0, \infty)$, $l=(l_{0}, l_{1}, \ldots, l_{L}) \in \mathbb{N}^{L+1}$ satisfy $l_{L}=1$ and $\sum_{k=1}^{L} l_{k}(l_{k-1}+1) \leq \mathfrak{d}$, let $D \subset [-b, b]^{0}$ be a compact set, let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ be a probability space, let $X_{m}: \Omega \rightarrow D$, $m \in\{1,2,\ldots, M\}$, and $Y_{m}: \Omega \rightarrow [u, v]$, $m \in\{1,2,\ldots, M\}$, be functions, assume that $(X_{m}, Y_{m})$, $m \in\{1,2,\ldots, M\}$, are i.i.d. random variables, let $\mathcal{E}: C(D, \mathbb{R}) \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $f \in C(D, \mathbb{R})$ that $\mathcal{E}(f)=\mathbb{E}[f(X_{1})-Y_{1}]^{2}$, and let $\mathfrak{C}:[-R, R]^{\mathfrak{d}} \times \Omega \rightarrow [0, \infty)$ satisfy for all $\theta \in [-R, R]^{\mathfrak{d}}$, $\omega \in \Omega$ that

$$\mathfrak{C}(\theta, \omega)=\frac{1}{M}\left[\sum_{m=1}^{M}\left|\mathcal{K}_{u, v}^{\theta, l}\left(X_{m}(\omega)\right)-Y_{m}(\omega)\right|^{2}\right]$$

(cf. Definition 4.4.1). Then

- (i) it holds that $\Omega \ni \omega \mapsto \sup _{\theta \in[-R, R]^{\mathfrak{d}}} \left|\mathfrak{C}(\theta, \omega)-\mathcal{E}\left(\mathcal{K}_{u, v}^{\theta, l} |D\right)\right| \in [0, \infty)$ is $\mathcal{F}/\mathcal{B}([0, \infty])$-measurable and
- (ii) it holds that

$$\mathbb{P}\left(\sup _{\theta \in[-R, R]^{\mathfrak{d}}} \left|\mathfrak{C}(\theta)-\mathcal{E}\left(\mathcal{K}_{u, v}^{\theta, l} |D\right)\right| \geq \varepsilon\right) \leq 2 \max \left\{1,\left[\frac{16 L \max \left\{1, b\right\}\left(\|l\|_{\infty}+1\right)^{L} R^{L}\left(v-u\right)}{\varepsilon}\right]\right\} \exp \left(\frac{-\varepsilon^{2} M}{2(v-u)^{4}}\right) .$$

Proof of Corollary 12.3.10. Throughout this proof, let $\mathfrak{L} \in(0, \infty)$ satisfy

$$\mathfrak{L}=L \max \left\{1, b\right\}\left(\|l\|_{\infty}+1\right)^{L} R^{L-1} .$$

Observe that Corollary 11.3.7 (applied with $a \hookrightarrow -b$, $b \hookrightarrow b$, $u \hookrightarrow u$, $v \hookrightarrow v$, $\mathfrak{d} \hookrightarrow \mathfrak{d}$, $L \hookrightarrow L$, $l \hookrightarrow l$ in the notation of Corollary 11.3.7) and the assumption that $D \subset [-b, b]^{0}$ show that for all $\theta, \vartheta \in [-R, R]^{\mathfrak{d}}$ it holds that

$$\begin{aligned}\sup _{x \in D}\left|\mathcal{K}_{u, v}^{\theta, l}(x)-\mathcal{K}_{u, v}^{\vartheta, l}(x)\right| & \\
\leq \sup _{x \in [-b, b]^{0}}\left|\mathcal{K}_{u, v}^{\theta, l}(x)-\mathcal{K}_{u, v}^{\vartheta, l}(x)\right| & \\
\leq L \max \left\{1, b\right\}\left(\|l\|_{\infty}+1\right)^{L}\left(\max \left\{1,\|\theta\|_{\infty},\|\vartheta\|_{\infty}\right\}\right)^{L-1}\|\theta-\vartheta\|_{\infty} & \\
\leq L \max \left\{1, b\right\}\left(\|l\|_{\infty}+1\right)^{L} R^{L-1}\|\theta-\vartheta\|_{\infty}=\mathfrak{L}\|\theta-\vartheta\|_{\infty} .\end{aligned}$$

Furthermore, observe that the fact that for all $\theta \in \mathbb{R}^3$, $x \in \mathbb{R}^{\ell_0}$ it holds that $\mathcal{N}^{\theta, \ell}_{u, v}(x) \in [u, v]$ and the assumption that for all $m \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}$, $\omega \in \Omega$ it holds that $Y_m(\omega) \in [u, v]$ demonstrate that for all $\theta \in [-R, R]^3$, $m \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}$ it holds that

$$\left|\mathcal{N}_{u, v}^{\theta, \ell}(X_m) - Y_m\right| \leq v - u. \quad (12.180)$$

Combining this and (12.179) with Proposition 12.3.9 (applied with $d \hookrightarrow \ell_0$, $\mathfrak{d} \hookrightarrow \mathfrak{d}$, $M \hookrightarrow M$, $R \hookrightarrow R$, $L \hookrightarrow \mathfrak{L}$, $\mathcal{R} \hookrightarrow v-u$, $\mathfrak{e} \hookrightarrow \varepsilon$, $D \hookrightarrow D$, $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P}) \hookrightarrow (\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$, $(X_m)_{m \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}} \hookrightarrow (X_m)_{m \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}}$, $(Y_m)_{m \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}} \hookrightarrow ((\Omega \ni \omega \mapsto Y_m(\omega) \in \mathbb{R}))_{m \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}}$, $\mathcal{H} \hookrightarrow ([-R, R]^3 \ni \theta \mapsto \mathcal{N}_{u, v}^{\theta, \ell}|_D \in C(D, \mathbb{R}))$, $\mathcal{E} \hookrightarrow \mathcal{E}$, $\mathfrak{E} \hookrightarrow \mathfrak{E}$ in the notation of Proposition 12.3.9) establishes that $\Omega \ni \omega \mapsto \sup_{\theta \in [-R, R]^3} |\mathfrak{E}(\theta, \omega) - \mathcal{E}(\mathcal{N}_{u, v}^{\theta, \ell}|_D)| \in [0, \infty]$ is $\mathcal{F}/\mathcal{B}([0, \infty])$-measurable and

$$\mathbb{P}(\sup_{\theta \in [-R, R]^3} |\mathfrak{E}(\theta) - \mathcal{E}(\mathcal{N}_{u, v}^{\theta, \ell}|_D)| \geq \varepsilon) \leq 2 \max \left\{1, \left[\frac{16 \mathfrak{L} R (v - u)}{\varepsilon}\right]^\mathfrak{d}\right\} \exp \left(\frac{-\varepsilon^2 M}{2(v - u)^2}\right). \quad (12.181)$$

The proof of Corollary 12.3.10 is thus complete. $\square$

**Proposition 13.1.1.** Let $d, M \in \mathbb{N}$, let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \mathbb{P})$ be a probability space, let $X_j: \Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^d$, $j \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}$, be independent random variables, and assume $\max_{j \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}} \mathbb{E}[\|X_j\|_2] < \infty$ (cf. Definition 3.3.4). Then

$$\left(\mathbb{E}\left[\left\|\frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} X_j - \mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} X_j\right]\right\|_2^2\right]\right)^{1/2} \leq \frac{1}{\sqrt{M}}\left[\max_{j \in \{1, 2, \dots, M\}} \left(\mathbb{E}\left[\|X_j - \mathbb{E}[X_j]\|_2^2\right]\right)^{1/2}\right]. \quad (13.1)$$

*Proof of Proposition 13.1.1.* Observe that the fact that for all $x \in \mathbb{R}^d$ it holds that $\langle x, x \rangle = \|x\|_2^2$ demonstrates that

$$\begin{aligned} & \left\| \frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} X_j - \mathbb{E}\left[ \frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} X_j \right] \right\|^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{M^2} \left\| \left[ \sum_{j=1}^{M} X_j \right] - \mathbb{E}\left[ \sum_{j=1}^{M} X_j \right] \right\|_2^2 \\ &= \frac{1}{M^2} \left\| \sum_{j=1}^{M} \left( X_j - \mathbb{E}[X_j] \right) \right\|_2^2 \end{aligned} \quad (13.2)$$

(cf. Definition 1.4.7). This, the fact that for all independent random variables $Y : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ and $Z : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$ with $\mathbb{E}[\|Y\|_2 + \|Z\|_2] < \infty$ it holds that $\mathbb{E}[\langle Y, Z \rangle] < \infty$ and $\mathbb{E}[\langle Y, Z \rangle] = \langle \mathbb{E}[Y], \mathbb{E}[Z] \rangle$, and the assumption that $X_j : \Omega \to \mathbb{R}^d$, $j \in \{1,2,\ldots,M\}$, are independent random variables establish that

$$\begin{aligned} & \mathbb{E}\left[ \left\| \frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} X_j - \mathbb{E}\left[ \frac{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} X_j \right] \right\|^2 \right] \\ &= \frac{1}{M^2} \sum_{j=1}^{M} \mathbb{E}\left[ \|X_j - \mathbb{E}[X_j]\|_2^2 \right] + \frac{1}{M^2} \left[ \sum_{(i,j) \in \{1,2,\ldots,M\}^2, \, i \neq j} \left\langle \mathbb{E}\left[X_i - \mathbb{E}[X_i]\right], \mathbb{E}\left[X_j - \mathbb{E}[X_j]\right] \right\rangle \right] \\ &= \frac{1}{M^2} \sum_{j=1}^{M} \mathbb{E}\left[ \|X_j - \mathbb{E}[X_j]\|_2^2 \right] \\ &\leq \frac{1}{M} \left[ \max_{j \in \{1,2,\ldots,M\}} \mathbb{E}\left[ \|X_j - \mathbb{E}[X_j]\|_2^2 \right] \right]. \end{aligned} \quad (13.3)$$

The proof of Proposition 13.1.1 is thus complete. $\square$
