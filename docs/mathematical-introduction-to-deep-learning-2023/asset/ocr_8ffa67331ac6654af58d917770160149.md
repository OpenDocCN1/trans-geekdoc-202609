Fubini's theorem (see, for example, Klenke [**248**, Theorem 14.16]) hence implies that for all $u \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})$ it holds that

$$\begin{split} \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - u(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x = \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x + \int_{[a, b]^d} |\mathbb{E}[X_x] - u(x)|^2 \, \mathrm{d}x . \end{split} \tag{17.6}$$

This ensures that

$$\begin{split} \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x \\ \geq \inf_{v \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})} \left(\int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - v(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x\right) \\ = \inf_{v \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})} \left(\int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x + \int_{[a, b]^d} |\mathbb{E}[X_x] - v(x)|^2 \, \mathrm{d}x\right) \end{split} \tag{17.7}$$

The assumption that $[a, b]^d \ni x \mapsto \mathbb{E}[X_x] \in \mathbb{R}$ is continuous therefore shows that

$$\begin{split} \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x \geq \inf_{v \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})} \left(\int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x\right) \end{split}$$

$$\begin{split} \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x \geq \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x . \end{split} \tag{17.8}$$

Hence, we obtain that

$$\begin{split} \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x = \inf_{v \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})} \left(\int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - v(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x\right) . \end{split} \tag{17.9}$$

The fact that the function $[a, b]^d \ni x \mapsto \mathbb{E}[X_x] \in \mathbb{R}$ is continuous therefore establishes that there exists $u \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})$ such that

$$\begin{split} \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - u(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x = \inf_{v \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})} \left(\int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - v(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x\right) . \end{split} \tag{17.10}$$

Furthermore, observe that (17.6) and (17.9) prove that for all $u \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})$ with

$$\begin{split} \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - u(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x = \inf_{v \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})} \left(\int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - v(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x\right) \end{split} \tag{17.11}$$

it holds that

$$\begin{split} \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x \\ = \inf_{v \in C([a, b]^d, \mathbb{R})} \left(\int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - v(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x\right) = \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - u(x)|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x \\ = \int_{[a, b]^d} \mathbb{E}[|X_x - \mathbb{E}[X_x]|^2] \, \mathrm{d}x + \int_{[a, b]^d} |\mathbb{E}[X_x] - u(x)|^2 \, \mathrm{d}x . \end{split} \tag{17.12}$$
