**Corollary 2.2.5 (Lower and upper bounds for the numbers of parameters of parallelizations of fully-connected feedforward ANNs.)** Let $n \in \mathbb{N}$, $\Phi = (\Phi_1, \dots, \Phi_n) \in \mathbb{N}^n$ satisfy $\mathcal{D}(\Phi_1) = \mathcal{D}(\Phi_2) = \dots = \mathcal{D}(\Phi_n)$ (cf. Definition 1.3.1). Then

$\begin{align*}
\Big[\frac{n^2}{2}\Big]\mathcal{P}(\Phi_1) \le \Big[\frac{n^2+n}{2}\Big]\mathcal{P}(\Phi_1) \le \mathcal{P}\big(\mathbf{P}_n(\Phi)\big) \le n^2\mathcal{P}(\Phi_1) \le \tfrac{1}{2}\Big[\sum_{i=1}^n \mathcal{P}(\Phi_i)\Big]^2 \tag{2.60}
\end{align*}$

(cf. Definition 2.2.1).
