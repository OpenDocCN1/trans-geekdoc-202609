# ## Chapter 11

# Optimization through random initializations

In addition to minimizing an objective function through iterative steps of an SGD-type optimization method, another approach to minimize an objective function is to sample different random initializations, to iteratively calculate SGD optimization processes starting at these random initializations, and, thereafter, to pick a SGD trajectory with the smallest final evaluation of the objective function. The approach to consider different random initializations is reviewed and analyzed within this chapter in detail. The specific presentation of this chapter is strongly based on Jentzen & Welti [230, Section 5].

$$
\int_{0}^{\infty} x \mu(\mathrm{d}x)=\int_{0}^{\infty} \mu([x, \infty)) \mathrm{d}x=\int_{0}^{\infty} \mu([x, \infty)) \mathrm{d}x.
$$
