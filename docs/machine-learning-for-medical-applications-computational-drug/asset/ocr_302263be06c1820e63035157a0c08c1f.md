Solve.Care: Solve.Care is the healthcare ecosystem deployed on blockchain to facilitate payments, administrative and care programs through apps and smart contracts. This way, the platform captures patient-related activities, treatment, and diagnostic procedures, as well as administrative transactions related to funding and reimbursement for EHR management. Take Care of Itself.Wallet application provides an interface to securely view and manage a patient's health records; providers can see the records and contribute toward the care plan in a “traceable” fashion. Solve.Care’s blockchain solution for EHR makes sharing, reconciliation, and verification of data more reliable and effective, supporting patient-centered outcome delivery and healthcare administrative practice.

Hu-manity.co: Hu-manity.co was built using blockchain technology that allows customers to own, control, and sell their health data. It employs the concept of blockchain whereby records made for patients’ consent and agreements made for sharing of data are sacrosanct and can be audited for accuracy and authentication of patients, data. #My31 app on Hu-manity.co plans on enabling users to reclaim their data control of health information and blockchain contracts ensure that third-party data users shall only access the data that users have granted permission for, through data usage compensation agreements on the online platform. Hu-monition’s EHR solution is developed on blockchain technology and allows patients to own personal health data and be engaged in data-driven projects while data remains trustworthy and safe.

Coral Health: Coral Health is a healthcare platform that uses blockchain in a way that enables patients, researchers, and healthcare providers securely and accurately share patient data. The platform achieves protection of data and non-repudiation by applying blockchain technology to construct a secure, tamper-proof registry of patient’s EHRS. By holding the rights to the records’ privacy settings and access control, patients consolidate and distribute their data to various parties with Coral Health’s EHR solution built on the blockchain. For this reason, Coral Health integrates blockchain to create transparency, security, and trusted data management in healthcare for collaboration in care delivery and research.

The practical use of blockchain-based EHRS for the purpose of guaranteeing data credibility and historical transportability in the sphere of healthcare is outlined. Thus, utilizing blockchain technology, these platforms offer their users clear, unalterable, and patient-centered solutions concerning the proper handling of EHR, facilitating the issues of interoperability, patient privacy, and trust.
