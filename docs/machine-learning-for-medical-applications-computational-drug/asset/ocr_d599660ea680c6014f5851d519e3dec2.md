One strength of precision health pertains to the sensitivity to patient characteristics that the administration of symptom-relieving measures allowed. Popular AI-enabled technologies and interfaces can process data from various sources, including genetic makeup, medical history, and current health status, and suggest the right course of treatment. In particular, Figure 22 reveals how strategies of personalized and precision health movements have embraced the way of medical treatments therapies for patients, and improved the overall result. For example, smart pills for drug delivery enable modifications in dosages and the timing of administered drugs based on high-frequency feedback from wearable devices that measure the amount of drug in circulation and the body’s physiological signs. Likewise, owing to the information documented from motion sensors and activity trackers, rehabilitation programs that would be individualized can be developed, based on the functional limitations of an individual, and the recovery process can be enhanced.

Integrated with biomedical sensors and AI algorithms, predictive analytics provides an essential function for precision health: the assessment of risk and disease progression. By comparing data with the current model of a human body and closely related biomarkers parameters’ increase/decrease rates, one can predict individuals with high probability of developing certain health conditions. This allows for timely interventions that include screening and changing of habits to reduce disease risks. Proper risk assessment utilizing Big data analytics allows for optimized distribution of resources as well as increasing the efficacy of the general utilization and prevention steps and therapies.
