HIPAA, and the European Union via GDPR, require data protection standards on security measures like access control and anonymization of data, among others, standards that have enforced the use of secure storage solutions. It can be mentioned that a proper access solution involves the use of multifactor authentication mechanisms and role-based access, which prevent exposure to unauthorized access to data.

Adherence to the principles of data privacy act help in defending information pertaining to health. Most countries have health data protection standards that bar any data use for purposes not mentioned in the consent form; the US has the HIPAA act and Europe, the European General Data Protection Regulation. Implementation measures are the use of privacy policies, periodic assessments, and staff sensitization on measures of protecting and securing data. Businesses also need to draw guidelines for handling data breaches, which consist of notice and response steps in the case of a data privacy violation.

The general aspects of biomedical sensors and devices require that the integrity and accuracy of biomedical data are preserved. Data integrity compromises stem from problems such as those related to software and data tampering. In order to obtain valid and accurate health data, high levels of validation and verification as well as system audits have to be incorporated. Further on, the use of mechanisms that record data through reverse logistics and track changes offer an audit trail that can be used when investigating and resolving data integrity issues.

A lot of biomedical sensors and devices place high dependence on third-party storage processing services. Third-party risks are considered as the risks involved with the evaluation of security and compliance of other affiliated partners. Business entities should legally bind the parties involved in handling of data and frequently conduct checks on the third-party service providers. It reduces the risks associated with data sharing and outsourcing because every third-party service provider has an obligation to observe common standard and follow regulatory standards.
