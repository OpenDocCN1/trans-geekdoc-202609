# 6.3.2 Use and application of LIME in material models

When it comes to systems that involve a huge quantity of data on materials, LIME can be employed across the range of material modeling and analysis. For example, when making predictions about material properties from experimental data using machine learning models, what LIME does is shed light on the features (say, composition, processing parameters) most important in determining the predictions for the particular materials. One application of this capability is that when the relationship between feature space and material properties is not apparent, this capability comes in handy. With local interpretations, LIME helps researchers improve material models, guaranteeing that the latter, in addition to being accurate, also aligns with domain knowledge and expectations.

# 6.3.3 Benefits of using LIME

LIME, when applied in material science, proves useful in the following ways. First, it improves model interpretability, helping researchers grasp how certain features affect model outcomes. This is important for model validation and to confirm that the models are built on first principles and physical laws. Second, LIME aids in diagnosing model problems and improving these issues, particularly when models rely on irrelevant features. Last but not least, it is especially useful for explaining model results to nonadvanced users, such as material engineers.

# 6.3.4 Challenges and limitations

Some challenges are associated with the application of LIME. One of the issues identified was the selection of the interpretable model used in approximation: the quality of the local explanation was contingent upon the choice of this model. Also, LIME does not provide explanations that span the model globally; instead, it is specific to the local area. As such, LIME is helpful as a method to explain the prediction for a given individual instance, but it should be incorporated with other interpretability heuristics to achieve a complete understanding of the said model. The performance of LIME can be affected by the quality and samples of the space perturbation used for the training of the local model.
