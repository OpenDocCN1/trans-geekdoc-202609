It also means embracing the role of personalized medicine in the broadly conceived preventive framework including infectious diseases. The identification and determination of pathogens genetic map work aids in evolving localized vaccines and an outbreak treatment plan that considers pathogen and host polymorphisms. The application of this approach increases the efficacy of immunization campaigns as well as the lack of ability to control the spread and occurrence of the disease.

Personalized medicine also complements the prevention of diseases, thanks to development in imaging and diagnostic techniques. AI and machine learning are applied in tumor diagnosis by analyzing structural changes and faint features in medical imaging information that depict early disease. Such technologies enhance the specificity of diagnosis and facilitate early treatment that halts disease advancement and multifactorial manifestations.

There are several barriers highlighted from the attempt to adopt personalized medicine in preventive health and early-stage diagnosis, these include; concerns in accessibility, data protected act, and lack of standard protocols. Solving the problem of availability and accessibility of individualized healthcare and solving organizational ethical issues concerning genetics are significant for making use of these approaches more common.
