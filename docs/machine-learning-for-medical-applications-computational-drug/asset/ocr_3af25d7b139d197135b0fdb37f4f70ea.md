Potential areas for research and development in the use of Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) for predicting material properties focus primarily on enhancing model generalization and interpretability. Techniques such as transfer learning and domain adaptation are pivotal in improving CNN performance across diverse material datasets. Combining CNNs with other machine learning strategies, such as ensemble methods, has shown to significantly increase predictive accuracy. As data availability and computational power continue to advance, CNN-based models are expected to deliver even more impactful results in material science applications.
