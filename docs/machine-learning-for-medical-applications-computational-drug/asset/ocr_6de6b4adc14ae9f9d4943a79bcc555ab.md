# 2.6 Key components and architecture of blockchain-based EHR systems

The characteristics of blockchain systems and the materials required for their implementation also guarantee the secure and compatible management of patient health information. An illustration of the components and design of the health record systems based on the blockchain paradigm is presented in Figure 6 while arguing about the patient's privacy and protection of their data. Figure 6 shows the basic building blocks and architecture of blockchain-based EHR systems, as well as an explanation of how blockchain was incorporated in order to make EHRs more secure and sharable for patients and clinicians.

Distributed ledger: Clinical data of the patients is safely stored in the blocks, which constitute a major component of the blockchain EHRS. The ledger provides data security in that it presents a clear and time-tagged view of transactions. EHR's state change happens with each transaction that creates a blockchain that links to the previous block cryptographically.

Smart contracts: Smart contracts refer to terms that when fulfilled back agreements, perform actions without any supervision, and are written into the blockchain. It revealed that smart contracts control data access rights as well as other aspects of EHR systems based on the blockchain. Smart contracts mention who has the access to certain clinical information for purposes of data privacy and security.
