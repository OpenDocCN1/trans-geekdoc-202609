# 6.5.5 Market presentation and price-setting directions

Market demand is an essential determinant of the price strategies of biomedical sensors. The peculiarities of targeting and the competitive struggle in the highly demanding markets call for pricing strategies that contribute to production with acceptable at price levels and at the same time with high profits. One way to make higher prices acceptable is to create novel sensors with capabilities that the existing models do not satisfy. On the other hand, for sensors that are meant to be used in high volumes for different industries markets, cost-containment methodologies need to be put in place. There are very many pricing strategies that manufacturers of sensors can adopt so as to lower the prices, yet make very attractive profits; pricing models such as tiered pricing models can be used to meet this end.
