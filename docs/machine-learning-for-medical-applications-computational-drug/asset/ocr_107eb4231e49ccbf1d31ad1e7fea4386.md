# 6.5 Case studies

Examples of using ML techniques in biomedical material discovery consider the ability of such approaches in the identification, selection, and validation of new tissues and materials. These cases demonstrate a conceptual application of ML for facilitating material development and improving therapeutic benefits.

# 6.5.1 Important considerations in the identification of new drug delivery techniques

A rather good example relates to the use of ML in developing and improving pharmaceutical systems. For these purposes, deep learning algorithms were used to explore huge data sets of molecular structures and drug release curvatures. By applying these models, new polymeric materials for drug encapsulation with improved efficiency and controlled release were also studied. The time to find and confirm new materials decreased noticeably due to the ML-based approach that helped to create new delivery systems with better therapeutic outcomes.

# 6.5.2 The prediction of biocompatibility for biomaterials

Another example of applying computational methods in expression profiling was related to the prediction of biomaterials’ biocompatibility for implantation. ML models were trained on large data sets, which contain data about the properties of materials, biological reactions, and clinical results. Such models also contributed to the biocompatibility estimates of prospective new material candidates to reduce undesirable biological responses. This case of ML assistance helped in the faster shortlisting of safe and effective biomaterials, corrections for which would earlier call for sundry in vivo tests – these corrections proved beneficial for the quicker advancement of healthcare implants.

# 6.5.3 Tissue engineering scaffolds optimization

In tissue engineering, ML techniques have been used to develop models with better designs of the scaffolds used for tissue regeneration. Scientists used RL algorithms to continuously shift scaffold parameters, including CEM, porosity, and mechanical properties, in response to simulated biological activity. This approach helped to design scaffolds with better performance in supporting cell and tissue regeneration. In general, it was possible to benefit from ML to define the most efficient and specific scaffolds for tissue engineering applications by optimizing the search for scaffold designs.
