# 6.3 Opportunities for integrating ML with real-time data
analytics in clinical settings

Combining ML solutions with real-time data processing in clinical environment opens up new horizons to improve drug delivery procedure. This can go a long way in enhancing the lives of patients, and the efficient running of the health facilities and advancement of precision medicine. Integration of the models and other relevant data processes, and the introduction and use of wearable technology, EHRs, and remote systems help enhance the agility in the practice of healthcare delivery.

Some of the advantages transform medication management into a process that can be followed and changed in real-time, and one of these is the ability to monitor and adjust it in real-time. Several medical information are collected by using wearable technology and biosensors, including blood pressure, glucose level, heart rate, and medicine intake. To discover abnormalities, predict possible side effects, and enhance dosage schedules, these data streams can be evaluated in real-time by machine learning algorithms. For instance, the real-time glucose monitoring data can be fed to ML models to provide personalized dose recommendations for insulin supplements during chronic diseases such as diabetes. This in turn can helps WITH regulating correct glycogen deposits or glucose levels, thereby reducing the chances of the above-listed complications, PROVIDED it is followed to the letter.

Another big potential is the increase in accuracy of predicting future sales. It is understood that when appropriate data is fed to the ML models, such models are able to predict how an illness advances, and how a therapy works. By this way, healthcare workers approach emergent health threats and make more informed decisions. This done by updating forecasts with new patient figures on a regular basis. For instance, in cancer, it becomes possible to predict the development of the tumor and also adapt the dosage and type of the chemotherapy to the patient, according to the generalized perception from the data collected from genetic makeup, images, and reports of the patient. This possibility of predicting outcomes ensures that therapies remain effective and are easily adapted to accommodate changes in the patient status.

In real-time, it becomes easier to identify features inherent in any single patient that influence drug delivery. Machine learning is especially appealing because it has the potential to uncover patterns as well as links between data that would not produce them by regular methods. It can identify gene traits that modify the rate at which medication is metabolized and the degree to which it is effective, the choices people make, and other factors. When these variables are recognized, medical personnel effect medication delivery strategies to the requirements of the individual patient, thus enhancing the effects and diminishing the side effects of the medicines.

The discovery of new drugs can be made faster through the use of Machine Learning and real-time analysis of data collected during clinical trials.
