Since there is a growing number of biomedical devices that are connected and produce/detect data, protection of this information and resources is crucial. Since devices capture and transmit health information from individuals, quality measures to prevent intrusions and security breaches are necessary. The future considerations include fortification of encryption measures, improvement in data anonymization procedures, and compliance with legal frameworks, including GDPR and HIPAA. The creation of rules for the effective exchange of data and creating an interoperability environment must be crucial for restoring public confidence in patients and protecting their data.
