# 3.2 Gradient boosting machines (GBM) for enhancing predictive accuracy

Gradient Boosting Machines (GBMs) are a class of ensemble learning algorithms that combine the outputs of multiple weak learners typically decision trees to significantly improve predictive accuracy. Their strength lies in their ability to capture complex, nonlinear relationships between input features and material properties, making them highly valuable in materials science applications. In scenarios where single-model approaches fall short, GBMs iteratively refine weak models to construct robust, high-performance predictors. The simple archetypal working of GBM was to incorporate models one over another to rectify the mistakes within the previous models. Considering only the most unpredictable results, each model in the series was designed to minimize the remaining errors of the model preceding it. This works by reducing both bias and variance and fine-tuning the model through stacking; hence, the technique was called boosting. GBM was found to be extremely beneficial in the material science domain to predict finer details pertaining to the material, fine-tune parameters of the material, and even fine-tune the type of material classification. Due to the high ability of GBM to work with different datasets, the technique was used for a variety of predictive applications.

# 3.2.2 Real-world use of GBM for material design and discovery

In materials science, various GBMs are used for predicting the mechanical properties of materials, designing the composition of composite materials, and classifying the microstructures of materials. For instance, in an application such as reinforcing the tensile strength of various new alloys, GBM models can learn prior material data and make enormously precise forecasts for new compositions. GBM has been applied to determine the most significant factors impacting the processing conditions of advanced materials including ceramics and polymers. Due to the ability of the GBM method to deal with large and heterogeneous datasets and provide high accuracy in prediction, it can become an effective tool for the enhancement of the materials discovery and development process.
