# 5.4 Personalized medicine’s role in chronic disease management

The principle of individual therapy and care is the cornerstone of PM as it approaches treatment and management of chronic diseases based on patient's details. This approach enhances the intervention efficiency, lifts the corresponding patient results, and decreases resource consumption in chronic disease treatment.

# 5.4.1 Tailored treatment plans

This is the creation of individualized treatment strategies based on patient's genetic factors, environment, and lifestyle. In diseases like diabetes, prescriptions have to be tailored because factors such as the genetic makeup that determine how drugs are metabolized can be addressed. For instance, pharmacogenomic testing would help determine the status of genes that affect the response to antidiabetic drugs, the dosage, and the side effects involved. Structured treatment enhances the way in which patients with disease are cared for because the treatment regimen is based on the patient characteristics of each unique patient.

# 5.4.2 The development in disease surveillance and control

Personalized techniques improve the ways in which disease is diagnosed and treated by applying new and sophisticated instruments, and continuous tracking of the patient's condition. Computerized wrist-worn health monitors and smart phone health applications give constant data on biomarkers, including glucose, for those with diabetes and hypertension. Such real-time patient data facilitate early modification of the patients' treatment strategies and application of treatment techniques that help control the diseases. For example, the use of the continuous glucose monitors means that hundreds of values are recorded and analyzed and the dosages of insulin is more effectively regulated for patients with diabetes.

# 5.4.3 Patient engagement and thus medication adherence

Personalized medicine enhances patients' participation and compliance with the recommended treatment procedures through delivery of treatments that are closer to the patients' needs. Through engaging patients in the process of choice making and giving feedback, more are likely to follow through the doctor's recommendations and make necessary changes for disease management.
