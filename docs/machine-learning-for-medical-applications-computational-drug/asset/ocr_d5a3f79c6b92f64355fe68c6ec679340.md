# 5 AI-driven lead optimization strategies

# 5.1 Generative models for de novo drug design

As potentially capable of generating new chemical substances with required characteristics and activities, generative models seem to have become useful in the de novo drug designing process. There are numerous methods that generative models employ to navigate chemical space and develop new drugs with favorable pharmacological features: GANs and VAEs.

One of the key applications of generative models in de novo drug discovery is the generation of novel molecules with desired properties using probabilistic models such as variational autoencoders (VAEs). VAEs learn compact representations of chemical structure space from input molecular data and are capable of generating new chemical compounds by sampling from a learned latent space, effectively producing structurally novel molecules that still retain essential characteristics of the originals. In contrast, generative adversarial networks (GANs) employ a generator and a discriminator network, where the discriminator distinguishes between real and generated molecules, while the generator aims to produce molecules indistinguishable from real ones. As illustrated in Figure 11, the ability of GANs to learn from large molecular databases allows scientists to synthesize a wide array of new chemical entities with tailored pharmacological profiles.

The generative models allow for optimization of molecular properties and activities not only by adjusting the given parameters but also by redefining them. Although the LMMS is a generative model of molecules, the result generation is done in conjunction with a reinforcement learning function wherewith scientists can direct the outcome of molecular structure generation towards the achievement of some aims such as optimum potency and minimum toxicity pharmacokinetic characteristics. Akin to how a human learns through feedback on the preference of certain generated objects, reinforcement learning agents receive feedback regarding the likeliness of the generated molecules for reinforcement of better quality and diverse structures.
