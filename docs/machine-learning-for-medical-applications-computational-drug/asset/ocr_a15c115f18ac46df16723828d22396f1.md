These augmentations make models invariant to changes in image acquisition settings and enhance generality from one situation to another.

Synthetic image generation: To improve the training dataset, composite more realistic synthetic pictures using techniques such as GANs. New variations and situations not included in the training data set are introduced through synthetic photos.

A crucial step in image processing, referred to as noise reduction, assists in enhancing the acquisition of images by eradicating abhorrent distortions and impediments to assessment. A number of techniques are used to lower noise and improve the clarity of medical images:

Filtering techniques: In order to blur the pictures and reduce noise while preserving characteristics, techniques such as bilateral, median, and Gaussian are used. While median filters are excellent for removing salt-and-pepper noise by replacing a pixel's value with the median of the pixels in its immediate vicinity, Gaussian filters use a weighted average of the nearby pixels.

Wavelet transform: Symbolically breaks the images into different frequency bands and then attenuates the noise in the WT domain individually. It provides a good tradeoff between noise removal and detail preservation, qualities that make it suitable for enhancing medical images.

Denoising autoencoders: A class of NNs that learn a mapping from a noisy image to a clean image. They are trained to make clean images out of noisy inputs and, in so doing, learn to remove noise while preserving the relevant details.

# 5.3 Feature extraction methods: manual vs. automated

Prior to analysis and classification, enhancing medical images was one of the most critical steps in image processing, called feature extraction, which involved segregating and identifying specific characteristics. There are two ways to approach this process: manually and automatically. As with virtually everything in life, there is a positive side and a negative side to each of them.

Historically, feature extraction has been a complex process that involved designing and implementing a number of algorithms and heuristics with the purpose of extracting relevant features from medical images according to the knowledge and experience of the human expert in the field. This approach includes techniques such as

Edge detection: Boundary detection in images is achieved by using methods such as Sobel, Canny, and Prewitt. These methods usually emphasize thin lines around different structural areas, for example, when outlining a tumor.

Texture analysis: Measuring the textural features of areas in an image with statistical methods, including energy, contrast, and uniformity of textured areas, is needed to form an image. Texture analysis can provide information about the type of tissue and the structural arrangement of the tissues.
