Advanced integration technologies: MEMS and printed electronics offer a way of developing a range of sensing systems within a single, small form factor. They also enable us to monitor more in a single wearable device, while integrating data from various sensors.

Enhanced connectivity and data transmission: Wearable sensors have become more connected with Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and cellular technologies to send the collected data to smartphones and cloud for real-time monitoring and analysis.

# Wearable sensors practical use

Wearable sensors serve a broad range of applications in health monitoring and wellness:

Chronic disease management: Wearable sensors are used to track and manage chronic ailments like diabetes, hypertension, and cardiovascular illness. For instance, CGMs offer glucose level status in the continuous process while blood pressure monitor only shows the status of blood pressure.
