Based on case and clinical studies, practical application and outcomes of the biomedical sensors that have been advanced by artificial intelligence are brought to light. For instance, with a gadget like the Dexcom G6, a CGM that can go for about 10 days without needing a new sensor, patients’ glycemic control has been enhanced as well as the frequent incidences of hypoglycemia are detected. Non-wearable AI-driven ECG monitoring has shown enormous potential in identifying early-stage atrial fibrillation hence saving lives [25]. Telemonitoring systems like the Biofourmis system are effective in managing chronic diseases through early identification of the risks of worsening of a condition and/or frequent hospitalizations. Such cases present AI-hinged sensors as enablers that contribute to improving diseases control, diagnostics, and even telemedicine. The experiences from these case studies serve to improve the growth of biomedical sensor technologies as a continuous process.

In the future, the authors predict that biomedical sensors operated by AI are going to develop and progress in the field even further. It is suggested that the means of enhancing the sensors together with the advancement of healthcare can be brought to the next level with new technologies like edge computing AI algorithms. Edge computing reduces response time while accelerating response time by processing data on the sensors. Nowadays, emerging fields of AI as Explainable AI and Federated learning open new possibilities for succeeding model reveal. The important elements are the integration of these technologies with biomedical sensors, which form the next generation of health monitoring systems that are more accurate, personal, and efficient. Biomedical sensors that would be fueled by AI are going to be increasingly important in deciding affairs of healthcare as long as concepts continue to be expanded in terms of research and development.

# ### 2.1 Definition and purpose of biomedical sensors

The name “biomedical sensors” is used in the detection and measurement of several varieties of physiological variables in the body. These sensors convert biological signals to electrical signals in order that applications in medicine can process, analyze, and interpret the data.
