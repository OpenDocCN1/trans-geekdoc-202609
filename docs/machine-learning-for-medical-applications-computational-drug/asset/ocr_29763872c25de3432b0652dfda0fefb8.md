# 3.3.4 Bordering on personalized biomedical materials

In the area concerning personalized medicine, deep learning is used to customize biological products to suit certain consumer requirements. Deep learning models can develop individual content for prostheses, implants, and medication delivery by using patient-relevant records including their genetic and disease records. Customization is safe and effective because material qualities align with each patient’s concerns during medical procedures.

# 3.3.5 Improving material degradability

Artificial neural networks of deep learning have been used in optimizing the biocompatibility of materials used in medical applications. These models enable the analysis of quantitative data obtained from in vitro and in vivo experiments and can determine factors affecting the material/biological system interface. This application helps in the development of materials that prevent negative reactions and enhance patient outcomes.

The potential of deep learning in biomedical materials is enormous and quite transformative, and it is a vital tool for the discovery, design, and optimization of biomedical materials. When such deep learning approaches are employed, researchers are well-positioned to solve multifaceted problems in biomedical material science to arrive at a myriad of inventions that help augment the effectiveness of therapy and, in effect, the delivery of care to patients.
