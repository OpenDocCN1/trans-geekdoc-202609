# 4.3 Advantages of machine learning in handling medical images

Machine learning has a number of advantages that are changing the analysis and interpretation of medical images in clinical practice in the sphere of medical imaging. Such advantages include increased diagnostic accuracy, increased production rates, and the ability to manage complex as well as massive data.

Compared with traditional approaches, machine learning algorithms, particularly DL, have demonstrated a significant improvement in diagnostic performance. These algorithms are designed through the analysis of labeled medical photos and are also able to identify minute details and anomalies that easily escape the eye of the human observer. Sophisticated models, such as CNNs, are also highly capable of detecting and differentiating multiple conditions, like tumors, lesions, and others, with great accuracy. These two combined have proven to produce better diagnostic accuracy to enhance early diagnosis, hence improving patients’ lives.
