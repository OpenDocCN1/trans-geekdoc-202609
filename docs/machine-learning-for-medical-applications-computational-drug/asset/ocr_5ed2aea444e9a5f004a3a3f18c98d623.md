Automated Machine Learning (AutoML): AutoML systems naturally integrate hyperparameter tuning algorithms into their loops. Such frameworks combine grid search and random search, as well as nonconvex functions such as Bayesian optimization, to create an automated process that performs hyperparameter tuning alongside model selection and preprocessing. AutoML has been helpful in tasks such as hyperparameter optimization with reduced intervention and the enhancement of model performance.

Usually, to determine the effectiveness of hyperparameter tuning, cross-validation techniques are used. In cross-validation, the population is split into training and validation data.
