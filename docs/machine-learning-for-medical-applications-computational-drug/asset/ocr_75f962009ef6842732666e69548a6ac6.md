# 2.4 Generative adversarial networks (GANs) for synthetic material data generation

GANs, at the present time, are a modern and highly effective approach to the generation of synthetic data within the framework of the machine learning paradigm. GANs were first introduced by Goodfellow et al. in 2014 and are composed of two coupled neural networks: a discriminator and a generator, which compete with each other. While the discriminator verifies the similarity of the created data to real data, the generator creates synthetic data. This adversarial process makes the generator produce better data, which has been important in many industries including those involving biological materials.

# 2.4.1 A case study on usage of GANs in materials science

The generation and training of a new material dataset are provided effectively in the scenario of biomedical materials using GANs. In materials science, data collection techniques are usually costly and entail a lot of experimentation and trial. These difficulties are solved by generating high-quality synthetic data that mimic the characteristics and behavior of actual materials using GANs. This artificial data helps investigate material properties that cannot be easily measured experimentally, thereby enlarging the training datasets in order to enhance the model's performance.

# 2.4.2 Four benefits of using GANs for synthetic data

The application of GANs for synthetic material data generation has many potential benefits. First, GANs help generate myriad and extensive labeled data that otherwise could not be generated through experiments. This is especially helpful in regions where there is little information regarding the constitutive behavior of specific materials, such as those that are comparatively new or often used. Second, GAN-synthesized data further strengthens and widens the applicability of machine learning algorithms by creating a diverse range of patterns found within data sets. Third, GANs can sample data with specific characteristics, allowing researchers to investigate material properties by restricting their applications to research areas of interest.
