# 5.2 Data privacy and security

Security and privacy of personal information is a major issue in the use of biomedical sensors and devices, especially since much of the information is in digital format. Most importantly, the management recognizes privacy of health information as key to patients’ confidence, meeting various legislation standards and guarding patients’ data against cyber vulnerabilities.

Many biomedical sensors and devices are communication-enabled and send data over the wireless network. This transmission poses potential risks and could lead to data interception as well as unauthorized access. In Figure 17, we present aspects related to data privacy and security that involve the main factors and approaches to security of data. Cryptographic solutions such as AES are used to protect the data in transit to various destinations. Encryption makes sure that in the limited time, the data is encoded and it cannot be intercepted by interested parties. It can also provide a secure connection for the transferred data, as well as an ability to update encryption methods in response to the appearance of threats and risks.

The issue of storing and managing health data is an American concern of privacy and security. Biomedical devices and the associated systems have to guarantee that the data is stored and processed adequately, being compliant with privacy requirements.
