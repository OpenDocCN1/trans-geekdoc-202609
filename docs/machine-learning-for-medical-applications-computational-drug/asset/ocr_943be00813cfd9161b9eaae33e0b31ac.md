# 3.3 Enhancement of blockchain security in EHR management

Blockchain technology comprehensively eliminates the major shortcomings of typical EHR systems through several ways that enhanced security in EHR management. The adoption of blockchain technology eliminates the central administrator in managing EHRs, cryptographic methods, and consensus procedures enhance security. These benefits include:

Decentralization: A copy of the distributed ledger containing EHRRs is maintained on the file by each node in the chain of decentralized nodes of a blockchain. As a result of this decentralization there is a single point of failure, thereby reducing the risks of unauthorized access to EHR data. In blockchain, all nodes in the network contain a copy of the ledger and therefore even if the ledger of a particular node is compromised the blockchain remains sanctimonious.
