# 5.5.2 Challenges and considerations

Real-time feedback systems based on RL have some difficulties. One of the biggest issues is making RL algorithms stable and convergent, especially when working with noisy data. As in most RL processes, the model shows that the quality of the feedback has a direct influence on the performance of the RL agent, and poor feedback can seriously affect the learning results. The computational cost is often high in RL, particularly when the MC problem is large and complicated. Solving these problems requires careful planning of algorithms and parameter settings, as well as proper data management to provide the most accurate and informative feedback for the RL agent.

# 6 Explainable AI and interpretability in materials science

# 6.1 Feature importance and sensitivity analysis in material models

Feature importance and sensitivity analysis are fundamental components of Explainable AI (XAI) and are essential for ensuring interpretability in materials science applications. These methods help clarify how various input features influence the predictions and decisions made by machine learning models. Understanding which attributes have the greatest impact allows researchers and engineers to deepen their insight into material behavior, leading to more trustworthy, accurate, and transparent models. This level of interpretability is not only critical for validating model outputs but also supports material design efforts and ensures compliance with regulatory standards.
