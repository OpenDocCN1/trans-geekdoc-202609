MARL faces several challenges even in material discovery. There was a major issue in deciding what the rewards should be in order to guide the optimal behavior of all the agents as well as promote positive engagement. The control of the growth of these MARL systems, especially as the number of agents increased, was challenging and led to computational problems, hence the need for complex methods. Another factor that emerged as a problem was the coordination and communication between the agents: it was important to organize the information exchange and avoid both duplicate activities and conflicts in actions between the agents within the framework of the system.
