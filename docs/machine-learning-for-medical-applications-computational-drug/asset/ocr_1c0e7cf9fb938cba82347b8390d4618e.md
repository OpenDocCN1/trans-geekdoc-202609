# 2.2 Milestones in the development of personalized healthcare

Personalized medicine has progressed through several evolutionary milestones relevant to the given field, which has made great leaps. These achievements describe the development from fundamental conceptual models to advanced statistical models to considerate customized medical treatment management.

# Pharmacogenomics: historical perspective and development (1950–1960)

The idea of pharmacogenomics started to emerge as soon as scientists started to find the genetic differences affecting metabolite pathways of some drugs. Some of the earliest pieces of work clearly identified differences in the activity of enzymes, which can cause variations in drug metabolism among people. These years provided the basis for comprehending the issues connected with the genetic effects of drug effectiveness and side effects.

The Human Genome Project announced in February 1988 was formally launched in October 1990.

An initiative with scientific approach from all over the world known as Human Genome Project (HGP) focused on mapping the whole human genome. The astonishing feat completed in 2003 furnished anatomic details, which are part of personalized treatment, for instance, detailed genetic blueprint. It helped in increasing appreciation of inheritance and tailor-made solutions by offering an understanding of the varieties of genetic makeup the human body has concerning various diseases.

# Advancements of technology in genomic sequencing in the 2000s

Next-generation sequencing, the advancements of which formed the basis for the achievements of the HGP, began in the early 2000. Technological advancements such as next-generation sequencing (NGS) enhanced genomic sequencing in terms of cost and time thus embracing its use in clinical diagnosis. This development led to a criterion that allows genetic data to be used in the customized medicine system.

The latter was inaugurated by the arrival of targeted therapies in the course of the decade 2000–2010.
