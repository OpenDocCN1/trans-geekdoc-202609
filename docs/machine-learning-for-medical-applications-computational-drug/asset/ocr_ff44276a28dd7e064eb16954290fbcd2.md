# AI-powered sensors and devices for sustained health tracking

**Abstract:** The steady progress made in artificial bio interfaces and sensing systems has strong impacts on the development of continuous health monitoring systems in biomedical sensor technology solutions. In this chapter, the authors focus on enhancing biomedical sensors and devices using AI and underlining the need to incorporate complex algorithms and real-time analysis of large datasets. Some of the focal areas described include the utilization of smart sensor nodes and machine learning for diagnostic prognosis, and the optimization of data credibility. As the chapter seeks to discuss the future of health monitoring technologies in coming up with an improved advanced remote health monitoring system, it discusses the challenges in sensor calibration, data processing, and real-time feedback. The future prospects and challenges of personalized medicine and preventative care resulting from the direct application of AI to patient care are also described in relation to the potential for dramatically changing patient management and treatment algorithms.

**Keywords:** AI-driven sensors, biomedical optimization, continuous health monitoring, machine learning, predictive diagnostics, real-time data analytics

# 1 Introduction

The integration of AI into biomedical sensor technology marks a transformative shift in the domain of health monitoring [1]. As healthcare systems evolve toward more personalized and preventive models, the demand for sophisticated, real-time monitoring solutions has intensified [2]. AI-driven biomedical sensors are at the forefront of this revolution, offering unprecedented capabilities for continuous health surveillance
