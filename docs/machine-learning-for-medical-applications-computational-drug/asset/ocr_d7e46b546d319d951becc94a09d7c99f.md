# 4.2.2 Targeted drug delivery

Targeted drug delivery systems are intended to deliver therapeutic drugs to diseased tissues only, without affecting healthy ones. This precise approach reduces deleterious consequences while enhancing the curative advantages of pharmacotherapy. Such systems require biocompatibility, and these materials are often used as carriers that can potentially be functionalized with antibodies. To transport chemotherapeutic medicines specifically to the aforementioned tumor site, for instance, nanoparticles can be functionalized with coat changes that recognize receptors on cancer cells.
