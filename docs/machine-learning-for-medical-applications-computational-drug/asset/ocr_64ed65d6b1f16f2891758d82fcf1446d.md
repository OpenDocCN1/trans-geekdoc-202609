Another is AI in wearable ECG monitor application in early identification of atrial fibrillation (AF). Smart watches of today like the Apple Watch Series 4 and Series 5 even have intelligent algorithms that can work in real time to analyze information from the device’s ECG sensors that can detect signs of AF. Application of the devices in early identification of AF has been evidenced through clinical studies and enhanced patients’ management. Several researches have presented that AI-based ECG monitoring can effectively identify AF with high sensitivity and specificity, which is helpful information to avoid stroke and other cardiovascular events.

The application of AI-coupled biosensors in the area of remote patient monitoring has borne fruit, especially for managing chronic illness. For example, the Biofourmis platform includes wearable sensors and AI that help control conditions in patients with heart failure. As with any HEP platform, the studies revealed that the platform’s predictive analysis can predict cases of worsening and hospitalization effectively thereby eradicating overwhelming episodes of the disease. Frequently, the utilization of remote patient monitoring and the possibility to change the treatment plans based on the gathered information improves patient’s quality of life and decreases the rate of ER visits.
