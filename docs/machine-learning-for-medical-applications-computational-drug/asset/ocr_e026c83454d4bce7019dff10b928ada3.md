# 5.1.5 Model interpretation and refinement

Interpretation of the results returned by the predictive model involves the identification of different feature tendencies and overall patterns as well as the presence of bias. Some techniques include SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) values, partial dependency plots, and feature significance analysis to understand the reasons and ways different traits affect the kind of predictions made by the model. To increase the accuracy and reliability of the model, it should be improved based on these interpretations by altering the features and employing other techniques from new data.
