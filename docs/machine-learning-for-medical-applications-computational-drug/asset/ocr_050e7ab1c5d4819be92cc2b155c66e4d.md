# 4.5.2 Decision-making under conditions of uncertainty

The management of uncertainty in decision-making was also fundamental in the application of biomedical materials. Another well-developed approach to decision-making under risk conditions was Bayesian decision-making, which is a rational model of decision-making that estimates the possible consequences according to their probabilities and degree of risk. This approach makes it possible to choose material designs that are optimized between their performance and safety, despite data being scant. For instance, in implantable materials, failure mode effects can be assessed and ranked by likelihood using the Bayesian method effectively, so the design can achieve the highest probabilities of success while minimizing failure probabilities. This capability of making decisions that are fundamentally probabilistic in nature is especially valued in biomedical applications because material failure can have severe implications.
