In addition, personalized medicine assists in the exploration of advanced technique of treatment where treatments are performed depending on the patient’s unique genetic makeup. Technological developments have greatly enhanced the capacity to find new biomarkers for cardiovascular diseases through genomics and proteomics. Individualization allows the development of treatments targeting these aims since non-targeted interventions would be more toxic and less effective than the current therapies. For instance, targeted drug delivery systems and gene therapies are becoming potential solutions to enhanced and sophisticated cardiovascular diseases, which include heart failure and atherosclerosis diseases.

Mobile health, digital health, and wearable technology also help support the concept of personalized medicine in cardiovascular care. Pulse oximeters, blood pressure, temperature, and other health indicators help to gain on-the-spot information about changes in giving targeted interventions and to assess results. Personalized medicine also adapts these data into the management, which means alteration in the routine management plan according the feedback and overall condition of the patient.

# 5.3 Customizing treatment strategies for neurological disorders

Personalized medicine is an advanced treatment approach that tailors therapy for neurological disorders based on the individual characteristics of each patient. As shown in Figure 11, this strategy leverages emerging technologies and discoveries in genomics, neuroimaging, and biomarker identification to enhance the precision and effectiveness of diagnosing and managing complex neurological conditions, including Alzheimer’s disease, Parkinson’s disease, epilepsy, and multiple sclerosis.

Lack of simplicity and homogeneity are the characteristic features of most neurological sickness where multiple genetic, biochemical, and environmental factors determine the natural history and response to treatment. It is therefore of highly heterogeneous structure, and traditional treatment methods often involve applying an approach that may not be useful to many people. It is important to understand that, for example, certain diseases in certain patients for which medicines have not been developed yet can be a problem in general for personalized medicine and work is needed to correct these things and offer better treatment.

Among the main developments that enabled enhancing the approaches to managing neurological disorders, the development of genetic and molecular biomarkers is noteworthy. In the genetics context, a lot of genetic variations have been discovered to be linked with neurological disorders mostly through the studies of their causes and possible treatments. For instance, mutations within genes encoding amyloid precursor protein (APP) and presenilins have been identified to cause familial Alzheimer’s disease resulting in therapies that seek to influence amyloid-beta synthesis and deposition.
