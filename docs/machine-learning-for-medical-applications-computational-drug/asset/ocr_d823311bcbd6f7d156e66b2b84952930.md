Different data types can be easily incorporated to update the ML algorithms to build a comprehensive picture of synthesis and its effect on material properties. There are challenges to optimizing material synthesis with the use of ML as follows: The accuracy of any form of “prediction” using ML largely relies on the quality and volume of training data. The use of partial data creates wrong conclusions and leads to incorrect conditions during synthesis. Some material synthesis processes are complex and need efficient computational power and highly developed ML models, which are rather difficult to perform in certain research environments.
