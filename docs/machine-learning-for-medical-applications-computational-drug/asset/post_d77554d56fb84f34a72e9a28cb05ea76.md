## 4.4 Predictive analytics for early disease detection

In this continuous health monitoring, the major role of predictive analytics is to identify possible health issues before they turn into major medical diseases, and this is where AI comes in. The proactive approach that allows people to go to a doctor as soon as something is indicated in the data would not have been possible without sophisticated data analysis tools that translate big amounts of health data into insights.

Machine learning-based predictive analytics processes historical and current biometric data collected from biological sensors. These algorithms are quite capable of identifying correlations and patterns that suggest that illnesses began early. As an example, prognostic models can capture slight changes in vitals or movement, which precede the signs of chronic disorders such as diabetes or respiratory diseases.

## 5 Key AI technologies used in biomedical sensors

### 5.1 Machine learning algorithms

Machine learning algorithms form a core component of development and optimization of biomedical sensors for increasing the accuracy, effectiveness, and usability of the devices. These algorithms utilize techniques that enable the modeling of various health related data with a view to finding patterns as well as making informed forecasts, which facilitate correct health check and control.

The most employed supervised learning algorithms in biomedical sensors are used in classification and regression. They include algorithms that rely on labelled data to make sense of new sets of data and arrive at a certain decision. For instance, supervised learning models estimate blood glucose level from the past data collected using sensors to help diabetes patients. Supervised learning methods are support vector machines (SVM), decision trees, the ensemble methods of random forests, and gradient boosting machines.

Exploratory techniques are widely used to find and extract useful patterns within health data without any prior knowledge. K-means and hierarchical clustering together bring similar data points into tight knit groups and make the patterns in patient data easily identifiable. For instance, unsupervised learning can discover distinct clusters of patients with the same health needs and thus the clustering provides the necessary basis for customizing the approaches to patients.

Neural networks, which belong to the broader category of the machine learning, employ multiple layers of mathematics to map and interpret data issues. Computer vision is usually performed by special deep learning algorithms, like convolutional neural networks (CNN), which are best at analyzing image-based data like MRI scans. Making use of deep learning models a number of features are capable of being automatically extracted from raw data, thereby improving the ability to detect even small information patterns as well as anomalies.
