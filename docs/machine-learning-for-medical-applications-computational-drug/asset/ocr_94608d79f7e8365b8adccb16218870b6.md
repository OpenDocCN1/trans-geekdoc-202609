# 3.3.4 Transfer learning in biomedical research

Transfer learning involves using an already-trained deep learning model to solve a novel but related biomaterial design challenge. This allows the model to perform better by making use of the information that already exists. This methodology is especially advantageous in situations where data is scarce, as it applies knowledge from well-established fields to newly emerging biomaterial research sectors. Through the process of fine-tuning pre-trained models on specific biomaterial datasets, researchers can improve the speed and accuracy of design optimization and property prediction. Transfer learning leverages information from previously researched materials and designs to enable the quick adaptation of deep learning techniques to a variety of biomaterial applications, such as drug delivery systems and tissue engineering.
