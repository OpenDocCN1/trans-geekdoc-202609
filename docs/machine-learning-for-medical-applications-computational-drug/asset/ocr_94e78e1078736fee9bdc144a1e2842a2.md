# 2.4 Importance of data in biomedical material science

Data was crucial to the advancement of biological material science as the foundation for creating and testing ML models. As it was observed, the data quality and quantity that are accessible are directly associated with the effectiveness of ML algorithms regarding the material characteristics, design, and searching for new materials. In biomedical material science, information can be variable and comprise biological activity, molecular organization, patient individual data, and consequences of experiments.

In order to accurately train and validate machine learning (ML) models, the availability of high-quality, well-annotated data is essential. This is particularly true in the domain of biomedical materials, where the biological and chemical complexity of operational environments demands comprehensive datasets. These datasets must capture intricate details about a material's structure, formation, and interaction with living systems, thereby enabling deeper insights into its performance under various physiological conditions. The diversity and scale of such data facilitate the detection of subtle patterns and interdependencies relationships that would likely remain hidden using traditional, non-ML approaches. Consequently, this leads to improved prediction accuracy and greater efficiency in the design and discovery of advanced biomedical materials.
