# 5.2.2 Deep Q-networks: improving Q-learning with deep learning

A new approach was integrated with the Q-learning algorithm, namely involving DL methods in DQNs to help resolve the problems of large-sized state spaces. Therefore, while traditional Q-learning is not capable of dealing with highly complex data, DQNs are developed to solve more complicated material optimization problems because they use neural networks to approximately determine Q-values. DQNs can be employed in material science to decide on the refinement of methods that demand searches in incredibly large state as well as action spaces, such as chemical vapor deposition of composite materials. DQNs are used in the training process to allow for learning and to generate insights from approximately large data, enhancing the optimization formula by providing higher efficiency.
