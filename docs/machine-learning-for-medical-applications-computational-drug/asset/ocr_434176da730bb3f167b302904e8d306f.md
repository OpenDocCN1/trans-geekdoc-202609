Decentralized data exchange platforms work on the principles of multiple data systems and do not require intermediaries and a central hub to share data and allow EHR systems and healthcare facilities to exchange information.

Federated learning and data sharing networks: On the basis of blockchain technology, federated learning and data sharing networks enable cooperative studying and sharing of EHR data together with preserving data confidentiality and privacy. In federated learning localization, several decentralized nodes have a part of EHR data, and collaboratively train a machine learning model. When data is aggregated and analyzed, cryptographic approaches like differential privacy and homomorphic encryption safeguard patient privacy. Blockchain guarantees the integrity and traceability of model modifications and data contributions. Federated learning networks facilitate interoperability and knowledge exchange between academics and healthcare organizations by providing safe and private data sharing.

Patient-controlled data sharing platforms: These allow the ownership and control of personal health data and allow health and research recipients access to them only when appropriate. These blockchain-based systems allow clients to grant different parties access to specific EHR information while ensuring data privacy and control of access by use of digital signature and keys. Systems that patients control also means that patient data can be easily provided and accessed across multiple EHR systems and across the various settings where patients seek care.

Interoperable health information exchanges (HIEs): Interconnected HIEs leveraging information technology consult blockchain to enable a secure and standardized exchange of electronic data across greater healthcare systems. Smart contracts enable efficient handling of permission-related processes and contracts between several parties; blockchain ensures the availability and credibility of EHR data transferred through exchange implementation. Blockchain-based HIEs enable the sharing of information across care givers and patients, co-ordination of care between different providers and systems, as well as cooperation between patients through an interoperable sharing platform.
