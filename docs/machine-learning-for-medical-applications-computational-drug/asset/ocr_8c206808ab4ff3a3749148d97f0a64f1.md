Image and signal processing were also used to improve medical images and to reconstruct images from noisy data sets. In the same manner, architectures like denoising autoencoders and GANs have been employed to enhance image quality, minimize artifacts, and reconstruct lost characteristics in poor images. These enhancements are especially important in increasing diagnostic precision and rendering fine morphologic details that are concealed by noise.
