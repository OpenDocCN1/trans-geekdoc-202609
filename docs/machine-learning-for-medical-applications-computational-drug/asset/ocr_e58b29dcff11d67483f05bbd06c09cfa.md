# 6.4 Auditing and traceability features of blockchain-based EHR systems

High auditability and accountability afforded by blockchain-based EHR systems enhance responsibility, transparency, and credibility of patient health information management. Figure 12 shows how the use of blockchain in EHR systems provides guaranteed data integrity and transparency in records through a blockchain technology approach. The auditing and traceability capabilities of blockchain-based EHR systems are highlighted by the following features:

Clear and unchangeable audit trails: It is also important to know that thanks to blockchain, all transactions and change of data in EHRs are recorded, being immutable and easily traceable. Each and every bitcoin transaction was linked with every other transaction with some type of digital signature, and the blocks formed are immutably arranged chronologically.

Time-stamping: Blockchain attaches a date and time stamp for each transaction thus giving a chronological sequence to activities recorded throughout the blockchain. Various EHR actions performed on the EHR data must be accurately quantified and sequenced to assist with post-incident analysis and coverage for a range of issues including forensic, compliance, and reporting. Time-stamping helps healthcare organizations verify the origin and pedigree of information within EHR and monitor and audit all activities of interest, which could include, but is not limited to detecting an attempted unauthorized access of EHR data.
