# 4.3 Materials and manufacturing

There are nearly universal synergies between the progress of new materials and manufacturing technologies with state-of-the-art biomedical sensor and devices developments. Therefore, the choice of the right materials and integration of innovative technology production processes are critical factors when defining the performances of devices, their robustness, and biocompatibility. The above discussed technologies enhance the development of improved effective biomedical gadgets.

The use of biomaterials is considered to be pivotal in biomedical devices in terms of their performance and sterility. These are specifically developed materials, including biocompatible polymers, metals, and ceramics for use with the intended application, with properties of strength, flexibility, and ability to integrate with the biological tissues. Some of the most used polymers are polyurethane and silicone in implants prosthetics because they are flexible and do not interfere with the fluids of the body.
