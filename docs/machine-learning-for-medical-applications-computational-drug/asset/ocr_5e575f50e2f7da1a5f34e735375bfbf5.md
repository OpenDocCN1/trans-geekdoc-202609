Computational methods play a crucial role in data interpretation at the genetic level of organisms. Raw sequenced data is analyzed using advanced math and computation software to detect genetic variations and relate these variations to biological, functional, and medical effects. Software comprising variant callers, alignments tools, and genome browsers help in the detection of mutations and copy a number of changes and structural rearrangements that determine disease susceptibility and pharmacogenomics.
