# 5.5.1 Minimization of adverse drug reactions and healthcare costs as well

Personalized medication aims at avoiding and even predicting unfavorable reactions of the body to medication through tailoring therapies according to the genotype of the individual. Pharmaceutical providers decrease the instance of adverse reactions to medications by eliminating hazardous courses of treatment by heading prescriptions as well as dosage based on the discovered genetic information. As a result, this type of strategy reduces the tendency for costly and risky interventions and hospitalizations, while enhancing patient outcomes. For this reason, people as well as the healthcare systems make significant savings due to personalized therapy.

# 5.5.2 Improved treatment effectiveness and result

Pharmacogenomics enhances therapy outcomes through making it possible to use treatments that have a strong correlation with the biological aspects of the patient. For instance, molecular and personalized medicines related to cancers have been revealed to improve therapeutic indices and survival in patients diagnosed with tumors. Better treatment outcomes translate into improved health, less disease prevalence, and lower requirement for follow-up and supplementary therapies. This fact helps to solve the issues of economical utility and increase patient satisfaction due to the opportunity of attaining the treatment goal with less amendments.

# 5.5.3 Screening and early interference

Personalized medicine increases the chances of early diagnosis of diseases, in general, by genetic testing and various predictions. Detection of diseases, including cancer, cardiovascular, and hereditary diseases, means that treatments are initiated before the diseases progress, and reduction of severity is enhanced. Care outcomes deliver conclusions that prevention and early intervention are cost effective as it contrasts with more intensive and expensive interventions in the future, while improving patient’s quality of life.
