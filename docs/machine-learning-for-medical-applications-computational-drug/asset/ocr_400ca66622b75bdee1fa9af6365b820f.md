# 5.4.2 Uncertainty in combining machine learning into manufacturing process enhancements

Real-time monitoring and control of biomaterial fabrication significantly depend on the application of ML algorithms. Depending on the data gathered from the sensors and imaging systems, experts apply ML algorithms to establish an understandable relationship between the different parameters and the processes taking place in the plant. Models can also predict problems that may occur in the future, allowing changes to be made in advance to maintain optimal fabrication. Weighted based on the feedback of information gathered from real-time data, reinforcement learning approaches are capable of constantly adjusting the process parameters to enhance the quality of the material. When used together with monitoring systems, ML provides much better control of fabrication procedures, thereby producing superior biomaterials.

# 5.4.3 Issues related to the deployment of RTMS

There are observed challenges in incorporating real-time monitoring systems in the fabrication of biomaterials. The complexity of fabrication processes, based on the nature of targets (structural and other) and the variability in the parameters to be monitored, presents a major challenge to designing an adequate sensor network as well as the architecture and protocols for data integration. Real-time data analysis becomes very challenging in situations where a huge amount of data is collected concurrently. Additionally, improving the accuracy of sensors and handling data is a technical and computational problem since more data generated from multiple sensors requires more significant processing power. Remediation of these challenges requires the employment of sound data acquisition techniques, sophisticated signal processing, and competent techniques of data control in order to facilitate adequate and effective real-time data control.

Enhancing the accuracy and capacity of the monitoring models is most likely to be the main focus of further development in real-time monitoring and control for biomaterial synthesis. A better and higher level of examination of manufacturing processes should be achievable with such enhancements in probe devices and data compilation methods. Here, it is impossible not to underline the profound positive impact of ML and AI on the improvement of process optimization and, in particular, on forecasting capacities. In addition, smoother and more scalable monitoring solutions can be generated by expanding real-time monitoring with Industry 4.0 concepts like cloud computing and the internet of things. Further advancements in the monitoring and control of biomaterial fabrication should be possible as fabrication technology continues to evolve to enhance the real-time control of material synthesis.
