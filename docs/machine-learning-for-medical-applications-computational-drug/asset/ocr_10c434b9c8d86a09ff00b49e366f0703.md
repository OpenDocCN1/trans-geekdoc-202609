# 3.4 The role of patient data in personalizing healthcare

The application of patient data in healthcare personalization is an essential element of ASCO’s customized medicine implantation process. Usually, patient data is aggregated information that can be subdivided into genetic, phenotypic, clinical, and lifestyle data, all of which are incorporated into treatment plans for the patient, based on his/her unique characteristics.

# 3.4.1 Genomic data and the influence on personalized medicine

The resources provide necessary information concerning genetic makeup of a specific individual – with a focus on differences that determine how adept a person is to particular diseases and how is his/ her responsive to specific treatments. SNP and gene mutations can also give healthcare professionals an idea of which of the populace is at a higher risk of developing certain disorders. Then, therapeutic treatments can be modified for these patients. For example, genomic profiling helps differentiate specific mutations within cancers and allows the use of special medicines that are more effective, in view of the cancer’s molecular characteristics.

# 3.4.2 Clinical data and its part in decision-making

Digital clinical information, which comprises the patients’ medical history, laboratory and radiological findings, and therapeutic response, is relevant for individualized medicine. This information lets clinicians see how patients have reacted to past medication and treatment, how sick they are, and if they are prone to develop any other illnesses. Electronic clinical information can enable clinicians to tailor therapy to a patient’s experience and health condition by using clinical information in the decision-making procedures.
