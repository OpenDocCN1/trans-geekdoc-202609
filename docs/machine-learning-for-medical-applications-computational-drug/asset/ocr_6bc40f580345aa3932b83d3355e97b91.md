This paper shows that collaboration and partnerships are critical and are at the core of progress toward global health and accessibility. Working with IOs, governments, INGOs, and CBOs help to create and deliver biomedical technologies, where there is inadequate access to advanced technologies. Partnership areas include research partnerships; technology development and dissemination; and strengthening of capacity to address and respond to health needs at the local level. Such partnerships can assist in negation of barriers to access and enable augmentation of the benefit that is occasioned by technology on special populations across the world.

Ethical and regulatory are were significant, where these principles help to achieve compliance with the international regulatory frameworks to implement biomedical devices within different countries, culture, and values systems. Due to this, appropriate ethical standards must be developed for new emerging technologies to guarantee that their use is proper and fair. There is a need to overcome the differences in the required regulatory frameworks to achieve the needed safety and efficacy of devices as well as their compliance with the peculiarities of the local healthcare systems. With these considerations, developers and regulators of these biomedical technologies can shape public conduct toward their precise, reasonable, and fair usage.

# 6.5 Regulatory and ethical considerations

As the technology in biomedical sensors and devices gets more advanced, more effort should be applied in the regulation and addressing ethical concerns in relation to its use. It therefore becomes clear that future work in this field shall have to contend with these regulations and has to answer the following ethical challenges, which includes data management, patients’ self-determination, and influence of advanced technologies within the health sector.

The exponential growth and development of new biomedical technology, such as the use of AI and ML, requires new legal frameworks to counter new emerging issues. New guidelines must be defined to incorporate the AI-related characteristics, including algorithm disclosure, learning capability, and performance improvement capability of the devices. The future rules probably revolve around setting rules for formal AI verification to prove the work successfully and take the right and proper steps with all the different segments of population.
