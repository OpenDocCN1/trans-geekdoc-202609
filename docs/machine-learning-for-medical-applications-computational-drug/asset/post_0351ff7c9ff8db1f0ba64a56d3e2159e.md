### 6.4.4.1 Focus on Underserved Populations Typically Managed by Limited Local Practitioners

Personalized therapies are further strengthened by increasing the representation of minority populations in clinical trials.

The shift toward new therapeutic endeavors shows the stakeholders' concern with the progress of the technique and the methods of individualized treatment.

There is an expectation that ML and AI technologies would play an important role in the future of personalized medicine. ## 7 Conclusion
Personalized medicine is widely considered as the new general approach to disease diagnosis and treatment, as well as prevention. This chapter was devoted to the complex perspectives of the concept under discussion as it considers the synergy between personalized medicine and individual drug delivery systems and materials. The development of the concept of personalized medicine is built upon the more recent scientific achievements in genetics, proteins, and metabolites. These improvements involve not only a precise refining of diagnostic and treatment strategies but also an advance in patient outcomes and safety.

This chapter aims to identify the role of pharmacology and the drug delivery system and discuss the advantages of the personalized approach to treatment. Nanotechnology and biomaterials have come up with additional carriers that ensure that drugs are delivered directly to disease sites, hence increasing the efficiency of the procedures carried out and decreasing systemic toxicity. These continue to highlight the need to focus on the specific characteristics of patients when choosing therapeutic approaches for achieving maximum benefits.

I believe that the combining of data from multi-omics is a major step forward in the field of personalized medicine. Integration of genomic, proteomic, and metabolomic knowledge makes disease diagnosis and understanding disease pathways possible as well as the revealing of unknown biomarkers. Such an approach, at the same time, expands the options to create specific therapies and more specific treatment plans, which lead to higher efficiency of treatment, in general. According to the combination of different layers of omics, a better understanding of the biological and pathological levels is provided to design novel diagnosis and treatment approaches.

Molecular diagnostics, improved genomic sequencing, high-throughput proteomics, and artificial intelligence are among the technologies that have shaped personalized medicine. These technologies allow the possibility of working with extensive data related to biological sciences while providing practical information for use in clinical practice. As technological capabilities become more advanced, they also build on the use of precise and efficient individualized healthcare approaches.

As for the future, the further development of personalized medicine is indicated by present research and prospective improvements that would enlarge the sphere of application and the effectiveness of interventions. But the field also has to solve issues that pertain to ethics, data privacy, and bias toward providing personalized treatments. Whether it is in response to increased bureaucratic demands or improved pharmacogenomics research of the work, it ensures that these challenges are met with robust solutions, which are vital for the continued success of personalized medicine.
