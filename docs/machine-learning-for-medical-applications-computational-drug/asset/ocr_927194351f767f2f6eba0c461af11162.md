# 6.3.5 The achievement of interoperability with electronic health record systems

Interoperability of EHRs with digital health platforms for sharing health information and enhancing care coordination processes are two advantages of such platforms. Using the data collected by a digital health device with a patient’s EHR, it is possible for healthcare practitioners to have a comprehensive view of the entire patient record, their treatment plan, and the most recent data submitted by the device. This integration ensures that original data for the development of patient-specific programs are used and there is continuity of the treatment process, which increases the prospects of making correct decisions.

# 6.3.6 Prediction and transformation

Some of the studies also assume that while technology of information progresses, so does the integration of health application with personalized medicine. For instance, the development of more elaborate wearable gadgets is one emerging trend; secondly, the use of artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms; thirdly, the development of digital health assistants for particular diseases. Future advancements of PA consist in the development of improved predictive methods, increased personalization of the interventions delivered through digital technology, and compatibility with genomics and other omics data. All these advances propel the progress of precision medicine, in that it provides increased comprehensiveness and efficiency with regard to medical treatment.

# 6.4 Trends in personalized medicine research and clinical trials

The interdisciplinary area of the subject, called molecular medicine, is growing very fast due not only, but also owing to the progresses made in the research techniques and strategies, and clinical trials. Such trends are promising with regard to individualized approaches used in both research and clinical care recently, leading to the possibility of enhancing patient’s benefits and therapies efficacy.

# 6.4.1 The integration of multi-omics data

Personalized medicine research has observed a lot of progress in the analysis of multi-omics data including transcriptomics, proteomics, and metabolomics as well as genomes. The synergistic use of such forms of data provides a better understanding of patient heterogeneity and disease etiologies. Multi-omics approaches also hold the promise of discovery of new biomarkers, better understanding of complex biological pathways, and development of more precise treatment goals, for researchers.
