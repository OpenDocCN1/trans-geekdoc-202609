# 7 Conclusion

Personalized medicine is widely considered as the new general approach to disease diagnosis and treatment, as well as prevention. This chapter was devoted to the complex perspectives of the concept under discussion as it considers the synergy between personalized medicine and individual drug delivery systems and materials. The development of the concept of personalized medicine is built upon the more recent scientific achievements in genetics, proteins, and metabolites. These improvements involve not only a precise refining of diagnostic and treatment strategies but also an advance in patient outcomes and safety.

This chapter aims to identify the role of pharmacology and the drug delivery system and discuss the advantages of the personalized approach to treatment. Nanotechnology and biomaterials have come up with additional carriers that ensure that drugs are delivered directly to disease sites, hence increasing the efficiency of the procedures carried out and decreasing systemic toxicity. These continue to highlight the need to focus on the specific characteristics of patients when choosing therapeutic approaches for achieving maximum benefits.
