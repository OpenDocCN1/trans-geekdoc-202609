# 5 Personalized medicine approaches in drug delivery

# 5.1 ML-driven strategies for customizing drug delivery based on patient-specific data

Customized treatments for patients in drug delivery systems use big data and ML algorithms to reach acceptable efficacy and limited side effects. To make the thing work, various genomic, proteomic, metabolomic, and clinical data sets are enhanced by machine learning-based strategies to predict patient responses to drugs and ensure that drug delivery systems effectively respond to patient's needs.

Personalized medication delivery relies on the utilization of ML methods, including predictive analytics, to determine patient's characteristics for drug response. The large repository of cohorts can be used to train and test the machine learning algorithms like the random forests, the Support Vector Machines, and hopefully the neural networks, to get the understanding of which biomarkers are related to toxicity or effectiveness of medicine in which way. These models aid medical practitioners in selecting the correct treatment as well as the correct schedule plan to predict the reaction of a patient to a given drug. Retrospective clinical and laboratory variables, perhaps used in pharmacogenomic data analysis, can be employed in identifying the genetic anomalies that affect drug metabolism. This accounts for the development of techniques to arrive at the best therapeutic levels of drugs without being toxic.

A similar approach can also be seen as a unique strategy that is based on the use of ML algorithms to further enhance formulations and delivery systems with respective to drugs. If treating patients with a drug, then the drugs are given in the best manner to suit the given patient, considering his/her genetic profile, history regarding his/her medications, and the current state of the illness. This includes a selection of definite drug delivery systems, including liposomes or nanoparticles, in a manner that spares healthy tissues, while focusing on delivering medications directly to the diseased area. It can be seen that the application of the ML algorithms can enhance the therapeutic output with the least possible administrative doses by tailoring release profile of the formulations for sustained and controlled release.

Other AI-applied aspects also include the design and optimization of rationally up-to-date combination regimens. With the help of integrating patient-level data and drug combination network, the ML model can determine the synergistic drugs that give better therapeutic value compared to single-drug regimen. It enables one to predict the right molecule proportion and the specific dosage schedule to deliver the highest level of cure with minimal adverse effect impact. In the case of chronic illnesses such as cancer, the treatment of which requires the use of complex preparations that affect the multiple paths and processes characteristic of the disease process, this approach is very useful.
