# 5.2 Machine learning in biomaterial surface characterization

A key component of biomaterial research is surface characterization, which provides essential insights into the chemical and physical properties of material surfaces factors that critically influence biological interactions and functional performance. Traditional analytical methods such as Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM), Atomic Force Microscopy (AFM), and X-ray Photoelectron Spectroscopy (XPS) are widely used to examine surface topography, roughness, and chemical composition. These techniques generate detailed information about surface morphology and elemental distribution, which is crucial for understanding how biomaterials interact with biological tissues. The high volume and complexity of the data produced by these methods present challenges for data interpretation and analysis.
