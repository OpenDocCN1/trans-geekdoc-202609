# 4.3.3 Difficulties and implications of MCMC

MCMC techniques have great advantages in material design; however, they also present certain difficulties. The first major issue relates to the transition to the target distribution by the Markov chain, especially if the distribution is in a high-dimensional space or has multiple modes. Common mistakes made when selecting proposal distributions can slow down convergence and even affect the results. The use of MCMC techniques is accompanied by high computational costs, especially when it involves large-scale material design problems. This requires the application of better sampling techniques or superior parallel computing methods. The application of MCMC in material science is not without these difficulties; these challenges make it important to understand both the material system under study and the stochastic models being used.
