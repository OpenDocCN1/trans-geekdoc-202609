Simulation and computational modeling: Computational simulations avail the ML analyses by anticipating the nanoparticle response at molecular and macroscopic levels. Computer simulations at the atomic level model nanoparticle-biological system interactions, as well as drug release dynamics and nanoparticle stability. Data-driven insights and iterative refinement: ML models make use of information to enhance nanoparticle designs in several cycles depending on what ML observes either from empirical testing or computation models. Emerging trends: With the evolution of techniques in ML, the applications of nanoparticles in drug delivery are enhancing the efficiency of therapeutic and precision medicine, and translation to clinical levels.
