# 3.3.5 Patient and caregiver involvement

The involvement of the patient and the caregiver in the process of therapy is an essential part of the individualization strategy. An equally important aspect is improving patient’s and his family’s understanding of the nature of the disease, rationale for individualized management, and the possible course. Optimal coordination and mutual cooperation in decision-making also leads the patient to be more compliant to the set care plan and ensures satisfaction toward the received care.

# 3.3.6 Ethical and privacy issues

Personalized treatment is focused on the patient’s individual needs, and important ethical and privacy concerns can be attributed to the genetic and health information. It concerns their protection and confidentiality through specific consent processes, as necessary for patient information. Patients’ rights, together with the rules and laws referring to the practice of personalized medicine, must be respected to ensure that patients’ rights are well protected.
