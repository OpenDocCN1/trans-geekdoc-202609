# 6.5.1 New trends in artificial intelligence and machine learning

There is an expectation that ML and AI technologies would play an important role in the future of personalized medicine. The assessment of complex information improves due to progressive shifts in AI algorithms and the ability to make better accurate predictions of patient outcomes, the effectiveness of the treatments proposed, and the statistical probability of disease development. The ability to discover more biomarkers, enhance treatments, and enhance clinical determination abilities, all benefit from machine learning models. Personalized healthcare can increase its innovative and effective approach as AI and ML technologies develop.

# 6.5.2 Potential of precision oncology

Precision medicine is expected to be further defined and practiced extensively as subsequent studies aim at defining further unique cancer-related genetic and molecular markers. New therapies under development offer better treatment for cancer patients such as the ones going for treatment based on tumor genomics and their immune profiles. Technological development in liquid biopsy and tumor sequencing provides additional capacities to track disease development and personalize therapy strategies according to the patient's characteristics.

# 6.5.2.1 Personalized medicine in rare and genetic diseases

It is forecasted that application of personalized medicine in rare and genetic diseases will increase due to improved genetic testing and gene therapies. Enhancement of gene editing systems like crispr-cas9 paves the way toward the creation of disease-specific drugs. Increased knowledge of rare diseases from genomic studies allow for accurate diagnostic tools with individual therapeutic regimens, resulting in better treatment results of patients with diseases that were hard to manage before.
