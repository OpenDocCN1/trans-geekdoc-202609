There is a growing concern regarding the effective use of high-throughput data in biomaterials research, making the processes of data preprocessing, integration, and standardization critically important. The complexity and diversity of data sources necessitate the development of unified protocols and standardized formats to ensure consistency across research groups and institutions. This standardization is essential because comparing datasets across different models requires them to be in compatible formats, enabling accurate and meaningful analysis. Consolidating heterogeneous data types into coherent and structured datasets allows researchers to extract insights that might be missed when working with fragmented data. By adopting collective data storage and analysis standards, the biomaterials community can foster a collaborative and integrated research environment, accelerating the discovery and development of advanced materials. The prospect of high-throughput data acquisition in biomaterials design lies in the advancement of these cutting-edge technologies including artificial intelligence (AI) and IoT. Data collection systems using AI remain one of the most potent means of automating conventional experimental procedures, making it possible to select favorable experimental conditions and modify protocols based on real-time data analysis. IoT devices can monitor biomaterials’ exposure to real-world conditions without interruption, which is crucial for improving materials based on the data received.
