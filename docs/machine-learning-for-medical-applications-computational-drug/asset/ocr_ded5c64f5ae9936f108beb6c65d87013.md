# 6 Applications of machine learning in biomedical material discovery

# 6.1 Predictive modeling for material biocompatibility

Using ML and a data-driven approach, we were able to create predictive modeling for material biocompatibility, which marks a major leap in the adoption of AI-based approaches in the biomedical material discovery field. Another important feature experienced in tissue material was biocompatibility, which referred to compatibility with the biological environment where it was used without adverse effects. There has been a gradual application of ML approaches to define and forecast the biocompatibility of materials, which has proved to be an exquisite technique to enhance patient benefits and the rate of material innovation.

The very essence of predictive modeling for material biocompatibility lies in the creation of suitable methods that analyze how new materials behave in biological environments and the creation of methods that can predict the given behavior. These models use information derived from material properties, results from biological assays, and clinical findings to make inferences about biocompatibility. The combination of various kinds of data allows for the use of features that are not easily detected by conventional approaches, improving the capacity to model material interactions with biological tissues.

The most widely used technique in biocompatibility prediction modeling is the use of supervised learning algorithms. These models are trained with labeled datasets containing details of the biocompatibility of different materials. The model is trained in paradigms that correlate chemical composition, mechanical properties, and surface features to biocompatibility results. These features are then input into the model. Once trained, the model can predict if other unknown and unspecified materials are biocompatible, which saves time in the evaluation and minimizes the necessity of sophisticated in vitro and in vivo testing.
