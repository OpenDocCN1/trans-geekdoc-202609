# 2.5 Current market trends and innovations in biomedical sensors

The biomedical sensors market is also growing at a very high rate and is continuously changing, which is attributed to the increasing incorporation of sophisticated novel technology for health monitoring applications. This part provides an overview of the emerging trends that are prevalent in the most contemporary area of biomedical sensors.

# 2.5.1 Emergence of wearable sensors

Wearable sensors are used widely nowadays because of the comfort and simultaneity of measurements in comparison to other methods. These are wearable sensors that can be worn in devices like smart watches, fitness trackers, as well as smart cloth; the aim of these sensors is to track parameters such as heart rate, physical activity, and even sleep. New advancements have aimed at improving the precision and wearability of these gadgets and by using kinetic textiles with features of flexibility and elasticity that adapt to the body more closely, with increased performance and reliability. The integration of far more sophisticated biosensors in wearables has also enhanced their ability to monitor parameters such as blood glucose levels, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation, and deliver full-body health updates to the user.
