# 3.5 The interplay between personalized medicine and conventional healthcare approaches

Personalized medicine can be regarded as a great leap beyond the traditional systems of delivering an individual treatment, as it shifts to the core of creating and applying medical treatments. Whereas conventional medicine has always focused on standardized solutions applicable to as many cases as possible due to their universality, personalized medicine is built around the idea that has never been so much as possible due to holistic approaches combining genetic, environmental, and individual life characteristics of the patient.

Traditional medical management techniques are notable for their tendency toward uniformity and usage of general norms and average standards. Standard methods of treating an illness entail prescribing identical treatment procedures to all patients with the same disease, according to the averaged results derived from research studies. Although this treatment approach has worked well in many patients, its weakness is that it does not factor issues such as divergent disease presentation, differences in the rate of drug metabolism, and differences in drug sensitivity among patients leading to variation in the outcomes.

Personalized medicine overcomes many traditional constraints by aiming to deliver tailored healthcare solutions that align with each patient’s unique needs. As illustrated in Figure 6, recent advances in genomics have enabled detailed mapping of individual genetic profiles and their associations with disease susceptibility and treatment outcomes. When combined with data on lifestyle and environmental factors, personalized medicine facilitates the development of treatment regimens that are precisely aligned with each individual’s genetic makeup.

The complementarity of precision medicine with conventional treatments can be illustrated in several areas. For example, genetic and molecular forward practice improves the traditional practice through focusing on the patient individual characteristics. In oncology, for instance, while routine approaches to management involve conventional therapeutic protocols, individualized medicine entails molecular characterization of tumors to determine unique molecular changes and then prescribe drugs accordingly. Combination of individual approaches with standard treatment procedures elevates the effectiveness of interventions, besides making them specific, which in turn enhances the overall treatment processes.
