# 5.5 Long-term monitoring solutions for geriatric care

Intermittent monitoring systems are important in geriatric care because elderly patients often suffer from multiple chronic illnesses requiring comprehensive management. The use of long-term remote biomedical sensors offers significant advantages by improving the quality of care, enhancing health outcomes, and enabling timely interventions for older adults.

# 5.5.1 Wearable health monitoring devices

According to findings, wearable health monitoring devices are widely used in the management of chronic diseases in the geriatric population. These devices are easy to wear, avoiding any inconvenience to the patient, and are intended for constant monitoring of different vital parameters, including pulse, blood pressure, and blood sugar level. Some of the enhanced wearables also target aspects such as falls, physical activity, and sleep, which are essential for elderly users. For example, Smartwatches with cardiovascular features can help caregivers if a child is experiencing cardiac arrhythmias blood pressure.

# 5.5.2 Remote health monitoring systems

Tele-care technologies enable continuous acquisition and transmission of health information from the elderly to the healthcare givers without having to make frequent home visits. Such systems often include measurements by sensors of indices like glucose, pressure, and saturation of oxygen. Information is sent over secured networks to the healthcare givers, who in turn can work on the information to make informed decisions on the changes in the treatment plan.
