# 6.4 Future directions in leveraging ML to enhance drug delivery efficiency

The enhancement of drug delivery is soon expected to change significantly due to the improvements in the techniques of machine learning. By using real-time monitoring systems, applying advanced analytical methods and striving for individual approaches to the patients’ treatment, the integration of ML technologies offers potential to enhance the efficiency of medicines administration. These new paths are expected to revolutionize the pharmaceutical industry and consequently lead to improved patients’ experiences and effective treatments.

One of the most possible future scenarios is making highly customized medicine delivery systems. Patient data, particularly genetics, daily habits, and environment structure as well as features, should be input into the ML algorithms to come up with a proper administration of medicine for the patient. Thanks to such an approach, each patient gets the highest quality treatment with minimal side effects possible. Accomplishments can include the integration of the pharmacogenomic data into machine learning model that would predict patients’ metabolism of the prescribed medications, making it easy to adjust dosage or choose the most effective agents. These models, therefore, can be continually improved in terms of their accuracy and efficiency by -related refinement.

Future medication delivery optimization is considered to be highly dependent on the form of further sophisticated and superior predictive analytical systems based on machine learning. These models are fairly accurate at determining what is going to transpire in the disease progression, how a therapy is going to act, and even the nature of side effects. This includes the ability of the ML algorithms to identify minute patterns and trends with real-time and historical data in a way that normal techniques cannot. Healthcare professionals are able to prevent such occurrences by adjusting dosage regimes before negative occurrences occur, or select patients who stand to benefit from specific drugs, because of the use of predictive models.
