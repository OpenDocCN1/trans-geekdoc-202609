# 4.5 Emerging tools and technologies enhancing personalized care

Personalized medicine has thus undergone a paradigm shift since the development of new tools as well as technology that enhance the ability to fashion healthcare to meet individual patient's needs. These changes offer new opportunities for precise diagnosis, effective treatment, and improved patient prognoses.

# 4.5.1 Next-generation sequencing

The technology of NGS has revolutionized the genomic analysis due to the opportunity of sequencing whole genomes at a relatively low cost and in a short time. Whole-genome sequencing (WGS), whole-exome sequencing (WES), and targeted sequencing – these techniques produce comprehensive genetic data that is instrumental in identifying the corresponding genetic variations associated with different pathologies, large-scale genomic studies, and inclusion of genetic information into clinical practice, being enabled by the principles of high-throughput sequencing of tens of thousands of samples, which contributes to further developments of individualized diagnostic and therapeutic management strategies.

# 4.5.2 Next-generation proteomics platforms

High-throughput proteomics technologies such as mass spectrometry and protein microarrays are two exemplar ways that have greatly enhanced the analysis of protein expression, modification, and communication. These systems help in the examination of hundreds of proteins at once, revealing new insights into what goes on in cells, as well as what can go wrong. By finding and measure the protein biomarkers, researchers have a possibility to make individualized therapeutics and better understand the development of a disease. These technologies also play a role in identifying many new targets for therapies and designing treatment regimens based on specific protein signatures.
