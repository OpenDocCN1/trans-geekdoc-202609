These methods allow for considering relationships between genetic differences and disease manifestations since designed to address the problems of increased dimensionality and data heterogeneity in multiomics studies. Molecular methods provide great insight into etiology of certain health disorders by integrating the information obtained from multiple sources with the help of computation. This makes it possible to develop customized programs of treatment that incorporate all aspects of the patient’s biology.

Bioinformatics is again able to develop models that would help predict treatment plans of patients. Applying statistical models and machine learning algorithm on the patients’ data the efficacy of medicine in a given patient is predicted. Using these predictive models according to the genetic and clinical data contributes to the calculation of the optimal therapeutic process and minimizes the possibilities of using ineffective treatment options in the further analysis.

Further, bioinformatics tools can be useful for designing and fine-tuning of targeted drug delivery systems. Computer-aided tools are used to estimate the behavior of molecules and their relationship with a biological target and how they would be processed in different persons. This capability enables treatment through formulation of drugs and methods to reach the intended patient so that therapeutic outcomes improve while side effects reduce.

Bioinformatics in combination with clinical practice has made the usage of precision medicine easier in routine of a healthcare system. Clinical decision support systems and electronic health records are getting supplemented step-by-step with bioinformatics tools to provide implementable information at the point of care. The results of these instances are to enable healthcare practitioners to make informed decisions in relation to patient care and treatment by integrating the genetic information with clinical details – which is the aim of the personalized medicine.
