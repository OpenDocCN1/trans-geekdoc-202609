# 3.3.2 The use of images in the determination of material characteristics

In particular, image analysis tasks are well solved with the help of deep learning models, CNNs in particular, which are very beneficial when characterizing materials. In biomedical materials science, these models are employed to manipulate microscopy images and other image-enhancing data in terms of material morphology, defects, and features. Deep learning minimizes the extraction of information from complicated images and improves the time required to characterize the materials.
