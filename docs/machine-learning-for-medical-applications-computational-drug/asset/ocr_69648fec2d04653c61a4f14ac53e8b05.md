# 2.2 The role of genomics in shaping modern personalized medicine

Genomics have helped the development of the modern so-called individualized medicine, as it has transformed the conventional paradigms of disease management, diagnosis and treatment. The reflection on the novelty of genomics in the medical practice is evident, given that the availability of this sort of technology enables much better understanding of the relevant genetic roots of numerous diseases and the subsequent more refined technique of approaching their treatments.

# 2.2.1 Newer methods of genomic sequencing

The prospective and innovation of analyzing DNA sequences as well as their capacity has been enhanced by advancements in genomic sequencing, including first-generation Sanger technique to the second-generation NGS. The discipline is said to have experienced a revolution because of NGS technology, enabling the rapid and comprehensive sequencing of whole genomes, whole exomes, and targeted regions. This skill has facilitated the identification of research-based treatment strategies through establishing a variety of disease-associated genetic variants and thereby contributing to the easy development of alternative treatment profiles for each patient.

# 2.2.2 Discovery of gene signatures and diseases links

Genomic research has explained the complicated relationship between genes and susceptibility to illness by identifying these variations. By organizing variations in the form of insertions, deletions, and single nucleotide polymorphisms, several researchers have been able to associate some genetic anomalies with certain diseases. Based on this information, genetic markers that are important for risk assessment, diagnosis, and development of disease-specific patient treatments have been discovered.
