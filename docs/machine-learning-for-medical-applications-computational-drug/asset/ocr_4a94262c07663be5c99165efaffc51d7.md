After understanding the cause of those related diseases, began the discovery of what are today known as targeted therapies.

The mid-twentieth century marked a pivotal shift toward more precise and personalized treatments, largely driven by advances in medical research and technology. Increased understanding of specific disease mechanisms and the identification of molecular targets enabled the development of targeted drug delivery systems. As illustrated in Figure 1, this era saw the emergence of therapies directed at particular biological pathways, allowing for more tailored treatment approaches. Notably, the advent of targeted cancer therapies aimed at defined genetic markers in tumors represented a significant advancement over traditional, non-specific chemotherapy.
