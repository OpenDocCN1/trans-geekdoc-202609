Recent material engineering developments have made possible the detection of bio-sensors with enhanced biocompatibility, and increased their sturdiness. Emerging technologies include the use of flexible electronics, materials that can dissolve in the body after performing a certain function, and protective coatings that reduce sensor interaction with body tissues, instead giving them better interaction, while reducing inflammation. For example, stretchability allows the development of thin plane, form-fitting sensors for long-term skin-worn applications. Better biocompatibility also means that there are few issues with the use of such sensors in implantable devices, hence improving the lot of patients.
