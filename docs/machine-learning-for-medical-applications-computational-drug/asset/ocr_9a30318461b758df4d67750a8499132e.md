# 5 Challenges and limitations

# 5.1 Accuracy and reliability

Precision is the core characteristic that defines efficiency and clinical applicability of BM sensors and gadgets. When using these devices, it is very important that they would give accurate measurements and consistently provide correct results to allow correct diagnosis, therapy, and patient protection. Some of the factors due to which one can't get highly accurate and reliable results are the factors that can affect the success of such technologies and their acceptance in clinical practice.

Variability in sensor performance remains one of the main issues affecting accuracy and reliability of measurements. Biomedical sensors are prone to many enhancements such as environmental influence, user influence, and damage to sensors. For instance, changes in temperature with humidity and electromagnetic interfere affect the performance of the sensors, leading to deviation from true values. Monitoring the sensors’ performance at different conditions is necessary through meticulous periodic checks, profound control procedures, as well as keenly constructed design, to minimize interferences from external environment conditions on the sensors.
