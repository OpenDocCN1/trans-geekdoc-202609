# 5.3.2 Molecular and individualized treatment

Most diseases, and especially cancer, have been upgraded from the traditional way of treating to personal treatment. The ultimate objective of targeted therapy is to selectively interact with actionable molecular targets, which are implicated in futuristic causation of the disease. These treatments often enhance the drug efficacy and decrease adverse effects due to the focus on special genetic abnormalities. There are many examples of small-molecules, such as tyrosine kinase inhibitors imatinib in chronic myeloid leukemia (CML), where it has increased case outcomes and reduced side effects compared to conventional chemotherapy.
