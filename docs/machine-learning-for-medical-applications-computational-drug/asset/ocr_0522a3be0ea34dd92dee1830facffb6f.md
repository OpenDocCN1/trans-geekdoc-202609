# 6.5.4 Work to produce materials useful for the implementation of personalized medicine

A case study in the biomedical application of personalized medicine demonstrates how ML can help match specific biomedical materials to specific individuals. Using data on patients and their individual genomic profiles and disease states, the ML models determined the materials whose characteristics matched the respective patients’ needs in therapy. For instance, ML-based solutions applied to drug development created individual drug carriers based on the patient’s pharmacogenomic data. It enhanced the medical treatment’s efficiency and safety and showed how the concept of ML can elevate patient experiences in terms of materials.

# 6.5.5 A high-throughput screening of antibody-drug conjugate

In seeking potent ADCs, the application of the ML technique has helped reduce the high-throughput screening process. Using ML on big data sets containing interactions of antibodies and drugs, researchers have been able to identify candidate ADCs with desirable characteristics such as high binding affinity and cytotoxicity. Namely, the ML approach allowed for an almost instantaneous hit list generation, thus greatly enhancing the prospects for new ADC therapies in cancer targeting.

# 6.5.6 Application of regenerative medicine

A case study in regenerative medicine shows how one can apply this field of ML to predict the properties of materials used in regenerative therapies. Scientists use ML models with the intention of predicting the mechanical strength, degradation characteristics, and biological responses of new biomaterials. These models, based on historical data and simulations, offer key information about the behavior of various materials and open a way toward the creation of new materials more suited to specific regenerative applications.
