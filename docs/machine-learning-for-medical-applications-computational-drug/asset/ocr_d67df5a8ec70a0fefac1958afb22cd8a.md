# 5.1.4 Enhanced diagnostic precision due to the inclusion of multi-omics data

Combining genomics with proteomics and metabolomics data allows the clinician to pinpoint the disease pathology accurately because it covers different aspects of the abnormalities in the human body. This integrative approach enables researchers to identify biomarkers that perhaps are invisible across any singular omics range. For instance, integration of genomic information with proteomic and metabolomic profiles provides numerous comprehensive interactions and alterations that transpire as precursors of diseases and inflammation. This overall perspective consequently increases the accuracy of diagnostics and promptly identifies diseases.

# 5.1.5 Supplementary surveillance of disease status

Personalized medicine also enhances the communication on disease status since there is frequent diagnosis and data capture. Wearable health technology devices and remote health monitoring systems facilitate the monitoring of state biosignatures for disease conditions. This enables her to follow up on a given disease’s notifications and prognosis, indicating how a disease is changing and how a patient is improving with treatment. For instance, in diabetics, the monitors that measure blood sugar levels offer real-time information to aid in changing the management techniques to enhance the disease state.

# 5.1.6 Enabling specific client management

The fact is that with the help of increased diagnostic accuracy and early detection of the disease, individual therapeutic guidelines that are tailored to the individual characteristics of each patient’s illness are worked out. Clinicians select the most effective interventions after understanding the specific nature of the disease when making a proper diagnosis. Tumor profiling, for instance, can be applied in oncology where through genome analysis of specific mutations can help in targeting by individualized medicines, leaving out deadly and unsuccessful treatment options.
