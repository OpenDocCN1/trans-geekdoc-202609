When phenotypic information is introduced, healthcare providers are able to use targeted outcomes to treat displayed symptoms of a disease because the treatment is matched to the phenotype of the affected individual. # 3.4.4 Lifestyle and environmental variables in personalization

Factors such as life style, information about diet and physical activity, and exposure to environmental risks make up a considerable part of the concept of personalization of healthcare. Such factors affect the disease occurrence and response to therapies, therefore, their inclusion in individualized management enhances patient care. For instance, lifestyle information can inform the best nutritional changes routines that add onto medication treatments, improving the general wellbeing and helping the treatment to be effective.

# 3.4.5 Data integration and advance analytics

As many and diverse patient data sources are needed for the purposes of genomic, clinical, phenotypic, and lifestyle integration, enhanced analytics are needed to offer meaningful knowledge of the subject. Structured datasets call for integration and analysis to achieve superior values by using sophisticated analysis like artificial intelligence. By adopting these technologies, it is able to identify relationship patterns and trends that can improve prediction for healthcare management and tailor patient treatment plans.

This new shift of focusing on symptomatic, accurate, and personalized system of healthcare is evident from the proper utilization of patient data in personal health. Clinicians develop specific intervention plans to meet patients’ unique needs by integrating an extensive amount of data, leading to improvement in patient status and quality of care.
