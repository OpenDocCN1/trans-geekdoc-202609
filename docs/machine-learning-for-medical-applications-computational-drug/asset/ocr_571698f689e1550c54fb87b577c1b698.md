# 6.4 Integration of AI with phenotypic screening platforms

The combining of AI and phenotypic screening platforms is a major step forward in preclinical analysis, providing scientists with the chance to discover complex associations and potential drug targets. Phenotypic screening is the process of testing of small molecules on cell readouts that can reveal information about a biological process, a disease model, activity, and toxicity. The application of AI techniques in phenotypic screening assays improves the speed, selectivity, and reliability of the process enabling faster discovery of therapeutic leads.

Another significant application of AI in phenotypic screening lies in the processing and interpretation of high-content imaging data. High-content screening (HCS) assays generate massive volumes of cellular images that reflect morphological, spatial, and temporal changes in response to chemical treatments. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and other deep learning architectures can be trained on these HCS datasets to identify phenotypic patterns, quantify cellular responses, and predict small molecule activities.
