Feature engineering and selection: Feature selection is a vital step in determining useful nanoparticle characteristics that are associated with specific performance attributes. Different selection methods like PCA, genetic algorithm, and RFE are used in ML algorithms to reduce the burden of choosing features. The selection of those features that are most relevant to nanoparticle attributes of interest, such as encapsulation efficiency and release profiles, helps balance the complexity of the model, improve forecasting capabilities, and identify specific changes in the design of nanoparticles.

Supervised learning for predictive modeling: In the realm of supervised learning algorithms in frameworks of ML, usage of the labeled data that has been obtained from diverse experimental works and computation simulations and predictions regarding the attributes of nanoparticles and their performance are made. Regression analysis (linear, support vector) is used to measure the formulation variables such as polymer composition, drug loading, and the behaviors of nanoparticles, for example, stability and bioavailability. These models enhance approaches toward formulation and predict properties of nanocarriers as well as guide successive improvement of nanoparticle designs according to well-defined therapeutic goals, more accurately and effectively.
