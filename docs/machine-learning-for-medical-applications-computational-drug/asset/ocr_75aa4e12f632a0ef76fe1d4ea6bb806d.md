# Pharmacogenomics: The new perspective (2000s).

At the beginning of the new millennium, the phrase pharmacogenomics, the investigation of how a person's genes are affected by the reaction to drugs, emerged into mainstream society. Pharmacogenomics has offered new identification of suitable medication regimens that suit individual genetic make up to enhance therapeutic effectiveness, and lessen the side effects.

# Personalized genomic testing: Making strides (Phase 3: Contemporary)

With the appearance of the simple and affordable services of direct-to-consumer genomic testing and internet, people could receive information about themselves, including their genetic risks and ancestry. These tests have given the public useful knowledge regarding genetic information and how it can be used for health purposes.
