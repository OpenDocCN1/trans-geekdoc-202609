# 2 Historical evolution of personalized medicine

# 2.1 Early developments in personalized medicine

The genesis of the idea of PM can be dated back to the systematic inclusion of patient characteristics into medical practice though rudimentary means. The notion of individualized medicine can be traced back to the ancient civilizations, where people standards of management of diseases included initial adaptation of management according to patient’s distinguishing features and manifestations. The concept of developing dynamically unique medicine for every person has been in practice with the improvement of scientific and up-to-date technology.

The foundation of modern genetics and its relevance to health and disease was established in the early nineteenth century with Gregor Mendel’s discovery of inheritance patterns. Mendelian genetics introduced the fundamental principles of heredity, demonstrated through Mendel’s experiments on pea plants. These foundational concepts remain essential to the advancement of personalized medicine.
