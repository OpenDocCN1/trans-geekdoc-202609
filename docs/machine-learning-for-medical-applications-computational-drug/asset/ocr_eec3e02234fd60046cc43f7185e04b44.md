# 4.5.6 Advanced imaging techniques

New imaging techniques like MRI, PET scans, and multiple photon microscopy give a high-resolution and often time-lapse snap shots of the biological activity. Motorized imaging systems of this kind allows direct observation of the evolution of the disease, the effectiveness of treatment, and changes in the physiology of the body's tissues in the process of the progress. Incorporation of imaging with molecular information in the clinic improves disease profiling and outcome measures since this combines anatomic and physiologic information into one assessment.

By the steady progression of these novel instruments and equipment, personalized medicines' progress is moving forward as it provides improved diagnostic accuracy and formulating directed treatment and corresponding patient care. These technologies, therefore, hold the potential of expanding the capacity to individualize medical therapies to be of even higher value for strengthening our health and increasing the quality of care.

# 5 Benefits and impact on healthcare outcomes

# 5.1 Improved diagnostic accuracy and early detection

Customized medicine deal with early illness identification and diagnosis accuracy, yielding better patient outcomes. Again, the improvements in diagnostic technology and a combination of proteomic, metabolomic, and genomic factors are what are pushing this forward.

# 5.1.1 Greater accuracy when identifying the disease

In general, the delivery of precise and effective treatment is significantly enhanced through the integration of comprehensive genetic, proteomic, and metabolic data. Advanced tools capable of detecting specific biomarkers and genetic variations associated with diseases enable clinicians to improve diagnostic accuracy.
