# 6.4.2 Molecular/precision medicine/genetics: oncology

On top of PM research, there are early frameworks in precision oncology, an area that looks into targeting cancer treatments based on the genealogy and molecular characteristics of a tumor. Recent advances include the emergence of treatments from concept to the development of treatments, depending on the specific genetic alterations and utilization of NGS to identify mutations that are actively managed. It has become increasingly common for genomic profiling for therapy choice and evaluation of the efficiency of combination and target medicines in clinical trials. A more extensive pattern of enhancing cancer treatment and analyzing the outcomes is featured by the growth of precision oncology.

# 6.4.3 Patient-centric clinical trial designs

K-amplified patient-preference clinical trials are being conducted with increased frequency; patient preference clinical trials are clinical trials for which patients’ need and preference are given priority in the clinical trial process. This trend includes distant remote and data collection that are made possible with decentralized clinical trials as well as flexible trial design to allow for modifications based on the interim analysis. Patient-centered methods aim at increasing the connection between trial outcomes and measures of patient-reported outcome and quality of life, enhancing trial accrual/retention and enhancing the application of trials in different types of patients.

# 6.4.3.1 Fostering of real-world evidence (RWE)

There is also a rising trend in using RWE to complement conventional clinical trial data in personalized medicine studies. In its design, RWE provides details about the effectiveness and safety of treatments in more extensive patient populations and is derived from real-world data, including EHRs, claims data, and patient registries. Trials that incorporate RWE to examine new differential knowledge make valuable contributions, back up systematic decisions with clinical evidence, and offset contextual failures, since personalized medicine is grounded in research.
