Another important activity of applying AI in data processing is real-time analysis of the data collected by sensors. Currently, AI algorithms can analyze flowing data from multiple sensors to identify various issues, patterns, and deviations from typical healthy values. This real-time analysis is important in alerting the patients as well as healthcare givers thus maintaining close follow-up on any health complications likely to arise.

AI employs more advanced statistical and computational methods on data to increase the accuracy of the interpretation of the data. Stationary datasets, with many records are used to teach machine learning algorithms for pattern and correlation generation for diagnostic and prognostic purposes. Through such techniques, the models learn with time and are also adaptive to new information and this results in more improved health evaluations.

AI also facilitates ways to fuse and integrate data from multiple sensors. Information is obtained continuously from various sensors administrators in chronic health monitoring systems such as pulse rate, blood glucose level, and motion. All these multisensor data can be combined by appropriate AI algorithms and provide a full picture of a patient's health state. AI ensures that health information covers the entire picture as each source of information is considered.

There has been an acceleration of emerging data processing solution based on artificial intelligence for personal health management. Thanks to analyzing health information of an individual a patient, an AI solution is capable of indicating specific patterns and biomarkers unique to a particular patient. It makes appropriate health advice and treatment suggestions with solutions that can increase the impact of health interventions and advance the health of patients.

# 4.2 Enhancing accuracy and precision of sensors

It is worth emphasizing that AI has developed as an important supporting factor in improving the efficiency of biomedical sensors that are used in punctual health monitoring applications, more specifically, improving the level of accuracy that is provided by existing sensors. Through the combination of AI algorithms with sensors, some major issues concerning data credibility and measurement inaccuracy that are prevalent in the present system are solved, resulting in better heath evaluations.

AI polishes biomedical sensors through altering the manner in which data is processed to enhance the level of accuracy obtained. Among the machine learning strategies that are used to analyze the data of the sensors and identify those patterns, which are not apparent, support vector machines and neural networks are perhaps some of the most well-known. These algorithms can pick slightly different patterns owing to their training on huge databases; such differences suggest errors in sensors. Due to the real-time error detection and correction features, measurements are more accurate and more trustworthy.
