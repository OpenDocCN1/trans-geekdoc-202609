# 5.2.3 Individual preventive interventions

Genetic testing is also used in wording treatment goals to genetic test results to not only include therapeutic initiatives but also preventive ones. Known inherited susceptibilities for specific diseases can be established and can pinpoint people who are likely to develop specific diseases, including cardiovascular and cancer. These assessments make it possible to introduce individual preventive interventions such as changes in life styles, routine investigations, and chemoprevention. Thus, that approach is timely in checking disease incidences and managing the same, hence leading to perfect health in the future.

# 5.2.4 Patient participation and compliance

Treatments that have been developed considering the patients’ genetic makeup help to improve patients’ compliance to the therapy. If patient’s trust the treatment that was developed considering the genetic makeup of their body, they are most likely to understand the worth of the prescribed therapy and follow through on the treatment plan that has been given to them. Having increased compliance improves therapeutic outcomes and generally the satisfaction of the patients.

The incorporation of gene analysis into the treatment process is one of the biggest revolutions in medicine, which has many advantages: better diagnosis, the choice of the most suitable medication, better outcomes, and minimum side effects of treatments. Personalized medicine therefore applies genetic information to target and deliver more efficient and effective treatments, with the patient at the core of the treatment.

# 5.3 Enhancing drug efficacy and reducing adverse drug reactions

As a result of the improved medicines by disease- and symptom-specific medications, medicine effectiveness has gone higher, while the occurrence of ADRs has reduced, thereby enhancing the success of the therapeutic treatments. Issues related to medicine efficacy and safety of diverse patient groups are solved in personalized medicine that adapts treatments for individual patient characteristics like genotype, proteome, and metabolome.
