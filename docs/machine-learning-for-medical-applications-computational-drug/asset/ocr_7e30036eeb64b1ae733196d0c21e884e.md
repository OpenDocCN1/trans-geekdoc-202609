# 5.5.4 Better outpatients’ involvement and satisfaction

There are always generally individualistic treatments that are given by a physician, depending on the condition of a client and what they need to go through. This notion enhances patient participation since treatment plans consider patients’ preference and situation. Incorporating the patient into their care improves their compliance with treatment regimens, decreases the chances for complications, and even results in a more positive experience. Patient satisfaction is added to the quality of life by focusing on health and has a direct impact on the quality of health and on the organization of care.
