# ### 5.4.4 Minimization of the adverse drug reaction

Due to considerations of patient variability in drug metabolism and effectiveness, customized medicine significantly reduces the probability of side effects in chronic diseases stabilization. Through pharmacogenomic testing, it is possible to identify genetic factors associated with increased risk of side effects’ interactions. For instance, patients with heart disease can use genetic testing to select antihypertensive medicines and minimal side effects. This kind of approach makes therapies safer and acceptable and results in better outcomes for patients besides reducing overall the cost of the health facilities.

# ### 5.4.5 Inspiring resource management

Custom care enhances efficiency in resource management in chronic disease treatment because it aims at intervention, where it has the most impact. Understanding a patient’s biomarkers – including genetic predispositions – can help the medical system locate patients who are at the highest risk for complications to benefit from certain treatments and direct the resources to be used effectively. Such a selective model minimizes the exposure and risks from nonrelated treatment and hospitalization, thus minimizing healthcare expense and maximizing the effectiveness of the disease management program.
