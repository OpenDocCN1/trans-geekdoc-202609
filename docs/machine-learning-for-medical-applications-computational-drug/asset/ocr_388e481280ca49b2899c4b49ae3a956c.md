# 6.3 Addressing the challenges of model generalization

The integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI) into biomaterial design introduces significant ethical concerns, particularly regarding the transparent documentation of the design process. In line with the second research question, a key challenge lies in understanding how AI models make decisions, as many operate through complex and non-intuitive algorithms. To address this, a sufficient level of transparency is essential detailing not only the data sources used to train the models but also the algorithmic structures and reasoning mechanisms behind their outputs. Researchers are encouraged to implement systematic methods to evaluate and respond to the consequences of AI-driven decisions on the availability, effectiveness, and outcomes of biomaterials. The study underscores the importance of developing and adhering to robust ethical standards and principles that promote the reliable, explainable, and trustworthy application of AI in biomaterial development.
