# 4.4.1.1 Treatments based on clients’ individual characteristics

Fortunately, to solve such tasks, machine learning models consider patient-unique characteristics, which is an essential element of the individual therapeutic regimens. Thus, the indispensable algorithms would allow individual therapy recommendations based on the data of genetic researches, clinical trials, as well as outcomes of other patients treated until now. In cancer, for instance, machine learning algorithms look at traits of genes present in the tumor to suggest proper medications that are likely to be effective based on the patient’s particularity. This method minimizes side effects and optimizes the efficacy of therapy since treatment alternatives are individualized, based on patient’s biological characteristics.

# 4.4.2 Drug development optimization

Through using AI and ML, different stages of the drug discovery and development process are being enhanced. With the help of such algorithms as machine learning algorithms, one is able to explore the large number of records and look for possible drug leads, look for the possible effectiveness of the drug and, at the same time, look for possible side effects. The current approaches of using artificial intelligence in clinical trials help in designing trials with patient response prediction as well as the best design of clinical trials. For instance, AI systems can recognize those biomarkers linked to drug effects, which can help make bespoke drugs and shorten the time and expenses involved in making new drugs market-ready.

# 4.4.3 Integration with omics data

By integrating AI and ML with genomes, proteomics, and metabolomics, the ability to handle complex biological systems and design of drug treatments is enhanced. These AI algorithms search for relationships between variations in genes, proteins, and metabolism in multiple omics datasets. This integrated approach enhances discovery of new biomarkers, understanding of diseases’ origin, and development of personalized treatment strategies. In order to predict patient’s response to given medication, for example, proteomic and genetic information can be input into the ML models, thus delivering more personalized and effective treatments.
