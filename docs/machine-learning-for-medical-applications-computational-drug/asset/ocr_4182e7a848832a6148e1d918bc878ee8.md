Supply chain management and drug traceability: Smart contracts can offer a positive impact by helping track the origin and ensuring the genuineness and quality of pharmaceutical products right from the forecasted supply chain network. Smart contracts also offer immutable record-keeping of the entire supply chain process, used in manufacturing, various shipments, and dispensing through contract documentation on a blockchain network. Smart contracts check the compliance of the product and its authenticity and regulatory compliance, hence reducing the risk of fake drugs, drugs diversion, and fraudulent practices.

Clinical trials and research collaboration: Smart contracts as apply for clinical trials and interdisciplinary research can automate the contract negotiations and data share and be rewarded between researchers & participants and sponsors and researchers. This paper identifies six potential benefits of smart contracts in clinical trials: reformulating trial protocols, consent documents, and data sharing agreements into smart contract formats empowers researchers to simplify trial subject enrollment, consent process, and data gathering. Smart contracts enable research task tracing and documentation thereby providing data, compliance, replications, and audit trails.

Healthcare payments and revenue cycle management: Smart contracts have the potential for enhancing HC payment and revenue cycle by eliminating paper-based processing, self-executing billing, invoicing, as well as payment reconciliation procedures between the providers, payers, and patients. We posit that smart contracts in healthcare can reduce the time spent on payment transactions and improve accuracy since payment terms, fee schedules, and reimbursement rules submit themselves to logical and mathematical analysis. Smart contracts result in clear accountable recording of payment transactions to ensure that they reflect actual transaction and improve the overall trust for the payment transactions within the health smart environment.

# 5.3 Role-based access control (RBAC) in EHR management

Another access control technique employed in utilizing access to delicate healthcare data is the widely used RBAC controlling access to EHRs based on roles of people in an organization. RBAC prevents unauthorized personnel from accessing certain data and, at the same time, provides complicated information to employees, since access rights and privileges are delegated in relation to users’ jobs. RBAC is one of the main foundations in healthcare data management and is critical for ensuring data availability, confidentiality, and data integrity of EHR data.
