DICOM (Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine): DICOM is a standard for the communications and storage of medical images and related information throughout the healthcare systems. DICOM encompasses a full solution for protocols, data formatting, and methods of communication for integrated sharing, storage, and retrieval of images in various imaging specialties and healthcare organizations/centers. DICOM facilitates exchange of medical imaging data in a way that allows them to interoperate with EHRs; it guarantees availability of diagnostic images in the overall ambience of the healthcare systems.

IHE (Integrating the Healthcare Enterprise) profiles: IHE is a strategy for working with “clusters” of higher-level standards as well as defining guidelines and interoperability profiles for implementation. This defines various HL7 and DICOM use cases and how they have been implemented and exist to support various interoperability information exchange use cases such as lab result exchange, transferring medical images, and querying patient demographic. Through specification of connectivity profiles and supporting templates IHE ensures effective data exchange and integration of EHRs.

SMART on FHIR: SMART (Substitutable Medical Applications, Reusable Technologies) on FHIR was an open framework for developing cross-platform healthcare applications operating on the FHIR framework. SMART on FHIR includes specific principles and prescriptions for designing and building loosely coupled components that integrate with EHRs through FHIR interfaces. SMART on FHIR delivers through web-based technologies and applying open standards, empowers developers to build applications that are integrated with EHR systems to advance solutions for health information exchange and interoperability.
