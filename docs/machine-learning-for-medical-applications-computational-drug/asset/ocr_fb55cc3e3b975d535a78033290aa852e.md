# 6.3 Interdisciplinary research and collaboration

Appropriate interdisciplinary research is fundamental to the enhancement of AI-driven biomedical sensors and fundamental device technology. These technologies are relatively complex and their problems need more than one discipline set of knowledge and skills to solve them. Such synergistic approaches of researchers, engineers, clinicians, and data scientists are paramount to advancing and integrating such technologies for ambulatory tracking.

The freedom of engineering and biomedical sciences appears to be the critical for progress of biomedical sensors and devices. It is the engineer's knowledge in the conception and engineering of subtle sensors, materials, and systems, and the knowledge of biomedical scientists in physiological aspects and medicine. Interdisciplinary collaborative research in this field aims at developing sensitive, accurate, and biocompatible sensors. Technologies like wearables and implantable involve both engineers and biomedical scientists in their development and therefore enhancing the development of high-quality health monitoring technology.
