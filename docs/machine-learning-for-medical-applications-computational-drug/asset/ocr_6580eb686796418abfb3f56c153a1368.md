# 4.5.4 Smart clothing and personalized health solutions

Through information and communication technology, including digital health platforms, physiological parameters and other forms of health information are continually measured through wearable health gadgets like fitness trackers, smartwatches, and others. Information such as the pulse rate, metabolism, activity, and sleeping habits, among others, are captured by these gadgets, with lots of details of an individual’s health status. To achieve suitable understanding, there is a connection of the wearables’ data to mobile health applications and EHRs, in which timely interferences and personal health management are created. Telemedicine is also made easy by digital health platforms in the same way that remote monitoring is achieved while enhancing patient engagement and tailored treatment.

# 4.5.5 CRISPR and other gene editing technologies

CRISPR and Cas9 and other molecular scissors are new-generation gene editing tools with a high possibility of altering the genetic code sequence. Researchers study the functionalities of a particular gene, remove abnormalities in the inherited genes, and design new treatment modalities. It covers gene therapy for inherited diseases, targeted cancer therapy, and generation of disease-relevant cellular models for drug screening. While these technologies evolve, they have the possibility to revolutionize the way in which genetic diseases can be treated through personalized medicine.
