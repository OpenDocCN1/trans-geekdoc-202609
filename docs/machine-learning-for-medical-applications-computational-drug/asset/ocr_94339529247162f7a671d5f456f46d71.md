Also, the breakthrough of immunotherapy itself could not have occurred without developing a technique in gene editing. Working with separate cells, for instance, T cells, scientists can enhance immune cells’ ability to discern and treat cancer cells. For specific cancer antigens, cells are engineered to express chimeric antigen receptor (CAR), as in CAR T-cell therapy. This personalized approach shows rather promising results for the treatment of some types of blood cancer and finds further application in the sphere of oncology.

It is however crucial to note that there are in reality a number of challenges and considerations with regard to CRISPR and the gene editing technologies. As these technologies move towards the preclinical and clinical applications of the technology some of the concerns such as off-target effects, ethical concerns, and long-term effects need to be analyzed in detail. For this reason, constant research and regulatory supervision are crucial for gene-editing treatment to be safe and efficient for the patient.

With the help of CRISPR and other tools of gene editing in personalized medicine the ability to produce drugs-matched individual genetic codes has become possible. These technologies have drastic capabilities to treat genetic diseases, cancer, and other diseases through targeted therapies. The role of gene editing in personalized medicine is believed to grow with both research activities and application, which would open new possibilities for introducing effective individualized treatment of patients.

# 4.5 The contribution of bioinformatics and computational tools to personalized medicine

The use of computational approaches and bioinformatics has directly improved the possibility of targeted treatments – the heart of the concept of individual medicine, enhancing the distinct possibility of applying medical breakthrough in a manner consistent with a specific patient’s needs and constituencies. These technologies close the abstraction level gap between complex genomics and practical applications of genetic data by enabling efficient analysis, comprehension, and utilization of incredibly large amounts of biological information.

Bioinformatics can be defined as the science of analyzing bio sequences through computational methods, concentrating on genetic sequences. Because the amount and complexity of biological data as well as progress in high-throughput sequencing make it challenging to obtain valuable information from these data, bioinformatics is now mandatory. As a result of creating modern program techniques in bioinformatics, the functions of the genetic markers that are connected with diseases are determined, which allows finding new diagnosis indicators and targets for treatments.

Since the multiomics data include proteomics, metabolomics, and genomics, the analysis and integration of the given data require the use of computational approaches.
