Wearable biosensors have now become important in life-extending technologies, which facilitate tracking of health conditions. Such devices, which can be attached on the body of the individual or into fabrics, are used for monitoring of various physiological activities of the human body, including heart rate, blood glucose level, and respiratory movement. New developments in fabrics, composites, and coating, have improved wearability and bio sensitivity of wearable biosensors. Such devices afford the means by which individuals can always monitor their health status and take necessary action, based on the data acquired.

There is an escalating growth in remote therapeutics and telemedicine due to emerging and existing technologies in communications and virtual care. Thus, telemedicine stations facilitate consultations and diagnostics, as well as in defining a treatment strategy; therapeutic tele-devices involve the subject in a direct, individual interaction. Advancements in Virtual Reality (VR) and Augmented Reality (AR) technologies are improving accessibility of and support for remote therapeutic interventions. The use of telemedicine, in conjunction with diagnostic and therapeutic equipment, is to introduce new solutions that are important for the development of medicine, increase patients’ attendance, and contribute to better management of chronic diseases.

# 6.2 Personalized and precision health

Precision health and person-centered care are new approaches that integrate superior technologies to deliver unique medical solutions for patients. More importantly, it focuses on reaching out to individuals with tailored health solutions, according to their genetic makeup, environmental influences, and personal lifestyle, hence providing better solutions to clients. Biomedical sensors and devices remain a critical enabler of personalized and precision health since they offer highly specific and individualized health information.

Molecular diagnostics and genomic analysis are rapidly advancing fields in healthcare, enabling the development of personalized treatment strategies tailored to individual patients. Thanks to progress in human genome research, scientists have identified numerous genetic markers associated with various diseases, including cancer, cardiovascular conditions, and rare disorders.
