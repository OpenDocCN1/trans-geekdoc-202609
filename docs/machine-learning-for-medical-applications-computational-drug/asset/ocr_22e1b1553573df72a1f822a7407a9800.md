# 3.5 Integration with mobile health (mHealth) applications

The consolidation of biomedical sensors with mHealth applications is among the biggest developments in healthcare technology. The integration of these systems has changed the way that health data is gathered, evaluated, and used, promising new prospects to advance patient care and health.

Biomedical sensors when integrated with mHealth apps enable the recording of health information in real time and its immediate relay to mobile devices. Modern people with diabetes just scan a couple of buttons on their smartphone to monitor the changes of their heart rate, blood sugar, and physical activities. Their availability ensures that people take personal responsibility every time they need the health of a certain body organ to be enhanced by practicing new lifestyle changes in treatment regimens.

In addition to that, the mHealth applications also offer to boost the performance of biomedical sensors since they are able to carry out advanced analysis and visualization. By these applications, the users can get detailed analysis of the trends, graphs, and alerts based on the sensor readings. It helps the patients including the client and the healthcare providers to grasp complex data, hence comprehend better patterns that in appropriate decision-making when it comes to health.
