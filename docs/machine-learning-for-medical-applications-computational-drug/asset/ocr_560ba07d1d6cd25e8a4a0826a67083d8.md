# 6.4.4.1 Focus on Underserved Populations Typically Managed by Limited Local Practitioners

Studies on underserved populations are increasingly relevant in personalized care. The identification of specific genetic features associated with rare diseases has driven the development of targeted therapies and advanced diagnostic methods, thanks to progress in genomic technologies. Efforts are also being made to address health disparities and ensure equitable access to appropriate care, particularly for disadvantaged and multicultural groups. Personalized therapies are further strengthened by increasing the representation of minority populations in clinical trials.

# 6.4.4.2 Emerging trends in therapy

It is remarked that the search for new therapeutic approaches is among the popular topics in the framework of personalized medicine. This includes introduction of various gene therapies, cell therapies, and various immunotherapies that are unique to genetic background and disease profile. New modalities of treatment are already under development and testing, including CRISPR/Cas in oncology, and CAR-T cell therapies. The shift toward new therapeutic endeavors shows the states’ concern with the progress of the technique and the methods of individualized treatment.
