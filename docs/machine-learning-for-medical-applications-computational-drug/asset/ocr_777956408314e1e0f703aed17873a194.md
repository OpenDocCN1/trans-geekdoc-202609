Cryptographic hashing: A data block in the blockchain process in which we get a unique hash by applying cryptographic hashing techniques. These identities firmly link block elements safely and act as electronic labels of contents of the data. The members can confirm the trustworthiness of the blockchain and identify illegal modifications to the data using hashed values.

Decentralized network: Every node within a network established for the processing of blockchain records holds an updated version of the ledger. From the decentralized confirmation of this system, there are lesser chances of giving a point to be targeted when one weak point has been eliminated. In peer-to-peer networks, protocols have nodes that help them to link with each other and ensure data replication and synchronization within a network.

User Interface and Applications: Healthcare consumers connect to EHR systems on blockchain networks through user interfaces and applications tailored to the stakeholders of a particular healthcare provider. # 3 Security and privacy in blockchain-based EHR systems

# 3.1 Importance of security and privacy in healthcare data

Sensitivity, reliability of patient information, security, and privacy turn into critical factors when managing health information. In the healthcare data management system, the statement, “secure and private” is an important aspect to enhance patient trust while adhering to government requirements and protecting patient data from exploitative use.

The two practices to prevent cyber threats and unwanted access into healthcare data are defense measures. Patient information such as personal identification details and personalized/routine treatment plans are stored by the healthcare firms attract high monetary value by the cyber criminals. The consequences of violation of healthcare data are severe: financial loss, damage to people’s image, threats of identity theft, and possible harm to life of patients. To avoid situations where patient identity is disclosed proper measures such as encryption and firewalls must be working as required.
