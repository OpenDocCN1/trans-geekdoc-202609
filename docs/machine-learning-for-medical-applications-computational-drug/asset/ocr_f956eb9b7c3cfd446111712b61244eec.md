The analyses of SAR through artificial neural networks can help the prediction of activities of compounds for new chemical entities and assist in decisions regarding lead compounds essential for the experimental confirmation. Hypothesis-building approaches developed from SAR data ascertain the probability of compounds having a series of specified biological activities due to their structural resemblance to active compounds. Using these predictive models, researchers can select VS libraries selectively, focused CLs, and novel Lead compounds, which have more significant therapeutic application.

It is important to stress that the described methodology of AI-based SAR analysis of compounds can offer important insights on the action of drugs that target specific receptors, as well as define the relationship between the chemical structure of compounds and their interactions. When used to dissect the contribution of each molecular feature of the compound substructures to compound activity, SAR models can reveal pharmacophore elements, binding motifs, and intervals within target proteins. This mechanistic model of SAR makes structure-based rational drug design methods such as structure-based optimization and ligand-protein interaction analysis manipulate compound activity and this augurs well for potency, selectivity, and the drug-like properties.

AI-driven SAR analysis can provide information for successive lead optimization cycles by designing specific compound collections and by providing recommendations for the chosen series of analogs to be synthesized and tested. Combined application of SAR models, medicinal chemistry, computational chemistry, and experimental activities make it possible for the researchers to optimize the lead compounds that elicit a desirable pharmacological effect without causing off-target and toxicity effects. Integral to this process is the use of iterative approaches to lead optimization, so as to efficiently mine chemical space and quickly surface promising leads with desirable drug-like profiling.

# 5.4 Cheminformatics approaches for lead optimization

Cheminformatics is therefore crucial in lead optimization tactics because it offers a host of computational tools and techniques for analyzing, interpreting, and optimizing chemical information for drug discovery. The use of cheminformatics allows one to predict the properties of substances, draw through the 3D chemical space, and rationally design lead agents with improved pharmacokinetic properties by means of computation.

Chemical data analysis and visualization is another area in which cheminformatics approaches apply within lead optimization.
