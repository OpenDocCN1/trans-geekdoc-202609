# 6.5 Addressing interpretability challenges in deep learning models for materials science

The integration of deep learning (DL) models has revolutionized the analysis and design of materials, bringing new paradigms to the field of materials science. While these models are highly sophisticated and powerful, a significant challenge remains: their lack of interpretability. Understanding how DL models reach their decisions is critical, particularly in high-stakes applications, to prevent errors and ensure reliable outcomes. Model interpretability enables researchers to identify the key influencing factors, verify model outputs, and refine prediction strategies. In material science, where decisions directly affect material properties, performance, and safety, interpretability is essential for field validation, continuous improvement, and wider acceptance of AI-based solutions.
