These are especially good for making the diagnosis of paroxysmal tachycardia and syncope of unknown etiology.

Patch ECG monitors: Adhesive ECG patches are intended for short-term use, thus offering real-time heart rate information discreetly and comfortably. Outpatient, event monitoring is done and addressed through these patches.

# 5.1.2 Blood pressure sensors

Blood pressure sensors track the force of blood pushing on the arteries’ walls, which is indispensable for diagnosing/high blood pressure and managing cardiovascular diseases.

Wearable blood pressure monitors: This type of blood pressure monitoring device is wearable; most are wristbands to monitor the patient’s blood pressure. These devices operate on oscillometric principles to give reliable pressures.

Ambulatory blood pressure monitors: Emerging home and outpatient devices include ambulatory arm blood pressure monitors, in which patients wear these portable monitors; these can be worn for 24h. They give a detailed picture of the shifts in blood pressure during the course of normal day, helping in establishing hypertension diagnosis and in monitoring the effectiveness of treatments.

Invasive blood pressure sensors: Some routinely invasive blood pressure monitors include catheter-based, and this is common in the setting of the hospitals. These sensors give consecutive and accurate blood pressure information that is crucial for intensive care as well as for operations.

Noninvasive continuous blood pressure monitors: Modern continuous monitors include PTT that allow reading BP without constrictive bands.

# 5.1.3 Pulse oximeters

Pulse oximeters are used to measure the level of oxygen in the blood and the pulse rate to help understand respiratory and cardiovascular conditions.

Finger and Wrist pulse oximeters: Finger and wrist pulse oximeters are handy and convenient in use, and perform constant measurements of blood oxygen and pulse rate. They are most beneficial for sufferers of respiratory diseases.

Pulse oximeter patches: Noninvasive cuffless and adhesive pulse oximeter patches are easy to use and can be worn on the skin for all types of monitoring. They are more cumbersome and time-consuming as compared to traditional pulse oximeters, but can be used for short-term monitoring of blood oxygen levels. These patches are designed to allow for long-period monitoring in different environments such as home care and in hospitals.
