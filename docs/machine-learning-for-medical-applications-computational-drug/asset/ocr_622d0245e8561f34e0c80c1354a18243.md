Modern health monitoring systems have been designed to employ artificial intelligence to enable consistent and efficient real-time interaction between patients and healthcare givers. Notification alerts from such systems can be embedded in health apps, hence enabling the patient to receive a notification on his/her smartphone device. This actual access to health information improves patient interaction and fosters a culture of choice for individuals at the required time.

The ability of AI in handling real-time data and performing analytics is also useful in lightening the burden of the healthcare providers. Notifications and data processing can be done independently from the monitoring human intervention, freeing them for the more important duties, as well as patient healthcare. It does this not only in the sense of increasing the speed at which healthcare is delivered, but also in raising the general quality of care.

# 4.4 Predictive analytics for early disease detection

In this contusive health monitoring, the major role of predictive analytics is to identify possible health issues before they turn into major medical diseases, and this is where AI comes in. The proactive approach that allows people to go to a doctor as soon as something is indicated in the data would not have been possible without sophisticated data analysis tools that translate big amounts of health data into insights.

Machine learning-based predictive analytics processes historical and current biometric data collected from biological sensors. These algorithms are quite capable of identifying correlations and patterns that suggest that illnesses began early. As an example, prognostic models can capture slight changes in vitals or movement, which precede the signs of chronic disorders such as diabetes or respiratory diseases.
