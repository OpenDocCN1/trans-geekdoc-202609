Intracranial pressure (ICP) probes are implantable devices for monitoring the pressure of the skull in patients with diseases of intracranial dynamics, including traumatic brain injury. These sensors are most commonly located in the brain system and their role is to continuously record the ICP. Given the valuable information from the ICP monitors, the treating physicians and surgeons can go ahead with intervention measures such as changing medication dosages that are intended to alter brain pressure in cases affecting the patient.
