# 5.5 External validation

The external validation proved to be a critical step in machine learning models, especially to confirm high accuracy not only on the data set against which the models were trained but also on other data sets that had not been used in constructing those models. Figure 20 shows the external validation of the model in terms of the model’s ability and generalization to an independent dataset. This procedure helps in understanding the stability and transferability of the intended models, which is useful in areas of interest such as medication toxicity prediction, where accurate and consistent performance across a variety of datasets is desirable.

The threat of overfitting, a situation such as attaining extremely high results on training data but being incapable of generalizing to new unseen data, was checked by external confirmation. External validation, on the other hand, involves testing the model on data that the model has not seen before, giving a true picture of how the model performs in terms of accuracy with new and diverse data. This was especially important in drug safety, where models used for predicting adverse effects have to be robust to the variations of populations and experimental conditions of the test.
