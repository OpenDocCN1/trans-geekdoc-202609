# 3.5.1 Metabolomics: Profiling metabolic pathways

Metabolomics is defined primarily by the analytical process of the comprehensive identification and quantification of metabolites, the small chemicals involved in metabolic processes. The specific study area offers information regarding the metabolic processes that are active in health and diseases and are located in cells as well as tissues. To describe alterations in metabolism and to find differences in metabolic profiles related to different conditions, GC-MS and NMR spectroscopy analyze samples. Metabolic data point out alterations in metabolic pathways as also such alterations prior and during disease onset, characterize a disease. The use of metabolomic profiling, for example, has been used to define the biomarkers of the metabolic diseases or to understand the effects of dietary changes.

Integrative approaches: Synergizing omics
Combining the genomic, proteomic and metabolomic information provide a systematic view of the biological system. The integration of protein expression profiles with genetics variants and changes in metabolic patterns enable the identification of the relationships. For instance, researchers, learn how variation in certain genes affects protein performance and other metabolic processes. They also use a combination of genomics, proteomics, and metabolomics to study disease states. The approach results in increased understanding of the mechanisms of illness. It is equally possible for such an integrated analysis to lead to the discovery of new biomarkers and treatment targets as well as the individualized treatment of a patient, depending on his/her biological make-up.

# 3.5.2 Systems interoperability and technology solutions: applications and future directions

Integrated omics applications can be expected to cause a big change in the future development of personalized medicine. Using integrative approaches such as genomics, proteomics, and metabolomics, one can enhance the characterization of cancer, predict responses to treatment, and identify novel targets for cancer treatment. In the field of metabolic diseases, integrated approaches can improve illness diagnostics and therapy, if they reveal the necessary amount of knowledge about metabolic dysregulation. Some of these future developments are the development of advanced computational platforms and methods for the compounding and analysis of multi-omics data, and the expansion of the use of omics in treating other diseases and developing customized treatment regimens.

The approach of putting proteomics, metabolomics, and genome into integrative technique is very strong for the development of smart and specific medicine. A lot of fields of research and medicine create a better understanding of the biology and give better and quicker treatment to patients.
