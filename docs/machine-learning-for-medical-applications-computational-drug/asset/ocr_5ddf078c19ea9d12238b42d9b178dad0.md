# 4.3 Innovations in diagnostic technologies for personalized medicine

Modern diagnostic tools have significantly changed the state of personalization in the medical industry. These technologies enable actual and specific assessment of medical diseases, making treatment and patient care more individualized. The diagnostic technologies that share development of the area of customized medicine are highlighted in the following subtopics.

# 4.3.1 NGS technologies

For both assessing predispositions to diseases and identifying individuals who are candidates for specific treatments, NGS is a leap forward because it allows for inexpensive and relatively rapid sequencing of whole genomes of genes within the genome. The implementation of NGS technologies provides for complete genome sequencing, essential for generating a comprehensive list of genetic differences related to diseases, and risk assessment of diseases that affect an individual, as well as determine treatment pathways. Such technologies can be coined as whole-genome sequencing (WGS) and targeted sequencing technologies. Relatively rare genetic alterations of specific therapies tailored to one's genetic makeup are some of the uses that have been made possible by NGS. For example, NGS has been useful in characterizing cancer genomes to develop specific therapies built on those specific genetic alterations.
