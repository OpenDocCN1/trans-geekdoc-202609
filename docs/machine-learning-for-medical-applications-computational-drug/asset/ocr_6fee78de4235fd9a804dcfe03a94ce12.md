Both edge computing and AI benefit from the integration in a way that improves data privacy and security. This way, the approach ensures that a large amount of health information is processed at a local level, thus significantly reducing the amount of information transmitted over the network – and vulnerability to unauthorized access. Furthermore, edge computing makes it possible to apply encryption and other security measures closer to the sensor level and this also provides extra protection to patient records.

Lee establishes that edge computing enables the deployment and flexibility of AI-based biomedical sensors. Integrated processing functionality of sensors enables them to manage big amounts of data and advanced calculations without referring to the central system due to the currently limited network capacity. Such scalability is important for sustaining higher demands of the persistent and multidimensional health check.
