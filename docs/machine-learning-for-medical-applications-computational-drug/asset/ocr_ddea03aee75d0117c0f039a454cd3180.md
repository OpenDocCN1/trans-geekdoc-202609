Along with the great changes genetic and genomic science bring there are several challenges that have to be overcame. These are challenges such as the inability to analyze vast amount of genomic data, absence of guidelines for implementing genomics into clinical practice, as well as ethical questions touching on genetic privacy as well as utilization of genomic data. Overcoming these barriers in order to implement the ideas of personalized medicine at their best, it is necessary to advance research activities and the creation of new genetic technologies.
