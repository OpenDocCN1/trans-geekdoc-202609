# 5.2.1 Surface chemistry

The chemistry of a surface element, in terms of how it interfaces with living tissues and body fluids, depends on the chemical nature of the elements involved. Alterations in surface chemistry are implicated in protein adsorption, cell attachment, and immune interactions. The adsorption of proteins to material surfaces with appropriate chemical functionalities is favorable for cellular attachment and proliferation. On the other hand, materials that prevent protein adsorption from adhering to the surface minimize the chance of inflammation. In terms of physical modification of materials, surface modification and coating can be used to enhance surface chemistry and improve biocompatibility.

# 5.2.2 Surface topography

Microarchitectural features of the material surface and its topography, including the grooves and porosities as well as textural structure, affect cell response and tissue interfacing. Nanoscale topography of the substrate influences cell attachment, mobility, and phenotypic expression. For instance, the engaging surface topography at the micron level plays a critical role in bone-to-implant adhesion in orthopedic applications and tissue engineering products. The design of surface topography, with regard to issues concerning mechanical support and biological responses, has been recommended to be carried out in a way that allows the successful integration of the designed solution with existing systems and structures while simultaneously eliminating complications.

# 5.2.3 Surface roughness

Surface roughness was defined in terms of variations in surface topography and was one of the dominant parameters that helped to define how a material engaged with biological tissues. Rough surfaces enhance surface area contact between cells and implants and improve implant stability. It has been discovered that high roughness can cause undesirable tissue reaction problems such as inflammation coupled with fibrosis. The peak roughness that was favorable on the surface was relative to the application and the type of tissue. For this reason, there was a need to exercise a lot of caution when designing the material and processing.
