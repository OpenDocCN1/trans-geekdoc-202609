Serum creatinine and electrolyte levels are paramount in CKD patients with dialysis. Smart and bio-accepted devices like gems and chips can monitor some aspects such as blood pressure, fluid, and electrolyte ratios which, are significantly important to renal health. Dialysis machines that support intricate monitoring systems guarantee appropriate treatment by constantly analyzing and drawing parameters from real time. These technologies are useful in taking care of chronic kidney disorders by facilitating adjustments of the patients’ treatment regimes, assessment of complications, and general patient care.

# 3.3 Preventive and predictive health

Preventive and predictive health categories of technology minimize the chances of somebody coming down with a particular health issue before the situation becomes hopeless. These technologies, which involve using imageries and data acquisition devices, sensors, data collection, and analytics, and backed up by AI computing power, provide solutions for effective health management, early disease identification, and proactive health management. The incorporation of these tools into healthcare can in fact be observed as having the ability to alter the course of ailments and diseases that would otherwise constantly plague humanity.

Diseases diagnosis has a massive impact on successful outcomes of a treatment and on the overall percentage of people’s life, immediately after being affected by various health conditions. One of the main functions of the diagnostic sensors and imaging equipment is to detect diseases in their infancy. For instance, wearable electronic devices that record physical signs include heart rates blood pressure discrepancies that indicate additional problems. Likewise, screening devices that involve biomarkers and genetic information can always point out people with high risks of acquiring diseases like cancer and cardiovascular conditions. Early detection helps our bodies, lives, and overall health situation to improve through early intervention, and hence better prognosis.
