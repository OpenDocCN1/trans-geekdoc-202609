
## 6.4 Auditing and traceability features of blockchain-based EHR systems

High auditability and accountability afforded by blockchain-based EHR systems enhance responsibility, transparency, and credibility of patient health information management. Figure 12 shows how the use of blockchain in EHR systems provides guaranteed data integrity and transparency in records through a blockchain technology approach. The auditing and traceability capabilities of blockchain-based EHR systems are highlighted by the following features:

### Clear and unchangeable audit trails
It is also important to know that thanks to blockchain, all transactions and change of data in EHRs are recorded, being immutable and easily traceable. Each and every blockchain transaction was linked with every other transaction with some type of digital signature, and the blocks formed are immutably arranged chronologically.

### Time-stamping
Blockchain attaches a date and time stamp for each transaction thus giving a chronological sequence to activities recorded throughout the blockchain. Various EHR actions performed on the EHR data must be accurately quantified and sequenced to assist with post-incident analysis and coverage for a range of issues including forensic, compliance, and reporting. Time-stamping helps healthcare organizations verify the origin and pedigree of information within EHR and monitor and audit all activities of interest, which could include, but is not limited to detecting an attempted unauthorized access of EHR data.

### Cryptographic signatures
Blockchain transactions are basically digital signatures that incorporate the identities of the involved parties and confirm the validities of the transactions. Cryptographic signatures help guarantee that anyone tampering with the transactions featured in the audit trail is going to be caught red-handed, which in turn makes the whole system trustworthy and reliable. This is further enhanced using the various steps in a block linked to the digital signatures of the participants, rendering the blockchain EHR systems adept at tracking actions of the doers involved, which discourages prevaricators.

### Access control and permissioning
The method of implementing EHR using blockchain employs the use of access control, which involves assigning special assets to decision-making instances including smart contracts and permissioned blockchain to control data access of patient's health information. Privacy and security provisions are about the conditions and people who can read, write, update, EHR information. By enforcing access control measures based on preestablished guidelines and permissions encoded into the blockchain, smart contracts make sure that only those with the proper authorization access confidential patient data. Permissioned blockchain networks lower the possibility of unwanted access by limiting participation to recognized and reliable institutions.

### Alerts and real-time monitoring
Features such as real-time alert and notification on activities such as malicious attacks and other anomalies detected in the blockchain network are provided by blockchain EHR systems. Monitoring tools and analysis algorithms residing in the automated system scan the blockchain data for gross abnormalities, variations from the norm. Real-time alerting informs administrators/specified user of any strange activities to provide timely action in relation to threats to data confidentiality and integrity.

### Compliance audits and regulatory reporting
EHRs are alleviated from it through the use of blockchain EHR systems that offer expansive records of all the transactions and modifications made to the EHR information. In this aspect, the auditors check the records in the blockchain to assess the firm's compliance with regulations like the HIPAA and the GDPR as well as its internal policies and procedures. By using blockchain as audit trails, audits are made easier and cheaper, and show organization's stance on data and information authenticity, security, and compliance.

## 6.5 Real-world examples of data integrity assurance in blockchain EHRs

The effective application of blockchain-based EHR systems to guarantee data integrity and auditability in the healthcare industry is illustrated by a number of real-world examples:

### Medicalchain
Medicalchain is a blockchain-based EHR system that aims to improve patients’ care and patients’ data protection and privacy through decentralized and patient-controlled records. By means of blockchain, the platform enables patients to store and transfer their records securely to insurers, researchers, and healthcare stakeholders. Through a technique known as encryption the records of patients’ health are secured and every operation conducted on the medical chain is recorded permanently on the blockchain database, further enhancing its credibility. Users can easily improve privacy and confidentiality since they make their own decisions regarding their data, sharing it with healthcare providers sparingly. In the Medicalchain, the EHR data is arranged and secured through the blockchain system, which provides clear tracks of audit that can make it respond to the responsibility and confidence of the patient interaction records.

### Solve.Care
Solve.Care is the healthcare ecosystem deployed on blockchain to facilitate payments, administrative and care programs through apps and smart contracts. This way, the platform captures patient-related activities, treatment, and diagnostic procedures, as well as administrative transactions related to funding and reimbursement for EHR management. Take Care of Itself Wallet application provides an interface to securely view and manage a patient's health records; providers can see the records and contribute toward the care plan in a “traceable” fashion. Solve.Care’s blockchain solution for EHR makes sharing, reconciliation, and verification of data more reliable and effective, supporting patient-centered outcome delivery and healthcare administrative practice.

### Hu-manity.co
Hu-manity.co was built using blockchain technology that allows customers to own, control, and sell their health data. It employs the concept of blockchain whereby records made for patients’ consent and agreements made for sharing of data are sacrosanct and can be audited for accuracy and authentication of patients, data. #My31 app on Hu-manity.co plans on enabling users to reclaim their data control of health information and blockchain contracts ensure that third-party data users shall only access the data that users have granted permission for, through data usage compensation agreements on the online platform. Hu-manity.co’s EHR solution is developed on blockchain technology and allows patients to own personal health data and be engaged in data-driven projects while data remains trustworthy and safe.

### Coral Health
Coral Health is a healthcare platform that uses blockchain in a way that enables patients, researchers, and healthcare providers securely and accurately share patient data. The platform achieves protection of data and non-repudiation by applying blockchain technology to construct a secure, tamper-proof registry of patient’s EHRs. By holding the rights to the records’ privacy settings and access control, patients consolidate and distribute their data to various parties with Coral Health’s EHR solution built on the blockchain. For this reason, Coral Health integrates blockchain to create transparency, security, and trusted data management in healthcare for collaboration in care delivery and research.

The practical use of blockchain-based EHRs for the purpose of guaranteeing data credibility and historical traceability in the sphere of healthcare is outlined. Thus, utilizing blockchain technology, these platforms offer their users clear, unalterable, and patient-centered solutions concerning the proper handling of EHR, facilitating the issues of interoperability, patient privacy, and trust.
