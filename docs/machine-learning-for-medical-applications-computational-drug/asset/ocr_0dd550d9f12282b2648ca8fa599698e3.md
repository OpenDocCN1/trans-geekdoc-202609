# 2.4.3 Power supply and energy management

Management of power supply and energy is critical to biomedical sensors particularly in applications such as wearables and implantable devices. Key considerations include:

+   - Battery technologies: Structural merits including compactness and durability are achieved by incorporating new forms of battery technologies including lithium-ion and are flexible.
+   - Energy harvesting: Ambient power using such devices like thermoelectric generators for powering the sensors for longer periods, thus minimizing the requirement for battery replacement.
