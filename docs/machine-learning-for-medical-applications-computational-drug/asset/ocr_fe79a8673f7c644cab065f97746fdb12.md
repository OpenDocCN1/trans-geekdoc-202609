# 3.1 Vital signs monitoring

Supplementary to what has been discussed, the recording of the vital signs is a core part of the working process since it involves essential information of a patient's body functions. This practice makes it possible to spot trends that in turn point toward a medical condition that needs treatment, as well as management of chronic illnesses. Significant development exists in sensor technology, thereby strengthening the efficiency of the vital signs monitoring contributing to the positive outcomes of the patients as well as enhancement of the healthcare systems.

Heart rate and ECG are two important parameters in diagnosing cardiovascular disorders. Pulse oximeters convey the number of pulse repetitions in one minute, giving details of the pattern and efficiency of the heart. Smartwatches and chest straps provide prolonged input and analysis of the wearer's heart rate. ECG, on the other hand, records electrical activity of the heart such as heart rate irregularities, lack of blood supply to the heart muscles, and other heart-related ailments. The complex ECG devices help in diagnosing and monitoring the various heart conditions as well as offer detailed evaluations, apart from maintaining the long-term records of the patient.

Tracking of the pressure within the blood vessels is relevant in evaluating the overall heart health and treatment of ailments like hypertension. Dialysis monitors track blood pressure automatically and give accurate systolic and diastolic values to enable frequent checks and effective treatment. There is a need to monitor SpO₂ using pulse oximeters as a means of assessing respiratory compromise and, specifically, hypoxemia. A pulse oximeter operates through the use of an infrared light, which is used to estimate the percent of oxygenated Hb and is very useful for monitoring respiratory diseases as well as oxygen therapy.

Pulse rate measurement is a significant means of assessing respiratory health as well as general health status. By observing the respiratory rate over time, the client is easily able to identify any abnormalities such as tachypnea, which is indicative of a number of underlying health problems.
