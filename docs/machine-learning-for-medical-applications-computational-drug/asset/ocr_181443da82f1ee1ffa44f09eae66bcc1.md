# 6.4.4 Post-market surveillance and reporting obligations

Unfortunately, manufacturers of biocompatible materials cannot rest even after obtaining the required regulatory approval from the authorities; they have to submit to post-market surveillance and reporting. These requirements guarantee that any unfavorable circumstances or issues are immediately recognized and dealt with.
