Thirdly, significant importance must be given to data quality and its management. Fifth, transitioning toward obtaining regulatory permission and public acceptability in the execution of biomedical research using the ML approach demand answering ethical and regulatory questions. It means that there is a necessity of collaboration between regulatory bodies and other parties in order to determine rules regarding creation, verification, and utilization of machine learning models in the context of the healthcare industry. Privacy and confidentiality of the patient data means that one has to follow the data protection laws in the country of operation, which include the GDPR and HIPAA. It, therefore, should be noted that ML should be properly implemented with ethical models of algorithmic fairness, patient's self-governance, and eradicating conducive bias.
