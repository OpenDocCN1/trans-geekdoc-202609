Standards-based blockchain networks: Such networks leverage standard and recognized norms and procedures for conglomerate connectivity, for instance, HL7 FHIR, for the conversions and the reconciliations of EHR information among different blockchain networks and healthcare systems. Standardized blockchain networks ensure compatibility, interconnectivity, and interchangeability of data across the healthcare systems since they conform to set data format, messaging, and integration standards embraced by the various networks that make up a standards-based blockchain. This promotes interoperability and data sharing across varied EHR systems and stakeholders.
