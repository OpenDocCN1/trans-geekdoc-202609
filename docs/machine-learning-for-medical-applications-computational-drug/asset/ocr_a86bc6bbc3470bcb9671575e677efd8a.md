# 6.3 Remote patient monitoring systems

RPM devices involve integrated biomedical sensors and communication technology and are recognized as an indispensable aspect of modern medical practice to provide constant interphase vigilance without direct clinical control. Through these technologies, remote data collection, transmission, and analysis are accomplished, and it has some benefits in patients as well as healthcare workers.
