One of the key shifts in customized medicine was the development of targeted medicines. Imatinib for treating CML and trastuzumab for treating HER2+ BC were among examples of genetic breakthroughs that were used in creating drugs, which acted specifically once certain biological markers were identified. These treatments demonstrated more about steps that are used to plan and execute interventions that improve patient outcomes more.

# Big Data integration and AI (2010–present)

There has been a shift in the customized medicine subsector through the integration of AI and big data analysis. Genetic, clinical, and environmental data are gathered for a large number of patients and then preprocessed to look for tendencies and predict the responses by means of large and complex formulas and machine learning. Therapeutic and diagnostic treatments get enhanced by the use of AI solutions and help in designing individual therapy plans.

# Personalized medicine initiative development and partnerships (2010s onwards)

Several efforts and partnerships have been created to advocate the adoption and innovation of personal medicine. The most recent example was the Precision Medicines Initiative in the United States and similar initiatives in other countries. These programs aim at facilitating collaboration between academics and/or physicians on the one hand and patients on the other, and fostering research and exchange of data in the domain of personalized medicine with the vision of enhancing the latter's implementation in clinical work.

# Individualized therapy in mainstream ambulatory care setting: current and future

The latest achievement is the further entrenchment of personalized medicine into a myriad of typical clinical operations. Genome sequencing and Big Data utilization in medicine and precision medicine are making their way into routine practice more and more often. These trends have shifted towards more personalized care designated as an effort to enhance patients' outcomes and interventions and prevention processes.
