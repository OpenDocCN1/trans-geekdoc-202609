Access control and permissioning: The method of implementing EHR using blockchain employs the use of access control, which involves assigning special assets to decision-making instances including smart contracts and permissioned blockchain to control data access of patient's health information. Privacy and security provisions are about the conditions and people who can read, write, update, EHR information. By enforcing access control measures based on preestablished guidelines and permissions encoded into the blockchain, smart contracts make sure that only those with the proper authorization access confidential patient data. Permissioned blockchain networks lower the possibility of unwanted access by limiting participation to recognized and reliable institutions.

Alerts and real-time monitoring: Features such as real-time alert and notification on activities such as malicious attacks and other anomalies detected in the blockchain network are provided by blockchain EHR systems. Monitoring tools and analysis algorithms residing in the automated system scan the blockchain data for gory abnormalities, variations from the norm. Real-time alerting informs administrators/specified user of any strange activities to provide timely action in relation to threats to data confidentiality and integrity.

Compliance audits and regulatory reporting: EHRs are alleviated from it through the use of blockchain EHR systems that offer expansive records of all the transactions and modifications made to the EHR information. In this aspect, the auditors check the records in the blockchain to assess the firm's compliance with regulations like the HIPAA and the GDPR as well as its internal policies and procedures. By using blockchain as audit trails, audits are made easier and cheaper, and show organization's stance on data and information authenticity, security, and compliance.
