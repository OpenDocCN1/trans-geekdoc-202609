Biomedical sensors, when combined with high-end data analytics and artificial intelligence, play an even bigger part in preventive healthcare. In developing AI, algorithms are able to read the data acquired by the sensors, and subsequently diagnose potential health complications that are likely to occur before the symptoms show up. This predictive capacity proves particularly useful in chronic diseases management and in helping to avoid deterioration, hospitalization, and the resultant negative effects on patients’ well-being.

Biomedical sensors support preventive healthcare by enabling remote patient monitoring and treatment through virtual sessions. Portable devices that capture physiological signals and transmit them to caregivers facilitate continuous, virtual supervision particularly valuable for patients in remote areas with limited access to healthcare facilities. As shown in Figure 2, this capability enhances both the accessibility of preventive healthcare services and patients’ ability to receive timely responses and recommendations from healthcare providers.
