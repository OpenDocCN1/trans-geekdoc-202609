# 6.1.3 Emergence of immunotherapy

One of the most important advancements of precision oncology is immunotherapy, which uses the body’s immune system to eliminate cancer cells selectively. Such a therapy for various sorts of tumors has exhibited some encouraging results because of methodologies such as immunity checkpoint inhibitors, chimeric antigen receptor (CAR) T-cell therapy, and cancer vaccines.
