The two future paths that are considered important include: The coupling of ML algorithms with real-time monitoring solutions – Daily health data such as vital signs, medication compliance, and even physical activity, for example, the kind of real-time health data that wearables and biosensors currently monitor. Machine learning algorithms can analyze different data flows in real-time, which allows offering individuals dynamic and adaptive choices of medications. For instance, continuous monitoring of patients with chronic diseases can be done through ML algorithms, where information from glucose monitoring devices is processed and changes in insulin dosages are made in real-time to keep the patient’s blood sugar levels optimal. Full accuracy and responsiveness with such high measures are expected to significantly improve the management of some chronic diseases.

More significant opportunities emerge if machine learning is applied directly to the drug development pipeline. Using trial and error or actual data on biological systems takes nearly substantial time but by using machine learning algorithms to mimic and forecast their behavior, one can fasten the process in arriving at the design and testing of new drug delivery systems. The time and cost within the laboratory experiment can also be saved when using virtual screenings on medication candidates and their delivery systems. Also, better medicinal drug formulations and delivery systems can also be obtained by using optimization of formulations with the help of ML, hence leading to increased stability, bioavailability, and efficacy. This issue increases the tempo of new drug delivery solutions development with regard to specific therapeutic needs.

# 6.5 Recommendations for overcoming barriers and advancing ML in biomedical research

In the field of ML, there is a huge prospect to improving medicine administration and in research in the biomedical field. There are several barriers that must be addressed to harness the mentioned potential. Some of the tactical recommendations are as follows, in an effort to go past these barriers and enhance the use of machine learning in biomedical research.

First of all, it becomes crucial to bring together representatives of different faculties. Multidisciplinary teams such as computer science, biomedical engineering, pharmacological, and clinical medicine need to be included in order to advance the field with respect to the use of ML. Effort should be made to bring in experts in the biomedical domain as well as in the application of ML in solving such challenges. The lessons learned from the creation of interdisciplinary research centers and funding initiatives aiming at use of ML in healthcare can be used to ensure similar approaches in the future.
