Ease of storage and retrieval: Digital photographs should be electronically saved, reducing the odds of a picture getting lost, and no physical storage is necessary. PACS, an electronic storage system, is suited for managing archives and recalling images quickly. It helps in retaining medical records for a long time and allows easy retrieval of images to be used in providing comparisons.

Integration with other technologies: The digital modality of medical images provides interoperability with other IT systems. Digital imaging data can be linked with EHRs, where patients can be managed digitally from the start. It is also possible to share digital images in networks and apply telemedicine and consulting.

Development of advanced imaging modalities: The shift to digital equipment provided a basis for improving current imaging techniques. Nowadays, such methods have become available as digital subtraction angiography, digital mammography, and CT, which provide the possibility of improving diagnostic abilities and increasing the accuracy of treatment.

# 2.5 Impact of computational advances on image processing

Computational upgrades have dramatically influenced medical image processing information, ensuring extensive enhancement across the category of imaging systems. In its beginning, medical image processing was hampered by first-generation computational devices that set upper bounds on the sophistication of image analysis algorithms and the amount of image data that was manageable. With the advancement in computational power and the development of better algorithms, the potential to provide better interpretational analysis of medical images steadily escalated.

Advances in higher-performing and energy-efficient processors have made it possible to execute sophisticated image processing algorithms, which were heretofore unimpressive. Recent improvements in computational power have enabled the use of sophisticated computational techniques including image enlargement, image clearing from noise, and feature detection. All of these enhancements have a clear connection to an increase in image definition and the overall diagnostic capabilities, which result in lower probabilities of mistakes when interpreting medical images.

Through parallel processing and high-speed computers, the processing of large numbers of image data has become faster. Huge data volumes are produced in fields such as CT and MRI, which have represented great value here. These strong computational performances allow for the analysis and interpretation of medical pictures at a speed and with an accuracy never achieved before, due to new methods of computational intelligence such as machine learning and deep learning.
