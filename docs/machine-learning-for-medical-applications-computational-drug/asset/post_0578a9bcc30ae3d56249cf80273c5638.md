## 4.3 Innovations in diagnostic technologies for personalized medicine

Modern diagnostic tools have significantly changed the state of personalization in the medical industry. These technologies enable accurate and specific assessment of medical diseases, making treatment and patient care more individualized. The diagnostic technologies that shape development of the area of customized medicine are highlighted in the following subtopics.

### 4.3.1 NGS technologies

For both assessing predispositions to diseases and identifying individuals who are candidates for specific treatments, NGS is a leap forward because it allows for inexpensive and relatively rapid sequencing of whole genomes and genes within the genome. The implementation of NGS technologies provides for complete genome sequencing, essential for generating a comprehensive list of genetic differences related to diseases, and risk assessment of diseases that affect an individual, as well as determining treatment pathways. Such technologies can be coined as whole-genome sequencing (WGS) and targeted sequencing technologies. Relatively rare genetic alterations and specific therapies tailored to one's genetic makeup are some of the uses that have been made possible by NGS. For example, NGS has been useful in characterizing cancer genomes to develop specific therapies built on those specific genetic alterations.

These advancements in diagnostic modalities are key to moving personalized medicine forward, as they offer the ability to better, faster, and more individually assess the status of people's health. New prospects for the integration of such technologies are expected to be continuously elaborated to provide better substantiation for personalized therapies and optimize the overall outcomes of patients with various diseases.

## 4.4 The role of artificial intelligence and machine learning

AI and ML are now considered pioneers in the process of revolution of the modern approach to the treatment of diseases – individualized therapy. These technologies have helped in easier assessment of several and complex databases, and predictions and prognosis about the patients, and individual patient management. These technologies involve the use of high-end computers and efficient algorithms to identify features that a normal running procedure overlooks.

In this paper, the two concepts are predictive modeling and risk assessment.

Risk prediction is another critical domain within personalized medicine that leverages artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML), extending beyond the capabilities of traditional medical decision-making. As depicted in Figure 7, this approach utilizes diverse subtypes of patient data including electronic health records, genomic data, disease-specific characteristics, and lifestyle factors. Supervised learning algorithms are commonly employed to analyze this information and predict health risks. Based on individual characteristics, these tools estimate the likelihood of developing certain conditions, thereby enabling early intervention. For example, ML models can predict the onset of diabetes by analyzing large-scale patient datasets, allowing healthcare providers to initiate timely treatments and implement preventive strategies.

### 4.4.1 Enhancing diagnostic accuracy

Diagnosis is becoming less of a problem, given that AI is introducing tools that increase both accuracy and efficiency of diagnostics. Among the emerging technologies, ML, with its subfield, deep learning algorithms, produces accurate results in analyzing medical images, including MRI, CT scans, and X-rays. Most of these algorithms can diagnose anomalies with great accuracy, and at times, surpass the skills of the best radiologists in the world. For instance, AI solutions have been designed to detect the first signs by analyzing imaging data needed for a diagnosis. AI enables improvement of diagnostic precision and making precise assessments of patients.

#### 4.4.1.1 Treatments based on clients' individual characteristics

Fortunately, to solve such tasks, machine learning models consider patient-unique characteristics, which is an essential element of the individual therapeutic regimens. Thus, the indispensable algorithms would allow individual therapy recommendations based on the data of genetic researches, clinical trials, as well as outcomes of other patients treated until now. In cancer, for instance, machine learning algorithms look at traits of genes present in the tumor to suggest proper medications that are likely to be effective based on the patient's particularity. This method minimizes side effects and optimizes the efficacy of therapy since treatment alternatives are individualized, based on patient's biological characteristics.

### 4.4.2 Drug development optimization

Through using AI and ML, different stages of the drug discovery and development process are being enhanced. With the help of such algorithms as machine learning algorithms, one is able to explore the large number of records and look for possible drug leads, look for the possible effectiveness of the drug and, at the same time, look for possible side effects. The current approaches of using artificial intelligence in clinical trials help in designing trials with patient response prediction as well as the best design of clinical trials. For instance, AI systems can recognize those biomarkers linked to drug effects, which can help make bespoke drugs and shorten the time and expenses involved in making new drugs market-ready.

### 4.4.3 Integration with omics data

By integrating AI and ML with genomics, proteomics, and metabolomics, the ability to handle complex biological systems and design of drug treatments is enhanced. These AI algorithms search for relationships between variations in genes, proteins, and metabolism in multiple omics datasets. This integrated approach enhances discovery of new biomarkers, understanding of diseases' origin, and development of personalized treatment strategies. In order to predict patient's response to given medication, for example, proteomic and genetic information can be input into the ML models, thus delivering more personalized and effective treatments.
