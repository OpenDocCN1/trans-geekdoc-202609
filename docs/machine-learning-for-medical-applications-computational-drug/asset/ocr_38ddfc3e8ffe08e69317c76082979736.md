# 4 Technological advancements driving personalized medicine

# 4.1 Advances in genomic sequencing technologies

Substantial advancement has taken place in genomic sequencing and that alone has revolutionized the whole concept of personalized medicine. Some of these have enabled the study of the human genome in more depth. There is an appreciation of increased protective factors that the analysis of genetic biomarkers has been able to provide, aiding in the understanding of the genes associated with illness and the formulation of personalized treatment.

# 4.1.1 Next-generation sequencing

High throughput and cost-effective sequencing offered by NGS is another major step forward in genomic analysis. High-throughput sequencing by NGS technologies allows the analysis of millions of DNA fragments simultaneously; therefore, numerous accurate data are obtained. Some of the examples of the said technologies include Illumina sequencing and Ion Torrent sequencing. Because of this technology, sequencing is now much faster and less costly than before; which means that there are more opportunities for genetic analysis in clinical and research facility. The prevailing relationship between genetic profiles and different diseases depends on the identification of the genetic variations in the form of SNP and the structural variations that have been achieved by NGS.

# 4.1.2 Whole-genome sequencing (WGS)

WGS is used to comprehensively map a complete copy of a person's DNA, including discrete segments contain coding and noncoding regions. Advancements in WGS technology have enhanced both the precision and accuracy of genome mapping and enable the identification of rare and novel genetic variants. Due to WGS providing complete profile of an individual, it is useful in diagnostics of genetic factors to complicated aches and sicknesses. Individual patient approaches, treatments, and genetic disorder identification are all increasingly being powered by this technology, which is already taking place in increasing numbers of clinical contexts.
