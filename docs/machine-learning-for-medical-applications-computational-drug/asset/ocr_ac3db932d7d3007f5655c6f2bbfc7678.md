# 5.2 Application of smart contracts in healthcare

Smart contracts are a true revolution in the progressively developing field of healthcare owing to the numerous opportunities created for the efficient management of data, its protection, and access. Smart contracts that leverage on blockchain technology safely are transparent and automatically execute protocols, agreements, and transactions. This ultimately fiddles the effectiveness, responsibility, and reliability of the healthcare structures. Among the most important uses of smart contracts in the medical field are:

Smart contracts are used to handle patient permission for the sharing and use of EHR data, as well as to enforce access control measures. Healthcare businesses guarantee that sensitive patient information is only accessed by authorized persons by encoding consent preferences and access rights into smart contracts. Smart Contracts enhance security, privacy, and data protection laws because they automate consent management procedures and provide patients with centralized authority over who receives their health information and when.

Using an example represented in Figure 11, one can see how smart contracts work in building a healthcare system and eradicating the need in many intermediaries that concentrate on such procedures as patient consent, billing, information exchange.

Claims Processing and Insurance Verification: There is potential to effectively automate processes such as claims and insurance verifications in the field of healthcare thanks to smart contracts that can facilitate insurance claims decision making and settlement. Through the use of smart contracts, the insurer and healthcare providers are thus provided with the means of automating the process of checking the validity, admissibility, and number of claims to be paid against the entire policies, coverage, and reimbursement rules are issued. Smart contracts eliminate some of the challenges of using traditional claims processing approaches by enabling clear and accurate transaction tracking.
