Another main operational concern of biomedical devices is the need to safeguard the security and privacy of health data because some of the devices collect and relay such data wirelessly, while others relay them in protected formats. Having measures in the form of excellent encryption methods, proper means of transmitting information, and safe controls are essential in protecting patient's information from erosion. Regulation like HIPAA and GDPR have to be followed strictly so as to ensure that data privacy and security are adhered to. In Figure 20, system performance and operation are tabulated technically and tactfully, where improvements and optimizations are required. It thus becomes important that initiatives to improve and adopt measures toward the protection of users' data should continue toward avoiding raising further concerns.

User training as well as user compliance are very important in ensuring that biomedical devices run as expected. Users have to be trained well in its use, interpretation of data, as well as handling of alerts actions. Lack of training means that people do not use the gear in the right manner, making their resources less effective and posing health dangers. Using friendly interfaces, incorporating full instructional documentation, and offering supporting services enhance the specifics of using the devices as well as in making necessary adjustments to their algorithms. Further, recognizing user worries and adapting the device for the users' feedback could improve usability and maintain device usage.
