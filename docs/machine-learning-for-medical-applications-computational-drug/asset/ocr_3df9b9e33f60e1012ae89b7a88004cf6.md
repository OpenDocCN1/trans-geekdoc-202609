# 6.4.7 Informed consent and patient autonomy

Given the fact that the materials used in medical devices and treatments should be biocompatible, the issue of informed consent is rather broad. Patients should be fully informed of the various implanted treatments, the risks associated with those materials, the expected benefits, and other possible options. Furthermore, patient self-governance involves creating an environment that allows the individual to receive adequate information on how to manage themselves. This is particularly important in situations where biomaterials that could be used are still in the experimental category and their impacts have not been fully understood.

# 6.4.8 Equity and access to advanced materials

As biocompatible materials develop, there is an ethical necessity to make these developments available to every patient regardless of economic status. Some commodities and technologies could be unavailable to all due to limited availability and quality and therefore could deepen the existing discrimination in the healthcare domain. Ethical considerations should also address the possibility of making biocompatible substances affordable so that the benefits derived from the application of technology in the delivery of health care that the technology does not favor the elite but is equally available to all.

# 6.4.9 Environmental impact and sustainability

Another issue that arises from its use is concerns involving the ethical question of the sustainability of the elastic biocompatible material used in the production and disposal of products. The manufacturing of most of these materials includes the use of synthetic polymers, metals, and other substances known to cause an environmental impact. Additionally, it has been noted that some of the degraded products of several materials are ecological hazards. Some of the ethical issues that should be considered in the creation of biocompatible materials are sustainability, as it is important to avoid any harm to the environment, and the promotion of ecofriendly products.
