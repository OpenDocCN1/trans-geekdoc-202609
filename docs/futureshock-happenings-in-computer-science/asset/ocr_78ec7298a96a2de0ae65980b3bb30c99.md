+   - Suppose that $p \neq 0$ and there exists $m < k$ such that $m > i_p$ and $(i_1,\dots,i_{p-1},m) \in U$. Then choose the smallest such integer $m$ and add $((\chi_1,\xi_{i_1}),\dots,(\chi_{p-1},\xi_{i_{p-1}}),(\vee,\xi_m))$ to $T$.

Note that there exists a one-to-one mapping $F$ from $S$ into $T$ (as well as a one-to-one mapping from $U$ into $T$). Clearly, $\{\text{Mod}_{\mathscr{M}}(X) \mid X \in S\}$ is a partition of $\mathscr{M}$. Also, it is easily verified that for all $\sigma \in T$, $\sigma\mid_T = \wedge F^{-1}(\sigma)$. Let $\varphi$ be a formula. Let $\ell$ be the classifier for $T$ such that for all $\sigma \in T$, $\ell(\sigma)=1$ iff there exists $\mathfrak{M} \in \mathscr{M}$ with $\mathfrak{M} \models \varphi$ and $\mathfrak{M} \in F^{-1}(\sigma)$. We immediately conclude from Proposition 40 together with the previous observations that $(T,\ell)$ recognises $\varphi$, thereby completing the proof of the proposition.
