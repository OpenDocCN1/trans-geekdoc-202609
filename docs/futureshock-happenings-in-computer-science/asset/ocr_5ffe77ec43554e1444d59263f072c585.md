+   - (i) {p0, p1, p2}, {p0, p1, p3} and {p0, p2, p3} are the ⊆-minimal supersets D of {p0}, (ii) {p1, p2} and {p1, p3} are the ⊆-minimal supersets D of {p1}, and (iii) {p1, p2} and {p0, p2, p3} are the ⊆-minimal supersets D of {p2} such that making the members of D and the members of D only True switches the truth of φ from False to True.
+   - {p0, p1, p2}, {p0, p1, p3}, {p0, p2, p3}, {p1, p2} and {p1, p3} all have {p0, p1, p2, p3, p4} as unique ⊆-minimal superset D such that making all members of D and the members of D only True switches the truth of φ from True to False.
