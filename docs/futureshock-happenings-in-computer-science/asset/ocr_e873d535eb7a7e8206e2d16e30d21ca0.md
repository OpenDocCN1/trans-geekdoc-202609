# Corollary 44.

Let ℰ be an ℳ-embedding of the form (h,B) with dom(h) = (B,∨,∧,0,1). Let a member v of B distinct to 0 and 1 be given. The three conditions that follow are equivalent.

+   - There exists an ℰ-gradation tree T and σ ∈ T such that v is equal to σ̂.
+   - There exists n ∈ ℕ, finite subsets D0, …, Dn of B with D0 ⊂ ⋯ ⊂ Dn, and nonempty finite subsets E0, …, En of B such that v is equal to ⋁_{i≤n} ∧_{o∈Ei} (Di ∪ {v}).
+   - There exists n ∈ ℕ, finite subsets D0, …, Dn of B with D0 ⊂ ⋯ ⊂ Dn, and nonempty finite subsets E0, …, En of B such that v is equal to ∧_{i≤n} ∨_{o∈Ei} (Di ∪ {v}).
