+   - Write σ₂ as ((𝕏'₀,𝕧'₀),..., (𝕏'ₚ,𝕧'ₚ)), p > n. If there exists m < p with 𝕏'ₘ₊₁ = ∨ and a sequence σ' such that σ₁ is ((𝕏'₀,𝕧'₀),..., (𝕏'ₘ,𝕧'ₘ))·σ' with σ' being either empty or of the form ((∧,𝕧'))·σ'', then either m > n, in which case there exists a sequence τ such that σ₁ is σ₃·((∧,∨))·τ, hence σ₁ ≺ σ₃, or m < n, in which case σ₁ is ((𝕏₀,𝕧₀),..., (𝕏ₘ,𝕧ₘ))·σ', hence σ₁ ≺ σ₃ again.
+   - Suppose that there exists m < n with 𝕏ₘ₊₁ = ∨ and there exists a sequence σ being either empty or of the form ((∧,∨))·σ' such that σ₂ is ((𝕏₀,𝕧₀),..., (𝕏ₘ,𝕧ₘ))·σ.
+   - If σ₁ is of the form σ₂·((∧,∨))·τ then there exists a sequence τ' of the form ((∧,𝕧'))·τ'' such that σ₁ is ((𝕏₀,𝕧₀),..., (𝕏ₘ,𝕧ₘ))·τ', hence σ₁ ≺ σ₃.
+   - Write σ₂ as ((𝕏'₀,𝕧'₀),..., (𝕏'ₚ,𝕧'ₚ)), p ≥ m. If there exists k < p with 𝕏'ₖ₊₁ = ∨ and a sequence τ such that σ₁ is ((𝕏'₀,𝕧'₀),..., (𝕏'ₖ,𝕧'ₖ))·τ with τ being either empty or of the form ((∧,𝕧'))·τ', then either k ≥ m, in which case there exists a sequence γ being either empty or of the form ((∧,∨))·γ' such that σ₁ is ((𝕏₀,𝕧₀),..., (𝕏ₘ,𝕧ₘ))·γ, hence σ₁ ≺ σ₃, or k < m, in which case σ₁ is ((𝕏₀,𝕧₀),..., (𝕏ₖ,𝕧ₖ))·τ, hence σ₁ ≺ σ₃ again.

We conclude that in all cases, σ₁ ≺ σ₃, hence ≺ is transitive.

Let distinct σ, τ ∈ T be given.

+   - If σ is of the form τ·((∧,∨))·τ' then σ ≺ τ.
+   - If τ is of the form σ·((∨,∨))·σ' then σ ≺ τ.
+   - If there exists a sequence γ such that σ and τ are of the form γ·((∧,∨))·σ' and γ·((∨,∨))·τ', respectively, then σ ≺ τ.

We conclude that for all distinct σ, τ ∈ T, either σ ≺ τ or τ ≺ σ, thereby completing the proof of the lemma.
