early 21st century, manufacturing trends shifted globally, with outsourcing becoming more feasible, profitable, and popular. Nowadays, few countries have the sovereign capabilities to perform mass in-house manufacturing of electronic devices and components, and though the design of electronics continues in-house, most electronic devices and components in the modern era are now currently produced in vast factories in Asia, particularly in Taiwan and China.

This separation of design and manufacturing has led to extremely long and complex supply chains for electronic devices. Devices can have components from dozens-to-hundreds of companies based out of an array of different countries. The supply chains include dependencies on both integrated circuits and the printed circuit boards they will be soldered to. Each IC and PCB also has to be specified, designed, and then tested.
