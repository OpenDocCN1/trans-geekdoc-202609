Figure 2.46 shows three hierarchical clusters constructed on each individual transposon statistic for the first replicate of the Francisella Tularensis species. In other words, the genes have been pushed towards two ends with a long distance. In addition, one major cluster which has the longest distance with other major cluster has a smaller number of genes. Therefore, one cluster perhaps composed of the essential genes and the other cluster is likely composed of the non-essential genes for this data set.
