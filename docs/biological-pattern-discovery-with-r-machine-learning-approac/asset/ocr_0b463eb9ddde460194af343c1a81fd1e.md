In Figure 6.38, the gene contains two replicates with extremely low expressions. They are treated as the outliers with the same reason. After removing these outliers, the rest expressions show a well-structured unimodal distribution as shown as the inset histogram in the plot. Therefore, this gene may not be treated as a bimodal gene.
