Figure 7.11 shows the PCA map of the 3-mer word frequency of these 17 sequences, where ten SARS-CoV-2 sequences were represented by S, one SARS-CoV was represented by X and six HIV sequences were represented by H. It can be seen that ten SARS-CoV-2 were clustered and six HIV were clustered into two smaller clusters leaving the SARS-CoV as an outlier. However, in terms of the distance in this map, the SARS-CoV had a shorter distance with the SARS-CoV-2 cluster compared with the HIV clusters.
