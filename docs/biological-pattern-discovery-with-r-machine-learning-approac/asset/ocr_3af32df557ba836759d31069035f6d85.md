Figure 4.8 shows why the total regression error may not be a good fitness measurement. Two data sets had the same total regression error between the observations and the regressed means, which were 25.4217. However, two data sets had different slopes. Therefore, the variances of dependent variable were different. They were 29.595 in Figure 4.8(a) and 112.4522 in Figure 4.8(b). The $F$-statistic was 51.4 in Figure 4.8(a) and 1071.5 in Figure 4.8(b). Although two regression models had the same total regression error, their dependent variables had different variances. Therefore, a model with a smaller variance of the dependent variable had lower fitness measurement compared with a regression model with a greater variance of the dependent variable although two regression models had the same regression error.
