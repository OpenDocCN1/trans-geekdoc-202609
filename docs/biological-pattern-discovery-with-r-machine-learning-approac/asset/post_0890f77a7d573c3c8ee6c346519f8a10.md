
## Spectral Pattern Discovery

```
$f_{B=b|e,\vartheta}(\mathbf{b}) \propto \frac{1}{\sigma_{\omega F}^2}\frac{1}{\sigma_{\omega S}^2}\exp\left(-\frac{\mathbf{e}^T\Gamma e}{2\sigma_F^2}-\frac{\mathbf{d}^T\mathbf{d}}{2\sigma_S^2}-\frac{\beta_F}{\sigma_F^2}-\frac{\beta_S}{\sigma_S^2}\right) (5.26)
```

Maximising the posterior with respect to $\sigma_F^2$ leads to the calculation of $\sigma_F^2$ shown below,

```
$$\sigma_F^2 = \frac{\mathbf{e}^T\Gamma e + 2\beta_F}{2\alpha_F + 2 + K} (5.27)
```

Maximising the posterior with respect to $\sigma_S^2$ leads to the calculation of $\sigma_S^2$ shown below,

```
$$\sigma_S^2 = \frac{\mathbf{d}^T\mathbf{d} + 2\beta_S}{2\alpha_S + 2 + N - 1} (5.28)
```

When the posterior is maximised with respect to the baseline $\mathbf{b}$, the following equation is derived, which is similar to WH,

```
$$\mathbf{b} = \sigma_F^{-2}\mathbf{Z}^{-1}\Gamma\sigma \text{ (5.29)} (5.2)
```

**Z** is calling the mapping construction matrix and is defined as below,

```
$$\mathbf{Z} = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma_F^{-2}t_1 + \sigma_S^{-2} & -\sigma_S^{-2} & & 0 \\ -\sigma_S^{-2} & & \sigma_F^{-2}t_2 + \sigma_S^{-2} & & 0 \\ \vdots & & \vdots & \vdots \\ 0 & & & \dots & \sigma_F^{-2}t_N + \sigma_S^{-2} \end{pmatrix} (5.30)
```

Figure 5.11 shows the estimated baseline using BWH for the spectra shown in Figure 5.5. In Figure 5.11, the grey line is the raw spectrum and the dark line is the estimated baseline. An obvious benefit of BWH is that the peak spectrum generated by BWH will have small degrees of artifacts compared with those shown in Figures 5.7–5.9. The consequence is that the final peak recognition will be more efficient.
