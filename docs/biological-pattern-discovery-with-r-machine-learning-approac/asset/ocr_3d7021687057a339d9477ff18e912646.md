Fig. 63.3. The relationship between the delta expressions and the p values estimated using the Gamma densities fitted for three randomly selected genes from the lerozole drug data. (a) The gene ZMIZ2. (b) The gene ATP10B. (c) The gene ARHGAP2B.

Figure 64 shows the correlation between the raw expressions and the estimated delta expressions constructed from the Gamma density models for the delta expressions of these three genes. It can be seen that three correlation coefficients were very significant. The correlation test p values were less than 1.0e–5. This indicates that fitting a Gamma density function to the delta expressions for a gene is appropriate for investigating the delta expression significance and hence investigating the gene expression bimodality.
