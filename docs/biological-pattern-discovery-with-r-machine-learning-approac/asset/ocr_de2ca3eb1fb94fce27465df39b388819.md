Figure 6.26 shows the expression distribution of the gene 201600_ at the breast cancer data set. The OR p value was less than 0.01, but the DOG p value was greater than 0.01. The COPA, OS and MOST models also predicted it as a DEG, but the t test and LSOSS models did not. Removing four cancer outliers and one normal outlier (all were the extreme outliers, the gene became indistinguishable). This is why the DOG p value was 0.86.
