## 5.9 Analyse the bacterial and macrophage data

The data was used for discriminating between the bacterial and the macrophage based on the extracted chemicals from the Raman spectra data [Lau, et al., 2012]. The macrophage was J774.1 and the bacterial was Salmonella enterica serovar Typhurium SL1344. There are 11 replicates of macrophage spectra and nine replicates of bacterial spectra sampled at 30, 90, 150 and 360 minutes. BWH has discovered important signals which match the expectation.

Figure 5.23 shows the spline estimation of the baselines for 11 macrophage spectra. The advantage of the spline approach is its speed. However, the estimated baselines may have an accuracy problem.

Figure 5.24 shows the extracted peaks for the first three of 11 replicates of macrophage spectra based on the spline models. It can be seen that the estimated peak spectra contain too many artifacts. In addition, the peak spectra estimated using the spline approach need more work to align peak spectra.
