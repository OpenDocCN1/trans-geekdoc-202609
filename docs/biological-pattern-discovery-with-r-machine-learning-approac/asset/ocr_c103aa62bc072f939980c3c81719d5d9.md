AB899999 in 2003. In order to demonstrate whether the homology alignment approach will make a sense to species comparison, a sequence from a different family was used. The Yersinia pastis sequence (QANK01002681) was used for a demonstration. The aim was to verify whether two species of completely different families show a less similarity when their sequences are aligned. In addition, it aims to verify whether two of a closely related or the same family show a greater similarity when their sequences are aligned. Theoretically, this Yersinia pastis species should share less similarity with MT42778, the SARS-CoV-2 species.

The Needleman-Wunsch algorithm and the Smith-Waterman algorithm were applied to examine the similarity between these sequences. Table 7.19 shows the result. It can be seen that MT042778 (SARS-CoV-2) shares a greater similarity with AB899999 (SARS-CoV) than with QANK01002681 (Yersinia pastis). This was what we expected. The identity percentages were 76.2% and 96.5% using the Needleman-Wunsch algorithm and the Smith-Waterman algorithm to compare MT042778 (SARS-CoV-2) with AB899999 (SARS-CoV). It was indeed about 80% similarity as noted in an earlier study [Caldaria, *et al.*, 2020]
