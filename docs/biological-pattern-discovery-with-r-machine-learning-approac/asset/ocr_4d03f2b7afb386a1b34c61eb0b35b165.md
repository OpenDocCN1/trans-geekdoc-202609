Figure 6.24 shows the expression distribution of the gene with the accession number 211966_at of the breast cancer data. The $COPA$ $p$ value was less than 0.01, the $DOG$ $p$ value was greater than 0.01. The gene was also predicted as a DEG by the ORT model and the MOST model, but not the $t$ test and the LSOSS model as well. The gene expression profile shows a mixed distribution of the normal and the cancer replicates. Eight replicates of both the normal and the cancer replicates were treated as the outliers and were removed, the remaining two groups of replicates, which formed a tight cluster, did not show a significant difference.
