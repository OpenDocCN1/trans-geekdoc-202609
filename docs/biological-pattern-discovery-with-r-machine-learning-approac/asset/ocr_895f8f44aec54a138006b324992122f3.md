The DOG model also worked well for the down-regulated DEGs. This is because the DOG algorithm targeted both the up-regulated and the down-regulated DEGs when outliers are present.

Figure 6.20 shows two heatmaps generated by the negative base logarithm of the p values of seven test models constructed for the same simulated data set. In the heatmap shown Figure 6.20(a), the t test, COPA, ORT, DOG and OS models demonstrated a good performance while the LSOSS and the MOST models, especially the LSOSS model, did not show a good performance. As aforementioned, the COPA, OS and ORT algorithms were all developed to deal with outliers present in the up-regulated DEGs. Figure 6.20(b) shows a heatmap of the seven models constructed for the down-regulated DEGs. In this situation, only the DOG model worked well and the COPA, OS and ORT models even showed a worse performance than the t test.
