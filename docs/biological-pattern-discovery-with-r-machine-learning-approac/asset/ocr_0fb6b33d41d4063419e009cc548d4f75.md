Figure 4.24 shows how this package was used for comparing the importance measurements for the three constrained linear regression models constructed for the olive oil content data. The result is slightly different from the output of the glmnet function, which always ranked the fruit weight as the most important contributor to the olive oil content. This is because a vip model is constructed using the cross-validation approach. It shows that three models generated a consistency result. The stone weight, stone width and fruit weight variables were the most important variables. In addition, the paste water was found as a consistent negative contributor to the olive oil in olives.
