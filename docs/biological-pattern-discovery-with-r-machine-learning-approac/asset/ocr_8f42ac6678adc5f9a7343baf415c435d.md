Suppose an example of the max function was (bReK). In this example, b and e were the second and the fifth residue of the peptide x while R and K were the amino acids used in two rules, respectively. Below shows how the fitness of this rule was calculated. Again, two similarities were calculated at first. The fitness was calculated using the greater between two similarities,
