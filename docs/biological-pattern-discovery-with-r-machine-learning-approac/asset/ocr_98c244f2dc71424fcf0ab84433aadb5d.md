# 7.4.2.2 The accuracy comparison

Whether the alignment-free approach is accurate was also examined. It aimed to investigate whether the measurement between sequences of the alignment-free approach was correlated with the alignment distance of the alignment-based approach. One hundred pairs of pseudo nucleotide sequences were randomly generated. The mutation rate was between 0.5% and 5%. Every mutation was repeated for 500 times. Therefore, there were 50,003 trials in total. Both the alignment-based approach and the alignment-free approach were applied for each pair of random pseudo nucleotide sequences. First, the distance percentage was defined as the ratio of the alignment distance over the alignment length.
