Figure 4.39 shows the ranking importance spectra for four models for the targeted DMS cg00116234. Its host gene number was ILMN_1722261. The base pair of cg00116234 was 18464243. This DMS was located at the zero degree, i.e., at the right end. Although four spectra show some difference, obviously many remote DEGs had their differential expression pattern greatly impacted the differential methylation of cg00116234. In other words, the differential methylation status of cg00116234 was mainly contributed or determined by the differential expressions of many remote DEGs.

Figures 4.40(a) and (b) show a Lasso model and a RLR model for the DMS cg00116234. In the Lasso model, the closest distance between a DEG with a high-ranking score and cg00116234 was about 1.4 mega bases among the top-ranked genes. The maximum distance was about 42 mega bases. In the RLR model, the closest distance was about 1.6 mega bases among the top-ranked genes while the maximum distance was 100 mega bases away.
