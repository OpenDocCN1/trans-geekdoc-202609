# Gene Expression Pattern Discovery 259

A six-panel volcano plot can be drawn based on data in eBay model. It divides the whole two-dimensional space into six panels. Each panel has a unique meaning. Most importantly, this six-panel volcano plot can well demonstrate the information which is required for a better visualisation.

Figure 6.9 shows an example of the six-panel volcano plot for the prostate cancer data, where 29 down-regulated DEGs and 72 up-regulated DEGs were clearly displayed if the threshold of base two logarithm of fold change was one and the p value was 0.01. In other words, DEGs were determined based on both statistical and biological significance as well as user-defined thresholds.

Fig. 6.9. A six-panel volcano plot generated for the prostate cancer data.

Each panel of a six-panel volcano plot can be well-interpreted. For this prostate cancer data set, the upper-left panel was composed of down-regulated DEGs, which were 29. The upper-right panel was composed of 72 up-regulated DEGs. The upper-middle panel was composed of genes which were statistically significant but biologically insignificant. The lower-left panel was composed of genes which were down-regulated in terms of biological significance but statistically insignificant. The lower-right panel was composed of genes which were up-regulated in terms of biological significance. The lower-middle panel was composed of genes which were both biologically and statistically insignificant. The R code of drawing a six-panel volcano plot can be requested from the author via an email.
