Figure 8.1 shows an example of how multiple candidates compete with each other to arrive at the global optimum solution. The objective function has three saddle points with three objective function values, namely $y_1, y_2$, and $y_3$. One of them ($y_1$) is the global optimum (minimum) solution. There are five initialised model parameters, i.e., $x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4$ and $x_5$. The Newton’s method will generate five models. Each of the models updates its model parameters leading to these three saddle points. When the saddle point of $y_1$ is reached, the other two saddle points are thrown away. Therefore, it is less likely to miss the global minimum in this case.
