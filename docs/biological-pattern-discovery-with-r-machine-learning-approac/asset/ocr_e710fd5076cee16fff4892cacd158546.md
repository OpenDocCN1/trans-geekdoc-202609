Using the Dayhoff matrix, the learning was terminated at the 67th cycle. The fitness measurement and the AUC value of the top rule were 0.84 and 0.93, respectively at this stage. Figure 8.26 shows the evolving AUC values and fitness measurements along with the learning. In two plots, both the maximum and the median measurements were included for both AUC and fitness. Because the best rule was maintained unless it was replaced by an even better one, the maximum AUC curve and the maximum fitness measurement curve had almost no oscillation. However, the median measurements had oscillations. This was not a surprise because the new breeding rules had their performance varying all the time. Moreover, the periodically great oscillations were due to the introduced revolution process.
