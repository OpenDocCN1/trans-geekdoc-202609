Fig. 6.50. The varying performance (AUC) of the DSG models constructed based on the data with the varying overlap between the null density of non-DEGs and the alternative density of DEGs.

The next simulated data was designed in a two-dimensional space [Al watban, 2015]. It was also composed of 900 non-DEGs, 50 down-regulated DEGs and 50 up-regulated DEGs. To assess the performance of DSG, three other algorithms which can only detect DEGs for replicated data were used. They are the eBays, the SAMr [Tusher, et al., 2001] and the cyberT [Baldi and eLong, 2001; Kayala, and Baldi, 2012]. Figure 6.51(a) shows the ROC curves and the AUC values of four models. Their performance was similar although DSG outperformed the others slightly. Figure 6.51(b) shows the Venn diagram between four models for the two-dimensional simulated data. This consensus analysis also shows that four models demonstrated a high degree of agreement in DEG detection.
