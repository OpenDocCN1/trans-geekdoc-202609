Because expression levels can vary with genes, fitting a Gamma
density function to each individual gene may have a significant variation
of the delta expression significances across genes. Such a heterogeneous
property may lead to inconsistent interpretation of the discovered bimodal
genes for a data set. In order to provide more robust estimation of the delta
expression significance, a Gamma density function was fitted to whole
genome delta expressions for the letrozole breast cancer treatment
data. 6.36 shows the result. It can be seen that such an approach
sificantly reduced the fitting error, i.e. from 0.3664 to 0.000252.
Therefore, fitting a single Gamma density function to whole genome delta
expression may provide a more robust way to evaluate the delta expression
significance gene-wise, and hence very useful for gene expression
bimodality discovery.
