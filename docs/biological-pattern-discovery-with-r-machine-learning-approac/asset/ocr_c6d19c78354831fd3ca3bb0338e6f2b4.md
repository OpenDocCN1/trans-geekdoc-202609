After removing the estimated baseline from the raw spectrum, a peak spectrum is shown in Figure 5.12. An intensity within such a spectrum is treated as a potential peak or signal if it is positive, i.e., $p_i > 0$. However, not every potential peak is a true peak. Among them, some may be false peaks. For instance, the artifacts with very low intensities shown in Figure 5.12 may not be the true peaks. A true peak must have a significant height compared with its neighbour artifacts.
