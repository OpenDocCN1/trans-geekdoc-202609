# Whole Genome Pattern Discovery

Either a DNA sequence or a protein sequence is the basic component for most genomics research. It is well understood that a sequence is the main carrier of genetic information for any species. It is also no doubt that genomics research can hardly be successful without looking into sequence constituents. Importantly, most novel and significant discoveries in biology or medicine are based on sequencing data analysis nowadays. This chapter will introduce the commonly used sequence analysis approaches from the basic ones to the advanced ones and mainly focus on the sequence comparison approaches for whole genome pattern discovery. Moreover, this chapter will show how the sequence comparison approaches can be used to analyse the SARS-CoV-2 pandemic data.

# 7.1 The SARS-CoV-2 pandemic

The COVID-19 (Coronavirus Disease 2019) pandemic caused by SARS-CoV-2 (severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2) is still in a huge challenging situation worldwide it firstly emerged in Wuhan, China in December 2019 [Zhou, et al., 2020]. Till the 30th March 2021, there have been more than 128.6 million infections and more than 2.8 million deaths worldwide reported at worldmeter webpage. Until the 4th January, GISAID has collected 315,253 SARS-CoV-2 DNA genome sequences from almost all countries in the world.
