198. C. The most common motivation for hacktivists is to make a political statement. Reputational gains are often associated with script kiddies, whereas financial gain is most commonly a goal of organized crime or insider threats. Gathering high-value data is typical of both nation-state actors and organized crime.

199. D. Predictive analysis tools use large volumes of data, including information about security trends and threats, large security datasets from various security tools and other sources, and behavior patterns, to predict and identify malicious and suspicious behavior.

200. C. Identity fraud and identity theft commonly use Social Security numbers as part of the theft of identity. Tailgating involves following a person through a security door or gate so that you do not have to present credentials or a code, whereas impersonation is a social engineering technique where you claim to be someone else. Blackmail is a potential answer, but the most common usage is for identity fraud.
