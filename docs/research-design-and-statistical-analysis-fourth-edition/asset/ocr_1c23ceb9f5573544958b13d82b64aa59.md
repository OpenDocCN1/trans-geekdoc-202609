4.7 [Binomial hypothesis testing] The dataset EX4_7 contains data for two cases on variables, $Y_1$ and $Y_2$. Each variable is dichotomous; $Y_1$ has seven values of 1 and three of 0 whereas $Y_2$ has five values of 1 and five of 0. Test the hypothesis $H_0: \pi = .5$ at $\alpha = .06$ for both $Y_1$ and $Y_2$. Use the bin.test function in the [stats] package. In SPSS, start by selecting *Nonparametric Tests* from the *Analyze* menu, then *Legacy Dialogs, Binomial*. Add both $Y_1$ and $Y_2$ to the *Test Variable* list, and insert .50 for the *Test Proportion*, and then click on OK.
