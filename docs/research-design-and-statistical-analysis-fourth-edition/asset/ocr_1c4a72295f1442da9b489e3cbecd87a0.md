This tendency of predicted $z$ scores to be less “extreme” than the $z$ scores used to predict them is called regression toward the mean and is a characteristic of bivariate regression whenever two variables have equal variances and do not have a perfect linear relationship. The prediction equations we have discussed are given the name “regression equations” in recognition of this characteristic.
