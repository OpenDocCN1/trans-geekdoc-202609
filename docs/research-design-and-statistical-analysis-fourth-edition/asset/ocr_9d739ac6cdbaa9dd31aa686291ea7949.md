# 15.3.2 Partitioning Sum of Squares and Degrees of Freedom

As our analysis of the data in Table 15.1, we have two-participants sources of variance: A (problem difficulty level) and B/A (problems nested within difficulty level). In the present design, we have added the between-participants variable, C (experience), so participants are now nested within levels of C. The SV column in the ANOVA of Table 15.5 includes these four sources of variance (C, S/C, B/A) as well as additional interaction terms. The AC term is the interaction of difficulty level with experience, and $SS_{AC}$ is calculated in all past designs. Because all problems are present at both levels of experience, C also crosses with the problems given, giving rise to the CB/A term.
