3.1 [Basic probability calculations] Suppose an experiment similar to the one described in the chapter is designed to test for the existence of ESP. The major difference is that there are five cards in the pack instead of four. Suppose the subject, S, has no ESP and randomly picks one of the cards on each trial. Assuming independence, what is the probability that S is (a) correct on the first trial? (b) correct on each of the first three trials? (c) correct on the second trial but wrong the first and third? (d) correct on exactly one of two of the first trials? (e) correct on exactly one of first three trials? (f) correct for the first time on the fifth?
