# 13.7 Measures of Effect Size

Our analysis of the Seasons data, shown in Table 13.2, informed us that differences among the seasonal depression means are statistically significant. However, there are additional questions to be asked of the data. For one, we would like indication of the practical or theoretical importance of this question. There are several statistics that might be computed on the proportion of variability effect size based accounted for by our treatment, A. The measures, $\eta^2$ and $\omega^2$, were also discussed in the context of between-participants designs in Chapters 8 and 9, and developments here will be similar. We will also consider Cohen's f because of its relevance to power calculations.
