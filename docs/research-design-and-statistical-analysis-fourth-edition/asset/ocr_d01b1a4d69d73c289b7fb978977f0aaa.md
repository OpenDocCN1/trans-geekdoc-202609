# 18.3.3 The Semiparial (or Part) Correlation Coefficient

In addition to the partial correlation coefficient, it is possible to compute the semiparial correlation coefficient. This statistic has a useful interpretation in multiple regressions and will be discussed further in Chapter 20. For now, we simply indicate what it is and how to compute it.

The semiparial correlation coefficient $r_{Y|W}$ is the correlation between $Y$ and $XW$, where $X|W$ the residual $X$ is regressed on $W$. The coefficient may be obtained by regressing $X$ on $W$, and correlating the resulting residuals with $Y$, or by using Equation 18.11,

```
$r_{Y\{X|W\}} = \frac{r_{XY}-r_{XW}}{\sqrt{1-r_{X}^2}} (18.11)$
```

Notice that the part of $X$ that can be predicted by $W$ has been removed, but no adjustment has been made to $Y$.
