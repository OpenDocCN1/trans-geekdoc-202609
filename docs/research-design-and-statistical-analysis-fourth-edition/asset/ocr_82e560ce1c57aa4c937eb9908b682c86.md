Participants (S = id) and Items are random-variables. Note that the error terms for R, V, and the RV interaction are correction in the R output. We need quasi-Fs. For R, the error term is $MS_{RS} + MS_{Items/RV}-MS_{Items/RV} = 2.541$ with $S=id$; for V it's $MS_{VS} + MS_{Items/RV}-MS_{Items/RV} = 2.692$; for RV it's $MS_{RVS} + MS_{Items/RV}-MS_{Items/RV} = 2.763$. The error $df$ follow from Equation 14.7: for V, 12,482; for RV, 13,301. So $F_R = 12.794 (p=.003)$; $F_V = 3.042 (p.104)$.
