We have already noted that expressions for the correlation coefficient are symmetric in X and Y. The correlation between cholesterol level and age is the same as the correlation between age and cholesterol level. This is not the case for regression equations (see Figure 17.8). The equation for predicting Y from X will be the same as that for predicting X from Y only if (a) there is a perfect linear relationship between X and Y and (b) X and Y both have the same mean and standard deviation – that is, if Y = X.

5. Linearity. The Pearson correlation coefficient is a measure of strength of the linear relationship between X and Y. It provides no information about whether there is a systematic nonlinear relationship between the two variables.
