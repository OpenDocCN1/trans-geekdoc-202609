**5.1** [z scores] A standard IQ test yields scores that are normally distributed with $\mu=100$
and $\sigma=15$. Y is a randomly selected score on the test.

+   a) What is the probability that a randomly selected student will score higher than 115?
b) What is $p(Y > 130)$? (ii) $p(85 < Y < 145)$? (iii) $p(Y > 70)$? (iv) $p(70 < Y < 80)$?
c) What scores defines the middle 80% of the distribution?
d) What is the 75th percentile (score that it exceeds 75% of the scores)?
e) What is the probability that the mean IQ of a group of 10 randomly selected stu-
will be greater than 105?

**5.2** [z scores] On a new test of logical reasoning, the mean and standard deviations for a
population of students School A are $\mu=170$ and $\sigma=50$; for students School B,
$\sigma=200$ and $\sigma=60$.

+   a) What is the probability that a randomly School B student will have a score greater
than 170?
b) What is the probability that the mean of a group of nine randomly selected School
students will be greater than 170?
c) Assume that many pairs of School A and School B scores are drawn from
respective populations and a difference score, $d=B-A$, calculated for each pair.
What is the mean and standard deviation of the population of such differences
(see Appendix 5.1)?
d) What is the probability that a randomly selected School B will have a
score more than a randomly School A student? [Note: $p(B>A)=p(B-A>0)$].
