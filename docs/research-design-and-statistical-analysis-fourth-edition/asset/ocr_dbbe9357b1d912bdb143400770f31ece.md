# 14.3.2 Assumptions in the Mixed Design

As in repeated-measures design, the univariate F test requires the assumption of sphericity; it is assumed that within each level of A the population variances of difference scores for all pairs of B are level. Violations of this assumption will result in inflation of Type 1 error rates. However, in the pure-within design, the epsilon-adjusted F test provides an approximately correct p-value. Whether testing B or AB, the numerator and denominator df are multiplied by the Greenhouse–Geiser or Huyn–Feldt estimate of epsilon, reducing df and thus requiring a larger F for significance than would be without the requirement.
