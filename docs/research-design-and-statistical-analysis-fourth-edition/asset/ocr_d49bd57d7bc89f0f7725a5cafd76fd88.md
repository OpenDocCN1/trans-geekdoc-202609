# 6.7.3 Two Cautions on Effect Size

Although effect size provide important information to the researcher, we offer some cautions on their use. First, Cohen's d can be mis-estimated. All values of Cohen's d are positively biased, especially for small samples. With fewer than 32 observations (i.e., 16 participants/group for independent samples), d should be discarded as described in Section 6.7.1. In addition, estimates of Cohen's d usually assume that the group variances are homogeneous. When the variances for the two groups differ, d may be a poor estimate of d, and d* may not fully compensate. This other issue in using d as an index of effect size are discussed in several articles, together with possible solutions (Griss & Kim, 2001; Kelley, 2005; Olejnik & Algina, 2000).
