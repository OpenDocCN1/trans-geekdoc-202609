Should all the tests be considered a single family, controlling Type I error rates simultaneously for all hypotheses tested in the experiment, There are generally agreed-upon answers to such questions. We will make some recommendations; however, depending on their designs and the questions we wish to address, investigators may decide on different approaches than the one we take in this section. Whatever the approach to controlling FWE, it is important that any research report should exactly how the FWE was controlled so that readers may perform their evaluation of the significance of the results.
