Even though we can reject the null hypothesis $\rho = 0$ for the example in which we have
$r = .594$ in a sample of size $N=28$, we cannot be certain about the exact value of $\rho$ because
the 95% confidence interval is quite wide. It is sobering to consider how large confi-

t sample correlation of .594 from a sample of $N=100$, the 95% confidence interval
would extend from approximately .45 to .71 – still quite large. We strongly recommend
finding confidence intervals for correlation coefficients.
