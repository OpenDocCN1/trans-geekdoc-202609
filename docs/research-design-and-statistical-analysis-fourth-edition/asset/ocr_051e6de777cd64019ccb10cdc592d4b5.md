In Contrast 2, the weights on the means were calculated with Equation 10.7: The weight on the HS mean is the sum of the other three conditions; the weights on C, B, and GS are computed as -1 the group size. Results are reported when variances are assumed and when they are not; we will discuss the calculations for the case in Section 10.3. For note, that the assumption about variances can greatly influence the values of *t* and *p*.

Although the two contrasts are very similar in this case, equal weighting and weighting by frequency can yield different results; the distribution of group sizes is the critical factor. The reason results were similar in this example is that if we divide 109, -33, -37, and -39 by 6, we get 3.028, -.917, -1.028, and -1.083 – not very different from 3, -1, -1, and -1, which is equivalent to 1, -1/3, -1/3, and -1/3.
