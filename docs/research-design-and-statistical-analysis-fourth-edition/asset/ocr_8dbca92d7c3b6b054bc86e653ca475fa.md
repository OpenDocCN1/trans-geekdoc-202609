# 19.7.4 Independence

We assume that the error components, the ε_i are independent of one another. If they are positively correlated, perhaps because some important variables have been omitted from the model, standard errors calculated by the usual ordinary least-squares procedures may underestimate the true standard errors of the regression coefficients and the confidence intervals, and hypothesis tests based on them will not be appropriate.
