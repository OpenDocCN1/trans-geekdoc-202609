# 16.4.1 Exploratory Analyses

Table 16.1 presents the means for the delay x cell durations.² These means are also plotted, together with standard error bars, in Figure 16.1. It appears that participants find the clips more unpleasant the longer they are exposed to them; this is true of test delay conditions, although the increase in ratings with duration is slightly greater when participants rated the videos immediately after each clip. The means are also higher in the immediate than the delayed test condition. Finally, we note that the error bars are roughly similar in length across durations, indicating that the within-cell variances in the three durations do not differ greatly.
