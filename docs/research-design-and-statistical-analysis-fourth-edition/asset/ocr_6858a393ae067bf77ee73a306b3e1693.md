5.8 [Properties of estimators] Two random samples are available from a population with unknown mean. Sample 1 has $n_1$ scores and has a mean of $ar{Y}_1$, sample 2 has $n_2$ scores and a mean of $ar{Y}_2$. Consider two possible estimates of the population mean, $\mu_e$ that are based on both samples: One is the weighted mean of the sample means, UM,

```
UM = \frac{\bar{Y}_1+\bar{Y}_2}{2}
```

and the other is the weighted mean of the sample means, where

```
WM = \frac{n_1\bar{Y}_1+n_2\bar{Y}_2}{n_1+n_2}
```

In the following questions, remember that $E(\bar{Y}_i) = E(Y_i) = \mu_i$

+   a) Is UM an unbiased estimator of the population mean? Show why or why not.

+   b) Is WM an unbiased estimator of the population mean? Show why or why not.

+   c) Assume that $n_1=20$ and $n_2=80$, and the population variance, $\sigma^2$, equals 4. Calculate the variance of UM.

+   d) Calculate the variance of WM. Is UM or WM a better estimate of the population mean? Why?

+   e) Is either UM or WM a better estimate of the population mean than $ar{Y}_1$? Why?
