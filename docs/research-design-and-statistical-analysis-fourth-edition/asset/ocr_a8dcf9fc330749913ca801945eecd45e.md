# 14.2.1 The Structural Model and Expected Mean Squares (EMS)

We assume that $Y_{ijk}$, the score from participant $i$ in the cell formed by $A_j$ and $B_k$, is composed of a true score and error of measurement; i.e., $Y_{ijk} = \mu_{ijk} + \varepsilon_{ijk}$. We may rewrite this in terms of the grand mean and the deviation of the true score from that:

$Y_{ijk} = \mu + (\mu_{ijk} - \mu) + \varepsilon_{ijk}$

Under a nonadditive model, we assume that individual differences $(\eta_i)$, the effects of $A (\alpha_j)$ and $B (eta_k)$, and their interactions contribute to the difference between $\mu_{ijk}$ and $\mu$. Substituting these effects for $\mu_{ijk} - \mu$, we have

$Y_{ijk} = \mu + \alpha_j + eta_k + (\eta\alpha)_{ij} + (\etaeta)_{ik} + (\alphaeta)_{jk} + (\eta\alphaeta)_{ijk} + \varepsilon_{ijk}$ (14.1)
