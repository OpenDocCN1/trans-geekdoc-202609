+   15.1 [Understanding nested terms] To understand what the nested terms represent, it may help to note that sums of squares for nested terms as items-within-moods (B/A in Table 15.3) represent a pooling of sums of squares across the levels of the higher-order factor (e.g., Mood). A possible use of understanding is that it provides an approach to the ANOVA in the absence of software that calculates nested sums of squares.
