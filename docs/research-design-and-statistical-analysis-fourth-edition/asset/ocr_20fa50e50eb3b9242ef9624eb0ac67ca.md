The mean square, $\sum\Sigma(Y_{ij} - \hat{Y}_i)^2 / (N - a)$, estimates the variance of the scores around the conditional mean of $Y$. The nonlinearity SS term obtained by subtracting $SS_{residual}$ has 2 df used in estimating the slope and intercept of the linear regression equation; equivalently, $(N-2) - (N-a) = a-2$. The corresponding mean square estimates a quantity that is the sum of ${pure\ error}$ and a component that reflects the departure from linearity.
