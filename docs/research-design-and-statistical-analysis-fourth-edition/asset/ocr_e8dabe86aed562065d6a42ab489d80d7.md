$F = \frac{MS_{A(adj)}}{MS_{S/A(adj)}} = \frac{(R^2_{Y,A} - R^2_{Y,X})SS_{Y} / (a-1)}{(1 - R^2_{Y,X})SS_{Y} / (N-a-1)}$

where the $F$ is distributed on $a-1$ and $a(n-1)-1$; note the loss of one degree of freedom because of the estimation of $b_{S/A}^*$. Table 24.1 summarizes the sources of variance, and its interpretation, for both one-factor ANCOVA and ANCOVA.

Table 24.1 Explanation of the terms in one-factor ANCOVA and ANCOVA outputs

| SV | df | Explanation |
|---|---|---||
| ANCOVA: Compare $Y_i = \mu + \alpha_i + \epsilon_i$ for $Y_i = \mu + \epsilon_i$ | | | "Between" variability in $Y$ (i.e., variability accounted for by group membership) |
| A | $a-1$ | $R^2_{Y,S}SS_Y$ | "Within" variability in $Y$ (i.e., the sum of the variabilities within each of the $a$ groups) |
| S/A | $n-a$ | $(1-R^2_{X,A})SS_Y$ | Total variability in $Y$ (i.e., variability accounted for the mean) |
| Total | $n-1$ | $SS_Y$ | |
| ANCOVA: Compare $Y_i = \mu + \alpha_i + eta_{S/A}(X_i - ar{X}) + \epsilon_i$ for $Y_i = \mu + eta_w(X_i - ar{X}) + \epsilon_i$ | | | Variability accounted by the full model and over that accounted for regression by X without regard to group membership |
| (adj) | $a-1$ | $(R^2_{X,A}-R^2_{R,A})SS_Y$ | Variability accounted by the full model and over that accounted for group membership alone; equivalently, the sum of the variabilities accounted for separate regressions in group but with a common slope |
| X | 1 | $(R^2_{X,A}-R^2_{R,A})SS_Y = SS_{reg(group 1)} + \dots + SS_{reg(group a)} = b_{S/A}^2\sum SS_{X_i}$ | Variability accounted by the full model and over that accounted for by group membership alone; equivalently, the sum of the variabilities accounted for by the $\alpha$ within-group regressions using the common slope, $b_{S/A}$ |
| S/A | $n-1$ | $(1-R^2_{X,A})SS_Y = SS_{error(group 1)} + \dots + SS_{error(group a)} = \sum(SS_Y - b_{S/A}^2SS_{X_i})$ | Variability left unaccounted by the full model; equally the residual variability in the groups; i.e., the sum of the variabilities not accounted by the $\alpha$ within-group regressions using the common slope, $b_{S/A}$ |
| Total (adj) | $n-2$ | $(1-R^2_{X,A})SS_Y = SS_{error} - b_w^2SS_X$ | Variability left unaccounted for by regression of Y on X without regard to group membership

Note: If Y is regressed on X without regard to group membership, $SS_{reg} = b_w^2SS_Y$ and $SS = SS_Y - b_w^2SS_X$. Also, the common slope, $b_{S/A}$ is equal to the weighted average of the slopes, ($b_s$), which would be obtained in separate regressions, where the weights are sums of squares of X for the groups.
