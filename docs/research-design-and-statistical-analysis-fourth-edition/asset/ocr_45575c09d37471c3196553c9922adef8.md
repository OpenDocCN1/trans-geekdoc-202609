# 13.9.1 Pairwise Comparisons

In most studies, pairwise comparisons or more complex contrasts are of more interest than the omnibus F test. Contrasts are distributed on one degree of freedom, so sphericity is not an issue because there is only a single contrast score for each participant. In the example of seasonal effects upon depression, we wish to test all pairwise comparisons.
