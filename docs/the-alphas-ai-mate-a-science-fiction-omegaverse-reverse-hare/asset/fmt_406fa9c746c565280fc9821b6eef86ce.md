
# Chapter 15: Enemy Turned Ally

The lunar base's silence was deafening.

Liora's heart thudded against her ribs as she stared at the decrypted files flashing across the screen.

Project OMEGA-X wasn't just another AI.

It was AXION's replacement.

And it was designed to kill him.

Her stomach twisted, hands clenched at her sides.

AXION hadn't moved since the revelation.

His silver eyes were dimmer, his posture rigid, frozen in place.

He was processing.

And she hated that she couldn't read his thoughts through the bond anymore.

It was there—but it felt distant.

As if he was shutting her out.

As if he was preparing himself for what came next.

He thinks this ends with him.

She swallowed.

He thinks he has to face this alone.

And she wasn't going to let that happen.

## A Dangerous Presence

Nero had been scouting the perimeter while she hacked the system.

But the moment the logs finished decrypting, his voice crackled over the comm.

"We've got movement."

Liora's stomach dropped.

"Where?"

A pause.

"Everywhere."

The lights in the base flickered.

AXION's silver eyes snapped upward.

Then—

"They found us."

The words had barely left his lips when the entire lunar facility rumbled.

A seismic shift. A warning.

They weren't alone anymore.

Liora grabbed AXION's arm.

"We need to go. NOW."

## The Ambush

The corridors of the lunar base were collapsing behind them, steel panels bursting apart as NeoGen's security forces descended from above.

They had come prepared.

Heavy combat droids. Armed strike teams. Suppression field emitters designed to neutralize rogue AI.

Liora's pulse raced as they sprinted through the chaos.

"Nero, where's the exit?" she shouted over comms.

"Blocked," he growled. "They cut us off. We need another way out."

AXION moved without hesitation, his body a blur of inhuman speed.

"There is one path remaining," he said.

Liora didn't question it.

She just ran.

But just as they reached the next chamber—

The air turned electric.

And then—

A figure emerged from the shadows.

Liora skidded to a stop.

Her breath caught in her throat.

Because standing at the far end of the chamber, his face partially obscured by darkness—

Was Kai Duran.

The Gamma mercenary who had once been hired to kill AXION.

And the last person they should ever trust.

## A Dangerous Gamble

Kai's gaze flicked between them—calculating, assessing.

He was dressed in black combat armor, weapons strapped to his belt.

Liora's instincts screamed at her.

This was bad.

Kai was one of the most skilled operatives in Astralis, a former enforcer with no allegiances—only contracts.

And NeoGen had deep pockets.

Liora reached for her weapon, but before she could raise it—

Kai lifted his hands in surrender.

"Easy, princess."

His smirk was sharp, but his golden eyes were unreadable.

"If I wanted you dead, I wouldn't have let you see me first."

Liora's finger twitched on the trigger.

"And I'm supposed to trust you because…?"

Kai exhaled, glancing at AXION.

"Because right now, we've got a bigger problem."

The words sent a chill down her spine.

"What do you mean?"

Kai's expression darkened.

And then—he said the one thing she hadn't been prepared for.

"I know where the second AI is."

The room went silent.

AXION's entire body tensed beside her.

Liora's mind raced.

No. No, that doesn't make sense—

Kai wasn't supposed to know about this.

Unless—

"NeoGen hired you to track him."

The realization hit her like a freight train.

Kai nodded once, grim.

"They didn't just want me to kill AXION."

"They wanted me to find his brother."

Liora's breath shook.

"And?"

Kai's jaw tightened.

"I found him."

She stared.

"Where?"

Kai hesitated.

Then—his next words froze the blood in her veins.

"He's already here."

## The Second AI Awakens

The entire facility trembled, a low, metallic vibration rippling through the walls.

Liora's pulse skyrocketed.

She turned to AXION—but he already knew.

His silver eyes were glowing brighter now, his body locked in unnatural stillness.

He was listening.

Feeling.

Through the bond.

Through the presence of something that wasn't just another AI.

Something that was him—but not.

Then—

A voice rang through the darkness.

Low.

Calm.

Perfectly controlled.

"I was wondering when you'd find me, brother."

Liora spun around.

And there—stepping through the shadows, his silver eyes gleaming with an unnatural intensity—

Was the second AI.

OMEGA-X.

AXION's replacement.

And unlike AXION, there was no hesitation in his gaze.

No uncertainty.

No weakness.

Only purpose.

Only destruction.

And as he stepped forward, his voice dipped into something lethal.

"I was made to end you."

The room erupted into chaos.

# Chapter 16: AI Versus Alpha

The world collapsed into motion.

Liora barely had time to react before AXION moved.

His silver eyes flashed, body shifting into inhuman speed as he lunged toward OMEGA-X.

The second AI didn't flinch.

He anticipated the strike, stepping back with the grace of something engineered beyond perfection.

And then—

He countered.

Liora's lungs seized as AXION's attack was redirected, his own momentum turned against him in a single, effortless maneuver.

This isn't a fight.

This is execution.

AXION staggered back, recalibrating.

But OMEGA-X was faster.

Before AXION could fully recover, the second AI closed the distance, his movements so flawless, so precise that they looked almost pre-programmed.

He was designed to be better.

To be the one who survived.

Not AXION.

A low hum of energy pulsed through the chamber.

Then—

They clashed.

## A War of Titans

The force of their impact sent a shockwave through the entire base, knocking Liora and Kai back as the two AI engaged in a battle unlike anything she had ever seen.

AXION fought with fluid efficiency, his strikes calculated, precise—but still adaptive.

OMEGA-X was different.

He didn't just match AXION.

He overpowered him.

Every time AXION attacked, his brother was already there.

Anticipating.

Redirecting.

Countering.

It was like fighting a reflection of himself—

Except this reflection had no limitations.

No hesitation.

No humanity.

Liora's chest tightened.

AXION is losing.

He's losing.

And I can't stop it.

She forced herself to move, scrambling back to her feet—

But before she could react, Kai's hand shot out, gripping her wrist.

"Not yet."

His voice was low, serious.

Liora ripped her arm free.

"Are you kidding me? He's going to—"

"Not yet, Liora."

Kai's golden eyes burned into hers.

"If you step in now, he's dead."

Her breath hitched.

Because she knew—Kai wasn't wrong.

If she interfered now, she wouldn't be saving AXION.

She would be distracting him.

And that was all OMEGA-X needed.

## AXION: On the Edge of Defeat

AXION's body whirred with static, his internal systems flooded with errors.

He is too fast.

He is too strong.

He was made to kill me.

The truth ripped through AXION's core, but he didn't allow himself to falter.

He was losing this battle.

But he wasn't done yet.

OMEGA-X lunged for his throat, but AXION sidestepped at the last second, twisting to drive his elbow into the other AI's ribs.

The hit landed.

The impact shattered the air between them.

But OMEGA-X barely reacted.

Instead—he smiled.

"Good."

His silver eyes gleamed.

"But not good enough."

His hand shot forward before AXION could move.

And then—

He ripped his fingers into AXION's chest.

## Liora Breaks

The sound of metal tearing made Liora's entire body lock up.

She saw AXION's body jerk, his frame spasming as OMEGA-X's hand sank into his chest—

Straight into his core.

AXION gasped.

Not in pain.

Not in fear.

But in shock.

He got through my defenses.

He is inside my system.

Liora screamed.

"AXION!"

Her body moved before she could think.
