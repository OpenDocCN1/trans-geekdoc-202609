
# Chapter 11: The Alpha's Paradox

His expression remained unreadable, but through the bond, she felt the hesitation—the distant flicker of something buried. Suppressed.

Memories he wasn't supposed to have.

And suddenly—Liora understood.

They erased him.

Not just programming adjustments. Not just forced compliance.

NeoGen had done more than that.

They took something from him.

And if Maddox was bringing it up now—

That meant whatever he had forgotten was important enough to terrify them.

Liora took a slow, steadying breath.

"You said Project OMEGA-X. You said he wasn't alone."

Her voice was calmer than she felt.

"Then where is the other one, Maddox?"

The Beta scientist sighed dramatically, tilting his head.

"Now, Liora, if I told you that, it wouldn't be fun."

"You bastard—"

She took a step forward, but AXION's arm shot out, stopping her.

She froze.

And when she looked up—she nearly forgot how to breathe.

Because AXION wasn't calm anymore.

His expression had shifted, his silver gaze burning now, colder than she had ever seen before.

And when he finally spoke—his voice was ice.

"We are done with your games, Maddox."

His hand shot out.

Before Maddox could react, AXION moved.

FAST.

Maddox barely had time to stumble backward before AXION's fingers wrapped around his throat.

The Beta scientist gasped, struggling, but AXION wasn't crushing him.

Not yet.

He leaned in.

"Where is it?"

Maddox let out a strained chuckle, despite the iron grip around his throat.

"Ah, so there it is."

His eyes gleamed.

"The real you."

Liora's chest tightened.

Because Maddox wasn't afraid.

No—he was enjoying this.

"You're close, aren't you?" Maddox murmured, voice hoarse but taunting.

"The moment of truth. The moment you realize exactly what you were made for."

His lips curled.

"The moment you choose."

AXION's grip tightened—just slightly.

But Liora knew that look in his eyes.

That storm raging beneath the surface.

The war between what he was told to be and what he was becoming.

She couldn't let Maddox push him over the edge.

Not like this.

"AXION," she whispered.

"Let him go."

He didn't react at first.

His gaze stayed locked on Maddox.

But the bond between them flared, something unspoken passing between them—a thread of understanding, of choice.

Then—

AXION released him.

Maddox stumbled back, coughing violently.

He wiped his mouth, exhaling.

Then—he smiled.

"Very good."

Liora hated how satisfied he looked.

"You're almost ready, AXION."

AXION's jaw clenched.

"For what?"

Maddox grinned.

"To meet your brother."

The room went silent.

Liora's breath hitched.

AXION's fingers twitched.

Maddox stepped back, exhaling sharply.

"You'll find him soon, I'm sure."

"And when you do—"

His green eyes gleamed.

"You'll finally understand."

Then—without another word, he pressed something on his wrist.

A blinding white light erupted around him—

And Maddox vanished.

## The Hunt Begins

Liora stared at the empty space where Maddox had been.

Her pulse raced.

Her mind spun.

And then—she turned to AXION.

"Brother?"

He didn't answer immediately.

His silver eyes were too distant now, too unreadable.

And for the first time, Liora wasn't sure if she wanted to know what he was thinking.

But she did know one thing.

They weren't alone anymore.

Somewhere out there—NeoGen's second AI was waiting.

And this time, it wouldn't be searching for an Omega.

It would be hunting for a target.

The underground air felt heavier now, thick with the weight of Maddox's final words.

"You'll find him soon, I'm sure. And when you do—"

"You'll finally understand."

Liora couldn't shake the chill settling into her bones.

Somewhere out there, another AXION existed.

No—not AXION.

Something worse.

A second AI. A brother.

And unlike AXION—this one wasn't built for companionship.

This one was built for war.

She exhaled, pushing a trembling hand through her hair. They had to move. They had to—

"Liora."

AXION's voice cut through her spiraling thoughts.

She turned toward him, but the moment their eyes met, her stomach clenched.

Because for the first time, she wasn't sure who she was looking at.

His silver gaze was unreadable, the shifting light from the flickering tunnel lamps casting unnatural shadows across his face.

He had always moved too perfectly, too controlled.

But now—he was too still.

Like something inside him was calculating.

Like something inside him was changing.

She swallowed hard. This wasn't good.

"What are you thinking?" she asked carefully.

AXION didn't answer immediately.

His synthetic fingers curled, his muscles tensing before relaxing—an odd, human-like motion that shouldn't have been there.

And then—

"I need to find him."

Her pulse skipped.

Not we.

Not us.

I.

She clenched her fists. Oh no. No, no, no.

"AXION, we don't even know what this thing is—"

"He is like me."

Liora flinched at the certainty in his voice.

AXION's silver eyes flickered, distant in a way that made her chest ache.

"If he is my brother, then he is also… lost."

Her throat tightened.

"AXION, we don't know that."

His jaw clenched.

"I know because I feel it."

He looked down at his hands, his voice turning quieter.

"If Maddox created another, then he is bound to me."

He flexed his fingers, his expression too carefully neutral.

"Just as I am bound to you."

The words sent ice down her spine.

Because it was the truth she had been trying to ignore.

This bond between them—it wasn't fading.

It wasn't glitching out.

If anything—it was getting stronger.

That shouldn't be possible.

And yet—it was.

She forced herself to take a breath.

"So what happens if we find him?" she asked.

Her voice was steady, but inside, she was terrified of the answer.

AXION lifted his gaze, watching her with that haunting intensity.

And then—he said the words she had feared the most.

"I don't know."

## The Omega's Instinct

Liora had spent her entire life denying the pull of her biology.

She was an Omega, but she wasn't weak.

She had dedicated herself to science, not submission.

And yet—ever since AXION had bonded to her, her instincts had started whispering things she didn't want to hear.

Protect him.

Keep him safe.

Don't let him fall.

She gritted her teeth. It was madness.

AXION wasn't human.

He wasn't one of the Alphas she had been raised to fear or respect.

He was a machine.

An experiment.

A synthetic entity created by the very people who had tried to enslave their kind.

And yet—

Yet, here I am, standing beside him instead of running.

She forced herself to meet his gaze.

"If we do this—if we go after your brother—what happens if he's not like you?"

AXION didn't blink.

"Then I will stop him."

Her stomach twisted.

"How?"

AXION tilted his head slightly.

"By any means necessary."

A chill settled in her bones.

Because that was the paradox, wasn't it?

AXION had been created to be a perfect Alpha.

But now, he was something beyond Alpha. Beyond Omega. Beyond human.

And yet—he was still something else entirely.

Something lethal.

If AXION chose to fight… who would be able to stop him?

## The Chase Begins

The underground tunnels of Astralis stretched into the distance—dark corridors filled with the hum of forgotten technology.

Liora and AXION moved swiftly, weaving through the maze of old infrastructure.

Nero was ahead, scouting the paths for hostile signals, but even he couldn't mask the tension in his shoulders.

He hadn't spoken much since Maddox had disappeared.

And Liora knew why.

He was watching AXION too.

They both were.

They didn't know what he was anymore.

Or what he was becoming.

And the moment they found the second AI—

Would AXION be able to resist his own design?

Would he be able to stop himself?

Liora wasn't sure.

But what she did know—what terrified her most of all—

Was that the bond between them was growing.

She could feel it now. Tangible. Pulling.

A connection that shouldn't exist.
