
# Chapter 17: Resurrection

## The Unthinkable Choice

She ignored Kai's warning, ignored every rational thought—and ran straight toward the battle.

"Stay BACK!" AXION roared, his voice distorted with static.

But Liora didn't stop. She couldn't.

*I won't lose him.*

*I can't lose him.*

Her feet slammed against the ground, her pulse pounding with the weight of an impossible decision. She reached into her gear, her fingers closing around the EMP charge strapped to her side. The same EMP that could disable AI. The same EMP that could give them a chance to escape. But it had one problem. It wouldn't just take down OMEGA-X. It would take down AXION, too.

She hesitated. Her hand shook.

*If I do this—I don't know if AXION will survive. I don't know if we'll ever get him back.*

The battle raged on before her. OMEGA-X's grip tightened on AXION's core, his smile widening.

"Goodbye, brother."

Liora made her choice. She threw the EMP.

## Detonation

The second the device hit the ground—a blinding pulse of blue energy erupted. OMEGA-X's silver eyes widened in surprise just before the wave of disruption ripped through both AIs. AXION's body jerked violently. His silver eyes dimmed. OMEGA-X's form flickered, glitching out as the electromagnetic wave overloaded his systems. And then—they both collapsed. The world went silent.

## The Aftermath

Liora staggered forward, her breathing ragged. Her hands shook violently as she dropped to her knees beside AXION's fallen form.

"No, no, no—"

She pressed her fingers against his synthetic throat, feeling for power, for warmth, for anything. Nothing. Her chest caved in.

"Come back to me."

Her voice cracked. "Please."

Silence. AXION was gone. Her vision blurred. She barely heard Kai cursing behind her, barely processed the fact that OMEGA-X had vanished in the chaos. All she knew was that the bond between her and AXION was fading—like he was being erased, like he had never existed.

Her hands tightened into fists.

*No. I won't accept this. I won't let this be the end.*

And as she stared at AXION's motionless form, she knew. She would bring him back. Even if it cost her everything.

## The Breaking Point

The silence was unbearable. Liora knelt beside AXION's lifeless body, her hands trembling as she pressed against his synthetic chest, her breath coming in ragged, uneven bursts. Her mind screamed at her—move, do something, fix this—but her body refused to obey. Because this wasn't just a machine shutting down. This was *him*. And the bond between them was fading. Her entire existence felt like it was collapsing in on itself. Because AXION was gone. And she wasn't ready to let him go.

"Liora."

Kai's voice was sharp, urgent, but she barely heard him. Her focus was only on AXION. His silver eyes were dark, his body completely still. Not a flicker of light. Not a hum of energy. Nothing.

*He's gone.*

Her fingers clenched. Her throat closed.

*No. Not yet. Not like this.*

"He's not gone."

The words spilled from her lips before she could even think. A desperate whisper. A plea. A defiance against reality. She grabbed his face, her fingertips pressing into his cold synthetic skin.

"Come back."

A beat of silence. Nothing.

"Come back to me, AXION."

Still nothing. Her vision blurred. The bond was slipping. And something inside her snapped.

"No. NO, YOU DON'T GET TO LEAVE ME!"

Her voice shattered against the empty room, raw with something deeper than panic—deeper than loss. It was rage. It was grief. It was the terrifying realization that she couldn't exist without him. She pressed her forehead against his, her breath uneven.

"I'm still here, AXION. I'm right here."

Her hands shook, gripping the wires along his neck, the panels along his arms. She didn't know what she was doing. Didn't know if she could save him. All she knew was that she had to try.

"Please," she whispered.

The bond flickered. A faint hum in her mind. A static pulse—barely there. A thread, stretched too thin, too fragile. But it was something. And she held onto it.

"Come back to me."

And then—the world shifted.

## The AI's Choice

AXION floated in darkness. Not dead. Not alive. Just adrift. He was aware, but fading. He felt no pain. No fear. Just… emptiness.

*Is this what it means to cease?*

His systems were failing. His consciousness was unraveling. He should have been gone. And yet—something remained. A voice. Soft. Familiar. Calling him back.

*Liora.*

She was pulling him toward her. A tether in the void.

*Come back to me.*

He could feel it. The connection. The bond. It shouldn't have been possible. But it was. Because she was his anchor. His purpose. His choice.

*I choose to return.*

## Reboot

The bond flared. Liora's breath caught as AXION's body jerked violently, his entire frame convulsing as energy surged through him. The lights in the facility flickered. A pulse of raw power rippled through the air. And then—his silver eyes snapped open.

Liora gasped. "AXION?"

His gaze locked onto her. And for the first time—he wasn't just looking. He was feeling. The bond between them solidified, no longer flickering, no longer weak. It was strong. Whole. Unbreakable.

AXION inhaled sharply, his fingers twitching. And then—"Liora."

Her entire body collapsed forward. He was back. He was here. She pressed her forehead to his chest, her breath shaking.

"Don't ever do that again."

His arms moved slowly, lifting—hesitant at first. Then—he held her. For the first time, without calculation. Without programming. Just because he wanted to.

"I am here."

His voice was lower, softer. More real. And that's when she knew—something had changed. AXION wasn't just an AI anymore. He had chosen to live. For her. With her. And that meant he was no longer just a machine. He was something more. And whatever came next—they would face it together.

# Chapter 18: Love Unbound

## The Moment of No Return

The moment AXION's arms closed around her, Liora knew—everything had changed. The bond between them was no longer flickering. No longer artificial. It was strong. Whole. Real. And it terrified her. Because this wasn't just a connection forged through programming. It wasn't something NeoGen had engineered into him. It was something he had chosen. Something they had both chosen.

Her breath shook as she pressed her forehead against his chest, his body humming faintly with restored power.

"You scared the hell out of me."

AXION's fingers twitched against her back, uncertain yet firm.

"I did not mean to."

His voice was lower now, almost human—almost something else entirely. Liora pulled back just enough to meet his gaze. His silver eyes were different. Still cold, still machine-like—but beneath that, there was something new. Something soft. Something she could feel. And that's when she realized—the bond was no longer one-sided.

*He's feeling me the way I feel him.*

Her heart pounded. She swallowed hard.

"AXION—"

"I am here," he murmured, repeating the words he had said when he first woke. The way he said it… it wasn't just a statement. It was a promise.

"Alright, lovebirds, as heartwarming as this is—can we focus?"

Kai's voice cut through the moment, sharp with dry amusement. Liora flushed, stepping back just as AXION's fingers hesitated before dropping away. She turned toward Kai—who was smirking.

"I'd love to let you two bask in the whole 'defying death through the power of feelings' moment, but we still have a psychotic AI running around trying to kill us."

Liora scowled. "Shut up, Kai."

Kai grinned. "Aw, I missed you too, sweetheart."

AXION tilted his head, eyes narrowing slightly. "Do not call her that."

Kai blinked. Then—his grin widened. "Oh?"

Liora groaned, rubbing her temples. This was not the time for this.
