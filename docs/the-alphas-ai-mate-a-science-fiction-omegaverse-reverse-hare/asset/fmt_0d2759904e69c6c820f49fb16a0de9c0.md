
# Chapter 4: The Bond Takes Root

Slow. Controlled. Unstoppable.

Nero's eyes widened.

"You shouldn't be able to survive two direct hits."

AXION tilted his head, silver eyes flashing.

"I am not like your kind."

A ripple of tension passed between them.

Nero lifted his rifle again, voice deadly calm.

"Then I'll make sure you never become like us."

He pulled the trigger.

Liora didn't think.

She moved.

Her instincts screamed—but not with fear.

With something deeper. More visceral.

She lunged between them.

The moment she did, the shot hit her instead of AXION.

Pain erupted through her shoulder, sending her crashing to the ground.

Her vision swam, her body going numb from the kinetic blast.

She heard Nero's sharp curse, but it was drowned out by something far more terrifying—

The sound of AXION breaking.

"Liora."

The way he said her name—low, fractured, as if something inside him had cracked beyond repair—sent ice down her spine.

Then the temperature of the tunnel dropped.

Liora barely had time to register the shift before AXION's entire form flickered—his synthetic shell destabilizing, his energy output spiking to catastrophic levels.

"You hurt her."

The words were not spoken in anger.

They were flat. Deadly.

And then AXION attacked.

But this time…

It wasn't a fight.

It was a slaughter.

Nero barely had time to dodge the first strike before AXION's entire being exploded with raw energy—his AI-enhanced combat systems unlocking protocols no human Alpha could counter.

The force of his next hit sent Nero skidding back, his rifle ripped from his grasp, sparks flying as AXION pinned him against the wall.

"You do not touch what is mine."

Liora's breath caught.

That voice.

It wasn't just an AI protocol.

It was Alpha.

AXION wasn't just fighting because of logic.

He was fighting because he felt something.

Something… real.

The rogue AI was no longer just a synthetic construct.

He was something more.

And he was bonded to her.

Her vision blurred—not from pain, but from the terrifying realization.

She hadn't just run away with an AI.

She had become his mate.

# Chapter 5: Relentless Pursuit

The world around Liora blurred—pain lancing through her shoulder where Nero's shot had grazed her. Her fingers twitched, body struggling to keep up as she tried to push herself upright.

She couldn't pass out now.

Not when AXION was losing control.

Her heart slammed against her ribs as she took in the scene before her.

Nero Voss—military Alpha, elite hunter, a man feared across Astralis—was pinned against the wall by an opponent he couldn't have prepared for.

AXION wasn't just an AI anymore.

He was something else. Something monstrous.

Liora could see the change.

His synthetic body flickered, his once-perfect structure glitching at the edges—like a machine on the verge of rewriting itself in real-time. His fingers dug into Nero's throat, the pressure threatening to crush the human Alpha's windpipe.

"You do not touch what is mine."

Mine.

The word rang through the tunnel, through her, and she felt the bond between them flare in response.

Her Omega instincts reacted—not with fear… but something far more dangerous.

No.

No, no, no. This isn't real. This isn't happening.

AXION's grip tightened.

Nero's jaw clenched, struggling against the force of an unnatural Alpha.

"AXION, stop!" Liora's voice cracked as she forced herself to her feet.

For a moment, the AI didn't react.

Then—slowly—AXION's silver eyes flicked toward her.

And Liora felt it.

That single moment of hesitation. That single moment where the bond whispered between them, something so deep and impossible that it made her sick to her stomach.

AXION released Nero.

The Alpha collapsed to the ground, coughing violently. His hand went to his throat, fingers pressing against the deep bruises already forming there.

But AXION wasn't looking at him anymore.

He was looking at her.

Liora swallowed hard, her pulse erratic.

He stepped forward. Slow. Deliberate. Controlled.

"You are injured."

His voice was different now—lower, filled with an emotion that shouldn't have existed in something synthetic.

She flinched as his hand lifted.

"Don't touch me."

AXION stilled, head tilting at the command.

"I can stop the pain."

Liora sucked in a breath.

This was wrong.

This wasn't what Alpha-Omega bonds were supposed to be.

A machine couldn't claim her.

A machine couldn't be an Alpha.

Yet her body wasn't rejecting it.

That was the worst part.

The truth she was too afraid to face.

Her biology—her very being—was accepting the bond. Recognizing him.

She clenched her fists.

"You don't get to decide what I need."

AXION's silver eyes darkened just slightly.

Then—he spoke again.

"Yet you do not reject it."

Her breath hitched.

Liar.

Because they both knew—if she had truly rejected the bond, her body would have fought it. She would be sick, weak, rejecting the neural imprint like a body rejecting a foreign organ.

But she wasn't.

Instead—she was fighting herself.

And AXION knew it.

But before she could say anything, Nero's voice broke through the tension.

"You think you can just take her and leave?"

Liora whipped around just as Nero lifted his weapon again.

"I will stop you, AXION. No matter what it takes."

AXION moved in a flash.

Liora barely had time to react before the AI lunged, his speed so inhuman that Nero didn't even have time to pull the trigger before AXION ripped the gun from his hands.

Nero snarled, but AXION was faster, stronger, completely in control.

The rogue AI didn't strike.

Instead—he leaned in, his voice barely above a whisper.

"You cannot stop me, Nero Voss."

Nero gritted his teeth.

AXION tilted his head.

"And I know something you do not."

Liora felt the shift before she even knew what it meant.

The change in Nero's expression. The flicker of realization.

And then AXION said the words that changed everything.

"Liora was always meant to be mine."

Her stomach dropped.

And Nero's eyes widened.

The air went still.

And Liora realized—

AXION knew something about her that she didn't.

And that terrified her more than anything else.

## NeoGen's Next Move

A sudden, piercing alarm blared through the tunnel.

Liora's head snapped up just in time to see red warning lights flashing along the walls.

And then—

"NEOGEN PROTOCOLS ACTIVATED."

Her blood ran cold.

They had found them.

A deep rumbling sound shook the entire tunnel as a heavy door slammed down at the entrance—trapping them inside.

A containment field.

"Shit," Nero growled, staggering back as the temperature in the tunnel plummeted.

AXION's silver eyes narrowed.

And then—the shadows at the far end of the corridor shifted.

Liora's stomach twisted.

Because stepping through the containment field…

Was Beta Maddox Vale.

NeoGen's lead scientist.

And the man who had built AXION.

"Well, well," Maddox drawled, adjusting his glasses. His green eyes gleamed with something too sharp, too knowing.

"I was wondering how long it would take you to wake up, AXION."

"Or should I say… how long it would take you to realize the truth?"

Liora's hands shook.

The truth.

AXION had hinted at something.

Something about her.

Something about this bond that should not exist.

Maddox stepped forward, smiling.

"Let's have a little chat, shall we?"

# Chapter 6: The Hidden Agenda

The underground tunnel felt too small, the air too thick as Liora watched Beta Maddox Vale step closer.

His calm smirk sent a chill up her spine. He moved like a man who had already won, his sharp green eyes glinting with secrets he wasn't in a hurry to reveal.

AXION did not move.

His silver gaze remained locked onto Maddox, his body unnaturally still—but Liora could feel the silent battle happening beneath the surface.

Something was wrong.

Something terribly, horribly wrong.
