The authors regret that before the online release of our article, some information remained unnoticed during the proof correction. After careful consideration, we decide to make this corrigendum for our article. The corrected form of the expression of Eq. (1) is given as follows:

**Revision 1: Typographical errors in** [Eq. (1)] **and** [Eq. (2)]:

$$R^2 = 1 - \sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{(y_i-\hat{y}_i)^2}{(y_i-\bar{y})^2}$$  
(1)

The corrected form of the expression of Eq. (2) is given as follows:

$$RMSE = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(y_i-\hat{y}_i)^2}{n}}$$  
(2)

where $y_i$ is the dataset target value and $\hat{y}_i$ represents its corresponding predicted value, $\bar{y}$ is the mean of the target descriptor of $n$ dataset entries.

**Revision 2:** [Table 3]’s caption was mislabelled. Two Ti–Ni–Cu alloys in [Table 3] are misrepresented as ‘Ti–47.5N–2.5Cu’ and ‘Ti–42.5N–7.5Cu’ when they should be ‘Ti–47.5Ni–2.5Cu’ and ‘Ti–42.5Ni–7.5Cu’, respectively.
