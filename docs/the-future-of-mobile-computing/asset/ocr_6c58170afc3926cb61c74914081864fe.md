# Future Innovations and Trends in Smart Agriculture

Integration of AI and ML has increased for advanced data analysis and decision making in agriculture (Han et al., 2021). AI-powered solutions help maximize resource allocation and offer insights into crop health. In summary, as technology advances for agriculture to optimize these data-driven, sustainable solutions, it will be critical to address connectivity issues, ensure data security and privacy, and adopt new technologies focused on mobile solutions for sustainable agriculture. Furthermore, it appears more and more likely that changing careers will be successful (Sott et al., 2020).
