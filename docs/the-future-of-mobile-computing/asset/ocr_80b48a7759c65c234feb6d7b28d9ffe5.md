# Abstract

Within the dynamic realms of augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR), we e tremendous shifts in our understanding of the universe as a result of the constant evolution of modern technology. This chapter breaks down the technical jargon associated with AR and VR, highlighting their unique contributions to the transformation of our digital lives. Due to their increasing prevalence in a wide range of technological applications, it is imperative that one understands the precise differences between these terminologies. This chapter is primarily concerned with the impact that augmented reality can have.
