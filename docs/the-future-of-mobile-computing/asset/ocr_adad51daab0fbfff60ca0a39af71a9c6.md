# Crop Monitoring and Management Apps

Apps (Michels et al., 2020) for crop management and monitoring are essential to contemporary agriculture because they use technology to improve farming practices’ sustainability, efficiency, and productivity. These applications are made to help farmers monitor different parts of their crops, make data-driven decisions, and maximize the use of their resources.
