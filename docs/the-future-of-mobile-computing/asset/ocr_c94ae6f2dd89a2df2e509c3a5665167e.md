# Energy-Efficient Displays and Batteries

Sensors and automation capabilities are built into energy-efficient batteries and displays (Piguet and Christian, 2018), enabling homes to maximize energy use and drastically lower their carbon footprint while also extending the life of their devices, improving user experiences, and addressing environmental concerns.

Energy-Efficient displays include OLED Technology, Power-Efficient Backlighting, Low-Power Display Technologies, Adaptive Brightness and Refresh Rate. Lithium-Ion Batteries, Solid-State Batteries, Advanced Battery Management Systems (BMS), Optimized Power Consumption, Fast Charging Technologies can be used as energy efficient batteries. Ultimately, information science, engineering, and design are all involved in the complex process of creating energy-efficient batteries and displays.
