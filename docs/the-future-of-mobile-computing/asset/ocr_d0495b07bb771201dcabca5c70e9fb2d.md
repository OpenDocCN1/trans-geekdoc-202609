# Internet of Things (IoT) Revolutionizing Manufacturing:

The Internet of Things (IoT) has emerged as a revolutionary force in the dynamic manufacturing scene, radically changing how industries function and optimise their processes. Building a strong IoT infrastructure in manufacturing plants is the cornerstone of this revolution. In order to create a ubiquitous network of fluidly communicating devices, sensors and actuators must be strategically embedded into machinery. Numerous applications are made possible by this interconnection, with predictive maintenance being one of the most significant. Manufacturers can monitor the state of their equipment in real time and switch from reactive to proactive maintenance practices by utilising the Internet of Things. By predicting and preventing potential equipment failures, condition-based monitoring helps to minimise downtime and maximize operational efficiency. IoT is also essential to supply chain optimisation, since it transforms the tracking and management of both raw materials and completed goods. Manufacturers can see raw material movements in real time with the help of IoT-enabled tracking devices, which enhances supply chain efficiency and enables better decision-making. This makes it feasible to manage inventory in real-time, which lowers the possibility of stockouts or surplus inventory. Moreover, IoT makes demand forecasting easier by analysing data from several supply chain locations, allowing enterprises to react quickly to shifting market conditions.
