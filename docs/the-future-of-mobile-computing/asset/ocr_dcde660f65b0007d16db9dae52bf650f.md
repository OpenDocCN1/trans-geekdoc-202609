The prevalence of mobile devices owes its ubiquity to advanced hardware, diverse operating systems, and a vast ecosystem of mobile applications. These applications, ranging from productivity tools to entertainment, are instrumental in elevating user experiences and meeting diverse needs. The advent of wireless communication technologies, exemplified by the evolution from 4G to the now ubiquitous 5G, has further facilitated seamless connectivity, ensuring that individuals can stay connected anytime, anywhere. Moreover, the integration of cloud computing has significantly expanded the capabilities of mobile devices. This advancement enables storage, processing, and collaboration in the cloud, reducing reliance on local device resources and enhancing overall efficiency. As individuals increasingly leverage the cloud for various tasks, the boundaries of what is achievable on mobile devices continue to expand (Ghadi B et al., ... 2021; Bilogrevic I et al., ... 2011).

# Definition and Scope

Mobile computing is the utilization of portable computing devices, including smartphones, tablets, and wearable technologies, enabling users to access and interact with information and applications seamlessly while on the move. This paradigm extends far beyond the limitations of traditional fixed computing environments, granting users the freedom to connect, communicate, and execute various tasks from virtually any location. The expansive scope of mobile computing encompasses diverse activities, ranging from wireless communication and the utilization of mobile applications to the seamless integration of cloud services (Mollah M et al., ... 2017; Ghadi S et al., 2021).

This transformative shift in computing has brought about a revolution in how individuals approach work, socialization, and engagement with digital content. The emphasis lies on flexibility, convenience, and the immediate availability of information in real-time. Whether for professional tasks, staying connected with friends and family, or consuming digital media, Today, everyone relies heavily on portable computing. The rise of nomadic computing has transformed wireless infrastructure mobility and scope, exerting a significant influence on various facets of personal and professional life on a global scale. It is a paradigm that transcends the confines of traditional computing setups, providing users with the ability to harness the power of computing wherever they go.
