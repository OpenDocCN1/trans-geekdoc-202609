# Absence of Prompt Conversation

In a face-to-face setting, communication occurs quickly and makes it easier for students to get answers and clarify confusing concepts. In an e-learning environment, communication is often asynchronous, which means there is a gap between teachers and students. In these spaces, misunderstandings can easily arise; Sometimes problems can be shelved before we can solve them.

# Responses Not Received in Timely Manner

Providing feedback is one of the most important and valuable ways for teachers to interact with students. When feedback is delayed or delayed for weeks because of the online format, students may feel confused or uncertain about your expectations, their progress, and their performance in the classroom.
