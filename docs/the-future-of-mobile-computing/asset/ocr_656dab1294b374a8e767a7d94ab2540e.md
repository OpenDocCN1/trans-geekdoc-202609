# ## Current Challenges in Agriculture

Farmers need to adapt to the shifting demands of the environment as well as those of regulators, consumers, food processors, and retailers. The effects of soil erosion, biodiversity loss, and climate change are all becoming more and more of a burden, as are consumer preferences for food and worries about its production as specified in Figure 1. Furthermore, the natural world that farming interacts with—plants, pests, and diseases—continues to present difficulties. Farmers face a variety of challenges, such as addressing soil erosion, biodiversity loss, and climate change:

+   - Meet the evolving tastes and expectations of customers
+   - Satisfy the growing desire for more upscale food.
+   - Remain resilient in the face of external economic shocks.
