In order to implement good environmental practices, decrease e-waste, and promote the development and use of sustainable materials, businesses, regulators, and consumers must work together in this area. Additionally, the carbon footprint of the technology can be reduced by using green energy in data centers and production processes. The first step to encourage change is acknowledging the environment (Peng et al., 2021) issues associated with mobile devices. It is imperative for the mobile industry to proactively seek out sustainable solutions to guarantee that technological progress does not impede environmental preservation. For a more sustainable and responsible future in the digital age, environmental factors must be incorporated into the design, manufacture, and disposal of mobile devices.
