# The Function of Natural Language Processing (NLP) in the Analysis of Mental Health

The artificial intelligence subfield of natural language processing (NLP) has become a game-changer in the field of mental health analysis. NLP is primarily interested in how computers and human language interact. NLP provides a special talent in the context of mental health: the capacity to examine and comprehend the subtleties of human expression as it is expressed in text. People frequently use words to communicate their emotional states, especially those linked to anxiety and depression—whether in private chats, online forums, or social media posts (S. C. Yeh et al., 2018).

These textual signals can be interpreted using NLP techniques and tools, which can reveal information on emotional health. This chapter explores how NLP can be used to recognise and assist people who are depressed or anxious, emphasising the possibility of early intervention and the development of a society that is more sympathetic and caring.

It is crucial to understand that, as we set out on this adventure at the nexus of emotional analysis, mental health, and computational linguistics, our methodology is both profoundly human-centric and technologically new. The sections that follow in this chapter will go into further depth on the methods and strategies that support this forward-thinking method of mental health analysis, with the ultimate goal of enhancing the wellbeing of those who encounter these difficulties.
