# In Manufacturing, Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), and Mixed Reality (MR)

The integration of Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), and Mixed Reality (MR)—all of which fall under the general category of Extended Reality (XR)—stands out as a revolutionary force in the quickly changing manufacturing landscape. By superimposing visual instructions onto actual machinery, augmented reality (AR) with its hands-free applications redefines assembly processes and technician training, increasing productivity and reducing errors in complex operations. Additionally, AR develops become a potent tool for maintenance and troubleshooting, giving professionals access to context-specific, real-time information that helps them identify and fix problems.

Conversely, virtual reality (VR) is revolutionising product design and prototyping. By immersing stakeholders in virtual worlds, it simulates the functionality and performance of products, enabling iterative development and collaborative design experiences.
