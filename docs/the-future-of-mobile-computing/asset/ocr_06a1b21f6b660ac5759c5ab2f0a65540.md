# Prospects for the Future and Progress in Computational Emotional Analysis

Computational emotional analysis is still in its early stages of development. The knowledge gathered from this study opens doors for new opportunities and developments in the future.

+   - Multimodal Analysis: Computerised emotional analysis may go beyond text in the future to include other modalities including audio and visual data. Multimodal analysis provides an even more thorough understanding of emotions.
+   - Cross-Cultural Analysis: Researching how different languages and cultures communicate emotions differently is a promising field. Creating models that are sensitive to cultural quirks is essential for international use.
+   - Personalised Mental Health care: According to the research, real-time emotional analysis may be used to provide personalised mental health care. Future systems may offer advice and resources that are specifically designed for those who require them.
+   - More Complex Machine Learning Models: By incorporating more complex machine learning models (E. W.
