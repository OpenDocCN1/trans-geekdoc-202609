# Technological Gaps in Traditional Farming

Significant technological gaps frequently exist in traditional farming methods, impeding their productivity, sustainability, and efficiency. The limited application of sophisticated machinery and smart agriculture (Abbasi et al., 2022) technologies is one obvious gap. This results in inefficient resource utilization in addition to adding to the workload. Another technological shortcoming in traditional farming is the lack of access to real-time data and information. Using sensors, satellite imagery, and weather forecasts, data-driven decision-making is essential to modern agriculture.
