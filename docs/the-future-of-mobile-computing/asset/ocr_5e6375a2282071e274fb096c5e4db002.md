# Introduction

Mobile computing refers to the use of computing devices, such as smartphones, tablets, and wearable devices, that are designed to be easily transported and used while on the move. It involves the creation, transmission, and access of information using a variety of wireless technologies and networks. Mobile computing has become increasingly prevalent in modern society, enabling users to access data and applications from almost anywhere. Portability, Wireless Connectivity, Location Awareness, Limited Resource Constraints, Touch Interfaces, Diverse Applications, Multitasking are some of the mobile computing. Mobile computing is changing the way people work, communicate and access information. It also facilitates the development of a broad mobile application ecosystem for a variety of needs and interests. Businesses continue to evolve with advances in hardware, software and network technology, making mobile devices an integral part of the daily lives of many people and businesses (Singh et al., 2022).
