Major problems of efficient disposal and recycling of electronic waste are energy consumption by data centers enabling cloud services, manufacturing electronic parts and discarding outdated devices. To address these environmental issues, the mobile sector is starting to realize the increasing desire for sustainable solutions. Mobile device manufacturers, legislators and consumers need to collaborate in order to develop strategies to minimize the negative impact of these gadgets on the environment. Industry can take steps at various points in the life cycle of products from energy efficient manufacturing techniques, ecological design, and ethical sourcing of raw materials in order to reduce its impact on nature.
