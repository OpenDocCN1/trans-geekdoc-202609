# Multiple Camera Systems and the Evolution of Mobile Photography

The integration of numerous camera systems in smartphones is a fundamental component of the evolution of mobile photography, which has been greatly influenced by advancements in camera technology (Parulski et al., 2000). Some important information about multiple camera systems and how they impact mobile photography is as follows in Figure 1.

+   - **Dual, Triple, and Quad Camera Setups:** The majority of early cellphones had a single rear camera. But the fashion moved toward two-camera, then three-, and ultimately four-camera arrangements. Diverse lenses have diverse functions; examples include telephoto, depth-sensing, wide-angle, and ultra-wide-angle lenses.
+   - **Enhanced Photography Capabilities:** Because wide-angle lenses catch more in a single frame, they are ideal for group shots and landscape photography. These lenses allow users to get up close and personal with distant subjects without sacrificing the quality of their photos by enabling optical zoom (Delbracio et al., 2021).
+   - **Depth Sensing and Portrait Mode:** When in portrait mode, the blend of multiple cameras and depth-sensing technology creates a bokeh effect that looks realistic.
