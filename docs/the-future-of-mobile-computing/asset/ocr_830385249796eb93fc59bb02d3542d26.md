Sometimes, the Internet of Things devices have processing capabilities that are referred to as edge computing. Through the use of edge computing, it is possible to guarantee that the communication network is only exposed to the information that is the most significant and relevant.

Within a computer system, the protection, monitoring, and control of network traffic are all essential functions that need the implementation of a firewall security system. In order to guarantee the safety of the data that is continuously sent inside a smart city network, firewalls are installed to prevent anyone getting into the Internet of Things without permission network or the data of the city.

# Devices and Sensors

Data is collected from physical assets, machinery, and equipment via a broad variety of devices and sensors that are a part of the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) networks. A wide variety of sensors and devices, such as temperature sensors, pressure sensors, motion sensors, actuators, and others, may be included in this category.
