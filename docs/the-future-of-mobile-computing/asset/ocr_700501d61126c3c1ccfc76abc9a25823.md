# Kinetic and Piezoelectric Charging Technologies

Two advanced methods of converting electricity into electrical energy (Jiju et al., 2014) are kinetic charging and piezoelectric charging. Now let’s look at these one by one. The process of converting mechanical energy or kinetic energy into electrical energy is called kinetic energy load. These systems are often used by devices that can record movement (such as the movements of the human body or other systems) and convert it into electricity. Also, to convert kinetic energy into electricity, kinetic energy (Malla et al., 2016) chargers usually use electromagnetic induction, piezoelectric materials or other means. Fitness monitors, technology and some consumer electronics often use kinetic energy to charge products. The main advantage of kinetic charging is that it produces electricity from an external source and the limitation is kinetic charging frequently produces very little energy compared to other charging techniques

The foundation of piezoelectric charging is the piezoelectric effect (Elmannai et al., 2023) the capacity of some materials to produce an electric charge when mechanical stress is applied. Quartz, Rochelle salt, and lead zirconate titanate (PZT) are examples of common piezoelectric materials. These substances were picked because they can produce a voltage under mechanical stress.
