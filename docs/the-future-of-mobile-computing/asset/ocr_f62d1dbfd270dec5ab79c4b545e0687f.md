# Overview of NLP and Mental Health

A person’s mental health affects every part of their life and is essential to their general wellbeing and quality of life. Still, it’s a complex and multifaceted issue that affects millions of individuals worldwide with diseases including depression and anxiety (L. Minkowski et al., 2021). Thanks in part to technology improvements, the field of Natural Language Processing (NLP) has made enormous strides in identifying and comprehending mental health issues. This section establishes the framework for the rest of the chapter by highlighting the vital significance of treating mental health issues and how NLP has evolved into an effective tool for analysis and assistance.
