# EOSDA Crop Monitoring

A cutting-edge precision farming platform for food producers, insurance providers, input suppliers, and other agricultural stakeholders is called EOSDA Crop Monitoring. It is driven by satellite technology. Get data on crop health, weather, crop rotation, field operations, elevation, soil moisture, and more with ease. All of this data is smoothly integrated into an intuitive interface.

# CROPIN

Pioneers in the worldwide Ag Tech space, CROPIN created CROPIN. Cloud, the first industry cloud specifically designed for agriculture in history. With the help of CROPIN Cloud, different agri-ecosystem stakeholders may effectively use digitization and predictive intelligence to make decisions that improve sustainability, manage risk and environmental changes, raise farming efficiency, and scale productivity.

# Map My Crop

Map My Crop is a cutting-edge Ag Tech startup that provides farmers with advanced crop monitoring solutions through the use of satellite images, artificial intelligence, and machine learning. Farmers will be able to better understand their crops and make more educated decisions about their operations with the software’s help.
