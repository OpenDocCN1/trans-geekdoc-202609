+   - Without exchanging raw data, federated learning allows model training across dispersed edge devices. All that is transmitted are model updates.
+   - Collaboration from Edge to in some cases, cloud and edge-based machine learning models work with cloud servers. While inference is managed at the edge, model updates or training can be performed in the cloud.
+   - Energy Efficiency, the aim of creating learning models in edge-based systems is to reduce energy consumption in computing.
+   - Instant decisions, Edge-based models enable rapid decisions without the need for central servers or constant Internet connections.
+   - Adaptive Learning, it Adapting to Changing Environment or User Activity The edge-based machine learning model will include adaptive learning strategies.

The need for minimum latency, privacy protection, and resource efficiency is the driving force behind deploying machine learning models at the edge. As technology evolves, machine learning remains critical for intelligent use across multiple disciplines.

# Current Challenges in AI-Powered Mobile Computing

Today, mobile computing driven by artificial intelligence faces many challenges. Due to its reliance on the constrained resources of mobile devices, artificial intelligence algorithms provide a significant issue in light of the growing need for computational power and energy usage. Large-scale user data collection and processing by AI apps raises privacy and security concerns around data leaks and unauthorized access. Insufficient integration and compatibility will make it more difficult for AI solutions to be effectively integrated across various mobile devices. It is challenging to guarantee justice and accountability in AI-powered mobile applications due to ethical concerns about impartial algorithms and transparent decision-making procedures. To optimize the advantages of mobile intelligence while safeguarding user security, privacy, and ethical norms, these problems must be resolved (Elahi et al., 2021).
