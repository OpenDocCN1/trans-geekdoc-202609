# The Role of Wireless Charging in Sustainability

Because wireless charging solves a number of the financial and environmental issues that come with using conventional charging techniques, it contributes significantly to sustainability. The following are a few ways that wireless charging supports sustainability:

+   - Energy Efficiency: Systems for wireless (Zhou et al., 2019) charging can be made to be extremely effective, reducing the amount of energy lost during charging. In order to promote sustainable behaviours and lower overall energy usage, this efficiency is essential.
+   - Reduced E-Waste: By eliminating the need for throwaway wires and connectors, wireless charging reduces the amount of electronic garbage (e-waste) that is produced.
+   - Increased Product Lifespan: Device failure can frequently be attributed to physical connector wear and tear, such as that seen in USB ports. By doing away with the necessity for physical connections, wireless charging may extend the life of electronics and lower the frequency of replacements.
+   - Clean Energy Integration: A more sustainable charging ecosystem is facilitated by wireless charging infrastructure that is fueled by clean, renewable energy sources.
+   - Flexibility and Convenience: There is greater freedom in where and how gadgets can be charged when using wireless charging.
