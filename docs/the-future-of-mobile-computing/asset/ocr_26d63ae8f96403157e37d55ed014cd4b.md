# *Personalization and AI*

Personalization is the process of adjusting goods, services, or experiences to each user’s or customer’s unique requirements, preferences, and capabilities. The goal of personalization is to boost user satisfaction and engagement by offering advice or data that is relevant and helpful to each unique user. It can be applied to a wide range of responsibilities, including distribution of material, marketing, e-commerce, personalization, and user interface. Because artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms and computational models enable the analysis of vast volumes of data and the prediction or decision-making of the unique characteristics of users, their application is crucial for adaptability. AI may learn from user interaction and get better over time to offer more precise services or recommendations.
