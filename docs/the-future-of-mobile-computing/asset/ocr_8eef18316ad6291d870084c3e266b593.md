One of the main traits of edge computing is the ability to significantly less latency. By the processing of the data closer to its origin that will get out of the edge, it will also minimizes the time taken for the data to traverse from the edge devices to the centralized data centers and also back. This feature will help us for the instrumental in the applications of requiring in the real-time or the near-real-time responses, such as IoT devices and, autonomous vehicles, and in the industrial automation systems. This will enable the enable the delivery of timely responses in the critical of enhancing user for the experience of the edge and enabling swift decision-making in various domains. The tasks of the network administrators will be made easy without traversing the entire Wide Area Networks using the mobility services. Real-time analytics and big data analytics can be executed with enhanced accuracy and scalability. The next important characteristic is provided through the support extended for mobility in edge computing. This characteristic is rendered through the LISP (Locator ID Separator) protocol which provides direct communication with mobile devices by implementing the distributed directory system. The mobility support is extended through the LISP by the process of decoupling the host and location identity. This characteristic is termed a key principle in edge computing. The property location-awareness is used to access the services from edge servers near the location of the users. The devices can be tracked by the users through technologies such as GPS or using wireless access points. This particular characteristic proves highly beneficial for applications like vehicular safety and disaster management that rely on edge-based solutions.

The proximity characteristic of edge computing improves the users experience by making the resources available for computation at their levels of proximity. Also, the users can make service decisions comfortably since the resources required for computation are available in the local vicinity. Moreover, this can help the service providers to analyze the behavior of mobile users by extracting their device information in order to improve the process of allocating resources and services to users.
