Seghrouchni, 2005). They’ve got a lot of opportunities and a lot of problems ahead of them. We’ll go over each of these two:

+   - Limitations of resources: when mobile devices are equipped with battery life and computing power, they have a tendency to be limited. Creating smart agents that can respond well to those constraints is one of the main challenges.
+   - Security and privacy concerns: when communications between IMAs and servers are sensitive, there is a concern of security and privacy. Strict encryption and the protection of user data must be ensured.
+   - In combination with the Mobile Ecosystem: A major challenge is to be able to integrate seamless with various Mobile Platforms, Operating Systems and Applications. Infrastructure managers need to be able to adapt to a number of settings, in order to provide appropriate and effective support.
+   - Context awareness: Effective interpretation and adaptation of IMAs is required for mobile users’ dynamic contexts. To understand user behaviour, preferences and environment is dependent upon improved contextual awareness.
+   - Upcoming prospects: The future of Intelligent Mobile Agents Systems looks promising, thanks to technology breakthroughs and an increasing focus on improving the user experience as a result of cell computing. Networks are going to be faster and more reliable as 5G technology continues to spread.

# Getting Past Obstacles and Limitations

In order to overcome the obstacles and limits of intelligent mobile agents, a planned and iterative strategy will be needed. Some of the most important tactics are as follows (Wang et al., 2017).

+   - Optimise methods: in order to ensure the efficient functioning of IMAs, optimize strategies so as to take full advantage of low device resources. To this end, it may be possible to control the consumption of energy, improve algorithms and reduce memory use.
