# Discussion and Future Directions:
Conclusion Drawn from the Study

Particularly in relation to mental health, anxiety, and depression, the research discussed in this chapter has shed important light on the field of computational emotional analysis:

+   - Improved Emotional knowledge: By combining EmoLex with sentiment analysis methods, it is now possible to gain a deeper knowledge of how people express and deal with their emotions in textual data. It has illuminated the affective aspects of sadness and anxiety.
+   - Real-Time Analysis: The Flask-based WebApp’s development has demonstrated the possibility of real-time emotional analysis. Through user interaction, users can receive real-time feedback on their emotional states, which can lead to early intervention.
+   - Privacy and Ethical Issues: When handling emotional content in digital settings, privacy and ethical issues have been emphasised by the research. To ensure the proper use of technology in mental health analysis, several factors are essential.
