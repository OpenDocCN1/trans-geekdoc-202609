**Figure 1.** Distinctions between AR And VR.

Here, we list the precise distinctions between AR and VR as shown in Figure 1:

+   - **Definition:** Augmented reality (AR) is the overlaying of digital data over the real environment to improve user experience by seamlessly integrating virtual and physical aspects. Conversely, virtual reality, or VR, obscures users’ physical surroundings and submerges them in a computer-generated reality, so eclipsing the real world entirely.
+   - **User Background:** With augmented reality (AR), users can benefit from digital augmentation while maintaining a strong sense of realism. Here, digital content is overlayed onto the existing environment to allow for the simultaneous manipulation of virtual and physical elements. Virtual reality, on the other hand, immerses users in a virtual environment that completely replaces the real world with a computer-generated setting in order to create a closed and immersive experience.
+   - **Hardware & Devices:** In order to access AR experiences, users commonly use tablets, smartphones, AR glasses, and heads-up displays, among other devices. Conversely, specialised gear, like virtual reality headsets, is typically needed for virtual reality, which gives a large viewing field and isolates users from the outside world.
