# Data and Model in Smart Cities

When it comes to producing information that can assist decision-making, smart cities need many data sources and models that can be relied upon. In situations when a large number of intelligent devices and sensors are involved, the difficulty of the situation increases significantly. In this part, five sample applications for smart cities are presented, together with the data involved, the models that correspond to them, and the computational needs that are necessary for them.

# Transport and Traffic Management

In terms of activities that are associated with urban life, transport is one of the most significant factors. Sensors put in cars that are used for traffic or monitors that are placed along roads are responsible for the generation and collection of traffic data. Data that document people’s routine mobility in cities are referred to as commuting data. The data collected from geo-tagged social networks includes postings (such as blogs and tweets) that are tagged with geoinformation and are collected from social networks. Road segments and junctions are the two types of data that are represented by road network data. Intelligent transportation systems make use of a variety of models, including agent-based traffic management models, cognitive rationality-based executive models, and mixed-ranked logit models.

# Smart City Technology

Components that are used in the development of smart cities include software solutions, user interfaces, communication networks, and devices that are linked to the internet of things (IoT). The Internet of Things, on the other hand, may be considered their principal source of dependence. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices, for example autos, sensors, or home appliances, that are able to communicate with one another and exchange information. Examples of these types of devices include connected devices. Sensors and gadgets connected to the Internet of Things are the ones responsible for gathering and transmitting data, which is after that saved either in the cloud or on other servers.
