# Edge Services and Applications

Edge as a Service (EaaS) is the term used to describe the on-demand cloud-based delivery of edge computing resources, capabilities, and services to users. Without having to maintain the underlying hardware and software components, it enables companies and developers to take advantage of edge computing infrastructure. The users can access edge computing resources remotely like cloud services. To bring computation closer to the data source or end users, the edge infrastructure is typically deployed across a distributed network of edge locations or devices. The benefits of edge as a service include on-demand resource provisioning, reduced latency, simplified development and deployment, cost efficiency, scalability and agility, edge analytics and AI, and enhanced security and privacy. Edge service providers include Cloud service providers, telecommunications firms, or providers of specialised edge computing platforms.
