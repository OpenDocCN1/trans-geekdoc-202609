# Ethical Implications of Machine Learning in Mobile Environments

Questions have arisen as to whether user data can be collected and used without explicit permission from apps based on machine learning. Apart from privacy, accountability, openness, prejudice and justice are all part of these issues.

+   - Privacy Concerns: Personal information, e.g., voice recordings, location, behaviour patterns, and biometrics, may be extracted from the user’s data by ML algorithms. When using these data to identify features, preferences, and habits, a user profile can be created. However, since users do not know how or to what extent their information is being collected, it may be considered a breach of privacy to gather information without their consent or without explicit privacy policies (Char et al., 2020).
