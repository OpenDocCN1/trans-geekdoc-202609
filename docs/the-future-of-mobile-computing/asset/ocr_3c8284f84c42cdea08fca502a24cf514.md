# Abstract

Edge Computing is termed as an important paradigm utilized to address the problems related to latency, bandwidth, and privacy. It is required to create a taxonomy that offers a thorough understanding of the various aspects and components of edge computing. This chapter focuses on the taxonomy of edge computing that captures its various aspects including deployment models, application domains, infrastructure, and services. The chapter also discusses the components such as edge servers, edge networks, edge analytics, and edge devices. The range of edge computing-enabled applications discussed in the chapter include as Internet of Things, autonomous driving, augmented reality, video surveillance, and more.
