# Early Intervention and Student Support

Remote learning platforms could incorporate early intervention mechanisms that identify students who are struggling or falling behind in real-time. Through data analysis, educators can identify signs of difficulty and provide timely interventions, whether through additional resources or targeted exercises.

# Potential for Hybrid Learning Environments

The future might see the merging of traditional classroom settings and remote learning to create hybrid learning environments. This allows for a flexible approach where students can attend some classes physically and engage in others remotely.

# Incorporating Real-time Feedback Mechanism

As technology advances, the ability to provide instant, detailed feedback to students will become more refined (Pereira, A. et al., 2020).

# Conclusion

Through rigorous research and testing, it was found that the student multimodal online monitoring system effectively tracks and analyses various aspects of students’ online learning experiences. The system’s integration of multiple data sources, such as video feeds, screen activity, and engagement metrics, provides a comprehensive view of student engagement and performance. This holistic approach enables educators to identify patterns, assess the effectiveness of instructional strategies, and offer timely interventions to enhance student learning outcomes.
