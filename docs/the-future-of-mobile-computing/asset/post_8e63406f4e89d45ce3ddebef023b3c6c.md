### Abstract

When we talk about mobile computing, we are referring to the collection of information technology systems, products, services, and operational strategies and processes that make it possible for end users to access computation, information. The word “mobile” may also be used to refer to access in a fixed location via the use of equipment that users are able to move about as required but that stays stationary while it is performing its function. It is common practice to refer to this method of operation as nomadic computing. Currently, mobile computer technology can be found almost anywhere. A wide variety of specialized vertical markets, as well as commercial and consumer markets, industrial and entertainment sectors, and other industries, may all benefit from its uses. Desktop computers provide a greater number of hardware configuration options and a higher level of processing capability. Despite this, the great majority of end consumers gravitate toward mobile devices as their preferred option. One of the most major advantages of mobile computing is its convenience, since it gives users the ability to access information and computational resources whenever and wherever they want.

### Introduction

Mobile computing systems are typically comprised of a mobile device, which may be a laptop computer, tablet, or smartphone, in addition to a wireless network connection that is based on Wi-Fi or cellular wireless technologies, such as 5G. The majority of the time, a mobile computing system is comprised of these two components. Each and every one of these components is essential for the system to operate in an appropriate manner. It is not necessary to be connected to a network in order to access the data that is stored locally on mobile devices since these devices are equipped with the potential to save data locally.

In most cases, mobile computers are able to provide access to both wireless and wired technologies. As a result of the collaborative nature of work in the current day, it is very vital to have access to shared network resources. These resources may include mobile cloud-based services. Integrated rechargeable batteries are what provide power to mobile devices. These batteries are embedded into the gadget itself. The majority of these batteries are able to function on an alternating current (AC) power source when they are used from a fixed location.

There is a wide variety of mobile computing devices available for use in vertical and specialized applications, in addition to laptops, tablets, and smartphones. A variety of applications, including medical, surveillance, security, telemetry, and control, are included in this category. The selection of the device is determined by the application. As an example, laptops are particularly well-suited for the production of material, whilst tablets are often selected for the consumption of information. The screens on smartphones are quite tiny, and they use screen-based keyboards. Smartphones are able to perform the functions of pocket-sized computers and communication devices.
