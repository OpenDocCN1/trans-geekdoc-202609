+   - Challenge: Inadequate protection of data during storage, transmission, or processing, leading to unintentional exposure or leakage of sensitive information.
+   - Impact: Exposure of personal or confidential data can result in privacy violations and compromise the security of individuals and organizations.

# Malicious Apps:

+   - Challenge: The presence of malicious applications that may be downloaded or installed, posing threats such as data theft, unauthorized access, or the exploitation of device vulnerabilities.
+   - Impact: Malicious apps can compromise the security and functionality of mobile devices, leading to various forms of cyberattacks.

# Network Security:

+   - Challenge: Hazards related to man-in-the-middle attacks, eavesdropping on mobile communications, and unsecured Wi-Fi networks.
+   - Impact: Unauthorized interception of data during transmission can result in data compromise, financial loss, or exposure to sensitive information.

# Device Loss and Theft:

+   - Challenge: The risk of sensitive data exposure and unauthorized access arises from the possible loss or theft of mobile devices.
+   - Impact: Loss or theft can result in data breaches, identity theft, and potential misuse of personal or corporate information.

# Insufficient Encryption:

+   - Challenge: Inadequate use of encryption to protect data stored on the device, leaving it vulnerable to unauthorized access.
+   - Impact: Without encryption, sensitive data becomes susceptible to theft or unauthorized viewing.
