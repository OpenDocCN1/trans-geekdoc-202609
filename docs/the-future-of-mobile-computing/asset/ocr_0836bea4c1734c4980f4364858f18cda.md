# What Is Natural Language Processing

Natural language processing, or NLP, is the use of algorithms to determine and extract rules from natural languages such as unintelligible language data is converted into a form that computers can understand When the text has been provided, the pc will utilize algorithms to extract which means related to every sentence and gather the crucial information from them.
