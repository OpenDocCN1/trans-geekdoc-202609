## Preface

The book “The Future of Mobile Computing,” comprises 15 chapters that collectively illuminate the evolving landscape of mobile technology. Chapter 1, delves into a Systematic Literature Review of Edge Computing, and lays the groundwork for understanding the cutting-edge concepts shaping the future. Chapter 2 introduces a Taxonomy of Edge Computing, providing readers with a structured framework to navigate this transformative field. Chapter 3, deals with Edge Computing’s impact on enterprises and personal tech applications. Chapter 4 explores Smart Agriculture, mobile solutions for sustainable farming practices and contributing to a greener future. Shifting the focus to hardware and design trends, Chapter 5 focuses on Emerging Trends that will shape the devices of tomorrow. Chapter 6 brings the forefront to the symbiotic connection between Artificial Intelligence and Mobile Computing, highlighting how AI significantly transforms and enhances the capabilities of mobile applications. Chapters 7 and 8 delve into Intelligent Mobile Assistants and Edge Computing Technology, respectively, exploring the power of machine learning and navigating opportunities, issues, and future trends in edge computing. Chapter 9 focuses on Sustainable Mobile Technologies, unveiling eco-friendly innovations for a more environmentally conscious mobile future. Chapter 10 examines The Application of Mobile Computing in a Smart City, where technology converges with urban planning. Privacy and security concerns are addressed in Chapter 11, offering strategies for safeguarding user data in the realm of mobile computing. Chapter 12 explores augmented and virtual reality, shaping perspectives in modern mobile computing environments. Chapters 13 and 14 delve into innovative approaches at the intersection of technology and well-being, exploring AI-embedded blockchain security for smart student monitoring and enhanced computational analysis of anxiety and sadness in the context of mobile computing and mental health.
