# The Role of Robotics in Manufacturing

Robotics is a key component of the ever-changing industrial industry, transforming production environments and bringing previously unheard-of levels of accuracy and efficiency. An overview of industrial robotics demonstrates a wide range of robot types, such as manipulators and mobile robots, each designed to perform certain tasks in a production setting. Following the development of industrial robotics over time reveals a story of technological innovation, from the first robotic arms to the present day, which is distinguished by highly developed mobility, adaptability, and control systems. One of the most revolutionary uses is seen in automated assembly lines, where robots are essential to process simplification. These mechanical counterparts do repeated activities with diligence and precision, reducing errors and costs while increasing efficiency.

The introduction of Collaborative Robots, or Cobots, which mark a paradigm change in human-robot collaboration, is a noteworthy advancement within this setting. These robots are made to cooperate with human operators in jobs requiring dexterity comparable to that of a human. Because Cobots are cooperative, people and machines may work together harmoniously to maximise strengths and achieve increased flexibility and efficiency in the industry (Huang et al., 2020).
