Synchronised data can be sent to central systems for additional analysis or historical records after connectivity has been restored.

# Conclusion

Edge computing is a game-changing technology that has enormous advantages in a number of industries, to sum up. It revolutionises industrial automation by allowing for real-time data processing, localised control, and increased operational effectiveness. With regard to cybersecurity, edge computing offers a distributed and pro-active strategy to protect critical data and reduce online threats. Real-time analysis and individualised care are made possible via remote patient monitoring, which improves patient outcomes and lowers costs. Edge computing also enhances irrigation water management, supporting resource conservation and sustainable practises. Overall, edge computing improves efficiency, security, and response times across a variety of industries, spurring innovation and meeting changing needs in our interconnected world. Edge computing also offers scalability and flexibility, allowing for seamless system expansion and reconfiguration. By reducing infrastructural and data transport dependencies, it also helps with cost optimization. Edge computing is paving the way for cutting-edge technology, wise decision-making, and a more connected and effective future as it is incorporated into a variety of industries.
