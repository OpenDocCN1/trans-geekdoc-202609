# Precision Farming Technologies

Precision farming makes use of a wide range of cutting-edge instruments and technologies to track many factors and gather data about crop development (such as pH, soil moisture, etc.). It is called “precision” because its main goal is to provide the appropriate intervention (such as watering crops) at the appropriate time and location while addressing the unique needs of each crop and each piece of land with the highest degree of precision (Blasch et al., 2021) possible.

# GPS Technology in Agriculture

GPS technology has completely transformed the agricultural landscape, revolutionizing traditional farming practices. One of its key applications empowers farmers with accurate positioning information for their tractors, planters, and other machinery. This allows for optimal allocation of resources, such as water, fertilizers, and pesticides, by targeting specific areas in need. Moreover, the integration of GPS technology in Variable Rate Technology (VRT) has further enhanced efficiency in farming.

+   - Auto-Steer Systems: Tractors and other farm equipment can precisely follow predefined routes thanks to Auto-Steer Systems, which use GPS technology.
+   - Soil Head Management: GPS devices reduce crop damage and soil erosion by better monitoring variations in soil head and revolving machinery at the proper angles and locations.
+   - Yield monitoring: During harvest, crop rotation in the field is tracked and observed using GPS. In the future, these data will be useful for making well-informed decisions and assessing the effectiveness of implementation activities.
+   - Tracking equipment: GPS enhances fleet management by assisting farmers in tracking the location and movement of farm equipment.
