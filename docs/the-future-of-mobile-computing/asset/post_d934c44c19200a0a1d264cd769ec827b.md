## Data and Model in Smart Cities

When it comes to producing information that can assist decision-making, smart cities need many data sources and models that can be relied upon. In situations when a large number of intelligent devices and sensors are involved, the difficulty of the situation increases significantly. In this part, five sample applications for smart cities are presented, together with the data involved, the models that correspond to them, and the computational needs that are necessary for them.

### Transport and Traffic Management

In terms of activities that are associated with urban life, transport is one of the most significant factors. Sensors put in cars that are used for traffic or monitors that are placed along roads are responsible for the generation and collection of traffic data. Data that document people’s routine mobility in cities are referred to as commuting data. The data collected from geo-tagged social networks includes postings (such as blogs and tweets) that are tagged with geoinformation and are collected from social networks. Road segments and junctions are the two types of data that are represented by road network data. Intelligent transportation systems make use of a variety of models, including agent-based traffic management models, cognitive rationality-based executive models, and mixed-ranked logit models.

## Smart City Technology

Components that are used in the development of smart cities include software solutions, user interfaces, communication networks, and devices that are linked to the internet of things (IoT). The Internet of Things, on the other hand, may be considered their principal source of dependence. The Internet of Things (IoT) is a network of interconnected devices, for example autos, sensors, or home appliances, that are able to communicate with one another and exchange information. Examples of these types of devices include connected devices. Sensors and gadgets connected to the Internet of Things are the ones responsible for gathering and transmitting data, which is afterwards saved either in the cloud or on other servers. The convergence of the physical and digital aspects of the city is made possible by the connectivity of these devices and the use of data analytics. This results in an improvement in the efficiency of both the public and commercial sectors, which in turn enables economic gains and improves the lives of citizens.

Some Internet of Things devices have processing capabilities that are referred to as edge computing. Through the use of edge computing, it is possible to guarantee that the communication network is only exposed to the information that is the most significant and relevant.

Within a computer system, the protection, monitoring, and control of network traffic are all essential functions that need the implementation of a firewall security system. In order to guarantee the safety of the data that is continuously sent inside a smart city network, firewalls are installed to prevent anyone from getting into the Internet of Things network or the data of the city without permission.

Other smart city technologies include:

- APIs, which stand for application programming interfaces
- AI stands for artificial intelligence.
- cloud computing
- control panels
- machine learning (ML)
- M2M stands for machine to machine.
- mesh network

### Devices and Sensors

Data is collected from physical assets, machinery, and equipment via a broad variety of devices and sensors that are a part of the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) networks. A wide variety of sensors and devices, such as temperature sensors, pressure sensors, motion sensors, actuators, and others, may be included in this category.

### Edge Computing

When it comes to processing and analysing the data at the network’s edge, which is located closer to the user or to the source, edge computing is an essential component of an Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) system. By reducing latency, improving real-time decision-making, and minimising data transfer to the cloud, edge computing helps to enhance the overall experience.

## Future Urban Computing for Smart Cities

At this point, with increased connectivity to the cloud, the network’s periphery is increasingly serving as a mobile computing system. This is as a result of the fast development of 5G networks as well as the increased integration with cloud computing. There have been a number of instances in which the phrase “mobile cloud computing” has been used in the field of mobile computing. The whole computer network will be pushed to a new decentralised computing stage, and the process of developing smart cities will be hastened as a consequence of the interaction between how mobile computing and cloud computing can be used to handle data on demand. Both of these developments will occur simultaneously.

As the number of mobile devices continues to rise, it will become more obvious that there is a need for interaction with neighbouring edge applications. When combined with enhanced dispensation, computing, and power capacities, as well as the decentralised nature of mobile computing, edge computing is anticipated to deliver meaningfully enhanced throughput, enhanced performance, and real-time responses. This is accomplished by bringing computing and data closer to the user, as well as by tailoring the dispensation requirements of each individual user. Computing at the edge and computing on mobile devices are both capable of managing localised data for the purpose of facilitating rapid response for a certain range of area sizes. Nevertheless, centralised cloud computing is also being driven by the growing amount of data generated in metropolitan areas and the geo-analysis of data across cities.

For the purpose of detecting, recording, sharing, and analysing a wide range of human-related activities, a number of different devices will link to the cloud-based Internet inside the smart city. Computing on the cloud, when combined with the assistance of algorithms that are powered by artificial intelligence, will provide businesses, governments, and individual citizens with more intelligent solutions.
