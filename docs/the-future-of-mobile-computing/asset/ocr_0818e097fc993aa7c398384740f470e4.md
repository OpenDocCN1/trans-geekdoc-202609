+   - Personalized Experiences: IoT integration enables the creation of personalized reports tailor-made to character preferences. By analysing person patterns and records accumulated from sensors, clever homes can adapt to occupants’ behaviour, adjusting settings robotically for comfort, electricity performance, and comfort. Energy efficiency and cost One of the benefits of Integrated Internet of Things in a smart home is to improve electricity performance. Smart thermostats, for example, are capable of analysing the behaviour of occupants and changing their temperature accordingly so that they can save money in the long run by using less electricity (Baker et al., 2017).
