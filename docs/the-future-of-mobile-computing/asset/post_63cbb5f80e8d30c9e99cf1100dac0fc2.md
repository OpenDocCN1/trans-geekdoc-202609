## Proposed Solution for Monitoring System

In the Multi-model Student Online Monitoring System (Monitoring System), we envision an integration of various monitoring techniques:

- **Recording System:** Utilizing passive video and audio recording to capture the entire online learning session for later review, promoting transparency and accountability.
- **AI Proctoring System:** Implementing AI-driven tools to monitor for suspicious behaviors, plagiarism, and engagement levels, offering real-time alerts and data-driven insights.
- **Human Proctoring System:** Combining AI with human proctors for a balanced approach, allowing for immediate intervention in cases where AI alone might lack context or judgment.

## Data Collection Techniques

- **Real Time Video Analysis:** This component captures students’ facial expressions, gaze direction, and posture during online sessions. Computer vision algorithms analyze these visual cues to infer attention levels, emotions, and engagement.
- **Keystroke Dynamics Component:** Monitoring students’ typing patterns, this component detects variations in typing speed, keystroke intervals, and error rates. It provides insights into students’ cognitive load and potential distractions.
- **Mouse Movement Analysis Component:** Tracking mouse movement and click patterns, this component gauges students’ interactions with course materials and platforms. Anomalies in movement may indicate disengagement or confusion.
- **Sentiment Analysis Component:** Utilizing natural language processing, this component analyses written communication (chat messages, forum posts) for sentiment and emotional tone. It helps identify students’ emotional states and overall satisfaction (Kosheleva et al., 2018).

## Implications and Future Directions
