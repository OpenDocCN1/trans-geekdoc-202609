When it comes to the creation of smart cities, there are a number of information and communications technologies (ICT) that are absolutely necessary.

According to Cisco (2017), the number of devices that are linked to the Internet will approach or perhaps surpass 50 billion by the year 2020. The transmission system of the smart city is defined as the communication network, which is responsible for delivering data from sensors to computer platforms. Networks that are dependable, scalable, and possess high speeds, including both wired and wireless networks, provide the essential infrastructure for the transfer of such data. In order to discover city-related events that need processing and action, computing platforms provide assistance for the administration and analysis of relevant city data in a larger context so that they may be identified.
