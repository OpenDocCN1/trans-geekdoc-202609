The transformative impact of mobile computing is evident across different aspects of life. In the business realm, it facilitates remote work, improves communication, and provides efficient access to crucial information. On a social level, it fosters global connections and real-time interactions, while the educational landscape benefits from e-learning platforms and educational apps. Moreover, the scope of mobile computing continues to broaden with the introduction of popular technology such as Actual presence (AP) and Supplementary Presences (SP) introducing new dimensions to buyers’ experiences. Despite its numerous advantages, challenges in terms of security, privacy, and the need for efficient power management remain key focal points in the ongoing evolution of mobile computing. As technology advances, the trajectory of mobile computing is poised for growth, influencing and reshaping how individuals, businesses, and societies engage with the digital world. The dynamic and ever-evolving landscape of mobile computing reflects an ongoing redefinition of how we connect and interact in our increasingly mobile-centric world (Mollah M et al., 2017; Ghadi S et al., 2021; Imielinski T and Korth H, 1996).

# Evolution of Mobile Devices

The evolution of mobile devices traces a transformative journey through technological leaps and consumer-driven innovation, revolutionizing how we communicate, work, and engage with information. It began with the advent of the first commercial mobile phone, the Motorola DynaTAC 8000X, in 1983. These devices, albeit bulky and limited to voice calls, marked the inception of mobile communication. They swiftly evolved with the introduction of digital cellular networks, paving the way for better call quality and basic text messaging. The Nokia Communicator and BlackBerry devices of the late 1990s and early 2000s heralded a new era, offering email access and rudimentary web browsing, albeit on smaller screens and with physical keyboards. However, the mobile landscape experienced a seismic shift with the debut of the iPhone in 2007. Apple’s innovative touchscreen interface, coupled with the App Store, transformed the concept of mobile devices.
