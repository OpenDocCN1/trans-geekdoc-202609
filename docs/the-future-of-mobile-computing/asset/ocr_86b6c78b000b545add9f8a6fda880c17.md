**Figure 2.** Basic components of the A-flowchart fingerprint identification system.

+   - **User Convenience: Quick and Simple Unlocking**, to unlock a smartphone, users simply need to touch the designated area of the screen with their registered finger. This method is often faster and more convenient than entering a passcode while the device is in use.
+   - **Enhanced Security Protocols: Cutting-Edge Sensor Technology**, In-display fingerprint scanners use sophisticated sensor technology, such as optical or ultrasonic sensors, to capture a detailed image of the user’s fingerprint. This enhances the accuracy and security of the authentication process.
+   - **Counteracting Spoofing: Distinguishing Properties**, Anti-spoofing measures are implemented by manufacturers to prevent unauthorized access through the use of fake fingerprints or other biometric spoofing methods. One of these safety measures is the use of advanced algorithms to distinguish between authentic and fraudulent fingerprints.
+   - **Biometric Data Protection: Secure Enclave**, Fingerprint data is typically kept in a secure enclave within the device to add an extra layer of protection. This ensures that the biometric data cannot be easily obtained by malicious actors.
+   - **Dual Authentication Techniques: Together with Other Security Features**, In-display fingerprint scanners are often used in conjunction with other security features like as facial recognition or traditional PIN/password verification, to allow users to choose the most convenient method.
