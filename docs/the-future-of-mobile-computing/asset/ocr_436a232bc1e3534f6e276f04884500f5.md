# Introduction

The way we live our daily lives has been completely transformed by modern mobile technologies, which have brought about revolutionary changes in communication, entertainment and information access. Undoubtedly, the use of smartphones, tablets and other mobile devices has improved connectivity and convenience as well as brought about other benefits. Despite its significant contributions to global connectivity, the mobile industry is increasingly being criticized for its environmental impacts. Mobile technologies are important in promoting sustainable practices and minimizing possible environmental damage.
