# Blockchain, Data Meshes, and Their Effect on Innovation

# Concept of Data Mesh

A fundamental shift in how businesses handle data architecture is represented by Data Mesh. It promotes data as a product and calls for a decentralised, domain-oriented approach to data ownership. The problems with conventional centralised data structures are intended to be addressed by this decentralised approach (Wang et al., 2019).

The decentralised and secure distributed ledger technology known as blockchain guarantees data traceability, immutability, and transparency. Blockchain can revolutionise manufacturing by improving traceability, streamlining supply chains, and enabling smart contracts, all of which can disrupt established corporate procedures.

+   - Integration of Blockchain and Data Mesh: A robust and scalable data infrastructure is produced by combining the concepts of Data Mesh with Blockchain technology. Through the promotion of decentralised ownership and the use of Blockchain’s security characteristics, entities can guarantee data integrity, transparency, and accessibility throughout their communities.
+   - Impact on Collaboration and Innovation: Data Mesh and Blockchain work together to foster a more inventive and cooperative data ecosystem. They enable organisations to get important insights, make data-driven choices, and encourage innovation in a reliable and decentralised environment by facilitating safe and transparent data sharing.
