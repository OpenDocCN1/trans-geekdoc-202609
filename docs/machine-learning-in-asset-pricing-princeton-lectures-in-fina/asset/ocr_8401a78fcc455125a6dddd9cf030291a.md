# # Alibaba Group Announces March Quarter and Fiscal Year 2024 Results

HANGZHOU, China, May 14, 2024 — Alibaba Group Holding Limited (NYSE: BABA / HKEX: 9988) announced its financial results for the quarter and fiscal year ended March 31, 2024.

+   - Revenue was RMB 221,874 million, an increase of 7% year-over-year.
+   - Income from operations was RMB 14,809 million, a decrease of 3% year-over-year.
+   - Net income was RMB 971 million, a decrease of 96% year-over-year.
+   - Adjusted net income was RMB 24,418 million, a decrease of 11% year-over-year.

> > “Our results demonstrate that our strategic focus is delivering value. We are pleased with the progress of our businesses across the board.”
