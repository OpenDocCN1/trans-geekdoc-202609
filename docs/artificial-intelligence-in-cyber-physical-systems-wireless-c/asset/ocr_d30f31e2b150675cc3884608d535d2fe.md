# 2.1 INTRODUCTION

Wireless communications along with Internet of Things (IoT) are used in various aspects of our life, such as in the field of medicine, agriculture, entertainment, deep learning technologies to detect fatal conditions [1], Artificial Intelligence (AI) methods to provide smart technological systems [2] and in intelligent home devices. Body sensor network (BSN) is capable of monitoring health-related data of patients without requiring them to visit a medical expert [3].
