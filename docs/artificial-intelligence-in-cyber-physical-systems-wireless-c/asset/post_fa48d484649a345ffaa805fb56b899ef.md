Slipachuk et al. developed and introduced the cyber model of critical infrastructure network protection management using the integrated system of the national cybersecurity sector management in Ukraine. The functional components of the cybersecurity system of critical infrastructure objects have been described. The practical value of the integrated management system for dealing with the cybersecurity of critical infrastructure objects has been validated [5].

According to Wu et al., cybersecurity is one of the major threats for all kinds of cyber-physical systems (CPS). To assess the cybersecurity risk of CPS, a quantitative hierarchical risk assessment model consisting of attack severity, attack success probability, and attack outcome is proposed, which can evaluate the risk caused by an ongoing attack at host level and system level. Then, the definitions and estimation methods for the three indicators are discussed in detail. Finally, this chapter gives the risk assessment algorithm that describes the steps of implementation. Numerical simulation shows that the model can respond to the attack opportunity and obtain the system security risk change curve, so that it can help the user’s response to the risk to be optimal. The risk change curve can also be used to predict the risk for the future time [6].

In their paper, Pavlenko and Zeghdha proposed another approach to security assessment for cyber-physical systems. Existing security techniques applicable to information systems are insufficient for cyber-physical systems, which comprise cyber and physical components. The authors detail requirements for security assessment considering key characteristics of cyber-physical systems. Analysis of scientific research devoted to the development of a security assessment for cyber-physical systems has shown their inconsistency with the stated requirements. The authors propose a new assessment of a cyber-physical system’s security as cyber sustainability, which ensures maintenance of the system in a stable and consistent state under destructive impact. The authors also propose a computational measure for assessing cyber sustainability, based on self-similarity estimation. As a numerical measure, the Hurst exponent was chosen. Experimental tests demonstrated the effectiveness of the proposed approach and the high sensitivity of the Hurst exponent to destructive impact on cyber-physical systems [7].

Barrère et al. state that monitoring systems are essential to understand and control the behavior of systems and networks. Cyber-physical systems are especially fragile in some respects because they involve real-time constraints and physical phenomena that are not usually considered in conventional IT systems. Therefore, there is a need for publicly available monitoring tools able to take these aspects into account. In this banner/demo, we present our initiative, called CPS-MT, towards an adaptable, real-time CPS monitoring tool, with a specific focus on security research. We first present its architecture and main components, followed by a MiniCPS-based case study. We also describe a performance analysis and preliminary results. During the demo, we will discuss CPS-MT’s capabilities and limitations for security applications [8].

Teoh et al. proposed probabilistic models that can be utilized for forecasting time series data. They have seen success in different domains such as finance, bioinformatics, healthcare, agriculture, and artificial intelligence. However, the use of HMM in cyber security found to date is limited. We believe that the properties of HMM being predictive, probabilistic, and its ability to model distinct naturally occurring states form a good basis to model cybersecurity data. Henceforth, it is the motivation of this work to provide the initial results of our attempts to predict security attacks using HMM. A large network dataset representing cybersecurity attacks has been used in this work to develop an expert system. The features of attacker IP addresses can be extracted from our integrated datasets to create statistical information. The cybersecurity expert gives the weight of each feature and forms a scoring system by annotating the log history. We applied HMM to detect a cybersecurity attack, uncertain and no attack, by first breaking the data into three clusters using fuzzy k-means, then manually labeling a small dataset (expert intuition), and finally using the HMM state-based approach. Thus, our results are highly encouraging compared with finding anomalies in a cybersecurity log, which usually results in producing large numbers of false positives [9].

Chu et al. proposed a security assessment scheme for dependent cyber-physical power systems. The focus of the scheme is to evaluate the impact of node failures in the communication network on the overall power system. A contingency set is formed by failures of each node in the communication network, which is ranked by node importance. Two mechanisms to trigger cascading failures of the power network are considered, including redirecting of power flow and hidden failures of protection following the initial random event. After the contingency set is enumerated, the N-1 security assessment for the dependent cyber-physical power system is completed. The impact of each contingency is measured by the subsequent load loss of the power system. Simulation results of the New England 10-machine 39-bus test system demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed scheme [10].

As per Elfar et al., CPSs are generally supervisory control systems where a human-on-the-loop (HOL) manages one or more autonomous systems, while onboard autonomy permits the operators to intermittently attend to the system and other tasks. Therefore, it is critical that the design of any security-aware CPS considers the effect of human interaction with the system on security. However, there has been almost no work on the design of human-CPS that promotes human situational awareness for enhanced system performance, especially in terms of cyber-physical security and real-time protection against attacks. One of the main barriers to the rapid advancement of this field is the shortage of testbeds for evaluating security-aware human-CPS interaction.
