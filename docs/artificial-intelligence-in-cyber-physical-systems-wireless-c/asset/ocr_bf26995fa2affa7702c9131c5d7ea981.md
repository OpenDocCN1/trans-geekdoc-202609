The authors [29] proposed a density ratio estimation (DRE)-based anomaly detection method for AC microgrid. The authors [42] proposed a hybrid jamming attack model. To inform the administration of threats, the suggested detector uses an SVM and k-means–based clustering algorithm, enabling a classification of distinct forms of network attacks [30]. To detect correlated network attacks, in [45], the authors proposed combining learning approaches using a forward neural network (FNN) model and an LSTM model. In [46], a CNN-based network attack detector for supervisory-control and data-acquisition (SCADA) networks was proposed. The authors [31] suggested single-class SVM to secure against DoS assaults on SG-SCADA systems. The authors [32] proposed a lightweight ML-based software anomalies analyzer for an embedded system in a camera application.
