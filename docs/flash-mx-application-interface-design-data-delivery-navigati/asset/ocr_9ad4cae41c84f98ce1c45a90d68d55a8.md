While we are in our video movie clip there are a few more things we should do. A rectangular movie can look a bit harsh, and there is no reason for it to remain like this. Add two new layers above our video layer. Call the top one frame, and the other mask.

Select the fill and cut it, select frame 1 in the mask layer and use the paste in place option to position it back where it was but a layer further down. This layer should then be turned into a mask and the video layer into a masked layer.

![](bbox=[0.175, 0.216, 0.356, 0.363])

![](bbox=[0.603, 0.196, 0.798, 0.316])

Using the rectangle tool, with a corner radius setting of 15 points and a line thickness of 4, draw a box to match the size of the video clip. Don't worry about the fill of the box obliterating the video as we are going to use the fill as a mask.

Masking video content in Flash can be a very bad idea, as it is often an extravagant waste of bandwidth, but we are being careful here. We are only clipping the edge of our video, and as there is little movement around the edges of the clip, Flash is not having to process that much video that won't be seen.
