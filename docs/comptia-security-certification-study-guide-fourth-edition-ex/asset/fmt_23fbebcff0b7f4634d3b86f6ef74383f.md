##### Introduction to Risk Analysis

![Image](boxsq.jpg) Risk analysis is the process of identifying the different threats against your company assets and managing those threats.

![Image](boxsq.jpg) Ensure that you follow a process when performing risk analysis. The process should involve identifying the assets, spotting threats against the assets, prioritizing the threats based on probability and impact, identifying the mitigation technique, and finally assessing residual risks.

![Image](boxsq.jpg) The impact of the threat determines the value of the loss from the threat.

![Image](boxsq.jpg) You typically implement a mitigation technique, which involves using a security control to protect the asset from the threat.

##### Risk Assessment Types

![Image](boxsq.jpg) The two main types of risk analysis are qualitative and quantitative risk analysis.

![Image](boxsq.jpg) Qualitative risk analysis uses values assigned to the probability of the threat and the impact of the threat.

![Image](boxsq.jpg) Quantitative analysis calculates the financial loss in dollars and cents by using formulas to calculate the annual loss expectancy (`ALE`).

![Image](boxsq.jpg) Before you can calculate the `ALE`, you must first calculate the single loss expectancy (`SLE`), which is the value of the asset multiplied by the exposure factor (`SLE = value × EF`).

![Image](boxsq.jpg) The `ALE` is the single loss expectancy multiplied by the annual rate of occurrence (`ALE = SLE × ARO`).

##### Risk Mitigation Strategies

![Image](boxsq.jpg) Once you have identified the threats and prioritized them, you then decide how to manage those risks.

![Image](boxsq.jpg) Common methods of managing risk include mitigating the risk by implementing a security control, transferring the risk by getting an insurance policy, and accepting the risk and doing nothing.

![Image](boxsq.jpg) You can help mitigate risk by doing things such as performing regular security audits, configuring security controls, and ensuring that policies and procedures are being followed.
