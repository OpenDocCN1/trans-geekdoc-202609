### Identifying Physical Threats

### 4.

![Image](boxx.jpg) **A, C,** and **D** are incorrect. Although your sales manager may end up requesting a replacement device from management, the first thing that she should do is erase the data on the device by remotely wiping it or asking an administrator to remotely wipe it.
