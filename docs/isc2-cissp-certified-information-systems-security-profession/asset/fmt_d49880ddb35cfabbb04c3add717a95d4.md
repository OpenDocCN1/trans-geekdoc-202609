![images](images/clogo.jpg) The world of compliance is a legal and regulatory jungle for information technology (IT) and cybersecurity professionals. National, state, and local governments have all passed overlapping laws regulating different components of cybersecurity in a patchwork manner. This leads to an incredibly confusing landscape for security professionals who must reconcile the laws of multiple jurisdictions. Things become even more complicated for multinational companies, which must navigate the variations between international law as well.
