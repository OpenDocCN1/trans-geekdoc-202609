In short, deep learning is a subset of machine learning that the way humans think to a computer. It allows a computer to learn on its own, and is based on a high-level abstraction that represents the human brain with artificial neural networks. Neurons, nerve cells in the brain, are unique cells that carry electrical signals. The brain is a collection of neurons, with about 100 billion cells and 100 trillion connections. These neurons are connected to each other, and the result- networks are called neural networks, while artificially created ones are called artificial neural networks.
