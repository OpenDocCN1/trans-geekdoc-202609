# 2.2. Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-9)

The Patient Health Questionnaire (PHQ-9) was used to collect depression screening among the participants. The instrument consists of nine items with multiple-choice format. Each item is rated on a four-point scale, ranging from 0 (Not at all) to 3 (Nearly every day). The PHQ-9 score was calculated by summing the scores from 0 to 27 to determine the severity of depression. The score was categorized as follows: mild, moderate, moderately severe, and severe.

# 2.2.3. Generalized Anxiety Disorder (GAD-7)

A screening tool and scale used for generalized anxiety disorder among the participants. The instrument consists of seven items, each having points ranging from 0 “not at all” to 3 “nearly every day”. The GAD-7 score was ranging from 0 to 21. According to Osman-Samani et al. (2018), the scores were interpreted as follows: minimal anxiety (0-4), mild anxiety (5-9), moderate anxiety (10-14), and severe anxiety (15-21). The study yielded a satisfactory reliability, with a Cronbach’s alpha value of 0.851.

# 2.2.1. Independent variables

The variables explored in the study included: marital status, family income, residence, physical activity, smoking habits, social quality, relationship with partner, and depression. The assessment of physical activity level was conducted utilizing the Global Physical Activity Questionnaire (GPAQ), which was employed in prior research endeavors (Ranasinghe et al., 2013).

The Suicidal Behaviour Questionnaire (SBQ-R) was used in our study to assess suicidal ideation among the participants. The SBQ-R consists of four items, each having a distinct dimension. The measurement of lifetime suicidal ideation or attempt was conducted using a scale where participants reported the frequency of the suicidal ideation over the past two months. The study evaluated each using a cut-off score (>7) to identify the presence of suicidal ideation among the participants. The Cronbach’s alpha score was 0.78, which could be considered acceptable.
