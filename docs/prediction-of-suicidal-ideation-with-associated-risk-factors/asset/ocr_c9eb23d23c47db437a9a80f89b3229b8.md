# 5. Conclusion

Based on the findings of this study, it was reported that a significant proportion of university students located in the southern region of Bangladesh have 19.9% suicidal ideation. The findings indicate a significant association between the duration of students' mental health concerns, specifically depression, pressure status, and suicidal ideation. The XGBoost model demonstrated that depression and pressure are the primary factors, while gender was the secondary factor. The development and execution of campaigns and counseling initiatives are aimed at improving mental health and preventing suicidal ideation and behavior. To mitigate the crisis of suicide among university students, all pertinent stakeholders, including academia, government bodies, and non-governmental organizations, should devise and implement effective strategies. The model can identify patients' medical history to detect those who may be at a higher risk of suicidal ideation. This approach could encourage regular sleep patterns, addressing depression and avoiding potential suicide attempts.
