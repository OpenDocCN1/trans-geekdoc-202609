**Know to specify an encryption standard for data at rest and in motion.** To keep your data safe in the cloud, you need to consider possible states where your data may occur and what controls are available in those states. Data in transit and at rest is encrypted in Azure. A double encryption system uses two or more independent layers of encryption to protect against compromises of any of the layers. When data is encrypted using two layers, it mitigates threats that come with it. Various encryption models are supported by Azure, including server-side encryption using service-managed keys, key management in Key Vault, or key management on customer-controlled hardware. Keys can be managed and stored securely on-premises or at another secure location using client-side encryption.
