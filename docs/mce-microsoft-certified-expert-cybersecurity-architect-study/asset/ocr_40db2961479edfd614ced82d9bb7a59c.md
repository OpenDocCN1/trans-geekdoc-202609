**Azure Identity Protection** To protect your users, Microsoft has learned from its positions in organizations with Azure Active Directory, the consumer space with Microsoft Accounts, and gaming with Xbox. Microsoft protects customers from threats by analyzing trillions of signals daily.

**Azure Identity Governance** Identity Governance in Azure Active Directory (Azure AD) helps you balance security and employee productivity while maintaining visibility.
