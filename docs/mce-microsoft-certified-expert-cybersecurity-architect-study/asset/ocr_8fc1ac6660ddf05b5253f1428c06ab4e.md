Continuous delivery of value to end users is enabled by the union of human resources, processes, and tools in DevOps.

**Be able to specify priorities for mitigating threats to data.** Today’s cloud-centric world presents organizations with both the unregulated (such as files stored on cloud services) and the unknown (such as advanced threats targeting users’ email). As a result, it is harder to protect data from all sources: on-premises, on PCs, on phones, and in the cloud. The high impact of ransomware and extortion attacks and the likelihood that an organization will be targeted makes preventing them an urgent priority for organizations. Deploy Microsoft’s recommended remediation plan.
