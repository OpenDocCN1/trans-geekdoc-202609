# Question 414. A company’s IT department notices a sharp increase in account lockouts over the past two days, especially during non-business hours. While some accounts are from various departments, a majority are from the finance team. Which of the following is the MOST plausible explanation for these lockouts?

+   (A) Scheduled maintenance by the IT department
(B) Employees are sharing passwords within the finance team
(C) An attacker is trying to gain unauthorized access
(D) A recent password policy change requiring more frequent changes

Explanation 414. Correct Answer: C. An attacker is trying to gain unauthorized access. A sudden increase in account lockouts, especially during non-business hours, indicates suspicious activity. The targeted nature of the lockouts, especially on sensitive departments like finance, suggests an attacker might be attempting unauthorized access.

Option A is incorrect. Scheduled maintenance would typically be communicated in advance, and it’s unlikely to cause a sharp increase in account lockouts.
