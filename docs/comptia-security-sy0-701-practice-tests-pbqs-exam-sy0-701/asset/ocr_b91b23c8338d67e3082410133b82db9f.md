Explanation 356. Correct Answer: C. Data retention policy.
A data retention policy specifically defines how long data should be retained and the manner of its storage based on business needs, regulatory requirements, and other factors. In the context of decommissioning servers, adhering to this policy will ensure that Jane complies with regulations related to data retention.

Option A is incorrect. The minimum necessary principle pertains to healthcare and refers to only using, disclosing, or requesting the amount of information necessary to accomplish a specific task.

Option B is incorrect. The principle of least privilege pertains to access controls and means giving users the minimum levels of access necessary to perform their jobs.
