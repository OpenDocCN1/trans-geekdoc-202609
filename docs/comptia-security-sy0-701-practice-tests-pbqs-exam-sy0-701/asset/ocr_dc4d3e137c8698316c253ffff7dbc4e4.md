**Explanation 268.** **Correct Answer: C. Layer 7 Firewall.**

Layer 7 firewalls, often known as Application Firewalls, can understand and filter traffic based on application-specific data, commands, or functions. They operate at the highest layer in the OSI model and can make decisions based on the actual content of the traffic.

**Option A is incorrect.** Layer 4 firewalls, also known as transport firewalls, primarily deal with data based on port numbers and protocol. They wouldn't be as effective as filtering application-specific commands as a Layer 7 firewall.

**Option B is incorrect.** Layer 2 firewalls operate at the data link layer, primarily dealing with MAC addresses. They aren't equipped to analyze application-specific content.

**Option D is incorrect.** Packet Filtering Firewalls operate primarily at the network layer, making decisions based on source/destination IP addresses, port numbers, and protocol types, not application-specific content.
