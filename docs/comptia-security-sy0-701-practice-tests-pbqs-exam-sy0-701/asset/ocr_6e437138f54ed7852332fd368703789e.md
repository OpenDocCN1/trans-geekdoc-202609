**Question 132.** A cybersecurity analyst has noticed a series of sophisticated attacks against critical infrastructure systems in their country. The attacks are highly coordinated, well-funded, and appear to have specific geopolitical objectives. Which type of threat actor is MOST likely responsible for these attacks?

+   (A) Organized crime syndicates
(B) Script kiddies
(C) Insider threat
(D) Nation-state

**Explanation 132.** **Correct Answer: D. Nation-state.** Sophisticated, coordinated, and well-funded attacks against national critical infrastructure with clear geopolitical objectives align most closely with the characteristics and motivations of nation-state actors. They often have vast resources and specific political or strategic motives, targeting critical infrastructures to cause significant impact or gain a strategic advantage.

**Option A is incorrect.** While organized crime syndicates might be well-funded and capable of launching sophisticated attacks, they are typically driven by financial motives rather than geopolitical objectives.
