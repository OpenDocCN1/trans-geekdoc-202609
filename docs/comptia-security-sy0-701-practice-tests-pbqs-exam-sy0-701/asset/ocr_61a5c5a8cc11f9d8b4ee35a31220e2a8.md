Question 453. In preparation for a potential lawsuit, Meg, a cybersecurity analyst, has been asked to ensure that specific digital evidence remains intact and is not altered or deleted. What measure should Meg implement to ensure this requirement?

Explanation 453. Correct Answer: B. Initiate a legal hold.
