# **Question 172.** The IT department of a large organization receives reports from employees that they are unable to access certain resources on the network. Upon investigation, the department discovers that the Access Control List (ACL) settings have been recently modified. Which of the following would be the PRIMARY reason to review and modify the ACL settings?

+   (A) To balance the network load
(B) To update the organization's firewall rules.
(C) To ensure appropriate access to resources
(D) To update the organization's password policy

**Explanation 172.** **Correct Answer: C. To ensure appropriate access to resources.** ACLs are essential tools in determining who has access to which resources within a network or system. Regularly reviewing and modifying the ACL settings ensures that users have the appropriate rights, preventing unauthorized access and ensuring that authorized users can access the resources they need.
