Question 568. Alice wants to access a restricted online portal. The portal asks her to enter a unique username and a secret passphrase only she should know. This process helps the system ensure that Alice is who she claims to be. What security concept is the portal employing?

+   (A) Authorization

+   (B) Accounting

+   (C) Multifactor authentication

+   (D) Authentication

Explanation 568. Correct Answer: D. Authentication. Authentication is the process of verifying the identity of a user, system, or application. In the described scenario, Alice is proving her identity to the system by providing a unique username and a passphrase, which are credentials that supposedly only she possesses.
