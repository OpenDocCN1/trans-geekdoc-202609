**Question 524.** TechHaus has recently experienced multiple security breaches where unauthorized personnel have manage to infiltrate their server rooms after hours. - (B) Using ultrasonic sensors
- (C) Deploying infrared (IR) sensors
- (D) Implementing RFID readers

**Explanation 524. Correct Answer: C. Deploying infrared (IR) sensors.** Infrared sensors detect heat radiation emitted by bodies, making them effective for detecting human presence even in total darkness.

**Option A is incorrect.** CCTV cameras often require light to capture clear images, and they do not specifically detect based on body heat.

**Option D is incorrect.** RFID readers are used for tracking tags via radio waves, not for detecting human intruders via body heat.

**Question 525.** After a security audit, the IT department discovered that several employees were using unauthorized software applications to bypass security protocols. Which of the following describes the primary risk associated with this behavior?

- (A) Insider threat
- (B) Shadow IT
- (C) Social engineering
- (D) Brute force

**Explanation 525. Correct Answer: B. Shadow IT.** Shadow IT refers to the use of software or hardware within an organization without explicit IT approval, which creates security gaps.

**Option A is incorrect.** While the employees are insiders, an "insider threat" usually implies a malicious intent to harm the organization. The unauthorized use of tools for productivity is specifically Shadow IT.

**Option C is incorrect.** Social engineering involves manipulating people into divulging confidential information, which is not the focus of using unauthorized software.

**Option D is incorrect.** Brute force is an attack method used to crack passwords by trying many combinations, which is unrelated to using unauthorized software tools.

**Question 526.** A company wants to protect against attacks that trick employees into revealing their sensitive credentials through deceptive emails. Which of the following methods is most likely being used?

- (A) Steganography
- (B) Brute force
- (C) Phishing
- (D) Port scanning

**Explanation 526. Correct Answer: C. Phishing.** Phishing is a form of social engineering where attackers use deceptive emails or websites to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

**Option A is incorrect.** Steganography involves hiding data within another file, such as an image or audio file, to avoid detection.

**Option B is incorrect.** Brute force involves systematically guessing passwords, not tricking users through deceptive communications.

**Option D is incorrect.** Port scanning is a technique used to identify open ports and services on a network, not to harvest credentials.

**Question 527.** Acme Corp. is in the early stages of a potential lawsuit, and their legal department has issued a notice. As an IT professional, which of the following should be your FIRST action?

- (B) Identify and isolate the email accounts related to the lawsuit
- (C) Delete all emails that are more than years old
- (D) Inform the media about the upcoming lawsuit

**Explanation 527. Correct Answer: B. Identify and isolate the email accounts related to the lawsuit.** In the context of e-discovery, it is crucial to locate and preserve electronically stored information (ESI) that is relevant to the case to prevent data loss or tampering.

**Option A is incorrect.** While backups are important, the immediate priority is to identify and preserve the specific data required for legal discovery.

**Option C is incorrect.** Deleting potential evidence after a legal notice could be considered spoliation and can lead to severe legal penalties.

**Option D is incorrect.** Informing the media is not a professional step in the e-discovery process and can be detrimental to the company’s case.

**Question 528.** A cloud-based e-commerce company wants to ensure that inventory system automatically updates stock levels on its website and third-party sales platforms whenever a sale occurs. What should the company leverage to achieve this real-time synchronization?

- (A) Regularly backup the inventory system and restore it on the website and sales platforms
- (B) Rely on customers to report discrepancies in stock levels
- (C) Use Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) to integrate the inventory system with the website and third-party platforms
- (D) Conduct daily stock audits and manually update all platforms

**Explanation 528. Correct Answer: C. Use Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) to integrate the inventory system with the website and third-party platforms.** APIs ensure real-time data synchronization between different systems, allowing for automatic updates whenever a transaction occurs.

**Option A is incorrect.** Backing up and restoring data is not a method for real-time synchronization across multiple live platforms.

**Option B is incorrect.** Relying on customers for data reporting is inefficient and leads to inaccurate stock levels. 

**Option D is incorrect.** Manual audits and updates are too slow and prone to error to meet the needs of a dynamic e-commerce environment.
