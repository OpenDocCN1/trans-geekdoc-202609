**Question 95.** A company is preparing to roll out a new infrastructure deployment for its internal network. (B) Full-disk Encryption
(C) Partition Encryption
(D) Transport-layer Encryption

**Explanation 95.** **Correct Answer: C. Partition Encryption.**nPartition Encryption allows specific partitions or volumes of a storage drive to be encrypted. By encrypting only the partition that contains confidential data, the company can ensure the security of sensitive information while leaving other partitions, such as the one with marketing material, unencrypted for easy access.

**Option A is incorrect.** File-level Encryption would require each confidential file to be encrypted individually, which could be cumbersome.

**Option B is incorrect.** Full-disk Encryption would encrypt the entire disk, including the non-sensitive marketing material.
