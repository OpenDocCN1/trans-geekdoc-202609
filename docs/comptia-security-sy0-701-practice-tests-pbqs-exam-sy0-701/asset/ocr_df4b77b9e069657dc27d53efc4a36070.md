# Explanation 8. Correct Answer: B. Regular security awareness training for employees. Security awareness training is a managerial control aiming to educate employees about security risks and the necessary precautions they need to take. By regularly training employees, the organization ensures that all staff are aware of potential threats and their roles in cybersecurity.
