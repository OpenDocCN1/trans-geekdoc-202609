**Explanation 119. Correct Answer: C. Blackmail to extract money by leveraging sensitive information.** The direct threat of releasing personal photos in exchange for money is a classic indication of blackmail. The attacker is leveraging sensitive information (personal photos) to extort money.

**Option A is incorrect.** There’s no mention of seeking company secrets or competitive intelligence. The focus of the attacker is on personal images of the executive.

**Option B is incorrect.** While releasing the images might harm the executive’s reputation, there’s no direct intent shown to disrupt the company’s services or harm its operational standing.

**Option D is incorrect.** The attacker is directly demanding money in exchange for not releasing the photos. This differs from data exfiltration where data might be sold or leveraged in other ways.
