Explanation 443. Correct Answer: C. Threat hunting. **Option A is incorrect.** Incident management refers to the process followed when managing and responding to a security incident. In this scenario, Alex is taking a proactive approach to find potential threats, not responding to an identified incident.

**Option B is incorrect.** Threat modeling involves identifying potential threats and designing countermeasures to prevent or mitigate the impact of those threats. It’s more about planning actively searching for threats.
