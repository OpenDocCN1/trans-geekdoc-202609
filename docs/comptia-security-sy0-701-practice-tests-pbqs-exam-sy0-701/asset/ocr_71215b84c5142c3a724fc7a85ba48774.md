**Explanation 535.** **Correct Answer: C. Rules of engagement (ROE).** The Rules of Engagement (ROE) document defines the scope, boundaries, methods, and other critical guidelines for a penetration test. It ensures that both the testing team and the organization understand and agree on what can and cannot be done during the testing.

**Option A is incorrect.** A Service-level agreement (SLA) primarily deals with the levels of service expected, such as uptime, response times, etc. It doesn’t define the boundaries of a penetration test.

**Option B is incorrect.** A Non-disclosure agreement (NDA) is about keeping information confidential and wouldn’t define the scope of a penetration test.

**Option D is incorrect.** A Memorandum of understanding (MOU) is a general agreement between parties, but it doesn’t detail the specific scope and rules of a penetration test like the ROE would.
