Question 209. An organization’s IT department noticed a rapid increase in network traffic over the past 24 hours. Simultaneously, many employees reported that their systems have been slow and that they received a file named “updatePatch.exe” from coworkers via email, even though their coworkers did not intentionally send any files. What type of malware is most likely responsible for this behavior?

+   (A) Trojan
(B) Ransomware
(C) Adware
(D) Worm

Explanation 209. Correct Answer: D. Worm. Worms are a type of malware that self-replicates and spreads to other devices. The rapid increase in network traffic and the distribution of a file to various users without the sender’s knowledge is characteristic of worm behavior. They can consume bandwidth and system resources, causing systems to slow down.
