Question 421. During a routine audit of the corporate servers, the system administrator discovers that a week’s worth of security logs are missing from one of the key application servers. Which of the following is the MOST likely reason for this occurrence?

(B) There was insufficient storage space for the logs
(C) A malware attack aimed to erase traces of intrusion
(D) The time zone setting was incorrectly configured

Explanation 421. Correct Answer: C. A malware attack aimed to erase traces of intrusion. One of the primary tactics attackers use after gaining unauthorized access to cover their tracks.
