Question 599. A user receives an SMS claiming to be from her bank, alerting her of unauthorized activity on her account. - (A) Smishing
- (B) Vishing
- (C) Bluejacking
- (D) Bluesnarfing

Explanation 599. Correct Answer: A. Smishing. Smishing is a type of phishing attack where malicious actors use SMS to deceive individuals into providing sensitive information, typically by impersonating trusted organizations or contacts.

Option B is incorrect. Vishing refers to voice phishing, where attackers use voice calls instead of text or email to impersonate legitimate entities and scam victims.
