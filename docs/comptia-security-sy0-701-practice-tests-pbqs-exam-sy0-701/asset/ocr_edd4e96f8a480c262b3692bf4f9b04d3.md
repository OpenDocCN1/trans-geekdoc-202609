+   (B) Restricted

**Explanation 220. Correct Answer: C. Sensitive.** Phone numbers and email addresses are considered Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and should be classified as sensitive since unauthorized access could lead to privacy violations or misuse.

**Option A is incorrect.** Public classification is for data that is intended for general access and holds no confidential information.

**Option B is incorrect.** Restricted typically applies to data that is more confidential than sensitive data, such as financial or strategic data.

**Option D is incorrect.** Classified is a more general term and can encompass various levels of data classification, including Top Secret, Secret, or Confidential, typically used in government sectors.
