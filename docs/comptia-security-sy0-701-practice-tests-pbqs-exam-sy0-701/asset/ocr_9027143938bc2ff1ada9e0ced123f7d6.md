**Question 286.** An e-commerce company is experiencing attacks that specifically target the shopping cart feature of its web application. They want to implement a firewall that can understand web application-specific commands and provide protection. Which type of firewall should they consider?

+   (A) Layer 4 Firewall
(B) Layer 2 Firewall
(C) Layer 7 Firewall
(D) Packet Filtering Firewall

**Explanation 286.** **Correct Answer: C. Layer 7 Firewall.** Layer 7 firewalls, often known as Application Layer firewalls, can understand and filter traffic based on application-specific data, commands, or functions. They operate at the highest layer in the OSI model and can make decisions based on the actual content of the traffic.

**Option A is incorrect.** Layer 4 firewalls, also known as transport layer firewalls, primarily deal with data based on port numbers and protocol. They wouldn’t be as effective in filtering application-specific commands as a Layer 7 firewall.

**Option B is incorrect.** Layer 2 firewalls operate at the data link layer, primarily dealing with MAC addresses. They aren’t equipped to analyze application-specific content.
