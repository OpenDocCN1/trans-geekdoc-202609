## 1. Introduction to Association Rule Learning

### 1.1. What is Association Rule Learning?

Association Rule Learning is a data mining technique used to find relationships or associations among a set of items in transactional data. This technique is often employed to uncover patterns such as the relationships between products frequently bought together or co-occurring events.

#### Key Concepts

● `Association Rules`: If-Then statements that describe relationships between items.
○ `Antecedent`: The “If” part of the rule, representing the condition.
○ `Consequent`: The “Then” part of the rule, representing the outcome.
● Example Rule: If a customer buys bread, they are likely to also buy butter.
● `Support`: The proportion of transactions that include both the antecedent and consequent.
Support(A→B)=Count(A and B)Total Transactions
