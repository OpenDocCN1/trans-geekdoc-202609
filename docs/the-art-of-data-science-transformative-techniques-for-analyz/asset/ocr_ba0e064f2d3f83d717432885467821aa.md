Crucially, compelling storytelling with data extends beyond the visual layer to the narrative layer. The narrative serves as the connecting thread between data points, transforming a set of information into a coherent and captivating tale. The narrative thread weaves through the data, introducing characters (variables), setting the scene (context), and building tension

(analytical challenges). It leads the viewer through the growing action of discovery, the turning point of revelations, and the resolution of recommendations and conclusions. Data are transformed into a tale that appeals to the audience's understanding and intuition through a narrative arc, which provides structure and coherence.

One of the foundational principles of storytelling with data is clarity. The story will be understandable and approachable to a wide range of readers thanks to clarity. It condenses intricate studies into simple tales that avoid superfluous technical details and jargon. Straightforward storytelling with data enables stakeholders, who may not be data experts, to grasp the implications of the analysis and make informed decisions. The narrative serves as a translator, bridging the gap between the language of data and decision- makers, ensuring that insights are preserved in translation.
