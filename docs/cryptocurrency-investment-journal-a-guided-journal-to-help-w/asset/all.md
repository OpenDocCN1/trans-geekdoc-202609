A Quick Word

 

Is cryptocurrency a contemporary trend or the beginning of a fiscal revolution?

 

What began as a niche movement has bloomed with worldwide attention. Even some big financial institutions are jumping on board the cryptocurrency blockchain train. But are people moving too soon? It’s hard to tell, and like many things in life, no one can predict or guarantee the future – that includes the tarot card lady down the street. Even so, it’s probably safe to assume that cryptocurrency will be around for a while, if not indefinitely. If you plan on dipping your toe into the cryptocurrency market, this guided journal can help you get started.

 

It’s important to gather some basics and research the many digital currency opportunities available. This guided journal can be a nice starting point for helping you collect some basic data and information about a cryptocurrency.

 

There are numerous factors to consider before investing in crypto. The information gathered here won’t be exhaustive and it may only be a piece of the puzzle, so it’s imperative that you conduct research beyond this journal. Remember, always try to keep it simple and start with the basics. Also to be mentioned, this journal does not include definitions of investment terminology, so if you come across a term or word you do not understand (such as Market Capitalization or Trading Volume) then it’s advised that you look up that investment vocabulary.

 

This guided journal allows for fifty entries. Each entry consists of areas to notate the cryptocurrency name, current date, current price, trading volume, market capitalization, historical performance, summarization of basic data, the exchange you’ll use, the fees and costs associated with the exchange, buying and selling costs, and the security and privacy of the exchange.

 

Before buying a ticket for the digital blockchain train, please don’t forget to do your research. It’s important to know where your money is going.
