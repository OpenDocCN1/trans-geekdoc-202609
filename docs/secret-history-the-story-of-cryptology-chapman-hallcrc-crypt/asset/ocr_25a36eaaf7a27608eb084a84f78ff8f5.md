To start, we fill a rectangle with the alphabet. I and J are once again (see Polybius) equated:

Given the message

```
LIFE IS SHORT AND HARD - LIKE A BODYBUILDING ELF.²¹
```

we begin by breaking it into pairs:

```
LI FE IS SH OR TA ND HA RD LI KE AB OD YB UI LD IN GD WA RF
```

To encipher the first pair, LI, we find those letters in the square above. We can then find two more letters, F and O, to get the four corners of a rectangle.

F G H I&J K
