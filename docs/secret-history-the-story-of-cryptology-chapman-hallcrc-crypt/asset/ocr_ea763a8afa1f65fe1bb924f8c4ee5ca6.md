# 5.6 The Case of the Seized Manuscript

Despite his close brush with being charged with treason, Yardley prepared another manuscript, *Japanese Diplomatic Secrets*. Thomas E. Dewey, an assistant in the U.S. Attorney's office at the time, made the decision to seize it.³⁵ The U.S. government did just this in February 1933. The text of 1,000+ pages wasn't declassified until March 2, 1979.³⁶ It was later offered for sale as a pdf on a CD put out by Aegean Park Press.
