For example, in column 2 the most frequent character is P, yet this implies that E (with frequency 1) is T and L (with frequency 0) is A, values that do not seem likely. A better strategy is to look for the shift that maximizes the sum of the frequencies for E, A, and T, the three most frequent plaintext letters.
