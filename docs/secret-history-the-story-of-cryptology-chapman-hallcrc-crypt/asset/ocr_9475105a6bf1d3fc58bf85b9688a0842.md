The originality of our scheme was that the same one-time pad could be reused safely over and over again if no eavesdropping were detected. Thus, the title of our paper was “Quantum Cryptography II: How to reuse a one-time pad safely even if P = NP.”⁵ We submitted this paper to major theoretical computer science conferences, such as STOC (The ACM Annual Symposium on Theoretical Computer Science), but we failed to have it accepted. Contrary to Wiesner’s “Conjugate Coding”, however, our “Quantum Cryptography II” paper has forever remained unpublished (copies are available from the authors).
