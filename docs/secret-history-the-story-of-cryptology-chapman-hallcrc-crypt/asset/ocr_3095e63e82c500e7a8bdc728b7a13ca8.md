The graphs of the solutions take two basic forms, depending on whether the elliptic curve has three real roots, or just one.

We make the following work simpler by avoiding elliptic curves that have roots of multiplicity higher than one.⁵ When looking at complex solutions (as opposed to the real solutions graphed in [Figure 20.4](#)), the visualization takes the form of a torus (donut).

⁵ Another simplification involves not using fields of characteristic 2 or 3. The interested reader may consult the references for the reasons for these simplifications. These simplifications are just that. We may proceed, with greater difficulty, without them.
