Multiplying these last two together, we have

$$\begin{aligned}
(27)^2(28)^2 &= (2)(13)^2(2)=(2)^2(13)^2\pmod{391}\\
(27\cdot28)^2 &= (2\cdot13)^2\pmod{391}\\
(756)^2 &= (26)^2\pmod{391}\\
(365)^2 &= (26)^2\pmod{391}
\end{aligned}$$

So we get

$$\begin{aligned}
(365-26)(365+26)&=132,549=kn\\
132,549/391&=339
\end{aligned}$$

We get one factor equaling $k$ and the other equaling $n$ in this “factorization”—not what we wanted!
