# 5.3 Cryptanalysis of ADFGX

The French were saved by Georges Painvin (Figure 5.3), who exerted a tremendous effort over nearly three months, first breaking ADFGX, which used a 5-by-5 Polybius square, and then ADFGVX, losing 33 pounds (15 kg) in the process but finally obtaining solutions revealing where the next attack was to be.¹⁴ A former American diplomat, J. Rives Childs, observed that,¹⁵

> His masterly solutions of German ciphers caused him to become known as “artisan of the victory” over the Germans when Paris might have fallen but for the knowledge gained of German intentions by Painvin of where they would strike.
