# 2.12 History Rewritten!

At the beginning of the discussion of the one-time pad, I wrote that “historians long believed that it didn’t arise until the years 1917 to 1918, when it was developed by Gilbert Vernam and Major Joseph Mauborgne at AT&T.” In 2011, history was rewritten by Steven M. Bellovin, a computer science professor and a collector of code books. Bellovin’s hobby had previously intersected with his professional life when he gave an entertaining lecture titled “Compression, Correction, Confidentiality, and Comprehension, A Modern Look at Commercial Telegraph Codes” at the 2009 Cryptologic History Symposium sponsored by the National Security Agency’s Center for Cryptologic History.⁵⁰ But he struck gold without even trying when filling some leisure hours at the Library of Congress (and indulging his hobby) by examining some of the code books kept there. One of these, dated 1882 and written by Frank Miller, contained a description of a one-time pad!⁵¹
