However, there is no column that can be placed between 7↔4 and 10↔15 to place an H between each T and E. Recall, H is identified as FA in Case 1, so it is not enough to find a column that has the same letter pair in each of these two positions; we also want that pair to be FA. (12↔20 offers an FA in one of those positions, but then the other T-E would have some other letter in the middle, which is possible...) Now suppose 12↔20 leads into Case 1. Then we’d want an FA in position 17 of some other column (to fill in the H), and we don’t have it anywhere. Moving on to 13↔19, we’d want an FA in position 11. We are given this by 3↔9. The last case is the same: For 18↔14, we’d want an FA in position 11 and we are given this by 3↔9. So, there are three reasonable possibilities, with the last two being slightly favored, because they don’t require a non-H to appear between T and E anywhere. We examine these last two possibilities as subcases of Case 1.
