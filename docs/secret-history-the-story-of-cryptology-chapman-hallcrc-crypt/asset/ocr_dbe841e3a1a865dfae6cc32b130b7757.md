Group 2 must be LE, which then forces E to take position 6 in the square, which, in turn, makes ciphertext groups 13 and 14, CE and ED, respectively. Thus, the message ends with --CXCEEDUY. At this point, the attacker either may guess SUCCEED followed by two meaningless letters so that the ciphertext could be evenly split into groups of five characters or he may look at the recovered square again, which is now almost complete, testing the very few remaining for a meaningful plaintext.
