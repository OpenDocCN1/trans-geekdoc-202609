# # Conclusion

**GANs have dominated generative modelling from 2014 – 2021**

+   - Train a generator to fool a discriminator (the adversary)
- GANs are implicitly minimizing the Jensen-Shannon Divergence (mix of both KLs)
- Optimization can be tricky (unstable, no gradients, etc...)

**Diffusion models are typically easier to train and taken over most of of the field...**

+   - They also show more diversity for image generation (mode collapse)
