**Node classification:**
$	ext{softmax}(\mathbf{z}_n)$
e.g. Kipf & Welling (ICLR 2017)

**Graph classification:**
$	ext{softmax}(\sum_n \mathbf{z}_n)$
e.g. Duvenaud et al. (NIPS 2015)

**Link prediction:**
$p(A_{ij}) = \sigma(\mathbf{z}_i^T \mathbf{z}_j)$
Kipf & Welling (NIPS BDL 2016)
"Graph Auto-Encoders"

**No weight sharing:** Each message passing layer has its own weights
