# Variational Auto-Encoders

Faces produced by variational auto-encoders

+   - In comparison to other generative models (such as GANs or Diffusion), VAEs produce rather blurry images
  - More specific methods (e.g. hierarchical VAEs) can achieve similar performance to GANs
  - Diffusion is a different league

+   - Most likely causes:
  - Maximum Likelihood objective of VAEs
  - Restrictions on the family of distributions
  - The lower bound approximation
