**Positional Encoding:** A sequence of vectors $P_0, \dots, P_N$ to encode position

+   - **Every vector is unique** (and uniquely represents time)
- Relationship between $P_t$ and $P_{t+	au}$ only depends on the distance between them

$P_{t+	au} = M_	au P_t$

The linear relationship between $P_t$ and $P_{t+	au}$ enables the net to learn shift-invariant "gap" dependent relationships
