# Introduction

Crop yield estimation has always been a critical aspect of agricultural economics, serving as a fundamental predictor of food supply, market trends, and resource allocation. Traditional approaches, reliant on historical data and empirical observations, have provided valuable insights but are increasingly inadequate in the face of modern challenges. The need for more accurate, efficient, and timely estimations has driven the incorporation of innovative technologies such as AI and ML into agricultural practices (Vuppalapati, 2021). These technologies offer transformative potential in improving the economic sustainability of agriculture by enhancing crop yield predictions, thereby contributing to global food security and economic stability. The economic framework of sustainable agriculture is grounded in principles that balance profitability with environmental stewardship and social equity. Sustainable agriculture economics emphasizes the long-term viability of farming practices that protect natural resources and support rural communities (Thamilselvan et al., 2021; Chouhan et al., 2021). The introduction of AI and ML into this framework marks a significant shift towards precision agriculture, where data-driven decisions optimize resource use, reduce waste, and improve crop management. By leveraging vast quantities of data, AI, and ML can identify patterns and predict outcomes with unprecedented accuracy, thus enhancing economic sustainability (Araújo et al., 2023).
