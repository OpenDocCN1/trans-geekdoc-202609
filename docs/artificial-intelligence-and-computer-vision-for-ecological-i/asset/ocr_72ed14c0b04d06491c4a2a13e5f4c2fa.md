# Soil Analysis

The SAR and hyperspectral imaging assess dirt dampness content, texture, organic matter, and nutrient degrees. This info is crucial for recognizing soil fertility and making informed decisions about dirt management (Qiao et al., 2024). Geographical maps will determine dry areas requiring watering or zones susceptible to waterlogging that might need drain services. Hyperspectral information can discover variations in soil raw material and nutrients, directing the application of garden composts or fertilizers to enhance soil health and wellness (Jafarbiglu and Pourreza, 2022). RS aids in mapping soil disintegration and compaction, which is essential for preserving dirt structure and fertility. By supplying a spatial introduction of dirt problems, RS supports accurate farming practices that maximize soil monitoring. This brings about boosted plant development, decreased input costs, and much better sustainability (Chuphal et al., 2024). Moreover, continual tracking allows for the analysis of soil wellness in time, assisting in lasting planning and conservation efforts.

# Yield Prediction

Regarding yield production and prediction, RS enhances the precision of crop yield forecasts by supplying extensive and prompt information on crop growth stages, health, and environmental problems (Jafarbiglu and Pourreza, 2022; Kumar et al., 2024b). Evaluating historical RS information along with current period monitoring, models’ forecasts yield high accuracy. NDVI and Boosted Plants Index (EVI) are the most popular technologies originating from multispectral and hyperspectral imagery that offer an understanding right into plant biomass and health and wellness, which are directly correlated with yield potential (Tey et al., 2024). Thermal imaging can keep track of temperature tension, while SAR information supplies info on dirt dampness levels, both of which impact crop efficiency. Integrating these data points with weather forecasts and ground fact information enables the growth of durable yield prediction versions (Abid et al., 2021). These predictions are indispensable for farmers and agriculture in planning harvests, managing supply chains, and making market choices. Exact return forecasts additionally sustain food security preparation and policymaking by governments and global organizations, guaranteeing that sufficient sources are assigned to meet future food needs (Shanmugapriya et al., 2019).

# Disaster Management

Satellite imagery and airborne data use real-time information on the level and intensity of such events, allowing for prompt feedback and recovery efforts (Singh et al., 2022).
