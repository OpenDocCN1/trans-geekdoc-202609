# Introduction

Water has been the source of life since prehistoric times. And it became the principal source of life, continuing the human life-line tendency. That’s why we referred to it as a “Life”. Inevitably, the vast majority of humanity has utilized it as a source of exploitation, gradually intoxicating the environment and turning it into a demise event. That is referred to as “water pollution”.

What is water pollution? There are several ways to characterize pollution. When one or more compounds are released into the water that will negatively alter it, pollution of the water happens (Grégorio Crini and Eric Lichtfouse, 2018). These compounds have the potential to negatively impact the environment, humans, animals, and their ecosystems. Water contamination is categorized in many ways (Morin-Crini and Crini, 2017). “Point and non-point” can be used for the two main sources. On the other hand, the second refers to pollutants emitted from several sources. The first category includes pollutants that come from a single source, such as emissions from industry into the water.
