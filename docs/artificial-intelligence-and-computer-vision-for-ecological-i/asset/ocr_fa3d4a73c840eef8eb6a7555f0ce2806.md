Artificial intelligence (AI) is transforming numerous industries, including water quality monitoring. Gaining a grasp of these foundational concepts is essential to understanding how artificial intelligence (AI) may solve problems with conventional water quality monitoring techniques.

# What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence in machines with decision-making, learning, and thinking capabilities. Artificial intelligence (AI) is an umbrella term for a variety of technologies that allow robots to carry out activities like speech recognition, visual perception, language translation, and decision-making that normally need human intelligence.

# Key Concepts in AI:

The key concepts in artificial intelligence are summarized in Table 6.

+   - **Machine Learning (ML)**: A subset of AI that involves training algorithms to learn from and make predictions based on data.
+   - **Deep Learning (DL)**: A specialized form of ML that uses neural networks with many layers to analyze complex patterns in large datasets.
+   - **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: Enables machines to understand and interpret human language.
+   - **Computer Vision**: The capability of machines to interpret and make decisions based on visual inputs.

Table 6. Key concepts in Artificial Intelligence (Russell and Norvig, 2020).

| Concept | Description |
|---|---|
| Machine Learning (ML) | Algorithms that learn from data and make predictions |
| Deep Learning (DL) | Advanced ML using neural networks with multiple layers |
| Natural Language Processing (NLP) | Understanding and interpreting human language |
| Computer Vision | Analyzing and making decisions based on visual data |
