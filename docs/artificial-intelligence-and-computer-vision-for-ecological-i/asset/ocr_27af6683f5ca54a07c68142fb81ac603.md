# Dissolved Oxygen Prediction

An essential quality of surface water indicator is dissolved oxygen (DO), it symbolizes the overall condition of the aquatic environment and its capacity to support aquatic life. Heat, salinity, and air pressure are some of the variables that affect dissolved oxygen, which is a representation of oxygen molecules in the aqueous environment (Zaitsev et al., 2018). Fish and other aquatic life in water bodies depend on D.O. The wind and aerating action reflect the water’s dissolved oxygen content. The stability of aquatic creatures and fish life depends on the D.O. level, which needs to be maintained within a certain range. The higher the D.O. level, the better the conditions for aquatic life and fish survival. D.O. is utilized as an indicator to show the condition of any aquatic system, and while it is in the water column, it is vital to microorganisms (Chen and Liu, 2014). A linear polynomial neural network (PNN) model was utilized to predict the concentrations of DO in the Danube River (Siljic Tomic et al., 2018). A different squad of researchers (Solanki et al., 2015) presented a deep learning network model that outperformed supervised learning-based techniques in terms of accuracy and performance when it came to predicting the pH and dissolved oxygen contents of water.
