+   - *Social Equity*: Ensuring fair labour practices, equitable access to resources, and the well-being of farming societies are integral to sustainable agriculture. Policies and practices that support smallholder farmers, promote fair trade, and enhance rural development are essential.
+   - *Policy and Institutional Support*: Administrations and organisations play a critical role in encouraging sustainable agriculture through policies, subsidies, research, and extension services. Supportive policies can incentivize sustainable practices, provide tutoring and training, and facilitate entrance to markets and technology (Mishra and Mishra, 2023).

# Impact of AI and ML on Economic Sustainability

The advent of AI and ML technologies is revolutionizing agricultural practices, with significant implications for economic sustainability. These technologies offer advanced tools for optimizing production, reducing costs, and enhancing decision-making processes. Some impacts include:

+   - *Precision Agriculture*: AI and ML enable precision agriculture by analyzing vast amounts of information from sensors, satellite pictures, and weather predictions. This allows for precise application of inputs such as water, fertilizers, and pesticides, leading to increased effectiveness and reduced environmental influence.
+   - *Yield Prediction and Management*: AI models can expect crop yields with high levels of accurateness by analyzing ancient data, weather arrangements, and soil conditions. This helps farmers make informed verdicts about planting, harvesting and marketing their crops, ultimately improving profitability and reducing waste.
+   - *Pest and Disease Management*: ML algorithms can detect early signs of pest infestations and diseases through image recognition and pattern analysis. Early recognition permits for timely intervention, reducing crop losses and minimizing the requirement for chemical management.
+   - *Resource Optimization*: AI-driven systems can optimize resource use by providing real-time recommendations on irrigation, fertilization, and crop management. This not only reduces costs but also enhances resource sustainability.
+   - *Market Analysis and Forecasting*: AI tools can analyze market trends, price fluctuations, and consumer preferences to provide farmers with insights for better marketing strategies. This helps in aligning production with market demand, reducing surplus and scarcities.
+   - *Supply Chain Efficiency*: AI technologies enhance supply chain management by improving logistics, inventory control, and traceability. This leads to reduced food waste, lower transportation costs, and improved market access for farmers.
+   - *Economic Decision Support*: AI-powered decision maintenance systems assist farmers in making complex economic decisions by integrating data on costs, revenues, risks, and market conditions. This enhances economic planning and financial management.
