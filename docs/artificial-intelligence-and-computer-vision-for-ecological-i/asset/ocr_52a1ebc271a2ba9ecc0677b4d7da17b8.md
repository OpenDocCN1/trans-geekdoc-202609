# *Overview of Water Quality Assessment*

A complex process known as “water quality assessment” is used to determine if a body of water is safe to use for drinking, recreational activities, farming, or industrial processes. The measurement of physical, chemical, and biological parameters is included in this assessment. Physical characteristics that include temperature, turbidity, color, taste, and odour serve as fundamental water quality indicators (Neama et al., 2022). The solubility of gases and the metabolic rates of aquatic organisms are influenced by temperature, whereas light penetration and photosynthetic aquatic plants and creatures are affected by turbidity, which is the result of suspended particles (Petropoulou et al., 2023) (de Mello et al., 2020). Chemical parameters are used to quantify the concentrations of various substances, such as dissolved oxygen (DO), pH, nutrients, heavy metals, and organic pollutants.
