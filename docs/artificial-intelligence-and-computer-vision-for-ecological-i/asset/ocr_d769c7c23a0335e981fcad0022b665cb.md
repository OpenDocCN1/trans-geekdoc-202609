As we have comprehensive knowledge about AI to understand its quality based on the working pattern, aspects of it have a great role in the selection of its particular algorithm to run the process explained below;

+   a. Machine Learning (ML) (Murphy, 2012):
+     - An area of artificial intelligence that concentrates on creating algorithms which enable machines analyze data and make decisions or forecasts from it.
+     - Supervised Learning: Involves using a labeled dataset to train a model, where the input gives the proper output. Regression (such as catching spam emails) and classification (for example, predicting housing values).
+     - Unsupervised Learning: Involves training a model on data without labels. The model tries to find hidden patterns or intrinsic structures in the input data. Examples include clustering (e.g., customer segmentation) and dimensionality reduction (e.g., PCA).
+     - Reinforcement Learning: Focuses on training agents to make a sequence of decisions by rewarding them for desirable actions. Robotic control and gaming (like AlphaGo) are two examples of applications.
