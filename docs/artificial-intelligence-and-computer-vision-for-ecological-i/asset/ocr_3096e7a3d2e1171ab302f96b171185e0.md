# Internet of Things

Internet of Things (IoT) has transformed soil fertility assessment through the provision of uninterrupted, continuous surveillance and data acquisition (Narasegouda et al., 2020). This method facilitates the more accurate and effective control of soil health, thereby improving agricultural yield. The concept of IoT in soil fertility evaluation involves establishing a networked system that consistently gathers, transmits, analyzes, and utilizes real-time data from diverse sensors to enhance and automate soil management techniques (Parween et al., 2021). This technique results in agricultural operations that are more accurate, effective, and environmentally friendly.
