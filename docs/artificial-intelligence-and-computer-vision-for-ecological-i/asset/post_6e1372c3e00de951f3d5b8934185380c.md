## Challenges and Limitations

The incorporation of Artificial Intelligence in plant growth analysis poses substantial challenges and limitations that must be overcome to ensure successful implementation. Broader systemic issues encompass ethical and privacy concerns related to data ownership, the ever-changing regulatory and policy landscape, and the environmental consequences of deploying Artificial Intelligence infrastructure. Overcoming these challenges necessitates a collaborative effort among Artificial Intelligence researchers, agricultural scientists, policymakers, and farmers to create sustainable, efficient, and trustworthy Artificial Intelligence solutions in the field of agriculture.
