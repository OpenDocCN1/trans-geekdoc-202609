# Aerial Support Coordination

**Case Study: Mediterranean Region’s FireHub:** The European Union’s FireHub (2019) platform integrates satellite data and weather information to support aerial firefighting operations across the Mediterranean region. By predicting fire spread and intensity, FireHub assists in coordinating aerial water drops and other aerial firefighting tactics. This has proven effective in managing large-scale fires and protecting critical infrastructure.
