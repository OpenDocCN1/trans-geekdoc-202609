# Introduction

In today’s rapidly evolving technological landscape, chatbots have emerged as versatile tools capable of understanding human language and executing predefined tasks. Stemming from Joseph Weizenbaum’s pioneering creation of Eliza in 1966, these digital entities have undergone significant advancements since Alan Turing posed the fundamental question of machine intelligence. Today, they permeate various industries, serving as invaluable customer interaction and support resources. However, despite their widespread adoption, the effectiveness of chatbots varies considerably. The challenge lies not merely in their creation but in crafting chatbots that excel in functionality and user experience.
