# AI Techniques for Soil Fertility Management Decision Tree

Decision tree is another popular machine learning method used for classifying and predicting data in problems involving regression as well as classification. It represents decisions and their potential outcomes using a hierarchical structure like a tree. The decision tree operates by constantly dividing the dataset into smaller groups, or subsets, according to the value of a certain characteristic (Priyanka and Kumar, 2020). The objective is to enhance the uniformity of the desired outcome within each subset. This technique generates a hierarchical arrangement of choices that culminates in a definitive forecast.

Decision trees are useful in categorizing soil samples according to their nutrient content and other characteristics during the assessment of soil fertility. The nodes of the tree symbolize determination of nutrient thresholds, while the leaves symbolize the anticipated fertility status. This assists farmers and agronomists in making informed decisions based on information.
