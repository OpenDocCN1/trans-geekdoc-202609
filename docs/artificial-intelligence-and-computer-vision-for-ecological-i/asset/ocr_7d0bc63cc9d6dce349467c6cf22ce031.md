The applications and functionalities of chatbots within the Farmer ChatBot (FCB) framework encompass a diverse range of agricultural support services tailored to meet the specific needs of farmers. Firstly, chatbots play a crucial role in query resolution, providing immediate assistance on a wide array of agricultural topics such as crop cultivation techniques, pest management strategies, fertilizer usage, and soil health improvement measures.
