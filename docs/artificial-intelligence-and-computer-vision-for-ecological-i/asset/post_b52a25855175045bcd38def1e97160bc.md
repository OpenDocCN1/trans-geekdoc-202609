## Importance of Minimizing Food Waste

Food is important to human survival; its waste hinders the sustainability of our food systems. Food waste not only negatively impacts food security and its availability, but also contributes to increasing the cost of food material. Production of food starts from the farm and moves through the system up until it reaches our plate. Before going to waste, the production of food utilizes resources such as water, land, energy, capital, and labor. Additionally, this food that goes to waste ends up in landfills which leads to greenhouse gas emissions, contributing to climate change.

## Role of AI Tools and Techniques in Reducing Food Waste

In the recent past, a surge has been witnessed in the development of AI to assist humans in diverse domains (Furman, 2019). Artificial intelligence is known to mimic human intellect and intelligence. The inclusion and amalgamation of AI into the food industry hold huge potential for addressing food waste, supporting the circular economy, and enhancing sustainability in food production. There are various AI-based technologies for possible use to reduce food wastage and its valorization (Table 4).
