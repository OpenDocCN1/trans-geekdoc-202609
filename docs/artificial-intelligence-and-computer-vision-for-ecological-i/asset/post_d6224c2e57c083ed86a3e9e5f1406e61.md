### Basil Crop Detection Using Computer Vision and Deep Learning Approach

Rajneesh Kumar Patel,¹ Siddharth Singh Chouhan,¹,* Thota Chandan¹ and Uday Pratap Singh²

### Introduction

In recent years, applying deep learning models in agriculture has gained significant attention due to their potential to revolutionize farming practices. Precision farming maximizes crop yields while mitigating environmental repercussions and increasing economic returns by efficiently managing agricultural resources. Accurate detection and monitoring of crop health and growth are crucial for its success. Basil (Ocimum basilicum) is a widely cultivated herb with diverse culinary and medicinal uses (Sipos et al., 2021; Joshi and Bhavsar, 2023). The successful development and yield of basil plants depend on various factors, including adequate irrigation, nutrient supply, and pest management. Monitoring basil crops’ health and growth stages is crucial for optimizing these factors and ensuring a successful harvest. Precision agriculture has become increasingly vital in modern farming practices to ensure optimal crop management, resource allocation, and sustainability (Abbasi et al., 2023; Farooqui et al., 2023). Traditional crop monitoring methods involve labor-intensive manual inspections or basic imaging techniques, which can be time-consuming and require more precision for effective decision-making (Chouhan et al., 2019a; Chouhan et al., 2019b). The advent of deep learning and computer vision technologies has
opened new avenues for automating crop monitoring tasks with higher accuracy and efficiency (Zhu et al., 2024; Singh et al., 2023). Object detection, in particular, enables the automated identification and localization of specific objects within digital images or video frames. YOLO (You Only Look Once) is a pioneering object detection architecture known for its real-time performance and accuracy (Sukhadeo Bajait et al., 2023). It is well-suited for agricultural applications where timely decision-making is critical. YOLOv8 represents an evolution of the YOLO architecture, incorporating advancements in model design, training techniques, and computational efficiency.

By leveraging a combination of deep convolutional neural networks and efficient inference strategies, YOLOv8 promises improved detection performance and reduced computational overhead compared to earlier iterations (Yang et al., 2023; Kunduracioglu and Pacal, 2024). Basil crops are susceptible to various environmental factors, pests, and diseases, making timely and accurate detection critical for maintaining crop health and maximizing yield. This study aims to develop a practical and efficient solution using YOLOv8 to detect basil crops in real-time, enabling farmers and agronomists to promptly make informed decisions and interventions (Fuentes-Pacheco et al., 2024; Mao et al., 2023). The Research Questionnaire (RQ) we developed to conduct this study are as follows:

- **RQ1:** What is the reason for selecting the basil plant?

- **RQ3:** What improvements or additional features would you suggest for YOLOv8 to optimize its use in precision agriculture?
- **RQ4:** What challenges have you encountered with existing methods for crop detection and monitoring methods?
- **RQ5:** How effective are these technologies in terms of accuracy and efficiency?

### Literature Review

Precision agriculture has witnessed substantial advancements driven by integrating deep learning and computer vision techniques. Object detection has been pivotal in automating tasks crucial for efficient crop management (Bougettaya et al., 2022). In this context, YOLO (You Only Look Once) models have emerged as a prominent choice, given their real-time capabilities and accuracy (Badgujar et al., 2024). YOLOv8, a recent iteration in the YOLO series, stands out for its robustness and versatility. Numerous studies have showcased the potential of YOLOv8 in diverse applications, from pedestrian detection to vehicle counting. However, the deployment of YOLOv8 in the domain of precision agriculture, specifically for basil crop detection, remains a relatively unexplored avenue. The proposed research contributes to this burgeoning field by presenting a practical study that leverages YOLOv8 for basil crop detection, demonstrating its adaptability and efficacy in a high-value herb context.
