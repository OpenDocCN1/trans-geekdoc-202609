# Key Principles and Strategies: Reduce, Reuse, Recycle (3R)

The principles of reduce, reuse, and recycle (3R) are foundational to the circular economy, aiming to produce a sustainable system where resources are employed efficiently and waste is reduced significantly.

The principle of reducing involves minimizing the number of resources used and waste generated in the first place. For example; Companies reduce packaging materials to decrease waste; and use energy-efficient appliances and machinery. The principle of reusing involves using items more than once, either for the same purpose or for a different function, to extend their lifecycle. Let us say, reusable shopping bags, water bottles, and containers; converting and refurbishing and reselling used electronics and the principle of recycling involves the processing of used materials into new products to prevent the waste of potentially usable materials. For example, recycling paper products into new paper items; and converting used plastics into new plastic products or alternative materials.
