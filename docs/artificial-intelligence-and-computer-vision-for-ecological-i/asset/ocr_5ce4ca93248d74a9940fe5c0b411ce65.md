# Evolution of AI

Since the mid-1900s, when artificial intelligence first emerged, a lot has changed. The initial motivation behind AI development was the development of algorithms designed to address certain challenges. Large datasets are now readily available, processing power has increased, and algorithm design innovations have made artificial intelligence (AI) a game-changing technology.

# Key Milestones in AI Evolution:

+   - **1950s:** Alan Turing proposes the concept of a machine that can simulate any human intelligence.
+   - **1960s–1970s:** Development of early AI programs like ELIZA and Shakey the Robot.
+   - **1980s:** Introduction of neural networks and expert systems.
+   - **2000s:** Rise of big data and significant improvements in ML and DL algorithms.
+   - **2010s:** AI applications become mainstream with advancements in image and speech recognition (Russell and Norvig, 2020).

# Types of AI

AI is broadly classified into three types based on its capabilities as shown in Table 7:

+   > **Narrow AI (Weak AI):** Designed to perform a narrow task, such as facial recognition or internet searches. It operates under a limited set of constraints and is currently the most common form of AI.
+   >
+   > **General AI (Strong AI):** Possesses the ability to understand, learn, and apply intelligence across a wide range of tasks at a level comparable to human beings. General AI is still theoretical and has not been realized.
+   >
+   > **Super-intelligent AI:** An AI that surpasses human intelligence across all fields. This type of AI is also theoretical and involves significant ethical and existential considerations.

**Table 7.** Types of Artificial Intelligence (Goodfellow et al., 2016).

| Type | Description | Example Applications |
|---|---|---|
| Narrow AI (Weak AI) | Performs specific tasks within a limited scope | Facial recognition, spam filtering |
| General AI (Strong AI) | Exhibits human-like intelligence across diverse tasks | Theoretical, not yet realized |
| Super-intelligent AI | Surpasses human intelligence in all aspects | Theoretical, involves ethical considerations |
