# # Economic Benefits of AI and ML in Crop Yield Estimation

The amalgamation of AI and ML technologies in agriculture, particularly in crop yield estimation, has engendered a myriad of economic benefits. These advancements facilitate precise and predictive analytics that can significantly enhance agricultural productivity and sustainability.

# ## Improved Efficiency and Resource Management

One of the primary economic assists of utilizing AI and ML in crop yield estimation is the substantial improvement in efficiency and resource management. The deployment of advanced algorithms and predictive models enables farmers to optimize their use of inputs and streamline agricultural processes.

Data-Driven Insights for Targeted Fertilizer and Water Application AI and ML technologies provide granular data-driven insights that are instrumental in the precise application of fertilizers and water. Traditional methods of applying fertilizers and irrigation often result in inefficiencies due to the lack of specificity regarding the needs of different crop areas. In contrast, very precise predictions of crop requirements are produced by AI and ML by utilizing information from a variety of sources, including as weather forecasts, satellite imaging, and soil sensors (Benos et al., 2021).

These insights allow for targeted application of resources, ensuring that each crop receives the optimal amount of nutrients and hydration. This precision not only enhances crop yields but also minimizes the excessive use of fertilizers and water, thereby conserving resources and reducing costs. For instance, machine learning models can predict the nutrient status of the soil and recommend site-specific fertilizer application, which can lead to a 30–50% reduction in fertilizer usage without compromising crop yields (Nishad et al., 2024).
