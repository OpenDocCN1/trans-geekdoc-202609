# Future Aspects

Enhanced Sensor Networks and IoT Integration: Using Internet of Things (IoT) devices and advanced sensor networks can greatly enhance data collection efforts. These sensors can monitor water quality indicators in real time and at high resolution, providing AI models with continuous data streams. Water quality assessment systems will become more dynamic and responsive with the combination of IoT, AI, and CV technologies.

*Fabrication of Hybrid Models:* Predicting water quality can be made more accurate and reliable by fusing data-driven AI techniques with conventional mechanistic models. Hybrid models offer a more thorough knowledge of intricate environmental processes by combining the advantages of both approaches. This method can make models easier to understand and make it easier to incorporate domain knowledge into AI systems.

*Developments in Edge Computing:* By processing data closer to the source instead of depending on centralized cloud servers, edge computing can improve the effectiveness of water quality monitoring systems. AI algorithms can be deployed on edge devices, including sensors and drones, to do real-time analysis locally, which lowers latency and bandwidth needs. This will make it possible to make decisions more quickly and to act more quickly.

*Including Citizen Science:* Using citizen science programmes to involve the public in water quality monitoring can increase data collection and increase public knowledge of environmental issues. Communities can provide useful data by using inexpensive sensors and mobile apps, and this data can then be included in AI models. In addition to improving data coverage, this participatory approach can instill a sense of ownership in the local populace.

*Support from Policies and Regulations:* Adequate policies and regulations are necessary for the effective application of AI and CV technologies in the evaluation of water quality. To guarantee data sharing among stakeholders, standardize data gathering techniques, and encourage the uptake of cutting-edge technologies, governments, and regulatory organizations can play a critical role. Effective policy frameworks that encourage creativity and teamwork will be essential for tackling problems with water quality.

*Ethical and Social Issues:* Taking ethical and social issues into account is crucial to the correct application of AI in the evaluation of water quality. Building trust and acceptance requires protecting data privacy, avoiding biases in AI models, and encouraging openness in decision-making procedures. Furthermore, it is imperative to guarantee that technological innovations yield benefits for all communities, particularly those that are marginalized and neglected.
