# Utilization of IoT in Improving Waste Management

The utilization of IoT technology significantly enhances the efficiency and effectiveness of waste management practices in urban areas by providing real-time monitoring and data-driven insights. The Internet of Things (IoT) is being used to improve Agro-waste management (Koshyaria, 2023). Sensors facilitate monitoring temperature, moisture, and pH levels in compost piles, detecting gas emissions from manure. Provides real-time data, enhances process control, and improves safety and compliance. Smart waste bins may be used to improve waste management by providing early automatic identification of waste at the bin level (Ali, 2020). Smart bins are furnished with sensors to detect fill levels, compact waste, and signal collection needs. Reduces collection frequency and costs, and improves efficiency in waste management. Moreover, remote monitoring through tracking the performance of waste treatment facilities, and monitoring environmental impact facilitates timely maintenance, ensures regulatory compliance, and enhances decision-making.

# Comparative Economic Analysis Between Traditional and AI-based Tools

AI-based tools in Agro-waste management offer significant economic and operational advantages over traditional methods. While the initial investment is higher, the extended benefits for the long term such as reduced operational costs, increased efficiency, and lower environmental impact make AI-based tools a more sustainable and profitable choice. The ability to scale operations, adapt to market changes, and achieve higher quality outputs further strengthens the case for AI integration in Agro-waste management.

Table 3. Summary comparative of traditional and AI-based tools.

| Particulars | Traditional Methods | AI-based Tools |
|---|---|---|
| Initial Investment | Generally lower upfront costs but may require frequent maintenance and manual labor. | Higher initial costs due to technology implementation but offer long-term savings. |
| Operational Costs | Higher due to manual labor and less efficient processes. | Lower due to automation, optimization, and predictive maintenance. |
| Waste Reduction | Moderate waste reduction based on manual sorting and processing. | Significant waste reduction through precise and optimized AI algorithms. |
| Labor Requirements | High labor requirement for manual operations. | Reduced labor requirements due to automation and machine learning. |
| Processing Speed | Slower due to manual and semi-automated processes. | Faster due to real-time data processing and AI-driven optimizations. |
| Scalability | Limited scalability without significant labor increases. | High scalability with minimal additional costs. |
| Maintenance Costs | Regular maintenance is required for manual and semi-automated tools. | Lower maintenance costs due to predictive maintenance capabilities. |
