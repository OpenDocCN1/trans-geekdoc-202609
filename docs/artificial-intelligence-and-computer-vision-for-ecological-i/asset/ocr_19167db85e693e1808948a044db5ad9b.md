ROI is defined as the ratio of net profit to the entire cost of the investment. The formula for calculating ROI is:

$$ROI = \left(\frac{Net\ profit}{Cost\ of\ investment}\right) \times 100 \tag{1}$$

where:

+   - *Net Profit* is the difference between the gross revenue generated from the investment and the total cost of the investment.
+   - *Cost of Investment* includes all expenses incurred in the adoption and execution of AI and ML technologies.

# Factors Influencing ROI in Agriculture

Many factors influence the ROI of AI and ML technologies in agriculture:

+   - *Initial Investment Costs*: These include the cost of technology acquisition, installation, and training.
+   - *Operational Costs*: These encompass maintenance, data management, and system upgrades.
+   - *Increased Yield*: AI and ML can optimize planting schedules, irrigation, and pest control, prominent to higher crop productivity.
+   - *Resource Efficiency*: Reduction in the use of water, fertilizers, and pesticides due to precise application methods.
+   - *Market Prices*: Fluctuations in the prices of crops can affect the gross revenue, thus impacting the ROI.
+   - *Scalability*: The extent to which these technologies can be scaled to cover larger areas or different types of crops (Attri et al., 2023).

# Potential Economic Benefits

The adoption of AI and ML in agriculture can lead to several economic benefits:

+   - *Enhanced Productivity*: AI-driven insights can optimize various farming practices, leading to increased productivity and higher yields.
+   - *Cost Savings*: Enhanced resource management can result in important cost savings in inputs such as water, fertilizers, and pesticides.
+   - *Risk Mitigation*: Predictive analytics can help in early detection of crop diseases and pest invasions, reducing potential losses.
+   - *Market Competitiveness*: Farmers leveraging advanced technologies can be more competitive in the market due to higher quality and more consistent yields.
