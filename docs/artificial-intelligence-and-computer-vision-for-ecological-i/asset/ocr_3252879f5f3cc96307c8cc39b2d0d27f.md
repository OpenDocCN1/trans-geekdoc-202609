SVM’s effectiveness is attributed to its stability in optimizing model parameters and setting an upper bound on generalization error, minimizing errors more effectively than ANN (Kim, 2003). Real-time monitoring or predictive modeling is crucial for managing river systems effectively, with Bootstrapped Wavelet Neural Networks (BWNN) and Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) networks demonstrating proficiency in handling variable and non-seasonal time-series data on water quality (Zhi et al., 2021). Machine learning techniques including Support Vector Machines (SVM), Decision Trees (DT), Random Forests (RF), and Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) have been widely used in groundwater quality assessments in recent years. A comparative study of five algorithms for data mining—the typical DT, RF, chi-square automatic interaction detector, and iterative dichotomizer 3—was carried out to determine the critical factors affecting the quality of groundwater in semiarid regions and how they affect high-quality groundwater areas in Tabriz City, Iran (Jeihouni et al., 2020). Moreover, the groundwater quality maps that are produced via the application of geographic information system techniques are a typical way to identify groundwater contamination with greater accuracy (Lee et al., 2019). Machine learning is essential to wastewater treatment because it helps with the prediction, operation, and administration of wastewater treatment facilities, technological optimization, and monitoring of water quality. Before treatment, a water quality evaluation is required for both industrial and household wastewater due to the variety of contaminants present (Nishat et al., 2023). Soft sensors built on black box models provide real-time online monitoring, which improves the monitoring of indicators such as E. coli (Foschi et al., 2021). Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) and soft sensors work together to overcome maintenance and operating issues in wastewater treatment facilities, allowing for real-time ammonia and chlorine monitoring. Numerous factors affect the effluent quality of wastewater treatment plants, and machine learning helps with maintenance and operation management. While deep learning improves measurement accuracy in flow-measurement sensors, XGBoost and RF detect contaminants and their source spots in wastewater networks (Buras and Solano Donado, 2021). Machine learning applications in wastewater treatment facilities have chances to save expenses and enhance overall performance (Wang et al., 2021).
