# Conclusion

The cost associated with implementing deep learning algorithms has a significant impact on the speed and efficiency of the training process. The use of artificial intelligence (AI), remote sensing and the Internet of Things (IoT) are transforming the evaluation of soil health, enhancing its accuracy, efficiency, and comprehensiveness. Although there are challenges in making information more available and combining data, these advancements have significant potential for enhancing agricultural production and sustainability. More research is needed to fully utilize the capabilities of AI in the field of soil science. Future research should prioritize the development of durable and scalable artificial intelligence algorithms capable of managing large amounts of complex soil nutrient data. Additionally, there should be an emphasis on integrating AI with other technologies like sensor networks and remote sensing.
