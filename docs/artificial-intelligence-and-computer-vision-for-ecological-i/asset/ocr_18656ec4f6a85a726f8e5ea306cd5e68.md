+   b. Deep Learning (Murphy, 2012):
+     - An area of machine learning that applies multilayered deep neural networks. It is quite good at jobs like speech and picture recognition. For sequential data, Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and their derivatives (such as LSTMs) are utilized, whilst Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) are employed for picture data.
+   
+   c. Natural Language Processing (NLP):
+     - Involves communication between human (natural) languages and computers. Text summarization, sentiment analysis, and machine translation are important tasks. NLP techniques have advanced significantly with models like GPT (Generative Pre-trained Transformer) and BERT (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers).
+   
+   d. Computer Vision:
+     - Makes it possible for robots to comprehend and interpret the visual environment. Among the tasks are picture segmentation, facial recognition, and object identification. Computer vision relies heavily on technologies such as Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) and CNNs.
+   
+   e. Robotics:
+     - Combines mechanical engineering and artificial intelligence to create robots that can operate either entirely or partially on their own. Robotics applications include surgical robots, personal assistants, and industrial automation.
+   
+   f. Expert Systems:
+     - Artificial intelligence (AI) systems which simulate human experts’ decision-making processes. They control certain problems, frequently in industries like banking, medical, and customer service, by utilizing an inference engine and a knowledge base of human experiences.

# Applications of AI in Various Fields

+   i. Healthcare:
+     - Diagnostics: AI systems are capable of analyzing medical visual imagery, such as MRIs and X-rays, to identify diseases like cancer early on.
+     - Personalized Treatment: AI can help tailor treatments based on individual patient data, leading to more effective care.
+     - Drug Discovery: AI improves the search for new medications by forecasting the behavior of various components.
+   
+   ii. Finance:
+     - Fraud Detection: Artificial intelligence algorithms can identify odd trends and highlight possible fraudulent transactions.
+     - Algorithmic Trading: Artificial Intelligence systems can examine a huge amount of data and make trading decisions faster than humans can.
+     - Personal Finance Management: AI-driven apps can help individuals manage their finances by analyzing spending habits and suggesting savings strategies.
