To prevent worms, pests, and insects and prolong crop life, drone commanders have complete control over the drone spraying nutrients. In south-east Asia, the use of drones to apply spray treatments is already pervasive; South Korea uses drones for over 30% of its agricultural spraying. Drone sprayers can maneuver across highly challenging terrain, such as steep tea estates at great altitudes. Drone sprayers spare workers from carrying backpack sprayers through fields, which can be harmful to their health. To maximize productivity and reduce chemical costs, drone sprayers offer very tiny spray treatments that can be targeted to specific locations. Currently, laws governing drone sprayers vary greatly from nation to nation (Radoglou-Grammatikis et al., 2020).

## Soil and Field Analysis
The condition of the field and the health of the soil can both be monitored via drone field monitoring. Drones can accurately map the field, providing elevation data that enables producers to identify any field abnormalities. The ability to determine drainage patterns and wet/dry patches, which enable more effective watering practices, is facilitated by knowledge of field elevation. Utilizing improved sensors, some sellers and service providers of agricultural drones also provide soil nitrogen level monitoring services. This makes it possible to apply fertilizers precisely, removing troublesome growth areas and enhancing soil health for years to come. Powerful drone capabilities help with the time-consuming procedure of evaluating the soil’s health. The character and health of the soil may be monitored, modified, and maintained using drones, which collect and analyze data from monitoring systems. The soil can benefit from drone technology by receiving the nutrients it needs to become healthier. Drones use their data processing and 3D mapping capabilities to carry out this mission of monitoring the condition of the soil. Farmers can learn more about the soil characteristics of their land thanks to the drone survey. In-depth field soil analysis, irrigation, nitrogen-level management, and seed planting patterns can all benefit from data captured by multispectral sensors. Farmers can completely assess their soil conditions thanks to precise photogrammetry and 3D mapping (Olson and Anderson, 2021; Cucho-Padin, 2020).

## Irrigation Monitoring
Plant health monitoring is one application for drone imaging that has already been widely used with remarkable effectiveness. The Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI), a specialized imaging tool used by drones, uses precise color data to assess the health of plants. This enables farmers to keep an eye on crops as they develop so that any issues can be resolved quickly enough to save the plants. Simply said, this graphic demonstrates how NDVI functions. Drones with sensors equipped with hyperspectral, thermal, or multispectral technology can identify regions that are too dry or require farmer improvement. Drone survey provides calculations of the vegetation index for irrigation monitoring, which aids in realizing the health of crops and releasing heat/energy. This helps to increase water efficiency and reveal potential pooling/leaks in irrigation (Khan, 2020).

## Potential Pollinators
Drones are capable of acting as artificial pollinators. Drones may eventually overtake bees as the most crucial pollinators, even though insect vectors remain the most significant pollinators. Researchers are optimistic that drones will be able to transport and disperse pollen seeds in orchards and fields, despite the need for more research in this area (Khan, 2020; Brook et al., 2020).

## Agricultural Research
Intelligent approaches and high-end computing devices have offered academicians and researchers the opportunity to direct their study towards the beneficial aspects of the agricultural sector. Nowadays, it has been observed that, especially due to the low labor cost and efficient results, drone technology has been espoused in almost all applications of precision agriculture. Because the underlying technology is currently in its initial phase, it has numerous aspects to be improved and developed. Among them designing and offering a low-cost drone is the essential one. The sensors installed on drones also play an important role, they must be selected specific to the application. Overall, the hardware cost and easy installation process should be kept low to make drones affordable. A unique platform to counter various problems associated with the agriculture domain is a necessity, so research has to be directed toward the development of software for a platform offering solutions for all the applications (Khan, 2020; Brook et al., 2020; Osco et al., 2021; Gupta et al., 2016).

## Conclusion
Unmanned aerial vehicles have made a significant impact on the expansion of the agriculture industry. Their primary contribution consists of reducing labor engrossment, expenditure, and resource consumption efficiently. However, the adaptability of the underlying technology is limited within the range of a few farmers and domain knowledge experts. Several factors including security, privacy, economic, climatic, lack of understanding, trajectory planning, choice of sensors, etc., influence their adaptability. However, to sustain, enhance, and shelter the quality and productivity of the yield outcomes, the focus has to be directed toward the augmented practice of drone technology.

Several factors are responsible for influencing the health and productivity of crop yields. They can be categorized as biotic and abiotic. Timely, precise, and cost-effective mechanisms are a feasible solution to overcome these challenges. The advent of recent technologies more specifically, the accelerated development of drone technology combined with computer vision, deep learning, and high-end computing devices can offer promising solutions to handle the aforementioned threats.
