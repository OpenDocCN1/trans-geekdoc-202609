# Need for Drones

Drones have been widely espoused in Precision Agriculture (PA). They are capable of offering solutions to many problems that a farmer experiences on and off the field. These applications and their valuable impact have been extensively observed in the past few years. Because of this productivity, efficiency, and profitability in the agriculture sector have been boosted. Drones in agribusiness save time, help troubleshoot potential problems and offer better data and cost-saving solutions. Farm ranchers and other agriculture business owners have already started to adopt technological aspects for their assistance (van Wynsberghe and Comes, 2020; Boukoberine et al., 2019; Hassanalian and Abdelkefi, 2017; Jerhamre et al., 2022).
