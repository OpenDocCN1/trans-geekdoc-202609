# Assumptions:

+   - The farm produces a single type of crop.
+   - The market price for the crop is stable at $200 per ton.
+   - Operating costs include labour, equipment maintenance, seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, and water.

# Implementation of AI and ML:

The farm invests in AI and ML technologies for precision farming, which include:

+   - Soil health monitoring systems.
+   - Drone surveillance for crop health assessment.
+   - Predictive analytics for optimal planting and harvesting times.
+   - Automated irrigation systems.

# Benefits of AI and ML Implementation:

+   - **Increased Yield:** AI and ML technologies optimize resource use and crop management, leading to a 20% increase in yield per acre.
+   - **Cost Savings:** Precision farming reduces waste in water, fertilizers, and pesticides, cutting operating costs by 15%.
+   - **Enhanced Market Value:** Improved crop quality through better management increases the market price by 5%.
