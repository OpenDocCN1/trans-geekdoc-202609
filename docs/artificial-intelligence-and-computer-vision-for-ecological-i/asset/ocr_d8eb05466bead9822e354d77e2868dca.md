# Table 3. Applications in Satellite-based Remote Sensing and UAV Imagery.

| Aspect | Satellite-Based Remote Sensing | UAV (Drone) Imagery | Integration of Satellite and UAV Imagery | Applications |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Multispectral Imagery | Analyzes reflectance across different spectral bands to determine soil moisture. | High-resolution cameras detect fine changes in soil moisture. | Combines broad-scale data with fine-scale UAV data for comprehensive understanding. | Agriculture: Irrigation scheduling, crop yield predictions, optimizing water use. |
| Hyperspectral Imagery | Offers finer spectral resolution, detecting minor variations in soil properties and moisture. | Flexible data collection supports targeted soil moisture mapping. | Supports informed decision-making in agriculture, hydrology, environmental management, and climate change adaptation. | Hydrology: Flood forecasting, drought monitoring, water resource management. |
| Thermal Imagery | Analyses surface temperature distributions to estimate soil moisture, combines with other data for accuracy. | UAVs offer dynamic monitoring at specific times and locations. | Allows comprehensive analysis by combining multispectral, hyperspectral, and thermal data. | Assessing ecosystem health, biodiversity conservation, land restoration. |
| Data Acquisition | Provides comprehensive data on soil moisture dynamics across large areas. | UAVs with high-resolution sensors facilitate fine-scale soil moisture mapping. | Enhances soil moisture estimation accuracy with a combined approach. | Climate resilience, vulnerability assessments, adaptation planning. |
| Flight Planning | Captures data at varying spatial and temporal resolutions. | UAVs offer greater flexibility in flight planning and data acquisition. | Supports adaptive management in various fields with detailed local-scale data. | Enhances capabilities to address food security, water management, environmental conservation, and climate adaptation. |
