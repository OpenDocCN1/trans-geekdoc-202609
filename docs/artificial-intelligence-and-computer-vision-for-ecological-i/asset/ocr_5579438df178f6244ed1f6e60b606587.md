**Example:** A wheat farm has decided to implement an AI-based crop yield estimation system.

# Description

+   - *Farm Size:* 100 hectares
+   - *Initial Investment:* $50,000 (for AI system installation and training)
+   - *Annual Operational Costs:* $10,000 (maintenance, data management, etc.)
+   - *Expected Increase in Yield:* 15% due to optimized farming practices
+   - *Current Yield:* 4 tons per hectare
+   - *Market Price of Wheat:* $200 per ton

# Calculation of ROI

**Table 5.** Current revenue and costs (without AI and ML).

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Yield per hectare | 4 tons |
| Total yield | 400 tons (100 hectares) |
| Revenue | $80,000 (400 tons $200) |
| Operational costs | $20,000 |
| Net profit | $60,000 |

**Source:** Author’s calculation.

**Table 6.** Projected revenue and costs (with AI and ML)

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Increased yield | 15% |
| New yield per hectare | 4.6 tons |
| Total yield | 460 tons (100 hectares) |
| Revenue | $92,000 (460 tons $200) |
| Initial investment | $50,000 |
| Annual operational costs | $10,000 |
| Total costs | $60,000 |
| Net profit | $32,000 ($92,000 − $60,000) |

**Source:** Author’s calculation.

**Table 7.** ROI calculation.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Net profit (with AI and ML) | $32,000 |
