# Cost-Benefit Analysis:

+   1. *Initial Investment:*
+      - Total initial cost for AI and ML integration: $140,000.
+   2. *Annual Financials (Pre-AI and ML):*
+      - Total Revenue: $400,000.
+      - Total Operating Costs: $300,000.
+      - Net Profit: $100,000.
+   3. *Annual Financials (Post-AI and ML):*
+      - Total Revenue: $504,000.
+      - Total Operating Costs: $269,000 (including AI and ML maintenance).
+      - Net Profit: $235,000.

Table 4. Comparative analysis (annual figures).

| Category | Pre-AI and ML ($) | Post-AI and ML ($) | Change ($) | Change (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Source: Author’s calculation.

Payback Period: The initial investment of $140,000 is recouped by the additional profit of $135,000 within approximately 1.04 years (140,000/135,000).

The above example shows significant economic benefits from integrating AI and ML into agricultural practices. The farm experiences a substantial increase in revenue and net profit, alongside a reduction in operating costs. These improvements result from enhanced yield efficiency, optimized resource use, and higher crop quality. The payback period of just over one year underscores the financial viability of AI and ML investments in agriculture. Moreover, the long-term gains, including sustained higher yields and ongoing cost savings, present a compelling case for widespread adoption of these tools in the farming sector. The economic implications of AI and ML adoption in agriculture are profound, offering a transformative impact on productivity, cost efficiency, and profitability. This example provides a clear view of how these technologies can drive substantial economic benefits, justifying the initial investment and ongoing maintenance costs (Liakos et al., 2018).

# Return on Investment (ROI)

ROI is a crucial financial indicator that’s used to assess how profitable an investment is. In the perspective of AI and ML in agriculture, ROI measures the financial gains obtained from these technologies relative to their costs. The calculation of ROI helps farmers and agricultural stakeholders to determine whether the execution of AI and ML technologies in crop yield estimation is financially viable.
