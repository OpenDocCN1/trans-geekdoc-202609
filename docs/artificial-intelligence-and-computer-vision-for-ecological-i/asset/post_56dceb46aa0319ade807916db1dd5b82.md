## Impact of AI on Waste-to-Value Chain Management

AI technology has transformative potential for waste-to-value chains, significantly enhancing the efficiency, effectiveness, and economic viability of converting waste materials into valuable resources (European Commission, 2020).

## Value Creation, Delivery Mechanisms, and Capture Strategies

In the context of the circular economy (CE), value creation, delivery mechanisms, and capture strategies are crucial components that enable businesses to generate, deliver, and sustain value while maintaining resource efficiency and minimizing waste.
