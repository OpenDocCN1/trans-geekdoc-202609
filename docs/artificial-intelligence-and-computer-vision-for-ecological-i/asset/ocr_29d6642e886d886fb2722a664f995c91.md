# Introduction

The circular economy strives to reduce waste and resource use efficiently, transforming from the traditional linear model of production that follows a ‘take-make-dispose’ approach (Edirisinghe, 2024). The circular economy represents a shift towards a more sustainable approach. In contrast, the circular economy is characterized by its restorative and regenerative design, aiming to close the loops of product lifecycles through increased resource efficiency and sustainability (Kirchherr, 2017).

The existing linear economic model is unsustainable, promoting the unjust use of finite natural resources and generating excessive waste, which severely harms the environment. In contrast, a circular economy (CE) offers a restorative, sustainable, and regenerative alternative and is gaining global traction. Among all the existing digital technologies, artificial intelligence (AI) is a key promoter for CE, significantly facilitating its adoption and implementation in practical applications. This chapter explores the intersection of AI and CE and discusses policies for implementing CE principles using AI.
