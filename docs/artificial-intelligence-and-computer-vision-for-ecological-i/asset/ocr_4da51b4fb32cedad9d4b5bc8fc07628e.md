# Scalability

Compared to ground-based techniques, UAVs efficiently cover huge regions in a short amount of time, which makes them perfect for expansive fields.

# Cost-Effectiveness

UAVs are a more economical option for data acquisition when compared to manned aircraft.

# Flexibility

Their capacity to function at low altitudes makes it possible to gather comprehensive data that offers insightful information about crop health.

# Richness of Data

UAVs may obtain multispectral and high-resolution photos, providing a complete picture of crop conditions.

# NDVI Need for Crop Yield Estimation

Several important aspects make fast and accurate crop output estimation necessary as depicted in Fig. 4.

Food Security: Increasing agricultural output is necessary to feed the world’s expanding population (Nasi, 2018). Planning and resource allocation can be improved by estimating agricultural yields, providing food security for present and future generations.

Predicting the Market: Market pricing techniques are informed by precise yield projections. Farmers can minimize losses and maximize profits by anticipating possible shortages or surpluses and modifying their selling strategy accordingly.

Agricultural Administration: Accurate yield predictions enable informed decision making about the use of fertilizer, irrigation techniques, and pest management
