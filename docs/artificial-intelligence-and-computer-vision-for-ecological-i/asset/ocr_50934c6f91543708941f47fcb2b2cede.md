# The NDVI’s Power in Crop Yield Estimation

For farmers as well as other agricultural stakeholders, accurate crop yield estimation is essential. Finally, it fosters market stability, enhances the ability to make well-informed decisions, and improves global food security (Chouhan, 2021). Conventional techniques frequently fall short because they are not precise, timely, or scalable. This is where the usefulness of the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI) becomes apparent.

One significant benefit of NDVI is that it’s an objective, straightforward metric. The NDVI offers a consistent method for evaluating crop growth and health since it is based on the reflectance characteristics of vegetation at particular wavelengths. Rich in chlorophyll, healthy plant reflects more near-infrared (NIR) light and absorbs more visible light. As a result, the NDVI value rises, signifying a robust association with multiple metrics gauging crop health and productivity, such as biomass, leaf area index (LAI), and ultimately, the farmer’s harvest.
