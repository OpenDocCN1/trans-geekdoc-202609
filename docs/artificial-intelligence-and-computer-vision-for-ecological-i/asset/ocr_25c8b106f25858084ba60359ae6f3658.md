# Integration with the Internet of Things (IoT) and Smart Farming

Integrating RS with the Internet of Things (IoT) and intelligent farming technologies revolutionizes agricultural practices (Dhal et al., 2024; Kalinaki et al., 2024b). IoT-enabled sensors deployed in fields can collect real-time data on soil moisture, temperature, and other environmental parameters combined with RS data for comprehensive monitoring and decision-making (Rajak et al., 2023). Smart farming systems can leverage this integrated data to optimize irrigation schedules, apply targeted pest and disease management strategies, and precisely allocate resources based on crop requirements. The seamless exchange of information between RS platforms, IoT devices, and farm management systems will enable data-driven precision agriculture practices.

# Big Data Analytics and Cloud Computing

The increasing volume and complexity of RS data for agriculture necessitate the adoption of big data analytics and cloud computing solutions (Kalinaki et al., 2024c; Rao et al., 2023). RS platforms generate massive amounts of data, which require robust storage, processing, and analysis capabilities. Cloud computing infrastructures offer scalable and on-demand computing resources, enabling efficient data processing, analysis, and storage. Additionally, cloud-based platforms facilitate data sharing and collaboration among researchers, extension services, and farmers, enabling the development of decision-support tools and knowledge-sharing platforms (Kalinaki et al., 2024d; Rao et al., 2023).

# ML and AI Advancements

ML and AI (AI) techniques are increasingly important RS for agriculture (Kalinaki et al., 2024b). These advanced algorithms can be trained on large datasets to automatically identify crop types, detect anomalies, classify land cover, and accurately predict crop yields. DL models, in particular, have demonstrated remarkable performance in image classification, object detection, and pattern recognition tasks, enabling more accurate and efficient analysis of RS data (Kalinaki et al., 2023). Integrating AI with RS data will enable real-time monitoring, early detection of stress conditions, and predictive analytics for optimized crop management.

# Policy and Regulatory Considerations

As RS technologies become more prevalent in agriculture, policy and regulatory considerations will be crucial in their adoption and implementation. Data privacy, ownership, and security issues must be addressed to ensure RS data's responsible and ethical use. Policies and guidelines for data sharing, interoperability standards, and data governance will facilitate collaboration and integration of RS with other agricultural technologies. Additionally, regulatory frameworks may be needed to ensure the responsible use of drones and UAVs in agrarian settings, addressing concerns related to safety, privacy, and environmental impact (Mohsan et al., 2023).
