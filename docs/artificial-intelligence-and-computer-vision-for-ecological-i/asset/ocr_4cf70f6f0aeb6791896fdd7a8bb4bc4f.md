From assessing soil erosion and nutrient runoff to monitoring deforestation and habitat loss, remote sensing offers valuable insights into the ecological footprint of agriculture (Turner et al., 2015). By combining environmental monitoring with agricultural management strategies, stakeholders can work towards enhancing productivity through improved land management practices while safeguarding natural environmental resources.

Additionally, by identifying knowledge gaps and emerging trends, this review will guide future research efforts aimed at harnessing the full potential of remote sensing technologies to support sustainable and resilient agricultural systems.

# RS Platforms

The platforms employed in remote sensing, such as aircraft or satellites, are referred to as vehicles capable of transporting sensing devices, known as sensors, to conduct measurements from distant locations (Jafarbiglu and Pourreza, 2022). These platforms are constantly getting better in terms of operational speed, consistency, ease of use, and temporal resolution, which ultimately determines spatial resolution. As per Huang et al.
