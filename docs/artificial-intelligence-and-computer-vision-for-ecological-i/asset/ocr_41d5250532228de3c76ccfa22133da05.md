The chatbots are equipped with natural language processing (NLP) capabilities and machine learning algorithms, enabling them to understand and process agricultural terminology effectively. These chatbots are programmed to understand agricultural terminology and provide accurate information tailored to farmers’ needs (Singh et al., 2019; Jain, 2020). By leveraging these technologies, the chatbots provide precise, context-aware responses and personalized recommendations. Furthermore, the chatbots are integrated with extensive agricultural knowledge bases and databases, ensuring that the information provided is accurate, reliable, and up-to-date. The FCB model strives to empower farmers by making agricultural knowledge easily accessible and improving decision-making in farming practices.

By utilizing chatbots, the Farmer ChatBot (FCB) framework aims to enhance accessibility to agricultural information and advisory services, especially in rural areas where access to traditional support systems may be limited. Chatbots offer farmers a convenient and efficient means of obtaining timely assistance and guidance, thereby empowering them to make informed decisions and improve agricultural practices.
