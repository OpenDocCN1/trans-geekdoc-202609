# Case Studies

This section presents selected real-world applications of RS in bettering agriculture.

# Precision Agriculture in the United States of America

In the Midwest of the United States, a large corn farm executed accurate agriculture methods, using RS and general practitioner modern technology like AI to ensure precision fertilization. Multispectral imaging from drones was used to create comprehensive NDVI maps, highlighting variations in crop vitality throughout the areas (Kumar et al., 2024a; Tey et al., 2024). These maps determined locations with reduced NDVI worth, suggesting nutrient deficiencies. After that, the farm utilized VRT to apply fertilizers differentially based on the NDVI data. This strategy caused an over 15% boost in corn yield, an over 20% reduction in fertilizer usage, and lessened nutrient overflow, thus lowering environmental pollution (Singh et al., 2022). Integrating RS and VRT substantially increased productivity and environmental sustainability.

# Drought Monitoring and Management in India

In the drought-prone region of Maharashtra, India, RS up was employed to check and manage water sources better. Satellite thermal imaging offered data on land surface temperatures, plant water stress, and anxiety, while SAR was used to gauge the degree of dirt dampness (Kumar et al., 2024b). This data was integrated into a central data source available to local farmers and farming agencies to ensure drought monitoring and water management. The combination of thermal and SAR data permitted the advancement of an identical early warning system for dry spell problems, allowing farmers to apply water-saving actions (Chuphal et al., 2024). Optimized irrigation schedules, notified by dirt moisture maps, preserved water sources, and lowered plant losses by 30% throughout dry spell durations, enhancing strength, optimized irrigation, and improved crop resilience and sustainability.
