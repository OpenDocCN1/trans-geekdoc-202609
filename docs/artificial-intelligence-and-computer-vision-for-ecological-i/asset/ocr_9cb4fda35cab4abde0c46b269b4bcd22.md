# **Table 1.** Summary of the challenges and proposed considerations.

| Challenge | Summary of Challenge | Summary of Solution | References |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data Availability and Accessibility | Cloud cover, atmospheric conditions, sensor limitations, data sharing policies, and data discovery mechanisms can limit access to high-quality RS data for agricultural applications. | Improve data sharing policies, enhance data discovery tools, and develop advanced atmospheric correction and data fusion techniques. | (Jafarbiglu and Pourreza, 2022; Li et al., 2021; Martos et al., 2021; Wood et al., 2024) |
| Spatial and Temporal Resolution Limitations | Balancing high spatial resolution for detailed crop monitoring and frequent temporal resolution for tracking crop growth and conditions can be challenging. | Develop multi-sensor fusion techniques, leverage UAVs (UAVs), or explore new satellite constellations to improve resolution. | (Li et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2023) |
| Validation and Accuracy Assessment | Ground-truthing, vegetation heterogeneity, sensor calibration, and atmospheric effects can introduce uncertainties and challenges in validating agricultural RS data. | Develop standardized validation protocols, robust calibration techniques, and leverage complementary data sources for accuracy assessment. | (Bennion et al., 2019; Han et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2021) |
| Cost and Resource Constraints | Acquiring high-resolution imagery, advanced products, specialized software, hardware, and skilled personnel can be costly and resource-intensive. | Explore cost-effective open-source solutions, leverage cloud computing, and invest in capacity building and training programs. | (Kalinaki et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023) |
| Technical and Operational Challenges | Data processing, analysis, integration with other data sources, automation, and timely dissemination of information pose technical and operational challenges. | Develop efficient data processing pipelines, implement robust data fusion techniques, and establish effective stakeholder communication channels. | (Ahmad et al., 2022; Hariri and Goli, 2024; Kalinaki et al., 2023) |
| Scalability and Generalization | RS techniques developed for specific regions or crop types may not be easily scalable or generalizable to other areas or crops due to diversity in farming practices, environmental conditions, and crop characteristics. | Develop adaptable and transferable algorithms, leverage ML techniques, and incorporate local knowledge and data sources. | (Barbosa Júnior et al., 2024; Rušňák et al., 2023) |
