# Introduction

Remote Sensing (RS) has revolutionized various fields, including agriculture, by providing unprecedented insights and data critical for modern farming practices (Zhang et al., 2023a). Using RS in agriculture involves gathering and processing data from spaceborne platforms (satellites), airborne platforms (aircraft) and ground-based platforms – unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), to monitor and manage agricultural activities. This approach allows for the detailed observation of large farmland areas, enabling the assessment of crop health, soil conditions, and environmental factors that affect agricultural productivity (Kluger et al., 2021).
