# Introduction

Soil fertility evaluation is the assessment of the soil’s capacity to provide essential nutrients to plants. Assessing the fertility of the soil is a crucial component of an efficient agricultural system. Through comprehending the soil’s nutrient condition, farmers and agronomists may make well-informed choices to boost soil health, optimize crop yields, and assure the implementation of sustainable farming techniques (Salomon and Cavagnaro, 2022). Conducting regular soil testing and implementing suitable management actions are crucial for preserving productive and healthy soils. The basis for assessing soil fertility is on a thorough comprehension of soil characteristics and their impact on plant development (Guo et al., 2021). Soil fertility evaluation involves five primary procedures. Firstly, it examines the indications of nutrient deficiency in plants. Additionally, the second way entails conducting plant and tissue tests, while the third method focuses on cultivating higher plants and microbes. The fourth approach involves soil chemical analysis, while the last technique utilizes isotopic dilution techniques.
