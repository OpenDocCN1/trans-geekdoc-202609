# ### Introduction

Life on earth cannot exist without water. It is a finite supply and an incredibly valuable natural asset. Although the earth’s surface is predominantly composed of water, only a small portion of it is suitable for use making this resource highly scarce. Approximately 1.1 billion people across the globe do not have access to clean drinking water, and it is anticipated that nearly two-thirds of all nations will face water stress by 2025 (Ross Michael, 2016).
