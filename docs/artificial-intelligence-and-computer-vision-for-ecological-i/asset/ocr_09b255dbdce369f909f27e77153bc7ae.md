# Introduction

In recent years, applying deep learning models in agriculture has gained significant attention due to their potential to revolutionize farming practices. Precision farming maximizes crop yields while mitigating environmental repercussions and increasing economic returns by efficiently managing agricultural resources. Accurate detection and monitoring of crop health and growth are crucial for its success. Basil (Ocimum basilicum) is a widely cultivated herb with diverse culinary and medicinal uses (Sipos et al., 2021; Joshi and Bhavsar, 2023). The successful development and yield of basil plants depend on various factors, including adequate irrigation, nutrient supply, and pest management. Monitoring basil crops’ health and growth stages is crucial for optimizing these factors and ensuring a successful harvest. Precision agriculture has become increasingly vital in modern farming practices to ensure optimal crop management, resource allocation, and sustainability (Abbasi et al., 2023; Farooqui et al., 2023). Traditional crop monitoring methods involve labor-intensive manual inspections or basic imaging techniques, which can be time-consuming and require more precision for effective decision-making (Chouhan et al., 2019a; Chouhan et al., 2019b).
