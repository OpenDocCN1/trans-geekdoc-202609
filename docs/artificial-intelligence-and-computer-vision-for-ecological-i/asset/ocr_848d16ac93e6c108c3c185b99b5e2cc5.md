In our study of basil crop detection with YOLOv8, we meticulously track box losses, classification losses, and deformable losses during training and validation. These loss metrics quantify various aspects of the model’s performance: box losses assess its ability to localize basil crops accurately, classification losses evaluate its capacity to distinguish basil crops from background elements, and deformable losses add spatial adaptability for diverse basil crop orientations. Monitoring these losses provides nuanced insights into the model’s precision and resilience, highlighting its suitability for precision agriculture. They are fundamental pillars in optimizing the YOLOv8 model for basil crop detection, refinement, and enhancing practical viability in real-world scenarios.
