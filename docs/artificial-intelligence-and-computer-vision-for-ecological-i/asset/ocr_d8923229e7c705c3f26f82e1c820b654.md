# Introduction

Soil is an essential component of life, serving as a reservoir for the nutrients and water that support us. Air, water, and life can all be found in the complex combination of minerals and organic components that makes up healthy soil. Soil moisture is an important component of the climate system. It is also known as soil water content and refers to the total amount of water, including water vapour, in the soil’s active layer. Depending on the kind of soil and vegetation, the total thickness of this active layer varies, although it is usually in the first 1–2 meters (Robock et al., 2000). In addition to being an important factor in agricultural water security, soil moisture is a reliable indicator of crop output in the future (Tian et al., 2019).
