# AI and ML Models for Crop Yield Estimation

# Regression Models

Regression models are a fundamental class of machine learning algorithms used for predicting continuous outcomes, such as crop yields. These models are particularly useful in crop yield estimation as they can efficiently capture the complex associations between various environmental, and management factors that influence crop yields.

# Simple Linear Regression

Regression models that incorporate a single predictor variable are categorized as simple linear regressions. The predictor variable (crop yield) and the responder variable (a linear connection) are assumed by the model. Simple linear regression may be expressed using the formula:

ŷ = β₀ + β₁x
(2)

where ŷ is the predicted crop yield, β₀ is the intercept, β₁ is the slope, and x is the predictor variable.
