# Biogas Production

It involves the anaerobic digestion of organic waste to produce methane-rich biogas. Similar to anaerobic digestion, organic waste is placed in a digester where it undergoes microbial decomposition to release biogas, which provides a renewable energy source, decreases greenhouse gas emissions, and reduces reliance on fossil fuels.

# Incineration and Pyrolysis

Incineration is the combustion of organic waste to produce energy, while pyrolysis involves thermal decomposition in the absence of oxygen. Agro-waste is burned at high temperatures in incineration or decomposed thermally in pyrolysis units. Reduces waste volume significantly, generates energy, and can produce by-products like ash or bio-oil.

In general, the benefits and advantages of Agro-waste processing and refining methods are believed to be cost-effective and eco-friendly, as well as it could contribute positively toward more sustainable waste management and a less-carbon-intensive energy utilization path (Hoang, 2023).

# Biomass Valorization

Nzihou (2010), defined valorization as the conversion of waste and biomass to energy, fuels and other useful materials, with a particular focus on environmental indicators and sustainability goals, i.e., the process of converting Agro-waste into valuable products like biofuels, chemicals, and materials. Organic materials such as food waste, grass, and manure are being converted into VFAs, carboxylic acids, bioenergy, and bioplastics to help minimize waste and foster a circular economy (Onwucha, 2023).

# AI-based Technologies for Agro-waste Conversion

Current developments in AI-based technologies for Agro-waste conversion have shown significant potential for sustainable agriculture by improving waste management and resource efficiency (Taneja, 2023). Machine Learning Algorithms are utilized in optimizing anaerobic digestion processes, predicting compost quality, and enhancing biochar production (Cruz, 2022). It improves efficiency, reduces operational costs, and increases yield and quality of end-products. Image Processing facilitates monitoring crop residue levels, and detecting contamination in waste streams thus, enabling precise and timely interventions, reducing waste and enhancing resource recovery. Predictive Analytics are applicable in forecasting waste generation patterns, and optimizing waste collection and processing schedules and therefore, enhance planning and resource allocation, and minimize waste management costs. AI plays an important role in addressing food waste issues by monitoring and optimizing food production and supply chains, redistributing excess food, and supporting circular economy initiatives. By leveraging AI in Agro-waste conversion, the agricultural sector can reduce waste, enhance sustainability, and contribute to a more efficient and equitable food system.
