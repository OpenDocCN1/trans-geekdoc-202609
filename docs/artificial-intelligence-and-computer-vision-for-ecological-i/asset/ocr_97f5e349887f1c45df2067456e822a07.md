# Introduction

Agriculture, the backbone of civilization, has undergone remarkable transformations over the centuries, driven by technological advancements aimed at enhancing productivity, sustainability, and resilience. In the contemporary landscape, the convergence of data science and agriculture has sparked a new era of innovation, promising unprecedented insights into crop management, resource allocation, and decision-making processes. At the forefront of this revolution lies Federated Learning (FL), a decentralized machine learning paradigm poised to revolutionize agricultural practices and usher in a new era of precision farming.

The traditional approach to agricultural data analysis often entails centralizing vast datasets from multiple sources for analysis, presenting significant challenges related to data privacy, security, and scalability.
