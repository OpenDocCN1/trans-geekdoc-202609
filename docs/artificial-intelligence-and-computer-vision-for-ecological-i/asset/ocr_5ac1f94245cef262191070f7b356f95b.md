# RS Platforms

RS platforms are vehicles or devices that carry sensors and collect data. The leading platforms used in RS for agriculture are satellites, UAVs (UAVs), and aircraft (Okengwu et al., 2023). Satellites orbit the Earth and provide global coverage, making them ideal for monitoring large agricultural areas (Steven and Clark, 1990; Victor et al., 2024). UAVs are smaller, uncrewed aircraft that can fly at lower altitudes, providing higher-resolution imagery for smaller areas. Aircraft, including manned and unmanned options, are used for more specific or targeted data collection (Victor et al., 2024).

# *Types of Remote Sensing*

RS can be categorized into two main types: active and passive. Active RS involves signal transmission from the sensor to the target and measuring the reflected or backscattered signal (Ullo and Sinha, 2021). Examples include radar and lidar systems, often used for terrain mapping and vegetation analysis. Passive RS relies on detecting natural energy reflected or emitted from the Earth’s surface (Khanal et al., 2020). Passive sensors measure radiation intensity in different wavelengths, such as visible, infrared, and microwave. Examples include optical sensors, which capture reflected sunlight to create images, and thermal sensors, which detect emitted infrared radiation to assess temperature (Victor et al., 2024).
