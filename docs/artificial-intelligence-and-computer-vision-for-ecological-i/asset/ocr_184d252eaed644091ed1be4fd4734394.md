# Economic Viability of AI and ML Technologies

The economic viability of AI and ML technologies in agriculture depends on their ability to generate sustainable economic benefits while being affordable and accessible to farmers. Several factors contribute to this viability:

+   1. *Affordability:*
+      - *Cost of Technology:* The upfront and recurring costs of AI and ML technologies must be within the financial reach of farmers. This includes costs associated with hardware, software, data acquisition, and maintenance.
+      - *Financing Options:* Availability of favourable financing options, such as loans, grants, and subsidies, can make these technologies more affordable for small and medium-sized farmers.
+   2. *Scalability and Adaptability:*
+      - *Customizability:* AI and ML solutions should be adaptable to different types of crops, farming practices, and climatic conditions. Customizable solutions ensure broader applicability and effectiveness.
+      - *Scalability:* Technologies must be scalable to accommodate varying sizes of farming operations. Solutions that work for both small-scale and large-scale farms enhance economic viability.
