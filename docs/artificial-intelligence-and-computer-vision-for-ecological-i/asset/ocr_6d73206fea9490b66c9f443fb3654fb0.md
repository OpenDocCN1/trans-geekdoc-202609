# Introduction

Agriculture, a pivotal driver of economic development in nations such as India, is presently experiencing its fourth revolution, commonly referred to as Agriculture 4.0, due to swift technological advancements. Remote sensing (RS) technologies have revolutionized various fields, including agriculture, by offering crucial information about crop conditions, soil health, and environmental parameters influencing agricultural productivity. Additionally, RS techniques can be combined with advanced technologies like global positioning systems (GPS), the Internet of Things (IoT), and artificial intelligence (AI) to create powerful tools for the precise management of agricultural inputs.
