# Potential of AI Technologies in Revolutionizing Wastewater Treatment Processes

Taking into account the increasing need for sustainable systems and clean water globally, the Internet of Things (IoT) can transform conventional methods and tackle pressing issues in the domains of wastewater treatment and water quality prediction. The disruptive uses of smart IoT technologies, such as machine learning (ML) and artificial intelligence (AI) models, are explored in detail in this extensive essay. The successful application of an Internet of Things (IoT)-based automated water quality monitoring system that makes use of cloud computing and machine learning techniques to efficiently handle the aforementioned problems is one example. The Internet of Things (IoT) has been used to simulate, automate, and optimize several processes, including wastewater treatment applications, water-related agriculture techniques like hydroponics and aquaponics, and the monitoring and management of natural systems (Alprol et al., 2024).

Water is the lifeblood of humans and the industrial process, but it is currently under severe threat from hazardous pollutants that result from both natural and human-caused processes. Globally, there is a major problem with access to clean and healthful drinking water. As such, it is imperative to classify and monitor water quality; the underlying issue is that, given existing technological capabilities, suitable parametric quality indicators are not available (Ray et al., 2023). The activities of aquatic systems (aquaculture, aquaponics, and hydroponics) have risen due to the growth in the human population. This has led to an increase in the nutrient load that drains into water bodies, mostly nitrogen and phosphorus, which has harmed many aquatic habitats. Thus, employing cutting-edge technology, aquaculture effluent wastewater treatment has to be developed and enhanced sustainably (Shen et al., 2023; Yadav et al., 2023).
