# 8 Cryptocurrency and Blockchain

This chapter provides an overview of cryptocurrency and the blockchain technology. We first introduce the history of cryptocurrency, then we describe the design principle and major components of the first cryptocurrency, Bitcoin [15]. Next, we outline the vision and key components of Ethereum [23], which was developed not aiming to compete directly with Bitcoin as a cryptocurrency, but instead, as a platform for developing decentralized applications based on its smart contract implementation, which is in turn powered by the blockchain technology. Finally, we present common attacks on the blockchain technology.
