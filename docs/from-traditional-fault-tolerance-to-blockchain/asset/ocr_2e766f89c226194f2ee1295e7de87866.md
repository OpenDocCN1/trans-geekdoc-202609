The end-to-end latency results shown in Figure 2.16 prove that indeed the pessimistic logging contributes very moderate (often less than 10%) overhead to the performance of the system as observed by the client. For messages of up to 100KB, the end-to-end latency with and without pessimistic logging falls within 10ms. For small messages, the end-to-end latency can go down as low as about 100μs.
