This hash condition check is equivalent to the requirement of the PoW where the hash of the new block header must meet the target difficulty. Just like the mining node that first finds the solution to the PoW puzzle gets to win an award and create the new block, the coin that meets the hash condition first will lead to its owner to gain the right to create the new block and win a stake award. The loop terminates immediately when a coin is found to meet the condition, or it ends when there is no more coin in the selected coin set.
