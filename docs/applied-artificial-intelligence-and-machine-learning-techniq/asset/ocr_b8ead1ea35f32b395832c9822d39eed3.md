A chatbot was designed to accelerate the frequency of practice medical interviews. BOTES was delivered to 13 first-year Spanish Clinical Skills Club medical students at Creighton University, Phoenix campus. All of them were invited to participate in a voluntary and anonymous online survey conducted using the Qualtrics software. The survey explored satisfaction, advantages, and disadvantages and requested feedback for future updates. The Institutional Review Board at Creighton University approved protocol #2004602-01.
