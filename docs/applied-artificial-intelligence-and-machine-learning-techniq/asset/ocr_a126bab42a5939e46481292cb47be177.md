+   - \( I_i \): Represents the external input or input bias to neuron \( i \). It can represent sensory inputs, feedback signals, or any other external influences on the activation level of neuron \( i \).
+   - \( D \): Represents the leakage or inhibition rate of neuron \( i \). It influences how much the activation level of neuron \( i \) is suppressed by activity.
+   - \( E_{ki} \): Represents the weight of the inhibitory connection from neuron \( k \) to neuron \( i \). It influences how much the activation level of neuron \( i \) is suppressed by the activation levels of other neurons in the network.

Taking the case of a fully connected network where all neurons influence each other with at equal distance we can simplify Equation 9.1 with \( \{ C_{ii} \} = 1 \), and \( \{ C_{ii} \} = 0 \), when \( i \neq j \) and \( \{ E_{ij} \} = 1, \forall i, j \) as

\(\frac{dx_i}{dt} = -x_i + (B - x_i)f(x_i) - (D + x_i)\sum_{k \neq i}^N f(x_k) + I_i \quad (9.2)\)

In the current work we are interested in the application of the shunting network to biological phenomena of enumeration, thus we intend to confine the scope of the dynamics to positive values of activation. Moreover, we intend to compare the shunting version to the additive version explored in Sengupta et al. (2014).

Thus we choose the less than linear activation function:

\( f(x) = \left\{ \begin{array}{l} 0 \\ \frac{x}{1+x} \text{ for } x \le 0 \\ 1+x \text{ for } x > 0 \end{array} \right. \quad (9.3) \)
