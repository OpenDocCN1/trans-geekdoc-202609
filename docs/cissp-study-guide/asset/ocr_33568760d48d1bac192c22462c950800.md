ZigBee-Wireless Mesh Networks (ZigBee-WMNs) are recognized as a cost-effective and flexible solution for building automation and control. They have the potential to unify the methods of data communication for sensors, actuators, appliances, and asset-tracking devices. They offer a means to build a reliable but affordable backbone that supports battery-operated devices with a low data rate and low duty cycle to facilitate building automation and control systems (BACs) [13]. ZigBee offers three security modes: unsecured mode, ACL (Access Control List), and secured mode. As the name implies, unsecured modes have no encryption or filtering: data is still plaintext. ACL mode adds firewall-style ACLs, restricting which devices can send data to/from others, but it is still plaintext. Secured mode uses 128-bit AES to encrypt data. Secured mode is strongly recommended over the other modes of ZigBee.

# Li-Fi

Li-Fi (Light Fidelity) uses LED (Light Emitting Diodes) to transfer data. It pulses LEDs so quickly that the human eye sees lights and dots no flickering. This technology requires line-of-sight between the LEDs and the sending/receiving devices. Li-Fi is used in areas where wired devices are not (easily) used, and in areas where electromagnetic interference (EMI) makes traditional wireless technologies such as 802.11 or Bluetooth difficult or impossible to use. Traditional wireless technologies can also risk interfering with critical equipment, such as some medical equipment or devices. Hospitals often face these challenges.

Light has considerably more potential bandwidth than radio waves, so Li-Fi can light at 100 Gbps, faster than any current Wi-Fi technology: "Li-Fi is a Visible Light Communications system transmitting internet communications at very high speeds. The technology makes a LED light emit pulses of light that are undetectable by the human eye and within emitted pulses, data can travel to and from receivers. Then, the receivers collect information and interpret the transmitted data. This is conceptually similar to decoding code but at a much faster rate—millions of times a second—Li-Fi transmission speeds can go over 100 Gbps, 14 times faster than WiGig, also known as the world's fastest WiFi! # RFID

Radio Frequency Identification (RFID) is a technology used to create readable tags for animals or objects. There are three types of RFID tags: semi-passive, and passive. Active and semi-passive RFID tags have a battery. An active tag broadcasts a signal; semi-passive RFID tags rely on a RFID reader's signal for power. Passive RFID tags have no battery, and also rely on the RFID reader's signal for power.
