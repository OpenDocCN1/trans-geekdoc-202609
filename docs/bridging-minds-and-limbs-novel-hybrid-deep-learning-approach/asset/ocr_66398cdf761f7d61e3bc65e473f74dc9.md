EEG signals into independent components using ICA, for the identification and removal of EEG data processing is conducted using the EEGLAB (v20.2) in MATLAB R2021b. To improve efficiency, raw EEG signals are downsampled to 25 Hz. A 1 Hz high-pass filter and a 40 Hz low-pass filter are applied to remove irrelevant frequency components, such as low-frequency and high-frequency noise outside the typical motor imagery band (8–30 Hz). For artifact removal, the Artifact Removal Toolbox, an EEGLAB plug-in based on Independent Component Analysis (ICA). This toolbox decomposes the

EEG signals into independent components using ICA, for the identification and removal of EEG blink movements, artifacts and other sources [31]. The user manually or automatically identifies the artifact components, which are ensuring that the remaining signal reveals the underlying brain activity relevant to motor imagery.

The study comprises EEG recordings from ten participants, performing 150 motor imagery tasks. Each trial consists of 501 time points across 15 channels (including reference and ground electrodes). To train and evaluate the proposed model, the dataset is split 7:3. For each participant, 105 trials (70 %) were allocated for training, and 45 trials for testing.
