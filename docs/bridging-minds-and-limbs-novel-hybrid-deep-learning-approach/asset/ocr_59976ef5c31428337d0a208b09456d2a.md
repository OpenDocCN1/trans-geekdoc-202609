# 2.5.4 Attention Feature Extraction

In deep learning, the importance of various spatial locations or channels is dynamically enhanced by the Squeeze-and-Attention mechanism, allowing to concentrate on the most relevant to the task at hand [35]. In our model, we integrated the attention mechanism within the CNN network, adding attention layers. Instead of using all channels or frequencies, the attention mechanism learns their importance based on their contribution to our classification task. This data-driven approach enables the model to learn most important features without relying on prior assumptions. The detailed architecture of this is shown in Fig. 6.

+   - The first 1D convolutional layer (Conv1d) applies 16 filters with 10 × 10 kernel size to capture spatial patterns.
- The 2nd convolutional layer (Conv2d) refines the extracted features with 15 × 15 kernel.
- These layers are followed by batch normalization (BN) and ReLU activation, ensuring stable gradient and improved feature representation.
- A pooling layer is then applied to the feature maps, reducing computational complexity while preserving spatial information.
