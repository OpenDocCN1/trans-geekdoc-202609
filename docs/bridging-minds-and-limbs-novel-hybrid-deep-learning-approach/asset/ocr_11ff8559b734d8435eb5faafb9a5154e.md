# 4. Discussion

The results presented here demonstrate the potential of a hybrid deep learning model for detecting lower limb imagery, a task that is particularly relevant for stroke rehabilitation. The model achieved accuracy (72.14%) and demonstrated consistent performance across subjects, even with inherent variability. By integrating CNN and LSTM with adaptive feature weighting and PSO for parameter optimization, the proposed model feature enhancement demonstrates robustness and robustness. These components work synergistically to address the complexity of motor imagery tasks and provide improved classification performance.

Specifically, CNN captures spatial characteristics by local and global patterns in EEG topologies, which are essential for distinguishing motor imagery classes. The attention mechanism further captures the temporal dependencies, allowing the network to focus on spatial features. Bi-LSTM effectively models the temporal effects of EEG signals in both forward and backward directions, enabling better understanding of the imagery. Optimization of kernel sizes and filter numbers leads to faster convergence and improved generalization across subjects.
