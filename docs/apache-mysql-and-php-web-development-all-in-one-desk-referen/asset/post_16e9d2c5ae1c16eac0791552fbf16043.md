
## About the Author

**Jeff Cogswell** is an accomplished software engineer, teacher, and writer.
Having written ten books about programming, he is known to be one of the best teachers around. He has tutored and taught more students than he can count and has worked on many professional software projects. He has lived all over the United States but still hasn't been to Europe.

## Dedication

This book is dedicated to all my friends for supporting me through the changes in my life over the past couple of years.
