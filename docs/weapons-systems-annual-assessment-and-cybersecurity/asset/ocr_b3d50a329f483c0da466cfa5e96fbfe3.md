+   - Initial operational test and evaluation is a separate event that is intended to evaluate a system’s effectiveness and suitability under realistic operational conditions before a program makes a full-rate production decision.
+   - Consequently, programs can declare initial operational capability on the basis of full, partial, or no initial operational test and evaluation.
+   - We observed that 5 programs declared, or plan to declare, initial operational capability before initial operational test and evaluation begins. Another 11 programs declared, or plan to declare, this capability before the testing completes.
+     - Programs that declare initial capability before completing initial operational test and evaluation risk fielding systems to warfighters that are not operationally effective or suitable for the missions they will be tasked to perform.
+   - These totals represent a significant change over last year’s assessment, which we attribute in part to additional visibility we gained into programs’ operational test schedules, which improved our analysis.
