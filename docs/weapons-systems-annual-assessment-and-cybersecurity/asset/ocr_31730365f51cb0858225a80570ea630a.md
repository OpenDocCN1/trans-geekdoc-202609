# Indirect Fire Protection Capability Increment 2-Intercept Block 1 (IFPC Inc 2-I Block 1)

![](bbox=[0.12, 0.16, 0.35, 0.32])

The Army’s IFPC Inc 2-I is a follow-on effort to enhance and extend the range of the first IFPC increment, which provided a short-range capability to counter threats from rockets, artillery, and mortars. IFPC Inc 2-I consists of four separate subsystems: an existing sensor; an interceptor; fire control system; and a new multi-mission launcher being developed by the Army. IFPC Inc 2-I consists of three blocks. Block 1 adds the capability to counter cruise missiles and unmanned aircraft. We assessed Inc 2-I Block 1.
