A few programs in each of the two sub-portfolios (prior to 2010 and since 2010) drive a majority of the overall cost changes within the 2017 portfolio.

These programs include:

+   - Programs since 2010: the Air Force’s Next Generation Operational Control System, F-15 Eagle Passive Active Warning Survivability System, and KC-46A Tanker Modernization Program.
+   - Pre-2010 programs: the Navy’s Littoral Combat Ship and SSN 774 Virginia Class Submarine and the DOD-wide F-35 Lightning II Joint Strike Fighter program.
