# MQ-4C Triton Unmanned Aircraft System (MQ-4C Triton)

The Navy's MQ-4C Triton—an unmanned aircraft system operated from five land-based sites worldwide—will provide persistent maritime intelligence, surveillance, and reconnaissance (ISR) data collection and dissemination capability. With its design based on the Air Force's RQ-4B Global Hawk air vehicle, the Triton is integral to a family of maritime patrol and reconnaissance systems and part of the Navy's plan to recapitalize its airborne ISR assets by the end of the decade.
