# SSBN 826 Columbia Class Ballistic Missile Submarine (SSBN 826)

The Navy's Columbia class (SSBN 826) is planned to replace the current fleet of Ohio class ballistic missile submarines as they begin to retire in 2027. The program seeks to provide a sea-based, strategic nuclear deterrent that will remain in service through 2080. Navy plans call for the lead ship to make its first patrol in fiscal year 2031.
