# Airborne and Maritime/Fixed Station (AMF)

The Army's AMF program plans to acquire non-developmental, software-defined radios—named the Small Airborne Networking Radio (SANR)—and associated equipment for integration into Army rotary wing and unmanned aerial systems. These two-channel radios will provide simultaneous voice and data communications between Army platforms and ground forces.
