# Space Fence Ground-Based Radar System Increment 1
(Space Fence Inc 1)

![](bbox=[0.1, 0.15, 0.35, 0.28])

The Air Force's Space Fence Inc 1 program is developing a large, ground-based radar to detect and track objects in low and medium Earth orbit and provide this information to a space surveillance network. Space Fence is designed to use high radio frequencies to detect and track more and smaller objects than previous systems. The Air Force awarded a development and production contract for the first radar site in June 2014. This contract included an option that, if exercised, would enable the Air Force to acquire a second site under a separate program.
