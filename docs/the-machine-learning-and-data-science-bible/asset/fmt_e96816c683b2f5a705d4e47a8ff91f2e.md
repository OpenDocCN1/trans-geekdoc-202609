## Brand Tracking Case Study

A telecom company conducted social media sentiment analysis on customer reviews of their new 5G network roll-out planning. Using keyword searches and natural language classifiers, they analyzed:

*   50k tweets/week discussing the brand and 5G network coverage in major cities
*   Aspect-based sentiment on topics like speeds, reliability and cost of plans

This identified predominantly positive sentiment in areas where 5G was launched. However, analysis of complaints on Twitter revealed outages in certain neighborhoods requiring infrastructure upgrades. Customer support was also addressing dissatisfaction with 5G plan pricing compared to competitors.

The telecom used these social listening insights to prioritize further 5G expansion targeting areas with enthusiastic reception based on Twitter data, while decreasing prices of flagship 5G plans to better align with customer budgets inferred from negative tweets discussing rivals' costs. Regular tracking of 5G sentiment enabled timely response benefiting both network development and tuning corporate messaging to directly respond to public feedback.

## Expanding Use Cases

Novel use cases also apply sentiment analysis beyond traditional domains:

*   Education: Surveying student/teacher sentiments helps universities gauge satisfaction of online/hybrid learning experiences and collect feedback for enhancing curricula post-pandemic.
*   Healthcare: Analyzing discussions on medical conditions or drug trials aids researchers understand patient perspectives to improve treatments based on needs expressed online.
*   Sustainability: Tracking attitudes towards environmental issues helps advocate groups optimize outreach and identify subtopics requiring increased education to build support for initiatives.

As social media engagement continues growing globally across industries, creative applications leveraging real-time sentiment data promise further innovations enhancing consumer experiences, business operations and social impact by giving voice to online perspectives at unprecedented scale. Rigorous techniques will remain vital to these expanding use cases.

## Ethical Considerations

While sentiment analysis provides valuable insights, practitioners must also consider ethical implications when applying these techniques, especially to social media data which users may perceive as private conversations. Key issues include privacy, transparency around data collection and model biases. Anonymizing personal details and obtaining proper consent are important to establish trust. Companies should also disclose what sentiments are being analyzed and for what purposes in a clear, understandable manner. By addressing such ethical challenges proactively with oversight, the field can realize sentiment analysis's promise to empower organizations and individuals through new understandings, while protecting users' well-being, autonomy and right to opinion expression.

Machine learning is a subfield of artificial intelligence focused on developing algorithms and techniques that allow computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed. This chapter provides an overview of fundamental machine learning concepts and methods.

### Supervised vs Unsupervised Learning

Machine learning tasks are categorized as supervised or unsupervised based on the type of data used for training:

*   Supervised learning uses labeled examples to learn general rules for mapping inputs to outputs. It is used for classification and regression.
*   Unsupervised learning finds hidden patterns in unlabeled data. It is used for tasks like clustering, association rule learning, and dimensionality reduction.
