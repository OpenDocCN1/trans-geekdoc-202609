
While optimizing retention benefited Telco commercially, potentially coercive interpretations required vigilance. Clarified guidelines ensured recommendations aligned ethically:

*   Customers owned autonomy over engagement and were supported transparently in finding ideal providers.

*   Predictions focused retaining loyal customers through partnership improvements rather than artificially discouraging switching.

*   Model inputs considered demographics only tangentially by examining relevant economic attributes and contextualized customer value.

*   Progressive evaluation involving outside audits guaranteed continued responsible, consent-based practices respecting privacy and autonomy.

## Summary: Key Concepts and Formulas

The first step in any ML implementation is defining clear business objectives and scoping the problem appropriately. This involves assessing available resources and identifying high-impact use cases within business domains like marketing, logistics or customer support. One such impactful area is predictive analytics to anticipate customer needs or behaviors. For example, an e-commerce retailer analyzed purchasing patterns and recommended complementary products, resulting in a 12% increase in cart values.

Large corporations also face governance and oversight complexities when deploying learning models. Ensuring fairness, explainability and accountability is essential for bolstering consumer and employee trust. A major airline works closely with regulators to receive approval for an AI-powered pilot assistance system, demonstrating model outputs and failure scenarios through rigorous testing and documentation. Proper change management is likewise critical, as ML solutions may disrupt workflows or potentially replace roles over time.

Proper definition of business requirements is crucial for developing IT solutions that align with organizational objectives and add value. This involves capturing requirements accurately from the outset to guide solution design and development efforts. However, eliciting requirements is also an iterative process of progressive understanding as stakeholder needs become clearer over time. This chapter will explore effective techniques for eliciting, documenting and managing requirements to meet stakeholder needs.
