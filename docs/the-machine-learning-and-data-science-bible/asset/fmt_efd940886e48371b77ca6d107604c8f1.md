## Ethics in Data Science

As data and analytics transform industries, ensuring their responsible use grows imperative. Left unchecked, even well-intentioned technologies could propagate or exacerbate harms through unintended biases. This section explores principled approaches for data scientists and practitioners to address ethical concerns proactively.

### Fairness, Accountability and Transparency

Fairness entails developing solutions favoring no groups while avoiding detrimental impacts. Modeling privacy, legal compliance and broader societal costs alongside business objectives cultivates accountability. Transparency means clearly communicating model functioning, limitations and oversight procedures to establish trust. Addressing these factors upholds data science's role in empowering rather than marginalizing communities.
