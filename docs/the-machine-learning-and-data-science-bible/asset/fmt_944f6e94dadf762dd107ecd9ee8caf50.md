## Problem Definition and Goal Setting

Defining the problem is the first critical step in any machine learning project. It provides the foundation for determining what success looks like and guiding all subsequent phases of model development and deployment. This section will explore best practices for clearly scoping problems to establish shared understanding and set realistic expectations among stakeholders.

### Understand the Business Context

To properly define any technical problem, it is essential to first comprehend the nature of the business objectives and conditions that prompted the initiative. Discuss openly with key stakeholders like product managers, data scientists and subject experts to uncover:

#### Strategic Drivers

Understanding the strategic drivers motivating exploration of machine learning solutions provides important context for scoping the problem appropriately. This involves discussing with stakeholders the types of opportunities ML may address, such as enabling new products or markets, reducing costs, improving productivity or efficiency gains. Capturing these high-level objectives helps define success and potential value.

#### Industry Landscape

Examining relevant industry and competitive landscapes puts the business need for ML into context. Assessing trends, challenges, and how peers are addressing similar needs provides insights into whether current capabilities could be enhanced. This landscape awareness helps identify the most pertinent opportunities ML may help the organization seize.

#### Operational Constraints

There may be constraints around budgets, available resources, regulatory standards or expected timeframes for return on investment that influence the feasibility and design of potential ML approaches. Understanding these practical limiting factors early allows for realistic scoping aligned with operational realities.

#### Performance Metrics

Discovering what metrics are currently used within the problem domain to define success provides a foundation for establishing quantitative evaluation criteria. These metrics often relate directly to core business objectives like productivity, customer outcomes or financial goals. Baseline performance levels on such indicators also inform expectations.

#### Hypotheses Generation

Leveraging prior experience and access to relevant organizational data assets can help generate hypotheses about where within processes, products or services ML may provide the most benefit. These hypotheses serve as a starting point for discovery and help guide focused exploration of opportunities with the highest prospective impact.

Clearly capturing high-level context upfront orientations scoping to align with practical considerations. It also establishes partnerships crucial for iterative refinement and ongoing evaluation throughout model development.
