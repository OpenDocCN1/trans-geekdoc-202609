This glitch underscores the need not to take an AI’s responses at face value. ## Using the CAL to Boost Components of EI

It seems unlikely that current AIs can train `EI` beyond a basic level, but even that may sometimes be helpful. But rather than train `EI` in general, AIs might be more useful for training individual components of this complex ability.
