# Edge computing: A potential use of blockchain

The idea of edge computing is to bring the computational capacity at the origin of "instant data" itself. Therefore, this paradigm of edge computing may serve better for delay-sensitive jobs by pushing the cloud computational capability to the edge of that network [18]. The backbone of edge computing is its distributed computational framework. The purpose of this is to bring the storage capacities and computational abilities of the data sources, devices, nearer to the enterprise business logic where they are actually needed [19-21]. The working of edge computing is different; the difference is that edge devices process the computation locally, whereas the cloud computing centralizes it in a single data center [22], as shown in Fig. 4. Edge computing has advantage over cloud computing in terms of latency and bandwidth edge devices have to send only individual results, after processing on the local data, to the edge servers.

Internet of Things (IoT) is one of the possible uses of edge computing. Let us use an example of store of clothing, where local and batch, both types of processing, are needed for inventory and sales. Geographically distributed outlets tally their inventory and sales and then forward the computational results to the head office each day. The top hierarchy, head office in this example is not interested in each transaction that happens at the outlet (edge) but in the final statistics of sales and inventory computed by the outlet (edge devices). By exhausting the capabilities of edge computing, we reshape almost every industry. Use cases of edge computing including telecommunication, health, financial services, media and entertainment, manufacturing, supply chain, retail, insurance, pharmaceuticals, and automotive with smart and smart city. # 4.1 Edge computing architectures

The architecture of cloud computing and IoT may be expanded to the illusion diagram of edge computing architecture. Basilly, edge computing utilizes a multilayered distributed architecture to balance among between cloud layer, edge layer or edge network, and the edge layer that connects edge local server layer and edge device; a high-level illusion diagram of edge computing architecture is presented in Fig. 6. Fig. 7 represents a simplified edge architecture reference based on IBM edge computing architecture.

The top layer, or cloud microcloud, is used for analysis of large-scale data. It consists of a dashboard that may be the focus of device management and monitoring. This layer may have hundreds of devices. The size of software components as AI services and Models at each layer varies according to the size of data it has to process.

The second layer is the edge cloud or micro data centers that may be thought of as a local cloud for edge devices computing support. This layer is also considered as an edge network in the telecommunication field. Edge clouds can do most of the computing on their own without consulting the top layer, decreasing latency and minimizing bandwidth widths. This layer may have thousands of devices.
