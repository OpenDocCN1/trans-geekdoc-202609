IoT with edge computing is another weapon to use with blockchain technology and AI. With edge computing, our computational power can be increased many folds. The absence of a middle party or governing party is the beauty of blockchain technology, which is a double-edged sword. To remove the clouds of uncertainty that surround the blockchain, AI and ML can play a vital role. This article discussed lot of applications of AI and ML in blockchain technology.
