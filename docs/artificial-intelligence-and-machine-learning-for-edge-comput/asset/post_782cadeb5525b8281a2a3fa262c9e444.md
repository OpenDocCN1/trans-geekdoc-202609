The following are the general characteristics of blockchain technology:

- Consensus: All members in the blockchain network must agree on the validity of the transactions.
- Immutability: Once transactions have been recorded in the ledger, then it will become impossible to tamper with by any of the members of the blockchain network. If there is an error in the transaction, then that a new transaction must be executed to revert the error induced. The beauty of blockchain is that both transactions are visible to all, now.
- Source: The source of the asset is known to all the nodes of the blockchain as well as the change in ownership of the asset is visible to all, too.
- Inevitability: Blockchain gives the one-stop solution, in the form of a single ledger, to verify the accomplishment of the transaction as well as the asset's ownership.

## 1.2 Advantages of blockchain

Blockchain technology has the following advantages for a business:

- Less turnaround time: The turnaround time of a transaction in the blockchain is much less as compared to other traditional middle-party undertakings. The absence of central authority verification boosts the speed of the transactions.
- Impregnable fort: Blockchain applications may be thought of like an impregnable fort because of the security features it has. Hacking, cybersecurity, breach of data, or trust are not possible in the blockchain ledger.
- Cost-efficient: The following attributes of the blockchain network make it cost-efficient compared to other classical networks with authority.
- Low maintenance: There is no central authority to monitor the activities of activities in the blockchain. All participants contribute to make the network up and kicking.
- Peer-to-peer transaction: Members of the blockchain can exchange assets or services directly without any interference or central authority.
- No repetitive efforts: A single ledger is shared by all the members of the blockchain network, eliminating the duplication of efforts.

## 1.3 Blockchain trust builder attributes

Blockchain technology has become the talk of the town because of the trust it infuses into the system. Building without intermediaries is the unique selling point of this technology. Fig. 2 shows the blockchain trust builder attributes [11].

- Distributed: There is no central authority to trust with. Every transaction is mirrored in the shared ledger of blockchain members. The shared ledger is updated in real time.
- Sustainable: The sustainability of the blockchain network lies in its architecture. No individual entity poses any threat to the existence of a blockchain platform.
- Protected: Unauthorized access is checked by the crypto-graph tools and by the restrictions imposed on permissions.
- Confidential: Cryptographic techniques along with data partitioning techniques preserve the privacy of the trading parties and the transactions.
- Permanent: Once transactions are agreed upon the smart contract and the transaction is completed, no one can alter the transaction in the ledger. In case of an erroneous transaction, the effect of errors will be reversed by only the new transaction in the ledger.
- Transparent: As participants of the network have access to the same ledger, verification of the credentials of all the entities participating in the transaction, and the validation of each transaction are done, without help of any mediator, through various crypto-graph tools by each member of the network.
- Verifiable: The beauty of blockchain is we can verify in real time all transactions as the ledger are recorded in sequence and time-stamped.
- Consensus: Smart contracts are the soul of the blockchain and consensus algorithms are the crux of smart contracts. All stakeholders of the network must ensure the validity of the transaction.
- Transactional: Each smart contract of the blockchain can formulate its own terms and conditions for the transaction.
- Coordination: Participating parties can coordinate, design, and agree upon rules, terms, and conditions for each transaction in the blockchain platform.
- Elastic: The blockchain network is very flexible in nature; all business needs of all lives can be designed and developed through smart contracts. No approval is needed from any central or supreme authority.
