# 1.3 Blockchain trust builder attributes

Blockchain technology has become the talk of the town because of the trust it infuses into the system. Building without intermediaries is the unique selling point of this technology. Fig. 2 shows the blockchain trust builder attributes [11].

+   - Distributed: There is no central authority to trust with. Every transaction is mirrored in the shared ledger of blockchain members. The shared ledger is updated in real time.
- Sustainable: The sustainability of the blockchain network lies in its architecture. No individual entity poses any threat to the existence of a blockchain platform.
- Protected: Unauthorized access is checked by the crypto-graph tools and by the restrictions imposed on permissions.
- Confidential: Cryptographic techniques along with data partitioning techniques preserve the privacy of the trading parties and the transactions.
- Permanent: Once transactions are agreed upon the smart contract and the transaction is completed, no one can alter the transaction in the ledger. In case of an erroneous transaction, the effect of errors will be reversed by only the new transaction in the ledger.
- Transparent: As participants of the network have access to the same ledger, verification of the credentials of all the entities participating in the transaction, and the validation of each transaction are done, without help of any mediator, through various crypto-graph tools by each member of the network.
- Verifiable: The beauty of blockchain is we can verify in real time all transactions as the ledger are recorded in sequence and time-stamped.
- Consensus: Smart contracts are the soul of the blockchain and consensus algorithms are the crux of smart contracts. All stakeholders of the network must ensure the validity of the transaction.
- Transactional: Each smart contract of the blockchain can formulate its own terms and conditions of the transaction for the exchange activity.
- Coordination: Participating parties can coordinate, design, and agree upon rules, terms, and conditions for each transaction in the blockchain platform.
- Elastic: The blockchain network is very flexible in nature; all business needs of all lives can be designed and developed through smart contracts. No approval is needed from any central or supreme authority. Therefore, end-to-end transactions can be developed meticulously with flexibility.
