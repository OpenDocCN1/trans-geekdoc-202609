## 2.4 Blockchain initiatives by the government of India

Case studies from NITI Aayog’s vault (2020) are presented:

- Land Reserves: Creating a system to manage record transfer and ownership.
- Pharmaceutical drug supply chain: “Self-regulation” of the sector through blockchain-enabled trust.
- SuperCert: Antifraud, identity blockchain solution for educational certificates.
- Immunization Supply Chain: A new infrastructure development for the supply chain, which would be more unified and enhanced in nature.
- Chit Funds: A blockchain-based model to enhance trust and unlock value creation.
- Insurance (medical, automotive, etc.).
- Electric vehicle farming.
