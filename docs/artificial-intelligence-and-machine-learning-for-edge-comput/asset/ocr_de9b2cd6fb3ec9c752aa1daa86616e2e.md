The bottom layer of architecture is the edge device layer. Edge devices are very compact and sophisticated with limited resources like less storage capacity, but are equipped with artificial intelligence and machine learning-based high-end software-as-a-service. For most of the tasks, without any resources or home of an enterprise cloud layer and/or edge server, these edge devices take care of real-time computations.
Intel, Microsoft, Amazon, and Google are some of the early birds that have taken their cloud infrastructure for the edge rainbow of industry requirements. An edge infrastructure overview of these major cloud providers is as follows:

+   * For the Edge:
  * Xeon Scalable Processors: High-performance edge servers
  * Intel Deep Learning Boost (Intel DL Boost): AI applications
  * Intel Optane persistent memory: faster data processing and storage
  * Intel Solid State Drives/Intel Optane DC SSDs/Intel 3D NAND SSDs: Storage
  * Intel Ethernet Network Adapters and Controllers: Networking
