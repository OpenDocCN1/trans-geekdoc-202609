#  4.3 Applications of blockchain technology in edge computing

In edge computing, edge devices frequently communicate with each other and edge servers to do AI and learning-enabled decision-making. Communication networks and the devices themselves are prone to security threats, for tampering or leakage of information transmitted over the edge network due to possible attacks by hackers or failure of devices [23]. The tamper-proof ledger of blockchain technology can play a key role. Blockchain technology can ensure tamper-proof network communication and data storage on edge devices, the edge network, and the enterprise cloud. An AI-trust model in edge computing can be optimized and improved with the help of smart contracts and consensus protocols of blockchain technology [24]. The blockchain and edge computing technologies have great efficacy and the potential to provide solutions in diverse scenarios [25]. Blockchain-powered trusted, secure, and tamper-proof edge computing framework can provide effective solutions. Researchers have proposed several frameworks for blockchain-enabled edge computing.
