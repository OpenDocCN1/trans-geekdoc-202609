To validate the results, we applied a Wilcoxon signed-rank paired test. With a p-value of 0.0002441, we can statistically conclude that, with a significance level of 0.05, the mean of the query execution times on a 10 GB database without physical design is significantly higher than the mean of the query execution times on a 10 GB database with physical design.
