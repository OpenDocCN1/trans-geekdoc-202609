With the advancement of technology, clinicians can quickly update patient information in the CDST, and the patient CHD risk level will be immediately informed to the clinician. The use of a CDST will assist clinicians in making important clinical decisions faster; hence, administration of patient treatment will be earlier, preventing or reducing the coronary heart risk of the patient early.
