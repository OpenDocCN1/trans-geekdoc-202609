Also, the probability of selecting a document d is P(d), probability of picking a latent class z is P(z | d), and the probability of generating a word w is P(w | d). The earlier model assumes two conditions at grassroot level.

+   a. The observation pairs (d, w) are assumed to be independent; this corresponds to the “bag-of-words” approach.
+   b. Conditioned on the latent class z, words w are generated independent of the document d. Asserting the number of states to be smaller than the number of documents (K ≤ N), z acts as a threshold variable in forecasting w conditioned on d.
