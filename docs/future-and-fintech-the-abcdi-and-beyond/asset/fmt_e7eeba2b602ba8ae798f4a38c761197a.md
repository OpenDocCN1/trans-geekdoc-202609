
## Chapters 8 and 9: Data Management

[Chapter 8](#chap8.xhtml), "Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in Data Management", presents the methodologies, solutions, and platforms of AI and ML for data management in the Big Data era. A case study of extensive AI/ML integration in enterprise data management is also presented, together with the associated challenges and future directions.

[Chapter 9](#chap9.xhtml), "Technologies and Strategies to Leverage Cloud Infrastructure for Data Integration", provides an overview of Cloud data technologies for data integration and their strategic implications. In addition to common Cloud services for data ingestion, storage, processing and lifecycle management, major Cloud platforms, leading data tech companies, and open-source communities also provide various tools for delivering data security and sensitive data management. A case study on how a leading financial services company leverages Cloud data infrastructure to digitalize banking is also presented.

## Chapters 10 and 11: IoT — Smart Home and Biometrics

[Chapter 10](#chap10.xhtml), "Smart Home for Financial Services: Security, Privacy, and Solutions" and [Chapter 11](#chap11.xhtml), "Biometrics in FinTech: A Technological Review", discuss two important topics in IoT — the smart home and biometrics. The smart home is one of the most prevalent applications of Internet of Things (IoT). Biometrics information sensed by IoT devices, e.g., voice, face, fingerprint and behaviors, can be used to verify personal identity. Besides home appliance control, smart home could become the future personal financial center of comprehensive financial services with enhanced security and safety using AI, Blockchain, Cloud and biometrics technologies. [Chapter 11](#chap11.xhtml) further explains the techniques, applications, challenges, and future directions of biometrics.

## Chapters 12 to 14: DevOps for Digital Transformation

[Chapters 12](#chap12.xhtml) to [14](#chap14.xhtml) emphasize one of the most important techniques for digital transformation — DevOps, the integration of development and operation in the productionization lifecycle.

[Chapter 12](#chap12.xhtml), "DevOps: Speeding up Financial Applications Delivery to the Market", gives an overview of DevOps. Agile and CI/CD concepts and their tools (e.g., `microservice` and `Docker`) are also explained. A few DevOps success stories in the financial industry are also highlighted.

[Chapter 13](#chap13.xhtml), "DevSecOps: Integrating Security in DevOps for Financial Applications", further adds security concerns into DevOps, and analyzes the status of DevSecOps together with its challenges, practices, and tools.

[Chapter 14](#chap14.xhtml), "MLOps in the Financial Industry: Philosophy, Practices, and Tools", applies DevOps into the AI/ML lifecycle for MLOps, which can dramatically reduce the ML adoption cost and improve AI responsibility in the BFSI. Case studies are provided on how to implement DevOps, DevSecOps, and MLOps in large banks.

## Chapter 15: Open Banking Ecosystem

[Chapter 15](#chap15.xhtml), "The Open Banking Ecosystem and its Evolution", demonstrates the power of open banking with `BaaS` and `FaaS` for the platformization ecosystem. Their implementation strategies and concerns in different countries are discussed. In particular, we highlight their developments in China, and believe that future banking will be more open and smarter with the integration of FinTech.
