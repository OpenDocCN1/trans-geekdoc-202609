Y = \frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}C_{j,full} + \sum_{k=1}^{K}\left(\frac{1}{J}\sum_{j=1}^{J}\text{contribution}_j(x,k)\right)

where J is the number of trees in the forest, k represents a feature in the observation, K is the total number of features, c_full is the average of the entire dataset (initial node).
