### 10 Geometric Mean Absolute Error (GMAE)*

Defined as the n-th root of the product of error values.

\[ GMAE = \sqrt[n]{\prod_{i=1}^n |E_i|} \]

### 11 Fractional Absolute Error (FAE)

Evaluates the absolute fractional error.

\[ FAE = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{2 \times |E_i|}{|A_i| + |P_i|} \]

### 12 Mean Squared Error (MSE)

Measures the average of the squares of the errors.

\[ MSE = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n E_i^2}{n} \]

### 13 Normalized Mean Squared Error (NMSE)

Estimates the overall deviations between measured values and predictions.

\[ NMSE = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n E_i^2}{n} \]

### 14 Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)

Root square of average squared error.

\[ RMSE = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n E_i^2}{n}} \]

### 15 Root Mean Square Percentage Error (RMSPE)

Evaluates the mean of squared errors in percentages.

\[ RMSPE = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (|E_i|/|A_i|)^2}{n/100}} \]

### 16 Normalized Root Mean Squared Error (NRMSE)*

Normalizes the root mean squared error.

\[ NRMSE = \frac{\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n E_i^2}{n}}}{A_{mean}} \]

### 26 Golbraikh and Tropsha's criterion [18]

At least one slope of regression lines (k or k') between the regressions of actual (Ay) against predicted output (Pj) or Pj against Ay through the origin, i.e., Ay = k × Pj and Pj = k' Ay, respectively.

\[ k = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (A_i \times P_i)}{A_i^2} \]

\[ k' = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (A_i \times P_i)}{P_i^2} \]

\[ m = \frac{R^2 - R_0^2}{R^2} \]

\[ n = \frac{R^2 - R_{0^\prime}^2}{R^2} \]

- k and k' need to be close to 1.0 or at least within the range of 0.85 and 1.15.
- m and n are performance indexes and their absolute value should be lower than 0.1.

### 27 Q.S.A.R. model by Roy and Roy [19]

\[ R_m = R^2 \times \left(1 - \sqrt{|R^2 - R_0^2|}\right) \]

where

\[ \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (P_i - A_i^o)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n (P_i - P_{mean})^2}, A_i^o = k \times P_i^o \]

\[ = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (A_i - P_i^o)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n (A_i - A_{mean})^2}, P_i^o = k' \times A_i \]

- \(R_m\) is an external predictability indicator. \(R_m > 0.5\) implies a good fit.
