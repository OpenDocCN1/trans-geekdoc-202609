The first split from the K-Means model takes place at the compressive strength of 71 MPa. From there, columns of fc > 71 MPa are moved into cluster no. 1, and the rest are further analyzed. The next split occurs in columns with a length (L) longer than 4.3 m joining cluster no. 4, and the remaining columns continue the analysis. The third split occurs at the concrete cover (C) with columns of a smaller cover thickness (less than 36.5 mm) joining cluster no. 2. Columns with pin-pinned conditions are examined against the level of applied loading, and columns with higher rigidity (K ≤ 0.85) are examined against the steel reinforcement ratio.
