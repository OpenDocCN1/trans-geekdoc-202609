Let us dig a little deeper and try to link the above energy expenditure to carbon emissions.46 Here is a list of assumptions:

+   - The US Energy Information Administration [12] reports that 0.42 kg of CO2 is emitted per 1 kWh.
+   - Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, USA [13], estimates the unit annual energy consumption (AEC) for desktops to be 194 kWh/yr, with 20% of desktops consuming more than 300 kWh/yr and high-end computing desktops reaching 600 kWh/yr.

Most engineers are likely to use above-average desktops in their modeling (or a cloud computing service) for their work/modeling. Given the above assumptions, a typical desktop is expected to generate 0.42kg of CO2 / kWh x 300 kWh / yr = 126kg of CO2 annually.
