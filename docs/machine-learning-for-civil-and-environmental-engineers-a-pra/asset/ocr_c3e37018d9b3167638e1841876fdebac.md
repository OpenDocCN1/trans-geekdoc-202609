| 17 | Geometric Root Mean Squared Error (GRMSE) | Evaluates the geometric root squared errors. | $GRMSE = 2n \sqrt[n]{\prod_{i=1}^n E_i^2}$ | Scale-dependent. Less sensitive to outliers than RMSE. [11]. An extension of RMSE. Lower RRMSE values are favorable. |
| 18 | Relative root mean squared error (RRMSE) [15] | Present percentage variation in accuracy. | $RRMSE = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n} \sum (A - P)^2}$ | |
| 19 | Sum of Squared Error (SSE) | Sums the squared differences between each observation and its mean. | $SSE = \sum_{i=1}^n E_i^2$ | A small SSE indicates a good fit [16]. |
| 20 | Relative Squared Error (RSE) | Normalizes total squared error by dividing by the total squared error. | $RSE = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n E_i^2}{(A_i - A_{mean})^2}$ | A perfect fit is achieved when the numerator equals zero [9]. |
| 21 | Root Relative Squared Error (RRSE) | Evaluates the root relative squared error between two vectors. | $RRSE = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n E_i^2}{\sum (A_i - A_{mean})^2}}$ | Ranges between zero and 1.0, with zero being ideal [9]. |
| 22 | Mean Square Percentage Error (MSPE)* | Evaluates the mean of square percentage errors. | $MSPE = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (|E_i|/|A_i|)^2}{n/100}$ | Non-symmetrical [8]. |
| 23 | Correlation coefficient (R) | Measures the strength of association between variables. | $R = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (A_i - \bar{A})(P_i - \bar{P})}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n (A_i - \bar{A})^2 \sum_{i=1}^n (P_i - \bar{P})^2}}$ | R>0.8 implies strong correlation [17]. Does not change by equal scaling. Cannot distinguish between independent and dependent variables. |
| 24 | Coefficient of Determination ($R^2$) | The square of correlation. | $R^2 = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (P_i - A_i)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^n (A_i - A_{mean})^2}$ | $R^2$ values close to 1.0 indicate a strong correlation. Related to R. The addition of predictors increases R^2 (even if due to chance alone). Scale independent. Stable near zero [11]. |
| 25 | Mean Absolute Scaled Error (MASE) | Mean absolute errors divided by the mean absolute error. | $\frac{\sum_{i=1}^n E_i}{n/100 \cdot \frac{1}{A_i} |\sum_{i=1}^n A_i - A_{i-1}|}$ | |
