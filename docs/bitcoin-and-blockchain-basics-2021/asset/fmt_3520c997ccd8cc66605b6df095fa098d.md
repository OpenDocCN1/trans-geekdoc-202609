# Using Bitcoin as a Distributed Ledger

Bitcoin operates as a distributed ledger. It is a database synchronized and shared over different sites, organizations, and regions of the world. This feature makes bitcoin easy to use and accessible by various individuals all over the world.

In this case, transactions are visible to the public. Each participant at a node can access transactions and records distributed over the system. The users can even own or maintain a similar copy of such transactions. If any user alters any record, such records are released to every user of the network immediately.

In distributed ledgers, no central body oversees the activities of each user on the network. Every transaction is accessible to all connected to the network.

Therefore, the database of bitcoin is a distributed ledger. This is a ledger of contracts or transactions maintained in a decentralized format over several territories and handled by different persons.

Using distributed ledgers in bitcoin network helps in minimizing fraud including cyber attacks.

Distributed ledgers make communication and co-operation over the internet easy. This process makes audit trail easy to follow for account managers and clerks during reviews of financial statements.

The bitcoin distributed ledger is automated and accessible at any time of the day. It speeds up the amount of time required to handle transactions and minimizes inefficient operational processes, thereby lessening the cost of operations.

The bitcoin distributed ledger is valuable in different sectors of the economy such as various supply chains, finance, music and entertainment, precious metals, and assets, real estate, including artwork.

# Who Operates the Bitcoin Technology?

Bitcoin is not regulated or operated by anyone. It is operated based on the agreement of everyone involved in the network. However, a person or group of persons under the name Satoshi Nakamoto brought the ideas that produced bitcoin in 2008. The cryptocurrency was launched in 2009.

In 2011, the bitcoin currency gained attention as the value of a bitcoin got up to equality with the dollar. Nakamoto stopped active participation in the Bitcoin network in 2011. He shifted the responsibility of developing the bitcoin software to Gavin Andresen, a developer. He has been the chief developer of the bitcoin network all these years.

Therefore, the bitcoin technology is maintained and operated as an open-source network by Andresen. Moreover, a non-profit organization that promotes bitcoin is called the Bitcoin Foundation. They are responsible for managing the views on the bitcoin community and Andresen is a worker of the foundation.

# Chapter Seven: The Problems Confronting Bitcoins

Some of the problems confronting the adoption of bitcoin into the mainstream include:

1.  **The issues of volatility.** Bitcoin is a volatile financial tool. The value is yet to stabilize as a currency.
2.  **The issues of ease of use.** It is true that there are places where you can buy or exchange bitcoin (bitcoin exchangers), but accessing and using bitcoin is still a mirage to many people. It is not a user-friendly product.
3.  **The issue of general acceptability.** You may ask, "Is bitcoin generally acceptable buy everyone?" Any currency that is not generally acceptable is not fit to be a currency.
4.  **The issue of scalability.** This is a technical issue. The blockchain technology restricts much data to be stored on each block. Therefore, each block is expected to have 1 megabyte of data. With this, it can process about three transactions each second compared to the Visa technology that can process over two thousand transactions every second.
5.  It is reputed for aiding money laundering and other criminal activities, especially on the internet.
6.  Bitcoin has tax issues. The reason is that under the present law, bitcoin and other digital currencies are regarded as intangible assets. Therefore, they are not subject to tax.

## Variations Between Blockchain and Bitcoin

Blockchain and bitcoin are inter-related concepts, but they do not mean the same thing. To give you an elementary definition of blockchain, it is a file for storing data in a computer. You can also call it an open-distributed database or ledger. This definition implies that the information contained in a blockchain can be duplicated among a network of computers. This feature makes a blockchain to be decentralized and even transformational. Data is accessed and verified by all participants. This makes the blockchain secure and transparent.
