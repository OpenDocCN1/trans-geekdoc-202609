
# How to Set Up a Bitcoin Wallet

You can set up a bitcoin wallet on your smartphone or PC. By installing this on your devices, it will generate your first bitcoin address but you can create more if you wish. After setting up your bitcoin wallet and address, you can send your address to your friends, customers, and employers to be sending your payments into your wallet.

You can also send payments to your workers, friends and family's wallets. It is a type of online cash, but in simplicity, bitcoin is known as digital currency, virtual currency, or cryptocurrency.

## How to Get Bitcoins

You can get bitcoins in various ways such as:

### 1. Mining

Mining – the method of confirming pending transactions by adding them in the blockchain. Mining is also regarded as a distributed consensus system for validating transactions on the bitcoin network. This process enhances a chronological order within the block chain. It enables different computing systems to harmonize on the nature of the network. Mining also secures the neutrality of the system. If you want to confirm the transaction, payments must be organized in a block that adheres to stringent cryptographic principles subject to verification.

In this way, previous blocks will not be modified since this can make subsequent blocks invalid. Therefore, nobody can dictate or control what is added to the block chain. They may not also replace sections of the block chain to include their expenditures.

### 2. Payments

Payments – most persons prefer to be paid with bitcoins for services or goods. In this way, one can stash up the cryptos for future use.

# Using Bitcoin in Exchanges

Do you know that bitcoins could be used in exchange? A digital marketplace where traders can sell or buy bitcoins with various altcoins is known as bitcoin exchange. This online platform is an intermediary linking sellers of cryptocurrencies and the buyers as well. In professional terms, it is analyzed as an intermediary between the maker of cryptos and the taker.

This exchange serves like a brokerage where you can deposit cash using wire transfer, bank transfer, or bank deposit. Using bitcoin exchange usually has charges attached to it.

You will pay the currency conversion fee if you want to trade between cryptos. This is like trading different currencies in conventional banking system. Also, buying and selling bitcoin are based on the brokerage system where a taker sets a limit order that could be sold if a maker provides bitcoins that match the request.

Therefore, bitcoin exchange pairs takers with makers. In this case, they set the limit order or market order. When this limit is fixed and chosen, you are authorizing the exchange to trade at an available option or price prevalent in the marketplace.

After setting this limit, the trader will direct the platform to trade the cryptos at a price lower or above the present the bid.

## How to Transact with an Exchange

*   Register with the exchange.
*   Authenticate the user's identity by conducting different verification processes.
*   After authentication, an account is opened for the user.
*   The user transfers funds into the account before buying coins.

You can use money orders, direct bank deposits, transfers, bank drafts, gift cards, credit cards, debit cards, and various payment methods to deposit your funds with the exchange.

Also, withdrawing money from the account could be done using PayPal, bank wire, checks, cash, credit cards, and other options stipulated by the exchange.

Another type of exchange to use is decentralized bitcoin exchange.

This type of exchange is handled without a centralized authority. They permit peer-to-peer trading of cryptos with no exchange authority to initiate and manage transactions.

## Advantages of Using Decentralized Bitcoin Exchanges

1.  Decentralized exchanges correspond with decentralized structures suitable for most cryptos.
2.  Less personal information is needed from users.
3.  These exchanges uphold a basic level of user interest by maintaining liquidity and trading volume.
4.  Assets could be transferred to other members directly without the exchange interfering.
5.  Direct transfers of assets to the exchange will minimize the risk of hacking and theft.
6.  Less trading frauds will be recorded and no manipulation of figures or prices.
