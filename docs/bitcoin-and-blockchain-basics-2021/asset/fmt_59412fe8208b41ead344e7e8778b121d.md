## A stack of Bitcoins

In January 2009, bitcoin, a digital currency was created. The abbreviation associated with bitcoin is `BTC`. This phenomenon called bitcoin developed from the trendy and mysterious Satoshi Nakamoto, a pseudonymous personality or group of persons believed to be the initiators and originators of this currency.

Despite this guess, the inventor of bitcoin is a hidden personality with no clear-cut identity. Experts are of the opinion that the reason for concealing his identity is to evade some government regulations, probing, and subsequent incarceration, since this evolving currency is already disrupting the financial schemes of so many nations, multinationals, and individuals.

This digital currency is handled by a decentralized regulatory body. It is not managed or handled by any government or bank, and not valuable to be used as a commodity. Bitcoins exist as balances prepared on a public ledger. In this case, everyone has unrestricted access to the records.

The emergence of bitcoin has led to the evolution of other cryptos known as altcoins, even though it is not used as a legal tender. But the most popular cryptocurrency is bitcoin.

## The Origin of Bitcoins

On August 18th, 2008, `bitcoin.org` was registered as a domain name. However, the identity of the person or group of persons that registered it is not available to the public. The reason could be for privacy or security purposes. Therefore, the domain is regarded as WhoisGuard Protected.

Also, on October 31st, 2008, a publication was made on the Cryptography Mailing list. This was published on `metzdowd.com` by a group of persons or a person called Satoshi Nakamoto. It reads, "I've been working on a new electronic cash system that's fully peer-to-peer, with no trusted third party".

Now, this whitepaper was published on `bitcoin.org` with the caption "Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System". Nowadays, it has become the Magna Carta for bitcoin operations and management.

Again, on January 3rd, 2009, Block 0 regarded as the genesis block, the first block of bitcoin was mined. This block contains an inscription: "The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks". This text could imply that it was mined on or after that date. It is also useful for political commentaries.

Additionally, the first version of bitcoin software was published on the Cryptography Mailing list on January 8th, 2009.

Moreover, Block 1 of bitcoin was mined on January 9th, 2009. After that, bitcoin mining became a trending business.

## A Network of Virtual Payments

Bitcoin is designed as a network of virtual payments. It features as a web-based version of credit card swipe device enabling merchants to process orders through phone, internet, or mail.

Payments that could be difficult with credit cards can be effected using the bitcoin through virtual payment platforms. The vendor needs to sign up for virtual terminal payment service by registering his or her payment information. These data include Name, Address, Card Verification Value, Type, and Number of Credit Card. The information will be used for online retail transactions.

These virtual payment plans don't require specialized software or hardware. The transactions are done through the website of the virtual payment provider. Therefore, a third-party provider will ensure that the requirements of the `PCIDSS` - Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard are met.

## What is Satoshi?

The smallest unit of the bitcoin cryptocurrency is called the satoshi. This was coined from the name of the founder of the cryptocurrency, Satoshi Nakamoto. He designed the protocol used in bitcoin and blockchain.

The rate of satoshi to bitcoin is one hundred million satoshis to one bitcoin. You can split bitcoins into smaller units to ease and facilitate smaller transactions.

As I have said, bitcoins exist in the digital world.

Therefore, one bitcoin (`BTC`) = 1,000 millibitcoins (`mBTC`)

One bitcoin = 1,000,000 microbitcoins (`µBTC`)

One bitcoin = One hundred thousand satoshis

However, it is believed that Satoshi Nakamoto may own up to one million bitcoins. This is equivalent to 100,000,000,000,000 satoshis. You can convert bitcoins to other currencies and from other currencies.
