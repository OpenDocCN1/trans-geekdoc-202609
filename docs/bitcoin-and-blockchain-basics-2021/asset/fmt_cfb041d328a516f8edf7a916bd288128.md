# Getting a Cryptocurrency License

You can get a cryptocurrency license if you want to set up and run a bitcoin exchange. You need to register your business with `FinCEN`, since every money service business must register with them. This will enable you to get a money transmitter license.

If you want to apply for a license, you can register on the website of `FinCEN` easily. But getting the license is really difficult. Examples of organizations that have money service licenses are American Express, Western Union, and PayPal.

Sometimes, you may wait for a longer time before obtaining a Money Transmitter License (`MTL`). Therefore, obtaining a cryptocurrency license is possible through `MTL`, although the fee is very high. In most cases, it can take many years.

Despite obtaining a registration license, the process of regulating bitcoin exchangers is not certain yet, even in the United States. Therefore, bitcoin exchangers may not comply with regulatory laws.

Here are a few considerations to make before proceeding:

*   Decide the scope of operations your exchange can handle. You may decide to run the business within a specific territory, region, nation, or internationally.
*   The second step is to undertake the legal requirements. Under legal considerations, decide whether to consult a legal professional to assist you to determine where to set up your office, the operational procedures, and structure of your firm. Also, consider how to minimize your exposure to risks and other ways to reduce your liability. Check the types of financial regulations, requirements, and compliances in your area. You can use `Merkle Tree` to obtain an insightful advanced international map of how the regulatory fields will be.
*   Determine the types of banking relationships to establish that will enable you to do your business successfully. But banks may be unwilling or reluctant in providing financial services to online money exchanges.
*   Consider the volume of your operations. You need to have enough active, live volumes to maintain a successful operation. This will enable you to transfer liquidity from large exchanges such as `Bitfinex` to your own bitcoin exchange. You will also determine how to boost your media presence, design your brand and maintain it, manage your messaging services, and handle ads and retargeting.
*   Differentiate your brand from others by defining whether it is an advanced bitcoin exchange for investors and traders or a retail bitcoin exchange.
*   Consider the technical requirements your exchange. This may involve a complex technical architecture that can handle deposits and withdrawals, including pairing of orders. These technical structures include risk management system, accounting and audit, matching engine, payment gateways, and wallet management.
*   Adopt a better security structure for your firm. Some security measures to adopt include 2-factor authentication for participants, anti-money laundering solutions (`AML`), know your customer (`KYC`), and ensure a secure data center for network and hardware security.
*   The last consideration is a standard internal security structure among your workers. This is also known as `CCSS` – Cryptocurrency Security Standard.

## What is Buy and Hold?

This is a clear-cut strategy on how to trade bitcoin and make money. To hodl stands for buying and holding bitcoin. It is easy and a convenient method of investing in bitcoin.

Acquire a bitcoin wallet and buy bitcoins (`BTC`). After purchasing, have the hope that the prices will rise in the future before you sell.

Hodl was a typographical error for hold. But in bitcoin communities, it was adopted as hold on for dear life.
