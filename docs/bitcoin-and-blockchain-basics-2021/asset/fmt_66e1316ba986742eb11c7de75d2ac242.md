
# Using Technical Analysis for Bitcoin Trading

This entails using real-life events in predicting the future of the market. With this, you can view previous statistics of cryptos and factors like movement and volume. This analysis will also access the inherent value by seeing patterns and analytical chart tools where the strengths and weaknesses of bitcoin are expressed.

In Bitcoin trading, technical analysis is based on the Dow Theory, which states that the prices of assets in the market involve a consideration of everything. In this case, everything incorporates investors' expectations, knowledge of traders, present, past, and future needs, including regulations in the market. The price is an indication of emotions and sentiments already developed by investors about a particular product.

The trends in cryptocurrency prices cause a repeat of history in that what happened before in trading could occur a second time. Movements in areas of prices are not random and technical analysis is based on this principle. These price fluctuations follow long-term or short-term trends.

Technical analysis concentrates on what is happening rather than the reason why it happens. Therefore, the focus is on supply and demand, and you may not be more focused on various variables influencing the price of movements.

## Analysis of Margin Trading

Trading bitcoin gives you the potential to double or triple your assets favorably. This is also called leverage trading. It is a kind of trading whereby borrowed funds are used for trading huge amounts of a particular asset.

This is a risky trading to manage on the bitcoin exchange. In this type of trading, you can borrow funds at a high interest rate to boost your trading capital.

In some cases, you can borrow up to 100% of your holdings. That is if you have one bitcoin, you can borrow another one to give you two bitcoins.

There are two features of margin trading, which are margin calls and liquidation.

### Liquidation

Liquidation occurs if a margin trade loses more cash than the investor has, the trade will be liquidated. In this instance, the position will be closed automatically and the investor loses all his assets and holdings.

### Margin Calls

To prevent liquidation order, you will be asked to add more money in your account, especially when a trade goes wrong. With margin call, when a trader is not able to bring more funds to secure the order, such order will be terminated automatically.

## How to Assess the Trends

Accessing the trends in the bitcoin network is necessary to stay abreast of all the happenings on the blockchain. You can use some monitoring tools, which are available on the internet to get the latest information about this evolving technology.

Here are some online sites that can provide accurate and updated statistical data for the bitcoin network:

1.  **Bitcoin Explorers** – are used for detecting a complete history of all the transactions that had taken place in the distributed ledger of the bitcoin technology.

2.  **Tradeblock** – used for analyzing the statistics of bitcoin network data. It provides a good number of graphical charts that will display the historical and present statistics of bitcoin. On the user interface on the site, you will see the number of mined blocks, network diagrams and charts, mempool count, and transaction count. It checkmates the Ethereum blockchain, although it has its own peculiar blockchains.

3.  **Node Counter** – helps in tracking the nodes of bitcoins throughout the network. It includes Bitcoin Classic, Core nodes, XT, and Bitcoin Unlimited (BU) in a graphical setting. Each table displays various nodes in the network, together with pools showing alternate bitcoin clients. It highlights data with pie charts and line graphs.

4.  **Open Exploration tool (OXT)** – it highlights transactions happening on the system. It uses different charting tools and plotting tools in detecting and analyzing various types of behavior on the network. These information include transactions, fees, scripts, and others. It shows you scatter plots and different perspectives of bitcoin address behavior, including transactions.

5.  **Coin Dance** – this is used for displaying different charts and summaries of nodes over the network. Core and XT including other types of charts are shown by the graphical user interface of the network. It is a good site for analyzing statistics on networks such as Paxful volumes and Localbitcoins.

6.  **Blockchain.info** is used for seeing the contents of a bitcoin. It also helps you to identify a particular transaction and view all transactions occurring within a network. The information is released on the fee paid for each transaction including the block height the transaction was added in. It also shows the number of confirmations that happened in the blockchain. You can also see mining information, the statistics of a currency, price for each BTC, including average block size. Others are the transaction count for each day, mempool size, block details, and other activities on the network.

7.  **Satoshi.info** is a real-time bitcoin network statistics. On the dashboard, you will see node counts, fee estimates, mempool data, system metrics, bandwidth usage, and more. The dashboard is designed to show different charts including specific sections of network and protocol.

8.  **Blockseer** is known as an exploratory blockchain tool. With this instrument, you can follow the bitcoin and see the interpretations of transactions on the system including the link with individual addresses. There is a visual diagram used for tracing the origins of bitcoins by following the path of bitcoins moving throughout the blockchain.
