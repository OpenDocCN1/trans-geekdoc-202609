
# The Lightning Network: A Second Layer Protocol

However, the second layer is the **Lightning Network**, a protocol on bitcoin. In this case, bitcoin is applied as a native denomination. This entails that only persons with the original bitcoin can use Lightning. That means **Lightning Network** is a cluster of bidirectional payment channels.

This new functionality layer enables a speedy transfer of bitcoin from peer-to-peer with a marginal variation from the first layer. With this, you can change channel balances but don't need instant settlement on the initial layer. From the foregoing, I can infer that unsettled or unclaimed bitcoin transactions could be regarded as Lightning transactions.

This situation in the blockchain network is risky. The reason is that cryptocurrencies stored in the **Lightning Network** protocols or channels of the system can be stolen by fraudulent participants. This can happen if node operators are not safeguarding the base layer and checking the nodes. Such fraudulent acts can clear all the cryptos in such channel.

The **New Functionality Layer (Lightning Network)** is important since it is a fully reserved routing network with zero sum. You can access and use this protocol if you have original bitcoin. Also, all the routing charges incurred by liquidity users are fully paid to liquidity providers. With this system, the **New Layer** can function using one of the real features of bitcoin, which is scarcity.

Another important feature of the **New Layer** is that Lightning does not carry the load of confirming the first layer. This makes exchanging bitcoin easier and constant without using up block space.

The first layer transactions can be expressed as financial agreements. This situation makes the protocol is featured as a capital market layer. The **New Layer** is designed as a market for liquidity and capital. With this system in place, you can distribute bitcoin all over the world without waiting for confirmation or approval by anyone.

Therefore, the **New Layer Functionality** features two core aspects of a financial transaction, which are risk premium and time value of the asset.

## What are the Risks in Using Bitcoin?

Here are some risks associated in using bitcoin:

*   It serves as an investment opportunity but no collateral to back up your investment.
*   Also, no government regulation to protect your cryptocurrency.
*   A risk of financial loss, as it is treated as a Ponzi scheme.
*   Bitcoin technology relies so much on digital technology.
*   Pool miners can create new blocks and withhold it from the network.
*   It is not a widely accepted currency.
*   Fluctuating market prices.
*   Bitcoin is prone to hacking, cyber-fraud, and other online thefts of cryptos.
*   Bitcoin is a new technology. The future prospects are not yet known. Invest carefully.

## How to Avoid the Risks

Bitcoin has the potentials of becoming a global currency useful for online monetary exchanges across borders without the issues of a national currency. Online trading is gaining more grounds making the launching of bitcoin in 2009 by Nakamoto, a successful venture.

There are some risks associated with the use of bitcoin, which you should know and understand how to avoid them.

1.  A constantly fluctuating and changing prices in the market. It is better to make little investments in bitcoin and wait for them to appreciate than investing much with the potential of losing more too.
2.  Fake bitcoin exchanges can lead to fraudulent deals. The Securities and Exchanges Commission including the Consumer Finance Protection Bureau have warned against these transactions.
3.  The investment is susceptible to theft by cybercriminals through hacking. Lost bitcoins cannot be recovered. Most persons lose their money during exchanges or mining. Hacking occurs on bitcoin exchanges too often, especially on bitcoin wallet.
4.  Bitcoin is an evolving technology. No one can tell what the future holds for cryptocurrencies. It may be banned globally or went into oblivion. Therefore, tread with caution and prepare your mind either for the worst or best of bitcoins on the market.
5.  Misplacement of the keys of your bitcoin wallet. Ensure that you store your private key on paper or other devices for easy retrieval.
6.  A mining pool can create a block using computational power and withhold or hide it from others.
7.  No regulation such as Central banks or government controlling the activities of investors on the bitcoin network.
8.  Bitcoin is used as an investment opportunity. Investors buy bitcoins and invest such for profitability.
9.  It is not taxable. This makes it to pose a great risk to government currency, due to competition in the market.
10. Bitcoin is a technologically-based financial instrument. Mining of bitcoins is done online. Storage of bitcoins is done with virtual wallets. Therefore, digital technology makes bitcoin worthwhile.
11. It has a limited use as most persons and organizations are yet to accept it as a form of financial exchange.
12. It poses an avenue for financial loss. Some persons regard it as a Ponzi scheme. It is the initiators at the top that benefit more than others.
