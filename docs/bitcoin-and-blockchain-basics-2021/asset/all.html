It enables different computing systems to harmonize on the nature of the network. Mining also secures the neutrality of the system. If you want to confirm the transaction, payments must be organized in a block that adheres to stringent cryptographic principles subject to verification.

Using Bitcoin in Exchanges

Do you know that bitcoins could be used in exchange? A digital marketplace where traders can sell or buy bitcoins with various altcoins is known as bitcoin exchange.  This online platform is an intermediary linking sellers of cryptocurrencies and the buyers as well. In professional terms, it is analyzed as an intermediary between the maker of cryptos and the taker.

This exchange serves like a brokerage where you can deposit cash using wire transfer, bank transfer, or bank deposit. Using bitcoin exchange usually has charges attached to it.

You will pay the currency conversion fee if you want to trade between cryptos. This is like trading different currencies in conventional banking system. Also, buying and selling bitcoin are based on the brokerage system where a taker sets a limit order that could be sold if a maker provides bitcoins that match the request.

Therefore, bitcoin exchange pairs takers with makers. In this case, they set the limit order or market order. When this limit is fixed and chosen, you are authorizing the exchange to trade at an available option or price prevalent in the marketplace.

After setting this limit, the trader will direct the platform to trade the cryptos at a price lower or above the present the bid.

CHAPTER THREE

How to Store Bitcoins

 

Between 2019 and 2020, bitcoins experienced a surge far above all other times. This has led to increased hacking activities of the network more than ever. However, most new investors don't know how to safeguard and secure their digital currencies.

Other things that can cause loss of cryptos include the loss of access to keys, failure of your PC, stealing of tokens, and other issues.

You can store your bitcoins securely in different ways, such as offline wallets or cold storage and hot wallets. These wallets are created on a PC device, smartphone, or through addresses and private keys on printed on paper.

Every wallet has private keys that can enable the owner of the cryptocurrency to access the funds stashed there. But if you lose the private key, then your investments may be gone forever.  

Cold wallet bitcoin storage is one of the safest means of keeping bitcoins. These wallets cannot be accessed online. Hot wallet bitcoin storage is accessible using the internet but still secure for most bitcoin owners.

But one of the safest means of storing cryptocurrencies involves a hardware wallet. This is long-term bitcoin storage.

Let's understand all these storage wallets in details.

Cold wallet

The safest storage system on the network is the cold wallet. It is also known as hardware wallet or offline wallet. This type of wallet is not attached to the internet. Therefore, it is not likely to be compromised.

They store the private key or the address of a user on a device that is not web-based. They provide a corresponding device that enables the user to connect and view their crypto portfolio without risking their address or private key.

An example of a cold wallet could be likened to a paper wallet. This is produced out of different websites. It generates public and private keys that are printable on paper. Here, there is no corresponding user interface for anything. No malware corruption or computer failure can affect your keys and address. It has been proven as the most secure way to safe-keep your cryptocurrencies.

Hot wallet

Hot wallets are operated on web-based devices such as tablets, PCs, and phones. Hot wallets are regarded as online wallets. These wallets produce the private keys to your online currencies. Accessing and managing your funds on these wallets are easy but it may compromise the security. Your funds could get stolen in this type of wallet.

Hot wallets are used for small amounts of money. It acts like a conventional checking account for urgent withdrawals and payments, while the other wallets are used for saving or other investments.

Hardware wallet

You can store your private keys securely. This has benefits over hot wallets, since it cannot be affected by a virus attack. The reason is that private keys in hardware wallets are not connected to any computer system.

 

Categories of Bitcoin Wallets

There are various types of bitcoin wallets for every user to adopt and secure their wallets. These include paper wallets, hardware, web wallets, desktop wallets, and mobile wallets.

Paper Wallets

This was one of the foremost wallets used for storing cryptocurrencies. It is designed for keeping your cryptoassets offline by enabling you to print your private keys and addresses. Then, you transfer your cryptos on it.

However, this type of wallet is almost out of date due to improvements in technology and other types of wallets that are easier to handle.

Hardware Wallets

Are you a long-term investor, then you need the hardware wallets. This is the safest type of wallet in the world of cryptocurrencies. It is designed for keeping public addresses and private keys.

Hardware wallets are devices in the form of USBs with an OLED screen. They also have side buttons for scrolling through the interface of the wallet. There are desktop apps for various cryptocurrencies. You can easily link it to a smartphone or computer system using a USB.

In this system, your address is displayed on the hardware wallet window (external screen) including the private key kept on the hardware wallet. This process makes storing your altcoins easy and very safe.

Web Wallets

These are types of hot wallets linked to the internet and can accessed through various browsers such as Internet Explorer, Google Chrome, and Firefox. These types of browsers are used for storing the private keys. It has been proven that they are prone to DDOS attacks. 

An example of a web wallet is using a crypto exchange to store altcoins. But in this system, you don't have access to the private key of your bitcoin wallet. Therefore, if the network closes down or is hacked, you may lose your cryptoassets.

Web assets are also called hosted assets. In this type of wallet, you don't have access to the private key.  Some of these wallets include Coinbase, Binance, etc.

Desktop Wallets

Desktop wallets are designed for desktop OS like Linux, Mac, and Windows. Most cryptocurrencies usually set up their own wallets from the beginning. Now, since you usually connect your system to the internet, you need to install antivirus software for protection against malware. Several desktop wallets have been designed for bitcoin such as electrum, exodus, and bitcoin core.

Mobile Wallets

The easiest and common wallets for bitcoins are the mobile wallets. These wallets are handy and can be accessed anytime and anywhere. These types of wallets can be used with both android and iOS devices.

CHAPTER FOUR

How to Mine Bitcoins

 

If you are interested in mining bitcoins but don't know how to start, this chapter will give you a better understanding of the processes.

  1. Acquire a mining rig for bitcoins

Bitcoin mining requires you to own a mining rig. At the onset of bitcoin mining, miners were using common domestic computers. After a while, they upgraded into graphic cards. Nowadays, the usage of these machines in bitcoin mining is becoming obsolete because you may not get little in a very long period.

Today, there are special machines designated for bitcoin mining. These hardware are also used in mining other currencies depending on the algorithm. This hardware is known as Application Specific Integrated Circuit chips (ASIC). This particular machine applies less power and mines bitcoins faster. It is a costly device but with a quick production capacity.

It can solve up to 14 terahashes per second. This means, each second, it makes up to one thousand and twelve attempts in solving a block.

 

Choosing an ASIC mining rig

 

Before, you begin mining; consider factors such as hash rate, price, and electricity consumption. Then, check the review some miners made about the devices and then pick a suitable one.

Also, calculate how profitable the particular device will be using an online calculator. You will calculate Coinwarz and Nice Hash. Also, you should project for uncertainties and fees from the mining pool.

Choose a convenient place to put your mining rig because ASIC machines could be too loud. You can increase your profits by staying in a cool corner, reduced electricity fees, and proper monitoring.

  1. Set up a Bitcoin Wallet

After acquiring and setting up your mining rig, the next step is to setup a bitcoin wallet. This is where you will store your bitcoins and use them as you wish. With bitcoin wallets, you can manage your bitcoin addresses. These addresses come with private keys.

This is like a bank account. To receive bitcoins, you will send your bitcoin address to the person and they will credit your account.

Every transaction made in bitcoin wallets is traceable. You need the private key to access your account and manage your funds. But if you misplace the key, you will lose your cryptoassets. 

Select from various bitcoin wallets available such as hot wallets, cold wallets, and hardware wallets. Save your private key on the device and store it on another device to ensure you do not lose your funds.

  1. Participate in Mining Pools

Be a part of a large mining farm to easily produce bitcoins because even with the best ASIC miner, it is still difficult to get a bitcoin. It can take quite a long time. But if you can co-operate with miners in mining pools, you can achieve results easily.

In a mining pool, the gains are shared equally among miners but you need to pay a token or percentage of your reward like two percent to the administrator of the pool.

One of the recommended bitcoin pools is slush pool. Beginners may need to try it. Slush pool is the first bitcoin pool in existence and has maintained credibility over time.

It was designed and propagated by Marek Palatinus, a programmer from Czech Republic.

If you discover the website of a mining pool, set up your account by registering your details. Then, you will get a worker Identification (ID) for your mining activities.

  1. Install a Mining Client to run on your Device

Let a mining client run on your device after you have acquired a hardware, bitcoin wallet, and a mining pool. Some mining have their own software for mining. This links you to the blockchain and bitcoin technology network.

This software sends work to the individual miners, collects the results of mining, and put the data back into the blockchain. The program also monitors the operations of each miner and displays statistics such as hash rate, mining speed, temperature, and cooling effects.

However, there are several software used in mining bitcoins that can run on various operational software. A suitable mining pool is Nice Hash Miner that can multiply cryptos and selects a most profitable algorithm at a time. It is easy to understand and use. Simply download the software and select the device you can use in mining it.

  1. Begin to Mine and Earn Bitcoins

After all these are done, begin to mine and earn bitcoins. Simply connect your ASIC device to a power outlet. Connect it to a computer system. Then, install a mining program and start mining.

What is Coordinated Mining?

Coordinated mining is the process of mining bitcoin by a group of miners using an operator's website. These separate miners earn tokens from the rewards shared to each of them at the end of a period. The earning may not be much but it is steady and consistent.

Mining Bitcoin in the Cloud

Mining bitcoin in the cloud is the same process referred to as cloud mining. This method is used for mining bitcoin through rented cloud computing power. In this case, you don't have to install and run the software on the hardware you want to use.

Interested persons are allowed to an account and engage in the process of bitcoin mining remotely. This is done for a reward of a defined cost. It makes mining accessible to many persons globally.

Cloud mining is done over the cloud. Therefore, the cost of maintenance of equipment and energy costs are minimal.

In addition, cloud mining involves buying or leasing of mining equipment from third-party providers. They are also responsible for equipment maintenance.

Types of Cloud Mining

Two renowned cloud mining procedures include leased hash power and hosted mining.

Leased Hash Power or Computing Power

This model involves the computing power related to bitcoins. Leased hash power comes from a mining farm. An investor benefits from the overall profits from mining cryptocurrencies in the bitcoin farm.

This is a common method of mining for cryptocurrencies instead of bitcoin itself. In this case, you should open an account with a cloud mining company through the website. Then, you will determine the hashing power and duration of the contract.  

Hosted Mining

This is a familiar model of cloud mining where the investor acquires mining hardware through leasing or purchases. The hardware is kept in the facility of the miner, who is responsible for maintaining the device and ensures that regular operations are carried out.

In this way, investors can manage their cryptos properly. Also, since it is a large farm, high costs of mining procured through electricity and storage will be minimal.

  • Enables regular investors and interested miners with no technical knowledge to mine bitcoin on a daily basis.
  • Reduces recurring expenditures and the costs of investment in hardware.

Drawbacks of Mining Bitcoin in the Cloud

There are two features of margin trading, which are margin calls and liquidation.

Liquidation

Liquidation occurs if a margin trade losses more cash than the investor has, the trade will be liquidated. In this instance, the position will be closed automatically and the investor losses all his assets and holdings.

Margin calls

To prevent liquidation order, you will be asked to add more money in your account, especially when a trade goes wrong. Wit margin call, when a trader is not able bring more funds to secure the order, such order will be terminated automatically.
