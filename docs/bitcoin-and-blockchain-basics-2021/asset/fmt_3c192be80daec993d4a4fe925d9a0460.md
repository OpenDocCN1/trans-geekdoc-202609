## What is a Decentralized Network?

Decentralized network involves different separate machines that are interconnected to provide a pool of resources. However, every node is controlled separately with unique guidelines for managing workload and maintaining data on the network. These networks function in a distributed form as they share processing power including workload activities among all the servers connected in the network.

Also, each node functions like an individual authority having separate decision making power about the way it communicates with other networks. But controlling all these interconnected servers could be difficult. However, with `IoT` (Internet of Things) devices and edge computing, a decentralized network can manage a complex structure of computer devices located remotely.

It is better to scale a network than just adding another computer system. This can increase your decentralized computing resources that are available.

A decentralized network is reliable as each node can operate separately. These interconnected systems form a pool of resources making the network secure and transparent. With this structure, if one system is hacked, the other interconnected devices will not be affected.

But building and managing decentralized network is a herculean task. You need to continue updating and patching your systems to maintain efficiency. Information needs to go through different routes before getting to the recipient and this may take some time. Also, the devices may not be too efficient depending on latency and power consumption.

## Bitcoin and Hackers

Hacking is not possible in the bitcoin network due to encryption of data. But altcoins can be stolen from a person's wallet, especially if your private key is misplaced. Also, you can lose bitcoins in an online bitcoin transaction.

The reasons that make hacking difficult in the blockchain network are:

1. Bitcoin is integrated into a blockchain technology that is not easy to hack.
2. Information is stored in a decentralized server making it difficult to hack. Users of these servers are always accessing and verifying the data to ensure transparency across the system. Therefore, if you want to hack a blockchain network, you need to break into a lot of servers or systems to fulfill your mission.
3. Wallet can be hacked but not the entire bitcoin technology.
4. There have been cases of phishing, cracking, hacking, and hijacking of the system but no successful attempt have ever been made.
