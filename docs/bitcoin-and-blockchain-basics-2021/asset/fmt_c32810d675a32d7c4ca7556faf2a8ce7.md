# How to Manage Price Fluctuation in Bitcoin

Various factors can cause the process of cryptos to fluctuate in the market. You can manage price fluctuations in bitcoin. This is regarded as spot rate on cryptocurrency exchanges. You can use the volatility index to measure volatility in the conventional trading markets. But, bitcoin volatility index is designed to checkmate and track how volatile this digital currency will become by market capitalization over a given period.

Let us access some of the factors leading to the volatile nature of bitcoin.

*   **News events or bad news** can affect the prices of bitcoins negatively. Such news includes banning of cryptos by some governments or news of possible regulation by authorities.
*   **The store of value against fiat currency.** This is known as perceived sways in prices. The developers of bitcoin want to limit its production to about twenty-one million bitcoin. This is against the management and distribution of fiat currency whereby the authorities want to minimize inflation, maintain high employment, and appreciable growth by investing in capital resources.
*   **The future of bitcoin is unknown** making it volatile as some investors cannot determine its worth as a store of value in the nearest future. A store of value makes an asset predictably useful in the future.
*   **The price of bitcoins in nations experiencing high rates of inflation** is much compared to developed countries.
*   **If there are large amounts of losses**, it can cause double volatility on the system as fear of losses can chase investors away.
*   **The risks of large currency owners such as above ten million US dollars.** The fear of investors with large amounts of cash causing a shaking in the system if they move large chunks of their investment out of the system. This can cause volatility in prices.
*   **Handling security fixes** can expose the community to risks that can make the system volatile.

# How to Become a Bitcoin Small Scale Exchanger

Becoming a bitcoin small-scale exchanger requires you installing a bitcoin exchanger software. Here are some relevant guidelines that can help you start a bitcoin exchange business in your area:

1.  **Define the particular area** you want to start your exchange business. Get the necessary approval by the authorities through registration and licensing for running a bitcoin exchange.
2.  **Understand the policies** controlling such financial operations in your region or country. You may need to consult a legal practitioner to be abreast of relevant laws guiding your financial services. This involves `KYC (Know Your Customer)` processes. This is necessary to process payments using fiat currency. You can select a reputable bank in your area and other digital payment processors to make your business easy.
3.  **Create a business relationship with a payment processor or a bank.** To meet the orders of your teeming customers, you need liquidity for your exchange to run smoothly. Other ways to do this are by implementing an `API` interface connecting your bitcoin exchange to other exchanges. Simulate trading activity by purchasing and selling two artificial accounts in your exchange, and join other network of cryptocurrency exchanges.
4.  **Design a transaction history and liquidity on the exchange.**
5.  **Apply a premium security architecture.** This is needed to secure your funds and that of your customers.
6.  **Provide support and assistance for your customers.** You need a standby staff to manage requests from customers, handle complaints, process the deposit and withdrawal of fiat currency.
7.  **Ensure your bitcoin exchange software has a trade engine, `UI (user interface)`, wallet, and administrative panel.**

Here is a brief analysis of the components of a bitcoin exchange software.

1.  **Trade engine** – this is major part of the exchange software. it accesses the order book, conducts transactions, tallies buy/sell orders, and determines customers' balances. This is the heart of your business and determines the functionality of the whole system.
2.  **User Interface (`UI`)** – this is also the front end of your website. This is the platform of your exchange and determines how your customers will view your business. Keep your platform mobile friendly, user-friendly, and intuitive. It helps you to register and access customer's accounts, purchase and sell orders, view charts, current orders, transactions history, including users' balances.
3.  **Wallet** – The bitcoin daemon operating on the server of your exchange. The cryptos of the exchanger and his customers are kept in these wallets. They include hot wallet and cold storage wallet.
4.  **Administrative panel** – incorporates the Intelligent and Management software of the exchange. It helps to alter liquidity, edits the fees for spread and trading, and handles currencies such as `ETH`, `USD`, `BTC`, etc. It also controls markets like `Bitcoin/Ethereum`, `US dollars/Bitcoin`, etc. This panel helps to handle all the request sent in by customers.

# Chapter Eleven: How to Sell Bitcoins on the Internet

You can sell bitcoins to investors in an exchange with fiat currencies such as `USD`, `Yen`, `Euro`, or other cryptocurrencies. Selling bitcoins on the Bitcoin official page is available to Europe and UK citizens.

Here are the steps for selling bitcoins online through bitcoin official page:

*   Go to the official sell bitcoin page (`https://sell.bitcoin.com`)
*   Choose the currency you want to sell and the amount.
*   Select if you want to get Euro or Pound Sterling.
*   Enter the amount you want to receive.
*   Review the data you have entered carefully.
*   Then, put your payment details and complete the sale process.

If you are using peer-to-peer trading platform, which is for Bitcoin Cash only, here are some steps to take:

*   Navigate to `Localbitcoin.com` and create an account.
*   Create a new sell order by yourself.
*   Also, you can locate an old buyer that wants to buy bitcoin cash (`BCH`).
*   Create a trade with the specific buyer and transfer your `BCH` to the blind escrow account.
*   If the buyer has sent your payment through bank transfer or other payment method. Then, you can confirm receipt of such funds.
*   After this, the `BCH` you have sold will be released from the blind escrow and transferred to the buyer's digital wallet.

If you are selling from a bitcoin exchange:

*   Go to any bitcoin or crypto exchange.
*   Register an account.
*   Verify your identity by following the prompts.
*   Follow the guidelines and sell your `BTC` or `BCH`.
*   If you sell for fiat currency, you will receive the cash into your bank account.
*   If you sell for another cryptocurrency, you can send the cryptos to your bitcoin wallet. However, ensure that your wallet can accept the cryptos before sending to your storage account.
