# 3.2. 4S-TFSM construction

Once the 4S-TFS features of a single DC have been extracted, the 4S-TFSM of the oil well DC data can be constructed. The sensors collect data at a certain time, and it is a common practice to generate $l$ DCs of these data according to the production cycle of the plunger pump, as shown in Fig. 3(a). The method we use is to form a 4S-TFSM with $l$ DCs. As shown in Fig. 3(d), the signature matrix $M_i$ of the $i$th segment $F$ is generated with $l$ DCs, in particular, by the inner product between the $l$ feature vector pairs of $F$. $l=2,4,6,8,\ldots$ \hfill (12)
