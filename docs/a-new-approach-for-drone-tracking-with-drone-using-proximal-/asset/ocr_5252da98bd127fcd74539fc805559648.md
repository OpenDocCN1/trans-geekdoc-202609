deep reinforcement learning software is more successful than the non-distributed software. According to the data obtained from the experiments, the number of steps performed in each iteration is approximately 67% higher in distributed training. According to the reward graphs of the training results, the distributed algorithm was more successful. In the algorithms trained in 300000-time steps, the second successful experiment had an average of –54.78 rewards during training. This value is –559.49 for the non-distributed algorithm.

According to the test results in Section 4.1, it was observed that the success of the first experiment in the training phase did not change in the test results. Accordingly, it is concluded that the UAV trained with the distributed PPO algorithm is more successful in tracking the target drone. Our study will contribute to filling the gap in the literature on the synchronized use of reinforcement learning with distributed systems in unmanned aerial vehicles. It will also help in developing realistic DDRL applications for unmanned aerial vehicles.

Our future plan is to develop a novel algorithm for drone detection and tracking so that the tracking drone can perform its mission successfully regardless of the environment.
