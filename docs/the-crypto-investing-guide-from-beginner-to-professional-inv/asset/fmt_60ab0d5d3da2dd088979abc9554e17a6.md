
# TIME IS OF THE ESSENCE

Knowing when to buy and sell your cryptocurrency is the crux of crypto investment. It can be a difficult part of the investment for newer investors to become accustomed to because it is something that is learned over time. However, there are some strategies to negotiate the market that can give you the edge over other investors. Employing a variety of different strategies maximises the potential for you to become a highly successful investor. Realistically, if you have confidence in your abilities, the potential for profiting is limitless. Your investment day is the first day of the rest of your life!

## Do Not Wait Too Long

Waiting for Bitcoin, Ethereum or other prices to drop to an all-time low could be endless. There is no way to predict future prices, so waiting can merely mean that prices will continue to rise and you will miss out on your initial profits. Invest a small amount in the first instance and follow the progressive trends on the market. You may see rises or falls but the chances are that you will see some return on your investment. Timing the market exactly is almost impossible, so jumping in and starting your crypto investment portfolio means that you get to dip your toes in the water.

## Buy When They Sell and Sell When They Buy

When other investors start to sell a cryptocurrency, this is a neon sign that you should buy it. As others offload the currency, the prices will naturally decline. This makes a currency ripe for purchase and allows you to get it at a much lower price than you would achieve when the market is stable. Similarly, if you see that other investors are rapidly snapping up crypto-coins of a particular currency, this can be an excellent time to sell. When a currency is more in demand, the prices start to increase. Of course, they may cap out at a specific price but do not be tempted to sell too early. At the same time, do not wait too long. Selling at precisely the right time before there is a selling boom can get you the maximum value and secure the biggest return on your investment.

You can track the price of various coins on apps like `Blockfolio`, `CoinCap` and `Coinfolio`, allowing you to monitor the accumulation of cryptocurrencies. If a coin’s price has been slowly but steadily increasing for a prolonged time period, it can be a good investment that will continue to rise in the future.

## Sell High and Buy Low

Crypto markets regularly experience dips and troughs. This is an excellent time to buy. For example, if you buy one thousand units of a specific cryptocurrency for £0.10 and then sell it for £10 each one year later, you could make thousands of pounds worth of profits. From a small investment, you can experience an excellent return. You can then sink the money into more cryptocurrencies that are experiencing dips and repeat the cycle. Of course, you may not always experience these kinds of extreme highs and lows, but there are enough chances to invest in this way to give you substantial room for making money. It is important to remain patient, however. Buying or selling too early can hurt your profit margins.

## Stay with It for the Long Haul

Holding a currency for a prolonged period of time can be an extremely good way of making a profit. With this strategy, you do not need to worry as much about timing the market. Many cryptocurrency investors claim to have made significant sums of money by simply holding their currencies and waiting for them to accumulate. Holding your currency even during extreme dips can be profitable because there are inevitable rises on the horizon. In crypto terminology, investors who stick with a currency for the long-term, despite any sharp rises or falls are known as **strong hands**.

## Only Sell the Profits

One great way of playing the market is to retain your initial investment when cryptocurrencies experience an overall rise. For example, if you buy £1000 worth of Bitcoins and several months later your Bitcoins are worth £10,000, you would sell £9,000 worth of the coins and retain the rest. This is the equivalent of cashing out the interest on traditional stocks and shares whilst still keeping some of the shares as a part of your portfolio. Once you have sold the profits, you can then use them to buy other cryptocurrencies that are falling in price or you can wait until your current cryptocurrency falls and then repurchase those same coins and wait for the cycle to repeat itself.

# DAY EIGHT: DON’T DO IT ALONE

Investment in cryptocurrency does not have to be a solitary pursuit. These days there are forums on the internet that can allow you to discuss investment strategies with other investors. Many of the members on these kinds of forums are veterans and can give you current advice about what the best currency is to invest in today. Remember that the constant fluctuations in the market can mean that it is ever-changing, so getting weekly up-to-date advice can be extremely beneficial.

On forums, you can also discuss innovations in blockchain technology, ICOs and any new coins that are just emerging. This can allow you to get in at ground zero with a new currency and provide you with the opportunity to ensure that you do not miss out on rock bottom prices. Remember that lower prices tend to lead to the greatest returns on your investments.

Furthermore, you can cultivate your interest in crypto and stay abreast of the latest news. The crypto markets are exceptionally fast-paced and forums can even allow you to source the most recent updates from developers. This is great if you want to be confident in investing in a specific currency because new updates or more available currency can be a hint as to whether it is time to buy or sell your coins.

Many forums are populated by tipsters who can give some price predictions for imminent rises or falls in various currencies. Social media is also an excellent way to connect with these tipsters and you will find them on sites like `Twitter`. However, it is also important to have a modicum of wariness when it comes to taking tips from online sites. While many tipsters have a proven track record of investment, others are simply conveying their opinions. Therefore, also use your instinct as a guide, even when you are engaging with others online.

One of the great things about cryptocurrency investment is that it provides you with the opportunity to become an integral part of a close community. For some investors, these close connections are just as enjoyable as the process of investing itself and any profits they make on their investments.

# DAY NINE: TIME TO BUY

Welcome to day nine which is also known as **INVESTMENT DAY**! On day nine of your ten days, you are ready to buy your cryptocurrency and launch into the markets. At this point, you have acquired sufficient knowledge to understand which currency is the best for you, how to store it and which exchanges to use to facilitate your trading or investment needs. Of course, you want to ensure that you remember to maximise your returns while minimising your risks.

Today is the day to go ahead and buy one or more cryptocurrencies, bearing in mind everything that you have learned so far. You have everything in place to finally dip your toes into the water and take that step of making your first transaction. As this is your initial investment, remember that you do not have to invest a large amount of capital into the market. Only invest what you can afford, be it £10 or £10,000.

## Recap

*   Choose the right cryptocurrencies for you, buying at the lowest possible prices.
*   Find a safe and secure crypto-wallet to store your cryptocurrencies.
*   Select the right currency exchange that provides the perfect balance of security and simplicity of use. Remember to balance any fees that are charged with the level of safety that the exchange offers.
*   Apply your knowledge of risk minimisation and returns maximisation to your strategy.
