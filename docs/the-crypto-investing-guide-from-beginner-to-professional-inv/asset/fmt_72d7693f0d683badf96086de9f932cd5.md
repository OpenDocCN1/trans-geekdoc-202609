
# MAKE AN INVESTMENT PLAN

Investing in cryptocurrency can be a short-term or long-term investment. If you are looking for a get-rich-quick scheme, cryptocurrency investment can sometimes give you a good return on your money. However, to succeed at this, you would need to employ the highest risk strategies which could leave you lamenting substantial losses at the end of it. Within hours or days, you can see your profits start to roll in, although you can wait longer if you prefer a holding strategy.

In reality, cryptocurrency investment can be a longer-term investment for committed investors who are prepared to ride out some of the rises and falls in an extremely volatile market. This is an exciting proposition that can provide you with some exhilarating adventures along the way. Essentially, you are playing a game of strategy in the same way that chess players do. Your aim is to stay one step ahead of the game so that you can ensure you have full awareness of market trends and do not get caught out.

On day three, we are focusing on making your investment plan and deciding how to manage your investments.

## Long-Term Investment Strategy Considerations

When you are considering a long-term investment strategy, there are several things to bear in mind.

**Firstly, do not panic buy.** If you see the market is falling and think now is the right time for investment, remember that the price of the currency could continue to fall. Therefore, you might get a lower price if you are patient and wait a little longer before making that crucial investment. Of course, it is possible to wait too long and the prices will end up rising again. Therefore, you need to experiment with the markets for a while to get a good feel of the right price to make your investment.

**Similarly, panic selling can be a big mistake.** If you are invested for the long haul, do not worry when the prices of your cryptocurrency dip as this is natural. Fluctuations will constantly happen and a fall in the price of your Bitcoins or altcoins does not necessarily mean that the market is in freefall. If anything, consider that as the right time to make further investments because when the prices inevitably rise again, you could see a larger profit on your investments.

Cryptocurrencies can appreciate considerably over the long term. They can also depreciate in the shorter term. Therefore, you need to remember that one of the key skills that are essential for investors is patience. Unlike some of the assets on the traditional stock market or Forex investment, cryptocurrencies tend to bounce back, especially the more established ones like Bitcoin. This means that even if you are doubtful or worried about your investment, you can rest assured that you will likely see an increase in your investment over time.

**Remember to remain calm too.** Panicking can be counterproductive and lead you to make impulsive decisions, like selling your assets on a whim if they fall. Make a plan and allow yourself to be guided by historical and current market trends. While there may be times when you need to make snap decisions about buying or selling, try not to wildly deviate from your plan unless you cannot help it.

## Climb onto the Crypto-Ladder

You’ve chosen your cryptocurrencies; you’ve chosen your crypto-exchange and you’ve chosen your crypto-wallet. With everything in place, it can still seem like a daunting prospect to finally dip your toes in the water and make that first investment. On day three it is time to make a plan as to the investment strategies that you are going to use. As mentioned earlier, you now have some more choices to make: trading or investing. These two ways of playing the cryptocurrency market are fundamentally different.

### Investing (Long-Term Holding)

| Characteristic | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Holding Period** | Often involves the longer-term holding of assets regardless of dips in the market. |
| **Frequency** | Buy and sell new coins less frequently. |
| **Analysis** | Uses fundamental analysis. |
| **Risk Profile** | Suitable for anyone who is more risk-averse. |

Investors tend to look at the bigger picture and consider the longer-term futures of the market.

### Trading (Short-Term Profits)

On the other hand, traders are more concerned with daily and even hourly price fluctuations. The objective for traders is to make quick profits in the shorter term. As there is extreme volatility in some parts of the cryptocurrency market, this allows traders the scope to buy an asset in the morning and double their return by the evening. However, it can be a risky strategy because it involves in-depth predictions of how the markets are going to behave. It is generally more suited to people who have been following market trends for a prolonged period of time. Even then, it can still be difficult to fully time the market in short spaces of time.

Within the traders’ bracket, there are different variations of traders that conduct trading in different manners. These include momentum traders, day traders, swing traders and scalpers.

*   **Scalpers:** The most active form of trading is scalping and this can involve buying and selling crypto coins multiple times every day. Sometimes the trades can take place within the space of a few seconds. While the profits on each transaction may be extremely small, as there is a large volume of trades, it can ultimately add up to significant profits.
*   **Day Traders:** Day traders are slightly less active than scalpers in terms of transaction volume but they do not hold the coins for prolonged periods of time. Most day traders ensure that they do not hold any of their coins by the end of the day.
*   **Momentum Traders:** Momentum traders are similar to day traders in the manner in which they trade with the difference being that they will hold their assets for slightly longer periods of time, usually with a limit of a few weeks for any specific cryptocurrency.
*   **Swing Traders:** Swing traders attempt to predict where the large price increases will occur to take advantage of low prices before there are exponential increases.

One of the primary attractions for traders is that there is the potential for exceptional returns. Most traders are risk-takers so they are prepared to endure the high risks that are associated with trading in order to incur the potential rewards that can be reaped. The risks can be ramped up even further by engaging in what is known as **margin trading**. This involves borrowing money from a third party to trade. While the extra capital gives you the potential for bigger returns, it can be a disaster if you predict the market fluctuations wrongly. However, for anyone who enjoys the excitement of the riskier trading side of cryptocurrency, it can be a great way to make money.

### Investor vs. Trader Analysis

While traders tend to analyse the market by looking at an asset’s intrinsic value (technical analysis), investors are more focused on tracking the history of market data and forecasting the price rises and falls based on this method (fundamental analysis). Investors can therefore focus on the bigger picture and concentrate on longer-term investments that may not give the same scope for overall profits but still offer an excellent return on a slightly lower-risk form of investment.

Ultimately, if you are a new investor to the market, then making the choice between trading and investing or engaging in a combination of both will depend on how much risk you are prepared to take. The amount of risk can often be directly correlated to the amount of reward that lies at the end of the strategy. Some investors also engage in trading and vice versa so it is important to find the balance that works for you. At the start of your journey, you may want to dabble in both trading and investing. However, remember to only invest money that you can afford to lose because there are pitfalls that can trap both traders and investors in the crypto market.
