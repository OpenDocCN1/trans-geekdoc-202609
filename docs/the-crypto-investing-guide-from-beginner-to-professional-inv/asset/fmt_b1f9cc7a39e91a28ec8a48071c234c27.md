## 稳定币前景展望

Moving forward, it is likely that stable-coins will become more popular, although they offer the drawback that if the value of the collateral falls, then this can negatively impact the value of the stable-coin. This means that they are not a risk-free route into crypto investment. However, their relative stability compared with some of the more volatile cryptocurrencies means that they are an attractive proposition for newer investors who do not want to risk all their initial capital. Stable-coins can also be an excellent option for more seasoned investors who are looking to diversify their portfolios with some slightly lower-risk alternatives to the major crypto-coins.
