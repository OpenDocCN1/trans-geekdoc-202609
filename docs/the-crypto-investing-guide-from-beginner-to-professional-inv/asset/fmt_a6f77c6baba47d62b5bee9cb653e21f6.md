## Real-world investment strategies

When you are applying the strategies that you have learned in this book to your real-life investments, it is important to keep in mind that prices and returns can all be relative. For example, one Ether was worth US $111 in 2018. At the same time, one Bitcoin was valued at more than US $7,000. If you invested in 10 Ethers in 2018, this would have cost $1110. If you then traded them at their 2021 rate of $2,760 each, you could have made a potential profit of $26,490. If you spent $1110 on buying Bitcoins in 2018 and then traded them at their rate in 2021, the profit margins could have been more than $8,000. This highlights the value of going for currencies that cost less because the return potential is simply higher.

To some investors, it may look like Ethereum is only worth a tiny fraction of the overall worth of Bitcoin, even in the current day. However, because the relative increase on Ethereum is considerably sharper, this means that the profit margins would be larger. It is also important to remember that when you invest in a currency that gleans a lower price, you can simply buy more of it. Therefore, when you see a significant increase in the price per coin, you can have the confidence that you are ultimately going to make more profit. The same is true for many altcoins that appear to command a relatively low value compared to Bitcoin, which has essentially become the gold standard for cryptocurrencies in the modern-day and age. Altcoins might have a modicum more risk attached than Bitcoin simply because they have not been around as long and they are not as established as Bitcoins. However, they can translate small investments into larger returns for both traders and longer-term investors.

# DAY SIX: NEGOTIATING THE MARKET

As with any market, crypto markets can rise and fall and the value of individual cryptos can increase and decrease. For first-time investors, this can be a frightening proposition. However, when you know what factors can cause these rises and falls, it is significantly easier to negotiate the market. On day six, we are going to discuss how to successfully negotiate the market to understand the reasons behind rises and falls within it.

The natural supply of a cryptocurrency can affect pricing. As we covered earlier, many cryptos are mined. For example, if a substantial amount of Bitcoin is mined in a short space of time, this means that there is more of it available. When there is more of a cryptocurrency on the market, the lack of scarcity can make it slightly less valuable. This can drive down the prices. Conversely, if a currency is in short supply and the demand for it grows, then that will drive up the value and increase the prices that investors will pay.

When there is hype surrounding a cryptocurrency, whether it is a new or established crypto, this can cause prices to rise. The internet is often a source of this hype and regular posters on forums can often influence the market by starting trends that directly increase or decrease pricing. Even social media influencers who have a substantial following drive these trends. As traditional financial currencies and cryptocurrencies start to become more linked, this is likely to be a more crucial factor in how the markets behave. For example, as Visa starts to record more of its cryptocurrency transactions in Ethereum, the supply of the currency may fall and the price may be consequently driven to higher levels.

To calculate the predicted price of the cryptocurrency, you can divide the market cap by the circulating supply. While this is only a predictive equation, it can give you a general idea of the price rises of any crypto going forward. Remember that this formula does not account for an increase in the supply of crypto.

Tracking the prices of cryptocurrencies using various apps, such as Coinfolio or Blockfolio can allow you to negotiate the markets more easily. This allows you to access features such as setting alerts when prices reach a specific level. Using this technology to track the market ensures that you can stay ahead of the game with relative ease. Rather than constantly watching the markets, you can use these apps to your advantage and sit back and wait for the rises or falls that you are looking for.
