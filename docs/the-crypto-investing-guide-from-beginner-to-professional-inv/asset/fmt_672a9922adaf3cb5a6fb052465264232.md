## What gives cryptocurrency the edge?

Traditional investing is not dead and buried. In fact, many people who play the stock markets or engage in other investment strategies also invest in cryptocurrencies. So, what is it that makes cryptocurrency such a sound investment in the current age?

Firstly, when you invest in cryptocurrency now, you are getting into the market early enough to ensure that there is substantial scope for growth. While it is not an entirely new phenomenon, it is new enough that there is a significant amount of room to flourish within in. As an example, think of Google. When Google was new and innovative, shares sold for an extremely low price. Years later, you could make forty times the return for selling your original shares. Cryptocurrency is similar in that early investment gives you an excellent possibility for long term returns.

What’s more, is that many investors use the fluctuations in the market to their advantage. As prices can rise extremely rapidly, they identify the ideal times to buy and sell the currencies. This is not a skill that can be taught but it can be learned through practice and experience. By starting small and growing your portfolio over time you can pick up a feel for when the right times are to trade your crypto-coins.

Cryptocurrencies have become so popular, in part, because of the transactional advantages that they hold over traditional financial institutions. For example, with Bitcoin, you have no set hours when you can make a transaction. The markets are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week and 364 days a year. In contrast, most banks have limited hours and certainly do not have the same scope for opening that cryptocurrency provides.

When it comes to transaction fees these can vary between one cryptocurrency and another and will also change depending on the crypto exchange that you use if you trade or invest in cryptocurrency. Additionally, you will not need to identify yourself when you sign up for the majority of crypto exchanges or trade/invest in cryptocurrencies.

One of the main advantages of the cryptocurrency market compared to other potential assets such as the stock market is the maturity level. Cryptocurrencies are relatively new in comparison to the various global stock exchanges. This means that there is still extensive potential for growth. When investors buy stocks and shares on the stock exchange, they may need significant initial capital to make an ample return on their investment. The same is not true for investments or trading on the crypto market. As there are currencies available for all budgets, it can be a desirable way to use a small amount of capital to generate a large volume of profits.

Traders can also benefit much more significantly from the crypto markets than they could from other traditional forms of investment. For example, scalpers rely on the volatility of the crypto markets and this is not offered by the stock market. While there are rises and falls in traditional stocks, there are generally few wild shifts in the space of seconds or minutes. If you are intending to trade, the crypto market is the ideal forum for your requirements.

The relative simplicity with which you can start investing in the cryptocurrency market also makes it an easy and quick way to become an investor. If you wanted to buy shares on the stock exchange, you would need a broker and you would be restricted by the opening and closing times of the worldwide markets. On the other hand, the crypto market is constantly available for investors to buy and sell. What’s more, you can use a crypto exchange yourself without the need to find a broker to give you advice or facilitate your trades or transactions. Trading and investing are a lot swifter when you are dealing with cryptocurrencies.

While cryptocurrencies are considered to be high-risk investments, when you have a diverse portfolio of cryptos you can find that your assets are at a lower risk than they would be if you invested your capital in the stock market. This is because cryptos do not tend to rise and fall with market trends. The trends for cryptos are more singular and specific to each currency. In contrast, when the stock market crashes or collapses, all the stocks and shares that are listed on them tend to plummet in value. There have been many historical examples of this, such as the 1929 Wall Street Crash which led to an extreme and prolonged depression.

All in all, cryptocurrencies have a vast reach and they are not limited by country or territory. They tend to be more insulated when specific global events occur which gives more protection for your assets. It is a desirable facet for cryptocurrencies that they are unregulated and do not have substantial fees attached to them. This can keep costs down for investors and traders and allow greater scope for freedom when you are a new investor. Essentially, you ate not limited by arbitrary rules. While it is true that there are some associated risks and loss potential with cryptocurrency, for many investors the rewards considerably outweigh all of the risks.

## What does the future hold for cryptocurrencies?

Over the years, as demand for cryptocurrency has increased, the potential and scope for investors have also widened. However, while the volatile fluctuations in the market can be extremely exciting and enticing for a large percentage of investors, they can also lead to extreme losses for both novice and seasoned investors. Consequently, many aspiring investors are looking for alternative crypto investment strategies.

Many investors have turned to stable-coins like Tether as a way to ensure that their assets will not be lost even if there is a crash or collapse in the cryptocurrency market. Stable-coins are essentially a form of cryptocurrency whose value is backed by other assets, currencies or commodities. These forms of cryptocurrency reduce the underlying financial risks that are often associated with investing in cryptocurrencies because their risk factors are linked to the asset that backs the currency rather than the currency itself.
