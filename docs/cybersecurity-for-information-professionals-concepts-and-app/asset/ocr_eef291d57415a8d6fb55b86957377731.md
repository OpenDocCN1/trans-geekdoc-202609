In the ever-changing cybersecurity domain, it is crucial to help students keep pace with changing work roles and requirements in cybersecurity. CyberSeek (2018) surveyed job opening and created a heat map with visualizations to display their data collected between April 2017 and March 2018. In terms of understanding current job trends in cybersecurity workforce, we used the CyberSeek data grouped by the NICE Framework Cybersecurity Workforce Category to depict the proportion of job openings by categories (Figure 4.5). The breakdown of each category by percentages is dominated by Operate and Maintain (26%) and Securely Provision (24%), followed by Analyze (16%), Protect and Defend (16%), and Oversee and Govern (11%). Within the seven categories, Collect and Operate (6%) and Investigate (1%) have relatively few job openings to fill the various roles in cybersecurity workforce.
