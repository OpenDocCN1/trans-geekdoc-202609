# ## 14.1 Infectious Diseases the Invisible Enemies

Emerging infectious diseases are the global concern, causing millions of deaths annually. Understanding their behaviour and predicting outbreaks is fundamental not only for public health but also for advancing prediction techniques that can be applied in other fields.

Microorganisms, including bacteria and viruses, adapt and evolve at a rate much faster than humans. For example, the generation of bacteria can be as short as 20-30 minutes, while viruses replicate in shorter time frames. This rapid adaptation allows pathogens to evolve quickly, developing resistance to treatments and evading the host's immune system.

Infectious diseases follow a multi-stage progression that begins when the inventive agent, whether viral, bacterial, or parasitic, begins to thrive and multiply throughout the body² and the process of infection starts.
