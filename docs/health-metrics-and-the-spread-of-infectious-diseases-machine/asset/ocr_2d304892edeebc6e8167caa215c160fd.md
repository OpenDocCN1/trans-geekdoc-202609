Among the indicators available in the hmsidvr::gholifetables dataset, we specifically focus on the indicator denoted as eₓ. The expectation of life at age x (ex) refers to the average number of additional years a person is expected to live, given that they have already reached x. This measure is commonly referred to as life expectancy at age x. This dataset is part of the Global Health Observatory (GHO) data repository, which is maintained by the World Health Organization (WHO). The dataset also contains life table indicators, such as the number of person-years lived above age x (lx), the number of person-years lived between ages x and x+n (nLx), the age-specific death rate between ages x and x+n (n_x), the number of people dying between ages x and x+n (ndx), and the probability of dying between ages x and x+n (nq).
