```
simulated_data <- cbind(simulated_data,
```

Simulated data are split into **training** and **testing** sets to train the model and evaluate its performance. What this means is that the model is trained on a subset of the data (the training set) and tested on the remaining data (the testing set) to assess its predictive performance.

```
# Load required packages
library(randomForest)

# Split the data into training and testing sets
set.seed(123)
train_index <- sample(nrow(simulated_data),
                      0.8 * nrow(simulated_data), ]
```
