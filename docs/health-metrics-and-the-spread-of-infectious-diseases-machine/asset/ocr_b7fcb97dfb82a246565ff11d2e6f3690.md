The difference in number of cases and deaths between the US and China is striking. The US has the highest number of cases and deaths, while China has the lowest number of cases and deaths. The United Kingdom and Canada have a similar number of cases and deaths, but the United Kingdom has a higher case fatality rate CFR than Canada.

Let's calculate the DALYs for COVID-19, starting with the YLLs. We know that the simplified formula for YLLs is the number of deaths multiplied by the standard life expectancy at the age of death. The standard life expectancy for these countries varies, but we use the global average of 72.6 years.

Also, we group by 4 month cycles to calculate the YLLs.

```
COVID19_4month_counts <- COVID19_4counts %%
mmutate(month = as.numeric(format(date, "%%m")),
year = as.numeric(format(date, "%%Y")),
month_cycle = case_when(month %in% 1:4 ~ "Jan-Apr",
month %in% 5:8 ~ "May-Aug",
month %in% 9:12 ~ "Sep-Dec") %>%%
group_by(country_region, year, month_cycle) %>%%
sumise(cases = sum(cases),
deaths = sum(deaths),
 = round(deaths / cases, 3)) %>%%
mutate(ylls = deaths * 72.6) %%
arrange(year, month_cycle, ylls)
```
