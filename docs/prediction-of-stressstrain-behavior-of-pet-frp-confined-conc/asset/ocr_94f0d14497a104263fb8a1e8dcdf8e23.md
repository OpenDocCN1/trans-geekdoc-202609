# 4.1.2 Random Forest Regression (RFR)

RFR was initially proposed by Breiman [91] and adds a randomization layer to the bagging technique. Due to the Law of Large Numbers, this method’s strength rests in its reduced susceptibility to overfitting, making it a valuable instrument for predictive modeling. RFR excels at both classification and regression tasks through the intelligent incorporation of controlled variance. It constructs each tree of the ensemble using a distinct data aggregation approach.
