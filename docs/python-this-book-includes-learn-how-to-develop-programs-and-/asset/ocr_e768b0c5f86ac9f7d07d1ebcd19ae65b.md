The complete process of machine learning is much more extensive than just the development and application of machine learning algorithms and can be divided into steps below:

+   - Define the goals of the project taking into careful consideration all the prior knowledge and domain expertise available. Goals can easily become ambiguous since there are always additional things you want to achieve than practically possible to implement.
- The data pre-processing and cleaning must result in a high-quality data set. This is the most critical and time-consuming step of the whole project. The larger the volume of data, the more noise it brings to the training data set which must be eradicated before feeding to the learner system.
- Selection of appropriate learning model to meet the requirements of your project. This process tends to be rather simple given the various types of data models available in the market.
- Depending on the domain the machine learning model is applied to, the results may or may not provide a clear understanding of the model by human experts as long as the model can successfully deliver desired results.
- The final step is to consolidate and deploy the knowledge information gathered from the model to be used on an industrial level.
- The whole cycle from step 1 to 5 listed above is iteratively repeated
