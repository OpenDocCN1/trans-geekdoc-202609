Decision trees are the easiest of the predictive models, and here are some of their advantages:

+   1. It’s easy to communicate and visualize decision trees.
2. It’s possible to discover old patterns. Say you want to determine the voting pattern between two parties for an election, and you have data on education, sex, age, and income. You may identify a pattern where highly educated people have a very low income and vote for a certain party.
3. Decision trees make the least assumptions on the data.
