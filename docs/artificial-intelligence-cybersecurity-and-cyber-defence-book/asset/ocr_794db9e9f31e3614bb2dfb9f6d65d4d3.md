– The notion of “control” is common in the debates. Contributors insist on the necessity and the difficulty of “controlling” AI systems, but control is a strictly technical notion (ensuring that the system is secure, that it is not hacked, that its data is not altered, ensuring that the control of systems or machines called “automatons” is maintained) and does not create the challenges of police control or social control, for example.
