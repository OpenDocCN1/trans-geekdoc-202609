Despite their success, expert systems have been the subject of much criticism, in particular because of the mistakes they can make if the problems raised go beyond the strict framework of their area of expertise. For example, when faced with a victim of a gunshot wound, the symptom is the bleeding, the expert system specializes in searching for infectious organisms and it will search for bacteria responsible for the bleeding. The expert system is unable to leave its field on its own. The expert system only works in its own little world. It also has no common sense, it is unable to understand or analyze the effects of its recommendations on the context.
