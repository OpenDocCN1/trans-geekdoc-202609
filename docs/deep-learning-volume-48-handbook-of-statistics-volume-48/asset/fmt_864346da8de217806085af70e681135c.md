
Facial analysis technology has been around for a long time (`[Masi et al., 2018](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0530)`). Over the years, faces have become the leading biometric modality used in a majority of authentication-based applications. The wide adoption of faces as the biometric modality of choice has been attributed to its non-invasive nature of capture. Facial analysis tasks popularly include tasks of detection, recognition, and attribute prediction using face images (`[Section 2](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#s0015)`). These tasks have been studied extensively in the literature (`[Wang and Deng, 2021](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0835)`; `[Zheng et al., 2020](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0945)`). With the onset of deep learning-based algorithms, the accuracy of face recognition has peaked at 99.83% on the popular LFW dataset (`[Chrysos et al., 2020](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0155)`). As a result, facial analysis systems have become widely accessible. Besides being used for biometric applications, these systems often play a massive role in complex decision-making tasks such as risk assessment, criminal sentencing, and healthcare diagnostics (`[Garvie et al., 2016](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0280)`; `[Osoba and Welser IV, 2017](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0605)`; `[Washington, 2018](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0880)`), as well as in various daily-life applications involving smartphones (`[Ignatov et al., 2019](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0340)`). Consequently, facial analysis systems have become an inevitable part of today's society. When employed for complex tasks, the decisions made by these systems affect important aspects of our lives, especially when these decisions involve making predictions about individuals. These systems typically take a face image as input and provide the relevant output for the task at hand using an algorithm. With black-box deep learning models becoming the norm for facial analysis algorithms (`[Masi et al., 2018](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0530)`; `[Zheng et al., 2020](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0945)`) and the lack of evidence to support decisions made by these models (`[Castelvecchi, 2016](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0130)`), it has become critical to ensure that we can trust the model decisions.

The widespread usage of facial analysis systems revealed concerns about the fairness of these systems when several of these systems were found to be biased against certain demographic groups, as shown in `![Fig. 1 Illustrating biased predictions of deep models to favor/disfavor certain demographic subgroups (Singh et al., 2022).](images/S0169716123000020/f07-01-9780443184307.jpg)` (`[Osoba and Welser IV, 2017](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0605)`; `[Singh et al., 2022](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0755)`).
