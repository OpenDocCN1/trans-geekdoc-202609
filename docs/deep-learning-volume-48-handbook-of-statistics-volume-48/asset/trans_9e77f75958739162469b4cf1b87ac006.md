
-   [阿利等人 (2018)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0055) 提出了一种联合学习和遗忘算法，用于消除网络特征表示中的偏见。该算法能够在网络在极度偏向的数据库上训练时，消除偏见。一组算法旨在遗忘模型对敏感属性的依赖。[Kim et al. (2019b)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0420) 提出了一个正则化损失，用于最小化特征嵌入与偏见之间的相互信息，从而遗忘偏见信息。[Nagpal et al. (2020b)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0580) 提出了属性感知过滤丢弃，它在执行主要属性分类任务的同时，遗忘模型对敏感属性的依赖。所使用的方法如下 [Fig. 12](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#f0065)。[Tartaglione et al. (2021)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0785) 提出了一种正则化策略，用于在保持同一目标类特征纠缠的同时，解耦偏向特征。此外，还提出了使用特征蒸馏 ([Jung et al., 2021](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0390)) 和学习表征之间的相互信息的技术。为了理解不同的偏见缓解算法，[Wang et al. (2020)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0860) 提供了对现有偏见缓解技术的深入分析，并进一步设计了一种域独立的偏见缓解训练技术。[Chuang and Mroueh (2021)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0160) 提出了一个有趣的数据增强策略，即 *fair mixup*，用于优化群公平约束。作者提出在人口统计学群体的不同子群之间进行插值分布的正则化。最近，[Park et al. (2021)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0615) 指出，在决策过程中消除敏感属性信息的方法存在消除有益信息的局限性。为了克服这一限制，作者提出了 Fairness-aware Disentangling Variational Auto-Encoder，用于将数据表示解耦为三个潜在子空间。提出了一种去相关损失，用于对齐每个子空间中的整体信息，而不是消除敏感属性的信息。[Agarwal et al. (2022)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0020) 研究了上下文信息，并选择与其他属性共发生的 CelebA 数据集中性别数据的公平子集。通过选择更公平的训练数据子集，他们实现了性别分类的更公平表现。同样，为了实现更准确和公平的年龄预测，作者提出了基于分布的数据清洗方法，利用外出分布技术进行数据选择 ([Cao et al. 2022](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0125))。最近，[Park et al. (2021)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0615) 分析了受监督对比学习引起的偏见，并提出了公平对比学习损失（FSCL），用于在对比设置中实现更公平的学习。

The majority of the algorithms are focused on alleviating the influence of demographics on model predictions for enhancing fairness. However, [Terhorst et al. (2020c)](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#bb0810) demonstrated that face templates store non-demographic characteristics as well. The biased prediction of models could be due to the non-demographic characteristics stored in the face images. Therefore, considering the non-demographic attributes during bias mitigation can prove to be important for designing robust systems.

##  7: Meta-analysis of algorithms

The trend in deep learning models has been toward improving fairness for different demographic subgroups. As illustrated in [Fig. 13](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#f0070), newer algorithms have shown improved fairness across different racial subgroups. In [Fig. 13](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#f0070), the verification accuracy across different subgroups of the RFW dataset is depicted for several research works. Over time, the gap between the performance across subgroups has reduced substantially. [Table 7](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#t0040) further demonstrates the performance of recent face recognition algorithms on the RFW dataset. The last column depicts the *bias* in the system and is calculated as the standard deviation of accuracy reported across the four ethnicity subsets. For the task of facial attribute prediction, a reduction in bias has also been observed over the years. Using the UTKFace, MORPH, and LFWA datasets, in [Table 8](#Ch007_169-221_S0169716123000020.xhtml#t0045), the performance of different techniques is compared in terms of error rate as well as Degree of Bias and Precise Subgroup Equivalence. The models are trained using one dataset for gender prediction, and bias estimation is performed for the other datasets. Overall, the bias in the performance of face recognition and attribute prediction has reduced substantially across different ethnic and gender subgroups.
