learning models since the end-to-end learnable DNNs architectures make the attack easy and still some properties of DNNs have not yet been fully investigated. Overcoming the vulnerabilities of DNNs to the malicious manipulation of images and videos, either directly on the digital domain or in the physical world, is key for their adoption in real-world applications, such as commerce, security, and authentication, and safety critical autonomous systems, such as self-driving vehicles (Modas et al., 2020).
