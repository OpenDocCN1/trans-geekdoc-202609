# Biographies

Hassan Ugail is the director of the Centre for Visual Computing in the Faculty of Engineering and Informatics at the University of Bradford, UK. He has a first class BSc Honors degree in Mathematics from King’s College London and a PhD in the field of geometric design from the School of Mathematics at the University of Leeds. Professor Ugail’s research interests include computer based geometric and functional design, imaging and machine learning.
