A large literature also exists in topics not covered in this review, such as instance segmentation, domain adaptation, or low complexity deep learning architectures, among several others. Finally, object detection is frequently used as a preliminary stage of many other vision tasks, including pose estimation, image and video captioning, or visual question answering, among others. In summary, while current object detectors are orders of magnitude more effective than those of just a decade ago, much research remains to be done in this problem of fundamental importance for computer vision.
