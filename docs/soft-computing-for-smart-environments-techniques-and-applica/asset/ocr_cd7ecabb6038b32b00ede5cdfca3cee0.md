# 6.2.2 TOPSIS Method

In 1981, Yoon and Hwang created what is now known as the TOPSIS approach (Yoon and Hwang 1985). Alternatives are evaluated with the use of parameters by the procedure. The fundamental reasoning behind this approach is to find the positive and negative solutions that are most effective overall. The alternative that is the farthest away from the optimum positive solution is the one that is considered to be the best alternative. An ideal alternative is the closest to the optimal negative solution. TOPSIS assigns weights to choose options according to how close they are to both a positive ideal solution (PIS) and a negative ideal solution (NIS) simultaneously.
