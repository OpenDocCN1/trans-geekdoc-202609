where $(x_{ij})$, $i, j = 1, 2, ..., n$, and $x_{ij}$ is the valuation of the barriers to the $i^{th}$ element $B_1, B_2, ..., B_i$ regarding the $j^{th}$ element $B_1, B_2, ..., B_j$. Also, the key diagonal of the matrix equals 1 such that $x_{ij}$ equal 1.

Let $f_{kij} = (f_{kij}^l, f_{kij}^m, f_{kij}^u)$ is the initial direct-effect information that $B_i$ has on $B_j$ by $K^{th}$ expert by applying linguistic variables, where $f_{kij}^l, f_{kij}^m, f_{kij}^u$ refer to the lower limit, middle limit, and upper limit of the initial direct-effect information, respectively.

# Step 5: Collect all decision matrices created by all experts into a single matrix according to Eq. (4.3).

$\tilde{U}_{ij} = \tilde{F}_{ij} / r = (f_{ij}^l / r, f_{ij}^m / r, f_{ij}^u / r) = (U_{ij}^l, U_{ij}^m, U_{ij}^u)$ \tag{4.4}

where $\tilde{U}_{ij}$ is the standardized direct-effect information that $B_i$ has on $B_j$, where $U_{ij}^l, U_{ij}^m, U_{ij}^u$ refer to the lower limit, median limit, and upper limit of the normalized direct-effect matrix, correspondingly. $r$ is $Max_{1 \le i \le n} \sum_{j=1}^n U_{ji}^u$, and $Max_i$ indicates the maximum of $i^{th}$ IoT barrier.
