Step 15: Determine the positive and negative ideal solution A_j^* and A_j^-, respectively. If A_j are advantageous criteria, then A_j^* = max(A_ij) and A_j^- = min(A_ij). Then, if A_j are non-advantageous criteria, then

A_j^* = min(A_ij) and A_j^- = max(A_ij)  (2.11)

Step 16: Compute the group utility S_i by applying Eq. (2.12) and individual regret values R_i by applying Eq. (2.13) as follows:

S_i = sum_{j=1}^{n} w_j * (A_j^* - A_ij) / (A_j^* - A_j^-)  (2.12)

R_i = max [ w_j * (A_j^* - A_ij) / (a_j^* - A_j^-) ]  (2.13)

w_j is the weight of all sub-criteria.

Step 17: Compute the concordance index Q_j among the selected criteria and alternatives by applying Eq. (2.14).

Q_j = v [ (S_i - S^*) / (S^- - S^*) ] + (1 - v) [ (R_i - R^*) / (R^- - R^*) ]  (2.14)

where S^- = max_j S_j, S^* = min_j S_j, R^- = max_j R_j, R^* = min_j R_j, and v ∈ [0, 1] is generally 0.5.

Step 18: Rank the smart health technologies in descending order according to the value Q_j computed by applying Eq. (2.14).

Step 19: Conduct the two final necessities in the evaluation process of the smart health technologies by the VIKOR technique. Situation 1: (adequate advantage) by using Eq. (2.15):

Q(A^2) - Q(A^1) ≥ 1 / (m - 1)  (2.15)

where A^1 is rated as the top alternative rendering to Q and A^2 is rated second, and m is the count of substitutes. Situation 2 (appropriate stability): as the classification of Q, A^1 must be the maximum in the ranking of S and R.
