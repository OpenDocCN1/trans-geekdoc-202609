Step 7: Aggregate the valuations on criterion weights. The distinct expert valuations were combined by applying q-ROFWG presented in Eq. (6.1). At this point, $(w_j)_{1\times n}$ introduces q-ROF weight of jth criterion. It can refer to the connotation of Eq. (6.1) in the previous chapter (Chapter 5).

$$\text{q-ROFWG}(\tilde{Q}_1,\tilde{Q}_2,\dots,\tilde{Q}_n)=\left(\prod_{i=1}^{n}u_{\tilde{Q}_i}^{w_i},\left(1-\prod_{i=1}^{n}(1-\vartheta_{\tilde{Q}_i}^{q})^{w_i}\right)^{\frac{1}{q}}\right)\tag{6.1}$$

Step 8: Compute the entropy values of all q-ROFN of the collected experts’ assessments by applying Eqs. (6.2) and (6.3).

$$\mathbb{K}E_{q,i,j}(x)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left(\left((\mathcal{U}(x))^{q}\right)^{2}+\left((\vartheta(x))^{q}\right)^{2}+\left((\mathcal{U}(x))^{q}+(\vartheta(x))^{q}\right)^{2}\right)^{0.5}\tag{6.2}$$ 

$$\mathbb{E}N_{q,i,j}(x)=-\mathbb{K}E_{q,i,j}(x)=1-\left(\left((\mathcal{U}(x))^{q}\right)^{2}+\left((\vartheta(x))^{q}\right)^{2}+\left((\mathcal{U}(x))^{q}+(\vartheta(x))^{q}\right)^{2}\right)^{0.5}\tag{6.3}$$

Step 9: Compute the main criterion weights based on the entropy values according to Eq. (6.4).

$$w_j=\frac{1-\xi_j}{\sum_{j=1}^{n}(1-\xi_j)};\;j=1,2...n.\tag{6.4}$$ 

where $\xi_j=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{m}\mathbb{E}N_{q,i,j}}{\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{n}\mathbb{E}N_{q,i,j}}$ refers to the q-ROF entropy value.
