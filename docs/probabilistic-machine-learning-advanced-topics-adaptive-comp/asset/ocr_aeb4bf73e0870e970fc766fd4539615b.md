Figure 29.18: An HHMM represented as a DPGM. \(Z_t^\ell\) is the state at time \(t\), level \(\ell\); \(F_t^\ell = 1\) if the HMM at level \(\ell\) has finished (entered its exit state), otherwise \(F_t^\ell = 0\).
