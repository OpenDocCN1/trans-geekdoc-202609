

[Soh16]:

\[
\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} = -\mathbb{E}_{x,x^+,x_{1:M}^-} \left[ \log \frac{\exp f_\theta(x)^T g_\phi(x^+)}{\exp f_\theta(x)^T g_\phi(x^+) + \sum_{i=1}^M \exp f_\theta(x)^T g_\phi(x_i^-)} \right], \tag{32.16}
\]

where \(M\) 是负样本的数量。通常，嵌入 \(f(x)\) 和 \(g(x')\) 是 \(\ell_2\)-归一化的，可以引入额外的超参数 \(\tau\) 来重新缩放内积 [Che+20c]。与使用硬阈值 \(\epsilon\) 的三元损失不同，\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}}\) 可以通过将负样本进一步推远来始终得到改进。直观地说，InfoNCE 损失确保正对比小批量中的任何 \(M\) 个负对都更近。InfoNCE 损失可以与输入 \(x\) 和学习表示 \(z\) 之间的互信息的下界相关联 [OLV18b; Poo+19a]:

\(\tag{32.17}\)
\[I(X; Z) \ge \log M - \mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}},\]

and has also been motivated as a way of learning representations through the InfoMax principle [OLV18b; Hje+18; BHB19]。When applying the InfoNCE loss to parallel views that are the same modality and dimension, the encoder \(f_\theta\) for the anchor \(x\) and the positive and negative examples \(g_\phi\) can be shared.

## 32.3.4.3 无负样本损失

**无负样本表示学习**（有时也称为**非对比表示学习**）仅使用正对来学习表示，而不显式构建负对。与对比方法通过强制正对比负对更近来防止坍塌不同，无负样本方法利用其他机制。一类无负样本目标既包含吸引项，也包含防止坍塌的项。另一类方法使用仅包含吸引项的目标，而是依赖学习动态来防止坍塌。

The **Barlow Twins** loss [Zbo+21] is

\[
\mathcal{L}_{\text{BT}} = \sum_{i=1}^p (1 - \mathbf{C}_{ii})^2 + \lambda \sum_{i=1}^p \sum_{j \neq i} \mathbf{C}_{ij}^2 \tag{32.18}
\]

其中 \(\mathbf{C}\) 是来自两个视图的两批特征之间的互相关矩阵。第一项是吸引项，鼓励两个视图的表示之间具有高相似性，而第二项防止坍塌到低秩表示。The loss is minimized when \(\mathbf{C}\) is the identity matrix。Similar losses based on ensuring the variance of features being non-zero have also been useful for preventing collapse [BPL21b]。The Barlow Twins loss can be related to kernel-based independence criterion such as HSIC which have also been useful as losses for representation learning [Li+21; Tsa+21]。

**BYOL** (bootstrap your own latents) [Gri+20] 和 SimSiam [Che+20c] 只是最小化两个表示之间的均方误差：

\(\tag{32.19}\)
\[
\mathcal{L}_{\text{BYOL}} = \mathbb{E}_{\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{x}^+} [\| g_\phi(f_\theta(\mathbf{x})) - f_{\theta'}(\mathbf{x}^+) \|^2] .
\]

根据 Grill 等人 [Gri+20]，\( g_\phi \) 被称为*预测器*，\( f_\theta \) 是*在线网络*，\( f_{\theta'} \) 是*目标网络*。在优化此损失函数时，权重会反向传播以更新 \( \phi \) 和 \( \theta \)，但直接优化 \( \theta' \) 会导致表示坍塌 [Che+20c]。相反，BYOL 将 \( \theta' \) 设置为 \( \theta \) 的指数移动平均，而 SimSiam 在每次训练迭代中将 \( \theta' \leftarrow \theta \)。The reasons why BYOL and SimSiam avoid collapse are not entirely clear，但 Tian, Chen, 和 Ganguli [TCG21] 分析了简化线性 BYOL 模型的梯度流动态，并表明只要超参数设置得当，确实可以避免坍塌。

**DINO** (self-distillation with no labels) [Car+21] 是另一种非对比损失，它依赖学习动态来避免坍塌。与 BYOL 类似，DINO 使用仅由在线网络和由在线网络权重的指数移动平均形成的目标网络之间的吸引项组成的损失。与 BYOL 不同，DINO 使用交叉熵损失，其中目标网络为在线网络生成目标，并避免了对预测器网络的需求。The DINO loss is:

\(\mathcal{L}_{\text{DINO}} = \mathbb{E}_{\boldsymbol{x}, \boldsymbol{x}^+} \left[ H(f_{\theta^\prime}(\boldsymbol{x})/\tau, \text{center}(f_\theta(\boldsymbol{x}^+))/\tau') \right].\) (32.20)

其中，略有滥用符号，center 是应用于包含 \( x^+ \) 的小批量上的均值居中操作。居中目标网络的输出是防止坍塌到单个"类"所必需的，而对目标网络使用较低温度 \( \tau' < \tau \) 是防止坍塌到均匀分布所必需的。The DINO loss provides marginal gains over the BYOL loss when performing self-supervised representation learning with vision transformers on ImageNet [Car+21]。

## 32.3.4.4 技巧

除了视图选择和损失之外，还有一些有用的架构和修改，可实现更有效的多视图表示学习。

Normalizing the output of the encoders and computing cosine similarity instead of predicting unconstrained representations has shown to improve performance [Che+20c]。这种归一化将点之间的相似性限制在 \(-1\) 和 \(1\) 之间，因此通常会引入额外的温度参数 \(\tau\)，并在学习过程中固定或退火。

While the learned representation with multiview learning are often useful for downstream tasks，the losses when combined with data augmentation typically lead to too much invariance for some tasks。相反，可以提取编码器中较早的一层作为表示，或者在计算损失之前向编码器添加一个称为投影头的额外层 [Che+20c]。When training we compute the loss on the output of the projection head，但评估表示质量时，我们会丢弃这个额外层。

Given the summation over negative examples in the denominator of the InfoNCE loss，它通常对训练所用的批量大小敏感。In practice，large batch sizes of 4096 or more are needed to achieve good performance with this loss，which can be computationally burdensome。**MoCo** (momentum contrast) [He+20] 引入了一个内存队列，用于存储来自之前小批量的负样本，以扩大每次迭代中的负样本大小。Additionally，they use a momentum encoder，where the encoder for the positive and negative examples uses an exponential moving average of the anchor encoder parameters。This momentum encoder approach was also found useful in BYOL to prevent collapse。As in BYOL，adding an extra predictor network that maps from the online network to the target network has shown to improve the performance of MoCo，and removes the requirement of a memory queue [CXH21]。

The backbone architectures of the encoder networks play a large role in the quality of representations。对于视觉表示学习，最近的工作已从基于 ConvNet 的主干转向视觉变换器，从而产生更大规模的模型，在多个下游任务上表现更好 [CXH21]。

## 32.4 表示学习理论

尽管深度表示学习已为大多数应用取代了手工设计的特征，但关于学习哪些特征以及这些方法提供哪些保证的理论是有限的。Here we review several theoretical directions in understanding representation learning：identifiability，information maximization，and transfer bounds。

### 32.4.1 可识别性

在本节中，我们假设生成数据的潜变量生成模型，其中 z ~ p(z) 是潜变量，x = g(z) 是将潜变量映射到观测的确定性生成器。Our goal is to learn a representation h = f_theta(x) that inverts the generative model and recovers h = z。If we can do this，we say the model is identifiable。Oftentimes we are not able to recover the true latent variables exactly，for example the dimensions of the latent variables may be permuted，or individual dimensions may be transformed version of an underlying latent variable：h_i = f_i(z_i)。因此，关于可识别性的大多数理论工作都集中在学习可以置换和逐元素变换以匹配真实潜变量的表示的情况。Such representations are referred to as disentangled as the dimensions of the learned representation do not mix together multiple dimensions of the true latent variables。

Methods for recovering are typically based around latent-variable models such as VAEs combined with various regularizers (see Section 21.3.1.1)。While several publications showed promising empirical progress，a large-scale study by Locatello et al. [Loc+20a] on disentangled representation learning methods showed that several existing approaches cannot work without additional assumptions on the data or model。Their argument relies on the observation that we can form a bijection f that takes samples from a factorial prior p(z) = prod_i p_i(z_i) and maps to z' = f(z) that (1) preserves the marginal distribution，and (2) has entirely entangled latents (each dimension of z influences every dimension of z')。Transforming the marginal in this way changes the representation，but preserves the marginal likelihood of the data，and thus one cannot use marginal likelihood alone to identify or distinguish between the entangled and disentangled model。Empirically，they show that past methods largely succeeded due to careful hyperparameter selection on the target disentanglement metrics that require supervised labels。While further work has developed unsupervised methods for hyperparameter that address several of these issues [Dua+20]，at this point there are no known robust methods for learning disentangled representations without further assumptions。

To address the empirical and theoretical gap in learning disentangled representations，several papers have proposed using additional sources of information in the form of weakly-labeled data to provide guarantees。In theoretical work on nonlinear ICA [RMK21; Khe+20; Häl+21]，this information comes in the form of additional observations for each datapoint that are related to the underlying latent variable through an exponential family。Work on causal representation learning has expanded the applicability of these methods and highlighted the settings where such strong assumptions on weakly-labeled data may be attainable [Sch+21c; WJ21; Rei+22]
