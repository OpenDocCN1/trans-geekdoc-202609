| \( h(\tilde{x}) \) | \( C(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}') \) |
|---|---|
| \( \text{erf}(\tilde{x}^\text{T}\tilde{u}) \) | \( \frac{2}{\pi} \arcsin(f_1(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}')) \) |
| \( \mathbb{I}(\tilde{x}^\text{T}\tilde{u} \ge 0) \) | \( \pi - \theta(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}') \) |
| \( \text{ReLU}(\tilde{x}^\text{T}\tilde{u}) \) | \( \frac{f_2(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}')}{\pi} \sin(\theta(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}')) + \frac{\pi-\theta(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}')}{\pi} \tilde{x}^\text{T}\tilde{\Sigma}\tilde{x}' \) |

Table 18.4: Some neural net GP kernels. Here we define \( f_1(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}') = \frac{2\tilde{x}^\text{T}\tilde{\Sigma}\tilde{x}'}{\sqrt{(1+2\tilde{x}^\text{T}\tilde{\Sigma}\tilde{x})(1+2(\tilde{x}')^\text{T}\tilde{\Sigma}\tilde{x}')}} \), \( f_2(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}') = ||\tilde{\Sigma}^{\frac{1}{2}}\tilde{x}|| \cdot ||\tilde{\Sigma}^{\frac{1}{2}}\tilde{x}'|| \), \( f_3(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}') = \sqrt{(\tilde{x}^\text{T}\tilde{\Sigma}\tilde{x})((\tilde{x}')^\text{T}\tilde{\Sigma}\tilde{x}')} \), and \( \theta(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}') = \arccos(f_3(\tilde{x}, \tilde{x}')) \). Results are derived in [Wil98; CS09].

Thus the MLP converges to a GP. To compute the kernel function, we need to evaluate

```
\( C(x, x') = \mathbb{E}_u [h(u_0 + u^T x) h(u_0 + u^T x')] = \mathbb{E}_u [h(\tilde{u}^T \tilde{x}) h(\tilde{u}^T \tilde{x}')] \quad (18.181) \)
```

where we have defined \( \tilde{x} = (1, x) \) and \( \tilde{u} = (u_0, u) \). Let us define

```
\( \tilde{\Sigma} = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma_0^2 & 0 \\ 0 & \Sigma \end{pmatrix} \quad (18.182) \)
```

Then we have

```
\( C(x, x') = \int h(\tilde{u}^T \tilde{x}) h(\tilde{u}^T \tilde{x}') \mathcal{N}(\tilde{u} | \mathbf{0}, \tilde{\Sigma}) d\tilde{u} \quad (18.183) \)
```

This can be computed in closed form for certain activation functions, as shown in Table 18.4.

This is sometimes called the neural net kernel. Note that this is a non-stationary kernel, and sample paths from it are nearly discontinuous and tend to constant values for large positive or negative inputs, as illustrated in Figure 18.28.
