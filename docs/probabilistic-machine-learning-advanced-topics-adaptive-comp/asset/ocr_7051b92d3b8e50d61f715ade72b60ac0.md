This is called the **probit approximation** [SL90]. In [Gib97], a generalization to the multiclass case was provided. This is known as the **generalized probit approximation**, and has the form

```
p(y_*|x_*) \approx \int \text{softmax}(f_*) \mathcal{N}(f_* | \mu_*, \Sigma_*) df_* = \text{softmax} \left( \{ \frac{\mu_{*,c}}{\sqrt{1 + \frac{\pi}{8} \Sigma_{*,cc}}} \} \right)
```

This ignores the correlations between the logits, because it only depends on the diagonal elements of \(\Sigma_*\). Nevertheless it can work well, even in the case of neural net classifiers [LIS20]. Another deterministic approximation, known as the **Laplace bridge**, is discussed in Section 17.3.10.2.
