```
Algorithm 13.1: Sequential importance sampling (SIS)
1 Initialization: \( z_1^i \sim q_1(z_1), \tilde{w}_1^i = \frac{\tilde{\gamma}_1(z_1^i)}{q_1(z_1^i)}, W_1^i = \frac{\tilde{w}_1^i}{\sum_j \tilde{w}_1^i}, \hat{\pi}_1(z_1) = \sum_{i=1}^{N_s} W_1^i \delta(z_1 - z_1^i) \)
2 for \( t=2:T \) do
3   for \( i=1:N_s \) do
4     Sample \( z_t^i \sim q_t(z_t | z_{1:t-1}^i) \)
5     Compute incremental weight \( \alpha_t^i = \frac{\gamma_t(z_{1:t}^i)}{\tilde{\gamma}_{t-1}(z_{1:t-1}^i) q_t(z_t^i | z_{1:t-1}^i)} \)
6     Compute unnormalized weight \( \tilde{w}_t^i = \tilde{w}_{t-1}^i \alpha_t^i \)
7   Compute normalized weights \( W_t^i = \frac{\tilde{w}_t^i}{\sum_j \tilde{w}_t^i} \) for \( i=1:N_s \)
8   Compute MC posterior \( \hat{\pi}_t(z_{1:t}) = \sum_{i=1}^{N_s} W_t^i \delta(z_{1:t} - z_{1:t}^i) \)
```

# 13.2.3 Sequential importance sampling with resampling

In this section, we describe sequential importance sampling with resampling (SISR). The basic idea is this: instead of “growing” all of the old particle sequences by one step, we first select the \(N_s\) “fittest” particles, by sampling from the old posterior, and then we let these survivors grow by one step. In more detail, at step \(t\), we sample from

```
\( q_t^{\text{SISR}}(\mathbf{z}_{1:t}) = \hat{\pi}_{t-1}(\mathbf{z}_{1:t-1}) q_t(\mathbf{z}_t | \mathbf{z}_{1:t-1}) \) (13.22)
```

where \(\hat{\pi}_{t-1}(\mathbf{z}_{1:t-1})\) is the previous weighted posterior approximation. By contrast, in SIS, we sample from

```
\( q_t^{\text{SIS}}(\mathbf{z}_{1:t}) = q_{t-1}^{\text{SIS}}(\mathbf{z}_{1:t-1}) q_t(\mathbf{z}_t | \mathbf{z}_{1:t-1}) \) (13.23)
```
