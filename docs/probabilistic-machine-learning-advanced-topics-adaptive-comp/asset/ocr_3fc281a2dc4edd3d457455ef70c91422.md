problem:

$$
\psi^* = \underset{\psi}{\text{argmin}} D_{\text{KL}} (q(\theta|\psi) \parallel p(\theta|\mathcal{D}))
$$

$$
= \underset{\psi}{\text{argmin}} \mathbb{E}_{q(\theta|\psi)} \left[ \log q(\theta|\psi) - \log \left( \frac{p(\mathcal{D}|\theta)p(\theta)}{p(\mathcal{D})} \right) \right]
$$

$$
= \underset{\psi}{\text{argmin}} \underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{q(\theta|\psi)} [-\log p(\mathcal{D}|\theta) - \log p(\theta) + \log q(\theta|\psi)]}_{-\text{L}(\psi)} + \log p(\mathcal{D})
$$

Note that $\log p(\mathcal{D})$ is independent of $\psi$, so we can ignore it when fitting the approximate posterior, and just focus on maximizing the term

$$
\text{L}(\psi) \triangleq \mathbb{E}_{q(\theta|\psi)} [\log p(\mathcal{D}|\theta) + \log p(\theta) - \log q(\theta|\psi)]
$$

Since we have $D_{\text{KL}} (q \parallel p) \ge 0$, we have $\text{L}(\psi) \le \log p(\mathcal{D})$. The quantity $\log p(\mathcal{D})$, which is the log marginal likelihood, is also called the **evidence**. Hence $\text{L}(\psi)$ is known as the **evidence lower bound** or **ELBO**. By maximizing this bound, we are making the variational posterior closer to the true posterior. (See Section 10.1 for details.)
