Now, we want to find the probability of a message being spam, given that a particular word appears in it. We want to find the probability of a message being Spam, that the word “please” appears in it. So, we want to find $P(Spam|please)$.

Now, by Bayes Theorem, we know that

> $P(Spam|please) = \frac{P(please|Spam) \cdot P(Spam)}{P(please)}$

We have the values of $P(please|Spam)$, $P(Spam)$, and $P(please)$. So, we get:

> $P(Spam|please) = \frac{0+(2/3)}{12} = 0$.

Similarly, we can find the probability of a message being spam for another word like “asap”. We get:

> $P(Spam|asap) = \frac{P(asap|Spam) \cdot P(Spam)}{P(asap)} = \frac{(2/3) \cdot (2/3)}{2/12} = 2/300 = 1/150$.

Let us try one example. Let us determine the probability of a message being Benign given that the word “please” appears in the message:

> $P(Benign|please) = \frac{P(please|Benign) \cdot P(Benign)}{P(please)} = \frac{(2/3) \cdot (3/4)}{2/12} = 3/420 = 1/140$.
