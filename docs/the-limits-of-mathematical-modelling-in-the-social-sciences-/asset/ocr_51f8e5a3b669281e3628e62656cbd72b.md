# 2. A new logic to form a formal ethics¹⁹

Menger’s book Moral, Wille und Weltgestaltung, Grundlegung zur Logic der Sitten (Menger, 1974)²⁰ represented a formal approach to moral problems, based on an unambiguous definition of ethical language; it is a tentative to present a positive ethics.

When Menger’s book appeared in Vienna, in 1934, it was welcomed by Oskar Morgenstern as “the only examination of a strictly formal nature about social groups” (Leonard, 1995, p. 746) and Karl Popper as “one of a few books in the author attempts to depart from the stupid talk in ethics” (Popper, 1944-1945).²¹ Hayek also paid attention to Menger’s work on ethics.¹²
