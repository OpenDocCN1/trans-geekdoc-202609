# Economic constraints

There are two conditions that typically constrain the behavior of rational agents in economic models: (1) transversality condition (TVC), together with its mirror condition: no Ponzi game (NPG), and (2) Walras’s law.

In simple terms, the TVC prevents overaccumulation of wealth or savings (Kamihigashi, 2008). TVC requires the present discounted value of the agent’s consumption matches the present discounted value of his savings. In other words, asymptotically, the agent shall consume all his savings in the course of his (infinite) lifetime.
