## Chapter 6

### Is Classical Logic Enough?

#### 1. Introduction

The social sciences, and more generally the human sciences, have been profoundly shaped by the assumptions of standard, or classical logic. Most of us have spent our lives working in an environment where classical logic are taken as given. We do not suggest in this chapter that classical logic needs to be abandoned. We do contend, however, that many of the phenomena we encounter, not just in the physical world, do not always lend themselves to analysis via classical logic.
