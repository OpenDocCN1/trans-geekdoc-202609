2.  [2](#07_Chapter02.xhtml#ch2fn2a)如果你有较长的问卷并决定为变量 q1、q2、q3 等进行标签，可使用 **Label** 设施。在 **Label** 列中提供每个问题的描述。例如，若我们在咨询问卷中采用此方法，q4 的标签为‘咨询会话次数’，这样输出时会显示‘咨询会话次数’而非‘q4’，便于我们识别具体问题。此方法在较早的 SPSS 版本中更为常见，当时变量列受限于八个字符，常需使用缩写或难以辨认的名称。

3.  [3](#07_Chapter02.xhtml#ch2fn3a)举例来说，我们可能想区分因患者拒绝回答敏感问题而导致的缺失值，以及因问题不适用于患者而导致的缺失值。此时需要在 **Missing** 单元格中点击，右侧会出现三个点的按钮，点击后弹出对话框，你可以输入缺失值。该值应位于变量可能出现的取值范围之外：99 是常用的缺失值表示，但如果变量本身可能包含该值（例如年龄），则应选择更远的数值（999）。

# Chapter3 Descriptive statistics: frequencies, measures of central tendency and illustrating the data using graphs

In this chapter you will use SPSS to produce some basic descriptive statistics from the data: frequencies for categorical data and measures of central tendency for interval level data. You will also learn how to produce and edit charts to illustrate the data analysis, and how to copy your work into a Microsoft Word file.

## 3.1Frequencies

We noted in [Chapter 1](#06_Chapter01.xhtml) that the first thing a researcher would do with this data is to ‘run some frequencies’. This simply means that we want to look at the frequencies of our *categorical* data:

*   How many male/female patients are there?

*   How many patients were treated by each of the counsellors John and Jane?

This will provide us with an initial overview of our sample, or ‘population’. So let us start with our first categorical variable – sex.
