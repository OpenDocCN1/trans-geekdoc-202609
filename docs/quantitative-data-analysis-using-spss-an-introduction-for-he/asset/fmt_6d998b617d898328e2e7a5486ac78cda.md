Recode the data for number of counselling sessions into three equal groups representing ‘low’, ‘medium’ and ‘high’ attendance, and first cross-tabulate with ‘counsellor’ then with ‘sex’. What do these cross-tabulations show?

#### Answers

The first thing we need to know is the range of counselling sessions. From section 2 in [Chapter 3](#08_Chapter03.xhtml) we know that the number of counselling sessions ranged from 3 to 12 (running a simple frequencies analysis will confirm this). So we should divide the sessions into the following three categories:

1. 1Low: 1–4 sessions.

2. 2Medium: 5–8 sessions.

3. 3High: 9–12 sessions.

Now that we know our categories we can re-code counselling sessions in SPSS using **Transform / Recode into Different Variables** and then cross-tabulate counsellor with our new variable.

From this cross-tabulation we can see that John tended to hold a larger number of sessions: 50 per cent of his patients attended for between 9–12 sessions compared to only 12 per cent of Jane’s patients, the majority of whom (75 per cent) attended for between 5–8 sessions.
