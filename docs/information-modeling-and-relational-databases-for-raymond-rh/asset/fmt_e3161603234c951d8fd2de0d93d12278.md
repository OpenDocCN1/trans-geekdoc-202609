
Other constraint implication theorems are mentioned later in the book. One important result is the case where the target of a pair-subset constraint includes a functional role. For example, the left-hand pattern in [Fig. 6.29](#Ch006_211-271_B9780443237904000025.xhtml#f0150)(a) has a pair-subset constraint from the lower role pair to the top role pair. If we add a simple uniqueness constraint over the left-hand role of the top role pair, what does this imply about the left-hand role of the lower role pair? If the lower predicate is a binary, this role must also be unique! As an exercise, prove this. An important corollary of this result is that if the lower predicate is longer than a binary, then it must split in this case because there will be an implied functional dependency (FD) from the first role to the second role. This result is illustrated in [Section 14.6](#Ch014_681-771_B9780443237904000189.xhtml#s0035) when we discuss normalization.
