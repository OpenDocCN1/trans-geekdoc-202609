rest of the page is processed before the recordset is destroyed. It wouldn't do your form any good if you destroyed the recordset before you had a chance to populate the menu with its data!

ColdFusion users don't have to worry about this step, because it happens automatically behind the scenes any time you deploy <cfquery>.
