What's happening is that ASP, ColdFusion, and PHP are using a loop structure. In programming, loops are structures that execute over and over again until something breaks them out of the loop. Loops are flow control structures, just like if...else structures. The difference is that if...else structures exxecute once based on one or more conditions, while loops execute repeatedly until a given condition is met.
