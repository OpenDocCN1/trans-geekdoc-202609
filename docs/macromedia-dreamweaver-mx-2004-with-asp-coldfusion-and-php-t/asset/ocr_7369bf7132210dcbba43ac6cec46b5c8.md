As these problems are identified, solutions—the specific changes and enhancements that you need to make to the site—begin to materialize. This way, the primary force driving the site upgrade process is the needs of the client, and not something else, such as your own opinion about what the site could be or, as is too often the case, the hottest technology on the market.
