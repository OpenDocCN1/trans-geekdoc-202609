# Different Investigative Paths

There are many different career paths you can choose that involve cyber investigations. In an incident response role, you will more likely focus on investigating the crime than the perpetrators. If you work in a security operations center (SOC), you may be looking to validate and understand different types of potential threats coming at you from many different forums of data feeds.
Even then, your industry may dictate the type of investigative work you do, or your role could both proactive and reactive investigative work.
