# Wayback Machine and Search Engine Archives

Wayback, or the Wayback Machine, is a digital archive of the web. Wayback (Archive.org) will allow you to see the state of a website, as it was, on a historical date.

This book has already covered reasons why looking at a website's historical state is an important way to search for clues. This section will mostly focus on ways to automate your searches, but first let's look at some basic examples.
