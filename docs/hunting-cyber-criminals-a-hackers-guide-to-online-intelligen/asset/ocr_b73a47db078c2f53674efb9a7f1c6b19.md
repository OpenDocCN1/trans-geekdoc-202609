# Forums

Forums are, in my opinion, a gateway to the underground crime market. What I mean by this is that being on many of these hacker forums is like a rite of passage for more advanced threat actors. There is a point in every hacker's life when they were inevitably considered a skid (script-kidd). Like most children, without thinking and in doing so will leave a trail that sometimes leads back to their real identity.

That is why, in my humble opinion, the true art of threat actor identification will come down to a researcher's ability to gather and search historical information. Searching forums should not be limited to hacker and dark web (TOR) forums. Even threat actors hang on nonhacker forums, which can come in all shapes and sizes. Researching different forums or linking aliases to new email addresses provide pivotal clues.

One such forum that helped me in this regard has the BitcoinTalk forum. I can't explain why, but I have found that a lot of threat actors in the past seem to hang out there. My point here is that you should not limit your search category to one particular type of forum or site. Always expand your searches as wide as possible.
