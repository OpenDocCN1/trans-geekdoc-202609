# The Power of Screenshots

As a hypothetical, let's say we were only interested in the domain activities of WhitePacket.com around the year 2005. In this particular case, we can see the registration data from that year, let us pretend that it is not the case and the registration is private. We would be up Schitt's historical data. You never know what clues you will find in the archives. The case of DomainTools, it provides screenshots for you, so you don't have to do any additional digging.

They say a picture is worth a thousand. In this case, it's at least two: the first and last name of the website's owner in 2005, written right on the page (9.22).
