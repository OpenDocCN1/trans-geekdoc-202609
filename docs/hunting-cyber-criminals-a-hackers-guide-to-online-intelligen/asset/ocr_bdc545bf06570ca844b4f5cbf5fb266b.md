# ICQ

Last but least is ICQ, an older chat service from the late 1990s that seems to have survived and is still used heavily by international threat actors. ICQ usernames consist of random 8-to-10-digit numbers, so they are impossible to guess. But sometimes the less intelligent threat actors will do the work for us. In Figure 17.32, threat actor cr00k posted a message advertising the sale of Canadian and U.S. credit cards. In the thread, he posts his ICQ number as a way to contact him.
