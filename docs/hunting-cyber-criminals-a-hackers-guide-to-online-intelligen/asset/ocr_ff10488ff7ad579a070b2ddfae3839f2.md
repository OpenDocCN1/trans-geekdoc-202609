As the title suggests, this part of the book discusses many of the intricacies of network investigation and exploration. Investigators and black box penetration testers will often have nothing more to go on than a single IP address, so’s where our investigative journey begins. These next three chapters will look at processes and tools designed to thoroughly explore the network space and help to gather as much information as possible.
