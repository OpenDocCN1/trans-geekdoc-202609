# Whisology

Whisology (www.whisology.com) is a domain ownership archive focused on cybercrime investigations, corporate intelligence, and legal research. In short, Whisology is a service designed to provide you with detailed information regarding a domain's registration history.

Figure 9.9 shows the initial Whisology search page.

Let's start our research by investigating the owner of TheDarkOverlord.com. The likelihood of this matching anything useful is slim to none, but we still have to try. Figure 9.10 shows the results of searching Whisology for TheDarkOverlord.com.

Current WHOIS data shows us private registration and (unfortunately) nothing useful. That is the beauty of sites that provide historical WHOIS information. You can go back several years to try to find attribution to the domain owner. Figure 9.11 shows the historical options available for TheDarkOverlord.com.
