# A NOTE ABOUT ONE OF THE TARGETS

For this chapter, we are going to look at a domain called DualCrypt.com (Figure 5.1). The site offers a ‘decentralized smartphone’ based on CopperheadOS. The domain was only online for a short time (you can still view it on archive.org). The interesting thing is that the people who designed the site advertised themselves as a group of three well-known hackers from the “KickAss Forum.” I wouldn’t have known about this site if our friend Cypher (of the KickAss forum) didn’t post specifically stating that they were not related to KickAss. Seems a pretty good reason to investigate.

# SpiderFoot

SpiderFoot is an OSINT platform that’s based on the concept of information-chaining, meaning that one piece of collected data is used to automatically obtain the next piece of data in a recursive process until all possible OSINT of the target and related entities has been collected.
