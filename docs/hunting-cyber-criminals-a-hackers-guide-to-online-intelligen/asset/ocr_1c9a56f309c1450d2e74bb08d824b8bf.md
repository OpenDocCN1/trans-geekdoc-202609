# Chapter 1 Getting Started 3

I thought it would be really interesting to you, the reader, if I also included the opinions and experiences of some of those people alongside my own. I am writing a book on a subject, why not include the opinions of people who are also really good at said subject?

So I asked a handful of people I consider experts in their field to contribute a story, an opinion, or even a technique on some part of the information-gathering or investigative process.

I found each of their stories to be unique and thought-provoking, and I know you will, too!
