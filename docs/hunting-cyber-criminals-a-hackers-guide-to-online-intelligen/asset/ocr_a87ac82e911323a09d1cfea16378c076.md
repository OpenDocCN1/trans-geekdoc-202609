# Stradinatras

What Chris didn't realize that NightCat was one of my aliases. In fact, I only used that alias time, on Exploit.in, to communicate with a single person: Stradinatras.

Stradinatras also went as “Obfuscation” on the KickAss forum where his logo was a picture of a white hat (I guess he was masquerading as a white hat security professional). Obfuscation was very vocal on KickAss about liking Arnie, the suspected head of TDO.

> NOTE The names Stradinatras and Obfuscation are interchangeable—he would openly announce both aliases, with the jabber address obby@jabber.de.

Interesting that he started to use the word multiple times: ObbyLord, Dark Over.

I reached out to Stradinatras on Exploit because I did not want him to know my alias on KickAss—so I made a new one: NightCat.

There was a specific post on KickAss where Obfuscation was openly bashing Arnie and NSA@rows.io (Figure 19.3), so I thought he might be a good person to speak for few clues.
