> NOTE The great thing about Gmail (and most account recovery sites) is that it provides an exact number of characters in the recovery clue. This will allow us to connect dots later in our investigation without having to worry about making unnecessary assumptions.

Most sites provide more than one method for account recovery. When working with Gmail, clicking “Try another way” will show additional account validation options.
In this case our test account had only one form of account verification available, but to show you an additional example, Figure 17.14 uses my personal Gmail account to show a second verification option.
We see from this screen that my phone number is 12 digits (important to note when dealing with international numbers), and ends in 68.
