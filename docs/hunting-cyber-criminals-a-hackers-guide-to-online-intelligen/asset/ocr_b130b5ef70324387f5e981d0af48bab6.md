Then you have the category of insiders, and those have been going on for a long time. They are typically disgruntled insider employees who have too much system access, or remain after they've terminated, and they decide to mess things up logic bomb type scenarios, or just using their knowledge of system weaknesses and vulnerabilities to tamper with afterwards. # Investigating Cyber Criminals

Depending on your role, a cyber investigation may include exploration into the technical aspects of a crime (such as understanding how a hack has occurred), or may include research and attribution of the criminals themselves.

A key piece of any cyber investigation will inevitably involve some level of understanding of the threat actors and how they operate. Especially during a breach, understanding the perpetrating group and their MO (modus operandi)
