# WHOIS History

Following our exploration of the SSL cert and related domains, a logical step is to look up the WHOIS history of all the new target domains. We already found a handful by looking up similar registrant information, and in the previous section we found a few by looking for shared SSL certificates. The WHOIS tab on Iris is the most complete record of domain registration period. I have yet to find a source for registration data that goes as far back as DomainTools (which is nearly two decades). Figure 1.14 shows the WHOIS history after running a search on my personal domain, vinnytria.com.
