Because our search results dropped from 37 to 2, we can conclude that our target uses different email addresses for domain registration. As part of a normal investigation, we would need to look through each of the other domains to find email address(es) we use. Once we have those, we document them, and perform more searches to find additional domains that can potentially be registered under those emails that we have missed. As you can tell, the entire process can quickly turn into one giant rabbit hole.

> **NOTE** This is when using a mind mapping tool will really come handy. If you don't have a method for noting and expanding each of these different trails, I highly suggest looking into a mind map. Plenty are available. Mindnode is a great tool for Mac users. If you prefer an app, Coggle.it or Lucidchart are both good. You have so many to choose from. Just pick something, because writing everything in OneNote is not going to cut.

I can guarantee you will end up losing tracking or overlooking important clues—because I have. Many times. Save yourself the pain of having to go back and research data points and get into the history of properly documenting things from the start. Take screenshots of everythingeverything (another lesson I had to learn the hard way).
