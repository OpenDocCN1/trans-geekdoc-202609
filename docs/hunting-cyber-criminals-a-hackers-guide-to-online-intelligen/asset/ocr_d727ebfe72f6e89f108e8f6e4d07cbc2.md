Passwords, Dumps, and Data
Viper

I find it to be so amazingly cool to be able to say the following sentence: This is the chapter where we get to use a hacker's own hacks against them. What I mean by that is we will be using the data within a “data dump” (i.e., a company’s hacked data) to fill in the missing pieces from the previous chapter.

During my quest to uncover the identities of The Dark Over, I could not find a single tool with all of the historical data needed to reach the necessary conclusions, so I built my own: Data Viper (www.dataviper.io).

Though this tool is not commercially available (yet), I will discuss how I built the tool, and of course, how it was used to reach conclusions during my investigation.
