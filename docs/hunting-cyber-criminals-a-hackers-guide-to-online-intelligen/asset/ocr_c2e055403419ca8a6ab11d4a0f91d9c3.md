# Looking for Threat Actors

Now let's try a different search geared toward threat actors. A threat actor's early days are probably filled with dreams of becoming the world's greatest and most widely known hacker. A theme we will cover in future chapters is a criminal's vanity (or underlying need for affirmation).

Keeping in mind, there is a chance that your target will have registered a domain name for their hacker aliases (e.g., zerocool.com). So it's probably not a bad idea to search for your target's aliases and if similar matching domains have already been registered.

Let this test this on our buddy Cyber, as shown in Figure 9.8.

There are a lot of potential domains worth exploring. Clicking the WHOIS button next each domain will take you toDaddy's site to perform a WHOIS lookup.

I don't personally find this to be useful, but I don't want to discount it as a first search option. Depending on your goal this might be enough. However, even more than not many will have privacy enabled and you will see something like this:

```
Admin Name: Registration Private
Admin Organization: DomainsByProxy, LLC
Admin Street: DomainsByProxy.com
```
