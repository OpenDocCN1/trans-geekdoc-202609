## Summary

This chapter focused on finding hidden gems within a website by looking for hidden folders within its structure. This was accomplished by leveraging tools that try thousands of name variations in order to find working directories (i.e., bruteforcing).

## CHAPTER 8 Search Engine Dorks

Dorks are specially crafted queries used to find specific search results.

- XSS, SQLi
- Confidential information from websites
- Online shopping
- Information on printers

The process of dorking refers to using common error phrases to find misconfigured data.
