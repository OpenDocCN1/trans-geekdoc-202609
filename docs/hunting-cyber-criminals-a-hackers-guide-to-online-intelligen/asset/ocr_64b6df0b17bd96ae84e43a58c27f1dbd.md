# Why This Book Is Different

I have read a number of digital investigation books, and they all seem to just
list every tool possible, provide a short summary of what that tool does, and
move to the next. Almost like herding cattle.

Many of the OSINT and investigative books I read or referenced before starting
this book made me overwhelmed with information, like trying to under-
stand technical encyclopedia without actually giving you any guidance or
useful advice tied to what you are reading.

I feel this is different because I dive into the tools and try to pro-
vories behind actual investigations and how those tools were used in a
way that actually proved useful (or not).

Another difference is that the examples won't show you positive results
with every example. I hate when others do that because the results are
tically unrealistic. Real testing often yields useless information, which is
sm I will show when comparing tools.

# What You Will and Won't Find in This Book

This book will cover a lot of tools and technical uses of those tools. I will also
cover my thought process and the stories behind how I used certain tools to
an further investigation.

This book will contain a number of my personal experiences during actual
inigations or breach scenarios. While the names may be changed to protect the\companies or people involved (but mostly to me), the stories and scenarios
present are completely nonfiction. I have a very "out-of-the-box" approach to life,
so will offer life lessons and hacks along the way that may someday help you.

I also don't like that most technical books only feature the perspective of
single person (the author).

I will be the first to admit that I don't know everything about OSINT or digital
inigations. Many different facets of technology can come up during an inv-
ation may require a unique perspective or an understanding that comes
from years of hands-on experience, which is why I have always tried to surround
mys with people that I respect and that I feel are experts I can learn from.
