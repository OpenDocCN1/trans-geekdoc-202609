Most people will take this information and immediately start following the new rabbit hole digging into the new clues. This is where I would advise some restraint. As exciting as it is to follow a fresh lead, doing so would most likely mean that you will go back and finish searching through Archive.org (or whatever it is that you are searching on the moment). There can be more clues to find in your investigative process (and in this case, there are). Just a note of caution: if you immediately leave to go exploring, important clues (and will) will be missed. In other words, try to hold back on the impulse to jump to something new and finish your current task first. Spending a little extra time now can potentially lead to a bigger payoff later.
