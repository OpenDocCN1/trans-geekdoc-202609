# Microsoft

Another almost guaranteed hit is looking for Hotmail or Skype accounts. Skype accounts were heavily used by threat actors a few years ago before Microsoft did away with Skype's group chat (and pretty much destroyed the product). Even most threat actors have moved away from Skype to Telegram or Discord, the accounts are most likely still available.

A Microsoft account can be searched by username, email address, or phone number. Figure 17.26 shows the identity verification screen of one of my personal Microsoft accounts.
Unlike other sites, Microsoft discloses the full domain name. If the target was using something like gmail.com or yahoo.com, it would not be a big deal. In the case of using one of my personal accounts, the verification domain is not something that would be easily guessed.
