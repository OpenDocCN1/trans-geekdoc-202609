## DNSDumpster

People are busy. The busier they are, the more forgetful they tend to be
about cleaning things up. When looking into DNS records or even simply looking
for more information about target, I am always able to find information on
subdomains that have been decommissioned. It is very likely that developers-
ophers create “test” subdomains for development projects not yet pushed to
production. Developers aren't always great at cleaning up when working on their own development sites.
employ shortcuts, especially when working on their own development sites.
these subdomains and shortcuts can often provide a gold mine of information.

## Hacker Target

Hacker Target (https://hackertarget.com/reverse-dns-lookup) makes searching for subdomains and reverse lookups really easy. You can just query a domain or IP like this:

```
https://api.hackertarget.com/reversedns?q=url
```
