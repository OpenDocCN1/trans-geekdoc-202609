Looking at this data, it does not look like the WhitePacket alias is very active on Reddit. It looks like the majority of his posts are on a subreddit called CanadianMOM. Before you get all excited, this is not a porn subreddit. MOM stands for Mail Order Marijuana.

This information is significant because it will allow you not only to track the user's activities and interests but also to see how they interact with. Building a profile on your target's friends and acquaintances can be just as important as building a profile on the actor. One friend leads to two friends, and the more information you have, the more dots you can start to connect.

# A Critical “Peace” of the TDO Investigation

To put this into perspective and give a real example of how useful this type of information can be, the most critical moment of my TDO investigation was when I discovered user cr00 was actually “Peace of Mind” (aka Peace).

I knew to start looking for Peace, I was able to look into who his friends and associates were. It was not until that point that I understood who was operating TDO. By this point (somehow in 2018), there were more than enough threat intel reports suggesting that Peace and Ping (the owner of Hell, a popular darkweb hacker) were the same person.

Assuming this information to be true (I discovered otherwise), I started researching, all of his known associates, and all of the major players from Hell. Hell led me down the path of finding the historical forum data (which included SQL dumps and scrapes). Once I had, I was able to form a picture of who Peace's close friends were based on the forum conversation.

The old Hell forum posts made it very clear that Ping and another user, Revolt, were very close friends. At one point during the height of some media attention surrounding Hell, Ping made a statement that he was giving full admin control to Revolt while “he was away."

I don't know about you, but I am not giving admin access to anything unless I really trust the person I am giving it to. Keeping this in mind, if we know that Peace, cra00, is part of TDO, wouldn't it stand to reason that he was involved with people he really close with? What better place to start looking Revolt?
