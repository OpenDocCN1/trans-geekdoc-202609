# Finding Common Sites Using Google Analytics Tracker

Heading over to the Relationship Profile tab, you can see the Google Analytics ID in use on this site. You can often find related websites or subdomains by looking at the GA tracking ID. More than not, people will use the same Google account to set up analytics tracking for multiple websites. Analyzing the ID can give you a clue to some of their other domains. The Relationship Profile (Figure 6.2) will show a history of tags used on the site.
