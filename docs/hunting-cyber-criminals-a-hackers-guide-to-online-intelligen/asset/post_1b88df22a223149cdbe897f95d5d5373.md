> NOTE One of the reasons I don't start with a shotgun approach is the sheer volume of returned results. Having to mentally process that information in such a short amount of time makes it very easy to miss something. Unless you have a really solid system for cataloging and storing information, chances are something in these results will take you down one particular path, at which point it is very difficult to circle back and start over.
