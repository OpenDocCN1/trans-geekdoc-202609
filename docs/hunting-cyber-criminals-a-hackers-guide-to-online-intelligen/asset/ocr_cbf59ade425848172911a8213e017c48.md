# Keeping Notes

Every time you discover a new piece of content, you should be adding it to a mindmap, Excel sheet, or some “thing” that you can go back and easily reference. The further down the rabbit hole you travel, the more information you will uncover, and it won't take any time at all before you are forgetting and losing track of potential keys of information. You need a way to document and track all of the things you found along the way (I provide details on my own method starting in Chapter 16).
