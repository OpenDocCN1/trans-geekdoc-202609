After you enter the target server's IP address and click Save, you will be taken to the list of MongoDB connections (shown in Figure 14.2).

Clicking the Connect button will connect you to your MongoDB server. The first thing you will notice in the left column is a tree menu with a list of available databases and collections. Figure 14.3 shows the databases available on the test server.

![](bbox=[0.19, 0.566, 0.391, 0.651])

Figure 14.3

For easy reference, I will refer to the blurred database name in Figure 14.3 as PrivateDB.

Expanding each of the databases will show the list of collections for each database. Figure 14.4 shows the list of expanded collections for each database.

Since PrivateDB looks the most interesting, let’s continue to explore this database. Within PrivateDB, we can see eight different collections. Clicking the “users” collection will expand the application window and show us a list of documents available within that collection (Figure 14.5).
