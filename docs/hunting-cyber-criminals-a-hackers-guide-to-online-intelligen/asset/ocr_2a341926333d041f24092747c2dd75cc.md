# Scraping Wayback with Photon

Many other line-based tools can be used to scrape Wayback archives. One of them is Photon, a tool we discussed in a previous chapter. The Photon Wayback scraper will fetch all of the available URLs of a site as “seeds.” In this context, a seed is basically a list of other URLs that you can go back and visit/scrape. In other, Photon acting like a spider and will crawl the different URLs available on Wayback and give you a nicely formatted list.

There will be many cases where websites (or forums) do not require authentication to see their posts.
