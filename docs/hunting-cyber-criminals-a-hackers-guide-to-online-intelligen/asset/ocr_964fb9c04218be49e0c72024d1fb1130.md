When I went through and looking at the data, I saw a lot of references to a company called Regpack. It was a little bit weird because the data was presented as one thing (Blue Snap), but looking at the data implies another.

What I discovered is Regpack runs a registration service that you can embed on your web host, whether it’s a scout group or a school charity, you can now take payments on your site.

The quote from Regpack was “We have run the full security protocol implemented in these cases conclusively determine that our servers were not involved.” Then the next day, they turn around and issue the following statement that the data did come from them.

Their statement was, “We identified that a human error caused these decrypted files to be exposed to the public facing server, and this was the source of their loss.” Then they continue and say, “Regpack systems were not breached.”
