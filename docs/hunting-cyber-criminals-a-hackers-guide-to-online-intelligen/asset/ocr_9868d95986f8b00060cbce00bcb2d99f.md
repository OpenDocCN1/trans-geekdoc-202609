# EXPERT TIP: TROY HUNT

One of the things I see a lot of credential stuffing attacks against Spotify. I regularly get people who say, "Hey, I Have Been Pwned, I just picked up a paste (from pastebin) and it looks like Spotify had a data breach." And then you go and look at the paste, and realize, "Wow, this is formatted like every other ‘data breach,’ where’s actually gone username in cleartext and the type of membership, premium, or what they have.

Every single time I go back with some of the users, and it always comes back to bad reused passwords. I don't know if "garbage" is the word I'd use because they're legitimate credentials, but they're not to "data breaches." They're often passed off as data breaches by people saying, "Hey, we got this Spotify breach." You, don't you.

On the theme of fabricated breaches, another that I see a lot of people to try to pass off Twitter, where they'll say, "Hey, they're the 30 million accounts from Twitter." This always smells bad, and I actually reached out to the Twitter CISO to confirm some of the data, and it always comes back being part of another combo list of credential stuffing. It's just some recycled passwords being passed around over and over.
