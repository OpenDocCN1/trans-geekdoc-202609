Maltego (not covered in this book) also has free transforms available to install from the Hub. These are provided by blockchain.com and provide transforms to locate input and output addresses from transactions. This can be very useful as a low-cost visualization tool. Ciphertrace sells transforms to give access to its Bitcoin identification database from Maltego.
