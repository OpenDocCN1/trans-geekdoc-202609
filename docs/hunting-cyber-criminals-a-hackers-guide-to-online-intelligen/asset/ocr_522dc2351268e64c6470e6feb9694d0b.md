# Summary

This chapter discussed the building of a tracking matrix, which should be used to keep all pertinent information on your target. The Excel-tracking matrix contains three distinct sections: Accounts, Verifications, and Dumps.

This chapter covered several techniques used to fill up your Accounts and Verifications sections. The Accounts section is used as an inventory of all the sites where your target has an account, while the Verifications section is used to catalog the “account reset” clues provided by those sites.

The next chapter will focus on using the data leaked from hacked websites (aka password dumps) to help us complete our threat actor profile.
