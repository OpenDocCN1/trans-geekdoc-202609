# Worth the Money? Absolutely

Whisology is an excellent service that provides an affordable option for accessing a significant amount of historical domain history. Whisology also offers API connections to many of the tools previously discussed, such as Intrigue, io, SpiderFoot, and more. If you are looking for an affordable option (or do not want to pay a monthly fee), or something that easily plugs into your existing toolsets for the fastest possible results, this is your way to go. Whisology works on credits—it is $5 per day report—so you can only buy what you need without the monthly overhead. This is very useful when working with tools like Intrigue—you can use/buy credits as you need.

# DomainTools

DomainTools (DT) can be your one-stop shop for everything domain-related—if you are willing to pay for it. A great tool can easily become your “third arm,” and for me, this is one of those. If I were to prioritize the importance and necessity of tools discussed in this book, DomainTools would absolutely be in my top three.
