# Identifying Exchanges and Traders

Moving from transaction to transaction is straightforward but fundamentally meaningless unless we can identify a trusted and real-world resource that may have KYC information or log data such as IP addresses. Identifying these resources is key in many investigations. It should be noted that it is possible to track through an exchange. A deposit of a coin to an exchange followed by a withdrawal will not return you to the same coin more than paying $10 to a bank and then withdrawing it from an ATM will return the same name. Once a deposit has been made, only your change at exchange is tracing the job with to supply the required KYC data.

Several websites identify the addresses belonging to exchanges and traders; the most reliable are walletexplorer.com and bitcoinwho.com. The latter site also has a scam reporting function and also searches the web looking for mentions of the addresses you searched for.

Walletexplorer.com attempts to use clustering techniques to identify one address and then ownership of other addresses owned by the same entity. This is the same method used by the commercial software offerings but without the budget! The process is simple: make a deposit into an exchange and they withdraw a coin and you will see the input address. You now have two addresses you know owned by the exchange.

Next, use clustering techniques and algorithms to locate other addresses owned by the same entity. A full discussion of clustering is beside this introduction, but as an example, look for transactions where the known address and the input address are shared with other input addresses. It is likely that those addresses are owned by the same entity.

This is where the commercial tools excel, and if you are serious about investigating cryptocurrency, your life will be very hard indeed without purchasing one of the primary tools. All of the attempt to identify clusters of addresses owned by exchanges, traders, dark web sites, and others to assist the investigator. Most have visualization capabilities to make the job of tracing from your suspect transaction to an exchange or other known entity much easier.
