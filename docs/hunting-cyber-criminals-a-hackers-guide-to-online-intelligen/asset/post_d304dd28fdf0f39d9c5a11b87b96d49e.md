We have a literal swarm of domains that are sharing the SSL certificate as WhitePacket.com. In a best-case scenario, this will reveal domains that are all the same owner. This does not necessarily imply they are the same owner, just that they are all sharing the same Cloudflare account. I reached out to someone on Cloudflare's investigative team regarding the use of certificates to find related owners. This was their response: "In the vast majority of cases completely unrelated domains grouped together on shared SNI certificates."

As OSINT related, this is just one more technique you can use to gain more clues as you spiral down the rabbit hole. In our case, the information turned out to be valid. However, it's important to note that Cloudflare has pointed out this may not always be the case. Tread carefully.
