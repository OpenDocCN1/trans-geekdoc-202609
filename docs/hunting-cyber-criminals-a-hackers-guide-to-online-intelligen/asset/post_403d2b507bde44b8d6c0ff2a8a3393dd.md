
Looking at this data, it does not look like the WhitePacket alias is very active on Reddit. It looks like the majority of his posts are on a subreddit called CanadianMOM. Before you get all excited, this is not a porn subreddit. MOM stands for Mail Order Marijuana.

This information is significant because it will allow you not only to track the user's activities and interests but also to see how they interact with. Building a profile on your target's friends and acquaintances can be just as important as building a profile on the actor. One friend leads to two friends, and the more information you have, the more dots you can start to connect.

To put this into perspective and give a real example of how useful this type of information can be, the most critical moment of my TDO investigation was when I discovered user cr00 was actually “Peace of Mind” (aka Peace).

I don't know about you, but I am not giving admin access to anything unless I really trust the person I am giving it to. Keeping this in mind, if we know that Peace, cr00, is part of TDO, wouldn't it stand to reason that he was involved with people he really close with? What better place to start looking Revolt?
