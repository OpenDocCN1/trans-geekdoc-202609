# Developing a Business Model (with Lasers!!)

One evening circa 2011, I was browsing the Internet the same way most people do: with Burp suite active and running passive recon on all sites I visited.

I was telling myself about an awesome site that sells high-powered lasers. That was a hard no, but much to my surprise, Burp suite found a passive SQL vulnerability in the site. I had to check it out, and before I knew it, I was able to see the site's accounts with hashed passwords. Logging in to the site with one of the admin accounts meant to crack the admin's password hash, which wasn't difficult using any number of online hacker crackers given that the password was some variation of Admin123.

I logged into the site and had access to everything. System records, user accounts, information, and all.

> NOTE Yes, I now realize this action was not exactly "legal," but don't judge me. We all have to start somewhere. Plus, this is a happy ending.

It was that exact moment that I felt the entrepreneurial spark. What if I could take this information and give it to the site's owners so they could fix the injection bug, preventing others from accessing the site in the same way? Surely they would repay this random kind of kindness with their badass high-power lasers?

I am the now owner of a 2,000mW blue laser, and a 1,000mW green laser. Nice, right? The lasers actually burn stuff. They are pretty-bad.

More importantly, the closed the SQL vulnerability, and I had a model for a business to provide services that could actually help people.

In the process, I also learned an extremely valuable lesson: If you have a website, try to offer the solution to the customer and ask for a "tip" in the form of a product from their website, it could be interpreted as extortion.

Oops That clearly wasn't my intent, which I think came off in my email with the CEO, but looking back, I am sure I could have been in some trouble. While this particular exercise worked out well for everyone, I clearly had to do some work refining the business model.
