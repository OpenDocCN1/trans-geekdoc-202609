# Image Searching

When I was investigating the origins of The Dark Overlord, there were several images that first appeared as profile avatars. While this was seemingly insignificant, a closer look revealed that the images were distinct enough to be linked to other online handles. Hackers (at least while they are still skilled) will reuse images across forums, so being able to find multiple occurrences of those images, especially over social media, can be significant.

I typically turn to three tools when searching images: TinEye, EagleEye, and Google reverse image search. Let's look at all three, first with a word from Cat Murdock.

> EXPERT TIP: CAT MURDOC

One thing I've encountered multiple times is women who are now professionals who had compromising photos of themselves leaked data, earlier in their lives.
I think many people don't know the data is out there. They don't realize that someone put it, like a photo published to a Flickr page, someone's blog, and suddenly it is on Google Images.
I think it's an interesting benefit of OSINT that we've been able to identify some of information, and even help people get removed. It's different if somebody is exploiting or extorting a person, or is actually using the data to manipulate someone in some way... it becomes a much more difficult situation because of the emotions involved; it's never an easy solution to begin with.
