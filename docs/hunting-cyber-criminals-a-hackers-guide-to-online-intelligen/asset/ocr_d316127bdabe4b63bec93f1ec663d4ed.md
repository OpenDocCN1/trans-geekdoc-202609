# SpiderFoot HX (Premium)

SpiderFoot HX is the premium version of SpiderFoot. HX is purely cloud-based and contains a number of additional enhancements and capabilitites including improved scan speeds, multi-threaded/concurring scan, better visualizations, team collaboration, risk identification, and significantly improved automated data correlations.
