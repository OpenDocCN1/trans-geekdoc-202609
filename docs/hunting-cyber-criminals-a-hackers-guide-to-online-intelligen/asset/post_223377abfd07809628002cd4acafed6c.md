## What I Learned from Watching Lost

Lost is one of my all-time favorite TV shows. One of the things I especially loved about the show was that you never knew who or what to believe. After watching it a second time, occurred to me that the reason I was having such a hard time figuring out what was happening that I had a natural inclination to assume everyone was lying.

It's really interesting to review the show with that perspective. You will quickly find Benjamin Linus (one of the most mysterious and hated characters on the show) never actually lied about anything. He always told the truth about what was happening, but people chose not to believe him.

When watching the show for the first time, I assumed the "others" were lying about everything, which caused me to spend so much time trying to figure out what was really happening that I missed many obvious truths.
