# About the Editors

![](bbox=[0.105, 0.178, 0.283, 0.355])

Dr. Biswajit Jena is an assistant professor in the Computer Science and Engineering Department at ITER, SOA (Deemed to be University), Bhubaneswar, India. He received his PhD from IIIT-Bhubaneswar, India, in Biomedical Image Analysis, which employs Artificial Intelligence, and his MTech degree in Computer Science and Engineering from NIT, Rourkela. His broad research interests are biomedical image processing, neuro-oncology, radiomics, machine learning, and deep learning. He has published over 30 journal and conference papers and has contributed his knowledge as an editorial board member and reviewer for multiple international journals.

Dr. Sanjay Saxena serves as Assistant Professor in the Computer Science Department at IIT Bhubaneswar and member of board of Directors and CEO of Research, Innovation, Projects, Incubation and Technology Transfer (RIPITT). He obtained his PhD from the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU), Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India, and completed his postdoctoral research at the Artificial Intelligence Imaging Lab, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, PA, United States. His primary research area includes AI-based methods for brain cancer analysis, a broader focus on data science, machine learning, deep learning, and the emerging fields of radiomics and radiogenomics. His tenure under Professor Christos Davatikos at the University of Pennsylvania was instrumental in his significant learning about radiomics/radiogenomics. He has presented his work at globally recognized universities such as Imperial College London, Stony Brook, and Vienna University of Technology and has made substantial contributions to several peer-reviewed journals, conference publications, books, and book chapters.

Dr. Sudip Paul is currently an Associate Professor in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at North-Eastern Hill University, Shillong, India. He completed his postdoctoral research at the School of Computer Science and Software Engineering, the University of Western Australia, Perth, under the scheme of Biotechnology Overseas for the Scientists working in the North Eastern States of India: 2017–18, supported by the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India. He received his PhD from the Indian Institute of Technology (Banaras Hindu University), Varanasi, with a specialization in electrophysiology and brain signal analysis. He also organized many workshops and conferences, most significant are the Indian Academy of Neuroscience Meeting 2022, IEEE Conference on Computational Performance Evaluation 2020 and 2021; IRBO/ APRC Associate Conference 2017; IRBO Global Engagement 2021 and the 29th Annual meeting of the Society for Neurochemistry, India 2015. Dr. Sudip has published nearly 66 international/national journal papers and more than 40 conference papers on different international/national platforms. He has filed eight patents, of four which were granted. He completed 20 book projects with Springer Nature, Elsevier, I Global, etc., as an editor/author.
