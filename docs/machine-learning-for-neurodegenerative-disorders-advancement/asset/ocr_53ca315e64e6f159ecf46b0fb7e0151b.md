# An Empirical Study on Neurodegenerative Disorders

Event extraction's importance in biomedical research

+   - Illness progression: Event extraction offers important insights into illness trajectories and progression by assisting researchers and physicians in tracking the onset and progression of diseases through time.
- Identification of symptoms: For an accurate diagnosis and treatment planning, it is essential to note the symptoms that are documented in clinical records and literature.

Research in biomedicine using event extraction

+   - Patient monitoring: In EHRs, event extraction enables automated tracking of illness development and symptoms to support individualized patient treatment.
- Drug development: Determining disease progression and symptom occurrences from clinical trial data assists in determining treatment efficacy and safety. Automation of the literature review allows researchers to quickly and effectively collect data for studies relating to diseases from the body of scientific literature.

Problems with event extraction for symptoms and disease progression

+   - Language complexity: Event extraction is difficult because biomedical material frequently uses complicated sentence structures, medical jargon, and domain-specific acronyms.
- Ambiguity: In medical writings, ambiguous language and polysemy can result in incorrect interpretations of the course of a disease and the occurrence of symptoms.
- Absence of labeled data: Building annotated datasets for event extraction in the biomedical sector is labor and resource intensive.

Event extraction methods

+   - Rule-based approaches: Rules and patterns are used to locate and extract particular events and relationships based on linguistic and syntactic aspects. In order to extract events from annotated data, supervised learning involves deep learning and machine learning models on the annotated data. These models may include RNNs or transformer-based models. Rule-based and supervised methods are combined in hybrid approaches to take advantage of each approach's advantages.
- Performance assessment for event extraction: Metrics like precision, recall, and F1-score are used to assess the accuracy of event extraction models. These metrics assess how well they can accurately identify the events and symptoms of illness development while reducing false positives and false negatives. To evaluate the generalization potential of the models, cross-validation and testing on external datasets are performed.

# 3.5 SENTIMENT ANALYSIS AND TEXT MINING

Text mining and sentiment analysis are useful methods for researching neurodegenerative illnesses.

+   - Sentiment analysis: Evaluates the emotional undertones found in clinical notes, research publications, and patient comments to gain understanding of patients' perspectives on various therapies.
- Text mining: The process of taking important information out of unstructured text and organizing it for use in things like biomarker identification, pattern recognition, and trend analysis. Methods improve clinical decision-making, facilitate individualized therapy, and deepen our understanding of diseases. This is achieved through the integration and analysis of many data sources.
