# 3.8. EARLY SIGNS AND BIOMARKERS OF NEURODEGENERATIVE DISEASES USING NLP

Cognitive deterioration, motor impairments, and other disabling symptoms are caused by neurodegenerative illnesses, which alter the structure and function of the nervous system. For prompt intervention and successful therapy, it is essential to identify biomarkers for these illnesses early on. In order to analyze and gain useful insights from amounts of unstructured text data, NLP approaches have become essential tools in biomedical research. This overview shows the value of NLP in identifying early symptoms and biomarkers of neurodegenerative illnesses, as well as its applications and associated difficulties.

Healthcare practitioners can benefit from early identification and individualized treatment planning by incorporating NLP into clinical decision support systems. In contention, NLP is crucial in spotting early symptoms and locating potential biomarkers of neurodegenerative diseases. NLP supports early intervention, biomarker identification, and data-driven research by effectively analyzing biological literature, clinical records, and patient-reported data. The accuracy and usefulness of NLP in neurodegenerative disorder research will be improved by overcoming issues with data quality, ambiguity, and ethical considerations. This will improve patient outcomes and advance our knowledge of these crippling conditions.
