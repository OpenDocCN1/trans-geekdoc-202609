# 1.4.8 NEURODEGENERATIVE DISEASE RESEARCH

Techniques such as deep learning, clustering, and dimensionality reduction are applied to neuroimaging data, genetic profiles, and clinical information to uncover patterns and predict disease progression. ML models help stratify patients by disease states, predict treatment responses, and understand the underlying mechanisms of neurodegeneration (Turrisi et al., 2023). This research contributes to early diagnosis, personalized treatment strategies, and the development of new interventions. # 1.4.9 NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING IN NEUROSCIENCE

Natural language processing (NLP) applies ML techniques to analyze and interpret textual data from clinical notes, research articles, and patient reports in neuroscience. NLP algorithms, as entity recognition, semantic analysis, and topic modeling, are used to extract meaningful information from unstructured text, identify patterns, and track disease progression (Kawai et al., 2023). NLP aids in automating the retrieval of relevant information from large corpora, facilitating workflows, and supporting clinical decision-making. Analyzing language in patient interactions and scientific literature enhances cognitive and communicative aspects of neurological conditions and enhancing data-driven research. NLP techniques quantify vast amounts of scientific literature to extract relevant information (Ellwood, 2023). ML-powered tools summarize research findings, identify trends, and assist researchers in staying with the latest advancements in neuroscience.
