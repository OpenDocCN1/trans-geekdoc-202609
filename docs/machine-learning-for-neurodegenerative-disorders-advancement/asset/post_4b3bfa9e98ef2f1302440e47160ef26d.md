## 4.5.3 AI in Huntington’s Disease Research

Huntington’s disease, a genetic neurodegenerative disorder, presents unique challenges due to its hereditary nature and severe impact on both motor and cognitive functions [3]. AI is emerging as a powerful tool for researching this condition, offering new approaches to understanding and potentially treating it [138]. AI has shown notable effectiveness in pathway analysis, particularly in understanding Huntington’s disease. This process involves intricate biological mechanisms, such as proper folding of the huntingtin protein and its impact on numerous cellular pathways. ML models are adept at analyzing these biological pathways, providing insights into how the disease progresses at the molecular level. By processing large amounts of genomic, proteomic, and metabolic data, these models can identify key molecules and pathways that are altered in Huntington’s disease [139]. For example, AI algorithms can analyze data from patient cells to understand how the mutated huntingtin protein affects cellular functions such as gene expression, protein synthesis, and metabolic processes. This analysis helps identify potential intervention points where drugs might be able to modify disease progression. Elucidating these pathways in detail is crucial for developing targeted therapies that can more effectively slow or stop the disease [14].

Predictive modeling is another significant application of AI in Huntington's disease research. AI models can be trained on clinical data, including genetic information, biomarkers, and patient histories, to predict the onset and progression of the disease. These predictive models can assist clinicians in making informed decisions about patient care, as well as when to start treatment or how to manage disease progression [62]. For instance, an AI model might predict the age of onset of Huntington's disease in an individual based on their genetic makeup, or it could predict the progression rate based on early symptoms. This information is invaluable in planning treatment and care strategies, as well as in counseling patients and their families about what to expect as the disease progresses [140].

AI is also being reused for existing drugs for the treatment of Huntington's disease. The complexity of developing new drugs, repurposing these drugs represents a viable and quicker path to identifying therapeutic options. AI algorithms can scan databases of existing medications to identify that might have beneficial effects on Huntington's disease. These algorithms analyze drug properties, such as molecular structure and biological activity, and match them against the requirements for modifying Huntington's disease pathology [69]. For example, a drug initially developed for a different neurodegenerative disease, or a different type of disorder might show promise in alleviating some of the symptoms or slowing the progression of Huntington's disease. Identifying such drugs can significantly accelerate the availability of treatments for patients, as these drugs have already been tested for safety and efficacy in humans [141].
