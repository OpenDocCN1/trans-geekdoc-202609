disease-centric. The drug-centric approach focuses on understanding and expanding the potential
applications of a known drug, exploring its efficacy in treating conditions other than those for which
it was originally approved. This method leverages the existing knowledge about the safety profile
and mechanism of drugs [116]. On the other hand, the disease-centric approach starts with
specific search for diseases, whether either or discontinued, which might be effective
against it. This method is particularly useful for identifying treatments for diseases which effect-
therapie are lacking. Both of these approaches can be studied and facilitated by experimental
methods, such as clinical trials and in silico studies, which computational models and simulations
predict a drug's efficacy and safety in different scenarios [12]. These computational methods are particularly valuable for screening large numbers of compounds quickly and effectively,
making a crucial component of modern drug repurposing strategies.
