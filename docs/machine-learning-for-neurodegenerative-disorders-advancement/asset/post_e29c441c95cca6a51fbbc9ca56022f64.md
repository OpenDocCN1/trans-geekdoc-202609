# AI-Driven Drug Discovery and Repurposing for Neurodegenerative Disorders

Bancha Yingnam U Ratchathani University, Thailand

## 4.1 INTRODUCTION

Neurodegenerative disorders, a class of diseases characterized by the progressive degeneration of the nervous system's structure and function, significantly impact global health [1]. These conditions, including Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, and amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS), affect millions of patients worldwide [2–4]. Patients often experience a range of complications, from motor dysfunction to severe cognitive decline, which severely impacts their quality of life and that of their families [5]. Despite extensive research, effective treatments for these disorders are scarce, primarily due to their complex pathogenesis and the limited understanding of their mechanisms [6]. The public health burden of neurodegenerative disorders necessitates rapid and effective drug discovery [7]. Although valuable, de novo drug discovery methods are often slow and have low rates [8]. Moreover, newly developed drugs undergo years of testing and validation [9]. These challenges underscore the urgency of developing more efficient methodologies, not only for discovering new drugs but also repurposing existing drugs for new therapeutic targets. Drug repurposing, the process of applying drugs to different medical conditions, offers a quicker and more cost-effective approach.

AI applications in biomedicine range from diagnostic assistance and predictive analytics to personalized medicine and drug discovery [11]. In particular, machine learning (ML) algorithms and deep learning are adept at analyzing large, intricate biomedical datasets, which can be challenging to interpret otherwise [9]. This technology has the potential to significantly expedite drug discovery cycles, optimize molecular docking simulations, and predict patient responses to specific treatments [13].

The aims of this study are as follows:

- To provide an in-depth analysis of recent advancements in AI technologies and applications in drug discovery for neurodegenerative disorders. This chapter includes a review of how AI is shaping the identification and development of new therapeutic compounds.
- To explore the specific AI methods used in the field, ranging from data collection and standardization to advanced techniques such as molecular docking and in silico screening. The goal is to determine how these methods contribute to effective drug discovery and repurposing.
- To explore how AI is being applied to study and develop treatments for specific neurodegenerative diseases, including Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, Huntington's disease, and ALS. This will involve discussing AI-driven insights and breakthroughs relevant to each of these conditions.
