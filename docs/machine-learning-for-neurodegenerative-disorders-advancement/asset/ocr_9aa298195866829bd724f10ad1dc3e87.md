# 1.5.7 Treatment Planning, Monitoring, and Multimodal Fusion

ML assists in surgery planning by providing precise anatomical maps for neuro disorders (Jiang, 2023; Moreno-Rodríguez et al., 2023; Schmidgall & Hays, 2023). It can also monitor changes in brain tissue, helping evaluate the effectiveness of interventions. ML combines information from neuroimaging modalities (such as fMRI, EEG, and structural MRI) to create a holistic view of brain function and structure. This integration enhances our understanding of brain networks and their interactions (Jiang, 2023). These detailed applications show how ML enhances the capabilities of neuroimaging, enabling researchers and clinicians to extract intricate insights from brain data, data analytics, and develop more treatments for neurological disorders.

# 1.5.9 Classification, Diagnosis, and Predictive Modeling

ML models can differentiate between neurological conditions based on patterns in neuroimaging data (Alazaz et al., 2023; Gözütz et al., 2023). Through learning from labeled datasets, these models classify images as healthy or diseased, aiding in the accuracy and early diagnosis of disorders such as Alzheimer's disease, stroke, and brain tumors. Also, ML can be used to develop predictive models that predict disease progression or treatment outcomes using historical neuroimaging data and clinical information (Zarr & Brown, 2023). These models provide clinicians with valuable insights into how a condition might evolve, enabling more informed decisions regarding care.
