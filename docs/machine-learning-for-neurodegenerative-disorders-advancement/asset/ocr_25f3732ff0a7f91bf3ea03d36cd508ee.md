Xia et al. [5] introduced an approach for diagnosing AD by categorizing the resting-state electroencephalogram (EEG) of AD, MCI, and healthy control (HC). The deep pyramidal neural network (CNN) is the foundation of this model. It is a one-dimensional CNN model proposed for the three-class classification of EEG of AD, MCI, and HC.

To help families understand how their loved ones' disease evolves and take preventive action, Liu et al. [6] developed an approach that uses spectrogram characteristics collected from voice data to identify AD. Various ML techniques were used during the experiment process using the speech data of AD and HC.

Zeng et al. [7] proposes a framework for diagnosing AD, including principal component analysis, FE-processing of MRI images, and SVMs. To optimize the SVM parameters, a switching delayed particle swarm optimization approach is put forth. MRI scans from the ADNI dataset, this model is successfully applied to categorize AD and MCI.

Murthy and Koogaludi [8] aim to put forth the vocal and non-local regions by incorporating innovative structure-based features on conventional features. Formant attack slope (FAS), pitch, jitter, shimmer, formant heights from base-to-peak (FH1), peak-to-peak (FH2), formant angles at peak (FA1), valley (FA2) and FS are features that have been taken into correlation. Mel-frequency cep coefficients (MFCCs) and linear prediction coefficients (LPCCs) are other features that have been considered. Classifiers like random forests (RFs), SVM, and artificial neural networks (ANNs) have been taken into choice. The pertinent characteristics interest considering all dimensions; this process is known as genetic algorithm-based feature selection (GAFS).

Zhang et al. [9] present a framework for diagnosing AD, including FE, fuzzy k-NN (Fk-NN) algorithm, and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) image pre-processing. This aspect of the framework is in how it optimizes the parameters of FK-NN by integrating the Sobol sequence and stochastic fractal search (SFS) processes through an enhanced Harris Hawks optimization (HHO) algorithm. Using the Sobol sequence, the HHO approach improves the overall quality of the starting-population, and the SFS mechanism boosts the algorithm's ability to escape the optimum solution.

Shukla et al. [10] have the positron emission tomography (PET) and MRI modalities to implement result visualization for AD prediction. To ascertain which region is most crucial for the identification of AD in comparison to other subsets, it analyzes the subcortical structures using the ensemble model (EM) and additional ML techniques.

A deep learning-based method for identifying AD is proposed by Janghel and Rathore [11] using the ADNI database of Alzheimer's patients. The dataset includes images of real people and Alzheimer's patients with functional MRI (fMRI) and PET scans. Before the VGG-16 architecture of the CNN for FE, it to 2d sequencing and image scaling. SVM, linear discriminate, k-means clustering, and decision tree (DT) classifiers are utilized in classification.

Clinical trials, a text-based modification of the audio recordings of Alzheimer's patients, were employed by Khan et al. [12]. An approach to linguistic FS via hybrid swarm intelligence is presented, which uses the PSO, DO, and GWO algorithms to extract a composite feature set. Furthermore, a model based on transfer learning is known as HSI-LFS-BERT is put forth. It combines the BERT algorithm with the HSI-LFS approach.
