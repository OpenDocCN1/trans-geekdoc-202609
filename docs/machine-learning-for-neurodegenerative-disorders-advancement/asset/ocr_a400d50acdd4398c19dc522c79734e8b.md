# 3.10.2 NLP Experts and Neuroscientists Working Together

An effective strategy for enhancing our understanding of the brain and neurological illnesses is collaboration between professionals in NLP and neuroscientists. While NLP specialists provide skills in handling and analyzing natural language, neuroscientists bring subject matter expertise in the structure and function of brain. This overview highlights the needs for interdisciplinary collaboration between experts in NLP and neuroscientists, as well as its potential advantages and potential difficulties for researchers [37].

Ensuring the confidentiality and privacy of data when using human subjects in research is crucial. In joint work, it is important to properly credit the efforts of both NLP specialists and neuroscientists.
