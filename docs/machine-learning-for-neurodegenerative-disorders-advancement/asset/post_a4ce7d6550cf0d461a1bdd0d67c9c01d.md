## 4.5 Applications

### 4.5.1 AI IN ALZHEIMER’S DISEASE RESEARCH

Alzheimer’s disease, characterized by memory loss and cognitive decline, presents a significant challenge for healthcare systems and researchers worldwide [17]. The complexity of the disease’s pathology and the need for early diagnosis and effective treatments make it prime candidate for the application of AI technologies, especially ML and deep learning. 

The endeavor to create effective treatments for Alzheimer’s disease involves significant challenges in both complexity and financial investment. Research and development costs for new Alzheimer’s treatments are estimated at approximately $5.7 billion, a figure nearly times greater than for cancer drugs [1]. The most recent drug got FDA approval in this domain, aducanumab, targets amyloid a monoclonal antibody. Its primary action is to reduce the accumulation of β-amyloid in the brain. Although this treatment is notably expensive, costing an estimated $55,000 annually, and is mainly beneficial for patients in the early stages of Alzheimer’s disease who have an amyloid presence.

In repurposing existing diseases has become a promising and more cost-effective strategy. This approach utilizes drugs already approved for other conditions, taking advantage of their well-established safety profiles. This approach reduces the risks associated with drug toxicity and can accelerate the development of new treatments for Alzheimer’s disease.
