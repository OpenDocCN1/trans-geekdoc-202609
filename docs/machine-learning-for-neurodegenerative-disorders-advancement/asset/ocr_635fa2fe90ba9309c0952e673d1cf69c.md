# 11.2.1.3 Data Silos and Privacy Concerns

Data are critical to the development and progress of an ML ecosystem for neurodegenerative disorders, yet there exists a major hurdle in data silos coupled with privacy constraints. Actionable insights are hidden in disconnected data silos across hospitals, research institutions, and pharmaceutical companies, making innovation difficult because of the fragmentation [35]. Figure 11.3 illustrates the effects and consequences of data silos and privacy concerns in neurodegenerative disorder research. Furthermore, ethical issues regarding the privacy of patients generate one more obstacle to be overcome for their full potential [36].

Imagine a fragmented archipelago of information, an essential piece on islands but not within reach. The fragmented reality is here recapitulated in current neurodegenerative research data [37]. Clinical records, imaging data, and other diagnostic information are locked within individual healthcare systems in modern hospitals. Most research data collected in a university or clinical setup do not get clinicians and non-hardware researchers gain access to large datasets [39]. Drug trial and clinical development data, from pharmaceutical companies, are proprietary, restricted-access datasets that have limited ability to influence global research efforts [39].
