# 1.4.4 DIAGNOSIS AND CLASSIFICATION

ML enhances diagnostic accuracy and classification in neuroscience using complex datasets from neuroimaging, genetic profiles, and clinical records. Algorithms as ensemble methods, support vector machines, and deep learning models are trained to recognize patterns with various neurological and psychiatric conditions (Kawai et al., 2023; Kranzinger et al., 2023). These models can differentiate between disorders with similar symptoms, predict disease progression, and identify biomarkers for early diagnosis. ML aids in personalized treatment plans by integrating multi-modal data and refining diagnostic criteria based on empirical evidence, improving patient outcomes and achieving precision medicine in neurology and psychiatry (Turrisi et al., 2023). ML can also predict disease progression and assess responses.

# 1.4.5 PREDICTIVE MODELING

Models such as regression analysis, decision trees, and deep learning networks are employed to predict disease progression, treatment response, and cognitive decline. By analyzing data and identifying patterns associated with different outcomes, ML algorithms provide insights into future trajectories of neurological and psychiatric conditions (Jaison et al., 2023). Predictive models support early intervention strategies, personalized treatment plans, and risk assessment by integrating diverse data sources and capturing relationships among variables (Zheng et al., 2023). For example, ML can predict the cognitive decline rate in Alzheimer's disease patients, aiding in personalized care.
