# 4.5.4 AI in ALS Research

The application of AI in ALS research and patient has shown promising results, particularly in the fields of assistive technology, drug development, and clinical trial. AI has a substantial impact on ALS patients, especially in terms of the advancement of speech and mobility aids. As ALS progresses, patients often lose their ability to speak and move independently. AI-driven algorithms are at the forefront of creating advanced assistive devices that significantly improve communication and mobility for patients [142]. For example, AI-powered speech recognition can help ALS patients who have lost their ability to speak by converting their eye movements or brain signals into speech. These use ML algorithms to interpret subtle signals and translate them into verbal language, allowing patients to communicate with their family and caregivers [44]. AI is used in developing mobility aids, such as wheelchairs that can be controlled through eye tracking or brain-computer interfaces, enabling patients to navigate their environments despite severe motor limitations [143]. These AI-driven technologies significantly enhance the quality of life of ALS patients by providing them with greater autonomy and communication capabilities.

In the field of drug development, generative models are being used to formulate new drugs that can slow or reverse the effects of ALS. Generative models of AI can synthesize chemical compounds based on desired, such as in targeting specific proteins involved in ALS pathology. By analyzing molecular structures and their pharmacological effects, AI models can predict the proposal of new drugs that are likely to be effective against ALS [144]. This approach accelerates the drug discovery process by enabling scientists to focus on testing and refining the most promising candidates, saving valuable time and resources in the search for effective ALS treatments.
