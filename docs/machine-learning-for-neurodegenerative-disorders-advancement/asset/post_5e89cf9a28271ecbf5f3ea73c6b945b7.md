Surrogate end point: PD is characterized by a number of clinical biomarkers, such as taste and smell abnormalities, disrupted circadian rhythm, postural irregularity, tremors, bradykinesia, gait problems, muscle rigidity, incontinence, and impaired spatial and color cognition. # Relation extraction techniques

- **Supervised learning:** Using annotated datasets to train supervised machine learning models for relation extraction, such as support vector machines and deep learning models (for example, BiLSTM-CRF).
- **D supervision:** Using databases or knowledge bases that already exist to automatically labeled data for training, omitting the need for manual annotation.
- **Rule-based methods:** Hand-crafted rules and patterns to locate correlations based on grammatical and syntactic patterns.

Event extraction's importance in biomedical research

- **Illness progression:** Event extraction offers important insights into disease trajectories by assisting researchers.
- **Identification of symptoms:** For an accurate diagnosis and treatment planning, it is essential to note the symptoms documented in clinical records.

Research in biomedicine using event extraction

- **Patient monitoring:** In EHRs, event extraction enables tracking illness development to support individualized treatment.
- **Drug development:** Determining efficacy and safety from clinical trial data. Automation of literature review allows researchers to quickly and effectively collect data.

Problems with event extraction for symptoms and disease progression

- **Language complexity:** Biomedical material frequently uses complicated sentence structures, jargon, and domain-specific acronyms.
- **Ambiguity:** In medical writings, ambiguous language and polysemy can result in incorrect interpretations of the course of a disease and the occurrence of symptoms.
- **Absence of labeled data:** Building annotated datasets for event extraction in the biomedical sector is labor and resource intensive.
