# 4.4.3 ALGORITHMS AND APPROACHES

Classification algorithms are fundamental tools in the ML arsenal. In the context of drug repurposing, they are used to classify drugs based on their likelihood of being effective against specific neurodegenerative diseases. These algorithms analyze the features of drugs, such as their chemical structure, biological activity, and known pharmacological effects, and classify them according their potential therapeutic efficacy. For example, a classification algorithm might analyze a set of compounds and identify those most likely to inhibit a protein that is implicated in Alzheimer's disease. This approach helps prioritize drugs for further investigation and clinical trials, accelerating the repurposing process.

# 4.4.3.1 Natural Language Processing

NLP is a branch of AI that focuses on enabling computers to understand, interpret, and manipulate language. In drug repurposing, NLP algorithms are employed to mine vast amounts of biomedical literature. These algorithms can extract and analyze information from research papers, clinical trial reports, and other textual data, uncovering potential drug-disease relationships that may not be evident from structured data alone [127]. For instance, NLP can be used to identify mentions of off-label drug uses or unexpected drug effects reported in the literature, which could suggest new repurposing opportunities.
