# 3.6 HEALTH-RELATED KNOWLEDGE GRAPHS AND ONTOLOGIES

Neurodegenerative disease research depends heavily on health-related knowledge graphs and ontologies. In order to identify relationships among biomarkers, illnesses, and treatments, knowledge graphs combine a variety of data sources, including genetic, clinical, and therapeutic data. This facilitates pattern recognition and personalized therapy. In order to provide uniform data representation and enhance interchange, ontologies offer standardized vocabulary and classifications. They facilitate more comprehensive understanding of disease mechanisms and improved search capabilities. Collectively, they promote a thorough comprehension of neurodegenerative illnesses and aid in improved diagnosis and focused therapies.
