# 1.3.1.9 Behavioral and Cognitive Psychology

Although not exclusive to neuroscience, behavioral and cognitive psychology are crucial for understanding how neural processes translate into behavior and cognition. Behavioral experiments and cognitive studies provide valuable insights into perception, memory, learning, decision-making, and social interactions (Kawai et al., 2023). The landscape of neuroscience is dynamic, with constant interaction between these levels. Advances in technology, including imaging techniques, genetic tools, and computational methods, continue to drive our understanding of the intricacies of the brain (Ellwood, 2023). Collaborations across disciplines are essential, neuroscience requires insights from biology, physics, mathematics, psychology, and computer science to unveil the mysteries of the nervous system (Rutkowski et al., 2023) (Figure 1.4).
