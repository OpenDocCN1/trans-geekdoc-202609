# 4.5.3 AI in Huntington’s Disease Research

Huntington’s disease, a genetic neurodegenerative disorder, presents unique challenges due to its hereditary nature and severe impact on both motor and cognitive functions [3]. AI is emerging as a powerful tool for researching this condition, offering new approaches to understanding and potentially treating it [138]. AI has shown notable effectiveness in pathway analysis, particularly in understanding Huntington’s disease. This process involves intricate biological mechanisms, such as proper folding of the huntingtin protein and its impact on numerous cellular pathways. ML models are adept at analyzing these biological pathways, providing insights into how the disease progresses at the molecular level. By processing large amounts of genomic, proteomic, and metabolic data, these models can identify key molecules and pathways that are altered in Huntington’s disease [139]. For example, AI algorithms can analyze data from patient cells to understand how the mutated huntingtin protein affects cellular functions such as gene expression, protein synthesis, and metabolic processes.
