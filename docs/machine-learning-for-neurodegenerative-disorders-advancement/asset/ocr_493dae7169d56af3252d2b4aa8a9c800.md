# 1.8.4 TRANSPARENC, INTERPRETABILITY DUAL-USE CONCERNS

The lack of transparency and interpretability in complex ML models raises ethical concerns about accountability and trust (H. Zhang & Vargas, 2023). Understanding how model decisions affect the rationale behind predictions is vital to establish trust and ethical accountability. The knowledge gained from neuroscience research and ML applications could be used for beneficial and harmful purposes (Moridani et al., 2023; Shafik et al., 2020). Ethical considerations encompass developing regulations and ethical guidelines to prevent potential misuse, such as invasive surveillance or unauthorized manipulation of data.

# 1.8.5 UNINTENDED CONSEQUENCES, NEUROENHANCEMENT, AND COGNITIVE ENHANCEMENT

Applying ML to decode neural data could inadvertently affect an individual's perception of their mental states, potentially leading to anxiety or stress (Rutkowski et al., 2023). Ethical evaluation involves assessing these psychological impacts and safeguards to mitigate unintended consequences. The ethical implications of using ML-enhanced insights to improve cognitive abilities raise questions about equitable access, individual autonomy, and societal consequences (Diemann et al., 2023). Balancing the ethical benefits of cognitive enhancement with potential risks and disparities is critical.

# 1.8.6 RESPONSIBILITY IN AI-DECODED INSIGHTS

AI-generated insights based on neural data may infer sensitive information that individuals are unaware of. Ethical considerations include handling and communicating these insights with care and sensitivity to potential psychological implications (Shafik et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2023). Addressing these ethical implications requires interdisciplinary collaboration involving researchers, ethicists, legal experts, policymakers, and public input. Robust data governance and mechanisms for ongoing review and adaptation are essential for ensuring that the integration of ML in neuroscience benefits society while respecting individual rights and well-being.

# 1.9 FUTURE DIRECTIONS AND CHALLENGES

This section presents the future developments at the intersection of ML and neuroscience and highlights challenges that must be addressed for continued progress in this area.

# 1.9.1 ETHICAL DATA GOVERNANCE, PRIVACY SOLUTIONS, AND BIAS DETECTION AND MITIGATION STRATEGIES

Future directions could focus on developing innovative methods for preserving data privacy while enabling meaningful analysis. Techniques such as federated learning, which trains models across distributed data sources without sharing raw data, could be applied to neuroimaging datasets, ensuring individual privacy while benefiting from collective insights (Hakim et al., 2023). Further research could emphasize proactive identification and reduction of biases in ML models applied to neuroscience (Zarr & Brown, 2023). Developing algorithms for highlighting and mitigating potential biases and comprehensive reporting of model biases could lead to more fair and unbiased neuroimaging analyses.
