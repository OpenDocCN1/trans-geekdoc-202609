Prognostic biomarkers: Central nervous system (CNS) abnormalities are directly reflected in CSF, which makes it a valuable resource for identifying biomarkers of neurological illnesses, including PD. Diagnosing PD may be aided by CSF biomarkers such as α-synuclein, DJ-1, and other proteins. With respect to PD progression and its function in antioxidant defense, DJ-1 in particular is a prime contender. While lower DJ-1 levels are associated with advanced PD, the precise between DJ-1 oxidation and PD is not well understood, which could restrict the utility of DJ-1 as a conclusive biomarker. Additional options for finding PD-related biomarkers include plasma, urine, and blood.

3. Predictive biomarkers: The existing treatments for PD are less effective since the disease is frequently misdiagnosed until a large loss of neurons. Diagnostic difficulties arise from early signs and Lewy bodies, which affect only 10% of the population. Positive emission tomography (PET) scans are expensive. For early detection, there is a need for accessible biomarkers as well as knowledge of genetic history, risk factors, and the substantia nigra’s heightened encephalogenicity. Pharmacodynamic: A common technique for finding novel biomarkers for PD in CSF is quantitative protein analysis coupled with computational approaches. The marker α-Synuclein, often known as α-Syn, is important for assessing medication efficacy and diagnosing PD early. Lewy bodies and neurites, which are linked to neurogeneration and the onset of PD symptoms, are formed when α-Syn accumulates and clumps in PD.

Surrogate end point: PD is characterized by a number of clinical biomarkers, such as taste and small abnormalities, disrupted circadian rhythm, postural irregularity, tremors, bradykinesia, gait problems, muscle rigidity, incontinence, and impaired spatial and color cognition. There are also behavioral symptoms like impulsivity, sadness, and dementia. These indicators are used to diagnose PD, even though they may show up in other CNS illnesses. Linking these symptoms to clinical observations and additional indicators is necessary for an accurate diagnosis.
