## 1.5.7 Treatment Planning, Monitoring, and Multimodal Fusion

ML assists in surgery planning by providing precise anatomical maps for neuro disorders (Jiang, 2023; Moreno-Rodríguez et al., 2023; Schmidgall & Hays, 2023). It can also monitor changes in brain tissue, helping evaluate the effectiveness of interventions. ML combines information from neuroimaging modalities (such as fMRI, EEG, and structural MRI) to create a holistic view of brain function and structure. This integration enhances our understanding of brain networks and their interactions (Jiang, 2023). These detailed applications show how ML enhances the capabilities of neuroimaging, enabling researchers and clinicians to extract intricate insights from brain data, data analytics, and develop more treatments for neurological disorders.

## 1.5.8 Pattern Recognition and Functional Activation Mapping

These applications utilize the versatility of ML in neuroimaging and pattern recognition. By harnessing the power of advanced algorithms, researchers can extract meaningful insights from complex data, leading to a deeper understanding of brain function, better diagnosis and treatment of neurological disorders, and the development of innovative technologies to enhance brain health and well-being (Lockhart, 2023). ML algorithms analyze functional neuroimaging data, such as fMRI or PET scans, to create activation maps that showcase brain regions that become active during specific tasks or experiences (Cakar & Yavuz, 2023). These maps enable researchers to understand the neural substructures of cognitive processes and behaviors, providing insights into brain function.

## 1.5.9 Classification, Diagnosis, and Predictive Modeling

ML models can differentiate between neurological conditions based on patterns in neuroimaging data (Alazaz et al., 2023; Gözütz et al., 2023). Through learning from labeled datasets, these models classify images as healthy or diseased, aiding in the accuracy and early diagnosis of disorders such as Alzheimer's disease, stroke, and brain tumors. Also, ML can be used to develop predictive models that predict disease progression or treatment outcomes using historical neuroimaging data and clinical information (Zarr & Brown, 2023). These models provide clinicians with valuable insights into how a condition might evolve, enabling more informed decisions regarding care.

## 1.5.10 Functional Connectivity Analysis and Brain-Computer Interfaces

ML techniques analyze patterns of synchronized brain activity across different regions, revealing functional connectivity networks (Dieckmann et al., 2023). These networks shed light on how different brain areas collaborate and communicate, contributing to our understanding of brain networks and their role in cognition. Similarly, pattern recognition in BCIs involves neural signals generated by the brain and translating them into meaningful commands for external devices (Fahim et al., 2023; Rutkowski et al., 2023). This technology enables individuals with motor abilities to control computers, robotic limbs, or communication devices directly through their brain activity.

pattern recognition can predict an individual's cognitive state, such as attention levels, emotional responses, and decision-making processes (Kalinaki et al., 2023; Vedaci et al., 2023).
