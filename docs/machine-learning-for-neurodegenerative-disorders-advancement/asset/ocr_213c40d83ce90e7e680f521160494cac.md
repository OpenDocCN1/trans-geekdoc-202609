# 11.3.3.3 De-biased Datasets

The quest for effective treatments and cures for neurodegenerative diseases hinges on robust data, but that data itself is a double-edged sword. Biases embedded within datasets can perpetuate inequalities and hinder progress [93]. Here's de-biased datasets as the cornerstone for fair and equitable neuroresearch, ensuring benefits from advancements.
To consider an AI diagnoses on the training set, in which white males make up 99% of it, could potentially miss important signals in female or darker-skinned cohorts and, therefore, widen the gap and delay treatment [94]. Models do not work for certain demographic groups where there are biases in data collection underrepresenting them. These biases, woven into the model's structure in a nearly algorithmic nature, can compound existing biases and lead to unfair predictions [59]. Lastly, biases such as healthcare access or care be taken into account the datasets themselves, putting already at-risk communities at a greater disadvantage.

The problem with biased datasets cannot be understated, especially in neurodegenerative research [65], where AI-driven approaches should prioritize avoiding any form of bias. The goal is to ensure benefits from scientifically based developments, and a diagnostic procedure be gentler, with increasingly efficiency to prevent these diseases one day.
