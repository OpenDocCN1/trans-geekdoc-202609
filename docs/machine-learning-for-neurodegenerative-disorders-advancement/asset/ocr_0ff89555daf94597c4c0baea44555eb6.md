# 8.2.7 HOLISTIC COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS

Conducting a comparative analysis of voting and AdaBoost models, Clark and Taylor [18] take a holistic approach to ASD prediction in their work published in the Journal of Autism Studies. Their research addresses the understanding of the comparative effectiveness of these prominent data science models. The survey discusses various data science techniques used for predicting ASD, providing a comprehensive overview of the current methodologies and their effectiveness in this field [19]. In their paper “Enhancing autism severity: A fusion of convolutional neural networks and random forest,” R. Ramya and S. Parker Arokiaraj [20] focus on improving the accuracy of autism severity prediction by combining powerful machine learning techniques: convolutional networks (CNNs) and random forests (RFs). ASD diagnosis lacks reliable medical tools, relying heavily on historical data. The proposed hybrid CNN-RF model addresses this by leveraging the strengths of both models to achieve an impressive rate of 99.15%. The data used in this study was sourced from the Kaggle repository. Key metrics like accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and kappa statistic were used to validate the model’s performance, showing significant potential for real-world healthcare applications [20].

In the article “Enhanced Autism Severity Prediction: Hybrid Boosted Tree and Deep Learning,” Ramya [21] explores a different approach by integrating boosted trees (GBTs) with deep learning models to predict autism severity. The hybrid model benefits from the great boosting technique to reduce overfitting and improve prediction accuracy. This research demonstrates that the combination of GBTs and deep learning can produce a robust model that handles complex patterns in autism diagnosis. The paper underscores the importance of combining various models to enhance prediction performance and that this hybrid approach is particularly suited for datasets with intricate and non-linear relationships. These studies are valuable contributions to the field of ASD research, providing innovative methods to enhance the accuracy and reliability of autism severity predictions, which is crucial for timely and effective intervention [21].
