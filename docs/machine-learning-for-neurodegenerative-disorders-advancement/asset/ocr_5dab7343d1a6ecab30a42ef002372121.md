# 4.5.2 AI IN PARKIN'S DISEASE RESEARCH

The application of AI in Parkinson's disease research represents a significant advance in understanding and managing this neurodegenerative disorder. Parkinson's disease, known primarily for its impact on motor control, leads to symptoms such as tremors, rigidity, and bradykinesia, which can severely impair quality of life. With their sophisticated data analysis capabilities, AI technologies are being utilized in innovative ways to advance Parkinson's disease research. The primary use of AI in researching Parkinson's disease involves analyzing symptoms, especially by employing wearable technology. Sensors collect a vast array of data on patient movement, which can be analyzed by AI algorithms to diagnose and monitor the progression of Parkinson's disease symptoms [133]. For example, AI can analyze data from accelerometers and gyroscopes in wearable devices to detect and quantify tremors, gait abnormalities, and other motor symptoms in real time. This technology allows for more objective, continuous, and precise monitoring of symptoms than traditional evaluation methods. By tracking symptom progression over time, AI-enhanced wearables help clinicians adjust treatment plans more effectively. They can also be used in clinical trials to assess the efficacy of new treatments [134].

Another area where AI is significant in Parkinson's disease research is in drug design, particularly using neural network models. Deep learning models are being employed to generate new compounds that can interact with specific proteins or pathways in Parkinson's disease. These models can be used to analyze databases of chemical structures and their pharmacological properties, enabling them to predict new molecules that could modify the disease [12]. For example, AI can be used to design molecules that target alpha synuclein, a protein that forms clumps in the brain of Parkinson patients. By accelerating the discovery of such compounds, AI is helping to speed the drug development process, bringing new treatments to clinical trials more quickly [135].

Genetic data analysis is another critical area where AI is contributing to Parkinson's disease research. Parkinson's disease has genetic and environmental components, and understanding the genetic basis of disease is crucial for developing targeted therapies. AI algorithms can analyze large-scale genetic data and identify associations between specific genetic variants and susceptibility to Parkinson's disease. This analysis can lead to the identification of new genetic targets that significantly increase the risk of developing Parkinson's disease via AI models. These models can then become targets for new drugs or help in personalizing treatment approaches based on a patient's genetic makeup [137].
