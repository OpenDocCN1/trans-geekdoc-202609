# 4.3 ADVANCEMENTS IN AI-DRIVEN DRUG DISCOVERY

The integration of AI into drug discovery has ushered in a new era in biomedicine. Figure 4.5, which shows an overview of the AI-driven drug discovery process in the context of neurodegenerative disorders, illustrates a multistage process significantly enhanced by AI technologies. In the initial phase of target identification and validation, AI algorithms shift through biological data to potential drug targets. Concurrently, ML models the involvement of specific genes or proteins in neurodegenerative diseases, streamlining this crucial initial step in drug discovery. Moving to drug design and optimization, AI-powered molecular modeling creates new candidates, and ML refines these compounds for increased efficacy and minimized toxicity. In the subsequent stage of screening and identification, AI systems rapidly explore large chemical libraries to identify potential hits, with deep learning assisting in predicting interactions between these drug candidates and biological targets.
