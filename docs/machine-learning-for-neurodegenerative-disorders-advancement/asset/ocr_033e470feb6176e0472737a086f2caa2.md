# 5.5.3 FALL RISK ASSESSMENT

In patients, FoG significantly increases the risk of falling. Machine learning algorithms can use gait data to predict when FoG will occur, allowing healthcare providers to identify high-risk situations and implement fall prevention strategies. Fall assessment algorithms can also help in the design of assistive devices and rehabilitation programs that are tailored to the specific needs of each patient [39].

# 5.5.4 LONG-TERM PROGRESSION TRACKING

Machine learning can be used to track the long-term progression of FoG by analyzing historical data. These algorithms can help predict the FoG patterns may change over time, in disease management. Tracking FoG progression also allows healthcare providers to evaluate the efficacy of treatment strategies and make necessary adjustments [40].

# 5.5.5 TELEMEDICINE AND REMOTE MONITORING

Machine learning-powered systems can help with telemedicine by allowing remote monitoring of PD patients. This approach is especially beneficial for those who limited access to healthcare facilities or prefer to receive care in the privacy of their own homes. Real-time FoG analysis via telemedicine platforms can provide patients with immediate feedback and support, promoting adherence to treatment plans [41].
