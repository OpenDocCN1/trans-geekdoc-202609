# A Unique Learning Approach

Besides the AD filter furnishing an improvised resolution of the image, the dual integration of KM and GT segmentation methods are also implemented, bringing the best clarity to the properties of the lesion. When two ML algorithms are integrated, performance is enhanced and the meta-model learns to combine the base models' predictions in the best possible way, which frequently results in superior outcomes over each model individually.

K-Means has its drawbacks, such as its trouble in clustering datasets of varying density, and also limitations, sensitivity to the choice of clusters, initial centroids, the size and shape of the characters, and the scale that and dispersion of the data [34] might impact the application and efficacy of AI learning by clustering KM. But with the integration GT methodology, the proposed serves accurately in pulling the ROI from the large class of pixels clustered by KM, overcoming its setbacks.

In the future, the proposed KM-GT methodology with its computing efficiency can be introduced in the quantum platform [35] to even more reduce the execution time and bring the accuracy of the computed reports to near to percent. This implementation would need a quantum encoder to encode the pixels from 0 to 255 qubits, which could be derived from the principle of quantum mechanics with the detection of the intracranial lesion.
