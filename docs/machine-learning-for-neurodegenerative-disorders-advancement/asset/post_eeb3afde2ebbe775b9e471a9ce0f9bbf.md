## 3.6.2 ENHANCING NLP PERFORMANCE BY INTEGRATING BIOMEDICAL ONTOLOGIES

Normative terminology and hierarchical links between concepts are provided by biomedical ontologies, which are structured representations of biomedical knowledge. The performance and accuracy of NLP activities in the biomedical domain are improved by integrating biological ontologies. This overview explains the importance of integrating biomedical ontologies into NLP, its uses, and the advantages it provides to academia and industry professionals.

- Resource Description Framework: RDF is a common format for ontology data representation that enables semantic integration with other semantic resources.
- SPARQL protocol and RDF query language: Data from RDF-encoded ontologies can be accessed and queried using SPARQL.

In summary, integrating biomedical ontologies into NLP tasks is a crucial step in raising the effectiveness and precision of NLP applications in the biomedical domain. Ontologies help NLP tasks like entity linking, relation extraction, text categorization, and information retrieval by offering standardized vocabulary, domain-specific knowledge, and semantic enrichment. Despite obstacles, improvements in semantic web technologies and ontology upkeep ensure that biomedical ontologies continue to play a crucial role in advancing NLP research and applications, enabling researchers and practitioners to extract valuable insights from biomedical text data.

## 3.7 LITERATURE-BASED DISCOVERY FOR DRUG REPURPOSING

The process of finding novel applications for licensed medications in the treatment of neurodegenerative diseases is known as "literature-based discovery." Prospective novel drug-disease associations are found by researchers through data mining and integration of reviews, clinical trials, and research publications. By using this method, research on the efficacy of repurposed medications can be designed, findings validated, and hypotheses generated. Generating therapeutic alternatives for illnesses with restricted therapies, cutting costs, and speeding up drug development are all benefits.

### 3.7.1 LEVERAGING NLP TO IDENTIFY POTENTIAL THERAPEUTIC AGENTS

In biomedical research, NLP has proven to be a useful tool for a variety of tasks, including the discovery of therapeutic compounds for various illnesses and ailments. NLP makes it possible to extract and analyze data from scientific publications, medical records, and other texts, which shed light on potential candidates and their modes of action. The importance of NLP in drug development, and its uses in discovering therapeutic molecules, the opportunities and problems in the process are highlighted in this overview.

### Importance of NLP in drug discovery

- Vast biomedical: It is difficult for researchers to manually analyze and extract pertinent data from the scientific literature due to the abundance of information on drug-target interactions, biological pathways, and disease processes.
- Knowledge discovery: NLP helps with knowledge discovery by revealing hidden connections among medications, targets, and diseases, enabling fresh ideas and drug development hypotheses.
- Time and cost efficiency: By automating the information extraction and processing, NLP speeds up the drug development process while utilizing fewer resources and time.

### Utilizing NLP to identify therapeutic agents

- Literature mining: From scientific literature, NLP is used to extract drug–target interactions, DDIs, and drug–disease connections, assisting in the discovery of novel therapeutic agents.
- Repurposing existing drugs: NLP makes it possible to repurpose current medications and expedite clinical trials by identifying targets with potential therapeutic benefits for new ailments.
- Identifying novel drug targets: By examining gene and protein interactions relevant to disease pathways, NLP aids in the identification of novel therapeutic agents.

### 3.7.1 Drug Discovery Using Knowledge Graphs

Building knowledge graphs containing relationships among drugs, targets, and diseases improves the representation of drug-related knowledge and facilitates more complex studies. The seamless integration of drug-related information from many sources is made possible by semantic technology in the analysis of healthcare records and sensitive drug-related data, ensuring responsible data usage and patient privacy.

In summary, by locating prospective therapeutic agents, repurposing already-existing medications, and locating new pharmacological targets, NLP plays a critical role in the development of new pharmaceuticals. NLP speeds up discovery and development process by extracting and analyzing pertinent data from biological literature and clinical records [26]. Despite difficulties with data quality and ambiguity, improvements in NLP methods and knowledge graph construction help improve the precision and applicability of NLP in the identification of therapeutic agents, assisting in the creation of novel and efficient treatments for a variety of diseases.

### 3.7.1.2 Extraction of Drug-Drug Interactions for Safety Treatment Methods

Drug–drug interactions happen when two or more drugs interact in a way that impairs their efficacy or safety. To protect patients and improve methods, it is essential to recognize and comprehend DDIs. The automatic extraction and analysis of DDIs from a large body of biological literature and clinical records depends heavily on NLP techniques. The importance of NLP in DDI extraction, its uses in promoting safer treatment methods, and the difficulties in the process are all discussed in this overview. Knowledge discovery and DDI representation are strengthened by including pharmacogenomics data and DDI knowledge into knowledge graphs. Finally, NLP plays a critical role in extracting DDIs from scientific sources. NLP streamlines literature review, aids pharma pharmacovigilance initiatives and interactions. It improves clinical decision-making by automating the study of large-scale medication changes to ensure increased patient safety and personalized treatment, it is important to address issues with ambiguity and data privacy. By doing so, NLP’s accuracy and utility in DDI extraction will be further enhanced.

In order to analyze and gain useful insights from unstructured text data, NLP approaches have become essential tools in biomedical research. This overview shows the value of NLP in identifying early symptoms and biomarkers, as well as its applications and difficulties.

Healthcare practitioners can benefit from early identification and individualized treatment planning by incorporating NLP into clinical decision systems. In contention, NLP is crucial in spotting early symptoms and locating potential biomarkers of neurodegenerative diseases. NLP supports early intervention, biomarker identification, and data-driven research by effectively analyzing biological literature, clinical records, and patient-reported data. ## 3.9 CHALLENGES AND ETHICAL FACTORS

Neurodegenerative disease research and treatment encounter a number of significant obstacles and moral dilemmas. The overlapping of symptoms and the lack of good biomarkers make early diagnosis of many disorders challenging. Treating the disorders is more difficult due to their complexity and variability; available treatments mainly focus on symptom management rather than disease cure. Due to this, long-term care becomes more necessary and expensive, placing a heavy burden on healthcare systems.
