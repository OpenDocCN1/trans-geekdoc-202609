# 11.3.4.1 Real-world Studies

With the increasing prevalence of combating neurodegenerative diseases, there is an urgent need for effective treatments specifically addressed to these everyday life complexities [88]. Here RWS gradually occupying a deserved place as a shipman who help steer healthcare through the abyss between household implementation and generous colors of clinical practice [77]. In summary, this section discusses the transforming capability of RWS to replace and revolutionize our understanding and management of neurodegenerative diseases.

Historically, research has been the mechanism by which treatment efficacy and safety are explored. Yet, these studies are frequently structured in settings with participants who differ greatly from the broader patient population in routine clinical practice [14]. Here, RWS come into play with non-invasive (electronic health records EHRs, wearable sensors) and patient-reported data to capture this heterogeneity of symptoms, disease progression, or treatment as it has [81].
