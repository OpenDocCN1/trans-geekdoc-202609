# 3.9.1 Reducing Bias and Fairness in Literature on NLP FOR NEUROGENERATIVE DISORDERS

Using NLP is essential for gaining insightful information from the literature on neurodegenerative disorders. NLP models can, however, be subject to biases that can result in unfair or erroneous representations of the data, like any AI-based technology. This overview focuses on the importance of tackling bias and fairness in NLP, its uses in research of neurodegenerative disorders, and the difficulties associated with the procedure.

# Literature in NLP for neurodegenerative disorders

The data used to train NLP models may be biased due to previous imbalances in the scope of the research or the methods utilized to obtain the data.

+   - Representation bias: Certain populations or subgroups of patients may be underrepresented in the literature, leading to biased model outputs.
- Language bias: Biases may arise from language patterns that favor specific demographics or cultures.

Fairness in NLP for neurodegenerative disorder literature

+   - Fairness metrics: Fairness metrics assess how well NLP models perform across different demographic groups, identifying potential disparities in predictions.
- Fairness constraints: Implementing fairness constraints during model development ensures that the model's outputs are unbiased and equitable for diverse populations.

Mitigating bias and ensuring fairness in NLP for neurodegenerative disorder literature

+   - Diverse and representative data: Improving data diversity and representation in the training to reduce bias and improve model fairness.
- Preprocessing techniques: Techniques such as debasing, reweighting, or adversarial training can be used to mitigate biases in NLP models.
- Post-hoc analysis: Conductinging post-hoc analyses to identify and correct biases in model outputs.

+   - Fairness-aware learning: Incorporating fairness-aware learning algorithms that explicitly consider fairness constraints during model training.

Ethical considerations

+   - Transparency and explainability: Making sure that NLP models can be understood and explained, allowing stakeholders to recognize and correct any biases. Getting patients' and research subjects' informed consent before using their data for NLP research.

Managing bias and fairness presents challenges

+   - Lack of diverse and representative data: It is difficult to obtain diverse representative data for training NLP models, especially in the context of neurodegenerative disorders.
