Integrating ML principles into neural network modeling enhances our ability to simulate and comprehend the intricacies of the brain mechanism, a general process, as demonstrated in Figure 1.1. Such models help researchers test hypotheses and explore the consequences of specific neural circuits, shedding light on neural disorders and potential interventions (Vempati & Sharma, 2023). ML-driven analyses of individual imaging and genomic data hold the promise of personalized medicine in neurology and psychiatry. By identifying unique brain signatures associated with disorders, ML enables treatments and interventions, minimizing trial-and-error approaches.

ML-augmented neurological technologies could usher in a new era of cognitive enhancement. BCIs might be harnessed to improve memory, learning, and decision-making, benefiting individuals with cognitive impairments and healthy individuals seeking cognitive optimization. The intertwining of ML and neuroscience brings forth ethical considerations related to informed consent, data privacy, and the potential misuse of neural information. Addressing algorithmic bias ensuring fairness in neuro-ML applications is paramount to upholding ethical standards.
