## 8.5 DISCUSSION

The results from the VO-ADA model's evaluation provide a comprehensive understanding of its performance in predicting ASD. The model demonstrated consistent and robust performance across various metrics, particularly at higher values of K in the k-fold cross-validation, indicating strong generalizability and reliability.

## 8.6 PERFORMANCE METRICS ANALYSIS

The VO-ADA model achieved high accuracy, precision, and recall, with accuracy reaching 99.50% during the testing phase for K = 10. This accuracy suggests that the model can correctly classify a significant number of instances, making it viable for ASD prediction. The precision, which measures the model's ability to correctly identify true positives, also peaked at 99.98% at K = 10, indicating that the model produces few false positives. This is in a crucial setting, where false positives lead to unnecessary stress and further testing. Recall, which measures the model's ability to identify all relevant cases (true positives), reached 97.82% at K = 10. This high recall rate is essential for ensuring that most individuals with ASD are correctly identified, minimizing the chances of missing cases, as K increased. This is a trade-off between precision and recall as the number of folds increases, highlighting the importance of carefully choosing K to balance these metrics. The kappa coefficient, a measure of agreement between predicted and actual adjustments, consistently remained high across different values of K, peaking at 0.98 during the testing phase. This indicates that the model's predictions are highly reliable and not significantly influenced by random chance. The consistently high kappa coefficient also underscores the model's ability to perform well across different subsets, showcasing its robustness.

## 8.7 MODEL EFFICIENCY AND ERROR RATE

The model's efficiency, as depicted in the training and testing phases, showed a marked improvement as K increases. The high efficiency, coupled with a low error rate, shows the VO-ADA model is both effective and efficient in handling the dataset. The minimal error rate, particularly at higher K values, further emphasizes the model's robustness and reliability in making accurate predictions.

## 8.8 IMPLICATIONS AND LIMITATIONS

The VO-ADA model's strong performance metrics imply its potential as a valuable tool for early and accurate ASD detection. Such a tool can assist clinicians in diagnosing ASD, potentially leading to earlier interventions and better outcomes for individuals. This high precision and recall are particularly important in a clinical context, ensuring that most individuals with ASD are correctly identified while minimizing false positives. However, the decrease in F1-score as K increases highlights a potential limitation. The model may become overly focused on either precision or recall, suggesting a need for further fine-tuning. Additionally, while the model performs well on the dataset, further testing on diverse populations is necessary to confirm its generalizability.

## 8.9 FUTURE WORK

Future research should focus on exploring the VO-ADA model's performance across diverse datasets to ensure its applicability in various clinical settings. Incorporating additional features or refining the feature set may also enhance its predictive capabilities. Overall, the VO-ADA model shows great promise as a tool for ASD prediction with high accuracy, precision, and reliability.

## 8.10 CONCLUSION

In conclusion, this study addresses the complexities associated with ASD by introducing a novel approach that merges the capabilities of voting and AdaBoost. The combination of voting and AdaBoost achieves an impressive accuracy of 98.60% in prediction, demonstrating the potential of this innovative method. Evaluation of the model encompasses a thorough analysis of performance metrics, including accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, and kappa coefficient from K-fold cross-validation in training and testing phases. Furthermore, the primary objective of this research extends beyond just the presence of autism among the specified group; it aims to improve the quality of life for individuals. Suggestions for future research directions include the scalability and real-time applicability of the VO-ADA model. Investigating the addition of data sources, such as genetic markers or behavioral observations, could further enhance the effectiveness and robustness.
