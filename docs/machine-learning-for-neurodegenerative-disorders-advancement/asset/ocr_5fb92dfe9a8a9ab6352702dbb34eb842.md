However, it faces challenges, such as language complexity, data quality, and the need for biological validation.

+   - Clinical decision support: The results of relation extraction can be included in clinical decision programs to help with disease diagnosis and treatment decision-making. Establishing relationships between biomarkers and pathology may be difficult.
- Complex language patterns: Biomedical literature frequently uses terminologies that are peculiar to the biomedical field and complex sentence patterns, which makes it difficult for NLP models to effectively represent relationships.
- Limited annotated data: It takes a lot of work and may be difficult to produce large, highquality annotated datasets for relation extraction model training in the biomedical field. Biomarkers and diseases may have several names or synonyms, which makes it difficult to draw the appropriate conclusions about their respective interrelations [19].
- Negation and speculation: Recognizing negation and speculative assertions is important for precise relation extraction since these modifiers might alter the meaning of the relationships.

# Relation extraction techniques

+   - Supervised learning: Using annotated datasets to train supervised machine learning models for relation extraction, such as support vector machines and deep learning models (for example, BiLSTM-CRF).
- Remote supervision: Using databases or knowledge bases that already exist to automatically labeled data for training, omitting the need for manual annotation.
- Rule-based methods: Hand-crafted rules and patterns to locate correlations based on grammatical and syntactic patterns.

# 3.3.1.1 Rating the Effectiveness of Relation Extraction

Metrics precision, recall, and F-score are used to evaluate the accuracy of relation extraction models. These metrics measure the models' capacity to accurately detect relationships while minimizing false positives and false negatives. To assess the models' generalization performance, crossvalidation and testing on external datasets are used. To sum up, relation extraction is a critical NLP task in biomedical research, specifically for identifying links between biomarkers and pathology. Relation extraction helps researchers, and assist clinical decisions by precisely extracting these relationships from clinical and biomedical literature. Despite the challenges, advancements in rule-based and machine learning techniques, coupled with the availability of high-quality annotated datasets and distant supervision methodologies, have significantly enhanced relation extraction performance in the biomedical domain, offering valuable insights into disease mechanisms and facilitating the identification of novel therapeutic targets.

Identifying and extracting events or relationships from unstructured text is in the field of NLP. Event extraction is essential in the setting of biological research and healthcare for identifying illness and symptoms from a large volume of clinical notes, patient records, and scholarly literature [20].
