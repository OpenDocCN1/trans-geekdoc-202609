# 7.4 CONCLUSION

There are numerous varieties of dementia, as Lewy body dementia (LBD), vascular dementia (VD), and AD, a neurodegenerative condition that primarily influences the elderly. A patient will be born every three seconds worldwide, according to the 2018 Global AD Report. Globally, dementia affected over 50 million people in 2018. Three times as many people will have dementia by 2050—150 million people—as are now. As a result, AD detection might aid in more effective treatment. As a result, the disease’s progression can be slowed down. Accurately identifying patients with AD can be achieved by using ML algorithms. Future AD sufferers could have a higher performance categorization thanks to advanced ML techniques. This article proposes a hybrid MCDM-based framework to predict AD through audio data. This work presented a hybrid framework for the early prediction of AD. First, our voting-based FS approach extracts the essential features. After applying the processing-first framework, this work uses genetic features. SVM-SMOTE sampling method for handling the class imbalance. Then, the voting-based FS approach extracts the essential features. Finally, a TOPSIS-based classifier has been applied to these optimal features. TOPSIS is an MCDM approach. To assess the resilience and predict how the model will perform with unknown data, the suggested framework has been tested using classification techniques. The results shows that this approach is promising in boosting accuracy.
