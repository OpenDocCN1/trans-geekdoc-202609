# Preface

In recent years, the intersection of machine learning and healthcare has revolutionized our understanding, diagnosis, and treatment of various medical conditions. Among these, neurodegenerative disorders stand as formidable challenges, affecting millions worldwide and presenting complex patterns that traditional medical approaches struggle to unravel. In this dynamic landscape, the present volume, Machine Learning for Neurodegenerative Disorders: Advancements and Applications, emerges as a beacon of hope and progress.

Neurodegenerative disorders encompass a spectrum of debilitating conditions, including Alzheimer’s disease, Parkinson’s disease, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, Huntington’s disease, and others. Despite their diverse etiologies and manifestations, these disorders share standard features of neurodegenerative dysfunction and loss, resulting in profound cognitive, motor, and psychological impairmentsments for affected individuals.

Traditional diagnostic strategies for neurodegenerative disorders often face significant limitations. Diagnosis may rely heavily on clinical observations and expensive imaging techniques, leading to delayed identification and intervention. Treatment options are frequently palliative, aiming to alleviate symptoms rather than targeting underlying mechanisms. Moreover, the heterogeneity of these disorders poses substantial challenges for personalized medicine approaches.

However, in recent years, the integration of machine learning techniques with neurology and neuroscience has revolutionized our approach to understanding and managing neurodegenerative disorders. Leveraging vast amounts of data, including clinical records, neuroimaging scans, genetic profiles, and wearable sensor data, machine learning algorithms can uncover hidden patterns, predict disease progression, and identify novel therapeutic targets.

The chapters within this volume represent a comprehensive exploration of the latest advancements and applications of machine learning in neurodegenerative disorders. Expert contributors from academia, industry, and healthcare institutions offer insights into diverse topics. As editors of this volume, we are immensely grateful to the contributors who have shared their expertise and insights, shaping this compendium into a valuable resource for researchers, clinicians, policymakers, and students. Our fervent hope is that the knowledge contained herein will not only deepen our understanding of neurodegenerative disorders but also catalyze the development of innovative, driven solutions that ultimately improve the lives of those affected by these devastating conditions.
