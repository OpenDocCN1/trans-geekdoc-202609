### 11.3.3 De-biased Datasets

The quest for effective treatments and cures for neurodegenerative diseases hinges on robust data, but data itself is a double-edged sword. Biases embedded within datasets can perpetuate inequalities and hinder progress [93]. Training sets, in which white males make up 99% of it, could potentially miss important signals in female or darker-skinned cohorts and therefore widen the gap and delay treatment [94]. Models do not work for certain demographic groups where there are biases in data collection; these biases woven into the model structure compound and lead to unfair predictions [59]. Lastly, biases such as healthcare access taken into account put already-risk communities at a greater disadvantage.

The problem with biased datasets cannot be understated, especially in neurodegenerative research [65], where AI-driven approaches should prioritize avoiding any form of bias. The goal is to ensure benefits from scientifically based developments, and diagnostic procedures become gentler with increasingly efficiency to prevent these diseases.

### 11.3.4.1 Real-world Studies

With the increasing prevalence of neurodegenerative diseases, there is an urgent need for effective treatments specifically addressed to these everyday life complexities [88]. Here RWS gradually occupy a deserved place to help steer healthcare through the abyss between implementation and clinical practice [77]. In summary, this section discusses the transforming capability of RWS to revolutionize our understanding and management of neurodegenerative diseases.

Historically, research has been the mechanism by which treatment efficacy and safety are explored. Yet, studies are frequently structured in settings with participants who differ greatly from the broader patient population in routine clinical practice [14]. Here, RWS come into play with non-invasive (electronic health records EHRs, wearable sensors) and patient-reported data to capture this heterogeneity of symptoms, disease progression, or treatment [81].

The true power of real-world data (RWD) in neurodegenerative research is not observation but revolution. Generalizable ML models that account for various demographic characteristics and disease stages, real-world challenges such as adherence and social determinants influencing outcomes [82], enables precision medicine, allowing care to be based on individual patient requirements leading the path toward more effective health services for everyone.

### 11.3.4.2 Regulatory Frameworks

Real-world studies become neurodegenerative disease game changers. It is pivotal for regulatory frameworks to be organized around these modern challenges [36]. In this section, we attend the intricate pathways through which researchers can move toward ethical and compliant strategies that will benefit. In Figure 11.10, we show the regulatory pathways and elements needed to perform an RWS in neurodegenerative diseases.

Observational and experimental trials are separate study designs, necessitating regulatory pathways between the Food and Drug Administration (FDA). For example, the appropriate safeguards for data sources such as EHRs and wearable devices are different from those required by more granular patient outcomes via smartphones. In addition, regulatory frameworks differ depending on the individual neurodegenerative disorder (or each which has its own profile and approach to science) [90]. Tracking these intersecting workflows rigorously will ensure compliant, safe, and impactful progress in this new field of research [83].

Obtaining ethical clarity, by maintaining data privacy and security (through measures the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) compliance), is another key principle that researchers should never skip to follow. Our findings show that standardized processes of informed consent are needed, in which the risks and benefits have been well-known by participants [84]. Transparency and reporting accountable research progress, through guidelines with provision of findings in open access. Incorporating these ethical standards into the weave of RWS research in turn enables meaningful advancements to occur within this important domain.

In sum, while regulation challenge the core of RWS [85], they ultimately safeguard participants and help to guarantee reasonable usage endpoints. It is possible to release the transformative potential of RWS and offer something that we did not know for individuals faced with a neurodegenerative order if these are understood, working collaborations to elaborate on improvements while radically enhancing their adaptability.

### 11.3.4.3 Cost-effectiveness Analysis

With the advent of RWs as game-changers in neurodegenerative disease research, so too does an understanding of whether they are cost-effective for sustainable and impartial advancements [62]. RWS requires substantial infrastructure, expertise and resources for a range of activities, from acquiring diverse data sources such as EHRs or wearables to design, execute & monitor studies extracting meaningful insights from complex datasets in specialized methodological areas through proficient evidence synthesis tools [75]. By identifying these barriers, researchers can plan ahead and obtain the support they need to unleash RWS' tremendous potential for expanding knowledge of and treating terrible diseases [86].
