Table 8.3 explains the performance analysis of three models—vote, AdaBoost, and their combination VO-ADA—across values of K in a predictive task. Notably, the combined consistently outperforms individual models across various metrics. At K = 10, the combined model achieves the highest accuracy of 98.60%, surpassing VO-ADA. Similarly, precision and recall also peak at K = 10 for the combined model, indicating its effectiveness in identifying positive positions. The F1-score is higher at K = 10 for the combined model, emphasizing its superior performance. The kappa coefficient, a measure of agreement between predicted and actual classifications, attains its highest value at K = 10 for the combined model. These findings that the integration of VO-ADA enhances the model’s predictive capabilities, demonstrating the value of ensemble methods in improving classification accuracy and overall model robustness.
