# 4.7.3 GOVERNMENTAL AND INSTITUTIONAL SUPPORT

With respect to the crucial role of governmental and institutional support, this support is fundamental for advancing the research, development, and application of AI in this field. Government bodies, research institutions, and healthcare organizations play key roles in providing the necessary infrastructure, funding, regulatory frameworks, and collaborative environments needed for the successful integration and advancement of AI technologies [158]. Governmental support is pivotal in fostering an environment conducive to AI-driven research. This can take the form of funding for initiatives, which are essential for supporting the extensive research and development efforts in drug discovery [159]. For example, initiatives such the BRAIN Initiative in the United States, which aims to revolutionize our understanding of the human brain, provide significant funding for research employs AI and ML. These investments can accelerate the development of AI tools and algorithms for analyzing complex neural data, potentially aiding in the discovery of new drugs and therapies for neurodegenerative diseases [160]. Moreover, governments can play a key role in establishing regulatory frameworks that support and guide the ethical and safe use of AI in healthcare. Policies and guidelines that address data privacy, patient safety, and ethical considerations in AI are necessary to build public trust and ensure that AI technologies are used responsibly and effectively in healthcare settings. For instance, the European Union's GDPR provides a framework for handling patient data, which is critical in research where large datasets are utilized [161].

Institutional support is also vital for the advancement of AI in drug discovery. Academic and research institutions foster interdisciplinary collaboration, bringing together experts in AI, neuroscience, pharmacology, and other relevant fields. These collaborations can lead to innovative approaches and solutions in drug discovery and repurposing. For example, partnerships between AI research laboratories and neurology departments can facilitate the sharing of expertise and resources, leading to more effective AI models that can predict disease progression and identify potential drug targets [162]. Additionally, healthcare organizations and hospitals provide valuable data for research, contributing to large-scale databases for testing algorithms. Collaborations with these organizations and AI researchers ensure that the algorithms are developed and tested in real-world settings, increasing their reliability and applicability in clinical environments [146]. This support also has the potential to lead to breakthroughs in treatments and therapies, ultimately improving the lives of patients with neurodegenerative disorders.
