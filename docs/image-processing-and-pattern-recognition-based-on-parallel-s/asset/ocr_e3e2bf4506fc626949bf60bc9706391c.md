Orthographic coverage gives it a unique description for most images that distinguish it from other images. However, there are cases of complex symmetric images for which the function of the area of intersection in four directions can coincide. An increase in the number of directions with an orthogonal covering does not give an accurate representation of the intersection area. To eliminate this disadvantage, other forms of coating are used. The most effective and popular form of coverage is shown in Fig. 7.3 (Belan and Motorniyuk, 2013; Nicoladie, 2014; Bilan et al., 2014, Konstantinos, 2011).
