- 1. $h_1 = a \sin \alpha$ (2.7)
- 2. $h_2 = \frac{a}{2}(\sqrt{3}\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha)$ (2.8)

- 4. $l_1 = \frac{\sqrt{3}a}{\sqrt{3}\cos \alpha - \sin \alpha}$ (2.10)

When identifying the input image as a hexagon, we can determine the length of its sides using the basic parameters of the function of the area of intersection.

$$
\begin{cases}
a = \sqrt{\frac{2S_0}{3\sqrt{3}}} \\
a = \frac{X_{\max}}{\sqrt{3}}, \text{for } \alpha = \frac{\pi}{6} \\
a = \frac{X_{\max}}{2}, \text{for } \alpha = 0
\end{cases} \tag{2.12}
$$

The presence of several sections with different functions of the area of intersection complicates the process of handling such functions. Therefore, calculation of the function of the area of intersection of a regular 2n-setsquare in the book is not considered.

We define the function of the area of intersection of simple figures. The graphic description of the process of obtaining the FAI(x) of circle is shown on Fig. 2.6. The FAI(y) of the circle is similarly determined. The functions of the area of intersection of the circle for orthogonal directions of the shift are reflected in the formulas (2.15) and (2.16).

The distance of the shift \(x = OO' = q_x R\), where \(q_x \in [0, 2]\). \(q_x\) is an auxiliary factor reflecting the relationship of the shift distance and the circle radius.

If we exclude from the formula the radius \(R = \sqrt{\frac{S_0}{\pi}}\), which is not among the basic elements of the FAI, then \(q_x = x \cdot \sqrt{\frac{\pi}{S_0}}\)

$$
\alpha = \arccos\left(\frac{q_x \cdot R}{2 \cdot R}\right) = \arccos\left(\frac{q_x}{2}\right) = \arccos\left(\frac{x \cdot \sqrt{\pi}}{2 \sqrt{S_0}}\right) \tag{2.13}
$$

$$
\alpha = \arctg\left(\frac{2 \cdot CE}{q_x \cdot R}\right) = \arctg\left(\frac{\sqrt{4 - q_x^2}}{q_x}\right) = \arctg\left(\sqrt{\frac{4 S_0}{\pi \cdot x^2} - 1}\right) \tag{2.14}
$$

\( FAI(x) = 2 \cdot \frac{S_0}{\pi} \left( arctg \left( \sqrt{\frac{4S_0}{\pi \cdot x^2} - 1} \right) \right) - x \cdot \sqrt{\frac{S_0}{\pi} - \frac{x^2}{4}} \) (2.15)

\( FAI(y) = 2 \cdot \frac{S_0}{\pi} \left( arctg \left( \sqrt{\frac{4S_0}{\pi \cdot y^2} - 1} \right) \right) - y \cdot \sqrt{\frac{S_0}{\pi} - \frac{y^2}{4}} \) (2.16)

If \( x = 0 \) (\( y = 0 \)), then \( FAI(x) = S_0 \) (\( FAI(y) = S_0 \)). When identifying the input image as a circle, determine its radius using the basic parameters of the function of the area of intersection.

\( R = \sqrt{\frac{S_0}{\pi}} = \frac{X_{\max}}{2} \) (2.17)

We define the FAI of the rectangle with sides \( a \) and \( b \) and the tilt angle \( \alpha \) for direction shift \( \varphi \) (Fig. 2.7.):

\( A = a \cdot \cos \alpha + b \cdot \sin \alpha \)
\( B = a \cdot \sin \alpha + b \cdot \cos \alpha \)

![](img/d6ed6c69a61b1252a9084096bb4904c6_35_0.png)

Fig. 2.7: Graphic description of the process obtaining FAI (x) of rectangle

\( FAI(x) = (b - x \cdot \sin \alpha)(a - x \cdot \cos \alpha) = S_0 - Bx + \frac{x^2}{2} \sin(2\alpha) \) (2.18)

\( FAI(y) = (b - y \cdot \cos \alpha)(a - y \cdot \sin \alpha) = S_0 - Ay + \frac{y^2}{2} \sin(2\alpha) \) (2.19)

where

\( A = \frac{S_0}{Y_{\max}} + \frac{Y_{\max}}{2} \cdot \sin(2\alpha) \)

$$
B = \frac{S_0}{X_{\max}} + \frac{X_{\max}}{2} \cdot \sin(2\alpha)
$$

The parameters of the bound rectangles (A and B) are used only for ease in mapping the process of obtaining the formula of the function of the area of intersection and later replaced by a function of the main parameters ($S_0$, $X_{\max}$, $Y_{\max}$).

$$
FAI(x) = (X_{\max} - x) \left( \frac{S_0}{X_{\max}} - \frac{x}{2} \sin(2\alpha) \right) \quad (2.20)
$$

$$
FAI(y) = (Y_{\max} - y) \left( \frac{S_0}{Y_{\max}} - \frac{y}{2} \sin(2\alpha) \right) \quad (2.21)
$$

Formulas (2.17)–(2.20) use the values of the angle of inclination $\alpha$ in the direction of the shift $\varphi$. Variants of the formulas of calculating this angle will be described below.

When identifying the incoming image as a rectangle, it is possible to determine the quantitative characteristics of the figure using the basic parameters of the function of the area of intersection.

$$
a = \sqrt{\frac{S_0 \cdot Y_{\max}}{X_{\max}}} \ ; \ b = \sqrt{\frac{S_0 \cdot X_{\max}}{Y_{\max}}} \quad (2.22)
$$

The function of the area of intersection of a square where $X_{\max} = Y_{\max}$:

$$
FAI(x) = (X_{\max} - x) \cdot \frac{X_{\max} \cdot S_0 - x \cdot \sqrt{S_0 \cdot (X_{\max}^2 - S_0)}}{X_{\max}^2} \quad (2.23)
$$

$$
FAI(y) = (X_{\max} - y) \cdot \frac{X_{\max} \cdot S_0 - y \cdot \sqrt{S_0 \cdot (X_{\max}^2 - S_0)}}{X_{\max}^2} \quad (2.24)
$$

We use principle of triangle similarity for a parallel shift to determine the function of the area of intersection of the triangle:

$$
FAI(x) = \frac{S_0 \cdot (X_{\max} - x)^2}{X_{\max}^2} \quad (2.25)
$$

$$
FAI(y) = \frac{S_0 \cdot (Y_{\max} - y)^2}{Y_{\max}^2} \quad (2.26)
$$

When we identify the input image of a triangle, we cannot immediately determine the quantitative characteristics of the figure, using the basic parameters of the function of the area of intersection for a pair of orthogonal shifts. This happens because there are four unknowns (three sides of the triangle and the inclination angle of one side to the horizontal). The parameters of the triangle can be determined by its rotation. It is necessary to achieve a situation where the inclination angle of one of the sides to the horizontal is zero. In this case, a large side of the triangle will be equal to the maximum horizontal displacement.

Displacement will be maximum as far as possible. Then, with two sets of basic parameters of the function of the area of intersection, we can determine the quantitative characteristics of the triangle.

A graphical representation of the process of defining one of the triangle parameters sets in Fig. 2.8 is shown.

The relationship of basic parameters of the triangle and its quantitative parameters, refer to the following set of formulas.

$$
S_0 = \frac{a \cdot X_{max} \cdot \cos \psi}{2} = \frac{b \cdot Y_{max} \cdot \cos \varphi}{2} = \frac{ab \sin \gamma}{2} \quad (2.27)
$$

$$
\frac{4S_0^2}{X_{max} Y_{max}} = X_{max} Y_{max} + 2S_0 = ab \cos \varphi \cos \psi \quad (2.28)
$$

\(\frac{a}{b} = \frac{Y_{\max} \cos \varphi}{X_{\max} \cos \psi} ; \frac{X_{\max}}{\sin \gamma} = \frac{b}{\cos \varphi} ; \frac{Y_{\max}}{a} = \frac{a}{\cos \varphi}\) (2.29)

\(b \cdot \sin \varphi = l_x ctg \psi\) (2.30)

\(Y_{\max} + l_y = a \cdot cos \psi\) (2.31)

\(X_{\max} + l_x = b \cdot cos \varphi\) (2.32)

\(a \cdot sin \psi = l_y ctg \varphi\) (2.33)

\(Y_{max}l_x = X_{max}l_y\) (2.34)

\(\frac{l_x}{X_{\max}} = \frac{l_y}{Y_{\max}} = \frac{X_{\max}}{b \cos \varphi} = \frac{Y_{\max}}{a \cos \psi} = \frac{2S_0 - X_{\max} Y_{\max}}{X_{\max} Y_{\max}}\)
\(= \frac{b \cos \varphi - X_{\max}}{X_{\max}} = \frac{a \cos \psi - Y_{\max}}{Y_{\max}}\) (2.35)

\(\frac{X_{\max} Y_{\max}}{2S_0} = 1 - tg \varphi tg \psi\) (2.36)

To determine the quantitative parameters of the triangle, we need to perform its rotation. Thus, for example, the side \(a\) is to be positioned vertically. Then \(a = Y_{max}\), \(\psi = 0\), and all the above formulas are much simplified. Sizes of the triangle sides during rotation will not change. In stock will be two sets of formulas. This makes it possible to determine the values of the triangle's sides and the original inclination angle to the horizontal \(\varphi\).

The function of the area of intersection of the rectangle depends on the inclination angle \(\alpha\) to the direction of the shift \(\varphi\). Depending on the ratio of the sizes of the rectangle (\(a\) and \(b\)), there are three possibilities of inclination \(\alpha\) angle:

\(\sin \alpha \cdot cos \alpha = \frac{S_0}{X_{\max} \cdot Y_{\max}}\) (2.37)

\(tg \alpha = \frac{X_{max}}{Y_{max}}\) (2.38)

\(\sin^2 \alpha = \frac{S_0}{X_{max} \cdot Y_{\max}}\) or \(cos^2 \alpha = \frac{S_0}{X_{max} \cdot Y_{\max}}\) (2.39)

Options \(\sin^2 \alpha\) and \(cos^2 \alpha\) are identical in further calculations of \(\sin 2\alpha\).
