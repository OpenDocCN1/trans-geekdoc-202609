$$ B = \frac{S_0}{X_{\max}} + \frac{X_{\max}}{2} \cdot \sin(2\alpha) $$

The parameters of the bound rectangles (A and B) are used only for ease in mapping the process of obtaining the formula of the function of the area of intersection and later replaced by a function of the main parameters ($S_0$, $X_{\max}$, $Y_{\max}$).

$$ FAI(x) = (X_{\max} - x) \left( \frac{S_0}{X_{\max}} - \frac{x}{2} \sin(2\alpha) \right) \quad (2.20) $$

$$ FAI(y) = (Y_{\max} - y) \left( \frac{S_0}{Y_{\max}} - \frac{y}{2} \sin(2\alpha) \right) \quad (2.21) $$

Formulas (2.17) ÷ (2.20) use the values of the angle of inclination $\alpha$ in the direction of the shift $\varphi$. Variants of the formulas of calculating this angle will be described below.

When identifying the incoming image as a rectangle, it is possible to determine the quantitative characteristics of the figure using the basic parameters of the function of the area of intersection.

$$ a = \sqrt{\frac{S_0 \cdot Y_{\max}}{X_{\max}}} \ ; \ b = \sqrt{\frac{S_0 \cdot X_{\max}}{Y_{\max}}} \quad (2.22) $$

The function of the area of intersection of a square where $X_{\max} = Y_{\max}$:

$$ FAI(x) = (X_{\max} - x) \cdot \frac{X_{\max} \cdot S_0 - x \cdot \sqrt{S_0 \cdot (X_{\max}^2 - S_0)}}{X_{\max}^2} \quad (2.23) $$

$$ FAI(y) = (X_{\max} - y) \cdot \frac{X_{\max} \cdot S_0 - y \cdot \sqrt{S_0 \cdot (X_{\max}^2 - S_0)}}{X_{\max}^2} \quad (2.24) $$

We use principle of triangle similarity for a parallel shift to determine the function of the area of intersection of the triangle:

$$ FAI(x) = \frac{S_0 \cdot (X_{\max} - x)^2}{X_{\max}^2} \quad (2.25) $$

$$ FAI(y) = \frac{S_0 \cdot (Y_{\max} - y)^2}{Y_{\max}^2} \quad (2.26) $$
