# ABSTRACT

As an important information resource, patients’ medical data can not only help them to monitor their physical conditions and check the hidden risks of diseases;it an also provide strong evidence for doctor-patient disputes and promote the development of medical field.However, most medical institutions use centralized servers to centrally manage patients’ electronic medical data.Such centralized servers have opaque data storage and are prone to suffer from single point of failure,leading to easy tampering or even deletion of medical data.At the same time, different standards of data management among medical institutions make it difficult to circulate and share data among institutions.The purpose of this paper is to realize efficient data sharing and patient access control of medical data under the condition of guaranteeing the safe storage of bulk medical data.Combining decentralized blockchain technology with distributed file storage platform IPFS, a federated medical blockchain data storage access model based on IPFS is proposed.The model uses the multi-privilege attribute-base encryption algorithm (MA-ABE) to encrypt the data to ensure the safety of the patient’s medical data during transmission and the patient’s ability to control their own medical data.Meanwhile the private IPFS network is built to improve the upload and download rate of encrypted data.This paper introduces the components and principles of the model and elaborates the flow of data upload and access.In the mean time, evaluate the performance of the model, summarize the advantages and disadvantages of the model and the next research of the paper.

# 1 INTRODUCTION

This kind of problem often occurs in life. When patients go to the hospital,if the doctor wants to know the patient’s previous medical records, he needs to spend time reading multiple medical records issued by different hospitals. Due to the disadvantages of paper medical records, which are easy to be lost and damaged, it is difficult to obtain complete medical records of patients in most cases.Along with the development of the Internet and big data technology, informationization has gradually toward the medical data on track, but similar to the Electronic medical record system (Electronic medical records, EMR), Electronic health record system (Electronic health records, EHR), and Personal health record (the Personal health records, RHR) this kind of centralized medical data sharing platform, most of the management of medical data of the third party, not only easy to encountering malicious attacks,Data is tampered, hidden, lost, unable to ensure the security and integrity of the data;At the same time, due to the differences in management and archiving habits, data sharing among different organizations becomes a problem.Therefore, there is an urgent need for a tamper-proof, safe, efficient, transparent and reliable technology to solve the current problems in the medical industry.Blockchain, as the most concerned emerging technology at present, has become the preferred solution.
