**Neurons**: Signals go into the neuron and a few things happen.

+   A. First thing and the **first step** is corresponding weights of variables will be added together i.e. the **weighted sum** of all of the **input values.**
B. Secondly an **activation function** is applied to this **weighted sum.**
- Basically an *activation function* is a function that is assigned to a *neuron* or to this *whole layer* and it is applied to its weighted sum.
- The **activation function** decides if it **needs to pass on a signal** to the **next neuron** (or layer), i.e. depending on the **activation function** the *Neuron* will either pass on a signal it or won't pass the signal on.
C. And that's exactly what happened in **step three**. The **neuron** passes on that **signal** to the **next neuron** down the line.
