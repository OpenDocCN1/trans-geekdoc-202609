# Variation no. 3: Connected Forget-valve and Memory-valve with pointwise-operator.

Instead of having a separate decision for the memory-valve, now you have a combined decision for the forget-valve and the memory-valve.

Whenever you add something into memory, whenever you close the forget gate off this is 0, memory-valve becomes the opposite i.e. it opened. 1 pointwise-operator turns 0 into 1. So it makes sense to combine them.

# Variation no. 3: A very popular modification called Gated Recurring Units, GRUs, for.

It completely get rid of the C-pipeline (cell-pipeline or Memory-Pipeline), and replace it with the B-pipeline (h1, the Output-pipeline), which is the hidden-pipeline which we had before at the bottom.

It simplifies things, bit less flexible, but in terms of how many things are being controlled and monitored. It might look a bit more convoluted, but in reality it is a bit simpler.

You only have 3 valves, two are connected as well.

The constant behind it is to get rid of the memory cell (memory-pipeline) and just have this one pipeline to take care of everything.
