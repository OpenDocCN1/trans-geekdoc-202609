Here we are to see a 2D grid, with all the **final winning nodes**. For each of these winning nodes we will get the **MID**: the *Mean Inter-distance*.

**MID** of a specific winning node is the *mean* of the *distances* of all the neurons around the winning node inside a *neighborhood*. Neighborhood is defined by setting *sigma=1.0* is the radius of the neighborhood.

Higher the **MID**, the more the winning node will be **far** from its **neighbors**.

Therefore the **higher** the **MID** the **winning node** is an **outlier**.

Since the majority of *winning nodes* respect the *general rules* (of the bank). We will detect the *outliers/frauds* that are far from this majority, they far from the general rules.

For each *neuron* we will get the **MID**, so we will need to take the **winning nodes** that have the **highest MID**.

To visualize the result using **colors**. The winning nodes will be colored by different *colors* in such a way that the larger is the **MID**, the closer to *white* the color will be.
