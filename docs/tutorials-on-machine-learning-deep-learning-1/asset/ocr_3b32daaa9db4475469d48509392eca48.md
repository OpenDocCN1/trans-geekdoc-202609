```
Algorithm 2. k-step parallel tempering with M temperatures
**Input:** RBM $(V_1,\dots,V_m,H_1,\dots,H_n)$, training batch $S$, current state $v$ of Markov chain with stationary distribution $p_r$ for $r=1,\dots,M$
**Output:** gradient approximation $\Delta v_i$, $\Delta b_j$, $\Delta c_i$ for $i=1,\dots,n$
$j=1,\dots,n$
1 init $\Delta v_i = \Delta b_j = \Delta c_i = 0$ for $i=1,\dots,n, j=1,\dots,n$
2 for each $v \in S$ do
3 for $r=1,\dots,M$ do
4 $v^{(0)} \leftarrow v$
5 for $i=1,\dots,n$ sample $h_{v,i}^{(r)} \sim p(h_{r,i}|v^{(0)})$
6 for $t=0,\dots,k$ do
7 for $j=1,\dots,n$ sample $v_{r,j}^{(t+1)} \sim p(v_{r,j}|h_r^{(t)})$
8 for $i=1,\dots,n$ sample $h_{r,i}^{(t+1)} \sim p(h_{r,i}|v^{(t+1)})$
9 $*$ swapping order works well in practice [26]
10 for $r \in \{2 \leq s \leq M \text{ and } s \pmod 2 \neq 0\}$ do
11 swap $(v^{(r)}, h_r^{(k)})$ and $(v^{(s)}, h_s^{(k)})$ with probability given by (40)
12 for $r \in \{3 \leq s \leq M \text{ and } s \pmod 2 \neq 1\}$ do
13 swap $(v^{(r)}, h_r^{(k)})$ and $(v^{(s)}, h_s^{(k)})$ with probability given by (40)
14 for $i=1,\dots,n, j=1,\dots,n$ do
15 $\Delta w_{ij} \leftarrow \Delta w_{ij} + v_j - p(H_i=1)v - p(H_i=1|v_{i,j}^{(k)})$
16 $\Delta b_j \leftarrow \Delta b_j + v_j - v_{i,j}^{(k)}$
17 $\Delta c_i \leftarrow \Delta c_i + p(H_i=1)v - p(1|v_i^{(k)})$
```

In each step of the algorithm, we run $k$ (usually $k=1$) Gibbs sampling steps in each tempered Markov chain yielding samples $(v_1, h_1), \dots, (v_M, h_M)$. After this, two neighboring Gibbs chains with temperatures $T_r$ and $T_{r-1}$ may exchange particles $(v_r, h_r)$ and $(v_{r-1}, h_{r-1})$ with an exchange probability based on the Metropolis ratio,

> $\min \left\{1, \frac{p_r(v_{r-1}, h_{r-1})p_{r-1}(v_r, h_r)}{p_r(v_r, h_r)p_{r-1}(v_{r-1}, h_{r-1})}\right\\}$ (39)

which gives for RBM

> $\min \left\{1, \exp \left(-\frac{T_r}{T_{r-1}} * (E(v_r, h_r) - E(v_{r-1}, h_{r-1}))\right)\}\right\}$ (40)

After performing these swaps between chains, which enlarge the mixing rate, we take the next (eventually exchanged) sample $v_1$ of original chain (with temperature $T_1=1$) as a sample from the model distribution.
