Setting radius (how grouping happens): In the next step we set a radius around BMU, and every single point (output-node) of our SOM that falls inside that radius is going to have its weight updated to be closer to that row we matched up with.

The closer to the BMU, the heavier are the weights being updated. Spots near BMU are going to be updated the most, weights far from BMU are going to be updated less.

![](bbox=[0.145, 0.113, 0.44, 0.303])

![](bbox=[0.555, 0.113, 0.852, 0.303])

Think it as the nodes are dragging each other. Whole structure near the BMU is slowly pulled towards the same direction.

The closer nodes are to this BMU, the harder they will get pulled towards that row (input) that the BMU matched up.

So that's how the radius concept works.

How nodes fall into different groups: Now let's have a look at 2nd-row (2nd-input). Let's say 2nd-row has its BMU at the Blue-Colored node.

Here this BMU drags all the nodes that are close to it that fall into BMU's radius.

![](bbox=[0.105, 0.675, 0.892, 0.855])

For above two BMUs the nodes closer to Green BMU falls in 'Green-group' and nodes are closer to Blue BMU falls in 'Blue-group'. It's pretty simple.

For example, a node far away from the green BMU, is close to the blue BMU, it is pulled much harder with the blue BMU, and therefore it becomes like this BMU.

So now we've got some idea about how this SOM works.
