## Why we use Cross Entropy over MSE.

Several advantages of Cross Entropy over MSE. For instance:

- If your output value is too small at the very start of your back propagation The Gradient in your very START Gradient-Descent will be small and it won't be enough for NN to start adjusting the weights and propagating in the right direction.
- In case of Cross Entropy, there is logarithm in it. It helps the NNs to work with a very small error to adjust Weights and propagating in the right direction
- i.e. for a little error like 0.000001, NN will adjust weights, and NNs will detect very small numbers. Using Soft-Function is better because it is "logarithmic" in the Cross-Entropy.
- Actually Cross Entropy will improve your network get to the optimal state much accurately than MSE. In MSE this will be very low. In case MSE won't guide your gradient boosting process or back propagation in the right direction.
- Even if MSE the NN goes into right direction, it'll be like a very slow since it not enough power. But if you Cross-Entropy, even understand that there are very small adjustments and tiny changes in absolute terms, but in relative terms its a huge difference because we use Softmax.

NOTE: Important: It is important to note that, CROSS ENTROPY only the preferred method for classification. But we deal with Regression which we had in ANN then MSE is a Better option.

Cross entropy is better for classification and MSE is better for regression.
