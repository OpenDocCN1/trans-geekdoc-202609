Here we are to see a 2D grid, with all the final winning nodes. For each of these winning nodes we will get the MID: the Mean Inter-distance.

**MID** of a specific winning node is the mean of the distances of all the neurons around the winning node inside a neighborhood. Neighborhood is defined by setting sigma=1.0 which is the radius of the neighborhood.

Higher the MID, the more the winning node will be far from its neighbors. Therefore the higher the MID, the winning node is an outlier.

Since the majority of winning nodes respect the general rules (of the bank). We will detect the outliers/frauds that are far from this majority, they are far from the general rules.
