## Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBM)

### 14.2.1 Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBM) Introduction
And we are going to see how Restricted Boltzmann Machines (RBM) learns, and how it is applied in practice. Here we've got standard Boltzmann machine, we've got all of these intra connections. In theory this is a great model and we can solve lots of different problems. But in practice it's hard to implement; the reason is as you increase number of nodes, the number of connections between them grows exponentially.

- Therefore, a different type of architecture was proposed which is called Restricted Boltzmann Machine (RBM).
- Here in RBM, we've got exactly the same restriction that Hidden nodes cannot connect to each other, Visible nodes cannot connect to each other. We've also got undirected connections.

### 14.2.2 How a RBM trained
Now we are going to talk how RBM works, how it's trained and how it's applied in practice. For example, we use RBM recommendation system. Let's say our RBM is going to be working on movies. Remember, Boltzmann machine is a generative type of model it constantly generates states. By training data through a process called contrast divergence, the Boltzmann machine becomes a representation of our system. We make the recommendation system that is associated with our specific set of movies that we are feeding into this system. Through that process, RBM is going to learn how to allocate hidden nodes to certain features.

(img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb7905a3_1)

This process is very similar to what we did in CNN. For example, through training process, RBM might identify some genres A, B, C, D and E. But the important thing to understand here is that RBM doesn't know about those genres. Identifying features. Actually it doesn't have to, for example identifying genre A and B are important for recommendation system but there can be certain features such as an actor or director. Hence genes could be class A or B and might have liked (e.g., Action or Drama) or Actors (Tim Robbins or Morgan Freeman) or Awards (Oscars or Golden Globe) or Directors (Tarantino).

### 14.2.3 Trained RBM in action
Consider we trained our RBM, it is to pick out certain features based on data of thousands of users and their ratings. We are going to look at specific features, let's say Drama or Action, Leonardo DiCaprio as the actor, as Award (whether or not he has an Oscar for Best Picture), and Quentin Tarantino as director. These named features are just for our learning purpose. In reality, the RBM has no idea about those movies, Genres, or Directors. It's just picking out a feature. Let's look at a couple of movies. We're going to put a new row into the RBM recommendation system and we are going to see here we predict if a person will like certain movies or not. We've got The Matrix, The Fight Club, Forest Gump, Pulp Fiction, Titanic and The Departed. Now we're trying to make a recommendation for genres following:
- The Matrix: Seen & Didn't like it (0)
- Fight Club: Didn't see the Fight Club.
- Forest Gump: Seen and liked it (1)
- Pulp Fiction: Seen & Didn't like it (0)
- Titanic: Seen and liked it (0)
- The Departed: Haven't seen that movie

Now since the user hasn't seen Fight Club and The Departed, RBM will predict that the user will like those movies or not, so we can recommend movies to them. We're going through that step by step to see how RBM takes decisions. We're going to assess which of these nodes (features) are going to activate for this specific user:
- As the CNN analysis.
- Same thing here we're feeding a row into our RBM and certain features are going to light up if they are present in this user's tastes, preferences, and biases.

- **Drama**: Forest Gump, Titanic and The Departed are Drama.
- (We don't have rating data for The Departed, RBM can only learn from the other two.)
- Since this person liked Forest Gump and Titanic, based on that node is going to light up. Symbolically green means it is activated and means that this person likes Drama movies.
- **Action**: The Action movies we have here are The Matrix, Fight Club, Pulp Fiction and The Departed (it is also Drama).
- We have four Action movies but out of them we only have rating data for The Matrix and Pulp Fiction, and of both, this person didn't like, so it's gonna light up in Red.
- **Best Actor**: Leonardo DiCaprio is present in Titanic and The Departed.
- We only have rating data for Titanic and the user liked it. So the DiCaprio node is going to light up green.
- **Oscar**: Here we've got three Oscar movies. We only have rating data for Forest Gump and Titanic. The person liked both. The Oscar-node is gonna just light up green.
- **Tarantino**: The only Tarantino movie we have here is Pulp Fiction. The person did not like it. Therefore this Tarantino-node is gonna light up in Red.
