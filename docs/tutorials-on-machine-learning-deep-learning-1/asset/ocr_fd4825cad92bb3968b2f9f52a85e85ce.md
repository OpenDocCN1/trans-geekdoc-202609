Removing Non-significant Words: We are going to remove all the words like: that, and in, all the articles the
propositions. For example in "Loved this place". Loved is the important word that indicates this positive, but the
word "this" is not important because it occur in negative review as: this place sucks!!

Our goal is to reduce this sparsity in the sparse matrix.
