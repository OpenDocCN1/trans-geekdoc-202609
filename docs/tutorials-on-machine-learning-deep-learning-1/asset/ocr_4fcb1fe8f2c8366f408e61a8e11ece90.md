+   - What is the Entropy and how does it work in Decision Trees?

+   The Entropy measures the **disorder in a set**, here in a part resulting from a **split**. The **more homogeneous** your data is in a part, the **lower will be the entropy**. The more splits, the more you have to find parts in which your data is homogeneous, and therefore the lower will be the entropy (close to 0) in these parts. However you might still find some nodes where the data is not homogeneous and therefore the **entropy would not be small.**
