Anything below this line (and down part of the s-curve) will be projected downwards onto the zero line. Similarly for the upper portion, it is projected onto the $y = 1$.

- i.e. if your predicted probability of taking up this offer is less than 50% then you just fall to $y = 0$, i.e. you are not going to take the offer. So we are deviating to "yes" or "no" (0 or 1), (instead of continuous [0,1] interval)
