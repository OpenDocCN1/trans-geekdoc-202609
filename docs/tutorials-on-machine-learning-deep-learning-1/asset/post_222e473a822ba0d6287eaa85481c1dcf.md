
### 9.3.5 Fully-Connected-Layers Work

Let's look at a more realistic example. Here we've got an ANN in the **Inputs** that:

- five attributes on the Inputs that we have in the first *hidden-layer* [fully-connected-layer/] we have six neurons,
- in the second fully connected layer we have eight neurons
- then we have two outputs, one for Dog and one for Cat.

![](img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb79055a3_286_0.png)

**Why do we have two outputs here?** Before in ANN we used to have one output.

Note that, **one output** is for kind of when you're predicting a *numerical value* and running a *regression type of problem*.

But when we're doing classification, we have more than **two categories** for instance **dogs, cats and birds**; then you have to use a *neuron* for *each category* and that's why we're going to *practice* with **two categories**.

![](img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb79055a3_286_1.png)

So we've already done the *convolution*, *pooling* and *flattening* and now the information is gonna go through the ANN.

- a) At the first step the *convolution*, *pooling* and *flattening* happens and the information going through the ANN and a *prediction* is made.

- b) And for instance let's say, it predicted 80%, given image is a *dog's* image. But it turns out that actually it is a *Cat*.

- c) Then an error is calculated. *Cost function* in ANN, where we used to calculate *mean square error*. In CNN it's called a *Loss Function* and we use a *Cross Entropy Function* (that we will talk *Cross Entropy Function* later).

> *This Loss Function* tells us how well our network is performing and we're trying to optimize or minimize that *Loss Function* to optimize our Network.

d) After the error is calculated, it's Back Propagated through the network just like ANN and couple of things are adjusted:

- 1. The weights in the ANN Synapses (connected lines)
- 2. The feature detectors (filters) are also adjusted (those little 3x3 matrices that had in convolution step). it updates the features if looking for the wrong features. So next time the NN gets improved. Done with GD (or SGD) and Back-propagation.

![](img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb79055a3_287_0.png)

e) After Adjusting the weights and feature detectors the forward-propagation happens again. Then calculation and after that again Back-propagation.

This processes are going until our CNN is optimized and the network gets trained on data.

> Important: That same gate goes through the whole Network from the very start (i convolution, pooling and flattening again) to the very end.

- So same thing as ANN a bit longer because of the first three convolution, pooling, and flattening.

## How do these classes/output neurons work: Let's see how this image classification works.

Let's start with the top output-neuron (indicates Dog) the weights of the Synapses that are connected to Dog. These neurons can be absolutely anything we are just doing this for learning purpose. So that, we can say which of the previous layer neurons are actually important for the Dog.

- So let we've got above numbers in our previous (final) connected layer. They can be numbers but for argument's sake we are assuming these numbers between 0 and 1 in [0,1] literal.

- Neurons final- fully connected layer are actually finding the features. And means that neuron didn't find a feature it is looking for. so because at the end of the day these neurons like anything else on this left side.

- Even Though neurons are already processed, still they're detecting a certain feature or a combination of features on the image.

For example following glowing neurons are for features of dog nose, ears and eyebrows. These neurons do indeed fire up when the feature belongs to a dog.

![](img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb79055a3_287_1.png)

![](img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb79055a3_288_0.png)

On the other hand the **Cat neuron** will know that it's not a **Cat**. Even though it got *floppy ears* (one neuron with **J**) and **Cat neuron** will ignore this neuron.

Through lots and lots of iterations if this happens. The **Cat neuron** will ignore this neuron more (Synapse with **Cat-Neuron will be weak**. **Dog-neuron** will pick up those **3 neurons**. It will start attributing higher *importance/weight* to those *3 neuron*.

Hence we're going to say that **Dog neuron** learned these three neurons (eyebrow and big nose and *floppy ear neuron*) contribute to the *classification* of **Dog** and **Cat** through this iterative process with many samples *epochs*.

**Important:** One thing to note that the **signals** from **3-neurons** are actually going to **Cat-neuron** and **Dog-neuron** simultaneously.

Remember, these **ears** and **nose** and **eyebrows** are those very approximate (because of going through *Convolution. Pooling. Flattening*) or unrecognizable what they're looking for at the same time it is *something in the features of **Dog**.

![](img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb79055a3_288_1.png)

Now let's move on to Cat-neuron. But those 3-Synapses (how we've sorted out the dog using these weights) of **eyebrow neuron** and **big nose neuron** and **floppy ear neuron** are remembered.

Now how is the **Cat-neuron works?**
Well whether it is actually a cat, there will be *different values/numbers for the last fully-connected-layer-neurons* for different neurons. Here's consider again *3 different neurons*.

Notice these three neurons. **0.9, 0.9 and 1. They're interacting** to both the **Dog** and the **Cat** that the the **signals** from those 3-neurons are actually going to **Cat-Neuron** and **Dog-Neuron** simultaneously.

**Important:** This is again important to remember that those output signal goes both **Cat-neuron** and **Dog-Neuron**.

Remember; It's up to the Cat-Neuron and Dog-Neuron to decide whether take into account signal and learn from it or not (using synapses).

And Cat-Neuron and Dog-Neuron analyze the given photo/image of Cat/Dog.

- So Dog-Neuron will recognize Cats whiskers and Cats pointy triangle ears and Cats vertical eye pupil. It will recognize every time these neurons fired up it is not Dog.
- On the other hand the Cat-Neuron recognize these three neurons (whiskers, pointy triangle ears, vertical eye pupal) because most of the time these lights matches up Cat-Neuron 's expectation for Cat.
- Cat-Neuron is going to listen to these three neurons more. So basically it's listening to these three and it's ignoring the other few.

And so how these finals (Cat-Neuron and Dog-Neuron) learn: "which neurons in the Final Connected Layer to listen"
- That's how the features are propagated through the network and connected to the output.

- In reality these features don't have much meaning to them like floppy ears but they do have some distinctive feature of that class.
- That's how the network is trained during the back propagation, we're not just adjust the weights but also the feature-detectors.
- If a feature is useless it's going to be disregarded and replaced with feature which is useful because that happens through thousands of iterations.

So at the end in this Fully Connected Layer of neurons has of features from the image that are indeed representative or descriptive of dog and cats.

- How prediction/recognition occurs: Once our network is trained up, let's pass on an image of a Dog. The values are propagated through a network get certain values.
- Dog and the Cat Neuron don't know that it is a dog or a Cat. But they have learned to listen to what is being shown here. Dog-Neuron listens to these three neurons (Purple Synapse) a Cat-Neuron listens to three other neuron (Green Synapse) for features.

- Purple weights are how the Dog neuron views their votes. How importance it assigns to these neurons and those votes. And Green weights are how much importance the Cat's neuron.

So when the Dog neuron finds its corresponding feature neurons pretty high then gives it a high probability that 'green image is probability dog'.

And Cat finds its corresponding feature neurons very low. Then it gives it a low probability that 'image is a Cat'.

And that's how we get our prediction. The answer is: Dog. NN is voted high for a Dog. Voting is a term that is used for neurons in the Final Fully Connected Layer.

Same thing happens when you pass an Image Of A Cat.
