# ## 2.5.1 Decision Tree

+   - CART (Classification and Regression Trees): Decision Trees are divided into Classification and Regression Trees.

- Classification trees, as the name implies to separate the dataset into classes belonging to the response variable.

**How and where the splits are determined by the algorithm.** And it is related to the Information Entropy. When we perform split into our data (create a leaf), the amount of information increases (actually adding some value when we group our points). The algorithm finding the optimal splits of our data set into leaves.

+   - The algorithm knows when to stop and when there is a certain minimum information that needs to be added.
- And at certain stage the algorithm cannot add any more information to our set-up by splitting these leaves, then it stops. A value is what determines when to stop (for example less than 5% of your total points in leaf and then leaf would be created).

So there are different variations and different options. The most important thing is when the splits are happening.

+   - The final leaves are called terminal leaves.
