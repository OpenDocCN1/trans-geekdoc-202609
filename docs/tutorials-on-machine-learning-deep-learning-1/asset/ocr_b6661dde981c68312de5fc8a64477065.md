# 8.1.1 History of Deep Learning

Deep Learning was invented in the 60s and 70s, in the 80s so people are talking about them a lot. But that trend became slow and died during the following decades. The reason was, the idea was not clear and technology wasn't ready.

From 2000 to 2020, the storage capacity became large enough and the processing power of the computers increased. That's why this Deep Learning became popular in the recent years.

And in deep learning to work properly, you need two things: a lot of data and processing power (strong computers to process that data).
