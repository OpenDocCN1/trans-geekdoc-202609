# 7.4.1 Clean the Data

Next step is to clean the different reviews. This is a compulsory step in natural language processing (NLP) which consists of cleaning the text to make it ready for our future machine learning algorithms.

+   - We have to include this in the review we will create a bag of words model or bag of words representation and this will consist of getting only the relevant words and the different reviews.
- That means we will get rid of all the unnecessary words. Eg: The, on, and etc these are not relevant words which are the words that will help the machine learning algorithm to predict if the review is positive or negative.
- We will also get rid of the punctuation like "-".

+   - Numbers: We will get rid of numbers which can have a significant impact.
- Stemming: We will also do stemming which consists of taking the roots of some different versions of the same word.
- For example if we have a 'Loved' we will use past tense of the verb love and so stemming on this word will consist of only taking love here because we have have loved or love that gives the same hints on whether the review is positive or negative.
- We need to use stemming not to have too many words in the end. And to regroup the same versions of a word like love and love or even loving into a same word love.

Capitals: We will also have the reviews in lower case.

Tokenization: Once all the reviews are cleaned, we will proceed to the last step of creating our Bag Of Words Model which is the tokenization process.

+   - It splits all the reviews into different words (only the relevant words we do text pre-processing).
- And then we take all the words of the different reviews and will create one column for each word (we'll have a lot of columns).

And for each review, each column will contain the number of times the associated word appears in the review. We'll have a lot of '0's because each review word doesn't appear in the review and we'll have a couple of '1's for words that appear once/twice in the review.

We will get sparse matrix because we get a lot of zeros in the sparse matrix for all the reviews most of the words will not be in the reviews so we will have to do something about it. That will be the last step of our creation of bag of words.
