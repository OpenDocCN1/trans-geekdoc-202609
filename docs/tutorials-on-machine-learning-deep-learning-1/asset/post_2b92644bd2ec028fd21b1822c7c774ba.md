The Multi-Armed Bandit problem: Assume you have got five of these machines. You want to play them to maximize your return from the number of games that you can actually play. You decided how many times you're going to play, 10 times, 100 times or 1000 times.

How do you figure out which ones of them to play to maximize your returns.

The assumption here is: Each of these machines has a distribution herein (So there's a distribution of numbers for outcomes. You pull the trigger and it picks out randomly of its distributed numbers).

- [1] Each machine has different distribution. Sometimes it can be similar in some of the machines but by default they are different.

- [2] You don't know this distribution.

- [3] Your goal is to figure out which of these distributions are the best for you. With minimum try you have to figure out the best distribution.

REGRET: Basically regret is when you are using a non-optimal machine.

- So whenever you explore the non-optimal machine you have a regret. Which can be quantified as the difference between the best outcome and the non-best outcome. The longer you explore other non-optimal machines the higher REGRET.

- But at the same time if you don't explore for long, then a sub-optimal machine might appear as an optimal machine for you. If you don't get the best machine (real optimal).

- For instance: if we don't spend enough exploring we might find that the green-colored distribution is the best machine because it's quite a good (but not as good as orange one).

So your goal is to find the distribution at the same time to minimize the regret.
