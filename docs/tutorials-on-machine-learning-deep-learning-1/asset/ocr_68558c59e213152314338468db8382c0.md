Remember; It's up to the Cat-Neuron and Dog-Neuron to decide whether take into account signal and learn from it or not (using synapses).

And Cat-Neuron and Dog-Neuron analyze the given photo/image of Cat/Dog.

+   - So Dog-Neuron will recognize Cats whiskers and Cats pointy triangle ears and Cats vertical eye pupil. It will recognize every time these neurons fired up it is not Dog
- On the other hand the Cat-Neuron recognize these three neurons (whiskers, pointy triangle ears, vertical eye pupal) because most of the time these lights matches up Cat-Neuron 's expectation for Cat.
- Cat-Neuron is going to listen to these three neurons more than. So basically it's listening to these three and it's ignoring the other few.

And so how these finals (Cat-Neuron and Dog-Neuron) learn: "which neurons in the Final Fully Connected Layer to listen"
- That's how the features are propagated through the network and connected to the output.

+   - In reality these features don't have much meaning to them like floppy ears or whiskers but they do have some distinctive feature of that specific class.
- That's how the network is trained during the back propagation, we're not just adjust the weights but also the feature-detectors.
- If a feature is useless it's going to be disregarded and replaced with feature which is useful because that happens through thousands and thousands of iterations.

So at the end in this Fully Connected Layer of neurons has of features or combinations of features from the image that are indeed representative or descriptive of dog and cats.

+   - How prediction/recognition occurs: Once our network is trained up, let's pass on an image of a Dog. The values are propagated through a network we get certain values.
- Dog and the Cat Neuron don't know that it is a dog or a Cat. But they have learned to listen to what is being shown here. Dog-Neuron listens to these three neurons (Purple Synapse) a Cat-Neuron listens to three other neuron (Green Synapse) for features.

+   - Purple weights are how the Dog neuron views their votes. How importance it assigns to these neurons and those votes. And Green weights are how much importance the Cat's neuron.

So when the Dog neuron finds its corresponding feature neurons pretty high then gives it a high probability that 'green image is probability dog'.

And Cat finds its corresponding feature neurons very low. Then it gives it a low probability that 'image is a Cat'.

And that's how we get our prediction. The answer is: Dog. NN is voted high for a Dog. Voting is a term that is used for neurons in the Final Fully Connected Layer. The corresponding weights are the importance of their vote.

Same thing happens when you pass an Image Of A Cat.
