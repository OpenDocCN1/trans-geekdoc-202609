**What can we do if this logistic regression?**
We use it to **predict probabilities** for sure that something will will or not happen. Actually we predict probability. The probability here is called $\hat{p}$ (p hat) (anything you see in the ^ in the section means that it's we predicting.

![](bbox=[0.288, 0.417, 0.696, 0.573])

**How to predict:** Let's take some random values 20, 30, 40, 50 ages for the independent variable X. We first project those values to the s-shaped curve and then from the curve we again project to the y-axis (or $\hat{p}$ axis). The projected points on the $\hat{p}$ axis are the corresponding probabilities.

![](bbox=[0.238, 0.659, 0.746, 0.866])

+   The person who's 20 years old the probability of taking this off is very low 0.7%, 30 years has 23%, 40 years has 85%, and 50 years has 99.4%.
