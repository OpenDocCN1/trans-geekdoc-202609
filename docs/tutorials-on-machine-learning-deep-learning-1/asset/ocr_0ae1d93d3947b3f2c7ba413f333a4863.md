# 9.1.1 Convolutional-Neural-Networks (CNN)

Look at this image. Do you see a person looking at you or looking to the right. Here your brain is struggling to adjust. If you look to the right side of the image you'll see a person looking to the right. If you look at the left side of the image you'll see a person looking at you.

This proves that we see things is actually its **features**. Depending on the features our brain process.

+   * So you look to the **right side of the image** you see certain **features** of a **person looking to right** because they're closer to your center of focus and therefore your brain **classifies it** as a person looking to the right.

+   * When you look to the **left side of the image** you see more **features** of a **person looking at you** and therefore your brain classifies it as such.
