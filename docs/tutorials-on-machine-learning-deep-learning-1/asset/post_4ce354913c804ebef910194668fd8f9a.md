## So in a summary:

- We have an energy curve and the shape of this energy curve, is governed by the weights in the system.
- We also know the RBM will always get to the minimal energy state. Minimal energy state found through the Gibbs sampling process;
- Gibbs sampling process: when RBM feeds inputs, the hidden values then reconstruct the inputs it feeds again from reconstructed inputs and the process goes on reconstructed inputs converges to some values (at lowest energy state);
- We then redesign the system by redesigning the energy curve. We adjust the weights in such a way that we input our values, the system is already going to be in the lowest energy possible.
- And for that we use this Gradient Formula.

```
$\log p(v^0)_{w_{ij}} = <v_i^0 h_j^0> - <v_i^\infty h_j^\infty>$
```

- i. We start with random initialize weights,
ii. We input a value like one of our rows in the RBM.
iii. We go through this process of Gibbs sampling, we calculate $<v_i^0 h_j^0> - <v_i^\infty h_j^\infty>$, find out how to adjust our curve.
iv. On top of all of that, the Hinton's shortcut, we don't have to go to the very end $<v_i^\infty h_j^\infty>$ of the sampling process;
- We can do two passes, we go first pass, second pass, we do a CD-1, Contrastive Divergence one, and that will tell us how to adjust the curve.

We are trying to adjust the energy curve by modifying the weights, in order to create a system which in the best way possible represents our output, our training values and what we are doing, by using gradient formula.

## 4.3.4 Deep Belief Network (DBN)

DBN: A Deep Belief Network (DBN) is a stack (on top of each other) of several RBMs. Where the 1st RBM's hidden nodes are the input nodes of 2nd RBM and 2nd RBM's hidden nodes are the input nodes of 3rd RBM and so on.

## Deep Belief Networks (DBN)

![](img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb79055a3_475_0.png)

Directed & Undirected connections in DBN: In a Deep Belief Network, make sure that your directionality is in place for all of the layers except for the top two.

Basically you make sure that these layers one, two and three, there are directed connections between them, and are directed downwards. Whereas there is no direction in the top two layers (undirected connections).

> It's quite hard to explain what's going on here because this is a very complex, a very advanced type of network. When Hinton and his team find's DBN idea when the whole Interest in Deep Learning got revived in 2000s.

![](img/0aa092657a6eb1b9f2dd65bbb79055a3_475_1.png)

In terms of training, there's two types of algorithms in DBN

- i. Greedy layer-wise training algorithm: First you train the RBMs, with the undirected connections. You train them layer by layer as RBMs, 1st layer, 2nd layer and then 3rd layer.
- ii. Wake-sleep algorithm: In DBN, after the training has happened, make sure that all of your layers, except for the top two, are directed layers (connections between them are directed).
