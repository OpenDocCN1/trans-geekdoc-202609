Synapses: The Synapses, they actually get assigned with weights.
In short weights are **crucial** to **ANN** functioning. Because **weights** are how **neural networks learn**. By adjusting the **weights** the **neural network decides** in every single **case** what *is important* and what *is not important* to certain neuron.

What gets passed along or not. Or what strength to what extent signals get passed along. So weights are crucial. They are the things that **get adjusted** through the **process of learning.**

When you're **training an ANN** you're basically adjusting **all of the weights** in all of the Synapses across this whole **neural network.**
