### 12.3.1 Problem Description

Here we depict the stock price of Google. If you have some notions in financial engineering you already know that it's pretty challenging since indeed there is the Brownian Motion that states that the future variations of the stock price are independent from the past.

- So it is quite impossible to predict exactly the future stock price otherwise we would become billionaires, it's actually possible to predict some trends.
- So we are re gonna try to predict the upward and downward trends that exists in the Google stock price.

> Our model that we will implement will be an LSTM.

- Our LSTM is gonna to capture the downward and upward trend of the Google stock price.
- We're not going to try to predict exactly the stock price, we're gonna try to predict the trend, the upward or downward trend of the Google stock price.

> Our we re gonna train our LSTM model on five years of the Google stock price, from the beginning of 2012 to the end of 2016.

Based on this training, and the correlations identified by the LSTM of the Google stock price, we will try to predict the first month, January 2017.

Remember, in our working, there are two .csv files, for train and other for test.

The train data, Google_Stock_Price_Train.csv contains the Google stock price from beginning of 2012 to the end of 2016, and then the test data Google_Stock_Price_Test.csv contains the first month of 2017, that is, the whole financial.
