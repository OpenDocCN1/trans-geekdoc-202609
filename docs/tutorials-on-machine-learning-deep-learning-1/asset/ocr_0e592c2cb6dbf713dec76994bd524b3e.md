MCMC & blind walk: The MCMC techniques, Markov chains are related to the random walk and we can precisely blind the blind walk.

+   - To get our predictions of the test set ratings, we need to apply again the k step divergence!
- More precisely, we need to make k step of the random walk (i.e. 10 step for loop), that is 10 steps of the random walk?

Actually we need one step of the random walk (blind walk), we don't need the for-loop for k-step (i.e 10 step) to get our final prediction. So we only need one step of contrastive divergence.

+   - Also is not exactly the random walk because in the random walk the probabilities are the same, even if a Markov chain probabilities are not the same so it's not a Blind Walk.

The Principle Of The Blind Walk is, imagine you were blindfolded and you had to make 100 steps on a straight line without getting out of straight line.

You will be trained with sampling to make 100 steps on the straight line because you're blindfolded so you don't know how to make some steps and stay on the straight line, so you go a little bit on the left, on the right and after 100 steps it's hard to be on the straight line.

+   - But we wanted to make 100 steps, staying on the straight line blindfolded. and by doing some random steps.

It is close to random walk and the MCMC but the different is that in the random walk the probabilities are the same and the probabilities are different. And what you're trained to make 100 steps staying a straight line.

And so by principle of all this is that you are trained for 100 steps so that you can make one step.

+   - When you have to take the challenge to make only one step and still be on that straight line, you will have High Chance of SUCCESS.
