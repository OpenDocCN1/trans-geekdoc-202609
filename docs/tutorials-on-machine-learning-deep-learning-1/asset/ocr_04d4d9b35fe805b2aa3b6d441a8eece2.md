# 7.1 NLP: Natural Language Processing

+   NLP: Natural Language Processing or NLP is an area of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence concerned with interactions between computers and human through natural languages.

+   NLP is used to apply Machine Learning models to text and language.
Teach machines understand what is said in spoken and when it is the focus of Natural Language Processing. Whenever you dictate something into your iPhone / Android device that is converted to text, that's an NLP algorithm in action.

+   You can also think of Siri's Ai or Amazon's Alexa. These are NLP related algorithms, which is processing your language and converting it into data. Possible to run the algorithm for the desired purposes.

+   NLP history: The history of Natural-Language Processing generally started in the 1950s, although work could be found from earlier periods. Alan Turing published an article titled "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" which proposed what is now called the Turing Test as a criterion of intelligence.

+   Up to the 1980s, most natural-language processing systems were based on complex sets of hand-written rules. Starting in the late 1980s, however, there was a revolution in natural-language processing with the introduction of Machine Learning Algorithms for Language Processing.

But even more recent research has focused unsupervised and semi supervised learning algorithm (as especially as of lately a huge push into Deep Learning techniques for NLP). Research in this area is highly active and it's an exciting time for NLP related algorithms.
