# SOMs Learn?

BMUs radiuses shrink: One epoch completes after you through all of your rows in your dataset and all of these updates happen. In a new epoch when you go through your rows again, a unique feature of the Kohonen Learning Algorithm is applied and makes all the BMUs radiuses shrink.

+   - When the radiuses of all BMUs become a bit smaller, and this time when you're going through your dataset, your BMUs are pulling less nodes than previous epoch.
