## I. INTRODUCTION

COVID-19 has affected the whole world and it is considered the largest pandemic of the 21st century. Due to COVID-19, the world is fighting with malaria, at the same time it is also disturbed due to lockdown in many parts of the world. Symptoms such as fever, headache, dyspnea, cough, fatigue, and malaise are common symptoms of COVID-19. In many, no symptoms are observed. It is detected with PCR, and the patient need to wait for four to six hours. PCR test have high sensitivity and specificity rate 60% to 70%; Radiology can be used as an alternative diagnostic method for COVID-19. X-ray and CT scan can be much faster than PCR tests. In some circumstances, chest X-ray values were used to give a diagnosis about COVID-19 or not. However, several values were not normal for other diseases. However that the chest X-ray showed the abnormal features such as Ground glass opacities, traction bronchiectasis, reticulation sub pleural band, mixed consolidation GGO, vascular enlargement, and bronchial thickening were observed on patient. In this case, deep learning has made so much progress in image classification, computer vision [6] and medical image analysis [7]. COVID-19 affects the lungs and its abnormalities.

In this paper, four deep learning models VGG6, ResNet18, V3, and AlexNet deep convolution model are used on datasets. The limited data, all of the models were pre-trained, and the last layer was tuned with COVID-19 patient chest X-ray images. Another experiment was performed with ResNet18 in which the model was trained on the COVID-Xray-S dataset [12]. Models used on the option size of 24 the number of epochs was increased to 30 and ResNet18 was trained.
