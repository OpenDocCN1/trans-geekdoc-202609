# 4.2. Comparison of Different Molecular Fingerprints.

In this study, nine molecular fingerprints containing different lengths and structural information were employed to build classification models. From Figure 2, it is obvious that the Ext, FP, and Pub fingerprints have better performance than the others. By contrast, the models built on the AP2D and Gra fingerprints perform poorly in terms of the AUC and CA values. In addition, four of the top five models in 10-fold cross validation were built on the Ext and FP fingerprints, which were still robust for the test and external validation sets.
