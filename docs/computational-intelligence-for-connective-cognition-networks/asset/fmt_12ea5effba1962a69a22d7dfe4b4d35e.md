
# Quantum repeaters

Quantum repeaters can extend the range of quantum communication by correcting for losses and errors due to decoherence or noise without directly measuring the quantum information ([Azuma et al. 2023](#ch12.xhtml#r029)). These are used for enabling the reliable transmission of quantum information over longer distances without significant loss or degradation of signal. In local area networks or even in some metro area networks with short distances, quantum repeaters are not required as the qubit fidelity can be maintained easily. However, quantum repeaters become essential to implement as the quantum networks scale up to cover some larger geographic area over a country, region such as wide area networks (WANs), some larger metro networks etc. The working of quantum repeaters involves the breaking of the transmission path into shorter segments so that qubit fidelity can be maintained over these segments. Pairs of entangled qubits are created across each segment. Entanglement between adjacent segments is created and then entanglement is swapped over the longer distances to maintain the purity and integrity of quantum states. Quantum memory is a core component of quantum repeaters as it can store and release the qubit at a later time. This will enable the synchronization of entanglement over different segments (Azuma et al. 2023).

## 2.3.6 Quantum internet

The internet is playing an integral role in connecting people across globe and hence, become an important part in our daily lives. However, the current internet infrastructure is more prone to cyber-attacks posing a threat to sensitive information ([Aslan et al. 2023](#ch12.xhtml#r028); [Carlo and Obergfaell 2024](#ch12.xhtml#r034)). Quantum internet can solve this problem. The quantum internet is a theoretical concept of interconnected quantum computers and other quantum devices such that information can be sent and received using quantum signals ([Wehner et al. 2018](#ch12.xhtml#r074)). Many complex problems could be solved with the help of quantum internet in co-existence with the classical internet. The motivation behind quantum internet comes from the transformative impact of classical internet on our society. The development of quantum internet can have profound implications for security, scientific discovery and computing. The quantum internet can span various domains, including metrology, distributed quantum computing and quantum-enhanced secure communication potentially revolutionizing areas such as financial risk analysis, data encryption and material science ([Cacciapuoti et al. 2019](#ch12.xhtml#r033)).

## 2.5 Opportunities of quantum computing in quantum-based network paradigms

Integrating quantum computing into quantum-based network paradigms is a groundbreaking development that has the potential to revolutionize information processing. Quantum computing can significantly enhance the capabilities of quantum networks, leading to more secure and efficient communication. Let's discuss some of the opportunities:

1.  **Enhanced computational power**: Quantum computing can perform complex calculations at faster speeds than classical computers ([Farhi et al. 2014](#ch12.xhtml#r041)). This characteristic feature of quantum computers can bring transformative impact on industries such as cryptography, optimization and drug discovery.

2.  **Enhanced Security**: Quantum networks are based on the fundamental principles of quantum mechanics ensuring that any attempt to intercept information can be readily detected. Quantum networks like QKD had greatly enhanced the security in communication networks ([von Burg et al. 2021](#ch12.xhtml#r073)). Quantum computing has enabled more sophisticated encryption methods which are virtually unbreakable by classical means to improve the security features.
