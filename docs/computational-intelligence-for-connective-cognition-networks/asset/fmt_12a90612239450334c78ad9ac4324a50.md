Research examines how UAV technology interacts with the Internet of Things (IoT) and how this may affect smart cities in the future. The widespread use of drones in these metropolitan environments presents significant risks to public safety, privacy, and cybersecurity. The increased use of drones has spurred development on secure data gathering and storage systems such as `SecureIoD`, which uses blockchain-based storage methods, secure key establishment, and authentication to protect IoD data. Although difficulties with managing massive data quantities still exist, the integration of UAVs into the Internet of Medical Things (IoMT) has produced creative health monitoring systems that enable medical practitioners to access and analyse data remotely.

More autonomy and less human involvement are the goals of UAV technological advancements, with a focus on safe navigation methods. Strong cybersecurity standards are required in light of privacy and security vulnerabilities in drone networks. The use of ML techniques, like `Decision Tree` and `Logistic Regression`, has shown improved cybersecurity protocols for drone networks. Furthermore, a number of studies highlight how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and ML are revolutionising a number of drone-related fields, including intrusion detection systems, site monitoring, and communication security.

Blockchain technologies, 5G networks, and AI all work together to create potentially intelligent and safe drone communication systems as shown in **Figure 4.2**. Additionally, with an emphasis on security in smart cities, AI-driven 5G apps provide cutting-edge capabilities in virtual emotion systems. Blockchain-based solutions show promise in protecting networks from criminal attacks and network threats while also safeguarding communication in UAV systems. As this is going on, intelligent intrusion detection systems are being developed to combat common threats to UAVs. These systems use techniques such as `PCA` and single-class classifiers to identify and neutralise attacks like jamming and spoofing, protecting UAVs even in situations where communication is disrupted.
