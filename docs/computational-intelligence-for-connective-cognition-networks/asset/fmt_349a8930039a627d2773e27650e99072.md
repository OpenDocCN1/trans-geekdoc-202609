## 2.1 Introduction

Our universe shows a fascinating behaviour at the microscopic level, and there are many occurrences in nature which are hard to be explained. Have we tried to think why a quantum computer can perform better as compared to a typical computer? And what are the unique features it possesses? All these questions have their answers hidden in understanding the fundamentals of nature. The branch of physics, known as quantum mechanics ([Konishi and Paffuti 2009](#ch12.xhtml#r054)), explains the behaviour and dynamics of particles at the microscopic level.

As the research and advancements in quantum mechanics grow rapidly, the existence of quantum computers, quantum networks and now, the quantum internet has become possible. There are significant advantages of quantum computing over traditional or classical computing. Quantum computing harness the fundamental concepts of quantum mechanics like entanglement, superposition, etc. (Wineland 2013), which offer significant advantages over traditional computing methods to solve the complex problems like logistics, drug designing, banking, statistical science, secure communication, etc. The realization of quantum computers has become possible only with the remarkable developments in designing quantum software, quantum hardware research, quantum algorithm creation, etc.

The fundamental unit of information in classical computers is a binary digit. Each binary digit can exist in two states, that is either 0 or 1. On the other hand, the basic unit of quantum information in case of quantum computers is a qubit. The qubits can be prepared in various physical systems such as electronic energy states of atoms, optical degrees of freedom of photons, spin states of electrons or nuclei, etc. ([Bennett and DiVincenzo 2000](#ch12.xhtml#r030); [Bouwmeester and Zeilinger 2000](#ch12.xhtml#r032)). The properties of qubits are generally based on physical properties of the particle, such as the spin of an electron, and hence it can differ in various ways. It is known that an electron can have either up spin or down spin, but in quantum mechanics, there is a probability for an electron to exist in a state which has a spin in both directions simultaneously. This concept is known as the superposition of two states in quantum mechanics. This concept sounds strange to our general observation in accordance with classical physics. However, this type of peculiar behaviour shown by particles would be helpful in various ways. For instance, qubits can store much more information than conventional bits. A classical bit can take values as 0 and 1 only. With two bits, there exist four possible combinations: 00, 01, 10 or 11. While in quantum computing, a qubit can take value 0 and 1 simultaneously due to superposition property. Thus, with two qubits, all four combinations (00, 01, 10, 11) can exist at once. This property in quantum computing has increased the amount of information stored in qubits tremendously.
