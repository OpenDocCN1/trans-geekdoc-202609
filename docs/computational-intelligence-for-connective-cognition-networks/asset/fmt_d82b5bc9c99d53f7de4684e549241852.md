## 7.1 Introduction

The expedited digitalization of data caused increasing interest toward the paperless electronic documents; automating the process of document classification is becoming a necessity for categorizing the documents properly and knowledge discovery. Deep learning algorithms are used widely for the classification of the electronic documents such as E-mails, blogs, digital libraries, and online news to get some meaningful knowledge out of them. The large and increasing volume of the electronic documents creates the need for intelligent computational techniques to classify and organize the documents to their respective classes automatically. Most of the recent work has been done on the automatic classification of the same language documents. The present work focuses on the extraction of multi-lingual text from the portable document format (`PDF`) documents.

Classifying such documents from a collection of documents of different languages is an error-prone, tedious, and time-consuming process. The document classification engine can assist humans in the separation of documents of different languages into different folders. This prevents the document from getting lost if they are not properly labeled and accelerates the process of usage of these documents for proper application. The proposed classification engine can also ingest and classify the `PDF` documents containing the data in the form of the images. Such document classifier can find applications in various fields like text mining, information extraction systems, etc. The information extraction systems, which can work only on the documents of a specific language require these documents to be segregated before they can be applied for the extraction of the information. Therefore, the document classification engine serves as a prerequisite to classify documents to leverage extraction of information from a variety of `PDF` documents.

The classification of the documents [[1]](#ch17.xhtml#r168) aids in organization of the documents required for annotation, curation, analysis, and retrieval. A variety of methods have been studied by the researchers for the classification of documents. In general, the work focused on the information and document retrieval mainly utilizes the core technologies behind search engine such as dictionaries and indexing [[2]](#ch17.xhtml#r169). Considerable work has been performed using these fundamental technologies and improvements have been performed through query reformulation and feedback [[3]](#ch17.xhtml#r170), [[4]](#ch17.xhtml#r171).

Recent work on documents classification involved the use of support vector machines (`SVM`) [[5]](#ch17.xhtml#r172)–[[7]](#ch17.xhtml#r174). `SVM` algorithm rely on the use of kernel functions to provide separating hyperplanes. Various string kernels are used such as mismatch kernel [[8]](#ch17.xhtml#r175) and the spectrum kernel [[9]](#ch17.xhtml#r176) are used for information retrieval and document classification along with `RNA` and `DNA` sequence data.

For example, decision trees [[3]](#ch17.xhtml#r170) and naïve Bayes [[10]](#ch17.xhtml#r177), [[11]](#ch17.xhtml#r178) have also been used in the past. Additional methods such as topic modeling, deep learning, etc. have also been explored which can provide better accuracy [[12]](#ch17.xhtml#r179). Deep learning [[13]](#ch17.xhtml#r180), [[14]](#ch17.xhtml#r181) is also used extensively these days for varied other applications as well. The basic neural network architecture is made up of an interconnected network of nonlinear nodes which are organized in the form of layers. All these layers are organized sequentially in a neural network. However, the task of automatic document classification is becoming more and more challenging with time due to the rapid growth in the size of the corpus in the digitalized era. The size increase has occurred in almost all areas [[15]](#ch17.xhtml#r182).
