
Throughout our exploration of research in the field of UAVs and drone technology, we have uncovered critical advancements and challenges that shape this innovative domain. UAVs have become indispensable in both military and civilian applications, offering significant improvements in remote accessibility, efficiency and safety. The push toward fully autonomous UAVs represents a major leap forward, aiming to reduce human intervention while prioritizing safe, autonomous navigation. This progress has been examined through techniques for 3D obstacle avoidance and trajectory planning, addressing key challenges in the field. Simultaneously, the rapid expansion of smaller drones has opened new possibilities across various sectors, especially through the integration of the Internet of Things (IoT) for navigation services. However, this growth brings with it serious privacy and security risks within drone networks (NoD), stemming from inherent design vulnerabilities. Addressing these concerns, we explore a novel hybrid ML approach that combines supervised ML techniques, such as decision-based methods and logistic regression, to enhance NoD cybersecurity. This integrated, AI-driven solution shows promise in strengthening the security of drone networks.
