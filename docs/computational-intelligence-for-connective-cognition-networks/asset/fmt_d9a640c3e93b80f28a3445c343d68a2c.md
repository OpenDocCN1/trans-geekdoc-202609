## 6.1 Introduction

Road safety is a significant concern in a world where driving is an everyday activity for many individuals. Driver drowsiness is one of the primary risks on the road, leading to numerous accidents and, in severe cases, fatalities [[1]](#ch16.xhtml#r142)–[[5]](#ch16.xhtml#r146). To tackle this issue, the paper titled “Driver Drowsiness Detection Using Machine Learning” aims to mitigate drowsiness-related accidents through an intelligent detection system. This study leverages machine learning (ML) technology, which learns from data to develop a system capable of identifying early signs of drowsiness in real time. The ultimate objective is to enhance road safety by preventing accidents before they happen.

Artificial intelligence (AI) has become a transformative force across many sectors, with its application in road safety holding significant promise. Integrating AI into transportation systems introduces innovative solutions to improve safety, reduce accident rates, and save lives. With the rising number of vehicles and the increasing complexity of traffic systems, traditional road safety approaches are often insufficient. AI offers powerful tools to address these challenges effectively.

A primary way AI supports road safety is through predictive analytics. By analyzing extensive datasets, including traffic patterns, weather conditions, and historical accident data, AI can predict potential high-risk areas. This predictive capability enables authorities to implement preventive measures, such as adjusting traffic signals or dispatching law enforcement to accident-prone zones, thereby reducing the probability of accidents before they occur [[6]](#ch16.xhtml#r147), [[7]](#ch16.xhtml#r148).

Driver assistance systems represent another crucial application of AI in road safety. Advanced driver-assistance systems (ADAS) use AI to enhance driving safety and improve the driving experience. Features such as adaptive cruise control, lane-keeping assistance, and automated emergency braking help drivers maintain safe distances, stay within lanes, and respond promptly to potential hazards. These systems serve as an additional layer of safety, compensating for human error—a leading factor in road accidents [[8]](#ch16.xhtml#r149).

AI has also proven highly beneficial in traffic management. Intelligent traffic management systems can process real-time traffic data to enhance traffic flow, reducing both congestion and accident risk. By dynamically adjusting traffic signals based on live conditions, AI helps create smoother traffic movement and prevents bottlenecks that often contribute to collisions. Additionally, these systems can redirect traffic in response to accidents or hazardous conditions, further promoting road safety.

The rise of autonomous vehicles marks a major advancement in AI’s application to road safety. Self-driving cars, powered by AI, can make real-time safety-focused decisions. These vehicles maintain consistent, safe driving behaviors, communicate with other vehicles to prevent collisions, and handle unexpected scenarios with precision. By removing the human error factor, autonomous vehicles have the potential to significantly reduce accident rates on the roads.

AI is also essential in driver monitoring. AI-equipped systems can observe drivers for signs of fatigue, distraction, or impairment, and alert them if they become inattentive or engage in risky behaviors. In critical situations, these systems can even take control of the vehicle to prevent accidents due to human factors. This proactive approach to driver monitoring supports drivers in staying alert and in control, contributing to safer driving conditions [[9]](#ch16.xhtml#r150)–[[11]](#ch16.xhtml#r152).

Incident detection and response is another area where AI enhances road safety. AI can rapidly detect accidents and other incidents on the road, providing real-time alerts to emergency services along with critical information about the situation. This capability leads to faster response times, which can be crucial in saving lives and mitigating the impact of accidents.

Infrastructure monitoring is yet another application of AI in road safety. AI can monitor the condition of roads, bridges, and tunnels, identifying issues such as potholes or structural weaknesses that could pose a danger to drivers. Proactive maintenance based on AI assessments helps prevent accidents caused by poor infrastructure conditions.
