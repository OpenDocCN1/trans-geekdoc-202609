## 9.1 Introduction

With the recent advancements in deep learning techniques, the world of a generation of hyper-realistic media generation and manipulation has seen unprecedented growth, and the impact it brought along with itself is tremendous and far-reaching.

Over the last few years, the use of AI-generated content has substantially increased on the internet. Especially during and after the COVID-19 pandemic period, the dependence of the public on digital media has increased multifold, with people spending more and more time in the virtual world. As the dependence on digital media has increased, misinformation through fake media has also increased significantly. One of the biggest sources of misinformation on the internet is "Deepfake", which derives its name from "deep learning" and "fake", referring to media synthesized and/or manipulated by deep learning methods. Deepfakes are hyper-realistic audio, video, or image that easily deceive humans. Deepfake is one of the most bothersome applications of AI in recent years in terms of its potential use for cybercrimes and has been called the most serious AI crime threat [[1]](#ch19.xhtml#r273).

Damages resulting from the spread of misinformation through deepfakes encompass damage to financial resources, psychological/mental health harm, and damage to the reputation and trust of companies/celebrities/politicians, which is often irrecoverable, causing trust issues and chaos and unrest in society. According to a report by Sumsub [[2]](#ch19.xhtml#r274), there has been an increase of 245% year on year in deepfakes worldwide from Q1 2023 till Q1 2024, with multifold growth in countries where elections are being held or scheduled in 2024. It reported YOY increase of deepfake incidents in India (+280%), USA (+303%), Mexico (+500%), Indonesia (+1550%), and South Korea (+1625%). The magnitude and spread of deepfakes through the increasing pervasiveness of digital media in our personal and professional lives have made deepfakes a de facto tool for misinformation in today's time. As a result, the field of deepfake detection has gathered the attention of researchers across industries, academics, and the government. Thus, it has become important to analyze deepfake and related advances in deepfake generation methods and development in detection methods. In this review, we will focus on visual deepfake detection methods.
