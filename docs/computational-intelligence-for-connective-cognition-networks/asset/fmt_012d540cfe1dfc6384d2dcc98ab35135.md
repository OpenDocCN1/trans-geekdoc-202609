
## 5.6 Future work

This research work presents a reliable and comprehensible CNN-based model for image forgery detection. Despite great accuracy, CNN-based models can be challenging to comprehend and subject to hostile attacks. One of the primary limitations lies in the black-box nature of deep learning models, making it difficult to understand the decision-making process behind forgery detection. Future efforts should focus on improving the explainability and interpretability of CNN models through visualization techniques, attention mechanisms, and interpretable architectures.

Additionally, robustness against adversarial attacks remains a crucial area for improvement. Adversarial manipulations can deceive CNN models by introducing imperceptible perturbations, leading to misclassification. Enhancing model resilience through adversarial training, defensive distillation, and anomaly detection techniques can significantly improve reliability in forensic applications.

Furthermore, cross-domain generalization is essential for deploying CNN-based forgery detection models in real-world scenarios. Models trained on specific datasets often struggle to generalize across different forgery techniques and image sources. Leveraging domain adaptation, transfer learning, and meta-learning can help CNNs adapt to diverse datasets and maintain high detection accuracy.

Another promising direction involves integrating CNNs with hybrid approaches, combining deep learning with traditional forensic techniques. Fusion models—leveraging both deep feature extraction and handcrafted forensic features—can improve robustness and interpretability.

Lastly, the ethical and legal implications of image forgery detection must be considered. As forensic AI models become more advanced, ensuring bias-free decision-making, transparency, and compliance with legal standards will be critical for their adoption in law enforcement and media forensics.

Future research can concentrate on creating more resilient models that are more interpretable and less vulnerable to attacks.

*   **Increasing the diversity and quality of training data**: The training data’s quality and diversity have a significant impact on the accuracy of CNN-based models. To enhance the effectiveness of these models, future research can concentrate on creating datasets that are larger and more varied.

*   **Integration of numerous modalities**: In addition to looking at photos, other types of modalities, such as audio and text, can also be used to detect image forgeries. Future research can concentrate on merging many modalities to create more thorough counterfeit detection systems that can examine various media types.

*   **CNN-based models for mobile and low-power device modelling**: CNN-based models can be computationally expensive and may not be appropriate for deployment on mobile or low-power devices. Future research can concentrate on creating models that can operate on these gadgets without sacrificing accuracy.

*   **Creating CNN-based models to detect video forgery**: Detecting video forgery is more difficult than detecting image forgery, despite the fact that CNN-based models have been successful at doing so. Future research can concentrate on creating CNN-based algorithms that can recognize video piracy, including deepfake videos.

*   **Creating models for real-time forgery detection**: Real-time image fraud detection is essential for many applications, including social networking platforms and video streaming. Future research can concentrate on creating CNN-based models that can be incorporated into these systems and can instantly detect forgeries.

*   **Making multimodal forgery detection models**: Multimodal forgery detection entails spotting fakes that were produced by fusing various media, such as audio and visual. Future research can concentrate on creating CNN-based algorithms that can recognize multi-modal forgeries and offer more thorough analysis.
