## 10.1 Introduction

Large language models (`LLM`) have the potential to create or enhance existing solutions across various domains, and the current educational framework is one of them. In this domain, `LLMs` can be applied for tasks such as generation of quiz [[1]](#ch20.xhtml#r342), planning of lecture [[2]](#ch20.xhtml#r343), and suggestion for content [[3]](#ch20.xhtml#r344). In this same direction, there remains a notable gap in research concerning the generation of lecture preparation material specific to programming computer science courses. Literature suggests exploration of effective prompt engineering techniques to guide the language model specific to programming course-related lecture generation for neophyte coder.
