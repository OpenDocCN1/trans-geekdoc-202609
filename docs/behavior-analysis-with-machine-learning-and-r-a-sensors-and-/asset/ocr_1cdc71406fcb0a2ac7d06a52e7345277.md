The front cover depicts two brothers (Biás and Biranz) in what seems to be a typical weekend. They are exploring and enjoying nature as usual. What they don't know is that their lives are about to change and there is no turning back. Suddenly, Biranz spots a strange object approaching them. As it makes its way out of the rocks, its entire figure is revealed. The brothers have never seen anything like that. The closest image they have in their brains is a hand-sized carnivorous plant they saw at the botanical garden during a school visit years ago. Without any warning, the creature releases a load of spores into the air. Today, the breeze is not on the brothers' side and the spores quickly enclose them. After seconds of being exposed, their bodies start to paralyze. Moments later, they can barely move their pupils. The creature's multiple stems begin to inflate and all of a sudden, multiple thorns are short. Horrified and incapable, the brothers can only witness how the thorns approach their bodies and they can even hear how the air is being cut by sharp thorns. At this point, they are only waiting for the wait. ...they haven't felt any impact. Has time just stopped? No—the thorns were repelled by what appears to be a bionic dolphin emitting some type of ultrasonic waves. One of the projectiles managed to dodge the sound defense and is heading directly to Biás. While flying almost mile above sea level, an eagle aims for the elusive thorn and destroys it with surgical precision. However, the creature is being persistent with its attacks. Will the brothers escape from this crossfire battle?
