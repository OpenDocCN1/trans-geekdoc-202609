The number of actions is 27 and some of the actions are: right hand wave, two front clap, basketball shoot, front boxing, etc. More information about the collection process and pictures can be consulted on the website https://personal.utdallas.edu/~kehtar/UTD-MHAD.html.

# B.11 SMARTPHONE ACTIVITIES

Included: Yes.

This dataset is collected by WISDM³ and was made available by Kwapisz et al. (2010). The dataset has 6 activities: walking, jogging, walking upstairs, walking downstairs, sitting and standing. The data was collected by 36 volunteers with the accelerometer of an Android phone located in the users’ pant pocket and with a sampling rate of 20 Hz.

# B.12 SMILES

Included: No.

This dataset contains face images of 64 × 64 pixels and is published here: http://conradsanderson.id.au/lfwcrop/. This is a cropped version (Sanderson and Lovell, 2009) of the Labeled Faces in the LFW (Labeled) database (Huang et al., 2008). Please, download the color version (lfwcrop_color.zip) and copy all ppm files into the faces/ directory.

A subset of the database was labeled by Arigababu et al. (2016), Arigababu (2017). The labels are provided as two text files (SMILE list.txt, NON-SMILE list.txt), each containing the list of faces that correspond to smiling and non-smiling faces. The smiling set has 600 pictures.

# B.13 STUDENTS’ MENTAL HEALTH

Included: Yes.

This dataset contains 268 survey responses that include variables related to depression, accultural stress, social connectedness, and help-seeking behaviors reported by international and domestic students at an international university in Japan. For a detailed description, please see (Nguyen et al., 2019).
