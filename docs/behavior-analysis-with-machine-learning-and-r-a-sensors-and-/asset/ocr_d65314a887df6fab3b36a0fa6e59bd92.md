Gestures are a form of communication. They are often accompanied with speech but can also be used to communicate independently of speech (like in sign language). Gestures allow us to externalize and emphasize emotions and thoughts. They are based on body movements from arms, hands, fingers, face, head, etc. Gestures can be used as a non-verbal way to identify and study behaviors for different purposes such as for emotion
