In following the development of eBiz and their AI-platform we adopted an engaged scholarship methodology (Van de ven, 2007) where we worked closely together with this company instead of just relying on e.g. interviews.

The eBiz company we have collaborated is a global actor who has offices in 6 counties, including an innovation office in London. eBiz develops and provide an AI-powered web retail platform for 30 customers (global chains), and they have formulated their core business as being about “doing intelligent brand and product matching online to provide the best price for the customer”. They are accordingly supporting multiple stakeholders here (including other companies as their clients, as well as the online shoppers (endusers/customers). In doing so they acknowledge how the AI-system is of strategic value if aligned with the organization and its trading partners (Melville, et. al., 2004), and ways of integrating the synchronous development of IT, algorithms and business models (Gomez-Uribe, 2015).

eBiz also has a mission statement that is completely aligned with their AI-platform. This statement says that they deliver a complete and exact view of how all their client’s products are compare in price points with any competition on the online market. The comparison is data-driven, automatic, and their algorithms scrape and analyze the web on a daily basis.

This allows online retailers to curate assortment, increase volume, growth and target specific strategic and tactical goals
