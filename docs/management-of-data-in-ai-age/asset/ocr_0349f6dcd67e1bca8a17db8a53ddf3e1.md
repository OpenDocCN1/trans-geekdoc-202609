However, to make AI work also comes with some conditions and necessities. In our project we have focused on making it work for consumers, and as a core business component, by relying on the following simple model for applying AI in this context including the cycle of 1) monitoring, 2) acting/reacting, and 3) re-configuring. Here we briefly outline these three components:

# 1) Monitoring

The AI system we have focused on in this business case is configured to monitor changes online from the viewpoint of price changes for similar products. To make this work, the AI system is dependent on a set of variables that describe each product, and the system can then train its algorithms to constantly monitor these similar products online.

# 2) Acting/reacting

The AI system we have focused on can also act. Given any changes in the pricing of similar products online, the AI system can also take some actions. It can act/react on changes in the pricing of similar products, and it can instantly and dynamically adjust the prices of the online shops articles based on an analysis of how similar products are priced.
