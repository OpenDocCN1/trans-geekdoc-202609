# As a solution in the context of Growing Populations

Worldwide the populations are rising and this is become an important challenge in respect of the food productions. In this context various organizations, agricultural farms, industries, institutions etc. are moving well in respect of the better and productive agricultural foods, products and yields. With the help of Big data farmers and other stakeholders can get real time data on various aspects and areas such as rainfall patterns, water cycles, soil condition, crop condition, fertilizer requirements etc. and in this context it helps in productive agricultural foods.

This enables them to make smart decisions, such as what crops to plant for better profitability and when to harvest. The right decisions ultimately improve farm yields. Using mathematical modeling can be helpful in different activities and thus it helps in agro development in different context as well. The use of sensors for collecting data is worthy in data management and proper decision making.
