Speech separation is an application of AI-aided NLP that helps in identification of the voice of the caller of interest from the mixture of voices of other callers and thus, answering each of the callers separately. In addition, call recordings of the customers are analyzed by organizations to get insight whether the customer is happy or sad, his needs and future requirements [22].

Relevant NLP tasks in customer service include:

- *Speech recognition* – conversion of spoken language into text
- *Question answering* – Answering questions posed by humans in a natural language.
- **Reputation Monitoring** – Earlier business organizations were only confined to their internal databases for generating text analytics to understand customer demands and improving business strategies. But with the development of improved learning algorithms, businesses started adopting tools to analyze public data. Although, some of this data is structured and ready to be analyzed (e.g. census data, stock prices), but most of it is still in unstructured format especially in the form of human-generated text such as news, blog posts, forums, reviews, reports, social media like Twitter, Facebook etc. These sources contain abundant useful information, although unstructured, about how competitors, customers and the market as a whole are evolving. Organizations are now using these sources of information in reputation monitoring by identifying the mentions of their brands and products along with recognizing cases when they should act.

    Reputation monitoring includes following NLP tasks:

    - *Sentiment analysis*, which determines the attitude, emotional state, feelings, judgement or intent of the customer. This is done by either assigning a polarity to the text (positive, neutral or negative) or trying to recognize the underlying mood (happy, sad, calm, angry, etc.).
    - *Co-reference resolution*, which connects pronouns to the right objects. For example, if a customer writes: “Someone from the seller side called up to ask if I liked my new apartment or not.” I replied: “It is awesome.” Here, it is important to recognize that “it” refers to the apartment and not the person. In short, the customer is giving positive feedback about the product [].

- **Market Intelligence** – Business markets are highly influenced by market knowledge and information exchange between various industries, stakeholders, governments and regulatory bodies. It is crucial to keep abreast of latest industry trends and changing standards. NLP is a useful technology to track and monitor the market intelligence reports and extract the necessary information for organizations to formulate new business, market, competitors, customers, and other important details about their industry.

    There are dozens of tools available to help entrepreneurs monitor their competitors. NLP-powered engines like Zirra² simplify the process for automatically building a competitive landscape [23]. When Zirra analyzes something, it gathers a list of companies and ranks them from zero to one. This rank shows how closely these companies are related to each other using a multimodal semantic field. The algorithmic solutions like Zirra create the list of companies by scanning the Internet for articles and putting the data into an NLP module that closes out semantic relationships between companies.

## G. Concluding Remarks on AI in Business

From above mentioned facts and figures, it can be postulated that businesses that are adopting AI can expect to see increased productivity in future. New competences can be derived from streamlining tasks that previously took days to complete by people and improving work processes by pairing people and machines in new ways. Many businesses also believe that AI will accelerate and enhance innovation, thus creating new jobs and business perspectives. Some expect to see augmented consumer demand from more personalized and higher-quality AI-enhanced products and services.

At the same time, it’s a critical issue of debate that AI will result in eliminating jobs. For instance, virtual assistants being deployed to enhance customer support could result in many people working in customer care losing their jobs.

Even though, the use of AI in business is rising, but AI and machine learning are not yet effective enough to be deployed for every business operation. While there are many aspects of a business which can be automated, and necessarily be automated but tasks that require judgment, prioritization and trade-offs still require human intelligence.

To capitalize on AI technology, businesses will need to hire people with AI experience or the skills to analyze and use the data to encapsulate need of human supervision with AI. Businesses also need the computing power and system infrastructure to support AI-enabled products and services, and they need platforms to organize and integrate their data. Getting this infrastructure in place can be expensive.

Moreover, the data collected by AI offers another big challenge. How do businesses guarantee the validity of data collected in terms of correctness and reliability? What limits do they need to put on its use? Confirmation of the fact, that machine will carry out human orders as intended?

There are concerns that unintentional biases may find their way into AI algorithms or decision-making models. Businesses need to establish robust controls to prevent this from happening and monitor the systems that learn through AI. Letting stakeholders know about the business’s oversight can help establish trust with stakeholders that businesses are using AI reliably.
