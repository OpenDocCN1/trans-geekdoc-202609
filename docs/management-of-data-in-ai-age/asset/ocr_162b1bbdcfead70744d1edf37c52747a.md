It is an important fact that, worldwide many farmers are notable in doing Agriculture with proper methods, functions however millions of people are associated in Agriculture directly and indirectly. And in this context, Agricultural Informatics can be useful in the agricultural activities with the use of IT/Computing related components. Farmers have to face various challenges like heat, cold, flood, weather, drought, insect, pest infestations, disease of the agricultural crops etc and here agricultural informatics is effectively useful. According to its uses in the nutritional systems, global agro business development, ecological aspects also Agricultural Informatics are effectively useful [30], [31]. Therefore Agricultural Informatics can be useful in the following in broader context—

+   - Input including the Output systems of in pre production and post production.
+   - In the affairs of the weather forecasting systems
+   - Harvesting of the agro crops.
+   - In food security and food for all.
+   - To enhance the value-chain development in Agricultural Systems.
+   - In enhancing better Agricultural systems and sustainable growth.
+   - In Agro Marketing and better supply-chain systems, HR etc.
