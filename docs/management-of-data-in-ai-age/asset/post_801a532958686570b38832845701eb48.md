Personalized product recommendation is one of major applications of AI in businesses. The recommendation systems developed using AI were originally more focused on single user models that works by suggesting products or services to cater the needs and preferences of individual users. But, as per new scenario of online usage, it has been observed that users like to perform activities in groups such as playing games in groups online, watching movies etc. So, in order to accommodate such cases, AI techniques need to be devised that contend with the problem of group recommendations that would be gratifying the requirements of almost all the individuals in that group to a great extent [1].

The increased usability of mobile phones, digital gadgets and societal networking has augmented the importance and necessity of group recommendations in varied business areas.

The important factors that play significant role in group recommendation systems are social history of users, their societal relationships, resemblance in interests and choices, social interaction etc. Moreover, all these factors can now be easily extracted from the social network structure of a particular group of users [4]. Most of the research on group recommendation systems is mainly focused on exploring the core algorithms used in generation of group recommendations.

There are two major strategies that have been identified to generate group recommendations, as mentioned below:

- 1. Formulating a common group profile for a group of users through aggregation of their individual profiles and generating recommendations based on the group profile [10].
- 2. Generating personalized recommendations for the members of a particular group, and then accumulating them into a single group recommendation [9].

## Recommendation Engines – Business Use Case

Netflix, Amazon, Facebook, LinkedIn, all these big companies use recommendation systems for providing services to the customers and understanding the customer demands to attain higher customer satisfaction. The streaming services of Netflix use recommendation engines to generate recommendations that match user preferences for watching movies, shows etc. As per the preferences, age, interests, user express his views in surveys for rating movies which acts as user’s features that are learnt by machines to provide recommendations. Further, user’s response to recommendations is also examined to make better recommendations.

Recommendation systems have gained enough popularity these days, particularly as part of online business, and have been deployed across a broad spectrum of businesses to increase customer interaction and enrich shopping potential, as mentioned below:

- *Supermarkets:* Generating custom coupons for next purchases by considering the previous history of purchases and including the items that a customer would be interested in.
- *Retail industry:* Companies send emails and text messages to their customers following a purchase and give recommendation for new products like books, music albums, groceries, clothing etc. Many companies like Amazon and other similar sites offer product recommendations in real time based on the preferences of customers when they visit their online sites.
- *Stock Market:* Personalized stock recommendations are made for the investors by analyzing their previous assets, preferences, earnings and dividends.
- *Movie and Television services:* Companies like Netflix analyze each customer’s previous content choices and make recommendations based on them, as well as proactively recommend items in real time based on their browsing history.
- *Social networking sites:* Sites like LinkedIn and Facebook use recommendation systems to suggest additional connections or friends based on a person’s existing network.

## F. Natural Language Processing - Another Business Perspective of AI

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a subfield of AI that deals with the interaction between machines and humans using the natural languages. It is a blend of Natural Language, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence that collectively builds a technology to interact with machines in normal human language. The objective of NLP is to read, decode, understand, and make sense of the human languages in a valuable way. Most of the NLP techniques are highly dependent on machine learning algorithms to derive meaning from human languages.

Natural language processing is a scientific discipline that focuses entirely on making natural language accessible to machines. NLP addresses tasks such as identification of sentence boundaries in documents, extracting relationships from documents and searching and retrieval of documents and many more. NLP is a necessary means to facilitate text analytics by establishing structure in unstructured text to enable further analysis [1].

Basic NLP tasks include tokenization and parsing, lemmatization/stemming, part of speech (POS) tagging, named entity recognition, word segmentation, morphological transform or augment the text to achieve greater value and derive useful text analytics. [1]
