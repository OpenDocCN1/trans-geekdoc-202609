Here we contribute to this growing need by focusing on a company that applies AI on a large scale, by embedding AI in the development of their e-biz platform that reaches thousands of consumers. In doing so we follow the growing trend of focusing on what AI can do (in this case for consumers and for businesses), and we present the challenges they had to face when applying AI technologies in this particular context.

Before moving to this case of 3rd wave AI as applied in a business context we first examine the current approaches taken to the development of AI. In the next section we outline the existing approach including the machine-centered approach, the human-centered approach and the systems centered approach. We conclude that these approaches have been successful, but also that there might be room for a forth approach – to work with an applied AI approach – when it comes to questions concerning how AI should be used in a real-world context.
