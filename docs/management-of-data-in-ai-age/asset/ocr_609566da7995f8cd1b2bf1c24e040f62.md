The rise of Artificial intelligence, in this era, has marked an unprecedented boom in application of Machine learning (ML) techniques in numerous field which includes healthcare, banking, automobile, financial sector, manufacturing, retail etc. Although ML techniques have been known to be operating from quite a long time but due to recent technological advancements their use has become widespread in the business areas where it was limited previously. Even the enterprises that were not using ML at all due to higher implementation costs and lack of relevant manpower are also adapting ML based solutions [12].

Organizations dealing with retail business are using ML techniques to make analysis of purchase histories of the customers to understand their interests, demands and affordability to generate personalized product recommendations for the next purchase [1].
