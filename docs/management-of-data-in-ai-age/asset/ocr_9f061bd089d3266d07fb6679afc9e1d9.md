Deep Learning, a variant of Machine Learning, has gained enough popularity in recent years due to its potential to embody hierarchical processing using different layers of architecture. Moreover, deep learning has been identified to be best suited for high dimensional and dynamical data handling used in Bioinformatics, vision and speech recognition and sensor web.

Even deep learning has been found to work miraculously in e-commerce business for personalized product recommendations. The deep learning algorithm deployed in an e-commerce website traces the customer’s journey and activities. Based on the specifications of products viewed, duration of time spend on viewing each product, the customer activity before and after viewing each product, time of the day when customer logged into the e-commerce website, his location, gender and other relevant factors, the e-commerce website then offers the customer personalized purchase option that would appeal the customer ultimately. Thus, deep learning is considered to create more opportunities for improved business conversion rate and eventually helps to improve brand image by targeting customers with personalized content.
