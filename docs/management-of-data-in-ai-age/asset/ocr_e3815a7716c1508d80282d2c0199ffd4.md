The interplay between BDA and AI in view of businesses has also paved the way to the collection of data from the physical environment. In contrast to earlier online data collection techniques based on search queries, orders placed or purchased, clicks made by customers, many retail companies are now looking forward to gathering data offline as well for efficient decision making.

For instance, Amazon has incorporated corporate surveillance strategies in grocery stores to collect data related to customer preferences and behaviors. Actuators and advanced sensors have been installed in stores to accomplish offline data collection tasks. Furthermore, Internet of Things (IoT) based tools, Drones, and AI-based personal assistants are being used to transform customer actions into valuable data. This collected data acts as a driving force behind the price-setting algorithms that incorporates responses as per customer demands and reacts accordingly [1].

The potential showcased by AI and Big Data to generate new insights to facilitate product, process, and business innovations have made the adaptation of these technologies, a business priority.
