# The eBiz AI-platform in action

The AI-system developed by eBiz is a digital platform that their clients can use when building and developing their online webshops. The AI-modules runs in the background, and it scraps the web for competing products as to automatically adjust prices on the products their clients are selling over the web. At the current moment the platform has algorithms for data scraping and processing, and the system can act based on this (i.e. implement “intelligent pricing” for their clients). At the current moment the platform handles automatic and intelligent pricing of 600.000 items (web shop products), and these adjustments are made continuously in relation to competitors pricing strategies, and in relation to a wide set of factors.

The AI-system operates along a simple cycle of 1) input to the system (the e-retailers product feed), 2) scraping (of the web, and downloading of selected competitors products and prices, and 3) the processing of this data (to automatically adjust the e-retailers prices, and the further processing of this data as input to the e-retailers ERP systems).
