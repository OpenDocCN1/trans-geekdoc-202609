# AI vendors and small businesses

It is evident that insufficient data in the hands of small businesses coupled with small investment outlays they may have, makes it infeasible to build and develop their own AI systems independently. Lesser data and limited financial outlays with small businesses hinder the in-house development of AI based automation tools.

But, the small businesses can still adopt the AI based automation tools procured from AI vendors. AI vendors are the full time developers of AI systems for the industry segments they have specializations in. AI vendors pool small data from the businesses processes from different small businesses they work with and then the collected data is aggregated to build and train a standalone AI system for all the businesses falling into a particular market segment.

This way, a small business can benefit from the automated data of the HR department of another small business even if the former doesn’t have an HR function division. An AI system procured from an AI vendor may further help a small business to adopt automation in their business decisions and processes without investing on in-house AI development.
