## Smarter Agriculture: The Emergence and Role of Big Data in Agriculture & Allied Sectors

Data mining is about the mining of the data and it includes the discovery patterns in large data sets with the help of artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistics and database systems, and in this context Big Data and Analytics plays a vital role. Precision agriculture is possible with Big Data Analytics applications to ensure profitability, efficiency and these are ultimately helpful in future decision-making. Applications and managing of fertilizers, chemicals, crops, seeds etc. are possible with Big Data Analytics. Big Data Analytics in agriculture is about capturing relevant data from a huge number of sources and making actionable information for healthy pre-agriculture and post-agricultural business processes. With this, real-time insights can be possible to get, and by the advanced analytics even emerging weather etc. are also possible to gather. In facilitating better marketing, big data connectivity is useful, and thus companies seeking the benefits of Big Data Analytics. Here allied technologies are also useful like robots in controlling, analysis and planning in various agro related aspects, and in this regard agri-tech companies are providing various advanced approaches. The emerging autonomous vehicle can be connected with Big Data to measure soil moisture, even including nutrient, including predictive weather, image-capturing by the satellites, use of the drones etc., to know about the crop health [5], [29], [34].

These insights are extremely important since they tell the farmer when and how much to irrigate a field, crop health, weather predictions, pest infestations and even drought conditions. Considering the increasing labor shortages, finding out also becomes possible with big data, and therefore in manpower aspects also big data provides healthy advantage for agriculture. Emerging technologies become the urgent aspect in modern companies and start-ups, and there Big Data has made serious advancements. Though it is the fact that the vast majority of farmers are concerned about the soil health and trying to ensure sustainable farming, in this regard Big Data and Analytics are important and urgent. The satellite image of a plot of land can also be considered as important in agriculture, and in this context the geospatial approach and satellite monitoring connected with big data would be suitable for their decisions. As information is considered as power and valuable, therefore, in the agricultural industry also Big Data is useful in decision making including in precision farming. Further, with the help of Big Data Analytics, proper food and agro product tracking may be effective using sensors and analytics, and results in proper supply chains. The big data revolution has the potential for value creation for the farmers and also in industry, different stakeholders involved, etc. [10], [32].

## Suggestions and Facts

Information Technology is one of the important gifts from applied science and deals with the collection, selection, organization, processing, managing, dissemination of the information. Big Data is applicable in diverse areas in agriculture powered by other technologies and is growing rapidly.

- The *Efficient Agriculture Systems* become easy using Big Data Analytics supported Agro Informatics; here agricultural monitoring including agro based products management becomes possible effectively. Hence Big Data Analytics applications could be useful and applied.
- *Expansion of the Agro Systems* including commercialization of the agriculture, cleaning and purity including managing pesticides and fertilizers etc.
- *Speedy Agricultural Systems* with Big Data and allied technologies become possible in effective agriculture. Here use of robotics, analytics, HCI can be considered as important in digital agricultural systems.
- *Quality Production, i.e.* in the pre-production, big data is helpful in soil, crops, weather management, drone monitoring systems; and in this regard Big Data Analytics can be opted as important.
- *Quantity Enhancement of Agro Products* with real-time monitoring can be possible with Big Data Analytics in agriculture, and it will be helpful in enhancing the agro products, crops, plants. Even in harvesting, medication etc., and in this way Big Data Analytics can be helpful in quantity enhancement of agro products.
- Regarding the post production of the agricultural, Big Data Analytics can help in *Livestock Management* like supply chain management, commercialization etc., and in this regard Big Data Analytics can be considered as a need of the hour.
