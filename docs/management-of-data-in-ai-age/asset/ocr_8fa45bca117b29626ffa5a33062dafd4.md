# 4. Towards a forth approach? A call for Applied
Artificial Intelligence

Although we now have the AI technology in place, and as
we have it accessible as ready-made AI components that can
be easily integrated in systems and digital services, there
might still be a need for an additional approach here. If we
now have the technology in place, the next question to tackle
has to do with how to put it to use in different contexts? To
address this question, I suggest that we need to approach it
from the viewpoint of “Applied Artificial Intelligence”.
Although there is nothing new about the fact that AI should
be used, there is still no explicit approach developed that has
as its particular aim to focus on the process through which it
can be put to work. In this article I suggest that on a business
level, we should not just treat this technology as a tool, but
we should strive for more full-scale implementation and
integration projects where whole business models are built
around this technology as a core business component.
