# • **AI in Recommendation systems**

Retail companies are using the latest technologies i.e. artificial intelligence, machine learning to keep them ahead in the competitive market. These companies are utilizing recommendation systems on buying behavior of customers to increase the user interaction by gathering the details of their purchase preferences and enhance their shopping potential. Amazon.com makes use of recommendations on its website to gather the customer’s information regarding previous purchase, products viewed and accordingly it recommends the products to their users. It makes use of recommendation algorithm such as Collaborative filtering on item to item which gathers the information from the user purchase and compiles the list of recommendation from them so that the next time a user is thinking about any latest technology, the Amazon web page will suggest the latest device or gadget for its users. Another example is of Netflix which senses which movie or show is of your interest and you want to watch next.
