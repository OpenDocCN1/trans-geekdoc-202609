+   - **AI in Banking sector**

Due to rapid growth in online mobile banking service, the popularity of AI in banking is increasing where it is maintaining a high confidential dataset of user’s data [17]. AI powered chat bots are helping the customers to provide the services. Chatbot named Erica- A virtual financial assistant was piloted by Bank of America in the year 2017 which made available in June 2018 to download the Erica app. Erica is developed using AI and natural language to help the users to access their bank information, balance enquiries, transfer money etc. Through this app, customers are alerted using predictive analytics and on the basis of the average monthly spending of customers about their spending. If the spending of customer likely to bring their balance low, then in advance they are alerted. Customers were also reminded of their recurring payments and it helps them on how they can spend money on any purchase.
