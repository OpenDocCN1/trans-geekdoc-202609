In focusing on applied AI, and what it can offer we have in this paper argued that it is time to leave the previous “human-like” models of AI behind, and redirect our focus on what today’s “beyond human level” AI systems can offer. With this as an important point of departure we have in this paper explicitly focused on how AI can work, at the level of a global business, as a core platform component, and as core business.

As stated by Zook & Allen (2003) “*the most successful companies were able to consistently, profitably outgrow their rivals by developing a formula for pushing out the boundaries of their core businesses in predictable, repeatable ways*”. In the case we have presented in this article they are doing this expansion with and through AI. By aligning the development of their business model and the development of an AI system that perfectly reflect this business model they illustrate how AI can work as a core business component. Further, by having this business component installed, the system can provide them with realtime feedback on how the business performs. It can present analysis of the data the system already has, and since AI is to a large extend also a probability technology it can offer quite precise estimations of what will happen next.
