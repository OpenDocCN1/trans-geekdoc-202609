# C. Collaboration of Artificial Intelligence and Big Data in Business Innovation

Until before the evolution of Big Data, organizations were completely unaware of the power of data being generated by them on a continuous basis. The traditional business methodologies adopted by organizations were overlooking the potential of data in formulating business strategies. With the advent of new technologies to cater to the potential of data - both unstructured and structured, organizations are now adopting in-house data analytics tools to improve their business models. [1]

Big Data is much more than a mere collection of data as articulated by many researchers. The notion of three Vs: Volume, Velocity, and Variety defines Big data in a sophisticated way to gain insights that result in better decision making and improved business strategies.

In 2001, after investigating the challenges, issues and potential generated from tremendous amount of data, Gartner developed the ‘3V model’ that characterizes big data in context to three attributes:

> “Big Data is high-volume, high-velocity, and/or high-variety information assets that demand cost-effective, innovative forms of information processing that enable enhanced insight, decision making, and process automation.”
