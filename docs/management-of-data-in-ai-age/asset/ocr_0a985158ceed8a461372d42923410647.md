*Agro Sector Development* is theoretical in nature and aimed with following objective and agendas (but not limited to)—

+   - To learn about the basic of the Agricultural Informatics including origin, evolution and development.
+   - To know about the Agricultural Informatics and its applications in functioning towards advanced Agricultural systems.
+   - To get the idea of emerging Agricultural Informatics as an academic programs, nationally and internationally.
+   - To learn about the emerging Information Technologies in Agricultural Informatics and allied areas.
+   - To know about the Big Data including allied areas and with its features and characteristics in brief.
+   - To dig out the emerging applications of the Big Data and Analytics in Agricultural and similar areas.
+   - To find out the Big Data and Analytics applications in the Agricultural Informatics with the issues, challenges and potentialities.
