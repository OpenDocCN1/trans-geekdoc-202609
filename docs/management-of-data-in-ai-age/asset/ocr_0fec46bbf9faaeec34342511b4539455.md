# How can data science help small businesses?

Small businesses have small data. Generally, it isn’t difficult to manually process the lesser quantity of data but making this data, how smaller it can be, work for one or more objectives at the same time may save time and efforts of the small businesses generally having small teams to focus on relatively more critical business processes.

Having data analytics people or just having an understanding of data relevance and analysis can help small businesses get an edge in their market segments.

# Small business functions having prospects for AI, machine learning and data science

The AI based automation tools and data analytics can be used for real-business problems in marketing, sales, customer relations, billing & accounting, after sales customer experience etc. All these business functions are vital and pervasive in every small business and there are a number of large businesses providing AI powered support services for all these functions that small businesses may use.
