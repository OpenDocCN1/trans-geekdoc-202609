The concept of Artificial Intelligence is old but has gathered enough popularity recently. Fig 1.1 shows some of the milestones in the history of AI. From the year 1950 till date, there is a vast improvement and developments in the field of AI. In 1950, Alan Turing has proposed a Turing test to check the ability of machines to work intelligently as humans [16]. He suggested why can’t machines work and think in a similar manner as human being does. The term Artificial Intelligence was first coined in the year 1956 by John McCarthy at Dartmouth conference which led to the development of first AI program. Then he developed LISP programming language for AI. In the year 1961, first industrial robot was developed which replaced humans on the assembly line. Then in the year 1964, first Chatbot named ELIZA is developed which was an early NLP (Natural language processing) program to have a communication between humans and machine. During the years 1974 to 1980, there was no research and development done in the field of AI due to shortage of funds from the government and that time period was the first AI winter duration when no invention was made. After the AI winter duration, the invention came with the development of Expert system and then so many inventions in the field of AI continued till date with Siri, Google Now, Amazon echo etc. as shown in the figure below.

Earlier we had very small amount of data which was not capable to predict the accurate result. These days we have tremendous increase in the amount of data. Even the statistics suggest that by the year 2020, data will increase from 4.4 ZB to 44 ZB. Along with the large amount of data, these days we have high end computational power of system and large storage to store this vast amount of data. Thus, it is expected that more than 70 percentage of the enterprise will utilize Artificial Intelligence.

Artificial Intelligence is defined as a study of intelligent agents which can perceive, analyze and understand its surroundings and act accordingly to achieve its objectives [1]. AI is a technique which enables the machine to act like humans by replicating the behavior and nature [14]. Thus, it allows the machines to learn from past experience and perform task on the new input as humans do [16]. It allows the machines to train on the specific task by processing large amount of data and recognize patterns, this kind of intelligence is also known as Machine learning [8].

Thus, AI is a broader umbrella under which comes the subfields Machine learning and Deep learning. In our day to day life we all use artificial intelligence i.e. from voice-powered personal assistant such as Apple Siri, Alexa, to Netflix, Pandora, and Tesla self-driving car are some of the examples of AI which is based on Natural Language processing, deep learning. Thus, AI is making our lives better and efficient in many unforeseen ways. Fig 1.2 depicts the various approaches in Artificial Intelligence and how it can be used in various fields. Humans speak and listen and they converse through a language which is a field of speech recognition which is based on statistics, thus is called statistical learning. Humans can read and write text in a language which is a field of Natural language processing. We humans can see with eyes and process what we see and this is the field of Computer vision which is symbolic way of computers to process the knowledge and information. Humans have the ability to see patterns such as grouping of similar objects which is a discipline of pattern recognition and Machines are even better at recognizing pattern where the field of Machine learning came into picture. The brain of humans is a network of neurons which helps us to learn and memorize things. If we can recreate the structure and function of human brain then the machines will have the cognitive capabilities like human brain.

This is the field of neural networks which are complex structures which we can use to learn complex things which is a field of Deep learning. Thus, AI can be used in the field of Machine learning, expert system, natural language processing, robotics, pattern recognition, decision making, prediction, clustering and classification etc.

Machine learning is the most common type of Artificial Intelligence and the subset of AI which plays a major role in the development of business to process huge amount of data. It mainly focuses on designing systems and training them so that they can learn to make predictions based on learning from past experience. It deals with the extraction of patterns from the data set which helps in prediction.

The working of machine learning mainly focuses on training where the system is trained using Machine learning algorithm on labeled/ unlabeled data. Input data is passed to the ML algorithm and then the predictions are made. The prediction is evaluated for accuracy and if it is acceptable then only the ML algorithm gets deployed else it is trained again and again with the augmented data set.

Another is deep learning, which is subset of Machine learning [8] which utilizes ML algorithms to train deep neural network for achieving better accuracy where former was not working well. Thus, AI is a broader part allowing machines to execute in a smarter manner like human beings and ML and Deep learning are its subparts which are shown in the figure 1.3.
