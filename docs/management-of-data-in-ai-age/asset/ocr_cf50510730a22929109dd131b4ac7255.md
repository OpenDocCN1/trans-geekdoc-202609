In agriculture, precision farming is the area gaining importance where images are captured through drone for in-depth analysis and scanning of field, monitoring of crops to generate real-time alerts to farmers to improve efficiency in farming. There are various implementation of AI in agriculture such as FarmBot, Blue River technology, Prospera. Farmbot was implemented in the year 2011 utilising precision farming technology for growing crops at own place. It helps for end to end farming from planting seeds to testing soil, detecting weeds, watering plants using open source software system. In Agriculture industry, the major concern is herbicides resistance which has been responsible for financial loss of worth $43 billion of American farmers. In response to this big financial loss, Blue River Technology has launched a weed spraying machine – The See and Spray robot as a solution to reduce the effect of chemicals on the crops. This robot utilizes AI tools, cameras, sensors to manage the field by the farmers in the way they want. This machine uses deep learning algorithms and is taught with huge data set of around 10 thousand different plant and it sprays in the field after testing the soil and the crops.
