# Introduction

Agricultural Informatics deals with both Agricultural Science and Information Science. Therefore Agricultural Informatics is an interdisciplinary field. Moreover ‘Information Science’ & ‘Agricultural Science’ are also interdisciplinary and very broad field. Agricultural Informatics is gaining rapidly for the developing not only agricultural systems but also environmental systems. Agricultural Informatics is dedicated in higher productivity in Agricultural Informatics including ecological systems [1], [12], [26]. In pre production activities Agricultural Informatics is widely applicable moreover additionally in post production of the agriculture job as well Agricultural Informatics is effective and useful. Agricultural Informatics is needed in developing agricultural practice and has developed advanced and productive agricultural systems. The emerging smart agriculture is many ways depends on the better and healthy Agricultural Informatics practice. Due to its importance educational programs and research programs on Agricultural Informatics have been started by many universities, internationally.
