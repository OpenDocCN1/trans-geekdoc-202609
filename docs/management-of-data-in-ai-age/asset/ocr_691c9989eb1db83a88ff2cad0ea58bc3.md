+   - **Sentiment Analysis:** Sentiment analysis is being widely used as a social media and internet monitoring tool. It encompasses Natural Language Processing in order to comprehend and analyze the responses received on social media platforms like Twitter, Facebook, Instagram etc. in context to business messages published. It helps to examine the attitude, emotional state and feelings of the consumer engaged in posts and commenting related to a business posts. The product reviews posted on social media, sentiment are used to identify whether consumers are satisfied or dissatisfied with a product. Similarly, a business organization could use sentiment analysis to measure the impact of a new product, ad campaign or consumer’s response to recent company news on social media. [1]

Sentiment Analysis is also known as opinion mining. It is implemented through a combination of Natural Language Processing and statistics by assigning values based on positive, negative and neutral sentiments in the text which in turn helps to identify the underlying mood of the writer with respect to context (happy, joyful, worried, sad, annoyed, etc.) This application of NLP helps business organizations in gaining insights of consumers’ reactions and moods.
