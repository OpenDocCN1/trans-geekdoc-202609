3) **_AI as a core business component._** Existing approaches to AI views it as a technology or a “tool” to be developed. In the case we have reported in this article it is not possible to distinguish between the technology being developed and the business model. Instead, the AI-system is the core business, and it is through the in-house development of this system, and by running the system, that also the business develops. In short, this illustrates a 3rd wave AI-case where AI is being applied in society to work for its customers, as well as for the business developing this AI-system.

# Moving Forward with Applied Artificial Intelligence!

As we have now moved from the 1st and 2nd wave of Artificial Intelligence, towards the most recent 3rd wave of developments (Xu, 2019) several things have also changed over the last few decades. We now have AI technologies that can be applied to real-world problems, and there is no longer a single focus on developing AI for the purposing of getting a better understanding of the brain and how it works (and related high-level questions of what “intelligence” and “reason” is). Instead, the contemporary focus is much wider, and it includes questions on how to make use of AI in society, and questions concerning how it can be applied to real-world problems.
