Accordingly, and as AI is now increasingly applied, we suggest that it is now time to address issues related to “Applied Artificial Intelligence” (AAI) and accordingly expand the focus from technical matters of making AI work (as a technology), to making AI work in the context of its use and implementation. With this as our central point of departure we propose the term “Applied Artificial Intelligence” (AAI) and we define it as *the study, design, development, implementation and use of Artificial Intelligence technologies to address real-world problems.* In this paper we propose this term in an attempt to look beyond the technological developments surrounding the area of AI, and as an effort to redirect our focus towards what this technology now enables, for consumers as well and for businesses when AI is now increasingly applied in real-life settings.
