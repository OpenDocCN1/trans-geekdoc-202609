The concept of Artificial Intelligence is old but has gathered enough popularity recently. Fig 1.1 shows some of the milestones in the history of AI. From the year 1950 till data, there is a vast improvement and developments in the field of AI. In 1950, Alan Turing has proposed a Turing test to check the ability of machines to work intelligently as humans [16]. He suggested why can’t machines work and think in a similar manner as human being does. The term Artificial Intelligence was first coined in the year 1956 by John McCarthy at Dartmouth conference which led to the development of first AI program. Then he developed LISP programming language for AI. In the year 1961, first industrial robot was developed which replaced humans on the assembly line. Then in the year 1964, first Chatbot named ELIZA is developed which was an early NLP (Natural language processing) program to have a communication between humans and machine.
