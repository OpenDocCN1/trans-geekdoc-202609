## Application Areas of AI

As artificial intelligence is gaining importance in today’s world, it is very crucial to solve complex problems in an efficient manner. Using this cognitive intelligence technology, machines are working in a smarter manner and are being used in many areas, which are discussed below:

- **Healthcare** — As discussed in the case study, AI has a major role in the field of healthcare, where it is growing day by day and becoming advantageous for the healthcare industry. AI can be used to help doctors perform better monitoring and diagnosis of the patients.
- **Social media** — Facebook, Gmail, Twitter, Snapchat are some of the applications using AI to store and manage the data.
- **Construction** — The construction sector is widely using AI applications, which help in giving suggestions related to designing of buildings and help in giving recommendations for safety features. It will help the engineers perform tasks in a more productive manner.
- **Education** — AI is helping students and teachers make the education system smarter by creating a virtual learning environment to deliver the education in the best and smartest manner.
- **Agriculture** — It is known that in the future, our planet will need more food than is currently being produced. Thus, AI would be a solution to this ongoing problem. PEAT is an application which has been developed to test and analyse the properties of soil through scanning the recorded images. Thus, it will help in detection of infections in the crops and will help in crop management practices to maximize their output.
- **Data security** — AI can be used in cyber attacks, which are growing in this digital world, as there is a lot of data in every field. Thus, securing the data is a crucial requirement, where AI can help to discover the bugs, failures, and cyber attacks. AEG (Automatic Exploit Generation) is software used in smart devices to protect against security breaches.
- **Robotics** — In robotics, AI plays an incredible role, which can help in the world of business to perform tasks like humans through programming. Sophia and Erica are examples of robots developed so far. Sophia was developed in 2016, imitates the gestures and facial expressions of humans, makes conversations, and does analysis like humans.
- **Finance** — AI-based solutions are helping the finance sector make smarter decisions in various credit and financial processes to add more value and to save time. An example includes the robo-advisor Wealthfront, which tracks the accounts of customers and analyzes how they invest and makes decisions using AI, and gives recommendations in a better manner.
