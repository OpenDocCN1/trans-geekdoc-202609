# **B. Role of Artificial Intelligence in empowering Business models**

We all have witnessed the way global digital transformation, in the past decade, has significantly changed the business models around the world. Not only organizations, digitalization has also deliberately affected the lives of individuals and their work styles [II]. The use of digital technologies and underlying data has entirely transformed the way consumers and industries interact. Moreover, the blend of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and emerging technologies has revolutionized the commercial sector in a way how consumers and industries interact, framing new business strategies to enhance revenue generation and many more.

It is evident from the recent studies that the inclusion of AI technology in framing business strategies has been proved to be highly beneficial for different top-notch companies like Facebook, Google, Uber, Amazon, Alibaba, Tesla, etc [I]. Using AI technology, these companies have gained a competitive edge in the market in comparison to those who failed to adopt AI in their business models. However, in addition to the inclusion of AI technology in framing innovative business models, the top company executives also need to encapsulate an entrepreneurial and ingenious mindset to remain competitive and creditable.
