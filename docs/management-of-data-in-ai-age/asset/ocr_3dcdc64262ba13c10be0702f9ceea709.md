+   1) ***AI as a background technology.*** During the 1\u02e2\u1d57 and 2\u207f\u1d48 wave of AI developments the focus was very much on AI as a foreground technology. The focus was on developing an intelligence machine that we can interact with (e.g. the Eliza system, the DeepBlue chess playing system, or a robot).
