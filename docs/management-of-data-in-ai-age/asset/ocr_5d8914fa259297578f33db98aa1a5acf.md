With a point of departure taken in this recent shift in the development of AI that we suggest that it is now time to also 1) shift our analytical lens – from the individual (and human-like) to the large-scale (and beyond human), to 2) to explore the kind of artificial intelligence that AI can offer today, and to 3) explore this from the perspective of AAI – Applied Artificial Intelligence.

In this paper we do this exploration with a particular interest in understanding how *applied artificial intelligence* might be designed to support multiple stakeholders (in our case consumers and businesses), and how applied AI might work in the creation of new digital services for consumers, as well as how AI can be used as a core business component. Our turn to applied AI is motivated by the increasing adoption of AI to real-world problems, and studies that have most recently shown how AI technologies are becoming increasingly practically relevant (see e.g. Gomes et al., 2019 and Ågerfalk, 2020).

In this article we present how AI has developed over the past few decades, and across three waves of developments, and we illustrated Applied Artificial Intelligence by presenting our e-Biz corp case where a global actor is now applying AI as a core component of their online business.
