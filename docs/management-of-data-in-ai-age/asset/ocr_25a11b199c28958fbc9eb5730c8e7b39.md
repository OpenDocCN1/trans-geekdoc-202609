# Applying AI as A Core Business Component – The Ebiz Corp Case

Artificial intelligence is increasingly a background technology that is integrated in digital platforms, products and services – ranging from the integration of AI-components in search engines and streaming services, to the use of AI in healthcare (Rahwan, et al., 2019).

In our research project we have worked in close collaboration with an e-Biz company where we have followed their development of one such AI-based platform that operates in the background to support “intelligent pricing” for online web shops. In adopting the methodological perspective proposed by Rahwan, et al. (2019) we have followed this development during the last 11 months, and we have followed both the development of the technical AI-system, as well as the CEO, the Sales manager and the technical development team during this period in order to gain an informed understanding of how they develop their core business through the development of this AI system.

In our research collaboration with the eBiz company we have also been inspired by the work by Zook and Allen (2003) on processes that governs the growth of the business core, the work by Strassmann(1990) on how to align the development of IT with the development of business values, and the work by Zook (2007) on how to further develop the core business through the development of digital solutions.
