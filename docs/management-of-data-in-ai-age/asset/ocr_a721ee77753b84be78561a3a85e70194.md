Now, we come across the issue if the mere availability and quantity of data are the only basis of an effective AI or machine learning tool. Not entirely but largely yes. To train an AI based tool, we need more and more buckets of data. This data needs to be fetched from a gamut of processes and operations taking place across a particular industry or business organization. Why we are stressing here on the data coming from a particular industry or a business organization. The answer lies in the concept of relevance of data that is further based on the effectiveness of data science or analytics capabilities of a business unit.

Here come the small businesses in the discussion of this chapter. Small businesses are obviously having fewer market offerings generally operating in niche markets and thus have small data in hands especially in the infancy stages of their business. Even small businesses with established business presence and market for their offerings still have small data with them.

The small data may not be sufficient to build, develop and train an AI system. Furthermore, a small business may not have data for some business processes entirely that other small businesses may have. There are a larger number of small businesses not having HR departments, legal compliances divisions and other functional divisions that large businesses have.
