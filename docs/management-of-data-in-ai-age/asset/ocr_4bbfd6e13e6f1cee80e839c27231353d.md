Finally, and in adding to Xu’s (2019) definition of useful AI as “*an AI solution that can provide the functions required to satisfy target users’ needs in the valid usage scenarios of their work and life*” we would like to expand on this notion of “Useful AI”, and suggest that it needs to work, not only for users/consumers, but also for the businesses providing the service. In order to successfully apply AI to real-world problems it needs to work, for its users, and for those who provide digital services based on applied artificial intelligence.
