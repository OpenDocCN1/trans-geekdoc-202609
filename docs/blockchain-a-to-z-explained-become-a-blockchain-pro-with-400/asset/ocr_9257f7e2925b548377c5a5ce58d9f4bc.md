# Advance Comments on A2Z Glossary

Professor Lisa Short

DUniv., Grad. Cert Sc., Tch T&D, Dip. T&A Sys., Adv. Dip. OHS, BA (Economics, Geog., Fin. Mgt.) Dip. Ed.

Founder, P&L Digital Edge Ltd [UK] and Mind Shifting

The speed of digital change is so great that we now have ‘revolutions’ that take less time than a generation. Blockchain is a foundational technology of the Fourth Industrial Revolution that has jettisoned us into Society 5.0 and onto a vertiginous transformation curve that for some sectors is forty faster than it was a year ago. It is heralded along with Artificial Intelligence and the Internet of Everything as shifting simple networks based on how we connect and transfer information to smart networks that have the capacity to transfer value. It isn't just about speed and efficiency; it is about changing behaviour, redefining and redesigning the way we conduct business and are enabled to live our lives.

Blockchain is about valorising trust. Created in the aftermath of the 2008 global financial and trust crisis, it has become the building block of a brave new world where we have replaced our fundamental sense of governance and assurance with a technology that to be honest many don't understand.
