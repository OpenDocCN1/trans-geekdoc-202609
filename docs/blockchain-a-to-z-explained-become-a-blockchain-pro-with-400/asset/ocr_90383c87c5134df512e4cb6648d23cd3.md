# $Whale

$WHALE is a social currency launched in May, 2020 as ERC 20 token. It is backed by tangible and rare NFT assets, while promoting scarcity through limited issuance and driving values thereby. It has a maximum supply of 10 million coins. It value proposition is based on 1. The Vault, 2. Strong Brand, 3. Community, 4. Tokenomics, 5. Leadership and 6. Vault storing digital art and collectibles
