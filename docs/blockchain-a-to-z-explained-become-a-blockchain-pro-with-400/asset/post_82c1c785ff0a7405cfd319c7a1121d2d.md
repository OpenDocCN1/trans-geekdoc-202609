# Prologue

Professor Lisa Short

DUniv., Grad. Cert Sc., Bch TQD, Dip. TQA Sys, Adv. Dip. OHS, BA (Economics, Geol., Fin. Mgt.) Dip. Ed.

Founder, PL& Digital Edge Ltd [UK] and Mind Shifting

The speed of digital change is so great that we now have ‘revolutions’ that take less time than a generation. Blockchain is a foundational technology of the Fourth Industrial Revolution that has jettisoned us into Society 5.0 and onto a vigorous transformation curve that for some sectors is forty faster than it was a year ago. It is heralded along with Intelligence and the Internet of Everything as shifting simple networks based on how we connect and transfer information to smart networks that have the capacity to transfer value. It isn't just about speed and efficiency; it is about changing behaviour, redefining, and redesigning the way we conduct business and are enabled to live our lives.
