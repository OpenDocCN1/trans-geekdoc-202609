# NFT (Non Fungible Token)

A non-fungible token (NFT) is a unit of data on Blockchain digital ledger, where each NFT can represent a unique digital item; hence they are not interchangeable. NFTs can represent digital files such as art, audio, videos, items in video games, virtual assets and other forms of creative work. The NFTs can be bought on an NFT marketplace. NFTs have led to a significant growth in Digital Collectibles also referred to as Crypto collectibles. Cryptokitties, Cypherpunk, Topstops are some of the leading NFTs. They are sold on various market places – NBA Top Shot (Basket Ball), Sorare (Soccer), Decentral Land (Virtual Real Estate), Axie Infinity (Video Games).
