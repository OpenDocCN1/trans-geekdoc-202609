# ERC_721 (Ethereum Request for Comments_721)

ERC721 tokens, more commonly referred to as Non-Fungible tokens (NFTs) allow developers to tokenize ownership of any arbitrary data, digital assets or digital collectibles there by increasing the design space of what can be represented as a token on the Ethereum blockchain. They were proposed by William Entriken, Dieter Shirley, Jacob Evans and Nastassia Sachs in January 2018.
