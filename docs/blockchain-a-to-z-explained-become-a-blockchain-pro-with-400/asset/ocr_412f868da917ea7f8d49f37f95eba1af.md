# Escrow

Escrow is a legal arrangement in which a third party temporarily holds large sums of money or property until a particular condition has been met. Escrow services are now playing a major role in the field of Blockchain. Escrow can always pay obligations or debts, with the help of signature funds, allocated to pay those debts. Escrow's main responsibility is to help dispense money or documents as a neutral third party in different exchanges. BTC Asia, IBC Group, Bitrated andGlobal Escrow are well known Bitcoin Escrow service providers.
