# Hexadecimal

The hexadecimal numeral system, often shortened to “hex”, is a numeral system made up of 16 symbols (base 16). The standard numeral system is called decimal (base 10) and uses ten symbols: o,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9. Hexadecimal uses the decimal numbers and six extra symbols A, B, C, D, E and F. It is very compact and makes conversion with binary easy. In Blockchain world, 256 bit hash is expressed as a number in hexadecimal format.
