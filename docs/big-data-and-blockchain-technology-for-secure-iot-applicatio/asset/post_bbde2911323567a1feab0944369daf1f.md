## 4.2.2 Blockchain technology in smart cities
Within this subsection, an overview of modern Blockchain technology, including its concepts and characteristics, is provided. It explains the principles of decentralization, immutability, and consensus mechanisms in Blockchain. The use of Blockchain technology revolutionizes countless industries with its ability to enhance transparency, strengthen security measures, optimize operational efficiency, reduce costs, and build trust [24].

The concepts and functions of Blockchain have substantial effects on the future of information management and digital trust, as seen by its notable application in cryptocurrencies.

### 4.2.2.1 Core principles of Blockchain technology
Blockchain technology operates on a decentralized peer-to-peer network, typically represented by nodes. Unlike traditional systems that rely on a central authority or intermediary to validate and record transactions, Blockchain technology operates via a decentralized network of participants.

### 4.2.2.1.1 Decentralization
The process of decentralization offers many benefits. Firstly, the use of Blockchain technology improves transparency by ensuring that all participants within the network have equal access to a shared ledger that is continuously updated in real time. Moreover, it enhances security by eliminating any single points of failure [24]. Rather than relying on a single entity, the verification of transactions is achieved through the network's consensus, thus significantly hindering the ability of a malicious actor to control the system.

### 4.2.2.1.2 Distributed ledger
The basic concept underlying Blockchain technology centers on a decentralized ledger. The ledger acts as a comprehensive record of all transactions within the network, arranged sequentially through the creation of blocks, where each block contains a set of transactions. Linking blocks to form a sequential chain is responsible for the term “Blockchain.” Every participant within the network maintains an identical copy of this ledger [25]. The distributed ledger system ensures consistent access to the transaction history for all participants, thus significantly hindering any attempts to alter or delete information without obtaining consensus from the entire network.

### 4.2.2.1.3 Immutability
Once a transaction is recorded in a block and added to the Blockchain, it becomes extremely difficult to modify or remove. The immutability of Blockchain technology can be attributed to the use of cryptographic hash functions. Each block consists of a set of transactions and a unique cryptographic hash of the preceding block. This process creates a secure chain of interconnected blocks that, once built, exhibits a high level of resistance to any tampering attempts [26]. Altering a single block requires modifying all subsequent blocks, a computationally impractical process.
