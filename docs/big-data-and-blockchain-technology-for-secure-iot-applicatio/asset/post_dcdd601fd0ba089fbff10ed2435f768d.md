
## 4.2.3 Key features of Blockchain technology

### 4.2.3.1 Security and transparency

The high degree of safety and security in Blockchain is credited to its decentralized and unalterable qualities. Changing a single block would certainly demand the alteration of all succeeding blocks on the Blockchain, which is a computationally impractical task. The use of modern Blockchain technology improves its resilience versus illegal activities and cyberattack attempts. The inherent transparency of Blockchain technology allows all participants to gain access to and observe the total transaction history [27]. Although specific transactions are pseudonymous, meaning alphanumeric addresses identify them, the data remains accessible and verifiable. This attribute can be useful for objectives like accounting and ensuring accountability.

### 4.2.3.2 Smart contracts and speed and efficiency

The usage of modern Blockchain technology makes it possible to assist with smart contracts, which are agreements that are self-executing and have terms that are clearly encoded into the underlying code. These legal contracts are made to implement autonomously upon the fulfillment of pre-programmed conditions, therefore reducing the need for intermediaries in varied operational processes [28]. The application of modern Blockchain technology can substantially boost the speed and performance of transactions, especially in cross-border payments and supply chain management.

### 4.2.3.3 Accessibility

The promise of modern Blockchain technology expands past finance and leads to revolutionary changes in a number of areas, such as healthcare, supply chain management, voting systems, and others. The concepts of decentralization, immutability, and transparency, paired with qualities such as smart contracts, add to the reinforcement of security, effectiveness, and accountability in varied applications [28]. Nonetheless, it is critical to challenge challenges such as scalability, energy efficiency, and regulatory frameworks to realize Blockchain technology’s capabilities fully.

## 4.3 INTEGRATION OF BLOCKCHAIN AND SECURE IOT APPLICATION

In the 21st century, smart cities have become a transformative force in contemporary urban planning. The intensifying international urbanization trend needs cutting-edge options to deal with the difficulties of traditional metropolitan facilities. Difficulties such as ineffective resource management, rapid population growth, increased resource demands, security and privacy issues, and the importance of sustainability have actually pushed cities to explore sophisticated technologies [29]. Among these, Blockchain and IoT have emerged as promising technologies, holding enormous possibilities to change smart city development. Incorporating Blockchain and the IoT in smart cities represents a paradigm shift in urban planning and development. We explore the integration of these two transformative technologies in this section, delving into their strengths and examining how their integration can deal with crucial obstacles traditional urban infrastructure face. With a comprehensive analysis of the benefits, this section aims to provide an understanding of the profound impact of joining Blockchain and IoT together in smart cities [30].

### 4.3.1 Decentralized data management

Blockchain represents an important framework for decentralized and tamper-proof data management in smart cities. Incorporating it with IoT ensures that all transactions and data originating from IoT devices are securely recorded on an immutable ledger, therefore safeguarding data integrity and preventing unauthorized modifications from malicious actors [31] Given the extensive real-time data generated by IoT sensors and devices, Blockchain facilitates secure management without dependence on a central authority, mitigating data manipulation risk.

### 4.3.2 Secure identity and access management

Blockchain’s secure and decentralized identity management capability is a foundation for enhancing security and privacy within the smart city ecosystem (Khalil et al., 2022). An increased degree of security is attained by enabling residents, devices, and entities to store their identities on the Blockchain. This integration additionally addresses IoT devices’ authentication and authorization requirements, giving a robust framework for managing identities and access permissions [32]. This strategy ensures secure communications within the smart city network, strengthening the system’s overall resilience.

### 4.3.3 Smart contracts for automation

Incorporating Blockchain and IoT presents smart contracts that automate and implement predefined rules, removing the need for intermediaries. In smart cities, these contracts are essential in automating varied processes such as energy payments, waste management, and traffic control. IoT devices with sensors and actuators can trigger events based on real-world data. For example, integrating IoT and Blockchain-based contracts in tourism financing can streamline processes, automatically performing actions when specific conditions are met [33]. This integration improves efficiency and ensures sustainable economic practices in the tourism sector.

### 4.3.4 Supply chain transparency

Blockchain’s transparent and traceable ledger finds applications in supply chain management, making sure the secure recording of transactions connected to manufacturing, transport, and distribution of goods. The synergy with IoT sensors in the supply chain allows real-time data generation on items’ location, condition, and status [34]. When combined with Blockchain, this data ensures transparency and accountability, alleviating the risk of fraud and optimizing the overall efficiency of the supply chain.

### 4.3.5 Energy trading, data security and privacy

Blockchain enables peer-to-peer energy trading, developing a decentralized and transparent method for energy transactions. In the IoT world, smart meters and sensors monitor energy consumption and production in buildings. Incorporating this data with Blockchain makes it possible to make accurate and transparent settlements and implement dynamic pricing models [35]. This encourages energy conservation and promotes the use of renewable energy sources, largely at home. The secure and decentralized Blockchain design enhances data security, with cryptographic features ensuring encrypted access only for authorized parties. The sensitivity of data collected by IoT devices makes protecting privacy paramount [36]. Blockchain offers a decentralized control system, significantly reducing the risk of unauthorized access or tampering.

### 4.3.6 Citizen-centric services

Blockchain enables transparent and secure citizen services, extending tasks like voting, property registration, and healthcare records. Blockchain’s transparency improves reliance on government services by equipping residents with better control over their data. Citizen engagement is additionally boosted with IoT devices collecting feedback and real-time information, with Blockchain integration ensuring the authenticity of citizen input [37]. This fosters more accountable and responsive governance.

### 4.3.7 Real-time monitoring and analytics

As a secure and transparent framework, Blockchain facilitates the recording and sharing of real-time data. This becomes particularly valuable in applications where data accuracy and integrity are extremely important. IoT devices continually generate real-time data on various urban operations. Integration with Blockchain allows the use of this data for analytics, decision-making, and optimization of urban services, specifically in smart healthcare [38]. Therefore, collaboration between government agencies, private enterprises, and technology companies is necessary to incorporate Blockchain and IoT in smart cities efficiently. Standards and protocols for interoperability, security, and data privacy should be developed to ensure a seamless and secure smart city ecosystem.

## 4.4 IOT SECURITY AND PRIVACY CONCERNS

Incorporating Blockchain and IoT in smart cities ensures the transformation of data management and governance. Nevertheless, this synergy presents a range of security and privacy challenges requiring careful examination. This section examines these challenges, using a theoretical exploration of the potential pitfalls and necessary safeguards.

### 4.4.1 Immutable data and access controls

The fundamental immutability of modern Blockchain technology, which ensures data integrity, becomes a double-edged sword when considering the integration of Blockchain and IoT in smart cities. While this feature protects against data tampering, it raises significant concerns regarding the permanence of sensitive information. Within the complex ecosystem of a smart city, the inability to modify or delete recorded information presents potential risks to long-term privacy for individuals [38]. Developing robust access controls and privacy-preserving mechanisms is critical to navigating this challenge effectively.
