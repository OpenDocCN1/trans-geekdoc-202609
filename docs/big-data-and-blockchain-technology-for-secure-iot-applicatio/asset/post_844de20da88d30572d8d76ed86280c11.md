
The use of Blockchain and modern technology plays a critical function in strengthening information integrity within this vibrant ecosystem. Blockchain’s inherent property of immutability ensures that once information is recorded on the Blockchain, it cannot be changed or destroyed. In the context of IoT devices, this immutability property is particularly essential as it safeguards the credibility and reliability of the information gathered [53]. Each transaction or item of information from an IoT device is safely recorded in a block, and the decentralized and distributed nature of the Blockchain ensures that no single entity has control over the whole system, reducing the risk of unauthorized modifications. This development relies on the data generated by IoT devices. It provides a robust framework for numerous applications, such as smart contracts and transparent data-sharing systems in smart city infrastructure [54]. The guarantee of information integrity with Blockchain integration not only enhances the integrity of information but also contributes to the overall security and reliability of smart city systems.

### 4.5.3.2 Decentralization
Decentralization plays an essential role in strengthening the durability of smart city systems by implementing Blockchain technology. By distributing the control and storage of information across a network of nodes instead of relying on a central authority, Blockchain alleviates the risk of a single point of failure [55]. In the context of smart cities, this decentralized design ensures that if one node or component fails, the system as a whole remains operational. This increased resilience is particularly critical in urban settings where the integrity of information and systems is vital for different services, such as transport, energy management, and public safety [18].

### 4.5.3.3 Efficiency gains
The use of Blockchain and IoT technologies in smart cities can substantially boost operational efficiency. With its decentralized, safe and secure nature, Blockchain ensures the integrity and immutability of information collected from IoT devices. This is especially important in a smart city context where substantial quantities of information are produced from varied sources such as sensors, cameras, and other connected devices [56]. One crucial element contributing to performance gains is the transparent and automated execution of smart contracts. These self-executing contracts, made possible by Blockchain, can automate various processes in smart cities, ranging from energy management and waste disposal to traffic management. For example, smart contracts can facilitate automated payments for energy use based on real-time information from IoT-enabled meters, eliminating the need for intermediaries and improving the billing process. Furthermore, the decentralized nature of Blockchain eliminates the reliance on a single central authority, reducing the risk of system failures or data breaches [18]. This decentralization, paired with the secure and transparent audit trail provided by Blockchain, enhances the overall integrity of smart city operations.

### 4.5.3.4 Transparent governance
Transparent governance is a crucial aspect of integrating smart contracts on the Blockchain within the context of smart cities. Smart contracts, self-executing pieces of code with predefined rules, provide transparency and automation that can revolutionize governance processes. The immutability of Blockchain ensures that the terms inscribed in these contracts are tamper-proof, fostering trust among stakeholders [5,9]. This transparency minimizes the risk of illicit activities and allows people and authorities alike to trace and verify every step of a process. Automated execution of contracts further enhances governance, reducing the need for intermediaries and minimizing the possibility of human errors [59]. As smart cities progress, implementing smart contracts becomes a cornerstone in developing responsible and efficient governance frameworks, establishing a standard for a new era of transparent and automated public administration.

## 4.6 FUTURE DIRECTIONS
The future directions in incorporating Blockchain and IoT in smart cities hold tremendous potential for transformative developments. Research undertakings should consider exploring hybrid Blockchain solutions that integrate the strength of public and private Blockchains, offering a nuanced approach for scalability and privacy concerns. Exploring the synergy between Blockchain, IoT, and edge computers can alleviate latency issues and enhance real-time processing capabilities. Addressing security and privacy challenges remains critical, focusing on developing innovative methods to secure the sensitive data produced by IoT devices.

Future studies should also explore energy-efficient consensus mechanisms to reduce the environmental impact of Blockchain in smart city applications. Standardizing interoperability between diverse IoT devices and Blockchain systems is essential for seamless integration. Exploring the application of smart contracts for automating city governance processes and enhancing transparency in supply chain management represents promising avenues. In addition, scientists should contribute to the development of flexible regulatory frameworks to fit the evolving landscape of Blockchain and IoT technologies in smart cities. Interest in user-centric design, ethical considerations, and the integration of expert systems will collectively shape a sustainable and inclusive future for smart city applications.

## 4.7 CONCLUSION
Blockchain technology is driving a digital transformation, transforming how we perceive and manage trust and information in a connected world. Decentralization, distributed ledger technology, immutability, and consensus mechanisms are its cornerstones, changing transaction verification and data security. Decentralization allows peer-to-peer networks to accept and record transactions without a central authority, challenging hierarchical structures. This step will enable individuals to trust each other directly, eliminating intermediaries. The distributed ledger maintains an integrated and immutable transaction history, promoting transparency, and deterring fraud. Blockchain’s immutable ledger and cryptographic protection provide unparalleled data integrity and cybersecurity. Transparency and smart contracts enhance accountability and automation across industries, transforming commercial transactions. Blockchain’s efficiency can accelerate processes, reduce operational costs, and promote innovation in finance, supply chain management, healthcare, and governance. Blockchain is currently changing markets, yet scalability, power consumption, and legal frameworks remain concerns. It ensures the development of trust, transparency, and efficiency in our dynamic digital landscape. It is the foundation of a decentralized, secure, and trust-based future that will transform our digital age with lasting implications.
