# 7.2.2 Overview of clustering algorithms

In the contemporary era, the clustering technique in data analysis is widely employed to address the emerging challenges associated with big data. This analytical approach involves partitioning a dataset into two subsets, where one subset comprises similar instances and the other encompasses dissimilar instances [3]. Various clustering methods are utilized for this partitioning process, including bi-clustering, density-based, graph-based, grid-based, hard clustering, hierarchical, model-based, partitioning, and soft clustering. The primary objective of clustering is to group data points into clusters based on their similarities, distinguishing one cluster from another.
