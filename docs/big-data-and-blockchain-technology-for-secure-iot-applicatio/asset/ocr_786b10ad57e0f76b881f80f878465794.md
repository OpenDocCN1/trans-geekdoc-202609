# 10.3 TAXONOMY OF BLOCKCHAIN-BASED DEEP LEARNING FRAMEWORKS

This taxonomy discusses the organization of blockchain-based DL frameworks. DL algorithms and blockchain technology combine to provide a number of benefits, such as automated decision-making, data protection, precise forecasting, effective data market management, and enhanced system resilience. In this part, a thematic taxonomy is presented to classify, according to various criteria, the unionization of DL techniques and blockchain.
