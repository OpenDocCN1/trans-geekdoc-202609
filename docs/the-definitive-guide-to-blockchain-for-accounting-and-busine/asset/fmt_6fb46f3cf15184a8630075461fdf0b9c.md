
The tokenization of real estate can also add value to the supply side of the Japanese real estate market. As noted in the prior section, rising real estate prices are one of the top reasons why fund managers would overlook Japan. The rapidly inflating real estate prices can mostly be attributed to a limited supply of investment-grade real estate. Through tokenization, real estate assets can be pooled together and repackaged into interest-bearing digital securities. Diversification allows default risk to be spread, leading to an increase in the supply of investment grade real estate opportunities.

![image](images/C10-FIG15.jpg)

The tokenization of private real estate funds would be another application that would increase investment liquidity within the Japanese real estate market. As noted earlier, private Japanese real estate funds have an average investment period of 5+ years, while recently launched funds have an estimated lifespan of 9.1 years. These extensive fund periods keep capital locked up for an extended duration, which deters some investors from contributing capital to private real estate funds. This is especially discouraging, as private real estate funds often have opportunities to invest in high quality properties, driving outsized returns. Another downside of private investment funds is the high potential for a J-Curve effect ([Fig. 10.16](#CH010.html#fig16)), where fees are charged and returns are slow to come in as committed capital is not drawn down in the early years of the fund, due to a lack of viable investment opportunities.

By increasing real estate transparency, opening access to the international investment community, and providing greater liquidity, digital securities can ultimately contribute to maximizing price efficiency within the Japanese real estate market. As such, we believe that tokenization would drive further capital deployment to Japan from overseas investors. Furthermore, the Japanese real estate industry would be well ahead of its international counterparts when it comes to preparing for the new digital economy of Web 3.0.

### 10.11. CONCLUSION

Leaders throughout financial services believe that blockchain technology can transform the global financial system, serving as an underpinning to the financial infrastructure of the new digital economy. By increasing data efficiency, transparency and security, DLT can modernize or even replace processes typically performed by intermediaries. When coupled with smart contracts, DLT can bring about the next great evolution in financial product creation with asset tokenization.

Digital securities allow for the standardized issuance and ongoing transfer of tokenized assets on DLT systems. Similar to other novel financial transformation mechanisms of the past, tokenization will change the structure of once illiquid and uneconomical assets, making them available to a broader pool of global investors. Other unique innovations, such as built-in regulatory controls, will eliminate the need for wasteful compliance costs, further expanding the universe of assets that can be issued and traded.

We began this chapter with the goal of exploring the potential impact that tokenization might have on the Japanese real estate market. Much of the allure of DLT systems involves uniting parties around the world in common enterprise, further incentivizing information sharing to facilitate cross-border transactions. Digital securities offer a framework for achieving this goal, while also adhering to time-tested laws and regulations of the current world order. This study has outlined the benefits that asset tokenization can offer not just to Japanese real estate fund managers, but broadly to the global financial community, as society and markets prepare for the coming era of digitized finance.
