
The mining and mineral sector, a sector that is notoriously slow to adopt new technology, is moving rapidly to incorporate blockchain technology. As discussed in the next chapter, blockchain applications are being developed for recordkeeping and sourcing in the mineral industry. Here, we briefly discuss an innovative way that the mining industry is obtaining financing for its projects by leveraging blockchain and smart contract technology.

Mining is a capital intensive industry, requiring significant up-front investment and it is fraught with economic uncertainty. Economic volatility on raw material prices in the commodity market has been prevalent since the 2008 global financial crisis. The mining boom, pre-financial crisis, was fueled by rapid growth and industrialization in developing countries. However, since the financial crisis there has been a steep decline in both end demand and up-front credit availability. While many other sectors have recovered over the past decade, the economic recovery has not been as pronounced in the mining industry. A lower than expected growth rate in China, continuing debt problems in Europe, and tightening of credit worldwide have all impeded the recovery. The decline in commodity prices leading to falling returns and income has created a mismatch between miners’ long-term investment horizon and investors’ short-term return horizon. Consequently, miners have recently encountered a challenging environment to raise equity capital to fund ventures.

Against the backdrop of falling profits, tightening credit and greater investor demands, the mining industry is looking for creative financing options to fund their growth projects. They are combining traditional financing options with alternative financing sources.^[²](#CH008.html#fn2) Mining royalty and metal streaming financing have been particularly popular with miners as a financing source allowing access to early-stage capital without diluting equity ownership. A mining royalty is a right to receive payment based on a percentage of mineral production or of the revenues or profits generated from the sale of those minerals. The royalty-financing typically involves an up-front payment to the mining company from the investor, in return for a contractual undertaking by the mining company to pay a specified percentage of future revenue for a specified period.

The structure of royalty-financing is well-suited to implementation through smart contracts and consensus mechanisms. Hence, mining companies are combining the royalty-financing model with the digital wrapper and offering security tokens to investors. Moreover, the stream financing model – a metals prepayment structure – is amenable to tokenization. Thereby, the application of the leading-edge technology to an age-old problem generates an innovative solution that is beneficial to both parties, the investors and the mining company.

## 8.7. CONCLUSION
Businesses in need of capital are seeking out new financing options. For private offerings there are two options: a traditional private placement or STO. After performing a cost–benefit analysis, some businesses are deciding that a STO provided the most value to its stakeholders.

A STO presents some fundraising benefits, such as lower up-front costs, streamlined administrative procedures, condensed process timelines, as well as an expanded base of potential investors who were given the option to contribute both fiat and digital currencies over the internet. The streamlined process can be completed within several months, with the preparation of documents and legal reviews requiring the most time. In the case of Curzio Research, they were able to raise their target of $3 million within the first two weeks of the offering and a total of $4.1 million from over a hundred investors. The traditional mining industry has also employed this new vehicle to raise capital to fund new ventures.

A STO not only provides a cheap and quick way to raise funds, it also provides logistical advantages over the life of the tokens. In the case of Curzio Research, by operating within Securitize’s DS Protocol Ecosystem, the CEO tokens are expected to reduce compliance and operating expenses typically associated with a financial instrument’s lifecycle events. The tokens also provide utility, as users can access Curzio Research’s web-based content via its online platform. After the lock-up period, CEO token investors should realize enhanced liquidity from the designated STEs. Investors can also utilize Securitize’s platform to manage token-related events in one place, as these developments are recorded on distributed ledgers throughout the digital securities ecosystem.

### 9.1. INTRODUCTION
The good management of global business activities has become more challenging as society advances in its understanding of the impact of our activities on each other and on our planet. Business activities that a generation ago were unfettered, undocumented or unregulated are now becoming better understood, scrutinized, documented and regulated, and the consequences of undesirable activities are now much more visible. The term “responsible sourcing” has been used to describe the desire to assure sourcing that doesn’t compromise human values (such as using child labor or forced labor), harm society (such as supporting illegal or criminal activity, or forcing land resettlement) or negatively impact the environment (such as causing deforestation or chemical pollution).
