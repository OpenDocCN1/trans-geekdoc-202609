There are two things to note. The first is that I definitely did not click on the ‘download Zoom’ link. The second is to note the bottom message on the screen about Zoom_5a9bf98c8b046661.exe. I did not either run or save it. As previously noted, the fact that Zoom came up was from a previous install via a different browser.

So Zoom is very ‘helpful’ about installing onto your machine. As a malware researcher, I’m not sure how I feel about that. On the one hand, you can probably offer the meeting to anyone with a browser, regardless of whether they have a Zoom account or have ever used Zoom. (This does seem to be contradicted by another friend, who tried, rather desperately, to join a Zoom meeting but without any success.) On the other hand, as a drive-by download, it works great, and I’m not really thrilled about having stuff installed on my machine with lots of access that I never gave it.
