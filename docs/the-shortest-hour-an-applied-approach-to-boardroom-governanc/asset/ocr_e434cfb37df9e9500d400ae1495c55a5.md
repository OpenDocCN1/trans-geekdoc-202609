| Policy management | Policy structure | Does the company have a robust cyber security policy program in place?<br><br>Are the documents separated by policy, procedure, standards, guidelines, etc., or are they all combined in one long hard to read document?<br><br>Are the policies written (as much as possible) in language that is easy for employees who may not have technical knowledge to understand?<br><br>Are policies brief, succinct, and reference more detailed documentation such as procedures, standards, and guidelines?<br><br>Are the policies easy to access by all employees?<br><br>Is the policy management program automated to reduce multiple versions of documents being sent back and forth via email?<br><br>Is there a waiver process in place that allows for temporary relief from mandates to allow users/teams to come into compliance? |
