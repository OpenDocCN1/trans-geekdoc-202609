
## Question 99

A newly-hired Chief Information Security Officer (`CISO`) is faced with improving the security of a company with low morale and numerous disgruntled employees. After reviewing the situation for several weeks, the `CISO` publishes a more comprehensive security policy with associated standards. Which of the following issues could be addressed through the use of technical controls specified in the new security policy?

1.  Employees publish negative information and stories about company management on social network sites and blogs.
2.  An employee remotely configures the email server at a relative’s company during work hours.
3.  Employees posting negative comments about the company from personal phones and PDAs.
4.  External parties cloning some of the company’s externally facing web pages and creating look-alike sites.

**Answer: B**

## Question 100

When Company A and Company B merged, the network security administrator for Company A was tasked with joining the two networks. Which of the following should be done first?

1.  Implement a unified `IPv6` addressing scheme on the entire network.
2.  Conduct a penetration test of Company B’s network.
3.  Perform a vulnerability assessment on Company B’s network.
4.  Perform a peer code review on Company B’s application.

**Answer: C**
