## 3.2. Optimization of absorber

The band alignment between the absorber and ETL plays a role in controlling recombination at the interfaces, which in turn affects the open-circuit voltage ($V_{oc}$). By adjusting the affinity of the absorber layers affects metrics such as $J_{sc}$, fill factor (FF), and power conversion efficiency (PCE). Fig 7 shows the effect of Electron affinity on various PSC parameters. The results vary from 3.5 eV to 6 eV and an optimal value device exhibits optimal performance when the electron affinity is 0.4 eV (e.g. 3.5 eV), a spike appears when affinity is too low, which acts as a barrier to hole flow at the absorber/ETL interface, thereby increasing recombination, leading to lower PCE and FF as shown in [35,36]. However, optimization of band offsets is necessary to enhance perovskite solar cell (PSC) performance achieved efficiencies of 22.17% and 21.86% respectively when combined with ZnO. Therefore, CBTS is suggested as best HT material, offers a tunable band gap and absorption of light. Its non-centrosymmetric crystal structure and atomic size make it ideal for the enhancement of PCE of solar cells [24,34].

The defect density ($N_t$) in the absorber layer was varied in increments of 10 while maintaining the thickness at 600 nm. All parameters for the ETL, HTL, and absorber were changed. The findings reveal that high defect densities led to a lower cell efficiency. A PCE of 23.76% was observed for $N_t$ of $10^{14}$ cm$^{-3}$, after which started to decrease and a significant drop in PCE was observed above $N_t$ of $10^{15}$ cm$^{-3}$. A similar trend was observed in case of FF and $V_{OC}$ as shown in figure below. Meanwhile a significant drop in $J_{sc}$ was observed after $N_t$ of $10^{16}$ cm$^{-3}$.

From these conclusions we can optimize the device value with high $N_t$ of $10^{14}$ cm$^{-3}$ which gives a PCE of 22.17%. The defect concentration in the active layer is a crucial factor in determining device efficiency. High defect levels result in recombination rates, leading to issues such as film degradation, formation of pinholes, and reduced stability, all of which negatively impact the performance of the device [17].
