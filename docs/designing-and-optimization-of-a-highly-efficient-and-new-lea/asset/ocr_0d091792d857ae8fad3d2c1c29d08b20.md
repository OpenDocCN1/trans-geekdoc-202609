# 3.2. Optimization of absorber

The band alignment between the absorber and ETL plays a role in controlling recombination at the interfaces, which in turn affects the open-circuit voltage (Voc). By adjusting the affinity of the absorber layers affects performance metrics, such as Jsc, fill factor (FF), and power conversion efficiency (PCE). Fig 7 shows the effect of Electron affinity on various PSC parameters. The results vary from 3.5 eV to 6 eV and an optimal value. Device exhibits optimal performance when the electron affinity is 0.4 eV (e.g. 3.5 eV), a spike appears when y is too low, which acts as a barrier to hole flow at the absorber/ETL interface, thereby increasing recombination, leading to lower PCE and FF as shown in [35,36]. However, optimization of band offsets is necessary to enhance the perovskite solar cell (PSC) performance.

Barium thiophene, achieved efficiencies of 22.17 % and 21.86 % respectively when combined with ZnO. Therefore, CBTS is suggested as best HT layer material, offers a tunable band gap and absorption of light. Its non-centrosymmetric crystal structure and atomic size variations make it ideal for the enhancement of PCE of solar cells [24,34].
