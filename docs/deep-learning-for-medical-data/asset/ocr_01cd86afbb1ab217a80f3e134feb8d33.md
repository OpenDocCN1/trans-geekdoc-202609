# 2.4 CONCLUSION

We present a novel histopathology patch-classification model that outperforms a competitive traditional CNN by enforcing rotation and reflection equivariance. A derived patch-level dataset is presented, allowing straightforward and precise evaluation on a challenging histopathology task. We demonstrate that rotation equivariance improves reliability of the model, motivating the application and further research of rotation equivariant models in the medical image domain.
