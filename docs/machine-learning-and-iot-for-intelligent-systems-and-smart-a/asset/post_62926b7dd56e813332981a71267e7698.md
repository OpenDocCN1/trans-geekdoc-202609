From the proposed model, a recall score of 91% is achieved. As the medical systems are high-recall systems, which means a person with malignant cancer should not be predicted as benign case as this leads to the person's plausibly not going for further treatment. With image augmentation and the taking of more samples of the images, better recall score shall be achieved in future.
