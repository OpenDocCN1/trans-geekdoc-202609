Some of the bacteria develop resistance to antiviral agents or antibiotics; again, identifying antibiotic resistant genes, an organism can be developed which can render resistance to antibodies. Analogous to natural mutation, entire virlines may be created. By swapping out variant or synthetic genes or by inducing hybridization of viral straits, a new strain of influenza can be created.
