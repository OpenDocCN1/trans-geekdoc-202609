# DISCUSSION

This retrospective study showed that iDAScore Version 2.0 is able to predict FHB as well as, or better than, conventional morphological assessment. For transferred embryos with the same morphological grade, the FHB rate increased with the increase in iDAScore. This suggests that day 3 embryo selection based on iDAScore may achieve a higher FHB rate, and is safe and robust compared with conventional morphological assessment.

A significant difference in the quality of transferred embryos was found between SET and DET cycles. The majority of transferred embryos in SET cycles were of lower morphological quality compared with DET cycles. This may be related to the in-house procedure for SET allocation in the study clinic. Although there were no differences overall between SET and DET cycles in the observed FHB rate for embryos with KID within different iDAScore groups, it should be noted that the above analysis was based on embryos with KID, and the actual FHB rates were lower when DET cycles with only one FHB were included.
