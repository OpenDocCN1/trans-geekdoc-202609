# Distribution of embryos and FHB rate according to iDAScore

FHB rates were calculated based solely on embryos with KID, and thus did not include DET embryos with only one FHB. The iDAScore values of the embryos were grouped into score intervals of 1 (2.0–2.9, 3.0–3.9, 4.0–4.9, 5.0–5.9, 6.0–6.9 and 7.0–8.0). The distribution of embryos according to the iDAScore groups showed typically increasing numbers within the higher scoring groups for all cycles and DET cycles (FIGURE 1A). However, there were fewer transferred embryos with the highest iDAScore. As many patients only had one embryo available for transfer due to poor embryonic quality in their SET cycles, the ratio of transferred embryos decreased with an increase of iDAScore in this dataset (FIGURE 1A). Rates of FHB increased with increasing iDAScore in all groups, and thus additional analyses were conducted on all cycles, as the FHB rates in the SET and DET cycles were comparable (FIGURE 1B).

# FHB rate of iDAScore groups stratified by morphological grade

The overall FHB rate was 40.1% for all morphological grade groups, which rose with the increase of iDAScore to 25.91% in the 2.0–3.9 group, 43.88% in the 4.0–5.9 group, and 48.34% in the 6.0–8.0 group (TABLE 3). Furthermore, for subgroups of morphological grades A+, B+, B, C+ and C, the FHB rates with an iDAScore of 2.0–3.9 were lower than the rates in the higher iDAScore groups (TABLE 3) (A+ iDAScore 0.0–5.9 P = 0.015, all others P < 0.001, see Table S1, online supplementary material). Additionally, for the A+, B+, B and C groups, the FHB rates in the groups with an iDAScore of 6.0–8.0 were higher than those in all other iDAScore groups.
