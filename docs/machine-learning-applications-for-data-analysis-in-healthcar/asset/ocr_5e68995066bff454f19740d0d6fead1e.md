# ABSTRACT

The COVID-19 pandemic produced by severe acute respiratory
syndrome Coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2) has swept the globe, wreaking
unparalleled social and economic havoc. When compared to other coronaviruses such as SARS, it has high human-to-human transmissibility.
Predicting the future trajectory of the COVID-19 epidemic is critical to
developing appropriate preventative and control tactics promptly. In the
fight against pandemics, the ability to detect the pace where the virus
spreads is crucial. Monitoring the rate of spread at random, times may
aid public governments in planning human safety and policies to deal
with the pandemic’s effects. Recent research has proposed transmission
dynamic models to better explain viral dissemination in a given population and to provide preventative actions. The COVID-19 illness features
and a cumulative number of infections may be effectively analyzed using
time series models. Historically, epidemic propagation has been modeled
as a rate issue akin to fluid flow or heat conduction. The infection rate
must be known to simulate the spread of the disease, and the percentage
removal must be known to model fatalities and cures. This chapter highlights the introduction to the pandemic and the Facebook Prophet Model.
