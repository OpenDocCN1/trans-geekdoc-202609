### ABSTRACT

In recent years, the coronavirus has emerged as a highly concerning health issue for human beings due to its infectious nature and potential lethality. The virus is transmitted through exhalation, which spreads viral particles to surfaces that can be breathed in by susceptible individuals, leading to infection. Thus, it is critical to prevent ourselves and others around us from getting infected by practicing social distancing, hand cleanliness, and wearing masks to cover face in public. The widespread adoption of masks in public places globally highlights their importance in mitigating the transmission of the virus. The ability to identify people wearing masks is becoming increasingly important in public places. This research suggests a novel method for identifying face masks by combining the ResNet-50 model with a bilinear interpolation, bilateral filter, and fuzzy c-means segmentation. By improving details and segmenting the face and background areas in the input picture, our technique seeks to increase the reliability of face mask identification. We tested our approach on a publicly accessible dataset and found it to be effective, with encouraging results and high accuracy. Our method has potential applications in scenarios where face mask detection is required, such as airports, hospitals, and public transportation.
