### 8.6.4 IMPACT OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

AI is reshaping healthcare profoundly. It revolutionizes diagnostics by enhancing accuracy in interpreting medical images, aiding in timely and precise diagnoses. Operations in healthcare facilities are streamlined through AI-driven automation, freeing up healthcare providers to focus more on patient care. AI's ability to analyze extensive patient data enables personalized treatments and predictive analytics, foreseeing potential health risks. Moreover, it accelerates drug discovery and development, speeding up the introduction of new medications and therapies. Telemedicine solutions powered by AI facilitate remote patient monitoring, ensuring access to quality care, especially in remote areas. Its role in healthcare research is pivotal, analyzing vast datasets to identify patterns, and fueling medical breakthroughs.

### 8.6.5 THE CYBERSECURITY MESH

The Cybersecurity Mesh is an approach that aims to provide more flexible, adaptable, and responsive security measures across various digital environments. Rather than relying on a centralized security perimeter, the Cybersecurity Mesh focuses on creating a more dynamic and distributed security infrastructure. It does this by decentralizing security measures and extending protection to individual devices, people, applications, and services, regardless of their location. In the past, it was shown that more than 34% of the data were vulnerable to leaks and breaches within the network itself when security was focused all around the perimeter. With the Cybersecurity Mesh, many businesses anticipate improved cybersecurity. This security architecture was expected to be able to lower the cost effect of a security incident by 90%, according to some study.

### 8.6.6 CLOUD COMPUTING

Cloud computing in healthcare revolutionizes data storage and management, offering cost-effective on-demand services. It reduces capital expenditure, enhances operational efficiency, and simplifies tasks like sharing medical records and developing telehealth applications. The cloud ensures secure, scalable, and accessible storage, facilitating seamless collaboration among healthcare providers while streamlining processes for improved patient care and optimized resource utilization.

### 8.6.7 BLOCKCHAIN

Every time a linked device participates in a transaction of any kind, the blockchain system will produce identical blocks. A block is created on all devices to store the data locally whenever data on one computer is accessed, updated, shared, or otherwise processed. The medical industry has suffered greatly as a result of the inability to access and exchange sensitive patient data securely. But for true interoperability, blockchain will provide incredibly flexible openness while preserving the strictest security standards.

### 8.6.8 VIRTUAL, AUGMENTED, AND MIXED REALITY

Healthcare has seen significant technological advancements, especially with the adoption of virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality. These technologies hold immense potential for the healthcare sector in India. VR has shown promise in aiding vision rehabilitation, addressing conditions like cancer, autism, and depression. In mixed reality, the merging of virtual and physical realms offers clinicians a unique opportunity to analyze patient conditions more comprehensively. These advanced educational tools enable healthcare professionals to provide more precise treatments.

### 8.7 CONCLUSION

Healthcare technology is continuously becoming advanced in every aspect. Despite the industry’s improvements in security, evolving threats persist, hence necessitating a focus on prevention rather than reaction. This proactive approach is crucial to effectively manage these challenges. The quality and effectiveness of healthcare are expected to continue its improvement because of support and the evolution of the latest technologies like AI, machine and deep learning, and so on. Smart diagnosis facilitated by these technologies allows for a more accurate definition of a patient’s condition and disease status. This, in turn, enables the creation of personalized treatment plans. With the expert endorsement, the software can guide precise treatment pathways. It is imperative to collaborate with skilled software developers who understand the system’s requirements and objectives to achieve these advancements.

### ABSTRACT

The emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence (AI), the Internet of Things (IoT), blockchain, 3D printing, and 5G networking (referred as AIB35) in the context of “smart healthcare” are being greatly explored by “smart healthcare” providers to move forward in an all-encompassing manner. The field of healthcare requires scientists to implement intelligent solutions into the daily routines of “smart aging” patients to improve the efficiency and precision of diagnostic and therapeutic procedures. The concept of “smart aging” refers to a program that makes it possible for elders to have a superior quality of life by embracing high-end technology solutions to diagnosis and disease. Healthcare technologies are a wide range of innovations, including connected devices, appliances, and procedures, as well as drugs, medicine, and vaccine that improve the level and quality of care provided at economic cost structures. The purpose of this study is to first present a listing of the principal technologies that underpin “smart healthcare,” and then to present it in several significant domains. After that, we elaborate on the issues that now exist with smart healthcare and attempt to provide potential solutions to these issues. In the final part of this chapter, we take a glance into the road ahead and assess the prospects of smart healthcare toward building a smart society 5.0.
