# 6.1.2 HEALTHCARE SECTOR

Thinking about patients in a hospital is probably the most important area, both for how much money you can make and for the amount of work you have to do. Clinical consideration includes hospitals, medical tools, basic medical care, review appointments, virtual doctor visits, medical tourism, health insurance, and medical supplies. The healthcare industry in India is growing quickly because more and more people are using it. It has two main parts: public and private. The government provides basic medical services in rural areas and some cities, while the private sector offers more advanced medical care in cities and towns. India has many skilled doctors. India is cheaper and looks different compared to other countries in Asia and the West. An operation in India is about 10 times cheaper than in Western Europe. The Indian healthcare industry is expected to reach $193.83 billion by 2025. Increasing pay, better healthcare, more people getting sick from the way they live, and more people being able to get insurance are all important factors in progress. Healthcare services are becoming stronger in India. The money made from providing medical services increased by 17.16% to $739 billion in the fiscal year 2020. According to the Economic Setting Survey 2020–2021, the healthcare insurance industry saw more people getting insurance coverage in the fiscal year 2020. In the financial year 2020, 89% more families in Bihar, Assam, and Sikkim got medical services. In the year 2016, the country had 393 Ayurveda and 221 homeopathy government schools. In April 2020, there were 169,031 subcenters and 33,987 primary health centers. The business size was estimated to reach $372 billion by 2022.
