# ABSTRACT

Brain–computer interface is the term given to the process of converting human intents into patterns that may then be used to control equipment that is linked to the outside of the body. An electrocardiogram (ECG) is a visual representation of electrical patterns generated by the heart. ECG is a significant diagnostic tool widely used for people with cardiac issue. ECGs are often recorded on the chapter with a grid on them. The grid work on ECG recordings is made up of numerous small 1 mm square blocks. Each of these little boxes lasts around 40 ms. The transthoracic interpretation of electrical heart activity over time is known as ECG. Assessment of the ECG signal signifies the heart health. For the diagnosis of heart disorders, a number of techniques have been used, including fast Fourier transforms and wavelet transform.
