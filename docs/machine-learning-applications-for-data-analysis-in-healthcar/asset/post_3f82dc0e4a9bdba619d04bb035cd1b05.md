### 8.4.1.2 SECURITY OF DRUGS

The supply chain participants would be encouraged to conduct better testing, maintain better temperature control, and ensure the authenticity of their drugs by a blockchain’s transparency. This is because the blockchain tracking technology would make it possible for direct chain players. As a result, it would be easy to identify the location in the chain, for example, when one chain member committed fraud.

According to Schindler, “If I have any product, I can attach a virtual token to it, which is stored on a blockchain.” Therefore, this token must be given with the product. Let us imagine a business manufactures 100 units of a product, each of which has a digital token kept in a blockchain. For instance, they are given to the wholesaler, and this transaction is recorded in the blockchain. The wholesaler then transfers it to a different distributor, and once more, this transaction is recorded. If the distributor, for example, adds 200 copies of the product falsely, the blockchain would make it clear that only 100 copies were made, and the fake ones would not have a token attached to them. Therefore, it would be quite simple for any recipient of those shipments to doubt the legitimacy of the bogus goods. Additionally, the originating company might reconsider its business relationships in order to eliminate the fraudulent distributor from its supply chain.

In addition to maintaining the integrity of the supply chain, this visibility and ability to trace products might also enable pharmacists or even patients to view the data directly, giving them confidence in their medications.

## 8.5 SMART HEALTHCARE SYSTEMS DESIGN ASPECTS

Smart healthcare systems are crucial to design. Such systems have a variety of use cases and have many difficulties in the collaboration of involved advanced technologies. The following design aspects are necessary to take care while designing of smart healthcare systems.

### 8.5.1 COLLECTION AND MANAGEMENT OF DATA

Data gathered from the sensors on the patients’ bodies can be sent to the cloud using low-power nodes, such as their mobile phones. Machine learning algorithms can be deployed in the cloud itself using this enormous data, which will be produced quickly and will continue to increase in cloud storage. This gathered data will quickly take the form of big data and various big data tools would be required for handling it.

### 8.5.2 SECURITY FEATURES

Stringent access controls, including multifactor authentication, restrict system entry to authorized personnel only, enhancing data confidentiality. AI-powered threat detection continuously monitors system activities, promptly identifying and mitigating potential security threats or irregularities.

Adherence to stringent regulatory frameworks such as Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act and General Data Protection Regulation ensures compliance with data protection laws, prioritizing patient privacy and rights. Privacy-preserving techniques embedded within AI algorithms anonymize patient data during analysis, preserving confidentiality.

Secure APIs facilitate safe data exchange between different healthcare platforms or systems. Regular security audits and assessments identify vulnerabilities, ensuring system resilience against evolving cyber threats. Continuous training programs for healthcare staff on cybersecurity practices mitigate human error risks and unauthorized access.

### 8.5.3 PRIVACY ISSUES

Strictly using symmetric keys, medical IoT data and EHR are kept private. Only the encrypted healthcare data hash is present throughout the entire chain. Adversaries can only obtain encrypted data from nodes as a result.

Healthcare data are secured with a symmetric key that is additionally encrypted with the authorized user’s public key from a 2048-bit Rivest–Shamir–Adleman key pair. The healthcare data cannot be obtained by adversaries without the symmetric key. As a result, this plan offers a high level of data privacy.

## 8.6 FUTURE TRENDS FOR SMART HEALTHCARE SYSTEMS

Future of smart healthcare systems has the following trends:

### 8.6.1 INCREASING USE OF TELEHEALTH SERVICES

Telehealth offers a spectrum of services, including virtual consultations, remote patient monitoring, and telemedicine applications, fostering timely and convenient access to medical expertise. Its utilization spans various healthcare domains, ranging from primary care to specialty consultations, mental health services, and chronic disease management. Telehealth, facilitated by digital technologies, enables remote access to healthcare services, consultations, and monitoring, transcending geographical barriers. Particularly catalyzed by the COVID-19 pandemic, its popularity continues to soar due to its convenience, accessibility, and efficiency.

It uses technology to make things easier for everyone. Imagine having a doctor’s appointment online using your phone or computer instead of going to the clinic. It is like having a smart helper that keeps an eye on your health all the time. Moreover, it allows doctors to share information quickly, ensuring you get the best care possible. With digital health, people living in faraway places can also consult doctors without traveling long distances. Wearables and smart gadgets are able to monitor a patient’s every action, including their heart rate, calorie intake, sleep pattern, and activity duration. Wearable technology, for instance, can spot abnormal electrocardiographical patterns in patients and notify medical staff, lowering the risk of more catastrophic illnesses. They have enabled the methodical advancement of personalized healthcare, and they will continue to be crucial to the advancement of medical devices in 2022 and furthermore years.
