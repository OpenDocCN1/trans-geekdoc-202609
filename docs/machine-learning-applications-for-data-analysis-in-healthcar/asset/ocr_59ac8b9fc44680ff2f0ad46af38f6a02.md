# 6.2.6 VIRTUAL HEALTH ASSISTANT

Virtual health assistants (VHAs) can proactively help patients differently, for one, VHAs can support dementia patients by providing medication reminders, helping them stay on track with their prescribed treatments. Additionally, VHAs can offer guidance on managing common illnesses and provide personalized dietary recommendations tailored to patients' specific health needs. VHAs can also monitor patients using data-driven insights, enabling healthcare professionals to engage with both patients and pharmacies by sending reminders for medication refills and pickups. They can even recommend preventive health screenings. AI has also been employed to enhance clinical diagnostics—for instance, AI-assisted medical imaging from the Beijing-based company Infervision is currently used to improve the interpretation of CT scans and X-rays. Virtual Health Assistants represent a fusion of artificial intelligence and advanced decision-support systems, leveraging big data, NLP, and voice recognition technologies. These features contribute significantly to their ability to effectively engage and assist patients. Voice-empowered VHA will change the conveyance of medical services to patients through [9]:

+   - Navigate electronic health records
+   - Navigate diagnosis and prescription options
+   - Improve access to data health records
+   - Improve patient discharge and follow-up treatments
+   - Improve communication between providers
