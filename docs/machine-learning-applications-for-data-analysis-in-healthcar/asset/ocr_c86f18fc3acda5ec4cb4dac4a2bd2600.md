# 8.1.3 PIVOTAL INFRASTRUCTURE

It is possible to monitor the physiological conditions of the body with wearable sensors. Vital indications including body temperature, heart rate, and breathing rate can all be measured by the sensors. It is possible to add more blood pressure and blood oxygen sensors. The information from the sensor nodes is processed by a central node before being sent outside to another site. A specialized central node that only performs one function is preferred to reduce power consumption.

Healthcare is only now beginning to use artificial intelligence (AI) and related technologies. AI has already been proven to function at least as well as humans at critical healthcare tasks. AI is made up of a variety of distinct technologies. Digital data can be shared and recorded via blockchain technology without being altered.

# 8.2 CURRENT STATUS OF RESEARCH AND DEVELOPMENT IN SMART HEALTHCARE SYTEMS

Lots of research work is going on in the field of smart healthcare systems and their development. Chen et al. [1] worked on handling the emergency situation of patients and proposed the edge-cognitive-computing-based (ECC-based) smart healthcare system. Using cognitive computing, this system can track and examine the physical health of its users. Additionally, it adjusts each user’s health-risk grade when allocating computer resources throughout the entire edge computing network. The results of the trials demonstrate that the ECC-based healthcare system offers a better user experience, efficiently utilizes computing resources, and dramatically increases patient survival rates in an unexpected emergency. Uddin [2] suggested a smart system to monitor the patient’s activities without hospitalization and it is possible with the use of new technologies such as the Internet of Things (IoT), Edge of Things, and Cloud of Things. Recurrent neural network (RNN) technology on an edge device is used in this work to present a wearable sensor-based system for activity prediction (i.e., personal computer or laptop). The system’s input data come from a variety of wearable healthcare sensors, including magnetometer, accelerometer, gyroscope, and electrocardiography sensors. An RNN is then trained using the features. The RNN that has been trained is utilized to forecast the activities. In order to provide older persons, patients, and individuals with disabilities with a simple control system, Ismail et al. [3] presented an effective method based on voice recognition.
