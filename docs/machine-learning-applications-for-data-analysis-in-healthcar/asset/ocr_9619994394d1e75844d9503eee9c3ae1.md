The development of surgical robots has made it possible to do surgery at a higher level than ever before. Patients report better results and quicker recovery times as compared to traditional endoscopic surgery, and doctors have access to technology that provides them with greater flexibility and compatibility than traditional endoscopic surgery. The utilization of remote surgery will also result in greater levels of convenience (84%).

The utilization of technology that uses mixed realities makes the process of developing and carrying out the surgical plan more straightforward. Dr. Rajesh Wirth, a senior doctor of a reputed hospital in India reports that the virtual world, the real world, and the users of the system create a circle through matching results with the existing cases. He foresees that the development of this technology further will bring about revolutionary shifts in clinical practice, as well as medical education and research, as well as communication.

Chronic illnesses that are incurable, perhaps terminal, and expensive to treat require smart healthcare options. On the other hand, the conventional paradigm of health management, which is concentrated on hospitals and physicians, is unable to appropriately deal with massive data volumes. About 37% doctors responded favorably on the health management model that is now being implemented as part of smart healthcare and placed a greater emphasis on emerging patient autonomy in managing their own treatment. They reported that patients can themselves monitor and communicate instant feedback of health condition to doctor, leading to timely intervention and diagnosis. The development of smart gadgets that can be implanted or worn, as well as smart homes and health information platforms, all of which are connected by IoT technology, offers a solution to this problem (94%). Wearable and implantable devices combine advanced sensitive sensors, chips, leads, and wireless networks to regularly sense and monitor various physiological indicators of patients from time to time. These devices also reduce mental stress, give confidence to patients, and interact with other data available from diverse sources to reach expert conclusions. About 65% doctors suggested a transition to frequent monitoring and control procedures to obtain accurate diagnosis.
