Crizotinib is one of the major examples of the drug developed by using the concept of the SBDD [56, 57]. This drug has been regarded as a discerning and powerful c-Met dual inhibitor, which was Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approved in the year 2011 [58]. Thus, it has been proved that c-Met is a potent and striking target of oncology.

One of the FDA-approved drugs, Axitinib, has acted as a novel therapy for the treatment of renal cell carcinoma (RCC) [61]. The development of this drug was possible by utilizing the strategy of SBDD in which Axitinib served as an inhibitor on binding to the vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) kinase domain in the DFG phenylalanine configuration [62]. The family of VEGF has its occupancy in the signaling process which is further involved in the angiogenesis process. The signaling pertaining to VEGF was detected in the lump of the cells, and this process of signaling finds its role in the development of diseases that are in the malignant form. VEGF receptor serves as the ligand in the VEGF signaling process. These receptors are called as the family of tyrosine kinases called as FLT₁, KDR, FLK₁, and FLT₄. Blocking the mechanism of action of these receptors with the pan-AKT kinase inhibitor against the kinases family have been proven to be an effective manner of developing drugs against angiogenesis. ## 7.5 ADVANTAGES OF IN SILICO DRUG DESIGNING OVER EXPERIMENTAL TECHNIQUES

CADD has far more superior outcomes as compared to conventional wet lab techniques. CADD techniques provide a higher ratio of identifying compounds with the desired properties and increasing chance of a compound overcoming the barriers of preclinical testing. It reduces the need for animal testing and has shortened the timespan required for HTS of millions of molecules. Structure- and ligand-based methods are the predominantly applied tools in the drug discovery domain but the future lies in the upcoming synergistic techniques such as proteochemometrics. Combinatorial chemistry and proteomics have been infused to lead to remarkable results in the CADD era. Integration of omics technologies and high-scale accelerated development of machine learning algorithms has reduced the timespan of processing. Sophistications in cloud computing, data mining, data management, big data, and artificial intelligence have strengthened the data storage and data retrieval capacities in biological databases leading to accentuated virtual screening and molecular simulations. Metagenomics and metatranscriptomics have made it possible to analyze bulk environmental samples in one go and further add up to the availability of bulk-level genomic sequences and transcriptomes available on the servers. CADD is way ahead in drug designing and future lies in the biological data processing and integration with high-end computational technologies.

## 7.6 CONCLUSION

The future of drug discovery and development lies in higher advancements in CADD techniques related to virtual screening, lead identification, optimization, molecular docking, pharmacophore modeling, and molecular modeling. Identification of target structure, validation, and screening of lead molecules has become easier with available databases and tools like 2D and 3D QSAR. Life-threatening diseases like cancer can be combated in a better way using these advancements. There is still a need to further develop simulation algorithms with better efficacy than molecular dynamics simulation and Monte Carlo approaches.
