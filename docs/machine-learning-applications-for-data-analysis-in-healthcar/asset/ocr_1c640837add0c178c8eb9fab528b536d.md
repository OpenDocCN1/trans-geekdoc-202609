| S. No. | Types | Definition/Explanation No. |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| i. | VHD/HVD | The VHD/HVD comprises of stenosis, prolapse, and regurgitation. The stenosis occurs due to narrow opening of valves. Improper closing of valves leads to prolapse. The backward leaking of blood causes regurgitation |
| ii. | Pulmonary embolism | The blockage in one of the pulmonary arteries in the lungs leads to pulmonary embolism |
| iii. | CAD/CHD/IHD | The narrowing of heart arteries (supply blood to the heart muscle) is the cause of IHD/CHD/CAD |
| iv. | Congenital heart disease | This type of HD occurs due to septal defect hole between two heart chambers. It also occurs due to obstruction defect which blocks free flow of blood. The Cyanotic HD is due to shortage of oxygen. This congenital HD is present since birth |
| v. | Cerebrovascular disease | This type of HD affects the blood vessel as well as the supply of blood to the brain |
| vi. | Peripheral heart disease | If the blood vessel supplying to the arms and legs are affected, it gives rise to peripheral HD |
| vii. | Cardiomyopathies | If the muscle of the heart is affected, it leads to cardiomyopathies |
| viii. | Rheumatic heart disease | The streptococcal bacteria damage the heart muscle and valves. This gives rise to rheumatic HD |
| ix. | HF | The HF occurs if the heart does not pump blood properly around the body. It may be due to the effect of left and right side of the heart |

Notes: VHD: Valvular heart disease/HVD: heart valve disease, CAD: coronary artery disease, CHD: coronary heart disease, IHD: ischemic heart disease, HD: heart disease, HF: heart failure.
