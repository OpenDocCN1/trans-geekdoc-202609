# **FIGURE 8.3** Pharmaceutical supply chain management procedure.

+   - Step 1: When a new treatment or medication is developed, a process begins involving patent protection and extensive clinical testing. These data are recorded in a digital ledger, serving as a transaction entry.
+   - Step 2: After completing clinical trials, the manufacturing facility obtains a patent for prototype testing and large-scale production. Each product receives a unique identification linked to additional blockchain data.
+   - Step 3: Post mass production and packaging, medicines are stored in warehouses for future distribution. The blockchain records details like timestamps, lot numbers, barcodes, and expiry dates.
+   - Step 4: Transportation specifics, such as travel duration between warehouses (in), mode of transport, authorized agents, and related particulars, are also logged in the blockchain.
+   - Step 5: Third-party distribution networks transport drugs and medical supplies to healthcare providers or retailers. Utilizing in and out warehouses linked to all distribution endpoints, blockchain updates with new transactions.
+   - Step 6: Healthcare facilities like hospitals and clinics input data (batch and lot numbers, product owners, and expiry dates) into the blockchain to authenticate and prevent counterfeiting.
