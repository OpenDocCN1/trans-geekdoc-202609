## ABSTRACT

The COVID-19 pandemic produced by severe acute respiratory syndrome Coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2) has swept the globe, wreaking unparalleled social and economic havoc. When compared to other coronaviruses such as SARS, it has high human-to-human transmissibility. Predicting the future trajectory of the COVID-19 epidemic is critical to developing appropriate preventative and control tactics promptly. In the fight against pandemics, the ability to detect the pace where the virus spreads is crucial. Monitoring the rate of spread at random times may aid public governments in planning human safety and policies to deal with the pandemic’s effects. Recent research has proposed transmission dynamic models to better explain viral dissemination in a given population and to provide preventative actions. The COVID-19 illness features and a cumulative number of infections may be effectively analyzed using time series models. Historically, epidemic propagation has been modeled as a rate issue akin to fluid flow or heat conduction. The infection rate must be known to simulate the spread of the disease, and the percentage removal must be known to model fatalities and cures. This chapter highlights the introduction to the pandemic and the Facebook Prophet Model. The chapter will also enlighten the readers about the proposed AI-based framework to predict the pandemic.

### 2.1 INTRODUCTION TO THE PANDEMIC (COVID-19)

The recent pneumonia outbreak [1], which began in early December 2019 in Wuhan City, Hubei Region, China, is triggered by a new coronavirus (CoV) recognized by the World Health Organization as “2019-nCoV” or “2019 novel coronavirus” or “COVID-19.” CoVs [2] get their title from the protruding spike proteins that give them a crown-like structure. The biology of the virus depends on spike constituents. The spike component is the part of the virus that attaches to a human cell and permits it to infect it, allowing the virus to multiply and spread to other cells. The 2019–n-CoV’s exact origin, source, and biological storage are unclear, although it is suspected to be pathogenic and that bats may be to blame owing to sequence similarities to the bat-CoV [5, 13]. According to previous [3] epidemiologic studies into severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS)- and Middle East respiratory syndrome (MERS)-CoV, the bat is the major reservoir, the palm civet or raccoon dog is the intermediate (or susceptible) host for SARS-CoV, and the dromedary camel is the dromedary camel for MERS-CoV.

When an infected person breathes virus-containing droplets and very minute particles, COVID-19 [4] spreads. Others may inhale such particles and particles, or they may resolve on their eyes, nostrils, or mouths. In some cases, they may invade surfaces they meet. Individuals being within 6 ft of an infected person are at a high risk of contracting the illness. According to current information, COVID-19 has a gestation period of 2–14 days. This happens when an individual is healthy after 14 days of interaction with a confirmed COVID-19 carrier, they will not be harmed. This chapter [5] stresses the use of blockchain in future pandemic prevention. It also covers a number of blockchain-based applications that could help the fight against COVID-19.
