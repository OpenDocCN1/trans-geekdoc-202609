Chapter 1 of this book investigates the application of machine learning techniques for classifying in-hospital mortality among heart failure patients admitted to the intensive care unit (ICU), using limited historical data in low-resource environments. By employing advanced algorithms and feature engineering, it aims to develop a reliable and accessible predictive model. This research focuses on addressing the challenges posed by scarce data availability, aiming to enhance risk assessment and inform clinical decision-making, thereby improving patient outcomes and resource allocation in ICU environments.

In the chapter 2 this book explores machine learning and its potential in outbreak prediction. By using the power of advanced algorithms and data-driven approaches, it aims to forecast and identify potential disease outbreaks more accurately and rapidly. It explores various methodologies, data sources, and predictive models, highlighting the significance of early detection and proactive response strategies to mitigate the devastating impact of infectious diseases on public health and global well-being.

Chapter 3 explores the development of a mobile application designed to analyze and predict depression in individuals. By harnessing the power of data-driven approaches and machine learning algorithms, this app aims to provide an accessible and user-friendly tool for early detection and monitoring of depressive symptoms. It involves the integration of various data sources, including self-reported information and smartphone sensor data, to enhance the accuracy and reliability of depression prediction. The ultimate goal is to empower individuals and healthcare professionals with a valuable resource for proactive mental health management.

Chapter 4 presents a comprehensive review of machine learning-based analytics for the early detection of premature rheumatoid arthritis (RA) and osteoarthritis (OA) in clinical practices. By examining existing literature and studies, we explore the potential of advanced algorithms in identifying key biomarkers and patterns indicative of these debilitating joint diseases. The review emphasizes the importance of early diagnosis for effective management and intervention strategies. This chapter aims to provide valuable insights and guidelines for integrating machine learning approaches into routine clinical workflows, ultimately improving patient outcomes and quality of life.
