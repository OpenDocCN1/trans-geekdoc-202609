# 8.4 HEALTHCARE USE CASES

The healthcare field is drastically enhancing with technological advancement and according to the needs of current situations of the patients. Patients require smart care of their self or for their relatives who want special care of their patients during their busy schedules. Advanced technologies such as IoT, blockchain, AI, big data, and so on are promising technology to take over the current situation of healthcare systems. Smart healthcare systems have been developed and available as applications for mobile and another electronic gadget to keep a track of diagnosis and updates about health conditions. Quick and easy follow-ups are possible with doctors and better quality treatment facilities can be availed by patients through such smart healthcare systems. Authentic patient records will be available for further treatment procedures and patients will be able to avail hassle-free health services due to such advanced technology. Some patients may be disabled or have physical limitations and require wearable devices to assist their body or to get updates regarding body parameters. # 8.4.1 HEALTHCARE SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT

Healthcare supply chain management ensures that hospitals, clinics, and pharmacies have the right things at the right time. It involves lots of steps, like ordering, storing, and delivering medical stuff, while making sure everything is safe and of good quality. Various kinds of integration that define health supply chains are as follows:

+   - Process coordination and integration.
+   - Coordinating and integrating information flow.
+   - The blending and coordinating of planning procedures.
+   - The blending of internal and external organizational processes.
+   - Integration of a market-based strategy.
+   - Market development integration.
