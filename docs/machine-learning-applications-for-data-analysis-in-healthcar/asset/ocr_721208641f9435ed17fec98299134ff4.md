Chapter 6 explores the application of artificial intelligence (AI) in the healthcare sector, highlighting its benefits and challenges. By examining real-world use cases, we delve into how AI technologies, including machine learning and deep learning, are revolutionizing healthcare delivery, diagnostics, treatment, and patient outcomes. It discusses the potential advantages of AI, such as improved accuracy, efficiency, and personalized care. However, we also address the challenges surrounding data privacy, ethics, algorithm interpretability, and integration into existing healthcare systems. This chapter aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the opportunities and obstacles in leveraging AI for transformative healthcare advancements.

Chapter 7 examines into the design and development of anti-cancerous drug molecules using structure and ligand-based drug designing computational approaches. By using advanced computational techniques, we explore the vast chemical space to identify potential drug candidates with enhanced efficacy and reduced side effects. The chapter highlights the significance of structure-based and ligand-based approaches in rational drug design, focusing on molecular modelling, virtual screening, and quantitative structure-activity relationship (QSAR) analysis. This research aims to accelerate the discovery and optimization of novel anti-cancer drugs, ultimately contributing to the fight against cancer and improving patient outcomes.
