### 6.1.3.2 RULE-BASED EXPERT SYSTEMS

Advanced systems rely on different sets of if-then rules as the main technology for AI in healthcare during the 1980s and beyond. AI is widely used in healthcare for helping doctors make clinical decisions even today. Many electronic health record systems (EHRs) currently provide a set of guidelines with their software offerings. Master frameworks usually require people who are experts and designers to create a set of rules in a specific area of knowledge. They work well to some extent and are easy to follow and measure. ### 6.2.1 DIAGNOSIS AND TREATMENT APPLICATIONS

For the past 50 years, AI in healthcare has focused on finding and treating illnesses. Early frameworks that followed certain standards were able to accurately analyze and treat infections, but they were not widely accepted for use in medical care. Earlier AI systems were not significantly more accurate than human clinicians in diagnosing illnesses, and they often failed to integrate smoothly with clinical workflows and electronic health record (EHR) systems. Implementing AI for diagnostic and treatment planning remains challenging due to difficulties aligning with the day-to-day practices of healthcare professionals and the technical limitations of existing EHR platforms, whether the AI tools are rule-based or algorithmic. Problems with incorporating AI into medical care have been a bigger obstacle compared to how accurate the ideas are. Most of the AI used for diagnosing and treating patients is limited to certain areas of care and comes from specific software companies. Some companies that sell EHR software are starting to add simple AI medical analysis features to their products, but it is still early. To use AI in healthcare with independent EHR systems, companies will have to either do big integration projects themselves or use outside vendors with AI skills that can work with their EHR.

### 6.2.2 ADMINISTRATIVE APPLICATIONS

AI can be used in management and patient care in hospitals. But, using AI in hospitals is not as revolutionary as using it for patient care. However, AI in hospital administration can provide important savings. Computers can support healthcare in a variety of ways, including processing insurance claims, managing patient records, and handling financial operations. AI can be used in healthcare to combine information from different databases for cases and payment management. Guarantors and suppliers need to make sure that the many cases being submitted each day are accurate. Finding and fixing coding problems and mistakes saves everyone time, money, and resources.
