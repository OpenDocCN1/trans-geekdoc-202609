# ## 3.1 INTRODUCTION

Just like a therapist, this app allows the users to feel comfortable with his/her emotions by interacting with him/her and based on analysis (by asking questions—Q&A sessions) app tracks the symptoms and segregate (depends on input answers) them based on level of depression they are in finally app recommends the treatment using different methods like meditation, yoga, listening to audiobooks providing healthy diet chart, all customized to users for help in healing him/her.

# ### 3.1.1 MOTIVATION

Depression has affected more than 264 million people as it has been a common illness worldwide.

Globally, people of all ages can suffer as a prominent cause. Due to the numbers still increasing day-by-day, it has become a contributor to the overall disease index worldwide. As, there have been many causes like abuse, age factor, death in the family, certain medications which may lead a person to attempt suicide. Around 8 lakh people die due to suicide every year.

Suicide is the number two cause for the high rate of deaths in the age gap of 15–29-year olds. The developing countries and the underdeveloped countries face a high rate and unfortunately could not receive much help regarding that due to less knowledge and acknowledgment about it. With the help of this app, we want to help as many people as we can.

# ### 3.1.2 METHODOLOGY

This app opens with the logo of our app, that is, “worry not” and later shows a motivational quote to motivate the users. After the motivation quote comes to the login page where the user inputs “name,” “email address,” and “password.” This is done so that the app can differentiate between users and this way the user will have their data secured. After the login page comes the questionnaire pages where the app asks the user certain questions that will help in the detection of the severity of the user’s depression. The determination of the level of depression is done through a point (here “stars”) system, that is, each answer the user gives will have certain points allocated to them.
