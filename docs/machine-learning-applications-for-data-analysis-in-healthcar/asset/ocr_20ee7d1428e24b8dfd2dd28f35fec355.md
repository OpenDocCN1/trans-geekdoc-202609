Chapter 9 explores the transformative potential of technology-enabled smart healthcare in shaping a Smart Society 5.0. By integrating cutting-edge technologies such as Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence (AI), and Big Data analytics, it examines how smart healthcare systems can enhance patient care, optimize resource utilization, and empower individuals to take control of their health. The chapter discusses the various applications of technology-enabled healthcare, including remote patient monitoring, personalized medicine, and predictive analytics. Additionally, it explores the societal implications, ethical considerations, and challenges associated with the implementation of Smart Society 5.0. This research aims to inspire innovative solutions and drive the evolution of healthcare towards a smarter and more sustainable future.

Chapter 10 introduces ELM-HC, a novel approach for heartbeat classification based on a machine-human interaction model. By combining the strengths of machine learning algorithms and human expertise, we explore how collaborative decision-making can enhance the accuracy and interpretability of heartbeat classification. The chapter discusses the methodology, model architecture, and data pre-processing techniques used in developing ELM-HC. Furthermore, we examine the potential applications of this approach in healthcare settings, such as arrhythmia diagnosis and cardiovascular risk assessment. This research aims to bridge the gap between automated machine learning models and human expertise, providing a valuable tool for improved cardiac health monitoring and patient care.
