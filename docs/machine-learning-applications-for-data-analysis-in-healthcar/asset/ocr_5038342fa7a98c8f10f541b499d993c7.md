# 6.1.3.2 RULE-BASED EXPERT SYSTEMS

Advanced systems rely on different sets of if-then rules as the main technology for AI in healthcare during the 1980s and beyond. AI is widely used in healthcare for helping doctors make clinical decisions even today. Many electronic health record systems (EHRs) currently provide a set of guidelines with their software offerings. Master frameworks usually require people who are experts and designers to create a set of rules in a specific area of knowledge. They work well to some extent and are easy to follow and measure. As the number of rules increases—especially beyond a few thousand—they can begin to conflict with one another, leading to system breakdowns or reduced reliability. Also, if the rules in a certain area change in a major way, it can be difficult and unfair. AI is beginning to replace rule-based systems in medical care using advanced algorithms to interpret data.
