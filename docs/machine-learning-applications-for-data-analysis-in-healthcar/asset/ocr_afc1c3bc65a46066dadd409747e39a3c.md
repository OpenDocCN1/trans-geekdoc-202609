A recent study [19], focusing on the efficacy of ML models in comparison to the traditional approach of MTX (methotrexate), refers to a distinct set of features that can be useful for effective prediction. The logistic regression factor was used to analyze the models using the DAS28. The model's key classifiers are XGBoost, Least Absolute Shrinkage and Selection Operator (selection operator), and RF models. The model's experimental study results are evaluated using the performance of various parameters. According to the study, the logistic regression approach performs better at an 81% sensitivity rate, whereas the accuracy of MTX is around 69% when similar features are assessed. As a result, the study advocates the use of six critical features in ML.

In a study [20] conducted in Korea, the authors correlated aging and rheumatism by analyzing four key attributes for assessing RA conditions among patients: RA factor anticyclic citrullinated peptide, swollen joint count, and ESR. The proposed solution employs a model that employs K-means to study the threshold values of the four parameters on a regular basis. The approach used in the study is not limited to predicting RA conditions in a patient at the early stages but also takes the progressive stages into account. Based on the positive values of two of the four factors, RF and AC, the study was able to estimate the occurrence of rheumatoid disease. When RF and AC are used as metrics for assessment, experimental studies related to the discussion on the model refer to improved prediction accuracy. As a result, the ML approach can be used to detect rheumatic disease and improve the quality of life in an aging society.
