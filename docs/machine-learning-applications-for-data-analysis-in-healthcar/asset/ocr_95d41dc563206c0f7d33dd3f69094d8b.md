# ## ABSTRACT

Smart healthcare systems are the exigency of the present healthcare situation. Health services can be administered in a systematic and successful manner with smart healthcare records. Healthcare systems integrating smart blockchain, artificial intelligence enhancement, and Internet of Things (IoT) support help doctors to devote more time to patient care and reduce their need to search for clinical information. In today’s world, patients need to travel abroad to see their doctors for better treatment but during treatment they face many issues; smart healthcare records may help to reduce such issues. Within the healthcare framework, blockchain network serves as the platform for storing and exchanging patient data across hospitals, diagnostic labs, pharmaceutical firms, and medical practitioners. Blockchain applications enable the accurate identification of critical fraudulent activities.
