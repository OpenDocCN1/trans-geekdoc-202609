With over 8800 citations and 147 research papers in reputed international journals and conferences, he has authored and edited more than 54 books on intelligent decision-making, supply chain management, optimization, sustainability, and risk analysis. He has consecutively been recognized by Elsevier-Scopus and Stanford University’s global top 2% scientists list in “Operations Research,” Dr. Chatterjee has developed several widely cited MCDM methods like MARCOS, RAFSI, SRP, and PRASIAS. He has served as Guest Editor for various SCIE and Scopus indexed journals and leads multiple international book series with Springer, CRC Press, Scrivener-Wiley, and Apple Academic Press. He has received numerous awards including Best Track Paper Award at IEOM 2018 (Paris), Outstanding Reviewer Award from Elsevier, and a University Gold Medal. As a dedicated research mentor, he actively guides PhD scholars as a co-supervisor at institutions like NIT Patna and Aliah University, focusing on advanced decision-making techniques and sustainability research.
