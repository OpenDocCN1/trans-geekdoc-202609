# 8.6 FUTURE TRENDS FOR SMART HEALTHCARE SYSTEMS

Future of smart healthcare systems has the following trends:

# 8.6.1 INCREASING USE OF TELEHEALTH SERVICES

Telehealth offers a spectrum of services, including virtual consultations, remote patient monitoring, and telemedicine applications, fostering timely and convenient access to medical expertise. Its utilization spans various healthcare domains, ranging from primary care to specialty consultations, mental health services, and chronic disease management. Telehealth, facilitated by digital technologies, enables remote access to healthcare services, consultations, and monitoring, transcending geographical barriers. Particularly catalyzed by the COVID-19 pandemic, its popularity continues to soar due to its convenience, accessibility, and efficiency.

# 8.6.2 DIGITAL HEALTH TO THE RESCUE

It uses technology to make things easier for everyone. Imagine having a doctor’s appointment online using your phone or computer instead of going to the clinic. Digital health also includes apps that help keep track of your fitness, remind you to take medicines, and even connect you with a doctor instantly in case of an emergency. It is like having a smart helper that keeps an eye on your health all the time. Moreover, it allows doctors to share information quickly, ensuring you get the best care possible. With digital health, people living in faraway places can also consult doctors without traveling long distances. Wearables and smart gadgets are able to monitor a patient’s every action, including their heart rate, calorie intake, sleep pattern, and activity duration. Wearable technology, for instance, can spot abnormal electrocardiographical patterns in patients and notify medical staff, lowering the risk of more catastrophic illnesses. They have enabled the methodical advancement of personalized healthcare, and they will continue to be crucial to the advancement of medical devices in 2022 and furthermore years.
