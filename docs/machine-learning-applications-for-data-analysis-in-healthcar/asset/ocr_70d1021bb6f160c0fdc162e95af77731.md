Crizotinib is one of the major examples of the drug developed by using the concept of the SBDD [56, 57]. This drug has been regarded as a discerning and powerful c-Met dual inhibitor, which was Food and Drug Association (FDA) approved in the year 2011 [58]. Hepatocyte growth factor, a natural ligand, and its receptor have been known to play an imperative role in cell signaling [59]. There has been a significant amount of upregulation of the protein called as c-Met which has been perceived in the carcinogenesis of several patients [60] and an improper working of the c-Met signaling was detected in the solid and blood tumorigenic cancer. Thus, it has been proved that c-Met is a potent and striking target of oncology.

One of the FDA-approved drugs, Axitinib, has acted as a novel therapy for the treatment of renal cell carcinoma (RCC) [61]. The development of this drug was possible by utilizing the strategy of SBDD in which Axitinib served as an inhibitor on binding to the vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) kinase domain in the DFG phenylalanine configuration [62]. The family of VEGF has its occupancy in the signaling process which is further involved in the angiogenesis process. The signaling pertaining to VEGF was detected in the lump of the cells, and this process of signaling finds its role in the development of diseases that are in the malignant form. VEGF receptor serves as the ligand in the VEGF signaling process. These receptors are called as the family of tyrosine kinases called as FLT₁, KDR, FLK₁, and FLT₄. Blocking the mechanism of action of these receptors with the pan-AKT kinase inhibitor against the kinases family have been proven to be an effective manner of developing drugs against angiogenesis. More recently, Inlyta®, Axitinib in association with KEYTRUDA®; pembrolizumab, has been approved as the drug against RCC [63]. Moreover, NVP-AUY922; Luminispib has been known to possess a highly strong activity for the treatment of tumors in the carcinoma of lungs thereby directing mutations like insertion and models at the cellular level in an assenting trial.
