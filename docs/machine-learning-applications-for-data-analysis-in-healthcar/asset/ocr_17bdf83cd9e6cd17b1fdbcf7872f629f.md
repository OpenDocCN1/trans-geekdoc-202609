# 3.5 RESULT DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

# 3.5.1 GENERAL DEPRESSION ANALYSIS GRAPH

This study includes a number of cross-sectional experiments with a total of 41,344 participants. Outpatients from otolaryngology clinics had the highest prevalence of depressive symptoms (53.0%), followed by dermatology clinics (39.0%) and neurology clinics (39.0% and 35.0%). According to subgroup studies, the prevalence of depression and depressive symptoms ranged from 17.0% to 53.0% in different specialties. Outpatients from developing countries have a greater prevalence of depression and depressive symptoms than outpatients from developed countries. Furthermore, between 1996 and 2010, the prevalence of depression and depressive symptoms in outpatients decreased significantly [2]. Figure 3.4 shows a survey on depression analysis.
