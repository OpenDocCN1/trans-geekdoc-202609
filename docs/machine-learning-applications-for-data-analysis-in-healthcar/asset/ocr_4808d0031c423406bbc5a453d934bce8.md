They felt that it is now feasible for scientific research institutes to employ technologies such as AI or deep learning instead of manually diagnosing, prescribing drugs and supplying them to patient in time. About 56% doctors declared this as one of the crucial advantages of adopting these methods. Medical treatments and related hazards may be minimized, medical resources can be better leveraged, exchanges and collaboration across diverse regions can be facilitated, and self-service medical care and medicine through telecommunication can be facilitated using smart healthcare options. These benefits are now being effectively achieved through the utilization of these technologies in every aspect of healthcare shared by 47% respondents of this survey.

The diagnosis and treatment of illnesses have grown more sophisticated because of the application of technologies such as AI, surgical robots, hybrid AI systems, and virtual reality/augmented reality. By constructing the clinical decision support system with the help of AI, it has been possible to obtain several achievements, including the accurate detection of many rare types of malignant cancer. About 76% experienced doctors agreed that the accuracy of diagnoses produced by AI is superior to that produced by human physicians in terms of time, accuracy, and effort. They were all in favor of new technologies but in a regulated and continuously monitored manner (95%).

Systems that are based on machine learning are frequently highly specialized particularly in the fields of diagnosis and radio imaging. IBM's Watson exemplifies the field of medical decision support technique and is widely considered to be its most outstanding and representative example of smart healthcare system. The program has a significant impact on the accuracy of the diagnosis of both cancer and diabetes. About 65% doctors thought it was possible for them to provide patients with expert advice that is based on algorithms through smart healthcare devices. The diagnosis by medical experts was found to be accurate, missed diagnosis and misdiagnosis were rare, and timely prescription and cure became a reality. The state of the patient and the status of the disease may be more correctly characterized based on smart diagnosis, which assists in the development of a specific treatment plan, and the software has been validated by specialists.
