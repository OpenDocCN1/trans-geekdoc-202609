Goh and Ubeynarayana [5] used RF, support vector machine (SVM), K-nearest neighbors, linear regression, and naïve Bayes (NB) algorithms including classification of construction hazards. Among them, SVM scored F₁ points from 0.45 to 0.92 and was more efficient than other methods. Chokor et al. [3] used the K-means method to categorize injury reports. The types of accidents identified were “fall,” “hit,” “electricity,” and “canals falling,” respectively. Four sets of injuries were found and each set represented a type of risk. Fan and Li [4] conducted research on how to extract text with a criteria-based consultation process for retrieval of accident records. The result has shown that mining methods are superior in terms of recall and accuracy. Zou et al. [16] proposed an NLP approach with the expansion of the semantic query and vector space model to analyze similar accident cases. An approach for automatic extraction of root cause using semantic similarity has been developed by Cambridge University. To convert the unstructured text data, they followed a preliminary preprocessing such as semantic parsing, morphological analysis, subject–verb object extraction, and NLP-based approach [8]. The steps to draw bow-tie are explained by [1]. The relationship between the root causes, preventive barriers, protective barriers, and consequences are also explained using bow-tie. Bow-tie gives a structured way of visualizing the top events and of consequences accidents/incidents. However, there is dearth of literature where NLP methods are integrated with the traditional quantitative risk assessment techniques such as bow-tie [15]. Hence, in this study, we are trying to integrate NLP and bow-tie methods to analyze text narratives of accident reports. The main objectives are to find the root cause of accident using NLP, categories the accident root cause into four categories: (1) human factor, (2) management factor, (3) environmental factor, and (4) component factor and finally preparation of bow-tie model to visualize these accident causations.
