A self-defined convolution template of size 3 × 5 as a gradient operator is used in the spatial domain to minimize the noise and extract the lines feature. The method was then used to prevent making a wrong decision due to the varying position and different standing posture of the acquisition device during the collection of images. Eight different images were obtained by translation operation and one image original, as a whole nine image is used for confirming the user’s identity using the principle of the maximum cross-correlation coefficient. The experiments conducted on the FKP obtained a recognition rate of 95.68% at a 30-threshold value, which revealed that it is a reliable and accurate biometric trait.

Morales et al. [16] presented a novel approach for FKP authentication. In this technique, the author uses a Gabor transform to enhance the orientation information of the image, and SIFT descriptor is used to extract and represent the feature sets. El-Tarhouns et al. [17] applied multiscale LBP and PCA techniques to extract the local and global features from the FKP image and a matching attempt can be considered as a combination of both global and local features.

In Ref. [18], the author proposed a multimodal biometric system using FKP and Palmprint biometrictrait. This model extracted various features from both and computed scores are combined using score level fusion. Various combinations of experiments are conducted and found that SURF plus bidirectional empirical mode decomposition feature extraction technique provides better performance.
