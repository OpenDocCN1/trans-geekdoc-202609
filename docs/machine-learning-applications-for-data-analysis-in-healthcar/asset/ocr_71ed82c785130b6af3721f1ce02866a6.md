Structure- and ligand-based methods are the predominantly applied tools in the drug discovery domain but the future lies in the upcoming synergistic techniques such as proteochemometrics. Combinatorial chemistry and proteomics have been infused to lead to remarkable results in the CADD era. Integration of omics technologies and high-scale accelerated development of machine learning algorithms has reduced the timespan of processing. Sophistications in cloud computing, data mining, data management, big data, and artificial intelligence have strengthened the data storage and data retrieval capacities in biological databases leading to accentuated virtual screening and molecular simulations. Metagenomics and metatranscriptomics have made it possible to analyze bulk environmental samples in one go and further add up to the availability of bulk-level genomic sequences and transcriptomes available on the servers. CADD is way ahead in drug designing and future lies in the biological data processing and integration with high-end computational technologies.

# 7.6 CONCLUSION

The future of drug discovery and development lies in higher advancements in CADD techniques related to virtual screening, lead identification, optimization, molecular docking, pharmacophore modeling, and molecular modeling. Identification of target structure, validation, and screening of lead molecules has become easier with available databases and tools like 2D and 3D QSAR. Life-threatening diseases like cancer can be combated in a better way using these advancements. There is still a need to further develop simulation algorithms with better efficacy than molecular dynamics simulation and Monte Carlo approaches.
