The GBC aims to lead all blockchain-pertinent activities on a single, well-equipped platform. GBC can be used for healthcare collaborations also. GBC is able to establish a worldwide healthcare system that frees patients from geographic restrictions on their choice of hospitals and doctors. Other blockchains known as RBC are adapted to the locations of the patients for minor ailments that do not require a lot of doctor interaction or advice. RBCs are permitted, but the GBC is a permissionless blockchain. The suggested paradigm promises a decentralized framework that makes it easier for users and other parties to share, exchange, and integrate information (Figure 8.2).
