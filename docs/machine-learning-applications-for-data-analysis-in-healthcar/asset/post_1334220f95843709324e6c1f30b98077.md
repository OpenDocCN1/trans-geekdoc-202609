
## 9.1 INTRODUCTION AND LITERATURE REVIEW

The age that we live in is the age of information. Traditional medicine has gradually begun to get digital especially in recent years because of research-oriented advances in technology and scientific theory. There has been an emergence of intelligent and smart healthcare that makes use of the most recent inventions of information technology [9]. Smart healthcare is not simply a simple technology improvement; rather, it is also a transformation that affects healthcare on a comprehensive and multilevel scale [17]. Changes in the healthcare model (moving away from diagnosis to prevention and care), information construction changes (moving away from patient's data to other related family factors) [4], changes in mindset (moving away from general care to personal care), and changes in the idea of support and systems are all manifestations of this shift (from prescription to preventive medicine). The mantra is to act smart and move with agility toward a smart society 5.0 [16].

These adaptations place an emphasis on catering to the specific requirements of individuals while also boosting the effectiveness of healthcare coverage [10]. As a result, the overall experience of receiving medical and health services is significantly improved, and they point to the direction in which modern medicine will continue to develop in the future [8].

## 9.2 WHAT IS TECHNOLOGY-ENABLED SMART HEALTHCARE?

The term “smart infrastructure” refers to intelligent infrastructure that uses sensors that can detect information and relays information over the network. Supercomputers and Cloud are increasingly being used for this purpose. They are capable of coordinating and integrating many social systems, which enables to accomplish the dynamic and sophisticated administration of human civilization [14]. The trend is toward devices that can be worn-like accessories, the Internet of Things (IoT), and smartphones to monitors, connecting family and support systems who can actively respond smartly in time is referred to as “smart healthcare” [12].

To ensure that the needy are receiving the services they require, ensure stakeholders are acting responsibly based on accurate information, and facilitate the ability to allocate resources efficiently; smart healthcare has the potential to encourage interaction between all stakeholders in healthcare [13]. It is therefore an advanced level of information building in the realm of medical treatment using technology to the best of its ability [11].

As a part of this exploratory study, around 460 experienced doctors (ranging from 10 to 40 years) from various parts of the country were interviewed both objectively and subjectively. Their identity was not disclosed and because of which they gave their opinions freely without any inhibition.

## 9.3 THE ENABLING TECHNOLOGIES

Multiple stakeholders, including medical professionals and patients, healthcare facilities, and academic research organizations, are involved in the delivery of cognitive medical care. It is an organic totality that incorporates numerous dimensions, such as the prevention and monitoring of diseases, the diagnosis and treatment of illnesses, the administration of hospitals, the formulation of decisions regarding healthcare, and the conducting of medical research. Ref. [3] advocated in their research that together with contemporary biotechnology, there are others that also form the foundation of smart healthcare for example, IoT, cloud computing, 5G, artificial intelligence (AI), and so on. Liu et al. [1] advocated that these technologies are utilized extensively across the board in relation to smart healthcare. The concept of “smart aging,” that is, the patient is able to wear a device like an accessory which continuously monitors the vital statistics and if there is any deviation, it alerts to seek medical assistance through virtual assistants, and remote services can be taken advantage of within no time at all [15]. A doctor sees the same as a smart support system which improves their diagnosis and prescription. They get the vital figures within no time at all and even if it might not be very accurate, it still gives them a time advantage. An integrated information platform allows for the regular record of patient’s data by medical professionals. Ninety percent doctors agreed that using surgical robots and mixed reality technologies, more accurate surgery is now possible. Forty-eight percent respondents report that radiofrequency identification technology is also being effectively utilized to manage equipment and supply chain in hospitals. Thirty percent doctors suggested that this could be better accomplished by utilizing technology to collect data and subsequent analysis. About 60% doctors shared their experience of the utilization of mobile medical platforms which is now resulting in improved experiences for patients. They felt that it is now feasible for scientific research institutes to employ technologies such as AI or deep learning instead of manually diagnosing, prescribing drugs and supplying them to patient in time. About 56% doctors declared this as one of the crucial advantages of adopting these methods. Medical treatments and related hazards may be minimized, medical resources can be better leveraged, exchanges and collaboration across diverse regions can be facilitated, and self-service medical care and medicine through telecommunication can be facilitated using smart healthcare options. These benefits are now being effectively achieved through the utilization of these technologies in every aspect of healthcare shared by 47% respondents of this survey.

The diagnosis and treatment of illnesses have grown more sophisticated because of the application of technologies such as AI, surgical robots, hybrid AI systems, and virtual reality/augmented reality. By constructing the clinical decision support system with the help of AI, it has been possible to obtain several achievements, including the accurate detection of many rare types of malignant cancer. About 76% experienced doctors agreed that the accuracy of diagnoses produced by AI is superior to that produced by human physicians in terms of time, accuracy, and effort. They were all in favor of new technologies but in a regulated and continuously monitored manner (95%).

Systems that are based on machine learning are frequently highly specialized particularly in the fields of diagnosis and radio imaging. IBM's Watson exemplifies the field of medical decision support technique and is widely considered to be its most outstanding and representative example of smart healthcare system. The program has a significant impact on the accuracy of the diagnosis of both cancer and diabetes. About 65% doctors thought it was possible for them to provide patients with expert advice that is based on algorithms through smart healthcare devices. The diagnosis by medical experts was found to be accurate, missed diagnosis and misdiagnosis were rare, and timely prescription and cure became a reality. The state of the patient and the status of the disease may be more correctly characterized based on smart diagnosis, which assists in the development of a specific treatment plan, and the software has been validated by specialists. The actual procedure of therapy will end up being carried out with more accuracy. With the assistance of smart radiomics, for instance, the radiation procedure administered to a patient undergoing treatment for tumors may be dynamically monitored all the way through treatment. The radiation program can be optimized, the illness progression can be monitored, and the uncertainty associated with manual operation may be avoided. The development of surgical robots has made it possible to do surgery at a higher level than ever before. Patients report better results and quicker recovery times as compared to traditional endoscopic surgery, and doctors have access to technology that provides them with greater flexibility and compatibility than traditional endoscopic surgery. The utilization of remote surgery will also result in greater levels of convenience (84%).

The utilization of technology that uses mixed realities makes the process of developing and carrying out the surgical plan more straightforward. Dr. Rajesh Wirth, a senior doctor of a reputed hospital in India reports that the virtual world, the real world, and the users of the system create a circle through matching results with the existing cases. He foresees that the development of this technology further will bring about revolutionary shifts in clinical practice, as well as medical education and research, as well as communication.

Chronic illnesses that are incurable, perhaps terminal, and expensive to treat require smart healthcare options. On the other hand, the conventional paradigm of health management, which is concentrated on hospitals and physicians, is unable to appropriately deal with massive data volumes. About 37% doctors responded favorably on the health management model that is now being implemented as part of smart healthcare and placed a greater emphasis on emerging patient autonomy in managing their own treatment. They reported that patients can themselves monitor and communicate instant feedback of health condition to doctor, leading to timely intervention and diagnosis. The development of smart gadgets that can be implanted or worn, as well as smart homes and health information platforms, all of which are connected by IoT technology, offers a solution to this problem (94%). Wearable and implantable devices combine advanced sensitive sensors, chips, leads, and wireless networks to regularly sense and monitor various physiological indicators of patients from time to time. These devices also reduce mental stress, give confidence to patients, and interact with other data available from diverse sources to reach expert conclusions. About 65% doctors suggested a transition to frequent monitoring and control procedures to obtain accurate diagnosis.
