# 7.3.2 DOCKING ALGORITHMS

Docking uses different search algorithms, which help in predicting possible confirmation of complexes. These algorithms are based on certain parameters. On the basis of flexibility of the receptor and ligand, the search algorithms are divided into two types.

_Rigid body docking_: In this algorithm, molecular conformation remains rigid and ligand is fitted in by varying the orientation of ligand in various angles. It is also the first slant used in docking and it compile the below-mentioned functions such as it fixes the protein and ligand and helps in selecting molecules with moderates energy orientation and protein–protein docking is done by this.

_Flexible docking_: It is the most used form of docking. This ligand is flexible but the receptor is rigid. First ligands are selected to become flexible by moving firm parts through optimization. In this bond angle, torsion angles are modified and internal geometry is changed.

+   • Systematic search algorithms provide variations in the structure, and rapidly change ligand conformation. Examples of some systematic search algorithms are conformational search, database, fast shape mappings, and so on. It is further categorized into fragment and exhaustive search algorithms. Exhaustive searches clarify ligand forms by systematically rotating all possible bonds and torsion angles in a given period of time. The fragment-based algorithm is done by incremental construction of ligand conformations from fragments obtained by selecting ligands of interest [44].
+   
+   • The Monte Carlo algorithm creates a casual initial configuration of ligand in the active site which is scored on the basis of energy and other parameters. After this, small new changes are made and the novel confirmation is again subjected to scoring. The better confirmation with higher scoring is selected, also known as the initiation minimization program [45].
+   
+   • Genetic algorithms are built on the principle of population and biological evolution [46]. There is a set of parameters that decides the arrangement of protein and ligand based on translation, rotation, and confirmation.
+   
+   • Tabu search algorithm in this small change is made to the current configuration of ligand and on the basis of fitness, they are ranked [47].
