Most of the progress has been done in the field of molecular biology and structural advancement has been made for the determination of the spectroscopic structures. These methodologies have obtained the three-dimensional structures of more than 1 lakh proteins [12]. Besides the storage and retrieval of data, there has been a sufficient development of sophisticated techniques for the analysis of biological data computationally. Improvement in bioinformatics and Human Genome Project completion has paved out the way for the development of drugs due to the accessibility of innumerable target proteins. Three-dimensional structures of those proteins that are known to be therapeutic in nature are favoring the binding cavities identification and have formed the basis for drug designing according to its structure. SBDD is a speedy process for the lead optimization and discovery as it governs the three-dimensional structure of the receptor protein and houses the information about the illness at a molecular basis [13]. Most of the computational techniques like molecular simulations, molecular docking, and SBDD are most commonly used in SBDD.

SBDD is a recursive process as it progresses through more than one cycle leading to an enhanced drug molecule to medical tribunals. Generally, any detection prior to the drug involves four steps: phase 1: discovery; phase 2: development; phase 3: clinical; phase 4: registry.
