# Bow-Tie Construction from Accident Narratives: A Text-Mining Approach

ASHISH GARG, SOUVIK DAS*, AMARDEEP KUMAR, and J. MAITI

Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur, West Bengal, India

# ABSTRACT

In this chapter, we propose an integrated natural language processing (NLP) and the Bow-tie-based risk assessment method to analyze the accident or incident reports for providing preventive and protective actions to the organization. Over 500 accident/incident text narratives of distinct scenarios of workers at different working sites in an organization were collected. We first applied the custom-trained named entity recognition model and found out the objects involved and the potential root cause of the accident or incident from each of the text narratives. Second, using the elbow method, it is found that 4–6 clusters of root causes can be created. Then, the root causes are classified into four categories namely, human factors, component factors, management factors, and environmental factors, using term frequency–inverse document frequency vectorizer and linear support vector classifier. In order to visualize these accidents/incidents, root causes, and their categories, bow-tie methodology is applied. The preventive and protective barriers have been suggested for each potential top event. Thus, this work provides a qualitative risk assessment method to manage future risk events.
