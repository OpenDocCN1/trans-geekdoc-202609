In paper [6], named “Current Knowledge and Adoption of Mobile Health Apps Among Australian General Practitioners: Survey Study,” they surveyed many Australian practitioners where they provided insights that most of the health apps were recommended for patients and more than 50% of them were mental health-related apps. This survey helped us motivate toward making this app. Their limitations included a very low response rate and they were election bias which is not handled clearly for accuracy.

In a paper named, “Smartphone Apps: Review and Evidence-Based Recommendations for Future Developments,” they again surveyed and analyzed the approaches which can be woven into further innovative apps for mental health. The factor they mentioned is that it is appreciated if the apps are designed for use for those who have not sought treatment. So that they could get help easily and in the comfort of their home. Also, they mentioned that apps should be able to allow the user some tailoring with the schedule provided for their treatment which is why we segregated this feature.
