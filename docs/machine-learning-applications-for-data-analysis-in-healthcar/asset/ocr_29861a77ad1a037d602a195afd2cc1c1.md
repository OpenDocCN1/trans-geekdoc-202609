# ## 5.6 EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS

The performance measures during training and validation phases have been evaluated by simulating the naïve Bayes (NB), long short-term memory (LSTM), and generative adversarial network (GAN) models using MIT-BIH HD dataset. In addition, the ensemble model of LSTM-GAN is also simulated using the same dataset. The various results have been obtained and the details have been presented in Ref. [18]. Some of the reported results of this article have been provided in this section for ease of understanding and to carry out further research in this field.

The comparison of accuracy plots of NB-based CVD detection model during training and validation phases is shown in Figure 5.5. It is observed, as expected, that the accuracy during the validation phase is lower than that during training phase. The accuracy increases steadily during training until it achieves almost a constant value after successful training of the model. A similar plot for LSTM model is presented in Figure 5.6. In the case of GAN-based DL model similar accuracy plot has been obtained through a simulation study and is shown in Figure 5.7. This plot exhibits the comparison of accuracy of generator and discriminator parts of GAN. The receiver operating characteristic (ROC) plot of a model is very important as its area under the curve (AUC) signifies the performance potentialia of a detector. The ROC plots of various ML and DL models have been obtained using simulation study and by MIT-BIH dataset. The comparative ROC plots of various classification models are shown in Figure 5.8. Also the corresponding AUC value of each model is listed in the same figure. It may be noted that the higher the AUC, the better is the detection efficiency of a model. The three performance measures: Accuracy, $F_1$ score, and AUC of NB, LSTM, GAN, and ensemble of GAN-LSTM models obtained from the simulation study and have been presented in Table 5.6. It is observed from Table 5.6 that GAN-LSTM ensemble model provides the best performance of classification yielding 0.992, 0.987, and 0.984 values in terms of accuracy, $F_1$ score, and AUC, respectively.
