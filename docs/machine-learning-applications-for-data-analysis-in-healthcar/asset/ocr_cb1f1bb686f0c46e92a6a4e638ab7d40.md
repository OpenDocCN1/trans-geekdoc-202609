About 84% doctors reported here that using RFID technology, it is now possible to provide everyone with their own unique RFID tag, easily accessible through the use of handheld devices. This activity helped to ensure that hospital supplies are moved in a manner that is safe, reliable, stable, and effective. About 78% doctors felt that an integrated management platform can achieve tasks including service provision, quality assessment, evaluation metrics, optimization techniques and performance evaluation. Medical expenses are minimized to an extent, the usage of resources is economic, expert solutions are long-term sustainable and specialized medical institutions are able to take development decisions. In terms of the experience that patients have, they have access to many tasks, such as systems for physical examinations, online appointment scheduling, and confidential communications between doctors and patients. About 60% of doctors interviewed here report that the medical treatment procedures for patients are streamlined because of these automated solutions. About 89% doctors were of the opinion that patients now waited for less time and received service that is more personalized. In a nutshell, majority (95%) doctors surveyed agreed integration, more refining, and further automation are the three primary priorities for the future of smart hospitals and smart healthcare.

# 9.4 ANALYSIS AND THE ROAD AHEAD

The advent of intelligent healthcare has resulted in the development of fully developed ideas and structures. Despite this, there is still a significant amount of potential for improvement, and many new difficulties are currently coming into play because of the introduction of new technologies and new problems. Now, there is a lack of macrodirection and programmatic guidelines in the field of smart healthcare. This results in ambiguous development goals, which eventually leads to a waste of resources. In addition, medical institutions do not have similar standards across different locations and different organizations, and there is a need for changes in the process of ensuring that data integrity is maintained [2]. Because of the sheer volume and complexity of the data, both the exchange of information and the transmission of messages can be challenging.
