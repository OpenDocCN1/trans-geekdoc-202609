### 3.3.1 DRAWBACKS AND ISSUES IN EXISTING WORK

A study was done on more than 100 depression apps where it was analyzed that only 35% were clinically effective and only 3% of even them were actually accurate according to clinical standards. It also points toward the negligence and lack of valid information through five-star ratings for these apps. Altogether, these results suggest that there still are serious and overly neglected challenges that can be born from these killer apps if seen from a clinical quality perspective. Improving consensus is that most commercially available apps are not evidence-based which can be highly dangerous. Each person's lookout is different for each problem when suffering through depression and they have a different front on that, a single app provides a basic front/solution on the given problem which may not be able to fulfill the purpose.

Symptom monitoring has been highly praised as helpful and supportive with people who have mental issues. Despite that fact, there have not been many apps that provide this feature and on that front also even if some do they do not give official output as accurate. As some apps could only help for not more than two weeks, an attachment and getting comfortable with these apps can be highly dangerous for the patients as they might feel weak after not getting the same environment, even if they improved while using those apps.

According to the published papers on depression apps, the factors which are causing issues with existing work are mostly the factors related to lifestyle like nutrition, stress (financially and digitally), consistent fitness routine, and diet basically routine activities that keeps a person fit from waking up in the morning to getting a good sleep at night and to improve this when they are suffering from depression is what drives them to download these apps. Lastly, apps are very expensive and consist of premium packages which are very costly for users to afford.
