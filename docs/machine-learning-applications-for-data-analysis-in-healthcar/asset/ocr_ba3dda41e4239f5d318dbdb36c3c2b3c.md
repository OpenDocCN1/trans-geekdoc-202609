# 8.4.3 PLATFORM TO EXCHANGE PATIENT'S RECORDS

A platform for sharing patient records is like a digital space where hospitals, doctors, and clinics can swap important information about a person’s health. It works a lot like a big, safe, and secret locker for your health data that only authorized people can access. This locker keeps all your medical details safe and organized, like your test results, past illnesses, and prescribed medicines. When you go to different doctors or hospitals, they can open this locker to see what has happened before. It is like having a special book about your health that all your healthcare helpers can read to understand your story. This sharing helps everyone involved in your treatment to work together better. They can understand your health history, plan treatments, and ensure you get the right care. It is like a sharing library for your health information that only trusted people can use to help you feel better.

# 8.4.4 AUTOMATED SYSTEMS TO ASSIST DISABLES

Blockchain makes it possible for health records to be used properly by reducing needless administrative expenses. Additionally, the necessity for several middlemen to oversee the sharing of crucial health information will decline as a result of this technology. Delivering efficient, timely, and sufficient services to their people is the main duty of healthcare professionals. Thanks to blockchain technology, healthcare providers will already be aware of each patient’s medical background. Where blockchain provides security and transparency, IoT and artificial intelligence provide auto control to smart healthcare systems which may be really able to assist disabled persons. Wearable computing is the main attraction of this use case of healthcare.
