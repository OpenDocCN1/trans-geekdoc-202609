According to the authors of the study [22], a novel approach of patient-specific analysis and prediction model for OA is being considered. The study examines existing models in order to identify gaps in the early stages of OA disease detection. It emphasizes the categorization of various classifier-centric studies, as well as their accuracy conditions. A review of the summation study led to more detailed and implicit studies that discussed a large set of ML models for OA analysis.

Kokkotis et al. [23] discuss an evolutionary ML approach in which the study targeted the risk factors for KOA progression using a significant set of feature selection methodologies. The model addresses two major challenges: issues of dimensionality and heterogeneity for the data obtained from the database, and secondarily, imbalancy issues observed in the KOA progressors class in comparison to the nonprogressors class.

The study has primarily relied on the Gen wrapper feature selection model for applying the features for accuracy. The study used a genetic algorithm model to improve the model’s accuracy when it came to evolutionary models. The Shapley Additive explanation is used to evaluate the datasets used for the filtered feature selection efficacy. The proposed model is promoted as an effective risk stratification strategy that, when applied to KOA patient data prediction, can aid in more accurate analysis.

A recent study on KOA prediction focused on the patient’s need for total knee arthroplasty. The domain’s predictive model refers to KOA conditions in the general population, issues of TKA outcome in KOA patients, and the risk of knee replacement conditions in KOA patients. Using the PubMed databases, certain models from the literature were discussed, emphasizing the significance of feature selection and conditional approaches from algorithms that can help assess KOA conditions among patients more effectively. The model’s key aspects are prognostic prediction models that are qualitatively summarized. The study used a meta-analysis approach to identify quantitative metrics for the predictive performance of the models.
