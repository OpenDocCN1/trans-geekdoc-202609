# 4.3 Culture Medium

Cell medium is designed to support cell survival when they are removed from their natural environments and placed in vitro for expansion. Mammalian cells in vitro generally need a carbon source, usually glucose, nitrogen sources, i.e., amino acids, as well as vitamins, minerals and inorganic materials to maintain physiological osmolality. Growth factors are also essential as they activate signalling pathways and regulate behaviour. Animal-derived serum has been traditionally used as a medium in mammalian cell culture as it contains a mixture of proteins and smaller molecules for critical cell maintenance and viability. However, it becomes concerns because of how it is sourced.
