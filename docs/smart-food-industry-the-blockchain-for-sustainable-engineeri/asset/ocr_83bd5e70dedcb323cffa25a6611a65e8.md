Here I like to draw particular attention to the fact that the primary aim of veggie meats companies is not necessarily a concern for the highest level of health; rather, veggie companies goal is to offer very palatable products that satisfy both vegetarians/vegans and meat eaters' palate. Those burgers must be as greasy and heavy and dense and filling as beef burgers; and these veggie chicken nuggets must have, the characteristic texture, the saltiness, and the smell that traditional chicken nuggets have. That is how the meat alternative can be big and grow. The primary goal of the veggie meat alternative business, like others, is to sell to as many people as possible. Arguably, there are at least two important benefits: one is that companies like Boca Burgers, Beyond Meat, Impossible Burger, and others, are more environmentally responsible than conventional meat as they do not exploit (at directly) animals, and the veggie industry would seem to avoid the failure that livestock industry causes.

The second is that the production of veggie meats does not involve the same waste materials as the production of animal meat, which have environmentally negative repercussions. Another consideration is that veggie meat alternatives may contain several infamous substances known as anti-nutrients, like phytates, inhibitors, lectins, phytochemical acids, and phytates. Research shows that such substances can have deleterious effects on our health (Petroski and Minaj 2020). To mention a few examples, oxalic acid can cause mineral depletion and micronutrient deficiency by reducing the bioavailability of iron, zinc, potassium, copper, magnesium, and calcium. Polyphenols have a negative impact on the activities of digestive enzymes, and they can negatively impact the bioavailability of proteins and acids (Asgar et al. 2010).

Another substance that cause similar environmental issues is tofu, which is obtained from soy, and thus those veggie meats are made from soy. One would think that soy is an unproblematic product. However, according to the World Foundation, the “surging demand for soy is causing deforestation, a great deal of greenhouse emissions, and a significant use of water and pesticides (worldlife o.n.d.). Soy is a fundamental ingredient of veggie meat products, such as Tofurkey, Garden, Lightlife, Boca, Impossible Burger, and others. Therefore, their negative impacts on the environment must be taken seriously. The company Beyond Meat, on the other hand, does not use soy for the production of their products; they use pea protein, which seems to be environmentally friendly than soy.
