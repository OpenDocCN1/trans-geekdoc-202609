Avoiding antibiotics in medium prevents further spread of antibiotic resistance. There is also concern about the effect of antibiotics on gene expression, with a recent study identifying 209 PenStep-responsive genes, including transcription factors likely to regulate other genes (Ryu et al. 2017). Moreover, antibiotics were also found to have a detrimental effect on the growth of myoblastite cells with reduced proliferation by 26% over 6 months of culture (Kolkmann et al. 2020). The same effect was found when using adipose-derived MSCs (Skublas et al. 2017).

Serum-free media studies for expansion of myoblastite cells for cultivated medium production have been published to date (Kolkmann et al. 2020, Messamer et al. 2022). Kolmann et al. tested range of commercially serum-free media and two serum replacements for their ability to stabilize culture of bovine myoblasts. Serum-free media (SFM) tested Essential 8 Life (Technologies), Milenyen Biotech), StemPro SFM (Thermo Scientific), Stemmac HSC expansion (MesenCult Stem Cell Technologies), and TeSR E (Stem Cell Technologies), while the serum-replacements used Lipogo (RMBIO) and XerumFree (TNC Bio). When used with bovine myoblasts, they performed poorly probably because they were optimised for different species (i.e., human) and different cell types such as MSCs or PSCs. On the other, the Lipogo supplement was found to be successful as an FBS replacement for bovine myoblasts. However, it resulted in an adipocyte-like cell phenotype with fat droplets accumulation (Kolkmann et al. 2020).

Myogenic differentiation in serum-based cultures is typically induced by lowering the concentration of serum (serum starvation) (Messamer et al. 2022), while in a serum-culture, it would be induced by introducing ligands to receptors that are up-regulated during the initial stages of myogenic differentiation such as transferin, insulin, glucagon and lysophosphatidic acid (IPA) (IPA). For adipogenic differentiation, typical cocktails include insulin, IMBX, hydrocortisone and dexamethasone (Hach Zachar 2021, Dafai et al. 2021, Dohman et al. 2023) used in combination or without serum.

Mammalian cells are not efficient in the way they metabolize nutrients such as carbon and nitrogen sources, producing a metabolic waste at certain concentrations (Schop et al. 2009, 2010, Post et al. 2020). Regular medium changes need to be applied, to remove the waste products and replenish the nutrients. To mitigate this, fed-batch or perfusion processes can be used to perform regular medium exchanges to remove the waste products and replenish the nutrients resulting in increasing cell yields (Europe et al. 2000) and a more effective cell medium, Additionally, medium composition can be optimized to reduce substrate fluctuations. more efficient consumption of nutrients. Strategies have been successfully used to optimize medium for cell lines in the production of biopharmaceuticals (Bell et al. 1995).

Cultured meat is a product that needs to have a price tag several orders of magnitude lower than biosimilars. Therefore, efforts of reducing the cost, increasing cell yields need to be investigated, stretching the current boundaries of cell culture methods applied to the production of biopharmaceuticals. Food grade or feed raw materials of low purity need to be explored for their performance in media formulations. Crude raw materials, such as protein hydrolyates from plant, alg or microbial sources are also potential candidates of nutrients to the cells. Hydrolysates can be produced with enzymatic or acid treatment of the alg, plant or microbial biomass and have proved effective in promoting cell growth (Ng et al. 2020, Ho et al. 2021). However, hydrolyates are also prone to variation and unless a high level or robustness in the production process is achieved, their use can result in significant differences in performance.

Recycling the culture medium is another strategy that can be applied to reduce costs and most efficiently use the raw materials. Recycling can be achieved through chemical or biological process.
