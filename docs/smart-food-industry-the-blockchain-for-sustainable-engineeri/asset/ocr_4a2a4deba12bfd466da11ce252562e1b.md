# 2.11 Artificial Intelligence (AI)

There are three types of AI in industry: descriptive AI, predictive AI, and prescriptive AI (Olsen and Tomlin 2020). It is possible to improve process design and control by using descriptive AI techniques on the data collected from sensor-enabled devices. In order AI to be able to forecast and prescribe, it must connect the dots between what is happening and why. An example of predictive AI is condition-based maintenance, which relies on accurate predictions about the coming of future failures. Prescriptive AI generates ideas for operational action. Connecting food logistics needs with AI will be the first hurdle for researchers to tackle in this field. Despite the fact that decision support systems are not new, current advances in machine learning are increasingly being used in all three AI categories to transition from model-based methods to the use of training data approaches and statistical techniques that depend on limited or no models.

From a sustainability perspective, AI can be leveraged to impact various supply chain activities, including supplier selection, warehouse status and priorities control, food waste reduction, and shipment delays. Through process automation and intelligent integration of food production activities, AI has the ability to boost the efficiency of food supply chains. AI adoption could help in delegating decision-making authority, enabling adaptability to the changing circumstances and agility (Schuh et al. 2017).
