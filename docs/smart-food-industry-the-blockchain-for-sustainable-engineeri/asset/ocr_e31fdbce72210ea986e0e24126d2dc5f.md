# 2.1 Plum Waste and By-products

Plum (Prunus domestica L.) originates from Asia, and globally has a 12.6 million ton of production rate annually (Liandska et al. 2020). During the past 25 years, plum harvest has doubled worldwide, reaching 12.6 million tons in 2018 (Dolzynska et al. 2020). Plum has various bioactive compounds such as dietary fibers, phenolic compounds and vitamins (Savi and Sajic 2020).
