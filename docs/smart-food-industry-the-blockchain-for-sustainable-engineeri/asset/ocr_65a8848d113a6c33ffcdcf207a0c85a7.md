# ## 4. Consumption Safety

Not all insects are safe to eat, as certain vegetables are. When selecting insects, monitoring microbial contamination is the first concern, microbiological, chemical, toxicological, and allergic risks may be (Sun-Waterhouse et al. 2016, Mishna and Glumac 2021). The bioavailability and bioaccessibility of insect nutrients are poorly researched topics, and many studies have addressed nutrient and characterization, however just as any other food, the type of preparation and processing significantly affects the nutrient content that will or will not be bioavailable (Parada and Aguillera 2007, Mendez 2018).

The presence of anti-nutrients in some insects, such as tannins and phenolic acids, can hinder the bioavailability of some fractions of the compounds (Cardoso et al. 2015). In general, tannins in animal feeds are associated with reduced consumption, growth rate, efficiency, metabolizable energy, and protein digestibility, as they form complexes with protein, interfering with its bioavailability (Hammerman 2016, Mutungi et al. 2019). In animals, the bioaccessibility and bioavailability of compounds present in insects are variable; however, in the case of conventional foods, thermal processing can modify the flavor and increase the bioavailability and bioavailability of nutrients (Barba et al. 2017, Mandsitera et al. 2019).

There are natural variations in protein and amino acid profiles among different insect species, and the levels of these compounds are sensitive to processing methods, including cooking, boiling, frying, and drying (static, lyophilization, air circulation, vacuum, and microwaves). Minerals are less sensitive to processing conditions but can be affected by cooking style as they can be removed during drying (Zielinska et al. 2015, Mandsitera et al. 2019).

The digestibility of insect proteins is affected by the preparation and cooking style. For instance, some studies have reported that the roasting and drying of locusts reduces the digestibility of the protein, which does not occur when protein is applied to dehydrated termites. When boiled, Tenebro increases protein digestibility (Gravel and Djoyen 2020, Lifecaea). Some insects have reactive or toxic chemicals, others contain cross-reactive allergens that can induce allergic reactions to those associated with shellfish or mollusks (Barre et al. 2014, Lange and Nakamura 2021). The allergenicity of edible insects may resemble so-called arthropod and invertebrate allergy (caused by components such as the hemolymph, cuticle, and parts including exuvia, hair, bristles, and scales) (Broekman et al. 2015, Raheem et al. 2019).
