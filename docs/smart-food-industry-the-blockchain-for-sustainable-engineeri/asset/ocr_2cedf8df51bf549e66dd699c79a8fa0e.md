# 3. Green Analytical Chemistry + Foodomics + SDG = Green Sustainable Foodomics

So, after understanding the meaning of Foodomics and Green Analytical Chemistry comes the turn of the combination: Green Foodomics.

Nowadays, food analysis is one of the most challenges in analytical chemistry. Among the plethora of analytical steps, sample preparation can be considered as the main source of environmental toxicants, followed by separation techniques.
