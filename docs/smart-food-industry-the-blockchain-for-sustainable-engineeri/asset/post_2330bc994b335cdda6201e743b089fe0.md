The main intent of packaging is to get food safe from producers to consumers. Also, food packaging improves food safety by reducing bacterial contamination. On the other hand, food contact materials can transfer chemicals to food with unknown effects which also have a big impact on the environment.

Food contact materials protects food against contamination because of a huge amount of plastic spread out over the environment, several states have restrictions on using plastic bags and bans. This has led over the years to the development of various green, sustainable technologies. 

The development of new labeling systems will support risk assessors, innovators, policy makers and consumers, and address related concerns. Innovative methodologies and technologies will also aim to not only reduce food waste but also reduce energy consumption and ensure food safety.

Blockchain technology appears to offer exciting opportunities here. Alongside the risks of environmental pollutants and associated issues such as authenticity and traceability, research should also be developed on predictive toxicology, such as the safety assessment of new materials, the interactions between food tissue and microbiota while dieting.
