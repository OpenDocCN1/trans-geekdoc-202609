80% of hemicellulose is converted to pentoses. Subsequently, the slurry is neutralized with calcium hydroxide until a pH level of 5 is achieved. The mixture is then fed to the saccharification reactor where enzymatic hydrolysis takes place. The amount of enzymes fed is 20 mg per g of cellulose. The enzymatic hydrolysis is assumed to convert 90% of the remaining cellulose and hemiculose to hexoses and pentoses, respectively. The hydrolysate, which contains 14 wt of hexoses, 8 wt of pentoses and 65 wt of water, is filtered using a screw press and directed to fermentation. The lignin-rich filter cake is directed to the Utilities section for the generation of steam and electricity.
