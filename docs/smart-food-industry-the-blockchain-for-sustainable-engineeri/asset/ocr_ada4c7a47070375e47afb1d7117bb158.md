# 3. Animal Agriculture and The Environment

One of the main reasons veggie meat alternatives are so popular is that their tastes please both vegans and meat eaters. Moreover, it may be argued that veggie meats have several benefits, two of which are their lucrative business aspect, and their purported environmental friendliness. The production of veggie meats involves practices and substances that do not pollute as much as the livestock sector producing traditional meat. Furthermore, veggie meat production and consumption do not involve (direct) animal exploitation and suffering.

Regarding sustainability, the current world population is eight billion and growing. It is estimated that it will reach nine billion by 2050 (Bonny et al. 2015). While affluent countries enjoy an abundance of food, world hunger and food insecurity still exist. Since the number of people in the world is steadily increasing, in order to satisfy their appetite for meat, the amount of meat that is produced needs to be increased, too.
