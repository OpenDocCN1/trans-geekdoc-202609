# 6.2 Challenges with Current Processing Equipment

Except plant-based dairy alternatives, the substance of processing of plant-based novel foods, such as meat alternatives, seafood alternatives, and egg alternatives, is “restructure”. Kinney et al. (2019) from GFI summarized a general process flow diagram of whole meat products, which includes six major steps:

+   * Step 1: TV production;
* Step 2: Hydration/Marination;
* Step 3: Coating;
* Step 4: Cooking;
* Step 5: Extended Shelf-Life Processes;
* Step 6: Packaging and Storage (refrigerate or freeze).

In current market, whole plant-based meat attracts tons of attention, which displays the fibrous texture analogue to match the mouthful of real whole foods, such as beef steak, chicken, breast, and pork. Both dry and wet Vegetable Protein (TVP) produced by extrusion has been used. This list includes, but is not limited to, the global industrial machineries, such as Coper, Buhler, Wexler, Wenger, etc., as well as emerging extrusion machinery in China, such as Jinan DG machinery, Nanjing KY solution Group, Changsha Fumach, and well known lab scale extrusion equipment brands as Thermo Scientific HAAK Pheomex and Brabender DCE.

Currently, the extrusion industry is going through a fierce competition stage under international enterprises and manufacturers, which lead the application of extrusion technology and machine in chemical, plastic, and pharmaceutical industry.
