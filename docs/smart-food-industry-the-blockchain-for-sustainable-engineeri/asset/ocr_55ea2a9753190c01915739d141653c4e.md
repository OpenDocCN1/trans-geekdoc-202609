As a concluding remark, I would like to point out that my discussion about the morality of producing, selling, and consuming veggie meat alternatives serves to illustrate what a virtuous-oriented approach can enable us to show that other ethical theories fail. Our moral understanding has been shaped by certain theories that favor duty, utility, or rights. A peculiar aspect of such theories is that they advise we look at aspects of the world outside ourselves or specific characteristics of animals or people that have should motivate us or show us what we ought to do. However, I believe that their approach often fail to track our moral intuition and in some cases we fail to see important aspects of a certain issue, as I hope to have shown that it is in the case of veggie meat alternatives.
