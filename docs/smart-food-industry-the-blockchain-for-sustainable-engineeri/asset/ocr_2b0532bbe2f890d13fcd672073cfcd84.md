In the meantime, many studies revealed the potential of mealworms as a feed additive in poultry feed formulation to promote growth, increase flesh quality, enhance the immune system and stimulate disease resistance of poultry. Islam and Yang (2017) claimed that mealworm with probiotics, namely lactic acid bacteria, Lactobacillus plantarum, Saccharomyces cerevisiae can stimulate disease-resistant of Ross broiler chick against Salmonella enteritidis and Escherichia coli. Furthermore, Hussain et al. (2017) revealed that mealworms could help broiler chick stimulate disease resistance to Newcastle disease. Sedgh-Gooya et al. (2021) found that mealworms can significantly contribute to broiler weight gain and reduce E. coli in the broiler. # ## 5.2 Aquaculture
