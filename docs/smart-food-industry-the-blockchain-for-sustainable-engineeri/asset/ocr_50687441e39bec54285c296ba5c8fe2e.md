# 6. Conclusion

It has been a challenge to achieve the goals set by the UN for 2030 due to the recent pandemic,
ongoing conflicts, climate change and economic stability. To remedy the long longstanding
gissues of global food inequality and malnutrition, FSs should adopt a more holistic and coordinated
approach. The five action tracks at the UN Food System Summit should be implemented in
all countries considering the legal regulations and nations' nutritional status and deficiencies.

All stakeholders in the FS need to work together to achieve a comprehensive set of actions
that comprises: (1) improving food environments and consumers' food choices, (2) emphasizing
t inherent link between dietary choices and environmental sustainability, (3) raising consumer
aware (4) enhancing sustainable food production at a sufficient scale and by using methods to build
resilience to difficult conditions and finally, (5) developing more inclusive policies and strategies
for the disadvantaged groups in the agri-food sector.

Incorporating alternative protein sources into food products, implementing
sustainable sustainable processing technologies to provide safe and sufficient food supply, enhancing the
nutritional and health-related benefits of foods will be important targets for the FSs of the future.
