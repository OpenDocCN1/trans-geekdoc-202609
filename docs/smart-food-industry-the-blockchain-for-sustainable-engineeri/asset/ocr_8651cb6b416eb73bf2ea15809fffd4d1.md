Plum oil can be an alternative for the production of biofuel, cosmetics and pharmaceuticals thanks to high content of vitamin E. Also, plum seed is a great source of enzymes such as peptidase and angiotensin-converting enzyme (González-Garcia et al. 2018). It can also be an alternative for a cheap source of bioactive peptides, bioabsorbents and activated carbon (Kostić et al. 2016). Vladić et al. (2020) investigated the plum seed as an alternative source of edible oil with high to fatty acids and tocopherols.
