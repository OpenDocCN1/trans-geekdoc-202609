+   - fewer requirements of quality standards, often unnecessary and unjustified;
- fewer purchase by consumers of excess food, followed by their disposal after the expiration of the validity;
- minimum outdated methods and technologies in some countries, which generate high technological loss along the entire food chain;
- minimization of foodconsumption.

From all of these, food waste due to overconsumption is the one that can be interpreted as the most selfish, all possible to be reached, being clearly contradictory in the care for the population that is in a state of starvation, and at the same time for the protection of animals.
