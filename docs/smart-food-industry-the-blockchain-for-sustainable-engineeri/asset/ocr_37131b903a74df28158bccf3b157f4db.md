# Table 1. An overview of the main sectors of the food industry, unit operations considered, and additional process attributes under carbon footprint assessment.

| Food industry sector | Functional units | Main unit operations | Additional attributional processes | Higher environmental impact categories |
| Cereal and grain products | • 1 ha<br>• 1 kg | Farm production → harvest → pre-drying → threshing → selection → drying → storage → milling → packaging | Soil preparation and cultivation; External transportation; Fertilizer production; Semi-products' production, if applicable; | Energy demand; Depletion of soil nutrient resources; Soil loss; Water demand; Land and water contamination; GHG emissions |
| Fruits and vegetables production | • 1 ha<br>• 1 kg | Tillage → manure application → planting → hoeing and fertilization → pesticides application → harvesting → transporting → reception → sorting → cleaning → bleaching → peeling → packaging | Seed production; Fertilizer production; Auxiliary products as detergents for cleaning | Energy demand; Depletion of soil nutrient resources; Soil loss; Water demand; Land and water contamination; GHG emissions |
| Meat and animal products | • 1 kg (live weight)<br>• 1 carcass yield<br>• 1 kg protein | Animal production → pre-slaughter → bleeding → skinning → evisceration → inspection → carcass washing → cooling → storage → meats processing | Feed production; Animal breeding (including enteric fermentation); Manure management; Final production (curing, mixing and cooking, emulsion, pasteurization, fermentation, dehydration, smoking); Wastewater treatment; | GHG emissions; Energy demand; Water consumption; loss |
| Milk and dairy products | • 1 L<br>• 1 kg | Milk collection → reception → filtration → cooling → clarification → homogenization → pasteurization → filling → packaging | Feed production; Animal breeding (including enteric fermentation); Management; Final production (salting, drying, maturation, etc.); Wastewater treatment; | Energy demand; Water consumption; GHG emissions |
| Beverages | • 1L | Main feedstock → mixing → heating → filtration/decanation → fermentation; distillation → filling → storage | Fermentation/distillation; Clarification; | Energy demand; Water consumption |
| Food consumption | • 1 kg<br>• 1 portion pack | Packages → transports → cooking → consumption patterns | Transportation of the product to an average customer or consumer; Customer or consumer use of the product; End-of-life processes of any wasted part of the product; End-of-life processes of packaging waste | Energy demand; GHG emissions; Water consumption
