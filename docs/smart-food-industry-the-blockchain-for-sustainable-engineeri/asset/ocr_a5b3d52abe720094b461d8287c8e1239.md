It is known that dairy-products have a wide range of uses in different industries, apart from the food industry. Biogas, which is formed as a result of fermentation of organic wastes, is an alternative energy source for heating or electricity generation (Sozer and Yaldz 2006). In whey, biogas can be produced by anaerobic digestion of buttermilk and used as fertilizer. In addition, the biomass reduction from biological digestion can be converted into renewable energy (TÜGIS 2016). Andrade et al. (2019) produced edible film from whey proteins and used in sliced packaging, and proved that the whey film inhibited oxygen permeability and lipid oxidation. Whey protein film was also applied to various foods such as chicken breast (Fernández-Pe et al. 2014), salmon slice (Boyaci et al. 2016) and Ricotta cheese (Di Pierro et al. 2011). The utilization of eggs in various industries depends on its main component calcium carbonate, which contains about 40% calcium (Wei et al. 2019, 2020b). Since extraction of calcium from the eggshell is not difficult due to its high solubility, eggshells can be used as a calcium supplement. In addition, eggshells can also be a good raw material for calcium fortified foods. Daengrok et al. (2002) has developed a new functional food by converting calcium carbonate into eggshell powder, used in fermented pork sausage. A study conducted by De Paula et al. (2014), calcium citrate produced from eggs was added to roasted and ground coffee. Authors observed no significant difference in the sensory properties of coffee, even an increase in calcium bioavailability was achieved. The studies regarding the calcium fortification in bakery products by adding eggshell powder directly to formulations include chocolate cake (Ray et al. 2017), maffins (Afzal et al. 2020), and white bread (Platon et al. 2020). Eggshells can also be used as calcium supplement for animal feeds to meet the daily calcium requirements of animals. It is reported that egg powder used in poultry feeds plays an important role in providing calcium source to hens for laying eggs. Furthermore, calcium obtained from eggshell can also be used in the pharmaceutical industry for patients having calcium deficiency, since it is a cheap material unlike other calcium sources (Aditya et al. 2021).
