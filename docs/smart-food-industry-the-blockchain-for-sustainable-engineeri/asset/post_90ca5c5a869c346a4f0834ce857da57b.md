## Veggie Meat Alternatives: A Virtue-Ethical View
### Carlo Alvaro

In this chapter, I discuss some practical and ethical aspects concerning those processed plant-based, meat-like food products referred to as veggie meat alternatives or plant-based meats or mock meats or fake meats (and other terms). I would like to suggest that the production, sale, and consumption of veggie meat alternatives should raise important ethical questions. For example, in what sense, and to what extent, veggie meat products prevent or exacerbate environmental degradation and health problems? Assuming that such products can lead to positive environmental and health results, would it be better to produce, sell, buy, and consume them?

Based on the very nature of the subject, my discussion tangentially encompasses ethical veganism for at least two reasons: veggie meats were originally created for vegans, and the ideal goal of veggie meats seems to be the reduction and, eventually, the substitution of traditional meat with plant-based meat. Vegan, however, is not a diet, but a life choice to act in such a way to avoid as much animal exploitation as possible. As a dietary practice, vegans refrain from eating the flesh of animals or animal products such as dairy, eggs, honey, squid ink, casserole, and so on. For a number of reasons varying from fashion to morality to religion, vegans consume exclusively plant-based food. Their diets vary; yet consume plant-based substances. Vegan as a philosophy and as a diet goes back to the ancient world (Spencer 1996). For millennia, vegan meant consuming all of vegetables, human ingenuity created veggie alternatives. Plant-based products such as tofu (Shurtleff and Aoyagi 2013), tempeh (Babu et al., 2009), and seitan (Day 2011) have been around for centuries.

In addition to vegetables, fruit, grains, legumes, tempeh, seitan, and tofu, especially during the recent years, many vegan vegetables have been replaced by ultra-processed plant-based products that are often crafted to smell and taste, and behave like animal flesh like animal byproducts. Nowadays, such products have become quite popular.
