- Expanding food production to offer more nutrient-rich foods with greater diversity

- Reducing production costs through technology and innovation
- Implementing methods to ensure the preservation of nutrients along the food chain
- Reducing food loss and waste
- Collaborating with the public sector, non-governmental organisations and academia to develop a partnership strategy to deliver healthy and sustainably obtained foods (IFST 2017, Global Panel 2020, Miller et al. 2021, Reyes et al. 2021).
