I have two points in response to such action. First, the idea is that the temperate individual consumes only substances that are beneficial and conducive to flourishing. In the first place there would be no reason for an individual to ever consume sub-optimal or unhealthy substances. In the second place, the temperate individual does not abstain or force himself to avoid certain substances. The temperate individual will adjust his accordingly and will not desire undesirable substances or consume certain substances or engage in sexual intercourse only for taste's or pleasure's sake. In other words for the temperate individual not consuming certain substances will not be restriction or sacrifice. Rather, temperate individuals will not be psychologically dependent on any food in particular.

Second, since certain substances are unhealthy and not optimal for humans, it follows that the temperate individual will not merely avoiding consuming substances that are potentially unhealthy for humans, but also shun the production and sale of such substances. Again, the expression goes, “We are supposed to eat live, not to eat”. Going to modern technology, in affluent societies are constantly encouraged to consume new substances. Moral virtues are not habits; they are solid and reliable dispositions of one's character to act in ways and consistent with reason that are necessary for human flourishing. Moreover, to possess a virtue implies a complex psychological framework concerning right feelings, understanding, insight, and experience. Thus, in acting, the virtuous person will act with a certain attitude and feelings and insight in such a way she will express her virtue in the right amount, the right way, to the right person and circumstances, and (though not for every action) enjoying acting in such a way.
