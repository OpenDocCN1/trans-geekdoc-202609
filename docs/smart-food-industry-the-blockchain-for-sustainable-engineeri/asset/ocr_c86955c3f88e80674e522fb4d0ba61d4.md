While examining total WF by animal group (Table 2), beef cattle contributes the most (33%) to the WF of farm production, driven by dairy cattle (19%), pigs (19%), and broiler chickens (111). Over 97% of the WF related to feeding from grazing and fodder crops in the grazing system, and the WF is predominantly green (94%). The green WF accounts for 77% and 82% of the footprint in mixed and industrial production systems, respectively. The blue WF in the grazing system accounts for 3.6% of the overall WF, with drinking and service-water consumption accounting around 33% of (Mekonnen and Hoekstra 2011).

From 1995 to 2006, the global WF of crop production was 704 Gm³ per year (78% green, 12% blue, and 10% grey) (Mekonnen and Hoekstra 2011). Wheat consumes most of the total volume, accounting for 1087 Gm³ per year (70% green, 19% blue, 11% grey). Rice (992 Gm³/y) and maize (770 Gm³/y) are the other crops with high total WF.
