Some fat by-products are used as raw materials to obtain a mixture of fat acid esters and glycerol. However, animal fat production requires degumming process to remove phospholipid and phosphatoglycerols. Glycerides (triglycerides) can also be converted into a mixture of iso-, and normal phase via heterogeneous catalytic digitalisation to produce biodiesel (Toldra et al. 2016). Biomass-derived bio-fuel is biodegradable, non-toxic, sustainable, and can be used as an alternative to fossil fuels with greenhouse gas emissions such as CO₂, SO2, HC, and NO₂ (Hossain et al. 2015).
