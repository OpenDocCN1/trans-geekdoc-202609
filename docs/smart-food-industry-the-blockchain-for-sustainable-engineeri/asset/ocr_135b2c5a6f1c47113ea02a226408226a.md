# ## 2.1.1.6 IPA Recovery

The vapors from the P-70 dryer (Fig. 6) are condensed in a condenser (P-72), combined with the filtrate of the pectin centrifuge, and sent to the distillation tank (P-73) in the IPA recovery system (Figure 6). The IPA solution is then preheated in a heat exchanger (P-74) and a heater (P-75) feeds into a distillation column (P-76). The purified IPA solution, exits the top of the column with an IPA concentration of about 83% w/w, is used to treat the distillation feed in P-74. Then it is recycled back to the process via a flow adjuster (P-78), which adds fresh "makeup" IPA, and a flow distributor (P-79) shown in Fig. 5. The stillage from the bottom of the distillation column, which contains all the washed-out impurities from the pectin separation centrifuge, is cooled down to 35°C using a cooler (P-77) and removed as waste.
