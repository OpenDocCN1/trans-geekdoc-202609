This is a direct result of the two Newtonian regions of the Cross-type matrix viscosity \(\eta\) at the shear rate limits \(\dot{\gamma} \to 0 \text{ s}^{-1}\) and \(\dot{\gamma} \to \infty \text{ s}^{-1}\).
