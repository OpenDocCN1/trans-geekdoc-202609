# A.2 Coated layered materials with
incompressible coating and rigid core

For applications involving rigid inclusions in incompressible media, we
consider CLMs with an incompressible coating material \(m_2\) and a rigid
core material \(m_1\). Therefore, the spaces \(\mathcal{E}_r^A \subset \text{Sym}_0(3)\) have the form

\(\mathcal{E}_r^A = \{ B \in \text{Sym}_0(3) \mid B \cdot (n_r \otimes_s a) = 0,\)
\(\forall a \in \mathbb{R}^3 \text{ with } a \cdot n_r = 0 \},\) (A.13)

with the dimensions of the spaces \(\mathcal{E}_r^A\) and \(\text{Sym}_0(3)\) given by

\(\text{dim}(\mathcal{E}_r^A) = 3, \text{ and } \text{dim}(\text{Sym}_0(3)) = 5.\) (A.14)

Because the dimension \(\text{dim}(\mathcal{E}_1^A + \mathcal{E}_2^A)\) is bounded by the dimension of
the space \(\text{Sym}_0(3)\), such that

\(\text{dim}(\mathcal{E}_1^A + \mathcal{E}_2^A) \le 5,\) (A.15)

it follows from the dimension of the intersection of the two spaces \(\mathcal{E}_1^A\)
and \(\mathcal{E}_2^A\)

\(\text{dim}(\mathcal{E}_1^A \cap \mathcal{E}_2^A) = \text{dim}(\mathcal{E}_1^A) + \text{dim}(\mathcal{E}_2^A) - \text{dim}(\mathcal{E}_1^A + \mathcal{E}_2^A),\) (A.16)

that

\(\text{dim}(\mathcal{E}_1^A \cap \mathcal{E}_2^A) \ge 3 + 3 - 5 = 1.\) (A.17)
