Interestingly, the constitutive equations for kinematically unconstrained linear elastic solids and linearly viscous fluids share the same structure, see equation (5.19). Additionally, equations (5.32) and (5.33) are satisfied for layered emulsions by definition, because there is no slip between the phases, and the momentum balance (5.11) is satisfied. Thus, the homogenization problem for the considered layered emulsions has the same structure as the homogenization problem for laminates. As a result, the effective properties \(\bar{\mathbb{A}} \in \mathbf{M}^+\) of a two-phase layered emulsion of kinematically unconstrained materials \(m_1, m_2 \in \mathcal{M}\) can be computed with equation (5.23). If the kinematic field \(w\) is solenoidal such that the incompressible balance of mass (5.14) is satisfied, it follows from equation (5.32) that

\(\left[\left[\nabla^s w n\right]\right] \cdot n = 0.\) (5.34)

Also, if the materials \(m_1, m_2 \in \mathcal{M}\) are incompressible, it follows from equations (5.21) and (5.33) that

\(\left[\left[\sigma n\right]\right] = \mathbf{0} \quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad \left[\left[\tau n\right]\right] = \left[\left[p\right]\right] n.\) (5.35)

Therefore, the jumps \(\left[\left[\nabla^s w\right]\right] \in \mathcal{E}^{\text{ico}}\) and \(\left[\left[\sigma\right]\right] \in \mathcal{S}^{\text{ico}}\) live in the mutually orthogonal spaces

\(\mathcal{E}^{\text{ico}} = \{ \text{Sym}_0(3) \ni X = 2 d \otimes_s n - 2(d \cdot n)n \otimes n \mid d \in \mathbb{R}^3 \}\) (5.36)

and

\(\mathcal{S}^{\text{ico}} = \{ \text{Sym}_0(3) \ni X \mid X n = a n, \ a \in \mathbb{R} \}.\) (5.37)

The two operators \(\Gamma_1^{\text{ico}}(n)\) and \(\Gamma_2^{\text{ico}}(n)\), defined through their action on a traceless second order tensor \(X \in \text{Sym}_0(3)\),

\(\Gamma_1^{\text{ico}}(n)[X] = 2(Xn) \otimes_s n - 2(n \cdot Xn)n \otimes n\) (5.38)

and

\(\Gamma_2^{\text{ico}}(n)[X] = X - \Gamma_1^{\text{ico}}(n)[X],\) (5.39)
