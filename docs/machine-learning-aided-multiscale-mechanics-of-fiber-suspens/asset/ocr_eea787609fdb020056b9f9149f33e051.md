In the Newtonian case, the viscosity $\eta_{app}^y$ increases from 530 Pa s to 1581 Pa s as the fiber volume fraction $c_\mathrm{F}$ changes from 5% to 25%. In contrast, the elongational viscosity $\eta_{app}^x$ increases from 769 Pa s to 3468 Pa s as the fiber volume fraction $c_\mathrm{F}$ increases from 5% to 25%. Thus, the elongational viscosities $\eta_{app}^y$ and $\eta_{app}^x$ increase by 198% and 351%, respectively.
