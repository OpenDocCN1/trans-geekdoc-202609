In the Cross-type case, the elongational viscosity \(\eta_{app}\) depends on the shear rate \(\dot{\gamma}\), as highlighted by Figure 3.10 and Table 3.3. At a fiber volume fraction \(c_F = 5\%\), the elongational viscosity \(\eta_{app}^y\) decreases from 529 Pa s to 30 Pa s as the shear rate \(\dot{\gamma}\) changes from \(10\ s^{-1}\) to \(10^5\ s^{-1}\).
