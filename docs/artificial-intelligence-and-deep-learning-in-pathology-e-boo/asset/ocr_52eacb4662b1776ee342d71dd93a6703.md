Some of the tools for accomplishing these tasks have been presented in this chapter, and specific references that illustrate their use in real-world settings have been provided. Important caveats have also been noted. The field is growing at an exponential pace; a number of the references chosen did not exist at the beginning of this project. A major consideration for all of these involves the need for huge datasets. As shown, there are a number of approaches to make the generation of training sets more efficient and hopefully to reduce the size of these sets.

To be useful across laboratories, all these approaches are dependent on standardization of each stage of the process from whole slide imaging to final diagnostic output. It will also require consensus regarding annotation standards. However, given that this is rapidly coming to pass, the future is bright for AI as an augmenter and enhancer of human capability in pathology.
