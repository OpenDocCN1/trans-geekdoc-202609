"Cold" immune subtype of breast cancer visualized with a composite Tumor-TIL map, TCGA BRCA GM-A2DB-01Z-00-DX1. Tumor-TIL analyses in primary infiltrating ductal carcinoma of the breast identifies a predominantly circumferential spatial distribution of peritumoral TILs at the invasive edge of the tumor with focal areas of intratumoral TILs. Composite Tumor-TIL map: (A) low-magnification H&E WSI, (B) automated lymphocyte detection displayed as a spatial probability map, (C) automated tumor detection displayed as a spatial probability map, and (D) Combined Tumor-TIL map with tumor (yellow), lymphocytes (red), and non-tumor tissues (gray). TILs, tumor infiltrating lymphocytes; WSI, whole-slide image; H&E, hematoxylin and eosin.
