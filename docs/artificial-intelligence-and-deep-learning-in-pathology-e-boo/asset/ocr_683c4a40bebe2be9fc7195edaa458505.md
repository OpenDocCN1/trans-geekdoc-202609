In data driven deep learning systems the output classifications of images are derived from numerous calculations within “hidden” layers often without an explanation of the factors which influence the decisions of the learning system. Deep learning AI systems such as this convolutional neural network input large amounts of data with limited human labels. Convolutional and pooling layers may feed fully connected layers which in turn drive an output classifier. Errors in the output classifier are opportunities for human intelligence to correct the system and feed these new labels back into the input of the system.
