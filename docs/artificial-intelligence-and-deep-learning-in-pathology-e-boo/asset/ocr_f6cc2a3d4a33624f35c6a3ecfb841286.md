# FIGURE 8.2 Quantification of lymphocytic infiltration with the tumor's invasive margin or core.

(A) A whole slide image of a colorectal cancer tissue section labeled for tumor cells (green) and nuclei (blue). The image analysis in this figure is performed using Indica Labs HALO software. The red outline is the automatically detected deepest invasion of tumor cells and the inset shows a zoomed in example of the image segmented into an invasive margin (green) and the tumor core (blue). The purple square denotes where Figure (B and C) originate from in the invasive margin. (B) Multiplexed immunofluorescence visualized CD3 (yellow) and CD8 (red) positive lymphocytes. (C) Automated quantification of these lymphocytes within just the invasive margin of the tumor.
