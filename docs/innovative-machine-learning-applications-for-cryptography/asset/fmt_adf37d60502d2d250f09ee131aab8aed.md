
# AI-Supported Bio Cryptography: Challenges and Future Directions

## Challenges in Bio Cryptography

Despite the numerous advantages of AI-supported bio cryptography, several critical challenges remain to be addressed. These challenges threaten the authentication integrity of bio cryptographic systems and are discussed in three main aspects.

### Identity Spoofing

With the support of AI technology, personality spoofing can be created using various AI tools. This spoofing can occur in image-based biometrics, where AI techniques can generate mitigated identities for fingerprints, irises, faces, voices, and palms. Fake photos can be substituted to fraudulently prove authentication. This spoof mirage blinds human observers, making them unable to differentiate between real and replica biometrics. A solution for this challenge must be developed in the near future.

### Deepfakes

Deepfakes represent a newly reported threat where artificially created personalities are generated as moving images (videos). These fake videos create false proof and damage personal reputations. Deepfakes combine morphed images and voices of a person to create a moving image portrayed as real. The human eye cannot differentiate between real and fake content. This poses a significant challenge requiring immediate solutions.

### Legal and Civil Challenges

There is a pressing need for rules and regulations to protect human credential identities. Personal details can be compromised and revealed in the real world today. To protect these details, proper laws must be framed accordingly, and strong punishments should be enforced for defaulters. This regulatory gap is also considered a challenge that needs to be resolved (Incode, 2022).

## Conclusion and Future Study

Bio cryptography and authentication based on bio cryptography represent the future of protecting human credentials. Since there is no replacement for human individuality, this mode of protection may provide superior security. With the support of AI-supported algorithms, multiple human aspects will be mapped to create a relative cryptographic cipher lock for encryption. This encryption will be stronger, and the decryption key—the personal identity—will establish a better security bound for data.

This evolution may occur in the near future, within half a decade. Biometric-based authentication can act as a key for all kinds of banking transactions, locking mechanisms, medical record security, travel verification, and data protection via biometric keys. In the future, biometric authentication will act as an essential feature for all kinds of daily activities (Mthunzi et al., 2019).
