## 5. CONCLUSION

The proposed solution offers a novel way to prevent front-vehicle rear-end incidents. This paper has proposed a secure, lightweight encryption technology with ANN to protect information in vehicle brake light images. The method relies on an ANN developed and trained using a vehicle brake light image dataset. If the ANN determines that the driver will not be able to respond quickly enough to avoid a collision, an alarm will be triggered. The research found that the proposed method might cut the frequency of rear-end crashes by as much as half. These results imply that the proposed method may dramatically enhance road safety.

There are several ways the proposed method might be enhanced. For instance, the ANN may be taught using a more extensive collection of brake light information, enabling it to better understand driver behavior and make reliable predictions. The suggested method may be generalized to encompass a broader range of collision scenarios, including side impacts and frontal collisions, making it more flexible for road safety applications. In the future, we will work with AI approaches and ML models to avoid rear-end crashes.
