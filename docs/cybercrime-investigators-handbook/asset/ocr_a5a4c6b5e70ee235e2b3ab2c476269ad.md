# PROTECTION OF EVIDENCE

While preserving the chain of evidence is crucial, there is also a wide range of work that is required to be undertaken with exhibits to preserve their authenticity. Each person involved in examining, involving, storing, and transporting exhibits needs to understand their responsibilities to their safety and security. Seven considerations for investigation and exhibit officers follow:

+   1. Chain of custody
See the previous section, “Preservation of the Chain of Custody,” for discussion.
2. Secure storage
Securing an exhibit requires that it be placed in an environment where there is no opportunity for it to be interfered with and its credibility vanished. Law enforcement use designated storage units and locked facilities whose access is restricted and recorded. Only persons who have a lawful and necessary reason to access an exhibit should be anywhere near once seized.
First off, where a device has access to wireless or cellular networks, the device should be placed in flight mode (or similar method, depending on the device) and/or where feasible, placed into a Faraday bag similar container that inhibits network access. When a device is connected to a wireless network, you may disconnect the connection. With a cellular device, removing the SIM card will be of benefit. Record notes of your actions involving the device, as will affect the logs within the device. As a back up proceed a very basic way of helping to stop any potential interference with digital evidence connected to the Internet by a remote suspect or their associate is to place it in a clean metal container with a secure seal and/or wrap the device in aluminum foil.
3. Statements and exhibit schedulenThe statement covering the integrity of the exhibit is an explanation to the court that the evidence can be relied upon as being genuine. Should there be little effort to explain the integrity of the exhibit or show gaps in its preservation be identified, it opens a very large door to the other side’s legal team to get the exhibit removed from the case as unreliable.
