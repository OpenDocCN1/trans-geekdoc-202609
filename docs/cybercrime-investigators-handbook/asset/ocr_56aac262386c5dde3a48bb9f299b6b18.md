# Securing Evidence

The primary roles of the IR team are to contain the threat, mitigate it, and return the company to a higher standard of security. To accomplish this, the IR team has to work quickly and make rapid decisions, which may conflict with the wishes of the lead investigator. We previously explained the example of the IR team deciding that shutting down a virtual server and rebooting it in a state would eradicate a threat coming from a cloud server that has been infected. While this action may be the best option for containing the threat, valuable log data, which may provide evidence of the suspect and their activities, will be destroyed and not be able to be recovered.

In the course of the IR team's operations, the thought of commencing a post-event investigation to locate a suspect may not be surface. However, once the extent of the breach has been identified, the decision may be made to begin an investigation and refer the matter to police. Unfortunately, as stated previously, valuable evidence will have been destroyed and not able to be recovered.

The cyber investigator should seek all available evidence from a multitude of sources. However, evidence may not always be available without external cooperation, in the instances where third parties, such as cloud-computing providers, control systems hardware/software.

The investigator needs to be aware of all the different sources of evidence that can be generated by computer devices and ensure that they are properly secured.

> Tip: Pre-event training exercises highlight the role of the IR and investigation teams and how their objectives may complement or conflict with each other. Building the rules and duties of each party into a response plan will help to preserve evidence. A direction from management to the IR team that evidence is to be collected as a part of their duties will assist the investigator.

Where a live event is occurring and there is no IR plan in place, communication and respect for each other's role is required, with management providing direction to all parties.
