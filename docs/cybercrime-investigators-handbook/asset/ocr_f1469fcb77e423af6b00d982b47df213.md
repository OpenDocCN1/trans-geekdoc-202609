# Preservation of Evidence

Preservation of evidence—protecting it from loss, damage, or tampering—is a key responsibility of members of the scene team. This is everybody's responsibility. Whatever activity is taken regarding the exhibits from the time of discovery through presentation in court must be recorded. The team must also be able to explain to the satisfaction of the court why the evidence can be relied on and is in the same state when discovered by explaining the maintenance of its integrity and authenticity, including the chain of custody.

A key task is to record the team member who located the exhibit, where it was found, and who seized it.
