# CYBERCRIME CASE STUDY I

A multinational company installed new fraud-detection software in their accounting systems. A systems administrator stated it was a highly regarded product, which when turned on was "lit up like a Christmas tree". Over the coming months, the system continually identified fraudulent accounting transactions and provided warnings.
Members of the system operator's team were so annoyed by the constant warnings that they determined the application was working incorrectly and turned it off. Unfortunately, the application was working correctly and each of the alerts were an accurate identification of a suspicious transaction. The total loss to the multinational was in excess of $20 million, and much of that was lost after the fraud detection system had been installed and turned off. In this case, the technology was working correctly but was not understood by the operators.
