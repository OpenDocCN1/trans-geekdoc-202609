# Computer Hacking to Gain Access to System Resources

Corporations and educational and governmental organizations have access to large and powerful computer systems with processors and bandwidth. An attacker may break into a system to host a criminal site (such as a fraudulent online pharmacy) or to use the bandwidth to launch a Denial of Service (DoS) attack against another target. Alternatively, they may seek to use these resources to mine Bitcoin.

This form of attack may be lost in the traffic of normal day-to-day activity where there is a high volume of activity on internal and external networks; however, use of the bandwidth resources confers a cost to the victim company.
