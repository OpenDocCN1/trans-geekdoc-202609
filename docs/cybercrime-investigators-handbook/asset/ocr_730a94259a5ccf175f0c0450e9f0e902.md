+   * Rubbish bins and confidential document collection bins. These contain documents discarded by the suspect and may contain valuable information. Although going through else's trash may not be the most pleasant of tasks, it is worth to locate a valuable piece of evidence that links a suspect to the crime and to develop new lines of investigation.
