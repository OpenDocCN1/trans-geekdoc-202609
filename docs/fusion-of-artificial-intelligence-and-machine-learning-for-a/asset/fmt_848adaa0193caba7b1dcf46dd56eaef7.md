Cloud technology empowers IoT for regular appliances such as air conditioners, refrigerators, etc. The cloud offers vast storage and data availability, and the use of cloud with IoT also increases.

## 6. Provides Security and Privacy

IoT is incomplete without security, but the collaboration of both Cloud has made IoT more secure with preventive, investigative, and preventive measures. It has the ability to provide users with strong security measures through authentication and encryption protocols.

### 7.4.3 Challenges of Cloud and Iot Together Working

#### Data Handling

Handling a large amount of data that evolves due to the merging of IoT with cloud is a significant challenge for managing big data.

#### Protocols Used in Networking and Communication

In Cloud and IoT working infrastructure, a large number of machine-tomachine communications with various types of devices, having various protocols, are used. So, managing a variety of protocols is not an easy task.

#### Management of Sensor Networks

Processing and management of a large amount of sensor data and network is not an easy task, as it significantly increases the burden of cloud computing in handling data. Additionally, cloud computing introduces new challenges in aggregating sensor data within a cloud environment, which can lead to security and privacy concerns.

#### Security and Privacy Issues

The IoT devices have allowed us to automate tasks, but there are various security and privacy concerns associated with them. While the Cloud offers robust encryption and authentication protocols, making it a secure platform for users, there are still concerns regarding identity hiding and data misuse. The availability of data on the cloud makes this a significant concern. Therefore, the merging of IoT applications with cloud platforms is a topic of research from a security perspective.

### 7.4.4 Role Of Artificial Intelligence (Ai)

AI is used to solve and perform complex and critical cognitive tasks. It makes things possible that were not even possible before the revolution of technology. With the use of inference rules like forward and backward chaining, it tries to conclude results and mimic the human brain with a structured approach. AI is applicable to process large volumes of data received from various sources such as online applications, sensors, and structured and unstructured forms of data from social media. ML, incorporated with IoT and cloud computing, is used to analyze patterns and retrieve reliable and relevant data in various decision-making situations. The cloud plays an important role in enhancing the capability of applications that incorporate AI. Artificial intelligence (AI) includes ML, speech recognition, and other technologies used by cloud providers to offer API-based applications that are intelligent and AI-enabled. IoT connects AI-enabled devices available on the cloud environment to provide better control over home and business appliances.

The internet of things (IoT) is transforming the world by offering multiple solutions to various problems. It works more efficiently when combined with cloud computing applications.
