Mahendra Swain is presently associated with MIET, Jammu, as assistant professor. He has completed his BTech in ECE from Centurion University of Technology and Management, Bhubaneswar, and M.Tech from Lovely Professional University. He has published various research articles and attended national and international conferences in the fields of embedded systems and Internet of Things. He has chosen electrical and communication engineering with an intense urge to delve into challenging fields, such as embedded systems, wireless communication, electronic product development, and IoT.
