# Preface

The Internet of Things provides direct integration of the physical world with computer-based systems, which results in economic benefits, efficiency improvements, and reduced human exertedions.

The primary objective of writing this book is to provide a platform to the readers to get started with the Internet of Things with Raspberry Pi and Arduino along with the basic knowledge of programming and interfacing of the input/output devices.

The book provides a gradual pace of basic to advanced interfacing and programming of Arduino and Raspberry Pi. It is comprised of 4 sections and 12 chapters with a few case studies to make the concept clear and test its feasibility in solutions to real-time problems.

This book is entirely based on the practical experience of the authors while undergoing projects with students and industries.
