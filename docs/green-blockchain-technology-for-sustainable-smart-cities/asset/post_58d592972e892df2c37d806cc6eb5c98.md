## 1. Introduction

The innovation-driven economy and embarking on the path of green development has become a widely recognized goal of people around the world. Green technology innovation is playing a vital importance for organizations and communities all around the world. Investigations in these innovative green technologies have mainly witnessed an increasing trend over recent years. Moreover, environmental degradation has turned into a major threat to human well-being. A large number of organizations and communities have been targeted for green tech innovation, as it’s a strategy to achieve environmental protection and economic boom (Cao et al., 2021). To attain the sustainability, the people should not harm and overuse natural resources in extraordinary manner. Furthermore, environmental sustainability and economic profitability are great significance for future technological innovation, without green technology innovation; there can be no sustainable development in a real sense (Laut & Fliaster, 2020). As a global financial center with a growing strategic interest in green finance, this integrates environmental risks and opportunities into day-to-day financing and investment decisions for the development of financial services and products that make a substantial contribution to environmental sustainability.

The blockchain as an emerging technology can perfectly adapt to the information needs of sustainable management, given its significant potential for addressing environmental and economic sustainability taking place all around the world. Blockchain technology promotes effective monitoring, reporting, and verification and increases transparency and accountability in green finance (Tian et al., 2020). Green financing could be promoted through changes in countries’ regulatory frameworks, harmonizing public financial incentives, increases in green financing from different sectors, alignment of public sector financing decision-making with the environmental dimension of the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), increases in investment in clean and green technologies, financing for sustainable natural resource–based green economies and climate smart blue economy, and increased use of green bonds. Developing a greener economy has become a global consensus which needs the support of green technology to guarantee the coordinated development of environment and economy (Tan et al., 2021).

The other way blockchain technology can facilitate the advent of new green financial products is to package the returns of environmental financial products with those of other instruments and debt applied to financing environmentally friendly assets, and to do so entirely digitally. Financial exclusion is estimated to affect around 1.7 billion people worldwide who lack access to even the most basic of financial services. There are many reasons for financial exclusion including geographic barriers, lack of identity documents, and lack of trust in local financial institutions. Blockchain can help to achieve the solutions by supporting the creation of a trusted framework for transactions between parties who do not know each other and the use of smart contracts and to make the relation between fundamental design and blockchain applications robust (Krishnan et al., 2020).

In addition, blockchain technology can improve the carbon asset transaction system and provide global investors with a seamless and frictionless transaction solution. It can also make trading schemes more efficient through new approaches to price carbon credits and connect distributed carbon markets, besides decentralizing the trade of renewable energies and carbon credits (Thaker et al., 2022). The efficient use of smart contracts in the process will also facilitate automation, high-speed execution, traceability, and transparency in transactions.

## 2. Green finance

Sustainable finance or environmental finance is a financial innovation aiming to connect financial industry to meet the needs of environmental protection and apply financial tools designed for developing, promoting, and supporting positive and durable production activity, which has committed to solve problems like environmental pollution and the greenhouse effect and promote coordinated economic society. Green finance is becoming increasingly sophisticated. As it becomes more complex, it becomes harder to trace the financial flows and ensure investment is reaching its desired destiny. It includes many financial products and services, such as green bonds, green investment funds, carbon market, and energy trading, alongside initiative financial institutions, including green banks and green funds (Taghizadeh-Hesary and Yoshino, 2019). Green finance is a financial tool that improves environmental quality to support sustainable development. Green financial services have become the mainstream trend of financial development, and it paves the way of financial innovation in environmental protection, which acts as a link between the financial industry and the environmental industry.

### 2.1 Green financial development

Green finance is introduced to make this world a better place for corporate and individuals. Currently, the top-most organizations throughout the world are introducing and implementing green finance in their countries to create a sustainable and eco-friendly environment. Green finance mainly promotes the flow of financial instruments toward the development of sustainable business projects, social investment, social trade, and environmental policies. Green financing could be promoted through changes in countries’ regulatory frameworks, harmonizing public financial incentives, increases in green financing from different sectors, alignment of public sector financing decision-making with the environmental dimension of the SDGs, increases in investment in clean and green technologies, financing for sustainable natural resource–based green economies, and increased use of green bonds (Zhang and Wei, 2020). Since public budgets in green finance are insufficient, the market must change its approach to accelerate the flow of capital and investments to green projects for sustainable development.

It is important to recognize that the ideals on which green finance operates are not anything new. There have always been people who believed in investment that focused on more than simply returns. Credit unions, rural co-operatives, and many philanthropic groups were using sustainable investment principles (Wang et al., 2021). Nevertheless, the world of green finance today is far larger and more diverse than anything seen in history. The industry is made up of many stakeholders all of which play a critical role in raising and using capital in sustainable projects.

### 2.2 The revolution of green finance by adopting financial innovative technology

The green finance revolution is underway with a powerful beginning to a pivotal decade for climate action. The financial innovation shows the tremendous potential for acceleration in the green bond market. Reaching the vital milestone of the first $1 trillion in annual green investment toward clean technologies, transition and building climate resilience can become a reality by 2030. ESG goals. While green bonds have the potential to become powerful financial tools in creating positive climate action, there are areas for optimization and improvement in the traditional green bond model.

According to the report of the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), green financing is to increase the level of financial flows (from banking, microcredit, insurance, and investment) from the public, private, and not-for-profit sectors to sustainable development priorities. Green finance for many years was only a small niche of the investment world; however, now it is taking both the public and private sectors by storm. Green financial development may provide sufficient funds for activities of green technology innovation, which leads to improve energy efficiency, decrease carbon emission, and reduce extreme weather risks. Technological innovation with rational financial support can stimulate the environmentally friendly industrial scale, which result in attaining global goals. The interaction of innovation in green tech and green finance has served as a potential solution to problems of energy structure transformation, environmental pollution reduction, and climate change mitigation.

The United Nations and other organizations have standards like CERES (Coalition for Environmentally Responsible Economies) principles, which is a model corporate code of environmental conduct that tries to identify qualities to look for in green finance when evaluating projects or companies. There are also organizations that help to identify companies and score them for environmental, social, and governance (ESG) or socially responsible investors like Sustainalytics or Morgan Stanley Capital International. Each investor, firm, and government must decide for themselves what fits their goals.

There is the public sector of green finance which is composed of governmental entities that create incentives like subsidies or take on projects of their own like public transport or water management using bonds for funding. They can also work with the private sector using Green Banks. Green Banks, which can be established in numerous forms, set sustainable goals and then achieve these by leveraging large amounts of private capital using relatively small amounts of public money. Green Banks use multiple financial tools to do this such as credit enhancements and loan programs and then expect to be paid back. With proper management, green banks can have a dramatic environmental impact as well as be revenue positive. Often, projects taken on by the public sector are ones that the private sector will not provide adequate financing for due to either a lack of adequate return on investment or too much risk.

## 3. Embracing green technological innovation for sustainable development

The advancement in green technology reflects a highly positive impact on the environment, which helps us to utilize naturally occurring energy and reduce the negative impact of human activity on the environment. Investments in green technologies are blooming every year and more countries contemplate to adopt green technologies and ditch nonrenewable resources; this could help to build a more sustainable future.
