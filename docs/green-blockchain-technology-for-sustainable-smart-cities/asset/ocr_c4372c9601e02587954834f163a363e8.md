# 1. Introduction

The innovation-driven economy and embanking on the path of green development has become a widely recognized goal of people around the world. Green technology innovation is playing a vital importance for organizations and communities all around the world. Investigations in these innovative green technologies have mainly witnessed an increasing trend over recent years. Moreover, environmental degradation has turned into a major threat to human well-being. A large number of organizations and communities have been targeted for green tech innovation, as it’s a strategy to achieve environmental protection and economic boom (Cao et al., 2021). To attain the sustainability, the people should not harm and overuse natural resources in extraordinary manner. Furthermore, environmental sustainability and economic profitability are great significance for future technological innovation, without green technology innovation; there can be no sustainable development in a real sense (Laut & Fliaster, 2020). As a global financial center with a growing strategic interest in green finance, this integrates environmental risks and opportunities into day-to-day financing and investment decisions for the development of financial services and products that make a substantial contribution to environmental sustainability.

The blockchain as an emerging technology can perfectly adapt to the information needs of sustainable management, given its significant potential for addressing environmental and economic sustainability taking place all around the world. Blockchain technology promotes effective monitoring, reporting, and verification and increases transparency and accountability in green finance (Tian et al., 2020).
