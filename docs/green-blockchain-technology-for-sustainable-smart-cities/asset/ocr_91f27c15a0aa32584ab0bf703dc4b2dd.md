**Project Genesis:** A green bond issuance prototype launched by the Bank of International Settlements Innovation Hub and the Hong Kong Monetary Authority. **Green Asset Wallet:** The first blockchain platform for validating and reporting financial impact, exclusively designed to provide transparency, efficiency, and trust in the green debt market. It bridges sustainable investors with green investment privileges through blockchain. By scaling the green debt supply, Green Asset Wallet makes sustainable investments accessible for potential investors.

**WePower:** A platform that establishes robust connections among energy suppliers, corporate buyers, and energy producers by providing effortless green energy transactions. With the power of blockchain, it makes energy sourcing as simple as online shopping. The platform provides a high degree of flexibility in signing direct energy contracts. WePower sees blockchain and renewable energy as the next mighty couple of the energy market.
