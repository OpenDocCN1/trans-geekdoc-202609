# 5.5 Green blockchain technology can drive a future in sustainable finance

Integrating blockchain-enabled finance could lead to a more green and sustainable future. A game-changing approach of transition to clean energy and accelerating investments in green projects require groundbreaking infrastructure and pioneering green financing strategies. Blockchain technology can provide security, transparency, auditability, and traceability which helps to fill the green finance gap (Du et al., 2022). With powerful applications of green blockchain constantly surfacing, and serious thought in an investment toward reducing energy consumption and tackling social inequalities through blockchain can contribute to a greener future with potential to fill the green investment gap.

Innovative financial instruments and new capital sources are needed to enable green finance to achieve global green targets investment allowing a greater pool of investors to diversify their own portfolios (Falcone, 2020). In other words, in the returns generated from a traditional asset or instrument, combine green assets with the typical dividends and distributions. This will simultaneously build up investment in green finance while allowing a greater pool of investors to dip their toes in the water of green investing and, thereby, further diversify their own portfolios. By monitoring, verifying, and reporting on assets all digitally and offering environmentally friendly financial products combined with other tokenized instruments, it allows for diversified returns and proof of environmental impact in one.

# 6. Blockchain can power green finance

Blockchain technology and digital securities are a game-changer for all private market issuances because they automate manual processes, allowing the securities to be distributed in smaller units and to be traded freely on an exchange (Krishnan et al., 2020). Through blockchain technology and smart contracts, digitization can also significantly reduce upfront issuance and ongoing administrative costs, which have limited access to traditional bond markets to larger issuers. Tokenization is an excellent way to create traceability and capture ESG credentials that encompasses the overall organization's implementation and helps to manages risks and opportunities around sustainability issues. Internet of Things devices and artificial intelligence could also be used on location to collect real-time data to improve traceability and investor reporting (Saravanan and Srinivasan, 2018).

Blockchain technology, as Fintech, provides various solutions for green finance and sustainable development. Blockchain ensures the better tracking of green finance flows in real time. It takes sustainable finance to a new level through fund distribution. It also expands microfinancing and provides a matchmaking platform for investors and green project developers, helping green investment projects become a huge success (Schletz et al., 2020).
