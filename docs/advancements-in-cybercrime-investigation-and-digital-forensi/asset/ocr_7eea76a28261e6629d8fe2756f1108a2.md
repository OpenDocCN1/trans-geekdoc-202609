# 9.5.1 SSD DATA RECOVERY METHOD

Aiming at the idea of data recovery in three aspects: the logical layer, the firmware layer, and the physical layer of the solid-state drive, the researchers carried out a series of research and experiments and summarized three data recovery methods.

Select five small files as the files to be deleted, and note the position and the data of the first and last sectors to verify the integrity of the file. Add two large files with a size of 512 MB and write down the location (head sector and tail sector) and data size before deleting to verify file integrity. Delete two large files and five small files.
