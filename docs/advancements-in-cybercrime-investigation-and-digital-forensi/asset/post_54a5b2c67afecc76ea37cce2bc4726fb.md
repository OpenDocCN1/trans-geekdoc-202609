
5. **Website Design and Languages:** Good websites typically have an appealing design, and English is ideal. There are no mistakes in grammar and spelling, and the enjoyment of buying from a real website online is polished and trouble-free. A faux E-commerce website could have many mistakes, even on the important pages. When you notice language errors all over the website online, you need to be cautious about the site.

6. **Ads on the Website:** There would be commercials on every web page, as that is one of the ways to make money online. But if commercials outnumber the content, then it’s time to be careful. Be wary if you have to get beyond numerous redirects and popups. To get to where you need to be. To find out whether or not a website is real or not, you have to be very alert, and seeing too many commercials is one of the giveaways.

7. **Based on Payment Mode:** A genuine E-commerce website will receive all principal debit and credit cards and provide other non-card alternatives also. If a website asks you to transfer cash via PayPal or Western Union or use only cryptocurrency for the charge, that needs to be an exact red flag. If you have to pay through a way in which the payment cannot be reversed, you definitely must not purchase from this site.
