## 2.6 CONCLUSION

Digital forensics helps in investigation for the legal purpose involving the analysis of digital evidence. Digital forensics utilizes specialized tools and techniques for the investigation of various computer-oriented crimes such as fraud, illicit use, and many forms of computer intrusion. There are different kinds of digital forensics, such as drone forensics, network forensics, social forensics, and MF. Different types of forensics help in providing integrity of the computer systems and help in giving evidence to the court that leads to punishment of the culprit, which gives the appropriate efficiency to keep track of cyber criminals. Here AI and ML algorithms play a major role in digital forensics for intrusion detection, identity theft, cyberstalking, and slander spreading. Automated techniques such as RPA tools help in implementing data protection regulations with the support of AI and ML. It also provides encryption which helps the user to secure all kinds of data, and custom developed software helps in protecting the data, especially in e-commerce sites.
