The Pearson Addison-Wesley Signature Series provides readers with practical and authoritative information on the latest trends in modern technology for computer professionals. The series is based on one simple premise: great books come from great authors.

Vaughn Vernon is a champion of simplifying software architecture and development, with an emphasis on reactive methods. He has a unique ability to teach and lead with Domain-Driven Design using lightweight tools to unveil unimaginined value. He helps organizations achieve competitive advantages by enduring tools such as architecture, patterns, and approaches, and through partnerships between business stakeholders and software developers.

Vaughn's Signature Series guides readers towards advances in software development maturity and greater success with business-centric practices. The series emphasizes organic refinement with a variety of approaches—reactive, object, and functional architecture and programming;domain modeling; right-sized services; patterns; and APIs—covers best uses of the associated underlying technologies.
