Critical infrastructure encompasses a wide range of sectors, including energy, water, transportation, healthcare, and finance. These sectors are the lifeblood of nations, and any disruption or compromise can have far-reaching consequences. As such, ensuring the resilience and security of these systems is of utmost importance.
