The enhancement of $\mu_{orb}$, in the clusters with respect to the bulk moment $\mu_{orb}$ results from several correlations changes related to the local environment of the atoms.⁶⁹ The first contribution is the reduction of the local coordination number leading to an increase of the spin polarizations, which spin-orbit interactions enhance large orbital moment. The second contribution is the distribution of intraatomic Coulomb interaction, which favors the occupation of states with high $m$ and contributes to the enhancement of $\mu_{orb}$ in clusters. The final contribution is the presence of degeneracies in the spectrum of one-electron energies that allow for a very effective spin-orbit moment that enhances $\mu_{orb}$.

The magnetic moments of the Ni clusters are decreasing from surface atoms.⁴⁸,⁶⁹ The analysis of Wan et al. indicates that the orbital and spin moments of cluster atoms with atomic coordination 8 or larger are similar to those in the bulk $(\mu_{spin} \approx 0.55 \mu_{B}$, and $\mu_{orb} \approx 0.05 \mu_{B}$)⁷³ that is, the orbital moment is almost quenched for internal cluster atoms. In contrast, there is a large enhancement of the spin and orbital moments for atoms with coordination less than 8. This enhancement increases with the coordination deficit, and it is larger for the orbital moment.
