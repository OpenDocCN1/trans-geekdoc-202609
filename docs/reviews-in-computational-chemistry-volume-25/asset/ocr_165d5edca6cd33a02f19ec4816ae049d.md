The strategy is based on earlier observations that the Lanczos recursion regenerates vectors that have already been generated, because of the loss of long-range orthogonality. These redundant vectors will make contributions to the overlap $	ilde{c}_{m,k}$ that have already been accounted for. The above procedure essentially removes the contributions of these redundant Lanczos vectors from the prescribed vectors. In the ideal case where there is only one copy of a converged eigenvector, Eq. [52] is identical to Eq. [48], and eventually Eq. [46]. Numerical tests showed that the results obtained from the two SLP versions are alike with much precision.103

Both RRGM and SLP have been used to compute various transition amplitudes with high efficiency and accuracy.
