Given the definition of a bond distance, we can analyze species lifetimes. The lifetime of all species is less than 12 fs at 2.6 g/cc, which is roughly the period of an O-H bond vibration (ca. 10 fs). Hence, water does not contain any molecular states above 75 GPa and 2000 K but instead forms a collection of short-lived “transient” states. The “L” simulations at 2.6 g/cc (77 GPa) and 2000 K yield lifetimes nearly to that found in the “S” simulations (within 0.5 fs), which indicates that the amorphous states formed from the “L” simulations are closely related to the supersonic bcc crystal states found in the “S” simulations.
