CASPT2 excitation energy varies between 1.68 and 1.74 eV, and the oscillator strength that varies between 0.007 and 0.092, shows that the oscillator strength is very sensitive to the active space. The energy of this transition is a result of the $\delta$ bond, which affects the $\delta^*$ orbital at low energy.

In the region of weak absorption between 1.98 and 3.10 eV, (16,000–25,000 cm$^{-1}$), the first peak occurs at 2.19 eV (17,675 cm$^{-1}$) and has been assigned to a $\delta ightarrow \pi^+$ ($^1$$A_{1g} ightarrow 2^1$$A_{ig}$) transition located mostly on Re. We predicted it to be 2.29 eV, and it is a forbidden transition. Two bands have been assigned to charge transfer (CT) states. They occur at 3.35 eV and 3.48 eV, respectively. Thus, it seems natural to assign the upper of the two bands to this transition. A 3.35 eV band has been assigned to a localized transition.
