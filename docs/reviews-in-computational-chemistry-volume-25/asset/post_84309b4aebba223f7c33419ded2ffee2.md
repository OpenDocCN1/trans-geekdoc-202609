## CLUSTERS OF THE 4d elements

The 4d metals are nonmagnetic in the bulk phase. However, the free atoms are magnetic, and consequently, it is reasonable to expect that small clusters of these elements could be magnetic. Experiments$^{5,106}$ show that Rh clusters with less than 60 atoms and Ru and Pd clusters with less than 12 atoms are magnetic. Several calculations have investigated the magnetism of those clusters assuming model structures. Patterns across the cross of the periodic table have been shown by performing DFT calculations for six-atom clusters with octahedral structure$^{107}$ and the magnetic moments and the largest are given in Table 2. All clusters, except $Y_6$, $Pd_6$, and $Cd_6$, have finite magnetic moments and the largest occur for Ru, $Rh_6$ ($1.6 \mu_B$/atom and $0.99 \mu_B$/atom).
