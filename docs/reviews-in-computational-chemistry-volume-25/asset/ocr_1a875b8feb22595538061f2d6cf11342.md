The $\alpha$ time scale is also the time scale of the final decay in the scattering $S(q,t)$, to be coherent or incoherent scattering. This time scale is typically obtained by fitting KWW Eq. [9] time to the final decay of the correlators. In a finite temperature regime above $T_c$, the stretching exponent $eta$ of the KWW functions is independent of temperature. In this temperature range, $eta$ is momentum transfer dependent with values between 0.65 and 0.75 and approaching $q \to \infty$.
