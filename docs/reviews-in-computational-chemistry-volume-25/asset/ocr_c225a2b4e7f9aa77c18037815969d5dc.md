For PB, comparison of $^{13}C$ NMR spin-lattice relaxation times and nuclear enhancement (NOE) values from simulation and experiment over a wide range of melt temperatures revealed excellent agreement.$^{168}$ A comparison between simulation and experiment for two temperatures for different resonances is shown in Figure 17. Comparing the variation in $T_1$ for carbon atoms in different chemical environments with the mean waiting time between conformational transitions for the different types of torsions in PB ($eta$, cis-allyl and trans-allyl), one concludes that spin-lattice relaxation for a given nucleus cannot be associated with the dynamics of any particular rotation. Instead, the $^{13}C$ relaxation occurs as the result of multiple conformational events involving several neighboring torsions.$^{168}$ However, close correspondence was found for torsional autocorrelation times $	au_{	ext{tor}}$.
