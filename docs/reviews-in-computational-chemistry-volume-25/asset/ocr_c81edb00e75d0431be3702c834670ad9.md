The CASPT2 approach, by contrast, significantly overestimates the stability of bis($$\mu$-oxo) isomers.

The relative energies of $\mu$-$\eta^1$ (trans-end) and $\mu$-$\eta^2$ (side-on) peroxo isomers (Figure 4) of $Cu_2O_2$ fragments supported by 0, 2, 4, and 6 ammonia ligands have also been computed with various density functional, CC, and multfigurational protocols. Substantial disagreement exists among the different levels of theory for many cases, although completely renormalized CC methods seem to offer the most reliable predictions. The significant radical character of the end-on peroxo isomer is problematic for the density functions, whereas the demands on active space size and the need to account for interactions between different states in second-order perturbation theory prove be challenging for the multireference treatments.
