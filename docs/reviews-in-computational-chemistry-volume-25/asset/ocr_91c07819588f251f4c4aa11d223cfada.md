We have studied the structure and dynamics of the water environment on a uranyl ion using such an approach (the nonempirical model potential, NEMO, method), which has been developed during the last 15 years.$85,86}$ It has been used primarily to systems like liquid water and water clusters, liquid formaldehyde and acetonitrile, and the solvation of organic molecules and inorganic ions in water. A recent review$^{85}$ by Engkvist contains references on specific applications.

The interaction between uranyl and a water molecule has been studied using accurate quantum methods.$^{87}$ The information has been used to fit a NEMO potential, which is then used to evaluate other structural and dynamical properties of the system. Multiconfigurational wave function calculations were performed to generate pair potentials between uranyl and water. The quantum chemical energies were used to fit parameters in a polarizable force field with an added charge term. Molecular dynamics simulations were performed for the uranyl ion up to 400 water molecules. The results showed a uranyl ion with five water coordinated in the equatorial plane. The U-water distance is 2,40 Å which is close to the experimental estimates. A coordination shell starts at 4,7 Å from the uranium atom. Exchange of waters between the first and second solvation shell is found to occur through a path intermediate between association and interchange. This study is the first fully ab determination of the solvation of the uranyl ion in water.

# THE ACTINIDE–ACTINIDE CHEMICAL BOND

After studying actinide-actinide molecules, the next question that one tries to answer is if it possible to form bonds between actinide atoms and, if so, what is the nature of these bonds? Experimentally, there is some evidence of such bonds in the gas phase and in a low-temperature matrix. The uranium diatomic molecule $U_2$ was detected in the gas phase in 1974.$^{88}$ The dissociation energy was estimated to be $50\pm5$ kcal/mol. Andrews and co-workers found both U2 and Th molecule isolation spectroscopy.$^{89}$ Both molecules were also found in the gas phase using vapor labeling of a solid uranium or thorium target.$^{90}$ Small molecules containing $U_2$ as a central unit were also reported, for example, $H_2U_3.$^{91}$ and OUO.$^{88}$ Not much was known theoretically about the nature of the chemical bond between actinides before the study of $U_2$ by Gagliardi and Roos.$^{86}$ The molecule was studied theoretically in 1990,$^{92}$ but the methods used were not enough to allow for a conclusive characterization of the chemical bond.
