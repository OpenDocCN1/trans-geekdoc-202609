The strongest bond between the two Re atoms is the σ bond, with an occupation number of nb=1.92 of bonding and nh=0.08 of the antibonding orbital.. We estimate the effective bond order as (nb−nh)/(nb+nh), and for the σ bond, we obtain the value 0.92. The corresponding value for the π bond gives an effective bond order of only 0.54. Adding up these numbers results in a total effective bond order of 3.20 for Re2Cl5. The main reduction of the bond order from 4.0 to 3.2 is due to the δ bond. Note that the calculation of natural occupation numbers by a CASSCF description of Re2Cl5 deviate from zero and is indicative of the limit.
