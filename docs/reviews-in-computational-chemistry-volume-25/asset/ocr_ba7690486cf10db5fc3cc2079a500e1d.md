The behavior of self-diffusivity for these state points is displayed in Figure 2(d). It is clearly visible that the non-monotonic dependence of $D$ on density $ho$ is directly reflected by the configurational contribution to the entropy. In fact, the quantitative relationship between $D$ and $S_C$ predicted by Eq. [10] (i.e., the AG relationship) holds remarkably well for SPC/E water over this wide range of thermodynamic conditions, as is shown in Figure 13.

An investigation by Saika-Voyd, Poole, and Sciortino⁹⁹⁰ provides another convincing example of a network-forming liquid that obeys the AG relationship (in this case, silica using the BKS model¹²⁰⁰). Again, the authors compared the behavior of the temperature- and density-dependent self-diffusivity, which they computed via molecular dynamics simulations, to the途中 entropy, obtained by the methods discussed earlier in this text. The BKS silica model represents a particularly interesting case because its self-diffusivity shows non-Arrhenius $T$ dependencies at high $T$ (so-called “fragile” behavior) and Arrhenius $T$ dependencies at low $T$ (so-called “strong” behavior). This type of fragile-to-strong transition for dynamics is discussed in more detail in Ref. 121. The AG relation holds again to a very good approximation as shown in Figure 14.
