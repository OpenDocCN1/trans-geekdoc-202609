The matrix elements $U_{LL_2L'L_3}$ can be determined by two parameters, the average on-site Coulomb repulsion $U$ and the exchange $J$,

```
$U = \frac{1}{(2l+1)^2} \sum_{mm'} U_{mm'mm'} \ [61]$
```

```
$U-J = \frac{1}{2(2l+1)} \sum_{mm'} (U_{mm'mm'} - U_{mm'mm}) \ [62]$
```

This can be seen by expressing $U_{LL_1LL_2}$ in terms of complex spherical harmonics and effective Slater integrals $F^k$ as in Eq. [63]$^{70}$

```
langle m,m'|U|m'',m''\rangle = \sum_{k} a_k(m,m',m'',m'') F^k \ [63]$
```

where $0 \leq k \leq 2l$

```
$a_k(m,m',m'',m'') = \frac{4\pi}{2k+1} \sum_{q=-k}^{k} \lm|Y_{kq}|l'm'\rangle \lm'|Y_{kq}|l''m''\rangle \ [64]$
```

Because we are dealing with d electrons, $l=2$, and the notation for $U_{LL_1LL_2}$ has been simplified to $U_{mm'm''m'}$ in Eqs. [61] and [62]. The Slater integrals $F^k$, which carry the radial integrations in Eq. [63], are of the type$^{71}$

```
$F^k(s,t) = \int dr' P_{n,l}(r') P_{n,l}(r) \frac{r^{\k}}{r^{\^{'k}} \ [65]$
```

where the symbols $s$ and $t$ refer to two different electrons and $P_{n,l}(r)$ is the product of the distance $r$ from the nucleus and the radial function of the electron $R_{nl}$. The terms $r<r'$ correspond to the smaller (larger). Only $F^0, F^2$, and $F^4$ are required for d electrons, which can be linked to the $U$ and $J$ parameters through the relations $U=F^0$ and $J=(F^2+F^4)/14$, whereas the ratio $F^2/F^4$ is a constant $\approx0.625$ for the $3d$ elements. In this formalism, the Stoner parameter, which determines the splitting of the bulk bands,$^{67}$ is $I=|2l+J|/(2l+1)$. Wan al.$48}$ chose $I=1.2$ eV in their cluster calculations, to have consistency with the splitting of the bands of bulk Ni in LSDA calculations. They used a value $U=2.6$ eV, although other values ($U=1.8$ eV, and $U=3.2$ eV) were explored.
