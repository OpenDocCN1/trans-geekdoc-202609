# MEASUREMENT AND INTERPRETATION OF THE MAGNETIC MOMENTS OF NICKEL CLUSTERS

# Interpretation Using Tight Binding Calculations

Bloomfield et al.$^3$ have performed accurate measurements of the magnetic moments of size-selected $Ni_N$ clusters with N between 5 and 700. Their results up to $N=60$ are plotted as black dots in Figure 2. The average magnetic moment $ar{\mu}$ of the cluster shows an overall decrease with increasing cluster size, while oscillations are superposed on this decreasing behavior. In the small size range, for $N<10$, where $ar{\mu}$ decreases most rapidly, there is a local minimum at $Ni_{16}$ and a local maximum at $Ni_8$. Thereafter, $ar{\mu}$ displays a deep minimum for $Ni_{13}$ and another minimum at $Ni_{56}$. The latter is so close to $Ni_{55}$ that it is tempting to conclude that the Ni clusters grow following an icosahedral pattern (clusters with perfect icosahedral structure and two shells, shown in Figure 3, have 15 and 55 atoms, respectively)$^{44}$. A third important minimum occurs around $Ni_{34}$. The magnetic moment goes through a broad maximum between $Ni_{13}$ and $Ni_{34}$, and again between $Ni_{34}$ and $Ni_{56}$.

Theoretical studies attempting to rationalize the behavior of the magnetic moment of $Ni_N$ clusters$^{45–48}$ have relied on the tight binding method.
