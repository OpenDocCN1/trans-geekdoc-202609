When a non-orthogonal basis is used, a generalized eigen own will ensue. Its solution becomes more involved than a non-diagonal overlap matrix, but methods to handle such problems exist and are referred to other sources for details.$^{18}$ One popular approach is to remove the linear dependence by diagonalizing the overlap matrix. This-called singular value decomposition (SVD) reduces a generalized eigenproblem to a regular one. In some techniques discussed below, the solution of a small generalized eigenproblem might be required.

There are several ways to diagonal a real-symmetric matrix. The common strategy is to use orthogonal matrices to gradually reduce the magnitude of off-diagonal elements.${18,19}$ The most widely and well-known approach is the Jacobi rotation, which zeros one element at a time.
