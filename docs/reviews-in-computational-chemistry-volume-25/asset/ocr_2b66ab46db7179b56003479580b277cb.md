The nature of the spin arrangement depends sensitively on the interatomic distances. A comparative study of Cr��, Mn�� (Mn clusters will be studied in the next section), and Fe was made by Fujita⁷ by assuming a pentagonal pyramid structure for the three clusters and including the variation of the interatomic distance $d$ from the bulk value $d_{bulk}$ up to a value 20% lower than $d_{bulk}$. The results are summarized in Figure 11. For Cr�� with $d_{bulk}$, the magnetic moments are arranged in a coplanar, noncollinear configuration; that is, the vector moments lie on the same plane but point in different directions. When $d$ decreases, the magnetic moments are ordered in a parallel (P) fashion. The situation is in Mn�� for interatomic distances close to $d_{bulk}$ that is, the cluster shows a coplanar configuration of the spins. However, a noncoplanar configuration first appears when $d$ decreases, which changes to a antiparallel (AP) configuration with a further decrease of $d$. Finally the arrangement of the spins in Fe is parallel for $d \approx d_{bulk}$ and $d \approx 0.8$ $d_{bulk}$.
