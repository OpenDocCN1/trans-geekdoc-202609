The diuranium chloride and diuranium calculations were performed at the CASSCF/CASPT2 level of theory. The active orbitals were those that describe the U-U bond. Enough orbitals were included such that the method can make the optimal choice between the $5f$ and $6d$ orbitals in forming the bonding and antibonding orbitals. The number of active electrons was eight for the $U_2^+$ unit and six for $U^+$. A basis set of the natural orbital type, including scalar relativistic effects, was used.

The U-U and U-Cl bond distances and the U–U–Cl angle have been optimized by the CASPT2 level of theory. The ground state of $U_2Cl_6$ is a singlet state with the electronic configuration $(\sigma_{	ext{g}})$^2$ $(\pi_{	ext{u}})$^4$.
