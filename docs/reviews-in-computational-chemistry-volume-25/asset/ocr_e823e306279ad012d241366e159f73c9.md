To investigate the effect of the thermostat on the frictional forces, it is instructive to study underdamped or slightly overdamped motion. In the following discussion, we will set $m=1, \gamma=1, V_0=1,$ and $k=0.5 V_{max}$. Damping $\gamma$ and temperature $T$ will be varied, but will first consider the athermal case where $T=0$. With this choice of parameters, the maximum curvature of the potential $V_{max}$ will be greater than $k$ so that oscillations occur during sliding and lead to finite friction at small $v_0$ in the absence of thermal fluctuations.

We see that the kinetic friction is insensitive to the precise choice of the thermostat for small values of $v_0$, at least when the thermostat is sufficiently small.
