Although CC and DFT (in agreement with experiment) predict the peroxo structure more stable than the bis(\mu$-oxo structure by about 10 kcal$^{-1}$, CASPT2 overestimates the stability of the bis(\mu$-oxo) isomer, by about 30 kcal$^{-1}$, independent of the active space used. We believe that such result is from the inadequacy of the active spaces used. Similar results are obtained for the isomerization reaction interconverting the ends-on $\mu-\eta^1 : \eta^1$ and the side-on $\mu-\eta^2 : \eta^2$ isomers. Extending the CASSCF/CASPT2 approach to as to handle larger active spaces, up to 28 electrons in 26 orbitals, seems to give promising results.$^{60}$
