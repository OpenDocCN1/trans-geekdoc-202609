# 3.3.1 FACIAL RECOGNITION

Nowadays, there are many facial recognition algorithms and techniques available from developers around the world. According to Salakanidu et al. (2008), facial recognition becomes an interesting research topic and has wide variety of applications in healthcare and industry. Different machine learning and neural network techniques are used to implement facial recognition.

Facial recognition is a significant research problem across different disciplines and numerous fields. Facial recognition initiates with feature extraction from the stored image. Features are width of eyes, pupil, mouth and other facial points. The facial recognition system performs a comparison of a given face with patterns stored in the database, and finds the closest (facial metrics). Facial recognition has numerous practical applications such as security, access control and surveillance systems; that is why in the last decade, a number of algorithms have been published and show improvement in terms of identification. A formal method of classifying faces was first proposed by Francis Galton (1888). Galton proposed a method for collecting facial profiles as curves and finding their norm, and based on the deviations from the norm, classifies faces with other profiles. This type of classification is multi-model classification; it provides results in independent measures of vectors and these vectors could be compared with others in the stored database. Continuous progress in the algorithm and advancing technology has explored real-world application and implementation for facial recognition systems. Facial recognition in early stage works on detection of face patterns in clustered scenes; those clustered scenes normalize the images to calculate the geometric changes (Chellappa et al., 1995).

The appearance of facial patterns identifies the faces using classification algorithms, and the model-based schemes and logic feedback is used for post processes. The availability of large databases of facial images, development of algorithms and evaluation methods are the factors for rapid development and achievements of facial recognition. Facial-related applications have to provide a still image or a video clip, and the system has to find the person available in the image or in the video.

This intelligent vision uses Python as a programming language and uses Open CV and facial recognition libraries with it to recognize the faces. Basically, in this project, the code finds the encoding of the people images from the database which we have to provide, and after that—based on that encoding—the code predicts the name of the face captured by the camera at runtime.
