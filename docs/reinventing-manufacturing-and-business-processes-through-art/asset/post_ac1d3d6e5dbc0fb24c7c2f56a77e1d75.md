AI applications also help in motivating employees to go green by using performance software and data analytics. Companies access employees' performance on daily, quarterly, and yearly bases, and provide them with feedback for self-appraisal and in overcoming their weaknesses. This feedback helps employees to identify how they can reduce waste, what practices they can follow to maintain a healthy lifestyle and how to focus on creative solutions for maintaining a green environment. Companies monitor their employees' activities with AI applications and reward the employees following green practices. Organizations find it challenging to meet the standards of green performance management and as it goes through various departments and staff units. Companies are trying to understand these issues and are trying to upgrade their performance appraisal systems by initiating green information systems to manage the standards of green performance management.

Companies manage their operations like manufacturing processes, warehouse management, and supply chain management using AI applications that are smart and help reduce waste and save resources. With AI applications and technologies, the chance of error reduces and companies can easily adopt GHRM practices.

# 1.4 CONCLUSION

In this dynamic scenario, when companies are shifting their priorities on attaining competitive advantage rather than profits, it also becomes their obligation to preserve the environment. It will make organizations the preferable brands for job seekers, and they will be able to attract and retain talent. Thus, organizations take measures for implementing green HRM policies. With attracting and retaining talent, companies also have to take care of existing workforce, because any random change can create resistance among the employees (Gupta & Haque, 2015). The role of AI helps organizations adopt GHRM services by offering various applications and smart technologies which simplify the work and increase productivity. AI is also important to be undertaken because diverse workforce includes Baby Boomers (born 1946–1960), Generation X (born 1961–1980), Generation Y (born 1981–2000) and Generation Z (born 1995–2012) (Naim & Lenka, 2017); Gen Y is achievement oriented and motivated by growth needs; therefore, their motivation to adopt GHRM practices is different (Wong et al., 2008). Generation Y employees expect to be rewarded for adapting GHRM practices (Jain & Lenka, 2018). On the contrary, Generation Z is tech-savvy, empowered, more realistic; therefore, they can be motivated using technology-driven tools and techniques (Singh, 2014; Sharma et al., 2021). With diversified workforces, organizations have to use AI tools and techniques to adopt GHRM practices. Previous studies identified training, recruitment and rewards as important factors for implementing GHRM practices (Nobari et al., 2018). Thus, AI tools and techniques can be used for these processes to engage and motivate employees to voluntarily participate in green activities. Adoption of green HRM activities is beneficial for environmental performance and organizational sustainability (Obeid et al., 2020).

The present study unveils the role of AI technologies and applications in adopting green HRM practices. In detail, study helps in identifying the relevant AI applications for the same. Previous studies also suggested that for effective implementation of e-HRM practices, use of AI, Big Data analytics and HR analytics should be taken into consideration (Pobau et al., 2020; Prakash et al., 2021). However, this study is based on review of literature, which can be explored further through case study and empirical work. Future studies can be conducted to identify the role of AI in adopting GHRM practices and can also be researched through conceptual or empirical work. Future research work can also be done to identify the motivating factors at individual, group/team and organizational levels which employees are influenced to voluntarily pursue GHRM practices. Future studies can also take into account the relationship between GHRM practices and green innovation as a step toward sustainability.
