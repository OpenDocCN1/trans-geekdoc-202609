## 7.1 INTRODUCTION

Since the First Industrial Revolution, machining has been the most critical manufacturing process. Metal cutting is needed in almost every sector, like household items, medical, automotive, defense, aerospace. Primary processes like casting and forming fail to give higher dimensional accuracy and surface finish. The main challenge is to achieve higher dimensional accuracy and surface finish.

The Second Industrial Revolution introduced mass production and assembly lines. The Third Industrial Revolution brought electronics and computers, which allowed for automation and numerical control. The Fourth Industrial Revolution, also known as Industry 4.0, is characterized by the fusion of physical, digital, and biological technologies.

The integration of Internet of Things (IoT) in manufacturing aims to improve efficiency, quality, and productivity. By using real-time data collection and analytics, companies can optimize their processes and predict maintenance needs more effectively. This paper discusses the roadmap for implementation of IoT in metal cutting for process monitoring systems.

## Reinventing Processes Through AI

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming how businesses operate. AI-driven tools can analyze vast amounts of data to identify patterns, predict trends, and automate decision-making. In manufacturing, AI can be used for quality control, predictive maintenance, and process optimization.
