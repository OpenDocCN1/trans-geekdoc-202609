## 6.1 Introduction
The first person who proposed artificial intelligence was Alan Turing in the year 1950. He introduced the Turing test to determine if machines are capable of thinking like humans.

### 6.1.1 Evolution of Artificial Intelligence
AI has existed for decades. In its most basic form, computers were able to play chess and solve puzzles.

### 6.1.2 Machine Learning and Deep Learning
AI is defined as the ability of the human brain but not restricted but applicable to many fields like linguistics, biology, philosophy, mathematics, and computer sciences.

### 6.1.3 Need for Artificial Intelligence
AI has the potential to resolve developmental challenges such as the lack of infrastructure in many nations.

## 6.2 Impact of Artificial Intelligence
In terms of potential, AI has the ability to radically modify how a person accesses goods and services, collects information, and interacts. In emerging economies, AI offers an opportunity to reduce costs and barriers to entry.

### 6.3 Impact of AI in various sectors
AI is gaining popularity in rapidly developing countries like China. Machine learning and deep learning have made tremendous progress due to their rapid expansion and improvement.

## 6.4 Conclusion
AI can be considered a new source of energy for trade strategies and models, which can be used to identify viable business situations, forecast novel conditions, and yield brilliant and swift business decisions.

## 6.5 Reinventing Processes Through AI
Every country grows in prototypes. With the eruption of the dot-com boom, innovative trade ideas and prototypes have risen. E-commerce has also evolved along with other financial services such as digital payments like Paytm, Google Pay, and online banking. By augmenting processes and procedures, these services have fastened transactions.
