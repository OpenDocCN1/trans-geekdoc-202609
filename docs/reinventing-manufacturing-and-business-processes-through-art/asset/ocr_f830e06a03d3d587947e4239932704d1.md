Controlling: Controlling includes checking, verifying and regulating whether everything done according to plan. Controlling function of management reviews personnel policies, programs, job evaluation, measuring the employee's performance, training and employee development.

+   2. Operative functions: The pervasive functions include key areas like process, development, motivation, compensation, maintenance, integration and emerging issues.

Procurement function: It is the first important function of human resource management. It includes procuring people who possess required skills, knowledge and aptitude. It includes manpower, recruiting and selecting candidates, job analysis (job description and specification), placement and internal mobility (transfers, layoffs, discharge, separation).

Development: This function considers developing skills, knowledge, attitudes and creative ability, and improving employees' performance with training. It is the process of drafting and directing training for all employees based on employee potential and performance.

Motivation and compensation : This includes adequate and equitable remuneration to all working in an organization. The remuneration motivates employees to give their best to the organization. They are facilitated with both intrinsic and extrinsic rewards.

Maintenance: It deals with sustaining the psychological and physical health of employees. It aims at caring and preserving the people working in organization through a variety of welfare programs.

Integration function: Managerial activities that human resource and organization through various employee-oriented programs are integrated. It includes activities like grievance redress, discipline, teamwork, collective bargaining, employee participation and industrial relations.

Emerging Issues: Effective human resource management depends on refining human resource practices to changing conditions. Some important issues affecting HR nowadays are personnel records, human resource audits, human resource information systems, human resource research, human resource accounting and international HRM. All these functions keep employee motivated to provide their best in a dynamic and ever-changing human resource practice.
