Although the concept of artificial intelligence was discussed as early as in the 1940s by Alan Turing, its growth has been exponential in the last decade. Researchers who focused on AI as Lin et al. Artificial intelligence is the ability of a system to understand and analyze data, to learn and become familiar with it and to use the knowledge to perform assigned tasks and reached goals with possibilities to adapt to situations presented. AI can help reducing human error and also shift focus to productive areas. Smart manufacturing is notably projected among many businesses in recent years. The advancement is growing into a new area of “intelligent manufacturing” which clearly reflects the impact of AI smart technologies on the manufacturing sector. It is estimated that the “intelligent manufacturing” market will be over $200 billion in the year 2021 and is expected to increase to over $320 billion by 2022, showing annual growth rate of 12.5% (Duke et al., 2020). Major companies like GE, Bosch and Microsoft started their investments in artificial intelligence long ago, the significant goal use of machine learning–based programs to improve all aspects of manufacturing. The major reasons for using AI in manufacturing are to cut costs, decrease product defects, reduce unplanned downtimes and also to increase productivity (Wang, 2019). While it might be possible for huge corporations to look into the possibility of using AI technology development strategy in manufacturing, this may not be for smaller manufacturers as of. Despite AI being a manufacturing sector revolution, it comes with its disadvantages high cost of implementation. AI is also generally thought of as lacking adaptivity and creativity. High growth in artificial intelligence is considered a reason for global unemployment risk, but companies claim that it is a widespread misconception that AI will reduce human employment. Even though AI development is projected to beat a high pace, like many technologically disruptive innovations before, adoption of AI is slowed by concerns about costs, scalability and security.

This chapter aims to provide a bird’s eye view of the future changes in the field of manufacturing due to the impact of the use of AI. The opportunities, threats and changes that one can expect are also touched upon.
