Moreover, green HRM practices can be adopted and implemented in the organizations. Top management holds the decision to initiate any change in the organization, and they must have orientation toward green environment which helps them to support green HRM practices (Obraid et al., 2020; Sawang & Kivitas, 2014; Teixeira et al., 2012). With effective leadership and employee commitment, top management changes the organizational culture by adopting green HRM practices such as green recruitment and selection, green job descriptions, green training and development, performance appraisal system, green safety and health provision, green employee and labor relations (Ahmad, 2015; Chaudhary, 2019; Gholami et al., 2016; Guerci et al., 2016; Jabbour, 2013; Jabbour et al., 2010; Jackson et al., 2021; O’Donohue & Turga, 2016; Renwick et al., 2018; Rana & Sharma, 2019; Srivastava et al., 2020; Tang et al., 2017; Zibarbar & Coon, 2015). Green HRM practices helps organizations attain attitudes toward adopting green behaviors and developing a green workforce (Aboramadan, 2020; Cabral & Dhar, 2019; Cabral & Dhar, 2020; Chaudhary, 2019; Dumont et al., 2017; Fahehimni et al., 2020; Islam et al., 2020; Jackson & Seo, 2010; Kim et al., 2019; Lu, 2017; Lu, 2018; Misha, 2017; Aboramadan, 2020; Mukherjee & Chandra, 2018; Ojo et al., 2020; Renwick et al., 2013; Rubel, 2021).

Green recruitment and selection organizations attract more talented employees-ees to their policies as most of the Generation Y looks for employees with green HRM practices (Chaudhary, 2019; Ahmed, 2015). European companies like transform themselves as green employer to attract talent (Siemens, BASF, Bayer, Manesmann and Rover Group use environmental activities as part of their job practices (Wehrmeyer, 1996). A systematic review was carried out over twenty-two reviewed articles and identified the recommendations of recruitment and selection, along mediators and moderators, to establish a relationship between applicant attraction outcomes and corporate environmental sustainability (Pâm & Paillé, 2019).

In order to transform the employees into a green workforce, organizations are integrating environmental performance with management systems to prevent any damage to the environment (Epstein & Roy, 1997). Companies install environmental performance standards and green information systems to use data on employees’ organizational performance (Alfred & Adam, 2009). They also initiate green training and educate employees about managing environment, minimizing waste, water conservation, conserving energy and providing them with opportunities to identify sustainable innovations to preserve the environment (Zoogah, 2011).
