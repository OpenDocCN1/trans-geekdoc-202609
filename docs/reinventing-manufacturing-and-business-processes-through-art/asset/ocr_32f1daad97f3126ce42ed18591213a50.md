# 7.7.3.3 Predictive Maintenance

In predictive maintenance, the prediction of the equipment's failure is carried out by examining the current condition of the equipment. For example, in the automobile, the period of changing the engine is predicted from the engine's appearance. Due to the development of sensors and artificial intelligence, the reliability of predictive maintenance is increased. The prediction of bearing failure can be made by sensor data like vibration, sound, current speed and temperature (Cakir et al., 2021)

# 7.7.3.4 Autonomous Maintenance

The equipment carries out autonomous maintenance daily before starting the equipment. This consists of checking lubricant level, noise level, pressure, etc. The maintenance departments provide a set of instructions to carry out the autonomous maintenance. Augmented reality can be a very efficient tool for training the operator for autonomous maintenance (Jia et al., 2021).

# 7.7.3.5 Breakdown Maintenance

The previously mentioned types of maintenance do not stop the machine from working, but in case of breakdown, maintenance equipment cannot perform the task, so immediate maintenance is required. Even if a machine is in operating condition despite the breakdown, it is not recommended to operate due to safety issues. For example, in the case of an automobile, the vehicle cannot be operated if there is a puncture in the wheel. If the brake fails, the vehicle can be operated but this is not recommended due to safety issues. Breakdown maintenance causes delays in manufacturing, so it is essential to avoid equipment breakdown. By using the previously mentioned maintenance techniques, reduction in breakdown is possible. The digital twin can be used to reduce the repair time during the equipment's breakdown (Aivalialotis et al., 2019).

# 7.7.4 OBLIGATORY STOPPAGES

Obligatory stoppages are those stoppages essential for metal cutting operation; with these stoppages, it is impossible to carry out the machining operation. The obligatory stoppages time can be minimized using sophisticated devices, but economic justification is required to use sophisticated devices.
