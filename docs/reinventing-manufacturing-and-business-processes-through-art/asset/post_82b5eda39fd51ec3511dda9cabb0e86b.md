## 6.2 NEED FOR ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN GLOBAL BUSINESS

Artificial intelligence is not only used for technological development but also applicable in marketing and business like automation in business, getting visions from data or engaging workers (Davenport, 2018). Artificial intelligence (AI) provides a technical solution to challenges faced not only by businesses but also by individuals at the bottom of the economy. Integration of data from a variety of sources, such as social media, websites, and communication channels, will aid in the development of firm’s data management, successful business strategies, and reduction of barriers to doing business (Arora & Rahman, 2017).

Artificial intelligence can be linked to frameworks derived from traditional techniques in today's world with focus on emerging economies. The fundamental concepts of microeconomic theory can be reassessed for the manifestation of new devices for making and augmenting decision-making (Parkes & Wellman, 2015). The introduction of asset-based model which can fit for employment shift was proposed as a radical intervention by McAfee whose focus was the basic approach on the basis of socialism, allocating prospective assets put forward to build a new working paradigm. There is no need for people to work in arduous jobs and be free from toil, consequently one's value can be reconsidered. With the practice of AI technology, people may start working with pleasure, with no pressure of pecuniary concern or to remain idle, as everything will be very systematic and done (McAfee, 2013).

Artificial intelligence has much potential to rebuild skill demands, career prospects and the systematic distribution of workers and employees between industries. Although people involved in researches and policymaking are not sufficiently equipped for labor trend resulting from specific cognitive technologies like AI, technology needs to perform typical tasks that alters the demand for specific skills at the workplace (Sharma & Rana, 2021). The result of such alteration can be drawn all over the economy, increasing occupational skill requirements, career mobility and social wellbeing and workers will have some social identity. There may be some hurdles in the specific pathways to identify such alternatives which can be constrained by historical data and limited tools for modeling resilience, which can be overcome by prioritizing in-depth data collection, responsive to actual dynamics in the labor market, and regional inconsistency. Better accessibility to unstructured data from the résumé of candidates, job postings with latest indicators for technological changes like the use of patented data and models for inter-region and intra-region dependencies will facilitate a new and promising technique for better understanding and future forecasting of work. The improved technique of this data collection will help us use new data-driven tools, adding machine learning and systematic modeling which can more accurately reflect the complexity of the whole labor system. New data will lead to a new kind of research that enhances our learning and understanding of the impact of technology and artificial intelligence on the modernized labor market (Frank et al., 2019).

Artificial intelligence has reached a place where it can be used to take the practical world to financial decisions, chat with the public, play games against humans and work collectively with them. There is only AI-driven systems or intelligent agents (IA) behind these real-world applications, which interact with the environment in a repetitive manner to sense, think and act. Considerations from the world to make an informed decision based on input data and previous experience and perform an action affecting the environment. It can work like a machine in industries, home robots or self-driven cars; it can also work as a software agent, chatbot, recommendation systems, and more. AI algorithms take information in the form of images, sound, text, videos, etc., analyze and deliver AI-powered solutions (Soni et al., 2019).

AI not only gives reliability but also cost-effectiveness and solves complex problems and helps to make an accurate decision. Similarly, it controls the missing data call; that is why it is used in many fields of business and engineering. Reinforcement learning—which is based on testing success and failure in the real world to increase the reliability of applications—is one of the best tools used in AI, also has its limitations with competency and functionality (Sadek & Chowdhury, 2012).
