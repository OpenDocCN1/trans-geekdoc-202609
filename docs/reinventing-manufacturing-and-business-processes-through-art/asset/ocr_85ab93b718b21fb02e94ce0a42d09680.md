# 4.1.1.4 Advantages of Implementing AI in Manufacturing

Implementing AI in manufacturing will help organizations with defect detection as AI and self-learning capabilities can save many hours in quality control (Nikolic et al., 2017). Similarly, AI can be adopted for quality assurance as using image processing algorithms to spontaneously check whether an item has been perfectly manufactured (Kim et al., 2018). AI helps in assembly line integration by creating an interconnected application that extracts data from IoT connected equipment to ensure correct operation. Additionally, implementation of AI helps an generate designs for its products. The algorithm then checks all possible outcomes of a solution and suggests design alternatives (Hayhoe et al., 2019).

# Implementation of AI in Manufacturing Will Enable

+   - Mass customization (Wu et al., 2017).
- Implementation of robots (Sharp et al., 2018).
- Predictive maintenance that would help a firm to minimize its operation and maintenance costs (Nica et al., 2021).
- Software as a Service (SaaS): AI-based supply chain management solutions (Tao et al., 2018).
- Improve human–robot collaboration (Zhou et al., 2018).

AI and machine learning tools can create a unified ecosystem enabling continuous exchange of information. This makes the system self-sustaining, and also improves the process in every facet of supply chain management (Alonso, 2020).

# 4.1.1.5 AI as a Catalyst to Smart Manufacturing

AI can monitor thousands of data sources relating to manufacturing, with analysis of any news, event or incident that could affect production (Mao et al., 2019). It would help organizations to deepen their customer insight and understanding (Alexopoulos et al., 2020). Through AI, organizations can customize and create easy-to-understand dashboards that capture customers real-time alerts when any incident occurs during production (Ghabakhloo & Ching, 2019). It reduces the time taken to identify potential issues in production activity, enabling rapid response to address such issues before they escalate. AI quantifies and lowers the risk of incidents across the supply chain and during production (Gahramani et al., 2020) It enables more informed decisions regarding the overall manufacturing process. Finally, AI helps to improve customer loyalty and the bottom line.
