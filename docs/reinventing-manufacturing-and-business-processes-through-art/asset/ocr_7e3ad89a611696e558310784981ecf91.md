# 1.4 CONCLUSION

In this dynamic scenario, when companies are shifting their priorities on attaining competitive advantage rather than achieving profits, it also becomes their obligation to preserve the nature and environment. It will also make organizations the preferable employer brands for the job seekers, and they will be able to attract and retain talent. Thus, organizations take measures for implementing green HRM policies. Along with attracting and retaining talent, companies also have to take care of their existing workforce, because any random change can create resistance among the employees (Gupta & Haque, 2015; Gupta & Lenka, 2018).
