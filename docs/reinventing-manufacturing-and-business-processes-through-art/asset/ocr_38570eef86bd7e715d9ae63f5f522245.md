AI can be used in the evaluation of credit worthiness of users based on their past records and their current digital footprint, such as phone records. This has huge implications in loan disbursement and underwriting, where financial institutions accept financial risk for a fee. Another area where the power of AI can be leveraged is trading. AI can produce data-driven, timely and low-risk forecasts while considering a whole host of variables, something that would not be possible manually. Trading portfolios can be designed faster and corrective actions in real time to prevent losses. A new term, "bionic advisory," refers to the combination of in-depth machine calculations tempered by insights from human experience, is becoming quite popular. Finally, hours of mundane work can be automated, and employees engaged in such processes can be diverted to other important work, cutting down operational costs at all financial institutions.
