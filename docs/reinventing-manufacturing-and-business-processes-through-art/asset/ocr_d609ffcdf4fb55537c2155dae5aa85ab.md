Fourth Industrial Revolution or Industry 4.0 has many subparts and attributes where artificial is one of the most important element.

**Artificial intelligence:** Advanced AI can be called rational when control includes self-directed modifications to the operating system which can be performed through simulation. As per the latest revolution, the simulation modeling pattern facilitates modeling of the production process and other processes with the use of computer-generated manufacturing concepts. Simulations are completed with the concurrent data to symbolize the real world in a simulation model that comprises human beings, goods and machinery. Hence, machinists can augment the machine settings in a computer-generated simulation earlier applying in the real world, and this leads to reductions in machine setup times and increases the quality (Rodić, 2017).

**Data science:** Data sciences are a combination of statistics, information, computing, sociology and management which can control social aspects, and science which acts on data, environment and the so-called data-to-knowledge-wisdom thinking (Cao, 2017).

**Big data:** Data analytics has now become the recent topic in Industry 4.0, as it is very useful for predictive manufacturing and works in the direction of industrialized technology progress with advanced internet.
