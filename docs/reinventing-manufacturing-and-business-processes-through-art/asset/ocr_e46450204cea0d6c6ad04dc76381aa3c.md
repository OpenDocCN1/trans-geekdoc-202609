FIGURE 4.1 Proposed model to understand AI capabilities. AI capabilities refer to the organizational ability to adopt and use AI in value creation.

AI management capabilities refer to management's ability to use technology to create value for the organization. The potential is "strategic planning," resulting in better coordination and control.

AI expertise refers to the skillset and knowledge of the human resources of a firm specific to AI-related technologies.

AI infrastructure refers to the availability of all the resources—including the hardware, software, data, the required systems and network—for the effective implementation of an AI system.
