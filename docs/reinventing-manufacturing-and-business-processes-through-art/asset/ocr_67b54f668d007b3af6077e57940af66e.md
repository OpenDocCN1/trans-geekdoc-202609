# Impact of AI on Global Business Practices

AI in innovation and technology: With the adoption of AI, the world becomes smarter and more innovative. Use of Google Maps for route and traffic mapping, price determination of rides by Uber and Lyft, catching a friend and getting filtering spam emails, online shopping and recommendations and detection of disease are all of the examples of AI technological innovations which have simplified our lives. The unbelievable internet speed with which AI is entering every sector at the global level pressing organizations to adopt AI, which is also compelling business, policymakers, innovators, entrepreneurs and investigators to adapt for the development of new strategies and generation of new sources of business value (Soni et al., 2019).

Alibaba is the biggest e-commerce platform in the world, selling more than Amazon and eBay combined. Artificial intelligence (AI) is used to predict what consumers want to buy in Alibaba's daily operations. The company uses natural language processing to create product descriptions for the website. Alibaba also employs artificial intelligence in other ways. Alibaba's City Brain project, which aims to develop smart cities, is another example of artificial intelligence in action. By tracking every car in city, the project uses AI algorithms to alleviate traffic congestion. Alibaba's cloud computing, Alibaba Cloud, is also using artificial intelligence to help farmers monitor crops in order to increase yield and cut costs.

AI in the economy: By increasing efficiency, business process automation, financial solutions and government services, AI-based technologies can expand the market and create more opportunities. Inequality and poverty can be reduced by empowering AI in the public and private sectors to collaborate on leapfrogging solutions in emerging and global markets (Andrews et al., 2019). Technological upgrades in societal development, improvement in production, marketing and finance are all signs of economic development, and artificial intelligence is complementing these sectors to achieve better lives. Singapore has AI technology Singapore has chosen five major AI-driven development projects to resolve national challenges while taking into account the effects on society and the economy.

AI in society: AI has its impact on humans and society as well, where its involvement gives a new meaning of work as self-worth, people will work for pleasure instead of compulsion, stability and security at workplace will be ensured. Although machines are not creative, nor have empathy, compassion or emotional intelligence, working with such machines will increase their emotional quotients which will change the outlook and attitude toward life which will lead to societal development (Pavalou, 2016). In recent years, AI has got a lot of buzz and has become more accessible to us as a result of internet-based innovation. It has risen to the forefront of many current debates as a result of these advances, as well as interest in the technology's potential socioeconomic and ethical consequences, and governments are attempting to comprehend the implications of technology for their people. In Singapore, educational institutions have placed a greater focus on fields such as mathematics, statistics, computer science and information technology in order to comprehend the importance and trends of data science, machine learning and artificial intelligence.
