**Extended Data Fig. 5: Efficacy of transfer learning across different numbers of gestures.** Confusion matrices of the prediction results are based on a, one-shot, b, three-shot, and c, four-shot transfer learning for gesture recognition. In one-shot transfer learning, the minimum observed accuracy is 69% for gesture 5, which is considered suboptimal. This limitation is attributed to the fact that a single gesture signal is obtained from an individual only one posture: either sitting or lying down. When two or more shots are used in transfer learning, gesture signals during both sitting and lying down are collected. As a result, the recognition accuracy can stabilize and converge. Therefore, two-shot transfer learning has been adopted because it balances the time for collecting signals and the accuracy (Fig. 3c).
