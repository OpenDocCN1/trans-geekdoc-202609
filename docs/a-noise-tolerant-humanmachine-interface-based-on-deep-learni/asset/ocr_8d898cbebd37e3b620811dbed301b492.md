**Extended Data Fig. 2.** Uniaxial horizontal stretchability of the sensors. **a**, Photos of the sensors under horizontal 100-cycle stretchability test from 0 to 20%. **b**, Photos of the sensors under vertical 100-cycle stretchability test from 0 to 20%. **c**, Finite element analysis of each layer in the sensors under 25% uniaxial stretching. The analysis confirms that the maximum strain does not exceed 6.3%, indicating that the Ga electrodes remain well within their elastic limit and

do not yield or undergo plastic deformation. To mitigate computational load, mechanical simulations were performed on layers layers than the entire assembly. In the actual sensors, vertical interconnect accesses layers can lead to localized strain concentrations when stretched. Consequently, the simulations assumed a higher stretchability (25%) than the cyclic mechanical test (20%).
