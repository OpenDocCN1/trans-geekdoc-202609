Existing methods for mitigating artifacts in IMUs can be broadly categorized into hardware- and software-based approaches (Supplementary Note 2). For hardware-based approaches, reducing the device thickness or enhancing the adhesion between the device and the

skin can minimize artifacts caused by displacement of the skin–sensor interface. Although this method is effective in bipolar measurement, it is essential for IMUs, which inherently capture unintentional depletments during body movements.
