An example of these conspiracy theories are the claims that a charity tennis match in South Africa, featuring Trevor Noah and Bill Gates, was a cover-up for celebrities to arrange testing for the vaccines in Africa. The conspiracy gained legitimacy when a small political party formally put questions to President Ramaphosa regarding the claims, which was followed by media coverage [LER 20]. Further to the disinformation described above, social media was a significant factor for the riots and looting in July 2021 where it was used as a tool for incentive; additionally, videos unrelated to the riots were distributed and misrepresented to inflame the situation and further divide communities [BAT 21, NET 21, PIL 21].
