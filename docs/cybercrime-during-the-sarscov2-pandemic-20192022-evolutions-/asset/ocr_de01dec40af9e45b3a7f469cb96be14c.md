Interpreting CERT data alone, the pandemic period did not produce the drastic increase in cybercrime that many reports and articles around the world reported. It therefore becomes imperative to draw a distinction between specific cybercriminal phenomena showing a sharp increase (online fraud in Penang is reported to have multiplied by four in the space of one year, between 2019 and 2020, according to police data.
