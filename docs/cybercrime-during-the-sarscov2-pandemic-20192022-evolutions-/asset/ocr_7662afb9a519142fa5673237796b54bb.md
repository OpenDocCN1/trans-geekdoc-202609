**Figure 1.20.** Respective evolution of the "stay-at-home" index and the number of defaced websites detected in China since January 2020

Cyberattacks defacing websites are less and less frequent. This type of criminal activity, often politicized, tends to decrease everywhere in the world. China’s situation is part of this global dynamic.

Like others, the "backdoor infected websites" variable evolved independently, not being correlated with periods featuring lifestyle changes (lockdown and social restriction), and following a clearly downward trend. This decrease could be interpreted as the strengthening of Chinese cybersecurity capabilities, successfully preventing attacks against its servers. The pandemic seems not to have affected its cybersecurity capabilities.
