# 1.5.3. Heterogeneity and complexity of cybercrime typologies

The level of detail of cybercrime types used in each country can also be an obstacle to their international comparison. But this complexity can also act as a barrier in the process of entering information by police officers or CERT employees, or even by statistical agencies.

In India, for example, the categories of cyberimes taken into account in the statistics of the Ministry of Home Affairs are based on the IT Act (ITA) and the IPC (Indian Penal Code). These annual statistics, whose reports have been published since 1953, added the cybercrime category in 2002.
