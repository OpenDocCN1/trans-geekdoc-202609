The data on "cybercrime" used in this study have been drawn from CERTs⁴ (number of cyber incidents received and recorded) and police statistics (number of complaints received, cases processed, individuals arrested). These organizations publish statistics, whose frequency can be annual, monthly, sometimes weekly, and rarely, The level of detail of the information published is not standardized, which means that the categories of cyber incidents and cybercrimes or offenses taken into account are not identical everywhere.
