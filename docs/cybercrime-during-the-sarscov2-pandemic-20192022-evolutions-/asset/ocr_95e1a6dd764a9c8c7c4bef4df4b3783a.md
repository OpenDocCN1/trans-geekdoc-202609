# 16 Cybercrime During the SARS-CoV-2 Pandemic (2019-2022)

Finding 1.6: The evolution curve of the mobility index at the workplace clearly emphasizes the lockdown and teleworking periods at the national level. When the index increases, it means fewer people are moving to the workplaces. The cyber incident reporting curve evolved at the same pace, but in a diametrically opposite direction: the lower the mobility to the workplace, the higher the number of reported cyber incidents. This relationship remained constant throughout the 2020–2022 period.
