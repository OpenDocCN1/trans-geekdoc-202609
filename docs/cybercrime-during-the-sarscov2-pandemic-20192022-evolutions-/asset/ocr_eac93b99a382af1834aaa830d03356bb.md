The most important fact is that Brazil finally is in the ongoing process of acceding to the Budapest Convention. The Brazilian Senate promulgated Congress Decree No. 37 and published it on December 17, 2021 (Brazilian Federal Senate, 2021). The last step is the deposit of the ratification. We can assume the Covid crisis was fundamental for the country to pursue accession to this Convention.
