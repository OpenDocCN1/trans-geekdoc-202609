# 11.6 SIMILARITY DETECTION WITH SPACY

For example, some people believe that the works of William Shakespeare actually might have been written by Sir Francis Bacon, Christopher Marlowe or others. 5 Comparing the word frequencies of their works with those of Shakespeare can reveal writing-style similarities.
