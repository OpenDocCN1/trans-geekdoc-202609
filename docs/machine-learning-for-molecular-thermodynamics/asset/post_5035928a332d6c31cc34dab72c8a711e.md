## 4.2. Thermodynamic properties of alkanes

Alkanes are one of the most critical petroleum-based materials in chemical engineering. Obtaining their thermodynamic properties is necessary for chemical engineering design and discovery.

The Sun group developed an FNN (Fig. 9) to predict the thermodynamic properties, including density (ρ), and heat capacity (Cp). The building of the model was based on HT-FFS results. They divided the results into three parts, the training (70%), validation (20%) and the test (10%).

The prediction performance was measured by the predicted value and HT-FFS result. For all thermodynamics properties, ρ, Ei and Cp FNN prediction is in excellent agreement with simulation. The RMSEs in the test set are 3.1 × 10⁻³ g cm⁻³ for ρ, 0.30 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ for Ei and 2.8 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ for Cp.

## 6. Perspective

The examples above show the promising role of machine learning in predicting and modeling properties of chemical materials. Material design also benefits from the combination of machine learning and high-throughput simulations. Machine learning force field can accelerate the computation of thermodynamics in complex systems. However, application of machine learning in chemical engineering remains in a primitive stage, due to the scarcity of datasets in this area of champions. Some methods may be expensive, unlike in NLP or computer vision, prediction models in chemistry and material science are often scarce. The challenges include the uncertainty of the model. Thus, establishing databases with high data diversity and reliability is of great significance for future machine learning applications. We expect more applications that combine high-throughput simulation and machine learning. Another issue is the interpretability of another important application in the field of quantum mechanics and all-atomic molecular dynamics. Current machine learning potential fields face some challenges, such as the limitation of element type and sensitivity with input configurations. Machine learning potentials have been developed and successfully accelerated simulations and it is becoming a powerful tool for computational chemistry.
