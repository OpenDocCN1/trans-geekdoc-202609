# 6. Perspective

The examples above show the promising role of machine learning in predicting and modeling properties of chemical materials. Material design also benefits from the combination of machine learning and high-throughput simulations. Machine learning force field can accelerate the computation of thermodynamics in complex systems. However, application of machine learning in chemical engineering remains in a primitive stage, due to the scarcity of datasets in this area of champions. Some methods may be expensive, unlike in NLP or computer vision, prediction models in chemistry and material science are often scarce. The challenges include the uncertainty of the model. Thus, establishing databases with high data diversity and reliability is of great significance for future machine learning applications.
