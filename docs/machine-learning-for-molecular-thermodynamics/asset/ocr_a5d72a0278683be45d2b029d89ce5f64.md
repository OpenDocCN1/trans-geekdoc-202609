Fig. 6 describes the primary architecture of DeepSol. Bidirectional recurrent neural networks (BRNNs) [28] were employed as an encoder to extract crucial features from both solvents and solutes. Inspired by NLP, an attention layer was used to recognize important features from RNNs output. Attention mechanism [130] is an approach that can interpret the alignment between input and output. It has been used to improve RNN performance in sequence recognition and machine translation. This approach develops chemists' interests for it to produce interpretable results from machine learning studies [131,132]. In this study, the attention mechanism dominates the free energy prediction. The results show that polarity and hydrophobicity of molecular structures play important roles during solvation, showing consistent chemical intuition. Prediction of properties measured through 10-fold set all compounds in the test set, the RMSE and MAE were 0.657 kcal mol⁻¹ (1 cal=4.1868 J) and 0.330 kcal mol⁻¹, and the Pearson correlation coefficient was up to 0.96.
