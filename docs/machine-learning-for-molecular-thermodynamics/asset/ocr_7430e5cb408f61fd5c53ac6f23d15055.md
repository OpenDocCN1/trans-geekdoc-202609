4.2. Thermodynamic properties of alkanes

Alkanes are one of the most critical petroleum-based materials in chemical engineering. Obtaining their thermodynamic properties is necessary for chemical engineering design and discovery

The Sun group developed an FNN (Fig. 9) to predict the thermodynamic properties, including density (ρ), and heat capacity (Cp). The building of the model was based on HT-FFS results. They divided the results into three parts, the training (70%), validation (20%) and the test (10%).

The prediction performance was measured by the predicted value and HT-FFS result. For all thermodynamics properties, ρ, Ei and Cp FNN prediction is in excellent agreement with simulation. The RMSEs in the test set are 3.1 × 10⁻³ g cm⁻³ for ρ, 0.30 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹ for Ei and 2.8 J mol⁻¹ K⁻¹¹ for Cp.
