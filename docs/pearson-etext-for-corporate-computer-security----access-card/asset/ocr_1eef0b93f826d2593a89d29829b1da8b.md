# Figure 9-16 RAID Level 5

![](bbox=[0.3, 0.1, 0.9, 0.38])

Parity bits enable reconstruction of data on lost disk
Both parity and striping are used
Can recover from one lost disk, but not two
Requires a minimum of 3 disks

Recovery. Suppose in the shipping example there was a fire in Warehouse 3, as shown in Figure 9-17. Box 4, Box 6, and Parts 1&2 would be destroyed. However, the good news is that there is enough inventory in the other warehouses to reconstruct the destroyed boxes.

Inventory from Warehouse 1 (Box 1, Box 3, and Parts 5&6), together with inventory from Warehouse 2 (Box 2, Box 5, and Parts 3&4), could be used to re-create the lost boxes (Box 4, Box 6, and Parts 1&2). No inventory would be lost.
