In contrast to business continuity efforts, IT disaster recovery, as Figure 10-21 shows, is restoring IT functions after a disaster. IT disaster recovery may be part of a firm’s broader business continuity effort after a disaster, or it may exist on its own—say, when there is a fire in a data center.
