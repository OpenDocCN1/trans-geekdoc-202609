# Federal Surveillance Laws

So far, we’ve looked at organizations monitoring their own employees. You also need to be aware of laws that govern the monitoring and surveillance by the U.S. government. Corporations are also affected by a larger societal-level security environment. This macro security environment, typically shaped by governments, can affect new product innovation, new market entrants, competition among existing firms, international trade, and so on. Legislators struggle to find a balance between ensuring a safe society and providing enough freedom to encourage innovation.
