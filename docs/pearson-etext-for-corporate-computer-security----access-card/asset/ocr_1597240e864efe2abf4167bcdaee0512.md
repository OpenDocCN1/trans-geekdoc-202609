# R&D versus Espionage

Funding R&D projects for new drugs, technology, aircraft, and clean energy can be extremely costly. For some corporations and governments, these costs are too high. But there is a way to bypass R&D costs—espionage. Why develop a new drug when you can steal it at 1/100th the cost?

A 2018 report from the National Counterintelligence and Security Center (NCSC) detailed several threats to U.S. corporations in the form of economic espionage by

foreign collectors.⁹ The report notes that there is little risk of getting caught by their targeted corporations. The use of proxies, routing through multiple networks/hosts, and custom malware make identifying the crooks difficult. Even if the theft was noticed and the cyber crooks identified, complications with international extradition would make prosecution nearly impossible.
