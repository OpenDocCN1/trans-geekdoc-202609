# Confidentiality in Message Transmission

We have just seen that 18 U.S.C. § 1030 deals with multiple types of cybercrime. However, other parts of the federal code are also important in prosecuting cybercrime. For example, 18 U.S.C. § 2511 prohibits the interception of electronic messages, both en route and after the message is received and stored.

The law does allow e-mail service providers to read e-mail. This means that a company owning its e-mail system may read employee e-mail without asking its employees’ permission. However, if the company outsources e-mail, this freedom may not exist.

# Other Federal Laws

Of course, the federal government has many other laws involving fraud, extortion, the theft of trade secrets, and many other matters. If computer intrusions involve these other laws, the penalties in these other laws—which usually are substantially higher—will apply.
