The attack on Spamhaus illustrates the level of coordination, persistence, and technical resources attackers have at their disposal. A group with the technical ability and motivation to carry out a continuous operation against an organization is known as an advanced persistent threat (APT). The locations, motivations, and actions of well-known APTs are monitored and recorded on a regular basis.

A 2013 report by Mandiant detailed the operations of one of the largest and most prolific groups known as “APT1.”25 Mandiant research showed that China’s “People’s Liberation Army (PLA)’s Unit 61398 is similar to APT1 in its mission, capabilities, and resources. PLA Unit 61398 is also located in precisely the same area from which APT1 activity appears to originate.” The implications of possible government-sponsored cyber espionage against businesses are worrying.

The following are a few of the additional key findings from the Mandiant report.

+   1. APT1 is believed to be the 2nd Bureau of the People’s Liberation Army (PLA) General Staff Department’s (GSD) 3rd Department, which is most commonly known by its Military Unit Cover Designator (MUCD) as Unit 61398.
+   2. APT1 has systematically stolen hundreds of terabytes of data from at least 141 organizations.
+   3. APT1 focuses on compromising organizations across a broad range of industries in English-speaking countries.

+   4. APT1 maintains an extensive infrastructure of computer systems around the world.
+   5. In over 97 percent of the 1,905 times Mandiant observed APT1 intruders connecting to their attack infrastructure, APT1 used IP addresses registered in Shanghai and systems set to use the Simplified Chinese language.
+   6. The size of APT1’s infrastructure implies a large organization with at least dozens, but potentially hundreds of human operators.
+   7. In an effort to underscore that there are actual individuals behind the keyboard, Mandiant is revealing three personas that are associated with APT1 activity.
+   8. Mandiant is releasing more than 3,000 indicators to bolster defenses against APT1 operations.
