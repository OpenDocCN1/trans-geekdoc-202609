# ## Biometric Methods

**FINGERPRINT RECOGNITION** Thanks to crime movies, almost everyone is familiar with fingerprint recognition. **Fingerprint recognition** technology is well developed and inexpensive. Fingerprint scanners are cheap enough to be added to computers and even small handheld devices. Due to their low cost, fingerprint scanners account for most of the total biometrics market.
