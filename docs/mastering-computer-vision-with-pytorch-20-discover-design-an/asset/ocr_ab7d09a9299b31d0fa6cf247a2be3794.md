# Definition and Overview

Imagine being an expert pianist, and you decide to learn the violin. While these are distinct instruments, your knowledge of music theory, rhythms, and coordination transfer over and can make the learning process quicker. This exemplifies the essence of transfer learning in the context of artificial intelligence (AI): leveraging knowledge from one domain to expedite learning in another.

In technical terms, transfer learning repurposes the layers of neural networks trained on vast amounts of data, typically on large-scale tasks like ImageNet, to benefit smaller or related problems. This not only saves time and computational resources but also allows for the development of models in domains where data is scarce.
