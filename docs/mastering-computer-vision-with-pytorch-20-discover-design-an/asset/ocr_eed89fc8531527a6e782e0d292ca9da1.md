# Common Metrics and Evaluation Criteria:

+   • **Precision and Recall in Object Detection:** The twin metrics of Precision and Recall offer a dual lens through which the effectiveness of an object detector can be discerned. Precision indicates the accuracy of the positive predictions made by the model, whereas Recall measures the model’s ability to find all the relevant instances in the dataset. For object detection, these metrics are calculated with respect to detected bounding boxes and their overlaps with ground truth annotations, representing the model’s finesse and thoroughness.

+   • **Intersection over Union (IoU):** Intersection over Union is a fundamental metric in object detection that quantifies the overlap between the predicted bounding box and the ground truth box. An IoU score close to 1 suggests an excellent prediction, whereas a score nearer to 0 indicates little to no overlap.
